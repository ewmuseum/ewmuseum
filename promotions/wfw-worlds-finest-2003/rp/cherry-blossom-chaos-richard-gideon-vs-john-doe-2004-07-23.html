Cherry Blossom Chaos: Richard Gideon vs. John Doe

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 July 2004 Remember, all RP conducted for Cherry Blossom Chaos should be from various locations in the Washington, DC Area.
The RP/Angle deadline is Friday, August 6th 11:59PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Methos17 — 24 July 2004 (The camera fades in to the Mall at Washington D.C. Kids are playing on the grass, people are milling about and altogether enjoying a fun time in our nation's capitol. As the camera scans this bevy of sight-seeing, Richard Gideon is seen walking along the perimeter of the pool. As the cameras approach, he nods toward them and begins to speak)
Richard Gideon: Well, it seems we've come around to the home stretch here in the WFW. Cherry Blossom Chaos...here in Washington D.C., home of G.W., the Redskins and public humiliation via C-Span. After my match with Bartleby, I have to say I am in dire need of a tune-up....a "shake-up of my normal paradigm" if you will. You see folks, that mystery man rocked me and nearly beat me, because I made the mistake of getting cocky again. But for what reason? This time around, instead of being in the WFW World Heavyweight Title Bout, I am instead facing a gentleman by the name of John Doe. Before I would pout and complain this man isn't in my league. That he is merely a stepping stone to my return to the title scene. However, I understand that much like Jason in the Friday The 13th series, I'll always come back. So until that time comes, I'll do what I do best: wrestle anyone, anywhere.
(Gideon bends down to wave his hand in the pool and continues) Richard Gideon: John, you're going to be the second mystery man I've faced in as many shows....at least in name only. In talking with the WFW brass I find out that you're a decent wrestler, so I'm going to be quite excited to see what you got in that ring. In case you have been living under a rock, let me introduce myself to you. My name is Richard Gideon...also known to some as "The System". I'm the longest-reigning WFW North American Champion ever, and I'm one of the best and brightest here in the WFW. I sincerely hope you're bringing the A-game to your debut here Mr Doe....otherwise I'll be putting a tag on your toe and calling it a day quite quickly in that ring. And no matter what happens in that ring, realize this: I'm going into this match as if it's a Title Match. So that means you better do the same.
(Fade Out) John Doe — 27 July 2004 FADEIN…. Cameras pulling back showing at WFW backdrop and John Doe walking towards the backdrop from the left of the screen. John halts and turns looking at the camera. John grabs a stool from off camera and sits on it. He looks in the camera then titles his head.
DOE: Ah…it seems WFW finally came knocking at the front door and begged for me to enter. I mean after all my hard work in NEW, NWL, and EPW I wouldn’t doubt that they wanted me to be in WFW. So they call me over and I assume they are going to give me an opponent who can make some sort of logical sense, but of course I made a bad assumption.
Frankly, I really don’t care much for this match up, I see it as just another man that THINKS he can step up to me and THINKS he is going to beat me senseless…..the word of advise from me to you Gideon.
GET A F*CKING CLUE… Sure your three minute filled the air waves with random rants that are just pure stupidity, but hell I know you could have come out with a BETTER promotion than that crap I just had to sit through, honestly that was a waste of my time, I could have sat here and watched tape or watched South Park rather than see that, at least that keeps me entertained.
But no, you have been around WFW and have been here SOOO LONG and we mustn’t forget that you have beaten so many people…
Shove it… I can care less. The more your little lips flap the bigger beaten you are going to receive at Cherry Bloom Chaos But no, no, no, you are a eastern, you think I care one damn bit about you, the man I want to face is Sean Edmunds, test the true masters of the ring. You are just a standby until the fed heads, find that I am main event material. Until that time I face other JOBBERS. Which I hate to consider my self such a thing. But I guess technically that’s where we all start. Looks like you are going to get beat by the rookie.
Watch out people, Richard is going to treat this like it was a title match, that I should come out and treat it as thoguth it was a title match also! Well, I assure you Richard that I will come out and wrestle you, but I HIGHLY doubt i have to be at teh top of my game with such a man as you.
Though, I will coem out at the top of my game, which is three steps ahead of you. You know what, how about you save yourself teh trouble and just not show up. I mean it is going to save you a loss and a hospital bill in the long run.
But you went you of your way to find out about me. At least you know your opponent, at least you know why you are going to lose *chuckles*. But hell! You are "The System"! You can beat anybody...well Gideon, just to tell you....
SYSTEM FAILURE..... But hell if you are so great as you state that you are, than I should have heard your name somewhere. Or maybe I didn’t maybe it so happens that you are a no one. That you are a man that sees himself in the spotlight, but really and honestly is stuck no where. Hell I plan o turn heads here in WFW, just like I do everywhere else.
But hell the funny part about it is that you are goign to get teh beat down of your life, right here in DC. The nations capital, and I will beat you in a quick strong sweep. Just be ggrateful you get to step in the ring with me.
All I say is let your ignorance consume you, by the time it has finished you will be my b*tch and you will bow to me, then you will finally absorb the words said out loud 1...2...3. Guess we all can’t be as perfect as John Doe now can we?
Take this match as a Class A Lesson of How to Wrestle 101. Hell many men don’t get to learn this lesson, just be grateful that you are going to face off with the Great himself…John Doe
And don’t forget to say your prayers, maybe you’ll come out this match in one piece…. See you in the ring. FADEOUT… Methos17 — 6 August 2004 (The camera fades in to Richard Gideon seated cross-legged in chair on first street near the railway in Washington, D.C. Gideon looks over toward the camera, as people mill about, apparently not caring a WFW Crew is present)
Richard Gideon: John...John...JOHN! I can't believe you take the time out of your day to give me 14 minutes of grandstanding. I mean, I can understand you wanting to use up all that time to get a point across...but even a mundane "I'm better than you" should have taken less time. I mean, take me for example. I wasn't going to respond to that, but I figured I had five minutes before my train got here. So instead of getting back to the hotel and work out, I'm talking to you.
(Gideon stands up, and points to the chair behind him) Richard Gideon: John, before you soooo cunningly went the stupid macho route I was geared up for a tete a tete with someone who wasn't hell-bent on being a complete and utter tool throughout his career. Instead, I'm in the mindset of hurting you. Of bringing the Status quo back here to the WFW via your bloody and beaten carcass. And that's not a place I wanted to be. I sincerely hope you bring whatever it is you think makes you better than a trend setter, a record holder here in the WFW. And I sincerely hope that keeps you going when I drop you on your head.
(Fade out as Gideon storms off with the chair) John Doe — 7 August 2004 FADEIN… John Doe WFW backdrop…that simple DOE: God, Richard. What is this? What is this crap you are feeding the public? You use to be a champion? What the hell is going on around here? First you decide to YET AGAIN waste my time to hear this load of crap, and then you steal WFW property! You are truly an idiot Richard.
But let see, are you really that ignorant that you have no clue who I am. I am John Doe, I am Greatness….I am God! F*ck, you come talking to me as though you think you are the best damn thing to hit the fwrestling circuit. I have no clue who in god’s name you think you are but I guess and Cherry Bloom Chaos I am going to beat that thought out of your head.
Wait, my question is who in the blue hell did you beat to become a champion? I mean honestly….you are nothing, I mean looking at you in film you are as good as the former Nick Savage of N.E.W. You are just a bum, nothing great nothing special. Hell I think this is going to be a great match to debut in, defeating a former champion, I have no clue how you won that belt Richard, but sweet Jesus almighty it must have been someone that had no utter clue how to wrestle.
But Richard I want to warn you, you are not just going against any other man, you are going against that great one, the best to hit the damn air waves…me. I think this should be a great learning experience for you, one that should prove great, and or deadly, take it how you wish, either way I am going to kick your teeth in. Richard at Cherry bloom blossom I am going to show you the direct meaning of pain.
You keep training Richard, you keep pushing your body, it will not be enough to defeat me. You do whatever you want, be at you’re a-game, me, I will come as I always do, and yet again get my arm raised. I hope your 401K plan is working, because after this match your only income is coming from your retirement funds.
Take care Richard, see you in the ring…. I AM GREATNESS…I AM GOD.. I AM JOHN DOE FADEOUT...
