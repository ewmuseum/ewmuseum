Spirit of NGEN: Diablo vs. Andre Eian

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 This match is a Flaming Tables match, if Diablo wins the match, Andre Eian will retire from the WFW.
The RP/angle deadline is August 11th. Send in all angles to [detail withheld] Diablo — 6 August 2003 (FADEIN: Diablo is seated on the floor in his apartment, in front of a spot where a small diameter of carpet has been crudely scraped off to reveal the bare concrete. Diablo is sitting Indian style with a very pleased expression.)
Diablo: Yes…The darkness encroaches upon it’s grand conquest. But as it draws min for the coup de gras, it finds its once noble and fierce prey paralyzed with terror. No matter, as the yule log is also without words when it is placed upon the hearth. I promise, Andre Eian, to make this a grand spectacle. The modern-day plebeians will find the 40 or so dollars well worth it to watch your body crash through a flaming table, and see the flames dance about as your flesh is turned into a gigantic mass of oozing, bubbling, half-charred…(shakes head)…sorry, got a little too into the imagery. Essentially, this will be a showcase of the beauty of the flame, the violent elegance of DIABLO FUEGO!
(Diablo reaches into a pocket of his pants, pulls out a book of matches, takes out a match, and lights it. His face assumes an amorous smile again as soon as the match ignites. He throws the lit match onto the bare concrete spot.)
Diablo: Yes…YES! MORE! MORE! (Diablo picks several more matches out of the book soon has thrown even the book itself into the small blaze. He looks on with a twisted sneer, but then we close up on his face and the smile is gone, but his face is contorting, as if Diablo is trying to fight back something. He soon lets out a violent shiver and collapses to the floor.)
Diablo: Ohhh…No! No…not now… (Diablo soon rises back up, and the sneer is back, except much more sinister. He gestures emphatically at the small bonfire.)
Diablo: See, Eian? SEE? This will be the magnificence of your corpse when my blackness begins to course through your veins. You will fight, oh I’m sure you will fight, but your only reward will be the sweet release of the ringing bell as your limp body lies, having just had every idea of pain and anguish redefined in a few minutes. You will rise again, but only after what is known as Andre Eian has been reduced to ash. You will rise again, another infected minion. And you will be gone from WFW, only to spread the infection. Much as Diablo has, you will try to fight it, but you will only speed your own decay, and the spreading of my black disease. ARGH!
(As the small blaze dies out, then Diablo again collapses. We close up again on his face as it looks on to the smoldering pile. He tries to prop himself up on his arms, as his face bears an expression of exhaustion and alarm. His breathing is labored.)
Diablo: And the plague shall thrive. Evil, Eian. Prepare for the evil. This evil shall be put inside YOU!
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow." andreshadow — 10 August 2003 Fade in to Andre Eian. Nothing else, just him. More specifically, his face. He's wearing sunglasses, despite the apparent darkness around him. His hair is pulled back, as usual, and his goatee neatly trimmed. He leans back, and a large scar is revealed on his chest. It reaches from just below his right pec to his left collar bone. He smiles.
You see this? He runs his finger along the scar. This is the result of a minor altercation with an agressive drunk in a bar during my prolongued absence from the WFW. Why am I showing you this, you ask? Because that drunk, Diablo, is twice the opponent you'll ever be. So far, all you've proved is that you're capable of ambushing a man in his locker room and blow fire in his face. This doesn't impress me in the least. My dog could do better. At least he has some semblance of honor.
He laughs. Honor. The word is probably too big for you. You seem to be more obsessed with matchbooks and fire than the relevance of human dignity, which is another thing you have little of. You'll have even less when you're lying in the remnants of a burning table, sh!t in your pants and a cry of pain in your throat. You disgust me.
He leans forward again. Men with little honor and dignity have nothing to lose. I, however, have much to lose. My career, my way of life, is on the line. My body is on the line. My reputation as a wrestler is on the line. However, there is no way I'd rather go out. If you beat me, Diablo, it will be due to one of your little tricks, as usual. I don't care. Either way, I go out with my honor and my dignity. No mater how many sharp objects are forcefully shoved into my flesh, I will not scream for mercy. No matter how much it hurts, I will not tap out. No matter how much I want to, I will not give up my honor and dignity for physical mercy. You may think you have me scared, Diablo, but it will take more than a coward with a box of matches to scare me. Empty threats will do nothing. Promises of burning flesh will do nothing. When it all comes down to it, you'll have to beat me to retire me. That is not an easy feat, as many men can attest to. I promise you a fight, Diablo, and I promise you'll feel it long after.
He leans back again, reaches off screen, and takes a sip of a drink. I leave you with this, Diablo. It is a quote by wrestling legend Atsushi Onita. "In life there is defeat, but there is also tommorow"...
[i]He laughs, and the screen fades...[/font]
