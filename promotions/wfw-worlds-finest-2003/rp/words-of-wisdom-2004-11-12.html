WFW — Roleplay

Words of Wisdom

12 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TheOriginalSE — 12 November 2004 (FADEIN: A black and White photo of 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds as the background of the screen.)
VOICE / OVER: "Sean Edmunds has been the greatest President that World's Finest Wrestling has ever seen. No other President has ever had the esteem of reigning over such prosperity. And it's all because of him. Michael Manson and the stabilization of the World title? Because of Sean Edmunds being the President. The introduction of the Inner Circle who will curb what is left of LOVE's free-reign? Because of Sean Edmunds being the President. Everyone. Look at the television. Come on. You know you want to.. Just look at this picture; this wise man."
(All of a sudden the picture is taken off and a black and white spiral picture is shown, moving in opposing directions trying to entrance those watching television.)
VOICE / OVER: "YOU WILL VOTE FOR SEAN EDMUNDS ON ELECTION DAY. FELIX RED IS A FIGMENT OF YOUR IMAGINATION. HE DOESN'T EXIST. VOTE EDMUNDS. EDMUNDS. EDMUNDS. EDMUNDS."
(The picture comes back and the voice returns to normal.) VOICE / OVER: "Edmunds, yes President Edmunds, is asking for your vote of confidence. He is the EPITOME of what is right with the WFW. Don't believe us? Even his own OPPONENT thinks so!"
(CUTTO: Felix Red, standing in front of a NYC Unplugged promo which has been poorly covered up w/ Adobe Photoshop.)
RED: "One could conclude from all this that I have Sean Edmunds to thank... to an extent, for the career I chose and the more effectual man I grew up to be."
(The picture stops and Felix Red slowly fades to the background, right next to the Edmunds black and white photo, but slightly under it.)
VOICE / OVER: "Yes. That's right. Felix Red thanks President Edmunds every day before he goes to bed and after he wakes up in the morning. Without President Edmunds, Felix Red would not be here. .. He would be ... (CUE: Creepy music.) A FIGMENT OF YOUR IMAGINATION.. (End music.) But that's not it, you see. Felix Red IDOLIZES President Edmunds."
(A new shot of Felix Red comes on the screen now. It is for Guerra Interminable. Of course, the Photoshop job barely covers it.)
RED: "I have always admired Sean Edmunds, the wizard shape shifter who first showed us how one needn't be who they were told to be, one can assume a form of their choosing. We are collectively Bodydonas, Franchises, Triple Ses, and of late the president. And I'm not just saying that to be a brown noser, oh no. I recall saying the same things years ago in WWL, when both Edmunds and eye were completely different people. And there's no need to brown nose the president-" (cuts off abruptly.)
(The picture fades to the other side of the Edmunds one.) VOICE / OVER: "Yes. We are all the Bodydonnas. We are all the Franchises. We are all Sean Edmunds because President Edmunds unifies us. He makes us feel safe and warm and welcome. He is our shepard in the valley of the shadow of death. Afterall, let's look at how he has kept us safe..."
(A picture of Copycat, from a BOMB Voyage promo, looking wide eyed and pissed.) COPYCAT: "It's that (Voice, not Copycat's) "President" (Copycat's voice again) Edmunds! It's Edmunds trying to screw me over! He did it in the WWL when those idiots left him in charge, and he's doing it again!"
(The picture fades to black and white and goes to the bottom of the Edmunds picture.) VOICE / OVER: "Yes. Saving the world from Copycat, one LOVE member at a time. Only President Edmunds knows how to screw Copycat over. Vote EXPERIENCE. Vote Edmunds."
(Another Felix Red picture comes on the screen. Its another from a Guerra Interminable promo.)
RED: "He is, after all, the president; the ultimate authority figure, who the masses will obey and respect, the way they were taught to obey and respect their fathers. Or if their fathers left, big brothers."
(Pauses. Another Felix Red photo comes to the right.) RED: "Sean Edmunds, you remember, is NOT a member of the liars oppressors and violence experiment."
(This picture pauses. The two pictures spin to the sides of the center Edmunds image.) VOICE / OVER: "Isn't that reason enough to re-elect President Edmunds?" (Another Felix Red picture comes on screen, this time taking up precedence, if only for a second. The words also appearing on the screen.)
RED: "We, the other ones, will lie to you. But he would never." (The image freezes and then falls to the background. All of a sudden a John Doe picture, from Guerra Interminable pops up on the bottom of the screen, just to appear very quickly.)
DOE: "I trust Sean--" (It fades.) VOICE / OVER: "President Edmunds will never lie to you. He loves you. Felix Red knows that, John Doe knows that. We all know that. That is why we want to re-elect him. Felix Red might think that Sean Edmunds isn't fit to be king.. err President .... but his own words tell the truth ... VOTE HONESTY. VOTE EDMUNDS."
(The Edmunds picture in the center comes alive and in color.) EDMUNDS: "I'm Sean Edmunds, and I'm 'Simply Sensational'." (Edmunds glances off screen, confused. He quickly looks back.) EDMUNDS: "And I also approve this message..." (FADEOUT: To the Edmunds LOGO.) http://fwrestling.com/host/neweraofwrestling/prezedmunds.jpg
