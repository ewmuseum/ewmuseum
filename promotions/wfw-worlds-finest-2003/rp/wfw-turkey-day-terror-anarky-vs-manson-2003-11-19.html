Manson — 20 November 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black t-shirt and jeans, bandaged and stained red. He sits on a bench in a dimmly-lit room with a copy of Grendel by John Gardner open in his hands.))
MANSON: And so Beowulf said to the Grendel, "Whether you make the world a grave or a garden of rose is not the point." Then what is? You see, I've been in and around the WFW for awhile and the cycle repeats itself again and again. I get close and closer..but in the end..I fail.....I do everything possible..but eventually I'm outnumbered..I'm cheated..the inevitable possibility of being wronged carries on.
Hart, Michaels, Fink even Doc..even Nark now are all faceless. They all do the same thing, serve the same purpose. And the worst thing of it all is that they do the simple things. Things I laughed at back in the day. Because when I went out to destroy someone, when I went out to destroy someone's life, I'd think outside of merely using a gang to beat them up.
But here am I, letting all of this happen to me. What changed? Well, some would say that people started to like me, but they always did. There was always that black-clad section in the crowd. That's not much of a change. The announcers are always going on that about my hometown or helping someone else, things that I've never really cared about or even done. So why mention them?
Am I supposed to be doing that now? Is this what I've become? Alex Wylde is the president and I have not once gone, asked, or manipulated him into doing anything for me yet. There was a time when he and I brought entire promotions to their knees and here he is as the president with everything handed to him. And I still haven't done anything.
What is wrong? How am I different? Have I lost it? "It" being whatever it was that made me more than an ordinary man. Rat Fink antagonized me for months..Scotty Michaels in a mid-life crisis took a title from me..and now Shawn Hart..a member of a group that actually calls itself C.OC.K.S. beat me. Of course, I have excuses if I want them. Interference..cheating....handicaps....but when did any of this matter before?
It's all dust..like we end up in the end...blue..green..copulating dust. Why do I bother anymore? Am I outdated? I know I'm not too old. Why did I do so much before? What was the point if I can't do anything now?
My imagination used to be so great, everyone knows that. It still is, but where is it when I need it? Whatever happened to Michael Manson, the man of tomorrow? The scourge of the circuit? The Devil Incarnate?
I don't know. I don't know why I should keep trying. I'm supposed to have hope? I don't even remember if I did before. But, you'll know, Nark. You were there, you remember it all. I'm sure you do. People thought a snide remark would cause us to fight? No, not us. The level of intimacy we have is based upon the amount of suffering we have inflicted, on each other, the world, etc. Why would I strike you for reason?
Even now, even now, my mind moves like gears shifting. Always thinking, always planning. Because if I really am not into this anymore, if I reall deserve all this, I know you'll be able to maim me ten times over.
And I'm still here, still in there, you'll be the one to bring me out. Of course you might think that this is all just another one of my ploys. You should. But now, after everything I've done, there's no one that will come out to that ring to help me win a match. There's no one to come out to save me from a beating. Not even if it's just to hurt someone else.
All these losses, all these gang beatings.....are the results of what many people would say are my sins. Retaliations. The ignorant would say it's karma. But none of that matters, not anymore.
I had thought that if I won the WFW title, it would prove something to myself. But even that, in the end, is taken away from me. So, I need you, Nark, to dig deep..through skin and bone...and we'll see what's left.
If there's nothing. maybe I was nothing to begin with. And then maybe it's time I went back into nothing.
