Spirit of NGEN: Doc Silver (c) vs. Shawn Hart

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 July 2003 This match is a two out of three falls match, with stipulations to be named later, for the WFW World Heavyweight Title.
The RP/angle deadline is August 11th. Send in all angles to [detail withheld] ShawnHartXXX — 30 July 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jul-30-03 AT 01:34&nbsp;PM (EST)](Fade In: To the ever-unimpressive backdrop of a MYSTERIOUSLY dark, empty room somewhere within an undisclosed location. However, there to make the most of a dull situation, as well as disperse barrels full of wit and hilarity to the masses, is none other than the first contender to WFW's biggest title, Shawn Hart. Decked out in full wrestling attire and tinted shades, the man known as the 'Phenom' speaks the word...)
Hart: "DUUUUUUUM.. duh-duh DUN...." (He hums...) Hart: "DUUUUUUUUM.. duh-duh duhhh DAAAAAAAAAAHHH!!!" (He attempts to intimidate us all with a reeeeeeeally CREEPY eye!!!) Hart: "And soooooooo it came to pass that the good Doctor slew the mighy Rat Fink... and PROPELLED himself into the deul of all ages! Hart versus Silver... Silver versus Hart! And after having pinned the man in question last week already, the stipulations are mine to choose! All that being said, I have to admit... the anticipation for this bout is overpowering me. I can feel it coursing through my veins.. I mean, the sheer magnitude of such a contest is off the charts. It's like the Hiroshima of wrestling matches, cuz the very INSTANT the two of us step into that ring, laying it aaaaaaaaaall out there for the grandest prize in World's Finest Wrestling, the heavyweight championship of the WORLD, the FIREWORKS will be heard across the globe! But it doesn't stop there, JACKHOLES... ooooooooohhh nooooo!! Because IN CASE you didn't know, this battle of epic proportions... AS WELL AS several other thriiiiiilling and CHILLING fights, is available for YOUR viewing pleasure... LIVE on PAY-PER-VIEW!!! Don't be the only guy in your neighborhood to MISS OUT as HISTORY is being MADE!! Order WFW's PAY-PER-VIEW SPECTACULAR, The SPIRIT... of NGEN... for the RIDICULOUSLY low price of $29.95!!! You heard me right, only $29.95 for what will undoubtedly go down as the GREATEST WRESTLING SHOW in the HISTORY of HUMANKIND!! WFW presents the Spirit of NGEN... call your local cable or satellite providers NOW!!!"
(Hart begins to walk off of the shot, but the camera follows him, revealing that said dark room is ACTUALLY just a poorly-made set in the WFW's TV studio. The Phenom chats with the key grip and some of the foley artists for a moment, then continues on towars a cooler filled with frosty beverages. As Hart nabs an iced tea (brisk, baby!) and begins to chug it, the make-up artists emerge upon him with their powdered brushes...)
Hart: "Let's get rid of that shine this time, SHALL WE?!" Make-Up Girl #1: "Yes Mr. Hart..." Make-Up Girl #2: "Sure thing, hon." Hart: "If you do it right this time, maybe I'll show you girls just why it is they call me the Hart THROB!!"
(The women look at each other, painfully unimpressed, then continue their brush work...) Hart: "...Yeeeeeeeeah!!" Make-Up Girl #1: "........" Make-Up Girl #2: "........" (Crickets chirping can be heard in the background, Hart looks around in confusion, then shakes his head and looks into the camera...)
Hart: "Y'know... we may be presenting this next PPV in the spirit of good ol' NGEN, and it's common knowledge that Doc did his thing there and did it well, but the fact of the matter is that, as we forge ahead in this new millenium with WFW, it's time to do away with the OLD... and kick things off with something FRESH!! Something new and innovative... something downright JIGGY!! And as I stand before you today, in allllllllllll my wonderous....ness...., I can say, WITHOUT QUESTION, that something is ME!! Since the moment tihs company unleashed itself upon the masses, Precious Metal, M.D. has been nothing more than a PART-TIME champion, coming and going as he pleases, caring NOT how he disgraces both himself, his title, and HELL... this company as a whole! And lemme tell ya Doc, WFW brass... and the people in general... are getting fed up! The call for CHANGE has been sounded, and I'M the man to initiate that change! Think whatever you like about me and what I stand for, Doc, cuz either way.. when it's GO time, this splendidly precious foot of mine is gonna go right up your bum!! Your BUM, Doc!!"
(He turns towards one of the make-up artists...) Hart: "Dammit, watch the eyes!!" Make-Up Girl: "Sorry..." (The woman returns her misplaced brush to Hart's forhead as the man continues with his tirade...)
Hart: "Now listen up, brah... and you think long and hard about what I'm telling you here! You may THINK that I'm not in your league, that your some kind of gift to our sport sent directly from the heavens, but consider the fact that I've beaten you 1-2-3 in that ring twice already. Factor in that I've taken you to the limit each and eeeeevery single time we've faced. Heck, in the WWL I took your job and did it better than you did! This is our history, Silver... and like the old saying goes, HISTORY... repeats itself! With all this being the case, In The Spirit of NGEN... you and me for the WFW title, MARK MY WORDS... Doc Silver's OUT like Bob Hope! Call it an OUTRAGE, call it a MOCKERY, god dammit... it's the TRUTH!!! *he pauses briefly, then cracks a grin* ...Heh, at Paradise Lost last week, Brandon Jacobs said I was Satan... and this much is true. That being the case, next week Doc, when we go toe-to-toe, consider yourself DAMNED to HELL!!! Now FINISH my make-up so I can sell this show! The PHENOM... has left... the BUILDING!!"
(Fade to black.) DocKGB — 7 August 2003 (FADEIN: Doc Silver standing in front of a WFW banner, the WFW World Title over his shoulder. Doc is wearing his normal gear, and smiles wryly at the camera.)
DOC: The spirit of NGEN, a company who only ever had one champion, me, and now I defend this title, in that spirit, against a man who's choked twice, who in two multi-way matches for this very belt, has blown it and lost it all...And the third time...Will assuredly NOT be the charm...I mean, honestly, you're Shawn freaking Hart...You've never mattered, you've never rated anywhere, you are in fact, nothing...WFW must be really hard up for challengers if they keep feeling me do nothing go nowheres like you on PPV...I mean, really, the slim hope of you beating me isn't exactly causing an army to get on the phones to order this thing...
Because if by some fluke you put my shoulders down for 3 seconds, you have to do it AGAIN...Just THINK about that Shawn, I mean, really, how unlucky do you have to be to be stuck in a match where your only hope of winning, I.E. a fluke, has to happen TWICE in one night? (Smiles) it's beyond absurd to think it could happen...And that's got to hurt for you...
You say I'm Bob Hope...Shawn, when he died, a world mourned, if you were to drop dead tomorrow, maybe some of your family and couple rats you knocked up would care about it...That's the difference between a LEGEND and a never-was...And that's our difference...The fact that you are going to once again get into that ring, bright lights, big city, and you're once again, going to FAIL...Because deep down, you know you don't have what it takes to own this (Pats belt) and you know deep down that you are cursed to forever walk the endless path of misery...Only Happy When It Rains (FADEOUT)
ShawnHartXXX — 10 August 2003 (Fade In: To Shawn Hart, the Phenom of WFW, taking a stroll down the quiet Sunday streets of a suburban neighborhood as a crew of cameras shadows his every step. As he moves down the walkway, his flowing blonde locks and designer shades glistening in the bright light of day, he runs a hand through his hair, then begins to speak...)
Hart: "Y'know... Doc Silver, despite all that you've accomplished in this sport... despite all the accolades and notoriety you've amassed, you seem to be behind the curve a bit with what is fact and what is not, dontchu. There you are... still riding high and mighty, continuing down that holier than thou pathway, still oblivious to the reality of the situation. You see, just as you did in aaaaaaall our previous contests, you CONTINUE to mock my name and disregard my accomplishments. You still seem intent on writing me off as just another yokel, a ham n' egger... some kind of FLUKE, that's somehow found his way to the top through happenstance and serendipity, rather than a guy who's earned his spot atop the mountain. But hey, think what you want... you wanna call me a quote-unquote 'never-was', then that's your prerogative. Either way, there's what is... and what isn't, and believe you me, JACKHOLE... that trash you spit ISN'T how things are!"
(The Phenom comes upon a bench and takes a seat. He then removes his shades, rubs his eyes, and shakes his head in disgust...)
Hart: "Mistah Silver, while you like to deal with matters of opinion, me personally... I'm all about fact. I tell it like it is, my friend. I deal only in TRUTH. That being the case, it's a FACT that both times you've held that WFW title, your reigns have been lackluster, and your jackassery has tarnished not only your own image... AND the image of that belt, but that of the WFW as a hole. It is also a FACT that we've met in the ring THREE TIMES, and of the three meetings, I've pinned your shoulders TWICE! So next time you want to talk about this suppos-ed inability of mine to put you to the mat, check the record books... I have your number, brah. Funny, isn't it, that after having utterly DISGRACED you in 2 of our 3 previous meetings, we find ourselves meeting, on PAY-PER-VIEW, in a best 2 of 3 falls match! Needless to say, history repeats itself, and rest assured... this series of three will go down just like the last one did... with one exception. There's an X-factor here that hasn't even been brought to the table yet and, like I've said in the past... since your only happy when it rains, allow yours truly to RAIN on your parade!"
(Hart cracks his neck to both sides, rubs his hands together insidiously, then continues on...)
Hart: "Y'see, despite my previous success against you, ya still wanna claim that I won't be able to pin those shoulders and capture the gold. However, I regret to inform you that if push comes to shove, I won't have to. See, when I defeated you and Gideon, at the same time I might add, and won the power to declare stipulations for this match, it gave me NOT OOOOOOONLY the power to make it two out of three falls, but ALSO the ability to name stipulations for each individual fall. All that bein' said, since a voodoo doll you are most defenitely NOT when it comes to being pinned, I've decided to make the third and FINAL stage of this bout... a LAST MAN STANDING match! So if you manage to withstand the first two falls, which by the way, will be a lumberjack match and a cage match respectively... just to ensure that your boy toy GIDEON or any other stooge or lackey you may have up your sleeve doesn't get involved... if you somehow manage to withstand me up to that point, mark my words, you're gonna be at the mercy of the Phenom! I don't care what you say about my status, I could give a rat's ass about your NGEN tie-in, hell... it doesn't even matter who this so-called special guest referee is. NOTHING will keep me from that which is righfully mine! Call it an OUTRAGE, call it a MOCKERY, I swear to the gods above... it's the TRUTH! You've half-assed your way through this business for the last time, because in a few short days... I'm putting you out for GOOD! I just hope you're fully prepared for what I'm gonna do to you.. or you might not make it out of this thing in one piece. The PHENOM... has left.. the BUILDING!"
(Fade Out)
