WFW Paradise Lost: Psycho (c) vs. Golem

3 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 3 September 2004 Golem gets to face the opponent of his choice, either Psycho or Wells, at Paradise Lost for a title shot. The RP/angle deadline is Wednesday, September 15 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Devil666 — 8 September 2004 (A sadistic laugh followed by rhythmic taping upon metal can be heard as the shot opens and Psycho sits before a WFW backdrop. His hands are clutched around the North American title. He rocks back and forth for a few moments his fingers tapping as the laughter continues. Then all falls silent and he looks up coldly staring at the camera)
Psycho: Golem...you've chosen me? Am I the man you wanted a shot at? (He looks down at HIS belt) Psycho: Is this what you want to get your furry little hands on? Is this what you want to scrape your dirty claws upon?
(He smiles and laughs again) Psycho: Is this precious enough for you to get into the ring with me? (He pauses fro a moment and then stands up placing the belt over his shoulder) Psycho: Well good Golem...I'm glad you choose me. Ever since you attacked me I've waited for a chance to carve my own little piece of your hide again
(He reaches off camera and returns with a fork in his hand) Psycho: and that's just what I'm going to do Golem. Three men...TRIED!!!! They TRIED to take this from me. In the end...THEY ALL FAILED!!!!!!!! Pulsar...Iris...Rat Fink...all gave it their best shot...AND THEY FAILED!!!!!!! What makes you any different? What is it your claw? Is that your great weapon of destruction?
(He pauses and shrugs his shoulders) Psycho: Well I could care less either way. The North American Title...IS MINE!!! It's my precious...not yours. Do you remember the last time we meet? Do you remember what happened the last time you saw my face?
(He pauses for a moment scratching his head) Psycho: remember now...that's right I LIT YOU UP!!!!!! I threw a fireball in your face and I can imagine the smell of burning hair must still be stuck in your nose. Is it Golem? Can you still smell it? Well don't worry I can arrange a refresher for you, but this time I'll give you something extra
(He rolls the fork between his fingers) Psycho: Because just like Manson, just like Gideon...just like ALL the others...I'm going to give you a taste of your OWN blood and I'm not going to stop till you choke on it. Be ready Golem...you want my title...you TRY and take it. In return...I want YOUR BLOOD!!!!!!! If you want this title then you’re going to get the FIGHT of your life before you can take it from me. Still I invite you to try. Three men came before you...and they went home in defeat. At the end of the night when the ref counts three and the medics rush to your aid...
(He drops the fork and raises the North American title to the camera) Psycho: This belt will STILL be mine and like ALL the others...you'll have felt the terrible power of LOVE
(He begins to tap his fingers on the belt once more an again let's loose that loud sadistic laugh)
<FTB> Vertigo — 10 September 2004 (Cue Up: "Body Crumbles" by Dry Cell. Fade into an empty room. Suddenly, in the background, a spark is struck. Slowly lowered to a candle, the match ignites an otherwise completely dark room. In the light, Golem can now be seen. He sits there quietly, staring into the fire. At last he begins to talk.)
Golem: So, here we are again. Dancing the same dances, singing the same songs that we did oh so long ago. Psycho, my dear lad, can you truly be shocked I picked you to fight over the "Ragin' Fire" or whatever? It was you and, to a lesser extent, Felix Red who have made my record appear less perfect than it truly is. I would go to great extremes to extract my precious revenge on you, you have to be able to sense that fact. Hell, I was willing to take Jared's title as step one in my plan to eliminate LOVEAG for it's thousand injuries I have endured. Let's face it, Psycho, that wouldn't have satisfied me. Nothing seems to, anymore. I am too old, perhaps, forcing my body to do things it shouldn't to repay old debts that rightfully probably should've been long since forgotten. Or so I may have thought, until you refreshed my memory, reignited my interest, you might say.
(Golem lifts the small candle) Golem: Yes...you have engulfed my interest, amongst other things, Psycho. Fire...a fickle friend. Betrayer, perhaps it is, to it's master, it's conjurer, with the lick of a pet serving only the purpose of suffering. Yes, I have those words before, or ones like them...by someone, perhaps as though out of a dream, an unstoppable force, a champion of a million and one causes. I can barely remember his face, a cloudy image in the darkest recesses of my mind. Yet, indeed, I believe I understand now. This grand warrior, he is as I had wished. In the mask of fire, I saw that the champion, this destroyer of times, was Golem. And Golem....means...me!
(Golem's eyes glass over and a sinister maneuvers itself around his lips) Golem: It has been a true forever, a thick coat of fire and days since I have truly seen Golem. You remember him, I hope? This, Psycho is not the first time Golem has been in the frosty kisses of the fire, Psycho, and I will not let it be the last. Golem endures while so much else passes into memory. I will not allow my beloved Golem to become crooked or askew such as yourself or Anarky have. Hoarding victories over worthless nobodies - Iris, Rat Fink, and Pulsar - like they are honestly an actual accomplishment. Congratulations, Psycho, I am sure you expended well more than necessary energy to do so. No longer will Golem allow such transgressions. You see, Golem struggled so hard to begin the Dark Age of Golem, where the world swallows itself into shadows. And, through his own blind desire, he missed the fact that the mission had already been accomplished. It is, or was, a dark age, indeed, a grandiose regression, the end of Golem's evolution. Stagnant in darkness, Golem must dig himself out of his self-made grave. Sure, Golem has yet to be actually pinned, or forced to submit, counted out, disqualified or even beaten in any way, shape, or form in WFW...but it is not enough to merely be absolutely perfect. Like the Phoenix, I must arise from my own ashes in a glowing blaze of fire to re-assert myself on the peak of this industry. You shan't stop me, Psycho. No more will you be able to defeat me simply by your lying down to easy opponents. Indeed, while I engaged the supposedly unstoppable "World's Finest Wrestler", Michael Manson, you saw fit to share blows with Rat Fink, and in the end, couldn't even handle that. Yes, Psycho, your title will be mine, but I don't think I will be through with you for such a cheap price. You claim you seek my blood...but I could say similar, if not more honest things about you. About how I seek to flay the skin from your bones, about how I plan to make you whimper and cry more than Felix Red's hookers, or how I wish to purge of yourself and bury your will below the crumbled temple of your body. The reckoning is at hand, Psycho, and all that Golem has foresaw will come to pass. So, come now, like the old villain that Golem has seen only in memory, and walk like the pathetic piece of cattle you are before your master. Now, so shall you be laid in sacrifice that all others might look upon you and understand. Understand that the battle has begun, and only suffering can follow.
(Golem almost seething at his point, very much into what he is saying. He loses control and bursts into deep, uncontrolled laughter. The camera fades out to the echoing sound.)
Devil666 — 13 September 2004 'Click' (Flint strikes steel) 'Click' 'Click" (Flint strikes steel) 'FLASH' (A flame is ignited and in the darkness a hooded figure stares ahead at the camera. A flicking candle now waves with each inhale and exhale of his breath. From under the hood a familiar laugh comes forth. There's a moment of silence to hear the reverb of its echo before the flame dances as the word is spoken)
Man: GOLEM!!!!! I have awoken you. Tales of fire and pain have bought forth the mythical beast that once cursed man. That sent them away in fear and disgust at the damage he COULD cost. This I have done...this blight I have cast upon the world once more...this curse has befallen me.
(His words grow quite and the flame steadies and rebuilds in strength. Suddenly another brighter flash disturbs the screen as the light bulb above him comes on. A man opens the door inside the small storage closet. The camera turns to see a puzzled janitor staring at Psycho as he pulls the hood from over his mask)
Janitor: What the hell? Psycho: Hell...hell there is no hell. There's only the illusion and you've ruined Golem's Illusion. DAMN YOU MERE MORTAL!!!!! DAMN YOU!!!!!!!!
(The janitor looks confused and walks away. Psycho turns back to the camera and laughs) Psycho: Golem...did you expect me to be scared? Did you expect me to shake in my boots? Did your threat to return to an old way, to be recalled to a mission you'd thought already done would somehow paint a yellow stripe down my back? Grow me a tale to turn and tuck? Well it didn't.
(He pauses and looks around) Psycho: Because this is just a storage closet and like you...I'm a man in a hood. THERE IS NO GOLEM!!!!!!! There is NOTHING to return to. The mission...if there ever was one...well it's dead and buried. Your time has come and gone Golem. It's MY reign. It's MY North American Title. It's MY time. Take a good look...take a good look at your future
(He stands still a smile creeping under his old bulls-eye design mask.) Psycho: There is no Golem...not any more. Maybe there never was. For you see while the only reference people know you by is due to some book they read in High school or a movie they saw two years ago.
(He points a crooked finger towards the camera) Psycho: Well I'm a much different creature then you. I'm not just a name...I'm not just a reference...I'm a one and true Psycho: "The American Dangerman" and the WFW's lone "Crimson Idol" Hell i even have the doctors visits, the diganosis and the medication to prove it
(He points at his chest) Psycho: I am the future Golem. Bright, violent and unavoidable. (Again he points a crooked finger at the camera) Psycho: All you speak of is a calling of the past. Results of meaningless matches I didn't even care to win. About a calling you ONCE had. About bringing back a man I SHOULD fear, but don't anymore. None of us do. I don't CLAIM to do anything Golem. I have down right PLANS to and in return you offer me the threat of yesterday's man. I'd laugh in your face if it didn't sound more pathetic then funny.
(He takes a few steps forward and his masked face fills the screen) Psycho: SIT in your room Golem. STAY in your safety zone. Reside in the depths where you'll be safe. Think of the better days and write on the walls your broken plans to relive them. For I'll be here LOOKING you in the face and HOLDING the North American Title around my waist.
(He steps back and slings the title over his shoulder) Psycho: I'm glad I've gotten your attention Golem. I hope you try what you promise. SPILL my blood, CARVE my skin, CRUSH my soul...hell take your best shot. In the meantime I will be doing what you can only dream of.
(Again he takes a step forward his face filling the screen) Psycho: You struggled hard to make your Dark Age where the world swallows itself into shadows...is that what you struggled so hard to do?
(He takes a few steps back and spits on the ground) Psycho: Well don't worry Golem. You won't have to worry about putting forth that much effort. I'm going to make sure before any of that happens again...
(He let's loose a loud sadistic laugh) Psycho: I'm going to leave it drowned...CHOCKING ON IT'S OWN BLOOD!!!!!!!!! I'M The North American Champion....I'M "THE AMERICAN DANGERMAN!!!!!!!"........I'M "THE CRIMSON IDOL!!!!!!"...I"M THE FUTURE IN THE WFW!!!!!!!!
(He again points a crooked finger at the screen) Psycho: Your history...a corpse waiting to be seen on an archived tape. Come Paradise Lost...AT FENWAY PARK...I'LL SHOW YOU...i'll show you what a Psycho really is and that you CAN'T FIGHT THE FUTURE!!!!!!!
(He let's loose a loud laugh once more, turns his back and walks away) <FTB> Vertigo — 15 September 2004 (Cue Up: "The Day The World Went Away" by Nine Inch Nails. Fade into Golem, sitting in a wooden rocking chair. It quietly squeaks away in the background as Golem rocks, smirking and staring off into the distance...or the wall, whichever. Anyways, Golem slowly cracks his neck and eases his eyes back towards the camera.)
Golem: Ah...here we go again, it seems. Myself and another arrogant, self-righteous spear carrier, dancing around each other, wrapped up in our words. Meanwhile, as Golem wears his words as a bright and vibrant warning against the coming tempest, I cannot help but think that you, Psycho, wear yours as a smoke screen. You see, friend, your words do little to perk my interest. I have seen you in action, Dangerman. Perhaps, if I were more akin to a quipper, I would suggest that I give you "no respect". But that would be neither here nor there, so I digress. Rightfully so, I do believe. Anyways, I have seen you in action before. As a partner and as an unplanned attacker. Both times, I was mostly unimpressed. All you succeeded in doing is striking my ire and arising my blood as I assume you do not yet quite understand. Soon, yes, soon, I am sure you will be made to comprehend. And I do not think that you will like what it is you will glaring at. Soon, titleless, speechless, and broken you shall sit staring into a mirror and wondering how you got so horrifically decimated by a piece of history. Frozen in ice, melted and awoken in fire, Golem has come back from days perhaps when you yourself were back in high school reading stories about me and watching movies about me. Funny, I never knew I was that well known, but I am forced to take your word for it. You, after all, have your own hit movie, do you not? I assume that is the reason you hide in janitor's closets. Is it because you are trying to escape your hordes of fans? Or...is it because you are waiting to strike down the janitor, and cover yourself in his blood, you "Crimson Idol", you? You can be confident in saying that it is your reign. It is your time. It is your world, Psycho, and I am but a piece of the puzzle. The piece that will not fit. The piece that corrupts your pretty little picture. Try to insert me into the background, friend, and watch your everything become incomplete. Nothing. Worthless. Finished and dead. Your end of days is nigh at hand and your North American title, your time, your reign will sputter and die in it's wake. I am not sure my words are prophetic, or obvious, or wildly crazy sounding. But fact I know them to be, and as fact they shall come to proven. The world shall crumble into my hands and I shall embrace your ex-paradise as my paradise. Youth forgets though, Psycho, and soon you will forget that you were ever the North American champion or that you ever lost to me. You shall become enthralled in some grand new adventure and any remnant of the apocalyptic disaster that is due at Paradise Lost shall be ground out of your memory. You are future? Perhaps, but now you must realize or be forced to realize, that Golem is not merely the past. He is the Present, and before all is said and done he will have his hand in events that determine the future. You asked before, what is my grand weapon...if it was, perhaps, my claw?
(Golem raises his hand in front of his face.) Golem: Golem's curse...it, perhaps, is what makes me different. It, perhaps, is what drives me to make people like you suffer. But, no, Psycho. It is not my grand weapon. My true, greatest weapon is this...
(Golem taps his head.) Golem: Is it any wonder I am here, Psycho? Here, dancing with you, the North American champion, just shortly after my arrival? Of course, yes, I was destined to reach here soon enough by the sole fact I am a more skilled wrestler than basically this entire company, but are you shocked that I am already threatening the very, very real possibility will walk away with your title. I goaded you, Psycho, like the stupid little child you are. I embraced your attack and played the role of the innocent victim. Did I look hurt then? Do I look hurt now? I got what I wanted. I never expected to have to fight Wells. Everything went exactly as planned, as expected. You simple-minded fool, you have stumbled into the snare of the fangs of the serpent, and there really isn't much chance anymore for you to escape. All that is left is for you to give up, give in, and concede to the rising surge of poison that caresses your insides. You shall, whether you wish it true or not, find yourself on the short end of a long claw, and the rest shall be history. But, hey, that's alright, right Psycho? Because that's all it is. History. And you have a whole future full of equally embarrassing failures to look forward to.
(Fade to black.) Devil666 — 15 September 2004 (In the darkness, the sound of creaking wood can be heard in a rhythmic pattern. For a few moments we listen to the pattern as again a candle is light and a hooded figure is seen on camera. He pauses fro a few minutes and then raises a cloak covered arm to the camera)
Man: You kids. Damn you...Damn you Psycho...you little whipper-snapper. I'm going to get you. I'm going to get ALL of you. Yeah...it's my MASTER plan and it's sprung. I'm going to get all of you...and...and your little DOG TOO!!! Damn you...you kids. AND STAY OFF MY LAWN!!!!!!!
(Suddenly the overhead light flashes on once more and the janitor again opens the door. Quicklyy the man pulls off his hood to reveal himself as a masked Psycho)
Psycho: WHAT...WHAT DO YOU WANT!!!!!!! I know you can't comprehend timing...does the word atmosphere MEAN ANTHING TO YOU???
(The janitor pauses a moment before speaking) Janitor: But I need a mop (Psycho stares at him angrily then smiles) Psycho: You need a map? (He looks around, finds a mop and throws it at the man) Psycho: HERE!!!!!!! Here's your mop. KEEP IT!!!!!!! You can use it to clean up after I spill Golem's blood all over the place.
Janitor: Who??? (Psycho again pauses and smiles) Psycho: Exactly...NOW GET OUT!!!!!!! (The janitor walks off and Psycho inhales deeply before turning back to the camera) Psycho: Don't you get it Golem? Don't you see? You ask why I'm sitting here in this storage room.
(He stands and exits the door and he is now standing in the visitor lockeroom deep inside Fenway Park.)
Psycho: I've been sitting there for my promos because I'm MOCKING YOU!!!!!!! Can't you see that? Me...Me Psycho...I'm making fun of you. Yet you don't see that? You know a sign you've become the joke Golem is when you no longer see the humor. Perhaps that's why you would ask me such a foolish question. Do you not understand where I'm coming from?
(He pauses for a moment then snaps his fingers and laughs) Psycho: NO...no you don't, because this is your MASTER PLAN!!!!!!!! This is you evil genius stroke to get me right where you want me. Let's see you planned to be in two meanings less tag-team matches that would never prove anything win or lose. Then when you couldn't handle your own man and got pinned...well you'd blame it on me and attack me back. Somehow...in all that planning you'd then manage to get the WFW to give you a choice between titles to fight for and by chance one of them is mine.
(He again pauses dances his fingers in the air to connect the dots) Psycho: GENIUS!!!! Pure diabolic genius Golem. I’m truly stunned at the lengths you went. Why the only thing you could have done more calculated was...oh I don't know.
(His smile fades, shrugs his shoulders and he stares coldly at the camera) Psycho: You could have simply challenged me. Yeah that's right...no games...no schemes...just come out and ASK for a shot at me. Because look Golem. It's pretty clear I'm not afraid of you. No...Instead I get a story that would make John Kerry seem consistent.
(He holds up a finger) Psycho: Yet for a moment...unlike him...I'll give you the benefit of the doubt. Let's say this all went as planned. You still have one BIG problem Golem. It's that I don't think you have it in you. I don't think you still have what it takes. I think you should STICK to sitting where you are Golem, because if you get in that ring with me then I promise the next chair your sitting is will have wheels on it. IT'S OVER GOLEM...at Paradise Lost...IT'S ALL OVER!!!!!
(He again pauses and the smile returns) Psycho: I mean come on...are two bad sequels in a Deloran going to drop you off in the faint hope that you might be the third? What...give me a reason that I should fear all this TALK of yours...?
(He stops and his eyes grow wide. Slowly he raises his hand and tries to form his finger into a bent claw. After a few moments of trying the joint simply won't bend that far anymore and instead he points it upward as he faces the camera while staring at his finger)
Psycho: This..this is your glory stick. Your going to pull this from wherever it's stuck right now and PUT AN END TO ME!!!!!!!!
(He stops and looks at his arms) Psycho: WOW!!!!!!!! Nothing shaking...Not the slightest tremor of fear. Why it's simple. I WANTED THIS MATCH!!!!!!! Since the moment you attacked me I wanted you in the ring. Did a fireball in the face not tell you that or was it again another stroke in your master plan? I wanted this match Golem...I demanded it. because while you might not still be much of a threat...I'll at least give you credit...your still a name.
(He takes a step forward. His smile fades once more and his voice grows serious) Psycho: As for my name? I earned it. I'm crazy...I'm legit. You don't understand that, but you will. The fans in Japan know it...that's where the name came from. THEY called me "The American Dangerman" It's because I don't shy away from ANY fight no matter what the odds...or the stips. I'm "THE CRIMSON IDOL"...not because I needed a nickname, but because despite how much of my blood is spilled...I NEVER give up the fight. You don't SEE this...You don't KNOW this...
(He pauses and chuckles) Psycho: But you will. And you will very soon. (He takes a few steps back) Psycho: PARADISE LOST!!!!!!.........FENWAY PARK!!!!!!!.......GOLEM DONE!!!!!!!!!!...DANGERMAN RULES!!!!!!!!!!!
(He stands forward and again fills the screen) Psycho: Your time is done...the FUTURE is NOW...IT"S ME!!!!!!!!! At Paradise Lost...this won't be your master plan...won't be some movie...It'll be a LONG...COLD...BLOODY NIGHTMARE!!!!!!!!! And I'll enjoy every second of it. See you in the ring
(He spits on the floor, turns his back and walks away) <FTB>
