WFW Ghoulish Games: Mystery Man vs. Richard Gideon

21 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 21 November 2004 The RP deadline is Friday, December 3rd 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld] .
Paul Methos17 — 30 November 2004 (The camera fades in to Richard Gideon, seated and wearing a "Fun Bus" t-shirt. He is watching the latest results from the WFW primaries and appears amused. Noticing the camera, he turns to speak)
Richard Gideon: Well how do you like democracy in action? Seems like we get who we want in the positions of power, or rather the ones we tolerate. AH well, at least I still get to wrestle y'know?
(Gideon gets up and stretches a bit as he continues to speak) Richard Gideon: Which brings me to the reason I wanted to talk to you fine WFW faithful. I've been a little quiet recently, and no it wasn't a gag-order...it was self imposed. Y'see, I've been talking a good bit in my time here and it has gotten me to a few pinnacles, a few peaks if you will...but I've yet to get over that hump. I've yet to taste that WFW gold. My partners on the Fun Bus have, and not knocking them, but hey...The System needs a piece of that candy too.
But that means I have to get some wins under my belt again. So that means you are in a very precarious position Mr. Mystery Man....I think I'm to this point undefeated against at least that name...Mystery Opponent...and I'm not looking to change that wonderful statistic. So, you show up, I show up, we get it on like two men should and I walk away with yet another top notch win under my belt and you....you get to say you lost due to a cold...or a bum knee, or whatever excuse you want to concoct to keep your sleep patterns normal for the days following our match.
(Gideon taps his fun bus shirt) Richard Gideon: I'm a member of the Fun Bus and crappy name notwithstanding...I gotta represent the "old school" here in the WFW...and by crickey, I'm gonna do it. And now, if you will excuse me I have a sparring match with my name on it.
(Fade to white) PaulNJ21 — 30 November 2004 (OORP - Posting for the mystery man on his behalf) (Fadein, an empty gymnasium, where certain creaks in the seats and other noises echo throughout the building. The ring, tired and beaten...stands ready--not of due will, but ready nontheless for the next pair of competitors to step through the ropes. Various fantastic bouts are posted on the walls through posters and various-colored flyers. V.O: So this is "The System"? This is the man whose name and repetoire to the business has put himself a "Minority" among the rest of this organization, because he claims that quite frankly....everyone else is getting it, why not him too?? So if there was a line out the door to the nearest cliff Mister Gideon, and everyone else took a turn jumping off it, would you do so yourself as well?? "The Fun Bus" as I see it, has taken their turn at making statements, and well..."Having Fun" at the same time I'm sure...but being a member of the "Old School Community", for lack of better word...will only take you so far. You're in this match to get a head start on collecting wins again, perhaps to impress someone I'm sure. (The camera cuts over to a new, and more current lineup, this time of WFW Ghoulish Games.) But I'm in this match to make an impact....whether or not you're conscious for the duration of it, the answer is just something that.... "THE SYSTEM... Cannot provide. (Fadeout.) PaulNJ21 — 5 December 2004 (OORP: This got lost in my mailbox from Friday Night...) (Fadein, an empty room save one desk and a single Television with the screen facing the camera. The screen then comes on and shows successful teams of different sports as well as those that are single. Some that are longer on the screen are such powerhouses as the 1972 Miami Dolphins, the era of "The Red Machine" Cincinatti Reds, the years that comprised "The Murderers' Row" and the rest of the New York Yankees. Muhammed Ali. Babe Ruth. "America's Team", the 1990's Dallas Cowboys. 1996's 72-win Chicago Bulls. 2001's "Amazing" Seattle Mariner's with an astonishing 116 win season. Michael Jordan. "Iron" Mike Tyson as well as others.) V.O: Sometimes a team is put together so tightly knit with talent or a person is comprised with so much pride and faith in him or herself, that becoming unbeatable is so conceivable that he or she or even they....convey it to the public and those watching around the world so that they have no other choice but to believe it. As the unseen is witnessed, those that complete the task set before them are fighting "The System". "The System" is known as what everyone perceives "The Law". It takes alot of effort to challenge "Law", but once convinced....a NEW law is formed by either a technicality or just simply revamped altogether. Thus a new law made, a cycle is completed and we begin anew with someone else trying to..."BUCK THE SYSTEM." So you see, everyone you've seen here has either beaten the System or has been beaten so badly that for even a short time...that team or person... Steps aside. Now whether or not you seem to think that you're getting screwed by..."THE SYSTEM...I could care less. But you see Mister Gideon, in calling yourself "The System", and in that the man you receive your paychecks from has told me of your reputation for that of "The Old School" type of wrestling....this presents a problem. That problem you see Mister Gideon, is that I'm a man that doesn't mind alittle arrogance, but I hate pr*cks who deem themselves the type of competitor who sees themselves as... (The Voice pauses) Well, "Above The Law", if you will or perhaps someone of measured importance, at least in your mind. I'm fixing to change that Mister Gideon, and whether or not you think I'm jumping the gun is up to you, again.... I could careless. I'm coming to Chicago, and I'm coming to shut you...and "The System"....DOWN. Fadeout.
