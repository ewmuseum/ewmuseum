WFW Ghoulish Games: Christian Sands (With Troy) vs. Mike Lane

21 November 2004

PaulNJ21 — 21 November 2004 The RP deadline is Friday, December 3rd 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld] .
Paul JABolich — 21 November 2004 (FADEIN: White. Just... white. A plain white screen, absent of seams or shadows or lines that would indicate seams between walls and floor. A steel-blue chair floats amidst the center of the emptiness, its back facing the camera. The chair's occupant is a man of about six foot three and two hundred and seventy pounds, bronzed and muscular form swathed in a dark trenchcoat and sweater, eyes concealed behind a pair of dark shades.)
(Anyone who hasn't been living under a rock for the past few years would recognize Christian Sands instantly.)
"It feels like coming home, this match... After going from reigning World Champion to reigning Tag-Team Champion, I was getting a little lonesome for some singles competition. Then again, mano a mano issss my bag, babay."
"I'd introduce myself to the WFW viewing audience, but seriously - if you don't know who I am, get help, get educated, and get on the Christian Sands Adoration Bandwagon. Hop on now and I'll send you a free Rabesquedor the Borinator T-shirt."
"There have been a few voices raised by the boys in the back about the Inner Circle and how we apPArently are a bunch of million-dollar-babies who can't hang with LOVE's *ahem* finest... but I think Maelstrom addressed that quite nicely. I understand Jared Wells spent last night with his invisible Mexican friend, crying into his cheap imported beer and convincing himself that he really IS someone. But we all know better, don't we?"
"LOVE is dead. Its death warrant was signed the moment Dan, Mael, Lindsay, and I put our John Hancocks on that fat WFW contract. That stands to reason, since both documents are one and the same."
"Move along, boys - you're on borrowed time." "But how rude of me to spend so much time disrespecting the dead... time to move on." "So. Mike Lane - actually, hold up. Can I call you Mitch? Yeah? Great. Thanks, tiger." "So, Mitchell. What's your story?" "Actually, spare me, 'cause I already know. Let's just cut across the sanctimonious sermon about how you had it all and pissed it all away. I've heard it all before - and I have to say it takes some big stones and some little brains to throw a career down the drain just like that. But here you are, pluggin' away again... a modern-day Thomas the Tank Engine. Here comes Mitchell Lane, tootin' his little horn, chuggin' right along - 'I think I can, I think I can, I think I can.'"
"Charming, Mitch. But you know something? Where I'm involved, YOU CAN'T." "I can see your problem already. It's all that angst. Look at yourself - all feisty and tough and gutsy, ready to take on the world, f(BOMB) the man, screw the crowds, every Mitch for himself. Geez, Mitch... take a pill. At this rate it's getting hard to decide which you remind me of more - a pizza-faced teenager screaming about how mommy doesn't understand, or a Baptist preaching the evils of luxury. Maybe a little of both. Had any religious yearnings lately, Mitchell Lane?"
"Here's somethin' you can stick in your miter, Mitch. Maybe, JUST MAYBE, if you weren't such a FAILURE AT LIFE you wouldn't even BE in this position."
"News flash: I've heard the Mitchell Lane sob story before. Everywhere you go some wrestler complains about how he had it all and let it destroy him. You know what I say to that? I say that only P**SIES let life destroy them! You had your chance, Mitchell Lane, and YOU F**KED IT UP! What makes you think you DESERVE another shot at glory, huh? What makes you think you're enough of a man to catch lightning in a bottle one more time? What makes you think you won't just piss it away like you did last time? Huh? Answer me that, Mitchell Lane! TRY and convince me that you're not a pathetic failure."
"Any historian worth his salt will tell you that history is a cycle of repetition... what exempts you from that cycle?"
"The wheel's turning, Mitchell. It's turning, turning, turning back into that downswing that came way back when. You had your moment in the sun, Mitch. You came back and got your name out there again. But at Ghoulish Games the sun sets on Mike Lane for the second time. While you lie there in twilight again, Mike, I'll have left you behind and moved along with the dawn - because for me, wrestling has been nothing but sunshine."
"I'm man enough to take my success and capitalize on it." "Until you can learn from my example, you're just the same Mitchell Lane who came falling off the mountain all those years ago."
"From one corner of the globe to the other I've captured that lightning, and when I caught it I kept it locked up good and tight. I know better than to piss it away - because I am, as I have proven time and time again, the brightest diamond in this industry."
"Inferior stones are simply... cast aside and forgotten..." (SANDS gazes into the camera silently, a little smirk tugging at his lips as we FADEOUT) Badnews — 24 November 2004 (FADEIN on Mike Lane sitting on the floor of his den, leaning against a leather couch. There are several beer cans lying on the couch, floor and solid oak table in front, and a bottle of Wild Turkey about 10% full on that same table. The room is illuminated by a roaring fire from a giant fireplace in the background.)
LANE: I got a confession (takes a healthy sip of beer) I don't know who Christian Sands is... I don't, I've apparently been living under a rock all these years, months, weeks (shrugs) Exactally how long HAVE you been around Christian? How long have you been such an awe inspiring, attention grabbing cream of the crop performer in this industry? Cuz like I said I've been living under this four point five million dollar, eight bedroom, five bathroom rock for the past couple years I guess I missed your coronation as the hottest wrestling talent on this planet, and if I did I apologize.
LANE: (grabs the bottle of Wild Turkey, undoes the cap and takes a swig the puts his head back and stares directly up) :sigh: Maybe you are all you say you are Sands, I wouldn't know. You wanna tell me your the Chancellor of Germany then you're the chancellor of Germany. I'm not (Lane struggles for words and moves his hand around trying to get those words to come to him) I'm not up to date on all the current events. I don't interact all too often with the guys in the dressing room. I guess I live in this pathetic vaccuum. And maybe thats why Im here fighting you instead of competing for a world title. I've made excuses, I've said F the world countless times, its made me rich, and its made me a champion dozens of times over, but since a shmuck such as yourself takes issue with it, then maybe I should rethink who I am and make a change for the better?
LANE: If you want to consider me as a failure at life then thats definitally an arguable point. I've had ten times as many failures as I've had successes and I challenge you to find me any man on this planet with a better ratio than that. What you mistake as B****ing on my part I just factor it into my being a REALIST. I understand this world isn't raindrops, lolipops and moonbeams and that s*** does indeed happen. I've never made excuses Christian I've just told the TRUTH, a novel concept in this business I admit, but you could never label me a bulls*** artist.
LANE: And I'll tell you I DID have it all, but life has peaks and valleys and I'm somewhat quagmired in a valley right now. But don't cry for me Argentina because I'm still wealthy beyond all comprehension. I've made more money than you and the rest of your fly by night clique combined. And whether or not you measure success with dollar signs or world titles I've got you guys trumped on both counts.
LANE: (swishes the wild turkey bottle around a little, realizing theres a little less than a full sip remaining, downs that and chucks the bottle behind the couch) But you do seem to know me Chris, MUCH more than I know you, and I apologize again... you know... the rock. But you got a couple things wrong about me, obviously my name, but also where I went out. Because ya see before taking a couple years off to drink, snort, smoke and pop at will I was wrestling in three companies, two of them closed while I still held some gold. So I didn't flush my career down the drain Chris, I decided to walk away not because I couldn't cut it anymore, but because I didn't WANT to anymore. I'm not in this for ANOTHER shot at glory A**hole because mines still never faded. I was kicking around for a year before I got my first title shot in 96", the way the business is run today you've probably had forty seven in this year alone. Being on top doesn't kill me, not anymore, because I'll be back there again, I had to wait over a year the first time I came out, i have enough pateince, shockingly enough, to just hang around and hurt people until my number is drawn again.
LANE: But enough focusing on how inept you are, onto the match. It'll be over quick. Chris I have no intentions of pinning you, making you tap or having you pass out. I'm a new man at this point I'll take enough enjoyment in making you bleed, possibly smaking that C*** in the face and heading back to my not so humble abode. So don't F*** up the works, take your beating, I'll play along just don't be stupid and try to make your homecoming something more than the pedestrian beat down that it should be. That being said Chris, happy holidays or whatever time of year it is in this world :)
I'll see you at Ghoulish Games and I'll try to be the best Mitch Lane I can. JABolich — 27 November 2004 (FADEIN: A park bench somewhere. Clad as usual in his heavy trenchcoat, a smirking CHRISTIAN SANDS gazes into the camera through heavily mirrored shades. A close look would reveal the cameraman reflected in the lenses.)
"...I'm the Chancellor of Germany." "Don't worry too much, Mitchell Lane. I understand your little rock." "I'm a generous man, so I'm going to make note of something to you. When I first appeared in this company every viewer out there was shocked out of their socks. Why do you suppose that is? Here's a hint: It has something to do with the fact that in two and a half years there has been not a single talent on this circuit who has reached my level of prestige and infamy. Suffice it to say that when my face hit the airwaves on WFW TV people KNEW that the sh** was about to hit the fan, people KNEW that money was to be made, and people KNEW that buyrates were about to shoot through the damn roof, because everyone who is anyone KNOWS that Dan Ryan, Maelstrom, Lindsay Troy, and Christian Sands can singlehandedly carry a promotion. One of us is enough. Four of us is g**damn overkill."
"The minute the Inner Circle showed up in this fed WFW became an instant hot spot. And you don't even know why, do you, Mitchell Lane? I pity you, kid."
"I'm tempted to come out and notify you at just how many tens and hundreds of millions there are between the various multi-time World Heavyweight Champions of the Inner Circle, buuuut that'd make your claim of greater wealth somewhat ridiculous - and I think you're going to be embarrassed enough tonight that I should spare you this point, at least. See how generous I am?"
"So, Mr. Realist. You want to take that road, hm? Alright, let's talk facts. You came out and admitted that you took years off from this business, giving up World Titles in the process, so you could spend your life smoking dope. Translation: You care more about smoking up than you do about this business."
"Ah, but you've changed. You're suddenly cured and ready to take on the world." "But Mitch... You can take the dog out of the fight, but you can't take the fight out of the dog. And you can take the man out of the pile of sh(BOMB), but you can't take the pile of sh** out of the man."
"Makes you wonder if the old Mitchell Lane is really gone and forgotten." "Makes you wonder if you're really stronger than you were when you picked failure over glory."
"Pull yourself out of denial, Mitch. YOU PISSED YOUR CAREER AWAY. You think giving it up voluntarily makes it better, but you couldn't be more wrong - IT MAKES IT WORSE because it shows that YOU DON'T HAVE THE DRIVE to put this business ahead of your personal demons. And seeing you sitting on your couch with beer cans on the floor and a bottle of wine in your hand shows me that the same old Mitchell Lane is still in there, and he's having a blast watching you walk around in DENIAL."
"You claim being on top doesn't kill you -but it's obvious to me, Mitchell Lane, that YOU AREN'T GOOD ENOUGH TO BE ON TOP."
"Furthermore... it apparently DID kill you seeing as you stopped wanting to be there. How, how, HOW can you say you can handle being the top guy when you readily admit that you preferred to get high at will than to hold World Titles? Sounds like a conflict of interest to me, tiger!"
"But hey... you're the realist here, sport." "Shame your idea of realism sounds a lot to me like wishful thinking!" "So you want me to 'take my beating', huh? Well, just let me think about that one for a moment. Now let's see here. Should I be a good little boy, shut my mouth, and take the beating that Mitchell Lane has been so good to set aside for me? Or should I just say 'F**K THAT NOISE' and ensure that little Mitchell leaves the ring in a neck brace? Well, the answer seems obvious to me, so you'd better take a few measurements of your neck, Mitch - they'll need 'em when they're fitting a brace your size."
"'Cause maybe YOU aren't in this to pin me or make me submit - but bub, I'm a wrestler, and pinning people or making people submit is, y'know, my JOB. And one I've done quite well."
"So don't feel too bad when you're tapping that mat at Ghoulish Games, Mitchell... Not only are you not alone, you'll be able to run back home and roll a few spliffs to toke away your tears. Maybe crack open a couple dozen beers, y'know? Live that high life, man."
"But just remember, Mitchell." "No matter what you do, no matter how much sh** you talk, you will always and forever be... my B*tchell."
(FADEOUT) Badnews — 27 November 2004 (FADEIN on Mike Lane alone in a dimly lit bar in Boston with a Bourbon on the rocks at his table half drunk.)
LANE: (The camera slowly panning into Lane staring down at his drink twirling the top ice cube with his index finger. He looks up) YOU'RE RIGHT!!!! One hundred and fifty percent Chris, you are CORRECT. I don't have the drive I had back in the day. Why? Because I'm fighting guys like you week in and week out, win, lose or draw it's the same thing week after week heres Christian Sands, heres Golem, heres Johnny Midcarder. It gets boring, and it wears on a guy after time. It's possible Chris that I may be morbidly retarded for even bothering to show up when my music hits, I may be even more lacking in rational judgement to not give two S***s what comes out of my matches. Or maybe I'm smart enough to know I'm not going to be a richer man by beating you, and I won't be any worse off if you should beat me. It's the proverbial fly banging himself into the window. I'm not going to keep doing it because I know as hard as I try I'm not going to get past that window. So maybe I'm not stupid, and actually brilliant enough to be complacent in my surroundings.
LANE: I'm probably NOT that athlete that didn't lose a match for well over a year. Maybe a massive car accident and a couple years of further bodily abuse really wore on me. I'll give you that, but when I decided to make my comeback there were plenty of companies more than willing to take on this baggage for a buck. SO I whored myself out to the highest bidder, basically because I COULD, and here I am. I don't care about winning. I haven't for awhile. What would last week have done for me if I won? I'd still be wasting away fighting you. So bottom line Chris I ***DONT*** care about this business, its just a world I'm living in. I enjoy hurting and maiming, who doesn't? But Chris, anything I could accomplish at this point in my life would just be another notch on a belt, another sticker on the helmet. I did piss away what I had. You again are CORRECT, but what I have now is still assloads more than ninety percent of the shlubs in this business will be able to accomplish in a lifetime. I told you life has its peaks and valleys and as far as this industry goes I'm in a valley, and maybe I just don't have the fortitude to go ahead and make it any better?
LANE: I know how things goes Chris, again I'm a realist. You're going to win this match because you have the drive and determination and I could care less. You're going to win whether or not we have to matt wrestle for an hour or whether I come down to the ring and cut your arm off with a sword. SO put yourself in my shoes Chris, what would YOU pick? You don't get brownie points for putting forth a good effort, so I just dont care what the match looks like on PPV.
LANE: If the fans are looking for a good show then I'll run up into the stands and punch some dopey white guy in the face assuming he threw a paper cup at me. It wouldn't win me the match but it'd garner me national attention and they could rant about it on the news for a couple weeks.
LANE: Don't take me as a threat Chris. the worst that could possibly happen is you limp home, grab a few stitches and eat through a tube for a couple months, just in time to be ready for you BAD Title match at the next show. Because thats just how things work around here, I know it and I'm use to it. I'm not the Mike Lane that was around in 1996, 97, 98 or halfway through 99. I'm a little older, a lot more bitter and don't have the drive of a champion. Instead I have the bitter heart of a monster.
LANE: But I dunno Chris, in a few short days its you and me, your hand will be raised in victory, whether or not its attatched to the rest of your body? Well thats my call. But using me as a stepping stone is more often than not a plan destined for failure. Half the guys I've wrestled here haven't shown up in four months. The other half haven't won a match since. Maybe they all just suck, or maybe theres a curse attatched. Either way Chris at Ghoulish Games it'll be a lot of fun. When you vomit up bloody stool for a week, just remind yourself how great it was to beat a guy who was GREAT... in 1998
(FADEOUT)
