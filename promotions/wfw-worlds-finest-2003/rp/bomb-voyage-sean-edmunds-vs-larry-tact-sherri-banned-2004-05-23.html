Starbreaker — 24 May 2004 Fade in: backstage at Shock & Awe II. A cameraman heads into the locker room, calling out to see if anyone is around. Larry Tact comes walking through the locker room at one point, dressed in his ring attire of long navy blue tights and black boots. Sweat or water, maybe both, cover Tact's toned upper body. A towel with red blotches on it is pressed against his forehead. The cameraman approaches Tact, but gets shoved out of the way with an arm.
Cameraman: Wait! Larry, a word about your match! Please! Tact slams the door against the near wall when opening it, and walks out without saying a word. The cameraman walks out after Tact, and nearly catches up to him in the hallway before Tact turns around. The camerman, not expecting Tact to stop so abruptly, bumps into Tact, who shoves him back again.
Tact: What do you think you're doing? CM: Please, Larry...I just want to get a comment on your match. Just one! Tact: (frowns) Don't follow me, and don't call me Larry. Tact turns and begins walking away again. The cameraman stops Tact dead in his tracks about five paces out.
CM: Do you feel Copycat should get the respect he demands? Tact turns, looking deadpan at the cameraman. Then he gives a snort and a small, but noticeable smirk. Tact spits a wad of mixed saliva and blood on the floor in front of the cameraman. He turns and walks off again. Fade out.
Fade in: Black Rose walks the hallways during Shock & Awe. He comes to the Trainer's Room and finds Larry Tact exiting it. Tact has a patch covering the spot on his forehead where he held the towel before. Black Rose approaches Tact, who spots him and rolls his eyes.
Tact: No. No interview. Black Rose: Larry Tact, just one question and I'll be gone. Tact: Don't you people know when to quit? Black Rose: It's not in my contract. Now, after what we saw of you leading up to your match with Copycat, you seemed down on yourself. After wrestling Copycat, are you more satisfied?
Tact turns and walks away, uttering only one word. Tact: No. Black Rose chases after him, cameraman in tow. Black Rose: Is it to do with how you wrestled again? Tact: No. Black Rose: Then, you're satisfied with how you wrestled? Tact stops and looks at Black Rose, irritation in his voice. Tact: No. Look, I'm not going into it right now. Goodbye. Tact again walks away. Black Rose: So then, do you think you'll be prepared for your rematch with Sean Edmunds! Tact stops and looks back, eyebrows raised. Tact: Edmunds? Black Rose: He's rumored to be your opponent for BOMB VOYAGE. Tact doesn't speak immediately. Then he gives a small, lopsided grin. Tact: I'll be waiting for him on the boat. Black Rose: So you'll be ready? Larry, wait up! Tact: (walking away) I gave you more than your one question. Tact waves behind him and turns down a hallway. Fade out. Fade in: as the sun hits its peak in the sky. Clouds float around, teasing that they'll block the sun, but the bright rays shine down. Larry Tact and Cindy Winsted walk hand-in-hand on a park path. Tact has on blue jeans and a casual white collared, short sleeved shirt. He no longer has the patch on his forehead; a number of stitches are there in its place. Cindy has on a pair of blue jean shorts and a green halter top, matching the color of her emerald eyes.
Cindy: I don't know why you aren't happier about this match. I thought you really wanted to face Edmunds, with Sherri banned from ringside?
Tact: I did. And I'm not complaining about it, this is a good match for me. But don't expect me to start doing cartwheels over it.
Cindy: Do you think you can handle him? Tact: (looks at her with a frown) Of course I can. Cindy: (smiles) Glad to see you aren't putting this in the "WFW hates me" column. Tact: If you're talking about Copycat, you know the deal with that. Cindy: Yeah, I do, and you seemed to handle yourself pretty well against him. Tact: Pretty well. Not well enough. Cindy: Hey, you did something right at Shock and Awe. Otherwise Wink wouldn't have lined you up Edmunds, with the Sherri stipulation.
Tact: I got nailed in the face by Edmunds at Shock and Awe. Copycat made me pass out, something that's happened to me ONCE in my career, otherwise. I lost the match, the second straight. Even if my wrestling was sound, I still slipped. There's still something missing!
Tact's eyebrows lower and he looks frustrated. Tact: I don't get it. I thought my technique was it. Once that was fixed, I would be set. But there's something else....
Cindy: Here, let's sit. Cindy tosses back her shoulder-length, auburn hair and proceeds rubbing his back while they sit on the wooden bench. Tact's hands are clasped in front of him, his chin resting on them.
Cindy: Well, just remember what you said to Edmunds the first time. Stay focused on what you do best, and you should be fine.
Tact: That's just the thing. There's something missing that I can't put my finger on. Something that sparks the best in me. I was good against Copycat...real good, even. But I came up short for a reason, and it wasn't just the Cat's Claw. It wasn't just Edmunds. When I find it, I'll know it.
Cindy wraps her arms over Tact's shoulders and around him. Cindy: Well, in my department you've gotten straight As. Tact smirks and kisses her. Fade out. Fade in: as the sun descends. Tact walks onto the cruise ship and sits under an umbrella setup nearby the entrance, where a camera crew waits for wrestlers to do promos or interviews. Tact gets settled in a patio chair, setting down copies of FWI Magazine and FW Torch on the table. Tact leans against the back of the seat, stretching. His expression is serious, but level when he speaks, arms on the sides of the chair.
Tact: I didn't make a big deal of our first match, Edmunds. There's little point to it. Just the same, I filed it away in the "unfinished business" category, knowing we would meet again, sometime. And wouldja look at that...here we are. The one thing I can say about our first match and Shock and Awe II, fittingly, is that you and Sherri finally showed me something you two have in common. I thought it might have been Sherri was hiding something from the world down below all this time, but that's more something she'd have in common with MWG. Or that other nut Beau Michaels, maybe, if he goes for that kind of thing. But as for you and Sherri? Well, both of you like to be the ***** in a situation. She with you, and you with L.O.V.E. I'm sure you looked good to them, attacking-- correction, pitifully attempting to attack Copycat. Makes a good first impression...that you're as good in hitting your targets as you are holding onto positions of power. Like being the WFW Heavyweight Champion. And now, with that first "address" of yours, any thought you'd call this Presidency yours, and not L.O.V.E.'s term in office.
Tact chuckles and holds up a hand momentarily. He then takes a breath, now looking with a slight frown.
Tact: But the running joke that is Sean Edmunds isn't important. Let's get down to business, shall we? I'm not here to crack puns about you, after all, I'm here to make sure you know what's in store for you. I'll assure you right now, Sean, of this much: I may have hit a bad stride, but I'll continue to take pleasure in schooling you in the ring. I don't care if you want to credit yourself with a win last time, cuz everyone saw how it went down. I may be with a question or two, but I've got nothing to prove to you. Just like with Copycat, you still won't get respect from me. Only a match you don't want to deal with.
Tact crosses his arms. Tact: It's good you've got the attention of L.O.V.E., actually. Since I've got my sights set on one or two members of their group. I got sidetracked before, but the North American Title looks real bad around Psycho, and hell, the BAD World Title looks even worse around Jared Wells...despite the fact its trash around trash. At least the belt is useful trash, whereas Wells is just stinking trash. But if they're keeping an eye on you, then I can make use of that. You're the useful one right now. And I'm gonna use you how I need to, at Bomb Voyage. Don't think of it as anything out of the ordinary, Edmunds. While I'm showing you how worthless that Presidency is in the ring, just remember to be a humble. Otherwise....
Tact smirks. Tact: ...you'll get a humbling, too....and make a tactful surrender. Tact stands and walks back to the ship's entrance, where Cindy Winsted is boarding. A large man trails behind her, wheeling a cart with a few bags on them as Tact greets her. Fade out.

Starbreaker — 1 June 2004 Fade in: in the cabin area of the cruise ship. At the end of the hall we can see a door about half the size of the rest. It has a circular glass window embedded in it. It swings open and from it comes flying a height-challenged man. He lands with a "thud" on the floor of the hallway, and looks up rubbing his side. He's got a gruff look about him, and a scruffy beard to match a rugged set of clothes. At the moment he looks none too pleased, either.
Man: How...how dare you! That's it! I'm getting a room change! Out from the small door comes a woman about the same height as the man, with locks of blonde hair that settle on her shoulders. She wraps her arms around the man and speaks in an irritating whine of a voice, which sputters out her words in rapid fire fashion.
Woman: What a horrible man! Throwing you out of OUR ROOM just because HE had some trouble finding a place to stay. How terrible! Mario, are you alright?
Mario the Short Man: I'm fine, Daisy. Just fine. But WFW is going to hear from me, and so are you Mister!
Now emerging from the door -- though it's more of a crawl out the door -- is Larry Tact. He squeezes through the space allotted looking viciously at the man. Once he gets out of the room, he trips while rising to his feet, but regains his balance and tugs on his shirt a couple of times. Tact points a finger at the small couple.
Tact: I'm not to blame for this, ohhh no. You want to take this up with WFW, that's fine. In fact, I ENCOURAGE you to bring it right up with President Edmunds, because HE is the one to blame! NOT me!
Daisy the Short Woman: YOU'RE the one pushing my tubby hubby around and out of the door of OUR room, as if you own the place or something! You're lucky we were so kind to make room for you!
Tact: Make room? You call that "making room?" There's hardly enough space in there for me to SIT DOWN, much less do anything else! It's a midget room!
Mario the Short Man: HEY! You know, that's a slander towards my wife and I. Take that back right now!
Tact: What? You're a short person, whatever! Mario the Short Man: Don't try to cover up your words! You're just asking for a fight, aren't you Mister! Well I'm more than ready. In fact, I've been waiting to slug you one since you came into my and Daisy's life. These past few days have been nothing short of UNBEARABLE for us!
Tact: For YOU? At least YOU have your wife! Thanks to Edmunds, my girlfriend has to stay at the damn hotel down the street!
Mario the Short Man: Well, I DO find that unfortunate. I'd much rather she be here than YOU, Mister. At least she's got some great--
Daisy: MARIO! Don't give in to her temptations! That hussy of yours shouldn't be within a HUNDRED FEET of me OR my Mario. She's a...a...a VILE TEMPTRESS! The horoscope said I should make sure to keep close to my Mario, because a test of our love was approaching. She's an evil creature, determined only to lust away my Mario from me!
Tact: Actually, she's five foot ten and an attractive lady. Daisy: Blasphemy! Tact: You know, as much as I'd just LOVE to continue this utterly pointless discussion, I'm going to leave now.
Tact pulls a suitcase from the cabin door. He pulls a bit too hard and nearly hits Mario and Daisy, who shriek as the suitcase swings over their heads.
Tact: Well...that's one good thing about being, er...size deficient.... Mario: You're crazy! Daisy: Hey, he's got size where it matters! Tact: And that's...more than I cared to know. Tact begins walking down the hall but stops and turns back. Tact: What are you two doing here anyway? Why are you even on this ship? Daisy: Must you ask? Mario: Why, of course, we're here for the EPIC MATCH of EPIC MATCHES! The spectacular show of skills the likes of which only TWO men could display on this ship! It's the MATCH OF THE YEAR!
Tact: You aren't Manson fans are you? Daisy: Oh no. That man, one time, brought that monstrous Mexican creature, and he went to Mario and--
Tact: I don't want to know.... Mario: Daisy, I don't want to hear that! No, we are fans of TRUE competitors. The BESZT of the BEST! EL PHENOMITO and EL ARQUITO IRIS!
Tact: Ah...right. Should have seen that coming. Tact walks off. Fade out. Fade back in to the blazing sun, engulfing the cruise ship in its golden hue. Larry Tact sits above the pool, wearing blue shorts and a white t-shirt. He's got his feet dipped in the water, a pair of sandals set aside.
Tact: Edmunds, you know, you've tried hard to make things as unpleasant as possible for me this trip. I'll tell you, though, while I may be getting the stained carpet treatment from the caterers, and anyone who could change my room....I'm enjoying this. Yes, I've made the best of this cruise. And I'll tell you why, cuz it does pertain to you. See, I realized something when I from the moment I was given my room assignment with Mario and Daisy...paint them blue and they'd be the perfect fit for "smurf couple from hell." But anyway...I realized that, for all you've said in your promo, and your plans for me on this ship, it all points to one conclusion. You're acting out of desperation, Seany boy. You've got nothing to refute my in-ring skill, save for a couple of claims that you know little to nothing about. They're just hollow, crackpot notions that you'll hope I'll take into consideration.
Tact shakes his head. Tact: Not gonna happen, Edmunds. I know I might not be the pristine image of cohesiveness and stability as I usually am....but don't take that as a sign of impending victory for you, and impending defeat for me. Primarily because you're blowing hot air about any questions I may have, and secondly, you're the one who needs to be worrying about answering questions. Cuz Sean, the signs are all there for you. The signs of your OWN downfall. Listen to your little playmate Sherri for once, and you might see what I mean. If the best you've got to boast is that you've held the WFW World Heavyweight Championship in the past, and that YOU should be "Mr. WWL?" Well, then you're making a cardinal sin. You're going against your usual grain. You heard Sherri, you don't usually talk about the past. And you know it's true. Yet for some reason, you find it necessary NOW....when you don't have your most loyal trooper at the ready. When you don't have her, and you're going against ME. A wrestler who, in your own words, has been heralded as "the next big thing." Is that so, yeah?
Tact slaps the water, causing a spritz to patter the deck, as he chuckles and shakes his hand off.
Tact: I've been HERALDED? Have I? By who, I wonder, cuz nobody seems to take notice of me most of the time. I'm a guy who flies under the radar, for the most part, Edmunds. You should know I'm not the biggest boaster in the biz....but I'm also potent when I do make a claim. I'm subtle, but precise. In my words AND my wrestling. I don't need to be heralded...appreciated...hell, I don't need to be cheered. Whether I am or not, though, only one thing matters. I know, deep down, that yeah, I AM the "next big thing," if you want to call it that. I'd put it a little differently. Larry Tact isn't so much the "next big thing." Larry Tact is...the NEW ERA of wrestling.
Tact grins. Tact: I don't need anyone to know it definitively, but for one person...myself. As long as I don't lose sight of that, I know I'll continue striving to reach the top. I'll keep on moving up the ranks, slowly but surely. Maybe there'll be a bump in the road here, or a rung knocked out there. It won't keep me from continuing on. It won't stop me from battling on...surviving through opponent after opponent. And when someone continues to do that, no matter how long it takes, Sean -- and you should know this well -- that person WILL make it to the top. It may come sooner...it may come later...but eventually, the time will come. And so far this year, despite my difficulties here and there -- because there HAVE been difficulties -- I'm climbing higher than ever before.
Tact runs his fingers along the surface of the water, gently, causing the slightest ripples.
Tact: Subtly....precisely...steadily. These are how I make my moves, Sean. You may have been the WFW World Heavyweight Champion, but you aren't anymore. You may be WFW President, but you're discovering that the position has its limitations. And you may have beaten Jean Rabesque for the first time in your career, now 1 and whatever. It doesn't matter how many times you've lost to him, really. I applaud your victory over the man, in fact, cuz some people never have been able to do it in their entire careers. Some never will. But Sean, what DOES matter is, however good it makes you feel to have attained that first victory against him...I'm going to show just how much better I can be than you, because I'll beat him even SOONER. And Sean, if you want more concrete proof of my superiority to you, it won't be long. Because at BOMB Voyage, I'll show you first hand just who is above who. I'm going to enjoy schooling your political ***. After all, it's not every day I can force a President into a tactful surrender. But one of those days is coming, Sean-O. It's coming, and you've got nothing but the past to protect you. Unfortunately for you, the past is going to be about as much help for you as Middle Management McGee and Mighty Impala. Cuz just like with Blue Cat, all those two will be able to do to help you....is pick up the pieces of your pride, once we've battled in the ring.
Tact slides down into the water, looking with a smug grin. Tact: Who knows...maybe you ARE "simply sensational," Sean. But you'll find out that isn't quite enough to get the job done against me, when it's one-on-one. Cuz I'm not JUST simply senational, Sean. I'm simply...TACTILIZING. And you, Sean Edmunds...you are not. That's a simple truth you'll be learning the hard way, at BOMB Voyage.
