Superbowl of Wrestling: Iron Lion vs. Larry Tact vs. El Arco Iris

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 1 April 2004 The following match is an eliminated match. The RP/Angle deadline is Monday, April 12th 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
JABolich — 4 April 2004 (FADEIN: A truck stop off this or that highway. IRON LION leans against his blue Chevy Blazer, his arms folded across his chest and one knee slightly bent.)
Iron Lion: There's something fishy going on here. Last week, I stepped into the ring against the Mighty Impala and gave him the beating of his life. I didn't even break a sweat. Why is it, then, that I am opening the show while the man whom I crushed effortlessly is competing for a title? This is exactly what I've been talking about. Wrestling is going to hell in a handbasket, with incompetence and failure being met with rewards while those who SUCCEED are pushed to the bottom of the card, shunted away so that those with less skill and more political connections may make names for themselves without worrying about being shown up.
On the bright side... I at least made it onto this all-important event and am competing against at least two people with more than a shred of decency in them.
Larry Tact, you and I exchanged words before the So Cal Rumble two shows back, and I am obliged to say that you left a good impression. You strike me as one of the few men around here with a modicum of decency and sense - and humility. Good. It's refreshing to encounter men like you in a world rife with egomaniacs, psychopaths, and masochists. From the tape I've seen of you, you've got the ability to back it up. At the risk of sounding sycophantic, I'm looking forward to testing my skills against you, because squaring off against weak little men like the Mighty Impala has left me HUNGRY for a challenge! Granted, I would have preferred to face you one-on-one, but I take what I can get.
Speaking of which... It's surprisingly difficult to find a way to address El Arco Iris. I suppose I could fall into the same rut as everyone else and mock his attitude, but demeaning another man's positive philosophy smacks of dishonor. To be frank, I like your attitude, Iris. As overblown about it as you are, you're a good man, and it's a testament to your integrity and pride in yourself that you maintain your attitude of happiness. I'll certainly concede that you have strength beyond your stature, because strength is derived from pride and satisfaction with oneself.
I can't help but notice the stark contrast between the three of us. We have in one corner a technician of great merit. In another corner, a very happy daredevil. In the final corner, a monster. I look forward to testing my philosophy of combat against two wildly different ones. That said, I have confidence in my strength, intelligence, and abilities to bring me victory. Taking nothing away from either of you, there IS a rather distinct size differential in this case. How do you take a man down to the mat when you can barely budge him? And how can you fly all over the ring when you're being flung around by a man twice your size? Simply put, I was brought into this world possessing the gift of physical dominance, and I've learned to utilize that gift to the fullest extent.
At the Superbowl of Wrestling, I will do everything in my power to emerge victorious and prove to the politicans and degenerates of WFW that SKILL AND INTEGRITY are greater weapons than political favors and immorality. Through the combination of pride, strength, and honor I will fight my way up the road to the top - but I will do it of my own merit rather than milking favors. A victory here at the Superbowl will bring me one step closer to achieving my goals and proving that integrity is alive and well.
I look forward to our encounter, gentlemen. Take care. (FADEOUT as LION slips back into the Blazer) Diablo — 8 April 2004 (CUE UP: "Scatman" by Scatman John) (FADEIN: After the signature spectral array, we fade to a room in a children's hospital, where all of the children are sitting up in their beds, eagerly awaiting something or someone. A nurse emerges and addresses all the children.)
Nurse: Everyone, as you've all been waiting, our special guest is finally here! Please welcome El Arco Iris!
(El Arco Iris enters, in his mask, a tie-dye t-shirt, and neon green cargo pants, and cradling a book in his arm. He walks with kind of a limp, noticeably favoring his back. His smile, however, seems unaffected. He helps himself towards a chair in the middle of the room. The children greet him with shouts of joy and adoration.)
El Arco Iris: Hello, everyone, hello! El Arco Iris is simply ecstatic to see you all! Are we all feeling good today?
Children (collectively): Yeah! (One of the children props himself up on his arms and lets out an assertive voice) Child: But Iris, are you okay? You got really beaten up out there last week! Iris: Yes, El Arco Iris did not have much luck against the forces of this so-called "L.O.V.E.", but El Arco Iris shall grin and bear it as one must always do. Knowing they did not break El Arco Iris' spirit and did not destroy El Arco Iris' will is reward enough.
Another child: But what about Felix Red?! He's a bad man now, why did he hurt you? Iris: Ah yes, Señor Red...he is a man with his own problems, but he is afraid of being helped. He'd much rather wallow like a pig in the mudpile of his own fright and misery, so when El Arco Iris offers his friendship and to be his partner, Red naturally rejects it and must assault Iris for trying to break him free from his own prison of self-destruction. El Arco Iris will never give up on trying to make the unhappy finally let go of their problems, though, and neither should any of you give up on yourselves or on others, right?
All: Right! Yet another child: But what about the match you have now? The two guys you're facing don't seem so bad, and they don't seem to want to hurt you. Especially that Iron Lion, he's cool!
Iris: Yes, yes, El Arco Iris is very happy to be matched up with two great athletes who simply want to compete and enjoy the sport without spite and anger. El Arco Iris is particularly proud of Iron Lion for being a man of such strong character as he is. He's a rather large man, and he could be like so many other big men and simply think that size equals power, superiority, and pick on people smaller than him simply out of compulsion to prove that he is indeed better, but he has found a purpose to devote himself to, the pursuit of honor and success, so he has developed himself a decent person, and El Arco Iris must return his admiration and display mutual respect for him.
(El Arco Iris pauses to think, then looks around to see all of the children paying attention, before folding his arms at his chest in seriousness.)
Although, El Arco Iris believes that Iron Lion goes about his pursuits a little bit to the extreme. While pursuing honor is good, being obsessed with it can not be healthy for anyone. Iron Lion has become so obsessed with honor and combat, and has thus started to become conceited with his own power and abilities that he may be on the verge of becoming spiteful and hateful like those the both of us despise. If you become too focused on simply being honorable, you become overly scrutinizing of both yourself and others and you start down that horrible path...
Same child as before: So you're going to try and set him right? Iris: Precisely. At the Superbowl of wrestling, El Arco Iris can make sure that the new year of WFW can signal that the growth of exuberance and joy shows NO signs of stopping, even if L.O.V.E. stands in its way, because no matter how dark the horizon is, the sun is always on the other side.
Child: Yeah! Larry Tact seems to be pretty cool with you, too. Iris: Yes, El Arco Iris will give Señor Tact all the credit he deserves, he is an awesome athlete and an equally great sportsman, and all of his experience has not yet turned him bitter and hateful. El Arco Iris looks forward particularly to seeing the challenge Tact will provide, knowing his excellent skill and talent. It will be an excellent match and we should all be expected to give our all and prove our mettle. But, despite the skill and size of El Arco Iris' respective opponents, El Arco Iris knows he shall best the both of them in that ring because El Arco Iris can and WILL lift spirits like no other can. El Arco Iris can count on it. El Arco Iris can also count on the support of all of you, right?
All: YEAH! Iris: And El Arco Iris knows that El Arco Iris' work is all worth it when El Arco Iris hears that. El Arco Iris will make sure for all of you to take the faces of both Iron Lion and Larry Tact for all of you and put a big SMIIIIIIILE...(traces the smile on his mask with his thumb)...on them! Now...who wants El Arco Iris to read a story?!
(The children all give shouts of joy and affirmation, and El Arco Iris takes the book out from under his arm and opens, reading to all the enchanted children as we Fade to Rainbow)
JABolich — 12 April 2004 (FADEIN: A dark sitting room, illuminated by a dim tabletop lamp and the glow of a television, which is displaying an El Arco Iris match. IRON LION sits on a sofa with his back to the camera. He is, of course, recognizable by his mane of golden hair and his sheer stature.)
Iron Lion: You know, Iris, at first I was going to come out here and respond to you with the same old. You know, questioning your sanity, questioning how you can bring happiness in a profession focused around hurting people, that sort of thing. But then I noticed something you said.
You said I'm steering towards the wrong path. I was going to come out and deny it immediately, but I thought better of it, because if what you said were to be true I wouldn't be able to live with myself. I grew up in a country family. My parents taught me that the only way to succeed and be happy was to work hard, be strong, and respect others. If I were to somehow deviate from my present course, it would be to go against everything that I am.
Maybe I do come across as a little extreme sometimes. However, in a business as dark and corrupt as this, it's a necessary act. Who would take notice were I to sit back and apply my philosophies passively? No, Iris, the only way for me to truly change the future of wrestling is for me to actively share my views with the world, because in doing so I am showing future generations of wrestlers that there are alternatives to being devious and evil.
How am I becoming spiteful or hateful, Iris? I don't see it, certainly not towards so happy a man as yourself. I hold no malice towards ANYONE here in WFW, not even the egomaniacal Sean Edmunds. The only contempt I may show is towards the IDEALS upheld by those acting in arrogance, weakness, and dishonor.
And please, enlighten me on exactly how I qualify as conceited. Did you notice that I spent half of my last promotional segment trying NOT to show disrespect to your abilities or those of the distinctly absent Tact? But that's right... I'm conceited because I want to win. Would you rather I come out on television every night and proudly proclaim that I intend to lose? It'd be a change from the norm, but that's not my style. I fight to win, to show the world that pride, strength, and honor still live. At the same time I do not deny that losses happen. I do, however, strive to prevent them.
Our encounter, it seems, is nigh. I look forward to it, Iris - as I hope you do as well. Now if you'll excuse me, I have some last-minute cramming to do.
(FADEOUT as a new match flickers across the TV) Starbreaker — 13 April 2004 ooc: sorry...was a crazy week, and I went out of town over the weekend. Go Boston! -------------------------------- Fade in: on Larry Tact, dressed in black leather pants, boots, a grey, sleeveless shirt, and blue bandanna covering his colored blonde hair. Tact looks calm while sitting on a black stool, a smirk on his face.
Tact: I should have known the way things would work out here. Upon arriving, I showed that I wasn't worried about being overmatched by opposition. I was given a shot against Edmunds, someone considered 'top tier' around here. I went and showed I had the ability to match holds and compete on that level here...but somehow, I found my efforts forgotten. Overlooked, just like they always are. But I chalked it up to not having seniority here, and kept going. Months later, I find myself having gone through the same exact cycle. I got a match with Edmunds, supposed to be some sort of offering for retribution from the WFW hierarchy. I know it amounted to little or nothing, of course. Edmunds practically bailed from the match before it even started, pretty much admitting he wasn't anywhere near ring shape. He played the sympathy card, the fool. It served its purpose, though, I suppose. But I'm not going to harp on him. I barely had it on my mind that Sherri the Vegas prostitute had a hand in our last match, to the point where I didn't even request she be banned from ringside. Not that Wylde would have done it, anyway. But Michaels and I went out and did what everyone saw as a forgone conclusion, and beat the snot out of the gimp. So now we come to the Superbowl of Wrestling. I had challenged Richard Gideon for the WFW North American Title, but apparently he's too busy with the soap opera starring himself and Psycho, and WFW is busy milking it for all it's worth...which brings up the question of how it's still being milked.
Tact shrugs. His smirk fades. Tact: As for myself? Once again, I've been passed over for any significant matchup, as has been the case for pretty much the whole of my time here. Because let's face it, everyone....even Edmunds was just a warmup match for him. I knew it at the time, said as much, and had no problem with volunteering for that spot. But it seems the WFW hierarchy took that as a sign that I would somehow not mind being used as a filler for cards? Bull****! I'm not here to waste away my days. I'm here to find challenges, and make my own legacy. I've stated that since I walked into this promotion, but nobody listened. Why? Is this place just like the rest, where interfering in another person's match is all that will get someone up the ladder? I thought this was where the worlds finest wrestling would be...was I wrong? Did I step into worlds finest interference? Cuz you have Luster being taken out...Edmunds, wrestling's cripple, in a high profile match...Anarky, the resident intellectual headcase who interferes in his fair share of matches, also in a high profile match....and Michael Manson in a high profile match, which speaks for itself. Granted, I won't take away the fact that these are talented individuals, but there is definitely a trend. These people aren't solely talented in wrestling, and their wrestling isn't all that got them to the top. So to see myself at the very bottom of the barrel on this card makes me wonder just what is WFWs goal.
Tact crosses his arms over his chest, frowing. Tact: I came here for challenges, but what am I getting instead? I'm getting 'potentials'....people with potential talent that could take them to a higher plateau, rather than people who are in the higher ranks of this promotion. Iron Lion...Iris...they've got the potential to be great stars. But when I've had chances to prove myself in other places, I've done as much. Larry Tact is not a potential....he is established. I'm not going to get into the ring with two extremely opinionated wrestlers, who believe they can take everyone they come across and assimilate them, and be satisfied with wrestling them. I'm not here to wrestle missionaries. I'm here to wrestle in-ring challenges. These two are unique in their traits. Iron Lion is big. Iris is a small speedster and high flyer. But even together, they don't amount to what I bring. I bring a physique that matches both of theirs, and despite Iron Lion's size, I have the speed to beat him. Iris, you'll be lucky to knock me down, honestly, because I will catch you in my arms...before knocking your head off. I'm tired of being looked on as a test dummy. I'm not here to show WFW who can make the promotion cut. I'm here to show the I can cut it at the top.
Tact stands. Tact: So the Superbowl of Wrestling isn't going to be my plateau to the North American Title? Fine.
Tact smirks. Tact: I'll make it a train wreck for Arco Iris and Iron Lion. Tact walks off. Fade out.
