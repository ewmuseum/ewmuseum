New World Madness: Alex Wylde vs. Diablo

EGarrett — 2 October 2003 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM WFW'S PRESIDENT ____________________ INT. ELECTION OFFICE Alex Wylde is in his packed Jersey City Election Office celebrating his WFW Presidential victory. Wylde, dressed in jeans and a shirt with an untied tie, is moving amongst a crowd of supporters holding an open and foaming bottle of wine. Next to him in the group of what appear to be local strippers, gang members, and other assorted riff-raff, is Elaine the Campaign secretary. She's a light black woman, very pretty, with braided hair. She carries with her a cell phone and a clipboard. Wylde makes his way towards a microphone stand.
WYLDE (Over mic)"Well folks...this campaign was...like many good things in life... long, hard and almost never coming. But in the end...just like I knew would happen...the First Presidential Candidate is now THE...FIRST...PRESIDENT. And let me tell you...it's GOOD TO BE KING...uh...I mean PRESIDENT!"
The crowd gives a number of drunken cheers in classic Jersey accents. Elaine looks at the cellphone, puts in her earpiece, then whispers something to Wylde.
WYLDE (Over mic)"Now...I've just been informed that I've already been signed for my first match as President. Unfortunately, I don't have control for my own booking...and someone has seen fit to play a little joke on me apparently. That joke is to put me against a wrestler that anyone in their right mind knows does not exist. Diablo. He is not a man...but a falsity. A hoax perpetrated by Shawn Hart...the SECOND PLACE CANDIDATE...the FIRST LOSER...to make people think he has a friend. I know you all enjoyed watching me lead Team Wylde, with my partners Sean Edmunds and Pulsar...to victory over Hart, Wells and his imaginary friend. Now...I have the opportunity to concentrate on my initial Presidential duties...since I don't have to worry about this 'phantom' opponent. You see...my first show as President is called "NEW WORLD MADNESS." And I fully intend to show everyone that this IS a New World's Finest Wrestling. *MY* World's Finest Wrestling. And yes...to the uneducated...genius can often appear to be madness. I'm going to show you just that type of genius in a league that I am taking under my wing from now on...and it begins at the appropriately named card..."
Wylde stops to acknowledge the rabid cheers of the crowd once again. Elaine continues to fiddle with the phone next to him...apparently looking at a list and making a number of phone calls. She pulls on Wylde's shirt sleeve and points to her watch when he looks.
WYLDE (Over mic)"Let me speak to Diablo...or whoever will be playing him at this next card. My figment friend. You have the opportunity to be part of something very special. You're going to be more than a tune-up. You're going to be the first WFW property I touch as President. You get to be the first example I set for this league's populace. For better or for worse...whether you agree or fight...I'm going to put you under my authority one way or the other. And just like the rest of this league...in the end...you're going to be better off because of it. Now if you'll excuse me...I have a public service announcement to film for the rest of the WFW. Thank you!"
Wylde steps away from the mic and acknowledges the cheers of the crowd...before heading with Elaine towards an open door. In that room is a blue screen and a number of television cameras. He goes in and closes the door.
FADEOUT. _____________ THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING AND YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE
Diablo — 2 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-02-03 AT 09:31&nbsp;PM (EST)](CUEUP: "Ich Will" by Rammstein) (FADEIN: Nightime. Diablo's apartment is dimly lit, because of the flimsy electric light hanging from the ceiling. Diablo is seated on his couch, arms folded across his chest, with a smirk on his face.)
Diablo: Ah, Alex Wylde. So tightly wrapped in the security blanket of his newfound "power". When I first heard your recent statements my initial reaction was to refute everything you said, but after thinking about all the things you said about me...I began to like it. After all, what is Diablo? I know for sure, Diablo is no man or beast. Diablo is flesh, but is not being. Diablo...
(The smirk now twists into a sickening grin.) Diablo: ...Yes...Diablo...Diablo is merely a physical manifestation of all the horrors the mind creates. All the abysmal thoughts that you keep so securely locked in the back of your mind eventually escape, and then they turn into Diablo. Yes, I think your description was ever so accurate.
(Diablo chuckles to himself) Diablo: Diablo is the monster hiding under your bed, and in your closet, and in your eyes when you close them to go to sleep. Diablo is every ounce of apprehension, fear, anxiety, torment, and loss that plagues your psyche, ready to confront you, and swallow you WHOLE! Diablo is something that can't be defeated with political power, no. Diablo cannot be legislated away. Diablo can be out of sight, Diablo can be out of mind, but Diablo will always be IN YOUR HEAD. Diablo does not exist, Wylde. No. I am not here. I am simply you telling yourself that your end shall come as quick as your ascension to power.
(Diablo pulls a book of matches from his pants pocket, plucks a few out, splays them in his hand, and lights them all, watching them burn with reverence.)
Diablo: While the C.O.C.K.S. as a faction shall seem to be under your control, the darkness, MY darkness, as an entity, will be the hearth upon which your funeral pyre shall be lit. And the flames, oh the flames! It will be a magnificent sight as your body is reduced to nothing but blackened char! You have already prepared the kindling, Wylde. I have the flint. You shall stoke the embers, because this figment is about to become the most real thing you will ever know.
(Diablo clenches his fists around the matches, extinguishing them.) Diablo: Perhaps to get my point across, I will just have to permenently WARP any and all perceptions of reality and fantasy for you. Either way is fine by me, Mr. President. Just remember to dress...for warm weather.
(Diablo's grin cracks into a cackling laugh, as he flicks away the used matches and we FTB)

INT. STUDIO Alex Wylde's official "WFW President" logo appears on the screen, before fading to a red, white, and blue WFW flag graphic background. In front, seated in a folding chair and dressed in street clothes, is Alex Wylde. He chatters a bit into the earpiece of his cellphone, then tells the person to hang out and speaks.
WYLDE"Good afternoon, friends, fans, and opponents. This is your president here. It's good to hear that someone has decided to put on the Diablo mask and speak up on behalf of the phantom wrestler. This individual appears to be different from the cheap B-movie actor that appeared in Shawn Hart's skits a scant few weeks ago. Now I am being exposed to some type of peyote-smoking hippie who's read the 'Los Revelaciones' chapter of the Mexican bible one too many times."
The background fades to shots of Diablo's mic spots interlaced with images of the generic halloween Devil image...red shirts, a pointy tail and goatee with little horns.
WYLDE"Unfortunately it appears our friend El Diablo IV has misquoted our speech. You'd think someone would've warned him that En Sap summary translations aren't always accurate. I never said you were some type of unseen, primeval force. Nor did I marvel at your seeming presence at the base of every man's hidden fears of unspeakable and dramatic horror. I simply stated the truth. Diablo, the wrestler, is a man that doesn't exist. My recent audit of the WFW's financial records reveals that no paycheck was made out to you during at least the last three weeks. Now...the average man wouldn't have put it past you to have expertly negotiated a deal for one million dollars over one million years. Or something similar that would have you paid under the table with spare change from the popcorn man's tip jar. But combined with other evidence it suggests that you were created. Not by the Lord our God before he breathed life into Earth...but by our Interim Champion after he realized that he has no friends and no fans that didn't give birth to him."
The Devil/Diablo shots fade out, and the background is replaced with the Presidential logo.
WYLDE"So it's not exactly a spirit of evil that I'm facing...but more like a cheap cardboard cut-out with a picture of Shawn Hart with a pitchfork and red-greasepaint stuck on with thumbtacks. If you detected concern in my voice...it's because I'm worried about getting one stuck in arm or chest while pinning you. If someone should happen to come out and wrestle in your name at the card...then that's no longer a concern. You could be the Devil himself...the truth is that there are 3 billion people in India and China who've never even heard of you...but they damn sure pay their share to the Government. In that case...it would put the President over the Goat-footed one in terms of followers by a healthy margin. Either way you're going to lose. And when I remove my boot from your chest...I'll have made my statement plain and simple that science is fighting death...but Taxes are forever."
The lights go down...reducing Wylde to silhouette in front of the Presidential logo. FADEOUT. THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE AND WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING.
Diablo — 6 October 2003 (CUE UP: "The Trooper" by Iron Maiden) (FADEIN: Diablo, attired in a white bandana and tattered army green cargo pants, is reclining on his old couch, hands behind his head, with an even broader smug grin than before.)
Diablo: Again, it seems when one is confronted with an unknown force, and is too confused with fear of it, one tries to equate it with something he's more familiar with. Some things, though, just aren't what you'd like them to be.
(Diablo chuckles to himself) Diablo: You know, this totally narrow-minded comparison to the Christian devil really amuses me. I mean, why is it that everyone seems to hate Satan so much? Satan, or as they write about him, is nothing more than a punisher of the unjust, serving them eternal torment for their sins. Apparently he's doing nothing more than serving a purpose that needs be served. He's God's garbage man. He really should be thanked for what he does in my opinion. But no, Wylde. Diablo, however ironic that it is, is not the devil, or claims to be. Diablo is not a punisher. Diablo is a non-discriminating harbringer of agony. Diablo carries a disease of his own making. A dark consumption that was created out of the fear and hate that was locked inside him for so long, but soon BECAME him. You too deny the fear that inhabits your mind. Every man denies it. It is the denial of their fear that destroys them. Soon you, too, will carry your own plague. You will be consumed as well, by your own darkness.
(Diablo sits up and rests his arms on his knees.) Diablo: Fear is a very real thing, Alex Wylde. Your fear makes the darkness stronger, makes me stronger. Your hate is a precious fuel. You are just a bubbling crock of supressed anguish, flowing to the brim, and I have the ladle in my hand. You will make a fitting feast for the darkness, Wylde. Be it of my doing, or your own, it will overcome you.
(Diablo lifts up his hands and shrugs his shoulders, and then returns his hands to his knees.)
Diablo: It is true. Diablo, the wrestler, does not exist. But Diablo, he who breaks wills and ends careers, he who is master of the flame, and imprisoned in his own abyss, he who will watch on with glee as your flesh is seared by burning wood, is a very tangible being, who will show you his own perdition. One of which no man is safe, just or unjust. There can be as many devils that religious texts will force you to fear, but there can be only ONE Diablo. I am more than just ink on paper or cardboard, Wylde. I am your demise...PERSONIFIED. If you simply accept your fear, let it floooow, then perhaps you might last a while longer. Either way, it does not look to good. Darkness...draws power...from the light.
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow."
