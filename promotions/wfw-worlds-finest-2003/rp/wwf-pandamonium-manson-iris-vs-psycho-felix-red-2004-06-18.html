Manson — 19 June 2004 ((FADEIN: WFW World Heavyweight Champion MICHAEL MANSON in black silk suit with blood red dress and blackened tie sits at a posh wooden table. The WFW World Heavyweight Title Belt rests nears his arm as he pricks his finger to continue signing documents.))
MANSON(without looking up): Gentlemen, gentlemen, I wish I could say I have never been assaulted by pastry before, but I'm sure we'd all rather not dwell on formalities. Onward then. I have been attacked many times, by many people, and that's why I cannot look upon LOVE with anything but apathy. As a fellow artists, I could critique it with all its banality of being driven through a table and how it lacked the true psychological torment that I crafted for so long.
....but then...Felix Red is the one to bring in the pastry..to mark my chest....and actually try to make something of the whole thing. You see, I know Anarky, and Psycho seems much the same, and they both lean toeard the idea that just attacking someone is enough. Just enough to make them bleed.
While there are the more creative among us, I count Felix Red as one, who see things symbolically and on other playing fields, including the mind.
Which is to say, while Anarky lies awake at night trying to plan my demise, Felix Red is the one that will actually come close. He's the one with the title shot and, of course, the vision. Anarky might want to think he's a leader, but at the end of the day and night, he's just a thug. Which is why everyone would always elevate me above him.
And now they'll do the same with Felix. And him, I wonder about. Does he just want to be the WFW champion? Or does he want to beat Michael Manson to be the WFW champion? I know what Nark wants. I don't care what Psycho or Wells wants.
Therefore, we're going to play a game. As on now, these very documents I sign with my own blood, I am placing a bounty on myself. A monetary sum, but so much more.
The person, the man in the WFW that can take me out, that can inable me, will be granted either the first title shot while I lay wounded or the title itself, whatever our esteemed president decides.
I can make this happen, I almost already have by stating it. So don't worry about that. There's no time limit to this, and keep in mind, I have strict criteria for such things. Now you may wonder why I would place a target upon my back. Well, there's already one ot seven hundred there. I've never tag team partners before anyway, not even Irco, who might even see past the plague of happiness to take his chance within a steel cage.
But with LOVE...it comes down to what you want......do you want to really rule the WFW...really run things and not just be a generic group of thugs......e.g. overcome MICHAEL MANSON the world's finest wrestler...or do you just want the title?
Are you going to attack me just like everyone else.....or are you going to go out of your way to make sure that Felix Red gets his shot at me no matter what?
Decisions, decisions. But we have this cage match and we'll do this as we always do...act as we always do.....but I'm sure this will be on all your minds.
Just not mine. Because its not like this is any different for me than before. NotorisSTD — 20 June 2004 (CUEUP: "Cease to exist" by Charles Manson...) Then, apparently a retarded Australian fellow slammed my skull against the floor, and then he pinned me."
Usually it's easier to let people believe what they want. That night I dreamt of blue lines darting and curving and abruptly forming angles across a yellowish white nebula. The strange thing was, and maybe it was the oxycodone talking, but I can't remember the last time I heard a Marilyn Manson song, and the only thing from this dream that reflected anything from my conscious mind was "The Speed of Pain," which was playing in the background. There's what I want to believe, what I've been telling people, that I have you, Michael, right where I want you. That you're as good as undone and I'm as good as champion. Even if that's true, a song from the same record your walk down music is on is also the soundtrack to my surreal...
You said this isn't any different from what's already happened. As in, it's all happening again. As in, it keeps happening. I'm haunted by ghosts of the future. Memories of what's to come after now. Once you can envision something, you've doomed it to take place. Every prophecy is self fulfilling. LOVE, what cowards we are, thrashing you as we did. You outnumbered, you exhausted from just having narrowly overcome Gideon after an hour long match. I'm not stupid enough to think I was going to surprise you. If memory serves, you saw that coming immediately following the War Games scam.
For the record, the sharpie was my idea but the cake was all Anarky. Honestly I'm still not sure what the point of that was. The question isn't "why did LOVE do this?" it's "Why was it obvious from the start that LOVE was going to do this?" You think Anarky's sick of living in your shadow, but that hasn't been the case for quite a while. You have the one title that LOVE doesn't control, which is incidently the one title anyone really cares about, but we all knew well before you won the title, that it would come to something like this.
It's quaint. Copycat, in his never ceasing holy crusade for respect from his pears, can't seem to inspire anything besides contempt and mockery. I've gone to certain lengths to repulse whatever traces of admiration I'd otherwise attract. But here I am with the world champion equating me, not with the worms, but with himself.
Bravo for me. Wasn't it Andy Warhol who said you only get something after you don't want it anymore?
So then what do I want? What's the reason for all this? If you're right, and I am something like you, then I want the same things you want. Or the same things you wanted before the legacy of Michael Manson became more powerful than you yourself. To take madness and nihilism and general confusion and make them into something tangible. A landscape. To do this one must play the super villain, rip apart whatever doesn't gel with your vision, abstract as it may be. In my case, that would mean ripping apart pretty much everything and everyone. Except I'm not really a super villain, am I? And neither were you. Really, all we are as opposed to the rest of them is sociopathic. We'll attack a given adversaries heart and mind as well as their flesh. Most people would feel bad about doing that, but I tend to feel like I've wasted my time if I don't. Probably you do to. The problem you've run into, like I said, is your legacy, well, your tenure has become more relevant than you. You're their hero now. You're the same kind of person you used to ruin. You've become everything you used to say you despised. The Michael Manson of 5 years ago wouldn't come within 20 feet of El Arco Iris without nailing him to the nearest wooden object. It’s my guess, that this transformation was an accident. Unless it wasn’t. Maybe the anti-christ of wrestling persona was just a self destructive bid for attention, or even for love...
I have my doubts. Some time ago I denounced a good friend for selling drugs. I spent much of my time on the cruise ship doing exactly that, this thing I had conditioned myself to believe was wrong. Because really, in spite of how I wanted to see myself, I never believed in anything. People change. Nobody changes quickly.
You're "their" hero, not "a" hero. Which brings me to the dilemma I've run into. How do you assault the heart of someone who doesn't have one? How can you lie to someone who believes nothing?
You've put a bounty on yourself, which seems a little redundant, given money and fame are all most people seeking your demise are after anyway. I assume it's part of some master plan, which would scare me, if I was one of those people. I say I want to be world champion, and in becoming nullify the title and the WFW itself. I'm letting people believe I'm their villain. I'll
let you think, or even believe whatever you want. That I'm the most recent in a series of misguided upstarts, for all the wrong reasons seeking and ultimately failing to replace you. That your legacy, your lifes contribution, is anything besides a T-shirt. That even if I win the world title, shucks, even if LOVE succeeds where everyone else has failed and does away with you for good, you can pass out at night thinking Felix Red, Anarky, Rat Fink, Minion, whoever else, owe you something.
Really I don't think I'm that much like you. I'm not that different from everyone else. I'm just looking for a reason to not exist.
Which I'm not going to get, until it isn't what I want. For the time being, I suppose the world title will have to do... When I sit in the chair, it feels good to rest. But does that make it good? Could I make it evil by breaking it on someone's face?
No. It's just a chair. And I'm just Mike Manson. I don't care whether people like me or hate me. I care very much if they worship me because then I don't have to pay taxes in this country. I simply am, whether you would call that a force of nature or simply a person.
You have a point about me teaming with Iris, but I didn't make this match. People like to pair me with guys like this, they think it's funny. And while I'd like to crucify Iris and make a necklace of his teeth and sens the pictures to his extended family, I can't do that at the same time while winning a tag team cage match.
I may be the world's finest wrestler and one hell of a multi-tasker, but even I can't do everything. So, for now, he's necessary.
What LOVE did, does, tries to do, isn't. But as you said, you were going to do it anyway. I'm the champion, you, or at least of your compatriots wants to be champion, so you're going to come after me. It's the same thing I would have done, except I'd have midgets and flayed skin as part of my process.
In many ways, I can emphasize with you, Felix. I have a lot of the same questions, but you're not going to solve any of them here, not in the WFW. What's real for now is when you'r thrown off the top of a cage, whats real is when you're driven in te back of the head with a chair, or beaten down to the mat. They're real because they matter.
That's why the title matters, and really, I'd prefer not to sully it with the likes of Wells, Psycho, and even Anarky.
It's good that you stand up for him, but I doubt you'd hand him your title shot no matter what. And you get that title shot at the ppv, it should be written in stone.
Against the champion. Allow me to specify. My bounty is for someone, anyone, in or out the WFW to take me down to my satisfaction and they will be rewarded with a title shot at me while I'm wounded or the title itself (all this is decided with President Edmunds's discretion).
The question is, Felix, do you want to be the WFW World Heavyweight Champion or do you want to beat me to be the WFW World Heavyweight Champion?
You say you think you want some of the same things, well, you can show that. Anarky wants to be champion, but he wants more to have a jar of my liquid remains and also be champion. Psycho wants to cut himself, and Jared Wells lives in a beer commercial.
You have to decide, Felix. What will you do? Help me? Protect me? Or just stay the course. We still have this match, you know. A cage match. Supposed to be brutal.
You want to think you have me right where you want me, but so many others have thought that before. I always come back, Felix. You see the bounty before you (if you are so impatient) and everyone else. You wonder if I'm planning something.
