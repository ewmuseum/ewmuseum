I had my own idea for stipulations for this match, but they just never came to fruition. You see, because for every plan A, there's a plan B. You know what else I'm pretty good at doing besides outsmarting people? I'm a DAMN GOOD WRESTLER! For my doubters, I suggest you order the pay per view.. because you're going to see, THIS GUY, give all of the fans the MATCH OF THEIR LIVES! It's what I do, and I do it well.
SIXTY MINUTES! I've got the stamina, and I've got the endurance.. I'm in the best shape of my life. And I know, that I will beat you, Edmunds.
Like I've said before, I didn't win this championship by the end of 2003, just to lose it in early 2004. Edmunds, if you think that you have a shot at beating me... and getting more falls than me in the ironman match, then you've got another thing coming.
I'm walking in with the title, and I'm walking out with it... and that's all there is to it. Prove me wrong, Edmunds.. I KNOW that you can't.
