Almost Live: 2nd Round: Doc Silver vs. Rat Fink

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 27 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Mar-07-03 AT 00:12&nbsp;AM (EST)]I will begin writing the show on March 10th. If you want to send in an angle, send them to [detail withheld]
Cut your promos here... DocKGB — 4 March 2003 (FADEIN: Doc Silver sitting on a couch. He is wearing a pink and black "Garbage" T-Shirt and his normal sweats.)
DOC: For a company that's talked a lot about putting the Wrestling back into this industry...Well WFW seems to be a bit off...Seeing as how our supposed elite second round features a man who really wishes to let it be known that he likes men and women, is facing a bean counter...And I...The only person worth anything in this company...Am facing a man named...Rat...Fink...Yes folks...Rat Fink...I have no idea what's going on in this company, except for the fact that it would appear that I am some sort of Anti-Batman and I'm fighting every crummy named stock archvillain they can throw at me...If only Eli Flair was here, then Poison Ivy could be in his corner in the finals...
But tragically that won't happen, and tragically I'm forced to waste my time battling you Rat Fink...Forced to defeat a man so worthless he won his first round contest only by having his opponent miss a flight...Oh how I have fallen...<Shakes head> But Rat Fink...I'm not the Pittsburg Steelers, which was about your only hope, you see, you needed me to overlook you, to just put my name into the finals and go from there...No I am going to make DAMN sure I take care of you...And move on to the finals...And I will do so...Because I OWN WFW...And because you have no choice but to walk the endless path of misery...Only Happy When It Rains...<FADEOUT>
ChrisHorowitz — 9 March 2003 (Cue up "Dogs" by Pink Floyd) (Fade into another black and white scene, where Rat Fink is in his lab, tinkering away at some sort of large contraption, he turns to the camera and talks and stuff.)
Rat Fink: Welcome to my humble hideout deep in the Cliffs of the West Coast. After a long day of explaining to the youth what they can realistically can achieve in their lives, I come here to tinker with my passions, a legion of robot soldiers to use in the enslavement of humanity.
Rat Fink: Now my dear Doctor Silver, here we are, and here you are doubting me. You, the self proclaimed owner of the WFW is doubting the ability of another. You indeed must be the anti Batman Silver, a crotchetly old man who's money and position of power makes up for a lack of ability. Really Silver, if it wasn't for your hype would you still be able to achieve anything in the world today?
No matter, if I am to lead my robot legions to victory over the governments of the world, this will be my first test. To defeat a self proclaimed God, that could be the proof I need to draw my followers into line. Once the men of earth see I can kill God, they will see that I am their rightful messiah, and instead of simply a legion of robots, I will have a legion of human soldiers... AND A LEGION OF ROBOTS!
Imagine in Silver, you will be the stepping stone to a new world admininstration. Perhaps I will give you some token position, something to show the people I remember those people who helped me out in the beginning.
It's important as dictator of the world to show that you still remember the little guys, it makes the people feel like you aren't so out of touch.
But Silver, it is probably a bad idea to discount a man who's legion of robots will one day march on the capitals of the world. My talents in the ring as up to the level as my technological talents, and those are top notch, seeing as I am working on a legion of battle robots.
But yet you see me as some sort of a chump,s omeone who isn't to be taken seriously. Doc Silver, you're arrogance will become your undoing, for you will not just have to deal with me in that ring, you will have to face the spirit of each one of my followers, and the smiling faces of each child I've helped. Those children when hand in hand, united in song will sing of the deeds I have done, how I've helped each of them prepare for a lifetime of back breaking labor and helped free them of their illusions of a better life. That is a power you cannot fight, because that power is what gives me strength, knowing that I've done good on this planet, and that I must keep fighting so that I can do so much more as its ruler.
Because Silver, I do it all for the children. (Fade out)
