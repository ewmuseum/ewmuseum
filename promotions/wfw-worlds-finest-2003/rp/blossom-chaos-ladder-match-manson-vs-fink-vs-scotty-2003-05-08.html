Showtime24 — 9 May 2003 (FADEIN to Scotty Michaels, jogging down what appears to be Pennsylvania Avenue, Washington, DC. The morning weather is overcast, and it just feels like it's going to rain. Michaels is wearing a pair of Nike basketball trunks, and is sporting a cut-off t-shirt that says "SUPERSTAR" really big on the front. Michaels has a look of intensity on his face, which is covered with a light stubble. Michaels, slightly winded, stops and begins to speak with the White House several hundred yards away in the background...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Not many people thought that I'd actually do it. Not too many people at all. I got tons of e-mails and tons of feedback at www.scottymichaels.com and it all basically told me that while they appreciated the GREATNESS that I bring to this sport, that they just didn't think that I had it. Rat Fink, Manson, Diablo... they're all on the top of their game right now. They've been doing this for years straight, whereas I was not. GATES OF HELL, I proved them all wrong. I stepped into that ring with 3 of the meanest, SICKEST SONS OF B*TCHES that are in this sport today, and I did exactly what it was I said I would do. I EARNED my shot at the WFW North American Championship at the next pay per view.
I asked Manson, that when you opened the GATES OF HELL, who was it going to be that would answer that door. Well I guess that would be me. I proved that to each and every person here in the WFW that I am one of the World's Finest Wrestlers, and that I deserve a chance at the North American championship. And you know what? What a better way to win that belt... at the WFW's very first pay per view. Yes, the weird names for cards continue, but my winning streak will not. When I strap that championship around my waist, in the capital of our nation, I'm not only going to be doing it for America. I'm going to represent all of them CANUCKS up there in Canada, speaking that French that they do. I'm going to do it for all the migrant workers down there in Mexico... and hell, if I do win that championship... let's run to the border, Taco Bell on me.
Ya' see, Manson, Rat Fink... that North American title belt is bigger than what either of you even recognize. It's not about strapping that gold around your waist, it's not all about the Benjamins... it's all about RESPECT, not only for this great country... this great CONTINENT, but for each and every one of those people out there. I don't come out here acting like some crazed lunatic, and call myself Manson. Know why? I don't need to. I had a normal childhood, growing up in the suburbs of Philly. I didn't sacrifice squirrels as a child, or eat worms to fulfill my protein requirement for the day. I started out as a professional wrestler because I wanted it... I could feel it. Hurting people isn't my thing. People standing up, and saying now that's one crazy bastard.. I don't care about any of that. But I'll tell you what I do care about... giving the fans everything they deserve for their dollar, BOTTOM LINE! Manson, while you waste your time doing some of the SICKET SH*T that any of us have ever seen, you're doing nothing but poisoning your career... SHORTENING, for the HOT SHOT POPS in arenas around the country. I, on the other hand, am just a tad bit smarter than that. Almost like a wolf, hunting his prey. Everything I've done since I've returned to wrestling, it's all been calculated. Manson, you're just another pawn in this game.
Rat Fink, I've got no respect for you, man. While you're running away from Manson... you make me do all of the work in the match. At the Gates of Hell, you proved that you might be the world's greatest villain, whatever the hell that means. You come out and ramble on and on like you're some kind of evil genius. Well I'm not afraid, as a matter of fact... I don't even care. And these little games that you play, with cardboard robots... who does that? Don't you live a normal life outside of this sport? Well I guess when you spend all that times planning these STUPID things to say, that the opportunities of getting laid by a woman don't present themselves too often. I may be calculating, but I'm definitely not going to spend every second of my day, envisioning some of the stupidity that you and Manson gets yourselves involved in.
You know, it's kind of fitting that this card is taking place in Washington, DC... and it's kind of fitting that this match is a ladder match. Since I stepped foot into the WFW... since they hired me on as their VIEWER'S CHOICE, I've slowly ascended the ladder of success. I've worked my way up, and at Cherry Blossom Chaos.. I meet my goal. I win the North American title, climbing that ladder STRAIGHT... TO THE TOP! Manson, Rat Fink... don't underestimate me, because you've both done it before. You both got off lucky last time, because there was no title belt directly on the line. This time, it's the North American title. Either of ya' screw up again, and I walk out of there the champ.... you two, walk out of the wrestling ring with the same status you both have in life... LOSERS!
(FADEOUT, as Scotty begins to jog again... as the "Rocky" theme song picks up in the background.)

MANSON: President Lincoln, I realize coming to you might seem odd since I’m a communist and all, well I suppose I could have gone to the Jefferson Monument, but anyway…see…I’m the WFW North American champion…I don’t actually have the title belt, not that I care, but it’s the principle of the thing.

To finally come full circle and prove my ascension and all that poetic crap. But this Rat Fink’s persistence has proven a thorn in my side. I set his robot on fire, but still, there’s this gaping hole inside me saying I could do more than try to scar and burn his friends.
Then there’s Scotty Michaels who’s really been injected into this for no reason, but he wants to be North American champion, so good for him. But he keeps going on and on about some comeback and such, how he’s really good and everyone likes him and wants them to get their daughters pregnant so he’ll marry then divorce them 5 years later….and he thinks that there’s proof of this in pinning a tag partner I wanted as much as an adopted child. I mean, not even I can beat 2 men at once and having a spastic retard as a partner only made the odds worse..
There’s also the announcers saying my matches aren’t as close..aren’t as decisive as they should be…there’s Rat Fink saying I’m just not quite diabolical enough…there’s Michaels saying I’m a loser, well that or confusing me with a D&D roleplayer. There are still people who don’t realize Manson is an actual last name and believe I take it off a rock star or serial killer/cult leader. Apparently, I can’t appease everyone….well not that I care about any of them..but the inner Michael…the child inside is saying….”Do more….get the cross….crucify…burn the arena down.”
Now I’ve tried running down the ring, fire….what else is there for me? I realize, President Lincoln, that you aren’t the best authority on the matters of torture and torment. However, since I don’t really feel like finding Bob Dole, you’ll have to do. I have read that you were a wrestler yourself….surely you found yourself swinging wooden chairs and climbing ladders….combine that with how you won the Civil War..Mister Lincoln…is there a way I can efficiently harm these two men to satisfy my inner child?
((Manson waits. Seconds pass. His foot taps. He takes out a pez dispenser and eats a few pieces. Finally, he kicks the statue.))
I know what this is about. Hey, I didn’t know that was your old office..but they don’t tell you where the bathroom is on the White House tour…

Now, in my day, you have much more leverage. As an American, you are entitled to hurt another as much as you please. It’s a hidden clause in the constitution. It is not just a hobby, not just a job, it’s your right as an American. Think of all the brave men and women who gave their lives so that one day, you could light a man in a cardboard box on fire. It’s what separates us from the rest of the world..why so many people come to be Americans. From my place in presidential heaven, I used to watch you implement the right of free speech…the right to bear arms….illegal search and seizure….and the right to torture those in your path…((starts tearing up))…you Michael Manson..are one of the proudest Americans I have ever seen..it is your duty to go on to ppv….and electricute…burn..scar….lash….scratch….hang….and stab your opponents….not because of ant grudge….but because you sir, are an American.

I suppose I have to use this energy for something after all, vendetta or just a match. Because that’s all you are Michaels. Because I have decided that the NA title is mine..and that makes it important enough. And while it’s not the title I wanted, it’s not the match I wanted, I can make it so, or come close. To do this of course, I will wreak bloody, violet vengeance on Rat Fink..just because I’m me..but you Michaels..just because you’re there..get the same thing. By default, by luck, whatever you call it.

If I had to dig up every grave here, if I had to channel every dead soul here to learn their secrets to blackmail the world..I would. This is not underestimation though. No , because you and Fink are just first. You won’t be the last, that’s the difference. Because at the ppv, before that ladder you and Fink might as well be Doc Silver.
