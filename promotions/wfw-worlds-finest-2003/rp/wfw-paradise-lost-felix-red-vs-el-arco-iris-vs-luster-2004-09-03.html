NotorisSTD — 4 September 2004 (3 a.m., hotel room somewhere in the outskirts of Washington D.C. Felix Red sits slouching on the floor in black sweat pants. There's broken glass scattered about, and rain blowing in through a broken window soaking a badly damaged young man...)
"the boy who destroyed the world" I've received tonight, exactly 62 pats on the back, 30 something people telling me I had a good match, a few people told me I was robbed, and one or two were mumbling something about backstage politics. Not too loudly though.
There's really no point in my explaining how much I hate everything that breathes, is there?
There's no point in me detailing to you people, you loathsome hideous cattle, just how hard I hit the groupies recently departed from this very hotel room, how hard I laughed while they cried, or how hollow and alone I felt once they left...Then I threw the TV out the window. I hope it landed on a pregnant chick...
You keep telling me you don't understand, but it's more like you're not listening. Foul smelling, monstrous sh*t ticks, pissing on each others lives and jerking off to Hentai in blissful idiot euphoria, but never...ever...listening.
Manson heard me, I know, because it broke his face to do so. If he understood, it didn't matter, because he didn't care, because I only REIGN SUPREME if you're not a clothesline, or a roll of toilet paper, or a sex muppet valet, or Pulsar...
Do I sound bitter? Not such a very splendid show of sportsmanship, perhaps? I rose, from the dregs of bad comedy, warping worse pop culture, It took years, and I've run the tips of my fingers across eternity. But that's all I got. That single second. Or maybe three or four. Or five. I can’t articulate to you how much I didn’t want it stop. And now that I know how beautiful it can be, and how much I simply couldn't stop myself from falling short of....
Now, after all I've learned and experienced and felt and torn to shreds, split blood in it's face and smiled warmly upon. After everything...I have to fight sh*t f*cking El Arco Iris and Luster. Again. There is no time. The past is the present is the future. I've said it again and again but this isn't what I meant...
I get that this is supposed to be some sort of cathartic epiphany, where I'm sent back to where I started, I remember the slightly more innocent person I used to be and realize the error of my wicked ways. But I feel no remorse. And no regret. Because there was never a real choice. I never had a chance. If I did, I'd be a champion now. The upside of this, if you can call it that, is if I never had a chance, then Iris and Luster reeeally never had a chance...
I'm the perpetual runner up. Anarky's LOVE's dead-beat dad, Wells is the bastard son, Psycho's is the spoiled brat youngest born, and I'm the neglected middle child. I could come home with the good grades, try my hardest at as many extra curricular activities as I could fit into my schedule, dedicate myself to being popular and as nice to everyone as I can. Or I could sit in the basement shooting crystal meth and burning myself with cigarettes, but it's never good or bad enough to warrant your attention or approval.
At least I have that. Luster's never been anything but a punching bag with a cock attached. He just beat Shawn Hart, but only because I wanted him too, and even then, only because I didn't think Shawn Hart could be trusted to adequately embarrass himself. I was wrong about that too. Enter the twisted legend of a strapping young man; showing athletic promise, he takes a pseudonym combining the namesakes of his heroes, then watches Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas 50 times, then eats an ounce of mushrooms in a single sitting. And Richard Gideon, by god, who would have ever thought HE'D rise to stand against us?
After all this time, I'm still awaiting the wrath of the victim. The pot of napalm at the end of the rainbow. But I didn't receive my just deserts last time I fought El Arco Iris. Or the time before that. Or the time before that. Or the time before that. Or the time before that.
Iris is many things. A man of integrity and principal, A fine athlete, The lone voice of innocence and compassion in a world of cruelty and selfishness; Peter Pan, doomed to forever frolic about neverland, Out of any of us, the only true nihilist.
What he isn't is better than me. Regardless, I stand before you a boy no longer. Rather a bitter, ugly, hateful man, thrown to the mercy of those most eager to pass judgement on him, my miserable victims. I trounced Luster. I put a knife in Iris's back, and I have twisted it everytime I've had the chance since then.
Not only am I not sorry, I'm rather looking foreword to doing it all again. So alright then. You sad, weak, simpering worms. This is your big chance for retribution. Maybe your last chance. You're The Governattor, the Crow, Uma Thurman, Charles Bronson, and John Wayne rolled into one, and I'm the bad guy. You've shot all my lackeys, both my legs are broken, you're standing over me with the gun in your hand. Nearly beaming with righteousness, tingling with anticipation, for ultimate precious vindication will soon be yours...
You need only pull the trigger. And that, you nauseating sacks of pig sh*t, is where everything will go horribly wrong.
So g'head then. No silver lining to my cloud. No light at the end of my tunnel. No cherry on top of my sundae. I'm beat. I'm done. All I need now is for one of you to finish me once and for all.
I'm dying to watch you try. Diablo — 8 September 2004 (FADEIN: El Arco Iris in the FMLL gymnasium in Jalisco, or “Rainbow Gym” as it’s commonly referred to, dressed in a rainbow tie-dye sweat suit, going through chain wrestling drills with the trainees. After a couple of minutes, El Arco Iris claps his hands and sits himself on the second turnbuckle in one of the corners of the ring, and the students gather around him.)
El Arco Iris: All right, bring it in, folks…Excellent work, guys, you really busted it all out hard today. Any questions over what we’ve gone over?
(One young trainee raises his hand.) Student: Yeah, I have a question, but it’s not really about today. Iris: Go ahead, ask anyway. Student: Yeah, why did you come to practice today after that big loss in the title match you had this week in America? Don’t you feel the least bit upset?
Iris: (lets out a small laugh) That is a good question, a good question indeed. It does seem that after all that work to get to a title match, Sisyphus’ stone has rolled all the way back to the bottom of the hill, doesn’t it? Well, you remember your Greek mythology don’t you?
Student: Yeah… Iris: Do you remember what did Sisyphus do after the stone rolled down the hill? Student: He pushed it back up, but then it fell down again, it would still keep falling down no matter how many times he pushed it up.
Iris: Exactly! Student: Exactly? Iris: Exactly. Student: …Exactly…what? Iris: Sisyphus kept pushing it back up that hill no matter how many times it fell down, and that was his eternal punishment. But he conquers this punishment in a very profound way.
Student: Are you getting at something with this rock stuff? Iris: Just be patient, El Arco Iris always gets at something when El Arco Iris talks. So Sisyphus conquers his punishment. How exactly? He realizes that pushing the stone is what he is going to do for eternity, so he should just go ahead and enjoy the fact that he is pushing the stone. One could say all of our situations are a little bit similar, only you, and El Arco Iris, and all of us here have chosen our lot in life. We are wrestlers. Title belts and championships are sure nice, but we choose to be wrestlers because we want to wrestle. El Arco Iris didn’t win, for sure, but El Arco Iris left it all out there for everyone to see, and whipped out every move and tried El Arco Iris’ best, not just for the sake of reward, but El Arco Iris has a passion for this sport. That is why El Arco Iris came to practice right when El Arco Iris was supposed to, because El Arco Iris loves pushing that stone, and today helping you all push your own individual stones. You should all enjoy your training while it is happening, live for each day on this mat and take from it all you can. How many of you can honestly say you worked your hardest today in practice? Raise your hands.
(All of the trainees raise their hands) Iris: Exactly what El Arco Iris wanted to see. One’s eyes should not solely be focused on the light at the end of the tunnel, or you’ll stumble and trip over a rock in the path right in front of you. As you’re training, not only are you preparing for your careers, but you’re getting yourself in shape, you’re learning, you’re forging friendships and self-esteem. No title belt can be more important that what goes on here. El Arco Iris him self is still learning, still developing, and still live for every moment between these ropes. Granted El Arco Iris will see some of the same tired old faces El Arco Iris has seen time and time again, but while El Arco Iris is going full throttle every second of every day, it is all worthwhile. You know, I’m really glad you asked that question. If you all forget every single technique El Arco Iris teaches you in this gym, every kind of maneuver and every drill, the very least you can do is remember what El Arco Iris has just told you. Follow this example and you will become not only truly excellent wrestlers, but truly excellent people. Perhaps, just perhaps, this will help you lead your life with a smile on your face. Everyone understand?

NotorisSTD — 11 September 2004 (CUEUP: “Dead and Gone” by the Marvels…) (Felix Red in black dickies, boots, a “Mindless Self Indulgence” T-shirt, and a denim jacket with half inch spikes on the shoulders is hanging out with the bums on Newberry St, in front of a fashionably bohemian book store about a half a block down from the Virgin Death Star…)
“the boy who destroyed the world” Lets face it…Boston is just New York City’s little p*ssy baby brother…the bars all close at two, the liqueur store closes early on Sundays, there’s no where to get a good cappuccino at four o’clock in the morning. The Red Sox are perpetually the Yankees sex doll, and the Patriots don’t even really play here. The music scene is worthless, unless you haven’t grown out of the phase where you still think The Unseen and Toxic Narcotic are really cool. The cops start shooting people in the back when they’re not getting the money they want. Meninio is a Pig. The Herald would’ve fit right in if it was published in the USSR 25 years ago. The Globe is a very poor man’s New York Times. And I can’t f*cking stand college kids.
This is town for losers and yuppies. Which might explain why there’s a very prevalent WFW fan base here.
I’m experiencing something of a spiritual crisis. Reality continues to warp, but my control has become shaky. Rather Unreliable. I’ve found myself talking quite a bit about redemption and validation and things I’m supposed to want. But what if these things are, and always have been illusions, or simply eternally out of my reach? Even if they’re not, why did I care to begin with? There’s a difference between finding a particular subject intriguing, and actually letting yourself learn anything from it.
It’s fitting. I keep going and going, only to return, because for all I’ve experienced, I have learned nothing.
I’m supposed to want a lot of things. All I actually want is misanthropy, and the means to create more. The means and the end are one. There is no process, only an all-encompassing state of excess, madness, and violence.
I’ll admit I didn’t take my defeat in Washington D.C. gracefully. Grace simply was not an option that seemed worth exploring at the time. I don’t appreciate being called a little b*tch by someone who owes me a “Thank you.” While Luster’s benefiting through my action was completely incidental, I could have, lets say, told the Bartlebys to cut Luster’s hands off. But I didn’t. Actually, in retrospect, I’m glad. Because now I get to cave his face in myself.
And Iris…My Iris…How badly do we have to torture you, how miserable must you become before any of what I’ve been saying gets through to you?…
Look at yourself. You did a shooting star press off the Lincoln monument, and landed, ribcage first on the cement below. Only to lose, to one of us, again. Yet you try to convince me you’re in this for the sheer joy of the means, vs. whatever you expect to get from the ends? You’re in this because you feel you represent something important?
You make people happy. So do I. The difference is you put on a ridiculous rainbow mask and talk like a kindergarten teacher before you hurl yourself, still smiling, through a fiberglass wall.
Has it occurred to you that most of your so called “fans” aren’t old enough to dress themselves yet?
Have you realized how many of these students that you so adore, are huffing nitrous and mocking whatever ineffectual ideals that days sermon contained within 20 minutes after the end of class?
They don’t appreciate you, Iris. None of them do. Not your students, your fans, the other wrestlers, the bookers, anyone. They aren’t listening. They never listen. Their love will not redeem or validate you. It will eventually kill you.
If you want to save this world, you must first save yourself. I’d recommend reaching through the mirror. While roaming the netherworld, maybe you’ll find something useful. You see, Madness is enlightenment. Chaos is Beauty. LOVE is Hate. My faith and yours is in lies…
Perhaps someday you’ll save yourself. But these things take time. Sadly you don’t have enough to find salvation from me.
It’s all happening again, and really, what for? Like a child on a playground who’s head is blown to pieces by a stray bullet. Like a frat boy run down by a panicking drunk driver amidst a super bowl riot.
Calamity and Woe, my hapless, tragic, happenstance victims. RANDOM BUM: Hey Felix. You want us to dress up in black masks and jump somebody again? FELIX: Hush.
