WFW — Roleplay

BOMB Voyage: Manson (c) vs. Gideon

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 May 2004 This match is for the WFW World Heavyweight Title. The RP/angle deadline is Tuesday, June 1st 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld].
Manson — 24 May 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in long-sleeved "The Man More Exciting Than Jesus" shirt with the WFW World Heavyweight Title next to him sits on a green leather couch in a thinking man's postion as he looks forward at a TV screen blaring brightly, though it faces him.))
MANSON: I've been watching this over and over.....again and again.....listening to the commentary....going backstage to find out the framework....heard the perspective of all the players...the fans have voiced their opinions...
((MANSON shakes his head.)) I think here is where it all went wrong...so much potential...a brilliant cast....and so much source material to draw on...
((MANSON picks up the cover to the Smallville Season 2 DVD and flicks it back to the side.))
It was when they started focusing in more on the uninteresting teenaged angst relationships and though they could make Superman hip and edgy that they went down the wrong path, completely disrespecting the character. While John Glover and Michael Rosenbaum are tremendous actors, the writing for the Luthors became more and more over the top when it just started as a simple competitive, albeit, abusive, familial chess game between them. As they delved more and more into the Superman mythos, it seemed they never knew where they were headed and tried to draw the Luthors in and made them more maniacal than they should have been at the time....but of course whenever they would go ahead and make a change, they'd retreat so soon back to the status quo. And why so much time spent on Lana Lang? Both the actress and character are insipid. By the way, what the hell happened to Pete?
They cancelled Farscape and keep this formula-driven tripe on the air? What about the duality of Crichton and Scorpius? The stranger in a strange univere concept of the human as the alien? The layered relationships and character devlopment? The bizarre alien beings and cultures?
What has happened to television? ((MANSON grabs a remote control and shuts off the TV.)) I suppose there is always World's Finest Wrestling, if only because it has my influence and specter falling upon it week after week. Of course, according to everyone else, including President Sean Edmunds and the members of LOVE, I don't actually have any control over this promotion and that actually they do (not together, though, separately.)
Which would all be true if I weren't the World Heavyweight Champion and the World's Finest Wrestler. The last man to wield power on a grand scale was Alex Wylde serving as both president and champion...and it was me who brought him to his knees.
Myself at my worst and weakest, recovering and ascending, striking where I needed, doing what I had to.
All this makes me ...a man of influence. I don't choose between contenders and never really have had much interest in that, but that it is unwise to assume this stripes me of abilities to change things.
Case in point, history, or rather, the repetition of it. A year ago, Richard Gideon pinned Doc Silver to signal his presence in the WFW and eventually had a North American title run that extended longer than anyone else's, including mine.
One could assume that a pattern would form and history would repeat, coincidence can be odd like that. Gideon is now title-less and likely looking for that next step.
I normally abhor rewarding failure, since Gideon didn't actually win a title shot, but just didn't lose, but like I said, I don't control these things, nor do I want to.
I can control what happens in that ring, on that ship. I can grind people's bones to dust and I can shatter spine cords. I an also wrestle a match.
If the past year has taught me anything, it's a few things. First, history is nice, but it's basically there for you to learn from. To adapt. And all I need in the end is myself to do that, my own will is better than 4-5 lackies having to interpret my wishes. But people always have to learn the hard way.
Such as Gideon....who has to learn that you have to draw that line of achievement from year to year....span time....survive....and then..maybe...one day....you will be a man of influence.
But this isn't soon for you, Gideon. This isn't the North American title or the BAD World Title. This is the WFW World title, the position of the World's Finest Wrestling. And through guile, deceit, violence, and massive doses of pez, that's me. I intend to continue this.
You might have called yourself "the System", but the strap of gold next to me tells me that I'm the world.
Methos17 — 25 May 2004 (The camera fades in to Richard Gideon sitting in a chair overlooking the beautiful pool located in the front of the cruise ship. However, due to it currently being the early evening, all you can see is a faint glimmer of the pool as it reflects off of the nearby lights and the moonlight above. Wearing shorts and a hawaiin print t-shirt, Gideon stares into the water. Noticing the camera crew he sits up a little more and begins to speak)
Richard Gideon: Look at this. The entire WFW roster is getting a paid vacation, and then we wrestle in Hawaii? This is what we call, 'overkill'. But as long as the bill isn't paid out of my gate receipts it's all good with The System. I'm out here tonight to talk about something Manson said in his interview...how cool Farscape was. I mean come on, who wouldn't want to dump everything and try to save the universe weekly with a crew of people you don't trust? Luxons and Humans and Scarrans oh my!
(Gideon smiles and pauses for a second) Richard Gideon: But I digress. Great show aside, I want to give a talk-see about the one thing that binds us both with this match Manson....history. History is something that I find very important for this match. You see, you're right Manson...what happened last year this time won't happen again. I won't squander my shot at the WFW Title. I will not settle for standing toe to toe alone like I did with Doc Silver. This time around that belt gets deposited with Richard Gideon.
I'm very much aware of the tenous nature of this business and being on top in general now. Manson, you're the top of the heap, and I respect that. IN fact, you're one of the few legends left who I have no animosity toward. Until now. You see, that title changes people. I understand that too now. For three hundred and sixty four days out of the year I think you're a dangerous and highly entertaining guy....albeit cooky a little bit. Come Bomb Voyage, you are in the way of me finally winning that belt. And that makes me a little more dangerous, perhaps a little more cooky. I have to get the monkey off my back, and you sir are my ticket to freedom. But one thing I will not be is cocky. You see cockiness got me only so far, skill will take me even farther.
(Gideon calls a waiter over and asks for another coke, then continues) Richard Gideon: Lemme put it to you in Farscape terms, since we agree on that. That belt is my wormhole...and I've been clawing, I've almost killed to try and figure it out. And now, finally I think I have it. And much like Crichton would, I'm counting down the seconds until that match. And when it gets to zero I'll know if I was right, or if it's back to the drawing board. But this time, I know I am right...I feel it. Finally everything is lining up...No more Psycho haranguing me, no side alliances forced with Shawn Hart. Just you, me and that belt. And that ain't bad at all.
(Fade out) Methos17 — 27 May 2004 (Fade in to the high-energy atmosphere of the Bomb Voyage Cruise dance club. As the wrestlers and fans mingle and enjoy their time off prior to the show. The music stops and at the nearby stage lights up to reveal the DJ, beaming as he steps to the mic)
DJ: And now folks, it's time for some karao-kay!! Steppin' up to the plate is WFW's own Richard Gideon!
(Richard Gideon steps out of the crowd, in a pair of slacks and a floral shirt) Richard Gideon: Look, I said I'd do this because I was stupid enough to stay in the room when they asked for volunteers...let's get this over with. What's the song?
DJ: Well cats and kittens, Richard Gideon will be singing 'Sweet Caroline'! Richard Gideon: Sweet Caroline...I like that one...let's do it. (The music fires up, and Gideon starts to sing along with the music. After the first stanza, he gets to the chorus..)
Richard Gideon: Swe-eeeet Car-oooline..... Guy in the crowd: This song sucks... Richard Gideon: Good times never seem to go.. Guy in crowd: So what, who cares...(interrupts) Gideon: Alright. That's it! (Richard Gideon flies off the stage onto the guy, and has him removed by security. After this is done, the camera follows him back to the stage.)
Richard Gideon: Well folks, I was all ready to finish belting out my song, when that guy ups and interrupts my croonin' by being a tool. So, let's talk for a few minutes.
DJ: But this is the karaoke hour... Gideon: Not anymore. You want me to finish the song, I want to talk about my title match. Fair enough?
DJ: But - Gideon: But nothing. I'm a grown man and I have a title shot. Deal with it. Manson, I know you're out there...if you're not out here on the floor you're somewhere on this ship. When we finish the fun and we hit port in Hawaii we bring the proceedings to a close. I've been chasing that belt for a year now, and when the Bomb drops, I walk away WFW Champion. Finally. And I will bring the stability to yet another title you could not. And you know why? Because Manson, I make history, I don't repeat it. You are a great wrestler so don't misunderstand what I have to say next. I want this title for not only myself, but because I trust myself against the LOVE Train. Felix Red thinks he can put one over on El Arco...he tries that crap with me, I'll let the bunny die and make him pay with his own blood. I'm tired of wanna be thugs trying to run this league, and as champion I'm going to lead by example. So, at the end of the day, that is why I'm going to win. Deal with it Manson...just deal with it....
(Turning to the DJ) Now where was I....ah yeah....SWEEEE-EET CA-Roooo-line...bum bum bum!!!! (Fade Out) Manson — 27 May 2004 ((FADEIN: The engine room of the ship where WFW World Heavyweight Champion MICHAEL MANSON in a Punisher t-shirt and black jeans hangs from a pipe and does pull-ups. The WFW World Title Belt is slung on a nearby pipe. MANSON drops to his feet and leans against a boiler.))
MANSON: All I hear about lately is change. LOVE controls the WFW...and not the world champion....because Anarky screams to everyone that listens that he was never my lackey and never ran around airports on all fours like a dog through an airport for my amusement....Sean Edmunds has changed anything that resembled a campaign promise now that he's in office...remember how he said I could run amuck? And now Gideon says the world title changes you as a person, competitor, and so much more.
Moreover, he's the man to bring stability to a title as he claims he did to the North American title. He also implied that I hadn't, but I don't really see a months-long reign as erractic, but that's the only title he would actually know anything about, so I'll defer to him in this instance.
Of course, he doesn't know a thing about being a world champion, let alone the WFW World Heavyweight Champion. And he won't anytime soon.
Richard, I have adapted to countless bookers and promotions in my time. The thing that is ever the same is change. This has enabled me to have won more world titles than the number of good Chappelle shows on Comedy Central. Yet, all the same, whether at the bottom, whether with all my limbs broken, whether I'm a walking corpse on fire, and whether I'm drunk on pez, I'm still Michael Manson, the Man More Exciting Than Jesus, the Wrestling Industry's Most Indomitable Will, the World's Finest Wrestler, and the Hannibal Lector of North American Wrestling.
The only thing that has really changed recently is the fact that now they say "WFW World Heavyweight Champion" before my name.
And I'm the one that made that so. I have always been the eye of the storm, he who controls the changes and fluxations of this industry. I haven't decided that I want to lose the title...and I probably never will...so we'll see how that goes. But, rest assured, I have it within me not to lose it now. Not to you.
Not because I refuse to lose to a man who has the gall to stand next to a DJ and partake in karoake or because your nickname "The System" rips off the Matrix, yet you refuse to wear a trenchcoat. No, its because everyone needs me as champion....Mike Manson has to be the opiate of the masses.
President Sean Edmunds needs a champion he can't control to keep him busy in office...always planning and thinking....it's the only way he'll learn to be a better president....and if his desk erupts in flame...he'll also learn about fire safety.
LOVE needs someone to oppose, someone who can stand in for the WFW itself, someone unequivocally their superior because they've all wallowed in his shadow for so long. Especially Anarky. How far would any of them have come if they didn't have me to compare themselves to?
And you, Richard, you need me as WFW World Heavyweight Champion. To show you what stability should be like....what a world champion should be like....what a man of influence is like...to show you what you'll never have because I still draw breath and even if I didn't..I'd still have to deny you.
This isn't a new start for you, Richard, it's my establishment. It's a sacrifice to me.....because every atom and every limb...is a part of me.....or so it goes something like that. I am now World's Finest Wrestling....and it'll change to suit me. You're just the first to learn about it.
Methos17 — 31 May 2004 (Fadein to the beautiful island of Oahu, Hawaii. As the camera pans and shows the various people enjoying a nice Memorial Day in style, one vacationer appears to be having none of that. That vacationer is Richard Gideon, in shorts and a Sys_Error T-shirt doing win sprints from the edge of the shoreline back to the lifeguard stand. After three, he notices the camera crew and walks toward them)
Richard Gideon: You'd think even on a day like this, a scant few days away from my first WFW Title Shot out of the rookie bubble I'd have a little "Me-time". But alas, not to be. But since you folks are here today, let's chat.
I'm here, enjoying the sun as I can, working my body into a frenzy to prepare for a match that I've visualized several hundred times since I got on that boat. Despite the crap pulled by LOVE, despite the machinations of Edmunds and his crew...even in spite of the vitriol of Manson himself - I'm going into Bomb Voyage as the contender to the WFW Title.
You asked me where my trenchcoat is Manson...well I don't have one. You claim I'm a Matrix knock-off, when I'm just a guy who believes if you keep to the system you've been trained in, victory is assured. I've said it a thousand times to myself, and I'm going to say it to you and the WFW Fans again - Success is when Preparedness meets Opportunity. I spent the last year readying myself for an opportunity to become the WFW Champ, and I am prepared to do what I have to in order to win.
On the ship, I was laid back, enjoying all the cruise had to offer. You see, these experiences are a bit foreign to someone who had never been on a cruise. But the second I hit this beach, all that faded. Because when I was on the cruise, that was a mini-vacation. While I'm in Hawaii, I'm here to work. And work I shall. You think because you are a walking, talking, breathing sociopath the WFW Title can be yours and yours alone? I don't think so. That belt is coming off of you and becoming property of one Richard "The System" Gideon.
I could go into one of those over-used and understated, "because I'm better than you" but that's not true. I'm going to beat you in a few days, not because I'm God's gift to wrestling, but because as a man I'm going to go toe to toe with you and I'm going to want it more, Manson, I'm going to want it more.
Because I've never held a World Title before Manson....you have, in countless leagues and in places I've probably never heard of. But I haven't. I am going to finally get that taste, and when I do, it'll all be thanks to you. So thank you Manson. Thank you very much.
(Fade OUT) Manson — 31 May 2004 ((FADEIN: WFW World Heavyweight Champion MICHAEL MANSON in black "Neo Manson-Spider on Electric Web t-shirt and jeans sits in the captain's chair with the WFW World Heavyweight Title hanging on the arm, and the chair itself has been torn asunder and dragged down to the crisscrossing piped engine room.))
MANSON: You want it more than me? You, Gideon, want the WFW world heavyweight title than me? Because you haven't been in a world title match before? Are you reading this out of the Big Book of Wrestling Promo Cliches?
If you wanted this title more than I do, you would have it. You would have stopped at nothing and crawled over an army of dead bodies to get to it. You would have created your own opportunity, your own title shot.
Like I did. Gideon, you are not going to beat me. LOVE is not going to interfere and help you to win. Anarky, no doubt, has countless domination-slash- revenge fantasies swirling in his mind that all depend on him winning this title from me. I don't think Felix Red cares who he faces for the title, but between the two of us we might learn why the WFW said I brought him into the WFW in the first place. He also might prefer a challenge given he keeps making an ass out of you, and he doesn't even know if an "ass" exists, or if it might have a more secret, hidden function for the body.
And I might prefer facing him, since he actually earned a title shot, but that's irrelevant.
You talk about the taste, the hunger, and all that drivel about youth fighting for chance and success. There's a taste in my mouth as well. Every match, every night, it rises in my throat. It's the taste of my own blood.
That's what I earned from night after night, torment after torment, title after title. And despite the strangely coptic taste, despite how distracting it is to have when eating a sandwich, despite how it makes me wonder after my health, I still go for more titles, even if it makes the taste stronger.
That's why I'll win, Gideon, I've tasted blood, and I will again. And it doesn't matter to me. It doesn't make me a better man. It does make me the champion though.
Methos17 — 1 June 2004 (Fadein to Richard Gideon, in his dressing room. Outside you can hear the rumblings of fans entering the arena, milling about and hoping to see WFW stars in their preparation. Gideon is wearing the bottom half of his wrestling gear, but still has on a regular "Swoosh" Tee. Looking in the mirror he notices the WFW Camera crew and turns around, ready to speak)
Richard Gideon: Sooner or later, I figured you'd end up like the rest of em Manson. Sooner or later I knew you'd devolve to just simple name calling, though I hoped you wouldn't. Like I said when this started, I got no beef with you. But now you wanna put Felix Red ahead of me? That's clownshoes Manson, clownshoes...
You think because I didn't win that match I didn't earn the shot? We'll see in that ring, since obviously all the real talking will have to happen there. Obviously this isn't going to be a match anymore with two men who can share the respect of the title match; now it's just you overlooking me. You'd rather see Felix Red in this match? You'd rather see someone else in this match? Well tough. You got me. If I'm such a pushover, roll over me and get ready for the next dog on its way.
But you won't be able to do that Manson. You say I read out of the Big Book of Wrestling cliches because I don't wax philosophical like Red or constantly refer to self-inflicted wounds like you? You think because other people may have used the same words at another point in "wrestling history" my words are less valid. Fair enough.
Like I said earlier, we'll see in that ring. Because no matter how much a cliche it is to say, "we'll find out when we get into the ring" it's very very true. That ring will be the judge. I don't need LOVE, though YOU may get their help. I don't need it though. I won't stand here and tell you since I was a little boy I wanted to be the WFW Champion, but I knew I wanted to be the best. Tonight I finally get to prove it. Tonight I walk into a title match you say I don't deserve. So I unlike you have nothing to lose. If I walk into that match and get blown out, I'll know you were right and I was wrong, and as a man have to accept that.
But, if I walk out WFW Champion, I'll be waiting for you...I'll be waiting for you and anyone else who decides to step up for a shot at my title. But first...but first, we have the match. Be seeing you Manson. And bring the belt. I'll be needing it post-match.
(Fade out)
