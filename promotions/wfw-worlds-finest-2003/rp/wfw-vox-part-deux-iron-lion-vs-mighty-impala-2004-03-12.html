WFW Vox Part Deux: Iron Lion vs. Mighty Impala

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 12 March 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Monday, March 22nd 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld].
JABolich — 12 March 2004 (FADEIN: A large television screen, displaying what appears to be a documentary on Africa. Sedately, an impala jogs across the landscape, its head held high. However, its stroll through the savannah comes to an abrupt halt as a huge, tawny lion leaps out from concealment and pounces upon the impala, tearing into it with claws and teeth sharp as razors, rending the life from the slender beast.)
(The footage suddenly pauses, and a huge, golden-maned figure walks in front of it, entering from the right. It's IRON LION, garbed in jeans and a black "PRIDE - STRENGTH - HONOR" T-shirt.)
Iron Lion: Such is the way of nature. The impala is a graceful and elegant creature, but inevitably it becomes prey for the larger, stronger lion.
At Road to Glory, World's Finest Wrestling saw a taste of what I am capable of, as I scored the first and second eliminations in the So Cal Rumble by throwing out Pulsar and Ultimo Diablo without effort. And though I did not win the match, it took six men to eliminate me. Nevertheless, I take pride in my accomplishments, for none expected me to make so much as a dent in the match.
But this begs the question... Where exactly were you, Mighty Impala? Surely a man who thinks so ill of the rest of us common people would jump at the opportunity to prove himself better than all of us. Then again, I can hardly blame you for your cowardice, especially given your foolish pride, your weakness of mind and character, and above all your complete and utter lack of honor.
You, Impala, are IGNORANT. You believe that greatness is determined solely by virtue of having royal blood running through one's veins. Well, let me tell you something. I have no royal blood. My ancestors were peaceful village farmers who fled to America when the Second World War broke out. My family tree is rooted deeply within humble ground. Yet despite this, I am not lessened as a man. In fact, I am made STRONGER by it, for among such families as mine the virtues of HARD WORK and INTEGRITY are hereditary! I am content to WORK for my success - not like you, who would prefer to have it handed to him on a silver platter as a reward for being born into a royal family.
I doubt, however, that this little speech will convince you that merit and bloodline tend not to walk hand in hand. Thus, let us look into the realm of hard evidence. In your first match, you were defeated by Pulsar. When that same Pulsar attempted to battle me, however, his attempts to damage me were fruitless - in fact, he did more damage to himself than to me - and resulted in his rapid elimination from the So Cal Rumble. I say this not as an insult to Pulsar - he's a good man, and his exhuberance is actually quite refreshing - but rather to prove to YOU, Impala, that I can do things you cannot and have beaten a man you could not.
When you're done mulling that over, I also suggest taking a look at the size differential. I outweigh you by a hundred and fifty pounds. Good luck moving me, kid. Perhaps you HAVE been to some prestigious wrestling schools, but all the Greco-Roman holds in the world don't mean a thing when you're being flung around like a rag doll by a man almost twice your size.
Of course, having heard you speak before, I fully anticipate that you will ignore every word I have said and continue to deem me unworthy by reason of my common origins. Ah well. Believe what you will, Impala. But know this. In my den, bad things happen to those too weak of body and character to comprehend honor and basic decency. You have shown consistently that you lack honor. Thus, you find yourself in a highly precarious position.
Tread carefully, little one. Should you misstep, you may find that royal blood of yours spilled across the canvas... (FADEOUT)
