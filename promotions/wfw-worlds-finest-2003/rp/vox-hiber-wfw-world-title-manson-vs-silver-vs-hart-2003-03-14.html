Everyone wants to the champion and after it's crowned, everyone will be want to be the WFW champion, for all the obvious reasons. Of course there's the added impetus of throwing me down, which seems to appease some people. But if my status as an anti-person and the depraved man in the world adds importance to what should the most important title in he world, let it be. Said title can only be gained by overcoming 2 familiars..Shawn Hart and Doc Silver..the three of us having already seen each other all over the circuit. But that doesn't matter now. It might to them, but I always have to be looking forward, future-oriented you can say.

It's a chance, but I doubt you can actually do it. All this sexual porn-star innuendo gets you started, but you can never make it to the next level. That's all right though, because there is a secondary title and maybe you could win that. Even name it yourself, a marker for everyone that can only gets far, but then fall.
Then again, maybe you can, and your problem is that I'm here. If so, I suggest hoping for God finally stiking me down because otherwise, you have no chance. Still, you could always strive to be the best North American champion there is.
And the third..namely...Doc Silver. It appears what is old is new again. You know, after all time, I was thankful to you, Doc, really I was. Miller likes to laud it as one of his masterpieces, but the match we had so long ago, well long relatively, is what made me what I am. Some say a star, others a menace, but a highly-paid one nonetheless. Miller booked it, but it was me facing you that made it what it was and that match was why it was the Superbowl of Wrestling. Not the other overrated, narrow-minded sycophants having meaningless matches they thought could make them more than what they were. It didn't come down to us, but it should have. All that I have been able to do since is because of that. I don't credit you fully of course, so I had to do things myself and my wabut if it makes you feel better, go ahead and think that.
Because I did always like you, Doc. Maybe it was because nobody else did and because they similarly distrusted and isolated me....maybe we thought too much alike....we always got on well...remarkable given how most of my partnerships have gone on. Still, this is a new day. And now it's you or me. Mutual self-interest always seemed to be why we could work together and that's what propels me now. But what makes this title more to me comes from something else.
As the years went on...with all my evil schemes....revenge scenarios..sometimes I'd be in your shadow....and at times we'd work together for our benefits...and yes..there were times where I surpassed you. But even doing that, something lacked. No matter how vicious I was, how manipulative I could be, I could never be the champion, the all-reaching lord of the hell we call an industry..because in that mtch that made me a star..at the one and only Superbowl of Wrestling..I lost to you. Not a horrible thing, mind you, it seemed to have happened to everyone at one time or another. I refused to be Shawn Hart..and went on my way and path to being what I am..but the time never cam, he probabilities never converged for another bout...a match of importance. Until now.
I'm normally one to see the big picture and not care much for records. But if Lucifer is allowed his pride, so am I. Because me finally beating you, Doc,finally cements and realizes in my mind, in this world and universe, what I really know...deep down inside....what everyone who bothers to look can see..that I dragged my bloody body to your throne and deposed you a long time ago. That now I sit upon it, scars, pez, and all. I just never had a ceremony. Not until now..and I even get gold in the process.
