New World Madness: Pulsar vs. Dormant

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 September 2003 The winner of this match will face Rat Fink for the BAD World Heavyweight Title on the next show.
Cut your promos here. The RP deadline is Monday October 13th 11:59 PM. Send all angles to [detail withheld]. stuclif — 2 October 2003 FADE IN: PULSAR, AGAIN SITTING IN THE CORNER OF A BROKEN DOWN, TATTERED OLD RING IN THE SAME OLD DIMLY LIT GYM. RIBS BANDAGED UP AND HOLDING HIS LEFT ARM TO HIS CHEST
PULSAR: You know what, (GRUNTING AND GROANING, IN PAIN WHEN SPEAKING) at Almost Live, i had a visit with the Devil himself......you know why...... cause, on that night, 6 men went to hell and back. And to tell the truth, he's not a bad bloke and ill tell you why.
(STILL IN PAIN, PULSAR GIVES HIS OPINION OF THE MATCH AT ALMOST LIVE, NOT ENTIRELY FOCUSED ON THE CAMERA)
PULSAR: You see, only a being with that sort of evil intentions could occupy the combatants in such a match.... and out of all the possible scenarios.....he chose me to come out as victor.
(NOW GIVES HIS FULL ATTENTION TO THE CAMERA) PULSAR: Now this has got to have the young Dormant nervous, you see at Almost Live (ACTING IN A SHOCKED AND SCARED MANNER) there was tables on fire, bodies were slammed onto exposed concete and as i predicted... mountains of pain were dished out........NOONE WAS SPARED!! Thats right, not even myself (SQUIRMS AND LOOKS TO HIS RIBS). (GRITS HIS TEETH TOGETHER) But thats alright......... you see, i learnt at a young age that pain is just weakness leaving the body.....(NOW SEETHING THROUGH HIS TEETH) AND BY NEW WORLD MADNESS, THERE'LL BE NO WEAKNESS LEFT. (LOOKS AWAY SHORTLY, AND SNAPS HIS HEAD BACK TOWARDS THE CAMERA, TAKES A BREATH TO RELAX) The winner of this match is going to be given the chance of a lifetime, a shot at the BAD world strap, and seeing the we're both new here this isnt a shot that either of us can afford to give up.
(STRUGGLES HARD, BUT MANAGES TO GET TO HIS FEET, LOOKING WITH COLD, STEEL EYES AT THE CAMERA) PULSAR:(LOW STEADY VOICE) Ive already reserved a table for 2 at the Devil's table, cause this match will become hell... AND MY OLD MATE THE DEVIL HAS PROMISED ME PLEASING RESULTS.
FADE TO BLACK EGarrett — 5 October 2003 INT. JERSEY CITY OFFICE Elaine, the Presidential Secretary, is in the Presidential office leafing through some papers and making notes while the NFW feed plays on a monitor behind her. The sound of Pulsar's grunting and groaning from his mic spot draws her attention. She swivels her chair around and watches him grab his chest and grunt in pain. She then reaches for the TiVO remote, rewinds it and watches the footage again. She removes her free hand from the papers and rubs a finger across her bottom lip as she rewinds it again and pauses...then produces a cellphone and starts dialing.
FADEOUT aza — 6 October 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Oct-06-03 AT 08:04&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN: Dormant can be seen leaning forward on his chair eagerly watching a replay of his match at Almost Live backstage. The camera at his back where he can be seen continuously rewinding the part where he was hit with the ring bell by Michales, which cost him the match. The camera moves around from the back of Dormant's chair so he can face the camera. He has a bandage on his head from where he was struck with the bell.)
Dormant: Did you see that? (Dormant points to the television screen) My introduction to the WFW had been interrupted by Luster's homosexual buddies. The triad or whatever! More like the Threesome! They cost me that match, but congrats to El Arco Iris. I'm sure a lot of joints were smoked shortly after that win of his. But I can't be worrying about whether to beat up a few s**t wrestlers who have this pact to help each other out every time they're getting their a***'s beat. I have more important things to do in this business.
(Dormant stands up and walks over to a poster on a wall. The poster is a picture of the BAD World Title. He points at it and begins to talk.)
Dormant: This is the BAD World Title. This is no push over. This is something you have to earn! Something that you put your blood, sweat and tears into. I have a Number One Contendership Match for this belt against Pulsar. We have a few things in common. We're both new, we both were screwed out of our debut matches by the same Threesome group, and we're both f***ing hungry for this belt. But does anyone really know about this man, about Pulsar? (Dormant looks at the camera with a devilish smile on his face to personally address Pulsar) Pulsar... I know about you... I'm going to tell everyone who you are. You're a pretty angry guy who likes to snap necks, being friends with the Devil and all of that kind of stuff, but let me ask you, where did this all come from? Do you remember when you were little? Did your daddy used to hurt you or touch you in strange places man cos you are seriously messed up!?
(Dormant smiles as he makes fun of Pulsar. Crowd can be heard in the background cheering and laughing. Dormant's smile suddenly disappears as he walks up to a mirror in the change room he is in to remove the bandage covering his wound. He turns back to the camera to reveal a very deep grove in his forehead from the ring bell shot at Almost Live)
Pulsar, do you really think that just because you were abused as a child that I'm going to lay down for you to become the No. 1 contentder? I know you had a gruesome 6 man tag match. I was watching out back. I studied your match. I seen your strengths and your weaknesses. And you know what... You're strengths aren't enough to overcome an Eruption. Because NO ONE escapes an Eruption!
(Crowd can be heard cheering again. Dormant is now pacing from side to side getting more fired up. He stops pacing and looks straight into the camera. His eyes looking menancingly.)
Dormant: You might be in a bit of pain now Pulsar, but the thing you don't understand is thats what the BAD World Title is all about! (Dormant is real angry and blood can be seen seeping down his head from his laceration. Dormant wipes the blood of his head and looks at his hand. He then shows it to the camera) See this Pulsar, this is nothing to what you're going to be feeling. We'll see how long it will take before you encounter the Eruption. Then you'll know what pain is.(Dormant backs off from the camera points into it and chants.)
Dormant: ARE YOU READY FOR AN ERUPTION!? (The crowd can be heard chanting with Dormant and then cheering. Dormant turns and kicks the television over causing it to spark and catch on fire. Dormant turns to the camera. The reflection of the flames can be seen burning in his eyes.)
Dormant: (Talking in a calm, hoarse voice) Pulsar... Be ready. (Dormant leaves the room, camera focuses on the fire caused from the explosion of the television. FADEOUT)

Do you have that sort of committment in you? Huh? FADE OUT aza — 18 October 2003 (FADEIN: Dormant is sitting on a chair watching a television documentary. It can be seen that the documentary is something about space. The commentator of the documentary can be heard.)
Commentator: As you can see, black holes are vacuums in outer space. They suck light and other particles in space. Although we don't have to worry about black holes sucking us. They are millions and millions of miles away from Earth. Thanks for watching, I'm Cliff Orno.
(Dormant leans forward off his chair and switches off the television. He turns to face the camera.)
Dormant: Hey Pulsar. I was just sitting here watching a documentary on black holes. And do you know what... I think I found some similarities and how it relates to you. See, the first thing is that they both suck! And since you think you are going to beat me the other thing is that you are millions of miles away from Earth. Do you really think you have got what it takes to beat me!?
(Dormant stands up and starts walking over to a corner of the room where a CD player can be seen on the ground)
Dormant: I'm going to give you a gift Pulsar. I'm going to give you a song. Maybe it'll help you understand how our match at New World Madness will go down.
(Dormant leans down and hits the play button. "Rocketman" in karioke can be heard playing. Crowd can be heard not too far away cheering and laughing)
Dormant: I've got a match at New World Madness for the BAD strap Its gunna be a win cos the man I'm wrestling is crap He says in the black hole there will be only silence But I'm going to erupt and show him a whole lot of violence I've done some research on his moves and the black hole Sorry to disappoint Pulsar but you're gunna fall I learnt about the black hole from commentator, Cliff Orno And I also learnt about it from watching a dirty porno Pulsar you sound like you've been through a lot And wrestling me isn't gunna make it stop I'm not going to pretend that your nothing I'm going to be bring everything and the Eruption (Dormant stares at the camera looking pretty p****d off as his song finishes. The crowd is cheering louder than before. The crowd dies down awaiting for Dormant to speak. Dormant pauses momentarily.)
Dormant: You talk about silence. That pause is all the silence you're going to get from me Pulsar. Because at New World Madness it's going to be non-stop action. Punches here, blood there, suplex here, smashed table there. (Dormant starting to get fired up.) You've said you've met the Devil, you haven't met me yet!
(Dormant retracts from the camera to cool himself down. He paces side to side, looking at the ground. He remains pacing while he talks)
Dormant: You came here for money. I came here for respect. Now we're both here for the BAD world title. And do you what... (Dormant stops walking, lifts his head slightly and burns his eyes into the camera.) I'm leaving with 'em all!
