ViewersChoice — 4 April 2004 (FADEIN to "Superstar" Scotty Michaels. No elaborate intro necessary..) SCOTTY MICHAELS: Well look what we've got here. Shawn Hart crawls back up out of the hole that he's been in for the past three months, and decides to come after the man who may say, STOLE the championship away from him. You can say whatever the hell that you want to, but a lot's changed since you went into seclusion.
I lost the championship to Sean Edmunds, he lost it to Alex Wylde. Luster, he turned his back on me. And then, he's carted off to a psychiatric hospital. The WFW, turned upside down. For just about the entire first year that the WFW existed, it was about you and me. We were the begin-all and end-all in the WFW. You and me were the guys who were headlining shows.
Sure, you held the WFW world title a little while longer than I can say that I did, but that's not the point. If it wasn't for the star power and prestige that we brought to this promotion, it wouldn't be where it's at today.
You want to know the funny thing about it? This is a mid-card match. There's all this other glitz and glamour going on, and I'M STUCK IN A MID-CARD MATCH! Guys like Copycat, guys like Sean Edmunds... they're facing greats from other federations.
I don't know about you, Hart, but I say we take the WFW back. We put on the best damn match imagineable, and make everyone realize exactly who the best wrestlers are in this federation.
I don't have any hatred for you, Hart.. your payback was necessary, and expected.. at some time or another. But this time, Luster's not going to be my ace in the hole. He bit the hand that fed him, and now he's in the mental ward.
This time, it's about you and me. This time, it's about taking back what is OURS! Not Anarky's, not Alex Wylde's, and certainly not Sean Edmunds.
Your biggest weakness right now, you know what it is. The fans. They cheer you on, but they'll turn their backs on you as soon as you resort to your old ways. You know it'll happen sooner or later, the question is... what atrocity will you commit? I guess we'll just have to wait and see.
You better bring you're A Game, Hart, because what you brought back on New Year's Eve, it just wasn't good enough. I'll seeya at the Superbowl...
(FADEOUT...) ShawnHartXXX — 5 April 2004 FADE IN: The ideal stage for your stereotypical 80's style rant: generic photo background, bad lighting, and cheaply made federation logo hanging from the ceiling. There, amongst the antiquity, stands 'THE PHENOM' SHAWN HART.
HART: Well Mr. Michaels, it seems you've hit that ol' nail right on the head. My hole of cheap liquor and syndicated television had grown juuuuuust a tad too damp for my likings, so here I am... BACK on the saddle again, looking for dryer places to hang my hat!

Well congrats, mon ami, because I couldn't agree more! But y'see Scotty, when you have the GAUL to say that it was both the Phenom AND Scotty Michaels who held this league in place before the bottom fell out, YOU my friend... are SORELY mistaken!
The Phenom licks his lips. HART: You can go ahead and cut ME out of that whirlwind of title changes you and everybody else around here likes to yap about. I held onto that belt for MONTH after grueling MONTH of little pups biting and scratching at my ankles. Yeah, you're a LOVELY talent, and SURE... you headlined a couple cards with yours truly, but ALL similarities end there, friend. When you got to the top, you took a few breaths, fell over backwards, and bumped your head a few times on the way down. The PHENOM was just that, a phenomenon to behold. The very PILLAR of what World's FINEST Wrestling was all about! Call it an outrage, call it a mockery... hell, SCREW the petty catch phrases, it's STILL the truth!
Hart looks to the ceiling, gives his patented 'I'm disgusted by all of this' head shake, then shoots an icy expression back into the camera.
HART: As far as taking back the WFW goes, you may be on to something. Things have changed since I left, but see... that's just the reason why they changed. I, THE PHENOM, left... and you trying as you might to cash in on what I did and take some of the credit for what went down, just because you wrestled on the same shows as me, QUITE FRANKLY SPEAKING... gives me indigestion. If this match is to be mired in the mediocrity of mid-show status, fine. By the time the show ends, the people will still know who the REAL reason for this company's success was. Sure, they may turn on me... but that's their right. Either way, you, them, and everybody else around here will give me my just due!
The Phenom takes a deep breath and rolls his eyes arrogantly. HART: Regardless of what happens after this show, something needs to be made clear RIGHT NOW. My legacy is my own and NOBODY can ever take it from me. People like to say the WFW title's been devalued because of HACKS like you, but at the Superbowl... I'm gonna show the WORLD just how great it once was. The PHENOM has left the building...

Admit it, man.. since the moment the WFW opened it's doors, it's been about you and it's been about me. You may not show me the respect that I deserve, but I'll take what I can get. I don't need to prove myself to you, or any of these fans that sit in their seats.
They supported me at one time, but then they all got jealous of me. I was doing exactly what I said I would do... something that they with they could do. Look around these crowds. You see people missing teeth, you see mullets, and you see a parking lot full of Camaro's. It makes me sick. They all make me sick.
You know what else makes me sick? Anarky, Manson, Wylde, Copycat... all at the top, representing this great federation I once was proud to be a part of. NUH UH! No way, not gonna stay that way for much longer.
I've had enough of it, and it's time to take back what is mine, Hart. Don't you agree? Or are you all caught up in all the temporary cheers, and the pops that you receive when you walk out to that ring? Has the hype blinded you, and made you forget what you were really about... what you're really here for?
You care as much about any of them as I do... admit it. ADMIT IT! Get back to your roots, Hart... get back to what matters MOST! I know you've got it in you, somewhere deep down in that head of yours. You're just waiting, plotting... until the moment you can finally get that opportunity to reclaim what is yours.
And it's bigger than the world title, it's bigger than all of that. Open your eyes, man... OPEN YOUR DAMN EYES!
At the Superbowl of Wrestling, I begin my quest for REDEMPTION! A victory over you, it won't come easily... but you can bet it'll happen. I've guaranteed many things before, and I've followed through on EACH and EVERY single one of them.
You're no exception, Hart... you weren't before, and you're not this time. Superbowl of Wrestling, I take back... WHAT IS MINE! MINE! MINE! MINE! (FADEOUT...) FADE OUT PaulNJ21 — 11 April 2004 Sunday, April 11, 2004 To whom it may concern, For the vast majority of WFW's first year of operation, 'The Phenom' Shawn Hart set the pace for everyone involved in the establishment. Through the initial comings and goings of people that were expected to be cornerstones of the promotion, Hart remained steadfast. He bit, scratched and clawed his way back to prominence through week after week of hard work and dedication. The fruits of this labor came when Hart finally defeated Doc Silver to become WFW champion. Despite the numerous awards, accolades and titles he had amassed in his 5+ years of work, to Hart... this was his crowning achievement.
For several months, the Phenom held on to that title, facing numerous opponents and helping World's Finest Wrestling grow into one of the top promotions on the circuit along the way. That being the case, one would expect that Hart would get his fair share of the credit for the federation's growth, as well as the respect that comes with being a world heavyweight champion. However, due to the actions of people like Scotty Michaels and Luster, Hart did not receive that which he was entitled to. Instead, he was robbed of his title, denied a legitimate rematch, and swiftly forced out of the company.
