WFW Great Expectations: Jared Wells vs. El Arco Iris

29 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 January 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, February 11 11:59 EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
[name withheld] — 30 January 2004 {...cueup: Machine gun by Jimi Hendrix} {...camera opens up to Jared Wells wearing khakis pants, dress shoes, white polo shirt, black leather jacket, hair is all styled spiky and messy with the blonde top and brown sides. He is walking through a park on a cold sunny day as he gives a few people some autographs. Some fans are asking him about the entire change of style}
'RAGE 'o FIRE': You know if you wait long enough, good things do happen. I told the word before Mind Over Matter that things were going to happen. I told you all that the game plan was all laid out. What's funny is we are not done. Oh not by a long shot. The path of rage began at Mind Over Matter and will end until we are happy. In other words we are sick and tired of the crap that's being dished out in the WFW.
Anarky and myself have a long long history of this business. We finally went one on one in March of last year. A long career dream to face Anarky one on one. I got my chance and I put my heart and soul into that match. I lost by a DQ and the rest was history. After coming back from rehab and retirement after three years I learned something valuable in that match. No matter who you are, or what you do, a world title may claim your the man, but really your not the man. That night even though we didn't express it but both of us acknowledged respect. I've always respect the 'Nark man for what he does and what he represents. And I'm sure everybody is thinking what a kiss ass right? Nah, no way in hell. I've been around here for too damn long for somebody to come up to me and claim I'm a kiss ass. It doesn't work that way. I won't lie, 'Nark and myself really don't get along but the pieces of the puzzle just seem to connect.
At Mind Over Matter, it worked and will continue to turn heads. Everybody seems to overlook me when I come around. But when I get my chance, people get scared. They get a little worried because I stuck by my word. Now my words are more than just a thought....they turn into history. And for most, it will cost them.
El Arco Iris, how's it going? Are you enjoying your day? How's the kids? *PAUSES* Whatever. Iris, just like I tell all my opponents, I don't hate you, I just feel sorry for you. You were there at Mind Over Matter, how did you feel about it? Please excuse my actions Mr. Iris but that's just the way things are run and will have to run for now on. Playaz Inc failed, Copycat and yourself failed, and you all fell into our trap. Wham-Bam the rage bomb was laid and Copycat once again became another stepping stone. But I'm sure your wondering who the third guy was? Who was that guy in the black mask? *GRINS* Rainbow dude, thats for me to know and you to find out. Your a happy man all the time right? I bet I really could piss you off.
LOOK INTO MY EYES *****! SEE 'EM? Your in the wrong place at the wrong time. The plan will all come about, and once its all said and done.........you'll want to leave. All your happiness crap.....your your beliefs will become a nightmare.....and for myself and two people.......paradise. Are you ready to dance man? Well.........*PAUSES AND STARES AT CAMERA FOR 10sec*.......RAGE.........he leads.....you'll see my reflection change.
{...fadeout as Wells storms off} Diablo — 3 February 2004 (Cue up: “Face to Face (Cosmo Vitelli Remix)” by Daft Punk as the screen is bombarded by all of the colors of the spectrum successively, which fades to El Arco Iris standing in front of a rainbow backdrop, in his mask, ring attire, and rainbow satin robe over it all.)
El Arco Iris: El Arco Iris knows that El Arco Iris and Copycat were unsuccessful at Mind Over Matter, but as Copycat and El Arco Iris clearly show, we are too over ourselves to simply be upset with ourselves with a defeat when we know there is a task ahead of us that demands our focus. Failure is only temporary, and El Arco Iris must concentrate all of El Arco Iris’ energies on his next opponent, a man truly stuck in the much and mire of his own hate-filled heart. Jared Wells at least knows to respect his fellow human being, but he is still incapable of ridding himself of the chains of contempt and unbridled anger that bind him like Marley’s ghost. El Arco Iris must express supreme disappointment in you, Señor Wells. Your indelible hatred caused you to perform despicable actions against my partner. Copycat is a prankster, yes, but he performs his jokes so people will learn not to take themselves so seriously, as those who do are the ones most humiliated. He never has to hide his actions in the dark, use stealth or trickery just to make people laugh. Señor Wells, your actions were downright malicious, and furthermore, in the process of your attack, you bring in a henchman who defiles something very close and dear to El Arco Iris…
(The camera closes up on El Arco Iris’ face as El Arco Iris points to…) El Arco Iris: …The mask. Through your evil actions, you desecrate what it means for El Arco Iris to wear this mask. You use the mask as a symbol of cowardice, hiding one’s face in shame because of the evil you commit. You use the mask as a symbol of deceit. El Arco Iris’ wears a mask not to hid himself, but to show in full view the very meaning and purpose of El Arco Iris. El Arco Iris wears this mask so when someone thinks of El Arco Iris, they do not think of a face, a body, or a person, they think of actions in the ring, they think of a purpose to help every man, woman and child be freed from the shackles of desolation. They think of the smiles that El Arco Iris puts on the faces of all who see him. They see a meaning that transcends the body, that affects mind and spirit.
(El Arco Iris turns around so the camera can see the back of his mask) El Arco Iris: The back of El Arco Iris’ mask. Every color of the spectrum that human eye can see, is not just for aesthetic appeal. No color is excluded, as should never be in real life from anything. Every color is intertwined and interdependent on an other, a message against hate, that happiness is meant for every individual, not just for one or some. As the eye can see every color, the eyes of El Arco Iris can see the spark of happiness within the gnarled and withered hearts of the most forgone with the darkness of their own minds, much as El Arco Iris can see it in Jared Wells. Color comes from light, light from the sun, a kind of light El Arco Iris hopes to shine on every man, as the sun gives color to every blade of grass and every inch of sky and hair on the head of a newborn child.
(El Arco Iris turns back around) El Arco Iris: The front of El Arco Iris’ mask. A face, featureless, save for the eyes, that shine with brilliance and exuberance, and the smile. People have grabbed this mask, wrenched at it, showing their anger not just at El Arco Iris, but their anger and frustration at themselves, knowing the enlightenment this mask represents that they can, but do not achieve. This face could be any face. It could be the face of a man, a woman, it could even be your face, Jared Wells. El Arco Iris can look in your eyes, but El Arco Iris only sees a man who cannot look into his own eyes and realize what a prisoner he is. Look into your eyes, into your heart, Señor Wells, then look at the mask of El Arco Iris, and realize if you yourself can let go of your evils, you can ascend to higher levels than you already are. You would not be despairing over losses of the past and your own mistakes, and you would be more mature than to seek heinous retribution on your fellow man. El Arco Iris believes El Arco Iris is in the perfectly right place, because El Arco Iris can guide, you, reform you, and change your reflection for the better.
(El Arco Iris outstretches an arm and pointing finger towards the camera as he looks straight on.)
El Arco Iris: Jared Wells can learn to lead himself, and not be lead by his own rage. The mask of El Arco Iris will help show the true face of Jared Wells, and not the haggard countenance of “Rage o’ Fire”. If you grab and tear at this mask, you are merely trying to rend asunder your own chance to finally find the key to the lock that clasps the yoke of desolation around your neck. At Great Expectations, El Arco Iris will help you escape the prison of your mind, and see for yourself a great, big… SMIIIIIIIILE (traces the smile on his mask with his thumb) on your face!

Funny, the people who said my career was over.......seems like everything is just getting better and better. My legacy, my name, everything is at stake on this war.
