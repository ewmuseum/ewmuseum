Vote Wells!

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JLevinson — 11 April 2004 (CUEUP: The Star Spangled Banner as we fadein to a waving American flag... as it flaps in an imaginary wind, The Movie Guy starts talking in his usual "Movie Guy Voice"... )
MOVIE GUY VOICE: "America is about democracy... about choice... about freedom. And now, democracy comes to WFW. Each man has a choice. He can throw his vote away... or he can make a decision to improve his career."
(CUTTO: A shot of Iron Lion.) MOVIE GUY VOICE: "Some candidates have made empty promises about inceased security and fairness. But more security guards and referees cost money. Where is that money going to come from? Your pockets, of course. A vote for Iron Lion is a vote against your hard-earned money. A vote for Iron Lion is a vote for higher taxes and increased government influence. Do you want Big Brother knocking on your door at night, demanding your money? Do you want to have to drive a Lexus when you used to drive a Rolls Royce?"
(CUTTO: A shot of Sean Edmunds.) MOVIE GUY VOICE: "Other candidates have chosen to run under no platform at all except their own self-love. Sean Edmunds doesn't care about you. Sean Edmunds only cares about Sean Edmunds. Do we want to sit through another Presidential office that exists only to pat itself on the back? Voting for Sean Edmunds is a vote for vanity and fascism. Everyone knows Sean Edmunds is a Nazi. You don't want to vote for a Nazi, do you?"
(CUTTO: A shot of El Arco Iris.) MOVIE GUY VOICE: "And finally, some candidates are so delusional and psychotic that they are unable to even mount a campaign for their Presidency. And though we, as Americans, have always enjoyed liars and madmen running our country, shouldn't we draw the line somewhere? Do you want rainbows and lollipops all over the locker room? Do you want a candidate so removed from reality that he denies that his very job is to hurt people? A vote for El Arco Iris is not a vote for happiness, but a vote for insanity and inequality."
(CUTTO: A shot of Jared Wells sitting in an office with a keg cup in his hand, drawing beer out of a half barrel that sits on his oak desk.)
MOVIE GUY VOICE: "Here at LOVE, we believe that a candidate should never be sober enough to consider his own gain instead of others. Here at LOVE, we believe that a candidate should be nothing more than a puppet of the people, open to a variety of questionable actions and loose morals.
"A vote for Jared Wells is a vote for yourself. At LOVE, we value loyalty and committment to excellence above all else. We run under a platform of complete corruption and system of favors for favors. We want your vote, and we're willing to do whatever it takes to get it. You want a Title shot? It's yours. Need to get revenge in a handicap match? Done and done. Want a match against a variety of senior citizens and midgets? Your wish is our command.
"Jared Wells is LOVE, and Jared Wells loves each and every one of you like you were our brother or sister. Why waste your vote on a candidate that will never help you? LOVE is about the people; and you're a person, aren't you? So LOVE is obviously about you.
"Come Monday, you have a choice. You can throw your vote away on some egomaniac or someone making empty promises and increasing taxes, or you can vote for yourself. You can vote for your future. You can vote for LOVE in WFW.
"You can vote... for Jared Wells." (CUTTO: A shot of the LOVE log with a message that says "The preceding message is brought to you by The Liars, Oppressors and Violence Experiment. We LOVE you all."
(FADEOUT.) JABolich — 11 April 2004 (FADEIN to IRON LION sitting at a desk) Iron Lion: What a load of tripe. Since when are wrestlers taxed at work? Last I checked, money comes out of ticket sales, concession sales, Pay-Per-View buys, advertising revenue, and merchandising. Higher taxes my eye.
Considering that we're getting a shill for a slow-witted drunkard who's spearheading an organization whose first two initials stand for LIARS and OPPRESSORS, I think it's safe to say that Jared Wells is full of sh*t. A vote for Wells is a vote for dishonesty and Fascism. Edmunds may be an egomaniac, but the REAL Nazis here are the members of LOVE. So unless you want WFW to degenerate into indiscriminate persecution, I think you know who to vote for.
When you go to the ballots, you'll have a choice between a blithering egomaniac with no platform, a man representing a platform of LIES AND OPPRESSION, and me, a man representing a platform of Safety, Security, and Satisfaction. The choice, as they say, should be obvious.
(FADEOUT to gold text:) A VOTE FOR IRON LION IS A VOTE FOR THE THREE S'S! NotorisSTD — 11 April 2004 (CUEUP: “Baby, I’m an Anarchist” by Against Me!) (CUTTO: Picture of Jarred Wells standing around in a LOVE T-shirt, and kakies swigging from a bottle of Jack Daniels Animal House/Belushi style…)
