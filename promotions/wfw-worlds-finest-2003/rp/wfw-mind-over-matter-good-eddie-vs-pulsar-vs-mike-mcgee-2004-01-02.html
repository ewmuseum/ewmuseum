stuclif — 8 January 2004 FADE IN: AN EMPTY WEIGHT BENCH AND PUNCHING BAG WITH NOBODY USING IT. GRAONS AND SCREAMS OF PAIN CAN BE HEARD AS THE CAMERA TURNS TOWARD THE FAMILIAR OLD DIMLY LIT RING. PULSAR IS STANDING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING WITH THREE TRADITIONALLY DRESSED WRESTLERS (STANDARD BOOTS AND TRUNKS, GREEN, YELLOW AND RED) STANDING AROUND HIM APPERAING READY TO ATTACK. A PAIR OF LEGS CAN BE SEEN TO THE SIDE OF THE RING, NOT MOVING.
YELLOW TRUNKS MOVES FIRST, BEFORE HE CAN MOVE THREE STEPS HE RECEIVES A STIFF SIDE KICK TO THE STOMACH WHICH SEES HIM KEEL OVER ONLY TO RECEIVE A VICIOUS RIGHT BARE-NUCKLED CROSS THAT SENDS OUT THROUGH THE ROPES ONTO THE FLOOR MOTIONLESS. GREEN AND RED TRUNKS THINK SMART AND ATTACK AT THE SAME TIME. PULSAR STEPS TO THE SIDE OF GREEN TRUNKS AND THROWS A KNEE INTO THE GUT OF RED TRUNKS, BEFORE GREEN TRUNKS CAN TURN AROUND PULSAR GRABS THE HEAD OF RED TRUNKS AND SNAPS HIM INTO A BLACK HOLE (role of the dice) AFTER WHICH HE DOESNT MOVE. GREEN TRUNKS IS NOW LEFT ALONE, HE TRACKS CATIOUSLY AROUND PULSAR. GREEN TRUNKS THEN THROWS A WEAK LOOKING LEFT JAB, PULSAR GRABS THE ARM, TAKES IN THE SCARED LOOK ON GREEN TRUNKS' FACE THEN PALMS THE OUTSIDE OF GREEN TRUNKS' ELBOW.
THE CAMERA SWINGS AWAY TO AN EMPTY CORNER OF THE RING, AS THE SOUNDS OF BONES SNAPPING AND ADULT SCREAMING FILLS THE GYM. PULSAR THEN STEPS INTO THE VIEW OF THE CAMERA LOOKING CALM AND NOT BOTHERED BY WHAT HE HAS JUST DONE.
PULSAR: You people at home have all just witnessed the first lesson in the (BREATHING STARTS TO QUICKEN) glamorous world of professional wrestling.......it hurts!! (GLARING AT THE CAMERA) These brave young men have just found this out. This lesson will also determine what sort of men these guys will become, if these guys show up on the doorstep tomorrow waiting for another lesson, as battered and as bruised as they might... I mean will be, it will tell me that these guys are people that society will be able to count on. When life puts them on their arse they wont get up and run away and hide........ they'll get up and face life and ask for more.
PULSAR: (TURNS AND FACES THE TRUNKS') Hospitals two blocks down the street. (BACK TO CAMERA) What do you do Eddie? What happens when life puts a sweet drop kick on your chin? Wait.... I know, you crack the s##ts and take off back to the locker room, leavin' people, who are counting on you, hanging.
PULSAR: And to Mister Middle-Management Mike Mcgee, how many short cuts did you take back in the office days, how many people did you short change to save a buck or two....... Come Mind Over Matter, both of you will learn a lesson in wrestling and in life, seein' that you are both very average human beings. Hell, I'll even learn something, what its like to be back on track after WFW gold.
PULSAR: (CAMERA COMES IN TO A CLOSE-UP) There's been enough hurdles, enough crap, the same focused game plan is comin' back into play.......that means bad news for anyone in the way. Cue the excitement
GreggG — 8 January 2004 (CUT TO: McGee, in an executive office, sitting on the edge of a desk. On the far wall is a poster of Alex Wylde. Hanging above that poster is a banner that reads PRESIDENT ALEX WYLDE APPRECIATION DAY.)
MCGEE: You know something, Pulsar... (Points at the camera.) I DON'T LIKE YOUR TONE OF VOICE. How dare you insinuate that *EYE* have taken shortcuts. As this company's Assistant Human Resources Manager, I am in charge of approving your sick days, you comp time requests, your expense accounts, your 401K fund. I am not only worried about saving me some money-- I already have a good interest rate in my Wachovia savings account, pal-- but I am worried about the financial well being of this company.
And why am I worried? I can assure you it is not because of our leadership, President Alex Wylde. He is a man who has proven himself... TIME AND TIME AGAIN... to be beyond ethical reproach and to have wisdom beyond his years. Why, Pulsar, just check your Microsoft Office inbox account. Mr. Wylde periodically sends out e-mails discussing his vision for this promotion and where he would like to take it. Of course, he is a superstar in this industry who has already accomplished a Hall-of-Fame resume and is our leading light both in the ring and out. His vision and his ability to sell out arenas by himself will definitely assure us long-term success.
But what does worry me is other people on the roster... roster members such as you, Pulsar. And you, Fast Eddy. Pulsar, you say you wish to teach lessons in pain. Pulsar, you say that you want to be a star in this sport, that you want to procure championship gold. Well, Pulsar, having seen the marketing reports and television ratings... you certainly do not "cue the excitement" as you use as your tag line.
What you cue is boredom. What you cue is narcolepsy. Instead of training with hapless no-names, you should consider finding yourself a better marketing niche and establishing a better brand identity for yourself. If you wish, we can meet here in Jersey City where I, and President Wylde, can go over different ways you can help us help you.
And as far as calling me an average human being... I must assure you that I am most certainly ABOVE AVERAGE! I can go through your resume in my office if I want, but I can be rest assured that *YOU* were not named a 2nd-Team Northeast Regional Wrestler while wrestling at William Paterson State College in Haledon, New Jersey like I was. I can be rest assured that you did not make dean's list THREE different semesters while majoring in Corporate Communication like I was. I can be rest assured that you do not have another full-time job like I do, handling vacation time requests and processing time cards for this promotion. Did you intern in the marketing department for a pharmaceutical company while in college? Heck, I bet you can't even SPELL college... let alone pharmaceutical.
Pulsar... your harsh words have been noted. I view that as a form of harrassment. Luckily, today I'm in a forgiving mood so I will not make you come in and sign a performance report regaling your negative behavior. Any more bad words about me... and especially our president... then you will be punished... both IN and out of the ring.
That's a direct order from ME... your superior in Middle Management! SigilOfLeviBF — 9 January 2004 (FADEIN: Early in the morning, New Mexico, at an empty bar somewhere in a random town. The only man there is the bartender, opening up for business. The place is kind of dirty, and in walks Good Eddie.)
(Bartender looks up at Eddie) BARTENDER: You drink this early, amigo? (Eddie takes a seat) EDDIE: If you consider a dry Amaretto drinking, sure... BARTENDER: Coming up. EDDIE: (Gets up, walks towards the bartender) You can forget about the drink, but there is somethin' I'd like you to remember...
BARTENDER: (Nervous) What's that? EDDIE: The last time you saw a man in here that calls himself Pulsar... BARTENDER: Hey buddy, a lot of men be callin' themselves a lot of things around here, 'specially at night when they're fillin' themselves with drink. H-How the hell am I supposed to remember some idiot?
EDDIE: (Spits onto the counter) 'Cause I'm the man who's killed everybody... (Freeze frame on Eddie's face) (CUEUP: Western renegade music) (Early credits roll over the freeze frame) 'TWILIGHT AT SUNUP' STARRING: GOOD EDDIE as THE STRANGER WESLEY BERNARD as THE BARTENDER TROY JOHNSON as LITTLE PETEY MEG WILSON as BLONDIE and MIGUEL ESTRADA as MEAN PEDRO SCRIPT BY: ANON. DIRECTED BY: JOHN DOE (Freeze frame continues with the music for a few seconds more, then ends) EDDIE: Now what do you recall about a fellow named Pulsar? BARTENDER: Not anything you'd care to hear! He came in with another fellow, 'bout half his height; they drank some sh*t then left together! Happy?
EDDIE: (Draws a Smith and Weson six-shooter pistol) Every man is intent on meeting his maker, it's just a matter of where and when. Now I'm not much for sending you to meet him on a dry Sunday morning, but then again...that's your choice, not mine.
(We hear a the sound of a cocking revolver behind Eddie) VOICE: Maybe you'd like to meet him at the gates of Hell, GOOD EDDIE. EDDIE: On a day I haven't come lookin' for you, here you are findin' me instead. Pedro...
PEDRO: Who you're lookin' for is none of my concern. Turn around. (Eddie slowly turns around, his hand still pointing the gun at the bartender) EDDIE: I'd consider dropping your gun, 'less you're intent on killing two men... PEDRO: You're in no position to gamble...I call your bluff. On this day, your life belongs to me. PETEY!
(Little Petey walks in, holding a blonde woman who's been gagged, her hands tied up as well)
PETEY: Muchos gracias, Eddie. EDDIE: Now we've got four men in this room. Only three are gonna die though... PEDRO: Shutup Eddie, you're finished! We've got Blondie, and we've got you! You're finished!
PETEY: And don't worry about the woman, amigo. I already raped her an hour ago. She bleeds real nice...HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!
EDDIE: Well then the Good Lord won't mind if I shoot her too, save myself a hostage? PEDRO: It's over Eddie! (The two men stare at each other for a second) (Eddie movies his gun towards Pedro, causing Pedro to fire. However, Eddie drops down on his ass and fires at Pedro. Pedro's shot kills the bartender, and Eddie's shot kills Pedro. Eddie's still on his ass.)
PETEY: THINK TWICE, EDDIE, THINK TWICE! I have your b*tch...it's all over! EDDIE: With the drink man dead, and your friend as well, that makes two... PETEY: SHUTUP! EDDIE: And one of us makes three... PETEY: SHUTUP! EDDIE: But I haven't decided yet on shooting Blondie. PETEY: You won't have to, 'cause I'M gonna kill her! Get on your feet, SLOW, and drop your gun!
EDDIE: (Getting up) I don't think I'll shoot Blondie...at least not today. I'd rather shoot you, Petey...
PETEY: Your next move's your last! EDDIE: (Spins his gun upside down) Well I've given up my gun...now what are you gonna do?
(The two men stare at each other a second) (Eddie spins his gun back upright, aims at Petey. Petey shoots the same time as Eddie; Petey misses and hits a glass in the back, Eddie shoots and kills Petey.)
(Eddie takes the gag away from Blondie's mouth) EDDIE: I suppose nothing's stopping me from killing you now... BLONDIE: I suppose... (Eddie points his gun at Blondie's forehead) BLONDIE: He'll be in town a day from tonight...Pulsar that is. (Eddie spins the pistol away from Blondie's head, puts it back in its holster.) EDDIE: Clean this place up, it looks like hell... (Leaves) (Music cues) (Credits roll while the camera is still on Blondie, who drops to her knees and puts her head in her hands.)
