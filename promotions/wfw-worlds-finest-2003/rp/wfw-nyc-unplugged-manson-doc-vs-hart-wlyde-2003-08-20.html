WFW NYC Unplugged: Manson & Doc vs. Hart & Wlyde

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 20 August 2003 The winning team will face off against each other on the next show for a shot at the WFW World Heavyweight Title at the PPV. Cut your promos for the match here. All RP/Promos are due on Friday, August 29th at 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Manson — 21 August 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in plain black t-shirt and jeans, stands still, his arms crossed in a plain background without adornment or color. It is simply gray and he stares ahead, his face stoic.))
MANSON: Normally, I'm a fan of irony. More often than not, I play a large role in it. Which is why, it is not lost on me now. I can team with Doc Silver, I've done it before and I'll do it again if I have to. Winning brings me merely an abbreviated version of what I really wanted from the WFW.
A match for a title shot against Doc Silver is the not same as having the title match with Doc Silver. No, instead the first time, Shawn Hart was added and naturally, he loses the match. Then I have to watch as some backward remedial redneck who actually calls himself "Psycho" won the title. He lost, sure we all thought that, and now Shawn Hart, a man who leads a group called C.O.C.K.S. wins. Furthermore, he gives the BAD title, one I helped create, to a generic Hispanic rip-off of me.
Instead, I'm given a title, one I never wanted but decided to keep anyway for the sake of seeing if I could make something of it. But instead, because of me being me, the person who teamed with Doc before, who crucified men and mocked their livelihoods, every third-rate pissant who wants to get ahead fast without sucking off management, comes after me.
We have Rat Fink.....pretensious and at best, can only be taken seriously while drunk..Scotty Michaels going through a mid-life crisis....uni-sexual strippers like Luster,the before-mentioned Diablo..and meaningless bottom feeders like Eian who couldn't even beat Diablo.
And I...I am very, very tired of it. I imagine this is what Doc feels like some of the time. I never wanted to prove myself or use him to get ahead..no..I didn't need to...and I had my own selfish, self-indulgent reasons for doing so.
And now it appears, I'll have to find new, selfish-self-indulgent reasons. Because this can't be allowed to keep going on. Not for Alex Wylde..no..I'm going to have cut him at the knees before he can start...and not Hart so he can soldify himself as champion...and not for Doc as a partner or whatever else for whatever reason.
This will stop because MICHAEL MANSON cannot be seen as a stepping stone. He is to be seen as the end..the apocalypse....blood..hate.....Whatever description as long as you get the point.
If anyone makes a name off me again, it will be for entirely different reasons. ShawnHartXXX — 29 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-29-03 AT 12:57&nbsp;PM (EST)](The Scene: Mass hysteria at the penthouse in Orlando as the NEW WFW World Heavyweight Champion, Shawn Hart, along with his personal assistant/bodyguard, Tiny, and several floozies, are popping the tops off of vintage bottles of sparkling Crystal Pepsi...1993, in celebration of the Phenom's amazing victory last week. Cut To: A close-up of the WFW's top dog (who's decked out in an imported snakeskin suit), as he finishes gulping down a swallow of his soda delight...)
Hart: "And soooooo it came to pass that FINALLY... at loooooong last, GOOD has conquered EVIL and the WFW's very own suh-suh-suh-SUPAH HERO has suh-suh-suh-saaaaaaaaaved the day!!!"
(Hart drapes the WFW title belt over his shoulder. Tiny then steps into the shot...) Tiny: "Waaaaaaaaylll-uh hipandippety-dah, Shawny-HA! Uh-whoopa-whoptus dayum day dah dooey!!"
Hart: "Tiny, my friend, I couldn't have said it better myself, because in CASE you haven't noticed... the C.O.C.K.S. have PENETRATED that musty old shell that's held the WFW down... aaaaaaaand, in doing so, extended to lengths that have NEVER been reached before!!"
Tiny: "Hot dayum!!" Hart: "You bet your ass, brother! Buuuuuuuut it doesn't end there, OH NO! Y'see Tiny, guys like Doc Silver and Michael Manson may have rested their laurels on simply winning titles, but me... LE PHENOM?? ¿¿DIAAAAAAABLO FUEGO?? We've got bigger fish to fry! See, Diablo and I NOT ONLY set out to snatch the geratrics' straps, but to take said straps...and carry them to the next level. We're not satisfied with being the best in da bidness today... we wanna be the best this business has EVER SEEN!! *he chuckles to himself* ...Now, I don't like putting words in the mouths of others, especially when said mouths have the awe-inspiring ability to breathe fire, but I think it's pretty safe to assume that the BAD-ass himself is on the same page as me with this one!"
(As Hart sips some more of his Pepsi Cristal, DIABLO FUEGO emerges onto the scene.) Tiny: "D'ablo Don DAY!! Whe'dem dayum snatcher-snatchin' dem hoes fo a peesa boot-eh?!" Hart: "Come come Tiny, unlike yours truly, my fine latino friend here needs not the comfort of a woman..."
(Hart is bombarded by the floozies, Diablo breaks his silence...) Diablo: "I prefer my women don't stink of Pepsi..." Hart: "BAH! Why so glum, chum? After all, you're but 4 hours north of home!" Diablo: "That's CUBANS, Shawn..." Hart: "Soooooooo?" Diablo: "I'm Puerto Rican!" Hart: "What's the difference?" Diablo: "Well................. ...Maybe...... *he pauses* ...My people are automatically citizens, so no need to assemble crude pontoon boats with a truck and some oil drums"
Hart: "My friend, it's become painfully obvious to me that you're faaaaaaar too sober for a gathering such as THIS... if that sick mind of yours can concauct witticisms such as THAT. Why don't you go over and knock down a couple Pepsi's!"
Diablo: "What? Pepsi doesn't have any..." Hart: "Not another word! TINY... show this guy how to live!!" Tiny: "Wubbus-pow-pom!!" (Tiny drapes his arm over Diablo's shoulder and leads him toward the refreshments. Just then, a tabloid reporter approaches the Phenom...)
Reporter: "Shawwwwwn, baby! Tell us about your new title!" Hart: "New? NEW?! No, no, no, no, no, no, no... There's nothing NEW about this title. I've aaaaaalways been the one and onliest man deserving of such distinctions. The only difference now is that I've got the hardware to enforce the facts. It's kind of like..... a driver's license. Actually being licensed to drive isn't a little card or something tangible, it's more of a privilege, a STATUS you reach. So the way I look at it, this belt is nothing more than the physical representation of a status I attained long ago, minus the goofy picture."
Reporter: "Rrrrrrrrrright, rrrrrrright... and how about your upcoming tag match at Unplugged?"
Hart: "My tag match? Puh... I dropped Doc Silver like a bad habit at the pay-per-view, juuuuust like I always do. So, with that being the case, I don't see any reason that lil' streak could POSSIBLY come to an end here. And as far as Manson goes, he may think he has the pull around this place to spread that anti-C.O.C.K.S. rhetoric he's shootin', but lets face facts... dude's old news. I'm the WFW World Heavyweight Champion! And how's about Diablo?! The guy that Manson claims is nothing more than a generic, hispanic rip-off... what about him?! He's got the BAD title! All that being said, Mr. Manson, I pose for you this one, simple question... what do YOU have? What have YOU done recently besides disgrace yourself and continue that inevitable plunge down a ladder you once topped? Heh, factor in the addition of one Alex Wylde to the match, the same Alex Wylde who performed so exquisitely in putting Doc in his place, I'd say we have better chances of seeing the Detroit Tigers win the world series than we do seein' you JACKHOLES win this match! Like it or not, that's how the cookie crumbles!"
Reporter: "Excellent, excellent... So now that you've won the world title... and all but garunteed victory on TV this week, what's up next for Shawn Hart?"
Hart: "Big things, brah... BIG THINGS!! Ya see, guys like Manson, Doc Silver, Gideon, Scotty Michaels, and just about every other guy in this company, may think that they've seen all there is to get from the C.O.C.K.S. Head, but as us big whigs like to say, the C.O.C.K.S. Head is HARD to BEAT! In other words, don't get too comfortable kiddies, becauase the WORST has yet to come... and it all starts at Unplugged!"
Reporter: "Whatever are you referring to?" Hart: "THAT... I cannot say, but if I do recall correctly, we've got an election underway."
Reporter: "Are you referring to the WFW presidential election?" Hart: "I'm not referring to ANYTHING, I'm just here to bust moves to the grooves, nnnnnnndaddio! So why don't you scoot along so I can actually ENJOY my own party! The PHENOM...... has left........ the BUILDING!!"
(Fade Out) Manson — 30 August 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black t-shirt and jeans, sitting on a wooden bench in a dark part of the locker room. His hands are folded in a prayer like gesture and he sits still, stoic.))
MANSON: So he askes what do I have.....an interesting question..especially considering the source...so to reward this newfound perceptiveness I feel somewhat obliged to give him an answer, or as best as one as I can approximate.
I have my health, as some people say, but in years time I'll probably be a cripple with 6 different forms of cancer, so we'll skip that. I'm already an old man in a younger man's body to begin with.
I always have my ample supplies of pez..but not everyone sees that as the rightful treasure it is. So, we'll take that off the list and move forward.
Well..there's myself....and that can be enough. And being me, a few things come with that. First, I do know 101 ways to flay a person, especially you, Shawn. After all, everytime we come anywhere near each other in a ring I murder you like a Christian in the Roman Coliseum.
I know all I have to do is win this match, and then another, and I have another title shot against you, my frequent victim. Also, I know about lackies. I know how they think, I know how you would use them. I should after all, with the number of cults, gatherings, and free masons I've collected over the years. And if you really think a group called C.O.C.K.S. can stop me, you deserve castration. Probably the female version, come to think of it.
I know Diablo was handed a title, not just any title, a title that I helped create and make into something that another would yearn for. And I know his aim with a fireball could use a lot of work.
We're not on the best of terms right now, but I know Doc Silver. Even if he's silent, I know what he'll do, when he'll do it. All in all, I have a pretty good of what to expect from him. Something you're lacking in your own partner, Shawn.
But allow me to enlighten you to the first presidential candidate, Alex Wylde. He likes to create chaos, he likes to enfuriate, he also likes to take money to run errands. A hitman, a mercenary, whatever you like. I should know, since I've paid him off in the past.
So ask yourself now, did I do it again? Or maybe...just maybe..when I ask him to do something...he doesn't always need a price....
To finally answer your question....aside from years and years of cynicism and accumulated useless knowledge of ways to injure people...I'm always me. And I don't need a title for that. Having one though, is always nice.
