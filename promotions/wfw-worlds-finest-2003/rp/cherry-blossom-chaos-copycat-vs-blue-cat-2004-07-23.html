WFW — Roleplay

Cherry Blossom Chaos: Copycat vs. Blue Cat

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 23 July 2004 If Copycat wins the match, he will win a world heavyweight title shot as declared by Edmunds.
Remember, all RP conducted for Cherry Blossom Chaos should be from various locations in the Washington, DC Area.
The RP/Angle deadline is Friday, August 6th 11:59PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
GARTHIsTheLaw — 29 July 2004 (Cueup: "Look Out Below" by Closure) (Fade in on the Lincoln Memorial. Though the sound of a tourist or two milling around can be heard, it is mostly silent. The camera pans down from the top of the monument to its stone base, against which Copycat leans. He wears jeans, his trademark beret, and an inside-out black T-shirt. He looks at the ground as he begins to speak)
Copycat: For months now, I've been shot rumors about a title shot in the works for me. Ever since I was the last man eliminated from the So Cal Rumble...the fact that I eliminated everyone I held a grudge against? The fact that I eliminated more people than anyone else? The fact that there was something inherently unjust about one of the three people it took to eliminate me having already been thrown out? I'm not one to judge. I don't decide who gets title shots and who doesn't. And nor, it seems, does anyone whose opinion matters these days. I can't count the number of people who've said I deserve a title shot. And yet...
(Copycat looks up at the camera) Copycat: The man who would LEAST like to see me succeed is the man with the power to grant me that title shot. It's very flattering that people like Brandon Jacobs think I deserve a title shot, and I at least get the sense that I'm finally starting to get some of the respect I deserve...but not from anyone who MATTERS. Sean Edmunds is, tragically, the one with the power. And even though I've been promised a title shot if I win my match at Cherry Blossom Chaos, I'm skeptical.
(Copycat glances up at the memorial, then back at the camera) Copycat: I don't think it's so paranoid of me to doubt Edmunds' sincerity when he promises me a title shot with so few strings attached. After all, wasn't I supposed to get a match with Sean himself a month or so ago? All I had to do was beat Larry Tact, I was told. So I did. And not only that, Edmunds was to face me if he beat Pulsar, which was no difficult task. So that makes two guarantees that I'd finally get a chance to prove to the world what I've been saying for years -- that I am a better wrestler, in all regards, than Sean Edmunds. But Sean had the power. And he knew that a one-on-one match with me would mean I'd get my proof...so he came up with the most ridiculous solution and, just because he could, he implented it. This, then...
(Copycat shoots a bored glance at the camera) Copycat: ...This shows us that Edmunds can and will do anything to further his own ends. And that brings us back to my World Heavyweight Title shot. He may have promised me the shot if I beat Blue Cat, but if I know Edmunds, he's just hoping I'll lose. If I win, he could easily find a way to take my title shot away. Or maybe twist his words so it looks like he didn't change anything. What if he meant a shot at the BAD World Heavyweight Title instead? I just had a match involving both Jared Wells, the current champion, and Iron Lion, the potential next champion. And that's just one possible scenario. I can't imagine all the other ones. My mind won't stoop to such depths.
(Copycat turns and looks up at the memorial) Copycat: Honest Abe, they called him. An honest president is something I could use right now. I could say a thing or two about the honesty of the country's current president, but I'll hold off. The fact is, the WFW president gives me enough dishonesty to worry about. I might expect to beat Blue Cat, but my expectations of a World Heavyweight Title shot are a bit lower.
(Copycat glances back at the camera) Copycat: But I'll not despair. (Copycat turns to face the camera again) Copycat: Because while I am seeking a title shot, I'm also seeking something else. Something I haven't had as much difficulty obtaining. Something, in fact, that I've managed to get from some of the most UNLIKELY candidates. The most recent donor was Jared Wells. When I made him tap out at WWF Pandamonium. There was no room for sixth-grade toilet humor, no room for perceived controversy, no room for jokes stolen from Chappelle's Show. Jared Wells had no choice but to submit to the pain and give me the RESPECT that I demanded from him.
(Copycat's expression darkens) Copycat: Even a man like Jared Wells, who seems to have no capacity for respect, learned what he needed to do if he wanted to survive an in-ring encounter with the Cat. And he had the honor of being the first victim of the Scratching Post, a move that can teach respect to ANYONE. If I beat Blue Cat, will I get the World Heavyweight Title shot Sean Edmunds promised me? Maybe. But title shot or no title shot, I WILL walk out of Cherry Blossom Chaos with Blue Cat's respect. He may be no more than Sean Edmunds' lackey, but when he gets in that ring with me, he will LEARN to give me the respect that I command, just like Jared Wells did.
(Copycat leans forward) Copycat: At Cherry Blossom Chaos, one way or another...title shot or no title shot...promise kept or promise broken...I WILL get the RESPECT...that I deserve.
(Fade out) TheOriginalSE — 5 August 2004 (FADEIN: A huge campaign poster with a picture of Blue Cat licking himself. The writing says "BLUE CAT / EDMUNDS: Keeping the WFW clean!" There is no one in the audience despite the applause track. Sean Edmunds walks up to the podium and raises his hands, as if to tell the crowd to quiet down. The applause track cuts ... just dying off fast.)
EDMUNDS: "Ladies and gentlemen, as YOUR President, I am obligated to make sure that everyone in World's Finest Wrestling is always feeling their best. Because a happy employee works the most efficiently. That is why I gave Larry Tact that boost of confidence when his career was sagging. That is why I am being lenient with LOVE ... and that is why I am giving Copycat his long awaited Heavyweight title shot just for showing up to his match w/ Blue Cat. (flashes a cheesy grin) And my protege, Blue Cat, is also following in my foot steps .. or in his case, my paw prints. (Fake laugh track) But seriously, my friends, Blue Cat has learned from the best.. myself. Who better to coach him on all things Copycat than myself. Out of all the years that we've been around, I figured that sooner or later Copycat would eventually work himself up to the main event status and there'd be a match between us. Unfortunately, he hasn't taken that step yet... but don't worry, Copycat ... HELP IS ON THE WAY!"
(CUEUP: Fake cheers. Edmunds flashes a grin, and raises an eyebrow.) EDMUNDS: "Blue Cat and I have learned that the only way to truly raise the bar in the WFW was to institute harsher rules ... but also a loving hand to guide all the confused young ones out there to the promised land. Copycat may be stuck in the status quo .. but he will soon find out why Blue Cat is one of the most impressive protege's I have ever trained! Why, do you ask, is Blue Cat so impressive? Because he has mastered the one skill that I always find to be important ... and that is being able to bring your opponent up a notch, even for one night only. Ask anyone .. Richard Gideon .. the so-called longest reigning Television champion. I beat him. Wasn't hard.. but I left a mark on that poor kid's heart .. and he used my inspiration to go on to bigger and better things. Sure he's back to being a nobody, but no one lasts forever. Not even I.. as sad as it sounds."
(A graphic shows up on the bottom with a 900 number asking for donations.) EDMUNDS: "Ask Larry Tact. Another one who was stuck in mid-card land! After a match with Sean Edmunds, he went on to become a World Champion in another league ... It is all because I looked around the WFW .. saw that there were those who were less fortunate than I was, and helped them out. That's what this promotion was made to do. To elevate. To nurture. We do not succeed by turning our guys into whiners. To have them fill the airwaves with their calls of reform and higher pay.. That's why I chose Blue Cat.. Blue Cat will begin the final stage of his apprenticeship at Cherry Blossom Chaos.. He will attempt to raise Copycat to a higher level ... I began the cycle .. I gave Copycat his title shot ... but now Blue Cat needs to finish it."
(Pictures of Blue Cat in all sort of family photos, with Sherri and Edmunds, start flashing on the screen.)
EDMUNDS: "So throw us your support, ladies and gentleman. Send a message that the politics of despair ... of negativity ... of whininess has no place in World's Finest Wrestling. And as Blue Cat is out there at Cherry Blossom Chaos ... show your support by making anti-Copycat signs ... by chanting BLUE CAT over and over again. Hell, dress up like Blue Cat and run in and switch places for all I care ... but make sure you let Copycat know that he cannot continue to cry wolf. He claims that no one respects him .... and its because he doesn't respect himself! Once he learns to respect himself ... he can respect the WFW ... and he can earn the respect of the only two people who really care for him; 'Simply Sensational' and Blue Cat. It's coming .. all the tall tales that Copycat's been weaving are going to fall apart ... and you're going to see how a TRUE leader follows through w/ his plans! Thank you all for joining me .... BLUE CAT / EDMUNDS in 2004 .... because WE ARE KEEPING the WFW CLEAN FROM ALL TYPES OF TALENTLESS WASTE!"
(CUEUP: Applause track on repeat.... Finally, a portrait of Blue Cat's face comes on screen.)
EDMUNDS: "And remember ... don't adjust your television sets .... I *am* this sensational!"
(FADEOUT: As Blue Cat runs across the stage wearing a graduation outfit ... he tries to lick Edmunds' face, but he pushes him off.)
GARTHIsTheLaw — 6 August 2004 (Cueup: "Promises" by Adema) (Fade in on a street somewhere in Washington, DC. A group of protesters, complete with T-shirts and signs, are visibly angry about something President Bush has done as they walk by the camera. The camera then pans back to show that it is in an alley just off the street. The camera continues panning until we see Copycat leaning against a wall, watching the protesters. He wears jeans, his trademark beret and a black T-shirt)
Copycat: I've never been a big fan of back alleys, but I couldn't let the protesters see me. Those guys see a camera and you'll never get them away from it. Agree or disagree...I need my time to speak.
(Copycat turns to look at the camera) Copycat: More than any president I can remember, George W. Bush has drawn an enormous amount of animosity with his actions as present. Nixon was just before my time, Reagan was a little too entertaining to hate, and Clinton knew exactly what to say to get out of any trouble. Everyone makes mistakes, but George W. Bush's -- for whatever reason, I'll not get into my own political beliefs -- have received more focus than those of any president I've seen. Those protesters out there, they've got a specific reason. They've got something to put on their signs. But what? Well...let's examine the reasons why George W. Bush has been so maligned.
(Copycat raises a finger) Copycat: Number one, he's associated with some questionable people. I won't go into the whole Fahrenheit 9/11 ties-to-the-bin-Laden family argument, because that's all kind of spiky. But some of his other connections are a little on the shady side. Ashcroft has been known to get off-kilter on some pretty weird issues, Colin Powell has really gone downhill in his willingness to make a difference, and Cheney, Bush's own VP, is all over the board on this one...the oil thing, the stress thing, the "go f(BLEEP)k yourself" thing. Not to mention the rumors that Bush is following his daddy's agenda in Iraq. Before you can even look at what Bush has done or might have done, you can look at what the people around him have done.
(Copycat raises another finger) Copycat: Number two, you've got the status of his promises. A lot of Bush's campaign promises came in fairly obvious generalities, but anytime you want to appeal to the American people, you have to sacrifice some complexity, so we can let that slide a little. But his promises since taking the office have endured a bit of a pounding. We still haven't found Osama bin Laden, the war on terrorism seems to have only made the terror threats stronger, and our expedition into Iraq has yet to produce weapons of mass destruction OR ties to Al-Qaeda. One of the most popular reasons to dislike Bush's only real opponent, John Kerry, is to accuse him of flip-flopping. A favorite example is that Kerry voted for the war but then voted against "supporting the troops." In reality, though, Kerry voted for the war under certain conditions -- notably, that we would have the U.N.'s approval before doing anything rash -- and when Bush didn't hold up his end of the bargain, Kerry changed his standpoint. Now, Bush has still got some time in office to come through on those promises, so it would be unfair of me to pass judgment. But when it seems like promises are getting broken, the American people take notice, and THAT'S what we're really talking about here.
(Copycat raises a third finger) Copycat: Number three, you've got his verbal skills. This is one of the most popular reasons to make fun of Bush. The guy, let's face it, slips up a lot when he's speaking. And when he does speak without mistakes, he's not always all that convincing. No matter what his intents, though they may be good, he does have a tendency to say the wrong things. And even when the casual observer knows what he really MEANS, the slightest error here can pique that observer's interest.
(Copycat raises a fourth finger) Copycat: And finally, you've got his actions. Love 'em or hate 'em, things like the Patriot Act, the war in Iraq, the shunning of the U.N. and the gay marriage issue have gotten the American people real riled up. These are sensitive issues, and if not handled correctly, they can really hurt the man's reputation.
(Copycat drops his hand down to his side) Copycat: Copycat, the political aficionado? I won't claim to be an expert here. I'll even come right out and admit that I could easily be misinformed on any number of the issues I've raised here. Whether I'm right about the things I've said President Bush does, I can't be one hundred percent sure. Questionable associations, questionable promises, questionable speeches, questionable actions. Has Bush committed these sins? Maybe. But I'd be better off, I'm sure, talking about a president who HAS committed these sins.
(Copycat glances at the ground) Copycat: It doesn't take a genius to figure out who, I hope. (Copycat shoots a bored look at the camera) Copycat: The president of the United States deals with an untold number of people each day. But the president of World's Finest Wrestling deals with a much more select few. And when he makes mistakes, I damn sure know for CERTAIN that he's making them.
(Copycat, an annoyed look crossing his face, raises one finger) Copycat: Questionable associations. You had Alex Wylde, then it was Sensational Sherri, now it's Blue Cat. Blue Cat as a campaign partner? Blue Cat brings NOTHING to the table. He's going to bring the party down. While Sean Edmunds COULD be putting his own image on Cherry Blossom Chaos, he's sending Blue Cat to lose in his stead. Sean Edmunds knows he can't beat me. So he figures if I take out Blue Cat, it'll postpone the inevitable just a bit longer.
(Copycat raises a second finger) Copycat: Questionable promises. Elevating deserving talent? When has Edmunds EVER done that? Who has he elevated? Michael Manson was already up at the top, and lo and behold, he's still there. L.O.V.E. are all over the airwaves, even though they haven't done anything of even the slightest significance. Felix Red as number-one contender? Does ANYONE really think he's going to win? It's a joke. And who else has been elevated? Star power has only DECREASED since Edmunds took office. Alex Wylde is gone, Shawn Hart is nowhere to be seen, Scotty Michaels has been off TV practically the whole time, El Arco Iris is stuck in meaningless match after meaningless match, and me? No title shot, no match with Edmunds, just a filler match against a useless opponent with no guarantee of benefit for me.
(Copycat, his anger rising, raises a third finger) Copycat: Questionable speeches. Sean Edmunds' latest promo was the most half-assed, haphazard thing I've seen from him in years. He doesn't care about this match one way or another. He knows Blue Cat is going to lose, and the only thing that matters is that it won't be HIM losing. Pathetic.
(Copycat raises a fourth finger) Copycat: Questionable actions. What has Sean Edmunds done? ANYTHING? Alex Wylde was a terrible president too, but at least he got WFW some press. Edmunds does nothing but sit in his office, watch "The Fifth Wheel" and touch himself. He tried to run the WWL into the ground, and he damn near succeeded. Now he's doing the same with WFW. And the way I see it, that can be indicative of only one thing.
(Copycat drops his hand back to his side; both hands are balled into fists) Copycat: Sean Edmunds has NO RESPECT for WFW. None. At all. No man with respect for what he does would let L.O.V.E. think they run the show, or let Manson be forever the only person with any opportunity, or back out of a match and replace himself with a joke that's only funny to him. I know Sean doesn't respect me, and I don't really expect him to. But to have no respect AT ALL -- that's inexcusable.
(Copycat glances at the street) Copycat: Those people out there, they protest with signs and T-shirts and chants because it's all they can do. It's generally accepted as the way to get your point across in America. But in WFW, things are a bit different. I'm looking to do some protesting of my own at Cherry Blossom Chaos, but it won't be with a sign or a T-shirt or a chant. At Cherry Blossom Chaos, I will protest using the technique generally accepted as the way to get your point across in pro wrestling -- pain. I will show to Sean Edmunds and the world that I won't swallow his brand of bullsh(BLEEP)t anymore, and if I have to tear Blue Cat limb from limb, so be it. Jared Wells learned what it was like to be my Scratching Post at WWF Pandamonium, and Blue Cat is going to learn at Cherry Blossom Chaos. If I can't get my hands on Sean Edmunds...what I will do to Blue Cat will inspire in him the respect I command.
(Copycat picks up a sign from the ground; it was off-camera before. It's dark and at a bad angle, so we can't read it)
Copycat: Whether you protest with a sign or a slap, the spirit is the same. When someone who should know to have respect loses it, it's up to those affected by that loss to step up and make their concerns known. In November, I will vote to get my respect as an American. But at Cherry Blossom Chaos, I will fight to get my respect as a wrestler. And one way or another, I WILL get the RESPECT...
(Copycat shoulders his sign, which reads "BUSH DOES NOT RESPECT AMERICA") Copycat: ...That I deserve. (Copycat walks down the alley to the street to join the protest) (Fade out) TheOriginalSE — 6 August 2004 (FADEIN: A roundtable. Sean Edmunds is sitting there, cardboard cutouts of WFW wrestlers around him. Behind the table is a BLUE CAT / EDMUNDS sign, with the campaign slogan saying "CLEANING UP the WFW!" Edmunds smiles as someone tells him the cameras are rolling. Blue Cat, Blue Lioness, and Sherri are nowhere to be seen.)
EDMUNDS: "Welcome. I am 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds, YOUR WFW President. It has come to my attention recently that there seems to be a little tension between Copycat and the world. All we have heard the past few months are his claims of a lack of respect in World's Finest Wrestling. I will not let this misrepresentation tarnish our wonderful image. It would seem that our comrade Copycat has a terrible diagnosis of bipolar. A few months ago, he was the happy go lucky guy that we all knew and loved ... and today (pauses) .. today he is a shell of his former self .. he has become a victim of greed ... but fear not! As YOUR WFW President, I always look out for those under my cont... rul... er .. jurisdiction. While we are unsure of what has triggered Copycat's descent .. we are going to try our hardest to help him out of his mental rut. That is why Blue Cat, yes that lovable little blue hairball, has taken my place at Cherry Blossom Chaos."
(Edmunds turns to one of the cardboard cutouts and looks at it.) EDMUNDS: "Well, yes, the original reason Blue Cat was put in the match was because Copycat and his devil friend Black Cat used to beat up Blue Cat ... and didn't give him the respect HE deserved .. but Blue Cat and I have managed to look past that. We realize that the problem never lied with ourselves ... but with Copycat. And that is why Blue Cat is willing to go into the ring ... and set Copycat straight. Blue Cat is a wonderful person. While he may not say much, and may not do much outside of the ring, he has a genuine heart, and hates to see anyone fail. We are hoping that it is not too late for Copycat."
(Edmunds turns to another cardboard cutout and cocks his head.) EDMUNDS: "Yes, that had me perplexed, too. Copycat has gotten so dilusional that he made mention of an Alex Wylde ... and a partnership I had with him... (shakes head) Copycat, my friend, who is this Alex Wylde that you talk about? What Presidency has he held? Surely not my own! For everyone knows that *I* am the only President that the WFW has ever, and will ever have! But lets make one thing clear, Copycat. I didn't TRY to run WWL into the ground ... I did. Ask yourself, Mr. #1 Draft Pick, how well you managed to try to pick up my mess.. how long did the WWL last after I stuck the final stake into her heart. (Smiles) We won't dwell on the WWL again .. for I know how you hate being attached to it ... as if it was your calling card. "COPYCAT ... FORMER WWL WORLD CHAMPION." ...."
(Edmunds stops, and smiles ... he writes something down on a piece of paper.) EDMUNDS: "I know what might help you feel better, Copycat. To finally get rid of that last ounce of WWL recognition, I'll instruct the WFW ring announcers to stop calling you a former WWL World Champion ... now .. since you haven't accomplished anything yet in World's Finest Wrestling, despite being here a year, I will have to come up with something that's nice enough so you don't feel like a complete failure.. "COPYCAT ... *THE* WFW MIDCARDER!" Is that better? I mean, you might as well be the best of something, right?"
(Edmunds points across the table.) EDMUNDS: "YES! EXACTLY! (chuckles) What a perfect point, comrade. I cannot believe I haven't thought of that before! To think, all this time I thought that Copycat's fall from grace was because of some assinine reason ... but in fact, now that you've mentioned it, I can finally see... Copycat's latest ploy ... and I do mean ploy, is all because he was sick of being compared to 'Simply Sensational'! He is the former WWL World Champion ... I am the former THREE time WWL World Champion ... I am the former WFW World Champion, the WFW President ... and Copycat ... Copycat is nothing in the WFW."
(Edmunds stands up.) EDMUNDS: "But thats ok ... because at Cherry Blossom Chaos, Copycat will have a chance to redeem himself. Blue Cat is an idealist ... and perhaps, after facing Blue Cat ... Copycat might just get back some of that sparkle that he once had before ... and that ... THAT is the beauty of America ... the beauty of the WFW."
(Edmunds slams his hands on the table.) EDMUNDS: "Don't adjust your television sets .... I *AM* this sensational!" (FADEOUT: As one of the cardboard cutouts falls over to teh floor ... Blue Cat runs into the picture and 'pounces' on Edmunds, knocking him into the table, and knocking the rest of the cardboard cutouts to the floor.)
