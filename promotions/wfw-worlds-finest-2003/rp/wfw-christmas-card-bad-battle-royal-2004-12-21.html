du5tin — 30 December 2004 "Do not go where the path may lead; go instead where there is no path and leave a trail." - Emerson [The scene opens to a simple backdrop that sports the name of World's Finest Wrestling after the quote is spoken softly. An unknown man steps into view and looks directly into the camera with the deadest face known to man; his smoke grey eyes are almost hidden upon his pale face.]
"I'm here to leave a trail indeed, but this trail will be of defeated men." [The pale, heavy individual lifts his hand and softly strokes his thick black beard. He is a ghostly individual indeed. His head is shaved and his whole body is covered in scars that tell stories of many battles, but the truth is, nothing is known about this "warrior."]
"I have been placed into a battle royal, a battle royal with men I know nothing of, yet I can only assume they're fragile and unaware of the hell I'm to put them through in a few days. Rat Fink, Golem, Richard Gideon, Aaron Rage, Mike Lane, Luster, and Adam Benjamin. I have already heard a few of you spit your thoughts and threats to me on WFW's Hype Center."
[He shakes his head and proceeds to speak.] "Luster... I wouldn't be too worried about El Barco. He is of no threat at this time. I'm the threat! You should be worried about my fist going into your face. I'm not too worried about you, kid. I'm sure you'll be tossed over the ropes or pinned shortly after the bell rings. If I were you, kid, I would pray that someone else finishes you off before I get my hands upon you. That is truly something you don't want to happen, I promise you, kid."
[He clears his throat and continues to speak about his debut.] "Aaron Rage... I don't see any threat from a man who lifts tree trunks and talks about 'Rage Blasts.' I'm not even going to ask what this 'Rage Blast' is. Rage, you give this business, this sport, this passion of mine a bad name. Rage, you have error written all over your face and in every word you speak. Rage, your muscle means nothing to me. I'll make sure I get my hands on you when we step into the squared circle. You are a defeated man!"
[He stops to collect his thoughts before speaking again.] "I'm not worried about this battle royal; I'm not worried about any of the men who are going to be in the ring with me. This promotion will see the destruction and hell I bring and am capable of after I destroy each opponent in this battle royal. Gold. Yes, I have my eyes set upon gold, and no unworthy man is going to step in my way of getting the shot I want. All 'roadblocks' will be brought down! You will be brought down! You will be destroyed!"
[He turns his head and then back to the camera.] "World's Finest Wrestling... ...Akira's Ghost is your new wrestling messiah!" [He doesn't slap the camera or walk away; the man now known as Akira's Ghost simply continues to stare into the camera with his cold, ghostly dead face. "South of Heaven" by Slayer begins to play as the camera slowly zooms in on his smoke grey eyes; the scene fades to static and then to an action figure commercial of the newest superstars of World's Finest Wrestling.]

(Fade to black) ChrisHorowitz — 2 January 2005 (A Black Screen. White text appears on it, a paragraph at a time.) This place has reached a nexus. A great change is coming. Finally, the weakness of the past will be replaced with the strength to destroy the pathetic legacy of this federation.
Only one man is capable of this great mission. There will no longer be time for levity. Drawn out philosophy and pointless angst belong to the old order. The chaos of the past is to be made obsolete by the discipline and order of this new era.
The sad self assured specimens in the gene pool of WFW exist as a tribute to this old order, and more reason for the return of dominance. No longer will there be doubt as to the conclusion of a contest. No longer will there be even odds.
There will be no style. The only substance will be the brute force of one man. No ideology motivates him. No mind games distract him. His only reason for being in the arena is victory. The crowd doesn't exist. Showmanship doesn't exist. Glory is meaningless. Only the triumph of the perfect specimen, victory for victory's sake is important.
The contest is not to be decided by camera movements, pained acting, or by eloquence. None of these factors are even a factor. They are simply distractions from underprepared, underdeveloped, worthless individuals.
This warning will continue in 24 hours time. Then zoom into room 112 where you see a Ohio State Duffel Bag thrown in front of a double bed that has the Top flower pattern protector sheet half way down the bed. On the bed sits Steve Johnson wearing red mesh shorts and a sleeveless black t-shirt. His defined arms sit holding a glass of milk in his right hand and a remote control in the other hand. His brown brow is furrowed as maneuvers the control and watches clips of all the promos so far for the Battle Royal. He takes a sip of milk puts the class down and begins to speak in the camera.)
Steve Johnson: Wow it seems like WFW is bringing out a lot of interesting other rookies for this big event, at first I was intimidated but now I am just confused. Some of these guys don’t seem to belong wrestling but seems like they should be at some County fair. At Ohio State before big matches, we feared for our safety, but here, I feel like I am standing staring at Frat Row, and these are all the guys that didn’t get bids.

He flips through it and goes to a dog eared page and puts his finger to a certain point and starts to read slowly annunciating the words.)
SJ: “Do not go where the path may lead……. Blah Blah Blah. Some dead guy named Waldo Emerson said that, what does Waldo Emerson have to do with wrestling? Not A THINGGGG! I went to college and guess what no one in the ring wants to know what some teacher taught me in college. At Ohio State I learned how to wrestle not recite poetry by some guy in a funny picture book that’s always lost. For that matter what is an Akira’s Ghost? Does that mean he’s dead? (He begins to get angry and start pointing at the camera) And calling himself the MESSIAH, where I’m from we take 3 things seriously, Ohio State Buckeyes, the tire factory, and our Lord and Savior. Well he will be the first to go down (throws the book and nods indignantly) and feel what Steve Johnson calls the “The Love of the Game.”

SJ: These other characters are mighty interesting, and in any other world would probably be good guys to introduce to my parents and have over for dinner, but come the match they to will have to go down.

Furthermore this (opens hands to quotation marks) “ Rage Bomb”( closes quotations) you speak of, that sounds like something you’d either get in the cafeteria after Tuna Casserole Tuesday’s, or something you’d injected into yourself to be successful like Barry Bonds. Either way people won’t stand for any of these conclusions. You too will go down for offending America with your excrement or your desire to cheat.

In the WFW no body knows me, but at the end of the night they will all be standing at their feet again, because in their hearts they will be filled with the opportunity to ride with a rookie on everyone’s ideal dream of success.

Your college is not of importance. Your greatness is not of importance. Your ability to bring salvation is of even less importance.
All that matters is that the end of these trivial conversations has arrived. Substance's triumph over style comes on Christmas day. Not that those who stand opposed to this have much in the way of style. Their hackneyed rhetoric is of the past, it quite possibly lacked style years ago, let alone today.
Sit in your drab surroundings, pout, exault the virtues of your life, do what you wish, but no words will aid you in your battle. Your only option will be to seek hiding, but when man seeks sanctuary from a greater being, this being will shout down to him that there is no hiding place. There is no hiding place beneath the rocks for the untalented masses. There is no hiding place in the hills for the technicians. There is no hiding place in the valley for those who can only speak loudly, but cannot carry the stick that the hero of the moment carries.
The unbriddled energy of this man cannot be understated. His opponents cannot understand his power. They cannot even understand themselves, which shows in their attempts to intimidate their opponents.
One does not show force through strange acts. He does not show superior strength through his academic pedigree. He does not show an advantage through any of what has been performed by these confused fools.
The moment draws closer, continue to prepare thoughtlessly, under the false hope it will be of help at the time of reckoning.

(Black Text, White Screen.) We are now overdue. Those who seek their own destruction will get the gift of defeat. Those who seek victory will have it snatched from them. Only WFW could have the foresight to place itself in such a position.
They have allowed a man who can radically change anything he touches in a place where he can rise to the top with unthinkable speed. It is not that he will become World Champion that is important. Titles are meaningless compared to the truth of his brilliance.
But what a title does, is it provides a symbol. A symbol of the strength of a man. A symbol of this new breed that exists on this planet. A symbol that the Homosapien has been replaced by Homosuperior.
A man who is more than a man. Not that he is a god, bu tthat he is not the same as the usual dregs that walk the path.
A man that stands head and shoulders above the pack. This is the man who will hold that title, and show the start of the new era, the birth of the next generation, which this time will cause the death of the old. No longer will the old outdated models be allowed to cling to life. Now they will be removed from production, and from the shelves.
Like professional hockey, the inferior will be shut out from our lives by the brute strength of the new enemy of mediocrity. The hush that falls upon the crowd as he unleashes the swift justice that will seem old to many, but new to those who receive it will leave many speechless.
The old will not be able to modify their style to handle this. The young will not have the experience to adapt to his power. The strong will not have the intelligence to outwit him. He is simply a package that is unlike any other that has come before.
With such a field as the one in this battle royale, it is hardly a test for the new model. But, while it may not push the system to its limits, everything that is new needs a starting point, no matter its simplicity.
This will be the starting point for the new model. History will be made here, and will be written in the flesh of the fallen. Their fall will not be in vain, it will light the way for the glory of his conquests.
