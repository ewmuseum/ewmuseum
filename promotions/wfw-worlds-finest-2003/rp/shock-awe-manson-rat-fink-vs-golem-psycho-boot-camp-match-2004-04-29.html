Shock & Awe: Manson & Rat Fink vs. Golem & Psycho (Boot Camp Match)

29 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 29 April 2004 The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, May 12th 11:59PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
Manson — 1 May 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, in black leather jacket and jeans sits Indian style on a bench with the WFW World Heavyweight title hanging on hook above and to the left of him, the gold plates facing the camera.))
MANSON: And there's that feeling inside.....no...its not knowing everyone fears me...that's more like a tangy taste on the tongue...and it's not respect because that's more like liking copper and being able to stand up straight. No, that's the feeling of knowing that you are superior to others and now having physical evidence of it.
((MANSON, without looking, reaches up and taps the title belt.)) MANSON: So now I am the World's Finest wrestler. Officially, literally, figuratively, metaphorically, musically, and so on and on. The first business at hand is another Shock & Awe and another bootcamp match. This time, RAT FINK, is not the man across the ring, but rather someone serving as my tag team partner. This didn't work out that well before, but breakdowns in communication and incidents occur.
A year ago, I wanted to run you down with a truck and sell your spine back to your family at a marked up price. While, I do not forget, or forgive for that matter, I am willing to work together for one match, so long as this time you keep your mind things and follow the lead of the World's Finest wrestler. This a Bootcamp match, you were the BAD World heavyweight champion, so was I. We should both be good at this. But, as always, I can improvise if things do not work as planned.
((MANSON again grabs the title belt and hangs onto it from his seated position.)) Now, Alex, I told you, didn't I? I said for months that not another soul would make their name off mine. Least of you, who owe their livelihood to me. What kind of a man are you, Alex? Having lost the world heavyweight title, on the verge of being kicked out of office, you have gone from the self-proclaimed most powerful man in wrestling to another contender.
Over the past year in the WFW, I lost everything, I failed, I was cheated...and I made breakthroughs and such. Because in the end, I was still myself, still MIKE MANSON through it all. Resilient. Indomitable. It's your turn, Alex. Are you still yourself through it all? Can you rise again? Well, not as long as I'm in the way, but so many have said the same.....and I'm not just referring to the various religious denominations I've toppled. We''ll see soon enough, I suppose.
((MANSON drops his grip and lets his hand hang loose.)) Your partner, representing the latest incarnation of villains uniting (which possibly dates back to the Injustice Society during World War II, but I digress) L.O.V.E., is Psycho. The more narrow-minded and naive of thinkers, by which I mean Psycho, might have the gall to think that there are similarities between us. But those all exist on the surface.
I was the North American champion, and quite a fine one, but Psycho just won the title and has yet to show anything, but he hasn't had a chance. I am the WFW World Heavyweight champion. So was he, but I'm going to be champion longer than a week. Given his track record, I believe it is safe to say in the long view, I was a better North American champion as well.
Psycho, predictably, likes to engage in violent bloodshed. So have I, though, I would hardly be the World's Finest wrestler if that was all I was capable of, or anything remotely resembling a person who exists in the three-dimensional world, so I haven't gone on mindless rants about how I can just hurt people since I was sixteen and eating steak on Good Friday on the church steps, (well, maybe I still do the latter). A word of wisdom, Psycho, telling someone in advance that you're going to assault only works if they really can't stop you due to be exposed to gamma radiation and being the Hulk or because you are much more intelligent. Given your name, I'd say avoid the threat altogether.
I'd be remiss if I didn't bring up your lack of talent in the area of names. "Psycho"? Did you fall asleep during the Norman Bates film or did you wade through a thesaurus and just give up? I surmise that L.O.V.E. wasn't actually something you came up with, since it's almost clever and can have double meanings. You, I'd imagine, would have come up with a name that some really bad nu-metal band would take on. Hatecore, Hardkore, with a "KAY", because that makes it EX-TREME.
In fact, that's not such a bad idea for you guys if this WFW thing doesn't work out. ANARKY seems the de facto leader and so he can be the frontman and give the vocals a try..you PSYCHO..you like to beat on things so you can be the drummer....FELIX RED seems like a bass player to me...and JARED WELLS...well..honestly, I don't know what the hell he can do and that normally means he'd play bass, but given that's taken, he can be lead guitar.
Hey, you can even have ALEX WYLDE as the manager. He was the president of the WFW. He knows how it's done.
See, my first week and already, I've improving the lives of everyone on the roster under me.
ChrisHorowitz — 11 May 2004 Sorry about the delay, I've been without a computer for weeks until a few days ago. (Fade into the head quarters of N.A.R. where Rat Fink sits in his office, the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS standing behind him.)
Rat Fink: Once again the forces who's time has come and gone have stripped the NAR of what we have rightly earned. And now the revolution is stuck in a loop of continued fighting in matches that will not lead to the final victory of the revolution.
The WFW is complicit in this disgusting conspiracy to keep the people and the revolution down. Keeping the true power of evil from triumphing and allowing the surreal hacks to continue to win through shady dealings. It is sickening to see, sickening to remember, and sickening to know they will try it again. But, there is always hope.
The hope that exists is the reality that the enemies of the Revolution will have to face their worst nightmare once they enter that ring.
That nightmare stands before them stands behind me. It is, THE WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS. So many have fallen because of the fear it puts in a man, so many more have fallen after seeing the opponent it has chosen for them. And even more have fallen in the ring, utterly overcome by their chosen opponent. So few can compete with one man on this board, let alone all of the possible opponents on the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.
And look at our opponents in this match. The revolution must face the surreal creation of Golem, a man I barely believe exists at this point, and a man who cannot dare face up to the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS. He will claim he is ready for anyone, but the truth is he cannot match any man on this wheel's skill.
The same with his partner Psycho. The truth is both of them could not defeat Michael Manson, who is half the man of any member of the N.A.R. How can they hope to challenge both of them? How can they hope to challenge them when only now will they know who the other half of both of them will be?
But the time has come to end the mystery, and to end their fear of this wheel. Perhaps the fear has been so great that they will not even show up despite who their opponent is, or perhaps their fear will grow after they see who that opponent is. But now it is the time to turn the possible to current reality.
(Rat Fink stands, and reaches out to grab the wheel. Effortlessly he spins it, and the wheel spins for a moment then slows, coming to a rest on none other than Rat Fink.)
I almost pity them. They now must face the spirit of the revolution, the head of the revolution, myself. The entire revolution exists in spirit inside me, reality itself powers me, while the surreal slows you Golem, and confuses your dull partner.
For your sake I hope your fear will cause you to miss this bout, for my power is too great for you to ever comprehend, let alone counter.
(fade out.) Vertigo — 12 May 2004 (Cue Up: "The Day The World Went Away" by Nine Inch Nails. Fade into the one they call Golem, sitting in an old rocking chair, swaying slowly, back and forth, with a half grin on his face. The face is barely recognizable, half because of age, half because some of you have just never seen him before. But, alas, there is he. He chuckles away at a joke that only he hears as he turns towards the camera, perhaps to let the rest of us in on it.)
Golem: And so the ever-spinning wheel of time has once again rolled Golem out into the center of the ring. This time it's a WFW ring, staring across from both a...familiar face and one I have seen only on tape. But before I start to probe the words and minds and Mansonisms of my opponents, I would like to digress about my return. I suppose some people are happy to see me back. I am sure some would like another crack at Golem, a chance to end my career for good. Others probably believe that their enemy's enemy is their friend, so they have a "buddy" who they can have watch their back. Both of these groups of people need to be snapped back into the reality that says Golem did not return to quench their thrist or your dreams. I am not here to fill in any happy moments or play some beautiful role in one of your fantasies. I am a swift iron hand of vengence that has returned to this place to rectify previous scars and gash new ones. Those who are angry or scared that Golem has arrived, smile now for you have successfully picked the correct emotions. Also, it is smart to smile now because soon enough, the smiling will be over and that just isn't any fun at all, is it? So there you have it, kiddies. The reason I am back...because what would this league or any other do without Golem? It would plagued by Mansons, Rat Finks, and Psychos and that would just be nauseating on all parts, wouldn't it?
(Golem chuckles to himself as he picks up still frames from some of his matches with Manson.)
Golem: And it's almost appropriate that Golem enter here with the same little monkey on his back that bothered him in his not so recent past. But history is history and I am here with a blank slate. None of your wounds linger I hope, as all of mine have long since eroded away. It's funny, isn't it? The people standing atop this league had someone drop out of a match with you and Rat Fink, perhaps out of cowardice, and did they go to another L.O.V.E. member? Did they? No. Did they even go to anyone who was even ON THE ROSTER? No. They went out and hunted Golem down, and in my first WFW action ever, I am staring across the ring from the world champion. Tells you something about the "level of competition" that stood between you and that title, Manson? Perhaps it screams that you may have beaten the best here and now they need fresh bodies to throw in your way, stalling you while they build up a super match between you and, lord I don't know, Psycho at the next Pay Per View. But that isn't a realistic answer. Golem has been given this match because they would like to give ole Psycho a fighting chance and that is always admirable. I suppose that if he does a top flight job of riding my coattails, soon enough he will find himself with a tag team victory over a champion and an ex-BAD champion....though I wouldn't rub that in as a huge accomplishment.
(Golem reaches down and picks up one of his old scrapbooks of old matches. There is all different different kinds of matches represented, and Golem looks dissatisfied.)
Golem: So...it's a Boot Camp match, eh? Don't see too many of them. From what I gather from tapes and whatnot, it seems to be one of those come as you are matches, and that sounds like a mighty fine idea to Golem. Zero disqualifications, zero excuses, three excuses for wrestlers, and a Golem. I am not one who is huge on parlor tricks, Rat Fink. You can pretend to be a game show host, err...contestant, whatever...or you can pretend to be a wrestler. I am not afraid of either. You speak about a revolution. The only revolutions I saw were the ones the wheel was making. But hey, it's inside you, this revolution, all metaphorical, so I get it, right? Or maybe I am horrificly incorrect in this. Maybe such a revolution does exist, despite the fact it will never get off the ground. But go ahead, pat yourself and your partner on your collective backs and see all the far it gets you. It won't result in my punches hurter any less or your pride hurting any less when Psycho and I inevitably win. I mean, you hate Manson, don't you? He has tried to run you over by his own admittance, and yet, you stand there molesting my ears with an "Ode To Michael Manson". It's odd, after all, that I should be such a minute amount as good as Manson and still have already beaten him. Something it seems you, Rat Fink, have a problem doing. After all, that is why you are joining him, isn't it? If you can't beat them, you create wheels of unfathomable opponents and false compliments so they fight your battles for you, is that it? Perhaps I am correct, perhaps not. Certainly Shock and Awe shall do well to reveal all. Golem, too, could speak in these riddles, but why? I shall save those for another time and when I have to. Perhaps you, my darling duo, shall fire back at my with great fury and I shall find myself winding words. But for now, it just is not worth it. You, my sweet pair, are in for being the first in Golem's return to glory. See you two in the boot camp. Reserve a little of that anger you guys have for each other...for a true challenge. Oh...and Psycho...just try not to get in my way. Stall one as long as is necessary. Golem shall do the rest.
(Golem chuckles as he camera fades to black.) Devil666 — 12 May 2004 (The camera opens on Psycho standing before a WFW backdrop. Over his shoulder is draped the North American title. a slight smile under is mask turns to a laugh as he faces the camera)
Psycho: Sorry for my absence, but I'm a very busy man. I've got a lot more \ (pats the belts with his hand) Psycho: commitments to worry about now. After all at The Superbowl of Wrestling. L.O.V.E. showed everybody what a true force in wrestling really is. We all had our agendas and we delivered. Anarky showed Maelstrom the door. I finally stopped toying with Ricahrd Gideon. Jared and Felix showed that we'll even fight each other to gain a goal. None of you stopped us that night and the future will be no different.
(He shakes a crooked finger) Psycho: I spent a year tearing down Gideon. I spent a year haunting and beating him. Finally I put him down. I have what I went after. The North American title is mine. Yet the WFW isn't happy. Scotty Micheals isn't happy. I bet nobody is happy with all of us holding gold. No...there's a smell of fear from all in the back. A stench greater then usual.
(Again he pats the title) Psycho: Now I was looking forward to defending my title. To prove what a champion I am. Yet while I smell people trying to conspire to get us I can't help but almost thank them for this meaningless match. After all there's nothing at stake for any of us. Hell I'm stuck with a partner I don't like and one that doesn't like me. Yet there is a shining incentive to being in this match. when the bell rings I'll be standing across the ring from you Manson. After all you’re the big champ aren't you. A title you went to great lengths to point out I once held.
(He nods his head) Psycho: Well you right. I beat Doc Silver at Cherry Blossom Chaos. I used every violent tendency I had to win. Here's the funny thing though. It's not what they wanted. The WFW doesn't want someone like me as Champion. You said it yourself. I know no other way besides violence. That's why I'm no longer the WFW Champion.
(He laughs loudly) Psycho: Funny thing is Manson. I was never beaten for that belt...I consider it stolen. See I was never pinned. these shoulder when never counted down for three. I never submitted to Doc Silver. Hell I was even the first person out of that cage. Yet the title was awarded to him. AWARDED!!! I wasn't beaten for it and as for my rematch. Well that never happened. I didn't get one and I dare suspect that I never will. That why I jumped at the chance to join L.O.V.E.. I was excited that I could raise hell in a league that doesn't want me around. Well I'm the North American champion...and they'll never get rid of me...or the rest of us.
(He pauses for a moment and his smile grows wide again) Psycho: So Mikey...you go ahead. You make fun of my name all you want. I'll tell you this though. that when it's all said and done. that when the Summer of L.O.V.E. turns to a winter of discontent for this league. Well plain and simple as my name might be. You and the rest of the WFW will never for it...or the rest of L.O.V.E.
(He pauses for a moment and again points a crooked finger at the camera) Psycho: As for you Golem. Don't worry...I won't get in mine. I'll give you ALL the respect in the world. Just make sure you earn it. As for the rest of you. Well I live for bloodshed and tonight's match. Well it's right up my alley, I'll see you soon
(He turns his back and walks away) <FTB> Manson — 13 May 2004 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, the World's Finest Wrestler, WFW World Heavyweight Champion, sits on a coffin, the WFW World title set next to him, and the end of the coffin is open, revealing piles of green apple jolly ranchers. MANSON has a hand full.))
MANSON: Golem, Golem, Golem, in a way I'm glad you're here now. I'm pretty sure when Alex Wylde gave notice on his leave of absence that they likely went out onto the street and found the first guy they saw to replace him who happened to like to fur, but still, you allow me to go on a tangent and mention Stanislaw Lem.
I'm sure that none of you of this fine author, but he did have a character named "Golem", who was a computer with a higher intellect and consciousness than man. Of course, this has nothing to do with the Golem in the match here, since it doesn't take much in the way of IQ to notice how stupid Psycho's name is, but Lem had another concept, "the Silent Universe". And, Psycho take note, while this name tells you exactly what it is, it sunds quite clever.
I stood around and waited for weeks for someone, any of you to respond. It was like walking around a vacuum. I heard Golem had returned and yet, took him so long to emerge and rant about how he had excuses for all the times I took him down like we were cellmates in prison. While I was throughly excited to hear what he thought of Rat Fink, I was diappointed, but then, that's our Golem.
Of course, I tried to run him down. He hasn't taken it personally, he's not the sensitive type like you, but we have to be tag team partners for this and you don't get far if you dwell on little things like vehicular man-slaughter. As for you seeming to imply that he's fallen into line on my side, I should add that this title belt resting near me means that I am...the world's finest wrestler...
And not you, Psycho. And, Psycho, you took everything I said out of context. I have no idea whether or not the WFW wanted you as a champion or not. They seem to want me as champion and I've corrupted more children than the FOX broadcasting champion. In fact, I can be as controversial and violent as you are. I'm just a lot smarter, after all, I'm not in LOVE. The four of you can run around and air promo's that seem like beer commercials, but not one of you has anymore control of World's Finest Wrestling than anyone else. And as for me....I'm hardly just anyone else. Not only am I the world's finest wrestler, not only am I the WFW World Champion, but I'm Michael Manson.
Think about this, Psycho, you're nothing more than Anarky's glorified lackey. And for years, all he ever was, was my glorified lackey. You were WFW champion for one week or less, I don't really know because I pay about as much attention to you as I do to Spike Lee. But I know you were beaten, outsmarted, which seems to be the norm.
But I'm hoping that as the North American champion, a title I helped make, that you can be more. That you can form a coherent plan and follow through on it. Golem can help you, he at least, seems to know how to read. At the very least, he can loan you some green fur. You like to play with knives and such, I'm sure you can think of something to do with that.
At the very least, I'm asking that for one night, you remember that you are facing the world's finest wrestler, the man more exciting than Jesus, the demonic Johnny Carson...and not allow this all to be just a hollow...silent universe...
