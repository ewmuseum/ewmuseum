WWF Pandamonium: Rat Fink vs. Golem vs. Pulsar

18 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 18 June 2004 Falls Count Anywhere in the Zoo. This is an elimination match and the winner gets the first shoht at the BAD World Heavyweight Title after the PPV.
This show is a special fundraider for the World Wildlife Fund at the San Diego Zoo. The RP/Angle deadline is Wednesday, June 30th 11:59 PM EST. Send all angles to [detail withheld]
stuclif — 25 June 2004 FADE IN: EARLIER IN THE DAY: PULSAR IS SHOWN TAKING A TOUR OF THE ZOO. IS SEEN PLAYING WITH TIGER CUBS, THEN WITH A HUGE PYTHON WRAPPED AROUND HIM.
PRESENT: PULSAR STANDS IN FRONT OF THE CAMERA WITH A HUGE EAGLE PERCHED ON HIS FOREARM. PULSAR: Now would be a good time to break out my classic Steve Irwin impression..... Now.. what we got here(LAUGHS)... nah, nah, I wont do that to the people at home... I probably sound too much like him already. (LOOKS TO THE EAGLE) There is something about it though, isnt there? Its strength doesnt need to be felt for you to know that it would be able to crush anything that gets caught between its powerful talons.
(QUICKLY THROWS ARM TOWARDS THE SKY. CAMERA FOLLOWS EAGLE AS IT TAKES AND SOARS TOWARDS A TREE AND LANDS ON A HIGH BRANCH)
PULSAR: (CAMERA BACK ON PULSAR) The birds eye sight allows it to sit high up there and wait for a small rodent, or anything else that might look tasty, to expose itself... it will then swoop down snap up the prey and take it back to its nest and either feed on it for itself or feed it to the young.
(PULSAR WALKSD OUT OF THE SANCTUARY, CLOSING THE DOOR BEHIND HIM) PULSAR: I envy that type of patience, to be able to wait for an opponent to expose their weakness, it would save the feeling out, the not really knowing whats coming next, but it also cuts out alot of the fun (SMILES). Golem, Fink, you both know how this is going to go down... I wont be standing back waiting for my chance, I'll be creating my own chances. I'll be fighting the only way I know how, the only way the fans have ever seen me... and that is full-go 110 per cent, with nothing being held back.
As confident as I am that I will be successful at WWF Pandemonium, I am also well aware that I am going to have work my arse off. These other two guys know just as well as me whats on the line... a shot at the BAD World Heavyweight. Fink, you have got to be desperate to get back what you had to cheat to hang to for so long. And Golem, a guy who is still looking for a win here and also a bit of revenge on my part (SMALL LAUGH). Gentlemen.. I think we're all going to have a great time.
In the last couple of months I have pinned two number one contenders for the WFW title, Michael Manson and Felix Red, two names not taken lightly in this industry. After Pandemonium, I will be a number one contender.. someone who who will take plenty of joy in stealing away one of the crown jewels in LOVEs crown, and giving Jared Wells a mass beat down in the process. But for now, lets cue the excitement... it is for charity after all.
Vertigo — 27 June 2004 (Cue Up: "I'm Looking Forward to Joining You, Finally" by Nine Inch Nails. Fade into a shot of the outside of Golem's home. It's night time, all dark out, and only a silhouette of the house can be made out. Cut to Golem, sitting on a wooden rocking chair by a window, staring down over a small hill. The single visible tree sways in the heavy breeze. A blackened stump sits beside the tree.)
Golem: "Do you see that tree out there? That tree used to not be alone. Another used to stand beside it. They would dance happily and sway together in the breeze. They would bend in similar fashions and occasionally fight eachother, their branches entangled in a simple war of nature. But then, the storm came. In thunder and lightning, the mighty tree stood victorious over it's once friend and now scorched wood. This might sound like a worthless flapping of lips to you two, or perhaps you realize how this is applicable to our contest. Golem is the mighty tree who remains. LOVE is dead wood. They weighed me down and now they are gone. The difference is, no one has said, "man, both those trees got struck by lightning." Meanwhile, people seem to be working under the impression that because Felix Red and Psycho are unable to keep their shoulders off the mat for three stupid seconds, I am a loser. It does not strike me as rocket science. Rat Fink beats Psycho, therefore Psycho lost. Not me. Pulsar beats Felix Red, therefore Felix Red is unfit to be the number one contender because Pulsar is horrible. I didn't lose. I threw Pulsar off the boat, for goodness sake! Felix Red, I stopped Copycat from pinning and I was off fighting Copycat. In reality, that was one more favor than I owed you. Copycat was the only half talented wrestler on that team and you proved that you cannot compete with half-talents. And now, you're off talking about how you will be the first to beat Manson ever in time. He got the title after a year of failing. I'll save my applause for later, thank you very much. People always want to find excuses and loopholes to slow me down and this is just a glorified example of that. If I really cared at all about the BAD world title, I would have joined BAD. A worthless title that was worthless before the league melted unto oblivion isn't my prize for winning this three way elimination match. Oh no, it's a shot at this title. So, I am supposed to really, seriously care about a match which results in a match which results in overhyped nothing? The strange thing is, oh, how I do. You see, I might sound like a Copycat here, pun intended, but I believe it is Golem's turn to get his cut of the respect pie. As I see it, I did my job and then some in the matches against these two men and yet they took my meaningless victory. Some might say, "why do you care if it was meaningless?" To which I would reply, because no one takes anything from Golem without due retribution.
"Pulsar...the first to talk. And what an enlightening few moments of mindless mumbling it was. You are going to try hard. That's a real revelation. You are going to enjoy the best we have and come away with a vain sense of accomplishment for showing up. That is, simply put, the cutest darn thing I have ever heard. Pulsar, when we fought, I threw off a cruise ship. Does that suggest ANYTHING to you? Off of a ship! All the way off, into the ocean. I am not entirely sure how you came back, peddled fast or something, it doesn't really matter, by that point, Copycat had already picked apart the "number one contender" and left him laying for the pin. You did the most asinine thing possible by hitting your little worthless finisher on a prone man and almost giving myself time to recover from rescuing my team's other damsel in distress. But hey, I think you are in much better position now then you were before, because of the lack of ships to thrown off of. Now there are only lions' dens and the baboon cage to keep me entertained. And that is a right and charitable thing to do, to put yourself in harm's way just for little ole me.
"Rat Fink, you successfully slammed Psycho into a pool table or something, I am not sure, I wasn't really paying attention to you, but kudos. Perhaps I could've broken up the pin if I had known Psycho, for all his posturing, has all the damage-taking ability and toughness of a six year old girl. You took advantage of our friend's Psycho and his inability to remember that this was a two-on-two match. He got confused and slammed around, much the same as he did by El Arco Iris, who seemed very much in tune with the mayhem for being such a nice guy. The moral is, anyone can beat up Psycho. He is an old, hollowed, worn out shell of a wrestler who stumbled around in the ring like a drunk hobo. You were right before, I was slowed by the surreal, Rat Fink. And the surreal was a little something I like to call LOVE.
"Which brings me to my real reason for winning this match. When I beat you two second-rate cash cows and drain you of all that you are worth, I get to fight Jared Wells at some undisclosed time, assuming of course that he doesn't make a fool of himself and lose the BAD title any time soon. Then I can make a statement to the rest of LOVE that they should stay away and never tag with me again. And they shouldn't mess with Golem, either. Because you will lose, you always lose. For running WFW, LOVE, you guys are alot of failures. My best suggestion is, as part of the fundraiser, you guys donate yourself to the WWF. They sound similar, WFW and WWF. And in this new, um, league, you get to wrestle chimps. The chimps will get confused, of course, when you tag them into the match, but they will understand when you lay down on your backs to get pinned. They can't be that much harder than Pulsar, can they?
"You see, everyone, this match was designed to appease me and find some place to put you two. Don't take what happens personally. It is merely the beginning of something that is about to happen alot."
(Golem laughs as the camera fades to black.) ChrisHorowitz — 29 June 2004 (Fade into the WORLD HEADQUARTERS of the NEW AGE REVOLUTION. Cut to the office of RAT FINK, who sits behind his desk, next to the WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.)
Rat Fink: Once again I sit here in front of the wheel of fear. The wheel that causes its opponents to bleed their own blood. The wheel that can make a man forfeit his entire career rather than face his possible opponent.
Of course, you could simply be matched up against myself.. but the police of asked us not to do that after our last few opponents with me as the name penciled in have ended up hanging themselves.
So in favor of a reduced body count, we will continue with the awe inspiring WHEEL OF POSSIBLE OPPONENTS.
(Rat Fink stands up and approaches the wheel.) Rat Fink: This wheel contains the deadliest opponents on this earth. Men who in civilized nations not built off the surreal prison of hope and dreams would be in control of vast armies.
These men, these great men are enshrined on this wheel as a reminder of all that a man can be when he shrugs off the chains of illusion and embraces grim reality. These men are your future Golem.. these men are you future Pulsar.
These men, each one of them is many more times the man you could ever hope to be. One of these men, is your destiny and your destruction. This is the moment that both Pulsar and Golem have dreaded... (Rat Fink spins the wheel.) And it continues.. and continues... (The wheel comes to a rest on the name MR. CHE.) Rat Fink: Yes... MR SHE.. Che.. the man from the rising sun. Master of the Japanese Death match. Trained in his native China in 17 martial arts, and studied under masters of the art of wrestling in the land of the rising sun.
A man that many fear, and for good reason. His father was the greatest of the Viet Cong's warriors, himself a son of a survivor of the Long March, three generations of warriors, and Mr. Che the greatest of them all. A man who could have single handedly reunited Korea, but saw the truth of the world and joined the N.A.R.
Golem, you may be a freak... and Pulsar, you may be a... space thing... but neither of you can defeat a man from a family founded on war, created by conflict.
Neither of you can harm a man who has learned from two previous generations how to withstand pain. How to be unphased by blood, and how to see total war, carnage and death as ordinary happenings.
How can a freak and a fluke hope to match up against a man like Mr Che? He is a master of the deadly arts, and spends more hours a day honing his skills than such lazy fools such as this pair spend sleeping. This is a man who trains with every ounce of his energy, who has made sure he is a strong, agile, flexible, and durable as possible.
Where he comes from such dangerous animals prowl the wilderness and prey on innocent children. They are not kept in zoos as slobs the likes of Golem and Pulsar are used to. They are to be fought to the death if needed, with ones bare hands.
Mr Che has done all of this, he has lived all of this... HE IS, everything these men could wish to be. He is the greatest of us, possibly the greatest warrior to ever live.
And he has remained locked away, a secret to hold onto until he was called upon by the wheel. This day of destiny for the man from the land of the rising sun has arrived, and his arrival spells the end of two careers.
(Fade out.)
