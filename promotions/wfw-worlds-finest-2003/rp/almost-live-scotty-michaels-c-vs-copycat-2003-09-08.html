Almost Live: Scotty Michaels (c) vs. Copycat

8 September 2003

Showtime24 — 11 September 2003 (FADEIN to 'The Viewer's Choice' SCOTTY MICHAELS, driving through the streets of Philly in his Lexus...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Fall, I love this time of year. While it might not officially be fall yet, you can feel it in the air... you can almost smell it. I honestly didn't think it would take this long to capture a title... but here it is, the North American Championship.
(The camera pans down to the passenger's seat, where the North American title belt rests...)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: After match after match after match after match, I finally beat Mike Manson for what was his. I finally did it, and I know I could've done it a little sooner... but I guess the wait was worth it, because it makes me appreciate this championship even more. Six years, six long years... and I did it in style. Coming up at Almost Live, I'm stepping into the ring with a man by the name of Copycat. Now I have to say that usually I scout my opponents well in advance, and have some kind of clue as to what they're bringing to the title... but I was so CONSUMED in that feud with Mike Manson, that I was only able to catch word of the WFW debut of the man known simply as Copycat...
(Michaels pulls into a parking garage, and gets out of the car... carrying a suit, and the North American championship.)
SCOTTY MICHAELS: Copycat, I didn't win this title so that I could lose it in my first defense... I hope you don't honestly believe that you have a shot in Hell. I will hold the North American title as long as I want to, and that's when I win the world title. So Copycat, while I'm not too concerned with my title defense against you... you best bring your 'A' game, or you won't stand a chance in hell. Get this camera out of my face...
(FADEOUT..) GARTHIsTheLaw — 13 September 2003 OORP: Sorry for taking so long to post something, the ol' class schedule is whomping my ass...

Being as I can't remember it, though, I think the best way to show you the error of your ways is to relate your situation to my career. Copycat: WWL Tri-American champion, 1997. (Icekold pulls this photo down and tosses it aside. The photo underneath it shows a very modern-looking Copycat, minus the beret and goatee, holding a title belt for a publicity shot)

You win a title, and you think you're invincible, that there's no way you'll lose it right away. But the first defense is the easiest defense to lose. That's something I had to learn the hard way, and come Almost Live, it's something you're going to learn the hard way. I'm just feeding you a little bit of irony. I'm copying your style directly while you're indirectly copying my past style - when my overconfidence cost me titles as soon as I won them. Your overconfidence hasn't cost you your belt yet, Scotty. But even if that isn't enough, your underestimating the Smartest Player in the Game will be. I can tell you this, Michaels - I don't leave my house without my "A" game. And if you walk into Almost Live with the attitude you're sporting now, you're going to be walking out with a brand new attitude and a big empty spot around your waist where your North American Title used to be.
(Copycat opens his door and turns to the camera as he's about to exit the car) Copycat: And that, my foolhardy and overconfident friend, is just all there is to it.
