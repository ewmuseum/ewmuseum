WFW — Roleplay

Pure Werstling at its best!!!

11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam_Benjamin — 11 August 2003 (As the camera fades in we see the letters WFW on a fax sitting on a table. As the room begins to be seen more clearly the desk is seen in a corner of a gym were "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin is seen working out. Adam stopping for a break picks up the contract and walks in over towards the camera and begins talking)
Benjamin: Worlds Finist Wrestling? Correct me if I am wrong but if that was the case then why is this federation not a product of England. I mean England is the up and coming wrestling capital of the world. And the leader of this revolution is Yours Truly. Yes I am the standard of what the English Wrestler is all about. And I am now working here in the US, and for you unlucky fools I am working in the WFW!!!
(Adam Smiles) Benjamin: Some people in the past have labled me a talker, and man that likes to hype himself up. Well my goal in the FWF is the work my tail off and slowly dominate from the bottom to the top. I am bring a stiff hard hitting style to the ring. I lay it all out there and challenge those than stand against me to bring it more, and take what I got, and give there very best to beat me. I like a challenge, I back down from no man, and I fear no soul. I am the one man army that puts fears into wrestlers and moves into the almanacs.
(Adam points to himself) Benjamin: To get to the point this week I am laying out an open challenge to face me. I await the the response with open arms. For I am Adam Benjamin the and I will leave you with this quote of mine...
(Adam clears his throat) Benjamin: A brave man might not live for ever, however a caution man never lives at all!!! Benjamin has spoken now I leave the trouble soul that believes they have what it takes to take on Yours Truly to take the step of greatness and sign my open challenge!!!!
(Fade to black) FireDemon915 — 1 September 2003 (The scene opens with Demonite standing in his locker room next to a ladder smircking at Benjamin stating he is the purest for of wrestling and has this to say)
Demonite: You are the purest form? Well I am the Darkest form evil at it's best you issue an open challenge well I ACCEPT!!!!! I will take you to my world through the gates of hell you will soon see hell at it's finest...The match shall be a no holds bard anything goes if your ready to see hell accept that..
( As Demonite stands there holding a picture of Benjamin he sets it a blaze and laughs while it burns. Fades to black)
--Demon--
