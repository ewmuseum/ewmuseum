EGarrett — 15 December 2003 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM YOUR WFW PRESIDENT Int. Limo Alex Wylde and his associates are sitting on opposite corners of his limo as it heads away from the Turkey Day Terror arena. Wylde is still in his wrestling gear and unshowered...he sits with his arms folded and one bandaged leg up on the seat. He gazes downward in a silent, disgusted introspection. Elaine, the Camera crew, and others all sit silently. There is a gravity in the vehicle...a sense of tension as they watch every non-movement of their disgusted boss. Without changing his face...Wylde takes one hand and starts to unwrap the bandage around his leg...ripping the tape off with one forceful snatch at a time.
WYLDE ...I don't know if this is gonna work. The words hang in the air with all the weight of the silence that preceded them. The group across the limo exchange looks and Elaine speaks quietly....
ELAINE ...what? What's not going to work... WYLDE This situation. I'm a focused man. I'm not like other people. I do things better because I can control my attention. I'm here...I'm in this job because I can do it better than anyone else in this business. But that's for other people. I have my own agenda also. And right now it's not being met. It's not being met because I can't focus. Things are the way they are right now because of it.
ELAINE What do you mean? How are things? WYLDE That match. That match was tailor made for me. It was the easiest route for me to accomplish my agenda. And I have to be honest. If I wasn't concerned with the Presidency. If I wasn't talking to you and these idiots with the cameras. If I was on my own like the way I've been before...I'd have won it easily. Very. Damn. Easily. But I caught myself trying to share my wealth of talent.
He finishes untaping his leg...revealing a nasty purple bruise all around the side of his knee. He rubs it with one hand while he undoes the black tape on his wrist wih the other.
WYLDE You see Elaine...the talented man faces a difficult dilemma. It's one ninety-nine point nine percent of the people in this world will never know and might not understand. But when you have something. A resource...strength...intelligence or athletic ability...something given to you for no reason...you spend a lot of time trying to understand it. Trying to figure out why you're better than everyone else. You struggle to know the nature of your gift. The limits of it. But most of all...you struggle to understand what you're supposed to do with it.
He switches hands. Now untaping the other wrist. WYLDE A lot of average people say that talent shouldn't be wasted. They don't understand the intense pressure that talent puts a man under. When you're better...people watch you. They see how you handle yourself. They label it a "gift" and threaten you not to waste it. They live vicariously through you...they talk and write about how you "should" be spending your time. And all while this is going on you haven't even gotten out of bed in the morning. You've done nothing but been the beneficiary of random genetics or some other source...and people are yelling at you...pushing and pulling you and criticizing you. You're exalted and villified all at once...and everything hinges on your choices. What you do with that "talent."
He takes his swollen leg down and starts massaging the purple swollen mess that is his knee...pushing the swelling down with a slight grimace.
WYLDE But listening to others can be the kiss of death. I learned that lesson years ago. Because you see...people can tell you what the ideal thing to do is...but they never necessarily know what will get results. If you're smarter than they are...you can see things they can't see. They don't have your perspective...they don't just how good you really can be...so they speak to you from a simple and naive worldview. The advice of others is fool's gold. Garbage compared to the wisdom a man gains from pushing his limits and plumbing his depths.
But I erred in a different way. Knowing what I knew...and knowing what others didn't. I have been cocky enough to show others that I know what's better for them than they do. I took the position of president of this league while also wrestling for my own goals. In many ways I took a slice of my knowledge and creativity and spread it on everyone here. But I came close to becoming too focused on carrying others. I worried too much about proving just how deep my knowledge runs...instead of focusing it first and foremost on my individual success. And I suffered for it. Things I would've seen coming miles away before...the clumsy attacks of that fool Copycat...a noisy idiot approaching from behind with a weapon...escaped my normally keen senses. And now others are enjoying the fruits of my 'gift.'
He leans back in the chair...taking a more relaxed posture. Elaine has been listening intently.
ELAINE So what does that mean? WYLDE It means that my priorities were not straight. From now on the most useful information from my mind is going where it is supposed to go...to my own ends and my own ultimate goals. I can't waste so much effort trying to prove to the world that I can make them watch idiots like Jared Wells. Instead of lifting others' carcasses one at a time through great and inefficient efforts...I can hitch them all to one sled and get them seen through drawing others to watch Alex Wylde. That way...I can maximize my talent and achieve my goals. When I do that...and ONLY when I do that...will I worry about the well-being of others. I'll start the lesson by destroying two men at Merrython...and reminding them of why I am who I am. Cut it.
The camera lingers for a second...but Wylde says nothing else. The cameraman gets the message.
