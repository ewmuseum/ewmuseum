WFW Great Expectations: The Unexpected

4 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Off to Valinor — 4 February 2004 (FADEIN: A lone ray of sunlight cracks thru the mirror of an old pueblo, where 'The Wolf' MIKE RANDALLS sits on his knees, his eyes closed and hands by his side - he's in meditation...)
RANDALLS: "Some do not believe in something until their own eyes witness the evidence. The lack of faith these certain people hold can be quite alarming, when the truth of the matter is...it is only their stubborn egos that believe their minds have the power to control the spiritual laws of this world around them..."
(CUTTO: Twenty-thousand fans on their feet, photo bulbs flashing as MEPHISTO and 'THE PHOENIX' MIKE RANDALLS circle each other within a barbed wire ring enclosed by a steel cage. Three banners hang from the rafters - two of them pictures of RANDALLS and MEPHISTO, but all three have one thing in common: no English text, only Japanese...)
RANDALLS: "It has been a long time since I've heard the name, Mephisto. Those with faith understand, how life is a circle...how years ago, before my blood poisoned the sancitity of this sport - I did battle with a man to SAVE it..."
(CUTTO: Old stock NGEN footage of ANARKY and 'DEVASTATING' MIKE RANDALLS in a Mr. Vicious styled environment - both men's faces covered in blood, both men's bodies slumped in their respective corners. The crowd leaps out of their chairs as both men with enraged looks charge out of their corners...)
RANDALLS: "Within the circle, we go through changes - yet, since the years since I've last seen you...Harold...your motive remains intact and immovable...in your own stubborn mind, still."
(CUTTO: Back to RANDALLS - his eyes still closed...) RANDALLS: "I do not possess the desire, you believe embodies my soul. My days of heedless violence, of unmerciless torture are behind me. Left in the ashes of the lies that I've blindly followed for ten years. I have no need to unmask a man that has avoided my presence on this continent - for he knows the trials and tribulations of any further conflict against me. He has no faith when it comes to me, for he knows that I am not anything he can control...but no worries, I neither have a need to unmask a soul once more that seems to have forgotten a lesson it was taught long ago...that there are things greater in this world, capable of destroying the very livelihoods we fight for."
(RANDALLS emerals eyes open, a reflection of the sunlight bouncing off them.) RANDALLS: "No, the only thing I embody...that I possess... is the TRUTH. And it is this TRUTH that for one night will insure that NEITHER of you men take a shortcut from the River Styx you intend to swim. You two know nothing of a TRUE WAR...one where you must put everything on the line WITHOUT a net, without a fearful escape."
(RANDALLS stands up, he walks towards the camera) RANDALLS: "If it is DESTRUCTION you two seek, it will be that which you will find in this match. For too long, the world has dealt with the lesser of two evils...and it will be my PLEASURE to guide the world in LOSING at least one..."
(the camera closes in on RANDALLS - his eyes hardened) RANDALLS: "I know what's inside you BOTH, there will be nothing you can hide. Identities, agendas...I won't care which of you fails, or which of your survives - just that in the long run, I helped make this sport a better place for those with TRUE hearts and TRUE minds. This is your ONE NIGHT, Mephisto...Anarky...this is MY ONE NIGHT..."
(BLACK) RANDALLS V/O: "But its only one closer to the TRUTH...and the END." EGarrett — 5 February 2004 INT. PRESIDENTIAL OFFICE Alex Wylde is behind his desk in his Jersey City Presidential Office. He's dressed in a form-fitting black t-shirt and finishing a phone conversation. He reaches over and ends the callwhile still holding the handset...then looks up briefly.
WYLDE "Just a note. All fan and hate mail can be addressed to: Alex Wylde C/O World's Finest Wrestling [detail withheld] Jersey City, New Jersey 07971 Later..." He takes his hand off the button and dials another call. Off to Valinor — 5 February 2004 (FADEIN: RANDALLS standing in front of a 1991 IWGPN banner of MEPHISTO vs. THE PHOENIX...)
RANDALLS: "Careful, Alex...I know there's plenty of people out there that would love to find you and break your legs, specifically..."
(CUTTO: In Simpsonsesque fashion, a smorgasborg of names whizz past on the screen...BLACK.)
