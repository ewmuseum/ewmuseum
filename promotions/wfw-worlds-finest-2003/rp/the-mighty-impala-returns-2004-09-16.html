The Mighty Impala Returns

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GreggG — 16 September 2004 (CUT TO: THE MIGHTY IMPALA, his dark, black skin glistening against the piercing sun behind him. He stands triumphant with a desert backdrop, wearing a rainbow skullcap and long, flowery robes in bright red, green and yellow. His blonde ponytail peeks down behind his neck.)
TMI: Yes, yes. *EYE* have returned. The Mighty Impala-- the prince of the Mossi people, the young pride of Cote D'Ivoire, the greatest wrestler on this planet, trained and educated in the FIHN-est boarding schools in Europe. I am a man of NOBLE blood, of ROYAL Her-EEE-tage. I am not a man you, the poverty-stricken wrestling fan, is used to associating with. I do not EAT at Hardee's. I do not BATHE in a public sink. I do not SHOP at Target. For I am a man of finer things, of a BETTER lifestyle. And I ask... NO, I COMMAND... that when I once again arrive on your filth-infested shores... that you rise and salute me like a peasant in Abidjan would.
For I am not returning this time to simply show my skills as a technical wrestler. I am returning to MAKE THIS LEAGUE SUFFER. My first time here, as an invited guest of the former beloved president Alex Wylde, I was not treated with the respect a man of my ROYAL Her-EEE-tage COMMANDS. So this time, I am here with a NEW PURPOSE. I am here to PROCURE the WFW World Title... and to ensure myself as the most powerful man in this EEN-DUS-TREE.
But I will not be doing this alone. NO NO. There are many around the world who would do WHATEVER IS REQUESTED OF ME, many to DO MY BIDDING at the clap of my hand, for with my TOO-TU-LEDGE, they would become BETTER men. They would RISE from being an unwashed PEASANT and become closer to a man BORN OF GOD HIMSELF. I have been busy scouting and watching this promotion. And I have chosen ONE PERSON to have the HONOR of a lifetime-- to work FOR ME, to do as EYE command, to SERVE ME on my QUEST to become the most powerful man and WORLD CHAMPION in this promotion. In a few short days, EYE... THE MIGHTY IMPALA... will return to your coun-tree... and I shall offer the INVITA-SHUN of a LIFETIME to the person I have PERSONALLY CHOSEN to be my new MANSERVANT! And then I shall begin my quest to DOMINATE THIS PROMO-SHUN! (FTB)
