Paul NotorisSTD — 26 November 2004 (CUEUP: "annihilation" by a Perfect Circle...) (Felix Red is sitting against a brick wall, in black dickies and a "Wells - Bastard Son of Wrestling" tshirt and Bono sunglasses, with a row of tarot cardz placed in front of him on the ground, all of which have been turned over except for the last...)
"the boy who destroyed the world" I recently appeared on television, and through the third eye, through the portal in solid time, they watched as I explained how many drugs I've done. Then Sean Edmunds, who at one point said my drug use made me unfit to be president, went on television and made a case for his supposed substance abuse. A habit that may have reached a level surpassing even my own.
Thirteen at one point appeared on television, and established himself as a depraved, sadistic madman in keeping with the tradition of villains from Batman comics. I looked through him, past the sinister veneer, and saw
what Thirteen sees on the other side of the mirror. Then I pointed at him and laughed. And he got mad. And he called me out.
Then I smashed him up something awful. Now I'm in a position to do something much worse to him, and he hasn't anyone to blame but himself. He's yet to make a statement regarding this sordid predicament. It's possible this is because he realizes if he had kept his mouth shut to begin with none of this would be happening. I don't think that's true though. The lad isn't bright enough to make that connection...
Many things can cause a man to consciously choose evil over good. Some of them are not unsympathetic or even ignoble. But the only thing that causes a man to consider himself wickedness incarnate is gullibility.
I asked Thirteen to listen, and I told him he was weak and stupid. And I told him I would pummel the snot out of him. A smarter man in his position would have listened, or at least found out for sure if I was telling the truth before he asked for a rematch in advance.
But this is all insipid and pointless posturing, and nothing will come of nothing. Until something comes of nothing, and now I'm wondering if everything is coming from nothing...
I said I'd make Thirteen unintelligible. I said I'd break his teeth with my boot. And I did. But I only said this because I thought it sounded cool. I kick people in the face all the time. Generally they start talking again immediately after they regain consciousness. The only thing that made this time different
was my false prophecy that became the truth. I said abusing cough medicine and hard drugs would make me a better WFW president. It was a faceless self serving lie. And Sean Edmunds, who should know about lies, agreed with me.
What if there really is no division between my word and my deed anymore? What if everything I say will happen is doomed to come to pass? I'm have fun with this for a while longer. But what's the point of, lets say, going up against Dan Ryan and Maelstrom if all I need to do is chant "Circles good, Squares bad" at them until their skin melts off and their bones dissolve?...
Maybe omnipotence is another one of those things I'm only supposed to want. (turns a card over. Looks at it, stands up, and shows the card to the camera...) Of course it's the death card. But I just drew the nemesis card. So it's the death of my nemesis? Which one? Is the nemesis a metaphor for some psychological quirk? Does this mean I'll finally be able to stop biting my nails?
(stares at the card for a minute. Then pulls out a zippo, specifically the new black heart logo LOVE zippo, now available on WFWmerch.com, burns the card and tosses it away. Then he pulls off his sunglasses and glares.)
Okay, six plus seven...I know you can hear me. I don't believe in tarot because I don't believe in the future. I don't believe in linear time. I don't believe in much of anything. Least of all people like you.
But I believe in lies and chaos. Which brings us to what I'm going to need from you...And lets face it, as much as you despise me you owe me. No matter what actually happens to you at Ghoulish Games, your second match is a gimmicky grudge match against me. This will save you six months of matches against Pulsar and Luster, which you would other need to solidify yourself in the midcard. People are paying attention to you because I paid attention to you. The same way people think drugs are cool because I said they were. I same way I'll be elected WFW president. People will vote for me because I said they will, even while I say "F*ck everyone but me," and I'll also do that...
But if my word keeps imbedding itself in the consensus reality then that isn't really chaos, is it? And faith in chaos is pretty much what makes the rest of this run so smoothly...
Listen. See, compounding your physical and mental short comings, you're lacking in spirit. They said you were no match for me, and I've already proven them right. I say you don't have a chance now. I say you're worthless, and you're lucky because the greatest thing you could ever accomplish is getting added to my score. History will know you as a spectacular failure, one who dared to make a run at the natural order of things, and for his efforts got
his brains scrambled via repeated blows to the head. I say the best thing you can do for yourself is not struggle, because the more you try to fight back the harder I'm going to hit back and the worse off you'll ultimately be...
Every prophecy is self fulfilling, but nevermind that. In fact, I need you to forget I've said any of this, and I need you to forget how often I'm right. I need you, through your natural affinity to be clueless, not think, abandon all rational thought, and make this as difficult for me as you possibly can.
I need you to fake like you've got something of value to fight for. I need you to fake like this matters. When I win, I want to know that the outcome wasn't predetermined by the subtle mind control techniques that have worked so well for me recently. I want to know I've won a fight. I want to know there is such a thing as free will, and I just shattered yours...
I'm in danger of becoming bored, you see. And who knows? Maybe if what I need is to exert myself beyond speaking and thinking, what you need is to be destroyed in the most spectacular way possible. And if you manage to come out the other end almost whole, well then that's even better.
Maybe we can help each other out after all... "the boy who destroyed the world" You know what I really love?...overly complicated answers to simple questions. Why would a candidate who's only promise was to f*ck over everyone win so easily against candidates who aspired to make things better? There's a lengthy editorial on wrestlingeek.com, speculating that somehow LOVE rigged the election with computer fraud, or perhaps we struck some kind of deal with Edmunds...But the truth that I know is not brimming with intrigue and mass deception. The truth that I know is actually quite bland.
Humanity is weak and stupid. Of course you would follow me to hell. Moo Moo, cattle. You'll follow anyone who says they'll protect you, anyone who says they'll take the big decisions out of your hands, anything that claims to be or even vaguely resembles a solution, so you don't have to think anymore. So you can go back to jerking
off and sh*tting in each others lives in a state of complete blissful oblivion. The power was yours all along, but you copped out and handed it over to me. You miserable wretched cowards. I hate you all. And now I can do whatever I want, and force you to do whatever I want, or be whoever I want. Because you wouldn't have it any other way.
But there's no need to despair just yet. The real fun doesn't start until after the world ends. I can't give you credit for much, Thirteen. But at least you might be looking foreword to the end of everything. If you're seeking guidance from Charles Manson, my favorite real life Horatio Alger story, then
that should be the case. But you never have been a very good listener, have you?... I don't actually get advice from Saddam Hussein. I just buy drugs from him. And I've never even met Osama Bin Laden. You're confusing him with Osama Bin Chillin, which is understandable, but there are subtle differences. Osama Bin Laden is the ringleader of an international Islamic fundamentalist terrorist syndicate, responsible for the destruction of the twin towers in New York city on 9/11. Osama Bin Chillin is a wrestler with a tasteless ring gimmick. But you DO get advice from Charles Manson, which is supposed to make you what? A smelly hippie?
If Charles Manson was such an impressive super villain, then why did he get caught? Why is he locked in solitary confinement for the rest of his life instead of going on a global farewell tour to promote the release of his fourth greatest hits CD? If I really should be afraid of you, then why are you doing exactly what I told you to do?
Your boasting is transparent. Your randomly strewn together threats are faceless, empty. You've abandoned reason and notions of self preservation and wrapped your illusions of madness, hate, and power around you like a f*cking security blanket. Just like I asked you to. You're already broken, but when I scatter the pieces it'll feel like I'm accomplishing something. You're going to be ripped apart, and the best reason why is my ego needs stroking.
Even the blatantly obvious is an abstract to you now. I did beat you. You were tired when I did it, but I planned it that way. This is a gimmick grudge match. You have a personal vendetta against me, i.e. a "grudge," and the match is taking place in a cemetery, which is very gimmicky. You honestly believe this is going to be easy for you? That I'm going to whimper for mercy while you stab my eyes out with your serrated finger nails? Are you really that stupid?
You are. You are because you want to be. You are because identity is a behavioral survival strategy. You've been convinced honesty would destroy you, and lies and misplaced hatred will keep you free from all pain. People
like you try to convince the rest of us all you feel is pain, but that's pretty much the same thing as feeling nothing, isn't it? Pretty much the same thing as how loving everyone means loving no one. Jesus Christ was a nihilist. Behind the gothic imagery and morbid fantasies, you're a member of the f*cking 700 club.
You're like this because people like me and the Illuminati and the Viacom corporation made you this way. Because we like having pre-designated helpless victims around. Whenever we're bored, We can set you up to squirm and wither in anguish while we gang rape you.
I'm speaking metaphorically of course, and I don't want to give you the impression I'm trying to frighten you. If you don't genuinely believe in yourself, then I don't get to see the disillusion and panic in your eyes when you realize, far far too late, how wrong you were.
Hate me. Fight me like your life depends on it. Throw every morsel of rage, and every strand of muscle in your body into this. So after it's over, you can be carried away knowing you hated me because I wanted you to, you fought like you did because I wanted you to. Like the Manson family member you were born to late to be. Like a Holy crusader slaughtering children in the streets, Like an Islamic suicide bomber. We gave you this dream of innocence and rugged independence and individuality. But all you're good for is taking orders.
Listen. Focus. No one is a virgin to pain. For all mankind’s divisions, pain is the one thing that unities us all, and makes us susceptible to corruption. This is why we kill for oil barons masquerading as prophets and failed folk singers. Why I'm WFW president.
Why you'll finally be a part of the world when I kick your face in. Just tell yourself I was possessed by the spirit of Sharon Tate's unborn fetus, and it'll put things back in perspective.
