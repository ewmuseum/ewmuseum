Shock & Awe: Diablo vs. Manning vs. Lee vs. Aitsu

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 31 March 2003 Bunkhouse Brawl Elimination Match Diablo vs. "Zombie" Matt Manning vs. Bobby Lee vs. Aitsu Sugawara vs. Silly Simon Cut your promos here! PaulNJ21 — 5 April 2003 Song starts: Well you know my name is Simon, and the things I draw come true, Oh pictures take me, take me over, Climb the ladder with you."
::camera fades into a bathroom where Silly Simon is sitting in a bathtub, full of blood:: SILLY SIMON: Welcome everyone, my name is Simon and I just love to sit here in my bubble bath. It gives me a peace of mind that I simply can’t find elsewhere. I really love to draw. Would you like to see one of my drawings?
::holds up a picture of Diablo being decapitated which was drawn in crayon:: There is Diablo, there is some blood, and over here on the ring post is Diablo’s head on the corner ring post. Silly me accidentally knocked it off with a clothesline. The person with the big smile over Diablo’s headless body is me. What do you think? I have other pictures to show you.
::holds up another picture of a woman terrified:: This is a picture of Aitsu Sugawara throwing up at the sheer horror that she sees and running for the hills. Girls tend to get a bit squeamish around blood for some reason. I don’t know why. We go pee and caca all of the time. Poor little girl.
::shows a picture of “The Rookie Warrior” Bobby Lee with broken bones and arms laying down on the ground, screaming in pain::
I didn’t do anything Bobby Lee. This is what Diablo did. I like Diablo. Diablo is cool. The ewwy liquid around Lee is from Lee made poo poo by accident when Diablo scared him. Bobby Lee is funny.
I wish I had more time to spend with you but I have to get back to my drawings. Toodles to all.
::Silly Simon waves at the camera:: FTB Diablo — 6 April 2003 (Fadein: Diablo is seated on the sofa in his Spanish Harlem partment, dressed in a red tank top, faded army green cargo pants, and a white bandana caressing his auburn locks.)
Diablo: Yes…it is time to stoke the embers…the flame shall return…and shall grow into an inferno…the wag of the devil’s tongue grows fervent with hunger…no hay salvacion de este fuego…there is no rage known such as that of…¡¡¡DIABLO FUEGO!!! A fresh start, a new place…ALL who feel its touch will be reduced to ashen ruin! It is futile to try to extinguish it…for as long as there is fuel, it cannot be smothered. WFW…you will get burned. Mark my words.
(Diablo stands up, walks to the window, and looks out contemplatively.) Diablo: I must know, Simon...are you being silly just because you want to be, or is it all a facade? Was there some experience in your past that reset your psyche to three years old? If that is so, I would pity you, but pity helps no-one. I've never recieved any pity, and I learned never to give it. Perhaps my hypothesis is true, because you seem think I am cool, meaning perhaps you look up to me, and wish to emulate my ability to deal with adversity. But this is the jarring truth: you will never be like me, because you have never seen...
(Diablo now stares directly into the camera) Diablo: ...The darkness. The darkness that forever polluted my soul since I was a child. This isn't a darkness just like turning the lights off, no. This is the blackest abyss a man could ever imagine. It eats me from the inside. Yet, you wonder, why haven't I already collapsed from the inside after all these years? Because the darkness not only is just a parasite, but also fuels my body in combat. It takes over whenever its host is threatened. I lose all sense of remorse or compassion. The darkness will force me to reduce my opponents to huddled masses of what used to be human beings. And after their defeat...a little bit of my darkness infects them. They don't know it at first. They never do. I never did, until it was too late. But then they know the darkness is there, and it plagues them, making them another slave to the abyss. I'm going to show you the true devastation that is wrought by the darkness.
(Rubs his chin contemplatively.) Diablo: I'm sort of afraid though, that once your mind is taken over by the darkness that it will further retard you, and you'll become nothing more than a gaping vegetable. But hey, it's no skin off my back. It WILL happen, no matter how hard you try to stop it. Don't think I'm not paying attention to any of you others, either! It WILL happen to you, too! ALL OF YOU! Lee, Manning, and Sugawara, too... I WILL PUT MY EVIL INSIDE OF YOU!
(FTB) ------------------------------ "I blow your mind. You just blow."
