WFW Turkey Day Terror - Michaels vs. Copycat vs. Wylde vs. Edmunds

19 November 2003

PaulNJ21 — 19 November 2003 This is an elimination match. The first person eliminated gets the WFW North American Title shot at pre-PPV House Show. The second person eliminated gets a WFW North American Title shot at the PPV. The third person eliminated gets a WFW World Heavyweight Title shot at a pre-PPV House Show.
The winner of the match gets a WFW World Heavyweight Title Shot at the next PPV. TheOriginalSE — 21 November 2003 ((FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' is sitting in a study, with his feet lounged on a chair. He has a smoking jacket on and there is a lamp behind him. He looks up from his reading and smiles.))
EDMUNDS: "When I looked at the lineup today for Turkey Day Terror I could almost hear Copycat's head explode. What is this now, four matches where we have not been able to go one on one? With the exception of, well, our draw a few weeks back, but we all know that nothing has been settled. And once again, Copycat, nothing more will be settled at Turkey Day Terror. Isn't it funny, though? I mean, after all this time, after all the tag matches and the dog collared matches, maybe I'm enjoying the WFW's ploy to keep us apart. Because every match that we're in together with either Luster, El Arco Pato, President Wylde, .. well (pauses) maybe it just makes you a little easier to commit mistakes. Each match Copycat gets more and more uptight. We've both been aching for the opportunity to prove the other wrong .. but perhaps, just for a bit, I can wait patiently. Does that sound a little bit like truth, Copycat? Maybe I'm wrong, but what I'm picturing is Copycat chomping at the bits waiting for a match against 'Simply Sensational' ... and then being so eager in the match that he'll make mistake .. after mistake .. after mistake. But listen to me ramble on! I'm looking ahead rather than at the match right in front of my face. Copycat, Scotty Michaels, 'Simply Sensational', and our gracious President, Alex Wylde."
((Edmunds takes his feet off the chair and sits upright. He puts down the book and wrings his hands together, cracking his knuckles in the process.))
EDMUNDS: "I watched Scotty Michaels' match last show. I was half-expecting him to lose the title to Felix Red, whose not a bad competitor. But to Richard Gideon? (Stresses) RICHARD GIDEON? Oh sure, I'm positive that the WFW brass is trying to pass him off as a prodigy, saying he took Doc Silver to the limit! A 5th grade CROSSWORD puzzle takes Doc Silver to the limit, and most of the time, gets the better of him.. so saying that 'the System' took Silver to the limit is nothing to brag about. But I digress. While Gideon sits as the interim champion for now, Scotty has a chance to get a title shot to reclaim his prize. The problem might lie, however, in the fact that Michaels has his sights set higher. I say this is a problem, Michaels, because you're trying to bite off a bit more than you can chew. But hey, if you want to do that .. then go right ahead, because I'm sure there's a house show coming up that the WFW would love to book you in against 'the System'."
((Edmunds stands up and puts the book back in the shelf.)) EDMUNDS: "I would be lying if I said I didn't want to get the World title shot at the next pay-per-view. And I'd be willing to do anything in this match for it. But the simple truth is that there is one person who I know I am going to end up competiting with at the end of Turkey Day Terror, (smiles) and that is Alex Wylde. Do I throw all my cookies in a basket and try to win it all? Or do I defer to the one man in the WFW I actually respect? That's a tough question. I can't think of a decent answer right now .. but I'll know my answer by Turkey Day Terror ... and whatever it is, I know I'll be completely satisfied with my decision. But President Wylde, there are larger problems in this match that need to be fixed. Copycat and Scotty Michaels. These two, especially that waste Michaels, need to recognize greatness when they see it. Michaels will come out with glazed over eyes focused on Shawn Hart ... Copycat will come down with fire in his eyes, and will leave with humility, and once this match is over, perhaps they'll see that they were in the presence of true greatness in Alex Wylde ... and me? We'll, they, like everyone else, will know that Sean Edmunds is (pauses to smile) 'Simply Sensational'..."
((FADEOUT: As Edmunds hangs up his smoking coat, walks over to the lamp and turns it off. FADE TO BLACK.))

Alex Wylde's a factor...ever since he became WFW President, he's done nothing but make stupid decisions and hire names I've heard so many times before that I have to pronounce them differently to keep myself from getting sick...the Ay-nurk-ayes and Mee-nyans of the world. (Scotty Michaels' face appears over another background face) Copycat: Of course, I haven't forgotten about how Scotty beat me a few weeks back...he sure as hell couldn't get the job done by himself. It took four guys to beat me, so let's add in those other three.

(A picture of a title belt appears over another face) Copycat: Shawn Hart and I have had our issues in the past. It's been awhile since this league had a respectable champion...has it ever had one? Doc Silver, Psycho, Shawn Hart? I'm thinking a title reign with the Cat holding the strap would be just what WFW needs.

Point is, Sean...there's much more to this match for me to consider than you. In fact, since there's a guarantee that this match won't put a conclusive finish into our little feud, beating you doesn't really matter too much, does it? Hey, I've already got ONE indirect victory over you, what good is another one going to do me? It might do YOU some good by tying things up, but frankly, I'm thinking about the more important things here.
(All the images in the background fade out except for the one that was supposed to represent the WFW World Heavyweight Title)
Copycat: That's what I'm gunning for going into this match. If I beat you...it won't matter too much. But I'd be perfectly fine letting you get pinned by Scotty Michaels or Alex Wylde. The outcome I want is me coming out of this match the winner, and the number-one contender for the World Heavyweight Title come the next pay-per-view. While you, Sean, will just continue trying to take me down, not paying much attention to anything else until it's too damned late. And that, my inconclusive-finish-stricken friend, is just all there is to it.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE Alex Wylde is behind a huge pile of papers at the desk of his New Jersey Presidential Office. He rolls up the sleeves of his black shirt and smooths his hair back over his head as he shuffles them, then waves them off and stares into the camera.
WYLDE "Good evening gentlemen. I hope you don't mind if I sit in on the little meeting here. I'm sure you were hoping I wouldn't be able to find time with my busy schedule...what with constant contract negotiations and scheduling surgery for Good Eddie to remove my boot from his skull. Unfortunately though...I count multi-tasking among my many talents. In fact...it's quite simple...observe."

Elaine and a shorter young man in a shirt and tie come into Wylde's office. Elaine's face is visible but the young man's back is to the camera. He quickly points to the stack of papers on his desk. Her frown is answered with Wylde's gesture to the camera. She reluctantly grabs the papers and a pen from his desk...hands them to the young man and they both exit. Alex then takes a deep relaxed breath...puts his stocking feet on his now empty desk and folds his hands behind his head.
WYLDE "HEY...DON'T FORGET THAT I USE LOOPING E'S...not straight. People catch those sort of things. Y'know? But as you boys can see...I can take care of almost an unlimited number of tasks and not lose my focus on the match. It's a skill that is almost tailor made for this type of match, in fact. Since each of us face a trio of opponents...it will be the guy who can divide his attention the best that walks out with the shot.
"Cat...Michaels...even my party-member Sean...even if you gentlemen equalled me in talent...the fact is I can beat you all with experience in these situations. I got through school being able to focus on multiple student's test papers at one time. My first girlfriend overlapped my second and third. On rare occassions...I've even managed to wrestle perfectly while tolerating the feel of a quarter-pound of salt or cold pieces of metal in the front of my tights. But as with most esteemed leaders...you're better off not knowing about those types of things in my past.
"But anyway...while I have no qualms about doing what I have to in order to gain total control of the league...I do regret having to crush the spirits of some of the talent. And I use that word loosely in this case. Copycat was a name I heard once...marginally...when coming up in this business...and now that I've had the...privilege of witnessing him in action for the past several months...quite frankly I wish he'd return to the margins where he belongs. The last thing I remember about Scotty Michaels was him getting kicked out of the Frat because he couldn't finish a shot of Budweiser without passing out. Ah...college. And while I detect a plot from others to divide the members of the First Presidential Party...I fully expect Sean Edmunds to assist me in removing these first two fools from contention before we settle the matter of the title shot between ourselves. And don't forget who signed off on that early christmas bonus for your new loft in Escandido when the time comes. And if the "E" didn't loop in that signature...well...kindly disregard that.
"...and remember gentlemen. I don't want to beat you all. The belt does. It wants to be on the best wrestler here. It wants to go to me...and it wants to do so on the largest stage...in front of millions of starry-eyed and envious viewers. You should be happy that I'm allowing you to witness it as the front row of viewers. And what you will see might be called superior strategy. It might be called overwhelming talent. But the term I prefer is "Executive Privilege."
Wylde reaches down and reclines his chair...then produces a "Do Not Disturb" sign from a drawer and places it on the desk. He then folds his arms across his chest...closes his eyes and lets loose a relaxed sigh.
