WFW — Roleplay

Thirteen's platform.. or just a soggy stool.

5 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DmntdDuergar — 5 November 2004 *Thirteen is seen sitting in a high backed chair, smoking a pipe, in what appears to be an expensive suit. His hair is neatly pulled back and oddly enough… he’s smiling.*
Thirteen: “As most of you already know, I, Thirteen, have throw in my chip to ascend to the presidency of WFW. Unlike some here, I’m not looking to make radical changes to the federation. I’m not looking to personally give myself matches that will make me look good. No. I’m here to bring this federation to a variable utopia. I seek to create a peace and love throughout the ranks of wrestlers. I know some of you in the past have had certain run-ins with others, and that is completely normal. I understand how much that dirty snake Vengeance can cling to your heart. I will give each and every one of you the opportunity to release this vengeance from yourself, so that you may live peaceably.”
*Thirteen clears his throat.* Thirteen: “For those of you who love peace and prosperity for all, this is the part where you turn off your tv set and kindly vote for me. The number’s not unlucky, just sorely mistaken as such. Think of me as your rabbit’s foot. Good night.”
*Thirteen pauses, continuing to smile. After about a minute of staring there, unblinking, his smile fades and he looks into the camera as the Thirteen we all know and love.*
Thirteen: “As for the rest of you. Those peace loving tree hugging dirt sniffing animal saving fur coat banishing hooligans need to realize just how nice peace is. I know there’s some of you out there who want absolute chaos. I know there’s some of you out there who want to take those uptight pricks and bury them in their own compost heap to rot with the rest of their garbage… and I don’t blame you. If it were not for the likes of us, they wouldn’t have a place in the world would they? If it weren’t for us wreaking chaos and destruction, they wouldn’t know what peace is. If they did create the variable utopia, they would then look for something else to do. The next challenge from there… destroy utopia. But why should we let them have all the fun. I’m here to bring you the best of both worlds. Should you choose to try the utopia.. I’ll let you go at it. Should you want to wreak absolute havoc, pain, destruction…I’ll personally hand it to you. This company needs to be shaken up a bit. No more petty squabbles between two people because their mothers are fighting over who makes a better lesbian. I want a war. I want to see something happen. No more of this ‘My mommy said that a match against this person would do me good’ f*ck that sh*t. I want men. Not little boys. I want to see something happen. You want title shots, prove it to me that you can handle anything thrown at you, and I’ll give you the title. You want change, you want vengeance, you want peace.. tell me what you want… your wish is my command.”
*Thirteen smiles, this time with his usual sadism about it.* Thirteen: “Have a nice day ladies.” *Fades out*
