[name withheld] — 5 November 2003 ...9:42am ...New York City ...45 degrees, sunny, chilly (Jared Wells walks out of the doctor's office in a sling as he is wearing dark blue jeans, black leather jacket, silver glasses. Camera pans over around NYC then to Wells)
'RAGE 'o FIRE': I'm not gonna waste time here people....My arm is broken, I had to backout of the match between Edmunds and Copycat in a match called the WWL Bragging rights last month. Long story short, I'm coming back shortly, and I have some trash I need to clean up. I'm a little pissed off about the WWL Bragging rights match because it makes my name look old. Like I'm some piece of crap from the past. I've got nothing to brag about. But let's talk about when I come back. It looks like the WFW picked up a few guys....and I know them very well. My goals and my demands will happen when my arm heals. Edmunds, buddy 'ol pal....don't worry your not the only target. My reality will expand, and reality will hit some and they will not like it. Only Rage can bring it to the WFW with brutal instincts, crazy desire, unpredictable, and the biggest bastard you've ever met. You bastards ready to dance? Well RAGE, he always leads......See you soon WFW......
[i](...fadeout) TheOriginalSE — 5 November 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Nov-05-03 AT 11:24&nbsp;AM (EST)] ((FADEIN: To Sensational Sherri and Sean Edmunds sitting at a bar having a drink .. Wells' promo just finishes playing..))
SHERRI: "Did he say he always leads?" EDMUNDS: "Naw, I'm pretty sure he said he always leaves ..." ((FADEOUT: As Edmunds and Sherri start laughing and start drinking their drinks.)) [name withheld] — 7 November 2003 (camera opens up to Jared Wells walking out another doctors office in his sling, doing the usual daily visit. Wells heard what Edmunds had to say and responds)
RAGE 'o FIRE: (evil look) Soon Edmunds, very soon. Enough with the drama, enough with the history lesson. As soon as my arm heals....your ass is mine. WFW made a wise decision, they made a very smart choice....JARED WELLS will turn this entire party upside down. Give it time bro....give it time......
(FADEOUT)
