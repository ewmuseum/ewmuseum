WFW — Roleplay

From the Desk of...

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TheOriginalSE — 23 May 2004 (FADEIN: The newly furnished President's office. 'Simply Sensational' is sitting down looking over some papers in a suit and tie. He has those silly focal lenses that the elites wear as 'reading glasses'. Sherri brings him a cup of tea and some crumpets as he puts the papers down.)
SHERRI: "Out of his mind. Wink thinking that he can try and sabotage your return to the top of the WFW.."
EDMUNDS: "The Board has its own interests, Sherri. While they may not see eye to eye with myself, they're control over affairs of the WFW is inadequate to supress our goals."
SHERRI: "What are you looking at?" EDMUNDS: "My contract. Making sure that all the Is are dotted and Ts are crossed. As I am doing with each and every wrestler here in World's Finest Wrestling."
SHERRI: "Good. But why?" EDMUNDS: "As President of the WFW, I have specific duties that I have to address. One of which, my predecessor neglected to do, was to make sure that the wrestlers of this promotion are happy."
SHERRI: "Okay..." EDMUNDS: "I'm talking specifically about L.O.V.E." SHERRI: "Those sons of bit..." EDMUNDS: "Needless to say.. They had NO place coming out and making ultimatums. But I have looked over their requests, nonetheless."
SHERRI: "Oh god." EDMUNDS: "Anarky wants the ban lifted with concerns of Manson. Granted. But that match ... WILL BE MADE AT MY DISCRETION. Time, place, everything. The official ban is gone, but I dictate when the consequences will take place."
SHERRI: "But Manson and Anarky will kill each other." EDMUNDS: "Manson gets what he deserves. Anarky gets what he deserves. The title goes vacant, and I claim it. Everyone wins."
SHERRI: "So I guess Copycat's banned for life from a World Title shot?" EDMUNDS: "Definitely not. L.O.V.E. may think that my hatred of that mangy creature is thicker than blood, but the fact of the matter is Copycat has his hands full right now as it is. If he somehow, someway manages to get by my safeguards and gets his title shot, then by all means, he'll get his title shot. But you never know, Sherri.. That World Title shot just may be for that BAD World Title....."
SHERRI: "I don't think L.O.V.E. would like that very much... they said themselves that they'll make our lives a living hell .."
EDMUNDS: "Oh come on, do you think that they'd really want to mess with Mighty Impala and Mr. Middle Management?"
(Sherri rolls her eyes and stands up.) SHERRI: "I've gotta go. Wylde's ex ***** of a secretary called and wanted her stuff so I'm going to go throw it in the trash and then tell her that it must have gotten lost."
EDMUNDS: "Ah ok. And one more thing ..." SHERRI: "Yes?" EDMUNDS: "If you do happen to see Jared Wells out there ... tell him that he can make all the damn stipulations he wants ... because if I have my way, that title will be out of his hands faster than he can smoke a joint."
SHERRI: "Ha, ok." (Edmunds goes back to reading the papers..) EDMUNDS: "L.O.V.E.'s going to be my own personal Bin Laden ... HA ... more like they're going to be my Monica.."
(Fade to Black)
