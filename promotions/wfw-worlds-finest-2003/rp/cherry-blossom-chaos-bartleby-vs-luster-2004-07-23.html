coolmint21 — 27 July 2004 FADEIN: On the next Reality World: Hawaii, Luster gets fired. (Luster is in the house and thinking about what to do because he is the only roommate with no job. The phone rings and Luster answers.)
Luster: Luster’s Funky Dance School, may Luster be of service, lady? P. Nut: Luster… Luster: What do you want, Mandingo? P. Nut: The WFW wants you back. They need you, playa. I also need some gold teef’. I spoke to the Chairman and we renegotiated your contract.
Luster: Luster don’t be doin’ wrestlin’ no mo’. Luster gots to impress the ladies wit his actin’ skills. Luster gots them linin’ up. Luster is on his way to the top. Soon, I’ll be in porn and then feature movies straight to video. Luster gots it goin’ on. Those punka**es at WFW screwed me over and made of some lame storyline to give me the axe. Luster is the Funkiest wrestler in town and they let me go. They didn’t know how popular Luster be, Mandingo. Luster ain’t crazy and all the ladies love him, Mandingo. Tell WFW, Luster gots no time for them.
P. Nut: Luster, I needs the teef’, playa. You gots to do this. Luster: Later, B*tch. (Luster hangs up the phone and walks to the kitchen. He grabs Colt 45 from the fridge and Corn Chips from the table. He walks back into the living room as the phone rings again. Luster runs the phone. It is Luster’s accountant on the phone. Luster’s accountant Lester never calls.)
Lester: You’re broke, get a job. Luster you need a job. You can’t afford the Duster and all those gold teeth.
Luster: It ain’t no Duster. What did Luster say! Lester: Sorry, you can’t afford the Funkiest Pimp Ride This Side of L-Town and the all those gold teef’. You need a job.
Luster: Luster gots a Dance School. Lester: You can’t pay the bills with the pieces of ass. Luster: Luster gots this. (Luster hangs up the phone and picks up the phone to make a call.) (Hours later, Amy, one of Luster’s roommates, walks into the house and she surprised to see what has happened in the house. The house is empty. Luster walks in the living room shortly after.)
Luster: Hello, Lady. Amy: What have you done? Luster: Luster, sold everything. Luster needed money for the Funkiest Pimp Ride This Side of L-Town and all those damn gold teef’.
Amy: You sold everything for that piece of sh*t and some fu*kin’ teeth. (Amy runs to the phone and calls the producers of the show.) (Moments later, a producer wants to talk to Luster.) Producer 1: Luster, you are fired. Get you car and teeth out of the house now! Luster: You can’t fire me, main squeeze. Luster got all the fans. This show ain’t no Funkytown. Luster be out.
(Luster goes to his room and grabs his all his jogging suits, headbands, and g-strings. He walks back out towards the door and on his way out he slaps Amy in the face. Luster is back on the streets.)
FADEOUT: This concludes this season of Reality World: Hawaii. Since the firing of Luster, ratings have dropped drastically and the show was cancelled. What will Luster do? Don’t watch our channel because we don’t know.
