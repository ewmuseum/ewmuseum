Manson — 16 December 2003 ((FADEIN: MICHAEL MANSON, wearing a long-sleeved black shirt and leather pants, sits on a stone bench in a gray afternoon while it snows around him.))
MANSON: I suppose Anarky thinks he accomplished something. And, of course, he's wrong. I'm not any better off than I was before that match, except while I might be not what I was, I can see that he isn't either. A few years ago, I would have skinned him and made a jacket out of it. Now he had his chance to do that to me and still, he can't do it. I could probably sever my head except for an inch of neck and present it to him and he wouldn't be able to finish me. Maybe he could have before, but he'd still want to recite a poem about it first. So anyway, we're barred for 30 days and that's happened before and I always used to find a way around that. I can't even care now.
I'm thrown into this match for no real reason. I'm teamed with the guy who thinks he's John Wayne and the guy who's happy all the time. This is like casting for a reality TV show. If I were religious, I'd think this was punishment for my sins. I'm cynical, but I don't think this is a joke or disciple from management. What would be the point in that?
Someone might say it's a chance to go after Mason who attacked me at the ppv, but he was only one among many. And he has his excuse that he was just sitting around in his car in the parking lot during a ppv for some reason. I might have made the same excuse myself, except I would have proper grammar and made it sound so much funnier. As of now, I expect him to show up anyday eating KFC and having dreadlocks to appeal to white suburbia with every horrible urban afro cliche there is. And really, that's punishment himself.
There's also Luster who cost me another title and just showed me that I was downsliding for longer than I thought. This is the man who likely masturbates thinking of me. And now I have to actually compete in a match with him. Before, I would have sent him to the burn ward and would have had video footage of his family while they slept. I would have just broken Mason's neck and sent the bill for my laundry to his significant other.
But now....now....I just have to wrestle them. Because I have nothing else. Partners I don't even know, people I've never even bothered to see in the locker room. Good, bad, indifferent....I don't know what the hell they are. And I should. I would have before. I wouldn't have said that I would, but I had to. But that seems like so long ago.
And then there's Minion. This is a man who spent his whole life trying to be Tom Parker, or so he claimed. In actuality, he obsessed about someone and took over their identity because he thought that was something I would do. Because he thought it was something deranged and warped that only my imagination could conceive. The truth is that Minion has spent his whole damn career trying to be me...all because a long time ago I told him a few things....some might call it advice....I would have it manipulation.....but from that...came one of the few of my sycophants that actually stayed.
To think that the only reason he claims to be Tom Parker was because I didn't die. Otherwise, he'd be going around calling himself Mike Manson or Mephisto. He's just going to try to goad me like Anarky, thinking he might get a better result.
But I say to Minion, look at me now. Look at me, and see yourself. See your future. You'll end up like this....or worse. From that, I have my sole motivation. That is to show you, Minion, within the time of this match, the errors of choosing me as a hero. Whether through blood, mine or yours, apathy, and senseless destruction, there will be a broken man in that ring.
It might be me already. I still need someone to tell me, to show me though.
