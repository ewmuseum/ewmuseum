WFW Ghoulish Games - Alex Wylde vs. Good Eddie

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 24 October 2003 The RP/Angle dealine is Monday, November 3rd 11:59 PM. If all all possible, make your RP Halloween or Day of the Dead themed.
Cut your promos here. EGarrett — 26 October 2003 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM YOUR WFW PRESIDENT... INT. OFFICE Alex Wylde is casually reclined behind the desk of his Jersey City office. He's still nursing a few scars from New World Madness, and sports a band-aid on his chin. Dressed in street clothes, he puts his feet on the desk and talks into the earpiece of his cellphone.
WYLDENo...thank YOU my friend. Don't you love the feeling of a job well done? I know...and after Nark gets done with those boys I'm sure I'll love it too. It's really hard to be this brilliant...really. Oh wait, my call waiting is going off? It's...oh...I'm going to have to call you back. Okay? Later. (clicks over) This is Wylde.
He listens intently for a few seconds, his eyes scanning across the room. He then furrows his brow?
WYLDEI don't remember signing anyone of that name. I don't know if I WOULD sign someone of that name. Give me some credit man...Texas sucks. Oh...you have some other news? The PPV? They're airing Ads for the card already?...Against ME? (he grabs the hanging chord of the earpiece mic and pulls it across his mouth for emphasis)They're putting Good Eddie against me? I'll get back to you later. Goodbye...ah...uh... Yes...I forgot last time...honest. Bye.
Wylde clicks the cellphone off and turns to the camera, earpiece still dangling from his ear.
WYLDEWhoever's making these cards must not like Good Eddie that much either. I mean it's his first match in this league and they're already putting on a PPV...and against me. You're going to have to educate me about yourself, pal...because I'll be damned if I've heard your name around these Jersey parts. And while you're at it...please explain the name? "Good Eddie?" I think someone played a prank on you when they said your nickname had to represent your actual level of ability. If it was I'd have to be "A-Hole" Alex. And while it might be worth it just to witness stuff like "He'll Do For Now" Hart...or Michael "The Sadistic Devil With a Garage Full of Hermetically-Sealed Comic Books that he'll throw a hissy fit about if you open just ONE time" Manson...(his cellphone almost instantly goes off, he checks the number and clicks it off)... scratch that last one. Anyway...the point is...while it would be fun... I don't think the Marketing Department would be that jazzed about it. So, while it may be accurate to call yourself "Good" Eddie...or "Fairly Serviceable" Edward...you have my official permission to upgrade it to "Excellent" Eddie...if only so we can sell some more t-shirts. Get back to me...and I hope you can wrestle, pal. Because I think we've ALL learned by now...Texas and President's don't mix.
FADEOUT. THE PRECEDING MESSAGE HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY YOUTH UNITED FOR ALEX WYLDE AND WORLD'S FINEST WRESTLING.
SigilOfLeviBF — 3 November 2003 (FADEIN: A bar in Albuquerque; EDDIE's choking down whiskey like water, his face bowed in his hair. He begins to scratch his unshaven cheek, the bartender staring at him all the while.)
EDDIE: You ain't but know two things in life; birth, and death. All else is wedged somewhere in between, and don't quite fit right. Whether you're building the house your family's to live in, or staring down the cold round hall of my black chamber, a man still knows but those two things, and them only. You may have misgivings about what matters until your judgment's handed down, but make no mistake, it's about how long you last. (Takes out a stack of bills, begins to flip and count on the bar counter) That sound...you hear it? That's the sound of men. Each of these green dead presidents represents a man who never got to see my face twice. I make a living off of guys like you, Alex Wylde. You like names? Well I don't know any names. Hell, not even I got a name, but everyone's got one for me. Some know me as Good, some know me as Bad, and sure enough, you'll call me something too. I don't believe I was born with a soul, rather, a price, for which I can be bought and sold easy. That price rests on my trigger finger, on my first, in my fury; and when that price is over your head, I collect. I'm not comin' to fight you, friend. I'm just coming to get what's owed. I'm due.
(Finishes his drink, throws a bill to the bartender, and leaves) BARTENDER: "The Man Who Gets Everyone." Tips well, though...I like him. (FTB) EGarrett — 5 November 2003 AND NOW, A MESSAGE FROM YOUR FIRST WFW PRESIDENT... INT. OFFICE Alex Wylde is relaxing with his sneakered feet up on his desk in his Jersey City Presidential office. Wearing street clothes...he chatters a bit on his cellphone before hanging it up and speaking.
WYLDE"Hey there, Pilgrim. I understand you want to make an introduction...but let's not get ahead of ourselves. You may want to make a name off of me...but you should know that I don't have any control over my own booking. For you to be up against me in your first match...that means that somebody somewhere thinks you've been a very Bad Eddie. After all...there's a reason I wrestle and make the decisions around here. I'm not only the President...I'm also a client. It's almost like being the Sheriff."
Wylde takes his feet off the desk and leans forward, clasping his hands. WYLDE"You've told me about yourself...so let me enlighten you about where I'm from. I'm a big city slickah. I know you probably consider anyone who wears real fabric clothes to be a softee or a nancy-boy...but the fact is that life in City streets is a lot more rough than mounting livestock for a living. Now...I don't know what you do in the lonely parts of Okiepenokie...but in Jersey...men don't threaten other men with the sight of the "cold round hall of their black chamber." At least not if you 'shoot straight.' Get me? And while your cattle might be different after you get done...from my understanding it's more warm and starfish-shaped anyway. But I digress. The bottom line is that your signing happened completely without my knowledge. I don't like people from the South. You're slow, you talk funny, you wear stupid hats...and you think it's okay to wear crotchless leather pants over your jeans. But I'm going to have to make do. If money is what you're after...then I'll offer you 50 bucks to not even show up for the match. It'd be worth it to to be able to save my time pummeling you. If not...I guess you're here whether I like it or not. Welcome to my league pal...I hope you can wrestle...because you're going to get a hard introduction."
FADEOUT.
