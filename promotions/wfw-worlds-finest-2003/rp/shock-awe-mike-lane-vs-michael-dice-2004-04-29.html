Shock & Awe: Mike Lane vs. Michael Dice

29 April 2004

Badnews — 9 May 2004 (PRIMETIME logo zooms across the screen to a voiceover of Scott Pelley) (footage shows Mike Lane wrestling for the WWL World championship) Pelley: Mike Lane was barely hitting his stride back in 1999, he was a champion almost a dozen times over. The wrestling world was his oyster and it seemed the only thing that could stop him was himself....
(Camera shows Lane sitting in his house, yellow collared Ralph Lauren T shirt on, hands gesturing in front of his face)
LANE: Anybody can be a loser, losing is easy. I could overcome any obsticle ANYBODY threw in my way. Now being a winner, and being a winner CONSTANTLY thats some tough <BLEEP> right there<pause>. When you become so successful so fast and those obsticles arent there for you to overcome anymore, you start CREATING your own... You make your success THAT MUCH harder to obtain
(Camera cuts back to Pelley, this time walking down Boston Common, hands in pocket) Pelley: Tonight we'll examine the sometime great, but often sad story of a man whos greatest demon.. was himself, tonight.. on PRIMETIME.
(Opening credits role.. music and all... credits cut to 1996 Mike Lane wrestling Big Rodge for the NWFA Television title and winning via submission in a Texas Cloverleaf... Camera cuts quickly to Scott Pelley sitting across from Lane, hand on chin...)
Pelley: You.. You were basically a guy who from the very beginning made a huge name for himself in this business...
LANE: The business for me Scott, right from the start wasn't to be considered a great wrestler, Scott to this DAY thats still not the pinnicle I've reached for. Theres a ton of guys in my industry who are GREAT WRESTLERS, hell to be at this level you simply have to be just to survive. But Scott, the thing I thrived off the most, you know what really drove me to compete was being FEARED.
Pelley: Being feared...li... LANE: Feared Pelley: So being feared to you meant more than titles... more than anything... LANE: Scott, have you ever watched a minute of wrestling past 1957? This industry has taken off, taken off just in monumental numbers in the past few years. In this day and age it's not like being champion makes you the best. Theres no ladders to climb, no ranking system. You beat a couple of guys, kiss the right ass and come up with a catchy slogan and they're going to elevate you as far up as they possibly can...
Pelley: So you're saying... LANE: What I'm saying is if you can put some asses in the seats, if you can sell some T-shirts you're catching a boatload of exposure for yourself, you're making some scratch for the company, and they'll ride you to the promised land.. You'll be on TV, they'll having you hocking diet suppliments, energy drinks, spaghetti....
Pelley: But this is a bussiness.. and... LANE: <Puts both hands up in a STOP manner> It is a bussiness Scott, and I dont begrudge ANY MAN the oppertunity to earn as much cake as possible. But you gotta understand something when you're a guy whos as despised as me people dont want to buy your T-shirts, hell you couldnt get a manufacturer to MAKE THEM.
Pelley: I got to say Mike, I've seen the numbers.. um let me check.. (stares at paper) $3.4 million in 1997 (looks at Lane after) Uhh.. $5.1 million in 98' (makes the and so on and so on gesture with his hands) And in 1999 Mike...(stares foward) SEVEN MILLION... Seven Million dollars. I mean you cant say you werent making a healthy living during those days.
LANE: Scott, I was eating fine... Pelley: But the money... it... (CAMERA SHOWS LANE tighten his lips and nod his head) COMMERCIAL (PRIMETIME LOGO.. "Nothing Left to Give" DIRECTED BY [name withheld]) Pelley: <shrugs> so where'd the money go.. LANE: I'd say half went in the driveway... the other half up my nose... Pelley: You were a drug addict? LANE: That'd be making excuses. I don't need to use what I did back in the day as a crutch. I know I <BLEEP> up. You did the math, you read the figures. If I'd have stuck around I"d be the first guy in this industry raking in $10 mil a year... NO QUESTION.
Pelley: The first? LANE: Look at the big picture, there were guys who were great successes Scott... GREAT successes, you run down the list they held multiple times... dozens of times over... Great, but look where they did it Scott, they did it all in their own backyards. Scott theres a hundred companies out there with 100 world champions and they're all "the best". Bull<BLEEP>, they're the best where they're comfortable. Scott, I ran into the most hostile environments, places where people HATED me. People didnt bring signs they brought knives. I wasnt in WWL for two weeks when I started getting death threats..
Pelley: Death threats? There were people who actually disliked you enough to say they were going to kill you?
LANE: Dislike? the word is HATE Scott, they hated me, not for who I am, I'm a success, a winner... NO they hate me for what I do. Im not a Piker Scott. I dont work 9-5... I dont carry a lunchpail to work. And I do everything I SAY IM GOING TO DO... and thensome. Scott, I dont only SCREAM the talk, But I RUN THE <BLEEP>ING WALK, and theres SO MANY people in the world thats can't . THATS why people hate me, because people cant stand seeing someone they know is a better person then them. They cant stand knowing that this world isn't a balanced playing field that our founding fathers were full of <BLEEP> when they said all men were created equal Im living proof.
Pelley: Back onto the subject you were wealthy beyond your means. LANE: I bought this one house in Cos Cob, Connecticut// Third wealthiest town in America. It had a 4 car garage, so I filled it with 4 cars.
Pelley: More self destructive behavior? LANE: I didn't know any better. In 1999 i was 20 years old. I couldnt buy a 6 pack at the liquor store, but i could buy a Ferrari.
Pelley: Did it ever.... did it ever hurt your ability to perform. LANE: Scott, I operated on another level from everyone else. I was a "green" kid, into my late teens and early twenties and I was beating guys who've been running this industry for years. I didnt feel indestructable because I was delusional. I felt indestructable because I WAS.
Pelley: So what happened then, in your final match... (Camera shows Lane and fades out....) (PRIMETIME LOGO.. NOthing Left to Give DIRECTED by [name withheld]") LANE: (stares upward and lets out a deep breath) Pelley: Its tough isn't it? LANE: I wish I could answer.. I dont know what it was I dont remember. Pelley: It was a match for the WWL world title.. a triple threat match with you and Sean Edmunds facing the champ Keith Simpson.
LANE: Thats what the marquee read yeah... Pelley: So? LANE: Mike Lane wasnt there.. maybe in body, not in mind or spirit. Pelley: This was arguably one of the biggest matches of your life. I meanyou had won several titles here.. um Eastern States... Television, Extreme, Tag Team...United States... you just never got your hand on the WORLD title... but yet...
LANE: The night before I was out until 9 AM in the morning... 9 AM I was still high... rolling, <BLEEP>faced.. smoked 3 packs of cigs the night before... rolled out of bed around 1 AM... suprised I was alive.
Pelley: And the PPV was scheduled for a 5 PM start.... LANE: The guy who ran it... [name withheld] gave me a call. I had missed my flight into Houston. The guy had to hustle to get me a private jet...
Pelley: And so what happened then? LANE: Well, i got on the plane... we landed about 45 minutes before my match... the show was three quarters over... and I couldnt keep my eyes open... So I took a hit...
Pelley: a hit of... LANE: Dont even remember Pelley: So less than an hour before your biggest match.. the the the biggest match of your life you just took some random drug.
LANE: People tell stories among friends after a night of partying, yanno they'll say how the hell did I get home? They'll joke about it. But Scott. I dont know how the hell I got to that ring, I dont know how the hell i worked that match... and I didnt know till a week later I actually had the match.
Pelley: So why the comeback.. why now? LANE: Before I got foolish my life was put into this industry. MY last moment in this business wont be lying in the shower in a puddle of my own puke. When I'm done it'll be under my terms.. the way I want it to be. Kids and adults alike use to <BLEEP> their pants, scared of what I'd do next... I'll just let the rest of them know... keep a clean pair handy, because evil doesnt dress in all black, evil doesnt live in a graveyard. I AM EVIL.. If I have to snap your neck, if I have to dump your grandmother off a 5 story building I will. Because what I'm doing is making PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING my OWN
Pelley: Your own? LANE: Just watch.... (Sorry killed it so quick but its 2 am) M.Roberts — 13 May 2004 (Michael Dice is sitting in a plush VIP section of New York's renowned Club Z-No. A drink in his hand, smoked sunglasses covering his eyes and the purest silk covering his body, he smiles at the camera and raises his drink to the cameraman.)
DICE: Heyyyy, how you doin'? I'm glad you could join us tonight. For a man like you, this is a once in a lifetime opportunity. Not everybody gets to hang with Michael Dice, especially in the VIP, if you know what I mean. (Dice turns to his friends in the booth and shares a laugh with them.) The reason I got you here is because it's time for me to let the world know exactly who... Michael Dice is. You see, I don't have a sappy story for you to cry over, UNLIKE MY OPPONENT. But I do have some things to say about who I am.
(Dice clears his throat.) First off, I am your God. Secondly, I will always be better than you. And last but certainly not least, I am one sexy, sexy beast. (Dice's face glows with an overly cocky grin.) But seriously, who the hell is this punk Mike Lane? He's trying to dish out his own little E! True Hollywood Story... for what? Are we suppose to feel sorry for you that you snort cocaine like a mumbling idiot? Am I suppose to be scared of you because you ended it all on a dramatic tone? Or am I just suppose to laugh at you for trying so hard to make a strong first impression and ended up rambling on about sh(bleep) no one cares about?
I'm pretty sure the right answer is C. (Michael's crowd laughs.) Nobody really gives a damn about what you did in the past, guy. It's all about the present. Look at me, rich, young, successful... I am what you used to be. It must suck to watch a perfect and better reflection of yourself strut around and spend hundreds and thousands of dollars on property in Florida I visit once a year while your cokehead ass stands there like a sitting duck, a has-been who never reached a real prime. Yeah, you've had titles... so what? I did too. But we're in the big time now, sonny. This is where it all matters. Can you live up to the standards?
(Dice takes a swig of his drink and rests it on the table next to him.) Who do you really think you're scaring telling us you'll throw elderly people of the roof of a building? Come on, Mikey, At least you could of reached a little higher than that. Even a freakin' kid would do that with the right incentive. I'm tellin' you, whoever you're trying to fool must be a true idiot to believe you. You see, the difference between you and me is this... I know how to control myself. You were a train wreck then and you're a train wreck now. You think you can just come out of a 3 year coke binge and get back in the ring like someone sprinkled magical dust all of over your ass? Please, Mike. You're just begging for a rude awakening. Shock and Awe is a perfect name for the next WFW event, Mike. I'll let you think on that one.
Yeah, son. You're making professional wrestling your own... Your own hell. No one steps in the ring with Michael Dice and gets away without a beating, you remember that, Mikey.
I'll make sure your triumphant return is a very... VERY short one. Now get the hell outta my club!
(Michael Dice kicks at the cameraman, making him leave and returns to his entourage of gorgeous women, handsome men and dizzying alcohol. Once, that was the life of a competitor now sober, broke and trying to get back to the only thing that made him live... Once.
Man, sure sucks to be Mike Lane. --- BLACK!) Badnews — 13 May 2004 (FADEIN on Mike Lane calmly chewing on the nail on his thumb, staring away from the camera thats slowly panning in on him. he lifts his index finger up as if to make a point)
LANE: I guess you could say I'm not a pateint man. You could say it and nobody could question you. I've built that rep over the years. But I'm somewhat ashamed of myself, i'm ashamed I sat around all this time... waiting... Waiting to find out who MIchael Dice was... I waited, and waited. And just this morning I turn on the TV and there you are Michael Dice. Drink in hand, dressed to the nines, living the life... you looked....<shrugs> you looked good. But I learned something long ago, I learned you can slap fifty coats of metallic red paint on a 77' Pinto, tint the windows and drop a clever vanity plate on it, and your still left with... a <BLEEP>box. A <BLEEP>box that looks good, runs slow and blows up backing into a orange traffic cone.
(Lane rubs his hands together and then runs them down his face, he looks up and smiles LANE: But you know what never ceases to amaze me. Every fly by night kid who comes into wrestling to make "money" love to run their mouth... run it about ANYTHING, whether or not they have a GOD DAMN clue what they're talking about means nothing to them. Try doing your homework Dice, this isn't my first trip around the block. I'm not about making first impressions. I made my first impression in 96" and it was some hard talking kids head dead center in the middle of the canvas. Thats MY first impression. I didn't do it to impress anybody I did it to HURT somebody.
LANE: You know Michael Dice, I've wrestled 5 star classic matches and I've gotten slugged over the head repeatedly with baseball bats and thrown on piles of sharp things. A match is a match, anyway you choose to serve this puppy up I'll be more than happy to beat you that way. I don't know you Michael Dice. I don't know anything about you, outside of how you like to dress and what your beverage of choice is. I know nothing about you Michael Dice, and in the end... <grins> thats okay. Because you're not going to be around long. (shakes head) Naw, I'm not saying the throttling I'm going to give you at Shock and Awe will be enough to send you packing. But when those beatings add up, over time you'll be gone. And to me you'll just be "that kid". The same one I spoke of earlier, the one I made my "FIRST IMPRESSION" on. I don't remember his name, who he is, where he is now. He could have died in ninety-seven for all I know and for all I care.
LANE: I won't sit here and tickle your ass hairs saying I'm hoping for a competitive match and may the best man win, because thats bull(BLEEP). I want to break your skull open and swing your brain around my head by the spinal chord like a bola. I want parents covering their kids eyes while turning away, tears trickling down their faces. I want people downloading YOUR decapitation video on-line. And I don't want these things Michael Dice because you've done...a...single....thing...to hurt me. I want to do them because...well.. because you're there and its your own dumb luck you just happened to catch me at a time when I really DONT CARE. I'm coming back from nowhere, from nothing, with no place to go but UP. And to me Michael Dice, to me you're just another guy.
LANE: And what do I have to lose? A MATCH? HA! With all I've lost over the past few years having a sack of filth such as yourself lie on top of me while the ref counts to three isn't something thats gonna keep me up at night. SO Michael Dice, Shock and Awe won't be a time for celebration... not for you, not for me. I won't trumpet my own horn as a legend coming back.. I know better. At this point Dice, I'm much like you... just another guy,
