New World Madness: Edmunds vs. Wells vs. Copycat

30 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PaulNJ21 — 30 September 2003 WWL Legend's match... the winner gets bragging rights Cut your promos here. The RP deadline is Monday October 13th 11:59 PM. Send all angles to [detail withheld]. GARTHIsTheLaw — 5 October 2003 (Cueup: "Bottom of a Bottle" by Smile Empty Soul) (Fade in on a close-up shot of a hand holding a large number of strings, which appear to be attached to something off the top of the screen. After a moment, the hand releases the strings. The camera then moves to look up at the ceiling of what looks like a gymnasium of some sort as the balloons the strings were attached to float upwards - except for three, which drift downward. The camera focuses back on the hand, then zooms out to show that it belongs to Copycat. He wears jeans, his trademark beret, and a Tenacious D T-shirt. Next to him are the fallen balloons, attached to weights that keep them on the ground. Copycat glances at the fallen balloons)
Copycat: ...So. (He looks at the camera) Copycat: I now find myself in a triple threat match at New World Madness. My opponents? Two of the higher-profile opponents I've faced in my career. A couple of guys I haven't gone up against in the ring since 1999. March 23, 1999, in fact...in a league I won't mention the name of for posterity's sake. Now, I realize this match was four years ago, and likely has little bearing on the match I'm about to enter at New World Madness...but just for the record, that match saw me on the winning end. Copycat and Black Cat over Jared Wells and Sean Edmunds via pinfall. 'Course, it wasn't me scoring the pinfall, but a victory's a victory yes?
(Copycat shakes his head) Copycat: You'll have to forgive me for not making sense. I'm not in the best of moods after being screwed out of the North American Title by not one, not two, not three, but FOUR people. I've done some cheating in my career, but I don't think I've never felt the need to enlist THAT many people to help me win. I'll keep focused on the task at hand, though. Here's the tagline for my upcoming match, as I see it...something along the lines of, "After years of speculation, there will finally be an answer as to who is truly the top star of the WWL." And I'd consider that a pretty accurate tagline. Jared Wells, back in his "Rage 'o Fire" heyday, was the league's best right when it first started out. Sean Edmunds was the league's best during its really crappy middle period. And yours truly was definitely the best towards the end. So you could make the observation that I've got to be pretty excited about this match.
(Copycat shoots an annoyed glance at the camera) Copycat: ...But I'm not. The WWL had a good run, and it's one of the two leagues in which I've made the most name for myself. Trouble is...call me a killjoy...but the league is DEAD. And I don't see this match as much more than an attempt to cash in on the name recognition. Sure, I wouldn't mind a match with Jared Wells...he's going around saying the last time I met him in a ring he won, despite the fact that his last victory over me was in a tag team match in NINETEEN NINETY-F(BLEEP)KING SEVEN, wherein my partner was...fittingly enough...Sean Edmunds. And not only have I defeated him with Edmunds as HIS tag partner since then, I've also beaten him one-on-one - in the only one-on-one match Rage and I have had.
(Copycat cracks a slight grin) Copycat: And I wouldn't mind a match with Sean Edmunds either. Hell, I'd welcome it. Sean and I have had something of a...professional rivalry going for YEARS. And yet despite all that, I can't remember a time the two of us have actually met one-on-one in a match. There's been some tag matches here and there, but not even very many of those. A lot of the rivalry is based on nothing but years and years of trash talk. And getting a chance to finally bring that talk to the ring would be great. Particularly after Sean decided to show his cowardice by hitting me from behind with a chair in a match where we were supposed to be on the same damned TEAM. I think a good one-on-one with Edmunds would be just what the proverbial doctor ordered.
(Copycat chuckles emptily) Copycat: But that's not what this is. This isn't a culmination of years of rivalry in either case, because that's not the way Sean, Jared and I are viewed. We're not considered legends of the sport by WFW - we're considered legends only when the letters W-W-L are attached to them. While they continue to throw their weight behind such wastes of TV time as Alex Wylde and Scotty Michaels, we're looked at as nothing but special attractions. As much as I might not personally LIKE Sean Edmunds or Jared Wells, I DO respect them. More than WFW does. They don't see us as the prize pigs, that honor goes to the Lusters and Richard Gideons of the league - we're just the sideshow attractions, the two-headed chickens of the WFW County Fair. And as such, we're just going to be locked in a box and left to fight it out. Will the "bragging rights" we're fighting for have any meaning? Not that I can see.
(Copycat picks up the three fallen balloons by their strings and inspects them) Copycat: We're weighed down by this WWL stigma. The packaging, as WFW sees it, reads "LEGEND"...but the fine print at the bottom says, "Applicable only in WWL." And with Alex Wylde at the helm here, things can only get worse. Maybe this metaphor will inspire some realization...I'm not exceptionally hopeful. But the fact remains that as long as Sean Edmunds, Jared Wells and I won't get off the ground here until someone realizes that we aren't just WWL leftovers. Cast that off, and maybe then we really soar here.
(A pair of scissors is slid across the floor to Copycat. He picks it up and cuts loose the weights holding down the three balloons, though he does not release the strings)
Copycat: That's how Simply Sensational, Rage 'o Fire, and the Smartest Player in the Game can be elevated in WFW.
(Copycat releases the balloons, but re-catches them a second later and pops two of them with swift, brutal slashes from the scissors. He then releases the third)
Copycat: How well you'll be received after the Cat beats both your sorry asses at New World Madness, however, is somewhat debatable. And that, my equally stigma-laden friends, is just all there is to it.
(The camera follows the balloon up to the ceiling as we fade out) TheOriginalSE — 10 October 2003 ((FADEIN: Black and white film. There is old cheesy horror music playing in the back as the film plays. The camera follows the creepy scene as it goes through a house. First the living room, where an awful floral couch from the 1970's sits. They pan through the door into the dining room, where faux flower pots hang from the ceiling, with dust accumulating throughout. Finally the stops short as a pair of large scissors are raised in the sky. The camera catches a gleam which shimmers from the metal. All of a sudden the scissors come down and a sickening thud is heard. CUTTO: A nomral shot now, of the same kitchen, pulled back as we see 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds in a kitchen robe, with the scissors, carving out pumpkins. He's happily carving away even whistling. He looks up and sees the camera. Instead of stopping his carving he continues and speaks without looking at the camera.))
EDMUNDS: "I suppose if I was a whiny little (bleep) then I would feel for Copycat. Wah, wah wah.. I was screwed out of the North American title. Wah, wah wah.. Sean Edmunds hit me with a chair. Wah, wah wah.. the WFW is trying to profit off the WWL and hold me down. Jeez, Copycat. Lighten up a bit, eh? But then again, I suppose its better than listening to (mouths Jared) . Wah, wah wah.. Im going to beat Edmunds' ass. Wah, wah wah.. I lost to him. Wah, wah wah.. who took my coke and pot? (Edmunds covers his mouth with his hand) I hope I didn't blow your cover. (smiles) You see, with Copycat whining and (mouths Jared) lost in obscurity probably afraid of facing his fans after failing to live up to another promise, I'm stuck in a precarious situation. I could either sit back and be silent like Mr. Blazed o Fire.. or I could yap my mouth off like some lunatic whose upset that he's not getting his dues. But I guess since I'm bored with just hearing Copycat, I'll throw in my two cents worth. When I first saw that my match at New World Madness was against Copycat and (mouths Jared) I was excited. However, once I saw it was a 'bragging rights' match to see who the real legend of the WWL was, eh, then it lost its luster. You see, I don't understand the concept of this match. Put three men in the ring, to duke it out over a federation thats long dead? That's almost as insane as Richard Gideon claiming that he WAS the spirit of NGEN. I mean, as far as I'm concerned, there is no question who the WWL legend is. (looks up from carving) Me. (looks back down) After all, I am a three time WWL World Champion. I am a two time WWL Tag Team champion. And I did hold the WWL World Championship for the longest tenure of any champion past or pres... past. I single handedly revived the promotion. So why the big fuss? Money and hype. But that's ok. It's a sound marketing strategy and it'll work most likely. But as I said before, I'm not going to let it get me all riled up like some people have."
((Edmunds looks down at the carved pumpkin. He smiles as he turns it to the camera. It is a carved pumpkin cat, with tear drops coming from the open eye sockets.))
EDMUNDS: "So we'll start with Mr. Copycat. Let me start off by saying I had to do it. I couldn't let you get the pinfall, Copycat. That would have been like you winning a World Title and then allowing me to push you over with one finger and pin you. (Looks perplexed) Oh wait, (mouths Jared) was the imbocile that let me take his hard work from him. And besides, what other time would I have had the pleasure of smacking the back of your head with steel? I couldnt' obviously have done it after the match, because like you said leading up to the match, you would be watching me. So I had to improvise. I had to time it just right so that I could take you out without jeopardizing my victory. Now obviously we know that that failed because of someone butting their way into the match, but what harm came of it? At least I still got my point across. I don't like you, Copycat. I'm sure there are plenty of reasons why I don't .. none of which I can think of off the top of my head, but I know that I don't. I never have. You annoy and irritate me. (smiles) And yet strangely I'm drawn to you. Perhaps its because I see someone who is almost similar to myself in the fact that he prides himself on his talent. That you try to find any way, any way at all, to pull a victory out of the jaws of defeat.. and successfully pull it off. I know this WWL legends match irritates you, Copycat. Because you feel as if you're being held down. It's like the WWL is a rock shackled to your feet and you're falling deeper and deeper into a dark waterhole. (shakes head) You're coming into this match with the wrong ideals, Copycat. You're already focused on the negatives. Sure there are some positives because you get to beat on (mouths Jared) but really, what comfort is that? Everybody and their mom has done that. And you finally get a chance to wrestle me in something other than a tag match.. but then again, the red headed step child is in the way again. If you even desire to have the slightest chance at winning this match, whether you want the bragging rights or not, you need to wake up. You don't see me whining about it do you, Copycat? No. While I feel slighted because I even have to worry about defending my WWL career against (mouths Jared Wells') WWL career, I do not let it get the best of me. If you let it get the best of you, Copycat, which has already started to happen, then you will let Rage o Hot Air and 'Simply Sensational' get the best of you. And if there's one thing I know (smiles) its that you definitely don't want that happening."
((Edmunds looks at the Copycat pumpkin. He pushes it aside and takes a second pumpkin and starts away at it.))
EDMUNDS: "It's been a time, Copycat, since we've been in the same ring. Sure our tag match was just around the corner, but that doesn't count really, since we were on the same team (Edmunds chuckles as he says that) . God, I can't really remember much now that I look back. (licks lips) But I wonder, how is Icekold doing, Copycat? I can still taste her sweet lips on mine even after all this time. Her stiff upper lip, and that well defined jaw line used to keep me up nights way back then. I hope she's doing well, I'd hate anything to happen to her. But I know she's a big girl and can handle herself. Still, if she needs anything, she knows where to come ... (winks) "
((Edmunds stops carving the second pumpkin. He turns it around. It is a pumpkin that has bloodshot eyes and a doobie outlined near the mouth.))
EDMUNDS: "And next we get (mouths Jared) . The world's biggest underachiever. I've seen a lot of wrestlers imitate others to become stars, but there are a very few who I've seen ride anothers coattails as much as you. I brought you to the spotlight in the WWL, and then you used my name to invoke controversy and recognition in other places. You continually haunt me to try to bring yourself back in the spotlight so people can see that you exist still. (Mouths Jared) doesn't care about winning the bragging rights to be called the WWL Legend. He doesn't even care about the WWL. He only cares that his name stops being brought up on trivia games such as "Where are They Now?" Of course, if this match was fought by who lives vicariously through the past, then you would win hands down. Hell, by five, ten, fifteen feet! But sorry, (mouths Jared) this match will be decided by talent. And as much as you might hate to hear it, you are severely lacking in that department. I mean your credibility has been shot to (BLEEP) . You retire and come back and retire more times than anyone I've known. It's sort of the Sid Vicious syndrome. The first time you come back its like, hey wow, that's awesome! The second time you come back its more of a ok, I can kinda dig that. But by the third time you're dragging your sorry carcus out to the ring people are starting to wonder when the hell you'll retire for good! And time after time you've managed to disappoint those loving and adoring fans of yours! I mean, telling them that you're going to kick my ass all over the place? Then what happens, (mouths Jared) you blow it big time. You LOSE. (shakes head) That my friend is why after this match you should just follow the puff of smoke that will be eminating from under your lockerroom door right out the window and out of sight."
((Edmunds starts a third pumpkin)) EDMUNDS: "It's almost like I'm stuck in limbo. I've got Copycat, a stellar performer who has his priorities all wrong, and then (mouths Jared Wells) who has leeched off me since 1996 trying to keep his name in lights. You know both are like pumpkins almost. Copycat is full of light, burning brightly, but the candle overshadows everything, even his eyes so that soon they start to burn and rot until everything is a mess. And Mr. Fire over there, well his candle is burning hot too, except that if you look at the wick you'll notice it will be made by 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds, because without it he's just another hollow piece of (BLEEP) . But don't fear guys. Because while your two pumpkins fade away with time, the third one I'm making shall make it through the holidays. (Edmunds turns the third one around, it is not so much a face as it is writing, in stylized carving, saying 'Simply Sensational'.) There are many things to get used to in the WFW. Copycat needs to get used to being held back and screwed over. (mouths Jared) needs to realize that I won't always be so readily available for him to make a profit off me .. and the WFW management and roster will need to get used to the fact that 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is the only, and will always be the only, true WWL legend, if of course the title was more than just a shell of its former self."
((FADEOUT: Edmunds picks up the Copycat and Wells pumpkin as the camera goes black and white. He takes them outside to a "Windy" backyard scene as we're clearly back on a set again. The wind howls and leaves are blowing all around. Wells' candle goes out and his pumpkin goes dark. Shortly after Copycat's candle goes out and the pumpkin is just left with a dark spot around its eyes from where the candle burnt through. Edmunds smiles as he walks in the door and places his pumpkin, shielded by the window from the wind, in the window where it continues to burn.))
GARTHIsTheLaw — 11 October 2003 (Cueup: "Signals Over the Air" by Thursday) (Fade in on a dirty, garbage-filled back alley. Cheap, beat-up metal trash cans line the alley walls, overflowing with garbage. The edge of a a large metal Dumpster can be seen on the right side of the screen. Flies are buzzing around everywhere, and not to seem cheesy, but you can practically smell the filth even though as we all know smell doesn't travel well over film. After a moment, Copycat walks onscreen. He wears torn-up jeans, his trademark beret, and a Marvelous 3 T-shirt. After a brief pause to wrinkle his nose and swat at a fly, he speaks)
Copycat: So THIS is what it's like to hear Sean Edmunds cut a promo against you. I'd nearly forgotten. And I have to tell ya, I was tempted to just turn it off entirely when the first few seconds contained that lovable onomatopoeia "wah." But then I thought, "Surely someone as legendary as Sean Edmunds can do better than THAT. I'll give him a chance to redeem himself before I pass judgment." And I admit that the rest of it wasn't QUITE as terrible. But really...the fake crying, the crappy imitations, the ubiquitous Jared Wells drug reference...it took a lot to pull THAT one out of the trash.
(Copycat slaps the lid off one of the trash cans and pulls out a broken clipboard with a few sheets of crumpled paper on it. Holding it away from his face to avoid the odor, he reads a few things from it and then looks back at the camera)
Copycat: It's duly noted, Sean, that your mood is a little bit lighter than mine right now. You attribute that to what you consider your superiority to me, I'm sure. But then again, why wouldn't you be in a better mood than me? At Almost Live, you took on three people with two teammates of your own; an even match. Whereas I was taking on four people all by my damned SELF. I think I'm ENTITLED to my bitterness here. And I think, Sean, that if you were in my shoes, you'd be suffering from comparable, if not more, bitterness. But once again I'll focus on the issue at hand here. And the issue at hand is that, despite our differing temperaments, we seem to have - shock of shocks - similar attitudes about our match.
(Copycat squats down and and picks up something black from the ground. It looks to be a promotional poster for New World Madness, though it's crumpled up, torn in places, and covered in filth. He inspects it with a disgusted look)
Copycat: Those, of course, being that we're getting kicked to the bottom of the card for a meaningless "bragging rights" match. That we're being attached to the long-dead WWL again for no reason other than an ill-fated attempt to cash in on name recognition. Having us go one-on-one...that might have some merit to it. Like I've mentioned before, in all our years of professional rivalry I don't think we've ever gone one-on-one. And maybe that was the way it could have been in that WWL stigma weren't also attached to our good friend Mr. O'Fire. "So," WFW decides, "let's throw him in as well, we don't have much of a use for him either." And now we've got ourselves a grand old triple threat match.
(Copycat crumples up the promotional poster into a ball and slam-dunks it into the trash can he opened up earlier. He then looks back at his clipboard for a second before returning his gaze to the camera)
Copycat: Sadly, Sean...outside of some clichè shots at Jared Wells...excuse me...(mouths "Jared Wells" in a sarcastic manner) Your promo's credibility didn't last much longer. Claiming that you single-handedly revived the WWL...bragging that you held the title longer than anyone else...and from such STIFF COMPETITION, too! But the reality, Sean, is that while you may have been partially responsible for the revival of the WWL that one time...it isn't too hard to pull something out the crapper...
(Copycat pulls the lid off another trash can and tosses it aside. Turning his head to the side and closing his eyes tightly, he reaches into the mass of garbage and pulls out a championship belt of some sort)
Copycat: ...When you're the one who flushed it to begin with. Sean, the minute you won the WWL World Heavyweight Title from "Cruel" Keith Simpson, things started to go downhill. When the league came back from the dead, it wasn't because of YOU - it was because of [name withheld]. And then when he left, and put YOU in charge...that was one of the biggest disasters I've ever witnessed. I have serious doubts that the fact that I was put into match after match with the train wreck that was the HorrorShow while you owned the WWL was a coincidence...but that's okay, because that's what led to my breakout as that league's top dog, or top cat if you will. And THAT is something you can't dispute, pal - when the league split into three and the draft was held, I was the first draft choice for ALL THREE promotions.
(Copycat hurls the belt offscreen in the direction of the Dumpster) Copycat: But that's enough bragging. I do that any more and I'm going to feel like I'm writing one of YOUR promos. And yet, for all your claims, Sean, you seem to be coming up just a little bit short. You used such absolutely GLOWING terms to praise your decision to hit me in the back with a chair a few weeks ago, like it was the culmination of weeks and weeks of planning, and then you let Jared Wells, of all people, ruin your plans for you. To say you fail to impress is an understatement. Maybe I could go rooting in that Dumpster in an effort to find your credibility, but it's been in there too long...it's definitely gone rotten by now, and I don't feel like going too far in to find it. But maybe I'll just go take a quick look.
(The camera pans over to show the Dumpster full on-screen. Copycat hoists himself on the edge and rummages around for a moment)
Copycat: Well...what have we here? (Copycat drops back to the ground. In his hands he holds a smashed pumpkin, which he looks at with an expression of mock intrigue)
Copycat: Gee, it...it looks a lot like one of those ones you were using for such DRAMATIC effect, Sean. And what a coincidence that I should find something reminiscent of your latest promo in mine, since I saw something in yours that was a little bit reminiscent of mine. Maybe it's just that I haven't faced you in a while, Sean, but the old "setting significant to message" and "objects representing wrestlers" tricks look a damn sight like techniques I use. Of course, I wouldn't accuse of piggybacking a little off me, right? Accusing you of copying the Copycat...that wouldn't make any sense at all! Right?
(Copycat scoffs) Copycat: Sad fact, Sean...for all your own legendary status, for all the claims you've made over the years...we finally get a chance to go up against each other, and it's starting to look like the Cat's got a bit of an edge on you. The idea of using the pumpkins to represent Jared Wells and I? Clever. A little bit. But for a guy who used to call himself "The Original," your originality doesn't seem to stand up to that of a guy who goes by "Copycat." I get the points you were trying to make with the pumpkins, Sean. But I wasn't impressed. And it seems like, for all your hard work, I don't have to do much to shoot you down.
(Copycat hurls the pumpkin against the wall, causing it to burst into a shower of seeds and pulp)
Copycat: You got one up on me when we were on the same team, Sean, because I was thinking I wouldn't have to keep my eye on you until the match was over. You pulled a fast one on me, Sean, and for that I give you credit. But come New World Madness, my attention is going to be on you from the start. Jared Wells, I think we can agree, will be little more than an annoyance for both of us...just buzzing around the ring and getting in the way occasionally.
(Copycat swats away a fly) Copycat: But it's going to come down to you and me, Sean. And even though you got one up on me a couple of weeks ago, that was because I thought you were smart enough to save that until we'd won the match. Maybe you're not so smart after all, Sean...but come New World Madness, it's not really going to matter how smart you are. Because try as you might, you're not going to be able to outsmart the Smartest Player in the Game. I may not be real interested in the bragging rights, but one way or another, Sean, I will walk out of New World Madness the winner. And that, my Simply Sensational friend, is just all there is to it.
(Copycat walks offscreen) (Fade out) TheOriginalSE — 13 October 2003 ((FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is lying in his bed underneath the covers. He is smoking a Djarum Black as he stretches. He looks relaxed as he knows the three way match coming up at New World Madness is almost upon him.))
EDMUNDS: "Well I suppose its safe to say that (mouths Jared Wells) will not be much of a factor in this match. But then again, he never seems to be a factor in a match that he's in. His track record speaks for itself. So I guess that leaves me with you, Copycat. And while Rage o Fire will still be butting his head into things, you can bet that he won't take up too much of my concentration. That, my friend, is devoted simply to you."
((Edmunds takes one final drag of the Black and puts it out.)) EDMUNDS: "It's a nasty habit, I know. But sometimes you get the urge and you can't fight it. You know about that, don't you? Finally after all these years we'll be able to focus more on each other than partners. Finally, instead of having to worry about saving our partner's asses we can focus on our own. Because now we can't blame them when one of us fails. Copycat, while I admire your attempts to cast me as lacking in creativity, and even questioning my once important persona 'the Original', you do so in blind fashion. You, of all people, should realize that 'Simply Sensational' has never claimed that he oozes with creativity. And so it amazes me that you misrepresent my old monicker. 'the Original' was not a statement on my creativity, but a statement on my right to be given, without question, back the title that I never lost. And so, when [name withheld] decided to bring back the World Wrestling League and wanted to hold a title tournament to crown the World Champion I protested. When my appeals went unheard, tossed aside for reasons unheard of, I decided to go as 'the Original', because I was the original WWL World Champion beforehand and should have been again. And despite [name withheld]'s plan to keep the World title out of my hands I ended up with the last laugh, didn't I? In fact, it was all thanks to that easily influenced fool (mouths Jared) . You don't realize how simple it was to convince Mr. Rage o Fire to lay down for me. How easy it was to tell him we'd take over the WWL once more. (smiles) And you don't know how well it worked out that once that happened, Mr. Rage decided that he was going to visit Mrs. Ford's clinic for the third or fourth time, leaving me just the way I envisioned myself; alone once more atop the WWL pile."
((Edmunds smiles as stretches once more and turns another light on on the side table.)) EDMUNDS: "So yes, I have managed to destroy the World Wrestling League twice. Once after the first time I won the World Title, and then again when I convinced an eager [name withheld] to pass its reigns into my hands. You're very astute, Copycat. How nice of you to remember my plans for you once I had taken the WWL over. But you see, back then I didn't enjoy letting other people get MY limelight. So what better way to protect it than to drive the WWL into the ground? It couldn't have worked more perfectly. (chuckles) And then when [name withheld] demanded it back, I gave it to him, because I knew that my work was done. (pauses) Now Copycat, I won't be as brazen to say that the only reason you were drafted first in that laughable excuse of an experiment the WWL degraded into was because I wasn't in the draft. As everyone knows I had moved on by that point. As soon as the World Wrestling League was passed into my hands I was able to let go any sort of commitment or gratitude that had bound me to for years. So now you might see why I am not as bitter about this as you. I've let the WWL go, so to be continually associated with it does not bother me as it once did. Because I know that there is no longer that tie. I know that I have moved on. And while you continue to feel bound to the dead weight that was the WWL, I don't. And while you continue to get riled up over those connections, (smiles) I don't. I refuse to let something so petty bother me, Copycat. Yet you like to take that ball and run with it. So go ahead, Copycat, run for both of us. Run all the way to our gracious President Alex Wylde and complain. Because I just don't care."
((Edmunds pauses for a second and sighs. He fluffs the pillows next to him and then turns back to the camera.))
EDMUNDS: "If I were to let my pride get the better of me then I wouldn't care about pinning you to prove just who the better wrestler is. I would care about pinning you to salvage a badge that no longer has any meaning behind it. But, while the thought of you or (mouths Jared) being called the definitive WWL legend could somewhat irk me, its not my motivater. I want to pin you, Copycat, because you and I go back and forth for years without once pinning each other. For the tag team matches that we've had, none of them consisted of you pinning me, or vice versa. So while we've been jumping between promotions, either just missing each other, or never getting the chance to finally enter the ring, we've been setting each other up for nothing. And even now, while the third person in this match isn't worth the paper his name is printed on, he'll still be the one factor which will not settle anything.
