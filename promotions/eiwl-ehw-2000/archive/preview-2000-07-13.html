EIWL/EHW — Roleplay

EIWL/EHW Preview! Please Rate!!!!

13 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene 1 [The program opening starts. The camera is panning outside a small building. A sign in front of the building says "North Canton Hoover High
School".]
Scene 2 [The scene changes to inside a high school gym. It is a very nice high school though. There are glass balconies and lots of seating. A junkie old ring is set up in center of the gym. A man wearing a dark outfit and a mask from the movie scream is sitting in a wheel chair in a VIP area very close to the ring.]
Scene 3 [EIWL/EHW commissioner Scott Stud is sitting in one of the coach's offices inside the locker room. He is the only person back there. Stud is wearing a blue suit, and his long hair is flowing freely. Suddenly the phone rings.]
Stud: Hello! Unknown Voice: [Sounds like the killer in scream] Do you like extreme wrestling matches. Stud: Yes sir I do, but who the hell is this. Unknown Voice: Turn your TV to channel 55. [Classic highlights from the old EIWL are being shown, "Bulls on Parade" by
Rage against the Machine is playing. The camera focused on the TV is now just showing great highlights. Out of the blue EIWL/EHW flash's up on the screen in big blue letters.]
Tim 2 Tough: Hello wrestling fans and welcome to the EIWL/EHW preview match. We are live from Hoover North Canton High School, in Canton Ohio. I am Tim 2 Tough, and along side with me is Kodi Kidd.
Kodi Kidd: [Spits chew into his bottle] That's right this is xxxtreme wrestling, and tonight we have one very special match. It is our xxxtreme wrestling comeback preview match.
Tim: That's right, tonight "The One" is scheduled to face "Jack Hammer". Kodi: Jack who, that sounds like some kind of construction tool. Tim: It is you moron. Kodi: Oh Tim: Well we have our first interview of all time right now. Our J.T. is back in the locker room area with Jack Hammer, let's see if we can find out what is going on.
Scene 4 [J.T. acts as if he is going to knock on the locker room door, but then decides just to bust in. Luckily Mr. Hammer is just relaxing and drinking a soda pop.}
J.T.: Mr. Hammer you have a match with "The One" tonight. What do you think your chances of winning are?
Jack Hammer: I guarantee victory! Jack Hammer will not lose his first match, he will win big!
J.T. Well you sound very confident. So what do you think about "The One"? Hammer: I respect him because he is a decent wrestler, but he can't beat me because HE IS NEXT!
Tim: Hmm a moment of silence from J.T. I think he is a bit confused. Hammer: Well J.T. would you like a soda. J.T.: No thank you, I think I am getting out of here. Scene 5 [Back at the announcer's booth] Kodi: Well Mr. Hammer seems pretty pumped up for the big match. Tim: Yeah that's great Kodi, well it is time to start our first match. Kodi: I am so excited I can back hold it all in. Tim: Well we were to poor to buy a titan-tron for this, like the big time fed's, so we rented a big screen TV
[The TV comes on by the wrestler entrance. Green letters flash quickly changing all over the screen, exactly like in the movie the matrix. The crowd is silent in anticipation. A big man wearing a white wife beater, blue jeans, and sunglasses comes out.]
Kodi: Is this him, is this "The One"? Tim: No, who is this? "The One" was suppose to come out. This isn't him; this is Byron B. Buffington. What the heck is he doing out here, and wear is "The One". [Buffington makes his way to center ring, and grabs a microphone] Buffington: I don't know what happened to that jobber "The One", but I am sure that I am not as boring as he would have been.
[The camera flashes back to the guy in the wheel chair dressed like the killer in scream, then back to Buffington.]
Buffington: Well I guess it is time. Bring out that pathetic loser. Bring out Mr. Hammer!
[A song made by Slim Shady for Hammer plays throughout the gym. Hammer comes out with the lovely Courtney Wlakins by his side. Hammer is also a very big man.]
Kodi: It is good and time we got some babes here. [Mr. Hammer is wearing long black tights, and black boots. Mrs. Wlakins is also dressed in all black. Hammer is still sipping on his pop while he comes out.]
Tim: Hammer looks mad at this turn of events. This could be the battle of the titans. [The camera pans back to the guy in the wheel chair who is just clapping.] Kodi: Yeah I bet you could boil an egg on his forehead right now. Tim: I think you mean fry you moron. **********Match Summery "Byron B. Buffington" 0-0 v. "Jack Hammer" 0-0******** Hammer dominated most of the match with his power moves. Buffington had one excellent rally and almost put the match away with a BIG BOOT, but Hammer was able to get out of the way. The match was almost to clean to be extreme, and Mrs. Wlakins did not interfere at all. The match was locked up after Hammer hit Buffington with the T-Wall (triple choke slam).
Match Time: 8:27 ************************************************************************ Tim: Wow, Hammer really dominated, but look how kind he is. Hammer is trying to give him a hand up.
Kodi: What a gentleman. Tim: Who Hammer? Kodi: No Buffington, I love the way he slapped his hand away. Nice play has no room in our sport.
[Camera shows Kodi spitting chew in his bottle again. The camera switches back to the man dressed in the Scream outfit. He is showing little emotion and his just clapping very calmly.]
Tim: Yeah your right Kodi, but one question. Who the hell is that in the wheel chair? Kodi: I think your dad broke out of the home again. Tim: Errr, well folks that is all the time we have for this special preview tonight. Join us again Friday July 21, for our first regular Friday card.
Kodi: Were on Friday? Tim: Were out of time!
