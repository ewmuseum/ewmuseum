75 lines... The NYSW is just starting out so we need all components... wrestlers, card writers, and other useful staff members... Please read the rest of the packet and join... we need you! The NYSW will be looking to implement a lot to the wrestling world...
================== BACKGROUND ================== The NYSW federation was started by Johnny Risuti... Jonny is an old fashioned Italian... He gambles, has a lot of friends , and is not to be messed with. He isn't like Vince McMahon ... He doesn't always need to be the center of attention... He always makes sure things are running smoothly and if you upset him more than once you're gone. He has a great sense of humor, It wouldn't be uncommon for him to bust your chops. He is a very respectable man... He's rational, reasonable and can be very helpful.
