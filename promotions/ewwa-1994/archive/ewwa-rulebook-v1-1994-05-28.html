I How do I sign up?
Send me e-mail at
[detail withheld]
with the following data
on each of your wrestlers:
Name--her name. Real names should not be used, but takeoffs on them are acceptable (i. e. no Hillary Clinton, but Hillbilly Clinton is ok
(only one of her, though)). (Note: male players and managers are allowed, but only women actually step into our virtual ring.)
Manager--if she has one, its name and how it behaves.
Height and Weight--in metric or US traditional (be realistic here...
I've never seen a woman over 6-8, and even Kate Moss weighs 100#).
Hometown--where she lives.
Moves--see section II.
Finisher--be realistic, but not boring.
Music--if she has entrance music, identify it.
Alignment--good, semi-good, neutral, semi-evil, evil (choose ONE).
Notes--anything else you want me to know about her.
