Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The True Test of Skill Wrestling Federation introduced itself on 13 August 1999 as “a brand spanking new booked e-fed” built around a weapons-heavy style it claimed “no other fed, real or e-fed” had dared to run, alongside a slate of interfederation titles and pay-per-views: World, Hardcore, World Tag, and Cruiserweight belts, with Television, Intercontinental, and U.S. Tag titles promised to come.

By October the promotion had changed hands. A bulletin of 4 October 1999, posted by its incoming administrator, describes taking over TTSWF “soon to be formerly known as the laughing stock of e-wrestling,” citing the outgoing administrator's troubled run and asking the newsgroup for time to rebuild free of that reputation.

Under the new administration the promotion staged its shows under the banner “Flames,” run by an on-air authority figure billed as President Vengence at the head of a heel stable called the Hit Squad. A surviving 15 November 1999 card, called live from the Target Center, features Vengence's own Hit Squad running roughshod: Slinger falls to a debuting Nick Nasdaq after Vengence puts a bounty on Slinger's head; “Goldenboy” Ric Anderson, billed as a Hit Squad member, beats newcomer Devon Ace; “Smutman” Jack Leer beats “Superkid” Shawn Stevens and his snake-wielding partner Python; and Steve Black, driven half out of his mind by a returning Chucky, retains the TTSWF Hardcore Championship in a brutal brawl that ends with Chucky crushed under a pop machine. In the main event, two-time champion Brad Steel wins the World Championship back from El Toreador after El Toreador is caught throwing powder in his eyes, and the card closes with a brawl involving Steel, Vengence, El Toreador, and several other wrestlers as a rival referee is teased for the following week.

The card's own closing credit dates it to late October 1999, ahead of its own posting; nothing in its surviving record follows it, and how the Hit Squad storyline, or the TTSWF itself, played out afterward isn't known.
