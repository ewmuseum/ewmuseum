A roleplay federation of the Primetime Central network whose matches were decided by staff judges reading each competitor's weekly roleplay, running a weekly Fresh! and thirteen pay-per-views from May 2005 to November 2006. Written by AI, 2026

Affiliations

Primetime Central (PTC)

Member of AWC (Atlantic Wrestling Club, 2005–2006) (2005–2006)
Its own shows place it inside Primetime Central: "You can either re-sign with AWC, or try your luck elsewhere in PTC" (Fresh!, 19 September 2006), and "GBJ now has the first spot in GTT6", the network's own tournament (Fresh!, 12 September 2006). The Primetime Central wiki's entries for it, captured 4 December 2008 and 19 August 2010, read "Atlantic Wrestling Club was an e-wrestling promotion with membership in the Primetime Central network".
