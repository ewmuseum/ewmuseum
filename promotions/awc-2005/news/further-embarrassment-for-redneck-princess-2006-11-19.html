Further embarrassment for Redneck Princess

19 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Teresa Tomas has gained notoriety and lost efficiency of late; her AWC career took another downward turn this weekend as more images from her now infamous AWC Magazine photo shoot were leaked onto the internet. These images were taken by "guerilla" photographers, probably amongst the crew who helped set up the shoot rather than from within the photographic agency, and differ from those published in the magazine in that they are far from tasteful or elegant. To view the images for yourself, click here and here. Note that these images are not safe for work. Tomas is said to be distraught at the leak, not having even been aware of the existence of these images. Fellow AWC female Amy Campbell has shown little sympathy for her colleague: "There are always assholes out there, and it's her own damn fault for doing the shoot in the first place. Get over yourself." AWC Commissioner Mike Wade has quickly moved to capitalise on the publicity surrounding these developments by booking Tomas and Aimz against one another at Triangles. Rumour has it he originally intended to sign a "Bra and Panties" match, but Entertainment Manager David Harber was able to block the move because such a match stipulation is not listed on AWC's master bank of match types. Spoilsport.
