Trio not up to the challenge

13 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This week's edition of Fresh! featured a virtually unprecedented THREE title bouts, but it was still not enough to dislodge a single champion from their post. In chronological order, Jack Murphy saw off the challenge of an off-colour Red Rock, Johnny Lexicon boosted his Livewire credentials as well as wresting the King Prawn from Blue Ruin, and the "East Bay Executioner" Darcy Crisis avenged his pay-per-view loss with a success over Aimz. Crisis and Murphy now appear to have shown their solidity as Frontier and Relentless champions respectively. The last round of the Relentless Frontier Gauntlet this week will see Teresa Tomas challenge Murphy; the Bull himself then takes on "D Masta C" immediately afterwards for the Frontier belt as a result of his cashing in of part of the Grand Slam Package he won a year ago at Triangles '05. Just the Alliance championship shot now remains for Murphy, but with the contract set to expire at Triangles in eleven days' time, it is unlikely the Bull will find opportunity to give The Coalition their first challenge. Blue Ruin is reported to have migrated to live a life out on the ocean in search of shrimp as sizeable as that which now belongs to Lexicon. Friend Quinton C. Meischker describes him as "distraught". He left just this brief note for the King Prawn's new possessor: "To deshell the KING prawn, first hold onto the tail while gently removing the shell around the body".
