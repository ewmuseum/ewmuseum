Hips Don't Lie

13 June 2006

Date: 06/13/06 Anton sat on the edge of the chair, his eyes burning stigmata into his palms. He wore a white wifebeater—clean, to put the Nigerian outside the cliché—and dark shorts. He wasn't dressed for any sort of fight, yet he had to wage one. His insides were a roiling ocean, turning with anxiety, burning with emotion. It took all he had to school himself into not shaking. He finally closed his eyes and buried his face in his hands, drawing breath sharply through his compressed nose. Everything. Everything was in front of him now and Anton had not the slightest clue of what to do. He felt a pair of silky hands slide over his shoulders and he drew his head up a bit, turning to face Amber. Like a favorite concubine, she sat in her tantalizing short black one-piece on the arm of the chair, pressing herself into him and smiling. Anton groaned as she massaged his shoulders; they were tense, of course, and knotted, and her hands seemed to unwravel the Gordian tangles without using Alexander's cut. Anton sighed, relaxing himself. She had brought him this news, this wonderful, terrible news. She had been looking out for his best interest, she'd said. She had done everything—everything!—she could. She had told him so sincerely, with that look in her eyes, he had had no choice but to believe her… but why did it have to be this way? The salvation that he had secretly hoped for, wished for, was right in front of him, but why did it have to be this way? Why did it have to rip them apart? ''Anton, honey…'' Amber cooed, resting her chin on his shoulder. He brought his hand up and stroked her hair. ''Anton, you have to think of your future. You have to. Luis… Luis can fend for himself, can't he? He can find another. But you, I can't let you stay like this.'' She gave a helpless whimper. ''I can't.'' Anton's response was dead silence. He interlocked his fingers, squeezing his hands together. He was all anguish now, the cogs in his head churning frenetically. He could not stand to be under Sasha's oppression for much longer, under her judgment, but this… this was too much. He tilted his head backwards and drew in a deep breath, cooling his mind enough so that he could order his body to stand. Amber's hands slid away from him and she looked upwards, her eyes curious. Expectant. Anton opened his eyes, but he didn't look back at her. ''I must walk,'' Anton announced. He took another deep breath. ''Alone.'' Turning her aside was always hard—even now, icy fingers squeezed at his stomach—but he had to. His exit was swift, made by circumventing the couch and slipping out the front door. He didn't take keys or anything else except for himself. He had to think. This decision could not be made in haste. Anton left a temper tantrum in the house that went by the name of Amber. She managed to keep herself from breaking anything, but all the same, she was incensed. She was hurt, angered, enraged to find that Anton was not clay, that Anton had a mind and thoughts of his own. That Anton was loyal. Why could he not just listen to her and accept it as she told him? Why did he have to… to think? Amber curled in on herself on the chair that Anton had vacated and barely stopped herself from sobbing uncontrollably. Nneka disappeared back into the room she shared with Luis and closed the door, pressing her back up against it. So, that was Amber's game? She could get Anton papers—forged for the moment, Nneka assumed—that would allow him to go free of Sasha. To be his own wrestler again. Amber had everything ready to rip Anton away from Luis and leave Luis lying in dust without so much as a word of thanks or a goodbye. Everything ready to take control of Anton herself, to steer him as she wanted. So she could get her star on the rise, too, share in fame she had no part of making. Nneka wrapped her arms around herself. She hated Amber—she hated, hated, hated her—and she couldn't, for the life of her, figure out how to get Anton away from her. She wasn't conniving like Amber was. She wasn't good at this sort of thing. She had no practice. And Luis… well, Luis was having just as much trouble with her, and he was actually skilled in this area. A little light at the far end of the room flashed on and the ringtone—Chris Brown's 'Run It'—played quietly. Nneka slipped across the room and picked up the phone, hitting the talk button and pressing it against her ear. ''Hello?'' ''Fuck!'' came a thick voice from the other side. ''Nneka!'' Nneka recognized the voice after it called her name. ''Luis!'' she cried, making her voice low. ''What's wrong!?'' ''E'rythin','' Luis replied. His voice was desperate. '''S all wrong, Nneka. Give th' phone to the Lion.'' ''He's… he's not here.'' ''What!? Oh, fuck, you hadda find him! Quick!'' Nneka's heart was racing now. She glanced back at the door as if Amber could look straight through it before pushing herself into a corner. ''What's going on, Luis?'' Nneka asked. ''Tell me.'' She shot a look back up. She didn't know what the woman was capable of. ''It don't matter… fuck!'' Nneka's eyes widened as Luis stopped talking. What was happening to Luis? He was… he was in trouble. Nneka knew that much. But what kind of—''Girl, it don't matter.'' 11 millimeters of relief washed through her body; not enough to put her completely at ease, but it did make her feel as if she'd just been doused in ice water. ''You gotta wri' down this address, tell the Lion t'come here.'' Luis began giving numbers and Nneka burst forward. ''Wait!'' she cried. ''Wait, let me…'' If she could have pierced her skin, she would have clawed it into her arm if she thought it would help her get to Luis faster. She snatched up a permanent marker and uncapped it, holding out her arm. Her hand was shaking with fright for Luis, but she managed to write down the address. In a few minutes, Nneka was hurtling out of the apartment building and running down the street. She hadn't wasted time with putting on shoes, so her bare soles slammed repeatedly on the pavement. She didn't care, though. She only had a thought for Luis's welfare and for how utterly impossible it was going to be to find her big brother. She trusted herself, though. She had to. She couldn't let Luis down, not now, not when it sounded like he might die if Anton didn't reach him. She continued to run, turning a corner and not letting herself slow down. She tilted her head down, trying to make her body a little more sleek so that she could run faster. Faster, faster, faster, faster, and still… stopped. Her arm hit a snag—a snag in the air?—and she spun wildly, managing to stay on her feet and yank her arm backwards. Her eyes flitted up and a black shape loomed over her, inquisitive eyes staring straight into hers. Her heart skipped twelve beats and she nearly dropped to her knees. ''Anton!'' she forced out. ''Anton, I found you! Oh my—'' ''What is it?'' Anton asked, taking a step forward. He caught her by the shoulders. ''Why do you run so fast? To find me?'' ''It's Luis,'' she breathed. ''He's… in trouble.'' Anton shook a bit as Nneka delivered the news. Why was Luis always getting himself into trouble? Why didn't he just go clean, just give all this up? Anton made excellent money wrestling. Luis had no need to do this drug trade, but he still persisted. He still did it, despite everything Anton said against him. Now Anton had to bail him out. Again. He gritted his teeth. ''Trouble,'' Anton repeated stiffly. Of course, Anton could always leave him be. He could always leave him to whatever fate was coming to him—death or otherwise. Anton had thought about it multiple times, every time he had to step in and save Luis. Every time he had to keep Luis alive, to fight the little man's fights. Anton was, quite frankly, tired of it. It was his choice. Anton suppressed a wry laugh. So-o! He had come out here to think, to clear his thoughts a little, to help himself make this decision, and this is what it came to? He had wanted to stall, he figured, to put it off. He had wanted to do this calmly. God—or the gods or the spirits of his ancestors or whoever was controlling the universe—did not have that in mind, it seemed. It was the same choice that he had had to make inside, except now, it might involve Luis's life. Now, he had to make his decision in a split-second. ''Where is he?'' Anton asked. ''Take me there.'' ''Why did you bring him back here!?'' Amber shouted. Anton looked down at Amber; they stood in their room, the door pulled almost completely closed. Amber had run in here the moment that Anton had carried Luis into the apartment and now this. Now, when he tried to console her, she shouted at him. Anton couldn't win, it seemed. ''Because,'' Anton said plainly, ''he needed help. He lives here, as do you.'' ''But… but he got himself into that,'' Amber said with a quiet firmness. Anton gritted his teeth; he knew that better than anyone. ''He got himself into whatever situation, he deserves what he would have gotten.'' Anton leveled his gaze at her and shook his head. ''Don't say such things,'' Anton told her. ''It's true!'' Amber took a step forward, gripping his wrists and looking up into his eyes. ''It's all true. He's already fucked up so much, Anton.'' Anton lifted his chin, setting his jaw. ''Look at me. Please. He's already done all this to himself, you can't keep cleaning up after him.'' Anton did look back at her then, but it was only to shake his head again. ''I am going to see how he's doing,'' Anton said, turning around and easily pulling his hands away from her to head into the main room. Amber was quick to follow, but Anton didn't look back at her. In a few strides, he was overlooking the couch that he had lain Luis on. Nneka's arm was looped around Luis's body and she had lain her head over his chest. Luis's eyes were open now and he surveyed Anton curiously. ''Thanks, Lion,'' Luis said weakly in Spanish. ''I owe ya one.'' ''You always do,'' Anton replied, the language the same. Luis grinned, but Anton simply folded his arms over his chest. Luis's grin quickly dropped off. ''You know, Nneka told me what she's doing,'' Luis informed him, nodding his head towards Amber. ''I told you it's exactly what she'd do. I told you, Lion.'' Anton shut his eyes. He did not want to argue about this right now. ''You did say that,'' Anton said carefully. ''But—'' ''For the rest of us,'' Amber said in the only language she knew: English. ''Could you carry on this conversation so we can understand you?'' Anton glanced at her and quickly looked away, not desiring to see the fury painted on her face. He hated to see her so. When his eyes fell back on Luis, though, his eyes were searching the American girl with a curiosity. Like a hunter's eyes as he watches his prey, scouts it. Suddenly, Luis chuckled. ''I'm sorry,'' Luis said, now in English. ''My fault, pink pussy.'' Amber gave a little start, eyes wide as she stared at Luis. Luis glanced up at Anton. ''You know, Lion, that thing you said about her hips?'' Amber gripped her skirt. Anton blinked a bit, clenching his fists. He wasn't going to hear what he thought he was about to. He wasn't. ''You're right. It is fantastic. She also does this thing with her tongue that is just… wow. Hah. She is a really, really good lay. Ain't surprised you want her so bad…'' Anton had banged out of the door before he finished. Amber stood there looking after him, stupefied as if her brain had turned to jelly. Luis eyed her curiously; Nneka had stirred a bit, but he would deal with her later. Amber was the girl he had to take care of now. ''Go on,'' Luis told her, sniffing arrogantly. ''Go to him. Tell him what you told me. Tell him that sex isn't love. Tell him! See how the Lion takes it.'' His words followed the shaking woman out the door. He looked back at the girl with her arm around him and sighed. Now he just had to explain himself to someone he actually cared about. Luckily, Luis was good with words. Squatting on the roof, Anton watched Amber come up from the stairwell and he snarled, advancing on her quickly. ''Why!?'' he roared at her. Amber squeaked and jumped backwards, still in shock from what Luis had revealed. Anton made himself calm down. He feared that he would hit her. He couldn't hit her, though. He couldn't. ''Why would you do that!?'' ''I—'' ''After all you said about hating the man, you… you… you make love to him!'' ''I didn't 'make love' to him,'' Amber said defensively, folding her arms under her breasts. ''I fucked him. There's a big difference.'' ''A whore's difference!'' Anton bellowed, wheeling around. Amber sputtered and hurried forward to console him. ''Anton, please,'' she begged. ''I just—'' ''And you want me to go away from Luis for this?'' Anton snapped, turning to face her again. ''For you? For someone who cannot be… cannot be faithful to me, to let you manage me? To put my things in your hands? For what!? So someone can… can fuck you and take them?'' ''Don't,'' Amber said, thrusting her lower lip out. She lowered her head. ''Don't talk like that, Anton. Please.'' ''I'll talk however I want.'' Anton paced around the roof. How could she? How could she betray him like that? How could he trust her now, think of her as anything but a whore? A jezebel? ''I'll say whatever—whatever—I choose to you, Amber.'' He kicked himself inside as he continued to pace. Now that he had said that, he didn't have anything to say to her. Snarling, he rounded on her and put his face right before hers. ''So tell me, Amber. Tell me why I should give everything I have ever done, everything I I have come to this business with, why I should give everything up for a common… prostitute.'' Amber made a sound in her throat, a whimper. She hesitated before she spoke again. ''Because… because, Anton. Because I love you.'' Anton threw his head back and laughed riotously. ''She loves me!'' he howled, still laughing. There was not a hint of celebration in it; every decibel of it was mockery. ''The woman loves me!'' Amber sank to her knees, shaking with… tears? Anton supposed they were tears. He looked out across the city and shook his head. Amber mumbled something—''sex isn't love''?—and folded her arms across her stomach. She said it again. Now Anton was sure that was what she had said. Anton felt a very strong desire to kick the woman. Instead, he turned and walked inside, walking away from her for the second time tonight. Both had a very strong feeling that this time, though, might be forever.
