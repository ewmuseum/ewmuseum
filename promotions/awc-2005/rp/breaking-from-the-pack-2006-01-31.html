Breaking from the Pack

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/06 Hammer knocks on the door of the modest hotel room directly across the hallway from his own. After a few minutes some grumbling is heard coming from the other side of the door. “Hooouuuse-keeping,” Hammer says in an affected Spanish accent as he excitedly raps on the door again. “Shut the hell up, douchebag,” a gravelly-throated voice responds from the other side of the still-closed door. “I know it’s you.” Hammer finally hears the chain lock sliding off of its catch and the door slowly swings open to reveal Hammer’s Tempered Steel partner Anvil. Anvil’s wiry black hair is quite mussed, probably from sleeping and Temps’ big man immediately raises a hand up to shield his eyes from the light coming from the hallway. “Why are you waking me up, dude?” Anvil questions during a slow, sighing exhale, a clear indication of his frustration. “It’s 5 o’clock in the fucking morning.” Hammer takes a step back and raises his hands up in a ‘no mas’ sort of way. “Sorry man,” Hammer says with a genuine sense of regret evident from the tone of his voice, “I just wanted to see how you were feeling. I can’t sleep at all tonight thinking about my singles debut! And this hotel, this is a million times better then those blue-light cheap hotels we’d have to stay in when we wrestled in high school gyms and supermarket parking lots!” Anvil raises an eyebrow and nods before motioning for Hammer to enter his room. As he walks in Hammer cranes his neck to check out Anvil’s bed. “I’m not interrupting anything here, am I?” Hammer asks as he enters the room. “Yeah, right. The only thing you’re going to find out here in Indiana are ax murderers and heffers,” Anvil replies, smiling as if pleased with coming up with what he did, “and I ain’t interested in either kind, if you catch my understanding.” “Uh...” Hammer responds with a full dose of smarmy attitude dripping from each syllable as he speaks them, “you mean you’re not into axe murderers or heffers, sexually?” “Exactly,” says Anvil as he sits back down on his bed and runs his hands through his hair in an attempt to get it back under control, missing Hammer’s sarcasm completely. “Listen, Andy, I’m not booked tonight. I could really appreciate the sleep because how often do we ever get a nice day in the middle of the week where we don’t have to worry about deadlines, don’t have to worry about schedules – an entire day to just relax? I’ve got one today, sport. Now I’d appreciate it if...” “Listen up, Hayes,” Hammer responds, “I’ll let you get your precious sleep although by the look of your gut you should probably take today in the gym!” “Shut the fuck up,” Anvil flatly replies, unable to even look at Hammer as he says it. “It helps me with flexibility in the ring and you know it.” “You keep telling yourself that, buddy,” Hammer needles on, smirking gleefully as he does, “I always say it would help our image if you could get as buff as me!” “You better wipe that smile off of your face,” Anvil rips back, “or I’m going to make you pretty unmarketable pretty quickly, you prissy little fuck.” “Yo!” Hammer says as he laughs, “now there’s the Anvil we all know and love!” “It’s not really your singles debut either, genius,” Anvil says as he looks back over at his partner to gauge his reaction. “What are you talking about?” Hammer asks in response. “Why the hell do you think you have the day off? I’m in that Four Way Fury match tonight!” “Have you even looked over the AWC roster yet? I asked you to do that as soon as we were signed. They’re some people here who think long-windedness rocks and I want you to get familiar with them.” Hammer nods meekly in agreement, as if being scolded by a teacher. “But bro,” Anvil flogs on, “in case you hadn’t noticed this match is not all about you. This is about the Alliance Division. This is about breaking from the pack.” “Still not following you,” Hammer replies. “Collision Course, Norton & Bridges, Pleasure & Pain and Tempered Steel will all be represented in this match,” Anvil explains, “you idiot.” “Damn man,” Hammer responds, scratching the top of his head, “I didn’t realize that.” “Obviously,” Anvil replies. “And another thing, don’t wake my ass up before 10 o’clock on days when I’m not booked – please?” “I haven’t had a singles match in a few years,” Hammer continues, ignoring Anvil, “it’s just been us training or in duo matches and all that. I was getting sort of excited about the whole thing and it’s just strange...” “You can still make a mark,” Anvil replied, “but just remember this isn’t all about you. And about waking my ass up...” “Yeah, yeah,” Hammer mutters, as if just realizing something for the first time, “thanks bro. I’ll catch you later.” “Listen,” Anvil says before Hammer leaves the room, “you’ve got Jiro Sennosuke in there and you already know what he can do.” Hammer looks up indignantly at Anvil; he had already been mapping out his own strategy. “Try to stay away from Bridges & Russell, they’ve both got over 50 pounds on you.” “Really?” Hammer responds, “great advice, thanks. Should I also ‘try not to get pinned’ and ‘try not to submit?’ I’m out of here.” Hammer exits and crosses the hallway back into his own room and plops himself back down onto his bed. He leans backwards and stares at the filthy light fixture affixed to the cracked yellow paint ceiling in his room and exhales deeply.
