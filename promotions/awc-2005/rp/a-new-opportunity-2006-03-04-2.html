A new opportunity

4 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 4, 2006 Staff Sergeant Cage…CENTER! The 6’3 athletic physique sharply marches to his position in front of the commanding officer. The four squads of Marines wearing their dress blue Delta uniforms stair straight forward with the bearing the jarheads are known for. Officer: On this day, I hereby proclaim Staff Sergeant Victor M. Cage discharged honorably by the United States Marine Corps. He shall from here on out, carry on his civilian duties to his country which he has proudly fought for….Victor, you served me, your fellow Marines and you country well. We’re sorry to lose a great Marine like you. Take care and God bless. POST! After a few congratulatory handshakes and a nice plaque, Cage’s time in the Corps was over. After fighting in the same modern war on two different deployments, including an injury on his second trip, this is all this great American hero is given. A chest full of ribbons and awards and he gets a small monthly thank you check from the politicians that put him in that Iraqi hell for over a year. Being pushed back out into the civilian world is scary to most service members, especially after they’ve been brainwashed by the military for eight years. But this was Cage’s turn to face the world he left behind eight long years ago. While in the Corps, Cage drifted away from the one thing that was always there for him…the Catholic Church. Cage: Forgive me father for I have sinned. It’s been seven years and seven months since my last confession. Father: My son, your God is a forgiving one. What else does your heart carry? Cage: Father, since my last confession, I have killed many men in the name of war and peace and have never asked for forgiveness for spilling their blood. I also have no where else to turn. I’m looking for God’s help to guide me to my calling in life. Father: My son, the bible says not to murder thy neighbor. Killing in the name of war and more importantly peace is looked upon differently in the eyes of God. You can always to the Catholic Church for guidance. Cage: Father, how do you know God’s perception on things? How do you know what’s really right from wrong. If I didn’t feel wrong for killing those many people, I wouldn’t be here confessing today. Father: I rely on the Lord for answers to your questions of life and mine. He provides to me, my son. Cage: So you’re telling me, God talks to you and tells you what is right and what is wrong? Father, no offense, but that sounds a little crazy to me. Why can’t he talk to just anyone? Has the Catholic Church become so big headed that they believe God talks directly to them and not to everyone else? Why is my conscience telling me to feel bad and you’re telling me it’s ok? Father: Of course God talks to everyone. But his alliance is with his oldest and truest followers, the people of Catholic faith. You to could join the priest hood for guidance and see what God has to offer his true people. You could see the world of the priesthood and join a league of God’s elite. Cage: Pardon me father, and no offense, but you’re out of your fucking mind. Priesthood is far from my calling. I’ve had a hard time not killing a lot of people. I just don’t have the patience or the good heart to think about priest hood. And I’m too nice to my dick to put him through that much pain. Two deployments is hurtful enough…let alone the rest of my life. Cage walks out of the large aged cathedral not finding his guidance what-so-ever in the so called “graces” of the big headed ideals of Catholicism. With this discouraged attitude he did the only thing Marines know to do when life is bringing them down….he headed to the bars of Inner Harbor, Baltimore. Here he saw his long time friend and bartender Pauly. Pauly is of vertically challenged man with a big heart. He watched over Cage through his hard times in life. Cage was also at one time Pauly’s only customer. That was back before his bar started doing so well. The dark depressing bar was crammed with drunken and discouraged Ravens fans, cigarette smoke, and the lingering smell of a multitude of alcohols. Cage was enjoying his sixth Bud Select with a small smirk on his face when a drunken idiot was shoved in to him. Cage: Hey buddy, watch where you’re going. Drunk: I didn’t do it on purpose pal…my friend just pushed me…..n…n it wouldn’t hurt you…(hic) to say please…. Cage: PLEASE! watch where you’re going…. Even in the drunken state he was in, the town drunk could sense the sarcasm in Cage’s voice. Cage turned back towards the bar and put his bloodshot eyes back on one of the many TV’s showing the Ravens getting pummeled. Vic then felt two large hands grab his back as he was being heaved over the bar. Pauly looked over at the commotion and started backing people away from the mess. Beer, shot glasses, and ash trays went on the ride with Cage over the bar making loud noises and gaining attention. Cage was extremely ill tempered, and this didn’t help things. Once a man feels like his life has no meaning, no direction to follow, he has nothing to lose and everything to find, that’s usually the best time to avoid the drunk bar fights. This was the man that pushed Cage’s shit hole of a day over the edge. This drunken idiot just happened to be the man that dropped the straw onto the camel’s back. Except this time, the camel got up and started Armageddon in that whole in the wall bar. Cage stood up and was immediately met with a large hammer of a fist to his jaw. Blurring his vision and jarring his balance he was once again made to look like a complete idiot. The rest of the crowd at the bar just watched all the commotion, shocked at how fast everything had happened. This day and this fight were not going in his direction. Cage now bleeding from his lip begins having flashbacks of a few months prior to this night….in Iraq. (Marines…we will enter the bottom floor of this building, move to the basement and sweep our way up. It is vital to this strike tomorrow that we gain and hold this building. If anything moves…kill it! The squad of Marines moves silently and swiftly into the front entrance of the poorly built Iraqi structure. This was a vital fighting point for the Marines to hold in the attack on Uday and Qusay Hussein. This building had direct firing sites on their palace. A perfect spot for a squad of scout snipers to hold. A PFC entered the building first as the point man leading the way for the other twelve men to follow. Cage was the first to head down to the basement stairs. The building had no lights and night vision goggles were no use in such close combat. Slowly creeping down the stairs into the eternal blackness Cage bumps into a figure…an Iraqi! Startled, Cage relies on his MCMAP training and attacks the insurgent. Pinning him against a wall, Cage pulls his Kabar knife out and jams it into the struggling and scared Iraqi’s head, twisting the blade till the body lay motionless against the wall. Cage was covered in a pool of thick Iraqi blood. ) In what seemed like forever, Cage snapped back into reality and leaped through the air with his elbow leading his way. Meeting direct contact with the man’s temple, the attacker crumpled and fell to the floor. A second attacker wraps Cage’s arms from behind and starts squeezing with unreal strength. Cage manages a headbutt to the man’s nose and feels the thick blood pouring down his neck. He looks down at the two men lying on the floor. Their night was starting to turn out pretty shitty as well. Cage turns back to the bar when a third attacker annihilates Cage across the face with full beer bottle. Cage falls to the ground in a deep comatose state. The third attacker continues to brutally beat Cage’s body until the short fat bartender pulls his shotgun on the man. Still drunk and unsure of what is really going on, the third attacker sits down wide eyed at a nearby open stool. (Cage turns back up the stairs after this encounter with the insurgent. He regroups with the rest of the awaiting Marines in the dark first floor of this important structure. Using hand signals, four of the Marines form the tactical squad using their urban tactics training. They head down the stairs back into the dark unknown. Each Marine’s nerves are shot as they move as silently as one can with 70 pounds of gear strapped to ones body. Cage reaches the bottom of the stairs and sees no movement. The squad moves throughout the basement. Still nothing. From under the stairs a gun’s firing pin is pulled back. The Marines turn three M-16 assault rifles and one SAW (squad automatic weapon) to the stairs and open a sick fury of .556 rounds. The smell of gunpowder and adrenaline filled that small basement. Cage puts on his NVG and opens the small door to the room under the stairs exposing what ever Marine in Iraq loves to see…three dead dune coons. The Marines head back up the bullet filled stairs and meet with the rest of the Marines.) Cage’s mind finally reboots itself. Cage’s eyes are still slammed shut and his brain feels like it is receiving all the pain signals coming from his body in one big fury of pain. Shockwaves are hitting his brain at once and Cage feels pain on almost every inch of his body. Letting out a yelp of pain, Cage feels a hand touch his arm. Nurse: Mr. Cage are you ok?....Can you hear me Mr. Cage? As Cage slowly opens his eyes, bright neon lights shine through causing his pupils to retract. The light is so intense Cage feels his head pounding…much like feeling from a hangover from hell. With the overwhelming pain, Cage never had a chance to realize he had no fucking idea where he was. The last thought he had was a large Budweiser bottle slamming him in the face. He looked to his left and sees a woman wearing blue scrubs. Giving the room some more examining, he realizes he is in a hospital. Finally getting a bearing of where he is, he is still scared at the fact he doesn’t know how he arrived at that place. He tries to lift his right hand to feel his face; his hand is cuffed to the bed. Cage looks down, and then with a look of sheer confusion, he looks at the nurse, wanting an answer. She gently shakes her head and walks out of the room. Feeling sick to his stomach about being cuffed to his hospital bed, Cage looks over at the door as a figure enters the room in a police officers uniform. Cop: Good morning Mr. Cage. I presume you’re a still a little shaky about what happened the other night. Cage: The other night? How long was I out officer? What’s going on here, I didn’t start that fight. Cop: You took a pretty good smack on the face there Cage. You’ve been unconscious for three days. To refresh your memory, you were at a bar and got into a fight with three men. Cage: Three? I only remember two guys there. Cop: The bartender and other witnesses informed us of what happened. You… Cage: I was defending myself officer. Why the fuck am I cuffed to the bed? Cop: Calm down Cage, getting upset won’t help out a damn thing now. While you were defending yourself, you managed to put two of the three men in ICU. They’re holding on and luckily for you, it’s looking good for em. Cage: They attacked me first officer. It was self defense! Cop: You’re considered a lethal weapon due to your training Cage. You know the difference in defending and killing. Once the doctors release you, you’re coming with me down to the police station….the only good thing is all the witnesses at the bar coincide and the owner of the bar isn’t pressing charges. They saw what really happened and all the stories make you sound like you didn’t start the fight. Now we’re just going to let the legal system do the rest from here….with your service…I wouldn’t lose much sleep over this issue. Cage: What happened to the pussy that blindsided me? Cop: We questioned him at the station. Once this case starts rolling, he’ll be brought in and hopefully even charged with assault. Cage laid his head back down and started drifting off to sleep. This was one hell of a day for Cage. He laid his head back down and dove back into a deep sleep. Hours later when he woke up, and a strange man was standing in his hospital room. Cage: So when can I get out of here Doc? Unknown: Oh Mr. Cage, I am no doctor. Cage: Well then who the fuck are you and how the fuck did you get in my room? Unknown: Mr. Cage, you will have no idea who I am. How I got to your room isn’t important. Cage, I was at the bar the other night when you decided to have a little brawl with those three men. Quite impressive Cage. I had a little talk with your friend Pauly about you the other night and I have some direction for you. Cage: Some direction for me? Who the fuck do you think you are? Enraged, Cage tries to sit up in his bed. He looks down and the handcuffs are off. He rips the IV needle out of his arm and jumps to his feet. Cage: Mother fucker, if you’re not a God fearing man, you might want to start fearing Victor fuckin Cage! Unknown: Such intensity, such passion, Cage, our company can really use a man like you. You have the electric attitude we need. Cage: You better start telling me who the hell you are, or I’ll show you what I should have done in the bar. I’ve already done my time for the military, as a matter of fact; I was just discharged a few days ago. Unknown: Cage, just get your rest. If you’re wondering why you don’t have the cuffs on anymore, I know a judge and took care of your little problem. Since I did you a favor, the least you can do is just listen to what I have to offer. Cage: I don’t owe you shit. I don’t need your help. I can take care of my own problems. Cage can’t help but wonder who this guy is and what the hell he keeps talking about. Cage: I will listen to what you have to offer, but you just have to answer a few questions. For starters, who are you, and what do you want with me? Unknown: You can call me Pearl. Put some clothes on and I can tell you all about the Atlantic Wrestling Club at my office. Cage gets dressed and the two men take Pearl’s helicopter to AWC headquarters in Atlantic City. Pearl goes over everything with Cage about wrestling and Cage gives his background. Cage: So you’re telling me my first match is against a woman….not only a woman but a woman with an alcohol problem? Pearl, no offense but ya gotta give me more credit than that. Pearl: Cage listen, this is going to be your first match ever. You have no idea what it’s like out there, that ring is nothing like the desert of Iraq. You have fans. Teresa will be a good match for you to get a bearing for this sport. I wouldn’t underestimate here either. She’s pretty tough. Cage: Obviously not tough enough to say no to alcohol. But you make a good point about bearing. I accept Pearl, and thanks for the opportunity man. I sure hope I don’t disappoint you. Pearl: Just fight as best as you can Cage, that’s all I want out of you. You determine how far you go and how well you do. Good luck Cage. If I were you, I’d start training.
