Hello, Goodbye, and Hello

12 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, Goodbye, and Hello Kris Krimzon AWC Roleplay #4 Date: 4/12/05 For Show: N/A +=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= Please note that I am using a style that focuses on the life of Kris Krimzon being an indy wrestler. Also, this is just a character building roleplay. +=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= Stepping out of her car, Tamara Krimzon heaves her suitcase over her shoulder with a sigh. She shuts the door on the Camero, and taps a button on her key’s remote, and turns around to the sound of the car locking with a click. She steps toward onto a nice and clean front lawn with the words “Krimzon Residence” and “Krimzon own you” and etcetera spray painted upon it with “pride”. She walks up some wooden stairs and opens the house’s door, leading into total darkness. She steps in, sighing. She feels around the wall, looking for a light switch. She sighs, and feels around the wall, guiding her to her bedroom. She follows the wall to a door, and turns the doorknob, opening the door with a ‘creak’. She feels the right side of the wall, and flips a light switch. She tosses her bag onto her bed with leisure. She unzips the bag and hears the fizzing noise of a television. She steps out of her room, and carefully follows the noise into another room which lights are also out in. The television, which channel changes every few seconds, fizzes with static on every other channel. On the sofa in front of the television is the silhouette of Kris Krimzon, sipping a beer. Tamara sighs. “Kris, why are all the lights out in this house? I don’t know where any light switches are!” Tamara says. “Why do you think the lights are out, Tam? I’m in Lights Out Wrestling! C’mon, even A.J. could figure that one out, and he tripped on the stairs coming in!” Kris says in a ‘stating-the-obvious’ tone of voice. “Jesus, Kris. Just because you’re in Lights Out Wrestling doesn’t mean you can’t use light bulbs! That’s just the name of the wrestling association!” “Tamara, Tamara… so naive. How could you say that?” Kris laughs. “Anyways, the toilet’s broken…” “How the Hell did you manage to do that one Kris?” Kris grins. “Let’s just say that AJ didn’t like his hamster anymore, so I said that we could flush it like we did with my fish,” Kris says. Tamara’s eyes widen. “Those fish were $100 a piece! You said they died!” “Well-well… You said that my pet turtle ran away and joined the circus!” “Because your stupid and like circuses!” “Excuse me?! I DO NOT LIKE CIRCUSES!” Kris says, and sighs. “Those clowns are as scary as Hell…” “Wasn’t your mother in the circus?” Tamara asked. “Nah, she just liked clowns and stuff. Plus she thought that those small ass clown cars were going to be ‘in’ in 2005,” Kris says, chuckling to himself. “So Tamara, how do you like the house so far?” “Well, seeing as I’ve only seen the dark side of it, I don’t really know. I think if we turn the lights on I might be able to figure out if I like it or not, eh?” Tamara says with a glare in her eyes directed toward Kris. “Go ahead… But the darkness is way more cooler than light. Um… especially in the game room, so make sure to leave that light off… or just stay out of it completely, because that works too…” Kris says, directing his eyes back to the television which ‘happened’ to stop on the Adult channel. He quickly hits the “Menu” button on his satellite remote before Tamara sees it and goes into his Favorites list and adds a channel before Tamara directs her attention back to the screen. “Kris, I have two questions that you’ll be answering… first off, when did we get a game room?” Tamara asks, suspicion in her voice. “Well,” Kris says, “I love video games, so I went out and bought a PS2, a Xbox, a Game Cube, PSP, a computer, and all these other stuff.” “YOU SPENT ALL THAT MONEY!?!?!” “No, no…” Kris says. Tamara sighs, relaxing. “I bought like 100 games and all these old systems. It totaled to about six thousand, no biggy.” “AH! NO BIGGY?” Tamara yells, then her eyes widen and she sighs, relieved. “Wait, what about that $10,000 you got from CCWA?” “Yep, it’s gone. $6000 on the games and $4000 on the big screens. It’s okay, just don’t worry. Now what is that ‘second question’ of yours?” Kris asks. “Ugh… Okay, the second question… What did you DO to this gaming room of yours? Did you set THAT afire too?” Tamara says, expecting anything. “Oh, nah. AJ just sneezed with beer in his mouth and stained the floor, so don’t worry about it. We just don’t have any cleaner yet. Oh yeah, can you pick some up later?” Tamara sighs and steps out of the room. Kris grins, and turns back to his “favorites” list. Tamara steps back in the room with the “favorites” channel on. Kris fumbles with the remote and quickly taps the “Off” button before Tamara gets a full glimpse of the things her husband was watching. She takes her hand and flips the light switch and light spreads through out the room. Kris yelps and dives into the pillows of the sofa, blinding himself from the light. Tamara sighs and flips the light off again and leaves the room, and shuts the door to the room. After a few moments, the door swings open and the light switch turns on, going right into Kris’s eyes which have been used to the darkness. In steps AJ Vulgar, hand rubbing his forehead. Kris grins, laughing at AJ. “AJ, man. Honestly, how can you fall on the same steps twice in the SAME day?” Kris asks. “Shut up, man. It’s seriously not funny. Those stairs are god damn dangerous!! You should really get those sharp edges sanded down… I have an interview tomorrow and now I have a cut AND a bruise! WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU AND YOUR HOUSES?!” “Dude, it’s not my house.” “YES IT IS!” Kris sighs. “I guess you’re right AJ.” AJ nods, smiling. “I guess we really should consider getting our house retard-injury proof… we don’t want to see you getting hurt now, do we AJ?” “HEY! I AM NOT A RETARD!” AJ says defiantly. “Dude, don’t even try to play that. You know you are.” AJ sighs. “I’m heading home man. I’ll talk to you later.” “See you, Mister Retard…” AJ scowls, turns around, and walks straight into the wall. Kris breaks out in laughter as AJ walks out of the room with a scowl on his face. Kris grins. “Hello, Lights Out Wrestling…” As if on cue, the telephone rings. Kris sighs, and answers it. “Kris Krimzon,” Kris says, then coughs and continues, “andtamarakrimzon residence.” “Kris, Kris, buddy,” the other line says in a smart-ass kind of way. Kris sighs and replies, “Hey Kyle… What’s going on, brother?” “Well,” Kyle Krimzon replies, “I have some good news and I have some more good news. First, the good news: seeing as I am your agent, I got you out of the LOW.” ”What?! Already?!” Kris yelps. “But I did all the work to get my house to be all LOW-ish.” “That was only the good news. The more good news is that since I got you out of that crap-hole, you became a free-agent. And seeing as you’re a free-agent, someone wanted to sign you thanks to my brilliant agent-like skills that I have horned.” Kris rolls his eyes. “You mean honed?” “Sure. Anyways, I signed you to the CSW. Center Stage Wrestling, Kris. COME ON! CENTER STAGE! Not left stage or right stage, CENTER stage. That mean it is awesome. Have fun there. Bye.” “WHAT?!” Kris yells into the phone, but is cut off by the sound of a phone hitting receiver. Kris sighs and relaxes backward. “Goodbye, LOW… Hello CSW.”
