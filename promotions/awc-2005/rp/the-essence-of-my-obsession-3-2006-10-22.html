The Essence of My Obsession (3)

22 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 - 10 - 06 Faucet leaks in shadows Spilling from morgue lancet Caressed your fontanelle I've sworn to kill Every last one Every last one Panic in the shakes of the wounded Panic in the worms Onto the floor And out of your mouth Out of your eyelids 'Cassandra Gemini' by The Mars Volta - It was this very moment that I realized what I was planning on doing. In a way, I snapped out of my imaginary madness, if only for a second, and saw the ghost of who I once was. It was dark, and late at night. Why was I even in front of 'her' door? What made me think that she'd even be willing to see me less than fifteen minutes after our ruction. I don't want her to think that I'm a bad guy because, truthfully speaking, I'm not. I can't help my subconscience tendencies. I can't even schedule an appointment with a doctor because I don't have health insurance, so I'm left with nothing but the worst. I'm standing here, barefoot in a puddle of mud, with droopy eyes and a half-there disapointment for how this whole night had gone. The funniest thing is that I'd done this so many times before, and it's never turned out this bizarre. I've never, in the time I've been in the 'game', had a woman become so distressed over such miniscule matters. She can't deal with the person that I am, yet, she's leaving bars with perfect strangers. She can trust me enough to jump in my car and go for a ride, but she becomes sketched out when I talk common sense and it's like a cement wall to her. Her faults are dropped onto me, as if I was the firestarter. I liked Melody, for a little bit. I thought there was promise, and that impression was solely from how quickly and easily she took her alcohol. I thought there was potential for me to take advantage of the situation, get to know her, and have dysfunctional, strange sex. I wanted to see how dirty an innocent red-head could get while intoxicated. Is that really as wrong as people think it is? I didn't even have thoughts about slaying her until I realized how easily she can deprecate you until you're as small as a fucking apple seed. I literally felt like an animal when she stormed out of the motel station and ran towards her 'suite'. Was she running because she thought I was dangerous? Was she running because she believed she had become the victim? I don't know, and that's what kept me from actually opening her door. '..Michael? I said you could come in.' 'I don't think it's necessary, Melody.' It started to pour. I backstepped until my bare feet were out of the muddy puddle. 'I'm going to grab my cars keys and then I'll take you home.' I turned to walk away. This is what I needed to do, certainly before things became dark and uncivilized again, and certainly before that 'deadly' desire came back. She didn't deserve to die. She's young. She has a full life ahead of her that I'd be stripping her from. I can't do that to her. How could I do that to anybody? This was, afterall, the first realization of the sickness within. This was when I started to feel like I was being poisoned somehow, or just unbelievably overwhelmed. Once I shift into this completely different world, the pressure to initiate something horrible starts to take effect. It feels like I'm holding a massive boulder on my shoulders. My feet are sinking into the ground, sweat is profusely dropping from my face, and my shoulders are two 'cracks' from giving way. The painful sensation of pressure literally forces me to hunt for the fix I need to make it all end. Enter Melody; this heart-warming girl, from Minesota, who's dodging an abusive boyfriend and in search for a new job. There's absolutely nothing wrong with her, and I get this foul urge to kill her? None of this will ever make sense, but it's kind of like a rollercoaster. Once you're strapped in, and the train is moving, there's nothing you can do but hold tight and 'get through it'. I knew everything that was painted in front of me was just that, pigments of a tale that she coordinated to suit me a little better; a little better than the truth would. I knew there was something strange going on, as she's been pre-occupied through the entire car ride, and even when we made it to 'Froggy's'. Look, I know it's a seedy place and I'm sure Melody had 'rape' stamped all over her brain when she saw that my only intentions were to 'get a room somewhere'. I don't care if she thought rape was part of my process, because I know it wasn't. I'm a lonely guy, struggling with minor dementia, and wanted someone to talk to; someone who'd make an effort to 'understand' me. Whether she tried to make an effort or not, Melody doesn't understand me or my plight in life. I never gave the rundown of who I am and what I stand for simply because she never did the same for me. Melody did nothing but make me feel even worse about myself. In a way, she made me out to be a killer that I was trying not to be. I could've ended her life by walking into that motel room, but I came to the conclusion that it just isn't worth it. Her life is preserved because of me, and now all I want to do is drive her home and sleep the rest of this painful night away. Her door opens, slightly. I can see one of her sharp blue eyes through the cracked fissure, but that's it. 'I'm sorry' she whispered. 'What're you sorry for? None of what happened tonight is your fault. I'm sorry for being whatever I was. I'm gonna' get my keys.' 'I have reason to be sorry. I'm not who I said I was.' Dead in my tracks, I stopped. My heart seemed to sink into the sands of imbroglio and everything around me seemed to be covered in black veils. 'What do you mean by that?' 'There's a lot of things you don't know about me, and a fraction of what I've told you about me is somewhat... false.' 'False?' I immediately shot back at her. What the Hell was this weird bitch hiding from me?! She's not supposed to be hiding anything from me, I'm supposed to be hiding things from her. I started to walk over to the door when I noticed two blue headlights shooting down the road. 'Oh my God. He found me.' I wanted to question her, but I didn't. Instead, I stood there like a shish kabob, with my grilled pepper arms folded. I think I may have been on some kind of delay, simply because I was still held up in the brain traffic she casted on me after mentioning her lack of truthfulness. The words, 'Oh my God, he found me' isn't all that refreshing when a pickup truck is speeding towards you. It really convinced me that this was her ex-boyfriend, but how in the holy Hell did he manage to find us. We'd been driving for miles before discovering this cabana. Was it a, on the contrary to what I would've guessed, well known place? She flung the door open and latched onto my wrist. She started to breathe inexorably heavy, and I noticed a tear fall from the corner of her right eye. I felt immensely helpless in the situation. 'I don't want to be a part of your bullshit, Melody! What in the hell is going on?' 'Michael. Get in the room now, please! He has guns!' A needle-pricking pain embraced my whole body. Guns; objects that can penetrate skin without much labor involved. He'll pull the trigger without me ever knowing what hit me and likely kill Melody. I don't like Melody, she fucking lied to me, but she wasn't going to die before explaining herself. That abusive hick behind the wheel of that pickup truck wasn't going to be the one to pull the trigger on anyone. Fuck him if he thought he was going to ruin today any more than it already was. Who did he think he was, anyway? A villainous, relentless gargantuan that everyone's suppose to piss their pants over? I'm not urinating over any human being, let alone some degenerate stalking a girl who doesn't want him anymore. Instead of doing it 'Mcguyver Style', I allowed her to pull me into the room. As my body was swallowed into the suite, I could hear the faintest hollers of displeasure. The guy didn't know what the fuck was going on when he saw me with her. The door slammed behind us and Melody cruised into the bathroom, likely to try and escape through the bathroom window. I knew this simply because my suite had a bathroom window, too, but it seemed too small. I didn't think anyone could fit into it, and I knew I'd have a hard time. So, with that, I started to think while the door-pounding commenced. I love morbid excitement at early hours of the morning. It, in a strange, fuzzy, completely sub-human way, makes me comfortable. To kill or be killed. Knowing that an escape was the equivalent of an impossibility, I backstepped until I was beside the door. I let my body rest, like a piece of flattened cardboard, against the wall. I figured he'd blow the door down sooner or later. Melody, that bitch.. Let her fucking crawl through that window, so she can get stuck and knifed in the back without ever knowing what hit her. It's the least she should go through after leaving me with this box of turbulence. See what you've done, Mel? You're going to force me to kill your precious boyfriend. 'Let me the FUCK in, Melody! Now!' Kablam! Then again, when I start to think about it.. he isn't exactly the nicest person in the world. He, or so she states, abused Melody and probably wants to abuse her even more. I couldn't allow something like to happen, especially in the company of myself. I'd have to be a brick in the wall to keep myself from purging a physical breakdown, especially if he managed to muscle his way in here. So, instead of letting the guy break his way in, I decided to communicate with him. 'If you do, in fact, manage to break that door down, you have problems waiting for you on your arrival.' 'Excuse me, motherfucker? If I wanted to, I'd break the window beside me and easily let myself in.' 'But you're nervous that I may be some big ole' tough guy with a weapon too, right? You don't want to force vulnerability on yourself by struggling to crawl through the jagged glass. While doing so, I could just about do anything to you.' 'You'll get a bullet in your eye before you have the time to orchestrate anything, now let me in. Melody owes me money.' 'What does she owe you?' 'Privledged information.' 'For who? You, Melody, and your cock?' Kablam! 'You're not going to be going in anywhere if you keep barging yourself into the door. Be smart about this. What's your name?' 'Rodger.' 'Nice to meet you, Rodger. My name's Michael. I met your girlfriend at a bar a few miles south of here, and we decided to take a ride. We ended up renting two rooms for the night, and the only reason I'm here now is because I requested that I bring her back home. She's a pain in the ass, Rodger. I wouldn't reccomend her to anyone, and I'm sure you're just experiencing the same shit I have.' It was as if I connected with him for a minute. He didn't barge into the door this time. While there was silence, I started to scan the room. Defensive protection, there to help me in the event that he makes his way into the room. That's when I saw it. A shoehorn. The only device, in this skin and bones suite, that would suffice in such a situation. I sprinted over to the tiny table, which sat directly to the right of the queen-sized, flower-painted bed, and grabbed the shoehorn. Afterwards, I shut off the desk lamp, the night-table lamp, and the ceiling light. It was now completely dark in the suite, and I was ready to take advantage of vulnerability. I crouched down, beside the radiator and massive cooling vent, and closed my eyes. I wanted to laugh, if only because of the situation I was in. I could've prevented danger by keeping to myself. I should've drank myself to a drunken stupor, in that bar, and just left. I should've left it at that, but now I'm dealing with some red-headed troublemaker's financial woes. 'As I said, she stole money from me. Fifteen grand.' 'Fifteen grand? Are you kidding me?' 'NO. Open the fucking door. She's either going to give me the dinero or I'm going to break her fucking neck. She can decide.' 'What if I decided?' 'Do you have fifteen big ones?' I already figured out that this guy wasn't as big as he was making himself out to be. If he was, he wouldn't of let me hold him up this long with dragged-out conversations about things that really don't involve me. I doubt he had any weapons at all, really. I believed that the more I interrogated the situation, the more he backpeddaled. 'No, but I'm willing to compromise the circumstances.' 'Oh yeah? How so?' 'What do you want?' 'Let me get this straight, you met my girlfriend at some bar down the road, correct?' 'Correct, sir.' 'You met her for the first time tonight?' 'Yes.' 'And you're taking her problems into your own hands?' 'Well, I think I'm forced to. You see, she's not here. She vanished from the scene once she knew you were on her tracks. Thus, I'm here to make things better.' Then, from out of nowhere, a loud shattering of glass could be heard from behind me. I turned around, and the lights came on. 'Melody? What are you doing here?' There she stood, completely soaked and wearing nothing but a muddied, white nightgown. I turned back around, to face Rodger, and saw his shadow outside the window. 'I can take care of this, Michael.' 'You stole money from this guy? Are you fucking insane?' 'Melody, I want my money. I want all of it. It's been a month, and I haven't seen a Goddamn dime.' 'I don't have the money on me, Rodger.' 'Where is it then?' As they continued to talk, I walked over towards the bed and pulled down a 15X12 wall mirror. This wasn't going to continue much longer, as far as I was concerned. I had no interest in knowing either one of these two. All I wanted to do was go home and simply forget about this. As I started walking over towards the door, I heard Melody shout at me. 'Don't move, Michael.' She was holding a gun; a pistol, I believe. I stood there, with the mirror in my hand, and started to play prior events back in my head. Was this a cinematic production? Why was she pointing a gun towards me? I noticed I was beside the light switch, and quickly shut the overheard light off. It was pitch black again, and I found myself dodging bullets left and right. I could hear them ricocheting, and coming severely close to me. This was about the time that I heard the door hinges break off. 'RODGER!' I saw his silhouette, and dropped to the floor. Crawling over to the bed, I could hear the two of them wrestling. This went on for a couple of minutes, and ended abrupty with one gun shot. At the time, I didn't know what happened. I wasn't sure who had been hit, or if anyone had been hit, but I knew I was alive. That's all that mattered. I could perceive the floor creaking, so I decided to lay on my stomach. I could see, from underneath the bed, two feet walking towards where I was laying down. Before I found myself in the middle of danger, I stood up and forced every bit of energy out of me. 'Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh!' I screamed as loud as possible, and swung the mirror into the face of the unknown. I wasn't sure who I blasted but, afterwards, I could feel tiny shards of glass stabbing into me. I looked down at the floor and saw them laying lifelessly on the floor. Turning on the overhead light, I noticed a horrifying scene. Melody, the red-haired, lying bombshell, was spread-eagle on the floor, now with a gaping hope in her throat. I did, in fact, bash Rodger in the face with the mirror, as the border was still around his head. There was blood everywhere. Seriously; it was on the walls, the floor, the mattress, and even on the door. I didn't snap, however. Well, maybe I did, but I never would've realize it from all of the shock surging throughout my body. I stood there for a few complete minutes, without doing anything else but staring at the two of them. There wasn't an alarming sensation, at least there wasn't now, and all I could think about was the ride home. It was time to go. 'SLAM!' I shut my door, turned the ignition, and let my green Geo warm up. Gripping onto the steering wheel, I tried to make sense of what happened tonight. I tried to make sense of Rodger, and his relationship with Melody. It seemed so difficult, though. It might've been because I was left without an explanation. It might've been because I knocked Rodger out before I knew he blew a hole on Melody's throat. I would've killed Rodger for that, but he was unconscience. To kill or be killed. Rest in pieces, Melody May. 3*
