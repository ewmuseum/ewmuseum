The Man of Your Dreams?

4 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-4-06 It was the same dream she had almost every night. The man was out of her life, but not out of her mind. It began with hot steam rolling from a shower and filling a small bathroom. Behind the curtain, Teresa stood wetting her waist-length sandy blond hair. Her eyes closed as the hot water streamed down her back. A cool breeze passed over her bare skin as the shower curtain drew open. There he stood with that same warm smile he always gave her before he took her. His skin held a slight tan, his hair fell loosely on his shoulders, and his green eyes bore deep into hers. Teresa stood motionless watching as the man joined her in the tub and shut the curtain. The hot water continued to race down her back to the heels of her feet. The man cupped Teresa’s face with his hands and kissed her with such passion, she thought her knees would give way. Her arms wrapped around him giving into the emotions that soared through her veins. She felt her back pressed against the wall and his warm breath on her skin as he began to kiss her neck. She held him close wanting more. She ran her fingers down his back and back up to his shoulders digging her fingers deep into his skin. He grabbed underneath her knees and lifted her legs to his hips. Her arms wrapped around his neck for support. A sharp breath races down to her lugs once she felt him. Her inner muscles tightened as he continued to slide himself deeper making sure she feels every inch…… She awakened suddenly as many times before. Teresa found herself in a cold sweat and a pounding heart. She sat up in the bed of her New Jersey hotel. The room was still dark but shards of daylight cut through the sides of the heavy curtain over the window. The television she left on was airing the early morning news. “Good morning.” Teresa turned her head instantly and saw a tall lanky fellow sipping on a cup of coffee. The sun’s rays fell on his red hair and made it look as if his head was on fire. He sat his mug on the small table separated him from Teresa’s bed and smirked. Resting on her side of the small table was a fresh cup of coffee waiting on her. She reached for her mug. The red haired fellow never took his eyes off her. Teresa shot him a confused glare before taking a sip. “What you looking at me like that for? What’s so funny? And how the hell did you get in here?” His face was gleaming with amusement and his smirk only thinned as it spread over his long face. He leaned back in his chair and crossed his bony arms over his chest. “Some dream you must have had. I’ve only been here an hour and you were moaning like hell. Care to tell me all about it?” Teresa’s face reddened and she felt her ears burn. A mixture of embarrassment, frustration, and anger flushed over her. Whom did he think he was sneaking into her room like this? She sat her coffee mug on the nightstand and threw the covers off her legs. She wore a grey ARMY t-shirt her brother Eric gave her and a pair of Dale Earnhardt Sr. pajama bottoms. Her bare feet padded across the dark carpet of the hotel and into the bathroom. “Go to hell Bob.” “The dream was that good huh?” Bob spoke over the sound of running water. He would not have been traveling with her if he had not insisted. Ever since Eric shooting, her drinking habit had come worse. Any other woman and he would not have cared, but Teresa he has known since high school. They used to be close back then. After he become involved with the Organization, Teresa put distance between them and within months they were living in two separate worlds. He had planted his feet in the drug business and she in the pro-wrestling. Both organizations dealt in money, greed, backstabbing, and controversy. Somehow, he knew beneath that hard shell she had formed around her is a good girl. Despite her toughness, she still needed looking after. If her wrestling career did not kill her, the alcohol would. Someone needed to catch her when she fell. ------------- “You never did answer my question. How did you get in my room?” Teresa looked across the table at a McDonald’s. Bob shoved a forkful of pancake into his mouth and washed it down with orange juice. Teresa had already finished her Egg McMuffin and was sipping on another cup of coffee. They had separate hotel room next door to each other, but he somehow managed to find a way into hers. Bob gave her a mischievous smirk, pulled out his wallet and retrieved a card. “Since we checked in together, it was simple to tell the receptionist you locked yourself in your room.” “And she gave you the card that unlocks my door. Why didn’t she do it herself?” Bob shrugged cutting out another bite of pancake. He was beginning to annoy the hell out of Teresa and wished to all that’s holy that she didn’t agree to allow him to travel with her. He was inching his way into her private life and it was simply pissing her off. “If she done that, who would watch the front desk? Don’t worry Teresa, I didn’t dig through your luggage and try on your panties.” Amusement filled his face as it did that morning. He got Teresa pissed and he couldn’t help but find humor in her reddened face, tightened pink lips and the flair in her baby blues as she glared at him. He finished off his orange juice as he watched her stand up and took her tray to the trash can. She couldn’t get rid of him and she knew it. She had a five thousand dollar debt to pay him. So naturally, the looking after wasn’t the only reason he insisted traveling with her. She knew that too. Teresa was outside the door. Her back pressed against the red brick of the McDonald’s building and a Winston Light held between her fingers. If she wasn’t in public, she would’ve tossed the rest of her hot coffee on Bob’s face and the hell with the consequences. She didn’t even want him to go on the road with her but he weaseled his way into her life. The five grand she borrowed from him to pay Darrel’s debt off. Why didn’t Bob get his money back through Darrel himself? She puffed the last of her cancer stick as more customers flocked in. Bob soon stepped out, grabbed her elbow and led her to the rental car. “Come on. We got an appointment?” “Appointment? What you talking about Bob?” “You got a match Tuesday don’t you?” “Well yeah…” “Well, from what I’ve seen thus far, all you’ve done is get drunk and get your ass kicked.” Teresa slid into the tan Saturn and shut the door. She reached into her front pocket for her smokes. Bob came in through the driver side and turned the key. Soon they were on the road. It was now noon and the sun shone bright but deceiving for the wind still held onto the late winter chill. Teresa cracked the window about an inch and flicked cigarette ashes out of the window as Bob continued. “If you want to get rid of me quickly and I know you do, then you got to start winning matches. Understand?” “Yeah, ‘cause I have to give you my damn checks right? What happened to making runs for you like you said before?” “I said that before you decided to join AWC. How can I get my money back if you’re on the road?” “I could MAIL it to you.” “Nope. With your drinking problem, you’d drink your check away before you cash it.” Bob pulled the car into a parking spot in front of a gym reserved for AWC staff only. He killed the engine and turned to face Teresa. He looked to her with seriousness and an odd sense of compassion. “Don’t tell me you don’t have a problem. What was that stint about last week with Chainz? Do you realize how dangerous that man can be? Did you not see what he done to that girl or were you too drunk to notice?” Teresa stared out the windshield in silence as she puffed on her cancer stick. She reached for the handle of her door, but Bob reached and grabbed her wrist. “Listen to me Teresa dammit! Stay away from him or you will get hurt. You don’t have to be alone. I may not be the man in your dream, but let me at least be your friend. You don’t need the alcohol for comfort any longer.” A cold hard stare pierce through Bob. Teresa jerked her wrist from his grasp and pulled her door open. “Leave me the hell alone Bob. How do you know what I need? Huh? The man in my dream is ten times the man you’ll ever be.” “He’s not real Teresa.” “The hell he is.” “He’s not here is he?” “……..” “Go to hell.” Teresa stepped out of the car and slammed the door. Bob sat back in his seat watching her. The protective barrier she made for herself isn’t as hard as he originally though. He grinned to himself and got out of the Saturn.
