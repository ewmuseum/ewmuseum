Family Hatred

5 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/06 Curt Mitchell was the epitome of society’s biggest fears and anxieties. For whilst the majority of the world opposed his kind, there were many who sat with a niggling voice in the back of their heads. During a brief, fleeting moment, there were rational individuals who felt understanding and empathy towards the beliefs of a man like Mitchell. Publicly those people would vehemently deny it yet they could not escape the private thoughts they had had. In a world that was experiencing turbulence and upheaval, this man who was sat at his oak desk, prepared to address his new audience. Whilst the hair fitted the stereotype of his ‘kind’, Mitchell dressed to impress. A sharp black suit jacket covered him, along with a shirt of the same colour and red tie to go with it. His hands were clasped together on the desk and in front of him were a pile of papers where he had meticulously prepared his speech. Behind him was a huge glass pane where the backdrop of city life could be seen under the afternoon sun. Glancing down at the paperwork, Curt ran his hands over his shaved head and then looked up to the camera with a salesman-like smile. “Good afternoon, my learned friends and I thank you for taking the time to join and share with me, the message I have to bring to you.” His blue eyes were piercing as he looked into the camera and they alone could tell a story of the kind of character that was about to preach his gospel. Although he was clearly not an established celebrity or someone at home in front of the public eye, Mitchell was cool, calm and collected. In fact, he came across as someone completely in touch with reality and rational in his own thoughts. People like him, preaching the message that he would, were the worst kind. For when you begin to rant and rave, that is when you lose your attraction. Yet this man was educated and intelligent, that made him dangerous. “I am an average American from an average background, with a family who worked hard for a living, just like many of you watching today. My father put his health on the line in order to put food onto our table because of the love he had for his family. Even though times were hard for us, the community worked together to make a better place for people like me who were growing up there. Now I am sure that so many of you can relate to the work ethics my father displayed, yet how many of you can relate to being in a tight knit community with strong moral fibre? I can tell you the answer to that…Not enough of you.” He leans back in his chair for a moment and takes in his own words, digesting them in his mind before speaking again. “The world that we live in now is vastly different from the one that our parents and families knew. This is no longer a society built on good Christian values and the foundations that our forefathers established are crumbling beneath us. Women are being corrupted by power and greed, encouraged to turn their backs on being good family leaders in favour of becoming models, actresses and business women. They are being warped by the propaganda of feminism which tells them it’s okay to abandon the family and the mission that God created them for. Strong and good women like my mother are now in the minority against money and power hungry young girls who care nothing for the most powerful gift of all, pro-creation. Yet my friends, this is not the worst of it, no, not at all.” Curt Mitchell beckons with his left index finger and into the camera view from that side, comes a massive mountain of a man. He is clearly over seven feet tall and has a jet black Mohawk that all punks of the 70’s would be proud of. He wears a tight black T-shirt and leather pants with a facial expression that could kill. In his hands is a book which he slowly passes to Mitchell, who smiles at the monster, soon to be known as Leviathan. “Thank you, my fellow soldier.” He stands the book up in front of him and it is quite clearly a copy of “The Holy Quran”. The look on Mitchell’s face is one of repulsion as he then lets go of it, allowing the book to drop down onto the desk. “This book of propaganda has infiltrated it’s way into our country, chipping away at the strong Christian values that we as Americans used to hold in the highest of regard. With it, has come the Islamic invasion that has been working so hard to take our nation and make it their own. They know no boundaries and have resorted to criminal actions against us, slaughtering our brothers in acts of terrorism, raping our women and I promise that even our children are not safe from them. The hard working men like my father can no longer get a job because their kind have snuck in through the back door and taken the jobs that good Americans should have had. Even in the professional wrestling business, people like Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon have stolen a position in a company, that a strong young White man could have had.” Leviathan can be seen standing behind Curt Mitchell, head tilted to one side and a snarl engraved upon his face. “Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon is a confused man for he believes he is experiencing a crisis of faith. The grip of Allah seems loose on him but my friends, do not be fooled by this display. In your town, in your city, in your college, in your workplace, in the bar, you will meet people like him who claim to not be a member of the Jihad against our nation. Yet you should not believe them, you should not believe the intentions of people like Suleimon. For whilst they may not claim to be soldiers in the Islamic army, they have still come to our great country to bleed us dry. This man takes the American dollar into his pocket for the privilege of brutalizing our young white brethren within a ‘sporting’ environment. Already I have witnessed the depravity of just another company that has opened its doors to the Muslim cancerous movement.” With that, Curt Mitchell slowly rises to his feet and raises a closed fist to the heavens with Leviathan following suit. “My friends, I urge you to see with your own eyes the problems of this society that we are a part of. Everywhere that we turn, people like Suleimon are infecting us with their ways and draining our economy dry with their parasitic ways. I believe that our nation should be freed from the leeches that come here every day, week, month and year with their objective of taking America from those who rightly inhabit it, the white and the morally strong. Do not allow yourselves to be driven away and to be made to feel dirty for being proud of who you are. For we, the soliders of the American Liberation Army, shall not be ashamed of ourselves, we shall strive to free this country of the likes of Suleimon that wish to take our identity from us. We will rise above this parasite and show him the error of his ways.” His voice then raises to a loud booming shout as he screams out his message to the potential followers that may be watching. “RISE ABOVE THEM, MY BROTHERS AND SISTERS!” “FIGHT THE POWER!” “FIGHT THE POWER!” “FIGHT THE POWER!” The scene slowly fades to black with the camera view placed on the Quran. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Some hours later, Curt Mitchell sat in that same chair, smiling with satisfaction at the video tape he held in his hands. He held a cigar to his lips and pulled it away, exhaling a fine ring of smoke into the darkness of the room around him. Moonlight shone through the window and was the only source of light around Curt. Grunting and groaning could be heard but the source of this could not be seen. Mitchell turned slightly with a smile and glanced straight ahead. “Pick that fucking Towel-Head up, I don’t want him dirtying my floor.” Leviathan comes into the view with a young Arab male being dragged up by his collar and slumped onto the desk. He looks only about 19 years old and his clothes are torn with blood and bruises covering his face. His lips are swollen and stained a dark crimson colour through the beating that he has sustained. Leviathan stands over him with a sick smile as the youngster tries to plea his case in broken English.” “N-n-not…m-m-y…fault….” Curt stands up and walks around the desk to stand on the other side of his victim. Shaking his head in disappointment, Mitchell then drives the cigar down hard into the back of the Arabs hand causing him to yelp in agony. Grabbing at the youngsters turban, Curt tears it off and throws it to the ground, snatching at the exposed hair and pulling his head back. Eye to eye with his prey, Curt snarls his words quietly into the youngsters face. “Don’t you DARE poison my sister with your vile Abba-Dabba ways…She is a good, strong, American woman…Who will go on to raise decent young Americans with a WHITE…middle class man…I will NOT have her infected with your talk of Allah and your cancerous culture…I can save her and I WILL…” Hatred fills the air and the young Arab smiles as if he is punch drunk. “She…is….nothing like…you.” Curt Mitchell stands back up to his full height and looks down on his sisters’ potential suitor. There was far more to this man than a video tape and a seven foot monster by his side. This was not a story of just “black” and “white”, something was going on far away from the public eye and this innocent young man perhaps held the key to Pandora’s Box. Walking slowly over to the left hand wall, Mitchell flicks a switch which causes a light to shine within a large display cabinet. He stands with his hands clasped together behind his back observing the items before him. “Bring him here!” Leviathan drags the weary and beaten Arab over to the cabinet and hauls him to his feet, forcing to view the items on display. At the top of the cabinet is a National Socialist Battle Flag and below it, a Hitler Youth Knife with the scabbard next to it. Mitchell then salutes the flag and the pride in his voice is evident. “This is what we believe in, the liberation of our nation, the freedom from the grip of your kind and the chance to be true patriots without the frowning eyes of political correctness upon us. This is what my family stands for and you…Towel Head…know NOTHING….about my sister…” In an act of desperate defiance mixed with frustration, the youth turns to Mitchell and spits in his direction. “Fuck you…Racist…Dog!” Slowly Curt looks down at the spittle on his jacket and then gradually wipes it off with his hand. He shakes his head and straightens out his collar, tidying himself up and all the while looking completely bemused by what this young man just did. Pulling out his cell phone from his jacket pocket, he begins to dial a number as he starts to walk away from Leviathan and his prey. As he puts the phone to his ear, he turns back to Leviathan and motions to the Arab. “Use him as practice material; it’s all the Towel Head deserves.” With that, Curt heads towards the door and opens it up, leaving the office as the phone rings at the other end. As he departs, the breaking of glass can be heard followed by a loud scream causing a smug smile to appear on Mitchell’s face. After a few moments, a female voice picks up and Mitchell’s reply is one of stern response. “We need to talk…” The scene fades out to black as the realization that AWC is about to come under Nazi occupation begins to hit home.
