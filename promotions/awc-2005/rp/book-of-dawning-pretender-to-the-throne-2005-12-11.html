Book of Dawning - Pretender to the Throne

11 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/12/05 The innocence of youth is something that can never be paralleled. It’s something that is so inherent in their make up that it takes years and years to break down, even just a little bit of it. There’s just something about the youth that compels them to believe…to truly believe they can do anything in the world that they want to once they put their mind to it. Sadly it’s not the case. I hate to break all the hearts of those young still with dreams and aspirations but quite simply, you’ll never achieve what you truly want in life to the full. And I hear the cries of what about Bill Gates and people like him with hundreds and thousands of millions of dollars and I’ll still tell you…he hasn’t found his true happiness. His true dream and aspiration probably goes right beyond what he’s at now, but nobody will ever know unless he tells you so. And even when you think they do… …they have this funny way of not being what you expected at all… This is where Garza Arrigo now found himself. With the recent death of Farrelli, the entire Kingston underworld had opened up. Before he could even say anything, people were calling his name in hushed voices as the one to take over, to bring stability and strength back to the Kingston elite. Without even a word he had been dubbed the saviour of the Kingston underworld. It’s not that he minded, it’s more so that he didn’t even get a say in what was happening. The title of boss was thrust under his nose and kept there until he bit. That was all in the space of maybe a day or two, so quick was the syndicate ready to replace its headless monster with something altogether different, but still to their liking. For, in the past couple of years, Farrelli had lost his lustre, he had become boring and tiresome, the same thing over and over; and it wasn’t working. The general morale of the group had hit rock bottom and there was nothing that Farrelli could do to lighten the mood. It was as if he was Midas… …but everything went to hell. However his death had left them in both a predicament and an opportunity; they weren’t ready to take on the mantle themselves because they hadn’t groomed a successor to move in. However, they couldn’t let the opportunity to pass by as otherwise the big wigs up in New York city would send down one of their lackeys and everything would become like it was before, just a different name for their leader. So cue Garza Arrigo. To them Garza was a necessary evil, someone who was not what they required for the future but then again, someone who could be easily manipulated and coerced in the right directions for wherever the syndicate wanted things to go. It was quite simple really, he was going to be the puppet, and he didn’t have one clue that he was. So why had I chosen to come at this moment? For the very same reasons that the others were taking their chances…I was going to take mine you see. Just like there was a window of opportunity for their own progress, I finally had the chance to take something that had caused so much pain to me in my years. I finally had the chance to dictate to others what would happen. It was my turn to rule. It saved an awful lot of hassle also. Until recently the chance of being able to take over the Kingston syndicate (while small) was still relatively impossible. How could one man hope to overthrow the entirety of an organisation when he consisted of only one man? I may have had aspirations but I wasn’t fucking stupid. This was going to take time and the relative patience to find the opening that I needed to get in there with minimum effort and expenditure of energy that would be needed for other more important ventures once things were settled and the dust was gone. So now I stood outside the one building in Kingston that made a mockery of the town’s other small and historically kept housing. The building stood imposingly over everything surrounding it, casting shadows that cloaked the road in darkness at the brightness of times. I reckon that was the precise intention of the architect, it was all a bit too convenient. Grey pitch mortar was shoved onto whatever framework the building had originally and was extremely casual. Everything you can imagine about the historical buildings being beautiful…swap it with this one. It was ugly. But I still stood on the outside of the building looking into it and seeing who was inside. The shutter doors were keeping my prying eyes out of it though no matter how hard I tried. I had to go inside one way or the other. It had been this way for a long time, being on the outside looking in but it was something that I had enjoyed in a weird sense. At once I was part of the organisation, having history and being involved in what had happened but at the same time I was distanced and an informed outsider, someone who could be responsible for so many things. There was a reason they never tried to kill me. I knew bloody well too much for them to let go of me…and not just facts about inconsequential things. I knew things that they didn’t. Call it a perk of the job retirement scheme for an old mobster. Now I had to use it again. Without anymore contemplation, I walked inside, ready to face my demons. *** Inside was plush, just as I remembered it, a place to accommodate some of the seediest men you’ll ever meet…and they all need their creature comforts. Forget about having greasy hair and pretty bad skin, they needed antique furniture! But I wasn’t interested in admiring the décor (though it did look quite nice - I’ll remind myself not to obliterate it later) I was concerned with getting up to the main office that held my prize. Course the couple of hired goons by the lift weren’t going to speed me on my way. With the bag I had brought some pretty rudimentary stuff, in other words….nothing. Smoke and mirrors, all smoke and mirrors because if you learn anything from being a part of a gang like this one; everyone gets freaked when somebody suspicious has a duffle bag and is walking with a purpose. They’d much rather ignore the guy, plead the fifth if their boss survived , or offer their services due to them helping the new boss. Ah goons…the epitome of loyalty. So naturally once I stepped up to them they both looked at me, then the eyes slowly drifted down to the bag. I smiled naturally, almost a smirk on their face as I could tell what process was going through their heads right now. I saw one of them gulp and then quickly move aside, not waiting for the consensus of the other goon to let me by. I nodded and smiled briefly again at the one who hadn’t moved, making it perfectly obvious that I swung the duffle bag in his direction. He felt something brush against his leg and he reeled in terror. Alright to be fair there wasn’t nothing in the bag. A couple of bricks were there to give off the imitation of a device but really I wouldn’t be using it. I wouldn’t need to. I stepped into the lift and whistled away as I pushed the button and watched the doors slide close in front of me, both goons still tongue tied and unable to do anything. And I was alone again. The lift mimicked the foyer area I had just exited. There was a nice dark red carpet, almost like it was crushed velvet at my feet. The walls surrounding me were completely filled with mirrors producing an effect of multiple personality. It worked a charm on people as not only was it completely covered in mirror, the way it was angled that people could see their multiple reflections. However, just like in the circus the glass was done in such a way as to morph the bodies slightly out of kilter. So imagine an enclosed space with mirrors all around you that distorted you slowly but surely as the infinite reflections bounced against each other. I smiled, admiring how wicked my face became with each passing glance around the lift. This was accompanied by dead silence…no music, no muzak even. Just complete and total silence. One can only imagine the effect it had on people who were nervous about seeing the boss, having been summoned from wherever they were and having to go up in this lift. I applaud the architect yet again. But looking around it makes me begin to wonder what exactly I came here for, why I really want to have the pinnacle of the mountain in Kingston, why I’d want to take over something that has been plaguing my life for so many years now. And it’s rather simple when it boils down to it. Because I can do so much of a better job terrorising people than the current idiot that I deserve to have this title. I don’t give a damn what other people think and no matter how hard they’ve tried to hold me down, I’m going to be what they have most feared. I am going to climb to the top and they are going to have to deal with all this trouble that they’ve shoved under the rug for far too long. I’ll show them all…I told them that I’d be standing atop the mountain and this represents my best opportunity. I looked at the mirrors and the multiple personalities all fazed into one…one of utter and complete contentment with the chaos and anarchy that was about to come into reality. I would finally show them what they’d made me… A light ping of the lift heralded the end of my journey up to the top of the building, right to the penthouse area which would be the apex. *** The door opened and I was slightly surprised there weren’t more people in the room. Ah, six was better than no audience to witness my triumph. There would be more people at my crowning ceremony however, I’d see to that. The penthouse of this building was quite exceptional, the lavish decoration leaving the rest of the building to shame. Picture the scene; a long red rug with gold trim angling its way around the room right up to the desk…nay it was a throne at the head of the penthouse. Along this journey to the summit there were pillars of granite and faux marble to litter the trek either side. Between the pillars were paintings and other assorted choices haute couture of the antique world. If you thought of a time period you could not only see it, you could smell it. The senses were treated to a cornucopia of perfumes and palatial delights, pristine artefacts and the tinkling of running water. I honestly had no idea where the water came from but it always rose bubbling up just near the throne, right where you would end up. I liked the symbolism behind it, again the architect and designer had earned his keep. As for those there…they weren’t too important, mostly just lackeys…and one of them looked considerably drunk. Probably from all their celebrating. They were nothing in comparison to the man who sat on the throne, the one he pretended to own. Garza Arrigo sat slumped over the chair, one leg hanging over an arm and looked for all the world like he was coming on to his ‘subjects.’ He had a loose fitting shirt which was open all the way to his navel, revealing the mass of body hair that was all tussled and curled, flowing forth from his body like a river of regret. Non-descript pants hung awkwardly on his legs and they seemed a bit torn and ripped from whatever had occurred in the preceding moments. He was happily swigging from a goblet like glass and his face seemed like one full of mirth and happiness. This wasn’t typically Arrigo when he was drunk, he was more likely to be in a depressing stupor than the life of the party, but things change depending on the mood. Up until when I knew him, he didn’t really have much going for him. When he saw me though his eyes flashed and all sense of merriment were drained just as quickly as the contents of the goblet. His brown eyes were heavyset and dulled from the alcohol and looked…no glared at me. At once everyone turned to stare at who had pried into their social gathering unexpected. Several of them looked surprised, the one who was drunk already just hiccupped and promptly fell unconscious on the floor. A shame really, he was supposed to be the next in line supposedly. His drinking problems wouldn’t be getting any better now. ‘What do you want?’ He roared it across the room and at the same moment he chucked the goblet full force towards me. Lacking the co-ordination to throw dextrously he only succeeded in aiming it at my feet, the goblet rolling to a stop on the rug which I still stood on. I picked it up and admired the intricate detail. Engravings and inset gems adorned the cup, the fruits of countless hours of labour apparent. Tossed away by a phantom, a pretender. ‘Giving in so quickly are we?’ My mystical comment puzzled him and he looked at me stupefied, vaulting to sit upright and look at me properly. ‘The half-breed. I knew you would come eventually.’ A carry on from my time in the underground. Due to my success I had always been approached with a guarded sense from the ‘true borns’ as they called themselves in the underground. They felt I was an interloper, a man who did not deserve the position that they should have instead. So effectively I was called a half-breed. Arrigo spat, hawking it up violently and aiming it again at me, hoping to catch me properly this time now that he was sitting upright in his throne. It missed completely again, landing so far away that you would’ve believed he felt I was moving towards a wall. Course, with the way he looked, it was entirely plausible he did believe that. ‘I knew you would come…come to try and take away something that Arrigo has earned. You will not be my usurper…you will come and sit at the feet of Arrigo, come and be my right hand.’ He pointed his finger at me with this gesture, as if saying that I should be honoured and distinguished to have such a wish bestowed upon me. When I didn’t move at all, Arrigo’s cheeky grin which had sat there for the past five minutes turned sour. He barked at his courtiers to go and get me up here. ‘Take him! He will be my symbol!’ They all ambled forward, lurching like zombies except for the unconscious one who was now drooling uncontrollably on the ground. A poor show, for a poor successor. To tell the truth, the possibility of one of them randomly being able to shoot me was very real (they must have had firearms) but it didn’t enter my mind once for this entire encounter. Instead all that was noteworthy was hearing the cracking of bone, the crunching of teeth against my bare knuckles and the heavy thuds as knees met internal organs. In a matter of moments, they were on the ground. Turning to Arrigo I now approached. ‘Now…it seems you and I have some business Arrigo, but not of the kind you feel that you think I am worthy for.’ He didn’t say a word, transfixed that I had carelessly tossed aside his subjects and attendants. ‘These men, they care not for what you are…but for what you sit upon. That chair is the power in this community, not who sits upon it. Especially not you.’ ‘Lies!’ ‘I wish it were true Arrigo, but you know I would not lie.’ I was playing to his sensibilities, playing on the bond that had existed with him whilst I trained him, gave him the confidence to be something more than a common braggart. I had failed but in a placebo effect way - for all intents and purposes he was something more, he just failed to see the reality. For my training he was thankful, grudgingly thankful but thankful all the same. ‘What you don’t realise Arrigo is that there are things beyond our control. For some people it is the destiny to lead, for others the destiny to be led. You have simply lucked upon the second. Can’t you see that they have thrust you into this role and not the other way round? Did you not keep schtum when Farrelli passed away?’ He had nothing to say to this and sat, pondering. ‘Think back to their nomination…and don’t think I don’t hear these things Arrigo, it’s only a matter of talking to the right people because despite what some think, I do have contacts here. Think back to their eagerness to display you as the new head…and yet they did not honour themselves. Wouldn’t that be the most opportune thing for them to do?’ Still nothing from the man. ‘Wouldn’t they aspire and dream for such a title, such an honour that you now hold? Instead they threw it away to you for you to hold but in reality…who is the master of who? You have been locked within the prize and now merely become its safe keeper, guarding it until someone worthy will step forward to take the mantle and dispel the shroud of murkiness over this town. You are a middle man, a means to an end. Don’t fool yourself into believing these men would not desire such a thing, they want it like nothing else…but right now they don’t have the power to go it alone. They need someone youthful that can tend to the herd whilst they all scheme and meddle amongst themselves…discover who is truly the next in line.’ He was shifting uneasily in the chair, almost like needles were pricking at him. ‘You are a pawn…and one that can easily be dispossessed in times of strife. You are one dimensional and for the majority, are taken by the easiest of methods. You do not hold the power on this chessboard, that is left to the more powerful. Arrigo, you have the opportunity now to either stake your claim or be lost in obscurity for the rest of your life. Do you want success?’ ‘…’ The mouth moved but no words escaped. I looked at him as pathetic and worthless, just like I thought he’d be. ‘I see. It is too late now for words Arrigo. You have failed, you had the chance to take it…but you’ve become the pawn and now you are there for the taking.’ It must have seemed like a blur to him, seeing my movements before him but before he could react I had grabbed him and thrown him roughly from the throne to the floor. He hit like a thud and did not rise, not at first anyway. Once he saw that I was advancing on him he crawled towards something to try and pull him up. The last thing he would see is the full face of a boot advancing of him. He did not get up after that. And I stood, gazing upon all the destruction before me. I knew what I had to do next but that would come in a few moments. For now I moved back to the throne and swiftly sat, leg crossed at a perpendicular angle as I stared at the chaos. Part one is achieved now. Only one more part to achieve.
