rEady? Act II

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- R/Echoes/A/D/Y -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The next setting takes place in the dank recesses of an abandoned factory. The exact details of the purpose it held is vague, but most people say that the planes piloted by the kamikaze fighters used during WWII were built there. There would be a lot of memories behind that place, most grim, most angry, and of course, all of them ready to die. But now the building itself was dead, ruined in the quakes of industrialization; it stands as a memory and as an eyesore of society. Decaying walls, rubble piled up behind its tearing chain link fence. Inside was no better, the conveyor belts and building stations now dusty and rusted heaps of metal. It was a mystery itself as to if the belt still worked, but no one cared to try. No one even cared to look at the place when they walked by, unless they were remarking on how grotesque it was compared to the whole of the neighborhood. Its existence was similar to leaving a rotting corpse out on display, one murdered for its beliefs. “Yah, I know! I couldn’t believe it!” The sound of a girl’s voice could be heard speaking inside, her chipper pitch echoing off the walls through the whole facility. She was speaking to someone, but the other person’s voice was inaudible. “But did you hear? You know… about Tachibana? Chibi had the nerve to ask her out… I know! Can you believe it? Tachibana’s totally not in that shrimp’s league.” Another pause as the scene moves amidst the relics of a war torn time. Hanging chains and piles of overturn plane tires. The skeleton of an aircraft… “I mean Tachibana’s not really all that popular, but she is pretty. She’s just dumb as shit… you heard she has to study EVERYDAY! Cram school AND tutoring! I mean… the work’s not THAT hard!” Slowly, but surely, the image of smooth milky white legs come into view dangling from a seated place overhead, pink flip-flops dangling as well as the girl’s toes curl up to keep them on. “Well at least I know I can do the work. I’m no genius, but Sato’s a pussy of a teacher. You just gotta know how he grades things… that’s all.” Then the waist of the girl, clad in tight short pink jogging shorts lined in white stitching. Her tight stomach was exposed, showing a diamond studded into her navel as a white tube top clung to her petite, but ample frame. “Heeheehee… shut UP! You think you’re funny, don’t you?” All the while this girl’s voice has spoken, her echo copied off of her every word. “Ahh, but you don’t know what I know…” Her gaze went up from her phone as she brushed back a bit of brown hair. She looked around the factory, like she noticed something that didn’t quite fit the picture. After nothing grabbed her attention, she went back to her conversation. “No… not that at…” Her eyes looked up again, this time her smile starting to lessen its grip on her face. The phone didn’t leave her ear though, she kept listening to the caller while inspecting. “… yes…” she said softly. “I know… oh SHUT---UP!!” She started laughing again… but her laugh was cut short. She stopped with a morbid expression on her face, staring ahead. “I’ll call you back,” she whispered. Hanging up the phone, she stared ahead of her, breaths short in her chest. Then she took in a deep breath, and released. “HELLLLLOOOOOOOOO!!!” Every last bit of air left her lungs screaming through the factory. She sat there catching her breath, looking around with a wary gaze. There was no echo… not even faintly. As she began to panic silently in her mind, her hands went up to clutch her chest. Her heartbeat… no birds, no cars, no wind rushing past, no cell phone ringing… only her heartbeat, she could hear. And she never even heard the person creeping up on her, nestling down against her back, chin resting on shoulder. Those pink lips pursed against her ear… but she heard the tiny wet sounds of the lips opening… and each drop of moisture clinging to her skin as a tongue snaked from those lips, and ran up the length of her earlobe. Suddenly she could hear herself breathe, and she knew that she was afraid. “Hello…” her echo came back to her finally, hissed into her ear by another voice altogether. Outside, a small young man with scruffy auburn colored hair walked by dressed in a white T and blue jeans with white sneakers. With hands stuffed into his pockets, he walked as if he was upset about something, shoulders slouched and a scowl on his face. As he reached the gates of the factory, a woman’s laughter echoed out from the wreckage. He stopped dead in his tracks, startled by the sound. He stared towards the old factory for a few seconds to answer his curiosity… but when the factory doors opened slowly… his courage was the first thing to run off, followed by his body itself. Fear… it’s brought about by an uncertainty of the unknown… yet soon it will be birthed by being aware of the Masonna…
