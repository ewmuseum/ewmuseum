The Lavelle Chronicles, ch4

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th of May The Lavelle Chronicles, Chapter 4 - The Troubles An introduction to the minds and life of Robert Green and Lois Mahr, a side-step. Robert Green and Lois Mahr were two ordinary people. Ones which you wouldn’t even notice were in the room with you. Robert Green had a few peculiar tastes when it came to the life and its meaning. But, Lois felt extremely close to the college nerd. Robert lay silent on the bed next to Lois, while recapping the last few months in his mind, pondering about the choices he has made and his college degree. Watching Lois sleep, he rose from the ruffled bed and sat on the edge of the bed, opening up his laptop. He began to write down everything he had learnt about Physics these last few years of his life. Realising he might have a future with the woman lying next to him, Robert Green began to speculate about a place in Harbrook college. He remembered what Pierce had said a few weeks back, about saving enough money to make a new start. He soon began to calculate how much money he and Lois would need to make a start in Albany, New York. Needless to say, Robert couldn’t wait to start parading Lois around to Gavin and the girls and guys from Harbrook College, who he had become friendly with. He didn’t think they would believe that he and Lois were together. Lois however was a lot like Lilly Connelly, she had features which a lot of girls envied. Back in high school Lois had been the prom Queen, of course her prom King, she never mentions. The prom King being her oldest friend Jake Richmond, a man not exactly a virtuous companion. Lois had made a life for herself in Harrisburg, but, when she met Robert her life seemed somewhat meaningful. Lois being a lot like Lilly in the way that she didn’t have to put of a fake face to look beautiful, she already did. She had a saying for the girls with too much fake tan that they glowed orange: “You’ve been tangoed”. Tango being an old fizzy pop which was a bright orange colour. As Lois was sleeping, Robert continued his mathematical ways and logical thinking. “Rob, what are you doing?” Lilly asked as she rose from under the bed covers. The moons glow shinning off her perfectly tanned skin. A natural tan that is. Robert putting his laptop down, turned around to his girlfriend, with a sort of cocky grin on his face. “Just wondering about a few things.” Rob said, clambering back under the bed covers to be next to her. “Oh yeah! What things?” Lois asked. Lois coincidentally was also studying journalism and had a way in which she could stare you down for the answers she was looking for. “Just finance and whether me and you could have a life here.” Rob said. Robert became a little tense, in waiting for Lois’s response. Who by now had slid just a little away from the comfort of Robert’s arm. “You mean, you wish to stay here and not come back to Harrisburg?” Lois said, a little shocked by Robert’s response to her last question. Lilly also became tense, wondering if she could actually lose the man, to which she loved. “I think so Lois. I don’t have anything back in Harrisburg, apart from you. And, well you’re here. I have been reading up on Harbrook Lois, and it is the best College out there. Me and you could finish our last year and go onto to get jobs. We could live here, as Pierce’s grandfather gave us this apartment, free from rent. I just think it would be better for us, to start fresh, here in New York. I know it’s not a logical idea, but it is what I want.” Robert looked into Lois’s eyes, trying to consol her that he would always be there for her. Lois took a long deep breath, the one women usually take to hold back nerves, tears and any other uncomfortable things that might happen. “I think it sounds good, but I haven’t given in my notice to the Dean yet.” Lois smiled. “Really?” Robert was excited now about the prospect of life here in New York with Lois. “Yes, I think we should do it. I think it is time we lived together and if you think Harbrook is the right choice, then we will go to it. Although we should probably finish our final exams for our professors back in Harrisburg. One thing though, what about Pierce?” Lois said. Robert of course knew exactly where this conversation was heading. A long time ago, before Rob and Lois actually met. Pierce and Lois were dating. It made it difficult at first for Rob to accept the fact that he was dating Pierce’s ex-girlfriend. The only thing that still made it difficult for Rob was the fact that Lois still cared about Pierce, in the way a really good friend would. It made Robert somewhat jealous but he had a sort of calm about the situation as Pierce now, only had eyes for one girl, Lilly Connelly. “I don’t know what Pierce will do, but, it is really late. I think we should get some sleep.” Robert said, turning over and lying down, trying to avoid that conversation. Robert's and Pierce’s apartment, And so it begins! I sat motionless on the bed realising what the last few hours had meant to me. I didn’t know what to think of everything. Four hours ago my sister Rebecca rang, I could picture the tears running down her cheeks and her seated on those blue plastic hospital chairs, with her head in her hands. As I closed my eyes, I felt as though I was there with her in that exact moment, the troubles for me were finally here. I couldn’t accept that this could be the final moment in which I would have to pack my things and actually return to Harrisburg. I knew what was happening, my eyes could picture it and my ears could hear it, but my mind refused to believe it. My mother had cancer. Saying the words repeatedly allowed me to hear them, but the tears kept coming, it was something not many would see happen, but I was crying. I held a Kleenex tissue in one hand as I held my mobile in the other. I could hear Rob and Lois in the next bedroom, giggling and messing, oblivious to the fact that my mother could be dying. She can’t die I repeated in an angry tone. Feeling as though somebody was taking the one person away from me, that kept me safe from my fathers evil ways. I thought of a time when her mother died one afternoon in February from a sudden heart attack. How my mother had broken down into tears. Granny Kelly was a lovely woman, she always had the perfect dress sense, although she despised my father and I couldn’t blame her. I remember one time my mother and I ran to Granny Kelly’s house to get away from his forceful hand. During the night he came calling and I can remember so vividly my grandmother dressed in a night gown, with her cane, threatening my father. Of course anyone who knew Granny Kelly, knew that she used to play for a huge women’s baseball team as a child during the war, to bring in some money to those in her area. She had a ferocious swing on her. My mother wept for hours and all my father could say “Well isn’t that a relief, the old bags gone”. He was so cruel to her then and he is now. I began to realise how many times my mother would cry, it would be at least three times a week. She was so miserable and so frightened. There were times I would hold her tight to stop the tears, my father would be drunk down stairs, playing poker with some rich snobby folk. And me and my mother would sit on my bedroom floor and cry. Every time she cried, I would cry. After all I was only around 9 years of age. I hadn’t changed my close from my last time out, which would have been my date with Lilly. The sun was setting in the distance and needless to say Lois and Robert weren’t helping my depression. I stood up and decided it was time to take a shower. I needed to get in contact with my grandfather, after all he was my only way back home to Pennsylvania. I pulled out the same duffle bag, which I found under my bed in Harrisburg and began to put a few clothes into its void compartment. “You’re leaving?” A voice said from the door way. I turned around and I was a little shocked by Lilly’s presence. “What are you doing here?” I asked, a little baffled by her presence. She stood under the shade of the room, so I couldn’t make out exactly what was wrong with this situation. “You didn’t answer my question,” Lilly implied once more about the duffle bag and my crumpled clothes on the floor. “Yes I am going to be leaving, but not for good. Only for a day or two. I need to return to Harrisburg to sort out something.” I said, continuing to repack my clothes. I know turned to Lilly who was standing beside me now, dressed in a Harbrook college jumper and a pair of jeans. I noticed a thin gash on her upper lip. It began to bother me as the blood was fresh. “Why are you?” I asked again. “I am afraid I can’t see you anymore,” Lilly said, turning her head away from me. “What do you mean?” I asked once more, my voice becoming a little squeaky. “I mean I can’t see you anymore, I am sorry Pierce. Really I am?” Lilly said, now turning her back to me. “It’s the gash on your upper lip isn’t it and the bruise on your hand. Lilly did George threaten you?” I said, approaching her now, realising she could’ve been assaulted. “NO! I just can’t see you anymore,” She ran out of the room and into the hallway, slamming the large oak door behind her. Rob stood still staring at me, he had obviously heard Lilly’s yelling of the word “no”. Which was still reverberating off the walls of my bedroom. I couldn’t let her go back to him, not after what I had just seen. So many things I needed to fix. “What the hell happened there?” Rob asked. “I’m not too sure myself, Lilly doesn’t want to see me anymore. I can’t accept that Rob, I just can’t” I said, now sitting on the corner of my bed, turning of mobile to make a call. “I think you will have to accept it Pierce. I know you really care for this woman, but you can’t get hurt for her. Especially when she is with someone else. You are just asking for trouble Pierce. Are you listening?” Rob said. “One minute!” I began to dial Lilly’s number. The phone sent me straight to her voicemail. Hey, this is Lilly. I’m not around at the moment. Please leave your name and number after the tone and I’ll get back as soon as possible. “Lilly, this is Pierce. Please ring back after you get this message. I don’t quite understand what this is about.” With that I hung up. “Voicemail?” Rob asked. “Yeah!” I stood up now leaving my duffle bag on the bed, I went into the main lounge, to where Rob was standing. “You going somewhere?” Rod said, while looking at my bag, which still lay amongst the crumpled pile of clothes. “Yes, but I will have to leave that until later. I need to sort things out.” I noticed then that the red light was blinking on our answering machine. I quickly pushed the button, hoping that maybe Rebecca or Lilly had run and I had missed it. Instead I was greeted by the voice of the man I needed to see. “Pierce, listen lad, Suzie and I are opening up our restaurant “Verdici” in Madison Avenue. I would like you and your friends to come to the opening night. How does that sound? Well anyway, I am sorry about our last rendezvous. I know what your impression must be of me and Suzie, but please Pierce, you must accept it. I need you to accept it.” With that the machine went dead. The voicemail was empty, bar my grandfathers message. “An opening night in a posh restaurant. Think of the photos and glamour Pierce. Loads of famous people will be there. Pierce what is it man? You are so quiet.” Rob said, now noticing my edginess and my unwillingness to listen. “My mom is sick Rob, she’s got cancer.” I said. “Holy crap Pierce, I didn’t know. I am so sorry man. Come on I have an idea. We’ll get dressed and go to this dinner thing, then when you are there. You can talk to you grandfather about possibly getting a loan of some money to go home. You can’t stay here Pierce. I will go with you to Harrisburg if needs be. I am sorry I was so distant towards you when Lois dumped me, I didn’t think straight. But, I am here for you man. So come on, lets get dressed. Or changed out of old clothes and lets head to the party. We have three hours before opening night.” Rob looked up at the clock and saw it was now 7:00pm. I had already calculated that myself and Rob had 40 minutes to get dressed and to make it to the party for 8:30pm. “You’re right Rob, I need to see my grandfather. He needs to know too.” With that I ran into my room and found a pair of black suit trousers and dark blue shirt. I quickly put on my silver Rolex watch and stripped down out of my old clothing. My black fine polished shoes, lay underneath my bed. Within the space of ten minutes I was dressed in my posh looking clothes and smelling of Lynx. Robert and Lois were both equally quick. Of course, Robert being a big mouth; let it slip to Lois about my mother. When we dated Lois was very fond of my mother and she wouldn’t stop saying how sorry she was. It reminded me of a broken record. They made it seem like she was already dead. “Verdici” opening night on Madison Avenue The velvet red carpets lined the stone walkway, as thousands of paparazzi scattered around the metal fencing, built to hold back the herd of journalists and paparazzi. Robert couldn’t help but pretend he was someone famous as he and Lois stood on the red carpet having their photos taken, over and over. With some people shouting random things to Lois: “HEY SEXY, over here babe”, of course when she turned around, she would be greeted by a million flash bulbs. I on the other hand, remained calm and strode up the red carpet, towards the double glass doors. The doorman stopped Rob, Lois and I until he checked my name. “Name please?” The doorman said. “Lavelle” I replied. “You grandfather is expecting you. He is in the VIP suite, go to your right and down, past the neon lights and the dance floor. Until you come to a marbled stair case. Proceed up that stair case and take a left. Then on your way there, you must register with the VIP boss and you shall be allowed into the room. He is in the West wing suite. A green and red Ralph Lorraine area. Enjoy your night.” The doorman let us past. I felt as though I was been given directions through a large marbled maze. Inside however, it was a complete different site. Everybody was up and dancing. There was a large plasma screen up on the left wall. A stage was set up, in which my grandfather was seated. I knew I needed to get to him before the show began. The music was over bearing. The Black eyed peas came on and Rob shielded his ears to what we would call: “Manufactured rubbish”. I now wished they were playing some good solid, rock and roll. Unfortunately they weren’t. “Oh my god!” Gavin shrieked as he came across the dance floor. “Ah christ not him!” Rob whispered. “Pierce, Rob and a woman. What are you doing here?” Gavin said, holding hands with some tall Italian man. “I am here to speak to my grandfather.” I replied, trying to see if my grandfather had moved. But the bopping heads made it impossible to even see the stage area. “Really?” Gavin said. “Whose your grandfather?” He asked, while looking at Rob. “The owner of this restaurant, I said.” It was then I spotted him. I ran past Gavin and his lover and into the crowd of celebrities and dancers. I had reached the stage. Suzie spotted me first and helped me up onto the stage. My grandfather was constantly checking his watch. “Oh no, I am supposed to turn on the sound” Gavin said to Rob, unaware that Pierce was about to say something to his grandfather next to a microphone which he thought was off. “Ah, there we go. Power on.” “My mothers got cancer!” I didn’t realise what had just happened. Neither did my grandfather. The DJ had stopped playing the music and the bouncing heads, had stopped bouncing. My grandfather looked at me, and saw the tears coming to my eyes. Without my realising I had blurted my mothers condition to every single celebrity and newspaper. My grandfather would now pay the price. “Turn off the microphone,” My grandfather yelled, the power was off and the music continued. I hoped off the stage and my grandfather followed me. “Pierce would you please wait. This is important. We need to talk about this. Don’t you realise what you have just told me. We need to get in contact with Brad.” “No, I won’t let him hurt her anymore. He did this I tell you.” I said in anger at the suggestion to which my grandfather had implied. “Pierce you are not thinking straight. You know that cancers, benign or malignant; are already in the body as dead or sleeping cells. No human being on the planet could give you cancer, through a tough marriage. Please Pierce, you are racing a mile a minute. You have to stop. Relax.” My grandfather was right, my father didn’t do this to my mother, but there was no way in hell that I wanted him to be anywhere near her. Not while this trouble was among us. I couldn’t stop thinking, my mind was racing. So many things were happening to me and now, now I had to deal with everyone elses problems. “Pierce, how much do you need?” My grandfather said. “What?” I asked. “That’s why you are here isn’t it? That’s all your family has wanted from me; money. What makes you any different?” My grandfather was annoyed too. “How dare you accuse me of wanting to take all of your money, you shouldn’t judge your family but your gold digger for a wife. Why do you think Suzie married you granddad. Well it sure as hell wasn’t for you looks or charm. It was for you money and your mansion. A quick and easy way to get money.” I couldn’t believe what I had just said. “Pierce what is wrong with you man?” Rob said, hearing my response to my grandfather. “I can’t take this right now.” I turned around and left, running as fast as I could. The restaurant was soon behind me. I ran into a back alley and lay against the wall. Taking in a deep breath. I had cried too many times to day. I felt trapped. I needed to keep running. Something was unfinished with still and I knew what I had to do. Harbrook College I didn’t know what I was thinking, but I was now standing outside Lilly’s Residence. Without a single thought, I walked up to the front door. It looked so empty this time though, the lights were off and the other two girls were just arriving home as I began to knock. “Hey handsome, you looking for Lilly?” The brunette asked me. “Yes, is she in?” I was desperate now. “I’ll check for you hunny,” The blonde replied opening the door. “Lilly it’s Liz, babes you home?” It was then that I saw George-Scott Lewis standing in the hallway. He turned on the lights. He was dressed in a white sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of black jeans, with a large gold belt. Behind him, two more men stood. Lilly was sitting on the stairs in tears. “Lilly, you okay?” I said, now approaching the doorway. George charged towards me, taking me down. We fumbled down the long steps until we were both flat out on our backs. “Leave him alone George,” Lilly yelled from the top step. I felt stranded. It was like a clip from Jaws, only I wasn’t a hot brunette in a red swim suit. The three men circled me like three sharks, each one waiting for his turn. Waiting for me to make a move, so they could narrow in for the kill. I could smell the fine whiskey off their breaths. “Come on punk, why don’t you take a punch.” One of the men said. “He’s a coward” The other said. “A loser” George said. It was then he hit me in the side, sending me into the thrash cans. I was up quickly but before I could do a thing. The other two goons were on me like a pack of vultures. I curled my hands in a defensive position to protect my face as they began their clubbing blows into my ribs. My lip had been cut open from my descent down the stone steps. “Leave him alone!” Lilly yelled once more, while watching me take a beating. “Oh, Lilly doesn’t like me beating up a man she loves.” George said, teasing her now. “You’re right about that,” I said. “I do like Lilly” I felt stupid saying that aloud. It aggravated the beast within him. “What did you say punk!” George said, picking up a crowbar. I was a little wobbly from my beating, but I could still stand. The other two goons had backed off. Leaving it to just me and George. I could see the crowbar swinging by his hip. “You heard me, I like your girlfriend.” As he charged at me, I ducked and took him down with a sharp spear like manoeuvre which came in handy, at College wrestling. George fell hard to the ground. But, his two goons were back. Each one giving me a sharp kick to the gut. “That’s it boys, break it up. I’ve called the cops.” A man said, from behind the shadows. George and his two goons ran. I slowly stood up with the help of Lilly. “Why did you come Pierce?” Lilly asked. “Are you alright son?” It was then I noticed my father behind me, he helped me up. I shrugged him off. “What are you doing here? You’re supposed to be in Chicago.” I said. “I was at the opening night of my fathers restaurant. I heard what you said about Maria. We need to go home son.” My father said. “Stop calling me that.” I said. “But, you’re my son,” My father stated. “And you’re a terrible father. I didn’t need your help. I could handle them myself.” I said. “Pierce, please stop this,” Lilly said. “You need to stop being so angry. Your father helped you.” “That’s the first time he’s done that,” I said. “Pierce, this is enough. You are acting immature.” My father said. “No dad, I’m acting like Pierce Lavelle. The me you didn’t want. Remember?” It was then, that Rob and Lois appeared. “I remember son, and all I wanted for you was the best. I know I am a horrible man, but I don’t want your mother to die.” My father said. “She won’t die!” I yelled in the street. “Hey, Mr. Lavelle I think you should wait by you car. Just let Pierce cool down for a few moments. Lilly would you take him in and clean him up. I’ll go and get a spare pair of clothes from our flat down the road. Inside Lilly’s bedroom I sat on Lilly’s bed in nothing but a white beach towel. Lilly sat opposite me, with a cloth and some iodine to take away the blood and grit that laid embedded into my knuckles and arms. Every time she would dab the iodine gently onto my sore, I would flinch with the pain. I hated iodine, as a child, myself and Rob had been swinging on some monkey bars. I jumped and ended up on my back, flat out on some gravel. I had scrapped my arm from my elbow to my wrist and my mothers solution, iodine. I remembered the childish scream I had let out, as she dipped my arm into a bowl filled with iodine. Such an old method of curing cuts and bruises, but, it worked. “Why did you come back Pierce?” Lilly asked, now coming closer to me, until she was in between my legs. “Because I didn’t want to lose you as a friend, and to be frank with you Lilly. I knew that George hit you. It was a bit obvious. The swelling on your lip and the bruising around you wrist, which I take it he grabbed with some force.” Lilly however, didn’t respond to this. Instead she moved away and sat next to me, to deal with the cuts on my forehead. “I’m sorry if I made you look like a fool in front of your friends.” I said, realising that the blonde and brunette were there for the whole fight. “Don’t worry about it, besides, Rachael thinks you’re cute.” Lilly put on a fake smile. “Which one is Rachael?” I asked, pretending to be interested. “The brunette!” Lilly replied. “Ouch! That stings!” I said, as Lilly dabbed the gash above my eye. “Stop being such a baby,” Lilly laughed. I couldn’t help but smile and look into Lilly’s eye, even for just a brief moment in time. The more I spent with this girl, the more I loved her. I just hoped she knew it or, felt the same way. Lilly was about to do or say something when Robert entered, bearing some new clothes. “Hey champ, got you some new clothes. That must have been some fight, Pierce.” Rob said with a grin on his face. I stood up and took the clothes off him and returned the smile. I walked out of Lilly’s bedroom and into the bathroom. I was annoyed by Rob’s sudden appearance because, who knows what Lilly could’ve done. I just imagined what it would’ve of been like if she had kissed me. Well you can’t always be the ‘knight in shining armour’. In a few moments I was out of the towel and into a pair of baggy black jeans, with a flame stitching on the left leg, I threw on a white Thompson College jumper on over my simple white t-shirt. “Well you look a lot better,” Rachael the brunette said, who was waiting to use the bathroom. “Thanks,” I walked past her and into Lilly’s room, but, she was gone. Rob was downstairs waiting for me. A trip outside “Where is Lilly?” I asked. “Not sure,” Rob said, I knew he was keeping something from me. “Listen, the cops have arrived and have taken the names of the three men to which attacked you and Lilly. She helped with that area. She’s gone to her mothers house for the rest of the week. She said she needed to be alone. What did you say to her?” “I don’t know, I really don’t,” I said, a little baffled by what had happened over the course of four minutes. From the bathroom to the downstairs of Lilly’s residence; so much had happened. “Was there some sexual tension?” Rob asked. “WHAT?” I laughed to myself. “That’s for me to know and you to keep on wondering.” “Oh I almost forgot. Your father is waiting for you.” Robert said. “Great!” I took one last look at the pink hallway and then stepped outside and headed down toward the silver Nissan, to which my father had rented for his visit to Albany. “Pierce, I think me and you need to talk.” My father started off by saying. “No we don’t!” I looked him in the eye. “Yes we do. I am sick of this arguing all the time Pierce, your mother and your sister need us. We have to stop all the bickering and act like men. I know you want to be your own man and you can hate me all you want, but, the fact of the matter is, we both need to return home.” My father approached me now. “I never once fought with you, I never once disobeyed you. Instead you turned me into a lab rat. I was always too scared to cause rebuttal. Encase you hit me, like you hit mom.” I looked away from my fathers ice cold eyes and his glare toward Robert, who of course had a shocked look upon his face. Even though Robert knew me for a long time, he never knew about the goings on in my house. Only that my father was the wicked wizard of the west! “Fine, I deserve that. But, we do need to talk.” This time he was right, we did needed to talk. Especially about my idea to transfer my mother to the main hospital here in Albany. I had enough money in my bank account to fund for this so everything was planned out. I knew Rebecca had always wanted to see Albany and of course to go shopping here. So in a way it might take her mind off my mothers illness and more so on my credit card. “You’re right, we do need to talk.” I said. “Finally…..” I cut him off before he could say another word. “I’ve made arrangements for my mother and my sister to be taken here. Mom is going to be staying in Albany Memorial Hospital. Rebecca will be staying with me and Robert; and you, well I don’t care what you do, but, I don’t want you anywhere near me or Rebecca. I am glad you wanted to hear this Brad, because from now I am standing my grounds.” Of course I was harsh in what I had said, but, in situations like these I guess you needed to be. “Take good care of her okay. I’ll stay around nearer to Maria. I want to help her through this, as much as you do. Goodnight son, take care.” With that my father got into the silver Nissan and drove off back toward his father’s restaurant. Nothing had ever gone so smoothly before; was my fathers old age starting to kick in? View Pierce Lavelle 's Biography
