Three Fates, Slowly Entwined

18 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/8 Azagtoth was not 'all right'. Definitively not all right at all. The most harrowing fifteen minutes of his life had just ran past in the form of an overseas phone call, from one of the lower-ranking members of the Cult. The police were after Him. They wanted Him for Questions and a Little Talk. Azagtoth immediately realized that they had dug something up... Maybe sparked by the Ireland brawl with the security guards. Or maybe it was something else: one of the many broken, slashed, devastated figures left in the gutters along the way. Who knows? Any skeleton might come out of His closet as far as Azagtoth knew: He was the equivalent of Life, The Death and The Universe for Azagtoth, without a question or an answer. Leader, warchief, greatest fear... He. Azagtoth shivered and twisted his fingers as he tried to think up something to alleviate the situation. Maybe he should… No. How about?… No, not right either. Pacing about in the small hallway of their hotel room, the hotel room they had been forced to get for themselves. Those godly bastards, Azagtoth thought bitterly, injecting anger into his thoughts, FORCING it in to make fear and self-doubt go away. How DARE they deny Him a proper place to sleep in? He was the worthiest of all of these sacks of shit. And yet they left Him to rot in an Irish prison so vile not even the Irish were locked in there. Azagtoth snarled at nothing in particular and continued his little round. Oh yes, payback would be SO sweet… But not now. He was more important than petty thoughts of vengeance. THEY WERE AFTER HIM. As far as he loathed to admit it, Azagtoth realized that finally something had caught up to Him. His untouchable shield had been tarnished. His mighty armor had been chipped. He was no longer untouchable. Before, complete mystery to the normal world, a wispy enigma that couldn’t be touched. Now, something concrete, something FLESH, something that could be touched and seen and taken away for Questions and a Little Talk… Azagtoth knew all about Little Talks. His dad had given them to him quite often. But he wasn’t Azagtoth’s REAL dad, Azagtoth knew that. A father was someone you should be able to love. Someone you should be able to respect. Someone you would die for if requested. And He was a father to Azagtoth, a father he found only after being disappointed for many years and not even realizing it. He was someone Azagtoth could love and respect. Someone he’d die for, someone he’d kill for, someone he’d burn the world for and spread the ashes beneath His feet if He so asked. But He wasn’t here yet… And it would be clear if He ever got out in time. In time to slaughter the infidels and purge the world of believers. There would be only one kind of believer if Azagtoth could have his way. His believers. Believers of the Cult. And Him, atop a throne of skulls, holding the flowing ashes of the last Pope in a crusted chalice, laughing at the world he so rightly destroyed… With Azagtoth by his side. Azagtoth’s dream and fantasy were cut off by a sudden rush of thoughts. His return must be prepared. The troops must be gathered. The orders issued out. The legions of Hell itself raised up from the ground to march beneath His barrier. And He needed everything to be perfect before He came back… Azagtoth knew it. He demanded perfection. And Azagtoth gladly gave it. Maybe later Azagtoth could go and play with his Trophies? Yes, Trophies, the pretty Trophies… The airport security almost opened his bag because it smelt funny. They would have found the Trophies. Boy would they have been surprised. ----- Angela was excited as well, but not because of the same reasons as Azagtoth. Or, to put it the other way, she WAS excited for the same reason as Azagtoth was. Hate was coming back: her guiding light, her messenger of inner peace. He was coming back, released from his cold shackles any moment now and rising like the Phoenix. Angela stayed in her small room, with the door lock, laying naked on the bed and just thinking about Hate. Cold sweat made the sheets cling to her body, but she didn’t mind. She could hear Azagtoth through the door, pacing around, muttering to himself, occasionally yelping and muttering “Yes, my leader, yes…”, but Angela didn’t heed him. Angela had learned to shut the boy off: once Angela had lived in the same building as him, he had turned from the knife-wielding monstrosity she had first met into a troubled, twisted 19-year old boy. A youngster. What the hell did he know? Nothing, as far as Angela knew. He just spouted off his empty rhetoric and waved his knife around. It’s not like he ever really DID anything… Just picked on those weaker than him. A bully, Angela thought. Thinking of Azagtoth made another wave of shivers run up her spine… And then she remembered something. She had been locked in the room with Azagtoth’s bags. Staring at the two suitcases over the edge of the bed, strange thoughts ran through her head. Open them, the voices told her. Open them and see what makes Azagtoth tick. Slowly, Angela got out of bed. And walked to the first suitcase. Lifted it onto the bed (there is something red seeping into the carpet beneath it. Angela doesn’t see it) Laid it onto its side. (more red, now seeping into the clammy sheets. She notices but ignores. It’s nothing) Unlocks. (there’s a sharp smell in the suitcase. Pungent. Repulsive. Yet oddly inviting and comforting) Exhale. (ohmygodohmygodohmygod. It’s open, a little) Inhale. Open. (ohmygod. Trophies. His trophies) Close the suitcase. Hate must know about this. He HAD to know about it. He couldn’t let this go by unnoticed. He MUST know about Azagtoth’s luggage. He couldn’t have ignored it… Could he? ----- Crisp London air meets Hate in the face with a puff of cold wind. The fog, eternal and so cliched, rolls over the houses and the streets, enveloping them for the modern-day Jack The Rippers to work. Tomorrow, Hate would be driving through these streets with five other wrestlers, fighting for a championship… Or in the case of Hate, just fighting. Being stunned was no fun. Now Hate wanted blood. And revenge. And to fight. There was no greater spiritual catharsis than that caused by bloodlust and rushing adrenaline. Let them keep their piece of leather and metal, forcefully stitched together to “mean” something. Let Hate keep the blood, and the fear, and the chaos, and the streets. They were his. His only. “Cleanse the Impure. Raise the Warcry.” With a smile on his face, Hate walked into the foggy streets of London. To wipe the smiles off of everyone else’s faces.
