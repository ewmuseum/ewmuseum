A little visit to the past

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th August 2005 [We find ourselves on Little road in London, which is just 5 minutes round the corner from Earls Court, the venue which will host the Battle Of Britain. The night sky has just taken over and the evening chill has consumed the air. We stand opposite the illustrious Paragon Hotel where some of the AWC super will be spending the next few nights in preparation for their matches. We look up at the magnificent 502 bedroom hotel. The lights of the windows glow making the face of the building appear like a chess board. There are spot lights shining up the building making a huge glow travel up to the top of the building. Bellow there are the large glass pain windows exposing the lobby and restaurant areas, including the floor above seems to be some kind of bar. Just slightly further down is the entrance of the fantastic hotel. After a couple of moments a black London cab arrive, inside are the first few AWC super stars arrive. The cab slows down with the chugging of the diesel engines echoing through the night. The driver steps out of his vehicle and comes round to the back and pulls the door open. Two men step out each baring a backpack and holding a sports bag. Yes it is none other than Red Rock and Boolie. HOORAY!] Red Rock: Thank you chum! How much is that going to be? Cabbie: Thirty quid mate! [Red Rock reaches in to his pocket and pulls out some cash.] Red Rock: Bugger I've only got a twenty...hold on! [Red Rock reaches in to his pocket and pulls out two handfuls of shrapnel and begins to put it in the Cabbie's hands.] Red Rock: Sorry about this, they don't pay me as much as you'd think! [The Cabbie sighs and taps his foot as Red Rock sorts through ten pounds worth of pound coins, 50p pieces and pennies.] Cabbie: Look. forget it mate, I got an airport job in 20 minutes and you're making me late! Red Rock: Alright darling! Calm down! [The Cabbie walks away with his pockets jingling from all of Red Rock's loose change. Just as Red Rock's Cabbie pulls away two more cabs pull up and who steps out of each? None other than The British Bomber and Paddy O' Shea. Red Rock notices them both and rolls his eyes.] Red Rock: Oh no not those two... [Red Rock turns his back and puts his head down to try and hide from them both, but Red Rock's attempts to shroud himself are foil by Boolie who is incessantly waving at the two AWC stars with a goofy childish grin.] Boolie: HEY BOMBER AND PADDY YOOO HOOOOO WE'RE OVER HERE! Red Rock: Oh no shhhh Boolie! Don't call them over – OH Hey Paddy, Bomber how are you guys! Paddy: aye how ye doin' Red Rock fancy that, turnin' up at the same time! Bomber: Hey Red. [Red Rock nods British Bomber and acknowledges his greeting.] Red Rock: Well then, I suppose we better go check in then. Paddy: Aye! This place looks fancy! [The AWC crowd walk up the few steps to the front door and enter the building. They find them selves standing in the swish lobby. The floor is gleaming marble and the ceiling is also only a pearly white colour. There is a chandelier in the centre of the ceiling, glistening with the light reflecting off it. There are two large white circular pillars supporting the ceiling, which adds to the grandness of the place. The 4 men look around in amazement of the quality of the place.] Red Rock: why is it that AWC can afford to put all of us up in this place, yet I'm still paid $10,000 a year? Paddy: 10,000? that all? I wipe my arse with that! Red Rock: yeah thanks Paddy...lets just check in! [The 4 men, Red Rock, Boolie, Paddy O' Shea and British Bomber walk up to the front desk and checks in. Paddy and Bomber go first and receive the keys to the rooms and Red Rock shortly follows.] Receptionist: and who are you sir? Red Rock: I'm Red Rock, and this is Boolie. [The receptionist checks her book and winces her eyes.] Receptionist: mmm... [She runs her finger up and down the page searching for Red Rock's name but appears to be having difficulty.] Receptionist: I'm very sorry, but there doesn't appear to be any reservation under this name sir. Red Rock: What? Ah gay! Are you sure? Receptionist: I'm very sorry but you just don't appear to be here. I would offer to book another room but I'm afraid we're full up sir. [Red Rock frowns and puts his hands in his pockets.] Red Rock: Right O...um...I'll just call the personell guy, maybe theres been a mistake. [Red Rock withdraws his mobile phone from his pocket and swiftly dials Clyde Norris, AWC's newly employed Personell manager.] Norris: Hello Norris here! Red Rock: Hello it's Red Rock here...would you be so kind to tell me as to WHY THE FUCK YOU HAVEN'T BOOKED ME A HOTEL ROOM? Norris: Errr...sorry Red, simple mistake to make! Red Rock: You cunt Norris, I fucking hate you! What am I supposed to do now? Norris: Erm calm down Red, I'm not going to help you if you swear at me! Red Rock: Don't give me shit you bastard you didn't book me a room so you have to sort it out you little bitch! [Paddy, Bomber and Boolie look on from the other side of the room giggling as Red Rock stomps up and down in a rage over this mishap.] Paddy: I tink he need a drink aye? Boolie: He usually gets like this when stuff goes wrong, he'll calm down eventually! [And Back over to Red Rock.] Red Rock: So what are you going to do about it Norris? Norris: There's nothing I can do, can't you go somewhere else? Red Rock: I haven't got anymore money! Norris: Well you're going to have to share with another super star then. Look I'm extremely busy I have to go. * CLICK * [Red Rock grumbles as he shoves his phone back in his pocket.] Red Rock: Busy doing what? Fucking idiot! [Red Rock walks back across the room as other visitors stare at Red Rock. He walks back over to the others and lets out a huge sigh.] Red Rock: What are they all staring at? Boolie: You were using your outdoor voice again! Bomber: What was that all about then? Red Rock: They forgot to book my hotel room, so I have to share with one of you two. [Paddy and Bomber huddle and confer over who shall accommodate Red Rock.] Paddy: Well I can't do it, I need my privacy! Bomber: I don't want him anywhere near me! I think he's...you know...a bit poofy! Paddy: the only way I can think of is a game of Rock Paper Scissors! [Bomber nods and agrees.] ROCK, PAPER, SCISSOR Paddy throws paper. Bomber throws rock Bomber: FUCK! Red Rock: What? [Bomber throws his bags at a bell boy as another picks up Red Rock's bag and Red Rock, Boolie and British Bomber follow their bell boys to their room, as Paddy follows his to his own room.] A few moments later. [Red Rock and Bomber are now standing in their hotel room, number 69. The room is fairly large and over the other side of the room is the bathroom and next to the door to the bath room is a small table with a little flower in it.] Red Rock: heh, heh! Our room number is 69. Bomber: Don't get any ideas OK? Red Rock: I don't know what you're talking about. [Bomber pauses for a second and looks around the room and notices there is something amiss.] Bomber: So where is the other bed? There only appears to be one bed?! [Bomber's face turns pale and he clenches his teach and he frantically searches the room for another bed.] Bomber: WHERE IS THE OTHER SODDING BED?! Red Rock: There isn't another bed Bomber, looks like we're going to have to share! [Red rock and Boolie grin at Bomber who is looking a bit worried due to the fact he has got the impression Red Rock and Boolie are a gay couple.] Red Rock: Don't worry I won't bum you! [Bomber doesn't say anything, he just glares at Red Rock suspiciously.] Bomber: I'm going to go have a shower now...ALONE. Red Rock: Right o! I'll make myself at home. [Bomber grabs his bag and heads to the shower. He also places his wallet on the table by the door on the way.] Boolie: When he's done in there I gotta take a crap! This one is touching cloth! Red Rock: Yeah, I can smell thanks for that Boolie... [Red Rock sits down on the big double bed and bounces up and down on it to test the comfort. Red Rock reaches in to his bag and pulls out a piece of paper and begins to read it in silence. Red Rock now has fallen in to a state of deep thought as he reads the letter, it looks like an address. Boolie leans over to investigate as to why Red Rock is being so quiet all of a sudden.] Boolie: Hey Red [No answer.] Boolie: RED! Red Rock: Oh sorry Boolie, I wasn't with it! Boolie: What's that bit of moldy old paper? Red Rock: Oh this? Oh it's the address for the wrestling school where I trained to be a wrestler...I was thinking about going back there...it's not too far away from here and as seen as I'm in the main event for Battle Of Britain I figured I'm going to need to some advice...I can't think of anyone better than the guy who gave me my first break. [Red Rock sighs and reminisces over his early days training at the Duncan “The Bullet” Williams wrestling school.] Red Rock: I was trained by one of the greatest wrestlers in the UK, Duncan “The Bullet” Williams. He was a great man, 6'4 270 pounds, a head full of blonde golden hair and a body like a work of art! He taught me everything I know to do with wrestling! Boolie: But all you do is punch people in the balls! [Red Rock glares at Boolie and replies defensively.] Red Rock: Shut up Boolie! I can wrestle! I just want to go back to the old wrestling school to see if he can give me any pointers and maybe whip me in to shape a little bit, just so I can be ready for this match! [Red Rock's thoughts linger for a few moment before they are interrupted by the bath room door opening. The British Bomber steps followed by a cloud of steam he is wearing a towel round his waist and is drying his face with a smaller hand towel. He picks up his wallet off the small table and checks through it for a bit of money. Suddenly he drops his wallet and as the steam clears he notices Red Rock and Boolie sat down side by side smiling with their hands on their laps. Bomber looks down at his wallet and then looks at Red Rock. He gasps as he realizes that he will have to bend over to pick it up. An awkward pause is created as Bomber hesitates to pick up his wallet.] Red Rock: Well aren't you going to pick that up? [Bomber keeps his eyes locked on Red Rock as he thinks Red Rock may try and bum him.] Bomber: I...don't want to pick it up... Red Rock: Well that's just silly isn't it? Are you afraid I might bum you or something? Ha, Ha! [Red Rock laughs and looks over at The British Bomber who stares back looking quite horrified.] Red Rock: You think I'm gay? I'm not gay! I'm not! Bomber: o....k then... [Bomber slowly bends down to pick up his wallet and tightly grasping his towel to make sure it doesn't fall off.] Red Rock: Right...Me and Boolie are going to pop out for a bit...so you can have the room for your self for a bit, to a have a quick one if you want? [The British Bomber doesn't say anymore and just glares back as Red Rock awkwardly back away to the door.] Approx 1 hour later [Red Rock and Boolie find themselves in a dingy rough area of east London. The houses are all old and date back to the 1900's. The gutters hang off the rooms and tiles are missing. Red Rock and Boolie walk up the battered old roads looking around the familiar area. The sky is gray and depressing and it creates a dark atmosphere, which doesn't help Red Rock's nervous state. He turns left down a side alley. The grass is over grown by the bottoms of the walls and the ground is dirty and swamped with rubbish. Just at the end of the alley way is an old beaten up wooden shack type building. The paint is flaking and peeling and the wood is rotting and has green slim creeping up the wet rotten wooden panels.] Red Rock: Crikey! This place has gone down hill...fucks sake its only been 3 years since I left I even know wood could rot that fast...and it had that wood seal stuff on it. Boolie: Ha, you trained here? Red Rock: Hey don't knock it Boolie! It may be shit but it's my roots! [There is a slight sense on sentiment in Red Rock's tone, this is where Red Rock learned moves like his RDT, and the Rusty Junior hacksaw. He stares up at the building and remembers all the knocks and bumps he took to get where he is today.] Red Rock: Right-o lets go in! [Red Rock sucks in a big breath and marches forward towards the knackered old door. He grabs the handle releasing all his nerves and portraying an image of confidence across his face. He thrusts his hand out and wraps his fingers round the door handle and yanks it, but to Red Rock's surprise the handle rips right off the wooden door.] Red Rock: Fucking hell! Boolie: UUUMMM You broke it! Red Rock: Shut up Boolie! [Red Rock nervously looks around and hides the broken hand in a near by shrub, instead Red Rock knocks on the door. He steps back and awaits an answer from his former mentor. He gets no answer.] Boolie: Maybe he's not in? Red Rock: The guy never was married or anything, it's not like he has a wife and kids to be attending to. Boolie: A lot can happen in 3 years Red, maybe he moved on? [Red Rock ignores Boolie and knocks again with increased force. He steps back again and waits for an answer but to no avail. After a few frustrating moments on Red Rock's part he walks back over to the door and again gives is a vigorous pound and before he can step back the front door falls through, crashing to the floor. Red Rock's jaw drops and he stands before the open door way.] Red Rock: Bugger! Boolie: Well the doors open now! Lets go in! [The inside of the building appears to be cold and dark, there are no lights on but you can make out the old ring in the middle of the place. It was a boxing ring Red Rock has used which made training much more painful. Boxing rings didn't have the same kind of protection as a wrestling ring. Red Rock and Boolie enter. The old gym has an eerie haunted feel about it, the air is musty and it smells of wood rot and damp. Red Rock and Boolie cautiously walk around making sure they don't trip over on anything.] Red Rock: Hello, Duncan? [Red Rock's voice echoes off the walls as the venture further in to the building.] Boolie: Heh, this is like that scene in 28 days later when the blokey is in the burger bar and the zombie kid runs at him. Red Rock: Yeah just like that, but without all the bodies! [Suddenly somewhere in the building they hear a noise, it sounds like a random item dropping like a small lighter or something. Suddenly from the corner of the room a small red glow can be seen. It catches Red Rock's eye and he jumps back.] Red Rock: Bloody hell! Theres someone over in the corner Boolie! [Red Rock and Boolie stand frozen and tighten their fists. The person in the corner gets up and walks towards Red Rock and Boolie. He becomes more visible as he steps more and more in to the light. The man who stands before him is a fat slob with thick stubble. He looks desperately over weight and a servilely troubled man. Red Rock simply stares at the man seeming to bare and expression of disappointment.] Red Rock: Oh...my...GOD...Duncan?! What the bloody hell happened to you? Duncan: I knew you'd come back Josh, or should I say Red Rock? Red Rock: Bugger me matey you got fat! What the hell happened to you and what the hell happened to this place? Duncan: It all went tits up mate, it ALLL went tits up. Red Rock: Bu..But how? Duncan: Take a seat Josh. [Duncan, Red Rock and Boolie sit down on an old torn moldy leather couch.] Duncan: To tell you the truth Josh, after I let you go to America the whole business went down. You might not believe it but after you left no one wanted to come to the shows anymore. Red Rock: People liked me? Duncan: Yeah Josh, they did. I know I didn't make it very clear but you were the best I had. Red Rock: But you always told me how much I sucked and how worthless I was and how much work I needed...and you always made me wash the other wrestlers tights, including that one guy Dave David the one who always pissed himself and had to have a piss bag, remember? Duncan: Yeah, he always forgot to put it on Red Rock: What was the deal with treating me so bad? [Duncan sighs and pats Red Rock on the back.] Duncan: You're a good fighter Josh but an Idiot. You see the reason I did that is so I could keep you back here...I didn't want to tell you how much I thought of you because I knew you'd go for bigger things...I knew I couldn't keep it up, so I got you that chance to wrestle in the States. So now you're wrestling in AWC, I expect you're earning loads. [Red Rock pictures his shit dingy flat and his Job at the burger place.] Red Rock: Oh...oh yeah loads of money...don't know what to do with it all... Boolie: I thought we were really poor and we're on the verge of poverty? Red Rock: SHUT up Boolie! Duncan: So anyway after you went, people stopped coming and the money stopped coming in and I just broke down...so thanks to you Josh I've become some fat nobody. [Red Rock bites his bottom lip.] Red Rock: Yeah...sorry about that. But I've returned to my roots for a reason. I'm in for the match of my life! This week I fighting the main event of Battle Of Britain, and I'm up against the biggest and best in the federation, and I'm well don't tell anyone, but I'm scared Duncan! I've never had to fight a fight like this in my life. Of all my years and training and all the federations I've battled in I've never had a match like this. SIX men fighting in a tour bus and I need to be ready! I NEED to be the best I can be at this match. [The English national Anthem plays in the back ground as St Georges cross appears in the background.] Red Rock: I have to prove to the nation and I have to fly the flag for every aspiring young English wrestler by stepping on that bus and fighting for every bump and broken bone, for every lame humiliating gimmick and for every ounce of blood sweat and tears and every restless night! I need to go out there and kick ARSE! Duncan, Duncan “The Bullet” Williams I need your help, WILL YOU HELP ME? Duncan: HELL NO will I help you, you bastard! It's your fault I've been sleeping in here for the last 3 years and it's your fault I'm fat! I gave up the chance to be married and have kids for wrestling AND YOU RUINED IT FOR ME! I Hope they tear you apart on that bus! I'll be watching and laughing, LAUGHING as they beat the crap out of your skinny little body! [Red Rock stares at Duncan holding a bemused look on his face.] Red Rock: Oh...right...well...that was a bit of an anti climax wasn't it? Duncan: Go on! FUCK OFF! Back to your rich house and you rich car! You left me here to ROT and get fat! I WISH I WAS DEAD! I'm NOTHING NOW, NOTHING! People laugh at me and say “look at that fat loser” and I get spat at! People used to respect me and look up to me! NOW LOOK! [Duncan begins to cry and looks up.] Duncan: Hey where'd he go?! Just outside... Red Rock: What a fucking rasclart! Who needs his help anyway fucking fat bastard! Boolie: Yeah he stank of piss too and was really sweaty. Red Rock: Oh well, At least I can say that I've ruined someone's life! But what the hell am I going to do in this match...I can just tell they're all going to use me as their little bitch! Boolie: Ah you don't give your self enough credit Red, just go in there and do what you do best! Red Rock: Yeah, I'll just poke as many eyes and kick as many balls until no one can see! HAHAHA! Boolie: Lets get out of here it's getting pretty late now! Back at the Hotel [The scene comes to an end with a shot of British Bomber, laying between Red Rock and Boolie in the double bed, with his eye wide open and a petrified look on his face.]
