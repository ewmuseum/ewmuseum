The Game Don't Remember the Rich...

6 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/2006 The screen is black and the voice of what seems to be an old man is heard in the background. 'Vince, to be the man in this sport takes a hell of a lot more than beating the man. You gotta be on top of your game at all times. You gotta put in the work each and every day of your life. You gotta be willing to put it all on the line whenever and wherever no matter what the costs are, no matter how much pain you're feeling. Pain's only temporary. Failure is only temporary. All of that is meaningless. What don't kill ya makes ya stronger. Understood?' There is a break of silence. 'And whatever you do, don't believe the hype. Don't make the same mistake I made, Vince. When its all said and done your accomplishments will always be measured on how high you rose and how far you went. Your name is only as good as the career you've led. Always remember that, Vince...' The camera slowly fades into the back of a limo where Vince Jones, Reno Banks and the lovely Jasmine are seen sitting together enjoying each others company. Well, to be honest, Reno Banks and Jasmine appear to be the exuberant ones as they sip glasses of sparkling champagne. Vince Jones on the other hand after snapping back into reality, sits there in silence with an unfriendly aura surrounding him. Reno Banks: Mr. Jones, I must say your performance at Fresh a week or so ago was phenomenal. Vince Jones: That was two weeks ago. Reno Banks: (nods) Yes, yes. Two weeks ago to be exact. Anyways you appear to be clawing your way back up the mountain if I do say so myself. People were starting to doubt you, but I had faith. I know you'd come through. Vince Jones: (shakes his head) Yeah, whateva... Reon Banks: (grining) Its just great to see AWC's Phenom, The Man, The Myth, The Legend, on a steady rise back on up to the top. Next thing you know everyone will be in the arenas sporting 'Prepare to Be Silence' t-shirts or even 'Enter the Violence' ones at that. Oh, and that reminds me, we definitely need to see if we can expand your horizons and marketability with t.v appearances and some more endorsement deals. I'm telling you, Mr. Jones. We're blowing the lid off of AWC SKYHIGH, as we speak. Reno begins smiling from ear to ear as he envisions all the potential money he can make off of Vince's name. Reno Banks takes a sip of his champagne as does Jasmine. Jasmine and Reno finally take notice of Vince's demeanor. Jasmine: What's wrong, V? Reno Banks: Yeah, Mr. Jones. That's no way for a man like you to be acting. I mean, you're on your way. You are truly on your way. Vince Jones slowly turns towards Jasmine and then looks at Reno Banks. Vince Jones: What the hell you talkin' bout ,Banks? V. Jones is yet to do shit in A Dubb C. V sittin' in neutral no matta how the hell you slice that shit! Reno looks at Vince in shock of his statements. Reno Banks: What?? After all the work I've done for you behind the scenes you're gonna lash out at the situation like that? So, you haven't managed to snag the gold yet you'll make it. These things take time. You're 'The Violence' Vince Jones for cryin' outloud. Vince throws up his hand ready to slap Reno across the face. Vince Jones: Shut that shit up, man! You been feedin' V all that crap from day one! That shit don't cut it! V's fuckin' name don't mean shit if there ain't shit backin' it, mahfucka! That's the way it is! Reno Banks: What about the money? So, you've taken a few losses here and there. That's no big deal. You're living a good life. I've made sure of that. Vince hears this, just shakes his head, and turns away from them both. Vince Jones: You just don't get it! You're never gonna get it! Fuck it! This discussion's ova! Figga out how you plannin' on rackin' in V even more money, man since that's what you think all this shit's about! Since that's what you think the whole extent of what yo damn client be steppin' in that damn ring for! Fuck it! Just go ahead and make that shit happen! Reno Banks: (smiling) Glad you'd see it my way, sir. Reno Banks and Jasmine go back to chattering on about business ventures as Vince sits in silent thought. 'These cats just don't get it! No one will eva get it! It ain't about the fuckin' money! There's a whole helluva lot more to this damn puzzle! Man , V wishes you were still around sometimes. Maybe shit would be a whole lot betta. Guess V gonna be up in that ring again this week fightin' it up for that fuckin' main event paper! Yeah! Fuck the money! The game don't remember the rich!' Camera slowly fades to black.
