A deeper grave...

22 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/05/05 “When I first started wrestling, I was skinny, pale, lazy and damn, I had nothing going for me. I was a dumb 15 year old with a dream. Now look at me, I’m more muscular, more tanned, more energetic, and god, I must admit, I do look pretty fine!” Our scene opens up inside of a chat room, where Samuel Livingston is talking to a bunch of his fans. It wasn’t scheduled, but he saw there was a Samuel Livingston chat room, and entered, to try and give them a please. SamuelLivingston22 has entered the room. Samfan: Now he’s facing Alex Strider. SamuelLivingston22: Hey everyone! Samliston: omgomg! I no him! He’s sam livson! SamuelLivingston22: Yeah, I am. Samfan: Whatever man. ALEXSMUSTDIE: NO! HE IS YOU KNOW!!!!11 Samfan: Really? Samliston: omgomg! Sam is it reely u? SamuelLivingston22: Yup, me in ALL of my greatness. Samfan: Yup, it’s him. ALEXSMUSTDIE: LMAO! DAT WAS SOOO FUNNY! Samfan: So Sam, are you nervous about finally facing Alex Strider? SamuelLivingston22: Course I am, the little prick is gonna be HURT. Although he still isn’t my main priority. Dark Angel is. Moderator: Please, we do not tolerate any swearing. SamuelLivingston22: Aye Aye, Cap’n. ALEXSMUSTDIE: WHO IS DARK ANGEL? Samfan: Dark Angel is one of Sam’s biggest enemies. She doesn’t know it, but I know Sam does, ;-) SamuelLivingston22: It’s hard fighting her, she has such a gorgeous ass. Samfan: Whoooo! Go Samuel! SamuelLivingston22: :) ALEXSMUSTDIE: SO ARE YOU CONFDENT? ALEXSMUSTDIE: CONFYDENT* SamuelLivingston22: Anyway, I must go. I bid you adieu, but I have a lot of training to catch up on. I’m a little fatigued right now. SamuelLivingston22 has left the room. “Ugh, keeping up with my fans is harder work than I thought.” Samuel said, followed by a sigh, as we finally see the scene. Samuel is seated in a leather sofa, working on his laptop. (Working? Browsing) and watching former scenes of Alex Strider wrestling, mainly against Pierre, but who cares about him? Samuel doesn’t want to get into an in-depth discussion about a kid nobody cares about. Samuel has beads of sweat dripping down from his forehead. “I’m just digging myself an even bigger grave, acting all cocky. Damn, what am I doing?” says Samuel, as the scene closes.
