Vincent Webb part 2

27 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27/05 Steven Xandrous I step inside, and immediately the clerk of the building looks at me. I return an angered and mysterious glare. The clerk, a woman, turns her gaze away. I smile to myself. I then walk around to the pistol section of the building and walk towards the clerk, a scrappy-looking male. “Sir,” I say, trying to act polite. “I need your most powerful and hopefully the most concealable pistol. You got something for me?” The man smiles. “We got something for everyone.” The man walks over to the shelf to his right, grasping a blackened pistol. He holds it on it’s side using both palms. “A mark nineteen Desert Eagle. A gamer’s favorite. It will cost you twelve hundred and seventy-two bucks including ammo. You are in luck because today it is a tax-free day. Saved you plenty of money.” “Alright, alright,” I say hurriedly. I pull out thirteen hundreds and hand them to the man. He cashes the money calmly, but I can tell he is rather surprised. He hands me back my change and he hands me a case and a box off ammunition. I run off quickly, leaving the man a quite angry. Oh well… I check my watch. Ten minutes left until I am to be there. I stop and walk into a nearby alley. I open up the gun case and load the gun to its fullest. I stuff the gun in my pocket, covering it with my jacket. I begin my march to this “uncle” of mine’s dwelling. After numerous minutes of walking, I reach his residence. I step to the door and open it. Immediately there is a man on me, asking what I am doing here. “Look, big boy,” I say sarcastically, “You let me in or I will beat your fat ass out onto the street. Got that?” The man smiles and laughs. “Go on ahead. Webb said you had a nasty ass attitude.” I stare at him and walk on. How does this guy know every thing about me? Well the little bastard is going to get his… I continue down the hall and then yet another man directs me down a set of stairs. Shifty, I put my hand in my pocket and rest it on the item that the pocket beholds. Walking through the ugly cement hallway, I realize something is not right. If this guy is as rich and powerful as he claims to be, why isn’t this hallway all… clean and such? I just walked right into a pile of shit and I do not have the shoes for it. I step through the space where a door should be and find myself in a dark room except for lights shining on one spot. Sitting in a nice chair is a slightly old man, in his fifties or sixties, his age showing from weakness. His soul, however, is still young and fighting. I can see it through his eyes and I can damn well sense it. This man is out for something, and I bet that I am either the gateway… or the scapegoat. The man I see has on a nice, three piece suit, slightly nicer than the ones worn by his bodyguards. His hair, although slightly grayed at places, is gelled neatly into place. He smiles at my sight. “Steven,” he says, “I’ve been waiting for you. Please, take a seat. There are some things that I need to discuss with you.” So, I sit. A chair, sitting a few feet in front of him, is neatly positioned straight towards him. His smile holds its place. “Steven, I am Vincent Webb, your… uncle.” I simply nod grimly. He then makes a gesture, offering for me to speak. “Look, just tell me what you want and why you need me for it.” The man laughs deeply. “What I want? Son, I want the world, but I do not need it, therefore I do not fully desire it. However, you I do desire. I need you. You are the only Xandrous left to carry on the legacy. You are not going to be able to continue that legacy if all you do is wrestle. You need to do something with your life. And if you can’t do anything with your life, end someone’s who has,” he says, laughing as though it were a humorous quote. I glare at this obviously sadistic man. “Look, I’m offering you your own world. I am offering you your life back, Steven. Do not turn this offer down.” I look away, unable to look this man in the eyes. The man sighs and leans forward. “What if I tell you that I know who nearly killed you?” I instantly look up at the comment. If this man knows who took my life away, maybe I can take theirs… No, I cannot do that. I look up at the man, and I feel the gun in my pocket. I slowly pull my hand from it. “And,” the man continues, “What if I told you that I have these men in a nearby room?” “If you have these men,” I say in question, “And you know they’ve tried to kill me, why haven’t you done a fucking thing about it, huh? Why aren’t they dead yet, ‘unc’?” Vincent simply laughs. “Because, my son, I thought that you would like to do the honors.” My half-smile disappears at the thought of murdering someone who need not be. This man is a fucking lunatic… so the hand goes back in my pocket. “Just kill them and get it over with. I don’t care, as long as they are dead and cannot do shit to me anymore,” I say, trying to lure myself away from the situation. “Oh, come on, Steven. It is not that hard…” Vincent says. Instantly I pull my gun out and its barrel is parallel with his forehead. Vincent’s expression does not waiver. “Put the gun down, Steven…” I find myself breathing awful heavy. The gun in my hand begins to feel more weighted as I hold it longer. “You know, the cop was right. You and the rest of my family are just cold-hearted murders with no goddamn sympathy for anything if this world,” I say, finding myself screaming the words. “I could put a fucking bullet through your skull right now, you know. But I will not because I have something called compassion. Something that you will probably never give and you will never receive you sick bastard. You would probably put a bullet in me, no hesitation, right? You probably make an evening of it or something.” From behind me, I feel four arms constraining my own arms behind his back, along with them my gun… and my ticket to freedom. Shit… I look over my shoulder to see who it is that is constraining me, and, as assumed, I see two of Vincent’s guards. How many of these bitches does he have? I suddenly see an elbow coming right at my eyes, but I cannot move in time and the elbow connects with my nose. “SHIT!” escapes my mouth in pain. I hear Vincent laugh and I turn in his direction. “Steven, I can’t believe you tried to do that. Didn’t you think ahead? How would you have gotten out if you pointed a gun at my head, huh? I got so many men here that you would not stand a chance. Honestly, Steven, what was your plan?” Vincent says, smiling as though he has won. You do not win until I say so, bitch… Vincent stands up and walks towards me. When he begins to reach me, I swell up saliva in my mouth. Vincent becomes toe to toe with me when he says, “Steven, I lied. I am not your uncle. Actually, it is quiet the contrary. I am about to kill you uncle in truth. You remember when I said that I have your attempted murderers in a nearby room. I lied. That is your uncle. You see, the plan was to make you kill your own uncle so if I am ever caught, they cannot charge me for the murder. You would be left clueless. However, obviously I have to change those plans around. I will just kill you both. It will go slowly, of course, so I can have the pleasure to watch your defiant little ass squirm away during your last few seconds of life.” He grins that sadistic grin again. So I spit in his face. The man turns away, wiping the spit from his eye. A millisecond after, I am thrusted to the ground, my hands welded to my back due to a heavy knee. I curse under my breath. Vincent stands next to me, I can tell by the shoes. I then feel a hard thrust kick hitting me in the ribs. I let a breath of air escape my lungs, knowing I had something like that coming. “Pull him up.” His men do so, pulling my arms in a painful way, aching my shoulders. Vincent, his look now much more serious then playful, stares right at me, his eyes full of hatred and remorse towards me and my true uncle. I then feel the pistol still in my hand. I had not felt it on the ground due to the heavy man numbing my hand with his weight. “You know, Steven,” he says. I tighten my grip on the gun. “I actually sort of liked you, with your attitude and whatnot,” he says as I exhale heavily and pray to god that the barrel of the gun is pointed in the right angle, “but now, you do nothing but disgust me. Go put this rat in its cage, alright?” “Wait,” I say, begging for a chance. “Can I say something?” Vincent nods. “Well, after my experience and what I’ve learned… I just want to say…” Vincent looks deeper in my eyes, “I hope you fucking burn in hell.” I pull the trigger and the gun fires. I feel a horrible stinging sensation in my wrist as the bullet ricochets off the concrete, catching one man’s chest area. The man falls back in pain, relinquishing his hold on me. I turn out and under my arm, twisting the other man’s arm, which still had a hold of my other wrist. I sling the man in front of me and into Vincent, knocking him to the ground. I pull the gun to the man’s throat and I see my hand trickling with red blood. I quickly pull the trigger, putting the man to death instantly. I step over Vincent, gun pointed at his head. I attempt to pull the trigger but I feel an immense pain in my groin. The son of a bitch kicked me! I keel over and I feel a hard knuckle hit me right above the eye, sending me toppling over. Vincent gets up and quickly runs past me and out of the room. The lights separate, revealing this room to me. Numerous walls, ropes, guns, barbwire fields… it is like an army-training course! I hear the slam of a door and I run towards the hallway. As I do, a large metal plate closes the space where a door should be. I sigh, and turn, seeing no way out. Then I begin to hear a hissing. I look up and I see little sprouts of rushing air into the room. This place is a goddamned gas chamber! It is like a fucking movie! This guy is rich… Then at the other end of the obstacle course, I see a door, conveniently marked exit. Unbelievable… I sigh, and begin running towards the first obstacle, a simple wall and rope. I grab hold of the rope and begin to pull myself to the top, my muscles already aching from the day. I pull myself over, and see that I have much, much more ahead of me. Vincent Webb I brush the dirt off my shoulder as I walk away from the scene. I lock the doors, forcing the rat into the snake pit. I grin at the man’s demise. I walk over to an employee-only area that holds a complete view of the course area. I smile. The plan had somehow worked itself out. Not the way I planned, but it worked out. Obviously, it did not go on as I planned. Who plans to be staring down the wrong end of a pistol? Oh well, that is all behind me. Steven is behind me. No more worries about a possible uprising. The Xandrous pedigree has crumbled and there are no possibilities of any chance of reformation. The old man is dead, the uncle is in the palm of my hand, and the son is getting finished off right in front of my very eyes. What a pleasant day… After a moment of watching the still battlegrounds of my enemies defeat, I notice a flicker of motion. Suddenly, in one swift moment, all my hopes are shattered. Standing near the end of the course is Steven Xandrous, still running smoothly as though the gas does not affect him in any way, and the course is certainly not slowing him down. Shit! I grab my radio instantly and contact Hewitt. Steven Xandrous As I pull myself over the final hurdle of the track, I release a breath of triumph. I look up at the glass control room and smile. There is mister Webb, staring at me, completely horrified. Like his shit could keep me down. As our eyes meet, I simply extend my hand, and with it my middle finger. He grimaces at me as I pull out my Desert Eagle in one swift movement. I fire the bullet and it rockets upwards and shatters a piece of the glass. “See you later, motherfucker!” I yell, enjoying my victory. I turn towards the door and fire three bullets at the handle, forcing it extremely loose. I pull it off and shove the door open. I shut the door behind me as I walk away. As I turn the corner to leave the building, two men, both with pistols pointing directly at my temple, stop me. I curse under my breath. “You honestly thought that you could get away, you son-of-a-bitch? We are talking about Vincent Webb. He is not going to let this stand,” he says, as though he is pointing out the obvious. “But we hate that fucker. So come with us.” I look at him, confused. They lower their pistols, turn, and run. I follow. Hey, if they were going to kill me, they would not turn their backs to me. Vincent Webb “SON OF A BITCH!” I yell at the top of my lungs. “How did that FUCKER get out of here? Huh!? What the Hell happened?! Did you just LET him walk right out of my building?! You better hope that they FUCKING come back with his corpse, otherwise it is YOUR ASS, HEWITT! YOUR ASS IS ON THE LINE!” Hewitt stands there obediently, not saying a word. “ARE YOU LISTENING TO ME, DAMN IT?!” “Yes sir! I’ll personally find him and bring him back to you,” he says flatly. “Damn right you will. And if you don’t, you better find out where he is. Otherwise, it’ll be YOUR corpse. Got it?” I say rapidly. “Yes sir. Consider it done, sir,” Hewitt says. Hewitt turns and jogs out as though he is working for the militia. My militia messed up. “Son of a bitch…” Sorry if this is late, but I lost internet and I just got it back... please accept it. :-)
