i've got a syndrome and i don't know what

11 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: later “It’s time to go see Eddy,” she’s telling me. Her body language, in all its manifestation of physical glory, tells me that she is sick of waiting for me to get up. So I pull my pillow over my head and roll over. “Come on,” she says. “We aren’t doing this now.” Through a muffled pillow, I give my retort. A lesser person wouldn’t be able to hear what I have to say; she doesn’t even need to hear to know. “That’s ridiculous,” she replies. “We need to go, now. I’m tired of seeing this plain room and I’m notgoing to let you spend another day shackled up in this hotel. We’re leaving, now.” I pull the pillow off and turn my head, repeating my statement only now it was unmuffled. “I said, LEAVE ME ALONE.” Like a ghost, I return to the abyss under my covers. “And I already told you that is a ridiculous statement. Now get dressed. You have to look pretty when you’re going out in public to dance for the pretty people.” She still stands over my bed, waiting for me. Hovering. And if there’s an awkward position that I hate people being in, it’s when they hover. “Can’t you just disappear for a few minutes?” I say in to the bed. I obviously want sleep, but that probably wasn’t the best way of saying it. Women, corporeal or not, are always reading deep in to everything you say. Or they’re giving you that bullshit, “its not what you say, its how you say it.” So I take the pillow back off my head and move upward, committing the most movement I have in the past three days. Surprisingly, I don’t have bed sores. “I can’t believe you’d actually want that,” she says. The look on her face has moved from annoyed to that of being hurt with her arms still crossing. “You know what I meant, Shaunna. You, of all people, know exactly what I mean, so don’t take it as some sub-conscious dig.” I try to rationalize to her, appeal to what she knows is true. “Really? So you aren’t hiding things from me, now?” My mouth curls up in one corner, giving that ‘oh shit, didn’t think she’d catch that one’ expression. “You are so stupid if you didn’t think I’d notice that things were different. But I don’t get it, you don’t trust me anymore?” There she goes, like most chicks: citing the trust issue. It’s like she’s offended that I can’t have privacy—like sharing every aspect of my life is something I enjoy. Sorry, baby, but I like keeping secrets. “I trust you; why else would I keep you around!” I try and joke with her, but she doesn’t find it funny. Again, it’s not what you say… “You make things so hard for me, girl.” My frustration said that, I’d gotten the best of myself. So shaking my head and squeezing my fists, my brain works overtime to try and figure out the quickest way of solving this issue. It’s what I like to call, Repair-Mode. She starts saying “This relationship is difficult as it is, but I made it work.” my brain still scans for ways out of this She continues with “Do you actually think that I enjoy this situation whole-heartedly? Or that I wanted to be placed here, with you?” hearing everything said my brain registers but doesn’t acknowledge as it still tries to find a way to solve this And then she puts the nail in the coffin by saying “You killed me. I never asked to be with you.” so then realization strikes that there is no way out of this; man your battle stations because we are going to war “I KILLED YOU?!” I say. And by say, I mean scream. “I KILLED YOU?!? HAH!” I throw the pillow, for dramatic effect, and expect a loud crash. Instead it lands softly on the floor, by Shaunna’s feet. “Do you think I was wishing that you’d cross the street? Tell me, Shaunna; Do you think that I was praying to God that you’d j-walk at the same time that I drove past you?” “Well at least you’re finally talking about it! My God, ____, you are SUCH a coward that you won’t talk about your hang-nails. It’s been, what, six months since you’ve killed me and this is the first time we’ve talked about it. How long did it take for you to accept this situation, after you actually realized who I was?” I’ve always been crazy. It doesn’t take a guy a long time to realize he’s just got to go with the flow to survive. “I don’t like to talk about it,” I begin to justify, “because to I don’t like the sound, of saying I’M DATING A FUCKING GHOST! Do you know how crazy that sounds? Do you know how crazy I sound, do you? You ever sit here and think about the shit that YOU put me through? Going out in public is like walking on egg-shells ever since I met you—no, ever since I inherited you.” Every word, the truth strikes more and more pain. “You can’t dictate my life anymore, Shaunna Prather. You lost that right when you lost your last breath. You lost that right when you MADE A STUPID MISTAKE and lost your life for it. You think Dead-Girls get rights, honey? You’re in my fucking brain, you’re not affecting anyone but yourself and yours’ truly. And quite frankly, I’m tired of being your pawn.” “So whatever scheme you have planned, fucking forget it. Eddy Estrada, the Mutineer, that lame book and this stupid town; forget it all. Fuck this city and fuck everything else in it.” During my speech, Shaunna’s body language went from being hurt, back to being annoyed. Apparently she didn’t like my coming-around. “Are you finished?” she says. “Because everything you said doesn’t really mean a thing. You may hate me and the predicament we’re in, but at the end of the day you’re still going to be who you are. You have a purpose, and we aren’t through until you have fulfilled your duty.” “That whole rant you went on was pointless, regardless of how you feel about this situation. We have a schedule to adhere to, and you’re making things difficult. If you really want to figure out what that book means and what relation it has to you, Eddy Estrada, and those two psychos that find it so important… you’ll listen to me.” My relationship to Shaunna Prather is not similar to anything at all. I guess anyone would find being haunted by a teenage girl that you’ve accidentally killed to be unique, but the horror movies never turn out like this. “You’ve got to keep the book with you at all times, but don’t let anyone know you have it. Not Eddy Estrada, and especially not Josiah and the Mutineer. Both of their agendas are shady and we must discover the truth before we decide to act.” Everything she’s said in the past five minutes, I have no idea what she’s talking about. That book? Why is it so fucking important? I read the first five pages and found it all to be mindless babble. How can anyone understand that wordage?! It’s like a fucking puzzle for English nerds, man. So now that everything revolves around this stupid journal, its sort of hard to maintain my interests. “The truth about what?” I say. “This whole thing is soooo vague and for some reason makes me fear for my life. If I have no idea what I’m getting in to, why get involved?” “Because it is for your own sake as well, ____.” She tells me. “But before you will learn of your involvement, you have to remember the things that you tend to forget. Like last week, with Eddy Estrada.” I’m confused. I tend to block things out on purpose, because they’re too hard for me to deal with. It’s why I didn’t deal with killing Shaunna Prather for so long (and consequently, probably why I think I’m being haunted by her right now), it’s also why I haven’t really come to terms with the death of my mother. And it’s why I left everyone behind. If I can’t remember what happened, it’s probably for a reason. So… why would I want to remember it? “I cannot simply unlock your memories for you. You have to learn on your own. This defense mechanism you’ve created is unique, and must be dealt with as such.” I know I’m fucking crazy. There’s no way people get “haunted” by dead chicks on a daily basis. I’m crazy, and it’s only a matter of time before they lock me away. “I am crazy, aren’t I?” I ask her. For the first time, since we first met, she actually touches me. The intangible chick actually TOUCHES me, in a kiss on the cheek. “You think you’re the only person to be ‘haunted’ as you put it? Jesus, honey, what do you think I have you talking to Eddy Estrada for?”
