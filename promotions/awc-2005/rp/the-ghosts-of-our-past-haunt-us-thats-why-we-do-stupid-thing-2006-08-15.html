The ghosts of our past haunt us, thats why we do stupid things.

15 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th August 2006 What are ghosts? Most people use the word ghost to describe the spirit of a dead person who once walked with us on this earthly plane. But this does beg the question; is there any difference between a ghost and a spirit? Well yes there is, people use the word ghost to represent the visual image of a person who has died and the more general word apparition to mean all types of phantoms human and non-human. A ghost is a figure of fear; it is generally associated with huge historic buildings. The most scariest and malevolent type of ghost however is not the ghost wilding chains or carrying its head under one arm, it is not a dark phantom come back to deliver a message to the living, no. The scariest ghosts of all are the ones we live with every day of our lives, every waking minute and some times in our dreams. These are the ghosts of our past. Mr Ian Camfield, Red Rock’s tyrant landlord is a bitter and resentful man. He is the not a respectful proprietor but no less than a sleazy land lord who spends most of his incredible amount of free time turning the pages of his vast collection of pornographic material, or eating a tub of greasy Kentucky Fried Chicken, which is supposed to be aimed at families of four… not lonely 47 year old men. People, Red Rock included live in fear of this man and it is often discussed among visitors to his building as to why people fear him. He is a shade over 5 foot and hasn’t a days worth of exercise to his name, yet still when his tenants get a knock on the door it sends shivers down their spines. He is a cruel and unforgiving man with little in his live but the power over others. The only joy he gets is the sport of pumping fear through people’s souls. But people aren’t born like this, there is no way a man could willing allow an old man to die in the cold for the sake of a few quid or let a single mum go out on the streets with no where to go because she wouldn’t offer “alternative methods of payment” for a missed payment. There is a dark sordid past to this man, there is a lot more to him than anger and a heart of stone. But again we must ask what drives someone to this? Well Ian Camfield was born in to a home with a father who fought a world war and had been raised with relentless discipline. To fail brought immense shame to the Camfield family and perhaps the anger toward Ian was his father Arthur’s way of releasing the ghosts of his own past. But that would be too cliché this was not the case, his father Arthur was a bully the battle fields of WW2 were his play ground and when he got home his wife Jean and his Son Ian, they were his way of prolonging the play ground violence that he craved. Obviously in those times there were no options for a woman in her situation so Ian had to watch every day as his obviously mentally ill father would break bones for ludicrous things like looking at him the wrong way. Ian was showed little mercy either he wasn’t the son Arthur wanted he was smaller than the other boys at school and always under performed at sports, in lessons at everything. He didn’t take the time to realise that it was clearly the stress instigated by him that was causing this failure. Ian was sucked in to a vicious loop of despair with no prospect of escape. It didn’t end at home and it never does for people like Ian, he would still have to go to school and at school bullies run the show and Ian was the favourite play thing for the bullies. He was a typical victim, he was quiet small no self respect, didn’t answer back and there was certainly no lick of a chance of him fighting back. Bullying is the ghost of our past that can have the most adverse affect and linger the longest. It is one of the hardest to understand but the most common to have inflicted up on you. This is maybe where Red Rock and Ian would find common ground maybe to a smaller less emotionally devastating scale but Red Rock’s ghosts of his past still hold him back to this day. He found it hard to be himself at school… lets not beat around the bush Red Rock is a little but fruity not the typical image of what a man should be. He’d rather drink a cup of tea than a beer and he has no interest in football. He sits with his legs closed together, not as far apart as they’ll go like a typical male. He can’t grow real facial hair which makes him look more like a 13 year old boy than a burley red blooded male. But at the same time, in spite finding it hard to be him self he was still true, which in it’s self is an achievement with the “harsh but fair” play ground justice system. This left him subject to be branded with that playground adjective “gay”. Everything about Red Rock was gay, gay hair, gay bag, and gay shoes. Unlike Ian though Red Rock was still a fighting spirit in his youth would make movement one day by rising up against the bullies who plagued him. There are two types of people in this world, cowards and heroes and although Red Rock hides from awkward situations and does other things deemed as cowardly he has no idea what a hero he really is. You see you don’t have to pull people from burning wrecks or give hundreds to charity to be a hero, to be a hero all you have to do is fight on through these intoxicating hard times take it on the chin with a pinch of salt and move along and move along is what Red Rock does best. Inside the metal skeleton of a rusted up ford Transit Red Rock stares forward his hands on his lap looking around in disgust at the blue furry mould congregating on the remains of a BLT sandwich. He rolls his eyes to his right over to Ian (Mr Camfield) who has his hands firmly on the steering wheel, focusing his eyes coldly forward with a face of rage he typically expresses on a regular basis. Only one of the two men know what is in store for them at this point in time and because Red Rock is the one sweating the most it is clear that Ian is the wiser. The awkward silence has lasted beyond the point where is bearable but knowing full well Red Rock and Ian have never seen eye to eye since Red Rock first moved in over a year ago. Red Rock: So…Mr Camfield… Ian cranks his eyes to his left, almost to indicate that he is offended by Red Rock mentioning his name as Red Rock raps his fingers nervously against his thighs with a timid quiver in his tone. Red Rock: This is pretty weird… me and you being illegal jobs for a criminal psychopath who would have thought? I bet the BBC will pay good money for a sit com about this! Ian: I suppose if this is going to work I’m going to have to tell you what’s going on. Red Rock: O….k… Ian: You might want to cross your legs I don’t want to pissing all over my seats! Red Rock: Smells to me like someone already pissed all over yo – Ian barks at Red Rock, thus putting an end his smart arse remark. Ian: Dev Jackson needs us to do a hit on the Station Jewellers by Alton station. Red Rock’s body jerks with disbelief to the point where he bangs the back of his head against the glass. His cheeky face has been wiped clean and hastily replaced with an expression that spells out clearly what he is feeling and that is an overwhelming sensation of fear. Fear is the bully’s main weapon in their arsenal of physiological and physical bombshells. It is a different kind of fear to what you’d feel when faced with danger, it’s the type of fear that takes a hold of you and enables the bully to gain control of you. On this occasion Dev Jackson uses both in conjunction with one another to control two men in to committing his bidding. As we have established previously Dev Jackson uses Red Rock’s building as a secret warehouse of his dirty merchandise and the tenants inside he uses as his personal baby sitters. Mr Camfield has been a victim of Dev Jackson for over 10 years, in fact Mr Camfield was bullied in to signing over the deeds of his building to Dev Jackson because he thought that it would relief some of the strain Dev Jackson put upon him, but he had no idea that it opened him up for many more years of hell. Red Rock: Fuck that! Pull the van over! Ian: Don’t be stupid it won’t take long. Red Rock: Don’t be a dick head I don’t care what happens I’m not robbing a place for that dick head! Ian: I don’t care what you think you little SHIT you’re going to do this; I’m not having Jackson on my back because of you! Red Rock’s face exclaims the fact that Ian’s logic isn’t computing with him as his jaw hangs open slightly and his eyebrows prick up. Judging by Ian’s forceful expression and the way he has taken his eyes off the road to emphasise his point makes it crystal clear that Ian is not joking about this. Red Rock: You’re seriously going to do this because Dev Jackson told you to? What kind of pussy shitty arse fucker are you? Ian: Shut your stupid mouth! If I’m such a pussy then why are you here, why didn’t you say no if it’s so easy? Red Rock: You might not of noticed Dev Jackson pointing a gun to my head before you arrived… what’s your excuse? Ian allows Red Rock’s words to linger for a few silent moments whilst gazing at the M25 motor way heading back towards Alton. Ian fully understands what Red Rock is getting at and it hurts him as his ghosts talk to him telling him he should stand up for himself but he didn’t listen to them before when his father would thrash him and he would just repress them now like he always did and at this moment the brief silence is broken by another aggressive at burst from Ian. Ian: FUCK OFF! I don’t have to answer to a stupid kid like you! Red Rock: Alright, don’t get a boner! I’m still not doing this though I don’t care if Dev Jackson comes after me! Ian: You don’t understand. Red Rock: You’re scared of Dev Jackson? You’re the angriest hard nosed bastard I know! Ian: You REALLY don’t understand, you’ve only just met him and he’s already making your life hell… I’ve been his bitch for 6 years… he owns me. Red Rock: Sounds like you two should break up! Ian: THIS ISN’T A JOKE! Red Rock: I know a good divorce lawyer; seriously it’s not good to be in an abusive relationship. The lines of age on Ian’s forehead become more visible as Red Rock’s irksome comments persist. It is not long before we experience another of Ian’s explosive anger in the form of two fists being forced down on the steering wheel with tremendous aggression like a pair of blacksmith’s hammers. The impact on the wheel suddenly causes the car to veer sharply in to the middle lane of the motor way in front of an over taking car, narrowing evading a high speed collision. At this point Red Rock is pressing his feet down on the invisible break pedal on his side whilst clutching on to the dash board for his life. Ian manages to retain control of the vehicle but Red Rock’s face is stretched and strained with anguish as his heart beats at a much more rapid rate. Red Rock: Bloody hell Mr Camfield! I was only joking, no need to murder us both you shitting bastard! Ian: DON’T call me gay. I’m not married to him and I’m not gay! Red Rock: Alright, ok calm down I was just pissing about… Christ… Another painful silence falls upon the two men, allowing them both to dabble with their thoughts. Ian now has both hands firmly on the steering wheel and he is again focusing his gaze forward, making a point to ignore Red Rock’s presence. Red Rock rests his head on his hand, choosing this position as the most comfortable for him to survey the scenery as it speeds past. The one thing on his mind at this moment in time is whether Ian is serious about this robbery. He never had the chance to really get to know his land lord; all he saw from him was his brutal tyranny and callous anger. But a different side of Ian was shedding through his hard angry exterior to expose his cowardly insecure and fearful interior. With these thoughts in mind Red Rock quickly comes to the conclusion that Ian lives with such fear of Dev Jackson that there is no doubt that Ian is going to go through with this plan and there is little he will be able to do to talk him out of it. So a new plan is needed in order to reach normality once again. 35 minutes later. The scenery has changed from the tedious view of the M25 motor way and has been replaced by the tall aging buildings of Alton town centre. The streets are dry of the sound of the human hustle and bustle as the skies, in recent hours had open up thus inflicting a powerful rain storm. The awkward silenced invoked upon the pair has not yet been lifted since Ian’s episode. As the tattered white transit crawls to the brow of the hill a large multi-storey car park grows in to view, upon reaching the top of the hill Ian is the first to put an end to the silence. He has a stern and contemplative tone emitted in his voice, sustaining his cold bent on stare. Ian: That is where we’re going to park, the jewellers is right opposite. There are two exits for it; we are going in the one facing us and escaping out the rear exit. Red Rock: Mr Camfield, you do realise what you’re saying? Ian ignores Red Rock’s attempts to offer a voice of reason, as far as he is concerned he can not fail as the images of the previous savagery at the hands of Dev Jackson manifests in his mind’s eye. So with nothing but the sound of harsh rain beating off the metal roof they drive on towards the car park entrance. Ideas and thoughts now accumulate in Red Rock’s head as to how he can weasel his way out of this situation, he considers faking an epileptic fit, but swiftly remember he tried that one last time he missed a rent payment… in fact every weasely excuse in his arsenal he has used to escape the wrath of Ian Camfield, so he realises he requires something fresh. But as the sound of the rain desists he realises they have entered the car park for the next phase of the hit and time is running out. Ian manoeuvres the van adjacent to the rear exit, which leads straight towards the M3 motor way where they will make their hasty exit. Ian: You wait here I got our masks and stuff in the back, just keep your eyes peeled for any witnesses, ALRIGHT? Red Rock: Yeah alright…*cough* cock *cough* Ian hops out of the van cautiously, trying not to draw any attention to him self, whilst Red Rock suddenly judders as an idea to avoid this leaps around him like a hyperactive child. He wastes no more time before franticly trying to find his mobile phone, he pats his pockets up and down desperately searching before finding it in the glove compartment. Red Rock: YES! Red Rock swiftly searches through the phone book on his relic Nokia 3310 to find D’avid’s number and with the hope in his mind that D’avid is no longer whacked out on cocaine he pressed dial. RING-RING, RING-RING, RING-RING, RING - D’avid: Hello to you! Red Rock: D’AVID! You have to ring the station Jewellers in Alton, that’s the Station Jewellers in Alton and warn them they’re about to ge – D’avid: You are through to my fucking answer phone! If you want to leave a message leave it after the bleep OooooOooh Heh! BEEP Red Rock: D’AVID YOU CUNT! That answering machine message is not clever! Ah fuck it never mind! You have to ring the station Jewellers in Alton, that is the Station Jewellers in Alton and warn them they’re about to get robbed! Red Rock pushes the red button with his face dropping towards frustration; he runs his hand down his face exclaiming his emotions presently, Ian then returns and in his hand is a pair of balaclavas. Red Rock, realising he has no more options left looks Ian in his blood shot eyes with an austere glint in his own eye. Red Rock: Look Ian, this is fucking stupid I don’t care what Dev Jackson does to me and I sure as fucking hell don’t give a shitty piss what he does to you. So Fuck this I’m going home! Red Rock turns his back on Ian and reaches for the door handle but from no where the vertically challenged Ian Camfield lunges for Red Rock from behind, wrapping his arm around Red Rock’s throat. Red Rock’s face becomes strained at Ian’s surprisingly strong grip and Ian puts his mouth up to Red Rock’s ear, a little too close for comfort. Ian: I don’t want you here as much as you don’t want to be here, but this is a two man job I need you to fill these bags whilst I make sure no one tries any funny business. Red Rock: Would you ACK! Let go of me! Ian: No you fucking bastard, you’re in this as well and if you fucking run away I will kill you! Ian reaches to his back pocket to withdraw a small hand gun before pressing is against Red Rock’s temple, a situation Red Rock has become familiar with recently but the feeling of the cold metal of a gun’s barrel doesn’t get any warmer. Red Rock suddenly begins to co-operate as his breathing rapidly sky rockets. Red Rock: O..o..ok ok Ian calm down… don’t kill me! Ian: Just put this on and let’s get on with this! Ian shoves the balaclava in to Red Rock’s hands, who follows up by sliding it over his head; Ian also chucks two brown post office sacks in to Red Rock’s arms before delivering further instructions. Ian: Right now we’re just going to burst in there; lots of yelling, make sure they’re all really scared! None of this “please and thank you” shit! OK. Red Rock by now is trembling with fear as he finds himself face to face with yet another gun toting maniac. Red Rock: O…ok Ian: Good, lets fucking go! Ian jumps out of the drivers side as Red Rock reluctantly follows, feeling a little sick might I add. Ian pushes Red Rock’s shoulder like an angry father would to their child who just been caught shop lifting. Ian takes the lead as they exit the car park with the cold rain thrashing down relentlessly on them. Red Rock’s expressions cannot be seen through the black mask but he is feeling all kinds of emotions as he watches the old man behind the counter cleaning his counter, unknowing to what is about to happen to him. We can see what looks like a tear being produced in Red Rock’s eye duct moments before performing this heinous act of illegality. Ian throws the door open with such force the glass panel on the bottom of the door instantly cracks all the way across and the man behind the counter jumps out of his skin. Ian: GET DOWN ON THE FUCKING FLOOR! NOW! The poor old shop owner drops down to his knees with his hands in the air, shaking like a leaf. Ian: GIVE ME THE KEY TO THE SAFE! Red Rock looks down at the frail defenceless old man and catches eye contact with his. Red Rock has never seen this kind of fear in anyone in his life; he can tell this man is expecting to die as Ian points the gun to him. But to Red Rock, and Ian’s surprise the old man answers with a shaky tone. Shop Owner: N-n-n..n-n…no. No you can’t have the key… The old man closes his eyes tight expecting a bullet through the skull but Ian drags the man to his feet by the collar of his shirt and drags him so they are face to face. Ian: DON’T BE STUPID! GIVE ME THE KEY TO THE SAFE! Shop Owner: I’m…I’m not scared of you, you can’t have the key! Ian thrusts the owner backwards against the wall and presses the barrel of the gun violently against the owner’s forehead. All Red Rock can do is look on in horror at Ian’s actions, but he has a strange feeling of admiration for the clearly outmatched old man. Despite facing possible death he is standing his ground. Red Rock then turns to Ian, whose finger is tickling the trigger. Red Rock then makes a brave move by approaching Ian. Red Rock: Ian, don’t be stupid, look at what you’re doing just because of Dev Jackson. You’re better than him! Ian: Of fuck off don’t patronise me! Red Rock: Seriously Ian, this isn’t going to stop Dev from bullying you. You’re just bringing down other people, innocent people down with you! Ian: SHUT UP! It’s alright for you; you have everything to live for… all I have is a stack of porn! Red Rock: Then be somebody, don’t be the coward everyone expects you to be! Just stop this crap please… it’s not worth it. Ian looks in to the eyes of the scared old man before slowly lowering the gun from his forehead, leaving a small indentation caused by the barrel being pressed against his head. Ian: Fucks sake… this is what my life has come to… Ian drops the gun to the floor whilst standing in silence, then suddenly the old man dives for an alarm under the counter and before everyone knows it the shop is filled by the sound of sirens reverberating around the room. Red Rock: Aww fuck! LEG IT! Red Rock and Ian scramble through the door, not realising they had left the gun behind. They make their expeditious return to the van, completely ignoring the traffic coming from both directions. With the sound of cars screeching and colliding behind them they both jump in the van with incredible urgency. Ian fumbles the key in to the ignition and the pair make their getaway. Red Rock: You know Ian; you did a brave thing there. It takes a lot to realise that you’re doing the wrong thing. Ian: That’s easy for you to say now, but remember mate it’s not just me that has to answer to Dev Jackson… Red Rock falls in to silence with the truth in Ian’s words burning his ear drums he has taken the moral high road, but at what expense to his own personal safety? We must wonder if Mr Charles Darling can sleep tonight knowing that he has been unable to share the bravery of Ian Camfield as it is because of him that Red Rock is here in the first place.
