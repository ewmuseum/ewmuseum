Dickey Rection-Face

29 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/29/05 As night bleeds into morning, the darkness does not fade away. Instead, the darkness controls its territory of the land. Hours into the morning, rain begins to fall. The rain begins to fall, harder, harder, and clattering against the concrete of the road and the roofs of houses. The clattering of the rain is overcome by roar of an engine. That is if you can classify puffing and puttering as roaring. The engine is cut to a halt. The pop of a door opening breaks through the noise of the rain, and, moments later, a knocking noise breaks through as well. The engine is that of a truck, a raggedy and “old school” kind of truck. The sides are scratched up and the paint is fading away. Along with those flaws, there is a large dent on the side of the hood, about two inches imprinted into the metal. As though that isn’t enough to make the truck one of the worst, two of the four hubcaps are missing. The knock that was heard comes from the fist of the automobiles operator, his name unknown. The home is that of none other than Kris Krimzon’s. The man waits a few moments before knocking again. Shortly after, the door swings open. Standing in the doorway is Tamara Krimzon, wearing workout pants and a sweatshirt, the area around her eyes darkened from lack of sleep. Her eyelids feel as though they weigh one hundred pounds, but she does all she can to keep them open. “How can I help you, sir?” Tamara asks politely, yet a scent of irritation in her voice. “Hello, I was looking for mister Krimzon? This is his cousin,” the man says. “Can I ask your name?” “My name,” the man says, sighing, “is Dickey Rection-Face.” Tamara holds in a burst of laughter, nods, and heads up the stairs. Dickey walks into the residence and talks off his shoes, setting them on the mat. He takes off his jacket which is dripping wet from the rain, folds it up nicely, and sets it next to his shoes. Dickey is wearing clothes that are quite contradictory to his truck; a nice blue dress shirt and black dress pants. Dickey stands by the door patiently, until finally Kris comes running down the steps. Kris leaps up and into the arms of Dickey in joy. “DICKEY! Where have you been, man? I haven’t seen you in so long! The last time I saw you, you were at one of those… jubs or whatever,” Kris says smiling. “Err,” Dickey responds, “It’s called a job.” “Right. So how are you, Dick Head,” Kris says, laughing. “Uh, uh, Dick Boy… Umm, Dick Sucker… Err, yeah, uhh, Dick-a-palooza, Dicksplosion…” Dickey stares at Kris, “You really suck at that.” Kris mumbles and leaps over onto his couch, and Dickey follows him, sitting down at a different couch. “So how’s my uncle and aunt? Did they break up?” “Yeah,” Dickey says, “they broke up about a year ago, and my mother got remarried. That pissed dad off because he’s an ugly bastard and no one would even go on a date with him. I didn’t want to stir up turmoil in the family, so I took both last names.” “Dickey Rection-Face…” Kris says to himself as though he was examining the word, “That must suck man.” “Yeah, I know. I’m lucky I have a full-time job now. It took me forever to get promoted, but this old guy died so a slot opened up. Good old heart attacks…” Dickey says smiling. “I’m now an Operations Engineer, and I’m making plenty of money doing it,” Dickey says proudly. “Well, I’m rollin’ in dah’ cash as well… homie… dawg… Word. I get to bust caps in busta’s asses and I get dah’ bling-bling doin’ it foo,” Kris says, his hands in a ‘rapper-like’ position. After a moment of silence, Dickey finally responds, “Right. And aren’t you like 1-6? I think that you’re the one getting… ‘caps in your busta ass,’” Dickey says laughing. “Whatever. At least my name isn’t Dickey Rection-Face. What’s your job mean, anyways? Do you, like, check over gay pornos?” Kris says smiling. “No!” Dickey says loudly. “I’m… Operations Director for the Trojan condom company…” Dickey says, embarrassed. Kris laughs out loud. “Are you serious?” Dickey nods. Kris stops and then begins to talk in a whispering tone, “You think you can get me some of those for free?” “Yeah,” Dickey says loudly, “I can get you some free condoms.” Kris punches Dickey on the arm, and then falls back into his chair. “You want a beer, jackass?” Dickey laughs and nods. Three hours later… Drunken laughter echoes through the household as Dickey sits in his chair, his shirt soaked and unbuttoned and bottles of beer sitting all around him, the same goes for Kris. “And then the skeleton orders TWO BEERS AND A MOP!” Dickey says as they both break out in laughter. “Wai’h, Wai’h, I goh a better one,” Kris says. “Okay, these two guys ar’ drinking at a bar and this, this guy says to the other one, ‘you know, if you jump out that window, the air will pull you back in?’ And the other guy goes, ‘bull shit!’ So they drink some more, and the guy says, ‘fine, I’ll show you!’ He jumps out the window and the current pulls him back in. The other guy says ‘holy shit!’ A few beers later, he tries it as well. He jumps and lands SPLAT, right onto the ground. Then the dude at the bar says, ‘man, you’re an ass hole when you’s drinkin!’” They both begin to laugh hysterically again. “Wait,” Dickey says, “I don’t get it…” “Well, you can do that in that window you know!” Kris says. “Bull shit!” Dickey shouts. “If you do it, I’ll give you five dollars!” Kris says. “FINE!” Dickey says, running up to the window Kris had pointed to. He opens it and leaps out the window, straight into the rain. Kris quickly runs up to the window, slams it shut, locks to door, and walks upstairs to bed.
