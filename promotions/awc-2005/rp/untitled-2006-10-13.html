Untitled

13 October 2006

Date: 10/13/06 When I was a little boy, I always dreamed of growing up to be a king, a ruler of my land. My eyes were big and envious; my heart was beating furiously and with a sense of pride I could never handle; and my feet moved, moved to get closer and closer to that stature I always wanted to obtain. I needed to be that God, I needed to be that man who came out on top and ruled all others. As the time went by, though, and as I slipped closer and closer into the reality known as my community under God, me and my brother were less and less inclined to look at ourselves as the son of God. We were merely soldiers under His control, the type of servant who’d kill for his name without question. Real fucking eenie, meenie, minie, moe type mother fuckers, you know? Things, though, fell into place. We reached the top of the food chain. Instead of being the cow, we were the ones who were slaughtering. Our style was beyond the norm, there was nothing that was newer than us or whatever. As the originators of this new style of God’s child, we were looked up by all. The young and the old, they wanted to be the physical and mental specimens we were. I knew it’d all come down eventually, though. I just didn’t know it would hit like this. I didn’t know it’d all end so fucking… ---------------------------------------------- *SCREEEEEEEEEEECH* *SLAM* “Shhhh, quiet! Don’t wake these fuckahs up!” The two men froze in their apartment. They recognized the voices from earlier—the drug dealers. It only made sense that they’d come back, you know? I mean, how could the brothers not expect it? The fucking animals we have grown to love to hate were now the hunted. The aarows were being locked and loaded, but these wolves knew what was coming. The primal urge to defend made them less like Eagles, who grow more extinct by the second, and more into beasts. “Get ready, Liam.” But there was no time to do that. Like a fucking whirlwind, the door was blasted full with holes. As Tim ducked, Liam was shook to a stand still as each bullet hit him harder than the first. Eventually he was shot all the way back into the fridge, only to bounce off and fall flat on the floor. While he wasn’t lifeless, there was no hope. Once the door burst open, two of the men ran in, only to be taken out viciously through a relay of bullets. One man’s head fucking goes pop with a well aimed shot, dropping him to the floor; another takes one too many to the torso and just falls the fuck flat onto the table. Was that it? BANG! In the door way, a third gets a shot off before reaction takes the toll in the form of blasting. But the damage is done: Tim is now against the wall, falling slowly to his ass as he clutches his stomach. A few spurts of blood— “Gluck!” --shoot out of the mouth, landing in front of him. “TIIIIIIMMMM…ARGHHHH….” And all he can do is listen to his brother. ---------------------------- The best parts about the city are the winter experiences, when it’s cold and damp. Even during days like the death of the Martins, beautiful things were happening., You could see it all around as Boston celebrated the Christmas spirit and all that bullshit. First you get to Boston Common. All around are those fuckers with their smug little hats on and shit like that. You know, quite fucking beautiful. They come from Urban Outfitters all decked out in their beautiful attire, looking like individual group Abercrombie models. You stare at them like they’re fucking Christmas trees, all of them wicked crazy in their decorations. You can see the same shit on Newberry Street, where all the upper class and snooty wanna-bes hang out. Grab some ice cream at the Ben and Jerry’s shop and just chill out. Jump out of the cold into the argyle store. All this shit, fucking elegant. But the best part about Boston, stained with the blood in the sky, is home. Because it’s at home where we found the most solitude, at home where we find the things that we discover in ourselves. You see, no matter where the fuck you are, home is home. A box could be your casa of choice and yet, no matter what, it’s fucking home. So when the snow dampens on your roof and when death is just around the corner with every breath and all that shit, you gotta look around and think to yourself “This is my home, I’d rather be no other place.” ------------ When the Martins died, there was a noticeable void in the atmosphere. It could be fucking felt. Every tear that dropped to the pavement was another covering onto the whole fucking thing. As you moved along the streets, you saw it in the faces. The whites and blacks together mourned. The red eyes weren’t from the pipes smoked, as they were put down. For a day, at least, the entire city of Boston mourned for the greatest killers ever to roam the streets. Killers? Yes, fucking killers. Because when you have that type of reputation, that’s all you’re remembered for. Fucking killers. ----------------- I could feel the dust in the air as the silence grew upon me once Liam left. I knew I wasn’t far behind, since this blood wasn’t stopping, no matter how hard I gripped my gut. The combination of the dirt and the smell of the crimson in the room made for a sour taste on my tongue. FFFFFF….huuuuuuhhhh….. My mind started racing at a slow pacing, running through all the moments in my existence that I took for granted. I tried to stay calm, but even in my hindered state of mind I was still moving too fast. I didn’t want to die in a mess of thoughts that meant nothing to me. Instead…Instead… -------------------- He was an angel. Perfection was the greatest thing about him. Tim and his brother, Liam, were the model Christians, the type who even Jesus would look at and say “Hot damn, what a good son of a bitch.” Every Sunday was church. Every Saturday was mass. And every other day that was a religious holiday (I’m an atheist, don’t know these things), you were bound to see the Martins there, just chilling, doing they’re prayer thing. One day, though, outside of the church, there was a man. He wasn’t a holy man, he wasn’t a righteous man either. But he had an…air to him, if you know what I mean. “Hey, kid”, he said, “Get over here.” And Tim moved slowly and surely, cautiously. Liam must’ve been back in the church or something like that. “Whaddya want?” “You’re going to have a destiny…” “What’s this, some Deus Ex Machina shit?” “QUIET! You must know! You’re future…Tim…you’re future…” “What?” “Make peace in your future.” ------------ I didn’t really get it, and it kind of just came out of no where, the whole guy thing, but right now, I noticed it. I needed to make peace, I needed to make ends with it at all. FFFFFF….huuuuuuhh… I started crawling over to my brother, the blood dripping from under me and dragging behind, slowly and surely. It was hard to do anything as I started getting more tired and weak. I needed to get over there, though FFFFFFFF….huuuuuuuuh….. Eventually, I got to my brother and laid on my back next to him. I look at his dead eyes, which stared blankly back at mine with a certain sincerity that I can’t put into words. It was fucking beautiful. FFFFFFFF…….huuuuuuuh… And before I went I made sure to grab his hand, grip it tight, and give him a kiss on the forehead. I whispered “I love you” into his ear before I let go and fell on my back, staring at the ceiling. That fucking… FFFFFFFFF… …ceiling… …huuuuh. ------------------- As a human being, I become emotionally invested in everything I do. Every argument brings me to the verge of self destruction, every sad song is too close to flicking the hair trigger water ducts the right way, and every smooth word hits my heart with an “Oh”. The oddest thing, though, is that when you consider all that, I have no problem packing up and just going. When I went to college, I didn’t shed a tear. Not for my friends, not for my siblings, not even for my bawling mother. I was out, blah blah blah, all that nonsense. As I write the final, sort of anti-climatic tale of the Martins, though, my soul wrenches. These feelings I have right now are something that’s odd, but in the same sense familiar. While you read these words, it’s being revealed that I am leaving the AWC and any future of the Martins is becoming non-existent. (Sorry for ruining that for those who didn’t know) I’m not leaving a happy camper, though, and definitely not under my own terms. [detail withheld] In result, I am going. Why did I kill the Martins, then? The answer is simple in a not-so-simple way: because that’s how it was supposed to be. Wait, what? That simple a fucking answer? Yes and no. In one hand, you have the concept of the Martins killing everyone they see. The Karma aspect has to bite them in the ass, no? But in the other, they go out in death because I’m leaving here in pain. It’s unfortunate that my final RP is quite…shit. But that’s just how it is. I want to thank Pierre for having such a good fed. Fuck the nay sayers and keep it running smooth. Also, all the Larrys who were good, stay good. It’s been real. I hope you enjoyed my work. --Sam p.s. an untitled roleplay? Shit, man, just go with it
