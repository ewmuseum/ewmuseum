A woman can change things #1

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 9th 2005 For weeks The Farmer had been drifting through his life. With Crimson’s departure, Steven Xandrous’ attack on his shoulder and then his teasing about attacking his parents and then Mike Wade suddenly deciding to side with the Irish in AWC, he had just given up caring and began to see wrestling as simply a job again and not a career. Michael Fitzpatrick-McCarthy didn’t see the point in caring. He got paid regardless of the fact that he cared if he lost the Alliance belts or retained them against a pair of “twenty something’s”. So what if Mike Wade had suddenly decided that he wanted to shout his allegiance to his birth country from the rooftops? The Farmer didn’t have to like him. The salary would still be paid if Mickey welcomed him into the ranks of the Green Army or told him to get lost. And who cared what The Farmer cared anyway? Paddy O’Shea made all the decisions. Nobody cared for the fact that Mickey had rescued Paddy’s dad from Hate or even that Mickey had stuck by Paddy when Crimson had disappeared. That didn’t matter much. Mike Wade was the flavour of the month. Fuck him. Sunday 9th October 2005… 7:14am Rolling out of bed, The Farmer clutched at his pounding head and struggled to locate his trousers on the hotel room floor. The curtains were half-drawn and some beams of sunlight lit up the dingy room. The curtains were grey, the carpet was originally black but had faded to grey and the bedding was a sick colour, most likely from constant vomit stains and not actually housekeeping’s distinct sense of style. On all fours, the hung-over Irishman clutched at random fabrics draped everywhere in a search to find his clothes, however standing up, The Farmer realised he wasn’t in his motel room. As one thunderbolt of a thought raced through his cloudy mind, the AWC wrestler’s attention was griped by the rustling under the duvet that covered the double bed he had just rolled his shell. A head covered in jet black hair soon followed as Mickey watched the bump in the bed move and seconds later a smiling face surfaced and was lit up by a beam of morning sunshine. She was hot. The Farmer couldn’t move. He stood upright; frozen filled with a mixture of shock and confusion. Firstly who was this beautiful woman in the bed, had he cheated on his wife and was this her room or did it belong to someone else…so many questions, so little time. “Where you going?” she beamed with such a cute smile growing across her face, joining her cheeks with a beautiful, full red line. Still frozen in his position, Mickey’s brain hadn’t even registered the question. He was still piecing together whatever pieces of the previous 24 hours he could remember but none were jumping out at him. Tilting her head a little the lady in the bed, held her cute smile and peered lovingly at the Irish superstar. Finally snapping back into the room, Mickey was aware that his companion had asked him a question. “Sorry…” groaned Mickey, his voice sounding hoarse and rough. “Where are you going?” she asked again, her angelic voice only sounding more sweeter against the rough, harshness of the Irish accent that had proceeded it. Mickey raised his free hand; his right and scratched at his ginger curls of his head, his left hand holding whatever clothes he could find and suddenly he realised he was naked. Totally and completely buff with his tones highlighted in the morning sunshine. Cursing under his breath, The Farmer moved both of his hands towards his private area and cupped his twig and berries. Immediately, the lady in the bed’s smile grew a little. “Don’t be covering that up on my part,” she smiled and threw the Irishman a devilish look of pure intent. The Farmer needed to be out of there. Badly. His head was pounding, his stomach felt ill, and he had no clothes on. If he had woke up on his own he would have rolled over in his bed, a grin across his face happy that another night had been washed away with alcohol but waking up with someone else there was not a good thing. It simply left an uncertainty about the previous night that Mickey didn’t like. Previous night… Saturday 8th October 2005 8:00pm Pushing right shoulder first into the brown door, The Farmer was right on time. Every night of the week he had entered the quietest bar he could find, found a nice stool at the bar and got lost in the bottom of a whiskey bottle. The Irishman had been repeating the situation since the farmyard match. Each week he found a different bar in each city that AWC visited and spent numerous hours drinking away the evening and then stumbling back to his motel room in a hazy mess. Climbing into his seat, Mickey had quickly become a regular in the dusty Texan bar and the barman was ready with the whisky bottle and a shot glass. He had only been in the premises the previous three nights but quickly the barman had decided against arguing with the ripped 6’4 wrestler. Quietly pouring his first drink, The Farmer spent little time courting it and simply threw it down his throat and refilling the glass. Sunday 9th October 2005… 7:17am So the night had started like every night since Battle of Britain. But still Mickey couldn’t think of how he had bumped into this hot young woman. Previous night… Saturday 8th October 2005 8:32pm As if it was water, The Farmer threw back another shot glass filled with whisky and then slammed it down on the bar. “Hey stranger…” Looking to his left, Mickey was greeted by a young woman with jet lack hair. She was dressed in a flattering red top and black skirt and had perched herself on the stool next to The Farmer. Sunday 9th October 2005 7:18am Snapping back to the cold motel room, The Farmer had discovered the point of first contact with the woman that was now naked in the double bed. A silence had been drawn out between the pair. Mickey had perched himself on the bed with his back to the woman. She had simply lain down again and drifted to sleep, safe in the knowledge that her apparent lover was not headed anywhere as first feared. Previous night… Saturday 8th October 2005 8:33pm After a number of seconds eyeing the girl The Farmer snapped his neck to the right, doubting that she was actually talking to him and was really addressing someone else. “Yes you…” she added, sensing the doubt in Mickey’s mind and then proceeding to ask the barman for another second shot. The Farmer eyed her finger the sot glass as it was handed to her. “Fancy some company?” she beamed, flashing an open mouthed smile at the socially-inept Irishman. Nodding, Mickey poured her a drink and then filled his glass once more. Sunday 9th October 2005 7:20am And so they had started drinking… However from that point on The Farmer was drawing a blank. He had no recollection of who she was, what she had wanted or even what they had done. Standing up, The Farmer felt uneasy in his heart. Not because of a hangover, but because he felt like he shouldn’t leave but he really to be in the gym and get ready for the Alliance title defence. Despite refusing to train with Paddy because of Mike Wade, The Farmer still wanted to keep a hold of his title belt. Simply put, the Alliance belts were keeping Eire Og together. The who, what, where and why of his Saturday night would wait. Taking one step across the small room Mickey was at the door. Still naked he had found his underwear but would wait until outside to get dressed so as to not wake his guest. “For now, goodbye,” he whispered, sure that they’re paths would cross once again. He had no idea.
