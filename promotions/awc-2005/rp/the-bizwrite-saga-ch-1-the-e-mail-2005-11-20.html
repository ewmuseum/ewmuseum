The Bizwrite Saga, Ch 1: The E-Mail

20 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/05 TO: [email removed] FROM: [email removed] Subject: Come visit! Tim! How is it going? Long time no speak! What have you been doing with all your time? Geez, I never hear from you! What would you say to visiting me? Bring Liam along with you! Love, Liz ----------------------------------------- Everyone gets e-mails. It’s the new postal service, and I’d be foolish to think that the world revolves around me and that my e-mails are the only special ones. But this one is different. This one has some history to it. In fact, this is one that is bound to spark history from itself. … I jumped too far, didn’t I? Allow me to say welcome to the story! You’re about to embark on a journey, one Liam and I take to meet up with childhood friend Liz Bizwrite. After the Fist’s long absence, I decided to inform you why we were gone. To say the least, it was quite an adventure. It all started out with the e-mail at the beginning of this entry. Every day I receive e-mails from people I know and people I don’t know. I receive e-mails condemning my ways, I receive e-mails from people who love me, and I also receive e-mails from people who ant to know if I’d like to make my penis larger. Very classy. So, when I received this particular e-mail, I was touched. Liz and I hadn’t speak since she went to college and I began to work, so to say the least, I was thrilled to oblige. “Liam!” I called out from the computer screen, smiling with joy, “Would you like to take a vacation?” The silence from the other side of the apartment told me two things. One was that, above everything else, Liam was interested. Don’t get me wrong, us Martin boys are hardworking individuals, but even God rested. The other thing it said, though, was that he was skeptical. *Thump thump thump* Liam pounded his way towards me, his heavy foot steps showing his excitement. “Vacation? Right now?” He yelled out from the other room, the volume growing louder as he grew closer. “Yeah, why not? I mean, we’ve been working hard and everything and—“ Suddenly I looked up to see Liam filling the doorway, raising an eyebrow and giving me a glance that plainly said “Are you on fucking drugs?” “Tim, what’s up? You feeling ok? We’re on top of our game! We’re about to break out into the AWC! We’re going to be champs!” He was ecstatic and confused, trying to figure out what it was while reminding me of our current situation. Regardless, I wanted to tell him what my plan was. “Remember Liz Bizwrite, from two houses over?” Silence. “Liam?” Silence. I knew he was still pissed off. “What about her, Tim? What about?” “*gulp*, well, um, I just received an e-mail from her, and she wants us to come visit her…” Silence. The face that went along with this was heart breaking. The glaring eyes and straight mouth was as stern as ever, the attitude behind it knowingly pissed off. “Are you fucking kidding me, Tim? You’re telling a fucking joke, right?” Even though he was talking about laughter, Liam was not. He was pissed off, his words stabbing and hurting. “Well, uh, no, I thought…maybe…” “YOU DIDN’T THINK, FUCK-FACE! TELL HER NO FUCKING WAY!” As Liam stormed out of the room, I chased after him and tackled him to the ground. “LISTEN TO ME, YOU MUSH-HEAD!” I had Liam flat on his back when I started receiving a barrage of fists. *SMACK! SMACK!* Even though both landed square on my jaw, I still had the energy and will to put my hand clenched around his throat. As he gasped for air I put my face close to his and gritted my teeth, forcing my words out only with anger. “You better listen to me right now. Maybe she has changed. Just because something happened years ago doesn’t mean it’s the same. I know she is thinking right now. Don’t you go fucking judge her now!” I held his throat for longer until his whole face was red. When I deemed it ok, I pulled myself off of Liam and walked back to the computer, sitting in the chair and typing my response as Liam gained his composure… ------------------------------------------------ TO: [email removed] FROM: [email removed] Subject: Re: Come visit! Liz, I’m well and surprised to hear from you. How did you find me? I feared we’d never speak again. Of course I’ll come visit. Liam will be with me, no worries. Are you still dating Chuck? See you soon! --Tim --------------------------------------------- “So, what’s the deal?” “I just responded to her e-mail; we’re going.” “Fucking great. I’ll start packing.” Even though Liam didn’t sound excited, I could tell he was at least happy to be leaving the city and quit working for a few weeks. It had been forever since he had gotten a break, the work-horse he is. He put on a great “I-don’t-want-to-be-doing-this” face, though. As Liam began to pack, I got up from the computer and sat on my bed, pulling out my suitcase. I didn’t have much to pack, since my wardrobe rarely consisted of something other than jeans and a white t-shirt. I finished packing in an hour. Locking up the suitcase, Liam managed to finish packing just as quickly as I had. I could hear him in the kitchen pouring himself a glass of water and reading something as he waited for me. He was quiet, still trying to take everything in that happened in the last hour. “Ready?” “Yeah.” And we were off. We threw our things into the truck and hit the road, heading across the country towards Liz’ house. “So…why couldn’t this wait? Why did we have to pack everything up and go?” Liam was reading something, his question slurred by his concentration on whatever it was he was studying. “I don’t know, Liam.” Pause. “Wait. You don’t fucking know? We’re in a car going across the country to visit someone who has betrayed us for the stellar reason of ‘I don’t know’? Wow, fucking great.” Silence. “Yeah, great, don’t say a fucking word. While you’re at it, piss on my shoes and show a little more disrespect towards me.” “God damn it Liam, I don’t mean any disrespect at all!” “Then what is your reason, huh? Gimme one good reason why we should visit her after what she did. Inform me, enlighten me, PLEASE!” Silence. “Fine. Be an ass. I don’t give a fuck. Just tell me when you feel like talking.” And a few more moments passed. The silence was killing me, but what even hurt more was that I knew that I was going to be chewed out for my true reason. I spilled the beans regardless. “Well…she’s a smart girl, right Liam? I mean, God must’ve steered her back on the right path eventually, right? It has been YEARS since we’ve seen her, so something must’ve changed.” “You kidding me? I hope we’re telling jokes again! You’re talking about Liz Bizwrite, the woman who not only betrayed her family, not only betrayed OUR family, but betrayed the entire Eagle’s Nest. And now you think she changed? What good would changing do? She thinks God remembers scum like her? You think that she believes that, hey, maybe if she asks the Lord for forgiveness after everything that happened, then maybe he will! I mean, who gives a fuck right?” “She’s a good girl, though…” “Tim, she cheated on you.” “I know, but…” “Tim.” Pause. “She cheated on you with a fucking n*gger, Tim. A God damn fucking n*gger.” Silence. “And that is why I am wondering WHY this couldn’t wait. You know I don’t want to see her. I’ll fucking break her face in. But you also know that I’m your brother and that, since you’re taking this break from the AWC, I have to as well, right?” I nodded. “I’ll just warn you, Tim: If that n*gger is there when we arrive, I’ll fucking kill him.” I didn’t say a word because I knew the truth of this. Liam would not halt to kill a man. Hell, he tried to kill his own brother earlier. I just hoped that everything DID change… “Please Lord...” I said to myself, “Guide Liz back to you…”
