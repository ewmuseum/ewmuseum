'World of Peace and Imagination'

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/06 'The power of imagination makes us infinite.' John Muir Imagination is the gateway to possibility. It's the driving force of creativity. Some would say, that it's the brain of the whole operation and without it, we as humans beings would fail. It's out talent, knowledge, feelings, and power all mixed into one. It holds the key to a person's future, and decides almost every move you make. It's the power of the mind to form mental images of our future in a boundless world of originality. A warm breeze rustled through the brown locks of his hair blowing it in every direction. The grass stood long and tall as it unpredictably nestled in between his toes which wiggled underneath his crossed legs. His arms rested on his knees, fingers partially clasped, parallel from one another. In a single blink he closed his brown eyes and titled his head towards the sun which had been covered by a few puffy clouds. It was meditation. A daily process in which Sebastian Archer had completed for some time now to relieve himself from stress and the corruption of society. It soothed his soul in a sense and rejuvenated his body, like oil to a car, allowing him to continue on with his daily rituals. The presence of the sun against his skin made it even more worth his while. It reminded him of god, joy, and life. He had began to enter a world of imagination. A world of peace, for only a few seconds. 'Sebastian!' The voice of a woman cried out from behind him. 'Sebastian are you out here?' In fright, Sebastian jumped and opened his eyes trying not to make a slight movement. He rarely got these distractions, and was sort of used to avoiding them by now. He continued to remain in the earlier position, not knowing what was going on from behind. Once again he had closed his eyes trying to regain that sense of peace. But it was no use. 'Sebastian,' the voice continued. 'It's here. Your letter, it's here.' In a matter of seconds he could feel her presence bestow upon him. She kneeled down on her knees next to him, repositioning her long black hair behind her ears, and plopped down onto the grass next to him. She leaned back on her elbows and allowed the sun to gaze upon her sandy brown skin. Marks from where she had been tanned previously were evident around her bikini. Sebastian tired to ignore. 'Your letter from that wrestling club thingy is here.' She tried to tell him again. 'It just came in the mail a few seconds ago.' Sebastian grunted. 'Alright, Aulani.' He replied in a sluggish voice. 'Sit it down on the table in the kitchen. I'll read it when I come in later.' 'But Keona.' Which was Sebastian's real name. 'Don't you even want to hear what it says?' Sebastian opened an eye and glared over to his right side where Aulani was present. His lips curled underneath his nose forming a look of disgust. 'You opened my mail again after I told you precisely time after time not to?' Sebastian asked without any hesitation. 'That's an invasion of privacy.' Aulani chuckled. 'We've been living together for years now. What can you possibly be trying to hold from me?' She asked. 'Besides I know you like the back of my hand.' 'You do , huh?' Sebastian sarcastically questioned. 'How about you know your little self back up into that house and leave me alone before I get angry.' Aulani sighed. She got up from the ground and began to walk back towards the house doing what she were told. Before she could make it even a foot away Sebastian reached up and snatched the letter from her grasp. She ignored and because of his usual arrogant behavior she didn't even attempt to argue. Trying to cool him down was like trying to teach an old dog new tricks. Sebastian uncrossed his legs and stretched them out in front of him. He placed the letter, with the Atlantic Wrestling Club logo neatly imprinted in orange ink in the upper left hand corner, in both hands and opened. Out from the envelope came a letter folded in three sections. He pulled it straight. 'Congratulations.' He read aloud. 'You've been accepted to the Atlantic Wrestling Club.' It brought a smile to his face something that was very rare. The letter went into greater detail but he sort of skimmed through. 'Captain Suleimon, huh?' He chuckled. 'How creative?' He refolded the letter, placed it back into the envelope, and finally into his pocket. He shrugged and assumed the position he had been in previously. He wanted to reenter his world of imagination but he was once again disturbed. 'Aloha bro.' Came a random surfer walking up the hill. 'How are the rays?' 'Fine.' Sebastian replied. 'It would be even better if people like you would quit interrupting me.' 'Excuse me.' The surfer rudely replied before walking off. Sebastian just shook his head. 'Ignorance is bliss.' He mumbled to himself. He once again closed his eyes and tried to reenter that world of imagination he had once found. The world that he had dreamed of. The world where anything was possible. He could hear the music playing in the back of his head while he sat alone at the top of the hill, eyes closed, and head pointed toward Kahuna, the god of the sun. He imagined, gaining visual images of the butterflies that fluttered over the creeks and streams. Deer nestling with their young in their mossy homes. He was in his special place of joy and happiness. His place where he could not be disturbed. More importantly he was as focused as ever and ready for his first AWC match up.
