Killian O'Leary: American Idol

5 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 April 2006 “Hello. My name is Killian O’Leary…” The three judges were bemused as they looked at the man that stood in front of them. He had short brown hair, styled in a fashion to make it look un-styled. He had piercing blue eyes, that quickly attracted Paula to him, but it was his frame they were all concerned with. He was well turned out in a sharp black suit but the guy was at least 6’ 4 tall, and the suit struggled to hide what they expected were rippling muscles. “Hello Killian,” smiled Simon in his smug English accent. He was intrigued by this man, “What are you going to sing for us?” Killian smiled. He was nervous. That was okay. The three judges knew how to deal with nerves. “Just take your time,” Paula added, as Killian stared a hole in the floor. As contestant #235 prepared himself, Randy Jackson and Simon Cowell shot each other telling looks. Looks that could have said a thousand words. Mr. O’Leary over there was twice there individual size. If anything, both men were just a little worried any criticism they might hand out, may not be dealt well with by the big man, and therefore the two men feared for their safety. And yes, before you ask that is what they were thinking about. Killian O’Leary looked more like a linebacker or a wrestler, than a pop star. “I’m going to sing a Sammy Davis Jr.’s ‘Mr Bojangles’…” smiled Killian as he looked up at the three American Idol judges. Killian’s voice shook as he went over the words in his head. He looked the part of one of the rat pack, but did he have the voice? God no he knew he didn’t. That’s why he was here. Now, Killian was standing to far away, and was too deep in his thoughts to hear it, but as the young Irish-wannabe singer declared that he was singing a track by Sammy Davis Jr, there was a sharp intake of breath by the American Idol judges. All three were well that Mr. Bojangles was a classic. Killian would have to be an awesome singer to pull it off. “Whenever you’re ready…” finally stated Randy Jackson, realising that Killian was still looking at the floor. Looking up, Killian took in the three judges. Then he looked above them and looked out the large window that was behind him. And he took a deep breath. Killian O’Leary was preparing to make an absolute fool out of himself on National television just for a wrestling contract. A wrestling contact that he wasn’t guaranteed. He was kind of sure that he would get a deal. Hell, if that weird Chinese guy can release songs originally done by Ricky Martin, surely a wrestling promotion would show some interest in a guy that has just bombed out of American Idol; spectacularly bombed. Sorry, I think I’m getting ahead of myself. Meet Killian O’Leary. Killian was born in Dowra, Co. Leitrim on the 17th of March 1980. Yes, he is well aware that he was born on St. Patrick’s Day. And yes, he is well aware that he comes from the same shitty little village as The Farmer, formerly of AWC and Dual Halo fame. Killian left Ireland when he was seven. He was a big kid, with bushy, brown hair. Even in the New York playgrounds where his parents moved to Killian commanded respect and got it. He grew up like most kids in America harbouring dreams of being a star. But not just any star. A wrestling star. So at the age of the 18, and with the backing and love of his parents Killian set out to become a wrestler. The training went well, and he progressed just like everyone else. But that was the problem. Killian O’Leary had the same skills as everyone else. Killian had the same look as everyone else. Killian could do what everyone else could do. He was just part of the crowd, just one of the faceless Indy wrestlers that helped with crowd scenes in the major promotions. He was never close to his big break and it pissed me no end. So that’s why he finds himself here; standing in front of Simon Cowell, Randy Jackson and Paula Abdul. Killian O’Leary doesn’t want to be the next American Idol. He can’t sing. Killian O’Leary wants to be the next wrestling superstar, no matter what he has to do. Gay porn, reality TV shows, or maybe even shooting someone was definitely not out of the question. But as Killian looked up at the three judges, he just knew he couldn’t do it. It was a mixture of nerves, respect and self-respect. Nerves; well basically smart-ass he was nervous. He was about to appear on National television. How about you try it and tell me you wouldn’t be nervous? Respect; respect for Sammy Davis Jr. Mr. Bojangles is one of the greatest songs ever written and performed and here he was, an Irish emigrant about to absolutely destroy the memory of one of the greatest of all time. And self-respect; something about singing a really awesome song, really badly on television somehow got to publicity whore that was Killian O’Leary in a way that he never imagined. Killian O’Leary had pride in his self. Not a lot of pride, but some. But then Killian felt something else inside him. It was his lunch. And it was coming back up. -- Waking up in a cold sweat, Killian O’Leary sat straight up in the hotel room. Looking at the alarm clock it was 9:35 am. His alarm wasn’t set to go of for an hour. Today was the day of the American Idol auditions. That was just a horrible dream… Wasn’t it? RING! RING! The phone rang. Well obviously as you just seen the “Ring! Ring!” *sigh* I’m just trying to help you out. “Hello…” Killian was just about awake, and so the vibrant voice of the receptionist was a total shock to his system. “Good morning Mister O’Leary. I have AWC on the line. Would you like to receive the call?” ‘What the…?’ What did All-Star Championship Wrestling want with him? “Yes, I’ll take the call,” confirmed Killian, suddenly realising he had left the receptionist on the line for quite a few seconds as he woke up. “Okay… I’ll send it through…” What followed was the longest few seconds of Killian’s life. He could hear the receptionist clicking at bottoms. Each one didn’t connect Killian to his dream. Finally he heard, “Good morning Mr. O’Leary…” There it was; a voice hailing from the promised land. “My name is David Harber. I am one of the Entertainment Mangers with Atlantic Wrestling Club…” “Who?” “Atlantic Wrestling Club… You know, AWC…” “But the receptionist said ACW…” “Fuck… People are always getting us fucking mixed up…” “It’s okay,” reassured Killian as he to Mr. Harber, “how can I help?” “We heard about your mishap with American Idol…” “Fuck,” mumbled Killian. It wasn’t a dream. “…however we feel that the news of your accident, when the show is aired could work well in our favour and yours, publicity-wise if you were a member of our federation. So we would like to offer a trial period with us?” Let’s just say the rest will become history…
