Uh Spelleng Kwizz!

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/19/05 The daytime rolls in as a nice and clean vehicle pulls into a not-so-clean driveway. The driver parks to vehicle behind an average-looking vehicle. As the ignition shuts off, a click of a door opening is heard. The nice vehicle’s door pops open and a man steps out. The man is none other than Adam Masters, otherwise known as “The Educator”. Masters, neatly dressed as always, walks up to the door of the house that sets next to the driveway. He knocks three times on the door and waits patiently for a few seconds. Seeing as there is no answer, he knocks again. He then notices the loud music coming from inside the household. The door is soon opened and standing in the doorway is Kris Krimzon’s wife, Tamara Krimzon. Adam smiles and extends his hand. “How do you do, ma’am. I am Adam Masters. I am here to see Kris. Is he currently home?” the man recites politely. Tamara smiles and returns the hand gesture. “Yeah, he’s in the living room. Follow me.” Adam steps in and takes off his coat. He begins to reach for a coat rack, but then notices that the wall is bare. He smirks. “Should’ve figured,” he thinks. He throws his coat back on and knocks off his shoes before walking onto the carpet. As he follows the stranger woman, he notices that the music is getting louder and louder. As the woman leads him around a corner, the music is at its crescendo. He sees what he assumes to be a living room, and standing in the middle of that living room is his AWC tag team partner, Kris Krimzon. Kris is jumping around wildly to the music, a black and white Fender guitar strapped around his shoulders. Sitting off to the corner is an amplifier where the strange sounds are made louder, the sound coming from Kris’s guitar. The sound, however, sounds nothing like the beat the song has. As Adam listens closer, he hears the lyrics yelling, “Shove it” repeatedly. Offended by the obscenity, Adam walks over to the stereo system that plays the music, turning a small switch to “off”. The obscene music evaporates from the room, but the sounds coming from the amplifier continue as Kris continues to jump around. Kris, realizing his song has stopped playing, looks around confused. “What in the hell,” Adam says as Kris turns around to him, “are you listening to?” Kris smiles, “It’s a song. It’s called ‘My Own Summer’ by Deftones and it is really cool, you should download it.” “Excuse me,” Adam says quiet offended at the last statement, “I do not participate in said illegal acts. Nor do I appreciate the style of music I was just hearing, so there is no way that I will be purchasing their album.” Kris rolls his eyes. “You’re a hardcore party pooper.” “You don’t deserve the rights to talk about ‘hardcore’, seeing as you lost two weeks ago in a no disqualification match. Didn’t you lose to a midget?” Adam says teasingly. “Just tell me why you’re here, Educator,” Kris says impatiently. “Please,” Adam says, raising his hands in protest, “we aren’t on camera. Call me Adam.” “Alright… Tell me why you’re here, ADAM,” Kris repeats. “Well, even though you asked in a very rude fashion, I will answer your inquiry. I am here because if we are to be a successful tag team, you must acquire some intelligence,” Adam says. “Err… What?” Kris says, lost in the conversation. “In other words, if we’re going to KICK ass, you’re going to have to stop being a DUMB ass. Got it?” “Hey, that’s not fair. I am NOT stupid. I am so smart, hell, I put the ‘E’ back in ‘IQ’!” Kris says defensively. Adam laughs out loud at the comment. “You are quiet the riot, my friend. That was an interesting joke, I must say,” Adam says, still chuckling to himself. Kris looks at Adam, wondering what he is talking about. “Anyways, you will have three exams before our next match. First, and simplest, a spelling quiz. Second, a geography test. And last, but not least, a wrestling exam. The wrestling exam will include three parts: moves, how to reverse, and ‘name-that-match’. You up for it?” “No, but I’m down with it,” Kris says. “Err. Right. Anyways, we’ll begin the spelling quiz shortly. Do you mind if I go use your restroom facilities?” Adam asks. “Sure, they--” “I’m sure I can find them myself you mongrel,” Adam says in a flash of anger. “I thought you were going to be nice…” Kris says, mumbling. “Well, if you weren’t such an embarrassment to society, I probably wouldn’t have to!” Adam says rudely. Adam walks off, in search off the bathroom. Moments afterwards, another knock is at the door. This time, Kris answers it. Outside on Kris’s porch is who Kris identifies to be Jonny Sake and two others he cannot identify. Kris smiles. “Hey Jonny! How’s it going?” Kris says cheerfully, probably scared of the seven-foot two-inch tall man. “Not bad, not bad,” Jonny says. “Come on in, man. Make yourself at home. Jonny steps in and knocks his shoes off in a similar fashion to Adam Masters. Jonny goes on one knee and takes off the shorter of the two unidentified guests’, a female’s, shoes. The other takes off his own shoes. Kris leads his guests to the living room. Kris sits down on a rocking chair and the others all sit on the same couch. “So who are you friends?” Kris asks. “Um… These are my kids. This is Joe, he’s eight years old,” Jonny says, pointing to the taller of the two guests, “And this is Kaitlin, she’s only eight MONTHS old.” “Wow… Young,” Kris says. “Yep, sure are… You mind if I go get a drink?” Jonny asks politely. Kris nods, as Jonny walks over into the kitchen. Kris looks at the eight-month-old Kaitlin and uncomfortably says, “So you’re a baby… How’s that working out for you?” Before Kaitlin can “respond”, Adam Masters reenters the room, a frown on his face. “Kris, why didn’t you tell me where the bathroom was instead of making me search for it?” Kris attempts to respond, but is cut off. “You’re such a little brute, you know that? You’re lucky I’m your tag partner, otherwise I’d break your leg.” Kris mumbles as Joe laughs. Adam then notices the children, but otherwise ignores them. “Okay imbecile, spelling quiz. Go get a piece of paper-make that three so the others will be quizzed-and three pencils as well.” Kris scurries off and returns, three pencils and three sheets of paper in hand. The three children, including Kris, sit down at the table. This, of course, excludes the young woman who is set down in a chair by her father. A piece of paper and pencil are handed to them all promptly by Masters. “Okay, first off, right ‘Spelling Quiz’ in the center bar,” Masters says. He waits for the children, and Kris, to write. “Then write your name in the top-right corner.” Once again, he waits. “Alright, word number one: PROOF,” Adam says clearly in a schoolteacher tone. The pencils are lifted. “Word number two: in.” This continues for a couple of minutes until ten words are read. Masters publicly reads off the scores. “Well, the scores are quiet surprising. The top speller is Joe.” The young male smiles and high-fives his father. Kris sinks in his chair. “The second best speller is young Kaitlin,” Masters says grimly in Kris’s direction. “Wait,” Kris says, “What place did I get in?” Masters’ eyes widen. “Are you serious? YOU GOT IN LAST YOU MENTAL PATIENT!” “Oh… At least I have my looks!” Kris says proudly. “Look, an eight-month-old scored higher than you. At least she did not spell ‘in’ with two n’s and an e! And the other one did not, like you, spell ‘quiz’ with a ‘k’ and a ‘z’!” Masters yells. “Screw you man. I already told you once, no one cares who the smarterest is in the AWC. You just needs to be the bestest wrestler,” Kris says matter-of-factly. “Kris,” Adam says in a tone of finality, “If we lose this match against to two bad sitcoms, I am going to have to teach you a lesson that you certainly will not forget after a spring vacation. You got that straight you head-hollow imbecile?!” Kris simply nods to the remark. As he does, an extremely loud crash is heard. Adam is the first to react as he follows the origin of the sound. He walks into the living room followed by Kris. Inside the living room is a grungy car covered and dirt. The most conspicuous thing in the room is a large hole in the wall. The driver door opens and Kris’s pal, AJ Valor steps out. Kris laughs out loud, directing Adam’s attention. “F’ING AWESOME MAN!!”
