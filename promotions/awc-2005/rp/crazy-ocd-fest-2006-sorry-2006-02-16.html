Crazy OCD Fest 2006. Sorry.

16 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today ''YOU FUCKING MORON!'' Darcy looked up from his magazine. Amy was on a dead tear towards him with dripping wet hair and a towel barely covering what she normally would. The picture was pretty, but her mouth was quickly making up for that and, quite frankly, he was too confused to be amused. ''There,'' she grunted, ''I've been waiting to say that for days now. I can't stand another second of walking on eggshells and pretending it didn't happen just because you got a bump on the head. What the hell made you think you had a right to pull that shit?!'' Markson frowned. He knew it was only a matter of time before she confronted him with this. He noted that he was lucky said confrontation was taking place in his living room, rather than on national television... a place that the Amy of old might have preferred. ''I don't know, Ames...maybe because that piece of garbage was going to maim you. If anybody's going to take a steel chair to your head, I think it should be me.'' Amy cocked her head and looked about ready to kick him out of his chair, ''There's no fucking sentiment in that. I FIGHT for a living, Darcy. I've been doing it since you've known me. You, out of ANY-FUCKING-BODY, know that I can hold my own in front of a crowd and a camera, or between those flimsy little ropes. I don't know what you think I've been doing for the last five years, BUT I'M NOT NEW AT THIS.'' ''Well in case you FORGOT,'' Darcy retorted, raising his voice as emphatically as Amy had for him, ''You weren't doing a whole lot of fighting at the time. You were doing a lot of lying in the middle of the ring like a sitting duck. Is that some sort of new strategy the wrestling world came up with in my absence? All I did was stop Adam Dick from killing you, at my own fucking expense. YOU hit him with your finisher, and YOU got the pinfall... so what the fuck's the problem?'' ''I could've done it on my own, and in case you forgot - wrestling types don't work in 'could have's. You completely tainted what could've been my guarantee to a title shot.'' The little redhead grabbed a t-shirt that was hanging over one of Darcy's armchairs, an old 'DC Darcy Crisis' novelty shirt, and threw it on over top of her lovely little white towel ensemble, pushing the towel down to act like a temporary - and slightly more imaginative - skirt. She was still fuming, but had a kind of somber tenderness about her tone, ''Do you have any idea how much that hurts? I'm a woman and, not to be sexist, but we suck ass in this business. Anyone under 5'9' in this business has to work twice as hard to earn the same respect a 6'5' asshole does. Add tits to the equation and you're looking at needing to work for YEARS just to get someone to tell you that you're an okay player in the game. I was fighting the most famous and decorated guy in our company, and I could've had him. I could've had him, Darcy. Yeah, I eventually got the best of him... but now it's not going to be because of how hard I worked, it's gonna be 'because her little boyfriend had to help her'.'' ''Could've, Amy? COULD HAVE?!'' Darcy threw down his magazine, now that matters had become more pressing. ''News flash: You didn't have him. If I hadn't done something he was going to bash your skull in, wake up the referee, put you away and you'd be sitting on the shelf right now. No victory, no title shot, maybe not even a career. He tried to bend the rules... and so did I. Now you have a victory over the champ and you're going up against him again. If you're half as good as you THINK you are, Miss 'I'm-so-underappreciated' you'll relish your newfound opportunity and get the job done again. So how about you kiss my ass instead of going postal on the guy who damn near got concussed on your behalf?'' ''JACK MURPHY, Darcy. There's a third fighter in our match... so, guess what? Once again, if I win, it's 'because she needed help from a big strong manly man'. Even if you get that shit through your easily broken skull, I'm STILL gonna end this by telling you to go fuck yourself, you fucking wannabe. You're pissed it wasn't you in that match.'' She wasn't going to give up, and kept staring at him as her tone was suddenly infused with about four and a half years of hatred, ''You're tired of jobbers, but that's all you are right now because YOU haven't been the one fighting for the last five years. Don't try to fool me with your savior bullshit, Markson... you're a washup who's trying to come back by pushing himself into affairs that don't concern him. Trust me, asshole, you're years away from crossing any champions.'' Darcy was seeing red. Somewhere in his head, a tiny voice told him that he would probably regret what he was about to say. But it was a mere whisper, especially compared to the mountain of words he would expel. ''So it's like that, huh? I practically save your life and now all you can think about is tearing me down? Guess what, Amy... I don't care if I cross another champion in my life. Those days are behind me. If I were you, I would think back a few years ago... don't you remember what you told me? That I was self-absorbed jackass for only caring about my career, putting my World Title first and you second even though you were head over heels in love with me? Well look where you're standing now. Look who's the self-absorbed jackass now. You've got everything going for you and you're wasting your time trying to make me feel like shit.'' Darcy was staring her down, and Amy looked about ready to tear his head off... but tears were welling in her eyes. ''So FINE, Amy. I'm sorry that I risked my neck for you. Have fun on your little title hunt... you're so goddamn set that it has to be Aimz vs. the world, so have at it. Enjoy chasing gold and making a name for yourself. But I'll tell you exactly how it plays out when you get there: You'll be standing at the top of the mountain looking down at all the people you tore down, all the bridges you burned, and everybody you kicked in tbe balls to get there. There's a reason I gave up everything I earned for a girl I loved, Amy Campbell. If you have to learn that reason the hard way... be my guest.'' Amy was biting her lip nearly to the point of drawing blood, trying to conceal teary eyes as she spat her words out... ''Go. FUCK. Yourself.'' She took to her heels and set out down the hallway, to the spare room she'd been staying in, planning to pack everything she had there. He, however, was dumbstruck enough to follow. Of course, as soon as she had left the room Darcy was instantly sorry. He desperately wanted to run after her, drop to his knees and beg forgiveness for displeasing her. The man had his pride, and as a veteran of the innumerable Darcy/Amy confrontations that resembled full-scale wars far more than they did arguments, he had learned how to hold his own against the spirit that was as fiery as her hair. But the empty feeling in his stomach that followed every time he caused her the least bit of anguish never seemed to dissipate with time, no matter how many times he felt it. But this was no time to beg for forgiveness. Knowing Amy, she wouldn't have any of it, and she'd be out that door at warp speed, pausing perhaps only for a last few mincing words and some sort of physical assault. Exhaling a deep breath, Darcy pondered his options but as he heard the zippers of Amy's bags signifying their full capacity, he knew there was only one real option. Speak from the heart. And hope for the best. Amy had turned around to see the man she'd just grown to loathe more than anything standing in the doorway. She was poised to scream, but Darcy cut her off as he began to speak. Knowing that this could in fact be the last words she would ever hear from him, he fell back on an old yet quite appropriate standby: Casablanca, Darcy Markson style. ''If you walk out that door, you're going to regret it. Maybe not today, maybe not tomorrow... but someday, when you're on top of the world, when you've been where I've been - you're going to regret it. Of that much, I can promise you. So I'm about to ask something of you that might be the hardest thing you've ever had to do. Stop seeing red for once in your life and listen to somebody who, despite what you might think, actually gives a damn about you. Many years ago I burned a bridge with somebody that I cared a great deal for, and I've spent my life ever since paying for it. And if you're going to do the same, in the name of glory... I... I wish you the best of luck, and I hope I don't run into you down the road when you're the lonely, miserable, world-famous Amy Campbell.'' The redhead knew he'd made a good point, and that was most of what had her so upset. Her instinct told her to break his nose and spend the rest of her life avoiding him, but something underneath the aching sensation in the left chambers of her chest was speaking differently. Either way, she had experience at being torn emotionally, and their fight wasn't going to burn it all. Or she hoped it wouldn't, because she had a whole lot left to say. She bit her lip again, but managed to keep little more than a glassy sensation in her mismatched eyes, ''What am I walking away from here, Darcy? Why will I be so lonely, huh? Because I don't have someone to botch my matches, because I don't have that kind of a liability?'' Amy Campbell had successfully just done something she tried hard and failed to about five years before: Make Darcy admit to his real feelings, unguarded and not censored by any selfish pride. ''No. Because once you've built that legacy, you'll find out it doesn't mean shit. Everyone will love you but nobody will really know anything about you. And sooner or later you'll have to cede your spot to the next hungry superstar who wants what you have, and all those people who thought you were so important won't even remember your name. And one day you'll wake up and you'll remember the words spoken by the one person you only now realize meant more than all those people who chanted your name put together. You'll finally realize that she was right, and it'll be too late.'' Darcy wondered if she caught what an outsider might have viewed as a misspeak. But Darcy didn't say a word wrong at all... in fact, perhaps for the first time in as long as he could remember, he was speaking his true feelings. It nearly floored Amy, as she steadied her stance and sat on the edge of the bed - underneath a shelf of old wrestling trophies and photos, some even of the pair. ''Sounds like you're not talking about me, but maybe someone else I know? Would that be right?'' she muttered, ''Whatever happened in the past happened - it's not gonna change. But for once in my life, I've got a firm grip on my future. I'm not gonna fuck it up with another marriage or a pregnancy or a preoccupation with some chick who beat me once, forever ago. I'm not stuck in the past, and I've made my career in AWC by being the one person who spoke against living with some bullshit sense of nostalgia. I always get involved in melodrama, but this is five years old. I could say a million things now, about regrets and still hating you for choosing a dead career over a very real human being...'' Amy breathed in deeply, ''I don't care about what you're trying to tell me right now, and you know why? Most people don't get a second chance. You lost your career and you lost me. It's 2005. You've got a contract with AWC, there's your career. You talk about what's been done, the mistakes you made and how you wish you could take them all back. You don't stop saying it. But, Darcy... what the hell is sitting four feet in front of you? Your words and wishes for the past have completely shrouded the chances you wanted. I'M SITTING RIGHT HERE, and you've got a wrestling job to go to once a week. What the hell are you gonna do, keep talking about the things you lost and wished you had again? Again, Darcy... look in front of you and do something. You've been living your second chance for the past two months, but you haven't even seen it there to change your actions to the right ones.'' ''This hasn't been easy for me, Amy... and you haven't lifted a finger to make it any easier...'' Darcy responded, after what seemed like an eternity of silence. ''I came back to finish off what was left of my legacy, to put the past behind me and move on with my life. But now...'' Darcy sighed, deeply. ''Now every time I look at you I can't remember what goddamn year it is. I don't know if I'm looking into the past or the future.'' Now Darcy stood. Somehow he got the sense that what would happen next deserved its own chapter in his autobiography as he moved towards Amy. He knelt to approach her level, and took her hands in his. ''So I need you to tell me, Ames. I've got so many things running through my head that I no longer trust what I'm seeing. I need you to tell me, Amy...'' Darcy said, as he stared into her eyes. ''Am I looking into my past, or my future?'' ''I've always hated Valentine's Day, haven't you?'' ''...What?'' It seemed that Amy had broken the rather serious moment, but in her eyes she hadn't, ''Tomorrow's February 14th, that great big ball of pink trashy love crap. As Darcy Markson and Darcy Crisis, what you're looking at is the 'right now'. I'm the past and I'm the future, but the future's what you make of it. Not to be anti-climatic, but I'm tired and I think you need to get your head straight...'' She tried forcing a smile, though nervous as hell, ''Tomorrow, I'll wake up and I'll be one of two things. I'll walk out of this room and be the person I've been for the past four years, surrounded by your nostalgia and a sea of blind bullshit. OR... I'll walk out there to flowers that'll make me feel better about the shitty holiday, possibly flowers held by a man who's finally been struck by the lightning of foresight... and that man will be looking at a girl who's just taken the first step with him toward a future that hopefully won't be regretted in another five years.'' Amy stood up with confidence, placed a hand on Darcy's chest and, instead of any forward action, pushed him gently out the doorframe before closing - and locking - the oak barrier. ''I'll let you sleep on it,'' she mumbled through the crack by the lock, finally smiling knowing that it sacrificed no pride because it was entirely out of Darcy's sight. Earlier That Day... Brandan, Johnson, Scott, Marta, Katie, Deebo... all the old crowd. I'd been in car accidents and near-drownings with these people. Even though - and possibly because - I almost never saw them, they were some of my best friends. The only bad thing was that they'd gotten to know who I was when I wasn't single, during those times when I had a boy or a 'man' or whatever it happened to be. They knew me as a part of a unit who used their clean lifestyle to escape everything else. A bunch of straightedge, hardcore elitist assholes who I loved to death, with unconditional respect and no regrets. They were who I'd always wanted the willpower to be. Talking to Brandan was always the weirdest and best experience, because he was so raw. His band was really taking off, and he was finally happy with himself as he was. The funniest part was that he looked like he really should have been a wrestler, fighting up the ranks by my side. He went to the gym more than I'd ever imagined, and his completely tattooed torso was also ridiculously chiseled. His outfits were black and he had a kind of 'fresh outta jail', yard hard look about him... despite the fact that most of his sleeves were covered with tattooed butterflies and flowers. Completely gentle and sweet, he DID have a darker side that really, really hated girlfriends. That's why we normally didn't talk about relationships... he'd been burned by women as much as I'd been burned by men, and his command over a room of girl-pants wearing emo fuckbags was always considered a method of releasing stresses he'd felt since the age of 14. I can't pretend that he's not probably the most attractive person I know, but a relationship had always been out of the question - combining the fact that I'm the shittiest girlfriend on the planet with his high-maintanance emotional baggage would prove deadly for the both of us. Besides, he was much more interested in playing the role of my big brother. ''How's the wrasslin'?'' he joked, as per his usual entrance into conversation with me. I, of course, told him it was fine. I was winning and beating champions fresh out the gate. How could I not be happy with it? ''Darc' still naggin' ya?'' ''How'd...'' ''C'mon, Ames - he and I are still friends. Not as tight as you and I, but I've always kept a little spot in my heart for the dude.'' I couldn't help but shut up for a second. If they were still friends, then Bran knew I was... ... well, a weak bitch who crawled right back to a memory from four years before. NOT THE FIRST OR LAST TIME YOU'LL SEE 'VD' IN AN AIMZ ROLEPLAY ================================================================ If history had taught Amy Campbell anything, it was not to count for much on one Darcy Markson. The man had a flair for the dramatic, but he rarely showed that side of himself toward her in private. So as she exited Darcy's spare room on Valentine's Day, she had almost fully expected Darcy to renege on the ultimatum he'd been offered the night before, if he was even still home... but she was woefully mistaken. Amy awoke to find flowers everywhere. EVERYWHERE. How the man had managed to turn his living room into an arboretum on such short notice was a complete mystery to her, but far more impressive was how beautifully they had been arranged. Pictures of the two of them were interspersed throughout the arrangement, as if to celebrate the highs the two had shared together, both in and outside the ring. And the centerpiece itself...rather, himself, was Darcy sitting in the middle of it all, strumming the opening chords of 'Sara' by Fleetwood Mac on an acoustic guitar, gently reflecting the glimmering sunrays of the early Tuesday morning. Just as gently, Darcy sang along: Wait a minute baby, Stay with me awhile... Said you'd give me light, But you never told me about the fire... ''This...'' The little redhead, feeling not so much a 'woman' in her pink pajama pants and tanktop, wiped what seemed to be a tear from her eye as she stood in awe of it all. That little redhead, though, was still Amy Campbell. ''...is the gayest and most girly thing I've ever seen. Did cupid get trashed here last night or something? It looks like he puked all over the place...'' Just as Darcy's heart looked broken, Campbell chuckled and finished her thought, ''But... it IS more thoughtful than anything I thought you were capable of. I guess that's my roundabout way of being impressed, right?'' She smiled bashfully, and he knew full well that he was looking at a version of Aimz that nobody had ever seen. Her veil had been lowered and now she was spinning her wheels in an attempt to regain a sense of self. You know, that 'sense of self' that told her not to be happy or ever, ever, EVER in love again. But Valentine's Day was known for doing weird things to people, and Darcy Markson was even weirder. ''You know, I don't think it would kill you to be a girl once in awhile,'' came Darcy's response. ''What did you want, a live Hatebreed concert? 'Cause THAT'S romantic...'' ''I'm only a girl in the bedroom, and even then I'm hardly a lady.'' Recovering from her self-inflicted verbal wound, she sat on the petal-covered couch and bit the knee of her pajama pants in a nervous smile, simply staring at Darcy and all around him. ''Since I can't really... well... speak, I'm just gonna ask - where the hell did you get all of this overnight?'' ''That, m'lady, is a professional secret,'' Darcy answered, sounding as if he intended to treat her like a lady whether she liked it or not. ''All I'll say is that you better not be expecting me to work miracles like this on a regular basis.'' She smiled again, ''Well... we don't even know if we'll be around each other on a regular basis. After all, I'm kind of known for being that girl who does what she isn't supposed to and dates her peers. Usually I do it because it's easy, but I might need to make an exception and do it for more than just the sex this time. 'Cause God knows I've tried you out that way before, and I'm pretty sure I could get better from a walking erection like Adam Dick.'' Really, she wasn't capable of having an entirely normal or not-so-sarcastic conversation - especially not with dozens of rose petals sticking to her flannel ass. But her words were ones of hope, which caused Darcy to grin again. Sure, he looked like a complete tool, but it seemed to be turning out to be worth it. ''I suppose backhanded compliments are the best I'll ever get out of you,' Darcy said, setting aside the guitar, ''So thanks, babe... and happy Valentine's.' No, really - she couldn't stop. ''Oh, and don't think this'll get you that match of yours! OR into my pants. We're... you know, we've gotta be all slow and transitional and what not, right?'' she mumbled, her nervous glances giving away that Amy had absolutely no idea how to rekindle a relationship without a lot of melodramatic monologue and a man jumping on top of her, ''... I, uhh, don't really know what I'm talking about here. Words? Help?'' ''The way I see it, I consider every day we get through without throwing shit at each other to be a victory in and of itself. I say we handle things that way before we start throwing words around like 'relationship' and 'commitment.' And as far as getting into your pants goes... well, if an army of flowers and Fleetwood Mac can't get the job done, what the hell does a guy gotta do?'' ''Commitment and relationship, no no. Gotcha... But I think you're a few decades behind on what gets a girl going...'' Let's end this with the only thing that really rings out as NOT BULLSHIT in this entire work... ''It's 2006, you need razorblades and Bright Eyes to get the gal.''
