Ms. Anderson I Presume...

19 September 2006

Date: 09/19/06 The deepest human defeat suffered by human beings is constituted by the difference between what one was capable of becoming and what one has in fact become. ---Ashley Montagu The last few weeks have been lonely ones for Colby Korver. But it wasn’t supposed to be this way. It was supposed to be his shining moment. A last minute addition to the East team, he had so very much to gain in the Jewel of the Seas East versus West face-off. It was surely the perfect scenario for him to seize control and propel himself to the very forefront of the Atlantic Wrestling Club. But he completely and utterly failed. And it wasn’t just that he lost the match, it was the manner by which he squandered his chance, his opportunity…his golden ticket. The night before the pay-per-view, Korver found himself back in a very dark place. A place he had hoped to forever avoid. You see, Colby Korver hasn’t always been the poster boy for alcohol abuse and notches on a bedpost. Not so long ago, in a high school not so very far away, Colby Hughes Korver was the boy most girls are still waiting to sweep them off their feet. As a junior, he captained the football team as its starting middle linebacker and dated the head cheerleader. Although many assumed otherwise, he remained abstinent voluntarily, controlling his hormones for the good of his future. He spent Sunday mornings in church and Sunday afternoons at her family’s. It was the ideal relationship. They never fought. They never cheated. They never knew saw it coming… Then along came senior year, and with it, Katherine Anderson. The product of a broken home, broken parents and a broken public school system, Katie Anderson transferred just in time for the start of the school year, but not soon enough to select her class preferences. So the guidance counselor did what she could, and just managed to squeeze in one final elective—Colby’s 7th period Athletics class. And while Korver and the rest of the football team used this time to lift weights and watch game film, Katie Anderson used it for something entirely different. She was there to find her own golden ticket. And her very first day, as she watched Korver nearly pass out after squatting an inhuman amount of weight, only to bring his teammates to tears under a minute later with a timely quip, she knew that he was as golden as they came. And so her relentless pursuit began. When her seemingly innocent flirting came up empty, she moved on to “accidental” brushes of the skin and more blatant sexual innuendos. When those were ignored, she would leave notes in his locker detailing the “perks” of giving in to her wiles. But amazingly enough, Colby Korver did not concede, even under a barrage of propositions and suggestions the likes of which would make the cast of Nip/Tuck blush. And if only the story ended there, Korver probably would have lived happily ever after with his beautiful wife, a white picket fence and 2.5 children. But Katie Anderson didn’t believe in fairy tales. The turning point in the process of growing up is when you discover the core of strength within you that survives all hurt. ---Max Lerner, The Unfinished Country, 1950 Hank Cobb: “Korver, what’s gotten into you man? It was just a match, one match. It’s like Coach Spear used to say, “You’re never gonna win ‘em all, but that don’t mean you shouldn’t play like it.” Or somethin’ like that. You know I’m your boy, so what’s the deal?” Colby Korver: “I told you not to worry about it. It’s just something I’ve gotta deal with on my own. It has nothing to do with you anyway.” Hank Cobb: “Nothin’ to do with me? Don’t forget we’re a team, Korv. We got a big match on Fresh and a losing streak to get of our backs. We’ve been wrasslin’ like a couple of sissy babies lately and I’m getting’ pretty sick of it.” Colby Korver: “Well no shit Cobb. But you going Jack Bauer on me isn’t going to fix that either. So unless you’ve got a matter of national security you haven’t told me yet, I strongly suggest you get out of my face.” Hank Cobb: “Listen jackass, you know I’m just tryin’ to help you out. I know how hard it can be to get over someone like Kat…” Colby Korver: “You finish that sentence and I swear to god I’ll knock your big ass the fuck out right here and now.” Hank Cobb: “Alright, alright. I’m done. This obviously isn’t helping. But then again, neither is the pity party you’ve been hosting for the last couple of weeks. I’m fixin’ to head to the gym, but you better get your ass in gear or the only company you’ll be keepin’ is your buddies in the unemployment line. I’m out of here.” Colby Korver: “Don’t worry about me, I’ll be ready to fight.” Hank Cobb: “It’s not your body I’m questioning. You just better get your head screwed on straight. And fast.” Colby Korver: “The only screwing you need to worry about are me and my fucking groupies after I put the exclamation point on The Last Word tomorrow night.” Hank Cobb: “I hope so, boss.” Colby Korver: “Don’t hope. Know.” I fear we have awakened a sleeping giant and filled him with a terrible resolve. ---Admiral Isoroku Yamamoto, December 7, 1941
