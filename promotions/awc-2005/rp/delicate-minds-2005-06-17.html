Delicate Minds

17 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/06/05 The deep pulsating burn of defeat haunts the glow in my eyes. This hand never hurt as much are your slap, never will, never so red. Because this is the truth, this is the time to die, to let go and float amongst the gasses in the sky. This is not me trying to make this seem like a rhyme, this is just me trying to express this to you the best way I can. Please don't ruin my concentration. I'm on this level and I don't want to stop, like the blood running through your veins or catching yourself in the headlights of an on coming car. Stop. Heart skips a beat. Never felt so...alive. The lights go out in my mind, and in my surroundings. The ebb and flow of the stings catch my attention, and I hold my hand up to them. Why does it hurt so much? And why do I allow it to hurt as much as it does now? This never should have happened, forever thinking that theres something better, but I can never get it back. I lean against the wall, this position is just as painful as the last and gives me the same memories as the last. This isn't going to go away for a long time but...maybe thats a good thing. Because if I hold on to it, and decide to nurture it, I can turn this pain into my own glory. I don't want to hear the laughter anymore, infact, I don't want to hear anything any more, I just want to block it out. Out of ears, out of mind, mind out, ears out, listen. I eventually lie back on the white sheets that covered my bed...or was it red? It didn't matter now really. This is contemplation time, fuck the spelling and grammar of it all, its really not that important when you think about it, its all about how it comes across. Even though there are starving people out there, which I realise when I walk to the window and look out at the landscape of never ending lego-like skyscrapers, ugly looking things. Where was I? Ah yes, starving people. The images suddenly flash Even though there are starving people out in that big pile of piss called a world, or is this too depressing? Continue anyway, break through it if you have to. Even though there are starving people out there, i'm the one with the most hunger. FACT. The Irish, funny kind of people, retards, not politically correct, but who cares? Retards are also funny kind of people, so funny they make me laugh every now and then, but isn't that just the whole point of the humour? Who the hell put me here, who put me here to laugh at them? I'll tell you who, the person asking the goddamn questions, that persons me. All these people trying to screw with this, trying to make me make a mistake, and believe me, my concentration could slip so easily its untrue. Food, stuffed. There it goes again. Whys there flashing at my side I ask? I look down, I see the flashing lights of the police siren and then the faint sound begins to drift, that question appears to have been answered. I've got something for you right now, it just popped into my mind now but I want to save it for a later date, its that good, so i'll continue with the sirens. The bigger picture is, who are they chasing? Why are they chasing? Where will it all end? What is the cause? Who, why, where, what? My point exactly The music is so far back in my mind its the background, its the walls surrounding me, surrounding me so close, and yet not suffocating me, i'm letting it all in, suffocating would be against my will, but my wills fine. How's your will doing? I'd like to think throughout this this all makes some sense to you, but even if it doesn't, you know where i'm coming from? Because none of us are understood, not really, not by anybody, and thats true of me. But this isn't supposed to be misunderstood, thats why its been put onto this paper....hold it up...white, how many lives have I destroyed for this piece? Never mind, stay focused damnit. As I was saying, I want you to understand this, I want to understand this so much that you let it consume you, you let it take over your body, your lives, your soul, and you learn to live it, because only then....do we grow.
