Welcome to My World (edit)

5 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/07/05 'F....E....A....R' - Just four little letters, pretty much nothing when alone....but when put together, you have formed a word that even the strongest of mortals experience some point in their lives. It doesn't matter whether you have intellect to rival Stephen Hawking, You can experience fear. It doesn't matter if you are a battle hardened warrior like Crimson O’malec, You can experience fear. Fear is an inevitable part of everyone's life...along with the other inevitabilities such as death, pain and taxes. With the latter aside, it's these things that are forever running through the mind of one man and the term man is used very loosely....He's been through pain and suffering, he has experienced fear, he's faced death.....but where mere mortals would give up to everything they have seen and experienced....there are the occasional people who don't give into the instinct to quit, they let everything that's been thrust upon them to manifest deep within. To grow like a cancer within the very recesses of their soul.....suffocating it from within, until it's nothing but a host to the darkness...now consumed, the soul begins work on it's next target - The mind. This takes a lot longer than the soul, the darkness now living within the soul tortures it with disturbing thoughts, instincts and images. Slowly driving it's new host insane with paranoia, irrationality, bitterness and rage....Utilising the mind, it creates unimaginable nightmares and hallucinations, ones impossible to wake up from as you experience most of them while wide awake. Leaving you with two choices.....To embrace the darkness, or escape it from the only means available....your own death. This one man stared death in the face on one dark, stormy night and chose the former.....since then, he has never looked back.....since that night, he is no longer the man most people once knew.....He is consumed with the desire to inflict all that he has suffered, all of life's inevitablities on the people of the decaying world.....Pain, suffering, fear and death.....He is something else, something far above anything currently residing within HOW or the entire universe. He is unique, he is unparrelled, he is peerless.....He is The Angel of Death....He is Gabriel....and no-one in AWC, not even the men he faces this week, The Farmer and Drake Coleman....or even Piere H. himself can possibly be prepared for what is about to happen from here on out!!!!! Date: July 4th 2005 – 1 day after Solarized Time: 02:37am Location: Unknown..... ....I see a flash of lightning and hear the almost deafening sound of thunder....It lights up the dark night sky, a storm seems to be brewing overhead, no stars can be seen due to the sky being covered with dark clouds. The bright full moon can be seen though, on the horizon from my vantage point though....shining bright down onto the city below us. We ar clearly very high up, but despite the great vantage point and the shining moon, it is still two dark to see what city we are residing over. There are building lights in the distance, but in the area we seem to be located, no lights can be seen coming from any buildings. A power cut of some sort seems to have occurred, maybe due to the storm..... Voice: Beautiful isn't it..... A voice says suddenly, causing us to turn suddenly, as we do....we wobble a little bit and look down suddenly to see just how high up we are. A sense of vertigo suddenly hits, causing us to shake a little bit....looking up suddenly to divert our attention, we just about manage to keep balance, and stop ourselves from falling over the edge, by forcing ourselves against the wall. How we got here though is a total mystery. Everything before the flashes of lightning and thunder is a blank....like that was the point we awoke from a deep sleep. I look now to my right and see another man standing next to me, He is slightly shorter than me, maybe two or three inches and seeing as how I'm six foot two inches, I'll let you work out his height. I've never been much at guessing weights, so don't even attempt it and it's two dark to really take notice of his features and clothes, although I assume he's wearing dark items. I can tell he has short blonde hair though, and by his breathing, he is very much afraid. I can't blame him for that though.....so am I. The crisp breeze flows around, causing our skin to break out in goose bumps and our bodies to shiver. Voice: The darkness, I mean....It's just beautiful isn't it. The voice says again....At first I think maybe my mind is hallucinating. Creating the voice itself for some reason, making me think I am mad. That might also explain how I am up so high on a building at, wait, what is the time, I think to myself......I glance down at my wrist instinctively to see I am wearing a silver watch on my left wrist. It's too dark to make out the time, it must be late at night though. In this time I see I am wearing blue jeans, black boots and a white top, it's not very thick so doesn't prevent the cold from reaching my skin very well. For the love of god, why can't I remember anything.....Just then, the other man to my right speaks.... Man: Where's that voice coming from? The voice seems recognisable....Just as his words reach my ears, a flood of memories sweep through my mind....Everything suddenly becomes clear. My name is John King, I am a professional cameraman....and the person to my right is an audio technician I work with....His name being.... John: Chris? Chris Tucker? Man: Yeah.... The man replies, this gives me a little comfort, as does the fact he mentioned the voice....at least I am not mad. Although, In that case though, it leaves no answers as to why we are both up on this ledge on the side of a building sometime at night. John: I don't know where it's coming from....how did we get here? Chris: I don't know....The last thing I remember is leaving that bar and heading towards our car. I can hear the coldness Chris must be feeling in his voice, it causes him to pause....Just then, that memory returns to my mind.....thank god whatever amnesia I was suffering from was only temporary....Maybe it was alcohol induced, after all, waking up on the side of this building would sober even the worst suffers of alcoholism in a heartbeat, but question still remains how we got here. John: Why can't we remember?!? I ask, urgency in my voice now....Panic sweeping through me, Fear accompanying it....My chest tightens and my head starts pounding. I look towards Chris, my breath shortening and my balance fading, I feel myself falling forward....the vertigo returning to run alongside the fear, pain and panic.....I'm gonna die up here....or rather, down there, I say to myself within my own thoughts as i fall towards the edge of the building, until suddenly a hand reaches out and grabs me, I look along to see the arm belongs to Chris.... Chris: I don't know buddy, but we need to find away off and I don't think that's the best way. Relax...!! Easier said than done, I think to myself....my childhood fear of heights returning from whichever place I sent it when I thought I conquered itn six years ago with a bunjee jump on my twenty-first birthday. It was Chris' present to me actually, he'd done opne two years earlier for his own twenty-first and highly recommended it. I feel myself, mostly thanks to Chris' hand, push back against the wall.....Just then, the voice returns again... Voice: Bravo!! The voice is followed by a weak applause, this applause reveals more to the location of the source.....to my left, but a lot further left to which Chris stands. I'm breathing heavier than Chris now, but he still breathes heavy upon hearing the voice again, until then, he seemed to have calmed a little. Voice: I don't think that is the way off either, although it would have been interesting to see if you had survived. I highly doubt it though.....how about going up? I then roll my eyes upwards, feeling too scared to move and a little worried about what I may see....but I had no reason to be, all i see is the top of the building, which is about two story’s up. Even jumping we'd fall far short. We're trapped! Voice: No? Well how about the fire escape that's just to my left....It's a bit of a journey though, you think you are up to it. Chris then shouts a response, not the one I would have shouted, but probably the only one that was going through his head at the time. A logical one, now that I think about it. Chris: Who are you? Then another.....again, logical. Chris: Did you bring us here....? Chris seems to be on a roll, He suddenly strikes me for the first time, in all the time I have known him as someone who is calm within a crisis or at least is good at pretending to be. Chris: Why? There is a long tense silence, one that lasts so long I begin to wonder if the person speaking to us through the darkness has disappeared. I want to look, but I am too scared to move my head....and even if i did, I don't think I'd be able to see in the darkness. The silence is then broken by the sound of the voice speaking back towards us, he was just playing with us. Voice: Who am I?....I am that which you can not comprehend..... It's after these words are spoken that I hear a loud footstep against the concrete on which we stand...coming from mine and Chris' left. I then feel something on my head, I look up instinctively, causing myself to stumble forward a little again, thanks to the suddenly gust of chilling wind that flows around us. Thank god that Chris still have his arm across my chest, holding me against the wall. I'm still looking upwards to see the clouds opening.....it's starting to rain. I know this by the fact further things continue to hit my head and face.....drops of rain. Voice: Did I bring you here?....Yes, it was indeed I who bought you here..... Another few footsteps are heard by my ears now, as the rain starts to get heavier and more frequent. The wind picking up aswell, although dropping in temperature. Chris then whispers to me, but I don't hear what he says due to the elements which continue to rise in force. Voice: Why did I bring you here?.....Well, that's simple. So you can realise your destinies!! Chris has since repeated the words he is trying to whisper a few more times. the fear in his voice has reached fever pitch now and I can feel his hand which supports me trembling. Lightning flashes over head a few times and when it ceases for a few moments, the rain and wind lets up along with it....just in time for me to catch Chris' final attempt at whispering to me. Chris: Look!! Realising I have no choice now, I slowly turn my head on Chris' command....making sure not to look down. My hair getting soaking wet now, along with my clothes....my body trembling with cold now, the wind around me feels like ice against my skin, thanks to the rain....which are reducing my clothes to an even weaker state of defense against it. As my eyes turn to follow Chris' gaze, the footsteps get louder and evermore closer. Chris: What do you mean, our destinies? Just then, a flash of lightning flashes overhead, revealing a man walking towards us. Not bothered about the height on what he is standing, or the rain and wind or his clothes which are just as soaking wet as mine and Chris'....The man looks somewhat familiar to me, from his long black hair that flows down to just above his shoulders.....to his entire black ensemble....right down to his unlaced boots which hang loosely around his feet. His top is dripping wet and although probably a tight long-sleeve shirt, it seems even more tight now. The next flash of lightning brings his eyes to my attention....They are staring darkly right through Chris and I....I feel him pushing back against me, wanting to retreat....but my own feet don't respond, I'm frozen to the spot....watching the man edge closer and closer with every flash of lightning....moving in seemingly slow motion over the gargoyle in front of him and then the straight path....only six feet away now.....five.....four.....three.....two.... Chris: For fuck's sake John....Run!! The fear in his voice snaps me out of my trance like state, which is now partially due to recognition.....I recognise this man, I think to myself the second before i force my legs to go backwards....Chris follows me....The man smirks sinisterly as he comes towards us. Who is he? I ask myself again..... Approaching Man: Going so soon.....but the fun is just beginning my friends. Friends? I ask myself....does this man know us?....Is he a friend of ours? Is he just from a set of memories my mind has yet to recover from deep within my own mind....if so, why his he trapping us on the top of a tall building. Inches from falling to our deaths, forcing us backwards on a narrow ledge. Remember goddamn it, remember!! I scream within my own mind....but my mind fails me, choosing to remember and think about one word over and over....Trapped!! Chris: Why have you stopped? I hear Chris ask, he turns around before I can answer though and sees the reason for himself. I have backed into another gargoyle....There's nowhere to go now. We really are trapped.... Chris: Climb over it!!!! I hear Chris shout....the pressure and fear washing over me stronger than ever before. Today started off like any normal day, I'm a normal, plain, ordinary guy. How can this be happening to me, it only happens in movies and TV shows....Not real life. I think to myself as the fear grips me like a hand on my shoulder. The strong touch makes me think of the Grim Reaper and I feel myself going over a familiar saying, this time the words come out from my mouth in the form of a mumble under my breath.... John: I'm going to die!!! I then realise I am actually being touched on the shoulder....by Chris, who has turned to face me. The fear in his eyes speaks volumes to me and makes me start to turn around, but I am stopped suddenly be the piercing sound of an agonising scream within my ear....coming from Chris' lips. The scream seems to echo for an eternity on the wind around me. All instincts take me to a childhood memory when I was being beaten in the playground when I was six for some reason. My body goes to produce the same reaction i did back then....piss myself, but for some reason, I don't do that, instead I turn around to see Chris stumble around a little. I look down and see a knife protruding from the back of his right knee....he looks up at me in a second and then falls over the edge....his arm oustretched....I freeze. Watching my best friend and co-worked tumble to his death...My body then acts of it's on free will and with no thought of mine..... John: NOOOOOO!!! I scream, the adrenaline pumping through my veins and my hand reaches out and grabs Chris'....He swings hand into the side of the building, still shrieking in agony due to the knife in his knee gap. The force of his descent forces me down to the concrete, so I am now laying on my stomach. It feels cold against my soaking wet clothes. rain runs down my arm....my hand and onto Chris' own. Making it harder to hold on, but I am determined.... Chris: Please! Don't let go.....Please!! Chris says, somewhat tearfully.....Never before have I heard such fear in his voice....seen so much fear in his eyes. I use both hands to grip his one left hands. He continues to try and reach with his right hand, but keeps falling short. Come on, I say to myself over and over, using all my strength to try and pull him up. I estimated now that Chris is a little bit heavier than me. His weight pulling hard on the muscles in my arms and my body it's self which because of the rain is moving slightly forward....Chris reaches up again with his other hand and in what can only be described as a miracles manages to reach the edge of the ledge. The weight shifts slightly and I feel myself winning in the struggle against gravity. This victory lasts only briefly though as Chris lets out another eardrum shattering scream....This time it's by a boot pushing down on his hand, I can hear the boot pushing it using the heel into the concrete. The sound of flesh scraping on the concrete makes me cringe. Man: Are you scared?!? Do you feel the fear running through your veins?!? The man says down towards Chris, I can't look up as I am focusing too much on blocking out the sounds of fingers breaking under the boot and Chris' screams and of course pulling him up to the ledge, and safety....although how much safer it is up here, I don't know. Just then, a click goes off in my head. A moment of realization, an epiphany, which occurs just as light night flashes again, out of the corner of my eye I catch a glimpse of the man again, closer than before....it's then that I know him. I realize who it is.....He's that wrestler that just arrived to AWC a few days ago...... Chris: Yes, I'm scared.....Please don't do this to me!! Chris shouts, the pain and sadness in his voice along with fear is heartbreaking to a friend as close to him as I am. I struggle with all my might to hold on although I feel him slipping a little. The man lifts his boot off now, allowing Chris to pull his broken hand down by his side again, screaming at me to help him and not let him die....I struggle harder and harder. I cannot let go....I will not. I slide a little more along the ledge....the rain pouring down harder than before. I feel myself mumbling to god under my breath, asking him why is he doing this to us. Man: God? No....Not even god can help you now.....I above you mere mortals, I am above god himself....Your destinies, along with those of HOW belong to me, they are in my hands. Both your destinies tonight include making examples of you both, examples of fear and what the future holds for those who oppose my destiny. John: Why, Gabriel, Why? That's right isn't it....You're Gabriel aren't you? He smirks at me, as I continue to watch him out of the corner of my eyes. Adrenaline running through my veins.....Maybe I'm the one out of me and Chris that somehow finds courage under fire....Some reason trying to distract Gabriel....for what reason, I don't know.....it's instinct. He then seems to experience a moment of realization much like my own, as Chris shouts up to me to ask what I'm doing. My grip staying tight, but I can't hold much longer and we both know it. Gabriel: You are correct, I am Gabriel Reynolds.....The fact you know me, pleases me greatly....but I don't know you. Are you The AWC cameraman or the AWC audio guy? Gabriel says, confirming what I suspected, but baffling me at the same time as to how he knows we are either......Chris calls up again to ask what I'm doing, to pull him up. My arms are tired and aching now, every instinct in my body says I can't hold on anymore, but I'm not gonna let go....I can't. John: I'm the cameraman.... I find myself saying, trying to blink the rain out of my eyes.....wondering if now I'll find out some answers to the million questions running through my mind...Gabriel smirks again. I look at Chris now, before back to Gabriel out of the corner of my eye....staring from it into his dark eyes. Gabriel: As to why I am doing this......all will become clear, but only to one of you. I don't like the sound of that...... Gabriel: You see, everyone has a destiny......and it's my destiny to help people fulfill their destinies....and it's one of your destinies to die here tonight, after I put fear into you both. Fear beyond anything you could ever imagine.....and before you ask why, that's another question for the survivor to ask later. The only question to ask yourself now, who's destiny is it to help me and who's it is to be nothing more than a sacrifice.....To my new cause....My new destiny. John: Who? I ask suddenly...screaming it almost.....Gabriel looks from myself to Chris who is screaming not to let go still. How I've held on this long is something I continue to ask myself amongst other things. Gabriel then brushes his wet hair back from his face, to which it was originally clinging.....The smirk - which seems to epitomize the sadistic evil within him - is still ever present on his face. Gabriel: That can be answered with one action, as I believe in the expression.....actions speak louder than words..... It is in that moment, as quick as the lightning flashing overhead.....Gabriel reaches to his boot and pulls out something which is revealed as a sharp blade as he raises it into the air and light reflects off of the sharp surface in the moonlight. It plunges towards me....at that moment, I realise I am the sacrifice, it's my destiny to die in a bizarre twist of events that climax on the top of an unknown building, never will I learn the reason why. My eyes close and my whole life flashes before me like a movie playing on a screen, only the inside of my eyelids is the movie theatre screen. I feel the blade plunge into me, causing unimaginable pain....I let out a scream to rival anything heard from Chris within the course of the last hour or so.....although all sense of time went a long time ago, so any amount of time could have passed..... I open my eyes as the pain runs through my body and pierces my brain like the worse migraine possible. It forces my eyes open, while still screaming....My life has stopped playing on my eyelid theatre....and I stare straight ahead to see blood oozing from my right hand....Gabriel's hand resting on the handle, I following the arm up slowly, pain searing through my body, getting worse by the second....He then continues to smirk as he places his second hand onto the blade's handle and twists it, opening the wound even more and causing the blood to flow freely from it. He drips off onto the concrete and through my fingers onto Chris.....Gabriel then pulls the knife out in on motion, causing blood to run even more freely after a spurt of blood follows the retracting knife.... Chris: Nooo!!! Please?!? Chris shouts, very weakly now but still as urgent.....Chris and I can both feel him slipping through my grip now, due to only having one strong hand, that one only lasting until Gabriel plunges the knife into my other hand, stabbing so hard, it drive right through and the blade comes out through the other side. Blood flows out from both side, Gabriel again twists the blade to inflict the maximum amount of damage....The pain doubles in intensity as it flows through my body at that moment and it is that moment that my grip finally fails and Chris, with one final scream and look of fear in his eyes begins the long plummet down.....I scream his name for what seems like hours as I watch him in seemingly slow motion begin to fall towards the ground below.....I watch until he's just about to hit the ground, and then look away. The sound of cars screeching to a stop and in some cases crashing into each other can be heard, along with the sounds of people screaming and shouting. I look at Gabriel....He's on his feet know, using his wet shirt to wipe the blood from the knife.... John: You mother-fucking piece of shit!!! You murdered him.....I'm going to make sure you pay for this!! Gabriel: You're going to do no such thing except that of which I tell you.....From now on your abilities and skills are going to serve a real purpose....A higher purpose. You're mouth will remain silent unless it's spoken to first, by me.....or you and everyone you ever cared about will suffer the fate of your friend. Such is your destiny.... Gabriel says as he places the knife back into a section of his boot and stands on the edge of the ledge.....staring down as the chaos below....I can now see flashing lights in the reflection of his dark eyes....Probably ambulances or police....or both. Gabriel smirks yet again and looks back towards me...as I continue to lay on the floor, bllod pouring from my hands, pain pouring through my body and tears silently pouring down my cheeks.... Gabriel: Let's go...They'll be here shortly!! John: I'm going nowhere with you..... Gabriel: Oh you will.....or not only will your friend's death be in vain, but so will yours and everyone you've ever known and it'll all be on your hands. Gabriel then turns and begins to walk away, as I slowly get to my feet, despite the pain in my hands....I find the strength to obey his commands. I then begin stumbling slowly after him, staying as close to the wall as I can. Dripping blood everywhere, I keep my gaze as hard as I can from looking down........ John: What do you want from me? I ask as Gabriel begins climbing over the gargoyle which I saw him first climb over, before heading towards the fire escape.....without even pausing for a second, he answers me question..... Gabriel: You'll find out.....Soon enough!! TO BE CONTINUED!!!!!!!!!
