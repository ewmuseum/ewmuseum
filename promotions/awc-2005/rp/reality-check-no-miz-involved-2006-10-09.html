Reality Check: No Miz Involved

9 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/09/06 Hank Cobb: I gotta be honest with you, man, this ain’t exactly workin’ out the way I thought it would around here. Colby Korver: Well no shit Cobb. We can’t land a victory to save our lives; we haven’t so much as sniffed a title shot since we got here; and now we’re stuck on Fresh!FUCKING!burst. I’ve had just about enough of this shit. Cobb: I’m just as sick as you are, Korv, but I ain’t about to up and quit. I love this man. Stepping through the curtain; slappin’ hands with the fans; throwin’ haymakers—I was born for this. I mean, what else am I gonna do? Move back home and work the farm? That ain’t the live for me. And you know that. So you might as well stuff your sorries in a sack, mister. I’m here to stay. Korver: Well maybe it’s not the life for me, Hank. I bust my ass day in and day out, for what? So the fans can restock at the concession stand while I’m bleeding and sweating in front of their empty seats? That’s bullshit, and you know it. Cobb: Yeah, maybe it is. But I ain’t gonna cry about it. Maybe we ain’t givin’ them anything worth seein’ to begin with. We just got to keep puttin’ in our time. When’s the last time you went to the gym? Korver: Friday. Cobb: Exactly. Korver: Exactly??? That was only two fucking days ago, Hitler. Cobb: So why haven’t you gone since? Korver: Dude, I’ve been busy. Cobb: Horseshit. You wasted both mornin’s sleepin’ off hangovers and both nights chasin’ tail. Korver: I mean, you can’t really call either night a waste. I know you saw the ass on that… Cobb: See, now how in tarnation is that gonna help us in the ring? Korver: Well it might not, but it definitely helped me relax. Cobb: That’s the last thing either of us need. Maybe if we’d won a match in the last year. Maybe if we’d proven anything around here. But last time I checked, we were “relaxing” at the bottom of the fuckin’ Alliance Division. Korver: Easy big guy, you’re going to short out your pacemaker. Cobb: Everything’s a big joke to you, isn’t it Korver? Well the big joke around here is us. All the God-given talent in the world don’t mean shit if we don’t use it. And we ain’t used it in a long, long time. So let’s turn this thing around, man. There’s no reason we can’t start right here, right now. Who cares if we’re stuck on Burst? Let’s give them a reason to make sure it don’t ever happen again. Korver: You know, for a big country bumpkin, you occasionally do make your points. Some of that sounded a bit rehearsed though. Next time don’t rush through the inspirational speech, it kind of ruins the effect. Cobb: How bout the right here, right now part? Korver: No, that was good. Keep that. Cobb: Now let’s just make sure from now we keep that inspirational junk to a minimum, and the ass kickin’ to a maximum. Korver: …… Cobb: Too much? Korver: Too much.
