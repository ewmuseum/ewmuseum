Chronical Five: New Canada

17 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/17/05 [The scene opens on a cold, typical Toronto morning. Snow is slowly swirling in the air as Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric the Black Goth are looking at Mapleleaf’s pool right outside his hilltop mansion. The waters are swirling cold, and snowflakes are dissolving on top of the surface. The two are shaking their heads as they stare into their reflections. The trees, stripped of leaves, surround the mansion, a beckoning call of the middle of winter. Mapleleaf, wearing his red and white Team Canada jacket and designer jeans, spits to one side, while Cedric fixes his straight black, goth style hair.] Mapleleaf: Tis a tragedy. I pay all this money for a pool, and it’s too cold up here to use it. Cedric: Price you pay for living in Canada. Mapleleaf: Watch your mouth! This is the best place on earth. Cedric: Just saying… Mapleleaf: Say nothing! [Bruce, the newest butler at Casa de Mapleleaf, walks out, carrying two glasses of ice cold lemonade. He is a young, strapping buck with long black hair and a thin face, fresh out of Butler school. His green nature is evident from the goofy, child-like expression on his face.] Bruce: I thought I’d bring these out for you guys, Mr. Mapleleaf. Mapleleaf: Is that…Lemonade? Bruce: Yes, sir! Mapleleaf: When did I hire you? Bruce: Last week, sir. Mapleleaf: And you want to make a big splash around here? Bruce: Yes, sir, I do. Mapleleaf: Are you aware that it is almost 10 degrees out? Bruce: Well— Mapleleaf: AND YOU BRING TWO GLASSES OF GODDAMN LEMONADE! Here, splash this! [Mapleleaf pushes Bruce into the pool. He screams and squirms (A few days from now, he will die from pneumonia.)] Mapleleaf: Hmph. Come on, Cedric, I have an idea. [Cedric and Mapleleaf enter the house they walk up to the fireplace and crowd around. They are gathered in Mapleleaf’s trophy room which is…empty for now. The room is covered in wood paneling with a wood floor and a red Chinese rug in the middle. Usually Mapleleaf doesn’t buy from the Chinese, but he can make exceptions because he is better than you.] Mapleleaf: I have an idea. How much do you think it will cost to buy the Bahamas? Cedric: Umm, you can’t just buy a nation. Mapleleaf: And why not? I can easily buy it and enslave the citizens! Cedric: There are a few islands in the South Pacific for sale. I think we should seriously consider buying one. Mapleleaf: Good idea, let’s fly down today…TO THE MAPLE JET! Hours Later [Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric are standing on a beautiful island, resembling Hawaii. Palm trees are overshadowing them, and the sunset looks just right in the end of the sky. Mapleleaf smiles and plants a Canadian flag. The flag blows gently in the wind as it sinks into the sand. Ahead of them, there is a dense forest of trees. Behind them, the beautiful Pacific Ocean shimmers and gleams like some kind of eternal jewel.] Mapleleaf: I proclaim this…NEW CANADA! Cedric: How original. Mapleleaf: Can’t believe I own my own island! Thank God for ebay! Cedric: 14 million was a bargain. Mapleleaf: You rich? Cedric: No, I make minimum wage. Mapleleaf: Then don’t talk like you are! Cedric: Right-o. Mapleleaf: What the fuck are these things on the trees? Cedric: Coconuts. Gotta crack them on something hard. [Mapleleaf takes one, winds it back, and smacks Cedric on the head. The nut opens, and Cedric flies back like a bag of rocks. Mapleleaf sips the milk. He swigs and smiles.] Mapleleaf: This tastes like coconut rum! Cedric: OWWWW!!! Mapleleaf: What?! Get up you lazy piece of shit! This is no time for a nap! Cedric: My head is bleeding! Mapleleaf: Still…hey, what is that sound? [Shadows start to form, about 50 natives appear out of the woods. Mapleleaf and Cedric look at each other as the natives appear. They are wearing minimal clothing (some are naked). They have dark skin and long, black hair. Some of them are carrying flint weapons. A wet spot appears in the crotch area of Mapleleaf’s designer jeans.] Native: TOOT! Mapleleaf: The hell? NativeA: TOOT, OOT! Mapleleaf: Is he making fun of me?! Cedric: Shut up, Mapes! Native: SOOT TOOT TOOT OOT! Mapleleaf: RUN! [Mapleleaf starts to run, leaving wounded Cedric behind. The natives grab him and lead him away. Another group takes Cedric. They take the two men through the woods. The woods become thicker and denser as they walk through. Finally, they reach a small village. The village has houses made of mud and sod, and a large fire in the center. It is beginning to get dark, and the torches and fire are providing most of the light. Mapleleaf is thrown into one cage on one side, and Cedric is thrown into another.] Mapleleaf: Shit, what do we do now? Cedric: No clue. You were the one who came up with this idea. Mapleleaf: Umm…guys? We can work this out. I can give you all autographed T-shirts. The shirts say…Stick It, Neeguhs…but you all don’t know what that means. [The natives group around and glare at Mapleleaf.] Mapleleaf: They’re angry little negros, aren’t they? Native: KNEE….GROW? Mapleleaf: NEGRO! Native: Oh, that’s it, man…you’re gonna get some lead in yo’ ass. Mapleleaf: You speak English? Native: Of course! My name is Toot-Oot-Soot. This is my brother, Toot-Oot-Soot, and my wife, Toot-Oot-Soot. We are the Toot-Oot-Soot tribe. Mapleleaf: Well, I own all your asses now, so let me out! Toot-Oot-Soot: Hell no! No white devil is gonna tell me what to do! Mapleleaf: Let me out now, or this while village becomes the Patrick Mapleleaf condos! This shit shanty-town will be covered in cement six inches thick! Toot-Oot-Soot: We will let you go…but it will be more money than you can afford. Mapleleaf: I’m rich as fuck…shoot away. Toot-Oot-Soot: One thousand dollars. Mapleleaf[stifling laughter]: Wh-what? Toot-Oot-Soot: That’s the rule. We will leave and you can go. [Mapleleaf takes out 10 hundred dollar bills from his wallet.] Mapleleaf: Here ya go…1000 bucks! Toot-Oot-Soot: Really? Mapleleaf: Canadian. Toot-Oot-Soot: Wow…umm…thanks! Here you go. [The door to the cages open. Mapleleaf and Cedric are free men! The scene fades, and reopens with just Mapleleaf and Cedric, sitting around the fire in the village. It is empty, and the time is much later. Mapleleaf yawns and stretches out his arms.] Mapleleaf: Well, they’re stupider than I thought! Cedric: Because they don’t realize 1000 dollars isn’t much? Mapleleaf: No, because it was fake money! MUHAHAHAH! Cedric: What?! Mapleleaf: That’s right, sweetheart. Fake money. It had MY face on it! It was the Mapleleaf dollars I printed up for a short time last year. Cedric: So you jipped a bunch of poor natives! Mapleleaf: I saved your black ass, didn’t I?! Cedric: Well, true. Maybe we should keep this village as a tribute to the previous owners. I mean, we DID invade their land! And…hey, Mapes?! [Mapleleaf is already kicking down the houses and buildings with his shoe. They crumble to the ground into dust.] Mapleleaf: This is where the mansion will be. Cedric: But this island is huge! Mapleleaf: Yeah, and I need enough space for a Pizza Hut! Cedric: You truly are evil. [Fade]
