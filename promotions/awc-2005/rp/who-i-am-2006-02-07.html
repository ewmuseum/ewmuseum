Who I am

7 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/07/06 ~~~ 7 years ago ~~~ “Jamie you’re gonna be late! It’s the last day of school, and you have done so well this year. You don’t wanna miss the chance to say goodbye to all your friends do you?” Jamie rolls over in bed, squinting as the sun flows through the blinds into the bedroom. He rubs his eyes smiling a bit as he shouts back “I’m up…and besides mom, if they are my friends I will see them after this anyhow.” Jamie crawls out of bed and stumbles down the steps to the kitchen where his mom is cooking breakfast. He walks over and kisses her on the cheek. “Morning ma”. “Breakfast will be ready soon, go get a shower and it should be waiting on you.” “Eh not gonna shower this morning.” “What?!” His mom spins around shocked. “Coming from the boy who showers 3 times a day?” Jamie just laughs a bit, and reclines at the kitchen table feet stretched out in front of him. “Yeah, I figured I showered 2 or 3 times a day for the past 10 years, I’m clean enough to miss this one. Besides I took one last night before crashing.” “Last night? We both know you didn’t hit the bed before 4am. I still don’t know how you manage to stay up until 4, and be up at 7, and still be in such a good mood all the time. I get 8 hours all the time and still hate mornings.” “Guess you’re not as blessed as me, ma.” He ducks as she throws a piece of raw bacon at him. He chuckles a bit and picks it up off the floor and tosses it in the trash can, near the door. “I’m gonna go get dressed. I’ll be down in a few” “Ok hun, breakfast will be ready in about 20 minutes” Jamie jogs up the stairs and back to his bedroom and starts fishing clothes out of his closet to wear for his final day of high school. ~~~ Several Months Later ~~~ “Jamie, get up!” “I hear you! I’m getting there, damn!” “You know the rules! Out of the house, by 9, back at 5 or later. You don’t have a job, then you can spend the day looking for one.” “Not like there is an abundance of fucking opportunity in this goddamn town” “James Michael Paige, you will NOT talk to me like that in my house! If you’re father was here, you would be picking your teeth up.” “Whatever, I’ll be out of here in 10 minutes anyhow.” Jamie rolls out of bed, taking his t-shirt off and slinging it across the room. He drags on a collared polo shirt and slips on some khaki pants. He walks to the bathroom and tosses some water on his hair, and looks at the bristly face staring back at him. He hears a rustle behind him and turns to find his mom standing in the doorway. ”Can I please have 2 minutes of privacy around here?” “Jamie, what happened to you? It wasn’t even a year ago you would get up in the mornings, and enjoy life. Now over the past few months, you seem like you have come to hate anything and everyone. Including your own family. Including me.” “Stress ma. It’s fucking stress.” “Watch your language. What do you have to be stressed about? You have no bills, you live at home, you have no job? What’s to stress?” “Mom…I don’t want to live at home anymore, I want a job, but not in a freakin coal mine, like pop so I can die at a young age with black lung. The only other things out there for me, is fast food, or pumpin some old lady’s gas. It’s not for me. I need to get out of this town.” “How far will you make it without money Jamie? Even if you get a temporary job to fund a move to a place you would be happy, it has to be something.” Jamie pushes past his mom without saying anything else, and heads downstairs and out the door slamming it shut behind him. ~~~ Hours Later ~~~ “Ma, I’m home.” “Hey you, it’s early, is that good news?” “No, it’s crap news, but I got a job. I’m slingin burgers up at Promyburger.” “Well it’s a start, right? You can always look for something else while you are there.” “Sure, because there is all kinds of stuff to be optimistic about.” ~~~A few weeks later~~~ “Jamie can you come up here and watch the counter?” the manager calls back to him. “Uhmm, get Corey to do it…I’m on break.” “Corey is needed with me, we have some things to discuss, can you postpone your break please?” “I need a few minutes, I can’t come up there at the moment.” “Jamie, we have customers, up front NOW.” Jamie reluctantly appears from the back, as a young woman stands at the counter smiling a bit. Jamie looks at her then at the manager, and heads over to the counter. “Hey Jamie, haven’t seen you since school’s been out.” “Yeah, it’s been a while” “Well I’ll take a number 4” “Anything else for you?” “Yah actually there is…how come you never asked me out in school?” “What..?” Jamie stares at Lexi surprised. “I knew all along, yet you never said anything…” Jamie fetches her order from the serving line, and bags it up, and brings it back to the counter. “Well…umm, you wanna go out and do something sometime then?” He asks awkwardly. “Sure” she answers, and grabs her bag. As she makes her way across the restaurant Jamie is just about to stop her and tell her he needs her number so he can call her to set up a date, when she turns and looks right at him “as soon as you get a real job, loser!” Jamie can’t speak. The girl of his high school dreams has just forced into facing his harsh reality. He has in fact joined the wretches of society, and the pimple faced jerk offs that screw up your order every time you ask to hold the pickle. He walks to the back, and removes his uniform throwing it down in a pile near the time clock and walking out. Never to enter Promyburger again. “Fuckin whore, wanna come in acting like a goddamn tease. I hate people. I hate everyone. I am so over this shit hole goddamn town” Jamie mutters to himself as he speeds down the street towards his apartment that he had just recently acquired. Once he gets home, he walks across the street to his buddy Chris’s apartment. Jamie jogs up to te door and knocks loudly. “Hey Jamie, whassup man? Thought you were sposed to be working today?” “Eh took the day off, had some bullshit come up I didn’t feel like dealing with. Hey, you have any party pills?” “Yah, want one?” Chris replies “Actually I’m gonna have a small get together tonight, and wanna grab about 10 of em from you. I’ll buy em of course” “Sweet, whatcha got planned?” “Just reliving the past, having some drinks, the basics.” Jamie answers. “Yah that’s cool. One second, lemme grab em.” Chris disappears inside for a few minutes, then returns with a small bag with a handful of blue tablet pills in them. He hands them to Jamie who hands him some cash in exchange. “Thanks bro, I owe ya.” Jamie heads off back to his apartment. Once inside, he heads straight to the bedroom, swallows all ten pills and lies back on the bed. “Fuck this town. Fuck this life. Fuck you God…I quit.” ~~~3 Months Later~~~ “So tell me Jamie. When did you become so neurotic? What I mean, is everyday you show up with new cuts, new wounds, new sores, all kinds of stuff, but nothing life threatening.” Jamie looks at the man in the white coat, holding a clipboard, sitting across from him. “What’s it matter?” “Jamie you will be institutionalized forever with these answers you give me” “Fine. If you must know, it was when the pills didn’t kill me. At one point I wanted to end it all, I wanted to quit, I wanted to give up. But somehow, I didn’t die. For some reason, I didn’t lock my door when I went in. For some reason my ma showed up to bring me dinner. For some reason, they decided to put me in here.” “Jamie you can’t blame your being here on your parents. They put you here, because they were worried you would try to do this to yourself again. Your parents care very much about your well being and they hate to see you inflicting such pain on yourself.” “Look doc, if what you want to know is why, just ask. I didn’t say it’s because of my parents. I said they put me here. That day I tried to kill myself, I was full set on doing just that. I failed. Not for lack of effort. The scars you see know are reminders to me. Time I spent here, why I was here and what I need to focus on in life.” “I see. Jamie, I am going to put in my recommendation for your release from this facility. I mean, I would still like to meet with you on occasion about the scarring that you enduring, and doing to yourself, but I don’t see you as a threat to try and take your life again.” “Definitely no fear in that” Jamie gets up and nods to the doctor as he walks out of the room muttering to himself “because I can’t die….”
