Control

10 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/05 How could I have ever let it get this far? How could I have ever let it get this bad? That...bastard has tormented me for the better part of a month now - maybe longer. My head is so fried with this thing, I can’t even rely on my own memory anymore. My mind is just clouded with it all. His leering glances, his truly frightening demeanour...the way he just grinned at me when I finally snapped beat him like a child. Now, this week on Fresh! I am to team up with the lunatic in an inter-gender, eight man tag match! I very much doubt I’ll be able to keep my cool having to be in the same team as that piece of crap. I shuddered at the very thought it and refocused my attention to the man sat opposite me with a very smug - and slightly condescending -grin plastered across his face. The thought to just reach over to him and slap the smile - and his stupid-looking moustache - right off his face was so compel at that point I had to concentrate just not to follow through with the action. It wasn’t entirely the man’s fault, the very thought of Chainz made me angry beyond belief. I was sat on the rather comfortable leather couch and the very rear of my father’s office in the massive Stakiri Towers, downtown New York. The man sat opposite me was one of the his less official business associates. His name was Clyde - I didn’t catch his last name, nor did I want it - and on the last few occasions he’s been in my presence he’s tried to flirt with me. Of course, his efforts were not reciprocated and if my father found out about it, parts of Clyde would be turning up in various different dumpsters across the city for the next month or so. My father was currently in a meeting with the biologists in one of the sub sections of the building. It was obviously regarding something of great importance as he never usually made any kind of client wait for him. A hunch told me that it also had something to do with his interests outside of the world of business, because that was primarily the place where my father concocts his less-than-legal dealings. And the upshot of all that was; I have to stay with this moron until he came back. Then, Clyde leaned forward, resting his elbows upon his knees, both hands cradling his chin and his smile now transformed from being condescending to having serious undertones of lust. Again, he reminded me of Chainz and at this moment in time that isn’t place where neither he nor I need to be. “So Alexa,” Clyde began, “how are you?” I shot him an icy glare, but I could tell from his reaction that he enjoyed my hostility. I wonder how he will like it once I put his head through the wall? “Fine,” I told him, not wanting to engage in any more false pleasantries in the slightest. Clyde then reached across and placed a hand upon tenderly upon my thigh. I glare at the hand in severe distaste and then glare him in the eyes again. He winks and nods and grab his wrist and throw it away. “Don’t get any idea’s dumbass,” I said, crossing my legs. Clyde looked down for a moment and then raised his head to glare into my eyes once again, as though I’d be mesmerised into opening my legs for him. But there was something more there now; an undeniably sinister undertone breaking out of its once calm and calculated exterior. He reminded me of Chainz now more than ever. “Perhaps you don’t know who I am,” he told me, now rising from his seat. He was attempting to appear dominating, by towering over me. He didn’t have a clue. “I know precisely who you are,” Told him. Now, I stood too, showing the lunatic a small glint of defiance. “It’s just that I don’t give a flying fuck.” I saw the anger rise from his very core and I knew right then and there that something was going to happen. He hurled a open-handed right at me, which I stopped with minimal ease. He looked momentarily confused, expecting a reassuring and pleasing conclusion to his gesture. I removed that expression from his face with a powerful right hand, sending him sprawling, backward over his chair, to the thick carpeted floor with force. I jammed my left foot over the mans throat and stood there for a second, now towering over him, and watched Clyde’s face turn funny shades of red as I applied more and more pressure. “Care to tell me how rich and powerful you are now?” I asked him. “...gahk...fu...fuck you!” He responded, the colour of his face now shifting more to a dark shade of purple. I poured on the last ounce of pressure on Clyde’s throat and bent down closer toward him. “I’m sorry, I didn’t hear that. Care to repeat it?” I asked him. “And remember that if it’s not something that I like, all I need to do it to turn my foot slightly, to crush your larynx.” “...gahk...uh...hmmmmf...I’m sorry...” He responded. I removed my foot and left him spluttering on the floor as I left the office. I smiled as I passed my father and he looked at me strangely at first, but upon entering the room, he smiled and nodded in understanding. It’s so easy to shift the balance of control in most things in life and this situation is no exception to that universal rule. Not here, or in the AWC. I am ready for anything that Chainz has to throw at me, he can’t do anything to me that I haven’t experienced before. Like most men I know, he’s more than a little predictable and that weakness will ultimately serve as his downfall.
