Fame! (Not like Alan Parker)

25 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 25th, 2006 INT. CONVENIENCE STORE - DAY DELMAN DARCUSS, a white man in his very early twenties, stares down at a picture of his ex-wife, a blonde cheerleader-type girl, and his daughter, apple-of-daddy’s-eye cute, behind the freeze-dried noodles in the local 7-11. Bustling around him, the odd patron here or there. It was a bit of a down time for the STORE CLERK, a middle-aged Indian immigrant who was stoically wiping away at a sugar-topped, coffee stained counter as more ungrateful consumers swooped in like careless birds, dropping little tokens of “joe” on top of the soap suds as the worked so diligently to fight away its brethren. Delman looks around, a LADY placing her purse down near the fountain drink machine; an OLDER MAN previewing a newspaper while jingling change around in his pocket; a HIGH SCHOOL COUPLE, probably taking a break from the make-out session they planned for after skipping school today. CLOSE SHOT Of the Picture again, as Darcuss crumples it up. NARRORATOR Meet Delman Darcuss... NEW ANGLE Of Darcuss’s face, speckaled with a 5’ o clock shadow. He pulls out a small caliber revolver, and fires a around into the ceiling. Gasps and screams spread across the floor like wild fire. DELMAN DARCUSS (Flustered and unsure) Everybody be cool, this is a robbery! Freeze frame. NARRORATOR He’s had a bad day. He does a similar head count again. The lady has dropped her eighty-nine cent Dr. Pepper all over her Prada shoes; the older man is eyeing up the door, as he’s the closest to it; the high school sweethearts hold each other, this isn’t what they planned for; the store clerk puts his hands up, instantly surrendering. Delman points the gun at the old man. DELMAN DARCUSS (Emphatically) On the ground now! He swings the gun around, showing everyone that he means business. They all begin towards the floor. Delman starts towards the register. DELMAN DARCUSS Keep your fucking heads down or I swear to god ill kill each and every one of you! NARRORATOR A couple days ago, Delman came home from a long day at work to see the humble apartment stripped baron of all evidence of his wife and child, omitting a note that read: FULL SCREEN SHOT Of a hand written note on a napkin. NARRORATOR Look Delman, you and I were never gonna work out. Mostly because I hate your guts. Ill mail you the divorce forms. INT. BEHIND THE COUNTER - DAY Delman struggles with the register, he can’t seem to get it open. He turns to the clerk, who’s face down in his wash rag on the floor. DELMAN DARCUSS Hey, Turban-head, open this thing up! The store clerk hesitates, not wanting to move. Darcuss cocks the hammer. DELMAN DARCUSS (Impatiently) GET UP NOW! The clerk still hesitates, but starts to push to his knees. Delman grabs the back of his collar, and pulls him the rest of the way. He throws him into the register hard. DELMAN DARCUSS Get to it! The clerk gingerly presses a pair of keys, and volia, open sesame. There’s a dwindling amount of cash in the drawer, but enough to make Delman feel like this wasn’t for nothing. He digs one hand into the drawer and starts to stuff the bills into his pockets. Then suddenly, someone rings the service bell at the counter. Delman looks up gun first, letting his aim hit the beautifully woven tie of the always sprucely dressed B.O.T., who held in his hand a bottle of Sprite, and a bottle of a generic household cleanser. NARRORATOR This is B.O.T... He’s having a pretty good day, actually. DELMAN DARCUSS (Confused and frustrated) Hey! Didn’t I say get on the ground asshole! B.O.T. Listen, I don’t want to interrupt his what your doing here, but I just wanted to know, since you have the drawer open, could you ring me out first? NARRORATOR He was serious. Darcuss is dumbfounded that this peguin-suited giant could ask him that with a straight faced. He almost looked annoyed, like a customer who’s waited too long in line. DELMAN DARCUSS No I can’t ring you out. This is a fucking robbery, dude. Empty your pockets and get on the ground. B.O.T. First of all, jack ass, I wasn’t talking to you. Your not the one wearing the snazy red polo, are you? Delman looks down at his ragged clothes, and looks back up at B.O.T. B.O.T. Ex-fucking-actly, so put that stupid ass gun down for a second, and let... B.O.T. takes a glance at the store clerks nametag. B.O.T. ...Asad here ring up me up! Darcuss waves his his pistol in B.O.T.’s face, the gun metal barrel reflecting back at him the giant’s aviators. DELMAN DARCUSS What are you, retared? Don’t you know what this is? B.O.T. No, I'm not retarded, I'm thirsty, and I have to clean my bathroom. And that’s a Charter Arms .44 Special Bulldog. Do I win the prize now? Delman isn’t sure what to do about this guy; its visible in the bewildered frustration painted on his face. DELMAN DARCUSS Look, If I let him ring you out, will you leave? B.O.T. Thats all I’m asking, man. DELMAN DARCUSS (Conceding) Fine. You! Do it! The would-be burglar throws Asad into the cash register and closes it. B.O.T. hands him the Sprite first. He scans it across the laser beams quickly. He takes the generic cleanser next. And with a short beep, he plops it on the table. ASAD (nervously) Wo- Would you like a bag? B.O.T. Sure, why not? Asad pulls a bag up from under the counter. ASAD Five fifty-nine, sir. B.O.T. reaches into his pocket and pulls out a wad of the glorious green. B.O.T. Can you break a hundred? ASAD Sorry, there’s never more than one-hundred dollars in the register at all times. B.O.T. Well, how bout you, rude-man-with-the-gun, can you break a hundred? Delman pushes the clerk aside, and leans over the counter to press his gun into our heroes chest. NARRORATOR Boy, that won’t end well. DELMAN DARCUSS Alright! Enough of this bullshit, give up the money or I shoot you right now. B.O.T. You don’t want to shoot me? DELMAN DARCUSS Why not? B.O.T. Because im famous! DELMAN DARCUSS No your not. B.O.T. Yes I am. DELMAN DARCUSS What do you do? B.O.T. I’m B.O.T., the world-famous pro-wrestler. DELMAN DARCUSS (Suprised with a hint of dissapointed) Seriously? B.O.T. Yea man. DELMAN DARCUSS That’s a gay sport, ain’t it? B.O.T. (Offended) Excuse me? DELMAN DARCUSS Two men rubbing on each other, sweaty and under-dressed. Sounds pretty gay to me. In the blink of an eye, B.O.T. snaps a jab between the eyes of the unsuspecting Darcuss. Delman buckles back, and drops his gun. NARRORATOR Told ya... Asad grabs the loose gun and points it at the robber. ASAD (Exuding false bravado) Freeze asshole! He looks un-steadly back and forth between Delman on the ground, and B.O.T. over the counter. B.O.T. looks at Asad in disgust at his terrible “Hero Cop” impression. B.O.T. Your embarassing... NARRORATOR Though he probably just hit Darcuss because he called him ‘gay,’ B.O.T.’s right fist saved Asad’s bottom line, and a couple of bystanders's pocket monies. B.O.T. pulls out a debit card, and finishes his transaction. INT. THE BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING - DAY NARRORATOR The next day, B.O.T. walked into work a hero... MOVING SHOT Of the main floor of the school bustles with the rapid motion of nobodies looking to be somebodies, all of them reaching deep inside them selves to find the “It” factor, as DIMITRI CLEMANS barked words of encouragment to them from a shabby bench near the entrance. DIMITRI Come on, you choads, show some gusto! The entrance swings open and B.O.T. emerges from the glow of daylight. He immediatley turns to Dimitri. B.O.T. What’s the good word, big guy? DIMITRI Same old same, ‘Hero’ B.O.T. (Caught off-guard) Excuse me? Dimitri lifts his cane and points to the office across the floor. DIMITRI Your in the paper. B.O.T. The news paper? DIMITRI No, the toilet paper, jackass. INSIDE OFFICE OVER THE SHOULDER SHOT Of B.O.T. lifting a copy of the Courier Post off of a disshevled desktop. On the front page, a photo of a local 7-11 with police presence in the front. NARRORATOR The headline read: FAMOUS WRESTLER PREVENTS DISASTER FOR LOCAL BUSINESS MAN. NEW ANGLE Of B.O.T. reading the article, a smile curving on his face as he reads on. NARRORATOR In short, the article was a descriptive expository from some starving journalist, chronicling the tense moments of fear and the ceaseless flowing of bodily fluids from the store clerk Asad, and his undying need to repay the hero that saved his life. Touching really. MAIN FLOOR B.O.T. exits the office and waves the paper around over his head like a flag. B.O.T. This is excellent! He trots back over to Dimitri. DIMITRI Of course you’d like it. B.O.T. Look what it says here... CLOSE UP Of B.O.T. sitting next to Dimitri on the bench, shoving the paper in his face. B.O.T. “He smote the offender with a crushing right hand, never once intimdated at the offenders consistent threats at his own life.” DIMITRI You know what this means right? B.O.T. I’m awesome? DIMITRI Your famous again! B.O.T. leans back, relishing the idea of being famous again. NARRORATOR The Notorious One had always been relativly famous, but there was a time in his life where on the death bed of blind and deaf cancer patient, his last wish would be to shake B.O.T.’s hand. As of now he was a more subtle type of famous... FULL SCREEN PHOTO Of Steve Bucemi NARRORATOR Not to be confused with a ‘barely’ sort of famous... FULL SCREEN PHOTO Of Andy Dick NARRORATOR To Tenacious B, this wasn’t just his unintentional saving of a convience store. This was an springboard to more stardom! B.O.T. stands up. B.O.T. (determined) I’m gonna be famous again! FADE OUT. INT. DODGE CHARGER - THAT NIGHT B.O.T. sits behind the wheel of his brand new Charger, bought to replace his Prowler, vandalized at the hands of the Society. In the passenger seat, VIRGIL WHITE sits, tapping on his knees to the funky beats echoing through the speakers. NARRORATOR Later that night, on their way to have drinks with the other trainers, B.O.T. and Virgil White brainstorm about B.O.T.’s journey back into the limelight. B.O.T. So I was thinking about this me being famous again thing. My heroic exploits are the perfect doorway, I just gotta walk through. Any suggestions? They sit in silent. NARRORATOR B.O.T. never talked to Virgil much before this. He preferred not to. He always felt like he was detached and uninterested in everything, because he never had an opinion. Had B.O.T. paid more attention to the details these past couple of years, he’d realized what everyone else knew. He was mute. B.O.T. None? Well, I was thinking maybe ill make an appearance at some movie premiere or something. Take a picture of me smootchin’ on the leading lady, maybe a with a couple fans. The way I see it is, the more pictures I’m in, the more chances they put me in a magazine, right? Might even get some groupie ass, and that’s always good right? Again with the silence. A blank stare is chiseled into White’s face, as Jarrett is visably uncomfortable. B.O.T. Listen, while were both here alone, I think this is as good a time as any to discuss some qualms I’ve been having with your behavior. I mean, your a great wrestler and all, the students have taken aliking to you. But, well, you can’t expect to teach them without saying anything to them! And shit, at staff meetings, you never have anything to contribute. While everyone else is ratcheting off with the good ideas, your just sitting there, in your little corner, deep in thought. You know, I want to hear what your thinking sometimes. Well, as long as its not about ritual sacrifice after religious orgies. You can keep that shit your yourself... TEN MINUTES LATER NARRORATOR As B.O.T. continued to make an ass of himself, they would eventually arrive at their destination. B.O.T. ...and, what I’m really trying to say is, well, say something! Anything! The silence creeps us out. Virgil shoots him an annoyed look before thrusting the car door open and leaving the vehicle. B.O.T. gives the camera the “What-did-I-do?” look before leaving the vehicle himself. EXT. BAR PARKING LOT - NIGHT B.O.T. follows a perturbed towards AI, IVAN, and Dimitri, who are standing in a group in front of the door, along with another GEEKY LOOKING YOUNG WOMAN. Virgil walks past, not giving the others the time of day. B.O.T. approches. AI (concerned) What’s wrong with him? B.O.T. Nothing. Just gave homeboy alittle talkin to. IVAN (Regretful) Great, I told you we shouldn’t let them ride together. AI What did you do to him? B.O.T. Nothing that wouldn’t help him! DIMITRI I’ll kick your ass for whatever you did to him later. Right now, you’ve got a guest. Dimitri gestures towards the odd girl accompanying the usual suspects. B.O.T. gets a good look at her. Frazzled hair, funny little Old Navy t-shirt, mixed with the dirty jeans and the big round plastic glasses made B.O.T. feel like he was a New Kid on the Block. NARRORATOR She was a groupie, she had to be. Boybands used to fight through droves of them back in the mid 90’s. She had all the groupie symptoms: abnoxious glasses, “been-waiting-in-line-for-tickets-for-four-days” smell, that creepy “wow-a-guy-is-really-looking-at-me” smile on her face. Why she was outside of the bar waiting on him was a question he didn’t want answered. Though when it was all said and done, he’d do her... DIMITRI Her names Hannah. Shes your biggest fan. B.O.T. Well.. Er... hey Hannah...? HANNAH Oh my god! I like, so love you! B.O.T. Hey, you know I’m a wrestler, right? I don’t sing and dance, or anything like that. HANNAH Well, you had sung and danced before, remember silly. NARRORATOR She was referring to the time B.O.T. play a full set on an episode of ICW’s En Fuego four years ago, which ended in him getting hit in the face with a banjo. B.O.T. (wearily) Um...yea...that’s right? So, did you want to join us for drinks? HANNAH No, silly, I’m not old enough to drink! IVAN (mockingly) Yeah, silly, she’s not old enough to drink. HANNAH I just wanted to wish you luck on your match this weekend. NARRORATOR Then it hit him... His match was to qualify as number one contender for the Livewire championship. If he won the match, he fights for the belt. If he won the belt, then BAM! Famecity! B.O.T. (happy about his revelation) Kid... your a genius! He consumes her in a hug, and she melts in his arms. Her face twitches in a weird orgasmic pleasure, complete with the most awkward sounds known to man. The rest of the crew tries very hard not laugh. B.O.T. lets go, and she snaps back to reality. He smacks Dimitri on his shoulder. B.O.T. Lets go! They move into the bar, leaving Hannah behind, to savor the moment.
