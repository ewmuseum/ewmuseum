The Reunion

23 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-7-2005 The Reunion New York ,City - it’s a sterling kind of day in the “Big Apple” even when there is a “Heat Advisory” in effect for the city that is home to the Lamb theatre ,Times Square and of course Madison Square Garden. This city is one of the true melting pots in the country as people from different nationalities ,genders ,lifestyles and yes sexual orientations live and work without a problem. This city is home to the New York Knicks ,Rangers and even the New York Liberty of the WNBA but most importantly and the reason that we are here is that this city is also the home of what was formerly the “Old School Connection” and is now called the “Army of Old School” Now its no secret that this group has been bouncing around feds looking for that one place to call home for an extended period of time and so far they really haven’t found such a thing since moving into a new community called “Primetime Central” and success admittedly had been quite limited for them there but now things are different as they have hooked up with a fed called the “Atlantic Wrestling Club” which is run by the same gentlemen that ran the federation known as NEON named Pierre H.. At the “Complex” we travel to the home where the “Leader” as some call him in Joshua Curtis is seen in the kitchen cooking some Dim Sims ,which is a food that his “observer” for the “Army” in Quatre Raberba Winner absolutely LOVES! Why is he doing that you ask? Well Joshua sister hasn’t come home yet from her trip to Japan to visit their mother and obviously it kind of made Joshua worry a little bit because Ami was never one to stay too long due to the fact that she knows their mother’s schedule. Ok I know you are all going *Wait Joshua and Ami are brother and sister yet their last names are different* am I right? Well it didn’t help that they were separated as for some reason Dr. Mizuno was forced to give up Joshua to adoption and while the reason was never made clear to him Joshua could actually care less because he still loves her and would take a bullet for her in an instant if the need arose. So Joshua was waiting patiently for the noodles to cook when the phone rang in the kitchen and Joshua was very quick to answer it thinking that it would be his sister. “Hello this is the ;Old School Complex’ how may I help you today?” Joshua asked and the voice on the other end made him the happiest person alive that day! “Bro its me Ami. Listen I’m sorry I didn’t tell you that I was staying for a couple of more days at home but I tried to get a hold of you on your messenger and you were “away” or something. Anyways I was wondering if it would be all right for mom to come for a visit? She hasn’t seen you in a while and told me that if she didn’t get to see you she might go nuts.” Ami said somewhat jokingly and that got a small chuckle from Joshua before he regained his composure before continuing the conversation. “You know that mother is very much welcome in this house and that everyone here honors and respects her as much as anyone outside of the faction if not more. You just tell me when the flight is coming in and I’ll be at the airport with Zack about 30 minutes early to come get you.” Joshua told his younger sibling and she gave him the information before they said their goodbyes and Joshua couldn’t help but let out an enthusiastic “YES!” before turning off the stove and pouring the noodles into a bowl for Quatre and calling the young heir to Winner Corporations over to the house for lunch and no sooner did Josh tell him that he made a bowl of the noodles for him that the phone was hung up and a blonde blur came running through the walkway that connects the two houses and to the kitchen of the main house with a panting Quatre and Joshua started laughing hysterically at the sight of the young man with his hands on his knees. “Quatre hun you know that you don’t have to rush over here. The noodles would have been waiting here for you because I wasn’t going to eat your bowl.” Josh told his friend and Quatre smiled after regaining his breath looked at his friend and began to speak. “Sorry Master Joshua but you know when you make that that I’ll be over in a flash.” Quatre explained before deciding to ask about the phone call that Joshua got. “Was that Ami on the phone?” the blonde asked and Joshua smiled sweetly before giving his answer to the young heir. “Yes it was Quatre. She’s coming home and mother is coming with her so I have to make sure that I’m up in time to get showered and stuff for when I have to go get her.” Joshua told Quatre and as he did a familiar face happened into the room at that time. “Your sister is coming home Joshua? That’s great but hmmm I didn’t get her anything yet. *snap fingers* I know what to do for her!” Zack said as he quickly left and took Joshua’s Buick LeSabre out to a store that he knew in the state and this kind of left Quatre puzzled but Joshua was confident that Zack would come through in the clutch. 2 Hours Later… It was now 2:00 P.M. when Zack returned with the present that he had for Ami’s mother which was a miniature glass angel statue that he saw once at the Hallmark store and never thought to buy it until today. When Joshua saw it he almost cried but somehow managed to compose himself as it was a known fact that Zack thought very highly of Dr. Mizuno and wanted to show her the proper respect that not many other people would do. The rest of the day was spent hanging out with Milliardo ,Treize and the rest of the “Army” into the late night and surprisingly no one got hammered but then again when your first match back in the ring is in 3 days you know damn well that you can’t get hammered because you have to be able to think clearly in the ring and also with the fact that the first match that Joshua and Ami had to wrestle was a handicap match against a wrestler calling themselves “Hate” Joshua knew that it was time for him and Ami to do what GW wouldn’t allow them to do and that is be successful! The Next Day… More like the next night really for it was 3:30 in the morning when Joshua got to the Newark Airport on United Airlines and believe it or not Quatre was up and wanted to go with Joshua to pick up his sister and Joshua didn’t mind having him for company because it meant that he got to spend some quality time with one of the few people that he could honestly call “Brother”. Revelation? Not really because even before Quatre joined the faction Joshua was very much fond of the man from Saudi Arabia and the fact that in a prior fed called “Ring Revolution” the two formed a bond that you couldn’t script for shit! No one ,not even the “Mad Scientist” Paul Heyman could script the genuine emotion that came from that man when Quatre collapsed on the ramp during a match that Tina had and when Joshua was in the hospital with that man everyone that watched the show that night “Got it”! Joshua never had a biological brother and he always wanted one and the closest thing that he got that he was grateful for was Quatre and he wouldn’t give it up for anything! When they got to the airport Joshua got out and opened the passenger door for his friend and Quatre brought the present that Zack was going to give Ami but due to the time that Josh was leaving he wouldn’t be able to give it to her so Josh told Zack he would do it and as they waited patiently for Ami and Dr. Mizuno to get off the plane the nerves started to build up in Joshua but every time he felt them build he looked over at the young man that was to be leader of a multi-TRILLION dollar company and they went away real quick! Moments later and Ami emerged from the tunnel with Dr. Mizuno and Joshua ran up to Ami and hugged her fiercely as well as his mother! This was the first time that Joshua had seen his mother in years and the emotion came out of him in a rush which Quatre admired about his friend and didn’t say a word and just let them have their moment. “How have you been ladies?” Joshua asked and they both smiled as their responses before Joshua produced the present that Zack bought for Dr. Mizuno and the first thing he said was that he was sorry that Zack couldn’t make it but he wanted Dr. Mizuno to have the present. Dr. Mizuno at first didn’t want to accept the present but Ami insisted and she wasn’t upset that Josh didn’t have something for her because to her just Joshua being there was enough of a present for her and when Joshua said that they had a match in a couple of days Ami decided to change her tune for a second. “The best present that we could both get Joshua is us winning the match against ‘Hate’ on Tuesday ,so let’s whip that son of a gun’s butt on Tuesday!” Ami said and Joshua smiled as the quartet walked out of the airport as a family reunited!
