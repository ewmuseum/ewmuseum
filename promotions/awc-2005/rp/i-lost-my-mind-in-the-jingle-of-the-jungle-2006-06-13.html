I lost my mind in the jingle of the jungle...

13 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: june 13 (Note: This isn't really intended to be much competition this week. I lost a friend and my creativity for a few days... I'll bounce back for next week, promise.) I'm an opportunity, and I knock so softly... Everyone likes you more if they can sit back and laugh at the tragedies that stem from you trying too hard. Look at music - AFI, Good Charlotte... they were a lot easier to hate or love when they were really TRYING to tell you how punk or goth or full of angst and upset they happened to be at the time. Once they realized that everyone gained amusement from their constant bullshit, they were easiest to lose interest in. Go ahead and pick up a Guitar World right now. Look at Alternative Press, or anything that claims to be in tune with the 'underground' everyone strives for... if Davey Havok's disgusting mug isn't on the cover, then it's probably the face of AFI's androgynous, half-hearted excuse for a guitar player... ''... And I'm not just saying that because they're more successful than I ever will be, seriously.'' Amy was trying way too hard, perched at a magazine stand in Brazil and speaking to a young girl who probably didn't know who the hell she was, and was even less likely to speak a word of English. ''Yeah, I guess you don't really care. You like 'em anyway.'' The girl shrugged and, in the ensuing awkward silence, scampered off to better things. That left Amy on her own once more, feeling abandoned in a foreign land. Darcy was still somewhere talking to his secondary boss and accepting that offer that he naturally didn't know she'd heard, which only caused the pit in her stomach to grow. She knew what she had to do to keep him from taking the offer, but it was a huge leap of faith that she wasn't at all prepared to take. Marriage? Pregnancy? Been there. Done that with much lesser men, and it didn't even keep those assholes around. All she really needed was a little liquid courage for when he inevitably found her, but going to a club would be too predictable. She tried to think of the one place he wouldn't quite expect to see her... ...Not a fan of hot sun, since she burns easily and likes keeping her tattoos... you know... not looking like faded blobs... ...Hates crowds... ...Not a big supporter of being eaten by random sharks that - despite assurances otherwise - COULD very well grow legs and run her down on the sand... Okay, maybe that was some kind of an acid flashback, but regardless it seemed to be down to two options. Amy squinted into the sun to look up a hill, where some ridiculous statue was resting among oddly leaved trees. The rainforest, or... the beach two feet the other way. As much as the forest sounded interesting, she assumed that the spiders were about as deadly as the food in Brazil - so it was beach time. Luckily, she'd come dressed for such an occasion and quickly shed her baggy capri pants and tanktop. What, like you didn't expect a Canadian visiting RIO to be bikini-ready? NUDE... He'd searched all day. He'd figured that not too many tattoo-laiden, pissed-off diminutive redheads were traversing the city that day, but his luck didn't seem to be agreeing with him. His first stop was obviously the hotel; her bags were still there, so Darcy surmised that she had to be around someplace. But his offering of said description to the various restaurant owners, shopkeepers and police officers all turned up fruitless. While still determined, Darcy knew that ironically, searching for Amy while the Brazilian sun still adorned the sky would probably be useless. She's probably working hard to keep me off her trail, he surmised. I'd better wait until nightfall, when her behavior will be a bit more predictable… When night fell, Darcy began searching the clubs, judging that Amy would likely be on a quest for booze by now. However, his search through the Janeiro nightlife was proving equally as ineffective. And with the lines outside the clubs becoming longer and longer with each establishment he checked, Darcy was losing hope, and fast. Maybe she really doesn't want to be found at all, he thought, pessimistically, a term that he liked to call 'pessimised.' Whether or not he was looking to regroup for his investigation or simply clear his head, Darcy headed for a nearby beach. While not nearly as packed as it had been earlier in the afternoon, when it had certainly reached the brim of capacity, a respectable number of patrons were still enjoying the shoreline under the starlit sky. Darcy walked to where the sand and water connected as one, allowing his bare feet and the bottom of his cargo pants to be submerged in the salt water. It was the precise moment when he had given up all hope entirely that fate, in apparent triumph, was ready to reveal her. She was some distance away, but a nearby bonfire organized by some beachgoers brightened the area, and the glow of the flames bounced radiantly off of her fiery mane. Without the slightest hesitation Darcy took off, sprinting headlong in her direction. When he arrived close enough he found his assessment to be correct; while Amy Campbell might look like any other smoking-hot, 5'5' bikini-clad redhead from behind, the printed dragon curving around the back of her neck gave her away. ''Amy!'' Darcy gasped, attempting to catch his breath as he turned her around. She certainly looked radiant, with the light of the flames making her stand out from the rest of everything around him. But the look on her face, what looked to be in the manner of a sinister scowl, quickly chased any thoughts of her beauty from his mind. ''Jesus Christ,'' Darcy said, his breath now returned to him. ''I've been looking for you all day. Where in God's name have you been?'' ''What,'' she shrugged bitterly, ''You expected me to hang around that hippie hole all day while Jameson gave you a verbal butt-fucking to take some assignment that I'm already sure isn't actually worth your time?'' Amy tossed her cup of beer into the fire, illiciting howls from the festive locals perched about twenty feet away. Apparently the situation looked like some kind of sexy party to drunken Brazilians. To Darcy, it looked more like his girlfriend had just turned away alcohol so she stayed in a clear enough headspace to properly chew him out. ''Not exactly...but I also didn't expect you to ignore my calls and ditch me all night either,'' he replied. He certainly wasn't too keen on the hole she was trying to stare into him. ''I assume you're pissed off at me, that much I gathered. Would you mind telling me why?'' She snarled, and led him even farther away from the small crowd - more toward the water, a seemingly perfect place to wash a body away... but aside from that, ''I'm not as stupid as your fuckhead boss thinks. He wouldn't have flown us here, and you wouldn't have taken me unless you were buttering me up for something I wasn't gonna want to hear. If you thought I was hanging around the lunch room in that office, you're retarded - I was pretty fucking obviously 'overhearing' your talk with Jameson.'' The sea brushing her toes and reminding her of home didn't sway her temper at all, ''So go ahead, Darcy - this is where you do some oh-so-sweet and dramatic speech that tells me you have something you 'just have' to do, you know, for yourself. Self discovery and blahblahblah... just hit me with it now - I know you took the job, and I know no news is gonna be good.'' Darcy squinted. He now had a pretty good idea when Amy had disappeared from the Rio palace. Somehow he sensed that easing her newfound anger by telling her what she probably wanted to hear wouldn't be enough. But still, he had to try. ''Actually, if you had stuck around long enough, you would have heard me politely decline his 'oh-so-generous' offer. Then you would have heard him chew me out, terminate my spokesman services and essentially put an end to this dual life I've been leading. Normally this is where I'd get steamed for you not giving me the least bit of credit...but you know what? I've already had my ass chewed out once today, so let's go. If you want to scream at me, fucking do it. But don't waste my time.'' ''I doubt you actually did that. And why are we here in the FIRST place, huh? Because that moron couldn't take a simple phone call. I just hauled ass to this spider-infested hive ON A PLANE for nothing. I've got jet lag, I've got a headache, I've... fuck - whatever,'' she grimaced, waving him off and digging her toes into the sand, ''I don't have many words left up my sleeve... so tell me what I need to be doing right now. Appreciating, right? Being sooooo perky about standing on a beach in Rio, even despite the fact that I feel like shit?'' ''Honest to God, Amy. I told him no deal. He fired me. I didn't know why he brought me- sorry, US out here in the first place, but I can't imagine we'll be returning anytime soon. And on the subject of what I want you to do, I think I can call it even if you would just calm the fuck down. But now that I think about it, I'm getting the idea that this is about something completely different. You wanna come clean, or what?'' Darcy was spot-on. He knew that 'You don't want to know' look on her face. There was something going on between those little ears, and she wasn't gonna keep it in for long. She raised her eyebrow as if to ask if he felt ready to hear her side of things, and as Darcy stood adamant, she turned away for a moment to collect her temper. ''Alright,'' she said flatly, ''Marry me.'' ALIVE... I hadn't done E in the longest time. I stay away from drugs now... At least, I did. Something about this place and the music, and especially the company just makes you want to lose it - to let yourself be enveloped by the atmosphere of the jungle. That's why I took the pills when the dark-skinned candy raver handed them to me. I saw Darcy taking them, that's what triggered more than just poking at them to try discerning what the carved 'brand' - y'know, Red Playboy, Dolphins, or something sickeningly mixed with speed - was, and had me popping the three in my hand. That was my first mistake. Yeah, I proposed earlier. I probably royally fucked up my relationship by doing that... but my mistake, as in the one I have full ownership and consequence of, was taking those fucking pills. I knew what ecstasy did to me, I'd been practically hooked on the stuff before and it only ever led me to cocaine... to the life I left behind to regain Darcy, my career and everything good that I now have. I fucked up. I usually fuck up, but my conscience was going to repay me for this time. For the next few weeks, all I'll be thinking of will be drugs... medicating... hallucinating... getting out of my head. He probably thought I was tossing them down like a fiend, when I was really just waiting to see the glint in his eye that would tell me whether or not he'd be partaking in the candy of the E-tards surrounding us. Honestly, I can dance like an idiot to anything. I just like to escape daily life with a little dignity by leading Darcy to believe that I'm only a shitty dancer when I'm trashed. Looking around, though, I used to be one of them. I still act and dress like I am half the time, but I've seen what this shit does. I know exactly what popping pills and dancing like you don't give a damn gets you because, while it might seem happy and all for fun and love, your head's tricking you. You're getting addicted, whether it's to the music or the moves or the smiles or the drugs - you're fucking yourself up the ass by dabbling in any of it. That's not to say I'm unhappy about the night. There was a twisted little second where Darcy looked down at me and had this strange expression - almost like he was willing to take my ridiculous proposal any day of the week. That idea didn't scare me, and it wasn't the MDMA chilling me enough to like the thought... it was why I did it. As off the cuff as my proposition had seemed to Darcy, I wanted it. I actually liked the thought of waking up next to him forever. Today, tonight... this week - whatever - proved one thing to me: No matter what you put in front of us, between us or behind us, Darcy Markson and I are proverbial partners in crime. We'll stick together even when we're against one another. He and I both realize sacrifice... and we both know potential. It's a match made in hell, but a union that no pill or punch or belt can break. We danced like assholes and looked one another in the eye, knowing that the one thing in the room that wouldn't ever sway is what we have between us - whether it's lust, love, wrestling rings or wedding rings. That said, it's exceedingly difficult to recall anything beyond overall atmosphere and emotions... Where'd that scene go? How many fingers am I waving to this beat? The most I'll gather from the next day of my life is the fact that, as much as I love 'em... I. HATE. DRUGS. I can smile one more time at Darcy, knowing I'm not gonna know a god damned thing in a few hours. Drugged Content Awake Fading Tired Crashing Comedown Sleep. ...Mmm, old patterns.
