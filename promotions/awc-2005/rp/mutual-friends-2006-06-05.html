Mutual Friends

5 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/06 Back in the day, I mean we are talking a long time ago like 2002, yes I said it so 2002. Wayne and Tiara are still in high school, hell neither one of them know about each other. But this is the part of the story where they meet. So we are about to take back in the day, we are going to bring it back when they both were just simple minded sophomores heading into their junior year of high school. Kids are starting to get their licenses, kids are learning about sex, drugs and alcohol. Wayne was the first of all of his friends to get his license and of course he had his own car. It was a Friday night, the boys were about to go out and have a good time, they all had worked a hard week during the summer instead of having fun these kids had a job, unlike many of today’s standards (remember this is a long time ago, like what 4 years?). So after working their jobs all day long the boys get all decked up in nice clothing, get in their trucks and find some older guy to buy them some beer, back in the day it wasn’t as hard as it is today. One of Wayne’s friends who just popped his first top and speaks up about tonight’s plans, “Maybe we should go down to the lake, hell who knows there might be some people there hanging out and having a good time.” Before anyone else could interject another boy throws in his two cents as he opens his first beer, or as we used to call it brewski. “That sounds like a good idea!” Wayne without any hesitation agrees with a nod and starts the Chevrolet Pick up truck up and puts it and gear and drives away. They start heading down towards the lake and pull up, seeing as it is only about 7:30 pm the sun has yet to go down but it is slowly making it’s way towards the horizon. Wayne pulls up and all the guys get out of the truck, the music blasting and tailgate down the boys sit down enjoy the music and the beer. All of them sit back and reflect on how easy life is back in the day, none of them want to think about the future, the important life decisions they have to make the idea that gas might be almost five dollars a gallon but the idea that their lives are simple they enjoy. The boys sit there and drink their beers, they kill close to a twelve pack between the five of them in a matter of two hours, and the sun has now gone down completely. The moon is shining of the lake in a romantic way, of course all these guys are single so they do not realize it and just continue talking about the macho bull shit that teenage boys talk about. The guy with the original idea finally pipes up and says something of maturity/importance, “Look over there,” With that he points across the other side of the lake. All the boys look in that direction and not only can you see the reflection of the moon off the lake but you can also a reflection of a fire. “Are you thinking what I’m thinking Stephen?” Wayne has a sly look in his eye, he looks down at the bed of the truck and notices that they still have close to twenty beers or so nods his head along with Stephen. Wayne and the other guy Stephen hop in the front seat, a start the truck up, the others sit in the bed holding on for dear life, not just for themselves but for the beer also. After making what they thought was a straight line, of course after drinking a little bit the truck kind of made an S type path over to the other location. The two in the back and the Wayne and Stephen hop out of the truck walking closer and closer to the fire. As they get closer they start to realize it is some girls from their school, they are a little younger but they are having a nice quant yes I said quant fire. There about six of them and maybe one or two other guys. Stephen and Wayne are the first’s ones to walk over there and introduce themselves. After all the boring introductions are done they are invited to stay. Wayne starts walking back with Stephen to get the truck leaving the other two behind. Once the two of them are far enough away from the girls and the other guys they start chattering. “Dude that one girl, is smoking.” Wayne looks over at the Stephen as he says this. “I know, let me guess you are going after her.” Stephen makes the statement as if he always gets the second pickings of the liter of girls. “I will do my damnedest to get her, what was her name?” Wayne asks the question as they both jump up into the truck. Once inside and Stephen is situation he is ready to give his response, “Tiara I believe.” “Tiara…hmmm… that is an interesting name.” Wayne thinks a little bit as he is driving but he seems to like the looks and the intrigue of the name of this girl he just met. “Definitely not a name you hear every day but, hell give it a shot, her other friend looked pretty hot so I will talk with those girls.” Stephen says that not even thinking he is taking the back seat but in other words hooking his boy up with the top prize and settling for second. If you and your boys go out you know that happens to you sometimes, it is like a respect thing between guys. Wayne backs the truck up to the fire and the tail gate is put down and the beers are starting to flow. Wayne grabs another and throws one to Stephen. After that he turns to the rest of the people around the fire, “Would anyone like a beer?” The crowd is mostly hesitant especially the ladies as you know most girls don’t drink beer they think its disgusting. But one soul stands up and accepts the offer, Tiara. Who did you expect, I mean she is pretty and she can get along with the guys? “I will take one.” Wayne walks over to the cooler and grabs a beer, he then starts to walk across to the other side where Tiara was standing. He pops the top on the beer and hands it to her. “Thank you,” her gentle voice made Wayne wants her ever so much more. The looks, the sound of her voice, and she drank beer, from what he knows so far he likes what he is seeing. Wayne takes advantage of being able to make him more personable by standing next to her after he delivers the beer. For the first couple of moments where the two groups get acquainted it is a little awkward and not much conversation is sparked by anyone. But once the beers start flowing conversations are started. You see the small groups form, you have Wayne and Tiara kind of talking, the kid Stephen speaking with the other girl, her name was something like Shannon, then you have the other two guys just sitting there trying to talk to everyone seeing as they don’t really have what you could call “game”. The night goes over what seems to be pretty smooth, Tiara and Wayne are starting to get chummy, beers are been drunk and everyone seems to be having a good time by the fire. It’s getting close to almost midnight when Wayne says he has to go for a quick walk. “I will go with you.” Wayne’s face drops as Tiara starts to offer such a thing, he thinks it is rather courageous for some stranger to go with him for a walk, but he is more then happy to oblige. But before he can show anything but concern, Tiara speaks again, “That’s unless you don’t want any company.” Wayne doesn’t even hesitate and quickly replies, “But of course I would love your company.” The two start to walk away from the fire, they are a good 200 yards away from everyone when they finally start to talk to each other, at first they doing the boring shit, you know where do you go to school, how old are you, what do you do for fun, you get my drift all the shit you do at seventeen years old when you first me a new girl. But then they start talking… “So do you come hear often?” Tiara asked Wayne as she slowly moved closer to him, they almost stepped on each others feet as they took their next steps. Wayne not being an ignorant person noticed she wanted more, she wanted someone to hold her. Wayne before he could respond placed his arm on her shoulder, after first to push her away in a playful manner, in which she pushed him back but it actually lead to them locking up in a hug position staring deep into each others eyes. As Wayne starts to lean in for a kiss he realizes he might be moving to fast for her and starts to pull away and decides to answer the question, “ Tonight’s actually one of my first times down here, but I think if you hang around here I can only hope to come by more often.” That got a smile on the face of Tiara, for some reason Wayne always had a way with the ladies, he always seemed to say the right thing at the right time. Tiara looks down at the dirt and quickly looks back up grabbing him ever so tightly. He pulls her close and starts to hug her; she looks up at him and says, “I might come around more often then.” With that Wayne decides he needs to make a move, he leans in for the kiss, she seemed just as eager as him. The two locked lips for the first time, it was like fireworks were being set off, so enjoyable but also nerve racking. You know how it is the first time you kiss someone you like, not just someone you want to bang. They hold their lips against each others for a couple of moments, but it seems like eternity for each of them as they are in paradise. Of course they have no idea that this kiss would lead to a wonderful marriage down the road. They both pull away at the same time, she looks up at Wayne again, “Maybe you should come up here alone next weekend, we will be able to talk more just me and you, you know?” Wayne loves the thought of this, he holds her close, “I would love that idea.” “Maybe we should be getting back, it’s kind of late.” Words Wayne did not want to hear her say but he knew it was going to be coming sooner or later. ”Yeah, we should.” Wayne goes in for another kiss; this one was not as long as their first one, but still romantic and great feeling. The two of them walk all the way back to the fire stride for stride holding arms, until maybe 50 yards away when they both decided to not to let anyone know anything. Right before they get back to the fire Tiara leans over to Wayne, and hands him a piece of paper with her number on it, something she had in her pocket since he went to get his truck, the exact same time Wayne had first mentioned something to Stephen about her. They get back to a fire almost out, they say their good nights to each other along with everyone else, both wanting to spend more time together but knowing that it is time to part ways for now. All the boys get in their truck and Wayne drives away from the ladies, and the one he longs to be with even though he has only known her for such a short time, he is greatly attracted to young dame. The whole way home the guys berated him with questions, no one really getting anything out of him. The whole ride home Wayne wanted to know what she was saying to her girlfriends, he wanted to know what was going through her head at this moment. Then he wondered when he should call her, so many things going through his head, it was clustering his brain, he wasn’t thinking right, he was only thinking about the girl. He would not be able to get anywhere until he called her or saw her next week, he knew that but he couldn’t stop his mind from racing. Until next week it was, even though that’s not what he wanted, and without his knowledge that’s not what she wanted either, they were longing for each other already, and neither one of them knew it.
