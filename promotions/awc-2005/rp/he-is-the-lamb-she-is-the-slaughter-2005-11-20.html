This was exactly the light that Adam needed to see creep through his window; not that of a star billions of miles away. He needed revelation, not comfort. Adam and Dr. Handle could only watch on, Handle making his own personal revelations. Adam didn’t find any interest in this. Instead, he asked to use Handle’s cell-phone to check a message he happened to forget earlier. Why he felt the need to find out what that message said now, he didn’t recall. “You have—one—new message—and—one—old message.” The automated tone was always welcome. Fuck listening to Omnikon executives bitching unprofessionally about how fucked up their situation is, Adam doesn’t care about that. The obviously fuming Mr. Temple had disappeared out of sight. “Well, before you go, we’d like you to meet another one of our clients. You two may be working together shortly, may as well get to know each other now.” Adam was still deeply chained to his thought process. He didn’t pay attention to the people talking to him. “Mr. Demorto?” “I’m sorry?” “Oh, no, I apologize for bothering you. But there’s just someone I’d like you to meet before you jet off. Rubin Demorto, meet Adam Masters. Our prime specimen, thus far.” And then, the Educator stepped into Adam’s sight, with an extended hand. Yes, the Educator. “Pleasure to meet you.” He says, extending his hand. But Adam doesn’t move. He only can stare. “Didn’t your mum teach you it isn’t nice to stare? Such a rude set of manners on this tart, eh Frazier?”
