Like A Cretin On A Rape Scene

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/9 Azagtoth: ”Oh yes… Oh YEEESSS… I can smell it. Your FEAR. And it smells… good.” Angelica tries to draw further away from the hellish image of light and shadow, but can’t as her feet are bound. So are her hands. And her mouth. She isn’t moving, or speaking, merely watching as Azagtoth dances around. The warehouse is empty, void of any sort of human interference save the two figures on the far back wall of the building. Angelica, her clothes torn and ripped, bound and gagged and lying against the wall. Azagtoth, in all his unholy glory, face screaming white in the murky darkness, the pale silhouette cut here and there by holes and lines of black where the paint covers his eyes or mouth. In his hand, the wicked, saw-edged hunting knife that has seen and felt many a body in it’s time. Azagtoth’s clothes are soiled as well, but not too noticeably: it was Angelica after all who was dragged, bound and gagged, into the warehouse. All the way from the hotel. Angelica fingers the tape around her wrists and finds that it is not going anywhere. She feels herself go weak, every time Azagtoth comse closer and bounces the knife up in the air. Every step Azagtoth takes towards her sounds like the bells of Death in her ears, and yet when an equal amount of steps leading away from her inevitably follow, it does not make her feel any better. It is merely prolonging that which will happen sooner or later. Azagtoth can hardly contain himself. Seeing the arrogant whore in such a helpless, defeated state makes him want to burst into laughter. Every bruise, cut and abrasion on her skin makes Azagtoth’s blood dance with joy. Finally, after these long, long months, he could put an end to this travesty. How this wench was able to fool Him, Azagtoth doesn’t know. But he knows that she will be sleeping in the One-Eyed One’s gallows by the morning. His ravens would feas on her entrails. And Azagtoth would make sure that she was sent there in a glorious way. A glorious end for a vermin. Azagtoth: ”You stupid bitch. Did you really think that you could throw yourself at my leader like that? Drape your body over Him like you were some sort of a personal plaything? How could you! You arrogant, inane whore! HOW COULD YOU? HOW COULD YOU? After all that I’ve done for my leader, gave him my life, my soul and my blood… You, you WALTZ in like you had been there for ever! I will not let that, OR YOU, live! No-one takes my leader away from me!” Azagtoth walks over to the sheet-metal wall, tapping on it slowly with his knuckles, then, slowly, grinning a wicked grimace towards Angelica, he begins sliding his knife against the wall. Back and forth. Back and forth. The agonized screeches of metal meeting metal, neither parting ways, are disturbing. Like needles in your eardrums. And yet in Azagtoth they only seem to induce glee: he begins to laugh, his grating, whiny voice rivaling the wear and tear of the knife-edged symphony echoing forth from the tortured metal wall. Angelica can only cry: her tears fall in a steady order onto her shoulders, making her exposed flesh shine in the cold moonlight. And still Azagtoth laughs. Azagtoth: ”Yes, weep! Weep, whore! Your whining is like music to my ears. Oh, you have finally made yourself useful in some way… FINALLY I see what my leader must see in you! You shivering piece of flesh… You must make everyone feel superior to you. Because that’s what everyone else is: SUPERIOR TO YOU. You waste of life!” Slowly, still grating the metal with his knife, Azagtoth steps closer, moving along the wall like a shadow in a bad nightmare. Angelica tries to draw back, but fails as her bonds stop her. She can only stare in wide-eyed terror as Azagtoth advances, holding her future in his hand, running the edge of her destiny against the corroded walls… Suddenly, he’s next to her. Azagtoth’s breath smells cold and dusty like a morgue. His voice is low, far lower than his age would have it to be. He speaks like the Grim Reaper. His face is horribly distorted, the white and black contrasting and clashing like two armies on a battlefield. Angelica shuts her eyes: every moment of her abduction is starting to come back. How Azagtoth ambushed her in her hotel room, ripped her away once she was speaking to Hate on the phone. How he had juggled her from place to place, dragging her on all fours like a dog. The kicks. The punches. And always the threat of that wicked knife looming above her, waiting like the blade of a quillotine to have the final say. She hopes the blade does not fall tonight. Azagtoth: ”You think you’re so clever, going through my stuff? What did you hope to find? What? Something incriminating? Something that would label me as untrustworthy? You foolish little bitch. You should understand by now that nothing that I do can be viewed as untrustworthy by my leader. He understands me. He sees things the way I see them. He… he… I don’t want you to interrupt these things! And now, you have seen what I carry in my bag… what I look at every night, what I collect, what I CHERISH above everything else.” Azagtoth’s breath is right in her ear. Even with her eyes closed, a visual of the black-clad youngster burns deep into her conciousness. It is too much. She weeps again, and Azagtoth laughs, a sinister kind of laugh that sends chills running up Angelica’s spine. Azagtoth: ”You think my collection is disgusting. You think that I am a monster because of what you have seen. But my little mementoes, they are a part of me. They will always be a part of me. Tell me… do you want me to take a memento of YOU? I could keep you forever… Forever…” Angelica’s eyes spread open and she looks at Azagtoth, pure fear gripping her heart in it’s clawed grip. What… Mementoes? To end up in a bag like… who knows who else. End up in gruesome bits, to be fawned over by Azagtoth, cherished and loved for ever and ever… The gag in her mouth muffles her scream, and once it becomes aware that she won’t stop otherwise, Azagtoth presses two of his long-nailed fingers on Angelica’s windpipe and squeezes to stiffle her attempted scream into a gurgle. Azagtoth: ”You foolish whore, you’d like that, wouldn’t you? You’d like it if I did things to your body. WONDERFUL things. Things you can only dream about, and not remember them in the morning. Things that make you shiver on the outside yet make your soul burn at the merest thought of them. Things of ultimate pleasure. Because, you know, there is something my leader taught me. Pain is pleasure. And believe me… I have become quite skilled in both. You know I would do it. And you know you would enjoy it. But…” Azagtoth suddenly draws back and pulls Angelica over, making the girl fall onto her side and roll onto her stomach on the cold, concrete floor. She screams again, but her gag prevents any noise from coming through. Azagtoth’s voice is again furious, filled with poison that drips in pure black with every syllable he utters. Azagtoth: ”But you do not deserve it! You’re not worthy! Can you believe that? You better. No, I think I will slit your throat. Butcher you like a cow. And I will dump your body in the nearest woods I can find, and that will be the end of it. No more of 'my Angel”. No more of you. And no more of your eternal presence, trying to rob my leader from me! You have crossed a line I am not willing to forgive you. Now, it is my turn to cross a line. A line I have crossed in the past… And a line I am almost TOO eager to cross again. Your loss, whore.” Here it comes. Angelica’s eyes flicker upwards towards Azagtoth, as the boy slowly kneels down. His face is a mask of insanity, twisted in a grin not born of this world. The knife in his hand seems to sharpen the closer it gets towards her exposed neck. Violently, Azagtoth twists her head out of the way. She is completely helpless. In her terror, she tries to swallow her gag, thrash, do whatever to get away. But to no avail… It is of no use. The knife descents. Angelica closes her eyes, biting her lip until it hurts. Not like this. She can hear the blade slowly sliding through the air. Or maybe she’s imagining it all. She hopes she is imagining it. Unfortunately, she is not. Hate: ”Enough, Azagtoth.” The blade halters. And stops. Azagtoth turns around, completely surprised and stunned. The look has been wiped away from his face, replaced with fear. Angelica cries again, but this time they are tears of joy and not of terror. Hate, his massive frame cloaked in the black trenchcoat, his shaven head still blood red. He stands in the middle of the warehouse, the rusted double doors slowly swinging open behind him as if he walked straight through them. Hate is eyeing Azagtoth coldly, his calm exterior barely containing the rage burning within. His hands clench into fists, and Azagtoth hurriedly backs away, his knife skittling down onto the floor. Hate: ”Enough, Azagtoth. I see you have had your fun. Now, we do not have the time for this. There is still too much to do. And chasing down your personal vendettas is something we do not have the time for.” Hate strides forwards, ignoring the shivering Azagtoth who has sunk onto his knees, too afraid to even look at Hate anymore. As Azagtoth presses his painted forehead against the concrete, silently praying for his heathen gods to grant him mercy, Hate kneels down and rips the gag away from Angelica’s mouth. Her crying becomes audible immediately, but a sharp backhand from Hate shuts her up, turning her admiring gaze into a surprise, then into fear as well. Seemingly without much effort, Hate rips the tape binding Angelica’s hands into two and stands up, watching the girl get up on her knees in an uneasy manner. Hate speaks, cold like the night air, his words ringing like those on an obituary. Hate: ”My Angel… I do not know what you have done to upset Azagtoth so. But I insist you cease whatever it is that is causing this bickering amongst you two. But there is always two sides to a conflict. I am not resting the blame on your delicate shoulders, my Angel, but I advice you to keep your little tongue in your pretty mouth. Eventually, there will come someone down the line who will rip it out. As for you, Azagtoth…” Quickly, before Azagtoth can utter a word, Hate walks over to him and grabs him by his neck. With a single hand, Hate straightens up, dangling the skinnier Azagtoth in his grip. Azagtoth involuntarily claws at Hate’s arm, trying to breathe in the iron grip, but fails. Hate merely stares at his subordinate as the man slowly starts to pass out from oxygen deprivation, his tongue hanging out from his mouth like a fat, purple slug. As Azagtoth’s eyes begin to glaze over, the iris’ still locked on his beloved leader, Hate lets go, leaving Azagtoth in a pile on the floor. Hate looks down on his gagging, gurgling subject, slowly wiping his hand on his jacket. Hate: ”As for you… I am very disappointed in you, Azagtoth. What you do in your own time is none of my concern. But my flock… My flock is sacred, Azagtoth. They’re all equally protected, whether it is you, my Angel or one of our lowest acolytes. They are all deserving of my protection. If you feel like you can violate the sanctity of the protection I offer in my wisdom, go right ahead. Break it. Shatter my trust like a mirror into millions of pieces. But remember, those pieces will come back to cut you. And you will lose, Azagtoth. We have proven this once before: we can prove it again. You are nothing to the Fifth Horseman, you do not stand equal to Hate. Remember this, my disciple, should you wish to live longer.” Without looking at his battered followers, Hate walks out, his face screwed in concentration. There was an actual reason for him lashing out at his believers like this: it was AWC that was causing his mental image to be so upset. But why? Why should he care about a smoothed, pretty-boy imaged bunch like the AWC to be so upset? Slowly, Hate realized that the business he had so mockingly taken up had started to grow on him. But he hadn’t started to grow on the business. It still loathed him. But he craved IT. And Hate did not want to be dependant on anything beyond himself. And he would prove it, come hell or high water. Fresh! would see blood. Crimson red. Cleanse the Impure. Raise the Warcry.
