How Red Rock met Boolie

29 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th Sept 2005 We find ourselves once again in the shit pit flat where Red Rock spends most of his spare time watching TV, goofing around and pissing off his neighbors. Today the mood isn't as light hearted and joyous as it sometimes is and hasn't been for a while. Red Rock, for the last few weeks had fallen subject to a barrage of poison pen letters and the plagued Red Rock's mind since the 1st one arrived. It was something Red Rock thought he could handle and despite the fact Red Rock tries to put on a concrete front, he is but a man and like every member of the human race he has emotions and feelings. The letters hacked and clawed at his thick stink like a pick ax and eventually broke him down but not to the point where he was going to give in. Until last week on Fresh where it all reaches boiling point and Boolie had been kidnapped. It is raining outside and the room is all in black and white. Red Rock is sat down on a shoddy wooden stool by the window with his elbow placed on the ledge and his chin rested on his hand. His face is spelling out a feeling of loneliness and concern, his thoughts are obviously with his best friend as he runs over and over again in his head all the possible things The British Bomber could be doing. Over Red Rock's record player he is playing “Every Rose Has It's Thorn” By Poison as he stares out the window as the rain trickles down. Greg: Hey Red mate cheer up! Suddenly the scene reverts to colour with the sound of the record player's needle scratching thus stopping the music. Red Rock: Greg?! How did you get out. I though D'avid kept you chained up in the bath tub? Greg for those who don't often look in on Red Rock's escapades, Greg is the sex slave prisoner of his perverted dutch ginger neighbor D'avid. Greg: Oh, yeah...he's put this on me now Greg yanks what looks like a dog collar, which is around his neck. Red Rock: What's that? Greg: It electrocutes me if I get any further than 300 ft away from this place. Red Rock: D'avid really is a mentalist. Let me help you out of that I'll disable the device. Greg: No don't bother mate, to be honest and don't tell D'avid but I don't mind being his prisoner, back in Liverpool where I'm from I was about to be kicked out of my house so I get a free home and he feeds me. Red Rock: But I thought he bummed you and stuff? Greg: No, not at all. He's just a little obsessed with me all I have to put up with is a bit of electrocution every now and then. Red Rock: Right.... Greg: It's pretty sexy actually. Red Rock: O...k Red Rock raises his eyebrow as the realization that he is shacked up with a bunch of nutters. Red Rock: This is my life...I'm stuck living with a bunch of retards. I got D'avid, a ginger weirdo who keeps muscular guys captive to perv over, and I got a muscular guy who secretly likes being kept captive by a ginger weirdo. D'avid pops out of front behind the breakfast, his abrupt and sudden entrance in to the convocation makes Red Rock jump and loose his breath. Red Rock: GAH! D'avid how long have you been behind there? D'avid: six hours! Red Rock holds his hands out as if to say “what the fuck?!”. Red Rock: Si..Six hours? Doing what? D'avid: D'avid walks from behind the breakfast bar wearing a rubber glove and appears to be wearing nothing on his bottom half. Red Rock simply rolls his eyes and turns his back to D'avid. Where any other person would shield their eyes in shock Red Rock has been subject to D'avid erratic behavior ever since he moved in to his flat and this has become somewhat normal to him. D'avid: Any news on Boolie by the way Red? It sure is one person less quiet than usual here without him. Red Rock: No I haven't even received this video The British Bummer said he was going to send. Red Rock sits back down on his shoddy wooden stool, the legs of it are loose and the seat wobbles as Red Rock parks himself there. Red Rock: This does make me realize that I took advantage of Boolie. I always belittled him and made him out to be a retard. D'avid: He is a retard though. Red Rock: I know but I didn't have to remind him every day. We sure do have some fun memories though. I still remember where we first met back when I was 18 years old. Red Rock, D'avid and Greg all look up in to the air as the whole room goes wavy as we go back in time diddlydodiddlydodiddlydo! Yes it was back when I first started wrestling in the USA just after I finished training up in the UK with Duncan “The Bullet” Williams. Not many of the kids liked me or Boolie, so losers stick together. We find out selfs five years earlier at a small minor federation based somewhere in America. It looked like a gym where all the young aspiring wrestlers trained. There is all sorts of things around the gym that people can train with. There are about 8 young aspiring wrestlers, which included a younger Red Rock and Boolie. All the wrestlers stood in a row with Red Rock on the far left between some fat sweaty blond kid and Boolie was stood one in from the right between a skinny Indian lad and a tall muscular jock. They all stood nervous yet eager to meet their new trainer then suddenly the sound of the gym door echoed round the large high roofed gym. ???: Don't help me! I can do it on my own! The noises of a man struggling and grunting met the sound of the door banging. After a further 2 or three minutes of this the doors slowly opened behind the force of a man in a wheel chair backing up through the doors. All the wannabe wrestlers turned around and looked at each other as the man caught his breath and wheeled him self over to park in front of everyone. He sat there in a pair of baggy blue and white verticle striped jogging bottoms and a navy blue vest. He was extremely muscular and appeared to have a standard military hair do. His name was Brick Turner. Brick: Right you little BASTARD! I am Brick Turner and welcome to Brick Turner's school of Wrestling! The students continued to look at each other awkwardly whilst trying not to make it obvious they were trying not to notice this man was disabled...except Red Rock who was staring straight at him with his eyes wide open. Brick: Whatchulooking at son? Never seen a guy in a wheel chair? Red Rock: Oh...Err... Red Rock panicked and turned Red and has to think of something quick. Red Rock: Yeah...err there's this bus of window lickers who used to use the library at my school Brick turned purple as he slowly wheeled his squeaky wheel chair over to Red Rock. He grabbed Red Rock by his t-shirt and yanked him violently down to his eye level. Brick: I AM NOT one of those dribbling retard you get in buses! I lost my ability to walk in the ring LIKE A MAN! Just like you will one day! Red Rock: I hope not, that would suck! How would I piss if I was by myself? Brick reached to his side, still maintaining eye contact with Red Rock before pulling out a clear bag pull of urine. Brick: One of these mother fuckers! Red Rock: Ew! I don't want a piss bag! Brick: If you listen to me and learn to do things right, you won't NEED a piss bag! Just like if José Palmer...if he was taught to wrestle properly Suddenly shadows cast over Brick Turner as he reminisced of the day he broke his back and lost the power of walking. Brick: That punk shouldn't of been in the ring...I should be famous! I'll get him...I'LL GET HIM! Indian Kid: Sir...you seem to have you're hands around that boy's neck Brick had both his huge hands wrapped around Red Rock's neck as his face started to turn different colors. Brick: Opps..Sorry son! Brick released his grab and Red Rock yakked and gasps for breath. Brick: Right...lets not start any lawsuits. Right everyone pair up and practice some submissions whilst I go back to my office and cry... Everyone seemed to easily pair up not taking a blind bit of notice to Boolie and Red Rock. At this point Red Rock isn't showing much enthusiasm as he stands with a brattish frown on his face and his hands in his pockets. He was trying to show he didn't care that no one wants to pair with him, but deep down he was hurt and felt rejected. He was just 18 years old and a million miles away from home with no friends and lodging with a family of Mormons. Red Rock whimpered to himself before looking up, way up and saw Boolie staring back at Boolie with a mouthful of brace and long greasy unwashed hair. Red Rock stared at him breifly and looked around at all the others practicing wrestling holds on each other. He sighed and walked over to Boolie. Red Rock: Hi...I'm Josh. Boolie: Helloshh I'm Brian but everyone called me Boolie! Boolie's Braces caused him to talk with a funny lispy voice and also generated a lot of spit. This time was a prime example. Red Rock wipe his face and shook Boolie's monstrous hand, which wrapped all the way around Red Rock's little hand. Red Rock: Bully? Boolie: Yeah but I spell it B-o-o-l-i-e because I'm not really a bully, I'm just tall...and stuff. Red Rock: Right o! Is Boolie your wrestling name then? Boolie: Yeah I think it will be! What's yours going to be? Red Rock: I was think Red Rock? Boolie: That's well gay! Red Rock: Shut up it's better than Boolie! Red Rock and Boolie begun to attempt some holds on each other and found themselves paired up every day and eventually became the best of friends to this day. diddlydodiddlydodiddlydo We now return to the present day back where we left Red Rock, D'avid and Greg still staring at the ceiling. Greg: What are we looking at? Red Rock: I didn't notice that patch of damp on the ceiling. D'avid: I thought you were going to tell us how you and Boolie met. Red Rock: ... I just did? D'avid: You did? Heh, sorry I was thinking about a threesome with Sharon and Ozzy Osbourne ooooh! Red Rock: SICK! D'avid what is your problem? D'avid: I fucking love celebrities Red Rock: Ah screw who you like I don't care. You're my only friend now anyway... Then it hits Red Rock that his only friend is in fact a dutch sex pest. His face blanks as his jaw slowly drops. Red Rock: Fucking hell my only friend is a ginger! Fuck this I have to save Boolie at testimony not for Boolie but for myself...I need more friends. D'avid: heh... Red Rock: All my friends always seem to end up angry at me or turn in to a psycho homophobe who kidnaps my other friends. Screw The British Bomber anyway he's a bastard I never like Brummies anyway...all they do is steal things and talk like retards. Red Rock snaps out of his rant as Greg and D'avid stare at him strangely. Red Rock: Sorry was I ranting again? I can't help it...it's the way I was trained! But for now I'm going to think of a way to get back at British Bomber, I'm going to make his life more annoying than herpies! Heh...heh...heh! Red Rock walks off to his bedroom as he begins to scheme plans of vengeance against the British Bomber. Le End.
