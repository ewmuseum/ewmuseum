All in a day's work: Giant Haystacks RP

15 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/15/05 All in a day’s work ||+The Ways of the Farm Part 1: The Alpha Male+|| They loom over the dirt road leading to the base. The red paint and white two by four strips crossing each other on the side stare over the seemingly endless fields of grain and crops. It’s the barn. The ominous structure basks in the shade of the tall oak tree at the side waiting for action. Or has it found that already? A squeal flies out the partially opened door and dies off quicker then morning dew. Tranquility restored. The grass surrounding the old dirt road shows signs of age. The tire tracks running through to the dirt beneath still remain showing of the work done over top of it. Squeal That sound echoes through the air again. It travels with a partner this time. The scent of a corpse being dragged out of a recently dug grave. They walk through the front doors together on the search to eat at the nostrils of all around. No one is there to feast in the aroma though. At least…no one that is visible outside. The scent continues to linger in the air. The closer to the barn we move the tougher it is to bear. The crack in the door is wide enough to squeeze through. Inside the barn the root of the odor is visible. A set of pigs hang upside down. The floor below bathed in blood and organs found within. Standing in front of them is the head honcho. The top dog. “Don’t worry boys, this will only tingle.” The farmer says toward the line of pork he’s ready to carve up. The light from the rafters finds the steel blade the farmer holds in his right hand. It’s a lengthy blade. Razor sharp from its looks. Little of the actual metal can be seen thanks to the dripping red substance clinging onto it. From what is visible though, it looks to be a fine instrument. At the farmers side stands a few men working on the already sliced pigs. Some collect the body parts. Others silence the squeals with a pair of electric tongs. Looking back at the farmer, a smile spreads over his face. Maybe from the fact that he will be making money soon enough. Maybe from the fact that he enjoys to slice and dice the pigs he raised from birth. The answer would be for both reasons. To gain his money, he needs to kill off the crop. The need to kill has become a hobby of his now. He makes it a game. “Where’s the fight in you Abner?” The farmer asks with a laugh following up. Abner, his friend at one time finds the cold blade run over his leg. One sweep and there goes the main artery. The blood falls harder than hail on a boys face while skiing down a hill. It hits the wood planks under Abner and splatters across the Framer’s black shoes. He doesn’t care for the blood. It only makes him want to take a bigger slice. Slice Around the hide the knife goes. Cutting and digging until it’s off. The Farmer holds it up and looks onto it in the light. The pink skin means nothing to him though. He tosses it behind his head into a box of other hides. Back to work on dear Abner. The pig that loved to challenge others. The top pig of them all. He was a champion of champions. One slice though and he is no more than a rookie. Nothing compared to the farmer, a real champion. Cutting an artery may be the least he does… Slice Each leg finds a way off of the chubby body and into the hands of the farmer. With each cut, a snicker comes from the deps of his throat. Slice after slice. Chuckle after chuckle. Abner is finished. The farmer’s helpers make sure to take good care of each piece. He moves onto his next victim. He was a recognizable pig. Not the biggest of the bunch. It was his downfall. Heidi was her name. One of the favorites around the rest of the gang. She was well known by everyone. The Farmer saw this. That’s why he decided why wait for her to become the fat pig she could, why not cut her up now. This way the others would eat instead of drooling. Slice The process went the same for dear Heidi. Piece by piece she comes apart. Less meat to cut this time meaning less time to finish off. Her remains find their way to the box with Abner and many others. “What the hell is this?” The farmer asks looking onto the next pig. He was small. Not much of a sight at all. The other workers stopped their other tasks and looked onto the little one. He hadn’t even been given a name yet. “Is this a joke? Saddle up a little one like this?” It was a mistake when they grabbed him. The farmer continues to look onto the young pig. He ponders what to do with him. Back and forth his mind jumps until it’s settled. Slice. The little pig meets his fate as well. The artery spills blood just like the rest. The farmer seems to enjoy himself with this one. A fun little project to him maybe? Yes. Dealing with a little pig that doesn’t even have a name seems to be quite the enjoyable task. Slice after slice… Chuckle after chuckle… The days work is done… “Hang tight for the ride kids….Farmer Kris will make it quick….” ||+The Ways of the Farm Part 2: Harvest Day+|| Acre after acre of land dance to the winds wishes. It’s much like watching a puppeteer handle millions of puppets at once. A push in one direction and they all sway that way. A shove the opposite way and they change their path. The swoosh sound they create when traveling in unison is all we can here for the moment. VROOM The roar of an engine sends that one moment into the ground. The mechanical brute makes its way down the old dirt road. The dust makes its way into the air thanks to the PikRite harvesting truck. In the front seat is the same farmer seen before. He leans back inside the tractor portion with his hands gripping the wheel. Behind him is a cart attached to the harvesting system. The cart holds about eight different men. They chat fairly loud in order to hear one another. The harvesting machine finds the tomato field and looks to begin taking in the thousands of ripe vegetables. VROOOM The track turns on and off the farmer goes. He lines up with the first set of tomatoes and instantly they climb up the track. Up…up….and they disappear. They fall into the shaking portion of the machine making sure to break them from their vines. Set after set.. Line after line… The field is harvested… The farmer takes the time to stop the mechanism. After the motor dies down, the passengers of the truck leap out from the back and make their way behind the harvester. The farmer doesn’t take much longer than them to find his way to the back. “Listen, I want you to separate them between overripe…nearly ripe….and perfectly ripe ones…” The farmer says holding up a tomato for each category he calls out. The farmer knows what he’s doing best. He makes sure he doesn’t make his mistakes. Mistakes turn into a loss in his book. A loss in his book turns into a sour taste in his mouth. A sour taste isn’t what he wants today. Tomato after tomato he tosses them into correct barrels for each type. After about half an hour of separating, they still have two thirds of work to do. The farmer needs a break by now. He takes a seat on the edge of the machine and grabs himself an overripe tomato. He looks over it and applies pressure to the parallel sides. It pushes inward. This tomato is not meant to be shipped and made in a food. It’s nothing. A no one amongst the others. The farmer tosses it off to its barrel. He reaches into the truck once more. This time he pulls out a pinkish tomato with spots of green still. He presses on the edges like the last and sees that it’s not as bad as the other. A tomato like this is better known in the tomato community. It has potential and spunk amongst others. It’s not perfect though. Squish The juices coat the farmer’s hands and he flings the remains to the dirt. He takes one last dig onto the track and pulls up a stunning red one. The shine that bounces off it ricochets across the fields. On and on the light travels telling everyone he is champion of all tomatoes. It’s top class. The kind people like. The kind Farmers make money off of. The kind people enjoy to eat… Bite. The piece puts a smile on the face of the farmer. He knows that’s good. He takes bite after bite until finally, it’s gone. The champion of all others now lingers in his stomach acids. The farmer looks back to his men and sees them tossing tomatoes around playing a game of catch. He stands and raises his voice. “Let’s get to work! I don’t pay you to ruin my tomatoes!” The men heed to his words and make their way back to work. Back to separating…. “Whether you’re pathetic or perfect….Farmer Kris still does what he wishes…” ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ ||+Late Night With Kris CarMicheal+|| Knee deep in a puddle of work, a young attractive woman can be seen. She leans back in her spinning seat and a creak comes out. The poorly put-together chair snaps back when she leans back over her desk. A nametag is visible in the left corner of the desk with the word ‘Angela Klein’ engraved in. She runs her hands through her silky blond highlighted hair releasing some of the stress. Looking out the windows behind her it’s no question that it’s quite late to be working like this. Angela even wonders of the time as she glances to the far wall. ‘12:37’ The arms tell her. A sigh pours through those lush lips of hers. At this point she drops her head over the desk and shuts her eyes momentarily. “Why? Why is it that I always have to do this?” Angela wonders of herself. “Ok, let’s just go home Ang…work will be here tomorrow still…” She pops her head up from the folders and pens and opens her eyes up. “No, I’m almost done.” She nods and picks up a purple pen with the words ‘Young’s Pharmaceutical’ written in white. Young’s being the company she works for. She drags a paper toward her and resumes writing and filling out the many papers. Half an hour passes.. Angela still hasn’t moved from her chair. The addiction to finishing has kept her here until one in the morning so far. Click The point returns into the pen and she drops it over top of a manila folder. Angela stretches her hand from a possible writer’s cramp. It is. The PAIN of doing so much for so long has caught up to her. RING RING The office phone startles Angela. After realizing it’s noting to be scared of she reaches over and lifts it from the holder. “Hello….Hey hun……yeah, I’m leaving work now….yes I’m really working…..No I’m not having an affair!....Yes, I’ll be home in about ten minutes…..love you to sweetie…” Click Angela returns the phone back to where she grabbed it and thinks for a moment aloud. “An affair…come on now. I mean…It might be nice to get someone who can last a little longer but Tommy’s OK. What the hell am I thinking?! I love Tommy.” “But does Tommy love you Angela?” A mysterious voice echoes through the office. If we thought Angela jumped at the ringing of the phone, we were just proved wrong. She nearly falls from the chair. “He…hello…who’s the…there?” She asks standing up from the chair. “You wouldn’t know me Angela…let’s just say at this time of night…I’m bored…” The voice says once more with a chuckle following. Angela scans the room looking everywhere. No sign of anyone. She makes a dash for the door but when she attempts to open it she finds it’s locked. “Dear Angela…where are we going?” She fights to unlock it but it won’t open. The door that had once locked from the inside now has been reversed. Angela is locked in. “You sick fuck! Let me the fuck out!” That laugh returns. “Now now Angela…The assistant VP of such a job shouldn’t be saying such naughty things.” She turns around looking around the room again. She dashes over toward the back right corner and finds herself an autographed bat signed by Ken Griffey Jr. “I have a weapon! So…st…stay away!” What do you think I’m going to do to you dear? I’m only here to…play” “I have a husband…I don’t need to ‘play’ with anyone buddy.” “Now now…I never wanted to ‘play’ like that Hun. I meant…let’s play a fun game.” Angela shakes her head telling him no way. “Never!” That sinister laugh continues to fill the room and soon enough the sound is joined by a thud. The reverberation of feet hitting the ground fills the ear drums. There he stands. The defect looks onto Angela with those menacing hazel eyes. His hair stands on his head in the Mohawk fashion. Over his body is his usual garb consisting of all black material. Angela charges the defect with the bat. She takes a homerun swing at his shoulder and Kris takes it. He keeps his eyes on Angela the entire time and even laughs at the impact. “Come on…are you telling me you dislike games?” Angela takes another swing but Kris catches it mid-swing. “WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU!?” She yells. “So are you in for the game?” Kris asks ignoring everything that escapes her mouth. “Can’t I leave?” Angela asks with tears now running down her cheeks. Kris nods. “Of course Ang…I’m not crazy or something. All I ask is we play a game…” Angela’s tears still run over her cheeks. She only wants to live. By the look of Kris and nature of his attitude thus far though, it seems like death is inevitable. “Ok….I’ll play.” A smile emerges in the lips of Kris. “Good….now here’s a question. Have you ever wished you could….fly?” Angela looks shaky on this question. She looks back to the window and frowns. “I guess I did….when I was a little girl…” “Good…real good.” Kris walks toward the only window in the office. With one swing of the bat, the glass begins to fall out over the streets. Swing after swing…a large enough hole forms. “You aren’t thinking about…” Angela starts before Being cut off. “Don’t worry, I have special safety precautions..” Kris reaches to the ground and pulls up a backpack. “You see, we have parachutes. It will be just like flying…over top of a busy Detroit street.” Angela seems to feel a little better about the situation. She even walks toward Kris. He hands her the parachute and she slips it on. “Now the rules are we will race to the bottom. If you win, you can go home to Tommy and even walk away with a thousand dollars. If I win though, we’ll be playing a little game elsewhere…” She nods to the rules and even takes the jump first. Kris isn’t far behind. They leap out of the skyscraper looking building and continue to fall. Further and Further… Angela is first to pull at the cord. As many times as she tries though…the shute doesn’t open. “Help, mine is broken!” Kris continues to fall though. He doesn’t hear her pleas. Finally he pulls his own cord and a large black shute comes out. He glides down calmly. He hears screams though. Angela falls faster and faster unable to stop herself. It isn’t long before…slam! Angela breaks through the top of a car. Kris looks onto her dead motionless body. He reaches the ground and approaches much like many others around. “I guess I grabbed the wrong bag. At least she won…” Kris chuckles to himself and digs a fresh roll of bills out of his pocket. He lies them over her chest and walks off down the street. To him, this isn’t his problem…. “Don’t always think you’re so safe…you never know when a madman will be around…” ||+END+||
