Gimme Shelter

25 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/25/06 Imagine it. The room is dark, fucking pitch black bullshit as the guitars from the Rolling Stone’s “Gimme Shelter” starts to play in the background. The song gives it that kind of serene feeling that, honestly, won’t be held too much longer as the camera goes down the hallway for a bit, turning into the room. In the fucking room, the future is evidently dangerous. The walls are littered with stains of crimson and the fresh holes that blew out from the weapons that were only used moments before. The furniture is all over the place, which quite nicely compliments the door that’s doing nothing but swinging back and forth. The place is a fucking wreck. The cause of this absolute chaos is strewn across the room. One of the causes is in between that door and the door frame, the puddle of blood growing as the door taps against his body. Another is flat on the table, face down, still smoking from the relay of bullets he received. A third, too—dead. A fucking bloody mess…skull and brains all over the place. All these men, all young black men, all dead. But they weren’t the only causes. There was another, too, writhing by the refrigerator, moaning and grunting. The blood was all over him too; it wasn’t clear whether he was stabbed or shot, but it was certain that his time on earth was limited. “TIMMMMMMMM…*cough*…ARGHHHHH…TIMMMMMM…” His screams were bellowing and heavy, each word requiring all the strength he could muster. At the same time, though, the sincerity in the words were sharp and soft, if that makes any fucking sense. I guess you gotta hear someone dying to understand where I’m coming from. As the moans started to get fainter and fainter and as the man started to writhe less and less, we find the last cause of all this, sitting with his legs out straight and his back against the wall, staring off into a distance that wasn’t there for him. His hand clutched onto his gut, the blood slowly spilling through fingers with a smoothness that was super ironic. Each breath he put out there made a little squirt out. “Liam…” Once he looked over at his poor brother, he noticed that it was too late. The boy was lifeless and…well…gone. Tim started to breathe a little faster, being accompanied by the tears that came from it all. “Liam!...*sniff*…oh fuck…Liam…what have…” Each word was a mess of thoughts that couldn’t be relayed into the masses. The life of Tim Martin was racing by. A man who was once a king with his brother was now about to join his fraternal spirit in the next world. The Rolling Stones are still playing in the background as we stay close to Tim’s face, the expressions slowly getting lazier and lazier. In his last moments on earth, he had to look back. What had he done? What had he contributed? Was he worth shit to a bat now that he was gone? How would he be remembered? And then it hit him like a thunderbolt—the killers are remembered as the killers. And in the long run, the killers get killed. “We look…to the past…to predict…the future…” He spoke to no one in particular, smiley with a sarcastic glee as he realize what had happened. “…right?” (The scene opens up outside of a convenience store. Liam and Tim Martin are sitting outside. Tim is leaning back against the building on the bench they’re sitting in, smoking a cigarette, while Liam is leaning forward, doing the same. We’re joining them in the middle of a conversation…) Liam: Nah, Tim, no way. I can’t believe that shit. Tim: The fuck do you mean by “can’t believe that shit”? I’m laying the facts and no matter if they’re hard, because that’s what this shit is. Liam: No fucking way. Tim: Ok, then, let me put it this way. How many fuck heads do you think you’ve killed? Liam: More than I can fucking count. Tim: Estimate for me, asshole. (Liam stops and think for a moment.) Liam: I don’t know…30 something? 40 something? Tim: About the same for me. And, let’s push this set of thoughts a little further: of all those assholes, you don’t think just ONE isn’t going to remember us or know who we are? Liam: Tim, we’re in the fucking Eagle’s Nest! Any fucker who even THINKS about putting a bullet in us is dead before he can even comprehend what his brain just did to him. We live that life, Tim, the life of Gods. Tim: Uh uh, nope… Liam: Ok, that was an exaggeration. But still…c’mon! When was the last time you’ve gotten shit from anyone and not have had him dead the next day? Tim: I don’t know, and that’s what I’m fucking afraid of. One of these days, one of our guys isn’t gonna come back all smiles. One of these days, we’re not gonna get a returnee, we’re just gonna get a fucking death wish and you know that. Liam: … Tim: Well? Liam: Nah, Tim, fuck that. We’re too powerful, we’re too good, we’re too…oh my fucking Lord. Tim: What? (Liam points to the three black men across the street who are standing on the corner. They are witnessed handing something off to a man, presumably drugs. The view goes back to Tim and Liam.) Tim: Mother fucker… Liam: HEY! THE FUCK YOU THINK YOU’RE DOING?! (The three black men turn to the two men. Instead of answering they walk across the street. Both the brothers slide their hands into their pockets slowly, gripping something—probably a weapon.) Tim: *whispering* Just wait. They pull anything funny, we kill ‘em and run, got it? Dealer #1: Tha fuck y’all want? Liam: What was that fucking shit over there? Dealer #2: What shit, mayne? We was just chillin’, s’all. Liam: Fuck that, we aren’t blind, we knowing you’re dealing. Now, I’m gonna cut to the chase, right the fuck now, ok? You’re in no neighborhood to be dealing drugs. In fact, your close to ending up in the fucking graveyard, ok? And unless you want to get there sooner than later, I suggest you high tail it outta here. Dealer #1: The fuck you mean? This ain’t no gang hood, kid! This shit is free! So fuck off? Tim: Suppose you haven’t heard of the Eagles, then. Dealer #3: Eagles? Those religious fags? Fuck ‘em. Liam: What? Dealer #3: I said fuck ‘em fags. Liam: Well, you listen here, nigger… Tim: Liam! Don’t! Dealer #1: Niggah?! Liam: The Eagles don’t appreciate your dirt-trash ass here. You’ve made it this far without smoke rising from your chest, fuck head, and that makes you one of the lucky ones. Now I fucking suggest you hightail it out of here before you’re dirt. (Dealer #1 pulls out a gun. A flash of a moment later, all the people all around stop and stare. Everyone is frozen.) Liam: What…you’re not too fucking hard now, are you? Where are the guts? You thought we were some bums? Let me tell you something, nigger…one bullet even GRAZES us, and everyone on this sidewalk gets you. If you don’t get shot to death, then expect to find yourself hanging from a fucking tree, ok? (The silence goes on for a few moments, until Dealer #1 puts his gun away.) Liam: Now, fuck off with your lives. Dealer #1: You bettah watch your fucking ass. Liam: Mhm, now, go run along. For a moment, there was silence, but Liam quickly broke it. “Tim, you’re a fucking nut job. Now, shut the fuck up, Family Feud is on.” And the silence returned. While Tim sat at the table in this state of mind, all that clouded his head was death. He remembered from the other day just talking about with Liam, his bliss completely counter reacting to the seriousness that Tim approached the subject with. Beyond the whole revenge-upon-oneself thing, though, was a lack of true motive. Why does a man kill? What pushes him? At first, he thought it was for religion. But in retrospective, in looking back on the whole thing, God never fired those weapons. There was no Jesus who gave Tim that fucking knife. While religion was a good scapegoat for the entire thing, it was not even close to being the ultimate reason. So cross that off. To fuel the religion, there was the hate. We’ve seen plenty of people murder during the history of mankind because of hate. But, really, did Tim hate the niggers? Maybe he was blindsided at the time, but it wasn’t the truth. Tim just wants peace, and if peace came at the price of killing what his people wanted, then so be it. He was brought up to hate, but it wasn’t his lifestyle. In killing, there was no money or fun, at least not to talk about. Any sort of contracted kills were given out to the small time soldiers, the guys who could use it. And killing wasn’t something Tim did to relax—it always seemed necessary with fake importance. On that note, though, was it fucking necessary? Is any death necessary? How about the animals in fucking nature? Can we deem their actions stupid and useless and shit like that? And then it fucking strapped his ass—Tim was a fucking animal, and so was Liam, and so was every other killer. They needed to satisfy that primal urge, the need to just murder. But all animals die fast, don’t they? At least the killers. “Liam,” he said, “We’re nothing but animals.” “Well of fucking course, man, but what does that have to do with—“ *SCREEEEEEEEEEECH* *SLAM* “Shhhh, quiet! Don’t wake these fuckahs up!” (The scene opens up in a bar. Tim and Liam are sitting there, along with the rest of the people in there, when from out of no where a guy runs in, covered in dirt and blood,) Guy: OH FUCK MAN! THEY FUCKING KNEW IT! Tim: Knew what? The fuck? Guy: They were waiting for me! Liam: They were? Tim: Who were?! Guy: Those fucking…those fucking niggers… Tim: What the fuck! Liam? You sent…let’s go into the back room, now! (They grab the guy and head to the back room, leaving the bar area. They had back to a small room and sit the guy down on a chair.) Tim: Now, tell me what the fuck happened… Guy: Well…uh… Tim: No, NOT YOU!...I want to hear what the FUCK you were doing from THIS asshole first. Liam: Man, Tim, I sent him off to kill those fuckers! Tim: What?! Why? Liam: I know they’re gonna come the fuck back! Tim: Liam, didn’t you think about a word I fucking said? The whole revenge thing? We can’t play with our lives like this, man! Liam: Well, shit, I thought he’d get ‘em! Guy: No! They knew it! We busted down the door, no fuckers were there. Then…fucking everywhere. I don’t know how I got out clean…but shit, Steve… Tim: Steve? Guy: Steve Parsons. Tim: Steve Parsons?! YOU SENT STEVE PARSONS?! Liam: He wanted to! Tim: He’s got a wife! What’s Marcy gonna say? Why the fuck would you send a married man? Liam: HE FUCKING WANTED TO! Tim: You tell that cock sucker NO, THEN! FUCKING NO! Guy: He’s fucking dead now! Why the fuck does it matter? I’m fucked, and most importantly, you guys are fucked too! Tim: The fuck do you mean? Liam: They know you’re an Eagle? Guy: Me?...no, I’m nothing but another body bag to them. You guys…you guys are the fucking prize. “It funny, chain’t it.” Check the clip, lock it in, aim the gun. “Ch’always walk around hard, y’know, but damn, once you lookin’ down death, even the hardest nigga become soft.” Deep breath. BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! BLAT! The bullets ripped into S-Laughter and his wife, animating their bodies for a moment before they just fell limp. The blood splattered and puffed all over the place, with their fairly nice living room now covered in the mist and guts. S-Laughter’s jaw was creaked open a little as, with his last ounces of life, he looked around his living room, trying to find something. His wife was faced down on the floor. They were in tact as angels, looking like that till the fucking end when they finally closed their eyes. Once Liam turned his back on the bodies, he never looked back. Tim shook up from the dream, after seeing Liam turn around. When Liam told him about that, the scene of the death of S-Laughter, it became cemented into his brain. The image, the idea of this leader becoming nothing in dying. And all because of revenge or whatever the fuck it was? “Liam,” Tim said, “All this killing is gonna kill us.” For a moment, there was silence, but Liam quickly broke it. “Tim, you’re a fucking nut job. Now, shut the fuck up, Family Feud is on.”
