School Days, Part 2 (The 'Pierre may kill me for the reference to him' RP)

13 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday 10th January 2001 Mrs. Bradley: Ok, Scott? Scott: Here.. Mrs. Bradley: Pierre?.. A Girl: Er, he's never here on time miss.. Mrs. Bradley: Well, it's the first day back, so you never know.. and, the rockstar himself, Chad? Chad: Yeah, yeah, I'm here. Mrs. Bradley: Ok. Amélia, I'll need to add you to the register, so remind me later on. Talk amongst yourselves for a while, I'm going to go and do something I need to do.. And then she left the room. It was like I'd just been abandoned in the middle of a field, tied up, with no way to escape; my first day at school, in a completely different country, with faces I'd never seen before and would rather not have just seen for the first time. I might of got away with the large amount of upper leg my school uniform was showing off, but otherwise I felt that the worst was about to happen. I looked around myself shyly, noting the different faces in the room and trying to remember names from the register. The guy in the corner with the long black hair, showing off an electric guitar; I assumed that was Chad, but I couldn't remember. The shy yet good looking American girls near them, talking amongst themselves, and the typical geeky pair of boys on their own, one ginger, the other one with a huge pair of glasses. I didn't want to attract his attention. These three girls around me, though, had set their attention on me. And I suddenly snapped out my daze, realising they'd been looking at me for a few moments. We were all sitting on stools around a scruffy science room bench, myself facing two of them, with one on the end as if she were mediating us. I quickly glanced at the two in front of me; the one of the left had black hair, perfectly clear skin, and obviously a bit of make-up on. I thought that would be banned in US schools, like in Portugal, but nevermind. Then, the one on the right, with blonde hair, more mature skin but larger breasts than anyone else I'd seen in the room so far. I imagined that this was the 'man-eaters' I was sitting with. 'They'd get on with my sister' went through my head, but I'd been told by dad to not be prejudiced at a young age, so I kind of scrapped that impression. And then I realised I'd better speak, but they beat me to it. Blonde Girl: Are you just going to look at everyone, or are you going to speak? She smiled at me. I couldn't tell whether it was friendly or not. I looked at her, smiling back a little anyway, as if to try and do it in a heterosexual way. Amélia Silveira: Oh, emm.. I was just trying to recognise people.. The nerves came through a little, and I knew the recognising was a lie, but it sounded normal enough. Blonde Girl: Yeah, I know the feeling.. er, your name's Am-ee-tya, right? Amélia Silveira: No, it's Am-eel-e-a, so sort of close.. I was still speaking shyly, but this clearly confident girl had took a liking to me. Or so I thought. Blonde Girl: Ok, well.. I guess it was close enough. I sort of felt like I was being tested, though, or interrogated. I wasn't comfortable at all. Black Haired Girl: Your name's sort of weird.. it sounds foreign.. I thought it would've been obvious that I was foreign. Amélia Silveira: Emm, it is foreign, I'm not from America.. The black haired girl, who looked slightly gothic to me, sort of started to look interested. Black Haired Girl: Really? You didn't sound it at first, but, I wasn't sure.. Amélia Silveira: I'm from Lisbon. The girl on the end, who hadn't spoken yet, sort of looked confused. She was brown haired, and not as good looking as the other two, but not a monster in the same respect. Brown Haired Girl: Is that near Jamaica? I laughed unintentionally at her stupidity, before soon realising it might be normal to not know where Lisbon is. Amélia Silveira: No, it's the capital of Portugal.. Black Haired Girl: Cool, that must be great! Amélia Silveira: Well, I guess it's ok, yeah.. Brown Haired Girl: I'm Marie by the way, and this is.. She pointed at the blonde haired girl, who'd turned around and spoken to someone on a table behind her. Blonde Haired Girl: Yeah, I can introduce myself. I'm Claudia, and I'm sort of the cool girl.. And this next to me is Leanne.. Amélia Silveira: Ok, cool, I guess.. I was really thinking that now this person is a bit egotistical. She just looked more hostile suddenly. I got the idea of those American movies I'd seen where there was a leader of cheerleading team, all stuck-up and snobby, but popular with the best-looking boys. I suddenly imagined this Claudia as her. Claudia: I'm leaving soon, though, to go to a school in holiday, and start my career in acting.. Amélia Silveira: Wow, cool, that must be.. well.. I guess you're looking forward to it. Leanne: Your name's really weird, you know, can you not shorten it? Marie: Yeah, it's kind of confusing me.. Leanne: What does it mean? Amélia Silveira: Emm, I don't know.. Claudia: It sounds sort of old-fashioned. Sort of like Margaret and.. Amélia Silveira: I think it stands for.. Claudia: And Valerie and Vera and.. Amélia Silveira: Emily, or something? I don't know.. Claudia: Hey, new girl, I'm speaking here? Can you just shut up for a moment? Her two friends, as if on cue, just laughed. I suddenly got the impression that these three weren't as cool as they said. Claudia: Honestly, your gay accent and your weird name and everything.. Things gone quiet for a moment, and she turned back around. The girl with brown hair continued laughing for a bit longer, and I just sort of tried to look normal and dismissive of her comments. Marie: Can we not call you.. Amy? That's what I'm going to call my baby.. Leanne: Yeah, I guess that works.. Claudia? The big-breasted bitch turned around in response, laughing for some reason. Claudia: Yes? Leanne: Should we call her Amy? It sounds sort of better.. Claudia shrugged her shoulders, turning back to the other table. Leanne: Ok.. I'm going to go off to the, er, toilet now because I couldn't get in because of my bastard of a brother this morning. Claudia: Well, I'm not going with you, so don't bother asking. Claudia had turned around again, flirting visually with a tanned lad across the room, near to the Chad guy who was now playing air guitar. Leanne: Oh, why not? Come on.. Claudia: No, I'm busy.. can you not take the foreign import with you? I wanted to respond, but I didn't bother, because I didn't want to fall out with someone so popular quickly. Leanne: Er, ok, Amy? Amy Silveira: No, no, I don't want to.. Claudia: I think you'd better, because otherwise you'll piss yourself later on.. Amy Silveira: Well, I think I'd, like, better not. Claudia was looking the other way, so I didn't see her reaction to my defiance, but I imagined it wasn't positive. Leanne: Ok, suit yourself. Leanne got up, going out on her own, and then for some reason without being asked being followed by Marie. I was now on my own, but suddenly I heard a voice behind me, saying my 'new' name, which I happened to not mind. Person Behind Amy: Amy?.. I turned around, and seen a girl of about my height, with a slight suntan, good looks and light brown hair. A look up and down showed that she was wearing trousers. I wish I'd done that, as I'd preferred them to put off the perverts in the class. Amy Silveira: Er, hi.. My nerves were wearing off, which was good, as I'd begun to sound a bit geeky. Person Behind Amy: I'm Sophie.. You kind of look a bit lonely, do you want to.. er.. I knew she was asking me to go over and sit with her. I hadn't noticed her before, although I guessed she was sitting near Chad, where a decent congregation of girls had been sitting before. I glanced back to Claudia, noticing she was talking to some people on a table behind her, and I turned and smiled a bit. Amy Silveira: Yeah, why not.. I stood up, and followed her over to the other side of the room, brushing my skirt down a little as if it would make it a bit longer. I got the idea people were looking at me, when in reality, they probably weren't. I sat in a stool opposite her, which was on a long bench, with Chad at one end and a group of girls at the other. We were in the middle, and I was opposite her and another girl. Sophie: I sort of heard your conversation with the bitch over there.. trust me, she's not as cool as she says, she's just full of herself because she's getting a minor role in some crap movie this year.. I smiled a little, liking this person better already. Sophie: This is Lucy, by the way.. who only started here about nine months ago.. Chad: But we all love her anyway. Suddenly this rough, deep voice from the end came in, clearly talking to us. This tall, dark haired guitar guy was now looking at us, grinning. Even though he needed a shave, I looked, smiled at him, and then looked back to Sophie and Lucy. Before this Lucy person could speak, though, her attention was at the room's door. Lucy: Here he is! Lucy sort of said it loud, attracting a decent amount of the 30-person-or-so class, who looked at the door and back to their activities. Chad: Here we go.. Lucy was looking at the door, and at the two people who'd just came through it. One was Leanne, who I assumed wasn't who Lucy was excited about, and the other was this tallish dark haired guy with a fringe and a bit of acne on his chin. He was making his way over here, her heading to him, and I looked back to Sophie who was just laughing a bit. Sophie: That's, emm, Pierre, who so has a crush on her. But I don't think she notices. Anyway.. Lucy was now hugging Pierre, which didn't seem to be approved of by Claudia, but I wasn't too bothered about it. Suddenly, as if things had suddenly picked up for no reason, this guy who I hadn't noticed next to me was nudging me in the side a little. He wasn't there two seconds ago, but I recognised him as one of the people from the table behind Claudia. I looked at him, looking him up and down, and smiling. Guy From The Table: Er, hi.. you're from Portugal, Claudia told me.. Amy Silveira: Yeah, Lisbon.. I just moved here recently.. Guy From The Table: Cool. I'm going on holiday there in the Summer. Just thought I'd say hi.. Anyway, that was how it all started in America. I'd been nervous moving from Portugal, and expected the worst. I thought that had happened after an edgy start, but eventually I recovered and instantly started to feel better. I thought I'd blown it by seemingly being disliked by Claudia, and who knows, if Sophie hadn't tapped me on the shoulder I might of had a very different time there. I was happy with things then. I was wondering if my sister would be happy, but knowing her, she'd been trying to drag herself into private rooms to get into lad's private areas, and so I wasn't too worried about her. As if I'd kind of ever been. And, what can I say? It went well, and my first day seemed to start quite good. Maybe I wasn't missing too much having left Portugal - I mean, it seemed so much cooler here, even if I'd been really popular at home. Then again, first impressions can be deceiving. Like, why was I here in the first place? Well.. now I know that. Thanks dad. Maybe next time I'll cause permanant damage to my neck, and then you'll of got what you wanted, won't you? And the sad thing is, that even though I wouldn't ever think bad enough of him to think he wants me to be hurt badly, I wouldn't at all be surprised if it was exactly what he wanted. I guess it should all go like that first day at school - at first you think everything's going to go wrong, and when it's about to, someone saves you by doing one small thing which is worth much more than it seems. Someone needs to save me from wrestling. Maybe Mickey Fitz-McCarthy, aka The Farmer, nearly did that.
