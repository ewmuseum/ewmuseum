What it All Means.....I

8 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/11/05 What sits before me is nothing but a belt. Nothing but a fancily made belt with all the metal trimmings and markings to display it off. It holds the name ‘Livewire’ on the front of it, and to many people, it is nothing. But to someone who has been a wrestler for some time, this belt, or any belt means so much more. It’s sitting right in front of me, propped up on the wooden desk and it shines. It radiates the bearers and their legacies, even if that may not be the brightest. Even for such an infantile belt in its history, it has a storied group of members linked to it. The Educator… Stephen Xandrous… Red Rock… And now myself. For each person they came to the belt and fashioned it in their own image, becoming the champion on their own terms. For the Educator, the goal was clear, to improve and increase the prestige of the belt, aspiring to mark it as a belt worth to have. His was the toughest of all goals in the group so far. For he was the first and shall always be as such. He cannot be taken away from that record and still stands as the one who has held it for the longest. All other champions will be marked against him in the history of the company. Stephen Xandrous, searching for meaning in his own life, choosing to search and use the Livewire as a guiding light for his travels, to inherit it and cultivate the legacy, hoping its power could be used to show the way to his own goal. Perhaps a bit misguided in the end as it served no purpose but that is what it held for him. Then Red Rock, the last holder of it before me. Again, someone who used it to highlight his own personal goals but his was loftier than most, choosing it to extend his persona and win himself over in the eyes of the fans. One would call it slightly arrogant but for Red Rock it was simply the only way he knew how to do it. And for that he was successful. What road will it take now? That all depends…. But what I do know and understand is the power of the symbol. It’s an immensely intricate object that is so simple when you look at it. You’d be forgiven for thinking it was nothing really other than a shiny object in the case of the Livewire belt, and even though sometimes it is, in the hands of a figure of power, of control and dominance, it can be something great…something legendary. Think of it like this: for each and every memorable person in history there is a symbol of their power and actions. King Arthur’s Excalibur, William the Conqueror’s Domesday Book, Brian Boru’s standard; for each person something that is an extension of their beliefs, their ideals, their lasting achievements. When you hear of the symbols they do not exist on their own, immediately you remember who was the principal holder of the symbol. Immediately when you hear Excalibur you’re cast back to the times of knights of the realm and magic, sorcery and love tales, but irrespective of the lore surrounding it, the one thing that everyone will know is that it was Arthur’s and he was the one who wielded and controlled the kingdom with it. So how does a symbol come to such fruition? Through commitment, belief and strength, through the ability to take control and succeed where others have failed. This and many more are attributes required but it is different for each and every person who desires such status. No matter what the symbol, it must be a representation of the character at large. If it isn’t, it fails right there and then. Which is why nobody cares about this belt, this belt that lies before me right now, sitting propped up on a desk, because it has lost all of its power. If I asked anyone to give even more than two things about the Livewire belt, could they give it to me? They’d have trouble naming one for a start. And that’s because nobody cared about it, nobody did anything with it. Nobody bothered to treat it with respect. Did anyone care that Red Rock had the belt? Did anyone bat two eyelids when I told them he was two days off becoming the longest reigning champion? Did anyone remember who the first champion was? No, not at all. Nobody knew, but I did. It held something for me because I knew what was before, and now I am the one entrusted with the belt, the next standard bearer for its legacy. Where it is going is totally up to me and I plan to ensure that it becomes more than just an afterthought, more than just something in the back of people’s minds. But that’s for another day, another day entirely. The symbol that concerns me right now is laying right around the corner, waiting to be born. Triangles. The next AWC pay-per-view on the calendar and yet again a crazy, extreme concept from Pearl Harbor. But within its infancy lies the very fragment that Harbor has not counted on at all. The power of symbolism. To Harbor, the Triangles event represents his pinnacle of ‘entertainment’ for his own ends. He thinks that by putting on this display that he will bring in more fans, bring them into AWC and revel in the profits. He’s missing the point though. It’s not about this event in particular that’s the problem, it’s all his decisions. Week after week, month after month, Harbor thinks of one crazy idea after the other to try and put across how cutting edge we are. Look at Battle of Britain, which didn’t have a single ring involved, look at Testimony which was a bloodbath and look at Super Series where our showcase match…was a hardcore brawl, highlighted by another wrestler admitting to rape. It was disgusting. We are a laughing stock of an organisation and show the utter filth that we dwell in. AWC is harbouring disgusting, repugnant, putrid excuses for wrestlers and there is no one amongst the so-called top stars in AWC that are truly deserving of the title of wrestler. But now, there is the possibility to change all that. Harbor has foolishly lain open the doors for all and sundry to have a chance at the top prize in AWC…the Transatlantic Title. He has left it open for no less than nine places to be filled and have a chance at it. All if you get into the main event of Triangles, situated in the monstrosity. And here lies the perfect opportunity to stake my claim…and my symbol. The Pyramid Structure. Nothing is being claimed as grander, more epic and more fantastic than this structure for Harbor. It is his pride and joy and exactly the moment where I can make the everlasting statement to stop him from. Picture it if you will…many of the AWC’s roster laying down and out by the bottom of the structure and standing atop the structure is the new Transatlantic Champion…Jack Murphy. Has a nice ring to it, even if I do say so myself… Within lies the power of symbolism. This is Harbor’s crowning achievement and the antithesis of everything that I stand for. I believe in the power of wrestling and how these extreme concepts have no place in the wrestling ring at all. So what will it do, when I stand alone at the end of Triangles, atop his structure and more powerful than Harbor ever thought possible? Triangles will be destroyed as a symbol for Harbor and reborn as the ultimate symbol of my cause. It will be a site of pilgrimage and no matter what happens after the fact it shall always hold the very tenet that I profess and practice each and every week. That true wrestling is greater than any brawling ever could be…. It is one thing to be powerful, it is another to have a symbol that will link you always… That is true immortality.
