'The Urge to Fit in Series' Chapter 1: Introduction part 2

30 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/09/05 And now for Ian English In the wrestling business every week we see great battles fought. In AWC alone every week we see people kick, claw and scramble their way to the top of the mountain. AWC is a fairly new born federation and therefore as most would see it things are there for the taking. We see friends battle friends, foes face off, countrymen joining together and then imploding for the inside. In the short time I've been in AWC I've even seen contested the ancient Battle of 'Britain'. England vs Scotland vs Wales vs the Republic of Ireland. Through time Wales has battled England. Scotland has battled England and I'm well sure Ireland has battled England. The only one to come out on top by the way was Ireland but that's besides the point. We won't get into that right now. This tried at tested battle has been fought over centurys, decades and years. And it continues to this day with the biggots that each country posses towards the other....well towards England anyway and vice versa. Bloody battles, British Battles, even feckin pykeys battling. But there was always one premise, one battle on which professional wrestling was always based: Good vs Evil Every battle ever fought, every story ever told has been based around this. Even the bible is based around it. Go see a movie, any movie (except maybe one with Ben Afleck in it) and you'll see it. Wrestling was no different. You'd have your smiling good guys, the blue chippers. They'd play by the rules with a teethy grin from ear to ear, slap hands with the fans and always try to win the honourable way. For without honour where's the victory? Us fans knew our role here, oh we cheer these guys! Then there'd be those mean cheating villains. Slobbering, scathing, cutting, poking and proding, doing everything within their power to come out on top with the W. These gys were dark and broody and certainly no role models for the on-watching kiddies. What with their constant eye gouging and crotch punching, we would definitely point our thumbs down at these. And if they were really bad we'd hold our noses and pretend they stank of smelly things! There is was. Simple right? What nobody realised though is that line between good and evil was not a solid brick wall like we all thought. No it was simply a line drawn in the sand and when the time was right, someone just came along and stampeded all over it. This has caused an air of unceratinty where there really is no clear cut good guys and bad guys any more. No real babyface, no real heel, just mixtures of both. All Hail the Anti-Hero This sudden surge in it being cool to cheer for the bad guys brought on the era of what is called 'The Antihero'. An Antihero is defined as being a character who is characterised by the lack of traditional heroic qualities. Qualities like idealism and courage. Or in Ian English's case a set of balls. But despite these guys lack of idealism and courage we find ourselves, in this generation, cheering these loveable rogues. See when we go into this match on Sunday I will be classed as the 'babyface'- the good guy for all the world. Ian English will of course be the dastardly villain. But no matter how hard we try on TV to protray me as a good guy and Ian as a fucker the fans will make up their own minds anyway. There'll be fans in that arena on Sunday that will boo me and there's fans that will cheer me. There will even be some morons that will cheer Ian English- because ladies and gentlemen Ian English is a typical antihero. Now don't get me too wrong here I was always something of an antihero myself. Of course without the no balls bit! I never cared what anyone thought about me. I screwed people who thought were my friends along the way and said what I wanted. No matter who I screwed, no matter how many fans I cussed there would always be that section of fans cheering for Wade. So I too am a victim of the antihero. Did I wanna be one? To be honest yes. Why? Anything to fit in When Mike met Ian A younger time, 2003 in fact. We find Mike Wade backstage in an arena somewhere. He is seated in a dressing room with his ever present manager and hetro life partner and midget, Mickey Moore. Mikes saucy demenor tells the tale of a very driven and concentrated man. A man with a plan in fact. As Mike sits wrapping the tape around his wrists, gentle and free flowing, he'd done it hundreds of times before. The tape tonight though he felt needed to be extra tight. Because tonight he faced a test. His first test really in a new federation. Mike had recently received a contract with a new and up and coming organisation. PRIME they called it. 'Number 1 by definition'. Or at least that's what they claimed. LIke always Mike was seen as a threat almost immediately along arrival. Mike Wade to fans was like a red rag to a bull. He riled them up nice and good. Tonight he was in a tag match with some meaningless bozo against two of PRIMEs top stars. Joey Troy was one. Troy was really just a dime a dozen stoner wrestler who based his in ring personality (and aparently skill!) on Jay and Silent Bob. (More like Jay which is a pity the silent part would've come in handy) Troys partner that night was a man called Ian English. Ian and Mike seemed to have an immediate dislike to each other. Then again they could've had an immediate LIKE to each other. Neither really knew and probably still don't. Still tonight seemed like a good a night as any to find out. Tonights match was a little different than a normal tag match though. Because the bozo Wade teamed with that night was also the bozo he was feuding with at the time. And Troy and English were both feuding too. What a pickle! The slap of hand meeting wrist sealed the tape. Mike: Here we go. A confident Wade-- always a good sign. Mickey's compsure seemed startled by the sudden snap movement of Mike's wrist sealing his self made grip. Mike's readiness was met with sterling confusion on the part of the 2 foot 5 inch Moore. Mickey: So laddie let me get this right. Yer feudin with dis Stormm bozo? Mike: Correct. Mickey: But he's your buddy tonight? Mike: In wrestling we call them 'tag team partners' Mickey, not buddies! Mickey: Ah whatever. Mike: No not 'whatever' Mickey. I'm trying to make a serious impression here and I don't want you making people think that you think I'm bum bashing the guy I'm tagging with. Mickey: Sorry laddie....So you're feudin with this fecker? And the two gobsheens ye're fightin' are feudin too? Mike: Got it right on the money. A roll of Mikes eyes said it all, Mickey was getting on his last nerve....pissing him right off if you will. Mickey: Ya ready? Mike: As I'll ever be. Mike leaps from the bench that he had seated on all along. The sudden movement startled his little protoge for a second. Any sudden movements always bothered the jumpy little fecker. Mike began pacing up and down the room his chest rising and falling with each passing breath as he settled his nerves. The nerves he always tried desperately not to show. Mickey: So y........ He'd had enough of the questions. Mike: Listen Mickey I'm trying to get into my zone here. Mickey: Zone? What are ya feckin talkin about laddie? This feckin English fella have ya all startled. Mike: I'm sure I don't know what you're talking about. One of the 3 best lies in the world. Wade knew well, not only what Mickey was talking about, but that Moore was right on the money. Mike Wade for some reason was intimidated by Ian English, this was a rare thing. It may even have been the first time, in his adult life at least, that anybody intimidated Mike Wade. Suddenly a knock at the door comes Mike: Strange I'm not expecting anybody. Mickey: If it's a girl tell her I'm not here. Mike pauses before opening the door and stares at Mickey with almost a look of disgust. Mike: A girl? Mickey: Well a midget girl. Mike: Aha! It all made sense......Wade reahced for the door and pulled it open rather coyly. There he was. Standing there at about 6 foot 3 inches tall. Glowing orange hair and piercings to beat the band. It was Ian English. The first face to face these two ever had on live TV. Mike's face alone told the story. His frown suggested his disaproval not only for the ginger hair and the minging piercings but that this man had the audacity to knock at his door. The frown lines etched into Wades face as he grew angrier at every glance at the intruder. Mike: What the f..... Ian: Ha. He then turned on his heel and walked. As he made his exit he sinply shouted back a comment.... Ian: Just wanted to check ya out....Not up ta fackin much! That was it. Ian English took rule over Mike Wades soul with that one grieve comment. 'Not up ta fackin much' he said. These words haunted Mike Wade like no others. Mike had been picked on before as we'd seen. But none the extent of that. A simple knock, a glance, a cocky laugh and comment and the smart shootin' ever cocky Mike Wade was brought to his knees. Of course Mickey wasn't quiet so sure. Mickey: What the feck was that about? But Mike knew. He didn't even answer the question. Maybe he didn't hear it because his ears were filled with the shock of how he was treated. He was a big fish in a small pond before. But his shit would not cut it in the ocean, and now he knew it first hand. Of course Mike lost the match but he didn't take the fall. His shithouse 'partner' did. But it was still now Ian English 2 Mike Wade 0. Mike searched his brain for something, someway to even it with English. And he eventually found it. The PRIME 5 Star Title 158 Days- Ian English 129 Days- Mike Wade The only reason the word PRIME comes into play here is because (to be honest) it's what it's all about. Ian English has the PRIME record for a title reign at 158 days. Mike Wade held that same title for an almost record breaking 129 days. The reason that Mike Wade dropped the 5 Star title was almost his demise from wrestling. His own personal demon-- not drink, not drugs, not even the almighty booty-tang. It was the thing that leads onto the other 3-- Depression. Mike Wade set out with one goal from the second Ian English knocked on his door that night. To win the very title that Ian English made famous and rub it in his face. 04/04/04 Mike Wade did it. After beating 6 men to win a shot he had deserved all along Mike Wade let this go down: As Wade falls forward for balance Joey Troy drops his shoulder and the Marvellous One comes to rest across his back! The crowd chant, Nick cheers, Richard groans, Joey Troy drives up with his legs with any last remaining reserves of energy in his battle-racked body... Richard: NO NOT THE ULTIMATE VIOLATION!! Nick: TOP ROPE ENIGMATIC ENEMA!! Richard: NO NOOO!! Wade is going to feel so dirty and used... Joey Troy jumps off: it’s then that Mickey is revealed by the ring post hidden from the referee’s view by the two embraced wrestlers. At the last second on take-off he’s able to grab Mike Wade’s legs adding some unnecessary top spin, and rotating the finishing move. The crowd wince as the pair come down hard... Nick: A 180 TOP-ROPE PILEDRIVER!!! DAMN THAT DEMONIC DWARF TO HELL! Richard: YES, YESSS!! ...Joey Troy’s head lands on the canvas as Mike falls down on top of him: both men appear not to be moving but by some 5 Star fortune Wade has come to rest on top of the champion. Wesley James goes to make the pin: ONE . . . TWO . . . THREE!!! Nick: MIKE WADE HAS DONE IT - HE’S THE NEW 5 STAR CHAMPION!! The referee steps over the two prone men and calls for the bell. Richard: This is the greatest thing to happen to the 5 Star division since Ian English! If Mickey was closer I would kiss him!! Now that seems like a great night right? Not for Wade. It lacked one thing for him. The fact that he pinned Joey Troy's shoulders to the mat and not Ian English. It mattered not to him that Troy was the one who had dethroned English and on his first defense he lost the title to Mike Wade. It mattered that on HIS night in HIS crowning achivement Richard Parker says 'the best thing to happen to the 5 Star Division since Ian English '. English had FUCK ALL to do with this and he damn sure didn't need to be mentioned. But that was how it went down, it always went down like that. Green with envy always. Wade set out instead of beating English himself to beat the 158 day record he had set. It was along way ahead.....................but it was the only way to beat him. 'Fuck' Something had gone wrong. Mickey Moore came running to the living room in Wade Manor one day. In his hand he held some sort of Newspaper. He held it in front of Mike Wade so he could see. On top it said 'PRIME newsletter'. The big headline read: 'Ian English becomes head of 5 Star Title Division' Wade had clawed and fought and won for nothing. Ian English, the man he wanted to beat and better since forvever was now in early reitrment and was his boss. Game Over. Mike finally adopted the Michael Jackson 'If you can't beat em join em' appraoch and became an ally of Ian. However what Mike called an ally Ian English calld 'the man who I will live vicariously through' and did so. He basically acted as if he was Wade and was the 5 Star champion even making Wade hand him the title until he deemed himself worthy enough to carry it. It all became too much for Wade when a slip one night made him lose the belt and never to be seen again in PRIME. Was his pride damaged? Yes. Was it coincidence? Not in his mind. Ian English pushed him out. That's what he though. Wade gave and gave and gave. Ian only took. In fact Wade was so hurt that he made a list of all Ian Took from him. It reads: Tings Ian tuk off me: - Me 5 Star belt - Me feckin Midget (Mickey) - Bole of Spuds I was just about to eat (so he could heat his feet) - Me new Bonzai tree - Me pride :( - A packet of Milky Way magic stars - 2 doghnuts - A youghurt - Some sherbet The list goes on and on. However Mike Wade is a changed man. He has sat and pondered this moment for 2 years. The moment where he dethrnes Ian English. The moment he finally sticks it to him. AWC has granted him a second chance to finally rid himself of the shadow of that Limey bastard. This Sunday his chance comes. Mike Wade takes one more step toward fitting in this Sunday. And a new list has been made: Tings Mike will take from Ian: - His AWC Relentless title - My Pride - €20 Mike Wade Your (future) new AWC Relentless CHAMPEEN!!
