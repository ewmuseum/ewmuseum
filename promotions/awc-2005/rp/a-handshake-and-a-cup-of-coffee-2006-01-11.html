A handshake and a cup of coffee

11 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-11-06 The setting is a family owned café/coffee shop. It’s a small building with a few tables and fewer employees. The door separates one large window in the front. The rest of the decades old structure is made from fieldstone dug up in a local rock query. Inside are wooden-topped tables and thin cushioned metal chairs. The smell of fried onions, coffee, and burgers linger in the smoke coated air. Teresa sits alone at a wooden table. Her head rests in the palm of her hand silently cursing to herself, preoccupied with a stack of papers and a pen. An untouched cup of coffee rests beside her. With her brother Eric getting shot fresh in her mind and the guilt taking over her thoughts, the last thing she wants to do is paper work. Damn legal system. She didn’t even notice the figure stepping in. The bell tied on to the door rings grabbing everyone’s attention including the waitress behind the counter and the few patrons sipping coffee. They just stare. Stepping in is HSW’s own Covenant member, Deszaras. The Resident Evil is clad in a long leather trench coat, open just enough to see his uCw “Kings of Hardcore” t-shirt. He gives the watching eyes no satisfaction as he scans the small room. The only one not staring at him as if he was an attraction at a zoo was Teresa. Totally oblivious to anything around her, she continues cussing to herself staring at the page in front of her. Dez sits down in a metal chair in front of her. The padding is so thin it seems useless. His massive frame shadows Teresa’s face. She looks up and stares for a moment, surprised and shocked to see him. Dez picks up the top page off the stack of papers and gives it a good look, snarling before throwing it back down on the table callously, creased and wrinkled. “So, Teresa,” begins Deszaras coldly. “It’s official, hmm? You’ve jumped ship.” “Yeah, it’s official. This legal shit makes everything so tedious. Joey insists I sign all these papers stating I officially quit.” Her lip curls a little in what passes for disgust.” The Redneck Princess shrugs and sets the pen down. Teresa now picks up her coffee and takes the first sip. She had put 2 liquid creamers in it and not sugar. It’s just the way she likes it, hot and creamy with a mild coffee flavor. After she takes the sip, she sets the cup on the wooden table and sits back giving Dez her full curious attention speaking softly. “So, Deszaras, there must be a reason you have graced me with your presence. Please feed my curiosity…” Headstrong’s Resident Evil smirks as he strokes his beard. “I heard about this shit, and it immediately forced a thought into my head. Well, after the thought of grabbing a beer of course… and that thought was about ‘branching out’. I came to think of how good it could be for my Covenant to set up franchises elsewhere. When I heard about your trip to AWC, well, it made me want to ask you to join the Covenant.” Teresa nods listening to his proposition. A skinny redheaded waitress with a bad perm approaches the table with a pot of coffee, Teresa reclines the offer for more. Dez refuses the drink all together with an ignorant grunt and waves the poor girl away with a motion of his hand. Now, once again undisturbed, Teresa pushes the stack of documents aside. Her hands neatly folded in front of her. “Why should I?” Deszaras gives a little chuckle. “What’s in it for us is quite simple. You fly our flag and shout the word upon new grounds. But for you? For you comes the very simple, yet very effective comfort of knowing that your back is being watched. We may be very far away, but if you ever truly need us, we’ll be there. What more could a person want when walking onto a new battlefield alone, unknown and vulnerable?” Dez stands up, pushing the chair from behind him with a squeal. “So… what do you say?” Teresa sits still, quiet and pensive, for a few moments before standing up opposite him. Reaching out her hand, she says “You’ve got yourself a deal, Dez.” He takes her hand and shakes slowly before nodding his head and turning away silently, coat flapping behind him. Suddenly, Teresa finds all eyes are upon her. Recognition arise in those staring eyes and before she knows it, she has a small mob of customers and waitresses approaching her, wanting to know what the ‘deal’ was with her jumping ship like she had done and signing to AWC with no notice. “Oh shit..” Teresa mumbles to herself before gathering the documents and makes a bee-line for the door before they small mob, like zombies, trapped her against that small wooden table. She literally runs to her truck, tosses the papers across the bench seat, climbs and climbs in. Glancing out of the windshield, she sees the crowd rushing out toward her. “ I don’t think so..” She says out load before cranking the 350 of the Chevy truck, drops the transmission into gear, then speeds out of sight. Only thing remaining is the black smoke form her squalling tires.
