Explaining Myself

23 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/12/05 ‘Can you open the door please, I just want to talk to you that’s all.’ ‘No!’ Just picture me kneeling in front of the bathroom door in my hotel room and Jessica O’Grady planted firmly inside, unwilling to move. Turns out she’s a bit pissed off I just disappeared into the night. Let’s just say I’m lucky I didn’t tell her I was in a brothel, I convinced her I’d stayed in an all night bar. That had worked, just about anyway. ‘Look, I’m sorry I disappeared on you last night but you saw the way I was, I wouldn’t have been any aid to you with the state I was in. I needed to be alone.’ The door opened in front of me and she stood there, a look of rage on her face and full of bad intentions. She was dressed in simply clothing, ready for the journey off to Winter Warfare directly from Amsterdam so that I could get acclimatised as quickly as possibly and get some training done. For my benefit she had been at pains to make known. ‘NO aid? I was left with all those leering drunk men, especially Paddy O’Shea who was actually becoming more sober the more drink he had in him! I swear he tried to look down my top several times and if not for the fact the barman gave him more beer he would’ve continued! I was there as your guest and you treated me like a piece of meat!’ ‘What? No I didn’t!’ ‘Yes you did, I saw you with the looks you were giving people, like I was your next lay, I can’t believe you were acting like that, especially after last week…’ I was still on my knees but now began to push onto my feet, taking her hand and guiding her to the bed. She somehow complied, much against my expectation and sat down on it, never once taking her eyes from me. ‘Listen. I’m sorry for leaving you like that but my head was so messed up. Jessica, I know all the publicity work you’re doing for me and it’s great but you’re painting me to be this unstoppable menace. Let’s face it, I’m thirty-eight now and I’ve probably had my prime pass me by whilst I was slumbering in Siberia. I’m starting to wind down and no matter what you say I’m not going to reach the pinnacle of this sport the way you’re pushing me.’ I sat down beside her and brushed the hair away from her face. ‘It’s been a long time but I know that I want to have a family, I know that I want to have children, especially since…’ ‘I know.’ ‘There are more important things in my life than the wrestling. But damn it, there’s just something not right about the past few weeks. I’ve never had two losses in a row. To some people that isn’t a big deal but for me it’s everything. I just don’t know if I can cut it…’ She pressed her finger to my lips and I could see her complexion had softened, the anger had been risen from her mind. She was now more earnest and willing to aid me. ‘Ssh…you’re none of that. Who as the man that won the grand slam title shots only last month in AWC? Who was the man who was the Livewire champion in less than a month of his arrival? Who has been rampaging through the GTT these past couple of weeks? Sure there are setbacks Jack but look at all you’ve accomplished in the short time you’ve returned. If anything, your prime hasn’t even begun properly, you’re only now entering into it. So don’t think you’re starting to lose it, think of the future, and imagine that glittering prize that we both know you can win.’ With no more words she planted a kiss on my forehead and returned to the bathroom door, turning once more to leave a parting gesture. ‘Now, stay out of the bathroom I’m going to have a shower and relax before the trip. Don’t come in…or else.’ ‘Or else what?’ ‘Well, you’ll not know if you don’t come in. Your risk.’ Her eyes glinted and it left me confused. I wasn’t sure if…ah I wasn’t in the mood for flirting right now. I merely nodded glumly and she looked a bit disappointed before flitting off into the bathroom to relax. I fell back onto the bed and all I could think about was the night before. In the company of a delectable woman who knew exactly what to do I had simply let out all my pent up sexual frustration on her and she took it like a champion. She moaned when my crotch collided with her ass, giggled as she kneeled in front of me and screamed when I…well, that was an interesting situation… And I didn’t even have to use a condom. Always feels so much better without it. *** A bitter breeze greeted me as I stepped off the plane into Cologne, Germany. Jessica had arranged for the flight and a couple of press opportunities with me arriving. I tried my best to appease her but I couldn’t help being a moody bastard in every single type of shot they tried to take. Fuck ‘em, I wasn’t in the mood. Jessica noticed this also. ‘What’s wrong?’ ‘Just don’t feel like appearing in front of cameras right now, want to be preparing.’ ‘Well I’ve got exactly the right tonic for that.’ ‘Hmm?’ ‘You’ll see.’ Intrigued and very curious she told the journalists that I had not the time to be interviewed as I needed to get my preparation started for Friday. Pushing through the disappointed crowds there was a simple black BMW waiting for us. ‘Hop in.’ ‘Ok…where are we going?’ ‘You’ll see.’ The driver in the front immediately moved off once we entered the car and in no time at all we were on an autobahn to wherever the hell Jessica was taking me. Rolling hills and forests sped by us as we cruised along the extremely fast motorway to our destination. In no time at all, we arrived. ‘Well get out.’ ‘We’re here? Man that was quick.’ ‘Yeah, helps when you’ve a no speed limit motorway.’ ‘True.’ As I stepped out the breeze once again hit me, biting cold and chilling me to the core but I was warm once I saw what stood before me. Dan O’Grady and an outdoor ring. He was beaming. ‘Hey! You gonna come in and see what this is like or you going to stand there gawking for the entire time?’ Without hesitation I headed for that ring, uncaring about the fact I was wearing jeans and a heavy shirt, not even noticing the fact that Jessica watched me for every second. All I wanted was to get back to what I knew I loved for sure. The women could wait. Or suffer…
