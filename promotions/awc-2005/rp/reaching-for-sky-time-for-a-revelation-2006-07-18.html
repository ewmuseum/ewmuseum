Reaching For Sky - Time For a Revelation

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today 12:56 PM Krimzon Household First-Person (Kris Krimzon) Perspective In my life, everything has come easy. Every problem has been resolved with hardly any input from me. I’ve become so used to this that now I try to allow every problem to resolve itself. I’ve taken any burden that has been placed on me and transferred it to someone else. I guess I can’t “coast” through life any more. No, what I need to do is put it in four-wheel drive and fucking plow down everything that gets between me and my destination. It starts now. No longer will I wait for something to get out of my way, those times are long gone. It is time to take control and put things in full throttle. I guess it there comes a time in everybody’s life when they get a shock to their system and realize ‘hey, I need to grow up.’ I didn’t think it would happen to me. I thought I was invincible, that I could get away with anything and everything. If it didn’t kill me then, then it didn’t matter at all. If there wasn’t a direct problem, there was no problem at all. I guess everything has come full swing. The shit has hit the fan and I’m the one standing in front of it. Not my friends, not my family. Myself and myself alone. There also comes a time when one must questions one’s beliefs. When something bad happens, we work our asses off until we fix what has happened. Then what do we do? We thank the Lord. Why? If He wanted it be He could have not had the problem in the first place. We would have been fine. But no. Instead of taking credit, we praise the Lord. A natural disaster comes. If one thing is saved and the rest is destroyed, we praise the Lord. I remember when I was younger… I was fifteen years old, no care in the world. Grades didn’t matter, school came easy. Alcohol was in easy access thanks to friends. If something was bothering me, I’d drink it away, the next morning not remember there was even a problem to begin with. I always remembered growing from fifteen to sixteen. That little phase where it seems everyone and everything is growing up and you’ve wasted your childhood away. It is in these times that I felt so hopeless. There wasn’t a God damned thing I could do about it. My friends were already driving. These friends that I’ve had for years and I remembered playing basketball on the school court, getting in petty arguments, running around and painting the town red. I remember so many nights I couldn’t help but break down. I held it all in. Not a soul knew how bad I felt. Not my family, not my friends, not my girlfriend, no one but me. I knew that I had to get it out some how before I blew up on someone. All this canned in rage was bound to explode sometime. All the drama, trauma, pain, horror… all of it. Some day it would catch up to me and force me to do something I would regret. Many years I’ve known this… but never once did I act upon it. Too many times have I known something and not acted upon it. Too many times have I stood back as someone defiled someone or something that I cared about without even a whisper. Too many times have I decided upon a principle to stand by, only to ignore the principle when it had actually come into play. Too many times have I ruined a good thing. But no longer. No longer am I easy-going, laid-back, let-it-work-itself-out Kris Krimzon. It is time to grab life by the horns. It is time to set things right. It is time to be proud of who I am and it is time to do something I’ve always wanted to do. Make an impact. Step one of this plan… take out Paddy O’Shea. If I want to make an impact, I have to defeat the one and only Paddy O’Shea. There’s nothing better than to take a legacy of someone who has established himself and piss all over it. Fuck Paddy O’Shea. Fuck Adam Dick. Fuck Garbage Bag Johnny. Fuck David Harber. Fuck them all. It’s time for Shawn Harris and Kris Krimzon to take over. It’s time to establish myself as a true top contender. I’m done messing around. I’ve worked my ass off for AWC, but every time I make a joke I’m set back. No more jokes. No more fun. It’s time for straight-up ass-kicking fucking destruction. It starts with Paddy O’Shea. Now, I may be thinking a little bit too rash, but Hell. I got one life, might as well live it to the fullest. Might as well take chances, and if I fall on my ass then I’ll stand back up and try it again. If I die, then so be it. As long as I die knowing that I from this moment on, I worked my ass off. For the first time ever, I’m going to be completely and utterly focused on winning this match. Of course, I won’t show it all too much. On stage, I still have to be the old ‘fun-loving’ Kris Krimzon. Inside, I’ll be the God damn burning ember that refuses to die out. I will die before my shoulders are on the mat for three seconds. It’s time for a revelation and it’s time for an evolution. It is time for a new chapter of the Kris Krimzon legacy. No longer will I depend on the past. My father’s life was a lie. Everything I’ve been told, I bought. Never once did I question it. Now, I question everything. I don’t even know who sent the package, they didn’t bother to tell me. But fuck it. I don’t care anymore. People say I’m too immature. People say I shouldn’t even be in a wrestling show. It hurts, I’m not going to lie. If there is one thing I refuse to do, it is change myself because other people say I should, or change myself because everybody else is changing. I do what I wish, I change if I see fit. Now’a’days… I need a change. It seems like nobody takes me seriously, so I guess I’ll have to make them take me seriously. Paddy O’Shea is going down, it is as simple as that. It’s time to give him a revelation. I’m not the same Kris I used to be. Now … I matter. As usual, I shoot up from my sleep. I look to my right and my wife, Tamara, has all ready left the room. The light is on, the alarm is buzzing, and somehow it was my thoughts have woken me up. I roll out of bed, ruffle my hair a little bit for absolutely no reason and suddenly everything shuts off with a spark. The lights, the alarm, my television... Everything. Normally, I would be irate. There are two reasons why I’m not – one, I’m getting out of this damn house anyways. Two – I’m really fucking tired. I walk over to my window and pull the blinds open. The light outside is so bright that it actually knocks me backwards and onto my bed. I step out of my room, which is now light, and walk into the kitchen where Tamara is working away with food. “What time is it?” I asked, yawning. Tamara jumps. “God, you scared me.” I shrug. “It’s one o’clock in the afternoon, Kris. The power just shut off so I’m making cold lunches for us.” “It’s okay,” I say. I want to just get out of here. “I’m not really hungry.” Tamara stops working immediately. “Kris, is there something wrong?” “No,” I say shrugging. “Why?” “I don’t know… you just seem really weird lately.” “Well maybe I’m just tired of the life I’ve been leading.” “…what?” Tamara says, both shocked and confused. I sigh. “Nothing… I… I gotta go. I’ll be back soon.” “Where are you going?” Tamara shouts as I walk out of the door. A bad decision, I know. I don’t care though. I need to get away.
