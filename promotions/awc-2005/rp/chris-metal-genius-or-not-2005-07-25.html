Chris Metal = Genius... or Not...

25 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th July 2005 “That was a nasty brainbuster you landed on that girl…” As Mickey Fitz-McCarthy returned to his hotel room he was greeted by Chris Metal’s first sentence to him since he agreed the coaching job. As The Farmer embraced the words, he sensed disappointed in Metal’s words. The door swung shut behind Mickey as he tossed his sports bag at the foot of the bed. His red hair was wet as he stroked his hand through it and surveyed the room. He tossed of his green jacket and threw it on top of his bag, finally eyeing Chris Metal sitting in the recliner, the television remote in his hand and a video of last week’s Fresh! playing on screen. Mickey has got an amazing room in Madrid that was really close to the arena and a gym. The Farmer wasn’t sure if Chris was going to join him in Spain or even any leg of the European tour so he was a little taken back by Metal being in his room. The pair hadn’t spoken since the dinner that they met at, finished. Mickey strolled in and looked at Chris, deciding not to reply. He knew he lost. He didn’t need a glorified PCW jobber telling him that he had lost to a girl. Mickey sat on the edge of the bed, his head in his hands waiting for his lecture from his coach. “I like it…” beamed Chris, as he swivelled around to face his client. Mickey, realising what Chris had just added to his initial comment, raises his head from hands and looked across at Chris. “Wha’?” questioned the Irishman, not really expecting what Chris had just said. “I like what you did. That brainbuster is quite nasty, definitely something that you should use more often,” confirmed Metal, making sure that The Farmer understood where he was coming from. Mickey looked at his coach in amazement. Was he just approving of Mickey almost injuring another wrestler? Mickey looked on confused. “Now don’t get me wrong. I’m not telling you that you should try to injury all of your opponents. But that brainbuster was mean. You can use it to really shock opponents.” Yup, he was definitely giving Mickey permission to attempt to injure his opponents. Chris Metal was quickly leaving a mark as coach on his impressionable protégé. Chris reached out and pointed the remote at the television screen, letting the paused Fresh! show continue playing and that moment Mickey landed the crushing brainbuster on Amy’s neck. “It was an accident,” pleaded the Irishman as watched the screen and then looked up at his coach who was now standing over him. Chris watched the screen with a smile on his face. Mickey tried to figure out the reasons behind the smile. Had the years away from the ring caused him to hate it? Maybe he didn’t like the fact that women were now in the ring. Maybe Chris knew Hugo Silveira. They were both considered jokes in the wrestling world up until now, but Mickey needed any hope he could get. Finally, Chris was plucked from his happy place as Amy got the victory on The Farmer. Metal watched the screen and then turned to The Farmer. “Don’t worry about losing,” reassured Chris as he paced around in front of Mickey, back and forth between the door and the television. Mickey looked up confused. Coaches aren’t supposed to say it’s okay to lose a match. Maybe Chris Metal really was as much of a joke as most people said, and here was Mickey raising him up to be a wrestling god. “She has more motivation than you. Amy Silveira is doing it for her father, you’re doing it for money, meaning you win even if you win or lose.” Right there and then Mickey was confused. What exactly was Chris Metal talking about? He repeated Chris’s words in his head trying to make sense of it all and then it clicked. Amy was wrestling to please her father whereas Mickey had no real care if he won or lost. Maybe Chris was actually making sense. “So…” continued Chris, spinning on his heel and looking at Mickey, smiling a new smile. The Farmer looked at his stalled coach with some caution in his eyes. Chris smiled a twist, sick, happy smile. Okay so maybe that was too many adjectives but he had a twisted sick, happy smile on his face. Mickey stared at Chris waiting for Chris to continue, when suddenly Chris snapped forward with a belt whipped Mickey on his exposed arm. Immediately Mickey was holding his arm as a red mark appeared from the whipping he just received. Mickey grimaced as Chris continued to hold his new smile. “…we need to get you a reason to win…” Chris finished his sentence as Mickey rubbed frantically at his arm. Clearly Chris knew how to use a belt to whip someone. I suppose all his time in PCW with the Ream Team was well spent. Can I just take this moment to apologise to Pierre for abusing the Chris Metal character... … What do you men everyone does it? Well if you say so, back to the story. Chris cracked the whip on the floor at his feet as Mickey stopped rubbing his sore arm and eyed Chris, looking a little pissed off. “Like that?” asked Chris, his grin refusing to be moved. Mickey shook his head, although it was obvious from his reaction that no response was required. “Here is your reason to win…” continued Metal, not waiting on an audible reply from The Farmer, even though it was never coming, “you are going to win, because if you don’t you are going to be in so much pain…” Oh my god! This guy is a genius. Did everyone hear what he just said? Hold on, let me repeat it, “you are going to win, because if you don’t you are going to be in so much pain…” Mickey looked at Chris thinking a combination of emotions. He first of all questioned if this was the real Chris Metal that had been a wrestler. If he was a fake then I suppose his new reason for winning could be excused as an obvious attempt to be found out. If he was real, Mickey was really concerned for his former chaperon. Keith Chamberlain had introduced The Farmer to one of his former clients, turned wrestling coach who was the man that stood before Mickey in the hotel room, beaming with a big wide grin as though he discovered the meaning of life. Hold on, maybe he had. Moving on, Mickey stressed about his former chaperon who had talked up his coach to him. Chris Metal had just given Mickey the worst piece of advice he ever heard, even worse than his older brother Chris’s words on the day that he left when he said, “…when ye’r dealin’ with a few wee lassie’s, just rem’ one wee ting… if tha’ bitch doesn’t put out, get ‘rid of ‘er…” At the age of six the phrase “put out” had no bearing on the young Mickey, and even now, after living quite the sheltered life in the Irish countryside, The Farmer was still a little unsure as too it’s meaning. Maybe it was a Chris thing to be useless at advice. And so it continued, Mickey still tried to work the origin of Chris Metal and his brain and how it stopped working. “…so on Fresh! you are going to compete in a Spanish Bullwhip match…” A what? Spanish… Bullwhip…? Okay so maybe Chris’ theory had a purpose. Maybe Chris was shaping The Farmer to be a hardcore legend. Mickey “GRR!”-ed in his head as he looked around and then back at Chris, who flicked his wrist and cracked at Mickey’s arm again. “Son-of-a-bitch!” was Mickey’s immediate thoughts as he reeled back on the bed, frantically rubbing his arm as though it would make the pain go away. He continued to rub his arm as he returned to a sitting position and threw Chris Metal a stern look, knowing that he wouldn’t stand to be hit with the whip again. He then took a second to consider his hardcore debut. Spanish Bullwhip? Must mean there is a whip that can used was Mickey’s only explanation to the match title. But he thought there must be more to do it so he decided to ask his wonderful coach. Mickey looked up at Chris, “Spanis’? Bullwhip?” The Metallic One nodded with a grin. “Roughly speaking, there is a whip, and you can use it…” Mickey had been right and worked that much out as Chris gave his encyclopaedic interpretation of the match up that The Farmer was going to compete in. Again, Chris stepped forward and cracked Mickey in the arm with the belt. For the third time, Mickey reeled back in pain but this time he stood up and bee-lined for Chris Metal shoulder tackling him back into the door and through the door into the hotel corridor. He pinned the former PCW Rising Star contender to the ground and hit him in the face with a couple of right hands, bloodying his nose. And then Chris realised something. He smelt something. It was coming from Mickey. It was alcohol. Mickey was drunk… Another right hand landed and Chris was out cold, the remains of the door lying around his motionless body. Mickey stood above his coach breathing deadly, suddenly he deciding he wanted another drink. This was going to be an interesting wrestler-coach relationship and it was going the last time Chris would piss off a drunken Irish person. (If he has any sense it will be.)
