Pallas Athena 5: Irezumi

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/05 'That looks fabulous on you. You look so pretty.'Jin Osaka had become a friend in AWC, something Megumi was sorely missing most of her life. But even then, Jin was distant, as were most people toward Megumi. It was the way things had to be. The distance was created by Hamada. She didn't want to get close to people. Her line of work would not allow it. But Jin was a female and she was Japanese. She also was not a wrestler, which Hamada found difficult to friends with because of the fierce competitive nature of the sport. Jin was a nurse on staff for AWC. She was certainly a little different then most, but she was kind and she was friendly and the two hit it off. 'I look like a Christmas prostitute,' said Megumi looking in the mirror at her costume. She had on a red and white, tight fitting Santa suit, but Chris Cringle wouldn't be caught dead in something like this. The sleeveless top was skin tight, made of red fabric with furry white trimming around the edges. The red skirt with cottony trim was very short, and her red fishnet stockings and black boots were more appropriate for a bachelor party then a Christmas party. But Jin had picked it out with her and said that it would be a smash hit at the party. 'Here, put the hat on. It'll bring it all together.' It certainly did not, thought Megumi. Wouldn't some of the men get a kick out of this. The kick would be coming from her foot and straight to their heads. There was no way she could wear this in public, especially amongst her peers. 'Stop fidgeting. You look nice. It's what Harber would want you to wear. Wrestlers are role models and sex symbols. Now you wouldn't catch me dead in that thing...' 'Hey!' '...but it is going to look great when they take pictures for the Christmas magazine.' 'Pictures!?' 'Stop being so timid. You act like you're so innocent.' Jin went back to trying out different clothes she had laid out on the hotel bed. Megumi looked at her through the reflection in the mirror, trying to figure out if what she said was a backhanded insult or just that. Innocent. She turned back to her own reflection and began to finger the large irezumi that went from her shoulder all the way down to her wrist. Jin noticed her self-inflection and jumped in to apologize. 'I'm sorry. I didn't mean anything by it. Your tattoo, I like it allot. I have lived in America for a long time. This stuff doesn't bother me. I'm not stuffy like some old Japanese grandmother.' 'It's OK. Our people have always looked down on irezumi. The stigma is that anyone with a tattoo is yakuza, or in the mob. There is a heavy burden placed on people with them. I always wore long sleeves in Japan. In the ring, on the street, everywhere I went I hid my tattoo.' Megumi sighed but then smiled and looked at Jin. 'That is one reason why we came here, myself and Tozen. To be free from judgment.' 'I'm sorry.' 'For what?' Megumi turned to face Jin. The young nurse sat down on the bed, looking up at her new friend. 'For everything. For what happened to Tozen. And for how our people treat others they do not understand.' 'That's not your fault. Don't apologize for an entire culture, Jin. I am Japanese too and have the very same ideals.' 'So do I. But I am still sorry. You came here to America and joined the AWC and now you have a new set of ideals to fight.' Megumi laughed and turned back to the mirror. 'If you mean Jack Murphy, and Chainz, please. There is a Jack Murphy around every corner. It wouldn't matter where I decided to go, there would have been a Jack Murphy there to try and take away my dignity and honor. All I can do is show them that they are wrong. I think I did that last week when I beat him. And I will do it again this week. I will win this match by pinning one of our opponents and I will dedicate the win to Tozen. I'm going to prove to every one of these close-minded pigs, be it Murphy or Chainz, or those crazy Martin brothers, that Butterfly Hamada is for real.' Jin smiled and stood up, walking over to Megumi to fix her skirt. 'There.' Megumi smiled at her through the mirror. 'It's awful.' 'Yep. Just what the boss wants.' Jin's eyes washed over Megumi's tattoo. 'May I look at it?' 'Of course.' 'It's so BIG! Ha ha! And beautiful. So intricate.' 'My mother took me to a tattoo artist of very famous reputation when I was young. For every move she taught me, every lesson that I mastered, she would take me to the artist and he would ink one more scene. It took five years to complete.' 'It's so complex. Everything is intertwined together. One thing leads to another, and together they make another pattern. There is the samurai and a woman, and there is a dragon. A road, a temple, a mountain, and then they all make a picture of a butterfly. Wow.' Megumi smiled but she was more interested in not looking like a sexy elf rather then her tattoo. She had stared and stared at it for years, tracing the outlines of one picture into another. She had grown tired over the years of trying to understand the art and the skill involved in making her irezumi. She was honored by it however, because of her mother's gift to her. She didn't care if her own culture looked down upon those with tattoos. To her it was not a symbol of the underground, of criminals or thieves. It was a symbol of her mother's love and devotion. 'Last week, what happened?' Jin looked up at Megumi, who was caught in her thoughts. 'What?' 'Last week, inside the ring when you were wrestling against Murphy. The lights went out and there was a blue glowing butterfly in the ring, just like.... this one.' Jin pointed to the tattoo of the butterfly on Megumi's shoulder. 'What happened out there?' Megumi's fingers slightly traced the outline of the blue monarch butterfly on her arm quizzically. 'I don't know.'
