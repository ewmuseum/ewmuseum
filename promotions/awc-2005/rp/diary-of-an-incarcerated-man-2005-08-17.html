Diary Of an incarcerated man

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 august ----- Day 1 – 2nd August 2005 ------ Many a policemen caught me today outside of the hospital where the near decapitated Kris Krimzon lay. From herein my life was turned upside down, I was forced to share a cell with a man named Barry or ‘Barbara’ to his preference. Obviously it wasn’t just British prisons that turned people gay, but he had also been arrested in Malaga, but at the AWC event, And he was American some kind of Kris Krimzon fanatic, sadly he was the one driving the car that Krimzon landed on, so his dreams where crushed and so was his car. But he had perseverance and focused on a new superstar, a better superstar, and that was I. He kind of tried to form a ‘sexual bond’ between us but sadly I blew him off, but not in that sort of way. ------ Day 3 – 4th August 2005 ------ Today I was finally away from Barbara, who had given me enough sexual harassment to last me a lifetime. I had my own private cell, although it felt lonely, it was better than ‘Naked Raymond’ who always wanted you to pass him the soap in the showers. But up to now all is good, my bullet is a little red and sore but is feeling better. Hopefully Pearl will pay me bail and get me out of here soon, as I haven’t eaten since I got here. ------ Day 6 – 8th August 2005 ------ I finally ate for the first time in 6 days today, well maybe 5 if you include the meal I had before the AWC show. I tried the Spanish omelet today, sadly the herbs weren’t herbs they were cockroaches. But I suppose that’s probably the crunchiest and best tasting food you will get out here. But I dare not try and eat the Tapas, I would rather order out from one of the many chains of Kris Krimzon restaurants. Maybe when I get back to wrestling, or let me rephrase that, ‘If’ I get back to wrestling the fans may be seeing a skinnier, and leaner stick like Jonny Sake. ------ Day 7 – 9th August 2005 ------ I was allowed to watch AWC today, although I had to sit in a room of gay soon to be illegal immigrants, it was worth it to see one of the reasons that David ‘Pearl’ Harber would have to pay me bail. Yes, yes, the thing that I saw was a bunch of Irish twats trying to find there way around a stadium that they had probably visited about ten thousand times with the ring lay right at the heart of building, which cant be too hard to find, that was one reason, but ‘Actual’ reason, even though that was a valid reason, is The Educators announcement to have a match at BoB, with the LiveWire title on the line. And I should expect pearl to fly out anytime soon. ------ Day 8 – 10th August 2005 ------ As I suspected, I saw Pearl today, he was in a very… Risk Taking mood, and he thought that this prison, like Spain and Ireland is a load of shit, and hasn’t got Cable. Although that may be true for the Irish, we do in actual fact have a television with cable that receives AWC, and it was in English. But Pearl had once again underestimated Jonny Sake, and tried to put him through an amount of ‘Scary’ sayings, asking why he should be bailed. But I wish I had a camera when I saw the sourness of his face once he found out that I had seen the AWC taping and seen the Announcement. But sadly I still await his answer to the proposal of my bail. ------ Day 14 – 16th August 2005 ------ I finally got paid bail today, sadly for HATE who just got himself incarcerated, but heh who cares I’m free. I’m away from all these fags and rapists that all feed off of my every muscular limb. Thank the lord that pearl paid me bail, and thanks to the educator who brought up the Live Wire champion ship for pearl to pay me bail. But that will be at his downfall, as he will not be the champion come this Friday, he will be my bitch. He will get thrown and beaten around the ring, until he is a bloody mess that lies in a heap, and as for Krimzon I doubt he will even be at the match. and even if he is, he will be back In that hospital bed as soon as he steps foot in the arena. But that’s a story for another day….. ------ End ------
