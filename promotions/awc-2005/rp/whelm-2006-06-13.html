And I won’t just wait. ‘The Fall of Ambrose Bierce’ by Kyle Howard / The Stiletto Formal With my browser closed, all that exists is a blank page and a blinking cursor. I could fill this slate up for pages and hours; but without anything relevant to say, this story would still be nothing more than a white slate. Only now it’s a little dirty. A narrative like this wasn’t supposed to continue to a point like this. Wasn’t. But there are a lot of things that you can never expect, never plan for, never anticipate and the next thing you know, real life is fucking you in the ass. This isn’t real life, but it sure feels like it sometime. It’s a portion of who I am and whenever someone is dealing with a sense of self, they should take pride in that. Who the fuck am I to place myself on this pedestal, emotionally void as a “benefactor” of a glory that doesn’t really exist? I’ve pondered this for months. About ten months, to be exact, but that’s beside the point. The fact of the matter is that’s all I’ve done. I sat in the corner with a bitter respite towards anyone and everything for the simple sake that I wouldn’t be dealt with. As a scribe, my creation became less grandiose and ridiculous in nature; traded for malicious and meticulous thinking, abrasive and obnoxious speaking. It was a defense mechanism for what I had to do. Because who am I but someone’s employee? Here I am bending to the artistic will of a teen, about three years younger than I, because he’s the moderator of our competitive juxtaposition. It was rather clear that my antics would require planning and cooperation, and the ability to craft a wonderful story like I’ve been known to do from time to time. With odds stacked against me, what could I do but force my way to the top? So I hooked a program with the top-dog. The rest is history. I believe the words from our moderator were “I don’t want him up there so quickly.” Don’t quote me on that, but there was a bit of hesitance from what was an alliance between a man too trusting and another too flaky. There would be many of these alliances to come, many that I’d just observe from my corner and off-handedly say something striking like “Give it a week,” or the even wittier, “Give it a week until the orange gets to him.” And I was forced to build. Too many close calls came and went and to be honest, perhaps my creation wasn’t creative enough to achieve the top-bill. It was perfect that it eluded him for the time that it had, because it made the fact that he’d attained it that much more sweet. Once on top, there was nothing more to deal with now that his predecessor had pushed the hobby to the backseat. A few careless defenses and poor planning later, I’m back at that peg, one notch below. So, being that my competitive streak hadn’t been the strongest lately, and the fact that there were a few busy moments in-between real life once again, I decided to take an innovative approach to creative build. I sacrifice the dignity of my character and my reputation as a competitive participant; merely for the sake of looking mediocre. And when opportunity arrived, once again, I snatched away. Once again, I forced myself in a position where there was nothing I could do... but take it. Wow, and just in time for a wonderful summer break. But I’m digressing from the point. The one common factor in all of these events is because these contests are just that. And, by nature, they must be decided. Just like this piece right here. Throughout my first successful tenure in competitive roleplaying, and most successful attempt at this hobby in general, I’ve come to groom my own ideals and approach in terms of style and theme. It’s because every creation has a specific purpose, and every creator that drives more than likely has an expressive and personal story to share. So what was my creative drive? What was the reason of existence for something as bleak as an illustrious face-eater? NOTHING. That’s it! Not a damn thing. As a “benefactor,” I help my creation benefit by getting stoned and thinking too much about stupid shit until it snowballs, forming the stupid shit you’ve read before and you’re reading now. But that isn’t good enough, apparently. Before I thought it was about telling a story; compelling and introspective were all bonuses. I am more than capable of crafting a story that’s more than substantial, with those compelling and introspective bits gratefully accepted as icing on the cake. It was just one more analysis of the same thing I’m talking about now, dressed with comedic and subliminal undertones of what I’d really do given the chance. For that’s what I was doing; vicariously living through a paper creation. But nowadays, just ‘living’ isn’t enough. Now it’s about babies and drugs and murder and struggle. Oh, and racism. I’ve dipped my hand in the pot in a few of these topics but by no means am I going to be grounded by a simple theme. Sure, the topic of narcotics has been a backbone in my creation and how it progresses on paper and in story. But by all means, I figured that because most of my stories could be absolved of these elements and still hold a lot of weight, I was more than in the clear. I was wrong. Am I not introspective enough? Is my work not reaching out with a touching message like these people seem to convey so well with their ‘themes’? Do I need to write about Islamic fundamentalism and it’s collision with Hebrew placement? I’ve got personal issues I could touch up on; I’ve wanted to hide them from you if only to save yourselves, but if it’s personal struggle and irony that you want... Apparently, I need a reason for writing—no, no, a reason for living. Because a teenager half the world away, apparently, thinks babies are the tits and giggles these days. Is that what it’s all about? Is that how I’m supposed to get people gripped by my words? Or is it that certain sense of diction that I could never be able to duplicate, no matter how hard I try? Is it just the way that the words come together for the others; they just don’t assemble right when they’re pressed by my fingers? It’s a certain sense of personal frustration that resonates with the approach to every day life. Certain elements outside of the hobby tend to take shape, and of course to an over-active mind one would think God was shitting on them. I’m not the type, but it’s been a hell of a few weeks for me to notice them like I have. To relegate it all to electronic wrestling is fucking silly and stupid. The best I can do... is relate. That’s all I’ve ever done in this game; relate my reactions onto a character that I once created to be nothing like myself. Oh how subliminal the pen works from the hand. But now, just projecting your sense of self and attempting to tell a wonderful story is not enough. Now, I’ve got to tell stories about fucking babies, and personal crisis. Well since it’s been hand fed to me, let’s make this theme a baby cluster fuck. Maybe it’ll be enough to make a teenager, half the world away, puke in over-abundance of baby. It may not be as ironic as a racist adopting a black child, after committing murder no less, but hell... All we can do is try. and convince. The collision and pace of his steps mockingly imitated the substance of water, how he flowed back and forth with the purpose of a pendulum so fluidly. Senses of empowerment and exposition made their stake to overwhelm him, but his psyche was too strong and batted away at these notions like a reaction to an annoying fly. For now all he can do is reflect and prepare for the confrontation that would soon arrive. But what is worth the reflection? A tainted crown, dusty and unpolished sitting in the corner? The ability to break people into pieces? A near-even record? A girlfriend who’s loyal? A child? What subtle reasons presented themselves in the following session of circular thinking, constantly stumbling into logic traps that could not be explained, even with the slightest degree of level-headedness. They could not be combated as easily as a pesky insect. Instead they would lurk and breed and spread and now you’re covered in emotions that you can’t really understand. Before the call from Ally, Ellis’ sister, came to inform him of his newfound obligations; Adam was drifting face-up in his own sea filled with doubts and regrets. Careless to any other action, he merely felt the need to coast and think and if all else concentrate on the act of breathing. Inhale. Exhale. Input. Output. What you give, is what you take. And truly, what has Adam given lately? A lack of effort is all that could be placed as an answer, but regardless of his brevity towards the canvas the King still felt truly susceptible to being the best. No one should have forced over the imposing greatness he’d taken so long to craft. Yet then again, that’s what everyone should have seen in him. Flaws. Loopholes. Means to glory. Input. Payoff. Output. First it was defeat to Amy Campbell. Then The Furious Fists of God. The Furious Fists of God, three times over. The first two had both been flukes, while the first allowed Adam to take his time and craft another path to the top with the least amount of effort possible. Particulars didn’t happen to favor the victors in this outcome, as Adam could rely on his former ally in Mike Wade. The second time, however, was just Adam underestimating his abilities. The third, the charm, that should have belonged to him. Instead he forced an entire legion of his followers to stake their claim on the canvas and each and everyone fell to God’s Furious Fists. And yes the fury was felt. So here is the bane of my existence, this creation who’s done more to hinder than prosper has now forced itself into a position where there is nothing but corners and lions, and with a dead-end and certain doom binding ones’ fate there is little one can do but lose hope. It didn’t help that persons-of-power have chosen to take their shots at any chance they could fine. Now there’s a forced wedge in the alliance between Adam and his love with his prized glory on the line. The he risked everything to get is now an expense he thought affordable to lose. What with the arrival of Ellis into his scene, it was like David Harber was jumping at the chance to stack the odds against him. How was he supposed to react? With prowess and poise? His abilities had just been undermined for the second time in a row, a man who’s supposed to be the champion. Losing to the same people he lost the Alliance Championships all those months before. Of course, there is a sense of irony. Them, and Ellis, and Adam. It shall be a party to say the least, a party that Adam already had hesitations in attending. And then, she calls. The words from Ally shouldn’t have been conversed over the medium they had, but telephones are so convenient these days in a world where convenience is no longer a commodity, but a necessity. She shouldn’t even had been the one to tell him, but once again Ellis’ indifference had forced the people around her into a hard place. Ally found a certain responsibility in letting Adam know. She felt that he deserved the knowledge because it’s of his doing as well. Why should Ellis be the one to be the deciding factor? It’s only her body. What belongs to Adam is the seed. This plant would not have grown without him. So many thoughts traveled through him at a velocity that cannot be recorded in a physical sense. Like regaining memories, every notion of good and bad, every evil deed and redeeming scheme began to flood him without relent as if the angel and demon on his shoulder were also tugging at the strings that kept him steady. For shame, Adam, as the walls dance around you standing still. Not only is your career, what you used to consider your entire life, in jeopardy, but now you must contend with the woman who’s also the catalyst for your downward spiral in both personal and professional aspects. Defend on the canvas and protect in the home. How you’ll have to beat her silly just to keep what you hold dear, and put up with the fact that she’s carrying something that’s yours inside of her. There are many different defects regarding this situation. The losing situation, the defense situation, the pregnant situation, and the situation that Adam doesn’t know how to situate with Ellis. Is there even a doorway towards attaining any sense of normalcy between the two of them? Are grandiosity and carelessness substantially unable to mesh with the method that the two thought to work? There were so many QUESTIONS that remain! Surprisingly, a question that didn’t happen to present itself was the simplest one to debate of them all. “Is She Going To Keep It?” and more importantly “Do I Have A Choice?” Questions. Not the same ones, but questions that still present their need to plague Adam. An invisible need to speak with Ellis would not leave him alone but the anxiety and restlessness of accomplishment would not shy from resonating within him. The only cure to a cancer as detrimental as nerves was the weed that Adam found himself turning to time after time. It’s as he soaks and bathes himself inside his car, shut off and closed off inside his garage, in an effort to contain the smoke from every exhaled hit. Increasing the intensity of his stoney-high, Adam merely wants to smoke and bake and fry his brain a little more than he already has. No thinking, no dwelling, and certainly no questions should present themselves at this time. This is alone time. This is lazy time. A time that’s become more and more prevalent in the world of King Dick. Why should he re/act to anything? ‘Putting up with’ and ‘Dealing with’ are too harsh of ways to describe coping with the world, as even that implied a hint of effort is required to get by. Couldn’t one only do that? Get by? Coast through life without the slightest obligation or routine to be chained to? One doesn’t find the answer in weekly wrestling shows and embittered rivalries on a Pay-Per-View-ly basis. Again, thoughts present themselves and force Adam to betray his intentions of baking in the car, in the garage. Finding little use of himself but to pass out under the influence, a few moments pass but not few moments too soon. The mechanical door operates open by the will of someone else, and immediately the hum of an approaching vehicle and in-turn it’s headlights become relevant. Ellis Nash is behind the wheel of her own, and the sight is confusing as it should be. To her, the smoke bellows out, Adam is in the car with the lights on and he’s slouched back in his seat, and the pretty picture is painted for a pathetic and poorly executed suicide attempt. Betraying logic for care, Ellis leaves her vehicle on and parked in the driveway to check on Adam, racing against the pressure as if time were pressing a knife to her throat. She swings the door open and throws herself inside, waving the smoke away in the faintest attempt to keep her own creation somewhat safe. Then Ellis takes a slight smell of the pungent aroma that she usually would recognize like a hound. If only she wasn’t so protective and paranoid, she might have made it a good session. Considering the circumstances this scenario was probably for the best. Adam’s face read blank while Ellis mind can’t help but shout “Oh shit, I look kind of dumb.” A playful slap on the shoulder is followed by her quickly covering her face and rushing out the hazy garage. She takes deep gasps as her body retains a somewhat normal heart-rate. Adam slowly follows out, steps staggered and mind scattered from the situation in general. It’s almost as if he was awoken with a fist to the jaw; very unpleasant. The whole ordeal is forcing an unfortunate finish to a tragic process that was inevitable from the outset. She stares blankly, much like he does, only now his eyes are a bit heavier since the surprise has worn off. “I thought you were killing yourself.” She says half-heartedly. Her tone betrays her intent. “You gave up on me that easy?” He says half-heartedly. His tone takes a similar stance. She begins to say something, but Adam cuts her off with as much insistence as he has tenacity. “I know!” She looks confused, because she isn’t sure what he’s referring to. She knows, truthfully, but she wants to retain those few moments of holding a well kept secret. “I know about the baby, El. Pretty sure you can guess who told me, but you can’t blame her. We can’t really blame anyone.” Ellis thinks, remind me to kill Ally. “I could sit here and think of ways to be angry and scared. That’s probably why you didn’t want to tell me.” “I didn’t want to tell you becau-” She attempts. “I don’t care why you didn’t tell me, it doesn’t matter. Nothing really matters, El. The crown, the matches, AWC; none of that matters right now to me. The one thing my mind keeps coming back to is the fact that I’m going to be a father and there’s no real way out of it. That in nine months there’s going to be a shit-spawn of my own that the world, and we, are going to have to fucking deal with.” “Adam we ha-” He holds a finger to her lips. “Please let me finish. I just need you to hear me, please?” She nods empathetically, if that is possible. “I am not ready to be a father. Not ready at all. I’m still a fucking child myself and there’s no way I will be able to compensate for another human life that isn’t capable on their own. And the fact that I’m a disgusting person as it is does not encourage me to be thrilled with this fact. “I’m twenty-something years old with no sense of responsibility or respect of hard-work. My morals are nonexistent and I treat people like absolute shit. I am not looking forward to having any influence on any child, enough to the point of having to deal with them as my own. I have enough trouble dealing with children that belong to other people. Realistically, it should be a crime for a person like me to reproduce.” He hangs his head, and takes a deep breath. Mustering a fuel that’s intangible in form, he starts to unravel his mixed message. “But that doesn’t mean I’m going to puss out on some stupid shit like immaturity. There is never something I’ve not been able to do, El, besides pass a drug test but that doesn’t matter. I’m going to be a father and I’m going to try my hardest.” She’s heard enough. “Adam you need to listen to me.” “GO TO FUCKING BED!” someone screams from upstairs. It goes ignored. “You’ve kept me out of the loop for two fucking weeks and now you want me to listen?” “Okay, we are NOT doing the Darcy Campbell thing-” “Sorry.” “Be quiet for thirty seconds and listen. This is just as important as anything you think you’ve decided.” Her brevity and curtness startle Adam, but he stays standing. “I think I’m getting rid of it.” And the sore-thumb begins to poke Adam in the eye ferociously, mercilessly*. Punishment, because if he isn’t using his eyes then he doesn’t deserve to use them. *metaphorically speaking of course. end
