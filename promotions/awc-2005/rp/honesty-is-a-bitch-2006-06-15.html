Honesty is a bitch

15 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 15 OOC note: I posted this early because I'm heading off for Michagin tomorrow. Night was slowly falling, at last. The day had seemed so long, as though the night was hiding from something. Eh, not that it matters, it was still coming. As slowly as it did, night was coming. A strange, but intriguing noise echoed through a hallway of one of those houses where you don’t really want to be in. You know, the ones where dirt, rust, and mold grows everywhere, and it totally sucks ass. Of course, the owner didn’t give a shit. It is his house, he can do whatever the hell he wants. The owner’s wife, however, was much less than pleased with it. In the room were two men, two fully grown adult men. Both of them were adamantly staring and screaming at the television sceen, playing video games. Every night, this two would sit in the room and play Halo 2 non-stop until 5am in the morning, and then sleep all day. By now, it was routine. Together, they’ve gone through two Xbox controllers and one television. Isn’t it obvious? Gaming is corrupting the minds of this world’s adults. Fuck the kids. But then again… how could one possibly consider Kris Krimzon a true adult? An indescribable scream came out of Kris’s mouth, as his friend, AJ Valor, killed him on the video game. The game ended and Kris chucked his controller to the floor and laid back. AJ, pleased with himself, almost felt bad for his friend. Suddenly, his expression changed to excitement. Reaching into his pocket, he pulled out a brown paper bag. “Kris, you know what would make you feel better?” AJ asked. “Booze? Lots and lots of booze?” “Yes, but not what I was looking for. How about taking a shit in a paper bag?” Kris shot up instantly. “Are you fucking stupid? Why take a shit in a paper bag if we have a perfectly fine toilet?” “Um, so we can put it on someone’s porch, light it, and run?” “Why would we do that?” “Because we’ve played this game for over 100 hours in the last few weeks.” “Oh, okay. Gimme the bag.” Kris snatched the bag and headed for the back of the room. “NO YOU SICK FUCK!” AJ shouted. “Go to the bathroom!” “Are you fucking stupid? Why would I go to the bathroom if I have a perfectly fine corner?” “Because I don’t want to see my friend take a dump into a paper bag. Now get out of here!” “Eh, FINE!” Kris shouted, storming out of the room. Sounds of both pain and pleasure came out of the bathroom within the next few minutes. In other words, he was ripping total ass. The door opened, and Kris walked out, paper bag in hand. “DUDE LOOK AT THIS THING MAN! IT’S A FREAKING LOG!” AJ stared at him in disbelief, and shook his head. “Let’s just leave.” Minutes later… Kris and AJ have arrived at their destination. They don’t know where the hell they are, but they got there. “Dude, you’ve been pushing me to look at the turd the whole walk here. I’M NOT LOOKING AT IT!” AJ shouted. “Its just a turd, pussy…” Kris mumbled. “Okay, who lights it and who knocks?” AJ asked, ignoring Kris’s comment. “Eh, I’ll knock.” “Okay I’ll light it. Hurry up though when you knock.” “Can you get in trouble for this?” Kris asks, worried. “Nah, they did it on Billy Madison.” “Oh, okay.” AJ lit the bag on fire, as Kris walked up to a brick house and set the flaming bag of poo on their concrete porch. He knocked on the door, and took off running. The door opened, and a man came out, staring at the flame. He ran back inside, and that’s all Kris and AJ saw before they took off running to their house. An hour or so later… Kris was just heading off to bed. It was early for him, only about eleven at night or so. There was a knock on the door, so of course he went and answered it. Outside stood a policemen . Shit… “Hi,” the cop said nicely, “Is this Kris Krimzon?” Say no!! Say no!! Say NO!!! “Uh, yeah.” Dumbass. “Um, how old are you?” asked the cop. “Err, why?” “Because I don’t know any adult immature enough to do what you did,” the cop responded fiercely. Ouch… “Erm, I’m 27 sir,” Kris replied shakily. “Wow. Well, you know why I’m here I’m sure. Mind if I step in?” the cop asked. “Sure, come on in.” The cop stepped in, and Kris shut the door. ”Well,” the cop began, “I know what happened, I just need you to tell me.” “Uh, we took a bag and lit it on fire?” “That’s kind of vague.” “We put it on someone’s porch and ran?” If you already know, how is this going to help? “Tell it to me like you were telling a friend,” he said. “But... you’re not my friend.” Oh crap. I didn’t mean to say that…” “Just tell me EXACTLY what happened,” the cop said, growing increasingly annoyed. “Okay… my friend gave me a paper bag and said to shit in it. Then we walked to someone’s house, put it on their porch and ran.” What more do you want from me, damn it. “You know, I’m a guy that likes honestly…” the cop began. Shit, here’s a speech. “Honestly will get you pretty far with me, Kris.” I’m so fucking hungry, I haven’t eaten in a while. “People try to lie to me to get out of trouble, but end up in worse trouble.” I wanna play Halo. “So all you gotta do with me…” I think my shoe is untied. “Is tell me the truth, and you…” My back hurts! “Won’t be in any trouble.” This guy is still talking? “Okay?” “Yes sir,” Kris responded. “So in other words, honesty is your bitch?” DAMN IT! The cop simply stared. “Okay, so what is your friends name?” “AJ.” “AJ what?” “Valor.” “Okay. Who lit it?” “AJ.” “Who knocked?” “Me.” Who cares? No one. “Kris,” the cop said in a lighter tone, “Do you know what could have come from this?” “Um…” Kris paused for a second. “The guy could’ve got AIDS?” The cops’ eyes widened. “I mean, the house could have caught fire?” Despite the concrete porch and brick house. ”Yes, what else?” the cops asked. What is this a fucking pop-quiz? “Er.. He could have hurt his foot stomping in the flame? Uh.. the grass could have caught fire.. uh.. the air could have been polluting, causing global warming?” Kris said, unsure of all of his answers. “Kris,” the cop said, “I don’t think you’re taking this situation seriously. Do you even know the family?” “Uh, no.” “Well he had two kids in there. If the house caught fire, you could of killed them both.” Oh you’re breaking my heart. I can see it now: Concrete porch catches on fire causing brick house to catch on fire due to semi-popular wrestler.. Kris simply stared at the ground. “You scared the guy, and he ran in the house, got water and put it out.” Aw, he didn’t even step on it? A perfect turd, wasted.. Wait, I scared him!? HAHAHA! Kris started laughing out loud. “Are you serious? What a pussy!!” “Kris, seeing as you’re completely brain-dead anyways I’m not going to report this. I’m not going to call it in, you won’t be in the newspaper…” I’M NOT EVEN IN THE FUCKING NEWSPAPER?! “But just to let you know, fire is NOT something to joke about.” Blah blah blah another fucking speech. “I used to do this when I was a… kid… but I realized that its time to start thinking things through, and once you do you’ll get farther in life.” Do you ever stop talking? “Fire can hurt people, Kris.” Has anyone ever slapped you for talking too much? “It can kill people, destroy property.” Cause I’m really close to doing that, ya know. Seriously. “Just, be careful…” WRAP IT UP! Jesus Christ... “…okay?” “I will,” Kris said as he opened the door. The cop stepped out and Kris shut the door. “Asshole.” Seconds later, the phone rang. Nice timing, AJ. He answered it, and sure enough on the other end was the voice of his friend. “Did you get a knock on your door tonight?” “Yep, sure did.” “What happened?” “I punched that asshole in the face and told him never to come to my house again. Honesty is my bitch.' 'What the fuck?' 'Shut up. I'm just trying to end the damn roleplay.' 'GAH! YOU BROKE KAYFABE!'
