A Tale of Two Doctors

12 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th August Reclining in a grey leather armchair in the cool, air-conditioned environment of physician Dr. John Keaton's waiting room, we found Andy M. flicking through the pages of British 'omg celebs!!!!11' magazine, Heat. Andy's facial expression changed from astonishment to confusion to resentment and back again as he passed through the magazine aimed at females with a single-figure IQ. Wearing a pair of aviator-style shades, a black sleeveless t-shirt reading 'The Devil is a Loser and he's my Bitch,' some loose, light-beige trousers with a studded leather belt, and a pair of black and white Sketchers, Andy was not alone in the poorly-lit room. Directly opposite Andy, over one of those cool glass coffee tables, sat a young-looking tarty female, her arm in a sling, her eyes incessantly eyeing up the copy of Heat. One of those girls who spends an hour painting her face on in the morning, the blond was dressed in a low-cut pink top and a short denim skirt; more appropriate for lingering on street corners in the early hours of the day than a doctor's appointment. 'God damn, Free Willy got buff...' Andy proclaimed, pausing on a picture of British Pop Idol winner and Z-list celebrity elephant Michelle McManus. As he passed through the pages of the Holy Bible for the unwashed and uneducated, the woman of disputable reputation began to look at Andy in an even more negative manner... how DARE this man treat such a 'quality' publication with such indifference? Finally, she plucked up the coverage to try and retrieve her Holy Grail from the clutches of the Scottish King of Cool (no need for speech marks, bitch). 'Hey, you...' she uttered in a voice as rough and gravely as her 'lady of the night' exterior would suggest. Andy lowered the magazine, and glanced at the woman over the top of his shades, 'Yes? What can I do for you?' he asked in his typical warm, accent-tinged tone of voice. 'Are you finished with Heat yet? I've been dying to read about Brian McFadden's in-growing toenail all day!' Andy suddenly pulled the magazine close to his chest, as if he was protecting it. Quite why he was doing this is anyone's guess, given that this particular magazine should be considered nothing more than fire-lighting material. 'No, you may have it when I'm finished... I'm enthralled by its overwhelming lack of logic, intelligence, or reason...' and with that, Andy pulled the magazine back up, 'reading' on. The 'woman' huffed; she couldn't believe that things hadn't gone her way. 'Uh! Whateva!' she whined in a brattish manner, 'What are you, gay? That great magazine isn't for guys! Besides, you're not even reading it...' Andy raised an eyebrow, and again glanced over his shades at the female. 'Listen hoe, shut up, you may have it when I'm finished examining its contents, comprendé? And for the record, the Scottish King of Cool is definitely NOT gay! Not that there's anything wrong with it, or anything... I've got tonnes of homosexual brotha's... but not for me, thanks.' At this, she screwed up her face; clearly Andy's superior grammar was too much for her brain to handle. In the background, a telephone rang at the reception desk, only to be picked up by the otherwise idle pretty, bespeckled brunette receptionist. 'Hello...' she said informally in a sweet tone of voice. For a few moments she paused to listen to the voice on the other end of the line, before responding 'Okay, I'll send him through.' The receptionist then placed down the receiver, and quietly called towards Andy; 'Mr. M., Dr. Keaton will see you now...' However, Andy was too caught up in the ongoing battle for Heat! 'Do ya want me to get my boyfriend to kick your ass?' enquired the girl. 'Your boyfriend? Which one?! Stephen, Craig, Mike, Jim, Hank, Ian, Tom or Lee?' Andy spat back with venom. The woman scowled and extended the palm of her hand across the table, pretty much right in Andy's face. 'Whateva, talk to the hand 'cos the face aint listenin'!' she barked. 'What's wrong? OH SHIT! Yeah, that's right; I forget Keith, Chad, Rob, Patrick and David! Damn! Sorry about that...' After another failed attempt to attract SKC's attention, the receptionist sighed, and got up from her desk. Within a few seconds she had walked across to where Andy was seated, looking not very impressed. 'Mr. M.!' she commanded, Andy looked up, 'For the THIRD time! Dr. Keaton will see you now!' she didn't even wait for a reply, and turned back, knowing Andy had to have heard her this time. Andy scoffed at this, knowing he would know have to surrender the trashy magazine to the trashy person sitting across from him. 'Alright, slimeball, looks like you won this round...' he painfully submitted, sliding the magazine onto the table, where it was immediately picked up and embraced like a long-lost relation by the girl. 'But you do realise how badly I could beat you, yes? You'd better watch out next time you're out in public... if I don't get you, one of my 'crew' might...' The girl, of course, wasn't paying the slightest bit of attention to Andy, and was now tucking into her daily dose of uninteresting celebrity bullshit. Feeling as if he had redeemed himself, Andy got up from his chair, and made the short journey towards Dr. Keaton's office door. Without knocking, Andy slipped into the room quietly, gently closing the door behind him. Decorated in a similarly dull manner to the waiting room, at least the Doctor's room was a little better lit up, beams of light creeping into the room through gaps in the blind. Keaton, a short, bald, spectacle-wearing portly fellow (with impressive facial hair, it has to be said) sat behind his desk, decked out in a white coat and black pants, not looking entirely unlike a mad scientist. Andy glanced around the room, little had changed since the last time he had visited a couple of months ago; the place was still full of family photos and exotic potted plants... and, oh yes, the hospital-style bed/table/thing that Andy had found himself laying on many a time over the past couple of years. Wrestling injuries, what a bitch. Apparently, Keaton hadn't noticed Andy's entrance, as he was quite taken aback when Andy announced his arrival with a typically slick smile and a 'what's cracking, motherclucker?' 'Oh, jeesh, Andy...' Keaton said, startled, 'you took me by surprise... please, take a seat.' With a nod, Andy made the short walk towards the desk, and sat down opposite Dr. Keaton. 'So, this is the first time we've seen each other in a while... are you keeping up with those exercises for your back that I showed you?' Keaton asked as Andy pulled out a little, smelling the pong of garlic in Keaton's breath. 'Of course!' chirped Andy, lying through his teeth. 'Excellent... oh, and by the way, I know this sounds completely ludicrous...' the doctor began, chuckling, 'but my son said he saw you wrestling for some Atlantic Wrestling Club or whatever, on television the other night? That can't be true... hehe...' 'Oh shit,' thought Andy, who clearly hadn't told his physician that he had been wrestling against his orders. Nervously, Andy responded with a 'Uhh, hehe, yeah, that's the most ridiculous thing I've ever heard. You've got a pretty creative kid there, Doc!' Dr. Keaton opened his mouth to speak again, but before the words could escape Andy's cell phone began to ring loudly in his pocket. Startled by the vibrations, Andy pulled his phone out and answered it, much to the dismay of his doctor. 'Hey, Andy here, who's this?' Andy asked. ''Eeeey Andy, eet's me, Alvaro,' replied the familiar Portuguese tones of Andy's butler Alvaro. 'You have to get back 'ere immediately, senhor! Eet ees Snowball, she ate an uncooked haggis and now I theenk she ees seeck...' 'Mmmm?!' Andy responded, 'Snowball... sick, you say?' ''Ey, yea. She wont eat a theeng, her eyes are all glazed, and her flames have just, ran out... she tried to torch Cayle, but all that came out was a leetle puff of smoke.' Andy's jaw dropped, he spoke with panic; 'OH MY GOD! MY LITTLE BUNDLE OF FLUFF... I'LL BE RIGHT THERE, AMIGO!' Almost immediately, Andy hung up the call, and threw the phone back into his pocket. He leapt from his chair, as a look of pure confusion drew itself across John Keaton's face. 'What the? Where are you going, Andy!?!' he shouted. 'Sorry Doc, it's URGENT!' yelped Andy in reply, as he dashed out of the door like a lightning bolt, which was probably for the best; Keaton was pretty close to finding out that Andy had been wrestling against his orders. -------------------- Within about 30 minutes, Andy was back at the super-cool environment that is Andyville. The Andymobile, a clapped-out, multi-coloured Volkswagen hippy van reading 'Andy RULES!' on the side, came to a halt in front of Andy's country estate, skidding on the gravel and kicking up an almighty cloud of dust in the process. After quickly shutting off the engine, Andy leapt out of his vehicle like a man possessed, and slammed the door shut, almost knocking it from the hinges. Slipping on the gravel, Andy dashed towards the front double-doors of his home, and threw them open. Andy now stood in the grandiose hall that was the gateway to the rest of the house. With its high roof, chandeliers and massive curved staircase, it truly was a sight to behold, and looked like somewhere a king would live. In agony, Andy wailed the name of his prized Bolivian fire-breather... 'SNOOOOOOOOOOWBAAAAAAAALL! WHERE ARE YOU!?!' his voice reverberating around the hall. Almost immediately, Alvaro dashed out from one of the homes many reception rooms; his moustache as immaculately well-groomed as ever. 'Andy! In here!' he called back, dashing back into the room, followed closely by his Scottish boss. Andy's heart almost collapsed when he saw Snowball laying motionless (apart from small long movements through breathing) on one of his huge, wine-red coloured sofas. Andy immediately rushed to his badger's aid, embracing her as if she were his bridge. 'Oh, my dearest pet! The haggis has betrayed you...' ''Ey, I donno how she got dee damn haggis... I came into dee kitchen, and there she was, chowing down on some of Robert Burns' finest...' Alvaro shrugged. Andy looked up at his butler, and out of the corner of his eye, caught glimpse of his brother Cayle. Cayle was sitting in front of the massive plasma television screen, playing Fifa 2005 on a Playstation 2 console, and was dressed in a pair of long, red basketball shorts, a white muscle shirt and a dark red bandanna. Disappointed in his brother, Andy yelled at Cayle; 'Cayle! What the hell are you doing!?! Snowball is DYING and you're playing stupid soccer games!' Cayle glanced over his shoulder and shrugged, 'What do you want me to do, Andy? Alvaro already called the badger doctor...' With this revelation, Andy glanced back at his butler with a questioning glance. 'Ah yes, he said he'd be here soon, he ees a very busy man... but, een the meanwhile, he told us to keep her warm and use dee Badger Survival Kit he provided us...' 'Then why the hell aren't you doing that!?!' Andy barked back. 'Eh, Cayle and I were playing de game on de Playstation...' In a fit of rage, Andy slammed his foot down on the floor, 'GOD DAMMIT! GET OFF YOUR ASSES! Alvaro, you get the survival kit... Cayle, you get some blankets and pillow... MOVE IT PEOPLE!' Pausing the game, Cayle got up to his feet, and followed Alvaro briskly out of the room. Andy shook his head, cooling down a little, before kneeling back down to his poorly badger's level. Snowball's eyes were half open and half closed as Andy gently stroked her head... 'Don't worry Snowball, you'll be just fine...' -------------------- A few moments later, and Snowball was still lying on the couch, her owner at her side. However, now she had a pillow under her had and a blanket covering most of her body. She was also rigged up to one of the most bizarre things ever created - the Badger Survival kit, and had a thermometer hanging out of her mouth. It was essentially a life support machine for badgers, complete with authentic pulse-meter. Cayle and Alvaro stood above Andy and Snowball, with Andy's younger sibling looking massively bored. Suddenly, Andy's custom doorbell - a pre-recorded message of his own voice yelling 'Someone's at the door, bitch!' - rang out. Alvaro immediately dashed out of the room, knowing his role. 'Oh Cayle, I sure hope that is the doctor... I don't know how much longer Snowball can hold out...' Andy uttered, almost shedding a tear. Cayle rolled his eyes - thankfully, outside of Andy's vision - and fabricated a response in his head. “Don’t worry Andy; I’m sure she’ll be fine…” Cayle responded as his brother continued to mollycoddle his beloved pet. A couple of seconds later, an Alvaro was back in the room, followed closely by Flordia’s only badger doctor, Michael Gomez. A Mexican immigrant, Gomez had an unsurprisingly dark complexion, and was rather tubby. His mid-length black hair was slicked back, looking nice and greasy, and he wore a button-up short-sleeved white shirt, a black tie, some black slacks and a pair of well-polished black shoes. “Andy, Dr. Gomez ees here…” Alvaro announced. Andy glanced over his shoulder, got up to his feet, and welcomed the good doctor with a firm handshake. “Thank goodness you’re here, Doctor” he proclaimed. “Hello Mr. M., what is it this time?” Gomez replied in a soft, soothing voice. “I don’t really know, I was out at the time, but Alvaro said he walked into the kitchen and found her eating some of my god damn haggis… and now look at her! She’s in a right old state…” Without response, Gomez brushed past Andy towards the sofa one which Snowball was laying, placed his bag on the carpeted floor, and knelt down. He pulled the thermometer from the badger’s mouth, noting an exceedingly high reading, and turned towards Andy. “What breed is Snowball again, Mr. M.?” he questioned. “Bolivian fire-breather.” Immediately it hit Gomez. “Ahhh, jeesh…” he began, “that’s it, a Bolivian fire-breathing badger should never be allowed to even look at haggis twice! The seasoning can lead to an infection in the badger’s flame-glands… I can tell you right now that that’s what’s wrong with Snowball.” “Oh? And how do you cure it, then?” Andy asked with renewed hope. “Simple…” Dr. Gomez turned towards his brown leather bag and zipped it open. After a couple of seconds of rummaging around, he pulled out a small, sealed test tube containing some repulsive-looking dark green liquid. “This will waken her right up, an instant cure. The taste of the stuff is so damn repulsive, but it’s surprisingly effective when dealing with infected flame-glands, you know…” Gomez removed the cork seal from the test tube, ready to feed it’s contents to Snowball. “Oh? What’s in it?” enquired Andy. “A concoction of golden syrup, brussel sprouts and Vanilla Coke…” Screwing up his face at the thought of the ingredients, Andy watched on as Gomez opened up Snowball’s small mouth and poured the entirety of the test tube down her throat. Perhaps wisely, Gomez took a step back, and looked towards Cayle. “Eh, it might be a good idea to open those doors over there,” he said, pointing towards a set of glass sliding doors leading to the poolside area. “Why’s that?” Cayle enquired, raising his eyebrow. “Just do it! You’ll see why…” Gomez commanded. With a shrug, Cayle walked across the room and pulled one of the doors open. Andy piped up; “Now what?” “Just wait…” replied Gomez. Suddenly, the badger’s little head popped up, her eye’s looking as if she’d seen a ghost. Snowball suddenly leapt up from the couch, and raced as fast as her little legs could take her outside. Andy, Cayle, Alvaro and Dr. Gomez watched on as Snowball dived head-first into the swimming pool. At the sight of this, Andy quickly dashed outside, followed closely by his brother, butler, and badger doctor, only to find Snowball waddling out of the pool via the badger ramp installed by Andy. “Did it work?” Andy asked the Doctor, as Snowball shook the water from her fur. “Most definitely… after tasting how VILE that stuff is, it’s a natural reaction for the badger in question to wash it down with a massive load of water. She should be fine now, Mr. M.…” A grin crept across Andy’s face, as Snowball stood staring at her own and his cohorts. She let off a small burst of fire in their direction from a safe distance. “WOOOOOOOOO!” yelled Andy. Bye bye.
