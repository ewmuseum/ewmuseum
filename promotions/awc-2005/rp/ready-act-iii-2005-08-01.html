-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- R/E/Assumptions/D/Y -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The sound of flesh colliding with flesh cracked through the air. While it did not hold as erotic a tone as we think back on, it was still something that could catch breaths from the throats of most people. Right after that sound, a young boy hit the ground, lying on his side with a yelp of pain leaving his chest. Feet were scuffling against the black asphalt; a scuffle was afoot. From a low vantage point showing only from the knee below, three pairs of legs clad in blue slacks and assorted sneakers surrounded one person, wearing black knee high boots. They seemed to have that person surrounded, and bit-by-bit they were closing in on their prey. The boot-clad being jumped into the air as they neared; when his feet hit the ground, all three surrounding figures staggered back. Quickly the boot-clad person rushed in for the nearest person, fists whipping through the air smacking into the boy’s body. One leg left the ground, kneeing up into the person. A groan left that boy as he fell down to the ground, coming into view as a youth with brownish red hair spiked short. Another boy tried to come around and grab the boot-clad boy from behind. That didn’t seem to work though, the heel of the boot going into the soft toe of the sneaker. The boy hopped back, shifting all his weight on one leg, and just as he did the boot-clad boy kicked his heel into the side of that boy’s knee and with another smack of fist to flesh, that boy twisted around and fell flat on his face. Now there was only one boy left with the boot-clad boy… the two standing across from each other. There was a pause for only a couple of seconds before the boy rushed at his opponent. That… proved to be useless, as the boot-clad boy stood there just until he got into range, and tripped him, coming down with him in a reverse armbar. Both of them were clear as day now in view, the boy with slicked brown hair now messed up from the skirmish. And the boot-clad boy… … He wasn’t a boy at all. Clad in a pair of tight blue jeans with black kneepads and black knee high boots along with a black leather jacket and gloves, the black haired girl with the short, tomboyish cut pulled back on the boy’s arm, her elbow embedded into the back of his to push outward on the joint. He gritted his teeth in pain as she pressed in harder, a stern look piercing into him. “Oi,” she muttered in a low voice. “Now.” “OK!” he yelled in pain. “I’m sorry!” Making sure he learned his lesson, she reeled back on his arm once more to make sure the pain would linger before releasing him. She stood to her feet and brushed herself off with an aggravated look. Behind her the last boy rolled onto his back holding his arm, eyes narrowed with pain etched on his face. He started to push himself backwards away from the girl, sitting up against the wall looking at her with a strange expression. Most men would be downright pissed by just having their asses handed to them by a girl… and the other three guys might just be that way. But this boy… there was a look of intrigue on his face, even a bit of respect. As she began to walk away, he called out to her. “Nagi!” She stopped, sighing as she glared back at him. He smiled weakly, pushing himself up with his good arm and legs against the wall. “I see… I can see now why they call you the First and Last. You’re the worst student in the entire school… but still… you’re also the best.” Nagi, as he called her, kept her eyes focused on him as he struggled to stand there in pain. Then without a change in her cold gaze, she responded: “You’re still making assumptions, Saotome… don’t you know anything?” Brushing a bit of hair back behind her ear, she turned and walked off briskly. “The best is yet to come…” As she passed by the first young man that had fallen to the ground, he looked up slowly, eyes wide at the scene that acted out in front of him. This girl… she just took on the odds and in turn went against them. She didn’t back down… she would fight no matter what. The admiration shimmered in his damp eyes before they were lost in his tears… As everyone else, he had assumed Nagi to be the bad guy… but this moment in life now proved that he was wrong. Everyone assumed the Masonna was just a myth… and as they saying goes, “When you assume, you only make an ASS out of ‘U’ and ME when you’re wrong.” And you’re the bigger ass for not believing from the beginning…
