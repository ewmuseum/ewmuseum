Anyone need a tent?

24 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th Sept 2005 We find ourselves situated in a rather run down looking neighborhood, around us are 10 year old kids smoking and hassling people as they walk past. There are one or two burnt out cars and a few passed out crack addicted to add. All the buildings look old and dated, the are mostly tower blocks with the odd detached house with an overgrown front lawn and a mental old women rocking back and forth stroking a shot gun. A police car slowly rolls down the road as the officers inside inspect to see if everything is in order, or so it seems. Judging by the area it's more likely they're collecting some bribey wibey! The police car rolls by two men who don't appear to be from around here, they look them up and down suspiciously. One is wearing a white t-shirt with the slogan “Life getting you down? Go on Kill yourself” and the other is a red headed man wearing a black diner jacket. The police car rolls off in to the distance as the two men look at the vehicle awkwardly. Red Rock: I think they fancied us! D'avid: Maybe they'll come back with re enforcement..heh Ooooh! Red Rock: everything is a gay innuendo to you isn't it? D'avid: ...heh Red Rock and D'avid have found their way in to Boolie's old neighborhood where he had grown up. They look at all the houses as they walk past checking the piece of paper with what looks like an address written down. On their travels they walk past a parade of shop with some old wooden benches next to a bus stop. Red Rock: Look D'avid, a heroin addict! Lets have some fun! Red Rock walks up to the heroine addict and stands next to where he is slumped out and folds his arms whilst rocking back and forth in the balls of his feet and whistling a hearty tune. The heroine addicts eyes stroll over to Red Rock as he grins with an evil tint in his eye. Then the moment Red Rock is waiting for occurs. Heroin Addict: Heeey Man...you got some change for a sandwich? Red Rock stops rocking and whistling as he unfolds his arms. He turns 90 degrees to his left and looks down at the guy in his dirty stained parker jacket. Red Rock: You don't have to lie to me my friend, you want a fix right? The Heroin addict looks around and nods as if he was a child being offered sweets. Red Rock: I can get you a fix, but you have to do me a favor first. Heroine Addict: Su-sure man anything! Red Rock bends down to the Heroine addicts level and points towards a camping shop just over the road in the parade of shops. Red Rock: You see that camping shop over there. The Heroin addict nods Red Rock: I want you to go in there and steal me two tents! Heroin Addict: O..o..ok! The heroin addict shakily gets off his feet and drags himself across the road to the camping shop. Red Rock looks on trying not to burst out in laughter and D'avid watches expectantly waiting to see what will happen next. Red Rock: This is bloody great! I love doing this, I used to do it all the time at home! There is a brief moment of silence as the two stare at the entrance to the camping shop. The tension grows greater as Red Rock's smile stretches further to his ear. Then suddenly the alarms hit and blast through the air, scaring birds out of trees and turn heads in the direction of the camping shop! Red Rock: This is brilliant! The Heroin addict come bolting through the shop door with a tent under each arm getting chased by two security guards like two dogs chasing a terrified rabbit. Red Rock: BUWAAAA HA HA HA HA HA HAAAA! Red Rock rolls back on the bench slapping his thigh and holding his side. D'avid: heh! Look at him run! Red Rock: AAAHHHH HA HA HA HA HA HA HA! 20 Minutes later Red Rock: Ahh ha...ha woo! Ha ha..ha... heh heh heh! Oh Mercy that never gets old! Just after Red Rock finishes his uncontrollable fit of laughter and to his suprise the Heroin addict, two tents in arms walks over to Red Rock. Red Rock looks back with a shocked expression on his face. Red Rock: What the?! D'avid: heh, he is like a greased weasel and escaped from the security! The heroin addict drops the two tents on the floor and catches his breath. Heroin Addict: I...did...it... Red Rock: Err... At this point Red Rock has no idea what to do, as this has never back fired on him after all his year of doing it at Aldershot train station in his younger days. Red Rock: American heroin addicts are pretty fast... D'avid: I know! D'avid reaches in to his pocket and pulls out a chocolate bar. Red Rock: A chocolate bar? D'avid: Heh, yeah when living in Holland if you gave a heroin addict something brown i would remind them of it, don't ask me why? The Heroin addict extends his hand and D'avid break a block of his chocolate bar off and places it in the hand of the Heroine addict. He clutches it like a shy forest creature and scurries off over the road and sets off in to the distance. Red Rock: That didn't go as well as usual...Oh well At least we have a new tent each. Red Rock and each pick up their tents earned by the desperate efforts of the drug user and march on their way down the road, resuming their quest for Boolie's house. Red Rock: Boolie would of loved that more than you, he really enjoyed taking advantage of desperate heroin addicts... Red Rock sighs and hands his head as he runs the events at Fresh over and over again in his head. Red Rock: I'm going to sodomize the bast that kidnapped Boolie! Who ever it is... Red Rock clenches his fist in a heroic manor. Red Rock: But first it is our duty to inform his family of what happened. Red Rock and D'avid valiantly walk down the street in search of Boolie's family's house. Later that day... Red Rock and D'avid find themselves standing outside a detached house with a half-arsed effort of a front garden. There are a couple of old cards parked in the drive way with a few hundred dents in them. The house its self doesn't look like it's been decorated in years because of all the rotting wood work on the window frame. Red Rock and D'avid check the piece of paper with the address written on it. Red Rock: This is it! I remember it now I've been here before for Boolie's brother's birthday...I fucking hate Boolie's brothers! They are a right set of bastarding rasclarts! D'avid: I think I forgot to leave Greg something to eat! Red Rock: You mean you're still keeping that guy captive in your closest? D'avid: Yes. Red Rock blankly stares at D'avid, for those who don't remember Greg is D'avid's sex slave who he keeps locked up in his closet and performs depraved sexual acts on him involving jump leads and car batteries. Red Rock: You're a fucking freak D'avid I don't know why I let you near me. D'avid: Because Boolie has been kidnapped and you need a sidekick. Red Rock: That is true! Now come along D'avid! Red Rock and D'avid walk up the garden path and as they get closer they can hear the voices of Boolie's family talking and his dog barking. Red Rock and D'avid step up on to the porch and knock on the door. There is a delayed response before the door abruptly opens and standing in the door way is Boolie's grand father Bob. He is a tall man about 6'6 but not as tall as Boolie himself. His face is old and leathery and his clothes are very world war 2 vintage. He stares at Red Rock for a second as Red Rock nervously stands grinning back. Boolie's Mum: Who is it? Bob: It's Boolie's BOYFRIEND Red...Block or whatever...I don't care I need to empty my piss bag. Red Rock: Why does everyone think I'm gay? Do I look gay D'avid? D'avid: Well... Red Rock: I'm NOT Gay! Damn it! Bob: Are you coming in or am I going to have to empty my piss bag here? Red Rock rolls his eyes and enters Boolie's house. D'avid coughs as the huge amount of dust plays on his asthma. There are some old high school pictures of Boolie on the wall with greasy skin, long greasy metaller hair and a mouth of metal. Red Rock: Look at what a retard Boolie looked like! D'avid: H's gorgeous! Boolie's Mum: What the hell do you want? The noise of Boolie's mum's agitated voice bellows from the front room. Red Rock and D'avid walk through the door, stepping over Boolie's little brother Spud. Spud is Boolie's baby brother with a severe problem with the amount of drool deposited from his mouth, it has been known to be fatal. Red Rock steps over looking at little spud in disgust remember the time he had to baby sit for him and the path of destruction he caused. There are two other older looking men sat down in front of the TV, one is a tall dark haired man lazed back on the sofa, this is Steven. The other is a shorter, ginger freckly man this is Boolie's other (clearly adopted) brother Mark. These are Boolie's older brothers who Red Rock really, really hates. Mark: Hey Steven look who it is! It's Red Rock. Steven: Huh, huhhuhhuh qweeeeeeuuuuerrr! Red Rock: Shut up Mark, you're a fucking ginger and Steven, fuck you. Red Rock extends his middle finger in Mark's direction then his other at Steven with a sarcastic grin painted across his bitter angry face. Boolie's Mum: Hey Red....whatever. What do you want? Red Rock converts to a serious tone and takes a seat. Red Rock: I've got some...bad news. Red Rock dramatically pauses. Boolie's Mum: bad news? Red Rock: It's your son Boolie. He's been kidnapped Boolie's Mum's face drops to shock. Boolie's Mum: Oh my God! By who? Red Rock: There is this...person who has been stalking me and threatening me and trying to drive me out of AWC and...he's kidnapped Boolie. Steven: I knew it would be something faggoty involving you! Red Rock: Piss off Steven I'm not talking to you, you knob jockey! Go ride a knob...like a Jockey! Boolie's Mum: My God what are we going to do? Red Rock: It's okay The guy challenged me to a match, if I win he lets Boolie go. Boolie's Mum: Then it is up to you Red Rock, only you can save my son! At this point Mark buts in. Mark: Why don't we just call the police? Red Rock: Mark. If we wanted your ginger opinion we'd ask for it, don't you have to be ginger somewhere else soon? Mark: Not until later... Red Rock: Cunt! D'avid: I'm ginger you know! Red Rock: I know and I hate you too. But at the moment I need to get this guy and I won't let you down Boolie's Mum I will save your son! Mark: Mom, you can't seriously let this guy be responsible to save Boolie? Can't we just call the police! Red Rock: Shut up Mark, you adopted son of a bitch! Mark's face drops from a smug know it all expression to a sudden look of despair. Mark: Adopted? Red Rock: He doesn't know? Mark: I'm...adopted? Boolie's Mum looks towards the floor whilst Red Rock looks around the room. D'avid: you fucked up BIG time! Red Rock: I thought he knew! Mark: My entire life...a lie Red Rock: Aw for fucks sake! As if it wasn't obvious, you're ginger and short. Mark suddenly bursts in to tears and runs out of the house. Steven: Nice one dick! You get my brother kidnapped and emotionally crush my other one! D'avid: Well...technically he's not your brother, he's probably some teenage mum's bastard son. Steven: Shut up you ginger dutch tosser! Red Rock: I think we better go...I hope Mark gets aids, and Steven I hope you end up in jail and become some fat tattooed bastard's buttery corn hole. AS for Boolie I shall save him! Lets go D'avid! Red Rock storms out of the building as D'avid grins and waves good bye whilst Boolie's mum sits in her chair crying.
