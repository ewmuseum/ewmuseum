Date: 12th December 2005 Once again the unpredictable weather plays another cruel joke on the public, it's been a rather up and down week with temperatures fluctuating between warm and freezing with pounding showers of rain to ripping unforgiving gales. But today we're blessed with relentless sub zero temperatures which has painted an icy scenic Christmas card-esq image. The frost shimmers as rays of light purge through the thick white cloud and some robins flutter by and landing on a now bare tree. A cool winter breeze whisks elegantly through the air, dancing around the passing public and flipping hat's off heads and brings the blood rushing to the skin's surface. Our attention is drawn towards an old looking wooden park bench, it has suffered the rages of time and is subject to a lot of wood rot and it is green and moist from the melted frost. Just behind it is an illustrious war memorial paying credit to those who died in the two great wars. The trees arch over is casting some peaceful winter shadows over the plain of freshly cut grass. All these wonderful scenery aside we can't help but notice the two gentlemen occupying the haggard old bench. One of them sits with his arms folded to conserve the warmth in addition of bouncing his feet up and down. Although he is dressed in a thick green parker jacket it is still no match for the devilish wrath of winter, however he simply stares forward with a peaceful smile on his face. The other gentle man seems to be a slightly...unhinged character. He is clad in a full length brown suede winter jacket and on his feet he wears nothing but a pair of slippers. He has long greasy straw like graying hair and a hooked pointy noise, which is covering in broken blood vessels most likely caused by alcohol abuse. The strange looking man looks over at the other and tries to start convocation with an unhinged scary tone of voice. Strange Man: Hey...Hey buddy! Red Rock man looks over at The Strange man but doesn't say a word, just gives him an annoyed icy glare. Strange Man: You know what this is like? The beginning of Forest Gump, only I haven't got that gay white suit he was wearing at the beginning and you're not an old lady! The strange man giggles in a light hearted and cheery way. Red Rock is not one to be impressed by the light hearted will of strangers, especially ones who look homeless and possibly sexually perverted Strange Man: I suppose I could go get one from a charity shop or something, it's not like I have anywhere to be.. Red Rock rolls his eyes and shuffles further up the bench away from the strange man aggressively as he is beginning to find his behavior increasingly irksome. Strange Man: Sooooo...just out for a nice sit down in the park then? Red Rock: No I'm waiting for my friend Boolie, he's just picking up his little brother from the nursery over there across the road. Red Rock points across the road to Hutton Hill Day Nursery it is one of those fancy modern nurseries for rich and powerful businessmen/women to fob their kids off to whilst the slave away thanklessly behind a desk serving some toss pot who couldn't give the shit under their foreskin for them. The build it's self is a refurbished 1960's factory, which doubles up as outlets for various other businesses. There are lots of colorful pictures of Winnie the pooh in the windows and an attractive looking picket fence with a path leading to the door. Red Rock: It's pretty fancy there I don't know how Boolie afford to leave his little bastard brother there for a day... it costs like... I dunno a million quid? Just to dump a little shit there! ... Red Rock: I don't mean like actually taking a poo there....I meant leaving a kid there...obviously. The Strange man gears his head over in Red Rock's direction in an acrimonious fashion. Obviously Red Rock's chat isn't welcomed all of a sudden, as is attention has so abruptly been diverted to something else Strange Man: LOOK! Shut up. I'm trying to concentrate! I've been waiting for this all day. Red Rock: What? Weren't you the one so keen to bother me a second ago? Besides You're just sitting there doing nothing. Freaking knobber. The Strange man stares intently at at the front doors, he just sits waiting but the time he has been anticipating is now upon him as the grand glossy red door creeps open and this ravishing young woman emerges. She is a petite thing, she is hot in a geeky way with her thick framed glasses and her wavy chocolate brown hair. She is wearing a pair of figure hugging jeans and a plain white top. The sun light reflects off the large glass panels on the front of the building to create an angelic glow around this beautiful woman. This must be one of the play group helpers. She catches Red Rock's and his face beams like child on Christmas day. Red Rock: Oh I SEE, you fancy the play group leader...yeah she's a hotsie isn't she? Red Rock winks and giggles. The old man and unknowingly to Red Rock has slipped his hand in to his pocket and reached for his... “gun”. The play group leader walks away and leaves their field of vision but The Strange Man's lustful thoughts haven't ceased. Red Rock shortly notices this and his face switches to baffled. It's not long after this when the children start to leave the playgroup with their parents. A little blonde child is first to leave and the strange man's face tightens and he starts to grunt a little louder. Red Rock begins to feel the bench vibrate in and he also notices the children exiting the building...but not in the same way as the strange man. Red Rock: Hey! ...wait a minute. Red Rock scrolls his eyes slowly to his left and surveys the Strange Man's expression with a raised eyebrow before Red Rock begins to notice the vigorous hand movements coming from the brown suede jacket. And then it sinks in with a sickening feeling in his stomach. Red Rock: oh...my...GOD! Red Rock's face creases up and he leaps from the bench and shudders, pointing at the Strange man. Red Rock: YOU SICK WEIRDO! Strange Man: SHUT UP! You're ruining the moment! Red Rock: What moment? You're having a wank! Hold on...you're not even wearing anything under that coat are you? Strange Man: Nope! Red Rock: URGH GOD DAMNIT! Red Rock walks over the road making vomiting noises and shaking his head. As we see over Red Rock's shoulder 2 policemen stroll up to the Strange man who's face drops. He tries to run away but not before the policemen dive on the strange man and start battering the crap out of him with their truncheons before tying rope around his feet and dragging him away across the floor drawn by a police horse. The moment Red Rock reaches the other side of the road Boolie walks out on to the path with what looks like a bag of potatoes over his shoulder. Red Rock waves at his good friend who mooches over to meet him. Red Rock inquisitively observes what is over Boolie's shoulder and can't help but ask. Red Rock: What is that over your shoulder? Boolie: THAT Is my little brother? Red Rock: What? He a bit big for a two year old, I thought you were stealing something! Boolie: Well I come from a large family, what can I say? Boolie Is Red Rock's good friend, the met when Red Rock began his career wrestling in America. Boolie is a giant as he stand at 7'7, he is without a doubt the tallest man in the business. Over Boolie's shoulder is his little brother who is nick named “Little Spud”. This is because he is often likened to a sack of potatoes. He's not usually around Red Rock and Boolie but today Boolie's mother has called upon her eldest son to look after her youngest whilst she is out of town. Laura: Hello, over there? The voice of the gorgeous brunette Red Rock was ogling at earlier swims around in Red Rock's head as she heads over to Red Rock and Boolie. Judging by her tone of voice and body language suggests she is concerned about something. She addresses Boolie by tapping him over the shoulder because he attention is diverted to the police brutality being inflicted on the Strange man across the road. Laura: Hello, is it Brian? Boolie: Buh? Me? No my name is Boolie! Laura: Right...Anyway I'm Laura the leader of the play group, I'd like a word with you about you're little brother Spud. Red Rock grins foolishly as he buts in to the private convocation. Red Rock: My name is Red Rock! By the way... Laura doesn't respond she just simply brushes off and disregards Red Rock's courtesy with a pretentious look of disgust. Red Rock maintains the grin and points out of the circle of convocation. Red Rock: I'll...just go over here. Laura: Anyway. Boolie, I noticed today Spud was picking on some of the other children. Well when I say some I mean one. Boolie: My goodness that is very out of character, I'll have words with him for he WILL be disciplined Which is a lie, Boolie is too stupid and lazy to discipline anything. Laura: I'm afraid it doesn't stop there, he also stole the young boy's toy elephant. Boolie looks at what is in Little Spud's hands and realizes he is clinging on to a small tatty blue velvet elephant. Boolie: Oh dear, he certainly will be punished Miss Laura you can take that to the bank. Laura: Theres more... Red Rock: Oh come on! Laura stares a dagger or two at Red Rock who is trying to subtly listen in on the discussion. Laura: You see the boy who Little Spud stole from is Malek. Boolie stares at Laura blankly. Laura: The little half cast boy. Unknowingly to Boolie Red Rock is still listening in and his ears spring up at the accusation. Boolie: That's awful! Absolutely terrible, I'm shocked and disgusted! I'm going to take him home and give him a stern talking to! I'll take him home now, TO BED with him with no supper! Boolie has a serious look etched on his face, with a tone of responsibility in his voice. He stomps his foot and puts his hand on his hip. Boolie: I'm going to march him home right now and have a word with him! Boolie spins 180 degrees on his heel and marches over to where Red Rock is standing, who is looking a bit spaced out and bored. Red Rock: SO What did the she want? Boolie: Pfftt, fuck knows? I wasn't paying attention I was looking at her Boobies! Back at Red Rock's...wonderful? Flat. The door flies open with Red Rock and Boolie step in to Red Rock's flat. The over powering sound of Don't Stop me now by Queen playing over the stereo greets Red Rock and Boolie and they hit breaks and stop in their tracks as they are met with the nude ginger body of the dutch pervert down the hall. Red Rock: ARGH! D'avid there are children present! Make your self decent! D'avid: Heh...OPPS! Within the walls of this humble room is how Red Rock lives his life. He has a unwashed cream leather sofa positioned in front of a television set. Just behind the sofa is the original 1960's style kitchen complete with a ghetto tin sink full of week old washing up. The reason D'avid is dancing around naked in Red Rock's flat is because of a burst radiator soaking D'avid's flat about 6 weeks ago. It's probably just an excuse. Red Rock: You're going to have to start wearing clothes when Boolie's little brother is here! D'avid: Right, I'll go put my clothes on! Red Rock: Ohhh quick put channel 4 on we're going to miss Richard and Judy! Red Rock and Boolie dash over to the sofa and flick over to channel 4. Richard and Judy are interviewing some crazy scientist trying to convince the public that Scientology is the truth. Red Rock: Scientology? Whats that all about? You know they actually believe that some alien lord sent aliens to Earth millions of years ago? Boolie: But Tom Cruise is a scientologist and he's a movie star! Red Rock: Pfft! Tom Cruise? Red Rock and Boolie slump back on to the sofa as they leave Little Spud to play with his toy school bus and a packet of jelly babies. Red Rock at this point is getting bored of listening to the scientologist rabble on about some idiots Sci-Fi fantasy. He looks over at what little spud is up to as he joyous baby noises he is making. Red Rock watches with a heart warming smile as he envies Little Spud's care free life. Suddenly Little Spud drives his toy bus in to Red Rock's view and Red Rock's heart warming smile drops. Red Rock: Err...Boolie... ... Red Rock: BOOLIE! Boolie: WHAT? This is interesting, insightful and believable stuff! Red Rock: Look at this... Boolie looks over at Little Spud's playful activities. Boolie: aww look! He's playing with his little toy bus and using his jelly babies as passengers! Red Rock: I know Boolie..look CLOSER! Boolie leans over and takes a closer a look and shrugs. Boolie: I don't see what I'm supposed to be looking at. Red Rock: He's put all the black jelly babies at the back of his toy bus stupid! Boolie: ...I don't follow. Red Rock: Stealing from the black kid at the Nursery ring any bells? Boolie: Who did that? Red Rock: Fucks sake... Red Rock: I can't believe Chico was voted out! That was an injustice! The TV changes to an interview with Andy (black guy) and at this moment Little Spud starts to Cry. Boolie: Whats your point? Red Rock: Andy, is black and Euro Sport has no blacks...he's putting black jelly babies at his toy bus, and stealing from the black kids at nursery.
