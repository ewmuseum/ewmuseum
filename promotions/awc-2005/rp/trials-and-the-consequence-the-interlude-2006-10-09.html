Date: 9/10/06 From the files of ………… Interlude I The 14th of September, 2006 Anno Domini, Ah my heart lulls with the tumultuous times that are being lain before me in the path of the Lord. Despite the resolve that strengthen and steels me, I feel anxious about the urges and restrictions being placed upon me by those above me in this corporeal world. They ask too much, they look for many reparations, most of which I cannot give them. The strain being measured is reaching a point of no return and it irks me. I shall remain strong and content in the power of the Lord though, confident in his blessing for my works. He knows that some things must be done in order to wreak the greater good and fulfilment of his people, for otherwise why would he have made Moses and his people wander the desert for 40 years? Are we not destined to endure before we reap the seeds that we have sown? Amidst all of this however I pray for my precious child Sylvia. She is a shining light in the face of the darkness and even now I know the precarious position I have put her in. She will be tested thoroughly through her faith, but I am confident in her capability and will to hold herself back from the veritable apple dangling in front of her. As she well knows, it is the temptation of such objects that are the first choices down an inglorious path. With heavy heart I leave her though and the last moments before she headed out into her own journey were ones filled with tears and repentant apologies for things that were unspoken and unheard of in our lives. Words are meaningless when the power of the visual can achieve something so much more powerful. But words aren’t powerless for if they can create the visual aspect required then they become limitless in what they can achieve. Words are lasting, the visual an instant. I digress however, may the Lord shine on those who fight for the freedom and the continued message of the Lord God our Saviour, IN the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost, Amen. Suggest continued monitoring. *** The 22nd of September, 2006 Anno Domini, Time has moved swiftly since my last entry and yet my heart still lingers. My disciple even now is coming closer and closer to the den of iniquity which has been placed into my life most suddenly and harming. Her assertions of faith give me hope but the reasons for which she has now revealed why this must come to pass is quite disturbing. I feel a little of my faith extinguish with the mention of the important works of Dr. Kasidy Drake. She is arrogant and dismissive of anything that would be about to go wrong in this condition but I have more serious questions about the situation. She places herself on the tower of Babylon, spouting forth the imminent quandary of language after language, tongue after tongue to manipulate and coerce those around her to fall into place. This method I find disheartening and not at all what I hoped she would be practising. Such is the work of the Lord however. I have renewed my fervour in the study of the ancient works to placate my unsettled soul, searching for the answers to the questions that lay before me. I know that in my heart there are answers placed by the more prophetic and philosophical minds than myself. In this time of troublesome possibilities I confide my thoughts to Machiavelli, Hume and Plato, divulging my examples as to why they may or may not be wrong in their estimations as to the construction of the world. Hume in particular is a constant stirrer of the blood as he probes and manifests multiple fiery conclusions which I share little opinion with. I will return to my studies to bring about a revelation in my sermons. This is what I do best. IN the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost, Amen. *** The 28th of September Anno Domini, My soul rejoices in the name of the Lord as I have finally received word from my precious child. Sylvia has delivered unto me a most glorious message that shall be received by all as a crowning blow for the path of the righteous. This upcoming sermon which I have been preparing shall be all the more sweet as I enjoy the nectar and ambrosia of the Lord as he opens the path unto me. She called me henceforth only a few minutes ago about the proceedings and I was heartened by the sound of her voice at once. Her words were more than simply a victory, they were an angelic chorus and one that I had longed to hear for some time. When it was all revealed I couldn’t help but break a smile on the fortune that the Lord had showered upon us. I now sit in the church praying to the Lord even as I write this, hoping that his will shall be delivered to us irrespective of how the method. I am certain that now the path is being laid and walked by those who are more directly involved than I. I merely direct from afar, using them as pawns as best I can in the light of the Lord. There shall be much yet to come from the Lord but I am confident in his judgment. IN the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost, Amen. The subject is displaying a healthy interest in the politics and subterfuge that he is involved in and as such needs no more cultivation in that respect. Indeed he has been very quick to perceive himself as a motivational player in such a dance of manipulation. It will not be long before he is completely under our sway and I would recommend that we continue to feed him much power through our operative.
