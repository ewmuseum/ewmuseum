Fifteen Minutes III

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th July 2006 They led me from that animal enclosure; it was the first time I had paced more than five yards in a single direction in what felt like months. Gatsby looked even more distinguished in natural light… Hah, natural… his orange skin revealed that even in his old age, the Great Gatsby’s vanity and decadence never changed. I felt like calling him Jay. I probably would have. But you know… situations dictate that and all… … my fifteen minutes are up… It hit me hard – the pungent taste of blood and torn flesh pervaded my throat as I walked past the latest ‘accident’. They said accident… I mean, their fists might have accidentally hit him; several times… it was a taste I was familiar with. The reason I was thrown in the cage from the offset was because of… him… … my fifteen minutes are up… There he stood, The Penguin. Well… he looked like The Penguin. Possessed with the fiercest of small man syndrome, he looked like a shaken bottle of Doctor Pepper. His personality was rambunctious, nefarious… in essence a mangai chinabu. It was happening again. Don’t do it Mags… or Gabs… or GA I felt my fists clench as the gaze of the Penguin caught mine. Our eyes intertwined and began a fierce tango… his arrogant smile cocked slightly to the left widened… “Munga…” the words fell crisply from his fat mouth – unusual… anything crispy would normally have been annexed straight into that most unhealthy of orifices. Me? To me? His words… to me? A cunt? … What the fuck? I’m the Juggernaught bitch – yo’ fifteen minutes are up… My body recoiled, tearing myself from the nonchalant grip of Jay; he wouldn’t have caught me if I sneezed, but it was all an act. To teach the Penguin… imbue that element of fear… reinstall that feeling when I first cracked four knuckles across his nose as his baton attack did little damage to me… he got me later though – the conditions… I definitely had the plushest cell. Effortlessly pushing aside a rather miniscule guard, I stared deep down into the Penguin – his soul was black, blacker than the eye he was about to receive, blacker than any oil – he was corrupt, evil… an abrogation of morality. His head recoiled back into the wall, bouncing like an old school touchdown spike, time for the TO pom pom shake? Showboating never hurt anyone… … my fifteen minutes are up… Locking my arms around the Penguin’s throat in the tightest of kataha-jime choke holds, the pleasure builds to climactic proportions as the Penguin is launched over and down, landing straight on the back of his neck. Picture. Perfect. Two guards attempted some show of defiance raising batons, as if to strike me. In reality, they knew I’d take them, the four people behind me, Gatsby, the Penguin, the Suit all out to the buffet cart, eat them under the table then pick their plates for leftovers. Yes, I was that good. A fighter, yes, you are looking at the finest. Never defeated in mixed martial arts competition… somehow though, that feeling of ‘things are about to change’ was developing in the realms of my gut. Nope… It was the swift blow of a baseball bat. Ouch. I lay prone, expecting the blows to rain down in some cataclysmic show of hellish monsoon. The air cut in two as the bat made its way toward my skull… tut tut… I was never a Muhammed Ali, but to these fools I was the Bruce Lee to their five year old dog. Sweeping the guards legs out from underneath him, his ankle snapped in two after an aptly applied heel hook tore the bone in two. His screams of agony were a concerto to my eyes, some Jedi Mind Tricks, I Against I… my rap was just immaculate. … my fifteen minutes are up… “That’s enough.” The Suit’s slow and deliberate diction gave him an air of immutable elegance. His words forced everyone with intent to dispossess me of limbs to stop in their tracks. “You ALL know the agreement.” His voice picked up in tension, he must’ve been a lawyer or something… knew just what to say. Picking myself up from the ground, the welt from the baseball bat began to appear underneath the flea ridden shirt I’d worn for… months? “Gatsby…” I didn’t know their names, I could care less. “Gatsby, old guy. How long have I been here?” “You mean… you don’t know?” he expressed a notion of somewhat shock intermingled with a feeling that he too had served some time. Probably gave him that limp as well. Thank fuck they didn’t get me that way. “Well Gats, everything just seems to blend into an amalgamation of days… I couldn’t tell you the day, let alone the length of time I’ve been in there.” It hurt to speak of that place, although I was too hubris fuelled to admit, that place had left severe traumas on my conscience. The loneliness was a killer, and, there’s only so much you can talk about to yourself. “A few months?” it was a guess, nothing more. “Months? Ha, dear chap, try two years.” There was a definite tone of despair, however he masked it well with astonishment. I was in shock. It didn’t fee that long. Though I suppose, would anyone know or feel the effects of time if they didn’t see the passing of day into night? That fantastic light that shone brightly in his cell was testament to his fantastic sleep pattern. Through the miasma shrouding his judgement, Gatsby wasn’t a bad man. Well, on the face of it. … and your fifteen minutes are up… What is it with this fifteen minute malarkey…
