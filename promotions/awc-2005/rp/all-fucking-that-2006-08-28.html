All Fucking That

28 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/8/06 Clearwater, Florida, August 28th 2006 It had been over two months since Shawn Harris made his return to the Atlantic Wrestling Club and in that time he had confronted Vince Jones, injured his knee and lost a match to him. The two were set to meet again at Coast to Coast 2006. However that was not meant to be as Shawn was set to take down Vince on a personal level. He had planned to introduce Vince’s mistress to his girlfriend Jasmine he also was the reason why Vince lost the trophy as the mind games got to him and dampened his desire. Vince took his leave of AWC which left Shawn at a loss, his only salvation came when he and his tag partner made it into the finals of the Alliance division tournament to crown new number one contenders. Shawn needed this victory to put him in the record books of AWC, he had been looked over on so many occasions when it came to everything and it was starting to get to him. The debut of Pacific Wrestling Club caused quite a stir as Krimzon and Harris began hacking the AWC web site they then started playing mind games on both Sasha and Dave Harber. After their initial debut as the tag team they quickly began to lose steam as they failed to make a big enough impact. This was what it was like being outside of Jeff H.’ shadow, it was empty and lonely. When Jeff H. was around people took notice of Shawn because they wanted to see the son of the great Jeff H. at work but as soon as Shawn began rebelling and pushing his father out of the way they started losing interest. The fans didn’t want Shawn and Jeff fighting, they wanted them watching each others back and Shawn had only just begun realizing this. Shawn could have been the one man to stop Jeff getting sent to jail had he just listened to his father in the first place. Shawn blamed himself for Jeff coming out of hiding early last year despite the fact it was Jeff’s decision. If Pacific Wrestling Club couldn’t beat the Coalition at Coast to Coast then they will truly be a failed entity. Shawn had hoped that Teresa Tomas would be the next member of PWC but with their tag team defeat against her and Lavelle she made her choice. Things for PWC looked grim at the moment and there was no denying it, the future didn’t look much brighter. When Shawn met his father in prison back in February it gave him the desire he needed to get better and to get himself into shape for a return but from that point on he was on his own. Jimmy Thunder went back to Australia in March and things didn’t work out with Faye once she realized that Shawn had one true commitment and that was to AWC. She didn’t want him to go anywhere because she couldn’t stand for him to get hurt again. Things went full circle at that point as he seemed to remember his father being kicked out of his own home for the same reason by his mother; Stephanie Carter. Shawn had told Faye that he was ready to go back to AWC back in June which resulted in her giving him an ultimatum. Shawn of course picked the wrestling business, a decision he regretted at times but just like his father if he wasn’t a wrestler what would he be? If Shawn had one great accomplishment outside of wrestling it was freeing Faye from the abusive bastard she was with before, she could now live her life as Shawn was living his. Wrestling was in Shawn’s blood it always had been since he watched his father on the television when he was a young child. Going to his shows and capturing championships, he knew that’s what he wanted to do. He couldn’t let himself down anymore it was time for a change he loved joking around with Krimzon but once it got to the ring that’s when things got serious. Their hi-jinks needed to happen outside of the ring only, no more screwing around because Shawn’s L column was filling up quickly and he didn’t want the son of Jeff H. to be known as a curtain jerking jobber. Shawn was about to enter the beach house that his father had bought some years ago in Clearwater, Florida. He lived there in the summer and was given it as a gift from his father when Jeff went back to Las Vegas, once his dad was arrested and sent to prison Shawn then started living at the Las Vegas mansion instead. As Shawn enters the beach house he can hear laughing in the kitchen, the first time he’s been to the house in a long time and someone had broken in. Shawn picked up the baseball bat that was by the door and walked into the kitchen as he got there he could see the huge seven foot Jimmy Thunder with a beer in hand. Shawn hadn’t seen Jimmy in five months so there had to be a reason why he was standing in his kitchen. “Hey Shawny!” Jimmy shouted happily. “What the hell are you doing in my kitchen?!” Shawn yelled. “I was invited!” Jimmy yelled back but in a happy tone. “I don’t remember inviting you here unless I was drunk or something.” Shawn said confused. “No you didn’t invite.” Jimmy replied. “Then who did?” Shawn asked. All of a sudden a shadow emerged from behind Shawn and a voice said, “I did.” Shawn spun around to see his father standing there in the doorway. The man he hadn’t seen since February after he suffered his injury from Vince Jones. “Dad?” Shawn said in awe. “That’s right boy, daddy’s home. Surprised you hadn’t heard I was out already actually, I’ve been out for about a month now. Even found a job with FWF.” Jeff answered with a grin on his face. “Out for a month? I haven’t been keeping up with the news or anything because I’ve been travelling with AWC, how can you be out if you have a life sentence?” Shawn asked. “Turns out Simon Caine is still alive, he’s been photographed walking around London somewhere, pretty reckless of him actually I expected better than that from him. But whatever it worked in my favour and I’m not longer in that shit hole of a prison.” Jeff responded. “I hope you’re not going to go looking for him now that you’re out.” Jimmy said. “Oh no, there’s no point, I beat the hell out of him in Bloodbox and he has hardly any money left so what’s the use in putting myself to the trouble? No I have bigger things that need doing, like getting your wrestling career back on track.” Jeff said to Shawn. “What’s wrong with my wrestling career?” Shawn asked. “What’s wrong with it? What’s right with it must be the question; seriously what do you think you’re doing with Pacific Wrestling Club? The hell is that meant to be?” Jeff questioned. “It’s fun actually dad. Pissing off the management is great I mean sure it’s gotten us thrown out a couple of times but hey what do you expect when they can’t fight back.” Shawn answered. “Fun? It’s a joke is what it is. Do you think that’s what’s going to get you propelled into the main event? Why do you think you got so far in GCW? It wasn’t because of who I was or because you ran around spray painting things. It’s because you were serious about your career and about everything you did. That’s why you remained undefeated in that place now look at you Shawn; no one is noticing you in AWC. They’re too busy about Pierce Lavelle, Garbage Bag Johnny and that idiot Adam Dick. You couldn’t even beat Vince Jones for crying out loud.” Jeff snapped at him. “I was fighting injured I’ll have you know!” Shawn snapped back. “Injured?! You have no idea what injured is. So you had a bum knee, so what you have another one. I’ve wrestled with half my body incapacitated before and I managed a victory.” Jeff said almost bragging. “Well that was then and this is now. You know what, you’ve never faced Vince Jones so you wouldn’t know what it’s like would you?” Shawn asked. “I suppose not no, but I’ve seen him wrestle and your skills outmatch his by far!” Jeff replied. Shawn didn’t like what he was hearing but he knew it was true. Shawn had been on the losing end of things quite a lot recently since being paired up with Krimzon, despite the fact it had been Krimzon that got him the victories and what not. Shawn felt himself slipping and that’s not a good feeling to have, Jeff used to push him hard when he was training him back in 2003 but in Jeff’s absence Shawn wasn’t keeping up to his normal training regime, he was too busy enjoying spending his money on petty little things that haven’t really got much meaning at all. Slipping in the ring was also starting to get Shawn hurt as he had suffered to brutal landings on his neck on two consecutive weeks. Two suplexes both resulting in Shawn needing to take pain killers for several weeks. Shawn hadn’t told anyone about the injury however as it would mean him slipping down the ladder at an even more accelerated rate. Shawn loved the business but he wasn’t that obsessed about it yet like Jeff H. was. “I’m going to be there for your tag title match next week, I have a pay per view match my self that night but I’ll have time to catch yours first. You better pull through this thing son because the Coalition just should be a piece of cake for you. I think all you need is a kick in the ass and now that I’m back we’ll get you to the top of that company.” Jeff stated. “Did you not have any fun when you were younger?” Shawn asked. “You’re damn right I did, but when I was younger all I wanted to do is wrestle and I found that in it self fun. Even when I had funny gimmicks I still won matches because I kept training trying to aspire. Once you get back into it then the people at Atlantic Wrestling Club will truly realise your potential.” Jeff answered. “So you’re both here to get me back to the top?” Shawn asked. “Oh I’m not mate; Jeff invited me here to apologize for the way he acted after the reunion show a couple of months ago. I’m happy living in Australia; it’s where you both should be actually.” Jimmy said. “If Australia had any high paying wrestling promotions then I probably would be but wrestling just isn’t nationally popular over there is it? I’m treated well there anyway despite the fact I haven’t got anyone’s respect.” Shawn retorted. “How are things with your girlfriend going?” Jimmy asked. “Girlfriend?” Jeff inquired turning to Jimmy. “Yeah, he found himself a girl right after he visited you in prison. Faye isn’t it?” Jimmy asked turning back to Shawn. Shawn stood there; he knew that he’d be letting them both down when he told them the truth. He screwed things up with another girl that lives only a couple of doors down from the beach house about a year and a half ago because of his attitude toward the opposite sex. She knew this but the constant arguments they had about wrestling got to him in the end. Why was it any time Shawn or Jeff found a woman that woman always had a problem with the wrestling business. What was the big deal about being a wrestler? It’s wrestling that put the food on their table and kept the shirt on their backs yet most women seem to want them to forget where they came from and live a normal life. That was something Shawn couldn’t understand and he hated the fact that he was no longer together with Faye. He truly did love her and for a moment contemplated retiring for her but he knew he’d be letting his father down as Shawn for some reason always wanted Jeff’s acceptance. What he never knew is Jeff would have been proud of Shawn regardless of what he did. “We broke up, it wasn’t working out. She wanted me to leave the business and settle down and it’s like… I’m twenty one years old! I’m too young to settle down you know what I mean?” Shawn said. “You made the right choice then.” Jeff answered; Jimmy didn’t seem to agree with that statement and shook his head. Jimmy also didn’t share the same passion for wrestling as he saw it as just a job, something he could walk away from at any time. Jimmy had been retired for almost a year now since Bloodbox ended and Jeff got carted off to jail. He felt that strongly about not wanting to wrestle anymore that he refused to wrestle in the WFW reunion show that his other three best friends took part in. He didn’t see the business as a life like Jeff did unfortunately; Shawn’s name wasn’t Shawn Thunder. “I did?” Shawn asked pretty shocked. “Yeah I think so, your mother wanted me to quit the business right before I made it big time and you are too young to settle down, I mean, you’re filthy rich thanks to me giving you all my money before I got sent to jail but that’s no reason to give up what you love the most. Jimmy of course won’t agree.” Jeff said with a smirk on his face. “You’re damn right I don’t. I got a pretty huge pay off when Bloodbox closed its doors and I did retire with it. What’s the point of putting your body through even more punishment? Mind you, look who I’m asking, you’ve been killed in a wrestling ring before Jeff and you’re still going. We have to completely different views on this business, but you know I’ll have your back when you ask for it.” Jimmy stated. “So what are these Coalition guys like that you’re facing?” Jeff asked Shawn. “They’re not too bad I suppose. I don’t know if you’ve seen them in action all that much but I think I could beat them, Krimzon isn’t all retard inside the ring either you know. He’s the reason why I’ve won my only two victories in the AWC in the first place.” Shawn answered. “Yes I’ve noticed, it’s time for you to come to Las Vegas with me. We’re going to get you into tip top shape before Coast to Coast and I’ll get myself into shape before Summer Inferno alright?” Jeff said. Shawn always hated accepting help off of people especially his father, how can he impress him when Jeff’s the one that’s keeping him going? But even if Shawn hated the idea he did need help training for his match. Shawn just stood there and looked at the floor; he then nodded his head knowing that he was going to undergo some pretty rigorous training. Jeff walked up to him and patted him on the shoulder. “Good.” Jeff said, “I’ve booked a flight for the first thing tomorrow morning. Are you going to join us Jimmy? I’ve booked three seats.” “No thanks mate I gotta get back home to my misses, otherwise she might kick me out of the house heh heh…” Jimmy said laughing he instantly stopped however when Jeff and Shawn shot him evil glares. “Kidding… have fun though anyway, I’ll be looking on the internet for the results, either that or one of you can call me.” “Alright man later, thanks for coming.” Jeff said. “Later Jimmy.” Shawn repeated. They both shook his hand then he walked back out of the wrestling business once more, Shawn wish it was that easy for him, Jeff wished the same thing once but his obsession for the wrestling business grew. As Jimmy left the house Shawn walked over to the fridge and pulled out a couple of beers. The two then went to sit out on the balcony talking about Jeff’s time in prison. Today was a day of rest but Shawn knew that tomorrow was going to be hell. Fade.
