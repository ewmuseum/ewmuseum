Hindsight

23 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/06 The rustle of paper and the stretch of tape. There's Ellis Nash and Pierce Lavelle. The fevered obsessions of a long lost spirit, they look at him now. Garbage Johnny and Josh Marquez. They judge without jury, their eyes soulless and lost. Seymour Almasy and Agent Dash. They start to crowd him, some of them happy and some them not. Darcy Crisis and Scott Taylor. Finally he stops, life coming deep with a sense of completion as his breast falls heavy with the burden of fate. Across the walls and plastered over the door, posters and magazine ads, cardboard cutouts with name tags, catch phrases, and headlines. All of them, all eight, looking at him or looking away. Smiling, snarling, sometimes laughing or dramatic. They surround him on all sides and on all sides they come, an army of hopes and ambitions, personalities and plastic expressions. His breath is an extension of rhythmic control devising the beat upon beat within which he is destined. Here before the paper men and woman of his battlefield he must face himself before all others. Wednesday Night 'Lucy, what are you wearin?' Midas exclaims to the security guard as he walks through the double doors. The hotel lobby was well lit and no matter how good the lighting it couldn't add life to Lucious' grey sweater and pants. 'What?' The beak nosed austrian regards his attire. 'It matches.' 'It's ugly.' Midas gives him a look. Johnny chuckles quietly leaning against the side of his Rolls Royce. 'Lex.' Lucy calls. 'I look fine, right?' Johnny opens his door. 'We'll stop on Rodeo Drive and pick you out a dress.' 'Aww.' Lucious hangs his head and approaches the passenger side as Midas rounds to the driver's door and climbs inside. Johnny straddles the middle of the back seat, his arms stretching out to either side languidly. His black and white silk shirt looks sharp underneath the dark colored blazer and matching slacks. The top few buttons are left undone and hanging from his neck is a golden miniature King Prawn, shining almost as bright as fake smile number twelve. Lucious and Midas talk amongst themselves in the front seat and leave Johnny to his thoughts, and his thoughts stretch to being left out of the conversation. 'Oh yeah.' Midas is saying. 'Heroes is a great show. You watch monday's episode?' 'Of course.' Lucious is responding. 'Johnny got me wireless satelite for my portable.' 'Nice. Should see if he'll get me some of that for in here.' Midas is musing. 'I'll bet you he will. Just start complaining when he makes you wait outside.' Lucy is explaining. 'Like I'm not even here anymore.' Johnny is thinking. Wednesday Afternoon 'So what do you think Mister Lexicon?' The silky toned salesman crosses his hands before himself, standing confidently before his billboard of projected sales and income. Johnny puts his chin in hand and looks across the table at Midas. Both of them are dressed in suits and ties, intimidation tactics Midas had said. 'Can you give us a minute to talk?' Johnny motions his hand towards the door. The saleman looks a little uncertain of being asked to leave his own office, but after only a moments hesitation he replaces his smile. 'Of course.' He clears his throat on his way out the door. 'What are you thinking?' Midas leans over the table. 'I think I'm tired of all these meetings, you've got your mind made up already.' 'The kid's done his homework. It's a small endoresement package, just like you wanted. A little extra income.' 'Stop selling me, Midas.' Johnny finally sits forward and drops the casual facade. 'I don't care. Have slick draw up the contracts and we'll celebrate tonight.' Johnny walks to the door and brushes past the saleman without so much as a glance. Midas appears in the portal in his wake. 'Uh.' Midas replaces confusion with a smile. 'We have a deal.' Wednesday Morning Dappled beams of darling sunlight sneak around the edges of a heavy curtain. Johnny tip toes through the warming golden rays as he does the buttons of his button fly faded blue jeans. Sitting down quietly in a squeaky chair he slides first one and then the other foot inside the cotton confines of yesterdays socks. Throwing a white shirt over his shoulder he grabs his shoes and steps towards the door. 'So you're leaving again.' Her voice accuses coldly. Johnny looks to her with a warming smile. 'No.' He says and steps to her side. Blond hair spilling across her slender shoulders and neck, eyes of an icy blue to match his own. 'Just going for coffee.' Leaning down to kiss her forehead. 'I'm not leaving again.' She smiles a smile of her own and watches him slip his shoes on and walk out the door. She dresses quickly, brushes her hair perched on the edge of the bed and watching the portal. She lays back languidly and counts the seconds, staring at the ceiling, her heart climbing higher into her throat. Sitting anxiously on the chair, one leg crossed over the other. There's a knock on the door and Eva rises in a standing stride, Johnny slips inside with a wink; his shoulder propping a cell phone to his ear and a coffee in each hand. With an unexplainable relief Eva takes her coffee with a kiss and dissappears into the bathroom to do her makeup. 'Right. Yeah. Midas, shut up, I got it. Pick me up in an hour, I'll have a suit ready.' He clicks the phone shut. 'Hey.' He knocks on the bathroom door once and leans against the wall. 'I need to take off for awhile.' 'Where you going?' 'Just some meetings with Midas. I need to get a suit.' The door opens a crack and Eva pokes her head out. 'A suit? How long you got?' 'An hour.' 'I got it covered.' She dissapears with a wink and the click of the closing door. Tuesday Night Johnny pushes his way past a trio of men violently, his face etched in anger and frustration. The throbbing pound of music drowns out the curses of protest, people close together in the wake of the man as he marches on through the club. Down a set of stairs, somebody steps obvliviously in his way and without thought Johnny sets his forearm and shoves the man backwards into a group of girls. 'Johnny?' Eva calls over the music. Johnny stops and looks for her. 'Johnny!' Her voice sounds again and he finally spots her. She steps around a couple people standing back and watching the six foot six angry bear. 'What the hell?' She yells at him but Johnny grabs her hand. 'C'mon.' Is all he says, pulling her after him towards the exit, stiff arming his way past the mob of inebriated obstacles. Finally he reaches the coat check, Eva trying in vain to pull her arm free. 'Johnny!' She cries out. He ignores her and continues past one bouncer signaling to his friend at the door, a heavy fellow almost as tall as the wrestler. He steps into the way, 'This guy bothering you?' Is all he's able to get out before Johnny stiff arms him against the wall. Another bouncer to his left appears and the one down the hall breaks into a run. 'No no no!' Eva shouts, putting herself between them and Johnny as they back out the door. 'It's fine! He's my boyfriend!' 'Just get out of here!' The heavy set victim shouts at them and Eva pushes against Johnny with both hands. Once in the parkinglot Eva stops pushing and punches Johnny in the arm. 'What the hell was that?' Johnny paces and shakes his head. 'Tell me.' Eva waits. 'Tell me!' 'It's nothing. I just got some bad news and wanted to get out of there.' Eva plants both hands on her hips and shakes her head. 'That's not good enough Johnny.' Johnny takes a deep breath to calm himself. 'Boyfriend huh?' Ten Minutes Earlier 'Midas where you going?!' Johnny shouts over the music with a smile, approaching the bar with an empty bottle and leaning against it as Midas was about to walk away. The manager stops and turns back surprised. 'I'm uh, just going to the bathroom!' Fake smile number eleven. Johnny frowns a little, standing up straighter and looking over peoples heads for Eva, waiting for him by their table with Lucy. Sighing he ducks away from the bar and heads after Midas, pausing only as he passes the bathrooms and continues on towards the back door. He arrives just as the door is about to click closed and he catches it with a hustled step. Cracking it open he peeks through with a single eye. As he watches Midas walks out to the middle of the alley and stops. He looks around in a full circle and then shuffles his feet as if waiting for someone. He starts suddenly as a voice approaches from the street, off angle from Johnny's line of sight. They talk but Johnny can't hear them over the music in the club, the look on Midas' face is one of seriousness. And fear. Finally the man steps into view holding a small wad of bills, the blood freezes in Johnny's veins. Lupus Feral shoves the money into Midas' pocket and pats his shoulder with a smile. Johnny lets the door close with a growl and turns away. Tuesday Afternoon 'I'm starting to think this isn't coincidence.' 'I'm starting to think you're following me.' Eva replies with a smile. Johnny gives her a little chuckle, using his hand to shield his eyes against the sun. They stand outside an Albertson's Suits in the middle of the sidewalk, almost crowded by people walking this way and that but in their eyes, the streets are empty and there's nobody else around. 'You're looking good.' Cocking her hip a bit with a mock pout before laughing and touching Johnny's arm. 'You.' Eva hesitates. 'You wanna get some ice cream.' Johnny pretends to check his watch. 'I suppose I could squeeze you in.' 'You on a schedule?' They start strolling leisurely along the sidewalk. 'Midas wants to go for drinks at some club tonight.' 'Sounds like fun.' 'You wanna go?' Eva laughs, hooking her arm in his. 'Depends how long you're gonna stick around.' 'About that.' Johnny lowers his eyes. 'You don't have to explain.' Eva says seriously. 'Just. Don't do it again.' They stop outside a little ice scream stand, a balding mexican looking at them expectantly. He smiles assuringly. 'Uhm, weren't you about to go into that store back there?' Eva changes the subject. 'It can wait.' He doesn't look away from her eyes. Tuesday Morning 'Midas!' Johnny knocks on the door. 'Midas you alive in there?' Leaning against the wall in a pair of jeans and a wife beater Johnny raps his knuckles against the wood. 'C'mon man we've got things to do, I still don't have a suit for tomorrow yet.' He waits patiently, checking his watch and then knocking again. 'Midas-' The door opens and an uncertain looking Midas appears with a awkward smile and a sweaty forehead. Johnny frowns and straightens away from the wall. 'You alright?' 'I'm. I'm fine.' 'Alright.' Johnny nods slowely after a moment's hesitation. They step past each other, Johnny into the bathroom and Midas out of it. 'Listen Johnny.' 'Yeah?' 'I uh, I need the car later. Is it okay if I drop you off to get a suit and you take a cab back to the hotel?' Johnny pulls his head back surprised and waits a moment before answering. 'Sure.' The door closes and Midas wipes his brow, dialing on his cell phone as he puts some distance between himself and Johnny. He waits for an answer impatiently tapping his foot. 'Yeah hey. I can meet you tonight. Behind the club? I can do that.' He closes his phone and pauses looking at his hands. His fingers tremble. Wringing his hands together he takes a nervous seat on the edge of the hotel room bed and steadies his breath. 'Pull it together Midas.' He coaches himself. 'Just one more time. Then I'll tell him.' He looks to the bathroom door. Monday Night Shoes held in his hands Johnny tips toes in sock feet behind Lucious leaned back leisurely in his folding stool and watching a small television in his lap. He laughs quietly to himself and Johnny pauses. He settles back into his episode and Johnny opens the door; quietly slipping out into the hallway. He replaces his shoes and heads confidently for the elevators. His reflection in the brass doors parts to reveal a striking female figure with eyes the same pale peircing blue as his own. 'Eva?' Johnny asks surprised. The moment of shock subsides and Eva steps forward; bringing her hand across Johnny cheek with an audible slap. 'Asshole!' She spits with venom and swings again. Johnny catches her hand this time though, trying to spin her into his chest and hug her he gets an elbow in his ribs. Eva pushes him away and they square off in the hallway. 'You've been back for months and you didn't call.' 'What did you want me to say? 'Hey Eva it's Johnny, I'm not the crazy guy in a mask anymore, wanna hang out?'' 'Yes!' Eva looks about to slap him again but stops. 'I wanted to.' Johnny admits. 'But I couldn't.' 'Why not?' Johnny's lips curl into a sneer. 'Because I cared about you, and you betrayed me.' Now it's Eva's turn to lower her eyes. 'That was a long time ago Johnny. You were out of control, I didn't know Jordan wanted to kill you.' 'I know.' Johnny says. There's a silence hanging as Eva refuses to meet Johnny's gaze. 'I miss you.' Eva says quietly and swallows. 'Can we start again?' Slowely Eva looks up to him with a touch of disbelief. 'My name's Johnny.' He extends his hand to her. She hesitates for a long moment before settling her hand into his. 'Eva. Eva Van Lorne.' She smiles. Monday Afternoon 'Your total comes to one hundred and twenty nine ninety five.' Johnny passes his credit card over the counter. 'On credit. One moment.' The clerk, a pimpley faced youth, turns away to swipe the card through. 'Hey, thanks Lex.' Lucy says, turning the box for a wireless satellite dish over to read the back. 'Anything to make you stop complaining everytime you have to wait five minutes.' Johnny snickers. 'Three hours in a hotel hallway listening to you have sex is complaint worthy.' Lucious justifies. 'Whatever.' Johnny says distractedly looking out the storefront window at his manager. Pacing back and forth in front of the car he looks like he's argueing with somebody on his cell phone. 'Sign here please.' Johnny snaps out of his thoughts and turns to the counter, pen in hand he scribbles a signature and heads for the door. Stepping out into the sun just as Midas clicks his phone closed and props a curiously fake smile number seven. 'All done here?' 'Have to stop by the jewellery store.' 'Right.' Midas nods. 'That prawn necklace you wanted.' 'That shrimp is stinking up my bag.' Monday Morning 'I'm just tired of always having to wait for hours on end, at least if I had more than a couple channels to watch on my portable.' 'Lucy stop whining.' Johnny holds his hands up defensively. 'I'm tired of hearing it. Why don't we go find some kind of satelite dish or something.' 'See now that's thinking.' Lucy comments as they walk away from a small hole in the wall shop towards the Rolls Royce Phantom. 'So what'd she say?' 'Who?' 'The fortune teller.' 'Midas told you.' 'Well the sign on the door wasn't much of a clue.' Johnny follows Lucious' pointing finger to the advertisement poster in the window and snorts. 'She said I have a devil on my shoulder and an angel up my sleeve.' 'What's that mean?' Present Johnny kneels in his poster plastered room, surrounded by the images of his many Triangles opponents. His shirt is discarded and thrown into the corner and his breath comes in calculated measures. The golden prawn hanging against his chest rises and falls, glittering and almost glowing against his skin. So into his mediation he doesn't respond to the ripping of paper as the door opens. Eva creeps into the room, her hands sliding across Johnny's muscular shoulders and around to his chest, lips kissing his neck tenderly. 'What are you doing in here.' She asks, resting herself against his steady weight. 'I'm searching for the connection between all of these people. They're the heart and lifeblood of AWC, they're all connected to each other in one way or another.' 'There's somebody missing.' Eva comments. 'You're not up there.' 'Because I'm not connected.' He shifts off of his knees into a sitting position, looking into Eva's eyes as she slips into his lap. 'When I'm at these shows it's like I'm alone. Like I'm the poster on the wall. I don't fit in, or they don't want me to fit in.' 'You're not alone anymore.' Eva whispers. Johnny gives her a halfhearted smile. 'Why don't you go to bed, I'll be there in a minute.' Eva nods distantly before he kisses her softly and she rises from his lap, drifting out of the room. Leaning back on his hands he lets his gaze sweep around once more to the names and faces of his adversaries and opponents. Climbing to his feet he slides a peice of paper from his back pocket and stops before the single empty spot left. An inch by and inch at most, plugging the corners under other clippings he suspends a polaroid before turning to leave the room. In the picture Johnny and Eva lean back to back and make silly faces for the camera. The light clicks off.
