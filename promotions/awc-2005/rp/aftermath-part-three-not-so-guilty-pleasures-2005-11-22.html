Aftermath Part Three: Not So Guilty Pleasures...

22 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/11/05 Another loss and another truly embarrassing encounter with that asshole Chainz. What the hell is that sick freak's problem anyhow? In fact, scratch that mental note - I don’t particularly want to know who he is or what he’s done. Besides, I really couldn’t care less truth be told. I just hope that one day he gets what’s coming to him. Chainz knows that, as does everybody else unfortunate enough to have ever known or been in the company, of that animal. I guess, he just has that effect on people. And now he is the number one contender for the Relentless title after the pyramid scramble match at the AWC Triangles pay per view. How could someone like that get so far so soon? The backstage politics of the wrestling industry are complete foreign to me - there are times when I don’t think that I’ll ever understand it. And whatsmore, I somewhat comforted by that fact. However, this week on Fresh I have my shot at success. I get to fight Jack Murphy for the AWC Livewire title and I don’t intend to casually throw this opportunity away as one might a used tissue - I don’t know whether I’ll be getting another title shot anytime soon - but I know for a fact that Chainz will have something to say on the matter. It just makes his day fucking with my head every week on Fresh. This week will be no different, I'll wager. [---{|---|}---] I hurl myself to the right, with haphazard speed and direction, not really giving much thought to the inevitable bad landing that will be the direct resultant of such a rash action. Just then, I feel the bullet cut through the air, its slipstream buffering against my right thigh with cautionary grace, as I land, with a sizeable - and rather painful - thud behind a protruding metal ventilation shaft. During the impact, I hit my head pretty hard upon the concrete floor and I now feel the warm trickle of blood cascade down from the very top of my head. I resist the urge to panic and push all associated thoughts and feelings to the back of my mind, just as I had been trained to do. I need to focus on the maniac attempting to riddle my body with bullet holes, not to lose control and let my emotions take over. I hear a good number of concurrent rounds fly past me, a few panging against my cover of the metal ventilation shaft. That wouldn’t last long; a few steps closer and those bullets will inevitably cut through my place of cover as if the metal plating was nothing more than butter. I withdrew a throwing star from the small pouch strapped to my left leg. I need to buy myself time to come with a viable strategy of getting shut of this over-muscled buffoon, and cowering behind this ventilation shaft isn’t the most proactive way of dealing with this problem. I set the dial of the throwing star to a five second delay and then hurl it over the metal shaft, making sure the thing had enough hang time to cause sufficient damage. I have no idea precisely where the moron is standing, so I killing blow is out of the question for the moment, but this shot will at least incapacitate him long enough for me to come with an alternative plan of action. I wait until the star exploded in mid-air, before scuttling away to the edge of the building. As the sky clears and the smoke from my explosive device slowly dissipates into the morning air, I see Vandal reel back, a piece of shrapnel deeply embedded in his right shoulder, his face twisted in a distinct mixture of anger and pain. I smile at this and wink at the lunatic for good measure, as I drop down to the rooftop of the next building along. I know he will follow me and that is where I hold an advantage. I took out a couple of my throwing stars, set them on the detonate function and then hurl them into the side of the door leading into the building that this roof top belonged to. I then, quickly, flung myself into the air, placing both hands upon the roof of the doorway and somersaulted over the top. Upon landing, I then hide behind the doorway’s structure and wait for the big dumb lummox to fall for the traps. The first time I met Vandal, he was trying to kill a friend of mine. I may have mentioned this before, but nevertheless I got the idea there and then that although this guy was busting with steroid-induced muscle - and a latent homosexual I wouldn’t wonder - he isn’t exactly the smartest chicken in the coup. Or some similar metaphor that highlights, quite succinctly, just how much of a moron he truly is. This little trap of mine, not only plays to his lack of higher brain functions, but also plays upon his ego. It will not even strike him that a woman like me will be to out-think the great, walking lubricant advert - even considering what I did to him last time. I hear a thud about a hundred yards behind me. It can only be Vandal. He confirms my suspicions. “You might as well come out little girl,” He declared, obviously enjoying himself. “You can’t hide from your uncle Vandal.” I smile, knowing for sure that I will take great pleasure in wiping the smug grin he undoubtedly had painted across his face like a thirty foot neon sign. I remove the smile from my face and stood, my hands up in the air. I slowly walked backward, still not able to actually see the lunatic. Once again, he accommodated me in this regard, by jumping up onto the roof of the doorway construction and, as I suspected, he wore a stupidly smug grin. “Don’t hurt me,” I told him, desperately trying to fight back a smug grin of my own. I swear if this assassin gig goes wrong, my future lies upon the stage. “I give.” Vandal’s grin disappears, as he runs his left hand through the now severely bloodied flesh wound upon his right shoulder. “Don’t hurt you? But that’s the part I’m looking forward to the most!” I let the innocent helpless expression slide off my face like water from an upturned glass. I noted the confusion spread across his face, as I finger the underside of my wrist watch. The techs over at my father’s place decided that it would be the best place to hide the detonator for the explosive throwing stars. When I get back to my father’s building, I must thank them in person for their fantastic idea. I hit the button without remorse and watch, briefly, as Vandal is propelled into the air with utterly pleasing force. I coerce myself into turning my back on the instant gratification that was laid before me, and focused on the task at hand. Vandal was not the only hired gun Mr Chambers had hired to kill the target and this moron had wasted a good couple of minutes of my time. No doubt the other players in this little game are already well on the way to getting at the target. I have a lot of ground to make-up and I do not have to indulge myself, in not so guilty pleasures.
