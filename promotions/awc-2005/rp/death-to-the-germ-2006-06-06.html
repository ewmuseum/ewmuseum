Everyone was created for a sole purpose; to be confined to a situation where week in and week out we’d be placed in direct competition with one another to find out who is capable of telling a story and who isn’t. Characters and places, all written by a conglomerate of people all with experiences, fantasies, and commentaries they’d wish to share with each other. The fact of the matter is, they are brought together by a rather trivial means; the medium of wrestling. So week in and week out, we subject ourselves to this medium and learn to grow within its parameters. While interesting tales have been woven and told, potential is only realized at small increments. Every creator that places their stake has their voice, their story to tell. Whether it’s a Nicaraguan pit-fighter merely trying to make their way in an extravagant world, or a masochistic deviant with no regards to human life, or a struggling and drug-addled performer with an equal balance of addictions and lovers; everyone fits neatly into their pile. It’s like everyone wants to tell the stories they are familiar with and express no need or purpose to step outside their self-imposed boundaries. That’s understandable. Let the creators cater to their herd. Feed the same tripe. Be bound by a sense of realism. Mimic your life down to every crossed-T and dotted-I. Maintain rules that will only end up limiting the expression you can truly make. My benefactor and I, however, are coloring outside the lines. And we’re not afraid to use the wrong colors. Because while it is fun to delve into situations that so closely remind you of your own life, that is not the purpose of immersing ourselves in such literature. I’m not upset or confused or excited in the understanding of my existence. Frankly, I’m a bit indifferent. It just means that now I understand my further antics and escapades are designed by someone with my interests at heart, whether they be pain and suffering or joy and pleasure. They’ll be a story, and at the end of the day, that’s all that matters. I’m not trying to win any awards here. I’m not trying to reach any grand achievement through being. Neither is my benefactor. We’re here for the same reason you are here—to win. But through this medium comes a form of expression we’re all dying to create. Once again, our benefactors experiences, fantasies, and commentaries being expressed as a means of achieving victory. That’s where the relevancy of this monologue and the self-awareness comes to play. As a reader, you have to question it. For what purpose has Adam Dick broken the fourth wall? Why has the Illustrious Face-Eater learned that he is nothing more than words from a bored kid sitting behind a computer? Because it’s necessary to show that shattered wall to continue on with the story. Since we’ve realized that I’m nothing more than an avatar; a means for a creative person to live vicariously, why don’t we exercise the means and exploit its process? My introspective mode of thinking slowly begins to unravel as the pilot’s voice comes over the intercom once again. You’re a pathetic creation of someone who spends most of their time getting stoned and pretending he’s cooler than everyone else! You brag about cybering with an underage girl! WHO DO YOU THINK YOU ARE, KILLING ME?! I’m JEREMY J, DAMMIT!” “Dude. I’m an e-wrestler. I do whatever the fuck I want!” The bleakness of Jeremy J’s existence explodes into a bright light that encompasses the entire globe. His final action in life is screaming for Adam to fulfill one final request. “SIIIIIIIG MEEEEEEEEEEEEE.” In death, everything evil in the world becomes unraveled. This phenomenon on behalf of Jeremy’s death transforms happenings all over the globe; righting wrongs that he’d committed merely by living. Peace is restored to the Middle East. Jesus comes back but gets his ass kicked by Mohammad, who also comes back. Communism crumbles in Cuba. China and the United States become drinking buddies. Australia sinks and no one cares. Black people do something besides rap and play sports. And Joe S. becomes the successful leader of a cult he’s always wanted to start. And Adam Dick is left in an empty bathroom with a Platinum Dagger and a renewed sense of purpose. He composes himself, dusts the dirt off of his shirt, fixes his hair and begins to examine himself in the mirror. The King can’t help but feel relieved, as if he were Atlas finally absolved of his burden. “Finally, I’ll be able to sleep at night.” And so will I.
