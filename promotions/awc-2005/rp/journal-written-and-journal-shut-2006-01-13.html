Journal written and journal shut

13 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/13/05 The following is written in Suleimon's personal journal 18 December 2005 I stand by what I did last night at the Christmas party. What Red Rock said caused me great indignation, and he should never be allowed to propagate such ignorant opinions. I shan't even dignify his comments with a repeat in this journal or ever. The flight between Heathrow Airport in London and JFK Airport in New York City takes about eleven hours to complete. Funny how just as soon as he was stepping on a flight in Philadelphia International to head to Heathrow for his AWC debut, Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon was headed back to the United States. The bi-continental flavor of AWC is definitely what makes it unique, but for someone who had just signed his AWC contract, a little more stability was in order. But he was resigned to the get-up-and-go attitude of the capitalist pigs who ran the Club. Make a buck at all costs, no matter what kind of toll it would take on their wrestlers' bodies. It would only make you stronger, and in the end, it's just another obstacle he would claim he triumphed over when he gained his ultimate prize. Eleven hours would be a good time for him to relax, unwind. Even if he was the subject of one of those 'random' body searches before he got on the flight. And those London security guards... on a scale from nice to asshole, they were über-assholes. Sparing the details, the ordeal could be described in three words: body cavity search. And you thought the Americans were the only ones who were gung-ho about this War on Terror. Then again, the English wounds were more recent. Still, with innocence proven (hell, why would a celebrity member of two American wrestling promotions indulge himself in terrorist activity anyway?), he could now relax on his flight. No movie, no interaction. Just eight hours of sleep and another two or three reading some history books he had brought with him. Ottoman Empire... fascinating stuff. Suleimon was always looking to edify himself, make himself better. Tireless, he was, not only to make himself a better wrestler, but a smarter man, more aware of his past and more aware of the kind of people his forefathers were. And the more and more he read, the more fascinated he got, and the more of a romanticized image he got in his head. This was what a man should be like. Not like the boorish assholes who boarded his flight and sat with him on first class. Well, to be fair, they weren't all boorish assholes. Most of them kept quiet, kept to themselves. But there was one person on that flight who watched AWC television, who knew that Captain Suleimon was back on wrestling television after years of absence, and that he was as onery as ever when it came to the West. By some stroke of coincidence, as the Captain shut his book and placed it in his travel bag, he brushed his elbow up against the leg of that one boor, that one man who was the rabid AWC fan, who could actually afford to fly first class from London to New York. The man felt Suleimon's elbow against his thigh and then turned around, indignant that even through an accident, someone impeded his bee-line to the bathroom. Man: Hey, watch where the hell you're swinging your arms... Before Suleimon had a chance to reply, the man got a clear look at his face. Man: Oh wait, you're that whiny bitch Suleimon who can't get over what people say about him. Suleimon: Excuse me? Man: Yeah, you get your balls twisted because someone dares say something bad about you. I heard what Red Rock said to set you off, you little bitch. Why don't you grow some thicker skin and let it roll off your back. Just because people aren't pumping sunshine up the asses of Muslims or Islam or Arabs, you all have to get defensive. At this point, Suleimon was sure that this man didn't hear what Red Rock had to say. He still wouldn't repeat it; it hurt too much. But it surely didn't have to do with Islam or Arabs. Suleimon wasn't a good Muslim, and he wasn't an Arab; he was a Turk. Most learned people know that they're two different ethnicities. It would be like calling an Englishman Irish, or vice versa. You do that, and you're begging for a punch in the mouth. Man: You sandni(expletive deleted)s are all alike. At this point, many of the first class passengers are cringing. Feelings for the little brown man sitting on the aisle aside, they started genuinely feeling bad for him. The white man feeling bad for Suleimon... he certainly didn't ask for such humor from a loathesome people. Still, he kept his composure. Suleimon: Sir, I will kindly ask you to cease. I did not bother you intentionally. I don't think I deserve such treatment. Man: Oh, you don't think you deserve such treatment, eh? Bullshit. You're the one going on TV every week saying that us Americans are flabby and weak, yet you can't take it when it's said to you? Y'know what? You have a big mouth, but I bet you can't back it up. Nah, you just do that fake, twisty flying crap in the ring. I bet you can't back it up, you little pussy sandni(expletive deleted). At this point, Suleimon had two choices. He could engage the boorish asshole, cause an incident and perhaps jeopardize his employment with AWC, thus ruining his plan for bi-continental Ottoman domination in the squared circle. Or, he could ignore him, let him have his moment in the sun and at least try to enjoy the rest of his flight, even if it was at the expense of some of his pride. Wisely, Suleimon would choose the latter. If it were Red Rock, he could, seeing that he was someone of at least apparent physical equal. This guy though? Suleimon would eat him then shit him out like cheap hummus. Suleimon: Sir, this is a Transatlantic flight. If I were to accost you, I would break your jaw, and no one would be able to give you medical attention for ten hours. You'd probably contract gangrene and die because there's no bloody way we'd be able to land this plane to get you medical attention. Now, I wish not to have to demonstrate that my trade is not fake right here and right now, for it's not the time or the place. Now, please, run along and take care of your business and leave me be. Man: Leave you be? Fuck no. I wanna fight you, you big tough wrestler. I wanna kick your ass! Suleimon: You want to kick my ass why? So you can tell your buddies at home that you picked a fight with a wrestler and won? Or is it because you have a small penis and wish to overcompensate? Or maybe you drank too many beers because the slavemasters on Madison Avenue told you that a blond harlot would sleep with you if you did. Regardless of the reason, I am not going to give in. I'm not going to indulge you your wish and fight you on this flight. You Americans, you get what you want all the time because you think it's entitled to you, but it's not. I will not fight you because you feel like you can defeat me. I will not fight you at all. You can go to the bathroom and piss out whatever you have in you and sit down. Man: Pussy. He went off to the bathroom. Suleimon sat back and closed his eyes, wanting to go back to sleep. Five minutes later, the man came out of the bathroom and started making flippant comments to the Captain. For the rest of the flight. So much for rest and relaxation. But it wasn't important. Suleimon exercised amazing self-control, because nothing would jeopardize his goal of getting back at Red Rock for what he said.
