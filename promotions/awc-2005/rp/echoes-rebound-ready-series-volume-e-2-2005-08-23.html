Echoes Rebound (ReADY Series Volume E:2)

23 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kyosuke was nearing the end of his list that afternoon. There were only 4 more names that needed to be checked off, and he had promised Professor Iwamura that he’d have results for him by the end of the week. Friday was the next day, and if he didn’t produce results, it’d be the end of his creditability. His honor was everything to the morals committee president, and he wouldn’t allow it to be tainted so easily. The lone student in white waited near the edge of the playing field as the school’s girl soccer team raced back and forth across the field. He took this time to gather his notes together, but he couldn’t help but find himself interested in the scrimmage that the girls were having. Rather than enjoying the toned girls sweating and kicking out their long legs, Kyosuke actually had appreciation of the competition and the sport. He noticed the next person to be question, watching how she practiced with her teammates. This girl put her all into the game, and she loved it with every part of her body. That was a rather strange phrase, Kyosuke thought… quite perverse to love a sport with every part of your body. His consciousness came back to the field as the coach blew the whistle. Practice was over, but it was time for Kyosuke to get back to work. With his arms down by his side he stood and waited for the team to approach, the girls breathing hard and laughing. It was more than sport to them, it was game, and it was their dreams and desires. A number of girls caught glimpse of Kyosuke as he waited by the fence bordering the field, and their laughs turned to giggles as they waved and greeted him adoringly. He gave a short wave as he kept his eyes on the person he had come to see. “You want something?” Ishikawa stopped at the gate, soccer ball cradled against her chest. She was in a pair of short black shorts with a white sports bra, goalie gloves black and white as well. She was rather small for a goalie, but she only played temporarily. Her main position was center forward, and her teammates had named her “The Driver” because when she shifted into forward drive, there was no stopping her. Kyosuke pushed his glasses up on the bridge of his nose. “I do. I’ve come to ask you a few questions about Hibiki Jue, if you don’t mind.” With a shrug Ishikawa nodded, now hugging the ball with both arms. “Sure, ask away. Not like I got much to say anyways.” He was prepared to jot down notes, but halted when she said that. “Oh, so you’re saying you don’t know anything about the situation?” “Yep,” she replied, looking up at the afternoon sky blazed in orange. “Yeah, Hibiki and I didn’t quite see… eye to eye on a lot of things. We’re two different people, and that always put us at opposites. Like she would say I was a jock, and I would call her a bitch.” She tucked the ball between her knees to illustrate how those two titles clashed, two fingers drawn in towards each other only to be jerked away. “See what I mean?” Kyosuke nodded slowly. “I do. But you haven’t heard anything about the matter regarding her death, did you?” “Well usually I don’t pay much attention to the folk she hangs around with, Mr. President. They really annoy me. They’re the type that talk too much about dumb shit, and always think of themselves instead of others, and Hibiki was at the center of it all.” “I see…” that wasn’t what Kyosuke was looking for, but he had no choice on the matter. Ishikawa looked at him, and seeing the wrinkle in his brow he could tell he was disappointed in her. With a sigh she rolled her eyes and pulled the soccer ball from her knees. “Look… I know you’re trying to do something good, but sometimes you just can’t, okay? She’s dead, and that’s that. Everyone’s just gotta deal with it.” “You have a point, but I also have a duty.” “I don’t get you, Yagami.” The ball went back under her arm as she pointed at him. “You do a buncha weird shit that I don’t get, but the girls all love you for it. Not me, but everyone else. But… but if you’re gonna go ahead and do this, go see Kawada. She pretty much knows every damn thing. She’s in the library.” Kyosuke could see that in her own little stubborn way, she was trying to help; sure, she didn’t know anything, but she was pointing her in a direction of a possible resource. Coincidentally, Kawada was next on the list anyways. Giving the girl a short bow, he backed away from her slowly. “Thanks for your time.” Ishikawa watched him walk off, his white coat billowing behind him almost heroically. As he walked off, Ishikawa almost formed a smile; she could see a little bit why all the girls flocked to him. Behind her, a taller figure began to draw closer and closer… Ishikawa tossed the ball up in the air a few times as this stranger approached, and as she tossed it up for a fourth time, she suddenly flipped backwards, executing a textbook scissors kick and launching the ball at her assailant. There was a bright flash, followed by a groan of pain. Rolling backwards to her feet, Ishikawa glared down at the person, rolling the ball under her foot before kicking it up into her arms. “Damn pervert,” she muttered, walking towards the gymnasium. There was a red mark in between Jinnai’s eyes from where the soccer ball smacked him, eyes nearly crossed and mouth gaping with a gurgled moan. His camera was nearby, and if he hadn’t invested in a shockproof digital, he’d have lost a week’s worth of dirty pictures. Kyosuke walked briskly across the quad towards the library building on the other side of campus. Time was of the essence. Three more names to go… and with such limited contacts he was feeling cornered. As he neared the door on the west building, it flew open, causing him to slam his hand into the edge of it to keep from hitting him in the face. With an alarmed look, he looked down to see who was exiting as he entered. A small petite girl, with short light brown hair, and pouting lips: Miyashita Toka. “Ah, Miyashita,” Kyosuke exhaled as he relaxed. He was letting the case get to him, and that could possibly cost him in the end. Miyashita looked up, and her full lips formed a smile. “Oh sempai!” she gave a short bow to the older student as she stepped out of the door quickly. “I’m sorry, please excuse me!” Watching her polite behavior, Kyosuke couldn’t help but smile at her a little. “No, it’s my fault, my mistake. Heading home?” She nodded, smile still plastered on her face like it was carved on. “Yep! Just finished my work, now I have to rush home for dinner. See ya!” Standing in the doorway, he watched the younger student rush off, and as he turned to enter the west building, his eyebrow rose slightly. A thought ran through his head… Miyashita was carrying a large gym bag with her… … Miyashita doesn’t play any sports… He shook it off for the moment, thinking back to the task at hand. All this stress was starting to make him paranoid. He walked down the hallway and headed for the two brown double doors at the end. The library was like any other, rows of shelves stuffed with works both American and Japanese. In one corner of the room there was a sealed off smaller room where the computers were kept. From the front of the library he could see Kawada sitting at the front of the computer lab typing away with her notebook at her right side, and a few stacks of books on her left, one on top open. Ahead of him was the librarian, a slightly younger woman wearing a red blouse with a long black skirt and matching black flats. She looked up and smiled, brushing back a single strand of hair that had come out of place in her tight bun. “Ah, good afternoon, Mr. Yagami.” She was a soft-spoken woman that always refereed to everyone as Mr. or Mrs., even if you were on friendly terms with her. A librarian position was perfect for her, which is why the Professor snatched her up instantly for the beginning of the school year. With such a short time she had already gained popularity with both faculty and students, though she liked to trade the spotlight for a reading light any day. Kyosuke bowed to her and approached the desk. “Good afternoon, Miss Ozaki.” He pointed towards the lab at Kawada. “Would it be ok if I had a word with Miss Kawada please?” he said in a voice matching hers in tone and style. It was hard, but even the most guttural mouth in school found themselves speaking in ‘Mr/Ms’ style when speaking to Ozaki. She nodded with a soft smile, the beaded chain attached to her reading glasses dangling down around her neck. “By all means, dear sir. Are you hear to tutor her?” “Oh no, ma’am,” he smiled back. “I just have a few questions for her, that’s all.” “Ah, I didn’t think you would be,” she admitted, moving a stack of books from one side of her computer to the other. “She’s such a smart young lady… the two of you would make a lovely couple.” Kyosuke’s eyes widened at that remark, cheeks tinting a slight pink through his normally pale fair skin. “Uh, if you say so ma’am. Please excuse me.” he rushed off to question Kawada, though from Ozaki’s standpoint it looked like he was running away from the topic she brought up. The doorway was at the front of the computer room, and as the black carpeting reached through both the library and the lab, the walls of the lab were mother of pearl rather than the oak brown outside its walls. As soon as he stepped into the lab, Kawada’s head popped up. “Sempai.” She stood up, her earphones yanked from her ears clumsily. She tried to fix everything around neatly before bowing to Kyosuke as he stepped towards her. Kyosuke bowed back, standing across from her at the row she sat in. “Kawada,” he began, pushing a few chairs back to approach her. “I was told that you might be able to help me with something.” The mere thought of helping Kyosuke Yagami seemed out of place to Kawada, and her eyes drifted down to the ground slowly. “Me… help YOU? I don’t know… I don’t think I can, your grades are way better than mines.” He shook his head. “No, this isn’t about school. This is about Hibiki Jue.” That brought her eyes back up to meet his. “… Hibiki?” Kyosuke could see in her eyes that she indeed had something to share. “If you’d please, could you tell me everything you know about her?” Kawada glanced to the back of the room before nodding. “Well… she was in the lab sometime after school yesterday, but only for a minute. Then she rushed out after her cell phone went off.” It was a little, but a little always paved the way for a lot. “Did she? Could you tell me which computer she was using?” She glanced to the back of the room again, this time pointing to the terminal on the far left side of the room 5 rows behind her. “That one.” Kyosuke glided back to it and slid into the seat, moving the mouse to kill the screensaver. Kawada followed, standing by his side as she watched him bring up the Internet browser. “Checking the history?” she asked. He nodded as the list came up on the side of the browser. There were dozens of dated folders, it being a public computer that the students used frequently. His eyes scanned the list until he found the date that Hibiki was killed. Clicking it open, he found a dozen more files; porn sites, fashion pages, email providers, the basic things that teens would search for on the Internet. One thing caught his eye though. There was one site on the list… as BBS… the mouse hovered over the link and clicked. Kawada watched as a black screen loaded. And that’s all it was… black. Kyosuke clicked the screen all over, but couldn’t find anything. Defeated, he sighed, slumping over onto his elbows and his head hanging down. Kawada stood there, unsure of what to do in the matter. “Damn it,” he uttered under his breath. “I lost…” He felt a hand go on his shoulder, and he looked up over the rim of his glasses at Kawada. She gave him a warm smile as she tried to comfort him. “Yagami… don’t beat yourself up over things,” she said gently. “We’re only human… we can’t do everything, and we can’t always win. Life’s full of ups and downs, and we just have to ride them out.” Kyosuke watched as she pushed her black-rimmed glasses up the bridge of her nose. On instinct he did the same with his own, and through glass lenses they stared at each other silently. But the startling knock broke through their trance and caused them both to jump. Kyosuke and Kawada looked up to find Chibi standing on the other side of the glass window. -=-=-=-=- “I didn’t know what to think… it was like a dream, really.” Kawada watched from a close distance as Chibi stood in front of the much taller Kyosuke dressed in a pair of blue jeans and short-sleeved white t-shirt. They stood in the center aisle of the lab, which was the most private place in the school at that time of day. Chibi’s eyes seemed to see only the floor, unable to look up into the stern face of Kyosuke. “So, you know something, do you?” Kyosuke asked, slipping his hands into his pockets. “Out with it, Shiganori.” His real name was Shiganori Hyotaru, but as was evident, everyone called him Chibi because he was pretty much the shortest and youngest looking of them all. He was a 2nd year student, but he looked like he was still in junior high. His size and youth made him a prime target for bullying and teasing, so he mostly kept on the move to avoid everyone. Chibi began to look up slowly, but only reached Kyosuke’s chest when he replied. “I… I think I know who killed Hibiki.” Kawada’s eyes widened at the sound of such news, but Kyosuke kept himself stone-faced. This was a huge breakthrough, and if it was valid, Kyosuke would have succeeded. He kept his joy inside, disposition like a wall of granite as he nodded. “Who then?” -=-=-=-=- It was the scream that motivated Chibi to run for his life, but it was his curiosity that caused him to stop. What was that? What just happened? Did something serious actually go on… or was it just a bunch of kids screwing around? Either way, Chibi decided to muster whatever courage he had and turn around… and climb right up into the tree nearby. Once up in a high branch, the youngster could see from a safe, hidden vantage point whoever was about to leave the factory. His lungs were in his throat as anticipation built up. The gates were pushed open by a pair of black gloves darkened by what looked like a wet substance. The tall thin figure closed the gate behind him, standing there looking around. He fixed the collar of his black leather jacket, and walked off with his back turned towards Chibi. His eyes caught on to something as he walked off, and Chibi’s breath was taken away. His arms began to quiver, lips parting slowly in disbelief. He couldn’t believe what he had just seen before him… those gloves were soaked in blood, red drops falling to the sidewalk as the killer walked off. And as he walked off, Chibi knew just exactly who it was. And the name echoed in his mind the same way it would in a few hours from then. -=-=-=-=- “Kirima Nagi?!” Kawada was shocked at the name Chibi announced. Kyosuke stood there with his arms folded, eyes closed pondering the tale told to him. Kawada walked over to Chibi and grabbed him by the shoulders stiffly. “Are you saying there’s been a killer sitting with us all day and you didn’t say anything?!” Chibi looked away from Kawada’s frantic look. “I didn’t know what to think… I… I don’t know what to think anymore. At one point she’s my savior… and another she’s a murderer… I can’t tell if she’s an angel or a devil anymore…” “What are you talking about??” Kawada shook him a little to snap some sense back into him. “You saw what happened! She walked out of the factory with blood on her hands! She killed Hibiki!” Just as those words came out her mouth the lab door closed. Kawada looked back, finding Kyosuke gone, exited out the back door of the lab. She rushed over to the large glass pane to see him rush through the double doors of the library and disappearing into the hallway. “Where can he go?” she whispered to herself. “Can he find Nagi?” -=-=-=-=- Nagi sat hunched over on the edge of a fountain placed off to the side of the area. The area was a popular one for commuters, walking to the shops or the train stations or on their way home from school. It wasn’t common for a fountain to be set off to the side, park areas were always reserved and kept from the day-to-day clutter if people treaded through it on a daily basis, then it’d grow dingy and worn like a pair of new shoes under constant pressure. Sitting by her side was her pair of black gloves, laid out to dry. Every now and then a small drop of water would lose its grip and fall from one of the fingers of the glove. Scratching her head, she glanced up slightly. Kyosuke watched her look up, and the two of them locked eyes for a moment. The shorthaired girl stared at the perfect student… while he kept eye on the juvenile delinquent. Finally he had walked up the stairs to a point where their sights were cut off. He looked ahead, paying it no mind as he stepped into the center square. Kawaii was waiting for him in the soda shop, dressed in a short skimpy red skirt with matching red and white flip flops and a white sleeveless scoop neck top with a red flower in her hair. Now that she was out of school, she could let the makeup fly, caked on eyeshadow and lipstick like white frosting on her honey tanned skin. Kyosuke sat in front of her rather patiently as she rambled on about everything while drinking her soda… completely forgetting the reason they were there in the beginning. “So like I was saying those boots totally didn’t go with the outfit I had, ya know, so I had to walk all the way back to the train station to take them back to the shop for a full refund! Then I went to this other shop over in Shinjuku and got a pair of sandals, a velour tube top, and a yellow wrap skirt for the SAME PRICE! Can you BELIEVE IT!!?” Break time; Kawaii sipped on her soda as Kyosuke enjoyed the moment of silence. It was apparent though that those relaxing moments were short and sweet, because Kawaii would flow right back into speed with her incessant talking. Kyosuke would have cut her off to get the answers he wanted, but he figured that she’d come to speed at some point in the conversation and remember why they were there. But Kawaii was carrying on like this was an actual date instead of an interview session. “So I picked this piece up yesterday afternoon so I’d look good for today. Do you like it? Here, take another look!” Before Kyosuke could object Kawaii stood up and started to pose for him right there next to the table. Lowering his head embarrassed, he muttered something about the outfit being ok before she would sit down grinning like a bleached blonde bimbo. Kyosuke looked up with tired eyes at her. “Kawaii… do you remember by any chance the reason why we’re here?” Sipping on her straw, she nodded with a smile. “Of course!” Giving a sigh of relief, he sat up and pulled out his pen. “Good, now let’s get down to it.” “Okay,” she pulled out her cell phone and started to press a few buttons. “I’ve got plans this weekend, but I say we can go out again the weekend after, and during the week we can probably study together and get a bite to eat afterwards. Then at the end of the month I was thinking, maybe we can spend the day in Shinjuku, hit the shops, catch a few movies, and play some games before going to the club and maybe a bit of fun afterwards, eh HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!” Kyosuke stared at her with dead eyes as the girl laughed clueless. Of course, clueless was something that described her perfectly at times. Lowering his pen to his notepad, he raised an eyebrow and looked at her. “Kawaii… I don’t want to date you.” That put the brakes on everything right then and there. She looked up at him, smile still on her face rather oblivious to what he just said. Her mind registered it completely though, even though her expression would take some time to shift. “You… you don’t?” He shook his head. “You’re not my type, sorry to say. But I’m sure there’s plenty of guys that would want to date a girl like you.” “Then… then why’d you make this date with me?” He held up the notepad and pen to remind her. “Investigating the death of Hibiki… remember?” Finally her face caught on to what her brain already knew, the white-lipped smile dropping from her face and eyes lowering to the table. “So… so you don’t really like me then?” Kyosuke knew where this was going right away. “No, no, no… I think you’re a fine girl… just not my type, you know? Don’t start crying and make a scene, okay?” But he was too late in asking that, her ducts were already filled to the brim and ready to pour. Her head lowered and her hands were there to catch the tears and muffle the sobs from the girl. Kyosuke looked around before standing up and reaching over to pat her on the shoulder comfortingly. “Hey… hey stop crying… I did go out with you this once… you can still brag about that to your friends.” People were starting to glance in their direction, and so he decided to pull out the big guns. “Hey… how about you tell your friends we did date, but YOU broke up with ME? How’s that sound?” Her sobs died down to a little sniffle, face still covered with hands. “Uhm… good?” she asked weakly behind her hands. “Good, good, that way it seems like you’re better than me, because you dumped me. And everyone’s happy… okay?” She finally grew silent for a moment, and the looks left the two students. Then Kawaii looked up with a big smile, black mascara streaking down the white makeup layered around her eyes. “Okay!” Kyosuke reeled back seeing the ghastly sight of her runny makeup, and sat down slowly. “Good, now… do you know anything about Hibiki?” She nodded, reaching into her Gucci purse to grab her makeup mirror. “Yep! I know something BIG!” She fixed her face a bit, dabbing away at the black tear streaks until her face was decent again… if you could say so with the amount of makeup she had on her face. Then she placed the compact in her purse and motioned for Kyosuke to lean in close. When he did she looked around to make sure no one was listening, and then whispered her big news. “I… I think Hibiki tried to hit on me…” ] There was a vein pulsing in Kyosuke’s temple as he walked back down the stairs he walked up a half hour ago. That would definitely be something he’d try and forget for the days to come. He should’ve thought that she wouldn’t know anything relevant, but Kyosuke didn’t think about it at the moment. Maybe he was slipping… maybe he might actually be getting dumber? He looked off to the side, finding Kirima Nagi still sitting in the same spot she was when he passed by. This time she was lounged out across the edge of the fountain, her long legs crossed one over the other with her arms folded behind her head. Sometimes Kyosuke wished he had the time to lounge around like that, but there was always something he had to do to ensure his future. Surprisingly, she walked right past him. She went right over and picked up her gloves, slapping them against her thigh before slipping on the left. She bit down on the edge of the can, holding it as she slipped on the right glove. She made sure both gloves were snug before grabbing her soda and taking another swig. Blood started to leak out from her now uninhabited body, trailing almost directly for Nagi.
