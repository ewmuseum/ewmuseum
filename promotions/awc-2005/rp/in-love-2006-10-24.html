In Love

24 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 24, 2006 INT - OPERATING ROOM (BIRD’S EYE VIEW) A garbed up DOCTOR and his NURSES scramble across the floor desperately grabbing tools and the like off of counters and barking orders and updates back and forth to each other. The point of their obsession? A woman in labor, sprawled out on the table in the center of the ring. Though she’s not screaming in uncontrollable pain, like you would normally see on the Discovery Channel. She’s unconscious, her breathing assisted by an apparatus strapped to her face. Her face shows signs of bruising, and a large, deep cut on her cheek. MEDIUM ANGLE, moving back and forth between the Doctor and the Nurses, not dissimilar to your average medical drama. NURSE #1 Her vitals are dropping! DOCTOR Alright, if were going to save the child, we need to give her C-Section. NURSE #2 If we do it now, we can save the child. I don’t know about the mother. DOCTOR What’s the chance for both of them to make it? NURSE #1 30% at best. NURSE #2 If we wait to long, we’ll lose them both! DOCTOR Alright, lets proceed. A NEW ANGLE of the Doctor and Nurses. They begin to pass sharp objects back and forth between each other. The eyes of the staff members show a furious will to save the child, but a deep sorrow for the almost inevitable fate of the mother. FADE OUT In the darkness, you hear the healthy screams of a baby and the flat line of a heart monitor blaring in concert. INT. - BACK OF A LIMOSINE (MOVING)- NIGHT DATELINE: OCTOBER 14, 2006 A brand new, unbelievably clean Bently Limo sits parked in an inconspicuous lot near the Garden District of New Orleans. In the seat behind the driver, an stunning black woman, LADY HARKEN, dressed in silks and couldn’t be much older than forty. Her beauty is suddenly interrupted by an ugly scar on her right cheek. Across from her, the always well dressed pro-wrestler and TV personality B.O.T.. Between them sits a black duffle bag full of the reason he was here. SHOT FAVORING B.O.T., Lady Harken is off screen, Marcellus Wallace-style. LADY HARKEN I’m not going to bother to count it. I’m sure a busy man like you wouldn’t fly all the way from Leon, Spain to New Orleans, Louisiana just to try some movie hero bullshit and stiff me. B.O.T., visibly exhausted and anxious, reaches into his blazer for a cigar. LADY HARKEN Not like it would matter, I could find you anyway. B.O.T. You got a light? LADY HARKEN Those things will kill you, you know. B.O.T. Is that a no? B.O.T.’s eyes follow off screen movement cautiously. Harken’s hand appears in the shot holding a lighter. She flicks it like a pro, and B.O.T. leans in to light his smoke, never letting his eyes leave her. LADY HARKEN She told you not to take your eye’s off me, right? She retracts the lighter as B.O.T. welcomes a drag into his lungs. LADY HARKEN Of course she did. She’s a cop, and I’m a menace, right? Trust me, if this were a normal delivery of a normal half a million dollars from some normal two bit loser who got himself in too deep, it might behoove you to watch my every move. It wouldn’t save you, but it would behoove you. But your not a two bit loser, and this is far from normal. For me, and for you, I’m sure. Truth is, I don’t have it in me to hurt you. Not now, when it took me so long to find you. B.O.T. Can I go now? LADY HARKEN Why? Aren’t you at all interested in why I wanted to see you in person, alone? B.O.T. I’m just interested in getting back to the life I’ve been so very lacking of thanks to you. LADY HARKEN And what life is that? The one your trying to patch the void in your soul with? The fact that you think that hiding your broken heart behind Armani suits and your soul behind Ray-Ban sunglasses is a most disheartening thing. B.O.T. So says the crime lord wearing a fine African silk dress who owns the limo I’m sitting in. LADY HARKEN I wear these things to express myself. I know where my heart is. B.O.T. And where is that? LADY HARKEN Right in front of me… Son… B.O.T. pauses. B.O.T. Excuse me? LADY HARKEN You heard me. I’m your mother. B.O.T. My mother died days after she gave birth to me. LADY HARKEN Do I look dead? B.O.T. This is over. Take your money, and leave me alone. He reaches for the door. LADY HARKEN Hear me out, before you storm out of here, out of my life again. He leans back into the seat. LADY HARKEN Jarrett… heh. Its hard to call you that… You’ll always be Banastre to me. B.O.T. (annoyed) And why’s that? LADY HARKEN That was the name I gave you when I had you. Your father changed it years later in hopes to save you. B.O.T. Save me from what? LADY HARKEN From me. DISSOLVE TO EXT. TOP OF A TALL BUILDING IN MOSCOW - DAY AUGUST 1971 A younger Lady Harken, wrapped in what seems to be a costume that would make an animal lover cry, is laying over a ledge, a long, menacing sniper rifle pointed down at some unfortunate soul who someone has deemed too important to be alive… NEW ANGLE Of Harken looking down over the ledge at a POLITICIAN standing out of the sun roof of his limousine, waving to a parade of on lookers. She checks and readies the weapon as the voice over speaks. LADY HARKEN (V/O) I wasn’t always a mob boss. I started out as an assassin, killing in the name of whomever paid me the most. I was heartless, cruel, calculated, but above all… CLOSE SHOT Of Harken’s gloved finger squeezing the cold steel trigger. BANG! MOVING SHOT Following the slug in slow motion, as it sails to deliver a message of a sudden death to the Politican’s cranium. FADE TO BLACK You hear the screams of wives, husbands, sons, daughters, supporters, and naysayer, all trying to make sense of what has happened. LADY HARKEN (V/O) …I was good. FADE INTO THE BACKSEAT OF THE LIMO - NIGHT LADY HARKEN Your father, Barbossa, was the mob boss, not myself. He was running operations all over New England. He wanted to expand into New York, and most especially North Jersey. So he called me to help him. CUT TO A FULL SCREEN MAP OF THE NORTH EASTERN UNITED STATES A red arrow flies from out of Vermont, and into New Jersey. LADY HARKEN (V/O) Together we took over the North Jersey crime world. Fell in love in the process. CUT TO A STILL PHOTO OF DIMITRI Younger, sans the dreadlocks and stingy attitude. LADY HARKEN (V/O) By the time I was pregnant with you, he had already had a three year old son, your half brother Dimitri, from a previous marriage. INT. A SUBURBAN HOUSE - DAY In the den of a well-to-do house stands the unassuming mafia lord, BARBOSSA CLEMANS, looking at a small photo of his son. In the other hand, a large Winchester Rifle, sporting an axe blade at the end of the barrel. NEW ANGLE On Barbossa, who is standing over the brutally beaten body of a pregnant Lady Harken… LADY HARKEN (V/O) “I’ve already got a son.” he said to me. “I don’t need another.” I couldn’t belive he would dare hurt me. Dare hurt you. It was from then on, myself and Barbossa severed ties as lovers and partners, and built new ones as arch enemies. CUT TO: MAY 1973 INT - OPERATING ROOM (BIRD’S EYE VIEW) A garbed up DOCTOR and his NURSES scramble across the floor desperately grabbing tools and the like off of counters and barking orders and updates back and forth to each other. The point of their obsession? A woman in labor, sprawled out on the table in the center of the ring. Though she’s not screaming in uncontrollable pain, like you would normally see on the Discovery Channel. She’s unconscious, her breathing assisted by an apparatus strapped to her face. Her face shows signs of bruising, and a large, deep cut on her cheek. LADY HARKEN (V/O) I was rushed to the hospital, and was presumed that I would die soon after I had given birth to you. A NEW ANGLE of the Doctor and Nurses. They begin to pass sharp objects back and forth between each other. The eyes of the staff members show a furious will to save the child, but a deep sorrow for the almost inevitable fate of the mother. FADE OUT LADY HARKEN (V/O) It was close, but I made it. And was I woke up from my coma, I realized that he had already come to receive you, claiming you as his son. He disappeared shortly after. INT. - BACK OF A LIMOSINE - NIGHT CLOSE SHOT A tear begins to form in her eye. LADY HARKEN (slowly, struggling with the words) I’ve killed hundreds of men before I met your father, but I never wanted someone’s blood on my hands so bad in my life. I can still feel his hands, like bricks they smashed against my face. The cold steel of his bayonet carved my face up like a thanksgiving feast. But worse still, he had taken something from me ill never be able to heal, my pride. That’s when I created the Society, and vowed to take everything he ever had up from under his nose. Every territory, every gang. All mine, or all dead. I found out he changed your name to Jarrett after Barbossa threw himself off a building in 1990 after the despair my ambitions put him in. He was dead, and you and your brother were left alone. Dimitri was given custody over you, but he needed a job to sustain you both. I’m not sure why, maybe it was out of guilt for leaving him and my son fatherless, but I let him work for me, as part of the Society. ANGLE FAVORING B.O.T. Who is having a hard time grasping all the information. B.O.T. My brother worked a supermarket job and a laid tile to help us get by! There’s no way he had the time to do your bidding. LADY HARKEN Is that what he told you? You remember him coming home carrying wads of cash, telling you not to ask how he got it, right? I’m sure you found it, you’re an intelligent man. And I’m sure you were curious as to how, but never asked, because it made ends meet. You never asked why he would be gone for days before you’d see him again, either. These coincidences make sense all of the sudden, yes? B.O.T. leans back slowly, visually trying not to believe it. LADY HARKEN Don’t struggle too hard, there’s more. DISSOLVE TO EXT - ANOTHER ALLEY IN NEW ORLEANS - NIGHT Just as shady as the alley B.O.T. and Harken sit, discussing the past, this one is as foreboding. In it stand DIMITRI, arms crossed next to SASHENKA, both rather unimpressed by the three men across from them; BASS, the muscle, GLUM, the charisma, and ZEIK, the brains, who are holding them “against” their will. They stand in front of the car that brought them there. DIMITRI So is this your idea of a kidnapping? GLUM I wouldn’t call it a ‘kidnapping.’ I’d like to say… eh.. Er… Zeik ZEIK Yea? GLUM What do you call it when you take a jury away from civilization, to escape the peer pressure and other such junk? ZEIK Sequestered… GLUM Right, were sequestering you. SASHENKA What are you going to do to us? BASS Don’t worry about that, Cop. You’ll know when I tell you. Dimitri shoots a concerned look at Sashenka quickly, the word ‘cop’ the trigger. SASHENKA So you know? GLUM Of course we know. ZEIK Wasn’t that hard to find out. SASHENKA So then you know what I’m here for? GLUM Sure… To catch The Boss. SASHENKA Then you know there’s a legion of S.W.A.T. on their way to case the Garden District as we speak. GLUM That’s why you’re here, away from the action. So that if something like that does go down, we have permission to kill the both of you. BASS The Boss wont go down without a fight. SASHENKA (Insitantly) She is a criminal! DIMITRI Wait, you’re a cop? SASHENKA (Abruptly) Federal Agent, to be exact. I’ve been watching over you, your brother, and The Society for a long time. I’ve stayed up too many sleepless nights, pushing pencils at some dreary desk, setting up contacts and planning dates… All for tonight… Sashenka reaches behind her back, and draws a Browning automatic. She swings it back and forth between the men, jabbing it in each of their faces, hoping to establish dominance by pure fear. The Society members jump at the sight of the fire arm, as if they’ve never seen gun before. Infact, the shriek of a small child squeaks out of the mighty Bass. Dimitri puts his hands up as well, trying to remain calm. Zeik turns to Bass. ZEIK …Did you just scream? SASHENKA Everybody freeze, and I won’t be forced to shoot anyone. DIMITRI Be cool, Sash. SASHENKA You back up, Deetch! Your no more innocent than those bastards. Dimitri withdraws, as a crooked smile is born on Sashenka’s face. SASHENKA Everybody in the car! Your taking me to Harken now. GLUM Listen, I was just joking about the killing you part… She takes a few aggressive steps in Glum’s direction, swinging the gun as her negotiator. SASHENKA Shut up and get in the car! ZEIK We, can’t… SASHENKA Fine, Ill shoot you and take the car myself. BASS You don’t understand. We don’t know where she is. ZEIK She didn’t tell us… GLUM …Because we didn’t need to know. SASHENKA …You guys are useless. Give me the keys, Ill find her myself… GLUM Look, I know your in a… Sashenka clubs Glum with the butt of the pistol, and he falls like a sack of bricks. She sticks a knee in his chest, and smashes his cheek in with the gun. Glum peers up at her as she frisks him, eventually pulling the car keys out of his cheap, black slacks. She tosses them over to Dimitri, who catches them cautiously. He gives her a look as if to say “What now?” SASHENKA Get in. Your driving. DIMITRI Listen, I’m sure they just want to talk alone. There’s no crimes being committed tonight. Sashenka thrusts the gun in his direction, not needing to say a word. DIMITRI Fine, fine… He looks over to the Society. DIMITRI Your pathetic, each and everyone one of you. ZEIK (Sarcastically) That cuts deep… CUT TO: INT. - BACK OF A LIMOSINE - NIGHT POV SHOT As B.O.T. glances at Harken. REVERSE ANGLE Harken looks at him, taking a long drag of his cigar. The smoke curls like a sprawling ghost out of a crack in the window next to him. B.O.T. (slowly) This is… LADY HARKEN Hard to belive, I know… B.O.T. I was going to go with absolutely absurd, actually. LADY HARKEN What, you don’t think it could even possibly be true? B.O.T. Your story is ripped out of some of my favorate crime dramas… LADY HARKEN (argumentatively) Oh, and your life as you live it now isn’t something fit for some novel in the fiction section of a public library? Think of it this way… NEW ANGLE Of the two of them, sitting still and comfortable across from each other. LADY HARKEN Every time you’ve ever been heart broken, be it from a floozy you fell hard for, or a wrestling match that didn’t go your way, you’ve found yourself consumed by the need to exact vengance. You place blame on everyone else but yourself, and justify the unnecessary consequences they almost always endure in your head as them just deserving it. Right? B.O.T. looks away, almost ashamed of the truth. LADY HARKEN Don’t be ashamed, my son. It’s in our nature. People write novels and scripts, creating people like us from their greatest dreams, thinking that its all fiction, never knowing that we exist right above their glass ceiling. When people take from you, you take it back with interest. Your father tried to take my life, and tried to take you out of it. And look how he ended up before he finally killed himself. A drunk, disheveled shell of the powerful organized crime overlord he used to be. Its our lives to crush those who harm us. Its in our blood, Banastre. B.O.T. It’s Jarrett, and this doesn’t explain why I owe you money. LADY HARKEN Your father stole it from me. Before we had our falling out, we kept a separate account with two million dollars in it incase we had to skip the country for whatever reason. When I woke out of the coma he put me in, you weren’t the only thing he stole from me. When I found out he had been hiding out in Paterson, NJ, he had already spent half of it, you were fourteen when I finally found out. After your father died, instead of taking the money back, I instructed Dimitri to use it to make something of yourselves. The money would end up going to your college tuition and you and your brother’s wrestling school training. B.O.T. How can all this happen right under my nose? LADY HARKEN Talent, id say… They sit in a thick fog of silence, B.O.T. rubs the leather seats, unsure of what to say next. Harken is on the verge of tears, overcome with the joy of being able to sit and chat with the son she never got to raise. LADY HARKEN I wish he was here… I wish he was here right now., so that he could see this. Me, sitting and venting to the son he tried to take away from me! B.O.T. Why did you bring me here? LADY HARKEN What do you mean? B.O.T. It wasn’t just to tell me my life story, take my money from me. Maybe they were side dishes, but what was the entre’? LADY HARKEN Well, the truth is, I’m tired. I’ve been living a life of violence, greed, murder, and defiance my entire life. I’ve seen and done things to and for people that the civilized folk I pretend to be wouldn’t understand. I just wanted to have the only thing I haven’t had before I call it quits, and dissappear into the cloud of human selfishness and grizzly sadism that I was born out of. Sitting across from you gives me butterflies. The Notorious B.O.T., my son…You know, I wasn’t sure what to wear? I actually was nervous about what you would make of me. I was hoping you would be proud. B.O.T. (coldly) Well, if your done reveling, here’s your money. Let me go back to my life. Harken smirks, obviously hurt by his heartlessness. She dries a tear that’s welling in her eyes. LADY HARKEN Yes… you can go… B.O.T. immediately makes for the door. He’s halfway out before Harken leans forward to stop him. LADY HARKEN Bana… Jarrett… He stops in his tracks. NEW ANGLE FAVORING HARKEN LADY HARKEN I spent my whole life hating and fighting. It was my life, the only thing I lived for. It was my heart and soul, my moon and sky. Then I got older, and when it stopped being as gratifying, I realized that it was all I was. I couldn’t give it up, because I would have nothing less. That’s why I bothered you. Ripped you out of your world, selfishly hoping you could be a pillar for me to stand on in this empty, lonely life ahead. Apparently, I was wrong… If there’s anything I can say to you to make this trip worth it is this: Don’t let yourself end up like me. Find something your heart can really love. Something you can dedicate your life to and know that twenty years in, you’ll feel the way. And something that can love you back just the same. CUT TO CLOSE SHOT OF B.O.T. Who takes a second to absorb her words, then continues out into the frigid emptiness of the night. FADE TO BLACK You hear B.O.T.’s voice over a black screen. B.O.T. (V/O) (steadily) Thing about it is, Doc. When I left the place that night. I was cool. It hadn’t really sank in yet, I guess. But then I got back home, after talkin it over Deetch, who confirmed everything, I realized just what I big piece of my life was missing. I started to feel bad for the way I treated her that night… Started to miss her alittle… FADE IN INT. DR. SPARKS OFFICE- DAY DATELINE: OCTOBER 20th, 2006 EMILY SPARKS, B.O.T.’s personal psychiatrist, sits comfortably next to him, as he is sprawled out on the long leather couch, reminiscing. The rest of the office is accented in mahogany. Dark stained shelves hold mighty, leather-bound books. The window in the corner overlooks the city of Dallas. Sparks scribbles down important ideas and notes on his note pad as B.O.T. speaks. DR. SPARKS Its not that uncommon to feel that way, especially when you’ve finnaly found someone you think you can relate to. B.O.T. Yea, I guess… Its just that… I’m not going to get another opportunity to tell her all that… I wish I wasn’t so stubborn when people come at me like that. She probably thinks I hate her. DR. SPARKS She’s probably disappointed, but I’m sure she knows you still love her. B.O.T. That’s the thing, I don’t love her… I can’t love her. I don’t even know her. DR. SPARKS You sure its not because your afraid of what loving someone who actually loves you back might feel? B.O.T. What kind of sense does that make? DR. SPARKS Well, so far, you’ve got a history of falling for things that unrequited the sentiment. Tia Morrello died out of burning hatred of you because you broke her heart without even knowing it. You still wanted to help her fall back in love with you, while she just wanted a knife to fall into your sternum. And Sashenka Spence, who had been using your sexual attraction to her and the memories of your old love to try to nail a case on your estranged mother. Speaking of which, how did she take the news that you let her go without waiting for her? QUICK CUT EXT. DARK ALLEY - NIGHT On the screen flashes a small clip of Sashenka winding back, and sucker punching him in the face… INT. DR, SPARKS’ OFFICE - DAY B.O.T. She didn’t appreciate it much… Who really cares, though? I’m over her. DR. SPARKS Well, what about your love for wrestling? Sure, you’re a hell of a fighter, allegedly, but its seemed to taken more from you than its given back. Your health, your love life, your sainity. And for what? You said it yourself once, “You don’t run the business, the business runs you.” Your giving up the best part of being a human being just to be a big, muscly drone that can hurt people. You should be enjoying… B.O.T. That’s enough, Doc… Your supposed to be helping me… instead you want to preach that tired bullshit that everyone else throws at me. Wrestling is what I do, dammit. It is my health, my love life, and my sanity. DR. SPARKS Listen, I’m just here to give you some advice. And in my professional opinion, I think its time for you to find something else to do. ANGLE ON B.O.T.’s face, as he looks around, like a student who just got scolded by the teacher… B.O.T. Pssh… were done here… FADE TO BLACK In the darkness, after a short pause, you hear a clicking sound, steady and fast. Deep slow breaths start to get louder, clearer. DISSOLVE INTO INT. BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING - DAY OVER THE SHOULDER SHOT Of B.O.T. staring at himself in a full wall mirror as he jumps rope. The sweat on his tank top, and the puddle near his feet lead us to believe he’s been at if for a couple hours. His eyes are closed, chin down, deep in thought. DATELINE: OCTOBER 22nd, 2006 B.O.T.(V/O) I had my routine down pact. Wake up at five A.M., drive from my place in Moorestown to this dump in Trenton, jog 3 miles, warm up, stretch, lift, spar, rest, repeat. Every time I’m near home I follow it like the Commandments. On the road, I do the best I can… NEW ANGLE ON B.O.T. Another shot of him jumping rope. B.O.T.(V/O) The students ask me why I’m always here so early. I try not to be rude when I say its because of them. I want to be alone. Alone to thing, breathe, be… Time to balance myself before the chaotic cloud of stupidity washed over everything around me, and suddenly I would be dragged back down into the abyss of me having to pretend I’m better than everyone. Here, early in the morning, I’m my only competition. INSIDE THE RING B.O.T. stretches out, the sunlight breaking through to skylights like a spotlight. B.O.T.(V/O) I had been thinking about what Sparks, Deetch, Sash, Ai, and Harken had been saying to me over the past couple of weeks; finally, now that I wasn’t being chased by mob weirdoes, I had a little time to take in to consideration their worry for me. They’ve all been saying the same thing, in their own special ways. B.O.T. begins to sprint and bounce off of the ropes. Back and forth he goes, like an arrow from a bow as he is fired from one side of the squared circle to the other. B.O.T. (V/O) They were right. I hadn’t been the same. I used to be able to say that after I was done wrestling, I would be spending my time in some hotel, throwing some unnecessarily expensive party to celebrate the good mood I was in. The fun in my life was just that, to have fun. Its been awhile since I’ve been in the mood to celebrate for little reason. IN THE WEIGHT ROOM B.O.T. is squatting an unholy amount of weight on his massive back. His face shows signs of struggle as he fights to stand after each and every rep. B.O.T.(V/O) I think they all see that the game ain’t what it used to be, and that I’m nearing the end of my prime. And for a second, I thought that too… CUT TO B.O.T. leg pressing an equally ridiculous amount of weight, sweat beads, like raindrops, storming down his body, drenching every surface around him. B.O.T.(V/O) Then talking to my mother made me realize what it is I am. A guy who overcomes. There’s no reason what I young kid without a father from the ghetto should both go through college and get a business degree. Nor do the odds say same said child should take that degree, and use the knowledge it proved he had to own and franchise a successful sporting goods chain. Never, still, should said hoodlum ever get launched into wrestling superstardom, and be a worldwide figured head of the sport. Like my mothers gangster life, that shit only happens in movies, right? BACK IN THE RING B.O.T. is wrapping his hands and feet in gauze. B.O.T.(V/O) I’m the sort of person that overcomes. The kind of guy who shits on your logic and your odds. The sort of guy who can’t be held down the by the shackles of skepticism. The guy who, just when you think you have all the answers, changes the questions. BIRD’S EYE Of B.O.T. shadowboxing in the center of the ring. B.O.T.(V/O) I’ve found myself with a lot to prove lately, and very little time to do so. The people that think that the wrestling business, MY business, is taking its toll on me has been seriously mislead. Because I’m my recent turmoil, instead of being turned away from it for something more “rewarding,” I’ve fallen deeper into it. Because the truth is, this is my only love, one that I’ve consummated with my blood and tears time and time again. And it loves me just as much, rewarding my devotion with fame, fortune, and good luck. So what, I’ve lost a couple matches in my newest project, but Rome wasn’t built in a day. The odds tell me that when im up against a pretty good team with a tag partner I don’t like, my chances are nil. I think with that known its time to live up to my potential. A phone rings off screen. NEAR THE OFFICE B.O.T. jogs over to his gym bag, and pulls out his blaring cell phone quickly. B.O.T. (impatiently) What? …Who wants to know?… …Mr. Kae... How'd you get this number?… ...humph... Indeed. What can I do for you?… B.O.T. places a had on his hip, trying to stay interested in what the man on the other side of the phone was trying to tell him. B.O.T. Sir, for the record, were not even in the same book. But for this particular outing, ill pretend we are at least in the same library. Ill do my thing, You’ll sit back and watch, like that last puppet I was paired up with. He sighs at the response he gets. B.O.T. You’ve never had King Prawn then, have you Mr. Kae? He scoffs… B.O.T. I need you to understand something, friend. I don’t 'screw up.' There isn’t a better athlete to walk in that AWC ring then me, and ill bet my riches on it. Almasy and Lexicon are as good as beaten, as long as you know how to watch my back. I can't trust anyone involved from with the federation, and most importantly a person who takes the time to find my number and call me about them. Prove me wrong. A smile curves onto his face B.O.T. Your talking to a multiple time tag team champion, won all over the world. I know how to be 'coherent.' I’ve got a bit of an impact I still need to make, anyway, as a couple of issues have gotten in the way. Trust me, you happen to have the Unstoppable Force in your corner. A mediocre showing from you, at best, will seal the deal. Dial tone. B.O.T. looks down at the phone and shakes his head arrogantly B.O.T. Stupid prick… FADE OUT.
