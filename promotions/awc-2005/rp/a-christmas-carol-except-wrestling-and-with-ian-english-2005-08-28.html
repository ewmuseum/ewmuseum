It was time, Mike felt, for a change. So Mike in an extremely out of character move helped his rival Irishman Paddy O shea and saved him from an attack from Hate and his minions. Mikes eyes begin to droop as he falls into a little slumber. The Ghost of Wrestlings Past Mike awakes from his slumber and finds himself alone in the hotel room. Ian: Well first of all I think you're the muppet. That was a film way before the fackin muppets made it. One of the best writers of all time wrote that one. He made Mike trip over the bottom rope, be laughed at, pinned cleanly by an unworthy challenger and then have the commentators ruin his Company Killer 'gimmick' by saying 'So much for the Company Killer' as he was pinned by Yawnn.
