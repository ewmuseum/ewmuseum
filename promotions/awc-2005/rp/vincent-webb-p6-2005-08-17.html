Date: 8/17/05 The next morning, I awaken from my bed, relieved to get a night’s rest at home. I force myself out of bed and into the kitchen, pouring myself some homemade coffee to wake me up. I yawn as I look at the clock. Twelve o’clock noon. I haven’t slept this late since I was a young teenager. I tidy up to go see Steven, taking a well needed shower and combing my hair Moments later, I find myself sitting near Steven’s hospital bed, his eyes barely open wide but appear as though they are staring out into space. “Steven…” I say, subtly. He turns towards me. “Tell me who these people are… I’m confused. What is going on?” That’s the point where everything I thought about Steven was turned upside down. Steven Xandrous It was bound to happen. “So you want to know? Fine. Here it goes…” The skies bleed bloodlust red, the sun scorching its colors of orange and yellow. The night slowly begins to overwhelm the daytime, along with it the light. The roads, if you can call it a road, clutter with brown-red dirt, being tossed into the air as easily as a feather. The wheels of the incoming vehicle turn quickly, not showing any signs of stopping. Its driver remorsefully speeds up as the man in the seat behind him lays motionless, sitting next to yet another man, grinning, his black leather vest, showing the effects of the sun's rays which powerfully shine from the pinnacle of the sky. His black sunglasses show the mark of a dangerous man; the glasses are pitch black and spit clean, setting with perfect ease on his nose. His died-blonde hair waves from the wind blowing in from the opened window beside him. The man to his right is not as lucky. His throat bares a long, thin slash, blood pouring from within to the outside. His eyes are blackened from the blows of fists. His own fists are bloody and scraped, signs of a backlash from them. His hands are duct taped behind his back to prevent any further rebellion from the man; pointless, as the man is unconscious, as he has been for thirty minutes. The man’s eyelids flicker, and the man in the front spots it and points it out to his possible co-worker, who slaps the man hard on the face. The men grin, no remorse within their souls. The driver slowly begins to slow the vehicle. The vehicle comes to a full halt and the men step out of the vehicle, leaving their victim to lie within, showing no signs of awakening. The men look at their surroundings, and the driver speaks with a German accent, “Ist dies weit genug?” “Is this far enough?” His accomplice raises his sunglasses, revealing blue eyes. Of course, the blue was created through the contact lenses that wrap around his iris. He grins and nods. “Warum würde jemand hier herauskommen?” “Why would anyone come out here?” The driver nods in agreement. He nods his head toward the victim still inside the vehicle. He takes a deep breath and advances to the victim’s door. He opens it, and grabs the man by the extremely torn white shirt on his body and throws him out of the car, tossing him into the dirt, causing dirt to fly up around him. The two men step toward him, the blonde haired man delivers a crushing blow the victim’s sternum. A gasp of air escapes his body, satisfying the blonde man more. The driver looks away, grimacing at the furiousness of the scene. The blonde man then walks over the driver grinning, and playfully ruffles his hair. “Es musste, mein Freund gemacht werden. Ich kann Leute wie dieser Lauf um frei nicht lassen. Es ist gefährlich für mich.” “It had to be done, my friend. I can't let people like this run around freely. It is dangerous for me.” The man smiles. “Lassen Sie uns gehen nach Hause.” “Let us go home.” “Aussehen, es ist nicht gefährlich zu mir. Aber die Polizei kann dies hinter zu MIR verfolgen! Und ich werde das ein sein, das beschuldigt werden wird, nicht Sie,” the man shouts angrily. “Look, it is not dangerous to me. But the police can trace this back to ME! And I will be the one who will be accused, not you.” “Macht Sie je reden zurück zu mir Sie, Stück der Scheiße nicht. Erhalten Sie im Auto jetzt gleich, oder ich werde Sie auch töten,” the blonde haired man says in an angry tone. “Don't you ever talk back to me you, piece of shit. Get in the car right now, or I will kill you too.” He shoves the man to his door, and forces him inside of it. The blonde haired man steps into the passenger side. The car’s ignition starts again as the attackers drive away from the scene. As the many hours pass, the night fully takes over the sky, darkening it to an unimaginable extent. The victim stirs, his eyelids flutter open. His eyes stay open wearily, but his body is immovable. The man’s thoughts race. Oh my God… Where am I? What am I doing here? What the Hell is going on…? The man begins to shake in fear, his eyes watering with emotional pain. His questions grow, along with his fear. The man’s thoughts are then blocked by one single idea. I have to get out of here… His shakily body raises onto his buttocks. He rips a bit of the duct tape, and he then tears at it until his hands are free from their cage. He feels something on his neck, and realizes that there is a lethal cut, and instinctively tears strip of his shirt off, wrapping it around his neck to slow the loss of blood. Then realizing his actions, he shakes more. He gets to his feet, looking around at his environment. He holds his arm, and then feeling a strange awkwardness in his shoulder. Again instinctively, he pushes his arm upward. A crack is heard, one that sounds like popping of bone. The awkwardness is gone. He inhales deeply. A sharp stabbing pain is in his lungs. The man begins to walk, his thoughts clouded with blackness. He stumbles about five steps before collapsing, falling onto his shoulder hard. He pulls himself to his feet, and he leans over. The man vomits onto the dirt. He stands kneeled over, vomit flowing from his throat. He then passes out once more, falling into his own vomit, his shirt now soaked. After five minutes past, the man wills himself to his feet. He walks slowly, barely able to move. His legs move, however, but take much of the man’s energy. As the man advances, his vision blurs to full blackness. Suddenly, an image goes into his mind. He sees a man, seemingly dead, lying in a dark alley. The man is stripped of all his clothes, and his body is nearly completely covered in blood. His face is pale, his eyes, barely open, are completely absent of life. Suddenly, all he sees is the man’s face. His mouth mumbles two words, but the words are silent. A brilliant flash of light occurs and the man sees his surrounding. Shaking at his vision, he mumbles the words, “Oh my God…” Days pass, the man is dehydrated and barely able to walk, but refusing to quit, he wills on. On the fifth night of his weakened crusade, it begins to rain. The added weight brings the man to his feet, knocking his head on the ground slightly, but it is enough to put him into unconsciousness. He lays there for at least two hours. The rain has halted, but the darkness of the night remains. Suddenly a ray of light cracks through the darkness, and then the darkness around is then overwhelmed; the sound of squeaking tires frightens the man. He sticks one arm in the air in a poor attempt to stop the blow, but luckily the oncoming vehicle stops short of his limp body. Gasping for air on his hands and knees, he tries to pull himself up using the front of the vehicle, but slips and falls backwards onto his back. He hears the driver’s door pop open, but he cannot see anything because he closed his eyes to conserve a little bit of energy. He feels himself being pulled to his feet, but cannot bring himself to open his eyes. He hears another car door opening, and then feels the warmth of a toasty car, and he feels… something that he somehow knows to be leather. He lies back, shaking from the cold. He hears blurs of voices, but his racing mind cannot but the blurs together. The man with no identity blacks out for a few moments until the car begins to speed off. The man wills himself up and he sees that some is driving him somewhere. Where he is going, he does not have a clue. Who is taking him there is an even larger question. The man wearily pulls himself up to sit. The man who is driving the vehicle turns around at the sound of the leather squeaking, and sees the man with no identity awaken. He pulls the car to a halt, and looks at the man. The driver’s features are more visible. He has on large, thick glasses, and his white hair is mostly gone. His face has wrinkles from age. His voice cracks when he speaks the words, “Son, are you alright?” The man without an identity cannot respond with words, so he simply shakes his head side to side: “no.” The driver, upon seeing the identity-less man’s features, such as his bleeding throat and blackened eyes, mumbles the words that have been repeatedly during the memory-ridden man’s ‘life,’ “Oh my God!” He puts his foot to the gas, and begins to driver off but his passenger hoarsely says, “Wait… Pull over, please…” “But son, you’re life is slipping away! We need to get you to a hospital right now!” the driver says urgently in an effort to save the man. Suddenly, the man sees another flash of light, and all he sees is the vision. The nude man lying dead comes to mind, but he sees the man’s features more vividly. The man’s eyes are brightly colored blue. His face has two scars, and two blackened eyes. His bloodied body goes limp, as he sees yet another flash, and he is back in the seemingly unreal world of reality. He sees the aged driver once more. His voice more stern and deep, he repeats the sentence. “Pull over, right now.” “I cannot do that. Just please wait! We are only ten minutes away from the hospital,” the driver says. The victim mumbles incoherently and seemingly goes off to sleep. The driver continues to drive. Moments later, the driver’s eyes are lifeless. The victim pulled a knife from his pocket, coming from seemingly nowhere. Confused, he looks at it. He knows that it is a dangerous weapon, but he also knows that he could be in a dangerous situation. Acting upon his instincts, he takes the knife and gouges it into the unsuspecting man’s throat, quickly putting him into death. As the car begins to swerve, he takes his limp hands, puts them on the wheel, and steadies the car until it rolls to a halt. He gets out of the vehicle and walks to the front of the car, opening the driver’s door. He tosses the corpse out, and then begins to go inside of the vehicle, when he feels wet chunks in what is left of his shirt. He takes the shirt off, tosses it onto the corpse, and steps inside. He puts his foot to the gas and drives forward. He then looks in the rear view mirror, seeing himself for the first time. What he sees is the thing he least expects. In the mirror, he sees the man from his vision. The bloodied and nude man he saw in the vision was himself. The man uses all his psychological energy to focus on driving, ignoring what he saw in the mirror. The memory-ridden man drives on. For a while, all the man can see is darkness; he is unable to see where he is driving or what he is doing. Suddenly, lights flash all around him. He quickly hits the breaks and the car squeals to a stop. He finds himself breathing heavily, and he looks up and to his right, looking for the source of the light. For some reason, he finds himself being told to leave his car. He does not hear anything, but it is in his mind. He does so, leaving the door wide open. His visions blur, but he walks forward. He rests his right hand on the hood of the vehicle he has obtained. He looks at his hand, seeing something that seemed… out of place. It was red, and spreading. Blood. He did not know how the blood got to his hands, but he did not care. He began to walk forward, but then out of nowhere, a huge surge of white light flashes before him and he feels as though he is being thrown into the eye of the storm. His legs seemingly collapse from below him. His head still spinning, he forces his eyes open. He looks up, seeing a metallic-colored roofing. He looks to his side and he sees someone who he has assumed to be a “doctor”. The man attempts to pull himself up to sit in a normal position. One of these “doctors” hear the man grimacing as he tried to use his energy, as he inadvertently makes a groaning sound. “Sir,” the woman doctor to his left says, “Please, you need your rest.” The man attempts to respond, but finds it to difficult to do so. So instead, the man lies still, waiting for his opportunity. He waits for his opportunity to strike… The doctors talk, but all their talk is fizzed out by the man’s thoughts. The man finally discovers that he is not lying still at all; whatever he is laying on is moving. “Ambulance”, he realizes. The “ambulance” comes to a halt, as the sensation he was feeling stops. He feels a similar sensation, however, but this time he feels what he is sitting directly on top of is moving. He then sees the doctors moving the, what he guesses to be, stretcher, which he is laying on. He sees the environment around him warp and that he is being moved somewhere, but once again he does not know where. The man then realizes that now is the time for his move. He somehow jumps to his feet while on the stretcher, the doctors, dismayed by the action, release their hold on the stretcher. The identity-less man leaps towards the ambulance, grasping its roof. He pulls himself on top of it, and the doctors, still dumbstruck, begin to yell towards the would-be patient. The man runs forward, leaping off the hood of the ambulance and he grasps another ledge, this one much more rugged and dirtier than the roof of the ambulance. He pulls himself up and he feels the “whoosh” of wind coming from quick moving mass. He sees bunches of cars going opposite ways and takes a deep breath. He runs across the highway, making sure to dodge the oncoming vehicles. He reaches the other side of the road that bears a sidewalk, and begins to follow the sidewalk. He begins to run in an attempt to escape the “doctors” that had picked him up from wherever he laid. Another flash of light enters his vision. He sees various images, not like his other visions. He then clearly sees a street sign that reads “Lexington Rd.” He sees a shaky image of a little boy. The little boy is wearing just jean shorts, and his hair is very black. He hears voices which he can vaguely piece together to hear “Mommy, what is going on?” The vision plays as though it was a movie. He then sees a woman, a suitcase in hand. Her face is bruised and red, her eyes mimicking. “I’m getting out of here, Steven. Please, come with Mommy.” “No! I want to stay here with daddy and all my toys!” the little boy yells. The woman shrieks, and then the “view” turns to a man, a large shotgun in his hands. “Woman, get your ass in the bedroom, right now!” The woman yells and begins sob, frightened by the man. The man yells, “NOW!” The woman grabs her suitcase and launches it at the man. The suitcase connects and the shotgun fires. The vision ends, and the man is back in reality. He finds himself on his knees, which are now scraped from the rocky surface of the sidewalk. The man lets out a vicious roar, and people walking on the sidewalk turn to look at the man. The man gets to his feet and begins to run at full speed, ignoring those in front of him. Some how his mind tells him to look to the left, as he does. He sees a sign that reads “Lexington Rd.” Confused and frightened, he runs towards the sign. He follows the direction that the vision went, wondering where it would lead him. He sees the same house that he saw in his vision in the scattering of images. The house is made of bricks and is crafted very nicely. The house looks old, but yet new at the same time. Timidly he walks up the wooden steps to the porch of the house. He hears sirens roaring from behind him and quickly charges into the house. He ducks down under the window, out of view. He lays there in the corner, facing away from the window and into the room, when he notices a man lying on a couch sitting only a few feet away from him. He saw the same man that he had witnessed in the vision. He then sees what that man is holding. A shotgun, and it is the same shotgun that he witnessed in the vision. As he sneaks forward, the features of the man become more vivid until he can see every wrinkle in the man’s face. The man is wearing a wife beater tank top and jean shorts. Next to the couch are numerous empty beer cans, and a remote for the television that was left on. The man with no identity or recollection reaches forward slowly, and then in one quick, subtle movement, he grabs the shotgun and points it at the man. The man awakes with a start, wondering what had just happened. As he begins to lean forward, he sees the barrel of his own shotgun aimed right at his head. The sleeping man says quietly, “Steven… I thought that you were dead…” “Shut up!” the man called Steven says, surprised at his loud tone of, and the actual existent of, voice. “Who the hell are you?!” The man replies, “Steven, I’m your own goddamned father! You know exactly who I am!” “BULLSHIT! What did you do, damn it?! What did you do to that woman and that child!?” the man called Steven yells, his mind racing. The sleeping man has a confused and worried look on his face. “Steven, what is going on here? Are you all right? My God, what have they done to you?” “You had a shotgun…” the man called Steven says in a near whisper. “The woman was trying to leave, trying to go somewhere, but you pointed a shotgun—THIS shotgun at her… it was this fucking shotgun… What did you do damn it?! TELL ME RIGHT NOW! TELL ME NOW OR I WILL SHOOT YOUR FUCKING HEAD OF RIGHT NOW!” “Son, son! Calm down!” the other man says. “Just put the gun down and we’ll talk…” “FUCK YOU!” the man named Steven says as he pulls the trigger fiercely. The bullet within the gun rockets out at full speed, shattering the sleeping man’s skull. What is left of the man’s corpse drops onto the sofa. The man called Steven throws the shotgun down, confused at what he had done. For some reason he felt anger for the man. For some reason he wanted to kill that man… and for some reason, he doesn’t know why he didn’t do it earlier. His mind racing further, the man called Steven slumps backward but is caught by a small, wooden coffee table. As the man slumps into a temporary motionlessness, sound waves enter into the house, echoing and bouncing off the walls. The man is alarmed by the sound. It is the sound of authority, a constant whistle of a siren. He grabs a large cup of near-full water and pours it all into his mouth excluding the small bit that dripped out and onto his now bare chest. He gets to his feet, stepping over what is left of his “father’s” body. He reaches into the man’s pockets and feels a piece of leather and pulls it out, revealing of wallet. He opens it, flipping through the multiple dollar bills, all worth ten United States dollars or higher. He stuffs the wallet into his own pocket and walks swiftly into a nearby hallway, walking over its decorative carpet, brushing his hand on the wood of the walls. So many memories come back to him now, remembering things that he can’t know if they really exist, or had existed before the incident that left him without any recollection. He strides forward and at the end of the hallway, he turns right. He steps into what he knows to be a bathroom or restroom. In the corner of the room placed about an inch above a mirror, which is setting above the sink, is a window. The man instantly and instinctively climbs on tone of the toilet, then onto the sink, and opens the window wide enough for him to crawl out. He grasps the grass of the backyard and pulls his body mass out of the household and into the backyard environment. Five police officers, all of which are baring guns that are pointed at the house’s front porch. He spots two police cars and sees that the lights are flashing and the car itself is empty. He sighs with relief and then takes off running, slightly energized from the water he managed to get. As he runs, the noise of the sirens lessen, along with his energy. His will to “win”, however, remains high, along with his animosity. After multiple minutes of running, he slows to a fast walk. He wipes the sweat from his forehead and brow, tired from the events of the day. The night soon regains control, overthrowing the tyrant of light. He soon passes a large sign that reads, “Welcome to St. Louis”. The man stares at the sign, emotions and memories flowing rapidly and fluently. Shortly thereafter, the man finds himself in the middle of a grassy knoll, vacant of people due to the lateness of the night. However, four men, all dressed in shady clothes such as hooded sweatshirts and jeans, notice the man walking all alone in the middle of the night. They chuckle and walk up to the man with a devious plot in mind. One of the men, probably the undeclared leader, shouts to him. “Hey, what you doing out so late? Isn’t it past your bedtime?” he says, laughing. The man does nothing but stare as the others get closer. “What’s wrong, sunshine? Did you have an argument with your boyfriend or something?” The group laughs. “Look, just give us your wallet and there will be no trouble. Understand?” The man then speaks two words that ignite the fire. “Fuck you.” The leader shakes his head, and lunges forward with a forearm, knocking he who is called Steven backward. Two other men catch him as their leader continues with punches, hitting about three or four to his head. I did not do anything, and, Hell, they can but me through a lie-detector test and we’ll see the real truth.” Kevin’s eyes flare up in a rage as the words flow from Steven’s mouth. Police officers enter, fearing a war. “Sir,” the police officer says, “Do you want to put Mister Xandrous through a lie-detector test?” Kevin nods grimly. “Get this S.O.B locked up for good, you hear me?” The police officers do not respond as they lead Kevin out of the room. Moments later, they bring in a machine, hooking Steven up to it. Steven looks at it disapprovingly, but he is simply ignored. One of the officers read Steven questions. “Is your name Steven Xandrous?” the man asks, testing the machine. Steven grimaces. “I do not know,” he says truthfully, staring at the officer who is watching the machine intently. Hearing the answer, the officer looks at Steven, confused. He then sees the sincere face that is staring back at him. He looks at the machine once more. The officer assumes it is just a mere quirk in the device. “Are you twenty-eight years of age?” the officer asks again, this time his focus is directly on Steven’s face. Steven repeats in the same tone, “I do not know.” The officer looks back at the device. The officer holds up three fingers. “Do I have three fingers pointed up?” Still in the same tone, he says, “Yes.” The officer looks at the machine again, astounded. “Stay right there,” the officer says, walking out of the room quickly. He returns shortly with another officer. The officer takes a look at the machine and then at Steven. “Son,” the officer says, “Are you Steven Xandrous?” Steven sighs. “Sir, I do not know.” The officer glances at the machine, also astounded. The two officers walk out into the hallway and exchange words. Shortly after, they walk back in and unplug the machine, setting it off to the side. The new officer speaks first. “Do you know who you are?” “No,” Steven says simply. “Do you know where you are?” he asks. “A jail,” Steven says. “What town?” the man asks. “No, I don’t know,” Steven answers. The man leans forward in an attempt to intensify the situation. He, in a near-whispering voice, says, “You know that if you are caught to be lying that you will be sentenced to many years to prison. You got that?” “I understand those consequences, but I am not speaking lies,” Steven says in a stern voice of finality. The officer leans back, “Alright then. Follow us and we are going to take you to an empty cell. You’re staying here for the night,” the police officer says. “I can’t,” Steven says laughing. “And why is that? Is they something you want to tell us, son?” the officer says. “Yeah,” Steven says, smiling, “I’m handcuffed to the damn chair, remember?” Hours, many hours, later, Steven lies awake, staring at the ceiling. The shattered memories haunt him and they proceed to eat away at his soul as the time goes on. Finally, unable to take it, he stands up and walks over to the cell bars. He stares as a new officer that he has not seen before opens the bars. “You’re lucky, kid,” the officer says. “Not only do they not have any dirt on you, but they are declaring that you might have amnesia. They are going to let you go, but you are to come back here within one week, to see how you are doing. Got that?” Steven looks at him incredulously, nods, and walks on out of the station confusingly harmlessly. “What the hell?” Steven says aloud to himself. Looking around at the environment laid around him, his mind is warped. His vision is impeded and what seem to him as photographs flow fluently, vividly through his mind. He sees what he believes to be a relative of his, as they bare the near-same facial features that are on his. He sees them looking at him, their face helpless and their eyes watery. His hands are handcuffed behind his back as he is shoved on forward and into the building. Steven clutches his head in anguish, trying to get it out of his head. As quickly as it appeared, the visions disappeared. He leans over, finding himself breathing heavy. He walks on in confusion, the light of the sun leading him. Leading him into somewhere he does not know and to somewhere he may never know. Home.
