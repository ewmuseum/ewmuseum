I was feelin' near as faded as my jeans...

10 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/11 six days'till the day of my birth Didn't I make you feel like you were the only man? Yeah! Didn't I give you everything that a woman possibly can? Honey, you know I did! Each time I tell myself that I think I've had enough... Well I'm gonna show ya, baby, that a woman can be tough. ''Is your name Jane?'' The moment Darcy saw the redhead, he knew things were about to get bloody. He shot up from his seat, glaring at Jane and pleading with Amy at the same time, ''Amy, d--'' Oh man, are YOU about to be confused... ''Back the fuck up, Markson.'' She meant business, using only his surname... something Amy NEVER did, given the fact that she constantly complained about last names being too impersonal. Darcy honestly would have felt less pressure if he'd been handed a pair of wire cutters and all of three seconds to snip the right wire to prevent East Bay from becoming a fallout zone. ''Amy, PLEASE,'' he said, placing both of his hands firmly on her shoulders. Unfortunately, he ran out of bright ideas right there. She violently pulled away and wagged a finger in his face, and then pointing at Jane ''I swear to God, touch me again trying to be all friendly and 'it's nothing, baby!' and I'll break your fingers in sequence while I stomp straight through HER.'' He briefly considered his options. Could Jane pass for a sister? Distant cousin perhaps? 'No way... Amy's met my only sister, and Jane doesn't look anything like me.' An insurance salesman, perhaps? He chuckled at the absurdity of trying that one on his white-hot tempered girlfriend. It was worth the effort, but Darcy knew his only option was the truth... no matter what he tried to put by her, Amy would stop at nothing to find out what was really going on. ''Amy...'' he finally stammered. ''This is Jane. My ex-girlfriend. Until today I hadn't seen her in eight years, and I was really hoping that was going to be the last time.'' ''In about a half second, you won't have to worry about seeing her ever again...'' Amy lunged at the wide-eyed brunette on the couch, but had the wind knocked out of her when Darcy intercepted by wrapping both arms tightly around the little redhead. She would've kicked him between the legs, but she was clamped too tightly into his chest and relented only for a moment, because she knew when she was beaten. ''Get her out,'' she said with a cracking voice, shaking rather violently in Markson's arms, ''Now. I... I won't say it twice.'' Darcy looked over at Jane, who now looked increasingly alarmed by the situation at hand. ''I'm sorry, Darcy....this is all my fault. I'll get out of here.'' True to her nature of never wanting to impose, Jane not only quickly darted for the door to take her leave before things imploded, but she also gathered the nearby tissues she'd used to dry her eyes into her purse. With her hand on the doorknob she took one last look at her former lover, who had a vice-like grip on the predator aching to be released. In that brief second she and Darcy exchanged a knowing glance... one of desperation, one of fright, longing and one that could only be shared by two people with over a decade of history together. With no further words, she exited. And that's when the fun park really opened up for business. Amy immediately slapped him - no punching, a slap - when she was free of his embrace. She sighed and, still shaking, stated that she was going to calm down before 'the fight starts'. With that, she looked at Darcy not in a way that she'd need to know him ten years to understand, but with complete and utter disappointment. It was a look he remembered from Brazil, where he watched her heart break twice over in a simple second. Before he could say anything, she disappeared into the bedroom they shared. Of course, they had no need for locks, so the door was easy prey for Darcy if he chose to follow her. Instead, though, he decided that a moment to clear his OWN head was in order. He knew Amy would understand, she'd just gotten territorial and angry, knowing what Jane had done to him previously. She'd still be pissed when she emerged in a second or two, but it wouldn't go past that. At least, that's what he thought. Really, he couldn't have been more wrong. A full fifteen minutes passed before Darcy got really concerned for Amy's well-being, having been in his own head for at least ten of those minutes before realizing that she hadn't made a sound. He hadn't heard any crashing, wall-punching or angry screaming. It wasn't like Aimz not to come back for a fight, and that's what piqued Darcy's concern the most. He entered the room that he heard muffled sobs echoing from. He slowly leaned in to see Amy quickly mute herself, but with her face buried into a pillow in a pitch-black room, Darcy couldn't imagine what else she might be pretending to do. ''Ames....I'm so sorry. I swear to God it wasn't what it looked like. She burst in here sobbing, insisting that she needed my help. I was just about to turn her out on her ass when you showed up.'' Her response was the one that always indicated the monster welling up inside her, the dreaded ''I'm fine.'' 'I'm fine' meant she was a danger to herself. Darcy sensed a frailty in her, the insecurity she usually hid so well was shining through with an undeniable ferocity. The problem wasn't so much one of territory, but Amy honestly feeling like she was absolutely no competition to a woman much different and seemingly more together than her, especially one who had more history with Darcy than she could dream of. Darcy, however, didn't quite grasp that idea yet. The woman in front of him never ceased to be confusing as all hell, but he felt himself in a state of slight panic seeing her so fragile... especially while she was pretending not to be. It was strange, but he wanted her to be angry in that moment. He wanted her to rage and scream and punch him in the groin - whatever it took to help her deal with the sudden appearance of an old face. He couldn't take her being so veiled, so... broken. ''Amy, please...'' Darcy answered, sounding almost hurt. ''I know you're not. And believe me, I want you to be fine. There's nothing to get worked up over, I swear it.'' Amy squeezed her eyes shut and sat up in the dark, trying to be nonchalant about wiping her tears, ''She can't show up, not at this stage of the game. She can't... it's too strong...'' 'How do you think I feel?' He almost offered it as an overt reply, but thought better of it. 'The last thing she needs to know is that she does still have an effect on me.' ''I know. I honestly never thought I'd see her again. But she means NOTHING to me, Amy, Not a thing. Whatsoever.'' The little redhead didn't feel like caring anymore, it was too tiring... but she couldn't help it. Her head fell to one side, ''I'm dumb enough to ignore the look in your eyes that begged for escape when I proposed,'' she groaned, ''But I'm not letting this one go. Something was pulling your strings when I walked in, and I know you didn't want me to see it... but I'm not quite as self-centered and oblivious as you've been led to believe.'' He wasn't sure whether or not he was imagining or actually feeling the lump in his chest. But when those words caused the weight of the lump to suddenly double, the mystery was solved. ''I-I wrote her off, Amy,'' he stammered, having a much more difficult time hiding the emotion in his voice. ''She pulled my strings because this is the first I've so much as heard from her since I was a teenager, and to boot she needs me. Believe me, you have no idea how hard I wanted to slam the door in her face... but I couldn't just turn her away.'' ''You could have. You didn't want to.'' Darcy blinked slowly. What a wonderful day this turned out to be... ''I couldn't. She needed help, and for some reason the powers that be have decided that I, of COURSE, have to be the one to help her. I couldn't be any less thrilled about this. I can't turn my back, Amy. As much as you want me to, and as much as I'd beg to do, I can't do it. But there is NO reason this has to come between you and I. Can you please understand that?'' Amy shook her head, ''I'll give you a warning, and I'll give it right now. If you're gonna fuck her for old time's sake, don't lie to me about it.'' Before Darcy could speak, she continued, ''And for her sake, make sure that was the first and last time I'll we'll ever meet. In your worst nightmares, you couldn't dream up what I'd do to her with anything less than a German tank separating us.'' Darcy again closed his eyes, and a deep sigh escaped his lips. ''We didn't fuck. We're not GOING to fuck. And honestly, if we were, I'd at least have had the decency not to do it where you live. Now please... the longer you're worked up over this, and the longer we're talking about it, then the longer this is going to be a big deal, which it shouldn't be at all. Can you please just let me handle this? I swear to you, on my life, that you have nothing to worry about.'' ''If you break it this time...'' she began. Amy stammered, knowing that her coming words would be unusually ominous... ''...there's no way I'll wait another five years.'' MIDNIGHT PENMANSHIP: Dearest piece of paper... He's my territory. I'm even jealous of his male friends, and I know that I shouldn't be this way... but every second without him is my loss. I hate to lose, and hate even more to dumb everything between us down to easy labels and possession. I need to find out if there's some kind of happy medium between universally accurate love songs and a straight-up Lil' Jon track that objectifies and lessens the people - mainly women - around me to make them appear less intimidating by sticking a label like 'bitch' or 'ho' on their foreheads. If those labels are true, then the real love that the guy on the next song in my iPod is singing about really doesn't exist. If those labels aren't at all real, though, it means that every human interaction is meaningful in some way. If women aren't bitches and hoes, then Jane is an important person. But the idea of her being anything more than a dirty word doesn't fly with me, so where's the middle ground? It's hypocritical of me to whine when some drunken asshole calls me a slut or a bitch, then turn around and throw that label onto any girl I catch looking at Darcy or someone close to me. Of course, recognition isn't solution and simply knowing that what I'm doing is wrong doesn't mean that every inch of me will stop being inclined toward that behavior. I don't understand the extremes of feminism, but at the same time I find myself constantly simplifying everybody else's actions or reactions to stupid fucking labels that make me feel like they're less than me, if not only because I can 'pin' them to a name like whore or prick or druggie or stalker. In order to serve myself, I act in ways that disgust me and I think that's what's so confusing right now. How can I hate my born-in reactions to things, but feel so driven to act that way because it's the one way that feels right? I don't trust doctors, so this new sketchpad is my only way of organizing thought in a way I can systematically look back at and understand. Hopefully, this ends up helping me see exactly what I'm really thinking and feeling, despite what I trick myself to believe. ...But, if nothing else, it'll be fun to go back tomorrow night before bed and try to read between the lines of a written rant that I probably won't be able to remember anyway. This inferiority complex is gonna need some strong medication. FIGHT, PART DEUX: Feelin' good was easy when he sang the blues... Amy woke up happy the next morning, enjoying the sensation of being beside the number one man in her life. He was as handsome and readily loving as ever, so it was no surprise that he hopped onto her once she rolled to her back. ''Reeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeaper...'' she teased the stately, handsome young man, ''Where'd daddy go?'' ''Trying to figure out what to make you for an 'I'm sorry' breakfast,'' came a voice that most certainly didn't belong to the feline, ''And wondering why the first thing you do on a Saturday morning is talk to the cat.'' When Darcy appeared in the doorway, the furry little man that was sharing a bed with Amy scurried out, right between Markson's legs... most likely on the way to a mouse hole or litterbox. The sleepy redhead that remained just smile sheepishly at him. He was apologetic for the fact that he hadn't just slammed the door when his ex paid a visit the afternoon before, and moreso apologizing for the fact that he hadn't let Amy get a shot or two in before he booted Jane out. It was then that Amy noticed she didn't quite feel up to forgetting the day before, as much as she'd wanted to. ''The cat doesn't check out old pussy when I go for coffee, I like starting my day with something fresh and interested.'' ''I assume you're talking about me, even though it isn't true. And besides, I can't help who randomly decides to show up out of the blue after having absolutely no contact for half a decade. Can we please drop this so I can finish making it up to you?'' ''You're damn lucky I've been caught in trying to 'better myself as a person' and all of that yogazentaoist coffee table book crap, or I'd be pretty unreasonable right now. Y'know, like I was all last night... before I exhausted myself yelling and fell asleep with a balled fist.'' Amy writhed under the covers contentedly before continuing, ''Hey... know what'd make all of that crap up to me?'' ''What's that?'' Darcy inquired. But as the words escaped his lips, he could already tell, unfortunately a few seconds too late, that he'd walked into a trap. Even though the plan was obvious, the tiny redhead took a moment before she revealed her once more obsessive proposal, ''You. Me. Vegas.'' Given Amy's hatred of gambling that spawned from her father's constant abuse in childhood, and even more redundant borrowing of money in his daughter's more successful wrestling years, it was obvious that she wasn't asking to head to Nevada for the slot machines. Fuck. ''Please tell me you only want to go because you're a closet Celine Dion fan and you're dying to catch her show,'' Darcy said after an exasperated sigh. ''I'll still hate you for it, but PLEASE tell me that's why you want to go to Vegas.'' ''Never think so little of me again. Celine Dion is a disgrace to my native land. Though it WOULD be nice to see what wig she's wearing this week... I'm thinking you and I should have a date with Elvis. That is, unless you're still terrified by the thought of eternity with me.'' ''Petrified. And IF I ever get over that...and believe me, that's a big IF...I'm not tying the knot in a chapel that has an optional drive-thru lane.'' ''... Well, at least you've still got taste.'' Amy grunted, again rolling under the protection of the bed's fluffy white sheets, ''But I have a real question, are we okay? As in, this Jane shit doesn't weaken us... right?'' Darcy moved into the room, joining her on his side of the bed. ''It's going to take some getting used to to have her back in my life again. But believe me, she torched that bridge ages ago, and I'd nuke the hell out of the bridgesite altogether if anybody so much as thought about trying to rebuild it. I still hate Jane... and I still love you. I hope that's good enough for you, because it's God's honest truth.'' The redhead smirked at his oath, half-hiding her intentions by pulling the sheet up over her mouth, ''Maybe we should get you away from an address she'd be able to find for a few days after the event this week. It's pretty warm up home... maybe I can show you the beaches where I'm from?'' She expected little, having just proposed a trip to Canada to a Californian... but it didn't hurt to try. They both needed to clear their heads a little, and move slightly under the radar - Darcy with his ex after his time, and Amy with Michael Sloan after her hide. It couldn't hurt to get away. Darcy frowned. ''I'd love to, Amy. But there's just one eensy-weensy problem...'' ''Please don't tell me you invited her back...'' ''Not exactly. And definitely not voluntarily. But she's got some men with bad intentions looking for her, and she's scared half to death. I can't say I blame her, either. Believe me, she won't be hanging around at all if I can help it, but she wants me to keep an eye on her, just in case shit hits the fan. And somehow I'm finding myself obligated.'' Amy's first response was a cold stare. ''... Could you please leave the room, Darcy? It'd be best to do it quickly, and not come back in.'' Darcy threw up his hands. This didn't sit well with his girlfriend for obvious reasons, and it called for some serious damage control. ''See? This is why I didn't want you involved. She's trying to take care of her dad's financial problems, and she just needs a place to lay low for awhile until all this blows over. I didn't ask for this, and I know she didn't either. But that's the way it's going to be, just for a little while. I need you to be okay with this.'' ''I spend every day of my life taking care of my father's financial fuck-ups... why do hers matter more? You say you hate her, but you sure as shit don't act like it, Darcy.'' 'I can't turn my back on her, Ames, even if she turned her back on me. If I did that, I'm just as bad as she is. I know it's heavy, but I have to do this. I hate to play this card, but this is something you need to think about. If there's any hope for you and me getting married, then you need to get over whatever issues you're having here and learn to trust me.'' The look in Amy's eyes had turned during the course of his last sentences. Suddenly, she seemed gentler when she replied, ''As much as I want to slap you at the same time for everything you just said about me needing to 'get over' things... I'm proud to be with someone who's got such a strong sense of integrity. You're forgiven, for now... and I promise, I'm dealing with my inner problems. It's rough, but I'm slowly growing out of my anxieties.'' She sighed and eased back into her pillow, ''I guess you and I are pretty damned good for forcing one another to soul-seek.'' Darcy nodded, but Amy wasn't done. She yawned, then rolled to one side and winked with a cocky sarcasm, ''... But all of that soul-seeking requires effort, and I'm aiming to sleep 'till noon - so you won't need to start on that 'I'm sorry' breakfast in an hour or two.'' Darcy simply smiled and stepped out of the room, his gentle eyes showing the love he had for the woman turning to sleep peacefully in his bed. Sometimes, her obsessive offers seemed a little too good to be true, while other times they were terrifying. Actually, most times they were terrifying... but those little glimmers of happiness and pride in himself when she asked to give her hand to him were strong enough to remind Darcy exactly why he'd settled back into life in the first place. Of course, by the time he had something sweet to turn back and say, Amy was already oblivious and fast asleep. Such luck is life. MIDNIGHT PENMANSHIP, EVENING ENTRY TWO: Dearest shitty and overly analytical self... I'm known in my personal life for forgiveness. Not the intelligent, noble type of forgiveness, but the kind a fool hands out haphazardly, knowing they'll face whatever they just wrote off again. It's always worse the second time around. And the third. Reading back on what was written last night, and finding myself now laying beside a man I'm enamored yet almost constantly disappointed by, as opposed to being alone in bed again, I'm starting to see what I've felt all along. It's nothing about Darcy, really... I know my heart's with him, but I'm finding more and more that I'm a sucker for punishment. It seems obvious, but that truth has been evading me for quite some time. Michael Sloan never ceases to point it out and, despite my best efforts, I know he's not as full of shit as I want so badly to think. My eyes never decieve me, but my constantly beating heart and thumping fists will fool me without fail, every time. I can't cage my need for pain. Without hurt, I've got no parallel to live by; I wouldn't know happiness without first feeling real agony. What confuses me, like when I realized my hypocracy last night, is that I can't seem to live happily unless I'm dealing out pain twice as badly as I'm getting it. Knowing that I can't take pain without dealing it back in greater amount, I'm left wondering exactly what's going to happen now that I've forgiven - and likely spared pain from falling upon - Darcy. I know who SHOULD be feeling what I'm bundling up as I'm laying here, but I question exactly how much farther than necessary I'll go when AWC unleashes me this week. These weeks off have made it too long since I've felt blood in my mouth and sweat on my hands. With those questions in mind, I'm going to follow Darcy's lead and turn out the light. Sleep can't come too soon when I've got so much to wash away. Before I put the pen down, though, I need to note to myself... how is dealing out punishment on Sloan going to solve any problems with Darcy, especially since Chainz and I are the same - he'll take whatever I throw at him and bring it back to me tenfold. I'm actually disgusted that I wrote those words. 'Chainz and I are the same.' That looks a little too right on paper. (confused? read darcy's piece this week!)
