Date: Around May 12th, 2006 For months I laid there, staring at the ceiling. The ceiling. The ceiling. The ceiling. That fucking ceiling. I laid there and I stared at it. Every second, every minute, every hour, every day, every week, and every God Damned month inside that room I stared at that fucking ceiling. I was burned, I was tortured. The ladder collapsed and I fell. To the crowd, to the people of AWC, I feel into a pit of hot coals and fire. Celestial Fury won that title, and the Furious Fists disappeared. But appearances are always less cruel than the truth, and behind the closed doors there I was, staring at that ceiling. It didn’t take long to heal from the initial wounds. I suffered a concussion, tons of deep gashes, and light and second degree burns all over the body, but within a couple of weeks I was physically ready. If need be, I could tear the head off a fucking bull. Brick walls would’ve crumpled to my fists. Things collapsed, though, when I stared at that ceiling… At first, the ceiling was nothing. The ceiling was just that, a ceiling. My eyes would gaze upon it when the TV bored me, the books have all been read, and everything that I could possibly write was written. The ceiling was what was blank, what was bare. The ceiling was nothing. Things changed though. Things fucking changed. Soon, the ceiling was what I stared at every time I woke up in my healing bed and was the last thing I saw. The ceiling was becoming a fixture. And for months, it was everything I lived for. I woke up knowing that I would see the ceiling and I went back to bed knowing that, probably, I would dream about it. The ceiling was everything. The ceiling, though, was not just a God damn ceiling. In fact, it transformed into something way more than that. At first, it was just a ceiling. But as it transformed, I started seeing things in the ceiling. Whether I was dreaming or not, I didn’t know. But the ceiling became a portal, and to be honest, I wasn’t quite sure if I was speaking with the Lord or the Devil… --------------------------------------------------------- Two birds, sitting on a branch, singing. I led him deep into the alley way where he was sitting, far enough so that it was dark enough to not be seen but light enough to see his eyes. I could here him mumbling about something behind me. “…Yeah man, this right here, this prolly gon’ be some good stuff, right? I mean, shit, if you’re selling it for cheap…” Now, I had to do it. Now. Now. Now. Now. Now. Now. Now. Now. Now. Breathe, you’re a killer, Tim. Now. Now. Now. NOW! We were face to face, no more than an inch apart. The only sense of mine that was working as the blade stuck into his stomach was my sight. I stared into his eyes which didn’t blink. I stared and waited. At first, it was gasps. GYUH! HUH! MUH! The eyes closed with exaggeration, each breath forcing the lids to shut. But that’s not what I wanted. I waited. I waited. I waited. I fucking waited. There I stood there, staring into this man’s eyes as he died, and I waited. And then there it was. In the reflection of his eyes, I saw the man killing him. There he was, laughing. But it wasn’t me. There was no Tim Martin. A figure, white. Tall. Muscular. But there was something blank about him… Suddenly, I began to see things. While I held the knife in the gut and kept him close, he started changing. I focused on the face to see who it was, and it wasn’t the crack head anymore. John O’Neil, a family man who I murdered because he called me a “crazy skin-head fuck that makes the rest of the world hate the whites.” Hazbeem Mujaheed, a convenience store owner I murdered because he was trying to maintain a shop in the Eagle’s Nest. Billy Remy, a local black lawyer I slaughtered because he was trying to persecute an Eagle for murdering a black man. And the faces, they kept changing. All the people I murdered, they kept appearing. All the faces they had before they died, they kept on reforming. The haunting gasps they made, everything about it all was all coming back. One more time, I looked into the man’s eyes, I focused, and there was a demon there. He stood there, as tall as ever, with a sword in his hand. The fire encased him, but it was a white fire. And behind him were a bunch of angels with burnt wings, the horns beginning to grow from there head. His eyes closed. His body became limp. It fell with a thud, slipping backwards off the knife. -------------------------------------------------------------- One time I was in a restaurant, a fast food joint. There were a bunch of thugs sitting behind me, talking about whom they murdered and what not. I didn’t want to show them who was who or what was what—I was grossly outnumbered—so I just sat back and listened. While everyone was one-upping themselves, there was one guy, this little black kid, not saying a word. After he heard enough of who shot who, he spoke to a crowd that became instantly silent. “Anyone can smoke anyone, man. Shit, even puffy shot a fucker in a club. But, you know, it takes a real bad dude to stab a man and look him in the eyes until he dies. A real fucking devil, y’know?” I laughed at the time. But now?
