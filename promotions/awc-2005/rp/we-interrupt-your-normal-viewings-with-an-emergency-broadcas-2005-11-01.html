We interrupt your normal viewings with an emergency broadcast : CRISIS ON INFINITE DICKS!

1 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: there's a frickin' hole in the space-time continuum. i don't think 'dates' are important right now, bitch. The Illustrious Face-Eater has just poured himself a bowl of Cap’n Crunh with Crunch Berries. He sits at his kitchen counter with a glass of murder-free OJ. The Illustrious Face-Eater: I NEED A SPOON! The Illustrious Face-Eater has a spoon. The Illustrious Face-Eater: EVERY PARAGRAPH STARTS WITH THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER! Just then, a hole appears in the fabric of the Space-Time continuum! The light that rips through the hole is blinding, but Facey is still able to make out a figure of someone stepping through. ???: ADAM DICK! Your help is needed for the survival of the Universe! The Illustrious Face-Eater: What?! Who are you!? What are you doing here?! Why did you tear my Space-Time Continuum! ???: There is no time! Come with me, and I’ll explain everything. The Illustrious Face-Eater: What do you mean?! What trouble is occurring in the great yonder that requires the sexy attention of a demi-God like myself? ???: There is a Crisis that requires your attention... Whoever is ??? steps into the light, and reveals himself to be... THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER?!?!? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: A CRISIS ON INFINITE DICKS! Now, before we get into debate about parallel universes; YES, they exist. And no, it isn’t like Zelda where you need a magic mirror to travel from the light world to the dark world. It’s like Sliders, where there are infinite possibilities of infinite worlds. That said, I’d live in lesbo-world, being the only man there to fulfill their primal urges. I’d be taken and harvested as a sex-slave where they need my loins for the continuation of their species. Unless lesbo-world was filled with chicks-whom-also-hold-dicks. That’d be the worst surprise ever. Thank God I didn’t wish to live in lesbo-world. On second thought, I never got my wish at all! I’ve been had! Fuck you, Sebs! You said if I joined PRIME and quit AWC that a partner of my choosing and I get a shot at the tag-team titles! Wait, you promise that to everyone?! Ah, nevermind. The Second Illustrious Face-Eater takes the hand of the original Illustrious Face-Eater, and guides him through the rip. As they pass through a slip-stream where visuals of various moments in civilized history are on display, the original Illustrious Face-Eater turns to the second. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Do you ever see naked chicks on this thing? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Fuck, no. Sometimes we catch a clip of Mark Wahlberg from Boogie Nights, but we all know how that ends. The Illustrious Face-Eater: You mean it’s the same movie in your universe? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Well, it’s actually a good movie in my universe. The Illustrious Face-Eater: TOPSY FUCKING TURVEY! By now, it should be said that the second Illustrious Face-Eater looks a little bit older than the first Illustrious Face-Eater, in that he is wearing an eye-patch. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Sweet eye-patch. The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Thanks. The Illustrious Face-Eater: So, are you going to tell me what is going on?! The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Oh, right. Well, this journey is about the length of an explanation, so I guess we have time. Anyways, outside of where our plane of existence, um… exists, there is a binding element that every alternate universe is confined to. The Mucho-verse. The Illustrious Face-Eater: The MUCHO-verse?! The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Spain got to it first. Anyways, in the Mucho-verse there are various different outcomes of our planet, Earth. Where you come from, that’s Earth-Uno. The Illustrious Face-Eater: WORD! NUMERO UNO! Wait a second, shouldn’t it be Mundo-Uno? Since Mundo is, like, Earth for Mexican or something like that? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: That doesn’t even make any fucking sense what so ever! Mundo-Uno? You know how fucking stupid that sounds! Besides, it isn’t that simple. The Mucho-verse is very balanced in how diverse the outcomes are. For example, Earth-Uno Illustrious Face-Eater is a wrestling superstar in the AWC! The Illustrious Face-Eater: Wow! That’s me! The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: While on Earth-Dos, the Illustrious Face-Eater is a wresling n00b in PRIME. The Illustrious Face-Eater: I AM NO N00B. The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: On Earth-Dos, the alternate version of you is a n00b. Anyways, all these different Earth’s exist, and you—or even me, for that matter—are the anchor that holds them together. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Wait a second, if I’m Earth-Uno Facey, and there’s an Earth-Dos Facey that is entirely different than you or I, what Earth are you from? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Earth-whatever-is-the-Mexican-equivalent-to-thirty-six-because-I-don’t-know-Mexican. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Well, shouldn’t the narrator NOT call you the Second Illustrious Face-Eater? Shouldn’t you be the thirty-sixth? The Thirty-Sixth Illustrious Face-Eater: Uh……….. COME ON! OUR WORLD’S NEED US! Conveniently, a hole rips open in Space-Time again, and it poops out both Illustrious Face-Eaters. Adam Dick, the AWC Adam Dick (As we’ve come to recognize as Earth-Uno Illustrious Face-Eater) falls out of the hole and lands very awkwardly on his head. Lifting to see his surroundings, he wasn’t at his kitchen table awaiting a very soggy bowl of Cap’n Crunch. And no, not that OOOPS! ALL BERRIES bullshit, fuck the Cap’n for selling the gold flakes out. If I wanted sugary shit for breakfast, I’d have the stuff that comes out of Aimz mouth connected to me like a feeding tube, because it don’t get more sugary than that. Anyways, Adam landed in the dirtiest depths of Hell; the dark corners no soul ever dares to look. Adam gazed at his environment, finding no solace in peace or cleanliness or convenience or leisure. Oh no. Instead, Adam stands amongst a crowded masses, all huddled. The Illustrious Face-Eater: WHERE ARE WE?!? Numerous Other Illustrious Face-Eaters: Are you telling me you’ve never eaten at a Denny’s before? The Illustrious Face-Eater looked around. It was a Denny’s alright. Even worse; the smoking section. Adam glanced at everyone in front of himself. Strangely enough, they all were himself. Tons of them. Hundreds, maybe thousands, as unrealistic as it seems, huddled in a mass of Dicks. All in the smoking section of Denny’s. Adam looked at the many different versions of himself, wondering what could have been. He sees Alistair Dick of Earth-Ochenta y ocho, the 50th President of his world’s United States. He dried the economy’s resources in his national witch-hunt of the widely despised race of people: those with deformities. “No one wants to look at that shit” was his re-election slogan. Adam then sees Adelle Clitbox of Earth-Sesenta y nueve, AKA the Crazy Ex-Girlfriend. Guys would only date her because of her looks and usually drop her after (a) she gives up the sex, or (b) she drops the ‘L’ bomb for the first time, on their Myspace pages no-less. She was jumping over people to see what everyone was crowding for. Adam also sees Adamo Weiniero, gay film star. This prompts Adam to give his gay counterpart the usual pleas for smiting that any cowardly homophobe will give to an openly faggish man. In disgust, he turns towards the back of the commotion; behind all the chaos. In the reserves of a corner smoking booth, beginning to rip a two foot bong (because he couldn’t fit an eight-footer) is Adam Dick of Earth-Dos. He looks much the same as Adam Dick of Earth-Uno. Earth-Uno walks up to Earth-Dos. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Can I hit it? The Real Second, But Equally As Cool As The First Illustrious Face-Eater: Earth Uno, right? The Illustrious Face-Eater: Yeah. He doesn’t wait for an answer, and just takes the bong and hits it. The Illustrious Face-Eater: Okay, I think I’m in the right frame of mind to deal with this, now. Uh... what exactly is this? The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Dude, I was waiting for you to tell me! ??? (COMPLETELY DIFFERENT ??? THAN THE ??? FROM EARLIER. THIS ONE ISN’T ANOTHER ADAM DICK, ALTHOUGH YOU PROBABLY WILL WISH IT WAS AFTER.) I should probably repeat that so you don’t get confused. ???: YOU DON’T KNOW THE ULTIMATE BATTLE OF GOOD AND EVIL WHEN IT IS PISSING RIGHT IN YOUR FACE, PUNY MORTAL!?! The Illustrious Face-Eater: Who is this speaking! I, the Eternal Champion Of Eternity from Earth-Uno, demand to know! ???: Ha ha ha ha. The voice, it sounds so evil. So decrepit. So... dastardly. Yet it was vaguely familiar. ???: Isn’t it obvious? I’m the absolute evil that plagues every breathing being on this planet! In EVERY existence! The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: Sorry, a little more specific? ???: I haunt children’s dreams. I conduct mad schemes world-wide, ranging from global take-over to zombie empires to terrorist attacks! You name it, I’ve done it. Fuck, I’ve messed with those damn Mario Brothers! THOSE DAMN PLUMBERS! The Illustrious Face-Eater: Well, could you be a tad bit more specific? For the dumb ones? ???: Gah! I am the very essence of everything that is wrong in human nature. I am the little devil that sit’s on Satan’s shoulder, whispering even EVILER thoughts in his ear. RARRR! I am fucking evil, okay? Both Illustrious Face-Eaters: . . . . . . ???: Fine! Forget it! The mysteriously booming voice pushes aside the mass amount of Dick’s and makes his way, face-to-face with Adam Dicks of Earth-Uno and Dos The Illustrious Face-Eater: It can’t be! The Second Illustrious Face-Eater: I KNEW ALL ALONG! But I never really would have believed it could REALLY be him... ???: YES, IT IS I! DENNIS HOPPER.
