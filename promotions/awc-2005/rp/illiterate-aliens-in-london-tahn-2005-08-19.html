Illiterate Aliens in London TAHN!

19 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th August The last time we eavesdropped on the misadventures of The Scottish King of Cool, Andy M., our hero was commanding the Starship Scotchlin through the stratosphere, in search for new galaxies to conquer and influence with his overwhelming aura of coolness. However, things had not been going to plan; having been shot out of the skies by the heinous dictator George W. Moose, Darth Murrr and the crew had crash landed – right in the hard of London TAAAAAHN! A few distinctive cockney voices pierced through the sizzling made by the hulk of the Starship Scotchlin. The only survives of the accident – Darth Murrr, First Mate Cayle, Badass Dude Grobschnit, Kerry Bitter, Warp, Snowball, Kris Kitty and Steven Xandrocat – all glanced around nervously at each other. Well, apart from the two cats, who were still having sex with each other. As the mist cleared, a few shadowy figures began to emerge. “My god, what kind of a place is that!?!” a wide-eyed Cayle asked, his voice dripping with fear. As the dust continued to settle, the thick cockney accents on display made it quite clear to Darth Murrr where they had crash-landed. “Shit, we’re in London.” “Oh well, could be worse,” Kerry Bitter muttered, scooping up Kris Kitty and Steven Xandrocat – who were now both enjoying a tiny cigarette each – in his arms. “We could have ended up on the feared planet Zarg!” “What the hell are you talking about!?!” Darth snapped, “This is ENGLAND! Do you have any idea how little love the average Englishman has for the average Scotsman!?!” Kerry shrugs, dismissing Darth Murrr; he clearly couldn’t care less about the age-old rivalry between Scotland and England. Vision was increasing by the second, as Andy and his crew slid down the hulk of the spacecraft with intrigue. The ship had come to a halt directly on top of some kind of large building, which was now nothing more than a mess of broken glass and red bricks. “Blimey! Guv! Cop a load… it’s some kind ‘uv Star Trek fan club, innit.” Horrified by the accent, Murrr turned around – his jaw almost dropping at the sight of thousands of rabid Londoners eyeing him and his crew up. Oddly enough, the Scotchlin had come to rest right beside a street market, including stalls selling fruit and vegetables, crappy re-release CDs and straight-to-DVD films, and a Bric-a-Brac (A.K.A.: random garbage that is of no use to anyone ever). “Apples and pears! Britney Spears! ‘Ave a Butcher’s!” another cockney randomly chirped. What the hell does that mean, anyway? “Come on chums, CHARGE!” Darth Murrr yelled, attempting to spearhead his crew through the crowd of cockneys. Only problem was, Murrr was the only one to actually make the charge. Like a hot knife through butter, he cut through the ranks, before skidding to a halt, almost running right into the fruit and veg stall. Panicking, Darth looked around, noticing a large proportion of the Londoners staring at him with confused glances. He turned around and quickly grabbed the most threatening thing he could find, a cucumber, and began swinging it around like an axe. “BACK! BACK I SAY!” Andy wailed like a banshee. Slowly but surely, the cockneys closed in on him like a plague of zombies. WHACK! Darth Murrr managed to level a couple of the rabid Englishmen with the cucumber, but the numbers were too great. He was dooooooooooooooooomed They were clawing at his leg, pulling at his cloak… One managed to wrap his hands around Murrr’s neck… -------------------- As Andy awoke from his slumber in his warm, welcoming London hotel, he felt rejuvenated and somewhat confused, as ever. The sunlight was peeking through the slightest of gaps in Andy’s curtains, irritatingly shining straight into the Scottish King of Cool’s sleepy gaze. In discomfort, Andy rubbed the sleep from his eyes, and leapt out of his bed. He stretched with an almighty yawn, as he walked across towards the grand wardrobe, its doors left open. Andy pulled out a black t-shirt which read “FCUK Chavs,” pulled it over his head and headed for the bathroom. This windowless room was of a standard consistently high with the rest of the building, and as Andy flicked the lights on, it looked more like the bathroom of a palace than of a hotel room. Light reflected off the white tiles and onto the glimmering gold-plated fixtures, making the room seem brighter than it really was. The cylindrical mirror was huge, and gave a good all-round view of the toilet, bath, shower and washbasin, all of which were white, elegant, and fit for a king… a Scottish King of Cool, perhaps? The segments of the wall which weren’t occupied by white tiling were painted in a light sky-blue colour. Andy turned the cold water faucet on and let the water run through his fingers, as he began to mutter to himself, coming to a realisation. “Damn, I make one badass evil space lord,” he groggily uttered, “I should make a habit of such intergalactic adventures!” Before using a fresh bar of soap to cleanse his hands, Andy cupped his hands together, and threw a quick blast of cold water over his face, waking the rest of his body from its state of half sleep. Andy took a good, long look at himself in the mirror; all these thoughts of crash landing in London had shaken him up a tiny bit. “Shit, I AM in London…” Andy suddenly realised. With a shrug, he dipped his hand into a tub of hair gel, and took a few moments to adjust his hair a little – a little bit of vanity shining through from Murrr – when suddenly he heard the familiar chime of his mobile phone coming from inside the room. “Who in the name of Jesus calls you at,” Andy paused to check his silver Rolex, “10:30 in the morning!?! Why, that is PREPOSTEROUS!” Andy turned the faucet off, and glanced over his shoulder, and after briefly rubbing them down with a towel, headed back into the room. The ringing was coming from Andy’s black Alexisonfire zip-up hoody, which was laying on a chair in the corner of the room. Andy immediately pulled it from the pocket, and pulled it up to his ear. Beep. “Hello?” “’Ey Andy, eet’s mee, Alvaro…” the familiar voice of his faithful Portuguese butler, Alvaro, announced. “Alvaro, what the hell!?!” Andy ranted, “How come you’re bothering me at this ungodly hour? This better be good, you crazy Portuguese motherhubbard, otherwise I’m gonna have to sic Snowball on your ass…” “No, eet ees Grobschneet, he’s in trouble, you’d better get down here quickly before he does any more damage! He spent de whole night watching de adult movies, an’ refused to pay de bill… now, he’s gone and done a beat down on one of de receptionists, an’ dee other ees cowering in the corner!” Andy immediately knew what to do, and rushed out into a fabulously well-decorated hallway that was just as grand as the rest of the building, without evening answering his butler. The carpet was a deep tone of red, and was patterned with little golden crowns. The first metre-or-so of the wall was panelled tastefully with wood and varnished, which reflected light from the almost Western-style light fixtures. The hallway was almost completely empty, except for a couple of porters, dressed identically, chatting away at the end of the corridor. Andy turned, running away from the porters, and made the short journey to the elevator area, which was decorated in much the same way as the hall. Andy stepped up to one of the elevators, and pushed the button, tapping his foot impatiently as he awaited its arrival. Within a couple of seconds, however, the doors slid open, and The SKC stepped into the elevator. -------------------- The marble interior of the hotel lobby was stunning on the eye; certainly somewhere Andy would never have been able to afford to stay in had he not followed his heart and chosen to become a professional wrestler. Wait a minute… That was borderline seriousness. Bah. What was not so stunning about the hotel was the fact that one of the receptionists was indeed lying on a heap on the floor. The other was cowering behind the desk like a scolded dog, while Grobschnit stood at the other side, maniacally waving a broken bottle around like a lunatic. As Andy stepped out of the elevator he caught a glimpse of this and dashed across like Superman, to where his butler was attempting to calm the notorious G-R-O-B down. “FUX U FAGITZ! IZZ KIK UR AZZ U FUKAR!” Grobschnit cackled as the receptionist, quite literally, wet himself. Andy, hardly the voice of reason himself, immediately dashed in front of his alien sidekick. “Grob! CALM DOWN! You’re going to get yourself in trouble!” “OMG ANDEE! Deez fagitz bee givin meh a harrd tyme cuz eye wotched al da pr0n an nowe theye huv nut gut enee levt 4 utha gestz!” “Grob, listen to me…” Andy began, soothingly, “is it really worth going back to the penitentiary? For this, a couple of dirty films?” Alvaro butted in, “’Ey meng, it was a LOT more than a couple…” “Wuteva, eye don kare abowt prizon, wite mahn kannt hohld mee dwon!” Grobschnit proclaimed with defiance. However, suddenly, the guy he had been tormenting leapt from behind the desk in a massive panic, and dashed out of the front door. “WAYTE! FAGIT!” Grobschnit shouted, and attempted to give chase – only to run straight into Andy’s chest. “Dude, NO! Let it go… it’s not worth it…” “Butt… uhghghg… ‘Schnit r gett revunge…” Andy scowled down, unhappy with his pet alien. Finally, with a sigh, Grob let it ride, sensing that he was never going to overcome Andy in this matter. He dropped the broken bottle on the marble floor; needless to say, it shattered into thousands of tiny pieces. “ohh wtf. Lezz goe geht sum Kewl Aide, playa!” “YAY GROBBY!” Andy yelped, before embracing his alien friend with a massive hug. Noticing that Alvaro wasn’t doing anything in particular, Andy glanced at him, and said; “Come on Alvaro, gimme some love!” Cautiously, the Portuguese butler took a step towards the embracing couple, and joined in with the hugging. Aww, ain’t that cute?
