I'm Facing Superman, So I Can Post a Half Assed Roleplay This Week

8 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/06 So, since I won this Zero 2 Hero Tournament, I’m kind of a big fucking deal now. It feels good to finally get some recognition. It’s still a bit strange that I’m getting noticed now as something legitimate; something other than a dirty pothead, dragging on society, inconsiderate, worthless, never-amount-to-anything, and so on so forth. But it feels the same in pretty much most aspects. Winning does not change who I am. I won, I’d like to think, because of who I am instead of the other way around. I’m still the same dirty pothead, dragging on society, inconsiderate, worthless, never-amount-to-anything, and so on so forth kind of guy, but I guess that’s the new in this season. I’m not a hero. I’m not a role model. Please don’t let your kids grow up to be like me. They should be scientists or doctors or teachers. Anything. Anything good, you know? I fight, and I don’t even do it that well. I have no endurance when it comes to running; only in terms of taking a beating. I can get my ass kicked longer than anyone can stand to kick it. I’m desensitized to the pain. Fuck. Desensitization is my life. I’ve spent hours training my body to accept hazardous treatment to the point where it’s normal. I don’t remember what it’s like to feel clarity or health or motivation or that rejuvenated sense of vigor that you sometimes feel when you wake up early on your own and the sun is shining and the birds are chirping and the world is yours to hold. I don’t remember a lot of things. My mind is the cracked egg, yolk simmering on the frying pan. I let my hair dry naturally. I do wash it. Hair is important to me. I’ll be bald one day. That bothers me to death; more than death. I wash it and condition it, but I don’t brush it. I let it dry naturally, even though it tangles in the back, giving me oftentimes a half dreadlocked sprout of hair falling from the back of my head. I loathe toothpaste- well, not so much toothpaste as toothbrushes. The bristles and the sound they make are enough to drive me mad, so I only clean my teeth when I look in the mirror and notice the nicotine stained yellow film caked inside so that I can taste it. But it’s usually not half as bad as the scraping, back and forth, up and down, and in circles sound of the bristles on my teeth. Sometimes I think I’m an attractive man, when my hair is clean and shining, and I’m wearing a hat and a suit jacket…or if my beard is trimmed evenly, and my face isn’t oily or cut up. Sometimes I shave the little hairs in between my eyebrows. I always thought I’d keep my brow line sharp. My eyebrows are nicely shaped; very aerodynamic and masculine over hard brown eyes. They really make the whole face, you know. Sometimes I think I’m an attractive man; so attractive that it’s intimidating. But other times, I’m a mess, and there is no in between. Sometimes I love everyone and sometimes I hate everyone. It depends strongly on the temperature or the speed at which other people are walking. They’re silly reasons, I know, but I hate when people walk fast and have places to go. I hate summer in summer and winter in winter. I wait for the next season and then I hate that too. But I like the snow when I’m wearing a heavy greatcoat, and I like the sun when I’m out on my porch with a cigarette and an acoustic guitar. I like dogs a lot. Sometimes I wish I was a dog. Then life would be easy. But I can’t stand to watch people walking their dogs, plastic bags in hand, ready to grab pieces of dog shit with only a thin, clear, plastic layer of protection. It’s so degrading to be human, to have to worry about picking up shit. Babies, too. All babies are ugly. They’re defenseless little skin bags full of organs whose primary collective purpose it is to push out the oddest tints and shades of human excrement ever seen. I threw a small gathering in honor of my victory because I didn’t get a plaque or a trophy or anything tangible, so I thought a party would suffice. Lots of alcohol, marijuana, pills, the works. I told my friends to come, and I told them to tell their friends to come. I had my roommates invite the people they knew. I knew I wouldn’t like most of the people that showed up. I hate mostly everyone when they’re drunk or when I’m drunk. I hate people when they’re louder than everyone else, because they give off some false air of importance. There were people having sex in my bathroom. I hated them. And God, do I hate babies. I never liked talking to strangers any more than was absolutely necessary. I was never interested in anything people had to say, and I was awkward at inventing small talk. Usually, I’d just walk away, pretending to have other matters to attend to. I liked coming up with terrible ideas and suggesting them to a group. I always hoped someone would try one. Music was always a touchy subject at parties. I hated guests who thought hosts should be obliging. I hate music you can dance to. Daft Punk was on loop, so I stayed away. Strangers touching the stereo or bringing their own mix tapes were strictly forbidden. They risked arguments or fistfights, and that’s why I liked my roommate Shoog. He was much the same way. I liked Shoog when he was drunk. Most people didn’t. They were scared of him. He liked to break things, sometimes with his own skin. He kept things interesting, always boiling. Unless we started arguing about who did the dishes last. He did that sometimes. I don’t use any of the goddamned dishes. I lay in my bed, smoking a cheap, plastic bong with a cigarette dangling from my mouth and a bottle of Gatorade and Vodka. It’s too noisy out there. There are too many conversations I don’t wish to hear. Nobody really cares anyway. They’re all drunk. Some say congratulations. Some don’t even know who the fuck I am. Some are scared of me. I’m a bearded, pale ghost with scars on my limbs, unkempt hair, and yellow teeth. Or some are scared of me cause I’m too goddamned attractive, and witty to boot. But I don’t care. I kind of like it that way. I like the peace and quiet murmur of all the petty voices blending together to create an indiscernible hum. It puts me to sleep. It’s either that or the lots of alcohol, marijuana, pills, the works. When I’m alone, I never smile or frown. I don’t think most people do either. I used to be able to cry, but I don’t know how anymore. My eyes close, and the bong water spills all over the bed, the cigarette still dangles from my mouth, the edges of my lips barely curling upwards. I’m the same fucking guy. But I’m smiling now. Everything’s different now, and yet, nothing has changed. How the fuck did I manage to pull that one off?
