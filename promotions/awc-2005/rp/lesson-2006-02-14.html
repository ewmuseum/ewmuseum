Date: 2/14/06 Anton stood on the sidewalk, the night sky seeming to bear down on his dark frame. He wore a thick leather jacket and black denim jeans, making him look suitably imposing. His collar was turned up in a style that was fashionable in the 80's and his hands were jammed in his pockets. He frowned as was his wont, tilting his head back and exhaling through his nostrils. It was a muggy night; not the best circumstances for being out. Anton's shirt felt glued to his armpits. It wasn't a feeling he particularly liked. Nneka stood in the middle of the street, her slender arms folded under her breasts. Anton had managed to convince her to wear blue jeans, even if they were tighter on her frame than he would have liked, and she sported a fashionable baby blue top with short sleeves. She faced the ground, not wanting to face her brother or Luis. He wiped her nose with a handkerchief that she held in her hand before turning, looking anxiously towards the door of 198 Worthing Street. Luis finished his cigarette and tossed the smoldering butt away from him, striding towards the group. He didn't wear a suit, instead going in a dark red windbreaker and khakis. His greased hair and mustache gave him the look he had perfected: that of a sleazy money-maker, which he was. Luis glanced up at Anton and then looked over at Nneka. ''You idiots gonna sit there like lumps?'' Luis asked, in English so they could both understand. ''Or ya gonna… y'know, sit there like lumps. I don't need eeda of ya talkin'. Fuckin' up my deals 'n shit. Prolly say sump'n retarded.'' Anton frowned at Luis and took a step forward, pressing his thumb to the Venezuelan's chin. ''Don't speak to me like that,'' Anton warned in tense Spanish. Luis brought up his hands defensively, fear washing over his face quickly. ''Sorry, Lion,'' Luis responded in kind. ''Yeah, you're right. Just… just, I don't want this fucked up, understand?'' Nneka was looking away the entire time; she wasn't going to pay attention to them if they weren't going to talk in a way that she could understand. ''I don't understand why you brought us out here,'' Anton asked, shrugging his hands out of his jacket. He drywashed his hands together. Sweat lubricated the sliding, but Anton didn't like the feel too much. It was far too hot for a heavy jacket like he was wearing. ''We don't have anything to do with this.'' ''She doesn't,'' Luis admitted, jabbing his thumb at Nneka. She didn't hear him, or at the very least she didn't understand. ''I need you here to watch my back, Lion. You're just too scared that she's gonna do coke or fuck a couple truckers that you don't want to leave her alone.'' Anton nodded slowly, gesturing to his manager. ''Lead, then.'' Luis nodded and turned around, walking up the short concrete steps towards the door. He rapped his fingers on it and got up on his tiptoes, trying to peer through the eyehole. It was next to useless from his end, but Luis liked to know. Anton moved over and stood shoulder-to-shoulder with his younger sister, looking out the same direction. ''Keep your eyes down,'' Anton instructed sternly, speaking Igbo for her. ''Don't speak. Don't gesture. I wish you had covered yourself more effectively, but that's in the past. I don't know who is inside and neither—'' ''Don't speak to me, brother,'' Nneka snapped. She looked up at him, curling her upper lip. ''You don't own me. This is not Nigeria and I am not your wife. I can do what I like and that includes fucking.'' Anton spat. ''Don't be a brat, Nneka,'' Anton said, trying to remain calm. Her defiance was trying; it had been several weeks and she just wouldn't learn. ''I just want to protect you.'' ''You don't want to protect me,'' Nneka said. She looked back out at the street. ''You want to protect yourself. What would mama and papa say if this happened to me, yes? I know. You haven't spoken to them in so long, so if the first thing they hear is that you let my virginity get stolen… God forbid what they might do to you. They might even talk nasty.'' Anton snatched up his sister's ear, dragging her to face him. ''Listen!'' Anton growled. ''This is not about me, Nneka. Get your head out of your ass. One day you are going to overdose or you're going to get hurt or worse. I can't let that happen to you. It's bad enough that you've been experimenting with everything you can get your hands on.'' Nneka winced, gripping Anton's wrist and trying to pry him away, but it was no use. ''You aren't papa! Stop trying to be him!'' Anton gritted his teeth then, his face drawn into a cruel snarl. He didn't like his father. He accepted the man, but he had never liked him. The idea that he wanted to be like his father irritated him, even offended him. Anton slowly let go of his sister's ear and wiped his hand on his jeans, disgusted. Without a word, he blinked and ignored her pained stroking of her ear. Anton turned towards the door and found Luis standing there, arms folded over his chest. ''I been waitin' for you two idiots t' finish whateva, mang!'' Luis snapped in English. ''Less go!'' Luis turned on his heel and headed inside. Anton strode in after him and Nneka followed. She glanced behind her worriedly, keeping close with her brother as they left the street behind. Anton glanced at the man who had held the door open. He was a black guy, suitably muscled and wearing nothing but a wifebeater and a pair of fairly torn jeans. A black bandanna was wrapped around his head and he eyed the newcomers suspiciously. When his eyes fell on Nneka's backside, his look turned to one of pleased amazement, sucking breath slowly, but he simply closed the door behind them. Luis appeared to know his way through the place and headed up a flight of nearby stairs, prompting the two Nigerians to follow. Luis stepped through an open doorway into an occupied room. It was fairly large, with three sets of brown couches arranged around a small rectangular table near one corner and a television across from them. Half of the room was dedicated to a kitchen area, sectioned off by a low wall up to the entrance point. Sitting on the couches were four black men, all of pretty much the same build as the one who had opened the door. Luis lifted his chin, giving them a short grin and moving over to them. ''Wh'ssup, Luis?'' one of them greeted in English, rising just enough to slap Luis's hand. Luis shrugged as he took a seat. One of the men sat on the couch that Luis had plopped down on, but he moved courteously. Anton sat down and leaned backwards, laying his head against the top of the chair. Nneka smiled at the ravenous grins she got and took a seat next to Anton. Nneka leaned onto her brother's shoulder. ''Do they have guns?'' she asked him quietly in Igbo. Anton looked forward, calmly leaning away from her. She made to lean over some more and try and get a response, but she decided against it quickly and pouted. Anton resumed his previous position as if nothing untoward had happened. '''S a lotta coke,'' one of the black guys was saying as Anton refocused on the conversation. Luis was hunched over and he winced a bit, shaking his head. ''I gotta friend I'm gettin' it for,'' Luis said. ''Said I knew you guys were good t' me in the past, so I'd get it for him. How much you want for it?'' The dealer considered for a moment, stroking the short beard he sported. ''Fi'teen,'' he said finally. Luis's eyes bulged and he shook his head in disbelief. ''I only got ten, E!'' Luis snapped. ''I mean, I cu' get fifteen, but I don't got fifteen.'' ''Look, man,'' the guy called E explained, ''I already got someone who wants the ounce and it's as much as I got right now. You gonna hadda outbuy that motha if you want it.'' ''I only got ten with me,'' Luis reiterated. ''Fuck! Fuck!'' He buried his head in his hands and E laughed. ''You really shook up about this, ain't ya?'' E asked. Luis cursed again into his hands. ''Listen, man, you find a way we can make up that five and we call it even, straight?'' ''Okay, E,'' Luis said, calming down. ''How do you expect I make up five grand right fucking now? I ain't a fuckin' fairy. I got a fighter here, but unless you gonna pay five grand to see him kick the shit out of someone, I ain't got shit.'' One of E's friends leaned over and whispered something in his ear. E listened and his eyes turned to Nneka. He looked her up and down and he smiled. ''What about th' chick you got witcha?'' E asked. Luis's head snapped over at Anton, but surprisingly, Anton did not look moved. ''Her?'' Luis asked for clarification. ''Nah, E, y'… y' don't want her. I mean…'' The guy who had opened the door was already taking her firmly by the hand and standing her up. Nneka looked around fearfully, her throat locked up and preventing her from screaming. She simply breathed quickly, her eyes darting from Luis to Anton rapidly. ''She ain't no good, E! C'mon, man…'' E laughed, looking up at her. He ran his hands up her hips and whistled. ''Nah, playa, you shook up about this deal,'' E said. ''We just gonna fuck th' shit out yo girl and we call it even.'' The guy who held Nneka tossed her down onto the couch. E grabbed at her top and lifted it up over her chest, revealing a black bra. Luis scooted forward, but one of the others flashed a knife to deter him. ''ANTON!'' Nneka screamed. Her voice was like that of someone being murdered, ripping through the air. ''ANTON HELP ME!'' E backhanded her hard across the face, but she didn't stop; her legs thrashed and she tried as much as she could to get away. The screaming was incessant until E backhanded her again, cursing as he clamped his hand hard over her mouth. Luis looked at Anton, waiting for the Lion to spring up and save the day. Anton simply rose and slipped around the couch, walking out of the room. After a second of stunned silence, Luis's voice rang out after him. Anton didn't stop as he headed down the stairs, his hands jammed inside his pockets in a clear image of nonchalance. Anton leaned against the outside of the building, twisting a cigarette in his hands. He looked out at the street, patiently waiting. Luis burst through the door and rounded on Anton, his tiny frame pulled up as tall as he could against the big African. Anton arched an eyebrow at Luis as the Venezuelan jabbed him in the chest. ''What the fuck are you doing?'' Luis shouted in Spanish, irate. ''Your sister is getting raped in there, you fuckin' lunatic!'' Anton shrugged his shoulders. ''She wanted to be left alone,'' Anton responded simply. ''She can handle herself, right? She's a big girl now.'' Luis threw up his arms in anger and paced around, grabbing his hair. ''Are you some kind of idiot, Lion!?'' Luis cried. ''You're takin' it too damn far! What if they fuckin' gut her! What if they… shit, man! I don't even know!'' ''And what if?'' Anton asked coolly. ''What if she loves it? That's what it seems like to me.'' ''What if you're a jackass! You don't get it, do you? You're her brother, you motherfucker! You have a fuckin' responsibili—'' ''Don't!'' Anton snapped, stepping forward and pushing Luis backwards. Luis staggered into the side of his car and stopped, glaring. ''Don't talk to me about responsibility, you midget bastard! You threw that away when you got her high on coke. You threw that away when you taught her how to two-step right onto your fuckin' dick! You threw that away a long time ago. You want responsibility? Go fuckin' grab her yourself!'' Luis took two quick steps towards him, his fists balling angrily. He looked at the Nigerian for a long while, gritting his teeth. ''Fuck!'' Luis exclaimed, reaching up and snatching the cigarette away from Anton. He stalked off towards one side and lit it, smoking angrily. Anton simply shook his head as the two waited, one unwilling to do anything and the other unable. Nneka lay on Anton's bed, face up. A cigarette stuck out of her mouth and smoke rose lazily from it. Her eyes were closed, but she was awake. She wore a long night-shirt and modest black shorts that came down almost to her knees. Her arms were crossed under her head and she simply relaxed. Anton pushed open the blinds of his window, glancing out. ''I warmed up to it after a bit,'' Nneka revealed to him in Igbo. ''If you let yourself go, it's not so bad.'' Anton pursed his lips. ''You're a masochist,'' Anton responded. Nneka laughed, plucking the cigarette from her lips and blowing smoke into the air. Anton wasn't partial to the smell of tobacco smoke, but he didn't say anything to her. ''Don't call me that, brother,'' Nneka implored. ''I know you think I'm a slut, but it wasn't like that.'' ''Then tell your story better,'' Anton said. ''If you didn't like it like you're apparently saying, why are you discussing all the positions like I want to hear it?'' Nneka hesitated a bit before sticking the cigarette back into her mouth. ''Sorry.'' They sat in silence for a bit, Anton simply watching the scenery outside his window. ''Don't leave me like that again. Please?'' ''Then listen, you idiot,'' Anton snapped harshly. He let the blinds crash together and turned around. ''I told you what you should do, but you refused. You made eyes at them. You smiled. If you had been wearing a skirt, I'm sure you would have flashed your panties at them.'' Nneka smiled and Anton shook his head. ''You take things too lightly. They raped you.'' ''It wasn't like that,'' Nneka explained. ''It was just… payment. Besides, they weren't that rough.'' ''You see what I mean!? You're so naïve.'' Anton started towards the door, more than a little disgusted. ''Brother, I'm sorry,'' Nneka apologized. ''You're still not papa but… don't leave me like that again.'' ''I won't. I'm your brother. You had to learn, though.'' ''That way? You suck.'' Anton grinned. ''Rest.''
