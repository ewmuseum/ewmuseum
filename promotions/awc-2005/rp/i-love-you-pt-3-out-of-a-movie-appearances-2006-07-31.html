I Love You, pt. 3: Out Of A Movie; Appearances

31 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/06 Liam is not known for being a prepared man. Now, before I continue with that thought, I feel the urge on why I just jump right the fuck into Liam. No type of set up, no type of “It was a dark or stormy night” type bullshit. Just fucking Liam. But I think I’ve covered this before. I think, in past chapters, I’ve gone on this type of monologue. Which truly brings me to my ultimate problem, which is continuity and lack of memory. (lack of memory? Is that the right way to describe it?) For you see, without even knowing it, I was about to go on about Liam reminiscing about Liam traveling and remembering good times with his beau, his lady of choice, etc. I had this whole thing planned out, and then I sat back, looked at what I was putting out there, and said to myself “What the fuck am I doing?” So this is where I am, stuck, trying to wade through the bullshit I was going to put out there. I could go on to explain that Liam stopped at a convenience store and bought chocolates, the cheap kind that you can tell were bought for less than five dollars because it’s not even Valentine’s Day and they’re in the shape of a heart, something you’d find at Brook’s Pharmacy or a classy joint like that. I could explain that Liam thought that this was considerate, that this was the type of thing that maybe could give him some lee-way after essentially banishing her from the world of an Eagle, but even rather she was banishing herself. But why do that? I sit here and I stare at the screen, wondering why I even bother to develop characters like Liam to begin with. Why the fuck am I giving these two horrible people heart and character? Why the fuck am I doing this? The most intriguing thing about the whole situation is that I come back, every time they’re booked, and I write and I write and I write, I explain, I give life to these two characters who do nothing but kill and murder and swear and pray to a God that doesn’t exist. (I don’t believe in God, but I’m not saying he doesn’t exist, but rather there’d definitely be NO White Loving Lord, or could there? That’s for another discussion) I try to make these two despicable human being seem like people we can relate to. I give one a heart and I give another a problem. I give one someone who’s dying, someone who he’s loved in the past a sickness that I’d bestow upon no one. And when I write these characters, I try to make it in a way so that I can relate as well, people who I can give emotion and character. The hardest part though is that for all the remorse I try to infuse into these characters, I’m left with the blinking vertical line, thinking, thinking, thinking. My brain is tired, my finger tips are sore as I write and delete, write and delete. Really, how can you ignore the past of these guys? How can I try to lie to you, make it seem like their struggle is even worth it? This is where I call you out. This is where I cite the human heart as being the weakest organ in the body, the one I can twist and distort to a point where it doesn’t even resemble one anymore, something more like mush. I can give you heartless fucking killers one week and then give you a God damn Romeo and Juliet story the next. And what do you do? You fall for it, you love it, and if you don’t love it, you can maybe relate to it. And that’s what sickens me, not the lack of continuity bullshit I tried to accomplish earlier, but how easily I or any other writer can make the heart turn on itself. How fucking easily you can fall for this guy. How fucking easy. All this may seem pretentious, as if I’m sucking my own dick in my writing skills, but that’s not it. We see it all over the place, in TV, in film, fucking everywhere. The horrible people, the killers, all you have to do is make them seem human like you and me and they become that, but they aren’t. The sinners are no better than the rapists, the pedophiles, anyone else we let rot in jail. But if we give them that right twinkle, they’re ok, right? Right? ~~~~~~ “Roses are red, Violets are blue, You’re beautiful, And God loves you too.” The trash that came off my pen wasn’t beautiful; why else would I describe it as trash? I was no poet laureate, but fuck damn, what the fuck was I thinking? Here I go, meeting a nice pretty girl at a part, and I go off and try to become Walt Whitman? I know I’m no writer, but God damn. Ever since that night, even since that party at my buddy’s house, I’ve been trying to find my place, or my talent, or whatever it is. I’ve been trying to find my peacock feathers, the shit that attracts the opposite sex. And so far I’ve come up with a nice physique, but really, do I want a woman like that? Do I want someone who only wants hard body and hard cock? I’ll answer that: no. I want a woman who is more than looks, who has something in her brain, someone who can stimulate me. Am I thinking too hard? Are my sights too far above the mountain? Two nights ago I tried cooking, and all I could throw together was Macaroni a la Kraft. Last night, I pulled out that old guitar, and Hot Cross Buns never sounded so pitiful before. So tonight, yeah, I attempt poetry. I whip out my mighty fucking pen and write something, something that could possibly attract a femme. “When I look into your eyes, I melt. I don’t scream your name, rather I belt. Please be my angel, I’ll be your God. Don’t cross me either, or I’ll kill you like a fraud.” The problem with poetry, I can easily see now, is that I have a hard time translating that raw emotion into romance. I take the anger and the loneliness I possess inside of me and I make it into…anger and loneliness. Instead of continuing this travesty, I solve this problem in the form of envoying it straight to the trash. So what is it? What do I have to attract women? How can I romance the opposite sex? Vagina is fucking thrown at Liam, and yet he travels to God damn Portsmouth to find someone who probably won’t even live that long, and me? I couldn’t pick up shit if it fell out of my own ass. What can I do about it? What will I do about it? I think for tonight, I’ll just go get a drink. ~~~~~~~~ For as much as I tried to previously dehumanize or deheart, or whatever you want to say, Liam, I do have a soft spot for him. So much so that when he does think about how beautiful Mandy was the last time he saw her, I can only write about the smile on his face, the one that literally went ear to ear. And she was beautiful. Picture the perfect woman, and then increase her rack and make her about ten times more beautiful, and that was Mandy. And she was smart, too, a real intelligent lady. But, boy was she beautiful. As Liam drove into Portsmouth, he thought about what was going to happen, the type of situation he’d approach. He looked ahead, and knocked on the apartment door. A few solid ones, followed quickly by a door flying open and a beautiful Mandy leaping from the crack and diving onto Liam’s neck. Kiss, kiss, kiss, kiss, ohmyGodivemissedyousomuch, kiss, kiss, imsogladyourehere, kiss, kiss. And the smile widened and widened and widened, and Liam drove and drove, getting closer and closer. The beauty of Mandy was what kept Liam coming back. Liam wasn’t the smartest of guys, and when he could find someone as beautiful as Mandy, he never wanted to let go, even though she did. This is why he’s taking time out of his life, really, because of the memories, because of the reminiscing. Is that a weakness of character? Depending on the past to bring us to do what we do in the present? Or is that a strength, loving someone so much that you’ll end your life temporary to attempt to make her life better? Regardless, Liam kept on driving, that image of Mandy stamped into his brain, his fucking fuel. Motherfucker even ran a red-light or two, just to get there. That beauty…that fucking beauty… ~~~~~~~ I arrive at the bar, a place I’ve never been before, Julio’s. I’d figure I’d meet someone who didn’t know me, someone who could not judge me as that Eagle, y’know? So I stepped into the doorframe, and guess what fucking happens? Silence. It was like out of a fucking movie or something, y’know, where the protagonist walks in and everything stops: the fucking conversations, the fucking music, the fucking drinking, everything, So, there goes trying to make first impressions. Was it that fucking obvious who I was? Was it really? Moments later, though, also out of a fucking movie, the music hit up again and continued. I slowly moved past the frame and slowly approached the bar towards a man, a skinny Mexican guy who I’ll assume was Julio and leaned against the bar. “Old man, how ‘bout a Bud?” His eyes shot towards me while his body stood intact around the noise. He slowly moved closer and closer, his glare growing stronger and stronger, and everything getting something-er and something-er as Julio approached. “Old man? How about you fuckin’ treat me with respect, eh? Just cause you’re Tim Martin…” “Wait, what?” “Don’t think I don’t know who you are, man! Fucking Eagle’s Nest, White Power, all that bullshit…if you try any fucking bullshit here…” “Hey man, I just want a beer!” “And you’ll fucking get one, but if you try anything here…” He trailed off to trail off and fill the glass. Thankfully he was loud enough so only the patrons around me could hear him, but quickly I thought this wasn’t the right place. Could a reputation truly proceed someone THAT much? Could how I act, what I do, where I go, who I killed, etc., really have spread through out Boston that infectiously? I felt sick—no, scratch that, I felt morbid. But, thankfully… “Here’s your fucking beer.” So I grabbed it and I took a big gulp, immediately moving from one end to the bar to another. I situated myself between a drunken black man who was leaning against the bar doing nothing really in particular except be drunk and a slim white girl. The black man was nothing special, and no to be stereotypical, but he looked like all the other black people. Maybe I’ve garnered that opinion and mindset after years and years of praying to the White Lord and all that, but I really couldn’t tell much of a difference between him and any of the other fucking bums. The White girl, though, she was ok. Short hair to about her jaw line, but not dyke-ish, rather feminine actually. Thick red lipstick that complimented the subtle paleness of her skin. She had a simple elegance, a real beautiful type of beauty, the kind of woman I’d like to meet. So I tapped her shoulder and when she turned around, I made sure I had a good smile on, not the creepy “I don’t go out often” smile, but rather the “I’m generally a nice guy” type. “Hey, can I buy you a drink?” “Fuck off.” Wait, what? “Excuse me?” She scoffed and turned away, even though she was in conversation with no other person in the bar. Quickly she turned back, pointing a finger in my face. “You think we don’t know who you are? The only reason you’re not fucking dead is because we don’t want cops in here, ruining our place.” “What the fuck did I do?” So she starts looking around, steaming, until she suddenly stands on the bar. “Excuse me everyone…” It starts to quiet. “Excuse me. This here is Tim Martin, and he’s not sure what he did. Can someone explain it to him?” “YOU FUCKING MURDERER!” “You killed my neighbors!” “Where is my brothers son?” They all kept screaming, moving closer and closer to me as I tried to cower away. I knew what I had done, but I didn’t think it was…was this widespread. Things were opening up though, things were being revealed. My reputation, I guess, was bigger than I. Even in a fucking bar, not even IN South Boston, I was an enemy, I was a fucking sinner. “GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE!” And I abided. No longer did I want to meet women, but instead I wanted to be free. I lost interest in love and instead wanted interest in a new life. I found love in another race with that child, but now I couldn’t find love in my own anymore. The Eagles were no good, as I said before, because I wanted to find someone with substance. But I’ve burnt those bridges. I’ve sealed too many coffins and before I fucking know it a night where I try to find a date turns into a night where I fucking try to cover my ass, not wanting to get fucking blown away. As I left the bar with glasses thrown at me, I had to look at myself on the concrete. Not long before this I’d just been wandering, fucking wandering trying to find a purpose, and here I was again. It seemed I had no chance to find love, and now I had no chance to have stability in a city I used to call home. It’s all so foreign, though; who are these people, these fellow Bostonians of mine whom I’ve hurt so much so often? Have I really dug that many graves? Have I really staked that many crosses into the ground? Instead of finding answers, I ran. I fucking ran to no where and everywhere. And I ran and I ran and I ran. Inside my head, I lost site of trying to find a beau and instead tried to find myself. I was past looking at me as the devil, but more into a phase of looking into someone who wasn’t even there. There was no more Tim Martin, but rather an icon that people can throw rocks at. My name is a fucking novelty now, almost a fucking curse word. Every once in a while I’d stop when I passed a bar (Boston is full of them) and I’d step in the door, wanting to see the reaction. Without fail, it was the same every time. Things would stop, everyone would glare, and I would feel the hatred of a room full of people I in avertedly hurt. And it all fucking made sense, too. Because it wasn’t just thugs I killed, but rather friends and family mates. I never took accountability for my actions, and look where I am now? I’ve made a wall around my Eagle’s Nest, and every time I leave I’m the fucking enemy, I’m the fucking sinner. This time, though, I had no Liam to come home to, but rather a house full of reminders. As I ran the images of the weapons and the nothingness in my apartment just flew by, reminders of what I was. I tried to add depth, I tried to add some sort of attractive ability, but I’ve molded myself. My self, now, was quite simply a killer. And I guess when you’re a killer once, you’re a killer forever. ~~~~~~~~~~~~ Finally, Liam arrives. No bullshit, he’s fucking there, parked right outside the apartment building. He never though he’d make it this far, here again to see his former flame in her last moments, but here he is, parked right out fucking front. He is apprehensive, though, because the image of Mandy’s beauty freezes him. He’s afraid to see that beauty, unsure of how or when or what he’ll do when he sees her. Will he cry, knowing that this could be one of the last few days he’ll see her? Or will he smile, with that being the only reaction someone as beautiful as Mandy could possibly do to him? His mind is fucking racing, doing laps around his brain faster and faster until he finally forces his hand to open the door and his feet to step out, cheap-ass box of chocolates in hand. The scene replays in his head: the hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man.It’s out of a fucking movie, as I’ve declared many times before, but that’s how Liam thinks when he thinks about Mandy. That beauty can only be found in the fiction world, in a place where life can be molded and formed to whatever the writer wants. Liam only imagines Mandy as perfection. At the door, he buzzes her name, immediately being allowed in. The building itself was not what he expected: it’s large, rather immersive if that makes any sense whatsoever, not really that inviting at all. The floors and walls are only off in color by a shade, but they both blend together by the grime smeared across it. Any other state other than New Hampshire and you’d be bound to find crack heads littered all over the place, not sure of their own identity anymore. But this is fucking New Hampshire, so the crack heads have the decency of not existing. Regardless, the scene continues to play over and over: The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. The hand hits the door, the door opens, the women jumps on man. It was on loop, accompanying every fucking step Liam took, every fucking breath, every little fucking bit. Finally, he was there, apartment 213. Right out of a fucking movie, he slaps the door with his knuckles lightly. *knock, knock, knock* Nothing for a good long bit, but from the other side of the door Liam could hear her approaching. The film was fucking perfect as the steps got closer and closer until they stopped. The peep hole was filled with darkness, and the handle turned. The door opens… “Are you Liam?” And it’s not Mandy. It’s not her by a long shot. It’s so far off from her that I could say it was a five-foot-five black woman and be about 100% correct. What fucking film was this? “Uh, yeah, um, yeah, that’s me.” “C’mon in here.” She opened the door up wide and in Liam walked, and I’ll fucking tell you, he was not expecting this. The place was fucking barren. Decorations and shit, not a fucking one, just the basics, and even then it was sparse. There was a couch that faced nothing in particular which nicely matched the foldable table used as a dining area. It seemed like she just moved in, that’s for fucking sure, but Mandy specifically told Liam that she’s been living there for a time now…what the… “Mandy been expectin’ you.” The black woman had sass and a Caribbean accent that gave her a “Don’t you fuck with me” attitude that made sure Liam didn’t fuck with her, not one bit. He moved around with hips that shot back and fourth from the over abundance of wait that pressed down on them. Liam could guess very easily that… “I’m Mandy’s nurse, Karen. Nice to meet cha.” Politely a hand was extended, even though Liam still didn’t remain fond of the black race. But he was not one to be rude, not by a long shot, especially to someone who cared for the one he loved. Speaking of which… “I’m sorry, I don’t want to cut down on pleasantries, but do you…can you…” “She in there.” The finger pointed Liam’s head towards the only door in the place, other than the bathroom one. The door was shoot and underneath it there was no light. It was almost as if the door led to hell or something (straight out of a fucking movie, right?), scaring Liam to the point where he almost swayed away from it. He eyed it for a moment. What the… But this was no time for apprehension or any of that bullshit. He moved towards the door, knocking on it, but this was no fucking movie as the door slid open. Liam peaked in and through the darkness she whispered his name. “Liam? Is that you?” Instead of answering, he turned on the light… ~~~~~~~~~ I finally stopped running when I got to Boston Common. I shouldn’t have been here, especially not this late, as the Common was a place where the fags fucked and the killers killed and the druggies drugged. It was for the dirt, that’s for sure, but it was the one place where I knew there wasn’t an Eagle to bother me. I couldn’t return home, no fucking way, so I walked through the park. To be honest, though, I wasn’t walking through there to be scared, but rather I wanted to look at the pond. I don’t know if it’s famous all around the world, or even in most parts of the states, but if you’re from Massachusetts, and especially the Boston area, you know what the Boston Common park is known for, and it’s those fruity Swan Boats people love to ride, and the swans too. You always see the tourists and the shitty assholes and the rich people riding them, escaping the world of the real city and escaping the truths of the city and going on these swans so they can say to their friends “Oh yes, I love Boston, because you can shop on Newbury Street and shop at Fanieul Hall and ride the swans at Boston Common because they’re so pretty”, in which the friends inevitably say “Oh yes, Boston is such a beautiful place, isn’t it? I love it, oh yes, deary, I love it so.” Right now, though, the boats are docked and the moon slaps against the water with a sharpness that slaps back in my face as I stand looking into it. My city, my place where I live, has rejected me. My people, the people I live with, look at me as a killer. So here I am, with the only other thing in the city that has another meaning, which is the pond. I guess I’m trying to make a stupid analogy, but I take solace in being here with the pond. During the day, the pond is littered with pricks and foreign people, people who don’t appreciate it. They take it for granted, they treat it like a novelty. And the pond is littered with the trash that doesn’t fucking deserve it, the kind of people who should go back to their house in the Hamptons or their old Victorian on Chestnut Hill. At night, though, you won’t see those fucking bums, but rather the real bums. The people who sleep on the benches, serenaded by the serenity of a pond at peace. The fags who fuck each other, only lit by the moon on a clear night, otherwise fornicating in darkness. The washed up druggies who slowly die in the bushes, trying to find a life that doesn’t exist. And the killers, the guys like me who go to the pond because there is no other place to go. When the sun rises and the Swan boats ride again, the druggies will find an alley and the fags will go back to their lives and the bums will continue to beg and I and the other killers will go back to what we do. (I can’t speak for the other killers but I know that I fucking have a job) Our habitat is ruined by the fucking assholes who mask their own lives with a pond that rejects them, a pond that is meant for the dirt but lives because of the clean. Do I ever go by the pond during the day? Nah, never. It’s not the same. Because when the fakeness isn’t around and the moon is all that can compliment it, that’s when it’s at its best, that’s when I can look at the pond and associate myself with it. But nobody knows the pond for the darkness, and nobody knows me as that worker, apparently. ~~~~~~~ As the light flickered on, Liam’s face gazed upon the face of beauty. But the face of beauty was not there. The face of Mandy had disappeared and replacing her was someone else. Replacing those perfectly squinting eyes were sullen ones. Replacing that elegant tone of skin was paleness. And replacing that smile was a frown, a frown that was only more depressing with the grayness that made the lips. They barely moved when she spoke. “Liam…it’s been so long.” But this was out of a fucking movie, right? So Liam didn’t run away, Liam didn’t obey his instincts, and instead he moved in. He moved close to the bed, because in a fucking movie you never judge by looks, you never look at the down and kick them, at least if you’re the protagonist. So Liam moved in, he moved close, he moved with a smile. “Mandy…” He was at a lost of words. Only days before she sounded so healthy, so pure. Only days before she sounded like a trillion billion fucking gagillion bucks. But now? She looked…she looked… “You look great.” She spoke to break up the silence and to push that single tear out of Liam’s eye. But it was going to fall anyways. Liam sat next to her, pulling a chair up close. “Thank you…thank you for coming up…” “I’d have it no other way…” And the scene fades to darkness, because this is a fucking movie. Why leave you with a sour fucking note? Because Liam is a killer? Because Liam is that rotten character I described earlier? The only problem with that is that I would never do this to Mandy, I would never, ever make her sicker than she already is. While Liam is despicable, Mandy is an angel, and I’d never, ever fucking kick an angel when it’s down.
