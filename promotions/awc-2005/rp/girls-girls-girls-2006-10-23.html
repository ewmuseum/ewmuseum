Girls Girls Girls

23 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/06 The women's liberation movement has ingrained into our heads that women are equals of men. Over the years, they've made strides in making society believe it. Susan B. Anthony and the suffrage movement made the chauvinstic male society realize that women were intellectually up to snuff with the men. Billie Jean King proved that they were athletically able to compete with the men. While Gerald Riggs was hardly the paragon of male tennis superstardom, women were galvanized. If they could beat a man in a sport as rigorous as tennis, they could surely stand toe to toe with them in other arenas. Police, firefighting, golf... even wrestling. Especially wrestling. The proliferation of women in the wrestling business was extreme. All over the world of wrestling, females were competing with men. Lindsay Troy was the World Champion of Empire Pro Wrestling and held the PRIME Tag Team Championships. Karina Wolfenden held the Primetime Central Global Championship. The Atlantic Wrestling Club itself had so many women under contract. Aimz was victorious in the last pay-per-view main event for the Club. Ellis Nash has held the Frontier Championship. And Teresa Tomas... 'Admit it, you beat it to the magazine cover...' Dogan's flipness was intended to bait Suleimon. The Captain only grunted. 'C'mon man, she was hot, and I thought I saw a nipple.' 'You're incorrigible.' 'Incorrigible? Man, I think you're gay. Why don't you like naked women on the front of a magazine?' Suleimon rolled his eyes. 'You're a piece of work. She's degrading the business, using her floppy pancake bosoms instead of her wrestling talent, of which she has none, to promote herself.' 'Dude, you know she was forced to...' 'Bah. You're the one always harping on free will. She could have...' 'Just sit back and enjoy the tits.' 'Grrr...' No, Suleimon wasn't a fan of women wrestlers at all... Tariq's time in the hardcore division brought a lot of local notoreity, and more importantly, money, to Liberty Bell Wrestling. Out in the ring, Tariq, as Captain Suleimon, became among the most hated heels on the roster, drawing boos and thrown trash in his direction almost every second of every match and mic spot he was doing. His heat was nuclear, as they'd say in the back. Speaking of 'in the back,' Tariq was becoming among the most respected men in the company. Despite his hardcore style, he never hurt people excessively. Well, except for one Lonnie Jackson, but he was gone now. That was another thing most people in the back were grateful for. He made the backwards redneck make like a tree and leave. James Hopkins, of course, loved him for the money part. His mentor Rahman was becoming more and more impressed with his pupil's ringwork by the passing card. It was about a year now since Tariq first won the Hardcore Championship, eleven months to be exact, and you'd think he'd be happy. He was in a way. He had nothing to be sad or angry about, but he was getting bored. Same thing, day in and day out. Smack someone in the head with a chair, choke someone with a chain and maybe on a special occasion, he'd high-stick someone in a Broad Street Bully match. It was... monotonous. Hours before he was set to take on Sgt. Bloodinguts in a Boot Camp Match, Tariq sat in his locker room. His head was in his hands, not because he was sad, but because he had faced Bloodinguts twice before, once in a Hardcore Championship match where Suleimon defeated him with an Arabian facebuster, and in a non-title tag match where Bloodinguts pinned Tariq's partner, Billhelm Morris, after Tariq missed on an Arabian facebuster. He wondered if he'd have to pull the facebuster out again, and that's when he just threw his head back and sighed. James just happened to be in the doorway when that happened. He came in to tell Tariq that he was up for an impromptu mic spot to build his match, but he couldn't help but notice Tariq's outward signs of malaise. 'Hey there, Champ.' Tariq turned around, slightly startled. 'Oh, hello... uh... how long have you been standing there?' 'For the last five minutes.' 'Oh...' 'Yeah, I saw you with your head in your hands. Something wrong?' 'Well, uh...' Tariq didn't dare answer James truthfully. 'C'mon, you can tell me. C'mon, did someone give you a concussion?' Tariq shook his head. 'Did you have a run-in with a fan?' 'No, sir.' 'Are you getting... bored?' Tariq looked up in shock. 'Oh, you won't get in trouble if you say yes. I mean, you have been doing the same thing for the better part of the last year. I might be a little bored too. In fact, I've been thinking of moving you up a little bit.' Tariq perked up. 'Oh yeah?' 'Yeah... I was thinking maybe I'd bring you up to the tag team division.' 'Oh...' Tariq thought. He remembered back to his last tag match. 'I wouldn't be teaming with Billhelm again, would I?' 'Oh no, no, I've got a new recruit that I think'd be perfect with you.' 'Oh... okay. I won't be stripped of my title, will I?' 'It's up to you.' Tariq smirked. The next day, after yet another successful defense of his Hardcore Championship, Tariq paced his locker room. He was excited to meet his new tag partner. Rahman walked in the locker room, and Tariq jumped. 'Oh, it's only you.' 'What? Of course it's me. Who were you expecting?' 'James with my new tag team partner.' 'Oh... yes, he was telling me about that. I think you'll be a little surprised at his choice.' 'Well, as long as he's not a scrub.' 'Heh... he.' 'What was that?' Rahman didn't answer. He just went into the corner and lit up a cigarette. Tariq went back to pacing again. After twenty more minutes, someone else came into the locker room. She was a gorgeous brunette, wearing a tanktop and jeans. 'Hey... are you... Tariq?' 'Yes... and who might you be, you lovely lady.' 'Charming, aren't you? I'm going to like teaming with you.' 'Quite forward asking me out on a date when I don't even know your name...' 'Tariq, she's not asking you out on a date,' James said as he was walking in the room. 'She's our newest recruit.' 'Oh, are we starting a women's division?' 'No, silly,' she said. 'I'm wrestling with the men.' 'Huh?' Tariq looked dumbfounded. 'Yeah Tariq. We're capitalizing on Chyna's popularity in the WWE.' 'Yeah, but she's the size of a man! This woman... she's... she's...' 'My name is Clarisse, and I can hold my own with the men. I did it in the academy...' 'C'mon, Tariq, give it a chance. She's going to play a Jane Fonda type gimmick, an American who sides with the enemies of the country.' Rahman got up from the corner. 'C'mon Tariq, it'll be a challenge.' Clarisse got up into Rahman's face. 'What do you mean a challenge, old man? Are you telling me I can't go with the men?' 'Uhh...' 'Guys,' James interjected, 'Let's just be civil. C'mon, let's go and discuss things.' Tariq looked at Rahman, but his mentor shrugged his shoulders. Clarisse tugged on Tariq's shirt. 'C'mon! James said we're going to be booked on the next card!' Tariq muffled his sigh so that his new partner didn't have to hear him. Later that day, Tariq and Clarisse were training in the ring, going over some basic double-team moves, sparring with a local jobber named Biff. 'Alright, now let's try the double back body drop. I'm going to whip Biff off the ropes, then we'll do a synchronized toss. Alright?' 'Yeah, sure, I've done this all the time in school.' 'Yes, I know, but this is done at a different speed. It's much faster.' 'Chill out, man. I got it.' Tariq rolled his eyes then whipped Biff off the ropes. As Biff bounced off the ropes, he assumed the position. Clarisse tried to, but she tripped over her own shoelace. She fell forward, distracting Tariq enough so that Biff could knock him over with a shoulder block. Tariq got up indignantly and stormed over to a still recovering Clarisse. 'What the hell was that!?' 'I... I...' 'You have to focus! I told you this was a different speed than school!' 'But... but...' A single tear began to stream down her face, rolling like a boulder off the side of a mountain. She began to sniffle, holding back the floodgates. 'You... you... don't know what it's like... being different. Not having people respect you or take you seriously...' Another tear rolled down her face as she collapsed to a seated position. A look of remorse took to the face of the young Turk. 'Hey... that's not true,' Tariq said as his thoughts went back to his days under the Imam's tutelage, when he was ostracized for not being a practicing Muslim. 'I used to have to deal with not being respected. It sucks, I know...' Tariq knelt down to look Clarisse in the face. He raised her chin up so that her eyes met his, and then he wiped the tear from her face. 'Listen, I'll help you. I'll help you gain respect. You just have to focus.' Clarisse nodded as Tariq, showing the characteristics of the finest Parisian gentlemen, helped her to her feet. 'Alright, let's try it again.' Tariq whipped Biff off the ropes again, and this time, they nailed the move in unison, as beautifully as you see on television. 'Good job,' he said as he turned to the brunette. 'I can see you've got it in you. Let's try it again.' They practiced the move and several others for the rest of the afternoon. Finally, it was Saturday again, time for another LBW card at the New Alhambra Arena, and Tariq and Clarisse were ready for their first match as a tag team. They'd be taking on Sgt. Bloodinguts and Emilio Josias in a grudge match. The tag partners sat in their locker room. Clarisse, who'd changed in the privacy of the women's changing room, was putting the finishing touches on her 'Janet Hollywood' outfit. Glitter was very hard to apply. Tariq looked over to her and smiled. They had worked hard together for the entire week, and Tariq felt she came so far. As much as he was skeptical when he first saw he was teaming with a woman, he felt confident. 'Are you ready to show the world what you can do?' Clarisse got up and stood tall. 'You're damn right I am.' Tariq smiled. James shortly came into the locker room. 'You guys are up in five. Knock 'em dead!' Captain Suleimon and Janet Hollywood were introduced first to heavy heel heat. With Rahman, who had been accompanying Tariq as his manager for the last couple of months, waving the Turkish flag and Clarisse taunting the males by saying 'I only sleep with Hamas freedom fighters,' the trash raining down had become nearly merciless. That was, until Josias and Bloodinguts made their entrances. Bloodinguts was a towering man. Six feet, six inches and a solid 270 pounds. Josias wasn't as big, but people pegged him as the next Evan Aho or Chris Benoit, a technical marvel the likes of which most people couldn't match. The contest started off with an air of tension. This was the fourth time that Bloodinguts and Tariq had faced off in some form. Still though, the Sergeant decided against wrestling first and deferred to Josias. Tariq, the consummate competitor, of course went first for his team. Whatever notions the crowd had about Josias dismantling the Turk were laid to rest early on as Tariq took him off guard with several high-impact strikes. After a missle dropkick came within microns of striking his temple, Josias leapt over to the corner for Bloodinguts, who, not so surprisingly, didn't fare any better. Tariq kept him off-guard like he had in previous encounters. This was turning out to be a mismatch. Tariq was taking care of business when he heard a female voice calling behind him. 'Hey Tariq! Tag me in!' Tariq turned around and saw Clarisse with a look of fiery competitive passion in her eyes. He thought, 'Why not,' and tagged her in. He then whipped Bloodinguts off the ropes, and just like they had in practice, Tariq and Clarisse nailed him with the double back body drop. The arena was booing them to high hell, but Tariq smiled at her right after he went back to the apron. Sensing the pride from her partner, Clarisse looked to capitalize. She went to deliver a scoop slam to Bloodinguts, when suddenly, she couldn't budge him. She tried again, but then held her back. Tariq was holding his head in embarrassment. Rahman shook his head on the outside and the crowd laughed at her, a 158 pound woman trying to body slam a man 112 pounds heavier. To add injury to the insult, Bloodinguts leveled her with a lariat that would have put down Godzilla. The rest of the match was a blur for Tariq. The confident, cool and talented woman he was training with for the last week was looking like a defenseless spousal abuse victim. All her counters were sniffed out and snuffed out before she could realize them. She was being tossed like a rag doll around the ring. And every time that Tariq tried to help her, Josias or Bloodinguts, whichever one wasn't working over Ms. Hollywood, would intercept him post haste. The coup de grace came when Josias had Clarisse in a Mexican surfboard stretch. Tariq rushed into the ring, only to be blindsided by a Bloodinguts clothesline that slumped him to the canvas. From his half-groggy view on the mat, he saw out of hte corner of his eye Clarisse nodding her head to the ref's question of 'Do you give?' The bell rung, and the match was over. Tariq and Clarisse lost the match, but Tariq ended up losing a lot more than that. Back in the locker room after the card was over, Tariq sat in the corner, drinking bottled water and shaking his head, thinking about how he was utterly tricked by his tag team partner into believing she wasn't some two-bit scrub. Rahman walked in about fifteen minutes after the last match was over. 'Tough break out there, kid.' 'Shove it. The only tough break is that I had to team with a pair of ovaries.' 'C'mon now. That was her first match in anything above training, man. You can't expect her...' 'I can't expect her to hold up her end of the bargain, that's what. Women can't wrestle with the men, I'm convinced.' 'After seeing only one in action?' 'You only have to see one. She was helpless, and don't give me the excuse that it was her first match. I've seen plenty of newbie men go out there and fare one million times better than she did.' 'But that's only one person. Trust me, there are plenty of men who flounder like that.' 'No, there aren't. She looked good in practices... but she folded up. She knew she couldn't slam Bloodinguts, but she tried anyway. She got a big head about her, thinking she could hang with the men like other men do. She got caught up... and that's why she'll always ultimately fail, just like every other woman.' Rahman shook his head. 'Well, that's your opinion, but I'm telling you, she's not representative of all the women. I mean, I've seen some footage of this Lindsay Troy, and she looks like she could be World Champion some day.' 'Pfft...' 'Fine, you think what you want to think. I admit women are weaker physically, but they can compete. Just give her another chance.' The words went in one of Tariq's ears and right out the other. Tonight, he was convinced that women could never last in a men's ring. Clarisse, in his mind, wasn't a promising youngster. She didn't know a damn thing of what she was doing. She was a liability. She belonged in the kitchen. Tariq was the last one out of the arena that night. In the cold air of the Philadelphia autumn, he could see his breath in the flourescent brightness of the floodlights. He also saw the figure of a woman waiting for him.' 'Hi Tariq...' Clarissa stepped forward, head down. She raised her head up slowly. 'Listen, I'm sorry for costing you the match tonight... I guess I'm not ready for primetime after all, eh?' She chuckled, but no reaction from Tariq. 'Look, I feel really bad. I told James I'm not ready and I'm gonna go back to training so you can get another tag team partner to move up. I feel that's the least I could do.' Tariq nodded. 'Well, aren't you going to say anything?' He felt like saying 'Don't go back to training. Just go fetch me a sandwich,' but held his tongue. 'I'm sorry for overreacting.' 'Naw, it's okay. I shouldn't have pushed so hard to get in your corner. I just thought you were such a good wrestler... I wanted to be a part of your life that way.' 'Well, you can still be a part of my life... just not as my tag partner. But if you wanted to share a sandwich with me...' 'I'd love that.' Tariq smiled. While the thought that women made shitty wrestlers held fast in his mind and loomed large, he certainly didn't think they were useless. They were good for plenty of other things. Like sharing a sandwich from Tony Luke's at midnight.
