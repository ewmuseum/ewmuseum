Friendless

6 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th Dec 2005 Sheffield, England. Bomber’s current place of residence when back home, after buying a house at the Meadowbank area of Sheffield, well the Sheffield/Rotherham border. Bomber looked at the time, 10:03am it read, Bomber turned over wanting to fall back asleep but knew he couldn’t, he had a full day of preparing for his match against Red Rock, and he wasn’t prepared to throw it away. In previous years Bomber would have either thrown his preparation time away by either getting drunk, staying in bed, or staying in bed with a hot woman. But Bomber had changed since then, this was a new Bomber, an angry Bomber that had obvious hatred for Red Rock, maybe if it had been anybody else he would have stayed in bed but no, not today. Bomber crawled to the side of the bed and sat up, wiping the sleep away from his eyes and then stretching. He got to his feet and went into the bathroom, he looked into the mirror and said to himself “You have to beat Red Rock”. He did, he knew he did. He turned the tap on and proceeded to wash, and afterwards brush his teeth. He made his way back to the bedroom and got out his clothes, a bag, and his wrestling gear, ready for tonight. He took out a pair of jeans and an AWC t-shirt to wear now, and his traditional jean-shorts, his elbow and knee pads and boots to wrestle in tonight. Bomber: That should be ok. Bomber quite often talked to himself, he had nobody in his life, they had all abandoned him after he had ‘killed’ himself over a month ago, his daughter, his ex, and his friends. He had to make a new start, ever since his return last week he had tried ringing his daughter and ex but they wanted nothing to do with him, claiming it was all a sick stunt, and he only thought of himself, as ever instead of thinking of his family and friends. They were right, and he knew it but he was too stubborn to admit it. He put his gear in his bag and got dressed. He headed towards the rear of the house to the drive where he had parked his car, he was meeting with an old friend to try and patch things up after the stunt Bomber had pulled. He locked up and headed to the car, pressing the button to disable the alarm, and then as he got to the car unlocked it via the central locking. He climbed in and put his bag onto the passenger seat and started up the car, was Bomber going to patch things up or was he just gonna get a punch in the face for his troubles? He pulled up outside a café in the centre of Sheffield and parked up, he put his bag under the passenger seat and got out the car, he locked it and entered the grubby café. An old woman was stood behind the counter, staring at Bomber. Waitress: What can I get for you? “What a welcome” Bomber thought to himself, no hello, or how are you or anything, just what do you want? He took a seat across from his old friend and offered to shake his hand but the guy just shook his head. Bomber: Two coffee’s here please love. Bomber looked at his friend, looking for forgiveness? Looking for an argument? Only Bomber could answer that. Bomber: Come on, talk to me man, I am sorry about what happened but I just needed time off, and time away from everything. Man: You could have told me, I thought we was good mates and you screw me over like that. Some friend you are. Bomber: Look I am sorry, I just couldn’t tell anyone, not even my own daughter knew. She has disowned me, along with everyone else, you’re the only person I got left now, and I really do need you. The man just looked at Bomber, Bomber was obviously sorry for what had happened but his friend wasn’t buying it. Bomber: I got a big match against Red Rock later, and I need to talk to someone about it. Like I said, your all I got left and I need you mean, so come on, help a brother out here. Man: Listen Bomber, you fucked all this up, you could have sent me a text message, or called me to say you was fine and it would have gone no further but no, you obviously don’t trust me either, so I hope Red Rock kicks your ass tonight, you deserve it and for all I care… he can kill you! The waitress brings over the coffees and the man throws a cup of coffee at Bomber. Man: When I say I hope you get killed, I mean it, go to hell Bomber. Bomber jumps around, scalded by the coffee. Bomber: Asshole!
