Hotel

29 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-06 A thin cloud of smoke filled the small hotel room. Teresa sat on the bed with a remote to the television in her hand. She continuously pressed the channel button finding for the fifth time that there was nothing on the box to watch while subconsciously hoped that perhaps one more round through the channels that some miraculous movie would appear. Two armed guard were posted outside her room. Due to the current circumstances, she had to be both guarded and protected while continuing her duties in the ring. The men were of medium build and more or less rent-a-cops with guns. Teresa could easily take both of them down if she wanted, but with a possible murder conviction in the near future, she previously declined the temptation and allowed them to think they had the upper hand. As she finished her Winston Light, she thought about the man who had bailed her out. Her brother Eric had called him. Why, she couldn’t understand. The two men never did get along. What was really troubling her was why the man agreed to all this. He not only bailed her out, but also paid for a lawyer. Teresa smashed the cigarette but in a small ash tray that sat on the nightstand beside the bed and tossed the remote aside. As she looked around, the four walls seemed as though they were caving in on her. She had to get out. It’s been a week now since she’s had a drop of booze and the withdraws gnawed at her nerves like a termite on wood. Outside the door, two uniformed bodies are drug into a mechanical closet. A pair of steel-toe work boots treaded casually across the hall and into another room. Teresa opened the door of her room expecting to have to knock the two guards out with her fist, but find neither is at their assigned posts. She looked to her right and left and saw only an empty hallway. Teresa found this odd but to her advantage and stepped out and shut the door quietly behind her. Across the hall, a door to a room opened. A pair of green eyes watched AWC’s redneck princess step into an elevator and notes she forgot to lock the door. ======-----==== “And what do you think you’re doing?” The familiar voice filled the elevator. Teresa and the man were the only ones inside and at this moment she knew she was busted. Her current escort/bodyguard/what have you had returned with a sack full of groceries. He glared down at her. Their reunion hadn’t been a pleasant one and he wondered why he even agreed to help her out. Her baby blue eyes answered that question as she looked up at him. This caused his own features to soften and he released a deep sigh. “I know you need some fresh air and that room is killing you, but you have to remember your hearing and the court order for you to be under house arrest until after the trial.” “I know Scott, but I can’t help it. I haven’t had one beer since my arrest and it’s kicking my ass” “Maybe you should use it as means to quit drinking all-together. I mean you’re not as efficient in the ring as you used to be. I bet if all that alcohol wasn’t in your system, you’d do a lot better.” “Yeah yeah, and why ain’t you still in the ring? “I retired. Something you might want to consider.” Ummhmm. Screw you.” The elevator door opens and the two step out returning to Teresa’s room. The door is locked and Teresa used her key to unlock it. They stepped inside. Scott walked to the small kitchenette and set the sack of groceries on the counter. Teresa remained at the doorway. “Scott, this ain’t right. I left the light on before I left and didn’t you notice the guards are gone?” Scott stared at Teresa. He had not noticed the missing guards. The color drained from his sun tanned face once he saw the glimmer of a barrel and the figure that held it. He then spoke in a calm voice. “Teresa, duck now.” The figure was dressed in black. His dark green eyes glistened in the darkness of the hotel room. They held surprise once fallen on Scott then narrowed. The figure obviously wasn’t expecting him to return so soon. No bother. It only meant he must die as well. His finger rested on the trigger as he made a direct aim for the back of Teresa’s head. Teresa hesitated but ducked her head just as Scott threw an apple direct toward her head. She didn’t know what was going on until she heard the ear-piercing gun fire; the bullet missing the top of her head by an inch and embedding in the wall across the room and the loud groan behind her. She swung around; saw the intruder staggering backwards holding his head. He held a pistol in the other hand. She gave him a violent knee into his midsection fierce enough that made Scott wince. The intruder cursed loudly nearly collapsed to his knees. His hand reached for something to break his fall and found the door. The door swung open with is weight and he fell out into the hall. Scott starts toward Teresa but he wasn’t quick enough. She grabbed the intruder’s gun and ran after the man. By the time she got out the door, the intruder was gone. “What the hell Teresa? Who was that?” “I don’t know. Come on. We gotta get out of here. I don’t know who it is, but someone obviously has a price on my head.” Teresa turned around to find Scott on the phone. She was visibly shaking. This stuff only happened in the books right? At least in the books, the good guys always come out on top except with a few casualties. She swallows a lump scared to think who else could be killed before this nightmare is over. “What the hell are you doing?” “Calling the cops. I hate to say this but maybe a jail is safer for you.” Teresa a deep sigh and dropped the intruder's gun. With her prints all over the weapon, her case would only get worse. She cursed her stupidity and shut the door.
