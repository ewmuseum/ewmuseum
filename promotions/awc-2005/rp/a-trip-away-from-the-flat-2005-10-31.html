A trip away from the flat

31 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st october 2005 We find ourselves in a forest in the middle of no where, it is dark and the moon is full the stars are shining and nothing is to be seen, the fur trees stand tall and there are rocks and the floor and hollowed out logs, with squirrels, badgers and other forest animals humping each other. It is quiet a little TOO quite (other than the sound of hot badger love) no one is around the wind whistles through the trees and it gets colder… then all of a sudden there is a rustling sound coming from a large bush, what is it? Is it a bear or a yeti a fat woman? Oh my god here it comes…. Oh its Red Rock?! With his very good friends (and family) Boolie, D'avid, Dad , and Uncle Terry. Boolie is loosing his patients because his trousers got bird shit on them has and Uncle Terry is near to tears because he just broke up with his girl friend of 3 years. They look like they are lost in the woods and for a long time. They stop in the described spot and Boolie goes to sit on the hollow log and it breaks so he gets up and boots a tree and the rest find a comfortable rock to sit on Uncle Terry: I’m telling you its her fault we’re lost here Red Rock: How is it your ex-girl friends fault we got lost? Uncle Terry: Well if she didn’t dump me like a used tampon Boolie wouldn’t of suggested we got for a calm walk in the woods D'avid wouldn’t of lost the hunting guns I wouldn’t of lost the map and you wouldn’t of had the rock land on your hand! Red Rock: I can see your point but I don’t think its your girl friends fault I thinks YOUR FAULT FOR LOOSING THE MAP! D'avid: Hey I’m glad I lost those shooting irons, heh. Red Rock: Oh do shut up you ginger bastard! A rather shaken Boolie buts in. Boolie: Well I do think it you should all shut up because I don’t like being lost in the woods I think we should try and find our way out of here before we die or something Dad sarcastically adds in a pretend Mr T-esq voice Dad: No shit...fool Red Rock: Ok which way do we go from here? Boolie points carelessly in to no particular direction Boolie: that way Red Rock: OH HELPFUL! Dad: Ok lets spin this bottle and we’ll go that way and see what happens Dad spins the bottle and it points in the general direction of left Red Rock: ok left it is! D'avid: hey don’t you have that match today red? Red Rock: ah gash! you're right I am lost in the Middle of a forest some where! Ha ha im going to have a great time lying my way out of this one! Dad: Well lets get moving. Red Rock and his band of merry men the walk to the left and the first thing they see is a high way Red Rock: bloody hell a motor way Boolie: YES, YES YES I’M SAVED thank you Jesus ! Dad: calm down big guy we ain’t saved yet we don’t know where we are do we Uncle Terry: I’m telling you its that sleazy whore’s fault! Red Rock: what actually happened between you and you girl friend? Uncle Terry with a subtle feeling of relief over the fact someone has taken interest in his bitter ramblings Uncle Terry: weeell I came home one night and I grabbed a beer.. well I didn’t because they were not there, so I went up stairs and I caught her banging that skinny Japanese bloke from the chemists Boolie: did you kick his arse? Uncle Terry: yeah I kicked his ass! I beat him till he cried and stole his money and threw him out of the house naked! Then I left and came to Boolie and Red’s house! Red Rock: Interesting story but we have to get to to the arena damn it! Dad: that didn't happen at all did it Terry? Uncle Terry: ... Dad: wow you i never known someone with so many emotion issues Uncle Terry hangs his head in shame as Dad shakes his head in disapproval Red Rock: you know that’s all great and every thing but how are we going to get to the arena? Boolie: Call a taxi? Red Rock looks for his mobile phone and can’t find it and begins frantically patting his body trying to feel it Red Rock: arse I appear to have lost my phone! Boolie jumps in with a rather helpful observation Boolie: Hey look a service station! Maybe it has a phone! Dad : Come on I’m hungry we can get some eats! The merry men cross the road narrowly missed by several cars and they come up to they service station, it is a little run down the pumps are a little rusty and the posters advertising their deals are peeling off and there is a guy smoking by one of the pumps wearing a navy cardigan and brown trousers and a tweed flat cap, they walk into the shop and inside it has old food well past its sell by date, and a store clerk wearing a sweat stained shirt with the humour slogan 'i am sparticus' he is fat and stinky, Red Rock approaches him Red Rock: erm excuse me can we use your phone? the clerk licks doritos cheese from his stubby fat geek fingers Clerk: NO paying customers only! Uncle Terry: Look Buddy! I have just broke up with my girl friend and I have been lost for 6 hours in a forest and I think you don’t want to piss me off! Dad : and he's likely to piss his pants! Clerk: Oh dear what a pity never mind paying customers only! Uncle Terry: hey don’t make me hurt you man! Clerk: yeah right! Try it punk i got numchucks under this desk and reflexes like a ninja! Meanwhile Red Rock and Boolie confer over to who to contact to pick them up. Boolie: Who can we call to save us? Red Rock: God knows...erm...doesn't your mum live around here? Boolie: aww but my mum is a cock! Red Rock dials the phone number for Boolie's mum and shoves the phone in his hand whilst in the background Uncle Terry pulls the ninja clerk over the desk and get him in a head lock whilst the clerk screeches and attempts to pull Uncle Terry's hair Boolie: Hey mum, we're stuck in the middle of no where come pick us up Boolie's Mum: No you little bastard you're 24 years old why cant you learn to drive? Boolie: Mum, I can drive! Red Rock buts in and grabs Boolie's attention. Red Rock: What's she saying? Boolie whispers back. Boolie: She's being a bit difficult.... Red Rock: GOD! are you serious? i have to get to the arena for a wrestling match Boolie's Mum: What? you're still doing that queer 'sport'. Red Rock snatches the phone from Boolie and bellows down with a great sound of frustration in his voice. Red Rock: GOD! just come pick us up Red Rock slams down the phone Red Rock: Frickin' idiot! Dad: Ok Uncle Terry you can stop bitch fighting with that guy now. Uncle Terry and the clerk's hands flail around trying to grab hair or slap, with the odd attempt at a punch Uncle Terry: Just a minute, I got him! Red Rock: *sigh* Ok we’ll wait out side for you Red Rock and his good friends go out side and wait for his parents after a while Uncle Terry comes out of the shop with finger nail scratch marks and bite marks on his arms obviously feeling the true wrath of a full time internet user.
