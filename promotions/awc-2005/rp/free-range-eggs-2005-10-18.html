FREE Range Eggs

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th October 2005 We find ourselves down an unfamiliar street where we've never been before. The focal point appears to be an elegant large family home. Its got a long drive with big white walls and steel gates with hanging baskets and perfectly mowed lawns the house is big and white with several windows and a big wooden door with a small case of steps with expensive sculptures standing either side of the door. Suddenly we're now looking at a LESS than elegant house, which isn’t so glamorous it the grass is patchy and mud and a wooden porch with a woman with too much make up a pink crop top a pair of tight faded jeans and big gold hoop ear rings sits there scouring. The windows are dirty and the roof is made of rust damaged tin and the gutters are falling off and dripping, it also has sparrows in the gutter throwing bits of moss out of it to try and make a nest but then the gutter falls and the bird flies away. The woman jumps up and opens the rickety wooden door and goes inside and an argument breaks out between the woman and what sounds like her husband. After a few smashing noises and “ get out you ain’t no good for me” the husband comes out of the house wearing a black best and black jeans covering his head running out and he sits on the porch and puts his head in his hands. At this point a black van pulls up and it is, of course Boolie's black van and with him is none other than Red Rock! The van chugs to a halt as fume exhume from the back in a thick black sooty cloud. The doors clunk open and Red Rock and Boolie step out. Boolie: Uncle Terry, you know you have a few problems with your spouse don’t you. Uncle Terry: What? Boolie? What are you doing here? Red Rock: I don't think it's a good time... Uncle Terry: Nah we’ll clean it up later I think I’ll give her some time to cool off she is always tense like this when it get to that time of the month if you know what I mean Red Rock: Err... Boolie: Um... Uncle Terry: You know, she's got a Jam donut. Red Rock: ... Boolie: ... Uncle Terry: GOD you idiots, shes on her fucking period! Red Rock: OH! Jam DOnut I get it! So...she sounds pretty pissed off. Uncle Terry: Well I came home a little late only to discover she was sleeping with that porn star Russel the Love Muscle who lives in that nice place across the road. Boolie: Err...so why is she pissed at you? Uncle Terry: Well you know what women are like, they think “oh it's only a guy they don't have feelings” and they manage to twist everything around so it's your fault...stupid bitch... Red Rock: Sounds to me Terry, that you need to get the lawyers involved. Uncle Terry: Nah I love my wife, but I hate that BASTARD over the road with his big white house, I mean it looks so out of place with all the other crap houses around here, and what is he trying to prove with that thick hunky wirey chest hair that makes you want to run your fingers through and that seductive bronze tan and his flawless perfect white teeth? He is a grade A ass-hole! Red Rock: Well. He sounds...nice. Uncle Terry: Yes TOO nice Uncle Terry clenches his fists trying to hold back from breaking in to an uncontrollable rage. Boolie, who has grown up with his Uncle Terry had learned when one of these fits were about to occur, so he swiftly tried to take his mind of Russel The Love Muscle. Boolie: Hey whats your wife name again? Uncle Terry: Lillian. All of a sudden Uncle Terry’s wife come out the door and starts yelling at Uncle Terry Liilian: Are you shit heads talking about me? Get the hell off my god damn porch and get out of here before I throw the good plates at you! Red Rock looks at Lillian who is visible irate as she stands firm and angry. Red Rock is first to reply with a quick off the mark sarcastic retort. Red Rock: OOOO not paper plates! Liilian: SHUT UP you skinny little limy bastard! Red Rock: hey Uncle Terry, has she been drinking? Uncle Terry: Yes, yes she has Red Rock and Uncle Terry look at Lillian in her drunken state as she continues to humiliate herself in front of the neighbors, who have all gathered round to see whats going on and possible to gather some topics of convocation for their next coffee mornings. Lillian storms back in to the house and slams the door behind her but not long after comes belting out of the door again. Boolie: Sweet baby Jesus! she’s got a knife, Cheese it! Uncle Terry: AGHA! Crazy Bitch! Red Rock, Boolie and Boolie's Uncle Terry run away from the house and scramble in to Boolie's van just in time, Lillian begins to slash and hack at the glass with her knife as Boolie franticly tries to put the keys in the ignition. Uncle Terry: HURRY BOYS! SHES NUT! Boolie: I'M TRYING DAMNIT! Boolie manages to start the van with the loud grumbling noise of the 25 year old engine. Boolie floors the accelerator and Uncle Terry, Boolie and Red Rock speed down the road with a cloud of dirt in Lillian’s face. About one and a half hours later. Red Rock, Boolie and his uncle now find themselves idly driving around. Red Rock and Boolie hadn't expected to walk in on a huge domestic, so they were left with little options. Uncle Terry: So Boolie, what brings you and your friend to come see me? Boolie: Well we were bored at home and our crazy dutch neighbor keeps walking around nude. Red Rock: It gets a bit much sometimes. Uncle Terry: Right! Well...that's...super. So Red is it? Red Rock: Yes. Uncle Terry: What do you do for a living? Red Rock: Um...well I'm a professional wrestler. Uncle Terry: Wow, just like on TV? You must be loaded what you doing driving around in a piece of shit van like this? Boolie: HEY! Don't talk messed up about my van! Red Rock: err...well it's not as well paid as you'd expect...of course I found out the hard way. Red Rock slumps back in to his chair and remember the day he was accepted in to AWC thus creating an awkward silence amongst the three men. Red Rock tilts his head to the right and begins to stare out the window, dreaming of one day where he will become a rich wrestler with cool endorsements and interviews on big name TV chat shows. Until then he realizes it's just cold beans and sharing a flat with a nude dutch man and renting from a hobbit. He releases a subtle sigh and go away with his imagination. Maybe one day I'll live in a big white house and could score with lots of fine ass pussay. Suddenly Red Rock's trail of though is interrupted by a sign that catches his eye. He comes up from his day dream and looks over at Boolie and his Uncle Terry. Red Rock: Heeeey, check out what that sign says. Uncle Terry: “Free Range Eggs, 2 miles” Red Rock: Lets go get ourselves from range eggs! They're FREE! Uncle Terry: I think you might be confu - Boolie: Did you say FREE Eggs? 2 miles you say? Uncle Terry: NO they're Free range eggs which means the chickens are frr - Red Rock cuts off Uncle Terry's explanation of what free range eggs are as his instinct to be a cheap bastard overwhelms him. Red Rock: The chickens are free too? Fan-bloody-tastic! We have to go now Uncle Terry lowers his head in disgust at the idiocy of his Nephew and his friend Red Rock. They drive a little further up the road until the get to a second sign saying “Free Range Eggs this way” with an arrow point down a spooky dirty tracked road. Red Rock: Turn right Boolie! Turn right! Boolie does as he is told and the van veers right on to the spooky dirt track. Despite the fact it is still day time the road appears to be very dark as the trees arch over the road blocking out all the light. It's a narrow road and the shrubbery and forest is too thick to see through. Boolie cautious drives down the bumpy road as the three men in side get thrown about in their seats. The all sit in silence looking around, expecting something to jump out from the bushes at any moment. Red Rock: It's pretty spooking down here, I wish Derek Acorah was with us. Uncle Terry: Who? Red Rock: Don't you watch most haunted on Living TV? Uncle Terry: No...No I don't Red Rock shakes his head, Red Rock is a big fan of Derek Acorah. It's not widely known that Red Rock is a big fan of the paranormal and used to go on paranormal investigations with his good friends when he was young. Of course they were not to the stature of what they do on TV, it was more teenage fun and something to do to entertain themselves. The van comes to the end of the dirt track and in to a large opening. The area is still surrounded by tree but much wider, about 30 foot in diameter and the fact you still can't see through the thick forest makes the gang of 3 feel even more uneasy. There is an old rusted tractor parked here with a battered up trailer just sitting beside it. Boolie parks up the van near it and they all step out on to the gravelly floor. Uncle Terry: This place feels weird. Red Rock: My spirit guide is telling me there are some spirit people present. Boolie: I'm going to take your Most Haunted series 1 DVD away from you when we get home. Red Rock: aw... Uncle Terry: I think this place must be a farm or something, judging by the thick wooden gate and this old tractor here. Uncle Terry pats the tractor as he mentions it and the force of his hand makes it buckle. The three men back away from it with the fear of it falling on them in mind. Whilst their attention was focus on the tractor they all neglected to notice an old man who had appeared from nowhere. The old man looks about 85-90, his hair is very thin and practically bald on top with white tufts on around the back and the side of his head and is wearing a pair of sweaty red long johns. He appears to be lost and confused. Red Rock is first to notice the strange old man, and reacts in a less than calm manor. Red Rock: ARGH! GHOST!! Boolie: Wait! I think it's just a regular old man! Red Rock: ARGH OLD MAN! Another thing not widely known about Red Rock is his bizarre phobia of old people. Red Rock fears becoming old and on top of that he has early childhood memories of being taken to an old folk home to see his great grandmother and all he remembers is all the really old women reaching out at him like zombies. Red Rock: ARGH HE WANTS TO STEAL MY YOUTH! Red Rock runs away from the old man, but the old man appears to slowly hobble after him. Every time the old man gets near him Red Rock runs away to another part of the opening. Now imagine watching this from a birds eye view as Red Rock franticly scurries around the opening as the old man slowly follows him where ever he goes as Boolie and Uncle Terry watch and giggle. Red Rock: FUCK OFF YOU ZOMBIE BASTARD! Boolie! Help me this is a matter of life or death! Boolie: Why? Red Rock: Because I'll KILL you if you don't help me! The Old man has Red Rock backed up in a corner and the old man begins to utter a sentence in his wheezy and raspy old man tone of voice. Old Man: Help me I'm lost.. Red Rock at this point begins to freak out as the old man is now touching his arm. He panics and shoves the old man to the floor. As he hits the floor he whimpers the words “ow my hip” his lungs deflate in one final raspy wheeze. At the point Red Rock runs away over to Boolie and Uncle Terry. Boolie: Red, he's not moving! Red Rock: He shouldn't of tried to steal my youthful essence. Uncle Terry: Is your head screw on tight enough boy? Red Rock replies, slightly out of breath as his heart is still racing after his encounter. Red Rock: Yes, thank you! Now onwards to go claim our free eggs! Red Rock, Boolie and Uncle Terry press on to find where they can claim their free eggs. A Few moments of searching later. The three men now find themselves situated outside the old vintage looking farm. The sun is now beginning to set and the darkness is starting to cover the scene with a blanket of cold air. There is a rundown looking farmhouse with wooden cladding and a patchy slate tiled roof. The paint on the wood work has pretty much all peeled off revealing the wood rot and worm holes. Over in the distance is an equally run down barn, the bottom of the door has worn away from years of moisture eating away at it and it bangs silently in the wind. There is a strange eerie silence upon their arrival and all 3 men are beginning to have second thoughts. Red Rock: On second thoughts...maybe we should just buy some eggs from the shop. Boolie: But they won't be FREE eggs Red Rock: Yeah but this place is freaking me out...what if there are more old people who want to suck the life out of me...I'll become one of them! A Piss back wearing, liver spotted, leathery zombie! Red Rock cringes as he pictures his worst nightmare and just as the three men are going to retreat from the farm two men step out of the house. They appear to be middle aged men, one of them is a tall man with a dirty trucker hat and a pair of dungarees and the other on is a shorter fatter man who is wearing a pair of laborer's gloves and also sporting a pair of dungarees. The fatter man's name is Earl and the taller man is named Bob. They are the owners of the farm, inherited from their father who passed on not so long ago. Since then things haven't gone as well for these two men. Earl: Hello boys, you here to buy some eggs? Red Rock looks at Boolie with an expression of immense disappointment occupying him as his bottom lip flutters like a disheartened child.. Red Rock: Did he just say buy some eggs... Uncle Terry: I did try to tell you... Red Rock: Hey buddy! The sign said FREE range eggs, so we're here for our range eggs! Earl looks at his brother Bob in confusion as he rubs his head. Bob: No boy you got it wrong, free range eggs is when the hens are free to wonder around and not kept cooped up all the time. Uncle Terry: SEE I that's what I was trying to tell them! Red Rock's face of disappointment worsens as he sheepishly kicks the dirt with his hands in his pockets. Red Rock: aww bugger this then. Earl: Hey, don't get upset about it son. How about you come take some eggs anyway? we got plenty! Red Rock's face lights up like a happy child in conjunction with a huge grin and the uncontrollable urge to bounce up and down. Red Rock: Really? Wow! Thanks! Bob: Sure, follow us this way in to the barn. Red Rock, Boolie and Uncle Terry follow Bob and Earl to the barn. Earl: By the way, you boys wouldn't happen to have seen an old man in some red long johns around have you? Our grand pappy Joe wondered off this mornin' Red Rock bites his bottom lip and Boolie and Uncle Terry's eyes bulge. Red Rock: err...heh...no? Earl: O.K then! As the group of men approach the barn they share light hearted small talk about where they came from and what each others names are, things like that. Suddenly the barn door flies open and to Red Rock, Boolie and Uncle Terry's horror a man tied to a chair come running out. Red Rock, Boolie and Uncle Terry's faces drop and they turn pale as Earl and Bob look at each other with expressions that read “ah shit...”. Red Rock: RUN AWAY! Boolie: ARGHHH! The three men instantly realize they've stumbled up on the farm of a pair of psychos. Earl and Bob begin to pursue the three men. Bob: GET 'EM WITH THE DART GUN! Earl withdraws a dart gun, used to detain large animal and fires a dart at each of the three men. They try to battle the effects of the darts. But it's too late and everything blurs and fades to black. A couple of hours later... Red Rock is the last of the three to come round he goes to rub his head but finds that his hands have been tied down. It hasn't quite hit him what has happened to him yet but as his vision becomes clearer he notices that he is tied down to a chair, back to back to back with Boolie and Uncle Terry and all three men are stripped down to their underwear. Red Rock: Ah for fucks sake... Earl: Mornin' sun shine! Red Rock: You complete set of BASTARDS! Wheres my FREE eggs? Uncle Terry: AH Fucking forget the damn eggs! What are you two going to do with us? Boolie: Why do I always end up tied to chairs...damn it! Earl: Quit y'r belling aching boys, you're here for the long haul. Red Rock settles down followed by his typical rolling of the eyes exposing his cheeky attitude. Red Rock: Super... Bob: See this farm ain't the proud stable place it used to be Red Rock: Well obviously it's not going to be that stable if you kidnap people... Earl slaps Red Rock round the face. Earl: Shut up boy! I don't like y'r back chat! Anyway we've been loosing too much money and the bank ain't going to give us a loan, so we gotta get some cash some other way so we were going to rob you boys. Bob: But after looking through your stuff it seems to us that you ain't got nothing and you Red Rock, for a wrestler you don't have much. Red Rock: it's a shit business sometimes. Sooo we don't have anything to offer you, so I guess you'll have to let us go? Earl: Woah, woah. Slow down son, we can't just do that you'll go to the police. This is now a hostage situation. Bob: We figured that Red Rock there must work for a company with money, wrestling companies have money, so it's YOU that's our hostage. Boolie: Does that mean me and Terry can go? Red Rock: Oh THANKS Boolie! Earl: No, of course not, you'll tell the police. Red Rock: HA! Fucking gutted you're stuck here too! Earl: We looked through you're phone book so we're going to ring some of them and make our demands! Lets see what we got here, Dave Harbor? Red Rock nods and grins as he feels that Dave would be most likely to help. Earl: Nah, he doesn't sound important. Clyde Norris, PR guy? Lets call him Red Rock: OH NO NOT NORRIS! Clyde Norris, the PR guy for AWC who happens to think Red Rock is a waste of space and is unmarketable. He did once say to Red Rock that he wouldn't care if he got kidnapped and was never seen again. Red Rock signs and leans back against the chair he is tied to. Over in Clyde Norris' shite office riiiing ring, ring, ring. We now watch as we appear in Clyde Norris' office. It has tacky 80's wooden cladding on the walls and a wobbly second hand desk. Norris is watching the latest edition of Fresh on his small 12” TV, which is situated at the end of his desk. He is gorging himself on a box of sprinkled donuts as he relaxes with his feet up. He hears his mobile phone ringing, which is on his right hand side and he answers it still with some donut in his mouth. Norris: Hold on! Norris finishes his donut and prepares himself for speech. Norris: Clyde Norris here, what do you want? Earl: We have one of your super stars captive - Norris: SHIT! Please don't say it's Pierce Lavelle! Earl: Who? I don' - Norris: Paddy O' Shea? Earl: NO! AND back in the barn with the mentalist farmer... Earl: Well he didn't help. Looks like we'll have to let you go. Red Rock: What?! Really? Earl: Nah we're gonna kill you! Red Rock: Oh right...gay. Red Rock: Why do people ask that, are you going to pass on a message “OH HI I'm the guy who shot Red Rock, by the way he says “could you get my dry cleaning”. You pair of fucking idiots! Earl: READY Bob: AIM! Earl: FI - SMAAAAASHHH! Out of nowhere the barn door smashes through as 8 armed police officers burst through the broken pieces of wood and splinters. Boolie: WE'RE ALIVE! THANK YOU JESUS THANK YOU! Red Rock: I hope someone will UN TIE US NOW!
