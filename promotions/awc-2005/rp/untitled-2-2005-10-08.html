Date: 8/10/2005 The darkness of ‘Mare’s bedroom was offset by a square shaped sample of light edging past the blackout curtains. Just enough for the rising ‘Mare to see where he was planting his feet but not too much so as to have him wake with a sore head. He braved the cool morning air and placed his legs out of the bed, planting his feet on the cold wooden floor. His warm feet felt refreshed as he wiggled his toes, adjusting to the temperature of the floorboards. Scratching his head ‘Mare looked across the dim room to see a shadow being cast against the door of his wardrobe. Following the long shadow across the room a dark figure loomed, leaning against the wall opposite to the wardrobe. ‘Mare had no doubt as to who the dark silhouette belonged to. Safe in this knowledge ‘Mare remained silent as he rose to his feet, the warm duvet dropping down in a heap, slouching off the bed and down onto the floor. Leaving the sheets disheveled ‘Mare set off across the room toward his door. Nothing like a subtle wakeup call. Opening the door to the main section of his apartment ‘Mare’s eyes squinted at the harsh bright light that streamed through the opening in the door. Turning his head away for a second ‘Mare gradually allowed his eyes settle into the light before entering the main section of his living quarters, dropping like a lead weight into a small chair positioned slightly askew in the center of the main room. Attired only in his briefs, ‘Mare tucked his legs up in to his chest and hugged his bare shins with his forearms, locking one fist over the opposing arms wrist. Sitting there retaining the warmth in his tired body ‘Mare began to let his eyes droop. I have to stay awake ‘Mare thought to himself, jolting his head back up and opening his eyes as wide as possible. He knew that dozing off in the chair today was not an option. He had to prepare for his on-screen debut against an opponent that his advisor had warned him was no pushover. Although his contract with the AWC did not officially come in to play until the night of his debut, ‘Mare felt obliged to get himself down to his local gymnasium and train hard today. Over the months leading towards his major contract deal with the AWC ‘Mare had been a little under-zealous with his training regime and had slacked off. He was just coming around to the fact that he would be stuck in the indies for the rest of his in-ring career when the advisor had approached him with an especially interesting plot to further both of their careers. Needless to say it had paid off in dividends and now the two of them were on their way to lucrative careers under the AWC umbrella. Getting to his feet ‘Mare still felt tired, this feeling was subsided by a pain in the rear of his right eye. The swelling, he could feel, had subsided completely now. It had been a good few days since he had last seen the back of the advisors hand, ever since ‘Mare had been especially careful not to make any further mistakes. He knows best. Moving back into the dim room ‘Mare paced over to the drawn curtains and opened them. Light poured into the now empty room. I guess he left whilst I was dozing in the sitting room. He spent no time taking in the glorious scenery of the derelict apartment block that lay twenty feet across from his window but rather dragged his feet noisily across the wooden floor towards a set of drawers by his bedside, sliding two of the drawers open and removing fresh a fresh pair of socks, sweat pants and a t-shirt. Slipping into his clothes ‘Mare gazed out of his window at the aging billboard. Missing something? The words had always felt meaningless to him, especially when they were sub-headed by the logo of a familiar brand of condom, but as he read the words this morning he felt his heart sinking into the pit of his stomach. He knew that, though he had secured himself a contract in the AWC, his life was still empty. The nights were still as cold and empty as the darkness that enshrouded them. The apartment that he resided in was sufficient for his needs, but how he longed to have a requirement for a five bed-roomed house in the countryside, away from the hustle-bustle of city life. How he dreamt of taking a wife and for her to bear his children. His eyes closed as he sat shirtless on the edge of his bed. The image flashed in to his mind. He could see her face perfectly. Her long brunette locks flowing past her shoulders and down her back, her deep green eyes - valleys full of stories to be told. His two small children ran around her feet as she strode across a large reception area to welcome her traveling husband home after a long three-month tour of Japan. It was as if he had already been there, as if this was a flashback to a previous lifetime. The sound of a bird in flight jerked ‘Mare back to reality, his body jolting and his eyes springing back to life. The familiar sound of a pigeon soaring from the ledge just above his bedroom window, under normal circumstances, would not even be something that he noticed. Today was another matter entirely. ‘Mare had so many thoughts rushing through his mind, his debut against a well known competitor who was much higher in the rankings than he was, the orders given to him by his advisor, and on top of it all his own depressive state which was just about getting the better of him this morning. The last few months had been rocky. ‘Mare’s career had been going from strength to strength, but his personal affairs were working against him. He suffered from an immense feeling of self-loathing, a miserable feeling of self-pity and the old tired feeling of depression, which continued to get the better of him even today, a day in which he should be feeling on top of the world. He would expect a bubbling in his stomach in the mornings leading up to his on-screen debut but the feeling in his stomach right now felt more like a whirlpool, sucking all the energy from the rest of his body and draining him of any other human emotion. Sitting around here won’t help. ‘Mare dragged himself to a vertical stance and dragged the t-shirt he held over his head, flicking his hair from his back allowing it to fall down his back. After completing his usual self-maintenance tasks ‘Mare grabbed his backpack and headed for the door. “I’ll see you in a few hours!” he yelled, turning the knob on the door and leaving. Meanwhile, the drapes of the bathroom fluttered in the wind. * * * The reliable Crystler Neon sped down the freeway headed towards a local gymnasium where ‘Mare had been training since he moved in to his apartment. Everybody there knew of him, but not one single person inside the building understood who he was, nor, did it seem, did any of them care to. For ‘Mare it was simply a facility where he and his advisor could plan out their next move whilst keeping themselves in good condition. This morning it would just be ‘Mare who went training as his better half manned the fort back in the apartment taking any phone calls and finishing a few pieces of paperwork. He had been a great help to ‘Mare ever since they got together, and they seemed to have bonded well in a very short period of time. Working together they had gone from nothing, climbing the ranks through the independent scene and up into the National levels of professional wrestling. The car slowed before turning onto an uneven car park, gravel spitting up at the sides of the car. ‘Mare gazed out of his window up at the flickering Neon sign. ‘ProPower Gymnasium’ Open twenty-four hours. ‘Mare thought as he pulled himself out of the car, dragging his rucksack with him. The door slammed shut and ‘Mare set off toward the main door of the gymnasium. As he stepped into the reception room he looked at the notice board to his right. It looked completely out of place when contrasted against the rest of the reception rooms’ décor. Today the reception room was deserted, a rare phenomenon, especially for a weekend. ‘Mare gave a shrug and headed through to the main section of the gym. Through the door lay one large room filled with cable machines, free weights and cardiovascular exercise machines offset by a damp aroma to set the mood. A local radio station was playing loud over a small Boom box left on a shelf at the rear of the room. The cable machines were unoccupied, as were the treadmills, bikes, and rowing machines. In fact as ‘Mare gazed around the room he realized that the entire gymnasium floor was in fact, deserted. A feeling of concern began to grow as ‘Mare headed through a door to his left, entering the ring room. Heavy bags and speed bags hung from the rafters of the ceiling, across the room set directly in the center a small multi-purpose ring was set up. It was hardly of the contemporary WWE standard, but it worked well for its intended purpose. ‘Mare waded through the ocean of sand filled bags towards the ring. Dodging his last bag ‘Mare looked up into the ring to see a rather large, athletically built man stretching out his legs, using his right hand to hold onto the top rope steadying himself. Standing a good few inches higher than ‘Mare and sporting a moustache with connotations of an adult film star, the gentleman placed his leg back on the canvas and gave a forced smile. “I’ve been waiting on you. Your friend told me you needed some extra training this morning.” The rough voice of the strong man boomed across the room at ‘Mare. The tone of his voice making ‘Mare’s chest shake a little. ‘Mare knew as he looked up at the man that this was yet another well-organized piece of ‘training’, courtesy of the Advisor. Great. Dropping his bag on the ground ‘Mare slid under the bottom rope and began pacing around the ring. The two of them circled for a few seconds before ‘Mare took it upon himself to lunge in towards his opponent. The pain that shot through the lower part of his stomach was nothing in comparison to the throbbing he would feel in the back of his head when he woke up on Sunday morning. Patrick Mapleleaf is a deadman ‘Mare thought as his face slammed against the canvas for a final time. Darkness enveloped the mind of ‘Mare before he was welcomed home once more by the woman from his dreams.
