Thirty Minutes

13 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday the 13th, 06 'Do you have faith, Midas?' Johnny leans over his knees, seated on a worn blue bench, undoing the laces on his training shoes. A fresh layer of sweat catching dim lights off his tanned skin. Midas sets a bottle of water on the bench beside Johnny, taking a deep breath that expands the chest of his gold blazer. 'I'm Catholic.' He says, tugging a small silver cross from under his shirt. 'That doesn't answer my question.' Johnny sits back and takes the water bottle in hand. 'Yeah, I read the bible from time t' time, go t' church.' 'So you have religon.' Johnny kicks his shoes off. 'It's not the same thing.' And stands up. 'Oh no?' Midas goes from looking down to up as his only client towers several inches over him. 'No see religon is an institution that tells you what.' The shoes get shoved into a duffle bag. 'They tell you from a book written by man who to pray to and when, they even tell you the words to say.' The sweaty shirt finds a place next to the shoes. 'Religion is the oldest form of propoganda that still exists today.' Johnny smells a black dress shirt before shoving one arm through a sleeve. 'But faith.' The next arm finds a sleeve. 'Faith is the blind belief that you are not alone; that there is purpose and reason in everything we do.' Johnny does only the middle two buttons. 'So. Do you have faith, Midas?' Midas T. Gold regards Johnny with a speculative eye before nodding slowly. 'Yeah.' He clears his throat. 'Yeah I got faith. You?' 'Me?' Johnny grins and shoulders his bag. 'Now that's a silly thing to ask.' Midas frowns but instead of continuing Johnny turns to walk away. The small gym empty in all directions, machines left untouched and weights unlifted. Only the blaring ring of Midas' cellphone joins the cadence of Johnny's retreating footsteps. 'Hullo?' Midas puts the phone to his ear and moves to follow his client. 'Hello?' He speaks clearer, his step slowing as Lexicon walks through the door and into sunshine. 'Corner and eighty eight and what? Twenty minutes for what? Who is this? Hello?' He stops and hangs up, quickly dialing star six nine and putting the phone again to his ear, resuming his pace for the door. He presses one to listen to the number again. 'Nine five two three.' Decline the twenty cent direct connect option. Dialing the number he steps through the door and into the late California afternoon. The phone rings in his ear, a bag rings at his feet. Midas frowns. His phone rings, the bag rings. Midas looks around, 'Johnny?'. His phone rings, the bag rings. Johnny's bag. Johnny's phone. 'Johnny!' He approaches the smokey grey Phantom with limo tinted windows, hanging up and opening the door quickly. 'Johnny?' The car's empty. 'This shit ain't funny yo!' Midas' phone rings. He presses the button immedietly. 'Hello?' Dead air. Midas hangs up and frantically misdials star six nine, he hangs up and does it again. His thick thumb presses two buttons at once and he gets the wrong service. 'Fuck!' Launching his phone into the air. He stops. 'Shit.' He scrambles for the drivers side door, scrambles back and grabs Johnny's bag. He throws them into the seat next to his and starts the car with a purr, his door closing as he pulls out into the street. Holding the steering wheel with his left hand he pulls savagly at his golden tie when the bag next to him rings. He looks at it, glancing back to the road as it rings again. He changes lanes, speeds up past a family in a buick and cuts them off to avoid being stuck behind a slow moving truck at a yellow light. Horns blaring in his wake Midas pulls the bag closer and fumbles with the zipper. The phone rings again, clearer, spotting the blinking blue light Midas grabs the sleek silver flip phone and pops it open. 'Yeah?' 'Johnny?' A girls voice. 'Who is this?' Midas asks. 'Who is this?' Attitude in response. 'Who the fuck is this!' 'Listen ass-hole, tell my dead beat dad I need to talk to him. Tell him it's about Koryn.' 'Claire?' Midas's tone relaxes considerably. 'Who is this?' 'I'm a friend of your dad's.' 'So.' 'Have you talked to him at all today?' 'He called my cell a few minutes ago.' Claire speaks up hesitantly. 'He said - beep - and now I can't reach my mom.' 'What was that?' Midas says, interrupted by a blocked incoming call. 'I said 'He said - beep - and now I can't reach my mom.'.' 'Shit, I didn't hear you again.' He enters a tunnel at a hundred miles an hour. 'Claire?' No reception. 'Fuck.' Both hands on the wheel he looks at the clock on his dash, seventeen minutes left. His foot drops to the floor and he cuts around a mini van. Flipping open the phone he glances back and forth between the redial list and the traffic he's maneuvering dangerously around. It's only got three calls, one to Claire, one to Midas, and the first to an unknown name. He highlights unknown and sets the phone down. The speedometer rises past one hundred and twenty. He swerves outside a cluster, touching the wall briefly tears his mirror away. Pulls back into the fast lane just in time to keep going past a limo and hit a clear patch to the exit. The tunnel echoes blaring horns but Midas ignores them all; pressing talk the instant he clears the egress and holds the reciever to his ear. It rings a second time without answer, Midas steers through an intersection against the red light. A third time. He decides against the freeway at the last second and dives out of the exit lane, effectively cutting off a semi. A fourth ring and finaly somebody answers. 'Hello, thank you for calling pussycats.' Fake enthusiasm from the female answering the phone. 'Pussy Cats?' 'Uh, yeah, can I help you?' She asks but Midas hangs up. His tires squeeling as he hits an intersection sideways, sudden traction and he screams down a residential side street. Parents and kids on bikes swearing and shaking fists at his tail lights. 'What is this, Johnny.' Midas mutters to himself, checking his time. 'Thirteen minutes left man, what's going on?' He checks left, he looks right, he hammers the gas pedal and steers down a one way street the moment somebody steers in the right way. 'Shit!' A thousand expressions an minute tug and tweak at his sweaty face, a thick fingered hand wiping his brow and smoothing back over tight corn rows. He hits the gas and accelerates towards the on-coming car, the man blares his horn and hits his brakes. Midas steers into a recess in the wall built for a large double door back entrance. He tears the wheel back and his nose clips the corner, breaking the right head light but he gets around the car. A mercedes, nice one too, he realizes pulling out into the alley again, his right signal flashes and turns left as another black man steps out onto the pavement in light brown Lugz. 'Crazy niggas in this town.' He curses. 'Yo Lupus let's go!' A voice inside the car shouts. The man nods slowely, his head covered by a black du rag; long white mucle shirt clings to a muscular upper body and hangs over dark blue jeans. 'Get in the fuckin car nigga!' 'Yo shut up.' Lupus sneers and lowers himself back into his ride. 'We in a hurry man, ain't got time to smoke no fools takin wrong turns.' 'Man I thought I told you to shut up.' Lupus purses his lips and snickers, dropping the clutch and putting it into first. His seat suddenly jerks him forward and settles back. He sucks in a deep breath and lets it out fast when it happens again. Lupus looks over to his passanger, a hispanic with a circular tribal tattoo on his neck and a bullet proof vest on. 'What?' He asks of the look. Lupus' seat jerks forward again. 'Stop him from kickin m'seat!' He snaps. 'Chill nigga.' There's a click and the hispanic leans in his seat, pointing a gun into the back. 'You want me t' shoot you? Oh that's right, we already had t' shut you up. My bad.' He laughs and looks at Lupus, who's not laughing, and his tone turns more serious. Gagged with his feet and hands bound behind his back, Johnny slams his knees into Lupus' seat again. His eyes blazing with defiance and his nose wrinkling he does it again. 'Yo I said stop it fool!' Lupus yells. 'Fuckin hit him Havoc!' Havoc raises the gun and swings it down but Johnny maneuvers his shoulder to take the impact and stops hitting the seat. 'That's right.' Havoc spits on his captive. 'Best start showing some respect Johnny-boy.' He turns and settles back into his seat. 'Bet you never thought you see us again, huh?' A grin splits across Havoc's face. 'Funny, aint it, how shit comes around.' He can't help but look over his shoulder to see Johnny's eyes. 'Shut up, man.' Lupus shakes his head as they stop at a red light. 'Naw I ain't about to shut up. This fool right here fucked us over, he used us to make a name for himself and then ditched us.' Havoc looks back to Johnny. 'And then you made sure there wasn't a promotion in the world that'd hire us. Ya ask me, you deserve what you got coming to you. After they way you did us two years ago, I wish was me got to pull the trigger.' His voice drops an octave, his eyes narrowing and lips curl with malice. Reaching back Havoc pulls the dirty rag out of Johnny's mouth. 'You got somthin t' say?' Johnny takes a moment to rotate his jaw and swallow properly before settling his eyes into Havoc's. 'If I was a killing man, it'd be you first.' Johnny smiles malevolently. Havoc chuckles, at first, and then breaks into laughter. 'Shut up!' Lupus snaps as his cell phone rings. He checks the phone and sighs. 'Is that-' 'Yup.' 'Tell him we're almost there.' Lupus answers the phone, 'Hello?', as Havoc turns back to see Johnny reaching for him. 'We're on our way. What the fuck!' 'You're on your way?' The waitress asks and waits. Her dirty blond locks tied back into a bun with wisps of hair reaching down her slender neck and across her bare shoulders and breasts. She sighs and cocks a hip before pulling the phone away from her ear with a loud bang and then static. She shrugs and hangs up, free hand fishing about in the apron around her thin waist and almond shaped hazel eyes rolling. She walks out from behind the bar, a long 'L' shape made of dark mahogony. In fact everything about the establishment is dark from the walls to the patrons. Old men mostly, quietly seated by themselves drinking of never ending self pity and smoking their sorrows. Topless waitresses stroll casually about the small room, either getting drinks or fighting off ambitious hands. This particular waitress though walks past them all without stopping, without turning her head as a customer calls her over. She doesn't bat a lash as wandering fingers brush her leg, or a whistler throws a loonie her direction. She parts a beaded curtain and steps into an even darker room, a round table centered and covered in chips and cards. 'They're almost here.' She says. 'You need anything else?' Her tone is tired, looking around at the five shady men seated at the poker table. 'Another waitress will be fine.' The baritone that floats from the shadows is weathered with age but still strong. 'Good I'm off anyway.' The waitress turns and walks away. 'You don't want a tip?' The voice asks. 'Koryn, was it?' Those asian eyes look back as tempted as they are tempting. 'No.' She offers a reluctant smile. 'I just want to get home to my little girl.' She sighs as the sillohuette of a man nods and leans back into his seat. The beads rustle about as Koryn takes a hasty retreat, moving back to the bar and retrieving a shirt. 'You gettin out of here girl?' A tall brunette asks, holding a tray over her chest as she leans on the bar. 'Uhm.' Koryn swallows. 'Yeah. Have you seen my phone anywhere?' Brown hair waves as the waitress shakes her head. 'You missing it?' 'Yeah.' Koryn props her purse on the table and digs through it. 'But I swear I had it like twenty minutes ago.' 'That's weird. Claire called for you a few minutes ago, but you were in the back.' 'I know.' She looks exasperated. 'He kept asking me all these really creepy questions about Claire and Johnny.' 'Eww.' The brunette offers a sympathic tap on the shoulder. 'Yeah.' Defeated Koryn slings the purse strap over her shoulder. 'Can you take care of the back room for me?' 'Those creepy old guys?' The brunette wrinkles her nose as Koryn giggles. 'They're harmless, only one of them will talk to you anyway, don't worry. His name is Marcus.' Koryn turns to leave. 'Oh yeah.' Fingers snap. 'I almost forgot, they have more people coming. Tanya said they could use the back entrance, I'll prop it open on my way out. Oh and we're doing Claire's birthday party on Sunday, can you make it?' 'Of course! What's your address again?' The brunette grabs a peice of paper. 'House number twelve, corner of eighty eight and fourteenth.' 'Alright sweety, I'll see you tomorrow.' A few other girls wave as Koryn leaves, propping the back door open as promised and climbing into an Austin Mini. There's a pop as she puts it into drive and starts pulling out of the alley, pressing the brake as a black Mercedes Benz turns in. The windows are too tinted to see the driver but Koryn inspects the heavy damage to the front end of the obviously expensive car. 'That'd suck.' She comments, turning her signal light on and turning out into the road. Traffic is thankfully light as Koryn patterns her way through a practiced short cut. Her voice rises to uncomfortably high pitches as she attempts to sing along to the songs on the radio. Her voice though peters away as she comes to a horrible realization. A few blocks ahead is a cluster of spinning, flashing lights. Police cruisers block the road but Koryn creeps as close as possible, unable to get a clear view she climbs out of her car. 'Excuse me ma'am, I'm gonna have to ask you to return to your car.' An officer is quick to approach. 'No.' Koryn says, her eyes not on the officer. 'No!' 'Ma'am.' 'That's my house! Where's my daughter? Is she okay?' 'This is your house?' The officer looks over his shoulder and makes eye contact with a black man in a gold blazer. 'Yes! Where's my daughter!' Koryn moves to go past but the cop detains her. 'You daughter is fine.' The officer assures the quickely hysterical Koryn. 'Do you know that man?' A finger is pointed at Midas. 'I.' She frowns and squints at him as he starts walking towards them. 'I don't think so. Did he do something to my daughter?' 'No he saved your daughter, showed up just in the nick time. Apparantly two men were waiting for your daughter to get home from her dance lessons. Do you know of anybody who would want to hurt you or your family?' 'Koryn?' Midas asks as he steps up to her. 'Y-yes.' She stutters. His eyes catch the logo on her apron. 'You work at Pussy Cats?' She sighs and holds a hand up. 'Ugh, oh my god you're not one of those stalker-' Koryn watches Midas run away. 'freaks are you?' She finishes. 'Hey!' The officer takes off after him. 'Hey we need a statement!' But it's too late, Midas spins the Rolls Royce around and takes off up the street. He pushes a button on the nav com and presses 'P'. He waits for a list to be populated and after a few more buttons a voice directs him to: 'Turn left here.'. He spins the wheel to the right sharp, left. Traffic becomes a blur of 'Approaching left here. Take your next right. You have arrived.' and he hits the brakes. The tires squeeling as he comes to a stop across the entrance to an alley. His door opens and franticly he climbs out. 'What the hell happened?' Midas yells. 'Get in the car Midas.' Johnny instructs calmly. Closing the passanger side door of a busted up black Mercedes. 'What the fuck is going on?' He persists. Johnny walks towards the car, tugging leather gloves from his hands and pausing at the side walk to shove them down into the sewer. 'Get in the car.' Johnny says again and opens the door on his side. Midas does the same. 'Drive. Now.' 'You have to tell me what the fuck is going on Johnny!' 'Did you get to Claire?' 'That was? You sent me to?' Midas starts putting things together. 'Is she alright?' 'Yeah man, she's alright. What the fuck is going on, are we in some kind of trouble.' 'No.' Johnny's quick to respond with a weary smile. 'No we're not in any trouble. Not anymore.' He leans his head back against the seat as they put more and more distance behind them. 'Where are we going?' 'It doesn't matter.' Johnny sighs and leans forward to turn the radio on. 'You have a traffic tip for us?' 'Yeah man, my girlfriend says some gang just shot up Pussy Cats and now they're closing down all them roads. I can't get in to pick her up!' 'Hear that folks avoid east downtown now! What a half hour in east LA it's been. Maniacs on the roads, foiled kidnapping attempts and now mass murder in the tittie bar-' Johnny clicks it off and makes eye contact with Midas. 'Yo man.' He starts. 'Yeah?' Johnny raises an eye brow. 'You alright?' Johnny smiles and leans back into his seat with a chuckle. 'Are you kidding? I'm fucking spectacular.' Midas swallows hard and watches the road ahead. Allowing a long moment of silence to hang, a glance tells him Johnny is relaxing with his eyes closed and a smile on his lips. 'That faith and religion shit you were talkin earlier.' 'Yeah.' Johnny peeks. 'You didn't answer my question.' A side-long look of hesitation. 'Do you have faith?' Johnny closes his eye as the smile dives into dimples. 'Yeah. I have faith. Let's go get something to eat, I'm starving.' 'Sure. Long as you clean that blood off your shoes first.' 'Midas.' Johnny starts but his manager shakes his head and cuts him off. 'Everybody got secrets bro, you gonna kill to keep yours then I don't ever wanna know.' Johnny's grins just a little bit as Midas refuses to look at him. 'Was a nose bleed.' 'Dry air this time of year.' Midas glances over with concern. 'Hope that shit don't happen in the ring. I need you focused on winning that belt man, we need that belt. You're focused now right?' 'Focused on what?' Midas snaps his glare as Johnny starts laughing. 'There's no such thing as untouchable.'
