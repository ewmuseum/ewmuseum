I'm Still Afraid I Can't Help It

3 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/06 The Crusades. They were a series of wars fought over centuries in the Middle Ages in the Holy Land, provoked by Rome and eager monastaries in France in order to take the holiest city in Christiandom back from the Muslims. Those who fought in these wars on the side of Jesus were often romanticized in Western culture as Warriors for the Cross, who fought for the Church with the purest of intentions. They recall men like Richard the Lionheart, Emperor Frederick and the Knights Templar, and assume that through the Lord Jesus Christ, the Catholic Church defeated the Muslim infidels and reclaimed Jerusalem. People like that make Captain Suleimon sick to his stomach. It's those people whose erroneous grasp of historical truth who are the ones making the big Hollywood blockbusters and preaching to ignorant masses to perpetrate their false wars against anyone who looked remotely liked Suleimon. It's one thing to face discrimination, but a whole other thing when it's based in half-truths and warping of historical facts. If he had a soapbox, a megaphone and assurances from the local police that he wouldn't cause a riot, he'd get up on that soap box and tell everyone what really happened back then. That the wars were held to gain access to more tithes, tolls for pilgrims and other treasures reputed to be in the Holy City, not the least of which was the mythical Holy Grail. That Emperor Frederick II was actually educated by Muslims and wanted to make peace with them rather than fight them, and for that 'heresy,' he was challenged by other Christian leaders in Outremer. That Crusades were held to attack Byzantine Christians and French 'heretics.' That on their way to Outremer, Crusaders routinely held pogroms against the Jews. That Muslim forces routinely routed the Christians and eventually forced them out of the Holy Land in such an embarrassing fashion that the Church blamed the Knights Templar and framed them for blasphemy and idol worship as an ostensible cover for them scapegoating the group for the loss. Yep, those were the kinds of things that annoyed Suleimon. He was indifferent to the religious undertones. He neither felt pride for being a Muslim, even a lapsed one, and he didn't hate all parties because of his recent apostasy to all faiths. To him, it was more a struggle between races, the inferior whites against his superior Turks and other peoples of Middle Eastern descent. It annoyed him to see it in politics. He shook his head when Pope Benedict quoted a 14th Century Byzantine Emperor about the warlike nature of Muslims. Movies like Kingdom of Heaven made him roll his eyes. Even Dogan, who was the one who picked the movie out and made Suleimon watch it to give equal time to the West, cringed. Yes, Suleimon knew much about the Crusades. That's because at one time, he had to virtually reenact them. Tariq took to hardcore wrestling with ease and comfort after his incident in the warehouse, much to Rahman's delight. A diligent teacher, Rahman taught Tariq all the finer points of making the weapons an extension of himself. This, of course, after he was released from the hospital for the concussion Tariq gave him. It was alright though; Rahman didn't mind spending some time laid up in order to free Tariq from his inhibitions. Week after week, James Hopkins booked Tariq in hardcore matches and week after week, he not only won, but excelled. Other guys had started to find their own niches in the hardcore division. It gave Hopkins an idea. He decided to run it by Rahman first. 'Hey, Rock, you got a minute?' Rahman turned around to face his friend. 'Yeah, sure.' 'I want to start a hardcore division. Make a title for it and everything. I wanna give Tariq the inside track to that title.' 'Really? You really think he's ready for that?' 'Ready? He's been ready ever since he plastered Lonnie with that chair. Do you hear the reactions he gets for his matches? The fans are totally into him. They boo him the loudest of anyone out there, but I know they respect him for what he does out there. Posters on the LBW fan boards rave over him. I mean, it's uncanny how over he is.' 'Well, if you think he is, then I think he is. Let's go for it.' The next day, James gathered the roster in the arena to make the announcement. 'Alright guys, I'm pleased to announce the formation of a hardcore division here in Liberty Bell Wrestling. Accompanying that division will be a new Hardcore Championship. Now, to decide who gets to hold that title for the first time, I'm going to pit Captain Suleimon...' Before he could finish, he was interrupted by a familiar, Southern twinged voice. 'Yer gon' let this raghead thief represent yer fed with a title?' Lonnie shouted with a tone of indignation. 'Thief?' Rahman turned around. 'He's no thief.' 'Yessiree he is. He stole my Black Betty!' 'No he did not! You just misplaced it!' Well, the first part of that statement was right. Tariq didn't steal the chair. Rahman did. 'You lyin' sandnigger!' 'Hey, hey, hey, Rahman, let me handle this.' Tariq held his mentor back and stared right in Lonnie's face. 'Yeah, I have your chair. Spoils of war. What are you going to do about it?' 'See! See! He admitted! Arrest 'im!' James got between them. 'No, I'm not going to have anyone arrested. Lonnie, it's just a chair.' 'Fuck that you stupid Yankee! That wasn't just no chair, that was Black Betty! I want it back! It's... sacred to me!' 'Hey, Lonnie, why don't we settle this in the ring...' 'Oh no, no you God-danged raghead, I ain't fallin' for none a' yer tricks again. I want you in the street, one on one in the back fuckin' alley!' This time, The Great Karmazov, Barry Peters and JA got between the redneck and the Turk along with James. 'Jesus, we will not have a God-damned back alley fight. I think Tariq's got a good idea though. I think you should face off against him for the Hardcore Championship, Lonnie.' 'I told you, I ain't gon' face off against this asshole in the ring... unless I gots a big advantage. Make this damn sandnigger earn his title.' 'No, no Lonnie...' 'James... Lonnie, whatever you want, I'll do it.' Lonnie stroked his chin. 'Alright then. Why not a gauntlet match. I'll pick four other guys, an' you gots to go through them an' me if you wanna win that Hardcore Title.' 'It's a d...' 'I AIN'T FINISHED YET SANDNIGGER!' James shook his head. Lonnie continued. 'If'n I win, I get to have my Black Betty back. Y'hear?' 'Yeah I do. And it's a deal... under one condition.' Everyone perked up. 'If I win, you leave LBW... for good.' Lonnie thought about it. 'Ain't no way yer beatin' five guys. Deal.' Tariq stared Lonnie right in the eye with a look of confidence that belied the seeming hopelessness of his situation. 'I dunno what yer smilin' 'bout. Yer about t'get yer ass kicked! We beat yer asses in the Crusades, and now, we gon' do it again!' Tariq's look turned to disbelief. 'You're kidding me, right?' 'What'chu talkin' bout, boy?' Tariq shook his head at Lonnie's ignorance and just walked off. All the wrestlers in the arena widened their eyes and shook their heads. In the week leading up to the match, everyone tried to talk Tariq out of the match. It wasn't so much that they were concerned for the title as they were for his health. Five guys, all of them outweighing Tariq by at least fifty pounds. Barry Peters, JA, everyone in the locker room who hated Lonnie told him not to throw his career away like this. The only person who seemed to believe in him was Rahman. They were back at the apartment, the day before the match. 'Tariq... how are you feeling?' Tariq looked up and gave Rahman a waveringly confident look. 'You're not nervous, are you?' Tariq moved his head down a little bit. 'It's okay if you are.' 'It's just... five guys... I've never had to face more than two by myself before.' Rahman sat beside his student. 'Look, I know it's going to be tough... but you're in your element. You've become a much more ruthless and brutal competitor since I first took charge of you. Those are the kinds of things you need when the odds are stacked against you.' 'But I wanted it that way... I think I bit off more than I can chew.' Rahman shook his head. 'Tariq, nothing is more than you can chew unless you elect to take it out of your mouth.' The arena was electric that night. The hardcore gauntlet would be the main event. This was the first time Tariq would be on that late in the card. It was a new feeling. All the pressure in his gut, the two hours before his match went on felt like two days. It ground him down. Finally, James came into his locker room. 'C'mon Tariq. It's time.' Tariq came down to the ring and soaked in the boos of the crowd. He anxiously awaited as his first opponent, Claude LeCroix, from Montreal, Quebec. Shaved head, tattoos all over his body... he was a big member of the underground Neo-Nazi movement. That was kept under wraps though, because James, under pressure from Lonnie, had to push him as a face. Claude licked his lips as he pulled brass knuckles out of his pocket and stared at Tariq. The young Turk saw that and attacked. He kicked Claude's hand and knocked the knucks out. Claude responded by spearing Tariq hard. The match was all Claude for the most part. Tariq had a few good weapons shots and quick counters, but Claude looked like he was going to take Black Betty back for Lonnie before it really had begun. Tariq had to think fast. Then he saw the knucks he knocked away from Claude to begin. Out of the sight of Claude, he snapped up the knucks. Claude went to pick Tariq up, but... CRACK~! Down he went with the knucks. Tariq made the cover, and Claude was vanquished. The young Turk barely had time to breathe before his next opponent. Deutschland Dieter was next. As he was lumbering down the aisle though, he tripped over a wire and fell flat on his face. Everyone in the arena started laughing at him except Tariq, who took the opportunity to strike. He leapt out of the ring and hit the prone German with a springboard legdrop over the back of the neck. Tariq dragged the out-of-it Dieter into the ring and summarily pinned him. Two down, one easy, one hard. Next up was Lionheart Leonard, a young Englishman who wanted to make a break in America. He was also an impressionable youth who just happened to meet Lonnie first upon his arrival. Poor lad. He rushed to the ring and immediately took it to Tariq. He had energy and agility the likes that Tariq didn't see with his first two opponents. It was a very tight match all the way through, Lionheart meeting Tariq all the way through. He had the Turk reeling, sending him to the mat with a dropkick. He went to the top to go for his Lionheart Splash. He leapt, expecting to land his move. Instead, he got nailed by Tariq with a chair that he pulled from back of him in desperation. Lionheart was knocked out and Tariq covered for the pin. Tariq had survived the first three. The fourth man was an Italian, Lorenzo Campoverdo. Tariq decided he wasn't going to mess around. As soon as Campoverdo got into the ring, Tariq threw a chair at his head. And the Italian didn't catch it with his hands. He did with his head though. It turned out to be classic garbage fare, and Tariq was the one taking out the trash. He beat Lorenzo with everything he could get his hands on. The Italian just gave out, and Tariq won. Finally, it was time for the showdown. Lonnie Jackson and Captain Tariq Suleimon. Lonnie came down to the ring hooting and hollaring like he always did. He hopped into the ring, and Tariq tried the same thing with Lonnie that he did with Lorenzo. Lonnie caught it. With his hands. CRACK~! Tariq went down, but he wasn't out. His hands shielded a good part of the blow. Lonnie was still in control though. He did what he could to humiliate Tariq. He put the boots to the Turk. He whipped him with the belt. No matter what Lonnie did though, he couldn't put Tariq away. Finally, he grabbed the knucks that Claude had brought to the ring. Just as he picked them up, Rahman hurried to the ring with Black Betty in his hands. Lonnie didn't see him, not even as he slid into the ring with the chair. Rahman yelled out to Lonnie, and the redneck turned around startled. Before he could react, Rahman plastered him with the chair. Lonnie was down on the canvas, and Tariq knew he had to finish him off. For good. He grabbed Black Betty from Rahman and went to the top. He looked around and leapt, putting the chair underneath his bottom. The thud was sickening. Lonnie was plastered by the Arabian Facebuster, a move done in the arena several times before by one Sabu. Tariq rolled him over and covered him... one... two... Three. Lonnie was finally gone from LBW for good. Tariq raised his arms in victory, holding Black Betty in the air. The referee presented him with the Hardcore Championship, and he held that aloft in his other hand. Black Betty and the Hardcore Championship. Jerusalem and the Holy Grail all in his grasp. Tariq looked down at Lonnie and thought to himself... 'Who kicked whose ass in the Crusades, bitch?'
