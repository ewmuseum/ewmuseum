The Rise Of An Evolution: Soul Purpose

21 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/6/06 The wrestling business is a particular industry just about every wrestling fan wished they were apart of, those people who see their heroes on the television, they wish that they could one day stand next to them and stand proud. There are two different kinds of those people, the ones that only dream about standing in a real wrestling ring and dare to call them a professional wrestler and the ones that do something about it. Most wrestlers entered the business because they themselves loved the aspect of wrestling, to start off in wrestling camp, learning moves and how to take punishment then being signed to a promotion. If you only dreamed about being a wrestler but never actually committed to it, there is nothing wrong with that, being a fan is just fine but the other type of person that does something about it, there are also two types of people in that section too, there are the types that go through the training and they get signed to a promotion but the only thing some wrestlers accomplish is curtain jerking, putting over other wrestlers, sometimes you are promoted if you’re lucky but if you’re not then that’s just how it goes and they are happy with that. The other type is the type that doesn’t stay in the curtain jerking section of the card, they are not happy with their position and they strive to make themselves better, it usually only takes one great match to be noticed and then you will have a wrestling career. Being a jobber and being happy about being a jobber is not what anyone would call a career, you get paid less money, sure it’s the wrestling you love and not the money but the money is what’s keeping your head above water. Most low card wrestlers are getting so unnoticed that they resort to backyard wrestling, something I wouldn’t even consider wrestling at all, they’re putting their bodies on the line and nine out of ten times they’ll end up a cripple. I do not respect backyard wrestlers and I do not respect people who are happy with curtain jerking, I respect those people who go through the years of training, the years of disrespect and the demand it takes on your life. If you’re one of the types of people that want to be main eventing the big stage the pain never ends. Being a jobber, the only pain there is to your pride, there is a stress to being the big star, the stress of carrying the whole promotion on your back. I will admit that I was a dreamer when I was starting out my wrestling career, in a pathetic little run down training camp in Brooklyn with a bunch of failed wrestlers that couldn’t even make it in the independents and I didn’t care. I didn’t care because I was young and inexperienced, it wasn’t until my father showed me what it was like in his world, I knew instantly that I wanted to be the guy he was. I have tasted the main event, I won the Survivor Match which was something I never expected in my life to be apart of let alone win. Once you taste the main event you don’t want to go back, would I be happy walking around with a championship that means nothing to anyone? You’re damn right I wouldn’t because I know I am better than that, I look back to where I was several years ago and I laugh. Sure, I didn’t know any better, my father was ousted from my life by my so called loving mother. My wrestling talent was just about non-existent, people like Thunder, Masterson, Jeff H., Theodoric Solomon, Dangerous Intentions. They were the people that trained me, they took me under their wing, and I look back to my first ever wrestling match I had with a real professional wrestler. It was while my father was working for Furiously Addictive Wrestling, I was in the FAW Academy, Jeff spent most of his time down there not only training me but a few others as well, those others he helped train are now curtain jerking in independent promotions, I went up against a guy called the 9th Wonder of Foam, quite a weird guy to be sure he was also one of the best FAW had to offer. Jeff wanted to give me a real sense of competition and it was at that moment I decided I would be one of those guys that strived to be the best of the best. Furiously Addictive Wrestling Academy, June 2003 It had been three months since Shawn was reunited with his father Jeff H.. Shawn decided to leave his home of Brooklyn and hang around with his father who was a current FAW wrestler; Shawn was only 17 at this time and was by far the youngest trainee there, that didn’t bother him though because he was just happy to be there. He learned quickly and people dubbed him as a hot prospect but they thought that he would need a few years of seasoning before entering the big leagues, he was good but he was also small and even though size doesn’t usually matter it did in this case when everyone around him were about 6’3 to 6’8 and weighed between 235 to 300 pounds, so not only was he the youngest he was also the smallest at standing at just 6’1 and 215 pounds. The sheer size of his opponents would knock anyone else but not Shawn, he went out there and did his best, most of the time it resulted in him getting concussions. Every time Shawn got a concussion he used to explode with a flurry of words at the trainers and threatened to kick their ass if they did it again. Shawn was grateful for the opportunity given to him but everyone hated him because he got in there easy, his father was one of the best FAW had so naturally they’d sign Shawn, then other guys had to be put on a waiting list for so long just to get a crack at it. Little did the trainers know that Shawn would be the only successful guy out of the group. His 18th birthday had just passed, that meant that Shawn was finished at school and his father was able to take him on the road with him, Jeff knew that he wanted his son to travel with him and Shawn knew that he had the greatest chance at becoming a wrestler, but not just any wrestler, a great wrestler. As the weeks passed Shawn became better and better, he gained a lot of popularity and respect from the trainers and the fellow students, it was at this time when they decided to give him a shot at a real wrestling match with a real wrestler, many expected it to be Jeff considering the fact that they were related but it was not to be, the owner of the promotion had something else in mind, the opponent Shawn had to face was one of the best FAW had ever had not only that, but he was the World Heavyweight Champion, this is the first time that Shawn felt scared about something, here was a man that was just pure heel from top to bottom, he knew his father would be watching over the match but he didn’t want him to do anything, he wanted to do this on his own. The 9th Wonder of Foam, a funny name to be sure to most people but he was great and he had won inter-promotional tournaments, he and Jeff were a couple of weeks away from a match themselves, it would be Jeff H. vs. 9th Wonder of Foam for the World title, this match was purely a mind game from Foam’s point of view. The scene shows the Academy area where everyone trained, Jeff H., 9th Wonder of Foam, Shawn Harris, several trainers and several students were standing around the ring, the ring itself was actually nicely built, the ropes and ring apron were blue with the ring mat being black. Black steel steps were fitted to all four black ring posts. The size was just a little bit bigger than a normal FAW ring so that multiple people could train at the same time; Foamy was stood on one side of the ring looking cocky with the world title draped over his shoulder, on the other side Jeff was stood with Shawn getting him prepared for the match. Shawn is still clearly the smallest guy there but it was his lack of his and muscle mass that made him improves his actual technical wrestling ability. Foamy walks up the ring steps and gets into the ring, Shawn has a look of worry on his face. “Well I think you can take him, you’re a Harris and Harris’ never lose.” Jeff says encouragingly while standing next to Shawn, “The minute you ask for this match to stop and he doesn’t, I’ll be in there in about two seconds flat, one second to get into the ring, one second to knock him on his ass alright?” Shawn shakes his head, “No dad, I want to do this on my own, if I am going to become a great wrestler like you then I need to take him down, so what if he’s the World Champion, he only beat some has-been for the title anyway. If things go bad, that’s just the way life goes.” Shawn turns to his father, nods then gets into the ring, he squares up to Foamy who is slightly taller than he is, Foamy just grins at him then pie faces him to the floor. A trainer gets in the ring to referee the match and someone hits a bell, Shawn scrambles to his feet and runs at Foamy, he hits him with a stiff punch to the face, the punch itself was enough to make several trainers cringe. Shawn looks knocked out on the floor as Foamy walks over to him and smirks but that’s cut short though as Shawn hits him with a drop toe hold then rolls to his side and locks in a triangle choke, Foamy quickly gets out of it and kicks Shawn while he is down, he picks Shawn up and begins punching him hard in the face, the trainer threatens to disqualify him, Foamy hits a stalling suplex, picks him back up and hits him with the FoamyBomb. He picks him back up and hits him with another and another until finally deciding to go for the pin, the trainer counts the pin and the bell rings. Shawn lays on the floor with his lip busted wide open, Foamy begins laughing before picking him back up, and he grabs him by the throat and begins shouting at him. Jeff has seen enough, he gets in the ring and runs at Foamy, he clobbers him in the back of the head, Foamy turns around and begins punching Jeff in the face, Jeff returns the blows then throws him to the turnbuckle, before Foamy can think about doing anything Jeff has already crushed him in the corner with a splash, he takes Foamy down with the Chain Reaction, the trainers and students try breaking it up, Foamy is pulled away from the jaws of Jeff, Jeff and Foamy are both on their feet, Foamy thinks twice before doing anything else on this day and decides to get out of the ring. Harming another mans family member can be something not to get involved in especially if it’s Jeff H.’ family and especially if you haven’t got a sledgehammer defending you. Foamy takes his title and walks off, Shawn struggles to get to his feet then tastes his own blood, he gives a slight sadistic smile. “You alright son?” Jeff asks, rather concerned. Shawn looks up at Jeff, still smiling, he spits out some blood to the floor then turns back to Jeff, he nods his head. “This is definitely something I want to get into.” “Are you sure?” Jeff replies with a look of bewilderment on his face, “You’re face is bruised and you’re bleeding all over.” “Dad, dude, come on, I lived in Brooklyn, 9th Wonder of soap bubbles or whatever didn’t even hurt me, I was unprepared but still, I’ve had big gangsters hit me in the face with guns before..” Shawn says that then touches his eye which is starting to get swollen, he winces slightly. “They hit you with a gun? What the fuck kind of place was that?” “Oh it was a hardcore match, sort of, Guns, Whores and Rastas… Oh My, it was my friend Master D’s invention.” Shawn replies now looking a little less glazed over. “Whores, you… wha? Why would you have whores in the middle of a wrestling match?” Jeff asks while trying to hide a level of jealously. “Well, we usually had a hardcore match, with ‘original gangsters’ involved then we’d have sex with prostitutes. It was actually quite fun; it made us get more members actually. Anyway, I should probably get cleaned up.” Shawn says while trying not to spit out blood while talking. Shawn gets out of the ring and heads to the locker-rooms, he was lying, he was in pain and a lot of it, but if you want to make it in this business then you need to have some kind of pain barrier, he knew that he couldn’t back out of wrestling, so he took a couple of stiff shots from the world champion, he knew that he’d have to go through more than that in the future if he ever wanted to be a world champion, Jeff H. was once set on fire in a FAW Hardcore title match which Jeff won, Jeff’s had more stitches and broken bones in the last ten years than most people have in their whole lives, Jeff still came back for more, he was a badass, Shawn wanted to be like his father, he tasted his own blood and for some sick reason, he liked it, it gave him adrenaline like he never had before, it was just a pity Foamy was already gone by that point. Jeff walks into the locker-room and takes a seat next to Shawn; his facial expression is a little different than it was out there. “What’s wrong?” asks Shawn. “There’s something I need to tell you and you probably won’t like it.” Says Jeff. “I’m fired aren’t I?” replies Shawn, his face has fallen slightly. “Actually no, I couldn’t tell you out there but I have decided to leave FAW and go elsewhere, I mean, I don’t know if you want to stay here or not but if you came with me you would have a better learning experience, you see, after WFW closed some previous owner decided to open Dynasty Wrestling Alliance, I know, two weeks away from my World title shot in FAW but DWA is where it’s all at I’m afraid.” Jeff waits for Shawn to say something; there is an awkward silence for a moment. “Well, I say… Fuck FAW! I’ve seen DWA, it does look very cool. Do they have a training camp?” Shawn asks enthusiastically. “Well, not really. I mean, you will be trained you see when I debut I’ll be entering as the new member of Dangerous Intentions with Nick Kelly, Kaanen Thorne and good ole Adam Masterson, you’ll be training with us, me and Masterson especially, these trainers here, they’re good I guess but I can’t see a single star in any of them and I usually have an eye for these things, I could have become a trainer if my wrestling career was over but that’s besides the point. The point is, no one in this training camp is going to become a big star and why? Because none of the trainers knows what it takes to become a big star, you have to be one to pass on your secrets so you’ll be hanging around with Dangerous Intentions, the most elite group ever. You will become a big star one day Shawn; you’re going to be a product of Jeff H..” Jeff finishes then puts his hand inside his pocket and pulls out a pack of cigarettes, he takes one out and lights it up, and he takes a deep drag on it then blows the smoke into the air. Shawn leans back and smiles, “Alright then, I’m with you, I will become a big star one day and who knows what’s going to happen, I do know that my skills need to be severely improved and this is the only time I have realised this since being here, you see, in that run down wrestling school you found me, I look at wrestling tapes of you and your best matches then I think back to what the matches were like and I laugh, they’re pathetic. Their heart was in the right place but it’s a shame the talent wasn’t, I feel like I’ve improved though.” “You have Shawn, you definitely have and the best thing is, you’re only 18, you have years ahead of you, I mean look at me, I’m 36 and I’m still going.” Jeff gives a quick smile then flicks his ash on the floor. “Do you think I could ever win a World title?” Shawn asks. Jeff looks at Shawn then grins, “I have a feeling that you’re going to make quite the impact when you debut on the big stage as a wrestler, and do I think you could ever win a world title? It’s a fucking certainty mate. Anyway, you have your shower, I need to go and find the owner and tell the bitch I’m quitting.” Jeff slaps Shawn on the back then leaves the locker-room. Shawn gets to his feet then walks into the shower-room and Jeff leaves the building. It was at this time when Jeff told the owner to stick her job up her anally diseased ass, he left with Shawn and they headed for Dynasty Wrestling Alliance, the re-union between Jeff and Adam was made and the love obsession for Shawn lusting after Jean Deas began. As Shawn tried to hold back the pain in his eyes from the beating Foamy gave him, Jeff saw right through it, Shawn desperately tried to improve himself for his father, the pressure was there and even though he was beaten unfairly, Shawn felt like he had let his father down, it was a feeling like no other. Shawn knew it was his soul purpose to become a great superstar but he was disappointed that he didn’t pick it up quicker; the thing he didn’t know was that Jeff was more proud of him than ever.
