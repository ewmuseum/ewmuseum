The Rise Of An Evolution: The Thunder Within

8 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/06 Las Vegas, Nevada, February 15th 2006 Shawn hit the floor hard, his lip had been busted open, and stitches he got from the attack off of Vince Jones ripped open, blood was pouring out of every orifice and flowing down the street like a river. Shawn opened his eyes and coughed out blood before turning his head and given another impact to the face from what looked like a boot. He gets dragged up to his feet and thrown against a wall. He could see them; three of them all bigger than he was. Two of them being of the African-American persuasion the other one a pale white colour. They all take turns punching him in his already damaged ribs, Shawn screams out in pain after every hit. People are standing there in the background watching his pain, his torture but remain too scared to do anything but as the white guy goes for another punch he suddenly falls to the floor, the other two then follow suit and hit the floor too. “Are you alright man?” A voice asks. Shawn could barely see due to the amount of blood that filled his eyes, the swelling didn’t help either. He stumbles forward and collapses into the mans hands; he looks up and could barely recognize the face but he did and it made him smile for almost a moment before he passed out from the pain. It was Jimmy Thunder. Las Vegas, Nevada, February 17th 2006 Shawn opened his eyes again and looked around cautiously. The first thing he noticed was that he wasn’t outside anymore; he was in fact in a hospital. Since passing out he couldn’t remember a thing; the last thing he could remember however was the face of Jimmy Thunder, Jeff’s long time friend. After Bloodbox folded Shawn had heard that Jimmy went back to Australia and it made him think, ‘was it a dream?’ it could have been a dream; unless he was seeing things from the loss of blood. The last time Shawn had anything to do with Jimmy was back in Bloodbox, opposing the Dangerous Intentions group. The two had a history together that spanned a few years as Jimmy gave a helping hand in the training of Shawn at the beginning in DWA. The two eventually came to blows in GCW over the World title picture but the match never happened as the promotion closed. Jeff’s history with the guy was a little more light hearted as it was Jeff that had a hand in making Jimmy Thunder a top superstar, because of that Jimmy stayed loyal to Shawn’s father. Anytime Jeff H. was in trouble it would be Jimmy Thunder that came to the rescue and he stayed around making sure he had Jeff’s back at all times. The two together were unstoppable, when all four Dangerous Intentions members were together they were simply elite. Maybe this was why Shawn saw his face before passing out, it could have been someone else entirely that helped him out and only wished he had been there like he was for his father. The fact that the two men hated each other didn’t occur to him; he then began thinking who it could have been that saved him if it wasn’t Jimmy Thunder. Shawn begins coughing with his hands cupped over his mouth; he takes a look at them afterwards and sees blood on his palms. He could taste the blood at the back of his throat, a iron sort of taste and it didn’t go down well. The three men that brutally assaulted him was the result of protecting the girl he met at the prison six days ago. Since Shawn and Faye met they had kept in touch and started to hang out, Shawn was infatuated with her beauty, grace and personality. She did have one bad point and that was a history of going out with the wrong people, one of them a coloured guy named Jackson. A nasty brutal man that spent his money on drugs and knocking around the women he was with at the time. Faye finally took a stand and decided to leave him but kept getting constant threats from him, out with his friends two days go they saw Shawn and Faye walking together along the street on their way to a bar. Shawn despite having a heart of a lion like his father did not have his size and strength and was vastly outnumbered. Shawn was hoping that after awhile he would go numb to the pain and be able to fight back, despite a good start in the fight he took most of the punishment and he didn’t go numb. He felt every punch, every kick, he could feel and hear his ribs cracking and breaking. Shawn had no doubt in his mind that he would have died that day if it were not for being rescued. If there was one thing Shawn was happy with it was the fact that Faye did not get hurt. If there was one thing Shawn knew about pain it was that it hurt; obviously, yet his father never seemed to feel it. The amount of punishment of pain that his father took over the years made him numb to the pain; he could be set of fire and not even flinch. People say it was a result of being dead for the three minutes but something inside of his brain stopped him from feeling pain, maybe it felt sorry for him or something. Shawn wanted that ability, he wanted it without going through all his father did though but he knew that’d never happen. Shawn used the bars on the edge of the bed to push himself into an upright position; he began coughing again then drank water out of a glass that had been placed next to him. The taste of it hitting the back of his throat was a great sensation, he puts the glass down and realises that he was alone in the room, not for long though as a doctor then walked into the room and seemed surprised that Shawn had awoken. “How are we feeling today Mr. Harrison?” the doctor asked with a smile on his face. “Like shit.” Shawn replied. “Understandable, understandable, you did just get a beating off of three men.” The doctor replied with a smile forming at the cracks at the edge of his mouth. “How did I get here anyway doc?” Shawn asked. “You don’t remember?” The doctor responded with a question. “Evidently.” Shawn answered. “Well he said he was your friend, he was a tall man though. I’d have to say he was about seven feet tall. Came along with a much short woman didn’t get her name though.” The doctor replied. At that moment Shawn knew he hadn’t been dreaming, unless this was in fact a dream in it self but what are the chances of that happening? Just then Shawn began floating into the air then shot the doctor with a fireball… I kid. But now the answer begged another question, what was Jimmy Thunder doing thousands of miles from his home? Hopefully he was still about so said question could be answered. “Well I guess I’ll tell you your injuries.” the doctor said. “You’ve suffered two broken ribs, the broken ribs you suffered before that have also been re-broken, fractured collar bone, broken nose, your stitches had to be re-stitched and your lip needed stitching up also. A couple of black eyes, aside from that you’re absolutely fine.” Shawn shook his head and looked out of the window; the sun had just begun to set. “How long have I been out?” “About two and a half days. We thought you were in a coma to be honest.” The doctor answered, Shawn couldn’t believe it, it had only seemed like a second ago since he was being booted in the face now it was Friday. “I’ll be back to check on you later.” And with that the doctor put his chart back on his bed and walked out of the room not a moment too soon after though a huge figure walked through the door, Jimmy Thunder. Shawn looked on in shock and a little fear overcame him for a second. The last time these guys met, it was at the receiving end of Jimmy’s fist. Jimmy grinned in stylish fashion though and slammed the door shut; he took a seat next to Shawn’s bed and nodded. “So, you silly fuck. What have you been doing now? Have you been fighting like a bad boy?” Jimmy asked with a smile on his face. “What the hell are YOU doing? Why aren’t you in Australia?” Shawn replied with a slight scowl in his tone. “Came to Nevada to see your dad actually. Heard he got himself locked up, man I was standing right there when he crushed that fucker Caine.” Jimmy replied, doing hand movements signifying a crushing motion. “Yeah answer the question.” Shawn snapped back. “That’s no way to speak to the man that saved you.” Jimmy snapped back. “Last time I looked we weren’t friends, so I am just wondering why the hell you did what you did and what the fuck did you use?” Shawn asked. “A baseball bat and as I told you, I’m here to see your dad. I was on my way there actually and I saw you getting your ass kicked by a gang of thugs. Didn’t know who it was though when I first took them down. I was quite shocked because I thought you lived in Florida. But I thought I’d bring you to the hospital anyway. You were in bad shape my boy, on life support, iv drip and not a fuckin’ barbeque in sight!” Jimmy replied. Shawn grinned slightly, “I did good though, I kicked one right in the balls. Where’s Faye? Is she alright?” “No.” Jimmy answered, Shawn then stared at Jimmy horrified and fearing for the worst. “I’m just fuckin’ with ya.” Shawn then looked relieved. “She’s fine, she hasn’t left your side actually since you were admitted into the hospital… neither have I actually. Despite the fact you’re a pain in the fuckin’ ass you’re a pretty good kid overall. Remind me of your father actually but I’m sure that’s not the first time you’ve heard that. Faye and I had a chance to get to know each other a little though. I told her about you.” Shawn then got another horrified look on his face. “What did you say?!” “Nothing bad, I told her you were a professional wrestler.” Jimmy answered. Shawn looked relieved again and said, “I still am a professional wrestler.” “Yeah? What company do you work for? … Exactly. Told her about our past accolades then I started talking about Jeff. She looked pretty shocked after what I told her he went through since I’ve known him. Said good things about you though, hard to find them at points.” Jimmy said. “You’re not an angel yourself Jimmy.” Shawn retorted. “Thank you for saving me though Jimmy, I thought I was dreaming at first when I saw your face, where are the fuckers that did this to me?” “Two of them are in this hospital, I fractured one of their skulls with the bat and he’s in worst shape than you are at the moment I think. The other one has a neck brace on so I’m thinking I did some damage. The third guy got arrested. Enough about them anyway, what I came to talk to you about was getting you ready for the ring again. I’m not sure if I’m going to wrestle again at the moment, most of the places I joined were only to help out Jeff when he was in trouble. I’ve won four World titles so I don’t really care anymore. You though, you have a future in this business Shawn. It was in your blood since the beginning and I’m sure Jeff told you at some point to never give up. You’ll get to the top eventually, when you first started wrestling regularly in GCW you were hyped up as the son of All Fucking That. You were that hyped that you were thrusted into the main event. I mean yeah you won that Survivor Match thing but really, they were all curtain jerkers are best. Thing was you have the talent to back up the hype, partly thanks to me. I still believe that’s true now, I think you’re going to be big and even though you may have believed you were destined to be the youngest World Champion in the business you’ve now realised it takes a while I think.” Jimmy said. Shawn knew he was right, it did take longer than a few months in the business to win the World Championship, at the beginning Shawn started believing the hype, and he still does now in fact but not so much. Had GCW not closed that destiny could have been fulfilled, but now at the age of 20 and soon to be 21, he knows he’s not going to fulfil that destiny. It burned at him inside; if he had won the Championship then it would have instantly broken him away from Jeff and got him out of his shadow. He knew that one day somewhere he’d win a World Championship, he had another 20 to 30 years left of a career, unless he suffers a career ending injury then he’s going to be around for a long time to come. “You know something Jimmy, I’ll agree with everything you said. But you seem to forget the fact that even you wouldn’t be where you are now if it wasn’t for my father. Because you were let into Dangerous Intentions you were hyped up to the max and you eventually became a main event player, maybe even quicker than I did. We started somewhere around the same time Jimmy, yeah you were already a wrestler and I had a lot of learning to do but we have sort of gone down a parallel path. You’re happy with conformity Jimmy, I’m not, I like being unique. Unique is being the youngest World Champion ever, but that‘ll never happen for me now. And you know what? At this moment in time, I really couldn’t give a shit. I was knocked out cold for two days, my father; you know what’s up with him?” Shawn asks. “No what?” Jimmy asks. “He can’t feel pain, I don’t know how he does it but he can take a beating like no other and that’s when they actually are able to give him a beating. You have no idea how weak I feel at the moment, before I thought, no I won’t give up but now I’m beginning to second guess myself. Am I supposed to feel this much pain for a wrestler?” Shawn replies. Jimmy shakes his head, almost in disappointment. “Don’t be fuckin’ stupid. Of course all wrestlers feel pain, just because you lived in Brooklyn for a chunk of your life doesn’t mean you should be hard as a rock. You think Jeff didn’t feel pain? Do you know why? Because he was hopped up on pain killers most of the time. It hurts him to breathe; he hasn’t got an addiction to the pills though because he’s always in pain. I’ve seen him before after a match when he didn’t have any painkillers left and the effects began to wear off. Thought he was gonna rip me fuckin’ head off! Pain killers is Jeff’s secret to wrestling, it’s not actually illegal either if you have doctors permission to take them, since he came out of hiding I know full well how he’s been physically, truthfully Shawn, I’m glad he’s in prison because now he can finally rest.” “You think he’s resting there? Do you know how much they’ll try and get to him just so they can show that they took down a big man?” Shawn asked. “Nah I don’t think so, as long as Jeff gets his pain medication and he stays calm, he’ll be alright. He was a wreck and he needed the time to recover, since Bloodbox closed you’re probably the only wrestler that’s gone somewhere high profile and I bet that it wasn’t because of who your father was.” Replied Jimmy. “Speaking of rest, you need some of it too.” “No I don’t.” Shawn snaps back, “I’ve been a fucking sleep for the past two days; I want to go home away from all these sick people.” “Well you’re going to be here until they fix you up so get comfortable, I’ll bring you some shit in, PSP, portable DVD player and what not. Out of your money of course. I took the liberty of taking the card out of your wallet so you needn’t worry heh heh.” Jimmy says laughingly. Shawn shot him an evil glare but that ended as soon as a light knock was heard at the door. “That’ll be Faye wanting to see you. I’ll give you one piece of advice Shawn, don’t be an asshole to this one alright? She’s a good one, bit of a battered wife but hopefully you can change that.” Shawn exhales some oxygen and nods his head. “Alright, thanks Jimmy. Despite our past I’m glad we can look forward to a brighter future.” “We’ll be friends one day Shawn, you’re showing signs of growing up anyway and that’s a good start. I’m going, have fun with the woman.” Jimmy said. He then stood up out of the chair and shook Shawn’s hand, the first handshake since 2003 when they first met. Jimmy then opened the door, Faye stood there looking happy at the fact that Shawn was awake, she walked into the room and Jimmy looked behind the two chatting, he grinned then shut the door behind him knowing that Shawn now had a great chance at a brighter tomorrow ahead of him. Fade.
