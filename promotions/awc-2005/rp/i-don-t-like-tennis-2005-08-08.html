I Don't Like Tennis

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th August It was another warm, sunny Floridian afternoon when we found Andy M. around the rear of his 'Super Cool Scottish Kingdom of Brilliance', or 'house', take your pick. Sweltering under the mid-day sun, Andy was taking full advantage of the latest addition to his country estate; a tennis court. Wearing a white headband, white polo shirt, white shorts, and yup, you guessed it, white trainers, racket-swinging Andy was attempting to strike tennis balls being launched at him by a machine on the other side of the net. Naturally, the self-styled Scottish King of Cool is failing miserably; shown by the vast amount of yellow balls which had gathered behind him. 'God dammit!' Andy puffed, 'this tennis shit isn't all it's made out to be!' Another ball was flung in Andy's direction by the 'cannon', this time, however, Andy did manage to connect; just not very well. The ball ricocheted off of the frame of the racket, sending it flying over the wire-mesh safety barriers, and straight into Andy's pool, where Andy's prized fire-breather, Snowball, was enjoying an early afternoon dip. Not that Andy noticed, however, he was more concerned with the latest ball slung in his direction, and his complete lack of ability to hit it. 'Andy!' a familar voice yelled out across the court. Andy turned to his right, to see his younger brother Cayle closing the court's gate behind him. A smile across his face, Cayle was wearing a sky-blue coloured baseball jersey, a bandana, and a pair of beige coloured cargo pants. 'What the hell is going on with the tennis court?!' Andy parted his lips to respond, but before he could, a tennis ball thudded against the side of his head. 'Ahhh!' Andy yelled, rubbing the side of his head and cursing under his breath, before taking a few steps towards his brother, so as to avoid any further tennis ball strikes. 'What do you mean, brother?' he asked. Cayle chuckled, 'Where did it come from all of a sudden? And why!?!' 'Ahhhh, you see, I have a plan!' Andy announced with a smile on his face and a glimmer in his eye. 'See, rumor has it that there's some punk-ass kid running around the tennis world, apparently he's the 'next big thing,' and he's stolen my name, god damn!' 'Oh, I know who you mean,' Cayle said, raising an eyebrow. 'So, what does this oh-so mysterious tennis player have to do with this court?' 'I have a dream, brother! I shall practice the sport until I am on a level comparable to this name-thief! And when the time is right I shall have Grobschnit dispose of him... I'll take his place and do some ass-kicking at Wimbledon, whilst being able to able to sleep easy at night, knowing that some spotty little faggot isn't stealing my thunder!' Andy nodded, believing this incredibly awesome plan to be nothing but foolproof. Cayle, of course, knew that the whole idea was preposterous, but he had learned a long time ago that it would be damn pointless to try and convince Andy otherwise. Instead, he nodded agreeably, and said; 'Uh sure Andy, that sounds like a superb idea.' 'Indeed Cayle! Nothing but coolness...' Andy responded, raising his hand to his brow, wiping the sweat away. 'Anyway, enough practice for one day, I'm knackered...' 'How long have you been playing for?' 'Ohhhh, at LEAST ten minutes! SHATTERING work!' Andy sighed, as he wandered across the court towards the ball cannon, where he had set down a towel and a chilled bottle of mineral water; Highland Spring, imported from Scotland, of course. Firstly, however, Andy switched the cannon off, thus halting the near-endless stream of tennis balls that had been fired from it. Leaning over, Andy picked up the bottle and towel, throwing the towel around his shoulders, and taking a swig of the cool, fresh liquid. 'So Cayle, what brings you to Andyville on this fine afternoon? Did your girlfriend kick you out again, hmmm?' Cayle grimaced, 'No, she's gone down to see her mother for a couple of days, so I figured I'd come over here and throw twigs at Grobschnit, or something like that.' 'Visiting her mother? Why didn't you go with her?' 'Because the last time I went down there she kept calling me 'Kyle,' tried to pleat my hair and put it in pigtails, before using my bandana as a handkerchief...' Pausing from taking a sip of water, an almost shocked look came across Andy's face as he hear this. 'God damn, she's the psycho... why, that's the craziest thing I've heard since some nutjob came up to me in the street and told me that my 'loss to Nalbandian made them choke on their strawberries and cream,' what the hell is that all about!?!' Cayle shrugged, and followed his brother as he walked out of the court towards the swimming pool area. Like most of Andyville, this part of the estate was pretty ludicrous; the poolside area was rife with sculptures of such 'legends' as Noel Edmonds, Jeremy Beadle and David Hasselhoff, and the tiling at the bottom of the pool read 'Andy Is Cool Than You!' Marvellous. Snowball, by now, was lying belly-up on one of the sun loungers, drying off after her daily swim. As the brothers walked past the resting badger, Andy acknowledged her. 'Hey Snowball! What's up you crazy-ass motherfucker?' Startled, Snowball rolled over onto her stomach, glanced at Andy and Cayle, and responded in the only way that she knew; with a small burst of fire, followed by a quick nod. “Alright playa, keep it real…” uttered Andy in response, with a wink to his fuzzy gangsta homegirl. “Damn, that think creeps me out,” Cayle proclaimed. Andy merely nodded a response, before stopping at an open set of French doors leading into the M. mansion. He pulled the towel from around his neck, and wiped some more sweat from his brow, before proclaiming; “Well, I’m going to hit the shower, and then go and chill out in the Andycave for a while. You can make yourself at home; take Snowball for a walk, or something like that… don’t feed her too much though, unless you want her to scorch your punk ass.” Cayle raised an eyebrow, “the Andycave, what the hell is that?” he asked. “What!?!” Andy yelled, a surprised look coming across his face. “Have I never shown you the greatest part of Andyville before? Why, it is a Mecca for all that is truly awesome in the world! Come on!” -------------------- The Andycave was indeed a rather spectacular place. Built underground directly underneath the mansion’s main hall, it looked more like a super-hero’s cave hideaway more than anything else. It was a sight to behold; its black expansive surroundings lit up by numerous bulbs. A multi-levelled facility, the Andycave was accessible by a secret elevator hidden behind a bookcase in the mansion, with the only methods of “escape” being the elevator in question and a trip up the long, steel ramp, which led out of a secretly removable patch of grass and out onto the lawn. The cave was home to many things; an odd patch of grass with a soccer goal, the Andymobile (a multi-coloured, clapped-out Volkswagen hippy van), a pretty huge stereo system with an impressive array of CDs, massive, 30ft posters of the Aberdeen FC first team, and you can bet your ass that it was air-conditioned. On the mezzanine level, the elevator came to a halt with one of these “bing,” noises, and the doors gently slid open. Cayle stepped out with his brother (who was now clad in a black sleeves Motorhead t-shirt and a pair of sandy coloured cargo shorts), looking around in awe at his surroundings. The Andycave, although rather ludicrous, was quite possibly the most incredible thing that Cayle (and you, for that matter), had ever seen in his life. “Wow… Andy…” Cayle managed to utter a few words, although he was pretty much speechless. Andy nodded smugly. “Welcome, my brother to the Andycave! Home of all that is good and pure in-“ Andy began, although immediately, something caught his eye. He looked across the mezzanine level, to find a certain green O.G. sitting behind Andy’s computer, glancing at obscene pictures of slimey green aliens, wearing a set of booming headphones. “Wait a minute… ‘Schnit!” Andy immediately jogged across to his intergalactic friend, and, knowing Grobschnit should even be down in the Andycave, pulled the headphones him his head. “Wtf!?! AHHH FAGITZ!” Grobschnit, clad in a baggy white t-shirt, baggy jeans and a bandana tied around his head like cool ghetto homeboys do, almost leapt out of his chair. Immediately he closed the window, to the chuckles of Cayle. “Grobschnit, dude, I let you out of your dungeon for ONE FREAKING DAY, and already you invade the Andycave!?! And how the hell did you find the Temple of Cool, anyway? THIS PLACE IS MEANT TO BE HIDDEN!” Andy shouted. Uneasy, Grobschnit searched the back of his expansive mind for a response that wouldn’t piss Andy off even more. “Wel Andee, hyding itt beehind a bookase iz nut da bezt playse, cuz evreewon duz dat…” he explained. Andy shook his head, and grabbed the ‘Schnit by one of his Shrek-like ears, pulling him up from the chair. “AIEEEEE!” whelped Grobber in pain. “Cayle,” Andy began, dragging Grob along towards his brother by the ear, “care to take this green faggot back to his dungeon? Make sure you lock him up good and proper, he’s in there for at LEAST another month…” “OMG U FAGIT!” wailed Grobschnit in protest. Cayle smiled, as he took over captivity of the creature from Andy. “No problem…” he said, as he turned around, and headed back for the elevator. “Thank you,” Andy said in acknowledgement, as the elevator doors gently slid shut. He shook his head, before turning back to face the computer, which was still up and running. With a shrug, Andy walked across, and sat himself down in the plush leather chair that ‘Schnit had been occupying, before grabbing the mouse, and loading up his internet browser. “Hmmm, well, I suppose I could check my inbox for uber fan mail of doom!” Andy proclaimed with a smile, as he loaded up his official AWC inbox. Soon enough, the words “Welcome, Andy, you have 579 new emails,” followed by a tediously long list of emails. Andy shakes his head, “God damn, how do I work my way through all this crap… I know, I’ll just pick some at random, I’ll get Alvaro to type up some generic crap for the rest, wooo!” Andy clicked randomly on one of the many pieces of mail, and soon it was up on the screen. From: Shylo K. Elliot ([email removed]) To: Andy M. ([email removed]) Date: 3rd August, 2005 GO BAK 2 SCOTTLAND AND DYE U SHEPE-FUKING IDOIT At this, Andy raised an eyebrow “Uhh, what the hell?” he said, as he hit the “reply” button. Hey, What the hell are you talking about, you crazy son of a bitch? How old are you, dude, like 11 or something? Seriously, go and watch Spongebob… or a Jonny Sake match, that’s always good for a laugh too. And what the hell kind of a name is Shylo anyway? Your parents must really love you, that’s almost as lame as calling your kid “Terrence” or something. Bemused by the current grammatical situation of the internet generation, Andy sent away his response, and immediately opened up another random email. From: Bobert ([email removed]) To: Andy M. ([email removed]) Date: 4th August, 2005 Dear Irish-fucker, Andy Murphy. I am a new viewer to, and have been appreciating the Atlantic Wrestling Club. Things were wonderful...until you came, you big dumb fuck, go back to your homeland, with your sheep-banging, and Guinness, and off my TV. Sincerely, Bobert. Chuckling as he went, Andy began typing a response. Bobert… what the hell, another silly name? Do you know this “Shylo” guy, by any chance? Anyway, get your facts straight little boy; I’m the Scottish King of Cool, baby! I’m not Irish… nothing against my homeboys Paddy and Mickey, but Guinness is bloody disgusting! The only thing that Guinness is preferable to is drinking bleach. Stupid, stupid boy, go away back to your trailer park. “Hasn’t anyone got a good word to say these days?” Andy questioned, as he opened up the next piece of mail. From: Trevor ([email removed]) To: Andy M. ([email removed]) Date: 6th August, 2005 OMG OMG OMG!!!!! I CANT BELIEVE I AM SENDING A EMAIL TO ANDY M.!!!!111oneone I R SO LYKE UR BIGGEST FAN ROFL!!! U AUTOGRAPHED MA HAND AT AN AWC SHOW AN I GOT IT TATTOOED!!!! I LUV U ANDY M. U R THE ROXORZ!!!!! I COME TO ALL DA SHOWS AN I TRY TO COME TO UR LOCKER ROOM BUT THEY NEVA LET ME!!!! CAN I HAVE A BACKSTAGE PASS!?!?! KTHX!!! “What the fuck?” Andy questioned. Hello Trevor, Thanks for all the flattery buddy, but please, I think you need to get yourself some help there, okay? You got my signature tattooed on your hand, what the hell? I mean, there’s not much better than the Scottish King of Cool, but that’s a little insane… and I’ll hook you up with a backstage pass as soon as you go get some therapy are something. My name is Andy M., not Jesus! Sending away a final reply, Andy eventually grew tired of reading through the emails, and closed his browser. “Psychos… how come I never seem to attract people without mental defects, eh?” Andy shook his head, as we left him in the comfortable surroundings of the Andycave.
