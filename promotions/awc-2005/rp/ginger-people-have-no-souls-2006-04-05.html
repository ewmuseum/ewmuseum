Ginger people have no souls

5 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th april Irishmen and Pirates, two of funniest looking people in the world, and they all have on thing in common, they are only sober for 8 hours a day – that includes sleep, which normally includes a midnight binge. But here is a recent story about an Irishman, an orange pirate, and an American soldier. ------ Gadget: Right…so that 555 – 9860 for OrangeBeard and 555 – 4326 for Killian’s Hotel room. A yellow pages is spread out on the polished redwood table in the heart of Gadget’s kitchen, he has his Verizon Wireless cell phone in his left hand, dialing in the numbers of Orangebeard’s home, and the number for the Plaza hotel room Killian is currently residing in. Gadget:Right, I only hear one dial tone, is it supposed like that? I cannot get used to this bloody three way calling, why cant everyone just use Army regulated radio’s and speak bleeding English? Orangebeard: Yarrrr! Who be thar? Killian’s answer phone: Hello, I cannot get to the phone right now, as I am probably out drinking or watching The Wizard of Oz and listening to YMCA, please ring back later. And don’t leave a message after the tone! Orangebeard: Yarr? It seems to be some futuristic voice box! How do the elves get in? Gadget:No its an answering machine you ginger oaf! Orangebeard:(in a startled voice) Olly, the elves are having an uprising, DON’T LET THEM GET THE RUM! Olly: Olly: Take your f$%^ing rum and shove it up ya mother's a$$ you ginger b£$%@^d Gadget: (whispering) Someone didn’t have their Nescafe cracker this morning. Orangebeard: Yarr! Yarr? Yaaaaarrrrr! Gadget: What the hell you talking about? Yarr! Yarrr? Yarrrr! Orangebeard: What did you just say about my mother you swashbuckling little… Olly: $&!t, I’ll bum your chickens and call you sally. Gadget: Will you shut up and let me speak?!? Orangebeard: Yarr! Gadget: Thank you, right…so where was I? Oh yeah, you are going to meet me in 3 hours, so 30 minutes past that freckle on your right wrist in your time zone, at the Nag’s head inn on 45th street. Kapeesh? Orangebeard: My name isn’t Kapeesh, and be there treasure in this here inn? Gadget: No! Orangebeard: Yes there is, there’s treasure everywhere, even in your ring of fire. Gadget: Please tell me you are not referring to my anus, and if you want there to be, there is treasure in the pub. Orangebeard: See, isn’t it better to admit you were wrong? Gadget:(Sighs) it sure does. Orangebeard: Yarr!! I’ll have Olly drive me there for then. Gadget closes his flip-phone and hangs his head in shame, as he finally realizes that he will be sat in a pub, surrounded by completely sane people (maybe a few drunks, and Irishmen) but the majority will be sane. And he will be sat with a bearded ginger pirate, and a parrot that swears at random people and thinks it’s a chauffer. I think gutted just sums up this situation. --------3 hours later (or 30 minutes passed that freckle)--- A large, Sandy coloured, Humvee pulled up into the parking lot of the Nags Head Inn. The left hand door of the humvee opened with a large screech (well its hard to have a perfect sounding door when its taken heavy fire from a fixed machine gun). And out popped Gadget, dressed in his army gear (its good for free drinks), helmet under his arm, a shameful face, and a clenched right fist. He approached the front door of the pub, turned round, and watched as Orangebeards hand painted (to look like a pirate ship) sedan pulled up next to the Humvee. Gadget stood in aw, as the man that emerged from the car looked worse then he had anticipated, although he knew looking like a complete Pratt was imminent, but this was a stretch too far in the wrong direction. He was wearing a skin-tight black robe, with a scissor slit bandana, swashbuckling boots (including brace straps) and a belt made of sellotape with a rubber duck stuck to the front, and to top it all off, the parrot was on his shoulder. Gadget turned and ran into pub, just before Orangebeard could see him past his eye patch (that for some reason had an eyeball size whole cut out of the middle of it. ------- Inside the pub The pub was not as expected, it was split into quarters, there was the “Grufty Mans Quarter”, which was basically all large men, in leather waistcoats, with bald heads, tattoos from head to toe and a large pint of Cobra. The next quarter seemed to be the “Trekkies Quarter” which was filled with well…. you guessed it, Trekkies. The quarter after that was the “Slaughterhouse rejects”, they were basically men eating slabs of meat, with large knives, forks, spoons and one even had a pickaxe and an axe to attack the 100 oz steak that was slumped before him. And the final quarter was the “Businessman’s Quarter” which was men, with square eyes, touch typing on work supplied laptops. Gadget stood and scanned the room for an empty table that was no-were near the slaughterhouse rejects or the grufts. And then he spotted a secluded cubicle in the middle of the trekkies and the businessmen, which he quickly reserved with a large, and free, pint of Strongbow. He sat down, put his helmet next to his pint, and awaited the arrival, of his extremely deranged frie….sorry acquaintance. ------15 minutes later Gadget: Where the hell is the ginger git? Orangebeard: Yarr mi harties, it be I Captain Orangebeard, here with Olly the random parrot, and my friend the hook. Orangebeard had barged through the door, bellowing his balls off, and waving a broken coat hanger hook he just shoved up his sleeve. Gadget: get over here before, you embarrass yourself. Orangebeard: Yarr! It be too late for that Andre lad. Gadget: How the hell! Do you know my name? Olly: Take your name and shove it up that trekkies a$$ with that rejects pickaxe, you daft c^*! Orangebeard: Yarr! I will sit on the trekkie side then, they share the same passions. Trekkie 1: In what way do we share the same passions? We do not look for treasure, we do not have intercourse in the galley, and we don’t use old-fashioned modes of transport, you spongebob wanna-be. Orangebeard: Yarr, the pirate ship could use an upgrade. Gadget: Its not a pirate ship, it’s a hand-painted sedan. Say it with me se-dan … se-dan. Orangebeard: Yarr, well it is better than your ship. Gadget: That is an army standard Humvee, which I kind of erm…liberated from my platoon. And you don’t want to see the power of that machine gun. Orangebeard: Yarr, but it is know match for my sword. Gadget: Well it shoots 400 rounds a minute, that’s better than your sword. Orangebeard: But my sword is made from the finest resources in the world, the dudgeon is made from a product which was grown under the ground for one thousand years. And the blade is made from a simple material, but can create the worst kind of pain if struck with the right kind of blow. Gadget: So the handle is made of plastic, and the blade is made of paper. Orangebeard:Yarr. Olly:Your mother uses that sword to f#^k herself doesn’t she you Slaughterhouse reject. SH Reject: What did you say? Pirate? Unfortunately for Orangebeard, Olly had just uttered those words to the large reject, which uses a pickaxe to munch on steak. Orangebeard: Yarr. I said nothing, it was that damned Olly. The Reject turns round another one hundred and eighty degrees, and makes some intimidating eye contact with me, completely ignoring the fact that my name badge says Sgt. A Jacobs. SH Reject: Was it you punk? Gadget: Yeah, Olly is really spelt with an A. SH Reject: Duh!?! What you talking about? Gadget: Listen you obvious elementary school dropout, read the name badge you dopey git. I think in the sheer shame of illiteracy, the reject turned round and carried on picking at his steak. Gadget: Now that that is ov…..what are you doing Orangebeard? Orangebeards ears are twitching, and a look of sheer determination came across his face, or maybe it is stupidity, or maybe even constipation, but gadget, being his inquiring self, unfortunately delves further into the matter. Gadget: (said with a faint chuckle in his voice) what are you doing? Orangebeard: Yarr! The sound of treasure is in the air. Gadget: How can the sound of treasure be in the air? Orangebeard: Yarr! Quiet listen to what the trekkies say, that talk of treasure, and they are heading to the bathroom, so that must mean that there is treasure in those toilets. IM COMING TREASURE!!!! Orangebeard swiftly gets up from the cubicle, runs and dives over the bar, into the back room, and then emerges with a plunger where the coat hanger hook used to be. Orangebeard: Yarr! Yarr! There’s treasure in that there toilet bowl. Orangebeard does another James Bond dive over the bar, and runs into the bathrooms, gradually letting his selotape belt come loose, and in turn, he is now naked running into the boy’s toilets. Not exactly the best plan of action, but he’s obviously mentally ill so everybody lets it slide…kinda. But Gadget, trying to act cool, sits back, takes a swig of his Strongbow, and lets the good times roll. Gadget: Well this can only end badly, cant it Olly. Olly: The b£$%@^d is only coming back with one treasure. Gadget: Well if the rumors are true, he’ll get to taste some of their treasures…if you catch my meaning. Olly: You do realize your talking to a parrot don’t you, a$$wipe. ----- 10 minutes later Orangebeard emerges from the toilets, stark naked, with a few red marks on his backside, which I would not like to discuss, and well, he could not have picked a worse time to leave the toilets really, because in those ten minutes of pointless treasure hunting, in a toilet basin, I might add, a war had basically broken out, Gadget and Olly were both drunk, the slaughterhouse rejects were fighting the trekkies, and the grufts were learning how to touch type from the businessmen. And the only thing the three people who were not fighting could think of doing was singing, that timeless classic “These Boots are Made for Walking”. And as pointed out by Gadget, this is like the video to the cover version by Jessica Simpson, and Orangebeard was wearing the attire every straight man was wanting her to wear, absolutely nothing. Gadget, Olly and Orangebeard: These boots are made for walking, and that’s just what they’ll do, one of these days these boots are gonna walk right over you. Singing this line over and over again, Gadget and Orangebeard joined hands and began to skip through the bar, avoiding all contact with the fighters, eventually emerging at their cars, the three collapsed on the floor, singing the song lyrics over and over again, until gadget finally came out with the perfedct climax to the story. Gadget: And the moral of this story is….Ginger people have no souls. Fin.
