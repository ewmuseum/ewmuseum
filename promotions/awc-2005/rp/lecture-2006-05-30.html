Lecture

30 May 2006

Date: 5/30/06 Amber settled the laptop on skirted knees. Her tongue slid unconsciously over her smooth lips as she flipped up the top of it, turning it on so that the screen flashed in her face. She was seated in what passed for the living room in the apartment, the lush white couch she sat on pushed up against one edge of the spacious alcove, facing the grand shelf that supported the stereo system. Her eyes didn't grace the plush carpet or well-painted off-white walls, though. She was intent on the computer screen, intent on willing it with all her phrenic energy to hurry up! She tapped her nails incessantly on the hard plastic below the keyboard. It was necessary that she do this quickly, necessary that she not be seen doing this. Luis would call it betrayal. He would scream his head off and, for all that Anton wrapped his strong arms around her at night, Anton would see it as betrayal, too. Treachery. Amber fought down a sneer. What Luis had done was treachery. Work his way into Anton's confidences so much that Anton couldn't see that she, she was doing what was best for him. It was only that what was best for him was… her. She impatiently typed in her password and proceeded to the general Windows interface. Broadband internet had its perks, and right now the one she was grateful for was the permanent internet access. Up went Internet Explorer, and then to her e-mail account. Her breath was rapid, more rapid than she'd ever thought it could be when she was doing so mundane a task. Her fingers seemed to take ages to type in her username and password. She thought she could hear every sound made in that apartment, feel every second sliding across her fair skin. She pursed her lips, trying to push the feeling of being watched away. Luis and Anton were off training and Nneka was… somewhere. Amber didn't know or care for Anton's sister. She supposed she should, especially if she wanted to be serious with Anton, but she couldn't. The girl was insufferable, an obstacle rather than the confidant that Amber had hoped she might be. And Luis… Luis had been the worst of it. A sexual predator, a megalomaniac that clearly had a problem with being short, a creep of the worst sort—yeah, it had been easy to dig up dirt on Luis Ferrara. Coupled with the fact that he was so deep in the world of cocaine that it seemed he should have appeared a floured ghost at all times, she could dispose of him whenever she wanted. Yet… she almost didn't want to. Especially not after she had lain with him, let him into her. As much as she wanted to deny it, he knew exactly what he was doing. It made her feel dirty. She noticed that she had stopped typing and she ran a cursory glance around the area to make sure that no one was there. Once she'd reassured herself, she let her eyes rest on the white-backgrounded screen again. New message. Her demeanor brightened considerably as she opened it and looked it over. Her lips, before a tight line of thought and mild discontent, began to spread in a smile. She pushed a lock of hair over her ear and leaned in a bit to read. This was… this was exactly what she wanted. Exactly what she needed. Luis Ferrara was done for. Utterly, completely, done for. And more importantly, Anton was hers. Anton twisted as he sent a roundhouse kick whipping through the air, a fierce current formed by his dangerous foot. He continued twirling, his fists lashing out, then his heel again. He landed square and leaned backwards just a bit, throwing out a long fist to where his opponent's throat might be. He was covered in sweat as he generally was when he trained. Today, under the blue sky and smiling sun, he wasn't training fully. He was still dressed in jeans; though he was bare above the waist, it was really the movement of the legs that was important. Mobility, agility, all stemmed from there. Well, the legs and the spine, anyway. ''Tell me again that little thing she does in bed,'' Luis inquired eagerly in Spanish. The small man was seated upon the red brick parapet at the edge of the roof, wearing a black t-shirt and wide denim shorts that came below his knees. A cigarette was settled between his lips, smoke trailing upwards in a serpentine manner. ''I've already said it a dozen times,'' Anton said, drawing back into a neutral stance. He wasn't really practicing—just keeping himself limber—yet Luis was distracting him. Luis was always distracting him. He closed his eyes and took a deep breath before lashing out with the heel of his palm again, turning that into a spinning back kick and continuing fluidly from there. No need for too much exertion. ''Say it again, man,'' Luis pleaded. ''It's funny.'' ''It is not funny,'' Anton countered, stopping again. Damn the man! He turned to look at his manager, squinting because the sun was right behind the smaller man. ''You just like hearing me describe the way her hips move.'' Luis shrugged his shoulders. ''Say it.'' ''No.'' Anton settled into his neutral stance for a split second before deciding it was worthless. Luis was just going to stop him again. He glanced over at his own shirt, tossed over a metallic box of some sort with wires running from it. Not the safest place, he supposed, but if the box was exposed, it couldn't be too much harm. ''Why do you want to hear so much about her, anyway?'' ''I don't want to hear so much about her,'' Luis argued. ''All I want to know is how she acts when you fuck her. Common guy stuff.'' ''Don't mock me,'' Anton said gravely. ''That's not on your mind at all.'' Anton squatted down, dead center in the middle of the roof. Luis frowned at him, using lip action alone to move the cigarette from one side of his mouth to the other. ''Do you think you can blackmail her because she makes me tell her that I love her?'' At that, Luis broke out in guffawing laughter. He leaned over, almost choking with the intensity of it; he even had to take the cigarette out of his mouth. Anton snarled wordlessly as Luis spat down the side of the building. ''Say you fuckin' love her, man!'' Luis laughed. ''Fuckin' bimbo. Fuckin' insecure bimbo. And yeah, you say you love her, don't you?'' ''Yes.'' Anton wasn't ashamed to say it, but Luis's grin broadened as if he should have been. '''Cause you don't want to get out of her pussy, huh?'' ''Because I love her,'' Anton said flatly. He always said that. Every time Luis had asked. But this time, Luis studied him closely. Luis snorted contemptuously. ''You love her.'' ''Yes, Luis, how many times do I—'' ''You're a fuckin' idiot, Lion,'' Luis snapped, rising from his seat. ''You love her? You love her!? You don't know a thing about love.'' Anton stood as Luis paced around, rubbing his wrists in a dangerous manner and letting his gaze fall ominously on the smaller man. Luis wasn't cowed, though, quickly moving from side to side. ''And you… you know about love, do you?'' ''I know everything there is to know about love,'' Luis said grudgingly. He shook his head, looking out across the city. After a while, Anton felt the urge to look that way as well. There was nothing except for cityscape and, beyond that, sky. Anton tore his eyes away to look back at the Venezuelan. ''I've had it all. First time, man, first time, I thought I loved the girl.'' Luis chuckled ruefully and turned away, taking a deep drag on my cigarette. ''I really did. I got her pregnant and I thought I was gonna raise the kid. She gets gunned down by a couple of idiot cholos in the middle of the street. Lose the baby, lose the kid. But me? I didn't shed a fuckin' tear. 'Cause really, man, I was glad to see her go. She was baggage. I'd convinced myself I loved her, but that was 'cause everybody said I should love her. Ain't the same.'' Anton sniffed. ''And you say that you know something about—'' ''Second time, Lion. Second time, I got with this chick, thought she was amazing. Held her in my arms into the small hours, shit like that. If I could sing, I woulda sang to her. If I coulda bought her a mansion, I woulda bought it for her. Anything, anything she wanted me to do, I woulda done for her. And one day… one day, she up and leaves. Doesn't tell me. Moves on. I hear later she's got a couple of kids in Mexico City, hear she's hooked on heroin and just lookin' to die. Hear those kids are in an orphanage. And the thing is, the real fuckin' thing is…'' Luis took another pensive drag, eyes closed and looking as if they were barely holding back tears. ''I think she loved me.'' ''So why didn't you chase her?'' Anton's face was a mask of confusion, but Luis shook his head with a small smile as if he saw something funny. ''Why did you give her up?'' ''What the fuck do I know?'' Luis asked, sparing Anton a glance. Anton thought that maybe Luis was crying. ''Maybe she hated my guts. Maybe she hung around 'till she got what she wanted from me and then she left. Maybe… maybe a hundred things, Lion. Maybe a thousand. Maybe she loved me but she didn't want to love me, so she left. Maybe she cheated on me and felt so bad about it, maybe thought she'd eventually break down and confess, that I'd hurt her. Truth is, I don't fuckin' know what's going on in her head, Lion. I don't know and I never will.'' ''But what I do know,'' Luis continued after a heavy sigh. ''What I do know is that broad you got on your shoulder don't love you. I can see it in her eyes. I can see it in the way she walks. She's never loved you and she ain't never gonna. She's the kind of bitch who'll rip your heart out and stomp on it when she's through with you. You don't want to wait it out to find out that she's gonna do that. I'll tell you. 'Cause the third time, Lion, the third time, I got played on like a bitch. Third time's a charm?'' Luis spat, but he looked ready to kick something over. ''Fuck that.'' Anton eyed his manager soberly and turned away, walking towards his shirt. He picked up the blue thing and slipped it over his head. ''Amber won't do that,'' Anton protested quietly. ''She wouldn't.'' Luis's answer was shrill laugh, a sound of pure mockery, not like the delightful chuckle he had before; this was completely forced. ''She wouldn't!'' ''You—don't—know—shit!'' Luis cried, rounding on him. ''You don't know shit for shit for shit! I am fuckin' telling you, I'm warning you that you aren't gonna be the same cat after this! Lion, if you trust me, man, if I'm the only fuckin' two-steppin' cokehead you can ever dream of trusting, then trust me on this! She is gonna use you up, and not in the way you want.'' Anton settled his t-shirt over his form and lifted his face, shutting his eyes. Luis might be right. He might be. But what right did he have to intrude like this? What gave him the right to give him advice? To lecture him? Anton had always been the levelheaded one, the coolheaded one, even when Luis had to be the business one. Anton had always been the rock. He was still a rock, but he was all indecision. He was all uncertainty. He knew that he loved Amber, though. He thought he did. He did. He did. ''Do you love her?'' Anton said after a lengthy pause; he said it in English, the change offhanded and barely noticed. ''Nneka, I mean. Do you love my sister?'' Anton heard the sharp inhalation of Luis taking another drag. ''Fuck no, I don't love your sister,'' Luis answered. ''How could I—'' He was cut off by the sound of something dropping. Anton turned, his eyes snapping open. There, at the stairwell leading up from the building, Nneka stood wide-eyed. Luis's eyes bugged out of his head as well. Before Anton could say anything, she had turned and fled down the stairs. ''Goddammit!'' Luis cried, tossing away his cigarette and hurrying at top speed after her. Anton watched them go, watched them until darkness enveloped them. ''You don't love him,'' Luis said. ''Stop making him believe that you love him.'' Amber's naked body heaved under him, gripped at his shoulders. He narrowed his eyes. ''Stop it.'' ''No,'' Amber said. ''I do love him. I do.'' Luis shook his head. ''Would you say the vows with him?'' She hesitated. ''Yes.'' Luis could almost see the ''No'' forming first on her lips. He suppressed his victorious smirk. ''What about with me?'' Luis's eyebrows lifted and, after she registered the question, hers did, too. ''You're a guttersnake,'' Amber said, winding her arms around his neck. ''I'd never marry you.'' She pressed her lips against his neck passionately and he stroked her hair. It occurred to him exactly the thing he had needed to say to Anton and exactly the thing he hadn't. Sex isn't love.
