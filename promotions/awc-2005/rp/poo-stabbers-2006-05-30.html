Poo stabbers?

30 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/30/06 Memorial Day remembers those who have fallen fighting for the freedom of decency and liberty. It’s a day to take pride in the United States of America and all the fruits of her plains. An outsider may not understand this civic pride and its not something that can be explained. It’s just a birthright inherent to the few, the proud, the Americans. Duke Williams embodies what a patriot and an American hero is all about. While he’s never served his country in uniform he certainly has represented it in the ring. Duke has been a bearer of traditional American values for years and is proud to tell you so. What a better way to kick off a week then to face some sand-nigger in a wrestling match in Germany. Oh sure the N-word isn’t politically correct but neither is Duke Williams. It’s liberal sensitivity that protects a word, yet not a person. Many in parents in Germany would wash their children’s mouths out with soap for saying “kike” yet looked the other way when a race of people were being slaughtered. It’s backwards and Duke Williams and the rest of the intelligent world get it, and understand how the world works. So on this day of patriotism what does an American Icon do to celebrate while stranded in Germany? [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] Girl: So you are facing Captain Stabin? Duke Williams: Yea something like that. He’s one of them towel wearing A-rabs who should be driving a cab or serving me babagenush? Girl: What is this babagenush? Duke Williams: One of them A-rab foods. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] Girl: Is this guy really a captain? Duke Williams: Doing my research I would say he is. Girl: Wow that is impressive! No? Duke Williams: About as impressive as the police man in the Village People. Girl: Who are these Village People? Duke Williams: A band of poo stabbers from the fairy part of town. Girl: Poo stabbers? Duke Williams: You know, ass jammers and what not. You see Captain Suleimon is an Arab ass jockey and Captain of the dudes who do dudes donkey brigade. Or semantics they are better know as the DWDDB. Girl: Are these DWDDB’s dangerous? Duke Williams: Only if you have an open wound and they bleed on you. No I’m not worried as I’m a legend and he’s well…he’s a guy stepping in the ring with a legend and all of Allah’s excitement couldn’t save his ass. Duke lets out a Middle Eastern cackle of celebration. Girl: Well I wish you had more time to rp but you fucked up and fucked me instead and waiting to the last minute. Duke Williams: Yea that’s true plus I just got home from a night out in these German bars. So I’ll just wrap this son of a bitch up and hope for the best by saying I’m going to send Captain Turbanroo….Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. =w=
