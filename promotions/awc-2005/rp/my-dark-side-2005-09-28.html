My Dark Side

28 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th September 2005 We see The British Bomber at an autograph signing. Bomber is sat behind the table, with a couple of piles of photographs of himself waiting to be signed. He is at his local HMV store in the UK, in his hometown of Birmingham. Bomber sat back in the chair, relaxed but all dressed up looking smart, it was around 8:50am so the store wasn’t due to open for another 10 minutes but Bomber just wanted to reflect on what had happened over the last week. Bomber: Come on open the damn doors, I want to go home and get away from these poor excuses for human beings. HMV Boss: It won’t be long, it will probably take an hour or so, and then you can go home. Bomber: An hour!? God damn it, I have stuff to do yet my damn agent sends me here to sit in front of them twats for an hour? Jesus Christ you lot better be giving me good money for this. HMV Boss: Erm… course we are! One of our employee’s is just dying to meet you, would you mind posing for a picture with him? Bomber: God, must I? I hate it here, it smells, I had some shit try shooting my last night, and I really am beginning to hate this country as well. HMV Boss: Oh come on, it won’t take long. And what do you mean you hate this country, you were born here! Two months ago you loved this country! Bomber: Yeah two months ago, I finally came to my senses. If this guy really must then send him to me for a photo, but as soon as it’s done he goes away, I hate having these people near me. HMV Boss: Alan! Get over here, he has said he will take a photo with you! Alan came skipping over in a rather gay fashion, Bomber looked at Alan, displeased at the fact that a grown man was skipping over to him, looking like a poof with a dildo up his ass. HMV Boss: Bomber, this is big gay Alan. Bomber looked at Alan horrified. Bomber: My god, look at this big poof! He as bad as Red Rock! If not worse! That man ain’t coming anywhere near me, and if he does, I’ll throw his gay ass through that window right there. HMV Boss: Awww come on, its always been an ambition of his to meet you, and has waited for 10 years to meet you as well, just have your photo taken with him. Bomber: I am not having my photo done with that faggot! I have to put up with Red Rock, and I am not putting up with any more, so like I said, get him out of my sight or I will beat him so bad, that his mom wont even recognise him when I am done. Alan had stopped and just stared at Bomber, he didn’t like what he had heard from his hero, what Bomber had failed to realise was that Alan wasn’t really gay, but he had just been given the nickname because he was quite feminine. But Bomber didn’t care, he had enough of the gay antics coming from Red Rock and certainly didn’t need it at an autograph signing. Bomber: Hey Alan, did you see what I did to Red Rock’s boyfriend Boolie? You know if you come near me, I’ll do exactly the same to you, then I’ll cut you up into little pieces and mail you back to mommy. And I will tell you what you little poof, you watch on Friday evening, because at Testimony I have a surprise lined up for Red Rock that will throw his mindset before the match. Big Gay Alan: Oh please don’t hurt Red Rock, he is my hero as well, I am a big fan of you both. Bomber: You like…. HIM? Listen if you like him that much I can try and arrange a date with him for you, I mean he has tried coming onto me no end of times and to best honest I want to get him off my back and out of AWC. So maybe you could take him on a dinner date, take him back to yours, and lock him up in the closet then I can win the title by default this Friday and once again be a title holder. Bomber begins day dreaming of the last time he held a title, which was way back in July 2003. Bomber had to get rid of this gremlin, this was a new start for him, a fresh opportunity. He could see it now, up in lights “NEW AWC LIVEWIRE CHAMPION : THE BRITISH BOMBER” Bomber then drifted into the day dream of how the match would go, a top rope powerbomb through two tables and being declared the winner. He also started day dreaming past that and right onto Christmas where he would still be champion, and one of the longest reigning champions in AWC history, and the most decorated. HMV Boss: Bomber! God damn it he is day dreaming. Bomber: Jesus man, I was having a good day dream. HMV Boss: Well, since you went into your day dream Alan has ran off, we are 5 minutes late opening up, and you have around 100 people out there getting restless. Bomber: Ok number 1, I don’t care about that poof. Number 2, never ever interrupt one of my day dreams, and number 3, screw the fans, I could care less. The doors opened and they all came rushing in, Bomber wasn’t prepared and fell backwards off his seat. Everyone started to laugh, this just made Bomber pissed off. He got to his feet and folded up the chair and smashed it on the table, sending the photo’s flying all over the place, along with the pens he had on there. Bomber: Listen you freeloading bastards, I am not signing any photos for you to sell on eBay, I am not signing any t-shirts, I am not signing any flesh, I am not signing anything. I hate this country, and I hate you lot. I don’t need the money from this, I am rich enough as it is. You’re all pathetic, and you know what…. Bomber pointed to the HMV Boss and signalled for him to come over to him. The boss of the store came walking over to Bomber and stood next to him. Bomber: Now I am going to give you all a preview of Friday, of what will happen to Red Rock. The boss started walking away slowly, knowing what was coming but Bomber grab his t-shirt and kicked him in the gut, lifting him up and power bombing him through the table. A holy shit chant started up, and the people at the front of the now dis-organised queue had a concerned look on their faces as the owner of the store laid motionless in the wreckage of the table. Bomber: That is what will happen to Red Rock on Friday night, and then the referee will lift my arm into the air as he gives me the Livewire title and I become the new champion. As far as I am concerned, they can make this a pinfall table match, because I will put Red Rock through table after table after table. The owner of the store started to stir, but still couldn’t get to his feet, Bomber gave him a stiff kick to the head to ensure he wouldn’t get back to his feet. Bomber: People started taking me for granted, for the last three years I have given you my all, and what have I got in return? Two measly title reigns, I have thought about that for the last week and I decided to go back to my dark side where I was one of the most decorated wrestlers in FAW at the time, so why not bring back that dark side to AWC and for my match on Friday because it’s the side that nobody likes, it’s the side that Red Rock will hate. It’s the side that Boolie hates because I have him locked up in a padded room, living on bread and water. The tide of the good side to me has gone, the bad side is back and I love it, and you know what? I can guarantee victory on Friday because I am at my best, you will see me in that ring, not as the person that goes “Oh yey, I lost but the fans love me!” You’re going to see the side that wins and thinks “fuck it, I won, I don’t give a shit about people like you!” The crowd was starting to disperse rapidly, afraid of what Bomber would do next, they hadn’t seen this side of Bomber for years, it was a side of Bomber they didn’t like and was hoping to never see again, this was the sick, twisted sadistic Bomber that put his ex-fiancée’s life in danger, this is the sick, sadistic Bomber that ended his bosses wrestling career and career in the business. This was the Bomber that would do absolutely anything to win, he would sell his soul to the devil, and then kick the devils ass just to get it back. This is the Bomber that has ended careers before. This was the Bomber that would kick the boss in the balls, just because he could. Bomber: Go on, you all run along now, go cheer for your nobodies! Go cheer for bums like Red Rock and Mike Wade! Just like you did at the Battle Of Britain, you want to see a 5 star match? Fuck Mike Wade! You wanna see a 5 star, vintage performance? You watch my match, because that whore cant wrestle, just like Red Rock. I am gonna drag Red Rock out the gutter, show him how to wrestle and put on a 5 star match for you little shits, and I am gonna show them both how you win a title convincingly! I am the future of this business, I am the future of AWC, I am the figurehead of this company! You watch me Friday, just like Mike Wade will, just like Tim Shipley will, hell, just like the entire company will watch…. And learn! Friday marks the start of a new era in AWC, mark my words, I am the future. Bomber nails the boss of HMV one final time as the store is now empty as the last person runs out. Bomber dusts himself down. Bomber: That takes care of that.
