Book of Dawning - For Every Action...

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/05 ‘You’re going to do what?’ The look of shock on the face of Dan O’Grady was a sight to behold. He had gone a crimson pink and the colour was rapidly draining from his face as the statement I made just entered into his brain. His arms were spread out wide, almost in a crucifixion style but now he pulled them back, turning it into a more pleading gesture. Something I knew that was coming. ‘Jack…don’t be stupid. Going to talk to Garza is suicide. Do you realise what he’s like?’ ‘Of course I know Dan, I trained the guy in the first place.’ Garza Arrigo; one time low key gangster based in Kingston who had experienced a meteoric rise upon my departure from America. Currently he was the head of the Kingston operations but that was only due to Farrelli’s death. Right now he was on shaky ground and had nothing to hold him up with. Cement shoes were a knocking if the cliché wasn’t bad enough. But it was true, I had trained him. The guy was ruthless from the get go and nothing I said or did could keep his head in check. I asked for moderate and I got extreme, coolness and I got blind rage. The guy was a complete instinctual being. No wonder he was called a butcher and an animal in the same breath by his henchmen. The guy was a nut job and to see him still around in the first place was surprising in itself. But leading? Uh oh were the words that came to mind. Which was exactly why I wanted to go and meet him. ‘But why?’ ‘I don’t think you’d understand Dan. There’s too much involved in this to have you caught up in it right now. It’s bad enough that I have to risk one life in the event of this going through. I am not risking a second.’ ‘Well then don’t risk anyone! This isn’t a game Jack, you know as well as I do that this guy is nuts.’ ‘I know it’s not a game but thankfully for me, he won’t.’ ‘What?’ ‘I’ll explain when this is all over.’ I went for the door, duffle bag in hand with everything inside that I required. All I had to do now was get this whole thing done and I’d be well on my way. When I saw the death of Farrelli in the paper I was exultant, overjoyed almost…but I was also depressed and melancholy. Whilst I had been the one to get rid of him…I had been the one to get rid of him. So many things just crept into my head once it all sank in and my mind was awash with the thoughts of a dreamer. Hopes, wishes, realities, possibilities, all these things and more were open with the door being unlocked. But most of all… I was free again. No more hiding from a debtor who would’ve come to collect sooner or later, no bones about it. But now, I had the liberty to get on with my life, be anything I wanted to, really strive for the success that I had always wanted. I had the opportunity but strings held me back…held me back to the past and wanted me to languish there because I owed it to myself to once and for all become what destiny had in store for me. It wanted death and power. And I was only one that could provide it. It was the opportunity to keep my future safe for the rest of my life. And honestly, in all the time I had been a part of the underworld, I had grown accustomed, grown to like the seediness of it all. But I didn’t just want to be a part of it…I wanted to shape it, to control its growth and scope of area. The door was open, it was up to me to walk through it. But right now Dan was still standing in the way. ‘Get out of my way Dan.’ ‘No.’ The mid sixties man was using his entire frame to block the doorway. He knew it was futile…why do it? Why stop something you knew you couldn’t? ‘I’m not going to ask again Dan.’ ‘I know.’ The words were cold and lacking the warmth from before. I could sense the severity in his tone, knowing that he wouldn’t be changing his mind no matter what I said. I looked down at the floor of the hallway, fully aware of what was about to happen. ‘I’m sorry Dan…but it’s for the best.’ I lashed upward with a right to the face and caught him on the jaw, sending him back into the doorway. He reeled slightly but despite his age the chin was still strong as ever. He immediately moved forward again, still blocking and doing nothing else to impede my progress. ‘Jack you know this isn’t right. You know they wouldn’t want it. So don’t do it.’ I swung wildly with my left and missed his head, flying right past and connecting with the wall. I cursed loudly and turned just in time to receive a boot to the ribs which caused me to fall backwards to the ground. He stood still, bearing down on me but not an action. ‘I’m going Dan…whether you like it or not.’ He cried in surprise as I caught his left leg with my boot, just at his weak shin. He fell slowly like a tree trunk and his smash to the ground was a dull heavy thud. He instinctively held onto his shin and didn’t move. I picked up the duffle bag which lay strewn on the floor and stepped over his body. A last desperate plea came from a hand on my leg. ‘Jack…please.’ ‘I’m sorry Dan…but it’s for the best.’ If it had have been anyone else I would’ve kicked back into his face but Dan deserved no such humiliation. I would be back besides. God damn I wasn’t going to get killed by Garza. No, he wouldn’t be quick enough for that.
