Roses & Thorns - First Impressions

19 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/3/06 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Room 224 The sound of running water splashes playfully from side to side of the basin, eking out its existence in the best way it knows; to simply go with the flow. It swirls leisurely around, catching every iota of space in the marble basin, enveloping everything that it sees fit to do so. Just riding the crests of its own waves, riding them all the way to the bottom. A tsunami was added to the effect as I threshed the water with my razor, foaming the water up with the shaving cream. Almost looked like I was polluting it. You know, when I’m considering the effects of what happens to water due to shaving foam, you know there’s something up. Even more so, I’m shaving in the first place. I don’t generally do that. I guess this occasion was different though. I just didn’t want to screw up. There isn’t anything I can do about that now though. Just get to do the best I can. ‘Ow!’ Nicked my face. I always nick my face for important things. It’s an affectionate gesture by my brain to remind me that no matter what I do, I can’t run away from my own paranoid induced huddle. I watched the blood drip into the basin, polluting it even further, watching the blood dissipate into the water and corrupt and mutate the body. It’s how it starts you know. First you’re doing fine and dandy, then along comes something that excites you, tempts you and you get sucked in, only to have it then slowly take away every semblance of your life as it stands. Everything but bitterness. I stood up and took a look in the mirror, then glanced at my watch. Only have about twenty minutes to go now. *** 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Room 324 Water makes up for the impurities our own bodies have. It is a force which sanctifies and cleanses the body of its daily slog, the daily tug of war with society. It seems so appropriate that the longer you traverse these lands the longer it is you need to stay under the water. It is a noble compound for it asks for nothing and yet soaks up all your problems without any hesitation. All to be loved. All to be loved. I let the water rush over my body, searching every ounce and fibre to find the pores that are clogged up, the hair that captures all the dust and dirt from the long day. It helps to know I can stand here for as long as possible, as long as it takes to wash away the pain. It’s getting longer and longer by the day, much faster than the days can cope. It sends shivers up my spine from moment to moment, refreshing and revitalising the mood. Tonight is important and it has to go well. As such I have everything prepared for the evening’s activities. The dress, the heels…everything carefully considered to have maximum effect. I will not be outdone or outsmarted on this evening. I even know the perfume that I wish to wear. The favourite one. It should be enough…no, it will be enough. I pass my hand up to the nozzle, switching the shower off, watching the last droplets of water dying on my body, soaking the dirt and using themselves as sacrificial lambs so that others may go pure. I’m fully aware of this. *** 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Room 224 The soles of my feet walk across the padded carpet in the room, admiring the soothing feeling that it entertains on me. The calm before the storm as it were. I looked around the room and cursed loudly as yet again I had forgotten to take out the clothes I needed to wear. I only had ten minutes to find them, put them on and be down in the lobby. I was not going to be late because dammit this was going to be done my way or not at all. I sifted through the bag that I had with me, looking for the right clothes. Is it always fussy for this? Are we that narrow minded that we can’t just be ourselves at these kinds of events, just trying to be ourselves and forgetting the expectations of others? But they’re important, least they can be, so you’ve got to play the game, for however long it takes until they get used to you and then you cab really be yourself. It’s too late by then for them to get away of course because you’ve suckered them into it. I did not want that to happen again. ‘Finally…’ I plucked the suit from the bag and then my jaw dropped in frustration. Damn thing was creased. This should be fun, using a hotel iron. Joy. *** 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Room 324 Just the last application of lipstick and then all will be done. I stood in the mirror and admired my qualities. Slim, toned, attractive and ready to murder you if the notion flitted across my brainwaves. Perfect. Still there was a hint of butterflies. I hate that. I really shouldn’t have it by now, at this stage in my life but then again things aren’t always that simple. I should know by now that this is always an important event and a place where guards are established, boundaries tested to see how far they can be teased and probed until we can meet again and test them once more. It’s a protracted duel of wits and perspectives, ever-changing, ever moving and not once boring. Well, boring they can be, that’s for certain. I’ve endured enough of them to know that there are those types who no matter how hard they try are just infinitely boring. They’re such good competitors but never the winners if you understand the meaning. This one had the feeling of it being a winner. A gut feeling sure but a feeling nonetheless. Well, just needed to put on my heels now and then it was down to the lobby. Ten minutes to go. Piece of cake. *** 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Room 224 A piece of cake is the most inaccurate statement for such an occasion. Nothing ever goes to plan and no matter how hard you try, there’s always going to be something which is going to run round the corner and crush you dead in your tracks. Nick on the face? Check. Couldn’t find clothes? Check. Burnt but no longer creased clothes? Check. Just great you know. Top it all off, I had a bruise on my forehead from trying to pull on my trousers. Stupid hopping. I was not going to be late though. Dammit I wasn’t going to be late. There was only one thing for it unfortunately. I grabbed the shoes, shirt and jacket and ran right out the door for the elevator. For once I rode the luck and I got in in time. Now all I needed was to be at peace in here, with enough time to be ready once I got downstairs. Fat chance. *** 10th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Lobby I could hear the tapping of the shoes barely twenty metres in front of me and I knew then I was late. Right now though I was concentrating on at least being presentable when I eventually talked to her. After all, I only had trousers and shoes on right now. No shirt, no jacket. The shirt would be enough to get on and approach. Propping myself up behind a suitably large plant I slipped on the shirt quickly and roughly, slightly tearing the cuffs. Bah, I’ll call it the latest fashion. Finally ready I walked up to her and patted her on the shoulder. ‘Hey.’ ‘You’re late.’ ‘Better late than nothing huh?’ ‘I guess.’ She cracked a smile like that and nodded. We headed off in the direction, testing the boundaries. *** 11th March - Westin Providence Hotel, Lobby, Early Morning We both meandered back into the hotel, utterly exhausted night out. Things had gone as well as I thought they could have. He seemed to enjoy the event. It wouldn’t be the last, not by a long shot. I wasn’t sure if she had enjoyed it though. She must hate these things. I hope he didn’t find it too boring. Well, I better say goodnight. ‘Goodnight Sasha. Same time tomorrow?’ ‘Yes. These meet and greets don’t run themselves you know?’ ‘Yeah that’s true.’ ‘Don’t be late this time.’ ‘I won’t.’ With no more words to say, we went our separate ways.
