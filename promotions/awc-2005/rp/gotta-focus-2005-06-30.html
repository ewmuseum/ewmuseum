Gotta Focus

30 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/6/05 1. Numb Sitting in a first class seat of a Ryan Air plane, Paddy O’Shea feels numb. Although not even on his mind at this time, Paddy is still not used to these luxuries that is entitled to him through his commitment to the AWC. He can have champagne when he wants, he can view a selection of movies, the seats are highly comfortable and he has all the space he could want…But Paddy doesn’t want to drink or watch films or fall asleep in the comfortable seats. He just wants to figure out how he’s going to get his father back. Paddy has his eyes fixed on the back of the seat in front of him, in deep thought, rarely blinking. He is sitting completely still on the brown leather seat that he seems to sink into. His hands are clutching the arms of the chair for grim death but not because he has a fear of flying but because of the frustration building up inside him that is being processed to pure rage. For the second time in recent weeks, he is wearing a suit. It consists of a black jacket and matching trousers, and a white open collared shirt. He isn’t wearing his usual hat either but instead, his hair is neatly combed. He actually looks quite handsome and his mother has insisted he behaves like this from now on now that he is a professional wrestler. His mother was obviously trying to hide her grief…her husband was kidnapped and all she can do is tell her son how he should dress. He supposed she was putting on a brave face and acting strong so that no one else would. His mother always was strong. Back when he was about five, she beat a nine-year-old boy within an inch of his life for throwing stones at her gypsy son. That boy didn’t show his face much after that. But inside, Paddy knew she must be a tortured soul, the thought of what the kidnappers are doing to Danny O’Shea now scaring her to the bone. Paddy didn’t tell her what James at the police station told him – that the kidnappers were likely enemies of Paddy in the AWC and that they couldn’t do a lot if he was over there. If he had have done that she may have demanded he stayed in Ireland, away from the evils of the world…but then who would save Danny? Now as he flies to New York for his show at Solarized, guilt washes over him like water from a shower. He is going to wrestle for petty titles while his father is possibly being tortured, perhaps being starved or even dying at this very minute. But then if he didn’t fight, he would let Crimson, his partner down, after he chose him to fight in the Alliance league in the first place. Paddy couldn’t help from feeling torn Then he remembered another reason why he was fighting. If the kidnappers were connected to the AWC, they might reveal themselves. He might smoke the fox form the hole so to speak. And right now, there were three teams of suspects…Krimzon Karnage Inc., TAN and The Full House… And Paddy had the chance to fight them at Solarized and to beat answers out of them. For the first time in three days, Paddy found himself smiling… Aye’m gonnae knock those feckers within an inch of their lifes…and tha’ be after they tell me wha’ aye want t’know Paddy suddenly realised all he could do was wait now and that would be the hardest part. He would have to wait for revelations. Yes, he could beat the hell out of those teams but it might not be them and if it was, they could deny it until they passed out… But if someone kidnapped to get at him then they obviously wanted something from him. Yes, it was becoming clearer now. They would reveal themselves and at the first sight of them, Paddy would strike “Would you like a drink sir?” This voice came from a stewardess and brought Paddy out of his trance. She was an attractive woman in her late twenties with blond hair tied back under the typical stewardess hat. Her uniform was navy and she was pushing a small cart that contained a bottle of wine in ice and a number of wine glasses. “Maybe aye’ll have one sure…aye, I’ll have one thanks” Paddy said with a smile. This smile was false and he was sure she could see that but at least he had tried to smile. She returned his smile anyway and poured him a glass and handed it to him. “Here you are sir. And if you need anything, all you have to do is call” Paddy nodded at her in silent gratitude and took a sip of the wine. Paddy wasn’t a big fan of wine and had difficulty really telling much difference between white wines but this was quite suited to his palate and he savoured the taste. Here’s t’ye da…don’ worry, aye’ll find ye if it be the last thing aye do… 2. Ready For Solarized Paddy was sitting on a hotel bed when the scene reopened. Paddy never liked hotels, didn’t like any fixed home in fact. He just didn’t trust them and didn’t like their immobility. His caravan could take him anywhere and he would never be home sick in the conventional sense. His caravan was temporarily parked in Tampa Florida. He could have flown there and drove up to New York for Solarized but he would have been about a half a day too late. So for now, he was confined to this ‘prison’ Despite Paddy’s outlook, the room was beautifully decorated. The walls were coloured a warm peach colour that seemed to radiate the whole room. The bed was a four-poster monster of a bed. There was a mini bar and a pool table. Most people would have felt like a king in a place like this – this was something alien to Paddy as well in fact. Three weeks ago he lived with nine others in a cramped caravan in Ireland. Now with an AWC contract, he has the world at his feet. And again, he felt the guilt. It didn’t feel right he was in a beautiful five star hotel while his father was likely tied up bleeding on the concrete floor of a warehouse only God knows where. Before he could contemplate on this further, the phone rang Paddy walked across the room to the phone and lifted the receiver in one hand and the body of the telephone in the other “Hello?” Paddy asked quizzingly, half expecting to hear the voice of his father begging for his life. “Hey there Paddy” Paddy knew the voice instantly as Crimson. He didn’t know if he felt relieved that his father hadn’t been on the other line or disappointed he hadn’t made contact. Either way, at least he had someone to talk to about it. He began to walk over to the balcony as he carried the phone “Top o’ the mornin’ Crimson. How are ye?” Paddy asked with forced cheerfulness “Oh, I’m great. I just can’t wait for Solarized…We’ve got belts to win, you know?” “Of course yes…where are ye at?” “I’m driving to New York as we speak. I’ve got ASS along with here. Say hello ASS………I haven’t got two words out of this guy since we met. Well anyway, you’re ready for the PPV then?” “Yes, yes…” “Is there something wrong, something I said?” “No, o’ course not…. It’s just…me da…he’s been kidnapped” Paddy found it difficult to say this and actually had to fight back tears. He didn’t get emotional much but this had just come out of nowhere “Jesus Paddy…I don’t know what to say…Who? When? Why?” The concern was clear to Paddy over the phone and he could hear the genuinity behind it and he was thankful. “Aye don’t know the answers t’ any of those questions…the only idea they have is tha’ the kidnapping has something t’do with the AWC” “You mean the AWC did it?” “No, aye mean if an enemy in the AWC did it…” “Well…at a time like this…I couldn’t ask you to fight at Solarized…I mean-“ “No, I want t’fight” Paddy interrupted. “If it is someone in the AWC, they’ll show themselves and then aye’m gonnae kill them.” “…Well…if you’re sure. I suppose it’s okay. Look, I’m coming up to New York now. Me and ASS will be with you in fifteen minutes so just hold tight” “Okay” Paddy hung up the receiver and paced over to the stand and placed it back on neatly. The only thing that remained now was Solarized in the quest for the Alliance titles and he still felt guilty…but right now he had to put everything behind him, and push all thoughts of his father to the back of his mind. Right now, he had to wait. He would repay his favour to Crimson and fight but at the same time, he would wait for revelations. Come Solarized, there would be answers, Paddy would make sure of that END
