Roses & Thorns - Delicate Planting II

17 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/02/06 4th of January - Kingston Hospital It’s strange but you’d think with the kind of career I’ve had that I’d be well used to being in and around hospitals. Thankfully, that hasn’t been the case. Hospitals have, and always will give me the everlasting creeps right till the day I die. Probably because I’ll be lying in one when I do. It was the smell you see, that was the thing that bothered me the most about it. People always go on about the fact that the doctors and nurses make you feel so comfortable and at home while you have to stay there but no matter how many times they say that I can always bring them back to the smell. It was stale and dull, not an overpowering one that would envelop you but one that silently wafted round the hallways and remind you that this was no ordinary place that you walked into. Oh no, this was a place of utter and complete separation form anything that resembled normal life. It was a conscious decision when designing a hospital that comfort was dispensed with in favour of more clinical, bare and minimalist furniture to achieve the effect that you were in some sort of high tech institution that would take care of you. Everything about hospitals is psychological when it comes down to it. To see that you don’t have to go any further than the walls themselves. They’re always white or a pastel colour, nothing else. You know why? Soothing qualities, it makes you relax and try to calm down. Nothing more, nothing less. It’s sad the way that works out but hey who am I to judge such an institution? *** And it came to me then that every plan Is a tiny glance of father time As I stared at my shoes in the ICU That reeked of piss and 409 And I rationed my breaths as I said to myself that I’d already taken too much today As each descending peak of the LCD Took you a little farther away from me Away from me All you could hear was the beeping, the solid beeping of the machine which held onto any signs of life for Dan ‘Firebrand’ O’Grady. It was slow and steady and with each beep you could see snapshots of the hubbub of activity that occurred in every single hospital across the globe. You could transplant things like this to every sort of situation in a hospital. A nurse flip open a chart. Another one prime some sort of injection. The drip bubbling as more liquid passed through the entrance into Dan’s vein. Noise from behind, like a trolley passing by with only a solitary pair of legs driving it. And Jessica O’Grady, face changing at every moment. It pained me to watch Dan as it stood but to watch Jessica was just as, if not worse. To see the one person who had been a part of her life ever since she was born, the one who had been around for the entirety of her formative years, taken care of her, brought her on the road, living the life of a traveller to be with her dad. This was her everything right in front of her and currently he was in bad shape. She had her head slumped over the end of the bed, staring intently at Dan’s face which was serene right now, no pain, no discomfort, just plain and banal. I stood by the doorway, studying the picture. I had been for the last four hours. Once again, I wasn’t able to do anything to affect the happenings around me, and it didn’t matter what the hell I tried, this was one more thing beyond my reach. I could try, I could strain but it would all be for nothing because of how distant it was. This was a battle that I couldn’t win, it had to be done by Dan and Dan alone. He was lying in the bed there, motionless except for the slow rhythmic sighing of his chest, pushing air into the respirator that was on him at the present time. No marks or scratches crossed his face or arms to reveal what could have happened. He could be faking it the way he looked it because he was in prime condition for a man of his age. That’s the way a heart attack operates though, sudden and menacing. No warning to it, simply a jolt and then you’re under starter’s orders. Makes you think could he have known, could he have understood what was going to happen? Makes me think how immaterial life is, it’s not something you can grasp at, not something you can hold onto by the scruff of the neck until you feel you’re ready to let go. You don’t get a say in things like that. ‘Jack…’ The voice was very low and shallow, rasping almost and it came from Jessica. She had looked up for one of the longest times and it was there you could finally see where the marks went to. Went straight to those around the loved one. Her face was littered with scratches and red blotches from crying and clawing at her face, trying to make the pain stop, trying to forget what on earth was happening. It obviously wasn’t working for her though as a lot of tears threaded through the eye socket and began to seep downwards. ‘What is it Jess?’ ‘Please…come on in.’ Carefully, I wandered into the room, treading so lightly I may as well have been hovering above the lino floor. There’s another thing about hospitals. There’s never carpet, never have I seen any and it kind of bothered me that they instead put in a surface that was quite slippery. Maybe they didn’t get enough patients as it was. Gluttons. I moved closer to Jessica and put my arm over her. She glanced upwards and attempted a smile but nothing too exhaustive before returning to her worried, worn face. There was a grit and determination about her that marked her out as Dan’s daughter and it was easy to see from this perspective. But inside that head…who knows what was running through. Brain was acting on impulse by now, there was no reasoning for this. Looking at her stiff and stoic stature it was time for a change. ‘Jess…do you want to take a break? You’ve been here all day. He’ll still be here in a little while ok? Please, get something to eat or drink.’ ‘No, I want to stay.’ She clutched at his hand, clenching it tightly, perhaps hoping that she could transfer her own blood to him directly and make sure that he’d be ok. The desperation in her face told me that. So I grasped her shoulders and pulled her up. ‘Come on, it’s time for a rest.’ ‘But I don’t want to go Jack.’ ‘Don’t worry, I’ll be here the entire time so if anything happens, which it won’t, you’ll be the first to know.’ ‘But…’ ‘No buts, go to the restaurant. It’s just down the hall and to the left. Go.’ She went to grasp at him again but this time she relented, small tears escaping her eyes once more as she slowly drifted out of the room. And then it was just me…me and Dan left alone in the room. I glanced towards him but he was just peaceful and relaxed. Whatever drugs he was on right now were definitely working. What made you the man you are Dan? What made you the whole person, the man who was able to take things in hand and take care of business? What made you competent enough to deal with things in the right manner, never slacking and always putting forth the effort? How come your fire didn’t burn out? ‘How come it didn’t burn out?’ ‘Well I do have red hair you know…’ My head peered to the left and sitting slightly further up in his bed was Dan, the respirator pulled to the side so that he could talk properly. A toothy grin greeted me and he looked happy, a bit zany…in other words exactly like Dan had always been. ‘I was wondering when you’d wake up. I told you not to try and take on those goons outside the gym.’ ‘You think I’m bad, you should see them!’ His tongue flickered out of his mouth as he winked at me from the bed. I couldn’t help but smile at him, even with the amount of tubes and such that were injecting and exploring his deepest orifices. If that sounds bad in your head, you weren’t there staring at it all. Believe me. I stood up from the chair that I had sat down in for a short period and moved closer towards him, clasping his own hand with mine and smiling at him. All things considered he actually looked pretty good. ‘Do you remember much?’ ‘No. Last thing I can remember is making some cheese and bacon sandwiches.’ No wonder. He’d been told well over two years ago by now to watch his cholesterol and to take care of what he was eating. Jessica had helped in this respect, monitoring the food that came in and out of the household and to be fair Dan had kept up some pretty impressive training regimes considering his age but I’d always figured he was sneaking things in, especially since Jessica took on my PR job. ‘You sly old fool.’ ‘You would’ve done the same Jack. It’s hard to break habits at this age.’ His eyes watched me more closely now and there was a hint of regret tinged deep inside the crystalline appearance. I could see slow drops of water welling up deep down. ‘I know I don’t have long Jack…and it did burn out.’ ‘What?’ ‘The fire. You think I didn’t understand you earlier? It’s burnt out, it’s been gone a long time now…too long.’ His words were full of resentment…but not towards me, not at all. This was a self-inflicted attack. ‘It’s been gone…ever since Jessica’s mom passed away. It flickered and died that day and I never got it back.’ ‘What are you talking about?’ ‘Life Jack…it’s not about making choices, it’s not about finding paths…none of that. It’s living for the moment…because the moment never lasts. For the last thirty years I’ve let the moment slip by and now it’s too late to get it back.’ ‘But I don’t understand…’ ‘Just listen you stubborn boy.’ The smile cracked over his face again, the wrinkles all running towards it and upending their wealth of knowledge and experience into his shallow voice. ‘When I was your age…my wife died and I was left with Jessica. To many people it seems I did the right thing looking after her and sure she turned out ok, but I forgot about the other side of my responsibility. A responsibility to myself as a person to live life to the full. To achieve my goals…to reap the benefits that life could give me. I let it all slip away because I didn’t think I could manage the two.’ His eye were glazing over now and they no longer met my gaze. Instead he turned his head towards the nearby window, looking out into the landscape that met him. ‘Time is something that is precious to us all Jack and I wasted it. I worried far too much about the future and the past and never cared to take a look at the present…and as the years went by it was only in the hindsight that I realised how much I lost out on.’ ‘But the gym…and Jessica…and the wrestling…’ ‘No…they were never for me. And even if they were, they were never fulfilled. How many wrestlers do you know Jack that have never held a single title in their entire career…especially a career that lasted for well over fifteen years? Not even one title at all! Would you call that fulfilment?’ He coughed and hacked some blood up in spit onto his chest. Coughing roughly again he drew his hand over his mouth and shielded me from viewing what he was doing. ‘Dan…’ ‘I’m alright! None of those things granted me the pinnacle of what I could have achieved, you know and I know it. I’m trying to warn you Jack…you’re at the same crossroads as myself all those years ago and I don’t want you to make the same mistake; are you forgetting about living in the moment because of the future and the past?’ ‘I…I don’t know.’ ‘Well you better find out! Now, would you get my daughter so I can speak to her? And some water…god damn I’m parched!’ I couldn’t help but chuckle as he attempted to slam his fist down on the bed and make a thunderous noise like he used to at home. All that happened this time was a dull thud to echo along with one of the beeps from his machine. He leaned back then and stopped talking to me. Well, he didn’t give me a choice now did he? *** Amongst the vending machines And year old magazines In a place where we only say goodbye It stung like a violent wind That our memories depend On a faulty camera in our minds And I knew that you were a truth I would rather lose than to have never lain beside at all 5th of February - Kingston Hospital, Midnight I paced the corridors of the hospital, searching for Jessica and it took me only a couple of minutes to find her sitting in the restaurant. It was a very eerie moment when I found her. All the lights in the room were out except for the dull light coming form the nearby pastry case, displaying a veritable hoard of “interesting” choices you might say. In the background the humming of the can machine by the door was accentuated by the ticking of an old clock that hung over the other end of the room. It didn’t even feel like a restaurant, more like a cafeteria as each and every table was long with benches from which to sit. Along the floor black and white tile criss-crossed, attacking one another from each side, creating some mystical pattern that was lost with so much furniture on top of it. It could have read as one big note to the decorators for all anyone knew. Jessica was alone and sat at a bench by the window in the dark. Her image was silhouetted against the cold hard steel of the fridge wall that sat near her, managing to make the shadow more like an incorporeal being than anything else. The varying texture of the fridge allowed this being to dance and flicker with the light, almost like it was entertaining Jessica. She sat, hunched over a cup of coffee which had plumes of steam lifting over it. As I stood there, watching and waiting for any sort of movement, the shadow pranced and delighted in the steam, almost using it to infuse itself with its power and grow stronger, bigger. She sat away from me and I couldn’t tell what way she looked but I had a pretty good idea. Pondering on whether or not to move towards her, I wasn’t given the chance to decide as Jessica abruptly stood up and seemed to rush towards the door in which I was frozen. Her head was low and her hands covered her face, masking the terrible thoughts she was trying to lock deep within. All I could see were the eyes, piercing brown and booming out. But now they were scared, small and fading in response to the other fading lights in her life. Stopping, the shadow continued to play and frolic behind her, almost relishing in the morbid ominous tones that followed her. ‘He’s going to die isn’t he.’ It wasn’t even a question…just a statement. ‘Yes.’ ‘I see.’ She spun round again and refused to budge, locked to the floor, her legs standing square apart while her arms were crossed violently. As she spun her hair twirled in ringlets and against the steel wall behind her the shadow made a twirl much like a ballet dancer would but…almost like it was grasping some sort of weapon. And in the background the clock ticked on and on. ‘Jessica…’ ‘What.’ ‘He’s awake. He told me to come get you.’ Slowly her body twisted. It was achingly slow and time seemed to stop at several occasions. When her face finally came into view (some of it - the shadows kept one half cloaked) it was one with dim hope, the eyes telling it all to me. ‘Really?’ There was a greater sense of hope but one of desperation too. I knew that she was clinging onto the precipice right now and to say one wrong thing…then all would be lost. ‘Yeah. Come on.’ ‘No…wait.’ Slowly, again very slowly she crept up to me and puts her hands on the outside of my own and looked deep into my eyes. Now I could see her properly, dried mascara and all. The black mascara slid down either cheek, making her sunken eyes drift downwards with no clear ending. Her pupils were bloodshot and it all looked…so exhausting to me. So much effort had come forth from her eyes over whatever amount of time it had been. I looked at the clock…only half twelve at night. Still a long way to go. Her left hand moved up to my lip and pressed a finger right up against it. Closing her eyes to cover them from harm she gently swayed forward into my body, hoping for my arms to wrap around her and keep her safe and warm. All I felt was awkwardness. Her head tilted upwards, without opening her eyes and craning her neck her lips touched mine unexpectedly. I didn’t know what to do. I couldn’t tell her of the recent goings on, that would be too much right here, right now. So…I had a knee-jerk reaction and pulled away, a step back to make her almost fall to the floor. But she didn’t…. Tears did though. ‘I think you better…’ ‘Don’t.’ Her hands shoved half-heartedly in an attempt to be melodramatic and poignant, like Jessica felt she was in some sort of soap opera and this would gratify her feelings. Of course I didn’t budge and I guess my sullen face said it all. She fled in minor heartaches, not willing to talk anymore. And still the clock kept ticking. What did I do to deserve this? Sometimes the weight of this world decides it’s a great idea to rest limp all at once and see how it feels to me, see if I can stand it. A thank you is all that I can give and god damn it’s a hollow one. I have tried to be optimistic, I have tried to be the one to carry the burdens for the family, I have tried to be the one that was willing to give everything up so that… Wait. Is this what Dan meant? Does he honestly mean this? ‘Ha ha ha ha ha….’ I just couldn’t help but laugh, bellow even. Let the mirthful tones flow into the night and resound into the dreams of those asleep, potentially turning them into nightmares as I became the hollow sick laughter of a nemesis or monster. In fact, I revelled at that inclination. It seemed so…appropriate for the moment. Why not dance with the devil after I have already sharpened the sword on one woman’s body already? It was all so simple when it came down to it; all Dan wanted was for me to realise I could control exactly what I could and couldn’t do in my life. He had urged me to be true to myself and to recognise that you can’t please everyone in your life…but you can please yourself. My time was now and nothing should shirk me from knowing that. I needed to keep that focused. The past was irreparable. The future was uncertain. The present…was my own device to shape and form; to bend to my will. Please myself…while I still had the time. It’s strange how Dan could come out with something like that, especially considering the past two months I had endured. But then again, Dan was…is a remarkable man and someone who always understood me implicitly. Irrespective of how much he ran me into the ground in the gym or in that ring, there was nothing he instilled in me more than his empathetic bond that he could extend to everyone that he touched. And he had done that exact process tonight. Finally, someone had given me the chance to make a decision on my own…with no outside factors. To live life on my own terms and fuck everything else…I was going to live however I bloody well damn felt. The ring of my laughter had long since faded away down the hall out of the restaurant that I was still standing in, but it rang true in my heart. It would forever more, a conscious reminder of the careless nature that I could enjoy from now. Procrastination was not something I couldn’t even consider anymore. Now was the time of impulse, gut instinct and feeling. Nothing else should come into it. Not anymore anyway. Spinning on my heels I headed back out into the hall, better than I had started. And in the background, the shadow smiled at its success. *** Then I looked around at all the eyes on the ground As the TV entertained itself Cause there’s no comfort in the waiting room Just nervous paces bracing for bad news Then the nurse comes round And everyone lifts their heads And I’m thinking of what Sarah said That love is watching someone die 5th of February - Kingston Hospital, about one in the morning Nothing had changed with Dan’s condition. He was resting now and again was serene and calm in his bed. It pleased me that he was doing things his way, just as he had told me to do so. What didn’t was the mood with which Jessica was in. Nothing could get her to lift her head in the corner of the room and so I ceased in trying to pander to her emotion. I had nothing to feel ashamed for; it was on her impulse on what had happened and I merely reacted. She was a friend, nothing more. It wasn’t my fault she took the wrong time to try something. ‘Do you have any idea when the doctor is coming back?’ Her comment was quite surprising but nevertheless I answered it, resolute to be as stubborn as hell if that’s what she wanted. ‘No…you were here before me so…’ ‘So…?’ ‘So you would’ve heard anything about when the doctor is making calls and the like.’ It came out matter of fact but there was nothing I could do to hide that. She was just itching to get at me and this was her opening. ‘Fine, be that way.’ ‘Be what way?’ ‘Pigheaded. Arrogant.’ Where was this coming from? Her tone was very serious and grave, almost like she had been hit and was now bleeding herself, bleeding the very pulse that kept her going. ‘What?’ ‘You know what I’m talking about Jack.’ ‘Oh do I now? Thanks for reaching inside my head and telling me what to do. God forbid I have some sort of autonomy.’ ‘How DARE you speak to me that way.’ ‘Speak to you in what way Jessica? You’ve just called me arrogant and pigheaded like I had offended you. Could you explain to me where this came from and then I might consider taking back what I just said.’ Her mouth opened and she went to say something but she halted, her speech coming out in parched air but nothing more. Her nose wrinkled, a sure sign that something was bothering her on the inside and she couldn’t let it out just yet. Inwardly she was cursing, I knew because I had done it thousands of times before. Like when I had exiled myself to the abyss for almost two years. I had learnt the lesson of self-reflection many times over and I wasn’t going to put up with it. Seeing her failure to come to terms with it herself I gave her the answer she wanted. ‘Fuck it. I’m going home. I’ll be back in the afternoon to check up on you guys. Maybe you’ll have a calmer head then.’ The jaw dropped. That’s all I saw as I turned away and out into the corridor. It wasn’t just women who could leave on such statements. It was perfectly within my power - and certainly the power that I now knew was in me - to do such a thing. If she was going to sit there and press down on her feelings, it wasn’t my problem. Proudly holding my head up high I headed for the exit of the hospital. … … … … *** 5th of February - Kingston Hospital, two in the morning POST SCRIPT: - recorded on the 16th of February You’d think that was that wouldn’t you? But sadly it wasn’t. A lot of things happened that night. The darkness, the light…the epiphany in a career that had always been shrouded in darkness and the weight of the world. A cry for love…a rejection, but it wasn’t the end of the proceedings. To be perfectly honest, it’s taken this long to write the ending to this chapter because I didn’t understand my own actions and wasn’t aware of the consequences of them until much later. I had to wait to find out about them. Because when I walked out that door and into the corridor I wasn’t alone. Someone else stood close to me, to the side and outside my peripheral vision, but close enough that I could take in the scent, the aroma and the intoxicating perfume she wore. It was delicate…almost floral in its texture but prickly all the same, showing the bite that was behind it too. The hand grazed my own and curled until it could lead me towards another destination, one opposite to my prior venture. When I saw her she pressed her finger to her lips and pulled me into another room, next to where Dan was laying in rest. There was no halting in her movement and she swayed along with the silent ticking of a clock in the darkness. All I could make out were porcelain white legs dancing and playing, much like the shadow of before. The shimmer of a dress briefly glinted in the moonlight before it fell to the floor and revealed a greater, deeper understanding of her to me. My eyes saw red, blood red lust pour into them and I smelt the urge rising within me. “It’s time to take the bull by its horns,” she said and she licked her lips. For an instant I saw the blood by her mouth and she suckled on it like it was her life giving breath. Then she reverted to normal and came much closer. Her hands were all over me and I couldn’t resist but calling out my pet name for her. “That’s it my rose.” And the rest…would be too explicit for you to read now. Maybe in time…ah what a wonderful notion. So with hindsight and my new approach to the journey of life…what the hell have I learned? Time catches up with everything. It’s how you make of it in the present for yourself that was what’s important. Don’t let the driving forces behind your destiny be ancillary and secondary characters. Claim the limelight for yourself. This post script is dedicated to my mentor and teacher, Dan O’Grady, who showed me the door to greater things. He died that night of massive heart failure. Apparently there was no pain. I missed it because I was living life the way he wanted to…but couldn’t. Not until it was too late. He will never be forgotten. Dan ‘Firebrand’ O’Grady 1st of March 1947 - 5th of February 2006 So who’s going to watch you die? So who's going to watch you die? So who's going to watch you die?
