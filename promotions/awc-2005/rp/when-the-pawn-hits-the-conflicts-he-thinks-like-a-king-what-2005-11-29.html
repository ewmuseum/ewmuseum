Date: phx jack move With the pedal to the medal, drifting down the soggy road You grip the crank without feel, screaming things won’t go awry There will be time to meddle when consequences aren’t bold When the water looks still, you’ll pretend you can fly But now we must try to stay calm in the face of our fears Keeping foes in our sight and our cannons to load When our mistress hides dawn with her curtains kicked near Her wet hands smacking friends, they fight not to let go If God may be watching, I pray your call be heard So I may well-wish to the ones that I love And when I feel the sting, I’ll know it’s well deserved `Cause before this I was numb I never cried out the call to arms; I keep the cynics bane Whatever intentions I had, though good, were not what cast the vote A vengeful storm taken after a star, her fury was wet with pain In the end it wouldn’t matter who stood, if on water you would choke But my knees will not tremble when I keep you close With you keeping tight to the sleeve of my will And our attempt will not stumble if we keep to our post Where you’ll stay right at the nip of my heel Your voice was enough to keep me steadied in our tries But I hate to admit at the crack over the ship When the same God took his hammer across the skies That I witnessed our positions flip Now you stare back, looking to me for hope As the sky’s cracks rip the front of sail With nature’s wrath gaining the lead on our souls Tatters are made of our ship and of our faith as well If God may be watching, I pray your call be heard Only then my slate will be clean And when I feel the sting, I’ll know it’s well deserved Because the prayer wasn’t intended by me. The sail, made of oak, splinters then plummets below While the morale of our crew does much of the same Their will to keep fighting, like the wood, brakes close They wish for the ocean to once again remain tame A breach in the hull wants to make matters worse Now more than ever I believe our fates are bleak Think of the hunts that found us out here first And know now, more than ever, that greed made us this weak You wouldn’t be gasping among water and air If it weren’t for my horribly skewed views So I should stop masking what makes me care When my awkward intent is hardly big news I’m up to my ankles in water and chokes A matter of time before we start going down We look for a faith and we hope it will float When it was always known we’d be going down Why the reef has a vendetta against you or me It’s a fact that we will never get to know But it resists like butter through the structure we keep Halting whatever chance we had to grow Its all over now, I see us heading down But I’m still proud to know that you’re at my side Because if we’re going low, you’re the one I’ve found Who kept my spirits coming along for the ride We’re starting to sink with wood and warm bodies But the water makes due to change their state Our choice not to drink and avoid being copies With cold working hard to destroy my heart rate If God may be watching, I pray your call be heard Because we can’t leave without saying goodbye And when I feel the sting, I’ll know it’s well deserved For I never, not once, had the need to cry You’re still standing strong, with your neck peaking out Still rising above the water and the occasion to match And I realize all along, that you’ve never let me down You stayed my support whenever we’d attack The sail is broken, the hull is cracked The crew is scattered, our chances have ceased At least my hopes don’t fade to black They die with my mate, who accompanies me a mother fucking editor's note: the preceeding was written by a very stoned Adam
