Roses & Thorns - Delicate Planting

7 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/06 Have you ever felt like the things around you just click into place like one big jigsaw puzzle? The morning after the night with Sasha they did for me. Everything seemed to be fitting so perfectly without me even trying to acknowledge a pattern of events. It was just happening at my own pace like I had simply willed it to. But of course, for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, the standard physics crap that we’re all tied down to. The more you think about it, the more that it makes sense. Creepy huh? I was just about to become very aware of this phenomenon whether or not I believed in it. Life is never easy you know? *** 4th January - Kingston Man it was cold. Guess Europe wasn’t the only place that had experienced a bit of a cold winter but I wasn’t complaining, far from it. In fact, I was pretty positive about the whole winter getaway. It had recharged the batteries, it had given me new purpose, it had given so many things that I was looking for. Most of all, I had the drive to keep wrestling. How? It’s really quite simple. Spending those last few days with Sasha had been a dreamlike state, something out of this world and on a higher plane. To say that we were suited was a gross understatement. We were perfect for each other and the time we had managed to spend with one another was so well fashioned, orchestrated and strung that I couldn’t have done any better myself. Everything was the way it should have been. Just perfect. Sasha was a wonder to me, a complex individual who shared my passions in life; the want to make something of ourselves, the drive to become everything that we deserve and truly it’s amazing that we had such similar ways of going about it. With her I forgot about the entire problems of wrestling and whether or not it was what I wanted to do anymore. Within moments she had frozen it all and eviscerated it clinically and fully. It was only a few words that were required from her that made me realise the folly of leaving now. ‘You’re too good and too strong for that.’ That‘s all I needed, just those simple, plain words to remind me of the well of talent that rose within me from the very earth. I was solid as a rock, strong as a bull and ready to shake the very mountains if necessary in my quest for fulfilment. Really all I had lacked was a little belief in that ability. Sasha reminded me of that. It was a blissful few days with her, right up to New Year’s before she was forced to leave and return to her business with AWC. The goodbye was short and brief but the passion I knew was burning inside her. I knew that it wouldn’t be long before I’d see her again. Or so I had thought. You see there was one problem that was about to come crashing down right in the middle of this whole affair and one that would not be going anywhere in the short or long term. A certain agent who was responsible for my publicity both in and outside the ring. If she heard what I’d done, there would be no more and I would be looking for another agent. So I did what any self-respecting man does when treating his women with respect and admiration: he lies right to their face. Not a big lie, just a small white one so that it would camouflage better against the bareness of my cover. If anything I wouldn’t cover it very well so that hopefully I’d be able to show to her bit by bit what was going on and that she would accept it and be able to work with one another. The last thing I wanted was a divide between my professional and private life which were pretty linked by now. Well, you can’t always get what you want. Pulling in to the drive way of the O’Grady’s all looked calm and well. The flowers were still in their early stages, still budding to become something after shining so brightly the year before. It would be their hope to reach the pinnacle, just like mine. The rest of the traditional American house was as it should be; organised and tidy. Dan had always run a tight ship in so far as presentation was concerned. But then I noticed a little bit of rubbish lying in the corner, just by the door. It looked totally out of sync with the norm. What was…? Then I saw her, standing in the doorway. There was no happiness, no smile, no nothing coming from her, just total and complete bleakness. As she knew I had seen her she ran towards me, locking her grip on my body and not letting go for a second. I felt tears staining my shirt. Cupping her face with my hands I let her face, covered by tears, look at me. ‘What’s wrong Jess?’ ‘It’s dad….he’s gone to the hospital after having a heart attack.’ Her head buried right back into my chest again as the fullness of the statement caught fire in my head. I could never have expected something like this to happen, not in a million years, especially with the way he worked out. You can never see it coming…. ‘Jess…let’s go.’ She looked up at me and attempted a smile but ended up simply nodding. Without another word we got back into the car and headed for the hospital.
