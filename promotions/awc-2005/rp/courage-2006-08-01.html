Date: blah He who makes a beast out of himself gets rid of the pain of being a man. Darcy had been missing in action for hours already that day. The pattern of the past few days found Amy waking up alone every morning, and being stirred to life late at night by her boyfriend's return to bed. He could only be sleeping an hour or two per night, which concerned her, but beyond that... he was obviously on a wild goose chase for Jane. The man was oblivious to the fact that he shared a bed - if not briefly - each night with the person who knew Jane's whereabouts. Even though the kidnapping fiasco was Jeph taking a joking comment too far, Amy felt obvious responsibility and awaited the 'perfect moment' to tell Darcy. Preferably a moment when Jane wouldn't ruin the entire thing by trying to pin the scheme directly on her shoulders. Him not knowing kept him away - not pleasing, and quite panic-inducing for the little redhead. Him finding out that Jane's life was actually in the hands of his jealous and overly violent girlfriend? The result would be MUCH less pleasant for Amy, so she kept distance within the situation. Her opportunity to spill the beans would arrive... maybe... hopefully... kinda soon... In the meantime, her conscience was killing her. She absolutely had to know where Darcy was, and exactly what he was doing in his quest to save his ex. He had his cell phone and wasn't answering it half the time, so checking his voicemails were out of the question and most likely a useless tactic anyway. Since a letter in the year 2006 is rare, that left e-mail as the only tool to use in her spying. Thankfully, she knew Darcy's passwords - whether he was aware or not - to almost everything... and aside from that, they were saved on his computer. In her mind, he was almost begging someone to invade his privacy, and that way she could justify her actions. Besides, if you accidentally kidnapped someone's ex, hacking their e-mail isn't exactly a stretch. She was laying low in a hotel until I could give her that ring back, but evidently she wasn’t laying low enough. Somebody must have broken in from the back, because there was glass all over the place when went around to look for her. Worse yet, it’s been a few days now and there’s been no sign of her. And worse still, we can’t call the police. I never got the details exactly, but Jane’s been working with Senator Edwards, the congressman from North Carolina. Politics are a tricky game, and if word of this gets out to the public not only is her career effectively over, but so is anybody’s who was the least bit connected with her. But there a couple of silver linings here. One, Jane took care of the problem with the police. She registered herself in the hotel under a fake name, so now all the news stations are reporting on a breaking and entering at the Lombard St. Super Motel 8, and that a girl named Sara Fleetwood has gone missing. So the cops will be busy on a wild good chase for Mick Fleetwood’s illegitimate daughter, which effectively takes them out of the picture. The second silver lining….well, I think I have a lead on her location. I found a ransom note inside her room, and I got it out of there before anybody else had a chance to see it. Her dad is in a world of shit with some big-time gambling boss, and they’re holding her in exchange for money. Which is good, because it means she’s probably still alive…but also real bad, because they’re asking for way more than anybody I know could come up with on short notice, and that’s including if Jane had hawked her mom’s wedding ring. But here’s the kicker: whoever dropped that note left his greasy prints all over the paper. I know a guy down at the station who was willing to keep his mouth shut about all of this, and I’ve spent the last 48 hours slamming down Red Bull and searching their crime lab database to find a match on those fingerprints. My eyes are red as hell and I’m currently fighting off delusions, but I think I found the guy. Some lowlife named Murph McAlstott – he’s been in and out of jail for petty theft, and apparently the boys in blue have been keeping an eye on him for suspected mob activity. But here’s what seals the deal; when I went to Jane’s hotel room to deliver the ring, some guy walked by and practically stared a hole into me when I was like 10 feet from her door. And he looked just like this guy that the police have a file on. They’ve got some basic info on him, including an address…it’s just gotta be that guy, I can feel it in my bones. So here’s the sitch: Number one, I need your absolute silence on this. You can’t tell anybody about this – not the police, not your mom, not Liz, nobody. And number two…well, this is going to sound rough, but I really need your help on this one. The police can’t get involved, so it’s up to us to get Jane back. If we pay a visit on this McAlstott character and…coerce him into telling us where Jane is, we can get her home safe and sound with nobody else being the wiser. I know, I know, this plan is dangerous as hell, maybe even stupid. But I haven’t got a whole lot to work with, and this is the best I’ve got. He was obviously driven halfway insane by the lack of sleep. If that request wasn't bad enough, she knew from the e-mail to Johnny that he was about to try dealing with mafia-tied men by himself. From past experiences and common sense, Amy knew very well that Darcy was going down a path that would force his life into hell... as if living with her wasn't bad enough already. That's why she had to make the call. ''Jeph,'' she began speaking to the voicemail on the other end of her cell phone, ''He's looking for her. He's going to some mob guy's fucking HOME with Johnny to try intimidating him. We have to tell him... I wouldn't be able to live with myself if he got hurt or in trouble dealing with real criminals. Call me back YESTERDAY, asshole... bye.'' A few minutes passed, and no call came back. Amy grew more panicked, and eventually decided to confess... ''Johnny... pick the fuck up. Fine, I've done some investigating, errr... look, just tell Darcy NOT to go find that guy, because I... err... I found Jane already. Just tell him. Bye.'' ... Well, sort of confess. Now all she had to do was sit back and pray for the planets to align to a point that Johnny would actually tell his best friend such an outlandish thing. The problems only grew more complex with time and, to top it all off, she was a few days away from having to fight for a title all over Huntington beach. Weaponry crossed her mine, but Jane was too heavy on it. There was no bright side in any of these equasions, and all she could do was hope that Darcy was accepting when the truth came out. She was packing, praying and relentlessly calling around in hopes of finding something out, or calling Darcy off of his hunt. If it saved him, she'd take the blow to their relationship squarely in the jaw and make no bones about it. At that point, she had little choice. It was time to be selfless... if she had it in her.
