When Harry met Johnny

14 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/11/05 The first time it happened it was because there were only so many guest list places at the Soho-And-So jazz bar club that John could blag. Harry understood that John had to take Lindsay Church instead. Sometimes it’s alright to abandon your friends to get entirely intoxicated and fuck a sexy photographer; so Harry put his Armanis in his wardrobe and laced up his trainers. Harry jumped in a black cab, “West End ways driver” and coughed deeply in a menacing tone that told the driver that he needed to reduce his chirpy levels to zero. Harry began to tape his hands up, the driver, feeling daring asked “What’s that for then mate?” Harry no sold the comment and as they approached the less exclusive clubs Harry scouted the ones with the biggest queues. “Here will do driver” “That’s 23.60 than guv’” Obliging and furthermore heavily tipping Harry responded “I don’t want to fuck up my knuckles do I?” “The Gautano” club was neon. The music was monotonous, poppy and some of the worst trance ever. The kids who went there smoked grass on the way, took advantage of £1 shots and £2 pints of shit lager before taking pills incase they weren’t already on their way to acting like a wanker and vomiting. Harry marched to the back of the queue, which stretched right around the corner to the next stale club through strewn kebab meat and lettuce. He proceeded storm through the trash, the tarts and the lads, spilling tins of lager barging shoulders and pushing limpet couples. By the time Harry had stormed the Red Sea had parted for Harry and he was at the front an angry bouncer only managed to blurt, “Now just what the fuck-“ before Harry broke the former amateur boxing champion’s previously in-tact nose with a six foot drop kick. A melee of cross buttock throws, rugby tackles and headbutts followed before Harry darted into the nearest limousine. He was lucky; Harry’s father had once had an affair with Jessica Swanson who was currently sucking off a wealthy man in said limo. Harry managed to get a ride to the Soho-And-So jazz bar club and a bottle of champagne by way of blackmail. Lindsay Church was a minx. John had began to use all his best lines and then realised he was already game on. She laughed too much at all his mediocre jokes (he knew they were mediocre, but also realised this had a charm) and bit her lip after the ceased laughing every time. She drank lots, she drank fast, she accidentally on purpose got herself wet when she popped open a bottle of champagne. John was well in when he got a text from Harry saying he was outside. “Hey Harry, where have you been?” “Training John, I’m in a bit of bother as it happens” The bouncer interjected, “No trainers, no tracksuit, guest list only son” “Fuck off you poncey council estate chimpanzee, I’m bringing my mate in” The bouncer wasn’t best pleased with John’s drunken outburst and charged. John knelt instinctively and caught the bouncer with a winding headbutt to the gut, Harry threw his knee into the now doubled over bouncer’s face (third broken nose of the evening). The bouncer no sooner fell to his back than John fell out the sky with a sharp elbow to the ribs. Harry grabbed the floored man’s hand and stepping on his chest fell back, causing the most tremendous stomach throw. The bouncer was lifted off the floor this time by John who lunged into an Irish whip, dashing the poor man into Harry’s renowned rugby tackle. “So like I said John, training” “We really ought to go Harry” Lindsay Church staggering down the street yelled “Taxi!” and the three left. “So we should probably be a tag team Harry” “Yeah, that was alright” “We should probably use that back there in a ring” “Yeah, needs a silly name really. Something that describes us” “Do you boys wanna come up for a Doublemochalattefrappacino Decaf?” And so began the Trendy-Twenty-Something’s wrestling partnership. That evening John and Harry tagged off on Lindsay Church, who incidentally went on to take the photos used to the TTS 8 x 10’s on sale at the AWC merchandise stands.
