What Empowers 'The Violence' Vince Jones?

11 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/2006 The screen is black and a message slowly fades in on the screen in white letters. It reads: 'There are many things in life which empower a man and when he comes across these particular things he feels invincible. He feels as though nothing can stop him. He feel as though he is ontop of the world. He has no fear because the empowerment brought to him by this item brings him security. Then, there are times in which a man becomes complacent. He believes that what he has will always be there and will sometimes forget its real value. Sometimes this complacency can further grow to the point of a man believing that it is within his own self that he derives his power. He becomes blinded to what truly keeps him strong and keeps him going; but, when the time comes and he is endanger of losing that which empowers him that is when its real worth is re-realized. A man never wants to lose his empowerment and when the time comes he will fight to keep what keeps him going...' The camera slowly fades in and we are in a small bedroom. The light gently penetrates the blinds and dances on the floor slightly illuminating the room. The camera pans around the room past the dresser, a small nightstand, and catches sight of Vince Jones who is fast asleep in his bed with a cocky smirk plastered across his face. He wears a black doo rag on his head, a white tanktop t-shirt, and a pair of black boxers. All of a sudden Vince Jones slowly begins to stir to life after a goodnight's sleep. He stretches, yawns, sits up, and begins wiping the sleep away from his eyes. He turns around and looks at his empty bed and suddenly becomes alarmed. Vince Jones: (to himself) Fuck! Where the hell that damn trophy at? Vince slaps his hands down against the bed in fury, quickly rises to his feet, and begins searching around the vicinity with his eyes in a rather frantic manner. He drops down to the floor and peers under the bed, but all he sees is a few crushed, empty beer cans and a box marked 'Maxim'. Vince Jones: Damn! It ain't there! He hops up to his feet and rushes out of his room. He makes his way down the small hallway and reaches another bedroom. He turns the doorknob, swings the door open and peers inside. Vince Jones: Jasmine! Jasmine: (from down the hallway) I'm in the den, baby. Vince peers around the vicinity of her room but she is nowhere to be found. He slams the door in frustration and begins storming down the hallway to the den where he sees Jasmine sitting on the couch staring at Vince's Countdown Trophy with a large smile on her face. As he approaches she glances up at him and greets him with a bright smile. Jasmine: Hey V! Glad to see you're... Vince reaches over and tries to snatch the trophy out of her grasp but she begins to cling onto it tight not wanting to let it go. Vince Jones: Hand that shit over! Jasmine: But I just wanted to look at it, V! Vince Jones: Nah! Fuck that, Jasmine! Get your hands off the trophy! Jasmine: But I... Vince raises his backhand and glares down at her. Vince Jones: Woman, you better let go of this damn trophy before you get the taste slapped out your damn mouth! Jasmine glares back at Vince in defiance and Vince stares back and shakes his head at her. Vince Jones: Say V won't! Say he won't! Now back the fuck down, Jasmine! Jasmine glares at him for a moment longer and then finally releases the trophy and pouts like a child. Jasmine: I just wanted to look at it! That's all! Why you being so uptight about that damn trophy anyways? She turns around and Vince can be seen looking that trophy up and down very closely inspecting it for damages. Jasmine appears a bit peeved by his attentiveness to the trophy and just rolls her eyes. Jasmine: This is exactly what I was talking about, V! Vince continues turning that trophy around in his hands as he continues inspecting its surface. Jasmine becomes livid. Jasmine: V!!!!! Vince Jones: What the hell do you want, woman? Shit! Jasmine: Thank God! I finally managed to get your attention for a second! Anyways, ever since won that damn trophy off of that redneck bitch, Teresa, you've been paying so much attention to that damn thing that you've been neglecting me of my Jasmine time. Vince Jones: And? Jasmine: And, I think you should put the damn trophy down for a moment and spend some time with me. Vince eyes the trophy and then looks at Jasmine and shakes his head in disagreement. Vince Jones: Nah, V thinks he'll spend some more time with the trophy if that's alright with you. Jasmine: (gritting her teeth) No, that's not alright with me, V. That's not alright with me at all. So, why don't you make yourself comfortable with me on this couch. She pats a spot next to her on the black, leather sofa, and looks up at Vince with a large smile. Vince looks down at her and the couch and appears a bit disinterested as he continues to attend to the trophy. Jasmine: (in frustration) So, you'd rather be with that trophy than you would with me. Is that what you're trying to say? Vince Jones: Huh? Jasmine quickly rises to her feet, marches over to Vince and slaps him across the face. He looks at her in confusion. Vince Jones: What the hell was that for, huh? Jasmine: For being stupid! That's what that was for! Vince lowers the trophy by his side and just shakes his head at her in disappointment. Vince Jones: So, why you gotta get all bitchy on V, huh? Jasmine: Bitchy? I'm not being bitchy! Vince Jones: Yeah you are! Jasmine: No, I'm not! You know how hard it is for me to get your attention sometimes? Vince Jones: No... Jasmine: Too damn hard, V! Vince turns to the side and then looks at Jasmine once again a bit angered by the fact that she's angry with him. Vince Jones: Well, you obviously got V's attention right now! What the fuck else do you need, huh? Jasmine: Yeah I've got your attention because we're arguing! Seems like that's about the only time I ever have your attention at all these days! Vince Jones: (shakes his head) That ain't true. Jasmine: (nodding) Yes, it is! You're always so wrapped up in this and that to the point that the only time I really get the chance to spend with you is when we're on the road. And even then its all about business and wrestling related issues. Its never about stuff that truly matters to me. Vince Jones: (nods his head and folds his arms across his chest) So, what you sayin' is that you don't care about V's career in this game? Is that what you sayin, Jasmine? Jasmine: (shaking her head and rolls eyes) No, you're not listening to me. Vince Jones: V heard you perfectly clear, Jasmine. Jasmine: You heard my words, but you didn't really hear me (points to herself) Vince Jones: Oh, here we go with the crazy ass female talk and shit! Vince Jones throws his hands up in the air in frustration, pushes past her, and plops himself down on the leather sofa. He slams the Countdown Trophy down on the table, folds his arms across his chest, and gives her an agitated gaze. Vince Jones: What? You gonna lecture V on some shit that don't mean nothin' to him or somethin' now too? Jasmine folds her arms across her chest and just gazes at Vince as he looks up at her waiting for her to speak. Vince Jones: Well, you obviously had something important you wanted to say! Well, go ahead and spit that shit out! Jasmine: I...Oh, there's no use even talking to you, V! You probably kiss that damn trophy and sing to it at night! Vince looks at her and just laughs to himself. Vince Jones: You must be out your damn mind, Jasmine! Why the hell would V do some shit like that, huh? ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The scene switches to the streets of New York City where Vince Jones can be seen sitting in the car with a rather large smile on his face as a faint melody can be heard playing in the background. Vince Jones: Damn! Its a good day out in New Yawk! What you think 'bout that, huh? (pauses in silence and then laughs to himself) Nah. You ain't gotta say a damn thing. V knows. So, sun ain't shinin' on you too much is it? Vince turns to his right and the camera spots the Countdown Trophy sitting on the seat next to him in his car. Vince whips out a handkerchief and begins shining it up. Vince Jones: Yeah. We gonna have a great day. All of a sudden Bill Withers 'Just the Two of Us' begins playing in the background as the scene switches to an ice-cream parlor where Vince Jones can be seen buying not one, but two ice-cream cones as he holds onto his Countdown Trophy. 'Just the two of us We can make it if we try Just the two of us Just the two of us Building castles in the sky Just the two of us You and I' The scene then switches to a park where Vince can be seen flirting heavily with a beautiful woman with long hair. He leans in and is about to give her a kiss when he spots a little kid digging up dirt nearby and dumping it into his Countdown Trophy with a large grin on his chubby face. Vince pushes the woman to the side, hops up to his feet, stiff arms the little kid to the ground making his cry, and snatches up his trophy. Vince appears to be yelling at the kid and throwing a fit as he dumps out all the dirt ontop of the kid's head. The scene now switches to photo booth where Vince Jones can be seen posing with his Countdown Trophy taking picture after picture after picture. He suddenly stops and pokes his head outside the photo booth as an angry couple is standing outside of it with their arms folded across their chests. Vince raises his backhand to them and they scurry away. The scene switches to a pool where Vince can be seen sitting in a hot tub surrounded by a group of beautiful women. Sitting right next to him is his Countdown Trophy. The lovely ladies can be seen rubbing Vince up and down his chest as well as the Countdown Trophy. The scene switches to his bedroom where he can be seen kissing the Countdown Trophy right before he climbs into his bed and goes to sleep. 'Just the two of us We can make it if we try Just the two of us Just the two of us Building castles in the sky Just the two of us You and I' The camera slowly fades to black. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The camera fades back into Vince's apartment as he snaps back to reality. Jasmine can be heard calling his name. Jasmine: V!! Vince Jones: Huh? Oh yeah. Nah! V don't be kissin' that damn trophy and shit! He don't do any of that shit! Jasmine: Well, its pretty obvious to me that you care more about that trophy than you do about me. Vince just shakes his head and looks at Jasmine in disappointment. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Let V make somethin' perfectly clear to you, Jasmine. This ain't (points at the trophy) just a trophy here. This is like a steppin' stone. This damn trophy be representin' what could come for V in A Dubb Cee. V on his way up the fuckin' charts and he takes one step closer to gettin' where he wanna be. Jasmine sits down on the sofa beside him and smiles. Jasmine: That is true. You're right. I guess I just overreacted. How about a kiss for the Countdown Trophy holder? Jasmine leans in closer and closer. Vince suddenly lifts up the trophy between the two of them and gives it a kiss. Vince Jones: V loves ya, babe Jasmine opens her eyes and sees Vince kissing the trophy with a smile on his face which infuriates her to no end. She rises to her feet. Jasmine: And there you go again! Well, you know what? I think its about time I take a walk for a little while! I hope you and your trophy have fun together! And wear a rubber while you're at it! Jasmine marches over to the front door, exits, and slams it shut behind her. Vince just sits there in silence and looks at the trophy with a blank stare. Vince Jones: (points at the trophy) See, what you did, mahfucka? Vince leans back on the sofa and smiles. Vince Jones: V still loves ya though and he ain't gonna let anyone take you away. And that's real... Vince closes his eyes and attempts to take a nap as he props his feet up on the coffee table in front of him. The camera slowly fades to black. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- {{Flashback}} The camera slowly fades into a dark and dank prison cell a few years ago where Vince Jones can be seen sitting on his bed with his head hung low. After being locked up behind bars he has more than enough time to think about his mistakes and in doing so his spirits are low. At the moment Vince appears to be wallowing in self-pity at the state of his life at the moment wishing for something better than a life in a prison cell. The sound of chatter from other inmates can be heard ringing out through the prison, but Vince remains silent. The faint click clack of a footsteps can be heard coming closer and closer from outside of his cell. All of a slim warden appears at his jail cell with a letter in hand. Warden: Got some mail for ya, Jones. The warden tosses the letter towards Vince through the cell's bars and continues on his way. Vince reaches out for the letter and snatches it up from off the cold, dusty prison cell floor. His eyes light up when he sees Jasmine's name written on it. The camera slowly fades to black as he tears open the letter.
