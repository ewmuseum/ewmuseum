Time for a Change

28 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/29/06 On the afternoon of February 17th, mere hours before the Atlantic Wrestling Club’s Bloodlust Pay-Per-View, newly hired wrestling coach Trent Marshall is set to once again pour over tape of Norton and Bridges Fresh tag team match against Celestial Fury for the umpteenth time. Dressed casually in black sweatpants and a black University of Iowa sleeveless t-shirt with “HAWKEYES” written across the front in bold, yellow lettering. Armed only with a pen and a pad of paper, Marshall situates himself on a black desk chair in front of a small combination television/VCR and presses play. ~~~Stalling for a second Russ stares at Butterfly and then storms toward his much smaller opponent. With his chest out and arms ready to catch her, Russ quickly bundles the shocked Butterfly to the ring mat with an unorthodox Lou Thesz Press. Hash Brown attempts to capitalize on the situation and throw right hands at Butterfly’s face but the only woman in the match manages to wriggle free from under Hash Brown and retreat across the ring. ~~~ Come on Bridges, you have to lock her down. There’s no excuse for not sprawling and using your weight to your advantage. That’s the very reason there are strict weight classes in amateur wrestling, because it’s so much easier to establish a nice, strong hold against a smaller opponent. It takes so much energy just to get back up to a vertical base, let alone mount any sort of offense, that you can really tip a match in your favor from the outset, just by sticking to the wrestling basics. We’ve got to work on that first thing next week. ~~~As Hash Brown returns, Butterfly uses his momentum to hit a simple drop toehold crashing the overweight star to the floor. As Russ holds his face, Butterfly takes the opportunity to climb the corner and tag in The Green Grappler. Walking across the ring apron, Celestial Fury perch themselves on the turnbuckles and watch Russell Bridges. AWC’s King of Breakfast Stuffs takes his time getting up, but seems a little confused that Hamada is not in his immediate area. Turning round, Hash Brown Bridges can only watch the tag team of Celestial Fury perform matching Missile Dropkicks on him and then crash to the mat with him. ~~~ Even my junior varsity wrestlers know never to turn your back to your opponent, and they just wrestle one man at a time. Now you’ve got Bridges staring down at the canvas for what seems like an hour after a simple drop toehold—completely inexcusable—and this is just in the first few minutes of the match. If I don’t get these boys in shape soon, I’m going to have a cardiac arrest on my hands. But that’s nothing a couple of days running stairs can’t fix. ~~~Rolling away, Butterfly Hamada retreats from the ring as The Green Grappler quickly applies an Abdominal Stretch to his opponent. The complete rookie quickly screams out in pain and is very uncomfortable with the move. Holding the move for quite a few seconds really drains Russ Bridges of his fight fairly quickly. ~~~ More like a couple of weeks. Should have named themselves the Suicide Bombers, these guys blow up so fast. ~~~As Russ enjoys his few seconds of break he turns around as The Green Grappler tags in Butterfly Hamada. Although being screamed at by his tag team partner, Hash Brown decides to fight and rushes the Celestial Fury corner at pace hoping to catch the mixed gender duo off guard… but sadly he doesn’t. ~~~ What’s the point of wrestling in a tag team if you’re going to treat it as a singles match? Get the fresh guy in the match and catch your breath on the outside. The only thing worse than their physical conditioning seems to be their mental preparation. Man, I had no idea these guys had so far to go to get where they need to be. But hey, that’s why I’m here. ~~~As they do, “We Going to Be Alright” by 112 kicks in on the speakers. Looking up Celestial Fury notice Tiara Belle Russell and Wayne Russell stepping through the entranceway carrying guitars but unconcerned they simply return to the task in hand. However, the music clicks with Chuck Norton who immediately jumps to his feet and runs to the bottom of the ramp way. ~~~ Now this pisses me off. So not only are you going to show up out of shape, but then you’re going to turn around and leave your partner double-teamed while you gallivant after a couple freaking guitars? I’m going to run the hell out of these two Monday—this will not happen again. ~~~In the ring, the Green Grappler comfortably connects with a cradle piledriver on Russ Bridges. On the stage Tiara and Wayne are posing with Norton & Bridges’ lost guitars and then proceed to slam them together in a scene from a Garth Brooks live show. Butterfly Hamada sails through the air with a corkscrew moonsault. As Chuck Norton runs up the ramp to his broken guitar, Celestial Fury, together pin the limp body of Russell Bridges. ONE! TWO! THREE! ~~~ Wow, that’s a heck of a lot worse than I ever expected. I can forgive inexperience—that’s the whole point of bringing me here, to coach them up—but the lack of teamwork and heart they showed is a whole lot more troubling. But I’ll give them one thing, they are a couple of big boys, and you can’t teach size. Ok, so Monday I want to concentrate on watching tape of their first couple of matches. Tuesday we can get to the conditioning they so badly need. We’ll start with some work on the stationary bikes and then build up to wind sprints. Then Wednesday… The vibration of Marshall’s cell phone interrupts his train of thought. Pulling the phone out of his pocket, he flips it open to answer the call. “Hello Marshall speaking.” “Gone, but they have a match in a few hours on Bloodlust?” “So is there anyway I can convince you to consider another course of action?” “Well I appreciate you calling me, looks like I’m going to have to start working on the old resume again.” “It’s not that I haven’t thought about it before, I just didn’t think the opportunity would present itself so quickly, especially in a place like the Atlantic Wrestling Club.” “Well I appreciate that, but there’s no need to put it off—the answer is yes.” “Absolutely, I’ll be by tomorrow to go over the paperwork and get this thing in writing.” And just like that, Norton and Bridges’ careers in the AWC come to an end… …and Trent Marshall’s begins.
