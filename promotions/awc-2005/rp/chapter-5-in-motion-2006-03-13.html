Chapter 5: In Motion

13 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.13.06 As the dark blue of dusk began creeping into the evening California sky, Darcy Markson silently chastised himself for not taking in more of these beachside sunsets. Having lived in California for nearly a decade, it was easy to get spoiled by the beautiful sight as the natives often did. It was definitely easy to forget to be captivated by a sunset, no matter how stunning it was, when the treat was available nearly every night – a fact that Darcy could definitely attest to. But now that the Nova Scotia native Amy Campbell was back in his life, California had charmed yet another to marvel in its striking view of the horizon…one who would remind Darcy to do the same. Hand in hand, the two ambled near the shoreline as the increasingly crimson sun crept closer to the horizon. Catching some glimpses from a few fellow beach patrons, Darcy smiled to himself, wondering if any of them thought that he and Amy were on an awkward date after they’d answered each others’ personal ads. The cliché ones, to be precise – where both of them mentioned that they enjoy candlelit dinners and long walks on the beach, not because they actually did but rather a pathetic attempt to seem deep and emotionally inclined to the opposite sex, and then the two actually would agree to go on a long beachside walk even though they’d rather be eating Cheetos on their couch like they did every other night. But before Darcy could delve any deeper into his ungainly daydream, his counterpart called him back. “Isn’t this beautiful?” Amy asked. “How are you not out here watching this every night?” “I have to admit, I’ve been here eight or nine years now and it never gets any less impressive. But since when are you a sunset aficionado?” “Since I got a chance to watch them when it’s actually above freezing,” came her reply. “Plus I figured this would give me a chance to try relating to you on your level, Captain Emo.” Darcy attempted to ponder a retort, but gave up on the endeavor when he saw that Amy’s gaze had returned to the setting sun. This was yet another thing Darcy loved about taking in this view; it removed the need for attempting to fill every silence with passing conversation. You could easily spend a great deal of time with someone without saying a word, allowing the opportunity for silent thought and deep introspection…or in Darcy’s case, a chance to figure out what the hell he’d done to piss off the Middle East. Darcy wondered at what point in his career he developed the knack for drawing the ire of Arabia. He noted the irony of the situation; Darcy had meant many an ignorant redneck in his time, with inane credos such as “THESE COLORS DON’T RUN!” and “THE ONLY GOOD ARAB…IS A DEAD ARAB” despite the fact that they would likely never cross a Muslim in their sheltered little universe. Yet Darcy, the free-thinking liberal who had no problem with any group of people, let alone Arabs, had been coming to blows with them left and right for years. Darcy counted them off in his mind; The Singh brothers in UGWA…Killian Salah in Pier Six…”Muscles” Mustafa in DangeR Wrestling…and just last week, simply by trying to have some fun with a guy and his ridiculous “Knowledge Challenge,” Darcy’s jaw was still smarting at the hands of Captain Suleimon. And it appeared it wouldn’t even end there. In a few short days Darcy was set to match up with Anton Assault, who…well, it turned out he was actually from France. Darcy cursed under his breath, hating when his theories didn’t quite pan out. Still, the amount of trouble Darcy had managed to come by from that section of the world was impressive nonetheless. Was it an unfortunate coincidence? Or was Darcy somehow incredibly over in the Middle East, so much so that Arab wrestlers dreamed of making names for themselves by derailing the D-Train? Darcy himself had his money on explanation #3: a secret international plot spearheaded by the CIA and Arabic oil barons in response to Darcy making their lives so difficult over the past few years with his tireless government lobbying. He suspected there was no way to know for sure…and even though he told the good Captain that he “made it his business to know things,” the last thing he wanted was the CIA coming after him for knowing too much. Maybe some mysteries are better left unsolved, he mused. Besides, it wasn’t important. Nobody remembered Darcy’s epic struggles with Salah or Mustafa…because they were hardly struggles at all, but rather just two more names on the growing list of those that had gone up against the Darcinator and found themselves eliminated. Sully would be no different; he was less a Middle Easterner than he was an assclown whose ego far surpassed any physical prowess. He’ll soon get his, Darcy thought. There were more important matters at hand. Like those actively trying to screw me. This referred to Amy, as well as the TransAtlantic Champion, Jack Murphy. In their minds, of course, there was no problem - a win was a win for the champ, no matter how dubious, and in all likelihood Darcy wasn’t even on Murphy’s radar screen as he busied himself with fending off the next hungry challenger. And Amy’s reasoning (at least, the story she gave Darcy) was that she’d only done to him what he’d done to her a few weeks before; stick her nose where it didn’t belong. She likely considered them even, and thus the matter closed. And had it been anyone else involved but his girlfriend and AWC’s franchise player, and had the unfortunate series of events taken place anytime before the twilight February Fresh!, Darcy might have been inclined to agree with them. After all, even if he had interfered with Amy’s match with then TransAtlantic Champion Adam Dick to save her skull from getting caved in, he knew Amy would never forgive him for getting involved. And he couldn’t even really blame Jack Murphy for taking advantage of the situation – even if Aimz was hellbent on getting herself involved in Darcy’s match in kind, it was Darcy who took his eye off the ball, allowing himself to be taken in by one of wrestling’s oldest tricks: the distraction immediately followed by an illegal rope-assisted rollup for a quick victory. If Darcy Crisis really was the accomplished veteran he claimed to be, falling prey to such a tried and true swindling was no way to prove it. But these two picked the wrong week to make Darcy look like a fool. Only minutes before the debacle went down, Darcy had gone before the public and, on record no less, proclaimed that night to be the Darcy Crisis coming out party. It was supposed to be the return of the wise-cracking, glory-hounding, take-no-prisoners and spare-no-asskickings Darcy, the attitude that had sparked his ascent to the top of the wrestling world so many years ago. And if he was to stay true to his word that the Darcy of old had returned to claim his rightful spot as a wrestling icon, and if he expected anyone to take his word seriously, something had to be done in the way of exacting revenge. There were several differences between the “true” Darcy of old, the brash man of twenty-four who had been in the prime of his career, and the current incarnation living within a man now cruising toward thirty. Darcy was now certainly older, but more importantly wiser; he could now see past his own ego, and had a grasp on other people’s feelings and reasoning behind their intentions. He could see why Aimz and Murphy had done what they’d done, and could almost…almost forgive them. But unfortunately for them, their timing had been just a little too poor, and their profiles just a little too high to ignore. For all the things that had changed over the years, there was still one constant that linked the Darcys of past and present - Nobody screws with Darcy Crisis. Nobody. Not the TransAtlantic Champion, not even my girl. Jared Irons found out the hard way when I damn near crippled him after he stole my North American strap…Eddie Osbourne found out the hard way when he found himself tapping for his life, just before his PSW World Champion walked out on his company, with Eddie’s girlfriend and the company’s Heavyweight Title in tow. Jack Murphy will be no different…and as much as it’s going to pain me, neither will Amy. Amy would probably blow a gasket when she caught wind of the plan that was already in motion. Unbeknownst to her, or Murphy, or anyone else for that matter, Darcy had approached fellow California and AWC Entertainment Co-Manager David “Pearl” Harber with his proposal to get even with the two… ***************************************************************************************************************** Last week, behind closed doors during the waning hours of Fresh! Closing the door behind him, Darcy felt a sense of apprehension as he entered Harber’s office. It was silly, really; Darcy was an adult, and had been for the entirety of the 21st century. Not only that, but he was quite used to utilizing his powers of persuasion to convince others to see things his way. But nonetheless, David Harber was his boss, and all his life Darcy Markson had had trouble getting along with authority figures. Perhaps all those years of trying to dodge the eyes of parents, teachers, and every once in awhile police officers made him feel like he always had something to hide when dealing with someone who had the least bit of control over his life, even now when the only thing Darcy had to hide was his plan, which he came here precisely to reveal. Silly, really…but Darcy couldn’t shake the feeling. “Ah, the up and coming Darcy Crisis,” the Entertainment Co-Manager exclaimed, glancing toward one of the chairs in front of his desk. Obliging, Darcy took a seat in the chair, quickly surveying the massive amounts of paperwork covering Harber’s desk. But before he crossed the fine line between surveying and outright prying, he looked back up at the man across from him. “Mr. Harber,” Darcy said, nodding. “Come on Darce, call me Pearl,” he replied. “It’s a terrible nickname, I know, but it stuck years ago. Besides, every time someone says ‘Mr. Harber’ I start looking around for my dad. Now what can I do for you?” “Well, you saw what happened to me last week, Pearl,” Darcy said, somehow feeling more at ease now that the two were apparently on a first name basis. “And if you know anything about the kind of man I am, you know I can’t let an incident like that go without a fight.” Pearl looked at Darcy with a frown. “I see where you’re going with this, but there isn’t much I can do. A lot of guys are gunning for Jack Murphy at the moment, and I have to take their aspirations under consideration. No offense, but you had your chance, Darcy. And as far as Aimz goes-” “I know,” Darcy interjected. “I don’t want a throwaway match with her just because she chose to show up for one of mine. And I know she’s been vehemently against facing me one-on-one. But when that match finally happens, and I do stress when, we’ll make a lot more money on pay-per-view, when she wants nothing more than to tear at my throat. As it stands, we’re not ready.” Pearl nodded, but also gave a look to Darcy that suggested he was rather perplexed. “Well, if you’re not here for a match with Aimz or Murphy, what exactly are you looking for then?” “I want them BOTH, Pearl, and I want them in the ring. They make a pretty formidable team…so far their combined efforts took the TransAtlantic title off of our beloved Face-Eater, not to mention screwing over yours truly.” Pearl nodded again. “Formidable indeed.” “Two-on-one, that is,” Darcy said, his eyes now gleaming. “ I’d like to see how their little teamwork game fares when the odds are evened up.” “Two-on-two, you mean,” Pearl responded. He stroked his chin, seemingly pondering over the idea for a few moments. “And just…who would be your second?” “You leave that to me,” Darcy quickly replied. “I’m sure there are plenty of people who would love to increase their stock around here by taking down the champ. Bill it as Aimz and Murphy against Darcy and his ‘mystery partner.’ ” Truth be told, Darcy had put out a few flyers to make up the second half of his duo, but he had received no concrete offers as of yet. One might think that Darcy was crazy to approach management for a duo tag match with no real duo set up, but his choice of partner was a detail to be worked out later as far as he was concerned. His plan, like most others, followed a philosophy that his mother often liked to refer to as “flying by the seat of your pants.” Darcy never really understood the analogy, but it was important to convince Pearl that he knew what he was doing if his plan was to come to fruition. “Well Darcy,” Pearl finally said, apparently done pondering. “I think it can be safely said that your proposed opponents would not be happy with such a match, least of all Aimz…” “…But?” “How did you know there was going to be a ‘but?’ ” Pearl asked. “I have a sixth sense for these things,” Darcy said, with a smile. “I knew it was either ‘but,’ or ‘buttress.’ ” Pearl laughed. “Well they won’t be happy about this, BUT I believe you deserve a chance at some redemption. So if you can find yourself a partner, I’ll make this happen.” Darcy’s eyes lit up like the Fourth of July in the middle of Texarkana. “Thank you sir,” Darcy said, shaking Pearl’s hand. “I promised Amy some ratings, and I’m promising you as well. You won’t regret this…but I know some people who will have a few regrets when all is said and done.” ***************************************************************************************************************** After being lost in thought and flashbacks for some time, Darcy was somewhat startled to find himself no longer walking on the beach, but instead in a far less sandy gondola overlooking the shoreline. The sun had finally disappeared beyond the horizon, leaving the sky significantly darker but now dotted with the light of countless other suns burning millions of miles away. It was a sight that one would think would go unappreciated by people oft referred to as “The Red Raver” and “Tha D-Masta’ ” Yet here they were, and while they might never admit it to each other, both of them knew they were looking at a site with such remarkable beauty that it could be rivaled only by the beauty they saw in each other’s eyes. “I really love it here, Darcy…” Amy said, finally breaking the silence. “Really,” Darcy said, his voice trailing down to make it more of an observation than a question. “You’re telling me you wouldn’t rather be candy-flipping at some rave to the stylings of DJ Queef?” “DJ Queef?! Is that some sort of joke, or is that the shit you listen to when I’m not around? Anyway…no, I really wouldn’t. A girl has to slow down sometime…” The two again returned to overlooking the shoreline, with Darcy’s arms draped around her from behind. Anyone who knew just how nasty and brutish the redhead could be would probably be shocked to see her draped in the arms of Darcy Markson…Darcy included. But for some reason, she was prone to treating him differently. Business was business, and both of them were well aware of the fact that once they entered an arena to lace up the boots, anything could and probably would happen. But outside the arena, outside the spotlight…Darcy couldn’t say for sure, but it seemed as if the notorious loner Amy Campbell was finally, after all these years, beginning to let her guard down around him. He hoped the stunt that he had planned for the very near future wouldn’t compromise that. But there was no turning back. Again, it was simply a matter of timing that had set all of this in motion – Amy had asked to see the Darcy Crisis of old. The fans were clamoring to see it as well. And on the Darcy Crisis coming out party, a challenge was made to his manhood…a challenge that could not go overlooked. Almost on cue, an instrumental version of “Jumpin’ Jack Flash” by the Rolling Stones began to emanate from Darcy’s right pocket. Releasing his grip on Amy, he reached into his pocket to see who was disturbing him. “Couldn’t leave the phone at home, Mister Romantic?” Amy asked with more than a hint of sarcasm in her voice.” “Sorry babe, but I gotta take this,” Darcy answered. Amy once again turned to the view as Darcy exited the gondola. He didn’t recognize the number, but he was familiar with the East Coast area code. Either Johnny had drunk himself so stupid that he woke up on the other side of the country…or one of the flyers Darcy put out was about to be answered. He answered the call. “Hello?” It definitely wasn’t Johnny. The voice on the other end began listing some potential concerns, which Darcy defused as best he could. But just as he had completed the first half of his plan by convincing Pearl to make the match, the next two words Darcy heard assured him that the second half was now complete as well. “I’m in.”
