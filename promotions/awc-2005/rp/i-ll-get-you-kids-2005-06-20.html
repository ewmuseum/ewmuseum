I'll get you kids!!!

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After that spelling test, that was obviously brutal on Kris’s self esteem, I had to leave to go get ready for my match the day after on void, I kind of see this match as a RE- ASS KICKING instead of a RE-MATCH. so I entrusted my teacher, Kris Krimson, with my Children while I go get ready. ---- I clambered into my Black BMW X-5, and waved to my kids who were stood at the door with Kris, who had a very disgruntled look on his face, obviously he did not really want to look after my kids after they showed him up. I drove away down the street, heading for my house to go train. --- Kris walked the kids into the house and sat them down on the light blue couch in front of his 10 inch portable TV. Kris : So kid…….how is being a kid working out for you two? Joe : Shut up you dick, and get me a drink. Joe kicked Kris in the shins and grabbed him by the ears. He kneed him in the head, while Kaitlin crawled round the back and gnawed at his ankles. Kris : Hey, I’m the bestest wrestler in here. Joe : That’s what you think. We got trained by our Dad. Kris : Well I trained your dad. Joe kicked Kris again but this time in the nuts. Joe and Kaitlin pulled the now lay down Kris into the back room where his own private wrestling ring is situated. They threw him into the ring and started stomping on his head. --- I pulled up to my house and grabbed the black bag out if the trunk. I pushed open the pine door and put in the alarm code to stop the beeping. I headed off up the stairs and placed my bag down in front of the reinforced glass gym door. I rummaged around in the side pocket and pulled out a set of headphones and a white plastic swipe card. I placed the swipe card in the black “swiper 5000” and the door opened up. I unraveled the Panasonic headphones and placed them into the sound socket on the weights machine. I turned the LCD screen on, that was on the ceiling, of which I was facing. --- Back in Kris’s house, They were battling it out in the ring. By sons first encounter of the Wrestling kind, and Kris’s first encounter of An 8 year old that can keep him down for the count. Kaitlin chewed on Kris’s knee caps and brought him to the floor. Joe grabbed Kris and pulled him over to the turnbuckle, Joe climbed up and pulled off a perfectly executed 450 splash to the abdomen of Kris. Kris : WOMBAT JUICE!!!! Joe looked at Kris with a blank stair as he tried to work out why he had shouted that. Kris climbed to his feet, shook my kids hands and took them into the living room. Kris : ok, ok, I’ll get you the drink. Joe: ok but no wombat juice. --- Sake : 302, 303, 304, 305, 306. After 306 bench presses I decided to give up seen as though I had worked on the punch bag the treadmill and shoulder press. I went into my black bag and pulled out my AWC towel with my name sewn into it. I wiped my face, unplugged my headphones and walked out. I locked the door and Sprinted down stairs. Just before I left to go to Kris’s house I grabbed the bottle of Lynx Touch from next to the door and sprayed myself. --- I pulled up to the house, climbed out and walked up the pathway. I rang the door bell that made the surprising sound of a didgeridoo. Kris pulled down the door handle and opened the door. His hair was all out of place and his shirt was ripped. Sake : What the hell have you been doing Kris. Kris : your kiddies have been prowving that they is the bestest wrestlers, and they won. Sake : hahahaha. You got beat by and 8 year old and an 8 month old. Kris : They are a tough couple of S.O.B’S Sake : Come on kids I’ll take you put for ice cream. My kids came running past Kris, both kicking him in the shins, as they walked past. Kris : I’ll get you one day kids, one of these days, WOMBAT JUICE
