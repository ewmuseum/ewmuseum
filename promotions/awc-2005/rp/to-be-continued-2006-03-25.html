To Be Continued...

25 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/06 Luis Ferrara pushed through the crowd at the rave, his nose turned up at the fetid smell of unwashed dancers all around him. He was wearing a dark leather jacket and blue jeans, very unassuming clothing for the usually pristine Venezuelan. His slick hair hung down to his shoulders, seeming to bob with the devilbeats hammered out over the PA system. His brow was furrowed in annoyance as he knocked aside another raver, snapping at the person in Spanish. He drew his jacket tighter around himself as he soldiered on. The muscular Nigerian trailed only a step behind his manager, cutting a wider swath than the smaller Latino could make. He was dressed in dark clothing as well, his loose black t-shirt and dark blue jeans seeming to almost blend into the deep brown hue of his skin. He didn't hesitate to snap his palm into the chests of those who got too close, giving them a feral snarl to chase the drug-addled partygoers away when they persisted. His upper lip appeared to be taped in a curled position and his nose flared incessantly. Scenes like this sickened Anton. Dens of the utmost debauchery and idiocy, stripping seemingly normal people of all dignity in the face of this… shit music. Waving around glowsticks, chomping down on pacifiers, everything about this place was infuriating to the Lion. He finally managed to purse his lips, giving a skinny raver a particularly hard shove before ducking into a short room after the Venezuelan. He was more than glad to be out of that frenzy. The bass thumped behind Anton as he closed the door to this room. He let his gaze swivel over the place and he sighed. It was more agreeable than outside, but here was still not his element at all. A few people looked up at him, but they barely paid attention to him. Their eyes were downcast on their mirrors or their bowls, readying a myriad of illegal substances. It certainly didn't look like they were doing clinical research on the substances. Still, Anton found himself a seat and relaxed a bit. Luis took a seat next to a thin, shaking man who was preparing a line. Even though the man seemed to be jittering all over, his hand was surprisingly rigid when sliding the coke into lines. Anton snorted a bit as Luis rubbed his hands together in anticipation for the cocane. As Luis slid out a straw, the man next to him glanced up and sneered. Luis jabbed his thumb over at Anton and the shivering man let his gaze fall upon the hulking Nigerian. Anton glanced away, folding his arms over his chest. Of course, he was used to being the threat in a given party and he had accepted the role quite a while ago. He tried to ignore whatever Luis might be saying, but there was not much else to focus on but the drugs being meted out. Upon his gaze, the users drew their stashes towards themselves as if they were petty misers. Anton scowled, resolving not to do much more than shake his head. After all, they were not worth much more of his time. He felt a light jab against his ribs and he swiveled his head to the side rapidly, his eyes falling upon the person he had inadvertently sat next to. She was a slender woman with tanned skin, black hair falling down to her shoulders and over a tight red top. She gave him a weak smile and tugged her tartan skirt down a bit to better cover her luscious legs. Anton arched an eyebrow at her, his eyes concentrated on her face and nothing else. Her smile became more disconcerted as she noticed that he was actually concentrating on her and not her looks as so many other guys did. ''Uh, if you wanted to toke up or something,'' she said in English, her voice slightly timid. Her eyes danced up and down Anton's muscles before she tried to meet his gaze again. She failed, using the action of brushing her hair as a premise to break eye contact. ''I've got a good bit of hash on me that I don't mind sharing.'' Her smile broadened a bit and she glanced up at him, meeting his eyes for a split second. ''I mean, I'm not trying to be pushy, but…'' ''No, thank you,'' Anton responded. ''I don't smoke.'' ''Clean boy, huh?'' she asked with a slight giggle. ''I used to be squeaky clean, too, but I couldn't resist. It's just too good to be true, isn't it?'' Anton shrugged his shoulders dismissively as she delved into her purse, slipping out the bag of reefer. She gave him a tilted look and furrowed her brow. ''You're not into talking, are you? Strong, silent type?'' Anton looked at her, slightly confused. ''Type?'' he asked. ''I suppose I am strong, but no type.'' She gave him a condescending look, but Anton's confusion didn't waver. ''Well, it's okay,'' she said, disregarding her words. ''I know the guys here aren't all that keen on sharing the wealth, but hopefully you don't get that from me. My name is Amber.'' ''Anton.'' Amber nodded and began to pack her bowl. Anton focused on the green she thumbed into the ceramic valley and Amber noticed, giving a short sigh. ''You're absolutely sure you don't want to hit this?'' Anton nodded wordlessly and Amber fished a lighter out of her pocket. ''Suit yourself.'' Amber lit the weed and inhaled deeply, drawing the thick smoke into her lungs. Anton watched her intently, his eyes alighting upon the burning red. Suddenly, Amber pulled the bowl away and coughed a bit, the smoke spilling away from her lips gently. ''Who's your friend?'' Amber asked after a bit, punctuating her question by blowing smoke out of her mouth. She jabbed her thumb at Luis and Anton looked up, seeing the Venezuelan having a quiet argument with the man who had been shuddering before. ''Do not worry about him,'' Anton said, but Amber's eyes were focused on Luis. Anton glanced away, seeing where her interests lie. He stood after a bit and slipped out of the back room, unnoticed by Luis. He was tired of sitting in that place; the smoke was getting to him. Luis didn't look like he needed him there, either, so he was content to leave him alone.
