A Moment With the Fists

23 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-8-05 Day in and day out is a struggle. The constant pressures and temptations surround me, tell me to go against the grain. When I’m feeling blue, the urges are even higher. I start thinking of thinks that I hope to forget, and I choke to death on a sun that never sets. It’s because of the life I chose, the right path, that I’m stricken with this. My brother and I are known through out the city and we are targets. We rarely leave the Eagle’s Nest because of this, because of the fear. Sure, we speak, look, and act strong, but really we’re scared to death. We know that there is a constant target on our heads, that every gang out there wants us dead. No one dares enter the nest to get us, though…they know what kind of trouble there is. You think I’m kidding? Let me tell you a story. The other day, Liam and I were driving to pick up some wood to do some construction on the house. The place was tattered and fucked up beyond belief after weeks of not paying one damn bit of attention to it, and it needed a little woodwork. Once we got to the Home Depot, we noticed some wet back Mexicans eyeing us down…I recognized their tattoos, they were the Los Stupid Shitheads, or something. Anywho, we leave the depot and start driving away when, low and behold, there they are! And it’s not like it’s some skinny brown skinned fuck and his greasey hair cousin…oh no, no, no…it’s a whole car full of those fuckers, armed to the teeth. I’m sure they were just WAITING for us to pull over so they could blast us. Liam got a little worried…”Shit, Tim,” he said, “Those burritos are following us…we’re dead men, Tim.” I chuckled for a second, just to calm Liam. I was nervous as well, but I knew what I had to do. I didn’t say a word and kept on driving, and the SECOND we hit the first of the Nest houses, those fucking slime bags stopped, turned around, and headed back to their “hood”. That’s not just a story of how Liam and I aren’t always super tough guys, though…that’s another example of how a community under the White Lord gains his strength and strikes fear into all the other dregs. Still, I’m left questioning things… Why would the White Lord let all those other things live? I mean, if we really were the superior people, wouldn’t we be the ones left standing? Why is it that they constantly are achieving great things, from entertainment to politics to local success? These troubling questions struck me, and I needed guidance…I needed help. Like my father always said, if you need help, ask the priest. So that’s what I did. The Nest’s church was a large Church because, on Sunday, EVERYONE who was able went. For all our work for the community, Liam and I had a seat right up in front, and Father Daniels knew us well. “Tim!” he yelled when I walked in there to seek his guidance, his voice exuberant and excited. He was not really huge, but his belly showed from under his robe. He had a well trimmed gray beard that matched his short gray hair. You could tell that, at a younger age, he was a handsome man, more than likely able to capture any young lady that he would like. Ultimately, though, he believed in the teaching of the Church in the Nest and became one of the cloth, forgoing any sexual intercourse with any lady. Regardless, he was still popular with the elderly femmes of the neighborhood, who would constantly cook him casseroles and the like. I walked up to the Father and asked how it was going and how things were. “Oh, Tim, things are excellent, just excellent!” he said, showing that beautiful smile he seemed to posses. “Did you appreciate the sermon yesterday?” I said yes, because Father Daniels always does a good sermon. Yesterday it was about how the White Lord doesn’t just give out the power…you have to earn it through hard work. The Father also said that we should be thrilled to have this privilege to have this power… -----([([([END FEED])])])-----
