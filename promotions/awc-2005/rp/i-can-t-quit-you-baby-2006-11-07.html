I can't quit you, baby

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.07.06 I was so close... So unbelievably close... The beleaguered man slammed his fist against the dashboard of his '92 Dodge Dart as he pondered his most recent setback. He was right there! Right there for the goddamn taking! Indeed, for the first time in quite awhile, he'd been able to track Markson to a location where he knew beyond a shadow of a doubt that he was alone. The trip to Las Vegas had proved fruitful – Darcy had arrived three days prior to his scheduled competition, this time with his preferred choice of whore, whom he'd recently come to know that Markson planned to make his wife. A pity the harlot will be widowed before she gets the chance to wed... It should have happened that very night...and in fact, she'd nearly handed him the opportunity on a silver platter. The jaded stalker was disappointed to track down the hotel that Markson planned to spend the evening, only to see him enter the premises hand in hand with the girl. Another body to have to deal with complicated matters a great deal, but the problem was suddenly alleviated when he spotted her leaving the hotel in the wee hours of the morning. Like most of the hotels in Vegas usually did to its patrons, this one appeared to cost the two a hefty sum, and any late-night accommodation she could possibly need was likely already provided for. Whatever had brought her outside would probably keep her away long enough for his plan, while slightly improvised, to be implicated. But it was not to be on this exchange...and now, thanks to that impudent slut, maybe not ever. When she'd evidently been missing too long for Markson's tastes, he burst out of the hotel himself in a desperate search. His vengeful predator dutifully followed at a safe distance, solving the mystery of the girl's disappearance at the same time as her devotee. It looked as if someone or ones, perhaps former clients of hers, had roughed her up a bit. He couldn't tell if there were witnesses, but those who were onlooking joined the man in seeing Markson lift her from the scene and return to the hotel, where they remained for the night. ”GODDAMMIT! he exclaimed, his voice thundering as he again slammed his fist in frustration. As quickly as God had provided him with the opportunity he'd been salivating, He'd snatched it away out of spite, in such a quick and forceful manner that seemed to suggest no such similar possibilities could arise. Now that the girl had been assaulted, both she and Markson would definitely be on the lookout for further trouble. The ultimate trump card he'd held up his sleeve for so long – the element of surprise – had been cruelly taken away in the form of street thuggery. He couldn't help but let the anger come. When he became angry, the ever-lasting pain in his shoulder burned as fiercely as his temper. This made him angrier still, causing more pain, continuing on and on in the most tragic of cycles. But the searing pain helped him to remember his goal. And the goal of apprehending Markson and forcing him to answer for his transgressions burned far deeper in the depths of his soul than pain or anger could possibly hope to reach. It seemed almost too good to be true that he would capture him in the infamous Sin City, and now he knew his gut feeling to be true. Like it or not, Darcy Markson would pay for his sins, whether it be in the City of Sin itself or any other city he dared travel to. Because of that cursed man and what he'd inflicted upon him, he had a wound that could never heal. Because of that abominable excuse for a human being, he'd spent the whole of the third millenium watching his life crumble away. He'd had so much time to contemplate the accelerated entropy around him, but it had only recently become so clear. It all pointed back to that one terrible day, where the course of his life changed forever. Two lives went on distinctly different directions – his straight into the toilet, and the man responsible for that...became a success. A star, even. All because Darcy Fucking Markson had to be a hero. He thought of nothing else since. If God refused to deliver justice where justice was due...then justice would have to be extracted the old-fashioned way. Closing his eyes, the man in search of vindication pictured it ending the old-fashioned way, which he hoped upon hope that it would – with Markson's body dangling from the end of a noose. He suddenly found the shoulder pain subsiding on par with his anger, for now the matter was in perspective. It was merely just another setback – another fluke case that allowed the wretched demon to slip from his grasp. For the time being, his life would be spared once more... But sooner or later, boy...you're going to run out of miracles. *** ~All of our lending services representatives are currently busy. Please continue to hold for the next available officer...~ “BlahblahblahBLAHBLAHBLAHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! I just want to cancel my Visa so the jerkoffs who stole it can't go on a shopping spree or some kind of luxury cruise for tiny criminal minds!” Amy “Markson” Campbell hung up the phone after venting her frustrations. His timing impeccable, Darcy just so happened to stumble into the room to find his embattered fiancée lied out in the bed, who elicited an audible sigh the moment he entered the room. Whether or not the two events were related was up for debate, but he found himself cut off as he opened his mouth to inquire. ''Johnny sent a post card,'' she said, flinging an envelope that was sent whizzing past his ear before crashing to a heap on the dresser. ''Read it. Track him on mapquest... do something for a half hour or more so I can nap before I whine about my leg?'' “That so?” Darcy inquired instead, turning to peruse his delivery. “I don't remember Johnny saying anything about-” Until he did. Johnny had in fact made Darcy privy to his recent travel whereabouts, and the postcard was the ultimate reminder. Flipping the card over, Darcy found his suspicions confirmed. Several rows of warmly-hued flowers stretched on into the distance, where they stopped upon running into a row of evergreen foliage stretching up toward a pale blue sky. Drawing the eye was a bell-shaped tower to which the blades of a windmill were firmly attached, frozen in this snapshot of time. Slightly off-center, halfway up the length of the highest blade was the name of where the scene could be found, its letters deliberately spread apart from each other for some sort of aesthetic value: H O L L A N D. “Johnny sent me a postcard from Amsterdam!” Darcy exclaimed aloud, with all the giddiness of a 15-year-old. “And that can only mean one thing...” He flipped the card, where the word H O L L A N D was again repeated in the same font and aesthetic spacing as before. Underneath it read both the English and Dutch versions of the phrase “Greetings from Holland!”, and underneath that read the message Johnny had left for him: EVERYDAY I'M HUSTLIN' EVERYDAY I'M HUSTLIN' EVERYDAY I'M HUSTLIN' EVERYDAY I'M HUSTLIN' Get on w/ your blackass, Johnny HAWK The message, of course, were only an inane tribute to the Rick Ross single the two couldn't get enough of. It was inconsequential – the real value of the postage lied in the corner with the ₤0.85 stamp. Specifically, underneath. This wasn't Johnny's first trip to the Netherlands, and each time he went he made sure to send a gift of Holland's finest hidden stealthily under the postage currency. ''If it's a tab of acid, I'm gonna have your balls under my feet SO fast...'' Darcy shook his head. “Negative. It's just hash. A little dry, I have to say, this isn't up to his normal standards. But impressive nonetheless.” Darcy suddenly found himself unexpectedly excited. He'd given up his journeys with Puff the Magic Dragon for some time now, and hadn't missed it in the slightest. But a gift along the lines of the Johnny Hawk tradition brought back memories of old, where they would gladly throw an evening to the wayside stoned off their gourds watching Sifl & Olly. ''... Seriously?'' “Come on, Amy...quit looking at me like I'm Ellis Nash,” Darcy said, throwing his hands out to his sides as if physically trying to appeal his good nature. “It's a gift from Johnny...I can't turn it down.” ''Readiness to marry is sometimes a sign of maturity. I guess, in your case, it's an excuse to turn fourteen again? Seriously, Darcy... you never noticed the whole 'Amy's a complete addict just getting on the mend' thing? You're funnelling beer to an alcoholic.'' Darcy frowned, now looking intently at the scene on the front of the card again. “Well, since I'm already committed to doing the most grown-up thing I can think of...don't you think I should knock out all the immaturity I've got left?” he inquired, peaking just above the card's edge like a pathetic puppy. A half-smile escaped Amy's lips at hearing his question. It was still difficult to say for sure, but he had a feeling he just might get his way... ''Just don't take up the big TV watching some stupid movie, and do it outside - NOT in the car, not anywhere I might be within the next day. You can thank me for letting you bypass my usual complaints by sending you to smoke up when we're picking out bridesmaid's dresses.'' BAM! The battle cry of the esteemed Emeril Lagasse bellowed through his head – another victory for the home team. If Darcy knew what was good for him, he wouldn't utilize this newly-won opportunity too terribly often; he would definitely need to be in tip-top mental condition to maneuver deftly with his bride-to-be, a match far more harrowing and subject to physical torture than his upcoming contest with her in the ring. He turned for the door, but just before he could lift his leg to make the first step, he turned back to look at her once more. “Last chance to join me...” he told her. “Those internet videos get about 85 times more hilarious.” ''The funniest thing on the internet right now are elitist message board jerkoffs who spend half of their time bragging about what you're about to do... which I stopped finding impressive around the age of seventeen.'' Apparently not interpreting that as an answer to his question, Darcy simply stood there, offering no response other than to widen his eyes, enticing her to finish her point. And apparently disappointed that she needed to, Amy audibly sighed once more. ''That means 'No, thanks', in case you were serious.'' Seeming disappointed himself in losing out on a partner for the affair, Darcy did his best to hide it by shrugging his shoulders. “Suit yourself,” he said, trying to sound indifferent. “Get some rest and get that leg healed up...I want it good and healthy to go so I can put it back in a sharpshooter.” Amy blew off his taunt with a simple yawn, turning away from him to catch up to Reaper in the sleep department. Smirking, Darcy flipped off the light switch and exited the room. He wasn't sure how much longer Amy would allow him to continue getting away with getting his way before putting the hammer down. Nor was he sure he could bring himself to the level of ruthless efficiency he typically reserved for defending his beloved Frontier Title against the girl who looked so unbelievably adorable curled up against his longtime feline friend. But truth be told, there was only question to which Darcy sought an answer: “Now whaaaaaaar's me lighter?” *** TWENTY MINUTES LATER... “The internet...” A snapshot of environmentalist Al Gore, former Vice President of the United States and alleged creator of the internet, scrolls across the screen. “Er-it, uh, a, it's-uh, it's th-” In digital lettering, the universal all-purpose greeting of the world wide web flashes brightly: LOL “It's not something that you just DUMP something on...” Cue a crudely drawn man carefully investigating a series of confusing directions... “Th-th-th-th-” The view pans down, ominously revealing an enormous, concentrated network of wires running into and out of some mysterious, formidable-looking towers. “It's NOT a big truck -” The record needle abruptly lifts. “It's a series of TUBES!” Giggling like a moron, Darcy fell over with glee as he watched the DJ Ted Stevens video on YouTube for the ninth straight time. Stevens, the stuttering U.S. Senator heading the government task force charged with the daunting task of regulating the internet, never failed to amuse Darcy with his hilariously minimal knowledge of the internet. Especially the part where he complained that his staff sent him an internet at 10:00 on Friday morning, and he'd only received that internet yesterday. Poor guy. Unfortunately, Darcy decided he couldn't sit through the rest of the video another time to make it to that high moment in unintentional comedy, and clicked the X to send the video to an instantaneous grave. Without even thinking, as if it were an unconscious reflex, Darcy opened a new browser window as his newly acquired copy of Massive Attack's Protection play hummed silently in the background. As he stared at Firefox's default homepage, which might really be better served to be called Google II, he cursed the hashish's tendency to completely eradicate his short-term memory. Pondering what had motivated him to open the browser, Darcy's mind soon drifted back to what Amy told him about Internet and Hilarity: “'The funniest thing on the internet right now are elitist message board jerkoffs who spend half of their time bragging about what you're about to do... ” She certainly had a point. But Darcy's mind now pondered in another new direction, approximately the 47th time in the past ten minutes – just how deeply did it run? He knew, of course, of the infamous, outspoken group in the AWC message boards, where Darcy's greatness was proclaimed on at least a semi-daily basis. But were there others? Was the internet really abuzz about his doings? Once again enthralled with his own celebrity, Darcy gave his mystery new window a purpose, typing his own name into the search bar. His heart sank on finding that only a scant three pages worth of hits came back in response, hoping it would have been at least twice that. But the pages he found were well worth the search. Most of the hits that came up were stories of his own life, containing many private thoughts he'd shared with no one but himself, written in novel form and mysteriously posted for the entire world to see. Wisely, he chose not to question their existence. But in between those lied the real treasure – transcripts of the television shows that had aired half a decade ago, all for the old promotions of which he'd been a part. And from there lied even more goodness. When he had his fill of reliving all the bad gimmicks, including the awful “One Man Degeneration” shtick he'd utilized in the old DangeR Wrestling Federation, he moved on to the character bios: Aaron Marty. Jon Zombie. The Green Weasel. Darcy couldn't help but laugh at seeing all those names again, names that had unfortunately vanished from the wrestling world almost as quickly as his own fall from grace. Then again, the world probably deserved to be spared from a contemporary version of Eddie Osbourne's younger brother attempting to pass himself off as an extremely wealthy, ass-kicking flesh-eater. Zombie, Marty and Weasel, as entertaining as they might have been, probably would have been blown out of the water by the Red Rocks, Johnny Lexicons, and Pierce Lavelles of the world... Lavelle. Try as he might, he'd found a way to slip back into Darcy's mind once more. He kept coming back to his stand-off with the former TransAtlantic champion the week before. It might have been brought on purely by accident, as well as made worse by senseless argument. In truth, Darcy felt guilty for originally agitating and further provoking the clearly disturbed individual. Yet at the same time, he found himself wishing that their encounter had really led to their coming to blows. He wish he knew why. And suddenly, in the form of a name proudly adorning the top of the old Pier Six Wrestling roster that Darcy hadn't even thought about in years, he had his answer. *** Flashback – April 22, 2001. Some of you might remember this as the infamous date of Pier Six Wrestling’s final broadcast of WrestleCade, their biggest pay-per-view of the year. Actually, I take that back – it seems highly unlikely that any of you remember that day to be anything special, unless you at one time lived in or around the California area, and watch far more professional wrestling than is normally recommended by a qualified doctor. Darcy Markson would definitely qualify as one of those individuals. But then, he had a legitimate excuse – he was booked on the show. And what a show it turned out to be for the ol’ Darcinator…that night he competed in career-best (and probably record-best, although it can’t be proven and this author is far too busy to look it up) five matches, winning them all. His first three matches were the culmination of a tag team tournament, the result being Darcy’s capturing his second PSW Tag Team Championship. He prevailed in the evening’s main event, securing his first (and to this date, only) professional wrestling World Championship, going through the best of Pier Six’s roster to get it. And the night ended with Darcy’s notorious tumble from atop three cages, permanently injuring his back and sending his career and life into a tailspin. With so much going on for Darcy Crisis that night, it would be easy for fans of his to overlook something that transpired in between. Even without counting his own efforts, there were so many sanctioned contests on the WrestleCade extravaganza that a match or two could get lost in the shuffle. In this case, it was a match sandwiched in the middle of the card – match number four for Darcy. But until he won the main event some thirty minutes later, it was easily the most important match of his career…and it had everything to do with his opponent. Coming into WrestleCade, Darcy Crisis was considered one of the fastest rising stars on the Pier Six scene. He was already a multi-time champion, and had in fact never lost a title match to that date. Early in his PSW stint, he was forced to abdicate the North American championship due to injury; he was stripped of the Tag Team gold he’d earned due to a lull in the tag division, leading to the fruition of the tournament at WrestleCade. And the Extreme Violence Championship, which he’d earned by defeating the Gutter Rat in a Buried Alive match, still rested proudly on his shoulder as he took down challenger after challenger. But as the old saying goes, you’re only as good as the guys you beat. And to be honest, the guys Darcy had defeated were nothing to write home about. Guys like Fazel, the goofy-ass clown who looked like a cross between Bozo and Shaggy 2 Dope. Guys like Sion Barzahd, who competed briefly within the Pier Six ranks until it was discovered that he was in fact a fictional character, blatantly ripped off from the PlayStation 2 launch title The Bouncer, which was as terrible of a video game as the wrestler who’d evidently leapt out of it to job around PSW. Guys like “The American Zero” Steven Wright, whose name alone spoke volumes of his “greatness.” Guys like Saint, who seem to bounce around from promotion to promotion without ever really attaining much success. Even the Gutter Rat, who had posed the biggest threat to Darcy in his PSW tenure, wasn’t much to write home about – his arrival on the scene led to his immediately gunning for the Darcinator, and he disappeared as quickly as he was dispatched. Indeed, it seemed Darcy had a knack for always getting the upper hand…but in reality, it meant nothing. Kind of like how certain blue-chip talents are billed as “monsters,” when in truth their “remarkable” winning streaks were padded by world-class jobbers who no one had ever heard of, and no one ever would. Enter Adam Trimm. Trimm was a thirty-something year-old talent hailing from somewhere in Canada. He’d bounced around the confines of various federations for a decade or so before achieving prominence in Pier Six, where he was a part of the company since its inception. A founding member of the original version of the Elite, who at that time was a fan-favorite faction (due at least in some small part to Eddie Osbourne’s penchant for being a huge dick not yet being public knowledge), Adam Trimm enjoyed a run at the top from Pier Six Wrestling’s first day in the books, while Darcy Crisis, still an unknown, worked his way up from mid-card curtain jerker in a mad effort to wrest some of the spotlight for himself. Their paths finally crossed at WrestleCade, in the aforementioned “lost” fourth match of the night for Darcy. His Extreme Violence title was on the line to give the bout an extra jolt of clout, but the match was about far more than that. It was Old vs. New. Legend vs. Hotshot. A man fervent on keeping his status as one of PSW’s top dogs vs. a man desperate to achieve equal ground atop the mountain. The encounter culminated with Trimm tapping out to the Darcinator, securing the second third of what would become Darcy’s Triple Crown Championship run, where he would finish the night simultaneously holding all three of PSW’s most prestigious goldwares. But the victory meant far more than a mere title retention, of which Darcy would need an extra hand if he hoped to count them off by the finger. This victory, over arguably the best Pier Six had to offer (the only other real candidate was Aimz, who Darcy would have to wait over half a decade to get a crack at), made the statement he was hoping to make and have count for quite some time. It proved he belonged. The match inevitably paled in comparison to the 3Cage affair later in the night, where Darcy prevailed to obtain the icing on the cake to close out far and away the most bizarre, intense pay-per-view he’d had the opportunity to be a part of, both before and since. But his newly acquired PSW Championship only placed the visual proof on what he’d cemented in defeating Trimm. The night had begun with PSW being owned by the likes of Osbourne, Trimm, Aimz and all the rest who had done so much to hallow its halls. By night’s end…PSW belonged to Darcy. And only a resurgent Elite assault, a bad back and a torrid love affair could combine to stop him… *** It was just then that Darcy, in his highly suggestible and inebriated state, put what he thought to be two and two together. That spliff wasn’t left here accidentally…it was destiny. It all makes sense now. I was meant to see this. Pierce Lavelle is my new Adam Trimm. “Destiny” seemed a little far-fetched, to say the least. But admittedly, the similarities to what Trimm represented in PSW to Lavelle in AWC seemed to correspond almost perfectly. Like Trimm, Pierce Lavelle's stay with the company far outlasted the majority of the current roster. Like Lavelle, Adam Trimm had a run of dominance atop his respective promotion so vociferous, and so lengthy, that Darcy couldn't hope to hold a candle to them. Both had nancy-boy haircuts – Pierce with his gel-tastic massacre, and Adam sporting a lovely high-end ponytail normally sighted on female softball players. Above all else, both had risen to a plateau that despite all the victories, despite all the titles and accolades, and despite his overwhelming desire, Darcy constantly struggled to attain. They had broken through the glass ceiling of greatness; Darcy, try as he might, was still only on the rise. The sidebar on the AWC website said it all: star of 2007. He'd been with the company since the tail end of 2005. All throughout '06 – spring, summer, the whole kaboodle – many were quick to project Darcy as the breakout talent of the year. Now here he was, nearing the tail end of 2006 and fast approaching his one year anniversary of joining the Club...and now his stardom wasn't projected to begin until 2007. Truth be told, Darcy had been bogged down with a plethora of separate affairs. From losing his post with the Tomorrow Institute to dealing with the return, kidnapping and rescue of Jane to surviving relationship purgatory with Amy, he hadn't had a whole lot of time to reflect on the value of his stock with the Club. Ironically enough, even though Darcy was facing perhaps the most trying stretch of his AWC tenure in the gauntlet of title defenses, in comparison to the aforementioned difficulties it was a welcome chance to breathe. For the first time in quite a long while, Darcy could relax in the comfort of his own home without wondering if Amy was waiting around the corner for a well-placed shot to his nether regions, or whether some psycho or his henchman was looking to put a bullet between his eyes. With that chance to breathe came the opportunity to contemplate his present occupational situation, as well as plan the next big career move. Marijuana, as some of you probably already know, has a peculiar side effect in that it can considerably alter the mind's ability to correctly perceive the significance of a particular thing or event. If you haven't spent twenty minutes shivering in your bed, completely overpowered by the fantastical goodness of chocolate chip cookies and the unstoppable lifelong addiction you know now awaits you...you probably haven't done a whole lot of surfing at the Maui Wowie Seaside Resort. Darcy faced a similar scenario here. His confrontation with Lavelle on last week's Fresh!, at least on the surface, appeared to be merely an unfortunate misunderstanding coupled with a dose of bad timing. Lavelle had been deliriously stumbling through the halls, happened to run into Darcy, they both managed to piss each other off with a few choice words, and while they very nearly came to blows, cooler heads eventually prevailed. And with his hands full with the gauntlet, which was now set to throw Amy Campbell at him once more, not to mention Jack Murphy waiting for him in the wings in the culminating Frontier-Relentless unification...it seemed both logical and prudent for Darcy to leave the Lavelle incident as just that – an unfortunate incident, now closed, that bared neither the need to re-open the matter nor the need to press it any further. But as we've concluded, in Darcy's highly intense, suggestible mental state that he owed to staving off the green stuff since Bill Clinton was still rousing feathers as America's 42nd commander-in-chief, his investigation into the stand-off with the “legendary” Pierce Lavelle ran far deeper. He couldn't say for sure whether he felt it as strongly as he did now, with a chance to look back after assessing and analyzing, than he did as the event itself unfolded in front of his eyes...but he knew precisely what he felt. Quite literally, when Lavelle stood up to him, and it looked as if they were going to drop the gloves right then and there, it was as if an electrical storm had unfolded all around them, the bolts passing around and through them, coursing through their very veins. While Darcy had over-exaggerated his reaction to learning that Pierce Lavelle knew who he was, he'd certainly meant what he said. Their meetings up to that point had been few and far between; certainly to that point Darcy would hardly have sufficed to put up a blip on Pierce's radar. But now, not only was Darcy evidently on screen after all, he suddenly found himself front and center, targeted for Piercination. And indeed, in being centered within the sights of one of AWC's alpha dogs, it was as if a time machine had swooped out of the sky to take Darcy all the way back to WrestleCade, 2001: Old vs. New. Legend vs. Hotshot. A thin smile escaped the pursed lips of the Darcinator as he closed his monitor down, shutting the computer off for the evening. It was getting late, and Darcy could feel himself starting to come down from either the best or worst idea he'd ever had. The time had come to pack it in and find some sleep, lying next to the biggest threat to his keeping the Frontier Championship to date. Again, it was difficult to predict what would unfold in the very near future – she might have gotten the better of him in their brutal encounter at Untouchable, but the two knew each other so well, so throughly...the outcome was a toss-up. Likely, the match would come down to whoever made the one fatal error – and neither planned on doing so. But as he trudged up the stairs toward that nefarious foe, who would likely greet his presence with unavoidable cuddling, Darcy knew that there was one prediction he felt a little safer if he'd had to bet the farm on it... Destiny isn't done with Darcy Crisis and Pierce Lavelle.
