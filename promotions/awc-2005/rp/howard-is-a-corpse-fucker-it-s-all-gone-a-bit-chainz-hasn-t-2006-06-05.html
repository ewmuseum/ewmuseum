Howard is a corpse fucker. (It's all gone a bit chainz hasn't it?)

5 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th June 2006 Hindsight is one of those thought processes that we all use nearly everyday whether you realise it or not. We always get in to situations where we have to solve a problem or have to make a decision. We always have to chance to dabble with hindsight to use as a learning curve to improve your self for future occurrences. We can discuss what we should have said that would have been much wittier or would have made something run much more smoothly. But then there are some problems or situations where there is no going back and there is no changing anything. It is a spontaneous off the mark decision where no amount of hindsight will make a lick of difference, no matter what way you choose to solve this problem. It doesn’t matter because all paths are blocked with more disaster and more anguish leading to an uncontrollable web of spontaneous solutions. It’s fear that drives us to make these decisions, you can put someone in any situation where they don’t understand what to do and panic kicks in, but its those problems where you know if you don’t do something then and there terrible repercussions will be waiting. It goes without saying that Red Rock isn’t mentally mature, he can barely look after himself, he finds it difficult to keep his living space clean and he barely knows how to make toast. It’s no wonder he lives in fear most of his life because he fails to keep up to date with his bills and standing orders. Every knock on the door sends another shock down the spine of Red Rock and it’s for these reasons that makes Red Rock the last person in the world you want making any of these spontaneous decisions. Unfortunately for Red Rock he is soon going to find himself having to perform under those circumstances. Last time we left our Anti Hero he finally discovered that his estranged flat mate who claimed to be a computer programmer was in fact a body fucking rape faced necrophiliac weirdo. So with D’avid at his side they hastily made a swift exit, which brings us straight up to today. We now find our selves in the dank and dark hallway just outside Red Rock’s flat. The atmosphere today is just that little bit darker and just that little bit danker because that feeling of fear is scraping the canals of Red Rock and D’avid’s minds. Red Rock stands at the end of the hallway cautiously dawdling towards his front door with D’avid cowering behind him closing his eyes tightly. Red Rock: He can’t really be a body fucker…we’re probably just being stupid we’ve been watching the horror channel too much…damn it’s addictive low budget B movies with their irrational let slightly plausible plot lines… D’avid: I think I might have just made a shit in my pant! Heh… Red Rock, who keeps his gaze straight ahead, swings his hands backwards to smack D’avid any where his hand will reach in the general direction of D’avid’s head. Red Rock: Remember D’avid, when we get in there just act like nothing happened then we wait for him to go out and we break in to his room. D’avid: And if he’s in? Red Rock: Don’t say that D’avid! The two timid men slowly creep right up to Red Rock’s front door and with a bead of sweat cascading from Red Rock’s brow. He anxiously picks his key and inserts it in to the lock and his keys jingle together from Red Rock’s irrational quivering. He turns the key with the clunk of the mechanism functioning thus heightening the tension whilst Red Rock winces, hoping Howard won’t hear their entry as he reluctantly pushes the door ajar. D’avid: Is the body fucker in there? Red Rock: SHHUT up bastclart! We can’t let him know we know! Red Rock who still is maintaining his timid yet cautious hunched over stance creeps through the door in to the kitchen area. Fortunately Red Rock’s open plan flat means he doesn’t have to creep around several rooms to check if Howard is hiding all he has to do is check the bath room on the right and the two bedroom doors on opposite sides of the adjacent wall. Red Rock’s head careers sharply from left to right as he surveys the area and a sigh of relief is expelled upon the realisation that Howard is no where to be seen. Red Rock: He’s not in here… he could still be in his room though! D’avid: oOh this feels like that time I broke in to that girl’s house and stole all her French panties. Red Rock: That was you? You do realise you’ve been featured on crime watch? D’avid: Heh! Oooh Jill Dando, now I bet she’s a hot dead body! Red Rock shakes his head in disapproval at D’avid’s sick perversion over the long dead Crime Watch presenter whilst trying to sustain focus on the task in hand. He now concentrates on Howard’s bedroom door, half expecting Howard to pounce out naked wielding a knife to murder Red Rock and make love to his rotting corpse. It is another well documented fact that Red Rock gives in to pressure like a WW2 French solider so it is no surprise that he has just this second closed his eyes tightly with the sweat marks under his arms growing by the second to the size of small plates thus kicking off a foul onion like odour. Red Rock: I don’t want to do it D’avid I’m scared! D’avid: He’s not in there Red, look. Red Rock opens his eyes and D’avid is stood calmly in front of Howard’s bedroom door, which was left open the whole time. Red Rock quickly wipes the sweat slipping from the pours on his face as he starts to play it cool as if he was never scared. He now takes a more confident stance with the knowledge that Howard has vacated the building. Red Rock: Yeah, good thing too because I was SO going to ask loads of questions about this! D’avid: … Red Rock: Hey! Shut up I was play acting to lure him into a full sense of... ah fuck you D’avid! With Howard’s absence and the fact his door has been left open does beg the question ‘where has Howard gone’ usually Howard’s bedroom security tighter than a preteen but it has been left uncharacteristically open and upon reflection they have realised that the ludicrously uncomfortable sofa Howard brought with him when he moved in all those months ago has disappeared leaving nothing but four indentations in the carpet where the legs stood. Red Rock: Hang on; the sofa of prolonged discomfort is gone! And his room is open… He’s only gone and done a runner hasn’t he! D’avid: The cunning bastard! Red Rock: Let’s check his room! Red Rock pushes the door open and enters Howard’s pristine bedroom, once occupied by Red Rock’s old friend Boolie who left Red Rock a few months ago. Red Rock hasn’t entered this room since Boolie left and he is slightly set back with emotion but that emotion is clouded by surprise at how eccentrically clean the room is. The smell of glade air fresheners glide and swoop around the atmosphere but there is another smell that the over powering air fresheners can’t quite mask… the smell of rotting meat. Red Rock: Aww no… he’s defiantly had a dead body in here! Red Rock brings his hand across his mouth and nose to try and block out the strong rotting stench, where as D’avid wonders around checking all the draws for no good reason other than blind curiosity. D’avid: Nothing but used tissues covered in semen in here! Heh. D’avid pushes the bedside draws closed before a large Victorian style oak wardrobe catches his eye. It’s not the beautiful carpentry around the base and the top of the wooden cupboard that catches D’avid’s eye, nor is it the glowing original brass handles. It is the large stainless steel bolt locked with a bulky brass padlock across the doors that raises D’avid’s curiosity. D’avid: Hey check out the wardrobe! It’s locked…how very curious. Red Rock: Oh I bet there is a stonking great dead body in there! Oh shit bags what if there is? D’avid: Heh…let’s find out! Red Rock turns towards D’avid to try and talk him out of it but D’avid already has a blue crowbar in his hands. Red Rock looks slightly disturbed at D’avid’s grin, which impresses the words “insane” in his mind’s eye. Red Rock: Where the hell did you get that crow bar? D’avid: That doesn’t matter let’s get in that wardrobe! Red Rock: D’avid, I don’t think I want to see this! But it’s already too late D’avid lunges the crow bar behind the lock and begins to pry it out from the grip of the oak wood grain. The sound of cracking and creaking wood claws at Red Rock’s ear drum as all he can do is watch and wonder what is inside this giant example of beautiful Victorian carpentry. D’avid: NNEERGH! Nearly got it off! D’avid brings all his body weight down on the crow bar, pivoting it up; tearing the lock away from the wooden panels. So with one more gutsy strain on D’avid’s part the lock drops to the floor creating a menacingly heavy thud and a loud clink. Adrenaline begins to flow through the two men’s bodies as the heavy oak door falls open ever so slightly creating a small dark gap, barely wide enough to fix gaze on what the wardrobe bares. Red Rock: I can’t look! Red Rock covers his eyes like a girl watching a horror film, but he peers through a small parting between his index and middle fingers. D’avid who is becoming a little frightened himself extends his arm and hooks his fingers around the brass handles one finger at a time. D’avid: Ok…one…two…THREE! And on three D’avid throws the door open! Red Rock: HOLY FUCKING SHIT! D’avid: AIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEE! Red Rock and D’avid run from the room screaming whilst covering their mouths creating a duet of loud lung bursting wretches… 35 minutes later Red Rock and D’avid have now been sitting on opposite sides in perfect silence at Red Rock’s dining table. Both men sit clutching their hands together and holding then close to their faces in a vulnerable and fearful gesture. It is only a matter of time before the silence is broken and what better time than now? Red Rock: He actually was keeping a dead body in his wardrobe all this time…there was no computer programming job…he was a full time necrophiliac. D’avid: I don’t even think I’d fuck a dead body and there are a lot of things I’d fuck… heh. Red Rock: What are we going to do? We have to call the police! Red Rock ascends from his seat only to be stopped in his tracks by D’avid grapping his arm. His mannerisms give Red Rock the impression that D’avid is being serious this time so Red Rock sits down looking quite nervous at this uncharacteristic behaviour on D’avid’s part. D’avid: We can’t call the police Red… Howard has disappeared they’ll think you’re involved. Red Rock: D’avid, don’t tell me what I think you’re going to say… D’avid: It’s ok Red I’ll be here for you. Red Rock: D’avid you’re scaring me. Just at this moment thick black clouds shroud the sun and the room falls in to a brief moment of darkness. A sinister shadow is cast across D’avid’s face making look exceptionally demonic. D’avid tilts his head up to Red Rock making eye contact, still with his hand around Red Rock’s wrist. D’avid: We need to dispose of it. Red Rock wastes no time with his reaction as he springs up from his seat over to the other side of the room with his hand running backwards through his hair and his heart pounding like a jack hammer. Red Rock: D’avid, you can’t be frickin’ serious! Dispose of the body? I can’t do that D’avid I’m sorry. D’avid hops off his seat and approaches Red Rock; he puts his arm over Red Rock’s shoulders but the gestures is ill received by Red Rock as he swats D’avid’s arm away. D’avid: I’ll help you do it you don’t have to worry Red, we’ll be fine! Red Rock: D’avid this is fucked up I don’t know what to do… D’avid: Trust me Red we can take it far away from here and dump it somewhere no one will ever find it! Red Rock paces up and down the open plan flat over the place where the sofa uses to sit biting the follicles on his thumb. The creases on his forehead appear as his eyebrows lower in an expression of anguish and insecurity. Then suddenly Red Rock stops, shoves his hands in his pockets and stares across the room towards D’avid. Red Rock: Ok…let’s do it. My career is just picking up I can’t hold it up with dealing with the police and answers questions. D’avid pats Red Rock across his lower back. D’avid: We’re doing the right thing for us, no regrets. Now get me some bin bags and some gaffer tape. Later that evening… So several bin bags and a rolls of gaffer tape later our anti hero had completed the unspeakable. The time is now 1:09am and the streets have emptied only the sound of the wind cutting through the branches of the trees can be heard. The only other sound that can he heard is that of Red Rock and D’avid struggling with the cold rotting corpse of this dead woman they don’t even know. D’avid who is holding the feet descends the stairs step by step with Red Rock holding the upper part of the body. The two men try to keep their strained grunts to a minimum in fear of waking up the tyrant landlord whose window is just on their left. Red Rock: Shhh! Mr Camfield will hear… D’avid: I think this would have been easier if you went down first because you’re taller than me! Red Rock nearly slips off the last step and comes very close to dropping the body to the ground, luckily Red Rock manages to maintain grip on the shoulders. As the two men approach the boot of D’avid 20 year old Peugeot 205 the images of wrapping torn up black bin back plastic around the cold decomposing limbs of the dead woman, the sound of the gaffer tape ripping from the roll and the quite crispy noise of the tape sticking the to the plastic replays over and over again in Red Rock’s mind. His face is now very pales and the light from the street lamps glisten off his shiny sweat stricken face. D’avid: Relax Red, no one is around we’ll be perfectly fine! Now hold on a minute whilst I open the boot! D’avid struggles to get his car keys out of his pocket whilst still trying to hold the body. David grins and unlocks the boot of his car before looking back at Red Rock. D’avid: No going back now Red. Red Rock: I can’t believe I’m doing this; this is the kind of thing I’d expect from Chainz! Red Rock and D’avid shimmy over a little bit and slowly place the body in the boot. The car lowers slightly under the weight of the dead woman as D’avid positions her legs to fit comfortably in the boot. Red Rock wipes the sweat from his face using the sleeve of his dull green park jacket and D’avid does the same with his maroon velvet coat. Red Rock: So where are we going to take her? D’avid: I don’t know? We’ll just keep driving until we find some woods and we’ll take the body as far as our legs can take us and bury her there.. Red Rock: Ok…let’s go. D’avid unlocks the passenger side for Red Rock and Red Rock boards the vehicle; checking to see if anyone is watching them. He slams the door shut as D’avid enters the vehicle on the driver side. D’avid pulls out the manual choke and turns the key when suddenly there is a tap at the passenger side window! Red Rock and D’avid jump out of their skin and Red Rock smacks his head on the roof of the car. Red Rock: ARGH! Ren: Hey guys! Ren appears from nowhere she must have been visiting her best friend Sarah who lives in the flat opposite to Red Rock. Her words are blocked out by the glass, and D’avid begins to look a big nervous, gesturing Red Rock to get rid of her. Red Rock proceeds to wind the window down whilst trying to play it cool. Red Rock: Oh…err… HI…Ren what’s up? Ren: Where you guys going this late? Red Rock: Oh…no where. Ren: Do you think I could bum a lift home? I don’t want to get raped you see. And on that note D’avid lunges over Red Rock’s lap to being winding up the passenger side window. D’avid: Oh well I guess you’ll have to get raped. BYE REN! Ren’s face drops at D’avid’s callas words and the way D’avid is frantically trying to start the car. Red Rock: He’s just joking Ren… we can give you a lift! Ren: pfft… don’t worry I’ll walk. Red Rock smacks his head against the dashboard but then from behind the car Ren’s voice is heard once again. Ren: Hey you guys left the boot open! Red Rock and D’avid exchange equal looks of horror upon realising the stupidly forgot to shut the boot. Ren: What is that in the boot? Red Rock: DON’T LOOK AT IT REN! Red Rock and D’avid franticly burst out from the car desperately trying to neutralize Ren’s curiosity. Ren: OH MY GOD! Red Rock: NO REN IT’S NOT WHAT YOU THINK! Ren: IT’S A FLIPPING DEA - D’avid slaps his hand over Ren’s mouth to stop her from shouting to the world whilst Red Rock tries to fumble with an explanation. Red Rock: Ren, it’s really not what you think. Please listen! Ren sees the desperate and serious expression on Red Rock’s face and trusts her instinct and allows him to explain. Although her heart is racing and she has become short of breath she doesn’t run away. Red Rock: Don’t ask why or how but for some reason I never realised. Ren: Realised what? Red Rock: That Howard was keeping a dead body in his wardrobe and we have strong reason to believe that he was making love to said corpse. The other day we found this out because I had some rash caused by bugs that live only on dead bodies and seeing as I have never slept with a dead body I must of caught it from using Howard’s towel. Ren appears to be dumfounded by Red Rock’s explaination, and although Red Rock is expecting Ren to run away screaming to the police she doesn’t. Ren: I knew that guy was weird! I remember hearing weird noises coming from your flat. Red Rock: Yeah but the problem is he’s disappeared and we don’t know where he’s gone. Ren: Why didn’t you just call the police? Not that it matters now because your DNA is all over the body now. Red Rock: D’AVID! You didn’t say anything about DNA! D’avid: Heh…sorry… I figured you knew because you always watch CSI on living TV Red Rock: Yeah but I never pay attention I only have it on whilst I wait for Most Haunted to come on! Fucks sake! ... Red Rock: So now Ren you must understand that we have to do this. I can’t have my career held back by jail… and police questioning. Please don’t tell the police! Ren stops and stares at Red Rock and D’avid for a moment whilst pondering. Ren: Ok…only if I can come with you. Red Rock: Say what? You want to help us get rid of the body? Red Rock is took back by the thought of Ren wanting to come along to help get rid of the body but Ren seems to be strangely enthusiastic about the adventure. Ren: Yeah why not? It sounds pretty exciting! Red Rock looks over at D’avid, who his is shaking his head from side to side trying to dissuade Red Rock from allowing this. Then Red Rock’s stupidity kicks in once again as he juggles with the idea that if Ren comes as well it could be a golden chance to get with her and make amends for all the weird stuff he has gotten himself in to in the past. So with a goofy grin Red Rock replies… Red Rock: Sure you can come as well! D’avid slaps his hand across his own face and rolls his eyes whilst Red Rock opens the rear passenger seat for Ren. Ren hops in to the back seat with a smile upon her face as Red Rock jumps in the front passenger seat with a smile on his also. There is no going back now, no time for hindsight just focus on what needs to be done. No time for what ifs and buts just time for action. D’avid: Red, you idiot!
