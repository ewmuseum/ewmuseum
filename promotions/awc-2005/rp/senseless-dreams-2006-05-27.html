Senseless Dreams

27 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/05/06 Authors Notes Please note that the title does actually mean something towards the RP, some of these dreams don’t link with the others, so don’t worry if you find yourself confused by the end of this. “Why is she talking to me like this?” Gadgets face was totally blank, he was not blinking, and there were no signs of even the slightest breath, but still his lips moved. His lips moved with the power of twelve Jackie Chan’s, but only on every second movement did he utter a word. Although the inactivity of Gadgets face is deceptive, inside his mind is a different story. Beneath that thin layer of skin, and large layer of bone, was one of the most corrupt minds in the world. And the sad part is, Gadget does not even know that its corrupt, he just thinks that he has minor blackouts that will pass in a few weeks. The thing is, I know the real story inside Gadgets head, and I am about to share it with each and every one of you. ******* Images of explosions and soft sweeps of dead bodies and family snapshots plague Gadgets sight, until suddenly as this mental slideshow finishes on a picture of Pvt. Jacobs (yes the great Sgt. Jacobs was once a private – ed.) as a young boy in fathers arms, and his vision becomes clear, and he finds himself smack-dab in the middle of Kenya, during the first term of his active service. Pvt. Jacobs: wh…where am I? Gadget begins to scan the area, all around him are dead bodies and capsules of used ammunition. ???: Get down soldier! Gadget without question, drops onto his stomach and hides in the long grass. Then without warning his body goes completely stiff, and the voice that told him to get down slices through him, and a sudden tingling overwhelms the stiffness. Pvt. Jacobs: (muttering to himself) I know that voice, but where? Where could it be from? Gadget then rolls onto his back, and watches as a large man, who must be about six foot nine at least, walks towards his cowering body and utters some words that sound utterly out of place, and are not in the voice that made his body go stiff. ???:C’mon, its time for your sponge-bath. The young private lay in aw as his, who looked like him at least, drill instructor uttered the totally…well downright strange…words, and then faded into his surroundings, just before the catastrophic bang of a Kenyan’s shotgun barrel, and the bullet that would have no doubt killed him in one blow. Pvt. Jacobs:Oh shit! Being completely freak out by the sudden happenings, Gadget rolls back onto his stomach, looks straight forward, and without a second thought the young private crawls into the woods that lie ahead, and appear to contain other stray American soldiers. Whilst crawling, Gadgets vision and mind began to play up again, he once again became immersed in graphic images of dead bodies, but that picture of Gadget in his father’s arms as a young boy kept re-occurring, except now the image seemed to have more depth, and was animated in some shots. But once again these images faded, and the young private found himself once again immersed in unusual surroundings, but yet these surroundings were also familiar to gadget. Pvt. Jacobs: Wh…whe Gadget began to stutter, as he found himself in those very woods that he was crawling towards, surrounded by people from his tactical teams in Iraq to the people he enrolled with. Pvt. Jacobs: Butch? Zero? I thought you were dead. And Pvt. Williams, you got killed in the explosion that fucked my knee up. Pvt. Williams: Death is only what you make of it sergeant. Butch:He’s right Gadget, people can live full and plentiful lives even after death. Pvt. Jacobs: What…What are you talking about? Gadget begins to shake his head in astonishment at the fact that some of his closest, departed, friends where talking complete verbal diarrhea to him. Pvt. Jacobs: Ouch, crap my head. Once again it would appear that gadgets mind was playing up again, but this time it was a mezzotint picture of him and his father, but his father kept becoming 3D. He almost looked like…you know those pin things you buy in The Gadget Shop, where you press your hand in and your hand looks pixilated, no? Well it looks like on of those…and by the end of the 3d movement, a full colour image of Gadget and his father appears, with some text in the bottom right hand corner: I’m sorry son, I am really going to miss you. What could this mean? Could Gadget be dead? Is this the afterlife that Gadgets soul Is forever doomed to walk, with painful interruptions of the mind? Maybe not, but what Gadget will see when this slideshow finally stops, could give him better perspective. Pvt. Jacobs: (shivering) Good god, where am I now? After the second to last set of images had finished, Gadgets surroundings had totally changed, and drastically. He was no longer sat in luscious plains of Kenya, but he was sat in a dark old attack, propped up against a rickety old window, unable to turn around. But the creepy thing for gadget was that he could hear footsteps behind him, and the sharp yet soft sound of a leather whip slapping into a pair of gloves. Gadget: Who…who’s there? Gadget struggles, trying to spin his seating apparatus round to face the figure he could just make out in the reflection of the window. Gadget:What do you want with me? What is all this? ???:(raising the whip) You’ll see! CRACK~! Gadget:Fuck, who are you? Oh, and FUCKING HELL~! Gadget, whilst obviously pre-occupied with this strange saga, forgets about his pain until the last minute, and tries to figure out who this is. The womanly voice sounded familiar, but he could not place it. Woman: Does this sound familiar? “Andre, your dinner is ready” Then all of a sudden, gadget began to shudder as he finally placed the voice. Gadget: MOM?!?! What are you doing? Mom:Your father was a twat! I’m glad he’s dead! Gadget:Da…Da…Dads dead? Gadget’s eyes become teary, and he starts to think: “Why is she talking to me like this?” ****** Gadget, then suddenly awakens in the armchair of a hotel ward, next to a coffee machine. His face is sweaty; he stinks to high-heaven and his pulse I racing faster that a fat kids at a school bake sale. Gadget: Where am I now? Gadget once again scans the area to see were he is. In front of him is a hospital room, with the writing “Brian Jacobs – Deceased – 14:40 pm”. Gadgets eyes became teary once more. But being his strong self, gadget stands up and enters the room, in front of him. And what he saw now completely changed his life. For what he saw is worse than seeing your parents having sex. Lay in a row on the bed where his fathers dead body lies, was his mothers body, his little sisters body, and of course like just mentioned, his fathers body, all covered in blood, all dead. Mysterious Voice: (Softly) Gadget wake up, its time for work. ****** Gadget arose once more, but over him stood a beautiful woman, and luckily this time he knew he was awake, due to the fact he was kissing he beautiful girlfriend. Gadget slowly pushed her away from him, and wiped his teary eye. Girlfriend: It was only a dream…it was only a dream.
