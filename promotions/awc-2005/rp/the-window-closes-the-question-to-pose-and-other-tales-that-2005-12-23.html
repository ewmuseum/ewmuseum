'The window closes the question to pose' and other tales that may confuse you.

23 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: the now +Musing I’ve never been to Amsterdam. Never. Any other pot-head like myself would tell you that’s a sin, even if they haven’t been. So when AWC told me they’d fly me out to the Netherlands on their ticket for a Christmas party, did I have a choice to decline? No! “Christmas Party” is the business-morse code for “Let’s get fucked up.” In the formal sense that is. It’s a chance for everyone to come around and get emotional because of the holidays and because of booze. But Amsterdam? Amsterdam isn’t about getting drunk off of foreign vodka. Amsterdam is about TRIPPING THE FUCK OUT. Even the alcohol in Amsterdam turns your experiences into a cartoon; ever hear of Absinthe? Was I honestly expected to show up at that party? Wade told me he wasn’t going to go, so what the hell was I supposed to do? Try to touch Ellis Nash’s tit again? Make small talk with Tim and Purse? The way I look at it, aside from that paragraph filled with questions, is that I wasn’t expected to go anywhere. But don’t get me wrong; I did want to go. Christmas is so fun; especially when you’re spending it with the ones who hate you. There’s nothing more than envious eyes on the eve of the most giving holiday of the year. But, like I said: TRIPPING THE FUCK OUT. If you’ve never heard of it, Absinthe is the drink of choice in Amsterdam. I don’t really know that but it was all I drank while I was down there, so you really shouldn’t ask me. Absinthe is distilled in wormwood, which is a process that turns the alcohol into a hallucinogenic product, depending on how much you drink. Do you know how people shoot Absinthe in the bars there? They fill a small bowl up with about three ounces, and then pour out a few shots; for however many people are brave enough to take them. A match is dropped on the bowl, and the alcohol level is ridiculous enough to keep the bowl of neon green aflame for a good while. Each shooter takes a tablespoon of sugar and holds it over the flame just long enough to caramelize it. As the sugar turns brown, they dump it in their shots. Looking through a clear glass, you can watch that intimidating, bright green liquid become disgustingly black as the grains of sugar mix with the distilled wormwood. It’s bad enough that the Absinthe tastes like Jagermeister multiplied by ten, but after this process it even looks the part. I should have been smarter about the entire ordeal, because the Party would have been fun. Maybe if the chance arose to be more conservative, especially in terms of what I drank, things would have turned out alright. Within thirty minutes, I forgot the reason why I was even in Amsterdam. Well, I guess you could say I forgot the main reason I was in Amsterdam. AWC was an afterthought at this point. Bizarro Facey losing to Danny Ferguson just ushered in the feeling. All of these signs and pent of frustrations were telling me I had to lose some stress. So I’d smoke. And after getting high, drinking sounds like a good idea. So once I’m drunk I decide Mushrooms would be fun to take. It became a cycle of getting fucked up, then having the need to get even more fucked up. People will always tell you things to do when you travel; places to eat, landmarks to see, stores to shop. When you’re in the kind of lifestyle I am, people tell you what drugs to buy and where you can get them. What strands will give you the best high for your personality; they tell you that kind of shit. They encourage that. One place that I feel everyone has to go to, if they ever go to Amsterdam, is a Smart Shop. +Informing Question: What is a smart shop? Answer: Smart Shops are stores littered throughout Holland that are licensed to deal, exclusively, various forms of hash bricks and psychedelic mushrooms; better known as ‘shrooms.’ Question: Are mushrooms legal in Amsterdam? Answer: Are you kidding? Fucking children that haven’t even been born yet is legal in Amsterdam. Question: What do magic mushrooms do? Answer: Fuck you up. If you ask a stupid question, you’ll get a stupid answer. Question: Well, scientifically speaking? Answer: Shrooms are fungi that contain properties that root in the user’s mind, causing a psychedelic manifestation. Commonly reffered to as a “trip”, the average mushroom instigates this process with the chemical psilocybin. This substance instigates effects on the brain and body that can best be described as… hmm, this is hard. Question: What’s hard? Answer: To explain; this is. Let’s just say your body goes through phases of body-warmth. You are established with a form of mental clarity, so to speak, that often is a result of looped-thinking or introspective-thinking. Open and closed eye visuals can result, though aren’t as common as the other side-effects, like auditory hallucinations so-to-speak. Question: That sounds awesome! Where can I get me some Shrooms? Answer: Whoa, slow down Liberace, we’ve still got to go through the opening act. First of all, Shrooms are not for everybody. Certain people can feel overwhelmed by the feeling and struck with confusion, paranoia, and fear. That’s when people do stupid things. The most common emotion/feeling someone on Shrooms has is a feeling of restlessness. Question: What do you mean, restlessness? Answer: A user of mushrooms will often feel like there is an overall objective to achieve while they’re in the midst of their trip. It is a vague feeling that any user can pair with any activity they need to complete, but it is often confusing and can cause a bad trip. Question: That sounds kind of scary. Answer: Only if you’re a bitch and/or pussy, and that wasn’t a fucking question! Come on, asshole, you’re starting to jeopardize your single purpose in the Roleplay—make with the questions! Question: How long does the effect last? Answer: A half-hour after ingestion, most likely the trip will last for six-plus hours. Question: SIX HOURS?! That seems like a long time. Answer: Time flies when you’re having fun. Question: So what kind of fun is Adam having? Answer: Well, there are three strands of mushrooms. I’ve already mentioned those with the chemical psilocybin, but there is another strand that contains the chemical muscimol, an agonist substance that triggers related effects on receptors in the human brain. Question: Yeah, whatever, what about the third kind? Answer: You don’t really, uh… you don’t need to know about the third strand. It’s a little harsh and- Question: Don’t pussyfoot, bitch. What’s so dangerous about the third kind of mushrooms? Answer: Well, the scientific name of the chemical contained in the third strand is ergoline alkaloid, which is the closest thing to LSD there is in psychotic drugs. Basically, if you want to get fucked up as your dad at Woodstock without dropping a tab of acid; take these mushrooms. Question: But I don’t want to get that fucked up! Who would take those? Answer: Not a whole lot of people, actually. The ergoline chemical actually has adverse effects on the brain and body. Ever hear of ergotism? It’s a side-effect that basically slows down the circulation of the blood to your limbs. It causes gangrene, and in turn amputations. If you take too much of that certain kind of mushroom, you’re bound to have ergotism. And that isn’t fun. Question: Wait a second, you never answered; what kind is Adam taking? Answer: Er...goline Alkaloids. Question: Are you kidding?! That could kill him! Answer: Potentially. It sure as hell would be fun to watch though! Question: Can we watch? Answer: Of course. +Ingesting It didn’t matter that the entire matter was legal. Growing up in a society such as the United States created an eternal feeling wariness in the young Adam Dick, being that he was a frequent drug user in his recreational time. There was always a need to watch your back for concerned citizens and cops with a penchant for violence. That type of feeling carries over, even in countries like this where it is completely legal. This paranoia, coupled with the amount of Absinthe he took earlier, wasn’t exactly the healthiest combination. Making drug deals inside an everyday store was not where he pictured himself this week. The claustrophobic feeling found him once again confined in the small Smart Shop, the walls littered with figures of Jerry Garcia, Trey Anastasia, Bob Marley and Dean Martin. Yes, Dean Martin loved mushrooms. Throughout the entire process, from asking for the shrooms to paying for them to actually receiving them wrapped in a cute little drug bag, Adam thought that a SWAT Team would be repelling from the rooftops, kicking in the large store-front windows with machine guns pointed at their heads in possibly the most unrealistic drug-raid of all time. Paranoia was hitting him bad. The look of the shrooms wasn’t exactly soothing either. It looked as if someone picked tall and skinny mushrooms in their backyard, dried them out, and decorated them with gold flakes. The caps were small and stout with a collage of colors that looked similar to a raku-fired piece of pottery. “If you go to Amsterdam,” his friends said, “Make sure you try these mushrooms.” Then they’d pull up a picture on the internet. The mushrooms Adam purchased and the ones he saw in pixels looked one and the same. That affirmation hit him in the stomach, it was a bit overwhelming. “OELKA ALJS GUNTAB.” Said the store clerk. Actually, he said Relax, or you’ll have a bad trip but the thick accent was too much for Adam to understand. Frightened by the foreigner’s comments, he snatched the bag from his hands and ran out the door to escape all the irrational fears haunting the Smart Shop. He didn’t understand a thing about the culture, even certain aspects of a topic he thought was so universal (drugs), and sought it best to find a more comfortable setting. Making his way out of the Red Light District, off-duty Face-Eater came across a hole-in-the-wall breakfast diner and sought it suitable to eat there. Being the only patron in the restaurant Adam had the exclusive right of precedence. He chose a corner table and ordered a glass of orange juice. The old wives’ tale for Mushrooms was that vitamin-C always enhanced a person’s trip. What Adam didn’t know, as he began munching on the fungus, was that he didn’t need any enhancements for this trip. This was a ‘balls to the walls’ type of deal. His teeth would grind up and down on the dried texture. As the saliva would coat the mushrooms, they would become stickier and softer. If caught on the wrong part of your mouth, it could have been vomit inducing. Most newbs like to use peanut butter when eating their mushrooms in order to help with the taste. Adam wasn’t a pussy, he liked that shit raw. Even if it meant getting all the sticky and gooey chunks stuck in the depths of his bicuspids; that just meant more hallucinogenic treats for later when his tongue could worm them out. If you’d never taken them before, you’d swear you were chewing unsalted pumpkin seeds. That’s the best way to describe the taste and texture to a person who hasn’t eaten them. These were a bit different, though. The stems and caps began to flake off in his mouth in fine threads that felt like splinters. Some would stab his gums and create a irritable feeling in his mouth, but Adam didn’t find this strange. He just finished his one-eighth-of-an-ounce sack, and downed his glass of orange juice in a matter of seconds. And then he called the waiter back. “I think I’ll have an order of flap-jacks, please.” +Forgetting “He is a fucking woman, you know?” “Right,” he said. I don’t think he understands me though. “Not right, man, not right at all.” How could he understand me? He’s a fucking washing machine. “Our company is being represented by a guy who can’t even fight back when faced with pressure.” I took a pause. My last pancake was getting cold and my replacement syrup was taking forever for my waiter to bring out; I may as well just eat it now. “Maybe you go about things differently, tex,” he said. I took three bites that effectively took care of half of the pancake. “Reckon he is the Champ’een for a reason.” “Watch it.” I tell the homeboy. “It doesn’t matter how he got there. He wasn’t facing me. Get me in a one-on-one encounter, not that Triangles bullshit, and I’ll rip his fucking face off.” “Easy, techs, you’re shootin’ from the mouth and writing programs that won’t function!” My mouth began spitting phrases I hadn’t begun to process yet, as if it were on auto pilot. I didn’t even know what I was talking about anymore; all’s I knew was that I was mad. And this thing doesn’t even know what I’m mad about. “Like I can’t? Bro, I’ll grind his bones with my fucking nutsack.” “Ah-so, ah-so, but who’ll git-yer title belt all clean if the fella’s pre-determined with that therr broad o’ ye transferred to ‘im.” His raspy voice had clanks in the hard noises. I thought it was a speech impediment at first. I guess it’s safe to say it still is. I’d have a speech impediment, too, if a computer was tellingme what to say. “You’re right. Kelli’s too big of a bitch to keep her hands to herself and I can’t think about her at this point. Did you know she broke my heart?” “’Member that there time, tex, when that broad corrupted your heart-drive?” It was illiterate non-sense and it taunted my ears. The wax in there had built up enough over the years, too much for a candle to stop, and I didn’t want him cluttering it up any more than he had. “What do you know about corrupted heart-drives? Are you seeing her too? Is everyone else in this fucking bar having sex with my ex-girlfriend?” What a stupid question! Of course not, well, not at the same time. They were taking turns—that was the rumor. “Easy there, techs, or that great code in the sky may just deal you a hand ye’cannot handle.” “Oh?” I quip back. “And you know everything that I’m going through? You know how to make me feel better.” “O’course. Ain’cha my daddy?” “I don’t know, kid, I could be a lot of people’s daddys. Especially in this state. What’s your name?” “Big Nasty.” The dish-washer said. He opened his mouth, clank clank clank, and out came his title. “Big Nasty, the Robo-Cowboy.” “Huh, Big Nasty, eh? I bought drugs off of a big, nasty fellow one time.” “Oh yeah, techs? What classification of drungs?” Big Nasty wasn’t your average robotic cowboy. Dude was a walking kitchen appliance wearing a pair of chaps and a cowboy hat, with a knack for cattle-rangling and code-cracking. Word on the streets was that he could interface with any stable and produce the best outcome, but I really don’t know what that means. Apparently he was robotic cowboy—I couldn’t tell. I didn’t really want to find out anymore about it, I just wanted to pay the bill and get the fuck out. Like all I was put on this earth to do was pay my bill, and these foreign servers won’t allow it. But Big Nasty would. He’d dispense that cash all the way to the bank. “What did you take, tex?” “Alkaline batteries.” “No, that’s what III take.” “Oh yeeeee-haw, that’s right.” “I deducted that you’re taking magic mushrooms, you feelin’ em yet?” Hmmm. “No, not yet. But I’ll let you know, Big Nasty the Robotic Cowboy.” +Peaking Colors lost shapes and forms lost borders. Tones and diction spill from their pre-determined structures and begin to fade in with the scenery. What was once your face is now my dollar, and what was once my dollar is now in the toilet. This is the way we buy our food. This is the way we eat things. People say you earn paychecks with hard work but I’m not confined to that jibberish. I flow. I write. I do as I please and it usually is to other’s chagrin. There is no duty that binds my psyche.. No life-force that guides my hand. It’s not like I haven’t thought about my life before, and that I don’t deserve it. There are so many people who can do good in my steed, and I have to wonder ‘am I really cut out for this?’ and then I wonder if I should cut out for this. Mike Wade says were Unfuckable but that is seemingly no the case. I’ve been stuck, fucked, chucked and ducked in my short time here. Why do I even belong here? I guess I could ask the Robo-Cowboy, he seems to be content with helping. Helping. Helping is what Franz Ferdinand did by dying. Helping is what Robin and Huntress of Earth-2 did by sacrificing themselves. Helping is Dennis Rodman retiring. Helping is winning Survivor: The Moon. Helping is something that I am not capable of. Helping doesn’t have a point, and neither does this. I feel like laughing because my father just died and they say laughter is the best medicine. So I laugh and hope he wakes up, and when he doesn’t, I start to cry. And he tells me “Buck up, Boy. You’re just a child and with that type of attitude you’ll die the same.” He tells me laughing is like crying is like moaning is like sighing is like screaming. I feel all of the emotions that evoke such actions at once. I contemplate suicide. I feel my life is near complete. And then, there is restlessness. An eternal restlessness that doesn’t die with the fungus’ effects. This restlessness will drive my car off a bridge. Timmmmmmber. Crash. Explosion. Disintegrate. Nighty night. My drive is full, and I have the will to win.
