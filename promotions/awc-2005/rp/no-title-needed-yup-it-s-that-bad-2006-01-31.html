No title needed... Yup it's that bad...

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/01.2006 Nerves. They have a weird on some people. They can turn the most confident person into a bumbling fool. They can the most relaxed person into a neurotic freak. They can change a straight A student into struggling to get a C grade. Or they have a positive effect on others. “At first he was afraid, He was petrified… Thinking he could not beat three men, Without Chuck by his side…” Chuck, get out of here! Chuck Norton: What? Russ Bridges: Chuck, this is my moment. Bradley was doing a deep meaningful monologue. Chuck Norton: About nerves? Russ Bridges: Yeah, about nerves. It was going to build up to show me thriving on nerves and being amazing. Chuck Norton: Wow. That is inspired. Where did you steal that from… “The Kyle Lamen guide to introductory promos?” Russ Bridges: Nope I checked that but it said something about faking your own death, so I checked the Paddy O’Shea guide. I couldn’t understand it, so finally I read the “Chris Metal guide.” Chuck Norton: Oh god! You have to be joking? Russ Bridges: Of course I am, like anyone would publish a guide by Chris Metal. Bradley came up with the idea of the nerve thing… Chuck Norton: Bradley? Yes. Chuck Norton: When did you come up with the nerves idea? This morning. Chuck Norton: Bradley, don’t lie to me. I’ll replace you with Seth. Seth? Chuck Norton: The parallel universal narrator. Okay. I came up with it in the early 90s around the time that e-feds started popping up. Chuck Norton: You see generic, boring ideas. So? It works. Russ Bridges: It obviously doesn’t. You two have been jabbering on and my promo is on the point of collapse. To be honest, I don’t it was ever “built” for it to collapse. Chuck Norton: Then build it… Russ will need some momentum if he is to beat three other guys without me backing him up. Russ Bridges: I could beat any amount of people without your help. Hold on a second. I’m not going to sitting here and listen to you two bicker. Chuck Norton: Then what do you want to talk about? Hold on, let me think. … … Just for you Pierre… … … EUREKA! Chuck Norton: Who says “Eureka” these days? Russ Bridges: *shrugs* EUR-FREAKING-EKA! Chuck Norton: What? Tell me about the past couple of weeks. Russ Bridges: What? We haven’t spoken in like three weeks. Tell me what you’ve been up to training. Russ Bridges: Wow… what a genius idea! What the fuck are we paying for? Well have you got any better ideas. Russ Bridges: Yeah, go back to the nerves monologue. Nope. I can safely say Chuck killed that with his song. Chuck Norton: No I didn’t! My cover of “I Will Survive” added to the intensity of it. Russ Bridges: It didn’t! It didn’t! Chuck Norton: It did! Trust me, I know these things. It didn’t. Anyway… tell me about your past few weeks. Chuck Norton: Well, um, we signed contracts with the Atlantic Wrestling Club. No shit. That’s why I’m here. I’m helping you guys do well by helping you with your promos. Chuck Norton: Well if your “Nerves” thing is anything to go by we won’t be expecting to carry any title belts for a long, long time. Well if you guys could go with it a couple of times we might get somewhere… Russ Bridges: I can go with it… Chuck Norton: Yeah, me too. Fine. Tell me about the last two weeks. Chuck Norton: Well we signed contracts with Atlantic Wrestling Club. *sigh* Russ Bridges: And since then we have been working on new material for our next album… New material for your next album? Chuck Norton: Yeah… I have written three songs.. “The Life and Times of Ben Affleck”… “One Step Out of the Fire and three into Bed with the Devil” and my personal favourite, “I Could Eat You Up I Would Because I’m A Cannibal.” Wow… They sound… interesting? Chuck Norton: Well the last one is because it’s based on the early years of my life where I lived in the Amazon Rainforest and surviving by killing explorers that crossed my path. Russ Bridges: You never lived in the Amazon Rainforest. Chuck Norton: Well, it’s a song about what my life would have been like if I had… Did you guys do anything to do with wrestling? Chuck Norton: Like? …Training? Collective: *giggles* Chuck Norton: Yeah right. Seriously? Russ Bridges: We have been busy with our songs… And you actually expect to win your 4-Way on Fresh! Chuck Norton: When you say it sounds dirty… So you guys have spent two weeks writing songs and coming up way s to annoy people instead actually trying to train and prepare for being in a wrestling promotion. Russ Bridges: When you say that you make it sound naughty… Chuck Norton: Are you flirting with us? *sigh* I’m out… Chuck Norton: Where’s he going? Russ Bridges: He’s probably on his period… Chuck Norton: Must have the same cycle as Aimz… Russ Bridges: Isn’t she like forever on her period? Chuck Norton: Apparently not, apparently she is just that pissed off all the time. Russ Bridges: Good luck to the man marries her… Chuck Norton: Yeah… … … Russ Bridges: I miss Bradley… … Russ Bridges: He gave our discussions a point… Maybe we should actually do some training and he’ll come back. Chuck Norton: We’ll be fine without him, just you wait and see. … … … Chuck Norton: Okay, we need him back… To the gym…
