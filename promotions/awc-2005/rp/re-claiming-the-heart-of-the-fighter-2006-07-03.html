Re-Claiming The Heart of the Fighter

3 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/03/2006 The screen is black. A message in white letters slowly begins to fade in onto the screen. It reads: 'There comes that span of time in every man's life in which he believes that he is down and out. He feels as though the whole world is weighing him down. He fails to see the sun. He fails to taste the bittersweet tastes of life. Instead, he partakes in the sour. Sometimes a man in this state feels as though he is trapped with nowhere to go, no way of ever seeing the light of sun. Then, there comes that small glimmer of light at the end of the tunnel, that small ray of hope that showers him with a new feeling of strength to overcome and rise up. The time has come. Exit the night and seize the new day...' The words slowly fade to black. The camera then fades into an empty locker room inside the MTS Center mere minutes after the Shawn Harris/Vince Jones match at AWC's Divide and Conquer. The door swings open and a rather solemn Vince Jones trudges inside. One would believe that after coming out on top in a match at a pay-per-view extravaganza like Divide and Conquer, that 'The Violence' Vince Jones would be feeling as though he's ontop of the world; but, by the expression on his face its seems as though Vince Jones is carrying the weight of the world on his shoulders for one reason or another. Vince closes the door behind himself and is greeted by an exuberant Jasmine and Reno Banks. They rush to his side carrying bottles of champagne, shake them up, pop the tops, and immediately begin showering him down with the contents of their bottles. Reno Banks: (with a large smile on his face.) Ah Mr. Jones, splendid work out there in the ring tonight, simply splendid. You've proved once again to each and every last member of AWC that you know how to get the job done when the time calls for it. I'm proud of you, Mr. Jones. Great way to dispose of that piece of trash, Shawn Harris, out there. Reno pats Vince on the back as he laughs outloud in amusement of the outcome. Reno Banks: I mean, what made this guy even think that he'd just show back up in AWC, (points a finger at Vince) your stomping ground, and just pick off where he left off thinking he'd come out on top? That's ignorant on his part. Its insane. Its just plain dumb. Well, I have to hand it to ya. You really put him in his place out there and I'm proud. Vince just sneers at Reno and shakes his head as if to express that he's obviously not as thrilled about the victory as Reno is; but, Reno fails to realize this in his excitement. Jasmine dumps out the remains of her bottle of champagne bottle on Vince's head in delight. She leans in close and gives Vince a large kiss on the cheek in admiration of his win out there. She also appears to be a bit oblivious to his demeanor at the moment. Jasmine: V, you really took care of business out there. I'm so proud of you. So, what are we gonna do next? We going out downtown? We going to celebrate over a candlelit dinner for two? (she smiles in his face brightly and nudges him playfully) Maybe a little bit of dessert afterwards for that nice victory of yours? (winks at Vince) Vince places a hand on her chest and shoves her to the side, shakes his head in disappointment, and walks right past her shaking himself dry of the champagne from the champagne shower. Vince Jones: What are we gonna do? V don't know what the fuck you two gonna do; but, he leavin'! Jasmine: (in surprise) What?? Vince Jones whirls around and faces both Jasmine and Reno. Vince Jones: Why is it that wheneva V. Jones speaks lately you two be actin' like he speakin' some foreign language or somethin' here? What's so hard for you two to understand, huh? V leavin' this hell hole! 'Nuff said on that! Reno waltzes over to Vince and lays a hand on his shoulder, but Vince slaps it away. He looks on at Vince in frustration. Jasmine just appears shocked by his demeanor. Vince Jones: (points at Reno and then Jasmine) Don't you two look at The Violence like that! We done here! What had to be said has already been said! And that's all there is to say 'bout that! So, let's just kill the chatta right here! Aiight? Reno Banks slowly makes his way towards Vince as V. Jones continues huffing and puffing hot air. Vince Jones: V said, aiight? You followin' V here? There ain't nothin' else that needs to be said here! Reno Banks gets square in the face of Vince. Reno Banks: Mr. Jones, get ahold of yourself right this minute! Vince shoves Reno backwards and rips his dark shades off his face and chucks them to the side. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Who the fuck you think you are gettin' all up in The Violence's face like that, huh? You lookin' to get that skull of yours busted open or somethin', man? Is that it? Cuz V (begins cracking his massive knuckles) can surely make that shit happen, Reno! Jasmine rushes across the room and steps in between the two of them trying to hold them apart from one another. Reno Banks: Mr. Jones! (grunts, places a hand on his forehead in anger, and just shakes his head) Forget it! We've had this talk with you on countless occassions, but there's just no getting through to you if you don't want to be reached! You know what? (throws his hands up in defeat) I don't really care what you do at the moment, Mr. Jones! I think I'm pretty much past caring about what you do because you just don't listen! (points in Vince's direction) You never do! And that's where the problems in this business relationship lie! Vince Jones looks from side-to-side in shock of Reno's bold statement, glares Reno up and down, and takes a step forward while Jasmine struggles to hold him back. Jasmine: V, just chill out! We can all talk this over! We don't need to do this right now! You just beat Shawn Harris! You should be happy right now! We should all be happy! That's the way its supposed to be! No, that's the way it will be! Vince Jones: (looks down upon Jasmine and quickly shakes his head) Nah, nah, nah! Fuck that shit, Jasmine! V. Jones sick and damn tired of havin' to sugar coat shit! This whole situation is nothin' but shit and its startin' to smell real bad here! Ya heard? V 'bout to set things straight with this bitch made right here and right now! (focuses his sights on Reno) Reno, the only problem with our fuckin' business relationship here is the fact that you (points at Reno) and V (points to himself emphatically) deep down inside haven't seen eye-to-eye on a whole lot of shit since 360WE, mane! And that's just the cold hard facts! No matta how the hell you slice that shit its true! Reno folds his arms across his chest as nods his head in agreement. Reno Banks: Yeah! You're right about that, Mr. Jones! Vince Jones: Hell yeah V right 'bout that shit! And V 'bout to fill yo lil scrawny ass in another fact! The problem don't lie with V! The problem (points an accusing finger at Reno) is you! That's where the problems have originated with this relationship since the beginning! Yo greedy ass been cloudin' V's head with shit that don't matta to him since day one! Reno Banks laughs to himself and just shakes his head in disbelief with Vince's remarks. Reno Banks: Oh is that so?!? Is that really so, Mr. Jones?!? What? You don't want to be successful? Is that it? (begins pounding his fist into his open hand to hammer home his point) Because all I have ever done, since day one, is been a facilitator for you to display your talents and be nothing but successful in this business. If it wasn't for me (points to himself) where would you be, huh? Where would you be, Mr. Jones? Answer me that and give me an honest answer, not one of your self-serving ego boosters of a reply! I want an honest answer out of you, Mr. Jones! Vince stands there in silence fuming with rage. Jasmine continues to stand between Vince and his agent worried about any sudden actions out of the two. Vince Jones: (in a slightly slow fashion) Yo idea of success ain't V's fuckin' idea of success, man! That's the shit V been tryin' to hammer in yo fuckin' head since day one, but you neva listen! All you eva thinkin' 'bout is how you can further cash in on V's name, but you neva thinkin' 'bout the important issues! Where the fuck is The Violence's title strap, huh!? Where the fuck is his glory at!? Vince stands tall and folds his massive arms across his chest and sneers in Reno's direction. Reno places a hand on his forehead and just shakes his head as he is obviously growing more and more frustrated by this whole discussion. Reno Banks: Look, we've talked about this time and time again, Mr. Jones. The gold will be sitting around your waist in due time. Trust me! It'll happen! I guarantee that! Jasmine looks up and beams a bright smile in Vince's face. Jasmine: You hear that? He guarantees it. Now let's just move on, V. Vince totally ignores Jasmine and keeps eyes locked and focused on Reno. Vince Jones: Yo constant guaranteein' is gettin' old, Reno! Its all gettin' really old here! When the fuck is that time gonna come fo sho, huh? Reno Banks: (shrugs and responds under his breath) I don't know... Vince Jones: WHEN?? Reno Banks: I don't know, Mr. Jones! Only time will tell that; but, in the meantime I've always managed to get you some good money to sit on! How? Because I help to make you a marketable figure in this business! Those t-shirts, the action figures, the commercials, all of that is because of me! The place you stay and all the nice things you've come to own are because of me! And don't you ever deny that, Mr. Jones! You know how many other clients I have in my ring? I could be spending more time with them, but no! I choose to provide the best I can for you because you've got that special little something! People told me I was wrong in pursuing a good for nothing street thug and criminal like yourself, but I... Vince's eyes grow wide as he is simply livid at that statement. Reno Banks: ...told them otherwise! I told them this would be an opportunity of a lifetime if it actually worked. People would eat it up like... Reno finally stops and realizes that he's just said the wrong thing and begins to squirm with discomfort. Vince Jones: Is that what you really think 'bout V, huh?? Is that it? Is that how you view The Violence? Well, you know what?? (Vince pauses for a moment) Ah fuck it! Just forget about it! V done arguin' here! Vince releases a loud hiss of anger, pushes pastJasmine and Reno, and makes his way to the locker room door. Jasmine reaches out and tries to grab a hold of Vince but her attempts are in vain. Jasmine: Don't leave, V! C'mon! Vince Jones: See the both you back in New Yawk! Vince reaches the doorway yanks it open. Jasmine rushes up behind and begins tugging on him in an effort coax him into staying. Vince whirls around and snatches her by the throat. Vince Jones: If you follow V back tonight it'll be yo ass, Jasmine! V fuckin' out! 'Nuff said! He shoves her to the floor, exits, and slams the door shut behind him. Reno just shakes his head in disappointment with Vince's actions and yells back at him through the door. Reno Banks: You don't want to be seen as a thug or a criminal, but yet you stoop to the lows of a two bit, good for nothing thug and criminal! Yeah! That's really classy of you, Mr. Jones! Go ahead and keep pushing around little guys like me and helpless women like Jasmine! Go ahead! I'm surprised its gotten you this far in life! Jasmine turns and eyes Reno in disgust at his presence. She hops up to her feet and walks up to Reno. Jasmine: A street thug and criminal?? Why would you even bring that up with him? Reno Banks: (a bit flabbergasted) Well, I... Jasmine slaps the taste out of Reno's mouth which leaves him in shock. She begins backing her way towards the door. Jasmine: He can't help that he came up on the wrong side of the tracks, Reno! Why would you even go there? He's paid his dues! Reno Banks: Look, I made a mistake! I'm sorry about that; but, he really needs to get it together! I don't care what the man has been through or seen in his lifetime. I'm not in the business of caring, Miss Jasmine. I'm in the business of making sure his talent is visible and recognized by the public's eye... Jasmine: And making money off of him! Reno Banks: (pauses) Yes, that too; but, moreso to make sure that he can enjoy the best career as possible and last in this business. And frankly, I can't do my job effectively when I have to constantly tip-toe around his explosive temper that flares up into tantrums like these! And be warned, I've been around the tracks for years in this business. I've seen many come and seen many go. What keeps the guys like Mr. Jones afloat rather than sinking to the bottom of the sea and being forgotten? Its guys like me (points to himself), Miss Jasmine, the guys that handle the business in the background... Reno suddenly stops mid-sentence and begins thinking to himself. Reno Banks: Wait a minute! Don't try to push all the blame on me. You're in this for the same reasons that I am. Why else would a beautiful vixen like yourself ever be mixed up with a guy like Mr. Jones anyways if it wasn't for the money? Why else would a woman constantly subject herself to shameless ridicule and whatnot if it wasn't for the money? Jasmine's eyes grow wide in shock at his Reno's accusations. Jasmine: What? I can't even believe that you'd even go there with me, Reno! Reno Banks: Oh, I did, Miss Jasmine. I mean since we're all being frank and honest with one another I thought I might as well clear the airways a bit too. You go on about how much you care about Mr. Jones, but in the end you're no better than I am. And those are the facts! So, don't you ever try to get on my case and at the same time try to play the part of the loving and caring girlfriend and valet. Just face it! You're in this situation for the same reasons that I am! And that's all there is to it! Reno folds his arms across his chest and turns his nose up in the air at Jasmine. She quickly turns around and storms out the locker room as well leaving Reno by himself as the camera slowly fades to black. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The camera slowly fades back in and we are now in the passenger bay of a what appears to be a rather quiet passenger plane. A gentle murmur of voices can be heard throughout the First Class passenger area of the plane, but for the most part the scene is rather calm and subdued at the moment. The camera pans around the calm scene as a beautiful, blonde-haired stewardess is seen pushing a small cart of food down the aisles. She turns and catches notice of one of the passengers, stops, and flashes a bright smile in the passenger's direction. Stewardess: Hello, sir! Would you like anything to eat, maybe a snack, a meal or even a beverage at the moment? The camera perspective changes and we see none other than Vince Jones seated alone with a scowl of discontent plastered across his seat. He is leaning back in his chair and rapping the armrest with his large fingers. He sighs with his eyes still focused straight ahead and shakes his head. Stewardess: Very well, sir. The stewardess turns and takes a step forward, but decides to stop in her tracks and turns back towards Vince Jones. Stewardess: Umm...is there anything that I can do for you at the moment, sir? Anything at all? Vince slowly turns and faces her with his eyes locked on her intently. Vince Jones: (shakes his head) No! Stewardess: (nodding) Very well, sir. I hope you enjoy the rest of your flight. The stewardess pushes her cart along and leaves Vince alone to himself. Vince shakes his head, slowly turns and faces forward once again. He squirms around a bit in his seat as if to try and get into a much more comfortable resting position. After doing so he remains in silence and leans back in his chair and slowly shuts his eyes as the camera fades out. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- {{Flash Back to About 9 Years Ago}} The camera fades back in and we are in a vaguely familiar fully-furnished office. In front of him sits a desk that’s loaded with paperwork.On his right sits a small filing cabinet. Behind the desk sits the big man himself, Big Lex, who is looking back at Vince Jones with a pleased expression on his face. Big Lex is the man behind the NYUFC, the beating heart of this illegal gambling ring, entertainment, and blackmarket trade center. He is wearing a gray pin-striped business suit and has a shiny bald head with a well-trimmed goatee on his brown-skinned face. Behind Big Lex is a harem of beautiful women, black, asian,white, Hispanic, Indian, etc. Vince Jones steps forward and looks down at Big Lex. Vince Jones: V's here, Big Lex. Big Lex: Ahhhh V.J, its so good to see you. I always enjoy your presence, my boy. You know its not quite often that the NYUFC has the fortune of coming across a diamond in the rough like you. (nods and chuckles to himself) I've yet to regret the day that I gave you a job here in my world. Vince Jones: (nods) Good deal. Glad V hasn't been a disappointment. Big Lex: Yeah. Well, I must be boring you with all this idle chit chat. You didn't come here to talk you came to collect your prize money. Vince nods his head in agreement. Big Lex: That's what I've always admired about you. You've got your head on straight. You know what's important in life. (leans forward in his chair and points in Vince's direction) You know the value of green. Keep that up and you'll go far in my arena, V.J. You'll go really far and that's a guarantee. Big Lex reaches his massive, ringed hand into the pocket of his pinstripped suit and whips out a roll of fresh, clean cash. Big Lex wafts the roll of cash under his nose and appears to be nearly enchanted by the scent. Big Lex: Ahhhh...the smell of fresh cash money. Well, its yours now... (tosses the wad of cash into Vince's hands) for a job well done out there tonight. Enjoy... Vince Jones: (smiling) Thanks, Big Lex. Vince is about to turn and exit, but is beckoned to stick around for a moment by Big Lex's extended hand. Big Lex: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Where you going there so soon, V.J? Don't you want your bonus? Vince Jones: (in confusion) Bonus? Big Lex turns towards one of the women in his harem and claps his hands. A beautiful, busty, hispanic girl with long flowing brown hair and piercing brown eyes steps forward. Big Lex: V.J, how 'bout you take Mercedes for a spin tonight on me? Vince sees the hispanic girl and tries to remain composed but can't help but cracking a smile at his 'bonus' courtesy of his boss. She slides across the room towards Vince and caressing his chest in a rather seductive manner as Vince replies. Vince Jones: (nodding) Oh, that can be arranged... Big Lex leans back in his chair with a smug expression plastered across his face as he folds his arms across his chest and nods. Big Lex: Good! Now you make sure she gets back here in one piece. One scratch on her and that's your ass, son! Vince is a bit taken back by Big Lex's threat. Suddenly Big Lex begins roaring with laughter at his expense and pounds the desktop with his open palm. Big Lex: Its a joke, V.J. Lighten up a bit; but, to be serious she better make it back here in one piece. (points in Vince's direction) Understood, V.J? Vince Jones: (nods his head in agreement) Understood. Big Lex: Gooooood! Well, enjoy yourself in the meantime and I'll be sure to have you locked in for another prize fight very very soon. You're dismissed. Big Lex motions for Vince to leave and he obeys with the beautiful hispanic girl hanging from his arm like an ornament. They both exit Big Lex's office, walk down a small dimly lit hallway, and exits though the door at the end of the hallway. Upon exiting Vince is greeted by the expansive New York underground mecca run by his boss, Big Lex. The scene is filled with a mix of seedy individuals, businessmen, a few police officers roaming about the scene, hoes, pimps, and people from all walks of life who are partaking in illegal gambling, blackmarket trade, enjoying NYUFC prize fights, etc. The camera pans around Big Lex's kingdom as Vince catches sight of a familiar,aging, black man with graying hair who appears to be well built. He spots Vince and Mercedes exiting the doorway and he just shakes his head in disappointment with him. Vince passes by with a large grin on his face and stops in his tracks upon spotting the old man who is glaring back at him. Old man: Well, well, well. You win a few fights around here and now you're suddenly starting to act like a 'hot shot'. Mercedes glances at the old man and then at Vince in confusion. Mercedes: (points at the old man) Papi, who is this? Vince Jones: Baby, he a friend of mine. That's all. Mercedes: (in a tone of disinterest) I see... Old man: (shaking his head at Vince) I really to hate to bother you, but you and I really need to talk about some things here if your (points at Mercedes) new lady friend here doesn't mind. Vince Jones: (to Mercedes) Yeeeeeeah. Uh, he's got a point here, girl. How 'bout you step to the side for a few while V and the old man here have a few words? That cool with you? Mercedes: (rolling her eyes and pouting) Well, if that's what you want, papi. I guess I'll just be hanging around, but don't keep me waiting too long now. I don't have a lot of patience. Mercedes walks away. Vince turns towards the old man and appears to be livid. Vince Jones: So, what the fuck is all this about huh? What we got to talk about here, old man? You just gotta pick the finest times for shit like this, don't ya? Old man: This is a finer time than any for me to set you straight, Vince. Vince Jones: (in confusion) What? What the fuck are you talkin' 'bout, man? What the hell V need to be set straight on here, huh? Do tell. Old man: Alright. I'm gonna hit you square between the eyes (jabs Vince square in the forehead with his pointer finger) with this so I hope you're ready for this. Vince Jones: (chuckles to himself) Oh, this is gonna be good... Old man: (with a serious expression on his face) You're going soft. Vince looks at the old man in shock at what he has just said. He looks to the left and then the right and the back at the old man in confusion. Vince Jones: Let V get this straight here. (points at the old man) You think that V goin' soft? You honestly think that V is goin' soft? Old man: (nods his head) Yes, that's exactly what I'm saying. Vince Jones: (shrugs emphatically) And how you figga that, huh? They just some things that V been pickin' up along the way, man. That's all that shit is. Old man: (nods but is unconvinced) Well, you're slowly getting caught up in these 'things' of yours. You'll only hurt yourself much more than you'll ever help yourself in the long run. Understood? There's gonna come a day when I'm no longer around to help you. If and when that day ever comes I want you to be prepared to handle things on your own. When times get rough I want you to be able to summon your inner strength and get yourself through because even in the roughest of times there's always hope. You've just gotta be able to find it. Vince Jones: (nodding rather sheepishly) Yeah... The camera slowly fades to black. {{End of Flashback}} ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The camera fades back in and we are in the passenger bay of the airplane once again where the stewardess from earlier can be seen gently nudging Vince Jones trying to get him to wake up. Vince finally begins stirring. He stretches a bit, lets out a long yawn, and wipes the sleep from his eyes as he is welcomed back to the land of the living by the stewardess after his slumber.
