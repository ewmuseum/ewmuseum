Cause and Effect

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-8-05 It was pitch black on the driveway which seemed to go on forever. The only light that could be seen was from the mansion, and even then it was only a few lit rooms. The air was muggy and sticky and the mosquitoes were as present as ever, buzzing around and biting everything. On the porch of the mansion two guards were standing there, sporadically slapping at the air. It was a serenity, a disturbing peace. About half way down the drive way trouble was brewing. The bushes were rattling, two figures mumbling in it. “Christ! God damn bugs! Fuck ‘em all!” “Shut up, Liam! Be quiet!” Tim and Liam were sitting there, staring up at the house. They both had dark, concerned looks on their faces, keeping their gaze locked on the front steps. They peered from the bush, keeping themselves well-hidden. “You sure he’s in here, Tim?” “Yes, Liam. We saw him drive in, we saw him park, we saw him get out, and I’m pretty fucking sure we didn’t see him leave. Now, unless Mr. Parsons is superman in his free time, I’m pretty sure he’s still in there.” The two of them went silent for a second as they both seemed to be analyzing the situation. They kept a steady gaze on the two guards, trying to figure out a way to get in. “That Parsons…he makes me sick.” “I know, Liam.” “I mean, that dirty fucking rat! I mean, first he comes through The Nest and is flashing his hoo-hah and what not like he’s trying to be the White Lord himself. Then, before you know it, he’s turning his back on us.” Parsons was running for mayor and, after gaining the support of The Eagle’s Nest, he then told the rest of the city that he was going to turn many of the housing units in the Nest into a large strip mall. The Nest was mad, and they decided that Parsons was to be taken out of the running. So they sent their two best and brightest, Tim and Liam Murphy, to do the job. “Liam, I know you’re angry, but once we do this, you have to stay quiet. You can’t let your emotions get to you. The misses is out, but not for long. So we have to go in, eliminate the rat, and get out.” “I know Tim…it’s just so frustrating. I mean, after all the hard work we do for the Lord, he helps scum like this Parsons make it to the top?” *SMACK!* Liam was quickly on the receiving end of a dope slap from Tim, who kept a finger pointed in Liam’s face. “The White Lord does not help a rat like that! He took his power from the Devil, Liam, and we’re here to show Parsons and the city that the Devil cannot hold a candle to what the White Lord can do. Now, put on your ski masks and lock and load.” The two pulled masks over their head and pulled out 9mm silenced pistols which they cocked back. They then started creeping up towards the front steps until they were close enough to hit them accurately but far enough to not be seen. Sitting in the bushes, Tim leaned close to Liam and started whispering. “Allright, Liam, you get the one on the right, I’ll take out the one on the left. Once I give the word, start firing. Make sure you only land body shots, and try to take him down quick before he can do anything. If you can land one in the head, that’ll do.” “Sounds good. On your word.” Slowly, the two lifted their guns and aimed them through the bushes. They held them steadily, keeping their sights on the two guards. “NOW!” *PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO!* Six shots were fired, two from Tim’s pistol and four from Liam’s. Tim landed both shots in the head, while Liam landed all his in the guard’s torso. While the guard Tim shot died, Liam’s was still writhing. “Quick! We have to finish him!” The two scurried up the steps and, making sure no one was looking in the windows, the unloaded bullets into the guard who, after a few rounds, stopped moving. The two brothers stood without a word and quietly creaked the door open, looking in. Nothing. Once they stepped in they looked around the house and kept quiet. Tim brought a finger to his mouth, signaling Liam to be quiet. The two waited. Nothing. Nothing. SLAM! Both heads darted towards a light in another room. Liam looked at Tim who waved an arm, bringing Liam with him as he crept towards the light. The room, apparently, was the kitchen, and in there Parsons was standing in a blue robe making himself a drink. The brothers snuck up behind him and, at about three steps away, Parsons heard them and turned towards them. “What the…” *SMACK!* Parsons was dropped to the ground with a pistol whip, lying there and moaning. Liam grabbed him and sat him on the counter, placing a pistol against his jaw. “What do you want from me? What is it? Money? Here, take anything! Please, don’t kill me!” Parsons was shaking with fear, his fat gut jutting out from his robe. His eyes kept on shifting back and forth as the sweat beat down his forehead. “This is bigger than Money, Parsons.” Tim’s voice was stern yet focused, his gun at his side while Liam kept his on Parsons. There was a small moment of silence, when, suddenly… “Baby, what are you doing…OH MY GOD!” A woman who looked more like a hooker then a female walked into the door way in pretty much nothing and then tried to dart out, only to be foiled by a few bullets from Tim’s gun. She dropped like a sack of bones, he brains splattering all over the place. Tim then turned and pressed his gun hard into Parson’s head, pushing it against the wall. “So, let me get this straight. Not only are you a liar, but you commit adultery as well?” “Who are you guys? What do you want?” The two chuckled and flashed a look at each other before simultaneously putting both their weapons in an eye of Parson’s, one barrel in each eye. They held the guns there while Parsons began to breathe heavier and heavier. “We’re from The Eagle’s Nest. We’re here to show you and the rest of the city what happens if you try to cross us.” *PLOO! PLOO!* Parson’s brains were splattered against the back wall as his body became limp and fell forward onto the ground. Tim and Liam searched through his pockets and took out a wad of cash and pocketed it, laughing as they did. As the exited the kitchen, they came to find something horrible. “FREEZE!” It wasn’t the police, because Tim and Liam wouldn’t draw their weapons and keep them aimed if it was. Instead, it was Parson’s wife, who had a beautiful innocent beauty to her. The thing that wasn’t beautiful was the hand cannon she was holding that was bigger than she was. She kept it aimed at the Martins as they had their weapons aimed at her. “PUT THE GUN DOWN! DROP THE FUCKING WEAPON! DROP THE MOTHER FUCKING WEAPON!” “*between sobs*…What’d…what’d you do…what’d you do to me husband?...” “DROP THE FUCKING WEAPON! DROP! IT NOW!” *BANG!* *PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO! PLOO!* She made the mistake of being a poor shot, missing horrible as she fired one shot at the brothers, who immediately opened fired and brought her down. Once they stopped firing they sprinted out of the house and into the darkness, leaving the door open in the house. ------------------------------------------------------------- It had been years since Liam and I took out that rat and now they finally caught up to us. Five years, to be exact. After we took out Parsons our very own representative from the Nest took power. Then, things started happening. The police took investigation, then the FBI. The FBI actually made a few arrests, though they were on false leads. They even thought Funny Man Bob Smith had something to do with it. That’s how off they were, that they were thinking that a low-life construction worker was involved in the coup. Somehow, someway, they traced something back to us. Things were puzzling, though. The FBI wasn’t acting like the FBI I had seen in the movies. They weren’t using their super-whatever power to do this or that. I could tell they were drawing on nothing when they were interviewing me. “Mr. Martin,” the agent would say as he stared me down in the dark room, the bright light shining in my face. “Do you remember the late Mr. Parsons?” I laughed, chuckling to myself which caught the agent’s attention. “What’s so funny, Martin?” “Well,” I started, “That’s a stupid question. You’d have to be in comatose to not know about Parsons. And, even then, I still think you’d have heard a little bit.” *SMACK!* He ran over and struck me right in the jaw and out of the chair. It was a hard, stiff hit, caught me by surprise. I didn’t even think about striking back, though. I didn’t need that trouble…I’m sure I’d go away for a longggg time if I struck a member of the FBI. “Enough of the funny shit,” he screamed, “We know you had something to do with that! Five people dead…those guards, Parsons’ pet, his wife, and Parsons himself, and we want to know what you had to do with it! We have information, and I’ll pry the rest of it with my fists if I gotta.” I shrugged before I said a thing. “Beats me, sir. I don’t even know how to hold a gun.” *SMACK!* He knocked me out of my chair again and then slammed me back into it. This went on for hours until, finally, they brought me into another room where Liam was sitting. I thought I was looking rough, but he was looking horrible. His left eye was black and blue, his face covered in blood. The tattoos covering his arms seemed to be a red ink now. He was beaten up. “Jesus,” I said, “What happened to you?” “Oh, they asked a few questions, and I gave ‘em a few answers they didn’t like.” He smiled at me, all his teeth thankfully in place. He had the fire in his eye and the spirit of the White Lord within him. He was strong. “Yeah,” I said, “Me too. What kind of shit did they ask?” “Fucked up shit. They asked if hitting that old lady back in France was anything like murdering that whore in the mansion. It was fucked up. They pretty much just beat me up, though. I’m pretty sure they lost the key’s to their weight room.” As he winked at me I let out a laugh when, abruptly, two agents burst into the room and started beating us. We were knocked out of our chairs and, while we were on the ground, our handcuffs were undone. Then, we were dragged out of the room and down a hallways. Not knowing where we were we were quickly thrown out into an alleyway into a few trash bags. “We’ll be back, shit heads. Just know we’re watching you.” And like that, they slammed the doors and went on their way. I got up and helped Liam to his feet. He had a slight limp, and as I queried about it, he shook me off. “Well,” he said, with a smile, “They were nice.” I laughed as I helped Liam walk out of the alley way and into the street, hailing down a cab to bring us back to the Nest… -----([([([END FEED])])])-----
