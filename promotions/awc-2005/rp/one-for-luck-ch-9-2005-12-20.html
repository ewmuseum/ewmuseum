One for Luck, Ch. 9

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th of Dec. One for Luck, Ch. 9 (Prometheus - And so it continues) -A flashback- *** “A man more sinned against than sinning” The wind whistled against the open shutter, clashing its wooden planks against the soft timber lining of the opened window. The rain fluttered through its opening, drenching the moving blind as the wind billowed behind, creating a ghostly façade in the hotel room. Adam Dick swayed in motion, his hands buried into his pockets as he stood, smug, gazing at Kelli, who sat rigged on the bed, her cheeks were a rosy red. Her mascara running. “Why?” she muttered behind the droplets of tears and the nervous squeak in her voice. Adam Dick chuckled, finding Kelli’s heartache amusing. He knew it was time to tear Pierce Lavelle’s world to sunder and now that Kelli had managed to open the key to Pierce’s heart, it was perfect timing. “Where is he?” she muttered again, this time moving and wiping away the tears, leaving a line of mascara across her cheek. “Good question!” Adam Dick said with a cocky tone and opened up a bottle of wine and poured himself a glass. Kelli remained seated; the rain continuously fluttering through the open window into the dimly lit room, where two omniscient people remained, foreshadowing all the truths behind each whim and plot that had happened in Los Angeles within the last month and a bit. “What did you do to him?” she questioned. This time Kelli’s posture wasn’t slumped or fragile, she was standing, her fists clenched. Her heart pounding in her chest. She had never stood up to Adam Dick before; even though she was a hard-ass woman on the outside, Adam Dick was a dangerous man in her eyes. “Kelli! Kelli! Kelli!” Adam Dick muttered, swirling the wine glass within his grasp. “You should choose your words carefully,” he said and took a sip. “You promised this would end …” Kelli had now fallen back into her weak state she had started off in. She was feeling insecure and now worried for the safety of Pierce. Adam Dick placed the wine glass onto the small glass coffee table and leant against the wooden sill along the counter-top of the kitchen partition. “Oh, it will end, once Pierce Lavelle falls and I get what I want…” Adam Dick said and moved closer toward Kelli, who remained seated on the bed, more tears running down her face. The moon glowed through the gaping curtain as the wind began to bellow and thunder lit the skies. Los Angeles was in for a storm, and Adam Dick had played both Kelli and Pierce like pawns. “You see, I’ve ruined his career, we’ve set him up and now all we need to do is break his heart, so that I may take what is rightfully mine,” Adam Dick stood in front of Kelli and bent down, whispering in her ear. “His title and his sanity…” Adam Dick straightened himself and landed a kiss onto Kelli’s lips. She struggled, slumped over and grabbed the phone on the bedside table and swung it in Adam Dick’s direction. He fell forward and grabbed the side of his face. “You stupid cow!” he yelled. “I’ll scream…” Kelli shivered, staring at the anger in Adam’s face. “You think they’d hear you? These walls are made with reinforced concrete…” Adam Dick moved toward the mirror and checked the side of his face, it was red, but nothing had been damaged, but he knew it would bruise. “That’s going to leave a nasty bruise,” he said as he stared at his bleak reflection in the mirror, only to find Pierce Lavelle stood in the hall, staring at the two in the bedroom. “…Adam…” Pierce muttered, not realising quite what was going on. Kelli froze; she didn’t know what to do. Her heart was now jumping out of her chest and into her throat, the beats getting louder and harder. She was perspiring with nerves as Adam Dick turned around slowly, gazing at the mess that Pierce Lavelle was in. His clothing ripped, his arm in a sling and his head scratched and bruised. Pierce Lavelle looked as though he had been in a war zone, and came out the wrong end of it. Thunder ripped across the sky, flashing a dart of light into the hotel room. Pierce Lavelle remained shadowed, standing with the rose still in his hand. He gazed at Kelli on the bed and then again at Adam. “…Kelli…” Pierce muttered again. She lifted her head and tried to speak but Adam quelled her by placing his hand on her shoulder. “Took you long enough!” Adam Dick said with a grin on his face. “What?” Pierce said with an uncontrollable emotion that was welling up inside him. The very man that had turned Pearl against him was standing in his hotel room. “You don’t get it, do you Purse?” Adam Dick said, picking up the wine glass and taking another sip. “A lot of strange things have been happening to you in Los Angeles. Some, a little bit too coincidental, don’t ya think?” Adam remained standing, continuously staring at the wine glass in his hand. His master piece had now been created; all he needed to do was add the finishing touches. Destroy Pierce’s sanity and all that he holds dear. “Adam…please…don’t d-!” Kelli tried to speak, but Adam chucked the remainder of the wine in her direction, splashing her in the face. Pierce Lavelle went rigid, he moved forward but Adam Dick held out his hand and smiled. “Hold on, you haven’t let me finish…” he said and leant against the wall. He soon became cocky and arrogant and who wouldn’t, he had created the perfect ploy out of something that couldn’t be done - an evil Einstein! “I wouldn’t go to her aid, lover-boy! Kelli has been playing you from the start, Purse. I hired her to follow you, of course you falling in love with her was a bonus as it allowed us to get to you even more…” Adam crossed his arms and stared at the drenched curtain. “I…” Kelli muttered. “Purse, you are quite the idiot aren’t you! If you had have done your research you would have known that Kelli used to work for me in CORE. She gave me all the information I needed on what you were following, but it doesn’t end here, Chump!” Pierce Lavelle looked as though he had been hit with a truck. He stood, his face clenched and the rose petals falling to the floor, fluttering in the air until they gently breezed the carpet, he was stuck, he couldn’t move. “I was the man that got the ball rolling. I am the mystery caller that called you in Harrisburg. James and I had a sort of arrangement. You fell right into our plan and followed him to Hancook Park; of course, I didn’t want to get caught, so I gave your position up…” Pierce turned sharply, his hood falling off his head and landing on his back. “Oh! It. Gets. Better,” Adam Dick said slowly. “Who do you think was calling Kelli away all the time? Everything you learnt, she gave me, but of course, she fell for your stupid ass and soon became very evasive of me, but a little mention of Zack and she proves to be very obedient,” Adam Dick chuckled to himself, enjoying watching Pierce look like a rabbit caught in headlights. “You pretended to be Rubin Demorto,” Pierce Lavelle said, now finding that everything was fitting into place. He could feel his heart sink, his mind in a state of denial as he looked at Kelli. “NO!” Adam Dick said with sarcasm. “You don’t say! You see Purse,” Adam Dick took a breath and unfolded his arms. “I left you bread crumbs and you followed, so did your senile grandfather, now that was something I didn’t plan on, the under cover agent from Omnikon, but still, it was a minor glitch in the bigger picture. Me, destroying you…I was watching you in the Omnikon building, Kelli came back for me, but you got shot at and then taken prisoner. How do you think I knew about the torture and the hospital treatment you received?” Adam Dick looked toward Kelli and smiled. Kelli turned away, more tears and whimpering coming from her. She had never wanted this to happen, she wished she had never met Pierce and helped Adam ruin his life, he didn’t deserve it. “Then the pay-per view comes along and again, you managed to beat me and retain the belt, but what makes it harder is that you were on the drugs. So, I decided I wouldn’t just stop with your life, I’d move onto your career. It was the best FRESH I have been in, watching your career crumble and the fans boo you. But Kelli says you’re clean, but who can believe her?” Adam Dick moved forward toward Pierce Lavelle, he was face to face with Pierce, but Pierce did nothing, he remained frozen, broken and hurt. “As Rubin Demorto it allowed me to get GOONE and Omnikon after you more, and allowed me to evade their watchful eye. How was your talk with the two hit-men, it seems they failed…” Adam Dick had a very large grin on his face as he poked Lavelle’s wounded shoulder. He watched Lavelle’s eyes widen with shock and the realisation that one man could actually tear his world to sunder. Adam Dick moved passed Pierce, brushing his shoulder and slowly left the room. The thunder still flashing across the sky, creating bursts of light. Pierce remained frozen, Kelli sat rigid on the bed and Adam Dick had a sinister grin on his face as he closed the door and left. The door slammed leaving a resonating sound echoing throughout the room flowing around its motionless bodies that remained frozen in time. Pierce Lavelle fell down into the couch, letting go of the rose stem. Kelli turned around, wiped her eyes and relaxed a little now that Adam Dick had left, but she knew no matter how hard she tried, things were not going to improve. She had helped destroy Pierce’s world. Pierce stared at the wine glass on the coffee table in front of him - the very one Adam Dick had drunk out of. Pierce rose, lifting the glass with him and threw it across the room, yelling as he did so and watched it shatter against the opposite wall. Kelli ducked as shards of glass scattered throughout the room. “Pierce…” Kelli cried out and jumped off the bed toward a fuming, broken hearted and shattered man. “Don’t…” Pierce mumbles and pushes her away and looks in the other direction. “WHY?” he yells, continuously looking away from her. “I… I didn’t want to. I- I - I never meant to hurt you. Adam…He… Threatened me with posting bail on Zack, I had to…I’m sor-” “DON’T SAY YOUR SORRY,” Pierce turned to Kelli and looked at her in the face; tears on both of his cheeks. He had watched his world fall apart and opened his heart up to the very person that helped, that acted as an accomplice in the destruction of everything he held dear and tried to help with. “Pierce,” Kelli grabbed his good arm. “Don’t let him ruin you, don’t let him in…” she said with desperation. “He’s already done that and you helped…YOU HELPED!” Pierce pulled his arm away sharply and moved toward the closet and pulled out his duffle bag and began stuffing it with his clothes and other belongings. Kelli stood, her hands over her mouth as tears continued to come from both Kelli and Pierce. Pierce stood, staring at his duffle bag and all his other clothes, things that didn’t seem so important now. He had been destroyed, royally. Adam Dick was good, but this was his best yet. Pierce had watched him mess with Tim Shipley, but he had never gone as far as to destroy his life, but he had with Pierce and it’s what made him question…why? “Pierce, please…don’t go,” Kelli cried as she watched Pierce zip up his duffle bag. Pierce took a step back and stared at the dimly lit room, then at Kelli, who remained standing, tears dripping from her chin, her eyes gazing at Pierce. She was pleading with him, but no matter what she tried, he wasn’t listening, she knew he could never look at her the same, not right now. “You can stay here. I’ll find somewhere else…” Pierce said silently and moved away from her, ignoring her pleas as he made his way to the door; he stared at the door and the door handle, this was it for him, he knew it. His life was ruined. Pierce opened the door and stared at the brightly lit corridor. Kelli ran up behind him. “I … love …you,” she said, but it had no effect. Pierce shut the door. Kelli turned around and leant against the door. Pierce was gone, she knew it. She began to cry hysterically and slid down the door. Pierce stood on the outside, listening to Kelli’s cries of heartache and pain and he knew he couldn’t go back. He sighed and made his way down the hall toward the elevator, wiping his own tears. He couldn’t go back, not now, not ever. *** -Back to present day- After the FRESH show Desperation had become a theme of many different tragedies, plays, television shows and movies and Pierce was experiencing it first hand. Was this to be his life? He had become ‘nothing’ and was alone in the world now. His acts on FRESH were seen by many as desperation and as hurt, but the truth had been revealed about Adam Dick and that was good enough for him, he just didn’t know whether he could do what needed to be done. Pierce had turned to the drink once more that night that his world had fallen to pieces. He was depressed and alcohol was his only supplement for the pain. As he stood in front of the mirror in the dingy motel room, he couldn’t recognise himself. His eyes were a blood shot red, he had about two weeks growth of stubble on his face and he had shaved his hair. He was a different man, broken and slipping into madness, the descent of many that had nothing left, but he knew there could be some hope at the end of the desperate time, it was all that kept him going. He turned around, dressed in nothing but a pair of black sweatpants and removed the dark crimson coloured bandage that was draped over his shoulder. He had opened three stitches during his match against Jack Murphy - that match was the very first time the fans had remained silent, the final blow to his fading career. Pierce applied some iodine to the wound and cringed with pain as it stung on impact. This pain was nothing compared to pain he was feeling mentally. He hadn’t slept in four nights and was becoming increasingly stressed. A knock broke his chain of thought and the familiar voice of Sarah Kennedy rang against the door; she had apologised on FRESH to Pierce, but he couldn’t open the door, he couldn’t let her see him this way. He knew no matter what, if Sarah Kennedy saw him like this, she would report it back to Pearl and it seemed Pearl was still perturbed about the sides he should be on. “Pierce, open the door, will you?” Sarah Kennedy muttered from behind the fake-timber door. She drilled her hands against the door and became persistent. “Alright!” Pierce mumbled and put the iodine bottle down. He opened the door on its latch and Sarah Kennedy forced her way through the slim gap that Pierce had managed to create. Of course the latch wouldn’t hold, the screws were unscrewing and the hinges weren’t up to exact maintenance, well that’s what you get for staying in a cheap-dingy motel he thought to himself as he watched the latch fall to the floor. “Pierce, you look …” Sarah Kennedy stood perplexed at Pierce’s visage. “Terrible, I know,” Pierce said and began to wipe away the iodine and blood that had trickled down his chest. “What do you want, Sarah?” he asked, staring at the cloth that had now turned an orangey-reddish colour with blood and iodine mixed. “I came to see if you were okay,” she said behind her long golden locks. “I guess by appearance, you aren’t,” she said again and grabbed a fresh towel, dipped it into the basin and placed it on the back of Pierce’s shoulder where the bullet had emerged. “I don’t need your help,” Pierce said defiantly, but he knew he did need somebody to talk to. “Well, you are getting help,” Sarah said with her own hint of defiance. “Pierce, you need to see a doctor, you are bleeding and your stitches are loose,” Sarah Kennedy said and placed the soaking towel back onto the table next to the basin. The flashing neon light that advertised the motel glowed through the black blinds, highlighting the two figures in the run down room. Pierce picked up some of the spare bandage that the medic on FRESH had given him. He began to wrap it around his shoulder, without Sarah’s help. “Why are you here Sarah, really?” Pierce said as he pulled the bandage over and under his arm and shoulder and around again. “I know it isn’t an interview you want and I can‘t believe it’s because you were worried about me,” Pierce muttered as he held the end of the bandage in his mouth and pulled out the small fastening clip. “You didn’t show up at the five star hotel after the event and with what happened on FRESH - I wanted to check you were okay, I was worried about you,” Sarah said. “Well, you know I’m not,” Pierce replied. Pierce sat down on the small pull-out bed and pulled on a t-shirt with great difficulty. He remained silent and pulled out a packet of cigarettes - he had reverted back to the alcohol and cigarettes, the only way to calm his nerves and distract him from his pain, also a great way for serosis of the liver and lung cancer, but he wasn’t thinking straight. Sarah Kennedy marched forward and pulled them off of him. “Snap out of it, Pierce,” she said with defiance and a caring tone. Pierce knew that you had to be cruel to be kind sometimes, but he just wanted to be left alone, he decided against arguing with the blonde interviewer and remained seated. His mind flashing back to that night. The sound of Kelli crying behind the hotel door had stayed with him for the last week; he couldn’t let her go... He still cared for her. Pierce rested his head in hands and began to think of other things, to take his mind off of Kelli. “Sarah…” Pierce said from behind his hands. Sarah Kennedy sat down next to Pierce on the small bed and knew Pierce needed comforting. He had been through a lot and was not his usual kind, confident self. “Yes,” she said and placed a hand on Pierce’s good shoulder. “Promise me you won’t tell Pearl about this, I can’t have him know what a mess I am in, he might go back to Adam Dick about it. Adam Dick has already ruined everything else in my life, him knowing he‘s damaged me would only excel him,” Pierce had a squeaky tone in his voice. “I think he already knows that, you did confront him on FRESH … and …” Sarah knew this wasn’t really what Pierce was asking. He just didn’t want Pearl knowing where he was, he didn’t want anyone knowing where he was. Sarah Kennedy nodded to herself, a frequent thing she did, and a way of noting what not to do and what to do. She knew this was important to him and didn’t blame him for his appearance or his mental state. She had had the unfortunate pleasure of running into Adam Dick a couple of times, but to see a man in such a frail, broken and weak state that Pierce was in, it just showed how terrible Adam Dick could be. Pierce straightened up and got off the bed, leaving Sarah Kennedy seated in her expensive suit and high heels. She remained seated, despite the fact the bed covers probably had never been washed, but it didn’t bother her too much, it was only a two thousand dollar suit! Pierce knelt down beside his duffle bag and pulled out some pain killers and popped two back for his increasing migraine and the insomnia that he felt was creeping up on him, he was about to speak to Sarah once more when her phone rang. “Yes, I’ll be right there,” Sarah said and shut the phone. She stood up and placed her phone into her black handbag and made her way to the door. “Promise me you’ll see a doctor about those stitches Pierce,” she said. Pierce nodded and watched her leave. He was alone again and the nights sky was drawing closer, but it would only be another night he didn’t sleep, another night of remembering the pain he was feeling and the hurt of betrayal. He packed his bag again and found the razor and foam he had bought in the 99c store down the road. He could no longer stay in this room and wallow in self-pity, that would be exactly what Adam Dick would want. *** Los Angeles Los Angeles, the city of angels, or so they say, was a hive of life as people scurried in and out of the mainstream shops buying last minute Christmas presents and jumping in for the early sales. The roads were a wonderland of dazzling lights and decorations, the snow gently grazed the ground as it fluttered around the highest sky scrapers and descended like little tiny feathers to land on the surface of L.A.’s streets. The winter storm had passed and the cold winter weather was blowing in from the North. It was going to be a ‘white Christmas’ in Los Angeles so says the weather man on the radio in the G.A.P store. John Lavelle is dressed in an expensive suit, with a trench coat and trilby hat on, with little flakes of snow resting on his shoulder. Some might think he had a bad case of dandruff, but then everyone would… Susanne Lavelle stood in the dressing room, trying on as many different gowns and dresses and skirts that the shop attendant could find. Of course, it was only John’s money she was spending. John Lavelle looked around for a seat and heard his watch beep, it meant it was time for another pill. He had been diagnosed with a serious heart condition four months ago after he had felt his first heart attack, since then he has been taking special pills everyday, but today he couldn’t find them, he was panicking and unsure what would happen if he didn’t take his medication. “Hunny,” Susanne’s voice cried from behind the cream curtain in the dressing room. “I’m going to need at least a size 8 in this, the size 10 is too big,” she said again, placing her hand around the curtain and removing it. Susanne moved forward, dressed in a size 10 dress that hung loosely over her feminine features, for most women a size 10 would be either too small or just perfect, but she found it was too big. “Hunny! You listening to me?” Susanne said, starring at the mirror, as she subconsciously checked if her ass was too big in the dress, which it wasn’t, it was barely noticeable. John Lavelle was gasping for air behind the clothing rack. He had fallen off his chair and was experiencing an excruciating pain in his chest and arm. He rattled the clothing rack to try and gain somebody’s attention. Susanne jumped and noticed John lying on the floor, in pain, gasping for air. She ran around the clothing rack in the expensive dress and fell to the floor. She too, was in a panic, she had never been prepared for things such as heart attacks. “Where are your pills?” Susanne said, rooting through each pocket in John’s jacket and suit coat. They were nowhere to be found. A young attendant ran around the counter and came to the scene of a distraught woman sat on the floor next to an old man having a heart attack. “Oh my god! M’am, is your father alright?” she asked. Her trainee badge in big bold writing on her chest. “He’s my husband!” Susanne said with anger in her eyes. “He’s having a heart attack, call an AMBULANCE!” Susanne yelled. John Lavelle could feel his heart straining in his chest, he could no longer feel his right arm, but he couldn’t go yet, not now - he thought to himself as he stared at Susanne and then at the snow falling to the floor and gently breezing into the shop. His grandson, Pierce Lavelle, couldn’t be left alone to face what he had found in Los Angeles, what he feared was he might be too late. He needed to get a message to Pierce. He stared at Susanne and with his good hand pointed to the inside pocket of his trench coat. She didn’t catch on and held his hand. John Lavelle could no longer talk, his heart attack was stopping his mobility and ability to talk. Before he could point to the pocket again some paramedics rushed in and gently moved Susanne out of the way. They placed a breathing mask on him and loaded him onto a stretcher. “Hold on sir,” one paramedic said with a caring tone as he gazed into the eighty year olds grey eyes. John Lavelle couldn’t take any chances, he removed the mask and turned to Susanne. “…If anything happens to me…” John used his only free arm and removed a letter. “…give this to…Pierce…” He soon faded as he handed the letter to Susanne. “He’s gone into shock,” the other paramedic yelled as they raced John Lavelle into the snow covered streets toward the ambulance. Susanne stood in the snow, staring at the letter that read “For Pierce”. She gazed at her husband, then again at the letter and dipped the letter back into John’s coat. *** The final flight to Los Angeles began to board as Pierce ran through the terminal, his duffle bag slung over his shoulder. He had shaved, dressed in some jeans, a shirt and a coat and managed to get his stitches seen to, but he couldn’t keep with deadlines for the life of him. “Final boarding call for flight 307 to Los Angeles,” the voice on the overhead intercom rang out. Pierce mumbled to himself as he hopped over some luggage that lay on the floor; as he was running down the terminal his mobile began to ring, he stopped dead in his tracks, gazing at the gate for his flight and at his mobile. “WAIT!” Pierce yelled as he watched the lady almost close the door. “You are late,” she said. “Yeah, thanks for pointing that out,” Pierce said and handed her his tickets and continued running down the corridor toward the plane door. -Meanwhile in Los Angeles- The doors burst open as John Lavelle was raced down the corridor toward the emergency room where medics were waiting for him. He had slipped into unconsciousness, his heart failing. His shirt had been ripped off his chest as the paramedic was now seated on top of him trying to perform CPR to keep the heart moving, it wasn’t working, but he wasn’t giving up. “One…Two…Three…” the paramedic blew into John Lavelle’s mouth. “Come on…” he yelled in frustration. The double doors opened and the paramedic hopped off and helped load John Lavelle’s limp body onto the spare bed. The medics now began to charge the AED machine as the other doctor continued the cardiopulmonary resuscitation. “Clear,” the medic said and delivered a shock to John Lavelle’s chest. {/\---/\/\/\---------------------/\} John’s body bounced and there was only a slight murmur on the machine . The medic returned to the CPR and continued with his counting and breathing. The other medic charged the AED machine once more. “Clear,” the medic said again and delivered another shock. {-------------------------------} The machine showed he had flat lined - the ringing that many doctors had to face, as did the families. Susanne stood behind the double doors, a mobile in her hand as tears raced down her cheeks. The paramedic continued with the CPR, but the other medics had given up, they knew there was no chance of resuscitation. “Time of death 1:40 pm,” the medic said and removed her gloves. “That’s enough Luke,” a nurse came from behind the paramedic and placed a hand on his shoulder. The paramedic, Luke, stood back and took a breath as he switched off the heart machine. “Somebody should speak to the daughter outside,” the other medic said and pulled a blanket over John Lavelle’s body and face. “That’s his wife,” Luke replied. “I’ll tell her,” he said and moved toward the door where Susanne stood. She knew what was coming, but couldn’t accept it, she burst into tears and fell into the arms of the paramedic. Susanne straightened up and wiped away the tears, so not to ruin the brand new dress she had bought. She placed the phone into her handbag and asked to go to the family room, she knew Pierce would find out soon enough. -Meanwhile, the plane touches down in LA- Pierce stood on the snowy tarmac of Los Angeles international airport and gazed up into the white abyss. His eyes were a deep blood shot red, he had taken one too many painkillers and with the lack of sleep he was slipping into madness, but he needed to take care of two more things in Los Angeles and then he would be gone, that’s all he kept saying to himself as he braved the tears and emotion the city had brought him. Two nights ago, when he stood in his room and stared at himself, he had become ‘nothing’ than a man with a failing career and a broken heart, but he knew there could be some hope throughout this. He had managed to hold back the insanity and lack of sleep with a couple of vodka’s, but now there was nothing he could do, but hold on just a little longer. He stepped toward the taxi depot, but found himself stood in front of a large limo. Charles stood in front of him, he was still dressed in his posh chauffer suit, but the snow had created a white cloak above his head and shoulders. “Mr. Lavelle, I have to bring you to Los Angeles emergency centre,” he said and opened the door. The last time Pierce had got into a car with two men he had been abducted and shot at and almost died, all because of Adam Dick. What’s to say Adam Dick wasn’t paying off Charles to do the same. “Please, Mr. Lavelle,” Charles said again. Pierce couldn’t, no matter how hard he tried, he couldn’t get rid of the nasty feeling he felt in the pit of his stomach, something wasn’t right. “Mr. Lavelle, we don’t have time to dilly-dally,” Charles said with a stern look on his face. “I…what’s going on?” Pierce said, nerves kicking in. “Your grandfather has had a heart attack,” Charles said and slid into the driver seat and began to start the engine. Pierce knew Adam Dick was twisted, but he wouldn’t do something like this, Pierce put the paranoia aside and slid into the seat. He just hoped he wasn’t too late. *** The limo came to a screeching hault outside the main doors for Los Angeles Emergency Centre. Pierce flew out of the limo passenger door, before Charles’s had managed to switch off the engine. He raced towards the main doors, whilest skidding on the snow and ice that had formed on the black tarmac surface. The doors burst open as a gail of wind and snow found its way into the clammy hall of LA medical. Pierce stared dumbfounded at the silence and the lack of movement. Christmas was supposed to be the biggest time for accidents, yet the place was silent. He continued forward until he found Susanne seated in the family room, with paramedic Luke. “Where is he?” Pierce demanded as he stared at Susanne. “Pierce…he’s…” Susanne muttered behind tears. “He’s what? In surgery? In intensive care? What?” Pierce demanded again. “Okay, easy kid,” the paramedic said. Pierce gazed toward the paramedic and then toward Susanne, who had tears flowing down her cheeks into her lap. You didn’t have to be a genius to put two and two together. The only other time Pierce had been in the family room and watched others crying was when his grandmother Gloria had died from lung failure. “No…” Pierce said in denial. “No…” he said again and raced out of the room and down the hall where he saw a body, covered by a blanket in the emergency room. Still in a daze and under heavy medication himself he pushed the doors open and moved toward the body. His hands quivering. He stood next to the dead body. He placed a hand on the blanket and watched a small white enveloped fall to the floor. He bent over and picked it up, his eyes widened with the name on the letter. His own name stared back at him, which meant the man under the blanket was his grandfather. He took a step back and lifted the blanket from the head to the chest. His grandfathers pale face stared back at him, his eyes shut, his lips blue. Pierce couldn’t maintain it any longer, he broke into tears with shock and the realisation hitting him all at once. He held his grandfathers hand and continuously cried. “Pierce…” Susanne said from behind him. Pierce continued to hold his grandfathers hand…it was cold and motionless. “How?” Pierce asked, his back turned to Susanne. “Heart attack,” Susanne managed to say with the feeling of nausea growing on her as she stared at her dead husband. “…” Pierce could no longer speak. He stood up, placed the letter into his pocket and wiped his eyes. He said goodbye to his grandfather and pulled the blanket back over his frail face. “Pierce…where are you go-…”Susanne didn’t have time to ask questions. Pierce marched on down the corridor and out into the snow where he let out a large scream, toward what, I have no clue. He headed toward the limo and Charles immediately understood what had happened and sympathised with Pierce. “The nearest off licence, Charles…” Pierce said, tears in his eyes. “Sir?” Charles questioned. “Just do it…” Pierce said and got into the car.
