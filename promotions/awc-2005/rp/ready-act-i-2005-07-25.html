Ready?: Act I

25 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Rumors/E/A/D/Y -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- A view of a breezeway is shown, a glassed in space with white tile built to connect two parts of a building together. Past the glass glimpses of students engaging in their own activities could be seen, along with green foliage of evergreen trees and small plots of Jasmine and Amaryllises adding color to the exterior of the school. “You won’t believe what my mother told me last night.” A young man’s voice spoke over the scene, though he himself was nowhere in sight. “What?” a high pitched girlish voice asked with intrigue. “She was talking to me about sex and crap like that, and told me if I didn’t watch myself Death himself would come after me!” With its bright and chipper appearance however the breezeway is a cold, foreboding location. The faint fluorescent lighting above was kept to a minimal with the thought that sunlight would overshadow it, but it instead made everything inside look pale and lifeless, the two ends of the breezeway out of the lights reach and thus gives an eerie sensation upon walking from one end to the other. “Death?” the girl repeated, traces of fear squeaking in her voice. Another voice responded, a girl’s voice with a lower pitch closer to the boy’s. “That black-robed person… we saw on the roof the other day… “ “Geez, not again,” the boy said exasperated. Footsteps could be heard approaching from one of the dim areas near the breezeway. They were slow, each step made gingerly, as if they didn’t want to make such a clutter with their rubber soled sneakers. Soon from the semi-darkness stepped a young Japanese girl, long dark hair trailing down around her collar with hints of auburn highlights. Still in her school uniform of navy blue skirt and white shirt with baggy white socks and blue and white sneakers, the girl was pretty, her youth only bringing about the more innocent and somewhat gracefulness in her appearance. She stopped at the edge of the breezeway, as if she didn’t dare step into the light. Instead she stood there before the hallway, staring ahead with a distant look on her face. “Shut up, Jinnai!!” the second girl protested, “I’m telling you…that was Death we saw! They say this Death appears in the form of a girl, and tempts people with sex and their desires. Then at the peak of their happiness she kills them… and disappears without a trace.” A third female’s voice spoke up, a voice more mature than the other three, but still told her young age of 16. “Hmm, sounds like the tale of the snow woman to me. Lured men to their deaths like the Sirens of Roman mythology. It’s funny how creatures transcend cultures. Everyone’s got their own versions of ghosts, zombies, demons and monsters.” The second girl snapped back, “If that’s the case then there’s got to be some truth to it all!” Then the girl began to walk ahead, her steps still slow. She took her time getting halfway down the path, her eyes lowering from the darker hallway ahead to the tiles below. The light above seemed to make the tiles brighter, the girl stepping her tiny foot into each ceramic block as she shuffled forward. She gripped the handle of her Japanese style school briefcase with both hands in front of her, the case pushing out a little with each leg stepping forward and resting back against her legs. The first girl spoke up softly, as if all this talk about death and monsters spooked her. “Uh… this Death… what did you say its name was, Ishikawa?” Finally the girl had made it the entire way across the breezeway. She stopped at the mark where brightness met dimness, just as she did at the other end of the path. The girl looked back, her eyes checking the area behind her. It seemed somewhat like she was looking for something, or even waiting for someone. “Masonna,” was the ominous response of the girl named Ishikawa. The girl sighed and continued on through the school’s hallway, making a left into the next hall. “Tachibana, what do you think?” She who had taken such ginger to cross a hallway, the one named Tachibana, stopped dead in her tracks as she turned that corner, looking on to find the three girls and boy standing there. A blonde haired tanned Japanese girl held a dustpan while a short haired girl with a tomboyish cut swept dust into it, and nearby a dark brown haired girl with glasses was tying the ends of a trash bag into a knot. Leaning against the side of the wall with sling-pack hugging his neck was the male, Jinnai. Tachibana looked on with a surprised look. “Uh… about what?” Jinnai grinned at her naivety. “The Masonna, you think it exists or is it just another fairy tale?” She looked around at their faces before shaking her head. “I… I don’t know.” Then she bowed deeply and began to back up slowly. “I’m sorry, I have to go. See you tomorrow.” Tachibana turned on her heels and headed the opposite direction, leaving the four friends with confused expressions. Of course the blonde’s expression looked dimwitted from the beginning. “Strange,” the blonde said in a slow, though high-pitched voice as her head tilted to the side slightly. The spectacled girl shook her head, eyes lowering back to the bag she tied. “Not really, Kawaii. Tachibana’s been having trouble with her studies lately, so she has to go to cram school every night.” “Yeah,” the tomboy agreed, “and I heard that Sato was even helping her after she got back from cram school. She barely gets any time to herself because she studies so much.” “Damn…” Jinnai looked up just thinking about having to study so much a day. “That just sucks. I’d kill myself if I had to go to school all day and then study all night!” then he nodded towards the spectacled girl with a teasing smile. “Or just end up like you, Kawada.” Kawada rolled her eyes as Ishikawa looked up from her sweeping and sneered at him. “Would you rather study to death or have the Masonna kill you?” “That’s easy. Masonna, of course!” The girls groaned at his perverted smile, going back to their classroom cleanup. Lagging around any longer with him might be hazardous to their mental health.
