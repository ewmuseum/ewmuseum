God and Contracts

14 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/14/06 Luis blinked as he coughed up blood, staring at the bright red spill on the black asphalt. He was on his hands and knees and, at the moment, he didn't think he'd ever breathe again. He closed his eyes and choked a bit, trying his hardest to suck in air, but he didn't succeed. The heavy leather sole of a boot collided with his temple and he rolled over onto his back, groaning. His new, silk dragon-printed shirt was ruined. Ruined! All ripped and scuffed and everything. He wiped some blood away from his lips and tried to look up. It was nighttime in Nevada, the full moon hanging high overhead. Stars twinkled merrily in the night sky, giving their goodwill to the three muscle-bound men who were currently laying into Luis Ferrara. One of them wore a black headband twisted around his bald scalp and all of them were dressed in typical toughman fashion, ripped denim jeans and jackets, black muscle tees, and heavy boots. The boots were the worst. Luis hated them, especially as they were smashing into his face. 'Goddamn spic don't know what the hell's good for 'im,' one of the English-speaking bikers snarled as he stomped Luis. A chain was wrapped around his fist, the end of it dangling onto the ground, but he contented himself with stomping Luis into the asphalt for the moment. 'Don't bring your ass back around here!' The others chuckled and the chain-wielding man looked up with a victorious grin. Luis spat up more blood, the crimson tide washing onto the man's shoe. His black hair was strewn all across his face, making him look ever more the homeless man. For some reason, they hadn't touched the gold watch wrapped around his wrist, but Luis didn't doubt that the godless pigs were going to come for it in due time. It had been a lot of hard work getting that watch, a lot of fight nights that had to go by successfully. Without a hitch. These idiots clearly didn't understand all that; if they did, they might show a little more respect. The man who he spat on looked down at his shoe, noticing the damage. Growling, he brought his leg back and sent a kick full force into Luis's mouth. The Venezuelan's head snapped backwards and he gave out an anguished yelp. They resumed their stomping of him, muttering racist remarks amid accusations of hustling. Finally, the chain-whip was brought into play, the iron links leaving crude lacerations on Luis's body. He arched his back in pain each time it hit him, the agony searing his body. A human-sized shadow moved into the vicinity all of a sudden, like some sort of guardian angel. The three men turned and before any words could be said, one of them found their jaw smashed in by a fierce punch. As he tottered backwards and landed on his back, Luis recognized his savior. He grinned, blood seeping through his previously-immaculate white teeth. It was about time. Anton whipped about quickly, throwing a vicious side kick into a second man's side. He stepped forward, knocking him quickly off his feet with an uppercut to the jaw. Anton's light black jacket flared out behind him as he fought, the rest of him clothed in a black t-shirt and blue denim jeans. A spray of blood erupted from the man's mouth as he crumpled to the pavement. Anton made to turn, but he found muscular arms wrapped around him, pinning his right arm to his side. The man squeezed tightly, trying to force the breath out of the Lion. Threading his leg through the other man's lower limbs, he swept the biker's legs out from under him and shifted his weight, forcing the attacker's back hard into the pavement. As the hold was released, Anton kipped up to his feet and quickly spun around on a heel, smashing his foot into the man's temple. When the biker curled over awkwardly from the impact, Anton finally saw fit to turn his attentions to his battered manager. He looked down on Luis, taking a second to smirk before leaning over and gathering up the wiry man. Luis was pathetic. He had never bothered to learn how to fight for himself. He always had other people to do that for him. He had other people who needed him, other people who relied on his being alive to fight for him. It was something that Luis always had to look forward to and something that Anton hated about the man. It was something that Anton, unfortunately, had to deal with. Anton was one of the people who relied on Luis's being alive. Luis hung limply on the support of Anton, groaning with each step. He still managed to beam as he looked up at him, however. 'Lion, I can't fucking drive like this,' Luis complained in Spanish. 'Look at me, I'm all… beat the fuck up and everything. There's no way I can drive.' 'You have to,' Anton reminded him as he practically dragged Luis over to his car. 'I can't drive, remember? Suck it up a little, okay?' Luis snarled, his cheery disposition gone the instant he remembered his duties. Anton shook his head and let Luis lean up against the Jaguar and catch his breath. Luis laid there for a bit before blinking and getting up quickly, kicking the hubcap. 'You fucking idiot!' Luis snapped. 'Don't do that! I don't want blood on my fuckin' car!' Anton simply pursed his lips as Luis had his little fit, moving silently over to the passenger's side. Luis fished the keys out of his pocket and slammed the car key into the keyhole, opening the door and getting inside. He muttered under his breath about having to clean the car up after this, but he closed the door and hit the ignition. Anger probably fueled him right now; as long as it got Anton home, he was fine with it. 'The hospital, then?' Anton asked. He meant it to be rhetorical, but Luis waved his hand dismissively as he pulled out of their parking space. 'Fuck the hospital,' Luis said. 'Me bein' alive after those two bastards took me out for a beating? That's an act of God right there. Going to a church.' Anton simply stared at Luis as the smaller man took off into the street, recklessly swerving into the road and shooting forward. Reason had long since left Luis's mind. Anton sat in the back pew of the modestly-sized church. Everything seemed very natural and pure here, despite all the hypocrisy that Anton percieved. Luis, of course, was right in front, on his knees and praying fervently. Other than the manager/wrestler duo, no one inhabited the chapel building. Luis was a hypocrite, too. After all, he was not a regular attendee. He had only come because some veiled God had shined his providence down on him. Once. One time. Anton reclined, trying to think. He had a career ahead of him. He had to train. He had to improve. He did not need to be weighted down with the hand of some higher authority. He shot a suddenly pious Luis a baleful glare before twisting about in his seat. This was idiotic and Anton had half a mind to march over and haul Luis off his knees and drag him out of this church. There was business that needed to be taken care of. This was a waste. Luis finally made the sign of the cross one last time and stood up, straightening his clothes. He shot a look back at Anton, nodding at him. Slowly, Anton drew himself to his full height and sidled out of the pew. Luis's quick steps brought him to Anton's side and he reached up, clapping a hand on the African's shoulder. ''I feel like we've done a good thing here today, Lion,'' Luis said in Spanish. He beamed as the duo made their way to the church's exit. ''I think I'm gonna make some calls, y'know, and get some shit done. Basically rest up for whatever we have next. I think you'll like it.' ''Like what?'' Anton asked curiously. The air outside was a sharp contrast to that inside and Anton felt it, shivering. It wasn't cold, per se, but the church had been sweltering. Anton had half-wondered why he wasn't sweating now. Luis shrugged his shoulders, still limping a bit for all the speed of his motion. ''I got you a wrestling match, man,'' Luis informed him. ''But more than that. I managed to attract some big name scouts there, convinced them that you're something to pick up on. All you gotta worry about is being on your motherfuckin' A game, Lion, and you'll be in the big leagues.'' Anton glanced over at his manager, standing rooted in position as the greasy Venezuelan started towards the Jaguar. So, Luis had finally done something worthwhile? This was a change. Shaking his head, Anton started after him. He'd have to see this to believe it. He didn't trust Luis so easily. 'Big leagues' could be some bushman federation in the valley and 'scouts' could be teenage idiots who thought anything involving fists was excellent. Luis was too slippery to simply trust. Anton fired a high kick to the temple of the man calling himself X Infinito. The masked wrestler stumbled backwards into the corner and Anton followed him in, grabbing him by the neck and tearing into him with an uppercut. The crowd groaned as the well-muscled form of Infinito sagged in the corner, easy prey for Anton's rapid-fire kicks and knee strikes. X Infinito put his hands up to defend himself, but Anton simply dragged him out and locked him down with an elevated armbar. Anton had to be dominant. He had to totally destroy this man. If he had a give-and-take bout with this lower-card idiot, he would not prove himself to whatever scouts Luis had managed to dig up. X Infinito's screams drove Anton to cinch the hold in further. As he rose his hand to tap out, however, the Lion released him and rolled to his feet. He could not end it so fast. Anton hauled the man to his feet and quickly took him over with a release Northern Lights suplex. He scrambled to his feet and mounted the man, pummeling him with fists. His face was drawn back in an expression of rage as he battered the masked wrestler. Finally, the referee made him relent. Anton stood and took a few steps back, waiting for the man to rise. A change of pace now. He had taken the time to show considerable submission abilities and he was always in top-form strike wise. Now he had to display his technical abilities. A well-rounded wrestler was a good wrestler. X Infinito slowly came to his feet and Anton leapt at him, not giving him any chance to recover before flooring him with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex. Anton kipped up immediately and caught hold of Infinito's hair, heaving him up and flattening him with a full cradle suplex. Anton kipped up to his feet again, taking a deep breath. X Infinito was off of his feet and it looked like it was for good. Now Anton needed to make the kill. Anton had decided before the match had even begun that it would end with a Tiger Trap Leg Scissors, so he moved towards Infinito's legs to lock the move in. In an unexpected burst of energy, Infinito grabbed Anton and tugged him backwards, smashing him face-first into the turnbuckle. Anton shook his head and got to his feet quickly, spinning around. He saw X Infinito on his feet and he lunged at him, grabbing him around the neck and throwing him over. X Infinito landed flat on his back, writhing in pain. For a split-second, Anton was aware what he had just done. Snap mare. He had executed it. Perfectly. If that wasn't impressive, Anton didn't know what was. Now it was time to end the man. Painfully. Meanwhile, Luis signed his name on an AWC contract and waited patiently for Anton to finish his match to sign his own name. The big leagues indeed.
