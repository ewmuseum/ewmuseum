He tried to keep focus, remember that he was drugged, and he wouldn’t always be like this. He would get through this, and he’d be alright. He’d get through it in as few as eight hours. One work day. He slid his hand across the wall to try and maintain his balance, but he caught the light switch instead. With an accidental flick, the apartment was black now. It was like he was sitting in a vial of ink. No lights anywhere, alone with his thoughts and fears. Ugh, she hated even the name of it. This wasn’t what life was supposed to be like after college. She was supposed to be able to get a job that she would love, like writing about horse shows in Australia, or political unrest in the Middle East, not…professional wrestling. With a sigh, she puts her glasses back on and gets ready to delve back into the world of Alex Strider. First stop: A trip to Atlantic Wrestling Club’s webpage to see any of the latest developments. Two short clicks and she was there. ”Hmm. Alex is facing Pact.” Reading Pacts bio, she took it with a grain of salt. Pamela was led to believe wrestlers often embellished on their history in order to build their legacy. Still, even if half of what was written about the man was true, he would still be quite an imposing opponent. “He’s a little bigger than Strider is. They really stay wrestling into their forties like that? Obviously he’s been around the block. Death-matches? Well, this should be pretty one sided. Strider seems more like a pretty boy to me. He’ll probably get hurt,” she thought apathetically. Pamela was lost in her boredom again, looking around her messy apartment she started to get depressed. The door to her apartment opened.
