The Death of a Dream: Chapter One - Betrayal Begets Betrayal

25 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th June 2005 Pact I looked into her eyes, their soft blueness resonating back at me with pure love, touching something deep within my soul that I hadn't felt in so long. 'This is fucked up. I mean, really fucked up. We can't do this. It's wrong.' She sighed and took my hand. 'How can it be wrong? We're both here. We're both alive. We're only human after all.' 'But what if he finds out? You know what his state of mind's been recently. This could push him completely over the edge.' 'Then let it. To be honest I'm past caring. It's not him I love – it's you.' With that, I took her into my arms and loved her, feeling a connection that had been absent from my life since Reiko passed. I was her and she was me. It was perfect and beautiful. It completed me. --- Later that night, I lay in bed and watched her sleeping, her soft form rising with each intake of breath, falling with each sweet exhalation. And watching her as I did I couldn't help but feel guilty. I owed the man nothing, that was sure, but still there are things that remain taboo. Sighing, I walked out onto the balcony of my hotel suite and lit a cigarette, inhaling deeply. He's just a kid. I had no right to do what I did. But still, it felt so good. I haven't felt that close to anyone in years. Las Vegas sprawled out below me, its twinkling lights baiting me, goading me into returning to a life I'd left behind long ago. It put me in a reflective mood. The anger began to rise within me, unchecked. I tried to force it down, back to where it came from but it was no good. THE CUNT. HOW DARE HE? HOW DARE HE MARCH BACK INTO MY LIFE AFTER ALL THIS TIME? WHAT RIGHT DOES HE HAVE? WHAT RIGHT? THINGS WERE GOING GREAT FOR ME. And I guess that's the problem. He always seems to pop up when I least expect him – a constant reminder, a spectre of a past I've tried my hardest to escape from. That was the issue. Kitsune was destined to be a perpetual presence in my life. It was an issue I'd have to accept. My soul belonged, inexorably and inextricably, to him. I sat myself down on a deckchair and let the cool evening breeze wash over me. I can't worry about that right now. I have other problems. Like Crimson O'Malec… Alexi Things were rapidly getting out of control and there was nothing I could do to stop the chain of events that had been set in motion. I couldn't help but feel, despite my best efforts and good intentions, my dabbling in Pact's affairs had only hurt him. At this stage however as bad as things seemed they were destined to get worse. The re-emergence of Keitaro Kitsune as a factor in Pact's life, coupled with mounting injuries, was not only threatening his physical well-being but his mental well-being to boot. But it was too late for regrets now. Much, much too late. Pact's encounter with Kitsune on Fresh! had left him broken and disheartened. He finally felt as if his spirit had been entirely and deliberately crushed. Even at this point when he was still in contention for three of the AWC's four singles championships, he considered his career to be pretty much over. And that made me worried Worried for his safety and that of his opponents. The hardcore fight format suited him down to the ground, but he could no longer compete to the level he was accustomed to. His back just wouldn’t hold up, and deep down he knew that. Nothing good was going to come out of any match he had at Solarized. And everyone could see it but him. Pact I left her behind, sleeping soundly in my bed, and took my bike out across the Nevada desert to watch the sunrise. The wind tousled my hair to a ridiculous degree – I looked like one of those geriatric models that advertising companies use to show that middle-aged people can have fun on a Harley, too. I thought about Crimson and mused upon his sudden and meteoric rise to the top of the pile in the Club. From the minute he arrived in this league he seemed just so positive, so full of life an energy and ready to make a name for himself. But now…after seeing what he's capable of…it makes me wonder whether the standard I'm working at can any longer be considered viable. I was the shit in this company. No-one could touch me in terms of reputation. But the rep this guy's building up…and the short amount of time he's done it in…are both frightening and threatening. The sun began to peek out from behind the horizon, little by little spreading a searing orange light across the desolate landscape. I brought the bike to a halt at a little prefab diner to drink the spectacle in. I swear I've never seen a sun as big and as red as the Nevada sun, nor as bright. It chased away the darkness and dampness of the night and left everything around me blazing with an inherent brilliance. I stood in the shadow of the diner and watched Mother Nature's awesome demonstration of her power and was utterly breath taken. How much longer can I actually do this for? I thought to myself. How much longer until my body finally gives out? Is that what he wants? Does he truly wish for me to suffer that much? What have I done to him to make him so intent on destroying me? What? I'd understand it if his actions were bourn out of vengeance… I forced all thoughts of Kitsune-san from my mind. His actions were not the most pressing concern. I had battle royal to consider and prepare myself for, and my ensuing challenge for the Relentless Championship. Crimson…I fear for you, son. Because you can't see the truth that's staring you smack in the face. There's no doubt – you are simply amazing. I've no trouble bigging you up. You do things in the ring I could never dream of doing…but still…Big fuckin' deal. Life's hard, and that's a fact. No-one knows more about hardship and suffering than me. At the end of the day, Crimson, I see the truth that you refuse to consider. You fear me. You fear me because, as undeniably amazing as you are in the ring, I am still just that little bit better. I have no doubt that our match will degenerate, rather quickly, into a game of one-upmanship. And if it does, Crimson, if you try and outdo me, it'll be the end of you. I swear it. Because no-one does it better than me, and if I have to kill you in order to prove it I’ll do so without batting an eye. I'll do it without a pang of remorse. I've killed better men than you. Soldiers. Warriors. Gladiators. You're small-fry compared to me, and you know it. Once the sun had assumed its customary position in the sky, I returned to the hotel. When I entered the bedroom, she was gone. Her smell lingered behind. I stood out on the balcony and watched Las Vegas sweat in the heat of summer.
