A Brief Moment

Date: Monday, August 8, 2005 Vignette II “A Moment” -[name withheld] I think I’ll take over telling the story from here. Unlike my exceptionally rude partner, I have the decency to introduce myself. My name is Oswald Anderson and I’m all of twenty-one years old as of February. I’m actually the youngest – Cletus, believe it or not, is the elder of our two. I think it’s kind of funny how, if he hadn’t gotten held back a year in the eighth grade, we would have never met in high school. Not that that was that pleasant a meeting. I’m still kind of sore. That was five years ago. But anyway, you don’t want to hear some boring, dusty old story do you? It’s the present that we’re supposed to be chronicling anyway. “She’s a former NGW Women’s Champion,” Cletus was reading pleadingly. “Considered a pioneer in the field of women’s wrestling!” “Yeah, but Cletus…” “Rodney McArthur came up to her and tried to book her in one of those bra an panties dealies, and she refused…” “Cletus…” “She then went on to become the first and only president of Next Generation Entertainment – she eventually took the job of the pig that made her lay down for her title!” “Cletus,” I breathed in exasperation and he finally stopped rattling off facts. “I get it. She’s a minor celebrity, and a nice person. I have no problem with Cynthia Thomas-” “Cynthia Thomas-Riley,” Cletus interjected, but I pretended that I hadn’t heard him. “What I have a problem with is the way this is all starting to go. I mean… making us kiss and hold hands for our entrance? Having Janet Jackson for our entrance music? I don’t even like Janet Jackson!” I looked across at Cletus sitting on the opposite side of our private compartment on the train. He was giving me one of his world famous “get on with it you git” looks and I sighed and suddenly felt as if I were talking to a stone statue. “My point is,” I pressed on. “I think we’re being exploited.” “Exploited?” “Yes,” I stressed furrowing my brow. “Exploited. Everyone else in the AWC gets to be themselves except us.” At this comment, Cletus actually scoffed. “Are you claiming that we’re NOT flaming homosexuals Mr. Little Dick?” Unsure of which comment to repute first I rolled my eyes for a moment, “What Ben Myers is making us do… that’s not us. Our names are Oswald and Cletus. Not Sam and Pat.” However, the train went on.
