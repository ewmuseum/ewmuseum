Murderous Betrayal

21 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/05 August 18, 2005 New York, New York For the first time in my life, I had things going decently for me. I had a place to sleep, food to eat, beer to drink, a television, a nice car, some nice clothes, a steady stream of income, and a job wrestling for Atlantic Wrestling Club. I was laying down on the couch, asleep but having the television that was on pervading my dreams. Buy OxyClean Now! No thanks, man. I finally get around to opening my eyes, and raising to a sitting position on my couch. I take one sniff at myself and realize I don't smell too good, so I walk to the bathroom and hop into the shower. Since this isn't a gay porno, I won't detail about the shower. I put on a black shirt and some black jeans, because I didn't plan on going out too much today. I'm not that much of a fashionista. Unless I really need to put something nice on, I'll just put on the first things I see. The television had some feature on the New York Giants, but I don't really care too much about football. I was about to get up and make a bowl of Rice Krispies or something, but someone knocked on the door. I walked up to the peep-hole, and checked to see that it wasn't anyone who wanted to kill me. As one of Paolo Borronti's men, there were bound to be some people who didn't like him, and by extension, didn't like me. Through the peep-hole, I saw a black man who was wearing a Curtis Martin black alternate jersey. He didn't look like he wanted to hurt me, so I let him in. 'What's up, dog?' he said as soon as I opened the door. His enthusiasm was annoying, but I let him in anyway, but not before 'Who are you?' 'My name is Michael, but you can call me Big Mike.' 'Okay, Big Mike.' He came into the apartment and sat down on the couch. His name described him very well. He was taller than I was, and he looked like he weighed more than three hundred pounds. 'So, what do you need?' I asked. 'I come here, because I know you're very reliable. Paolo Borronti knows you're very reliable, and you've been the one guy to singlehandedly turn around his fortunes. This is why what I'm going to ask of you is going to be very hard for you to take.' He obviously knew about me and needed some of my help. While I swear allegience to Mr. Borronti, I've never been above helping anyone else if it meant money for me. I agreed with what he said. I had been very reliable. I've blown up shit for Paolo Borronti, I've killed people for Paolo Borronti, I've stolen stuff for Paolo Borronti. The only thing that didn't sit right with me is what he wanted. 'Jack, I know this is very hard for you to consider, but I want you to kill Paolo Borronti. He's been messing with my crack operation, and I want you to kill his ass.' I know he said more after 'kill Paolo Borronti', but it was hard to listen to him after he said it, so I didn't hear him. I shook my head though, as I know how Paolo Borronti has been screwing me. However, I didn't want to take part in any of this unless I got paid handsomely. 'You know I'm a mercenary, Big Mike, you know I'll kill for anyone who has enough cash. However, Mr. Borronti has given me direction in my life. I won't kill him willy-nilly.' Before I could continue, though, he put his hand in front of my mouth. 'I know all about that, that's why I'm going to give you all sorts of shit if you carry this out. After you carry this plan out, you're going to work for me now.' I replied, 'I like the sound of that. However, if they know I killed him, what am I going to do to protect myself? All kinds of mobsters are going to come after me because they're loyal to him.' He calmly replied, 'It's ok. No will know that you killed him, and if you do, I got a nice hideout in California.' 'I like your style. I'll think about it. By the way, what's stopping me from telling Borronti about you?' He looked calm for a second, but then he pulled out a pistol, leaned over to me, and pressed it against my head. 'I don't think so. I got a man over there who works for me also, and if you even LOOK at Mr. Borronti like you're going to tell him ANYTHING, I'll have him make sure you're a dead son of a bitch.' But he pulled the gun back and extended his hand. 'But, you know we're cool. Right?' I slapped his hand. 'Yeah, we're cool.' 'Alright then, I'll be seeing you around.' 'You gotta show me his corpse, man!' He left the apartment, shut the door behind him, and went on with his life. I decided that I should probably take a little visit to Borronti, tell him about what's going on, double agents be damned. I decided that I should probably tell Mr. Borronti what was going on. I got into my car and made my way down to Mr. Borronti's residence. When I came in, he was talking to his two bodyguards. They were both very tall and very muscular. They were in suits. When I came in, he turned away from his two bodyguards, and turned toward me. 'Hey! Why aren't you dressed up?' I was still in my black shirt and black jeans. I replied, 'I didn't really feel like it today.' 'Ah, I see.' I wanted to know what he was planning for today. 'Hey man, what are you going to do today?' Borronti replied, 'I haven''t sent anyone out today.' 'That's nice and all, but I really need to tell you something...' 'HE'S TRYING TO KILL YOU!' shouted one of the bodyguards. Mr. Borronti pulled a knife and tried to stab me with it, but I kicked him away. I pulled out my Colt .45 pistol and jumped up, and rolled, and hid behind one of the plants in his house. The traitorious bodyguard pulled out a Beretta and fired at Mr. Borronti, but as old as he was he managed to roll out of the way as well. Borronti drew a gun of his own and fired two shots at the bodyguard, but missed. I called out to Borronti, 'Come on, hide behind this!' but he didn't trust me. 'He's going to kill--!' stopped as the traitorous bodyguard tried to put a bullet past the giant plant, but the plant took the fire and the bullet bounced off the stem. I had to pull Borronti out of the line of fire and into hiding with me. He asked 'Are you trying to pull something, you son of a bitch?' and put his gun barrel to my head for the second time today. I didn't really feel like having cold steel against my flesh, so I said, 'Come on, man, you know me, I ain't going to go back on you like that. Just put the gun down and let's kill this son of a bitch.' He pulled the gun away, but he said, 'Alright, but after this, you gotta tell me all about what happened.' I said, 'Alright.' After this sappy moment, which I don't like to have too often, the bodyguard shouted 'DIE MOTHERFUCKERS!' and tried to shoot us, but we were still pretty well hidden. Even though Paolo Borronti's bodyguards are very strong, they aren't very bright, and Mr. Borronti always does this on purpose. Dumb guys are more likely to accept authority more than intelligent guys. That's just the way things are. Borronti had to reach his arm out to shoot the bodyguard, and he saw this. They both shot each other at the same time. Borronti was shot in the arm, and the bodyguard was shot in the arm. The bodyguard dropped dead, and Borronti's arm was bleeding. He weakly said, 'Go find a pair of scissors.' I quickly rushed into the kitchen, and found a pair of scissors. 'I hate to do this,' I said, 'but it has to be done.' I had him take off his jacket, and I cut a portion of his sleeve off. I then tied the long portion of the sleeve I cut off and tied it around his wound. 'Don't worry, you can buy another one.' He didn't feel like talking, though. I picked him up, and carried him outside. I set him down for a second, opened the car door, and settled him into the passenger seat. I buckled the seatbelt for him, then I rushed to the driver's side of the car, quickly buckled myself in, and went for a joyride. I dashed through the streets of New York, trying to get the local hospital. I had to dodge and weave through traffic, and I was speeding, so I had to worry about the cops. I didn't want them to check on me and see I was Jack Vance, and have them put me in prison for longer. Prison is about the shittiest place on earth, I've had prior experience, and I don't want to go there again. I finally made it to the hospital. I rushed to find a parking space, and quickly parked. I got out of the car and had to carry Mr. Borronti again. I rushed to the Emergency Room section while carrying him. When I got in, I sat Mr. Borronti down in a chair and quickly went up to the desk. Because both of us are wanted, we had to use aliases. 'My name is Jason Locke and I've got a man who's been shot. I need you to work on him as soon as possible. His name is Alexander Lee.' As soon as I said that, she called for a stretcher to be brought out. I ran to where Mr. Borronti was, picked him up, and placed him on the stretcher. They carried him to a room in the back as I told them to 'Be careful.' I sat down and waited for whatever hand I would be dealt. -------------------- August 20, 2005 New York, New York I didn't really care for Paolo Borronti, but I didn't trust Big Mike either, so I had to keep the status quo. I went back to the hospital today, to see how the old man's doing. Hospital rooms tend to be impersonal; this one was no different. The walls were white. Equipment was everywhere, and I didn't even know what most of it did. Paolo Borronti was resting in his bed. His arm was bandaged and connected by wires to a system which kept track of his vital signs. 'How are you doing?' I inquired. He snapped out of his resting state, and was back to full consciousness. 'Don't worry about me, young chap. The doctors told me that everything will be alright, they performed surgery on me as soon as you left, and it was successful. I'll have to stay in here though, to recover from my wounds.' I said, 'Great. That's very awesome.' He looked at me again, and he said, without the usual coldness of a crime boss, but with the warmness of a friend, 'You really saved my life. How can I ever repay you?' I knew exactly what I wanted. 'I want you to start to trust me again, and I'd like a raise.' We both chuckled. 'When I get back online again, I'll make sure I get you done. But while I'm like this, I can't do anything. That's why I want you to take control of the operation until I get back.' I was stunned. I had never had control of anything for the first time in my life. 'How do you know that I'll be the best man for the job?' 'Come on, you said it yourself! I gotta trust you, so I am.' I smiled as I said, 'I'll make sure this ship stays running on course until you come back.' He said, 'That's good to hear, but I think I need to get some sleep now. Oh, and one more thing. The Spanish have an expression. Mi casa es tu casa. You're free to stay there if you need it.' With his good hand he took out a key. 'This is to my house. Use it as you wish.' I started to get up and get back home, but as soon as I got up, my cell phone rang. 'What is it, now?' I muttered. I picked it up and answered it. Apparently Big Mike had gotten a hold of my number. 'I thought we were cool, man! You killed one of my men, and now I'm going to kill you! If I were you, I'd watch my back!' He hung up. Good. I didn't want to hear his fat ass anymore. I'll kill him as soon as I find him. Borronti must have heard the cell phone go off. 'Who was it?' he asked. 'Oh, nobody, just that punk bitch who took it up the ass from his bodyguard. You know, the one he killed?' 'I know.' -------------------- August 21, 2005 New York, New York I was back at the Borronti residence. I was in his planning room. It didn't have much in the way of furniture, except for a long business-style table and several chairs. We had a long table sat up, where all of his men sat. At the head of the table, I sat, since I was now the leader of this operation. 'Paolo Borronti was shot a few days ago. He's going to be okay, but he's in no condition to make plans. So I, Jack Vance, will be your new boss.' Everyone at the table groaned, as they wanted to be the boss, but only smart men get to be the boss. 'Don't worry, I won't make you do too much. The only objective I have for you, is to kill a man who calls himself 'Big Mike'.' 'He's a black man who weighs over 300 pounds, likes to wear NFL jerseys, and is generally an annoying bitch. I'm sure you won't have any problems, right?' Everyone else in the room repeated, in unison, 'Right.' 'If you need anything, come ask me. I'm going to make everything right for us. Now get out there and bring me a corpse! The first one who brings it to me will be paid handsomely!' There was a rumbling in agreement. Then everyone else got out of the room, because they all wanted to be the first to get the corpse, and thus get paid. I would have done it myself, but I didn't want to get my hands dirty, and besides, I didn't want to get in any trouble before I flew out to Birmingham, England, to face Grady in my first Atlantic Wrestling Club match. I don't know much about him, but I've fought lots of men before, and I won't be any less ready for this one.
