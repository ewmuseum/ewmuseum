Roses & Thorns - First Impressions II

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/03/06 23rd March - Hotel All is quiet in the hotel room, now that the hubbub is over. The door clicked shut and the sliding of the bolt signalled respite, sweet and peaceful respite. The drabness of the room sank into my pores and slowly crept up about me, very slowly indeed. I didn’t even question it because really what was the point. It at least was an object with which I didn’t have to question its motives. It was blunt, straight forward and to the point. I didn’t have to question. I shouldn’t have to question anything, if anything I should be expecting a clean, open faced reality. Not a cloak and daggers arena where everyone is on the stage set to perform if need be. Everyone a performer, everyone a part to play and everyone unaware of who might be pulling their strings, everyone totally uncaring of that fact. Questions open the door to paths with multiple pathways and in truth they don’t lead to any concrete answers but instead to hastily made alibis and notions, figments created on the spot to humour whoever poses the question. Questions in fact should be based on the already present concrete knowledge…not motives. I see questions everywhere and I see motives nowhere. I shouldn’t have to question. But I do…because nobody is man enough to put themselves out there. They would rather stand behind their masks and playact than take responsibility. They would rather act up to the camera and show their best side, rather than their worst. You might not blame them for that to begin with, but really it isn’t doing you any favours in the long run. It certainly doesn’t do who interacts with you any favours. For where once they might have had one question, now it builds and builds into eternity…into the sky and all they are left with is question marks everywhere they go. Questions are all I see about me. The walls are so dank and dull and this amuses me. Clean slates everywhere…everywhere to play with. To put questions to! Maybe they’re the ones who can answer my questions, my problems might be solved then. All I have right now are questions and my belt. The questions are starting to take control of even that. I hastily scrabble through my bags until I find what I want…what I need. I test it slowly and surely on the wall in the corner. Delighted with its imprint I begin to tear the paintings and subsequent attempts at art from the wall until I have my slate ready to be written upon. So many questions…so many questions but all of them have a basis. I scrawl the words smack in the middle of the wall and draw a circle around it. I even underline it, jamming the marker back and forward underneath the name of Adam Dick. Playfully I begin putting question marks all around inside the circle I have created. Questions are his friend, he uses those questions. *** After fifteen years, it’s becoming increasingly difficult to understand why and how I came to choose wrestling as a professional career choice because everywhere I look now all I see are stumbling blocks and veering paths that don’t seem to help or add to the thrill of the chase anymore. Instead they make the journey hazardous and far less rewarding, instantly making this career choice a mockery and a sham of all that it has come to be renowned for. Right now, you’ve got to look at the industry as that of a poker table. Each player has their hands and each has their bargaining chips for the night. It’s all bluff and pomp and circumstance as everyone tries to barge their way through and use their talent off in the most underhanded way. Honour, respect, honesty, bonds, friendships. These words are what the industry is renowned for and they are few and far between in the wrestling industry today. The more I see it in myself, the less I see it in those around me. Really it’s the bitter older hacks of the business that we have to blame and now it’s all coming full circle as I finally begin to see why exactly they get this way. They come to realise that the only way to get ahead in this game is to become the most hated thing of all in their heads up until that point: cynical and possessive old hacks. You see they have to embrace this ideal in order to reap the rewards at the top that few ever truly come to find. They have to sacrifice their very integrity and common decency in order to achieve such nefarious ends. Theirs is a significant cost for a gain that is oh so fleeting. This I have come to learn. Now however, there is a much more sinister and worrying trend that has rippled through the wrestling industry like a plague and it is one that if becomes epidemic will surely kill it off. It is of course highly appropriate that the very person who stands in my way at the PPV is one of the chief proponents of this method. None other than Adam Dick. He’s a new breed of the tired old hacks in that he’s learned quick on the uptake with how to play the game. He hasn’t dilly dallied around and tried to play nice at all. He’s doing whatever it takes to reach up another rung and it doesn’t matter what it takes to get it. The big difference that stands between Adam and the rest of the old tired hacks? He never had the common decency in the first place. He’s got nothing to lose and everything to gain and right now he’s feeling pretty smug because of the way he’s played the game. A master manipulator you might say and he’s certainly earned it because now, not only is everyone in the business wary of the old hack who guards his hoard like a slumbering dragon, they’ve also got people like Adam Dick watching them, eyeing them up and seeing exactly how to play them so that he can trade off the decency that those he uses once had for his own ends. It’s a masterstroke on his part. Really quite genius you might say, but one that sickens me to the very core. Adam Dick is the very core and instigator of this mess, playing everyone on the poker table for their bluff until he’s holding all the aces. By then, it’s far too late. *** 23rd March - Hotel Oh the questions, the questions make me hurt, they make me feel pain, they make me cry. Did no one stop to think that they might be harmful in some way to whomever they were used against? I’m even using them as I scrawl upon the wall, adding more questions as I beg the question, plead the question, search the question….where did the questions begin? Again I draw the line and it points to Adam, that Adam, the questioner as I like to call him. He feeds everyone with questions he does. He feeds them and nurtures them, aiding in their ability to find out the way to truly question and create even more from just their touch. I see how he does it. Oh I do, after all I’m the one who’s amassing them so I can destroy them and take away his questions. Then what will a questioner do without his questions? Ah it hurts again as I make the gesture, a dark supplicant to his faith without even knowing it. We all are and there is nothing I can do about it. I can destroy them all I want but they only come back, just as big and bad as the big bad wolf. He didn’t have questions but he still got his karma in the end. My hand has been idly scribbling in a corner and I know without even looking that it’s a question mark. I can feel it from the rhythms of my hand. I look and it hurts so I pull it away and begin scrawling again, pulling arrows out of the circle and pointing to other things…other names and questions. I’m adding to his pool of possibilities but it is the only way… Only way I can question… He’s made me even say it over and over. Question, question, question…you think if I keep saying it I’ll lose all meaning and fade away? Ah there it is, his symbol again and I hurt. I know the master is grinning with glee at this and I know he’s flicking his hands together, tendrils waiting to lash out with impunity. He enjoys the questioning, he knows that the questioning goes nowhere unless you have the original answer. Answer sounds so comforting to me right now but it’s so far away. It may even be impossible to reach the answer to all this. It is locked beneath so many questions. It is the questioner who started this all of course. I scrawl the arrow from his name out to the two other names of Sasha and Jessica. They look pure but they are plagued by question marks, floating about their names menacingly like carrion. The carrion are winning also and there is nothing I can do to stop it. Only the answer will provide the key…only the key will provide the answer. Which is which? There’s the pain again…. *** With all the cards in the hand, there’s a simple matter of how to destroy each and every player now for Adam. Now, in his way, he hasn’t just played them all off of himself, he’s played them for each other. All the cloak and dagger conspires to make sure that he is innocent in the grand scheme of things and that no matter what happens, he’s completely stain free in the game. He watches the mere mortals scrabble for the scraps of food from the master and reaps the reward as not only does he climb the ladder, but he has obliterated the level beneath him. Wash, rinse and repeat as they say. It’s this kind of trailblazing that has instigated the current environment in this business. When I first entered you had the hacks who held onto their fame for as long as they could but then beneath that you had all the aspiring guys who kept a good bond. The back was typically bonded together, even including the hacks for whatever reason they felt necessary to keep in touch. As such, you didn’t have problems because if there were any, they were solved pretty quickly in the event of anything cropping up. Because of Adam Dick though, all chaos has broken loose. Now nobody knows how to trust one another. Everyone keeps their true selves guarded and behind a smokescreen so that no one can get into the exterior and develop any sort of true bond. They live in a constant state of permanent guardedness. What bonds do develop aren’t created out of any sort of transfer of common goals or a shared vision of interest, they’re artificially created to form fronts and cloaks of shadows to push other people away from them. I’m guilty of this too certainly but I can see it happening each and every day. People turning on each other because they simply want to be the biggest or the best when they’re forgetting the fact that when it all comes down to it, once somebody can draw, everyone goes away happy with a payday. People are forgetting that fact in this industry. Despite the marketing of single superstars, this is a team orientated industry. It doesn’t look like it from the outside, but that’s the way things are. Sasha and Jessica have been most vulnerable to this of all things simply down to their inexperience of naivety with this place. They simply don’t get that people are this conniving and would do anything to get by and have a payday. A lot of the guys right now in AWC have had to slog through backyards and indies which show no prospect of ever becoming big but they can’t pass up opportunities for the simple reason it could blow up. There are a litany of men around this business who would give their arm and a leg if they could go back to when they had the opportunity to wrestle for ECW in the early days, knowing what they know now. And that’s just one promotion among hundreds, if not thousands out there. How can you tell which will be the next ECW? So this kind of mentality of scrapping for everything is lost on these two women and frankly they are dead in the water once they’ve touched it. But strangely they’ve been used in an altogether different fashion than usual for Mr. Dick. He hasn’t stripped them of their decency, far from it. He’s done the opposite in order to try and strip mine. *** 23rd March - Hotel I know the answer you see but they don’t believe me. Believing in the answer is only how it will come true but right now they have so many questions that they can’t face the possibility of belief, they are too muddled with facts and figures, conundrums and chaos, numbers and notions. All these things are floating around and crippling everything that could possibly be happening. They have the opportunities but they simply aren’t able to address them with all the questions in the way. They are victims of their own inabilities. Ah the questions they are hurting again, they are multiplying on the wall, the clean slate that has been corrupted by itself…by me. I can’t even look at it, I can’t even look at it! I’m shaking and rocking, the question marks are everywhere, everywhere I turn and there’s nothing I can do. They are taking my friends, my allies away from me and using them against me. Everyone is a performer, everyone is willing to act and stab me. I jut the marker into my chest, staining my white t-shirt. That’s what it’d look like if everything was black and white and I was bleeding. Maybe everything is black and white? Ah the pain and I don’t know from which it comes from! I can see the questions sprouting forth from my forehead, watching the thoughts and notions spiral out of control and upwards to the ceiling, bobbing and floating up there, unable to reach any higher than the glass ceiling which is stopping and any all advancement. I can see a bit of light through them and I know the answer is up there…I just have to be able to get through it. I grab a chair. *** I’ve known that Adam has been doing this for weeks, my gut probably knew from the beginning but I simply can’t do anything about it without him admitting to it. You see, he’s managed to bargain his way into the upper echelon of AWC. He’s managed to create the fabled ‘Glass Ceiling’ effect for all and sundry. With his relentless subjugation of the masses right here in AWC, he’s picked up something on each and every single person as he’s moved on and before they know it, Adam has all the keys to the castle. He’s untouchable backstage because whether Sasha knows it or not, she’s being used, day in and day out to impart knowledge and decrees at his leisure, under a cloak of massive proportions that she has no idea is being used. In essence, he’s controlling how and where people get to in the pecking order. This is why I’ve been marked out for special attention, because he needs to get that title back first. I’m still underneath the ceiling as it were, but he can’t knock me away until that title is gone from my waist. The title holds this possessive quality and he needs to strip it away before my damnation is complete. And so begins the game of toying with me, aggravating me until I’m simply too pissed off not to take the match. I think he’s made an error in his judgement simply because, now he has to face his entire plan. If he wins, he can get away with it and I stand alone, broken and left at the bottom. If he loses, his house of cards comes falling down from underneath him. What’s more…the very vein with which he has fashioned this house unsettles me because he has utilised several tactics, tactics which very few would be proud of. So they’re right up Dick’s alley. Chief on the list is the very one that is so talked up about in the sports industry and rightly too. It’s something that is another plague and just like all good strains of viruses, Adam has chosen to amalgamate something that is bad enough on its own, but destructive with him in tow: drugs. Such a dirty word and one that surprisingly has a longer history in wrestling than you might think. Steroids are such common place in this industry that sometimes it’s shocking how easily people are getting them. They’re supposed to be illegal and yet they’re widespread. I’m waiting for some kind of drug squad to kind of have the eureka moment in their think tank and just flick on the channel to SpikeTV and stare at one of the litany of WWE superstars and go…. ‘D’oh!!’ Regardless, steroids are everywhere and everyone has had their time to abuse them. Even me…even me. Anyone who has ever seen old footage of me, knows that I’ve paid for it before, and I’ve learned not to do it again. It still stands as a record in the past and one I am not liable to forget. Adam has been able to use information like this already and to great effect in AWC. Need I even mention Pierce Lavelle? The incriminating evidence suggests that Pierce Lavelle, like the Educator, had been a user of performance enhancing drugs in order to get ahead in the industry. So now we’ve got the year long ban spiked on Lavelle and while it looks convincing, the underlying truth is that deep down people know Lavelle wouldn’t have done such a thing. It’s just not in his character. He’s too pure and unstained. That’s why Adam had to do so much in order for it to happen. He had to execute a master plan in order to get across this point so thoroughly that everyone was fooled. He’s used the fear factor of the drugs to get his way and ultimately he ended up with what cemented him in the top echelons of wrestler; he got a world title out of it. Trouble is, it didn’t last too long and now he’s got to claw his way back towards it. The mentioning of the drugs seems inconsequential and that is the very point of my suggestion of them. Dick didn’t care about what he did in the process to get what he wanted. He didn’t care that he could be destroying a perfectly fine and technically gifted wrestler. He didn’t even care that he was sullying the very sport that he was attributing all his glory to. He just wanted a trinket to cement himself. It didn’t matter that the methods were so underhanded, all that mattered was the end result. This is why I need to deal with Adam Dick. He’s going to destroy everyone’s livelihoods in this industry if he carries on. The old hacks are wheezing and falling like flies as the new game in town arrives to dole out his own form of court. The house of cards have been stacked and he’s propped firmly at the top. To get rid of him is presenting a monumental task. That’s why I’m the only one who can do it. I represent the ultimate trade off for the poker player. The biggest win of them all and he takes home the prize for good. If he can win. *** 23rd March - Hotel I’m getting closer to the top now, I can see the light and I know that it’s the answer, I can see the pain and shock it is in being goaded and grabbed by all the question marks. I have the marker and I hastily try to write them out of the way, but all I realise is that I am writing out the light. I can’t do that, I must not do that! I use my elbow to rub it away and it works! Thank heavens for that, I’ve got my light back. Ah!!! The question marks, they are written on my arm now and they are moving, they are slowly churning and convulsing on my arm. I look at it and immediately begin to question, sealing my fate, questioning myself and only heeding the greater speed of growth of the creatures. What? Who? Where? Why? How???? I scream and stab out violently at my arm with the marker, trying to blacken them out. They jump and spark and play and flitter with my every move and I miss and only create more questions. What? Who? Where? Why? How??? The last I remember is feeling the chair fall from beneath my weight and all the questions tumbling down. There won’t be any of the king’s horses or any of the king’s men to put Jack Murphy back together again. *** 24th March - Hotel I jolted awake sweating in the bed and I sighed in the darkness, completely out of relief. That had better have been a dream. Sitting up I push my hand out to this very diary, finding it by the bedside table, still intact and with the pen sitting neatly on top of the diary. I sigh again and let things settle once more. I try to anyway. Because I can’t quite rest with one thing still niggling in the back of my head. There’s something that’s going to trouble me in my sleep, in fact I won’t sleep unless I check it out now. I flick on the light at the bedside table. I sigh again. And stare at the almost black walls, ceiling and floor…discarded markers lie by one corner of the bed. Question marks and names, possibilities and thoughts that I’ve had for the past month are laid bare all around me, staring coldly back at me. A literal diary entry, right there on the walls. I flicker through the pages of my diary and it’s all there, cataloguing each and every moment of it. Names appear more than once but only one is important. Adam Dick. This is all where it began and all where it will end. He’s been the one who’s started this entire thing and he’s the only one who can end it all. Twilight of the Gods is the place where I can beat it out of him and let the pain stop. That’s why I signed on for the match, not for everyone to see my dominance or to see my superiority, hell I don’t even think he’s that far behind me, no I signed on because I want him to admit the truth and prove to everyone one thing…. I’ve been right all along. All along. There’s nothing like vindication for your actions and Dick can give that to me. More than that he gives me the peace of mind…that I wasn’t losing in the first place. He is the conduit through which the entire masquerade he’s pulled can be freed. I can only find peace through Adam Dick. There are so many things that are sitting right now in my head and no matter what I do, they can only be unlocked with the key. The answer to this whole question is the key…and the key is the answer. Twilight of the Gods is coming and I’ve come to take my peace back.
