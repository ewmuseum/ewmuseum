Date: 23/12/05 The Spiralling Highs…the crippling lows Like a cat in a bag… Waiting to drown… This time I’m coming down… “Ah…Miss Sanford…you said you needed me urgently?” the brash Doctor Bamford muttered, his eyes swimming amidst a clipboard or charts, graphs and sheets of general analysis. In his arrogance, he had failed to even look over the counter to acknowledge the young receptionist’s courteous smile. He noted his conceit and smiled. He had every reason to be brazen. After all, this was hisjungle. He had spent eight years of his life at Yale, studying methodically the workings of the mind, gorging on every detail. He had been named the most promising young psychologist after only six months from leaving university in Times magazine and deservedly so after ‘the young upstart’ (as his fellow ‘peers’ on the psychology board had soon named him) put in place the theory of an equilibrium of chemicals working in conjunction to create the emotion of confusion. His career had gone from strength to strength. He left the psychology board of the small Welsh University of Cardiff, feeling the crusty old farts were more content to weigh him down than to get things done. He soon had his own small firm, his clients usually of the middle class background. Mostly men who’s Fathers had beaten them around and now they felt a deadwood to society. Yes, yes there, there, it’s alright, you are have merely taken the situation and construed it wrongly. Jesus Christ, he could have put that response on tape and played it after the bastard fucked up. Throw in a few big words, give him a prescription for baby aspirin and hey, hey, everyone’s a winner. This wasn’t to say he was a fraud. He dealt with the situation in the best way he could. These people were so fixated on their negativities as people, they had lost the ability to acknowledge good. The placebo he gave them reset this process. But Goddamnit, he was better than having to cheer insecure people up. And so he climbed higher. His reputation grew, his youth and charisma an instant appeal to his superiors who wished to adopt him as the son to the Alliance of American Psychologists. He did well. He got a 5000 dollar suit for his thirtieth birthday, a Porsche for his thirty-first. And now, at thirty-two, he stands here, challenging the tasks God puts in front of him, unravelling the greatest puzzle of them all, the human brain. At the right price of course because his services just wouldn’t be respected without the right money. He deals with some of the worst cases of mental disease in the state of Washington, in his own building of padded cells to keep the Candymen from destroying his lovely walls. “Yes…we have a man just brought in. He’s an employee of the Atlantic Wrestling Club who are-“ “Paying on his behalf. Good,” Bamford smirked. He liked doing that, taking the words out of others peoples mouth. It made him feel superior. Bamford edged his glasses up along the bridge of his nose and cleared his throat before producing a biro and scribbling down a signature onto a piece of paper in front of him. “He is displaying aggressive characteristics though he is consistently swinging into fits of laughter. He was brought in by his Mother and Father who feared for his safety. It would seem just in time do as this dossier shows.” Kate rose from her seat, taking a second to smooth down a crease on her knee length black shirt. She was a pretty girl, petite with a dazzling smile. But it didn’t matter how much blush she applied, it would seem Bamford had long since abandoned the notion she would be anything more than the Receptionist he liked to express his intellectual superiority over. She was fun in that respect. Kate handed over the dossier, flicking her electric blue eyes up to catch a glimpse of the handsome Doctor then sighed and looked away as he snatched it from her hand and walked away hurriedly in the opposite direction. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “So…Mr O’Shea…how are you?” Sanford cooed, the ‘charm’ now very much on. Different mentality with patients. You play with these guys, even hint to them you’re teasing, and they’ll snap your neck. The fact the man before him was strapped down to a chair, a straitjacket holding him rigid didn’t matter. He’d seen Lethal Weapon, and what that weiner Mel Gibson could do, anyone could do. “Aye’ll be a lot feckin’ better when ye LET ME OUT O’ THESE FECKIN’ STRAPS!” Paddy swung violently from side to side on the chair. The chair was bolted to the ground so technically, it was only him doing the swinging. Although calm only moments ago, right now, Paddy’s face was turning a faint shade of purple as he fought the straps desperately. Bamford felt a little nervous at the man’s unrelenting rebellion and glanced over his shoulder to assure himself the two ‘heavies’ were still very much there at the other side of the glass doors. Wait it out Ben…he’ll tire himself out soon enough. Three minutes later and Paddy was still screaming-resting-rebelling-rest-screaming whilst Bamford waited patiently on. He realised this large padded cell might be too large for the rowdy individual and began pondering the availability of the cells to the West wing when- “Fine, ye want t’ talk, let’s talk!” Paddy hissed, his eyes darting about the room looking for any means of escape. Bamford didn’t smile in satisfaction. Once again, dragon baiting wasn’t a good idea. “Okay…first of all, I want you to know this is a safe environment. I want you to feel comfort-“ “Cut the crap. Aye’ve been in these places before. Aye know yer type with yer prodding and all tha’. So skip tha’ and just get whatever ye have to do, o’er with,” Paddy barked, his eyes now unmoving from the sombre expression of the Doctor. “Fine…I suppose you’re too intelligent for me to feed you that. So let’s get down to business then, shall we? Okay, what do you remember, prior to being in here, what was the last thing you did?” Paddy started, holding the gaze another few moments, before looking away disgusted. He remembered, that was obvious but whether he chose to recall said events was another issue. “Aye…well….” Paddy looked away damningly and shut his eyes making the memories that much easier to bear. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “Aye’m the devil! HAHA!” Paddy cackled as he clenched his grip around his mother’s arm, that much tighter. He was angry, maybe not at his mother or anyone indeed in his family, but the voice inside his head bade him over and over again: Make them weep and cry for their forgotten son! Let them feel your pain! Paddy looked into his Mother’s eyes. She lay on the ground, whimpering, holding her face as if she had been cut with a blade. Paddy seen this and it pleased him. His nails dug further into her arm. He fleetingly glanced over to his side and seen his Father unconscious on the floor. That also pleased him and he grinned again. “Paddy…please….aye did nothing-“ Sheilia mumbled from underneath a flood of tears. “Silence woman! The devil stands before ye tonight!” Paddy let go of his Mother momentarily, struggling to recall why he indeed was here. He had been at the bar that much was certain, and he had been drowning his sorrows in something he had already forgotten. Mission accomplished P, pat yourself on the back. Then he had staggered back to his family caravan and promptly decided to be Satan for the night. He didn’t want to hurt them, just see how far he could push them before they cracked. Just like him, fragmented China on it’s final plunge to the floor below. Only thing was, his Father had turned violent and his screams of pain for mankind, so he had been forced to get violent back. “Tonight…Paddy ends all his worries, hates an’ pains in a sacrifice. Ye are chosen t’ be tha’!” Sheila screamed. The memories of her husband being knocked out with a vase being swung unprovoked form her son, being replaced with genuine fear. Paddy clasped a hand in front of her face and brought his head close to hers. He was finding it difficult to speak, his eyes opening and closing systematically, yet he still found the energy to say the words cycling amidst his brain. “Ye…my family…have hurt me so much. Aye’m sorry, but aye need closure.” Paddy, his foot holding his mother to the base of the caravan, slipped his hand into his jacket pocket and removed a switchblade. He flicked it open, and with a menacing grin, crouched down on her. She screamed, he advanced, she screamed again, he swiped and in his drunkenness, swiped too short. He raised the knife over his head when he was centimetres from her, making sure his blow was good, and then swung. Then he was cut off. Pieces of another vase sprinkled over his eye sight as he felt himself drop. Lurching backwards then violently forwards. He reached an equilibrium then was knocked from this by a blow to the back of the head. He fell forwards now, twisting in the air so that he missed his Mother and lay face up. He gazed up at the man he had moments ago, crushed, the base of an old vase in his hands. He was crimson with fury. “Ye son of a bitch! Aye’ll kill y-“ Then Danny O’Shea swung his boot and there was no more. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “So….in a fit of unprovoked anger, you assaulted your Father and attempted to kill your Mother. You state they provoked nothing and that you only travelled to their residence for accommodation. Do you stand by this statement?” Paddy covered his face with his hands. “Tha’ is none o’ yer business…now jus’ let me out of here, okay?” Bamford sighed. He didn’t like uncooperative patients but had found techniques to deal with them. It was a challenge, one he accepted though not enthusiastically. “Let’s just talk some more…Now Paddy, you hit the lows right there. It’s something in the business we call the crippling lows. To get these lows, you need to be brought down from the highs. Something gave you a feeling of euphoria because nothing short of this situation leads to such a violent bring down…so why don’t you tell me about it?” Bamford peered over the top of his glasses, curiously analysing the aging wrestler’s face. He found no understanding there. He continued to stare, occasionally scribbling a note or two while Paddy thought. “It’s…none o’ yer business…” Bamford smiled sympathetically. “Look, I need to know the past to help you shape the future. Please…anything you say here will he confidential.” “Fine…” Paddy murmured, his face beginning to blush. “Aye’ll tell ye...but if ye laugh, aye’ll kill ye, aye swear!” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ It had come. Too long he had contemplated, too long he had dilly dallied around. His feelings were clear to all that seen them, like a museum on show to the masses. He loved her. He never had had a girlfriend before, gypsies weren’t that lucky. He had struggled through his teenage years with crushes, always confusing lust for love, always failing to distinguishing what his heart and what his brain wanted. There was no genuines back then so when Shelly came to him, he really should have known this was it. This is love, so what took you so long hm? Mike…Wade. Aye can’t…won’t let him get her. He won’t hurt her through me…by help me God… And so, arriving in Shelly’s hometown of New York mid week, he had decided enough was enough, they would be the inseparable through thick and thin. He would hold her and care for her, never letting harm fall on a single hair on her head. He’d seen death and my, it was overrated. People weeping then letting it go. It wasn’t true emotion because mourning took the grief away. This was true, this was everlasting, this was right. Paddy, knocked on her door, and suddenly he felt nervous, all his thoughts of Romeo and Juliet suddenly evaporating into the atmosphere. All those teenage years gone. All linking a prosperous present to a painful past. He knocked again and finally heard a clack clack clack from the floor inside. Aye love ye Shelly, more than anythin’ aye have…an’ aye need t’ show ye tha’. The door swung open and there stood Shelly, slightly taken aback by her on off boyfriends’ unexpected appearance. She began to open her mouth, the corners of her mouth, teasing her auburn hair that clung to her cheeks. “Aye need somethin’…” Paddy murmured, fearing to raise his eyes encase his boldness suddenly drained away. “Er…what’s that…?” Shelly murmured back. She ran her delicate hand over her jaw, temporarily shielding the bashfulness, rising from her cheeks. “Aye…need t’ kiss ye…t’ show ye ay-“ Paddy was interrupted as Shelly dived in with her lips, her kiss firm and warm and sweet. Her breasts pushed against his T-Shirt, her hips against his crotch. Her hips moved away gently and he plunged his hands into her hair and then moved against her, on her doorstep. He pulled away for a second, his lips mere millimetres from her. “We…never change…an’ aye can’t do this without ye… Aye love ye.” Shelly teased his lips with hers and uttered a little gasp before putting her face against the side of her neck. He felt the hint of a tear on his skin, warm and joyful. “Come on…quick…” She took his hand and pulled him into the house. He slide the door shut closed with his heel. Then he was being guided upstairs. All the emotions intermixed suddenly became distilled. Was it excitement? The fact he was facing an ultimate bond between man and women for the first time? Was it nervousness? Fear of the future suffocating his very breathe. Was it guilt? That, yet not one hundred percent commitment, he was diving into choices he didn’t know he was making? Or all of the above? There was insane joy, there was crippling fear…these feelings would not mix pleasantly yet felt necessary. Pain before pleasure he whispered. He thought fleetingly of his beliefs, his chastity and the bosom of virginity he had nestled in for so long. He was a Roman Catholic, strong and content. And now he was walking away, being guided towards a bedroom he doesn’t know he’s ready for. Creeping into his thirties and the fearless gypsy from Galway was afraid. Shelly, not looking at him, turned the knob and pushed the door open before leading his inwards into the darkness. They were inside and she turned and looked at him, eyes bright and her cheeks flushing different shades of crimson. Her chest was rising and falling rapidly. He took her in his arms suddenly, his knees quivering from the feelings dancing in his brain, and was overwhelmed by the rightness of the situation – the circle of present and future, all developing into one seamlessly. Triumphant and strong. He kissed her, and then kicked the door shut clumsily with one foot. She breathed laughter into his mouth. “My heart-“she uttered and then lifted his hand and pressed it to her chest. The racing pulse boomed against his hand. She back stepped and sat down on her bed instinctively. He sat beside her and kissed her once again. Her hand slipped inside his shirt then out again, her finger tracing a straight line down his chest. She rubbed his toned torso; outlining individual abs as muscles he didn’t know existed sprang to life centimetres below. He pulled her away for a second and looked into her grey eyes. Her smiled faded as he looked down. “No regrets,” he said distinctly and proud as if vindicating his reasoning to his very self. Her smile returned, her lips drawing together into a tiny smile that reminded him of a women pulling the strings of her purse closed. “No regrets.” Their lips touched, buttons unbuttoned, she arched and he entered. I love you he thought. I love you more, she thought back. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Paddy O’Shea was beaming a faint shade of red as he looked into the young doctor’s eyes. He felt embarrassed. He was a modest man, not one to boast of sexual ventures. He loved her and then loved her, his lips still trembling from his touch. But it was his feelings and sharing them was hard. He waited for the Doctor to speak, refusing to explain himself. “I see…” “Can aye go now…so aye had sex…it really isn’t anything is it.” Don’t lie Paddy. Don’t lie to yourself. “All I’m doing is building a portfolio. You went from incredulously in love, to an attempted murderer. So…what happened between those events? Something brought you from the spiralling heights to the crippling lows. What was it Paddy. What hurt you so much you couldn’t stand?” Paddy looked down at his knees, and then clanged them together. It hurt, the tinge of bruising ensured this. Here is your gypsy king peasants! Ready your missiles, ready your insults. The gypsy king walks amongst you. Transfer your hurt to him, he can take it. Put the burden on him. He will hold. Though he struggles. He will hold. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BANG! “Gah?” Paddy lifted his head from his sweat drenched pillow. Two days had passed since that momentous night, something that had left him floating higher than the plane that had carried him home. I love you he had murmured all the way home, allowing the sound to click off his tongue. BANG! BANG! Is that the door? Now he was back in Hartford, England, spending his tenure here as the AWC trailblazed through the East Atlantic tour. Back in the little town, so backwards and hateful that they had been planning for two weeks, how they would remove that ‘pykie’ from the park. Yet Paddy hadn’t seen this. BANG! BANG! That was your final warning Paddy…sorry. Paddy was pushing himself from his bed when the caravan door swung open, forced by a shoulder charge. He sleep encrusted eyes blinked lazily as three men rushed in, their faces hid by balaclavas. They rushed towards him, baseball bats in hand. “Consider this your warning….” SMACK! A bat struck his spine and he curled upwards as a cry of pain fell from his lips. Then he sunk back into the bed, too slow to the react as bats were raised overhead. “Pykie’s aren’t wanted here!” SMACK! The bat cracked his left knee and he almost screamed as he felt his joints bend in ways they should never have had to bend. SMACK! There goes the other knee. How ironic, he’d left Ireland to escape kneecappings. For some reason he wasn’t laughing at this present time. “Like I said…last chance…get out now or else!” SMACK! The bat came down on his head and he knew no more. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Just another tale from a broken home… Attack on all sides. He was feeling it everywhere. His job, his home, his country his abode. Everywhere, everyone, wanted a piece of O’Shea. “And so you cracked…you’re alcohol fuelled binge taking you to perhaps the root of all these trouble…your family.” Paddy bowed his head. Mike Wade, the man he’d loved. The Judas who’d cut him deeper than any man ever could. Hate, the man who’d tortured him in every thing he did, now suddenly perhaps helping him? The men who’d broke into his home like so many before them, in their fear hurting the one man afraid that much more. The one true love he held in his arms days before. What’s to say that she wasn’t taken from him by any of them? Why do you hurt me God…? Is my infidelity that much harder to bear for you? “Yes…aye cracked…” Bamford sighed, his conclusion already drawn his head. Smiling beside himself, another puzzle solved, he cleared his throat. “Manic depressant I suspect. The Spiralling highs to the crippling lows have brought this upon you. I don’t blame you. To feel such euphoria to be brought down that much is natural. Amidst a backdrop of hate and pain, this was the only thing your mind could do to neutralise you, to save you from going insane.” Manic Depressant. Have they hurt me so much aye’m losing my mind? Paddy bowed his head yet further, until his forehead was clutched between his knees. Manic Manic, maniac going down further and further, going down. “And that’s why…you need a break from the AWC, the source of your problems, starting from now.” No…everything that’s ever been worked for is millimetres away. “No…aye…need t’ think about everythin’ please.” Bamford nodded and pushed himself to his feet. Then he exited the room and locked the door behind him. Another love crazed success-aholic, he thought. Then he thought about his girlfriend Shelly and the fine ass he’d described.
