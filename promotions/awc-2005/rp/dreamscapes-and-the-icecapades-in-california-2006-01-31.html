Dreamscapes And The Icecapades!... in California.

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 31st Focusing Darcy Markson's home, California... Post-Fresh! In the middle of his home, the most beautiful woman he'd ever seen was laying on her back with legs stretched, shins to the tip of her nose. Her arms laid flat on the floor, and her pants were doing some interesting things - not to mention her slightly shifted tanktop. All of those things in mind, and all he could do was offer her breakfast. ''I don't eat in the morning.'' She shifted to her stomach in a smooth motion and bent her legs backwards at the hip, nearly touching her outcropped shoulderblades to toenails. ''That, uhh... that yoga book you were reading on the way really... umm, yeah. It's working miracles...'' Since when did her body look this good? The girl he'd known had always had a fairly soft belly and poor posture. Now, he saw grace and the lines of gorgeously stretched muscles. This wasn't the sloppy, farthest thing from an 'athlete' to compete in a sport he knew. After winning her tag bout with Ellis Nash, she'd come back home with Darcy Markson - back to where they both once lived - and had used the excuse that it was easier than flying home to Canada between shows. Obviously, he wasn't the only one thinking about the odd situation in that moment, either... She stretched her arms out to the sides, ''Just so you know, I'm only here because that idiotic road trip last week reminded me that I'm best when I'm not alone between shows. It's nothing like what you probably think, so keep it in your p--'' ''Amy, as I recall you're the one with the supercharged sex drive. I was about to tell you the same.'' '' 'Supercharged sex drive'?!'' Suddenly, it was as if the aura of calm and beauty in her movement had been shattered. Amy spun around and sat with her back half-bent and arms resting on her knees, staring daggers through Darcy from her position on the floor, ''What the hell's that supposed to mean? Sounds more like a dream than anything.'' ''Remember Amsterdam? YOU were the one coming on to ME. Not to mention half the guys in the bar. No offense, but if I just wanted to fuck you I could have done it right there, without the difficulties of flying you out to California with me.'' That was hard. Darcy hadn't expected it - the misguided anger and displaced passion that ultimately drew a rift between them was gone. Sure, she was still angry and passionate, but something was... different. Darcy was captivated, but he still had his pride. ''There's a big difference between flirting drunkenly and getting off on someone,'' she huffed, ''A difference you obviously haven't yet discovered. Now gimmie that damned bagel you were offering before.'' ''Flirt? Since when does Amy Campbell flirt?'' Darcy asked, as he fetched her a bagel from the bag. ''From what I remember your courting attempts are even more caveman than mine - 'YOU. MANBEAST. COME TO AIMZ.' '' As she guffawed, he twitched. FLUX ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You can't fight a fever. You can't fight an army. She woke up, strapped to a table in absolutely unfamilliar territory. Everything was white or silvery, every stare was cold and complacent... but she knew where she was. It was the future, a time where she didn't belong. But she was being made to. The sting of a doctor withdrawing a needle hit both of her arms. Something cold was pulled out from under her kneecaps. One of the doctors had two white eyes. Obviously mechanical. She wasn't sure where her eyes even were. Conscious of what was around her, but not even the timeline made sense. There was cold air behind her eyes, behind the nagging headache. Lightheadedness overtook her and washed away most senses, except the pull in her spine. When she went to speak, Amy found that she had no mouth. There weren't words. There wasn't a language for her feelings. We're creating a machine. Sit quietly, Amy... and don't you dare move. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hobbies. PAST times. Continuing... ''You want butter or cream cheese?'' Amy grimaced, ''I hate anything cheese-related. If you can remember what I was like 'courting' you, you've gotta remember that.'' The odd face quickly turned to an even more unusual form of sneer, ''Unless you were... I dunno, forgetting important things in favor of remembering what you wished you'd seen? Nothing with tits would have to 'court' you, Darcy.'' No matter what had changed about her focus in life, that little redhead would never lose the speed with which she could unsheath her claws - that was for sure. ''Oh come on, that's unfair. You know I expect my tits to at least hold up their end of the conversation. And I'm a man of class these days, I'll have you know...you're not the only one who's done some growing up the past few years.'' That much was certain. While Darcy may have been an ''adult'' when the two were together, at least from a legal standpoint, he in many ways resembled the misguided teenager that Amy had been. He handed her the bagel, but she set it at her side with little interest and continued staring at him. Part of her wanted to smile, because he was wearing the Guano Apes t-shirt she used to sleep in when he was away... but more of her cringed at that thought, ''You? Growing up? Sorry, I haven't seen it.'' She sighed, ''Maybe you'll eventually prove it without abducting me to California or running after me like a puppy humping my leg for a decent match. How was last week, by the way? I can't help but notice that you're absent from the card this week... management didn't have any nobodies left for you to fight?'' ''Well of course you haven't seen it. The last time you had a good look at me, Puddle of Mudd was still allowed to make my fucking eardrums bleed.'' Seeing that Amy had given up on that poor bagel, Darcy snatched it and had a bite. '' 'Nnd you know,'' he added, swallowing, ''It's not my fault they keep throwing hacks at me. AWC lobs them up, I knock them out of the park, I guess. Maybe they didn't throw another one my way because management wants it to get through your skull that yes, I CAN beat you.'' FLUX ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You can't fight a fever. You won't fight an army. Her arms tingled and her chest was hard, rock hard and full of a sensation she'd never felt before. She was having a heart attack on that table. They pulled a node from her ear and it replaced the feeling of cold air with a sharp burning. She tried to cry out. Her head turned involuntarily, and a wire was being extracted from the back of her neck - right at the base of her head. It didn't hurt. The extractions were starting to feel like squeezing a pimple. The pain only lasted until that refreshing emptying burst, to then be replaced by an uncomfortable ache. Everything in her stomach tightened, and her vision was replaced by a sequence of deadly images: War, genocide, bloodbaths, broken bones... fighting rings. You're changing. You've been the same for centuries. There's an Amy Campbell in every era, even if she was named Joan of Arc or Cleopatra. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Handling Continuing... ''Christ, Darcy, get over yourself!'' Amy went back to her yoga poses - flattening onto the ground and pulling her knees to her chest, ''The most physicality I've seen from you in four years was when you handed me that damned bagel a minute ago. I'd be amazed if you had the energy to masturbate, let alone fight ME.'' ''Well how about you drop those sissy aerobics and put your money where your mouth is, Amy? I can take you here in this kitchen with nobody watching or in a wrestling ring with thousands taken aback by the sheer brutality.'' Within a half of a second, Amy was smiling from ear to ear. The prospect of a real fight always stirred something deep within her, the confrontation caused overwhelming joy... ''Alright, we'll do it. I just wanna see if you can still pack any punch.'' ...Basically, she was a nutcase and proved it by loving no drug more than a good punch in the head. And she stood up, licking her lips through her bared teeth. She bit her lip and got within a foot of Darcy, making no attempts to intimidate him. Rather, she was waiting. ''C'mon,'' the redhead patted the left side of her face, ''On the chin, on the cheek... surprise me and make the first move.'' ''Are you sure? The last time you asked me to clock you you ended up on the ground tapping out. Maybe you'd like to try a different approach...?'' Amy opened her arms, but it wasn't for a hug, ''I'm prone. Shut up and take care of business, unless your threats were empty.'' Darcy offered his arms out as well, and the two locked up. With an obvious strength advantage, Darcy overpowered and moved her into a headlock. ''Sorry I took that bagel from you...perhaps I could interest you in some lovely armpit?'' Darcy quipped. But before long, Amy slipped out and gave him a sharp kick to the thigh. ''Let's see how you handle real wrestling, jackass.'' FLUX ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You can't fight a fever. You don't fight an army. Bloodbaths. Fighting rings. Exterminating the opposition is the only way to survive in this future. Everybody needs to be stronger. They need machines. They need to make themselves the machines. Not robots, but monsters. Elite creatures, beautiful on the outside but ghastly in their force. The future in her head, visions of what she needs to be. They pulled the pins out of her elbow crooks and took the binds from her ankles. The needles through her were still paralyzing her hands. One came out of her right palm, and the ensuing spasm garnered grins from the scientists. Her breathing sped up again. A feverish slumber is a gift. You can find many things. You can realize the breadth of what you need to be. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Shadow Boxing Continued... Quickly manuevering behind him, Amy lept onto his back and applied a reverse sleeperhold. Darcy frantically waved to move her off, which was precisely the opening she was looking for. Amy released the hold and took Darcy down by the shoulder, pressing all of the weight she could muster down on him as she did so. ''Not so funny now!'' But Darcy wasn't done. Lowering himself, he brought Amy to the ground with a tackle. She tried to regroup, but Darcy already had a hold of her leg. Within moments, she found herself trapped in a figure-four leglock in the middle of his tiled floor. '''Well, well, isn't this a familiar position,'' Darcy said with a quick laugh, ''And look, you're even fighting to keep from screaming!'' He was right, it hurt. It couldn't not hurt, and she hadn't been psyched for a fight up until thirty seconds before the hold was applied. It wasn't a good position to be in, especially after all of the talk she'd spit. As hard as she could, Amy tried to concentrate on calming every muscle in her body. From her arms, to her shoulders and neck right down to her legs, she went limp. Next was breathing... steady, in and out until her chest didn't feel so rock-solid. The near meditative state kept her from considering pain - it was a trick she planned to use from then on in the ring. She had to survive this without twisting a muscle the wrong way, though... and that was proving to be difficult. While he wouldn't injure her, Darcy knew how to keep something locked in - especially the emotional grip he'd had on her for nearly five years. The wrestling hold hurt a little less, but it was definitely a more urgent affair. She wriggled before applying the same calming technique to her body again, surprising Markson by sustaining herself through the uncomfortable hold for as long as she was. A minute had passed, and he was tightening the grip every ten seconds or so... but she remained breathing steadily. That was DEFINITELY something she needed to learn to channel and use in the ring... but again, there was a more pressing matter at hand; Escaping the leglock with some dignity left. That, however, didn't seem likely. The kitchen tiles were causing an irritating friction against her arms, and she'd started to get lightheaded from the yoga breaths. Finally, she relented. ''You win. Cheaply, but...'' she groaned, trying to wriggle from him, ''You still win. I can't get out of this right now.'' ''I'll let go, but only if you promise to raise my arm. And you have to announce me the winner. AND you have to do it in the James Brunt voice!'' Darcy said, as he cinched on the hold just enough to allow Amy to pull herself free. ''You want me to touch you and talk like a man?'' Amy snickered, rubbing sore legs and seating herself back on the floor, inched against a cupboard for back support, ''Now it's like old times.'' Darcy rolled his eyes. ''Well I'm not gay, but there's something about the Jimmy B voice that gets me going,'' he said as he offered her his hand to pull herself up. ''Come on, get up off the floor... I paid a lot for these chairs, you know.'' When Darcy walked to sit at the table, Amy followed. It was an odd transition, but she'd been beaten fair and square. Only when her pride was bare could Aimz have a normal conversation, and Markson had just twisted that pride enough to entice her to listen to him. She grabbed a bottle of water from the counter and sat down, still aching. FLUX ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You can't fight a fever. You will fight an army. Human perfection had played out as the most painful process imaginable. When they pulled the final blades from her wrists, the doctors - scientists, or whatever they were - smiled. Perfect. Electricity split her psyche with a wicked headache. It was as if she'd been shocked back to consciousness. She shook, and she rose. Her existance had been fighting. In a production that had played out in her fever-induced nightmares, she was taken and made to be a warrior. She was improved upon, and the features she needed to leash had been honed. A dream can only do so much. It's in your mind, it sorts your purpose out for you. Take it from here, Amelia. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Reopening Your Favorite Book Ending... ''Talk.'' What? Nobody ever said that lowering her pride made her any more polite. ''Would it kill you to be a little cordial for once? You might as well try to act like you enjoy talking to me, since you came out to the other side of the continent to see me and all.'' ''Come on, Darcy - I'm a Canadian, it's currently snowing like a bitch and WELL below zero in Nova Scotia... my motivations for being here don't need to revolve around you, you know.'' ''Well, what are they?'' Amy sighed, ''I needed some company while I was clearing my head. There, you've got it. If I was home alone, I'd be obsessing over winning and boosting myself up to the point that anything short of killing my opponent would be considered failure. I need to get healthy and alive again, and I can't do it sitting at my place watching it snow and looking at internet stats.'' That wasn't exactly what Darcy was looking to hear. The smirk on his face that he'd held since forcing her to submit just moments earlier was suddenly gone. ''Somehow that doesn't add up, Amy. Nobody you could spend some time with besides your ex-boyfriend/coworker who lives thousands of miles away? Either you're using me, or you're holding something back...both of which are scenarios that make me feel like I did the right thing asking you out here.'' ''I'm using you. Isn't it so obvious?'' she shrugged and gave a hearty laugh, ''Seriously... you're worse than me for conspiracy theories. Just because you're not privy to every thought or feeling I have doesn't mean I'm being sneaky or secretive. I'm not going to fuck you over here, Darcy. Hell, I'm not even sizing you up for some big eventual matchup. Up until twenty minutes ago, I was relaxing and trying to get myself into the shape I want to be, and in peace - without my obsessive little head fucking me over. It doesn't matter what I think of you, but take it as a good sign that I'm here.'' Darcy sighed. Somehow it all seemed so simple - 'Invite her on a road trip. Bring her back to the place where things had disintegrated all those years ago. Let the emotions that followed you two for so long to swirl up again.' Yet he hadn't expected the chill in her heart; he knew that getting her to open up would likely be a chore, but he didn't think the same thing would happen to him as well. ''Believe it or not...it does matter what you think of me.'' ''Believe it or not... you won't find out.'' It had to be the hardest conversation either of them had with anybody in years. Amy looked him square in the eye from across the table. ''I've made the mistake of losing my grip almost every day of my life,'' she continued, ''And that's over. There's a reason I'm not granting you the right to get me in a ring. You can physically hurt me all you'd like, but I'm too easy to overcome psychologically in there. I've spent four years with this funny little chronic pain in the left side of my chest, and I'm gonna find a way to end it. I'm not letting you open up whatever wound you left there all over again.'' ''Amy, I don't want to hurt you. I don't even expect you to trust me. But there're some things you have to know, whether you believe them or not. I didn't set out to cause you pain. I never forgot what I did to you, and there isn't a day that goes by that I wish things had gone differently. As much as I wish I could, I can't change the past...but I would like to put it behind me.'' She blinked at him for a moment, digesting what was said... and her reply was in true, frank Campbell style, ''How do you propose to do that?'' ''That's why I've been pressing so hard for this match. I want to close the old era. We NEED to close it. After that... that's all I ask. After that, if you want me to disappear...I'll do it. I need to know how you feel, Ames...'' ''How I feel about what? 'Closing an old era' by wrestling you? Fighting might be life for us, but we don't have to do it... for Christ's sake, Darcy. It's not like me not to say exactly what I think, but you're too lost to hear any of it anyway.'' ''I'm not lost, Amy... we're just on different wavelengths. I can't get over that my career didn't end the way it should have. You can't get over what happened between us, personally. I want to reconcile them both...really, I do. I don't know how the hell to convince you I'm not such a bad guy, but I'm willing to try. But you have to realize that this is important to me. And it should be important to you too. We both deserve a chance to tear the house down, FIVE years overdue, and my career deserves the proper ending... to finally stand face to face with the girl who I owe what little fame I have to.'' The little redhead sat in an awkward silence before she realized exactly what she needed to set aside, and said it. ''We'd need to get past the personal parts before I can safely step between the ropes with you, Darcy. I can't have that hanging over me. It'd be nearly impossible to reconcile what happened to us...'' Her eyes were cast downward until she raised her head with an oddly soft smile, ''But that doesn't mean we can't try, right?'' Coming from who they were, the words were shocking. She was preparing to cast all things aside to better herself, to retain focus and most of all, to improve and harness the negative energies that overcame her so they could be used to gain advantage over anything and everything. The night, sleep and the flu that she'd been trying to distract herself from might not be fun to endure, but with prolific agreements and the beginnings of insight, that day was bound to end well. FINAL FLUX ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You can't fight the fever... She awoke in a sweat and complete darkness, save for the moonlight peeking through the blinds of the guest bedroom. She scanned her body, grasping and pulling at limbs until her dream-induced was satisfied. It had only been a nightmare... but what was it saying about purpose? Her whole life HAD been fighting. She'd worked the earlier day to calm her body and tolerate pain, both mental and physical and in the presence of a rather distracting human being. Her nightmare had stemmed from a subconscious need, something that needed to be let out. Dreams are dreams, but purpose had become suddenly clear. It might not make sense to anyone else, but it struck a chord within her that couldn't be ignored - an urgent need to improve upon herself and channel the features that made her the fighter she was in certain moments. Leashing that energy would prove to be difficult and painful, like pulling pins from yourself... but the sense of self would make it all worth the work. Aimz shook, and rose to begin her second day of focus. ... but you're ready to fight an army.
