Stressing Thoughts

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/06/05 I opened my light blue eyes as my chrome Sony alarm went off at 6:30 am. I was tired as my body had not yet recovered from the beating I took as I left the GTWF arena for the last time. But my choice had been made and it was the best choice I ever made, I joined the ATLANTIC WRESTLING CLUB. And I was going to be in my first match on the Fresh! Show next week on the 14th of June. I was certain I was going to win. I wanted to make a name for myself as a wrestler and winning this match was the only way I was going to get my recognition. Although I would be wrestling a couple of people I have never heard of nor anyone else has. But then thought came to me, apart from the GTWF I was unheard of. So as I thought before I was going to win this match and get my recognition, but I had more of a drive now, I want to be in the top ten, but most of all I wanted the, Relentless championship. As the morning past and my hard thinking stopped I climb out of bed and threw on a black tank top on, a pair of black adidas sweat pants and a pair of nike tn’s and headed for the gym. I walked through to my red Gym room, climbed onto my treadmill plugged my earphones into it and started to watch the AWC newcomers show on fox, and saw the physical standard of evolution and void. Void only stood at 6”ft tall which may seem large to many a person but I could seriously say he was small, but evolution is the smallest only standing at 5”7ft. although these guys are pretty rippling in a muscular way, they did not seem to intimidate me. I am a 7”2ft giant and I’m facing a couple of pieces of chewing gum on my size 16 feet. I started to remember my GTWF matches though and how a 5”4 man beat me down. But my GTWF days are behind me, but I could not get the thoughts out of my head. So I tried to focus on my match. In one corner I’ve got ‘Void’ who thinks he’s a member of G-unit and thinks he’s a bad style person because he stole a catalogue from in front of an old women’s house when he was 9, and know because he’s a heel he thinks he’s going to be the most hated person in a federation to date. But in the other corner I got a face but he thinks he at a ‘higher level’ of wrestling than everyone. But stepping in the ring with me will change that once and for all. I carried on watching the show and saw Void go on about him hating Russians and egotism. And how heartless he was and unpredictable and about his scars. After he steps in the ring and gets a BDB he will have more scars then he can count. See me, I think he should calm down. Because he says he hates people with big ego’s but he has one. But here’s me going on about Void when I’ve got another bitch facing me as well. But hey, I think I’ll leave him out of this at the moment, he isn’t exactly going to be a ‘problem’ in the match. I perceive him as more of a Extra, like on will and grace, the people that never talk or do anything. But Void may be more of a threat, because well he is a little piece of crap that will easily get on my nerves and in my way, and you shouldn’t get in my way. I climbed off my treadmill and went onto my brand spanking new Step-o-meter, stair climber machine thingy. I’m not really technical with these things. And began to ponder the possibility of me losing, at the moment I know myself that I am jumping to conclusions to these superstars. They are good enough to get into the AWC so they must be tough enough. The possibility at this moment is a 30% - 30% - 40%. The forty percent being me. But no offence to the little guys, hehe, little guys. But I suppose they are thinking the same stuff as I think of them but about me. I bet there calling be a cocky giant that smokes pink cigars. But this is how I perceive them and this is what matters to me. But I have to get back to training so lets stop thinking about the way I perceive them and the way they are. The way there physical strength is and not the way there height is or how they look. They look like a couple of tough S.O.B’s. I stepped off the step-o-meter and went downstairs. My mind is not it’s self seen as though I am making transactions and major changes in my life ate the moment. Maybe I need a sleep, I have been training for three hours. I staggered upstairs trying to figure out if what I thought was true and if the stress is getting to me. So I lay down and fell asleep…..still going over things in my head. {Sorry if I offended anyone in this RP, I am trying to show how my character is and how the stress of moving shows/programs can change a persons way of thinking and really stress them out} View Jonny Sake 's Biography
