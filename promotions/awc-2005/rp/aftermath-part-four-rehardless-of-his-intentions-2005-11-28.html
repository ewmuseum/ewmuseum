Aftermath Part Four: Rehardless of His Intentions...

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/11/05 Yeah, I know precisely what you’re thinking: what is the deal with that lunatic? What is his malfunction? Why is Chainz such a freak? I jumped across the adjacent building, landing with considerable grace, not stopping to swim in my own glory - I’ll save that for once the task is completed. I use the momentum continue, at full pace, to the edge of the building. I was within ten feet of edge, when it happened. A small explosion, which crumbled the delicate brickwork and also propelled me backward, with forces. I hit the concrete flooring, hard, grazing my left elbow upon impact. I mean, come on! Dressing up in my clothes, prancing around in front of a mirror in makeup and a wig? Nutbar can’t describe Chainz well enough. I wheeze, the combination of force from the explosion and the impact, knocking the wind out of my body. Lying on back, I see them both gather around, standing over me, with menacing grins spread across their faces. The twisted nature of them remind me of him, the lunatic stalking me, leering over me as though I belonged to him. Obsessed with me. Wanting me in ways he’ll never experience. I think he knows this already, but that does not stop him from staling me. That does not stop him from wanting me. I very much doubt anything will. The one on my right grins at the one on my left. “Sure this is her?” The one on the left sighs and glares at his friend. “She was jumping from building to building wasn’t she?” The one on my right develops a blank expression. “Oh, right. You want to finish her off?” The one on my left nods and pulls out his sidearm. “Yeah.” “Good,” the one on right my declares, “I’d hate to kill something with such a pretty face.” And who would’ve thought he could pull off a womanly voice so well? Hell, if I wasn’t so weirded out by the whole situation I would’ve commented on it, or at least complemented him. Pulling off such a good impression and the, almost, professional way he’d put on the make-up, it’s almost as if he’s done this many times before. I’d heard enough. Learnt enough. I performed a kip-up and was on my feet immediately. The two clowns were surprised, but that would only last a moment. I knocked the gun out of the hands of the one of the left, then spun around with a roundhouse kick on the one on my right. He flew backward, crashing into a small wall with force. This allowed the one on my left to recuperate enough to throw a punch in my general direction. I parried the blow with my left hand, then hit him with a vicious palm strike across his jaw with my right hand. He fell to the floor motionless. More damn assassins trying to get in my way. I’m not even near the target yet and I’ve two sets of these clowns trying to stop me. I’ve never even seen, nor heard of these clowns before. I have to wonder how many more of these asshole’s am I going to run into before I reach my ultimate goal. I’ve already encountered too many for my liking. Regardless of his intentions. Regardless of his crazy-ass nature. I’ve had enough and I have decided to get the point across of Chainz the only way he’ll understand. Violence.
