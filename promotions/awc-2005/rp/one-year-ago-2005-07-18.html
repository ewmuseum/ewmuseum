One Year Ago

18 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/18/05 One year ago… sometime in March 2004 to be precise… “What do you mean I can’t return this???” My life officially sucks. And I’m not just saying that because I’m 22 years old and working at the customer service desk of a Best Buy. I’m saying that because a little over a year ago I was a starter on the Washington Huskies football team. It’s actually a relatively short story to how I ended up here a year later, my dad died and I had a hard time dealing with it and eventually left the team and school. I could probably get into more detail on that subject, but I really don’t feel like it at the moment. A few months of doing nothing and the rest of my family on my case to move on with life led to nothing but a crappy eight dollar an hour job where I basically got paid to be bitched at for hours. “Like I just told you, our return policy is only 30 days and it’s been almost a 100. You can’t return this.” Bitched at by idiots to be more specific, right now I’m dealing with some middle aged loser in a suit. A typical loser with a inferiority complex because he works at a typical cubicle office job where the boss probably rides on his ass all day so he has to come into stores and give the lowly retail employee a hard time. “Well it’s certainly not my fault that my computer stopped working-“ “Yes, I’m aware of that-“ before I can say anything else, I’m rudely interrupted. “Let me just talk to a manager! You obviously don’t know what your doing!” I start to turn around to get the manager the jackass requests, but then I pause. I don’t have to take this kind of shit anymore. I can either continue living my life like this or I can do something about it. “Hey, you know what buddy,” I start off, “I don’t need to hear this kind of bullshit from you. The computer was bought almost three months ago, you can’t return it call the manufacturer if you have a problem with that.” The idiot’s face gets all red; he’s the type that can’t stand to be shown up like this, especially in public. “Well, I’m going to find the manager myself and you won’t have a job within the hour!!!” he screams at me but I’ve already taken off my name tag and polo shirt. I toss both items in his face, “I’ll do you one better buddy, I quit someone else can help your sorry ass.” Back at home, my older brother wasn’t too happy with what had just gone down. “Jack, what the fuck is wrong with you?” “Ricky I really don’t need to hear your shit right now.” “Jack you were only there for what four-five months? I’m really worried about you-“ I could tell Ricky was disappointed, I really did respect my brother, he never had the athletic ability I did and he worked hard in school to get where he was now. But right now I wasn’t listening to a word he said. Ricky eventually got tired of yelling at me and stormed off into his room. At that moment I realized I had to leave the house. Not just the house, but go somewhere far away. I was thinking into a different state- maybe across the country. I couldn’t deal with any of this anymore; I needed a change of scenery. I grabbed my car keys and drove off, and I wasn’t planning on stopping anytime soon.
