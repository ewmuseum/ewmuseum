For the fans...(part two)

21 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/05/05 “Things have become really hectic lately” said Samuel in a low voice. Samuel had made an attempt to get to the Xtreme Hell showing, but it seems as though he had failed. Switching from AWC to IwE constantly is harder than one would think. “Dammit. Looks as though I’d have to kiss some ass tonight.” Samuel said jokingly. Despite being the IwE World Champion, Samuel knew that he was Main Eventing, so he may make it just before his match begins, but unfortunately for himself, he’d have no time to get ready! Samuel was teaming up with the owner of IwE, Zane Landers; to face the diabolical dastardly duo of The Critix. Despite being more than gladly to take them on his own, Samuel could never reject a chance like this. “Although,” Samuel continues to talk to what seems like himself “at least I’ll get a few days break, until I have to go back to AWC, to take on their World Champion, Alex Strider.” Samuel was rather worried by this, yet being World Champion in another fed does give him a slight boost. Despite acting cocky and arrogant in-ring, Samuel is rather the coward he seems to hate. “Damn. Alex Strider,” he continues “This is going to be so fucking hard. I have to make sure that I don’t botch anything. I need to make this comfortable for him, so maybe another time down the road we can meet again, for that title of his. Yeah, that would be great. Going up against him once more would be great, although I’d be as nervous as hell. The scene finally shows us where Samuel is. The setting is the inside of the crappy trains of the London Underground. Dirt everywhere, piss on the walls, and a big pile of wet faeces floods the corner. The smell is disturbing, and overall, Sam chose the best compartment. He has his laptop seated on his lap, and is using a wireless internet connection. He is browsing on the internet, going through the AWC match results, with a huge smile on his face. “Hmm…I got a three star rating for my match. I guess there are some things I desperately need to improve on if I am to look somewhat similar to a challenge in this place. It’s going to be tough, but I feel as if I am making a decent name for myself. HOLY CRAP!” Samuel says, and a load of people look in his direction. Everybody then continues to mutter to themselves, “I got placed the second best performer of the week. Who the hell is Pact?” Samuel shrugs, shakes his head and continues, as the scene closes. Samuel is seen training in an unfamiliar territory to all of the avid AWC fans, but to all of the IwE viewers, it is seen as somewhat a comfortable place, a place where they know only too well, Sam’s personal gym. I call it a personal gym, all it has is a set of dumb bells, a treadmill, and a benchpress. It’s not very well, but as you can see, Samuel is the IWE Champion, so it does get the job done. “Hmm…I may need to do a bit of work, so I can get ready for my matches. The Critix will prove to be a tough opponent, as will Alex Strider. Plus, getting only three stars (although ranked higher than him) it’s going to prove to be difficult, VERY difficult.” Samuel walks over towards the dumb bells, and begins to train. “I don’t really know what to do anymore, I’m still unsure whether to stick with IwE, where I have the Gold, the only Gold that matters,” Samuel picks up his IwE title and kisses it, “Or AWC, the place where I may not achieve much Gold at all, but it will definitely improve my career, it will make me a bigger name, so maybe I can go with the big leagues, like the WWE and such. Damn, I’m seriously stuck for ideas. I have another contract offer here. I may be able to find the time to do it, maybe under a part-time contract.” Samuel thumps his head on the table “What the hell to do? I said I’ll go with both, FOR THE FANS, but I’m seriously unsure what I can do. I might be able to make it, but I’d get on average 2.5 hours of sleep per day, which isn’t good. Maybe I can sleep before the shows and stuff. Oh I don’t know, maybe I can pull a Jeff Hardy and not show up to some events. No, that won’t work, he got fired for doing that crap.” Samuel smirks and shakes his head. He continues to do reps with his dumb bells. “God, I wish wrestling was as easy as acting cocky in-ring. I’m seriously unsure what to do with myself now”. Samuel lays down on the benchpress and closes his eyes as this segment of his life is finished. View Samuel Livingston 's Biography
