Cardinal Sin

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/10/05 *BANG!!* My hand pounded into the canvas, frustrated and annoyed. This was the fourth time in a row O’Grady had managed to lock in a modified STF and it was damn sure hurting now. With nowhere to go I tapped out furiously, pissed off with myself. ‘Come on Jack you’ve got to react quicker! You’re not getting old on me now are ya?’ That was the last straw from Dan. I got up and went for a wild left, easily dodged by him, before catching my arm for an armbar. ‘No, no that’s not it at all. You’re not trying hard enough Jack.’ I cried in anger and wrenched my arm, pulling him over my own body in an attempt to break free, however Dan has his arms wrapped so tightly it was impossible to see him letting go. I lashed out at him but he dodged. Eventually I just gave up. He let go and I skulked out of the ring. O’Grady watched me leave but didn’t follow, not just yet anyway. I barged through the main door and into the cool breeze of Kingston. It was chilly to say the least but my body heat was keeping me warm, especially after that ass kicking. I did not like being beaten. The night was tranquil and still, the only movement was the breeze, carefully lilting pieces of wastes across the streets like feathers floating on a river. A single lamp flickered randomly above me, alternating between placing me in the light and the dark. I would have said it annoyed me but in truth it didn’t. I didn’t even care. What I cared about was getting ready for the Battle Royal on Fresh this week. At least Pearl finally had some cop on. At least he finally knew I was for real. At least he finally knew what I signalled and that was money. It didn’t matter what he thought before, now he had to deal with me, because I was simply that damn good. As good as it gets. But getting ready for all seven opponents is a completely different matter. How can you prepare for seven people? How can you prepare for one? Because I couldn’t tell you, there’s a lot of wonderings and theories, musings and perhaps but nothing sticks. Everything is malleable in preparation, it all depends on who it is. Not the opponents but yourself. And so it was with me - focusing too much on what the other people were and not on myself… Because I hadn’t a fucking clue how to approach them from my own point of view. Sure Dan was trying me out through everything he could think of from the encyclopaedic knowledge of wrestling that sat in the back of his mind, but he can’t truly prepare me that way. He just doesn’t know them…so what do I know? I was pacing underneath the lamp again and in the distance I heard the church bell peeling for its congregation to gather and worship. Off in the distance, glimpses of cars could be seen moving towards their destination, all able to focus themselves. They had their purpose, their preparation laid right out in front of them each and every single week, given to them on a silver platter and all they had to do was a simple gesture of thanks to their lord. Why couldn’t everything be that fucking easy? The door clicked open behind me and I paused to see Dan standing there, sweaty towel wrapped over his shoulders and looking pretty worn out. His matted red hair was thinning again and it wouldn’t be long before his head took on a nice glean and shimmer. He padded his way over to me slowly and put his arm on my shoulder. ‘Come on back inside Jack, you’ll just get a cold out here.’ ‘No.’ ‘We can go over everything from the beginning again, just give you those details…’ ‘No.’ There was a long pause and O’Grady’s furrowed brow displaced him from the scene at large. After what seemed forever he responded. ‘Well what do you want?’ ‘I…tell me about them here.’ It was pathetic that answer, no substance, just desperation to it. I didn’t care though because I was desperate. I needed something to grasp, something that would help me. I would have felt infinitely more ashamed by admitting I hadn’t a clue what I was doing, that would’ve been plain disrespectful to Dan as a teacher. ‘Alright…well first off we’ve got the Japanese girl…’ ‘Leave her, she won’t be posing a threat.’ ‘Are you sure?’ ‘Dan, women have no place in a wrestling ring, I am not going to even consider her doing anything of note. You could give me any man other than her and he’d have more of a chance than her. Simple as.’ Dan was a little taken aback but he was pleased because he was just like me, he couldn’t even contemplate the serious thought of a female wrestler participating against her own, much less again men. Not only that, she was bloody seventeen, not even half my age. Furthermore everything she’s done that I’ve seen so far has been random, unadulterated and pathetic. To say she has a style is an insult to any wrestler that has come before her. She is a disgrace and will be eradicated long before I have to deal with her. ‘Fair enough - well there’s Red Rock, a bit of a comedic character, but able to back it up with his brawling skills.’ ‘Tempered by the threat of activity however. Have you seen him bother himself to move unless he actually has to? The kid has no motivation and is insignificant. It’s not like he’s going to give a damn about this.’ ‘That’s true but personalities can be surprising.’ ‘And they can be as open as a book. All you have to do is read it.’ Dan simply nodded and acquiesced to my comment, no bones about it. He pushed a hand through his hair and blew out some air before continuing. ‘Both ‘Mare and Alcaeus are similar, very powerful and controlling in a match if you let them.’ ‘And that it is what they desire, power is what drives them…however power can be reversed in the blink of an eye. They have no conception of grappling and shall fall by this struggle for control. They cannot deal when they do not have it.’ ‘Well…’ ‘Enough, let’s just go inside and you can go through them with me as we’re sparring.’ Without another word I pushed past him and entered the gym again, ready to get back to work. What I found however was a person from the past, shoving his nose where he shouldn’t. ********* ‘Hello Jack, I’m so pleased to finally meet you again. I’d heard of the rumours but I never actually believed you’d return. But here you are.’ Antonio Vico stood right in front me, arms stretched out wide and looking as if he was welcoming me, but you could see the glaze in his eyes, the distant hatred that plagued the hearts of all the men that belonged to Farrelli’s. I couldn’t blame them really, I’d be pissed off too if basically my father disowned his children and chose to take a foreigner as a successor. Especially Vico, he was the son after all. Vico oozed the unsubtle sleaziness of a Mexican playboy, transported into a spotty gangly Italian. His limbs moved in such an awkward exaggerated fashion that he was as graceful and light-footed as a bear. As such, Antonio was not exactly a successor type material and it was unsurprising his father didn’t see him as being up to the task. So naturally he did what he could to try and destroy him, making sure that he would never get anywhere close to becoming the successor as he was shunted to upstate, away from the prying eyes of the mafia underworld. Naturally. So he’d been posted to around the Kingston area and I was a little shocked that he hadn’t come sooner. Well he decided to arrive, however late it was. He was still waiting for me to hug him and his calm demeanour was quickly fading away. He tilted his head, looking quizzically at me. ‘No hug? No welcome Jack? I thought you’d be glad to have a reminder of your past, can’t forget that…’ ‘You trying to talk pretty with me? Don’t bullshit, what do you want?’ It was now his smile faded and things became more serious, subdued and dim. Vico sighed and took a couple of steps back and forth around the general hall of the gym, touching some of the equipment now and then. ‘You see Jack, Dan owes us some money….not an awful lot, but enough for my dad to become a little displeased with him. Dan has been a resource of our community for years and years and my father has no intention of wanting that relationship to change. However, for some reason Dan feels the need to have it go this way. So I’m here to ask why? Here to ask why and then to collect what’s owed. Then, everything will be fine and dandy again, you get me?’ ‘He owes you nothing, if anything Farrelli owes him gratitude for the service provided by him. After all, without Dan, your father would never have realised all the protégés that were out there waiting for him, totally absent from his numb-minded, awkward, excuse for a son. Don’t talk like you’re a big shot Vico, you’ve been out here for nearly two years, do you think there’s any prospects for you? You’ve been tossed into mafia wasteland with nothing to look forward to but your own impending doom when your father finally initiates his plan to get rid of you forever and mould somebody else in the fashion that was meant for you. Nice life Vico.’ Vico’s eyes had collapsed into small dour gems and were thoroughly trying to bore a hole through me now. I flickered a small smile in his direction and it looked like he was going to react badly when he came back with something that was not what I wanted at all… ‘So Jack…do you dream of your wife being shot?’ *BANG* He chuckled to himself but he had no time to react…none at all as I kicked him right in the face. Blood spurted from his mouth, unaware of what had happened and he flew back, trying to reassert his thoughts but I gave him no such liberties. Although it wasn’t me, it was my first sin… …the sin of wrath. Everything ran into a blur as my motions became disjointed, melded, rapid, slow, all of this at once. My mind switched off and let the pain flow through me, enjoying it all. And oh yes I enjoyed it, I enjoyed feeling his blood splatter across the floor, his bones snapping between my powerful arms - feeling the suffering and horror as he experienced pain like no other. I revelled in it, washing my body with his blood, and pain. And it all opened up before me then…. Each and every competitor was guilty of a sin that held them back in one way or the other but none…absolutely none knew how to harness them. Not like me, for I was wrath and now I recognised it. More than any other sin I embodied the spirit of wrath in its purest, vilest form. That’s why I revolted against such hardcore wrestling, because it became all too easy to feel the blood flow from someone’s body…but when it came because of the hands of your own doing, then it was special, deserved and right. I had been given a gift and I was going to use it. It was immaterial what the others could do, their sins would keep them from becoming what they truly were…but I had the power, the control to harness my own…and nothing could stop me. At some point I stopped hitting Vico and left him to lie on the ground, leaving him for another day as the sin commanded. My sin would direct the path and guide me along it. I had my goal and that was to become a true embodiment of evil…relish and appreciate each and every sin, using them to create a stronger, more powerful Jack Murphy… As I walked out into the cool air, Dan O’Grady looked at me and was in awe. The lamp no longer shone or flickered… It had gone out. I looked out towards the direction of the church and felt its loathing. It was so close, yet so far…but I would have my day there. It would become as one with me. ‘I know the way before me Dan…and it is one of pain and torment…for all…’ It’s time I let others share the pain inflicted upon me.
