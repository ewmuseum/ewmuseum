First Impression (Rp for Winter Warfare)

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/05 :::: Why is Buddy Kingfisher in the Atlantic Wrestling Club? It is a fair enough question that is going to be asked many times over in the next few weeks. Why would the man who was on top of the Core wrestling world, as Legacy Champion, part ways with the company and choose to come to a place that is still really trying to find it's footing on the slippery slopes of the global wrestling landscape. It's a fair enough and simple enough question that any credible person in the wrestling industry is going to ask. The answer, however, isn't as simple as the question might indicate. So you have to ask yourself why would a person in their first major wrestling promotion of their career, who is holding the most sought after prize in that company and one that is also known for being one of the most sought after in the entire wrestling world, and making a pretty decent living for a guy who had once been a part time worker for Wal-Mart choose to walk away from it all? Think about that question in your head for just a moment. Take in the serious nature of such a decision in the career of a professional wrestler and ask yourself what might make you leave. Would it be for more money? Would it be for the challenge of conquering another mountain? Would you say that it was simply cause you didn't feel there was any challenge left within the company you had become the most recent star, icon, legend, and champion in? Would it be a combination of all three of those things? Have you got your answer in your head yet? Can you picture that perfect dollar amount that would make you leave a company where you're the king and join a place where you're just another face in the crowd of a company who is fighting to make itself known as the premiere company in world? Can you picture yourself facing that new crop of talented challengers that is surely going to give you that rush of competition again? Keep that image in your head. Savor it. Take it all in as if it was the be all and end all answer to the above question. Got it? Good. Now what would you say if I told you that Buddy Kingfisher left all the money, fame, titles, fortune, and fast track of success cause he felt guilty? I know what you're saying right now. I can see that look in your eyes. You think I'm an idiot. Clearly I'm too stupid to have any clue about the wrestling business and wouldn't know a thing about what drives the average wrestling superstar. You'd be right about that. I would be ignorant to the thought process, motives, and drive of your average wrestling superstar. That is why Buddy Kingfisher isn't your average wrestling superstar. He has that little something different that sets him apart from all the others. The thing that makes him special and unique. The ability to be humble. Again I see those eyes rolling. I hear all those loud sighs and the sounds of flesh slapping against an exposed forehead. I hear you all out there groaning and talking amongst yourselves now about how utterly retarded this old man must be. Once again you no doubt have earned the right to be sceptical of the sincerity of a wrestler who is 'supposedly' humble. Let us take a look at who Buddy Kingfisher really is and then you'll understand. You'll know that I speak nothing but the truth on this matter. You'll know that this old man isn't just some crazy, nut job, wrestling fan who thinks he has his fingers on the pulse of what makes a guy tick. You'll see and you'll leave here shaking your heads once more. This time though you'll either be shaking them saying, 'Damn the old guy is onto something', or you'll just smirk, laugh a little bit, and shake your head while saying, 'Sure pops. We'll believe that one when we see it.' So shall I begin? Yeah? Good. Sit down. Pay attention. Let me take you back, in your mind of course, to the middle of May this year. We're talking a mere seven months ago from the point and time we're at right now. Spring time with summer just around the bend and Buddy has just walked through the doors of Core Wrestling for the first time. A fresh face who had just signed his six month contract to work for one of the biggest companies in the world and it was only his first time under the bright lights of federation not on the Indy circuit. Ok. So here is the big red head, with the goofy smile, the fun loving personality, and not a care in the world. He walked into the arena that first night with his best friend Joesph, a gym bag, and nothing else but a whole bunch of dreams and goals he wanted to accomplish over the next few years. The business hadn't yet robbed him of his smile, his love, and his geniune intrest in pleasing and entertaining the fans. The worrying about competition, who was pissed off at him, who wanted to see him permanetly injured, and the ego of just taking shots when he wanted them hadn't yet taken him over and tarnished him. That stuff would come later. It wouldn't be much later but it was still later. So you with me so far? We all up to speed to this point? Good. I'll continue then. Fast forward from that night. A win in a dark match on his first show. It was nothing special. It was just there. Then a month later everything has already changed. From a dark match to open a Pride show Buddy found himself in the finals of the first ever Star Search Tournament. The winner to be crowned with loads of prizes that most Core Wrestlers had to work years to get their hands on. There they were for Buddy. On a silver platter if he won a cage match with Arafat. One month into his big time professional wrestling career and he had a shot to get future Legacy and Pride title shots as well as a Ringmaster first round bye. Sounds spiffy don't it? Who wouldn't want something like that just handed to them? Wait for it and you'll know. Anyway. As I was saying before you decided to interupt me with the sillness of how grand a thing that really was. He walked in that night and lost. Only his second loss in Core. Tapped out inside the cage to a far more experienced and superior wrestler in that. No shame. Problem solved. No shots, no fast track, no big time fame right from the start. WRONG. Just by showing up a Legacy Title shot was his to claim. Imagine that. Show up at a show, lose, and still get a Legacy Title shot. Isn't America grand? So now you've got a rookie, a month into his career in the big leagues, with a golden ticket to the top of the federation and people don't seem to see something wrong here? Alright lets put it into a more clear picture here. Many people in Core, the likes of Veronica Paige, Nick Worrell, and Smiley had never even recieved shots at the biggest prize to this point. Nope not a single one. I see your heads shaking in disbelief like I'm a fool. It is the facts though. People who'd been in Core for over two years hadn't seen a sniff of the top prize yet a rookie now has it as his for the taking a mere month in. Look at who had gained the belt by that time. Munson, the champion then, had worked very hard for a very long time to get his first shot at the title. He had to work his way up. Had to hold the Pride title for a then record amount of time to prove he was worth the shot. Are you people telling me you honestly don't see where Buddy might feel a little uneasy about something like this? If you really believe that then you've never had a pissed off Montanian Madman beating down on you for pissing in the face off all his hard work. Now take the time to realize that during this time Buddy did next to nothing to prove himself worthy of that shot. After winning it he went on to feud with Hiroshi Ryusaki. A brutal affair that had more to do with pride than anything else but still a bloody war that hadn't even really begun when Hiroshi took Buddy out of action for about six to eight weeks with broken ribs. Just a month prior to the injury Buddy had finally done one thing that he had actually set out to do. Prove himself worthy of some future Pride title shots by securing a Classic Championship reign after winning a battle royal for the belt. So lets check the score cards here. Less than 4 months with the company, 1 Classic championship reign (lasting less than a month and lost in the first defense), and broken ribs add up to...Legacy Title worthy? Yeah. Tell that to Paige who'd won every single title that a person could win at that point, outside of the Legacy and Tag team titles (four of five titles in all with something like eight or more reigns), who'd yet to sniff the Legacy belt. Well of course except when it was around her husbands waist but that is besides the point here. She is the most dominate superstar in Core history and she doesn't equal out to be worthy enough of a title shot? Yet Buddy can come in and do next to nothing and he is? Grand American scheming here. What does all this do for Buddy exactly? Sure it made him famous. Sure it made him money. Sure it gave him a title shot that he'd always hoped he'd get sometime about two years down the road. But what it really gave him was the worst present of all. It gave him an ego. Most people didn't even notice it cause of how good a guy Buddy is. He still wanted to entertain the fans each week but that became secondary to showing off the muscle of his extremely fast rise in a company where so many others had slaved away far longer and never gotten near the point that Buddy was at. The ego was never more evident than when he was ready to come back from the rib injury. Did he come back and finish his business with Hiroshi? Did he come back and attempt to reclaim the Classic Title that he disgraced with his awful showing prior to the injury? Of course not silly tool bags! Buddy simply went over everyones head and cashed in his shot at the Legacy Title. Munson had just won it back and surely the world wanted to see Buddy beat Munson again. Surely Buddy had already become big enough star that the lower titles and setting his issues with Hiroshi were minor league compared to the superstar he'd become. Yep. They were just that. Nothing. Nadda. Ziltch. Small bleeps on the radar screen of Buddy becoming the huge star of Core Wrestling. So he took his title shot. A title shot he really hadn't earned, mind you, and ran with it. One week after Munson went through yet another grueling match in his long standing series of classics with William Gwynn he was forced to face Buddy Kingfisher. There Buddy was again. Fresh. Ready to go. Jumping on the oppurtunity of facing a worn out, injured, and classy champion. Spitting in Munsons face once more like everything he'd done prior to that, all the work he put in, all the blood he spilt, all the sweat dripped had meant nothing. Nothing as long as Buddy could feed his new found best friend....Mr. Ego. Great huh? Couldn't be better? Except that when you're Buddy Kingfisher you can only act like a tool bag for so long. Eventually, as in about two weeks after winning the belt, it all caught up with Buddy. He'd pissed on the legacy of Core, it's main title, and all those who worked far harder than him to get it. He hadn't earned nothing. He simply had it handed to him. In doing so, and then demanding that he get the shot at it, he basically cheated himself. Cheated himself out of what you ask? Are you that deft? Are you really ignorant of the wrestling business enough to ask such a retarded question? Don't you know what the single greatest thing in all of wrestling is? You don't? Then shut up and listen. It's the chase. That's right I said the chase. Don't be stupid. You don't think the fact that being champion is the most fun? You're the champion then dumbass! There is nothing left to prove. You're at the top and there is only one way to go from there. You guessed it swifty. Down. Down hard and fast like a paid hooker at a coke party. Where is the fun in that? No stupid not the hooker. I'm talking about the falling from the top. Does that really sound like fun to you? If it does smack yourself in the face. Twice. Really hard. Why? Cause you're an idiot and it is the only way you'll learn. The chase is where it's all at my friends. It is where the careers are cemented. It is where the respect, love, honor, and pride of being the champion is really formed. You can't respect what you haven't earned. You can't know the honor and pride that is involved in winning something so important when you've had it handed to you. Wake up and smell the fresh brewed coffee my good kiddies cause you've got a lot of lessons to learn and that is what Buddy Kingfisher is here to do. Why am I here? Are you smoking something? Have you been hanging out with Nova and taking in the wonderful world of weed smoking? Pay attention son. I'm the guy who sets this stuff up. I'm the guy who gets the message across. Buddy teaches through actions and the way he carries himself. I'm here to point it all out to those of you who have a hard time following along at home. Now take out your notebooks and write down this weeks homework. You heard me right I said homework. I didn't slur my speach or speak in tongue. You ready? Good. Write this down. Learn the art of being humble. Got it monkeys? Good. Take it home. Read it. Study it. Learn it. Live it. Now get your butts out of here and put that homework to good use. Oh and by the way. Next time bring some apples. Teaching you people isn't exactly the easiest job to do on an empty stomach. Now run along and play. Just remember...humble. It's the word of the week. Learn to love it. :::: Amsterdam: Night of AWC Christmas Party: A rocky start to an otherwise good evening Buddy: Excuse me but do you happen to know where the hotel that Atlantic Wrestling Club is holding their Christmas Party is located? Buddy asks a woman with particularly large breasts and short brown hair. She looks at Buddy while lighting her smoke. After taking a drag from it, a long, slow drag she removes the cancer stick from her mouth and just sort of smirks. Lady: So you're looking for a party are you baby? Buddy just grins at her as he shakes his head up in down in the manner you'd expect of a six year old child on Christmas morning if they were asked whether they were ready to open their presents. Lady: Well sugar I know of several places that a party is taking place at. The question is what type of party you're looking for. Buddy gives her a bit of a confused look as he stands there on the sidewalk and stares at the first stranger he had the guts to ask directions from. Being in a very foriegn place, specially one like this that is so not Buddy Kingfisher at all, is always tough but even more so for Buddy cause he can be very shy. Specially when it comes to speaking to the ladies. Buddy: I'm looking for a Christmas party. It is being hosted by AWC. Lady: I've never heard of this AWC. I'm sure she is a lovely lady, silly nickname and all, but I can take you anywhere you want and make it a party honey. All you have to do is show me the cash and we'll party all night long. Buddy: I have to pay you to get to the party? Lady: Of course. You don't think the parties are given away around here do you? Buddy: Well back home parties of this nature are always free. I've never paid a dime to go to a party like this one. Lady: Then the ladies 'back home' aren't exactly the brightest crayons in the box. Buddy just sighs as he rubs his hands together and tries to think for a moment. Would he really want to drop some cash in order to find the AWC party when he could just look around some more himself? After a moment he realized he didn't have all night and had to pay up. Buddy: So how much will it cost me to have you show me where the party is at? Lady: Usually it would cost a young man about a hundred and fifty bucks. The woman pauses for a moment and looks Buddy up and down for a moment before simply smirking and continuing. Lady: Seeing as how you've never been to these parts before and by your general appearance I'll cut you some slack. A cool hundred even and we can get the party started. Buddy: A HUNDRED BUCKS! HOLY SPONGEBOB! Are you serious? Lady: That is a discount for someone of my stature around here sweety. Anyone else and you're going to pay at least double or triple that amount. Buddy: HOLY SWEET CHRISTMAS! Normally three hundred dollars to be taken to a party? I've never heard of such thing. I sure am glad I don't live over here. I'd be broke inside of three days. Lady: Quite the party guy are you? The Lady asks as Buddy takes out his wallet and pulls out some bills equaling the amount she was asking for. Buddy: I get around back home. I've been told that I'm quite the life of the entertainment. The Lady raises an eyebrow as she looks Buddy up and down once more. Her thoughts begin to wonder if this is going to be a better night than she had origionally thought. Lady: Well then we should have a wonderful time here tonight on our hundred dollars.Let's see...follow me. She takes Buddy by the hand with her left hand as she stuffs the money down the front of her shirt and into her bra. Buddy simply obeys and begins to follow her. A moment later she begins walking down a dark alley and Buddy begins to get a worried feeling in his stomach. Which is a pretty large worry considering the size of Buddy's stomach. After a moment as they walk down the alley Buddy questions the womans directional ability. Buddy: Do they really hold the parties in dark alleys like this over here? I can't imagine that the hotel we're looking for is going to be found in such a place. Lady *smirking in front of him*: Oh honey we don't need a hotel for our type of party. These alleys are used all the time for this type of thing. I know that over in America you'd get busted for that type of thing but over here you'd only get applauded for your performance. Buddy gives the woman a look of complete shock, which she obviously doesn't see with her back turned to him, as she continues to lead him down the alley. A few moments later they begin to see the light from the other end of the alley and they see the shadows of three people standing in the darkness of the alley. It isn't possible to make out who the people are but it is fairly clear that the people appear to be getting their party on with each other. As they get a bit closer a few more can be seen standing out of the alley, in the light from the street lamps, and Buddy immediately stops. Buddy: That is Peter Vetra of PRIME! Lady: So? Is that important? Buddy: It is to a wrestler. We must be getting close to the party now. You're a better guide then I thought. Lady: I told you I knew where the parties are at. So you're another one of those wrestlers who are in town huh? Those two seem to have already found their party. Buddy looks past her to find that the man in the shadows of the alley is playing a game of hide the weasel with two particularly busty ladies. He then hears the voice of Peter Vetra as he all but begs the women out on the sidewalk to please him in the same manner. To no avail as they want nothing to do with him and simply decide to walk into the alley to join the other man. Buddy jerks his hand from the woman and looks at her in shock. Buddy: What is this? I asked you to show me to the party! Lady: This is the party honey. You even said you knew that other wrestler fellow there. He is here to party too. Now just relax and let me help you out of those pants. The lady starts toward Buddy who quickly starts backing up only to fall over backwards over a pile of garbage. Fear is written all over his face as he tries to scramble back to his feet. Buddy: NO! NO! What are you doing? I don't need to take my pants off to get into a party. What kind of sick woman are you? The lady stops and puts her hands on her hips while giving him a very annoyed look. Lady: Look you came up to me asking for a party. I simply am doing what I'm paid for. If you didn't want to party then why in the world would you pay me a hundred bucks? Buddy: TO SHOW ME WHERE THE CHRISTMAS PARTY IS! FOR GOODNESS SAKES DIDN'T YOU PAY ATTENTION? Lady: I sort of tuned you out after you said party. Buddy *calming some what*: Look I don't know what type of party you thought I was looking for but this isn't it. I just need to get to the AWC Christmas Party. I'm suppose to be there and now I'm late. Lady: Well since you've already paid me the hundred bucks you want to just go ahead and let me work for that cash? She asks Buddy as she begins to unbutton her shirt and starts tugging at the skirt she is wearing, pulling it toward the ground. The site of a woman he doesn't know undressing in front of him and the carnal sounds of the strangers only fifty or more yards from them is too much for Buddy. Buddy: EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKK!!!!! The shriek catches the woman off guard and she stumbles back causing her legs to stick straight up in the air as Buddy runs off in the opposite direction like a mad man screaming like a chick in a horror flick the whole way. Scene ends This is where the story ends this week for goofy Buddy Kingfisher. What a horrible way to start his new career in AWC. Picked up by a hooker and didn't even know it. Having to run all over the city until he finally found the hotel just in time. Then spending an extra two hundred dollars to get a guy to dress up like reindeer in order to look more authentic as Santa Claus when he arrived. That was all the bad stuff that happened. Of course seeing Patrick Mapleleaf make a fool of himself and leave covered in food made it all worth it. Nothing like sticking it to a guy who hates Christmas, Santa, and wrestling fans. All in all a good first week for the red headed dynamo of wrestling entertainment. What will happen next for Buddy? Tune in at Winter Warfare to find out.
