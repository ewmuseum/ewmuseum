The Domino Effect: Gateway Drugs

5 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/10/06 'Yesterday I signed the biggest match of my career. Yesterday a horde of people chanted my stolen name. Yesterday I was worried about sponsors and taking viagra, Rogain and Brazilian body waxes. Yesterday wasn't the day that I died. I think that makes yesterday the best day of my life. If only I had known it at the time, I might have done something different. I might have ate a little more or fucked a little longer, I might have stood in the ring and made them cheer louder. They told me that I'd see my life flash before my eyes the moment before I die but all I see is yesterday.' Mankind has achieved so much that its glory becomes obscured behind an ever thickening veil of competition. For what is evolution but a series of minor tweaks and adjustments? Running faster, leaping farther, getting stronger, and learning always greater things. We are an ever evolving species, constantly growing and bettering ourselves from one generation to the next. We are the beings of limitless potential, we are the masters of fate, the directors of destiny. And still we all find the greatest of happiness in the most simple of things. 'YES!' Johnny's voice echoes through the corridor. 'I AM MAN!' Bounces down the dimly lit and boringly decorated hotel hallway. Midas T. Gold lifts his head to this, hazel eyes moving to the door with a 'Do Not Disturb' sign hanging from the knob. Lucious Vandermein, recently hired by Midas as Johnny's personal security force and dog walking service, sits in his foldable stool leaning against the wall. Headphones wrapped around his head and the contented snoring of deep sleep is joined by Titan's: The young golden retriever Johnny had bought for his wife the night he caught her cheating and sent her back to Russia. The rather expensive divorce proceedings are still in court. Smiling slightly he gathers his empty bucket of ice and continues on his way, a hotel robe hanging from his shoulders and tied loosely around his waist. Title defenses, title shots, main event matches and all Johnny's been thinking about is being able to get it up. Midas thinks to himself. I can understand when man hood is threatened, but I'm left trying to scramble together a semblence of a title run by myself. All the while he keeps my name out of the contracts allowing me to do so. Midas scoops his bucket half full aggressively, his mind turbulent. He may have brought this little party together, but it's me who keeps us this way. It's like he doesn't care if the rest of us make money, not as long as he can pitch a tent. 'Midas.' Drowning beneath the waves of his own frustration and jealousy he didn't see the man approach until he was speaking his name. Turning to regard the stranger Midas realizes he is no stranger at all. In fact Lupus Feral had held a contract with Anarch Agencies over a year ago, Midas had been forced to release that contract when Lupus was convicted of trafficking charges. Shortly before he himself quit when Jordan White became major share holder. 'Lupus, I had heard. Well I read that you were.' 'Dead?' The powerfully built African man dressed in a rather sharp black suit smiles charismatically. 'That was exactly the deal me and your boy made.' 'Excuse me?' Midas glances towards the Do Not Disturb sign, the slumbering security guard and watch dog. 'Me an Johnny, we struck a bargain few weeks back.' 'I don't understand, did he not pay you or something?' 'Actually he did, but he forgot about one thing.' Midas narrows his eyes, expecting Lupus to continue. When he doesn't it prompts the 'What?' he was waiting for and Lupus steps forward imposingly. 'I don't like him very much.' Not particularily the fighting type, Midas feels himself cringing. Natural reflex, he'll later tell his ego. 'So I came up with a new deal.' Lupus' voice slips like silk into Midas' ears. 'It's called, black mail.' His hand reaches for Midas and the manager flinches, with a chuckle the heavily built Lupus slips a disk into the pocket of Midas' robe. After a tense moment he hears the steady beat of retreating footsteps and opens his eyes, Lupus makes his way towards the elevators. 'What do you want?' He calls after the man but he doesn't turn around. Tentative fingers explore the pocket and tumble across the hard plastic case of a CD. A DVD to be more specific, holding it up to his eyes he reads the words 'Watch Me' written across a piece of tape. Midas leaves his bucket of ice in the ice machine and heads for his room. 'You say something?' Lucy clears his throat, standing and straightening his shirt. Eyes trying desperately to blink away the weight of sleep. 'No.' Midas says. 'Just getting some ice.' He disappears into his room. Had he been more aware Lucious may have asked why Midas didn't have his ice. He may have been curious about the worried look in the normally cool man's eyes, or the sweat on his brow. But instead Lucy reclines again into his stool; listening for the first moans of pleasure from Johnny's room before replacing his headphones over his ears. Midas paces before the foot of his bed, wringing his hands fervently as a nubian hooker pulls the covers aside. 'What's the matter baby?' She asks in sultry tones of desire. 'Nothing.' He lies, taking to gnaw on his thumb nail. 'You have to go.' 'You have another forty minutes.' She says, her voice becoming serious. 'Just get the fuck out!' 'Gimme my money, all of it ass-hole!' Midas throws a wad of bills at her. 'Get out!' 'Mother fucker.' She swears, gathering the money along with her clothes. 'Don't get mad at me cuz you can't last.' She snaps, stumbling to get a heel on her foot. Midas doesn't respond as she taunts him, slamming the door in her wake he pulls the disc from his pocket. He looks at it fearfully, he'd known something bad went down that day in Los Angeles. He had hoped in the back of his mind it would never come up again. He had prayed he'd never have to know the full extent of what Johnny orchestrated that day. His prayers, it seems, have gone unanswered. The screen comes to life a few seconds after it finishes loading. Midas lowers himself nervously on the edge of his bed, the remote clutched in both hands like a weapon. The image is in black and white, the angle suggests a rearview mirror camera and it's aimed directly into the back seat. He recognizes both of the faces, Lupus is driving and the other is a man named Havoc. One of the original Anarchs back in two thousand and three, he was terminated by three promotions for reckless violence, hurting people in the ring. Both men had called Johnny a traitor for dropping them from his label, Havoc though wasn't picked up again like Lupus. As he watches on Lupus ducks to his right and starts to yell, there's no sound but he's not caring about that. Lupus gets out of his seat as a face emerges in the back, Johnny's face with his mouth tapped over. A few minutes later Lupus gets back into his seat and Johnny starts to kick it. Havoc swivels his position to deal with him. There's an obviously heated exchange before Havoc removes the tape from Johnny's mouth. Something Lexicon says sparks the hispanic into aiming a gun into the back seat more than a few times as he yells and postures. Havoc laughing, looks at the driver. It's on Lupus' face as he looks over and makes eye contact. The grim expression devoid of whatever humor Havoc indulges. The camera angle catches Johnny freeing his hands even though Havoc doesn't; it happens fast from there. Johnny surges into the front seat, his right hand stiff arming Havoc's face out view and obviously into the window. Midas' expression drops into horror as he sees Johnny get the gun away from his captor. Aiming the pistol off camera and without hesitation send a shower of blood across the front seat. The scene leaps forward and cuts, turning into waves of static that illuminate the open mouthed frozen face of Midas T. Gold. His pose and expression are the same several minutes later, when there's a knock on the door. He intends to ignore it, snapping out of his reverie and sucking on his dried tongue, when it comes again. He moves to it, anxiousness rises in his belly with a wave of sudden panic and he presses an eye to the peep hole. He clicks the handle and opens the door not seconds before Johnny pushes himself in. 'I saw it, man. I saw it!' Johnny looks excited, his bedsheet wrapped around his naked waist. Midas blinks and with a shake of his head asks: 'Saw what?' 'Livewire Lexicon. Just picture it, Livewire. Lexicon. Hey.' He pauses and looks at the dim light, static on the tv screen, and clothes discarded haphazardly on the floor. 'What are you doing in here?' Midas looks around. 'I was uh.' 'What where you watching?' Johnny asks. 'Porn.' Midas blurts, his dark skin getting darker. 'I was lonely. Uh, didn't you have a girl in your room.' 'Right!' Johnny heads for the door. 'Remember.' He hesitates. 'Livewire Lexicon.' ***** 'I hate the desert.' Johnny comments from the back seat of his luxurious Rolls Royce Phantom. His face etched with distaste as he watches the sand dunes roll on for miles into cliffs and canyons. 'Nothing good in the desert.' 'I suppose you're right.' Midas glances into the mirror. 'It was your idea to drive to phoenix.' 'Spent our flight money getting you a hooker last night, I couldn't stand seeing you like that.' Johnny grins. Midas doesn't reflect the well intended jab, his expression cold. 'You alright, man?' Johnny scoots forward, leaning into the front seat. Midas flinches as Johnny reaches for the radio. 'You haven't looked me in the eyes all day.' 'It's nothing.' Midas cuts in quickly, forcing eye contact. 'Just stressing over this Livewire defense and the Triangles match.' 'Right.' Johnny smiles and settles back. 'Don't play around, man.' Midas is quick to steer the direction of the conversation. 'It's called shooting star syndrome.' 'Shooting star syndrome?' Johnny grins, looking out his window. 'Right. Whatever goes up must come down, you can only pull out wins so much. Seymour Almasy and GBJ are decorated world class competitors and you need to take this seriously. You've got a Livewire title around your waist making you a target and you didn't even show up last week.' 'I was busy.' Johnny remarks boredly, tracing the horizon with his eyes. Satisfied that his client is now trying his best to avoid a conversation, Midas turns half of his attention back to the incoming text message. PILLS IN GLOVEBOX Midas closes the phone and leans over as casually as he can. Flipping the handle he lets the little door hang open and inside, settled silently upon an owners manual, insurance, and registration is a little brown bottle with a blank pharmaceutical sticker. He closes the glove box as Johnny leans forward again. 'Midas, let me ask you something.' 'Of course.' Midas takes a deep breath. Cradling his open phone in his lap and splitting his eyes between the screen and the road. 'You honestly think I'm going to crash and burn?' 'Huh?' Midas responds distracted. Pressing the send button he replies: What for?. 'What did you say?' 'I asked if you thought I'd crash and burn.' Midas glances into the mirror, into Johnny's steady, waiting, eyes. He swallows and produces plastic smile number five: sincerity. 'Not if you'd listen to me once in awhile, give me a little more deciding power. I came to you because I saw that limitless potential.' 'You came to me because I'm the only wrestler you know.' 'Well, not anymore. I know plenty of wrestlers now that'd jump at the chance to be represented.' Johnny settles back into his seat. 'Like who?' 'Like Lupus Feral.' Midas says distractedly, eyes on the screen in his lap. Johnny's eyes though snap to the back of his managers head. 'What did you say?' 'Huh?' 'You said a name.' 'Uh, Brutus Tyrell.' Nervous glance into the mirror. 'Sent you his resume like two weeks ago. Don't you read your mail?' Johnny shakes his head with a chuckle, returning his gaze out the passenger window. 'If I did that I'd be left with a ridiculously expensive driver.' Midas looks at his client's reflection with a wrinkled nose but keeps his mouth shut. Looking to the road he attempts to forget, at least for the next few hours, the final words on his phone: MAKE HIM WIN ***** People filled the hallways, staff and crew mostly rushing about like rats in a maze. Scurrying to this job or that in final preperation of the impending masses, the sacrificial procession of souls for entertainment. Already dressed in his flashy golden suit Midas shoulders his way against the tide like a horny trout. Finally reaching the sanctity and relative calm of the dressing rooms He steps inside and shuts the chaos out. Putting his back flat against the portal and allowing a deep breath to settle rattled nerves. 'I thought we had to share the room?' Midas asks of Johnny as he emerges in a wet towel and dripping hair. He shrugs. 'Not here yet I guess.' 'So. You feelin it?' Midas rubs his hands together and steps away from the door. 'You could say that.' Johnny ties his hair back before pulling his open duffel bag onto the bench. 'Big night, first title defense.' Midas paces slowly. 'Sure.' Johnny applies deodorant absently. 'I mean winning it is one thing, defending it proves you're worthy. I mean, you wanna win tonight, right?' Johnny grins and looks over his shoulder but the smile fades as he sees the seriousness on Midas' face. He looks at it hard and his manager doesn't falter. 'Yeah.' He finally nods his head and looks back to his bag. 'I want to win tonight.' Tone suggests of admitting a crime. Midas nods along with him, putting his hand to his chin and his other in his pocket as Johnny collects a few hair products and makes for the bathroom. Midas pauses, looking hesitantly over his shoulder as the big man disappears. His hand comes out of his pocket and produces the bottle of pills. He looks down at it long and hard, debating within himself. Lowering into a crouch he spills a pair of the little white soldiers onto the bench, using the bottle he crushes them into a powder. Grabbing Johnny's half empty water he's careful to watch as much of the powder go in as he can before blowing away his evidence and straightening his back. 'I was thinking.' Johnny walks back in. Midas spins the lid on fast before turning around to watch his client, now in his tights with the towel over his shoulder, make his way for the open bag. 'I was thinking you're right. Maybe I should give you more deciding power.' He moves a few things around before spotting the water in Midas' hand. 'There it is.' He reaches for it but Midas holds his grip. Johnny looks about to protest when the water is freed, he gives Midas a funny look but the manager pretends a nose itch. 'Really? That's great.' He produces without enthusiasm. 'You sure you're alright?' 'Fine, really, I'm fine.' 'Alright.' Johnny continues to watch Midas for a few long moments. 'Anyways, all those sponsorship deals you've been throwing at me for weeks, go ahead and set up a couple meetings. Something small, I trust your choice.' Johnny pats his shoulder. Midas nods and forces a smile. 'You got it, boss.' Johnny pats his manager's shoulder one final time before turning away; tilting his water bottle back for a few big gulps as he heads for the bathrooms. Yesterday I was blind. If I had seen the signs coming yesterday it might not have been too late to stop it. I suppose it's my own fault, to forget about my part. I truly did nothing to stop myself from pushing that first domino. The yesterdays and each one before it are of my own design, but alas I say 'Today it's not' but perhaps I'll die tomorrow.
