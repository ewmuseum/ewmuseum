A Debut To Remember?

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th August 2005 It was around 10am in the morning, The British Bomber started to stir, a few days ago Bomber had a meeting with AWC officials about getting a contract from them to wrestle, he had signed it and last night had gone on a massive bender and got so drunk that he had to be carried home. He got himself out of bed, rather unwillingly, but he got up, and made his way over to the fridge to grab a bottle of milk. Bomber was still in the clothes he went out in last night, a slight smell coming from him, mainly due to the sticky atmosphere when he had been out, then sleeping in his clothes and it getting hot during the night. He took the milk from the fridge and took the top off, then taking a drink. He made his way over to the door to check his mail, in all he had three letters, a bill from the gas company, a letter from AWC and a videotape. Bomber: Hmmm should be interesting. Bomber opened up the bill from the gas company first, only to read he owed £30, he put it to one side along with a couple of other bills. He opened up the letter from AWC, and he read it, just smiled the put it down, it just said he was in which he knew anyway, and his salary which he also knew from the meeting and when he signed the contract, he also found out his opponent, the arena details and his debut show. Bomber Oh come on you got to be joking. “The British Bomber vs Red Rock, at L’Astroballe, Villeurbanne, France.” Bomber: Who the hell is Red Rock? Bomber looked confused, and then puts down the letter, and opens the videotape and puts it in the VCR, turns on the TV and sits back in the chair. He hits play on the remote control and it turns out to be video from a private investigator. The first clips opened up with the words “Red Rock – Scene One”. He sat back interested in what was going to play, Bomber never really scouted opponents before so didn’t know what to expect. It opened up with Red Rock in his bed, and after a minute of so it became evident he was dreaming of Emma Bunton, formerly of The Spice girls. Bomber: The Spice Girls? What sort of guy is this, is he a woman trapped in a guys body? Dude, they was an 8 year olds favourite group back in the mid 90’s, I bet he loved the music, danced and pranced around and whatnot. OH I GOT IT! He must be gay~! The video went on a little more, and it then it showed an image Bomber really didn’t want to see, a guy sitting in his pants, and Red Rock talking to him. Not only that but the guy, named Boolie had pointed out Red Rock had a boner. Bomber: NO! That’s wrong damn it! This guy had a boner, dreaming of The Spice Girls, and his mate, or boyfriend, whatever, points out that he has a boner! What sort of place is that? A gay parlour?! Red Rock then proceeds to tell Boolie he needs shower in the next clip, that “his crotch was kicking off a right pong”. Who did this guy think he was? Avid Merrion? Bomber: This guy is a complete idiot, and I have to wrestle him tonight? Damn it man, this guy is gonna end up touching me up. He touches me up I’ll punch him square in the face. Bomber was becoming more frightened, well not frightened, but more ‘afraid’ of Red Rock, Bomber wasn’t so much bothered if Red Rock was gay or not, more the fact he might make a move on him. He decided to fast forward the video a bit, and it came to the next scene where Red Rock and Boolie had gone round to D’avid’s to try and borrow some money for rent. D’avid had invited in Red Rock and Boolie. Bomber: Man look at that place, that entire place looks like a gay knocking shop. I don’t even want to watch this now, this is getting disturbing. Bomber ejected the video, and put it to the side. He got up and made himself a cup of tea, the time had flown by, it was near enough half 10 and at 2pm Bomber had to get the flight to France. Just as he sat down with his drink the phone rang, he got back up a bit annoyed at the fact that the phone could have rung a few seconds earlier while he was stood up. He picked up the phone from the receiver. Bomber: Hello? It was silent, nobody spoke, all of a sudden. Caller: HELLO! Bomber dropped the phone, the sudden shout of hello had given him a fright, he picked the phone back up and was getting annoyed, his day wasn’t going to plan. Bomber: What the hell you playing at! You scared the crap out of me! Caller: Sorry, I just wanted to surprise you. Bomber: Surprise me, you nearly gave me a damn heart attack, I really do wish you wouldn’t do that to me. Caller: Sorry daddy… It was Bomber’s daughter on the phone, at 15 he had un-protected sex with his childhood sweetheart, the two remain close, along with his daughter. There is a lesson to be learnt there folks, always use a condom when you have sex, otherwise your girl could end up pregnant. Bomber: Listen TC, I am glad you phoned, I need you to come by and watch the place for me tonight, I got a match tonight over in France so it’s likely I will be staying overnight there. TC slammed down the phone, she wasn’t happy with what she had just heard. Bomber was in trouble, not only with TC, but probably her mother as well. About half an hour passed, the time was quarter past 11 and Bomber was expecting TC anytime now, if she was coming that was. Bomber had packed his gear ready for the show tonight, he was starting to get a little nervous, which he always did before his matches, it was just natural for him to do so. There was a bang on the door, Bomber got up and went to answer it, just as he was about to the door flew open, nearly hitting Bomber flat in the face. Things just weren’t going his way today. Though the doorway walked his daughter, TC along with a small rucksack. Bomber: Why didn’t you just use your key and get in, instead of nearly knocking me on my ass? TC: You deserve it, you just can’t seem to quit can you? First 5SW, then FAW, now what? What place is it now then? Bomber had grown used to his daughter’s rants at him, she didn’t like the idea of him wrestling since his knee surgery last year after FAW closed up, Bomber sustained the knee injury a few years back and never really recovered from it, struggling to find the form that had won him his first career World Title. Bomber had only properly recovered from the surgery two months ago, and was brought back to the ring by the fans. Bomber: Hey this is a good place, this is the place I feel I can make it big again. You should have been there, the guys at AWC made me feel at home, they knew who I was, they have seen me before at FAW, this is the one, and I know I can be the next big thing there. TC: Yeah yeah yeah, that’s all good, but who do you have in your first match? Bomber: Some guy called Red Rock, suppose to be pretty good. TC started laughing, Bomber was a bit confused. Bomber: What’s so funny? TC: You not heard, there are a load of rumours that he is gay, he’ll be touching your ass, grabbing your balls and everything in that match! Damn I wish I could watch, that would be funny: Bomber wasn’t impressed, and nor did he find it funny, at all. TC: Don’t worry you will be fine, I don’t think he’ll try it on with you, although he may think you’re kinda cute. TC just burst out laughing, again Bomber didn’t find it funny at all, and it wasn’t helping him for tonight. Bomber: If your trying to help me for tonight it isn’t working, your suppose to help me prepare for my match. Not wind me up about it. TC: Ok jeez I am sorry, you need to calm down and relax dad. Just think of it as another old FAW match, go into it loose and relaxed, enjoy the moment and listen to the crowd roar for you. You know you can be the best again, I know you can, and so does mom. But if you get yourself wound up by a bit of fun, you’ll bum tonight, or get bummed. TC just exploded with laughter, rolling around on the floor. Bomber had decided he couldn’t stay any longer, he needed to concentrate not have the piss taken out of him. He decided to head for the airport early. He went to grab his bag, and started to leave. TC: Don’t I get a kiss daddy? Bomber wasn’t really in the mood but he didn’t want to piss her off because she was doing a favour for him. So she got up and went over to her dad and he gave her a kiss on the cheek. TC: Good luck tonight dad, give me a call later so I know how you have gone on yeah? Bomber nodded, and then left for his car. Bomber had made some good money over his career, mainly with FAW and the odd sponsorship deal here and there. He had a pretty decent car, nothing too flash but it was pretty good. This was Bomber’s main opportunity to make it big again, he knew that if he failed in AWC then he could pretty much count out his options, he knew he couldn’t make himself look bad in AWC and just go with the flow of things. None of the backstage bull he had with other companies, he was just in AWC to wrestle, and help bring the crowds in. He got into his car, and begun the long drive down to Heathrow, the problem was, was he going to be heading back from Heathrow tomorrow victorious, or was he going to be returning a defeated debutant? Bomber arrived at Heathrow, it was around 1pm. He parked up his car and grabbed his bag. He got out the car and locked it up, then made his way to the departures lounge. As he arrived, people began to notice him, and started coming up asking for autographs, Bomber didn’t mind this as he loved the fans so decided to sign a few, then he had a little kid come up to him. Kid: Hello, can I get your autograph please? Bomber: Sure thing, what’s your name? Kid: It’s Dan. Bomber: Well Dan, its nice to meet you, how are you? Bomber signed the autograph for Dan and personalised it for him, Bomber enjoyed talking to his fans, especially the younger fans. Dan: I am fine thankyou, do you have a match soon? Bomber: Yeah I do, I just signed a contract with AWC and fight this guy called Red Rock tonight, and I will tell you what I’ll do, if you can come here tomorrow around the same time, I will give you a video of the match, how’s that for you sonny? Dan started jumping around like a wasp had just sting him on the ass, he was obviously delighted with what Bomber had said, what he hadn’t told Bomber was that he is one of his biggest fans. Dan: Oh thankyou! That would be great. Bomber: Alright Dan, well I got to shoot, I got my match to get to and prepare for, watch the video tomorrow carefully ok, I am gonna see if I can make a gesture for you. Dan: Thankyou Mr.Bomber, and good luck for tonight. Bomber: Cheers kid. Bomber then walked off, tonight was his debut, and tonight was the beginning of what could be something special. The problem was, Bomber knew he had a big task on his hands and he wasn’t taking Red Rock too lightly. All his years of experience was going to come into use tonight, was he going to set France alight or was he gonna fail big time? Only time would tell.
