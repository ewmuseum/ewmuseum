Do You Believe In Magic?

28 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28 Do you believe in magic? It’s a simple question, really. One with varying answers depending on who you talk to. Some people will flat out say no. Others will give an unqualified yes. And then, there are the myriad souls in between who’ll waffle a bit, or ask you more questions. “What is magic,” they’ll ask. That’s a more complex question. Most people think of pulling rabbits out of hats, or Harry Houdini, or any of that usual shit. But to young children, magic is far more simple. It’s seeing your favorite characters, as large as life. Not on a movie or TV screen, but right in front of you for you to touch and see. It’s going fast and high. It’s doing things you don’t ordinarily get to do in your everyday life. It’s Disney World. And on that note, cue “Princess” Samantha Taylor, AWC’s newest signing. The bubbly young woman, proud owner of a season pass to the Magic Kingdom ever since she was five. Needless to say, SHE believes in magic. Samantha Taylor: Thank you! Cheerily, she waves to the security guard who checks her things briefly, before allowing her in. She’s on a first name basis with most of them by now. After all, she’s here nearly every day, whether working or not. Ah yes, working. Considering the fact that she’s a complete Disneyphile, are you surprised at all? No? Good. Because, after graduating from Florida State University, the young woman sought gainful employment at her favorite place in the world, and she got it. You know the princesses who wander the park and pose for photo-ops? Yup, Samantha, more often than not, was Belle. But today, she wasn’t in the park for working. No, she was making her final visit before officially becoming a member of the Atlantic Wrestling Club roster. In a day or two, she would make her debut for the promotion by competing on Fresh! for the very first time. Before she stepped into the ring, though, she needed to clear her head. And this was the best place on Earth for that, as far as she was concerned. Samantha Taylor: This truly is the happiest place in the world! She bounced down Main Street USA, looking around her at all of the shops. Ahead of her was perhaps her favorite, the Main Street ice cream parlor, home of the best cookies and cream in the whole wide world. Even so, though, she pouted, the first sign of disappointment she’d exhibited all day. Samantha Taylor: Bother! I want my ice cream…but I’ve been training for this first match, I don’t want to ruin all that work now. Yes, Miss Taylor was rather sad. But only temporarily. After all, she’s far too naturally happy to remain upset for long. Samantha Taylor: Ah well. I’ll have it when I come back, after my first match! I’ll be on TV, it’s gonna be SO COOL! The lure of television and fame was strong, even for this one. Love of the spotlight had been ingrained in her from an early age. Ice skating, dancing, and gymnastics had trained her for this coming moment, her debut, blessing her with agility and flexibility beyond the comprehension of most. Could she take a punch? Probably not, no. She’s got strong muscles and tendons, but probably wouldn’t stand up so well to a hard right cross. But hell, if her strategy works, she’ll be able to dodge all those, so she’s got a fighting chance! Samantha Taylor: Hmm, who was it that I’m facing again…it’s that girl…oh yes! Ellis! She squees. If you want to know what a squee is, essentially it’s the making of that sound. Squee! It’s rather amusing/annoying after you’ve heard it 5767805367 times, as you likely will by the time Miss Taylor retires. Samantha Taylor: I have to get her a present! This is, after all, a momentous occasion! My first match…I need to get her something to thank her for being my first opponent! Yes, yes, I know. It makes no sense. Keep in mind that Samantha’s not quite familiar with this AWC thing yet. She’s not especially aware of Chainz and his wacky ways that make various organizations threaten boycotts. Or, for that matter, the undeniable douchebaggery of the Unfuckables. Nope, she’s just an innocent Disney-loving girl, fresh off of being trained, getting ready to make her debut. And as she disappears into a nearby shop, to find one Ellis Nash a present, one can only think to pray for her. Because AWC is the sort of place that challenges innocence, really. I mean, in addition to the aforementioned Chainz and Unfuckables, you’ve got the Furious Fists of God running around everywhere talking to divine dildos. I don’t think Samantha even knows what a dildo is. But she’s ready to meet the challenge. In a sport of treachery and injustice, she champions good sportsmanship and niceties. She’s PG, PG-13 in an rated R world, but Rated PG-13 Superstar doesn’t make as cool of a shirt or a catchphrase. AWC, this is Samantha Taylor. …please be gentle. Please?
