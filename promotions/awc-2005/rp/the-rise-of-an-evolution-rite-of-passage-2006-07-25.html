The Rise Of An Evolution: Rite Of Passage

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/7/06 Grand Rapids, Michigan, February 7th 2006 “This the shit that happens when you fuck around with V. Jones...” Vince exclaims. All of a sudden the camera fades into a parking lot outside of the Hinkel Fieldhouse the location of Fresh! just a week ago. Shawn Harris can be seen standing outside of his rental car feeling around in his pockets for his keys. All of a sudden out of nowhere 'The Violence' Vince Jones comes charging onto the scene and nails Shawn Harris from behind square in the back with his black, Louisville slugger bat. Shawn Harris drops to his knees grimacing in pain. Vince Jones snatches him by his hair and glares down at him in fury. ” Son you fucked up big time last week! You dropped the fuckin' ball and lost our tag match, bitch!!” Vince growls at Harris. Vince Jones slams Shawn Harris' head square into the side of the rental car. Vince does so again and again in succession. Finally Vince Jones lifts Shawn Harris to his feet whirls him around and slams him head first through the glass of the driver's side window. Shawn Harris rolls backwards and falls to the ground unconscious as Vince Jones looks down at Shawn Harris and shakes his head in disgust. Vince Jones can once again be heard speaking in the background. ”...yo punk ass gets silenced! Any questions, A Dubb C?? Enjoy your early retirement, Harris!” Vince Jones yells at the fallen Harris. Nevada, February 11h 2006 Those four words spoken by Vince Jones just four days earlier rang through Shawn’s ears constantly, Shawn couldn’t sleep, for three days Shawn had been laying there on his bed helpless, there was no doubt about it, Vince sure had done a number on him. Suffering several broken ribs and a fractured skull, the doctors told Shawn that he may never wrestle again, “enjoy your early retirement” wasn’t a lie in Shawn’s mind, and he thought he was done for… For about three seconds. Shawn knew what he had to do, he knew that he’s been injured before, he saw the injuries his father suffered in his many matches, “All Fucking That” Jeff H. died as a result of a match and even though he was revived, Shawn felt like a few broken ribs and a sore head was nothing. There was no doubt about it, Vince Jones did win that exchange, Shawn knew he should have seen it coming, a man like Vince Jones hasn’t got the balls to fight face to face, he only wins altercations by attacking from behind. When the doctors told Pearl Harber that Shawn may never wrestle again, he made the conscious decision of releasing Shawn from his contract with the condition that he can return if and when he’s medically cleared. So in a matter of days Shawn had lost his spotlight and his job. Shawn was used to losing things however, his time in Bloodbox became a very bitter one for Shawn, one moment he stood in the main event for the World Championship only to be screwed out of it by his father’s old rivals. Then his father comes out of hiding and takes the spotlight, Shawn then lost his job after losing a loser leaves Bloodbox match against Jeff. While Shawn did return later in the year he wasn’t the same, he thought the Atlantic Wrestling Club was his salvation, a true wrestling company with top talent from everywhere, Shawn learnt fast that he had a very long way to go, being screwed over by the selfish Vince Jones in the debut match. Shawn’s debut match turned out to be his only match, a contender for a World Championship against one of the greatest wrestlers of all time, Jimmy Thunder, reduced to nothing in a one year period. Shawn had just two accomplishments in wrestling to his name, there was no way he was going to leave it at that, in Shawn’s mind, he was destined to be the youngest World Heavyweight Champion of all time at the age of just 18, when Shawn missed out on that chance he counted that as another loss, if there was one thing Shawn realized after everything he had been through since being reunited with his father, it was that he was nothing like Jeff H.. Jeff was a monster, he responded to pain with extreme pain, if Jeff was ever put down, it would surely be the biggest mistake of your life as he would get revenge. Jeff was always like this and it made Shawn sick to the bone that he felt pain, Jeff is an immortal in everyone’s mind as not even death could keep that man down, Shawn felt puny to that. The reason Shawn turned on his father in Bloodbox was because he felt that he could overcome the odds, if he beat his father it would be his rite of passage, Shawn would no longer feel pain, he would feel nothing, but all Shawn felt during that time was guilt, he knew his father came out of hiding to help him and even though Shawn thinks he doesn’t need it at times, he also knows his father knows best. Shawn set out to kill Jeff however in Bloodbox once and for all, but ended up joining up with Jeff, only to be turned on by his father, revealing a months worth of rampage to be nothing more than an undercover operation for Jeff. Before Jeff resigned his role as Co-Owner as Bloodbox and gave up the Championship, he gave his son a Belt of Excellence championship match, which Shawn won. However he never saw his father again after that as soon after he was arrested for the murder of Simon Caine. To say things ended badly between the father and son is putting it lightly. With Jeff H. gone, Shawn had nothing, he was in the world alone, only a few short years ago, he had his father, Jimmy Thunder, Adam Masterson, Theodoric Solomon, his biggest influences in the business to hang around with, they trained with him, ate with him, they all breathed the wrestling business. They all soon fell out of Shawn’s life, he was brought out of the dump that is Brooklyn and brought into money and friends. Shawn’s immature attitude lost him those friends and now he was just a man with his father’s money. Shawn had been alone before in his life, but this time it was different, because this was the first time that he realized that he had absolutely no one to fall back on, before it was always Jeff’s best friend Adam Masterson, a man who always had the ability to keep Jeff centered in the most extreme cases, a man that looked after Shawn when Jeff wasn’t there. Adam Masterson, now gone from the face of the earth living what life he has left with his family somewhere in Destin, Florida, the last thing on his mind would have to be, “What’s Shawn Harris doing with himself?” Somehow though, Shawn knew deep down that he’d be back in the AWC, hoping for something that he hasn’t felt in his life before, accepted. Accepted by his peers, the wrestling community and his fellow workers. The main problem with Shawn is that he wants things now, only now does he realize that he has to earn their respect. He can’t be living off of Jeff H.’ fame anymore, despite the fact he hated doing so, that’s what he was doing, he hated being known simply as Jeff H.’ son. Jeff gave Shawn a true wrestling career, he improved his wrestling skills now if was time for Shawn to get out of his fathers shadow. Shawn needed something though, he needed to see his father, Jeff had been arrested for killing Simon Caine when he went back to his house in Las Vegas, Nevada, narrowly missing the death penalty he was given life imprisonment with no chance of parole, Jeff hated that he wasn’t going to be put to death, he felt that living in jail for the rest of his life was going to turn him insane, for three months, Jeff has been residing in a high security prison in Nevada, no one had visited him in that time, not Adam Masterson, not Jimmy Thunder and not Theodoric Solomon. One thing that was killing Jeff inside was the fact that he failed his friends and the fact that he would never see his greatest friends ever again. Every time visiting hours were called his fellow inmates rushed to the visitation room to see their loved ones, Jeff was one of the few that were forced to stay in his cell. After all the carnage Jeff had been through, he valued his peace and quiet immensely, being in a prison such as the one he was in would have it’s violent moments for Jeff, moments that Jeff would be able to overcome, but there was a thing as a little too much peace and quiet, peace and quiet soon turned into downright boredom. As the scene fades into view, Shawn is seen in his fathers BMW Roadster, a car Shawn took from Jeff a couple of years back, he began racing down a road in the middle of the day, along the side of Shawn’s face was a huge cut, stitched together by no less than 25 stitches. His beautiful face was battered and bruised, a few other cuts resided along side those bruises, his left eye swollen, he wore a baggy black shirt, only a few buttons were fastened, the bandages around his ribs were in clear view, his blue eyes glistened against the sun light that was pouring on mother natures creation. As Shawn is driving he sees several more cars in front of him heading towards the prison, visitors such as these are what keep the prisoners going in their time as downfall. Most of the criminals there were killers, malicious animals, would kill their own mothers if it meant that they’d get a packet of smokes out of it, Jeff H. was a convicted murderer, it was however justice, Simon Caine was killing the wrestling industry, Jeff snapped and made a mistake, even though Jeff claims he’d do it a million times over if he could. Shawn arrives at the prison car park, the prison itself is pretty intimidating, towers were in view with guards on the top holding what seem to be sniper rifles, Shawn parks the car, gets out, locks it, after all, there’s criminals about then follows some of the other visitors into the building, each person is searched before they’re able to go into the visitor room. Most of the guards looked crooked as fuck, if you paid them off you could probably sneak a gun into the prison, that fact alone scared Shawn just a little bit. A guard was then stood at the door taking peoples names before letting them into the visiting room. As Shawn walks up to the tall overweight guard, the guard looks at him, pen at the ready. “Name?” The guard asks. A little grin forms at the edge of Shawn’s mouth, “The Evolut…” Shawn stops, coughs and shakes his head, something was wrong in his brain, “Shawn Harrison.” Shawn finally answered. The guard looks at him funny then checks him off the list. He then motions for Shawn to go into the room. Shawn walks in and takes an empty seat opposite a glass window surrounded by a chain link cage. Shawn looks around, just about everyone looked like they could be prisoners themselves, there were the exceptions where some fine looking women had brought their children, there was dried blood on the floor, history filled the room, apart from the blood however the room was pretty clean. The prisoners then began filing out of a door and searched for their visitors, they took their seat and began chatting away to their loved ones, a few of the prisoners began crying with joy, others with sadness, Shawn waited patiently but no one else came out. Each man was chained up by the feet and wrists. Some shouting is then heard on the other side of the door, some chains are then heard jangling, a tall muscular man with long black hair and scars all over his face then steps out, it was Jeff H.. Jeff walked out from the back, no chains on, Jeff looked around, he saw a battered and bruised Shawn sitting there, Jeff had a look of bewilderment on his face he then took a seat in front of Jeff. Shawn looked back at Jeff, wearing blue prison gear. Shawn for the first time in his life was speechless; he wanted to see his father but didn’t know what to say to him when he finally got there. Jeff smiles at him kindly, something you won’t see often. “Son.” Jeff says quietly. Shawn tries to cover the huge cut on his face with his hair, “Hey dad.” He replies. “So, you’ve come to visit your old man have you? You’re not high are you?” Jeff asks, grinning. “No I’m not fucking high.” Shawn snaps. “It’s just, the last time we saw each other, and things didn’t go down all that well did they? You claiming I took your spotlight and all; it’s not my fault you know, I came back for you, then you just get caught up into the action, it’s hard to walk away you know? You think that you can step away when the time is right but then another opportunity presents itself and before you know it, you’re World Heavyweight Champion. The last thing I wanted to do was hold your career down.” Jeff said whole heartedly. Shawn looks up at Jeff and nods. “I realize that know,” he says, “but at the time I was bitter, I wanted too much too soon, now look at me, I have nothing. Nothing! And it’s all my fault. Yeah I may have been jealous that you were receiving all the attention but you were the man, you worked years, I was there for no time and I expected everyone to love me. They did, they just loved you more. When you were around, you kept things together, you were the glue. Now Theodoric Solomon has disappeared off the face of the earth, Jimmy Thunders taking yet another rest from the business and Masterson is spending time with his family, I have no one dad.” Shawn flicks the hair from his face and Jeff notices the huge gash down the side of his face. Jeff snarls a little. “Who did that?” He growls. Shawn lightly runs his fingers down the side of his face. “A man called Vince Jones; he said he knew you from Lights Out Wrestling. I joined a promotion called Atlantic Wrestling Club a few weeks ago.” Shawn replies softly. “Ah, AWC, David Harber is a good man, never done any business with the man but I have met him before, Vince Jones on the other hand is nothing but pure scum, I kicked his ass in LOW. He did this to you?” Jeff asks. “Yeah, he attacked me from behind in the car park and slammed my head into a car window. Fractured skull too and a few broken ribs, the doctor said my career might be over.” Shawn replies. “Bollocks. I’ve had a fractured skull before and I pulled through, you’ve seen the amount of injuries I’ve been through, nothing kept me down, you are a Harris and you will survive Shawn.” Jeff says optimistically. “I don’t know dad, you are known as a tough son of a bitch, you’ve been through more wars than anyone, you’ve been set on fire, punctured lung, broken ribs galore, everything you’ve gone through you pulled through but I’m not you dad, you don’t feel pain like others do, I survived being thrown off a huge cage, but I keep getting blinding headaches, you are the unstoppable Jeff H., you can’t be killed, I on the other hand, am a mere mortal.” Shawn retorts. “You think I was always the type of guy that could just shake off pain? When I was your age I got hurt more times than I can remember, I sat there after matches, almost crying because it hurt that much, I don’t not feel pain, I’m just used to it, I’m able to block it, send it to the back of my mind and bring the object at hand forward, it’s a skill you’ll learn over time Shawn, you will feel pain in this business there is no doubt about it, but you have to realize that it takes time. Don’t quit because you feel sore now, you will go back to the AWC when you’re 100% and you will show Vince Jones just what a Harris is, you’re the new All Fucking That now Shawn, whether you accept that title or not is up to you, Vince Jones is just some gangster wannabe, walking around with a bat, while you’re gone, he will probably rise up the ladder but you’ll always be there to stop him and put him back in his place. He’s made the grave mistake of pissing you off, never let those feelings go, hold the resentment until you feel you’ve done enough damage to him. When you get to my age Shawn, you won’t be just like your old man, you’ll be better. You are the Evolution in this business, you’ve made great strides already and your career hasn’t even begun. Vince Jones will know who the man is when you’re standing there with the Transatlantic Championship.” Jeff says. Shawn looks down at the floor; he looks back up at his father, tears streaming down his face. “Dad…” Shawn says, choked up. “Why can’t you just not be in jail? I need you dad.” “I am in here for a reason; I did something I believed in, something illegal… Take my hand son.” Jeff says, Jeff puts his hand against the cage. “What?” Shawn asks. “Give me your hand.” Jeff answers. Shawn puts his hand against the other side of the cage, his hand touching Jeff’s. “Now you listen to me, I have never been disappointed in you, never. You are my son, Four years I was reunited with you and it was the greatest day in my life. The way we’ve been with each other, it reminds me how I was with my father when I was young, the apple doesn’t fall far from the tree. I’m proud of you and I couldn’t ask for anyone better to carry on the Harris name. These cuts on your faces, they’ll heal, you’re 21, in 20 years time, and you’re going to be the man on the top of this business.” “I love you dad, I couldn’t ask for a better father.” Shawn replies, tears still streaming down his face. “If you ever need anything, you know where I am, I’m not going anywhere.” Jeff says, almost disappointed while saying it. “Time!” One of the guards shouts, signifying the end of the visiting allowance. The guards walk up behind Jeff with shackles in hand. “I have to go now son, good luck, I will be keeping updated with your business alright?” “Dad don’t leave.” Shawn says, saying the exact same thing he did when Jeff was forced to leave Shawn and his wife years ago. “You’ll be alright, own those fuckers for me son.” Jeff replies Jeff squeezes Shawn’s fingers then gets to his feet; the guards put the shackles on and escorts him and the other prisoners to their cells. Shawn takes a moment to recollect himself, he gets to his feet and wipes the tears away from his eyes. Shawn felt a little embarrassed in front of everyone else that the little boy in him came out, but they all understood. Shawn makes his way out of the prison, following everyone to the car park, a few of them spoke to each other, obviously meeting each other previously, a young woman with a child walks over to Shawn, the woman didn’t look anything older than 20, the young child was about three, a beautiful girl. “Hey are you alright?” The young woman asks. “Sorry do I know you?” Shawn replies with a question. “No, my names Faye, I saw you crying in there.” Faye replied. “Yeah, I was just visiting my father.” Shawn says. “I was visiting my father too.” Faye says as she flicks her long flowing dark brown hair from her face. “So, are you alright?” Shawn looks at Faye, looking at the cut on his face in particular, she knew that Shawn had just been through something intense, Shawn had a look on his face he hasn’t had before, and it was a look of relief. He took a deep breath and nodded at her. “You know what? I think so.” Shawn replied. For the first time in years, Shawn felt something inside of him, it was happiness, acceptance, he was glad that he was able to patch things up with his father, it was something that Shawn always wanted to do, he didn’t want to fall out with his father in the first place, he just wanted to make a name for himself, Jeff’s acceptance of his son almost made Shawn feel whole. Suddenly the pain in his face and ribs seemed less important, there was one thing on Shawn’s mind now and that was getting back to the ring, Atlantic Wrestling Club hadn’t seen the last of Shawn, there is no margin for error, as the clock continues to tick, Evolution continues to pass people by. Shawn walks towards the outside of the prison while chatting and laughing with fellow visitor Faye. Fade.
