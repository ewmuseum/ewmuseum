The Urge to Fit in Series (Part IV) Even Countries Aren't Safe!

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/10/05 The scene starts inside a Grade School class room. As the camera pans around the room we can see the small children, only aged about 8, scrambling while seated in their desks. They seem to be in tip top exctiement laughing and joking, throwing papers at each other and all the usual things grade schoolers do. Each little child has a piece of paper in front of them. The camera finishes it's pan facing the blackboard where the teacher, Miss Spears is stood in front of a blackboard which has the words 'The Person I most admire' written on it. The petit, blonde Miss Spears calls order on her rowdy class of eager youngsters. 'OK class let's settle down' And they did. Miss Spears was hot. Not that any of these little boys knew it though. They were only 8. Girls were icky and had 'cooties'. We're not sure what these are but I hear they're some form of American child Herpes. But anyway. Miss Spears had this soft but thick bottom lip. You would like to just suck right on that thing. Look at it......ahhhhhhhhhhhhh. Ahem. Anyway. Many young kids balls will drop to that woman. Miss Spears: OK I trust you all have todays assigment done? CLASS: YES MISS SPEARS She knew they would. Who could say no to her? Miss Spears: OK well let's have our first reader up to the class. Let's see here............. She panned the classroom. Which young boy would she select to read his assigment out loud. He would have to be brash, have some form of 8 year old self confidence and not be as thick as a big bucket of shit, like most 8 year olds are. She looked left, then right, then straight ahead. AHA! She had him. Miss Spears: Gary, Gary Weinman will you be so kind as to go first? 'OF course Miss Spears!' Gary was a teachers dream. He was sure to have a great assigment done. He took his place at the top of the class actually anxious to read his work to the class. They looked on, eyes wide, waiting to see just who Gary admired. Miss Spears: OK Gary who is it you admire? Gary: Mike Wade! There was a a couple of 'Alright's' from some of the kids in the class. But the ones with more brains, class and dignity and from the teacher there were only blank faces. Miss Spears spoke for the entire class. 'I'm sorry who?' Gary: Oh he's a pro wrestler and he's the greatest. She thought for a second, then realised he was only 8 and thought it cute. MIss Spears: Oh very good Gary. Anyway you know how you must start the presentation. One word to describe your hero................ Gary paused. Almost dramatically. Almost as if he was controlling his crowd he held in front of him. He answered............. 'UNFUCKABLE' ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yes Gary was a naughty boy. 8 year old boys don't worship wrestlers and they certainly don't say fuck in front of their hot teacher!! Gary was right though. He was doing what he loved. But if America was in this bad of a state, imagine what Ireland was like!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The scene starts with the word “Ireland…..” on the screen. It is written in traditional old Irish writing. Like that seen on beer mats and Guinness t-shirts around the globe. We are then shown pictures of the beautiful Irish landscape. Big green fields, with cattle herding around, sheep laying on the ground in the sunshine. We are shown men, happy as you like drinking pints and sitting in pubs with their “Paddy caps” on as a lone singer plays an accordion in the corner. The words “diddly eye” come to mind as we hear howls of “Yyyyyyewwww!!” from the old drinker’s mouth. They are enjoying their session in a warm Irish environment. We are shown an old lady walking down a small Irish village with her bags as a younger man, possibly her grandson laughs and talks with her as he shares her load of 3 shopping bags filled with the recipe for todays Traditional Irish stew. However the image starts to become distorted. It begins to flash like as if something is interfering with it’s signal. Then another word pops up on the screen….. REALITY!!!!! A loud rock riff then comes in as we are shown pictures of what Ireland is really like. Not the beautiful countryside and impression of brotherhood and drinking together. We are shown inner city Ireland with tall chimneys with black smoke festering the clean air. A small skin headed child is seen running down a street with a womans handbag in toe as a poor defenceless old woman lies with her knees cut on the ground and 3 bags of shopping on the groud, eggs busted and rolling like a yellow see form the bags. We are shown inside an Irish “pub” as glasses have been smashed, a brawl has broken out and there is a body lying in the centre of the pub as a man with a balaclava stands over the limp body with a pistol in his hand, the mouth of it still smoking. The camera follows the rise of the smoke up to the ceiling as the scene changes yet again, this time we see Mike Wade exiting the gates of his “Wade Manor”. He walks out of his 3 bedroomed “manor” and into the company of the rest of the housing estate. He is wearing a large overcoat as the Irish winter is being cruel at this time. Frost sheets the ground and the roofs of the houses as the whole estate looks like the front of a Christmas card. Unfortunately no Christmas spirit exits at this time, it’s February and the country in ruins. The hate and crime in the country at the moment is higher than ever and in fact just last night there are stories that a man was gunned down in a pub in Dublin last night, what is becoming of rural Ireland. As W.B. Yeats said back in 1918- “Romantic Ireland’s Dead and Gone it’s with O’Leary in the grave”. Mike: Jaysus the country is in an awful state isn’t it? Well never fear I have decided to brighten up all ye’re days. To show you that Ireland isn’t just a country full of council estates and bums. I mean look at the beautiful housing estate that Wade Manor is situated on. People like bank managers and accountants live here. Not like the inner city out here let me tell you. This is the suburbs where us well to do people live. No scuff balls out here!! I decided to go for a walk this morning, alone for once, shows you how good fuckin’ timing I have. One morning I decided to get up early and go for a walk and it looks like Santa’s fucking Village out here!! Ah well on I go!! Mike begins to take pace and starts to walk around the small, but content housing estate that is “Collins Avenue”, as it’s technically known. It is about 8.30 am and as Mike walks past a house a man emerges from it with his 2 young boys. The boys cannot be more then 8 or ten. Both are warmed up to the nines, gloves, hats, scarves, you name it, all to keep them warm on the way to school in the car. Both boys have their schoolbags on their backs and look quite melancholy at the prospect of another day at the dreaded school! However the sourness on their young naïve faces soon changes at the sight of their neighbour. Mike sees that the boys have notices him and he looks less then pleased. He lets a sigh out and it appears as if smoke is coming from his mouth, rising up from his belly, however it is just the cold. Young Boy: Look Daddy it’s Mike Wade!! Younger Boy: Daddy, Daddy, can we say hello? Dad: I dunno son we have to be going if you wanna make it to school on time!! Young Boy: Ah but please Dad this is Mike Wade!! Dad: Ah ok!! Excuse me Mr Wade?? Mike then keeps walking as if pretending not to hear. To Mikes dismay the dad reaches in through the already open car door and beeps the horn at him. He jumps with a bit of a startle. Mike: Fuck sake are ya trying to frighten the life outta me?? Younger Boy: Huh!! Daddy Mike said fuck!! Dad: I told you that’s a bad word you shouldn’t be saying it!! Young Boy: But Mike Wade said it Daddy. Younger Boy: Why the fuck can’t I say fuck Daddy? Dad: Just because Mike said it doesn’t mean it’s alright kids…. What a great role model for my kids you are Wade!! Mike: Ah fuck off! Dad: Come on kids let’s go, you have to go to school that’s enough from Mr Wade!! Young Boy: Ah fuck off Dad we wanna say hello to Mike!! Mike: Ha ha!! Younger: Yeah Dad fuck off!! The two boys run over to Mike as the father looks on in astonishment in the fact that his 10 and 8 year old sons just told him to “Fuck Off” Young Boy: Hey Mike you kick butt! Mike: It’s arse son not butt, doesn’t your Daddy teach you anything? Younger Boy: And this week you’re 2 other fellas arses aren’t you Mike? Mike: I sure am k….Hey wait a second are you the little fucker I saw taking a shit on my lawn last week!! Younger: Um….. Mike: You little fucker!! Dad: Actually Mike, Little Johnny here has a problem!! It’s not nice to mock him. He has a very rare disease called “Dogitivitas”. Mike: And it makes him take a shit in my lawn? Dad: Johnny thinks he’s a……sniff…..a dog!!! The Father starts to cry a little at his young 8 year olds disease. Mike: How the fuck did he climb over the gates and get into me fuckin house to shit on the lawn is what I wanna know!! Dad: STOP THAT JOHNNY!!! The camera then pans around to show Johnny sniffing his older brothers arse. Young Boy: I like it when Johnny humps my leg like a dog!! Dad: No you don’t dammit!! Now let’s go!! The rather grabs his two sons and piles them into the car. He quickly reverses down the driveway and starts to drive off. As the car disappears we see little Johnny’s head pop out of the back window, with his tongue hangin out and him shouting.. Johnny: I’m a dog!!! Dad: NO YOU’RE NOT!!!!! Mike: Poor kid. If he shits on my house again though I’ll kick him in the balls!! Ah Mike Wade. Ever the carer for kids. Yeah it sounds cruel, but what was he to do? Let the little retarded kid shit in his grass? No way Mike Wade had class and most of all he was Unfuckable! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Back home ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ We are back in Wade Manor and we find Mickey Moore and Bob the Bullshitter eating breakfast. Mickey: Jaysus Mike ya must be a fast walker. Mike: Na Mickey I just wasn’t arsed didn’t realize that all the children were going to school. Couldn’t be listenin’ to those squeaky little voices all the time. “Oh Mike give us an autograph!!”. I’ll give em an autograph alright!! Bob: Then what’s the problem? Mike: What? Bob: You first said that you didn’t want to give them an autograph. Then you said “Oh I’ll give them an autograph alright”. So if you will give them an autograph then what’s the problem? Mike: Shut up Bob. Mickey: So what’s da plan for da day laddies? Mike: Well it’s 8.30 in the mornin’ so I’d say I’m going back to bed. Mickey: Back to bed? No way Mike tis time for the pub!! Mike: The pub? At 8.30 in the morning? Mickey: Aye. Mike: What kinda pub opens at 8.30 in the morning? Mickey: McGinty’s. Best pub in all of Ireland. Mike: Let’s go then. Bob you coming? Bob: Ah I dunno if I can or not Mike. Mike: Why? Bob: Well the last time I was in a pub this man came up to me in the bathroom. He said to me “Hey You, I’m gonna offer you a chance to go to space are you in”? Naturally I said yes. So then I was waiting at his rocket all day long waiting to boldly go where no Bob has gone before and….. Mike: So you’re not going then? Bob: No…. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Pub ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ We are given a shot first of all from outside the pub. It reads on a badly constructed sign “McGintys- An Irish pub”. The sign looks as if it’s been made by the children of the proprietor and has been home to many happy woodworms for many a year. We are then taken inside. Now when you think of a traditional Irish pub what do you think of? Small, smoke filled dump, one bar man standing behind a bar. 3 taps, each of which sell Guinness Draft. 3 musicians in the corner, one with a fiddle, one with a banjo and the other with a tin whistle, all playing didly-eye like there’s no tomorrow. A couple of locals sitting at the bar and staring at anyone who comes in the door. Right? WRONG Inside it looks like any normal non-Irish pub!! There is a large bar area with beers and even wines from around the world. 3 beautiful barmaids are serving behind the bar. There are three musicians sitting in the corner. But there is not a diddly-eye to be heard. One man laying a double bass, another a trumpet and then other man has a harmonica. They are a jazz band. Mike and Mickey take a table and then go up for some bar food. They are greeted by Tina who knows Mickey as a regular. Tina: Ah Mickey, early as usual what can I get you? And who’s your friend? Mickey: I’ll have the Duck au l’orange I think Tina. And this is Mike my friend. Tina: Mike how do you do? And what can I get you sweetheart? Mike: You can get me a pillow baby because you just knocked me off my feet and I don’t wanna hit my head off the ground when I fall!! Tina: Ah Mickey you didn’t tell me he was retarded!! Mike and Mickey then take their places at their table. Mike: Hey who the feck does that slut think she is? I'll give her retarded! Mickey: Where would you get one? Mike: One what? Mickey: You said you'd give her a retarded where would you get one? Mike ever so slightly hits himself with disbelief. Mike: Your moms house. Suddenly there seems to be a bit of commotion around the pub and a shout comes… TOURIST!!!!!!!!!!!! Suddenly a big scatter occurs. Mickey Moore throws his Duck Au L’orange off the table and replaces it with a big boll of Irish stew. The beautiful barmaids put blackening on their teeth and pub on fake moustaches. The 3 musicians in the corner replaces their bass, trumpet and harmonica with a fiddle, a banjo and a tin whistle. They also put Paddy Caps on their heads and cigarettes in their mouths. The barmaids quickly cover up the world wines with bottles of “Paddy’s Irish Whisey” and the beers with stickers saying “Guinness”. The menu is replaced with a new menu with one thing on it “Irish Stew”. And the piece de la resistance: A BIG picture of Ray Houton celebrating his goal against Italy in USA 94 above the bar. Suddenly in walk two American tourists. We know they are American because it is about 9.30 on a snowy Irish morning and they are wearing shorts, t-shirts and socks pulled up to their knees and a pair of big sunglasses each. The also have big fuck off cameras around their necks. Male: Oh my Gosh Mary would ya look? It’s a traditional Irish bar. Female: It’s not a bar you douche bag it’s a pub! Male: Ah man a pub. Look some locals!! They approach Mike and Mickey Male: Hi there we’re from outta town!! Mickey: Top o da mornin’ to ya!! Male: ah my God would ya look he said it!! Mike: Hi. Female: Oh this guy must be from outta town too!! Where ya from? Mike: I’m from Ireland. Male: What? But you can’t be when you say hello to an Irish person they say “Top o the monin to ya!!” Mike: What? Where the hell did you…. Mickey: HA HA!! Cousin Mike would ya shut up b’God. Ha ha he thinks he’s from Ireland sure he’s from America like you. Listen to he’s accent tis all wrong!! Har har. Male: Ah I thought so. Female: My gawd look they even got Guinness here!! Male: Can ya tell us where we can spy on the leprechauns?? Mike: You serious? Male: Yeah we know they’re your little secret and you’re not supposed to tell us but we won’t tell anyone honest!! Mickey: Don’t ask!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Where does it stop? Even the whole country of Ireland has to play up to some stereotype to fit in. It just ain't right. What ever happened to the good old days of eating stew for the sake of it? To saying 'Top O d mornin to ya' because you wanted them to have the best of there morning. Now it's all changed. And it's people like Paddy O Shea and Crimson O Mallec that keep this hoax going. That's why Mike Wade and Adam 'The Face' Dick have gotta set the record straight. The only way they can do that is by winning the AWC Alliance titles. Byt taking the titles from those plastic Paddys and preserving their Unfuckable nature. They become as it were 'The Masters and rulers of the world'.
