All in the Family

They say blood is thicker than water; flesh of my flesh, and blood of my blood. Someone should’ve told that to the three staring into each other’s eyes right now. Three in the morning, and the DeNucci household was in an uproar. Only five people in the uptown penthouse, but you would’ve thought a riot had erupted. Shards of crystal scattered over the ground glistened under the soft golden chandelier lighting, as precious as they looked their jagged edges cut into the Persian carpet under grounded feet. We had imposed on quite a private affair here, one that would be kept tight within the DeNucci and Stallone families. If anyone knew of this travesty, it would break out in a civil war between Mafioso families. Blood would overflow the streets… and it all would come from a stain on the rug. “Do you think I’m a fool, Vincenzo? The proof’s right there in your face!” The head of the DeNucci family looked down at the open manila folder on his desk, photos spread in front of him in an organized fashion. Proof of his degradation was right there in black and white, taken with a high-powered zoom lens. Leaning back in his seat he exhaled deeply, keeping his eyes on the photo as the heavily accented Italian woman continued. “Who is she? Some whore you picked up on the way from the club? Or is she your late night meetings with the Giovannis or the Provolones?” Smoothing back some of his dark hair, he shrugged his shoulders and glanced up at her. “I don’t know.” “Bullshit!” The glass exploded as it hit the carpet, an elegant dark haired woman stepping forward in a long black dress with a thigh high split up the right side and matching heels. She tossed her crimped hair back as she pointed at him with eyes that would’ve impaled him if blades. “Vaffanculo a lei!” “All right!” Gripping the arms of his chair he looked up at her angrily, standing slowly to his feet and buttoning the jacket of his black suit. “Fine, yes, you’ve caught me. So what do you want? A divorce? Half of everything?” He walked around to the front of the desk and leaned against it, glancing at the red wine stain on the white carpet with disapproval. “If it makes you feel any better, the whore cost me more.” She bit her bottom lip, her fiery eyes narrowing more, “No, Vincenzo, I don’t want half. I want it all.” “Isa!” A younger woman in a blue blouse stepped up from her post near the silk draped windows, her hands leaving the back pockets of her tight jeans. “Cool your jets now, let’s not get out of hand!” “But you know your sister, Fantasia,” he gave her a smug smirk as he looked at her in his corner of his eye. “Isabella wants everything or else she pouts and whines about it. Well quite frankly, I’m done with listening to her bitching.” “You’re going to hand over full control of the business or else I tell my father about how you’ve been cheating all the families out of their full percentages for the last 5 years.” That smirk swept right off his face, stolen by his wife Isabelle. Fantasia stared on with mouth agape, keeping close tabs on the reaction from Vincenzo. He wiped his mouth, his hand halting at his chest as he pointed at her with a half smile. “You… don’t have proof of that. You don’t have access to the books.” “Really?” she folded her arms under her bodice, her cocky smile growing further. “I’ve always wondered why everyone called Jimmy Biamonte ‘Fingers’. Now I don’t have that query anymore.” The sound of his teeth was like the sharpening of a knife on sandstone to him. Now that all the teeth were shown, they would see who’s bite is worse than their barking. “How… disappointing this is to hear, especially from my own wife.” his hands went to his pockets, thumbs hooking outside. “All this time I’ve wasted my money, when I had a whore right under my own roof. Then again, she knew how to do the job right, so I guess the money was well invested.” It was that Italian temper that would be her undoing, her arm cocking back to bring her palm straight across his face. Catching her in mid-slap, he jerked her around so her back was pressed against his chest, his chin resting against her shoulder. It would be romantic if there wasn’t so much hatred in the situation. “Vinnie!” Fantasia stepped forward to help her sister, but stopped herself. Really, there was nothing she could do at this point. Isa always got herself into trouble, and it was the older sister that would take care of her problems by her own terms. “Fannie, be a dear and go get Tommy, please.” He turned slightly, Isa looking up with a painful expression. Her arm was pulled behind her back, wrist twisted in a crippling hold. “I think my wife needs to be escorted off my property.” Before anyone could make a move the oak doors flew open across the room, heavy black boots clomping across the hardwood floor followed by a pair of expensive looking black leather heeled boots. The boots stopped once they stepped on the white sofa, just to grind dirt into the fibers. Those boots buckled up under the knees, a pair of slender legs covered in tight denim with a studded white belt and a tight black halter top. A raven haired teenaged girl stood there smiling, pierced in her right lower lip and eyebrow. She smiled as she held her arms out in a warm gesture. Obviously she didn’t give two shits about what was going on right then and there. “Mother, Father…” The young woman towering from behind was quick to respond.
