History

11 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-8-05 God has blessed Liam and I with power and intelligence. He has granted us an ability that no normal human could ever possess. With our faith in God, the White Lord, he has blessed us with a combination of angelic quickness, holy strength, and an overall knowledge that nearly rivals Him. Often, though, we are taunted. We are pinned with cruel and unfair tags such as “RACISTS!”, “FUCKING PIGS!”…we’ve heard them all. They all think they are better than us, that they are so much better because they are ignorant. The problem is, I know they are not. I know that The Nest and everyone in it are the most powerful beings on Earth. We step on the rest of the dregs and reap the benefits of a loving and kind God, while everyone suffers because of our blessings. Ever since we were all kids growing up it has been this way. The Nest has and always will be the most powerful crew in the city. Growing up in our house was the first time we truly experienced the power of the Nest. My father, Gary Martin, was a single man. My mother apparently was a whore and my father disposed of her. He always told me that she would’ve upset me enough to make the White Lord strike me down, so good riddance to her. Anywho, it was a Saturday morning and Liam and I were watching cartoons. At the time I was 10 and my brother was 8, both young and just coming into form. My father was still sleeping at the time when we heard a ruckus outside. “Fuck, man, get it going!” Liam and I went to the window, and what did we see? Two dumb n*ggers jacking the car. As my father always said, “N*ggers are good for two things…stealing shit for ya and keeping the prisons full for ya.” I’ve never had any respect for a black man because of this, and I treat them like the dirt on the bottom of my shoes. As Liam and I watched, worried that they’d break into the house next, my father came storming down the stairs with in his slippers and his robe. In his hands he was clutching his pistol, his arm shaking with anger. “Dumb mother fuckers!” He yelled while walking down. “Watch this, boys, and learn how to deal with the rats.” He burst outside the door and started blasting. Those n*ggers didn’t even know what was coming for them. The both of them were dead before they even hit the ground, their bodies hitting the ground. At about this time, Liam and I began to worry that pops was about to be taken away by the police. A miracle happened, though, an act of God. Coming out of the houses, the neighbors saw what happened, and they instantly knew what to do. The Eagle’s Nest functioned perfectly and everyone sprung into action. The men jumped into their cars and blocked off the roads while another car pulled up and backed in. A few men walked up to the car and picked up the bodies, dragging them to another car. As they threw the bodies into the truck, my father shook their hands and embraced them with a smiled. The car then pulled away and everyone went back to their business. My father walked past the puddles of blood like it was nothing and then walked inside and sat next to us. “I know that might have been shocking to you. I know you’ve never seen daddy kill anyone before. But if any low-life scum threatens anyone in the Nest, you must exterminate them. I expect if anything happened to me or anyone in the neighborhood, and it was some dumb rat, you’d bear arms if you had the chance. Promise me you will…PROMISE ME!” Liam and I promised, and our time came about 7 years later, our ultimate test. Liam and I just got off the bus from high school and we were walking home. Outside, though, there were members of the Nest everywhere, all crying. Bob Smith, a good friend of my father’s walked up to me. “Go see your father,” he said to Liam and I, the tears rushing down his face. We rushed into the living room only to find bullet holes and blood everywhere. In the middle of the room on the floor, spread out, was a dead black man, as lifeless as can be. On the couch, though, I found my father being attended by a couple Eagles. They moved aside and let Liam and I come in. “It was those rats…call themselves the King Street Killers…I…I beat up one of their guys the other day…and they come in hear guns blazin’ and take the TV…typical n*gger shit…” I could feel the blood flowing from the wounds in my father’s body, the life exiting him fast. “Avenge my death. Show them what it means to be and Eagle.” And like that my father, Gary Martin, was gone. Right then, I knew what I had to do. I went upstairs and into my father’s gun closet and pulled out a couple pistols and two shot guns. I make sure both were loaded and I gave a pistol and a shotgun to my brother, and kept one of each for myself. As I walked downstairs and out to the car, strapped to the brim. The Eagle’s nodded me on, knowing what I was planning to do. I jumped into Dad’s car and, with one tear sliding down my cheek, backed out and drove off. King Street was one of the most rat-infested, dangerous streets in the city. In the same sense that we were all Eagles, all those fucking rats in King Street watched each other’s back…sorta. They were all divided into smaller gangs, for no one trusted anyone. Liam and I were planning to take out the King Street Killers, the mother fuckers who took out my dad. Driving into King Street, we received glares from everyone. All the little posses of black men stared at us, keeping a mean and watchful eye on us. I kept my eyes straight as so did Liam until we arrived at the house we were heading for. We stopped in front of it and, as we stepped out, we immediately started firing. We took out about 4 men out front, dropping them before they could even pull their weapons. We then headed straight into the house, firing and blasting any rat that got in our way. We dropped every mother fucker who tried to blast us when we finally made it into the back room. Firing slugs at everyone, we dropped the whole room and left one man alive. It was the head rat, a man who called himself “J-Killer”. One of us hit him with a bullet and he was laying there, breathing heavily. We both stood over him and, pistols to his head, blew his fucking brains out. We then turned and walked out the front door and, to our surprise, everyone just stood there watching. We didn’t make eye contact at all, and as we drove off, they just stared at us. Once we made it back to the house, all the Eagles were still standing there, solemnly looking at us. Walking out of the car with our weapons and covered in those n*ggers’ blood, they brought us inside as a congregation and we mourned our father. That is what the Eagle’s Nest stands for, that is what we are all about. When one of us falls to the hands of a non-Eagle, it’s Death for them. If any of the Eagles are threatened, then it is death to the offender. From that day on, we truly became members, and soldiers, of the Eagle’s Nest. Until Liam and I graduated and were able to make enough money to support ourselves, the community helped us make payments on the house and pay for taxes. Everyone helped and chipped in until Liam and I started our own landscaping company, Martin Bros. Landscaping. We employed many of the Eagles to work for us and, once we became members of the AWC, we hired even more. For what the community did for us, we tried to give back as much as possible. What do we ultimately owe all our success to, though? Do we owe it to the community? In a sense. Do we say “by ourselves, we achieved all this power”? Not at all. Ultimately, it comes down to our faith. Our belief that the White Lord will lift us and the Eagles above all else is what has brought us to where we are today. It is this that has made Liam and I the forces that we are. …And if anyone comes against us, you only have to guess what will happen to them. History, as they say, tends to repeat itself…
