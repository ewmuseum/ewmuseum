Beriddle: Ac I

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 18, 2005 The broad storm roared through the night. Rapid rainfall... vain cries of the angels attempting to wash away the brutality and deceit that had become a custom of this universe. The winds blew ferociously, challenging any steadfast might that happened to oppose it, and edging it convincingly. The screeching howls of the wind maybe tree limbs shiver and kept the streets of Blackpool, England vacant for quite some time. The wrath of the Gods above marked its destiny across the tarnished plain of the Hguyen household, where inside a curious boy sat, watching in fear as the flash of lightning lit up his otherwise dim room. He jump, startled by the unexpected bolt, cringing his teddy bear for immediate comfort. It was a school night, but the storm on the horizon prevented this young kindergartner from gathering the rest and assurance required for him to succeed another long day of his young educational experience. He attached his eyelids tightly, hoping that enough darkness would result in immediate rest, but with each roar of thunder or bolt of lighting, he found himself only peeling them open once again. His parents were asleep in the room across the hall; resting comfortably in the assumption that there son was as well. Realizing that sleep was far from being triumphed, the little boy snagged the nearby remote control and powered his small television set, hoping that a little television would prove as his calm against the storm. It was late at night, though, and the majority of stations were hosting adult programming and talk shows. The little boy sighed, finally settling at a re-run of 'Kids Next Door'. Joke after joke of content that appealed to him at one time became nothing more than predictable dialogue, giving the boy a sudden feeling of both boredom and tremble. He clutched the remote in his hand, slamming it on the bed mattress in frustration. Finally, he crashed his head back in his pillow, stuffing the sides of the pillow over his ears in a vain attempt to block out all of the rambling that was going on around him. His frustration level wasn't very massive to begin with, and it was pretty safe to assume that it had reached its breaking point. He finally threw his legs out and climbed out of bed...next step, parents. All of the lights in the massive home were out, making it difficult for the young boy to find the path to his parent's bedroom, but finally after bumping into the walls various times, and a scar from falling, he reached his destination. He slowly pushed the door open, cringing as the creak echoed throughout the room...hoping not to awake his parents to early. His father was a big shot Lawyer and his mother was employed by the government...in a nutshell, they really needed their rest in order to complete a full day of politics. Of course, none of this logic ran through the small, tamed mind of the boy as he crept gently into the room. He reached the bed and climbed on top, pressing his hands against his father's bare chest and shaking him as much as his small might would permit. Finally the father began to show signs of awakening as his eyes parted slowly, beckoning to return to their state of closure, but prevented by the agonizing child before him. The twisting and turning of the dad, and bouncing of the child against the bed springs ultimately woke the mother up as well, leaving both parents staring agitated at their only child in hopes that this would not be an all night situation. 'Mortimer...why aren't you sleep son?' Questioned the father as he stared into the wide bubbly eyes of his son. 'I can't sleep with all the noise outside.' The boy responded, leaving his dad to sigh and look over at his wife for advice on just how he needed to handle this situation...but of course, the wife gave nothing more than a Don’t know what to tell you look, leaving the father empty. He took another deep sigh and sat up completely, looking his son in the eye, knowing that he wasn't about to return to sleep without dealing with the situation at hand. 'Okay, Mortimer. What do you want me to do?' Asked the father. 'Well, since I couldn't sleep I began thinking about some things.' The boy began. 'Go ahead' responded his father in a less than energetic manner 'Okay...where do kids come from?' '...Ugh...Well, son...The-...' 'And where does metal come from?' 'I'm not su-...' 'And who is Jimmy crack corn? And why do women have more hair than men? And why is the sky blue? And who created school? And how exactly is paper made of wood? And how did dinosaurs die? And how was the first human born? And which came first, the chicken or the egg? And what is oral sex? An-...' 'MORTIMER!' screamed the father, stopping his son before any more questions could be asked. 'Look...its late and me and mommy really need sleep to get up in the morning, and you need sleep in order to be prepared for school in the morning. Now, just know that the world is a very complex place...a riddle, if you will...one that no man will be able to solve. So go back to bed and let mommy and daddy rest, okay?' stated the father in the most calm manner he could. '...Okay Dad.' Said the boy, lowering his head as he climbed down the bed and slowly walked out of the room in sorrow. Deep in his heart, these questions were real...he wanted to know. He walked out of the room and closed the door behind him, standing in the hallway for a moment as he thought again over what his father had stated. Just know that the world is a very complex place...a riddle, if you will...one that no man will be able to solve. Suddenly, the boy turned around and glazed at the door as if it were speaking to him...he then calmly spoke.... '...I'm going to solve the riddle of our world, dad...someday.' And with that, he turned and made his way down the path back into the bedroom, corrupted by the brewing storm. The thunder roared. ...Mortimer finally began to sleep. I remember that day. Location...Downtown Manhattan, a city plagued to its core with crime. Crime had become a passion for me. When a life in my father's footsteps as a lawyer failed me, I turned to vengeance. I wanted vengeance on the corrupt officials who placed me inside of a prison for committing sideline fraud. I wanted vengeance on the justice that was so proudly portrayed in this country. Vengeance is what I desired...crime is how I achieved it. But this was more than crime, because now I had met a challenge that even I couldn't muster my way out. This obsession with brainteasers and mid puzzling activities led me to become a man of high intelligence. I could decipher the age of a random person without any prior knowledge of said person. I could crack any one of Einstein's theories within minutes. There was no quiry that extended beyond my abilities. No man could equal me in a challenge of the minds... Except One. Chief Arnold Sabbot. I met Chief Sabbot when I was still an esquire. He was the one whom shut down my illegal operation and arrested me. He was a good friend of my father, therefore he kept me under his eye, scoping out my every move. Everywhere I turned, he was there, ready to convict me...Every time the thought of illegal activity crossed my mind, he was there to stamp a corruption into it. Ultimately...he was my reason for being here, in this correctional facility. How, you ask? It's simple. Dan Croni, big time crime boss in these parts...but now he was under trouble, not with the law, but with an opposing crime boss. His men were falling like soldiers, his family members were being buried week after week, and the killer was unknown. I was abducted, force to stare Croni face to face as he deliberately threatened me. He knew of my high intelligence...my ability to decipher any problem with an accurate solution, and now he wanted my services in revealing the killer of his family members and hired men. I did it... not for the 500,000 grand. not for the security it would provide me. But for my life. Chief Arnold Sabbot was right there...he busted me on the spot, stating that he had grown tired of my involvement in illegal activity. He arrested me, sending me to this facility where I've served five long grueling years...awaiting the day in which I would be released to the common world as a free man. Bracing the day in which I could start over, hit reset on this game called life and live a life of fame, glory, and honor...the way it was meant to be. Shift.Reality I stood before the facility officer in a manner of glee, overjoyed at the situation that was at hand. Two guards stood to each of my sides, ready to jump on any situation that may occur. Besides those two was the correctional officer, a woman by the name of Joy Vardaman sat behind a desk, reviewing the contents of a clipboard. In contrast, she wore a white lab coat, which prevented exposure of her under garment, and glasses. Her hair was blonde, which in my mind was a good thing, and her skin was as tan as the color itself. And of course I, who sat steadfast in a cold wooden chair awaiting what would be my infinite fate. Never in my life had I been so fearful of the unknown as now, but knowing the stakes of this one meeting…I had every reason to be. 'Mister Hguyen-...' 'Uh...It's Quiry now. Norman Quiry.' “...Right. Okay, Mister Quiry…I have some good news and I have some bad news.” I took a deep breath, ready to intake the ever so familiar bad news that I had become accustomed to, but at the same time reassured in the fact that good news was only a breath away. The last time someone told me they had good news, though, it was that I would only have to suffer having my head cut off and fed to a goat as opposed to the previously proposed chopping of my entire body if I didn't complete an illegal sideline mission. Nonetheless, I figured this couldn’t be that bad, so I held persistent ready to embark on what was in store for me and my future as a citizen of these United States. “First…the good news.” She began, automatically telling me that since the good news was coming first, ultimately the bad news was going to serve as a restraint to it. “We are very proud of your progress here at the Norshield Correctional Facility, and we are pleased to inform you that the eight months remaining on your five year sentencing will be lifted, thus releasing you as a fully gratis citizen of the United States border.” With every word, the smile firmly planted on my face grew in width. Four long grueling years and four even more excruciating months in this hell hole gave me every reason to be animated. This place was as hideous as my eyes had ever witnessed. The walls looked as if they were on the verge of caving in, the floors were polluted with cracks and dirt, and the food tasted of rubbish…and that’s not including the punishing drills we had to endure. As I jumped for joy inside of my heart, I tamed myself…knowing that ahead was still a bask of bad news that I’m sure wouldn’t serve pretty. ”However, considering your cautious track record, you will be placed under probation. Any violation of said probation will result in your return to this confinement. Under specific clause, if any violation revolves around the acts and/or motives of Violence, Forced Exposement, and/or hazardous persuasion…you will be shipped back to Blackpool, England to serve in the Glencoe Asylum. Is that Understood, Mr. Quiry?” She expressed. I nodded my head in agreement, making little effort to waste time for the fact of knowing the sooner I was to get out of this place, the sooner I would be released into the country as a free…ordinary man. I could feel the grunts of the security officers standing beside me, maybe signaling the pleasure they’d have in bringing me back into this place…or maybe just something to shake me up a little bit just incase the thought of crime even crossed my mind. It didn’t bother me any, though…for I was on the verge of freedom and I wasn’t going to give it up for anything nor anyone. “I’d advise you to find employment soon, Mister Quiry. If your track record makes that difficult, then the facility will assist you in finding employment. If I could leave you with one tip, Mister Quiry…its to start over. Forget about your past and start anew…because failure to do so may keep you from ever becoming a free man again. Now, with that said…Mr. Quiry…you are free.” She stated, standing in her chair as she did so and placing her clipboard under her arm while offering a handshake. I followed and stood to my feet, extending my hand and shaking hers’ firmly. I smiled and simply stated… “…Thank you, Mrs. Vardaman.” With that, she gave a nod and released my hand, leaving me to turn around and walk out the door of the Norshield Correction Facility for the very last time and unto United States soil as a free man… The air in this place was very thick, the tension mounting high as I primed myself for the dawning of a new era in my life. This was it. For so long in my life I had lived a existence of nothing more than illegal sideline activities and drugs, all as a result of my obsession with the mind. A bullet to the back of my neck landed me inside of a rehab prison where I learned that I had suffered from a disease, which affected my memory of childhood. I know nothing of my origin, nothing of my parents, nothing of anything prior to that faithful meeting with Satan himself in which he spared me my life. Everything that comes out of my mouth that even relates to my past is from what I’ve heard, read, or assumed. My life has been regenerated, and I look to make the best out of it. My Colorado Springs mansion played host to a couple of my gathered friends and a representative from AWC. I had invited them all to become a part of watching me sign over a new life by attaching my John Hancock to an AWC application. It was a glorious event in which I had to share with the people most close to me…which weren’t many. Half of them were only here for the wine and fancy rations that were present…the other half were friends of Quiz, who were also here for the wine and fancy rations. Nonetheless, they were here, and that was all that mattered to me. “Mister Quiry…you have a special visitor in the front lobby whom awaits you” stated George, a waiter whom I hired for the evening. Taking a moment to place down the glass of champagne in my hand, I made my way down to the front lobby to greet whoever it could be. Maybe it was a special guest, informing me that they couldn’t stay but giving me a check instead. Or maybe it was just someone wanting to give a formal goodbye. As I reached the front hall, my heart dropped at the realization that none of my assumptions were true…or even remotely close for that matter. This man before me had no check in hand, he was no friend giving a formal goodbye, he was just a bitter man covered in a black trench coat… Chief Arnold Sabbot. “Funny…I don’t remember inviting you, Chief.” I calmly stated, less than pleased at his presence before me. “Someone told me I’d find you here.” He responded in his usual ‘Calm, cool, and collective’ demeanor. “Please…if you don’t mind, exit my home while you still have a chance to do so under your own free will, Chief.” I asked of him, but I knew just as well as he did that ideal threats wouldn’t appeal nor alert him in the least. “Not until I find out what you’re up to, Hguyen.” He stated, “What ever do you mean, Chief?” “You know what I mean. Ever since your little incident you lived nothing more than a life of illegal sideline activity. You’ve cost this city millions, and out of all these years I’ve been the only one who could tame you. I know you better than you think, Hguyen…and I know this little innocent act that you’re portraying is a fraud, so before I have to take physical action…just go ahead and tell me what you’re up to.” “…Look Chief, I-..” Suddenly before I could finish my sentence, I felt Sabbot’s massive hands grab me by the collar of my very expensive custom made suit and lift me up against the wall. He began to shout in my face…his breath wasn’t even fresh. “DON’T GIVE ME BACK TALK, HGUYEN. TELL ME WHAT YOU’RE UP TO NOW!” “What going on down here.” I heard the representative from AWC say as he made his way down the stairs and unto the floor. “Put him down Sabbot.” “This man is a liar and a deceiver. He’s up to something and I’m going to figure it out…so you step back.” “What you’re going to figure out” snapped the representative “is that if you don’t put down our employee, in a matter of seconds you’ll be hauled out of this mansion in cuffs. Now what do you think the media will think of ol’ honest Chief Sabbot then?” “…Employee” Stated Sabbot. “Yes…Employee. Mr. Quiry has signed a deal with the Atlantic Wrestling Club.” Suddenly I felt Sabbot’s palms loosen up until he released the full grasp of my collar. His sharp eyes cut through me like a knife but I returned the favor with a smile, looking to agitate him a bit. “You don’t know what you’re getting yourself into hiring this man. As for you, Hguyen…Or Quiry…or whatever you’re calling yourself…remember what I told you. I know more about you then you think and I know of your obsession with mind puzzles and riddles…they all lead you to your illegal acts. You can’t let go…it’s become a part of you. Sooner or later you won’t be able to fool these people any longer and you’ll slip up…and when you do, I’LL be there to apprehend you. Goodbye, Mr. Hguyen.” And in one swift motion, Chief Sabbot was out of the building. I took a deep sigh as I felt his words lingering through my mind. The representative came over and wrapped his arm around my neck to comfort me. “Don’t worry about him, Norman.” He said. “He’s just bitter…that’s all.” “…but he’s right. I can’t change. I have an obsession with the mind…riddles and queries are my passion in life. Sooner or later he’s going to catch me and I’m going to be right back where I started off. Sooner…or later.” With that, I drew the man’s hand from around my shoulder and slowly walked into my master’s bedroom with my head held low leaving the man to shake his head in shame and leaving me pondering over one thought that corrupted my life for so long… '...I'm going to solve the riddle of our world, dad...someday.'
