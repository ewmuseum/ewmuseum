Weapon of Chooooice~!

3 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 03, 2005 Nights are always the breeding time for dangerous occurrences. Night is always the time in which the evil of this world come out to fight the good that once harbored the souls of this generation. Night is always the time that men seem to crave for, to hunger for. Night is the time of everything… except for Kris Krimzon. As the night rumbles on, scuffling feet and mumbling voices fill the stuffy and humid air. The trees, still glistening and wet from the morning showers, flow ever so gently. The streets water system is in “first gear,” still draining the soaking water that fell from the formerly blue skies. Candy wrappers, aluminum cans, and various objects lay out of place on the grass or the road, carelessly tossed to the side. Ambient sounds fill the air; sounds of angry dogs or hungry cats echo throughout the nearly abandoned street. The houses, also abandoned or left vacant for a short period, hold broken windows in their sills, crooked wood, and a home for various bugs, one of them being spiders. The scuffling feet and mumbling voices are those of Kris Krimzon and his friend, AJ Valor. Kris holds his white t-shirt over his shoulder, his chest and upper body bare, excluding the scars and scratches. To his side is AJ Valor, holding his arms, shivering. Kris looks at AJ funny. “AJ, how in the Hell are you COLD? It is seventy-five degrees out here! Do you have a cool or something?” AJ reaches into his pocket and hands Kris a cigarette. “Now shut up. Take your Kool and shut up you stupid loser.” Kris punches AJ hard in the ribs. AJ moans in pain. “AJ, I don’t smoke. You know that. I meant, are you sick or something? If you have a cool, that means you are sick you idiot.” “I don’t think you’re one to talk mister Heroshizzo. Why are we out here anyways? We could be at home being warm and stuff.” “I’m looking for my weapon of choice for my Rentless title match. You know I had to buy me and Tamara’s summer home? We were just renting it, but since I am going to be the Rentless champion, I can’t rent stuff anymore. So you might want to hurry and return the porn video that I… I mean YOU rented. You porn addict you,” Kris scolds. “I didn’t rent that porn video. You rented it because T-,” Kris silences AJ with his hand. AJ grins. “Okay, okay, I rented it,” he says with a lying grin. “Now what about this weapon of choice thing?” “Like I said, dumb ass, it’s for my Rentless title match against Christopher Ashton, parenthesis loser parenthesis, Crimson O’Malec, a name stealer, and Pact,” Kris says. “Dude, what the hell? You said parenthesis!” “So? I just wanted to make sure you knew what I was talking about… It is not that big’a deal. You’re just a loser,” Kris says. A scream is heard, interrupting Kris’s conversation. Kris looks around, frightened by the high pitch. Kris begins to run in the direction of the scream to find a woman sitting in the corner of a fence, cornered in by a vicious shadow. Kris sees the shadow and follows it to its creator. Standing in the moonlight is a gleaming red, or crimson if you will, poodle, foaming at the mouth. Kris grins with excitement. “WEAPON OF CHOOOOOICE!” Kris yells. Kris begins to run towards the rabies-infected poodle. The poodle, sensing an attack from the crazy man, begins to run underneath the rotting wooden fence. The poodle makes it through but Kris runs straight into it. Kris falls onto his bottom, and the women, still scared, begins to thank Kris. Kris ignores her, chasing his weapon of choice. Kris leaps the fence, scraping his knee. Ignoring the blood, he follows the strangely colored poodle into a rundown house. The poodle goes through the doggy door. Kris dives, barely squeezing through. The dog heads up the flight of stairs, and Kris still follows, avoiding cracks in the weakened floor. Getting to the second flight, the wood cracks. Kris’s right leg plunges through the wood, but the left stays up putting immense pain onto his crotch. Kris’s face morphs with pain, but Kris trudges on. He goes up the second flight and sees the poodle in from of the window. Kris notices the foam from his mouth. Kris gasps, he has got soda somewhere! I must follow him to his secret, underground hideout! The poodle leaps out the window, landing on his feet. Kris moans, and then sprints back down the stairs, following in hot pursuit of the poodle. Kris stumbles on the hole that he created, and is thrown into the wall. The wall breaks down, and Kris is sent flying outside into the wet grass. Kris runs in the direction of the poodle. Kris picks up a trashcan on the way, assuming he can capture the animal inside. He runs, the trashcan on his back, and then launches the trashcan at the poodle. The trashcan hits the poodle on the head, knocking it into unconsciousness. Kris, panting, victoriously slams the can over the poodle, trapping it inside. Kris then passes out, his body left to drape on top of the can. =+=+The Next Day Kris jumps awake quickly, wondering what happened, and what has become of his weapon of choice. Looking around at his environment, he realizes that he is in his bedroom, and in the corner is his trashcan, and inside is the echoes of claws on the inside, scratching. Tamara walks in with a water bottle in hand. She smiles. “Good job Kris. You’re being sued for animal abuse, and we have a group of picketers outside. You better be proud of yourself,” Tamara says, amused by the situation. Kris looks at Tamara with disbelief. “Being sued by capturing a rapid, crimson-colored poodle?” “Rapid?” Tamara looks at Kris confused. “Well, yeah. It was foaming at the mouth. The woman I saw said ‘rabid,’ I believe.” Tamara stares at Kris, still confused. “The poodle trapped a woman in the corner of the fence. I was wondering what she was talking about, because I figured the foam was just soda pop… But I then I rationed the percentage of my correct assumptions against the percentage of my incorrect assumptions, and my hypothesis of the foam being soda pop, I assumed, would be incorrect.” Tamara is left bewildered at the scientific vocabulary which escaped Kris’s mouth. “Wait, what the hell did I just say?” “Anyways, the poodle is registered as your weapon of choice. Good look getting to the arena with those picketers by the way... They are out for blood, I tell you!” View Kris Krimzon 's Biography
