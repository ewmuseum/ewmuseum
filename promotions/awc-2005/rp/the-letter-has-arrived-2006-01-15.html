The letter has arrived....

15 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.15.06 From His Eyes There she stood, the white veil ever so gently placed over the back of her well kept hair. I have never seen her look so beautiful in my life. Well I probably have but this moment was like none other. I stood there staring in her eyes as I held her hand and the pastor started reading the wedding ceremony. The masses were here, we had friends, family, and hell we even had a couple of our closer enemies in attendance for this wonderful event. The pastor talked, the words went in one ear and right out the other, not that I didn’t want to listen, but more like I was amazed by this beautiful woman standing in front of me. Soon to be Mrs. Wayne Russell, something I have wanted for a while. The words continued to come from this gentleman’s mouth nothing that really stuck out to me until he uttered the words, “You may now kiss the bride.” At that point I look at her with a huge smile on my face and leant in for the kiss. This point is where she became overwhelmed with emotions and the tears started flowing. I ignored them and gave her a gentle kiss, one I had never given before. This time it was to my new bride. From Her Eyes As I stood there listening to the words from the pastor, his hands interlocked with mine, all I could think of was the past, present and more importantly the future. I was about to marry the man of my dreams. The funny part was everyone was seeing him for what I see him for. No one was judging him by his tattoos, his piercings, or even his anger problem. Everyone saw this nicely groomed man who was holding my hands. This is the happiest moment of my life. The words every woman dream of, “You may kiss the bride,” were uttered. No longer could I hold it in, the tear ducts started to water, my face was engulfed in tears coming down my face. He leaned in and gave me a gentle but sweet kiss. One like I have never felt before, the feelings were immense, the emotions were imminent, and the love was there. That is all that matters, the love was there. I closed my eyes and held onto the kiss for as long as I could. I was now Mrs. Tiara Belle Russell. Present Time It is a rather chilly day in Newton, Massachusetts. The clock has just chimed in, noting that is was eight o’clock in the morning. The brisk cold breeze is the normal for New England during this time but is vastly different then the abnormal temperatures that many New Englanders have been facing over the last couple of weeks. The mail slot slides open allowing the mail along with a brisk breeze to come into the nice raised Ranch in suburban Massachusetts. The slim and slender five foot six frame of Tiara Belle Russell makes her way over to the door to see what mail has just arrived. As she gets closer she retightens her bathroom as the breeze made it a little chilly. “Honey, the mail is here,” She yells up the stairs as she bends over to pick the mail up. Her beautiful blonde hair accentuates her frame and would be something any woman would wish to have. Slowly she flips through the mail glancing after each piece to see if her husband was on his way down yet. She is not able to get past the first six pieces of mail before she hears his foots step come from upstairs. “Did we get anything good?” A deep voiced man is heard as he walks down the stairs as he only uses every other showing the significance of his size. The man who is dressed only in a pair of sweat pants is showing off his numerous tattoos and piercings His look is something similar to one you would find in the Hell’s Angels biking group or the one at the local bar room brawl. “So far it is all trash and bills.” She says with the softest voice you have ever heard, but it might not be it may just seem like it compared to his. This relationship seems to be a weird one. You look at them standing next to each other and they are complete opposites. I guess the saying opposites attract is true in this case. We have Wayne Russell who is almost pushing 300 pounds but then we have his wife who is barely 160 and that is soaking wet. She is quiet and very pretty; he seems to be the aggressive and loud one. Wayne finally reaches the bottom of the landing and places his arms over her shoulders and lays his chin on the top of her head. He looks at the mail as she slowly flips. They notice the usually shit cable bill, credit card bills, the average junk mail, you know the bullshit that no one wants. That brings up a good point? Why does everyone rush for the mail when they never get what they want? That is something I guess no one will ever know, maybe it’s the adrenaline of getting something to you weren’t expecting. “Did we receive that letter from AWC yet?” Wayne asks knowing both of them are on the edges of their seats; completely hypothetically of course, seeing as they are both standing. The letter Wayne was referring to was the couple’s first assignment for their recent contracted signed with AWC. AWC is a small growing wrestling federation out of the United States. The couple had joined looking for their big break and they were the first federation they were able to contact about having the mixed gender tag team unlike many of the others had said no to. Tiara Belle continues flipping through and gets to the second to last envelope. The return address states the address of AWC headquarters, located somewhere unknown to Tiara and Wayne but they figure the main headquarters are in some cheap country, similar to Ireland or England. “Here it is,” the face of Tiara Belle illuminates as she sees the letter from the headquarters of AWC. She quickly hands the letter to her husband, or so she thought she was cause as she was handing it backwards he was reaching to pull it out of her hand. Wayne is quick to rip it open and starts to skim the first couple of lines. Dear Mr. and Mrs. Wayne Russell, We are glad to announce your employment with the AWC has been accepted, as you already know. But this letter is not to welcome you but more to inform you of your first scheduled appearance at an AWC event. We have scheduled you in a match at Fresh! On January 17,2006. You are expected to arrive at the arena with enough time, the rest of your information is on the next page. Thank you and welcome. We both stood there as we were in shock, we wanted to be in this federation so bad, and we had finally done it. The time had finally come, we had been accepted and we had given our first match. A chance to prove ourselves, something we will not take lightly.
