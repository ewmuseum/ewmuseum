The Ceiling, pt. 5: Good Bye to the Devil

23 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/23/06 “What the fuck are we gonna do, Tim?” The question rung like a bell, the shaking ferocity of it echoing in and out of the two brother’s heads as they sat there, staring at that black fucking baby who just stared the fuck back. This little child, this essence of love, was now a cause for problem. Just moments ago they realized that their world couldn’t possibly accept this future that they wanted to embrace. The Niggers!, Joe said, or something like it. The niggers! So here the mother fuckers were, wondering what the fuck they were going to do. They had some options, but each one was scarier than the one before it. Each one had its own little thing that made you shudder when you considered the consequences of it. “We’re in a fucking hole.” You got that the fuck right. And these Larrys, they weren’t getting out easy. Let’s go over these options, what the Fists could possibly do to save themselves and this child. Option one is run away. The obvious benefits are that the Eagle’s would just shame on their name and that’s all, leaving them to live a safe life wherever they might head off to. A problem, though, is that their lives, the entire things, were spent in Boston, Massachusetts, and the towns and cities that surround it. Never again would the Fists be able to traverse through the city, seeing all the sites they loved. Boston Commons, the Half Shell, all the places they loved of Boston would be forever lost to them. And the people they loved, they’d be gone, too. You couldn’t just walk back one day and say “Hey, we changed our minds, fuckers!” No, you had to fucking suffer. You had to live your life knowing your trash to a community you dedicated everything to. You became dirt in the eyes of the people you love. And when Tim and Liam thought of this option, they shuddered. Option two is try to confront the community about having the child and, quite possibly, potentially raise it. I mean, what the fuck is having your own fucking prodigy that was an outcast all of five seconds ago be the center of the community? It’s out of a fucking book or something. But there is the problem—that shit will never fucking happen. The community will say “HEY! A FUCK-AH YOU!” and just spit in the face of that baby. Obvious death. Obvious bad. Option three is to give it away to some orphanage. Out of the hands of the Martins, the baby could possibly find guaranteed safety. But that’s the problem. The Martins aren’t going to leave Boston with a baby, that shit is suspicious. So, what would they do? They’d leave it on the steps of one of the orphanages or a hospital or something like that. Then we know where that leads off to, the never ending cycle. Some family adopts him; if he’s lucky, he’ll go to some great place. If not, he’ll end up back in the city. It only requires one time of not knowing where the Eagle Nest boundaries start to get your head fucking exploded. It only takes one time. And option four was the worst. Option four was to do to all the black people they’ve already met before. Option four was an option that was all too familiar, an option that could take the soul out of an angel. But option four was not too foreign for devils like the Martins. Sure, we’ve been preaching how great or how bad they’ve been before, but the Martins have the blood of devils. So when option four came up, all they saw was misery. Option four froze the brothers. The idea of it spawned fucking horrible things. Demons, circling around their heads, stabbing and prodding at their skin. Human beings, ripping towards a state of inside-outness while their screams echoed in the vivacious wind. The calls for help, none to be answered as the devil slashed at the souls he had acquired. Option four was hell. Option four wasn’t human. Option four was to put a bullet in the head of the baby. And the image of their child, S-Laughters blood son, laying there on the table moments after a bullet went through his skull, burnt into the minds of the Martins. They placed the baby on the table and scurried away, afraid of him. “Tim!” “Liam, I know…” “But…but…” “We can’t do it…” But they could. Oh, they fucking could. There was a line for this thing, and the Martins have repeatedly jumped over this line and pissed on it from the point of no return. The Martins have done foul things before…all in the name of fucking God, though, right? Well, what kind of God would condone this?” “What kind of God would condone this?” What kind? What kind of mother fucking God would bless these two children of him to make it acceptable to murder a child? What kind of God would smile and say “You have done me proud.” What kind of fucking God is this?” “Liam, there is no God for us. Only Hell.” And the two brothers stood there, the tears slowly pouring out of their eyes. These giant men, muscles making them look like fierce, ancient-style warriors who could break diamond-bladed swords off their chests, wept like the baby that scared the fuck out of them. Their whole worlds have been ruined, and because of what? Because of what? “The ceiling.” “Tim, the fuck?” “I’ve looked into it before for guidance, I can see it again.” “Tim, please! We can’t take this crazy bullshit you’re speaking right now!” But there was no other option in Tim’s mind. I mean, it’s the damn ceiling. For so long the ceiling had relayed the truth to Tim. The ceiling was the portal into worlds where truths became lies and lies were more real than you could ever fucking want. So there was no other option. As Tim stormed to the back of the apartment where is room was, Liam followed, desperately trying to stop his deranged brother. Liam was distressed, but looking into a fucking ceiling for guidance? “Tim, the fuck! Stop this fucking shit! Look, you’re going insane!” But before Liam could make a difference, the door slammed on his face. The sight of worn out oak sunk his heart. ~~~~ The room is dark. I see nothing but darkness, darkness as I lay here. Outside the room, I can hear my dear brother, but he is there and I am here, and there is nothing but darkness. A darkness that no long can illuminate. A darkness that has been brought upon myself by that child, that fucking child. So here we return. The fucking ceiling. The place I’ve been so many times. I think this is my final guidance, my final choice. This concerns the child, ceiling. This concerns his fate and, in return, me and Liam’s as well. This is about the future. But don’t give me some sideways bullshit, ok? I want the fucking truth. I want it how it is, how it’s going to be, and all that fucking boosh, ok? And… And here it fucking comes, I guess. It twirls and swirls and shoots out like cannons, exploding light into the former abyss. The images, though…they’re not fucking there…just these lights and colors…what the fuck?...HEY!...I said images… “Tim…” Who the fuck? Who said that? “Tim…” What the fuck…who the fuck… “Option…four…” And I snapped, back into darkness. What the fucking Christ, I remember thinking, what just fucking happened? I couldn’t remember a damn thing…except…option fucking four. ~~~~ As the view of worn out oak pulled away, the sight of Liam’s brother replaced it. In only a matter of minutes Tim went from crazed and youthful to crazed and aged, the bags under his eyes hallow out the rest of his face. Liam looked upon his brother’s face and saw an empty man, prompting worry and fear. “Tim, what the fuck! I heard you screaming!” “The ceiling.” “The what?” “The fucking ceiling. It told me,. Liam. It told me everything.” “What the fuck is everything?” Tim brushed past Liam, not answering the question and instead fumbling towards the fridge like a barely drunk man, ripping the door open and pulling out a beer. He plopped down at the kitchen table and popped it open, taking a quick little sip. Calmly, Liam sat down next to him, staring straight into Tim’s eyes with a concern that wasn’t returned. “Tim…” “Liam…I have something I need to tell you. Remember the Baptism of Fire match?” “Don’t remind me.” “Well, me more so. After that match, I was bound to my bed so I could recover, trying to regain composure or stamina or what the fuck have you. You know, always just laying there, day in and day out…” “Yeah, Tim, you were pretty fucked up.” “Fucking right I was. Well, after a while, and this might sound fucking crazy, but I started seeing things in the ceiling, y’know, crazy fucking shit.” “Tim, that doesn’t just sound crazy—that is crazy.” “But, no, the fucked up thing is that every time they’ve been right! They saw the original devil in me, they saw me going down the inevitable path…” “Wait, wait, wait…original devil?” Tim paused for a moment, silently contemplating what he just revealed. The ceiling is MY secret, why am I letting this go? What the fuck am I doing? He tried to think of some type of lie…but there was no point; Liam had to know the truth. “Liam…I’ve seen myself as the devil. I’ve looked upon that ceiling and I saw the devil, staring back. And then just to test it, I killed this basehead, and what did I see in his eyes as I gutted him with my knife? The devil.” Tim expected something, anything, but not what he received. Instead, Liam dug his head into his hands and the tears began to flow. And he cried, he cried like the little baby he was moments ago. “Tim…” he said, words muffled in his hands, “I saw it too…I saw it in Joe’s eyes…” “Saw what?” “THE DEVIL!” And the two brothers knew their fate was sealed right there. Sure, Tim was already aware, but now Liam was too. There was no White God. There was no savior waiting for them at the end. Instead, there was just an eternity of nothingness and darkness, even worse than the mightiest flames. All the while, though, the baby was just laying on the table, crying its eyes out. Liam and Tim were fucking screaming the whole time, and everyone knows that babies don’t like screaming. “What do we do with him now, Liam?” With one swift motion, Liam made his choice. He whipped out the gun tucked between his skin and boxers and stuck it in the child’s face. The hammer was pulled back but before the trigger could be swatted, Liam was flung back on the ground, tackled by his brother. As Liam struggled to get up… *CRACK!* A hard right to the face. Liam’s fury prompts him to struggle again, but… *CRACK!* Another one. Right to the fucking face. And then there it is, a fucking fore arm to the throat by Tim. The gargling from Liam trying to gain breath was the only sound being emitted by him while Tim used all his might to keep Liam on the ground. He struggled and threw blind punches, but Tim held him. “NO! OPTION FOUR IS NO OPTION AT ALL! WE ARE NOT DEVILS!” *CRACK!* And Tim is thrown off of Liam as Liam bats Tim’s jaw with the gun. Liam struggles to get up and simply just aims the gun again, but all plans are foiled when Tim uses what little strength he has left to tackle Liam to the ground. The gun goes flying across the room as Tim starts slamming fists into Liam’s face. “TIM! THIS CHILD!...” *CRACK!* “I don’t want to hear you, Liam! WE! WILL! NOT! MURDER! A! CHILD!” “THAT’S NO CHILD! THAT’S A DEVIL!” “The fuck you just say?” “ONLY A DEVIL COULD MAKE US SINNERS, TIM!” *CRACK!* Tim rolls off Liam semi life-lessly as he was not expecting that hard right to the jaw. He rubs his own mouth for a second but then realizes that his brother is up to no good. And by no good, I mean wanting to shoot a fucking baby in the face, which is a little more than no good. So Tim musters the last he has and grabs a drinking glass from the cabinet while Liam muddles through the living room, searching for the gun. Stalking, he sneaks behind Liam who is still on his hands and knees, and before Liam can do anything… *CRACK!* He’s out the fuck cold, flat on his stomach. Tim looks at his formerly crazed and now unconscious brother and shakes his head. He doesn’t understand…option four is no option…even though…even though… The ceiling? No way…the ceiling…was it not showing Tim’s life? Was it not projecting him as the devil? Maybe…maybe Tim had the heart… He had to know. He had to fucking know. So he returned to the room where he saw the ceiling and laid down, slamming the door shut. He didn’t care if Liam awoke now, he had to know if what he saw… And then the lights. And then the images again. The devil leading the army. The man getting shot. The moth. The parent with the child. Everything. It was all Liam. That sinister face from hell…Liam epitomized it. The type of being that could be so easily manipulated as to become on the side of the devil himself. He wasn’t a bad person himself, but his beliefs, the beliefs I unknowingly crafted with a good heart were actually with a bad one. But then the images continued. And I saw what it was. A chase. Through the alleyways I clenched onto that baby while those demons behind me were in hot pursuit. I could feel their breath, blowing on the back of my neck and raising the hairs I had there. But I just clenched onto that fucking baby, not letting a soul harm him. The lights, the images, though, they all ceased, which I knew was bad news: Liam awoke. I could hear the rustling of the ottoman out in the living room as he struggled to gain balance, so to be a step ahead of him I jumped out of the room and into the kitchen. I turned and saw him there, crossed eyed and not knowing where the fuck he was. “T-Tim…the b-b-babye…killlll it…” Even though he wasn’t thinking straight, his intentions were still clear. He wanted that clouded perception of reality at any cost, and if killing a child was it, then so be it. I couldn’t allow it, though, so I grabbed the baby and bundled him up, running out the door. Behind me I could hear Liam attempting to chase after me, becoming more and more physically adept with every step, so I moved faster. The street was dark as the sun set a few hours ago, the street lights barely lighting things up. I ran as quickly as someone with a baby in their hands could run, trying to escape the death for this child. “TIMMM! GET BACK HERE!” His voice boomed through the air as I could hear him at his finest, putting on a full sprint towards me. I had to make an exit, I had to escape somehow, so I darted into an alley way. I could still hear him behind me… “TIMMM! WE HAVE TO DO IT!” But I kept churning. I moved as fast as I could, trying to escape, trying to blend in. The odd thing was that the child wasn’t even wailing. All it was doing was just smiling and giggling as it laid its eyes on me. But there was no time to find it cute. Once I reached the other side of the alley, I heard Liam start running through it, crashing through the trash bins that laid inside. He wasn’t necessarily speeding up, but he was keeping pace. The noise from him died down some as I kept on going, jumping into another alley way. I kept on moving, not even sure of where I am. The time became a blur as I kept on darting and dashing, the child clutched in my hands as I could hear his death keeping pace, getting closer and closer. I kept on moving, though, I kept on moving, until I reached the end. The end, though, it wasn’t a good end. It was a fucked up, crazy end. It was a fucking dead end alley, a building that had a nook right in the middle. And here I was, staring at it through the darkness. All around me was trash and grime, just muck. And the graffiti? A mother fucking devil. How God damned appropriate. I stared it this wall, but there were no lights, no images, nothing. This wasn’t any fucking ceiling, because this fucking thing didn’t lie. This was the end, and that was that. Eventually, I could hear Liam get closer and closer, running full speed. Once he was within ten feet of me, I turned around to see him slow down to a halt. While I wasn’t feeling the bit fatigued, he was panting like a mad man. “Tim…*cough* you can’t…you can’t let it go…” “Why not? Why the fuck can’t I? Why the fuck does he have to die?” “Because he’s…” “The devil? This isn’t no omen shit, Liam! This is real fucking life! And there are no devils for children, you can’t be born evil. You have to CHOOSE that path!” “But…” “But you’re perfect? You’re pure, you’re everything, right? You’re a God damned mother fucking Eagle, aren’t you? Well, mother fucker, that’s not how it is. We crafted out own beliefs and we followed the path of someone else…” “Our father…” “Yeah, but we followed. We didn’t choose our own ways, we tried to be just like pops. Shit, he was a big powerful man, wasn’t he? He was someone you could really aspire to, eh?” “He was loved…” “He hated humanity! He hated the best and the worst. And there can be no pure when there’s any kind of hate.” “But…but Tim…the community…” “Fuck the community! Fuck the Eagle’s nest! This child…this poor, innocent thing has to be blown the fuck up…for a bunch of thirty something white guys who hate niggers? That’s what it has to be about?” Silence. “Liam, we’ve been had. But we’ve made our choice. We get rid of this child, we go back, and no one knows a fucking thing. You know why? Because we’ve already got our space reserved. We’ve killed the innocents and the worthy, all in the name of a White Lord? It’s no good. It’s no good at all.” And Liam stood there, seeing the truth for the first time. Before he’s had it clouded before, he’s seen the truth the way he wanted to see it. But now here it was—the God damn mother fucking truth. “So…so what do we do then, Tim?” “What do we do? We give the child away.” “Away?” “An orphanage. Leave it on the steps of a house, somewhere we can trust, wherever. But we CAN NOT keep him, no matter how much I love him…” “I love him too, you know…” And the truth. “I know, Liam…” “I didn’t…” “Shhh…I know the truth. Now, let’s go.” So the two brothers walked away and found a nice place to leave the child, blanketed up. They placed him there and turned away, afraid for what will happen to it but also happy. They returned to their apartment and cried the night away, saddened and joyous in a sense. While all seems to have finished up in a sense (but things will never be the same), I can’t neglect a not that was left in the blanket of the child. It reads: “For whom it concerns, This child is no ordinary being. This child is the brace between the white and the black. This child will break boundaries one day, making hate a thing of the past. But for this child to do that, you must take him in and raise him in a world where there is no color, a place where he can grow. He’s already got the heart—now give him the mind.”
