Worst. Intro. Ever.

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th January 2006 Let me introduce the latest AWC duo. This is Chuck Norton. Chuck Norton: “Hi!” *waves* Chuck stands at 6’1 and weighs 300 lbs. He is a little on the chubby side. Chuck Norton: “Hey! That’s not fair! It’s my glands!” Sorry, it’s his glands. Chuck Norton: “Much better…” Chuck was born in New York City, New York just over 28 years ago… Chuck Norton: *cough* What? Chuck Norton: “28?” 27? Chuck Norton: *cough* 26? Chuck Norton: “Much better…” Chuck was born in New York City just over 26 + 2 years ago to Jack and Susan Norton. Growing up, Chuck resented the smoky, horrible life of the city. He longed for the fields. He longed for the open air. He longed… for the countryside. So what did Chuck? Chuck Norton: “The Chuck became a country and western singer…” Did you just refer to yourself as ‘The Chuck’? Chuck Norton: “Yeah, it’s a new nickname that I’m trying… The Chuck… What do you think? That that is the stupidest nickname ever. Chuck Norton: “No it’s not… Calling someone Steve when their name is Frank would be the stupidest nickname ever…” Why would someone do that? Chuck Norton: “Let’s find out… From now I’ll call you Brad…” But my name is Narrator… Brad makes no sense… Chuck Norton: “Brad, could you please the introduction?” Are you being serious? Chuck Norton: “Come along, Bradley… These good people that are kind enough to read this are quickly getting confused and bored…” Fine. Chuck became a country and western singer. And along with Russ Bridges… Russ Bridges: “Hello!” Chuck Norton: “Hey! This is my spot! What are you doing here?” Russ Bridges: “Well he mentioned me!” Chuck Norton: “How are you hearing him? He is the narrator in my head!” Russ Bridges: “Oh my god! He is the universal narrator… What are you? Zach Braff?!” Chuck Norton: “What?” Russ Bridges: “Zach Braff! The guy from Scrubs!” Chuck Norton: “The characters name is J.D.” Russ Bridges: “But the actor is Zach Braff…” Chuck Norton: “Does Zach Braff hear voices?” … Chuck Norton: “Check-mate… Gin… and Check-mate!” Russ Bridges: “You said Check-mate twice!” Chuck Norton: “You were wrong!” Russ Bridges: “But you repeated yourself…” Chuck Norton: “(whiny voice) But you repeated yourself… You were wrong!” And as you can see, Chuck and Russ are best friends and lovers. Chuck Norton: “We’re not lovers…” You’re not? Chuck Norton: “NO!!” My notes must be wrong. Russ Bridges: “What? I’m not good enough for you…” Chuck Norton: “We are not doing this again… Bradley, continue…” This is Russ “Hash Brown” Bridges, Chuck’s country and western partner. Russ was born 27 years ago. Collectively they are Norton & Bridges. Russ Bridges: “Why does your name come first?” Chuck Norton: “Alphabetically…” Russ Bridges: “What? That would mean I was…” Chuck Norton: “Actually I mean reverse alphabetically… Anyway it sounds better with my name first…” Russ Bridges: “Bridges & Norton… Norton & Bridges… Bridges & Norton… Okay, you win…” Collectively they are Norton & Bridges. The latest addition to the AWC Duo roster and a worthy adversary of any of the teams already on the AWC payroll… Hold on… What makes you two worthy adversaries? Chuck Norton: “Look at us… We are worthy…” Russ Bridges: “Yeah… Exactly!” But what wrestling training have you had? Collective: *shrug* So you guys have never been in a wrestling ring before? Chuck Norton: “Nope…” Russ Bridges: “I have!” Really? Chuck Norton: “When?” Russ Bridges: “By accident… I got lost one day when I went to a show and ended up in the ring…” What? Chuck Norton: “What?” How can you get lost and then climb into a ring? Russ Bridges: “Well not so much a wrestling ring… as an ambulance… and not so much lost… as passed out from being so drunk…” So you’ve never been in a wrestling ring? Russ Bridges: “Never…” SMACK! Russ Bridges: “Ouch! What was that for?” Chuck Norton: “Wasting valuable lines…” So… Norton and Bridges… The latest additions to the AWC roster have never been in a wrestling ring and have never even wrestled before. Collective: *nod* Wow. How did you guys get signed? Whose wife is secretly having an affiar with Pearl Harber? Chuck Norton: “Isn’t he really Ben Affleck?” Russ Bridges: “No… Ben Affleck was in Pearl Harbour…” Chuck Norton: “Ben Affleck is gay!?! I knew that girl from Alias was too good for him but I didn’t know they were actually hiding his gay-ness. Eww.” Russ Bridges: “He’s not gay.” Chuck Norton: “Finally the whole J-Lo thing is making sense. I always knew there was something weird about him. This is inspiring me.” *walks off* Where’s he going? Russ Bridges: “He’s just been inspired… That can only mean in about twenty minutes we are going to have another song on our hands…” But he just walked out on your introduction to the legions of the AWC fans. Russ Bridges: “Like it was going well anyway…” True. Shall we just leave it at that? Russ Bridges: “Why not? I’m sleepy…”
