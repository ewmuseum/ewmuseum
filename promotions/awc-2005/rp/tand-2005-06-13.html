TANd~!

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/ [The scene opens on the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. HIV Joey and Lamar the Kebbler Elf are walking around in their Carrot costumes like nothing is wrong. They are drinking V-8 as they pass such horrors as the Wrestling Sausage, Pepe the Giant Poodle, and Quagmire the Barbarian. As they walk past the Lake of Jobbing, they begin to chat about their upcoming match.] Lamar: 'Hey Joey.' Joey: 'Yes Lamar?' Lamar: 'Do you really have HIV?' [Joey shakes his head.] Joey: 'Nah. I only have (holds up his can) V-8.' Lamar: 'Oooo...' Joey: 'We're fighting the Emerald Isle this week.' [Lamar about chokes on his veggie drink.] Lamar: 'WHAT~?! They'll kill us! We don't know how to wrestle! Hell, John Grisham can wrestle better than us!' Joey: 'I know. Just ask Ethan Phoenix.' Lamar: 'How come so many wrestlers are named Phoenix?' [Joey shrugs his shoulders.] Joey: 'Dunno. Maybe they like Arizona.' Lamar: 'Man, screw Arizona.' [Joey tries to raise an eyebrow but it gets caught on the top portion of his carrot costume.] Joey: 'Don't you mean *fuck* Arizona?' Lamar: 'But we can't say *fuck* on TV, can we?' Joey: 'The FCC is not onto wrestling yet. They're too busy groping Howard Stern.' Lamar: 'Weren't we talking about our match?' Joey: 'Yes.' Lamar: 'Then how did we get to here?' Joey: 'We got here from then.' Lamar: 'But isn't then 'now?'' Joey: 'How can 'then' be 'now' when we're now and then is then?' [They stop and ponder that question only to run into the greatest worst wrestler ever, the Nookie Monster! He loves carrots because they remind him of his favorite thing in teh whole entire universe of wrestling and misfits...NoOkIe~1~~!~!11!!!1111!!!1111!!!] NM: 'Me NoOkIe MoNsTeR... (looking for nookie) ME WANT NOOKIE~!' [The Nookie Monster looks on a nearby table and sees a fortune cookie just sitting there. He goes wild.] NM: 'Me want nOoOkKkKiIiIiIeEeE~!~!~!~!~!~!~!' [The Nookie Monster charges past, knocking over our two favorite carrots and a couple of other misfit wrestlers in order to get to the cookie only to find that the cookie isn't nookie. It's a fortune cookie. He pouts and walks away as our heroes try to get up, but can't because they keep tripping over their carrot costumes as the scene fades out.] [Cut] View The Annoying Neighbours 's Biography
