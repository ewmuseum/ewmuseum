Crossroads

6 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/06/05 The scene opens in a dark, bleak, shadowy forest somewhere in the New England states. The trees are dead and leafless. Weird shadows appear on the ground. Footsteps can be heard walking through the deadwood forest. A figure appears in the distance. 'Pain...' The figure takes a few steps closer to the camera. 'Suffering...' The figure laughs under it's breath as it edges closer to the camera. 'These are the things dreams are made of.' A long pause as the figure walks toward the camera, edging closer to the line of visibility but keeping itself in the shadows so you still can't see who it is. 'But these are the things that will become your reality Pierce.' The figure steps into the light coming through trees between the shadows and you can see that it is one James von Doom, the current UWF North American Champion. He has a cold expression on his face. He dressed like Johnny Depp in the movie 'Sleepy Hollow.' He looks like he's gone back to the Gothic ages, almost as if something close to him has died. JV: Pierce, we've come to a crossroads. A non-AWC wrestler competing on an AWC show in an actual AWC ring...it's unheard of. James steps out of the shadows and looks directly into the camera. JV: The end of the world is near. (pause) Well, the end of you world and everybody else's. James has a sense of calm about this that is very eerie. He almost seems to have sinister intentions. JV: Oh, in know what you're thinking. I'm just joking around. I bet you think this is all a big joke, huh? What did you think was going to happen? That'd I'd come out here with a Giant Sperm and play Retard Karoake? James is trying to keep from laughing. JV: What did you think? That 'Big' Dick N. Uranus and the Horny Mathematician would come on here and try to win a 'Who Says the Most Sex Jokes in Five Minutes Contest' instead of me being out here in the wilderness? James grows cold and more sinister as his smile quickly fades away. His eyes are blazing red with fury. JV: Well Pierce, you need to wake up and face the facts. We're living in the wild. The wrestling world is our jungle and only the strongest will survive. James stares down at the ground. JV: Did you know that most rookies in this business don't make it past the first year? James then looks back up at the camera. JV: Most of them don't have what it takes to cut it after their rookie year filled with gags, ribs, pranks, countless jobbings, and quick pushes to the top that turn them into egomaniacs. Most of them don't survive the first year... James eyes the camera intently. JV: But we survived it, didn't we Pierce? We, two champions of the wrestling world, survive the bitter cold of our rookie years and have now made it to the top of this industry. But now we both face the toughest tests of our careers... Each other. James starts walking as the camera follows him. The gravel on the ground makes crunching sounds as he walks along. James stops to motion around. JV: Look into the bleakness of our reality. We are the hunted, Pierce. Out here in the wild, we are the hunted. The common mice that live in these parts are hunted by the owls every day just like we are hunted by our challengers at every show we appear at. They are the hungry owls waiting to snatch us up off the ground and eat us for a meal. We are the stepping stones across the river that they are looking to use to springboard their careers to the top of the wrestling world. They can say 'Yeah, I beat James von Doom' or 'Yeah, I beat Pierce Lavelle.' James smiles coyly before he continues walking along the path. Dark clouds start appearing overhead. JV: But they will never know who was the better man...until now. We will clash like two beasts in these very woods. Like the animals, we will fight to be at the top of the food chain here in the AWC. James steps over a fallen tree limb as he continues walking. JV: But, oh yeah. I forgot. I'm only a guest here. I don't even work for your company but yet I've made a bigger impression than anybody you've got here. Oh yeah, these people see them every week but we all know who they're talking about. James von Doom, the guy who's everywhere. The guy who is competing in the PTC's Infinite Gauntlet for the fourth time. The guy who is entered into the UWF Regal Rumble and has the biggest target on his back because both the AWC guys and the GCW guys are after him. The guy who is trying his best to get into GTT5. The guy who has held the UWF North American Championship longer than anybody in history. And the guy who invaded the AWC single-handedly. Everybody is talking about James von Doom and not you or the rest of the pitiful AWC roster. James cackles dementedly. JV: Yes. Let's talk about some of the miscreants around here. Let me start off with the Farmer, some hick from the middle of nowhere who can barely speak English and who you people treat like a Main Eventer. PLEASE. Give me a break. 'Big' Dick N. Uranus has more talent in the tip of his penis than the Farmer has in his entire body. James makes a turn on the path as he walks along, doing his running commentary as the sky darkens above him. JV: Next on the list of deviants is some guy calling himself the Illustrious Face-Eater. James tries hard to contain his laughter but can't. JV: Who the FUCK is this idiot? I mean, some of my ideas are out there but DAMN! Your name is almost right up there with the Demonic Hamster of DOOM~! in terms of stupidity. But the next man on my list is Ellis Nash... James stops and pauses for a long time. JV: Oops, sorry. Thought your first name was Kevin. James smiles devilishly before continuing along the trail through the seemingly uninhabited woods. JV: Next on the list is the super Ewok Crimson O'Malec. Yeah, you've been a target of mine for WEEKS but you and your Irish bum buddy Paddy O'Shea are two of the most dangerous wrestlers in the AWC with just your body odor alone! I mean, COME ON! Don't they have bathhouses or something in Britain? Don't they have somewhere where you two can wipe your arses with toilet paper? You know, now that I think about it, England is a pretty nasty country. They used to shit in the streets there in the Dark Ages. The Queens used to wear fifty pounds of make-up and wash themselves only once a year. Yeah, such a pretty country. Bloodshed runs through your country's tortured history. Scots and Irishmen used to slaughter Britons for fun with swords and arrows. The barbarians took away reading and writing from all but the priests. Your women were raped all the time because they believe the superiority of men. James shakes his head. JV: What a sick bunch of fucks you guys are. William Wallace is a God to the Scotsmen but the fact that he slaughtered the Brits is no consequence to you people. You probably just blame it on the IRA like you do everything else. But here in America, the land of the free, we do things a little bit differently. James smiles deviously as he stops and looks over at an abandoned cabin in the woods and then turns back to the camera. JV: You see, we fought your arses off good back in the 1700's didn't we, mates? Your country once ruled the world but now, all you rule is a couple of islands. Hell, your own country is really like three or four different countries. Oh yeah. I can hear it now. 'That's why they call it the United Kingdom.' James shakes his head and motions toward the cabin. JV: That cabin is where a brutal murder took place and people say it's haunted. James shrugs his shoulders. JV: Gimme a break. That's like saying that the United States is owned by the Queen of England. Say, didn't Lieutenant Frank Dreben of Police Squad hump her old ass when she came to see an Angles game back in '88? James laughs evilly and continues walking along the path. JV: Now, I have a message for all of you AWC guys out there. James stops again and clears his throat. JV: Chacka! Mmmph...gotta get ready for this one. All you limeys'll love this... James then smiles for the camera and starts singing. JV: Oh say can you SEE By the dawn's early light What so proudly we hail James then laughs and continues walking along. JV: Yeah, AWC. America's coming ta kick yo ass BABY~! James then strikes up a triumphant pose. JV: In the form of the UWF led by James FUCKING von Doom BABY~! YEAH~! James claps his hands together and walks along. He starts hearing noises in the woods and looks around. JV: Yeah. I bet you fuckers sent a hitman after me or something. Fucking cowards. Yeah. Mae Young at the 2000 Royal Rumble is scarier than all you motherfuckers combined! James starts trying to act tough. JV: Oh yeah. I heard the 'You Screwed Crimson' chants at the UWF show. Yeah. I screwed him when I invaded just like I screwed your momma's last night! Big Ben was watching me. He didn't like what he saw so your old ass two hundred year old clock stopped. James smiles evilly. JV: Maybe I should go to DOOOOOBLIN and take a dump on Dave's front yard. Then, to get your revenge, you'll send the Rainbow Flag to have gay lesbian sex on my front yard. Yeah, nice revenge. Getting all the hornballs in my neighborhood to come over and watch lesbian sex. Yeah, this war sounds good. James then gets back to what he came to say. JV: So AWC, what is it? Am I going to have come on your show and smash a bunch of eggs on Dave Harber's head in order to get some action around here? Am I going to have to invade again? (growing intense) DON'T ME MAKE ME BRING TAN OUT! DON'T MAKE ME DO IT AGAIN BECAUSE I CAN! DON'T MAKE ME STUPIFY YOUR SHOW LIKE I DID THE LAST TIME WHEN I MANAGED THEM HERE~! James suddenly calms down. JV: Ok. That's fine. Don't take me seriously. But let me tell you this. The ENTIRE UWF is ready to kick your fucking asses...except for Red Rock and Boolie because they're too busy coming on AWC television to suck Pierce Lavelle's dick to care about either group. James nods his head as he walks. JV: Yeah Red Rock. Don't think I forgot about you SELL OUT. You joined the enemy. Now, you must DIE in BOTH feds at my hands. You'd best hope I can restrain myself long enough to let you kiss my feet and beg for forgiveness before I kick your fucking ass. James continues walking until he reaches an unmarked grave. He turns and offers a sick smile for the camera. JV: Lavelle, this is your future. James laughs. JV: This is one of the reasons why I have brought you out here to the middle of nowhere. You see Pierce, this grave is of someone who no one bothered to care about. I mean, look. James motions toward the wooden cross and dirt mound at the side of the gravel road. JV: No name, no remembrance. No one even cared to put a name on the grave of this poor young Confederate soldier. James smiles deviously. JV: Just like no one will care about you anymore Pierce after I demolish you in the center of the ring this week on Fresh! James looks intently at the camera. JV: Pierce, what will you do? Your Chronicles will be nothing more than a vast, empty nothingness forgotten by all and cared for by only the purists in this business who have to keep the history alive. But what will they do when their hero falls to the guy they all love to hate? What will they do when they have no more heroes to cheer for? What will they do when they see that James von Doom is the NEW AWC Transatlantic Champion? James does the belt motion on his waist. JV: You know what will happen? NOTHING. They're all just stand there, blank faced and silent as the FIRST non-plagiarist beats you in the ring. An outsider will then hold two AWC Championships. But what to make of what will happen. What will management do to Pierce Lavelle? James cackles dementedly and motions to the unmarked grave again. JV: They will bury you in an unmarked grave such as the one behind me. And they will forever bury the failure that was Pierce Lavelle under a mound of dirt and an unmarked wooden cross at the end of some winding dirt road in the middle (motions around) of a deserted forest. James waves goodbye to the unmarked grave and starts walking toward the end of the road. JV: It's coming up soon Pierce. The reason I have brought you and all the idiots at home here to the forest. Weird animal noises begin to be heard in the background. James stops, smiles, and then continues on. JV: Nightfall is soon. That is when the creatures of the night come out and have their nocturnal feasts. The local villagers claim that these woods are haunted by demons. James stops and looks evilly into the camera. JV: If they meant me, then they were right. James continues walking along, brushing his hair back as the dirt road begins to curve now. JV: But the end of your title reign isn't the end of the world, Pierce. Think about it. You will have shown the entire AWC your true weakness. The weakness that you have is that you always have to be the hero. James snickers at the very thought. JV: The real truth is, however, that you're a glory hound and have let the adulation of all of these idiot AWC fans get to your head. Pierce, the end of your world is here. However you deal with it, is up to you. James looks forward and spots what he came for. JV: There it is, just up ahead. James continues walking and then stops. He then turns around for the camera to see. There is a fork in the road. The path turns into two. One leads left and the other one leads right. James smiles and motions a hand in each direction. JV: Pierce, as you can see, we are at a crossroads. The question I have for you is this. Which path are you going to take? James motions to the pathway to the left. JV: The road to salvation... James then motions to the pathway to the right. JV: Or the road to hell? James turns back to the camera. JV: It's your choice Pierce. I leave it up to you. The right choice will take you to your destiny. The wrong choice will leave you the bloody, crippled heap that I keep saying I'm going to turn you into. So Pierce, go pull your balls out of your purse, put on your little panties, and get ready to do something that doesn't happen that often now days. ...Wrestle. James smiles and then turns to the path on the right. He starts walking down it while still talking to the camera. JV: I've chosen my path to righteousness. I think it's time that you choose yours... Before reality comes crashing right down on you... James then Super Duper Kicks the camera out of nowhere but the feed is still coming through albeit static filled and snowy. James shoes are rigth in front of the camera. He then turns and walks down the right pathway as the scene fades out. Cut. End Scene.
