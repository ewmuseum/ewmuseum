Into The Night I Go...

15 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th September 2006 Wiping the fresh blood from my knuckles I look down at the ground with a snarl to my lips. He’d played me for a fool and that was one thing that nobody did to me. Not even when I got something from him in return. NOBODY played me for a fool and got away with it. Faint coughing rang out in the dark damp parking bay. Life would never be the same now. He’d ruined what could have been and what they could have been. Now all that would happen would darkness. Revenge. Turning round I drag my feet closer to him once again. Hearing the sound of liquid being broken by my boot I cringe. Knowing all to well that it is not rain which my foot has just landed in but him. His blood. I told him. I told him not to play me for a fool but he didn’t listen… Leah Adams: You just didn’t listen to me…why?? then this wouldn’t have had to happen… The groans grow louder as I step closer to him once again. If he’d just listened to me I wouldn’t have had to do this. We could have got on with our lives but he tried to make me out to be something I am not. He tried to change me into something I am not. And now he must pay. He must pay for everything he has ever done wrong within life. For every wrong move he has taken he must pay. Crunch! In goes my hard boot cladded foot to his ribs once more. The scream of pain that escapes his lips is like nothing I have ever heard. He knows its over so why not just give in? I look down on him and I see…I see in his eyes the light fading as the life escapes him. For one split second our eyes connect. He sees into me for who I am. Ironic really the first time he sees who I really am is the moment before I take the very life from his body. Slipping my hand around the cool handle I pull the gun from my belt. The faint light from the fire exit light catches the glistening clean metallic barrel. His eyes widen. Yes that’s what I want to see. I want to see your fear. Your vulnerability. You made me feel so small so pathetic. Well look at you now. Lying in your own blood. Your eyes begging me to quit with fear so fierce it could rip anyone’s hearts right from them. But not me. Not I. Leah Adams: All you had to do was love me…treat me like something more than an expense account…all you had to do was treat me as what you created… Turning my head from him I click the safety cap back. The click rings out into the night like nothing else. Then with one swift movement I place my finger on the trigger… Leah Adams: All you had to do dad was love me like any father should love their daughter… The ear shattering bang fills the air. His lifeless body lay at my feet. Choking back vomit I throw the gun at the floor. Wiping my glove cladded hands against my thighs I slip into the darkness leaving him. Leaving him alone in the cold night like he had to me so many times. As I step further into the night I pull out my phone. Covering the mouthpiece with cloth I speak… Leah Adams: I would like to report a shooting…park central car park…forth floor…yes…hurry… Hanging up I slip away. The night eats me into itself. The darkness welcoming me as another companion. Finding comfort in the coldness that surrounds me I wait. My eyes burning as I stare and wait. The noise of sirens fill my ears as they come screeching in. Voice: Quick…get the paramedics…sir hold on you’ll be fine…Shit Calum he’s in a right mess can you find a pulse? Voice 2: No Dave I can’t…hold on…bleep…Josh were are you? Voice 3: I’m here…what we got? Holy shit…clear me some space…IAN get here now! Watching the four men busy themselves with my father I know it’s a hopeless case. He’s gone I can feel it in my bones… Josh: Sorry man but he’s gone…whoever did this had no intention of leavin the guy a hope in hell of livin… Dave: Fuck…you any idea who he is Josh…you find any ID? Josh: I don’t need to find ID I know who this man is…he’s Paul Adams…he’s worth millions in the publishing world…he’s one of the biggest names in the industry worth millions. What I don’t get is why anyone would want to kill him…and where’s that person who phoned in the shooting…surely you hang around… Slipping silently through the door I look back once more as the four men busy themselves with the clear up and the new unsolved murder case. The door clicked behind me as I slip out into the cool night air. The lights twinkling around me as I take a deep breath of the damp yet some what crisp air into my lungs. It was done. He was gone. And best of all nobody knew. Nobody but myself and the now cold body that lay on the concrete floor with blood pouring from his body. And lets face it he was hardly going to be bragging to the local cops about the mysterious killer. I can just see the headlines tomorrow “Worlds greatest literature publicist shot dead in brutal attack last night. Witnesses are encouraged to step forward. Our hearts go out to all his family and friends and especially his daughter Leah Adams. The world has lost one of it’s greatest but why?” WHY?? That’s what they will all ask…why was he killed? Why would someone do such a thing to such a kind man? Why this? Why that? You want to know why cause he was a sick, twisted, manipulating, back stabbing wanker and he had not right…NO RIGHT to breath the air from this very earth… Leah Adams: You deserved to die…you had to pay for all you did to me for all that you took from me… Whispering to only myself and the breeze that blew through the softly humming midnight street I confirm that my actions although violent were totally necessary. See if you’d asked me two months ago if I thought I would have just put a bullet to my fathers heart I would have laughed in your face. But how times change. How people change. How he changed. Dragging my blood stained boots through the murky puddles I lower my head in a moment of silence for the man who helped create me. Looking back up a sly grin spread across my lips, playing gently with the corners of my mouth. A lust of power burnt within me. A desire…a hunger so great for all that is dark glinted deep within my burning eyes. Yes the time had come. The time had come for Leah Adams to be unleashed onto the world. Just like my father hadn’t four hours ago…AWC does not know what has hit them. All I can say is let the fun and games begin. Disappearing into the dark as the night wrapped itself tightly around my body I let out a snigger of delight as to what was to come.
