Telephone Conversations

29 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/8 A phone rings. Rings again. Rings a third time. Someone picks up, but only silence can be heard. Angelica: *sounding distressed* Hate, are you there? Silence. Angelica: Please, Hate, answer me. I know you're there. Answer me, please. I have something I need to tell you. Silence. Angela can be heard breathing deeply, as if getting more anxious by the minute. Angelica: Hate... I know you are there. Answer me, PLEASE! It's about Aza... Hate: My Angel, you should not tell me what you know when you only have a hunch. If you do not know where I am, do not go blindly running about crying my name like the proverbial boy for the wolf. Angelica: Oh! Hate... I'm... I'm sorry. I... I just... Hate: Well, my Angel, it can't be helped now, can it? Be brief, Angel. I do not have all the time in the world. The cause won't rest for a single man. Angelica: I... I need to talk to you about Azagtoth. It's important. Hate: Yes, my Angel. I cannot help but notice the bad blood between you and Azagtoth. And I see you both trying to overshadow the other in an effort to please me. In a way, it is amusing, like children racing against each other in front of their parents. But it is also very, very tedious. Azagtoth with his enthusiastic self, misinterpreting my orders in his zeal and not looking at the big picture. You, you and your... I am not above the vices of the flesh. I even enjoy them, from time to time. But you seem to be so far below the flesh that it is the only thing you know and can see. Angelica: I... Hate: Your flesh does tempt me, every once in a while. But not every night. I cannot go to sleep without seeing you, spreading yourself like you expected me to be interested. It is not the only way to please me. Angelica: ... I... I'm sorry. I didn't... Hate: Of course not, my child. But no matter. What of Azagtoth? Angelica's voice picks up in speed and pitch as she starts explaining. Angelica: I found his bags when we were in London. I... I looked in them. And I found something... Hate: Yes? Angelica: Something... Something awful. Hate: ... Angelica: Like... Memories. No, that's not the word... MEMENTOES. Sick little mementoes... Like, severed, and... Bloody and... With things sticking out and... Hate: Calm down, my Angel. You are not making sense. Angelica: It was awful! All those things, arranged and... And numbered! Like in a shop! Or a museum! And they had... Marks on them, like he had... Oh my God... Hate: My Angel... Angelica: Oh my God! He's here! Hate: Yes...? Angelica: I can hear him! He KNOWS I'm here! He KNOWS I looked into the bag! He... He's going to hurt me, Hate! Stop him! Make him stop! Hate: Azagtoth? No, he wouldn't... Angelica: Yes! He's out there... In the next room... OhmyGod he's coming here! He's coming! Angelica starts to weep slowly and some rustling can be heard. Hate speaks in a low, calm tone, apparently not fully graspin what's happening at the other end of the line. Hate: Look... My Angel, Azagtoth would not... Angelica: He's here! HE'S HERE! STAY AWAY STAY!.... The voice suddenly cuts out. Hate's voice doesn't change at all, even though Angelica practically screamed a moment ago. Hate: Azagtoth's little trinkets are not for us. They are his and his alone. And he would never harm you. He does not have the guts. Click.
