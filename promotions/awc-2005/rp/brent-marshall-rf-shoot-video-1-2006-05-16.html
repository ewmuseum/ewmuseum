Brent Marshall RF Shoot Video #1

16 May 2006

Date: 05/16/06 ~~~The following is part one of an RF Video Inc. interview featuring 'Mr.' Brent Marshall of the Atlantic Wrestling Club. RF Video Inc. is not responsible for the content or views expressed by Brent Marshall during the interview.~~~ Interviewer: 'We're happy to have with us today Brent Marshall, who began wrestling for the Atlantic Wrestling Club in January of this year under the moniker 'Mr. Marshall.' Brent, you enjoyed a highly-successful amateur wrestling career at the University of Iowa and only just recently entered the world of professional wrestling, what has been the biggest single adjustment you've had to make?' Marshall: 'At the time of this interview my AWC record is 1-4 and you want me to single out one particular adjustment? Well first of all, let that be the last time you refer to this occupation as 'professional wrestling,' because real wrestling has very little to do with it. Let me apologize in advance if any AWC fans find my comments confusing, because they're so used to getting the cookie-cutter, nice guy rhetoric of 'Mr.' Marshall that they might not even recognize the man sitting before them today. That character is a joke, the product of a lazy writing staff with no fucking knowledge of their fanbase. You really think fans are going to get behind a former school teacher who grounds and pounds his opponents? Of course not. They first brought me into the AWC as a wrestling coach to the inexperienced tag team Norton and Bridges, which is about as close to reality as you're ever going to find around here. Those two schmucks couldn't wrestle their way out of a pair of paper bags, but instead of actually letting me train these guys, upper management decides that their inexperience will garner sympathy from the fans and help them get over. What a fucking joke. So at that point I knew that the AWC was more interested in building cartoon gimmicks and eye candy then actually promoting a wrestling product. But I decided to stick it out, hoping that the fans would be able to look past the failures of the writing department and instead focus on my natural abilities. Well when you grow up wrestling in front of knowledgable fans, who recognize the beauty of a switch or a single-leg takedown, you forget just how ignorant professional wrestling fans really are. I never had a fucking chance to get over, the only thing they've ever popped for is when I slammed a chair over AgentDash's head. A chair. A fucking folding chair. So that's what it's come to, when one of the most accomplished amateur wrestlers of the last twenty-five years can't get anywhere near the same reaction as an inantimate object. Absolutely pathetic. ~~~This concludes part one of the RF Video Inc. shoot interview with Brent Marshall. Look for excerpts from part two to be released within the next few weeks.~~~
