Deceptive Times

28 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SUMMARY OF PREVIOUS TWO ROLEPLAYS – IF HAVEN’T READ THEM. • The return roleplay – Fallen Whispers Chapter One – was about Pierce’s appeal to get the drug ban revoked. With the help of his lawyer, Lois Wood and FBI agent Tony Rush, they managed to get a corrupt cop Ramsfield to confess in court. • The Prometheus organization was run by Gustav and Fine, however, a mutiny began in which Vladimir and Michael Vaughn took over killing Gustav and Fine and mentioning things would change. • A court scene in which Lois questions Ramsfield, until he admits that he forged evidence against Pierce, along with Adam Dick’s affidavit – clearing Pierce of intentional use of the Prometheus drug and also Kelli Stoner’s letter of confession. • In “Fallen Whispers Chapter Two” – Pierce was charged ‘not guilty’ by the court and the drug ban was revoked. • Vladimir and Vaughn unhappy with this and their need to re-establish their narcotic line, ordered a hitman to take out two people – innocent bystanders are expendable. • Pierce goes to visit his cousin – Vinnie Marino Lavelle ( A corrupt bonds agent with a lot of criminal contacts). Through Vinnie, Pierce learns about Vladimir Ulrich and goes to La Vie – a restaurant downtown where Vladimir does some of his business. • Pierce arrives at La Vie and talks to Vladimir – asking him to leave him alone in return for his silence – Vladimir mocks Pierce, has him thrown out as well as threatening his family. • Tony Rush takes Ramsfield downtown on the freeway – they get stopped by a white van in which opens fire, killing Ramsfield. A suspense scene in which the motor fluid connects with a flame. • Pierce goes home, where and he and Lois share a beer. They talk. She leaves and when she goes the hitman emerges shooting her in the stomach, Pierce tries to save her. • She is in a coma, unresponsive. He leaves after being dismissed by her family and heads to Europe. THIS IS NOW THE CONTINUATION…. Title: “Deceptive Times” Show: Fresh Special Live On Huntington Beach The Baltschug-Kempinski Hotel, Russia One week ago The chill of night crept unwelcome against the cooled marble slabs beneath his bare feet; his blood shot eyes burning onto the small plastic container in his hand, a medical brand along the side. The moon’s glow would glisten every so often along the floor in a small streak and illuminate him briefly between the tossing linen curtains, fluttering in the wind. The prescription pills rattled in the small plastic container, his eyes wandering through a hazy mist of sweat trickling from his forehead down his eyes, nose and chin. Dependency on a medicine that would only prolong the withdrawals, he knew, but found it simple to take a pill and the shakes would end. It was his way of solving the Prometheus drug use, without proper rehabilitation for the injection of the drug, he was now left dependant on pills. ‘Methadone’ the small pharmaceutical label read. Coughing with a pain in his throat, he gripped a small glass of water and took a sip, opening the container he knocked two red pills into his hand, it shook uncontrollably, the pills dropping to the floor. ‘Damn it…’ Pierce yelled, holding his shaking hand he curled up in a ball on the bed, his breathing wheezy. Sweat trickling from every orifice of his body, his hand holding the one that shook as his vision blurred. Hallucinations, a recurring vision, a searing pain, names and faces all drifting through the moons glow, dancing along the white washed ceiling. ‘Agh…’ He cried in pain, a migraine gripping his last shreds of patience, he wrapped his hand around the container of pills, one remained. He knocked it back and wandered with a hovel into the bathroom collapsing next to the bath, heaving his breaths into the fan above. A worn and helpless face stared back at him in the glass sink - blood-shot eyes, pale skin, stubble and scars. His stomach was in knots; words and whispers surrounding his body. His sweaty hand gripped the towel rack as he heaved with a pain into the bath tub, watching the little he had eaten wash down the plug hole. ‘C’mon son, cough it up. You were never good enough at anything…’ His father’s maniacal voice now filled his thoughts, his face was in front of his blurred eyes, his mocking smile and his blue eyes penetrating through his shell crushing him once again into a feeble state. Moaning in pain and holding his hand, he curled against the cold floor next to the bath. ‘Alleviate symptoms! Bullshit! You crave it! Go and get it…’ A voice crept deeply into his thoughts, a familiar and resounding voice, bringing pain, heart ache and distrust. Do it! Take the damn drugs! Didn’t stop you before…Quench the thirst… The man’s betrayal and devious plans still lay within Pierce, but vengeance became a fight of his morals. You’ll never get rid of me! Feed the hunger! I made you damn it! Take the god damn drugs!!!! ‘NO…’ Adam Dick was now a fading face, mocking him, taunting him. He squeezed his eyes shut, the shaking stopping, the medicine working, his migraine pounding but his vision clear. The opaque droplets of sweat, glistening against his skin as he rose under the light, his legs buckling as his frayed nerves controlled his motions. He stood on the slippery tiles, drenched in his sweat and breathed slowly to quell his racing heart, the voices’ dissipated as quickly as they had come. Staring now in the mirror, teary eyed, muscles clenched up with pain, he could no longer bear to look at himself. Clenching his fist he shattered it against the mirror, watching the vision obscure into tiny fractures, till he faded with the shattered glass… Los Angeles, Downtown, Route 110 The scattered rays of sunshine glistened through the gaping holes within the Sedan. A howl of sirens courses through the stilled atmosphere. A group of people stand, masked expressions on their faces. News crews are backed behind crime scene tape as the local LAPD begin to herd in the neurotic FBI. ‘Once again the streets of Los Angeles are littered with more gang land violence, are we really safe?’ The officer sniggered at the reporters comments and turned his attention to the arrival of more FBI agents. A small medical team begin to unload stretchers, two bodies, unjustly taken in a split second. ‘Mr. Ramirez,’ a voice called from behind a black van. Ramirez strode with a saddened expression to his grey Gucci suit and black shirt. His green eyes blood-shot, bags forming below his wrinkle lines.
