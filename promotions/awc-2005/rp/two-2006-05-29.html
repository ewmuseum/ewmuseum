Two

29 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29, 2006 It’s a soft and quiet morning. Birds are chirping. The sun is shining. The stake out guys are sleeping... Wait, what? It’s morning. The two goons in the car are still in there, though they’re kind of asleep. Which is a bad thing if they’re supposed to be watching the hotel, isn’t it? Lucky for them, though, one of them starts to stir and wake up. “Nnngh...” Blinking a bit as he realizes that he was asleep, he sits up quickly and looks around, severely hoping he hasn’t missed anything crucial. That’s when he grabs the binoculars and looks off at the hotel entrance door. “Oh shit...” * * * * * * * * * * * * LXG is standing in front of the hotel, strapping his duffle bag to himself and getting it twisted around his body really quickly. He clambers up onto a bicycle (something he’s purchased with an advance from Sasha, no doubt) and gets all nice and comfortable. He’s taking his sweet time, though, like he doesn’t have a care in the world. Being the type of guy he is, though, he probably doesn’t. It’s strange, though, because as soon as he’s comfortable on the bike, he looks of in the direction of his shadows. He practically looks dead in their car and offers them a holier than thou smile, before pushing off down the street, pedaling softly. * * * * * * * * * * * * SMACK! “Wake up!” the one guy shouts, tossing the binoculars behind him and starting up the car. “He’s on the move!” His partner grumbles and starts to sit up, rubbing at his eyes. “Who huh wha?” “One.” Seatbelts are grabbed and clicked into place, ever so fast! But, when the car starts trying to back up... it’s not moving as well as it should. Something’s wrong. “The hell?” Shifting the gear again and putting the car in park, the driver swings open his door and looks out. “Son of a bitch!” “What?” The passenger flings his door open and looks out too. “... mother fucker...” The tires are ever so flat. Ever so. Looks like LXG’s one step ahead. Driver grabs his radio and calls it in, “He’s on to us. We’re out of commission. One is on the move. I repeat, One is on the move. Get his punk ass.” * * * * * * * * * * * * LXG’s calm pedaling may be a bit too calm, considering what just happened. He rides himself up to the street corner, looking over his shoulder and smiling. He’s just too good. Way too good. OR IS HE?! The light doesn’t even get a chance to turn green, because swerving around the corner is the most obvious of obvious black vans. And its headed straight to block up LXG’s path. Coming from the other side? A smaller vehicle, nonetheless attempting to assist with the boxing in of the young wrestler in. LXG blinks, realizing what’s going on. He can tell shadow cars when he sees them. He’s been being followed ever since he left home. That’s one reason why he came overseas. Hoping he could get himself as far away from Meredith as possible. Can’t be putting her family in danger, just because he doesn’t know who he is... He backpedals quickly, braking and spinning the bike around on the sidewalk. A quick weave between people and he’s hopping off the curb and into the street... and into traffic. This causes the cars to have to back up and K turn to give chase... which they do. LXG’s legs work overtime as he tries to put some distance between himself and the cars. “Okay, Lincoln. Let’s go with B...” he mutters to himself, looking over his shoulder at the incoming vehicles of evilness. Soon as he passes the hotel again, he slams on his brakes and cuts down into an alley on the side of the hotel. It’s much too small for either of the cars to fit through, but they don’t even try. They take the corner with ease and floor it... coming around the block in record time. By the time they show up on that block, LXG’s popping out of the alley and pedaling for his life down the street. Both vehicles are weaving in and out of traffic, but with less ease than LXG and his bike. But they’re closing in fast, thanks to being in CARS. LXG may be a genius, but super powers he has not. He does, however, have an escape route that he’s working to the best of his ability... “There’s the chapel...” he whispers to himself, as he passes by a church. Landmarks are helping him out here, it looks like. About ten feet from the chapel, is a construction site for some priceless building that’s being restored. He looks over his shoulder and frowns, “Come on... keep up.” He’s got to get the timing on this right... * * * * * * * * * * * * “He’s heading inside!” “I got him, I got him!” “We’ll go around! Cut him off!” “Roger that, we’ll stay on his ass.” Don’t you just love radio chatter between military goons? * * * * * * * * * * * * LXG speeds into the site, gaining looks and yells from the workers. The sign of the sit is knocked into the air when the car comes in behind him, not less than 20 seconds after! Of course, now the workers and such are seriously furious. Which is what LXG’s counting on. He swerves and cuts past a couple of people on break, The tires of his bike lift up and he plants them down on a board, balancing perfectly as he rides across the top of a wet cement area. He comes off the board on the other side, turns his bike and skids to a halt, looking back to watch the show. The car’s speed doesn’t allow it to slowdown in time, even though the driver notices what’s going on. It hits the edge, the front of the vehicle going up, before it smashes into the cement, spinning tires digging it into the hardening stuff further and splattering it all over the place. The doors on the car open and out falls those inside. Though, they’re quickly approached and surrounded by a collection of pissed off hardhats. LXG just smiles and speeds off on his bike again. “One down...” * * * * * * * * * * * * When LXG comes out of the restoration site, the black van is waiting for him. Tires screech like crazy and LXG has to pedal hard to not get run over. He swerves immediately, cutting into a park and taking the sidewalk there. The sidewalk that runs side by side with the street... which is just how he wants it. It seems that the black van and LXG are racing or something, considering the way they keep pace with each other. Both of them have to swerve constantly... LXG having to do it to people and joggers, while the van driver tends to be stuck dodging cars. Both of them come to a screeching halt, though, at a small bridge. What happens next is either a really good or a really bad idea... but either way, never try this at home kids. Ever. LXG looks at his watch and then over the side of the bridge. The van driver revs his engine in anticipation. They lock eyes with each other for a moment... but by the end of that moment, the chicken race is on. LXG pedals furiously.... The van driver floors it... Sirens wail in the near distance... They get closer... Closer... Closer... SMASH! LXG’s bike is totally wrecked by the van! But that’s okay, because LXG’s even on the bike anymore... * * * * * * * * * * * * The van driver growls and stares out the window, eyes on LXG, “SON OF A BITCH!” * * * * * * * * * * * * LXG’s fall is a quick one and when he smacks into the bales of hay on the back of a parked truck, he winces. It still hurts, but at least he’s not dead or anything. Grunting, he rolls off the back of the truck and stumbles off down the street... holding his side. He landed wrong. * * * * * * * * * * * * The van driver, though, has stupidly kept his foot on the gas, while watching LXG’s escape. Thus, it comes as no surprise at all that he smacks right into the cars of authority that have swarmed onto the bridge. CRASH! “... oh shit.” * * * * * * * * * * * * With the airport shuttle rolling up, right on time, LXG smiles and drags himself onto it. He heads to the back, falling into a seat and finally getting a chance to catch his breath. * * * * * * * * * * * * Crisis averted. Barely, but averted. Whatever they want from me, it must be seriously important. They’re coming on stronger now. I really, really, wish I knew what it was. Somebody's going to get hurt... I’ll worry about it later, though. I’ve got a plane to catch. Fresh! Here I come.
