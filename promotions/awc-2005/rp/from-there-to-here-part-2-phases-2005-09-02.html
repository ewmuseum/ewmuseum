From There To Here Part 2 (Phases)

2 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The baby was coming. 3 months early and the baby was coming, this wasn’t a stomach upset, these were contractions, but something else was wrong, the intense burning in her stomach told her that. She’d called for an ambulance, but now the pain was so intense she had no idea how she’d even get to the vehicle, every movement led to pain shooting all the way through her body, every single inch of her body burned. Falling backwards onto the bed she let out an ear-piercing scream. Reminiscent of the scene from Alien she clasps her stomach, the pain is immense, she can feel herself blacking out, as her eyes slowly begin to flicker, losing consciousness and awareness of the world around her. She manages to murmur a single word. “John…” With that her eyes roll backwards as she lies on the bed. An escape from the pain. A few moments later the paramedics rushed into the flat and saw Louise unconscious on the bed, what they noticed first though was the blood that had begun to seep through her trousers from her crotch. “We need to get her to hospital NOW” ~~~ John Edwards hurt, every single muscle in his tired and broken body hurt as though a rhino was sat atop him. It had been nearly a week since he and Karina Wolfenden had bought the house down. It had been over a week since he had lost his Omega Championship and he had to admit, it hurt. A lot of things had been hurting John lately. The fact that his jaw was now permanently two inches to the right, due to the boots of the Negasonic Lupine. His already fragile neck now creaked with every move. That was when it didn’t hurt so badly that he could move it. John’s path was being laid before him; everyone who knew his history could see it. Three months to a year and he would be done. Sure comebacks were possible; Dave Gibson was testament to that. A man whose neck was so brittle that just one botched move could mean death. John had been warned about Wolfenden, she was electric in the ring, bouncing around like a rubber ball, but as every warning had indicated. She was reckless, blows which otherwise would simply glance careened into his face. For Wolfenden it was just about getting her rush and John hoped that she got it last week. His loss of the Omega Championship had hurt him, because he had been made to look like a fool. Only a week prior to that he had spent a considerable amount of time, telling everyone exactly how he would bring prestige to the title, how he would defend it every single week, to make it a title of worth, that the naysayers would eventually take note of the title and stop their snide comments about it. Comparisons had been made between him and Emilio Rage, a man who had gone on to win the PTC Global Title, something, which wasn’t a walk in the park. Maybe it was the pressure, but he just couldn’t do it, no matter what he did, he could not put Karina Wolfenden away and he hated himself for that. Although deep down he knew that there was no shame losing to Wolfenden, hell only three people had beaten her in PRIME, John couldn’t get over the fact that he had given it his all and it wasn’t good enough. This of course led to the natural train of thought which was associated with any loss. Am I good enough? Should I really be here? Would anyone really miss me if I left? Generally the self-imposed view to any of those questions, is no. However in the aftermath of the match he had heard something that he hadn’t heard in a long time, not truly. A positive reaction from the crowd. He had been an asshole, he had taken the fight to the fans precious Karina Wolfenden, yet as they shook hands in the center of the ring, he felt the respect of the crowd. That had turned his opinion right on its head. The cheers indicated that he could still get a reaction, he could still draw a cheer and as long as he could do that he knew he would have to give it his all in the ring. Which was a comforting thought as he lay in bed, the pain shooting through his body so sharply, that the urge to tear off his arm and beat himself to death was quickly becoming a prominent decision in the forefront of his mind. Mind you the pain would probably have subsided somewhat, if he had used his painkillers. He wasn’t a stupid man, well that was up for debate, however, painkillers and alcohol didn’t mix and the sweet sweet liquor was something that John could not pull himself from. He now knew that he had a problem, his painkillers had always been a lifesaver for him, some had accused him of being addicted to those, on some level he probably was. It was his addictive personality, gambling, drinking, drugs they all had that appeal of being wrong for you, but so right at the same time. Empty bottles of various vodka were strewn throughout John’s bedroom. The flickering television in front of his displaying images of samurai swords for sale on some god awful shopping channel. Lying unconscious on the bed was John Edwards, the double bed seemingly a lot larger without Louise to share it. ~~~ John was walking through his house. Everything around him seemed distant and blurred, as though this wasn’t real somehow. The world around him twisted and turned as he continued his journey, eventually reaching the kitchen. Not a sound could be heard within the room, not even the dull hum that often accompanies electricity, just the blissful silence that becomes to feel uneasy. You can feel your heart beat quicken as your surroundings and awareness become heightened. You know that something isn’t right here. Stepping further into the kitchen, towards the stove in the far corner of the room is a task, with every step it feels as though the floor itself is moving, slipping and disintegrating under foot. Upon reaching the stove he turns on the gas, igniting it with a soft blue flame which flickers and dances, despite there being no wind. It was at that point you know that someone is behind you, or something at least. Yet the fear stops him from turning around. When he entered the room he was alone, yet now he can feel the presence of someone else, directly behind him. Too scared to turn around, for fear of what he may see. A deep breath blows lightly against John’s neck, not sinister, just enough to let him know that it’s there, whatever it maybe. That he is not alone. The flame on the stove had blown out of its own accord; no amount of trying will put it back on. He can now feel the beads of sweat forming on his forehead, dripping independently onto the floor, which has taken on a substance like jelly. This was the part of the dream he hated the most, but it always ended here, all he had to do was turn around and it would be over. But the face he saw would always stay with him, even when he woke up, for a few brief moments it would stay in his eyes. He allowed himself to turn around, to face what he had faced countless times before. His brother staring back at him, his face melting away as he screamed in agony, till there was nothing left but scar tissue and bone, flames licking wildly out of the now empty eye-sockets. Instead however he was faced with a small figure crawling towards him. He allowed his eyes to gaze upon this creature, its legs and arms seemingly moving independently of his own body. It was the naked body of a child, is head looking towards the ground, its eyes fixed a few feet in front of it, the effort clear on its face, with every movement, every inch forwards, until it stops dead in its tracks and looks up at John. Cold lifeless eyes. Slowly its lips part, a row of gum, no teeth. “Daddy” ~~~ John bolted upwards, his eyes blazing with fire, quickly though he collapses back into the bed, pain surging through him, causing him to screw his face up tightly, his body felt as though he was on fire. Palming around the bed his hand found a bottle of vodka, twisting off the top he took a large swig, the clear liquid burning down his throat as it made it’s way to his liver. With gritted teeth he waited as slowly the pain wore off, allowing him to sigh silently, clutching his neck. The nightmares were daily now, which really didn’t help with a sore neck, well sore was an understatement, but that’s not the point. The nightmares wouldn’t shift and there was nothing that he could do, to change that. Herbalistic medicines had not worked and neither had drinking himself into unconsciousness. The dreams were now becoming more and more real, if he was walking down the street, he would imagine seeing the baby in the reflection of a shop window. Or when he was alone he would hear the shrill lifeless words “Daddy” echoing in his ear, only to turn around and for there to be nothing there. It was driving him insane. Tentatively he rolled over in his bed, his ribs rolling over the edge of a bottle of Jack Daniels as he did so, his eyes fell upon the clock, the neon red lights indicating it was mid-afternoon. He couldn’t remember falling asleep, he couldn’t remember at what time he had fallen asleep, or passed out whichever it was last night. Though to be frightfully honest, he didn’t care. The assignments and travel details for Revolution 73 had been faxed through to John a few days before hand; an absolutely fun filled trek to North Dakota was on the cards this week. The traveling was always fun, hour-long flights or drives, surrounded by people that didn’t know him, people that judged him, what was wrong with drinking a bottle of vodka on an hour-long flight? After putting on a show of a lifetime at ReV 72 he had not been granted his re-match, far from it. He was back in the position he knew so well, languishing around mid-card, the sort of area when the crowd had been pumped up, had a few drinks in them and contemplating going to the toilet, for fear of missing something better later on. Paul Cain and John Edwards, it didn’t exactly have the firepower of say, Ellis Easton vs. Dave Gibson. In PRIME’s defense though, they had tried to spice it up though. A pick your poison match, essentially meaning it was a restricted hardcore bout. As little as a year ago, John under the name Moraless, would have thrived in a bout like that, however after a match with Wolfenden the week before, a whole number of conspiracy theories began to shoot around John’s head. They want me out! They’re trying to hurt me, injure me so they can give me one big pay off. Oh dear god they’re trying to kill me! It’s amazing what constant alcohol abuse will do to the brain. Still it wasn’t a major issue, John had defeated Paul Cain before, a man who was once great in NWC, but had as of yet, struggled to find his footing in PRIME, one thing that John knew he had to be wary of was his Singapore Cane, having already been on the receiving end of it at Colossus II. Cain was a dangerous man, but he seemed far too preoccupied with other people, his unhealthy obsession with the dark age, which it should have been pointed out to him, was long gone. Cain was a man who was set in his ways and his rants. Everything was black and white to him, boys should not lose to girls etc. Cain had made it known that John should be disgraced for losing to Wolfenden, clearly choosing to ignore everything that she had achieved in her life. That’s just the way some people worked, if it made him feel better, all the better for him. The mindless ponderings of John are swiftly disrupted as his mobile phone begins to play an irritating buzzing tune, inches away from his head. His phone had been off for quite a long time. As he pulled the phone up towards his eyes, looking onto the caller display he saw who was calling him and paused silently. ’Louise Mobile’ Flashed constantly in front of his mobile phone, he was overwhelmed with emotion. Joy, exasperation and happiness suffused his very soul. He had waited for this for so long, it seemed silly but in John’s head he could see everything falling back together, a family, a second chance. “Louise hi…” John began as he clicked the phone onto speakerphone, too tired to raise the phone up himself. “It’s our baby” Louise spoke…her words were lifeless and cold, suddenly John’s happiness and joy turned into fears and nightmares, suddenly everything began to make sense. He eyes close as he shook his head, mumbling no over and over again to himself. “I..I’ve had a miscarriage.”
