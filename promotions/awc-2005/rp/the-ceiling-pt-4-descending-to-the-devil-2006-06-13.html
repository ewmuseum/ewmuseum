The Ceiling, pt. 4: Descending to the Devil

13 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/13/06 So there it was. In the hands of Liam and Tim Martin was the son of S-Laughter, who was the recently slaughtered black angel. They held him above their heads and stared, not to a point of glaring but rather closer to the path of admiration. This little child, this soft, beacon of hope for the other race was now theirs. And they kept him in their hands. The tears began to collect in their eyes as they stared at this child who was oblivious of his true standings. This little boy, he was the key. But the key to what? Heaven? Hell? Peace? Destruction? “Look at him.” Liam said admiringly, staring up at the child he held onto. “You fucking looking at him?” And the silence was the only confirmation that was needed as the two men, these two killers and heartless mother fuckers, were captured by the innocence and beauty of this child. This baby was able to take away all the fury and hate that these men have amassed for a being of his color. And now they could only admire. And the continuation of this staring, almost magnetically on the child, ceased to continue with a loud bang. It wasn’t what got them the child, though, as no shells were spilt; just a simple knock on the door. ~~~ *BOM! BOM! BOM!* “Who is it?” “It’s Joe.” Shit. Fuck. The child. Have to hide it. Joe, this fucker, he isn’t any sort of specimen, but all it takes is talking. He tells one man, that man tells another, and before you know it, the entire Nest is in front of our apartment and all three of us are hanging from our necks. Shit. Fuck. Tim! What? Take the child! Go! Sit in the bathroom, don’t say a fucking word, and if Joe asks or comes to the door, you’re spilling your guts out of your ass! Liam…why are you worried about Joe? Just go! And so he goes, running into the bathroom (well, more like a quick scurry) with baby in hand, door slamming behind him. And here I am, attending to the door of the same type of importance. I walk over and pull it open, just slightly, but slightly more than enough to peer through frame and door to see Joe standing there, wanting to hear the news. Hey there, Joe. “Liam, I hurd shome crazy things!” For a bit there was silence, but then I let the door creak open a little more. Joe pushed his way in and stood between door and door frame, looking up at me. He wanted a slight bit of silence; having someone killed is secret shit, y’know. Joe, I said, it was fucking crazy. You shoulda— “Did you get him?” Yeah. I got him. He and his wife. Don’t worry about your place anymore, buddy. “Just him and the wife?” And there I paused. Did I miss someone? Joe couldn’t have known about a child; I mean, fuck, it was a damn child. Maybe there was a cousin or a brother. I can understand that, those fuckers can come back to murder you in your fucking sleep. But…it couldn’t… “You didn’t see the child?” And right there I crack. I fucking went. Who the fuck…? What the fuck…? This man, no, this fucking asshole, wanted me to just murder that innocent thing? I saw him, Joe, but I didn’t do nothing. “What?!” *CRACK!* The stiff right I threw into Joe’s face dropped him between the door and the door frame, sliding back out into the hallway. I pulled him back into the apartment by his feet and got on top of him, cracking him a couple more times. What the fuck do you mean by ‘What?!’, Joe? What the fuck is that? You wanted me to murder the fucking infant? To kill it? YOU WANTED ME TO KILL A FUCKING INFANT?! “Yes! I did! I wanted the whole family done!” For what, Joe? What was the reason? You wanted death of the innocent because some asshole father of his destroyed your store? “Yes!” And I saw the demon imposed on this human being grow, the fire burning behind the pupils. His hatred was immense, and in the reflection I saw me. But it wasn’t just me, but Tim was there, too. And his hand…there it was on my shoulder, staring into this Demon. “You see?” He mouths, but no sounds coming out. “You see?” ~~~ I heard the scurrying and the yelling outside of the bathroom, but finally I had this moment. I’ve just gone from suicide to father in a matter of seconds, and now I needed this time to stare at my new child. This new…love of mine, here with me. As I sat on the toilet, I held him in front of me, legs dangling and arms flailing as he tried to discover this new world he was a part of. He was far away from where he came from, and now he was in the Nest, the one place he would’ve never imagine to be, that is if he could understand the importance of it. Like the ceiling, I stared into him. I lost myself in his potential, and I imagine the worlds he would experience, the places he would bring me and Liam. Slowly I drifted off into the imaginary world that I’ve been so familiar with… ~~~ We sat in a park, but the park was not Boston, and it was not Watertown, and it was not Everett, and it was not Chelsea, and it was none of those places in the general Boston area. Rather it was a sunny state, a place where the weather is always beautiful and everything is just fucking peachy and all that fucking jazz. In this place we sat in a park. But it wasn’t recent times, it was the far fucking future. “Yo, pops, why we always go here?” The young Martin was sitting there, baggy jeans with a nice polo shirt on, leaning on his knees. He seemed bored, he looked bored…he was fucking bored. I mean, shit, it’s a damned park, and kids don’t really appreciate that type of shit. The young Martin, though, would never say something rude to his “pops”. The man there, the Tim of the Liam and Tim combination, sat back in the bench and just stared off into the distance. The lines of age were beginning to hit, but he was still in decent shape. Sure, he wasn’t the huge mass of muscle he was in his prime, but he was still a large man, great shape for his age. Next to him, leaning against the bench, were a pair of crutches, more likely than not for the knees that deteriorated over time. Age fucking gets you. “Whaddya mean ‘why’? This place is fucking beautiful! Why wouldn’t you want to be here?” “Man…” The boy didn’t understand it, but he was young and foolish. Tim had been through a lifetime of worries and mayhem. He’s seen the guns and the blood and the chaos. He’s put countless men six feet under, founding entire graveyards under his name. At this park there was a sort of refuge, a place where he could go to escape the world he abandoned in Massachusetts. There was life and beauty and overall vibrance, something he could never find in the hell he lived in. The boy, though, he didn’t understand a thing. Whenever he inquired with his father on why he lives with two brothers, they told him to shut up or go do chores or some bullshit. Whenever he asked about why his parents were of a different color, they dismissed it. And there was always an argument waiting to happen whenever he asked about his real dad. Today, though, the boy was pushed over the edge. For too long he’s wanted to know about the real father. And today, that was the day. “Pops, I got something to ask ya.” Tim shifted on the bench for a moment, leaning forward while managing to not maintain any type of eye contact. Was he preparing? Or, rather, was he still lost in the nature he was so in love with, his final escape? “Shoot.” “It’s about my pops.” “Me and Liam?” “Nah, man, my real one.” And the fury of the fire just burnt. For so long, this conversation has been avoided. And every time it was brought up, Tim could only grow furious. He knew that Liam had slaughtered the father, and the only chance he had of keeping it a secret was a defensive nature and an attacking spirit. “Don’t insult me with that bullshit!” “Fuck you!” “Don’t you say that to me!” “Then tell me, why haven’t I heard from my father or nothin’?” “You’re adopted!” “Bullshit, I ain’t adopted, you ain’t got the papers from the orphanage! Where am I from?” And the truth boiled and bubbled with every shell that dropped and ever gun that blasted, ever hole ripped into the body of S-Laughter, the real father. The Truth stung like a fucking hornet, over and over again until the skin swelled into a mass of pain and deliberate lies. The truth was a fucking right hook, and it was about to land. “Allright, allright. This isn’t gonna be easy…and you have to be ready. Can you be open? Can you?” “Yeah, dad, I can.” A sigh. A breath. And the truth. “Your father was a gang banger. A thug, a no-good nothing, and Liam was sent to murder him. His name was S-Laughter, and he was the leader of his little clan. Thing is, when Liam was sent, the whole neighborhood fucking knew about it. And they waited, as did he. He fucking knew about it too. And he was there, waiting with your mom.” Silence. “Liam walked into the apartment and found your dad there, with you in hand. He knew his time had…come, so he handed you over, wanted us to give you a better life. Right then and there, he…” “Stop.” And the boy buried his face in his hands, rubbing away his sorrow. This family he grew up in was forged by blood and steel, a lifestyle that had no kind of backbone whatsoever. It fucking hurt like the god damned truth should hurt. “I’m sorry…” “What does Liam think about this?” “They called your pop an angel. And Liam felt he was doing the work of God…the White Lord.” “The White Lord? What the fuck is that shit? You two were racists?” And this is what we call the fucking curveball, a twelve to six dropped that slams into the batter with a vicious sting. You see, a kid changes everything. A kid is like a fucking nuclear bomb hitting the ground and completely fucking shit up, making everything look different. As the transformation from the far future from the not so far future occurs in Tim’s visions, if you will, you’ll see why. Because love for a child will make you drop everything you have and want to give it the best. You could abandon all your beliefs for one of those fuckers. “Tim, we have to get out of here!” At the table Tim feeds the child a little porridge, out of some fucking British nursery rhyme. The child eats it gleefully, unaware of the problems that are about to occur. “Why, what the fuck is the matter?” “He was seen!” The four eyes drop onto the innocent, then dash back at each other again. “So?” “The Nest…they’ve disowned us.” “What the fuck? After all we’ve fucking done for them? After all the shit we’ve given? After—“ “Bob is dead. They torched the fucking construction site. Shit’s fucked, Tim. The Nest is falling apart.” Bob was one of the head guys in Martin Brothers Construction, the biggest construction company in Boston. And now with most of the equipment flamed, it was only evident that the apartment was next. “All because of one child? One innocent little fucking nub of a thing? They don’t even know that it’s ours!” “Tim, it’s a black baby!” “IT’S A HUMAN FUCKING BEING!” Silence. “A HUMAN FUCKING BEING!” And there it was again, the truth. Lifestyles went from shattered to stained to tattered to everything far between from the murder to that not so far future. From far away you could hear the screeching cars roaring towards the complex. Death drives like a maniac, they say. “I know that, brother! But they…” “We’ve tainted them.” “Huh?” “We’ve tainted them, Liam. We did this. I’ve been thinking about this a lot lately and I’ve realized that we’ve led ourselves into this. For too long now we’ve been shoving down their throats all this stuff about the niggers and spics and whatever. But…whether it’s true or not…we can’t escape the lifestyle, even if we only want to raise this child. When I look at the boy, I don’t even see race; I just see something for me to bring up. But when they see it…” “Nigger. That’s all.” And the truth was there again. There like a fucking nightmare. “So, what are gonna do now?” Liam’s words continued off the last ones he said, causing pause. What the fuck WERE they gonna do? They cut off no ties and, realistically, knew of no other place to go. Shit was fucked. “We’ll talk to them. Tell them we’ll get out of their city when this whole fire mess blows over.” “Tim, they fire bombed out shit!” “Don’t argue. Let’s just go.” With baby in hand, Tim and Liam trudged out of their apartment and made their way down the building to the front, approaching the oncoming mob of people. There were about twenty or more of them, armed to the teeth with all the finest of weapons an Eagle should have. And they were pissed, too. “THERE’S THE NIGGER!” “GET IT!” “DON’T LET ‘EM GET AWAY!” As they sped up, Tim shot a hand forward to halt the crowd. They were restless and ready for blood, but they still somehow had respect for the man who had just shat on their faces. So they listened. “You come after us for what reason? Because we house the child of a man we slaughtered for our cause? Because we didn’t have the heart, or lack there of, to put a bullet in the head of this child?” The crowd stood there, mutteringly silent. “It was not the child’s choice to be born to that thug! Look at him! Does he think that he wants to be a drug dealer or a killer? Do you possibly believe that this child has mal-intention towards our religion?” “All the niggers do, Tim!” “Of course. And this is my demise. I set myself up for this, didn’t I? I’ve preached these words and verses for too long now, and here they are coming back, ripping my shit up like a food processor. But what happens now?” Silence. “What the fuck happens now? Here I am, your leader, the man who instilled these notions and reinforced them over and over into your life, and what will you do to me? Am I the one who is abandoned now? Are we not allowed to raise one of these children in a life of freedom and no pain? I still hold the beliefs of you all—the thugs of the earth need to be ridden. But what happens now?” For an answer, you’ll never fucking believe it. You’d think this guilt trip of a testimony would make these fuckers change their mind. But when that barrel lifted up towards the face of Tim Martin, his heart sunk. Inside that chamber was the only thing that could truly demolish all of Tim’s worth, everything he was trying to be. The changes, the new heart…gone. Soon, the bullets started going off and the former beings known as Tim and Liam Martin flailed into corpses that slammed into the ground. The blood splattered and puffed from the holes ripped into the skin, the life escaping with the exit wounds that dug into the building behind them. When the bodies hit the ground, though, there was something missing. It was only two bodies—a third one was not there. That’s because there were only two dead. The black child stayed clenched to Tim’s chest, crying from the loud noises. It screamed and it screamed, wanting this new guardian back, not enjoying this new world of violence. Whether or not he lived from that point on was irrelevant, because the only noise that could be heard was crying and crying and crying and crying and crying… ~~~ Liam stared into the eyes of Joe, laying there in pain. The devilish figure over his shoulders, Tim rather, was still mouthing those words. Liam mouthed them back, but this action transcended from crazy hallucinogenic image to reality. “Liam, what? I shee, I shee! Just stop, please dere!” With a quick snap of the neck Liam was back seeing into the eyes of Joe. There was no demon in there, burning a hole through the retina of Joe. It was just Joe, scared fucking shitless of the giant human being who was hulking over him after laying a pummeling. And the pummeling was fucking hard; I’m talking bruised eye, bloody lip, all that jazz. Liam now felt bad and picked Joe to his feet. “Joe…Joe, I’m…” “Oh, Liam, it’s ok, I guess. I mean, I can understand, yer yerng and werked up, and here I are comin’, all bossin’ and tossin’…” Joe dusted his shirt off with two hands, using his fingers to elegantly sweep away the thin layer of dirt he just accumulated from being thrown to the ground. Liam led Joe to the kitchen where, already brewing, a pot of coffee was waiting. “Whatcha got dere?” “Nothin’ special, Folgers.” “Eh, Folgers ish special ter me!” As he pulled the chair away to sit down, Joe creaked and squeaked with old age. He wasn’t really a tough guy anymore, but you could tell in his day he acted like when. Whether he could actually walk the walk was another question. “So, Joe, why the fuck you want me to kill a little kid?” He sat there fidgety, knowing the last time he tried to answer this question he got really intimate with the nice hardwood floor the Martins just had installed in their apartment. He didn’t need that again. “Oh, I dern’t know, Liam…I just hate em all…” “Who’s this?” “The niggers, Liam! And all dem udder colors, too.” “Oh, yeah, right. That. But, really…a hatred for a people can blind you that much?” “Whaddya mean blind me?” “Well, think of it this way, Joe. You got kids, right?” “Oh, yesh, two of dem.” “What if, when they were just itsy bitsy little things, some nigger just straight up and killed your child cause they were white?” “Den I’d fuckin’ kill da fuggers!” “Why? Because they killed your child?” “Yesh!” “So why should I kill the child of a black man? He has those same feelings too, you know.” “Liam, the niggers only care about—“ And right there he began to ramble and it was more than evident that it was all lost. There was no talking reason into these fools. They had their own intentions, their own agenda about everything. And the scary part is that Tim and Liam led them down this road, the same two guys who are trying to steer them away. I know that I’ve been reinforcing that idea for a long time in this now, the whole concept of Liam and Tim being stuck down there. But, really, it’s some scary fucking shit. And it’s everywhere in the world. Shit, I mean, you see those Muslims get their cocks all erect when there was that Danish cartoon? Can’t someone tell them to calm the fuck down? No, they led themselves down this path of insanity and craziness. How about in fucking Iraq? All those crazy fuckers just blowing each other up. Shit, they blow themselves up. And for what? For a fucking belief that someone led them down, so far to the point that they’d do anything to defend it. As you can see as well, it’s not so easy to back on your own beliefs either, is it? ~~~ There was one final scene that came to Tim’s mind, a sort of counter balance to the previous one. It was in a dark alley, the same kind he went to not-so-long ago when he stabbed that man and only saw the devil looking back. This time, though, there was no knife in hand, but rather a pistol, one he grew oh-so-familiar with. Clutched in his other arm was the child, the same one we’ve been with this whole time. He’s still cute as shit, but you can’t see him that will, with the skin pigmentation and the lack of light and the dark blanket covering most of him. Tim scurries deeper and deeper into this alley way with the baby in hand, the child letting out little squeaks of innocence and all that fucking hoopla. But Tim, he pays no attention to it and just goes deeper and deeper, this endless building so deep that when he looks over his shoulder, just a glance, he can’t even see the fucking road. Finally he finds his spot, stopping and gasping for breath once he leans against the wall. He doesn’t even know where the fuck he is, he just had to be somewhere that was far away. Far the fuck away. Tim’s ass slowly slides down the side of whatever building he’s leaning against until it plops down on the ground. The tired look on his face is still there, even when his eyes somewhat light up upon glancing at the beautiful child. But now, he’s only shaking his head. Shaking his head. Lifting up the gun to the child’s face and shaking his head. The only way he can escape this reality…shaking his head. Boom. And Tim shakes himself out of that image, that scene. And Tim shakes himself to a cold present, only leading to a colder future. ------------------------ 'There's no diversity, we're all burning in the melting pot.' --Immortal Technique
