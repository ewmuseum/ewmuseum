Roses & Thorns - Epilogue

16 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/06/06 There are many reasons why people get tattoos. Some people do it to commemorate events, to remember the good times that they’ve had, the lasting memory of an event that’ll never happen again, to mourn the loss of a good friend. Other people use it to define who and what it is they are in today’s society, sort of like a badge for those needing a distinction. They cover themselves in the desired depictions and hide behind them like fools. They speak of different reasons for the different tattoos but half the time you know they’re just making it up. Those are the ones desperate to stand out and make a statement for not only themselves, but also for whatever desired subculture they wish to infest and degrade with their own insipid presence. They are searching for identity in all the wrong places, but then again, how will you ever find something without first making a couple of wrong turns? Granted, the turn shouldn’t involve an expensive piece of body art that is practically permanent. So why did I get one? Well, let’s just say I fall into the first category, but not for any good, fond or distant memory of the past that’ll live with me in remembrance. No, not at all. Since I started writing this journal that was supposed to be a category of the enfolding story between myself and other individuals, things began to warp and twist, unravelling and untwisting like cracking threads in the spine of the very book. As such, the tattoo made perfect sense to bookmark it forever in my head. Now…if only I could figure out why I have a paragraph of writing on my back as well…. *** Thorns These past few weeks since the end of Twilight of the Gods left me in a complete and utter tailspin, teetering on the precipice of the very sanity I was grasping at just before. The result of Twilight had left me sick to the soul and seething in my own private hell. In fact, I even contemplated returning to Eastern Russia where I had been for two years in my life. My birthday came and went without incident because nobody knew where I was. I think deep down Sasha felt some sort of regret at her actions over time and my absence from both competition and events were never picked up upon by any of the administration. To tell you the truth, I couldn’t care less what she tried to do to make peace with me. Her true emotions were summed up by the inexcusable actions with one Pearl Harber in the following weeks. I…I can’t even begin to write the feelings that are coursing through my veins right now as I discuss this. So…I won’t. It was never a question that I would return. It was only a question of when. Despite the feelings of many that I would be back as soon as possible to break up the whole party that Adam Dick seemed to be enjoying in AWC, I felt no need or desire to return. But, that leaves the simple question of why? Why wouldn’t I return when the very reason I was so infuriated and riled up was succeeding in the one thing that I never wanted to happen? It’s simple. I didn’t care for AWC. Nobody had shown any outcry or shock at the manner in which Adam Dick manipulated and cheated his way to a second title. Nobody cared to mention about the manner in which he won that title. Nobody admonished or punished George Cassidy for his reprehensible actions in that match. I don’t blame Truth Waters, in fact he was probably the only person to have given a damn about me after that match, going so far as to apologise to me in the back and then leave a message on my answering machine. Only he understood the ramifications of what Adam Dick did that night. Only he cared for the consequences of that night. Only he was in the knowledge of the wrestling industry and the result it has given to AWC. In the last journal entry that sits above this piece of writing, I spoke of many things, scattered on the page and in disjointed and cryptic mannerisms which I was not familiar with. What did make sense however was the very thing I predicted. What I assumed, correctly, was that with the rise of a person like Adam Dick, others would follow. Others would join the crusade of manipulation and underhandedness in the industry. His success and achievement made it ‘ok’ for others to attempt this path. And what have we seen since then? Mike Wade following his blood brother in kind, pulling the big swerve that they always enjoyed…on his own partner. Tag team wrestlers attempting to usurp and revolt like whining babies. They nearly did it too, if not for Adam having the energy at just the right time. Guess Nash wasn’t in the mood the previous night. We saw chaos in the hierarchy further down the card. I don’t think anyone can honestly tell me of what the hell is going on there. So forgive me for whomever might read this, but dammit, I just don’t give a fuck about the AWC as it stands. All the momentum they’ve gained has simply faded into obscurity. What chance they had to truly race ahead of a promotion like PRIME who also experienced difficulties has been lost. So really can anyone blame me for abandoning a promotion that just didn’t care about itself, let alone me? There was one other person that did care though… Roses Sweet, pure Jessica. The scent of rose about her calmed to no end as I sit and write my feelings on the past couple of weeks out in this journal. She asks why I must sit at the desk and I know I could easily just stop right here and now and none would be the wiser of the intricacies of the mind. That would be far too easy for me now though. Because after Truth Waters, one more person held the light of hope for me in that promotion. Jessica. She had wronged me just the same as Sasha but her mind and body and soul knew that she hadn’t meant to be that way. It was tentative at first what way she would go though. I didn’t fully comprehend what possibility she might entertain. Facing facts, she knew that she had to endure the contract that she herself had entered into with Adam, irrespective of whether or not she felt it right. In essence she had the integrity and honour to respect the contract. And in turn, I respected her for it. It inspired me to think about my decision, to contemplate whether or not I had judged far too soon. I was all ready to drop and pack up, to leave with my one distinguished belt, a belt I have never lost on fair terms. But her simple morals brought me back, it brought back from the brink of no return. She deserved more than to be abandoned, just like I had. She deserved company. It was slow at first, because I wasn’t sure how to make the connection, to break the ice and thaw it away. It’s hard to come back after the words with which I left her. Telling someone that you…love, that they are wrong is hard to do but I was sure on that point. Coming back to that person after you have proven right is even harder. I was not without transgression however either…my assertion and rebuttal of her, along with movements towards Sasha were not overlooked. I just had to come clean and approach her with an open mind. Luckily for me, her disposition to the entire ordeal was very favourable and not at all against the way I wished it to be. So it became so much clearer and easier from there. Summation As Jessica lies on the bed and pads it slowly, hinting not so subtly as to her intentions, I feel that I can finally return, with purpose to AWC. It is hard to write and explain the varied reasons for why I left and what occurred during that time period, but they will rise up out of the depths in time. Right now however, I simply want to enjoy one of the finer things in life. I’m going back to simply wrestling. So this coming show I’ll step back into that ring and face another opponent, this one from the past of AWC. In many ways people are looking towards me as one of the older, veteran guys in AWC now. It’s quite true, I’ve been here the longest apart from one or two mentionable and I think it’s a testament to my character that I have been able to stick it out. The two titles I have won since this incarnation of my career proudly adorn the walls of my house…our house, I best get used to writing that. But nevertheless I feel…happy to be wrestling again. That’s always a good feeling.
