Fresh Burst! Pre-Warm Up

24 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-24-2005 Before anything can be said you must first think about it these days. Coming off a win Titan goes into Burst! against Harry from the team of TTS. It should be an interesting bout as both of these stars are from two different worlds, but both have amazing talent. This week we decided to followup with Titan the day before to see how he is doing. Last time we saw him things were pretty rough and he seemed a little out of it. Let's see what's in store this week... Titan: Heh, last week was alot of fun. Voice: Really? Even though it didn't turn out the way you thought? Titan: Well yeah, I expected to win just not like that. Voice: A win is a win my friend that's for sure. Titan: Well enough about me for now, how have you being? It's being like what, five years since we last spoke. Voice: It's being good, haven't wrestled much but I've being keeping busy raising kids and repairing my marriage. Titan: Yes, I do remember the last time we spoke things were pretty rough with the wife, so what about the kids? Voice: Three now. Titan: THREE?!? Voice: Surpised I see, well it is alot of work but it's worth it in my opinion. Just seeing them makes me feel young and alive again. Titan: You are still young, it's just your injury that has plagued you and made you feel like that. Did the doctors say anything about your recovery? Voice: ... Titan: ... Voice: ...Well... Titan: Well what? Voice: It's quite possible I could return to the ring if the next check-up goes well. I'm not saying that I would ever come back, but the possibility is there if I ever thought of coming back. Titan: Boy I remember the old days. You and I dominating the ring as a tag team. Voice: Memories... As we lean back and think to ourselves, what's this all about? Well it seems in lieu of his singles match tonight, Titan has decided to give his old tag team partner a call and see how he is doing. His name you ask? Titan: Ken, you know you could come back as a part timer one of these days even with me. Ken: I don't know about that. Titan: What about with me? Stay in my corner and have me watch your back? Ken: (laughs) Titan it would be alot of fun but you've got your hands full in AWC right now, I mean your wrestling tommorow aren't you? Titan: Of course, and how convenient against a second half of a tag team. Ken: Advantage you... Titan: Well in some aspects, but I'm not counting him out since he does have experience and he is no rookie. I mean I'm going in as a solo wrestler and he is used to being a tag team but come on, my mindset is to expect anything especially from a wrestler like him. Ken: Wow, you've changed since the old days. Titan: What do you mean? Ken: Before you took no guff and no crap from anything or anyone. You had respect but you never talked like that. Titan: Times change. You've being out for over four years now so I don't expect you too understand how the world works now. (snickers) Ken: Yeah Yeah. Just keep in mind who was the better half of our tag team. Titan: Me of course. Ken: Get outta here! Titan: Seriously, I did most of the work and I even saved your butt a few times. Ken: Listen you may have done a fair share of the work, but when it came down to winning titles and beating the teams that really counted, it was me who usually saved the day. Titan: One time and I hear about for the rest of my life... Both: (laughing) Titan: So back on the topic of you returning, do you think there is a slight possibility? Ken: Like I said before, let me hear from the doctor first, and even if I do return I don't think I'd have much interest in a tag team again since we pretty much did enough of that for one lifetime. Titan: Well than your career is over. Ken: What do you mean? Titan: Coming off an injury like that Ken, I don't think most organizations are looking for someone to compete in solo competition with that kind of liability. You could cause more harm than wanted. Ken: I guess your right in a way, but if the doctor has good news than maybe it won't cause too much if I ever did get injured. Titan: Well look at it this way Ken, when it comes to solo competition are you willing to put yourself on the line, or do you have to take it easy. Ken: Your always being the smart one aren't you. Titan: That's my job. Ken: Well I'll let you know what the doctor says next week, but tommorow good luck and hopefully everything goes well. Titan: Oh it will, don't worry about that. It seems as if Titan's mood has taken a better turn this week, Titan is happier and seems a little more confident than he did last week. Also there is a possible chance that he might try and bring an old friend back and maybe hit the tag scene again? The future is endless for this guy right about now, but after coming off a victory he is pretty excited about his upcoming match...
