Boyz 'N Da Hood

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th August [The scene opens up about ten years ago, before Grobschnit was introduced to professional wrestling, where we find DA ‘SCHNIT chilling in his pad with his Compton brotha’s Crunk Dawg, Mac Daddy and Homey G. In the background some hardcore gangsta N.W.A. shit is playing, and the playas are all sipping on their 40’s.] Mac Daddy: Yo, dis shit is whack. Homey G: Word. Crunk Dawg: Tru dat, playa. [Suddenly Grobschnit jumps up and begins to freestyle, for no apparent reason.] Grobschnit: Yo, itz mee itz mee, itz da G-R-O-B, an izz gona mayke u lo0k lyke a baybee… izz gott skillz n thrillz, rimez n nynes, izz stayin’ troo 2 ma croo fo’eva yo! Izz so kool I mayke Noel Edmonds looke lik a foo, dat’s cuz I rool!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!1111one Mac Daddy: Preach on bruddah. Crunk Dawg: Gawdayum dat be some crunk-ass shit fo’ shizzle dawg. Grobschnit: Wurd lyfe, mye skilz r l33t. Homey G: Dayum bro, I be all outta liquor! Lets go down to da store. Mac Daddy: Okay gangsta. [The ghetto crew leave the room and head out the house, but suddenly a bullet hits Mac Daddy straight in the chest! Oh no, it’s a horrible drive-by! The assailants speed away in their lowrider. Living in the ghetto is HELL!] Mac Daddy: Fuck. Grobschnit: OMFG! Hez ded. Crunk Dawg: Gawdayum nigga! Man we gotta get outta da ghetto fo’ sho’! Homey G: Aight brotha rest in peace. Lets go to the store yo! [Just like that the homeys leave their dead brotha, and jump in Grobber’s lowrider. After bouncing around like a bunch of O.G.’s with the crunk-as-fuck hydraulics, they reach the store and notice a couple of old women staring at them funny.] Crunk Dawg: Wut’chu lookin’ at, BIATCH!?!?! [The old women quiver with FEAR of the scary gangsta, as the homeboys enter the store, heading straight for the liquor. They gather up some bottles and head to the checkout, manned by a pasty white boy.] Grobschnit: Yo, hurree uhp u dumazz whiteee. Homey G: Whitey be holdin’ us down, yo. [Suddenly the boy transforms into Eminem!] Eminem: Gawdayum I’m crunk. Grobschnit: Fagit. [Grobschint leaps over the counter. The green alien ‘o doom, Homey G and Crunk Dawg beat the hell out of him and leave the shop.] Grobschnit: U don wana fux wiff uz! Homey G: Tru dat. [Wooo goodbye.]
