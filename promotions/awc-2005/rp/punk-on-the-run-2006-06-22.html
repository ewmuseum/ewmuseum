Well these are the risks you have to consider when you make that vital decision to defy the law and take matters in to your own hands. Hindsight was something we spoke of previously and when you’re on the spot there is no space for it in the front of your mind, but when you are on the run the mind allows a little more room. Questions are begged, regret rears its ugly head creating a sickening feeling in the stomach, sleep is lost and you’re left feeling alone in company. But the only down fall with hindsight is the fact that no matter how much you juggle the word in your mind it will never change a thing, because what’s done is done. All you have to look forward to is the fear of getting caught, the paranoia that maybe that man staring at you from the other side of the road knows or has that passing police car been given instructions to pull you over and discover what’s hidden. Ren hands Red Rock a spade but it D’avid has already removed his jacket and his shirt revealing his ginger wiry chest hairs and small man boobs. The sound of metal penetrating dirt echoes off the trees, making it sound like it is coming from all directions. Red Rock sluggishly joins in by stabbing the spade in to the ground and tipping a scoop of dirt to his side. Ren spectates with an expressionless face with the cogs in her head turning, finally starting to question what they are doing is really the right thing and maybe wishing she never asked to come in the first place, but only Ren knows that. Red Rock: I think I hear someone! Red Rock drops his spade and sharply surveys the surround area. Red Rock: I swear I just heard foot steps! D’avid: It was probably just a fox! Keep digging! Red Rock: I swear I heard someone. Red Rock continues digging but he can’t shrug off what he heard, neither can he block the procrastinating thoughts that there capture is imminent. The hole is now beginning to take shape; it is nearly deep enough to shroud the unknown dead girl. D’avid stops for a second to wipe his face with his tattered and blistered hands whilst Red Rock struggles on solo. Then one more time Red Rock hears the same noise he heard before, causing his whole body to judder. This time he slams the spade to the floor in frustration whilst sharply pointing his finger at D’avid. Red Rock: HOW COULD YOU NOT HEAR THAT? D’avid: Hear what? Ren: Red, I think you’re hearing things I don’t hear anything. The sound draws nearer and Red Rock throws his body from left to right in search of the sound source. The levels of frustration are on the increase and it is shown through the frantic way he is acting. Then behind him he senses the presence of an unknown figure and now Ren and D’avid can’t deny a thing as a man stands behind Red Rock wearing a long trench coat and a hat. Howard: Red. Red Rock’s face drops to the ground because he instantly recognizes the boring nasal monotonous voice of his flat mate Howard. The very man who sparked all this and some how he has tracked Red Rock down. Red Rock: HOWARD?! Oh my God! Howard: Red, you took my one love and now you will pay. Red Rock: Guys! He’s fucking nuts do something! Ren and D’avid are both frozen in shock and don’t respond to Red Rock’s pleas. Howard advances at Red Rock who hastily backs off, but Howard pursues with a cold stare and an eerie swagger in his step. Red Rock’s heart rate sky rockets as he stares in to the eyes of a mad man. Howard steps forward as Red Rock steps back in unison without realising he is stepping closer to the grave he just dug out. Red Rock: Howard, don’t do anything…else…crazy! Howard: I suppose you’re wondering how I located you Red. Red Rock: well, that wasn’t the 1st thing I was thinking of, I was hoping you won’t kill me. Howard’s face is now right up against Red Rock’s and Red Rock recoils back before suddenly stepping in to the shallow grave. He yelps like a beaten puppy as he is lays flat looking up towards the unhinged Howard. Howard: I’ll tell you how I know you were here Red. Red Rock shields his face as he prepares for the worst because Howard kneels down getting closer to Red Rock. Howard: BECAUSE YOU JUST BEEN PUNK’D! All of a sudden a camera crew appear from the trees and large spot lights illuminate the area. Ren and D’avid jump up and down clapping their hands and laughing as Howard reveals that he was Ashton Kutcher star of MTV’s hit prank show Punk’d. Ashton Kutcher strips off his disguise and has a huge ear to ear grin expecting Red Rock to deliver a typical response but Red Rock does nothing but stare blankly before responding with a lower pitch tone of disbelief. Red Rock: You what… Ashton Kutcher: Red Rock, AWC Relentless champion, you just got PUNK’D Red Rock: Right. OK. Ashton Kutcher sustains his over excited ear to ear grin, still expecting Red Rock to take the prank like everyone else, and when he doesn’t get the desired response he grows a little concerned. Ashton Kutcher: You ok dude? Red Rock: Yeah, I’m fine. Just bare with me a second! Red Rock pulls himself out from the shallow grave and wraps both hands round a near by shovel. Ashton Kutcher: Woah man! Woah! Ashton Kutcher extends his hands in a submissive gesture but not for long as Red Rock ploughs the spade round the side of Ashton Kutcher’s head and he falls to the floor like a sack of shit. Red Rock: YOU FUCKING CUNT! YOU HAD ME THINK I WAS SHIPPING AROUND A FUCKING DEAD BODY! I FUCKING HATE YOU! ARGGGHHH! Ashton Kutcher’s entourage runs to his aid as Red Rock tosses the spade over his shoulder. A sheet of guilt is wrapped around Ren and D’avid who knew all along. Red Rock: I hate you guys… So there it is literally days before Red Rock’s toughest title defence and here he is in the middle of a forest finding his self at the butt of a joke. That is his life story; he is just the butt of a joke and at Divide and Conquer Red Rock will achieve his crowning moment by defeating former Translantic champion Jack Murphy the very man who took his last title from him. With a title and a winning streak of 7 he has everything to loose in this match.
