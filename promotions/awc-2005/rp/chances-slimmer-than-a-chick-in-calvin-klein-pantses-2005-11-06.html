chances slimmer than a chick in calvin klein pantses

6 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: earlier god says… can i sell the sunrise in return for a sunset? can i just be here one more day, until my sunburn fades away. Wednesday, October 26th, 2005. Disguised by the glare in his windshield as an annoyance, the sun hung at an infuriating angle against the glass. Adam always had a hard-time sleeping. Windows of a two-door coop don’t exactly double as comfortable sleeping facilities, but he still tried to make it work. Needless to say, it wasn’t working. Mike Wade had noticed his friend’s eyes fluttering out the corner of his own. He’d been begging for human interaction for the past hour driving to the airport, while Adam stay asleep. When Adam first asked his Unfuckable partner for a ride, Wade didn’t think it’d be as much of an inconvenience as it was. But Adam and Mike didn’t mind each other’s “inconveniences,” it’s what made this partnership work. Sure, Wade could have hit critical-mass on Adam; bitching about how selfish Adam is by asking for rides in the early morning, only to taunt the driver with his sleep. But Wade wasn’t a bitch, and hardly acted like one without reason. “I need a pair of fucking sunglasses.” He says bitterly. And against the aggravating soon, if you were staring, you would wish for nothing else, but the exact same thing. Polarized lenses would be a life-saver in this position. Adam yawns. “We’d better be close.” “I’m a fucking mad-man on the road: We were there thirteen minutes ago.” Mike replies, gripping the wheel at both top-ends tightly. “Should I be as sore as I am?” Adam complained slightly, another tired yawn accompanied by a drawn-out stretch. He was speaking of the Unfuckables’ first win as a team, of course. Mike examined his partner’s movements and limits. He’d noticed at what point of stretching where Adam became sore, unable to extend the limbs to his full desire. “We got some great offense, so I shouldn’t be too worried.” “I keep feeling like I pulled my deltoid or my lats or something.” Adam said, rubbing the inner-part of his bicep. This incident incites Mike Wade to chuckle. “I don’t even think you know where those are.” Adam shoots a petty glare. “We haven’t really talked about where you were last week.” Adam turns and begins to peer out the road again, watching each sign as they sprint past. “You caught a flight out to Los Angeles, didn’t you?” Mike’s head would turn from the road every once in a while, just to get a grip on what Adam was feeling. Most times, the face read it all. Any nuances or annoyances could be read by a person’s facial expression when they’re as animated as Adam Dick. But then there’re those times where Adam goes in reserve-mode; thinking to himself and rubbing his hair, the world becomes a blur to him as decisions and processes run through his brain. It was a thinking process he could never kick, and annoyed everyone around him. “You’re going to go out again, this week, aren’t you? Adam still didn’t answered. It wasn’t the silence that bothered agitated Wade, it was the fact that Adam could hear him yet still chose to remain quiet. He had an answer lined up from the first question asked. Hell, he had an answer for Wade before he even left for L.A. And something had been off about Adam their entire time they’d spent together. Was this early partnership turning sour already? Don’t bet your fucking last dollar yet. “Adam; what happened in L.A. last week?” “Well.” Adam began, still staring out the passenger window and the glare that accompanied it. “Let’s just say shit’s going down.” adam says… looking for ways to avoid the charade, because when voices are laid, choices are made. be not afraid. James Lawler is a careless bastard, and that shit that comes to him is deserving. You’re probably wondering how I can sit so high and mighty, in judgment of a guy whom I barely know. If I tell you it’s because he was supporting that shit-head the Educator in all of his cock-sucking endeavors, you’d call me a spiteful son of a bitch. But it goes beyond that. How do I know James Lawler is a careless bastard that deserves death? Because I wasn’t the only one who felt this way. And when two people, completely unrelated, are thinking the exact same thing about the exact same person, they must’ve gotten something right. James Lawler was careless and let me see top-secret information that he shouldn’t have let anyone see. But if there’s anyone that shouldn’t see this shit, it’s me. He must have known who I was if he’s a friend of the Educator; as much as I’ve been talking about him, he’d ask for anyone to save his ass from me. He must know I’m coming for him. Or he must know someone else is. Either way, I know I’m not alone on this hunt. So I go to Hancook Park. It’s a small place; quiet and near the suburbs of Burbank. There isn’t one piece of flat terrain aside from the basketball courts, lined with concrete. The entire area is a set of hills, designed to make the biggest inconvenience for even the craftiest of bicyclists. It’s twelve AM. Apparently I’ve got a few minutes of scouting time before shit goes down, but I’ve also got to remain prepared. Kelli checked the city-logs for me, told me that the recycling trucks pick up the waste every Monday. It’s Thursday, I’ve got less than a week’s worth of old news papers crowding me as I hide out in the recycle bin of the park. It’s large; slightly smaller than a dumpster, but just enough room to get good clear breaths. You might be saying, I’m creepy. Hiding in a dumpster is something creep’s do, right? Well it isn’t a fucking dumpster, so get off your high horse and shove that dick up your own ass. Chances are, if you’re sitting in judgment of me, you’re about as careless as that James Lawler guy. Because this was never a difference of opinion, and why people think the things that they do. This is shit hitting the fan, in every sense of the word. It sprays, and it gets messy, and it becomes everyone’s problem. All because someone wants to have a little fun. It’s the very definition of shit hitting the fan. It doesn’t matter what I do in this, as long as I get to the Educator. Because just like James Lawler, I’m careless. Not careless enough to wind up dead, though. We’ll see who comes in an hour, I’m going to take a bit of a nap. god says… mama told me one day this was gonna happen, but she never told me when. she told me it would happen when i was much older, wish it would’ve happened then Time passes. It is without a watchful eye that the second hand on the clock ticks faster. It is with an occupied mind that time slips without warrant. It is inside a dumpster, where the Illustrious Face-Eater lets his sleep deprivation get the best of him. Ruffled voices slip through the cracks of his rusty box, shrouded in paper. And unemptied glass bottles that create soggy paper. And used food containers. Adam thought that hiding in the recycle bin would be a lot cleaner than the trash can. What Adam didn’t think, was this plan through. “Drr err kerr errrerr.” “Rrrrf.” “Grrrrf.” The voices made Adam’s eyes flutter open, as well as the sound of raindrops crashing into the metal that never ceased.. In a fit, he suddenly remembered what he had to do and forgot how long he’d been sleeping for. He jerked to his feet, forgetting the metal roof overhead. Adam slams his head hard and falls back into the pit of paper. “Fuck.” He mumbles, rubbing the resulting bump on his head while feeling for the opening so he could stick his head through. He sees, through the rain, the basketball courts. No lights. He squints his eyes a bit more than needed, and finally is able to make out five distinct shapes. One being James Lawler. The other four being very dangerous. The man all stand in a row before James Lawler, who looks very cowardly amongst the massive frames before him. One man speaks for the most of them. “I told you to keep an eye on possible targets.” James looks at the ground, digging his tow deeper than the concrete as if it were possible. “I... did, but... it’s—“ Someone else of the bigger men cuts him off. “It’s what?” “It’s hard.” James tried to reason. He then looked up at these four men, his eyes begging for an ounce of humanity. He wouldn’t be so pathetic or so drastic, but these were different kinds of people that he was dealing with. “The young men could die from the drug.” “Die?!” One man said, almost in a taunting laugh. “I see.” He turns to one of the others. “Burg, deal with him.” It was panic that raced across James’ face. He was just a simple businessman from London, never expecting to be caught up in a game like this. It was just as quick as he’d started, that realization came when he saw silver being untucked from apparently Burg’s inside coat pocket; this was the end. But Adam wasn’t watching James Lawler’s untimely death. Instead, from the confines of his hot-box, he could see a pair of blonde heads peaking through the bushes. The way they watched from the confines of shrubbery, they were invisible to everyone who stood around; everyone aside from Adam Dick, who was at a very rewarding angle. What he saw, was Pierce Lavelle, and his woman, Kelli. Pierce Lavelle, and Adam Dick’s woman. Pierce Lavelle, being held tightly, by Adam Dick’s woman. Pierce Lavelle, comforting a scared woman, that who belonged to Adam Dick. So the thought creeps through his mind. “That bastard thinks he can have whatever he wants.” It is the pride in Adam that doesn’t allow him to recognize this is all his fault; that he drove Kelli into the arms of Pierce. It is in this pride that Adam feels the need to sabotage whatever work he might’ve had going for him. He takes the first piece of hard material he can, searching the confines of the recycle bin. Adam comes across half-empty soda can. He sticks his body fully outside the trash-can, and amidst the chaos of the shooting of James Lawler, chucks it in the direction of Pierce and Kelli. Adam retreats back inside the can, and for a slower second is able to sit in recollection of the moment. Here he was, orchestrating the events. He never counted on Pierce coming here, he did that on his own sly merit. What he was angry about, was that Kelli didn’t tell him this was happening. He found it comical, being in the ultimate seat of power over his rival. Now it was merely a race to the finish, and to see who could survive. It was the chaos outside that snapped Adam back in to reality, where he realized he’d put his girlfriend’s life in danger. “Did you hear that?” Adam gazes out, seeing Pierce grip Kelli tightly by her arm and quickly drag her away from the scene, bullets and goons chasing her every step of the way. “Catch them, you idiots!” Two men were off after Pierce and Kelli, while Adam watched the other two deal with the more pressing matter at hand. “What do we do with his body?” “I don’t care—the usual. What’s important, is where’s the dossier? I thought Lawler said he’d have more specimens for us.” “He did, or he does. Apparently it’s a prime one, who hasn’t shown as good results since that Masters kid.” That name rung a bell. “Well the file isn’t on him. Fuck, we’re going to have to do more dirty work. Call Goone, let him know there’s some retrieval we have to do.” Their operation was interrupted by the faint sounds of sirens. How unalarming they may seem now, their source was creeping in and getting louder and louder. The two remaining men knew what that meant for the troubled guy, laying without a breath on the middle of the courts. “Shit, five-oh!” “What about the body?” “Fuck the body; we’re gonna have to come back for it.” “Come back for it?! The fuck are we going to do with it now.” Adam watches on, seeing one man lift the lifeless body of James Lawler over his shoulder. “Find temporary storage and hope the fucking cops don’t find it. When do the recycle drivers come around?” “Every Monday.” Responds the other one. Both men begin to approach Adam, sitting inside the recycle bin. “Fuck.” He mutters. He retreats back into the confines of the trash bin, only to be joined by the lifeless and unwilling body of James Lawler. “Let’s get the fuck out of here.” And just like that, the mother fuckers are gone. Gone, leaving Adam with a dead body, leaking on top of him, while police were on their way to investigate the scene. Oh, happy days.
