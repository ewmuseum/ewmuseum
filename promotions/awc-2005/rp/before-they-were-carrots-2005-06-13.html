Before they were Carrots...

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/05 Last week... [The scene opens outside of James von Doom's house. There is a rustling in the bushes. The camera pans in to hear two men quietly arguing about something. One then pushes the other out of the bushes and the other one pounces on him. They start rolling in the grass like Padme and Anakin on Tatooine. But, a moment later, they suddenly stop and smile at each other. One is a smallish man wearing some lame '80's hand me downs and the other is taller and dressed like he's trying too hard to be cool. Their names are Joey and Lamar and they are TAN - The Annoying Neighbors (of von Doom).] Joey: 'Let's not fight.' [Joey smiles at Lamar.] Lamar: 'Let's not.' [They help each other up and dust off all the grass from their clothes.] Joey: 'Ok, we're going to see James about the thing.' [Lamar looks confused.] Lamar: 'What thing?' Joey: 'You know. The thing.' Lamar: 'No. I don't know.' [Joey gets aggitated and points to the bushes.] Joey: (snarling) 'Why we were hiding in the bushes!' Lamar: 'OH...that thing...' [Joey shakes his head and slaps his forehead.] Joey: 'Yes. That thing you moron. (motions for Lamar) Now come on before we get caught before we get to the door.' [Lamar nods his head.] Lamar: 'Right.' [Joey leads the way up to James' door. He stops and waits for Lamar. Once Lamar is there, Joey turns to him.] Joey: 'Now be on your best behavior. This is an amazing man. He escaped from Yahoo.' Lamar: 'Wow. Not many people have done that.' Joey: 'But he has.' Lamar: 'Will you quit kissing his ass so we can go inside already?' Joey: 'Oh...right.' [Joey rings the doorbell. They wait for a moment before James von Doom, trainer, agent, manager, and wrestler opens the door. He gives them a look.] JV: 'Oh, if it isn't HIV Joey and Lamar the Kebbler Elf.' [Joey looks displeased.] Joey: 'HEY! It's not my fault I like the Count!' Lamar: 'YEAH! And it's not my fault I'm short and like Kebbler food products!' [James laughs coyly.] JV: 'Boys, boys. I've got you the deal with the AWC.' [Joey almost jumps out of his pants.] Joey: 'REALLY~!?' [James nods his head.] JV: 'Really.' Joey: 'REALLY REALLY?' JV: 'Really really.' Lamar: 'You're not just messing with us again like that time when you told us those two women wanted to have sex with us and they turned out to be men.' [James starts laughing hysterically.] JV: 'That was great, huh guys?' Joey: (grumbling) 'Yeah. Really great.' [James smiles deviously and pulls out some papers, handing the papers to them.] JV: 'Now go. You haven't much time.' Joey: (reading the paper) 'But it says here the show isn't for another...' [James shuts the door in their faces like he usually does.] Joey: '...week...' [Joey turns to Lamar.] Joey: 'This is SO great Lamar! Think about it. HIV Joey, Lamar the Kebbler Elf, AWC Tag Team Champions!' [Lamar nods his head.] Lamar: 'Yes! That will so own!' [Joey slaps him in the back of the head.] Joey: 'It's pwn you moron. Pwn. (motions for Lamar) Now let's get out of here before he sends Collingwood and Psycho Bunny out to beat us up again.' Lamar: 'Right.' [They quickly scamper back to their house as the scene fades out.] [Cut.] View The Annoying Neighbours 's Biography
