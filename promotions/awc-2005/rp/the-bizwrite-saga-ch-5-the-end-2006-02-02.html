The Bizwrite Saga, Ch 5: The End

2 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/05 Staring at the mirror in hopes to see clearer, I was the one recognizing I was weirder. There was no enemy that wasn’t once a friend to me, It’s hard to understand, but that’s how it’s supposed to be. Life took large tolls on my heavenly goals, I could jump over buildings, but I tripped over small rolls. Was that God smiling, blessing my dark soul? Or was he the one at the grave digging the hole? I’m receiving emotions, but I can’t perceive these notions, The bible touches me like a stinging, burning lotion. And now these kamikaze words are killing me, this shit is real, And no one I know is feeling me, a big deal. Every day I try to wake up and get out of bed, But the second I do I’m pounding in my head. So now I’m standing in the mirror, crying aloud, Upset because I know I could never make pa proud. I was the weird one, and he had the privilege to inform, I felt scorn and nothing else, but really, I was torn. \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\ The Bizwrite Saga was not one that ended as expected. There was no reconciliation. There was no final solution. There was…nothing. In fact, we turned around. … We turned around? Yes, we turned the fuck around. We wanted to get to California, that was the destination, and by Utah, I just said, “Fuck it” and turned around. “Turn around?” Liam said, “We’re going to fucking turn around?” Already the truck had been stopped and facing back towards Boston. Already we were on our way, going along. I was calm as the ocean on a blue day while Liam was acting like a fucking looney. “We get as far as fucking Utah…and we turn the fuck around?!” “Yeah.” “What the fuck is up in your mind?!” And, to be honest, I didn’t know. I was a mess, a blurr. I kept on seeing Liz, sitting there, waiting for me with a smile. I kept on seeing her. But I had to turn around. “Say something, Tim! Explain your fucking self!” I didn’t know what I was doing. Going out there…what the fuck was I thinking? “Explain your God damned ass!” We had been on the road for weeks, the truck barely making it this far. We had spent hundreds on gas, not to mention all the money spent on food and accommodations. This trip had cost us a fortune. And yet…it was all done now. California would not be visited as far as I was concerned. “You don’t want to know.” We were to go back to Boston, and that was that. No question about it. Boston or bust…the Eagle’s Nest was where we were going…home. “Well, of course I want to fucking know! YOU JUST WASTED WEEKS OF MY LIFE, ASSHOLE! We could’ve been in the AWC! Fighting! Earning money by working hard! And you take us out here…for some fruity, devilish road trip to Utah?” What was I thinking, leaving my comfort place? Did I think I could find something new out in California? Did I think that Boston wasn’t the only place I could survive? Was the Eagle’s Nest not my life to live?” “You really want to know, Liam?” So here I was, driving back to Boston, my brother acting like a freaking nut bag and getting all over my shit. I did just waste his time…but maybe I didn’t. “Tell me right the fuck now.” Sigh. “Liam, when I got that e-mail from Liz…I was brought back. Memories, man. Memories. Remember that time when she came over and we played twister?” Dad wouldn’t let us play…twister was an “unholy game for sinners”…but since Liz was like a sister to us, we snuck a few games in. Our 10 year old selves felt pretty badass. Well, in the middle of the game, Liam pulls down my pants, my boxers, and, voila. I was embarrassed…but it didn’t matter! We laughed. That’s how it was…anything could happen and I’d feel comfortable around her. “Yeah, Tim, dad would’ve KILLED us if he found us doing that.” “Yeah, well, this isn’t about dad, this is about her. I wanted shit like that again.” “We can play Twister whenever you’d like, Tim. I mean…if that’s REALLY what you want…” The buffoon. “No! It wasn’t about that! It was her! The experience we had! Everything about that day…was perfect! The two loves of my life, brotherly and romantic, right there coinciding in harmony…and it was perfect!” “Well, it isn’t that way anymore.” And of course I knew that. I wasn’t a dummy…I realized that she moved on to blacker things and Liam and I stayed in the whiter ways of life. We had parted ways. The paths we chose had two different signs. “Well, yeah, but that’s not the point. I still wanted to see her.” “Even though you know she wouldn’t be the same, you dumb ass?” “I didn’t know that.” But I did. I knew it. She might’ve sounded the same on the phone, in the e-mail…but she changed. She wasn’t that young girl anymore…she was Liz Bizwrite, the elder. Liz Bizwrite, the Californian. Liz Bizwrite, girlfriend to the banished. “Bullshit.” “No!” “Bull-fucking-cock-sucking-shit. Tell me right now that you knew she’d be different. Tell me right now you asshole.” I was reluctant. I couldn’t admit it…the shame overcame me. Something so foolish, so dumb…how could I even be part of it? I knew beter…my faith in God should’ve guided me otherwise…but now this?... So I cracked. I cracked like a fucking egg going 3 billion miles an hour into a brick building. I cracked and spilled my guts. “Allright, allright! I knew! I knew…but don’t tell me you’ve never done this before!” “Done what? Dragged my loving-but-annoyed brother half way across the country, only to just say ‘fuck it’ and turn around?” “No, not that at all! I’m sorry for that, Liam, I really am. But I had a dream. A vision. Something I needed to live. Liam, for the past…oh, who knows how long it’s been, I’ve been telling myself that I’d see Liz again. That maybe once again she’d be mine. We’d be clutched together, gripping onto each other and never let go.” I continued along, taking a short breath. “But that’s not the way it is. She’s with some damn n*gger, probably having mulatto babies until the end of time. She’s ruined, she’s nothing. She’s something we spit upon, you and I. Liz Bizwrite is a name that is not mentioned in the nest. It has the same level of contempt as Benedict Arnold. She’s a rat.” I paused. “None of that mattered, though. I had an image, a scenario. I was going to be a God damned hero, Liam. I was going to drive across the country and take back my maiden, bring her back to the promise land. We were going to neck all the way back, too. It was going to be love, just like how it was. And that n*gger wouldn’t have done a thing about it.” A sigh escaped from my mouth. “But once I got to where I turned around, it hit me. It hit me fucking hard, too. Why was I dwelling on this? Why was I trying to believe that anything could change? What would I say? She is the one that had changed, so why couldn’t I?” I shook my head, keeping my eyes on the road. “And the fucking reason, Liam, is because *I’M* the one who changed, not her.” “Lies!” “No, hear me out! It was ME. She followed her heart…she wasn’t meant to be an Eagle. *I* should’ve let her get what she wanted…I should’ve let her go. But I kept her trapped in my cold, dark heart. And in there her soul rotted away until she was nothing more than the motions of something else. She became a robot, a fucking sex toy.” A tear dropped down my face. “So when I started this journey, I wanted that toy. I wanted the joy, the fun…but I was fucking foolish. When a man changes, Liam, so does his life. And she was no longer my life. I had just never realized it, y’know?” \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\ We stood above the grave. It was a cold day, and perfectly, the rain was dropping onto the grass. It had only been minutes since we arrived back from the abrupt ending to our trip, so we needed to talk to dad. As we grew more and more drenched as the time went by, the silence was killer. Other than the drops and the noise of the city, not a word was spoken. It was a verbal silence that had to be respected. We kept it that way…Liam, Tim, and dad. We looked down on him, and he looked down on us from above. A conversation was necessary…we had left him so long. “Our father, who art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy children come, thy will be done, on Earth as it is in Heaven. Give us this day our daily sins, as we forgive those who sin against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory forever. Amen.” The pray echoed from both our tongues simultaneously, followed by the silence. Our gazes were stone as we stood there, staring at the tomb stone. “Here lies Father Martin. His Soul Lives in the Nest Forever.” “He wasn’t really a father, was he?” “Nah. But they called him that.” Liam and I carried the conversation for him. How can a dead man speak with the living? Impossibility, so we could talk and it would be sufficient. “What’re you thinking about, Tim?” “Oh…I don’t know. What dad would say about all of this.” “I think he would’ve been royally pissed.” “Why’s that?” “Well, there you go abandoning work for love, and bringing down you brother as well? He wouldn’t like that at all!” “Yeah, I agree with that. But royally pissed…no, I don’t believe so. In fact, I think he would get over it. And, in a sense, he would understand.” “Understand? A man like him understand that you tried to coax a harlot like her back to the Nest? Really?” And I didn’t say a word. Silence was a signature to our conversations. He stared at me, and from the corner of my eye I stared back. But I had to think about it. Dad would get mad… “Nah, Liam, I don’t. Not at all.” “Well, you’re wrong.” “Remember those stories he told us about mom?” “Yeah, sorta. Why?” “Remember the one about walking into that club?” “The one where he punched that crazy n*gger in the face for hitting on mom?” “He wasn’t hitting on mom. Mom was fucking his mouth with her tongue.” We both shot a look over to mom’s grave, right next to dad’s. They were so perfect together. “I didn’t know that…” “Well, you never asked. Y’see, when dad started figuring out how in love I was with Liz, he told me the story. He said he was SO FURIOUS, he just beat that n*gger until he was unconscious. But that’s not the interesting part.” “What was?” “What was? How about mom kicking dad in the nuts? I’d say that was interesting.” “That’s fucked up.” “I know, isn’t it?” We didn’t say a word for a few minutes, taking in the story. Liam waited for me to continue, and I waited for the thoughts to come together. “But, Liam, dad obviously got back with mom. Right?” “Right.” “Well, dad did the most fucked up of things. He felt guilty. He felt remorse. And then, he went to the God damn hospital and APOLOGIZED. APOLOGIZE TO THE MAN HE ALMOST KILLED!” \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\ The room was dark. As the light turned on, the patient on the bed was revealed. He was cut, he was bruised. “Hello.” “What da fuck do YOU want?” “May I come sit down?” Pause. “Sit.” The visitor sat down, nervously. He was at the feet of the bed, and as the patient beckoned him to come closer, he scooched his way to his side. “Now, you bettah make this important. I ain’t said a word about you, and it gon’ be REAL suspicious for an Eagle like yo’ self up in here.” “Wait…you haven’t said a word?” “No man, not one. And you know why? It’s cause I respect a man like yo’self, a man who loves enough to fight for his love.” The patient coughed, writhing, while the visitor solemnly waited. “But here’s tha problem, man…you disrespectin’ that lady. You sayin’ ‘fuck you’ when you kick my ass. You let her choose…she ain’t yo’ woman! She is NOBODIES woman! She wanna be with me, deal. She wanna be with you, deal. But you better RESPECT her. Y’hear?” \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\//\/\ “That’s fucked up, Tim.” “I know.” “So what happened?” “Well, dad just went to mom, told her he respected her, and she fell in love with that. They got married, and you know the rest.” We paused again for silence, taking everything in once more. This was a hard conversation for the two of us, as it had been a long week. “So, Liam, the way I figured, if I went there and told Liz that I respected her, it’d be fine with dad. He’d tell me ‘You did the right thing, son’, give me a pat on the back, and we would never really talk about it again. I think dad would’ve respected it.” “I guess…but what do you think Liz would’ve done? Jumped into your arms and leave that n*gger in the dust?” “I did. I wanted to believe that. But, man, that’s why we’re here, because I changed and she didn’t. Sometimes, Liam, respect doesn’t end with what we’d like it to end with, you know?” “Yeah, I know.” Silence. “Well, dad, I guess that’s all we gotta say.” We both nodded, turning and walking towards the exit of the grave yard. Behind us were dad and mom, smiling 6 feet under. I’m sure they both became like that when they were buried. So that’s it. That’s the Bizwrite Saga. It’s not extravagant. It’s nothing special. It’s just a man realizing shit. What is it you must take out of it, then? Writing should mean something, and when I write something like this, pour my heart out, it should affect you some how. I loved that girl. That woman was my life. Enough of my heart was hers to spend weeks traveling across the country, only to stop shirt. I changed. But, really, once you change, it’s not okay to feel the same. If you’re going to move on, do it. And if not?
