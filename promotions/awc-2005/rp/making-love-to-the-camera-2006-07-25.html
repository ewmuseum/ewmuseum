Making Love to the Camera

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/25/2006 The camera slowly fades into what seems to be a photography studio where there are different models assembled in various areas posing and flaunting there stuff for the camera. The camera pans around the scene and catches sight of a beautiful, long-haired, thin-bodied, blonde who is smiling brightly and glancing back at the camera with her hair blowing in the wind. The camera continues to pan to the next area where a vivacious raven-haired, vixen is also posing and pouting for the camera. The camera continues to pan as it reaches the next area of photo-shooting where ‘The Violence’ Vince Jones can be seen standing there looking totally confused as to what to do with himself as he grasps his Countdown Trophy in hand. The camera switches to the male photographer who lowers his camera and just frowns at Vince’s demeanor in disappointment. He puts a hand on his hip in a rather dainty fashion, leans and cocks his head to the side. Photographer: Umm, Vinnie baby. What are we doing? Vince Jones shrugs his shoulders. Vince Jones: How the fuck is V to know? You the expert at this shit, Rapha… Photographer: (rolls his eyes) Its, Raphael, honey! Vince Jones: Yeah, whateva. (glares back at the photographer) And what’s up with this ‘honey’ and shit? You ain’t V. Jones’ woman! Raphael: Oh yeah? Well, I could rock your world better than any woman ever could if I wanted to, big boy.(winks) Don’t you forget that. Vince Jones appears to become a bit nauseous at the thought. Vince Jones: Don’t ever say that kinda shit ever again, man! Look, we gonna shoot these damn pictures and V gonna get the hell on outta this joint! Raphael: (nodding) Very well, baby. But, if we’re gonna get the best results I’m gonna need your full cooperation. Vince Jones: (in confusion) What the fuck is yo problem, man? All you gotta do is take some damn pictures of V. Jones here. What’s up with all this cooperation and shit you needin’? Just take the damn pictures and let V go! Raphael: You just don’t get it, Vinnie. I am a professional. I produce professional work. I look for the best. I make you look good. When I conduct photo shoots it is not just picture taking anymore. It is art! Now I don’t know what experiences you’ve had with operations like these but when you work with Raphael, honey, you have to be a part of the work. You have to be one with the camera. You have to make love to it if you’re gonna make it work. Vince Jones: (in confusion) What the fuck!? Make love? Raphael: (nodding) Yes, baby. You have to make love to the camera. Vince Jones turns to his right and just shakes his head at the thought of love making and cameras being associated with one another. He then directs his gaze to Raphael once again. Vince Jones: Aiight, money! We ain’t shootin’ no porno flicks or no shit like that so you ain’t gonna be seein’ V makin’ love to nada up here, mahfucka! Get it!? Got it!? Good! Oh, and quit usin’ the fuckin’ pet names! You ridin’ V’s nerves with that shit, man! Raphael: (stomps his foot in frustration) Its an industry term, Vinnie! Now work with me here! Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! You betta watch ya tone, Peter Pan! V’ll break that damn camera across your fuckin’ head you keep that shit up over there! Raphael hears this and begins to pout. He slowly walks over towards Vince and begins caressing his arm in a loving fashion that begins to freak Vince out a bit. Raphael: I’m sorry, baby. I won’t do that ever again, but will you promise to cooperate with me on this shoot so I can make you look beautiful in the mag? I mean just look at you… Raphael reaches up and pinches Vince’s cheeks. Raphael: Such a beautiful man you are… Vince slaps Raphael’s hands off of him and shoves him backwards. Vince Jones: Aiight! That’s enough with the gay shit, mahfucka! V’ll do whateva so we can get this damn photo shoot complete and shit! Fuck, anything to get the hell away from yo horny ass! Raphael: Very well, let’s do this! Raphael begins clapping his hands together and skips back into place as giddy as a little schoolgirl. Vince just shakes his head and mutters to himself in disappointment of the sight. Raphael readies his camera and smiles. Raphael: Alright Vinnie, you’re the king of the world right now. You’ve just come out of your latest match and you feel invincible. You’ve left your blood, sweat…mmm I love sweat… Vince raises an eyebrow half in confusion and half in disgust at the remark. Raphael: …anyways you’ve just won your match and you’re on cloud nine. You feel untouchable. No one can stop you. Envision that, Vinnie baby. And make that happen. Vince Jones: Uh, okay? And what about the trophy? Raphael: (in confusion) What about the trophy? Vince Jones: What’s the trophy gonna do in this picture, man? Raphael: I don’t know. Whatever a trophy does best. Now let’s make this happen. Vince closes his eyes and lets his imagination run wild for a second. He finally opens his eyes, kneels down on the floor rests his elbow on his knee with his arm standing straight up and puts rests his chin on his knuckles as if he’s in ‘The Thinker’s” pose as he holds the Countdown Trophy. Raphael just shakes his head in disappointment. Raphael: What are you doing? This isn’t a prison picture. Get up! Vince grunts as he stands to his feet and readies himself in another pose. He stands in front of the white backdrop with his feet spread apart, a smug expression on his face with the Countdown Trophy cradled in one arm as he flashes the handsign of his old street gang, the EDW, with his other hand. Raphael snaps a shot with his camera and nods in satisfaction. Raphael: Now we’re talking, Vinnie. Oh, so sexy (licks his lips) Vince lowers the shades on his face and rolls his eyes. Raphael takes another picture and chuckles. Raphael: Now you’re really getting into this. Alright. Let’s set the foreplay to the side and go a little further. Vince Jones: Foreplay? Aiight. You getting’ a lil weird with this shit, man! Raphael: Forget about that right now, Vinnie darling. You’re doing so well. Now, you’ve just defended that trophy of yours in a hard-fought battle. You love that trophy. Don’t you, Vinnie? Vince looks down at the trophy, nods, and smiles. Vince Jones: Yeah. Raphael: (to himself) Ooooooh, this could be good. ( To Vince) So, how much do you love that trophy, baby. Vince Jones: A whole lot, man. Raphael: (nodding) I see. Well, show me just how much you love that trophy. I want you to kiss it. Vince Jones: (shrugging) Aiight. V can do that for you. Vince raises the trophy to his face and gives it a quick peck on its shiny surface. Raphael turns up his lip and just shakes his head a bit disappointed with the outcome. Raphael: Alright. You’re doing nothing for me here, Vinnie. Vince Jones: (in confusion) What? Man, you told V to kiss the trophy and he kissed the damn thing. Take the fuckin’ picture. Raphael: (leans to the side and folds his arms across his chest) No, you don’t get it. Why are you kissing that trophy like you’re kissing your dear old, grandmother? How am I, Raphael, supposed to work with something like that? That doesn’t get me all hot and bothered. That doesn’t get me all worked up. I’m not feeling the mood here. You’re not showing me anything, Vinnie. Now let’s try this again. I want you to kiss that trophy like you mean it. I want you to pretend its your lover… Vince Jones: Like its V’s lover? Uh, V needs a moment to discuss this shit. Raphael: (in confusion) You need a minute with who? Vince Jones: With the fuckin’ trophy, bitch! Now the shut the hell up for a moment! Raphael: Very well then. Talk with your trophy. Raphael shakes his head and just watches as Vince turns his back and looks down at the trophy. He then looks over his shoulder at Raphael and just glares back at him. Vince Jones: Hey! A lil privacy here! Raphael turns his back and Vince goes back to looking at his Countdown Trophy. Vince Jones: Aiight. This shit’s getting’ kinda weird and all, man. This bitch made across the room (points at Raphael) wants me to kiss you like you V’s lover and shit. V ain’t quite down all that crazy ass shit. That fool takin’ things a lil too far here! (pauses and nods) Yeah. You feelin’ V on this shit too. V would whup that mahfucka’s ass, but V tryin’ to look good in that magazine and all. So, let’s just do the damn thang with these pictures and we get the fuck outta this joint afterwards. (pauses and nods) Yeah, yeah, yeah. V knows how you feelin’. Last week you got turned into an ice-cream bowl by that crazy LaShonda bitch but it was all good. V cleaned you up and had you shinin’ like new, man. (pauses and nods) Look, its just gonna be one kiss and shit, maybe two. That’s all. Aiight? (pauses and nods) Aiight we cool. We can watch your favorite DVDs and shit when we get back to the crib, man. Vince turns and faces Raphael. Vince Jones: Aiight, man. We ready. Raphael rubs his hands together in anticipation as he whirls around on his toes and beams a large smile towards Vince. Raphael: Gooood! Now go ahead and kiss it. Vince Jones slowly raises the trophy to his lips and just gazes at it. Raphael: Alright. Go ahead, Vinnie baby. Kiss it. Vince inches his face close to the trophy and then hesitates. Raphael: You’ve just defended the honor of your trophy in one of the most hardest fought matches of your career. Right now you see that trophy as if its one of your long, lost lovers. You want nothing more than to rip its clothes off right now and make sweet love to it caressing it and kissing it all over every inch of its body making it hot and wet, dripping with its own sweat. Vince appears a bit weirded out by the whole description but tries to play along anyways as he licks his lips and inches even closer to kissing the trophy. Finally Vince gives it a nice kiss. Raphael: Give me more, Vinnie. Vince begins kissing the trophy a bit more passionately as Raphael begins snapping shots from the camera. Raphael: More, Vinnie baby. Give me more. Vince turns up the passion of the kissing of his Countdown Trophy even further to the delight of Raphael who continues taking photos like a madman now nearly cooing in approval of the sight. Raphael: Oooooh yes, you’re doing it now, Vinnie. Now stick your tongue out, Vinnie. Vince raises an eyebrow at the idea, but just goes ahead with the direction and sticks his tongue out. Raphael: Yes, Yeesss! Now lick that trophy, Vinnie baby! Vince slowly begins licking the trophy with his tongue and Raphael continues snapping shots and nods his head with wide eyes as he licks his lips nearly lost in the moment. Raphael: Yessssss!! Lick that trophy up and down, Vinnie! Lick it good, baby! Vince continues licking the trophy, still slightly weirded out by the whole situation. Raphael can now be seen nearly panting like a dog with excitement over the scene. He tries to calm himself down, but it is no use at this point. Raphael: YESSSS!!! GIVE IT TO ME, BABY! GIVE IT TO ME ROUGH! Vince finally stops licking the trophy and just glares at Raphael who has now stopped taking photos, has his eyes closed while rubbing his body all over, and now looks as though he’s about to fall out on the ground and have a seizure in ecstasy. Vince Jones: Ah hell to the nah, ya sick fuck! The camera switches to the streets of New York where Vince Jones can be seen storming out the building with his Countdown Trophy in hand with a rather disgusted expression plastered across his face. Raphael comes rushing out the doors chasing at Vince with his now broken camera in hand. Raphael: It wasn’t as good for you as it was for me? Vince stops in his tracks and just beams an angry glare in Raphael’s direction. Vince Jones: Fuck no! You supposed to be a professional and all with this shit and yo sick ass up in the studio tryin’ to take advantage of V and his damn trophy! Fuck you (points a finger at Raphael) and this photo shoot! Raphael: (whimpering) But, I thought we were making magic in there, baby. Vince Jones: Magic? You crazy, bitch! Raphael: (folds his arms across his chest) That’s alright. You’ll be back, Vinnie. They always come back. Next time you do a photo shoot you’re gonna realize that we had something special. And when you do you’re gonna come crawling back to my studio begging for The Great Raphael to conduct your photo shoot again. Vince Jones: Yeah, whateva! V out this bitch! You one crazy ass, bitch! Raphael: (turns his nose up in the air) Hmph! Well, I don’t need you anyways! You were just a little something on the side! A-Rod and Derek Jeter will be here in an hour for their Yankees’ photo shoot and those are two fine pieces of man meat right there. Mmmm mmmm good, baby! Vince Jones hears this and suddenly feels as though he wants to puke. He rushes off to his car, hops in and drives off down the street as Raphael just watches the car leave in disappointment. Raphael: That was one beautiful, black man right there. I’m gonna miss him. Always loved the dark meat. The camera slowly fades to black. ----------------------------------------------------------- The camera slowly fades back into Vince’s apartment where he can be seen entering the doorway cradling his Countdown Trophy in his arms trying to console it after the rather awkward episode at the photo studio of Raphael. Vince Jones: Man, V sorry ‘bout all that shit from earlier. V should’ve known that cat was nuts when he looked at him. He talkin’ ‘bout make love to the camera and shit with his gay, punk ass. That shit wasn’t even cool, man. Vince begins rubbing the trophy and holds it tighter in his arms as he makes his way across the room towards the small kitchen past the living room space. Vince Jones: You feel as violated as V felt up in there, man? (pauses and nods) Yeah. Thought you would. Look, let’s just try and forget about all of that shit today and just chill for a bit. Vince reaches the refridgerator, opens up, whips out two bottles of Corona light, and shuts the door. Vince Jones: We’ll sit down, have some Coronas, and watch some t.v. That cool with you, man? (pauses and nods) Good. That’s what we’re gonna do. Suddenly Vince hears a rustling sound coming from somewhere down the hallway. He now hears the sound of random things being tossed about. Vince sets the two Coronas down and he motions for his Countdown Trophy to be very quiet as he creeps out of the kitchen and down the hallway towards the source of the commotion. The sounds appear to be coming from inside of his room. Vince Jones: (whispering to the trophy) Hey! Somebody’s up in V’s room, man. Shit might get a lil hectic so you betta wait here, man. V kneels down and sets the trophy on the floor beside him. Vince Jones: (whispering to the trophy still) Shhhh!! I know you wanna roll in there and whup some ass too, but V don’t want you gettin’ hurt, man. If something were to happen to you ain’t no tellin’ what V’d end up doin’. We cool like that, son. Aiight. Just wait here. V got this shit. Vince rises to his feet and creeps closer towards his door and readies himself to bust in. Vince suddenly kicks open the door and comes darting into his room. Vince Jones: Surprise, bitch! You ‘bout to get fucked up! The camera pans and catches sight of Jasmine standing in the middle of the floor of Vince’s now trashed room. She suddenly jumps in shock and just stares back at him in surprise. She drops the pair of jeans she had of Vince’s on the floor and just smiles nervously. Vince Jones: And what the fuck do you think you’re doin’ in here, huh? Jasmine: (nervously) Cleaning? Vince takes a moment to look around at his room which now has his clothes and possessions all scattered about the room in a huge mess. He folds his arms across his chest and shakes his head at her. Vince Jones: You were cleaning… Jasmine: Yeah…I was Vince Jones: (motions his hand towards the mess all about his room) Well, you’re doin’ a great job at that shit right about now, Jasmine! Jasmine: It was supposed to be a surprise. Uh, I didn’t expect you to return from your photo shoot with Raphael so soon… Vince Jones: Bullshit! What the fuck is goin’ on, Jasmine!? Jasmine: (now glaring at Vince) You know what? I don’t owe you an explanation for shit after what you did to me last week, you and that LaShonda bitch! Vince slaps himself in the forehead and rolls his eyes. Vince Jones: Oh, so all this shit’s about this imaginary bitch, LaShonda again! We already talked about that shit last week, Jasmine! V never has and never will be creepin’ with some bitch named LaShonda! She was… Jasmine inches closer to Vince in suspicion. Jasmine: She was what!? Vince Jones: She must be some imaginary friend of yours or some shit cuz V don’t know the bitch and never met a LaShonda before! Jasmine: GRRR!!!! Jasmine pushes past Vince and storms out of the room. Vince Jones: (turns towards Jasmine) Yo, you just need to chill the fuck out about this LaShonda shit and act like you got some sense for a change! V’s story ain’t changin’. There is no LaShonda! Jasmine: (shakes her head) You know what? Don’t worry! I’m gonna figure this all out! I’m not imagining anything! I’ll find this bitch LaShonda and when I do she’s gonna get it! Oh, and I’ll fix your ass too! You better believe that, V! Vince Jones: Yeah, whateva… Jasmine turns around and spots Vince’s Countdown Trophy sitting on the floor next to her. She just glares at and shakes her head in frustration at the sight of it. Jasmine: And fuck your damn trophy too! Jasmine kicks it over and storms off down the hallway and into the living room. Vince quickly kneels down in shock of her actions. Vince Jones: (to the trophy) You alright, man? I can’t believe she’d do some shit like that with her crazy ass! Must be that time of the month and shit! Vince glares down the hallway. Vince Jones: Hey! That shit ain’t cool, Jasmine! Why the fuck you do that, huh!? Get back here and apologize! Jasmine: (from the living room) NO! Fuck you both! Vince gently picks up his trophy and cradles it in his arms. Vince Jones: (to the trophy) It’ll be okay, man. She just a crazy ass bitch. That’s what she is. Jasmine: (from the living room) And quit talking to that damn trophy, V! Its driving me crazy! Vince Jones: (to Jasmine) HEY! Don’t say that shit! You’re hurtin’ its fuckin’ feelings here! The camera slowly fades to black as Vince stands to his feet and slowly walks on down the hall towards the living room.
