The Costumed Loser Fields

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/20/05 The next day... [The scene opens with Joey and Lamar frolicking in the Costumed Loser Fileds in the central section of the Island of Misfit Wrestlers. Yes, they are still wearing their carrot costumes. The sky is sunny and there isn’t a cloud in the sky. The grasses in the fields sway in the gentle breeze. They are happy and bouncing around. Joey then trips over his costume and falls flat on his face. Lamar points and laughs.] Lamar: 'HA HA HA! You silly carrot!' [Joey looks up and spits out some grass that got in his mouth.] Joey: 'Silly carrot. Carrots are for vaginas.' [Joey tries to pull himself up using his carrot powers but can't. So, instead, he decides to lay on the ground and get TANd~!] Joey: 'Boy, this sun sure does feel good.' Lamar: 'Yeah, man.' [Lamar lies down next to him.] Lamar: 'We have to show off our righteous bodies to all the babes at the next show.' Joey: 'Oh yeah. They haven't seen anyone as TAN as us before.' [Lamar nods his head.] Lamar: 'Rightio. We'll be even oranger for this show than we were for the last one.' Joey: 'Yeah. We'll have the most REALISTIC gimmick this side of Crimson O' Malec at this rate!' [Lamar turns to Joey.] Lamar: 'But isn't he one of our opponents?' [Joey scratches his crucial head.] Joey: 'I think so.' [A shadow suddenly appears above them and they shriek like little girls.] Joey/Lamar: 'EEK~!' [It's only James von Doom, who appears from under the Sun.] JV: 'What the hell are you two women shrieking about?' Joey: 'I...' Lamar: 'Ummm...' JV: 'Shut up and listen. You guys have to win that Alliance Championship league tournament, otherwise DRASTIC measures will be taken.' [Joey gulps.] Joey: 'What drastic measures?' [James smiles.] JV: 'Well, for starters, I, James von Doom, will be wrestling here in the AWC!' [Lamar and Joey almost shit their carrots after hearing that one.] Joey: 'WHAT~? You're coming here.' [James shakes his head.] JV: 'No. I never said that *I* was coming here.' Joey: 'But you just said that!' [James chuckles to himself for a moment.] JV: 'What I meant to say is that after my UWF contract expires and if the AWC becomes the newest PTC fed, then I will be competing here, that is.' Joey: 'HOLY SHIT!' Lamar: 'HOLY CARROTSTICKS!' [James cackles evilly.] JV: 'Oh yes. If someone would say, perhaps, want to team with me for the Alliance Championship series, I might just go for that.' [Joey looks flabbergasted.] Joey: 'But that means that you might have to face us!' [James nods his head.] JV: 'Yes.' Lamar: 'But that would suck!' Joey: 'Yes. That would porno suck!' [Lamar shakes his head.] Lamar: 'No. That would hooker suck!' [Joey shakes his head.] Joey: 'No. That would Monica Lewinsky suck!' [They both nod their heads.] Joey/Lamar: 'YEAH...' Joey: 'That's the worst kind of suck there is.' JV: 'But nothing sucks as much as TAN.' [Joey and Lamar both nod their heads.] Joey/Lamar: 'YEAH...' [Then, almost as if they are both struck by a lightning bolt, they suddenly realize what he just said and get hoping mad.] Joey: 'What the hell are you trying to say James? We don't suck!' [Lamar nods his carrot head.] Lamar: 'YEAH~! We may be in carrot suits, but we sure as hell don't suck!' JV: 'But you guys sure look like it when compared to the Emerald Isle. They're PWNING you guys after that crap first promo you put out.' Joey: 'What do you mean by crap? You wrote it!' Lamar: 'Yeah James! What the hell do you have to say now?' JV: 'Well, uhm...' Joey: 'Yeah. That's what we thought.' JV: 'Let me explain...' Lamar: 'No. No need to explain. We're TAN because of you. We're the most realistic tag team in existence because of you. And now, we're the biggest idiots on TV because of you.' JV: 'Well, at least you're on TV!' Joey: 'Who cares? We're going to get our asses handed to us by a better team and no one will care! Thanks a lot!' [Joey and Lamar turn to leave.] JV: 'But you're in the Main Event...' [They both turn around at the same time an give him a look.] Joey: 'What are you talking about?' JV: 'You two are Main Eventing FRESH! this week!' [Lamar and Joey high five.] Lamar: 'SAWNAGE~!' JV: 'Now, let's not overreact here. You're facing a tough team.' Joey: 'We know that.' JV: 'And you're going to have to...' [Just as he says this, they trip over each other's carrot costumes and fall into the grass. James can only shake his head as they pick each other up.] JV: 'Boys, boys. What was that?' Joey: 'A celebration.' JV: 'It looked more like a car wreck to me.' Joey: 'Shut up James!' Lamar: 'Yeah. Shut up James!' [James mockingly acts like he's scared.] JV: 'Oooo...watch out! The carrots are getting pissy!' [Lamar turns around and puts his hands on his carrot hips.] Lamar: “We are NOT getting pissy!” [James turns and smiles at him.] JV: “Now, if only you showed this much anger in the ring. Then, we’d be getting somewhere.” Joey: “We’re aggressive!” JV: “Yeah, for garden foods.” [Joey looks pissy.] Joey: “HEY~! We are not garden foods! We are TAN, the greatest team ever!” Lamar: (trying to sound tough) “YEAH~!” [James slaps them both across their carrot heads.] JV: “GUYS, YOU ARE GETTING YOUR ASSES KICKED BY A MIDGET! Let me tell you something. Their team is more refined than you guys. They know more moves than you guys do. And they are going to kick your asses. So let me tell you something. When the misfits roam free and plague the wrestling world, then you will be accepted. Until then, you are nothing more than a sideshow. Understand? A side-show tag team that wears carrot costumes and tries to act tough when, in reality, you are nothing more than jobbers. Jobbers who are under the wing of the most out of control talent EVER in wrestling, James von Doom. But that doesn’t matter, does it? Sure, your opponents can recite poetry, which I often do, and talk about angels and demons and some ‘Ghoulies’ shit or something like that. But it won’t matter. They’ve already won.” Joey: “ How is that possible?” JV: “Because you let them. It isn’t an easy thing to hear, but it’s true. So I say go out there and give it all you’ve got. You know why? Because that’s all you have. There’s no way you can with. Comprehend that and you have won. But, if you let their overjumbled psychological madness get to you, then you guys are really fucking screwed and not in the good way. So let’s get off this insane little island, go to Fresh!, and TRY to kick some ass. Are you with me?” [Joey and Lamar nod their heads.] JV: “Now let’s get the fuck out of here before the Nookie Monster comes back.] [James leads the way for his team as they walk toward the slowly setting Sun and head toward their destiny…losing in the Main Event to the Emerald Isle as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.]
