Saving the Kingdom pt 2

14 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/05 OOC: Does anyone read these? The home was large. The walls made of solid stone, the floors too. It's inhabitants, a family of seven, full of love for eachother and for their fellow man. The McSlade's existance in Pop-Tart Land ran back ages. The first, Sir Louis. A brave knight who faught to his unfortunate death against a large man eating sponge. Each generation produced great knights, but none greater than the present: Sir Kuffington. He plays in the garden with his family. His wife Lillian, and his 5 sons: Kuff Jr, Kuff Jr the 2nd, Kuff Jr the 3rd, The Other Kuff Jr, and Bill. His servant arrives through the gate, a piece of paper in his hand. 'A message from town sir,' the servant states. Kuff rises to his feet. A large man, towering over everyone. His hair fixed into dyed red and purple dreadlocks. He grabs the paper from his servant, and effortlessly tosses him over the house with his free hand. 'What is it?' his wife curiously asks. 'A menace is disrupting Pop-Tart Land. I must rid of him,' Kuff crumbles the paper and heads for his house. 'Noo!' his wife faints, over reacting to the situation. 'I will be back boys, make sure your mother doesn't go on a crack smoking spree like last time,' he says before entering the door. He moves to the side of the house, where he stands in front of a large sculpture of a nude Pamela Anderson. He grabs the right breast and presses the nipple in. The wall behind it opens up, revealing a secret room. Inside, the walls are painted lime green. A strobe light hits while a disco ball lowers down from the ceiling. Random Lil Jon songs hit while 187 1/2 topless dancers appear, the 1/2 being a midget of course. They surround him with their flopping goods. He snarls and pushes them away, but they are persistant. He runs to the opposite wall and snatches an AK-47 and begins opening rounds on the dancers. He then steps inside some type of machine just big enough for him to stand in. A door closes infront of him and the machine begins spinning slowly. He makes 4 complete turns before the door opens. Kuff steps out, now dressed in black Ecko Brand armor. He grabs a medium-sized bag and heads out. --------------------------------- The people stand in the street, all seemingly focused on the man in the middle. He wears a long, black trenchcoat with a large top hat. It is none other than Mr. Planters (the peanut guy). He growls at the people before him. 'So, you won't buy my peanuts, eh? Well, you can all just die then!' Planters begins laughing while throwing dirt. The people all scream in panic and begin running around in circles, waiving their arms. Mr. Planters uses his cane to bash the heads of the closest victims, laughing every moment. Blood spills over the ground as his havoc continues. Suddenly, he falls slat on his face. 'Ow what the hell?' Mr. Planters gathers to his feet, rubbing the back of his head. A paper football lays beside his feet. He turns to find the culprit. Kuffington stands, a grin on his face. He lets out a battle cry and charges for Mr. Planters. Planters smiles, moves to the side... and trips Kuff with his leg. 'Ha bitch!' he laughs. Kuff jumps to his feet, and spits directly in his face. Mr. Planters wobbles backwards, stunned from the sudden attack. He wipes his eyes, screaming in horrbile pain. Kuff lets out another yell, but is denied! Mr. Planters grabs his eye piece and launches it into Kuff's REGIONS~! Kuff collapses to the ground, grabbing his crotchital area. 'You, bastard!' he yells at the peanut being, grimacing in pain. 'Ah ha! I have defeated the great Kuffington! Now Pop-Tart Land will feel the wrath of my nuts!' Mr. Planters yells in triumph. Suddenly, everyone within hearing distance turn to him, gasping, even Kuff. 'What?' Mr Planter asks. Kuff laughs out loud. 'No one will feel the wrath of your nuts. In fact, you don't even have nuts, you nutless freak. You don't even have nut sack!' Kuff tells him. Just as Kuff ends his sentence, all the towns folk look at Mr. Planters' crotch area. They all burst into a hysterical laughter. Mr Planters frowns and looks toward the ground, almost in tears. 'I have a nutsack!' screams Planters. 'Look!' He takes off his hat and reveals a sack of walnuts. The people of Pop-Tart Land takes a look at the sack. 'It even has hair on it!' A little boy says to his father. 'Yes it does son and someday, your sack will have hair too.' his father answers, followed by heart-warming background music. Kuff finally picks himself off the ground, wiping the dust off him. 'Alright, enough with all this nut talk. I'm starting to feel hot and bothered.' Kuff reaches to his side for his sword. Instead of taking out the sword, he takes out a rather large nut cracker. 'Oh shit,' The peanut man mumbles under his breath. Mr. Planter takes off and runs through the streets, screaming like a girl. Kuff follows him, with the abnormal sized nut cracker lifted over his head. Mr. Planter turns around and dumps out the sack of walnuts before Kuffington. Naturally, Kuff begins to slip and falls face first to the ground. The peanut man giggles and presses a button on his cane. His cane transforms into a jet pack. He puts it on and begins to fly through the air. 'You will never catch me, Sir Kuffington!' yells Mr. Planters. Kuff stands and looks at the flying peanut. 'Damn him!' he yells, fists rasied high into the air. Kuff reaches into his pocket and pulls out a condom. He throws it to the ground and a cloud of smoke appears. After a few seconds, the smoke goes away and in place of the condom, Puff the Magic Dragon is standing in its place. 'Okay Puffy,' Kuff says to the dragon. 'Follow that nut!' 'Whatever you say, buddy.' Puff tells him, his speech slightly slurred. 'Oh no! You're drunk! I knew I should of taken that Yoshi condom instead...' Kuff decides to take a chance and he hops onto the dragon. He follows after Mr. Planters, who is playing a 50 Cent song from his jet back. Kuff takes out a big red dodgeball and throws it toward the jetpack. It plainly bounces off and falls to the land below. 'Ha! That was nothing!' Mr. Planters laughs at him. Suddenly his jetback explodes and he falls toward the ground. 'SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT!' he screams as he plummets to the ground. 'Now, I will end this. Puffy, let us land!!' Kuff exclaims, pointing downward The dragon nods and goes back down to the ground, wear Mr. Planters is laid out, slightly cracked. He coughs up some peanut butter as Kuff lands. He hops off the dragon and takes out the large nut cracker once again. He walks on until he stands over the peanut fiend. 'So, this is where it ends, huh Kuffington?' asks Mr. Planters, between weak breaths. 'Yeah. Any last words?' Kuff asks, weilding the weapon up high. 'Your momma sucks,' Mr Planters tells him. With that, Kuff wraps the nut cracker around Planters body and snaps it in two. Peanut butter flows from the broken body. 'Fatality!' announces a loud, deep voice. All the town folks come over to view the body. In unison they point at him and scream 'E-C-DUB! E-C-DUB! E-C-DUB! E-C-DUB!' 'I guess that's all she wrote,' Kuff cracks a manly smile and poses as the scene pauses, the credits beginning to roll up the screen. ... Kuff looks around after about 5 minutes, seeing the pause is over. He begins heading home when a man runs over to him, panting. His face is red, his eyes wide with fright. 'Sir Kuffington! Oh thank god I have found you at last! I went to your house, but you were not there,' the man explains. 'What is it, little man?' Kuff asks, rasing one eyebrow. Rocky chants are then heard. 'The land of Quinnville is under the attack of the evil Ill Bostwhick. King Quinn has asked for your help! We need you!' the man is still short of breath. 'Hmmm... My services are not cheap, especially for an out-of-town job,' Kuff rubs his chin, pondering. 'King Quinn will pay whatever you ask. Please! Help us!' the man lowers to his knees in a praying fashion. 'Fine. I will rid Quinnville of this, Bostwhick... Say, you didn't touch my wife while at my home did you?' Kuff asks. 'Of course not!' The man jumps up in defense. 'Did you look at her?' He asks with his head cocked to the side. 'No!' the man shakes his head. 'You lie!' Kuff grabs the man by the throat and folds him into a cookie. He pulls a glass of milk out of his pocket and dips the man cookie into it before eating him. He then heads on to his next journy.
