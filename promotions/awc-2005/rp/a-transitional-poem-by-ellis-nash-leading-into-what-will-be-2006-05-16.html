a transitional poem by ellis nash, leading into what will be next week's roleplay, because lia is tired and dead

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 May 2006 i. what i say to men now is: don’t turn me into a cliché honey, sweetheart it’s a bit too late for that. ii. here’s the progression - ugly until a rain came, blossomed up under wind, word-sounds torn away and hurried into a weather - turn, thrown out and forgotten in the way of growing, of hurt, of all those tracts washed away as the tide comes in. i’m sorry. i still can’t stop interrupting myself. iii. when i was ms. brilliant, i had no wrong - you own everything you do, remember. your body remembers your hands remember and your mother remembers even if you think she never knew now someone told me i was stupid, as i hitchhiked the transit lines back home, as i missed the train and stood out in a burst of sun over the whole wide shoreline. i’m stupid now, and all my stories are entirely original, and grand. i don’t write them down. iv. the big joke is that some men want to think they make me bad. they don’t believe i was ever ugly. sometimes i want to bring pictures. see see see see see: over and over, the mantra saves some nights for me: you didn’t make me pretty mister and you sure didn’t make me bad. v. i have talked to murderers in languages that only the ghosts breathed and knew and carried with them as they sank into the deep secret waters, the sun still sparking in their eyes, necks smooth and still and curving. i have brought down polygamists and rapists and thieves. i have sacrificed my life for this country, my state. and what have you done? vi. i wanted to be: hardened cruel, thoughtless, exciting an adventure, a mystery, alone at morning with no money and last night’s dress, forgetting names, hungover, blown out, illicit, somebody, somebody’s, nobody’s, my own, sleepless, gorgeous i am. vii. for you? it’s like a birthday surprise, a cake clean in white frosting, a song you think you know - but don’t - but like anyway - that bird your grandmother told you about, sings in a city-spring, the river showing your face the first time you look - for me? i open my mouth and it makes noises but i’m thinking of apartment prices in our fifty biggest cities you imagine yourself a teacher? Baby, there’s nothing I haven’t done. viii. this is for every man i’ve fucked: I WILL NOT REMEMBER YOU IN A YEAR ix. how i’ll fall in love is easy - i won’t sleep with you. we won’t talk we won’t drink we won’t have any white midnight together - tales we won’t have a moment shared we won’t have anything but a sudden clearing where we look at each other, smile, walk away, and realize there’s nothing purer than what has just happened. sleep well in your bed, your thousand-hundred girls. i’ll do in mine. we’ll be happy forever knowing someone better has already arrived. has already moved on. x. here, listen. i can be ten million voices. Believe me, this voice here never lies. once-upon- a-time i thought sex was evil, would hurt, would bleed. it never hurt. if i had known that then, maybe i wouldn’t be compensating. nothing hurts, although afterthoughts aside, i’d warn the princess in the cottage glen the numbness is worse - not esoteric numbness, idiot - the evening hour when your teeth disappear, and then your gums, and your lips, something eating up all your face and your so fast you can have ten million faces, all passing so soft nothing feels, you kiss men that way, you kneel down in bathroom stalls and forget where you left your purse. i guess the swan doesn’t end the story, it ends with flight, and restlessness, and being too good for yourself at long last, at the pinnacle of disgust with some things you can’t grow beyond and here, here are some names to remind the baby duck, the gray baby swan that someday honey you’re going to lose your tragedy, your brave poetic disfigurement: todd/tim/stefan/jake/andrew/mike/chandler/chad/logan/adam holy shit xi. what i say to men now is: don’t turn me into a cliché honey, sweetheart it’s a bit too late for that. what i say to men now is: nothing at all. xii. BTW: AIMZ ZUCKZ thank you and goodnight
