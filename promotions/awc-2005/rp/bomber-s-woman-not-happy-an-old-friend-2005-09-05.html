Bomber's Woman Not Happy, An Old Friend

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th Sept 2005 The British Bomber was fresh, relaxed after a week off from in-ring competition following his victory over former main eventer Ellis Nash. During the AWC shows he had been relaxing, visiting family as well as attending a few autograph signings, and also having to put up with more gay antics from Red Rock at Fresh!. Bomber had picked up a letter from the office at Fresh as all superstars do, basically confirming the upcoming card. He hadn’t yet opened the letter but had only just realised that, and decided to take it out of his travel bag and see if he had a match. Bomber had travelled back to London overnight and got some sleep along the way, so was sort of with it and sort of not with it if you get what I mean. Bomber was on the bank of the River Thames in London, he pulled the sealed envelope out of his bag and opened it up. It read as follows: “Before The Main Event” The British Bomber vs Mike Wade vs Andy M. vs Adalia Delorian Four Way Fury Match – “Best Of British Rematch From Battle Of Britain Bomber knew he was in for a tough match against three of AWC’s top guys, he convincingly lost at the Battle Of Britain after a poor showing, he knew that and he worked hard to continue to work off the ring rust for his match against Ellis Nash and was about there, but since he took his break from wrestling things had gone to another level, the bar had been raised. Things were changing quickly in the wrestling world and Bomber hadn’t kept up with the times, so he had enlisted the help of an old friend to get him with the times, someone that had been around the wrestling business constantly for the last few years. Since enlisting this guy’s help, who Bomber did not want to reveal due to legal reasons, he had gotten a bit better, more of a grip on the recent times in wrestling and picked up a few new things also. He was heading for the final session in the catch up, and picked up his bag and set off down the bank of the River Thames towards the guy’s house, it was a nice day so he also caught some sun. He arrived half an hour later and started to discuss the match with his friend. Friend: So, a rematch and right before the main event, you hit it right on the money this time huh Bomber? Bomber: You could say that yeah, listen thanks for your help over the last week, it’s been a real help to me. Friend: Hey don’t worry about it, you looking forward to it then? Bomber: Kind of yeah, I mean don’t get me wrong I am looking forward to it but kind of nervous as well. It’s a big match, and so close to the main event spot as well, I just have to get back into the flow of things properly that’s all, that will take a few more matches but I should be fine. Friend: Listen, you did well at FAW, I know it’s been a while but you can carry it on over, your good enough for sure. It’s just difficult to make the transition from one fed to another after so long, just keep working on it and you will be fine, trust me. Bomber: Yeah I suppose, cheers man. Friend: So, over to Ireland tonight hey? That should be fun, Wade will defiantly be the favourite with the fans for sure. Bomber: Yeah that’s true, but I haven’t just got Wade to worry about don’t forget, I got Delorian and Andy as well. They are three tough competitors in that match and it wont be easy, I just got to get my head down and plan a strategy otherwise I am screwed. Friend: That will be easy enough, remember that time you won the 8 man match in your first year at FAW, that took big balls man, that took planning out, how long did that take you? Bomber: Erm… I don’t know, about half an hour to an hour? Friend: There you go then, its only a four way match and that shouldn’t take long, the guys at FAW was a lot tougher than the bunch of pansies in AWC right now, so don’t worry about it and you’ll do fine, trust me. Bomber: Yeah your right, I just gotta work hard and see what happens then, listen, I know this sounds kind of crap but I gotta cut this short, the woman is bugging me that we will miss the flight later, so I got to go meet her. It’s been good to see you again after so long man. Friend: Yeah you too Bomber, and good luck for your match on Fresh, you will be fine like I said. Bomber: Thanks, maybe we can speak again next time I am in Britain? Friend: Sure, look, go before she kicks your ass. Bomber: I’ll catch you later. Bomber left going to meet his woman, would she kick his ass for being longer than expected? How would Bomber fair against Wade, Delorian and Andy, Bomber’s woman didn’t like that he was away from her for so long and nearly didn’t get this finished. Bomber wasn’t in for no loving tonight, but Bomber had a match tomorrow to worry about. Only time would tell…
