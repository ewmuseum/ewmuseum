Big Bad Bob Bye Bye BOOM!

4 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Cinco De Mayo The door to Teresa’s hotel room opens. She is sitting in a thin cushion chair watching cable TV and smoking a cigarette. Expecting Bob to return with dinner, she chokes in inhaled smoke as she watches her brother walk in. “Eric, what are you doing here and what’s with the gun?” Teresa puts out her cancer stick. She watches as Eric cautiously walks in and shuts the door behind him. A look of desperation covers his baby face. “Where’s Bob?” “He went to get lunch, why?” “I have to get you out of here. He’s dangerous Teresa.” “Dangerous? What the hell that supposed to mean? You don’t even know what’s going on Eric. He’s as harmless as puppy.” The doorknob turns. Protectively, Eric steps in front of his sister and aims the gun to the door. Teresa steps aside and pulls on Eric’s arm. “Eric! Put that down. Have you gone crazy?” ========= “So, Miss Tomas, you say the incident was self defense. Explain.” The time is about 5p.m. Teresa is sitting in a small room. In this room are a long table and four chairs. It’s your typical questioning room you see on crime drama series on TV. The walls are concrete and a two-way mirror is right beside the door so that those standing outside the door can see in the room, but those inside the room cannot see who is watching them. Her elbows rest on the table in front of her, her chin resting inside the palms of her hands. From the looks of her, one can safely assume she has been in a bloody fight. Her navy blue sleeveless t-shirt is torn in the back and the front is speckled and spotted in dried blood. Her worn Levi’s are torn at the knees and also decorated in dried blood. Her head is bandaged from her bathroom brawl with Ellis during the Fan Festival. A black eye and busted lip adds to her appearance. Across from Teresa sits a middle-aged man built like a rock and a receding hairline. The wrinkles and scars on his face reveal a lifetime of dedication outside the headquarters. A notepad rests in front of him on the table. He twirls a pencil between his fingers as he sits back in the fold-up chair. A photo ID is clipped to his jacket pocket. His name reads “Agent Daniel Riggs”. Daniel’s hazel eyes are set on Teresa as she stares at her beaten reflection though the two-way mirror. Daniel doesn’t miss a blink of the eye or the rise and fall of her chest as she breathes. Not only her answers, but her body language will determine where she will spend the night, in a bed or in a cell. Teresa takes in a deep sigh and releases slowly. Her baby blue eyes are bloodshot and tired. “I’ve already told you. He was going to shoot my brother. I couldn’t just stand there and watch it.” “And how was this self defense?” “Because, once he shot Eric, he would’ve shot me! Don’t you damn idiots get it?” A KFC bag rests on the small table of the hotel room. The aroma of chicken and biscuits fill the air. Eric stands in front of Teresa while Bob aim’s Eric’s gun at Eric. “It was a mistake to follow us Eric.” “So what are you going to do now Bob? Finish the job? I know you paid Miguel and his gang to get rid of me then day I came to your pawn shop looking for Teresa. You told me where she was and knew full and well once Miguel pulled his gun at my sister, I’d leap in front of her and take the bullet.” Teresa stares at Bob in disbelief. Seeing this in her eyes, Bob bites his lower lip. His finger teases the trigger. =========== Agent Riggs silently rises to his feet. He drops the pencil on the yellow notepad, shoves his large hands in his pant pockets, and begins to pace. Teresa watches impatiently. Her only thoughts besides what transpired 3 hours ago are get out of here, but she has a gut feeling that won’t happen anytime soon. “You pointed your gun at Mr. Turner and pulled the trigger with the intent to kill.” Agent Riggs appears to look at his reflection through the two-way mirror. He watches Teresa’s reflection for a reaction, especially her eyes. The eyes don’t lie. “I don’t know where you get your sources, but it’s not true! I was in your hospital room with Teresa when Miguel shot you. I even bought the flowers she gave you because she didn’t have enough money to buy them.” Bob says this as Eric takes a step forward. The end of the hand gun inches from his heart. Teresa grabs Eric’s arm. The color in her face leaves her pale as a ghost. Teresa reaches behind her with her eyes focused on Bob. “Interesting you mention my sources, because my main source works for you. That’s right. He is your main supplier and he also MY family. See Bob, even though ‘Cousin Darrel’ may have shit for brains, he’s not completely dumb. He knew the day I paid him a visit with the Feds, he was busted. So, to save his own ass, he ratted on you; told them everything.” ===== What Agent Riggs sees is Teresa’s eyes flare and her face hard. He keeps his back to her, watching as she rises to her feet knocking over her chair. “I’d advise you to keep your cool Tomas. An attack on a federal agent will only make your case more difficult. Are you sure you don’t want to call an attorney?” Teresa stares at the back of Agent Riggs. She clenches her fists feeling her blood pressure rise. The urge to knock this man out is held back by the knowledge of the consequences. She picks up her fallen chair and slumps down. “What do you want from me? I didn’t kill Bob for the hell of it. I was saving Eric. I’m not a murderer. And no, I don’t need an attorney.” “Suit yourself Teresa.” Agent Riggs returns to his own chair and retrieves his pencil and begins to tap the eraser on the table. He believes her story, but it’s not him she has to worry about. “Here’s the deal. You intentionally killed a man. Give me what you knew of Bob, names, contacts, anything you have and maybe you can walk out of here on your own.” Silence passes between them. The clock on the wall ticks the seconds away. Teresa grows more annoyed as Agent Riggs grows more impatient. The gun she used on Bob is hers. It is registered in her name. She even has a permit to carry it. As a loyal member of the National Riffle Association and like her American Express card, she never leaves home without it. Bob’s expression grows hard as he thinks of Darrel betraying him. He sees in Teresa’s eyes that she too has turned her back on him. It’s all Eric’s fault. They all will die, starting with Eric Dwayne Tomas. The next five seconds tick by as if they are minutes. Bob’s finger begins to press the trigger. Teresa kicks the back of Eric’s knees causing him to fall to the floor as she draws her own pistol from behind her and aims it at Bob. A fire is shot. Bob falls to the floor. Teresa is left standing with a smoking gun. ====== The time is 7pm. Teresa is sitting on a thin hard bed inside a cell. Her clothes, blood-dried and torn still cling to her perspiring skin. The air is as hot as it is humid. She sits with a slouch. Her elbows are resting on her thighs while her back is hunch over. Her still bloodshot baby blue eyes stare ahead at nothing in particular wanting nothing more than a hot shower, clean clothes and a cold beer. The clicking of heels is heard echoing down the corridor. Teresa hears but pays no heed until a pair of thin legs stops in front of her cell. Teresa looks up to see a woman in a dark blue pant suit with a light blue undershirt. She has an olive skin tone with dark brown shoulder-length curly hair and a pair of thin-framed glasses. “Are you ready?” The woman speaks in an Italian accent. This catches Teresa’s attention and she sits up giving the woman a confused expression. “Who are you?” “Call me Samantha. I’ll be representing you if you so chose, unless you find this hot jail cell more comfortable than your hotel room.” Teresa stands and approaches the woman. Her long fingers wrap around the bars that separate her from the world. She spends a moment studying this Samantha. “So, you post my bail, if I decide to hire you.” “That is correct, although, you will not be paying me.” Before Teresa can even ask any questions a tall, well built masculine figure approaches. He stands tall, over six feet, dressed in loose fitting jeans, a dark blue tee shirt tucked in, and a long, black ponytail cascading down his back. The short sleeves that ride up due to his toned arms reveal hints of tattoos; a black knotwork cross on the left arm and a green dragon on the right that covers his entire forearm, wrapping around in a circular pattern. His voice is deep and smooth. 'She isn't posting your bail, I am. You won't pay her either since I'm doing that too.' He stops in front of the jail cell and folds his arms over his chest. He stands nearly a foot over both women and just casts a narrow eyed look to Teresa. Her baby-blues go wide and her mouth falls open. 'You......?'
