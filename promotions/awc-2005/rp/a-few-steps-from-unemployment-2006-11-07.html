A Few Steps From Unemployment

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.11.06 The summer was almost gone and fall was solidifying it’s foothold throughout most of the United States. Leaves were descending from radiant green through burnt orange to their final resting point as brown slush on the streets of the nation. People were starting to get interested in the NFL again as the business end of the season approached and television was beginning to be overrun by adverts for toys and games, tempting the public to lavish each other with gifts to remember a very important baby being born. In Las Vegas though, you’d never know. In this city, time has no meaning. Minutes roll into hours, hours into days, days into weeks and weeks into months without seemingly anyone noticing or caring. The only differences between a mid-July day and the opening weeks of November out on The Strip was a slight temperature drop and some seasonal lights. We’re talking 93 degrees instead of 102 though, so nobody even really noticed that and as for the lights - the whole city is just a big ball of neon anyway, so the addition of a few giant flashing angels and Kris Kringles were barely noticed. Well, not by Josh Marquez anyway. When you’d been in this metropolis for as long as he had, minor details like the festive season approaching didn’t even register. It took something big to shock him or grab his attention. Like a new casino, or a juicy homicide, or that insanely hot new cocktail waitress at the Wynn. Actually, now he thought about it, why the fuck did he agree to meet his accountant here? Granted the women working here weren’t exactly unattractive (definitely not like that meatball down in Miami), but the legs on Wynn-Girl…damn!!! Too late to change now though, looked like he was just going to have to put up with the toga-sporting Caesar’s waitresses, the Roman numeral price list be damned. Honestly, sometimes these places just took the gimmicks too far. Still, he had a good view of the sport book and the Mojitos weren’t half bad. When Jeff (could you get a more accountant-like name?) called him and said he’d be even later than he’d anticpated, Josh accepted it with a sigh and another Mojito. He’d been waiting for around an hour and a half now and he was pretty sure that the seven empty glasses on the table belonged to him. Damn strong Mojitos. He had just started on number eight when the sport book display changed. The 16:15 race had just finished and…oh for fuck sake! That’d be the last time he put money on a horse recommended by Hector. So engrossed on the screen was he that he didn’t see the two men come and take a seat at the table next to him. He sure as hell heard them though. Two fat, greasy, loud, obnoxious, armchair sports fans, the kind who slate Vick for not being ‘a real quarterback’ but whose idea of exercise is getting up and walking to the fridge to grab another block of lard to eat. The sort of men who wear ball caps and replica uniforms despite having the athletic ability of a three legged warthog on Prozac. In other words, the complete opposite to Josh Marquez. If he’d been sober their constant too-loud chat would have pissed him off but with the copious amount of alcohol he had running through his body he took it with surprisingly good humour. Sure it was irritating to hear those fat bastards waffling on about shit they knew nothing about, but it was also kind of funny. Mainly because they were so pathetic. So he sat there, sipping the Mojito, listening to them discuss the wide world of sport. Unsurprisingly, Mr Controversy, Terrell Owens was high on the agenda. Was he a good signing? Could he make that much of a difference with Bledsoe not producing the goods? The usual crap that fans talk about. Then they moved onto basketball - is Wade better than Lebron? Is Shaq past it yet? Would Steve Nash make an all-time starting team? Then, without any warning, they moved to pro-wrestling (I know, what are the chances?!). Obviously GTT was the main topic of conversation, who was the favourite now? What fed will do the best overall? Josh was vaguely interested by this, though it still wasn’t exactly gripping, where the hell was that accountant? What? Rewind a bit. Did they just say Fresh? More to the point, did they say Fresh and Josh Marquez is in the same sentence? This week? This got a lot better. “Thing about Marquez is he’s lazy. He’s got all the tools, just he never seems to use them.” “Oh yeah man, he just can’t put it all together. Take that thing with O’Shea, he managed to get in a great position then let it slip away.” “He was number one!” “I know, that’s what I mean. You seen this week’s card?” “Yeah, talk about a demotion. It’s obvious that he has no chance of qualifying.” “And that’ll be him off the pay-per-view. He’s just falling down the ranks.” “I give it a month before he’s gone.” “He’ll be lucky to last that long.” These guys knew jack shit about the business but this really hit home with Josh. If average Joe’s like this could see he was under performing then the powers that be sure as shit could. Efforts would have to be doubled. “Your Mojito sir.” “Actually, I’m alright.” And so it began.
