Fight, flight or forni-- wait, that wasn't how it went. Maybe Captain Sully would know...

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: march 24 What's the number one thing that guarantees Darcy Markson won't sit next to Amy Campbell? Whether she's air-drumming to Metallica or not. And, through the entire flight back to California, the elderly lady sitting beside her had to put up with a mimmicking of the unenthused drum stylings of one Lars 'Hey look I'm in Metallica' Ulrich. It was fine, since she'd ignored him and even travelled to the airport separately, despite saving him and then... well... trying to kick his teeth out. They were the epitome of a love/hate relationship, really. They'd loathed one another on sight, but neither could deny the attraction for too long. He was the rough and tumble, but together older brother type, only too physically appealing to remain in the 'friend zone' with her. She was the tattooed girl with the killer attitude, and the only girl he'd met who didn't think Operation Ivy was just the name of a secret military experiment in botany. Obviously, they had a lot more between them than just those basics, but the chemistry was undeniable from the moment she suckerpunched him years before. Sparks flew between them, sometimes shrouded as violent acts... they brought an energy to one another that was occasionally misused - like in cage matches - but always ended with her having to get away from him. At least, that was the case when she was otherwise spoken for by a boyfriend and boss in wrestling. Amy lost her cool in front of him, but in a very different way than she'd ever experienced with the run of the mill assholes who wanted world titles. He'd always piqued her interest, and her boyfriend at the time - Eddie Osbourne - hated that. Eddie saw the backwards glances that Amy never noticed she was giving. He saw every stare that darted to Darcy during staff meetings, and the sudden lapse in her attention span when the man known then as Crisis filled the arena monitors. Needless to say, fatal attraction won everything in the end and the two brought down an entire federation by turning their backs on the people who'd owned Aimz. Their pairing was a fantastic thing for both... for a few months. ' 'Master of Puppets, I'm pullin' your striiiiings'... I love this song...' Five years later, they were still riding in the same planes. Just like when they screwed over the entire upper layer of the cheddar federation PSW, Amy and Darcy had both made rash decisions, riding entirely on in-ring whims. It had all ended with her sacrificing herself to keep him from getting injured by an enraged Jack Murphy who, coincidentally, was also her tag partner for the affair. Afterwords, she promptly rearranged Markson's jaw with her sneaker. She almost constantly found herself wishing that she could react less to him - that maybe he wouldn't have such an effect on her, and as a result she wouldn't have to go through feelings of hurt, mistrust and the worst of them all... missing him when he wasn't around. After a four year hiatus, one would expect her to be a little more impartial to Markson, but she wasn't and wouldn't ever be. All the feelings from those years dragged her down when she was away from him even for a second. It was unhealthy for her, and she'd never actually tell him, but she found herself missing Darcy in every stubborn, silent moment. 'Stop looking! Screw his dumb ass, I'm not gonna waste a second wanting him over here when all he'd do is try to book some kind of t-shirt sale-a-thon.' For the duration of the flight, she was feeding herself the same crap. Don't miss him. Don't think about him. Don't look his way. Of course, she was glancing over her shoulder with every passing crash of the cymbals in her headphones, checking on Markson. He was peacefully asleep and completely oblivious to her presence. It gave her an extra moment to look him over, and the bruise that was already forming on his jawline put an aching in her big toe - not to mention the bigger one in the left side of her chest. Something deep down inside was ill, perhaps the fact that they'd been infuriating one another with mixed signals for weeks. Having to fight him again was one of the most trying things she'd had to endure in years. A cage match in a week would be peanuts compared to the mental preparation opposing Darcy had required. Cages... Mexicans... a hob--wait, a hobo? What've I gotten myself into... When the flight finally landed, she didn't even see him in baggage. Their eyes never met, their voices never touched the ears of one another. They'd no doubt meet again within a half hour, though - you can't enter the same home and not lock eyes. But for the time being, she'd lost sight of him in a sea of elderly people and college students with California dreams. Her hatred of airports grew exponentially when she had to trek through them alone, and if it weren't for the fact that she needed to get her car out of the paid parking to drive it home also without company, Amy would've hit up one of the airport bars in an instant. I never lose in a cage. There's too much to fly off of, and just enough to knock their skulls on without it being illegal. Why worry? The drive home was entirely uneventful. She found herself astounded by what other people could do while driving; There was a lady putting on eyeliner, a businessman typing on a laptop, a girl managing to talk on a cell phone AND fiddle with her Blackberry, people eating and lighting cigarettes with two hands... it quickly reminded her that she paid more attention to the on-road wannabe socialites than the pavement itself. Within minutes, though, she was turning off of the freeway and toward the beautiful beach, one step closer to a good and well-earned nap. Darcy's car was nowhere to be found when she pulled into the driveway, which was halfway expected since he had a penchant for driving like an old lady with a few beer in her. It was fun enough to entertain the thought that he'd stopped to get her flowers or an overstuffed teddy bear for saving his backside between the ropes 24 hours before, but the truth of it was that he'd probably just been sitting in traffic for the past while as a completely unoffending and cautious driver who'd never dare to pull out and pass another vehicle in hopes of returning seconds early. It is a lot of metal, though, and I'm pretty tired... She blew past the front door and set her bags down beside one of the livingroom's leather sofas, kicking her sneakers off just as she collapsed onto the softest thing she'd felt for days. The TV seemed to offer nothing, but she was feeling like silence for once in a blue moon anyway. Her eyes shut, and she was soon fighting the urge to pass out for a few hours and ignore Darcy's return entirely. A few minutes into the redhead's battle with all-encompassing nappage, her hypersensitive ears picked up footsteps coming up the walkway, then someone's clicking attempt to open the door. She looked at the clock and was surprised to find more than an hour had passed - sleep had won - so she groggily crept toward the door to greet the intruder. ''He never did figure out how to use a key,'' she groaned to herself. Amy had hoped he wasn't hurt. She'd spent most of the previous night with the trainers, trying not to be seen as they treated him but constantly checking up on any unforseeable diagnosis. They'd said nothing was out of sorts, she'd indeed saved him, and he'd just be a little sore in the morning. Caaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaages... Opening the door in her second act of kindness toward the man she could've killed a day before, the tiny redhead chuckled a little at how wide his eyes grew when she dipped into his chest for a tight hug. The silence she'd so craved crept up on both of them for a fleeting minute, but he soon had to speak in what was no doubt some cocky, overly male defense. ''Amy, I...'' She smiled slightly into his shirt, completely contradicting the emotion in the embrace, ''Fuck you.'' ''...What?'' ''Fuck. You.'' ''Look, Amy...'' She felt no inclination toward hearing his argument. In fact, Amy had refilled herself with the bitterness she'd experienced throughout the fight, ''How could you do that to me? How could you put me through that? How could you make me watch you get your life… your CAREER, Darcy! Your career get squeezed away from you, and put it all on my conscience? Seriously, could you be any more selfish?'' He was silent, and she was ready to smack him. The chain links of a steel cage lined her skull, and silence would only make it worse. ''Say something, for chrissakes!'' '' Things...'' he stuttered, ''Things kinda got out of hand. I admit it. And I honestly don't know what to say or do at this point, except to say thank you.'' Amy sighed, ''Try again. You tried making me responsible for what happened to you in that match, even if it was by putting me on the other side. You didn't think the fucking CHAMPION wasn't gonna try to cover his own ass? He was determined to win, at any cost. It seems like you forget I'm the only person in AWC who gives a damn about whether or not you're wheelchair bound. I almost wonder if you didn't expect me to save your ass in a pinch - it was a guaranteed win if your partner wanted the victory and half of the opposing team wanted to see you come out of it okay.'' ''But...'' she continued, ''I don't honestly think you're that smart when it comes to me. You just wanted to see me squirm there as some kind of sick fucking revenge. You've got problems, especially when it comes to responsibility. You just tried to pull off putting everything on MY chest, and minutes later your lips are smacking saying you really care and thanking me for taking the brunt of your bullshit. I don't get you. Not at all.'' Darcy's shoulders slumped, knowing she was completely in the right. He could talk until his voice ran hoarse about how they were both wrestlers, who wrestled, and when their paths would cross their relationship changed nothing. But if their relationship knew boundaries... what was the point? ''I got carried away. When I promised that the old Darcy Crisis was gonna start kicking ass like it was 2001, nobody was more excited than me. And then that night, I was seconds away from beating the champ. It was intoxicating, Amy... that's what this business has done to me for so many years. I was THIS close to earning my spot back at the top, THIS close before you took it away from me. I should have known it wasn't quite as malicious as I was making it out to be, but I was flying on adrenaline. I made a bad decision and I was forced to go through with it. And you're right, it hardly even occured to me that I put you in harm's way to satisfy my thirst for glory. I can only thank my lucky stars that you're not quite as sadistic as I am.'' She laid back against the couch and scoffed at him again. There'd been enough crap between them to fill an arena with trashing tension, but this was the thick of it. So, she reacted accordingly. ''Enough of the fake thanks. I keep taking the punches here, and even though I've been so close to pullin' out to win, I kept taking your shit. No more. You're playing with my life, trying to recreate your glory days, and I'm not gonna take it for another damned second. The only decisions you make are for your own personal gain, and god forbid you ever admit to caring about me, but I'm gonna put this on YOUR shoulders. You tell me, what the hell is your intent with me? I've put myself on a platter and you've only taken what suits you... so, is that the way it's gonna be, or do you wanna go about things like a grown-up for once?'' Amy sighed again. She leaned back and shut her eyes again, enduring a calming silence until Darcy had a retort. There was no intention on her part to speak again until he stepped up to the plate and answered her like a man, so her eyes stayed closed. Darcy moved to join her on the couch. He ran his hands slowly through his long dark locks, taking a few moments to search for precisely the right words to say. ''You know, Amy... up until now, you haven't exactly made your intentions forthcoming either. But I understand now... you tipped your hand last night. You made your priorities clear as crystal. I owe you the same.'' He started shaking. She was pretending to be half-asleep with eyes still shut, but it didn't stop the little redhead from easily sensing the tension and feeling building within the man she'd known longer than almost anybody. It was hard to fight off a smile from forming, because she knew he was getting ready to be completely and brutally honest, good or bad - but without pussy-footing around as usual, for the first time in a long time. ''You have...'' It was on its way. Part of her wanted to jump up and urge him to continue. ''You've no idea how hard this is for me. But it's time you know. Your selfless act opened my eyes. I put you through hell and you somehow found it in your heart to show me compassion. And in that, I know now who I truly am. My name... my name is Darcy Markson. And I love you, Amy Campbell, madly and stupidly. I love you first... and Darcy Crisis second. That's the way it's going to be...'' he swallowed hard, ''...that's the way it HAS to be. And that's the way it's going to stay, until you give me a reason to feel otherwise.'' 'holyshitholyshitholyshitholyshitholyshitholyshitholyshitohmysweetjesusfuckingchristonacupcakenoooo no no, don't open your god damned eyes, he'll... bah, you're already smiling - stupid bitch!' Amy's eyes shot open, then squeezed shut for a moment to comprehend exactly what the hell Darcy had just said. She was trying desperately not to show that her heart was dancing harder than Beyonce, and managed to keep a relative appearance of calm that he most likely saw straight through... but it was worth a try. ''...Took ya long enough. Five damn years, sheesh!'' The only words she had, completely tactless. At least it hadn't changed her. ''You know, I totally let my guard down over here...'' he'd said, ''Don't you think it might be nice to offer up the same courtesy?'' ''I said it already, four damn years ago!'' she argued with a slightly sarcastic smirk, but soon relented, ''Fine. Darcy, I...'' Amy chuckled, ''I love vodka. And puppies. You're pretty alright too, I guess.'' Markson sighed almost instantly. ''Oh, come on. I love you. I love you waaaaaaaay too much more than I should. It's unhealthy, honestly, and the only reason I've put up with you for this long. If you're really good, and give me money, I might someday be inclined to have your babies,'' she sighed, ''And I've loved you since I was eighteen. How's that for having no life?'' He smiled, and she returned it... but looked sheepishly toward the floor. This is the part where, in the movies, they'd suddenly embrace and make torrid love on a table, with angels and fireworks and the like. While Amy envisioned the Hollywood ending, however, Darcy slipped back into his boyfriend-ish role and flopped onto the couch, switching on the TV. ...So much for relaxing her body after a tough fight. ''What... what're you doing?'' ''Believe it or not, I actually have STUDYING to do. Captain Sully really knows his shit. I’ve got from now until the bell rings to cram my head with as much useless knowledge as I possibly can.'' Preparing for a match? Already?! SHE WANTED TO PREPARE TOO, DAMNIT! Her idea of a workout just didn't involve sitting in front of the History Channel. ''Good God… don’t tell me you two are going to have a TALKING match. What is this, the internet?'' His arm was around her, and he laughed for at least a minute - causing a reverberation in her own light chuckle - but took no steps toward... well... anything. She was almost ready to ask if he was going to make ANY kind of move, when he started talking again. ''All the same…it’s too late to back out now,'' he sighed, ''And I’m NOT going down in my first pay per view match in five years just because I couldn’t figure out when the second Crimean War took place.'' 'Maybe I'm acting like a teenaged boy, but we just did that whole mutual 'I love you' thing, and I haven't gotten any in... oh God, I don't even want to start that count.' ''Nuts to that,” she finally growled, standing up and grabbing Darcy by the wrist, “It’s time for your first of many study breaks.” She led him to the spare room he hadn't really entered since she moved back in, with the determination not to spend another night alone on that ungodly uncomfortable mattress. If there was a cage match ahead of her, she'd be ready for it... after all, women burn like a million calories in bed, right? Who the hell says you need a gym to train for a match? I don't need to lift weights to make me take a shitload of steel to the face. ...And on the plus side, if she played her cards right, there might not be enough blood in her upper body to cause any serious gashes in the cage!
