alright depression?

23 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd December 2005 The time is 5am in the morning, and the night time tags in day time as a new day is beginning. We find ourselves in Boolie's run down 19 year old black van. Red Rock and Boolie have just returned home briefly for Christmas after Red Rock's GTT5 victory. Red Rock is sat in the passenger side and he is fast asleep, he is wrapped up in a big green parker jacket and is leaning against the window with a small glimmering trickle of dribble oozing from the side of his mouth. Red Rock is completely exhausted and totally burnt out. He has been going hither and tither from America and the UK with his Wrestling and personal commitments. He still has to look forward to one trip over to Germany before he can enjoy Christmas. Red Rock looks ever so peaceful as he drifts further in to a deep sleep. It is a change to his usual agitated persona. When he sleeps he feels safe and nothing can bother him, that's why he enjoys it so much. Boolie on the other had is wide awake, in fact he is buzzing on 12 cans of red bull and just as many handfuls of sherbet he purchased in bulk just a few days ago. They are currently driving down the M25 on the way to Gatwick Airport where they will board their flight to Germany. Red Rock will be fighting against Tim Shipley and from his mental state it doesn't look good for him. He's never done so much traveling in his life. Before joining AWC he'd never been abroad and this is something that he hadn't yet adapted to. Red Rock shuffles around in the seat a little bit just as Boolie notices a sign for a service station. Boolie indicates left and heads up the slip road leading to the car park. Boolie: Hey Red wake up! Boolie grabs Red Rock's shoulder and pulls him and pushes him to wake him up. Red Rock's eyes spring open as he gasps in shock. He wipes the trail of drool from his face and looks around with a look that would be likened to a lost puppy. Red Rock: Where are we? I was having the most wonderful dream where I was dead! Why did you have to wake me up? Boolie: We're at a service station, I REALLY need a wee wee. Red Rock: Oh...righty o. I could use a sandwich I suppose and I think the kids want to go to the pool. Boolie: What kids? Red Rock: never mind... Red Rock and Boolie walk through the large car park. Because of the early hour there are not many cars. The area is surrounded by tall trees but they don't block out the sound of the cars zooming down the motor way. Red Rock and Boolie walk up the stairs to the front door, they stand in front of the door expecting it to open before Red Rock realizes that it isn't an automatic door. Red Rock: Piece of shit! Just inside the building gives the view of all the shops and the main floor of the service station. There is a Macdonalds and a KFC, an arcade, a café and a shop. Boolie: Right I'm going for a wee! Red Rock: I'll go get a sandwich then Red Rock and Boolie separate. Red Rock heads over to the brightly lighted shop. He looks around at the various items looking for something that tickles his fancy. He is instantly attracted to the fridges where all the sandwiches are. He reaches out for a BLT sandwich but is appalled by the price. Red Rock: £3.99? THOSE FLEASING BASTARDS! Red Rock drops the sandwich back where he found it. He can't be dealing with spending much more than he needs to. He has always been a little bit tight with his money, even when he had it. And now his tattered financial where he is facing eviction from his less that glamorous home, which is constantly visited by debt collecting agencies. It is a matter of time before the bailiffs rear their ugly heads his way. So he can't be wasting his money on trivial things like food, electricity and gas. Red Rock sighs and tries to ignore his grumbling stomach and heads over the other side of the service station where there are tables laid out for the customers of the café where he will wait for Boolie to finish pissing. He sits down with a miserable look on his face, he remembers the time when he was 18 years old and such a strong willed person. He used to never let things bother him and he used to make people laugh and always smile. But now he is living in poverty without gas or heating, his TV is about to be cut off and he hasn't paid his rent in months. There isn't a bank in the world who will touch him, let alone lend him any money. He just isn't the person he once was and for this reason he is sinking further in to depression. As Red Rock spins the ash tray on the table in circles trying to entertain him Boolie creeps up behind him to try and scare him. Boolie: BOO~! Red Rock jumps out of his chair and his heart rate booms. Red Rock notices it is Boolie and rolls his eyes before slumping back down and burying his face in his hands. Red Rock: Piss off Boolie... Boolie: Sorry... Red Rock's lack of humor in the situation creates an awkward silence, sort of like the one you get with a girlfriend who doesn't understand you're humor when you make a joke. Boolie: Hey Red, smile matey it's not that bad. Red Rock: I have nothing to smile about... Boolie: It's Christmas in TWO days! Red Rock: Marvelous...I get to give gifts to people they won't appreciate and receive gifts that I don't want.... Boolie: OK, well you can look forward to your match against Tim Shipley. Red Rock: You know, I just this second remembered about that. I don't really think you understand how much shit we're in at the moment. Boolie: I wondered what that smell was. Red Rock shakes his head and he buries his head further in to his arms as he mutters to Boolie. Red Rock: Sometimes I wish I was like you Boolie. Boolie: totally sexy? Red Rock: No, a care free moron who doesn't understand the pressures of the real world. Boolie: It is pretty sweet! But still a shot at the frontier title though! That's pretty good don't you think? Red Rock: Well I suppose so...it might help our money problems a bit, we'd be out on our arses if I didn't win the livewire title before and the bonus for that has just run out. The rest of the money I make is going towards paying off other arrears and shit. To be honest I hope Tim Shipley kills me. Boolie: Oh Red come on, don't think about it like that. Go in there and give it a shot, it's not like you have anything to loose so go in there like you're a desperate hobo fighting for a scrap of food! Red Rock: Our situation isn't ACTUALLY far from that Boolie. It slowly begins to sink in to Red Rock's cluttered mind just how much is riding on this match. He isn't thinking about the glory that will come with the Frontier title all he wants is to make enough money to make ends meet. If he wins the bonuses and prospects of endorsements that will follow will be the God send Red Rock needs to consolidate all his horrific debts. Red Rock: Well... I suppose I might as well give it a go, we better get on our way or we'll miss the deadline for check in. Boolie: Yeah its only like an hour and half away. Red Rock picks his burnt out and tired body off the chair and heads towards the exit with the thoughts of how this final lift line could be his only chance to keep his life together.
