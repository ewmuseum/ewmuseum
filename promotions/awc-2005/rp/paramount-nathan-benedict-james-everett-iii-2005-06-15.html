'Paramount' Nathan Benedict/ James Everett III

15 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/6/05 EXT.CORPORATE HEADQUARTERS OF JAMES EVERETT III.DAY Nathan Benedict's limosouine pulls up to the carport under Everett's prestigious Headquarters in NYC. The driver, anonymous, routinely opens Benedict's door in front of several unsuspecting civilians. At the sight of Benedict... Woman: OMG! Nathan Benedict!!! The streets surrounding Everett's skyscraper suddenly erupts into a frenzy. Benedict, attempting to avoid his lowly followers at any costs, quickly runs towards the guarded doors of the HQ. Fans: Wait, wait! Benedict: (Before walking through the doors) Your right, how rude of me. Nenedict pulls out a pen and signs a quick autograph, not even including his whole name, but his two initials. He tosses it into the scavenger crowd, which convulges on the piece of paper. Benedict:(Walking away) Rodents... The inside of Everett's lavish buildings include plush leather couches for executives, golden name plates for supervisors, and hundreds of drones at James' disposal. Benedict draws more than a few stares from the worker bees, but nonchalantly makes his way to the eletavor, where he pushes the Signature Chairman button when he is interrupted by the elevator boy. Elevator Scum:Umm, Mr. Benedict I know you may be a little tired from shooting your movie and signing many autographs...but my daughter...she is such a huge fan of yours. I took her to see Bellamys Charm the first day it came out. We had to have gone like 3 times that week. I know it would mean the world to her if you could give her your autograph. He holds out a brochure for Everett's business, the only item that he his holding. Elevator Scum: If you can make it out to Grace that would be wonderful. Benedict stares at this man...this man, who works long hours day after day to support his wife and daughter. This man, barely getting by on his nine to five, barely able to keep it together after all these comes to Benedict for a little salvation. If he were to present this autograph tom his daughter, the joy and exhileration that she would feel would no doubt transcend itself onto him, making his life a little better... Benedict:You see...here is the dilemma... Benedict quickly looks at the floor monitor which reads 25. Everett's office is 34. Benedict:Whenever I encounter a fine fan like yourself, who requests my signature, I find myself facing the same problem over and over again. 26th 27th 28th Benedict:If I were to give you my autograph, for your daughter, and the next gentlemen were to see this. I would be forced to also give him one. That would set a chain... Quickly looks at the floor monitor once again. 29th 30th 31th DAMNIT! Benedict:of events that would keep me occupied signing my name all day. Now if I were to do that, how would I be able to accomplish anything else on my important agenda. Soon, my autograph would be meaningless...you understand right? 33rd 34th Elevator Scum:Well sir... DOOR OPENS Benedict:oh I am sorry, this is my destination. Benedict walks away without even contemplating turning around. Everett's office starts with two tall wooden doors outlined with golden trim. The letters JE3 border the door's mammoth appearance. Two knocks then the entrance, signature Nathan Benedict. Nathan Benedict: Hows business James James Everett III: You know, corporate buyouts, conglamorate strangleholds, pretty much the same as always. Nathan Benedict: Nice to see the passion is still there. James Everett III: Speaking of passion, I was skimming through the AWC programming guide this week and it appears you are to be in your first match. Nathan Benedict:Wait...there's a programming guide? James Everett III:This isnt a nickle and dime corporation we are dealing with Nathan. Sure they pale in comparison to what I currently own, but do not be over confident, they appear to be serious in their endevours. Nathan Benedict:James please. James Everett III: Look you arrogant pretty boy fuck, I dont care what you think. All I care about is succeeding in what I have put my hard earned dollars towards. You convinced me to believe in you, and I have. You better not fuck this up. Nathan Benedict:No you look you third generation beneficiary. I wasnt born with the silver spoon like you were, I had to earn everything I got. You think maintaining my good looks and physique is easy? You must be fucking kidding me. I dont give a rat's ass who they put me up against this week. Period. James Everett III:Actually, it is two people you will be fighting. (Everett twirls a pen in his right hand.) Nathan Benedict:And... James Everett III:And...I will need your signature to allow me to accompany you ringside. Nathan Benedict: James, I appriciate the kind gesture, but I think I will be just fine on my own. James Everett III:Listen to me you stupid piece of middle-class shit. You WILL sign this paper or all of your deals, all of your appearances, all of your FAME...will be cancelled. Don't you EVER forget who runs this show! Benedict lets out a slight groan and signs the paper. The puppet master always wins... View Nathan Benedict 's Biography
