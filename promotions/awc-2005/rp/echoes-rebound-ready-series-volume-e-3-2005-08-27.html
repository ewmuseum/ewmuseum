Echoes Rebound (ReADY Series Volume E:3)

27 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Breathing a sigh of relief, Yagami Kyosuke walked down the long narrow hallway leading towards the library. Though shielded behind his silver framed spectacles, his eyes held matching luggage filled with bricks. The worn look on his face managed to give the librarian a polite smile, laying his hand on the counter in front of her as he passed. Miss Ozaki glanced back at her, peering through her own glasses to watch the young lad approach the door to the computer lab. She smiled warmly, turning back to her book inventory while she reached for her cup of coffee. She was such a sweet person with faith in her students; she knew everything would be fine in the end. Well… beyond the point of Hibiki Jue’s death. Kyosuke closed the door behind him gently to not disturb those within the lab. It was early in the day, third period wasn’t upon the campus yet. He walked down the aisle to the back computer and sat on the one on the far wall in the corner. With eyes closed narrowly, he plugged his flash drive into the USB port and pulled up the word processor. Those fingers went to work with swiftness and precision: In the light of current situations, the campus can do nothing but mourn the life of one Hibiki Jue, third year student, class B. Death is nothing but natural in life; some would say that the meaning of life itself is to very much die. It’s our destinies from birth, yet we never know when it comes, and we mourn when it befalls prematurely. Hibiki Jue was very loved by a number of students, and each of them is taking the loss in their own way, though every way is troubling. -=-=-=-=-=- Title: “Echoes Rebound” Subtitle: ”In Conclusion…” -=-=-=-=-=- “I think I’m dealing with it very well. Kirima Nagi, dressed in her school uniform, had her seat firmly planted on the edge of the roof with her back facing the 4-story drop potentially awaiting her. Her chin was tilted towards the sky, her thoughts running amok with the clouds. She leaned back with her hands gripping the edge, pushing herself out with her long limbs to the furthest extension possible. It looked like she was trying to fall back and kill herself, head tilted back with her eyes closed. Maybe she liked the feeling of sitting on the edge of life and death… maybe she actually wanted to cross over at some point… but there was something holding her back, something holding her here.
