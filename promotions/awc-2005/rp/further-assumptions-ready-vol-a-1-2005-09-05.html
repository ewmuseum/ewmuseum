Further Assumptions (reAdy Vol a:1

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kirima Nagi Age… 18. It’s without question to say that the girl known as Kirima Nagi is definitely one of the most intimidating young women in her time. Standing at 6 feet tall and weighing 11 shy of 150 pounds, she appears to be a lanky, awkward young lady. The scowl on her face often suggests that she herself has been trying to cope with her body’s measurements, and thus she stays still a lot of the time, to avoid movement of any kind. A third year student for a second time, the other students have learned to keep their distance of Nagi quite quickly. On the first day of school a few girls decided to pick fun with Nagi, calling her a freak and other names of the nature. Two of the girls left with cuts and scrapes. The other three left with busted lips and black eyes. And the ringleader… well she didn’t come back to school for a month or two. That gained Nagi a few weeks detention and a month in school suspension. After that incident, no one has said a word to Nagi that might start up her rage yet again. At least they try not to say anything in earshot. “Whaaaat??! Are you crazy?” one young student asked another, looking on at him with shocked eyes. “Not likely,” the other student turned out to be Saotome, who looked back at his friend with confidence. “I will do just as I’ve said.” The previous year, Nagi was expelled from school. She had come to school one day brandishing a switchblade, and had whipped it out against a teacher when they had bumped into her. Aside from spending a night behind bars, she was kicked out of school for the rest of the year, and it was only through sheer persuasion by her relatives that she was reinstated into school… though it wasn’t the school she was in before, it was still a school that would accept her. “You’ve got to be out of your mind!” a third student joined the conversation. “Saying something like that is suicide!” Saotome looked back at him, a slight smile coming across his face slowly. “Is it?” Word spreads quickly, that’s a definite fact. Rumor was quickly verified with that incident with the girls encountering Nagi, and her reputation turned yet again into the terror it once was. But there was an equal truce… no one dared bother Nagi, and in exchange she kept to herself. But as with any truce, there are those who test its limits. Chibi brought up the rear, he himself had no idea just how mad Saotome was. “You can’t be serious… she’ll kill you.” A dirty look came from Saotome, the other two with him also matching gaze. “I don’t recall asking your opinion, Chibi-chan. If you don’t think it’s wise you’re more than welcome to walk away.” “Don’t you listen to reason?” Chibi protested, his brown furrowing at the sound of that name. “I’m not gonna stand around while you put us all in danger. If you say that to Nagi she won’t just kill you… she’ll kill us all!” “I gotta agree with the runt here,” the first boy said looking at Saotome. “I mean I heard that she was kicked out of her old school because she killed her father. Look at her! Her father HAD to be HUGE!” Saotome couldn’t believe what he was hearing from what was supposed to be his friends… aside from Chibi. “Look, I will do this because I have to. I won’t sleep easy knowing that I haven’t done this yet. Say what you want, you can’t stop me.” Two out of three students looked at each other, and shrugged their shoulders before grinning at Saotome. “Sure,” the second one nodded. “It should be a thrill anyways.” Chibi of course was the one third left over. Saotome patted his two accomplices on their shoulders. “You’re good friends. I know you won’t fall SHORT on me.” Glancing down at Chibi, the three of them turned their backs on him and walked off. Chibi glared at Saotome, tiny little fists balling up at his sides. Nagi had a habit of wandering around the Shibuya after school, decked out in a leather bomber jacket, a fitting black cutoff t-shirt, and tight blue jeans with boots and kneepads and shin guards. No one knew what she was doing, but she would go from place to place, stopping at certain spots and sitting there. She’d stare out at the city, watching the people pass her by before moving on to the next place, almost as if she were patrolling the area herself. Saotome had learned of a certain spot she stopped at nearby their school. It was a clear patch of blacktop near the train station that was originally planned as a walkway, but no one used the area because it was too far off everyone’s destinations. There were two main paths towards the train station, and they met up in the circle right before the stairway leading up into the platform. This blacktop path was off a ways, and had its own stairwell leading to the back of the platform, where no one desired to go. Saotome had planned to await Nagi’s arrival sometime around 5pm, and then he would continue on with his plan, how reckless it may be. Kirima Nagi didn’t take long; she was rather prompt with her wanderings Saotome had mentioned to the two boys before. She stepped into the area and stopped upon finding intruders in her space. It was in direct violation of the truce drawn up between the monster and the peasants… though there was nothing actually drawn up in paper; it was more o less common sense not to stir a silent beast. Cutting across time, Saotome is now sitting on the ground, leaning against a brick wall with a white t-shirt and black gym shorts. In the distance the sound of his fellow students could be heard participating in physical education, but he himself had just finished his own developed lesson. Maru’s head lay resting in his lap, one arm wrapped around Saotome’s waist while the other ran up the length of his leg, tickling at his leg hairs slowly. As was now common in the presence of Saotome, she was graced with a look of pure euphoria. And as was also common, Saotome didn’t look like he had a care in the world for this girl. “We should get back to class now,” Saotome stated flatly. Maru whined through pressed lips, eyes closing tiredly. “Mmmm, I don’t wanna.” Saotome didn’t give her the option, standing up abruptly. Maru’s head slid down his legs, catching herself before she could hit the ground. She rolled over onto her elbows, watching Saotome fix his shirt and shorts back into neat order. She stretched her milky legs out in front of her before she started to push herself up. “Here, gimme a hand.” She reached up for Saotome’s arm, grabbing him by the wrist. Gritting his teeth, Saotome jerked his arm back, and Maru halfway to her feet fell back on her ass again. She cried out before looking up at Saotome concerned for him rather than for herself. “What’s wrong?” she asked urgently. Clutching his arm, Saotome glared at her silently. Pushing herself up to her feet, Maru looked at the arm he held before looking at him tenderly. “There’s something wrong… your arm’s been bothering you for a while now. Please… tell me.” The sound of flesh colliding with flesh cracked through the air. While it did not hold as erotic a tone as we think back on, it was still something that could catch breaths from the throats of most people. Right after that sound, a young boy hit the ground, lying on his side with a yelp of pain leaving his chest. Saotome looked away, his face taking on the emotion of shame. One day, me and my friends were walking home from school… and we… we ran into Kirima Nagi…” Feet were scuffling against the black asphalt; a scuffle was afoot. From a low vantage point showing only from the knee below, three pairs of legs clad in blue slacks and assorted sneakers surrounded one person, wearing black knee high boots. They seemed to have that person surrounded, and bit by bit they were closing in on their prey. The booted being jumped into the air as they neared; when his feet hit the ground, all three surrounding figures staggered back. Quickly the booted person rushed in for the nearest person, fists whipping through the air smacking into the boy’s body. One leg left the ground, kneeing up into the person. A groan left that boy as he fell down to the ground, coming into view as a youth with brownish red hair spiked short. Maru looked on concerned as Saotome continued. “Katsumoto was talking, and didn’t see Nagi sitting over on a bench… and he didn’t actually say her name, but he said something that she thought was for her.” “Uh oh…” Maru knew what would happen next. Another boy tried to come around and grab the booted boy from behind. That didn’t seem to work though, the heel of the boot going into the soft toe of the sneaker. The boy hopped back, shifting all his weight on one leg, and just as he did the booted boy kicked his heel into the side of that boy’s knee and with another smack of fist to flesh, that boy twisted around and fell flat on his face. Now there was only one boy left with the booted boy… the two standing across from each other. There was a pause for only a couple of seconds before the boy rushed at his opponent. That… proved to be useless, as the booted boy stood there just until he got into range, and tripped him, coming down with him in a reverse armbar. Both of them were clear as day now in view, the boy with slicked brown hair now messed up from the skirmish. And the booted boy… … He wasn’t a boy at all. “She was… no, she is an animal. Nothing more about it. Katsumoto and Otomo, they only got beaten up. She did something to my arm though… well, you’ve seen it.” Clad in a pair of tight blue jeans with black kneepads and black knee high boots along with a black leather jacket and gloves, the black haired girl with the tomboyish cut pulled back on the boy’s arm, her elbow embedded into the back of his to push outward on the joint. He gritted his teeth in pain as she pressed in harder, a stern look piercing into him. “Oi,” she muttered in a low voice. “Now.” “OK!” he yelled in pain. “I’m sorry!” “I’m sorry,” Maru said, leaning her head against his shoulder hugging him. “I didn’t know.” “Well yeah,” Saotome rolled his eyes while she couldn’t see him. “You wouldn’t. No one really knows that Nagi beat us up aside from the three of us.” Making sure he learned his lesson, she reeled back on his arm once more to make sure the pain would linger before releasing him. She stood to her feet and brushed herself off with an aggravated look. Behind her the last boy rolled onto his back holding his arm, eyes narrowed with pain etched on his face. He started to push himself backwards away from the girl, sitting up against the wall looking at her with a strange expression. Most men would be downright pissed by just having their asses handed to them by a girl… and the other three guys might just be that way. But this boy… there was a look of intrigue on his face, even a bit of respect. As she began to walk away, he called out to her. “Nagi!” “But something has to be done about her. I mean, if she’s losing control over things that didn’t have anything to do with her, then there’s no telling what she might do if something actually does piss her off rightfully.” “I heard she killed her father,” she said, leaning off his shoulder and looking at the ground. Then her eyes came up to his. “I’m glad that she didn’t do anything more drastic. “But…” Saotome’s magic was beginning to go to work right here. “I feel rather dishonored, getting beat by that girl. Though she’s no longer a mere girl but a demon… nevertheless…” She stopped, sighing as she glared back at him. He smiled weakly, pushing himself up with his good arm and legs against the wall. “I see… I can see now why they call you the First and Last. You’re the worst student in the entire school… but still… you’re also the best.” Nagi, as he called her, kept her eyes focused on him as he struggled to stand there in pain. Then without a change in her cold gaze, she responded: Maru stared into Saotome’s eyes, the two of them locking in. Her eyes didn’t move from his as she made up her mind. “I’ll do it.” “You’re still making assumptions, Saotome… don’t you know anything?” Brushing a bit of hair back behind her ear, she turned and walked off briskly. “The best is yet to come…” “You’re the best.” Saotome gave his girlfriend the first words of praise since they started dating. Maru looked up and smiled. “Well don’t worry. Your secret’s safe with me.” As she passed by the first young man that had fallen to the ground, he looked up slowly, eyes wide at the scene that acted out in front of him. This girl… she just took on the odds and in turn went against them. She didn’t back down… she would fight no matter what. The admiration shimmered in his damp eyes before they were lost in his tears… As everyone else, he had assumed Nagi to be the bad guy… but this moment in life now proved that he was wrong. There were things much more worse than her… lurking out there under the guise of goodness.
