Ask it anything, because it loves you dear

15 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.15.06 Alright folks…let’s get something straight – Ego death is not an easy affair. There are quite a few people around the globe who consider themselves “hardcore” for various reasons. Some like to jump out of airplanes at 30,000 feet. Others chase tornados barreling down the landscape at lethal speeds. Still others crave gratuitous violence, be it in pummeling a shirtless adversary in a street fight, breaking each others’ bones in martial arts contests…even spiraling dives through flaming tables from twenty feet in the air. Roller coaster junkies, stock car racers, off-road venturers, even heavy drinkers all attempt to lay claim to the title of most hardcore, most extreme, most willing to laugh in the face of danger. But none, mind you, NONE compare to the resolve of the psychonaut. It is difficult to describe exactly what that resolve entails. Is it bravery? Is it recklessness? Valor? A sheer lack of willingness to continue participating in reality? All I know is this: I’ve no reason to be afraid of a man whose idea of living on the edge is punching others in the face or jumping from a building harnessed to perfectly reliable safety equipment. But a man who is willing to have his mental defenses stripped away from him, to lose his identity and place within the confines of our mere three dimensions to face whatever might await him, a man who truly allows his ego to perish in effort to expand his knowledge of the universe – that’s the man who would make me nervous to stand against. Of course, this was all familiar territory to The Nameless One...but this knowledge was not acquired with any sort of ease. His first few tries at this had been somewhat difficult, to say the least. Ego death, in its more powerful forms, literally feels like death; the emotions one experiences in this state are so bizarre and profound, so unfamiliar and unique that one truly believes that the only possible way they could have come to know them was to cease living. But with time, it became easier for The Nameless One to accept the rules of transcending reality. It is certainly nerve-racking to lose a sense of oneself, and to be convinced that your life is winding down in front of your very eyes. But with practice, one can accept these terms as inevitablities. The Nameless One, as evidenced by the cryptic title, did not have a name. The Nameless One did not know its birthplace or origin. The Nameless One only knew that it had once had these things…and when the time came, it would reclaim them all. Where and when remained a mystery, but The Nameless One felt secure in letting this suffice with the awareness that the mystery would eventually be solved. This information, in effect, resembled a very important key that would allow The Nameless One to travel the whole of the universe if it wished, within the confines of a spiritual realm locked to most others. While frightened to do so at first, The Nameless One would warm to the idea of utilizing this exclusive opportunity. Without any notion of prior experience or past history of this kind of trek, The Nameless One floated effortlessly through the cosmos knowing only that this was not its first time in this realm. The Nameless One had no destination at first. It is hard to have one in mind when one hardly even has a concept of self. After all, how can one have a future with no past to guide them along the way? But another, far more apparent explanation for The Nameless One’s difficulty in obtaining a destination for its voyage was that there was simply so much to take in, so much that left it in awe. As The Nameless One floated along its path it witnessed various spheres of hulking rock, concentrated gas and mammoth chunks of ice, all of various intensities. The Nameless One hovered toward a simply enormous ball of gas, able to pass right through it as it bared witness to the gas giant’s never-ending chaotic lightning storms. From there The Nameless One came upon an immense sphere surrounded by rings of ice and rock that looked nearly as sharp as the blades of a powerful saw. Floating on, The Nameless One then saw a smaller version of the previous orb, with similar rings that surrounded it perpendicular to the former; curiously, almost as if the sphere had been titled to its side. The Nameless One knew not what to call any of these things, but as it glided away from them it began to recognize them for what they were. Planets, The Nameless One thought to itself. It did not know what the word meant, but was able to distinguish them as separate entities from the rest of its surroundings it was traveling through. The Nameless One also understood that it, too, called a planet home. Whether it was any of the ones it had just seen or one that remained in the distance was still shrouded in secret. With this knowledge now able to be drawn upon, The Nameless One continued floating on its interstellar trip without paying much heed to these planets, knowing that in time its final destination would likely be the one it called home. There were a plethora of miniscule illuminations blinking at The Nameless One, burning fuels to illuminate themselves from great distances away. These were so far the most favorite entities that The Nameless One had observed so far. These illuminations dotted the vast expanse surrounding them, providing The Nameless One with a truly remarkable spectacle surrounding it from every direction. Again, The Nameless One knew not what to call these things – only the wonder that they inspired in it, a wonder that would surely be shared by anyone or anything capable of laying their sensory organs upon them. However, one light shone brighter than the rest. It was much larger, too. The Nameless One was able to reason that this meant it was closer to it than the rest of the tinier illuminations. Intrigued, The Nameless One now targeted its sights on this larger, shinier object and propelled itself in the appropriate direction. As it drew nearer The Nameless One was overcome with a sense of familiarity. There was no way to describe or understand why it was feeling this newfound emotion…but with so many questions that needed answering, it stood to reason for The Nameless One - who was now beginning to reason more clearly and fluidly – that a source of familiarity would perhaps lead to those questions being answered. *** From the moment Adam Dick laid down his monumental TransAtlantic challenge, Darcy Markson knew he’d screwed up big time. It would be easy to convince himself that being caught by total surprise with such a challenge was the reason for his poor showing in his title encounter with the Illustrious Face Eater. After all, Dick’s choice to put the TA strap on the line against the Darcinator was more than likely not a spur of the moment decision. He’d very likely spent a good deal of time mulling over the decision before springing it on Darcy at last week’s Fresh! That mulling time was indeed likely utilized by Adam Dick to physically and mentally prepare himself to obtain the precise amount of focus and intensity necessary to get “into the zone,” so to speak – a realm that true champions find a way to enter en route to prevailing in the battle for professional wrestling’s most ultimate prize. Darcy, on the other hand, was completely unaware that his star had rose enough to take shape as a blip on King Dick’s radar screen. Dick may not have known that Darcy Crisis was, in fact, male – but he knew Darcy Crisis was a potential threat to the Adam Dick monarchial reign. Better to get Crisis out of the way early with a seemingly spur of the moment title shot, one that the self-styled “D. Masta C.” would be a fool not to accept. But this excuse was not good enough for Darcy Markson, not by any stretch of the imagination. Following his AWC pay-per-view debut and victory at Bloodlust, Darcy utilized the subsequent sabbatical to get as far away from the sport as he could. Physically, Darcy sorely needed the rest – his back may have been healed from the life-changing injury, but it still remained to be seen if his back could again endure the unavoidable wear and tear that came with his return to full-time wrestling. Mentally, both he and his counterpart Amy Campbell were also in need of a break. Following an appearance by Aimz at an AWC house show, both the Red Raver and the Darcinator seized the opportunity to abandon the monikers lavished on them by the wrestling world and fled to Spain, giving them a chance to flee the public eye and be what they both greatly desired – merely Amy and Darcy. It had seemed like such a wonderful idea…that is, until Darcy found himself challenging for Adam Dick’s TransAtlantic title. Suddenly, the break that Darcy had given himself in effort to be in ideal condition for marquee competition down the road worked against him now that said competition was immediately thrust upon him. Had Darcy still been in his early 20s like Adam Dick seemingly was (apparently the Face man believed himself “unbornable” – in reality, Darcy would not be terribly shocked if Facey was unable to buy himself a drink in the States), he might have been able to bounce back from his wrestling sabbatical in championship form. But it was now 2006; Darcy was approaching 30; and he’d only been back for several months after originally being forced into early retirement. Thus, even though he would have been a fool not to step up to Dick’s challenge, he was thoroughly embarrassed at the hands of the champion. But this was not something that sat well with the man called Crisis. He knew his work was cut out for him, and he went into the match more pessimistic about his chances than anything else…but he was not prepared to be embarrassed as badly as he was. His lone (lone! Darcy couldn’t remember another match where he’d fared so poorly that he only had ONE) offensive maneuver was, albeit one of the sickest of bumps, still a desperate slam to remove King Dick from the ring after several failed attempts at regrouping on Darcy’s part. Dick was quick to recover and put Darcy away immediately – it was almost as if he’d been toying with Darcy all along, and there was nothing that Darcy could do to put a stop to it. He was a man who prided himself on working harder, being better prepared and most importantly having more desire than his fellow competitors. To be so handily and completely decimated at the hands of Adam Dick screamed in the face of all of Darcy’s ideals…and the most frustrating part of all was that he knew there was no one to blame for himself. These thoughts all weighed very heavily on Darcy’s mind as he relentlessly pressed hundreds of pounds of weights off of his chest, within the confines of the small workout room of his Southern California abode. After all, Amy had been right all along: if he wasn’t working for AWC to perform to the very best of his abilities, he had no business working for them at all. Speak of the devil, Darcy thought as he saw an upside-down version of his girlfriend enter the room. She’d been uncharacteristically quiet the past few days, almost as if she’d been avoiding talking to Darcy following the both of them coming up short in their title matches the previous week. He wasn’t sure if that was indeed the case, but for his part Darcy was almost too ashamed to approach her as well. Not only had he been embarrassed by Adam Dick, he’d also failed to prevent Dick from ruining Aimz’ match as well, having been unceremoniously laid out by Chainz, yet another jackoff intent on ruining her life. Indeed, it was a sad situation for the two who had set out to take AWC by storm working together only to find one of them injured and the other with a tarnished reputation, but Amy’s appearance seemed to suggest that things weren’t quite so grim. “Need a spotter?” Amy asked, approaching the bench press table on which Darcy was lying. “I’ve got it. But thanks.” Darcy replied in between breaths, not even turning to look at her as he continued pressing the bar away from his chest. “Well take it easy, would ya Muscles? You’ve practically been at this all day. We should go do something…there’s no way I’ve seen all the clubs in SoCal.” Even Canadians get suckered into calling it “SoCal” Darcy thought, chuckling to himself. However, the joke quickly came and went as Darcy pushed the bar off his chest one final time and replaced it on the rack above him. “Normally I’d agree with you. But obviously ‘taking it easy’ was what made me look so stupid in the first place. So if it’s alright with you, I think I’ll pass,” Darcy said, wiping his brow with a nearby towel. “Alright Darcy, this is stupid. So you didn’t win your match…so what? Neither did I. Life goes on, you know.” Darcy glared at her. “Oh, that’s right…I guess I did technically win.” “And you had more than one offensive move, too,” Darcy replied, with a tone of voice that was practically cutting. “You might be content to sit back, but I’ve clearly got a lot of work to do if anybody is going to take me seriously.” “I know it looked bad, Darcy,” Amy said, gently massaging Darcy’s shoulders from behind. “But you’ll bounce back. You should have more confidence in yourself.” “This I’m getting from the girl who just last week promised she was going to rip Ellis Nash’s throat out and take her title. Last I checked, she still has both,” Darcy replied, still cutting. “What the fuck, Darcy? Are you trying to pick a fight?” Amy shouted, ending her generous massage. “Because yeah, I’m pissed about what happened last week. I’m just smart enough not to DWELL on it. But if you want me to vent that anger, I’ll be more than happy to take it out on you. Ass.” Darcy turned to face her. “Alright, alright…can we just not talk about this? Nobody has seen me wrestle more than you, you know how badly I handle losing. Especially when it was an atrociously bad match.” “Then we’re going to talk about something else. Something else you probably don’t want to talk about, but I’m tired of being kept in the dark about it.” Darcy raised a brow. “I’m not sure I’ve kept you in the dark about anything, Amy.” “Except that every time I ask, or even allude to it you find a way to dodge the question,” came her reply. “Do you happen to know who won the Oilers game the other day?” Darcy asked, with a smirk. “6-3 Oilers,” Amy said, not missing a beat. “And the only reason I know that is because every time I turn on a damn TV in this house it’s automatically on ESPN. Or Skinemax, you perv. See? You always do this. It’s time to fess up.” “Alright, shoot. I got nothing to hide,” Darcy answered, cracking a smile for the first time in what seemed like ages. It was to be short lived. “So, after we left PSW, and you left me…where did you go for four years?” *** It wasn’t long before The Nameless One arrived at its new destination. Amazingly, despite how brightly the object had shone from such a distance, it was not nearly as large as the planets The Nameless One had just visited. It was much closer in size to The Nameless One itself; it had just come to grasp the body it occupied – while it lacked a solid form, The Nameless One could perceive what appeared to be limbs, ending in finely formed digits, coming out the side of a slightly larger support structure. Atop that lied a distinctly separate appendage of which the Nameless One’s thoughts seemed to emanate from. And this, it was able to reason must be why I was drawn to this…thing. For it too seemed to have a body quite similar to The Nameless One’s. It was a little larger – perhaps twice as wide and four times The Nameless One in size. Yet The Nameless One did not feel dwarfed in this object’s presence. It seemed to glow warmly as it held out its symmetrically matching limbs, almost as if it were welcoming The Nameless One into its presence. “I. Do. Welcome. You.” came a deliberate voice from the top of the object. The Nameless One watched in awe as the object began to take a clearer form the closer The Nameless One got to it. Long, thin shards protruded from the top of the object, though they were not rigidity of solid objects. They flowed to and fro almost as if it was the hair of a powerful warrior flowing in the breeze. And as it came into focus the object began to take on delicate features. A face began to emerge from the object’s top appendage, then a neck, and a curved torso. The Nameless One had only one thought – it was meeting the divine. “What…what are you?” The Nameless One spoke aloud, suddenly aware that it had the ability to do so. “I am Lavyxana’Tova,” replied the entity, more clearly than before and now having taken on a completely feminine form. “And you are Paica.” *** “…after wrestling was gone, and then you were gone, I became pretty despondent. I started to feel like there was nothing left for me. At 23, my life had already peaked. And I wanted no part of the ride as long as it was downhill from there.” Amy felt a sense of concern…but not too much concern. “We’ve all been there, Darcy. But we don’t all disappear off the face of the planet for nearly half a decade.” “Well before things turned too depressing,” continued Darcy, “I decided that it wasn’t that nothing was left for me…it was that nothing was left for me here. So I pretty much picked up and left the country. Didn’t really bother with goodbyes…I wasn’t sure I was coming back, and I didn’t want to have to deal with a bunch of tear-filled farewells.” “Where did you go?” Amy asked. “Anywhere. Everywhere. I basically lived out of a suitcase for a couple years. I convinced everyone that tried to keep tabs on me that I was doing research for a book I planned to write. But really, I was just trying to find a reason to keep going.” Amy sat down next to Darcy, who was now staring down at the floor. She could tell this was hard on him…but she was desperate to know where he’d gone. “Any specific locations you can give me?” she asked. “India. China. Israel. I didn’t- I mean, I don’t really consider myself religious, but I wanted to know what makes these people believe in something so strongly. I met a lot of amazing people. Jewish soldiers, Buddhist monks, holy men from practically every continent. But they couldn’t give me the answers I was looking for. They all told me the same thing, in different languages: I wouldn’t know peace again until I quelled the fire inside. I asked them how to do that, but none of them seemed to know. None…except one.” “Who?” Amy asked. “I met a medicine man in Peru. I could never pronounce his name…I just called him Bob. Bob and his family and some other native families lived in the rainforest out there, totally removed from the modern world. Bob told me to try living with them for a little while, so I could see and experience for myself how they lived. It seemed like a longshot…but the only other place I could think to go was back to California. And I knew nothing better lied there.” “So you went with him?” Darcy nodded. “It was truly amazing. They lived as if they were just another living creature sharing the rainforest, the same as all the life surrounding them. I admired their connection to nature. But it was still more of the same. I couldn’t live like them…there would always be lingering doubts about what I left behind, still the pain shivering down my spine an ever-constant reminder of what I felt was an ugly divorce from my destiny.” Amy grimaced at his choice of words. “But just before I packed up and left, Bob asked me to try some of his medicine. It was a some kind of tea, that…induced dreams.” *** “You’re hurt,” the entity, Lavyxana’Tova declared. Paica, formerly The Nameless One, knew not what she meant by this…but he could feel that it was true. He nodded. “Physically?” asked the entity. Paica nodded, again knowing it was true without understanding why. “No…” Lavyxanna’Tova replied, gently reaching forward and through Paica’s transparent chest. Until now the former Nameless One had no ability to feel anything it encountered, nor they it. But now, Paica could feel that this striking entity had a hold of something inside of him. Him! Paica thought, knowing it to be true. And this entity was a her…and whatever she had gripped inside of him, it somehow released an uncountable multitude of emotions from inside him. Love…Grief…Despair…Awe…Pride…Jealousy…Rage…Joy…Sorrow…and Fear. “It’s…too much,” Paica gasped. “Make it stop!” “I cannot make these things go away,” Lavyxanna’Tova declared. “Not once you know them. But I can help you control them, Paica. I can help you sort through the chaos in your heart. But you must promise me something.” Perhaps as a sign of good will, the crisis in Paica’s mind began to diminish. But it was replaced by something Paica had not previously felt; doubt. “Can I trust you?” “That is for you to decide, Paica.” Paica swallowed – again a new ability. He looked to his left and could swear, even though he had been floating through the vacuum of the cosmos, that before him lie a canopy of trees beginning to block the dotted heavens. Time is running out, Paica thought. I’m returning… “What do you want me to do?” Paica asked. “You are about to take leave of me, Paica. And when you return, you must promise me that you will follow your heart,” Lavyxanna’Tova answered, as she squeezed his heart once more. “Remember that you once allowed yourself to trust in it. And remember that you can again.” *** Amy stared at Darcy, wondering if he was going to continue. Darcy shook his head vigorously and did so. “And…and that’s all I can tell you. To be honest, I’m still trying to work out how I got back here. But I promise, I don’t regret it one bit.” Amy smiled. “Well look. I know it will never sit well with you what you’ve been through, both last week and the last few years. But as far as those royal jackasses are concerned, they want us to be pissed at what happened. They want us to doubt each other and fall into despair so they can safely talk shit from a distance.” Amy now took Darcy’s hands into hers. “But we’ve been through worse, Darse…much worse.” Darcy sighed. He wished he could tell her the full story – what he saw, how he knew that he would forever be unbalanced without her back in his life. He may not have been able to trust her…but he made a promise. “Now that I think about it,” Darcy said, sporting a smile that would live much longer than the last, “…we could use a night on the town.”
