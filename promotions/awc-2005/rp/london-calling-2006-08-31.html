London Calling

31 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
''Mornin'.'' When Amy stretched under the sheets, she was reminded of her nightmare - the pain in her side was beyond terror. ''... I see I still got shot.'' She groaned and continued, ''Hey, can you grab my Tylenol 3 from the drawer over there?'' Rising from his seat in a nearby chair, Darcy moved to honour her request. But as he felt the cool plastic against his sweaty palm, he could feel the calmness in him rapidly vacating. He took a deep breath, but on exhaling he knew there was no going back. Nothing could quell the anger inside him now. He turned to look at Amy, tossing the bottle in the air before catching it again himself. But then, without warning, Darcy gritted his teeth and hurled the bottle as hard as he could into the opposite wall. It unfortunately didn't shatter on impact as he'd hoped, but the point was still made. ''Jesus!'' Amy fell out of the bed, guarding herself behind the mattress at Darcy's sudden outburst. She grew up nervous around men lashing out, and Markson's sudden home-run with her pill bottle sent her nearly to panic, and on guard as best she could be. ''What the hell was that about?'' ''If you haven't figured that out by now, I'd suggest you lay off the fucking painkillers for a while,' Darcy growled. 'You know damn well what this is about.'' The redhead did know exactly what the outburst was about. She pawed her way back onto the bed, covering up slightly with a sheet to shelter herself against Darcy's eyes, ''I told you, ask Jane for fuck's sake! It was something I said to Jeph that he took too far... and I'm the one who got shot for it!'' ''I'm AWARE of this,'' Darcy shot back. ''And I've played it cool for as long as I could. But the reason you got shot is because you're so goddamn psychotic. You nearly got yourself killed, and you nearly got ME killed. But worst of all, an innocent fucking girl got caught up in all of this because you evidently have no ability to keep your emotions in check.'' ''I'm sorry for showing up somewhere to save your fucking ass! Jane and I went there because you were going to get YOURSELF shot. YOU endangered ALL OF US by not getting the police involved in any of it. You think you're some bigshot hero who's going to save her career by putting the situation in your own hands? If I didn't have her, she would've been LONG dead before you ever even got your fucking skull screwed on straight. I can't speak for her, but being unemployed is a plus over BEING SIX FEET UNDER,'' she screamed, ''And you didn't think I would get involved... you were ready to kill people over your EX, and I'M THE ONE WHO TOOK THE BULLET FOR YOU IN THE END. Literally! Why the fuck am I even in your bed right now? Maybe I should leave your dumb ass to take the girl you really think is worth it.'' Darcy's eyes seemed to gleam an almost sickly shade of red. Without thinking, he picked up the chair that he'd been sitting on previously, hurling it against the wall as well. Unlike its plastic pill-holding counterpart, the wooden device splintered into several pieces upon contact. ''Fuck you, Amy! FUCK. YOU! If, at any point, you had had the balls to tell me what was really going on, nobody would have had a gun pointed at them. But you didn't. I can't imagine why, except for the sick little jealousy you have over a girl I broke up with eight years ago. You kept your mouth shut, and all I could do was search like mad for this girl under the circumstances she gave me, hoping to fucking CHRIST that I would find her before the coroners did. Don't you fucking put this on me, or on her, or on anybody else but yourself. And if you think that bullet wound is going to make me feel guilty, you better fucking think twice. That's your penance for fucking things up in the first place, but it sure as hell doesn't clean up this mess.'' When the chair flew, Amy backed off... but her mouth didn't. ''I deserved to be SHOT? You piece of shit...'' ''Maybe you did, Amy! Don't you fucking get it?' Darcy harshly replied, confirming that he didn't take the injury quite so tragically as she. ''I'm tired of this shit. Every time something goes wrong, every time you feel the LEAST bit threatened, you fly off the handle. Jane was in my house for ten fucking seconds before you tried to rip her throat out. You wanted her dead, Amy. Admit it. That's why your slum ass fuckface friend pulled this shit in the first place. You wanted her out of the picture, and for what? This was already hard enough on me as it was, just to see her face again. But you had to make things 11,000 times harder than they needed to be, to serve no purpose except to make you feel a little bit better inside. So fucking EXCUSE me if I feel a little bit better inside that you came out a little worse for wear.'' The redhead coughed, trying not to groan from the pain in her side, ''If I fly off the handle so much, and you swear I wanted her dead... don't you think I was perfectly capable of making that happen? If I really wanted her dead, I could've just thrown her to the mob. I could've done it myself, for christ's sake! Use your head for once.'' ''What the FUCK does that have to do with anything?'' Darcy fired in return. ''I'll answer that for you - not a damn thing. It doesn't matter what your intentions really were, but this allllllllll started because you couldn't get a grip. And it went on because you couldn't find it in yourself to tell me what was really going on. I can't take this shit anymore, Amy. The jealousy, the lies, any of it. You went too fucking far this time.'' ''Jane was SAFE with me, you prick. I didn't want an innocent person to have their life ruined, either, and I SURE as fuck didn't trust her in your hands. Call me dishonest all you want, but you're the bumbling idiot who nearly got all of us killed. YOU are the reason I have bullet fragments in my side. I get you your career back, then fuck up ONCE by trying to protect you AND your motherfucking ex girlfriend, and then you stand here telling me I deserved to be shot? Go fuck yourself. You wanted to 'save' Jane to sickly justify your place in the world, not because you gave a shit. You just wanted that little badge of fucking honor, but your bullshit would've gotten this house burned down while we were sleeping. By having her here in the first place, you put everybody in DIRECT DANGER. They could've taken MY family, Darcy. They could've kidnapped me in exchange for Jane. Did you ever think of that? OF COURSE NOT, because Darcy Markson needed to wear the big boy pants so he could feel special outside of a wrestling ring. This whole relationship seems to be a fucking wayside joke to you, but you're the clown in it all.'' That's when the floodgates really opened. Amy wasn't one to cry in front of anybody, but she couldn't take saying another sour word without remorse. Remorse that Darcy apparently didn't share. ''Wow.'' Several seconds passed. Darcy's jaw was practically gaping as the anger in the room was so thick, you could cut it with a knife. ''I can't believe it. You just said four or five things in there that couldn't be farther from the truth. You think I wanted ANY of this? You think I threw everybody in the line of fire so I could be a fucking hero? I went in ALONE, Amy, because I had to. I didn't ask you to come, I didn't want you to come. I wanted to save Jane because I realized that yes, I actually do give a damn about her...as a human being. The only joke here is that you couldn't turn the blinders off and see that I can care about somebody without it having anything to do with our relationship.'' Darcy paused. The shouting had disappeared from his voice, but his forthcoming words carried more punch than anything he'd screamed at Amy thus far. ''You don't even know me,'' Darcy said, ever so softly. ''And I can't believe you wanted to get married...'' That's when Amy snapped. Tears grew fast in her eyes, but she tried as hard as she could to keep them back. His constant rejections hurt, but nothing had ever been more powerful than the pain of him mocking the moments where she had showed him more of her true self than ever before. She clawed her way across the bed, stood up in front of Darcy and slapped him as hard as she could. It was open-handed, and stung his cheek - but only made him angrier. ''You didn't mean that,'' Amy whimpered, ''I know you better than that...'' And that's when it happened. Without thinking, Darcy bared his teeth like a mother lion who had encountered a marauder wandering too close to her cubs. In a fluid motion he violently shoved Amy back into the headboard, where she impacted with a sickening thud. Instantly he regretted it; instantly he knew this had gone too far, and his only instinct was to rush to her aid, to take it all back and console her until she cried her eyes dry. But he couldn't. He couldn't even apologize. Instead, he uttered the only words that could possibly apply. ''You better back the fuck off, Amy.'' For the first time in his life, Darcy watched Amy react with outright fear to something. It was nearly heartbreaking when, the moment after her skull cracked against the headboard, she recoiled and nearly flew to the opposite corner of the room, wide-eyed and trying not to sob. She curled her body up in guarding, but the tears couldn't hold back for long and eventually she was sobbing against the wall. ''Please,'' she begged him for some unknown act, ''This isn't you, Darcy. I'm sorry, for all of it. I'm so sorry...'' Darcy just shook his head, in equal parts both to her pathetic apology and for his own actions. ''Fuck this. I mean... no. Just fuck it. I can't take this shit anymore. I can't stand that you make me feel like this. I feel like my brain just went to a million pieces. I don't know who's the bigger low-life... me for putting my hands on you, or you for driving me to it. But this isn't healthy. And I can't take it anymore.'' With that, Darcy turned and made his way for the bedroom door. ''Where are you going?'' Amy asked, with glossy eyes. ''I don't know. And I don't really care. But I can't even look at you right now... I need to get out of here. I could give a rat's ass whether you stay here or not, but don't count on seeing me for awhile.'' -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yesterday was another day and with it came another fight. Thing is, most fights don't leave me clutching a bottle of wine just to get to sleep the next day... and the worst part? A production meeting today. AWC brass like the t-shirts I sell, but they're not particular fans of me as a person. Case in point are the fines I have on file - I think I owe them close to 17 grand for various antics I've pulled, mostly involving violence towards 'unwilling participants' like Tracy Stanton. As I sat down, all I could offer them for a defense was ''There can't be ANYTHING you can fine me for today, I've been careful. I haven't injured anyone or thrown any fits. Come on, guys...'' ''This is more about business than your attitude, Amy. As you know, Coast To Coast is looking to be our biggest event of the year. Ratings in your feud have been substantial, to say the least.'' ''Speaking of which, I find it kind of odd that I get fined, but the shit Sloan pulls doesn't get any reprimand...'' ''You can't punish a man like him, it just doesn't work. Besides, people believe that his actions are purely a character,'' said the faceless suit, ''Now, you and I are alone, and this conversation won't go beyond these walls, I assure you...'' I braced myself for whatever it was he was about to do. It seemed like the perfect set-up to some creepy kind of sexual abuse, but I think he knew I was capable of breaking his neck with little remorse. Plus, I don't think executive types are overly interested in girls who knock people out for a living. I nodded to him, but he seemed nervous. He was tapping his fingers in no particular pattern on the table we were at. Maybe he felt awkward because we were the only two people in a conference room, at an oak table meant to seat upwards of twenty people. I personally hated the open space myself, and his panic wasn't helping ease mine in the least. Still tapping, he started asking questions that he didn't seem too rushed to get the answers to, like his first... ''You and, ahem... you and Darcy Markson. We, uhh, we were wondering... you two are friendly?'' ''If you're asking whether we're serious or just sleeping together...'' ''By no means!'' he stammered, trying to recover from the poor timing of the question, ''I was... well... you're seen together quite often, and some of the crew members have caught a glimpse of affection here and there, but you two seem to almost flat-out avoid one another at the shows. I suppose my question is, why?'' ''We work together.'' ''Yes, but you're a popular couple off-camera, why not let us promote you a little more? Higher ratings mean bigger pay stubs, Amy.'' ''We really need to change the subject. Like, now. I'll talk to you about this in a week or so.'' He sensed trouble in paradise, it was easy to see. Thankfully, he went with better judgement and changed the subject. ''Ooookay, back to Coast To Coast and how you can boost the overall show! I need to ask you to, errr... let discretion fall to the wayside a little bit?'' Oh God. ''PLEASE don't tell me this has something to do with Sloan...'' ''Amy, look at me,'' he ordered, and I complied rather unwillingly by death-glaring the living shit out of him, ''You and I both know that you're being paid next to nothing while these fines stand. Front office can make all of those little charges go away. Consider it paying us in ratings, not money.'' ''What do I have to do?'' ''Just give Michael a little leeway. Participate. You know, be easier to work with...'' ''You know what I won't do...'' ''No! Nothing of that nature. Well, I think...'' he stuttered, ''Just play along with him for a little while. If we can get some cameras on any interaction between you, it almost guarantees ratings. This is our biggest show, and we want to go all-out.'' ''At what cost to my dignity?'' ''To be frank, dear... the fines and trouble you rack up in this company are worth far more than your dignity could possibly fetch.'' ''Okay.'' He stared at me, and I'm pretty sure his eyebrows shot to the back of his head, ''Really? That's it?'' I nodded. A suit couldn't possibly know that up until now, I cared about my future. They thought I was all about being the crazy bitch. This guy will go back to the company and tell them that they broke me... that I'm finally coming around and fetching the frisbee instead of throwing it back in their faces as per usual. Joke's on them. The truth is, I gave a shit until I woke up yesterday. Maybe this is just a phase, but my previous lack of luck tells me it isn't, and that I should probably start shooting for a few new goals. That's why I never listen to myself. If I find and follow Darcy, yeah - it could put us in a bad place... maybe even worse than we are. But I'm really relying on the off chance that I get lucky and he wants to listen. I can't rely on myself, I can't rely on my employment and I sure as hell can't rely on my friends, but I'm hoping I still have some ground with the Markson family. If he's not back tomorrow, I'll start making calls. I'm not sure about his parents, but I'm his little sister likes me enough to let me know anything she hears. And if I know anything about Darcy, it's that he can always find solace in that family. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- A day and some change later, Amy stepped off of a flight to Colorado. She called Marie Markson's cell phone for directions, and eventually found herself on the youngest Markson's doorstep. Looking as if she'd seen a ghost, Marie hugged and 'shush'ed Amy at the same time, pointing to the stairs and mouthing 'Darcy'. ''I'll tell you how pretty your house is in a few minutes, 'kay? Thanks for distracting him,'' Amy smiled, beginning a silent ascent of the stairwell. When she got to the top, the redhead knew she was only a few feet from the man who wanted nothing more than NOT to see her at that moment. That's when her 'entrance speech' came to her... ''Don't turn around. You said you couldn't stand to look at me... and I didn't fly all this way to see you fall.'' Darcy suddenly froze, dropping the copy of Catcher in the Rye he'd had his nose in. ''Unfortunately, I think I have to turn around. And when I do, it sure as hell better be Marie, playing a cruel and unfortunate trick on me with her dead-on Amy Campbell impression.'' But as he did, he found himself staring into the eyes of his three-days estranged girlfriend. The disappointment was clear to her as he mouthed a very dirty word upon seeing her face. ''Well, you don't loathe me enough to forget my hatred of that lovely 'C' word,'' Amy grimaced, taking a few daring steps toward Darcy, ''Marie worries about you, you know. She doesn't like seeing you unhappy. I guess that's why she hated me until a few months ago...'' ''Marie, like a few other people I know, needs to learn a thing or two about staying out of her older brother's business. And if she wanted me to feel better, she should have known better than to make it possible for you to show up here.'' Darcy muttered a few other words to himself that Amy couldn't quite catch without the aid of superhuman hearing. But after a deep breath, he spoke loud enough for her to hear once more. ''Well, you did come all this way... what the fuck do you want?'' ''I want you to be a little nicer to me than that, for one thing.'' Amy was muttering under her breath, but eventually grew brave enough to take another few steps toward the elder Markson sibling so she could continued with something more honest than almost anything she'd said to him in the past three weeks, ''Whether you like it or not, you love me. You wouldn't have ever been angry enough to give me a concussion if you didn't, so how about you just fess up to it instead of fighting so hard for once?'' ''You just don't get it, do you?'' Darcy inquired, burying his face in his hands. 'This isn't about how I feel about you. This is about YOU, needing to take a chill pill for once in your life, and fucking get it through your skull that it isn't worth freaking out every time it looks like I care about something other than you. It certainly isn't worth kidnapping my ex-girlfriend.' Amy sighed. ''I didn't kidnap your ex-girlfriend. By now, you know THAT much. And I'm not here to continue a three day old fight, either... so why don't you relax and take a minute to think? I didn't swallow my fear of planes just to get screamed at and smacked around again.'' 'Well I can't imagine what you were expecting to happen otherwise,' he retorted, with an air in his voice that clearly suggested that he was attempting to blow her off. 'So I'll ask you again: what exactly do you mean to accomplish by stalking my ass to Colorado?' Amy winced and let her head lean to one side, trying to bite back any tears and remain calm, ''Because, as much as I REALLY hate to say it to you and absolutely shouldn't right now, I don't think I can go more than 48 hours without you safely. On top of that, I had no clue if you were safe or taken by the mob or...'' she sniffled, ''Jesus, why am I even bothering? You tried cracking my skull a few days ago over a mistake that only got me injured in the end. You made fun of my proposals, which hurt far worse than my head denting the wall. I guess that means I don't KNOW why I'm here. Maybe it's a lost cause...'' Damn. He could see it in her eyes. She looked legitimately frightened - her voice was shaking harder than San Fran's '88 earthquake. Even though he'd physically accosted her, even though he'd disappeared to parts thousands of miles away, she somehow found it in herself to track him down. He tried desperately to cling to his anger, to remember why he left in the first place...but seeing her like this made his rage flee. ''Amy, listen to me,'' he started, his voice eerily calm. ''We've been through this once before. I left you all those years ago because even though you were beautiful, smart, and so much fun to be around...all I could see was a scared little girl who could never get through this life on her own. Now...I'm a little older. It's hard to say if I'm really any wiser...but I'm smart enough to know when I'm seeing history repeat itself. If you don't give me some goddamn space, and let me sort through everything I just had to fucking go through because you can't go two days without being reassured that everything is hunky-fucking-dory...what makes you think this relationship has any more of a shelf life than the first go-around?'' ''Because I went through five years on my own not knowing if you were alive, crippled or smoking rocks somewhere in a cardboard box. I get through every day, and I'll keep getting by, but you're the only person I've ever trusted enough to BE that 'scared little girl' around. You had a half decade of space to sort yourself out, so don't pretend that you not wanting this enough is on my shoulders. I've done everything I can. I've waited and I lost faith completely at one point, but this is the one and only thing in my world that I've ever actually believed in. If it isn't fit to survive, then I have to curl up and die knowing I lived a complete joke - but I know it can stand the test of time if you have even half the faith in life that you've given to me.'' She took another step toward him, but avoided getting too close when she continued, ''I didn't come here out of fear. If I was the scared girl you mentioned, I wouldn't confront you like this. I wouldn't admit to having faith, and I sure as hell would have NEVER proposed marriage, as much as you think that's a complete joke. This time, I'm not scared to be on my own in the least, but I AM scared to lose the one thing that's always felt right to me. Every time I touch you, everything goes blank and calm... it's like home. I can't deny that level of belonging. If I was so insecure and afraid, I would've spent the past three weeks back in Canada, giving you your 'space' and covering my ass.'' ''Well I'm sorry to hear that you've got all your eggs in my basket,'' Darcy replied, his words as cold as ice. Instantly he regretted such a harsh retort to what had obviously been a pouring out of her heart's passionate sentiments. He tried again. ''I don't really know what you want from me. I don't have the answers this time, Amy...I just don't. I don't know if I'm coming home. I don't know if we're going to make it through this. I don't have a clue in the world, except that I know I can't figure it out in California, and I can't figure it out by being anywhere near you. All I can offer you is that I won't be taking another five-year exodus. But this is the only way I know how to handle this, and if you keep following me I'm just going to get back into my car and drive all the way to the Mississippi. Even farther, if that's what it takes. But this is something I have to do, by myself. If you really have any faith in me, or in us, you'd honor that by letting me figure this out on my own.'' Amy heaved a frustrated sigh and sank onto the end of the guest room's bed. She had to make him see things her way - the right way - somehow... ''Darcy, I'm the only thing in your life that makes sense. You can't just run away whenever you feel like it. You have to face things sometimes...'' she plead, ''And, quite honestly, I'd like to think I'm not that bad to stand nose to nose with.'' ''Excuse me? Did I just hear what I thought I heard?'' Moving slowly, Darcy stepped closer to give the girl what she wanted. She made no move to back herself away, and the two stood practically nose to nose, just as she had practically asked. After a moment of staring into her eyes, as if he were doing his best to peer directly into her soul, he spoke. ''NOTHING about this relationship makes sense. Not a damn thing. We hated each other for years, only to get hammered and crawl into bed with each other. You're the tattoo-spackled girl from up north with the checkered past, and I'm the clean-cut California boy who grew up surrounded by people that always wanted better for me than being a professional fucking wrestler. We never get along. We constantly fight. You drive me up the wall.'' Darcy turned away from her again, although he remained just as close. ''I used to love that about you. I used to love that our relationship made zero sense. But now...I don't know what I feel anymore. Not after this. It was cute before, but it nearly got me killed.'' Her stare remained, and she didn't back up in the least, ''Say what you feel, not what you ought to. I'm still the one with the scars from all of this. I might have two-toned eyes, but they don't make me blind... you're struggling with every word, you know by looking at me that everything for us fell into place long ago. You wouldn't have come back if this was as simple as a nonsensical fling when I was 18. I've cried a lot over you, Darcy - especially in the last few days - but I'm the ONLY person who hasn't ever given up on you. Not once. I have and WILL have your back on anything. I saw a man aim a gun at the person he felt most threatened by, and I'm lucky he was a crap shot, because he was trying to shoot you and I could only jump partially in his way.'' She was shakey and trying to hide it from him. Two days without any substantial food and more anxiety than George Bush at a Muslim birthday had worn hard on her, and she was feeling hot tears well up in her eyes again, ''And maybe I'm crazy, but neither of us would be standing right now if this didn't make some kind of sense.'' His arms now folded, Darcy let out a deep sigh. He had to give her credit - she was putting up one hell of a battle for the both of them, something he hadn't quite expected. But unfortunately, she was putting up an equal battle on his psyche, which was clearly wearing down under the brunt of it all. ''It's true,'' he said, barely above a whisper. ''In a funny way, I guess it does make sense that things ended up like this. If this giant disaster we somehow managed to survive has taught me anything, it's that it's far too dangerous for me to pussyfoot around with you anymore.'' Turning around again to face her, Darcy extended his arms and placed them gently on the tops of her shoulders. Instantly she tried to recoil, but he responded by affirming his grip, not allowing her to escape his grasp. As she relented in the predicament, so did he, returning to his gentle stance. 'We're at a crossroads here. You evidently have an ENORMOUS problem with sitting back and riding to wherever this relationship takes us. I tried to ignore it, but after this whole debacle with Jane, it's very clear to me that I can't get away with ignoring it any longer. You want an answer, and I'd like to give you one. But let me make this as clear to you as I possibly can: This is a very, very, VERY important decision I need to make. Possibly the hardest I'll ever make. But the longer you hang around here trying to force the answer you're looking for, the harder you're making it on me. I don't want that, and I certainly don't need it. And believe me, the last thing you want to do is make this any harder than it needs to be. Now if you can just fucking back the fuck off of me so we can get through Coast to Coast, honor our previous engagements, and I can actually have some fucking time to clear my head, we just might all come out winners here. Now is that okay with you, or do we need to argue around in circles for a few more hours until one of us snaps?' ''Are you threatening me, Darcy Markson?'' Shaking his head, Darcy retracted his arms, again returning them to a fold across his chest. ''However you take it is your business. But it's the truth. And unfortunately, that's all I can offer you right now.'' Amy's head and heart sank in unison, ''Then I'm out.'' She headed for the stairway, but turned back for a split second to lock eyes with Darcy once more, ''And I love you, but until you figure out what that means... go fuck yourself.'' -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hey... yeah.
