Perfect Symmetry

5 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/05 My favourite time of the day is the night because of the one basic truth that I find necessary to do what I have to do: there are even shadows when it is night time. They protect me from the illumination of the stunningly radiant glow of the full moon and the equally illuminating, yet manufactured, glow of the street lights. The shadows are where I work the best. No body can see me and I can position myself to strike and my target never sees it coming. Ever. Sometimes I need only to hurt them, sometimes I must kill. I break from my cover within the shadows of a particularly low-brow advertising board selling bra’s I think, across the road from my intended target. Out of the shadows, during a critical assignment such as this one I feel about as vulnerable as turning my back on a furious tiger. As soon as I slip into the shadow of a second advertising board, I instinctively breath a sigh of relief, despite knowing for a fact the guards at the target warehouse adjacent to my position could not see me. I slip my backpack off of my back and place it delicately down before me, making sure not to make even the slightest of sounds. Then I unload the contents of the backpack. First the main part of the rifle itself, then a number of magazine cartridges and the finally followed by the scope. I go to assemble the rifles, but realise the cartridge and the scope are not symmetrical. I move the cartridge slightly to the left with my index finger and look at the two items again. Parallel, perfectly so. Obsessive Compulsive Disorder the scientists called it. They told me when I was born that the grafting of another persons memories into this genetically modified shell would have some after effects, but they had no idea that this would be one of them. Some of the scientists theorised that it was a result of that grafting, others believed that the other persons memories and personality already contained the defect. The scientists deemed the disorder to be inconsequential, but it is times like this, attempting to complete my assignment and being severely hindered by the stupid thing. I realised that rifle itself was now out of symmetry and so adjusted it accordingly. I knew I had to get this job done. I wanted to get this job done and out of the way, but I just couldn’t get around to actually assembling the rifle until everything was perfectly symmetrical. Now that all three parts were now aligned in the correct fashion I quickly assembled the weapon before my mind found something else wrong with the whole situation. I just don’t need it right now. I set up the rifle on it’s stand then adjust the sights of the scope until it focused in upon the upper left hand window of the warehouse building. It was the office of local mob underling Johnny Scarlo and he truly believed that he was safe in there. He thought, for reasons which quite honestly elude me at this point, that nobody would find out about this hideout of his. He clearly underestimated just how much his enemies want him dead. He won’t get another opportunity to make that mistake again - and they say that you always learn from your mistakes. Ha. I examine the office thoroughly and discover that the target as yet to make his presence felt. It would only be a matter of time. The predictability of idiots was as common as grass in a field. Whilst I waited for the moron to make an appearance I found myself unable to rip my mind away from the AWC and my wrestling career. I’d already received a rather generous shot at superstardom in a battle royal style match up a couple of shows of Fresh! ago, but I have to say the lack of symmetry in such a match up really affected me. That is not to say there I am providing any excuses for me loss - I’m not. Alcaues won it fair and square, after all he’s had more experience than most. Terrible joke I realise, but I feel no shame in making it nonetheless. Some things just have to be done. I realise that now, more than ever. This week at Freshburst! I must win against Tim Shipley. It must be done in order to get my career back together and climb the hierarchy of the AWC. All of this must be done, it’s just a matter of when and how. Nothing more, nothing less. My rise through the hierarchy of the AWC is just as predictable as this moron entering his office. Through the scope of the rifle and see Johnny Scarlo enter the room. I smile, totally unsurprised by what I see. I pull the trigger and watch the blood splatter up against the back wall. The guard at the base of the building has absolutely no idea what has just happened. Pretty much akin to the hierarchy of the AWC - they won’t see me coming either.
