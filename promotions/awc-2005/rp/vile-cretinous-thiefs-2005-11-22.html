Vile Cretinous Thiefs!

22 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/11/05 (I think...) The corridors were dark as the creature Grobschnit stealthily padded through them, plodding along on his tiptoes for fear of being caught by the great enemy. Glad in a black ninja costume, Grobschnit knew that if he were to be seized by his nemesis he would surely be done for. Never the less, Grobber had outfoxed the deadly, moustached foe many a time before, and there was no way in hell a green alien ninja from 2,000,000 BC like himself was EVER going to get caught. He reached an intersection, and slid up against the side of the wall instinctively, decreasing his visibility. The Notorious S-C-H-N-I-T gingerly popped his head around the corner, and breathed a sigh of relief when he saw that the coast was clear. Not long now… not long before the ‘Schnit would sample the sweet taste of victory once again. There was nothing quite like the thrill of the steal, particularly when the prize that the Green Phenom seeked was an object of such extreme value. Grobschnit stepped up to the door leading into the room where he would finally snare his prize. He glanced around the moonlit room shiftily through the eye-slit in his ninja costume, before gently turning the handle and slipping silently inside. There before him, sat high on top of a cabinet, sat the much sought-after prized that he was seeking… the purpose of his existence… the one thing he wanted more than anything else in the world. He shambled towards the holy item like a creature possessed, fixated on the joys that its capture would bring him… Suddenly, the trance was snapped by the sound of footsteps coming from behind the ‘Schnit. Grob immediately pivoted round on his heel to see the silhouette of a rather tall figure approaching ominously on the other side of the door. “Schit, kno tyme 2 hyde,” Grobschnit thought to himself, “betta gett reddy two doo da xtreeeeem kunng-foo!” As Grobschnit stood in some ridiculously over-the-top martial arts pose, the door clicked open, and the sleepy figure that had just entered fumbled around looking for the light switch. Finally he found it, and the lights flickered into action, causing this dark, mysterious, and surely evil, figure to rub his eyes which were struggling to adjust. The Schnit’s pulse began to race as the character, who was wearing a black dressing gown with gold trim along with a pair of Sonic the Hedgehog slippers which were large, fluffy and blue, looked around the room, still squinting. “What the smeg is that?” the dude croaked in a groggy voice, obviously seeing the ninja’d-up Grobschnit standing at the other side of the room. “Grobschnit? What the hell are you doing in the kitchen at this time of night!?!” Grobschnit growled, and dropped his stance, knowing that he would stand no chance in a duel against the figure stood before him. For the man before him truly was a force to be reckoned with, renowned across the land for his unbelievable aura of coolness and for, of course, being the most badass man on the face of the planet. Who was this majestic crusader and purveyor of aceness? Why, none other than the Scottish King of Cool, Andy M., of course. “Fuk Andee, hoew dud u katch da Grob?” Grobschnit enquired as he pulled the ninja mask from over his head and discarded it on the tiled kitchen floor; no need for it now. Under the mask, it was refreshing to see that everyone’s favourite prehistoric, intergalactic motherfuckin’ alien was still as hideous and green as ever. Andy chuckled to himself, “Dammit, Grob, you’re so freaking loud I could have heard you from a mile away! I heard you creepin’ around down here and thought I’d have a wee look around. To be honest, I thought some crazy honky had just bust into Andyville, I was getting ready to go medieval on someone’s ass,” he proclaimed, somewhat relieved that the intruder was indeed his intergalactic homeboy Grobschnit. “So tell me, brotha to brotha, what are you doing down here anyway, hmmm?” Andy enquired, raising an eyebrow. “After my cookies again?” Grobschnit glanced over his shoulder to the top of the oak kitchen cabinet, on top of which sat a glass jar packed to the brim with double chocolate chip cookies. This now unattainable jar had of course been the treasure so vehemently hunted by Grobschnit, who turned back to his owner with a shrug. “Kno, eye woz jist chassing aftur sum dam hippee whu wuz runnen arowned…” Andy raised an eyebrow to this, recognising instantly the bullshit that his little green buddy was attempting to feed him. “A hippy, you say?” Murrr enquired, humouring Grobschnit. “Yez, itt hud lowng hare n ‘e waz stinkerz ov pott zo i r kik hiz azz, fagit, buht itt eggscaped da ‘Schnit,” blurted Grobby, attempting to cover his tracks. Now if you can translate that, you’re a freaking genius. “So what’s the deal with the ninja suit then? You’re all dressed up like some crappy Bond villain all because of a damn hippy?” “Kno, da owtfit juzt lookz kewl dawg.” Chuckling, Andy took a step towards Grobschnit, and spoke the words “Alright Grob, whatever, lets all go back to sleep now, alright?” Letting out a disappointing sigh with one final glance at the cookie jar, Grobschnit hung his head. He glanced up at Andy with his large, black, orb-like eyes and opened his mouth to speak again, but before he could do so, both man and beast were startled by a sudden shriek. Curiously, they both turned their heads in the direction of the sound – towards the back lawn of this exceedingly grand house – and Andy mouthed the words “what the fuck?” Again, there was a muffled shriek from outside. “What the jiminy is going on out there?” Andy asked rhetorically, when suddenly, across the blackened lawn, a jet of flames illuminated a small area of grass. Recognising this sight immediately, Andy made a mad dash for the French doors leading out of the kitchen and into the “garden”. As he fumbled around with the key – which was already in the lock – Grobschnit watched on, utterly bamboozled by the whole “flame” situation. “Andee! Wutz dat!?!” Grobschnit yelped, as the Scottish King of Cool hurriedly struggled with the lock. “We’ve got trouble, Grobby!” proclaimed Murrr, as he finally managed to swing the door open. He turned around to face his comrade, and said; “The fire-breathing badger security system must have come across an intruder! There’s no other explanation for it!” Again, a burst of flame caught Andy’s eye, followed by another scream from the suspected assailant. This time Murrr wasted no time whatsoever, and dashed out the door like a bolt of lightning. He was followed closely behind by Grobschnit, who in spite of his physical appearance, managed to run like Linford Christie with a red hot poker up his ass. As the dynamic duo drew closer and closer to the “war zone,” the screams of the assailant became more and more audible; in fact, it sounded like he was begging for mercy from Andy’s patented Fire-Breathing Badger Security System ™. Eventually, Andy and Grobschnit found themselves up close with the badger and the poor, tormented soul who had incurred it’s wrath. The assailant had a look of pure, unbridled terror across a youthful face that looked no more than 25 years old. He was also dressed in a plain black t-shirt, blue jeans, and wore a pair of glasses so thick that they’d make the average emo kid weak at the knees. Not only that, but he had a bulky camera hanging around his neck. He lay on the ground, on his ass, trying frantically to back away from the bursts of flames that were being shot at him from the mouth of Andy’s prized fire-breathing badger, Snowball – identifiable by the purple ribbon which was tied around her neck. “HELP! GET AWAY FROM ME!” the poor kid yelped in a voice that would strip paint. Andy, after taking some time to survey the situation and plan his next move, called out the badger’s name; “Snowball!” Recognising its name, the badger turned around, and ceased shooting flames at the assailant. As if like clockwork, Grobschnit walked up behind the guy, who was little more than a kid, and stood solidly behind him, to prevent any escape attempts. “Yooz inn lott uv trubble fagit!” Grobschnit announced, startling the dude. The assailant turned around to face the hideous, green creature of incredible awesomeness and let out another loud yelp; Grobschnit was unlike anything he had ever seen before. “Sup?” Snowball enquired in the ghetto-est of ghetto voices. Well, she didn’t really; we all know that badgers can’t speak, that would just be ridiculous. But if they could, that’s what Snowball would say, ‘cos that’s just how she rolls. No, instead of that, the badger stood up on its two hind legs and pawed at Andy, as if it was expecting some kind of reward. Andy rolled his eyes at this, and delved into the pocket of his dressing gown. He soon pulled out a couple of what looked like teabags from his pocket, and tossed them at Snowball. Snowball immediately leapt after them, and devoured them like a fat man would devour fried food after a 24 hour famine. “Well done, my pretty, once again you have earned your pay!” Andy said, petting the badger’s head gently. Snowball turned around to face Andy, and winked, before plodding off as fast as her stumpy little legs could carry her, to resume the rounds. Andy, meanwhile, turned his attentions to the frightened guy who Grobschnit was incessantly prodding with his foot. “Wut do wee doo wit dis fagit, Andeeroo?” Grobby asked, a devious grin planted firmly across his face. After taking one puzzled look into the eyes of the kid, Andy leapt up to his feet. “TO THE ANDYCHAIR!” -------------------------------- The Andychair, in the eyes of Andy, was the most fearsome torture device known to man. Known and feared throughout the lands, the mere thought of being strapped to this unholy device was enough to send shivers down the spines of those stupid enough to trespass in the independent state of Andyland. This, of course, was a load of bollocks. The Andychair was little more than a wooden armchair sitting in the middle of a blandly-decorated bedroom in Andy’s house. Unfortunately, Andy had never been forced to actually tie anyone to it before, as all previous intruders in Andyland had either escaped or managed to completely avoid the fearsome badgers in the first place. And so, the poor intruder on Andy’s property sat on this rather uninteresting chair, tied up as tight as the alien supergenius Grobschnit could muster. The room was clearly one that Andy couldn’t be bothered to decorate, as the walls were painted with an uneven coat of white paint, and the flooring was but bare floorboards. The room was illuminated poorly by the faint trickle of the morning sun, which was beginning to seep through the blind drawn down across the window. The only other piece of furniture in the room, other than the chair, was a medium-sized wooden table, on which sat a telephone, a CD player, and a collection of CDs, which Grobschnit was shuffling through. “Ooh boyee, weez gun hav sum funn!” Grobschnit cackled to himself, as suddenly, the room’s only door burst open. Andy M. stood in the frame, clutching a pen-sized torch in his hand. He had thrown some clothes on, and was now wearing a white “I Love NYHC” Sick of it All t-shirt which was a little baggy on him, a pair of brand new black jeans, and some heavy DM boots. “Okay bitch, lets talk!” Andy barked at the guy, who was technically now his hostage. The Scottish King of Cool stepped up to the guy, who probably by now must have decided that Andy was some kind of psychopath. “If you don’t co-operate, I’ll acquaint your eyes with this Mag-Lite, motherfucker!” Andy raised up the tiny little torch, treating it as if it were some kind of deadly weapon. “And over there, Grobschnit has a fine collection of dodgy emo CDs, with which we will not hesitate to aurally DESTROY you! Give us a burst, Grobby!” With a nod, Grobschnit lowered his head and pressed a button on the CD player. Almost immediately, some hideously horrible music blared out… And will you tell all your friends, you’ve got your gun to my head… “AHHHHHHHH NO! TURN IT OFF! QUICKLY!” Andy yelled; clearly Taking Back Sunday were too much for even the Scottish King of Cool to tolerate. He breathed a sigh of relief as his partner in crime turned it off. Andy nodded, thanking the ‘Schnit, before turning his attentions back towards his hostage. “So, motherhubbard, the question is, are we going to do this the easy way, or the hard way, hmmmm?” Andy raised an eyebrow, as the kid uttered “Whatever, just let me go…” “Not until you’ve answered a couple of questions for me first!” Andy proclaimed, circling his captive like a lion stalking its prey. “Firstly, I see you’ve got a camera around your neck, what are you, press?” “Yes, as if it weren’t obviously enough,” the guy replied in his rather irritatingly squeaky voice. Andy immediately took offence to this, and turned on the Mag-Lite, shining it at full power in the eyes of the dude. “Don’t you give me any of that cheek, vile treacherous trespasser! FEEL THE FURY OF THE MAG-LITE!” yelled the S.K.C. almost maniacally. Unfortunately, the size of the torch meant that it’s bean was little more than a pest to Andy’s hostage and little else. “Who are you, and what were you doing on my property?” Andy asked, backing off a little. “My name’s Garth, like I said, I work for the press. I was looking to get a couple of snaps… what with your recent AWC comeback, I could get a lot of money for some of those babies, you know.” “But come on dude, sneaking onto my freaking land in the middle of the night? That’s not cool. I’m afraid you’re going to have to pay my friend… Grob…” And sugar, we’re going down swinging… “NO NO NO! MAKE IT STOP!” Garth wailed, clearly feeling the pain that such horrible music could cause to the human ear. With Andy’s nod, Grob eventually did cease. “Now don’t make me unleash Hawthorne Heights on you!” All heads suddenly turned to the door, as once again it swung open. This time, Andy’s younger brother Cayle stood in the doorway, looking rather sleepy; clearly he had just woken up. He wore a bright blue “Vote Bush; For More Wars!” t-shirt and a pair of knee-length beige cargo shorts, as well as a dark red coloured bandanna. He looked around the room, bemused at what was going on. “What’s the craic with this?” he asked, noticing the guy strapped up in the chair. “Fallout Boy and Taking Back Sunday at seven o’ clock in the morning? That’s just pure evil, Andy!” “Cayle, my brother! I caught this young snot rag running around the place! He must be punished…” Andy told his brother, before turning back to Garth, shining the puny little torch in his eyes once again. Barefooted, Cayle walked across to the Andychair, and watched on with much confusion; he knew better than to interrupt the Scottish King of Cool in such a situation, even if he did think it was more than just a little bit bizarre. “What do you think, Cayle, what do we do with him?” Andy asked, still fixated on Garth. “Well, don’t hurt him, whatever you do…” Andy seemed somewhat shocked by this idea, and turned to face his brother. “What? God no! I’m not some kind of maniac…” “Obviously not,” Garth spat, his voice dripping with sarcasm. “QUIET BOY!” Andy yelled back at him, before turning again to his sleepy brother. “I figure we take the film from his camera and send him on his way, that way he’s got no satisfaction out of this escapade…” Cayle nodded, as Andy turned back towards Garth, and damn near tore the camera from around his neck. “Hey! Watch it…” Garth cried in vain, as Andy scrambled around looking for a way to eject the film. Eventually, he pressed something he didn’t mean to, and a small hatch opened on the bottom of the camera. The film slipped out, and bounced off the floor, and back up again into the palm of Andy’s hand. Grinning as he slipped the film into his back pocket, Andy addressed his brother and Grob; “Untie this honky!” Like minions serving their master, Grobschnit and Cayle M. set about releasing Garth from Andy’s sub-standard boy scout knots; which didn’t take very long. When he was free, Garth immediately got up to his feet; his facial expression had changed from pure terror to just anger, having realised that the only man Andy M. posed a threat to in the room was himself. Andy thrust the camera back into his arms. “Lesson of the day; don’t enter Andyland with out my permission, feel homey? Now get the heck out of my house!” Garth snarled, before Grobschnit suddenly grabbed his arm, and damn near dragged him out of the room. Andy smiled with satisfaction, “Well, that was fun. Sort of… okay, actually, it was a bit lame, but hey! I’m not having some pleb running around my garden, yo!” “Yeah, fair enough,” Cayle conceded, “what now though? That guy could go to the police, or something…” “Oh whatever, we’ll worry about that if it ever happens. Anyway, lets waste no more time! For soon, the badgers will need feeding! And believe me, you don’t want to catch one of those motherfuckers if they haven’t been fed…” And on that note, we left the Scottish King of Cool and his brother in the majestic dream land that is the home of Andy M. Righty-o, man.
