Fly away, sweet bird of prey.

31 August 2006

Date: 8.31.06 closing lyrics courtesy of poe’s “fly away” thank you, and enjoy ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “Here. You need to take this, once and for all.” Darcy reached into his pocket and felt the familiar velvet exterior of the small black box. Removing the box, he placed it squarely in Jane’s palm, finally ridding himself of the burden. Jane looked at him as he did, with an expression that seemed to ask if he was really sure about this. But no words were necessary, and neither was the question. The ring was now merely a reminder of an old, broken promise; one of destiny’s impromptu left turns. Darcy had always imagined that he’d give the ring to Jane quite differently than the manner she was receiving it from him now, and from the look of it he surmised that she hadn’t quite pictured it this way, either. But as the two stood in front of Darcy’s East Bay abode, finally reunited after their separate odysseys, the moment ten years in the making had finally come to pass. So it wouldn’t be presented as intended after all, as an engagement ring…but just as her mother had always wished, Darcy finally did give the ring to Jane. The significance was not lost on the bearer, nor the recipient…and as Jane clasped her fingers around the velvet, the two shared the moment together in silence. “Well…I really should be going,” Jane finally said, breaking the calm that had washed over them. “I doubt Amy would be happy to know I’m still hanging around.” “She’s resting upstairs. And if I know her at all, the painkillers already hard at work knocking her into next week,” Darcy replied, almost looking sad to see her go. “But you’re right. I think enough damage has been done around here. The sooner everybody’s lives go back to the way they were, the better.” “ ‘Back to the way things were?’ Does that mean this is it? So long, have a nice life?” Jane suddenly threw at him, catching Darcy off-guard. Evidently she wasn’t so ready to let him go either. Darcy nodded. “For now, anyway. The biggest problem with keeping the police out of this whole mess is that those guys are still at large, and looking for you harder than ever. It’s really best that you and I not be in the same place at the same time, for safety’s sake if nothing else.” Jane leaned against the door, her posture indicating that she knew Darcy had more to add. In response Darcy forced a weak smile, indicating that she still knew him all too well. “But also…I still need some time to sort through this all. I’m not sure how you live your life these days, but I don’t typically run into old flames and rescue them from certain doom. I still find it hard to believe I helped you in the first place, let alone risking thirty bullets in my ass for you.” Jane nodded as well, although her chin seemed to quiver as she did so. With apparently nothing left to be said, Jane turned and gripped the golden-colored latch, the door swinging open as she pushed down. Appropriately, the California sunset glared in the distance as she attempted to make her exit. But she made it all of one step before Darcy placed a hand on her shoulder from behind. “Jane…wait.” The golden-haired girl turned around to face her former love. “I’m…I’m really sorry. About all of this.” Darcy stammered out. “I’m not sure what the hell came over Amy, or that miscreant friend of hers. But I never meant for any of this happen. I hope you know that. Believe me, Amy’s going to get an earful about this.” Jane sighed. And without another word, she shot forward to capture him in an embrace. Too shocked to respond, Darcy merely stood there with eyes as wide as saucers until she retracted her arms from around his neck. “I know,” she said as she stepped back. “But listen…go easy on her, huh? I mean, she obviously has some anger control issues, and maybe she doesn’t make the best decisions…but I was kidnapped because she loves you. She really does, Darcy. In a strange way…I can respect that. And I’m really happy for you.” Darcy suddenly found himself staring at the floor. She certainly had a point – Amy did love him, and this kidnapping incident only proved that her love for him was far and away more fierce than anyone in his life that had come before her. It still didn’t excuse her actions, though…Darcy gritted his teeth at the thought of the eventual altercation, of which there was no escape. Even if he didn’t confront her about Jane’s being kidnapped, she would surely have a few words for him about his suicide mission at the dance club, nearly getting all three of them killed. Darcy looked up and was surprised to see Jane no longer standing in front of him. Always one to make a quick exit, he was reminded. He looked out the door to see Jane walking around the side of her rental car. She didn’t say where he was headed…which was probably just as well. But there remained one more thing left to be said. “Hey Jane!” Darcy shouted, just as he heard the driver side door slam shut. He feared that his message would fall on deaf ears, but was relieved to see the passenger window roll down. But it was too late. As Jane’s car lumbered away from his house, taking her out of his life once more, Darcy’s message fell upon deaf ears. *** U.S. Highway 50, eastbound toward Utah. The most desolate highway of them all. With the advent and rapid ascent of technology in the communications department over the last century or so, some have already deemed it impossible to escape the always watchful eye of civilization. Cell phones and global positioning units, moving rapidly from their infancy to become backbones of cultures the world over, have worked in union to ensure that no matter where you live, people are capable of finding you. But somebody overlooked the stretch of road connecting Northern California and the barren Nevada desert. Actually, this is a misnomer – everyone overlooks the barren Nevada desert. Along Highway 50 a man can look for miles in every direction without finding so much as even proof of human existence in the western United States…save, that is, for the endless stretch of gray concrete plodding on in search of the other shining sea. A perfect place for somebody looking to live off the grid – say, for instance, if one were looking to escape the tireless efforts of a deadly time-traveling cyborg. And the perfect place for Darcy Markson to seek refuge from his own troubles. He was still a good six or seven hours away from his intended destination. Darcy’s younger sister Marie had recently graduated college and moved into a house in the tiny, piss-ant mountain town of Crested Butte, Colorado. When Darcy hopped into his car, he simply intended to drive. Drive away from everything. Drive until the sun changed spots with the moon…maybe even until they switched again. But a few hours into his aimless trek, Darcy realized that what he really needed was a place to stop and think. And there was hardly a more perfect place to do that than the site of so many of their family vacations…so perfect that Marie had elected to move there permanently. So Darcy ventured forth toward the Centennial State, still hidden some eight hundred miles in the distance. To both pass the time and occupy his mind, Darcy brought along a book-on-tape copy of Jon Krakauer’s “Into Thin Air,” depicting the author’s harrowing climb to the top of Mount Everest, and the tragedy that would ensue on his descent. But as Krakauer’s almost monotone voice recalled the various members he traveled with, Darcy found himself unable to concentrate. He could only think of the events that had unfolded that morning… *** “Hey, can you grab my Tylenol 3 from the drawer over there?” Rising from his seat in a nearby chair, Darcy moved to honor his beleaguered girlfriend’s request. But as he felt the cool plastic against his sweaty palm, he could feel the calmness in him rapidly vacating. He took a deep breath, but on exhaling he knew there was no going back. Nothing could quell the anger inside him now. He turned to look at Amy, tossing the bottle in the air before catching it again himself. But then, without warning, Darcy gritted his teeth and hurled the bottle as hard as he could into the opposite wall. It unfortunately didn't shatter on impact as he'd hoped, but the point was still made. ''Jesus!'' Amy fell out of the bed, guarding herself behind the mattress at Darcy's sudden outburst. She grew up nervous around men lashing out, and Darcy’s sudden home-run with her pill bottle sent her nearly to panic, and on guard as best she could be. ''Darcy, what the hell was that about?'' It was something I said to Jeph that he took too far... and I'm the one who got shot for it!'' ''I'm AWARE of this,'' Darcy shot back. ''And I've played it cool for as long as I could. But the reason you got shot is because you're so goddamn psychotic. You nearly got yourself killed, and you nearly got ME killed. But worst of all, an innocent fucking girl got caught up in all of this because you evidently have no ability to keep your emotions in check.'' Amy looked incredulous, but she fired back with remarkable fire. ''I'm sorry for showing up somewhere to save your fucking ass! Jane and I went there because you were going to get YOURSELF shot. YOU endangered ALL OF US by not getting the police involved in any of it. You think you're some bigshot hero who's going to save her career by putting the situation in your own hands? If I didn't have her, she would've been LONG dead before you ever even got your fucking skull screwed on straight. I can't speak for her, but being unemployed is a plus over BEING SIX FEET UNDER,'' she screamed, ''And you didn't think I would get involved... you were ready to kill people over your EX, and I'M THE ONE WHO TOOK THE BULLET FOR YOU IN THE END. Literally! Why the fuck am I even in your bed right now? Maybe I should leave your dumb ass to take the girl you really think is worth it.'' Darcy's eyes seemed to gleam an almost sickly shade of red. Without thinking, he picked up the chair that he'd been sitting on previously, hurling it against the wall as well. Unlike its plastic pill-holding counterpart, the wooden device splintered into several pieces upon contact. ''Fuck you, Amy! FUCK. YOU. If, at any point, you had had the balls to tell me what was really going on, nobody would have had a gun pointed at them. But you didn't. I can't imagine why, except for the sick little jealousy you have over a girl I broke up with eight years ago. You kept your mouth shut, and all I could do was search like mad for this girl under the circumstances she gave me, hoping to fucking CHRIST that I would find her before the coroners did. Don't you fucking put this on me, or on her, or on anybody else but yourself. And if you think that bullet wound is going to make me feel guilty, you better fucking think twice. That's your penance for fucking things up in the first place, but it sure as hell doesn't clean up this mess.'' When the chair flew, Amy backed off... but her mouth didn't. ''I deserved to be SHOT? You piece of shit...'' ''Maybe you did, Amy! Don't you fucking get it?' Darcy harshly replied, confirming that he didn't take the injury quite so tragically as she. ''I'm tired of this shit. Every time something goes wrong, every time you feel the LEAST bit threatened, you fly off the handle. Jane was in my house for ten fucking seconds before you tried to rip her throat out. You wanted her dead, Amy. Admit it. That's why your slum ass fuckface friend pulled this shit in the first place. You wanted her out of the picture, and for what? This was already hard enough on me as it was, just to see her face again. But you had to make things 11,000 times harder than they needed to be, to serve no purpose except to make you feel a little bit better inside. So fucking EXCUSE me if I feel a little bit better inside that you came out a little worse for wear.'' ''If I fly off the handle so much, and you swear I wanted her dead... don't you think I was perfectly capable of making that happen? If I really wanted her dead, I could've just thrown her to the mob. I could've done it myself, for christ's sake! Use your head for once.'' ''What the FUCK does that have to do with anything?'' Darcy fired in return. ''I'll answer that for you - not a damn thing. It doesn't matter what your intentions really were, but this allllllllll started because you couldn't get a grip. And it went on because you couldn't find it in yourself to tell me what was really going on. I can't take this shit anymore, Amy. The jealousy, the lies, any of it. You went too fucking far this time.'' ''Jane was SAFE with me, you prick, '' Amy responded. ''I didn't want an innocent person to have their life ruined, either, and I SURE as fuck didn't trust her in your hands. Call me dishonest all you want, but you're the bumbling idiot who nearly got all of us killed. YOU are the reason I have bullet fragments in my side. I get you your career back, then fuck up ONCE by trying to protect you AND your motherfucking ex girlfriend, and then you stand here telling me I deserved to be shot? Go fuck yourself. You wanted to 'save' Jane to sickly justify your place in the world, not because you gave a shit. You just wanted that little badge of fucking honor, but your bullshit would've gotten this house burned down while we were sleeping. By having her here in the first place, you put everybody in DIRECT DANGER. They could've taken MY family, Darcy. They could've kidnapped me in exchange for Jane. Did you ever think of that? OF COURSE NOT, because Darcy Markson needed to wear the big boy pants so he could feel special outside of a wrestling ring. This whole relationship seems to be a fucking wayside joke to you, but you're the clown in it all.'' “Wow.” Several seconds passed. Darcy’s jaw was practically gaping as the tension in the room was so thick, you could cut it with a knife. “I can’t believe it. You just said four or five things in there that couldn’t be farther from the truth. You think I wanted ANY of this? You think I threw everybody in the line of fire so I could be a fucking hero? I went in ALONE, Amy, because I had to. I didn’t ask you to come, I didn’t want you to come. I wanted to save Jane because I realized that yes, I actually do give a damn about her…as a human being. The only joke here is that you couldn’t turn the blinders off and see that I can care about somebody without it having anything to do with our relationship.” Darcy paused. The shouting had disappeared from his voice, but his forthcoming words carried more punch than anything he’d screamed at Amy thus far. “You don’t even know me,” Darcy said, ever so softly. “And I can’t believe you wanted to get married…” That's when Amy snapped. Tears grew fast in her eyes, but she tried as hard as she could to keep them back. His constant rejections hurt, but nothing had ever been more powerful than the pain of him mocking the moments where she had showed him more of her true self than ever before. She clawed her way across the bed, stood up in front of Darcy and slapped him as hard as she could. It was open-handed, and stung his cheek - but only made him angrier. ''You didn't mean that,'' Amy whimpered, ''I know you better than that...'' And that’s when it happened. Without thinking, Darcy bared his teeth like a mother lion who had encountered a marauder wandering too close to her cubs. In a fluid motion he violently shoved Amy back into the headboard, where she impacted with a sickening thud. Instantly he regretted it; instantly he knew this had gone too far, and his only instinct was to rush to her aid, to take it all back and console her until she cried her eyes dry. But he couldn’t. He couldn’t even apologize. Instead, he uttered the only words he could think of. “You better back the fuck off, Amy.” For the first time in his life, Darcy watched Amy react with outright fear to something. It was nearly heartbreaking when, the moment after her skull cracked against the headboard, she recoiled and nearly flew to the opposite corner of the room, wide-eyed and trying not to sob. She curled her body up in guarding, but the tears couldn't hold back for long and eventually she was sobbing against the wall. ''Please,'' she begged him for some unknown act, ''This isn't you, Darcy. I'm sorry, for all of it. I'm so sorry...'' Darcy just shook his head, in equal parts both to her pathetic apology and for his own actions. “Fuck this. I mean…no. Just fuck it. I can’t take this shit anymore. I can’t stand that you make me feel like this. I feel like my brain just went to a million pieces. I don’t know who’s the bigger low-life…me for putting my hands on you, or you for driving me to it. But this isn’t healthy. And I can’t take it anymore.” With that, Darcy turned and made his way for the bedroom door. “Where are you going?” Amy asked, with glossy eyes. “I don’t know. And I don’t really care. But I can’t even look at you right now…I need to get out of here. I could give a rat’s ass whether you stay here or not, but don’t count on seeing me for awhile.” *** “Darcy! Oh my gosh, it’s so great to have you here!” Darcy forced a weak smile as his younger sister practically jumped into his arms. Marie Gabrielle Markson was four years Darcy’s junior, but what she conceded to Darcy in both age and height, she more than made up for in energy. As a kid she was always bouncing off the walls, and her high-octane demeanor likely greatly contributed to the well-toned form she sported now. Much to Darcy’s chagrin, practically everyone he knew confessed their hidden aspirations to bone his sister even before she’d obtained her driver’s license. Like Darcy, her natural hair color was a deep black, but she’d altered the look from her brother’s by flawlessly dyeing it a dark maroon that seemed to almost bear a purple tint. She wore a cerulean cardigan sweater atop a practically skin-tight pair of blue jeans. “I’m only sorry I wasn’t awake to greet you when you got here last night,” Marie added, as she pulled herself away from Darcy. “And even sorrier that I had to run the blasted coffee shop all morning, or else I would have made you breakfast. So when do I get to hear what I owe to have the presence of the great Darcy Crisis here in Crusty Butt?” This time, Darcy didn’t have to force a smile. The “Crusty Butt” play on words that even local folks affectionately used to refer to their tiny town notwithstanding, Marie had always been Darcy’s biggest wrestling fan. Unlike practically everyone else, who had seemed highly skeptical of Darcy’s decision to leave college and pursue a career in the squared circle, Marie had been a closet wrestling fan since her days in diapers. She was the first person Darcy told when he’d been approached about signing a developmental deal that would leave him stranded in Bumfuck, Canada for nearly six months. Marie was thrilled; not only for her brother for following his heart, but also that he’d chosen his kid sister as the first to hear the news. Their relationship strained when Darcy raced toward adulthood and left his sister behind, but it was that moment that brought them back together, and they remained close to this day. “Well I realized I hadn’t seen your place yet, which is practically inexcusable,” Darcy said. “But I also had to get out of California. Shit’s kind of hit the fan lately.” “Oh? How’s that?” Darcy took a seat on his sister’s darkened-crimson sofa, which seemed to tie the guest room he occupied together. The sofa was shaded only slightly brighter than her hair. “Did you hear Jane came back in town?” Marie froze. “Oh my…what happened? Did you give her a piece of your mind?” Darcy frowned. He loved his sister, and certainly trusted her…but the kidnapping affair didn’t need to be spread farther than the people who were directly involved, let alone members of Darcy’s family, who unbeknownst to them nearly lost the eldest Markson child. Besides…there were more pertinent things he wanted to discuss with Marie. “I’ll get to that in a minute,” Darcy said. “What’s more important is what she’s made me realize.” Marie shifted her hips slightly, placing her hands on each of them. “Alright buster, I’ll play your game. What’s on your mind?” “I did a lot of thinking on the drive over here –” “Not much you can do on that drive,” Marie interrupted. Darcy chuckled. “Tell me about it. But I realized something. Mom and Dad were footing the undergraduate bill for my short stint in southern California, so there was no getting around explaining things to them. And it’s true – if it hadn’t been for the shit that Jane pulled, my life would have been a lot different. I probably would have even made it through the first four years of college, at the very least, bound for some kind of white-collar job pushing pencils for the next 60 years. The thought of that nearly just made me throw up in my mouth, but it’s true. Set up a 401K, knock out a couple of kids, maybe even settle down in a seven-figure house in Marin County… As Darcy was talking, he somehow missed the sound of the front door to Marie’s house creak open. Sounds wild, I know, but I enjoyed thinking about it. There’s something comforting at that age about imagining that you’ve lived your life absolutely correctly, that things are coming together, and all you have to do is sit back and enjoy the ride.” The intruder inched closer to the guest bedroom, unbeknownst to Darcy has he continued. “But what Jane did changed everything. I don’t have the answers this time, Amy…I just don’t. I don’t know if I’m coming home. I don’t know if we’re going to make it through this. I don’t have a clue in the world, except that I know I can’t figure it out in California, and I can’t figure it out by being anywhere near you. All I can offer you is that I won’t be taking another five-year exodus. But this is the only way I know how to handle this, and if you keep following me I’m just going to get back into my car and drive all the way to the Mississippi. Even farther, if that’s what it takes. But this is something I have to do, by myself. If you really have any faith in me, or in us, you’d honor that by letting me figure this out on my own.” Amy heaved a frustrated sigh and sank onto the end of the guest room's bed. You have to face things sometimes...'' she plead, ''And, quite honestly, I'd like to think I'm not that bad to stand nose to nose with.'' “Excuse me?
