Cause And Effect

23 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/10/05 Cause and Effect Apparently, my good friend Jonathon Winters is in rather a lot of trouble as a resultant of appearing with me, on AWC Fresh, last Tuesday night. He was only there to offer comfort to me, and morale support leading up to my debut. I, similar to Jonathon, do not fully understand what reason the WWA official have for deciding whether to chastise him or not. He wasn’t betraying their company name, nor image. He didn’t bad-mouth them in any way, shape or form. In fact, he barely mentioned them at all. In his mind he was there just as one human being comforting another human being. Anything else does not matter. I know that he does not blame me for what happened, but that does not negate me from blaming myself. After all, he was only there because of me. If I hadn’t been so weak, he would never have offered to accompany me to the show. He would never have been filmed at Fresh and he would be enjoying his time as the WWA Television champion, rather than having his victorious moment marred by backbiting and politics. Welcome to the world of wrestling, I guess. Despite this incident, Jonathon’s morale support forced me through extremely tough match with his namesake, yet not relative, Laura Winters in a particularly taxing match up – and I thought that my other line of work was dangerous. Nevertheless I came out victorious, defeating my opponent with relative ease. I just hope that my momentum is carried forward to this week’s Fresh in Hattiesburg, Mississippi. It is an extremely tough-looking, but oddly intriguing event. The resultant of which may place me in the main event of the next pay view, triangles if I’m victorious. Interesting. Very interesting indeed. Battle Royal I had to get out of hiss office. My father was going on another rant again about how wrestling is too dangerous and that I should be focused more on the job that I was created for. Then he went on to say that he didn’t know whether it was natural for a daughter to be so stubborn or whether it was just the memory implanted in this shell rebelling. I stormed out of the office and headed directly for the roof, from there the limits were boundless, but I knew that I had a task to do and I figured it would be better for me to complete it now to get it out of the way. That way, I will have more time to focus upon my wrestling career, more accurately the awesome opportunity I have been given this week. I want to train hard for this. I want it so badly. No matter how hard I tried to deny, to myself and more predominantly my father, I was doing it to make him proud of me. Not my father; he can go to hell for all I care. I mean Jonathon Winters. He’s the only person who’s stood by me throughout all of this and I just want to make him proud. Sounds corny, right? Well, I don’t much care what you think. Twenty minutes after leaving my father’s rooftop I had successfully navigated my way through the city, jumping from building top to building top at high speeds, just to get to the designated location, adjacent to the popular Italian restaurant. My father’s sources always provided me with up to date information about the whereabouts of my targets at any particular time. I am the assassin and therefore I do not do intelligence work. I should have to do it and I won’t do it. The very idea of it seems a little creepy to me, I mean basically, it’d just make me a glorified stalker. Tonight’s target was nothing spectacular. Just a solider in some mob boss’s organisation who’s taken more than his fair share of green from more than one job. Angelo Imperioli was his name. He should know better. He’s been a member of that particular mob boss’s operations for nearly a year now, he should know the rules and regulations and more importantly he should know the penalties for breaking them. Just another cocky kid getting ideas above his station. Nothing spectacularly ground breaking, but he is my target nevertheless. I stood in the shadows of the rather large billboard behind me and used my genetically developed vision to keep an eye on the door, then touched my earpiece once to receive the latest intelligence report from my father’s assigned operative. Apparently, the target was just around the corner. So, I readied one of low-yield explosive throwing stars from the assigned pouch half way down my left leg. I don’t particularly want to blow up any civilians; I’m not getting paid for that. But then, my attention was ripped away from the Italian Restaurant over the road, as I heard a number of heavy footsteps clomp up the fire escape stairway - on the other side of the billboard - and step gingerly upon the same rooftop. Them stepping gingerly told me one thing; they expected trouble and in all likely hood expected me. Quickly, I scaled the billboard and crouched at the top, attempting to masque my visibility. I was still in shadowy territory, but if any of these guys were good, they’d still be able to see me. I wasn’t trying to hide from them. They were all wearing long, trench coats, varying in colour, and they weren’t trying to hide the fact they were carrying a piece. However the strangest thing of all was: I recognised one of the men. He worked for my father. A rather shady character capable of anything, I sure as hell didn’t like where this was headed. I hurled my throwing star at the feet of the man I recognised, making sure that the explosive charge captivated inside the small metallic star was nullified and no danger before hand. As soon as the star hit the ground he looked up at me – whereupon I waved at him – and then dove as far away from the star as possible. The rest of them followed in suit. I sighed. My father could really pick ‘em. “Get up dumbass,” I called to him. “I nullified the charge.” The man stood, obviously embarrassed, absently brushing down his trench coat. “Very funny.” “I was trying to be funny,” I told him. “Yeah, whatever,” He replied. “I’m about to do a hit here,” I said, not really feeling it particularly necessary to explain the situation to him one bit. “Fuck off.” “We know,” He told me. “Look, don’t take this personal Alexa, but your father has told us to come here as part of an extra curricular training exercise while out in the field.” “Training exercise?” I asked, stumped for meaning. “Obstacles while on a hit. Standard training really.” The man explained. “Obstacles?” I questioned, the meaning still not visibly apparent to me. The man glanced around at the rest of his crew. “Us.” “Let me get this straight,” I began. “You’re here to be an obstacle to me making a hit?” The man simply nodded in response. I watched his right hand lower slightly, toward his piece, strapped in his holster. His two other friends did the same. “Fantastic,” I muttered. The lead man went for his piece – as did the others – but I reacted far more quickly than they’d expected. I’d jumped down; feet first, at the lead man before he could even fire one off. That put me in the middle of the other two, were stupid enough to fall for the trick. They both fired and I moved out of the way. Each of them caught a slug in the shoulder from the other and they slumped to the floor in agony soon after that. I shook my head at their pitiful resistance, then made my way over to the shadowed area at the edge of the building, overlooking the Italian restaurant just in time to see Angelo Imperioli’s car pull up outside. Quickly, I fished in the pouch attached to my left leg and pulled out another throwing star. This time I set the explosive time with my thumb and hurled it at the side of the car, near to the gas tank. Imperioli hadn’t even managed to step out of the car before it exploded in a ball of flames. Job done. I just hoped the battle royal main event at this week’s Fresh would be as easy, though I had a feeling at the pit of my stomach that it would not. Nevertheless, I figure the best and toughest person will win the match. For all of my fans out there; don’t worry I intend to.
