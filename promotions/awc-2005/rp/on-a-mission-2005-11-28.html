On a Mission

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 28th Mike Carman kneeled down and examined the man who was spread out on the floor. He shook his head and chuckled a bit, AND then slapped the unconscious across the face. After many attempts had failed, Mike gave the man one last slap, leaving a red hard print. “Ah-what the hell,” he blurted, as he sprung into the air giving Mike a terrifying expression. “Kyle, you’re a dumb ass,” Mike replied, still laughing at Kyle’s grimace. The two stood up and exchanged looks. Kyle rubbed his face while Mike still giggled a bit. “What are you laughing at,” Kyle asked, giving Mike a grizzly roar of anger. This only humored Mike more, so he continued to point, stare and laugh while Kyle’s face began to resemble that of a tomato, red with anger. “How was the drinking last night?!” “Fine, why do you ask?” “You- you have barf crusted to your face, pfff!” Mike fell to the floor in a state of hysteria; he rolled around while Kyle peered down at him, with a stern look in his eyes, he kicked Mike in the side and that’s when the fight broke out. “Why you little,” Mike sneered and attempted to take Kyle down by his legs. It didn’t take much to take Kyle down to the floor. The two wrestled around for a little, but it was easy for Mike to pin Kyle down. As Mike hovered over Kyle’s body, the two suddenly began to laugh and all seemed well, until they heard a knock at the door. Knock. Knock. Knock. “Hey, Mike?” “What?” “That barf still crusted to my face?” “Shut up…” Mike rose from the ground and glanced over to the front door with squinty eyes. Mike gazed over at Kyle, who looked back with a curious expression which was centered on both their faces. “Mike, I thought you said no one knows where we are.” “I did, and I have no clue as to who this could be. Wait here.” Mike slowly crept over towards the door. The wooden floor creaked with each step and his eyes grew wider with each passing second. He reached it in no time, but he staled for brief moment. He then reached out and grasped the doorknob with a smile preparing for the unexpected guest(s), but for some reason he stood there. With his body growing heavier, and his heart racing, he took the initiative to check by using the peephole. He pressed his eye against the glass, and then jumped back with a startled look in his bold eyes. “Oh shit,” he whispered to himself, though it still was loud enough for Kyle to catch. “What, who is it?” Mike turned and looked at Kyle, who still sat there on the floor looking concerned up at the door. Mike held his finger to his mouth indicating silence. Mike proceeded to open the door. As he turned the doorknob, he swallowed hard and braced himself for a rude awakening. As the door hinge squeaked to life, a scraggly looking man appeared at the doorway. Mike peered up at the man, who smiled, displaying the yellow decay that rotted within his mouth. “Hello Mike, it’s me, Josh.” He poked his head into the doorway and looked down at Kyle. “There he is, excuse me.” Josh pushed by Mike and staggered over to Kyle, who now tried to scurry away. It was no use, Josh reached out and grabbed him by the neck; Kyle squealed and tried to squirm out of his grasp. Mike closed the door and turned back to observe the frenzy. “You know what I want Kyle, where’s my god damn money?” “I- I don’t have it right now.” “Don’t play with me, kid.” While Josh kept Kyle restrained with his death grip, he reached into his coat pocket and slung out a small pistil. It wasn’t until the gun was pointed to Kyle’s head for Mike to be some what frantic. “Hey, Josh what are you doing? C’mon man, he’ll have your money in a week, I swear. Mike took one step forward but was stopped in his tracks with the sound of a hammer cocking back, but what really made Mike jump was the gun itself, pointed right for him. He stumbled backwards and pressed his back into the door. “Whoa, put the gun away Josh, let’s talk this out.” “He had his chance, and if I don’t get my money right now someone’s going to die.” He swung the gun back to Kyle’s head in demand of his money. What needs to be known is that Kyle was held responsible for selling some of Josh’s weed, but like in the movie Friday, he ended up just smoking it all. Mike shook his head and mumbled under his breathe, “God damn pot head…” “Bet you boys are wondering how in the hell I found you. Ha, I got my connects and you should know by now not to run, because I’ll find ya, one way or another. It’s too bad that one of you is going to die, but whom? Might just waste both of you, like anyone would miss you too fuckups.” The gun was pressed firmly in the back of Kyle’s head. Mike looked on in amazement, he couldn’t do anything so he just stood and prayed for a happy ending. Mike had an idea though, one that could possibly get him killed. “Ah fuck,” Mike screamed, catching the attention of Josh. His head twisted and his eyes met those of Mike’s. It was too late though, Mike was already charging towards him. “Stop, get bac- ahh.” Mike dove in the air and took Josh down. It really was surprising due to each others size. Mike, who was six one drove a monster down to the floor with ease, but a problem soon, would arise. “Kyle,” he screamed, “help me out.” For an instant he looked on in shock and after thinking for a moment he did what he was told and took off towards the others. He slid across the wooden floor as he desperately raced to save his friend, jumping through midair he managed to create a dog pile which consisted of Josh on the bottom while both Mike and Kyle lounged on top. It was Mike who wrestled around for the gun which still was gripped by Josh, but once Kyle aided with his teeth the gun slipped right out of his hand like magic. “You sonuvabitch, you bit me,” Josh erupted in anger and pain but couldn’t do much in this situation. The two still applied pressure and tried to keep the mad man down, but something happened. He suddenly ignited in rage and threw Mike and Kyle off at the same time. The two smacked against the floor while Josh brushed off his suit with a glimmer in his eye. While the two tried to obtain their balance, Josh realized the gun was in Mike’s possession, only because Mike held it point blank in front of him. “Isn’t it a tad silly that you’re willing to kill someone over some god damn bud? Get the hell out before I blow a hole through your god damn skull, fucking prick.” Josh took a deep breathe and exhaled with fear locked in his eyes. “No- no you can’t do this,” Josh muttered. Mike shook his head spontaneously with a plain expression sprouting into a devious smile, which really was disturbing. Kyle overlooked the situation with no real remorse for Josh; if Mike were to pull the trigger Kyle would probably be thankful. So what if he cheated Josh, he disliked the man for what he had caused. So there Kyle stood, still eyeballing both men with no intention to intervene. “Kyle, open the door,” Mike shouted with out taking one eye off Josh. The M-9’s hammer was cocked back while Mike still clamped the gun firmly within his hands. His finger never left the trigger, he was ready. Kyle nodded his head, turned the doorknob and swung the door open. A cold rush of wind swept through the cabin furiously. All in the room covered their eyes as the snow shot in through the door. It was his only chance, Josh noticed that Mike was not paying attention; he jolted towards the door with no concern of the gun. Mike was pummeled to the ground as Josh shouldered through him to reach his destination. BANG … BANG BANG “Holy shit, Mike…” Mike had cold feet, it wasn’t from the cold it’s self but because a dead man laid face down in the snow with three bullet holes in his backside. Would anyone care though, that this drug lord lay dead before two men. Who would miss such as stern and heartless man? These two men certainly would not, but Mike still in shock, let go of the gun. It rattled as it hit the floor, and Mike found himself dead inside. He never imagined that a force so strong could make his insides wither away with a sickening numbness that left him feeling empty. The emotions were hard to illuminate with words but he knew and never would forget that sensation. Mike turned his attention to Kyle, who was busy digging through Josh’s pocket. “What in the hell are you doing?” “I have to check for ID, something, anything.” “Why, he’s dead, what difference does it make?” “Well, he might have a little baggy of weed too.” “WHAT THE FUCK!” “—huh?” Mike ran over to Kyle and threw him away from the corpse. He peered down at the body, but quickly turned away and faced Kyle with an angered mug. “Kyle, how can you think about that during a time like this? What we need to be worrying about is what to do with this god forsaken body” Mike protested as he shoved Kyle away from the bloodied corpse. As the two men proceeded to dispose of the body, a crimson stain trailed the two as the lifeless body was dragged through the snow. There was no time to stop and tidy up, for a man had just been killed and the last thing these two needed was a life sentence in the big house. “Now what,” Kyle asked as the two men hurled the body over a nearby ravine. Mike watched as the body slowly tumbled down until it no longer could be seen. He turned his attention to Kyle and with little hesitation he simply replied. “Get as far away from here as possible.” “Yeah, I’ll go pack.” Mike nodded his head and watched while Kyle scampered through the snow to reach the cabin. He felt better knowing that his friend had taken his side, and wasn’t too shocked by the incident. Though he was certain Kyle would have done the same, he was hesitant to pull the trigger because of him. It was done and over with, he wanted to forget about it and move on. Other more important things were on his mind. Well, a more important thing, wrestling. One week earlier “I made it, they accepted me,” Mike yelled in joy, as he ran over to Kyle with a letter in hand. Kyle peered down at the paper and a small grin overcame him. “That’s sweet man, ROAD TRIP” “Hell yes. First stop, the cabin.” The two men danced around with big smiles. Mike had finally found a home; he just prayed that it was the right one. Present “AWC, my first big gig. I’m ready for my first match, Acey Lavado doesn’t stand a chance.” Mike Carman nodded his head and followed Kyle’s trail back to the cabin. The two men packed quietly, but both had a lot on their mind. Everything is clear to me now. I know now what I have to do. I have hopes and dreams that I once never thought possible. I believe I can accomplish them and make a name for myself. AWC is where I belong. I can already tell. My first match will be a great test. Can I make it here on my own. I guess I will soon find out. I do believe I have what it takes. Acey Lavado stands in my way. Not for long, though. And my dream begins. I know what I have to do, I know this is what I want and I’m not going to let anyone stop me. Acey is my first opponent here in AWC, and in my wrestling career. I’m not sweating it; I believe I have what it takes. My style is unique and I would be quite impressed if this man could counter. I guess I will just have to find out though, I just hope I start out well here. I don’t want to be a low carder, I want to work my way up and earn title shots, I want to become a legend. All of that will soon come, but for now I must worry about other things. I just want this to be real, I want to become what I’ve always wanted. I want to be successful and rich, I want to be a professional wrestler. This is my chance. I’m not going to waste it.
