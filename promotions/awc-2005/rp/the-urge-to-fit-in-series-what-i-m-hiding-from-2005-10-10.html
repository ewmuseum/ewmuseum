Date: 10/10/05 'Didley-eye-dil-do-dil-da-Didley-eye-dil-do-dil-dee-Didley-eye-dil-do-dil-da-Didley-eye-dil-do-dil-daddy!'. Ah yes it was time for celebration on what they called 'The Emerald Isle'. Yes The Emerald Isle, Irelands nickname. Not to be confused with the 2 shithouse tag teams formed from a nacker and 2 fake Irishmen. The cause for this celebration? No it wasn't St Patricks Day, no we didn't retreive our 6 counties back. Hell it wasn;t even because Paddy O Shea, the biggest disgrace to Ireland since Tony Cascarino pretended to be Irish, is probably dead. No the celebration is because Mike Wade, Irelands FAVOURITE son, is now AWC Relentless Champion. As he is paraded through the steets of village, town and city alike streamers float into the air and fall from grace with the windiest of turns sprinkelling down onto the new champion. Praise is herralded from every man woman and child as Mike sits aloft a red cadillac waving as if he were queen of England with his cupped hand rocking back and fourth.......... Ok it is a slight exageration. There is a certain celebration. But it is confined to the sitting room of Wade Manor, Mikes residence. And is confined also to only Mike Wade and his ever present straight life partner Mickey Moore. (Who also happens to be a 2 foot 5 inch 'leprechaun' fo those of you who've been living under a rock.) Our hero, Mike Wade, lies on his leather couch a broken man. Yes he is a champion and yes he has successfully completed another swerve and returned to his truely evil roots. But for some reason he seems unsatisfied. The only satidfaction on this day is not his thick golden belt that he took home after beating an almost forgotten nemisis, not his spectacular swerve involving new friend Facey. No on this day his only satisfaction is as he buries his head deep in the leather couch his nostrils pick up that new smell of leather...........What? Is he the only one?? Mickey looks concerned for his pal. His 256 year old forehead always showed signs of ageing, but his friends increasing unhappiness had worried him the last few weeks, now the wrinckles accross his forhead were like great crevaces in the underpants of life....yes I said that. He leaned towards his masters chair..... Mickey: Laddie what's the matter? Mike: Urrfffghhhhmmmmmmm Mickey: Jaysus laddie ya answered me in me native toungue. A language I've not heard since long long ago since me pappy was but a........ As Mickey rabbits on with what is clearly complete bullshit Mike raises his weery head. His usually handsome(ish) face is riddled with little cuts and bruises after his hefty fall last week. Mike: I told you to fuck off, but through the leather it sounded like, Urrfffghhhhmmmmmmm. Mickey: Arrggghhhhhhaflammmmm Mike: Mickey I did not speak in toungues how many times do I have to fucking tell you? Mickey: How did you knwo that's what I said? Mike: WHAT?? Mickey: I asked you how did you learn to speak in toungues. And you said you didn't! Mike you're a natural bisexual! Mike: Mickey I'm sure you mean bilinguist, as I do not like the sausage as well as the cake. Mickey: I'm not quite following. Mikes patients were wearing thinner than normal ith his dim witted partner. Mike inhaled deeply so as to gather his anger. Mickey, stupid as he his picked up on the vibe. Mickey: Laddie what's wrong? Mike: Huh? What do you mean? Mickey: I mean I thought you were unhappy first because the Farmer wouldn't believe you wanted to be friends with him and Paddy O Shea? Mike: I was! Mickey: But you didn't want to be friends with him and Paddy O Shea! There it was again. The deep inhaling. Mike was thought the technique a couple of years back when he had a penchant for punching people who pissed him off. It was no way to treat ones Granny! He bit his lip and explained. Mike: Yes but I wanted him to believe it so it would be funnier when I swerved the feckers! Mickey: Ah I see. Mickey sat back in his seat, almost satisfied but not quite. Mike knew there was more coming but silence didn't cease. Mickey went back to his almost fetal position in the chair but Wades interest with sniffing his couch was gone. He sat there nearly anticiapting another mini investigation from Mickey. There it was....... Mickey: Do you m...... Mike: I fuckin knew there was more. Mickey: I just wanna know? Mike: Look Mickey I already told you when a man and woman are very much in love they decide to have a baby. He takes his 'stick of love' and puts it in the womans 'love pocket' and.......... Mickey: No not that......although we'll get back to that later! I just wanna know why you DON'T wanna be friends with Paddy and the rest of the Irish wrestlers. That was it. It was the question Mike Wade wanted to be asked since he walked into AWC. DId he know himself why? Too fuckin right he did! Mike: It's very simple Mickey. See I walked into this industry 6 odd years ago and was told that Irish wrestlers on a national scale just did not work. Because with certain nationalities that were not American, came certain stereotypes. It's like a reflex. The first thing you think of when you hear a nationality is it's stereotype: German.....................Nazi!! French......................Bread lovin pansy!! Pakistani..................Apu!! English....................Posh fuckin prick!! Irish.........................Scruffy pikey that loves drinkin, fightin and being an all round scruffy fucker! If there was one thing I wasn't going to be in this business it wasn't gonna be............... Mickey: Pat Pattersons own personal whoopy cushion? Mike: What? Who the feck is he? Anyway................. I spent the last 6 years of my life and the 6 years of my career trying to break down the walls of that Irish steroetype. That I didn't live in a caravan, I didn't only eat potatoes, although they are tasty with everything. Mickey: True. Mike: I was going to be remembered for my wrestling ability, my charisma, my good looks and the fact that I'm so cool that I could wear black and navy together and pull it off!! However. I walk into AWC and what do I see? My county, my beliefs all being made a mockery of. You have one Irishman going around dressed up like a homeless pikey. Talking like they think we talk with his 'top o d mornin ta ya' gimmick and his flea ridden hair, Paddy O Shea was everything that I battled against. And you know what made me sick? He was considered a bigger star than me! They consider a scruffy, homeless, inbread person like him to be higher in the pecking order then a man that has held titles throughout every federation he's been in. One of the most decorated wrestlers in the history wrestling. I turned on Paddy O Shea because he is a disgrace to my country. His reckless playing up to the fast minority that is the travelling community has sent my work 6 years back and gotten me back to where I've started. The only way, in my mind, to get some sort of respect back was to fuck his shit up......for lack of a better term. Mickey: So all that REAL Irish stereotype attitude really bothers you? Mike: Oh yeah.....like some of my reltatives. Man they're so primative.... As Mike is complaining about his embarrassing relatives Mickeys face slumps. Lower and lower until his little chin is almost hitting the ground. Mike: ............you've brought them haven't you?? Mickey: No no........ Glee accross Mikes face Mike: Thank God. Mickey: Just one........ Panic, panic now Mike: Which........one It looked as if Mike had swallowed a basketball in between the two words. His forehead had started to perspire. Thoughts of his image being ruined ran through his head, over and over. His brain, hs conscience felt like a hamster running through the ever turning wheel. Never getting anywhere on the verge of cracking and then.... SUPRISE!!!!!!!!!! There she was. Standing in the threshold of Mikes living room, Nana Wade. Her pink floral dress lit up the room enough, he didn't need her 'blooming' personality. Yes the dress was the remnants probably of the set of curtains Anne Frank used to hide from the fuckin Nazis, that's how old it was. It as the fugliest thing he'd ever seen, until the camera panned up to show her wrinkled old haggered face. Cottage Cheese had nothing on the melted fuglyness of this woman. And her ignorance? For us it was bliss, but for Mike? No. Her first move was every Nanas first move, she went for the face.....ANYTHING BUT THE FACE NANA!! Nana Wade: Ah Mikey laddie give yer Nana a feckin kiss! She reached for Wade and locked on with her hands. The locking continued, this time with her lips locking his. Mike: AH!!! Twas like kissin a feckin wrinkey auld whore! Nana Wade: Ah he loves he's Nana don't ya Mikey? Mike: Nana nobody calls me Mikey it's just Mike now. Mickey: Is that right..........Mikey?? Mike: Shut your mouth! Nana Wade: What's that on your face laddie? Nana clearly pointing to the bruises Mike sustained not only in his gruelling match with Ian English but in his huge fall from the suspended cage with Paddy O Shea. Mike: Nothing Nana. Nana Wade: Notin? It don't look like notin. We can almost hear Mike groaning at the atrociously bad grammar his Nana posesses. Nana Wade: Let Nana wash that right off..... Mike: Its not wash off-able Nana it's a br...... Without even finishing his sentence Nana has reached into her purse and pulled out the dreaded tissue. She unrolls it from it's ball ,God knows where it's been. Up Nanas nose? Up Nanas arse for all we know! She unrolls, and then proceeds to hock a loogie in it and rub it straight into Mikes face causing severe distess and embarassment. Mike pulls away as if some ugly bird has just drop the jaw on him. Mike: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Nana Wade: There washed it right off! Now show Nana your new champion sash. Mike: It's not a sash Nana it's a belt! Nana Wade: Nonsense Michael belts are for faggots. Now show Nana you sash. Mickey sits bemused in the corner as Mike dies of embarassment while lifting up high his newly won AWC Relentless title. Nana Wade: Very nice love......hey where's that Sancho chap you used to hang around with? The coloured fella. Mike: He died last year Nana....... Mikes voice almost cracked a little for his fallen friend. However Nanas ignorance and impassion lived through. Nana Wade: Good I didn't like him. He was brown. I don't like brown people! Mike and Mickeys head rise almost at once knowing that if people catch wind of this promo surely there will be racial uproar! Nana Wade: Now I'm all for getting a tan like anyone else. But that fella had a year round tan, it never went away and that's just not good for business!! Make Nana a cup of tea there midget!!
