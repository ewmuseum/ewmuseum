Unleashed

17 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/17/06 The commissary of the Cintas Center was bustling with activity when Megumi came in from the parking lot. A part of Xavier University, the Cintas Center was a small arena in Cincinnati, Ohio, not well known for it's rich wrestling history. But AWC was going to change that with a big show. It wasn't Riverfront Stadium or the Cincinnati Gardens, but if Dave Harber thought he could pack in five to seven thousand in a wrestling starved city, he was more then welcome to it. It didn't matter to Megumi Hamada what kind of crowd the next Fresh! would draw. She was just happy the trip was over and they were back in the United States. Walking into an arena the night before a big weekly show was much more nerve wracking then one might think. In order to pack the house and bring in the fans everything had to be perfect or the next time around the horn business would suffer. So everyone was running around making positively sure everything was in place. The wrestlers themselves would come in to go over their matches, discuss traveling plans, and try to find company for the night. Friends would talk, enemies would argue, and the place was always abuzz with dealmaking and clandestine meetings. Megumi walked in to the banquet hall, head down, not interested in talking to anyone or listening to anything going on. Jack Murphy had beaten her in at Winter Warfare. News had come to her that Tozen Fujita might not possibly walk again due to severe spinal injury. She kept her head low and her profile lower. She wanted to drop off her bags and head back to the hotel. But here eyes were narrow and sharp, making sure she wasn't being followed. The CYDONIA Hunter could strike at anytime, anywhere. She wouldn't let another of her friends down again. Butch Radder, AWC security, was a dour, silent, beer-swigging brute whose sandstone eyes and frowned eyes missed nothing under his scrutiny. But for all his wildness and blue collar demeanor he had taken a liking to Megumi right when she entered AWC a few months back. Megumi had no friends in AWC but Butch had become a close associate and he watched out for her whenever he could. Megumi didn't know why Butch had taken a special interest in her, but she didn't mind. Whenever she saw him he would smile at her and help her out with anything she might need. As usual, he met her as she walked through the door with a low brow and the crack of a smile curling ever so slightly on the corner of his lips. 'Genki des ka, Megumi-san?' 'Genki des,' she answered him politely. 'What can I do for you kid? Place is kinda rowdy today. Shows tomorrow, everyones nuts after getting back from the winter break.' 'I see. No thank you Butch-san. I just want to drop off my gear and head back out to the hotel,' she said demurely. 'Lemme know if you need anything.' 'Actually, I do, please. I would like to be seen.' Butch looked at her with a smile and a question in his eyes. 'Beg your pardon?' 'I haven't been around since we left Europe. I was not even around much when we were there. I don't want people to think I am a--- um, how do you say?' 'Stuck up?' Butch smiled at her. 'You shouldn't give a donkey's turd about what people think about you, Megumi. Punch in, punch out, show up for your matches, be on time, and don't worry about snot.' 'Snot?' Megumi thought she knew what he meant and he face crinkled. 'Snot. You bet. Now lemme carry your bags, madame. To the locker room we go. Good enough?' Megumi held back a laugh. 'Thank you, Butch. I will have definitely been seen walking with you.' Butch started heading for the dressing rooms but stopped short, and Megumi bumped into him. 'I almost forgot, darlin'. You got someone here to see you.' Her mood darkened instantly. Butch pointed across the banquet hall to a small corner of the room, hidden partially by a corner which led to the kitchen. When she caught a glimpse of orange and black fur she sighed heavily. 'Problem? I'll tell 'em you ain't here.' Megumi continued to look at the far off table attempting to gauge the meaning of this surprise visit. 'No, not a problem. That is my mentor and my teacher. Tyger Shuri-ken.' He has come a long way. But I did not receive any notice of his arrival. Perhaps he is here to talk to me about Tozen.' Butch nodded. 'Whatever it is, he's an obnoxious son-of-a-bitch.' Megumi understands how her former trainer can be very curt to the point of rudeness. She looks at Butch apologetically. 'No sweat, eh,' Butch reassures her. 'I'll make sure nobody disturbs you, hon.' 'You are a nice man, Butch,' Megumi stated. 'I been called a lot of things sweetheart. That one's a first. I'll drop your stuff off, no worries.' Megumi crosses the room with her head bowed, trying to keep a low profile, but everyone seems preoccupied in catching up or doing their jobs. Turning the corner of the room she sees Tyger Shuri-ken sitting at a table surrounded by what she assumes are some of his young students. Tyger stands up, and bows to her, his tufted orange and black mask not uncommon in these circles. Dressed in a suit, he is an odd sight to see nonetheless, a grown man wearing a Halloween mask. But no one has ever seen Tyger Shuri-ken maskless before, the man wearing it everywhere he goes. 'Your surprise visits never cease to unearve me,' Megumi murmured with a smile as she greeted her teacher. The masked man dismissed her comment absently. He snapped his fingers and his students dispersed. 'Business is business. Your last venture went well, I trust?' 'If it didn't I would either be in a hospital or in a British jail cell.' She handed Tyger a small note she had kept in the breast pocket of her jacket. 'The money owed to CYDONIA was returned, deposited into the bank account you see there. And can you see to it that the informant, Big Daddy, gets the rest of his money?' 'Of course.' After a brief silence Tyger got right back down to the point. 'I heard of Tozen's attack. A shame. He was a good wrestler, a good man. I am CYDONIA Inc. will miss him.' 'As will I,' she replied softly. Tyger's words were a polite formula required by formality. Megumi's revealed genuine emotion. She looked up sharply. 'When did you hear of Tozen's attack?' 'Weeks ago. But I did not want to bother you. I did not want to lay another burden upon you. I made inquiries, but nothing has come of it.' 'Oh?' Tyger Shuri-ken regarded his pupil keenly. 'You know, of course, that this hooker... this shooter, was after you.' Megumi stared down at her clenched hands. 'I've come to that conclusion, yes,' she said evenly. 'Now, if you don't mind, could we please speak of other matters? Why are you here? Do you have another assignment for me?' 'No, I came here to discuss the CYDONI Hunter and his attacks,' Tyger said. He leaned forward to emphasize his words. 'I'm concerned about your safety, child. You must take steps to protect yourself from this attacker.' 'What would you have me do? Hide? Not a chance.' 'Far from it,' said Tyger. 'You must seek out the CYDONIA Hunter.' 'From what I have heard, many seek him.' 'Ah, but they are not looking in the right places. As a prize CYDONIA agent, you can find him or her. And the best place to look is in the ranks of CYDONIA itself.' Megumi drew in a sharp breath. 'The hunter is a CYDONIA member?' she demanded to know. 'Yes,' Tyger noted. 'Or a CYDONIA client.' She considered her teacher's words and nodded slowly. The thought was an appalling possibility, but it made sense. CYDONIA was a corporation that handled clients and wrestlers, managers and promoters. Many in the wrestling business were members but there were also many who were simply clients and brokers, working for a piece of someone's contract or a cut of a gate of a wrestling event. Many affiliations were kept secret for business reasons. It didn't seem all that incredible to Megumi that somewhere, somehow, these business dealings and under the table alliances would go sour and someone would take it upon themselves to impart out some revenge. And Megumi trusted Tyger's instincts. he had trained her for 5 years, and was a member of CYDONIA Inc. for much longer. If he thought there was a hooker ending careers within the ranks of the corporation, he was probably right. Tyger's urgent voice broke into her reflections. 'You must find this hooker, and soon. The wrestling world puts its' trust in CYDONIA and what we offer to everyone from wrestlers to promoters. If we cannot find and stop this renegade hooker, it will damage the companies reputation, something that in this game is very difficult to get back.' Megumi's eyes were suddenly in a far off place. She was being asked by her teacher and mentor to hunt down a reputed hooker whom was possibly out to end her career for reasons completely unknown. This didn't bother her as much as the fact that she had come through her second month in the AWC and couldn't even beat the likes of Chainz or Jack Murphy convincingly. Would she be able to keep her commitments to both CYDONIA and the AWC? And what about Liam Martin and their upcoming match at Fresh? Tyger paused for a moment, noticing Megumi's hesitation. 'Have you any idea the implications of this? If CYDONIA were to break, its' reputation marred, then many people would be out of jobs. You would be back in Japan in no time, your sponsorship here in the United States dried up. Joshi is dead in Japan. Mixed Martial Arts has taken our country by storm. Our profession is dying a slow death as we speak in our country--' 'I know what CYDONIA has done for me,' Megumi said with a touch of impatience. Tyger had lectured her on the importance of CYDONIA for her country and its' national sport before many times, since she was eighteen years old. She knew his arguments by heart. 'Do you have a plan?' 'Yes. I would suggest you ferret out this attacker. And the best way to do that is to start to win your matches in AWC.' 'You noticed,' Megumi said with a low tone. Tyger sat there, eyes black as coal, not saying a word. Megumi shook off her slumping attitude. 'Yes, you are right.' A slight humorless smile flickered across her face. 'At any rate, it is better then doing nothing. Just waiting for the hooker to come to me is intolerable. I can't keep it up much longer. If I win, I will be noticed. The higher my name rises in the rankings, the more he will be tempted to strike against me and then we can finish this.' 'I sense... you are unnerved by all of this. You have been in fights your entire life. You are a premier shooter for CYDONIA and your very life has been in danger more then once.' Tyger paused and eyed her keenly. 'Is there something else?' 'There is,' she admitted reluctantly. 'For some time now, several motnhs actually, I've had the sense that I was being watched. Followed. But try as I might I could find no trace of anyone or anything.' 'Yes?' She expected more, a question, a comment, maybe even criticism. 'You don't look surprised.' 'Your hunter could be in this very room.' Tyger responded evenly. 'Hookers and shooters are a dying breed, but they are also a highly skilled and determined lot. They know how to blend in, how to not draw attention. And if this is truly a hooker, then he will make sure he is not detected until the very last moment. Do you understand me, girl? You are dealing with a professional hooker. Their job is to hurt people, permanently. Do you think he will just walk up to you and challenge you to a fight? This is why you must take the offensive. Find him before he decides to strike against you.' 'Agreed.' With a wave of his hand Tyger quickly changed the subject.'Now what about Liam Martin?' His gesture was meant to belittle Megumi's next opponent. 'It is time for me to start winning, you say. And I agree. And win convincingly at that. The Furious Fists of God are a disgrace to this profession. The Martin brothers prophecize and preach hate and racism. There is nothing more in this world I would like more then to beat Liam Martin inside the ring, 1-2-3. What would that tell them about their white supremacy? What would that teach them? Probably nothing. Men like that are ignorant and blind to the truth. Just like Chainz, just like Jack Murphy. Even after I beat Jack Murphy in the ring he would not repent, he would not admit that I was more then capable of handling myself in a fight. The Martin brothers are the same. Stupid and stubborn.' Anger crossed Megumi's face for a moment, but it quickly passed. 'I am going to demolish Liam Martin in the ring, embarrass him, humiliate him. Not out of hatred or anger. Because I am simply better then him. Not because of my beliefs or his, but because I have the fighting spirit within me. The fighting spirit that was instilled in me since I was a little girl, training day and night in the dojo above the old restaurant where I would serve tables during the say. My life I have given to fighting. Liam Martin has given his to paranoia and persecution of things he does not understand and fears.' Megumi looked deep into the black eyes hidden behind the mask of Tyger Shuri-ken, convincing him in no uncertain terms her intentions and her focus were clear. 'Let him and his brother make fools of themselves on TV every week. Let them lie, let them advocate fearfulness and stupidity. Before the night is over, I may just convince them they DO have something to fear from Butterfly Hamada. My job is to win matches inside the ring, not convert people to my way of thinking outside of it. White, black, Asian, it makes no difference to me. I am here to fight and I am here to win. They have their agenda and I have mine. When all is said and done, I will bring my best game to the ring... and let God sort out the rest... and Liam Martin.'
