Crack Head Chronicles - Chapter II

6 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-06-2006 † In the Mind of a CRACK HEAD † Taking risks is something that you have to do in your lifetime. If you don’t take risks then you will become a failure. For Jason Ortiz, the risk of making it big in AWC was something he had to overcome. He wanted to become the best. He could see that his future in AWC looked good. But there were other things besides his wrestling career that he would need to take a risk at. In his past he had be involved with the local Miami gangs. Preferably, his former gang the Seven’s. At times he wanted his gang to succeed on the streets of Miami, Florida. He took the risk of trusting people that shouldn’t - well, be trusted. Jason was stabbed in the back. Betrayed. His gang was dismantled and was know longer had the POWER and PUSH that it once did. At one point on the street’s, Ortiz was considered the king, the mayor of Miami gang scene. This gang scene was different from what people are used to seeing. This was not about pride and honor, going around and killing each other over the color that they wore, no - it was about POWER and PUSH. To have the power on the streets of Miami, to tell anyone where to push there drugs gave Jason Ortiz that adrenaline rush. He had been more about running small business. Most of his money, before he got into wrestling had come from his businesses in the North District of Miami Florida. J-Dizzal had owned almost one-third of the North District. Technically he didn’t really OWN the property, but he had the POWER to go into a local business and tell them how he wanted it ran. If they didn’t obey, there would be consequences. Jason was never a violent guy, and resorted to using violence as his last draw. He had used his members of his gang to do the violence for him. Now, since the fall of the Seven's, Jason Ortiz has lost a lot of street credit, in fact, he is a nothing on the streets of Miami, Florida. You go into a local business, or ask the youth who Jason Ortiz is and they will answer you with... WHO THE FUCK IS THAT? Exactly. Since he was betrayed by his gang, and half of his members were either dead or no longer living in Miami, it would be hard for him to get back on top. Sure he recently went out of retirement and came back to wrestling, but wrestling is not a twenty four-hour job. He couldn’t live off of wrestling. Having all of the power in Miami was what he lived for. Jason wanted it back. He wanted to be on the top again. He wanted to be the man in charge. After the fall of his empire, the streets had been run by mostly the Cubans and the Russians of Miami. J-Dizzal wasn’t afraid of the Cubans, knowing that they could be easily taken out of power, but the Russian’s, that was a different story. They had been his biggest problem while he was in power. They were always testing him. Seeing if he was always on his feet. Since he Jason is no longer in power, most of the businesses that he has run are being run by the Russians’. There were many reasons why Jason wanted to get these scumbags out of power in Miami. The Russians were all about using there power to bring in more drugs in the country- preferably cocaine. That was the Russians weapon of choice. They used these small business like grocery stores or small clothing shops to ship their drugs too. They were also using these small business to sell out of. To make a quick dollar. A profit for themselves. Ortiz was not about that. When he was in power, he used these small business to make money for him. Jason Ortiz would make them invest their money into stocks and bonds. This way he was guaranteed to make money. Jason was not a drug dealer; he was not some fucking drug addict that would use the youth to make money off of. This is why he wanted to get back in the scene of the Miami streets. He had heard from his Nazi friend Steve Scott that the Cubans and Russians were combining together now as one unit. Jason heard that the Russians were using the Cuban’s ties to getting drugs from Cuba rather than Russia. This was a lot easier to access drugs form Cuba than Russia. They could get it cheaper and in bigger shipments. The police in Miami weren't doing shit about it. They were to busy worrying about the prostitutes and the robberies. What they need to focus on is how to get these fucking Russians out of Miami for good. Jason Ortiz has to put a stop to these fucking pricks. But how? How can he get back on top and take back what was once his, the North District of Miami Florida? Location: North District. Miami, Florida Place: Steven Scott’s Apartment Time: 2:07 p.m. Jason Ortiz‘s body moved around the small apartment living room rather slowly- avoiding every obstacle that was in his path. There was used beer bottles on the floor. Newspapers and cut up magazines. He glanced around the room, seeing that no light what so ever was coming through the windows. His eyes tried to adjust to the darkness but seeing that his Nazi friend lived like a fucking slob he couldn’t really confront him about it. That’s why when Steven moved in with Jason for a short time at his Miami Beach house, the whole house was trashed. It took several days to clean up the mess that the Nazi left. Steven Scott’s apartment was the same way. For a few weeks now Jason hasn’t really seen much of Steven Scott, mostly because his mother was sick in the hospital again and he had to take care of her. That’s pretty much all the man lives for. His mother. He has no other people to care for, except for Jason Ortiz. Out of all of J-Dizzal’s friends, he is the only one that has stuck by him through think and thin. When things were tough on the streets of Miami, when his empire was falling, Steven Scott stood beside him until his breaking point where he lost everything. Now, it was time to get back the empire that was once there. The empire that ruled the streets of Miami. There were a few different ideas of how he was going to make this run different from his last run as leader. Jason was going for more of a smaller group than his last gang. Before, there was over fifteen different members in his gang, that is not including the sub-members who weren't really in his gang, but had aligned themselves with Jason Ortiz. This time it would be a group of four people. Jason Ortiz, Steven Scott, his longtime friend Blake Hudson and - well there wasn’t really a fourth person yet. He had this persona of hiring someone for the job, but that’s the problem. When you hire someone how can you trust them? Are you sure that once you trust this guy he isn’t going to fuck you over. It has happened before to Jason Ortiz. You take someone into your family and then... boom you get fucked over. Jason had a few idea’s who he wanted to fourth person to be, but he had to go through the process of interviewing them. Talking to them- to make sure they are right for his empire. “When’s the last time you ever used a vacuum Steven,” muttered J-Dizzal as his shoulder plowed into a stack of newspapers, forcing them to fall from the coffee table onto the living room floor. Steven came running into the room and stared at Jason Ortiz for a second. He looked into his green eyes, then his left eye wondered from Jason’s face to the stack of papers on the living room floor. He grinned. “Hey, I'm the one that has to live here Jason,” Steven continued, “your not the one that has to clean up the fucking mess.” A Mess. This whole house was a disaster pit and it wasn’t because of Jason, know, it was the fact that the fucking Nazi was to lazy to take twenty minutes out of his busy schedule to clean up his apartment. Jason Ortiz stared at him for a few seconds before speaking to him. A few seconds of silence could be heard. “Are you fucking ready yet Steven?” The plan for the day was to go down to the local grocery store, which happened to be ran by the fucking Russians, get some drinks, come back to the apartment and watch a couple of movies. Well, that’s what Jason hoped would happen. He didn’t except that there would be any trouble between him and the Russians. Most of them stayed away from the places that they ran. There was always that possibility that he would run into someone that he disliked. Both men proceeded out the front door; Steven took the lead with his massive body taking up half the stairway. Jason Ortiz was not that far behind, staying close to his Nazi friend. He had his back if anyone wanted to start some shit. This was quiet apartment complex and nothing really BIG ever happens hear. Occasionally you get the old lady next door screaming about Steven’s music, but that’s about it. His apartment was on the south side of North District, behind the Quick and Go Car Wash. It was rather easy to get to the grocery store, all you really had to do is cut through the Car Wash parking lot, take a left and it was a mere three minute walk. Steven needed the exercise so this was good for him. He had become a little obese and was starting to have problems with his heart- well that’s what his doctor told him. It was a typical winter day outside, a little cold but still around eighty degrees. The humidity is what kills you in Miami. “Get the fuck off my feet!” snapped Jason Ortiz, his foot almost broke into two as Steven Scott jumped out of the way. J-Dizzal lifted up his right foot and started to rub it through his sneakers. He could see a little bit of frustration was growing on his face. “Maybe you should learn how to walk,” Steven spit, “and then you won’t walk underneath me.” The fact was Steven was so fucking huge he just walks all over you. The man is clumsy. He can’t walk for a short distance without bumping into you, knocking something over, breaking something. Jason took a deep breath in. He tired to hold his anger in with it. “Move your ass out of the way and stay at least fucking two feet from me, then you won’t run into me.” Steven smiled. “Did I hurt your feeee..,” he began to stutter. “Feelings?” The Nazi always tried to push Jason’s buttons. Actually Jason did the same thing. It was part of there love and hate relationship as friends. They had made their way through the Car Wash parking lot; the wet mist from one of the sprayers had brushed against J-Dizzal’s face. He let out a quick sigh. A few seconds had gone by and both men hadn’t said a word. Reaching the end of the front door, Ortiz grabbed the back of Steven’s shirt and pulled him back a little bit. Jason gave him a wink and Steven leaned down. “This place is owned by the fucking scumbag Russians. Keep an eye out, look for anything suspicious in the store, old boxes with Cuban flags or Russian flags on it,” Ortiz continued. “ I’m going to try and sneak around back, see if I find anything.” Steven Scott nodded. Both men stepped in the front door. The bell rang, letting the cashier over on the other side of the bulletproof glass that someone was entering the store. “Hello,” said the cashier, his voice cracker. “How are you guys today?” Both men answered him at the same time. “FINE.” Steven took a quick left down the isle with all the alcohol and beverage drinks. He stared into the glass looking for a twelve pack of Miller Lite. Jason, on the other hand, had made his way towards the frozen food section in the store; he kept his eyes wide open. He hadn’t blinked in over a minute. The way into the back was right next to the milk section; he turned his body around, glanced at the cashier to make sure he wasn’t looking before peaking his head around the corner. His body brushed up against one of the walls as his head peaked inside of the back room. There was un opened boxes in the right corner, to the left was what looked to be a break table, with two chairs next to the table, and a few stacked up against the wall. There was a small room next to the break table, it was hard to see if anyone was inside of it, so Jason took a few steps in, acting like he was lost in the store. He strutted around the room for a few seconds, feeling the cold air from the freezer hitting the top of his head. Ortiz’s arm lifted up- while scratching his head he heard someone as the back door opened. From where he was standing he could see that there was three men, two looked to be Russian, and the other looked to be of Cuban or white decent. It was rather hard to see from the position he was standing at. He found it kind of strange why there would be three people in the loading dock. “Don’t the trucks bring all the good’s thru the loading dock at night?” Jason asked himself. The door stayed open for a few more seconds, Jason noticed for those few seconds that on one of the man’s arm he had the distinct tattoo that the Russian gang lords have in Miami Florida. So this was one of the many places that the Russians had been doing their business out of. J-Dizzal wasn’t going to risk getting caught over finding out if it was true. He would rather wait until it was dark out. Sneak around back by the loading dock and get some answers then. For now, he had all the information he needed to start his journey back to the top of Miami Florida. It was time to collect Steven and get the fuck out of here. He rushed out of the back room and into the store again. The cashier was still in the same position reading what looked to be a magazine. He had seen nothing. There was no sight of Steven Scott. Jason Ortiz jumped high in the air- hoping that he would see the top of Steven’s head over the isles. Nothing. His body shifted, and he took a long walk down the frozen food isle. A few boxes were stacked about three feet high, and you could see that someone was behind them. Knowing that Steven would do something stupid like that, Jason knew it was him. “Steven,” screamed Jason from afar. “What the fuck are you doing.” Steven stood up quickly with a bottle of booze in one hand and a bag of what looked to be frozen green beans in the other. He launched the green beans at Jason Ortiz. J-Dizzal had no time to react, the bag smacked him right in the chin and it opened up all over the tile floor. He was shocked, and dazed for a few second. The Nazi threw something else, but it flew high and over Jason’s head into the back wall. Ortiz took cover from behind the sunglasses rack. He peaked his head around the corner and seen an empty bottle of beer on the floor. Yep, he was already getting drunk. Steven’s speech began to slur. “UGH!” “Put that shit down Steven, you’re going to get us in trouble.” He wasn’t listening to Jason Ortiz. “KILL THE RUSSIANS! KILL THE RUSSIANS WITH GREEN BEANS!” Typical Steven Scott, always trying to get a laugh out of the situation. “All right man, I hear you, but this game is over,” Ortiz pronounced, standing back up and walking towards Steven’s box shelter. The Nazi’s brow raised. “NO! I MUST KILL A RUSSIAN!” Standing up, Steven held a case of corn dogs, he arched his arm backwards and held it with both hands, it took Jason a few seconds to realize what he was going to do with the case of corn dogs. Out of the corner of Jason’s eye, the casher came running up to the both of them, waving his hands in the air. “Stop! Don’t throw anything in my store, you must leave!” With that said, Steven took one more look at Jason before launching the box high into the air.... What happened next would be a whole different story.
