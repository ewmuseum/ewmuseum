Philosophy of Me chpt #1 'Finding The Heart'

11 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th Sept Throughout life and throughout time man has asked many a question, questions no one knows the answer to and will never have an answer. Many philosophers and scholars have tried to find answers to these questions and each one failed their weak and worthless attempts. ‘If a man dreamt he was watching television, and then woke up in front of the television, would he remember his dream?’ this is a question man has pondered throughout time itself, although it may not have been in that exact context it has still been pondered and never answered. But today will be a day for many questions to be answered and many questions to arise. ------ The scene sets upon the ancient Jade Empire, I stand-alone in the center of a large temple, the symbol of fire is painted on the surrounding walls and a large Jade Buddha sits before me upon a stone shrine, and in front of it sit Four---What look to be ---Martial arts masters. They are sat deep in meditation with there eyes screwed shut and their legs crossed. The three Male masters hair is gray and tied up at the back with a long and have a straight gray beard that reaches down toward their torso. Their attire is of the poorest fashion, just merely cloth that has been strewn over them to protect them from the wind. But the single woman master, she was dressed in some of the finest silk garment I had ever seen, they where pink with a bold gold trimming around the cuff. Her hair was Brunette and her ponytail was grown down to her waist. “What am I Doing here? How did I end up in such a regal place?” I moved my head from the frozen position it was in, and I scanned my surroundings. The noise of battle that lay behind me grew louder and louder and I began to turn and walk toward the temple door. As I approached the large clearing, where the doors would be once closed, the view across the large Inner courtyard became clear and the view of battle was now visible. “What has caused all this bloodshed? Why do these people never cease from battle?” In the midst of the battle stands a large man, his hair is cut short, almost bald, and its fine bristles are a jet black. He has on a red plated armor; he has a long sword tucked under one side of his belt and a small dagger placed in a pouch dangling from the left side of his belt. He looks like a tough man, he is around 7 feet tall and his body is well built and he has the look around his face of a fearsome warrior. But he just stands there while 6 men pummel him and strike him with swords, but he dose not move, he holds no expression on his face he just stands and receives what is being done to him. “Why just stand there? Why not turn round and knock him out” ‘You ask many questions young one, many you must answer for yourself’. A deep but surprisingly refreshing voice came from behind me. I turned and looked at the shrine where the four masters sat. The first one of the masters sat with his hazel eyes wide open, he stared straight at me but it also looked like his eyes glazed over me as well. “Why does he stand their sir? Why does he not fight like the rest of them?” ‘He would fight, but he cannot. He is driven by madness and corruption he cannot fathom, he no longer holds emotion or feeling for any other human being, and fighting inside this temple stops his corrupt body from even defending himself until he has fathomed enough good to cleanse his spirit and give him ‘Rebirth’” I looked blankly, at the master as he opened his mouth to carry on what he had just said. ‘He is your heart, he is all that is wrong with you, he is what drove you to do what you did to Kris Krimzon. And only you can make him fight once again’ “And how do I do that?” ‘Only Time will tell’ End Chapter One
