Beating The House

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25.07.06 During the day time, Las Vegas is a ridiculously hot place, boasting a dry heat that most visitors never experience. Or if they do choose to step outside into the natural sauna that is Sin City, it’s only for the five minutes it takes to walk between casinos. At night it’s usually a more tolerable temperature but again only a minute fraction of the tourists spend any time in it. Unsurprising really, when you consider the wealth of activities that are available to them when the sun goes down. Sure, a lot of the stuff is open during daylight hours - the casinos, the boutique malls and the various rides and attractions. There is however, one facet of Vegas life that only opens come dusk, a major part of the city’s make-up and appeal to people, mostly males. That’s right, the strip clubs. Accounting for something like 6% of the total reported income of the city, you’d think that they were a pretty minor part of the nightlife in the city but when you consider that the casinos account for another 91% it becomes pretty clear that women taking their clothes off is a very attractive proposition for a lot of sightseers, whether they are gay or straight, man or woman or a bit of both. Like any business though, there is fierce competition between these establishments. There are the young upstart places, boasting a handful of dancers who are over worked and haven’t yet accumulated enough money to move up that cup size or two. Then there are the more established clubs, with a mixture of career strippers who are on the downward slope towards joblessness and those who have burst out of the tiny places after getting the surgery. The Black Mamba doesn’t fall into either of these categories. It’s one of the elite, the club that girls from around the country want to work in and one of the few that can afford to be picky about who it hires. Fake tits? You’re gone. Love handles? See ya. Not bilingual? Try somewhere else. It’s places like The Black Mamba that the high rollers go to when they want a lap dance and some good company. This is a place that is proud of being a gentleman’s club, with no prostitutes to be found and frequented by those with enough money to buy good taste. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Peter Donloe is a straight man, married, no kids, 35 years old and with a new inheritance burning a hole in the pocket of his Salvatore Ferragamo slacks. Peter’s daddy was a rich man - rich in the Tom Cruise sense of the word. Sadly for him, he was in a head on collision that apparently the designers of the Bugatti Veyron hadn’t allowed for in their blueprints. So, after the assets were split up between the most recent gold digger, the housekeeper, the gardener and the rest of the kids, Peter got a nice chunk of cash tax free. Money which he immediately decided to gamble in Vegas to help cheer himself up. Either that or to spite his wife, who doubtless had designs on using the money to buy a little cottage in Provence. And if there was one thing Peter hated it was those fucking French. He’d been hitting the blackjack tables pretty hard all day, dispensing with his casino host after only an hour or so but making Caesar’s Palace about a hundred and fifty grand better off in the process. It’d eventually registered that maybe blackjack just wasn’t going to cut it today so he’d moved onto roulette, then craps, then slots. When he checked his watch it was eleven thirty in the PM and he’d dropped another forty grand, pocket change to him if the truth be told but still, when you’ve been gambling for the best part of twelve hours you’d hope a win streak would come your way at some point. So, bored of the casino, with a lot of money to still waste and with the motto of the city in his head, he decided to hit a strip club. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* It’s a little known fact that the people who are the most knowledgeable about Vegas aren’t to be found in information stands or the tourist office. No, the men and women that know all the ins and outs of the city are none other than the valets so visible at every entrance to every hotel and casino on The Strip. Fortunately, this was something that Peter was aware of and as he stepped out into the neon lit night he was able to turn to a smartly suited Hispanic man with a name tag that read Juan and ask him for his opinion. “Well sir, that all depends on how much you’re looking to spend and what exactly you are looking for.” “Money’s no object. I just want somewhere with beautiful women and a well stocked bar. Nothing else, look at the ring.” “I see sir, I always like to be sure though. Well in that case I recommend The Black Mamba, a very nice club just a mile or so away.” “Thank you, could you call me a cab?” So Juan hailed a cab, gave the driver directions and received a very generous ten dollar tip for his troubles. Peter just sat back and enjoyed the view of the metropolis during the short journey, the driver’s voice startling him as the cab pulled to a stop outside a relatively nondescript building. It had the neon lights, is there anywhere in the city that doesn’t? Everything else though screamed, or should that be whispered, low key and exclusive. From the heavy wooden doors to the custom suit sporting doormen, the building said high class. “That’ll be six bucks.” The driver got a ten, a pretty reasonable tip for such a short journey, as Peter got out of the cab and walked over to the door. The bouncers carried out a quick search and a credit check, the second involving a look inside Peter’s wallet, before pulling open the mahogany doors and allowed him inside. “Have a good night sir.” Something in his tone made Peter feel sure that he would. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* It was about one thirty AM when Donloe decided to call it quits. There were after all, only so many tits and asses you could see before they got boring (yes, it is possible). The only problem was that he didn’t feel tired in the slightest and having gotten his ass thoroughly handed to him by every game in the casino, wasn’t really in the mood to go back. It was at this point that his cocktail waitress came over with the cheque for his bar tab and a very interesting proposition (no, not one like that). “Here’s your cheque sir and a small invitation for your consideration.” She then sashayed off back to the bar, Peter’s Platinum AmEx card in hand. He, meanwhile, was reading the small slip of card she had given him. At first he was uncertain about accepting the proposal given the way his luck had been going today and the fact that he’s already dropped nearly a quarter of a million dollars in less than 24 hours. Then again, if he lost, there wouldn’t be any hope of that house in France. Yes, his mind was made up, he was in. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Little did Peter know, however, that the private high stakes game he’d just accepted an invitation too was far from spur of the moment. In fact, this particular game had been a regular occurrence at The Black Mamba for the last year. It came round a few times a year, always at a weekend so as to get the visiting businessmen involved and one of the eight participants was always the same. This particular game, you see, was a nothing more than a scam. Although The Black Mamba made its owners rich, as is the way, they wanted more. Prostitution was deemed too risky to the club’s reputation, as were drugs. A spot of illegal gambling though, well, what gentleman would be opposed to that? Thus, the game became a staple of the establishment, taking advantage of out of towners with too much money and too large egos. The two hundred grand buy in meant that only the richest of the rich could be involved and the presence of one Julius Brooks meant that the house was almost guaranteed victory. Julius was one of those men with an uncanny skill for the game of poker. He possessed a blank stare and a scary ability to read others. If he’d wanted to he could have made a fortune on the professional tour but after weighing up the risks he’d decided to accept the rather tempting offer made by The Black Mamba club. To him, the pro tour carried too many risks, gave a guy too much notoriety. Here, on the other hand, he could be anyone he wanted and could relieve middle aged men of their money as easily as taking candy from a baby. It was a nice little racket. The club owners put up his stake and he got to keep five hundred grand of the winnings. He hadn’t lost a game yet and none of the saps who’d been in the games had the slightest idea they were being scammed. Tonight would be no different. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* The word had been put out not just through the club itself but through a number of waitresses at casinos along The Strip. A lot of time and observation went into the choosing of each and every participant in the game. They had to be spending a lot, have nobody else with them and most of all they had to be arrogant enough to believe that they would win. Donloe didn’t seem overly cocky but Kitty, the waitress who had extended the invitation to him, was sure that underneath the seemingly bored exterior lurked an overconfident streak that all rich guys had. Julius trusted her judgement, after all, she was his significant other. The other marks seemed to be much the usual assortment. Texas oil tycoon, son of a Greek shipping magnate, English estate agent and assorted others from around the fifty states. From the short reports included, it didn’t seem like any of them were on a particularly lucky streak today but were still throwing cash around with abandon. Perfect. The game was scheduled to start at two thirty which gave Julius just enough time to get a drink and get into character. Tonight he fancied being a New York stockbroker, unmarried, three kids - Janine, 7, Scott, 4, and baby Julie, 14 months. His wife would be Diane, who he met while at university in England. She was a paediatric nurse on a career break. That would do nicely, a perfect family in upper class America. There hadn’t been one of those for a while. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* It was down to just three - Julius, Peter and Edgar, the Englishman with a property portfolio of close to half a billion dollars. The other five had gone surprisingly quickly, much faster than usual, and Julius was sitting happily, looking forward to being half a million dollars richer within the next hour. That new Aston Martin would soon be occupying a space in his garage. Then, suddenly and surprisingly, Edgar was gone. He’s been a conservative player all night but totally out of the blue he’d decided to go all in. Julius had folded early in the hand after being dealt an abysmal eight-two hand, unsuited as well. Peter, however, had stayed in and ended up calling Edgar. As it turned out, the Englishman had been bluffing and Peter won with a simple 3 thru 7 straight. Julius looked on at all this impassively and prepared to move in for the kill. He’d probably give Peter a few low money hands to goad him into a move, then he’d wipe him out completely. As it turned out though, things didn’t exactly go the way Mr Brooks had planned. The first part of the scheme had been fine, he’d let Peter beat him on a few hands that he probably could have won but when it came to the hand that was meant to finish Donloe off, something strange happened - Julius got beaten. He couldn’t quite explain how it happened, having been dealt what was, ninety nine times out of a hundred, an unbeatable hand. Two aces to start with, then a flop of the ace of spades, the ace of hearts and the four of spades. The turn came up as the five of spades and it was at the stage that Julius went all in. Peter had fewer chips but agreed to make up the difference in cash if he lost, matching Julius’ bet as the river card was revealed. It was the 8 of diamonds and as the cards were turned, Julius readied himself to shake the other guy’s hand and say hard luck. Julius’ cards were turned first, revealing the four aces and causing a ripple of disbelief to pass round the room, where the vanquished players still stood, looking on. Donloe though, just smiled slightly. His cards were turned and there it was - Peter’s original two-three of spades hand combining with the ace-four-five in the community cards to make a beautiful straight flush. Julius’ face dropped as Peter pulled the huge mound of chips towards him. The odds were astronomical, he couldn’t believe it. He’d lost. His employers were not going to be happy men. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* It would seem that this tale should be heeded by one Josh Marquez, being as he is, a gambler. However, it is more applicable to Mr Pierce Lavelle, one of the greats in AWC. Lavelle is a lot like Julius in our story you see - supremely confident with a proven track record and fancied by those in the know to clean up. Just like Julius though, Pierce is subject to strange twists of fate and improbable odds that can bring him defeat rather than the victory he will doubtless expect. When he steps between the ropes on Fresh! to take on the AWC newcomer Josh Marquez, Lavelle will be a hot favourite. Anyone who puts money on Marquez is clearly a mental case, such a fool’s bet is it. It’s not too much of a stretch to say that Lavelle is the favourite to triumph in the battle royal that will constitute the main event on the very same show and really, it’s no surprise that this is so, given Lavelle’s track record. Though maybe, just maybe, the impossible will happen. Perhaps, on one rare occasion, the house will lose, despite it’s best laid plans. And if that does happen, well, imagine that.
