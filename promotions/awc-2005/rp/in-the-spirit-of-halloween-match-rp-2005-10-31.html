In The Spirit Of Halloween (Match RP)

31 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/05 'In The Spirit Of Haloween' refers more to the type of story I'm about to tell, not to the theme of the story itself. Enjoy my little excursion outside the box. It’s cold outside. Raining. Evening. Johnny Noble sits at his kitchen table. A tape recorder lay on the table in front of him. His chin is covered in stubble, think, rich, almost a beard. His hair is greasy and matted, he hasn’t washed in a while. An eerie sombreness adorned his face. He pressed record on the player. “Dead - all of them. Just one short year ago… I’ve thought about it a lot. I don’t see how I could have thought about much else recently. I doubt you’d be able to free your mind of the type of tragedy I’ve had to endure. And the worst part of all of it – answers. There are none. Not one. All I know for sure is that I’m alone here and that’s how it’s been the last seven days. The emptiness… well… that’s been lingering around the house since the accident…” “Let’s get a movie, dad!” proclaimed Benny from his side of the dinner table. Benny was a plucky 10 Year Old with a vivid imagination, brown hair and brown eyes. He liked to laugh a lot. Johnny shook his head, “I don’t think so, Benny. It’s a weeknight.” “C’mon, dad! I don’t have any homework or anything!” Johnny smiles, always one to give in to the pleadings of his children. On the verge of breaking he says again, “Sorry, Benny. You have school in the morning and you need your sleep.” The older sister, Lisa, thought she’d try her luck. With A’s and B’s on her report card and at the near adult ago of 13 she assumed she was a shoe-in for the honour of a movie and a late night. After all it was Thursday, practically the weekend. “How about me, dad? Can I get a movie?” “What is this? Tag up on dad day?” Johnny says with a mischievous smile, “Well, I’ll have you know that I have a tag partner of my own… mom, what do you have to say about this?” Sara, a beautiful woman by anyone’s standards. Easy on the eyes, yes. With a flattering, not flaunting, figure and delicate features. She had brown hair like her son’s and bright blue eyes. She smiled and turned on her husband. “Actually…” “What!?” Johnny declared in shock, “Well, if I’m outnumbered then I guess we simply have no choice!” The kids get all excited and begin bickering over what movie they’ll rent, but mom quickly jumps in and sets the record straight, “Hey, guys! No fighting allowed! You can both pick out a movie when we get to the store, but dad and me will decide which one gets rented! Now, you’d better get this table cleaned up before I change my mind!” The Noble kids get straight to work at tidying the place up so as not to jeopardize their chance at a weeknight screening. Before long they’re off and on their way. “You guy have your seatbelts on?” Sara asked cheerfully. “Yes mom.” The kids said in unison, followed by the familiar click of the buckle. “Blockbuster?” asked Johnny. Sara nodded, adding, “No late fees.” Johnny pulled the car out of the driveway and onto the road, the video store was ten minutes away, just on the other side of the highway. Either way as soon as he was out on the road Sara put her Cyndi Lauper CD in the stereo. “Time After Time?” Johnny asked. “Mais oui.”, ‘But of course’ Sara replied in a terrible French accent. Lyin’ in my bed I hear the clock tick and think of you… the whole car sang along. All smiles, until they crossed the highway. A green light doesn’t always mean go. A tractor-trailer lost compression in it’s air brakes and it hit the car at about 60mph (100 kph) sending it into the air, wrapping around a light post, and bending it before coming to rest upside down in a ditch on the far side of the intersection. “That was almost a year ago. I remember waking up as they were ripping the door off the car to try and free me. My family was gone. Time After Time was still playing in the stereo. It was skipping on the word ‘time’. I remember getting to the hospital. My family was all in the same room…” “Wait a minute,” Johnny started, “There’s not enough beds here.” “Please, Mr. Noble. We need to get you settled in. But Johnny looked around and there were not enough beds. Benny had one across the room, Lisa’s was adjacent to her brother’s, Johnny’s was on the opposite side of the room and that was it. “What’s happened to my wife!? Where’s Sara!?” The doctor, Dr. Languor, was genuinely confused. He scratched his head and checked his charts. “I’m sorry, Mr. Noble, but we have no record of your wife being admitted here.” “Was she taken somewhere else? Is she dead!?” Johnny started choking up with fear while lashing out in anger. “You tell me where she is!!” The doctor bit his lip, but all he knew was what was in the police report he was given a copy of. “The only female found at the scene was your daughter, Lisa. No one besides you three were found at the scene. “What about the truck driver?” “Whoever hit you must have kept driving. There was no one else.” “That doesn’t make any sense! I’m going to find my wife!” Johnny started pulling out his various cables and tubes so Languor jumped in to restrain him and called for a nurse who gave him a mild tranquilizer, he’d be out for an hour or so. “We were released the next day. My daughter and I were. The doctors said it was truly amazing how physically well we were after a crash of that magnitude. I’d say things were anything but ‘well’. My wife had vanished and I couldn’t get answers anywhere in that hospital. My son was unconscious and in critical condition in the hospital. Nothing was good about it. Except Lisa. I was so happy that she was uninjured. I knew what I had to do…” “That’s right. Yes. No, I don’t care what it costs. You find out. I’ll pay it.” Johnny Noble was talking to a private investigator the day he got home. “Thank you, Mr. Mortir. I’ll meet you there in an hour. Good bye.” Johnny was going to head back down to the crash site. It had been two days since the crash and he needed some answers. Within 20 minutes he was there with the P.I., Jack Mortir. They examined the site. Jack Made an auditory assessment. “Well,” Jack started, “Looks like you were hit there,” pointing at the middle of the intersection, “based on the skid tracks left by your car, which seem to stop shortly, I assume your vehicle left the ground at that point. Looks like your car was hit on the driver’s side… you say YOU were driving?” “Yeah” “That’s odd.” “What?” “An impact of that nature is usually crippling, and yet you’ve come away just two days later with no permanent or severe injuries. That’s… strange. Anyway. While airborne your car seems to have connected with this light post. At that point you were caught up in circles from the sudden change of direction caused by the post. Landing you on your roof… there. Were there any windows open in the car?” Johnny thought a minute, “No.” “Hmm, see, the thing is that the momentum was pushing everything in your direction, but you were still in the car, so no one went through you. As well, your daughter, who’s also fine, was in the seat behind you. So no one went through her window either. Which means they didn’t leave the car until you hit the ditch.” “Couldn’t she have been thrown through the windshield?” Johnny inquired. “Well, I suppose it’s POSSIBLE, but none of the momentum was going that way, and certainly not enough to push her through the windscreen.” The two stood, silent for several moments. Johnny was pensive and angstful. “Look, where’s the car?” “Unitow lot, I would guess.” “That’s what I thought. They’re the government recognized towers for this area. I’ll go down there and investigate. Mr. Noble, I’ll get to the bottom of this and I’ll keep you updated every step of the way.” “Fair enough.” The men shook hands and parted again. Later that night Johnny made a trip to the Hospital with Lisa to see Benny. They walked into his room and both of them panicked! Johnny started tearing the room apart and Lisa ran into the hallway screaming, “Where’s my brother!? Where’s Benny!? What have you done with him!?” Nurses and medical staff that were nearby rushed into the room to deal with the situation. Totally unsure of what exactly was going on all they saw was an hysterical girl and a man ripping up one of their rooms. “SIR!” One of the security guards bellowed, “WHAT are you doing?” Johnny turned around at the waist and looked over his shoulder at the man. Anger in his voice and rage in his eyes… “Where’s my boy?” Very quickly Dr. Languor was in the room. Johnny was slightly de-escalated by the sight of a familiar face. Letting his guard down for a moment, he asked the doctor where his son was and the response was not what Johnny expected. “Go slow, I fall behind.” started Dr. Languor. “What!?” uttered a confused Johnny. “Who are you talking about?” “My son! Benny! He was admitted with my daughter and I just two nights ago!” “I’m very, very sorry, Mr. Noble, but you simply must be mistaken. I remember that car crash. Only two of you came in here. And both of you were released yesterday morning.” “That can’t be right!” “MR. Noble, I’ve told you time after time, it’s simpl-” “What’d you say?” Noble asked. “I punched him in the mouth. I remember that. That’s about the only gratification I’ve had in the last year. Security hauled me off for it, too. But the doctor never pressed charges – thankfully. By that night I was out and I picked up my daughter from her friend’s house. We got back home and I got a message from Jack Mortir…” BEEP “Hey, John, this is Jack Mortir. Listen, uh, I don’t know how to break this to you. Sara Noble doesn’t exist. It doesn’t fit, like when a drum beats out of time - No parents, no relatives. The only evidence that this woman ever walked the face of the earth is the pictures you have. Sorry, Mr. Noble. I’ll keep my retainer, but you don’t owe me anything else. Sorry.” BEEP Johnny grabbed the answering machine and threw it against the ground and fell to his knees crying. His daughter went upstairs to her room. They didn’t talk again for the rest of the day. Johnny devoted himself to finding his family. “I searched high and low. Through the valleys and the fields. Of course I’m being facetious, but I spent the better part of the last year looking for my family. I wish now that I hadn’t become so consumed with it. I drove away the only good thing left in my life. I loved her. Please understand that. She never came home today. I guess she ran away. Of course no one’s seen her. Just another figment in my family. Well, I don’t plan to vanish. That’s why I’m leaving this recording behind. If you’ve found it… just know that I was here… I had a family and I loved them. Now I have nothing. I think it’s time to move on. After my picture fades and darkness has turned to grey…” Johnny pulled a small handgun out from under the table and shoved the barrel of it up into his jaw. He hesitated, tears in his eyes, and finally pulled the trigger… 5 Minutes Earlier “Ah, Mrs. Noble, good to see you again this evening. You certainly are faithful.” Said the nurse at the hospital. Mrs Noble replied through a earnest smile, “I got my little girl back today.” She started choking up, “She’s doing well and Benny’s adjusting well to not having his daddy around like he used to be.” “I bet it’s been really hard for you, the last year, I mean.” “Yeah. Ever since… the accident. Do we have any idea when Johnny will come out of his coma?” “It’s very hard to say, Mrs. Noble.” “I know,” she started, “It’s unpredictable. Could we be alone?” “Of course,” the nurse replied. She left the room and closed the door behind her. “She sings him that song every night.” The nurse said to an orderly as the two of them watched through the window. Sara sat beside her husband’s bed and she sang… “Lying in my bed I hear the clock tick, and think of you caught up in circles confusion-- is nothing new Flashback--warm nights-- almost left behind suitcases of memories, time after-- Sometimes you picture me-- I'm walking too far ahead you're calling to me, I can't hear what you've said-- Then you say--go slow-- I fall behind-- the second hand unwinds if you're lost you can look--and you will find me time after time if you fall I will catch you--I'll be waiting time after time after my picture fades and darkness has turned to grey watching through windows--you're wondering if I'm OK secrets stolen— HELP! SOMEONE HELP!” The nurse rushed into the room and the orderly ran to get help. Johnny had gone into sudden cardiac arrest. “His heart’s stopped!” yelled the nurse. “Why?” cried Sara. “I don’t know…” Within seconds the doctor was in the room with a team working on Johnny. The nurse pulled her aside. “Spontaneous Cardiac Arrest is most commonly caused by a sudden traumatic experience, something that’s too much for the person to deal with.” “How can that be? He’s in a coma.” “I don’t know ma’am… I don’t know…”
