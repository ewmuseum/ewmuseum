Ted Williams vs. Joe DiMaggio

14 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 14, 2006 Ted Williams and Joe DiMaggio were two of the best baseball players during their time and they both have set records that still stand today. Williams still holds the record for the highest batting average in a season with a .406 batting average. He is the last player to ever have a season average over .400. Joe DiMaggio still holds the record for the longest hitting streak at 56 games. You may be asking what do Ted Williams and Joe DiMaggio have to do with anything? Well it has to do with The Chosen Ones, the AWC’s newest tag team. The two men, Sentice Vargas and Jose Lopez, are avid baseball fans. This may pose another question with the fact that the two are from Trinidad & Tobago and the main sport there like everywhere else is soccer. However baseball is their favorite sport and though they are not brothers it is one of the many things they argue about like brothers so often do. {Two baseball cards sit beside each other on a table with a light shining directly on top of them. In fact it is the same light that is used to barely light the room up. All that can be seen is a table with the cards on top and the rest of the room is dark. The baseball card on the left is one of Ted Williams and the one on the right is a Joe DiMaggio card. Shuffling noises are heard and Sentice Vargas appears from out of the dark and leans against the table and grabs the Ted Williams card. Jose Lopez who grabs the Joe DiMaggio card also comes from the dark. The two men study the cards in their hand and then look up at each other then look down at each other’s card.} SV: Jose, you mean to tell me that you think Joe DiMaggio’s hit streak is a better feat than Ted Williams single season batting average? JL: Of course! I mean anyone can hit .400 for a season. It takes more poise and skill to hit in fifty-six straight games. SV: You see that is where you are wrong my friend. It is harder to hit .400 throughout the whole season than getting a hit in fifty-six straight games. I mean Ted Williams is the last person that has hit .400 in a season. In case you haven’t figured it out yet, these two men love to argue with each other. Like I said before they are not brothers, but they have grown up with each other and have always disagreed with each other. However don’t be fooled by the disagreements on simple subjects because when it comes to taking care of business these two men watch over each other and get the job done. JL: Sentice you know that getting a hit in fifty-six straight games is much harder than hitting .400. I mean you can ask anyone around and every single person would agree with me. SV: Whatever! There is one thing that these two men have in common and that is both men were put on this Earth by the baseball gods to be two of the best players there were and to make history. JL: You know that sounds very familiar. I mean you and myself was put here by the wrestling gods to take the wrestling world by storm and make history doing so. SV: I actually agree with you Jose, but it will probably be the only time I do so. This causes for another narration. In case you haven’t read up on The Chosen Ones these two guys feel like they were put on Earth by who they call the “wrestling gods” to wrestle and to be the best tag team ever. They believe that they were put here to dominate and they believe that whole-heartedly. JL: Sentice we are going to settle this once and for all. SV: And how are we going to do that? JL: Lets call someone. SV: And who might that someone be? JL: There is only one person that comes to my mind and that is Mr. Fuji. SV: Lets do it!! {Jose pulls a cell phone out of his pocket and dials a number. As he is waiting for Mr. Fuji to answer he just smirks at Sentice and shakes his head. Mr. Fuji finally answers the phone and Jose’s smirk turns into a smile as he turns on the speakerphone.} Mr. Fuji: Hello? JL: Mr. Fuji this is Jose. I called because I had a quick question for you. Mr. Fuji: Shoot! JL: Well Sentice and myself are having a little disagreement on something and we wanted your opinion on who you think had the better accomplishment; Ted Williams hitting over .400 in a single season or Joe DiMaggio’s hitting streak? Mr. Fuji: That is a tough one Jose, but I am going to have to say the most impressive would have to be what Ted Williams did. JL: WHAT!!! THAT IS ABSURD!!! {Jose quickly hangs up the phone and Sentice can tell by his reaction that Mr. Fuji just agreed with him. Jose slams the phone down on the table with a look of disgust and can’t look up at Sentice.} SV: I am going to take it that he agreed with me! JL: That is unfair! I mean the old guy always agrees with you no matter what the scenario is! SV: Well last time I checked you were the one who said to call Mr. Fuji. Anyways now that we have this settled I am going to bed. JL: Don’t forget there is always next time! SV: Yeah! Yeah! {The two mean stand up straight and disappear into the darkness while the two baseball cards are focused in on.}
