I Tend To Fall Apart

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: january 24th ONE seat atop a chariot... No matter how often I sit sick on the hood of my car after a show, it doesn't make the bruising or defeat any easier to bear. It eases the coughing of the cold I always seem to have between October and April, though. I always get a few laughs when I do it, too - just sitting there. I watch everybody coming out of the building, noting which wrestlers are first and which are last. It's always the wrestlers who leave first, barring a few cameramen. Of course, I'm partially at fault... after all, I'm out there every time. I can't stand to stick around in arenas, I guess it's like how I used to fall asleep before I came down from a good high. You don't want that cooling off period, that time when everything suddenly slows and you realize that it's all over for another week. When you stay around after the show, it's not only all over but you're left watching everything crumble week in and week out. That's not my kind of thing. If I lose, I leave. Win? I leave. I'd rather go try to get a precious few hours of sleep than watch a bunch of grown men bitch and talk about how hard life is to a bunch of photographers and journalists who're being paid to soak up every minute, hoping the hits on our website will rise with this week's juciest comments, candid photos or stories. I don't even stay for the trainer to look over me, unless I'm unconscious. I prefer local emergency rooms. I get my little Canadian Insurance card if I'm stateside, and I go to whatever hospital I find... if I can walk. I love to see the locals and have those little 'So, what's wrong with you?' conversations while I wait. You get some great stories and experiences out of those overcrowded and anxiety-inducing rooms. If you think I'm crazy for thinking that, you really need to learn how to curb your anxiety and take in the feel of a room. I prefer being babbled at by an old man whose wife is dying of cancer and has a son from New York paying their medical bills than standing in front of a room of faceless people cheering for whoever we tell them to. Sometimes, the utter defeat that the people suffering through the American health care system projected would make me feel cold, instead of instilling in me the compassion and want for charity that it normally did. That was the old Amy Campbell coming through. Aimz. The chick who'd take out anyone in the dirtiest way possible just to get a minute ahead in a lineup. I though I loved my life when I dedicated it to keeping a World Title in a shitty federation four, maybe five years ago. I went through everyone, and I used the fact that I had the entire upper tier drooling over me to do it. I hurt them, I hired others to hurt them, I used them and I was successful for it until one night, one occurance... one fleeting moment where I felt the compassion and caring that the world of wrestling had hardened out of me. The goons I ran with in that time fucked up my biggest match to date, and most of that had been planned. What wasn't planned, however, was my jackass boyfriend of the hour ordering them to completely decimate my opponent. I smiled for the cameras and acted like I was the stereotypical evil bitch... but when I watched that man fall, my heart went right down with him and hit every fucking branch along the way. He was Darcy Crisis, and I guess you could say he made me feel what the business portion of wrestling had taken out of my young heart. To be entirely honest, I loathe Aimz. I don't like who I was... hell, I barely even like who I am - but I still get those moments, stemming from those years when the world had hardened me to everything but an overblown sense of self-worth. I had to channel a lot of that for the sake of wrestling and confidence, and that sucked enough as is. Now, I have the shell of what I was; The cocky girl who knows that a loss only means she'll beat the bastard next time, and that it's only a matter of time before she works her way to the top or gets held down for not being a godly backstage political candidate. I'd still do anything to win, but within a physical means. I started working out a little and becoming more dedicated to fighting styles that weren't the simple brawling I'd learned in bars and high school. I was trying to keep my body, because I absolutely refuse to be the idiot who busts their ass only to get to the middle - the person who works and works and has their body, back or knee give out before they get a firm grip on the top step of the ladder. To avoid that, I have to play the political game every once in a while. I have to smile at their sad excuses for fighters, or team with these ridiculous women who probably think the same about me. I don't even get enough to be a hype machine... I'm just a pimple that won't go away and occasionally busts out all over your favorite hat or shirt to cause a stain that's as determined as the initial blemish. I have to play in their all-girl matches, and I have to... well... lose some. It's not that I didn't try in there, it's just that... ...Maybe... ...JUST maybe... ...Maybe that Nash chick is as good as me. Maybe she's just lucky. I can only hope that her luck will hold out in the ridiculous fucking match we've got ahead of us next week. The girl blindsided me, and I'll probably still be reeling from it when she steps up to me to talk strategy, but I'll grin and bear it for the sake of focus and taking out a couple of jerkoffs. A win is a win, I guess, and I'm fine with it as long as I don't have to get too dirty. I'm not going to risk my self-respect just to pin two up-and-coming sacks of shit from some PTC barnyard. Four and a half years ago, I risked my self-respect to try pinning a guy I thought I hated with every ounce of my being, and ended up in his bedroom later that night in an attempt to excuse my behavior. While it created what's arguably the only good relationship I've ever had, it lead to four years of bitterness far worse than the year we'd spent completely loathing one another beforehand. I was almost single-handedly responsible for the guy doing years of rehab and praying he'd get back into the business with his newly bummed back. Yeah, I think too much about Darcy Markson. We professionally went wrong because we got together behind the scenes, and we personally went wrong because I was still too young and too much of the person I needed to be for cameras. That was a peculiar dilemma, and I can't say I was at all right in any of it. In fact, I was the one constantly fucking up... but after almost five years of self-defense, how do you admit something like that? I haven't stopped fucking up, either. He won this week, and I lost miserably after calling him a wash-up. Suffice to say, I hate the way my sneakers taste. Speaking of my feet, I'm wearing skate shoes with little DC logos on each side. Darcy Crisis hasn't seemed to have left my life or consciousness at all, because if he did... well... I wouldn't be sitting around having a mental dialogue about my favorite clothing company having a huge conspiracy to make me think about my ex and possibly, maybekindasorta future (again?!) rival. The sickest part of it all is that I'm sitting around in chill air, thinking about my future with Darcy instead of heading back to the hotel and sleeping. I'm sniffling and snorting and coughing and hacking, and I've gotta get on a plane in a few hours. Everybody's piling out now, limping to their rental cars or talking to the 'fans' - better known as groupies - who're waiting just outside of the back parking lot's fence. I love to watch those girls, all dolled up in hopes of getting a deep dicking from one of these wannabes. They don't even reserve their vaginas for title holders... it's a free for all, and most are repeat offenders. I sometimes see them in bars after the shows, and get to find out exactly how small my next opponent's cock is. It really isn't as refreshing as you might think, though ring wood can be both hilarious and disgusting. You turn on the TV and see something pokey at the front of Jericho's tights, and you try not to think about it. Try being inches from it - it gets a lot more noticable... and don't even imagine the guys who wear jeans to the ring. I guess I should be happy. In-ring erections making me look bad... that's one less thing on my plate every week. Speaking of walking erections, there goes Adam Dick and an entourage. Surprising, for him. Oh, and there's... ''Clearing your conscience in the midnight air?'' Damnit. I try to scratch my foot, attempting half-heartedly to cover the obvious DC Skate logo so they don't spark further conversation. That's his favorite company, no surprise. ''Clearing my throat, actually.'' He knew exactly what I looked like when I was experiencing unabashed defeat, and he knew that his presence only made it worse. This conversation was a reminder that he was walking around with his head held high, while I was looking at the losers shuffling out with a magnifying glass. Of course, it wouldn't stop him from bringing it up, ''Now... I don't wanna start this on a bad note, but...'' ''I know.'' ''Know what?'' ''That you DO want to start this on a bad note, now continue.'' He smirked a little, ''Well, I did win my first match. I guess I've got an excuse to be chipper.'' I couldn't even make eye contact. I slumped my shoulders and stared at the bugs on the bumper of the god-awful rental sedan I was perched on, ''Want a fuckin' cookie? I'm sure some of the chicks I just fought have some cooked up...'' ''You don't have to get snippy.'' ''Darcy, you've seen me snippy. This isn't snippy,'' I growled, ''Now get to the point. I was wrong, and you got lucky. We're done here.'' Naturally, we weren't done. He was still standing there, and I was trying as hard as I could not to look him in the eye... but the time eventually came to, and I noticed that he was looking back at me with an abnormal tenderness. He saw me shivering, and he'd obviously noticed the constant sniffling my nose was wracked with. Even after so many years, he knew I hated the common cold more than almost anything. In my wildest dreams, however, I didn't expect what followed... ''Drive to the next event with me.'' Are you fucking insane? ''ARE YOU FUCKING INSANE?!'' I coughed at the end of the first curse word. Embarassing, but I couldn't believe the balls that one small win had given him. ''You're sick, you're not gonna want to get on a plane, and I'm the only person here who knows how to take care of you.'' ''You'll poison me with cough syrup and convince me to fight you.'' ''Minus poisoning you, you're probably right,'' he took a brave step forward, ''Come on, Ames. Scared it'll be fun?'' Scared I'll kill him, that's what it is. But, if I deny he'll keep coming. That, and I really don't want to get on another fucking flight. I love road trips, and he's taking complete advantage of that fact... but it was guaranteed to be more interesting than sitting alone in an airport through delays and red tape, coughing the whole way along. I guess I could think of it as a practice in self-discipline. ''Alright.'' He looked ready to piss himself. ''...Really?'' ''Really. I'll meet you at 5:30 in the hotel lobby. Try to get some sleep.'' Now that I was the one holding the element of surprise, I slid off of the hood and opened the driver's side door to the crappy sedan. I nodded once to Darcy, repeated the ungodly early hour when I'd sleeplessly be meeting him and started the engine. It would at least save me a flight home, then back through the USA.... and a drive through the south was guaranteed to be filled with fights - especially between Darcy and I, given his shitty slow driving. I bolted out of the parking lot and nearly hit a groupie while I was enjoying the look of Darcy in my rearview, still stunned that I actually agreed to his cocky proposition. It'd take a lot more than a few dinners to convince me to fight him. I hated wrestling with my past, and never imagined having to do it in such a physical sense. No matter what happened, it would be better than flights and climate change fucking with my head cold. I needed to win after the catastrophe I just put forth. They haven't been giving me near main event spots for nothing. This is a chance to put talent before politics and show them what I'm made of. I already left my cell number on Ellis Nash's door, even though I hoped she wouldn't call. Hopefully, I'll show them all what I'm made of without getting any snot on their shirts. TWO souls to duke it out... ''Whoaaaa. Hey, what's goin' on? Beautiful. Fuckin' beautiful. You know, I've gotta say... you're the first breasts we've seen on the UK tour - thank you, London! Because we actually draw ninety nine percent dudes...'' Machine Head talking about tits on my MP4 screen. The hotel staff would probably be banging the door down if the heavy metal didn't stop soon. Music aside, nothing about any of this was right. I hadn't slept in more than a day, my stomach held four tequila shots too many and my bags were too heavy to carry on my sore shoulders. I'm a pussy in every sense of the word, and one of the last people I currently feel like spending time with is Darcy Markson... but after already missing my flight for this roadtrip, I do need the ride. It's no time to be backing out, but the waiting game is killing me. I have to be down there in ten or fifteen minutes, and he'll be sitting there with a smile on his face - knowing that all of this bugs me beyond average human comprehension. But I'll grudgingly accept it. Like I said, much better than a plane ride... even if I've gotta still be awake at this hour. I reached for another shot of tequila, plugged my nose and took it like a ma--errr--woman, scoffing in disgust at the Elections Canada frontpage on my laptop - it was telling me that, according to the polls, my country was about to elect a Conservative Prime Minister who had a hand so far up George Bush's ass, even Tony Blair would be envious. I closed the computer, tossed it into my bag and put the flask of 14 dollar tequila in my back pocket. The minutes were bringing me down to it, and I still wasn't entirely sure why I'd told Darcy I'd go with him. I'd initially agreed just because I wanted to see the look of utter disbelief on his face again - it was almost as if I'd just agreed to that match of his. In his eyes, I almost had... the whole trip would likely continue his campaign to challenge me. I expected him to be somber and akin to a doormat... after all, he hadn't really expected me to go, had he? Thankfully, I always packed enough clothing and booze to last me a week. In this case, I might need to make a few liquor runs... and I sure as hell wouldn't be driving. Dragging two bags behind me, I emptied the mini-bar -- compliments of the wrestling expense account -- into my lame ass excuse for a 'purse' and hiked out of the room. This hotel was lame, my knee was killing me and my little alcohol buzz was evening out the dread of the following few days. I thought slightly of Ellis Nash, Shawn Harris and Vince Jones and exactly how - or if - they'd get to the show. Ellis was probably still too busy celebrating her win to think of next week, but I sadly had little to celebrate... unless you consider Darcy whipping Jason Warr to be a good thing for me. Years ago, I'd have been hopping up and down with joy to be seeing Darcy first thing in the morning. Hell, most of the time he was the first person I WAS seeing in the morning. I looked at him sooner than I looked in the mirror - and I looked in the mirror a lot back then. ''Yeah, you did.'' ''Did I say that alo--'' ''Yeah, you did.'' Apparently he'd wanted to make sure I was serious about following up on my word. I almost muttered 'You don't have to...' when he swept two bags to save my arms, but I was supposed to be thinking that he deserved the heavy load. I'm still a mean bitch, right?! A few silent moments later, he was slinging my bags into his SUV, which he'd thoughtfully already packed with his own. Apparently he DID plan for me to follow up on my word. ''Jesus, Amy,'' he snuffed, ''What do you carry in those things, your collection of human heads?!'' Heated leather seats. Not bad for such a nature-loving cheapskate. ''Yeah, heads and disembodied testicles. It's getting to be a pretty big menagerie.'' He seemed to shrug off the sarcasm and started the hybrid as I continued, ''Honda Insight... nice choice. Still drive like an old lady?'' ''You know, I believe that if you were to conduct a poll most people wouldn't consider driving under 100 freaking miles an hour 'like an old lady.' '' The shots had already started against me. Whatever, though... I still had my tequila. The flask was really getting down there, though. ''I guess, but it's partly what you drive... these wagons! And you call yourself a punky hunk. How was the win, though? Still feels nice and fuzzy?'' I tipped the flask back again, nudging it his way, ''Might as well share...'' So what if he was driving? I wouldn't mind dying in a fiery crash. Darcy glanced at the flask for a second, but he wasn't even considering it. Instead, he frowned my way, ''Exactly how much of the past four years did you spend sober? Just curious...'' ''Completely sober?'' I sighed, ''Uhh, not really... well... any time. But I haven't been doing drugs for a few months, and I stopped for a year at one point. Y'know, when I was married...'' THAT was a tender subject, possibly moreso for him than it was for me. Something inside me wanted to talk about it and make him jealous, but that compassionate side was bleeding through as usual, ''But that was a pointless time. What about you, had any problems?'' ''Surprisingly...no. If you ever want to know what having a quiet, boring but astonishingly stress-free existance, try hanging up the boots for awhile. You'll love and hate it all at once.'' I shrugged again, ''I had that time, and it was a time where I was pretending to like my life. Pretending. I hated it more than almost anything. I've spent too long loving and hating THIS life, I don't need to try another again.'' He just nodded, driving silently until my heart had a question... ''So... uhh... had any girlfriends over the years?'' That was one of the most real thing I'd said to him since he came back to the game. It left me vulnerable and uneasy, but like I said... it was real. The smirk on his face was the exact one he knew I hated more than anything, ''Something tells me you wanted to ask that question a lot more than you want to know the answer.'' Fuck you. ''I, uhhh...'' Why couldn't I just say it? ''Maybe. I... pah, just answer the damned question, alright?'' ''There were a couple. My work mostly kept me busy....or 'got in the way,' depending on if you're asking me or them. But yes, I moved on with life...which is the answer to the hidden bonus question you're sneaking in there.'' ''There wasn't a bonus question.'' ''Was too.'' I grimaced, ''You're too cocky, acting like you had some kind of effect on me.'' When I looked over, he was laughing. Laughing! AT ME! ''Amy, when the second question out of your mouth is 'So had any girlfriends?', then yeah, I'm going to think I had an effect on you. And I'm laughing because it's still the same old Amy. We know each other so well, and yet you put up the same old front. You don't have to... that's why you're in my car,'' he sighed, ''That's why we're doing this. Let your guard down for once, I'm not going to bite.'' ''Oh, so that's your reason? Pull me off my guard? I'll have you know I...'' This is useless. ''Forget it. Just forget I said anything. I've just... seen you around, being interviewed by these women and trying to pull your usual charm. You've been trying to fuck with my head. I had to agree to this to prove that you can't.'' He was chuckling at me again and, if he wasn't driving so fast, I'd probably have slapped him. ''Believe it or not, I don't really need to fuck with your head. You do a pretty good job of it to yourself, Miss Conspiracy Theory. Why do you think I'm trying to get into your head?'' My eyes rolled nearly to the back of my head. How in the hell could he ask a question that obvious? I'd always thought of him as smarter than that... ''Darcy,'' I started, ''Seriously, now. Why else would you ask me to do this little trip? If it's not for you, it's because Vince Jones or that Harris jackass begged you to throw me off...'' ''I don't know...to be nice? I thought it was a little strange that I've been working with you again for what...nearly a month now? And we spend all our time as far away from each other as possible. I was hoping we could patch things up a little.'' ''There's a reason we spend our time as far apart as possible...'' Here comes the cocky facade I've been working up for the past ten minutes, ''The last thing I need is you dragging on my heels, I've got Ellis Nash doing enough of it this week. You and I? We don't make up. There's a reason we've spent four years not speaking, and I really don't feel like putting the gears to that little tradition.'' Of course, if I didn't... I wouldn't be in the car with him. But that was beside the point. Kinda. Maybe. Sorta? ''So why did you agree to come with me? To spend the next three hours in silence?'' ''To...'' Hold on a second. Three hours? I need to get better at my geography. How the hell do two people take an entire week to drive from one event to the other, and why didn't that thought hit me beforehand? Maybe tequila wasn't the greatest comforter after all. ''...Wait a minute. If we're doing a week long road trip... where the fuck are we going? We can't possibly spend days driving just to get to Kentucky...'' ''We're not going to Kentucky...I try to spend as little time in the red states as I can. We're going to California.'' ''Cali...what?!'' What the fuck did I agree to?! ''What? We've gotta make a pit stop at my place,'' there was that cocky little grin again, ''It's not like new territory to you. Don't worry about it, you'll be at the next show in one piece.'' Alright... ''Fine. But, Darcy?'' ''Yeah?'' ''When we get there? You're fucking dead.'' If he even lives that long... New plan? Califuckinfornia, then Kentucky. We're barely on the road, and the next week of my life already looks like it'll be straight from a horror script. At the very least, I'll be pissed enough to do some damage at Fresh... so long as the baggage who lucked out against me last week keeps outta my way. If there's anything I hate more than surprises, it's surprise tag stipulations. Nothing in my life has ever been certain, especially when it comes to other people - no matter how well I know them - and this ridiculous oversight has only proved that. I might as well have just invited him to kidnap me. I might as well lay down next week, it's inevitable that Nash will fuck things up for both of us. Whether I'm Amy Campbell or Aimz, four years ago or in 2018, one thing remains the same: I can only count on what I do for myself.
