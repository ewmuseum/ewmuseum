Think of all the times you once had; write them in a letter that says 'goodbye'.

25 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: here This is the continuation of a journey; one man’s path down the road of stability traveled in the lightest of baby-steps. For you see, before the Illustrious Face-Eater was called the Illustrious Face-Eater, and even for a time after, the man would hardly share a moment to his own self. Forever plagued, or ‘haunted’ if you will, by another’s presence was a curse presented in a most harmless form and accepted with open arms. So, for longer than he could seem to bring himself to remember, he had two opinions. He had two views, two personalities. Sporadically clashing with seceding, the Face-Eater was a man left with no options, and no room to plot deceit. And then another discovery was made. As if a trick was being played on him, and he were none the wiser, he was susceptible to a single night of freedom. Every month, the lunar cycle passes a phase we all call the Full Moon. Now, depending on the angle of the Earth’s alignment, in conjunction with the moon and the sun, with no overcast, it is possible to see it at the brightest moment. Every month, we get a different full moon. Here. Early June. We already have what is called the Strawberry Moon, hence the pink tinge that plasters across its face. For the first few months, he didn’t know what to make of it. The Face-Eater felt as if she would leave him alone for a few minutes until he began to plot her demise, and then she would return bringing Hellfire that no one will ever see. So he kept a normal routine on these days. With time, coupled with a little piece and quiet, the man would have leisure-filled days spent to himself in a relaxed state, just glad to get away for a day. He couldn’t anticipate their arrivals, for he never stopped to watch the patterns. But after a few long nights of driving home late nights, he couldn’t ignore it any more. The Face-Eater had cracked the lunar pattern, and that was the first chip in the wall of his prison. He’d been in Gary for less than a week and already he knew where everything in the town was located. Every liquor store and bar, every strip club and every “meat packing plant” this man knew like the back of his hand. If he was going to be staying for long he’d have to blend in with the crowd. The last thing wanted was to be noticed in Gary, especially with two psychopaths with a taste for theatrics running the bill. So he hastily head for the first place he needed to be, of course; the Estrada Institute. Just talking a quiet walk on the streets, to enjoy the piece, was always a routine whenever the man had a moment to himself. While walking down the muggy streets of Gary hardly making an impression on the locals, the Face-Eater thought it would be best to take care of this unlikely situation early, at least while the opportunity was jumping around. Staring at the pink moon that was enough to inspire Dean Martin’s classic (Thought: Dean Martin > Frank Sinatra), and the Face-Eater actually had a moment of inspiration with his head tilted at the sky. Had he have some form of artistic talent he might be able to express it. No, instead he’d find himself training to kick people’s asses and look cool doing it. The notion at least was better than keeping his mind on present times. The city was filthy and dangerous and would no doubt corrupt him by the finish. Such thoughts were ignored, though, which was rather difficult when stuck in a city where the sidewalks had bigger pot-holes than the streets. And now coming across the station where he’d come to seek out glory, he thanks himself for the moment alone and avoiding all the strange stares that talking to yourself always seems to get you. He stood in front of a dingy brick & mortar building, with all glass tinted to the point of midnight, that displayed in a few dimly lit letters; The Estrada Institute. Someone who wasn’t looking would confuse themselves and the building for something else. The way it was set-up, you couldn’t call it a wrestling school. It was a giant room with a bench press and a wrestling ring where a sole source of talent would teach you all you needed to know. This was called Nutritious; raw material. Instant-Legend, so to speak. This was where the Illustrious Face-Eater was going to gain the credibility he lost, no matter the edge that he deservedly lost. Before it was just his will driving him, and everyone knows that isn’t enough. You can have the Will to be the best, to be the brightest star shining in the sky. But if you don’t have the passion, you’re viewed blindly by everyone else. So now the Face-Eater comes across this single dilemma: Do I dedicate every ounce of my being in the destruction of that shit-stain who raped my glory, Tim Shipley? In decided favor of the lesser of two evils, let’s just say we hope that the Face-Eater is not over-reacting (when circumstances state this as fact, but we shall over-look this). The Face-Eater approached the tinted glass doors with the name of the institute plastered in a white, sun-stained decal. Not able to see inside, he opened the door and found himself staring down a cylinder that very well decided the man’s fate. A glass bell shook against the glass as he swerved it open almost too easily. Considering it a well-oiled door, he took a blind step inside to notice someone holding the door open and the glare of the moonlight catching his eye. ”What the fuck are you doing here?” a mook said, shaking the barrel of a .22 in the Face-Eater’s face that he tried to catch the moon’s reflection in the metal. And now, he was nervous. Not a fan of guns and the circumstances they seem to portray, the Face-Eater froze up staring at the person who shoved the iron at his cheek, and stupidly tried to ignore every coming to that place. He turned around to leave, very non-threatening and very frightened, until the gun was pressed in to the back of his head. ”It’s too late now, step back inside, slowly.” The Face-Eater began to turn around to walk in the building, but the gun shoved his head to a tilt. “Backwards, so you don’t try anything funny.” Now forced in an awkward situation, the Face-Eater couldn’t help but wonder, as he took ridiculous steps while blindly pressing in to a gun as if it were his map, why in the name of Test-tube Baby Jesus would there be generic thugs with guns at a wrestling school. “Slowly, watch your step.” The kind man who threatened to kill the Face-Eater said. He lifted his foot for the most awkward of reverse steps, missing the obstruction of a raised floor area; the hallway leading from the glass doors down to the work-area was a barren wasteland of stucco and linoleum and dry-wall. Hardly anything to point out on the journey, and trust me, the Face-Eater had a lot of time to analyze that hallway (he actually WAS walking backwards). But once he stepped out and got a reverse-look at the frame of the building he stood sheltered in, that overwhelming feeling of determination, giddiness, and greed again began to shroud him. Despite being in a situation where a gun was pressed to his head and he was literally walking blindly in to a room where there was no-doubt to be more danger within, the Face-Eater was surprisingly excited. He didn’t have to look, but he knew a wrestling ring was behind him. “Anybody recognize this asshole” the shaking gun to the back of his head spoke softly. ”We can’t see his fucking face, turn him around.” The Face-Eater was forcefully turned around to find three guns (because they make him nervous and it’s the first thing he noticed) being held by rather threatening-looking men. None of these objects were pointed at him, and they all stood to the right of… the wrestling ring! FINALLY! HE MADE IT! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER COULD FINALLY BEGIN HIS TRAINING! “Hey, we’re sort of in a situation right now, and we don’t know who the fuck you are.” He heard, taking his attention and focus away from the Wrestling Ring which he so desired to exercise his will upon, and turned to the two people that stood behind the three guns and the men who held them. These were the two people that the bodyguards were protecting. And judging by their appearance and demeanor; they didn’t need bodyguards. ”You interrupted me and my colleague as we were discussing a business situation with our friend, the Wrestler, right here.” He said, referring to a Mexican man that stood in the ring. The man who had been speaking to Adam the whole time finally stepped closer. He wore a black trench coat that his chest stuck out almost unbelievably from, indicating that this man was wearing a Teflon vest. His skin was pale and hairless, but was most intimidating was the design of facial tattoo that patterned the lower half of his head. A series of tribal markings around his chin that made it, from afar, look like facial hair in its growth-pattern. People with tattoos on their faces sort of freaked the Face-Eater out, especially someone who is undeniably bigger than him and has his own bodyguards with guns, so he decided to examine the other… …who was just as freaky. This man bore no facial tattoos, sported the same attire of trench-coat and Teflon, yet had dark skin. It was hard to see how dark the man was, because the only visible skin was at the base of his neck and his hands. You see, this man wore a mask. A black satin mask, with a red star plastered over his right eye with a single hole to see. Because it was satin, it was very breathable and easy to see through the material with nothing but a tint to your vision. This made the darker man very scary. “Now I know you’re new. And I know you don’t understand how things work, but you have to know who I am. And you have to know who my friend is. And you have to know who the Wrestler is, or else you wouldn’t be here. But the ‘why’ to all of this is very confusing. So now, you are going to explain to me, in full detail… who you are and why you are in our town? Because I know that your type wouldn’t fall in line with the Resistance, I would just prefer if you would not try to fuck with us.” The Face-Eater was confused. He stood in front of a half-dozen people, most of which were all for the idea of killing the new-comer and he hadn’t the slightest idea why. So he finally decided to clear the confusion he had, and maybe make some unsuspecting friends in the process. ”Your name is Josiah, also known as Pointless. You fought in the cage in Asylum for the better part of a year.” The Face-Eater had now identified the man who had been addressing him. Now, he turned to Josiah’s partner, the man in the black mask. ”You are called the Mutineer. You used to wrestle in RPW for a brief time, but there were rumors that you were the leader of some communist faction, or something.. right?” The black man who we now know as the Mutineer nods, shedding no emotion from underneath his mask. The Face-Eater turns to look up at the Mexican man in the ring, who stood by himself and seemed displeased at the visitors that have taken over his gym. ”And you, you are Eddy Estrada, AKA Deft. You wrestled in the Squared Circle and Action! Wrestling and you were slated to be the next big thing and the technical answer to all those high-flying show-boaters.” Eddy Estrada raised his head and let off a hair-line smile. Being recognized for greatness wasn’t a situation that Eddy found himself a part of at many times. Before The Face-Eater could continue, Josiah smiled and gave a sarcastic clap that again warranted all attention. ”Well congratulations, we have a wrestling fan in attendance.” Josiah joked. ”Actually, I don’t watch wrestling. Estrada is the only person I came to see, and really could care less about the rest of you. If I’ve heard your name, it’s through channel surfing and I want to make it clear that you aren’t important to me.” The Face-Eater shrugged upon finishing his statement. For a second, he half-expected to get jumped right at that moment. But Josiah continued to smirk, and the former fighter we know as Pointless pointed at his friend. ”You’re telling me you’ve never seen those AWESOME public speeches my friend here, the Mutineer, has become known for having?” The Face-Eater shook his head. ”I didn’t think he spoke.” Josiah placed his arm around the Mutineer’s shoulder. ”Just wait and see, he’s Charisma Personified…” I am not the revolution. I am not the fascist cancer that your Capitalist leaders warned you of in the past, and again of now. I am not another cog in the machine that drives aristocracy, I am not you. Brothers, I am merely the man to inspire a change, a change planted by the construction of your American machine; A change our corporate executives have been fearing since they first exploited the minimum wage-masses. I am not the revolution, friends. I am the man to nurture this proletariat plant, so we can watch the roots break past our borders and limits that have been cemented through Democracy. This plant shall thrive; bask in the limitless laws of growth and prosperity. Democracy has failed our world, and with it the glories of science and technology are tools gone to waste. With technological advancements in production and commerce, we are always evolving and always growing. We will continue to feed our proletariat cause with numbers and masses. This is not an uprising; it is the natural progression of change. We did not plan this; the design of our society’s structure has pre-determined this change. For too long have we been content with the “privilege” of health-benefits and annual low-income tax returns. The fuse is lit. I am the match. The Capitalist machine will be seen as nothing more than useless shrapnel of the past.
