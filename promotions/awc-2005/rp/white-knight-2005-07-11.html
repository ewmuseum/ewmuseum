White Knight

11 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-11-05 “I want this pain in my head to die,” Alex thought to himself as he chewed on the inside of his cheek, “It’s impossible to think clearly.” His eyes darted aimlessly around the courtroom, searching for any sign of hope. The energy was stagnant, like he was at a hated aunt’s funeral. No one here cared about the outcome, about servicing justice, he felt. “Everyone here is just punching the clock. The old stenographer. The judge. My lawyer.” The members of the jury didn’t care either, he could tell. They patiently heard their instructions, but all the while they just breathed out their mouths and the only thought that ran through their head was “I don’t ever want to go back to my job. This trial can go on for weeks, for all I care.” “Why should they care?” Alex thought, “It’s not their future on the line. What a joke. These people weren’t my peers. They were maggots. Swine feeding at the tit of the government.” These people didn’t know who he was, didn’t know what he was about. He scanned each of their faces, discerning who they were. He saw an aging mother, a bald tax accountant, a watch salesman. “Damn it. They’re going to take one look at the parents and count as each tear runs down their cheeks, and then they’re going to take a look at me and see Scott Peterson. Damn it.” Alex checked his watch. It was quarter to ten on Wednesday morning. Two days before Madison Square Garden. Two days before Solarized and yet another high profile match against Pierce Lavelle. And instead of working on the things that matter, like training and preparing, the whole week had been a disaster. ”What a fucking waste of my time,” Alex muttered to himself, shaking his head. “Hold on, Alex. Try not to look so full of angst, the jury can sense it.” Garcia said. The lawyer straightened out paperwork and let out a nervous cough. ”Well, what the hell is keeping the judge? I want to get this damn opening statement over and done with,” Alex growled. “Don’t worry about it! The first hour is just going to be the prosecution and I setting up the case. You just have to sit there and look innocent. The facts I’m going to lay out will back that up, o.k. champ?” In two days Alex was going to lose the belt to Pierce Lavelle, but he didn't expect that would happen at all. Alex never thought anything bad could happen to him, but things often did. Alex shook his head, smiling out of the corner of his mouth, “Yeah. It’ll be real easy for me to look innocent since I am innocent, Garcia. But I don’t think that Mother Hen over there’s going to give me much credit, so pardon me if I’m a little concerned, pal.” the AWC Transatlantic Champion said, thumbing toward the middle aged woman seated in Juror Number Six’s seat. “Alright. I’ve never said you weren’t right to be concerned, but right now is not the time to freak out about it, Alex.” Garcia adjusted his tie. “You’ll just have to trust that this situation is out of your hands now. You’ve done all you can.” “All rise for the honorable Judge Louis Aday,” said the bailiff. Everyone rose as the judge stepped out from his chambers. Alex squinted, as he thought he saw something on the judges face. When the judge finally sat down, Alex realized what it was: a giant red and blue splotchy birthmark covering the left hand side of his face. Garcia noticed the disgusted look on the professional wrestler’s face, and with a low cough brought Alex’s attention back to the matter at hand. But now that Alex had the trial underway, all he could think about was the other night, and dinner with Pamela, and what a mess that had become. ******** Alex remembered what happened merely days before in New York City... Alex had spent thousands of dollars on Pamela for no real reason that day. He wasn’t trying to woo her. At least he wasn’t trying to woo her on purpose at any rate. But there was a reason, and he couldn’t quite put his finger on it. Alex thought he was doing it just to be compassionate, because he could be. What good was all his money that he wisely saved up and invested if not showered on his friends? But that was the sticking point in his head. Was Pamela really a friend? He’d talked to her more than anyone else in months. But would he be confiding in her this way if she weren’t writing a story on him? ”Why are you doing all of this, Alex?” Pamela asked as he pulled out her chair in the V.I.P. section of the Atelier. Pamela looked around, awed at the splendor in front of her eyes. The restaurant had cream colored walls with soft lights in the high arching ceiling. The dark wood tables matched flawlessly with the wall jambs. Beautiful French landscape paintings adorned the walls, creating a very romantic atmosphere. Pamela had never seen anything like it before. In her small hometown of Clarksville, Pennsylvania, they didn’t have a single restaurant. Clarksville’s only general store closed when she turned twelve. She was in awe. “You already asked that, and I told you. This is just what I do, don’t sweat it. I’m not coming on to you, if that’s what you’re worried about,” Alex said with a wry smile. Pamela lowered her gaze and pretended to read her menu. “No, that would be ridiculous,” she thought to herself, “Alex Strider trying to come on to me. I’d be stupid to think that, even with the new haircut and great dress he’d see me any differently.” “Just relax and enjoy the night,” he said assuredly as he picked up his menu. ******** “Today, we’re here not to decide whether or not Alex Strider murdered the young Sophia Riley. We already know that he didn’t physically kill her: An overdose of heroin did that. Today and for as long as it takes, we are going to show you that he is still responsible for her death. Sophia Riley was just eighteen years old, nine years younger than the accused. A freshman in her first year of college and her life was robbed of her by being corrupted. Yes, she was adult by age. She was adult by age, but who could expect a young woman to resist the charms and wiles of an older, sinister local celebrity?” ******** They ordered and ate a fine three course meal. Pamela had glasses of fine wine, while Alex drank down expensive bottles of Perrier. They talked as friends, and she laughed at his jokes. He was actually being comfortable, and entertaining. For the first time since she met him, she found his anecdotes about wrestling to be more than tiresome. ”And so then, Shawn just tipped the whole Port-O-Potty over with his bare hands! It was amazing, because Eli dropped out of the door, all six-foot-eight of him or whatever, completely covered in the blue sanitation muck!” ”Oh my God, that’s horrible!” Pam said, smiling and laughing. “Right, so I’m doubled over at this point, almost puking my guts I’m laughing so hard. So, Eli stands up, like a furious giant who forgot his pants were STILL around his ankles, and he takes off running after SHAWN! He was dripping the blue good everywhere, but then he tripped over his pants and landed on his face! Shawn jumped in his truck and sped off down the road…” ”He just ran off like that? “Superman” Shawn Stevens?” “Hey, I don’t care how tough you are, nobody wants a bear-hug from somebody wearing twenty gallons of Port-A-Potty sanitation rinse!” They both laughed together in unison. Pam took a sip of her deep red wine, smiling all the while. Her cheeks were red from the laughter. “I’m glad you’re having such a good time, Pam. You’ve been so stressed out lately, I really think you deserved some time off from whatever’s been troubling you.” Pamela swallowed her taste of wine, shaking her head. She was beginning to feel so comfortable, she didn’t want to think about anything negative. She didn’t want to think about having to return home to Kevin and his pampering. Ever since he pushed her down the stairs, he coddled her like a baby, crying as he was scared she was going to leave him. “Listen, I’ve just got a lot of work to do. Don’t worry about it, O.k. Alex?” she said slowly, concentrating on not slurring her words. “Got it. But before I leave that subject alone for good, I want you to know that I brought one last gift for you.” ”No!” she exclaimed, “I won’t accept any more gifts, Alex,” Pamela said with a stern look that quickly turned around into a smile that she couldn’t hide. ”Yeah I knew it. Don’t worry, I think you’re going to like it. Actually, in all honesty, you’re going to hate it. But that’s o.k., because you need it.” Pamela looked over at Alex through squinting eyes as she tried to read his intentions. “I picked this out especially for you.” Alex laid a small box on the table, wrapped in delicate pink wrapping paper and white ribbon. “Go on, open it,” he beckoned to her with a wink. Quietly, she looked down at the package. All of Pamela’s Christmases were sparse, so she wasn’t used to receiving gifts. Speechless, she timidly tugged on the package until it was dragged over to her side of the table, like a mouse pulling a golden corn cob by it’s teeth. Carefully, Pamela untied the white ribbon from around the present. The wine made her dizzy, and she could barely contain her excitement. Though Alex had given her so much already that day, this was the most personal gesture he’d made all night. Gingerly, she unwrapped the tightly wrapped pink paper, revealing a soft cream box larger than her two fists. “It’s so big!” Alex nodded and smiled. Her hand pulled the lid off of the box… ******** “You’ve already heard the truth from the prosecutor, but it was surrounded by gross misrepresentation of my client. The truth of this case is this: Sophia Riley was a young woman, who died of a heroin overdose. It’s up to you, the jury, to determine why that occurred. The defense is going to prove to you, that this was a sad, sad occurance, but it was not the responsibility of Alexander Strider. Sophia died of an overdose in Alexander Strider’s apartment. There were no drugs found in his complex. Alex Strider has never failed a drug test. There is no evidence that Alex supplied the drugs to Sophia Riley. Alexander is only guilty of inviting her back to his apartment… where HE was victimized. He never expected to be drugged by the nice young lady he met. Sophia Riley was a confused young woman, who reached too far, too soon…” ******** Pamela Saunders looked down into the cream box in front of her. “What is this, some kind of a joke?” she muttered. “A joke? No, listen. Alright.” Alex smiled, trying to diffuse the situation before it became too hot, “ That’s something that I thought you might need.” ”Alex, why the fuck would I need a GUN?” she asked as she slid the box across the table back at him. Frowning, Alex picked up the snub nosed revolver. ”Hey, now, don’t get all pissed off! I know how you feel about guns, o.k.? But you know that I was attacked last week, right? I can’t bear the thought of you getting hurt because my brother wants to see me in pain. If you’re going to continue to follow me, you’re going to have to be able to protect yourself. I won’t be able to be there for you every second of every day, alright?” The patrons in the fine restaurant started to notice the commotion. Alex deftly tucked the revolver into his pocket to avoid any further trouble with the law. “Well I don’t need a gun, Alex. I don’t want one, and I don’t want to carry one just so you feel like I’m safe.” Pamela turned her head coldly to the side “Pamela, this isn’t a joke. There were three armed men who came after me. If Darius figures out that you’re seeing me as often as you do, he’s going to target you. Can you take on three men at one time? No, you’re just a woman. This gun might make somebody thing twice before attacking you.” Pamela smacked her palm against the table, near shouting “That GUN could get me killed, Alex!” ”Excuse me, I’m sorry, but if you two continue this outburst, I am going to have to ask you to leave.” The maitre’ de cocked his head down at the two, like a disapproving parent. “I apologize, it won’t happen again. We’re almost done here anyhow.” Pamela said, her eyes burning at Strider. ”Check please.” Alex said, raising his hand. ******* Alex felt stifled in his seat, watching another man fight his battle for him. Listening to the outrageous claims of the prosecutor, Alex felt sick to his stomach. Even though the courtroom was air conditioned, he felt as hot as if he were wearing a fur coat in a sauna. Alex took a drink of ice-water. He was sweating bullets, because the only thing he could think of was how messed up his life could become, and he was powerless to stop it. “The prosecution calls Autumn Riley to the stand…” Alex watched as a mirror image of Sophia walked up to the stands. He recognized her instantly as a younger sister. He swallowed as she was sworn in. Alex turned his head to his lawyer, “This is ridiculous, dragging her family into this so deep. Why? They weren’t there, and they obviously didn’t know she was involved with drugs, so what is the younger sister going to say?” “They’re trying to establish the case through emotion. Don’t worry, we’ll hit ‘em with the facts, Alex.” Alex looked back at the young blonde taking the stand. Can facts really beat emotion? “Emotion usually wins, hands down.” Alex mused to himself. ******** Alex and Pam were walking out of the Atelier. The two hadn’t spoken since he got the check. The silence was uncomfortable, and Alex didn’t like it. ”So, you look great today. You should let me dress you more often,” Alex offered. She turned her head away and did not respond to him. “I don’t think I want any more gifts from you,” she finally said after a cold moment. He nodded, though it went unseen. Moving over to the cab, Alex opened the door for Pamela. “Hey! What the fuck are you two doing?” Pamela’s ears perked instantly, as she recognized the voice from her waking nightmare. She turned around, and saw her fiancé, Kevin rushing toward them. ”What the fuck is this? You’re out on a date with your model?” ”Calm down, Kevin. This isn’t a date,” Alex started. ”You don’t fucking talk to me, this is between Pamela and I.” His breath was loaded with the familiar whiskey trail, his eyes bloodshot. He was quite an odd scene, standing in front of one of the finest restaurants in New York City, wearing his number 3 Dale Earnhardt Nascar T-Shirt, tattered blue jeans and scruffy beard. He looked like a homeless man. Kevin got in Pamela’s face. “So this is really why you came to New York, eh? A little vacation with your boyfriend?” ”Kevin, stop it! Why are you here? You followed me all the way to New York? Are you crazy?” “You’re fucking him, I know it. Look at you, you look like a slut!” “What?” Alex said, confused. ”You’re crazy!” She yelled out, “I’m doing my JOB here!” ”OH yeah? It’s your job to go to some restaurant and get gifts from him, and wear this dress that I KNOW he bought for you because we sure can’t afford it, and get your HAIR DONE? WHAT DO YOU TAKE ME FOR?” Kevin screamed. The cabbie stepped out from the cab, “Should I call the police?” Alex waved him off. “Give us a second…” Alex said, unbuttoning the top button to his dress shirt. ”Kevin, you’re drunk again. Alex took me out to dinner as a way to thank me for writing the article about him, what is wrong with you? Why would you drive all the way out here to follow me? You’re going to lose your job!” ”I don’t give a FUCK about the job! You think I’m some damn idiot who’s just going to let you go out and fuck all these other guys, you’re the idiot.” ”Watch it, pal,” Alex said. “You’re getting a little too close there. Why don’t you go home and sober up.” ”And why don’t YOU try doing something other than fucking another guy’s woman, you faggot!” Alex rolled his eyes. “This is pointless.” Pamela stepped in between the two of them. “Kevin, go home. Go. I’ll be home soon, but this is insane, o.k?” Kevin didn’t respond with a word, but cocked his fist back and let loose with a sloppy throw, aimed for her face. She cringed, turning her head, awaiting for the painful warmth of the punch. It never landed. Alex grabbed his fist before it got anywhere near her. Pushing Kevin back as he held his fist, Alex frowned, a furious anger rising inside of him. “Get in the cab,” Alex growled at Pamela. She moved slowly to the cab, mesmerized. Kevin looked up at Alex, a sneer curled across his lip. “What, first you fuck my girl, and now you want to fight me?” “I’m not going to fight you, you pig, because I’d kill you. But if you ever lay a hand on her, I will kill you. Do you hear me, you fuck?” Alex said, shoving Kevin backwards. Kevin slid across the concrete sidewalk, an “Ooof!” escaping from his lips. “You’re lucky I’ve got a Pay-Per-View match tomorrow, bitch.” ”PAAAMELA!” Kevin yelled out as he was laid out on the ground, like a wounded alley cat. Alex calmly walked over to the cab and sat inside next to Pamela. Pamela was looking out through the rear window, her hand covering her mouth, tears in her eyes. “Take us to her hotel,” he said, his face flushed from the anger.
