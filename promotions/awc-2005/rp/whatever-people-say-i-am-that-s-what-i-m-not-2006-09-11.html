Whatever People Say I Am, That's What I'm Not

11 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday 11th September 2006 So, Ignatius Lisieux was in Newport Beach, California to look for somewhere to live - he figured that now he had an iron-clad contract that he’d go and see what all the fuss was about in the land of the Orange County. The late morning sun beat-down on the body of his silver car and Ignatius was busy thanking the God of A.C. before his attention was drawn by the radio, which was belting out the Arctic Monkey’s first hit record: ~“I bet that you look good on the dance floor…”~ “… Well, you’d be wrong monkey-boy – I look hideous on the dance floor, like some sort of malfunctioning teenage robot…” Engaging in talk with songs is the kind of thing you do when you don’t have any friends or family to speak of - Lisieux was particularly good buddies with Howard Stern - and the “MVP” was well-versed in responding fast enough so that the song might continue uninterrupted – he was struggling to keep pace with Alex Turner, though. ~”I don’t know what you’re looking for…”~ “…I’m looking for King’s Place… off Cliff Drive…” He loved it when a song was in context with his current activity and as he merrily motored along “Dover Drive” he spotted his desired turning point and signalled for the manoeuvre… ~”Dancing to electro-pop like a robot from nineteen-eighty-four…”~ “…Yeah, right – Arctic Monkeys, you suck…” He really didn’t like them, actually – he wasn’t much for “Brit-Pop”, “Brit-Rock” or “Brit-anything” at all and he had no qualms in admitting it. As he chundled along “Cliff Drive” he hit “scan” on his radio and turned onto “King’s Place” just as his cell-phone started to whir inside of his jeans’ pocket… ~”In a field outside of town… we can always be alone… Carry a blanket, maybe a basket…”~ That ring-tone was more like the shizzle that Lisieux liked; corny pop-punk with no real meaning or thought behind it: what he liked to consider “easy listening” and his staple music-diet of his adult lifetime. “Hello?” he asked immediately (as you do when you answer a phone), before pausing a little to take-in what he was being told… “…yeah I saw that the traffic was a bitch. How long do you think you’ll be? …No? Can’t guess, huh? …Well don’t worry about it. I’m here now anyway, I guess I’ll just have to wait for you to arrive. Okay? See you soon…” He clicked his phone shut and quickly stopped acting so polite… “Damn realtors… the fucking parasites.” He scoffed a little and tossed his phone into the passenger seat, before something again gripped his interest from the talking box mounted in the dash: ~“You’re listening to eighty-nine-point-nine “The MARK” here in California and welcome along to our flagship show! It’s twelve-noon and I’m here, Crazy King Mike, as usual, with my in-studio panel: John Heller is a former multi-time, multi-promotion world champion and has been in professional wrestling for almost thirty years while Monica Carter has been a wrestling reporter since ninety-nine…”~ Lisieux smirked at the title of the show and also at the mention of Heller, a guy that he’d heard an awful lot about from Johnny Noble over in PRIME and perhaps one of the best wrestlers still around from his era today. He moved his seat back into fully-reclined mode and stretched out as the host continued… ~”…As usual you can get in touch with us on the phones, via e-mail or with text messages: we’ve got four-four-one-MARK on the phones, the-mark-at-KCLA-dot-com for e-mail and our SMS is four-one-oh-six-oh! Be sure to get in quick because we have one hour of pure wrestling discussion coming your way and our first topic of the day, with PRIME on hiatus, has to be the Atlantic Wrestling Club…”~ He couldn’t help but smile again – these things tended to happen a lot to Ignatius Lisieux. What were the odds that he could stumble across a strange radio station in an almost-alien part of the country and find that they’d be talking about his new employer? It was times like this that he thought he might be related to Jesus, or at least a descendant of Noah, or Moses… or something. ~”Fresh off the back of the HUGE Coast to Coast production the AWC is headed to Portugal for a show in Lisbon where one of the major talking points has to be Ignatius Lisieux making his competitive debut for the company after his switch from PRIME… John, I know you have second-hand knowledge of Lisieux from your old buddy Johnny Noble, what are your thoughts on his arrival in ‘the Club’?”~ ~“Well Mike, it’s a huge coup for the AWC, absolutely… I know for a fact that Lisieux had already been approached by FUSE before AWC came-a-calling and he was literally moments away from signing for them exclusively… As far as my knowledge of him goes, well, I’ve seen him on TV like everyone else has for the past few years and Johnny Noble could never speak highly enough of him a few months back. Literally, he could probably be called one of the best PRIME has ever had, he’s certainly up there with the likes of Ian English, Tony Rolo, Joey Troy, Mike Wade, Black Angel, Tchu, Killean Sirrajin, Hoyt Williams, Karina Wolfenden… he might even be the best.”~ ~“I don’t know about that, John”~ The interjection of a female voice caused a little consternation for Lisieux, who was quite happy in having a wrestling legend toot his horn before the interruption… ~”…This guy didn’t leave PRIME of his own accord, if you remember correctly… Ignatius Lisieux was fired from that company because Chet Worth didn’t believe he had what it took anymore to hang at the top level – and I guess Ignatius’ loss to Wolfenden at C-Three proved that outright.”~ The French-Canadian winced a little at the going-over of old ground once again, but was soon appeased as John Heller returned to the airwaves with an extremely well-made point: ~“But I mean, you can just point to the example of Mike Wade… he didn’t leave PRIME on the best of terms and he’s gone onwards to become arguably the most important part of the AWC fabric to this very day. The guy absolutely dominated when he was active there and now he has a big say in things from his position as Commissioner - he basically had the last word at Coast to Coast so my thinking is that Lisieux only needs to follow his example if he wants to make something of himself with his new company.”~ ~“Well it certainly seems like a divisive issue that we have on our hands here” interrupts the host, “…on one hand you have the school of thought that thinks someone with the name value of Ignatius Lisieux can’t help but be a boon to the AWC and then there’s the people that believe he’s lost his fire and that he can’t “go” anymore… we’re looking for your opinions on this one folks, you know the numbers and our switchboard has been lighting-up since we opened the show , but first I’d like to read a couple e-mails we’ve had from AWC and PRIME fans alike: ~‘Dear Mike, as a PRIME fan since the early days it sickens me to see Ignatius Lisieux over in AWC. He was the last guy on the roster that embodied everything that the company I love used to stand for and with his loss I’m not sure where we’re left in terms of commitment to the brand.’~ …that one was from ’Steve’ in San Diego… and on a sort of follow-up to that… ~‘Yo, as an AWC fan I’m not too sure about Lisieux. He comes from a place that he obviously loves and so can we expect the same kind of commitment to our product from him?’~ …we got this one from Jonathan in Fresno… so, another interesting angle to take on this whole thing: commitment. Everyone and their dog knows how Lisieux felt about PRIME, we all saw how torn apart he was upon having to leave PRIME… can the AWC fans expect the same commitment from the French-Canadian?”~ He was annoyed, but he figured it best to just let it slide. One part of him was desperately saying “you can’t please everyone”; where the other half was telling him to call the show up right away and set the record straight… the former won the “battle”, for now, as Monica Carter started again… ~“It’s a good point, most definitely… in PRIME; Lisieux was the embodiment of the fans in his final run there, after what happened to him on ReVolution Fifty at the hands of the Dark Age he was made into something of a martyr for the ‘old school’ of the company and was probably rightly heralded as a hero… I mean, PRIME is the only place that Lisieux has ever wrestled and his time there was not exactly without problems – so I think it’s far from certain that AWC are going to see the ‘best’ of him.”~ ~”I’m sorry Mon, but I’m gonna have to disagree with you once again there…” chimes in John Heller, “…the whole reason Lisieux was apparently ‘sacked’ from PRIME was due to his lack of ‘fire’ as Worth put it – which was nothing but B.S. in my eyes – at C-Three I witnessed perhaps the most committed and brutal performance I’ve seen all year from that kid and maybe he didn’t win, but dammit from what I hear he ended up sitting in a hospital bed for a week afterwards! I think people are quick to forget what this guy has done since getting back for his third run: he beat Ozric Mortimer in a Last Man Standing match, he beat Hoyt Williams in some God-awful match, he beat Tchu and then got annihilated – unfairly – by Killean Sirrajin… I mean jeez, the guy barely lost a match and people are saying he wasn’t committed!? I don’t know what PRIME’s motives have been in this whole thing, but they’ve got rid of one of their brightest stars and he’s been picked-up by their nearest rival.”~ He was appeased once more by Heller’s interjection and was growing increasingly convinced that Johnny Noble had been absolutely correct about his former colleague – he seemed like a stand-up guy to Lisieux, someone who knew the business from an inside perspective – not somebody like Carter who relied on hearsay and rumour to gain her knowledge. ~”Well, apart from the e-mails that we’ve been flooded with on this subject we’ve also had a bundle of text messages to our number and I suppose it’s only fair that I give a few of them some airtime so that we might try and get to the bottom of this topic before our first interval, Johnny in San Francisco writes: ~‘I’m not sure what Lisieux’s motives are gonna be in AWC, he seems to be trying too hard to get the fans to cheer for him after what he did to Drake at Coast to Coast.’~ …and on a similar note we got one from Dawn down in Tijuana… ~‘If Ignatius Lisieux had any brains he wouldn’t have laid a finger on Kasidy Drake… Iggy’s career might be over before it has even begun in AWC.’~ …finally we had Tom up there in San Francisco, again, writing off Lisieux before he’s even started… ~‘Our fans will get bored of him quickly, AWC is totally different to PRIME and Lisieux doesn’t have the following here.’~ …seems like the text messages are pretty clear – people don’t think that Lisieux made the best career-move by what he did to Dr. Kasidy Drake at Coast to Coast and some of our listeners don’t think that he’ll have anywhere near the same impact inside of the Atlantic Wrestling Club as he did in PRIME… John Heller, you’ve been an ardent supporter of the French-Canadian so far this afternoon, what’s your take on the matter?~ ~”Firstly I have to disagree about Lisieux’s actions at Coast to Coast – although nobody can know for sure what the repercussions are going to be just yet. It already seems as though he’s been demoted to the very bottom of the card and he’s been given an awful debut match against Jack Murphy – someone perfectly capable of beating him – so it seems to me like Lisieux has already burned his bridges with Drake. However, you have to see it from Drake’s perspective… while he’ll be hurting about what Lisieux did to him on live television he also knows that a lot of the fans will have instantly warmed to the French-Canadian because of that act. Drake is all about making money and Lisieux has a proven track record of drawing crowds, what draws crowds more than a guy like Drake getting an ass-whipping at the hands of the hero?”~ ~”It’s all well-and-good making a splash like he did at the pay-per-view” begins Monica Carter, not allowing John Heller to properly finish, “…but what happens after that? Okay so Lisieux has got people’s attention by signing a multi-dollar deal with AWC, but where does he go from here? Jack Murphy is probably one of the most difficult opponents that Lisieux could have been given to open Fresh! This week and if Murphy beats him comfortably – which I think he will – then I can’t help but feel that people are going to start seeing right through Lisieux, he might be only twenty-six but I think he’s finished.~ The host took over the broadcast as a seething Ignatius Lisieux gripped the steering wheel of the rental car as tightly as he possibly could… he didn’t know if he’d ever met Monica Carter of if he’d ever done anything to personally offend her, but the nature of her opinions about him was cutting him down to the bone. While Lisieux did find it hard to argue about some things she said – like Jack Murphy being an impossibly difficult first match – some of her comments just seemed too personal to properly register with him. He had his cell-phone in his hand and the number of the show in his memory, but he was still managing to resist… ~”…let’s take it to the phones where we have Molly from San Marcos waiting to speak to us… hey Molly, what do you have to say?”~ ~‘Hey Mike, hey John and hey Monica… gotta say that I totally love the show and I’m a huge fan of John in particular… “HELLER TIME!” …Heh, but anyway… yeah I have to say I like totally agree with Monica about this one – sorry John! – I’ve been a huge AWC fan since day one and we’ve done just fine without any of these so-called “megastars” of wrestling invading our ranks – outside of his name I just can’t see Lisieux bringing much to AWC and I can’t see him fitting in here – he’s self-destructive.~ ~“…I’ll let you take this one, John, as Molly seems to be siding with Monica…” ~”Firstly Molly; thanks for your support… but I must say: show me a single wrestler in the world who isn’t self-destructive? Listen, we go out there night after night and take beatings and bumps, we bleed, we bruise and we beg… but it’s not an easy job, no way. Wrestling is a difficult profession to make friends within as the competitive aspect is so overriding. I understand that Lisieux had his troubles with Noble in PRIME, but what I see these days is a guy desperate to settle down and find some rhythm in his life. He needs to protect himself in the ring more before concentrating on rebuilding his life outside of wrestling.”~ ~”Good answer John and thanks for your call Molly… now we’ve got Tony from Sacramento on the switchboard… Hey Tony, what you saying?”~ ~‘Another huge John Heller fan here, Mike… great to speak to you all… anyways, I must be like the only PRIME fan happy to see Lisieux leave… The guy was a relic, he’s part of the past, Karina Wolfenden proved that at Colossus Three and Killean Sirrajin proved it at ReVolution One-Hundred… in this business today there’s no place for goodwill or sentiment and I always felt that those aspects of Lisieux held him back from ever truly being great. He was at his best when he was with Motoki in the AI but he’s been a pale shadow of himself since ReVolution Fifty…’~ Inside of a rental car somewhere in Newport, there was a livid AWC superstar… and he was dialling, oh boy was he dialling! ~”Seems like Lisieux is struggling for allies outside of you here, John… Monica, would you agree with that assessment?”~ ~”Do you even need to ask!? …The wrestling business has changed from John’s time and I know that the older generation still have a lot to say for morals and standards and it seems like Lisieux is born out of the same ilk. I have to agree that he was at his best in the AI days and also that in today’s society there is increasingly less call for doing what is ‘right’ and ‘just’ – more and more wrestling fans want to cheer for guys who do things ‘wrong’ and that’s just another reason why I struggle to see a good future in AWC for the French-Canadian…”~ ~”I’m sorry Monica but if we’re just happy to crucify our moral crusaders for the sake of entertainment then this isn’t the business that I got into thirty years ago…” starts-up Heller, fire most definitely noticeable in his tone, “…there is still a lot to be said for empathy, for courage, for compassion… I think if a few more people were like Lisieux and had these qualities then this world would be a far, far better place. You’re telling me that you think wrestling fans want to cheer the bad guys for cheating and acting how they do!? I’m sorry but I have more faith in our audience than that…”~ ~”You only need to listen to the people for proof, John, you’re living in the past if you think-”~ ~”Sorry to interrupt you there Monica… we were just about to lead into a break, but folks… on the line right now, from Newport… we have a special caller by the name of ‘Ignatius’… “The MVP” Ignatius Lisieux on ‘The MARK’! …Hey man, this is a huge-”~ “Yeah, hey Mike, hey John… Monica… I’m in California today looking for somewhere to lay down some roots and I’m just waiting on the realtor when I flick on your radio show…” back in the rental car Lisieux is now sitting fully-upright to speak into his cellphone, his left hand still tightly grips onto the steering wheel as he speaks, “…so okay, where do I start? Coast to Coast probably makes sense, huh?” ~”Yeah, Ignatius… why did you attack Drake?”~ “Monica… I had a feeling you’d be the first to ask me something…” chides back Lisieux cuttingly, “…well, here’s the deal… I’ve heard it said that it was reckless, that it was self-indulgent… that it was purely for the fans. Maybe that’s all correct, but I’d be damned if I was going to be joining The Empire and acting how I did when I was a part of the Asian Invasion back in PRIME… since those days I’ve learned a hell of a lot and I have to say I agree with John about his stance on things. There’s still a lot to be said for upholding everything good about this business - why do we have to pander to society? Why not carve out our own little niche where ‘good’ is ‘good’ and ‘bad’ is still ‘bad’. There aren’t many facets of life today where we get to influence people in such a strong way as we can in AWC and that’s why I’m here… it’s a fantastic company with a worldwide audience and some strong family values, something that I desperately tried to cling onto in PRIME…” ~”What about how it affects your future?”~ “My future is fine, I have my contract and my insurance payments to PRIME are over with… if you’re talking about me being demoted to curtain-jerker status then I wouldn’t have it any other way? What have I done in AWC? …Absolutely nothing, that’s what – so why should I leapfrog a bunch of guys who’ve worked their nuts off for this place since day one? I have no qualms at all in starting from the very bottom – did you ever stop to think that maybe I expected something like that to happen after Coast to Coast? Drake couldn’t fire me, he wouldn’t fire me… how else could he get revenge?” ~”How about the debut this week buddy?”~ “Cheers John, good to hear from you and thanks for the support…” begins Ignatius respectfully, before his mind turns to Jack Murphy, “…well I just said I didn’t want it easy and I guess that my wish has been granted. What can you say about Jack Murphy that hasn’t already been said? The guy is pure class, he oozes it… Jack Murphy seems to be to AWC what I was trying to be to PRIME. He’s all about the wrestling and he holds a lot of the same values as I do. Granted, we probably have a heck of a lot in common outside of the wrestling ring but in Lisbon later this week you can bet your bottom dollar that we’re going to put on one hell of a show. The guy has held two AWC titles and is a whole lot bigger than I am so I’m under no illusions about my chances… If I manage to sneak out a win, then great, but I’m not gonna cry if I lose to him, there’s no shame in being beaten by someone like that.” ~”What are your motives for joining AWC?”~ “I know people think there’s something shady going on, something underhand, I can promise that I’m one-hundred-percent committed to this cause. Obviously I don’t have the relationship with the AWC fans as I did with the PRIMEates - yet - but AWC pays my wages and keeps me alive, AWC puts me on international television and AWC lets me wrestle… it took a lot of courage to give me this shot after the way I was blackballed in PRIME and I’ll be damned if I don’t give my absolute best to this place. I know a lot of the guys already and I’ve watched as the likes of Mike Wade have made themselves legends here – I want the same as that – if I can do half as well as Wade has, then I’ll be happy. AWC isn’t far off being the biggest and best in the world and I hope it just needs a few people like me to help get it there. I can promise work-ethic and professionalism to the nth degree, I’ll use Wade as my success model and Murphy as my attitude model… if I get anywhere near those two guys then I’ll do just fine.” ~”Don’t worry, Lisieux… those of us with any knowledge about this business are right behind you. We’re with you all the way.”~ That’s all he needed to hear from Heller – and he loved how much it must have burned Carter. There were enough people writing the French-Canadian off before he’d even began and even his colleagues were questioning his motives and his direction - but he kept the faith. In Lisbon later in the week he started his quest to help take AWC to the top with his debut match against the mighty Jack Murphy and after the match he hoped that he and his opponent would share a few beers and trade a few stories. Ignatius Lisieux was here to make some friends, to make some money and salvage his career, so any wins he picked up along the way would be mere bonuses, only the uneducated would claim that any match in AWC was an “easy” one and a start against Jack Murphy was the stuff dreams were made of for the “MVP”. He was straining at the metaphorical leash to get to Lisbon and open his account with his new company, but first he had a house to buy… the realtor had finally decided to show.
