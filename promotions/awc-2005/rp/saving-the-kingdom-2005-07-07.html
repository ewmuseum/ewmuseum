Saving the Kingdom

7 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/05 OOC: Don't get confused, Kuff's not in this. Enjoy! Quinnville. A land of beauty. A land of love. Fresh water runs from the mountain tops to the fields below. The skies, clear as the eyes can see. The townsfolk are of a kind and generous type. A perfect place for the perfect mind. But no more... This land is now ravaged by dark forces from which has never been seen before. A constant struggle between these forces and the Quinnland monarchy has created a less than enjoyable experience for the citizens. New battles erupt each day, destroying everything in its path. The dark force's leader, Ill Bostwhick, has made it his goal to take over all that is good and wholesome and unleash his rein as overseer. The many settlers fight for what is right, but in their heart they know they fight in vain. They cry each night, hoping a brave soul will hear. King Quinn has sent countless statements of riches and glory to any who can rid the land of Ill Bostwhick and his minions. Many have tried, and all have failed or are on the brink of failure. All seems lost... 'Sir! Bostwhick's forces are attacking the east!' a young servant announces. The room is large and glamerous. The walls are lined in red silk with gold endings. A large chandelier hangs in the center of the room, overlooking a humongous table with which candles are decoratively set. A delicate cloth is placed over it. The floors are golden colored, the light from the windows beaming off. At the far end of the room, put against the center of the wall is a lavish throne. Three steps lead to the throne of which a man sits. This man wears a large purple and red robe with gold frigneds of some type of fur. This man wears a large crown upon his head. In his left hand he holds a large cane like object with a a crystal ball on top. He also sports a large bling blingish chain around his neck with a large diamond Q hanging from it. This man is, of course, KING QUINN! 'Shall I alert the remaining troops?' the servant asks. 'You might as well. No matter how many people I send, he seems to have an answer for it. It's as if he is a better military leader than I. What kind of shit is that? I'm King Quinn! If only I hadn't fallen asleep during all those classes at Royalty College...' King Quinn shakes his head as the servant begins to leave, 'Oh, servant boy.' 'Yes your Highness?' the boy asks. 'Bring me back an ultra sized chocolate shake. I need re-energizing,' the boy then runs out of the large room. Quinn arises from his throne and slowly moves to one of the windows. He peers out. The land outside the castle seems tathered and torn. What was once full of beauty and prosperity is now shot down from war. 'Bostwhick is destroying everything I have. How long before he attacks my home? I cannot bare to lose this place, with the chrome exterior walls, the diamond embedded staircases... Why does he challenge me?' Quinn continues staring out at his beaten kingdom. The screams of those dying fill the air as the battles blaze on in front of King Quinn's eyes. In the distance multiple explosions are seen, smoke filling the atmosphere. The battle cries of Ill Bostwhick's men are heard, followed by a chilling laughter. Quinn reaches into his pocket, and his hand returns with a large tomato, one seemingly too big to fit in his pocket in the first place. He hurls it out the window with all his might, yet it only reaches the distance of 3 feet before falling to the dirt below. 'Damn you Bosthwick! Damn you!' Quinn's fist pumps in rage. Footsteps are heard behind him. Another servant appears in the room, wearing a strange hat with a feather. 'Your majesty,' the servant grabs King Quinn's attention, 'I have something for you.' 'What is it servant number 802?' Quinn asks. 'A message for you,' 'Well, let's have it,' The servant hands Quinn and very large cell phone. Quinn holds it to his ear with both hands. 'King Quinn. It is a, pleasure, to speak to you,' from the voice. 'Who is this?' Quinn asks in confusion. 'You mean you do not know the voice of your next ruler? I have over-estimated you, King Quinn,' a chuckle is heard. 'YOU SHIT FUCK!' Quinn erupts in fury. 'Quinn,' Bostwhick chuckles, 'let us meet, face to face. There are certain issues I would like to discuss, and I feel we should do so in person,' Quinn suggests. 'How dare you ask to see me! You destroy everything I own, and now you want to have a tea party? Well Mr. Ill Bostwhick, I do not drink tea!' Quinn nods in approval of his own dissing. 'Huh? ... You really should accept my request. If you decline, it will anger me. You don't want me to be angry,' Bostwhick's tone indicates a subtle ruthlesness. 'Alright fine. Meet me under the oak tree with the engraved heart in Quinn Park Area 2,' Quinn answers, stroking his long gottee that all of a sudden appears on his face. 'No! We will meet on my terms, King Quinn. I shall see you at 12:36 inside the Starbucks on 5th and Maine. Do not be late,' the conversation ends as a dial tone is heard. 'Damn this evil being!' Quinn slams the incredibly large cell phone onto the floor. It doesn't break. He tries once more, getting the same result. He then places his hands over his face and runs out the room crying. ------------------------------------------------ The dirt road is full of debri, almost hiding the street itself. The buidlings along side it are hardly standing, the weight of the war bearing down upon them. The shouts of the soldiers still fill the air, as little else can be heard. The only remaining piece, seemingly untouched, is a Starbucks. It's invisible forcefield and massive corporate domination has sheilded it from the battles that took place here. It's paint still glistends in the sunlight, which for some reason is seen no where else in Quinnville but here. Outside the establishment is parked a large brown caravan. The animal that was used to pull it, is just that... Some type of animal. Its fur is black, its head is large and oddly shaped. Spikes strike out of its sides at all angles. A horn is heard. A different caravan pulls in behind the brown one. This one is white. The length is extreme, almost ridiculous. A large purple Q is seen on both sides, outlined in various emeralds. Seventeen white horses pull this vehicle, along with one reindeer with a red nose. A driver, a short, rather unpleasent looking fellow steps out and makes his long journey to the back. He opens the door and out steps King Quinn. He wears his favorite Quinnville Gangster's football jersey. 'Oh ye who ye bring thy ye to ye,' Quinn speaks towards the driver. 'What the hell?' the driver is undoubtidly confused. 'Ye!' Quinn pushes the driver down and runs inside giggling. Inside the Starbucks is empty. King Quinn looks around for any sign of life, when a faint sound is heard. He begins searching for the source. The sound gets louder and louder as it now begins to sound like someone speaking angrily, followed by laughter. Quinn continues searching until he turns a corner near the front of the Starbucks. There he finds Ill Bostwhick weilding a large black wip. He takes strikes at the employee working behind the counter. 'That's right! Get my coffee bitch! GET IT!' Bostwhick laughs in delite. Quinn grabs a nearby chair and hurls it for him. He misses rather badly, hitting the employee instead. 'Shit,' Quinn reacts, this getting Bostwhick's attention. 'King Quinn! You startled me,' Quinn looks down at Bostwhick's wip. 'I can explain. You see, this--' 'I do not care, Ill Bostwhick,' Quinn cuts him off. 'I am here, as you requested,' 'Hmm, so you are... Well, King Quinn, we both see what is going on here. Innocent lives are being taken in this foolish war,' Quinn speaks with his hands. 'Because of your own doing!' Quinn lets out emotionally. 'You would say that, King Quinn. But you see, it is truley your doing,' Bostwhick grins. 'What!? I am not the one ordering my troops to kill off everything in its path!' Quinn slams his fist onto a table. Blood pours out. He screams and runs to the back. He returns with a bandaid. 'Your, 'highness', if you would simply relieve yourself of command, no one would have to die. If you would hand over your power to me, this war will not continue. It's that simple,' Bostwhick nods his head while smiling. 'You would destroy all that there is. You would turn everyone into slaves for your own amusement!' Quinn's face shows anger. 'Yeah, so? The strong rule the weak, right? You were strong for a while, but now, you're nothing. It's time for a change. It will happen regardless, and you know it.' 'False. All I know is what I know. Because what I know, is something...that I know,' responds Quinn. 'Uh, right. But I do have one question for you, King Quinn. What did the five fingers say to the face?' Bostwhick asks nonchalantly. 'I don't recall,' Quinn says, confused. Just as he says that, Bostwhick reaches over and slaps the shit out of King Quinn. Quinn stares at him, shocked. 'And that was, COLD BLOODED!' Bostwhick sings to him, laughing. Seeing that Quinn is still looking shocked from the slap, Bostwhick breaks out in a run for the door. He jumps through the glass door and rolls across the pavement. Quinn goes after him. 'You will pay for that!' Quinn shouts at him. Bostwhick runs into the forrest that is just behind the Starbucks. He peers over his shoulder, looking for Quinn. No one. He stays on the move, stepping over the thick foliage of the forrest ground. After about five minutes, he takes another look around. 'Good. I think I lost sight of him,' he says to himself. 'But I can see you!' a voice yells. Bostwhick turns around fast, looking for the voice. 'Who is that?' Bostwhick asks the air. A small giggle is heard, followed by faint footsteps. Bostwhick follows it with his ears. He walks on until the footsteps stop near a large oak tree. 'Okay, enough of these silly games! Do you know who I am? Show yourself, coward!' demands Bostwhick. Just then, a small man, barely five inches high, appears on a branch. Bostwhick stares at this man in disbelief. 'No fucking way! DAVID THE FUCKING GNOME! HOLY SHIT!' Bostwhick raises his arms in joy. 'Yes, it is me,' speaks the little man. 'You goddamn right! You even have the red pointy hat. Where have you been?' asks Bostwhick. 'Just hanging out. After they canceled my show on Nickalodeon, I lost everything,' David sighs. 'Even your wife? She was a true MILF. I always wanted to titty fuck her,' Bostwhick admits. 'Don't go there! She left me for one of those damn Kebler elves. Damn them to hell!' the little knome speaks in anger. He then beings to cry. 'I just want to be loved, Bostwhick. That's all,' David sobs. 'Well I can't help--' A rather artificial gun shot cuts off Bostwhick's voice and David the Gnome falls quickly to the ground beneath. 'What the hell!?' Bostwhick shouts. 'That little fucker owed me $500 dollars,' a familar voice responds. 'QUINN! How did you find me?' Bostwhick yelps in surprise Quinn appears out of the shadow, clutching to his NERF gun. 'I placed a bat tracker on you. Now it's your turn to die!' Quinn smirks. 'Not yet,' replies Bostwhick. Out of nowhere, Bostwhick throws a Hadoken fireball at Quinn. Quinn takes cover behind a tree, dodging the fireball. Quinn whips out a little flute. He plays a recognizable tune on it and a roar is heard. Loud thuds can be heard and the ground begins to tremble. The famous Dragonzord appears, with custom spinning rims. Quinn jumps tremendously high into the air and climbs on board. 'It isn't over yet!' Quinn yells while the zord rumbles into the shadows.
