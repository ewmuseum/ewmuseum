Another Boy Drowning

16 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Almost a day late :S For every pure soul like mine, there is a demon, bursting at the seems. He lies to himself and to everyone around him, a spitting image of what you don’t want to end up like. These demons possess us, make us obsess over the insecurities we never knew could possibly ever haunt us. They live our lives worse than we’d ever hope, seeping into our blood streams and taking over our minds. No more do we become human when we turn to these demons. Every day I wade through the streets, trying to live. I breathe the air, and my lungs hurt. I look at the faces, and my eyes squint. It is blinding, the fact that everything around me is infested. The niggers, the spics, the Jews, those slit-eyed fruity pies…they all try to hold me down to something that I am not. How do I live? How do I manage to get through every day without buying that dime bag from Uncle Tom on the corner, flashing a smile as I turn away to smoke myself into oblivion? How do I avoid teaming up with these street ghouls as they tempt my body and my mind into doing their naughty, horrible deeds? I have thought about it. I have questioned it all. I’ve asked myself “What is it about these people that makes me puke, that makes me want to hurt myself?” And when that question hits me, I stop and I think. My mind wonders as I gaze into a world that only exists in my heart… Think about it…a world where everyone gets along. An Eagle can help a Jew make a movie, or an Eagle can help a n*gger make that next batch of crack. It’s tantalizing, isn’t it? What is stopping us White Eagles from helping out the community? And then it snaps back into place. I grimace, trying to chase those thoughts out of my head. I feel the numbness overcome me as He scolds me. While I am punished, the answer is also laid upon my front step, waiting there like a headline in bold letters: WHITE LORD IS WHITE FOR A REASON. Of course. Why should I question it? There is not even world, there is nothing of the sort. There is just me, my brother, and the rest of the Eagles under the White Lord’s embrace. We sit here like servants, doing his biddings and thanking him for the chance to be the superior humans in the world. And everyone questions that shit. They say “Why should the White human being be the superior? Why not a fucking n*gger or a slant-eyed gook, or any of that bullshit?” Soon, panties get wrenched into a bunch about it all. I saw a lot of this in “Roots”, which I watched the other day. Well…most of it. My brother and I put it in out of request of a neighbor. He wanted us to see “How God damned foolish those n*ggers were back then.” A lovely viewing experience. When we watched it, though, my brother and I didn’t feel that superiority. We didn’t feel the same old of “GOD DAMN N*GGERS BLAH BLAH BLAH!” like we usually do. There was no bible thumping there was… …remorse? …hate? …sorrow? No, there was just blank. Now, I will admit: what my brother and I do is wrong sometimes. The Lord giveth and the Lord taketh, that is true, but not every n*gger flashing us a bad look wants us cooked. That’s just not the way it is. I’ve stomped a few to death that sure as hell don’t deserve it, that’s for sure. When we saw Kunta Kinte get hauled away, we were taken back. His whole struggle…touching. How could someone enslave somebody like that? What was up with that?! When my brother and I saw it…it was horrible. But that’s just the thing. The fact that that wasn’t real, that that was a pseudo reality is what is really fucked up. This is what was being fed to us. Not the right. We don’t see something that promotes what is correct in our world, that the White Lord is over all…no, we see mother fucking Kunta Kinte, getting taken away by the evil White man. Picture this: A story of two young, White males, trying to make end’s meet. Suddenly, the black man which they have battle for ever is suddenly let free. Now, they have to fight to preserve the White Lord’s territory. … You can’t? And that’s because that is REALITY. You are living in a fucking dream world right now, one where White and Black fight like enemies. One is trying to come out victorious, and right now the Black devil is on top. The reason for this? It’s because the God damned Asians, those fucking spics, those Jew media whore…they all want a piece of the Black devil when he takes over. And that is where my brother and I step in. We are not ones to sit on the side and say “Oh, yeah, come on in! Make yourself at home!” We don’t have a fucking welcome mat that says “You’re home, n*ggers!” And we sure as hell are not thumping the Devil’s manuscript. You don’t see any of that fucking shit, do you? All day, every day, there is the bible, and there is the White Lord. He’s the one who makes your God Damned breakfast, helps you win times are tough, makes everything in the good for your bitch ass. He is the one that saves your fucking soul. He is the one that you should be loving. And who is it? Who is it that you actually love? What dick do you suck while you stay there on your knees, wearing away at that boney cap between your shin and your thighs? What seamen is filling your mouth with lies and deception, dick suckers? Samuel L. Jackson’s. Nelson Mandela. The Reverend Al Sharpton. The fucking Boondocks. The 50 Cents of the worlds. They spit those n*gger lies at you, and you do nothing to repel it. You don’t even try to find God to help your too-far-gone ass anyways. All you can do is curl up in a fucking ball and die. And when you die, what the fuck happens then? There is no White Lord waiting there at Heaven, shaking his head and saying “Oh, you, you fucked up pretty bad! Oh well, c’mon in.” Hell. Fucking. No. You go down to the fiery pits of hell, your skin to be charred by the Black Devil himself. There you will be tortured eternally, your soul blasted away from existence. And you cry and you wail forever, wanting some sort of salvation. You think to yourself that it may all end. And you pray to your God that you’ll never feel so much pain again… …But the agony has just begun. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- I needed cigarettes. Bad. Three whole fucking days without one God damned cigarette. Which would explain why I was at the 7-11 at 3 a.m. But this wasn’t an Eagle run 7-11. This was it. This was that God damned American melting pot right here. I could take a color chart out and point to a splotch and I could’ve seen it in that room. I was grossly outnumbered. With Liam at my side I could’ve cleared the place out, but he wasn’t. So I stood there in line, waiting to go. In front of me directly was a sketchy looking character, shaking like a God damned crack addict. In front of him was a big dude, tough looking. In the store there were plenty of other people as well. I stood there in line for what seemed like forever. The Indian shit head behind the counter couldn’t understand what the big man wanted for scratch tickets. He kept on grabbing the $5, the $10, the $1…he was a God damned mess. “Nah man, the fucking Monopoly one!” the big black men kept on yelling. “Here you go. $2.” “Nah man, fucking Monopoly! That’s five dollars, son!” And this went on like that for the longest time. I was growing impatient. I was growing jumpy. I needed my fucking cigarettes. Thankfully, someone else was growing impatient as well. *BANG! BANG!* And like that, the big black man was down. While it was nice to look at for a moment, the blood slowly pouring out of the body, the crack-head n*gger in front of me had a gun out. Looking at the way he was twitching all over, I don’t think I really wanted anything to do with that either. “Yo man, get all dat cash outta dere!” The gun was pointed at the Indian man, who was totally freaking. His lips were quivering and I could see a tear or two forming in his eyes. This must’ve been his first night in the store…no, first night in the country! He was looking for salvation. While everyone else in the store was quiet, either hitting the ground or throwing hands in the air, I stayed cool. Nothing will upset a crack addict more than someone who is in a fury. I kept my hand on the back of my pants, ready to… “Yo, who da fuck are you? Get da fuck on da ground, bitch! He saw me on the mirror. I could see his angry eyes looking into mine. The pure hatred of that man towards me because I wasn’t fearing him was immense. I bet that he thought that the gun made him a God. Well, that God sure as hell wasn’t mine. “Nah, I’m allright. Just get your money and get out.” A confused look formed on his face. Who the fuck was I to be telling him what to do? Didn’t I know that he was a gun-toting, hard ass mothah fuckin’ n*gga from South Boston, ready to end any mother fucker in a second? What was I, fucking crazy? “Yo, you deaf kid? GET YO ASS DOWN!” And like that, I was done with this. *SWOOOSH!* My gun was out, placed on the back of his head. His gun was still out, faced towards the now-sobbing Indian… “PLEASE JUST TAKE THE MONEY! PLEASE! TAKE IT!” While I stayed cool, my arm straight and cold as it had the gun pressed into his head, he was nervous, shaking more than before. He couldn’t handle this, it was obvious. All this was leading to two bodies in this store now… “YO, WHAT DA FUCK! Drop it man! Drop it o’ I’ll drop you!” “You aren’t in much of a position to be telling ME what to do, ‘man’.” “Yo man, you don’t kno’ me! I’m O.G.! I’ll kill yo’ bitch ass in a second, kid.” “Ok, here’s the deal: I’m not average ‘cracker’. I’m Tim Martin…” The place went silent. Not because they didn’t know who I was…more because every minority in Boston did. “…of the Eag—“ “Shit, I kno’ who da fuck y’are now! I ain’t scared, though!” “No one is. And I’m fine with that. You n*ggers think you need to be hard, and that’s understandable. You need to have something going for you. And that’s why I’m giving you two options. One you might like, and one you won’t.” Pause. Silence. Acceptance of choices… “Number one is you put your gun on the counter, turn away, and walk out. I never see you again as you never come here ever again. I walk up to the man behind the counter, ask for a pack of Marlboro reds, and he gives it to me out of kindness. The fact that I save his job should be reason enough to get a free pack of cigarettes.” “Ain’t havin’ that. I’ll take option b.” He started to spin, but, like I said, he was in no position to do anything like that. I threw him to the ground and put the gun to his head, looking the devil straight in the eyes. *BOOM!* Right through the eyes. The splashes of blood shot back at me, my wife-beater sprayed with crimson. I stared at that fool on the ground for a second, his lifeless gaze being ruined by the blood pouring onto his skin. Then, I looked up and everyone was silent. Waiting on the counter for me was a pack of Marlboro reds, the Indian man not crying anymore. There weren’t tears all over his face. “…and that was option b.” I grabbed my cigarettes and started to walk out when I noticed that everyone here was silent. I couldn’t leave on that. I just couldn’t. “You’re probably wondering what the fuck, right? You’re thinking, who the fuck is this guy, coming in here and just killing guys like nothing? Am I some sort of demon? Am I the angel of death? Do I just go around, killing people like it is nothing.” Pack is open. One in placed between my lips. “Nah. It’s nothing like that. I’m just a Furious Fist of God, and may He have mercy on all your souls…” --------------------------------------------- “You pray to your God that you’ll never feel so much pain again.
