The worst that could happen, and other fun scenarios!

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: tuesday, nov 7, 2006 Somewhere in Northern California, a little redhead was jubilantly celebrating her very biggest... uhh... something? On one hand, Sarah McLachlan was crooning through the stereo, and the redhead was working her most off-beat little dance while tightly gripping a champagne bottle and a single glass. She was humming a tune and assuming she was entirely alone, wiggling that little extra mile just for her own grins. Of course, when THIS particular girl assumes, she tends to be wrong... and today wasn't different from any other day -- she wasn't alone. A rather large man had invaded her home, and he was stalking behind her ever so slowly... When he caught up and wrapped arms around her waist, she elbowed about as far into his ribcage as physics would allow -- alongside a gorgeously surprised scream and the threat of a stabbing. Uh-HAW! came Darcy's greeting, as he doubled over toward the ground. ''.....good to....see you too.....'' Okay, so my dramatic monologue sucks. I spun around to put my hand on Darcy in some feeble attempt to apologize. ''Sorry, if I'd known it was you I would've used my glass to carve a big 'J' in your chest to remind you of Engagement Screw-Up numero uno. That's really all I can offer as far as an apology goes... that was a pretty dumb move on your part.'' Anywho, back to my sexy narrative... After taking a moment to regain his breath, Darcy rose to his feet in an attempt to console her. She was right - even though he hadn't sought out to do it, going to see Jane two weeks after popping the question wasn't the best idea he'd ever had. Hopefully he could come up with the right words to ease her troubles. 'If it's any consolation,' he said, after the moment passed, 'the sex was only so-so.' ''Even if you ARE -- which, by the hand of God, you had better be -- joking, you just lost at least a week of actual sex with someone whose head doesn't need to be covered by a brown ba--'' That's when Amy paused. She actually stopped herself and, for once, thought about what she said. ''Wow... I'm not normally this catty. I guess my celebration's off. Congrats, Darcy -- your kind words allowed me to turn my heart into a quivering blob ALL over again... and here I was drinking to the fact that I thought I'd grown up enough not to give a damn if I was about to marry someone who'd run off to hang with his exes at the drop of a hat,'' she sighed and put her bottle down, ''I appreciate it, really. For the first time in a long time, I was almost able to get over something... but your sense of humour's uglier than Jane herself, and it brought me right down with it.'' Amy stopped again, then finished, ''... DAMN, I'm a mess.'' ''Hey, come on...'' Darcy said, now feeling quite sympathetic, as he held his arms outstretched, hoping the all-purpose invitation for a hug could restore her broken self-esteem. ''...it was a botched joke.'' Oddly, it was a gesture that Aimz accepted. With her face pressed into his chest, Darcy could barely hear the words coming from the little redhead, but it sounded a little something like 'OHDEARGODNO!'... but that might have just been his own mental diatribe, because her REAL words were... ''What I'm actually celebrating is the fact that my credit card wasn't too overcharged for me to order two tickets to Vegas and complementary coupon to recieve fifty bucks off of the hundred dollar ceremony at the Palms Casino Chapel, WHICH -- by the way -- is where I'm inviting you to stay with me for a fun-filled weekend of needlessly spending money, getting stupid tattoos and laughing at hookers who wear way too much makeup.'' Suddenly, she clammed up, ''Err... damnit, that was the part I had to say AFTER you got drunk off this champagne, which actually cost more than what we'd be spending on the wed--errr--shutting up now, I swear...'' Releasing her from the embrace, Darcy stepped back to assure her that her fears need not be warranted. 'Relax...you can say as much as you want, so as long as you quit hitting me. Besides, I definitely know better than to get tanked when I'm with you and within at least a hundred miles of Las Vegas.' Amy sighed. ''Does that mean you'll go there sober?'' she said, followed with a sardonic smirk, '' 'Cause I REALLY want to get the brunt of it over with before my mom gets her hands on another bridal magazine. We can have the 'real' ceremony whenever we want, and I know that it's a short engagement and I'm pretty sure Marie is the only member of your family not awaiting my swift death and replacement with an oh-so-nice preppy girl with a real job, bu--hey, did you ever notice that I ramble when I'm nervous of what you're going to say? Stop me ANY time now...'' 'I'm all for doing this quietly,' Darcy said, as he collapsed onto the couch in a heavy heap. 'You know that. But if you think getting your mom off your back qualifies as an excuse to breeze through the engagement period, you can keep dreaming. I'd rather be locked in a room with the two of you picking floral designs for six hours if that's what it comes to.' She laughed, knowing that reverse psychology really DID work, ''Really? Great, 'cause that's what we're doing Friday and, since my dad didn't want to come even with a gun to his head, we needed an extra opinion.'' ''... When did you become a girl?'' ''This isn't GIRLY, Darcy, it's... fine. Fuck you, I'm a girl. That's right, I said it!'' she threw her hands in the air and chuckled, ''I love the ring I'm giving you such a hard time about. I love the attention and the 'ooohs' I got when I went to the mall looking for bridal registries, and I REALLY love planning a day that revolves around me wearing a gorgeous dress, giving my life away to the man of my dreams.'' ''And THAT,'' she continued, ''Is something you won't hear me repeat again in my lifetime.'' 'Well I'm glad it's settled,' Darcy said, propping his feet up on a nearby coffee table, covering an old issue of Newsweek in the process. Everywhere else in the world, this particular Newsweek's international cover story was an in-depth look at the losing effort in Iraq. But the American version?.........an in-depth look at Tom Cruise and Katie Holmes' new baby Suri. One of many reasons Darcy was proud to call himself American. 'But trust me. Do yourself a favor and enjoy being Amy Campbell while you still can,' he continued. 'Because once 'Markson' gets tacked on, it ain't NEVER comin' off, baby!' ''Amelia Markson... no, wait; DOCTOR Amelia Markson -- that's the most official sounding name ever! How could I not want that?'' she grinned, ''C'mon... one last chance on those Vegas tickets, it cost way more than I'd care to disclose...'' ''Hell, we can paint the town up red, if you want,'' Darcy said, adding a quink wink. ''It's been awhile since I've blown thousands of dollars exclusively on sin and debauchery. But NO shotgun wedding for you, 'Doctor' Antsy Pants.'' ''Vegas it is, then...'' The slightly evil 'Mwahahaha' that followed indicated to Darcy that Amy hadn't allowed herself to hear the words 'NO WEDDING' in his last sentence, but he was confident that Vegas would overwhelm the small town Canadian enough to make a shotgun ceremony the last thing on her mind. Man, my life seems so much more interesting when it's peppered with narration. PART TWO Veeeeeeeeeeeegas --------------------------------------------------- That's right, we made it to Vegas. He still hasn't agreed to marry me in a 20-minute chapel... but it's inevitable, right? Anyway, we've been to the airport, the hotel, the casino... now it's time to settle in for the night. Of course, no first night in Las Vegas is complete without a mountain of candy -- and THAT is why I'm on my way to the corner store, baby! I find that watching multiple episodes of Scrubs in succession really improves the wit of my inner monologue, which effectively makes simple things such as thinking far more entertaining than they should be. I wish I could apply that humour to the sneaking fear of committment that's crawling up the walls of my stomach, begging to spring out of my mouth past joyous matrimonious cheering and color selections for centerpieces. Mainly, I think the fear comes from my youth and inability to make a decision -- such as what my breakfast cereal of choice (coughfrootloopscough) may be -- without someone else's assistance or input. That, or the fact that I have very random moodswings during which I occasionally (okay, maybe constantly) develope an attraction to random female singers. Actually, that's a fine thing because Darcy finds it incredibly hot, right alongside that time I slept with Karina Wolfenden... at least, that time I THINK I did... Alright, so maybe I'm afraid because it's natural to have second or third thoughts -- but it would be SO much easier to deal with if I could excuse it as some sexy lesbian fantasy or maybe a little too much caffeine before bed. It could easily -- and truly -- be linked to the fact that I go through a vicious round of stress-induced fridge raiding every night at about 2 AM. But tonight, instead of accosting my refridgerator, I'm in downtown Las Vegas searching for an ATM -- which happens to be right next to the hotel -- so I can take out some cash to buy chocolate bars for Darcy and I to enjoy while we saunter around our hotel room half naked. While I'm not the biggest fan of wandering around after dark, what's the worst that could happen when you're well-lit by neon signs? ''Hey, bitch!'' Naturally, as with every time I ask myself 'What's the worst that could happen?', a not-so-fun scenario had presented itself in the form of a few jackasses with weapons in need of quick cash. When I realized what had just happened, the understanding that I couldn't feel the right side of my face followed. Well, couldn't feel it... aside from the swelling and agony in my eye socket. Part of me wondered if those assholes would come back and, dare I say, the thought worried me... while another part of me was kicking my own ass for not thinking to fight back. Instinct was slow to kick in tonight, perhaps because I was too busy devising a scheme I can only assume would have been very clever and result in Darcy marrying me in front of Elvis. And just as Darcy springs back into my mind, I notice the small sea of people - some who actually care and others who just want the ATM cleared for their use - who're asking the usual 'Omigawd are you alright?!' to my clearly battered body part to reveal my future husband. Sadly, it wasn't Jason Statham or that sexy beast who plays Dr. House... just Darcy. A very jilted Darcy, at that. Apparently he got tired of waiting around the room for me and decided to run around under the sparkly little lights of Sin City to find exactly what the hell was important enough at the corner store to get me out of bed for a whole ten minutes. What he found clearly wasn't what he'd been expecting, as I was barely three steps from our hotel, laying on the ground surrounded by worried passers-by as I rifled through my wallet and noticed that basically everything short of my all-too disgusting driver's license (with photo!) was missing. Now I was down a little bit of money, way too many credit cards, and the pint of blood that I was spitting onto the ground as I made a pathetic attempt to stand up. The crowd around me faded with Darcy's appearance, which means that he was either hushing them away or pretending to be a doctor again... either way, I can't say I wasn't glad to see him. I'll be honest -- I'm currently terrified and feeling rather vulnerable, but the fact that I'm almost crying is really disappointing to me. I need to make a mental note to remind myself that I need to kick my own ass later for being such a pansy. For the moment, though, I'm feeling a little dramatic and scared... and the word 'ouch' comes to mind. I can't even voice much of what I'm thinking, because my lip feels about eight sizes too big for my head. I guess now I know what being Angelina Jolie might feel like... only without the sexiness, foreign children and the concept of me being even somewhat attractive to a guy like Brad Pitt. ''Come on, babe...'' Darcy said gently, maneuvering to get his arms underneath me -- which suddenly made me regret my constant late-night snacking. ''I need you to hold onto me so I can carry you out of here before somebody calls an ambulance.'' I groaned to Darcy that I was okay as he carried me back into the hotel, while I pondered why the thugs didn't just take my whole wallet... is my driver's license picture REALLY that ugly? Maybe they were just thoughtful and wanted me to be able to legally drive even after they took the money I would have paid my insurance with. Vegas has such kind criminals. While we're on our way back to the hotel room, and in between my defeated whimpers, I realize something. Being assaulted in 'the real world' is always so much different from being hit in the ring, whether it's with a stiff punch or a leg sweep. I like to bask in that sweet post-battery sting, it really reminds me that I'm alive. Does that make me crazy? Wait... I'll answer my own question on this one -- YES, it does make me a little nutty, but why not try to take some good away from a bad moment? Yeah, NOW who's crazy?! Screw negativity. ... Ow. Back in the room, Darcy couldn't be trying to hurt me more if this was Untouchable! I need a hug, not a glass of water. His bedside manner really does leave something to be desired -- why can't this be like House, where we exchange witty banter and the giant mystery of 'Why Amy has a black eye' gets solved much to the chagrin of some cocky, moral-sticky interns? Wait, I just got the snot beaten out of me in an entirely random incident. Why do I care about my favorite TV show right now?! Oh, Gregory House... keeper of my he--errr--uhh... Back to reality, Amy... Darcy's starting to look concerned by your randomly looping in and out of consciousness while mumbling the names of TV actors. Pay attention to the fingers in front of your face, tell him that you see all fifteen of them. No, that's five! Five fingers, damnit... pull yourself together! Alright, so the ice pack is a little cold and not exactly soft, it's your job to show improvement for his sake. Try to smile, but at least spit the blood out of your mouth first. What did those assholes look like, anyway? You should find them and... uhh... do that stuff you do. You know, the thing you do when you're on that canvas thingy. Fight! That's the word. Why is my eye so sore? How come the ceiling's going all spinny...? In moments of semi-unconsciousness, I always come to understand that everything has a rhythm. My heart beats in my ear, my lungs fill and wheeze at the same time and my fingers shake in the same pattern almost constantly. Waking up again gave me a fresh perspective on... uhh... Darcy's ability to care for me? ''Oww.'' ''Just relax for a minute, Ames... we've gotta clear that bloody lip up a little.'' Why don't I just go to a damn hospital? Oh yeah, because excusing Darcy-induced injuries from the ring as 'Just a day at the office' never flies with doctors. Even though I got mugged by random idiots, I'd have to excuse several other injuries carried over from the pay-per-view to a bunch of underpaid quacks searching for a nugget of interesting drama as something other than spousal abuse. That's right, I've got the only job in the world that condones and requires woman-beating and spousal assault. I've run out of clever phrases, really. Who'd have thought, robbery tends to take a lot out of a gal... PART THREE Grocery store in East Bay, CA --------------------------------- I'm not one of those lame-list 'celebrities' who'll wear massive sunglasses in a grocery store or to the post office. Hell, I'm currently perusing Wal-Mart on full parade. It's probably because I've always had tattoos or some odd fashion spinning around me, so I'm used to people staring at me in weird ways. In my own way, I like to pretend that even if they do recognize who I am, they're only staring because A) I'm pretty or B) I have a monkey on my head. Sure, those are dream scenarios (especially the monkey part... man, those little guys are hilarious), but I'm not the biggest fan of being recognized as the chick who's on TV not because she's doing something important for the world, but because a bunch of beer-bellied hicks like to watch her bounce around a ring with sweaty men. It's like being a porn star, only without the blatant sexual prowess. It's sad that I get less respect than Jenna Jameson, and an even worse reputation because guys expect me to throw fake punches when they see me, not suck them off or deep-throat ten dicks at once. They like the fantasy that porn stars give them, whereas wrestlers just make them want to boast a male ego by challenging one of us to an arm-wrestling contest or, more often, a fist fight. I thankfully usually just get some nervous ass-grabbing or guys asking if AWC pays for boob jobs -- maybe because they think I need one? Either way, being recognized isn't quite the inspirational thrill that you'd think it would be. My best case scenario is if someone tells me that my actions at an event inspired them to stand up for themselves at school, whereas rock stars -- the lucky bastards -- have fans fawning over them, citing that each and every one of their songs 'touched' them somehow. Yeah, I don't think I've ever heard 'The way you punched so-and-so that one time really spoke to me'. There he is... drinking from his sippy juice cup, silently judging me. I've got half a mind to toss his mommy over the counter and smack that adorable orange ballcap right off of his head! He's the only guy in this place who doesn't recognize me even slightly. Oh, and I pointed out that I'm not normally a celebrity who wears dark glasses to avoid recognition... but normally, I don't have a black eye induced by an unlucky mugging in Vegas. Now I'm standing at a checkout in East Bay with a baby (That's right, the baby is the 'he' who's silently judging me while swigging away at his juice!) and his mother in front of me and a million beady little human eyes all over. Being me can get really odd, especially when I have multiple choices to give cause for people to stare at me: I've got some crazy tattoos, a big ring in my nose, a black eye and fat lip, mild celebrity... ... And did I mention that my eyes are two different colours?! Natural complications can really, really piss me off. For instance, having to play rock/paper/scissors with myself to decide exactly why all of aisle 5 happens to be glaring my way. I got beat up, I work on TV and I've got Bowie eyes but somehow manage to be twice as masculine as Bowie himself due to the whole 'professional wrestler' thing. The baby's staring now, and his mom keeps casting me glances from over her shoulder. You know what? Screw their eyes, and the 'Aimz sucks!' shouts from the teenaged assholes behind me can go straight to hell. I'm giving it my all right now, even if I'm only giving my all to standing in line at a grocery store. When I next get in the ring, I'll be sick with a flu, recently assaulted by strangers, recovering from having my first injury time in almost forever AND getting ready to break one of my fiancee's nose. Frankly, if I have to lose despite giving everything I have under these circumstances... then so be it. I can't say I didn't try, even if it hurts my ego to lose to Darcy and have that hanging over my head until the next match. Everything about this job is 'until the next match'... nothing's for certain. I'm not going to grow complacent, but I'm sure as hell not going to worry myself sick over a match right after having a few weeks off. I'll be rusty and yeah, I'll probably lose... but in the end, it's just another fight, and I get to go home to a brilliant man at the end of it. This all seems random, but at the end of the day, my bravery has a meaning... promise. When I get to the front of the line to pay for my box of donuts, Yoga Instructional DVD and an AWC action figure with my name on it, it's nothing but relief. The cashier looks at my toy purchase and then back at me about fifteen times after she rings it through, but never says a word aside from weather-related pleasantries like 'It's warm today'. The lady who was in front of me is shuffling out of the store with her demon-eyed baby who was damning me with a bad attitude the whole time I was in line, and things seem to clear up in my head. It's one of those moments where you can suddenly see clearly, because you know you're out of the thick brush with both feet on concrete, ready to run home freely. I paid for my goods and made a mad dash for the door, when suddenly... ''Omigawdit'sAIMZ!!!'' No! Avoid the little boy with the pen! ''Can you sign this? Hey, can my buddy get a picture? We thought it was you, then you got closer and we knew!'' I tried pretending I didn't hear, but he and his friends trapped me by standing right in front of me. All I could think in the moment was that if I needed to, I could probably throw them all into the dairy aisle and make a break for it. No, there are too many, and one has a camera! Damnit! Now I HAVE to play nice, or my face is posted alongside the word 'Bitch' online even more than it already is. And then I said what would trap me for at least three quarters of the next valuable hour of my life: ''Sure.'' PART FOUR Mattress in Easy Bay, CA --------------------------------- After the grocery store, it's time for a nap. Those kids only drew attention to who I was, and suddenly people who don't even watch wrestling, but assume I'm important because some brat's asking me for an autograph are accosting me for photos and signatures. All I want to do now is snuggle up next to the cat and take a good twelve hour nap... Napping is the hobby I've taken up aside from wrestling, drinking and bugging Darcy. For example, Reaper always expects me to be in my bed; And I guess you know something's wrong when your CAT isn't shocked that you're constantly neck-deep in a pillow, and kitties sleep 18 hours a day! The secondary man of my life -- coincidentally also a chunky fellow with long, black hair -- likewise expects me to be in bed whenever he can't find me. And I never disappoint... but right now he happens to be at the gym, working out muscles that he'll use to pummell me again this week. It's kind of sad that I've spent a few weeks out with an injury that Darcy gave me, only to debut again to another beating at his hands. I don't think AWC brass likes us -- or me -- very much. I'm probably seen as the dead weight holding the golden boy down. All of our lending services representatives are currently busy. Please continue to hold for the next available officer... BlahblahblahBLAHBLAHBLAHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! I just want to cancel my Visa so the jerkoffs who stole it can't go on a shopping spree or some kind of luxury cruise for tiny criminal minds! I hung up. Probably faster than I should have, because I could've sworn someone picked up just as I tossed the phone onto the pillow next to me. Maybe it was just the sound of Darcy barging in just as I almost fell back to sleep. Time to distract him from his pre-match boasting so I can get a few winks in... ''Johnny sent a post card,'' I said, flinging an envelope at him, ''Read it. Track him on mapquest... do something for a half hour or more so I can nap before I whine about my leg?'' While Darcy isn't the nicest or most caring of all men, he's also not the worst. That, however, doesn't mean that he isn't completely incapable of not acting like a total jock every time something comes down to he and I having to work with one another in the ring. Thankfully, his ego was temporarily sidelined by the introduction of a mystery. ''That so? I don't remember Johnny saying anything ab--'' he paused to read, ''Johnny sent me a postcard from Amsterdam! And that can only mean one thing...'' He was giddily looking under the postage stamp, and THAT only meant one thing -- the Asshole From Amsterdam sent MY golden boy a present. I sighed. The frat boys were in full swing, but that didn't mean I couldn't break up the party, ''If it's a tab of acid, I'm gonna have your balls under my feet SO fast...'' ''Negative. It's just hash. A little dry, I have to say, this isn't up to his normal standards. But impressive nonetheless.'' For the love of God... is this high school again? ''... Seriously?'' ''Come on, Amy... quit looking at me like I'm Ellis Nash,'' he tossed his hands to his sides, ''It's a gift from Johnny... I can't turn it down.'' You sure can, I thought, Especially if I... Damnit, look at those puppy dog eyes. I can't even finish my thoughts... guess it's time to put up a nagging front and eventually relinquish the win. If only his life could be as easy in the ring. ''Readiness to marry is sometimes a sign of maturity. I guess, in your case, it's an excuse to turn fourteen again? Seriously, Darcy... you never noticed the whole 'Amy's a complete addict just getting on the mend' thing? You're funnelling beer to an alcoholic.'' ''Well, since I'm already committed to doing the most grown-up thing I can think of... don't you think I should knock out all the immaturity I've got left?'' In all honesty, he had a point. I guess that's why I was half-smiling. ''Just don't take up the big TV watching some stupid movie, and do it outside - NOT in the car, not anywhere I might be within the next day. You can thank me for letting you bypass my usual complaints by sending you to smoke up when we're picking out bridesmaid's dresses.'' ''Last chance to join me...'' he smirked, ''Those internet videos get about 85 times more hilarious.'' ''The funniest thing on the internet right now are elitist message board jerkoffs who spend half of their time bragging about what you're about to do... which I stopped finding impressive around the age of seventeen.'' I sighed, ''That means 'No, thanks', in case you were serious.'' ''Suit yourself. Get some rest and get that leg healed up... I want it good and healthy to go so I can put it back in a sharpshooter.'' How could he be that insensitive? Just because Johnny Fratboy would appreciate him frying a few braincells in his honour didn't mean he had to shove me RIGHT back to the verge of drugging up for kicks. I'm in bed and he's outside having fun by now. It's kind of like the 'She said yes!' celebration after he practically cut my leg off at the last pay-per-view. Speaking of which, I'm not sure forgiving him for that was the right idea... it really cut down on the aggression I'd need for the match coming up, which the jackasses at AWC so kindly gave me upon my not-so-glorious return to the ring. Nevermind being angry now, though. Let him keep giving me reasons to want to kick his ass this week... Maybe letting work and play mingle isn't such a bad idea. After all, bringing aspects of my relationship into the ring has gotten me a win and a diamond so far. What's the worst that could happen?
