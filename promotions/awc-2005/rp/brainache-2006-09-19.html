Brainache

19 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One... two... three. Three lights on one unit, hanging high above my head - below the flourescent rectangles that have the room around me lit. They put monitors on my chest... the cold, sticky things... then a man puts a black mask to my mouth and tells me to hold it tight, breathe in and tell him when my body starts feeling tingly. I can't remember much past that, except that I was trying to fight the effects of the concentrated dose of laughing gas I was breathing in. He kept asking me questions that I don't recall, and I remember wondering if my 'uh huhs' really sounded as squeaky and odd as they did in my head right then. I wiggled an arm and he said 'We haven't done anything yet'. Past that? No clue. They didn't have the IV in me - the laughing gas was there to sedate me enough to keep me from losing it at the sight of a needle. It must have gone in just as I blacked out, because I woke up next thinking that I was on Darcy's couch with a bad hangover. I felt a warmth in my stomach and remember finally opening my eyes to the sight of a hot pink set of scrubs on the elderly nurse who was over my bed. She was grumbling to another nurse, ''Two incisions, and the sheet says she'll need a shot of Gravol,'' which - even in my grogginess - I understood as true, because my mind shot past the pain in my body and straight into the nausea. More than anything, I hated throwing up so I concluded, in that little recovery room, that I wasn't going to... no matter how much the anasthetic pushed me. Leaning my head to the right side made me feel a little less ill, so I rested it there and glanced through squinted eyes at the woman in the recovery bed next to mine. There was a bald guy at a desk ahead of me who waved as I peered at him. Other than that I saw no men, so I suspected this was recovery for women who'd just had their tubes tied, or something similar done to mangle their ladybits. It took me a few minutes to remember what the doctors had planned to do with me, but the searing in my belly reminded me that they were removing old scar tissue and fixing a ruptured vessel in my lower abdomen, compliments of the Honda I dove through at the last pay per view. My organs must be made of rice paper, because I'm the undisputed queen of random internal injury. After what seemed like an eternity of me trying to get my eyes to focus and my stomach to settle, a nurse came and informed me that she was going to take my IV out. I tried to look, because I hadn't even known where the damned thing was, but ended up popping my head back down and falling asleep. My next memory was the sound of descending in the elevator, still on a bed and this time with the rare sight of a male nurse pushing me. It was a sight that faded as soon as I put my head back down. The next thing I remember was utter loneliness. I'd been home for hours, sore as hell, and nobody was there to rub my neck or wish things better. Called mom, told her I was alright. Called Marie, told her I was alright... and Darcy was still nowhere to be found. When my cell rang again, I was hopeful - but it only lasted a moment. ''Amy?'' ''I'm sleeping. Go aw--'' ''We've got to talk business, whether you're coherant or not. I've got to do my job, and we did just renew your medical insurance...'' For a second, I wondered who'd have the gall to talk to me like that, but then I checked the number. Some yuppie from head office - probably the same asshole that convinced Darcy to type a bunch of sweet talk about me on the internet last week. Either way... ''Hurry.'' ... I had to listen. Whoever this is, I guarantee that they're up the ass of the guy paying my bills. ''Are you aware how many t-shirts you sell?'' ''No.'' ''Do you know how many t-shirts Darcy sells?'' ''Nope.'' ''Do you want to?'' ''Nuh uh.'' ''A lot.'' ''Then how come I'm not laying here in a mansion while manservants rub the imprint of Chainz' ass off of my foot?'' I don't think he had an answer for that. ''... The point is, you two are mainstays for AWC right now, and it's a perfect time for your popularity to skyrocket. We once had Adam Dick, Ellis Nash... but honestly, they're old news - aren't they?'' Buttering me up would work if I had a few shots with the morphine... ''You guys didn't pay for enough painkillers to make me as dumb as you're treating me.'' As the rest of the conversation would prove, being blunt with these guys was never the right plan of action. I seemed to push and push until I got an awfully firm ultimatum, and it wasn't one I'd planned on. But, after 45 minutes talking shop with some jackass in a tie on the other end of the phone, I was more than ready to crash for a few days. 24 Solid Hours of Sleep Later... When I'm bored or just looking for someone to laugh at, I go on myspace. Yeah, I said it. I check up on the people 'back home', including stoner buddies and kids who - for some ungodly reason - look up to me. A few years ago, I taught this one guy how to play guitar. I think he's 19 or 20 now, and I just found out that he's been sleeping with this tiny tramp I never thought was more than 12. Apparently she's 17, but I can't imagine why they're together. Remember high school? If you weren't a virgin, do you remember the kind of people you were fooling around with... you know, back when sex wasn't actually any fun, but just something you got to brag about at noon? Why were we ever with some of those dirtbags? Can you remember why you were, if you were 'lucky' enough to have been seeking sex in high school? I'm rambling. I guess I just miss when life wasn't any more complicated than getting stoned in the passenger seat of a random buddy's Tempo while discussing the finer points of Kirk Hammett's solo in 'One'. A lot of people haven't grown past that yet, but they're miserable now. Girls their age want guys with cars, or at least jobs. We don't brag about banging the cute guy who delivers pizzas anymore. Wait... what the fuck? I own a miniature skateboard for a dog I don't have and when I went to Subway tonight, I got the combo deal so I could get the cookie Darcy likes best. The cat sure as hell isn't using either of them, and there hasn't been anyone else for more than a week. Only now do I realize that I've been driving around in the dark, chewing my nails to this internal soundtrack of cynicism and bitterness. It's the Percoset. I become very alive, incredibly awake - then I get hungry and, before I can make a stack of sandwiches, I get so tired that I'm dizzy. Sweet painkillers. Maybe I shouldn't be driving, like it says on the bottle... I wouldn't wanna become the little black and white, broken stick figure stuck under the car on the sticker. I'm halfway there, anyway - pretty much a mess, and not even an overly insightful mess. It'd be one thing if I could write a fruity poem or take up smoking, but my outlet only comes once a week and lately, it's been more of a fight for my sanity and freedom than any sort of entertainment, show or expression. Pays the bills, though. I need to concentrate... anything to stop my mind from going this fast. There's something at the core of all of it, and it's that what bothers me more than anything right now is the fact that Darcy's got a title while I'm sitting here picking pieces of a sedan out of my ass, and he's the one who's still pissed off. That's probably why I've been driving back toward the house, and find myself nearly crashing into it as I pull off a gorgeous parking job with enough medicine in me to kill Black Beauty fifty times over... And that's why this story is TO BE CONTINUED! (dun dunn dunnn) sorry, it's been a week [detail withheld]
