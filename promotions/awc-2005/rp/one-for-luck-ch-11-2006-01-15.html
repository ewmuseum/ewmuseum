Date: 15th January 2006 One for Luck, Ch. 11 : Disappearing Truths. A New Year’s Eve party in Koln [Back track] The snow flakes dotted the streets of Koln. People were busy about their usual business, arriving home from a long day of work or going out to various parties. Fairy lights twinkled against the moon-lit sky. It was a beautiful contrast to the troubles Pierce was facing back in America. Pierce had managed to retain his belt; he was sore and worn, but things were picking up, he knew it. Pierce decided he would dress smartly for his date with Sarah Kennedy and had spent an hour deciding on what shirt and trousers to wear, until eventually he decided on a pair of black trousers, a dark navy shirt and a black jacket. He looked the real deal, he thought to himself as he braved the elevator toward Sarah’s hotel room. He was extremely nervous about his feelings for Sarah. He knew he felt something that he had never felt before, but couldn’t quite understand what it was. ‘Come on, you can do this’ he continuously muttered to himself as he watched each number tick on by. His nerves welling-up inside him, his throat dry. ‘Here goes nothing…’ he thought as he watched the doors open. He was greeted by a red carpet that was dimly lit by lights that dotted the ceiling. Pierce checked in his pocket for Sarah’s hotel number, he was just hoping that he wouldn’t bump into a fellow colleague, but he didn’t feel embarrassed or insecure. A slight cough came out of Pierce as he tested his breath and knocked lightly on the oak door. There were a couple minutes of silence and the anticipation was killing Pierce. His nerves had managed to get the best of him, he could feel them creeping up on him, from butterflies in his stomach to tingles along his spine. The door opened and Sarah Kennedy, stood, balancing on one foot as she tried to put on her high-heel. Pierce was taken aback by the image before him. Sarah was dressed beautifully. Her blonde hair wisped around her shoulders and her black silk dress accentuated her feminine features. She wore a small pearl necklace and some matching earrings. He was speechless until he realised Sarah Kennedy had been standing in front of him, staring at him too. “Eh…Hey,” Pierce said as he awoke from his slight trance. “Hey,” Sarah smiled and pulled Pierce by the arm into her room. “You look, really, beautiful,” Pierce said with his hands clasped together uncomfortably. “Thanks, you look really good too,” Sarah said with her back to him as she bent over a vanity chair to get her watch. Pierce, trying not to stare at her rear-end, focused his attention on the large screen TV in the corner of the room, where a news reel on the events of 2005 played over and over, showing the many disasters that had inflicted upon the world in one year. It was 11:45 pm and it would soon be January 2006 - Pierce was hoping for a better year, an improvement on the many bad points that he had experienced in 2005. “Ready to go?” Sarah asked, standing behind Pierce. Pierce jumped slightly, his mind elsewhere. He turned to Sarah and felt his heart pounding. He was shaking, his blood pulsing. All he knew was that he really liked Sarah Kennedy, but the rest was all new to him, the feeling almost foreign. He had never felt like this with Lois or Kelli. “Course,” Pierce smiled and opened the door for her. They were heading to the top floor of the five star hotel. A New Years Eve party was being held above them. Okay, it wasn’t the best place to take a first date, but Pierce had no clue about the sites in Koln or the language. The last thing he wanted was to do was get lost. Pierce and Sarah walked toward the elevator, side by side. Pierce was becoming nervous, his heart was beating. He had never looked at Sarah in this way before and he wondered why it was only happening now, but he liked the feeling, she was beautiful and it amazed him that a wonderful woman such as Sarah would like him. It was clear he had been a mess these last few months, but he had fallen for Sarah in the last few weeks; even with the year they’ve know each other, he still felt slightly on edge about his feelings. He didn’t know if she felt the same. The doors opened and Pierce let Sarah go in first and soon joined her. Silence filled the elevator, both a little nervous on what to say. Pierce hated uncomfortable silences and felt like saying something, but when Sarah held his hand he was perfectly fine with the quiet, as she seemed to be too. He held her grip lightly, continuously staring at the gold coloured doors. In the corner of his eye he could see her smiling and it brought him joy and tingles down his spine. The doors opened and Pierce and Sarah walked into a large ball room; it’s ceiling dotted in lights and the floor covered in balloons. Many people were dancing to the quick speed music that a DJ played from a distance. The bar was filled with many different arrays of drinks. Pierce gazed up toward the sparkling lights still holding Sarah’s hand as she led him toward an empty table toward the front. Pierce didn’t try to stop her, he let her lead him. He was too busy staring at Sarah and how beautiful she looked under the light. She had already attracted the attention of many single men at the bar. “Em, you want something to drink?” Pierce asked, pulling a chair out for Sarah. “Yeah, please. I’ll have a water,” she smiled. Pierce nodded and joined the many men at the bar, who continued to stare at Sarah Kennedy seated at the table. One man turned to Pierce as he ordered two waters and held them in his hand. “Nice bird,” the Australian muttered. Pierce just stood next to him, turned around and gazed toward Sarah again. She returned his gaze and smiled. “Yeah, she really is beautiful,” Pierce whispered back. Pierce, inside smiling, just nodded his head for a goodbye and tried to avoid the many dancers on the floor. He was relieved when the music began to die down. The crowd, not leaving the floor, continued to dance. Pierce re-joined Sarah, a smile across his face. He gave her the water she ordered and sat down next to her. “Someone you know?” Sarah asked with a smile. Her heart was pounding as she gazed toward Pierce’s blue eyes. They held a glow she hadn’t seen in months, his infamous smile lit his face and it made her blood rush. She really liked Pierce; she just hoped he felt the same. “Eh, him, no,” Pierce said, sipping nervously on his drink. His hand tapping against table to the beat of the music. “What were you talking about?” she asked curiously. Pierce moved in closer toward Sarah and rested his hands on her hips as he made sure not to trample her feet. He saw on a large clock that it was a minute to midnight. Pierce, without even realising, had moved in a little more as he feels Sarah rest her head on his chest. The countdown for the New Year rings throughout the room, but Pierce doesn’t care, he’s completely caught in the moment with Sarah as he moves gently around in circles, the balloons following their footsteps. The countdown has ended and the chorus drowns out the ‘Happy New Years‘. Many have rejoined the dance floor now, singing along as best they can. Pierce’s heart is beating heavily in his chest as he nervously leans down toward Sarah and takes a breath. “Happy New Year,” Pierce whispers in her ear and straightens his back. His hands gently grazing Sarah’s hands as he comes back to a straight position. His skin has broken into a large tingle, not from the cold, but from Sarah’s touch. Her blue eyes pierce through Pierce’s as she gazes toward him. The song is reaching its end and Pierce not wanting to break away from the moment, continues to stare at Sarah. She moves in closer, her hips gently grazing his. Pierce shivers, but likes the feeling and leans in toward Sarah; his hand brushes away a loose strand of blonde hair that gently fluttered around her chin. Her hearts pounding just as much as Pierce’s, he can feel it. He leans in for the kiss and Sarah leans in too. He pulls her in tightly, and kisses her as the rest of the crowd are singing. “You want to go out for some air?” Pierce smiles and holds her hand as they head toward the roof exit. The roofs dotted in snow and Christmas lights. Fireworks light the sky as Pierce stares toward the dark abyss, his heart continuously pounding. His mind racing and his lips tingling.
