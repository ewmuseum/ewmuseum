The Roots part 1 ... 'Something to Chew On'

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 09th, 2005 'Back To The Roots' Lavon's History & Training LA A Few Days Earlier The office is as hard to find, and intentionally so. Located near the end of a narrow, curving lane, in a quarter of L.A more renowned for its nightlife than its tragic past, the entrance is marked only by a small brass plaque bearing the inscription 'Wrestling History & Training LA'. The security system, installed by an obscure firm based in Oakland, is formidable and highly visible. A camera glowers menacingly from above the door. No one is admitted without an appointment and a letter of introduction. Visitors must pass through a finely tuned magnetometer. Purses and briefcases are inspected with unsmiling efficiency by one of two disarmingly pretty girls. One is called Reveka, the other Sarah. Once inside, the visitor is escorted along a claustrophobic corridor lined with gunmetal-gray filing cabinets, then into a large typical chamber with pale floors, a high ceiling, and bookshelves bowed beneath the weight of countless volumes and file folders. The donnish clutter is appealing, though some are unnerved by the green-tinted bulletproof windows overlooking the melancholy courtyard. The man who works there is untidy and easily missed. It is his special talent. Sometimes, as you enter, he is standing atop a library ladder rummaging for a book. Usually he is seated at his desk, wreathed in cigarette smoke, peering at the stack of paperwork and files that never seems to diminish. He takes a moment to finish a sentence or jot a loose minute in the margin of a document, then he rises and extends his tiny hand, his quick brown eyes flickering over you. 'Ethan Phoenix,' he says modestly as he shakes your hand, though everyone in LA knows who runs this training facility. Were it not for Lavon's well-established reputation, his appearance--a shirtfront chronically smeared with ash, a shabby burgundy-colored cardigan with patches on the elbows and a tattered hem--might prove disturbing. Some suspect he is without sufficient means; others imagine he is an ascetic or even slightly mad. One woman concluded he was suffering from a permanently broken heart. How else to explain that he had never been married? The air of bereavement that is sometimes visible when he thinks no one is looking? Whatever the visitor's suspicions, the result is usually the same. Most cling to him for fear he might float away. He points you toward the comfortable couch. He asks the girls to hold his calls, then places his thumb and forefinger together and tips them toward his mouth. Coffee, please. Out of earshot the girls quarrel about whose turn it is. Reveka is an Israeli from Haifa, olive-skinned and black-eyed, stubborn and fiery. Sarah is a well-heeled American , more cerebral than Reveka and therefore more patient. She is not above resorting to deception or even outright lies to avoid a chore she believes is beneath her. Reveka, honest and temperamental, is easily outmaneuvered, and so it is usually Reveka who joylessly plunks a silver tray on the coffee table and retreats in a sulk. Lavon has no set formula for how to conduct his meetings. He permits the visitor to determine the course. He is not averse to answering questions about himself and, if pressed, explains how it came to be that one of Israel's most talented young archaeologists chose to sift through the unfinished business of the Holocaust rather than the troubled soil of his homeland. His willingness to discuss his past, however, goes only so far. He does not tell visitors that, for a brief period in the early 1970s, he worked for Israel's notorious secret service. Or that he is still regarded as the finest street surveillance artist the service has ever produced. Or that twice a year, when he returns to Israel to see his aged mother, he visits a highly secure facility north of Tel Aviv to share some of his secrets with the next generation. Inside the service he is still referred to as 'the Ghost.' His mentor, a man called Ari Shamron, always said that Eli Lavon could disappear while shaking your hand. It was not far from the truth. 'Something to Chew On' Streets of L.A Present Time [ The sun shines brightly on this particular little corner of LA. where Ethan Phoenix makes his home. The weather letting us know that spring has arrived in full force with the heat haze in the air and the ever present background hum of air conditioning. One could ask then, if it is so durn hot, why we happen to be outdoors. One could ask but one won’t. The name of the eatery we’re at isn’t clearly visible at the moment but it doesn’t really need to be. The crowd of teenagers chatting loudly two tables over tell us that it isn’t somewhere particularly fancy and the brisk yet professional service lets us know this is a place where turnover is king. ] [ Hmm…faux southwest décor, college age waiters, enormous portions of food…anyone in the mood for some cheesecake? ] [ Ethen Phoenix is seated at an outdoor table at this particular venue, tucking in heartily into what looks to be today’s lunch. It’s a…well, the word “sandwich” doesn’t quite apply as what Ethan is eating seems to be about two inches past the legal definition of the word. Essentially, it’s a big ol hunk of protein topped with a regular produce section’s worth of vegetables, all put together between two pieces of bread. Ethan seems to be demolishing large sections of this construction with each bite, washing them down with swigs from the glass of ice water on the table in front of him. The sleeves on his blue short-sleeved shirt ride up slightly on his biceps with each upwards movement. ] 'Hello Atlantic Wrestling Club, I know you are grateful that I could be in apperance here this afternoon. It is of course the day after one of the most shocking Fresh! in recent memory. One filled with a lot of downs, and a few ups here and there. For example, Ethan Phoenix, arrived on the scene not a minute too late, and showed the world why I am the greatest acquisation in AWC's history. By far, Ethan Phoenix, proved to the world why the world hasn't been able to forget about Ethan Phoenix. The impact I made a few short years ago, the multitude of fans that surronded me, the lime light and the gold. Everybody thought 'finally, a man worth watching'. I was the bread and butter of each show, every magazine that year of 2002 had Ethan Phoenix's face on the cover. The smirk that made ladies melt, the build that made men jealous. Then, in a blaze of glory, Ethan Phoenix disappeared. The crowds were shocked, disappointed, as though there was nothing to live for. New organizations rose up and fell with less money then they started with. Few made it over the 'two month hump' and those that have any reputable status earned it through lying and stealing. Wrestling fans lost hope. Surely, Ethan Phoenix will return. And then, on some local television station somewhere, there was a little show called Fresh! Old men like Pact, young men like LaVelle, and the in-betweeners made some fans wonder if AWC could be the answer to the boredom that captivated our senses. And then on a Tuesday Night, June 7th, 2005, every prayer, every meditation, every single hope and wish was answered. Ethan Phoenix returns. Now this bull-shit little company has a pilar to lean on. Now this wrestling business as a sight to set their eyes on. Now, is Ethan Phoenix's time to shine.' [ Ethan takes a drink of the glass of water in front of him. ] ' This business was just getting boring. Tired. Not fun. I mean, I fought Sean Laughreah five times, and it seemed that he was the only person in the fed that could give me a run for my money. And then I got used to his style, and that stopped being challenging. Then they started throwing emerging stars at me, telling me that they are the future. I was happy about this, because I thought maybe they had improved enough to become more competition. It only took two matches for me to find out they were also a waste of time. Well, I had been feeling unhappy before. That was obvious. But until then I had thought I had no option but to stay and keep fighting the poor competition. But this time, I decided to call it quits, tell the wrestling world to shove it, turn my back and leave. Then David Harber showed up. He told me he could offer me something. Happiness was the main factor, as I always point out. But he told me if I went to AWC, I could have all the competition I desired. And for the rest of the night at Fresh!, I walked by the other wrestlers’ locker rooms and took at look at each of them. And every time I saw one the same question came into my head: “Can this guy actually beat me?” [ Ethan shrugs ] 'So I took an upclose look on Tuesday night, I went and checked these three 'up and coming stars'. I was less than impressed. Then I walked around, scouted out Pact, scouted out Strider, from a distance watched these numbnuts walk around with a chip on their shoulder as though they were God's gift to wrestling. Nothing... notta... nobody. I am more convinced now then ever that I am God's gift to AWC. Stepped into the squared circle, put Ellis Nash on her face where she belongs, knocked another guy out that I have never heard of, got my hands on that Fredrock fella', and then almost broke the wrist of a grandmother. And the evidence is in the pie, the proof is in the pudding, the world will see Ethan make his return to the ring, live on Fresh!... with a [sighs] mystery partner. I know, I know... how does this look for this generation's superstar? How can this possible be good for the crem' - de - la - crem'? Well let me break this down for ya...' [Ethan takes another sip of water, clearing his throat and continueing ] ' It appears my first opponent's in this Alliance Championship league is none other than, Kris Krimzon and The Educator. [Nods] Yep, I know... I know what the masses are thinking. You mean to tell me that those two clowns who were off - and - on the show, consistantly with their whole teacher 'n student gig, are actually going to be my opponent's! As if it weren't enough that, Ellis Nash, a chick who couldn't stand face - to - face with me, got slotted in this Main Event match. Putting the flaming jobbers aside for a moment, I listened to your promo Ellis, I actually never watched it because I hear visual exposure to your naked body can lead to blindness, male pattern baldness and herpes of the inner earlobe. And they put you in the Main Event? Your like a man trapped in a gorilla's body.' [ Ethan shakes his head, taps his finger on the table, looks down and then looks back at the camera, annoyed and partially laughing at the last comment. ] ' But now Ethan Phoenix has to face against two of the most annoying character's ever to grace this business. Someone may want to know my opionon on these two slobs. Well, Kris Krimzon is the characterization of the typical high school drama queen. There are times you want attention, and you whine and carry on when you don't get it in the proper fashion. There are times you want everyone to realize how much better you have to be than everyone else, so you start with your gossip and insinuation to try and muddy someone else's reputation. Then there are the times you put off the image that you're playing 'hard to get', as if you were trying to get some lovestruck boy to notice you exist -- mainly by disappearing and having other people talk for you. He's in this business, doing this, because this is all he can do. His clout doesn't carry beyond his little Kris Krimzon-make-believe world, no matter how hard he tries. And to make things worse, he tags with 'The Educator', whose whole apperance and very personality screams mediocrity. Outright mediocrity. If he isn't too busy studying the 'Grammatical Wave For the Next Generation', he is trying to correct somebody else's grammar. Its been a long week, Educator, I've been training and preparing for this return for quite sometime. But even still, the worse part of this upcoming week is going to have to listen to your nonsensical prattle. So do me a favor, hit the books, spend the week in them. [ Shrugs ] And come Tuesday Night, I'll show you a real education. ' [ Ethan's smile is very condensending, and he does it very well. ] ' And in regards to the partner, [scuffs] I don't even need one. Let it be known that if your Ethan Phoenix's partner: you can consider yourself one luck bitch to have the opportunity to even have your name beside mine. Ethan Phoenix. ' [ With another sip of water, Ethan sets the emtpy glass down and nods ] ' This generation's superstar ' [ Cut. Edit. Send. Play. All that jazz... ] 'Back To The Roots' Lavon's History & Training LA A few days earlier He is quiet around his guests, just as he was quiet around the squared circle when his sptlight once shined brightly. He is a chain smoker, but if it bothers the guest he will refrain. A polyglot, he listens to you in whatever language you prefer. His gaze is sympathetic and steady, though behind his eyes it is sometimes possible to detect puzzle pieces sliding into place. He prefers to hold all questions until the visitor has completed his case. His time is precious, and he makes decisions quickly. He knows when he can help. He knows when it is better to leave the past undisturbed. Should he accept your case, he asks for a small sum of money to finance the opening stages of his training. He does so with noticeable embarrassment, and if you cannot pay he will waive the fee entirely. He receives most of his operating funds from donors, but Wartime Claims is hardly a profitable enterprise and Lavon is chronically strapped for cash. Yet, somehow he always offered Ethan a drink as a young man, or helped finance his car, or helped in any way he could. Lavon saw something in Ethan Phoenix before the world did. He saw a bright exuberance, an exciteable spirit. He saw what was a kid who knew little or nothing about wrestling, blossom into a great all-rounder. Lavon never encouraged going to the top rope, but for some reason Ethan always did. Lavon's exciting is clearly seen at seeing Ethan's face, but some disappointment is there as well... Lavon was crushed to hear Ethan was leaving the business. After his other 'child' Wyatt Connors was taken out of action on a stretcher, Lavon lived to see Ethan. When he heard Ethan was through with the business, Lavon's heart was crushed. Too old now to take another young man under his wings, but still not old enough to forget the memories of the glory he once had in the ring. That's why it was so exciting to see Ethan. He knew Ethan wouldn't trot down those halls unless it were to get back into the ring... and Lavon loved the idea. To be continued... View Ethan Phoenix 's Biography
