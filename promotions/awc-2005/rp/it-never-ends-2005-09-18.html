It never ends

18 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/18/2005 Glory Gold Success Three things running through Titan’s head as he sleeps at night, but yet he can’t seem to concentrate on the one thing he desires most. Crowd Pleasing His entire career has being focused on just that and it seems as if the last few months it has being almost another completely different agenda for him. During recent interviews and show tapings he has being caught show boating and also talking about different things like only out there for himself and winning everything for just him. Well on Tuesday he will have a different agenda, it seems that he is partnered up with a tag team and he will have to work together and not just on his own. “Go figure I get stuck with the team that lost last week. You’d figure someone doesn’t like me right now but I guess it’s just fate.” Mutters Titan. As we see more clearly now Titan is working out at a gym and he seems to be getting a lot of looks from various people in the crowd. It must be out, they know he is an AWC superstar. “Everywhere I’ve gone in the past week has being incredibly busy and it seems everyone either knows who I am or knew who I used to be. I guess the AWC does that. So far with me it’s brought out all sorts of crazy things. Like look at this for instance, I can’t even think straight lately, it used to be about the fans and about the friends and now all I seem to care about is the glory and the gold, I mean why?”. He grabs a towel from a gym bag and begins wiping sweat off of his face. “I better change that train of thought for Tuesday though.” He wipes some more sweat off of the machine he was using and than puts the towel around his neck. “I’m partnered up with a tag team known as Deliverance and last week they proved nothing by losing to O’Shea and McCarthy. Well boys get your act together this week because we’ve got a hard road ahead of us. Locke, Shipley and something else...well I’m guessing someone but his name sounds dreadfully familiar...Delorian that’s it. Trios tag match. Well I guess there really putting me to a test here so there’s nothing else I can do but impress them back. I comply with any decision you make and I’ll face anything you put down in front of me.” Titan walks over to the vending machine and puts in some money for a bottle of water. “Last week was rather interesting on a separate note and you fans out there who didn’t get to go missed a hell of a match. Mapleleaf of course ended out being the one on top but hey, kudos to another Canadian is all I can really say. Next time though I will be a little more prepared and also have a little more under my belt at that time as well.” “But back on topic, before I leave you to whatever it is your normally do Tuesday seems to me like it’s set and ready to go. To my three opponents, all I really want to say is just show-up and give it your best because I’m definitely going back to my old agenda and that’s being a crowd pleaser.” As the scene fades one has to wonder, Titan seems to be battling it out with a lot of inner demons right now, up is down and down is up, day is night and vice versa. How will this affect his match on Tuesday? Will Deliverance even put up a fight, or will they hold out so Titan suffers a beating at the hands of his opponents? Watch Fresh! though and maybe we’ll find out more...
