Date: September 11th, 2005 Forget Bret Hart, Vinnie Mac and Calgary. Fuck Matt Hardy and Edge, or even Magnum Wolf and Ivan Stanislav – THIS is the question that all the insiders need the answer to: …What’s so important about spandex? Why is it that after a wrestler’s brother concusses him with the aid of a sledge hammer, before revealing that he has been sleeping with his girl and, just for added kicks, neutered his cat – the first thing said wrestler does is reappear with a snazzy new pair of tights and brand new entrance music by MotorHead? I don’t know about you, but after a night of pain I either cry, scream or both. So what is it that causes wrestlers to hit their hotel room after the worst, most dramatic day of their lives, and immediately get to questioning whether they should choose red or yellow wrist tape? After years of smarks pondering, Julie of the T.T.Ses finally had the answer (you’ll wanna kick yourself when you hear it). “Well it’s all about image, isn’t it” No question mark needed. “See…” She paused, as she popped a cigarette between her lips, then fumbled with a lighter/zippo. Yes, Julie was not only a T.T.S, but also part of the select group of people who start a sentence, only to irritatingly interrupt themselves with a cigarette or spoonful of food or drink. Apparently it’s a sign of selfishness. “…See” she eventually continued “…you can’t just turn up looking like some...err…back alley brawler, or something. Yes, it’s a combat sport, but it’s still entertainment. The general demographic of people who attend these shows are fairly young men, and what they want is to be dazzled. They want everything to be big and animated. That’s the only way you’ll make money, I assure you” She said; which, in summary, meant “Worship me, for I studied business at Esher”. “Yah, men like that sort of thing.” Added Rachael, sticking to a subject she was fairly confident in. “I once went out with a guy who still read comic books.” She added, rather pointlessly. “Exactly.” Julie agreed. “It’s like boxing” Sally gave an animated shake of the head, to remind the group that as the token ‘nice one’ she was against boxing. “Boxers have all the tassels on their shorts and the corner men, and all that razzmatazz. It’s all just for show, really. They’re never even really that badly beat up – because nobody could get that hurt from just punches. It’s all just to give the crowd what they paid for” Julie continued, finally proving that she knew nothing of what she was talking about. The rest of the TTSes nodded in agreement. …Well, all except Harry. He grunted a shallow, monotone grunt as a flamingly camp man placed a tape measure to his “inner thigh”. The TTS stance on homosexuals is a complicated one. First of all, all female TTSes must display a basic tolerance of homos’. It is their duty to smother them under their nurturing wing to shelter them from discrimination and ignorance. The gays don’t mind, of course – because everyone knows that because they like bumsex, they automatically enjoy hanging out with women, shopping and listening to their problems, too. However, this tolerance is in it’s most basic form. For a more advanced tolerance of the ‘other team’, TTSes must make a choice: An entity of TTSes may only bare an advanced tolerance to one of three…umm…things. Those being either homosexuals, non-whites or domesticated animals. Cruel as it may sound, those are the rules, and no TTS has dared to challenge them since the dawn of time. Let me give you some examples… Paris Hilton – She’s always carrying around that little rat-dog. It’s her ‘thing’ (along with being junkie, I guess). As such, you don’t see Paris around either ethnics or homosexuals (pseudo-lesbians clearly don’t count) Here’s another… The Sex and the City girls – They have that gay lackey. Therefore, they all play the “we’re vulnerable princesses, and are scared of animals” card, and don’t interact with non-whites (well there was that one black guy who screwed the ginger one, but she hastily dumped him after being informed that she could be fined for breaking the rules). As for the guys from F.R.I.E.N.D.S, they bend the rules ever so slightly. They had animals in the form of Marcell the monkey and the awesome duck/chicken duo, but they also had that black chick who was with both Ross and Joey – HOWEVER, she wasn’t in the show at the same time as the pets, and therefore escapes punishment. …But I’m going off on a tangent. Let us return to the tailor’s where AWC’s resident TTS clan are gathered. Outside, their Black-British driver, Tim (though they seem to have chosen to call him Dwayne) waits with their shared silver Mercedes. Inside, Harry – who’s tolerance only extends to his black driver, ‘Dwayne’ – began to grow extensively uncomfortable as the tailor slid the tape measure further up his inner leg. “Stop!” He spewed, leaping off the pedestal and instinctively brushing the illusory dirt from his leg and shoulder. “That’s enough!” He added; because he felt that a single syllable wasn’t really enough for a good exclamation of disgust. “What? I am doing what you wanted, yes?” The camp tailor-come-designer added a flick of his limp wrist just to remind anyone who had forgotten his sexuality. “You wanted tight, spandex biker shorts, yes?” Harry didn’t like the look in his eye. “NO!... I mean, yes… Just…I’ll do it myself.” “But, Darling, AWC specifically assigned me to design all the gear for their official guys.” Homotailor eyed up the hulking ex-rugby player. “And how exactly do you plan on making your own gear, darling? Cue an interjection from Julie - “We have connections.” There was something particularly satisfying about saying those words. And with the coordination of a well-oiled machine, all seven TTSes poured out of the tailors, and into they’re chauffeur-driven Mercedes. A series of Julie-led phone calls later and the TTS Tag Team of John and Harry had themselves an original pair of wrestling shorts, made by exclusively by Next (a brand that’s a staple of the TTS lifestyle). The tailors had to guess the measurements and, as a result, they were slightly tight around there - but, in the words of Harry, “Anything’s better than having a queer run a tape measure up your inner thigh”. “What about having a queer run his tape measure up your inner thigh?” Chirped in John. In unison, the entire clan burst into the type of laughter that was only designed to draw attention. And as the laughter turned to awkward sniggers, they sipped on their decaff’ double-latte-chapfrapalatachino coffees and waited for the next joke to be told so the cycle can begin again. Because that was the T.T.S way.
