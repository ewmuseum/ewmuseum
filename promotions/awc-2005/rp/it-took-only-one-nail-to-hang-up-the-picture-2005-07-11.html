It Took Only One Nail To Hang Up The Picture...

11 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/11 Azagtoth: “99 boy-fucking priests on the wall…” The grating voice, hoarse yet wielding a hint of pleasure, pierces the otherwise silent darkness. Muffled thuds and yelps of pain emanate slowly from behind the door, barely audible whenever the singer takes a pause in their grotesque little rhyme. Azagtoth: “99 boy-fucking PRIESTS…” There is light in the room, but not much. Out of the three lamps, two have been knocked out. The third one is barely clinging to life, as if it was unsure whether or not it wants to witness what is happening. For the three people lying on the floor, their hands tied behind their backs and their mouths covered with rags, the light is too much. For the person singing while methodically swinging a baseball bat up and down on the prone bodies, it’s just enough. Azagtoth: “You take one down, cut his heart out…” The youngest man, the last in line, looks over at his comrades with terrified eyes. The droplets of cold sweat on his bald head reveal his fear to the assailant, which only makes him laugh and sing a little louder. The hog-tied youngster can only lay there and look at his friend and his lieutenant, lying in their own pools of blood, constantly coughing up more of the bright red that is quickly making the floor slippery. Every hit, every swing from the bat is accompanied by a cry of pain and a fresh batch of blood soaking through the rags in their mouths. Azagtoth: “98 boy-fucking priests on the wall!” He didn’t ask for this. He never knew a life outside of racism: he was raised on white supremacist propaganda. It was only natural for him to join the local chapter of KKK, and several other right-wing groups. All his friends did it. His family did it. The lieutenant assigned to him was actually his uncle: now his uncle had a cracked eye socket, the bleeding covering his left eye in a sheen of red that made his face look even more broken than it actually was. All they had done was beat up some weirdo in the abandoned part of the city… It’s not like the freaks had a group of their own. Or so they had thought. Until tonight. Azagtoth: “98 boy-fucking priests on the wall…” Oh God, he was starting it all over again. His uncle was done: he was out. His friend had pissed himself out of fear, and was crying openly as the black-clad psycho stepped onto his back and applied pressure to his neck with a black leather boot. The rag in his mouth was ripped in half: he had bit through it after a nasty shot to the spine. Underneath, the young skinhead could see his friend’s mouth filled with broken teeth and more blood. He was becoming numb at the sight of the red liquid, it seemed to be everywhere. Pretty soon, he realized, it would be on him too. Azagtoth: “98 boy-fucking PRIESTS…” The psycho laughs again, a morbid sort of laugh that is full of the chill of the grave and sharpened by genuine bloodlust. The young man had never seen or heard anything like this: sure, he had been a part of nigger mugging or two, but not like this. Not like this. Azagtoth: “You take one down, cut his heart out…” A sickening thud tells the remaining skinhead that something had broken in his friend’s head. Fresh red gushes to the back of the inanimate head. The bat has spatters on it now, gleaming droplets of crimson that had sprayed from the force of the blow. The psycho stands on the back of the unmoving body, like an executioner over a fresh kill, and then kneels down… Raising the bloody, limp head towards his face… The young man swears he can see a tongue darting out from between ink-black lips, a tongue redder than anything pouring out on the floor… And it was prodding, feeling, TASTING… Azagtoth: “97 boy-fucking priests on the wall!” And now the same psycho was on him, stepping in front of him, laughing and still singing that damn song. The young man looks into his boots, splashed with blood and hair from the floor. One of the boots prods him in the forehead, violently, and then a hand clamped down on the back of his neck. Colder than grave. And he can feel himself raised from the floor, with strength not belonging to the slender frame of the bat-wielder. And in moments, he is on his knees, and he can see into that face… And into those eyes. Azagtoth: “97 boy-fucking priests on the wall… Now, how many do you suppose I can take down before you pass out?” The face is painted black and white, like a mockery of a human corpse. Raven-black hair descends onto the paint like heavy curtains, framing and obscuring cruel, sharp features. The mouth is stained with blood, HUMAN blood, smeared over the lips and around the mouth like something resembling a child’s breakfast. Shuddering, the skinhead knows where the blood came from. And the eyes… They gleam with predatory lust, savage desire to feel the fresh kill. They are devoid of human emotions, something belonging to a perfect killing machine that has forfeited their humanity for the sake of all this… For the sake of a dark room, a nursery rhyme and three bodies to abuse. And this devil is sneering at him, laughing at him, taunting him. Calling him weak. Stupid. Cattle. The skinhead can feel a sharp jab in his ribs from the baseball bat, can feel the beginnings of a good-sized bruise, and yet he can’t help thinking that it was nothing compared to what was coming. Azagtoth: “You know… I have to tell you. You did a number on Trey. I admired your handiwork. Dragging someone chained to the back of a pickup for two miles? Classic. Southern style, I believe? You poor shmucks just missed one thing… You missed this on Trey.” The cackling psycho rips his black jacket open to reveal a bare, bony chest, pale beyond normal humanity. On the chest, covering the sternum, is a tattoo of a dark-clad figure from which emerge Famine, Pestilence, War and Death. A figure labeled “HATE”. The skinhead has seen a tattoo like this before. On the paint-faced freak behind the abandoned warehouse, just before he had stomped his steel-toed boots into the white face and chained him to the truck… The tattoo suddenly becomes the most burning thing in his mind. The speech of the psycho is barely audible to him. Azagtoth: “Well, nevermind. You happened to catch a lucky break, I tell you. We were out of town, can you imagine that? But you should have known something, you know. You never, ever harm the Cult of the Fifth Horseman. Even the lowliest initiate is considered protected. Even the lowliest worm that serves the Cult will be avenged. You just picked the wrong guy in the wrong place at the right time, no? To think that the Fifth Horseman himself would hear of this incident only after a couple of weeks… You probably thought you got away with it, didn’t you? Ha, that’s what I always hated about you Aryans. You never think about the consequences of your actions. You march blindly into war and then have your useless eyes picked out by the ravens of the One-Eyed God. If you have such a death wish, you merely have to ask. Have fun at the gallows, my ‘friend’… I hope you taste better than your friend.” He would like to explain, would like to offer an excuse, say that he was only following orders, but the psycho is already standing and lifts the baseball bat up, slowly. He grins again. And swings. It’s a damn good hit too. Probably would have been a homerun if he had been hitting a small leather ball. But he isn’t. The skinhead doesn’t really taste better than his friend. The leader of the skinhead group was currently two rooms down the corridor, bound to a chair not with rope but with a simple stare. For the past half an hour, the only thing he has been able to do is listen to the horrible sounds emanating from beyond the walls of the room, and stare at the silent figure watching him. The intruder had scarcely moved in the whole time, and yet the leader was more terrified than ever before. The man’s manners and posture were not threatening, but his eyes promised swift and brutal pain. And his appearance… With his head shaven and painted red, his trenchcoat screaming blasphemies in white on a black background, he wasn’t anything like the leader had ever seen. And those eyes were on him now, had been for way too long. They hadn’t said anything to him yet. They had just come to the apartment complex where the skinheads spent their days at and proceeded to tear everything apart, man and furniture alike. He could still smell the acrid smoke from the broken chairs and sofas these crazies had set ablaze. And what they had done to his subordinates was beyond description. They had tied them down, bound their hands and feet, made them helpless. And THEN they started the fight. They tortured and mutilated them. And the scariest one of them all, a thin man with a face of black and white, who was seemingly going crazy from bloodlust. Last time the leader had seen the crow-like freak was when he had dragged three of his most trusted soldiers into a room. He couldn’t hear anything from there anymore. The leader’s thought process is interrupted by a rap on the door. The bald, muscular figure slowly stands up from his chair and opens the door with one hand, still staring at the leader. The skinhead nearly jumps out of his skin as the same psycho he was just thinking about steps through the door, carrying with him a baseball bat and a hunting knife. Both covered in blood. The bald giant merely taps the psycho on the shoulder and he moves to the far wall, obedient yet eyeing the last skinhead with hungry eyes. The leader can’t take his eyes off. Black lips rounded off with deep red, a tongue licking the drying residues from his own face, the two weapons gleaming in the light of the room in a sinister way… Hate: “Do not mind Azagtoth here. He is merely a bit wound up from his duties tonight. Please, act like he wasn’t there AT ALL.” Like he wasn’t there at all!? The leader looks back at the speaker, his eyes wide with terror. How could he say that? He spoke as if nothing was out of the ordinary, as if the crazed, bloodied man in the corner was nothing more than a passing thing that shouldn’t be heeded. The redheaded man smiles wickedly and then walks over to the skinhead, who tries to press himself through the back of his chair. If he could disappear, he knows he would. Like a child, he now wishes he could just close his eyes so the boogeymen would go away, but these monsters are REAL. They are the real creepers that come out of the closets at night, and they really do want your blood… Whether you’ve been nice or naughty makes no difference. They are death personified. Hate: 'Now, shall we get down to business? Good man, why are you shaking so? I simply wish to talk and lighten my heavy burden with some answers from your part, if that is at all possible. There is no need to be so afraid... Nothing bad will happen to you. At least, not now.' Hate's voice is laced with malice and hints at sinister things, but the skinhead is already terrorized beyond such trivial things. He gulps loudly and grips the chair's arm rests, squeezing his knuckles white in an attempt to gather even a shred of courage to defend himself against this pure mental onslaught, but nothing comes to him. Gulping loudly, the skinhead can only gawk at Hate, who slowly shakes his head and draws his head back, looking down at the skinhead as if appraising a piece of meat. Hate: 'I must say... I am slightly disappointed. I must admit, I never expected anyone to have the cheek to attack the Cult. When I left for Japan to accomodate the request they placed for a skilled fighter, I could never have quessed that some sort of a commotion would break out in my absence. I guess Trey was merely unlucky, then. Out in the harbor, late at night... Who was to determine what kind of people he was to meet out there? Instead of whores, junkies or bums, he met you. And you decided to have some fun on this boy. While his masters were away, shipped beyond a great glimmering sea by a noble cause, you lowlifes took advantage of this poor boy and ravaged him like wolves. Did he offer any resistance? No, I doubt that he had the chance. He is still young, after all. His mind is ready but his body is not. And you beat his face open with tire irons and smashed his knees in with your little boots. So brave, so patriotic. Who did he remind you of? Polynesians? And then carving that little message on his back with a shiv... So classy, so classy.' Hate leans closer again, and the skinhead once again remains bolted to his chair, trying to maintain his composure without breaking down like a scared child in front of these two. That would mean that they had won. He refused to let these sickos have the pleasure. Hate: 'May I ask... Are you a religious man? Only, your message for me, and I do take that a message carved on the flesh of one of my followers is meant for me, was somehow religiously themed. How did it go again, Azagtoth? My memory seems to... Fail me.' Azagtoth stands in attention and then whips out his hunting knife, slamming it into the wall next to him. The blade penetrates two inches into the cheap wood, and the skinhead watches as the deadly weapon slowly vibrates on the wall, slowly swinging back and forth as Azagtoth speaks, his words full of anger. Azagtoth: 'They had written 'God Hates Fags' on Trey, my leader.' Hate: 'Ah, thank you, Azagtoth. 'God Hates Fags'? I hate to be petty, but are you implying something here? Are you making rather strong references at the sexual preferences of Trey, me and everyone who chooses to be like him or me? Not that I mind, as even the stupidest cretin is given the benefit of an opinion. But merely suggesting that Trey was different based on the way he dressed and invoked my name while being trampled under your boots does not mean that he has warranted any sort of divine retribution or judgement, and from a racist God none the less. But it is not your fault. After all, how can I blame you when you have clearly been brainwashed by age-old doctrines full of lies and deceit? I cannot place the weight of the quilt emanating from centuries of shit onto your fragile mind.' Hate draws back and walks over to the other side of the room, to whisper to Azagtoth. The skinhead now breathes more easily as the space around him clears. Maybe he was going to survive. Maybe they wouldn't kill him. The leader realized how incredibly naive his thoughts were, but he still clinged to the slim shard of hope now offered to him. As the skinhead goes to stand up, however, Hate speaks again, freezing the man in his spot. Hate: 'However, the fact is that you and your group transgressed against the Cult of the Fifth Horseman. I am Hate: I am the epitome of revenge and retribution, the primal urge that allows every single act of payback to be made. And as such, I cannot let this slide. Azagtoth has already meted out a punishment for your troops, and executed it without much haste. Now all that remains from your little troupe is you and your punishment. It shall be quick, but I'm afraid not altogether painless.' The man nearly jumps out of his skin. A cold wave of dread washes over him and he turns to Hate, his face a mask of fear. Not now! Not now! He had been so close to salvation, so close to escaping without harm, and now the tables had been turned on him. Shivering visibly, the man tries to plead Hate with his eyes, but meets only a cold, calculating machine staring at him in return. Sinking into those dead, emotionless eyes, into the oblivion behind them... Suddenly the skinhead snaps and lunges at Hate, swinging wildly. Hate stops him by grabbing his wrist and quickly and effectively forcing him around. His shoulder snaps instantly, and Hate breaks his wrist as well just for fun. The skinhead cries out in pain and thrashes, his useless limb held in an iron grip by Hate who fishes out something from his pocket before leaning closer to the skinhead and whispering into his ear. Hate: 'You see, I am a religious man as well, as well as a philosophical one. Have you ever heard this one? What is the difference between Jesus Christ and a picture of Jesus Christ?' The skinhead is nearly out from pain, but from the corner of his eye he can see Azagtoth advancing with a wicked smile and some unidentifiable objects. As Hate's voice softens, the skinhead is aware of something being slowly slid over his head, muffling his sights and sounds. Hate: 'It only took one nail to hang up the picture.' Too slow and too late the skinhead realizes the plastic bag around his head. As Hate pulls the bag tight, the skinhead can see through it to Azagtoth brandishing a hammer and two nine inch nails. The weakened body tries to trash, but Hate's grip deprives it of oxygen too fast. Soon, it is limp on the floor, then lifted on the wall, arms spread, head leaning on the chest... The hammering echoes for a while. The next person in the room is in for a surprise. Cleanse the Impure. Raise the Warcry.
