Journal Entry 1: The Mission Begins

9 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 9th, 2005 The following is an excerpt from the journal of Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon... also known as The Captain. 7th December 2005 Last night, the mission was begun anew. In front of a crowd of more than ten-thousand English mouth-breathers, I let the Atlantic Wrestling Club, and thusly the world, know of my intentions to show Ottoman dominance in this day and age of Western decadence and complacency. Not much to my surprise though, I was met with jeers. I was met with trash flung forth into the ring. I was met with hatred and ignorance. They looked at me with scorn through the barbed-wire fence that caged them in. Even those of my brethern, the Turkish and the Arabs and the Pakistani and the Iranians, they scorned me with their timid silence. They have been scared into silence by the arrogant English who have stripped them of their heritage. Yet for that, I do not blame the English, or the Americans. I blame them for letting their heritage be stripped from them. Their cowardice is second only to that of these Western 'powers.' For a culture that claims to teach tolerance through empty gestures like banning piggy banks across all financial institutions so as not to offend Muslims, they surely do not practice it. Why, the other day, I was boarding a bus in Birmingham, and I was accosted by two constables, who said they were doing a random safety check. Random my left buttock. They profiled me, if for only the color of my skin. Oh, how I desired to slam the butt of a rifle between both of their eyes right at that moment. Yet, those desires, if acted upon, are the ones that stereotype myself and my brethern as uncivilized. If they only knew, if they only studied the cultural advancements under the Sultans. They would not be so quick to call me a 'camel jockey' or a 'raghead' or a 'terrorist.' If they only knew that I do not need to act as those such as Osama bin Laden. Much like my forebears, my wish is to fight head-on, to show the world how flabby the Englishman is, and how lazy the American is. I see this Christmas party scheduled to take place in Amsterdam, and it turns my stomach, to lie down in such a den of filth and degeneration. I have been in this company for less than a week, and it already turns my stomach. But this is what I had expected, or else I would not have signed the contract that Mr. Harber had put in front of me. And I expect it to be a long, arduous journey. Not because I expect each individual test to be tough. No, because these are the flabby and weak men and women... women! I still cannot get over how they expect women to compete with me! But when looking upon the size of the roster, the sheer amount of fat I have to clear away to prove my dominance, it will be taxing and tiring. Heavy lifting takes a toll, even if it is easily lifted. Take for example my first match. I looked at the booking sheet and saw that I was signed to not one, but two opponents. One is Sage, a typical American jumping bean, someone who cares more about flash than punishing his opponents. The other, Damien D'Angelo, I have not been able to find a lick of information about. This shall be a great test, if only because I must watch out for treachery. No doubt they will try to double up on me, and sneak me from behind, to take out the brown threat, the one who represents the enemy to them. Yet I am stronger than that. And no matter what depths of treachery, by Allah, I shall make examples of them. I shall treat them as the constables who wronged me. I am Captain Suleimon. This is my mission. And it is only at its beginning.
