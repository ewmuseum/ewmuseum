The Death Of A Dream: Chapter Three - Longing For Redemption

30 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June 2005 !!!PLEASE NOTE - THIS RP IS NOT LATE, IT WAS SUBMITTED TO MANAGEMENT FOR MODERATION!!! !!!THANKS AND ENJOY!!! !!!KIT!!! Alexi 'I don't know about you, Kei…but this shit makes me real nervous,' I said, throwing a copy of today's New York Post down on the tasteful mahogany coffee table. The old man cast a casual eye over the front page story – 'Blaze At Home of Professional Wrestler Kills Two – Police Suspect Arson.' 'I wouldn't pay it too much heed, Kalashnikov – these things have a habit of blowing over.' 'BLOWING OVER? WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU MEAN? HE TORCHED HIS FREAKING HOUSE – THE HOME HE GREW UP IN!' Stone-faced, Kitsune stared back at me. I could sense the rage brimming inside him. 'Alexi, you seem to have forgotten exactly who I am. For starters, we do not know for certain that he was involved…' 'Oh please, spare me…' 'NO. The time has come for you to stop talking and start listening. As I was saying, firstly we do not know for certain that he was involved. For all we know it could just as well have been Eiji.' 'I thought you had him on a tight reign.' 'I do, but he's his own man. He's consumed by the hate I planted inside him. He's unpredictable.' 'He's also in Tokyo.' 'Yes…and you and I are the only two people who know this.' He lit a slender cigar before continuing. 'Secondly, if he was involved – IF he was involved – I have friends within the department. I can make the problem go away.' 'Okay. Fine. But we both know that's not the issue here. Sure, he could go to jail – boo hoo, we lose a bit of cash, I mean all we do is find another sucker to utilize. The predicament as I see it is…well…if he can do that, who's to say what he's capable of? Things are getting awfully sketchy real quick, Kitsune, and I don't like it. If he finds out what’s really happening he'll kill us both.' 'And what's the likelihood of that coming to pass? The thing with Pact is, whilst he is undeniably a fantastic in-ring competitor and a cunning and ruthless human being…he's not the sharpest knife in the drawer. Do you really think that my re-emergence into his life would've come as such as shock to him if he was?' 'Well…' 'Exactly. Lex. Trust me; you've got nothing to fear. Everything is under control. Pact's our meal ticket, he's our path to fame and fortune. Let him believe what he likes about me – I'm not here to persecute and subjugate him. I'm here, as you well know, to exploit him for every cent he's worth. And so long as you,' he said, pointing at me, 'don't lose your nerve, everything will be fine.' I cast my eyes downwards. 'Man…this isn't right. I mean, sure, I want to turn a profit – every guy in the business does – but he's a friend.' 'Friends mean nothing anymore. This is your friend,' he said, holding up Pact's paypacket for the week. 'This is your purpose, your raison d'etre. Any friendship you had with Pact died the moment you called me. You accepted that as fact from the very beginning. Don't back out on me now.' 'No…sir…I won't.' Pact 'EXXXXXXXXXPUUUUUUUUROOOOOOOOODAAAAA SUUUUUUUUUUUUPUUUUURRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEXXXXXXXXXAAAAAAAAAAAAA!' 'Yeah, man! Get him!' Waiting in Pearl's office had proven to be a boring business. To relieve the monotony I had a browse though his library of old puroresu tapes and came across a NOAH show I appeared on a coupla years back. Feeling the warmth of nostalgia rise inside me, I put it in the tape player and kicked back in his leather La-Z-Boy, waiting for the other participants in the Solar Power match to come and plead their cases for positions. The match featured a younger, more healthy me taking on Jun Akiyama in a straight wrestling match – a rarity for me (at least in Japan – I was considered to be a deathmatch wrestler, albeit more solidly grounded that Atushi Onita or Mr. Pogo). I watched the match enter its closing stages – I picked Akiyama up across my shoulders and nailed a picture perfect Embrace the Darkness ('EMBURRRRRRAAAAAAASSSSSSSS SA DAKNEEEEEEEEEEESSS!'). Here is your winner and still NOAH Heavyweight Champion. I smiled as the tape played my celebration with the adoring Japanese fans. When did I change? When did it all go wrong? How did I get from that to this in just two short years? Has my age really made me so cynical that I can no longer believe in the faith of my fans? I was approaching the end of my career. In fact, if I had any say in it my career would already be over. I knew that the level I was competing at was somewhat below my usual standard, and the thought of it killed me. The thought that I was going out to these indy shows, busting my gut, risking paralysis, stingers and worse and STILL not being as good as I used to be was discouraging to say the least. My record in AWC proves I'm not the force I once was. And I guess it's not that I've actually got that much worse – it's just everyone around me is getting continually better. I'm a dinosaur of a dead age of wrestling and as such I've no right to be challenging Crimson O'Malec for his Championship. Guys like him are the future of this business – not me. Despite my frustration with Keitaro Kitsune's involvement in my current situation I'd accepted it as yet another inescapable evil. The man was destined, from the moment he saw me, to be a spectre casting a shadow across my life. His reappearance after all these years proves it. I've no say in the matter. I've just gotta get on with it as best I can. Maybe I can even benefit from it in some small way. I was snapped away from my thoughts by the ringing of my cellphone. 'Hello?' I said, flipping it open. 'Hey, honey – how's it going?' I sighed deliciously. 'Slowly, darlin' – looks like I'm gonna be here for a while. I'm sorry.' 'Don't apologise, it's not your fault!' she replied, cheerily. 'Just hurry back to me, Bear.' 'I will. I love you.' 'I love you too.' She rang off. God…despite everything that's going wrong, at least I've still got something good in my life. Something to look forward to when the time comes and I finally hang up my boots. Something pure and good. I love her so much…I know that so long as I have her, everything will be fine. I closed my eyes and let the thought wash over me as I heard a knock at the door. 'Enter.' It swung open and Pearl strode in. 'Pact, how's it going?' 'Terrible, man – I've had one guy in here all day.' 'Ahh, there's been problems all over the city. Heatwave's caused a riot a few blocks down, it's total gridlock all the way to 5th Ave.' 'No wonder I haven't seen anyone…damn.' 'Hey, it's no big. Just give me the order now.' 'Sorry, big guy,' I said, grabbing my jacket. 'No can do. I've not decided yet.' Pearl looked at me with consternation. 'Then what've you been doing here all day? Watching videotapes of yourself or something?' I laughed sheepishly. 'No…uh…no.' He rolled his eyes. 'Just get it to me as soon as you can, right?' 'Sure man, no problem.' With that, I exited the office and made my way back to the Four Seasons. --- 'Honey, I'm home!' No answer. 'Honey? You here?' I entered the bedroom and found a note on my pillow. 'Gone to Saks with Summer. Back soon. XXX.' I lay down on the bed and let out a sigh. Women…
