Looking Forward

17 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/6/05 I just wanna fly… 1. U-Turn Dare aye say it but I think…aye’ve made it… Even in his mind, Paddy can’t stop himself from sounding so smug. It’s been less than forty-eight hours since Emerald Isle had defeated Krimzon Karnage Inc. at Fresh! when the scene opens in a small park over looking a pond. In this confused new world to which Paddy has subjected himself, this is his refuge, he knew that after the first time he clapped eyes on it upon a stroll in his new surroundings. The sky is cloudless and enriched by a sun that is currently half eclipsed by the skyscraper that is the building of AWC offices. He’s just emerged from talks to finalise terms of his contract and everything went as smooth as could be hoped. To Paddy, the sky has never looked so blue – the blue itself seems to be emitting waves of colour in Paddy’s eyes especially in the light of the sun’s temporary disappearance. The pond is clear and clean… Jus’ like back home… It seems that Paddy was meant to be at this pond today because the day’s gayness is a perfect metaphor for his mood - life has turned a corner and is heading just the way he always wanted it. Now all he has to do is sit back and enjoy the ride. I mean, what more could a wrestler want? All his confidence had been kicked out of him (literally) upon his departure from PCW those three years ago. His recuperation healed him physically while at the same time his mentality was submerged in the netherealm of desperation. He had made as good a start as any back then, better infact as he thinks about his immediate title success…but then everything went pear shaped. He broke his foot and was kicked out when it seemed he had ripped tendons as well. PCW had cut their losses and deserted him, spat at him, made him feel like a goddamn fool for ever trying. And he wasn’t a fool. He was no bodies fucking fool. He didn’t have the brain’s that can only be acquired through formal schooling but he was as sharp as a knife and had enough life experience to make his head burst if it was a physical form. This knowledge were more essential than any of course. And now, after all that self-loathing and self-doubt, he realises what a waste it has been. But not in a negative way. It should be negative but he’s too he’s too happy now that he’s overcome a mountain; happy that he has the future he had always wanted. He is going somewhere, not stagnant like the pitiful rotation of work that was going on in offices all around him at this moment. So now only the heavens can restrict his shoot to stardom and even then, he’s sure he can overcome that too when he gets there… Paddy himself is wearing a pair of three quarter length khaki combats. They’re loose and were worn to make sure his crotch was well aerated. That’s good because the day is quite hot although he has the relief of a light cool breeze sweeping his windswept face. He’s wearing a dirty old white tank top that he just about doesn’t fill out but at least its sleevelessness is another counterattack to the sun’s heat He’s sitting on a bench at the minute, watching life go by…people hurrying to and fro, here and there. Always panting and always in a rush. Paddy smiles at this…what idiots; don’t they know life is for living? One man walks past and actually throws him a dirty look. He must be either reproachful of either his dirty vest or perhaps the fact he’s at least two days unshaven. Or maybe it’s the fact he’s wearing sandals that are usually accustomed to a day at the beach. Much to the man’s surprise, the man can only smile and he walks on flustered - a $200 suit freshly pressed and neatly parted blond hair wondering how demented that man actually was. But who cares? Paddy didn’t need that asshole or any of the others who had so kindly went to the trouble to treat him with contempt that day? Who the hell needed them, after all, they would be gone in this busy city of New York within seconds, never to be seen again by Paddy in this urban jungle where skyscrapers were the trees and the men with the suits were the animals. Any he had a rock now…Crimson O’Malec 2. Thinking Back It had been one day after Paddy had agreed terms with Mr. Pearl over the phone on his arrangement with AWC (Mr. Pearl had insisted upon being called Pearl but force of habit from Paddy meant this request would likely never be fulfilled) that he had flew out for the US basking in the thoughts of what lay ahead. But he was worried. When a child enters school for the first time, they are filled with a fear of abandonment. Generally their mother/father/guardian has been with them their whole lives. Standing behind them so they don’t fall over, feeding them so they won’t put the bowl of food on their head. To a child, their parent(s) are their rock and a foundation on which they can build their lives. The fear of abandonment is unlike no other at that age because without a solid foundation the house will fall without doubt. Although a child will never delve into these complexities at that age, their instinct tells them that it is infact true. But this fear is not restricted to the first day at school or to a child. To Paddy, the fear of being abandoned by the company when he was over in the US had been niggling at him from the back of his mind for a while now. It had happened before after all. He feared being just like the people he now sees before him. People who are slaves to their work who have nothing to depend on in their silly rotation of the day. He needed someone to help him in this crazy new world to get his feet Paddy’s fear was quickly calmed when he met Crimson O’Malec. He could tell from square one that O’Malec was a rock Paddy had been told by Pearl to lay low for a few weeks because the AWC booking team already had plans and that he would be amalgamated some time after the PPV. Sitting in a bar local to where Fresh! was going on on Tuesday, Paddy was watching the action unfold on a television erected on a wall. The bar was nice Paddy remembers…a whole light-year from what Hugh’s was…A nice cosy atmosphere that can be associated with the colour of light orange. He was sipping at a pint of Guinness at the time. And of course being ignored by all including the barman who looked at him like something that had crawled back up the toilet for revenge. He was Irish AND a gypsy. What more could he expect? But then Crimson came in. No contempt, no dirty look. He was as downtrodden as Paddy was when he sat on the barstool next to him he remembers and he forced a smile. Hello, are you enjoying the action he asked as he recalls. “Aye am thanks, aye be joinin’ up with them in a few weeks, mister…?” “O’Malec…But just call me Crimson” And he had extended his hand to be shaken, despite the fact that he had obviously acquired a waft of O’Sheas special brew by now. And still no contempt. “Aye’m Paddy O’Shea…uhhh…Just Paddy.” He wasn’t exactly a master in social interaction and Paddy would admit that himself. “So O’Malec. Irish that be. Aye be right yes?” “That you are Paddy. My parents are Irish but I’ve been raised over here. But don’t worry Paddy, I know where I come through” Crimson clapped Paddy on the back. Paddy hadn’t changed since he’d arrived from Ireland and he cringed as he felt the sweat push against his skin. If the remnants of Paddy bodily fluids sitting on Crimson’s hand didn’t scare him away… But it didn’t and he just smiled “Don’t worry. I’m sure you know I can work up a sweat too. What did you think of my match last week when I won this” And Crimson had revealed his relentless belt that had been hidden under his shirt. Paddy’s eyes had lit up. So pretty… “What be ye meaning Mr. Crimson?” “You know, when I won that three way. And call me Crimson just I told you” “Oh sorry. What be a three way? Be ye some kind o’ sex freak or something?” Crimson had laughed at what he had perceived as a joke but when he seen the dead seriousness in Paddy’s eyes, he had straightened up and cleared his throat “*Ahem*…Well I…I won it at last week’s Fresh!…I’m a wrestler there, didn’t you know?” “Well naw…. aye mean, aye’ve only been in the country since yesterday.” “Well…I am *laughs* and a three way is when three people fight at once, it’s not some kind of sex orgy” They both erupted at that. Paddy didn’t mind being laughed at at that moment in time. Someone was actually being a nice guy for once “I’ll tell you what Paddy, since I’ve got a soft spot for the people back in the country, I’ll teach you the ropes before you debut. A couple of weeks you said yes? Don’t worry; you’ll be ready to be world champ by then. I’m just surprised you know so little about wrestling seeing as you were in PCW. Or so I heard.” “Yeah but aye never heard of a ‘three way’ before. Aye don’t even like wrestlin’ fer fecks sake” Crimson had laughed long at this. They had talked for a little while longer, mostly on trivial things but everything seemed so important to Paddy. A while later, Crimson started to get up. “I’ll see you backstage then next week. Nice meeting you” Paddy got up too and shook his hand almost aggressively “And ye too. Thanks fer everything Mr. Crimson” “Paddy, you better learn to lose that Mister thing if you’re going to get anywhere with me” Crimson joked as he began to walk away “Ok…Crimson” Paddy finished just as Crimson walked out the door And he didn’ look at me funny like the rest o’ them It wasn’t thirty minutes later when Crimson came running in again much to the surprise of the few stragglers now left in the bar. He was pulling him. Infact he had pulled him halfways up the street before Crimson answered Paddy’s shouts of what’s going on? And then Crimson had set Paddy’s torch alight, gave him that break into the AWC he might not have achieved in few weeks. Gave him a match, a partner in Crimson, one half of a team, Emerald Isle was the name they quickly decided. And what a team they were. Krimzon Karnage Inc. were no easy shakes but they had beaten them with Paddy finishing off. The rest they say is history… …which brings us back… 3. Looking Ahead …here. The day is beginning to end now at this little park where Paddy sits which is being supervised by the various towers swirling around his head. The sun has returned from behind the building but is now beginning to disappear into the brick and mortar horizon of the city. Paddy has been sitting here for at least two hours, just reflecting… The suns intensity has increased because as it’s squeezed between the sky and land stopping Paddy from lifting his eyes from the pond which has been stained a dazzling range of colours from it’s rays. The wind is stirring up mini waves that only increase the colours complexity by shattering the picture, which reminds Paddy of the workings of a kaleidoscope. Constantly changing, which is of course the only constant they say. Now as Paddy looks at all the opportunities ahead of Emerald Isle he smiles. Booked right up to the PPV, Emerald Isle has every chance to reinstate its claim for long-term victory with the alliance titles in mind. And my haven’t things changed. He’d do anything he thinks just to rub it into the cynics back home in Athenry who singled out a gypsy as their focus of discrimination. A gypsy would earn more this year than they had ever earned. Become more famous in four days he’d been here than they could muster up in their whole lives On the twenty first of June, Emerald Isle will face The Annoying Neighbours as part of the Alliance League proceedings. And in a main event. The mere prospect excites Paddy to the extent that hot flushes and cold shivers race up and down his body in parallel directions. They are the two teams at the top of the Alliance League table. Both achieved the wins they set out for on the 14th, which guarantees an interesting battle. Especially because of the fact that they will be wrestling a duo of aspiring carrots Paddy snickers in what sounds like a cartoon crooks laugh. The sound of it makes him laugh more until his hat begins to slip down his face. But that’s only the charm of them isn’t it? Just a duo having a laugh and that’s how Paddy feels. He feels like laughing, he doesn’t know if he can ever get rid of this smile that seems to have been carved in his face. All he has to do now is make contact with the family at home which will be more difficult than it seems because of a lack of a phone line but he’ll come up with something…maybe even bring them over, who knows? That’s the beautiful thing about the future for him; he can do anything and fly. He and Crimson can fly. Roll on the future END View Emerald Isle 's Biography
