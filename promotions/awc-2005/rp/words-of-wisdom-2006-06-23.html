Words of Wisdom

23 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/23/2006 The screen is black. The sound of a door opening can be heard. The sound of this opening door seems to echo the expanse of what must be a large room. There is a moment of silence. The sound of the door closing is heard followed by soft footsteps moving across the floor. The voice of an old man is now heard of the sound of the echoing footsteps. 'Vince, you don't seem to be too motivated today. Why are you so down on yourself right now? What's wrong?' The camera slowly fades into an old rundown gym where a slightly younger Vince Jones can be seen standing alone in a ring. He is seen leaning against the turnbuckle in the corner with his back turned to the source of the voice. The old man approaches the ring and just watches Vince who is just sulking to himself. Old man: Vince, what's the matter? Vince just shrugs his shoulders and lets out a sigh. Vince Jones: Just got some shit weighin' down the mind and all, man. That's all. Old man: Just some shit? Is it really just some shit or is it more than that? Vince Jones: Nah, just some shit, man. Vince slowly turns around and he appears to have a tear rolling down his cheeks. He quickly wipes away the tear trying to hide it from the old man's view. Old man: There's no need to hide your tears, Vince. Vince Jones: Tears? Fuck that! (shakes his head) V don't shed tears for nothin'! That's the way it is, old man! V like a rock, cold and hard! Old man: You don't have to act all big and bad around me. What is it that they teach you kids these days? What? Just because you're a man you're not allowed to cry? Vince Jones: Men don't cry! Old man: Men do cry, Vince. And it takes a real man to admit that. There is nothing wrong with tears. Tears are the soul's way of cleansing itself sometimes. Tears wash away pain. Tears make things better sometimes. So, what is it on your mind. You've obviously got something that's eating away at you. Go ahead and share. I'm all ears, Vince. Vince Jones: There ain't nothin' to share, old man! Old man: Well, if there's nothing to share then I guess you'll be going back to your training for today. Am I right? Vince just stands there and looks back at the old man in silence. The old man turns his back on Vince. Old man: Good day, Vince. He begins to walk away. Vince Jones: (reaching out a hand) Wait... The old man stops in his tracks and turns back towards Vince. Old man: Yes? Vince Jones: Aiight, there are a few things on my mind right now, old man. The old man makes his way back to the ring and sits on the apron. Old man: Alright. Well, tell me what's on your mind then. He pats a spot beside him on the apron and motions for Vince to join him. Vince obeys and slowly makes his way out of the ring and sits right down next to him. Vince Jones: Man, its about my past... Old man: (nods) I see. And what is it about your past that's got you so down on yourself, Vince? Vince Jones lowers his head in disappointment. Vince Jones: Everything... Old man: Everything, as in? Vince Jones: Man, sometimes V be wishin' things could've been different. Like why'd V have to come up with next to nothin', huh? What's up with that shit? And why'd his pops have to walk out on my moms and the rest of us and shit? Why'd V have to scrap for every damn thing he had to get in life while other cats livin' large from the get go, huh? Why V here in the NYUFC still scrappin' and scrapin' to get by? It ain't fair! Fuck! All this shit ain't even fair, man! Sometimes V be wishin' that it was all different! V be wishin' that he could get that easy ride through life and all, man! Old man: But would you really be happy, Vince? Vince Jones: (in confusion) Huh? What you talkin' 'bout? Old man: Its just what I said, Vince. Would you really be happy just coasting? Would you really be happy if everything you've ever got in life was just handed to you? For some reason, I don't think you would. Vince Jones: Well... Old man: Vince, do you know how many people are out there in this world who just live and never face adversity like you have had to face? Vince Jones: Nah... Old man: Probably millions. And do you know what they spend most of their time doing while they're living 'carefree', Vince? They're wishing that they could live lives like you and me. They wish for the day that they're met with even half of the situations that guys like you and me have had to go through. Are their lives happy? No, their lives are idle. Their lives are in neutral. They don't move forward or backward. They are stuck forever in the middle. And that's why we're fighters, Vince... Vince looks up at the old man in confusion. Vince Jones: V ain't followin' you, old man. The old man lays a hand on Vince's shoulder. Old man: Vince, don't you see? Everything you've been through in life makes you who you are today? Do you think you could ever be half the fighter you are today if it wasn't for your past, Vince? No! People like you and me are cut out to be fighters because its guys like you and me that have seen the worst and still manage to get by. We make a way for ourselves to thrive out of nothing, Vince. You're not always gonna be on the dominating end of a fight. You know that yourself. There are times when things get rough and it looks as if there is no hope, no chance. Do you end up giving in? No, you press on and you look for a way to scrap your way to victory. That's the way of the fighter, the lessons you learn from the streets, Vince... The camera slowly fades out and fades into the present where Vince Jones is seen sitting in a locker room with his head bowed and a black towel over his head. The words of wisdom of the old man continue to ring through his mind as he he sits there on the bench in utter silence. '...So, don't be ashamed of who you are, Vince. The life you lived shaped and prepared you for the challenges ahead. The man you are as a result of your experiences is what allows you to thrive. Fighters fight and fighters win; but, even in failing a fighter lives to fight another day, another battle, never losing that will to carry on.' Vince remains in silence for a few moments. He then removes the towel from his head and looks up towards the sky. Vince Jones: Old man, you always knew more than V. Jones could ever imagine 'bout the way things go in this game, in life, in the world. Tears begin to form in Vince's eyes and he wipes them away. Vince Jones: And that's why V. Jones sometimes wishes you were still around cuz everything would be simple, a whole lot simpler. If only you could be there for V's matches like you used to do back in the day when everything made sense. (shakes his head) Shit can neva be that simple for V though. That's the fucked up shit 'bout life. V just wishes shit was simple again for him... Vince lowers his head for a moment and then slowly raises it and looks upward. Vince Jones: ...but that's not the way you'd want things for V, old man. Guess that ain't the way V. Jones'd want things for himself either. (sighs) Sunday night... The camera slowly fades to black as Vince bows his head once again in silence.
