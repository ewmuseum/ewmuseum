Maggotman: The Sudden Finale

17 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/1/06 This is the end for a single person. One man or woman will not walk home from this. If you are familiar with the Maggotman, good for you, though how you are familiar with that person remains a mystery. Not much has been revealed: indeed, this might seem like out-of-the-blue for things to happen like this. But life is not always endless lines of cause-and-effect. No matter what the mathematicians say, there is always a random integer, something you cannot measure or predict. That something is just now holding a young, 16-year-old girl by the throat and threatening to slash her face off with a hunting knife. --- Despite being in a completely submissive position, Angelica found herself surprisingly calm. Oh, on the outside, she was shaking and crying. But inside, well, it wasn’t that bad. Sure, she had Azagtoth’s painted face inches away from hers, with a ghoulish hand grasping a knife held point-first at her throat. Her eyes, however, were working on their own, moving from one end of the room as if in a haze. Her field of vision was blurred in other parts, sharp in the others: where the room was just mess and junk, she couldn’t recognize an armchair from a broken table. But the features she could see seemed to be glowing with something not of this world: Hate, standing in silence, his arms raised in front of him not to protect but to confront. The massive frame, clad in the leather jacket, the head still showing taint of red paint. How many times had she spread her legs in front of that figure? Every moment, even ones she thought had only been figments of her own imagination, seeped into her head. Funny how you could remember something like that, she thought idly as Azagtoth pressed the knife even closer, barking something at Hate over his shoulder. Death felt so slow. Azagtoth’s face filled half of his world: the sharp, bony features of the boy’s face were only accentuated further by the mix of white and black paint he wore. Right now, the lines and etchings on his face seemed to have a life of their own, twisting and contorting as Azagtoth spoke, one fear-filled word after another. The raven-black hair that flowed from his head likewise seemed to be alive, darting this way and that, at one moment obscuring the face and in the next intant framing it in abyss-like darkness. Azagtoth’s eyes postively jittered. They flew here and there, never staying in the exact same spot for longer than a second, constantly moving onwards to the next point. Angelica thought that she might have been disturbed by all of it, if she hadn’t made peace with her mind moments ago. So ever-increasingly slow. Her head snapped as Azagtoth grabbed by her torn shirt and pulled her closer, placing the edge of his weapon parallel to her throat and screaming… --- Azagtoth: ”All for you, my leader! Everything I do! I tell you what lies in my soul and my mind, I indulge you with every observation and bit of rumor I hear. I open to you like I would not even open up for the great One-Eyed One! And, my leader, you reward me by replacing me! All because of this bitch with an extra hole for you to fuck! Fuck like you have fucked the Cult and fuck like you have fucked me, my leader! My LORD!” Despite being in complete control of the situation, Azagtoth was far from the stone-cold killer he so desperately wanted to be. He was more akin to a mental patient slashing around with a pair of scissors. Except he didn’t have a pair of scissors: he had a sharp hunting knife, tested and found good. As far as mental patients went… Most of them sat in a corner and mumbled to themselves. Azagtoth mumbled to himself when he was beating on other people or gathering ’trophies’. But now he didn’t have time for mumbling. His hands shook, with anger and fury and fear, as he held the Whore of Babylon against the wall behind her and stared over his shoulder at the man he had followed and worshipped ever since he had been picked out of the gutters. The pit of his stomach felt so tight it was about to burst. The cramps correlated with the movements of his hands, the blade of the knife jumping next to the white-skinned throat of that little concubine. Azagtoth had never been afraid, not once in his life. Yet now, Death felt like the most terrifying thing ever. In his mind, he stared at Hate, straight on, not straying away even for a second. But at this point, his mind was lying more than providing accurate information. He looked at Hate, and at the same time looked all over the room. His paranoid mind was filled with figures lurking in the shadows and reaching from around Hate, trying to grab him. Even the beaten bitch wouldn’t stay down, but rather moved and flared like a misshapen human form rather than a living female being. Hate, his leader, stood in silence at Azagtoth’s outburst: but the muscles tightening in his arms and neck told Azagtoth things words could never convey. They promised an end, one way or another. Deep inside Azagtoth knew he didn’t have a hostage, nor did he hold any kind of leverage. He held nothing except a knife, and even that would be insufficient against The Fifth Horseman. Suddenly, Death was closer than ever. --- They had made it back to Philadelphia before the next date Hate was set to wrestle on. The New Year’s had passed: no celebration touched the Cult. It was uncertain when their leader was going to cherish them with his presence once again: most of them hadn’t seen Hate for months. Rumors and mistalk spread like wildfires. One person head this, the next one heard that. Hate had been shot. The police had got him. They were going to be shut down. Azagtoth had taken over, though these rumors were usually quietly and quickly dealt with by Azagtoth himself. None of them knew the truth. The truth was that Hate had spent most of his days in a heroin-induced haze, lying on this or that floor, his eyes the size of quarters, veins pulsating on his forehead and mouth gibbering silent words which no known language could ever dream of conceiving. Watched over by Angelica and Azagtoth, two silent figures of different spectrums. A white and a dark angel, still kids who had been dragged into things far greater than their understanding and both unable to grasp that they were in way over their heads. When Hate was out, they did not talk: they stood or sat on the opposite ends of the room, positioned exactly so that they were facing each other, staring at the unmoving body of their leader and hoping to be the first one he saw when he finally woke up. But when Hate woke up, he did not look at either one of them. He breathed deeply, spat vomit on the floor and got up, hung over from the drug dosage. Then things continued like nothing had happened at all. But rumors still flew. And rumors spread uncertainty, uncertainty bred anger, anger bred a wish for change. And a wish for change forced people to decide. Most decided to leave. A few decided to stay. Azagtoth decided to act. So it came to pass that the night Azagtoth finally put his plan into motion and took Angelica hostage at knife-point once more was also the same night detective Jonathan Reeves commanded a group of police officers and DEA agents to stage a perimeter around the abandoned hotel serving as the lair for the Cult of the Fith Horseman. --- Bach: ”Come on, Joe, this is ridiculous. This is DEA business, we have no need to be here. Especially in this capacity.” Jonathan ”Joe” Reeves didn’t say a thing. He secured the last strap of his bulletproof vest and then secured a pair of protective goggles on his head. His expression was gloomy and unforgiving, and detective Bach knew that there was nothing he could do. There hadn’t been anything he could have done to help the situation in the months preceding this. All he could do was watch as the whole case slid even deeper and deeper into a one-sided war between his friend Joe and the bald cultist who had become Reeves’ obsession. And for what? For a teenage daughter running away? Teens did that all the time. Bach knew it perfectly well: he had fathered three of such menaces. Little snot-nosed punks with too much metal on their face and a head full of ideas so stupid that in Bach’s youth they had been beaten out of his head with a stick. It was all well and normal, every teenager did that stuff. But Reeves had taken it personal… No, way more than personal. This was a vendetta. Bach: ”You’re really going to get him, aren’t you? You really are. I can’t believe you, Joe. This isn’t anything like you.” Reeves’ sharp turn was only made more surprising by his angry voice, launching at Bach with fury. Reeves: ”Cut the hippie crap, Bach. This is a standard operation, and I am eglibed to join. So you just stand back if you don’t want to come with us.” Bach shook his head and fished his pockets for the cigarrettes he knew he had put there, but for some reason couldn’t find right now. Damn Reeves and his personal revenge. He was going to raid the hotel with the narcotics people, and that was fine, except that he wasn’t there because of the kids doing drugs. That could have been done by an entirely different branch of their department altogether. He was here because of that guy, what’s he called… Hate. The anger Reeves felt towards Hate made even Bach shiver. He had often talked to criminals and taken them down: and he knew that the way Reeves talked now was very much the same tone with a man about to rob a bank. Just with more violence to be involved. Bach: ”Just don’t do anything rash, Joe. You know I don’t want to take the message to that fugly wife of yours.” Reeves smiled, out of protocol. It was a cold, dead smile, devoid of emotion. Bach sighed and walked back to his car as Reeves loaded the magazine into his standard-issue sidearm and confered briefly with the DEA officer next to him. Reeves: ”So, we approach slowly, then announce we’re here and make those bastards…” --- Pay. Azagtoth knew that he was going to pay for this, and pay dearly too. There was no way he could survive. He looked at Hate and saw an unstoppable behemoth, slowly inching towards him, fuming with anger. Azagtoth’s arms quivered as he stared into the eyes of Hate, calm and not betrayring the storm inside. Those arms looked ready to grasp Azagtoth’s neck at any moment, twist it like a twig and leave him lying on the floor in a heap. Azagtoth could feel the fear crawl in his heart and creep up his spine. The shivers were colder than the worst winter nights he had experienced in his short life. He grasped Angelica tighter next to himself and spun her around, using her as a body shield. Azagtoth: ”My leader! I don’t want to do this! You know that! But you must understand, my leader, this is better for us both! SHE is the reason all of this has happened! Ever since she appeared, nothing has been good, the whore! You have been distracted, my leader, distracted with earthly matters that should be far below your level, my LORD. I do not want to see you suffer like this, and I know you do suffer, my leader. You escape into the worlds inside your head with more and more frequency! You fight battles that have no place in the hearts of the Cult or in your heart, my leader. None of it matters! You fight for MONEY, and why? BECAUSE OF THIS BITCH! THIS SHE-DEVIL! SHE is the real reason why you come home humiliated and bruised, my leader. You could smash all those pathetic weaklings you face, but not with this tramp by your side! Give me the order, my leader, and her blood will spill out to celebrate the night of the One-Eyed One and give his ravens a mighty feast of black, tainted blood!” Azagtoth’s voice rose higher and higher until it was a high-pitched scream. Slowly, he moved back, with the girl in front of him, the knife in place, backing up a step by step. If he only could make him listen, make him understand the pain he had caused to himself, to the Cult, he might come to his senses. Then…Then what? Would he realize the pain he had caused Azagtoth? Doubtfully. But Azagtoth had decided to try: and so he walked, and continued to scream. Beyond the walls and the doors of the room he could hear people stir. Let them: let them witness an apocalypse contained within three people. Azagtoth: ”This is the truth I speak, my leader, and only the truth! I could never dream of lying to you, oh no. And you know that! You KNOW that I am your most devout follower, that no other soldier will do your bidding as well as I will, that no other person can satisfy you as I do. But you refuse to ACKNOWLEDGE this fact, my leader! And for what? So you can replace me with this SLUT? This sinful whore not fit enough to hold my knife, much less taste its steel? You have walked away from us, my leader! I do not wish to harm you, my leader, but I will SKIN her and feed her to the dogs! TRUST ME, my leader, like you have trusted me before! Let me END this for once and for all! Let there be nothing between us, my leader! The obstacles I shall sand away, the path I shall clear, the…” Hate raised his hand, and Azagtoth shut up instantly. This was it. Azagtoth now knew his fate: it would all end here. His arms fell limp, and Angelica could have easily pulled herself free. But she didn’t: she, too, was fixated on the silent figure of Hate, one arm in front of him, the other one at his side. Azagtoth and Angelica both could see the thoughts forming in his head, could see his lips forming the first syllable. It was the longest moment of their lives. They waited as Hate slowly bowed his head. And then Hate opened his mouth. He spoke: --- Hate: ”Azagtoth… You claim much of my knowledge. But now, Azagtoth, what can I certainly know? What can I know in the state I currently am in, Azagtoth? You claim that I know this and that I know that, but in truth I cannot offer you ready answers on a silver plate. I am sorry, Azagtoth, but the knowledge which you speak of does not exist within my mind. You say that I should know you better than anyone else, and it is indeed true that your history and habits are well known to me. But what of your mind, Azagtoth? I cannot crack it. I cannot see into it. And despite what you claim, I cannot know what is right or wrong in your case. I can know the right or wrong of my own thoughts and various forms of actions I can undertake, but neither you nor Angelica fall into this category of knowledge. You ask the impossible of me, Azagtoth.” Hate pulled his head back up and lowered his arm. His combat-booted leg took a step forward, quickly followed by another step. Azagtoth and Angelica remained glued to their places, their eyes panning upwards to stare into the eyes of Hate. Children, Hate thought. Mere children who shouldn’t be here. And yet I have taken them away from their homes and made them mine, he continued. He took another step. Hate: ”What the last few months have held for us have indeed been times both dark and troublesome. I cannot make an excuse for my behavior, for there exists none. I have… I have… No. I have done thing that I am not proud of. I have assaulted people and organizations beyond my grasp as of now. I have smashed my head into a wall for so long that I have become disoriented of my real goals. Breaching that wall has been my goal for too long now, Azagtoth, and for that I apologize. I became obsessed with what lies beyond that wall called AWC, that I have repeatedly tried to crack it. I have brought you all into it, made you work as a part of the machinery meant to smash me a hole into the promised land. And yet I KNOW there is no promised land. I KNOW I will never be accepted there, and indeed I do not even crave such acceptance from the weak. I KNOW that what I am doing is disgusting and opposite of all that the Cult stands for. But I cannot stop, Azagtoth, for I am addicted. Addicted to that place. Addicted to losing. Addicted to winning. Addicted to things that make me more human than I would ever want to be.” Step, step, step. I remember when you came in, Azagtoth, Hate thought as he stared into the scared rabbit-eyes of his right hand man. I remember when we met, and when you joined the Cult, and when you became the Azagtoth I now proudly call my own. I remember when you came in and all you could think of was carving your name into the chest of every living person you encountered… And then you wanted to carve my name on those chests. I remember it all. Step, step, step. Hate: ”I have no excuse for my actions, Azagtoth. You do have to realize the level I am forcing myself to go to in order to say this. I have made mistakes. I have felt the need to hang with the crowd of AWC, not realizing that the only way they would let me hang was with a rope around my neck. And I happily tried to squeeze my head through the noose. I did not realize this until later, and indeed have not fully realized it even as we speak. It is a slow process to crawl up to the top, my dear Azagtoth, but a fast crash to come down. A failure counts for more than a thousand successes, for after failure success as a concept becomes impossible. I have failed harder and more often than ever in my life. As I said, no excuse can be made, only reprimands to the flesh and the mind. There are things I desperately want to tell you, Azagtoth, but which I cannot say as of now due to my failures. You must accept this, Azagtoth, as for now your leader is not perfect. He is vulnerable, easily defeated. But he will start crawling again, on all fours like a lowly mutt dog, to get to where he used to be.” I will tell you things, Azagtoth, Hate thought. I will tell you that I know of your ’trophies’. That the police know as well. That they are closing the circle around you, choking you out of breath. And that I won’t be there to save you, Azagtoth. I will not stand next to you when they catch you, Azagtoth. I will stand to the side and watch: and I will not feel anything for you. But you must understand, Azagtoth: that is all your leader has done in his life so far. Hate stepped closer, offering his arms in front of him not in a gesture of combat but in a gesture of friendship. His face was emotionless. He waited for Azagtoth to response before changing his mask of stone. Azagtoth jerked… --- Azagtoth jerked and hurriedly stepped back, releasing his hold of Angelica. The arm that clung to his knife felt heavy like lead, hanging to his side like the useless limb it now was. Azagtoth’s mind raced faster than anything ever before. He tried to stomach the apology he had just heard, tried to fit it into the context of mayhem and murder in his own mind. He wanted to slash the girl out of the picture and throw himself at Hate after that, gladly dying in a heroic combat. But at the same time, Hate was going to forgive him, right here and now, because he wasn’t perfect either. But was it worth it to worship a creature that wasn’t perfect? Azagtoth bit the back of his free hand and took another cautious step backwards. He was near the windows now: the street below him was empty, for now. Empty like his heart, Azagtoth suddenly thought. And then he stood his ground. Slowly, Azagtoth reversed his direction, walking towards Hate, with suspicion seeping into his very posture. He was ready to pounce, ready to run, ready to fight… Ready for everything. Almost everything. --- Out on the street, detective Reeves watched as the spotlights were wheeled into place and aimed at the hotel. Let the cockroaches come out, Reeves thought grimly as he checked his sidearm once more. Let them come out and then go in and flush the queen. Crush it with one blow. Reeves: ”OK, guys, light those fuckers up…” And lo and behold, there was light. --- And Azagtoth didn’t see shit. His whole world was a canvas of golden color. He couldn’t see anything. He stumbled, and pulled his knife up in front of him. Somewhere in the room next to this one, the scream of ”FUCK, NARCS!” could be heard. But that didn’t bother Azagtoth. He wanted to see Hate. Wanted to see so badly… --- Angelica, who had her eyes closed, blinked in the bright light, and laughed. The room looked to be standing still: neither Azagtoth nor Hate seemed to move. Angelica laughed at the whole situation, everything unwinding inside her. She could just throw herself out now and have the beams of light lower her safely on the ground. Daddy had come to her rescue. Angelica then saw Azagtoth, a few feet away from her. Heading towards Hate. With a knife. Gently, Angelica pushed Azagtoth in the back. The light-framed boy moved forwards with surprising ease. He was light, light like a girl. Angelica laughed again. --- Azagtoth saw only shapes in his vision. Black shapes upon golden shapes. And one black shape was where Hate had stood. It was now much closer, however. With arms raised. Azagtoth realized what it was: a trap. A trap to rule him in, a trap set up by the whore and his leader. He had been betrayed. Without wasting a breath, Azagtoth let out a scream and slashed. And then stood in horror, realizing what had happened as spots of red appeared on his own cheeks. Azagtoth ran. --- Hate slumped onto his knees, holding his left forearm across his chest. Azagtoth had struck him in the arm, pierced his skin with his knife and drawn blood. This wasn’t how it was supposed to go. Not at all. The high-powered spotlights moved, and Hate could see his blood slowly dripping to the floor. Azagtoth had already run away from the room. Hate turned, oblivious to everything else but Azagtoth and the lack of his presence. He yelled, his voice echoing in the halls and rooms, going up and down through the floors and ceilings, piercing the whole building with its strength. He screamed a single word, a word full of hate and misery and fear and loathing. A word that came to his lips harder than anything else he had ever said, and yet was the easiest thing in the world to scream out loud. Hate: ”AZAGTOTH!” --- Azagtoth stumbled through the crowd of druggies and whores who were running about, trying to flush their stashes down non-functioning toilets and lighting up their last joints. Here and there people were passed out: some were bleeding and trampled upon by the press of the people in the corridors. Azagtoth pushed himself through, poking here and there with his dagger to make way. He made it to the front door, when he heard the scream. The scream of Hate. Azagtoth stopped and looked behind him. And for a moment, everyone looked bald. Every head he saw seemed red. And they all wanted him. He had tried to murder his leader, the only man that had ever meant anything in his life. And now he wanted back at him. Azagtoth stumbled through the doorway and straight into a spotlight. The warm yet stinging light covered him, bathed him in divine radiance. Azagtoth stopped, not sure what to do. He was confused, exhausted and lost. He was no longer a cold-blooded killer and mutilator of women, Azagtoth. He was instead Trey, a 17-year old boy who had never had a girlfriend, was obsessed with extreme metal music and masturbated twice a night. And that boy was holding a knife in front of armed policemen aiming at him. Trey screamed in horror. Azagtoth licked his lips and charged. The sounds of pistols going off echoed through the murky night. --- Reeves: ”CEASE FIRE, GODDAMNED!” Hate watched from the windows, pressing his bleeding arm against the glass. Through the streaks of red he saw Azagtoth’s body jerk around, his body snap this way and that, and finally collapse in a way a puppet does when its strings are cut. It was unreal. Yet the red splattering all over Azagtoth’s clothing and face was far from unreal. It was very real indeed. So real it hurt. For Azagtoth, it would never hurt him again. For Hate, it hurt him unlike anything before. Suddenly, Death was there behind him, holding him by the shoulder with one bony hand and holding the scythe in the other. And Death grinned.
