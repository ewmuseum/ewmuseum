All Things Must Come to an End....

22 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/06/06 Hugs and kisses were what I got after Fresh!burst. Hugs and kisses. Kisses from Jessica and several hugs from different people in the back. Truth was one of the first to arrive after the show finished, congratulating me on my fine return to action. ‘Truth, I should be thanking you to be honest. You kept me going in the wilderness. Don’t think I didn’t check my messages?’ Waters’ smile grew a little bit wider and he winked before patting me on the back and leaving me to it. Others made their way over in short bursts, mostly the road crew and overall it couldn’t have been a happier evening. I was finally back, and it really was good to be back. ‘Jack?’ ‘Yeah Jessica?’ ‘Can we go home?’ ‘Yeah Jessica…let’s go home.’ She smiled, pressing her body tighter to me, not willing to let go just yet. I didn’t have any problem with that right now. Making my excuses from some of the road crew I headed off with my girlfriend, back to a normal life. Girlfriend…that sounds good. *** ‘The emergency exits are located…’ I drowned the rest out, pulling on the simplistic but effective headphones which would nullify all babies, whining children and loud, enthusiastic movie watchers. Curiously there didn’t seem to be any of those types when I looked around however. Then it clicked, it was Sunday after all and to be honest the amount of people flying out of Newfoundland on a Sunday after partying all weekend was going to be pretty small, let alone families with children. No, what we had was a bunch of businessmen for the most part. Jessica sat to the right of me, calmly reading the in flight magazine. She looked rather bored, but I was in no mood for entertaining her on this trip. I’d had a lot of adrenaline from the last couple of hours, but it was wearing thin now. Time for sleep. *** ‘Ow!’ The smack of a hand brought me shuddering back to life. Jessica had slapped me, hard. It stung but in all honesty I couldn’t blame her. I only wake up when I’m ready. The fact that nobody was left on the plane had something to do with it I reckon. ‘How long was I out?’ ‘Three hours.’ ‘That all? I thought I’d get at least four.’ I smiled wickedly. She grinned and shoved me playfully in the seat. ‘Come on you big baby. We’ve got to go and get our bags.’ I pulled the headphones (still in my ears) and off to the floor of the plane carelessly. I went to grab my bag from the overhead compartment but there wasn’t anything there. Sheepishly I looked back down. Jessica held up my bag. ‘Right. Should’ve thought of that.’ Opening hand my hand towards the door, I let her lead the way right out of the plane and eventually out of the airport. I inhaled deeply once we exited to get a big gulp of the air outside Newark. Felt good, great in fact. The taxi awaited to bring us home. *** 19th of June The sun was flickering violently overhead, trying hard to stay above the horizon and eke out as much daylight as possible. It didn’t want to be snuffed out, not just yet. Its pale castings lit the house of the O’Grady’s perfectly, giving the well worn wooden lacquer a deeper, fuller feeling. This was how this house was meant to be. It should always be this way. The smell too was intoxicating as a nice aroma of rose lilted about the place. It seemed the wood was instilled with its namesake flower and it filled the house. Jessica was enjoying this moment too, spinning round in the kitchen which was glorious to say the least. Now the source of the smell was to be found as someone had thoughtfully placed some roses on the dining table. ‘Must be the Andersons’ doing. They always used to leave roses for when me and dad were coming back from road trips. Still haven’t forgotten after all these years. Oh, there’s a note.’ Jessica picked it up and her eyes drifted across the page in quick time, taking it all in and smiling as it reached its conclusion. She passed it to me. ‘Here, it’s for you too.’ I was a bit surprised at this, since I never had honestly spoken to the Andersons for longer than five minutes at a time. They were just a wholesome middle-aged couple that was entering the twilight of their years. The note read simply: “Dear Jessica and Jack, Hope your trip was fine and everything is in order. We figured that the tradition was too long standing to break now and so long as somebody in the family is wrestling, it will always be appropriate. Hope it wasn’t too imposing of us to do so. Yours sincerely, John & Geena. A warm sense of happiness washed over me and made me feel even better than before. I couldn’t help but turn to Jessica and share in this well-being. ‘You know, things are going so well lately Jessica, and I have so much of it to thank you for it. I really do mean it when I say you were the reason I came back.’ ‘Aw…’ ‘No, hear me out. This whole experience has changed an awful lot of things but most of all, perspective has shown me so much. I’ve come to realise that I have a passion for wrestling, but that it is only realised when I have something to fight for. I’m thankful to say that I’ve found that passion again and it’s because of you.’ I moved round the table and looked at her. If I was a stupid teenager, I probably would’ve proposed to her right now. But, I’m a thirty-eight year old man with a niggly knee injury. If I kneel…I might not get back up. No, something far more important had come into my head. ‘I have an idea. There’s been a nagging at the back of my head for the past while now ever since your father passed away and I think I’ve finally figured it out. I don’t think I’ve really taken it upon myself to pass on his legacy…but now I’m ready to do it. I want to take on the gym, re-open it and pass on the knowledge that he gave to me.’ Jessica smiled and put her hand in mine, clutching it tightly, looking like she never wanted to let go. ‘That would be great Jack, it really would. But how?’ ‘It’s simple. I can spend some time making refurbishments to the gym in between dates and then once it’s nice and fresh I can begin to take on protégés. All I’ll have to do is hire a couple of other trainers…’ She laughed at my frenetic pace, spitting out the words like a machinegun and emptying the caged words thoughts. ‘Ok, ok! I trust you Jack!’ She pushed up against and kissed me, long enough to leave a taste, but not long enough to really enjoy it. ‘Well, I’ll be off to clean up some of the dust. I trust I’ll see you in the bedroom soon?’ ‘Of course.’ Soon Jessica, but not that soon. I had planning to do first. *** The next morning, refreshed and revitalised I awoke. Images, ideas and intricacies of my grand design flashed to and fro across my brain, imparting their own little wisdoms as they each passed. There were so many things to be done, so many calls and tasks to be achieved if I was to fully realise the grand gesture I was to make to my blood brother and mentor Dan O’Grady. I drove into town as soon as possible, unlocking the gym doors and heading in for the first time in well over three months. It had become somewhat dilapidated but it wasn’t impossible to restore it. A few slight alterations….but nothing impossible. Once the shops opened on this bright Tuesday morning I made my way to the photo deck, home to the only copier in the town as far as I knew. I had exploded my intentions onto a page to reveal to the rest of the community. Seeing as Kingston was the hometown of this idea, it would only be right to offer to them first my opportunity of creating a thriving academy. Academy had a nice ring to it…I’d keep that in mind for later on. Armed with well over two hundred copies of the proclamation, I set out to paint the town red with them. Two hours later and I was back in the gym, making phone calls and finding construction workers to get things going as soon as possible. This followed for the majority of the day, meeting with workers, finding out quotes and the like. At the end of the day I sat back in the gym and remembered some of the better days, learning bits and pieces from Dan, discovering better ways to out think and out do my opponents. It was tough work, but I always enjoyed it in the end. When I spoke of the veterans of a company over two months ago now, I spoke of their ease with holding down the youth of the industry. In retrospect, I paid some of them a disservice. For some of them, whilst holding back the young from the casual onlooker, are actually preparing them for the rigorous journey that awaits them. They don’t keep them away out of spite for their youth and possible superior talent, instead they are asking for those below them to take their time, not jump ahead into the deep end before it is too soon. For Red Rock, this is ultimately true. Ever since I have been a part of AWC, he has always been a mainstay, sometimes wayward and a bit lost in the mixture, but always a fixture in AWC. He has the talent and will rise to be a star someday, but not yet. Divide and Conquer is as far as he will get with the title that he now cherishes. Being in a position of relative safety in the roster allows me to make these sorts of judgments without trying to come across as mean spirited. If anything, I would envy Red Rock. He is in a situation where success has come to him whilst still at a young age, still young enough to go even further and greater things. I only hope that what I will teach him at Divide and Conquer will be a lesson that will only strengthen him in the long run. When I said that I would not face Adam for the title again, I meant it. My time right now is not to be served fighting over something that is so tarnished. It needs to be restored and rebuilt, but I am not the person to do it. Instead I shall provide my strength where it can be better utilised; to elevate and improve those who will go after me in this promotion. If I can manage to say that someone from amongst the ranks of the AWC beats me on their way to winning the Trans-Atlantic championship from amongst the current crop, then I will have no ill will towards them. They will have earned their way and perhaps I may have helped them along too. That’s the kind of philosophy that I want to promote in this academy of mine. It’s really what I want to be known for now. The past is past, now is the time to build my legacy. It’s time for this day to end, and for myself to return to a loving girlfriend. *** The next day… If the past two days were sparks to the flame of my renewed dreams in wrestling, then today was the antithesis. As I drove into the centre of town, intent on continuing my work, my inspiration, the warning signs were all about me. I noticed a couple of the posters I had made were either ripped, torn or erased altogether by newer posters. It was strange, but probably more likely that there was a storm last night. It had certainly felt like it when I was up during the night so it was probably inconsequential. But the more I drove, the more I realised that something was amiss. When I finally came to the gym, the door was plastered with one particular poster. MONDAY THE 26TH DISCUSSION GROUP TOPIC: “Has the spread of violence, sexism and nudity gone too far in the sport of wrestling?” WHERE: St. Peter’s Memorial Church Discussion led by Father Dave Pearson My fists were balled up. I tore the page right off the door and stormed into the gym. As I paced back and forth, it came back that every poster, EVERY poster was covered over by this same poster on the door. Only two days in and my dream was being attacked. I couldn’t let this happen. I won’t let it happen. Sunday would be my first statement to the world at Divide and Conquer. I fight for wrestling, that’s why I’m in this industry still. I didn’t have to come back, but Jessica reminded me why I was here. I wasn’t going to shy away again from the world because of one meaningless attack. This dream wouldn’t be shattered. I was going to pay my respect to Dan. Homage him. On Sunday, the Relentless title would be the perfect tool to display my intent. Just like the namesake of the title, I would go at this goal for the rest of my career. I would be relentless.
