Date: 5-15-06 Story I No Reason to Live She wanted to die... She wanted to be with her son... Wendy Cavanaugh lay in bed, emotionless, numb, as her husband made love to her. What could a six-year-old child to save his eleven-year-old older brother’s life? Thomas should’ve brought one of his older sisters along with him, not Michael. It was Michael’s fault. It was all his fault that some thief stabbed his older brother. All Michael did was held his dying brother in his arms. What could Michael do to combat someone who was over twice his size and carried a knife? Oh course, he wouldn’t step in to defend his brother, he’s too young and small. He wouldn’t have given his life to save his. In fact it was the other way around - Thomas sacrificed himself to save little Michael, but Wendy would never see it that way. Because Michael tagged along with his older brother, Thomas was dead, and the whole mess that happened two years ago would’ve been prevented. All of it would’ve been prevented if Wendy was home. Story III Wrestling Training is Hell “On your feet, O’Reilly, you’re beginning to aggravate me.” I lay on my back in excruciating pain. I’ve never felt this much pain before in my life. In boxing, you only use your hands, trying to knock your opponent out, but in wrestling, you use your entire body to manhandle your opponent. Story IV The Announcement “So... will you marry me, Stacy?” I popped the question only a year ago. I held out the engagement ring in front of her, waiting for an answer. She stared at the engagement for a long time, then looked up at me, tears rolling down her eyes. “I’d love t’ marry ye, Michael, but I dunno if I’m ready fer tha’ yet.” I frowned a little. That wasn’t the answer I was expecting. “But, Stacy, you and I’ve been together for almost ten years, have two beautiful boys together and I’ve come to love your daughter, Meryl. I’ve started a family with the woman I truly love, and that woman is you, Stacy.” I frown deepened, as if a thought had occurred to me. “Don’t you love me back?” She nodded her head, sniffling and wiping her tears away. “Aye, I do, Michael...
