Looking Deeper

7 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-7-06 click We see the slightly hazy view from a handheld digital video camera. We know this because the battery icon is still flashing in the top right corner, and the time (blinking 12:00) is still in the bottom right corner. The camera is focused on a brown leather recliner, and behind it, a classic wooden bookshelf. No one sits in the chair. A soft, gentle voice, pushed to unnatural loudness, pipes up from behind the camera. 'This is the psych evaluation for KS Sports Management's Client #006 - Jiro Sennosuke. Conducting psychoanalyst is Dr. Evan McClusky.' click There's jumpy cut, and suddenly, a young woman with lovely hair, and an angel's face moves into the frame, and sits in the recliner. She gently crosses her legs, her short skirt riding up a little as she does so. That same voiceover returns. 'Your name?' The woman starts to speak. 'Allison.' 'Your last name?' Allison opens her mouth to say something, then closes it. 'Look, just call me Allison, okay?' 'Alright, then.' There's a brief awkward pause before continuing. 'Ms. Allison...how do you know Jiro Sennosuke?' Allison looks at the camera with her deep, shimmering eyes, and answers quickly. 'Uh, well...my boyfriend Joey works with him. He's his tag team partner. They're professional wrestlers...but you know that already, right?' There's a faint murmur of assent from Dr. McClusky. 'And Allison...do you mind if I call you Allison?' Dr. McClusky doesn't wait for a response. 'Allison, what do you know about Jiro?' Allison thinks for a moment. 'Not very much, to be honest. I mean, Joey and I have only been dating for a few weeks, and yeah, I see a lot of Jiro...but he doesn't talk about himself much. I mean, I've known some pretty awful guys before, and he doesn't stand up to guys like that but...' She hesitates for a moment. 'But what?' She looks away from the camera. 'I dunno. You're not a woman, so I can't really expect you to understand. Sometimes douchebag guys will look at you, and they check you out. They undress you with their eyes. And that's okay, I mean...it's not really OKAY, but it's something that comes with the territory.' She takes a moment, then talks again, her words spilling out one after another. 'But sometimes, guys who're kind of nerdy or shy...which Jiro seems to be like sometimes, when they stare at you, it's not just like they're undressing you...they look at you like they're trying to see INSIDE you. And, like, you'll make eye contact with them, and it doesn't even faze them. And sure, it could be a weird sexual thing, but I dunno. It doesn't seem like that...it just seems...weird, y'know?' She stops suddenly, worried as if she's said too much, and wringing her hands. 'Um, he's not going to see this, is he?' 'No, Allison. He certainly won't. Is there anything else?' Allison shakes her head at first, then stops. 'Well, after he lost those first two matches, he looked weird. I mean, like really weird. He came up on Joey in kinda the same way he came up on Michael...Chainz on Fresh! Like he'd done something awful and unforgivable. And it's just a match, right? But he also was quick to defend Joey when they confronted Chainz, and I think he wants to do the right thing...I just...I don't know. He doesn't talk to me.' She breathes out the last of these words, and her face is a little crestfallen. click Now, we see the scrawny body of Joey Six, and instead of sitting upright in the leather chair, Joey is draped across it, and seems to be picking at the fingernails on his right hand. He's wearing a tracksuit in maroon, which is really, really awful, and a maroon Florida State University baseball cap backwards. 'This is Josiah M. Farnsworth, affectionately know as 'Joey Six' to his fans and friends, though neither seem to be in abundance. Mr. Farnsworth is currently the tag team partner of Mr. Sennosuke in the Atlantic Wrestling Club. The team calls themselves 'Collision Course', and with Mr. Fransworth's demeanor, I can certainly see why.' 'Doc, you is bein' TREY overdramatic with this voiceover stuff. Dial it down to a nine, brohim.' Joey flashes a quick 'thumbs-down' at the camera, then returns to picking at his fingernails. 'Joey...what do you think of Jiro Sennosuke.' Joey takes a quick look at the camera, and then returns to his nails. 'J-Dog needs to take a bath in a vat of Xanax. Motherfucker is SUPER uptight, he's up in that shit stronger than a nun's asshole.' There's nothing but a nervous cough from behind the camera. 'He's good in the ring, but he needs to RE...LAX. So he lost a few matches? Big deal...we're in the big show now, playing our game under the big bright lights. He should just be happy we're here. We gots time to be the showstoppers later.' 'How exactly did Jiro react to losing these matches.' Joey actually sits up a little, and focuses on the camera. 'It's scary, my old homie. He got up in my shit like I had a bill in my mouth, and he was looking to get that cashmoney OUT. It's one of the few times he'd actually talk to me other than to give me orders. Look, I know you've got to be on motherfuckin' point in this game, but for real.' There's a brief pause. 'And shit, what he'd do to the tackling dummies and shit at the dojo afterwards? It's like he was Jaws, or Cujo, or some fucked up Jurassic Park dino or something. He went at that shit like it was dinnertime, or like it was Angelina Jolie's titties or something. I've never seen anyone act like that.' 'He acted like an animal?' Joey picked at his nose. 'Yeah, it was the fuckin' Discovery Channel at the Academy. Dunno why he doesn't get like that in the ring, but I'm kinda glad he doesn't. Shit's weird as hell.' click We're in a different location, a nondescript booth in a small eating establishment, it's unclear as to where. The camera is pulled back enough to see two distinct figures sitting at the booth, and both are familiar to fans of professional wrestling. One of the men is former CCW/PCW/PRIME wrestler Jason Core, and the other is his student/surrogate son, current PRIME superstar Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen. Lamen's blue hair is spiked, and he looks more nervous of the two men, as he jitters and sips at a steaming coffee, but Core is relaxed, wearing his forty years of life on his face like a badge of honor. Nothing short of nuclear war can faze a man who's seen the things he's seen, and lived the life he's lived. 'I attempted to speak to a young man named Kyle Lamen, who is the closest thing to a real friend Sennosuke has, but he has a..., well, an aversion to the medical field, particularly psychologists like myself. I met with him in an informal setting, along with his mentor, Jason Core. We filmed this meeting at a Seattle's Best coffee shop in the warehouse district a few days ago.' Kyle again eyes the camera apprehensively, then pulls away. 'Mr. Lamen...Kyle...what kind of person is Jiro?' Kyle shrugs. 'He's kinda like a lot of Japanese kids these days. Quiet, works hard. Never went out a whole lot, but he was a hell of a student when I trained him in Japan.' 'He has natural aptitude for violence?' Kyle puts his hands up. 'Whoa, I wouldn't really say that. I never saw him try to hurt anyone, other than his wrestling training...and compared to some people, he's not very violent. Yeah, he works a strong-style, but he's definitely not excessive.' Jason Core pipes up in his rough and gravelly voice. 'Yeah, I've had him in my dojo working with other kids for months, and he's never taken any liberties with any of the students. He's a good kid, I think.' Kyle takes a look at his mentor, then goes back to the camera. 'For sure. There is something a little strange about him...I kinda wish he'd get out of the house a little more often or something. And he's never had a girlfriend, as far as I can tell. Or even a boyfriend. Dude keeps to himself.' Jason chimes in again. 'And a hell of a natural talent. Got great body control, and fantastic instincts.' Kyle nods. 'A born fighter, right?' click The voiceover comes back on. 'I then interviewed Mr. Sennosuke. For over an hour, I attempted to get Jiro to open up about his family, his past in Japan, and some of the details of his life. He refused to speak to me about his personal life in any great detail. I found him...apprehensive. Resistant, anti-social, even.' click We see Jiro, with his dark eyes boring a hole through the camera as he sits straight-backed in the chair. His mouth is a thin straight line, saying nothing. 'Jiro Sennosuke's lack of disclosure was unnerving to say the least. My initial analysis is that he lacks the basic social skills to make meaningful conversation with a stranger happen. Judging from his body posture and demeanor, I wouldn't say that it's a shyness issue, but I will confer with my colleague, Dr. Chitose Yamaguchi, about certain cultural and societal differences that could account for this.' click The camera closes up on Jiro's face, where it does not even look like the young man is breathing, his face is so calm and still. 'Mr. Sennosuke seems to be in a state of meditation now. click We're even closer in on Jiro's face, a closeup on his eye. It does not move, no minor rapid eye movement...no blinking. 'As both Allison and Josiah have indicated, Jiro is prone to long periods of silence and calm, punctuated by small bursts of frightening passion and rage.' . 'Mr. Lamen and Mr. Core affirmed that these bouts of fury do no present themselves in wrestling situations, so it is this professional's belief that Mr. Sennosuke does not present a risk when inside a wrestling ring.' click Jiro is gone. Dr. McClusky sits in the leather chair himself, looking mildly exhausted, and absently combing his bushy white beard with his fingers. 'One of my colleagues in particular would be extraordinarily interested in this case study, given his history with people in the profession, and dispensations towards...unusual cases.' Dr. McClusky pulls his hands down from his face, and sets them in his lap. 'While not a danger inside a wrestling ring, as proven from prior experience, it is recommended that he be watched in situations outside of the ring, for bursts of violent or anti-social behavior. I also certainly recommend a strenuous drug, alcohol, and performance-enhancing drug testing schedule for Mr. Sennosuke, as any one of those things could lead to extreme, and irrational behavior shifts.' click We go back to the extreme closeup of the unnaturally dark, almost black irises in Jiro Sennosuke's head, and how they fail to move, even under such intense scrutiny. 'Forgive me for waxing poetic, but Jiro Sennosuke is like one of the great wild tigers of the East, yet seems to wear a veneer of spohistication over this wild inner turmoil. It would be quite fascinating to know what drives this young man, but I am quite afraid of him at the same time.' 'He is not, under any circumstances, to be trifled with.' click
