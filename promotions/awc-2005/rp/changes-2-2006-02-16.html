Date: Feb. 16/06 Aftermath The walls of the Van Andel Arena enclosed a man who had already worn out his welcome, and his expression made it painfully clear – he’d rather be anywhere else in the world. Charles Kensrue had just come off one of the worst debuts in his wrestling career, and the possibility that maybe this lifestyle just wasn’t for him piloted his thoughts. Like a man captaining a ship with no destination, bound for failure, his face was barely recognizable from the same mug millions of people witnessed earlier. Looking like he had just been roused from a hangover, his sunken eyes were shadowed by dark circles, but difficult to distinguish under strands of long, uncontrolled hair. To say the night hadn’t gone well for the AWC rookie would be an understatement. Under normal circumstances, this man would find losing his debut match unacceptable. Well, the circumstances presented on that evening’s Fresh! were far from ordinary; after being pestered by a follower preceding his match, his focus was tainted with thoughts of his disregarded past. Perhaps the series of events was exactly what the doctor ordered – something to open his eyes. A former CIA agent broadcasted on national television was bound to turn some heads, and Charles did exactly that. Still, he was a little surprised that his presence was detected so quickly. Maybe, just maybe, if the situation had been presented a little further along this path he would’ve been prepared. There was no ignoring the dilemma he faced: this path would be full of obstacles. Laura Williams laid claim to his first challenge in AWC, and she certainly wouldn’t be the last. And he would make damn sure that he was ready from now on, whether he had to use a mirror to look around every corner or not – he’d make decisions similar to those of a wanted man. Charles was a stubborn man, however, and it upset him that he failed to see it coming. Not only did it taint his reputation, it also affected him in his debut match, and needless to say he deserved to end up in the loss column that night. Hence, the little ego that Kensrue had left was now gone, and along with it went the respect from his boss, David Harbor. He had succeeded in doing two things he had evaded his entire career: losing his debut match, and getting on the wrong side of the boss. This was not the man Kensrue had spent years gaining a reputation for. And for that, he was disappointed in himself, which is why he sat like a zombie in his dressing room hours after Fresh! had ran its end credits. The other reason for it he was still unsure of, until he heard a knock at the door. Kensrue lifted his head slightly, looking towards the door. “It’s open,” he responded grimly, shoving his face into his hands and rubbing his eyes. To his displeasure, the knocker decided to enter the doorway, his hands shoved casually into his pockets. “Mr. Harber would like to see you now, sir,” the messenger said, and then left without any further discussion. Kensrue heaved a sigh at this news, as he had anticipated it, then got up from his chair and headed towards the sink in the corner of the dressing room. Charles turned on one of the taps, then let the cool water flow into his cupped hands. He splashed the cold liquid into his face, rubbing it gently into his cheeks and eyes at a feeble attempt to make him look a little more alive. Once his hair was mopped with water and hung wetly in his eyes to mask his sunken look, he left his dressing room. A quick tour of the Van Andel arena lead him to an office that was marked “David Harber” on its door, which he promptly pushed open and lead himself into a familiar scene: the displeasing look on his boss’s face. The very scowl that made Charles feel guiltier than he already was. It also appeared that Pearl was on the verge of finally getting out of the venue, which didn’t help Kensrue’s cause either. It seemed the club rookie was fighting a losing battle, but fighting nevertheless, and that’s what mattered. Pearl glared at him. “What, did knocking go out of style?” he accused. The wrestler shrugged, “No. You invited me.” The boss sighed tiredly, and then looked at his guest. “I don’t know about you, Charles. When I said that I’m not giving up on you, earlier on, I might have been exaggerating. It’s no secret that you and I aren’t on the same page right now, and barging into my office without a polite knock does not help the circumstances,” he explained, then added, “On your part, anyway.” “I understand,” Charles replied. “So I expect, in return, that you understand that I’ve had a long, tiresome night, and am a little shaken up right now.” “Welcome to my world,” Pearl said tediously. Charles smiled. “I’m glad we’re on the same page in that sense, then.” “Let’s cut to the chase,” Pearl said. “You’re a promising talent, there’s no doubt about that. You proved earlier in your loss to Crisis that you have the goods to wrestle in this club, so I’m not questioning that.” “Funny, I am appalled at my performance earlier.” “That isn’t uncommon. Wrestlers usually think they have what it takes to prosper in AWC, then after losing their first match that the competition isn’t full of pushovers,” Pearl praised. “That’s not what I was implying,” Charles shot back. “I’m just—” “Used to winning?” Pearl interrupted. Charles nodded. “Yes, your record in Core seemed quite impressive. Only one loss in what, six matches?” “Yeah. I’ve already doubled that loss total in a third of the matches here,” he said bitterly. “Again, this isn’t a question of your talents. But if your past keeps coming back to bite you in the ass, and in turn, me having to cover your ass, then certain … changes might have to be made,” Pearl said confidently. Charles nodded in agreement. “It’s unfortunate that this is something out of my control. But, I am doing everything in my power to avoid it ever happening again.” Pearl frowned unknowingly. “And what does that imply?” “I have my ideas, and I’ll let you know if they go into action,” Charles stated. “Well, as long as those actions don’t result in an accident similar to tonight’s incident, I suppose I’ll go along,” Pearl agreed, then peered at his watch. “But it’s getting late. I’ve been here for the excess of ten hours, and I’m tired.” “Thanks for your cooperativeness.” Pearl smiled weakly. “Don’t thank me yet. This is far from being cleaned up.” Pearl gathered his briefcase and followed Charles out the door. “Well, I guess the next I’ll be seeing of you is at Bloodlust.” “If all goes well, I’ll be back in the country by then,” Charles said casually. “What?!” Pearl reacted, surprised. Charles smiled. “I’m kidding. Of course I’ll be back for the PPV, I need to win a match in AWC sometime.” “Well, if that’s the case … where are you going?” he asked curiously. “London.” “Ontario?” “Well … just a few time zones over.” Reunion - 2 days later The sprawling metropolis just inland of the coast in the British Empire was an extraordinary place. Home to a few of the world’s notable landmarks like Buckingham Palace or the Big Ben, London was also capital to England, a country with one of the richest histories known to mankind. A truly remarkable city to visit, Charles Kensrue took in the beautiful scenery upon his exit from the International Airport, wishing that he was here on vacation. But this type of activity was out of the question – our former CIA agent had a destination in mind, and significant business there. Kensrue hopped into a vehicle and was taxied to the western corner of the city, rolling into a beautiful neighborhood complimented by towering trees and considerable-sized houses of old-fashioned architecture. Unfortunately, the guest had never visited this area before, so felt a little uncomfortable when he told the driver to stop at house number seven on the drive. The yellow automobile came to a stop out front of another breath-taking house of old architecture; Charles thanked and paid him, then stepped out of the vehicle. He drew in a deep breath and exhaled a sigh before walking up the path that leads to the front entrance of the home. His intentions were to visit someone that he hadn’t been in contact in for a few months; ever since his planned departure from the CIA. It wasn’t coincidence that the woman he wanted to see had left her post at the agency around the same time period, either. But we would learn the details of that in due time. For the time being, Charles ascended the concrete steps slowly. This wasn’t a surprise visit, but some sort of euphoria gave him the feeling that he didn’t belong here. His choices limited, he knocked slowly on the door and drew in another deep breath and watched his breath exhale in the cold winter air. Rubbing his hands together, he silently hoped for a warm invitation. The woman who opened the door was older than the one he needed to see, but she was welcoming nonetheless. Her comforting smile and appearance relieved Charles, as he had never had the chance to meet this lady before. He grinned back at her, thankful for the hospitality he was ready for. “Charles!” she beamed exuberantly, leaning towards him for a hug. He embraced her contently, and backed away, grabbing the door with one hand and motioning him to come in. “Come in, come in … it’s chilly out there,” she continued truthfully. Charles didn’t hesitate to accept her offer, and stepped over the threshold and into the entrance room. Looking around blissfully, it didn’t take long to embody the sense of comfort that the elegant furniture and hardwood floors gave off. Removing his coat, he continued to look in awe at the home, as the woman took his coat. “It’s so nice to finally meet you, Mrs. Porter,” greeted Charles. The woman blushed, then offered, “Oh dear, please call me Francine.” Charles flattened his ruffled turtleneck sweater and looked down the hallway to his right, then glanced up the stairwell in front of him. It was made potently clear he was looking for something. “So, where is—” Charles began, but halted imminently. The sight of a woman moving gracefully down the hall towards them caused Charles to pause, as if time had stopped moving for a split second. “—She,” he finished slowly, not taking his eyes off her. The saying goes that a beautiful girl can make you dizzy, and clearly she had this effect on him. She had wavy dark brown hair that hung down to her shoulders, and basked in the presence of light. Her hazel brown eyes were enough to keep any man paying attention to more than just her body, which was slender, fit, and to this man … perfect, just like everything about her. The scene seemed to be cut right out from a movie, with Kensrue standing there in polite silence, with the reflection of a beautiful girl twinkling in his eyes, as if he was reminiscing of their past. “Now Charles, you act as if you’ve never seen her before!” Francine said, adding a laugh. He looked at the mother for a quick moment; awoken from his daze, then immediately back at her twenty-four year old daughter. “I—” Charles began, but he couldn’t seem to find the words. “Natalie, I—” “Charles, don’t say anything,” she said quietly, smiling. She moved towards him and cupped her hands around his chin, then brought him in for a kiss. They released and both of them smiled, as if from some chick flick. Well, one involving two CIA agents; like a Jackie Chan movie or something. “It’s been a while,” Charles said lamely, following suit to the romantic moment. Natalie rolled her eyes. “Jeez, you could’ve come up with something better than that!” she responded sarcastically. “Hey, I’ve never exactly been the most romantic guy to walk this earth,” Charles claimed, with a shrug following. “That, coming from somebody with a background in the CIA and wrestling, is an understatement if I’ve ever heard one,” she stated truthfully. Charles laughed. “The truth hurts.” “So does love,” she repeated softly, adding a small smile. Charles returned the half smile, and then embraced her in a hug. “It’s great to see you again, Natalie,” he said quietly, kissing the top of her head. Francine was beaming in the background, and her expression almost begged to jump in with a motherly comment. She followed through with, “Oh, I wish I had a camera!” Both of them rolled their eyes almost embarrassingly in response, followed by all three of them joined in on a laugh. The atmosphere was full of love, something that Charles had been deprived of for weeks, and this was made imminent by the delightful look on his face. Contrary to the expression that masked him following Fresh! two days ago, he looked as if he had forgotten that disastrous day. At that moment, a tyke entered into the hallway, running gleefully towards the three, and Charles’s face lit up at the epiphany of the child’s presence, as if it was his very own. That’s because it was. (in)Famous - Next morning “Seth Ronald Kensrue!” With the rays of the morning sun basking through the windows of the English mansion, the three year old ran aimlessly into the living room, dropping his chocolate ice cream on the spotless white carpet. He giggled innocently as he crawled into his father’s arms. Charles Kensrue sat on the coach, a smile spread across his face like it had been there for hours. The 28 year old played with his son while the grandmother came in the room with a cloth, scooping the ice cream off the carpet, while Natalie filled the dishwasher with dirty plates from breakfast. “Children shouldn’t be eating ice cream after breakfast anyways!” Natalie said accusingly. She had been overrode by Charles and Francine, who let Seth have some ice cream. “He’ll be bouncing off the walls by noon!” Charles laughed, and Francine seemed to have a similar attitude towards the situation. It truly was a stereotypical scene, with the man sunken comfortably in the leather couch with the two women tending to all the housework. At the very least, however, Charles had some incentive; he hadn’t seen his son for a few weeks, and wanted to spend time with him. With the introduction of Seth Kensrue, the son of a former CIA agent and current AWC wrestler comes a whole new tangent. We can now infer that the boy was the reason for Charles hanging up the agent clothes, and having James Graff explain to the media that he was let go due to “controversial reasons”. That was partially true, but also concealed the fact Charles was a father. Normally, this wasn’t a problem for CIA agents, but Charles had encountered and enemy who discovered his identity. In regards to his personal, and most of all, his son’s safety, his decision to choose a different career path was a reasonable one. Natalie was also apart of this ordeal, as a former agent herself. As co-workers, Seth’s parents had developed a relationship, and knew that hiding it forever was the wrong choice. Needless to say, the agency remained fumed that the two had chosen this path, but were accepting of it. And here we are, with details still clouded in a painfully confusing series of events, with Charles playing with his toddler, looking like the happiest man alive. But as delightful he was, he couldn’t help but have his ear catch the news story that sounded in the background. His face fell from a smiling look to a serious one, and he set Seth to his side, grabbing for the remote. The volume from the television increased, breaking the new silence in the room. “And now we have an interesting story of a CIA agent who decided to go with a different career choice - wrestling. As unbelievable as it sounds, we here at BBC have confirmed with a reliable source that a man by the name of Charles Kensrue left the American secret service and is now under a contract with Atlantic Wrestling Club, based out of U.S.A as well,” the anchorwoman reported. Charles looked on in interest, but didn’t flinch. She continued, “Also, we have learned that the reason for him leaving from the CIA was ‘a controversial past’. Hardly a valid claim, but we’ll take it for what it’s worth, and also show you a scene from February 7th’s Fresh!, a weekly show of AWC, that you might find interesting.” The footage of Charles’s encounter with Laura Williams at Fresh! followed the report, with ‘Courtesy of FOX News’ printed at the bottom of the screen. Charles calmly reached for the remote, then the television died at the push of a button. He collapsed into the leather couch, putting his face into his hands and rubbing his eyes. “Charles?” Natalie asked uncertainly. The Atlantic wrestler turned his head and looked at her. “Yes?” “What does this mean?” she asked with a similar tone of voice. Charles sighed. “It means that we can hope for the best. We can hope that the agency has done its job. We can hope that in my missions, nobody found my name, knew my face, or anything like that,” he explained, basically saying ‘It means that I have to rely on something out of my control’. Natalie bit her lip nervously. “There has to be something we can do,” she suggested. Charles shrugged. “I was a CIA agent. So were you. We can look after ourselves, if worst comes to worst.” Natalie didn’t like the sound of it. “I know what we need to do,” she claimed confidently, moving into the living room. “What?” Charles responded, almost trying to hid his curiosity. “Change.” Changes - One day later Charles Kensrue tossed his light carry-on backpack into the overhead compartment of the 10:00 flight to Virginia, but not before pulling his mobile phone from inside of it. He had forgotten that he brought it along, and noticed that it was off – either a dead battery, or he hadn’t turned it on since turning it off on the airplane ride to London. He concluded that it was the latter, because it came to life when he pushed the power button immediately. The screen blinked at him in urgency. 8 new messages The plane was bound to take off in minutes, but Charles thought he had enough time to check the messages before they took off into the air, in which all mobile activity was meant to cease. The familiar robotic female voice of “You have – 8 – new messages. Check unheard messages, press-” came from the phone, but Charles knew the routine, and pressed 1 twice to receive his new messages. He immediately recognized the voice almost immediately even though it came through with static. “Charles! It’s Graff … did you see the news?! Call me back.” James Graff, his former boss at the CIA, spoke quickly and urgently, which Charles couldn’t blame him for. This was exactly the type of situation they wanted to avoid, and the next 6 messages were all from Graff, each one having him sound a little less patient than the last call. The last one was what most interested Charles. “OK, screw it. Once you get this, come to the agency immediately. Bring every piece of ID you own.” Click. Charles closed the phone immediately. It wasn’t coincidence that his flight was bound to the exact location that Graff spoke of – the CIA headquarters in Langley, Virginia. He knew that the CIA could help him in changing his identity. And now, it was only a matter of preparing himself for the process. - James Graff paced his office impatiently, trying to forget the fact that he hadn’t slept in over twenty-four hours. The mug of coffee clutched in his hand was a clear indication of this, and Graff took a quick swig of the black poison, enjoying every trickle of the hot liquid that went down his throat. His addiction for the American pass-time was not out of an enjoyment for it – it was more of a need, and this is what caused him to like it in the first place. Still, even the caffeine couldn’t mask his weary face, even though it did keep his eyes open. Barely. And in the recent weeks, this too was common of the CIA director, for he had been a prime target in one of the most popular Virginian news stories of the year. Unfortunately for him, this was difficult for even a man of his reputation to stomach. But he could stomach another swig of coffee. Oh, sweet goodness. Collapsing into his leather chair that was positioned behind his desk, he couldn’t help but notice that FOX news was cycling another story about the departed agent, and the controversy surrounding it. The amount of times he’d seen it thus far, it was like a permanent clip in his brain, so he shut the television off angrily, out of disgust for the media, and maybe even a little self-pity. Not that anger or self-pity would make any leeway, for he had already tried that approach. That had only resulted in more people criticizing his job as CIA director, and he truthfully didn’t blame them. He fucked up. One of his agents got knocked up, the other was the father, and they were more concerned about their child’s safety than the CIA’s reputation. And he didn’t blame them either. In general, all the shit just piled up on his doorstep, and he was supposed to clean it up. And the mess was not getting any smaller. Graff leaned forward in his leather chair and propped his elbows on the desk, putting his hands into his face. He thought about calling Charles again, but thought it was useless, because that approach had already failed eight times. For all Graff knew, the former CIA agent could be on his deathbed right now. It simply wasn’t good news when an agent’s identity was available to the public. Do a little bit of math, and the identified agent is doomed, on the account of a little motivation people like to call revenge. Rather, vengeance, if you really want to get a grasp around the danger of the situation. Do a little more proverbial calculations, and things just can’t get any worst for James Graff. That is, until Charles Kensrue walked through his office door. The director saw it fit to look at his guest solemnly, and felt that it was suitable to the predicament they were in. Both of them knew that clawing their way out of this hole would be simple in any way, but they also were trained in this sort of activity. Well, as trained as anybody can be in attacks from the media, who were like starving wolves looking for a full course meal. Soon they would want an interview with Charles’s son, the greedy bastards. Another swig of coffee. Dammit. Empty. Graff looked into the cup, and swirled the grinded remains with false interest, avoiding eye contact with the man that just entered his office. He had a strange feeling that Charles was staring at him. He looked up and his assumption was proved true, because Charles’s eyes bore directly at his tired face. Well, as much of a bore that a man in his state could muster, considering the ex-agent was barely recognizable under a pale face with shadowed eyes. Graff set down his empty mug and looked interestingly at Kensrue, sighing deeply, then moving his lips in an attempt to find words to break the silence. Charles did it for him. “Yeah … we’re gonna need more coffee.” The director mustered an amused grin in return. “I hope you don’t like decaf.” Drama - 1 day until Bloodlust “What!? You’re changing your name!?” Pearl again. This time he’s a little on the angry side, and who we currently know as Charles Kensrue on the other end of the telephone can’t blame him. The meet with his former boss, James Graff, had went as planned and they had shredded all of his current identity. He was now equipped with the name of Cedric Riley, and also, looked rather different than when we last saw him. The former CIA agent was barely recognizable with a new haircut that had rid him of his longer hair, and a patch of facial hair on his chin. The most noticeable difference was the pair of square, black, thick-rimmed glasses that rested on his face. New identity, new face, but still the former CIA agent we’ve grown accustom to. Still, David Harber, Entertainment Co-Manager of AWC, wasn’t the least bit pleased. “You know what this does, Charles?” Harber shot at him over the telephone angrily. “Cedric,” he corrected him. “What does it do?” “It … I don’t even know where to start. Contract issues for one thing, did you even consider that?” “Not really-” Charles, rather Cedric, began. “Did you consider anything?!” Pearl interrupted crossly. “I considered my safety!” Charles shouted. A momentary silence followed the rogue dispute, and neither of them could find the write words to say. Pearl sighed, “I think it’s about time you told me the truth.” Another momentary silence, but Cedric broke it. “Okay, but there isn’t much to tell.” “There still is something to tell, and I want to hear it.” “When I was in the CIA, I was involved with another agent,” he began, followed by a pause. “We had a son together. He turns 2 in March,” he finished quietly. Pearl didn’t respond immediately. Instead he looked for the right thing to say, then replied, “Well … I …” “Listen,” Charles interrupted. “How about I wrestle one more match under Charles Kensrue at Bloodlust, and we worry about everything else after?” Again, it took a while for AWC’s ECM to respond, but he eventually replied, “Okay. See you then.” One end of the line went dead. After Bloodlust, the name Charles Kensrue would too.
