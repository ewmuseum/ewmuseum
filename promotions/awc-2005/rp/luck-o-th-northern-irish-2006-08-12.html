What a weird dream, I reflected, as I trudge into the bathroom, getting ready for a shower. In less than ninety minutes, I’ll be out of this country and back in Boston where I belong. I didn’t enjoy my time here in Moscow, Russia. The weather was horrible, most of the Russian weren’t very nice and it seemed like I didn’t fit in at all. But there was one good experience about all of this: I get to leave Russia as the AWC Frontier champion. After my shower, I got out, dried off, got dressed, collected my things and left my hotel room. I made it to the airport with about thirty minutes to spare. A half-hour passed, I got up from the chair I was sitting in, board the planet and it was destination Boston, Massachusetts, after that. I wish never to go back to Russia again. During that lunch hour, that had gotten to know each other, and the man she’d met fascinated Kiyoko. Jack Parker said he was a professional wrestler, goes by ring name Jack Williams. She had her crushes, some of them she thought she could fall in love with, but it was never meant to be. Part of it was because she was too shy, and the other part she couldn’t bring herself to walk up to that crush and say “Hi, my name is Kiyoko, and I would like to get to know you.” She felt if she tried to walk up to someone and introduce herself, she would get shot down and she would never want to go through with that again, so she stayed away from her crushes. Now Jack Parker had entered her life and all those crushes she had in the past no longer applied anymore. She had found her crush and she felt - knew - in her heart that she had found the right man, and she wanted to spend the rest of her life with Jack. A lot of people would think she was rushing into this, but that didn’t matter to Kiyoko. She found what she was looking for and there wasn’t a damn thing that could change her mind. There was an age difference between Jack and Kiyoko - Jack being twenty-four and Kiyoko being seventeen - but that didn’t matter to her neither. To Kiyoko, age was only a number to signify how long a being have been living on this planet. The bell rang, ending the school day.
