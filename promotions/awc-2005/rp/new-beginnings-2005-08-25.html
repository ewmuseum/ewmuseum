New Beginnings...

25 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-09 Where to begin? So much has changed and the returned to the same. I left the clubs of St. Louis where I was nothing more than muscle to go to NOWrestling. Now there was a thought, profesional wrestling. Why not? It didn't really seem all that hard of a concept. I had been fighting for a long time... the only difference in NOW was that there were rules, but then again, rules are meant to be broken aren't they? All that is gone now though, here I am in St. Louis, bouncing at the same clubs. It seems like everybody has something to prove. I don't care though... it's the same every day. Sometimes I just don't see the point of it anymore. Why not just let it all end... come to a big fucking crashing halt. Nobody would care... except maybe one person. Ever since my sister passed we have been like brothers. He is always there for me. Kris Kanton, a nice guy through and through... he was really excited to hear when I got into wrestling. He thought I was a natural talent. I had the size, the strength, the look... it's the whole package. It wasn't meant to be though... and here I am. I sit up in the bed and my head's ringing. Goddam I drank a lot last night. I look to my side and there are two girls. Nothing amazing for me though... they look wore out. Same thing happens every time, just the women keep changing. I can't even keep track of them anymore, but I really don't care. They're nothing to me. The phone on my nightstand rings and I discover the source of my ringing head. The shrill sound of the phone sends a chill down my body from head to toe as I fumble to turn on the light. The phone rings again and I'm almost tempted to throw it into the wall, but I think twice. Something inside of me calls for me to answer. I pick up the phone and slowly bring it to my ear as I look for a smoke. A man's voice flows into my ear, but I don't recognize it. I think to myself, this had better be important to be calling this early in the morning. The alarm clock reads three AM. Damn, this had better be good. I ask the man what he wants and he tells me he has an offer for me. God, not another fucking bar. I'm good at what I do... almost too good. People always seem to want my services whether it be legal or... less... than legal. Money is money though so long as I get paid, and like I said... rules are meant to be broken. The man contines as I pull the smooth brown filter to my mouth. He appologizes for the time, but aparently it's important. The AWC wishes to acquire my services. I light the cigarette and take a drag as I think to myself, AWC? What the hell is the AWC? I'm interested though so I breath out the smoke from my lungs and ask the question, what is the AWC? Profesional wrestling, yeah I know what you're thinking. This could be the new shot I'm looking for, but... why should I do it? I go home to different women every night, I make good money, and I make my own schedule. Who would want to give that up? Something digs at me inside though. I feel the urge and I remember the rush I got when I competed the last time. It was like a new high... an irresistable drug that was begging for trying again. What the hell I said and he was delighted. What am I getting myself into this time though? After getting off of the phone I discover that I'm off to England. He explained that the AWC travels between Europe and America... it's a chance to see the world. More places to see... more women to meet... more things to try... more glory to win. Glory... there was something I wanted. I take another drag on the cigarette to finish it off. I smash it into the ashtray as I crack my neck back and forth. A light catches my attention though. The sun has begun to rise... and with it comes a new day and new choices and new possibilities. The light shines on an all to familiar object though. A white mask stares back into my eyes as it hangs on the wall... no worries this time I think to myself. No worries... Soon I will be on the plane and it will all begin again. A new opportunity... A new home... And this time, I only take orders from myself.
