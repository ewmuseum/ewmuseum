What Now?

18 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-18-06 Within a dark room, a single flame erupted from a ninety-nine cent lighter sparking the end of a Winston Light cigarette. The flame illuminated Teresa’s face giving it a slight glow only for a few seconds before it’s extinguished. She slinked her arm behind her head to prop it further onto the soft pillow. A thin sheet covered her bare skin. The rest of the bed covers were mingled and twisted at her feet. Beside her laid her lover, her life partner, her friend.. His shoulder-length dark hair was as frayed as hers and his deep breaths cut through the silence of the hotel suite. Just moments before, he was on top of her pouring out his feeling in ways only love could allow. Teresa had once told him recently not to leave her. He gave her that promise and he meant it. Time for lover’s games was over. Now more than ever this woman lying beside him blowing nicotine smoke into the darkness needed to feel loved. He will be that man and she will feel alone no more. “Hey Babe” He spoke with a Southern U.S. accented whisper. Teresa took a long drag from her cancer stick. She hadn’t spoke much since they left the arena after Coast To Coast. With the situation concerning her half-sister Tiara Russell out of the way, a feeling of emptiness had planted itself within her. What now? The woman revealed the truth to her which turned her world upside down and the life as she knew it gone, a lie, and irreplaceable. “This ain’t working.” Her words sliced the moments after silence like a sharp blade through steel. She drew the last drag from the cigarette before feeling for the ashtray beside the bed. Her lover sat up and reached for the lamp. When he turned the switch, her face held an expression as empty and lifeless as the words she just spoke. A mixture of emotions rushed through his veins as he continued to watch her stare at the ceiling. He felt his muscles tense half tempted to shove her out of the bed. “What do you mean ‘This ain’t working’? We just made love for Christ’s sake!” “I’m sorry Scott.” Teresa could feel the hurt in his voice. When he reached for her hand, she sat up on the side of the bed. With her back to him, she wouldn’t have to see the hurt in his face and he wouldn’t have to see the nothingness in hers. He at least deserved an explanation, but what could she tell him? She just suddenly lost feeling for anyone and everyone in t he span of just one week? He wouldn’t understand. “Teresa Baby, talk to me please. Why are you doing this?” Scott pleaded as he pulled his jeans up. The smell of her skin and the sounds of her soft moans were still fresh in his mind. He watched as she slipped a white t-shirt over her head. He carefully approached from behind. His large hands gently grasp her arms. This time she didn’t resist. “Don’t make this harder than it already is. I can’t love you anymore. Not the way you deserve.” She could feel him squeeze her arms. He deserved more than what she had given him. After all they’ve been through over the years he has always reappeared in her life somehow. That’s just how things were. Scott turned Teresa around and with is thumb, carefully tilted her chin up so that their eyes would meet. He could see within her baby blues the absence of the spark that she only gave him. “Tell me just one thing.” He spoke softly. “Do you love me?” She said no. She lied. -------------------------------- An empty pack of cigarettes was strewn across the night stand. The remnants of her last Winston Light shook slightly in-between her fingers. In her free hand held a half-empty bottle of whiskey. It has been two hours since Scott left. She hated herself for crushing his heart, but he would never be completely happy with her. Her life since she met Tiara Belle Russell has gone to shit and she’d be damned if she brought him down with her. That’s how much Teresa does love Scott. Another long swig of the burning liquid is forced down her throat momentarily numbing the ach in her chest. She exhales the final puff of her cigarette and stubs it into the full ashtray. So, what are you going to do with yourself now? You’re can’t see your mother anymore, especially after you beat the shit out of her nurse. Your brother is fighting in a war he may never come home from. And now, you pushed way the only man that has ever truly loved you. Teresa looked up. Her thoughts seemed louder than usual. It must be the alcohol. She stood up and walked to the closet. Overhead on a shelf sat three shoeboxes. What do have to live for now? Your career? What career? You’re career is a joke. She sat the whiskey bottle on the nightstand beside the ashtray and retrieved the second shoebox from the shelf. What you got there Teresa? A gun? Oh you naughty girl. You’re not allowed to own a firearm anymore. The shoebox lid fell to the floor. Inside rested black .22. She had purchased it from a guy ran into in the States that needed some extra cash a week after her court hearing when she shot Bob. You’re no stranger to the gun are you Teresa? You’ve not only shot wild game, you’ve shot another person. “That was self defense. Bob was going to shoot Eric and myself.” Of course it was. As was that guy you literally beat to death back home. Remember? He gave you a ride ‘cause your truck broke down? “He knocked me out with a bat and when I woke, he was trying to rape me.” Teresa reached for her bottle from the nightstand and took a deep breath, then sat down on the bed. She sat the sat the shoebox beside her and ran her fingers over the smoothness of the barrel. You’re life’s been a lie. You’re mom’s a whore and now she’s paying for it with her sickness. Eric probably will die in Iraq and you’ll never see him again either. What about Scott? Well, you did that on your own. All he ever did was love you and you couldn’t love him back. “BULLSHIT!!” Teresa screams and tosses the whiskey bottle across the room. It smashes against the wall leaving what was left of the hard liquid running down the wall. Her eyes return to the gun. Her hands tremble as she reaches for it. Don’t lie to yourself Teresa. You know it’s true or you wouldn’t be thinking it. You’re nobody. You always have been and you always will be. Now, pick up the gun and do something about it. Tears streamed down her cheeks. Never in her nearly 30 year did she ever think she would sink so low. The pistol felt heavy in her hand. Her life as she knew it was really over. There was only one thing to do about it. What are you waiting for? You’ve killed once. Now do it again. The pain will go away. It will all go away. Her hands began to shake violently. She didn’t want to do it, but it hurt too much. It was too easily to give in and take the easy way out. The metal felt cold inside her mouth and the taste reminded her of blood. Soon it would be her own. Do it.. She whimpered. Do it! Click. She forgot to load the damn gun.
