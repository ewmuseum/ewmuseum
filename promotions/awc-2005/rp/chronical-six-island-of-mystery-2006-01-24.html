Chronical Six: Island of Mystery

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/05 Last time we left off, Patrick Mapleleaf had just acquired the deed to an expensive South Pacific island…from ebay. His idea to build a mansion and a Pizza Hut on the premises has become a dream. The island, known as New Canada, previous belonged to the Toot-Oot-Soot tribe. Mapleleaf’s generous payment of 1000 Mapleleaf dollars took care of the deed. Now, we will join our little anti-hero in progress as he relaxes and looks at the water, thousands of miles away from Old Canada, the country that he loves so much. What he is yet to realize is that this island is not ordinary in any sense of the word. It is on this island that Mapleleaf will lose something, gain something, and meet his destiny… [The breezes bounce gently off the ocean as Patrick Mapleleaf sits, lying on a lawn chair, enjoying the beautiful weather and atmosphere of New Canada, his new winter home. In the background, the sounds of construction can be heard. The construction is due mostly to the fact that the new mansion is going to be multi-leveled, with built in Jacuzzis, a complete stereo system, and lots of expensive artwork. Patrick Mapleleaf’s face folds into a little smile. He cannot help but realize that everything in his life is falling into place. He has a great career going…his nads are the size of grapefruits, and most of all, he never has to lift a finger.] Voice: Excuse me? [Patrick turns around. Standing behind the chair is a man with a black suit, much like one you would find in a James Bond flick. He has gray, thinning hair and a pencil thin mustache. He seems like the kind of butler that would only go by his last name. I guess we will find out shortly. Patrick Mapleleaf lays back, stretching his legs while he wears his maroon bathing trunks with pineapples on them. These are, indeed…] Voice: Sir? […the best days of his life…] Patrick Mapleleaf: Yes…you? Grayson: My name is Grayson… [We were right!] Grayson: …and I want to see what it is you want to eat for lunch, sir. Patrick Mapleleaf: Why don’t you go and find me some do-do eggs and a Tyrannosaurus Rex T-Bone steak? Grayson: Sir, those species are both extinct… Patrick Mapleleaf: Did I ask you if they were? Grayson: No, sir. Patrick Mapleleaf: Then go…NOW! [Grayson rolls his eyes and walks away. Patrick Mapleleaf takes out his new T-Mobile sidekick and begins to text message. Another voice interrupts him.] Voice: Patrick? Patrick Mapleleaf: Oh, for fucks sakes…what?! Oh, Cedric! [Cedric the black goth is standing in his shadow. He is wearing leather black pants and a sparkled black shirt. His hair is flattened against his face, and a silver tongue stud radiates in the sunlight. He has fake tattoos all up and down his arms. He crouches besides Patrick Mapleleaf, who is more than a little annoyed at this point] Cedric: I found some runes in the old village. There is mention of an opal mine on this island. Patrick Mapleleaf: So? Cedric: Which means you can mine the opals and make big money. Patrick Mapleleaf: So? Cedric: …so you can become rich. Patrick Mapleleaf: So? Cedric: Well, that’s it. Patrick Mapleleaf: How in the world is that—DID YOU SAY RICH?! Cedric: Yes! Patrick Mapleleaf: Well, get a shovel and a hard hat! You’re going mining! Cedric: Me? Patrick Mapleleaf: Yes, you, darkie. Remember, back in the old US, they used to have your kind do this shit all the time. Cedric: It was known as slavery! Patrick Mapleleaf: Fine institution, if I may say so myself. Too damn bad they got rid of it. Cedric: I cannot do this! It’s setting my people back decades! Patrick Mapleleaf: Hey…what is that? [Patrick Mapleleaf looks up at the sky. An airplane is flying exceptionally low, about to hit the tops of the trees. He and Cedric run off to the side, as it is evident that the plane is about to hit the ground. The plane becomes larger and larger. It is a private jet, seating about 20 people. Grayson comes out of the construction zone. He starts to run out of the way as well. Patrick Mapleleaf knocks into him, causing him to fall. Grayson falls to the ground. Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric move a safe distance away, but the plane falls and crashes right on top of Grayson, crushing him to death instantly.] Cedric: Look what you did! Patrick Mapleleaf: I didn’t cause the plane to crash! Cedric: But Grayson! Patrick Mapleleaf: Who? Cedric: Hmm…I forgot… [The plane doors open. About twenty youths walk out, dressed in white shirts and blue shorts. They are all boys, dressed to go to a soccer match. Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric look at each other, mouths wide open in shock. The plane empties out completely, and the boys circle around Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric.] Boy #1: Wow, where is this place? Patrick Mapleleaf: This, my lad, is New Canada! Tell me, where are you all from…and who are you? Boy #2: We’re the Cincinnati Championship Boys Soccer Team. We were on our way to Australia to play a charity soccer game when the plane crashed. Our coach and the pilot were killed instantly! Patrick Mapleleaf: Well, a regular Lord of the Flies, huh? Well boys, I don’t much care for the soccer pussy shit. I’m more of a hockey man myself. But my yacht hasn’t arrived yet, so we have no way home. Why don’t you all stay here for the duration. Boys in Unison: HURRAY! Patrick Mapleleaf: Uncle Cedric will lead you to a nice little place where you can all rest. Cedric: Wow, Mr. Mapleleaf, you’re being pretty generous! Patrick Mapleleaf: Come here, you. [Cedric walks over to Patrick Mapleleaf. Mapleleaf backhands him in the head.] Cedric: What was that for?! Patrick Mapleleaf: Because I felt like it! Listen, you gotta lead these kids to those mines. This is perfect! Cedric: Child labor?! Patrick! How cruel can you get?! Patrick Mapleleaf: It’s them or you, nigtard, and I say better them! You’re fat and lazy anyway! Cedric: Fine…as long as you pay them! Patrick Mapleleaf: Okay, okay…Kids! Listen up! Uncle Cedric is going to give you a little job to do. You are all going to work the opal mines for me. After all, you can’t expect to stay here for…free. You will all be paid 7 dollars an hour. Now, get cracking! Boys: YAY! [Cedric leads the boys to the mine. Patrick Mapleleaf smiles slyly…in a way that makes the reader suspicious of his sudden generosity. Meanwhile, he gets out his laptop computer. He opens the browser to yahoo! news and opens a link to a news story.] Britney Spears Swallowed by Hippo In a shocking turn of events, pop-diva Britney Spears was coming out of her house, when all of a sudden, a hippopotamus came out of the corner. It promptly opened its mouth and gobbled young Spears up in one bite. Spears was reportedly very pissed off. Long times critics of Spears are already making jokes. One noted critic was reported as saying, with a chuckle in his voice, “Well, I guess Spears really is a piece of shit now.” Patrick Mapleleaf: Ha! Britney Spears! She should have never dumped me. Cedric: Actually, you never dated her. You bought a cardboard cutout of her and cut a whole in the crotch. Patrick Mapleleaf: Oh yeah…that shit hurt, man. Hey, what are you doing back here, anyway? Cedric: We’ve run into a problem. The boys don’t want to go into the mine. Patrick Mapleleaf: What? Oh, geez, let me handle this. [Mapleleaf walks with Cedric down a trail. The trail cuts into the woods, and is made of a light mixture of sand and gravel. The two men make their way up a small hill and down a path that is completely covered in thicket. They find a narrow opening to a cave, where the boys are crowded around. Many of them are completely covered in filth, coughing, and are miserable out of the freaking minds.] Boy #3: We can’t do this. Boy #4: It’s dark and scary! Patrick Mapleleaf: I wasn’t going to say anything, but now I have no choice. I wanted it to be a surprise. I have hid some chocolate chip cookies in there. If you work the mine, you will eventually find them. Boy #5: Really? Patrick Mapleleaf: Hell…I mean…heck, yeah! [The boys race into the cave. Mapleleaf grabs a bolder from the side and slides it over the cave’s entrance. The boys scream in protest while the bolder blocks off the entire entrance.] Cedric: Oh, real nice. Patrick Mapleleaf: Hey, they agreed to do this. They can get out once they get those stones. Now, let’s go smoke a bowl. Cedric: Now we’re talking. [Mapleleaf reaches into the pocket of his trunks and pulls out a dark green and black pot pipe. He loads a small bag of marijuana into it, takes out a lighter, and lights it. He takes several deep inhales, and lets the smoke come out smoothly.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Ahh, that’s nice. Nothing like good old fashioned Canadian weed. Cedric: Give me some. Patrick Mapleleaf: Let me take one more puff. [Mapleleaf takes several puffs of the pipe. He then hands it to Cedric as he blows the smoke out.] Cedric: It’s empty! Patrick Mapleleaf: Well, I’m sorry! I’ve had a rough day! Cedric: You’ve done nothing since we got to this island! Patrick Mapleleaf: Now you’re splitting hairs. Anyway, I wanna go see how the construction is going. I’m trying to talk George Bush into giving me some weapons of mass destruction to defend this mother junker. [Cedric and Patrick walk back down the path. They hear some sounds coming from the bushes. Alarmed, the two men stop. They look off to one side, and in the bushes is a tiger. The two men look at each other, jaws dropped, and about to run.] Cedric: Don’t…move…a muscle. Patrick Mapleleaf: What?! You crazy? Cedric: Stand still. If we make sudden movements, he will chase us. [The tiger sniffs around the area and crosses the path. It starts to come toward the direction of the two men. They look at each other and shake their heads, starting to become afraid of what will inevitably happen – becoming the tiger’s lunch.] Cedric: Nice knowing you, pal. Patrick Mapleleaf: Wish I could say the same, but you really get on my nerves. Cedric: Great. Is this the last thing you want to say before we both go to Heaven? Patrick Mapleleaf: Cedric, my good friend…it’s quite clear that I’m going to hell. I’ve already had some contractors give me estimates for a nice condo near the sulfur pits. [The tiger looks at both men in the eye. The two men look at each other and utter curses. Suddenly, the tiger gets on its hind legs, reaches into a fold of skin, and pulls out a gun.] Patrick Mapleleaf: The hell? Tiger: All right! Freeze! Just stand there real quietly and no one gets hurt. Cedric: Is this…happening? Patrick Mapleleaf: This weed is some good shit. Tiger: Just be cool…now…hand over your wallets! Patrick Mapleleaf: Wha-? Tiger: Just do it! [Cedric pulls out his wallet and throws it on the ground. He prompts Mapleleaf to do the same. Reluctantly, Mapleleaf hands his wallet over to the tiger. The tiger leafs through it and smiles.] Tiger: Very nice, boys. What is this? Patrick Mapleleaf: It’s a condom! Tiger: How long have you had this in here? Patrick Mapleleaf: Today. I needed a fresh one after last night. Tiger: Says here that this condom expires in June of 1997. Patrick Mapleleaf: Shut…up. You hear me? Just shut up! Tiger: You can have your old condom back, lover boy. I’ll just take the cash and credit cards. All right! Goodbye, student loans! [The tiger growls, gets back on all fours, and runs away. Mapleleaf and Cedric just stare into the woods. The turn to each other slowly.] Cedric: Did we just get mugged by a tiger? Patrick Mapleleaf: I think what scares me more is that this isn’t the weirdest thing I’ve seen today. Cedric: Oh? What is? Patrick Mapleleaf: That. [A man lurks around the path. He is completely hunched over, has a cane in one hand, and a pouch of chewing tobacco in the other. He has a wrinkled face, gray eyes, and almost no hair on top of his ancient head. He is wearing a beggar’s outfit, a torn shirt and faded jeans with torn boots. He takes a look at the men and holds out the tobacco.] Man: Take…what you need. It is all I have…left in the world. [Mapleleaf takes the pouch. He opens it up, smells it, and throws it to the side.] Patrick Mapleleaf: This stuff smells like shit. I don’t chew shit, old man. Who…are you anyway? Man: I used to be…a successful writer. But now I am washed up here…alone…ashore on this ancient island. I have nothing left in this world…my name….is Philippe LaShute. Patrick Mapleleaf: That…was my dad’s name! Phillipe LaShute: Jean? Is that you? Patrick Mapleleaf: Daddy! [Mapleleaf reaches out to give his daddy a hug. Philippe shakes his head violently.] Philippe LaShute: What the fuck? Don’t you know only queers hug? Cedric: Gee, now I see the resemblance. Philippe LaShute: And what’s this, son? Hanging out with a dark-skinned? Patrick Mapleleaf: He acts white, I swear! Philippe LaShute: No excuse! How’s your mother? Still a fat wall of crap? Patrick Mapleleaf: A big pile of garbage is more like it! Philippe LaShute: Stop it. Only I may be clever. Patrick Mapleleaf: Sorry, daddy. But I thought you were dead! Philippe LaShute: No, but seeing you now, I wish I was. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I’m gonna go to the construction site of your mansion. Son, I’m moving in! Patrick Mapleleaf: Yay! [Mr. LaShute walks down the path, slowly limping. Patrick smiles, the first genuine smile we’ve seen out of him, and turns to Cedric.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Wow, my dad! My hero is back in my life! Cedric: He treats you like garbage! Patrick Mapleleaf: Quiet, you! My father is an inspiration! BOOM! Cedric: What was that?! Patrick Mapleleaf: The mine! Cedric: You think there was a cave-in?! Patrick Mapleleaf: I hope not! I don’t want anything to happen to my precious opals! [Mapleleaf and Cedric run as fast as they can up the path. The bolder is seen, broken into pieces off to the side of the entrance. The boys are there, carting out wheelbarrows full of opals. Mapleleaf turns to Cedric and smiles…his second genuine one of the day. He then walks over to the boys and puts his hand on one of their shoulders.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Great job, men! But how did you break the bolder? Boy #2: We found some explosives. Boy #4: And these cool wheelbarrows! Patrick Mapleleaf: Nifty! Wow…there are…so many here! What a fine job! Cedric, didn’t they do a fine job? Cedric: Why do I feel like I’m setting myself up? Patrick Mapleleaf: Well, Cedric is apparently unappreciative, but I sure am not! Now, I suppose you boys want your pay. Boys: YEAH! Patrick Mapleleaf: Well, I am a man of my word. For a job well done, each of you receives your full pay…except…a few deductions. [Mapleleaf takes out a small calculator from his pocket. He starts to add up figures.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Let’s see…first, I take out your health insurance fees…union fees…okay, there we go. Now, let’s take out some taxes…Taxes in New Canada are high, ya know. Ooo…oh, boy. I don’t know how to say this, but I had to deduct so much that you boys each owe me three dollars. Pay up! [The boys groan, open their pockets, and each hand Mapleleaf three dollars. He pockets it greedily and walks away, with Cedric at his heels. The two men walk towards the construction site. There is a small tent set up next to it, with a burnt out fire in the middle. Mr. LaShute is sitting on the ground, in praying mantis position. Patrick looks excitedly at Cedric.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Isn’t he the coolest? Cedric: If you say so…what is he doing? Patrick Mapleleaf: I have no idea. I bet he’s plotting something good. I can’t wait to bring him back home! [Mapleleaf and Cedric run down to where Mr. LaShute (Mapleleaf) is in his position. They approach him, but he does not move a muscle. Cedric jabs Mr. LaShute on the shoulder. Philippe LaShute springs awake and kicks Cedric in the face from the sitting position. Cedric falls over.] Patrick Mapleleaf: Wow, dad, that was great! How did you learn that? Looks like you’re doing that hippy yoga shit. Philippe LaShute: I am, son. This stuff is great. Let me knock the shit out of your charcoaled friend. Patrick Mapleleaf: Of course. I love yoga. It’s the best! Philippe LaShute: We have much to discuss, monkey brain. Here, sit down. Patrick Mapleleaf: Yes, sir! Philippe LaShute: Now, I’m not gonna lie…your mother and you weren’t exactly what I expected. Patrick Mapleleaf: What did you expect, daddy? Philippe LaShute: Some family that was good…made me proud. For fucks sakes, son, you carried that goddamn blanket around until you were 15 years old. I finally couldn’t take it anymore. I escaped and came here. When I heard you bought the island, I wanted to see how you turned out. Patrick Mapleleaf: And how did I turn out? Philippe LaShute: Pretty shitty, to be honest. But fear not, daddy is here. Patrick Mapleleaf: Yay! Philippe LaShute: And henceforth, you will do as I say…or else! Or else, I will kill some small animals like I did with Snippy, your pet dog. Patrick Mapleleaf[sob]: Snippy… Philippe LaShute: That’s what you get for joining the scouts. Now, pick up your hurting friend here and come with me. Patrick Mapleleaf: Okay. Cedric, get your lazy up! Cedric [groaning in pain]: You…truly are…evil… [Fade]
