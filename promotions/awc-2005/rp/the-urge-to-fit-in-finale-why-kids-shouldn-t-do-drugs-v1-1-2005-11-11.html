The Urge to Fit In Finale- Why kids shouldn't do drugs v1.1

11 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/05 .................................well it turns people into him! Part 2 BANG ……click……. BANG Gun in tiny hand the 12 year old boy took aim, his father placed at his right shoulder willing him on the fire at his man sized target. “Take him down son, make him feel your pain”. The cries of a desperate man perhaps. The cry of a father possessed. BANG ……….click…….. BANG . Another two shots both hitting their target and each leaving their significant mark. “Click”…..Empty. The father pats his young son on the back to show his pride in the boys shooting. No a vile, parentally advised muder was not just committed this was just one man teaching his youngest son how to stay alive- another day at the local shooting range………BLIP. A sleepy Mike Wade wakes up. He looks stunned and quite frightened. His usually gelled to perfection hair is tossed all over the place. Mike: I don't think them flashbacks are ever going to stop happening. I knew I shouldn't have watched that programme about what happened to Michael Jacksons victims. Now it's haunting my dreams. I see these feckin things all the time in dreams like as if they happened today. Hell I even dreamed assaulted a dead mans wife and daught..........Oh wait I did that ha ha ha. Mike moves back the covers to reveal bruises and cuts and gashes all sustained at the hands of the Unfuckable fuelled riot last week on Freshburst. Mike: How am I supposed to prepare for a PPV match against about 20 people when Im torn to bits from the feckin stampede. Ah well think I'll get it up! Just as the sentence and words are out of his mouth a 2 foot 5 inch man dressed as a leprechaun and a skinny big nosed, big eared freaky looking chap come running through Mikes living room chasing a monkey dressed up as a ballerina. Mike simply hangs his head at his attempts. The men are of course Mike Wades long time friend and manager Mickey Moore and Bob the Bullshitter. Mike: What the feckin hell is this? Mickey: Ah Mike laddie what’s de feckin story? Mike: I’ll tell you what the story is I was just dreaming about some shit and having flashbacks on my feckin trips and thinking about all this dramatic stuff going into the PPV and then you and Bob coming running through here chasing a monkey dressed in a tu tu!! Bob: You’re point being? Mike: Well it’s hardly dramatic stuff is it? Mickey: What? Mike: 2 feckin eejits chasing around a monkey. Mickey: I’d prefer if you stopped calling him that. Mike: What a feckin eejit? Mickey: No, a monkey! Mike: That’s what he feckin is!! Mickey: His name’s Clarence!! Mike simply sighs. What else do you do to that? Mike: You know what I don’t have time for this right now. Mickey: Mike laddie why don’t ya just chill out put on a tu-tu and join me and Bob here. Mike: Cause I have no desire to be a ballerina. Bob: You know Mike ever since you returned to wrestling you’ve really become a private, grouchy bastard! Mike: Dammit Bob that’s a lie! Mickey: It’s actually not laddie. It’s the only time I’ve ever heard this prick tell the truth in his whole life!! Mike: Ah you're imagining things. Mickey: Na Mike laddie he's right I thought you loved wrestling? Mike: Look guys forget about it I’ll be back soon…… Mickey: And….. Mike: And what? Mickey: You’ll bring back what………….? Mike: Aw……… Mickey: And you’ll bring back what Mike…………..? Mike: And I’ll bring back sweeties. Mickey and Bob both high five each other. Well Mickey high fived it was more like a low 5 from Bob as Mike takes his leave. Mikes destination is unknown. But as he sits into his car outside he stops and thinks. And like a mad man, begins to talk to himself out loud. Mike: They thought I loved wrestling............I'll give them a grouchy bastard. I'm AWC Relentless Champion and damn near the most hated man in the comapny and I'm not even involved in this Triangles match. I've tried everything I could to fit in here. Being nice to people, that didnt work. So I turned my back and that didn't work either. Maybe causing that riot last week will change their mind. Man I haven't like this since the night I took those trips......actually I think I could get so................ Drivining a car, not a good time to get a flashback............ CRASH!!!!!!! The sound of car hitting car filled the air. Airbags deployed, blood spilled and breaks shrieked as did voices. Two days before the biggest defence of his career and Mike Wade crashed his car. A drug flashback. That dancing baby. The British Bomber one week, Mike Wade the next?????? Find out next time AWC Triangles live on Pay Per View!!!!!
