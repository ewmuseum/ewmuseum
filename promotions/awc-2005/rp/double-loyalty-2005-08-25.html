Please enjoy. ++++++++ ~Moscow~ Journal entry Date: 20050725 – 3.45a.m. I can’t seem to feel anything else but disdain for what I have done for the past two years of my life. Here, there, I see nothing else but pain and sorrow. Sometimes I am able to help, to ease that pain of living through this misery I see in their eyes. I should be thankful really but why does my heart pounce so much when I bring them to their final resting place. Why is it that after seeing so many things, all those horrors in this world, I am still not at peace with my own deeds? The prayers had gone unanswered much like the questions I had mumbled to God. I have been part of our society all my life and I wonder if I should have listened to my uncle and reaped the glory of the underground instead. I am quite capable, after all, of many things and have been excelling enough for the Vatican to recruit me. Of course, I had to keep that little and certain secrecy about my purpose with them and I am just waiting for a time to return to Rome. Did I make the right choice? Was this the work I am meant to do in order to further the Kingdom of Heaven nowadays? It’s been two years I have been chasing a ghost. It’s been two years I have roamed through these fading lives in search of a hint. Uncle Petr and I have been helping each other. It’s family really but the more I do those things, the more I feel pulled from that light I have learned to love all my life. The underground here is as worst as my time in Africa or those incursions within the dark societies in the Orient. Violence is money, life is worthless and all I had tried to do was to pick up pieces to get some hints on the whereabouts of this killer I had meant to redeem in the name of God. I have my doubts. I have my thoughts on where she would be now but no tangible proofs. Is this why I have not received any orders from Father Piccello? Is this why I am still in my mother’s city of birth? What is it I am supposed to seek in this chaotic city? Moscow is not what I remembered; its gloom would depress most of the ones I know back home. But I have to keep the faith. I have to uphold my words I have given to our organization. It’s one bullet at a time; one squeeze of the trigger and absolution is to be given for one last time. Does God hear them scream? And now... The thin smoke came out slowly from Chorinsky lips, the scent of the expensive cigar lingered in the air while the balding man stared at his guest in front of him. He didn’t want to give the wrong impression and was careful to have also selected two of his sharpest looking men to stand at his side. It wasn’t everyday you would be able to meet a celebrity nowadays. Most celebrities would avoid dealing with people of his kind: those whose brotherhood meant for them to dwell in the underground of a revolving city. Moscow had been a gold mine since the Communism Fall and some had taken the chance by the balls, slapped it around and called it fortune. It took an iron hand to grasp what was available but it would take much more to keep on building what would almost become an empire. The Black Hammer was the one that controlled or was respected most of the biggest Moscow criminal syndicates. The man blinked back to attention, his guest had remained silent as if waiting for him to start some conversation. The problem was he had no idea what to say to someone like that, he knew this one was part of The Black Hammer’s family and had to be given the owed respect but it was also a known fact that his guest was also a skilled fighter and assassin. Chorinsky pulled another puff from his cigar, which should have given him some satisfaction yet today would prove to be different. It was not because he was shocked one of The Black Hammer’s family members would come to visit him, they often did and much pleasure was taken from these moments, but they were all males. Chorinsky paused his Cuban cigar onto the crystal ashtray on the table. He glanced around at his restaurant and the few customers who came in silently and nodded their heads as a sign of recognition and respect. The old man didn’t need to hide his identity, he was quite proud of his status and of the work he’d done to be where he was today. Moscow might have been a brutal battlefield between the different criminal associations and syndicates living in the Russian city but he was one who believed he had helped bring a certain façade of peace. The government and the police could be under one of the syndicates’ payroll but tempting the untouchables within the high up would be a great waste of time and money. Both of which, Chorinsky would prefer spending on other more pleasurable ways. Yet again, this time now was coming as a waste to him, the man might be old but he still had a great appreciation of female beauty and not being able to do more than look appalled to his senses. He looked on at his guest’s soft lips and lowered his glare to her chest albeit only for a few quick seconds. The young woman was dressed casually and not too flashy: worn out leather jacket, black denim pants and a silky top which did not reveal the outrageous soft mounds he would imagined she would possess. Chorinsky had heard of her and of the few hits she had made, each of them were impressive to say the least, and much more than little fables. What made her reputation, as a killer was nothing to the rumours based on her beauty, the old man tried to remember what he had heard about her. There was nothing else but respect from the other Black Hammer for this young woman. Something The Black Hammer would not give until proofs had been given on more than just a few occasions. It was a shame she was working for someone else than her motherland’s syndicates or at least solely so since he did hear she took contracts from The Black Hammer as favours. Chorinsky wondered if she was armed, The Black Hammer’s were above that normal formality and anyone, including his bodyguards knew well enough to be aware of such a matter. Touching one of them, in the morning, one would find his hands, rings and all in between the teeth of a street dog. The old man liked his hands still attached to the rest of his body and for somewhat reason; he clapped them strong enough to make even one of the waiters jumpy. The young woman did not move and continued staring at him. It was time to break the silence. “So Katerina, why is it you are here to see an old man like me?” He said with a little smile, his voice glinting with a little Ukrainian accent. She might be stunning but this woman could possibly mean business as well. If it were to be so, maybe her visit would not be such a waste of time. “I mean you could have asked one of your syndicate to look for whatever you want no?” The young woman continued to remain silent for a moment, her eyes still staring intensely at her interlocutor before she would let a slight smile draw on her lips. “I could but I know they would come to you when it comes to the Orient. It’s your kind of expertise isn’t it?” Her calm voice reflected a certain kindness instead of the expected confidence surprising Chorinsky for a short moment. The Russian she spoke was flawless, after so many years abroad; it was quite a feat as she even kept her Ukrainian accent. He let her continue after nodding to her. “I know you have quite a few connections to the Chinese and Japanese undergrounds. I can’t approach them as you might, uncle. So this is the reason why I need your help. I don’t believe in getting a middleman even if he might come from my family. I know I can come to you in confidence.” “Go on Katerina, uncle is listening but…” He knew she used the affectionate term as a way to show him a bit of respect. Smiling, Chorinsky thought to himself and about that game he had played long enough not to fall into such easy ways to divert possible profits. “I know about the Kazegawa Oyabun and the couple of Sun Yee On triad’s bosses you had…Mmmm…”cleansed” away? I don’t believe it would be a healthy choice for you to go there.” Chorinsky looked at her smiling, maybe a glint of a pride she must have felt when she had taken those men down. They were not her most impressive hits but he had only heard but rumours on the others. The old man was a believer in many things but other than going to church, what he had believed in he could grasp with his two hands and seen with his own eyes. The stories he had heard about her were a bit too impressive even for a Black Hammer. “I’m looking for a Japanese woman…an assassin so to speak and what I know of her is that she doesn’t belong to any of the Yakuza or Triads.” Katerina said simply. The old man had burst with laughter at the simple description but stopped after realizing he was the only one seeing this as funny. He shook his head, leaned against his chair before waving the matter on. He poured himself a bit of Porto and took his time to taste it. He had the luxury of being able to drink, smoke, eat the best of the best and at his age, he was still lucky to enjoy all of it with the companionship of ravishing young things. He stared at the Black Hammer’s favoured girl, imagining how she would look in more revealing outfits before finally replying to her. “It’s not much to go with Katerina. There are a few of Japanese female assassins in the Orient, well I am sure you know that much.” He said nonchalantly while giving a look toward the front of the restaurant and the few more customers getting in. It would be soon dinnertime, he thought, and wanted to get out of the place to eat with the rest of his family. “Can I ask why you would be looking for such a person?” “Another time maybe uncle.” She said with a slight smile, aware that Chorinsky was looking to get more information from her to sell it possibly to whoever would want to keep a tab on her. “Right now, the important thing is to find this woman at all cost. I’m sure that a woman killing others would be checked by the Japanese or the Chinese.” “Right now my dear, I am trying to see why I should take my time on this.” The old man looked into her green eyes with a grin. The Black Hammer would certainly be able to pay the price but the question would remain as of why they should do that. Even for their precious little Katerina, Chorinsky’ services and prices were still high to be taken on lightly. There would be questions and The Black Hammer would then turn to him for answers as of who is spending their money on him. All affairs and major spending were to be passed and approved by The Black Hammer himself. Knowing from past endeavours, he was not a fickle money burner. She smiled and nodded to him before removing a long black steel tube from her brown leather jacket, leaving it on the table for Chorinsky to glimpse onto. The old man recognized right away the tube to be a silencer before he hit on the table with his fists. “This is not the way you will impress me girl! You want to do business with me, you will got to pay!” He yelled out before signaling to one of his two bodyguards to close the door of his office. The more muscular framed of the two closed the door before staying in front of it. Chorinsky looked at him and turned his attention to the young woman again. “You are part of The Black Hammer’ syndicate. Do you know what relationship I have with them? You might have forgotten since you went to the Vatican! Did they brainwash you so badly you don’t remember?” “I am part of The Black Hammer and will always be. I have quite a good memory uncle.” Katerina nodded again as she then pulled her handgun from the inside of her coat with one hand. The young woman, without taking her eyes from the old man then took the silencer and screwed it at the end of her weapon’s barrel. “But when it comes to my line of work, loyalty sometimes can be taking a back seat.” “What?” Answered the man, almost choking on his own words as he stared blankly to the now armed woman. Chorinsky glanced behind him and then to the door where both of his bodyguards were still there, unmoved by the situation. “You stupid cockroaches! She is armed and you do nothing?” “I don’t believe they are to blame uncle. You see, they had been contacted by my Family and made aware of what may happen here.” Katerina said gently before putting the firearm down on the table. “So what if I gave you a second chance to live for the work you would do for me? Fair bargain?” The old man laughed before looking at her again and barking away, his face had turned to a darker red. “Look Katerina, if you were any other girl, I would have sold your ass to the brothels for speaking to me in that tone. I suggest you remove your bubble behind off my restaurant and never come back.” “Such words from you uncle.” She said with a shake of the head, rapidly took the gun off the table, pointing the weapon in his direction and squeezed on the trigger twice. A moment of silence and then the man dropped to knees before falling on his bloodied face. “Loyalty does take a backseat uncle…it does with most people when it comes to money but not to me.” Chorinsky’s own white shirt had some blood spilled on it, the old man trembled for a few seconds before being able to turn his head down and stared at his bodyguard. He then glanced toward the other bodyguard whose eyes were transfixed to the front of him. Katerina smiled to the scared man, raised the gun in her hand with the end of its barrel pointing upward. “What happened? Why did you kill him instead of me?” Asked the old man, taking his seat back again, his breath had gone louder. He looked at her blond hair before setting his sight on her green eyes. “As you asked uncle, I do know your relationship to our syndicate well enough. This man had been bought by Truchniev’s organization and had been on tap since a little while now. The thing is, we didn’t know if we should let him kill you or let you live. I am the one who suggested we keep you alive.” She spoke on as the other bodyguard walked away from the door and pulled his dead companion by the feet. The slender but curvaceous young woman stood up, pushing her chair away with the sole of one of her dark brown boots. Pointing the gun again but this time directly toward Chorinsky’s head, she spoke on softly. “I know you are important for us and what use would you be to us, dead?” Katerina looked at the old man’s face colour changing again but to a paler tone before she pulled the gun away. “Coming back to my little request. It’s a simple favour really. I just saved your life uncle, you owe me this much unless you don’t see your safety as that much worth.” Katerina walked away from the table before unscrewing the silencer from her firearm again. She turned her head then, slowly, revealing a splendid smile. “Maybe you could see this as a little token of your faith in God?” The old man turned around, after rubbing his forehead and gave her a nod. The woman slipped the silencer into her pocket and then tucked the gun back into her holster inside her jacket. The old man went on with a bit of sarcasm. “Yes, right! Tell me then Katerina, is this what God tells you to do?” She walked on before opening the door and then stopped for a moment. The restaurant was filled with customers, she took most of their attention away as she waved back to Chorinsky without looking back. “You know the saying…God works in mysterious ways.” Chorinsky’s eyes glanced to her curvy lower back, shook his head and sat down again, her words made him chuckle. He opened one of the drawers from the nearby cabinet and pulled a dark silver dark laptop with a steel design of a black hammer on it.
