Full Circle

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: may 23 'I've got the cell block bluuuuuues, baby!' They don't even give you a pen to play with in jail, so all of my thoughts were nothing more than little dreams. I could scratch the number in my head onto the wall... but even that was illegal. We'll get to why I was even in this penless perdicament in a minute. So, the numbers game continued torturing me... 8.99 At Wal-Mart. That's the price of the poorly constructed chairs AWC lets its employees destroy nightly. Big spenders, huh? Eight dollars and ninety nine cents. That's American, too. It's a funny thing, when you get smacked on the head with one of those chairs. Especially if you're getting cheaply beaten with an even cheaper heap of screws, staples, cardboard and supposedly 'steel' tubing that bends with a curious ease for such a strong material. It's funny, because when you see the 'MADE IN CHINA' sticker on the chair coming toward your head, you can clearly hear your boss talking. He's telling you that your skull's not even worth ten bucks in a fucking bargain bin. Fuck you and your Atlantic Wrestling Club; My favorite band's Machine Head, I love getting my head crushed. I'm immortal in everything BUT my dreams. Few people ever notice that, actually - the immortal thing. I can take chairshot after chairshot, barbed wire, baseball bats... I've taken knives and a fucking bullet, not to mention enough overly dramatic overdoses, suicide attempts and/or car crashes to kill a cat with nine THOUSAND lives. I'm still here. It's actually pretty weird, given that all of those events point to a running theme of piss-poor luck in my life. I never quite lose the biggest prize of all, but I always come close. It's an ever-lasting setting sun. I feel immortal almost always, even though I'm faced with mortality on a daily basis. It's always there, teasing me. I don't wanna live forever, but it looks like I just might... at least until my years are up. Two decades of a legend already in the making, and every day brings me closer to realizing exactly who the hell I am. Despite my apparent invincibility, the headache stayed beside me for a week. It followed me from Oslo to California, to Sweden and back again. It was that little nagging notion that, no matter what I do, something's always going to be there to kill my buzz. It happens with drugs and everyday life. As soon as you get that essential high - it could be finishing or winning something you're extremely proud of - it all goes downhill. When you DON'T get to your high, the comedown is somehow even worse. You psyche yourself up only to get knocked down. Teenaged girls lose weight, starve themselves and puke after dinner, but as soon as they get to their perfect, Paris Hiltonmegastick goal, Nicole Ritchie loses a pound more... and you're suddenly eight steps back, still struggling to doggie-paddle your way to an ever-morphing ideal. Here's a scenario... You've worked years at a dead-end job. You've handed in ten times as many reports as any other maggot in the office. You've got nothing more in life to justify yourself by than the fact that you're the best at what you're doing. Of course, what you're doing is just toiling away at entirely meaningless figures day in and day out, but it's yours. You can be proud of it. So, you're gunning for that big promotion. You get out of the cubicle pit and into a little office with no windows, but the desk'll have your name on it. You crave to be entombed by those four off-white walls, because you'll be somewhere other than where you've been. You'll be special, recognized. Redefined and justified. Paltry as it might be, that little office is your reason for sitting down at your Home Depot bargain bin, three-wheeled chair every morning. But there's a problem... everybody is like you. They all want that office, and you're constantly reminded that you don't have it yet. There's another desk-jockey a few rows down. He doesn't show up to his shifts half the time, and spends his lunch breaks getting stoned in the bathroom stalls. He's a little bit more attractive than you are... hell, he might even have a girl who's impressed by the fact that he gets to wear a tie every day. You can't wear a tie flipping burgers! He should be, but that's only part of the point. This guy's inexperienced, but by some miracle, he got his job before you even left law school... and he's kept it. Let's make this even shittier: One day, your boss emerges from his office - which has two windows! - and comes down the paper pen with an envelope in hand. It's promotion time! Today's the day that all your hard work pays off. You'll get the windowless office that has your name engraved on the door. You get the meaningless title of Assistant Personel So and So. He's looking straight at you and a smile's creeping onto his face... ...As he walks past and hands the envelope to the kid, whispering something in his ear that lights the stoner's face up. What dues did that guy pay? Is it just because he's a little fresher? It's obvious now that you work endlessly to please the people who care the least about your efforts. Now, what do you want to do to that little brat? If you're me, you're gonna take him out back on your smoke break and make sure he'll never reproduce or chew solid food again. It's not because you're bitter, really... it's just because he clearly didn't deserve the nod from the boss you're constantly pining to impress with your meaningless reports and figures. I've been getting that feeling lately. I'm shouldered out of everything, but it's not the fault of anybody I work for - it's the fucking punks who're at my throat to get what I have for themselves. Nobody wants to simply work for something anymore, they're just after that ever-changing goal - the perfection they can't attain - and they'll cut your throat or slash your tires or eat your damned first-born to get there. They don't care. If you can sum my worth up to the nine bucks it costs to buy a chair at Wal-Mart in whatever shit town the federation's in this week, I'll cut your throat just as easily as the newbies will. But my problems - these buzzkills - they go far beyond just my job. I just royally fucked myself over, but I won't die with my legacy being some 'used to's and 'almost's. I'm not ending it in a dive bar in Califuckingornia. I'm not going down in this cell. That's why I looked away when he walked in. I cast my eyes aside just like it couldn't really be happening. I closed my eyes to the mid-afternoon sun and leaned in until I could clearly smell my five dollar Jack and Coke sitting stagnant on the table. ''I almost didn't recognize you with dry eyes!'' Leave it alone. Walk away. No... just don't look up. You don't know who it is. You're not Amy Campbell. It's just a crazy stranger who thinks you look like a girl from TV. ''I'll have whatever the lady's having. One more for her, too.'' Sleazebag. Scum of the earth. Dirt on my shoe. A smudge on my already shitty record. Eddie. ''I almost didn't recognize you without a bank loan in your hand.'' ''Make that two more my date, here.'' Eddie Osbourne - the literal bane of my existance. This guy owned the first half of my professional career. He owned the last portion of my childhood. His hungry blue eyes had been over every inch of me countless times, and here they were again. He planted his filthy ass down on the second chair at my table and sneered. ''So, Ames... what brings you to California? Still pawing the ground after Darcy?'' he snickered, ''Wait, you two got back together... that's right! He finally decided his sorry ass really didn't deserve to smoke anything more than a dimebag.'' Oh yeah, did I mention I've fucked him? I mean, him and half of professional wrestling if you read the internet... but seriously, this prick had me for over a year before I left his borrowed money, security and 'fame'. I didn't just fuck him in bed, though - Darcy and I made it a threesome. We fucked him ROYALLY in the ass by leaving his shithole company, titles in hand. Yeah, we'd ridden off happily into the sunset. A cripple and a never-was, screwing 'the man'. Problem was, the cripple didn't really want me around sometimes. What nobody knows about me - not even my mother - is that I crawled back when Darcy left me. I didn't have the love I thought I'd attained, so I crawled on hand and knee back to the love I never wanted in hopes of getting a job back. We could play it off as just a storyline, I'd said. 'Come on, Eddie... take me back.'. ''Jack and Coke'll get you pretty drunk, Ed. I sure hope you're planning to drive home.'' Every glance at him reminded me of the absolute lowest point of my life. The drugs, I'll admit to. Promiscuous? Sure, sometimes I was. Pathetic is a given, but asking this sick excuse for a man to forget what I'd done to gain my freedom so he could go back to nailing me professionally from behind... not even I could own up to that. ''There's the firecracker I remember! Now, to what do I owe your appearance?'' I fucking hate coincidence. ''If I remember correctly, I was here first.'' ''Oh, right. Sorry... I'm just so used to seeing the back of your head, I must've forgotten.'' The waitress brought over our drinks, and I gave her a sympathetic look when she noticed that Eddie had been checking her out. She gave me the same glance, but I didn't deserve it. I could leave the situation whenever I wanted... it just might get messy. I couldn't help it, though - this could only be entertaining, and more reason for me to be getting drunk at 2 in the afternoon. It wasn't even the snide little comments that got at me. It was the fact that my shithouse luck had to bring HIM, of all people, around. He knows what continent I'm on, and that alone is far worse than any stupid whore joke he throws my way. ''C'mon, Princess - you're not the quiet type.'' ''In the right company, I sure can be.'' ''So you say. I'm in California doing a romantic reality show. The networks loved my idea.'' his eyes were wandering away from my grimacing, ''And all of these tattoos... yeesh. I thought you were bad. Here, pull down that top and lemme see what junk you've got written on your chest now...'' He leaned over to put a finger on the edge of my tanktop. Up until THIS very second, I was doing a fantastic job of keeping my temper. I shot out of my chair and snarled, ''Seeing you, that's bad enough. Not having Darcy here so we could both laugh at your latest scheme and filthy, sweat-soaked hide... that sucks. You touching me? That's just pushing it.'' ''I expected you to try killing me halfway through my first speech.'' ''It takes me longer to plan beating down a hobo these days. I'm not jailbait anymore, so I didn't think you'd be going for my tits...'' ''Watch your mouth, or I'll see you don't have a j--'' ''Shut the fuck up, Eddie. You know you're nothing in the wrestling world, and you're even less in Hollywood, despite what you tell yourself everytime you tug your little nubbin over some 14 year old who wants to be a star.'' ''You have no...'' I was ready to deliver the line that was in my head the second I saw him. It had nothing to do with running away or crawling back. It wasn't about Darcy or my past... it was about power, and letting Eddie fucking Osbourne know who had it now. ''It's not 1999. I could buy and sell you and any production loans you've got out.'' That's when he slapped me, and you know what? I wasn't even mad. If that was the best he had, he could keep it. I was out of there, until... ''Sure, take off. You'll be back, tuggin' at my belt for somethin' better in about five minutes,'' he said, ''I'm sure AWC doesn't pay burnouts to get boring DQ results every week. Say hi to Darcy for me, and call me when you find out how shallow his pockets are.'' I was about to please him by doing exactly what he knew I would. I crawled back. I turned around, and I came back to him with my hand out. I think he might have even expected that the hand was for his throat. When I slammed him against the wall and had a deathgrip on the loose, sweat-sticky skin of his neck, he shut up quickly. What he hadn't expected, though, was that I wouldn't be apologizing or getting too intimidated to continue. Even if he can affect my life as it is, I don't give a flying fuck, so I started tossing fists into his ribs. My forehead snapped his nose pretty cleanly, which nearly had me gagging until he shoved me back into the table. Sometimes I forget about the girth of a man until he's on top of me, but I didn't give him a chance. The table flew into the wall when I used its support to kick him in the sternum. The waitresses on duty had dropped her tray and ran out back, but we were alone in the bar and I knew how slow the cops were in East Bay, so I lifted a chair... This chair was wooden. It probably cost about five bucks at a yard sale. It was hardly 8.99 or Wal-Mart quality. It was an appropriate worth for a piece of shit. It made an odd sound when I slammed it into the side of his head and shoulder. I think the shoulder is what broke it, but I wasn't gonna stick around to examine the leftovers. The waitress was still out back, so probably still panicking on the phone to the operator for a cop or two to show up, so I ran the fuck outta there while I still wasn't hearing sirens. That waitress had a built boyfriend. When I ran into his chest, it knocked me on my ass. He looked concerned that he'd knocked me over, but wondered why I ran... and that's when she came out. I assaulted a man, he was still inside, arrest me! Arrest me? I propped myself against the closest car, which I couldn't help but notice had 'POLICE' next to my head... but no audible sirens. How the hell was I supposed to anticipate that this girl's boyfriend picked her up after their afternoon shifts were over? How was I to know that some guy with handcuffs and a gun was about to enter the bar just as I was taking off? Why would a cop date a slutty looking waitress with no apparent direction in life? Thanks a lot, luck. That was cute - tossing me at a horribly mismatched relationship when the LAST person I wanted to see would be someone with a badge... such at Slutty Waitress' Boyfriend has. Eddie fingered... eww--Eddie POINTED ME OUT as the person who assaulted him, but he wasn't gonna press charges. That was the worst of it. He had something to hold over my head again. That was something that HE could walk around knowing he controlled. I would be free because of him... ...That is, if I can post bail. But with my wallet, belt, hoodie and shoes in the police lockup, it's gonna be kinda hard to walk to an ATM. Oh, not to mention the fucking cell I'm in. I knew my options, and considered calling my parents before I'd dare ask Darcy, but I was out of time... because he's already here. They must have called my primary residence. He IS considered common-law... so closest of kin. Hearing his voice mingling with that waitress' boyfriend is kind of surreal, but the sick feeling in my stomach hadn't kicked in yet. Now, he's on his way over, and I can see the look in his eye. Yep, there's that sick feeling. ''You never cease to amaze me.'' That's all I get. He glared at me, mumbled it and kept walking with the officer. I knew the process, they were off to arrange for the proper paperwork... but Darcy looks BEYOND pissed. This won't be an encounter I'll soon be permitted to forget. You know when a parent or superior says something to you that might be good or bad, but their tone alone tells you that they're supremely disappointed in you? Yeah, it was that tone... and it only made the pit in my stomach grow. A week ago, I let MYSELF down. Now I'd disappointed the only person more important to me than me, and over what? A man who's worth less than a penny in both of our minds. Darcy would NOT be happy to know that his bail money and disappointment in me wasn't just a cost because of my usual mistakes, it was a cost over Eddie Osbourne. I started my career and adult life as an attention-whoring slut type and, as much as I might still seem like that, I've only gotten wiser. That, however, hasn't translated into being smart about my actions sometimes. Smarter, but not smart enough. In short? I'll probably be hitchhiking back to Canada tonight. Later... In the car, on the way home from the station. ''So how'd you get here this time? Some drunk bitch call you fat? Oh, I know, somebody must have ripped on Metallica. That's usually enough to give them a broken jaw and you a 12 hour vacation in the drunk tank.'' His driving normally makes me sick, but I've got that creeping queasiness in my stomach already. It's not a full-blown pit yet, though... I've still got some time to recover from looking like a complete jackass. At the very least, I'm not totally drunk... so there goes the first part of his argument. This has been a hellish 30 seconds of driving so far, and we're barely even out of the police headquarters' parking lot. ''Actually, I killed a man in Reno just to watch him d--'' His eyes were cold, and it almost put a chill to my skin. That stare meant that my antics were definitely not gonna fly in this soon-to-be fight. ''Cut the shit.'' ''Fine,'' I relented, ''I kicked someone's ass, which I'm sure they told you. They probably didn't mention that it was Eddie Osbourne, though, did they? There's no way you can be mad about this...'' I watched his rage swell and subside at the same time. How dare Eddie Osbourne show his hide near here? It was more than shocking, but I'm not sure it was quite enough to get me in the clear. ''Eddie fucking Osbourne? Are you shitting me? What the hell was he doing in East Bay?'' ''Pitching a shitty game show and trying to touch my chest,'' I sighed, ''What else would he be doing in East Bay?'' ... But wait, I was still the one who fucked up! He couldn't forget that. I could've stepped away, and I didn't. Darcy shook his head. ''... whatever. That's not the point. Why did you have to beat his ass? Why couldn't you just walk away? It seemed to work well enough the first time.'' The first time, when I went back... I shook that out of my head quickly. ''The shit he sa... you know what? I don't need to justify it. Take me to an ATM and I'll return you the bail money, then you don't have any right to criticize.'' I knew I was wrong in saying that. He had every right to do everything short of knocking me out for the shit I pulled on him today, or any number of days for that matter. It just stung, knowing I was completely wrong and without any proper argument to save face. Humility blows, especially in front of someone you've tried to impress. ''Actually, I do. It's a little right I have that comes with the territory of being your boyfriend. I also get to ask you nifty little questions like 'What's the matter with you?' and 'When are you going to stop living so goddamn recklessly?' '' Now he was wrong. ''That's the thing... NOTHING is wrong with me. You desperately want to think I've got some kind of issues, but I don't. I just want to keep living at the top, and that isn't reckless. To be reckless would be not to have a plan or a goal.'' ''Do you want to tell me what part of not living recklessly involves beating the living crap out of everyone who looks at you cross-eyed? It works just fine in a wrestling ring, but NOT in the real world, Amy. You're lucky as hell Eddie didn't press charges, but one of these days you're going to run into somebody who won't be so generous. And then where will you be?'' ''Jail, I'm assuming.'' I usually think I'm pretty clever, but I hadn't BEEN with Darcy for the last four years... I forgot his inherent ability to cut my smartass remarks dead in their tracks. ''You're unbelievable. Did it ever occur to you why I split in the first place?'' See? ''I'm guessing it's something along the lines of me being a miserable bitch who you can't control?'' Second attempt at smartassery... ''In so many words. But here's the longer version of the story. I helped you get out from under that slimeball Eddie's grip and I walked away from Pier Six with you on my arm. You know why? Because I felt sorry for you, Amy. I felt like you got in way over your head and I couldn't sit back and watch you waste your life away with him. I thought THAT was why you were such a crazy bitch... because your future was so helpless. But once we started living together, the drinking didn't stop. The random assaults didn't stop. And then it hit me: you were just crazy. You were determined to throw your life away and you needed me to bail you out once you got in too far. And I couldn't take being your babysitter anymore.'' FAILED! He threw me the heaviest load on the planet with that statement. That's why I couldn't fucking believe it. ''You felt SORRY for me? That's why we're together? I... I can't even respond to that,'' it's true, I was stuttering, ''How the hell can you be saying this right now?'' ''Because that's how I felt, Amy. I cared about you, I really did, but I had to leave because I was in no place to help you grow up. But I was never the same afterwards. I spent five years of my life trying to figure out what the hell I was missing. I searched practically the whole of the earth looking for answers, and I was led right back to you. That's when I realized that I was in love with you, Ames. That's when I realized I had to be in this for the long haul. So you know what? Go ahead, continue throwing your life away. Live like there's no tomorrow. And you can count on me to be there for you every time, to pick up the pieces and make sure that you get home safe and sound.'' He started to pull into our driveway and I, for once, was speechless. So, Darcy continued... ''But I want you to know how much it breaks my heart to see you do this. And I want you to know how scared I am that you don't have a snowball's chance in hell of making it to see 30. At least not on the outside of a jail cell. Maybe you don't care about yourself, but maybe you'll find it in yourself to think about somebody else for a change.'' Dead weight, flat on my chest - that was the feeling when his comments came down and, still without a word, I got out of the car. By the time we got into the house, I swore I'd have something to say... but we never made it that far. Once I was out of the vehicle, he threw it into reverse and took off. That didn't bother me as much as the fact that our 'conversation' was still up in the air. I knew he'd be back... ... Alright, so I hoped he'd be back. I don't think he'd just leave a mortgage with his name on it over one little kinda big spat. I got inside, and eventually (read: after about an hour and a half, a bag of popcorn and three pillows later) came to ponder my talents... technically, you could call me a professional athlete. I think that's why I take such pride in my horrible eating habits. No woman should be laying around at 12 AM, eating chips and drinking Coca Cola. At least, that's what most Cosmo magazines or self-respecting doctors would say. I'm not really a Cosmo fan, though. Too many photos of supposedly attractive, but realistically lame 'hotties'. My pride comes from the fact that I can eat like a horse and still be technically in shape. Sure, I can barely run a mile... but I can threaten someone to run it for me, so you see that this laziness didn't come without heaping rewards. That, and I'd become a conniseur of sleep by this time. Expert, right here... just under that mountain of blankets. Any problems for me are either solved in a ring, or in a bed. I'm a champion at napping and hopefully I'll be a champion wrestler again. See, life is always much easier between the sheets, no matter what you're doing. I strip down and fall into bed like it's my true savior - which kind of shoves aside the whole 'Jesus' thing, but that guy couldn't even make comfortable sandals, much less an awesome mattress. Mary Magdalene might have disagreed, but I don't think she'll be answering to me any time soon. Speaking of not answering, I seriously doubt Darcy's regained any affection for me after our rather monumental squabble earlier. Our relationship works BECAUSE we're always at odds. There's no room to breathe or to doubt, or else you might end up wrong in the eyes of the other. Most people would hate it, but he and I keep one another on our toes; We make it interesting by always changing, always growing and learning about our likes, dislikes and boundaries. I guess I found one of those boundaries somewhere between 'Don't disappear to get drunk mid-afternoon', 'Don't get arrested' and 'DEFINITELY don't make Darcy post the bail money'... because he was pissed. Royally. Honestly? Can't blame the guy - though I'd probably have been happy to hear who it was he'd be arrested for beating in my situation. The weirdest part of it all is that I'm laying here, a few hours after we got home and he drove off... and the bedroom door is squeaking open. In case it's him, I squeeze my eyes shut to feign a deep sleep, and I don't even hear an 'Are you up?'. The sound of unzipping is a precursor to feeling the opposite side of the bed gain some extra weight, and it's not long before I feel an arm draped across my shoulder. Still, though, I won't move. I'm asleep, remember? ''Crazy little redhead,'' came Darcy's voice, uttering in a low tone, ''Can't seem to stay away. No matter what you do, I'll follow and try making a path of my own.'' He was convinced that I was really asleep, because Darcy would NEVER say something so endearing if he thought I could actually hear him. The fact that he came back at all has got to be proof that I must give fan-fucking-tastic head, because no sane man would otherwise put up with me... let alone keep crawling back for more. A few minutes passed and I hear heavier breathing, then slight snores, so I open up my eyes. He's sober, calm and seems to be over our little scuffle... sound asleep, dreaming lame little Darcy Crisis dreams of title belts and benchpresses, no doubt. Me, on the other hand? I'm just back where I belong. I'm living my very own dream and, even though every day has the potential to be a nightmare, things just seem to keep working out for me.
