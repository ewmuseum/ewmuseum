reaDy?: Act IV

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- R/E/A/Destinies/Y -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “What happened to your lip?” A youthful girl in a short red skirt and white top with matching sneakers and ribbon tying her black ponytail up high clung to the arm of that young man. He stared ahead as they walked down that street, empty except for the occasional pedestrian walking past them in the opposite direction. She shook him by the arm, and he was brought back to reality as a startling look came over him. He grunted, stopping to snatch his arm from her quickly. “Saotome! What’s wrong?” He cradled his arm for a moment as the pain disappeared, and then looked back to her with a suave, calm look. “I’m fine. Please… stand on this side of me, Maru.” Quick to obey the girl rushed around Saotome and clung to his left arm with affection. She smiled, though he himself acted as if he could care less that this cute girl was all over him. They continued down the road, the two of them in silence before the girl started speaking again. “I wonder what happened to Hibiki, she said softly, a trace of worry in her voice. “I was talking with her yesterday, and she said she’d call me back to tell me if she’d be going to the party or not. But she never did…” “Mmhmm,” he didn’t seem too interested, which is why Maru didn’t get the idea and kept talking. “Saotome… I’m glad you could come to the party tonight,” she looked up at him giving a warm smile. “I didn’t think you were going to come at all.” “Oh… yeah, no problem.” He mumbled as he stared up at the stars. Pleased with gaining a response out of her heartthrob, she leaned her head on his shoulder as lovers did. “This may sound corny, but I kinda knew it’d be our destiny to be here together… alone on this street.” That word caught his attention, and took his eyes from the heavens down to the angel next to him. “Hmph, what do you know of destinies?” Not hearing the mocking tone in his voice, she actually took it as sincere questioning. “Oh, I know enough! I know that you and I were destined to be together, and that our destinies belong together.” Her words were something that seemed to bring thought upon Saotome. Maru didn’t pay attention as his eyes went back to the sky. As a matter of fact, she kept talking. “I mean, since the first day I saw you I wanted to be at your side. I kinda felt it was the place I belonged… and now here I am, by your side. I feel… at ease here, like it was a place that I called my own in a past life with you. If that’s so, then our destinies are linked from this life from the last life, and definitely into the next!” “Ah, I see,” she said only to sound like he was paying attention. “I think you might be right, Maru.” Her bright little eyes lit up as she looked up at him. “Really??” He nodded without looking at her, still looking at the stars and moon. “We are destined to meet. I knew that from the day you invited me to your party.” “Wow! This is wonderful!” she said, her hand slipping down into his and leaning closer on him. “Tell me more!” As most girls her age, she would be satisfied with the illusion of a romance and love, even if it didn’t exist at all. Most likely Saotome’s motives were purely sexual, as a senior would look upon a freshman. “Maru… do you think your destiny requires you to live for someone?” Such a thought provoking question wasn’t something she expected, but she tried to answer it as best as she could. Of course she had to decipher the question as best as she could. “Uhm… yes. If you love someone, you’re destined to live for them.” Saotome smiled at that answer. “So… would you be destined to die for that person as well?” “I… guess… so?” such morbid topics weren’t her best topic, so when in doubt, always say yes; that’s how she saw things. Basically she wanted to say the right thing to Saotome, so yes might make him happy. And indeed, it did, as his smile didn’t’ leave his face. “Well then, Maru… your destiny lies with me. I’m certain of that. Till death… do we part.” Such ecstatic words were ever heard in that girl’s lifetime, her happiness expressed with but a big bright smile and a flutter of giggles. Some people were just as excited about their destinies, because then they knew exactly where they were going, and they could prepare for it. Some people would rather not know, and enjoy the spontaneity of life. But whether you like chaos or peace… life is nothing but an anarchic calm, insanity is always upon us. We just choose to accept the slow points as peace... rather than embrace the madness every day of our lives. If you can do this, then maybe you might be one step closer to knowing the Masonna…
