Lifes a bitch!

12 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/03/06 Life’s a bitch!! We’ve all had days like this haven’t we, where you feel like your life has become a total battle, and you feel like every route you take has a 20 foot wall at the end. And this would normally be called a mid-life crisis, but if you have felt like this every day since you were 10 years old then that must mean your life is a crisis in its self, no? Well if you look at my life at in one swift glance then no, but if you go back right to the roots and watch my life in one endless video tape, then you will understand how I can bitch about life. The only two things in this world that salvage me from sheer corruption and mental suicide are my Cars and my Computers. They give me a whole new outlook on life, on how to experience new things, and how a simple coast-to-coast drive in a Porsche Carrera GT, can make life’s problems seem so small. I used to have a third love, Killing people. You will all have read that and made your final assumption of me, that I am a cold-blooded killer and am a despicable person. But I am neither of those things. See I was born with a gift, I was stealthy, fearless, a great leader, and I could look at every situation from 20 different angles and complete a task without even having to dip into a medical kit. And to the Military, I was a gift from god. But I ended up being so good in fact, that they could not decide what to do with me. In my 9 years of service I had been in every military division, squadron, camp and platoon you could think of outside of the Air Force and Navy. I was put in “Mother Natures Lean Mean Killing Machine” The marines, because of my fearlessness, and it was that same quality that got me placed into the 180th airborne, it was my leadership that put my in 3rd battalion in the army, and my stealth that ended up having me in a “One man Army” as it was called, going behind enemy lines as an almost assassin to take out the Aces in the Deck of 52. But sadly that is all over due to a knee injury, from a very poorly constructed smoke grenade that imploded, sending a shit load of shrapnel into my right leg, instead of exploding, to give me cover from enemy fire. I think it’s pretty ironic really, but then again you had to be there to see the irony for itself. And that is a perfect example of the Dead End Route. I had a perfectly good life road, and then BAM! I turn round and flatten my face on a twenty-foot high brick wall. In this case it’s my right leg, but that’s a story for another day, even though I have already told you the story. Anyway lets get back on track, I think I have found a new path, and this one should not have a twenty-foot high wall at the end, even though it may have a 6”5 bloke at the end of it. And that path is through the hallowed halls of the Atlantic Wrestling Club. Here I may not be a gift from god, but I sure as hell will fit in, and not be moved from place to place like a piece of meat, that erm…. gets moved around a lot. So yeah it should be fun, drinking beer, hanging out, kicking ass and no routine drug tests yay! Oh shit they do have them don’t they…just forget I ever said that. And I am going to go before I dig myself an eve bigger grave.
