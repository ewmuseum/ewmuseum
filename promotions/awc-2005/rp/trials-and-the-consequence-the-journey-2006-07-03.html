Trials and the Consequence - The Journey

3 July 2006

Date: 3/07/06 Have you ever seen what can happen when a priest gets his flock whipped into a frenzy by the sheer power of his words? Making them drool over the very essence and inflection of what he prepares to say? It’s a strange thing, and one I had been a part long ago…but not anymore. Now I had the opposite end of the leash. The cruel and harsh one. Yet, this was the only way to go about things. I had to take the brunt of the whips and barbs, all while turning the other cheek. Strangely, fighting was not my answer on this day. Instead I had to make the long walk from the bottom of the community of Kingston, up to the hills in the centre where the St. Peter’s Memorial Church would be standing, strong as ever to put forward my case. I would still have to make some trips along the way though, stops to test the conviction of how I would do, but in truth it bothered me not. What must be done will be done and that shall be the way in which it will be. The church was a pockmark on the community, a bloated puritan church that stood out like a sore thumb at the top of the hill, a grim relic of the past history of the centre of life here. Grey was its tone and ne’er a truer word was spoken about what lay inside the halls of the church. Darker shades of grey to be more precise. But more of that later. For now…the trip awaited. *** Monday, the 26th of June The din of the alarm resounded around the bedroom, piercing through the last remnants of serenity I had from my restless sleep. Things hadn’t gone exactly as planned last night at Divide & Conquer certainly and my body was no rebelling against the punishment. This kind of wear and tear couldn’t continue it said. I shrugged it off, pulling myself up from the bed and put my hand out to the locker. The icepack greeted mushily, squishing between my fist. Stupid heat… My feet padded across the wooden floor, moving towards the bathroom from the antique bedroom. A flickering light reminded me I wasn’t alone. I was pretty pleased about that. Slowing down to be just that little bit more sneaky, I slid past the door like a cat, getting right up behind Jessica. Her smile widened in the plain mirror as I wrapped my arms right around her. ‘Well, it looks like my lumbering ox has awoken from his slumber eh? How are you?’ ‘Sore, tired and aching a fair bit, but that’s the way I like it Jess.’ I winked at her mischievously and gave her a swift tickle before returning back to the business at hand. Grabbing my toothbrush and some paste to go with it, the simple task went by quickly. Of course, while I’m stuck with this in my mouth, Jess decides to talk to me. ‘So, are you going to Fr. Pearson’s group thing today?’ ‘How did you…?’ ‘Like I don’t see the pamphlets on your desk and in your bag. Plus, all those notes you’ve got stockpiled up on your locker, debating the finer points of why wrestling…’ ‘Alright! I get the point. Yeah I’m going, after I take care of a few things of course. Like opening the gym and doing a bit more work.’ ‘That’s fine. Well, I’m going to be gone for the day, starting off for some more scouting for your gym ok? I know you’ve a busy schedule and seeing as I’m the agent looking for people…I may as well find some more prospective talents.’ ‘Thanks Jess. I really couldn’t do it without you.’ ‘Ah shut up you grizzly vet! And speaking of which, shave, the feeling of stubble against my…skin isn’t exactly a turn on.’ Well, that was news to me. I think that deserved a response. ‘Oh really? Because…’ She pushed her finger right up onto my lips and shook her head. ‘No words, trust me.’ She gave that knowing smile again and without another word turned and went. Ah Jessica, ever the tease and with the one up on the conversation. Still, I revelled in such situations with her, it really endeared me to her. To work though, I had things to be doing… *** Outside Dan’s Gym - round about 2 o’clock I pulled up to the gym in a state of organised chaos. Picture if you will about twenty people, of varying dispositions and social backgrounds, randomly marching in front of the gym. Old met young, black met white in this twisted cornucopia of passion. I really wasn’t sure what the hell was going on with the conflagration of people but I soon found out when I read one of the banners. Quite simply…they didn’t care too much for the existence of the gym. Then a large, messy milkshake impacted on my windshield, dispersing its contents all over and ruining what had been a fine car wash job (even if I do say so myself). Well then, this was going to be interesting. Lovely, thoughtful and serene comments greeted me as I got out of the car. ‘Heathen!’ ‘Pariah!’ ‘Unforgiven!’ Jeez, I’d only stepped out of the car, I wonder what they do if I pissed somebody off? Looking to the group (who’d by now clumped together into a swarming mass), I managed to spot what looked like the leader of them all. She was short enough, spectacled and for all the world looked like she was straight out of a bad porno, waiting for her plumber. Not that I watch that. Arms akimbo with one eyebrow furrowed she was content with all her followers backed up behind her. I could sense a little trepidation from her as I approached but in this time of trouble she clutched to the one thing important to her: her bible. ‘So, can I ask why you’ve just thrown refuse on my car and hurled insults towards me?’ ‘You come to me heathen when it is clear why we do what we do? You shall have no answers when you don’t deserve them.’ The crowd murmured in assent for their leader, some of them pumping fists. It was like a bloody rock concert affair. ‘I don’t quite follow you. Could you please enlighten me as to why you’re parading around in front of my property?’ ‘Listen to him! He speaks of enlightenment like he is the one that knows what it is to be enlightened.’ ‘Who are you?’ The crowd laugh heartily, like it was some kind of joke. This is really pissing me off…what the fuck are they doing? This little miss porno has her words of wisdom again. ‘We are the ones whom you should fear. We are the ones who will be your audience, your watchers, your judges. You should be co-operative and simply observe our good choice of approach. What is happening here today is the least of your worries.’ ‘Ok…enough with the crap miss and I say that in the nicest possible way. You’re speaking a bunch of irrelevant, nonsensical language in the hopes to appear high and mighty. Do you really think that by doing this that I’m going to be worried by your appearance, whatever you’re doing? Look at you, you’re just normal people with normal lives, hell I’ve seen most of you around town doing your jobs. And most of you welcomed me back when I returned…so why now do you dissent?’ ‘The Lord…’ Like I was done then, I just wanted to take a breath so I didn’t pass out in front of them. ‘The Lord nothing! What you cling to is simply something that soothes your worries, it does not solve answers.’ Her expression and demeanour suddenly went very cold. Perhaps I should have chosen my words a bit more carefully. ‘Disgusting is all I can say to you right now because we have approached you in a peaceful manner and unprovoked you have attacked the root of our faith. We have nothing more to say.’ They all dispersed in a tangled mess, pushing past me and leaving me standing on the pavement, all alone again. Despite their lactose attack on the car, nothing else was damaged. They were true on their word of being peaceful, dairy aside, but the entire incident troubled me. None of it had made any sense beyond not wanting me here. Why I didn’t understand, since my past was well known and nobody had ever decided to call me on that one. Secondly, who were they? Cryptic answering is all the rage I know, but that last piece was another level. All I knew for certain was the association with wrestling. Seeing a can on the ground, I kicked it out of frustration. *** 2nd Try Diner - ah, we’re pushing 5 o’clock now If you think of diners across America, this probably isn’t your typical one. The almost standard red leather booths that you see in almost every TV show or film are nowhere to be found. Instead are sleek black chrome tables mixed with pure silver steel chairs. I think in the owner’s mind he’d heard about minimalism being all the rage and decided to go with it despite not knowing what the hell it was. One thing that definitely did fit well with it was the menu. We’re talking four choices at best. But that wasn’t why I was there to be perfectly honest. Sitting at home hadn’t helped my quest to go over points of view to debate with Fr. Pearson as there were far too many distractions. The only place I could find open that was quiet enough was here. Armed with my dossier of notes and theories I stood at the waiting area, ready to get to the grit of it. ‘Heya, how can I help you ?’ A cutesy little waitress in her outfit had bounced over to me. I guess some stereotypes never die, certainly not in certain parts anyway. ‘Just a quiet table for the evening…Lucy.’ She smiled politely and pointed towards the corner, a corner which had a little slit of sunlight to keep things nice and shady for me. As if I didn’t look suspicious already with lots of random notepaper. Ah well, it was too late now. Sitting down Lucy took my order of a big cup of coffee and some of their ‘patented’ blueberry cheesecake to keep my mind running but other than that I was done for the evening. I was simply going to sit it out here and now till about seven in the evening when I could finally meet my maker up on the hill. There wasn’t anyone of note in the diner beyond the staff and myself. Time passed slowly at first simply because there wasn’t anything happening and the lack of activity didn’t really allow me to focus. Despite the absurdity of the statement it was true for my focus. When things were busy around me I was able to get things done a lot more efficiently and effectively. Call it a big event mentality or simply call me bizarre but that’s just the way it was. What I had was pretty simple; three particularly succinct points which should hopefully make enough sense that Fr. Pearson may back down a little bit. Perhaps those random dogs of his wouldn’t, but all I had to do was try. Firstly, I had my point of wrestling as both a source of fitness and also employment for many of the townspeople if they so chose to take that path. My gym would be an opportunity for anyone if they wished. Secondly I had a more broad based point on the part of the respect and sense of honour that can be taught there where few other sports do such a thing. Sure they teach companionship and team spirit, but what wrestling offered was totally different. You learned to respect yourself, to understand how other people deserved to be treated and that it was a test of skill first and foremost. It was not meant as an avenue to beat people up. Thirdly… ‘Need a top up?’ Lucy was back, loaded with coffee to dispense if need be. I smiled up at her, leaving my hand placed on the page of where I had got to in my ramblings. I really must find a way of keeping things all in one piece. ‘Sure.’ She moved to pour out some more coffee for me and then it all went to shit. Her hand slipped on the piles of paper and the coffee spilt forth from the container, going everywhere and staining it all. Then, (in a move that was a little too choreographed for my liking) she fell all over the papers, pulling them to the floor. By the time she was done with her theatrics, they were useless. I tried to decipher my writing but everything was torn to shreds. ‘I’m really sorry! My God I didn’t think I had the power!’ What a barefaced lie. I looked up to see the owner of the diner looking on intently, watching like what was occurring was all important. The minute he saw me his head turned away, handily to another customer to serve. ‘I can get you another coffee if you’d like?’ ‘No, no thanks. I think it’s time I left.’ ‘Alright, that’ll be six eighty-eight.’ I left ten dollars. Sure she didn’t deserve the tip but I couldn’t force my hand by looking like an asshole too in the diner. I was trying to turn the town round, not alienate them. They were doing a pretty good job of ostracising me right now though. As I turned to leave out the door, the owner looked me square in the eyes this time. From the look he gave me it was obvious; he didn’t want me, or my grand designs in the town either. Things weren’t going well but it was time for the final ascent up to the church and hopefully to something a bit more fruitful. *** St. Peter’s Memorial Church - eight o’clock on the button Remember when I said there darker shades of grey in this church? Well, let’s give you an example of how it all comes together. Puritan, by the very definition of the word is a lifestyle free from the trappings of luxury and material wealth. The term comes from a Protestant group from the 16th Century who advocated such a lifestyle and this is where the church stems from. Despite it no longer being a protestant church (there’s more than likely some strange back story to that one), the Catholic group that moved in was certainly a group which was inspired by the puritan movement. So…why oh why was this church so…gaudy? Dull gold and silvers littered the small, bloated church, filling it to the gills with reliquaries and adornments to Yahweh. It felt like a complete façade of gross proportions on the part of Fr. Pearson but nobody in the community, or for that matter in the church diocese, was able to determine that Pearson was out of bounds in regard his vows. The church itself was now very, very still, calling to those who entered to hear the winds as if they were spirits. It was spread out into a very plain format as desired by the puritans. It was shaped in a cross formation, with the altar mounted at the top of the cross. The burden of the cross weighed heavily on the structure as the rest of it seemed sunken and dilapidated. I would’ve stayed to admire the relics (some of which had interesting patterns and framework) but I had more pressing matters to attend to. The doorway to the meeting was obvious as the light split out through it. Breathing in a little, I pushed it open and into the behemoth of the situation. *** Fr. Pearson turned towards the door, stopping mid speech to see who it was that was entering the room. He hoped it was the man he wanted to see and God was kind to him on this day. In walked the heathen, the one who was trying to rebuild what Pearson had finally thought was expunged from his loving community. And now he was in the room while he presented to them shocking footage. Pearson wiped some of the sweat off of his forehead from the strangely warm day. He licked his lips, taking in the salty sweat that had dripped down his face. This would be a good moment, a defining moment he told himself. This would be the battle that would finally win the war. Looking around the room, he returned to his speech. *** ‘As I was saying…’ Pearson was in full swing with his pulpit mode. He was wearing his robes from the order, whichever one it was, and he looked in full sway. Beads of sweat were pouring from his bald forehead, slipping through the multiple creases to give the effect almost that his face was melting. It certainly was going to be interesting to see how he was going to present his argument. ‘So you’ll see when I present to you this footage the full horror of professional wrestling. Hah! Professional seems a far cry from what you’ll see unless what they profess to be professional for is pornographic, and frankly, disgusting and abhorrent material.’ This does not sound good. ‘Well, I think it’s the perfect moment to display this footage, especially now that you’ve joined us Mr. Murphy. Kelly, would you press play please while I dim the lights?’ The girl sitting in the front row of the group nodded and hit the button as soon as Pearson cut the lights. The television screen (next to Pearson) flickered into life and immediately you could hear the roaring of the crowds. As I put my eyes on the screen, only one thing came to mind. Fuck. Corg slices away the string of Aimz’s Thong with his knife, then cuts the edges of the undergarment, leaving her bare assed to the world, with not even a single piece of fabric keeping her vital parts from the rest of the world….or Corg’s bulging member contained in his black, leather pants. Robbie: EVEN I CAN NOT TAKE THIS!!! THIS IS A FUCKING OUTRAGE!!! Aimz screams for help as Corg reaches for his own belt, and begins to unbuckle. The fans are ripping through guardrails at this point and are being beaten back by armed security. The commotion in the crowd has caught the attention of those on other parts of the mountain, even though many of them do not have a clear line of site to what is going on. On top of the mountain, Reno Davis is and Kid Wonder are taking turns keeping Howard Porter down, then notice the commotion in the crowd. Kid Wonder’s eyes then grow wide, as he looks at the Jumbotron in the America West Arena, and sees Corg now unbuttoning his pants with his right hand, while his left hand caresses Aimz’s nude buttocks and other parts in that region, in an attempt to perhaps “get” her in the mood. Kid Wonder slaps Reno on the shoulder, and shows him the tron. Reno’s blue eyes flash red with anger. Robbie: DAVIS IS FURIOUS, AS ANY NORMAL PERSON SHOULD BE!!! Walczak: She’s lovin’ it… Nate: (obviously just returning to ringside after breaking free of security) THIS IS A FUCKING TRAVESTY, AND EVERY MEMBER OF THE OSW ROSTER SHOULD BE OUT HERE RIGHT NOW DESTROYING SECURITY TO GET TO CORG!!! Robbie: This reminds me of the scene from the General’s Daughter… Nate: WHAT A SICK FUCKING THING TO SAY!!!! Robbie: Now that you are back, I need not be facish… Nate sighs. Davis rushes to his gym bag, which is still sitting in the corner of the top tier of the mountain, and this time, he does not pull out gauze….he does not pull out a towel. Instead, he pulls out his trusty, and ever present skateboard, throws it to the canvas, and begins to roll towards the edge with reckless abandon. Nate: THAT’S SUICIDE!!! Robbie: THAT’S BUYRATES!!! Back down on the first tier tears run down Aimz face, as Corg lowers his leather trousers, and his bulging member springs forth, being digitally blurred by cable and Direct TV providers. Steven: DAMN CENSORSHIP!!! Nate: YOU FREAKISH ASSHOLE!!! YOU CAN SEE THE DAMN THING LIVE, YOU MORON!!! Steven: Oh…. Aimz lets out a scream as Corg approaches her…closer…closer…. Back up top, Reno flies over the edge of the tier, and floats in mid air, catching the second part of the incline, and almost wiping out!!! The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOooooooo!!!!# The entire room was in shock. It…was quite possibly the worst thing he could’ve presented to them to show the horror of wrestling. Trust him to pick something that could be linked too. And to no surprise he did exactly that once the light came back on. More like a spotlight now. ‘Can you explain these…actions to everyone here Jack? Can you tell me why this happened? Better yet…can you tell me why you were involved in such a promotion, after this event happened if you claim that wrestling is such a noble sport and you are vowed to stay away from such…things?’ The distaste with which he spat out the sentence was full of bile. He had no like for me whatsoever and the last run in I had with him was still fresh to the mind it seemed. I rushed to think of something…but it was blank. Curse that woman. I came up with the best that I could. ‘Why haven’t you included the rest of the footage Fr? Because if anyone has ever seen the rest of that, you’d know that what was threatening to happen, didn’t.’ ‘The fact the action was not carried out was a blessing mired in revolting filth. Just like your words since you can’t even deny your involvement with such a promotion. Which brings me to my next point. You are currently a part of the Atlantic Wrestling Club are you not?’ ‘Yes why?’ ‘In good time my child. It seems dear people that Mr. Murphy has not only been a part of one travesty of wrestling, but is also employed by another! Sex, drugs and violence I think adequately describes such a promotion. I don’t think I need to mention Chainz after this statement do I?’ Ugh. I had nothing. Pearson smiled and moved on. ‘I didn’t think so. You see what is present in our community? Someone who is nothing but a drain on our morality. He is sapping the youth of their minds and providing them with immoral topics of discussion and encouraging our youth to take part as if these were everyday occurrences in our lives! Mr. Murphy, I think it would be best that you leave this discussion before you embarrass yourself even further. I have nothing more to say to you and you are welcome to stay of course…but I would not advise this course of action. I have been more than lenient with you.’ It hurt…more than losing to Red Rock last night but I put my tail between my legs and left. As I walked out of the church, my head was swirling, still unsure of how I could’ve lost this. It became slightly more clear once I pieced it together. All throughout the day I had been cut off and put off by various followers of Pearson. They had simply been employed to make sure that I arrived ill prepared and off guard. It had certainly worked. If there was one consolation for all of this, I suppose you could say that on Fresh! I’ll get to take out some of my frustration but…there’s not much I can do. Round one to the community. I would have my rebuttal though…I just wasn’t quite sure where I was going yet. # - Ascension writing credited to: http://community.primetimecentral.net/showthread.php?p=71004&mode=linear and OSW.
