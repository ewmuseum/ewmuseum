Heartbroken: Assume Rumors?

1 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Was everything back to normal? You could look around the school and ask yourself that question everyday. The echoes of Hibiki Jue’s death had hit everyone hard, and to think that things would just smooth over like the ripples of a pond would be ignorant. This was much more than just the death of a girl. To many, it was the death of a friend, the death of a loved one, and the death of a heroine. Never had so many people carried gloomy faces since the quarter exam grades were posted. Everyone had gone to Jue’s big party the night before, and the results were less than desirable. Still, they didn’t mind the grades; they were just happy to have had fun before, and they thanked Jue for it. Jue made people feel good about themselves by doing whatever she could to make it happen. That’s how selfless people saw her. It was her looks, her personality, and her ability to please people that made her the Superstar of Shinyo that she was. But now… she’s gone… and the question still remains… Was everything back to normal? Kirima Nagi still leaned against the railing of the bridge leading towards school; staring down at the street with what many thought was malicious intentions on her mind. That’s normal, of course. Nagi was the type of creature that didn’t change her ways very easily. Stubborn and focused, she’d drive forward with whatever purpose she had, be it terrorizing the student body or whatever. She watched as Saotome and Maru made their way to school, Maru the cheerful always cradling her boyfriend’s arm as if it were the most precious thing in the world. Saotome always looked down at her as if there were a piece of shit hanging from his arm and he couldn’t scrape it off no matter what. That was normal, no doubt about that. Behind them the Mishima Twins walked, the two almond haired siblings just about inseparable no matter the situation. YOH dragged on, his hands slipped in his pockets as he stared sleepily at the ground, while sister Uki kept close by, two cups of coffee in her hand. She held the left one out to her brother, who took a sip through the straw and winced. It was this tender display of family bond that told Nagi that maybe things were just about normal again. After the Blitz incident a few months back, the twins weren’t as cheerful as they’ve always been, but now seeing them smiling some, maybe they had forgotten about their past. Of course the Blitz incident wasn’t just something everyone could forget about. There’s been many a melee on these school grounds that the Morals Committee or even the Principal himself couldn’t control, so they’ve let it run its course and once things had died down, punishment would be dealt out like poker cards. The student body anted up putting their bodies on the playing table, and each and every time there was only one person that left with all the winnings… The 4 were making their way in as well, Ishikawa, Jinnai, Kawaii, and Kawada. Nagi was used to the same routine from them by now. Kawada would be reviewing notes, while Kawaii would ramble on with Ishikawa and Jinnai, Jinnai ogling all three girls while Ishikawa kicked around her soccer ball. The same plain platonic predictability expected from them. Nagi glanced over to see Juri Hyoba--- Wait, go back to The 4… Kawada… Kawaii… Jinnai… where was Ishikawa? Nagi rolled her eyes, sighing as she pushed herself away from the railing and turned around. “Good morning Na--!!” Chibi was cut off with a shove, knocking him flat on his back. Nagi stopped, looking down at him with a stern look. “Watch yourself, short stack.” He watched as she ran off, Ikari coming to help him up. She glared in Nagi’s fleeting direction, grabbing Chibi’s arm and pulling him up. “What a bitch. I still say you’re nuts for liking her.” “Shhhh!” Chibi put his finger to his lips hushing her. “You want everyone to know that?? Geez!” “Sorry!” she hugged Chibi and started to walk towards school again. “I just can’t… you know… its just madness to me!” There was one thing that Ikari had to learn about life. No matter where you go, what you do, and how you do it, or whom you do it with… there’s always madness afoot. Madness makes the world go round. People may have loved the popular ones, pitied the weak ones, and ignored the ignorant, but one thing is certain. It’s the actions of a psycho that people remember. -=-=-=- Two girls stood at the gravesite. Didn’t this seem familiar? And coincidentally enough, one of them had a soccer ball, shuffling it around with her feet kicking her navy blue skirt up. It was the same scene all over again, except this time Nagi wasn’t in her fighting clothes… and she wasn’t digging up the grave. She walked around the corner and slapped her forehead, cracking her knuckles with a hard exhale. “Some things don’t change, do they?” The two girls turned around… and to much surprise of Nagi neither girl was Ishikawa OR Hibiki Jue. They looked at her warily; this after all was Kirima Nagi, the First and Last that terrorized Shinyo. Nagi lowered her hands and tried to look as casual as possible, which was a failing task in itself. “Uhh… so like what’re you guys doing here?” It was more jarring to see Nagi try and be friendly, but the girls answered quickly less they make the monster impatient and angry. “I ran into Tachibana on my way to school and we decided to pay a quick visit to Hibiki’s grave.” Nagi recognized Tachibana, the slender girl without the soccer ball. She was always having problems with school, and had to get tutored and take cram school after hours. Nagi approached the two girls slowly, folding her arms across her chest. “Is that so?” they parted as she walked through them, standing on top of the grave looking down at the picture of Hibiki. The last time Nagi had seen this grave the photo was knocked over… someone had come by and made things right. “You should let Hibiki’s spirit rest. She’s been through a lot.” The two girls looked at each other. This was strange, hearing a noble thing like that from Kirima Nagi. She turned around and looked at the girl with the soccer ball. “Kurosu, have you seen Ishikawa?” It was the mention of that name that made her face shift from fear to surprise. Nagi looked over at Tachibana, giving her a stern look that made the girl back up a few steps. “I think I’ll be heading to class. I’ll see you later Kurosu.” She reached out for Tachibana to grab her arm, but Tachibana was already making her way out of the cemetery in a brisk jog. In Kurosu’s eyes, Tachibana was the only thing keeping her at ease in front of Kirima Nagi, a witness should anything happen. There were greater odds in numbers, but one on one with the First and Last… much like the Masonna, the odds were against you. Folding her arms in front of her again, she started to walk through the cemetery. “Well… let’s hear it. What do you know about Ishikawa?” Kurosu hugged the soccer ball against her chest tightly, looking down at the checkered pattern. “Ishikawa? Well she’s probably the best player on the team, at least in my opinion. She’s always helping me get better; I love her for that.” Beating around the bush was something Nagi didn’t have patience for. Slapping the ball out of her hands, Nagi grabbed Kurosu by the collar and pulled her close, glaring into her eyes. Fear ran down Kurosu’s face like tears, which coincidentally began to well up in her eyes. She could barely feel the ground, her toes tapping pavement lightly. “Don’t feed me bullshit, Kurosu. You know what I’m talking about. Otherwise we wouldn’t be here, so now… what do you know about Ishikawa?” “All right! I’ll tell you!” She dropped her, folding her arms as Kurosu took a moment to catch her breath. Then she looked up at Nagi, though her head sunk down into her shoulders. -=-=-=- “Well by now you know there’s some connection between Hibiki Jue and Ishikawa Jun, right?” “I wouldn’t be here otherwise.” Ishikawa was alone in her room, a very plain room by girls’ standards. There were very little wall decorations, only a cluster of photos near her desk and bed, and one soccer poster of Mia Hamm. Ishikawa sat on her bed, wearing gray sweats and a white tank top with her hair tied back in a ponytail. Her eyes were cast to the floor, eyes shimmering as if she wanted to cry. “From what you know there’s no correlation between the best soccer player in school and the most popular girl in school. One’s a tomboy and the other’s considered the school princess. Neither of them would be seen together, and neither of them are the type get along.” “The only correlation I know is that Ishikawa is drawn to Hibiki in some fashion. I take it they were friends though.” “Right. They used to be friends… a long time ago.” Lying back in her bed, she closed her eyes and sighed. She draped her arm over her eyes, lying there silently. Her parents were at work right now, so she was all alone in the house. It was rare for her to be alone, listen to the silence, especially with a little brother always running around. That’s why she skipped. She needed peace and quiet, a moment of clarity to understand everything going on in her life. Did she really see Hibiki Jue… alive and standing in front of her that night? Was all of that real, or was it just a dream turned nightmare? Was that entire night real at all? She rolled over, burying her face in the pillow and folding her hands behind her head. “Back before high school Ishikawa and Hibiki used to play soccer together. They played for the same school team, but they always played against each other just for fun. You could probably call them best friends then, because they were always together. Back then Ishikawa was still the best; Hibiki was just as good as her, but there was something that always made her second best when it came to team ranking.” “Okay, get to the point I care about.” “All right, all right. Well they started growing up, and their interests started changing as fast as their bodies did. All the boys were paying attention to Hibiki, and where she started focusing on her popularity, Ishikawa kept her eye on the ball.” Scooting up on all fours, Ishikawa sat with her legs folded under her, taking her pillow and hugging it tightly against her. She rocked slowly, tucking her chin into the pillow. She wanted support… she wanted comfort… she wanted someone to be there for her. But the only person that was ever there for her was dead… and now, she felt that she should be as well. Dead… “It got to the point where Hibiki dragged Ishikawa around like a lost puppy, taking her everywhere she went trying to make her like herself. Ishikawa just didn’t get into the whole popularity deal, primetime dating and stuff like that. She was a tomboy since she was young, only interested in sports and health. All that stuff just wasn’t fun to her.” “So they went their separate ways.” “It would seem that way… but I know better.” The desk drawer opened, and she found her tool of choice. The box cutter was in her hand, her thumb sliding the blade out slowly. She used the cutter to place notches in her shin guards to show whenever she won a game, but now she’d make a notch in her throat, to show how she lost the game of life. “See… Ishikawa told me a while ago that they never really broke up. They just stopped hanging out in public. A friendship that strong could never be broken up by a simple shift in interests. She told it was more than friendship… that it was love. They loved each other like sisters, and they wouldn’t be split apart. So when Hibiki’s death came about, it probably hit Ishikawa just as hard as it hit her parents and the school… maybe more.” Nagi nodded slowly, looking around at her surroundings. They had walked almost all the way back to school while they talked. “I see. So that’s why she acted the way she did. So how do you know all this?” “I was on the soccer team with both of them back then. The three of us went to the same school. When Hibiki left the team that made me the second best, so I tried as much to be like Hibiki to make Ishikawa feel better. I guess it didn’t help too much, because Ishikawa told me to top trying because she and Hibiki were still secret friends.” Lying on the bed, Ishikawa allowed her head to lie over the edge so her neck was exposed. The blade was to her skin, the edge scratching lightly against the flakes of her skin. It would soon press deeper past the flesh and piercing her veins. Then her life would flow down her face to the floor… all making her closer to her best friend. She laid there smiling… because her dream had come true. Kurosu stooped down to pick up her soccer ball. “As sad as it is, I know Ishikawa will always love Hibiki. She’s probably at home right now crying about it, but as long as the love you have for that person remains strong, then Hibiki Jue will live forever in her heart.” Nagi stopped dead in her tracks. “What?” Kurosu looked up at her. “I said if the love in her heart stays true, then Hibiki Jue will live forever in her heart.” “Shit!” Kurosu watched as Nagi sprinted off back the way they came… back towards the cemetery. She didn’t know what came over her, but since she was so close to school, she might as well come in tardy. She’d be reprimanded and punished, but it was a meager punishment in comparison to what Nagi would’ve done to her. She was more worried about Ishikawa at home. A person with a heart so broken, so shattered… there was nothing left for them in this world besides defeat… -=-=-=- A leather jacket lay draped over the headstone of Hibiki Jue, her picture knocked away. Now this was definitely deja vu. Dirt was piled next to the grave, and still it flew from the hole in the ground. The shovel came next, landing on the picture of Hibiki and cracking the glass. Inside the hole, Nagi cracked her knuckles, wrinkling her nose as she stood over the casket of her dead classmate. She hated doing something over again; it just reeked of mundane repetition, but she was forced to check one last time on the body of Hibiki. If the rumor was true… immortality through an undying love… then Nagi would definitely be in for a shock. But then again, Nagi assumed rumors to be complete and utter bullshit. She opened the casket and waited for the dust to clear.
