Date: 02/26/2006 † In the Mind of a CRACK HEAD † When you say that it is time to retire you know that one-day you always come back to the thing that you love most. For Jason Ortiz it had been wrestling. For a year now he had been retired from the thing he loved most. A year ago if you would have asked him if he would have the desire; the pleasure to return to the ring, he would say FUCK NO. The passion for wrestling had died for Jason Ortiz after leaving Lights Out Wrestling. After losing his World Championship, by lying down. The worst thing imaginable. He hated it more than anything, To lay down to someone that didn’t deserve the title made him sick. Believe it or not it was the most logical thing that Jason could think of doing. Sure he was at his prime of his wrestling career, but a lot of people think that is the best time when you retire. When you’re one top. Not at the bottom. Ortiz couldn’t see himself a thirty-eight year old wrestler, competing with the youth. Jason Ortiz was still in his youth. Twenty-Five years old. That was still very young in the wrestling industry. He still had ten years to go before he was even eligible to retire again. So why all of a sudden would he decide to come back to wrestling again. Did he have something to prove? Was winning the LOW World Title not enough. Guess not. The thing that driven Jason Ortiz back to wrestling was the FAME. The hype of being everyone's favorite CRACK HEAD. Since his wrestling debut a few years back he had been living on that hype of a crack head. It had fueled his desire... his passion to be the best in the business. Sadly, it had worked for him. By believing that he was a crack head, that he had done this drug to make a fool out of himself and to hype of his fan base, it had made him a celebrity. That’s why he has fooled thousands of people into thinking he does this drug. Believe it or not, he has never used CRACK. It wasn’t the high of the DRUG that he was addicted to; no- it was the HYPE from it. He could get high all day being called the crack head. This was what he was all about. Now, he had the opportunity to come back to wrestling, a place called Atlantic Wrestling Club. This organization had appealed to Ortiz for quite some time. It was a smaller wrestling organization similar to LOW. He thought to himself that if he gave himself one more shot. One more fucking shot at being a champion again, he wouldn’t regret ever going into retirement again. He had nothing to lose- or did he. He was financially set. Jason Ortiz had made plenty of money being a wrestling, with endorsements, appearances, that he didn’t need the money from wrestling to make him happy. The feeling of being a champion again, and being loved by his fans brought him back. He had seen some of the wrestlers in AWC, and quite frankly- he was impressed. This would give him a challenge. Do what he does best. Be everyone’s favorite CRACK HEAD. So here he is, his first debut match at AWC’s FRESH. This would be his first opportunity to shine. To make a grand entrance, and you think for one second that he will just debut like every asshole out there. FUCK NO. Ortiz had been one hundred percent for months. He had been one hundred percent when he retired. He would be one hundred percent when his debut match came. The thing that he had to overcome was the fact that he had to tell his wife, Jessica Devine that he wanted to return to wrestling. She was glad that he was retired. He could work around the house. They had all the time to hang out and bond like husband and wife. Jason Ortiz didn’t want that shit. He wanted to be happy- that wasn’t doing it. The only thing that would make him happy was... Wrestling in AWC. Period. Location: North District. Miami, Florida Place: Jason Ortiz’s Home Time: 11:24 a.m. Jason Ortiz had been awake for some time now, thinking about how he would tell his loving girlfriend that his first match was in a few short days at AWC. Stressed out like no other, we find everyone’s favorite crack head on his white leather couch, located on the left side of the living room, which was at the west wing of the house. The heat in the room was at perfect temperature, so perfect that he was laying around in his typical white t-shirt and black board shorts. This was kind of his trade mark style. Living in Miami, Florida you had to keep the style year around. It never gets cold. As a small cold breeze rolls in from one of the side window’s in the living room, his short hair beings to feel the coldness and he lets out a sigh of relief. “Do you release that half the jobs in this paper require some sort of computer experience,” Ortiz asked, his head leaning back over the couch as he glared at his wonderful wife sitting on one of the three barstools in the kitchen. Jessica’s left leg began to switch positions in the chair as she raised on of her eyebrows at her boyfriend. You could tell that she hadn’t been happy with him in a few days. The had been fighting over the most stupidly shit. Taking the dog out to take a shit. Taking the trash out. Mowing the lawn. Jason Ortiz had always been stubborn when it comes to household chorus and the whole “HOUSEWIFE” thing. If she was suppose to be the housewife, she was suppose to do all those things right? He was the one bringing in the cash. He was the one that had bought this house. He was the one that pays the bill’s? Is that not right? Every man in the world thinks the same way that Jason thinks. Don't they? Him, reading that local newspaper in the living room and poking fun at his Jessica Devine wasn’t helping their situation out. She grinned. “You should gain some skills thing.” You could cut the tension in the air with a knife. Ortiz stared around the room as his brown Labrador retriever crossed underneath his legs. His massive legs pushed being to stop the poor dog as he reached down and began to stroke the top of his smooth head. “Skills,” he muttered, his voice began to crack. “I have many skills. You just don’t know it because you’re to fucking worried about the damn dog shit, the fucking trash and me doing everything in this fucked up house.” Jessica didn’t want him to do everything in the house. Just some of the work. She wanted to reason with Jason, she personally hated fighting with him because he always wants to be right. Like ever man in America. Ms. Devine had known that Jason was arrogant with arguments that why she avoid it. Talking to him was like talking to a brick wall- you get nowhere. The only person that was more arrogant than Jason Ortiz was his fucking O.C.D Nazi friend Steven Scott. Jessica had no idea how these two were friends. They were nothing alike. Here is Jason Ortiz, former gang member, a wrestlers, a crack head, friends with a man that was a racist Nazi bastard and hated the world around him. Steven Scott had been known for his conversation about the world. This man had claimed that he was a philosopher when it comes to politics, but he just speaks of ignorance. Jason even knew that. When Steven gets into a conversation you cant get him to stop. That’s beside the point though. The point was that Jason never shuts up and does something that he is told. He has to argue his way out of everything. But he wasn’t going to argue his way out of this. No he fucking wasn’t. Finally letting go of the dog, Jason gave his full attention to his girlfriend. “Name some kind of skills you have, and don’t say being a wrestler, nor a CRACK HEAD, we all know you are good at those things,” she continued. “Tell me something that will help you out in the real world?” Jason had to think about this for a second. The little light at the top of his head began to blink. He had a response. “I have very good social skills.” A huge smirk came upon the face of Jessica Devine. You could see that a laugh was coming on her face. “Are you fucking serious Jason,” he laugh turned into a quick chuckle. “You have good social skills. You can’t get along with anyone. Every time you meet someone, a week later they either turn into an asshole or a loser. Jason, you can’t keep a friend. Think about it. You have three friends, a Nazi, a Mexican and a drugged out old fucking redneck. How social is that?” She was kind of right. Jason Ortiz had avoided social parties. He had never gone out to clubs and meet knew people. Hell, the only friends that he did have were childhood friends that had grown up to be losers in his eyes. They all worked at dead end jobs, made minimum wage and had nothing to show for themselves. Jessica Devine stood up out of the stool as her leg began to cramp. She walked towards the living room, sort of near where Jason was previously sitting. He had now laid on arm of the couch, pushing his spine backward to crack it. That hadn‘t worked. He smiled. “I have skills...” You could see that he was thinking of something to say. Nothing clicked in his head. “I have skills in the bedroom!” snapped Jason Ortiz, his girlfriend had crawled on top of his body and gave him a kiss on his cheek. She leaned back and gave him a quick laugh before giving him another soft kiss. She didn’t want this fight to keep going, so the only way they could resolve this was to give Jason Ortiz the only thing that he loves. A piece of ass. “You’re not even good at that Jason.” “You must be kidding;” he drooled down on his left side of his lip. “Don’t tell me that I am not good in bed.” A huge smile began to show on his face. This fight seemed like it was about over, but that’s what always happens, they become friends again for a few days then... BOOM. Another fight. It was like a never-ending story. Jessica Devine lifted her body up and she was just sitting in-between Jason’s stomach, she began to press her long nails into his ribs, he scuffed a little bit. “Will you please do at least one of the chorus for me Jason?” She pleaded with him. “Please Jason, just do one thing for me.” “What’s that Jessica?” While grabbing the top of his head, she put most of her weight into her arms as Jason’s eyes began to roll over to the right side of the room. He examined the room a little bit before noticing something. A huge pile of dog shit in the middle of the carpet next to the coffee table. How did he not notice that? You would seem that the smell would reach him faster than he could think, seeing as the window’s were all open and the smell would expand faster in the room, but it had done the opposite. It had evaporated before the smell reached his nose. The look on Pretty Boy, Jason Ortiz face was unforgettable, it was almost a half grin, half shock look. It was a big pile of dog shit. He wasn’t going to lie about that. “Ummm....” you could see that Jessica was holding in her laugh. “Pick that up for me?” He took a second, and even a third look at it before saying anything. “ARE YOU SERIOUS?”
