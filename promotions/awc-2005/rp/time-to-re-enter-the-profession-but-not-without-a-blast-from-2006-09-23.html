Date: 9.23.06 It had a been a while since Wayne Russell had stepped into the squared circle on AWC television. The last time he was seen was when he watched his wife Tiara get beaten by Theresa Thomas on Coast to Coast. Where had he been many had wonder, but those numbers seemed to be out numbered by those wondering why he was even trying to make a return. During his fist tenure here in AWC Wayne did not make the greatest of impressions with his wife on national television or backstage. This time around he would only hope that it would be a better reaction from the fans and those they worked with. I guess only time will tell but they are surely wishing upon a star in that scenario. As he enters the arena for the first time in months Wayne will be dumbfounded, no not by the shock but the intensity, by the way the blood is flowing thru his body. He has wanted to get back into the ring for sometime now, just being held back for numerous reasons made it impossible, the time has come they time for retribution is upon us. Not many things these days get his blood pumping, the adrenaline flowing the sweat on his brow to start to sweat down his face; wrestling was his only glory anymore. He got the girl at a young age that was no longer an important feat for him, the money was next, it really wasn’t all that important to him, and the money came with the girl, they made plenty. What else does he have to make him look macho? What else makes him strive forward, it was such a complicated question with such a simple answer, hell it is only one word, WRESTLING. Wayne sat in the gym in the basement of his house, his muscle contracting after finishing his work out, sweat pouring down his face, breathing heavily he looks over into the corner. The corner which held his wrestling boots, he had not put those particular boots on in sometime, this week on FRESH! Was the time for him to put them on his feet again, once again he looked to achieve glory, he was wanted his name to be up there with the names of other well known wrestling tag team tandems, with only one difference. His partner was his wife, who else will make the wrestling hall of fame with a mixed gender tag team? None that he’d known of, and better yet even if they were out there they were nothing in comparison to the fact he is married to his partner, he dealt with her not only in the ring but also outside the ring. Wayne sat and pondered about numerous events that have happened in his life each thought going further and further back in history. Of course starting with the two of them making their tag team debut on television just a couple of months ago on FRESH, then he goes back to thinking the time they had in wrestling school. While everyone else went home to their significant others and spoke about the sport they loved so much to someone who cared so little, Wayne and Tiara shared the same passion, they shared the same feelings of the sport, and for each other. Wayne remembered the first night they came home from wrestling practice, they were both exhausted, but they had that look in their eyes, they loved what they were doing but they also loved each other. Wayne remembers to the minute staring at her while she ate the pasta she had cooked, and while she slowly chewed on the meatball he found himself dumbfounded and staring at his wives beauty. That’s when he realized he was doing the right thing, he reached over with his right hand placed his palm on her face and got her attention, she slowly stopped eating the returned the glare of love back to her husband, with that he leaned in for the kiss. However this kiss was like no other kiss, they had not felt this much chemistry since the first night they meet, they had not felt this type of connection to each other since the first time they had made love. The two stopped eating completely and never even paid attention to the food in front of them instead glaring into each others eyes they started to make out sending each of them into a fiery passion of love and lust. Each of them extremely exhausted but only continuing at the thoughts of each others lust. The two of them felt like teenagers engaged in each others passion only wanting for each other, not caring about anything or anyone around them. A plate of ziti slammed to the ground during their passion, neither looked down nor even flinched they were engulfed in each other; nothing was going to stop them. Slowly they made their bedroom, with each step they got hotter and hotter, making them urn for each other more and more, nothing would do them more justice then to just have each other right now, that is what their plans were, forget dinner, forget how exhausted they were, forget that they had neighbors. They wanted each other and they wanted each other right now. Wayne slowly started to play with the hair of his wife, then he grabbed the back of her legs and flung her into his arms. Never in her life had she felt as secure as she did right now, in the strong arms of her dominant husband the man whom she adored ever so much. With each step she started to puff harder, with each step she started to kiss him ever so gently but more aggressive, with each step she wanted him to herself right now. Wayne was in the same situation he carried her, felt her warmth in his arms, felt the kisses get deeper and deeper; there was nothing that was going to stop him from getting the woman of his dreams tonight, his wife. Without a hesitation he kicked his foot into the bedroom, it flung open the door, not worrying about the damage he might have caused. He thru her onto the bed and ripped of his shirt in lust, thus turning to her slowly kissing her neck and removing any garments she has on her upper body leaving the others intact for now. The two of them were remember what it was like to be back in high school, two kids in the back of a car having crazy hot sex hoping to not get caught, only difference now is they are married and they are actually adults. The two of them paying no attention to the surroundings, they have both lost all control of their feelings and are letting them be shown instead of verbally more physically at this moment. The two engaged in the most passionate night of sex they have ever had since the first they actually first encountered each other. They did things that blew each others minds, both exhausted but neither wanting to stop. The feelings they were having about each other were more then sexual they were sensual they were the feelings they really had for each other but instead of expressing them with a simple “I love you.” Each of them expressed it physically, each wanting and urging for more, trying to push each other to each others limits. Until the moment both had the greatest feeling they had ever had in their existence. Once the moment past they both laid there on their backs staring at he ceiling, gasping for each breath, they said nothing but the feelings were on the table; they lay holding each other till morning. “Wayne!!!” Tiara screams from up stairs… with out a response she screams down the stairs again, “Dinner is ready.” Wayne shakes his head, he remembers what it used to be, when they were in training not when they were on the road all the time and living their lives in a abnormal situation. They for some forsaken reason were home getting ready but he still wished they could be back in their youth, he wants to relive his past, his love his lust. “Ok Honey be right up,” Wayne is quick with his answer, brunt and straight forward but that is not how he feels. He wishes that when he goes up stairs he will see that plate of ziti, start eating and relive the moment the just reminisced about, but will it happen is his only question. He can only hope, but he feels the passion has been lost, not just for their love life but also for her love of the sport.
