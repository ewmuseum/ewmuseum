Special Blend

28 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/06 “It was all just a dream, yet it seemed so real” Neurotic mumbles to himself. He stumbles from his bed and staggers across the small room of his apartment to a half broken down recliner chair in the corner. He plops down in the chair and lies back recounting the events from his dream. “Hey Mister, I gotta special deal for you tonight!” comes the ragged voice of an old man standing alone in the darkened alley. Neurotic turns to face him and the man is hidden in the shadows, and his location only generalized by the sound of the voice echoing through the dark. “It’s a real doozy!” Neurotic: Yah? Whatcha got for me tonight old timer? The old man comes limping from the shadows and grinning ear to ear. “Let’s just say it’s something that will change the way you do things. It will give you a whole new outlook on life.” Even though Neurotic had never had any interactions with this man before, he felt like he knew him. Like he was able to trust him. Neurotic stands there staring at the old man wrapped in rags and missing the majority of his teeth waiting for him to speak again. “Names James” the old man stammers. Neurotic stares silently not revealing that they share the same name. The old man extends a hand that Neurotic only stares at. James: Well let me get right down to my proposition. The old man reaches into his coat, causing Neurotic to get a bit uneasy, and pulls out a small vial of something. It is a solid steel vial, so you can’t see what is inside of it. Neurotic stares at it for a sec then starts to ask the old man what it is, but is cut off by the mans sales pitch. James: THIS is the FINEST powder your money could EVER buy you. The powder in here will have you flying so high you will not only feel no pain, but you will also never want to come down. Neurotic: Cocaine. I’m not interested. James: Don’t be silly boy. Did you hear what I said? Feel NO PAIN. You are a wrestler, are you not? Neurotic: How did you… James: I know more then you could possibly imagine. Anyways, the powder in this vial, is special. It is a blend of my own doing. It gives you a never ending high. The dose in this vial will make it so you NEVER come down, and you NEVER feel any pain. How unstoppable would you be, to never know pain? Neurotic: Not interested. James: Don’t be stupid lad! 1 sniff, and you can be the greatest of all time. You could fly to the heavyweight contenders spot. You could be the name in lights above Madison Square. Neurotic stops for a second to recall the things he just said. He starts thinking out loud. Neurotic: Well, one time couldn’t hurt…and champion, I could definitely live with that. He stares back at the grinning old man, and the vial. Neurotic: How much? James: That’s where the “Special deal” comes in… Neurotic: How special? James: Special enough to be free… Neurotic: What’s the catch? James: No catch. If you recall I said it was my own special blend, so I am giving away a few small samples to people I can watch, without having to run it like a clinic. I’m no doctor after all. I can watch you on television and see the progress. Neurotic: Well that certainly sounds like a deal…. James: Indeed…. The old man shoves the vial out at Neurotic who takes it in his hand and holds it up against the moonlight, catching a glint off the side of the small steel container. Neurotic starts to twist the top off the container but is stopped by the elder man. James: Wait, not here. Let’s go somewhere a little more private. The old man scampers back into the shadows leaving Neurotic to make chase. They weave through a couple more alleys and into an old warehouse. Nobody had been in this warehouse for ages, aside from the old man. There is a makeshift table setup with a small glass mirror on it. There is a gas mask of sorts laying on the table as well. James: Here… Neurotic: What’s with the mask? James: I don’t want to ingest any chemicals so I can be clear headed for my research. Neurotic: Makes sense I guess…. James: Of course it does, now have a seat. James and Neurotic sit at the table for a second as the old man straps on the gas mask. Neurotic uncaps the vial and pours the contents out on the mirror. Neurotic: I’ve never done this…. James: Nothing to it, here…. James scrapes the contents into a shallow line, and hands Neurotic a little yellow straw piece. Neurotic leans over the table and in one sniff inhales the contents. He rocks back a bit, eyes wide open, rubbing his nose. He squints a few times, then stares at the old man, who is fading from focused to blurry. Neurotic: Is it supposed to sting…actually it damn right burns…. James: Oh yes, this blend does that to you. Neurotic: I’m feelin…dizzy… James: Yes, you will. Just lay back and let it work. Neurotic stares as the room starts spinning and everything fades to black, then back to focus, then back to black, and back into focus again. Neurotic: I don’t think this stuff is working right… The old man peels off what appeared to be his face, but was in fact only a mask and standing in front of him, is Trent Marshall. Neurotic stares wide eyed. Trent: It’s working fine Neurotic…you see that special blend, was merely ricin. Note te mask I was wearing. It was nice knowing you. Neurotic collapses in a heap, and everything fades to black…. Neurotic opens his eye again sitting in his plush recliner… Neurotic: Only a dream….
