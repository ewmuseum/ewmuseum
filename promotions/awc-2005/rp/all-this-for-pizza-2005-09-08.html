All This For Pizza!?!

8 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-8-05 ***Lunch Time*** I sat leaned back in my comfy recliner chair, lost in a world of my own as I fingered the handheld game system clinched in my palms. 'Grygruugh!' Ah I hate it when he does that! What happened to him in prison that would make him just up and quit talking? 'Ghost, I know your hungry. Your always hungry for something, be it food or just pounding on someone.' 'Hreughjop!' 'Okay, okay just give me five more minutes of video game time. Deal?' 'Gughj...' Tetris is so unrealistic, ever had the feeling you're in a computer game? Ever thought that your life is controlled from above somehow? Ever wondered why there are so many falling colorful cubes? 'Grythioto!' 'Yeah, me neither. But seriously, aren't the computer games getting more and more realistic? It's only a matter of time before we can't tell apart reality from virtual reality. And that will be the day when reality will be virtually indistinguishible from virtual reality, which in turn will be virtually similar to reality. It will be the day when you will have to sneak around as a Delta Force soldier, which may generate a cultural clash with the local police force. Or you may get an urge to do a loop around the golden gate bridge, under-over-under... oh come on, I know you've tried that in your flight simulator! And then when you're tired of doing these things, you might just run off to a huge green field to build a city from scratch, or perhaps you decide to declare war against the romans, only because you screwed up and they built the great library first. But think about this, what will you do... ...when the first colored cubes start falling down? Now, how will you handle that?' 'Juirdhy...' What else can I do but shrug my shoulders at Ghost failure to develope a working imagination? But then again maybe that's all life is to him now is just one big video game. Maybe he can't speak because he is too enthralled with the beaming lights and the undercover operatives knocking at his minds door. 'Nah that's crazy talk...let's eat!' 'Ghthjbshiop!' 'Pizza it is bro, pizza it is.' ***Chow Time*** As Ghost and I entered 'Bigsby's' a grocery mart by our house I noticed something...the store was dead. Usually customers filed in and out all day long, but because of the heat, everyone decided to stay in their air-conditioned rooms. The hotel just across the street provided many customers for the convenience store. The attendant on duty, an iranian fellow, sat behind the counter reading a magazine from the newspaper stand. Finally a customer walked in, a tall blonde-haired man, around thirty-five. The iranian fellow behind the counter took note of these details of all the people when they walked in because you never know what the next criminal is going to look like. The blonde man walked up and down the aisles looking for something but really nothing in particular. I noticed that behavior pattern before. This chump was displaying all the telltale signs of a potential hold up. Me and Ghost sat back admiring the pizza heating up in the display case as the blonde man worked his way to the back of the store. Another customer walked in. An older woman... Her hair was put up in a scarf and she lugged a shopping cart with her, the kind that elderly people use when they shop for groceries by themselves. Nothing was in the cart except for a brown paper bag, probably goods from another store. A sensible black purse hung on her shoulder. That awful old people smell crept up my nose grasping my apetite for pizza and tossing it out of my stomach. Finally the blonde man made his decision and approached the counter with his purchase. He placed two apples onto the counter. At first I was taken back by this. I didn't know why but then the iranian fellow rang up the guys order and reached underneath the counter for a paper bag. When he emerged from under the counter, he stood face to face with a .40 caliber Berreta. The blonde man held the gun to the iranian fellows face, his arm fully extended. Poor sonuva b**ch prolly hadn't been trained to handle himself in this kind of situation, his mind was prolly a complete blank from the pressure of the moment. The blonde man demanded all the money in the register in the paper bag, along with the apples. The hunger rolled off of his voice. Guess maybe I should let Ghost do something about it. Nudging Ghost he was too taken in by the smell of pizza sauce and pepperoni to listen to me. Ah well Crucifix to the rescue I suppose. The iranian fellow opened the register and transferred the paper bills and coins into the bag. Realizing that what was in the register was more than what I made in a month thoughts raced through my mind, no wait...I'm suppose to stop not aid this a**hole. As he placed the money into the paper bag, I approached from behind..quite as a kid at christmas time. SMASH!!! Down goes jolly ol' saint nick with a bottle of cheap vodka. The elderly lady shuttles her cart out of the store and the cashier start mumbling something about Alah or some sh*t. 'Ghost get your damn pizza and let's go before the police show up.' He grabs as many slices as he can with his bear claws of hands as well as carrying one in his mouth...makes me laugh inside seeing him so happy about such little things. 'Ghjrhkud!' 'Yeah I know buddy, let's head to the gym and work on our move's. Been awhile since we had any ring time and were up against the champs ya know.' 'Terjhhsaujo!' 'Yeah I know you don't need any practice, it's for me. I just need you there for...let's say moral support.' ***Training*** BAM!!! 'Sunuva*** I forgot how much this stuff hurts. Try and take it easy on me ya big loof!' 'Frysjkhduy!' He shrugs like a little child not knowing what he did wrong. Takes me back...back when mom and dad were still alive. Before the night they were taken from us. Would I still be the outcast I am now if they hadn't died...would Ghost be talking more fluently than I am now? What if is all you can say...what if. 'Okay bro let's try it one more time. Now remember, you have to catch me and spin me so that I can kick our opponent coming off the top rope okay?' 'Roroadsjf...' 'Um ok let's try it one more time for the books.' Climbing the turnbuckle I can already feel the smack of the canvas against my skin. If only I could understand more or better yet make him understand better...and off I go. 'YES! That's it buddy, just like that.' 'Fruehdu!' 'Awesome stuff Ghost!' Again he bounces around springing rope to rope. I can't see but I am almost definate that he is smiling like crazy underneath his mask. Whew...talk about needing a breather. 'Okay Ghost, let's take ten. Come on over here and get some water.' 'Hurhuhsu.' Sitting at the bench press systems I pull the sheet of paper that the speech therapist gave me to help Ghost. It is the most ridiculous thing I have ever said but hey if it helps my brother who gives a damn, right? 'Ghost, wanna try your words today?' 'Ghsgytd.' 'We'll take it nice and slow, ok.' 'Jurrybyhd!' 'Okay here we go.' Reading from the crinkled sheet of paper my vocal cords project the words: 'I know. I know that I know. I know that I know that I know. You might know, also. I know that you might know. Do you know? Do you know that I know? Aha! Yes! I know that you know that I know! You must know this, now that I know that I told you. What? And, you already know? You know that I know that you know? How do you know? Now, enough of us know that we can say we know that we know! We know that we know! They don't know. They don't even know that we know that they don't know! What if they know? Do they know? Do they know that we know that they know? They don't know that we know that they know the we know! I know! What do you know?' 'Gyrhjdg know, kjkd I nkdow. Know jhggy i ghrjd' 'Nice bro, we'll be talking in no time flat.' 'Fhygrhd.' 'Hungry again? Where does all that food go man?' 'Dofunde.' 'Yeah we'll get something to eat, but if someone sticks up the place you better help me out. Not that I needed it last time or anything.' 'Riofhdtk!' 'Your crazy, I took that guy out like yesterday's garbage. Haha. Come on let's go home, we'll grab some snacks on the way.' 'FHGftd!' It's funny...the circumstances that brought us two together here weren't that pleasant but now that were here and I'm focusing my energy towards something good, I feel we have hope. Hope. That's a word I thought would never come out of my mouth. -Fin
