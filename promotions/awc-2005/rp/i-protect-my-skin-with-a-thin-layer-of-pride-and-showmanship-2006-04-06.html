I protect my skin with a thin layer of pride and showmanship.

6 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: april 6/06 Very few times in my life do I find myself sitting alone at night, crying to the point that I could throw up. I'm not in the throes of a passion gone wrong. I'm not upset. I'm not without. I'm actually quite alright. The only thing that could drive me to create this situation is reflection. My day started out with an appointment to inject a chemical into tiny scars on my arms from a childhood illness and a lot of different injuries, scrapes, cuts and even a stab wound. I wouldn't do this, as I'm horribly phobic of medical needles, but I look ridiculous with any degree of tan thanks to those scars, and that's my excuse. The reality is that I'd never go through a pain so bad as having seventy skin-deep needle injections in my arms just to make myself look somehow perfect. If I wanted perfect skin, why would I get tattoos? These needles, the injections and the pain are a necessity if not only because laser surgery won't fix these scars to the point I want. I don't want to be able to see them, but I don't do it because of my bad tan, I do it because I have the mental scarring to go right alongside the little white dots and lines in my skin. I don't need the physical reminder of every bad or painful thing that's happened to me. Why would someone put up with that? The reason for my tears is reflection. When I have a breakdown that has no current application, it usually stems back to a day or two of touching back on my past. I face everything I've done and had done to me. I sob as if I were dying. In reality, looking past years and problems directly in the eye lets me truly face it. With this painful recollection, I am free. Every time I do this, a part of who I was rots away from the grand landscape my life has become. A lot of people have been talking shit. They like to poke at what they don't have, I guess it makes them feel closer to it or some sick thing. The hobby of some tweaks is to try painting people as fools for caring about others when, quite honestly, I'm not sure if you can be a fool for love. I know that's a little out of place, but I pondered it throughout the day. You can't know love until you've thrown yourself at it like a maniac again and again. Then, love ends up throwing something back at you. Something good. If you catch and can run with that treasure carefully tucked under your arm - giving it breathing room, but maintaining grip - until you reach your life's end zone, you're in love; Dealing with the real thing once you have the equipment to play the game. It's a hit and miss situation within which you're guaranteed to fail at least a million times, but if your fingertips are calloused enough to stop and hold that one perfect pass, it's all worth it in hindsight. Sure, that's a little heart-heavy, but I've never been one to back down from raw thought or speech just because some jerkoffs on the internet wouldn't approve and might... dear God... USE THE EVER-SO-STINGING MEDIUM OF TEXT TO MOCK ME! If it's in the New York Times, I'll pay attention - but if I'm reading something on a forum populated by kids who don't have the capacity to understand more than Jackass: The Movie, I'm not taking it to heart. They can sink back into their college routines and continue experimenting with drugs for the sake of bragging rights, I really don't have any vested interest in the lives of the lifeless. Back to my day. After lunch with old friends and a rather odd situation where I stumbled upon old photos on a digital camera card I hadn't used since I'd bought a bigger stick two years ago, I wasn't in any mood for thinking or talking about my past. Darcy decided that we were going to have a night out. A night in Hollywood, of all places. Heat of the Jungle------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Club Tokio 11:54 PM He couldn't help but smile - he'd missed this. Not so much spending time with Amy. The two, save for some spats here and there, had been practically inseparable as of late, so much so that close friends on both sides were beginning to find it sick. Rather, the man bearing the sheepish smile couldn't help but let it creep across his face because of the commotion in front of him. Looking over at his company, Darcy Markson noted that Amy looked, at best, uncomfortable. Despite her penchant for finding herself in the spotlight, usually due most directly to her explosive temper and impulsive nature, Amy Campbell never handled having eyes on her with the greatest of ease. Darcy, on the other hand, couldn't get enough of it; the ever persistent dreams of a thirteen-year-old desperate for attention were what led Darcy to wrestling in the first place - to have all eyes on him. The past few years that he'd spent out of the limelight made awestruck fans pointing and whispering to each other merely at the sight of his presence occur far less frequently. But with his career resurrected and dominance restored in AWC (not to mention that he now found himself in league with Amy, who never seemed to stay out of the headlines), the so-called 'attention whore' in Darcy Crisis could do nothing but sit back and enjoy himself. Amy, on the other hand, was having a bit more trouble. While Darcy was seeing nothing but glory, she was ducking her head and nodding sheepishly, shaking and slapping the hands of the stream of people. Every few seconds, someone in the crowded club would recognize them and sit down, or lean near them for a moment. Every time was almost the same, 'Dude, you guys are those wrestlers.', 'Hey, it's Aimz!', and the occasional passerby who claimed to have partied with her 'back in the day'. Most likely, those who knew her were at the club to peddle cocaine, but that didn't occur to Markson. Amy didn't like hearing the same things over and over, especially when they were mixed with semi-sarcastic barbs about the spectacle and circus of the business she was in. Life is a side show. When you're Aimz, you have to grin and bear the fact that people either treat you like a circus acrobat or a threat to their personal safety - sometimes both at once. She could easily see that Darcy was loving every second of the attention - of being recognized once more as 'Holy shit dude, you've been kicking ass!' - but Amy's discomfort was growing more present with every passing moment. While envisioning what setting fire to the entire room might do, she turned to Darcy in hopes of engaging a conversation that would act as a temporary buffer between onlookers and the two. It was Hollywood, after all, and Good Charlotte were known to frequent the club, so shouldn't the sickeningly trendy scenesters have better people to guffaw over? She squeezed his arm lightly and leaned into his ear, drink firmly gripped, ''Hey... what do you think most of these people are thinking right now?'' ''I'll tell you what half the guys in this room are thinking about...'' Darcy said, turning to look at her. ''They're probably working out how to get you to go to bed with them. Although I doubt any of them have worked it out past step one, which is hitting me in the back of the head with a beer bottle.'' Amy smirked, ''They'd at least need an Absolut bottle, much thicker. It'd really club the fuck outta you. But maybe we should turn in a little early, huh? My friends haven't showed up at all, and these tattooed guys are actually starting to freak me out. I don't know if it's possible to be raped by stares, but it's starting to seem like a realistic concept...'' Darcy seemed less than enthused by the idea, since they'd just stood in a lineup for a half hour to get in, so the little redhead decided to take her chances with the crowd... ''Alright, nevermind that, then. I'm gonna hit the bar again... need anything?'' He simply shook his head and kept a hand on her lower - lower back - as she stood up and sauntered into the crowd. Feminist as she could be, Amy was amused by Darcy's very firm gestures of territory. She crossed a few crowded couches and coffee tables before glancing back at him, in all of his glory, looking like the king of the world, so proud in their seat. She didn't have to worry about women recognizing him - no girl in California would be caught dead watching wrestling - but they certainly seemed to be casting him interesting looks the moment she was out of attacking range. It didn't sit well, but Amy proceeded to the bar and ordered another Kamikaze. ''Hey, it's that macho TV chick... whatsherface, the one with the boobs and the cute boyfriend.'' Apparently women in California did occasionally catch a glimpse of wrestling... but Amy tried hard to ignore the obvious words aimed her way. ''Isn't she, like... one of those rollerderby girls?'' ''Nah, my little brother watches it all the time. She's a wrestler... you know, a wrassler.'' ''I wonder if she knows The Rock?!'' ''I wonder where the cutie she's always on TV with is. He's a hot ti--'' Amy's ears couldn't completely shut off. She left her drink on the bar for a moment and turned around to interrupt one of the typical Hollywood girls, ''I know it's hard to see anything behind all of that cover-up and eye shadow, but I can hear you.'' ''Uh, excuuuse me Miss... uh... Chyna, is it?'' one remarked, ''We were having a private conversation, so if you'll just...'' Another howled, ''Oh, she went there!'' ''C'mon, we're taller than this 'wrassler' girl... maybe we she wants us to pick on ugly skanks our own size.'' ''Anyone could be six feet tall if they wanted to subject themselves to those ungodly heels you've strapped on,'' Amy sneered, ''Speaking of ridiculously overt exaggeration of the body... shouldn't you be trolling around for men?'' An obvious leader had emerged in the girl who'd just likened Aimz to Chyna. Despite Amy being the shape, height and build of a normal woman, the 'pro wrestler' schtick never failed to stick to her, and women loved to use that. ''We're talking to you, so it's close enough! Where's your boyfriend, anyway? I think he'd like a woman he didn't have to break his back bending over to kiss.'' Amy shuddered and steeled herself against the snickers coming from the threesome of blondes and a brunette with their attitudes and fake breasts, ''Come on... we're all girls here, and I don't need to leave with your ovaries hanging on my rearview mirror - so how about you leave the slutty passes at the guy I'll be going home with out of this and find one of these drunk, hotdogged scene boys to lick your clit while you watch Sex In The City reruns later?'' ''You won't be goin' home with him once I grind up on him. And I see exactly where he's sitting now!'' With her team of blondes in tow, the girl adjusted her open-necked top and turned her back on Amy, strutting in the direction of the man Campbell would give her life to keep. A few people had taken notice of the conflict and patted Amy on the shoulders as her ears and cheeks flushed with blood. ''I fuckin' hate getting arrested for such petty shit!'' But she couldn't help herself, and followed after the girl who Darcy had just noticed was walking with an intent to pounce him. He looked disgusted at first, but grew alarmed when he saw a streak of red hair just behind the shoulder of said hussy. Room behind the girl began to clear when it became evident that Amy had already floored her two friends and was waiting for the perfect moment to pounce on the head girl... who was STILL headed for Darcy. That moment came when the woman - who Darcy was looking past to mouth 'No fighting!' to Amy - got within a few feet of the man they called Crisis. That was when the real crisis began. The girl invited herself to sit beside him, in the seat Amy had vacated only minutes before. That was also when she was turned around enough to notice that the redhead had been behind her the whole time. A quick shout of 'FIIIIIGHT!' broke out from a few of the drunken clubgoers when Amy grabbed the girl by the throat. She squoze until the girl could only whimper. ''YOU. Do not. Touch. Him. Clear?'' Then she let go... and drove her sneaker into the seated girl's gut. ''You... do not ever speak to someone like you just did,'' she growled, kicking the girl again, ''Especially when they can take their oh-so-fake 'wrasslin' moves and beat the life out of you. Finally...'' Amy was taking off her own necklace, and wound it tightly around a closed fist. That was when Darcy saw a very, very grave problem approaching and shot out to grab Amy before she did something she - and her criminal record - would very seriously regret. At the same time, an apparent friend of the assaulted women had entered the scene... and he wasn't little, or very happy. Darcy assumed he was just another member of the crowd trying to hold the murderous redhead back from the beaten girl, but that was a BIG mistake. Once the guy punched Amy in the mouth in honor of his friend, she threw her fist - chain necklace and all - into his nose. ''I'll fuckin' KILL you!'' was her warcry, breaking free from her shocked boyfriend and pouncing on the downed man - her new target. Blood appeared on every fist as it rose into the air, and she was soon pulled back by members of the crowd. Apparently the party planners hadn't exactly booked security. Actually, the guy she just shattered the face of was security... but it didn't stop him from screaming obsceneties. ''GET YOUR WHORE OUTTA HERE!'' he roared at Darcy, trying to get into a sitting position, ''Or my boys'll deal with that dirty gutterslut. UNDERSTAND?!'' Darcy's eyes shot open wide as saucers on hearing that. Stepping between Amy and the downed guard, he pulled the man back to his feet with one swift tug. Their faces now inches away, Darcy spoke softly. ''You use choice words with my lady friend again, Hoss, your boys will have to deal with THIS gutterslut.'' With that, Darcy palmed his broken nose and shoved him back toward the crowd. Amy made one last attempt to cause some damage, but Darcy turned to stop her, and with his grip on her restored he escorted her out of the establishment. The car ride back to their hotel was filled with little more than Amy rambling about how she 'So had them all'. Darcy was entirely silent, UNTIL they got to the room. That was when the adrenaline started wearing off, and his redhead was a little calmer... The War Room------------------------------------------------------------------ ''That was actually kinda hot,'' she chirped, heading for the mini-bar, ''I never feel all girly and shit... but you totally defended my honor, or what little honor I have left.'' ''If some creep made a beeline for your pants I'd have done the exact same thing. That's what I call honor...'' Darcy said, silently chuckling to himself that there was no way to say that without sounding corny as hell. ''C'mon though, lay off the mini-bar...that shit's expensive, and you're already pretty loaded up.'' Amy grimaced, then laughed as she reached for a bottle of white wine, ''At least we're not sitting around, blanketed in weed. I'd rather be drunk... gives me fun match ideas.'' ...Which may have been exactly why she always managed to get badly injured. That aside, though, she continued, ''I've got Cap'n Sully this week at the house show. I think he wants to get back at me for the pay per view, since it was apparently 'unprofessional conduct' on my part. Either way, that jerkoff bugs me with his 'I'm against Americans!'. I'm the only one around here who rebels against specific groups, damnit! PCW, men, morons and now every fuckbag who holds a title they don't deserve. This guy's moving in on already cliche and signature territory. That, and I just wanna punch him until his mouth's too swollen to open. I've got too quick a temper, though, and management isn't really approving of it. If tonight gets me sued, fighting Suleimon might be my last chance at a win for a while. Wine?'' The redhead attempted to pop the cork out of the wine sloppily with her teeth before relinquishing it in an offer to Darcy, trying to cover that she was actually unable to open it and hoped he'd accept the offer so she didn't have to do the deed herself. Darcy glared at her for several moments before accepting the bottle. Maybe it won't be so bad if I get just as tanked as she does... he pondered. Moving his thoughts to other matters, he answered her previous request with a shrug. ''I dunno... whatever happened to old-fashioned beat-the-crap-outta-the-other-guy matches? I hate being told how to kick another man's ass.'' ''I started in AWC taking a barbed wire-wrapped bat everywhere, and it worked until they told me to stop. Then stipulations got added to matches and, blah... they like opponents to REALLY hate one another, but won't let us go all out half the time,'' Amy replied, ''And your pay per view match was the last thread in the leash management has on us. People they like - like, say, Nash or Dick... even Sully - see, they get the velvet ropes around them. I can't go into an arena and tell Ellis Nash I want the title she jumped on a bandwagon and played her cards cheaply to earn, and I certainly can't beat the ever-loving snot out of her. They're not gonna let me do ANYTHING I want to - even though it's not televised - to the Captain at this house show, and it's complete garbage. It's so annoying to have to worry about who you're hurting, and how badly as if it's going to affect your career,'' she continued, but not before sipping down half of her wine bottle and reclining against the headboard of the bed, ''It shouldn't. I shouldn't have to worry about managing a fight and a god damn political campaign.'' Darcy rolled his eyes, ''Amy, please....what are they gonna do, fire you? Suspend you? Believe me, if I know anything about the fans of AWC, it's that they don't respect no-talent ass clowns who spend all their time stoned off their gourds babbling on about tropical fruit as their champions. They respect sadistic, hardcore, take-no-prisoners and spare-no-mercy violent nutcases... such as yourself.'' ''Aww, you know just what to say,'' she chuckled, ''But really... what do I do? Kill Suleimon at this house show and use that leverage to go demand a title shot? Seriously, I'm not used to thinking past the part where I beat the hell outta somebody at any cost...'' Her partner mulled over the question for several moments, taking a long swig of the wine to keep up with Amy in the process. She looked unusually nervous at the prospect of going full-tilt for everything. ''Normally,'' he said, ''I'd say back it slightly away from KILLING the man, as another thing AWC fans don't respect is when their future champions aren't around to defend the title because they're facing a lawsuit from the family of the deceased. But in Sully's case... he should have known better than to step in the ring with you and not expect to get hurt. I guess some assholes have to learn everything the hard way.'' Amy sighed, reflecting sleepily on the sudden fire that had been lit under both of them - in a wrestling sense, of course. ''You know, once we start this all-out attitude, we're stuck with it. We can't rest for a second. I've really gotta clean up and get consistant again, really start taking out anything in my way instad of having one good week and two lazy, off weeks. You... you're already there. You barely lose. I'm the one doing the adjusting here. Are we seriously ready for the drama coming our way if we go full-tilt for everyone and anything they've got?'' ''That's really not the question to be asking. I know full well what I'm capable of, both in and out of the ring. I damn sure know what you can do. Drama is for little bitches who can't sweat it when life throws them a curveball or two. We're better than that. Besides, it won't matter much when it's the two of us standing at the top of the mountain, with a pile of broken bodies and crushed dreams all around us. The real question is... are they going to be able to handle it?'' She took a final sip of wine... ''This is gonna get messy.'' No holds barred, all out and absolute. I think it's time to check what I'm really made of.
