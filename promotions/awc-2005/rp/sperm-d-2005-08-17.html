SPERM'd~!

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/17/05 [The scene opens outside a barnyard. The Giant Sperm floats over and sees some cows.] Giant Sperm: HAHAHA! Feeble mortals! You are no match for the Giant Sperm! [The Giant Sperm cackles evilly.] Giant Sperm: I am the Giant Sperm and not even your eggs are safe from me! [The Giant Sperm looks at the cows.] Giant Sperm: Cows are no match for me! [The Giant Sperm then floats over and starts knocking the cows over before floating back to where the camera is at.] Giant Sperm: You see! I can beat up cows and now, I am going to beat up wrestlers from all over the world! [The Giant Sperm smiles, his tail wagging like he's some sort of happy dog.] Giant Sperm: You are no match for me! I can swim up your girlfriend's river in 10 minutes flat! Nothing you have can stop me! Bwahahahahahahahahahahaha! [The Giant Sperm starts twirling around and then stops to look at the camera.] Giant Sperm: I will impregnate your eggs and then, I will create a bunch of half-man, half-monster creatures that will help me take over the world! [The Giant Sperm cackles maniacally.] Giant Sperm: Who can stop me? Ian English? Please. Kris CarMichael? HA! I went into his girlfriend's egg last night, so there's no way he can stop me! James von Doom? [The Giant Sperm starts laughing hysterically.] Giant Sperm: What a fucking loser! William Curr? PLEASE! He is nothing compared to the Giant Sperm! [The Giant Sperm smiles deviously.] Giant Sperm: 'Insane' Chris West? Eliminator? Grobschnit? GIVE ME A BREAK! I will destroy them all and then go on to rule the world! Bwahahahahahahahahahahaha! [The Giant Sperm then looks demented as he stares into the camera. He then lunges forward and starts to penetrate the camera. Soon, the Giant Sperm has gotten totally inside and impregnated the camera's egg. The Giant Sperm then exits the camera as a half-man, half-camera creature is born. He then smiles and swims back up your girlfriend's river as the scene fades out.] [Cut.]
