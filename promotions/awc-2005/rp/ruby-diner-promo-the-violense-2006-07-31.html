Ruby Diner Promo: The 'Violense'?

31 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/06 The camera opens up displaying Tim Martin, sitting in his apartment. He is staring into the camera, intently, fiercly…and etc. “An odd occurrence, no? A Martin, directly addressing the fans in a manner of a promo, yes? I was shocked, too, when told I would NEED to do this in order to even be in CONTENTION to beat Vince Jones at Huntington Beach. To be honest, I hadn’t a clue on what to say.” “So, before you know it, I was ready to pull myself from this match, to not even WANT to compete. I mean, why make an ass of myself when it’s not necessary? I looked at my options, and shaming a White Lord by speaking truths and talking trash and all that, it wasn’t in the cards.” “Things changed, though. And all it took was a visit to the local corner bodega. It was your average trip, buying a pack of Marlboros because, obviously, my other pack ran out, but I ran into the unexpected, an innocence, rather, of a little girl. A little black girl, too. At first, I ignored her, as she did no wrong to me, but then I noticed she was wearing an AWC shirt!” Tim leans back in his chair, shaking his head. “Upon further inspection, though, it turns out to be a Vince Jones shirt! My God, I said, no wonder he has such a fan base, who else are the niggers going to cheer for, Anton Assault? The fucker can barely speak English, never the less ebonics! Good luck trying to get a fan base!” “But that’s off topic, back to the shirt. You see, I don’t mind Vince Jones selling shirts. Sure, he’s dirt, and sure, his mouth sounds frighteningly familiar to two ass-cheeks flapping from the hot air shooting out, but if he sells shirts, he sells shirts. The White Lord may not approve, but there’s not much He can do, as America is a free country. But what REALLY got my goat is what it said. Let me hold up a shirt, I even went out of my way to buy one…” Tim holds up a shirt. On it is a picture of Vince Jones, but the word “Violence” is spelled “Violense”. “Yes, you read it right. Our man Jones can’t even have his shirts spell his name correctly. At first, I laughed this off. I figured Jones must’ve dropped out of school and started selling drugs like most of the other niggers did, and I put it in the back of my head. But then it started getting to me. Something that big, the spelling of his name, and he can’t even make sure it’s right? My God, is our Countdown Trophy holder that thick?” Tim pauses for a moment, taking a few deep breaths. “But the thickness, that’s not the problem. It’s the matter of the fact that the man is holding a coveted prize in the league, the Trophy. When people see the league, they see Vince Jones, holding the Trophy, and they laugh. One of our stars, one of our faces…endorses a t-shirt that can’t even spell his nickname right? Is that what type of league we are, one that rewards the half-asses?” “So I walk into this match with a mission. I don’t give a fuck about any of the other assholes, to be honest. Garbage Fag Bonnie, Anton Assault, Lacuna Beach, whoever else is in it, fuck them. To me, they are nothing. If Vince Jones continues to hold this trophy, continues to maintain his dominance, AWC will close down. Not because of poor performance, not because of anything other than lack of credibility. Because, let’s be honest, how seriously can you take someone who can’t even think that of them self?” “Some of you will ask, hey, does Vince even know this shirt exists? And to that, I say, what’s the importance? Why hasn’t anyone told that little black girl to not wear it? Why hasn’t anyone ever said to her ‘Hey, your fucking shirt is stupidly spelled wrong?’ So, instead, I asked. I asked why she keeps on wearing it, even though the typo is so horrendous. And you know what she said?” “Because Vince Jones in my hero.” Tim smirks. “So when I crush Vince Jones, when I show him what ‘Violense’ really is, it won’t just be a trophy I’ll win. It’ll be the satisfaction that I’m destroying another half-assing human being…” Tim pauses. “Oh, no, I’m sorry…another half-assing *nigger*, and I'll be destroying all the niggers who look up to the ultimate one, the ultimate spec of dirt. But really, Vince, are you any different than the rest? Because to these eyes, no matter how famous you are, no matter how big your name is, no matter how many fans you have or how smooth talking you are, you're just another nigger, just another spec of dirt.” Tim shakes his head one final time. “Vince, may God have mercy on your soul.” ---([([END FEED])])---
