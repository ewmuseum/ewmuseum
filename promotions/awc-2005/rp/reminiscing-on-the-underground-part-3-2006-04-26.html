Reminiscing on the Underground (Part 3)

26 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/26/2006 The camera slowly fades back into the Manchester News Arena where Vince Jones can be seen sitting on the mat leaning against the turnbuckle in the corner of the ring with his head bowed still in thought and contemplation. 'Its amazin' what a man will do to get by in the game, in life, in whateva. Does it amaze V. Jones that he did all he did to get by and move up back in the NYUFC days? Nah. Hell nah. If V. Jones had to do it all ova he'd do all that shit all ova again in a heartbeat. It was worth it...' Vince closes his eyes. {{Flashback}} The camera switches to the inside of a small gym where other fighters are gathered around lifting weights and training in order to prepare for their respective NYUFC prize fights. The camera pans around the scene and catches sight of Vince Jones who can be seen sitting on a bench doing dumbell curls at the moment. All of a sudden the man we have come to know as Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright walks past Vince and looks down at him and sneers with disgust at his presence. Vince looks up and glares right back at him as he passes him by. Jack Wright: How the hell Big Lex gonna allow a piece of street trash like that to come into my gym. This is some bullshit! Hmph! Whatever! '...Anything to get his hands on Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright the source of all of V's problems back then. He was the man that represented everything that V wanted to be in due time. Wright was the man that sat on top of the mountain lookin' down. That's where V wanted to be and to get there he eventually had to eliminate The Hellraiser...' All of a sudden Big Lex comes walking into through the doors of the small gym with a large grin on his face as he spots Jack Wright approaching him. Big Lex: Ahhh! Just the man I've been looking for, Jack Wright. Come with me. We've gotta talk about a few things. I've got some big plans for you and believe me they're gonna be huge. Big Lex puts his arm around Jack Wright's shoulder and leads him out the door. Vince Jones just glares at the door in fury from across the room and slams the dumb bell on the floor in frustration... '...And that's just what V planned on doing; but it would take a lot of time. With every head V busted in the Lion's Den his name grew. He was gettin' that respect and shit...' The camera switches to the inside of the dank, dark, dungeon-like arena Vince Jones once called his hell and his home, The Lion's Den. Fighter after fighter can be seen being taken down at the hands of Vince Jones. On one night Vince can be seen punching an Asian fighter across the jaw with a chain-wrapped fist knocking him out cold. In another prize fight Vince Jones can be seen choking a Jamaican fighter until he goes limp. In yet another fight Vince Jones can be seen nailing an opponent across the back of the head with a lead pipe knocking him out and dropping him to the concrete floor in a heap. '...Cats were startin' to figure that V. Jones was a force to be reckoned with and Big Lex soon took notice too and soon had a proposition he couldn't refuse.....' The camera switches to the inside of Big Lex's office where he is seated at his desk. Vince Jones is seen seated in a chair in front of the large desk. A beautiful, busty blonde is seated in Big Lex's lap at the moment. Big Lex: An empire needs an army, V. J. Armies need dependable soldiers... Vince Jones: And what's all this got to do with me, huh? Big Lex: Everything, V. J. I'm basically giving you the opportunity of a lifetime here. I want you to be one of my right hand men. You agree and you could quickly be moving up in the world. You can have all the talent in the world around here, but to go places I have to notice that talent. Take Jack Wright for instance, he was once in your shoes slowly moving up the ranks. He accepted my offer and soon he was skyrocketing through the ranks. So, what's it gonna be? Big Lex rises to his feet and extends a hand towards Vince Jones. Vince appears a bit reluctant at first but he finally stands up and shakes his hands with Big Lex. 'And so it was set. V. Jones was locked in and ready to make leaps and bounds and shit up the ranks in NYUFC like never before by doing whateva, wheneva for Big Lex no matter what the costs...' The camera fades and fades in as Vince Jones can be seen walking down the street with a few other men who are all dressed in black. They whip out some ski masks and put them on to cover their faces as they reach a small Italian restaurant and walk inside. They are greeted by a round bellied, bearded Sicilian man who appears to be the owner of the restaurant. The owner: Uhhhh...how can I help you gentlemen? He greets them with a smile, but one of the men shoves him backwards and sticks his hand out as if he were looking for money. Lead man: Shut up! We're not here for small talk! Big Lex sent us here to collect! Now you got your payment ready, Rufio, or are we going to have to get a lil rowdy up in here? The owner shrugs and tries to explain his situation. Rufio: Well, you see money's been a little bit tight lately and... The lead man of the group steps forward snatches him by his collar and gets in his face. Lead man: Wrong answer! Now do you have the money or not!? Rufio: I need an extension. I don't have the money. Lead man: (shakes his head) That's a shame! The lead man turns towards his crew motions to the other men and Vince Jones and they all begin knocking tables over and running people out the restaurant. They begin smashing windows in as the owner watches in horror. Lead man: Get your money situation straight next time, Rufio, and we won't have to go through all this shit! The camera fades and fades back in to another part of the city where Vince Jones can be seen sitting in the driver's seat of a small, white dry cleaning van which is parked in an dark alley. Vince is currently wearing all black with a black ski mask on his face. He can be seen grasping the steering wheel rather nervously while he awaits the arrival of someone. Vince Jones: When the hell they gonna get this shit done? V tryin' to get the hell on out of here before someone comes snoopin' around or somethin'. All of a sudden a couple of gunshots ring out in the air which startle Vince and prompt him to start the engine of the van and get it ready to go. A few moments pass by and all of a sudden four or five other men dressed in all black and wearing ski masks come running around the corner, down the alley, reach the van, fling the doors open, and leap in side. Lead man: Aiight V! Floor it! Floor that shit! Vince Jones slams his foot on the gas and the van goes screeching out the alley and down the streets. '...even had to do some things that V. Jones still almost regret to this day to try and make it to the top...' The camera slowly fades out and fades into a night scene in another portion of New York City. Vince Jones meets up with a group of four other men who are wearing all black as well black ski masks on their faces. Masked man #1: (whispering) Where you been at, V? We almost thought had punked out. Vince Jones: (whispering) V ain't no punk! You got that? V was meetin' up with a friend for some barbecue ribs and shit earlier and almost lost track of the time. Masked man #2: (whispering) Damn man! What the fuck is up with that? Masked Man #3: (whispering) Aiight! Enough about all that meaningless shit! Duck down V he should be here at any moment. That bitch think he meetin' up with the Feds, that fuckin' NARC! We got the slip on him though! We got an inside man that made a call and set up this rendevous point. All of a sudden another man is seen walking down the street wearing all black with his face covered as well. Masked Man #4: Its him! Be ready! The masked man stops and awaits. All of a sudden a man who appears to be a detective comes walking towards him from the other end of the block with a smile on his face. He meets up with the masked man and extends his hand. They both shake hands and begin talking with one another. The masked man hands the detective a folder or some sort. The detective nods his head and begins talking something over with the masked man, turns him around so his back as facing the street, and makes a small motion to the attackers across the street. Masked Man #1: That's the sign! Let's bag this bitch ass! The attackers along with Vince Jones all come creeping out from behind the van. The camera fades out and fades back in as the detective and the attackers are all kicking the masked man into submission. The camera fades out as a gunshot rings out. The camera fades back in as Vince is seen kneeling beside the dead man and rips off his mask. Vince looks at the face in horror. The masked men try to get Vince to regain his composure as they hurry to try and move the body and clean up the scene to cover their tracks. '...Carlos was a good friend and V hated to see him die, but that's what happens sometimes. When you want something so bad, when you want to make it to the top so bad, things happen. Sometimes you lose a friend or two along the way. Sometimes people gotta be pushed to the side and shit. You hate to see 'em go, but in the end when you lookin' at all the shit you got and where you at you gotta be thinkin' that it was all worth it. Big Lex was a man that could really make a man forget about any regrets for the short-term atleast. He had his ways of influencin' cats...' The camera fades out and fades in as Vince Jones can be seen cruising the streets of New York City in a brand new, red ferrari. The scene switches to a hot tub where Vince Jones can be seen drinking champagne with four lovely ladies. The scene switches to the office of Big Lex where he is seen sliding a large stack of money across the desk in Vince's direction asking him to accept it. Vince Jones gladly accepts it and slips it all into his duffle bag with a grin. '...the more Vince did for Big Lex the more Big Lex made V. Jones' life even more comfortable than it was. It was almost like a god crownin' a king or somethin' cuz the man was givin' V. Jones a taste of the good life like he had neva seen before. In the meantime, V was steadily risin' the charts in the NYUFC and finally gettin' some crazy recognition...' The camera fades out and fades back in and a poster with Vince Jones on the cover which reads 'The Violence of NYUFC, The Next Big Thing' is being posted right beside the betting booths. The scene switches to a dark alley where Vince Jones can be seen walking all alone. 'Ah, that night. Man, that was some bullshit! All of a sudden some headlights flash on behind Vince Jones and the sound of some screeching tires can be heard as a car comes speeding towards Vince. Vince tries to duck out of the way, but he gets nailed, and falls to the ground in a heap. The car door opens and outsteps Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright. He laughs to himself and walks over to Vince Jones who struggles to try and fight, but is too injured to make a move. 'That son of a bitch could never handle the fact that V. Jones was gunnin' for his punk ass! He always knew there was on man who could wreck him and knock him off his fuckin' perch!' The camera fades out and fades back in as Wright can be seen stomping away at Vince. All of a sudden a gray-haired man appears on the scene and Wright hops into his car and flees the scene. The gray-haired man attends to Vince as the camera slowly fades to black. The camera fades back into a hospital room where Vince Jones can be seen lying motionless in bed. The sound of a door opening can be heard. Vince Jones suddenly snaps back to reality. Vince Jones: Vachon! His expression is intense at the moment and his eyes dart around the scene as he finally realizes once again that he was only daydreaming. Vince calms himself back down once again and closes his eyes in silent thought. 'V. Jones knew he was on his way, but there was one day in particular that really put shit all into perspective for him, the announcement of the brackets for 'King of the Underground'... The other fighters are gathered together in a small locker room. The usual locker room chatter can be heard. All of a sudden the familiar voice of Big Lex can be heard as a monitor flashes on in the corner of the room. Big Lex: Well, as you all know here in the NYUFC we have a little tourney we like to call 'King of the Underground'.... The fighters all hear his voice and spot his presence on the monitor. They all clamour towards the monitor in anticipation of good news. The room goes silent as they listen. Big Lex: ...King of the Underground is the biggest event of the year, an annual event which pits the eight most dangerous and successful fighters from the NYUFC against one another in a single elimination tournament of prize fights. As always there are no rules within as well as outside the Lion's Den. The fighter's cheer. Big Lex: So, you can either consider your name being dropped as one of the eight participants as a blessing or a curse. It all depends on how you look at the situation here. Being named as one of the elite eight is only half the battle here. You've got to make it to tourney in one piece and you all know there a whole lot of other guys in that locker room with you all right now that are gonna be gunning for your heads to claim what's yours; but, that's the fun part about this whole deal...survival. All of the fighters begin peering at one another as the feelings of paranoia begins to rise in the room amongst them all. The door swings open and Jack Wright steps inside. The animosity begins to intensify in the room as he chuckles to himself. Jack Wright: Bet you all are wondering right now why your champ would ever consider being in the same room with the lot of you idiots. Well, I just wanted to get an up close and personal look at who's gonna be in 'King of the Underground' and who won't... He turns and glares directly at Vince who glares right back at him. Big Lex: As you all know, the winner of this tournament will be given an automatic shot at our champ, Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright... Jack Wright: (points to himself and laughs) That's me. Big Lex: ...whenever I see that fight fit to take place. Jack Wright: And I look forward to destroying whoever the hell that might be. Big Lex: And now for the participants: 'Killer Kay' Hernandez, The Heat Seeker' Brian Kowalski, The Assassin, Black Death, The Abomination, 'The Street Prophet' Troy Saunders, Tino Carackas, and V.J 'The Violence of NYUFC'. Good luck to you all. The monitor turns off and Vince turns towards Jack Wright who appears a bit shocked by the news. The room suddenly goes into uproar over the selections and omissions from the tourney. Jack Wright: What the? How the fuck did you make it in? Nah! That can't be right! Fuck that! Vince Jones: (nods) Yeah. That's right and the fuckin' memory remains! V knows what you did and how you tried to erase him from the scene and shit! Jack Wright: Is that right? Is that so? Well, let me remind you about something here. You may have been named to the tourney, but you still gotta make it there in one piece like Big Lex already mentioned. Don't be too happy yet. From this day on your life is gonna be hell around here. (points at the other fighters) They all might hate me... Vince Jones begins looking around the room at the other fighters in distrust. Jack Wright: ... but they hate yo ass too, Mr. V.J 'The Violence of NYUFC'. I don't believe the hype though. Guess I'll have to pray that you make it and I can find out just how violent you can really be. Until then, I'm out. Jack Wright exits the room and Vince Jones suddenly feels as though he's being watched by some of the other fighters in the room. Vince keeps his eyes on them all as he makes his way over to his locker. The camera fades out. {{End of Flashback}} The camera fades back into the Manchester News Arena where Vince still has his head bowed in thought. 'It was a great day, but at the same time it felt like a cursed day to V. Jones. From that point on until 'King of the Underground' V. Jones would feel like a marked man havin' to watch his back even in the presence of his friends just to make sure he lasted until the tourney; but it was worth it. A chance to rise to the top was and is always seen bein' worth of V. Jones doin' whateva, wheneva to make it. The more things change, the more they stay the same.Once again V hits that point where he got another shot to rise, this time its A Dubb Cee's 'Zero 2 Hero'. Eight men enter and only one comin' out with a shot at King Dick. Well, it goes like this. Years ago V. Jones reached this point and did what needed to be done to get his. The Violence defeated all. This Sunday night The Violence steps up to the plate once again lookin' do whateva, wheneva, to whoeva to once again take his. Whoeva stands in his way will be silenced and that's all there is to it because that's the only option. History will repeat itself. V. Jones gonna take A Dubb Cee and make it his underground...'Nuff said!' The camera slowly fades to black as he remains in silence.
