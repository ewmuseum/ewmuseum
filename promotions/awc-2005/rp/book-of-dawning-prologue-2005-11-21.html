Book of Dawning - Prologue

21 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/11/05 ‘You know, I think you should come with us.’ The words, so simple had brought a dangerous choice to me. Here I was sitting in the kitchen of Dan and Jessica O’Grady and they were propositioning me. Well, they were giving me news that had filtered the town but somehow I had been unable to notice it. Fr. Pearson was offering to meet with me, face to face in front of the church once more, except this time in a public forum style. He wanted to air the grievances I’ve got against the church and had entitled the debate the emancipation of Jack. Dan thought it was laughable the title but Jessica was more concerned about what would happen. ‘Jack he’s going to try and humiliate you in front of the town, to show how narrow minded you are. He’s going to turn them against you and then you’ll be facing it tough.’ She had a point but if she knew me by now she knew that I was as stubborn as hell. Dan certainly did. ‘Jess, there’s nothing you can say that’s going to stop him from deciding either way. Listen it’s not for a couple of weeks and we can work on your side of the debate. Besides, he said he’ll narrow what it’s going to be about anyway. You don’t have to worry about it now.’ I couldn’t help it though, the church has never sat right with me from the beginning. Just like I’d thought so much about recently… No I wasn’t going down that path again. ‘I’ll think about alright guys? Just leave it with me.’ ‘Alright Jack, but don’t forget; it’s the Catholic Church after all…’ ‘Can we please stop talking about it.’ ‘What’s the matter? I just said…’ Jessica hushed her father and told him that it was time for newspaper reading time. Dan started to pass remarks but was soon shoved out of the room. It was left to just the two of us then and she smiled and wrapped an arm over my shoulder. ‘Is everything ok?’ ‘Fine, just…just forget about it.’ ‘If you’re sure.’ She turned back to the dishes that were mounting on the counter, dripping with grease and the usual dirt that comes with having an Irish breakfast on a Sunday morning. I looked at her and it seemed different for a change. There was something about her appearance that inspired a sense of curiosity in my head. Wasn’t the way she was dressed, her hair, her eyes, her body, nothing of those. It was….something else, but I couldn’t quite put my finger on it. I shrugged. If I didn’t know now I wouldn’t know for a long time. Instead I turned back to the table and picked up a magazine, flicking through the pages slowly and surely, slipping a finger to thread through them. Nothing was really hitting home in my head, just irrelevant celebrity gossip that meant nothing to me. I was jus finding the back page… …When a knock on the door interrupted the flow of thought. I stood up and moved towards the front door, brushing my hand past Jessica as I did so, letting her know that I’d take care of it whatever it was. In the other room Dan was asleep, the newspaper sprawled over his thinning frame, neatly folded all the same and covering his upper torso. I could hear the light blowing of his snoring like a breeze as I passed by; knowing full well that it’d be a lot louder than that in no time at all. Out of the corner of my eye I could see something on the back page but I was stopped dead in my tracks when glass went flying from the door. Instinctively I had curled inwards, letting the shards of glass fall all around me. Suddenly whomever it was that had instigated this whole thing came barrelling through the door and shoulder charged me to the ground. His grip was wrapped tight around my neck in no time and held me down as best he could. Thankfully the corridor was too tight for him to mount properly and he had no leverage. A swift kick to the gonads saw to that anyway. The attacker slumped to my side, groaning in pain at the severe damage I had been lucky to inflict. Pulling myself upwards I could now see he was dressed pretty smart for someone who had the intention of attacking. He had black hair and an olive complexion, nicely bronzed to the hilt. I pushed him onto his back and hadn’t a clue who he was. I soon found out though. ‘Fuck!’ I’ll give him that swear…I did have my knee on his nuts now. ‘Well….what the fuck are you doing in this house?’ He tried moving, to break free. I leaned harder on his nuts. ‘OW!’ ‘Jack you know at some point you’re going to feel the floor again your pushing your knee so far down.’ It was Dan in the doorway and he had a little smirk on his face. He came over and grabbed the guy’s arms and pulled them behind his back. Just to make sure he didn’t flinch I pushed down on his sack again. A nice whimper was elicited from his lips. I chuckled. Dan gave a stern look before producing the jump rope that was in the sitting room, tying his hands up and then bringing him into the living room and propping him up on the chair. ‘So…what are you doing in this house?’ ‘For you!’ The accent was distinctly Italian and now he became a bit more familiar…. …He’d been there when my family was killed. He’d been one of the laughing, evil faces. So I snapped. ‘Jack! Stop it! Stop it!’ Dan grabbed to hold me back from doing any more severe damage. Given long enough I’d kill him. Screaming out of frustration I skulked back into the kitchen. I ran my hands slowly through my hair and blew out some air. The tears soon came. Images flashed before my eyes… A bright sunny afternoon, the picnic laid out before me and my family right beside me. Rua dancing in the grass, chasing some small insect like it was the most important thing in the world, his red hair flowing and just now starting to grow a bit longer…. ….And Laura, sweet precious Laura lying cradled in my arms. Her black hair cascading all over my hands and enveloping me like I always wanted. Her blue eyes looking warmly up at me and tilting her head to kiss me…. ….And she pulls back and she’s lying on our bed, completely naked and just…a picture of feminine beauty….her smile beaming at me and whispering how much she loves me…. ….But the blackness turns into the darkness of that warehouse and the shot…then another… And I couldn’t bear to think. ‘Jack? Jack come here.’ I had curled into a ball on the floor and Jessica now pulled me to her and instinctively I moved inwards, resting my head against her breast, her hand on my forehead and the other round my shoulder. It was then that I began to see about earlier…but I refused to let it move any further. Not now, maybe never. ‘Thanks…I better go back in.’ ‘Oh hush, the police are here and they’re taking him away as we speak.’ ‘What? How long…’ ‘About an hour. Don’t worry they’ll be gone soon and they didn’t see you.’ ‘An hour?’ ‘Hush now.’ She smiled warmly again and just for a moment…no it was gone. Within a moment Dan came in and gave me a reassuring look before heading back in. Just before he did though he handed me the paper. ‘You might want to read the back page.’ Without a word he left. On the back page I scanned it quickly before noticing the photograph, the glimpse of which I had seen earlier. It all made sense now. In bold just above a photo of a man read the lines… FARRELLI PASSES AWAY IN HOSPITAL My tormentor of before, the one who had troubled Dan in the gym, the one I had sent to the hospital…had died. The door was open for my ascent…would I take it?
