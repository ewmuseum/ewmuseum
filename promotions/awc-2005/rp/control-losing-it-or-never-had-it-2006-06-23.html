Date: end +Procrastinating While they set the alarm to go off at eight am, neither Ellis nor Adam actually prepared themselves to wake up this early. Its cries are a foul awakening to the morning routine that, aside from the odd time and depressing notion this particular day brings, the couple has gotten used to over the weeks. Adam hits the snooze twice, plummeting back to sleep with ease in the depths of his California king-sized bed. Indeed the man could sleep forever if it weren’t for the inconvenience of daylight. On the contrary, Ellis is always capable of coming to with the first ring. It’s somewhat of a struggle for her to keep her eyes open today, but she still follows through with routine by going to the bathroom then returning to bed. At her bedside from the night before is a blunt that Adam rolled for this exact moment. It’s a routine he’s followed through with every night they’ve stayed together. He often said, “Nothing better than a wake-and-bake,” but then again he also applied this phrase to many facets of life. Only this week, things are strikingly different for Adam. This week he’s a father to be, and as he covers from the sun to stop disrupting what precious minutes he has, he can’t help but wonder if Ellis feels any guilt at all while she sparks up the session. Because, half-asleep he can still hear her click that lighter, and he can’t help but feel a bit wrenched; just for the fact that something is still living inside of her. Something that’s his. Today they have an appointment to solve that problem, but Adam still wants to know why she’s acting so... apathetic. Even though Ellis’ answer could be so obvious, that she knows her child is going to die anyways so there is no reason to act any differently, he just wishes she’d say that to him. But Ellis never attempts to clear the air and he’s always been a push-over in regards to El getting her way so in the end he merely shrugged and said ‘alright.’ That doesn’t mean the slow-turning wheel froze in place, oh no; it became perpetual. Ellis puffs on the blunt until it’s about a third of the way through, coughs accompanied with watered eyes and dried lips, and then passes it to Adam as it’s realistically the only way to wake him. He begins to smoke as if keeping consciousness requires it. On mornings when Ellis was feeling frisky, the two would share the blunt in halves while taking turns going down on each other but no way does one person’s mouth touch the blunt after touching the other’s genitals because that would be fucking gross. There would be no such occasion this morning, what with their awkward arrangement to fulfill. They weren’t really sure what to say to each other because they both knew no set of words could put their minds at ease. Instead they just procrastinated for the few minutes they were still able to. Adam puts his arm around Ellis and she maneuvers her head onto his chest, ear cupped on his heartbeat. It goes for a few bars until they are interrupted by the third round of the snooze feature. Unable to wait any longer, the King and Queen push out of bed and into responsibility.
