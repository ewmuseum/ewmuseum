Bleed By Example

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 23rd, 2005 Sunday. It’s Sunday morning. Angelica can tell it by the faint sounds the bells at the local church make to attract the early flock of faithful to their weekly dose of brimstone. Angelica stretches lazily in the large bed, her naked body covered by a single sheet, and then lays her arm on the spot next to her. Empty. Surprised, Angelica lets out a small “Huh?” and then turns over to look where the Fifth Horseman lay down last night to sleep. Cursing under her breath, Angelica fumbles out of the bed and pulls on a T-shirt. Fighting with her jeans, Angelica dances around the room for a little while before she is suitably clothed. Rushing out of the bedroom, she turns left in the small corridor of the apartment and heads for the kitchen, where a figure familiar to her slouches over the kitchen table, clad in nothing but jeans. Angelica stands in the doorway, admiring the sculpted and scarred back of Hate as he keeps his eyes fixed on something on the other side of the kitchen window, something down at the courtyard. Angelica stares at his bald head and his muscled arms, and then lets out a girlish giggle almost unwillingly. Hate doesn’t seem to notice this, however, and Angelica quickly grows bored of her bystander role and walks up to him, wrapping her arms around right arm and peeking over his shoulder at the courtyard. Angelica: “What are you looking at, my love?” For the first time, Hate seems to acknowledge Angelica’s presence with a grunt. Hate straightens up on his chair and looks over his shoulder into Angelica’s face. She returns his stare with one of her own, before Hate shakes his head and slowly peels her arms off of himself. Hate: “Now, my Angel, don’t you think that is stretching the closeness of our relationship a bit? I am merely curious about where you got this idea of calling me ‘your love’, of course.” Angelica: “Hmm, let’s see… Maybe I got the idea from, oh, I dunno, 1:30 this morning, my love?” Hate cringes at the 16-year-old, who merely makes a pouty face and latches onto Hate again. Hate sighs and turns his attention back to the courtyard, and Angelica’s eyes follow him. Hate: “My Angel, look there. You see Azagtoth, building a shrine for Odin near that lonely tree? That is the kind of love I tolerate. Utter, blind commitment to me. That is the kind of love and loyalty the Cult of the Fifth Horseman needs if it is ever to take its message to the mainstream population. The love of the flesh has no place in the Cult, and you should know that, my Angel. Or are you deliberately not listening during our lectures? Even after I go to the lengths of arranging private tutoring for you, you purposefully mislead yourself. My poor, foolish Angel.” Angelica looks over to the courtyard, where the black-clad Azagtoth is stringing up a rope and a dead cat over his small rock shrine. Some girl would be missing their cat tonight, she realized, and would find the little furball gently swinging in the wind, hanged over a bunch of Nordic runes written in dried-up blood. The thought, which would have made her sick mere weeks ago, now goes by her head without much action. She draws herself closer to Hate, who becomes tense despite looking uninterested. Angelica: “But my love, you know that Azagtoth is just a pet for you. He is rigid and unbending and stupid to boot, and looks like he was beaten with the ugly stick. A lot. He can’t do half the things I can do to you, my love, and you know it.” Hate turns his head towards Angelica, who smiles and plants a deep kiss on his mouth. As she draws her head back, however, she is surprised to find Hate’s hand beneath her cheek, ready to grip her throat at a moment’s notice. Hate’s eyes shine with curiosity as he watches on how his newest disciple handles this deadly snake by her windpipe. Angelica quells the urge to scream, but still gulps involuntarily. Hate slowly uncoils his hand and taps Angelica on the cheek, releasing the tension in both of their bodies. Hate: “Now, let that be a lesson for you, my Angel. I take care of my own, and Azagtoth is my most loyal follower. You have no right to paint a picture not in his likeness, my foolish little apprentice. Azagtoth has been with me for years: you are barely starting your own journey, my Angel. You still cling to your wings, despite my attempts to rip them off. Probably better to let them rot and fall off on their own, then, if that is what you want. But please, leave Azagtoth to me. I like my people unquestionably loyal.” Angelica nods quickly and then takes a few steps back, rubbing her throat where the fingers almost squeezed her. Her loyalty towards Hate is fueled partly by lust and partly by fear, fear of the man who might take up arms against his very own kin any given moment. A man who actually has taken up arms against his own kin. Angelica walks over to the kitchen cabinets and fishes out a pouch of tea, quickly preparing a mug of boiling water to soak it in. While her little breakfast is cooking, Angelica stretches again and turns towards Hate, who has again settled down to watch Azagtoth’s painstakingly detailed work. Angelica: “You do realize that the people who actually live here are going to be back today, right?” Hate barely notices her remark, but inside she knows that he has already calculated this into his plans for today, tomorrow and next week as well. Angelica still has a hard time to grasp the situation where Hate, Azagtoth and she broke into a temporarily empty apartment and lived there like it had been theirs for years. It was strange, stranger than anything else she had witnessed or done so far. The complete lack of respect for private ownership, the lack of respect for the rules and laws of the society made her attracted to Hate more than ever. She had surrendered her body to Hate almost every night since she had met him, every night with newfound vigor and conviction. Some nights Hate took advantage of the offering, sometimes he dismissed her naked body to the bed without a second glance. How his mind worked, Angelica could only guess, but she was determined to crack her leader’s secret one day in the future. Angelica: “So, my love… Tell me. What is the story behind you and Azagtoth? How did he ever come to you?” Hate’s head turns like on a swivel and shoots a sinister, malevolent gaze at Angelica’s direction, freezing her on the spot. Spots of perspiration appear on her forehead, and she can feel white-hot lead being poured on her spine, shocking her entire system to the core. The sudden, silent ferociousness of her leader sets her back, and she knocks over the cup of tea with her elbow in an effort to push through the stove. Finally, Hate’s eyes melt to their normal, calculating look, and Angelica begins to breathe easier, still nailed to those eyes. Hate: “Now, my Angel… Why do you assume that there is a story behind us two meeting? Why do you assume that our fates have been entwined in an epic chronicle of adventure and heroism, of modern-day swords and sorcery-tales of knights and dragons? Is not the simplest answer often the truest one as well? The story behind us is no more spectacular than the story between a captain and a soldier assigned to his squad. Azagtoth is with me because he wishes to serve a worthy cause, and serve that cause by spilling the blood of others. I cannot say no to a skillful soldier when one offers themselves to me, no matter how much I wished for the meeting and mutual acceptance to look like a B-grade buddy movie. I am a simple captain to Azagtoth, a captain to be followed and served until the bitter end. And for me, Azagtoth is nothing but yet another tool at the Cult’s disposal, at MY disposal. Never forget the selfish motive, my Angel: there is always one. Even I cannot escape this taint, no matter how hard I try. But since all my motives, selfish and otherwise, are for the Final Solution, I can claim to be better than others. As harsh as it sounds, it is still the truth. To everyday minds, the truth is too cruel: it hurts like only the truth can.” Apparently finished in his mind, Hate yawns and stands up from the table, walking towards the bedroom. Angelica follows him, however, and catches him sitting on the bed while pulling on his black combat boots encrusted with blood stains and notches from this fight or that. Angelica leans against the doorframe and crosses her arms over her chest while looking at Hate in the gloom of the room. Angelica: “My love, I would wish for nothing more than belief in that, but I cannot bring my mind to accept it. There is something more to you and Azagtoth than what meets the eye or the explanation. I have seen and heard the way he acts towards you, and the way you act towards him. Azagtoth is completely in your control, a puppet without strings to be controlled by you. And you abuse him like no other. I have seen you order your other men rest after a hard day’s work, yet you have only worked Azagtoth further. And the worst of all, he does not say a word against you, does everything you ask no matter what he has done before that. You do not view him as a human being! You see him only as a… As a… As an animal, your personal little plaything.” Angelica challenges Hate with her stare, but Hate merely grins at her defiance and stands up, pulling a black wifebeater-shirt over his head. Hate stretches his arm and looks meaningfully at Angelica, smirking at her as her confidence begins to erode away moment by moment. Hate: “You speak with courage too large for your chest, my dear Angel. Why do you observe Azagtoth and me to this extent? And why do you analyze us like scientific experiments running in a maze for a piece of cheese? Your words amuse me. The way I treat Azagtoth is simply because that is the way he intended it to be. He offered himself to my will, utterly dedicated to whatever I might do with him and his future. I could order him to slice his wrists, and he would do it in a heartbeat, then stand in attention until there would be no strength in his bones to hold him up. That is the kind of story Azagtoth and me have. We are merely a master and a servant, an owner and a slave, and he is filling out his position in the best way suited for him. I pray this satisfied your curiosity, my Angel.” Angelica: “I don’t buy that. Not a single word of it. No person could do such a thing for himself or herself, even for you. I refuse to accept it.” Hate shoots another poisonous glance at Angelica, but quickly replaces it with a sly smile while he pulls on his trenchcoat. He looks at his sleeve, adorned with the name “Idi Amin” near his elbow. Hate sighs and then walks over to Angelica and pushes past her before turning around and winking at her. Hate: “Ah, but that’s where you went wrong. People will do it for me. In fact, that is what I EXPECT of them. So far, only Azagtoth has gone this far. But in the future, I expect the others to follow. You too.” Angelica is shocked and just about to protest, but Hate walks out of the apartment before she can say anything. Cursing under her breath, Angelica pulls on her clothes and then quickly leaves after Hate, not even bothering to close the lockpicked front door. She flies down the stairs and then stumbles out of the main doors of the apartment complex onto the silent courtyard, shielded from all sides by sleeping buildings. There, on the other side of the courtyard, Hate stands over Azagtoth, who seems to be praying on his makeshift shrine, over a pile of runes and under a hanged cat carcass. Angelica slowly inches forwards, fully aware of the resentment the ghoulish man feels towards her. As she comes within hearing distance, she can hear Azagtoth muttering the last of his prayer to his One-Eyed God, Odin. Azagtoth: “And grant me my wish, oh Raven-Haired One, so that this whore might sleep on your gallows her eternal sleep, and suffer at the hands of the True Ones.” Hate chuckles as Azagtoth takes his hunting knife and taps it on his forehead twice before touching both of his painted cheeks with the cold steel. Hate ruffles Azagtoth’s long black hair, an act that seems extremely disturbing to Angelica not only because she knows of Azagtoth’s violent temper from first-hand experience but because this only further seems to confer the image of a master playing with his pet. Azagtoth then gets up from the kneeling position he was in and turns around with Hate. Hate seems impassive, but Azagtoth’s face immediately twists into a vicious mask of hate as he sees Angelica standing there, and for a moment he seems to weigh the knife in his hand as if planning on a lunge, but then he sheaths the cruel instrument and instead falls back into the shadows his hair makes on his face despite the early morning sun. Angelica gulps and then looks from Azagtoth to the gray cat hanging on the piece of rope, grateful that it is not her. Angelica: “Did you kill this cat, Azagtoth? That poor thing…” Azagtoth scoffs and then looks at Hate, as if sharing a realization about what sort of an idiot this girl is, before turning back to her and speaking in an overtly sweet voice hiding pure venom behind the faked front. Azagtoth: “Yes, although my leader helped me… I spotted it with my eyes before it spotted me, and I chased it down with agility beyond its little paws, and then I struck it down with the ferocity of a thousand claws. All for the One-Eyed One and the Fifth Horseman.” Disgusted, Angelica sneers at Azagtoth, who smiles triumphantly and is just about to continue his verbal tirade, but Hate stops him from doing so and instead motions them both towards the walkway that leads out of the courtyard and into the streets of the city. Hate: “Come, my soldiers. A brand new day awaits the word of the Horseman. And tonight, we even have some TV to make.” Both Angelica and Azagtoth cringe, she because of being lumped together with Azagtoth as a “soldier” and he because he cannot express his feelings about this tramp hanging along, nevermind the fact that he’d have to endure another night at the set for AWC’s Fresh! programming. Slowly, the trio walks out of the complex, while a car pulls up nearby, containing the elderly couple who have unwillingly and unknowingly hosted the quarters for the absolutely last people on Earth they would want to meet in a dark alley.
