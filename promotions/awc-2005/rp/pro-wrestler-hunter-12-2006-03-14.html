Pro Wrestler Hunter #12

14 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/06 'I hate the window seat,' Grapps ventured toward Butterfly as they got on a plane for New York. Megumi nudged him forcibly with her carry-on. 'Take the window seat. I don't even want to sit on a plane next to you, if I had a choice.' She looked around at the cramped airplane, coach filling in with weekend travelers. 'You would think we could get seats up in first class, being AWC tag team champions.' 'Tell me about it, hun.' Grapps agreed as he slid across the aisle seat. He tugged at his green boa as it almost choked him. 'I think I am going to pay Pearl Harber a visit soon on a Fresh! and see what he has to say about this.' Grapps settled in his seat after almost garroting himself with one of his feathered fashion accesories. 'Oh, and the fact that two of his wrestlers have been mysteriously injured and he hasn't done or said anything about it.' Hamada looked around as if she was suspicious of something, although she said nothing to Grapps. He in turn looked nervous at his company's body language. 'Sweetheart, you need to fix this thing before it explodes like like a case of diarhea.' 'That is disgusting,' she chastisd her eccentric team mate. 'But once we get to New York this will come to a conclusion. Let this 'thing' that is out there come to me. We will be on the east coast this entire trip. I have friends in New York. And I am tired of looking over my back. This person... this 'Pro Wrestler Hunter'... he is going to make his one move, and I am going to catch him in the act. And then we end this.' ************************* Four hours later and Megumi Hamada and The Green Grappler walked into their hotel room at The Ramada, in Manhattan. 'You should be happy. There are two beds,' Grappler said with a smile. 'Hardly,' Megumi returned. 'Why didn't you take the taxi to another hotel? Or at least another room?' 'Listen sister, with the nutcases running around AWC, I'd rather have you watching my back.' 'Yes,' she said with a wry smile of her own. 'But I don't think I am conformatble with you constantly watching my back.' Grappler watched as she settled down on one of the beds and jerked the blanket over herself. 'Would I be a gentleman if I parted ways with a young lady, her first time in New York City?' 'If you know your way around, maybe you can be of some help,' came the muffled response, now underneath a blanket. Grappler turned and looked at the clock. Almost seven and he was exhausted. But that was what long travel did to a body. They were lucky they found a place to sleep like this. Most times they slept in motels, which were much worse. More thoughts drifted into Grapps head as he sank down into the bed and tried to sleep. But it didn't come so easy. Even as he listened to Megumi's breathing become more measured and rhythmic as she fell asleep, Grapps thought about all that had happened in the past few weeks. Nothing seemed to bother him more then the man known as Zsasz. Back four weeks ago when he first met Butterfly, she had found a cigarette case on a man who was following her, and he mysteriously died. Grappler had recognized the initials on the cigarette case, 'LH'. They stood for London Hawaii, a man he knew quite well. And the man who Megumi took the cigarette case from claimed it was given to him by a wrestler in New York. That could be almost anyone, but who would have the kind of power and resources to program a man to die if he were to speak a name, share a deadly secret? The haunting image of Zsasz came into Grappler's mind. And if anyone was behind the Pro Wrestler Hunter it was reasonable to think Zsasz knew who it could be. He was definitely a man they should consider on their search. Grappler thought about Zsasz and the night they met, and how no matter how hard he tried he could not get under Zsasz's skin. The man was impenetrable, and Grappler considered himself a man who could get blood from a stone. He could not make Zsasz reveal anything he knew by putting him on edge, but he did manage to further alienate Megumi that night. And he felt bothered by that. He took a quick glance over at the sleeping woman. Half Mexican and half Japanese. That was something else to think about. Grapps laced his fingers behind his head and stared at the cracks in the ceiling. Since he had first seen Megumi's picture, he thought of her as simply a woman. That perception continued long after he had met her, and he'd come to consider her one of the most intriguing women he had ever met. Definitely stubborn and somewhat mysterious, but most intriguing to be sure. But last week for the first time he was forced to see megumi from another side. He realized that she considered herself more Japanese, then human. He could see it in her face during the sake ritual ceremony. Megumi had been born and raised in Japan, and held those values and traditions, even though her half Mexican heritage meant she was rejected by those around her. Grapps trusted his instintcs. For the past few months he had seen flashes of bitterness in Megumi too deep for him to imagine. He remembered the night after they had stolen The Furious Fists truck and spent the night driving through the backwoods of Kentucky, talking. She always resisted his questions but he had a way to make people talk and she would drop little stories or hints about her life past. Growing up in Japan where she was half Mexican must have brought upon her alot of racism and prejudice. For the first time, Grapps wondered what it meant to be in Megumi's shoes, trapped in two worlds, never truly part of either. He could see it in her, the longing to belong. The emptiness inside her for things Japanese. That was why she was drawn to Zsasz, charmed by his courtesy and manners and his acceptance of her as a true Japanese woman. He figured that Megumi was not used to such treatment, turning to Zsasz's compliments like a child seeking acceptance. She almost glowed when she spoke Japanese, she was so proud of who she was and had no one to share it with. I am going to keep a close eye on this newcomer, Grappler thought to himself. And his new boy, Mikey O'Reilly as well. Grapps sighed. he was surprised Megumi slept so easily. They were coming up on a huge tag team match against The Unfuckables, for the Alliance Championships. Add to the pot the possibility of Megumi's shadow somewhere out there hunting down innocent people and it sent a shiver down his spine. Peeking over at his slumbering roomie one more time, Grapps watched as her covers slightly slipped down her shoulder, revealing her tattoo. What the hell was that thing all about? He had seen the tape of the night Tozen Fujita had been attacked and Butterfly took his place in a match against Jack Murphy. The lights had gone out and her tattoo had glowed a fierce sky blue, and out of nowhere she pinned her opponent. Or the match versus Tim and Liam Martin, when she saved him by walking DIRECTLY into the fire pit, and on her arm, the small tattoo of the flaming throne glowing. Or what about that time he saw the protrait of the man and woman on horseback and it seemed as though they wer truly riding under a storumy night sky? Was this all a trick, a bending of light, a simple illusion? Grapps rolled on his side and gazed through the blinds of the window into the New York skyline. He felt an eerie brooding, an uncertainty. He lay there, thinking about everything that had happened and everything that was going to go down in the next week. He slowly drifted away into an uneasy slumber.
