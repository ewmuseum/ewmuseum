Feeling the anger

18 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/08/2005 What’s more important? Not to long ago there was a wrestler who called himself The Real Deal. He was known for being unpredictable and evil…but then again he was also known for being unstoppable yet caring. A man of many faces he was and still is to this day. I say face but I truly mean deformity to the maximum. You may ask why, but why dwell into dark places when such a magnificent event such as the battle of Britain. Actually, screw that, let’s talk about the magnificent athlete that is The Real Deal. Roughly 4 months ago The Real Deal was involved in a match in the DWO against, the almighty as it would seem, Inphino Blitz. Inphino performed a move which made sure that The Real Deal would not get up. Not to be one for bitching The Real Deal took this like a man as he knew the dangers of stepping into a DWO ring. The problem for him though, as he slouched in his wheelchair, was his wife Erin who would constantly yell and smash people who even dared to look at them while they were out together. At the exact moment in time that Deal’s head crushed against the mat against Blitz his mind went into torment. He knew what he was doing consciously but he also wanted to do so many things. Unfortunately he was left with the inability to move and soon became trapped inside his own head, something that is not exactly the nicest thing in the world. Trapped behind cells Four months on from the event that ruined his life, The Real Deal sits in his wheelchair in his home. His head is tilted back against a soft pillow as if someone had just pushed it back. -5 minutes before- Deal sits in his wheelchair, his crocked arms and fingers set across his chest. His eyes gaze into thin air as his wife Erin circles him waving her finger in defiance. “No no no!” she storms, “You are not going to enter yourself into a damn wrestling match!” Deal just continues to stare at the wall, his cold blue eyes swirl in the sunlight which hits his face from a window to his left. His face itself is a mess. His nose is bent to the right, his cheeks are inset and his eyes are wildly unsettled into a random formation. Erin stops for a second in front of her husband and turns to face him. “I know you can see me!” she storms again, “are you listening to me? Well?” Once again Deal doesn’t move, obviously because he can’t. Erin begins to get infuriated by this and puts herself face to face with her husband. “I’m going to make sure you don’t make it to that match!” she yells with much anger. Her face now becomes a bitter red and she reels her right hand backwards. “Don’t make me do this!” Deal sits perfectly still, not moving a muscle, not even his eyes. Erin’s hand comes hurtling towards Deal’s face like sinner towards hell. When she connects Deal’s head falls backwards after the initial ultimate slap. Erin turns on her heel to head out. -Current time- Deal hadn’t noticed all that had gone on minutes before and still doesn’t as he sits perfectly still. Inside his head though he is in complete torment…
