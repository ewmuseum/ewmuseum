Red Rock gets a Dog

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th May 2006 Having little or no social life does make life a little bit… tedious. Boredom is like a terminal disease, when you’re bored you descend in to a tight grip that David Blaine himself couldn’t escape. Things seem less interesting when you’re bored, it is a theory of Red Rock’s that it is possible to die from boredom, all you have to do is get caught in this vicious loop. Red Rock finds himself once again in this vicious loop, struggling for survival. Red Rock remains alone in his bedroom surrounded by his own filth he has accumulated over the last few weeks. Piles of dirty clothes fester; kicking off a rancid stale stench that can only be likened to cat piss. The empty bottles of coke clunk against one another and the rustling of empty food packaging and sweetie wrappers become more audible as Red Rock stirs in his bed. You see this is where Red Rock has spent the last few weeks, when other AWC superstars where busting their humps training and working towards furthering their careers. Not Red Rock though, he has been spending his time in his room becoming more and more reclusive. His new hair has since grown a few inches and appears to be disgusting greasy and a few black wiry hairs have grown over Red Rock’s top lip and the end of his chin. But this does beg the question, what reduces a man of relatively good health to become bound to his bedroom living on nothing but rustler burgers and coca cola? Well the answer is simple really… a woman. Last time we left Red Rock last month was in the deadly grip of misery after finally blowing his chances with the young lady he was falling for. His own stupidity let him down once again and he blew it, full on blew it. Red Rock takes another swig from a half empty crumpled up coke bottle before screwing the lid back on. The plastic pops and clicks as he places it back by the side of his bed. He releases a sorrowed sigh before switching his TV over to channel 4 to watch the Paul O’ Grady show. He just looks like half the man he was before, and even before that he was facing a lot of emotional problems with his in ability to clinch any sponsorships thus leading to a life of mild poverty, especially with rumours of cut backs. It wouldn’t be such a surprise if wrestling had even crossed his mind over the last month, Red Rock doesn’t even know he is defending the title this week against Anton Assault. Its no surprise everyone has written him off to loose it when he has barely left his room to piss. A small burp escapes Red Rock’s mouth just as Paul O’ Grady introduces himself to the nation. KNOCK, KNOCK Red Rock jumps with his eyes springing open like a mouse trap from the unexpected noise of someone at his front door. Red Rock throws his blanket off; moving some of the empty food packets with it. He quickly pats his hand across the floor, searching for a pair of trousers. KNOCK, KNOCK KNOCK! Red Rock: ALRIGHT BASTARD! Calm down I’m coming! Red Rock abandons his search for some trousers and decides to brave it in just his underwear. He negotiates his way over the piles of clothes and rubbish then exits his room. He steps in to the spotless dining room of his open plan flat, Red Rock’s strange computer programmer flat mate Howard manages to keep the place clean with his obsessive compulsion to keep things tidy. Red Rock scurries along the sound of his bare feet slapping across the wooden laminate flooring and opens the front door. Standing before Red Rock is a man dress in blue overalls and a red baseball cap. He is a rugged looking greasy man and in his right hand he holds a clip board. Next to him on the floor is a trolley with a large heavy looking box… no wonder the man is so out of breath, the broken lifts wouldn’t have allowed him to deliver the box easily. Delivery Man: Hello…I got a … package for a Mr Howard… Red Rock: Oh right…I don’t know where he’s gone to be honest. I’ll sign for it though. Delivery: Great, just sign there at the bottom. Red Rock takes the clip board and scribbles his signature in the correct box, and then he passes it back over to the delivery man who begins shifting the heavy box through the door. Red Rock: what is it anyway? Delivery Man: It’s an industrial cooling generator mate. Red Rock: A what?! Why does he want that? Delivery Man: Summer mate, I don’t blame him! Red Rock: but British summer is just rain and misery! The Delivery man shrugs and takes the clip board under his arm before leaving Red Rock alone with this huge box. Red Rock accepts the reason for having this industrial cooler anyway, but he fails to realise why Howard was buying huge bags of ice and taking them to his room in the middle of winter. Nether the less Red Rock shrugs and decides maybe he should make something to eat. The time now is 20 past 5 in the afternoon and he still hasn’t had breakfast. So Red Rock turns away from the delivery and over to the break bar where he finds some bread, cheese and some mayonnaise to create his own special recipe the “cheese and mayo” sandwich. Red Rock musters a sigh, in his time alone away from his friends he has had nothing to think about other than the last thing Ren said to him “I did like you, but you’re just a weirdo. Just leave me alone”. Red Rock: ARGH SHITTY FUCK CUNT! Red Rock drops the cheese grater to the floor. In his deep thoughts he grated the cheese all the way down to his fingers without realising. The blood from his cut drips on to the mountain of cheese on his bread. Red Rock hastily pops his finger in his mouth with muffled curses shooting from his mouth. Red Rock spins around 180 degrees. Red Rock: ARG! D’avid: EEEEEEEEEEIIIEEE! D’avid suddenly pops out of no where; scaring the arse off Red Rock causing his blood to drip all over the floor. Red Rock’s face turns red with anger directed towards D’avid. Red Rock: D’AVID! How the hell do you keep getting in here? D’avid: That’s not important. I’ve been worried for you Red Rock! Red Rock: Worried? …Why would you be worried? There isn’t anything to worry about! Red Rock tries to shrug off D’avid’s concern and dashes over to his first aid box. D’avid: No one has seen you for like a month, have you seen yourself in the mirror? You look like a 13 year old boy trying to grow a moustache and your hair is all shit and piss! Red Rock: I’m fine D’avid I just felt like having some alone time, you need some time alone don’t you? D’avid: Heh…yeah but you look like someone pisses and shit all over you! I don’t know if you know but the odds for you retaining the relentless title are like 100/1 and I don’t blame them! Anton Assault is on a hot streak, everyone is raving about him! And here you are sulking over some stupid girl! Red Rock places his hands flat on his dining room table with a poorly applied bandage around his index finger. The blood quickly seeps through but that isn’t going through Red Rock’s mind at the moment. The reality of knowing that he’s days away from being humiliated at the hands of one of the men he beat for his title in the first place hits him. But a stronger emotion over whelms him at this point; denial. Red Rock: Shut your filthy bastarding bastard mouth you ginger rasclart! I’m not going to loose to Anton ASS-ault. I’m still in good enough shape to win; you saw how good I was against Vince Jones. D’avid: Heh, that is true! You spanked him like I spank my willy! But that was before you had time to let Ren fester in your mind like a tampon from Sarah’s fangita. Red Rock: EWW Don’t EVER talk about FAT Sarah’s putrid vagina again! Anyway I don’t care about Ren so don’t talk about her either! D’avid: You don’t care about her heh? So that’s why you grated your finger? Red Rock recedes his lips and lowers his brow creating creases between his eyebrows and on his forehead in an expression of disapproval against D’avid truthful uttering. Red Rock turns his back away from D’avid and walks back towards his room, trying to contain his emotion. But D’avid doesn’t let him go that easily, he slaps his hand on Red Rock’s bare shoulder and spins him back around so they are face to face. D’avid: Come on Red don’t be a big gay! I thought of something to help you get over her! Red Rock rolls his eyes but hears D’avid out. D’avid takes an enthusiastic stance and applies a huge goofy grin, almost as if he was pitching an idea for a movie to a panel of Hollywood big wigs. D’avid: We go get you a puppy! Red Rock: A puppy? D’avid: Yeah you can meet chics by going to walk your dog it’s FOOL PROOF! Heh! LOADS of chics go down the park and walk their dogs Red Rock: But we’re not allowed pets! D’avid: I have a pet and Mr Camfield is ok with it! Red Rock: Your sex slave Greg doesn’t count as a pet, D’avid! Red Rock shoves past D’avid like an agitated teenager thus making a beeline for his room. D’avid stands and watches with a frown on his face, his concern for his only friend grows stronger, so he follows Red Rock towards his room only to get the door slammed in his face. D’avid swiftly opens the door and forces himself in Red Rock’s room…only to be fought back by the grotesque stench. D’avid: FUCK ME IT SMELLS LIKE MY DEAD MOTHER IN HERE! Red Rock: So? I like it smelling like your dead mother, if you don’t like it then you can get out! D’avid: You’re coming with me to get a puppy whether you like it or not, you can’t swim around in your own piss and semen anymore. Think about it, if you get a really cute puppy with a strange breed you’ll get girl come up to you ALL the time smothering it and that’s when you work you’re magic! Red Rock sighs and concedes to D’avid’s consistent badgering. He rolls over towards D’avid and wipes his greasy unwashed face. Red Rock: Fine… An hour or so later at the local dog pound. We now find our anti hero and companion D’avid Bulrat at the local dog pound in a semi abandoned industrial estate in the rougher parts of Aldershot town. There is a squalid atmosphere about the place; it is dim and very few of the lights work and the ones that do have the light bulbs flickering on and off. The electric cables are making a dangerous sparking noises but this is only drowned out by the reverberant chorus of dogs, barking, yelping and scratching. The sounds are complimented by the pungent foul odour of dog urine and dog faeces that is so old it has turned grey and developed a layer of furry mould, much to the hundreds of fly’s delight. Red Rock is now looking a bit more normal and less emotionally beaten. He stands with D’avid to his right and the owner of the pound to his left. The owner is an obese unshaven balding middle age man, who is sporting a tattered pair maroon jogging bottoms and a yellow sweat stained vest. He stinks of cigarette smoke and cheap vodka that he spent all day drinking thus impairing his balance and gift of speech. Honest Barry: So…ergh..here at Bonest Harry… I mean Honest Barry’s Dog pound we take the best care of our…errr Red Rock: Dogs? Honest Barry: Yeah thozze things, all our dog are well looked after… … Honest Barry: I didn’t nick ‘em…by the way…brugh… Red Rock: O…k… Honest Barry slurs another sentence whilst staggering around, much to Red Rock’s disgust. Red Rock looks around at all of the dogs and none of them really seem to catch his eye for the right reasons. Most of them seem to be covered in their own crap. Red Rock leans over to D’avid and whispers quietly in his ear. Red Rock: Where did you find out about this guy? I’m not 100% sure this guy is legitimate. D’avid: What makes you say that? Red Rock: His “dog care diploma” is written on the inside sleeve of the Darkness’ number 1 hit album “permission to land” for one thing, and all these dogs look pretty ill, that one impractically… I think that Labrador might actually be dead. Red Rock has his gazed fixed on the rotting corpse of a dead blonde Labrador, its stomach is swollen from the gases in the stomach caused by decomposition, in addition to that there are flying flailing around the eyes; laying eggs in the rotten sockets. Red Rock cringes and takes a few steps back whilst covering his mouth with the sleeve on his blue velvet jacket in an attempt to prevent him self from retching. Honest Barry: So what kinda dog re youz guys looking for? Red Rock: Where do you keep your puppies? Honest Barry looks down towards the floor where Red Rock is standing, then wipes some oozing snot from his nose whilst sniffing some back up. He causally points towards the floor whilst still trying to maintain his balance. Honest Barry: Down there mate. Red Rock: where? Honest Barry: Under your feet! Red Rock slowly hinges his head towards the floor and a trapdoor is now consumes his field of vision. A look over horror occupies his face because the realisation that there isn’t much noise coming from this trap door. Honest Barry: I’ll show you them, if you like. You can pick…urgh…pick your favo – Red Rock swallows and raises his hand in order to cut Honest Barry’s sentence short. Red Rock: Actually Barry…I think I changed my mind… I’m going to go…away now… Red Rock who is slowly turning paler as the fermenting smell from the trap door starts to rise through the gaps in the wood slowly backs away towards the door. Honest Barry begins to stumble around upon enjoying another swig on his bottle of cheap Asda smart price vodka, and then he falls flat on his face. Red Rock: come on D’avid let’s go! … Red Rock: D’avid? D’avid: I’m over here; I found the most gorgeous dog! Red Rock: Really? Red Rock mutters a sound of enthusiasm at D’avid’s potential find; he enters one of the kennels only to see D’avid on one knee petting a standard poodle. Red Rock stands in the kennel door way with a bemused expression carved on his face. The poodle is old, it must be about 11 years old, it is malnourished, thin and it has a lazy eye which is infected; causing it to appear that it’s popping out of the socket. It’s fur isn’t groomed in the typical poodle fashion, instead its fur is small black tight curls of hair with small grey wisps within them. Its head looks some what like a mop but in spite of all of this she is not without her charm. Red Rock: D’avid that is disgusting I can smell it from here! D’avid: But she is so beautiful I fucking LOVE it! At this point Honest Barry manages to get back to his feet, the sound of a sale awoke him from his alcohol fuelled coma. Honest Barry: That one’s called Whitney. Red Rock: Oh yeah I get it, her hair does look a bit like Whitney Houston. Honest Barry: Nah, its cas’ shes a black bitch that makes an awful racket. You want her or not? £100 mate. Red Rock: £100?! It’ll probably only live for another 3 month! D’avid: pleaseeeeeeee! Red Rock: NO! D’avid wraps his arms around Whitney’s neck and both look at Red Rock with puppy dog eyes, even though one of Whitney’s is swollen and full of puss. Red Rock tilts his head back with his hand on his hips; he lets out another loud sigh before giving his approval. Red Rock: fine! I’m sure she’ll do… D’avid: YES! Red Rock: But I want to call it something else, Whitney is a shit name. D’avid: How about Winnie? Red Rock: Winnie the poodle? Sounds alright. Red Rock reaches in to his wallet and take out some cash, D’avid skips around in the background; making Winnie very over excited, for an old dog she can still leap around like a dog of 1 or 2. Red Rock turns around only to find that Honest Barry has disappearing, a confused grimace replaces Red Rock’s previous expression. Red Rock: Barry? D’avid: Down there Red! He passed out! Ooohh Red Rock: Oh yeah so he has… CHEESE IT! Red Rock stuffs the money back in to his pocket and ceases the chance to walk away with the dog and the money. Red Rock grabs Winnie by her make shift collar made from industrial cable and heads for the exit. Red Rock: RUN D’AVID! … Red Rock: D’avid? Red Rock stops and turns around to see D’avid frantically trying to undo Honest Barry’s belt with some aggressive grunting that can only be likened to that of Heath Ledgers grunting whilst pounding Jake Gyllenhaal in that tent on Broke back Mountain. Red Rock: D’AVID! LETS GO! D’avid: Heh, I just can’t resist a passed out person they just don’t say no! D’avid scurries over to Red Rock whilst lifting his trouser back up. The two men and poodle slip away through the door leaving the shamefully red faced drunk Honest Barry to lie on his back and to inevitably choke on his own vomit.
