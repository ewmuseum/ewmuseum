Blow: Innocence Fallen

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-27-2006 My mind was spinning in circles, and for once in the past few months it wasn’t the cocaine that was causing it. I sat on my couch, with Sarah’s head staring back at me, her lifeless green eyes almost frozen in fear, with a tinge of pleading. The blood had matted her dark brown hair in certain areas, and her mouth was slightly opened. Almost like she was caught mid scream when it happened. I sat with the note in my hand, reading the two words over and over again, trying to figure out who it was that was doing this. Don’t Lose. XOXO Well, I hadn’ t lost. In fact, I’d beaten them both fairly handily, although Marquez proved to be more of a challenge then I suspected. Now he seemed to have a personal vendetta against me, what with the huge dramatic staredown at the end of the match. Whatever. He can try to come at me if he so chooses. I’m used to the attention. It seems like as soon as I step foot into a federation, and have a couple matches under my belt, I’m automatically targeted by multiple people. Flattering really, it shows their threatened by my talent level. They should be, I haven’t even begun to do what I’m capable of. I was a bit nervous for this upcoming match though. Not necessarily because of who, or what it was, but more because if someone cut off the head of someone I love for a filler match, what would their reaction be to me being in a title match? I needed to get my mother with me soon, and make sure she was safe. And Chad. As long as those two were with me, and in my sight, they wouldn’t be getting their heads lopped off by some psycho bastard who had it out for me. I picked up my cell phone, and scrolled through my contacts for my mom's number. .~. “What do you want with Tyson?” Sarah asked, her voice shaking from being cold, and scared. The man in the mask chuckled. “He’s a bad man Sarah, and he must get what he has coming to him.” He said, calmly. “He took something of mine that is very important to me, and now I must make penance for that by taking something of his that’s important to him.” “And what does that have to do wi…” Her voice trailed off into the darkness as it dawned on her what he was talking about. “Ah, the girl is smart I see?” He posed the question mockingly. “Fuck you.” She spat. “Such harsh words, towards a person who holds your life in his hands.” He said firmly. “You hold nothing. Kill me if you must, but you will get no information from me.” Her words were coming out with the force of a kitana now. Through his mask, you could feel his grin. “We shall see.” He started to walk towards the door. “So you’re going to kill me, without even telling me why?” She asked, her voice more defiant now. If she was going to die, she would do it going down swinging. He turned back to her. “My dear, whoever said anything about killing you?” Then he exited the room, leaving her alone once again. Scared, and now confused. .~. After the first night with Tim at the party, Tyson realized that he enjoyed the high of cocaine a lot more than he should, and it didn’t take long for the man with the addictive personality to latch onto the substance. It started casually, just at parties and what-not when people had it, and then turned into a need. A necessity. He would do anything to get it. Money, went towards coke. Work? Was no longer a priority, save to get money to buy cocaine. He had become a high class junky. It was a good thing that combined, he and Tim had lots of money, otherwise he’d have been a low class, broke junky. Tonight was no different than any other night that I had encountered. I was getting off a shoot, and about to head straight over to Tim’s house for a relaxed evening of porn, women, alcohol and blow. That was the trend that I was getting into, and I expected tonight to be no different than any other night. It’d been about 6 months since the first “taste” of my new favorite vice, and I was loving every minute of it. I arrived at Tim’s pad, and rang the doorbell, to which Tim himself answered moments later. “Buddy!” He exclaimed. “Hey bro, we all set?” I asked, already tasting the coke in my mouth. “C’mon in, I’ll show ya.” Tim said, turning and walking towards the back room. His house was a large two story house, with a lavish victorian contemporary feel to it. He’d had a decorator come in of course, but had added his own personal touch to it, with the likes of his weapons collection strewn about the house, and of course his favorite room of the house with the 72” Wide Screen TV, surround sound, theatre chairs, every video game console known to man, and tons of other goodies that every man in America would kill to have. We made our way through the hallway, down towards that room, and he opened the door for me. “Tada!!” he said. I peered inside, and saw a man sitting on the couch, facing the opposite direction of me. He didn’t turn, or make any acknowledgement that we were there. “Whose that?” I asked, warily. “A friend, c’mon.” Tim said. There was something in Tim’s voice that made me nervous, but I still went in, and jumped a bit when the door shut behind me. It was quiet in the room, not like the normal rucous that ensued when the girls were here. Instead, just a man, sitting in silence, myself and Tim. I mentally shook off any fears or uncomfortable feelings, and walked over towards the couch where the man sat. “I’m Tyson, what’s up?” I said, extending my hand to the man. He didn’t raise his hand. I was confused. “Tim, did you suddenly decide to be compassionate and befriend a blind mute??” Silence. “Tim, I sai…” I turned around to find the barrel of a pistol pointed directly at my face, with the sad expression of guilt, and fear on the face of Tim, directly behind it. “What the fuck?!” I exclaimed, jumping a bit. “Dude, wha the hell are you doing?” “I’m sorry Tyson.” He started. “I got scared, and didn’t know what to do, and I have to much going for me right now to let it get in the way, and you were here, and I didn’t know what to do…” he trailed off, shaking now. “What? Tim, you’re really freakin’ me out here. Quit it.” I said backing away. My elbow bumped the man on the couch who still hadn’t moved, and he fell forward, lifeless. “Oh Jesus…” I said, feeling sick to my stomach. “He’s…” “Dead?” Tim finished for me. “Yes. I killed him.” My mind was racing. “Jesus Tim, what the fuck!?” I said, throwing my hands back, and then sitting down, putting my head in my hands. “This isn’t happening, it’s not real…” I reached into my pocket for the small bag of coke I had on me, and dumped it onto table in front of me. I just needed to relax for a minute…just a moment. “I didn’t want to do this to you Tyson, you’re a good kid.” He said. “I have no other choice…” I looked up at him, and stood suddenly having the urge to run. And get as far away from this house as I could. “Fuck you Tim, you’re insane. I’m leaving now.” I said, staring at him. “I can’t let you leave.” He said, matter-of-factly. “What?” “You have to stay. And take this. It’ll look like a murder suicide attempt. I’m sorry…” His voice trailed again. I acted off of instinct, and the next few moments went by in a blur. I charged him, screaming at the top of my lungs. I saw the gun go off, and felt something hot in my stomach area. Then, I saw the ceiling, but it was getting darker, and darker for some reason. I saw Tim faintly above me, wiping something, and then bending down next to me. “I’m sorry friend…” he said, and stood. “You’ll pay…” I said, before everything went to black. .~. I dialed my mothers phone number as quickly as possible. It rang a few times, and I taped my fingers on my hip nervously. It clicked over to her voice mail. ”Hi, you’ve reached the home of Miranda DeBough. Please leave a detailed message after the beep.” Beep! “Mom, it’s Tyson. Call me as soon as you get this, it’s important.” I paused. “I love you.” I hit end, and then scrolled through my contacts until I found Chad’s number. Chad O’Brian was my best friend of my entire life basically. We grew up together, and our mothers were best friends, so naturally we became best friends. Chad was like my brother. The phone rang a few times, and he picked up. “Yo big man.” He said. “Chad, I’m glad you answered.” I said, relieved. “What’s up? You ok??” There was concern in his voice. I spent the next five minutes explaining everything that had happened over the past few days in detail. When I finished, he stayed silent on the phone for a few moments, taking it all in. “Wow.” “Yea, how do you think I feel man? I’ve got her head in my living room.” I said. “Did you call the cops?” He asked. “Fuck no. Whoever this is obviously has enough dirt on me already. I’d go down with them.” I stated firmly. “You know my past.” “Yeah, for sure.” He said, pondering the situation. “Look, I just need to figure this shit out…” I started. “Alright…I’ll be over in a bit.” He said reading my mind. “Ok, good. I just wanna get you and my mom around me until I figure this out. I don’t need more people getting whacked.” I said. “I hear ya. Want me to stop by your mom’s place on the way over?” He asked. “Ugh, that’d be great man. She didn’t answer when I called her, and it’s worrying me.” “I’m sure she’s fine, you know Miranda, always doing something.” He chuckled. “Heh, yea. You’re right.” I said, a bit more assured now that I was talking to Chad. “Kay, see you in a few.” I hung up the phone and sank back into my couch with a heavy sigh of relief. It felt good to know that Chad was ok, and he was going to get my mom. Once I was sure she was ok, I’d be able to get a grip on all of this, and start figuring out who the fuck was pulling these stunts, and then ultimately avenge Sarah’s death. Right now, I needed a distraction. I pulled up the AWC website, and looked at the card again. My third week into the AWC, and I was pit against Darcy Crisis, Frontier Champion. Why? It didn’t add up. I had a record of one and one, and granted I won my last match with style, was that any reason to gain a championship match? I laughed at myself. Listen to me. I’m a space case right now. They just see the talent I have, and see that Darcy is spending more time with his lover, than focused on the ring, and want to pu the belt on someone who deserves it. Makes sense. Crisis definitely had one hot woman at his side though. Amy wasn’t anything to cough about, that’s for sure. I guess she liked the long hair, rocker style shit though. Whatever, I suppose if that’s what you like, that’s what you like. Sarah didn’t like that. And just like that my mind wandered back to Sarah’s head on my living room table. And then, almost as if a sign, my scar started to hurt. .~. The door opened to the small unlit room once again, and the man with the mask entered once more. Sarah sat on the chair, hands still tied around the back of it, and raised her head to greet her intruder. “Oh, it’s you.” She said spitefully. “Yes, were you expecting someone else?” He asked. “Of course,” came the sarcastic reply, “my knight in shining armor.” “You should really keep that mouth of yours under wraps, it could have consequences.” The man said sternly. “Fuck off Jack-o. I consider myself dead already.” She spat. Her assailant stood, motionless for a moment, almost as if thinking about how to reply to her statement. For a brief moment, she felt triumphant, feeling like she stumped the madman who had kidnapped her. She had one upped him. Laid down her Ace, to find out how serious he was. Her intentions weren’t do die, but to live, so if she could call his bluff early, she would. He moved closer to her. “I would begin to be smarter with your words.” He responded. Then he stepped to the side. And Sarah screamed. .~. My doorbell rang again, and I raced to it, peering outside to see if it was Chad with my mother. Through the peephole, I saw nothing, and my heart sank, and I got a pit in my stomach. “Not again…” I said. I opened the door, and immediately looked at the ground. There was another bag. Tears began to well up in my eyes, and my breath shortened. I picked up the bag, and brought it inside. “No…please no…” I said over and over again. I opened the bag and looked inside, and screamed in anguish. Tears fell down my face as I fell to my knees, hurling all over the foor. .~. “Mommy?” A small voice called. Sarah stopped sceaming on the outside, but her eyes were soaked tears now, and all the feelings of strength and pride were blown away as she saw the small figure of her daughter Allison standing in front of her.” “You fucking bastard! You sick fucking bastard! Don’t you fucking put a finger on her!!!” She screamed, and then turned to Allison. “Ally baby, come here.” “Momma?” Her frightened voice said, and she ran towards her mother. Quicker than lightning, her kidnapper stepped in front of the little girl and caught her, snatching her close to him, and holding her as she frantically tried to get away, but her little body was to easy for him to hold. “DON’T TOUCH HER!!” Sarah screamed louder, over the terrified cries of her baby girl. .~. “It’s a girl!” The doctor’s voice echoed back to me as he lifted the small child in into the air. She was crying with her first breaths of life, and though she was covered in blood and fetus, she still looked absolutely beautiful. I stared down at Sarah smiling, and kissed her on the forehead. “She’s beautiful.” I said. “How about Allison?” Sarah asked. We’d been debating names back and forth for months now. And now, I didn’t care. Anything sounded perfect for my baby girl. “Allison it is.” I smiled at her. I’d never been so happy in my entire life. Sarah had filed a restraining order for her and Ally about a year ago. She didn’t want a “cokehead” around her or her daughter. Looking back I couldn’t blame her, but it still hurt like hell to not have my little girl around, or the love of my life. Sometimes I get myself mixed into things that I shouldn’t. I need to learn to fix that. .~. The man held Allison in his hands. Sarah calmed herself, realizing she needed to be the strong one for Allison right now. “Ally, calm down baby, it’s alright. It’s going to be alright. Look at mommy.” She said, in the most soothing voice she could muster at the moment. “You’re a good mother Sarah.” The man said. “Fuck you.” She said venomously. “Please, for her sake, watch your words.” He pleaded, mockingly. “If you do anything to her, I swear I’ll kill you…” Her voice trailed in anguish. He shrugged. “I’d like to see.” In one quick motion, he took Allison’s head in his hands, and twisted in opposite directions firmly, snapping her little neck like a toothpick. Her body fell limp to the ground before Sarah, who screamed... ...and screamed... and screamed. .~. I pulled myself back to my feet, and opened the bag more, revealing the head that was enclosed this time. I wiped my mouth of the puke, and pulled out the tiny head of my darling little girl. So young and innocent...she didn't do anything to deserve this. What had I done so wrong to deserve this horrifying punishment? “Why her…why…” I begain to cry again, but fought the sobs, and pulled the note off of her ear. Good win Tyson! I’m proud of you! Now, let’s go ride the open “frontier” shall we?? You know what to do.” -XOXO I threw up again.
