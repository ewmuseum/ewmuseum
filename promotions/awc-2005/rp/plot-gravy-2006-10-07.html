Plot Gravy

7 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 7, 2006 FADE IN: TEN DAYS AGO INT. BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING - DAY CAMERA is top down, in slow rotation over a busy wrestling ring. CUT TO: A close shot of young RAZOR, fresh out of high school, hitting the mat back first and hard. A larger student, BOBBY COMPTON, marches towards the fallen student with no good intentions in mind. He reaches down to bring him back to his level, but is caught by surprise when he finds his legs tangled and his back rolled up against the mat. IVAN KANNON, one of the head instructors, slides into the scene on his hands and knees, and feverishly counts the 1..2..3! Razor les Compton unfold, as he stands on his own feet and raises his hands in victory. CUT TO: A higher CAMERA shot of the same ring. The scene pans to the right, stopping in front a door that has scene better days. INT. OFFICE - DAY Wide shot of B.O.T. sitting nonchalantly in his threadbare desk chair, glancing up from his newspaper at DIMITRI every couple of seconds, who is pacing nervously back before him.. B.O.T. Can you stop that please? DIMITRI I’m sorry, I’ve just been thinking about those clowns who showed up a couple days ago. B.O.T. Why? DIMITRI Well, they were a bit intimidating. B.O.T. Didn’t you say you chased them away easily? DIMITRI Sure, but… B.O.T. But, but what? Remember when we went to the circus back when we were, like, ten? DIMITRI What’s that have to… B.O.T. Listen! Do you remember when we went to the circus? DIMITRI Sure, why? B.O.T. Were you intimidated by THOSE clowns? DIMITRI Maybe you’ve been hit in the head too many times to understand this, but this is a serious thing. Those guys are coming back, and there looking for money that YOU owe them. You better start filling in some blanks and getting this shit resolved, because I don’t want these guys to keep coming back here. B.O.T. Calm you palms, whisker biscuit. I‘ve got this week off, so ill hang around. DIMITRI I’m glad your so fucking calm. B.O.T. I’m upset your so fucking flustered. DIMITRI Jarrett, you don’t under… B.O.T. stands up and waves him off as he turns towards the door. B.O.T. Blah, blah, blah. Enough already, I’m gonna have a cigar. DIMITRI Well, make it quick, you still have to finish your half of this paperwork. B.O.T. shakes his head as he walks out, having just about enough of his “parental guidance.” EXT. OUTSIDE OF THE BSW - DAY Close shot of a Caucasian hand dialing numbers of a slick, sleek brick phone. INT. BSW GYMNASIUM - DAY Medium Shot of B.O.T. trotting out of the office, reaching into his finely pressed blazer pocket for a long brown Crown Royal. He pulls it up to his lips, and lets his stoic jaws do the work from there. EXT. OUTSIDE OF THE BSW - DAY Wide shot of a familiar black sedan parked conspicuously on an empty curb, reflecting the offensive rays of light beaming from that big, bright, beautiful balloon bounding across the blue blanket above. Three dark figures stand outlined in the windows. INT. BSW GYMNASIUM - GYM Over the shoulder shot of B.O.T. striding through the musty gym, the frame is just large enough to show the rampant action going on in the ring to his left as he passes it. The CAMERA bobs with his body, as well. Rockwell Gordy erupts in a small, muffed loop suddenly. All I want is to be left alone In my average home But why do I always feel like I'm in the twilight zone And I always feel like Somebody's watching me And I have no privacy I always feel like Somebody's watching me Tell me is it just a dream B.O.T. stops in his tracks. CAMERA swings around his shoulder to the front. He draws his sleek, sexy flip phone and, after a concerned gaze into the caller I.D. window, he proceeds into the CAMERA shot, blacking it out. FADE OUT FADE IN EXT. OUTSIDE BSW - DAY Establishing shot - CAMERA starts in the sun. It beams with relentless fury down to the earth below. It tilts down, passing fluffy white clouds, the ragged roof of the brick exterior of the Brothers School of Wrestling. B.O.T. eventually falls into the scene. He picks his phone up after leaning up against the door. B.O.T. Yes? VOICE Mr. Clemans? B.O.T. Who wants to know? VOICE Ill take that as a yes. B.O.T. What do you want? VOICE What you owe us. B.O.T. Who the hell is us? VOICE Does The Society ring a bell? A long, uncomfortable pause ensues. B.O.T. …No. VOICE You should, you have a hell of a debt to pay us. B.O.T. Why is that? VOICE Ask that girl you were with a couple weeks ago in New Orleans. B.O.T. Sashenka? VOICE Beautiful name, really. B.O.T. This doesn’t make sense. VOICE The only thing you need to understand is that you have two weeks to deliver us our money! B.O.T. You guys are real big on this money thing… CUT TO: Inside the black sedan, where GLUM sits on a sleek, brick phone in the back seat. GLUM Just protecting our investment, that’s all. B.O.T. Yeah we’ll how much will this investment cost me? GLUM Well, your remaining balance due to date, when rounded up, is about 500,000 dollars. B.O.T. American? GLUM Wh…Yes, of course American. B.O.T. Why to I owe this much, again? GLUM What are you deaf? I said ask the girl. B.O.T. Why can’t I ask you? GLUM Because I don’t want to answer you? B.O.T. Another question… You wouldn’t happen to be one of those three men of questionable moral fiber parked in that black sedan conspicuously on the empty curb out in front of my school, would you? GLUM Er…no. B.O.T. Are you lying to me? GLUM Er…no. B.O.T. So if I walk over there right now, and punch that through that window, the glass shards won’t slice your face into ribbons? GLUM Of course not. B.O.T. So if I ripped open the door to the back seat of that black sedan, took out the guy in the backseat on the cell phone, and beat his face in so badly, people would mistake him and the street as distant cousins, I wouldn’t be doing that to you right? GLUM N..no… B.O.T. What if I took a chainsaw and… Glum hangs up the phone quickly. CAMERA pulls up to the front seat, where BASE sits, ready at the wheel, and ZEIK rides shotgun. BASE You ok, boss? GLUM I think he knows were here. ZEIK I think your right. CAMERA swings to Zeiks POV as he looks out the window. He sees B.O.T. rolling up his impeccable sleeves, marching towards the car. GLUM Shit, drive! Base starts the car quickly. The sound of the engine starting signals B.O.T. to start striding towards the car. Before he can get to them, Base slams the gas and peels out. B.O.T. puts his hands on his hips, laughing to himself as he watches them leave. FADE OUT FADE IN INT. DINER - EVENING The grease spoon is bustling with early evening activity. Waitress dressed in drab pastels swing wide trays, balancing on their fingertips, up, around, and through the under brush of populated tables speckling the floor. The lights are bright, the chatter of patrons at a dull groan, the lousy oldies music just a hair louder. Medium shot of B.O.T., still ornately dressed, sits across from his more subtly equipped brother, Dimitri. B.O.T. looks under some sort of anxiety, as his dinner partner seems more on the confused side. DIMITRI So she…? B.O.T. (annoyed at his situation) Yea… DIMITRI When you were…? B.O.T. Uh huh… DIMITRI Why would she…? B.O.T. Dunno… But I’m gonna find out. And your gonna help. DIMITRI Not like I have a choice, if you don’t do something about these guys, there going to keep showing up at the School, and the students and staff are going to get suspicious. B.O.T. You think she got into some trouble during her stint in Europe? DIMTRI And brought it back to us? Most likely. B.O.T. And tricked me out of my money to save her ass. DIMITRI How’d you part with a million and a half so easily, anyway? B.O.T. I wanted to help the New Orleans rebuild effort. DIMITRI She gave you a blowjob, didn’t she. B.O.T. Yep… DIMITRI Christ, Jarrett. You got some dome, and now were all in deep shit. B.O.T. It was good, though. DIMITRI I mean, you never know how bad those guys are. They could come back and give us the Godfather treatment. B.O.T. It was really good… DIMITRI Do you realize you might get us killed over a 1.5 Million dollar hooker? B.O.T. Hey, that hooker was our friend, once. She told me she was apart of a charity organization and was looking for some help. I wanted to help out an old friend. DIMITRI I like Sashenka, too, you know. We all grew up together. Which is why Id like to know why shed put us in danger like this. B.O.T. Looks like we need to have a chat with her. DIMITRI I tried, I haven’t been able to get a hold of her. B.O.T. Figures. Hopes she turns up soon, I ship off again in a couple days. DIMITRI Where to now? B.O.T. Sevilla, Spain DIMITRI Estadio San Pablo? B.O.T. Yeah… DIMITRI Small venue. Probably no more than 7000 seats. B.O.T. That’s fine, I’m not there for the fans, anyway. DIMITRI Who’s your opponent? B.O.T. Couldn’t tell you… I just show up and beat the hell out of whoever is in front of me. DIMTRI Didn’t you lose the last match? B.O.T. So? Losing a match doesn’t mean I wasn’t a World Champion once. DIMITRI Yea, but now that your actually touring the world, it seems you can’t Champion anything. B.O.T. Listen, I don’t need your goddamn negativity right now. Positive thoughts, mother fucker…. One of the waitress swings by drops a steaming plate of something not even healthy in front of the duo. Also a pot of hot water and tea bags a sprawled on the table top. She struts away, but not after B.O.T. gives her skirt a glance. DIMITRI What do you presume I do while your away? B.O.T. Oh, you’ll be fine. These guys are jokes. If they were about anything, they would have done something by now. Suddenly, the booming sound of an explosion echos through the walls of the diner. A firey flash lights the eatery for a split second. CAMERA to shoot out of the nearest window. B.O.T. SHIT! A purple Plymouth Prowler is on fire, parked outside of the diner. B.O.T. runs into the shot, whipping off his blazer, frantically trying to put the fire out. He screams swears, as he realizes his efforts are in vain. CUT TO: EXT. OUTSIDE THE DINER - EVENING CAMERA pans right slowly, past B.O.T. who is angrily complaining to a pair of POLICE OFFICERS about the situation at hand. The pan continues, passing the smoldering car and a couple of FIREFIGHTERS surveying the damage. CAMERA pans into a close shot of Dimitri, looking on with disbelief. DIMITRI This is getting out of hand… Suddenly, the soulful voice of Jimi Hendrix pries its way out of the pocket its stuffed in. I have only one burnin' desire Let me stand next to your fire Let me stand next to your fire Hey, Let me stand next to your fire Oh, let me stand baby Let me stand next to your fire Let me stand next to your fire… Dimitri reaches into his pocket and pulls out a tiny sliding cell phone. Down comes the cover, and up comes the phone. DIMITRI I’m going to guess your not a telemarketer. FEMALE VOICE You got that right. DIMITRI You did this? FEMALE VOICE Not me, my men. DIMITRI What can I do to stop this? FEMALE VOICE This is why I’m glad I decided to talk to you. You were always the listener of the Clemans Brothers. You and I have a secret we need to tell your brother. And your brother owes me a little bit of money thanks to it. DIMITRI It can’t be… CAMERA cuts to a wide shot of B.O.T. pointing and flailing his arms around. CAMERA cuts back to Dimitri. FEMALE VOICE Barbossa was a fool, and I was a fool for ever getting involved with him. And though our son WILL pay for his mistakes, I owe it to him to tell him the truth. DIMITRI I don’t know how he’ll take it… FEMALE VOICE Well, now I’ve got his attention, so we’re one step closing to finding out. DIMITRI Listen, wait until after the 13th… He’s trying to get something good started right now… FEMALE VOICE Ill wait for now… DIMITRI And call off your dogs, or he might hurt them. FEMALE VOICE Ill comply with that too. But October 14, I expect you, your brother, and Sashenka on the next plane back to New Orleans, half a mill in tow. DIMITRI Deal. FEMALE VOICE See, I like you, Dimitri. I almost wish you were my child instead. DIMITRI (Sarcastically) Try not to kill anyone till then, Harken. HARKEN The Society is here… DIMITRI (Reluctantly)…And everywhere.
