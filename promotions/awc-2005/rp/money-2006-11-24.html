Money

24 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/06 James Hopkins' tag team experiment with Tariq had fallen on its face, an unmitigated disaster. Clearly, Clarisse wasn't ready for primetime, and he saddled one of his more promising wrestlers, a Champion in his fed, nonetheless, with a greenhorn. Even just watching that tag team affair, how inept Clarisse was in the ring, that was painful. He really felt bad and had a strong urge to do something to make it up to one of his most faithful roster members. The next day at the arena, James sat at the wrestlers' entrance in an attempt to catch Tariq, usually one of the first guys to walk in. As the door first opened, James stood up straight and proud, putting his best face on to inform Tariq of the news. The figure that walked in looked to have the brown skin and curly hair that Tariq had, but the glare from the morning sun made it hard to see. 'Tariq?' 'No, Tariq's running a little late.' It was Rahman. 'Oh.' James waited there as wrestler after wrestler piled in. Jericoholic Anonymous, Barry Peters, Sgt. Bloodinguts, everyone except Tariq. James started to get a little nervous. Finally the door opened one more time and in stepped the man he was looking for. 'Tariq! What took you so long? You're usually the first one here?' Tariq was taken slightly aback at his greeting. 'Umm, I had a late night last night.' 'Oh, that's okay. C'mon, I need to talk to you.' 'Umm, I'm not in trouble, am I?' 'Oh no, no no... what would have given you that impression?' The greeting, Tariq thought to himself. They walked down the corridor to James' office. Tariq was a little confused at everything that was happening. James couldn't be lying to him about being in trouble, could he? Tariq did all he could to try and pass off Clarisse as a competant wrestler against men twice her size, but she was too overzealous. She wanted to do too much. Finally, they got to his office. 'Take a seat.' Tariq sat down in the chair in front of James' desk, while James closed the door and took his seat. 'Now Tariq, about last night...' 'Listen, James, I can explain...' 'Oh no, you don't need to explain anything, Tariq. I wanted to apologize to you for making you team with Clarisse.' Oh, Tariq thought. 'You see, I tried to get her over by teaming her with one my most reliable hands, and it blew up in my face. You shouldn't be saddled with my projects. I should be pushing you to the stars. I know last night was pretty bad...' Not really, he thought. Teaming with an incompetant woman wasn't so bad since he got some action out of the deal. 'So what I'm going to do to make it up to you is put you in a position to get a title shot.' 'Wow... cool! Thanks!' 'Anytime, Tariq. Next card, you'll wrestle Emilio Josias for Number One Contendership to the LBW Championship.' 'Awesome... I can't wait to tell Rahman.' Tariq got up out of his chair and scurried out of the office. James finally let the tension out of his shoulders and sighed relief. He got up and went to seek out the other man in this match. He looked in the locker room area, but only found Tariq gushing to Rahman about his title opportunity. He looked out to the ring area, but Josias wasn't training out there. He checked the bathrooms, the areas outside the arena, he even went down to Tony Luke's to see if Josias was in line for a sandwich. All of those places turned up empty. Finally, on a hunch, James walked quietly over to the broom closet. He gently turned the handle and violently swung the door open. There, in the broom closet, sat Emilio Josias, rubber band around his right arm and needle in his left hand. 'Jesus Christ, Emilio, haven't I told you enough times to keep that shit at home?' 'Sorry sir, but... but... you aren't going to fire me now, are you? Are you? This job is all I have, I've been kicked out of too many other feds... please, please...' 'Relax, I'm not going to fire you.' In all reality, James should have fired Josias the first time he caught him shooting up meth, but in that same reality, Josias was one of his biggest draws. In a way, the meth helped his frenetically-paced character along nicely. The bottom-line was justification enough for him to be kept aboard. 'I've come to tell you that at the next card in two weeks, you're going to be facing off against Captain Suleimon for the number one contendership to the LBW Championship.' 'Oh, really?' Josias' ears perked up. LBW Championship. A higher spot in the card meant more money. More money meant... 'Thanks mang...' 'Don't mention it.' James walked away. As soon as he did, Josias got out his cell phone and dialed immediately. 'Hey yo, man, I got news...' By the time 6 o'clock, PM, rolled around, Tariq and Rahman were the only ones left in the ring area. Tariq was training extra hard in preparation for his big match. Rahman's eyes looked weary from a long day of training, while Tariq's sheer excitedness kept him going despite being out late the night before. Rahman started to get annoyed at the bright-eyed, bushy-tailed demeanor of his student. 'Don't you think we should wrap this up soon?' 'I don't know. I want to be on top of my game so I can get my shot at an honest-to-God World Championship.' 'For one, it's not a World Championship, because LBW only runs in the Philadelphia area. Two, it's getting a little late. You need rest and you need to eat.' 'I... I guess so. Let's go to Tony Luke's. I'll treat.' 'Look at you, Mr. Moneybags.' Rahman said playfully. 'Yeah yeah, c'mon.' Tariq and Rahman went back to the locker room, and the student reached in his travel bag for his wallet. He pulled the billfold out and noticed something was missing, namely all his cash, $300 worth. 'Umm, Rahman... did you happen to take any money out of my wallet?' 'No, why would I do something like that?' 'I don't know, to play a prank, pay off something and then pay me back later?' 'No, I would ask before I took money off of you.' 'Dammit, dammit, dammit, dammit! Someone stole cash out of my wallet then!' 'Are you sure? You didn't leave the money at home?' 'I'm God-damned positive that I had $300 in my wallet. I checked this morning after Clarisse left the apartment, and it was still there after I got money out of it to go to the vending machines.' 'Dammit... I'll go tell James.' 'Okay... I'll go see if I can find anyone else around here. Dammit.' Rahman left, but Tariq lingered behind. He made a cursory scan of the locker room before he left to make sure no one else was in there. As soon as he stepped out of his locker room, he came face to face with a man clad in a gray jumpsuit and mask. Before Tariq had time to react... WHAM! Tariq awoke 20 minutes later from Rahman shaking him. He was groggy, but otherwise, he felt okay. 'Tariq! Tariq!' 'Ugh... I've come to... stop shaking me.' 'I told James. He doesn't know who'd do such a thing. Let's get you into the locker room.' Rahman helped Tariq up and sat him down on the bench in the locker room. He got Tariq an ice pack to put on his head.' 'Do you remember what happened?' 'Vaguely... all I remember is coming out of hte locker room, and some specter in gray attacking me. Last thing I remember seeing was something shiny on a gray blob coming at my head.' 'Ah,' Rahman said as he applied the ice. 'Brass knuckles probably. That's why you were out.' Tariq held the ice pack to his head, and as the icy mass shot through his bump, it jolted his brain into idea. 'Hey... didn't Josias... my opponent... didn't he get busted for meth use?' 'That was rumored. I'm not sure how true it is, but yeah, there's a chance. Why do you ask?' 'Because... I think he might be behind all this.' 'That might be a stretch. I think that might be the shot from the knucks talking.' 'Regardless... tomorrow, I'm going to find out myself.' Tariq sat with the ice on his head, while Rahman lit up a cigarette and sat in the corner, keeping a watchful eye on his student. The next day, it was Tariq's turn to stand waiting at the wrestlers' entrance. One by one, the LBW roster filed in with no sign of Josias. Finally, he walked in, 40 minutes later than the last person as usual. Tariq wasted no time accosting him, grabbing him by his jacket and throwing him against the wall. 'Hey hey hey... I know we're wrestling on Saturday, but there's no need to be attacking me now, holmes.' 'Shut up. I know you were the one who stole the money out of my wallet last night.' 'What? You got robbed last night?' 'Don't play dumb with me, I know you did it. It's something your kind does.' 'Whoa, whoa, mang, don't you be flinging no racist crap at me. Not all Puerto Ricans...' 'Shut up, it's not because of your background. It's because of your meth habit.' 'Hey... HEY! No, I'm clean, mang. I haven't done that in a year, clean and sober. Don't you go accusing me of things like that, or else you'll really be looking for trouble.' Tariq let him down. 'Look me in the eye and tell me you didn't steal my money.' Josias lifted his head and stared Tariq down hard. 'I didn't steal your damn money.' Tariq kept the gaze locked for a moment more before turning in disgust and heading towards the common locker rooms. He felt his wallet bulging out of his back pocket; he had stuffed it full of money as a decoy for the person who had stolen his money last night. He walked into the locker room and set his bag down. He made another cursory inspection of the locker room before leaving. AS he walked out, again, he came face to face with the man in gray. Tariq jumped back quickly, prepared for him this time. The gray man swung again with his brass-knuckled fist, but Tariq caught it and disarmed him of the knuckles. The assailant deftly moved his other arm over and landed a grazing blow on Tariq's chin, enough for Tariq to break the hold on his other arm. While Tariq was still slightly dazed, the gray man quickly went behind him and grabbed the wallet out of his back pocket. When Tariq got his wits back about him, the gray man was already on his way out the door. Tariq saw which way he went out and gave chase. All through the corridors of the arena, Tariq chased the man in gray. The assailant tried to obstruct Tariq's path by knocking things over behind him, but Tariq hurdled each obstacle with little difficulty. The man in gray looked back, seeing that his efforts were at best keeping Tariq a steady distance behind him. He then looked forward... CRASH! He didn't see the janitor until he was too close to avoid him. The man in gray tumbled over with the janitor, and Tariq pounced on the man who stole his wallet. 'Alright, now we're going to see who the hell you are.' Tariq pulled the mask off, and to his surprise, it wasn't Josias, or any other wrestler on the roster. In fact, it wasn't anyone that Tariq had seen before. He snatched his wallet back and got up. Once to his vertical base, he stomped his boot on the assailant's chest, and without taking it off, he helped the poor janitor to his feet. 'Listen, go to James Hopkins' office and tell him to call the police,' Tariq said to the janitor, who then hurried off in the direction of where Tariq was sending him. 'See Tariq, I told you that Josias wasn't involved,' Rahman said to Tariq as they sat in the back waiting for their cue to come out for his match. 'I still don't understand it. Why would he get dressed up like that and not only steal from me, but assault me too if he didn't have anything to do with him?' 'I don't know.' 'And they didn't find any of the money on him. I'm sure he couldn't spend it all. He had to have given it to Josias...' 'Listen, he was probably a drug addict too. I mean, not everything's connected to the business, y'know? Maybe he just knew he could steal from the wrestlers here. Just drop it, okay?' Tariq nodded and said, 'I have to go to the bathroom.' The young Turk left the locker room. He closed the door behind him and then turned around to see the man in gray immediately swinging at him with knucked fist. Twenty minutes later, Rahman was waking him up again. 'Dammit... Tariq...' Tariq woke up groggy. 'He... attacked again...' 'Dammit... you're up. You've got to get up.' 'But... I...' There was no time for explanation. Rahman stood Tariq up and got him on his way. Tariq walked down the hall punch drunk, still woozy from the knuckle shot he got 20 minutes ago. As he walked out of the curtain, the arena of fans jeering at him seemed to spin around and around like he was on a carousel. He thought about turning back and waiting until he was healed, but if he did that, he'd forfeit and lose his shot at the gold. He staggered forth to the ring and wobbily entered. The bell rang, piercing his mind so much that he had to put his hands over his ears. In that moment of disorientation, Josias took advantage with a boot to the gut and a powerbomb. The ref counted three as the match was over in record time. Tariq lost his shot at an LBW Title match, and that realization made his head swim even more. He rolled around on the mat until something caught his eye. It was a gray piece of cloth, sticking out of the back of Josias' tights. Tariq focused his blurry vision on it, and an eyehole became clear on it. The mask... Josias had a mask too. It all became clear to him. Josias attacked him before their match, and he was probably behind the whole plot. What an asshole, Tariq thought. What an asshole... with a brilliant plan.
