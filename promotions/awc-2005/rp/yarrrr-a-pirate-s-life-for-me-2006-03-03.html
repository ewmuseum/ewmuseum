Yarrrr, a pirate's life for me

3 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/06 'You know, when Jack Murphy said that when wrestling was on the marquee, I thought he was making sense.' An offhand comment from Captain Suleimon reached the ears of Dogan, who was loading a small boat with shovels. 'But it really is, man,' Dogan said. Suleimon was caught off-guard, seeing that he didn't think anyone had heard him. Rahman was sleeping in the hotel, Hayvan out getting some food for their nautical journey. He thought Dogan was still gathering items for this 'training' quest he had thought up. 'I mean, when it comes down to it, everything is built towards a final conflict in the ring. I mean, even with that Knowledge Challenge you set out on Fresh... I'm sure you're only doing it to goad some guy in the back into a confrontation.' 'Oh, you know me too well, Dogan. It seems you've caught on after all those times I schooled you in the last month.' 'You haven't schooled me. You're still a sociopathic Anglophobe with serious attachment issues.' 'You and your fancy terms. I wonder, do you call the majority of Americans Islamophobes if you're into this Freudian nonsensical misdiagnosis?' Dogan wasn't going to have this conversation again. 'Just forget I said that. Still though... don't you think you're taking this just a little too far?' Suleimon furrowed his brow and shook his head at his friend. 'I am taking nothing too far. Jack Murphy is absolutely correct when he says that Harber is trying to take the wrestling out of the AWC. Seriously, a Boardwalk Match?' 'You have a problem with that why?' 'Foolish, foolish man. The object of the match is not to pin another man, or make him tap, or even shut him into an iron maiden. It is... to hunt for buried treasure. Buried treasure! How ludicrous!' 'But you have to fight the other guys to do that, you know. I mean, there will be plenty of wrestling action to go around.' 'Don't be foolish. For all you know, someone like Red Rock or Hate could just stick their arms into the stockpile and pick out the Relentless Championship belt and run off like a ninny. Don't think it hasn't entered the back of my mind that Harber has rigged this to make sure that the one who rightfully deserves the title doesn't get it.' Dogan stared a hole in Suleimon. 'You are one paranoid motherfucker, you know that?' 'Not paranoid... realistic.' Dogan shook his head and starts to head back over to the car to unload some more stuff, but the voice of the Captain made him turn his head back abruptly. It was something he thought he'd never have heard from the mouth of his hateful friend. 'So since Harber wants to play this game, well, I might as well prepare for it. When in Rome, do as the Romans do.' 'What did you say? When in Rome?' 'Yes. Did I stutter?' 'No, no... I'm just surprised. What happened to your fuck the American man attitude? I mean... I thought you were crazy to do this, but you're doing it to... conform?' Conformity to a Western concept. Suleimon hadn't thought about it that way, and the point raised to him by his sometimes belligerent friend almost made him throw up in his mouth a little bit. But he didn't. He had to think fast in order to save some face. 'It's not... it's not about conformity. Conformity will not get me what I want, but neither will complete and total defiance.' 'What do you want?' 'Right now? I want to save the Relentless Championship. I want to erase the stain of it being discarded, thrown in the trash heap like it was and to be gotten by this means of treasure hunting instead of wrestling. If I have to make a deal here, so be it.' Suleimon was no dummy. As militant he was, he still knew that war was only the continuation of politics by other means. At heart, all warriors are politicians. Maneuvering would get him what he wanted. 'So, since it seems that I don't have to wrestle to win this match, I won't train for it like it's a wrestling match. I'll train for it like it was an exercise in digging up buried treasure. Which is why we're doing this, as I told you earlier.' Dogan shook his head. 'Well, at least getting out on the sea is a little better than staying in that damn hotel room. Although I have to ask, are you going to be Blackbeard or Long John Silver?' Dogan's attempt at humor was not appreciated by Suleimon. 'When our pirates sailed the Mediterranean, they didn't need silly names. They took what they wanted.' More indoctrination. Great. 'And I will exactly do just that. Take what I want. Be it through wrestling hold or seizing buried plunder.' As before, Dogan shook his head and just stopped trying to reach Suleimon. At times, he didn't even know why he was still friends with him. But there's always more to people than their political leanings. Dogan knew that. Sometimes, he just wished that Suleimon would realize that as well.
