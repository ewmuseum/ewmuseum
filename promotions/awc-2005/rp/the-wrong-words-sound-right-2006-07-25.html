The Wrong Words Sound Right

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: july 25th Previous Wednesday, 9:24 AM Something was seriously getting under her skin. It was a cold, stinging sensation that came about in the presence of her arch nemesis; Not a person, though. The almighty Aimz could easily be reduced to jelly if shown so much as a pin. Despite her tattoos and even the piercings, she was afraid only of medicinal needles - possibly because she saw no physical improvment from their administration... just sore muscles and unsightly marks on her arms. Sight in her mismatched eyes was weakened and fuzzy, but the cause remained just beyond her comprehension... At least, until she tried moving her right arm. ''JESUS!'' The burning overwhelmed her, absolutely snatching the breath from her body for a fleeting moment. For a second, she was convinced that her tanktop had caught fire - but a look down through her hazy eyes proved that theory wrong, because she plainly saw herself wearing nothing but a hospital gown. She didn't remember getting there, and the panic that was induced by knowing she was physically injured wasn't helping to ease the confusion at all. Her luck pulled through, though, because a soothing voice popped up from somewhere in the foggy room. '' 'Afternoon, dopey,'' came Darcy's greeting, ''I know you feel like you're dying right now, but they just did a tiny scope on your shoulder to make sure there wasn't any major tissue damage. You'll be home when the morphine wears off a little, and then it's rest for a week until you get to go out and get your arm busted up again!'' A nurse came in and slipped something into her IV drip. It looked like she'd nodded at Darcy, who gave her thumbs up before they both looked back at Amy, who was staring back at them through fast-fading eyes... PRESENT... (Saturday) Jeph Howe had called her out to a commercial building he used as a jam space, art gallery and t-shirt company headquarters alike... and for no apparent reason. But she had a feeling it had nothing to do with clothing, art or guitars, just by the nervous tone in his voice. He'd spoken like a little boy caught with his hand in the cookie jar, which led her to wonder exactly what he'd gone out and done after they left the pool hall that morning. Of course, she then remembered that Jeph wasn't mentally capable of procuring any drugs to take, let alone harming anyone - accidentally or intentionally. He was the biggest of tattooed teddy bears. At least, that's what she thought while walking into the warehouse offices that afternoon. She was mostly wrong, and knew it when greeted with the sight of red duct tape, rope, a bungee cord, handcuffs and... an apple juice box? The man himself was standing over those tools, sipping at his juice and glancing around nervously. ''Did anyone follow you?'' he snipped, ''I can't have anyone following you. Darcy's not with you, is he? I told you not to bring h--'' Amy furrowed her brow, ''For Christ's sake, calm down. What's going on, and why is there a rape kit on the floor?'' Jeph was panting from nerves, ''Remember when we were dating, and I promised to make all of your wildest dreams come true?'' ''... This,'' the redhead grimaced, looking to the rope and handcuffs on the floor, ''Had BETTER not be going where it seems to...'' ''Oh, it isn't. But it IS what you want it to be.'' He put his hand on the knob of the shut door that stood ominously behind them both, and opened it to the garage area of his little office space, where Amy could hear muffled words and scuffing feet. She grew nervous, and found only shock when she saw the source of the noise. ''... What the FUCK did you do?! Jeph, this isn't funny...'' ''But, you said...'' ''I was talking shit, you fucking dolt. I DO THAT, REMEMBER?!'' ''Ames...'' ''No matter what I said, I didn't expect to be an accessory to kidnapping!'' When Jane saw Amy, her face was surprised, then relieved that her kidnappers weren't the mob after her father, and then even more afraid than if they HAD been the mob. In case you weren't paying attention, hell hath no fury... and all of that. ''I've gotta tell Darcy,'' Amy shouted, almost entirely ignoring any of Jane's expressions, ''Fuck, you took this WAY too far... I can't be held responsible...'' But Amy wasn't stupid. She knew that the moment Jane had seen her, the misdrawn scheme would be pinned to her shoulders. Even the truth that she hadn't meant that she actually wanted Jane to 'disappear', and that Jeph had taken the ball and run with it, wouldn't do... because with such illegal activity involved, nothing would sound quite right. And the police couldn't find out. With her record, a kidnapping wouldn't even give a jury a second thought... Whoa, her head was going too far beyond her hands. The situation was present, and she had to focus on not getting caught. ''I'm gonna puke,'' Jeph groaned, ''Look, I'm sorry... I was tryi--'' ''Just go to the bathroom and toss up, fuckbox.'' Speaking of caught, Jane was still handcuffed - and tied, for good measure - to a bolted steel shelving system, piled high with folded hoodies that Jeph had yet to sell. He was as bad a businessman as he was a kidnapper, but that was neither here nor there... Jeph filed out of the room, holding his stomach all the while, leaving Amy alone with her lover's kidnapped ex-lover. Previous Thursday, 9:24 PM Darcy stirred awake earlier than Amy, as always... or so he thought. She'd gotten into the habit of waking up early, after being knocked out by painkillers 18 hours a day since their win over Chainz and Anton Assault. She was already wide awake and relaxing, staring thoughtlessly at the morning news from her pillow. '' 'Mornin','' Darcy muttered, ''How you feeling?'' The redhead beside him grinned, gingerly touching a hand to her arm, ''I can't believe they let us on a plane when I was that medicated, with a completely ass-backwards shoulder. I don't even REMEMBER the flight. Hell, I can't even remember my arm actually getting hurt. Aside from that, I'm fine.'' ''That, my dear, is the power of modern medicine.'' ''You mean, highly addictive painkillers that were popped into me like candy by a bunch of scared EMTs?'' He chuckled. ''It's not like they had any doubt whether or not you could handle high doses of drugs.'' Amy smiled at the dig on her drug-riddled past, but didn't hesitate to get to the very first question on her conscious mind that morning... ''Has Jane curled up to die yet?'' PRESENT (Saturday)... ''Okay, look...'' She sighed. ''This is probably the only nice thing I'll ever say to you,'' said Amy, ''But this wasn't ever supposed to happen. I said some shit to Jeph over a few drinks, and he's a nutjob sometimes...'' In that second, Amy could've sworn she heard an expletive mumbled from behind Jane's bound lips. She waited another second, and it was confirmed - a very muffled, but audible 'Bitch' could be heard clearly. ''You really, really need to... y'know... not say that kind of thing right now. But, just in ca--'' the first sign of Amy's mean streak started to show in her eyes as she reached for the duct tape that was over Jane's mouth, ''Whoa, that looks like it'd hurt like a bitch to be torn off. Especially with those little pieces of your hair stuck in it...'' Not hesitating for even a second, she ripped the tape from her accidental enemy's mouth and actually chuckled slightly when she heard Jane's pained moan. ''While you recover and take a second to think of how you're going to thank me for getting you out of Jeph's feeble hands, I want you to know that I had no part in arranging or orchestrating this. I know that sounds like bullshit given, well, the fact that I honestly hate you more than basically anything right now... but you've gotta believe me.'' Jane hardly seemed amused and as she opened her mouth to speak - or scream - Amy quickly pasted another strip of duct tape onto it. Possibly for the pleasure of ripping it off again, but she'd done it mostly to keep Jane from saying anything that might spark the redhead's well-known temper. Satisfied with the momentary silence, Amy continued, ''I know what you're thinking, but I'm gonna tell you what I think everybody in this situation knows; If I actually wanted to get you out of the picture, I'd either do it myself or get someone FAR more capable and dangerous than Jeph. Bless his heart, 'cause he's one of my best friends, but he'd sooner shoot himself in the left nut than actually hurt a living creature. I think that's obvious, because he's currently in the washroom puking his guts out over how far he took this. He gets over zealous about doing what he thinks I want. You understand men...'' She paused again, ''Actually, you don't, or you would've known to keep the good thing you had... which, by the way, you won't ever be getting back.'' Jane's eyes widened at what could have been taken as a threat, but Amy corrected herself quickly, ''No no no, you're not gonna catch me in THAT. I just know Darcy... I think... and I know he wants no part of you in any way, shape or form. I'll go to my grave trying to make him happy.'' Without hesitation, Jane chortled from beneath her duct tape mask. ... That didn't settle well. Amy's eyes bugged out for a moment, before she regained composure by staring holes through Jane. ''You really need to stop doing that...'' That's when Jane rolled her eyes. ''This girly, catty crap won't cut it with me...'' Another chuckle from the hostage... ''Seriously, you know I don't have the best temper.'' An eye roll... and a one-handed choke at the palms of a redhead. ''Look, bitch, you need to cut the brave act before I become a real criminal here. I don't want to have to hurt y-- okay, I DO want to hurt you... but I'm not going to,'' she said, releasing her grip on Jane's throat, ''You just need to be cool about this.'' Jane simply nodded. Jeph returned to the room, wiping his mouth and dismissing Amy, who mumbled 'You're dead for this' into his ear as she passed him, by telling her that he'd handle their prisoner for the time being - not wanting to be in the same room for fear of another confrontation over his mistake. She complied, and stepped out into the office area to sit down and think things through... Can't tell Darcy... Can't let Jane go, because she'll tell Darcy... Can't get caught... Jeph had made a royal mess for her to sort out, but the obvious semi-solution was in front of her: Convince Jane that you didn't plan this. Basically, Amy had to do the impossible. Early That Saturday, 9:24 AM The world is filled with poses. One, the everyman at the bar. Mid twenties, dirty blonde whose scalp you can see through his military haircut. Polo shirt and cheap beer - still bottled, of course - in hand, he's scoping out the wildlife bent over the table across the room, possibly hunting for tonight's rump roast. Two, the bimbo that One sights like a prized game animal. It's a given that her rack is a bit more impressive than the one on the buck your dad shot when you were 5, but a bullet through the skull hurts a whole lot less than what she's wanted for. Three, uninterested in Two, but pretending for the sake of One. The traditional wingman. Four, the girl who doesn't mind being seen in a bar at 9 AM. Tattoos and blaze red hair. Three keeps glancing her way, One whispers in his ear and snickers. Two wouldn't dare look at her the wrong way. Five, running straight to Four. Five stereotypes, and Darcy wasn't even in the picture. Jeph Howe was the one running at Amy, and he feigned a punch her way. ''Jackass, Jeph!'' she grinned, gaining the attention of One and Two, who already looked at her with a sneer, ''You nearly spooked me outta my chair. I think that's worth a four dollar shot.'' Jeph cracked his tattooed neck and nodded at the bartender, ''Four of those four dollar raspberry thingies.'' Razztini shots. Half straight tequila, half red rasberry Sourpuss. Double liquor served with a smile, and Amy took back three at a time and Jeph had the fourth. That was their greeting, and it gained a bit more attention from the three others who were drinking at that early hour. Even through the bitter shots and hugs for her friend, Amy noticed Two glancing to One and heading to the bathroom. ''Afternoon delight,'' Jeph chuckled, ''You see that?'' ''Of course.'' One followed, Two was gone, they were about to multiply and Three was still alone, but now trying hard not to look at Miss Four Campbell. Amy sighed, ''Shot for the lonely Wingman across the bar there, on me. Whatever he wants.'' Three looked ready to strike up a conversation, but Aimz hated numbers more than anything and quickly ushered Jeph into the next room - a pool hall with a leather couch, two tables and a pair already playing. ''Pool?'' he queried, ''I haven't seen you in weeks, and you just wanna play pool?'' ''God no, I just like the couch over here.'' ''So why'd you wanna meet?'' Amy sighed again, ''Here? Because I knew I'd have to wait at least an hour for your ass, and numbering the locals is the only thing that keeps my mind busy for a minute. Why'd I wanna meet up in the first place? 'Cause girls tell secrets, and you're the best girlfriend with a dick that I've got. Darcy's ex is back.'' The two had history. Jeph had dated Amy briefly, and lived with her far longer. While she was poor and coming back from cocaine as she went into AWC, she was on his couch. He'd do anything for her and, though she'd occasionally taken advantage of that fact, she appreciated it more than anything. ''His ex, huh?'' he shook his head, ''And he's doing what, exactly? I'm gonna guess he isn't being a good boy by keeping his distance, otherwise you would've just called me instead of wanting a drunken sit-down.'' ''It's not that. She's in some kind of trouble, but I wish it'd catch up to her,'' said Amy, ''They've been spending time together, and I know he feels badly for her... I wish she'd get cancer or something.'' ''That's kinda mean to wish on someone.'' ''It's his past, Jepha. You know what happens when that comes back, and you know how many times it's fucked me over. I can't help but...'' ''Are you alright? Seriously?'' ''Yeah,'' sniffled the redhead, ''So much of me just wishes she'd disappear.'' PRESENT...(Saturday) ''Where's Jeph?!'' Amy was shrieking at Jane, who'd just emerged from the garage without binds or duct tape. ''He fainted, the poor thing, and now I'm getting out of here...'' Jane strutted toward the door, trying to avoid Aimz' path, but got slammed hard against a wall by the redhead. It was an unexpected move on Amy's part, but instinct had kicked in. ''Janey,'' she said, ''You really need to think, here. I can't just let you go now, 'cause the truth isn't going to fly if I'm telling Darcy that this was all Jeph taking one of my comments too far, and you're across the room in handcuffs. So, though I want to, I can't let you out...'' Jane struggled slightly, but kept back... it was well known that Amy was a dirty, rough fighter and even though her strength might be equal, few people could fight and beat her without tight rules. Jane cocked her head slightly, perplexed by this particular line of reasoning. ''Exactly how is keeping me locked up going to keep YOU out of trouble with Darcy?'' ''I need you to tell Darcy that I had nothing to do with this,'' pleaded Amy, ''Because if I tell him myself, that sounds too sketchy. I know my friend is a dumbass, and that you probably don't like me for now having the good part of the biggest mistake you ever made, but I'm not gonna sit here and have you run off not telling him exactly HOW fucking shocked I was when I walked in here. Until I convince you, or figure out another way to get myself out of the mess Jephass over there put me in, I can't let you waltz outta here.'' ''And who, praytell, is to say you're telling the truth?' Jane said, who was beginning to get incensed. ''How do I know you're not just trying to wipe your hands of this because your dimwit sidekick committed the most obvious kidnapping anyone has ever seen?'' Jane had a habit of letting her mouth run laps in front of her brain, even when she was clearly in no position to handle the consequences. Still, she shook her head at Amy with a hint of condescendence. ''Nu-uh. No way. I'm not letting you get away with what you've done to my family. You can rot in jail, or hell... whichever one you get to first.'' ''Your family? I don't even know you. I don't care about your family. If I was ACTUALLY kidnapping you, it'd be to keep you away from prodding into my life with Darcy, you dumb fucking cunt. If I was part of some mob out to get your family, I probably wouldn't have to fight for a living, genius.'' Amy growled, then cuffed Jane stiffly in the cheek, ''...And only I can talk about Jeph's dumb ass like that, so cram it. Since you just convinced me that you HAVE no honor, and that I can't trust you to tell my boyfriend the truth, I don't know what to do with you... but rest assured, the only angry mob you need to worry about right now is ME, and that's only if you don't learn how to shut up long enough to keep me from wanting your nose replaced with your forehead.'' Jane recoiled from the blow, falling back into the wall. She trembled as she attempted to regain her composure. Her pride hurt more than her face, but it was still enough to bring tears to her eyes. ''Darcy will f-find me,'' she said, doing her best to keep the sobs from creeping up her throat. ''You won't get a-away with this.'' ''Look on the bright side,'' Aimz sighed for the thousandth time that day as she handcuffed Jane and escorted her back to where Jeph was just stirring to consciousness, ''The real people after you want you so they can hurt your family, but I'd only ever want you to keep you away from my love.'' She continued... ''So as long as you're here, you're out of danger from the real kidnapping artists. If you don't piss me off, we'll soon have everything cleared up and you'll have spent some quality time under the radar.'' It wasn't a very positive 'bright side', but it would do. As happy as she was to have stricken fear through the woman she hated, Amy couldn't stand to see someone as upset as Jane was getting. ''Okay, I swear I'm not really going to hurt you, and I wouldn't even know how to hurt your family if I wanted to. You don't know me, but Darcy will tell you someday that I keep my word no matter what.'' Amy tried faking a smile, scared herself at the comedy of errors that had created the mess she was in. ''As much as I'd love a few free shots at you for what you did to Darcy, I'd never be reduced to kidnapping...'' That's when the weirdest words she'd uttered all day came out... ''... Just try to relax.'' Even in such a situation, Amy tried to keep her cool. Her head was going in a thousand different directions and making up a million more excuses, but nothing was clicking quite right. She'd stepped into a puddle of confusion because of her best friend's over-the-top way of expressing good intentions. While it would bode well for their bond of friendship, Amy was hardly pleased with Jeph - and even less pleased at what this meant overall... No, not Darcy being mad at her. That was manageable... ...But spending more time with Jane? Not so much.
