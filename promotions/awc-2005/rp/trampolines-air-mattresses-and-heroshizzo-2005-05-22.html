Trampolines, air mattresses, and Heroshizzo

22 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/05 Nature sometimes prevents human beings to think logically, to do what they know is right and choose the thing that can help them succeed over something that would be fun. Of course, nature sometimes fails. For example, Kris Krimzon. Sometimes, nature helps those humans have special talents in collateral to the elimination all other aspects of common sense. For example, Kris Krimzon. But who cares about nature anymore? The room is empty, abandoned of all furniture or otherwise. However, in the far corner of the room sets a miniature trampoline and an air mattress standing about five inches from it. Under the weight, the springs expand, and then as the weight lifts above it, they contract. A flop is heard; a flop of flesh, bones, and muscle onto an… air mattress, perhaps? Kris Krimzon, wearing a sleeveless wife beater shirt and only boxer shorts rolls off the mattress, onto his feet. Smiling, he rushes back onto the miniature trampoline, jumping repeatedly, then launching off and onto the air mattress. Kris repeats the process many times, a grin on his face the exact same fashion, as he was the time before that, the time before that, and so on. The door to the room cracks open; standing in the doorway is Tamara Krimzon. Her hair is a mess, her eyes encircled by dark ovals, and clothes creased. “Kris,” Tamara asks angrily, “what are you doing? It is six in the friggin morning! WHY ARE YOU JUMPING OFF THAT STRANGELY SMALL TRAMPOLINE ONTO THE AIR MATRESS I’VE NEVER SEEN?” “Well,” Kris says sleepily, “I did plan on going out and drinking but I figured that really piss you off. Then I decided to stay up all night playing video games, but I figured that would piss you off a lot too. So I decided on the trampoline, which I guessed would piss you off less than the other two. Did I guess right?” Tamara shrugs, answering his question with an unstated “yes”. She walks over to the air mattress, flopping backwards onto it, staring at the blank ceiling in thought. “I never thought that getting out of bed would be the hardest thing to do in one day.” Kris smiles and falls onto the mattress as well. “Well, it is only six in the ‘frigging’,” Kris says mockingly, “in the morning. You probably haven’t even THOUGHT about the shit I will pull during the day.” Tamara groans at the thought. “Please, take it easy today. Can’t you just go sleep all day like the way you usually do? It is quiet when you do so… Oh, how I missed the quiet. Of course, that was long, long before we were ever married…” “Quit complaining. I should be the one complaining. I’m going to be facing a geezer! I don’t want to break his back on a suplex or something… Sure, he’s beaten some other people, but hey, am I other people?” Kris says, ego blazing as usual. Tamara grins, “Nope, nothing like normal people, Kris.” Kris nods, not understanding the insult. “No, nothing like other wrestlers,” Kris says, who is cut off by Tamara’s laughter. “Err… Anyways… You want to jump on the trampoline? It’s über fun.” “Über? Kris, you’re not from Germany.” “Of course not, I’m any Nazi. I don’t even hate Jews!” Kris says matter-of-factly. Tamara rolls her eyes. “Yeah, Germany is still run by Nazis,” Tamara says sarcastically. Kris smiles, “I know Tamara, I’m not a complete idiot, or an American idiot, despite what Green Day claims.” “Kris, just shut up,” Tamara says. Kris goes to speak, “No, just shut up.” Kris shuts his mouth and lies back onto his back, and stares at the ceiling in conjunction with Tamara. +=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= Later that day… In the backyard of the Krimzon residence, Kris sits huddled in a corner by a flimsy, wooden fence, snickering with delight. In the echoes of the town, you can barely, just barely, hear the residue of screams sounding like “Kris”. A strange ‘thump’ is heard, but Kris’s attentions do not waiver. Shortly after the ‘thump’, a crashing of wood finally gets Kris’s attention. He turns around to a black, hooded creature, standing mysteriously by a freshly broken section of fence, blood trickling down his hands, which are barely visible. The figure stands up, breathing heavily. Kris cocks an eyebrow towards the figure in confusion. The hood falls, revealing the face of AJ Valor. Kris laughs. “Well,” Kris says, a smile on his face and laughter in his voice, “looks like you broke a new idiotic record, new champ.” Kris grins, and AJ gives Kris a slightly angered look. “Kris, I had something very important to tell you,” AJ says, “but I guess it will have to wait.” Kris smiles, “Just tell me you queer.” “My uncle is gay,” AJ says. Kris cocks an eyebrow again, wondering what that had to do with the conversation. “Anyways, guess what I saw today at the park!” “A hot chick?” Kris asks, and AJ shakes his head. “A green cat?” Kris guesses again, and AJ laughs. “A green cat?! That’s retarded!” AJ says, still laughing. Kris nods in agreement. “No, no, I saw a BLUE cat!” Kris’s eyes widen. “A blue cat?! That’s absurd… Yet strangely kick ass. Is that all you wanted to tell me?” AJ shakes his head, anxious to tell more. “I saw a blue cat,” AJ says, and pauses in an attempt to intensify the moment, “being humped by a green DOG!” “Okay… Then why would a green cat be so retarded? It’s better than a green dog.” AJ shrugs, and nods his head in the direction of the glow behind Kris’s back. “What’s that?” AJ asks. “Oh, I was playing with fire, trying to burn an ant farm because they attacked my sandwich I left out. I declared war upon them, and this is my atomic bomb to their Heroshizzo,” Kris says grinning. “What in the Hell is Heroshizzo?” “It’s that one Chinese place we bombed in the D-Day war. Trust me, man. I know my wars and my geegraphs better than you know the back of your hand,” Kris says proudly. AJ nods, “Well, seeing as I don’t even know what a geegraph is, I guess you’re right Kris… For once, you’re right.” AJ’s eyes suddenly widen, and Kris, puzzled by AJ’s emotion turns to the direction of AJ’s gaze. When turning around, he sees that his little “atomic bomb” plan caught near-by dry leaves on fire, which in turn catches the raggedy fence on fire as well. Kris panics and kicks down the fence onto a pile of more dry leaves, creating a larger fire. Kris, panicking still, looks around for a solution to the problem lying before them. He spots a container with the letters “G”, “A”, and “S” imprinted into the sides of it. “AJ! Toss me that container! QUICK!” AJ grabs the container and reads the side. He goes to talk, but is cut off by the urgency in Kris’s voice, “COME ON!” AJ obeys, throwing the container his way. Kris catches it and the weight of the container pulls Kris’s arms near the ground. Kris pulls the container to his chest and opens the end of a tube, and begins to poor the liquid down upon the growing fire. The moment the liquid hits the fire, the fire ruptures into a huge, uncontrollable beast. Kris is thrown backwards by the force of the explosion, and lands near the feet of his friend. They exchange frantic looks, and then both nod at each other. AJ sprints off and Kris, fumbling to get to his feet, follows, leaving the raging fire to continue its path of destruction. +=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= Later that night… Tamara Krimzon pulls her vehicle, a blue Hyundai, costly accessorized with neon lights and nice rims, into the two-car garage of her home. Seeing that the house is all in one peace, she releases a sigh of release. She steps out of her car and pops the trunk. She pulls out a bag of groceries. Why am I always doing the groceries? she thinks. She walks into the house, wondering why she does all the work that she does, like a typical housewife (she hopes). She sets the bag onto the floor by their shoe rack, and steps inside. She looks around the tables, looking for a note because she does not see nor hear her usually active husband. She sighs, assuming the worst. When she looks out the backyard window, her assumptions are correct. Seeing the pitch-blackness of the normally green grass, she rushes to the backyard, to see Kris lying restfully upon a neatly laid out towel, gazing up at the now starry sky. Tamara, sighing, walks over to Kris, and sees that he is sleeping. She sighs, and lies down onto Kris’s stomach. Kris wakes with a start, and Tamara looks at him with a face that says something around the basis of, “What did you do you stupid bastard?! YOU SET MY BACKYARD ON FIRE!” Kris gulps. “Well, I was bombing Heroshizzo with my atomic bomb when it caught the fence on fire…” View Kris Krimzon 's Biography
