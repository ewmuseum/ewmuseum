Roses & Thorns - Responsibilities and Expectations

27 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/02/06 All it is is one single belt. Just a mixture of leather, gold and engravings, nothing more. But it means so very much to me and I can tell you why: it is a sign of honour and respect. It is the culmination, the pinnacle of the sport to me right here and now. I’ve waited fifteen years for this momentous occasion and to tell you the truth I thought I’d be more prepared than this. Guess you don’t realise until it’s put round your waist. Thank you Dan. *** 26th of February - Liacouras Center, Philadelphia I never thought it’d be like this. You know why I didn’t write to you for so long? Because life got in the way, because life told me to realise that taking account of things needed to take a back seat to the present problems and challenges I had coming up ahead of me. Dan’s funeral took a lot out of me and reading back over that it of writing it’s easy to see how I couldn’t write after that. Things were just too chaotic, too muddled and confusing to put into words. Now though it was coming back to normal…well normal if you counted the hordes of people who apparently wanted to get right at me and tear any sort of memorabilia they could find from my person. The crotch area was disturbingly popular. Now I had something to talk about, now I was ready to face the door that greeted me after the closing of the other one. It was nine o’clock this Sunday morning and AWC production crew had arrived the night before. They had the leeway this weekend to set up at a bit of a more relaxed pace, largely due to the fact that the company had booked a kind of meet and greet with some of the wrestlers in the arena while things were being constructed. Since I had become the champion and Jessica was focusing ever more stringently on her job, I had logically been picked to meet the fans. Which was fine, I hadn’t had to do more than just sign autographs and such recently, no real talking. That was until Jessica approached me this morning. She looked good, in a nice pale purple business suit which had a slit up the side of her legs and showed off her nice legs well. Her hair was tied in a bun and for some reason she was wearing glasses. This I had to question. ‘Jessica?’ ‘Yeah?’ ‘Why are you wearing glasses?’ She paused for a moment and a flicker of danger erupted in the orbs of her eyes. ‘I thought it might be a nice way to tease the audience since I’ll be nearby. Plus some of the guys in the back thought it might add to my character.’ ‘Oh right. Fair enough.’ She was strangely perky today. Maybe it was the fact that since I was champion she was getting more attention as someone, maybe she just was pleasantly pleased with the business she was finally bringing in. I’d heard she was looking into scouting new wrestlers coming onto the circuit as a possibility. She’d learned from her father that nothing was stagnant that was for sure and was trying to capitalise on the business opportunities. But all the same there was still the sense that Jessica hadn’t quite come to the realisation…that Dan was gone and gone for good. In the days that followed his death we hadn’t crossed paths too many times like in the hospital that night but she held no love loss for me. On the day of the funeral her arms clutched at me tightly, unwilling to let go on the hold that she had, almost like it I was all that was left for her in this world. I didn’t truly believe that but then again I wasn’t Jessica. So all the same she was drifting along, slowly heading for the iceberg that would make her wake up and come to see the truth. ‘So Jack are you ready for this today?’ It was Pearl Harber of all people talking to me. He looked sharp, even though he was dressed in a simple t-shirt and trousers combo. Relaxation was the key word with Pearl and he was able to be calm under some of the more stressful situations. Since I had won the title I had gained a slight bit more respect for him, understanding now what he went through each and every day on a regular basis. He too seemed to be more amiable towards me, just like now and had no shallow core to him. He truly did want to talk to me. ‘Yeah I think so. I guess it’s not going to be too different from the other days now is it?’ ‘Well….that’s not strictly true to be honest. Did Jessica not brief you on this?’ ‘No?’ ‘Ah.’ I didn’t like the sound of that ah, that sounded like a bad omen to be sure. ‘What do you mean ah?’ ‘Well…’ ‘Pearl this is not the time to be stuttering your way through a conversation. What is it?’ He didn’t say a word though. Instead he kind of just looked towards the entranceway which had been constructed ahead of Tuesday. As such the curtains were raised and the actual arena was cloaked off from where I was. This subtle hinting made me more than curious and I slowly approached the curtain, taking a peek out of it. ‘Oh you’ve got to be kidding Pearl.’ Standing out there was at least two hundred fans around the ring, right now being talked to by Sasha. They all had their attention glued to her and I wasn’t in the slightest bit surprised. Just like Jessica she had a business suit on, this one black, and its slit was very revealing to say the least. Each pimple filled face was locked onto her legs and the swaying motion she brought about with them and the skirt. But as much as I enjoyed looking at her long, silky legs, there were bigger problems staring at me in the face. Very small formed problems in actuality but together they were colossal. ‘I want an explanation now Pearl. What are the expectations here?’ He’d seemingly regained his composure by now as his jaw had returned to its normal place and wasn’t slack. He cleared his throat promptly and seemed to reel it off like a statement of affairs; like a contract. ‘Well there’ll be the obvious and obligatory signing session. They’ve all got some sort of merchandise that they want to have signed in some shape and form, you’ll have to do that. Then there’s photos, if anyone has any cameras and expresses an interest then we’ll arrange for the kids to queue up for that, for a fee of course. And then there’s the main reason they’re here…the training session.’ ‘What?’ ‘You mean Jessica didn’t tell you anything?’ ‘What does it look like Pearl? No, she didn’t tell me anything. Maybe she was planning on doing that but never got around to it it seems.’ Pearl rolled his eyes a little. ‘Seems I better speak with your PR Jack after this. I don’t want this kind of situation happening again.’ ‘Yeah that’s great Pearl but what I need to hear right now is this training session. So spill.’ ‘Basically…you’re going to be showing them a couple of moves. These kids are at least fourteen so there’s no worries about you being ridiculously strong against them because they’ve all signed an agreement that if they’re picked they are at the risk of an injury and you, or AWC, is not responsible for this. They enter at their own risk. It’ll only be two or three kids so don’t worry alright?’ ‘Don’t worry? What do you want me to do? Spike them on their heads like I did to Adam and look at them and go, “See? There’s nothing to it!” while they’re carted off on a stretcher?’ ‘Don’t be stupid Jack. A body slam and a leg drop is all you have to show them. That should take long enough for the two hours you’re scheduled for.’ ‘Two hours? Why couldn’t you explain this all in one go Pearl?’ ‘Hey, talk to your PR next time before you show up. At least then you’ll know alright? Oh, before you go…’ He headed over to a briefcase which he’d left on a side table backstage. Ushering me over with his hand he flipped the two locks on the case and pushed it up. ‘I figured you wouldn’t want to wear a crown, and frankly neither does AWC. That’s purely a gimmick for Mr. Dick.’ Staring right at me was the Transatlantic belt in all its glory. Polished and shining like it was brand new. Each country that was on it beamed out at me and really hit home inside of me. I was the champion of AWC. I was the representative right now of the Atlantic coasts and all the countries that fell under that banner, including my own, Ireland. It was a strange thing to remember I was associated with that country after all these years but it was what had stuck with me in my wrestling career, despite having now spent more time in America than anywhere else. But what got to me most was the gold plate on the middle of it. It had two words: Jack Murphy. After thirty-seven years in this world and more than fifteen in this business, I finally had a world title to call my own. ‘Go get ‘em Champ.’ Harber gave me a pat on the back and offered me the belt to put on my shoulder. Taking it I held it closely and felt the cold leather against my arm. It felt good. He then pointed his hand towards the curtain. ‘There’s people expecting you out there. Don’t disappoint.’ *** Mayhem, absolute mayhem was presented to me once I walked through that curtain. The ensuing melee that occurred once the kids saw me and recognised who it was, well they went nuts. Some cheered, some booed but most of all they ran and jumped and clubbed and kicked. Manners get you everywhere kids, manners get you everywhere but obviously you’d never had that lesson. Time went by so fast because simply every single kid had more than piece of merchandise as Pearl had intimated. They took as much time as possible to berate, appreciate, tear, pull and attack me with their bodies, faces, spit, yelling and shouting bloody murder like their lives were no complete that they’d experienced this. Jesus this was intense. Of course they leered over Jessica who stood nearby but nobody was willing to go up to her, oh no, that would be too bold of them to do such a thing. Instead they stared at her legs too, just like they had (and still did) at Sasha’s. Both women were at a safe distance, conveniently on the other side of the aisle and over the barricade. All this time men were filtering through with all the kids who wanted a photograph and bit by bit a queue formed apart from the current mosh pit which was around me. When everyon had had their pound of flesh I had about…oh twenty seconds before the photos started. And they were just as fun as the signing had been. The kids clutched at my sides and made silly faces, some of them pulling and tugging at my goatee as I tried to smile along with them. Manners and etiquette guys, manners and etiquette. Then we got to the last kid who was waiting and he politely walked up to me. He looked pretty quiet and a kind of reserved kid so I tried to converse with him. ‘Hey, nice to meet you. What’s your name bud?’ ‘I’m not your buddy. I don’t even want this photo, I wanted one with Jessica.’ ‘Oh you did, did you?’ ‘Yeah, no offence but…’ ‘But I’m not a girl?’ I gave him a smile and his face brightened a little. ‘Yeah, something like that.’ ‘Alright, hang on one sec.’ The kid looked at me confused and I merely put my hand to my lips before vaulting the barricade and approached Sasha and Jessica. ‘Hey, you two sexy girls got a minute? Kid needs a bit of cheering up.’ They both looked at me, both of them eerily similar in their approach. Sasha bit her lip looking at me and I winked at her, hoping Jessica didn’t see it. Thankfully not because she nodded her head as did Sasha. So carefully I helped them over the barricade and over to the kid. ‘Well, I figured this would be a much more appetising photo for you eh…?’ I left my tongue hanging to see if he’d pick up, and he did. ‘James, my name is James.’ ‘Alright James, here’s your wish. Oh and here put this around your waist. Only a champion deserves girls like this right?’ His eyes popped and the kids who were now cordoned off looked on in distress at this development. Sure I was treating this guy better than them but he was the only kid who’d bothered to be calm and polite and speak his mind like an adult. Manners and etiquette guys. They took the photo, James absolutely starstruck as the two beauties put their arms round him and winked flirtatiously at the camera. He was in two minds; do I stare at the real championship gold or do I stare at the two hot ladies holding onto me? He took the natural choice…the ladies of course. Standing back and letting them have the spotlight gave me the first break I’d had in well over two hours. As usual these things took much longer than expected but I still had the demonstration to go and damn if this wasn’t going to be long. But looking around and especially at the photo shoot going on I couldn’t help but realise this was going to be part of my everyday life now. I had expectations and demands to live up to. It wasn’t just about the wrestling anymore. *** There was a loud bang as I vaulted into the ring and onto the hard canvas. All the kids were now surrounding the ring and myself and Sasha were inside. Jessica had left (she had more phone calls to make apparently) which left the two of us alone. Eyeing her up I couldn’t help but want to take her into my arms again and hold her…but I couldn’t. Lately things had been kind of cooling off and heating up in strange fashion. She would act very distant and away from me most of the time but then she would suddenly be all over me in some corridor and we’d get it on in a closet. After that it would be back to the cold shoulder and I’d have to deal with that. I was kind of being used…in the best possible way but still being used if you know what I mean. Still I couldn’t help wonder if this was all she wanted. Anyway she was in the middle of picking three kids to get in the ring and thankfully she picked James, the kid from earlier. He slid into the ring and I gave him a smile, which he actually responded to. I was pleased, guess his opinion had changed of me. The other two were in a different class to James though. He was pretty average sized for a boy of about sixteen but the other two…oh no they were different. The first kid to be picked was massive. He was easily two hundred and fifty pounds of pure flab and this was no joke. He couldn’t slide under the bottom rope under his own power so he had to use the ring steps and then slide in. He wore a giant t-shirt which read “Got Burger? I want!” on it and it was clear to me that he’d been successful in that venture more than once. In fact he was eating as he got in, some chocolate bar and it smeared all over his face. Great, and I’d have to deal with this one now, I couldn’t expect anyone else to lift him. The last kid was scrawny on the other hand and looked as thin as a branch. But that wasn’t the important thing. He had a crown, a homemade one, but a crown nonetheless. I’d seen him earlier, when he told me he was unfuckable and that I’d be beaten by Dick soon enough. I smiled at him then but I could see from the sneer on his face that he still held the opinion close at hand. Once he caught my eye he began to walk really slowly and jokingly, just like Dick would, spouting off a couple of catchphrases from the former champion. Oh hell this was going to be fun. All three of them stood before me and I gave the belt to Sasha to watch. She took this time to parade it around the outside and let the other kids get a closer look. ‘Alright you guys, I’m going to show you one move right now and if you’re good enough then I’ll show you something more advanced later ok? We’re going to start with a simple body slam first. First I need your names.’ ‘James.’ ‘Gary.’ That was the fat kid. ‘Karl.’ The Dick wannabe. ‘Alright Karl, you go with James and Gary you’ll pair with me. I’m sure you’ve seen this move thousands of times but here’s the basics. First you bend and put your hand between their legs, then with your other arm holding over their shoulder you use the arm between their legs to push them up and then over into the slam. Everyone got that?’ They all nodded and I looked at Gary who was drooling at the mouth from all the chocolate in his system. I smiled a very thin and fake one before I asked him was he ready. He simply nodded. ‘Ok, just watch me before you two try alright?’ ‘I then proceeded to break down the move for the purposes of showing him and by god was the kid way too heavy for someone his age. He would not last long in this world. I slammed him gently (if that was possible) and then looked at the other two. ‘Nothing to it. James you slam Karl first and I’ll watch.’ Without hesitation James picked up the scrawny kid and he executed a pretty spot on slam without a hitch. I was impressed. The Dick wannabe got up off the ground hastily to show that he wasn’t hurt and voiced his comment. ‘Pah, that was nothing. Wait till you feel the pain!’ He then grabbed James without warning but he wasn’t going for a body slam. No, he decided it was necessary to try an Eaterplex right on James. I tried to stop him but it was too late and James was unconscious. Karl then jumped up and began taunting me, telling me that that was what was going to happen to me soon enough and I wouldn’t have a chance to hold on to my title. I wanted to roughly grab the kid and show him the error of his ways but I couldn’t. So instead I turned to Gary. Who puked on me. Oh great. *** It took nearly two hours to sort out the mess. James was out for a good ten minutes from the full on move by Karl and they were nearly going to send him to hospital. Needless to say the parents weren’t happy. All the time Karl kept taunting me and James, at several points he spat in my face and told me that I was never going to be as famous as Dick. I ignored him for the most part but it bothered me to no end. After that I had to change, what with Gary’s puke still clinging to my t-shirt and trousers. Brown and chocolaty puke. But after two hours it was all settled down and I sat backstage. Hunched over a table with Jessica nearby. ‘Is this what it’s going to be like?’ Jessica looked up at my question to the masses and forgot about her magazine. ‘What do you mean?’ ‘Is this what I have to look forward to now? Being corralled around like I’m a one act show and used as a commodity? Is this all that I mean to AWC now?’ ‘Of course not Jack. But things like this need to be done. You have responsibilities and expectations that come with the territory now. Some of them are good, some of them aren’t. You’ll just have to deal with it.’ Responsibilities and expectations. I’ll tell her about responsibilities. All I want is to wrestle and show the world why I’m on top in AWC but I’m stuck doing mindless little things like this day in and out, not able to concentrate on what I’m good at. Wrestling. But I’m not just a wrestler anymore. I’m a spokesperson, a role-model, an icon for AWC. I can’t fade into the background and be lauded for my skills but always with the question of “Can he win the big one?”. Instead I’m in the driver’s seat now, I’ve been given the task and I’ve simply got to deal with it. But this is not what I expected at all. Because I’m not just a wrestler anymore.
