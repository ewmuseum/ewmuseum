Chronicle Two: Tied Up

18 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/18/05 Mapleleaf: Good GAWD! You call this a script?! [Patrick Mapleleaf is sitting inside of a studio in Anaheim, California…a recording studio, that is. Mapleleaf is wearing his red track jacket with a white Canadian flag on the top left side and Patrick stitched on the right side, with “Proud Canadian” stitched underneath. He is sitting in front of a microphone surrounded by equipment that God only knows how to work. Clearly disgusted, he tosses the script aside and sighs, taking a sip from his Poland Springs water bottle, making a face, and spitting it out onto the floor.] Mapleleaf: What is this crap?! Recording Engineer: Water, sir. Mapleleaf: What’s your name? Tom: Tom. Mapleleaf: Tom, I will pay you 5,000 dollars to drink a Poland Springs bottle full of my urine. Tom: Excuse me? Mapleleaf: That’s right, cause that’s what this is…urine in a bottle. Get a clue, Tommy boy…the best water comes from Canada. Tom: Can we just do the voice recording and get this done with? Mapleleaf: Not unless I can tweak the script. What is this? Nice job but I’m sorry, it’s incorrect? What the holy flying monkey piss?! Tom: What do you want to say? Mapleleaf: I’ll do the talking. Tom: Fine, but only if you agree to one condition. Mapleleaf: Fine…what? Tom: Please put your pants back on! [Patrick stands up to reveal he is only wearing a pair of red and white striped boxer shorts on his legs.] Patrick: You said make yourself at home. Patrick Mapleleaf NEVER wears pants at home. Tom: Fine, whatever. Okay, and rolling… Mapleleaf: Wrong answer, jackass. Tom: CUT! You can’t swear on this or call the learner names, Mr. Mapleleaf. Let’s try another one again…and action! Mapleleaf: Okay, so you got one right…blow it out your sockhole, jerkie. Tom: CUT! Pat— Mapleleaf: Mr. Mapleleaf, insect. Tom: Mr. Mapleleaf, you have to stick to the script of this DVD, or else this is never gonna work. Mapleleaf: Ha! Who else can you find besides me to do this DVD on teaching kids aBOOT Canada? I exemplify all the greatest points aBOOT the proud nation! [Three hours later.] Mapleleaf: I still can’t believe that son of a bitch kicked me out of the studio! Cedric: You were bitin’ his leg, shoop daddy! [Patrick Mapleleaf and Cedric, his roommate/best friend/disco fanatic are sitting outside of their hotel in Anaheim. Patrick is shirtless, wearing a solid red pair of swim trunks and a red pair of sunglasses. Cedric is next to him, wearing white trunks with a gold chain around his neck. His aviator sunglasses are over his eyes, and he fluffs his afro.] Mapleleaf: I’m bored. Why don’t we try and hit on those ladies over there? Cedric: Those foxy ladies? Face it, man, you ain’t got a shot! Mapleleaf: Care to make it interesting? Cedric: Sure. Mapleleaf: Fine. Stop talking, you’re boring the crap out of me! That will make things interesting enough. Watch, I’ll go over and talk to those ladies right now! Cedric: And I’ll work on my tan! Mapleleaf: Cedric, you’re black. Cedric: Why thank you, Pat. I HAVE been working on this tan a while. [Patrick gets up and walks across the pool area. Several beautiful people are crowded around the vicinity. The sun is high in sunny California, and the help for the hotel is undoubtedly even higher. Nevertheless, Mapleleaf walks over to the women. They are both wearing skinny bathing suits. One has a long mane of blonde hair and fair skin, while the other has brown hair and is swinging her hips. They both laugh in conversation. Mapleleaf swaggers on over, chest out front and the “I’m gonna sex you up!” look on his face.] Mapleleaf: Evening, ladies. Girl #1: It’s afternoon, man. Mapleleaf: Not on my head. For me, it’s always…ALL NIGHT LONG! OWW!! Girl #2: Do you party? Mapleleaf: Do I?! Why just last week, my nephew turned 3. I tell you, that was a humdinger. Cake and party hats all around. Girl #1: Wanna meet us at our hotel room in 20 minutes? Mapleleaf: I think that can be arranged. Girl #2: Cool. See ya there, tiger. [Mapleleaf grins ear to ear and walks over to Cedric. Cedric smiles and snaps his fingers.] Cedric: So, how did it go, cool doggio? You got yourself some company? Mapleleaf: I do. Guess you’ll be the one sleeping alone tonight, eh Ceddy boy? Cedric: Nah. I still gots my picture of Lena Horne. Mapleleaf: Dude…she’s like 100 now. Cedric: I like em’ old. Mapleleaf: I like them young! Cedric: Now don’t go there. You got yourself in some trouble last time. Mapleleaf: Court didn’t have enough evidence to convict me. Then again, going to a Junior High prom isn’t exactly the best place to cruise for chicks. Well, later…loser! [Now for a short commercial break.] Mapleleaf: Hi! I’m Patrick Mapleleaf, AWC superstar and inventor of Ab Crunch! Ab Crunch is the brand new cereal that tightens your abs as you eat. You see, it all goes down to the scientific term called [mumbles]. When you do [mumbles], your abs will get tighter and you don’t be a pathetic tub of lard anymore. Take it from me! This is me before Ab Crunch! [A picture is shown of an extremely heavy man with Mapleleaf’s head pasted on his body rather crudely.] Mapleleaf: So next time you’re out looking for something to eat and feed your fat face at the local grocery store, try Ab Crunch. It’s ABsolutely delicious! Fast voice: Warning! Ab Crunch can cause cancer, heart disease, paralysis, erosion of the bones, and is illegal in 48 out of 50 states. Ab Crunch is highly toxic and should not be consumed by anyone. [Back to action.] Mapleleaf: Aw yeah…that’s the stuff. [Mapleleaf is lying in a hotel bed, the covers over him, with the two girls at each side. He smoothes down his long blonde hair and takes a sigh. The girls get up from the bed, putting their clothes back on, and circle around him.] Girl #2: Did you like that, Mr. Figleaf? Mapleleaf: The name is Mapleleaf…and yes I did. How aBOOT another round? Girl #1: Well, okay, but we need to see some cash before we go on. Mapleleaf: Whoa, I don’t give women three things: love, respect, or money. Girl #2: You mean you wanted a freebee? Mapleleaf: A what? What in the name of Canadian bacon are you talking aBOOT? Girl #1: We’re call girls, scumbag. Now give us money! Mapleleaf: Or what? It’s not like you can return me? HAHAHA! Girl #2: We can take something of your’s. [The other girl gets out a large knife.] Mapleleaf: Good GAWD! You’re gonna chop celery and make a salad? Girl #1: No, dufus, we’re gonna cut off your… Mapleleaf: My Columbia House CD club membership? FINE! Do your worst! Girl #2: Your manhood! Mapleleaf: Not Pedro! Girl #1: You NAMED it?! Mapleleaf: Uhh…no. Fine, if you really want money, I’ll give you a 5…but you both have to split it! Girl #2: Five hundred dollars. Girl #1: Apiece. Mapleleaf: I can’t imagine how this could get worse… Voice: OPEN UP! It’s a RAID! Mapleleaf: Maid? Great, I’ll take two pieces of French Toast, no crust…GOD help you if a find a crust! [The scene fades. It opens back up with Mapleleaf in a prison cell. Cedric bee bops and scats his way into the holding room. The officer unlocks the cell door.] Officer: You’re free to go. Mapleleaf: Thank you, sir, but I must say…those fish sticks do leave much to be desired. Cedric: Let’s go, Mappes… [Mapleleaf and Cedric say nothing. They walk down the prison hall, but say nothing. They walk outside the prison, but say nothing. They get into Cedric’s 1973 GTO, and yet they still say nothing to each other until Cedric hits the highway.] Cedric: Toldja I didn’t want none of that! Mapleleaf: You love to say I told you so, don’t you? Cedric: Yep. Mapleleaf: I had to sleep on a bench. I have bench lines in my back! Why couldn’t you get here sooner? Voice: Sorry, but we were doing the hippity dippity! [Mapleleaf turns around. He sees 88 year old Lena Horne get up from the back seat, straightening out her hair.] Mapleleaf: You didn’t! Cedric: I told you I like a little grass on the field! Mapleleaf: That’s not grass…it’s a field! No offense, Miss Horne. Lena: Eat dirt. Mapleleaf: Right. Hey, those girls over there on the street corner are pretty cute. I love girls that wear skimpy outfits and sunglasses at 2 PM. Pull over. Cedric: Here we go again… [Cedric pulls the car over as the scene comes to a close.]
