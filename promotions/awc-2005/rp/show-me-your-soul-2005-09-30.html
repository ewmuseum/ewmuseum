Show Me Your Soul

30 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/9 Hate shifted uneasily in his chair, looking out of the small round window of the airplane. White clouds of water vapor and industrial waste gases hid the destination from his piercing gaze, but his thoughts were not on what his eyes could see. The bald man, with his trenchcoat open to reveal muscles covered in fading scars and with a fresh set of stitches running from the corner of his right eye across his temple and above his ear, is sitting all alone in the back of the plane. There is a circle of empty seats all around him, with people packed full into the rest of the seats. Only Azagtoth and Angelica dare to sit in the same section as him, and even they are spread out: Angelica looking out of the windows on the other side, and Azagtoth sitting a few rows in front of Hate, constantly muttering to himself and turning around to watch his silent leader. Hate, however, could not be more oblivious to his concerned troops. His mind is elsewhere, racing along with his pounding heart into the horizon. For the past few weeks, he has not been able to concentrate on things that matter: repeatedly, Azagtoth would bring him reports from the Cult back in Philadelphia, and repeatedly he would listen to them. Repeatedly, he would ignore them. His mind was being occupied with other things. Paddy O’Shea. AWC. He had sworn to himself that it was all about the money: all that he would gain from wrestling and fighting was money. No satisfaction, no revenge, nothing besides funds for the Cult. The Cult was his life, his blood, his beating heart… And he was ignoring it, turning his back on the thing that had made him into the Fifth Horseman. Ever since he had taken on the black mask to start his campaign of terror on the Ireland native, the wrestling business had slowly crept upon him. Like a moth to a flame, Hate was unwillingly drawn to it. He tried to fight it at every turn, told himself that it wasn’t meant for him, that he was just in it for the money. But how could he say that when he took personal offense on whether or not he lost or won a match? It shouldn’t have mattered, with only money at stake. Money, like all things material, could be hurt and broken over and over again without repercussions, as all material eventually grew back. But when you put the spiritual on the line, allowed your ego and pride take the hits instead of your body, that’s when you went in too deep. And every time Hate flamed with anger towards O’Shea, every time he cursed the decisions made by David Harber, he knew it was his spiritual side that was getting the beatdown, not his physical, corporeal body. Years before he had gotten accustomed to the fact that his body had grown dependant on a lot of things. Drugs, violence, what have you. But never his mind, his only beacon of sanity and stableness in an otherwise crazy world. His mind had never been dependant on anything… But now, it was getting hooked. Getting hooked on winning, getting hooked on the publicity, getting hooked on the reactions people gave him when they saw him. Before, they saw a wicked skinhead. Now, they saw a man who had nailed a pig to a wall, had carved the word ”DIE!” on the flesh of his colleague, had openly defied his boss and physically attacked him to the point of unconsciousness. That was the kind of a man they saw now. And Hate liked what they saw. Then there was the matter of Azagtoth and Angelica, two of his most loyal followers. Azagtoth, his trusted lieutenant for many a years. He and the painted teenager had grown together when Azagtoth was only 15 years of age, when the Fifth Horseman was forming his cult. He had beat Azagtoth’s scrawny ass all over the cellar they had happened into, then lectured the bleeding and broken youngster on the error of his ways. And despite the concussions, the broken teeth and the bleeding, Azagtoth listened. And learned. And finally, knelt down before his assailant and pledged his soul to the bigger man. And now there was Angelica, who had pledged her body to Hate, multiple times, in the middle of the darkest nights. They resented each other like spirit and flesh, a reaction that came only naturally from two such large entities trying to squeeze into a space fit for only one of them. Azagtoth had on numerous occasions laid his hands on Angelica, trying to pervert and thrash that angelic visage with his fists and weapons, trying to make it so that her body was no more attractive to Hate. And Angelica could not fight back, as the Body could not touch the incorporeal Spirit. Yet… The Body persisted. The Body healed, and the Spirit did not. Self-doubt and guilt gnawed away at the Spirit, slowly eroding it, slowly making it smaller and smaller until one day it would disappear, it’s driving flame extinguished and gone. And that day Hate awaited in the near future. Either one could come out on top: and the next time, he might not find the right warehouse in time, might not run fast enough, might not get the words out early enough… --- A similar internal struggle was going through Azagtoth’s mind as he fidgeted on his seat, constantly glancing at Hate over his right left shoulder, turning around the moment he thought his Leader might pay him attention and notice his curiosity. But Hate never looked at him, not once. He didn’t look at the bitch either, so that was fine with Azagtoth. He could suffer anything as long as the bitch got the same done unto her. Taking another look, Azagtoth turned to face the bench in front of him, staring at the texture intently and biting the backs of his hands. He thought of how things had gone in the past few months. Hate had increasingly used the Cult’s resources to further his career in this… This… TRAVESTY of combat called ”AWC”. Azagtoth could still not bend his mind around the idea, not even after he had been present on many of the shows. Granted, he wouldn’t have done it for the worlds if it weren’t for Him. For Hate, he would do anything. And to be honest, Azagtoth had enjoyed some of it. Chuckling to himself, he thought of slicing up that Irish pig who had dared defy Him, thought about choking the soft maggot who had wanted to punish Him, and finally smashing the Irish pig’s face with a hammer this past week. But all of that was meaningless unless He could enjoy it as well. And that was something He didn’t really do anymore. Azagtoth was falling second to ”AWC”. And this ticked the youngster off. Twisting his fingers and mumbling to himself, Azagtoth stole a look at Angelica, sitting absent-mindedly on the other side of the plane and longingly staring at Hate with a worried expression on her delicate face. How Azagtoth wanted to grind that face into the mud of the earth and stomp it in with his boot! But he had had his opportunity, and had failed. Mentally slapping himself, Azagtoth looked down on his arm, at the red crosses crowding around his elbow. That was the price he had extracted from himself for failing to solve the bitch problem. Even though it was Hate who interrupted them, caught him by the throat and nearly choked him alive, it was not His fault. It was Azagtoth’s, wholly and completely Azagtoth’s. And the bitch’s too. How easy it would be to stand up now, walk up to that whore and slip a blade between her ribs… It would be over before she could even realize what had happened. But then, there would be nowhere for Azagtoth to go. Nowhere to run and hide and to wait for everyone to lose interest. That’s what happened with the girl whom he had used for mementos before they went to Europe. And if Azagtoth was caught, if he was taken away… How could he be close to Hate then? Shuddering at the mere thought of being separated from his leader, Azagtoth turns to look at Hate, concern filling his face. For the past two years, Azagtoth had been by Hate’s side through good and bad, had fought alongside him like a madman and bled alongside him like no other dared. Ever since they had met in a dark, damp cellar, two beings from wholly different worlds, he had not been able to take his admiration off of Hate. He, a 15-year old runaway, a pathetic suburban kid running blindly in a big city. He, a dark, brooding, violent character of the underworld, betrayed by everything and everyone he ever knew. And there, in that cellar, he taught Azagtoth the meaning of betrayal. Beating on the impressionable youngster with his fists and his words until both his body and mind bled. There and then, “Trey” ceased to be, much like whatever had been there before Hate had ceased a long time ago, and was now replaced with Azagtoth. Named by Him, no less. What could be a greater honor than that? Azagtoth could not remember much of his past life. He had wanted to eradicate himself from society, to turn into an insubstantial ghost that only lurked at the very edges of mortal existence… Much like Him. But it was impossible. --- It was impossible. Hate stared down as the plane began it’s descent, not bothering to buckle his seat belt like they had asked over the intercom. No one would force him to do it anyway. No one had ever forced him to do anything… Except Pearl Harber, Paddy O’Shea and the AWC. All three of these had forced him into a situation where the only thing he could do was bend over and apologize for what he had done. Apologize? It was unthinkable. Hate had never apologized for anything he had done. He had beaten people into hospital, he had taken drugs to the point of passing out, he had… KILLED… without apologizing. How could such a petty organization as AWC force him to circumvent a lifetime of darkness and acts such as those he had done? Shivering a bit, Hate felt his face to see if he could touch the parts where AWC had crawled under his skin: it had to be there, because there was no other way for such a pitiful, meaningless thing to shift him morally to the point of apologizing for his actions. Weak men apologized. Strong men apologized as well. But Hate was neither weak nor strong. He considered himself outside of such scaling, outside of moralities, outside of all the laws in the world beyond those that he set for himself. What right had David Harber to categorize him as weak or strong? What would he know about such matters? Snarling to himself, Hate stretched his arms as the plane came to a halt at the end of the runway and began rolling towards the terminal. Testimony. That was what the fools had named their next big event, like the connotations of the name would make it sound bigger than it actually was. It was not going to be about testimonials; it was going to be about justice. Hate was going to be demanding justice, as perverted as it sounded to the ignorant masses. Justice for letting him become something he didn’t intend to be. A groveling, sniveling lackey for someone else, apologizing if he did something wrong in the eyes of others. That was not the kind of life Hate had intended on living. Be his life short or be it long, he had decided to live free, as a slave of no one. But what did it mean to apologize for one’s actions? Was it weakness, or moral strength to admit being wrong? Hate set his foot down on this: weakness would not be tolerated. And apologizing was admitting to being weak. And Pearl Harber had made him do it. Musing to himself, Hate’s mind began to broil with revenge once again. This would be far sweeter than those times he had lashed out against AWC and it’s employees. This time, he was justified to do so. --- Azagtoth followed Hate meekly out of the plane, walking after him like a beat dog. With his head hanging low, Azagtoth was lost in the masses, but his eyes darted from side to side, taking everything in. He parted ways with the rest of the group and headed to the conveyor belt on his own. The instant he saw his own bag sliding out through the curtain, he grabbed it and clutched it tight to his chest. They hadn’t caught him. Yet again he made it safely. They were too stupid and too busy looking for bombs and explosives that it seemed like they almost WANTED to miss his precious mementos in their rush. All the better for Azagtoth. Fingering his bag, Azagtoth made sure that everything was still in place before heading after Hate and Angelica, who had quickly disappeared into the mass of people. The smell of things his own made Azagtoth ill at ease. Everything was fine now, he told himself, over and over again as he pushed his way through the human cattle. His little collection was his pride and joy. He had harvested for it for a long time, studied and learned his tools. He had sought out willing donators, had made sure that they enjoyed it as much as he did, and then disappeared without a sight. He was so proud of himself, and every click and tinkle of glass jars inside his bag made him even more giddy. Ever since Hate had stopped paying all of his attention to him, had stopped grooming his lieutenant, Azagtoth had felt a void inside himself. Worship of the One-Eyed God Odin served as a form of a relief, but not by much. Odin reminded him too much of Hate: worshipping the One-Eyed One was like worshipping Him. It gave him no escape from the slow mental anguish his leader had started causing him. And then he had met the girl… The first girl. His first Memento. His very own little giftgiver. She had wanted for someone to help her cut herself, and Azagtoth had obliged. Feeling the sharp blade cut the skin was almost ecstasy for him, and he took a small piece of her with him. From there on, he sought out more and more, always looking for bigger things to cut and to take. He amassed a collection: he started saving them for long-term. Even now, every time he looked at those jars, they brought him a peculiar kind of peace. Even if the world turned against him, his parents turned against him… Even if He turned against Azagtoth, he’d still have someone with him. Always. Looking up from his thoughts, Azagtoth noticed Hate just ahead of himself, and began making his way to his leader. As he reaches his leader’s side, he saw something ahead, something he knew Hate saw as well. A police officer. Looking straight at Him. Azagtoth growled and ducked to the right, sealing a wall of people between himself and Hate. Slowly, like a predator, Azagtoth unsheathed his knife and pulled his hand up his sleeve, concealing the blade. The pig would pay, Azagtoth growled. Pay. --- Hate saw the police officer even before Azagtoth, and knew instantly what was going to happen. Maybe it was David Harber and his tricks again. Maybe it was Paddy O’Shea. Whoever it was, it was clear to Hate that this had something to do with AWC: something that further proved to him that he had been getting too involved. His past life didn’t lead the police officers to him. He could hide in the shadows, walk in the night, disappear into a network so deep and vast that they could never find him. But now, there were records of him again. Who paid him, what amounts, where they wanted him to go and at what time. All these records, and of course the authorities would have access to them. They could find it odd that they couldn’t find any other names for him. They could wonder why he didn’t have a home address. But that wouldn’t prevent them from appearing like this and hauling him away. Hauling away… So close to the fulfillment of his dreams in the AWC. After he beat these challenges, he’d be independent again. But if they took him away now, before he could fight his own demons… Hate prepared to fight, or run. And then he saw Azagtoth, knife drawn, advancing towards the side of the police officer, his tongue hanging out like a hunting dog, eyes fixated on the neck of the officer. And Hate saw two more officers, advancing behind Azagtoth. Slowly, Hate pondered over the situation. And then raised his hands up. --- Azagtoth made it so close. He could see the fat pig sweating in his uniform, his chest heaving with the efforts of breathing. Azagtoth would take it all away, oh yes, end all that miserable existence he now had to suffer through. One stab, one swipe, and the pig wouldn’t have to worry about anything else. Edging in like a hunter, Azagtoth bares the tip of his knife. Pushes two teenaged girls away and slowly starts raising his arm for a strike, a strike to end this and to make sure He gets away. For Him and the greater glory, for the Cult… And then a hand on his shoulder stops him. Officer: Trey Carmichael? Could you follow us, sir? Azagtoth turns to face the black officer and snarls at him, baring his teeth. He looks at Hate. And sees his leader surrendering to the officer waiting for him. Shocked, Azagtoth stares at the scene for two seconds, which feels like an eternity for him. He looks back at the two officers, waiting patiently for his answer. The knife disappears into the folds of his clothes. Azagtoth smiles, a twist, sick kind of smile that shouldn’t even exist, and shoves his bag to Angelica, almost knocking the stupid whore down. And Azagtoth raises his hands as well.
