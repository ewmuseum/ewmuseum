The Urge to Fit in Series Part V: Taking a break from fitting in!

31 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/10/05 The scene starts in a familiar setting, inside the always busy living room of Wade Manor. In the living room we find Wade Manors two most prominent residents, Mike Wade and Mickey Moore. Both are seated on the leather couch that is situated up against the back wall of the room and directed right at the TV. Mike sits looking rather uncomfortable with his AWC Relentless title wrapped firmly around his waist, a place it has grown accustomed to like Paddy O Sheas lips have grown accustomed to Crimson O Mallecs arse crack. Mickey sits with his feet not able to touch the ground also looking bored as hell. His little 532 year old face a picture of blankness. And his silly little mind probably singing the Circus song in the hollowness that is his brain. Mike on the other hand........well he's just feckin bored. Mike, it would appear is staring into space. Just looking ahead. However he is so bored in fact that he is counting the lint flying through the air. The boredom though was like preparing for the match. He was seeing what it would feel like to be as bored as a fan listening to a Juri Hyobanshi promo. The boredom however proved to be too much. But rather then study promos and matches of the boring Japanese bastard for his match Mike would rather follow lint, pick his arse....anything except endure him. But the boredom prove WAY too much.................... Mike: Dammit Mickey I hate it here in Waterford it’s so feckin boring. Mickey: Ah I know sure laddie. What is there ta do here in Ireland? We could go picking potatoes? Mike: Shut up Mickey you idiot. Stop trying to live up to the feckin stereotype. You know it just gives the opponents fodder to jeer us when you say idiotic Irishisms like that when we’re on TV. Just like that feckin Paddy O Shea does. We won't stoop to dirty tricks to get over! Mickey: Oh yeah laddie I forgot. Mike: You see Mickey that is your problem you forget too much. Now take me for example. I would never take cheap heat to get over........... Just as Mike is about to go off on a tangent about how great he is we hear a phone ring in the corner of the room. The camera swings around quickly as if it is surprised. We see a red phone, not unlike the bat-phone lighting up. The phone is covered in cobwebs to give it the “this phone hasn’t rang in ages” look about it. Mike: Mickey do you hear that? Mickey: Ay laddie what is it? Mike: Tis the charity phone. Tis ringing again!! Mickey: Dammit Mike laddie I wish you’d stop calling it that you’re lucky you didn’t get locked up for impersonating a chairtable organisation. Mike: Impersonating? You’re lucky I have to take this call or I would bitch slap you right now fruitcake! Mike rushes to the phone shakes the cobwebs off it, in the same fashion Juri Hyobanshi would have to if he ever got the chance to use his penis, he then picks up the phone and talks. Mike: Hello there this is the Help the Retards foundation Mike speaking how may I help you? Voice: Yeah hi there my name is Sue and I heard about your organisation and said I’d ring up and find out about making a sizeable donation. Mike: Sizeable you say? Sue: Oh yes very sizeable. Mike: Well sizeable sounds good to me. Sue: Well of course first I’ll have to hear of your policies and your work with the mentally handicapped kids. Mike: Oh of course!! We hear at the Help the Retards foundation love the little Tard bastards. Sue: Excuse me? Mike: Oh we love the little crazy feckers. Sue: You refer to them as Tards? Mike: Yeah they love that shit. Sue: I would’ve thought it was politically incorrect. Mike: No not at all they love it. In fact little Tommy the Tard is here with me now would you like to speak with him? Sue: Um…..sure. Mike: Hey Tommy come over and speak to the nice lady. Mike signals for Mickey with a beckoning hand. Flapping it to and fro' as if to say 'Come on'. The flapping getting harder and harder as Mickey resists more and more. Mickey seems insistant that he will have no part in the cheap heat crime which just insights Mike into grinding his teeth. Mickey always hated that so relectantly he gave him. He would be a midget posing as a leprachaun, posing as a retarded boy. You can picture it now huh? Mike hands Mickey the phone with glee and smiling Mickey: Hello? Sue: Oh Tommy hi there little fella my name’s Sue. Mickey: What are ya wearin Sue? Sue: Excuse me? Mike slaps Mickey Mike (whispering): Idiot you’re supposed to be retarded! Retards don't ask women prevertadly what they're feckin wearin! Sue: Tommy? Mickey: Uuuuurrrrggghhhhhh I’m a retard!!! Sue: Eh? Mike then snatches the phone from Mickey. Mike: Ha ha. Little Tommy the tard there always playin his games, crazy little retard! Sue: Mike I’m not so sure about this foundation you’re running here. What are your activities? Mike: Oh no no we’re totally legit. The activities? Oh they’re great. We take them outside and everything. We put them all on their little leashes and let them run around in the field next door to my house….I mean our centre. Sue: You put them on LEASHES??? Mike: Oh yeah…..but they’re those leashes that extend out real far like elastic so they feel some sort of freedom until its all taken away when I reel them back in, it’s great they love that shit. Sue: Well I must say you sir are a disgusting and despicable man. Mike: What? Hey it’s not as if I touch the little retards when they sleep or anything Sue: You sir are a danger to society and I will be reporting your “foundation” to the correct authorities. Mike: Hey lady I sensed you inverted commas jibe there! And anyway I’m the one helping the goofy little bastards not you, you should get your head and your arse wired together and then maybe pass judgment. Sue: How dare you sir!! Mike: Em does this mean you ain’t givin me any money? Sue: WHAT? Mike: You know money! Moolah. Dinero. Sponds. Notes. MONEY!! Sue: Excuse my language sir but I wouldn’t give you the steam off of my piss!! Mike: Well excuse me lady but I wouldn’t get up on you to get over a wall you dirty whore!! Thanks for wasting my time cheese tits!! Mike slams the phone down as a slight smile breaks on his face. Mickey, who had been looking on nervously after he fucked it all up then smiles. The smiles grow wider until it grows into an all out Irish laugh fest. Mike: Oh my God that was SO funny!! Mickey: I know laddie. Mike then suddenly stops. The laugh is over. And just like the flick of a switch his complexion has changed to such a grimmace that he looks like Ronald McDonnald would look if Kelly Osbourne got her hands on him. Mike: What the hell are you laughin at idiot? If it wasn’t for your actual retardedness the bitch would’ve believe you were a retarded ten year old. She thought you were some paedo that wanted to touch the kids so she wouldn’t give us money. Mickey: That’s not true. Mike: it is. Mickey: Are you sure she didn’t think you were touching me? Mike: ……………………why would she think that? Mickey: I dunno maybe she though you were a Priest? Father Mike! Mike: Shut up idiot!!!!!!! Mickey: Jaysus I’m so bored laddie. Mike: I know. Mickey: Any ideas of what we could do? Mike: None…….Oh wait we could……. Mickey: What? Mike: Na……..WAIT!! Mickey: What? What? Mike: Na…… Mickey: Hey don’t you have a match this week? Mike: Yeah. And it's a title match too! For my all powerful Relentless title. I'm fixing to bring some feckin respect to this title just like I did some other belt in some other worthless fed that's about to fall on its arse. Mickey: Yeah shouldn't you be like preparing for your match? Mike: No Mickey it's against some Japanese guy and I don't eat Chinese food so I have no way of preparing for it!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- How am I supposed to prove myself a worthy champion and contender here in AWC if they keep feeding me mindless jobbers week after week? British Bomber and Johnny Noble? Please myself and the Face ruined their arses last week. This week I want to bring some more presteige to the under rated belt that is the AWC Relentless title. How fitting is it though? The most underrated wrestler in the fed holding and defending the most underrated belt in the fed. We stick together like two peas in a pod. Just like me and the Face. On a quest to beat down the biggotries of AWC. I'm gonna start demanding respect here and I deserve it. For reasons that people here don't even realise! God-damn it I went out and won the Relentless title from one of the best wrestlers in this game, Ian English with something a lot harder to mend then a concussion or a few cuts or a few bruises. I wrestled for that title and won it with a break that could not be healed with a cast or pain killers. Because no pain killer being made today would’ve been strong enough to heal what I was feeling that night, and fuck it I’m still feeling now. Because back when I won the Relentless championship and everyday since and a little bit before then I was wrestling not with broken bones, but with a broken heart. I think everyone undermines the value of a broken heart. Because as I say with a broken bone you can simply sit in front of the TV or whatever and veg out while your broken bone heals. But with a broken heart you can’t. I mean I can’t sit there in front of the TV for fear that I’ll see a TV programme or a movie just in case I get reminded of it all. I can’t sit there and listen to music for fear that a song will come on, our song, any song!! It’s torture. Now it doesn’t matter who, why, where, how or what broke my heart all that matters is that it’s broken and to be honest I don’t think it’ll ever be fully mended. That’s something I’m going to have to deal with. Now they say there is no cure for a broken heart, only time. Time heals wounds they say. Well I may have all the fucking time in the world but why sit there and wait in pain when you can find your own cure? Now the way it is with most people with a broken heart, they blame themselves. “Oh why doesn’t she want me?” or something along those lines. “What have I done wrong?” “I can change” well fuck that! How could someone not want me? I never do anything wrong and no way would I ever change! Why should I? I’m Mike feckin Wade and if you don’t want me your crazy! Now with these other people, as I say they like to blame themselves, but not just that. They also like to HURT themselves. You know this self harm stuff? Maybe they might start with a punch to the leg, then move onto a punch in the face. “Oh that felt good, let’s vent this frustration!!” Then eventually the fuckin fruitcakes are getting their friends to kick them in the nads, they’re burnin themselves, they’re cutting themselves. Jesus, freaks! Now the way I look at it why should I suffer? Why do harm to yourself when you can do it to other people? Now thankfully in my line of work I can do harm to people and get away with it. So why not exploit that? Now I’m always game for a laugh if you hadn’t already noticed, sure I got the comedy entourage and all that but I think you all know by now I got a big Irishmans temper. You cross me and I’ll fuck you up. Now I attribute all of my success since I won this here title to the fact that I got my heart broke. Because it’s then you realise that you have to fight for everything, that you can lose everything in just 5 seconds. So every time I gotta defend that title or any night I even have a non title match I go out there like I could lose it all. My broken heart is no longer an obstacle, it has become my fuel, my drive, and in a way my broken heart has become my heart!!
