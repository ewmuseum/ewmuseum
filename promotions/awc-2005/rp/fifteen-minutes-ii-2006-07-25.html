Fifteen Minutes II

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th July 2006 He sat in somewhat solitude exacerbating questions that belied reason; stripped from a reality that was so harsh a dichotomy it defied comprehension. Who would do this to him? The room was bitterly cold, and a must of fermented sweat ebbed and flowed from every orifice, emanating from every crack infiltrating his nostrils. Every part of his body seemed to sway to the jutting beat of an out of tune symphony while a cacophony of sound battered his skull. …and your fifteen minutes are up… It had been six long months: solitary. No questions asked. There were no answers anyway. No contact. Hell, there was hardly food. Or what constituted food. …and your fifteen minutes are up… That dark mane of dreadlocks had deteriorated to an external reflection of his now emaciated self respect. Gone were the eyes that intimidated fighters across the planet, their deep brown gaze replaced by a vacant expression which had all but given up on existence. If he truly had committed such a wrong, he was in hell for doing so. …and your fifteen minutes are up… From the concrete coffin surrounding him, he had not heard voices for what seemed like an eternity. For those precious few moments when a bloodcurdling scream could be heard across the hall he felt human again. It’s a feeling of decadence. Humanity, that is. Being made to feel inferior, inhumane, caged like a… like a lion… a Fijian lion… shattering every concept of life you’ve once known into tiny shards of incomprehensible illogical garb. Life had done this to him, life had made him this. …and your fifteen minutes are up… He’d lost his name, his identity, his faith, his reason for existing… all… gone… his details of that night were sketchy, withered under the effects of his drinks being laced with surreptitious sedatives. When he looked inside, he wasn’t G.A., he was still UR… or was he? Every perception of life up until now had been built of misconceptions that the world was a good place. GA? If it kept him safe from the overtures of those willing to well… I suppose it would suffice… at least it wasn’t Jane Rose, or something of an even less masculine nature. …and your fifteen minutes are up… His body was still the same sculpted piece of art as before, every muscle thick and robust, every contour rigid, yet the lacerations across his face and back told a different story… …and your fifteen… “GA,” his body showed no sign of acknowledgement, his head had spoken to him many a time before. “GA!” the voice snapped with an air of impatience which clawed at his new body, or his old new body, or whoever he was. “GA!” this latest rendition sent a chill down the spine of GA… …and your fifteen minutes… The door burst open revealing two men. One immaculately dressed, Saville Row Tailoring, Jermyn Street shoes, the other in high ranking army uniform. GA looked up in disbelief at first, before taking a second glance. “How may I make your acquaintance, gentlemen?” GA spoke in a twang born from learning English under African, Fijian and Maori tutorship. “Ah dear boy,” he spoke like the Great Gatsby, “we’re getting you out of this quaint little cell.” He nodded to the suit. Gabriel’s eyes followed Gatsby’s across the room, there was something quite not right. He cared less. …and your fifteen minutes are up… “Come on then old sport, let’s be having you,” he motioned for GA to come with him. The suit was strangely silent, observing protocol perhaps. Later GA would call him the handler. …and GA fifteen minutes were up… Long live GA? Anything sure beat here. Anything? He'd think that one over before his time was done. Most people would give anything for their fifteen minutes. Some even kill. GA had taken his. Lost his. Pondered over his. Finally, return to his. Every minute is precious, precocious, packed with life. These fifteen minutes were up, but a new set would surely begin soon… This is the back story on my, his, our, life. Life. That four letter word meant so little to so many – GA would show them what they were missing. Show her what she was missing. Show them to how to exist. Only after a downfall can you soar to the heavens and proclaim. Even his thoughts perplexed him. They perplexed me, and I transcribed them. Too much Palahniuk, not enough F. Scott. Fitzgerald. So many questions, so little willpower, so little time. Who, why, where, what, how… Gatsby snapped him out of his self pitying stance. His fifteen minutes were certainly about to begin. “By the way dear boy... what is your name?” Gatsby spoke once more. The bad part was. We didn't know anymore.
