The Lavelle Chronicles, ch3

8 May 2005

The Lavelle Chronicles Chapter 3 - Strange Happenings. Rp for Fresh Albany NY Takes place at 6:00 am to 8: 45 am. The night before I had taken six “panadol” to ease the pain of an excruciating migraine. My first day of work in the AWC had left me with bumps, bruises and a few plasters. I had used muscles I never knew I had. Most of the night I spent lying on the floor; now I had retreated to the warm comfort of my bed. The mornings sun slowly crept over the horizon. The mornings dew glazed the small grass fields, that lined the street corners of New York. My alarm flickered in the background as the minutes slowly passed by. Amongst the shadows lay a crumpled pair of jeans and a t-shirt. I hadn’t slept well these last few nights. Now and then I might get the odd hour, for it to be interrupted by the ‘broken’ hearted Rob. Lois had broken up with him two days ago; since then he hasn’t budged from his room. Romantic music would play up to all hours, only to be turned down when our posh neighbours would show up. Gavin suggested a night on the town, but, if Rob were to see all the couples I doubt he would enjoy himself, perhaps he would break down once more. This was a pickle - a fairly large pickle at that. I had thought of ringing Lois and mending their relationship; but I knew not to over step my boundaries. My coffee mug sat in its same place, untouched. I had been drinking a lot of coffee these last few nights. My job at the Pizza Hut was great for money, but the long hours exhausted me. I had seen Lilly two nights ago; I only wished I had approached her. She sat still with a group of girls as Gavin served them their meals. He would urge me to approach, funny thing is when I finally got the courage her boyfriend walked in. Surprise, surprise, he was on the Harbrook football team. Gavin had spoken fondly of him; as though he was some saint. But, when my eyes met Lilly’s she told a different story. She looked nervous, a little on edge. George - Scott Lewis was her boyfriend. He did have a name after all. My grandfather had rung earlier in the day announcing his surprise visit. Although now he mentioned it, it wasn’t much of a surprise. I was looking forward to seeing him, it had been a long time come to think of it. The last time we met was on my 12th birthday. He had flown in over night to give me my present. It was a mini model BMW E32 730i convertible, one of the ones you could pedal down the street in. I had enjoyed every moment with it, until one day I sent it into the golf club open air swimming pool by accident. I had abandoned ship once the car spun out of control; but it was quite funny to see the look on the rich peoples faces as a black car came hurdling towards them. My father had blamed my grandfather for the incident, announcing to whole park that he was irresponsible and a disgrace to the Lavelle family. You can see exactly why my father, Brad Lavelle, wouldn’t win father of the year. The alarm clock flashed as 8:00 am came around the corner. I knew it was time to get up. I couldn’t lie here all day thinking. My grandfather would be arriving soon and I had to get ready. I could hear Rob was up and in the kitchen. I rose from my slumber and threw on a pair of pyjama bottoms and a dressing gown. Rob was seated in the kitchen, dressed in nothing but his boxers. He was eating some cereal. “So how are you doing?” I asked as I sat down opposite him. I hadn’t seen Rob in two days. “A little better,” Rob announced, munching away on his shredded wheat. “How about we go out tonight? Go to the movies or something. A change from the life indoors.” I said pouring a glass of orange juice. I knew no matter what I said, Rob wouldn’t agree to go anywhere. He hasn’t moved in two days, I doubt he would now. It began to worry me about this depression. “Rob you have to stop wallowing over Lois. Please Rob, just come out.” I said once more, looking for a reaction. “I have an idea on how to get her back!” Rob replied, blanking what I had just said. “Rob are you ignoring me?” I looked him in the eye. “Yes!” He replied my stare. I was shocked by his sudden tone change and the stern “Yes” he replied back with. He was ignoring me and for what; I wouldn’t know. “Why?” I asked, I was becoming increasingly annoyed by his attitude. “I am tired of you giving me advice on girls and woman, funny thing is; I managed to get the girl I love and you still can’t even talk to the girl you love. Ironic if you think about it, I took your advice about telling Lois everything and where did it get me! I have no girlfriend, and that Pierce, is because of you.” Rob stared at me, like I was the cause of his pain. My best friend had turned against me now too. “I’m sorry Rob, really I am. I have to go and see my grandfather. I’ll talk to you at work.” I walked away realising he was right about Lilly, but to be honest I couldn’t do anything but hold back until she was ready. Rob had never spoken so cruelly to me before and I knew it was the anger in him; but the words he said did hurt. The following takes place between 1:00 pm and 1:15 pm. The last thing I expected was for my grandfather to answer his own door. It belied everything I knew about Bel-Air and the high-status life of the rich and famous. A man with a billion-dollar company would answer the door for nobody. Instead, he would have a uniformed man, posted in full uniform to answer. And this butler would only allow me entrance after checking my ID and his masters guest book and after that I would be past onto a maid or another butler. But their was none of that here in this New York Penthouse. My grandfather stood before me, dressed in a black Gucci shirt with a gold tie. He was wearing Ralph Lorraine black pin-striped trousers with his silver glasses perched neatly on his grey combed over hair. “Pierce, my you’ve grown. I’ve been so looking forward to your visit.” He said, opening the door wider. “I never expected you to answer!” I said with a little comedy to my tone of voice. The room he led me into was huge, there were wall to ceiling windows, offering a great view of Albany. “Well I couldn’t fine a butler on such short notice,” He laughed in reply to my comical joke about his riches. “I was wondering if you would join me for lunch?” He asked once more. I hadn’t seen the old man in such a long time and lunch was beginning to sound great, seen as I had skipped breakfast this morning. “Sure, why not.” I said. “Where would you like to have lunch? I know this great place, in fact I work there at the moment.” “Sounds perfect, exactly what is the name of the restaurant?” He said. “Well it is a place called ‘Pizza Hut’ and it serves great lunches. I know it isn’t the rich class joints you and your bingo friends would go to, but it has great food.” I said, waiting in the doorway. “Bingo! I like that, only I don’t play it.” He laughed. “One more thing, Suzie will be joining us.” “Whose Suzie?” I asked, a little confused by the sudden talk of women in my grandfathers life. “My new wife; and your grandmother.” He replied. “Okay sure!” I replied. Of course Suzie was still as much of a mystery to me. I didn’t know what she looked like, or who she was. But my grandfathers butler, didn’t speak too kindly of her from what I remember of our last phone call. The following takes place between 2: 00 pm and 3:00 pm Pizza Hut was surprisingly quiet for a lunch time. Gavin of course was on duty, he usually works twenty-two hour shifts. Although that’s a little bit of an exaggeration. I was still getting to know my co-worker and to be honest the make-up trend soon became a distant memory. Rob however, now that was a story. We decided we would do shots one night here in Pizza Hut when all the girls came. Anyway Gavin had told me that he liked Rob a lot, of course I was drunk at the time so a lot of my memory is a fuzzy blur. Rob was sat quietly in the corner of the room texting Lois, when Gavin approached him and told him a few words, to which I won’t repeat. Of course Rob being the sensitive type was quick to leave. Since then I have been a large wall between the two, passing messages back and forth. Switching shifts with Rob. It is quite a shame too, as Gavin and Rob could’ve been good friends. My grandfather smiled as we entered the small corner restaurant building on western avenue. He noticed Harbrook on our way here, and implied about me and my college studies. He was shocked to find out I got expelled but at least he didn’t have a fit like my own father. He chose a seat near to the window and then pulled out his mobile. It was then I spotted Lilly. She was wearing a pale pink skirt and a white short sleeved shirt, with a waist line to show off the skinny waist. I couldn’t help but stare. He hair was perched so tidily on her shoulders and her books tucked in order under her arm. She waved at me, I was shocked. She acknowledged my presence, of course me being in a different world, I never waved back! I hit myself for it afterward. “So Pierce, tell me all about this girl you like. You became quiet in the car.” My grandfather said, removing his sun glasses. “I would love to, but she is sitting right there!” I said in a whisper and nudged my head in Lilly’s direction. “She’s quite something,” My grandfather whispered back. “So about Harbrook, I would like to know when you plan to finish your course in medicine. It would be a shame to waste that brain of yours on waiting tables and wrestling men dressed in tights on mats.” My grandfather added. “I’m not really that sure what I am going to do about my medicine degree. I have one year left, in which I have to decide on the course I wish to study afterward. Then I have three years until I can become a doctor or whatever. See it is such a long time and money I don’t have. That is why I have two jobs.” I said, looking at Gavin who was laughing with Lilly over something. “What about your father, what did he think of this?” My grandfather asked. “How do you think?” I replied, now doodling with a biro on a piece of paper. “Well knowing Brad, he probably had a cardiac arrest.” My grandfather laughed. “Listen kid, if you need money please let me know, I want to see you be successful in your life. I don’t want to see that my son Brad has ruined the mind of my grandchild.” He said with a concerned look on his face. “He hasn’t ruined any of my ideas grandfather.” I lied, remembering a time when I was eight and I spoke fondly about wanting to be an astronaut. My mother encouraged my ideas and creativity, but she paid the price when my father hit her the night she made me a spaceman outfit. Since then I haven’t been able to look my father in the eye, not with the anger and hate I used to. I shouldn’t of told my mother my ideas and fantasies, it would have saved her the hard times she had. “Hey Rob!” Gavin shrieked from the other side of the room. “What can I get you?” He asked. “I’ll have a bacon BLT” I replied. “I’ll have the same and for my wife she’ll have a salad dish,” My grandfather smiled, noticing the empty seat beside him. Gavin left with the orders. “She’s running later,” He said. It was then I noticed this tall blonde woman walk into the room. She was wearing a tight blue summer dress and her hair hung along her shoulders. She had attracted everyones attention, including mine. But, suddenly my heart sank and so did my stomach as she sat next to my grandfather and the two shared a passionate kiss. I felt as though I was going to vomit. I ran out of my chair and into the men’s bathroom. For the better part of five minutes I was looking into a toilet, trying to come to terms with my twenty something year old grandmother. “Pierce, are you okay?” Lilly said, rubbing my back. “Yeah I’m fine, I’ve just met my grandmother.” I said, turning around to face Lilly and wiping my mouth with a piece of tissue paper. “You mean that young blonde is married to your grandfather?” She had a stunned look on her face. “Yeah, I didn’t take it well either.” I said, a little embarrassed by my response to her arrival. “Don’t worry about it,” Lilly said, handing me a bottle of water. “It is no wonder that Charles called her a gold digger.” I said; knocking back half the bottle of “Volvic” mineral water. “Charles?” Lilly asked. “My grandfathers butler.” I replied. “If your family is rich, why do you work here? And what happened to your face?” She asked touching the scar above my eye gently. “I’m not rich, only my grandfather is. Why I work here is another story and too long to tell and I also work in the wrestling business, which would explain the cut above my eye. I think I should return to the table.” I said standing up. “If you ever want to tell that story I’ll be waiting. I think there is some large science exhibition on tonight, loads of seats and cool flashing lights. Perhaps you could tell me there. I have plenty of time, and George will be drunk with some cheerleader no doubt.” “Perhaps you should tell me your story too!” I said and winked as I walked out of the bathroom. When I returned to my table, my grandfather and my ‘grandmother’ were gone. Gavin approached me with a letter. “Your grandfather left this for you, he seemed in a hurry.” Gavin said. Hey sport, Suzie and I have left the building; sorry for our missed lunch. But my pumpkin wanted to go to a tanning salon so I said I would drive her. We shall catch up another day. Until then kid, follow you dreams and ambitions. S.Lavelle. The following happens in Rob and Pierce’s apartment in Western Avenue. I lay on my bed thinking about Lilly’s proposal. Trying to recap the moment she had rubbed my back and handed me her water. How she spoke of her ‘boyfriend’ and his little rendezvous with the cheerleaders. I saw that my chance with Lilly was something I could achieve now. The science expo wouldn’t be for at least another four hours. Which gave me plenty of time. I had no work today in Pizza Hut, due to its closing early for the VE Day celebrations. My grandfather and Suzie were like “Bimbo Barbie meets Hugh Heffner”, it was a nightmare for me. The fact that this woman, who was young enough to be my mothers daughter was married to my seventy five year old grandfather. I didn’t want to think about their pleasures. I only wondered whether my family knew of this. His letter was abrupt and simple, but a tanning salon, please, they should’ve come up with a better excuse; than a bed of radiation. My thoughts were broken by my mobile chirping under the ruffled duvet. “Hello” I said as I answered the phone. “Pierce, it is Rebecca. I need to talk to you.” Rebecca my sister had rung me. The words “I need to talk to you” sounded worrying. I hadn’t spoken to Rebecca or my family in almost a month now. I worried about her and mom, but I knew they would be okay. The last time Becca and I spoke, she was fine, her voice was innocent and filled with joy as she stated she was going on a trip to the mall. Now however, her tone was anxious. “What is it Rebecca, is everything okay?” I asked. I could hear whimpers on the other end. “No Pierce, it is mom. She’s sick, really sick. Dad has gone away to Chicago for a week and mom suddenly fainted two days after he left. I can’t get through to him, not that he would care. But mom is in hospital. I’m all alone Pierce, I’m calling from the hospital room right now. The doctors have her in surgery. What do I do?” This was it, the apocalypse was truly happening for me. Rob wasn’t speaking to me. Bimbo Barbie was grandmother and now my mother was critically ill. At the age of six I came down with two forms of pathogens. One was gastroenteritis and the other salmonella. My mother remained strong through my deteriorated state. Most of the kids who did get the viruses, under the age of six hadn’t made it. My mother feared for my life and stayed by my bedside. Even with a baby at home she wouldn’t leave me. I felt terrible sitting here in New York instead of being in Harrisburg. I couldn’t repay the favour, I couldn’t look after her. “Rebecca just stay strong for mom, okay. Remember when you got the flu on your eleventh birthday and then me and dad got it; who remained strong and healthy and took care of me and you. Mom did. Now you need to return the favour. I would love to come and help, only I have no money. Please Rebecca, you have to be strong for the family. Mom needs you now.” I waited for her reply. “Okay Pierce, will you try and come?” She asked. “Yeah, I’ll try. Keep me updated though, okay.” I said. “Okay, I’ll ring back in 48 hours to let you know how things are going.” She hung up. I couldn’t sit still for a moment. I knew this was trouble. Three knocks came from our wooden door, of course I would have to answer as Rob was locked in his room. I couldn’t believe what was happening to me. Today was a strange day, a time of weirdness is what Rob would say. When I got to the door I was shocked by the surprise guest. Lois was standing before me with two suitcases. She launched into my arms and said hello. “Lois?” I said, a little shocked. “Yeah I know, I need to see Rob.” Lois said, watching me bring in her bags. “He’s sulking in his room, just go straight down the hall.” I said. Lois stared at me, noticing my tears. She suddenly stopped her hyper activity and stared me in the face. Putting one hand on my shoulder. “Pierce you’ve been crying, is everything okay?” She asked. “Not really, but I don’t have time to talk about it. I have to go out in about an hour. Just go see Rob. He needs you.” In the corner of my eye I saw Rob, he was wearing a pair of baggy tracksuit bottoms, his hair washed and he had obviously gotten some sleep. Lois ran over to him and into his arms. The two disappeared into his room. I sat down on the “L” shaped sofa and rotated my phone over and over in my hand. I thought about these last few moments and I realised something, that I have a date! I have a date! My wardrobe held only four pairs of jeans and four shirts. I quickly undressed until all I was wearing, were a pair of “Homer Simpson” white boxers. I pulled out a pair of dark navy jeans and slipped then on. I stared at my four shirts until I found the right one. It was a long sleeved white shirt, with navy blue patterns of writing and scribble on it. Every guy that has ever gone out with a girl, shares a common bond with their opposites dilemma too. What to wear? It is a simple thing, yet we fret over it, day in and day out. Of course women go over board and ask questions like: “does this make my bum look big?”. But then men, they worry about smelling good and which shirt to wear. Not how their body features look in them. The first date is always the most important and while Pierce is seated in his room wondering about shoe wear. Lilly Connelly is enjoying a bubble bath, with her clothes laid neatly on an oak stool. Lilly Connelly was the type of woman that didn’t fret about her hair or make-up. She remained a calm and collected brunette, which seemed to look good in anything she should would wear. She was studious and didn’t attract most men’s attention. For they feared the education in her, her wits and also her good lucks. Most of her class mates were size 36D and had long blonde fake hair. Of course she could never understand the point in silicone implants or dying your hair blonde for male approval. But back to Pierce. I could hear Rob and Lois making up, it was something you couldn’t help but hear. Lilly had called just to make sure of our arrangements. I laced up my black “Wrangler” boots and put on my silver watch. My cologne I hoped was okay, as not every woman likes men’s cologne. I was to meet Lilly outside the residence in Harbrook, to avoid any suspicion from her friends, she mentioned I was taking her to see a science expo, which of course they found hilarious. The following takes place at Harbrook College and the Science Expo in the “New York State Museum” at the Empire State Plaza, Madison Avenue, Albany NY 12230. Be warned of a little detour. I stood in the cold outside Lilly’s door. I knocked twice on the door, I felt nervous being here. The thoughts of George - Scott Lewis coming up behind me drunk, sent a shiver down my spine. I wore a hip length leather jacket to avoid the cold harsh weather. There was no answer from Lilly’s residence. When I knocked again the door opened. “You looking for Lilly?” The brunette asked. “Yeah,” I replied. “Is she here?” I implied. “Course she is, she’s just getting dolled up. Come on in, before you catch a cold.” The brunette replied in her heavy New York accent. The residence was small, enough for about three women. One was seated in the living room watching “Dr. Phil” and the other, the brunette retreated to the kitchen to finish her assignment. I stood at the bottom of the pink stairs awaiting Lilly’s arrival. I noticed a picture of her and the two girls hanging on the wall in front of me, it was dated to three years ago, making Lilly a freshman. She would of been 18; yet she still looked beautiful. “Hey, sorry about being a little late. We probably only missed some pyrotechnics.” Lilly said as she came down the stairs, putting in some earrings as she did so. She looked beautiful. She was wearing a long dark red skirt, with a pattern on the left hip, she had a long black jacket over that and dark red heels. “You look beautiful,” I stated, my mind still in awe at the site of the woman before me. “Thanks, I never get to dressed up like this. You look really good too. I like the shirt.” She said, grabbing her small underarm carry bag and heading out the door. “So how do you want to get there? We have to get to Madison, from Western.” I stated. “We’ll have to walk a few minutes, there isn’t a taxi rank for about ten minutes from our residence.” She said, holding my hand. I was beginning to shake. Lilly was holding my hand, but it was only for balance as she was adjusting one of her heels on one foot. Needless to say I felt stupid, but when she didn’t stop holding my hand I embraced the comfort of her touch. “So what is it exactly you like about George?” I asked, to start the conversation. “To be honest nothing. I met him a few months ago, we aren’t really together and he’s not that perfect after all. I thought he was a blonde haired, muscular gentlemen, when in fact after a month of on and off dating; he turns out to be a drunk who likes cheerleaders on the side. I’m going to break up with him; I’m just scared to do so.” “What do you mean?” I asked. “He was brought into the cop station three weeks back for hitting a cheerleader in my class. Her name was Courtney. She had a broken nose. He’s violent as well. A great man indeed.” She laughed but then stopped. A little nervous about the back alley she pulled tighter into me. “Has he hurt you?” I asked, a little intrigued by this situation. “Mentally and emotionally, yes. Physically, no” She said, sensing I was a little relieved from the last one. “What about you. Have you got a girlfriend?” She stared at me, while we waited for a taxi. “No, I haven’t got a girlfriend. My college years weren’t that successful for me.” I replied. “Really?” She was surprised. “Well I know I am gorgeous looking, but no I haven’t got a girlfriend.” I replied. “Rob has though, she appeared out of nowhere today. I thought they broke-up.” “What?” She asked. I forgot that I hadn’t mentioned Lois and Rob or my life in Harrisburg to her before this. “Sorry, I’ll explain it later. It’s a long story.” I said. “Listen, do you want to skip the science expo and go for a coffee somewhere.” She smiled and held my hand once more. “Sure, how about that place there?” I pointed to a posh looking coffee house. “Great!” She said and we walked across the street toward the coffee house. An hour later. I had been surprised by Lilly’s calm reaction to my past in Harrisburg, about how my father treated me and now my life here in Albany. I even told her about my mothers illness. I had never been able to open up this much with a girl before in my life, but something about Lilly Connelly settled the nerves. I felt very close to her. She had shared a lot of her life with me too, she had spent a lot of her life searching for the answer to her younger brother and fathers sudden car crash. She soon gave-up when the cops arrived and told she would be in danger if she continued. She had lied about what she was studying though, she wasn’t studying history. She was studying forensics. She had said how if she couldn’t find the problem, she would help those solve the mysteries of their loved ones deaths. It is what made her become interested in forensics and crime scene investigation; her favourite programme in fact was CSI. On the way home to Lilly’s house we stopped by a small game store, and played on a few of the games. Of course Lilly was no match for me on the dance mat in her heels but it was fun. Her boyfriend had rung twice, but she had hung up on both occasions. She just smiled and said, he’s probably on the job with her again. Her was something I wondered about, who was this her she spoke of. “Listen, I think I should go home soon, before he shows up and hurts someone.” She said. I agreed to walk her home and helped her down off the wall she was sitting on, eating a small doughnut. “So you think he would hurt me or Gavin, if he found out about us?” I asked. “I’m afraid so, he doesn’t like me hanging around with men.” She said. “But he hangs around with women!” I stated. “I know,” She said. “One thing, can we still be friends. Despite what your boyfriend thinks. I’m not afraid of footballers Lilly, remember I was picked on by them before. Don’t worry about me, I can handle things my own way.” I looked her in the eyes, for just a small bit of emotion. “Sure we can be friends.” She said. I walked her up the long steep steps to her front door. She turned around and kissed me on the cheek and opened the door. “Nice cologne, see you tomorrow.” With that Lilly was gone, the door closed. It was just me and the nights sky. Lilly kissed me. Despite the strange day and my bad luck, it seems Lilly had become my good luck charm. View Pierce Lavelle 's Biography
