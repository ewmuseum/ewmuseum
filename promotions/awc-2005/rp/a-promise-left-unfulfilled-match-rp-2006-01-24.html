A Promise Left Unfulfilled...(Match RP)

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/24/2006 The camera slowly fades in as gunshots can be heard ringing out over the darkness. All of a sudden the sound of tires screeching and a car speeding off down the street can be heard as well. The camera fades into a city street scene at night and we see an old gray-haired black man falling to the ground in slow-mo with a heavy thud. The old gray-haired black man in his tan trenchcoat and matching hat looks down at the gunshot wounds in his chest with wide, shock-filled eyes. The blood begins to soak his trenchcoat and a slowly growing stain of red can be seen. The old man slowly touches his wounds and the blood sticks to his hands. The old man struggles to try and stay strong, to stay alive, but is fading fast. All of a sudden a man who appears to be a slightly younger version of Vince Jones drops to his knees in horror at the sight and tries to encourage him to keep going. Vince Jones: (frantically shaking his head) NOOOO!!! You gotta stay strong, man! Ya gotta stay strong! C'mon! C'MON!! Vince Jones begins tapping the man on the face trying to maintain his attention, but it is all in vain. The old man manages to muster enough life and energy to part his lips. Old Man: Vince...you gotta...go on...without...me. Vince Jones: (shaking his head) No! No! Don't say that shit, man! Nah! You gonna make it! Fuck that! You gonna make it, man! You gonna get up and shit's gonna be fine! The old man's eyes are now half shut and he slowly shakes his head.</ i> Old Man: No...I'm...its...my time, Vince. Vince begins frantically shaking the old man and shakes his head in denial of what's going on. Vince Jones: (half in tears) Don't say that shit, man! No! Don't say that shit! (quickly wipes the tears away from his eyes) Everything's gonna be alright. Right? The old man's eyes finally roll back in his head and he loses consciousness. Vince looks down at the old man in shock. Vince Jones: Hey! Heeey! Wake up! C'mon! Get up, man! Get up! GET UP!!! Vince Jones lowers his head filled with sorrow for his fallen comrade. Vince Jones begins sobbing as he peers down at the lifeless face of the old man. Tears stream down his cheeks and he immediately wipes them away. He quickly rises to his feet and looks around at the empty late night block in New York half in fury and half in sorrow. Vince Jones: FUCK YOU MOTHERFUCKAS!! THOSE BULLETS WERE MEANT FOR ME!! FUCK YOU!! V'LL HUNT YOU BITCHES DOWN!!! THIS SHIT AIN'T OVA!! The camera quickly switches to the present. We are now in a small bedroom. The light gently penetrates the blinds and dances on the floor slightly illuminating the room. The camera pans around the room past the dresser, a small nightstand, and catches sight of Vince Jones asleep in his bed. Vince Jones can be seen rolling around on the bed of his bedroom as if he's having a nightmare of some sort. He wears a black doo rag on his head, a white tanktop t-shirt, and a pair of black boxers. He can be heard yelling something to himself in his sleep. Vince Jones: Fuck you! Fuck you bitches! Fuck you! All of a sudden Vince Jones' eyes pop open and he sits up in bed dripping with sweat. Vince Jones: (looks around in confusion) Huh? Vince turns and sits at the edge of his bed. He lowers his head and begins wiping the sleep from his eyes. Vince Jones: Shit, man. That damn nightmare again! He sits there in silence and tries to collect his senses for a moment. All of a sudden there is a quiet knock at his door that catches his attention. Vince Jones: Its open. The doorknob turns slightly and another knock is heard at the door. Vince slowly raises his head and gazes at the door in frustration. Vince Jones: Its open!! A female's voice from outside: No, its not V. Vince Jones: (grunting under his breath) Damn! Vince slowly rises to his feet and walks across the room to open the door. Vince Jones: Aiight. Just a sec... He opens the door and sees his girlfriend/valet, Jasmine, standing in the doorway in her slightly open, pink night gown exposing a beautiful and fit figure accented by silky, pink lingerie. She gazes up at him with a pair of worry filled hazel eyes. Jasmine: Are you all right, V? I heard you yelling from down the hall and I just wanted to check up on you. Vince Jones leans against the doorway and nods his head. Vince Jones: Yeah. V alright. It was just a lil nightmare and all, girl. Just go on back to bed. Every thing's fine here. Jasmine: (surprised) What? Another nightmare? V, this is the fourth night this week. What's going on? (she reaches up and feels his forehead) Are you feeling alright? You can tell me, V. Vince chuckles to himself and gently pulls her hand from off his forehead. Vince Jones: (shakes his head) Ain't nothin' wrong. Jasmine gently lays a hand on his chest and lets it slowly slide down to about his waistline feeling every bulging muscle on the way down. Jasmine: V, you don't have to be all tough around me. You should know that by now. Something's on your mind. Get it off your chest and you might feel a little bit better. That always helps me. Vince Jones lowers his head and pauses in thought. Jasmine gently grasps Vince's broad chin in her hand and lifts his head up until his eyes meet hers. Jasmine: (concerned) Something is on your mind. Just tell me. I might be able to help you feel better. I'm a good listener, but I can't do a thing for you if you don't speak. So, what's it gonna be? Jasmine gazes into his dark, brown eyes and awaits an answer from him. Vince remains silent and his lips finally part. Vince Jones: Aight. This nightmare V's been havin' concerns his past life before the game. That's all. Ain't that big a deal. Its just a lil somethin' that V's gotta deal with on his own. Jasmine: (with growing concern) Yes, it is. If you're constantly having this nightmare it has to be bothering you deep down inside. Tell me, V. Tell me more. Vince lets out a sigh, rolls his eyes, and shakes his head at her. Vince Jones: Aight. See, eva since the beginnin' V be havin' this same nightmare. Everytime he starts shit up from scratch. He starts havin' that damn nightmare all ova again. Jasmine: (in confusion) Whenever you start what over? I'm not following you. Vince Jones: You know. Startin' ova in the game, in a new federation and all. It really gets in V's head. Jasmine: (nods) Oh, I see. What? Are you a bit anxious over your debut? What's going on? Why the recurring nightmares. Vince turns away from Jasmine for a moment and lowers his head. Vince Jones: (shakes) Nah. It ain't anxiety or nothin' like that. (laughs to himself) Shit V fightin' two bitches in the ring. You know that, Jasmine. That shit's already a wrap. (shakes his head) But, nah. Its somethin' bigger than that. Wheneva V has to start ova its like he's failed, failed himself and failed him. Jasmine looks on in confusion wondering who this 'him' it is that Vince is referring to. Vince Jones: Its almost like V ain't livin' up to the promise he made a long, long time ago before really gettin' involved with this wrestlin' shit. Jasmine's eyes are locked on Vince awaiting more. Jasmine: What promise? What do you mean by failure? C'mon V. We're really making some progress here. Vince Jones just shakes his head and looks down at Jasmine. Vince Jones: Look Jasmine! It ain't anything you need to be concerned with, aight? V got some ghosts in his closet, a few issues and all he gotta deal with. Forget we even had this conversation. Forgot what V even told you. You wouldn't understand the deal and V ain't expectin' you to understand all this shit. Its way too complicated. So, we ain't talkin' 'bout this shit anymore. Understood? Vince is about to turn and go back into his room, but Jasmine tugs on the back of his tanktop beckoning him keep talking. Jasmine: V... Vince jerks free of her grasp and Jasmine begins to pout. Jasmine: (in concern) Baby, how can you just assume that I won't understand, huh? That's not even fair How can you say that to me? I've been here for you and in your corner longer than you probably remember. If there's anyone that can understand you its gonna be me, baby. And that's just the way it is. Its true. Vince stops in his tracks but doesn't bother to turn and face Jasmine.</ i> Jasmine: You and I have been through a lot. And I really wish that you would open up a little more than you do. Vince just shakes his head and remains silent. Jasmine: Whether you like to admit it or not you're not alone. You're not alone in this world at all. I know you better than you know yourself sometimes. I know you've got this whole 'Its me against the world' attitude. I mean if I had to go through some of the things that you had to go through coming up maybe I'd feel the same way too. Your father, David... Vince tenses up at the sound of his father's name, turns around, and glares at Jasmine with rage-filled eyes which make Jasmine immediately take a step back. Vince slowly shakes his head from side to side and waves a finger at Jasmine. Vince Jones: Don't you eva mention that son of a bitch's name around V eva again! You got that Jasmine!? Neva again! We've been through this shit many times ova! Jasmine lowers her head in shame. Jasmine: I'm sorry, V. Vince Jones: You betta be sorry! An awkward silence passes between the two of them as Vince folds his arms across his chest. Jasmine finally speaks up to break it once again. Jasmine: But back to what I was talking about, V. I know where you're coming from. Remember, we grew up together. We've been through thick and thin together. We've been through the highs and the lows together. When you were rolling with the EDW do you know how hard it was for me to lie to my parents whenever you and I would go out together? My parents would've killed me if they had known I was going out with a thug like you at the time. My family is my heart. You know that; but, despite that, I did whatever I could to manage to spend time with you. And... and remember the day of your trial and how you got locked upstate with the rest of your crew? (she rests a hand on her chest) That day I felt like I had gotten locked up too. I went back and cried myself to sleep that night. And do you even know how long I must have sat around waiting for your letters to arrive in the mail, huh? Do you? You know how hard it was waiting for you to come back home? Do you know how hard it was to have to deal with guy after guy after guy coming by my house wanting to get a lil somethin' somethin' while you were away? Each of them with their own lil line 'Oh baby, forget about him. He's old news. He's locked up and he's neva coming back' or 'Baby, baby, baby. He's no good for you. He ain't got shit no more. He's probably forgotten about you by now anyways.' Vince leans folds his arms across his chest, leans against the door frame and listens intently to her words now. Jasmine: V, I kept the faith. Despite how hard it was to believe, I kept the faith. I knew you would come back. Two long years I waited and remained faithful until you finally got released. And remember your days in the NYUFC? Who was it that cleaned up your wounds every time you returned from one of your prize fights? Who was it, V? Who was it that was there with you crying thinking that your next match might be your last down there? It was me, baby. I was the one there for you. And I will always be there for you, baby. Vince remains silent and nods his head. Vince Jones: Ya know somethin' that whole lil speech of yours was touchin' and all, but V really wanna get some sleep. Goodnight. Jasmine: But wait! Jasmine reaches and tries to stop Vince from leaving, but he turns his back on her once again, steps inside his room, and shuts the door behind him. Jasmine just stands there looking at the door a bit disappointed with Vince's lack of feeling.The camera switches to the inside of Vince's room where he can be seen leaning back against the door cupping his chin and contemplating Jasmine's words in silence. Jasmine: (from outside the room) V I have just one last thing I wanna tell you. When you first got started with this wrestling thing I was the first one you called. You were so excited when you first debuted in NAPWF. I remember it like it was yesterday. You were nervous and I could hear it in your voice. You didn't know how you'd be received by the other wrestlers or the fans. You had doubts that a guy like you from the streets could ever make it in the business. You doubted yourself. While you and I were on the phone that night, I remember telling you to be strong and that you'd make it with no problem. You remember that, V? I also told you that I would always be there for you, backing you up one hundred percent of the way You then asked me to join you on the road to keep you company and I didn't hesitate to accept. (a slight pause) You wanna know why? Because for the first time ever I knew that I could be one with you and that you and I could build something great together for a change. Ever since that day I've been with you through the hard times in GWA and WWCW overseas to the high times in 360, 360WE, and beyond. V, you're not alone in this world, baby. I understand a lot more about you than you give me credit for. So, if you remember anything of what I've just told you right now please remember this. When you enter that ring at AWC's Fresh! this week I'm gonna be there for you like I always have been, one hundred percent. Goodnight, V. Sleep well. Vince remains leaning against the door in silence for a moment as he listens to Jasmine's footsteps trail off back down the hall towards her bedroom. 'Damn...that shit was pretty deep. V neva really knew Jasmine felt that way about him. Then again maybe he did, but neva really paid much mind to it. That shit was definitely eye-openin' though. She's right though. She has been there through it all. She been through the highs and lows. Despite how hard it has been she's always been a down ass bitch. She'll definitely be supportin' V and all throughout his time in A Dubb C. And it prolly ain't fair to keep her in the dark and all, but there some things she just don't need to know about. The last time someone close to V got caught up in his business they got shot up...' Vince lowers and shakes his head. '...and that's not what he wanted, not at all. That should've been V that got popped that day. It should've been V that was sittin' in that pine box at that funeral, not you, old man. (holds his head in despair.) Its some shit how you ended up havin' to lose yo life for all the shady shit V had gotten himself mixed up with...' Vince looks up at the sky. '...but, for that V. Jones will foreva owe you his life and all, mane, and that's real. He gave up the shady game for a legit one like you always wanted for him, but that still ain't enough to heal these old wounds.' Vince shakes his head. 'Nah man, not at all. When you got shot that day a part of V died and that ain't cool, not at all. V had to change shit up. He had to change his focus and really look at how he was livin'. Sooner or later V ends up in NAPWF and the rest is history. (nods his head) Well, once again V steps into another federation lookin' to rise even higher in this game. This time he hopin' its for the long haul. (nods) Yeah. V. Jones be sayin' the same to you every time he startin' off brand new again. You prolly up there lookin' down like 'Here we go again' and all, but this time he thinks this shit is different. Its just somethin' in the air. This time V thinks you really gonna be proud. This time V thinkin' he gonna achieve what you always dreamed of achievin' for yo self. Why? Cuz V gotta do it. He owes you that much in this life. And when he does it consider that V's way of sayin' thanks for bein' the man that V. Jones' father neva had the balls to be in this lifetime. Consider that shit as thanks for sacrificin' yo own life so that V. Jones could eventually find somethin' to truly live for...' Vince Jones lowers his head, closes his eyes, slowly slides his way down the doorway, and sits on the floor. '...Old man, V. Jones plannin' on makin' it to the top of A Dubb C by any means necessary startin' this week at Fresh! V owes you that much. He owes you his life. 'Nuff said on that shit. That's the way it is and will always be. ' The camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones just sits there on the floor in thought.
