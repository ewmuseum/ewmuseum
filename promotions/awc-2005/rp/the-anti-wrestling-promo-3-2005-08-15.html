The Anti-Wrestling Promo 3

15 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/15/05 8/15/2005, 11:30 a.m. [We open with James von Doom simply standing in front of the AWC logo just like he did on the show this week in what appears to be a normal wrestling interview area. He has a somewhat demented look on his face. Folks, it's just a dude with a microphone. That's all the explaining that needs to be said about the set. He's wearing a dark blue sweat jacket with a white T-shirt under it with blue jeans and dark blue Vans shoes.] After my actions over the past few weeks, the most important of which was invading the AWC with all of my characters, I'm sure I have some explaining to do the you, the fans of the AWC. I feel really silly about the whole situation and I think that everyone needs to know where my head's at going into your little pay-per-view. What's it call? Ballbusting in Britain or something like that? [He brushes his hair back and starts getting serious.] But, after seeing a sign that said 'Kill von Doom' in the crowd on the show, it seems as though you people have some explaining to do of you own. (smiles) But we'll save that for later. Because, you see, I am here to talk about a 'sport,' if you can call it that, that is near and dear to my heart. And that is professional wrestling. [His face has a weird, somewhat distressed look about it. It seems he has a lot on his mind.] But how can you call it professional wrestling anymore? I mean, how can you people actually believe that this (motions around with his hand) is wrestling? [James looks down and suddenly gets calm as he begins speaking again.] Then you people really seem to be fucking stupid if you believe that the AWC actually produces (pause) ...wrestling. [He wipes a fake tear from his eye before turning back to the camera with a emotionless look on his face.] I mean, (suddenly bursting with energy) COME ON! Amy Silveira a wrestler? PLEASE. You idiots fell for a guy ripping off John Grisham for crying out loud! [He smiles dementedly and brushes his hair back again.] Hell, I could come on here and do a modern version of Shakespeare's the Tempest and you people would believe it to be original. (smirks) If I came on here and ripped off a red and yellow 'von Doommania' T-shirt and you people would believe me to be the most original wrestler here among all these (motions around) rip-offs and PRIME and Core wanna-be wrestlers you've got around here. [He almost looks like he's going to laugh.] And how do you people greet me with boos, cat calls, and chants of 'von Doom Sucks!' Well, listen up buckos. I've won more championships than you're entire roster combined so I think you retards should listen to what I have to say. [He giggles a little bit to himself.] That's really fucking sad now that I think about it. All you have is a bunch of has-beens and never-was wrestlers around here, so no wonder they wanted me so bad for this, uh, what's it called? Barnyard Brawl Match? [He slowly goes from complete tranquility to all out madness within a period of five seconds.] What the intensity picks up here) FUCK KIND OF FUCKING BULLSHIT IS THIS?! I come to the AWC for some good old fashioned family entertainment and you retards ruining it by trying to put on an Orwell in the Park theater presentation of 'Animal Farm.' Now what am I supposed to think? Do you idiots want me to come on here and start oinking like a pig or something? [He has a slight smile on his face now.] I came to the AWC to entertain you, the fans. [He motions to the camera, still with a smile on his face.] But instead I get put in a carnival ride. I am a wrestler, not a circus performer. But oh yeah, that's right. Circus performers are allowed to wrestle in only three places. The WWE, Mexico, and here in the AWC. [He brushes his hair back again and suddenly gets more intense.] A couple of weeks ago, I came out here and invaded (pointing toward the camera) YOUR show. I came out proclaimed this to be the von Doom Wrestling Club. My head writer even appeared, but even he wouldn't come back to this craphole because it wasn't an actual wrestling fed. It has instead turned into a circus freakshow. [He seems to be growing more demented by the minute.] With wrestlers such as the Illustrious Face-Eater and the Farmer, how can anyone come to respect the AWC, let alone professional wrestling as a whole? [He starts pacing around a little bit as he speaks.] No matter how many matches I win, no matter how many times I invade, no matter how much blood I spill, no matter how many segments I'm in, I will never be the most respected wrestler in the business, (smiling dementedly) but neither will anybody else. I'll never get any respect. I'm the Rodney Dangerfield of our industry. I mean, come on. Let's face it. How can you be respected in a business that no one outside of the business respects? [He starts staring down at the ground now, not even acknowledging the camera.] And do you realize why wrestling will never be respected? Because James von Doom is a wrestler. [He moves his head back up and stares angrily into the camera.] Actually wrestling on television garners you chants of 'BORING!' and the ever popular 'YOU FUCKED UP!' if you screw up your spot. You see, I am an actual wrestler whereas the AWC roster is made up of a couple of Irish guys, (moving his hand) a couple of British guys, (pause) ...and the rest of these AWC idiots are merely jobbers from Core who got ass raped by Smiley and Munson using Matt Lanier's two inch penis. [He starts staring back down at the ground again with a little smirk on his face.] That's why I came out here. I had to show you people an ACTUAL WRESTLER. Weird, isn't it? James von Doom...a wrestler? (smirks) Hell, I'm the UWF North American Champion. Half your roster couldn't even win the UWF Jobber Championship! And that's against the Huckster and Layla's Bong! [He looks back up at the camera.] Every week, I turn on the AWC Fresh! television show and see a bunch of people come out here and try to act all cool. (moving around a bit) Yeah...I see guys like the Farmer come out and talking to vending machines and you people call that (using finger quotes) 'ENTERTAINMENT.' (chuckles) Hell, I could wrestle the entire card against yard tools and out on a better show than you morons! [He gets a slight smile on his face.] I mean, what'ca gonna do? Have the Illustrious Face-Eater come on here and eat my promo after I eat yo mommas out last night before I stuck it... (chuckles) ...using your language, STUCK IT UP HER ARSE~! [He brushes his hair back and gets ever more intense as he continues on, smiling the entire time.] People, do you realize that the UWF puts on good, clean, actual wrestling shows every week? (smiles) I mean, what's going to happen when James von Doom steals the show? Yeah. What's going to happen when I come out here and talk to a mop and that's the best segment? (smirks) What's Dave Harber going to do? Pull the plug from DOOOOOBLIN~? (turns to camera) What'ca gonna do Dave? Send my arse back to a REAL country like the United States? [He starts shaking his head.] No. You won't do that! You can't do that. I mean, if you do that, wrestling as we know it would fall apart at the seams! [He flips off the camera.] FUCK YOU AWC! I PWN YOU ALL~! [He quickly calms down and stares into the camera with a slight, demented smile on his face.] Let me ask you ass clowns something. Who is the most entertaining wrestler ALIVE TODAY~? [He pauses to let the fans try to answer at home. After a few moments of silence, he answers the question for you.] For those of you who answered James von Doom, you get a Tootsie Pop for a trophy. For those of you who didn't say James von Doom, well, (smiles) you're just too fucking stupid to see the truth. (suddenly getting more intense) And if any of you answered Hulk Hogan, I'll have to kill you. [He shakes his head before staring back into the camera.] Any answer other than me would equal the end of wrestling as we know it. [He brushes his hair back again.] My mission is now clear. I must save professional wrestling from its own worst enemy...ITSELF. [He acts like he's cleansing himself of it.] I must save myself and all of the fans from the mockery that professional wrestling has become. [He smiles, almost as if he likes it.] I must save you all from your own stupidity. I must save you all from the idiots on the AWC roster who are so self-righteous that only one of them had sack enough to call me out. Only (holding up one finger) ONE of your so-called (using finger quotes) 'SUPERSTARS' of the AWC had balls enough to call me out. [He is gleaming now.] That man was your Transatlantic Champion Pierce Lavelle. But, of course, his plate is full at the 'Battle of the Bulge' or whatever your little shindig is called. I forgot...honestly, I did. I'm sorry. I don't like to stare at crap for too long. (mockingly) I'm so sorry. [He begins speaking very seriously.] From what I saw, he's facing the entire roster in one match. Oh yeah, that's real good. Let's book half the roster against the champ and watch him retain. Yeah. Like we all haven't seen this kind of booking before. Yeah, let's watch him fight off the entire VWC with weak baseball bat shots week in and week out and expect people to watch it. Yeah, let's expect them to watch it over and over again until it becomes so unrealistic, it becomes WCW reborn. [James now is looking solemn and melancholy as he continues on his rant.] I must rid the world of professional wrestling and anyone involved in it because (suddenly becoming intense) it's destroying the very fabric of the universe! The way I look at it, the AWC thinks it's the center of the wrestling universe. Well, the AWC is WRONG. James von Doom is the center of the wrestling universe and I am the man who you all should be worshipping (becomes intense) RIGHT NOW! (smiles) Not some midget who lost his smile or some woman who everyone thinks is a guy. [He giggles to himself for a moment before continuing.] You want to look for leadership, look to James von Doom, not Angelo Deville or Karina Wolfenden because they're too busy fucking each other to care about the wrestling business. And certainly not that overrated idiot Pierce Lavelle... [He has a gleam in his eye as he says this, almost as if he actually believes the nonsense that he is saying.] Pierce, get back to reality. You come on TV, you rally the entire AWC roster against me, and expect me, the LEGENDARY James von Doom not to respond? (laughs) PLEASE! Calm the fuck down. And listen to what I have to say. [James quickly calms down and then gets a slight smile on his face.] I come on your show and try to get your company some publicity. What do you do? You take it as an attack and come out with steel chairs and try to take me down. (points to himself) ME, James von Doom. I'm the most known personality out there and you and your misfit miscreant friends try to attack me? [He shakes his head.] Shame on you, Pierce. You're biggest claim to fame is winning a title that's only been around, what, three, four months tops. (smiles) I've held the UWF North American Championship, which has been around for over seven years in various forms, for longer than anybody. Almost a hundred days now and you dare to call me out? [James has a very serious look on his face now.] Pierce, the day you decided to call me out was the day that the AWC died. (becoming almost psychotic) I mean, ME WITH A MICROPHONE IS BETTER THAN MOST OF THE OVERLY DRAMATIC BULLSHIT NYPD WANNA-BE CRAP THAT GETS SHOWN IN WRESTLING TODAY! [He brushes his hair back and has a sudden sense of calm about him.] People take their wrestling too seriously. But, oh yeah, that's right. It's a competition. Yeah, let's see who can put out the most overly dramatic crap. Hell, I could recap a game of 'Clue' me and two friends had and that can be a promo. I can open up Stephen King's 'Rose Red' and post that on the promonet and that could win a World Championship. People take this shit WAY...WAY too fucking seriously and that is why the AWC will die a boring, overly competitive death. [He gets a sudden smile on his face. It creeps up like a spider on your arm.] That's my dream. Wrestling has become a shell of it's former self. None of you idiots know how to have any fun and now, look at what's it done to me. It's turned me into a guy who comes on camera with a microphone and bland surroundings as opposed to what I normally do - come out with a lightsaber and slice demons and gargoyles into little pieces. [He looks around with a sense of glee on his face.] But I can't do that any more! Why? Because I'm not being a SERIOUS wrestler. (shakes his head) If being serious is a crime, then I deserve to die a most painful death. [He stops shaking his head and almost starts laughing.] You see, that's why I have to destroy the AWC. Why? Because it's feds like these that are destroying the fun of wrestling. [He motions toward the camera.] All you idiots out there (becoming very angry) MUST BE DESTROYED LIKE THE PARTY POOPERS YOU ARE! FUCK YOU! FUCK YOU ALL! (becoming demented) You've all done everything you can to kill professional wrestling. You've literally killed the dreams of every kid who goes to a wrestling show who says 'I want to be a wrestler when I grow up!' So that's why I have to win the Barn of Doom~! match or whatever you idiots are calling it. I must...I have to...I have to beat all of the nobodies and wanna-bes. (suddenly growing intense) I MUST KILL THEM ALL! [James has a demented smile on his face now.] So Pierce, when we finally do meet, let's give the people what they want. Let's give them hammerlocks. Let's give them wristlocks. Let's give them sleeper holds. Let's make them say 'By golly, I just saw a good ol' fashioned wrasslin' match.' And let's do it in front of the ten people that will be in attendance. Let's show PRIME and Core what they are missing. [He's starting to ramble now.] Let's show these people what it's like to actually do the thing that we were hired to do. Sure, you can do all of that those crap moves from your AWC roster page if you want to. That'll be alright. Let's give these people a show that they deserve, not some crap where one of us puts the other one in a headlock for half-an-hour and that'll be the match. Let's do something that these people haven't seen since they've been here. Let's have FUN. [James starts to get melancholy again.] You know, I've been thinking over the past couple of weeks. People have said that I don't have what it takes to be a top tier talent in this industry. Yeah. I know exactly what each and every one of you people, especially you Pierce, believe to be true about James von Doom. [But, after all of this ranting, he's still smiling.] I know what you people are thinking and I know what the entire AWC roster is thinking. 'James von Doom, you'll never be anything more than a jobber.' [James flips off the camera and starts speaking in a very pissed off tone.] FUCK YOU! I'm better than I've ever been in my career! NOBODY and I do mean NOBODY can do the things that I do! Why? Because you're all too busy being (going insane again) SERIOUS TO HAVE ANY FUCKING FUN! YOU'RE ALL TOO BUSY TRYING TO BECOME THE NEXT FUCKING STEPHEN KING OR J.K. ROWLING INSTEAD OF HAVING FUN! QUIT BEING SO FUCKING SERIOUS AND WRESTLE GOD DAMMIT! JUST FUCKING WRESTLE! [He brushes his hair back, calming down.] Hell, I was jobbing in the NWC and getting booked in midcard matches that nobody cared about and did it with a smile on my face. Now days, a wrestler loses one big match and then they whine. They get on the fed's forums, say some nice things, and then, when they lose again, they start bitching about a conspiracy and then leave after throwing a temper tantrum. [He wipes a fake tear from his eye. He's obviously trying to be emotional, but he's already been drained by having to wrestle in five matches at UWF's Stable Wars VII pay-per-view only to lose in the finals to the Player's Club and the series of practical jokes played on him by Killer Dude. Now, he is here...with a microphone...in a shitty little studio.] I will never be considered one of the greats because I never had my nose up the owner's ass and I never won a title in the NWC. I will always be one of those guys who almost was there, but didn't make it because he wasn't a kiss ass. I just wanna go home... [The camera pans back as he closes his eyes and looks down at his feet. He starts tapping his feet together.] There's no place like home...There's no place like home... [The camera zooms back on his face again as he continues looking down.] I thought I had a home here in the AWC managing TAN, but it became a waking nightmare. Now I have a home...in the UWF. [He looks back up at the camera and is smiling.] I've found a new home. A place where I can love and be loved. A place where I can wrestle and not be pressured into trying to become the next Stephen King. A place that I can call home. Thank you Gordon Gordon, owner of the UWF. Thank you for being the one glimmer of hope in the wasteland that is professional wrestling. [The camera pans back as James starts unzipping his dark blue sweat jacket. Once it's unzipped, it is revealed that James is wearing a T-shirt that has Gordon Gordon airbrushed on it and it says 'Gordon Gordon is my Handler' on it. It's absolutely hilarious. James still has that demented smile on his face.] Gordon Gordon will save you all. [James abruptly walks off the set and the scene fades to black.] [Cut.]
