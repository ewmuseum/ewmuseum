Please enjoy. ++++++++ ~Moscow~ Journal Entry date: 20050726 2.11a.m. It's hard to trust anyone here. I have seen so many people stabbing each other in the back for one reason or another. Power and money are the paramount ideals breaking whatever faith people would have. Whatever goodness there was, it fades away at the glimpse of a large amount of money notes. Maybe I am still a bit naive and hope to see someone who would be able to resist it. Unfortunately, I have yet to meet such a person and the more I go on, the more my hope seems to dim away. I am not sure about the rest of Mother Russia, I am not sure about any of the ex-republics that once formed the Soviet Union but I see nothing but a twisted reflection of the Wild West here in Moscow. There are gold mines in every place you want to take advantage of. No need for honesty really, there is no way anyone could make it if not linking their endeavours to the all powerful mob. Depending where one was and what was wanted to be exploited; a contact to the local or stronger syndicate must be done. Of course, that is unless that entrepreneur wanted to end up on the next morning news as another corpse found in the middle of the street. Like in the wild west, there are many showdowns. Call the Sheriff? What could a few do really against the strength and power of these underworld syndicates? The Cuban look like amateurs, the Sicilians are the friendly neighbours compared to the raw violence the Russian mafiya had marketed itself with. Extortion, murder, prostitution and anything that could be seen as lucrative is in the hand of the different families here. Would anyone say or do anything? Well, when an organization is made up of former KGB agents, spetsnaz Special Forces commandos and the most brutal of businessmen, anyone would to stay out of the way or with them. I love my country and what she represents for me. We have changed, gone to different parts of the world to taste that freedom during the communist regime only and unlike some others, we come back to make our land evolve toward a future. Future which seemed fragile and even today has turned from the crumbs of darkness to a greater light. No one said change would be painless. No one should say there was no will to survive. Russians do so everyday, pouring sweat and blood for everything we wish for and when this goal is made...When the object of our work is within our hands, we have learned to appreciate it even more so than anyone else. The Black Hammer is my life as much as The Vatican is. I do believe in God but above all else I know I am Russian. My family might have lived in Kiev for many years until my birth but it is in Moscow that our hearts beat. This is there that some of my beloved uncles (not like that old pervert Chorinsky) and cousins had raised The Black Hammer. It was hard for them to do so but seeing either their family starve to death or fight for the only way to survive then, a little Krisha (protection money) asked from a few businesses meant nothing then. One of my uncles became the main figure of The Black Hammer. I didn't hear it from my parents. I've read it in the newspapers. I've seen TBH on the television and listened to his rampage within the underworld, building his network and solidifying it so that all others came to fear and respect it. In my time back in Rome, it was he who had helped pay my rent, my food and my entertainment. I am grateful to know he was watching over me and my family in the States. This is our strength as TBH, each of the members is a part of the family much like La Casa Nostra or the triads in Hong-Kong. We see our unity as a major power and we know how to deal with those who have come to betray us in an expediting manner. It is under The Vatican's watch I had learned to be who I am today but it is also thanks to The Black Hammer I have been able to evolve and perform meticulously what my reputation was based upon...and it is the reason why my heart and soul will never be at ease. I seen unresolved to change this part of me. I killed yesterday and although I did see the necessity of having this man removed, I still feel disgusted with myself. I have taken the task from TBH and have done so without flinching. Another kill, another couple of bullets spent and could it have been different? Would it? It could have been real messy. There was no art in there, no precision or no satisfaction of a job done. I mean sure, Chorinsky would be on his toes now, he would do what I requested of him but still...I wished someone else would have come up and done the job before I would accept it. They look at me, all of them, and I hope God has not forgotten his daughter here...There...Lost in the darkness of a city I had hoped to be different for me. Perhaps it is a bit too egoistic of me. I need to pray and sleep. I am tired but I remember that first person I met here in the Motherland's underbelly. Her words still wake me at night. 'Wanna play a bit of roulette?' And now The place could have been chosen to be somewhere else a bit classier but here and there, cracks had appeared on the off white walls, revealing a need to have them plastered again. The furniture was nothing pretty or stylish but was simply Spartan in appearance: A table and a couple of chairs and a bit further off to their right, the end of the wooden framed bed could be seen. It was exactly the kind of studio a humble working man would lodge himself into. A hole in a wall, some would describe it, but it was what was needed to rest. She never cared for luxury Even if the decadence of it was but a mere call away. She felt it was decaying the mind and the will Numbing the senses and the hopes to end those goals But sleep was such a time. Luxury moment of peace... In a time of war and death. The draft of fresh air was plenty enough to cool off the stagnant warmth and humidity residing in the room. The ceiling fan had not done a good job but Katerina didn't mind, as it would raise her senses to be alarmed by this sudden change. It was not that noticeable for one that would have been asleep as the woman was meant to be. This late in the night or this early in the morning, everyone was supposed to be in his or her dreamland. Katerina glimpsed quickly to the electronic clock resting on her bed table and knew dawn was still a few hours away. Streaks of her blond hair swept through her face, hiding it from anyone who would come up from her back. The young woman didn't move in an abrupt movement, she slipped her left hand under the only pillow on the bed as if to find some cooling comfort. Satisfied at feeling the handgun beneath it, she brought her breathing back to normal while waiting for whoever had entered to approach. She listened to the careful steps enough to know they came from a heavier weight that she was. She frowned a little when she heard the intruder pull one of the chairs and sat on it. There was something in the air, a known faint smell that came to her nose but she didn't have time to think about the ingredients forming this perfume. Whoever it was. It would not be a robbery. Whatever it wanted. It would end up with bullets flying through the air. Sometimes, desperate people needed desperate remedies to their torments. A couple of steel plugs. Right there, piercing through their skins and flesh A revelation of crimson plasma splashing through the air and onto her home 'You can stop pretending Katerina.' He said in a perfect Russian, his English accent was one she could not recognize. The calm tone coming from the man was soothing compared to the rough voices she had heard speaking to her all week. 'I know you are awake.' 'Yes I am and it would take me a couple of seconds to pull my handgun and point it to your head. It would take another second for me to squeeze the trigger and enough time for you to curse the moment you have entered my place.' She replied back in the same language but spoke deliberately slower. She altered a bit the truth; it would have taken much longer to reach and screw the silencer on. She couldn't fire her weapon in her own place and alert everyone living around her. Nonetheless, her index finger was already on the trigger and pulled the safety off. 'So what is it that would make me stop from blowing your brains right there on my chair?' 'Just move to strike me cutie pie!' She ended up mumbling away. 'First, if I wanted you dead Katerina, I would not have taken the time to sit and ask you to wake up. People like you and me don't have the luxury of wasting time but I digress...I'm here for one reason and only His Excellence is aware of what I am about to tell you. I think you would be quite interested.' He said, looking at the young woman pull from her bed with a large handgun in her grasp. She was beautiful with her messy hair; he could see also why some within the different Vatican's networks would rather avoid looking at her. Men who shared her and his background would prefer to stay away from desire and the sin of lust. 'I don't think there is a need for totting a gun to my face.' 'I'll be the judge of that' Said Katerina after seeing the man pointing at her gun. She looked at him nod before she reached to turn on the light with her left hand. The light made her narrow her eyes for a few seconds but saw he had made no movements just yet. The man who was now smiling to her, sported a dark suit and Katerina's eyes glanced on his left breast side pocket at a strange designed cross sewn onto it. She stared up again at the man who must have been in his early twenties while he stayed there, elbow on the table with the side of his head pausing against the palm of his hand. The end of the barrel was brought up toward the ceiling, much to the appreciative relief of her interlocutor. Katerina studied the young man for a moment, his pale face, black hair and blue eyes glaring at her made a beautiful combination. He wasn't really tall either but was well proportioned. The suit was classic, maybe from Italy but it was nonetheless the cross design on his vest that picked her attention again. She remembered there were many cells within The Vatican; some even had gone beyond the control of His Excellency, while others kept on disbanding to be regrouped at a later point. Having a good knowledge of most of these networks, she wondered if he was of the latter group since the design was unknown to her. The young intruder smiled, her stare obviously had stayed on a bit longer than she had expected, and he tapped on the design on his vest. 'I see you are looking at the crest of my Order, Katerina and you are probably trying to guess as to which network it belongs, right?' He said in a gentle and quiet voice. His blue eyes flashed for a moment before he continued on. 'I am of a special Order, one you have no idea about and of course, you can be one to doubt and be suspicious. It wou…' 'Look, just answer the question I asked you okay? Seems to me people are having difficulties answering my questions since the past couple of days.' The woman answered, before extending her arm again and pointing the gun toward the man once again. He didn't bulge and went on smiling to her. He didn't appear to be scared of the weapon but she kept her aim before pulling from the cover of her bed. 'Your question?' He asked while quickly looking at the young woman in her shorts and tank top. She was beyond pretty, her shape and curves were drawn carefully and standing now in front of him, he considered her one of most beautiful women he had laid his eyes on. This Russian girl's body would damn a saint to Hell just for a single glimpse of her exquisite presence. 'I guess you were one of those who don't listen but I will not play the patient one with you.' After Katerina signaled him to move away from the chair, the man stood up and stepped backward before casually leaning against the wall. She sat down in the freed chair with a smirk, crossing her shapely legs as she went on. 'What are you doing here and please, don't think I am that blonde to think you can dupe me.' 'Oh but I did listen and I do not think there's a need to lie here. We are more of the same kind you and I. One day maybe, you will be able to see that much but the reason behind my intrusion here is that I know who you are looking for.' He said with a slight smile. 'You do huh?' She wondered if he had any knowledge of who she was hunting down. After all, she wasn't too sure herself other than the few facts given to her by her own network. 'Go on…' 'I know you are looking for a Japanese assassin if you could call her that but what I would like to find out is if you know who you are running after? I mean really know what you are up against.' The young man said to her after crossing his arms. 'What I'm up against? Nice vote of confidence but I could care less what you or anyone else think my chances are.' Katerina narrowed her eyes, frowning a bit in the process, while the young man in front of her chuckled away. She stood up, walked to close the distance between the intruder and herself to put the end of the barrel right in the middle of his forehead. 'Are you going to babble on or will you spill the beans?' The dark suited man nodded. Katerina stepped back, pulling the cold barrel of her gun off his forehead. 'I see you don't like wasting time at all,don't you? Nothing wrong really, I do like that.' 'For Heaven' sake, I don't see us going anywhere! You keep on talking like an American! Talk, talk and mean nothing! Fine, here is your last chance.' Katerina pushes the chair on the floor, making an awful squeaking noise before reaching her jacket. There, she removed her silencer from her side pocket before screwing it at the end of the barrel. Looking again toward the young man, seeing his smile had not faded away, she added. 'So here is my game smiley boy, I ask the questions and you answer. I have a doubt and I make you have enough holes to make my pasta strainer jealous. Fair?' The man shrugged before putting his hands in his vest pockets. 'Sure.' 'First off, please remove that smile off your face! Looks like I'm watching Joker without the facepaint.' He didn't really but someone smiling to her for so long either had mental issues or was thinking of something a bit too insulting for her to wander into too long. The young man chuckled before taking on a more serious face. It made her smile to see his lips tremble a bit. 'So we start…' 'How do you know I was here?' She asked him, her hand slowly bringing her gun to pause on top of her lap. No one other than her own network and The Black Hammer knew where she resided. Tough question maybe but she thought she had to know how secret her place had been. 'Father Piccello had mentioned your whereabouts and I dug into my own research. You can check with him whenever you need to.' He said moving his hands in his pockets. Katerina knew her spiritual mentor well enough to know he would never divulge her 'whereabouts' with anyone he could not trust. She nodded to the young man. 'Fine, now, how did you know about this Japanese Assassin?' She remembered Chorinsky's words about the number of female assassins out there in the Orient. 'I do not know per se who she is. I mean her name or her origin per se but I do know of her abilities. It's the reason why I asked you if you knew what you were going against.' He said, slightly nodding to himself and Katerina saw a bit of sadness in his blue eyes. 'She killed a few of our owns, Katerina, and I'd like to think that we are quite good fighters. We, like you, HUNT those who should cleansed by the Light.' 'She killed a few of your Order members and you have no idea what her name is? Very nicely done of your group! We would have done differently.' She pondered at what she heard for a moment, assuming that he was truly a member of a secret network, and then added. 'Why didn't you request reinforcements from the Vatican then?' 'Our goals are done and achieved in a discreet manner. There's a war out there you might not believe, but you of all people, you have seen a few odd things right?' He said, crouching and leaning a bit closer to her. 'Father Piccello mentioned to me what you have seen during your incursion in Hong-Kong's underworld.' 'What I have seen was surely nothing else but my tired mind pulling tricks on me. Nothing more.' She said a bit louder. This man had gotten a lot of information about herself from her mentor and it made her wonder what else he knew of her. 'You are part of an Order; you have faith in God and his doings and so you must see there are other things out there that go beyond our understandings.' He shook his head a bit, his eyes almost pleading with her for somewhat reason. 'If you are referring to a boogeyman or ghosts, I have faith in God as well as a few other things…But I can tell you I did cleanse a few subjects that were out there. Crazy and dangerous individuals…Those I have looked around for until I find them and see their blood spilled. They did not disappear nor did they change into anything else but what they are…Men and women gone into complete and unredeemable frenzy. The blood, their blood was red as yours or mine!' She responded, smelling his scent and remembering it was this that came to her senses when the fresh air came into her studio. 'I am asking you for one thing, as a soldier of God, your mind must be open to whatever possible or impossible is out there.' He spoke on before pulling the other chair to take a seat. 'Your faith Katerina is going to be tested to its limit and so I ask you again if you do intend to finish this task of yours?' 'Look, I have spent two years here instead of being somewhere else. I am not a girl who neither wastes time nor energy in something I don't need to see finished and dealt with.' She answered, turning on for some reason to English. 'Now, what do you know, what else do you know?' 'Are you ready to see Hell?' He said abruptly, his eyes flashing in a lighter blue hue, bringing her to back away a moment. 'Because if you do want to catch her, you will see more than most of your Order had ever seen.' 'Just tell me the facts! This assassin ordeal had been going on for too long.' She said, remembering to bring her gun up again but instead held it down between her legs with both her hands. 'What do you know about her? Her whereabouts?' 'She is in Japan right now but also flies regularly to the West Coast of the United States. What I did gather about her is that she is precise, extremely agile and quick. I know you are as well but I can tell you right now…You have never seen someone move as she does. She operates with a few other female assassins but I have no clue who these girls are.' He told Katerina with a colder tone of voice. She could feel he was not holding back anything and continued to listen. 'They are not affiliated to the Japanese or Chinese Underworld. They are organized and mobile. They are also very well armed and capable of wreaking havoc even here in Moscow.' The last words made her laugh, interrupting his flow of information, before she would speak on. 'You have no idea what 'Chorniy Molotok', The Black Hammer can do. Well armed you said? My family can trade the odd missile if the clientele would be available! So that part does not impress me whatsoever. They are killers and in a way so are we. What intrigues me is why you know all of this and done nothing to avenge the deaths of your comrades.' 'Your confidence is touching really.' He said before rising off the chair slowly. 'Look, I've come here to warn you against what you are tracking. Revenge means nothing to us. If you want my help, I will be around.' The young man turned around and walked toward the door of the studio. Katerina coughed a bit to bring his attention to the gun pointed in his direction. 'Did I say we were finished?' She looked at him turn his head toward the gun and glanced up to her with a smile. He shook his head for a moment before turning around completely; his hands were out of his pockets. 'Let me make you understand what I meant by this Japanese assassin being maybe more than you will bargain for.' He said while staring at her in a much more aggressive way. She looked at his whole slender body tremor before the lamp light dimmed away by itself. Katerina raised her eyes toward his blue eyes shining on in the now darkened room but smirked at him instead of possibly cowering away. The beautiful blond Russian slightly leaned her upper body forward and aimed rapidly toward the strange intruder. Katerina squeezed the trigger once,twice, thrice only to see the young man blur and avoid the bullets. He closed the distance before removing the gun from her grasp in a smooth single motion. She looked at him smile before he went to throw the gun on the bed. She smacked him on the side of the cheek before trying to deliver an uppercut in vain. This fellow was faster than anyone she had ever seen. He countered the blow to then sweep her off her feet and both fell to the left, on the nearby bed. The young raven haired man held both her wrists on the sides of her body while she resisted and tried to fight him off her. 'Shhhhh...listen!!! Listen to me!!!!' He said in a whisper to her at first, his face only a few inches away from her own. His breath smelled of a light mixtures of aromas: Tobacco, something else as well; sweet and fruity. 'I mean you no harm but you got to believe and bring back that faith you once had Katerina. Our lives will depend on it.' 'Our lives?' 'I told you…I will be around just to play the role of the cavalry for you.' He gently touched her face, with a cold hand, before blocking the strike from her right hand. The Russian girl snarled away but shivered under the lack of warmth. She stared at him rapidly move off her bed and toward the door. She did not lean over to pull the other gun that was just beneath the mattress as she listened to his last words. 'I've lost enough people in the past year. Let me watch over you.' 'Wait!' He stopped and turned his head toward her once more. The both of them nodded to each other, she didn't find her words before he opened the door of her studio and vanished away. Katerina sat there for a moment, not understanding what had just occurred before adding softly. Her hand rose to the cheek he had touched and thought outloud. 'What was his name?'
