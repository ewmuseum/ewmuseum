Authority

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 7, 2006 INT. DIMITRI’S APARTMENT - DAY ESTABLISHING SHOT Of DIMITRI’S humble abode. B.O.T. and Dimitri’s son EMILIO stand around a shabby round kitchen table. Very unremarkable countertops and cupboard doors spot the back of the room. ANGLE ON B.O.T., who is reading a standard sized piece of paper, with a smile curved around his half-smoked cohiba. NARRARATOR (V.O.) Welcome to the humble abode of Dimtri Clemans, where our hero, B.O.T. reads over his nephew Emilio’s book report. B.O.T. Look, I don’t see the problem. DIMITRI You wouldn’t. Its all about you! B.O.T. This is a perfectly good book report. WIDE ANGLE Fitting all of them in the shot. DIMITRI No its not. B.O.T. And why not? DIMITRI Because there are elements of novel storytelling that you don’t have the ability to get a grasp on. B.O.T. That's bullshit. I can “capture the elements of novel storytelling” as good as anyone. DIMITRI Right. And I can turn white and record a country record B.O.T. Hey man, if that gets you off… Dimitri snatches the paper from B.O.T., and jams it back at Emilio DIMITRI Try again. Emil slinks away, defeated. B.O.T. takes a long drag of his cigar. Dimitri lets out a big sigh. DIMITRI That kid worries me sometimes… B.O.T. I think id write the best book ever written… DIMITRI Enough already. You can’t write a book! B.O.T. Says who? DIMITRI Me! Your life isn’t interesting enough to be a book. B.O.T. What? Son, I’ve seen, touched, and tasted things you’ve never even heard of! Most of them by accident! DIMITRI (Unsure how to take that) That’s weird man… NARRORATOR (V/O) The truth was, B.O.T.’s life was full of interesting events. FULL SCREEN PHOTO Of the super sexy TIA MORRELLO, her long brunette hair is frazzled and barely covering the obsessively angry expression on her face. NARRORATOR (V/O) After a miscarriage, B.O.T.’s ex-fiancé went ape shit, and try to kill him. He had her institutionalized in upstate New York, were he had hoped that shed sit until she’d come to her senses. She’d later escape from the asylum and attempt to kill B.O.T. in his sleep. Ironically, she was hit by Dimitri’s car on the way, and would later die of internal bleeding. FULL SCREEN PHOTO Of a Spanish Galleon floating on the Delaware. On the side of the hull 'S.S. Bottitude' is painted in bold black letters. NARRORATOR (V/O) After winning his wrestling Alma mater, The ICW’s historic Internet Championship, B.O.T. decided he would celebrate by buying a Spanish Galleon and sailing it up and down the east coast. It would be confiscated after a situation involving B.O.T. harassing private boats outside of Virginia Beach, dressed as a pirate and asking for the 'Booty.' And that’s only scraping the iceberg. INT. DIMITRI’S APARTMENT - DAY B.O.T. Plenty of fucked up things have happened to me. And I’m famous! That’s like a formula for greatness. DIMITRI A: Stop swearing so loud with my kid next door. B: If anyone cared about your life, they would have come to you asking for you to tell them. NARRARATOR (V/O) From that moment forward, B.O.T. decided he would write a book. Though he found himself caught up almost from the get go. EXT. BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING PARKING LOT - DAY B.O.T. leans on the tall, weather worn brick walls of his school, next to IVAN KANNON, the Australian High flier. They’re both wrapped up in pea coats and gloves as a biting wind blows through the leaf-lain streets of Trenton, NJ. B.O.T. puffs on a cigar, deep in thought. IVAN (skeptically) So, you got a name for this book? B.O.T. I was thinking B.O.T.’s Guide To Awesomeness: The Trials and Tribulations of a Professional Sex Symbol IVAN Were still working on that title thing, right? B.O.T. Yea… IVAN That’s fine, bloke. They used to say back in me grammar classes that the best scribes make up their titles after they write their books. B.O.T. You went to school? IVAN For a couple years, yea. B.O.T. And you learned things? IVAN Don’t come with that bollock, or I'll slap your arse to the Land of the Long Cloud B.O.T. (psudo-sexually) Mmm...erotic... IVAN Pssh...faggot. NARRARATOR (V.O.) Despite his talk with Ivan, B.O.T. decided to wait on the title, instead putting his creative voodoo towards more important things: Teaching. He usually left the actual teaching to his trainers, but felt the urge to speak since his new calling. INT. THE BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING B.O.T., wearing a three-piece, minus the blazer, and standing in the center of one of the many squared circles that spot the floor of the main room of the building. Around the ring, like trees around an oasis, stand the numerous students enrolled in the BSW. Some watching intently, some not so much. Across the floor, in an isolated corner lit only by the bright minds who occupied it. Dimitri and AI KAWASHIMI watch from afar. Dimtri looks uninterested and skeptical, as if poised like a cat to have to break up the clamor because it would turn into another B.O.T. “lets-see-how-many-people-we-can-insult” contest. His attire suggests that he just walked in from outside. Kawashimi, also dressed for the winter, watches in wait, not too sure what’s going on in that brain of his. NARRARATOR (V.O.) While our hero stood in the ring preaching his gospel, Dimitri and the Lady of the Rising Sun, Ai Kawashimi, stand away from his preaching, to discuss his situation. DIMITRI Glad he’s actually working, today. AI Give him a break. He’s gone through a little bit these last couple months. DIMITRI Oh, I’m not knocking him. I’m glad he’s doing something for a change, ‘stead of just using the equipment for his own selfish ass. AI I’m glad he bounced back well from all that fucked up Society business. DIMITRI Would say “bounced back” per say. He’s been adamant about writing a book, lately. AI Really? DIMITRI Yeah. I called his psychiatrist. She said its probably just some relapse. He feels the need to prove his worth now that everything he used to know was shot to hell. AI Interesting. What kind of book? DIMITRI Autobiography. B.O.T.’s Guide To Awesomeness: The Trials and Tribulations of a Professional Sex Symbol. AI We’re still working on the title, yes? DIMITRI God, I hope so. NARRARATOR (V.O.) In the ring, B.O.T. delivers a lecture of sorts. IN THE RING B.O.T. is pacing around the ring, speaking slowly and profoundly. B.O.T. I don’t know why you guys do this. I don’t know why you guys continue to wake up early on your off-days, or come in late after work, to learn how to bleed, sweat and cry, or make others do the same. And I don’t want to know. Personally, as long as checks keep coming to my office from you, you could come here and get beat up for the rest of your lives. I could care less. He pauses and spins, to get a good look at the people behind him. B.O.T. I know why I do it though. There hasn’t been one memory in my entire career more powerful then my first dropkick... The crowd chuckles a bit. B.O.T. I’m serious. My first dropkick is the best memory I’ve had. You see, I never used to dropkick anyone. Hell, I'm six-foot-eight, three-hundred pounds, I don’t need to dropkick a goddamn thing, or leave the ground for that matter. I was a bit apprehensive to it. It was so unnatural for me to think vertically. My opponent was someone I had never faced before. His style was something to be marveled. Mine was still that of a predictable curtain jerker, who probably had no business being in the ring. Everything I threw at that son of a bitch, he saw right through and countered accordingly. I was being made a fool of because of my lack of preparation. Least that’s what I assumed at the time. I realized that sometimes you have to forget what your taught in order to do what you have to do. Sometimes you gotta fall away from your comfort zone, pull out all the stops, in order to get what you want. B.O.T. points to the corner where Dimtri stands. B.O.T. My opponent’s name was Trigger, and he’s standing in that corner right there. The heads turn in a large wave and rustle of whispers. A surprised and embarrassed look washes on his face. B.O.T. I didn’t beat him that night, but that old bastard tasted my boots. The feeling I had when I jumped, kicked, and flipped, like I used to see him do. I arched perfectly, landed immaculately, it was the greatest moment in the ring of my life. Now, thirteen years later, I've been training. Waking up on my off-days, coming in late after work, learning three martial arts, hundreds of different holds and ways to make people bleed, sweat, and cry, just so that I can find that “perfect dropkick” again. I refuse to retire until I do. He points to the office, the eyes follow it. B.O.T. I’ve got an office similar to that in my house, and in it hangs all my championships and awards, but the most pride I've ever felt was with an unremarkable move in a match that I lost. Just think about that as you work it out today. Carry on. B.O.T. briskly leaves the ring, his face showing a blank while strolling into the office. The crowd scatters back to their previous preoccupations. THE CORNER OF THE ROOM Dimitri shares a small chuckle with Ai as they watch B.O.T. enter the office. DIMITRI Look at him, actually had something meaningful to say. Ai looks on, hiding worry under a wary smile. NARRARATOR (V.O.) Most women had no choice but to be at least the slightest bit interested in Jarrett Clemans. His bravado can put off, as much as it could attract. For a lot of women, including Ai Kawashimi, it was a mixture a both. IN THE OFFICE SHOT FAVORING B.O.T. B.O.T. sits, feet propped up on the smooth steel of his Wal-Mart grade desk. In his hand, a picture frame, his eyes scan it like a book. OVER THE SHOULDER Of Ai walking into the tempest, taking a seat next to his feet on the desk NARRARATOR (V.O.) Ai and B.O.T. are good friends. Their relationship rivaled only by the bond of brothers. He loved her like a little sister, keeping her out of trouble and supporting her in all her exploits. How she loved him, though, was her biggest conflict. AI (curiously) Nice little speech. Where’d that come from? B.O.T. Did a little thinkin... AI About? B.O.T. What makes me happy. AI And what’s that? B.O.T. Haven’t thought it out completely yet. AI How far DID you get? B.O.T. Far enough to know that life is stupid. AI What, you’d rather die then live the life you live? B.O.T. No, that’s not what I ment. I love living, I just hate that life makes living so ridiculous. A confused look from slaps B.O.T. across his face. B.O.T. Just think about it. Once we find out we can do something well, we spend the rest of our lives trying to repeat it. Life is just a long interstate of filler bullshit connecting the many rest stops of living. We struggle, we toil, for what? Just so we can have that one moment of clarity. That one time where we can say we’ve done something that makes us feel pride in ourselves. The we struggle and toil somemore to try and recreate that. AI Where’d this come from all of the sudden? B.O.T. I’ve been on the interstate for a long time now. Every time I get close to a rest-stop, I get off on the wrong exit, and get lost again. I take whatever turns I can to try to make the drive enjoyable, but nothing has sated me yet. AI Is this book just another wrong turn? They share a deep stare. B.O.T. ...probably not. This will be written. I was deadly serious about that. I just want to have more good stories in it than bad. AI Does this unhappiness have anything to do with The Club? B.O.T. Can’t really say. Yea, I'm a little upset that I'm on a slide. Even the alpha wolf has its day. AI Well, you’ve been distracted these past couple of weeks, you need to get your focus back. You’ll never find the destination if you don’t have a map. B.O.T. Ok, I'm a little tired of the travel analogies. AI You started it, ass. B.O.T. I guess man. Ai stands up, pushing his feet off the desk with her. AI (playfully) We’ll, get your shit together, and kick someone’s ass for God’s sake. I’m tired of calling you a loser. B.O.T. You know what, get the hell outta here. She trots off, pleasantly. B.O.T. tosses the frame onto the desk. NARRARATOR (V.O.) He couldn’t help but feel like he was cheating the planet a little bit. Aside from his aforementioned duress, he couldn’t get past the idea that he was letting a lot of people down. B.O.T. represents a lot of people, not just himself, whether he wanted it to or not. For every championship he’s won, every award he’s been given, he’s got the names and reps of them men he beat for them, the men who beat him for it, and the companies that they work for’s collective reputations on the line as well. Though usually not one to care, B.O.T. couldn’t help it. If its one person B.O.T. respects, its a fighter. B.O.T. stands up, and grabs his long wool peacoat and Italian wool blazer from a stubby hook protruding out of the concrete blocks on the wall behind him. NARRARATOR That’s when he realized that it was way past time for some gin...
