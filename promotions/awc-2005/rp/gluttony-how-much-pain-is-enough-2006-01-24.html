Gluttony - 'How much pain is enough?'

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/06 The gym near the hotel was packed with sweaty bodies and loud with the clinking of weights and the grunts of men and women pushing themselves to their absolute limit. In one corner of the gym, Butterfly Hamada was doing just that. Except she was pushing it beyond her limits. 'Do you want to blow your pecs out? How about pop one of your breasts? How many pounds are you benching?' asked Sage as she walked over to Hamada's bench. 'Twenty-nine.... THIRTY!' Butterfly sat up from the bench, sweat dripping down her face. She looked up at Sage and nodded but she was too tired to do much else. 'You're usually all smiles, girlfriend, in your own demure way.' Sage cocked her head to the side, throwing a towel over her shoulder. 'What's wrong? You look like someone served you some bad sushi.' 'Hardly.' Megumi parted the wet locks that had fallen in front of her eyes and stared straight ahead. 'You remember me, right? Sage?' 'Yes, hello.' Sage sat down beside Megumi and put her hands on her knees, mimicking Megumi's stance and demeanor. 'I haven't told you this before, heck I don't really know you. No one has really said anything as far as I know... but thanks.' 'Thanks for what?' Megumi looked at Sage from the corner of her eyes. What was this all about, she thought? 'For sticking up to those assholes in AWC. Murphy, the Martins, Chainz, all of those guys are jerks.' Sage gave Hamada a crooked little smile, but it was sincere. ' They think women can't handle themselves in a wrestling ring but they are dead wrong. And you could have up and quit after losing to Murphy in Europe, or when you took that BRUTAL beating from Chainz, wow! I mean, he beat you to a pulp.' 'Thanks for reminding me.' 'Hey, Alexa Kendericks quit, right? But you didn't. You beat Murphy once, and hell if he would even acknowledge it. I bet, you could beat every guy in this promotion and they still wouldn't admit they were wrong. Because they're idiots. Women are the smarter and wiser of the species. And we are more then ready to admit that we are wrong. Those knuckleheads wouldn't admit to a damn thing if it meant messing with their pride, OR their heat.' 'I lost to Liam Martin last week. Maybe they are right.' Megumi looked at Sage for a moment before wiping hr face with a towel. 'Hell no, they aren't right.' 'Believe me,' said Hamada confidently. 'I KNOW they aren't right. I have nothing to prove to them. I don't care what they think. I couldn't change their minds if I beat them a million times. But I care what I think and I have to prove it to myself. Prove that I am not just a little girl, a Joshi wrestler from Japan that got in too deep. Do you understand? I do not just want to be known in the inner circles as a shoo-... erm, as a woman wrestler. I want to prove to myself that I'm a champion.' 'Yeah, well. I just want to thank you for sticking up for the women wrestlers in AWC and for women, period.' Sage smiled and got up to walk away. 'But as far as being a champion, be careful what you wish for.' Megumi was ready to chime in with a scalding opinion, ready to let Sage know exactly who she was and what she was capable of, but her thoughts and words were drowned out by a racket that sounded like a horde of cattle had stampeded into the place. A group of men had stormed into the gym and were harassing the front desk help. All of them were quite large as they stomped around, pushing people out of the way and obviously looking for someone. That's when Megumi saw the man in the wheelchair roll into view. Wearing a neck brace and pushing a control pad with his one arm, he looked sinister, like a James Bond villain. Fat to the point of obese, he barely fit in his steel rimmed wheelchair. His shirt more or less covered his fat belly as he sat there bellying out orders. Something about him made Megumi tend to smile a covert little grin. Maybe it was his Buddha-like waist. Or maybe his bald pate framed with whispy dark curls and his big bushy brown beard. Or maybe it was the neck brace and wheelchair that she had personally seen fit to putting him in. The fat wheelchair bound man slammed into a staff member, kicking their shin with his feet. One of his cronies grabbed the person by the collar and lifted them up in the air. 'Maybe you didn't 'ear me, mate. I asked you if Megumi Hamada was 'ere today. If you don't wanna answer me, my good man, well then, my friends here--' He jerked his head at the men scouring the gym. 'My boys here will take to questioning your patrons. Not good for business, considering all you have here are soccer moms and American panty-waist pillow-biters!' Sage turned to Megumi with a clear sense of concern on her face. 'You know him?' Megumi never took her eyes off of him. And she didn't answer Sage's question. She was thinking hard and fast of a way to get out of the building without being seen by these new arrivals. Sage turned to her directly, demanding an answer. 'I said, do you know him? He knows you, that's for sure.' 'Oh yes,' she said wryly, still watching the drama unfold near the front desk. 'That is Ron Von Dumpling, a third-rate wrestler from England who I met a few weeks ago. We had a disagreement.' 'Looks like he lost. And not a gracious loser, either,' Sage concluded. 'Hardly. Neither is he much of a challenge, but I am in no mood for this. I have... enough to think about.' So much for my plan to slip away from my mysterious shadow, thought Megumi. With Ron Von Dumpling causing such a stir, she might as ell stay right where she was and hang a sign: 'Megumi Hamada: CYDONIA Hunter Inquire Within.' On the other hand, she mused, all that racket could cause a diversion. Quickly, Megumi scanned the gym for any clues as to escape undetected, before Von Dumpling's men cleared a path to the back of the gym. All the way in the front of the building by the juice bar, she heard a squeal of laughter. Several very attractive gym rats were standing around the juice bar drinking their protein shakes and giggling like little school girls. One of their number was a young man, who was openly flirting with the women. They seemed to be intrigued, probably less from his intelligence, thought Megumi, and more because the man wore a full-fledged wrestling mask. A roar of laughter filled the air as a girl blushed and the masked man put his arm around her in a joking manner. Megumi spotted something at his back. 'Sage! Please, up in the front, that man. You see him?' 'Sage turned to where she was hinting. 'You mean HIM? The Green Grappler?' 'Yes, that masked man. See his bag on the juice bar? There is a green mask hanging out of it.' Megumi bit her lip and looked at Sage. 'Ha ha, good thinking! OK, no problem, I'll be right back.' In two minutes Sage had gone to the front of the gym, ordered a drink and when no one was looking she swiped the mask from the Green Grappler's training bag. 'You owe me for this wheat germ and tofu shake. Eww.' Sage took a sip and made a face. Then her eyes widened. 'You better hurry up, girl. Sounds like Ironside is coming this way.' Not understanding the reference, Megumi quickly put on the green mask and headed straight for the juice bar and The Green Grappler. One of the women hanging on Grappler's arm stopped flirting for a second to throw Megumi a speculative glance. She then threw an elbow into the man's ribs and walked away. The other women laughed. The masked wrestler looked over his shoulder and gawked at the girl wearing one of his masks, and then smiled. 'Well, good golly, Miss Molly. She's a kinky one, isn't she? Well met, my dear. I must be living right.' He marveled at her, extending his hand and taking hers in his. Megumi doubted it, but she answered him with a soft smile. The fool could take that as he wished. He bowed to her, which upset some of the other girls who left. 'Would you care to join me in a spirulina and avocado energy shake? My name is The Green Grappler.' The Grappler smiled at her, his eyebrow raised in a devilish manner. 'These shakes might sound a little stomach turning, but they ARE green, and they are very, very tasty indeed. And by the way, where did you per chance happen to find that mas--' “Excuse me, mate! But have you happened to have seen this woman around?” Ron Von Dumpling had rolled up in his wheelchair with two of his henchmen in tow, and thrust a picture of Megumi in The Green Grappler’s face. The masked man took it and gave it a quick glance. “No, can’t say that I have.” “Are you sure, lad?” The Green Grappler draped an arm around Megumi’s shoulders, smiling down at Von Dumpling as if they were both old friends. “Frankly, no. If you were in my shoes,” he drawled, squeezing the woman beside him, “why would you have eyes for another?” Ron Von Dumpling looked at the both of them in their ridiculous green masks and sneered. “Suit yerself there, mate.” Megumi relaxed. Now to get out of the gym and away. She would definitely have to take this Gren person with her; the convinced look on Von Dumpling’s face indicated she would not be bothered again as long as she was in his presence. Resisting the urge to peel the man’s arm from her waist and the mask from her face, she glanced up at her future hostage. The Grappler was leaning back on the juice bar counter, eyes narrowed and fixed intently on something. Megumi followed the line of his gaze. From his angle, he could see her hands, taut by her side, fists clinched. He appeared to be noting her tense hands and her whitened knuckles, and there was a speculative expression on his face. She glanced sharply at him. What had he guessed. Well, only the obvious, she figured. He looked up and met her eyes, and her suspicions faded for a moment. The man’s face was bland as oatmeal, from what she could tell behind that ridiculous mask. He flashed a charming smile that she was beginning to find very irritating. “You’ve worked up quite a sweat there young Green apprentice. Are we still on for that delicious shake?” “Why do not we work up a different type of sweat, hmm? I am a big fan of yours. I love the masks.” She purred at him like a kitten. Hopefully this numbskull wouldn’t realize what had just happened after all. “Well, looks like I’m set for the evening.” He waved at the crowd of people in the gym. “If I don’t return for a while people, don’t come looking for me. Ha ha ha! Weeeeeeeee!” Megumi encouraged the masked man’s cheerful talk with appropriate inane noises, subtly guiding their path out of the gym and toward her hotel. She could easily lose him, the mask, and any pursuers in the bustle of the hotel lobby. As they walked, a damp wind began to hint at rain. “Lexington, Kentucky, eh?” His ramblings continued. “What a strange beginning for my wrestling career. I assume you are here to watch me wrestle in AWC? Sadly I may just have a dark match on the show, but I can get you backstage, don’t you know.” As the Grappler talked, Megumi listened carefully to the sounds around her. Voices drifted from down the street, cars passed by, footsteps on the pavement. They were heading down the street where an alleyway would dissect their street. She noticed a very long shadow coming from around that corner, the sun to their right and still fighting through the gathering clouds. Right before they got to the corner, a man and woman who had been sharing a cigarette walked out and headed to their office. Never was there a suspicious sound but Megumi slowly became convinced that someone was following her. Damn! she thought vehemently. Why could she not shake this feeling, or find this shadow that had come to attach itself to her? If she could draw it out however, a fight would probably attract Von Dumpling and scores of police. As they were about to pass the alleyway she heard the sound of a can roll across asphalt. Keeping her face expressionless she slipped a hand on The Green Grappler’s shoulder, right below his gym bag sash. With a quick spin she broke free of his grasp and began to spin the gym bag over her head like a bolo. A shadow dashed from behind a trash heap and Megumi let the bag fly. With a bright green explosion of powder, the bag smashed into the head of a man who was trying to get away. He fell to the ground, choking on green dust. “My spirulina powder!” yelped the masked wrestler. With a Pele kick to the face Megumi brought the man back down as he attempted to get to his feet. “Why are you following me?” she demanded. The man tried to wipe away the green powder that covered his face. “I saw you in the gym. I saw you put on the mask… I thought I’d… you know.” “No, I don’t know.” “Well, it is obvious he wanted to mug you or something God awful like that.” Grapps shook his head but then put his hands on his hips in a heroic fashion. “Didn’t know she was with The GREEN GRAPPLER, did you?” “Quiet!” Megumi scolded. “Who are you working for?” “Ron Von Dumpling. The guy in the wheelchair.” “No one else?” “No!” In spite of guilty eyes, Megumi believed him. This was no hooker. This was not a man sent to break bones or to settle debts. She was about to let him go when she noticed a flash of silver from the man’s breast pocket. She grabbed it and drew out a silver cigarette case with a large insignia on the cover. It was familiar to her. She popped open the cigarette case. Seven Stars Revo cigarettes, from Japan. Megumi caught her breath. “Where did you get this?” she rasped, thrusting the case close to the man’s face. The initials on the cover were unmistakable, as were the brand. This case belonged to London Hawaii, one of the first victims of the CYDONIA Hunter. The man’s eyes filled with fear. They flickered back and forth as if seeking a means to escape. “New York,” he croaked. “I got it in New York.” “I KNOW that. Tell me more.” “From a wrestler, as a gift. In New York. That’s all I know, I swear.” “Does this wrestler have a name?” Beads of sweat broke out on the man’s face. “No, please! If I tell you he’s gonna kill me!” “If you don’t, I’ll KILL you.” “Life is just full of difficult decisions,” Grappler noted behind her. The unexpected sound startled Megumi. “You are still here?” She threw a glance over her shoulder. The masked fool was leaning up against the alleyway wall, arms crossed. “Well naturally,” he replied. “It’s dangerous on these mean streets of Kentucky. Who knows, there could be someone lying in wait to attack you.” “I don’t need protection,” she said emphatically. “You? I was talking about me! And I wouldn’t mind my extra mask back, you know.” “Stay right there.” Megumi turned her full attention to her captive. “The person’s name who gave you that cigarette case, now!” “I can’t tell you! He said in desperation. Megumi’s balled fist steadily climbed higher and higher. “Alright! Alright.” “Well?” “His name is---“ The ruffian’s voice snapped off as is he had been throttled. Megumi slowly lowered her fist, watching in disbelief as the man’s face blackened and his tongue bulged out of his mouth. She backed away, unable to takke her eyes from the horribly distorted face. A low, rattling gurgle burst from the man, and he slid, lifeless, down the length of her legs. “Holy maracas!” exclaimed The Green Grappler. “You killed him!”
