The Shit Is Hitting the Fan

4 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/06 Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon was pissed. Most people would say, 'What else is new?' The Captain was rarely ever in good spirits, having to deal with stupid white people and stupid people of his own descent and stupid people in general. Having to deal with folks like Red Rock and his dumb luck or Darcy Crisis and his insolence or Aimz and her meddling. But this time... man, was he irate. Flipping over tables, throwing vases, kicking his debris across the room to make it more debris-ified. General Rahman was cheerleading the action, screaming out Turkish obscenities and the like, but the tirades were too much for anyone else in his entourage. Dogan being worried was a given, given his skepticism towards Suleimon's attitudes concerning race and the West, but Hayvan, who had been drinking Suleimon's Kool-Aid like a thirsty child on a summer's day, he started to get a little antsy. Seeing that Suleimon was in full tirade mode, it was hard not to be. 'Dude, settle down,' Hayvan said. 'It was only an invitation.' Suleimon wasn't hearing any of it. He seethed. Steam came out of his ears. So why in tarnation was Suleimon unusually mad? For that answer, we go to his inbox. This e-mail, in particular: From: David Harber [[email removed]] To: Tariq Suleimon [[email removed]] Subject: Zero 2 Hero Hey, are you going to enter Zero 2 Hero? It'll be a great opportunity for you to maybe leap to the top of AWC. What do you think? Oh, and this one too. From: Sasha Volkyeva [[email removed]] To: Tariq Suleimon [[email removed]] Subject: Zero The deadline for the tournament is fast approaching. I expect your application so you can rise from this mire you're in. To normal people, these are words of encouragement. To the warped, deranged mind of Captain Suleimon... well. 'THIS IS AN OUTRAGE! How dare they equate me with the other zeroes in this sorry promotion! I am no zero!' There went the vase. 'Aaaaah!' The coffee table... in splinters. 'They shall not insult me!' The water cooler. You get the picture. 'Aargh! This... this would not be possible if not for....' Wait for it... in the meantime, a hole was punched in the wall. '...Aimz!' So, Suleimon calmed down. Apparently, being disrespected over being called a zero wasn't the only problem he had with the tournament. In a sit down interview with the Holzerman blog, he detailed everything. Blog: Hello Suleimon. Suleimon: Good day. Blog: Recently, you've come out and said that you are against the Zero 2 Hero tournament. It's also well-reported about your tirade after receiving notifications from both Entertainment Co-Managers about applying to the tournament. Are your reasons for being against the tournament wholly rooted in your feelings of disrespect? Suleimon: No. While I do feel being lumped in with the rest of the zeroes is a sign of gross disrespect for the standard bearer of the Ottoman Empire, that is not the reason I feel this tournament is an abomination before Allah and the sport of wrestling. Blog: Then why are you against it? Suleimon: Quite simply, it is yet another sign of the laziness and flashiness that the Americans have implemented in this fair business, rotting it from it's foundation up. No one wants to work their way to the top anymore. Everyone is looking for the quick fix, and this tournament is the epitomé of that despicable attitude. Blog: But the tournament helped kickstart the company you're in right now one year ago. Suleimon: Yes, and those circumstances were different in that the company was looking for an identity and homegrown stars rather than retreads and opportunistic free agents looking to be a big timer in a new fed after they faltered in PRIME or NFW. That was a pure, organic AWC. Now, it has fallen into the same trap every other fed has. Blog: So what you're saying is you'd rather get your Transatlantic Championship shot the old-fashioned way? Suleimon: Not only that, but, I shall destroy whomever it is who reaps the fruits of this tournament. Blog: The resumés of folks in this tournament are similar to yours or in the case of someone like Garbage Bag Johnny, better than yours. It's not like you'd be lumped in with losers. Suleimon: Ha, I scoff at that notion. For one, Garbage Bag Johnny... anyone who aspires to be a trashman doesn't deserve to be in this business. They deserve to be rotting in a Hoboken landfill. Secondly, you do not understand that if not for the meddling of rapscallions like D'Avid or Aimz, I would hold major pay-per-view wins over media darlings like Darcy Crisis and the current Relentless Champion Red Rock. I am a bona fide impact player in this business, and after this tournament nonsense is over, I shall prove that not only the winner of this sham is no match for me and the Ottoman Empire, neither will the Transatlantic Champion. This interview is over. And so when Suleimon was handed the booking sheet for the AWC house show, his face lit up like a Christmas Tree. Suleimon vs. Aimz. The opportunities were endless. A win over Aimz would be a major statement to everyone who would think Suleimon was a zero. He had the chance to punish Aimz for costing him the Trivial Punishment match (and perhaps giving him an STD in the process). And most importantly... the advancement of the Ottoman ideals... that women should stay out of the ring. A sick smile formed on his face. The devil himself camped out in his eyes. Dogan still looked worried... but for a different reason. Hayvan and Rahman breathed sighs of relief. And Suleimon... well, the War against the West was about to take a violent turn. And the Red Raver was going to be latest and most gruesome of victims.
