Trials and the Consequence - The Bait

23 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/10/06 ‘Open your eyes.’ Darkness was slowly lifted from my eyes as I felt the burning sensation of artificial light being pushed my way. My entire body was sitting limply on what felt like a chair, cold and unforgiving. I tried to move but I like a piece of granite, stiff and unmoving. ‘Open your eyes.’ The voice was very distant but at the same time I knew it could be too far away from me in the room - if I was in a room at that. No it definitely was a room because I could hear the echoes of the voice all around me. Not only that the feel of the ground beneath me surely had to be a room - I don’t think carpet is generally used on the outside too often. As for the voice, it was completely mixed behind some sort of vocoder, hiding the gender and tone of the person calling to me. It was monotonous and harsh, lacking any sort of emotion to latch onto. All that came forth was a robotic drone, calling out to me again and again. ‘I don’t have all day Mr. Murphy. You can either open your eyes…or you’ll never be able to see again.’ It was forceful and grim and the tone of the vocoder made the statement all the more chilling. My mind was slowly coming back to me and I realised what had happened finally. The last solitary image from earlier flashed back into my brain; the blank eyes of Sylvia stared into me again. Not really having a choice between closing my eyes shut or opening them I gave in to the voice, flickering open the lids to take in a blinding light. One of these days (if this wasn’t going to be my last) I would have to think on an idea I’d had years ago. If there’s one thing about mysterious kidnappings and interrogation rooms it’s that they’re all the same. I reckon you could create a flatpack style room for a government, military or global corporation for this specific purpose. You could make quite a pretty penny for a moveable, flexible interrogation room. Could even have some sort of self cleansing agent in the structure for those who’d like to use more “brutal” methods… I have no idea why I’m thinking this, largely as I’m sitting in an interrogation room but eh, it’s things like this that can get you through a potentially deadly situation. Regardless I was sitting in the middle of a dark room bar the ubiquitous swinging lampshade in the middle which was providing the spotlight. As my eyes slowly got used to the light I could vaguely make out the rough shape of a person in the corner but one that I couldn’t make out with any certainty the features. Shapes and forms but nothing concrete from which to go upon. ‘Ah, I guess third time’s a charm Jack isn’t that right? You know I would like to hear your retort or reasoned statement but frankly right now I think I should be the one doing the talking. Actually it isn’t possible for it to be any other way.’ I went to respond, my usual defiant and stubborn head needing to break whatever attempt on controlling me. But I couldn’t and there was a simple reason for it. The damn bastards had gagged me. And chained me to the chair now that I took the time to think about it. Great, this was just great. ‘Hmmph. I would’ve expected you to cop on to that just a little bit faster Jack but then again you are only a pathetic wrestler without a worthy cause. It is really quite sad to see you striving for something that is so unimportant in the grand scheme of things.’ The shape moved closer and stood at the edge of the light, letting it only just slide over the form and illuminating the body. Nothing was revealed by this however as all I could gather from it was the general shape of a human being, nothing more. I still couldn’t tell the gender as their body was in such a way that any obvious differences were expertly hidden. It was one big bait and switch, playing with my vision to see if I could trust it at all. It was nicely executed but I still tried nonetheless to see through it. ‘So just listen Mr. Murphy. There are many things in the world that have answers and many that are filed away as unsolved mysteries. Sometimes, some things are just better left unsolved. I appreciate the noble intentions of your quest to live in peace in Kingston, but that simply is not going to happen.’ The shape folded its arms, tilting its head slightly so I could see a hint of hair…it was long but that’s all I got before they consciously brushed it behind their head. ‘The reason it is not going to happen is because the ones who truly have the power don’t wish it to happen Mr. Murphy. It’s all a game of cloak and daggers and you are the pawns. We do what we like as we see fit not only for our own amusement but for purposes far above your comprehension or understanding.’ What kind of bullshit was floating around in this person’s head? It sounded like that shape had some sort of god complex intent on doing whatever the fuck they want. What made it all the more insane was that it probably believed what it was saying. It boggled my mind…though at the same time I felt like I was in a safe position right now. There wasn’t any hint of harm coming my way, at least not for now. The bleak vocoder continued its rant, the shape now moving around the edge of the light to use the full trickery that it could. ‘Places like Kingston, the Atlantic Wrestling Club and those who inhabit it are mere playthings for us. We relish in the interesting dialogues and interactions that are taking place all over the world because of us and our initiative.’ The mentions of multiplicity made the whole affair make it all the more complicated. Talking about the company which I wrestled for did give me one thing though. If anything was certain I knew that I was near to somebody who had been meddling in AWC far longer than I had been there. Dr. Drake. His company was involved somehow but at the same time, it felt like it wasn’t his machinations. He was more direct with his interventions, more hands on. This felt very convoluted and in the air. There was a touch of the for the birds element to it. The mad scientist, not the calculating kind. This was different, something far more to it than a mere use of drugs to enhance an athlete and control the Club how they felt. It was something more. ‘But, I feel that now it is the end of our conversation for the moment. Maybe I shall be speaking to you again Mr. Murphy, maybe I shall not. I know the answer to this because truly we control the power here, but let’s play the game of freedom of will for just a little bit more alright? I hope you enjoy the trip Mr. Murphy…it’s not going to be pretty.’ White hot pain exploded in the back of my brain. *** A violent thud woke me up, but unfortunately that thud was my body colliding with the hard pavement of a street. I felt the blood trickling out onto the floor and I grumbled looking around. It looked familiar…it was familiar. To be fair, they’d dropped me home. Of course, literally dropping me home wasn’t my first preference, nor was it that I would be all but naked apart from my boxers. Oh well, I stood up and looked around. Thankfully, nobody was around to see the mess I was in and began to cross the street to my home. Something ruffled in my underwear. Disturbed at this I pulled out what was a note. In fact, it was the document of the correspondence that myself and Sylvia had found in the whore house, Pearson’s intimate writings about how Sylvia was a spy. And then it clicked. I turned up the street. This couldn’t wait any longer. Time to talk Father Pearson.
