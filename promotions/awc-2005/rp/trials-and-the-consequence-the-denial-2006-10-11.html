Trials and the Consequence - The Denial

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/10/06 ‘Sylvia?’ ‘Yes?’ ‘Doesn’t the sun look fantastic setting?’ ‘Well…yes I suppose it does why?’ ‘No reason…I just appreciate and admire beauty.’ ‘Oh. Where has this come from?’ ‘Never mind.’ I shrugged my shoulders and forgot about trying to walk down that path. It wasn’t worth it, and besides Albany was just coming up ahead. All around the Pontiac the streetlights were beginning to flicker on and douse us in artificial light, eradicating the sun that once dazzled me with its beauty. A shame, but a necessary evil of the world in which we live in. No longer do the people rest as soon as the evening comes; we are truly a twenty four hour people that live and breathe off the business, the rush and the entertainment of the 21st century. It wouldn’t be hard to point out the differing qualities and types that aspire to this tradition as we pass through Albany but I’m not in the mood for it right now. Instead I had to think on exactly what it was Sylvia was going to show me on this night in Albany. It was already well past seven o’clock and being a Sunday everything that was worthwhile would be closed. It was this kind of thought which reminded me that the only thing which would expose Father Pearson would have to be something less than reputable in stature for it to be worthy. Sighing I turned the corner of another forgotten street to wherever Sylvia was calmly telling me to go. Her entire demeanour over the car journey had changed. Gone was the certain, composed Sylvia Carter that I had known for the last month or so with our frequent meet and greets, back and forth communication through emails and the like. Now she was irritated by something, she sat straight up in a very awkward position in the car, anxious and expectant. Her hands were clenched and the composure with which she spoke was now beginning to crackle with each simple direction to where we were going. Her voice was gravelling a little and no amount of repeated coughing could get rid of it. Something was visibly shaking her from the core of her soul outwards. ‘Is something wrong Sylvia?’ ‘No why?’ ‘It’s just…you’re very tense sitting in the chair. Are you afraid of something?’ ‘Not at all, I’m just…a little excited I suppose. This isn’t just for you as you well know.’ That was true, whilst for the past month it may have seemed like the only concern of hers was bringing my own case to justice, there were other concerns of hers which had just as much importance in the proceedings. Her very career was on the line pretty much. I was only fighting for a cause; I had no fear of losing anything dear to my heart beyond my love of wrestling. Sylvia on the other hand was putting her future in the hands of some wrestler whom she probably had no love loss for. That was fine with me, I didn’t wish to have much business with her beyond this present engagement. ‘It’s right around this corner Jack. Just pull up here.’ Following her lead I eased off the pedal and slowly brought the car to a stop. We pulled in behind a couple of plain rudimentary vehicles. Well, we were going to stick out like a sore thumb the longer we sat in the car. I went to go for the door. ‘Where are you going?’ ‘Do you honestly think I’m going to sit in a car like this when it’s obvious that we’ll look suspicious sitting in it?’ ‘You can’t get out of the car. Not yet.’ ‘Why not?’ ‘You just can’t!’ It was the first time I had heard her raise her voice beyond the quivering notes that lilted earlier. It was cracked and scared when she shouted. Something was off, that was for certain. ‘Alright Sylvia - what’s up? I’m not going any further with you unless you give me some answers. Tell me what you know - now.’ She wept openly into her hands and shied away from me. This completely confused me and I was left looking at her like some St. Bernard puppy with droopy eyes. Confusion was actually an understatement at this point. ‘What’s going on?’ It wasn’t an angry affirmative question like I was used to making, it was…quizzical. ‘Look, Father Pearson said that he is a very important man in these parts and…’ ‘He said? When have you been talking to him?’ ‘What I never said I was talking to him!’ ‘You just did! Now what’s going on?’ ‘It’s nothing! Alright let’s just get out of the car and get on with what we’re here for.’ I jumped out of the car once her mind had been swayed so quickly. I stopped her at the corner as she tried to break off and compose herself. ‘Sylvia, I’m going to ask you this again: when have you been talking to Father Pearson?’ ‘Never.’ ‘Do you know Father Pearson on a more personal basis?’ ‘No.’ She stood firmly in front of me and didn’t flinch, didn’t move her eyes, didn’t blink or anything. She stated it with conviction. Still, I didn’t quite believe her, but there was nothing I could do right now to prove her otherwise. So for the moment I had to go along with her word, however shaky it seemed to me at the time. ‘Alright, what’s around the corner then Sylvia?’ ‘Why don’t you look for yourself?’ I shrugged. Guess it made sense. As soon as I peered round the corner I was blinded by hundreds and hundreds of fluorescent bulbs beaming back at me in all the colours of the rainbow. I felt like I was back on the beach in the 80’s with all the lurid, putrid colours affraying themselves against tanned vile skin. Simpler, corrupt times. It was one hell of a red district staring back at me and it wasn’t ashamed of itself. It unabashedly shone and struck everyone that walked down it with the razzmatazz of a sleazy pimp, telling the wonders of the world won’t compare to what’s between the legs of his women. Pillars of granite echoed the ancient civilisations, a reminder that no matter how long you go back, you can find places like this. It was part of life and here to stay. ‘Shall we go Sylvia?’ ‘Sure, we’re looking for the Red Gusset.’ ‘Classy.’ ‘I know.’ The pavement was nicely laid, well taken care of and not worn in the slightest. Where you walked was well lit and in keeping with the plan of the district. It was a place of disturbing tranquillity, knowing what could be taking place inside. I guess in the minds of the proprietors of the establishments this was the modern day Elysium. A place where you can go and indulge in your fantasies and pleasures and enjoy the night, where nobody will judge you no matter how perverse that choice might be. As we walked along the path several different vendors tried to entice into their shows with varying offers of free alcohol, an opportunity to see a sex show or perhaps involve ourselves in a kind of twisted improvisation. Of particular note was one man we encountered along the way though and how he acted when he saw us come by. The man was dressed as if the world had never passed the timeline of 1989. To him his curious affection for boiler suits was not lost I’m sure on his clientele and employees. It was a lovely bright silver and allowed him to be an almost literal rainbow, reflecting all the fluorescent light off of him. He knew this for sure as he danced in time with the thudding bass line of the “Throbbing Bulge” as the establishment was affectionately called. His dancing was unfortunately appropriate as he had his own bulging to be doing. Out of the corner of my eye I could see Sylvia gagging as the man gyrated his way towards us and her specifically. He slithered in between us, using his hands like a snakehead to work its way around and up and down our bodies. As I took a look at his face I reeled slightly at the smell emanating from his teeth. They were cracked and yellow, full of holes where teeth should be. Most of all he stank like he had just woken up from a bath in a bin. Then he began to take off his clothes. ‘I’ll give you ten dollars to stop and not bother us.’ ‘Ah cracka it’s going to take more than that.’ He said this whilst putting his leg right up against Sylvia, humping her slowly. ‘How much more?’ ‘Eighty.’ ‘Fine, here take it.’ He took the money thankfully just in time before he began to slide off his pants to what I no doubt can assume would have been the beginning of his trouser snake dance. Sylvia finally moved away from him once she had the opportunity. The strange guy picked up his discarded clothes and watched us walk away, hollering as we went. ‘This another boy toy honey! Or you just like varying the age you go for? Because girl that last one was a piece of unadulterated flab! Him and his bald head…’ She rushed ahead, unwilling to listen to what he had to say and dragged me slightly. I kinda wanted to hear the rest but didn’t get an opportunity as we turned a slight corner again, following the long main street of this district. When she finally slowed down out of earshot of trouser snake, I halted her. ‘Don’t start Jack.’ ‘Who was with you when you were here last time?’ ‘Just a random Joe so I’d be safe and I had an alibi for wandering around here.’ ‘Who was it?’ ‘Just a random…’ ‘A name Sylvia.’ ‘David…David was his name.’ ‘Was it Father Pearson Sylvia?’ ‘No.’ Again the steely look in her eyes as she answered me question again. She looked like she had resolve at this and she was clenching her fists just like in the car, her knuckles turning white at the mention of the name. She wasn’t blinking. ‘Ok, fine.’ ‘Fine.’ We both looked around the street and found the sign we’d been waiting for: the Red Gusset. It was perched nicely between two other names which I’d rather not remind myself about. Suffice to say, they were worse than gusset. The outer appearance of the Red Gusset couldn’t be any more literal than possible. The red brick layout was suitably in a nice pattern from lighter pink to darker, more voluptuous red as it went inwards to the doorway which was a gaping hole. Pulsing red lights littered either side of the entrance and in a particularly classy move there was something dangling above the opening. I don’t think I need to explain that one. One man stood on the door, checking anybody who was attempting to enter beforehand. Really though he was just letting them by unless he had doubts. The two of us were no exception as he took one look, realised we had the money to enter and just stepped aside for us. Brooding alternative rock pounded from inside and the moans and screams of the singer of whatever track it was echoed in the hallway we were now situated in. The lights moved and danced to the motion of whomever padded their way from this hallway into any number of rooms. At least twice I saw briefly the asses of strippers scurrying to another performance. Other than the occasional bit of female flesh, it was mostly men slowly walking from room to room, tongues firmly on the floor and, for some, belts permanently unbuckled. Sylvia slid by me, squirming past and walking as confidently up to the a man who was standing by a staircase. Again her demeanour had totally changed. Gone was the nervousness as seducer took over. It was working well from what I could tell as the man was purring and flicking his tongue downwards at her. She smiled wickedly and planted a kiss on his cheek before motioning me over. The guy straightened up, trying to look official. ‘So you handsome thing me and my boy are just going to avail of your private services for the evening is that ok?’ He simply nodded. ‘That’s a good boy, don’t worry I won’t forget my end of the bargain.’ His lips went as wide as a Cheshire cat’s and turned to watch her going up the stairs. As she disappeared he looked at me. ‘Damn man, that girl is tight. I can’t wait to get inside that ass of hers.’ ‘Probably won’t be tight when I’m done friend.’ I left him then, playing along as best I could with whatever Sylvia had planned. At this point I could be going up to have sex and I wouldn’t know it. This is sure one elaborate plan to seduce me. Hell, she agreed to anal with the bell boy to pull this off. Again I followed Sylvia, confused as hell. At the top of the stairs Sylvia had returned to her usual self and was nervously walking up a small landing to a specific door. She called to me and I wandered over as quickly as I could. ‘This is it. Inside are all the records for the Red Gusset. This should have the attendance book which any VIP guests have to sign as a contractual obligation. Basically it proves they habituated here for any number of illegal practices and have agreed that they will provide for the establishment if it is ever raided.’ ‘Isn’t that kind of contradictory? I mean wouldn’t that book just screw over everyone who signed it?’ ‘Normally yes but as far as I could glean from one of the goons, they all go by pseudonyms. The document that contains their real name along with the pseudonym is in the owner’s possession, presumably far away from here.’ ‘Wouldn’t it make more sense to get that as well then Sylvia?’ ‘I haven’t located that yet. Besides this could be enough. Isn’t Father Pearson’s writing quite unmistakable?’ ‘True, it’s on every pamphlet he hands out.’ ‘Exactly, so it shouldn’t be hard to find his signature amongst it all.’ ‘Ok seems fair.’ She opened the door and I followed as best I could. Time must have passed quite quickly from when we arrived in Albany because now the darkness outside was starting to light. I knew this because there were a couple of windows in this small room. It was a simple office with nothing but a couple of lockers and table with chair. It looked like some sort of accountant’s office as there were reels of paper from tills on top of one of the lockers. Sylvia took no time in beginning to ruffle through one of the cabinets. ‘You search that one while I do this.’ I didn’t waste time opening up the cabinet and fingering through as many documents as possible. Lots of them were useless, from deals with certain subscription magazines to provide to clientele to orders placed for whole collections of old vintage pornography. I passed through this looking and searching for the one thing and I happened to find it. Sitting at the back of the cabinet was a leather bound book. Pulling it out I began to filter through it, knowing this was the book. Roughly a hundred or so signatures were in place in this book, each and every one a pseudonym for a VIP who had passed through the Red Gusset. They were all particularly crude and base names for the people, most of them unrepeatable, but what was important was that Father Pearson’s stood out like a sore thumb once you were looking for his particular. Genesis I laughed a little as I saw the priest was so arrogant to take the original book of the Bible as his name. Smiling still I stood up and noticed a flicker of a page fall to the ground. I looked at it and scanned it quickly. What I saw was this document: I couldn’t believe it. I finished reading the page and stood up, turning to Sylvia. ‘Do you know Father Pearson?’ And as the light began to creep in from the outside I hear another no, this time with a subtle smile on her face. A solid thud cracked into me and I crumpled to the floor. And as I lay there on the cold harsh floor, the last thing I saw before blacking out was Sylvia’s body dumped to the floor in succession, with a look…. …of pure horror on her face.
