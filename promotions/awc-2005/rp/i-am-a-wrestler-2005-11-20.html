I am a Wrestler

20 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20, 2005 The scene opens to a darkened gymnasium, illuminated only by the dimmest of lights generated by moonlight pouring through windows along the upper reaches of its walls. Through the darkness, a wrestling ring can be made out in the center of the room, and in it stalks a single figure, shrouded in the shadows. “When is it going to be about wrestling again?” calls out the man, to no one in particular. A loud snapping sound echoes throughout the room, as if someone threw a switch. Suddenly, a spotlight shines down to the center of the ring, revealing a handsome Caucasian man with shoulder-length brown hair. Beads of sweat pour down his shirtless, muscular torso, as he looks directly into the light and repeats his question. “When is it going to be about the wrestling again?” The man’s jaw tightens, as if someone didn’t give him a satisfactory answer. “They don’t care about your broken childhood. They don’t care about your lost loves. They don’t care about your poems. They don’t care about your dark thoughts. They don’t care about your nightmares. They don’t care about your addictions.' In fact, the only thing they care about is what you can do when you step through these ropes.” The unknown grappler grabs the top rope, pulls hard, before releasing the rope to accentuate his point. He then grits his teeth and shakes his head, as if he is disgusted that his non-existant audience is not listening to him. “You just don’t get it, do you,” he says. “None of it means a damn thing if you can’t dispense and absorb punishment right here in the middle of this ring. That is what the fans want and pay for. This is why people tune in each and every week. That is what this business is about.” The man spits in disgust, as the camera draws close to his face. “This is my life,” says the man, motioning to the confines of the ring. “This is my domain. I am a wrestler. I don't have some dark, sullen past or backstory to hang my hat on. I am just a wrestler. Is that not enough these days?” The man pauses as if he is waiting for reply. Silence serves as the only answer. 'Is that a no?' scoffs the man. “You want dark thoughts? I can provide those, if need be. Step into the ring with me, and I will snap your ankle, from bone to tendon, and do it with a smile on face and without a bit of remorse.” The man stalks around the ring, before continuing. “You want to talk about my mistress?” Her name is victory, and she orgasms when my arm is held high after watching my opponent tap out or listening to the referee count one...two... three...' “You want to talk about my addiction? “ The man motions with both hands to his waste to a non-existent belt, and then continues. “Championship gold is my vice and have a need that only the Transatlantic Belt can sate.” “If you haven’t figured it out, my life is about wrestling…” The loud snapping noise is heard once again, as power to the spotlight is cut, enveloping the ring, room and man in shadows as he whispers… “…and I am a wrestler.”
