Short But Not So Sweet

23 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-23-06 St. Thomas Hospital, Nashville, Tennessee The rhythmatic beats of a heart monitor was the only sound in the small room. Even the ticking seconds of the wall clock was silent. The patient slept peacefully due to pain killers and a few bandages. “How is she doctor?” The husband asked a white coat wearing a stethoscope and a name tag reading “Steven Price”. He was a short man of about five feet and maybe pushing forty in years with dark hair and light brown eyes. The doctor took his glasses off, returned his pin inside his jacket pocket, and led the husband outside his wife’s room. Once the door was shut and they stood in the hall, the doctor continued to lead the husband further down the all into his office where he shut the door and motioned the husband to take a seat. “Mr. Stewart, your wife will be fine. She has a couple broken ribs and a few bruises but with plenty of rest, she should recover one-hundred percent. As for the unborn..” “Unborn?” Mr. Stewart stood from his chair. His face held both shock and remorse. He hand his wife had been trying to conceive for months now and was not aware the small miracle had already begun. “Yes.” The doctor answered. “Unfortunately when your wife was struck in the stomach, she miscarried. I’m sorry.” Mr. Stewart fell into his chair. A mixture of emotions soared through his veins. The children told him Teresa hit his wife and Mama Tomas, bless her soul, couldn’t remember anything except her daughter came to visit and simply would not eat. The poor woman kept rambling on about how thin Teresa was. The bitch killed his unborn baby and had the nerve to wave at him before she left. He loved Mama as if she was his own and for her sake, he’s glad she’s loosing her memory because when he’s done with the famous Teresa Jane Tomas, they’ll be nothing left to remember. Leaving Dr. Price sitting in his chair, he stood up and quietly left the office. Once outside the door, he pulled out his cell phone and dialed a set of numbers. “Hello, I need to file a report.” Belgrade, Serbia He didn’t have to come with her, but he insisted. Despite Teresa’s objections, Scott was just as stubborn and he didn’t want to leave her side; especially after everything she’s been through. Her drinking has gradually gotten worse as well as her nicotine habit. He was worried. “We should get separate rooms.” Teresa told Scott. They had just arrived at the hotel after a long plane trip. Scott gave her a ‘what the hell’ look and dropped her luggage. “What’s your deal Teresa? You act like you want me around then you push me away?” Teresa picked up her luggage and brushed past him. All she thought about was a cigarette and a cold beer. They spoke little during the plain ride. Probably because she slept on his shoulder the whole trip. “I told you not to come with me Scott. I don’t want to pull you into my troubled life.” Scott jogged to catch up with her and grabbed her arm. She stopped but didn’t turn around. She could feel Scott’s hand slide down her arm and gently take her hand. “Don’t push me away Teresa.” A sigh exits Teresa’s chest. She gives his hand a squeeze staring to the hotel office in front of them. “How can you give a damn about me after what I’ve become; after all I’ve done? Everyone I’ve ever cared about is gone. I’ve even lost you” Still holding her hand, he stepped behind her and gave her neck a soft kiss. The feel of his lips on her tender skin caused her to take in a sharp breath. She bit her lip when she felt his breath as he whispered into her ear. “I’m here. Don’t you see? You won’t lose me again. I promise.”
