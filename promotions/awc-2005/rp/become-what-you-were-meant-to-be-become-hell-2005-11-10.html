become what you were meant to be. become HELL.

10 November 2005

Date: earlier god says... it’s up to people to brave on with experimentation. move forth the species by using our imagination. You wanna hear a story? Come in close. Things may get confusing so you’re going to have to pay attention. If you have to piss, go do it quick. And if you take a shit, please put it back. Are you ready, now? No bullshit this time? Sure hope your stomach’s full; things may take a while. You’re not leaving without a clear and conscious idea as to what’s going on. For a month this shit has been dangled in front of your face, just screaming for you to take a look or it’ll smack you in the face. It’s something hard to ignore, and amidst the entire Major League-Human Growth Hormone mud-slinging that’s going on, seems like only a matter of time before something like this blows over. Doctor Handle had, well, a handle on this situation well over six months ago. His intentions were... hidden, at best, as well as the origin of his fascination with this project. He was the perfect man for Adam to seek answers from. “Oh, yes come in Mr. Dick.” He’d say, after Adam would introduce himself on a short notice. Adam would walk in, with one item in mind; Tell me about the drug Promecil. “Well, first of all.” Doctor Handle begins his explanation. “It isn’t a drug; it’s a diet pill. Second, there’s a lot to learn about this pill. Tell me what you’d like to know?” Adam looked confused for a second. He thought Handle would go on to list an actual fucking fact list of nonsensical facts until he finally reached the meat of the goods, what Adam was looking for. To him, it was all a matter of connecting the dots: Why did James Lawler, the manager of the Educator, hold possession of such a vast amount of these pills? And why are they being endorsed by Alex Strider, another former AWC wrestler? Of course, Adam never asked these specifics; they contained material that Dr. Handle could never even know about. So the man decided to play stupid instead. “There has to be a specific reason why you came here, Mr. Dick. Maybe I can explain things a little easier for someone like you if you’d actually tell me what you need to know.” Fucking prick, echoed through Adam’s mind. He thought over this very carefully; choosing his words carefully as to not raise alertness to his intentions. Finally, a grand question came to mind that would seem as curious as any cat itching for a coke-bump. “What does Promecil have to do with professional wrestling?” Dr. Handle smiled. “Ah. The wrestling.” Enclosed in his brick-lined office, the Doctor adjusted himself behind his desk, before removing his glasses. He motioned for Adam to sit down, who politely declined. Apparently he didn’t think he’d stay for long. “In the words of Omnikon, the concept of diet pills has remained a mainstay in competitive sports. Catering to athletes extensive needs in standard maintenance of the human body, Promecil is a compound that his both nutritious and effective in the training process.” Adam didn’t look grim as much these days, having seen better times, but this was just ridiculous. “But we all know corporations spit more crap than politics. They’ll say anything that would look good on a press release. The truth is, that Omnikon actually holds exclusive contracts with certain athletes, in that these athletes agreed to take on guinea pig-type roles in the development of the pill. Apparently Omnikon is a company with vast resources and rewards their contributing athletes rather handsomely. Wrestlers happen to be the most prime test subjects, keeping to weekly routines of the most physically enduring activities. They’re a lot tougher than gymnasts and certainly less delicate than football players. While Omnikon has employed both members’ services in their research, none have tested as well as wrestlers in their arena. Things were going well for the development of Promecil, until your friend the Educator decided to make magic happen. That is what you’re curious about, right?” Adam was stunned. Dr. Handle mentioning the Educator really took him off guard, but he was quick to cover his tracks. “I’m actually wondering more about Alex Strider.” The doctor scoffs, and laughs. It’s a combination of humor and humility. “Alexander Strider? Please, he’s merely a has-been trying to claw his way back to glory. He’ll take any endorsement he can get, and I’d place my last dollar on the fact that he probably doesn’t know what’s in it.” Again, Adam looks confused. “Please, Mr. Dick. No more games. I’ve followed this story too closely, and it doesn’t take a doctorate to try and decipher your reasoning for coming here. Anytime you talk on camera at events you can’t go without squeezing a word in on how you’re going to end the Educator’s life for his blasphemous display he brought to the ring. Apparently, Omnikon couldn’t trick the world like they’d hoped and decided to return to square one on the development of Promecil.” “Wait a second. Promecil is a diet pill. The Educator was caught taking the Prometheus Serum-“ “Exactly.” Dr. Handle said, cutting him off. The smile on his face was growing; Adam could tell he was enjoying this. “You see, Omnikon isn’t developing this product publicly because it isn’t exactly legal. To the Food and Drug Administration, they’re just another post-Ephedrine pill trying to work out all their patents. They haven’t submitted the finished product in for testing, segueing to mass-distribution and marketing of the pill, because apparently they can’t figure out a way to trick the testers.” “I don’t get it.” “Of course you don’t, because you didn’t know that Promecil is a softened strand of the Prometheus Serum, engineered to administer the same affects on your metabolism and muscular system. They’re trying to create a publically acceptable synthetic steroid in hopes of gaining secure endorsements with every major sporting league in the nation.” Uh... “In English, please.” This was all over Adam’s head. “Omnikon is illegally testing their watered-down variation of the Prometheus Serum on various members of wrestling talent, until they come up with a brand that is potent enough to be significantly affective yet untraceable by any kind of exam imaginable. The type of contracts they’re holding with these athletes are a legal hell and really only constitute as a sponsorship, but these punks know what they’re getting into. Of course, all the kids that are taking this prototype of Promecil are receiving mixed results.” “But how can they be doing this on such a large scale? How can they be distributing all of their product without being noticed? It’s not like the Educator can’t go rat them out, right?” “See, that’s where I can’t talk. It’s hard to say how they’re pushing their product so quickly, but my spider-sense, so to speak, can only imagine a shady process to follow such shady dealings. How they can keep such an organized and off-the-radar development undergoing is pretty mind-boggling, then again I didn’t attend university to study white-collar crime.” Now the cards are starting to organize within Adam’s mind. Past occurrences with Goone, the meetings that Lawler had. They seemed like petty drug-pushers to him, but that was a mystery he’d have to keep his eye out for. “So a company like Omnikon is working ruthlessly, I take it? That they don’t really care for their test subject’s well beings.” “Apparently not. It’s such a malignant form of the Prometheus Serum; all polluted with garbage and dietary elements that further destroy your system. Prolonged exposure to the chemical even went so far as to causing the unexpected death of a regular patient of mine; Rubin Demorto.” “It’s funny you should mention that.” Adam began to unfurl a manila folder from within his jacket pocket. “Because that’s what led me right to you. Do you know James Lawler, or why he might have the last updated medical chart of your apparent client before he died? Because his death is news to me.” Adam tosses down the medical report he found in James’ storage locker. It only takes a quick glance for Dr. Handle to recognize his own hand writing. “I wrote this, no more than two weeks ago. It was amazing, Rubin was at the height of his physical peak when I made this report. Stronger and faster than ever, even though things felt different about him. It wasn’t until I went back and checked his hair follicles that I found the tiniest strain to date of the Prometheus Serum laced within. If Omnikon hasn’t actually cracked the code they’ve been trying at, it’ll be damn soon when they do.” “But do you know James Lawler?” Dr. Handle shook his head. “I’m sorry, it doesn’t ring a bell.” “He’s the talent manager for the Educator, or he was. I figure he’s dead now, but for some reason he had this file with the intent of giving it to someone. I overheard people talking about ‘specimens’?” “Probably because the program was about to adopt Rubin officially. I could only imagine that Omnikon found his testing results to be the most successful to date and decided to take him under a more secure and watchful eye. The rumor is that they adopt all their untapped potential in their facility for final examinations before placing them in a very successful placement program. Rubin was just waiting for his chance at PRIME.” “Doctor, this may sound strange and completely fucked up, but I have a job to do.” Handle looks confused, but decides to play into whatever Adam was really looking for. “Can you change this file to look like me? I need to convince a group of shady pill-makers that I’m an aspiring young wrestler who is perfectly capable of being a guinea-pig for a god-damned dangerous drug. You know, research purposes.” The doctor again resorted to a cackle. “For a second, I tried to believe you when you said this was not about the Educator, but I knew that was a sore mistake. So is pursuing this, Mr. Dick. If you follow this lead, you may actually find the Educator or you may not, we don’t know. “But Mr. Dick, some skeletons aren’t worth digging for.” Oh, Adam Dick begged to differ. “This isn’t a skeleton, Dr. Handle, this is a bitch we’re talking about. Dead people have more class than folk like the Educator.” adam says... i’m savage as fuck, and willing in the able. i never got clowned so when it came around i was down to put my dick on the table. Mike calls me for a status update. I was appreciative towards things like this; it’s what made the partnership work sometimes. I had to admit, my mind had been so convoluted lately that I rarely kept track of the events anymore. It was like I just showed up to wrestle. But Wade kept my head in the game. My ability was never waning, I was just unfocused. He wanted to change that, and let me tell you, he was helping. “So’d you hear about Triangles?” “Another lame concept that Pearl probably confused himself with?” “Oh, man, you don’t even know! Get this, matches are in rings the shape of triangles with three matches coinciding at the same time, with three different prizes. THIS SHIT IS BECOMING SO COMPLEX THAT I HAVE NO IDEA WHAT THE FECK A TRIANGLE IS ANYMORE! Pearl has raped all meaning of the word.” “Really.” Adam began. “Three prizes?” “The Belt, then a shot at every title, then some mystery prize that Pearl has hidden up his ass. It’s all shite, I tell ya. Did they even bother to give the Unfuckables the Duo gold like we so God-likely deserve. Conspiracy, I tell ya. A fecking AWC conspiracy.” “It’s only time, Wadey.” He didn’t like it when I tried to calm him down. Wade liked getting pissy at times, it was funny. “Time that should be spent sucking my salty nutsack, man! We’re the fucking build of that promotion now. Everyone’s lost steam! Pierce Lavelle hasn’t done jack all since he beat Hate. Hate hasn’t done jack all since he lost. You saw what happened to Paddy at Super Series, the man’s lost heat! I’m telling you, man, that federation needs to be building around us. We’re the best thing they have. But do we get a purely Unfuckable main event for the pay-per-view? No, we get you in bullshit qualifying matches for Main Events, while I get stuck defending my belt against six other sobbing newbs… AND I DON’T EVEN KNOW THE SLIGHTEST FUCKING CLUE ABOUT MY MATCH!” “Oh, Pyramid Scramble?” I asked. “I heard rumors about that.” “Fucking rumors, they always end up coming true around here. This shit is starting to become a real bore, man, waiting for these assholes to wake up and see the true talent that they have to work with. No one wants to see Trendy Twenty Somethings or Norman Queer or some chick who needed Jason Winters to get heat. These people have the Unfuckables, and that’s all they need.” “Word.” It was the only thing I could think of to egg him on. “We’re going to get shuffled out of the picture man. I can feel it. We have to do something, something drastic. To stay in the picture.” “Like what?” “I don’t know man. Maybe we should just kick everyone’s ass.” I started laughing. “Maybe we should start a riot.” And then Mike began laughing. Oh, when our imaginations would run it always became a grand old time. Just to be able to bullshit again after all this time away brought a smile to my face. “Oh, did you hear the new rumor?” His voice drops decreasingly low. Apparently it was big news that should not go unmentioned. Adam took an interesting grip of the steering wheel as he drove across Los Angeles. “Apparently, Pearl has been making phone calls lately. For the sake of the show, he wants everything to be a big surprise. But with the sudden drought of names, it looks like he’s getting for an old heavy hitter to be getting his name back in the game.” “Fuck Crimson O’Malec.” I said, jumping to conclusions entirely too soon. “I’ve shown him up more than enough already, he lost the steam and he isn’t going to get it back.” “It isn’t him, man. Apparently, word has it that Alex Strider is set to return.” I drop my phone and swerve across the road. The freeway is too busy for me to lean over and pick it up on the floor of the passenger seat; I might swerve into a car. So I just concentrate on the road and let Mike’s calls die out into oblivion, or until he decides to stop waiting for me to answer and hang up the phone. Entirely too much to process for a day. Alex Strider the drug pusher is now Alex Strider the returning thorn-in-my-side. All setting up for Triangles. Tim Shipley, my partner. Pierce Lavelle, my opponent. Alex Strider, my... whatever he is. The planets were lining up for something big, I could feel it. But there’s still things that stand in the way. Promecil, Omnikon. The Educator. Small tasks. Depending on what angle you look from. Because in terms of Adam Dick, there’s nothing bigger right now than the Educator. Kelli and Pierce Lavelle come in to play as well, Pierce also blending in with the other side. But none matter like the Educator. And then there’s AWC-me. The Illustrious Face-Eater. And then there’s Triangles. Paddy O’Shea. Red Rock. Crimson O’Malec. Doesn’t matter. I’ll drag Tim Shipley to the end, as I’ve seemed to be doing all along. The kid can’t live his life without me anymore. At first he said I had to capitalize off him. But now that it’s said and done, and the eyes are all settled, it’s no wonder he’s trying to stick around. People like seeing the bad guy torturing people. He’s just begging that I’ll turn my sights back to him. So come along, Tim Shipley. Jump on my back. And bring Patrick Mapleleaf along, because I know he’ll end up trying to drag me down anyways. Dude’s been doing less than O’Malec which is scarily true. But you can keep me from the blue ring, Harber. You can’t keep me from stopping Pierce Lavelle again. Because someone who’s under so much influence by my hand cannot be the one who gets the one up on me. I can’t let that happen, I won’t. This tool has almost gotten himself killed in every occasion so far, it’s only a matter of time before it happens in the ring. What a way to go out in unprecedented matters. Within the middle of the Triangle. You’re going to wish we never crossed paths, Pierce Lavelle. Because things get really interesting when there’s a relationship outside of work. Let’s see what happens when we twist the ends of this relationship together, shall we?
