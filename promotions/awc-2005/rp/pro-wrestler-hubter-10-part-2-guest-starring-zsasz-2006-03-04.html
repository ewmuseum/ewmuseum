Pro Wrestler Hubter #10 Part 2 (guest starring Zsasz)

4 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/06 (continued from Pro Wrestler Hunter #10) 'The AWC, hmm? An interesting promotion to say the least,' Zsasz commented over a cup of hot sake. 'Raucous, gaudy, but undeniably a colorful enough promotion where you can't miss if you succeed. Not up to my standards, but you can't deny that it's good for business. The accomodations here in Philadelphia could be better. Did you find yourselves a place to stay?' Megumi, and a reluctant but insistent Grappler had crowded around a small table with Zsasz to partake in a sake ceremony. Megumi had been taken by Zsasz's fluent Japanese and his gentle mannerisms. He invited her to take part in the ceremony and she obliged, to the dissapointment of The Green Grappler. So he forced his way into the situation by grabbing a chair from another table and sitting down. Megumi was irritated but nothing she could do would make Grappler leave. And what was even more annyoing was that Grappler seemed to know Zsasz. Did this fool know every wrestler, hotel menager, and bartender in America? 'Were you able to get our rooms settled across the street at the hotel?' Megumi asked Grapps. 'Rooms? Try room. The Shriners are in town,' Grapps corrected a bit sheepishly. 'The place is full up. Hmm, shriners. They are freemasons, right? That explain why you are here Zsasz? Conspiracy theories abound.' 'Don't change the subject,' Megumi blurted. 'One room. Sigh.' Another night with the green buffoon, she thought. She leaned back in her chair with a faint groan. Her reaction was not lost on Zsasz. 'Before, you said you had good news and bad news... that must be the BAD news of which you spoke, I imagine,' said the man wryly. 'Strange you should find it so,' Grapps countered mildly, apparently misunderstanding the gibe. 'Sharing a room with a beautiful woman doesn't strike me as a bad thing.' 'The young neesan ,' Zsasz corrected pointedly, observing Megumi's silent fury over Grapps suggestive remark, 'does not seem to share your enthusiasm.' 'Oh, but she does. It's just that, you know, Megumi is the very soul of discretion,' Grapps confided man-to-man. At that moment the waiter returned with their drinks. Megumi snatched the martini glass from his tray and thunked it down in front of Grappler. 'Drink this,' she suggested sweetly, 'and several others. I'm buying. ' Taking up the sake cup, Megumi plunged into the half-remembered ceremony of pouring and offering the rice wine. If Zsasz found anything amiss in her rendering of the ritual, he did say. The ritual brought a much needed change of pace to the conversation, which turned to gossip, politics, and- this being the night before an event, after all- business. Despite his promise to remain an observer, Grapps continued to verbally spar with the Slavic born Zsasz. The masked man scored a number of hits, any one of which, coming from any other man, could have been considered the grounds for a fight. Zsasz let the gibes pass without comment. He really could not do otherwise, for Grappler's barbs were issued with such friendly delicacy that responding with anger would seem as silly as swatting at soap bubbles with a barbwire bat. Megumi sipped her drink, silently taking measure of her strange drinking companion. Zsasz was charming to her, unfailingly polite even in the face of Grappler's foolishness. For someone with his reputation for being a cut throat businessman, and worse, he showed remarkable restraint and good humor. Perhaps the rumors were exaggerated after all. 'Can I interest you in dinner?' Zsasz asked Megumi, making a point to not include Grappler in his offer. 'I am sorry, but perhaps another time?' Megumi offered delicately. She did not know this man, although she felt she could trust him. But too bone-weary from travel left her unwilling to deal with the pleasantries of dinner, and if Grappler were to try to tag along she didn't think she could handle the constant verbal sparring these two were taking part in. 'The let us finish our drinks. More sake?' Zsasz smiled at Megumi and offered her some more rice wine. She smiled and raised her cup. 'GARSON!' yelled Grappler, mispronouncing the French word. 'More apple martinis, please!' The drinks seemed to improve Grappler's mood and his sense of humor, or lack thereof, even more, and he cheerfully engaged Zsasz in conversation, relishing the verbal swordplay. Megumi on the other hand was too tired to take part in the sparring. Nevertheless, she kept a keen eye on the room as she sipped on her rice wine. AWC employees were coming and going as were other patrons. Megumi stayed alert for anything unusual. Even through the heavy bass tones of the music playing from the dance floor she was able to make out conversations drifting about. What she caught in the air unnerved her. 'Branded right on the temple, like a cow on his ass...' 'They say this guy got past security on a freaking airplane. Can you believe that?' 'Pearl better step up security or else every one of these bodybuilding twits is going to be on the next bus headed for CORE with their tails between their legs...' The hair on Megumi's back arose like she was shot through with electricity. had someone else fallen victim to the Pro Wrestler Hunter? Megumi learned nothing from the fragments of conversation, but she noted with dismay that people were milling about and talking in hushed tones. Megumi prepared to make an excuse to leave. 'Hey! What's going on?' The young wrestler known as Sage walked over to the table and put an arm on Megumi's shoulder. Sage looked at Zsasz with a cocked eyebrow, but smiled at everyone at the table. 'Oh, hello Sage,' Megumi said, smiling at her friend. A shiver went up her spine as she looked around at the other faces in the club, many of them worried. 'Join me in a green apple martini young lady,' Grappler said, tugging at Sage's white blouse. 'I just might. I need a drink after hearing what happened to Jason Warr.' Grappler was about to break off on a strom of words and questions when Megumi responded by putting her finger to her lips. The gesture was hardly necessary. Sage began to explain and no one took their eyes off of her. 'Seems like Jason was on an airplane flying in to Philly. They thought he was asleep,' she said, grabbing a small glass of water from the table and taking a sip. 'When they tried to wake him up, he wouldn't. They found a brand, on his forehead of all places. It was like, burned, into his skin.' Megumi listened intently, but she also was aware of her surroundings. She noted a man's entrance and studied him with careful concealed interest. he was very tall, but he moved with the gracefullness of a cat. He was wrapped in a black woolen cloak with a cowl, but unlike the other people in the club, he did not take off his garb. He picked a table in the shadows, not too far from where the group was sitting. Megumi found this behavior very peculiar but she was also intent on listening to Sage's story about Jason Warr. She was a professional and she would not let her feelings get in the way of discovering what was going on. Maybe she was being paranoid. Regardless, she knew the Pro Wrestler Hunter was near. 'His head was buried in his pillow and one of the flight attendents shook him and he just rolled out of his seat to the floor of the plane,' she continued. 'Big 'CYDONIA' brand on his head. They took him to the hospital for tests. He's alive but barely. They aren't sure what the heck happened, but one thing is for sure. Warr isn't coming back. His manager and agent called and they think AWC has unsafe working conditions. Hell, they don't even know if he's going to recover.' As Sage went on about the latest victim on the list, she watched as the waiter brought the newcomer a drink. As he tipped his head to drink it, Megumi caught a glimpse of his pale yellow features. A man of about forty years, he was ordinary enough. There was something familiar about him to Megumi, maybe the squared chin and the determined eyes. Yet, she was sure she had never seen him before. She watched him for just a second more, and he did nothing but sit there, and she felt she was being overly-suspicious. Seeing shadows in every corner, spies behind every painting, is that it? Megumi thought to herself. 'Yes, I am seeing danger in every conrer. Aren't you?' Megumi blurted out to Sage, cutting off the woman in mid-sentence. 'Ever since I came to the AWC it has been nothing but violence for the sake of violence. Almost every wrestler has been this massive bulldozer, looking for intimidation through ferocity and force.' 'Well, I'm seeing if there is a boogey man under the bed. I'm going to go to my hotel room and check the closet for anything weird, know what I mean?' Sage elbowed Grappler who winced theatrically at her. 'Ha ha! BOO! Hee hee! Oh man, I need rest badly. Traveling can be killer.' 'Yes, it seems jason Warr found that out, didn't he?' Zsasz said coldly. 'Oooh! That was cold, Vlad.' Grappler shook a finger at him like scolding a child. 'And quite funny, if not for the fact that it's true. And it could happen to anyone, it seems.' The four of them stood there for a moment in silence, the music a backdrop for the thoughts running through their minds. It was a dark shadow looming. 'Long trip in, wasn't it? I am very tired.' Megumi broke the silence. Zsasz raised his hand. 'Say no more. I am sorry to keep you here so long. I'm a manager, not a wrestler. You let me settle with the barkeep.' 'Thank you,' Megumi said, kicking Grapps under the table to keep him from arguing the point. 'I am sure we will meet again,' Zsasz said with a smile directed at Megumi. 'Yes,' she said simply, and then they bowed low in a traditional Japanese gesture. Taking Grappler in one arm and Sage in the other, she walked toward the door with them. 'So, what about this hotel room?' she demanded from Grapps in a resigned tone. 'It's just one room, nothing luxurious.' 'As long as it has a bed,' she mumbled as they walked out into the street. 'Funny you should mention 'a bed' you know...' Grapps voice trailed off as they looked to cross the street. 'Ha ha!' Sage laughed as they neared the opposite curb. 'I'll see you guys later.' She headed off toward a different hotel entrance. 'I have to get my key from the desk. Good night.' Zsasz watched them go. he speculated, shrugged, and then snapped his fingers and called over a man who was standing in a corner, unseen before now. With him he had a briefcase, and he placed it upon the table. Zsasz opened it and produced a small laptop computer. He lifted open the screen and pressed a button. Within seconds the computer lit up with blue and green graphics. Static rushed across a small screen off to the top corner and then a figure appeared. Zsasz pulled out a small pair of earphones and jacked himself in. 'Greeting, Master Shuriken,' Zsasz said into a small microphone. He smirked at the title he had just called this man. 'It is late. What do you want?' the haughty voice demanded. The man's manners were not existent. Zsasz smiled, ignoring the insults, deadly as they were. He could afford to be tolerant tonight. 'I hear you pay a good price for information. I have some that you should find most interesting.' 'Well?' 'I met Megumi Hamada this evening. She and I now work for the AWC. And she seems to have come out of the cold, so to speak. I hadn't seen her once in two weeks.' He said this with a hint of curiosity. 'What an incredible creature she truly is.' 'What?' The man in the tiger mask looked very angry. 'I told you to stay away from her.' 'We work in the same company. It was bound to happen.' Zsasz noted. 'I won't have her associated with one such as you, Zsasz,' Tyger Shuriken spat out. 'I don't want her reputation to become tainted by you.' 'I doubt that, considering those in the know in this business are well aware that Megumi hamada is a shooter and an enforcer. And she works for the CYDONIA agency. But nonetheless, that is not why I contacted you. Megumi has found herself a couple of pets. One seems to have become her tag team partner and the other, I don't know, but she seems friendly as well.' 'I know this already,' Tyger said impatiently, his voice garbled by static interference. Zsasz continued, undisturbed by the interruption. 'But apppearences can be decieving. I believe I know who this Green Grappler is under the mask. And I believe he works for the same company that outfits Butterfly. I believe he is an agent for CYDONIA.' There was a flicker of interest, but only for a moment. 'What of it? What do I care if they send a baby-sitter. He won't get in anyone's way,' Tyger said calmly. 'This is not some egotystical gym rat they have sent here. And he hides his identity. Who knows what he is cable of doing,' Zsasz noted, trying to goad the masked man. 'Ha!' Tyger broke in scornfully. 'You think this Green Grappler is capable of anything but reporting to his superiors? I doubt it. I'll have you know I have an ear on every conversation that Megumi has with anyone. At any time. Think I did not know you were just speaking to her. I heard everything. I don't care about the green fool.' Zsasz smiled, one more trick up his sleeve. 'I also believe I saw an old acquintance of ours. Sangre De La Lanza?' Instantly, the image of Tyger on the screen sharpened. 'Yes, I thought that might get your attention,' Zsasz murmured, sa malicious glint in his eyes. 'Imagine all these years, and our mutual friend should just pop up here in Philadelphia. It's amazing what being black-balled will do to a man's face and features.' Tyger brushed aside the small conversation. 'Sangre de la Lanza was there? In Philadelphia? You're sure of this?' 'What a coincidence, huh?' Zsasz said with some personal humor. 'You did well contacting me,' said Tyger. 'Your fee will be the usual.' 'What about The Green Grappler?' Zsasz asked pointedly. 'I told you, I don't care about him.' 'If you don't care about him,' Zsasz said carefully, 'wouldn't it be amusing if he were to fall victim to the Pro Wrestler Hunter?' 'No, it would not be amusing. You have a strange sense of humor.' 'Yes, I know. Now what about The Green Grappler,' Zsasz asked. 'The terms are changed. I want my payment in a different form of currency.' 'Yes? Well?' 'The Green Grappler.' 'Done,' snapped Tyger. 'As I said, it doesn't matter to me what happens to the masked buffoon. Considering the large sum of money you are giving up, your pride has a high price, Zsasz.' Zsasz shut the computer off. As you will all learn, Zsasz thought, my pride has a high price indeed.
