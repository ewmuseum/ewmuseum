Date: 24th October 2005 As summer regrettably leaves us for another year and another cold and wet autumn lingers upon us we find ourselves in the streets of a typical autumn afternoon. Although in the busy city we don't see a great deal of trees surrounding us we still notice that the odd few decorative trees on the pavements are beginning to expel golden brown leaves to the ground. The winds are beginning to pick up and take wet brown leave spiraling up in the air as well as various pieces of rubbish like plastic bags or empty crisp packets. The rain slowly begins to descend from the thickening black clouds in conjunction with people withdrawing their umbrella and pulling up their hoods. The wind races faster and the rain explodes with a crack of thunder and a flash of lightning. The rain beats off the parked with such force that it could be mistaken for gun fire and the rain drizzles down the window pains of all the tall old 1900's buildings. People are beginning to run down the pavements , shielding themselves with anything they have. Be it news papers plastic bags or their hands. One man can now be seen walking down the pavement. He doesn't seem to be urgently runnings down the pavement but he is sporting an expression of discontent. This, of course is Red Rock on his way back from doing the weekly shop. His hair is dripping wet and his clothes are now heavy with rain water. In each arm he is holding two brown paper bags full of shopping. Red Rock: Fucks sake...why did they give me brown paper bags why can't they get bloody plastic carrier bags like normal shops... Red Rock mutters to himself a little more as he turns down his street. The water begins to seep through his shoes and start to drench his sock. Theres nothing that pisses off Red Rock more than wet socks. He describes is as “worse than cancer”. A person wilding an umbrella charges towards Red Rock, trying to fight their way against the wind and rain. It doesn't appear that the person has seen Red Rock due to the huge umbrella blocking their view. And as expected the person barges in to Red Rock who stumbles over, desperately trying to maintain his balance. Red Rock: YOU BASTARD! The person ignores the fact they just barged in to Red Rock and walks on. At this point Red Rock is hopping on one leg still trying to keep his balance. After a couple more seconds of desperate hopping Red Rock successfully stays afoot. He now is trying to catch his breath as his expression is wide eyed with an open mouth grin brought on by his luck of not falling over. Suddenly bottoms his brown paper bags split due to them swelling with water. The entire contents of both bags spill all over the pavement with the clanging of cans and the rustle of cereal. Red Rock still maintaining his previous expression looks down at the carnage. Red Rock: OH FOR FUCKS SAKE! He jumps up and down on his shopping and starts to pick stuff up and throwing it around in a childish rage. He picks up a can of tinned peeled apples and tosses it across the road. Almost instantly after Red Rock hears this noise he stops in his tracks and his face drops to one that would say “uh oh”. Voice: Who the FUCK threw that? Red Rock's eye almost pop out of his head upon noticing this weapon. The Skin Head runs across the road, weaving through cars as he pursues Red Rock. Red Rock begins to scarper down his street in a mad dash for his house, which is only a few hundred yards down the street. Skin Head: You're only delaying the inevitable! Red Rock's block of flats can be seen in the distance and Red Rock seems to have a good distance from the angry skin head. Red Rock begins to feel a sensation of joy as it seems he is going to get away. He reaches in to his pocket for his key and his feeling of joy disappears leaving a sick feeling of anguish. He vigorously pats down all possible places it could be in the vane hope he can feel it. Red Rock: NO! NOOOO!!!! Red Rock reaches his front door and checks for his key one more time. He looks to his left and notices the angry skin head charging towards him like an angry bull, or a very fast very dangerous freight train. Red Rock presses the buzzer and jumps up and down hoping again for a speedy response. But as he looks to his left again he notices the angry man getting dangerously close. Red Rock: shite! He disappears down the alley and jumps over some metal dust bins and wades through piles of uncollected refuse bags. Red Rock cringes as the smell of the weeks old rubbish lingers around him. He notices the fire escape ladder in front of him and jumps up to pull it down. Red Rock scrambles up the ladder and is now standing on the steps aside the building. He pulls up the ladder to buy himself some time and starts to look for a way in. There are two windows Red Rock eyes up. He dashes up to one of them and gazes inside. Red Rock: It's D'avid's flat. YES! Red Rock looks through and it is indeed Red Rock's weird ginger dutch neighbor.
