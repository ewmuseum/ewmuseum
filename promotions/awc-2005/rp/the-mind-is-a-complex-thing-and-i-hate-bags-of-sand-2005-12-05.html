The mind is a complex thing. And I hate bags of Sand.

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/12/05 The Office of Dr Herman Sherman Psychiatrist We join everyone's favourite AWC Double Champ, Mike Wade, inside the office of his Shrink. He lies on 'the couch' that has become a centrepiece of every Shrink office probably in the world. He lies feet facing towards the door, with his gelled head nestled nicely within the confines of the tan leather couch. It seems odd that a man with so much confidence, wit and titles (!) is seeing a psychiatrist. But very few know the real Mike Wade. Very few probably want to. The public persona differs from the man on the inside. The man who's had is heart crushed, who's left big promotions because off eelings of unwantedness and depression, the shell of a man who formerly build a flase wall of confidence around himself. Or the man who was once a boy who was so scared of the Boogeyman when he was growing up that he would piss himself every night! Oh yes very few knew that Mike Wade and he knew it. Dr Sherman leant forward. His notebook grasped tighly in his right hand. His pen half in his left hand and half within his slightly yellow over-bight. His hair was balding on top with his comb over not quite reaching the ear on the otherside of his head but trying it's best. He wasn't the best looking man nor the best dressed, with his navy shirt with black blazer, but an intimidating presence none the less. Mike had always been afraid of Doctors. When it be GP or DDS, it didn't matter. Going to the Doctor meant you were sick. Being sick meant being weak. And Wades didn't show weakness! At least that's what Thomas Wade, Mikes overbearing, overachieving, over rich father always said. This was a huge step for Mike. He had been seeing this Shrink for a couple of weeks now. Mike didn't like to pretend like there was something wrong with him, but his frequent change in moods needed to be seen to. And his recent 'drug days' flashbacks had really fucked his head up. Now with the added pressure of holding 2 titles, one which he seemingly defends every week, it was all getting too much for His Swerviness. Dr Sherman leant forward somemore. Mike, not being the most comfertable with men almost touching him leaned back. Dr Sherman: Tell me Mike. How do you think the progress is? This was something that Mike had luckily mulled over in the car on the way over. A second thought wasn't even needed here. Mike: I gotta be honest Shrink..... Dr Sherman: Mike...I already told you you don't NEED to call me that. Mike: It makes me feel more at home with you though little nickname you know. Dr Sherman was not ammused. Sure people referred to these type of Doctors as that. But not to their face! Mike: So anyway Shrink.....I gotta be honest. I thought coming to a shrink like you would be bad. And then I thought going to a shrink called Herman Munster would just be funny! Dr Sherman: It's Herman Sherman actually. Mike: Yeah I wouldn't be boasting Shrink. But now when I get here and we've done a couple of weeks I feel good. Dr Sherman: Really? The surprise was very evident in 'Shink''s voice. He obviously didn't think he was making much progress. After all the mind of Wade is a complex thing. Mike: Yeah sure. Talking about the stuff that pisses me off is much better than just holding it in. Dr Sherman: Who would've thought? How are you feeling lately then Mike? Mike: Well Shrink, last week myself and my partner won the Alliance titles finally from that Pykey bastard Paddy O Shea. Dr Sherman: Partner? Mike you never mentioned you were homosexual. Mike: HEY! Hang on now. That's just what we call our teammate in wrestling man. Dr Sherman: Oh I see. Sometimes Doctors can play dumb too. And finding out that a pro wrestler was openly gay and winning little gay titles around the world would be nice gossip would be great 'round the watercooler' gossip don't ya think? While it's true that Mike enjoyed his last week. Straight after he won his title last week he was dealt a challenger to his Relentless title. The challenger bothered him some. He felt the need to discuss the man with Dr Sherman. Dr Shelley: You mentioned a man you have to fight next week Mike. What bother's you about him? Mike: Well a lot of things Shrink. First of all remember I told you of my fear of the Boogeyman growing up? Dr Shelley: Of course Mike. And we agreed no such man existed. Mike chimed in. He had done some investigating. Mike: So we say Shrink. But I have evidence to the contrary. Dr Shelley: Oh? Of course the good Doctor was surprised. You didn't have to be a good Doctor to be surprised when a pro wrestler tells you he has proof the Boogeyman exists. Mike: I was surfing the net one time and I found this picture, the people on the site claim that he is in fact the Boogeyman!! Mike whips out the photo....... Dr Sherman examines the photograph carefully. Leaning foward more to get a better view. His eyes creep up as close to his spectactle lenses as they can go. His bushy eyebrows squint as he makes a final submission to the photo. Content in his examination he breaks his trance to dismiss this fantasy idea. Dr Sherman: Mr Wade I firmly believe this is a fantasy. Let's do the excersise we....... Mike wouldn't be defeated in his belief that easy. He was there to prove a point. A point that his head had created and had him shitting himself since last week when the Fresh! card was announced. In order to keep a clean head and clean underpants he needed clearance from the shrink. Mike: That isn't my problem Shrink. See here's a picture of my opponent this week: Mike: Notice a resemblance? A smile creeps over Mikes face. But almost an unsure smile. It was the first time in his life Mike hoped he was wrong. His biggest fear was snakes. But his second fear was that God-damned Boogeyman. Dr Sherman however had to admit there was something amiss here. Dr Sherman: I see Mike. Do you think this man has simply taken the form of your worst fear? Mike: Commitment??? Dr Sherman: No Mike the boogeyman. Mike: Oh! No I don't think he's smart enough. See he calls himself the 5th Horseman. Now not only are there only 4 Horsemen but you see that's a bible reference and this guy is supposed to be an athiest. So he's contradicting himself. He thinks God is non-sense, the bible is non-sense but yet nicknames himself from the bible? The Doctor scribbled down notes. Taking it all in and searching himself for an answer for Mikes problem. Mike on the other hand wanted to know why Hate was the Boogeyman. Dr Sherman: What is this man's name? Mike: Hate. Dr Sherman: Yes you may hate him but please tell me his name. Mike: It's Hate. Dr Sherman: Yes it may feel like hate each time you see him but please I need a name. Mike: The man's name is Hate. For the first time in the meeting Dr Sherman sat back. His pen afixed yet again between his simpson colour teeth. He crossed his legs and miffed through the pen filled mouth.... Dr Sherman: Hate Mike is a feeling not a name. Tom, Tom is a name Hate is not. Mike: That's what I keep saying! Now I have a plan Shrink. I've gotten garlic, a cross and a flashlight to melt his skin with when we're in the ring so I think I'll be ok. After revealing his 'plan' to Dr Sherman Mike gives a subtle wink. He was sure it was fool-proof. Dr Sherman: That's great Mike but those instruments you'll be holding are all the ficticious cure for a vampire. Mike: Oh. What about a full moon? Dr Sherman: That causes a man to turn into a wear-wolf. And I highly doubt you could bring the moon to the ring with you. Mike: True. So what do I do about a Boogeyman? Dr Sherman: You know Mike why don't you tell me about the drug flashbacks? A change of subject. That's why he makes the big bucks. Mikes imagination had always gotten the better of him. I think Mike even knew that. On the inside he was still the 10 year old boy, his failure to grow up was his real problem. If the shrink was any good he would've come into that conclusion. Although he probably has. Mike: I don't take drugs Shrink. Dr Sherman: You once did though? Mike: Yeah I took Acid once. I didn't know what it was. Tell you once thing I'll never do it again. I saw Predator, walked out of my body, Johnny Cash sang me a lullaby and danced with the dancing Baby in my bath-tub. Dr Sherman: I see. The problem with Acid is you can get a flashback at any point in your life Mike. The acid lodes at the base of your spine and can trigger at any time. Mike: Like when I was driving my car a few weeks back? Dr Sherman: Yes. To be honest Mike the fact that you are a pro wrestler surprises me. There was a silence. Mike wasn't the most mild mannered guy, and it didn't take much more then a 'yo Mamma' to set him off. But tell him he wasn't supposed to be a wrestler was a no no. The emotional scarring from his Father not accepting his chosen profession had not healed. And never will. Mike: You think I'm not good enough? Dr Sherman: On the contrary Mr Wade. I mean your record and titles speak for themselves. He was stroking Mikes ego. Mike was a sucker for that. The only problem with this was that the good Doctor was going to tell Mike some bad news. And Mike did NOT like bad news. Dr Sherman: The reason I say that Mike is because at any time in the ring or talking in an interview you could have one of these flashbacks and hurt somebody or yourself. A fan, a fellow wrestler, your 'partner', anyone. Did you inform your employers? Mike had to be honest he didn't. Until the car accident he had never thought any more about it. Why would he? Everyone does stupid things when they're young. Hey even Dr Sherman probably got drunk one night in college and let his male roommate fondle his balls right? But what was Dr Sherman suggesting? That Mike give up wrestling? Mike: And what are you suggesting Dr Sherman? That Mike give up wrestling? Dr Sherman: Worst case scenario Mike. Is there any other occupation you could do? Mike: Well.....I have thought about it before............. Enter Imagination Mode What’ll we do babay, without us? What’ll we do babay, without us? And there ain’t nothin we can’t love each other through (ooooooooooooooohhh ooooooh) What’ll we do babay, without uuuuuuuuuussssss, Sha la la la. There was no better way to begin then with the theme from “Family Ties”. Yes it was the TV show that brought us Michael J. Fox, Mallory, Courtney Cox Arquette and some guy with a beard, but it the song means so much more. This time the song does not signify that we are about to be entertained by a classic 1980’s American cheese-com. No this time there is a deeper meaning. This time it ties in with an emotional moment in all of our lives. That’s right folks let's take a moment and reflect on what would happen if Colin Farrell’s favourite wrestler was leaving the business. Mike Wade: Stand-Up Comic Mike had always been a fan of stand up comedy. Comedy in general. Laughter is the best medicine they say. Mike never did like medicine though. Funny that. Mikes favourite ever comedian though didn't even consider himself one and insisted that he didn't even know what was funny. But this wasn’t the part of his act that interested Mike the most. See Andy, like others, created little characters for himself to play up to on stage. His character “Tony Clifton” really interested Mike. See Andy wouldn't admit that he was Tony and vice versa. He played up as if they were two seperate people. You see Andy’s obsession with wrestling bad guys influenced him to create Tony Clifton, a Vegas Lounge singer who in between songs simply tore the crowd apart with abuse, because in Andy’s view, everyone loved a bad guy!! So Mike thought to himself, he's a bad guy wrestler why not be a bad guy comedian! Black Mills Comedy Club, Dublin Ireland No smoke rose from the tables in this comedy club. Irelands smoking ban made sure of that. Mike was happy though, he was always a non smoker and didn't want to lungs filled with other peoples bad habbits. If he was going to do he would kill himself thank you very much. The tables are packed right up to the stage which is situated in the corner of the club. The people bustling and chatting away as they await the start of the show. Various shouts for Guiness and Cider are made at the bar man. A big spotlight points down on the stage where there is a stool, a table with a glass of water on it and a microphone supported in the air by a mic stand. Cheesy music starts to play from the in-house PA system and a man comes running out with a blazer on that would make James E Cornette blush. He is there to MC the night, his name was Rodger Tonic, The World's Greatest Stand Up Comic. Rodger: Welcome everyone to Black Mills comedy club. Take my wife....no seriously please take her! A huge, almost canned laughter goes up but seemingly nobody in the audience is laughing. It is because, of course, that's the oldest joke in the joke book. Rodger: So I rang up the local building firm the other day. I said to the man, I wanna skip outside my house!! And he says “Go ahead I’m not stopping you!!” The same canned laughter again with no response from the audience. Rodger: We've got some great acts for you tonight..... As Rodger does his introduction and selling of the acts that are on tonight we see Mike backstage, a ball of nerves. He sits with the back of his head lying against the dressing room door. The 'Dressing Room' at the Black Mills club in Dublin is a little different then the dressing rooms in the MEN arena in Manchester and the other great arenas Mike has wrestled in all over the world. The main difference being they weren't also doubling as lavatorys! Mike is on first. A thought he is dreading. His first comedy night. His eyes close as if in prayer that his name won't be called, maybe somehow Rodger will take his place. Unfortunately Rodger's name is flase. He is in fact Rodger Tonic the World's WORST Stand-Up comic. 'Please welcome former pro wrestler..........MIKE WADE!!!' It was time. Now Mike knew how Eminem felt in 8 Mile. Knees weak, arms were heavy. No vommit on his sweater though after Mam's Irish stew. He's nervous of course, but as usual as Eminem said on the surface he looked calm and ready. He'd just dropped a bomb in the jacks before hand. (Moms Irish stew). But wait he keeps on forgetting the jokes he wrote down. On the announcement of his name the whole crowd goes so loud. Then he forgot about Eminem, 8 Mile, B-Rabbitt and rap music all-to-fucking-gether. It was performance time. And he was a nervous wreck. Mike made his way out onto the small, small stage. There was probably a crowd of maybe 100 people. It felt like he was in Shea Stadium. And that giant audience was his Andre the Giant. How would he pick them up and powerslam them? The bright light shone in his eyes almost blinding him and for that moment taking the crowd from his sight. 'Come on!' A heckler, and this early in the show. Mike had been taught by some of the pros with how to deal with this. But then he remembered it was Rodger F'N Tonic that gave him the advice, hence why he cast it aside. Ok Mike. Time to start the act, give em the best! Mike: Take my wife.......no seriously take her! Slience. One that would rival the darkest of moments. For that split second Mike was the unfunniest man in the world. Rejection flowed through his veins, until he came up with another 'gem'. Mike: OK I got one. Why did the boy fall off his bike?? Because someone threw a fridge at him!! Deafening. The silence of course. A bead of sweat dripped down the middle of his forehead. This was it. The worst nightmare come true. 'Whoa tough crowd' He thought to himself. But wait. It was as if Mike saw light at the end of the tunnell and at the end of it stood the spirit of the man who got him into comedy. Adny Kaufman. At the sight of Andys balding, but dead head, he thought of what Andy would do. And Mike Wade turned on the Tony Clifton mode. Because remember? Everyone loves a bad guy! Time to insult these mukes! Mike sees a rather overweight Gent sitting front row, centre. Perfect target Mike: Hey you fattie in the front. Why the hell are you so fat?? Fat Man: After my Daughter and wife died in a fire I developed an eating disorder. I'm nearly dead now too. The large, rolly-polly man wiggles up from his seat and waddles out of the room. All the while crying into his fat, baseball mit of a hand. This clearly wasn't going well. It was as if his worst nightmare had come true, again! But wait. He spotted a bald man near the back of the room. One last chance for embarassment. Ok Mike make Andy proud! Mike: Hey Baldy.....! Baldy: I’ve been receiving radiotherapy treatments for a tumour the last 2 months hense why I have no hair. Do you find cancer funny? Mike: No sir. Em….. Man in crowd: Hey this guys making fun of the helpless! Next you’ll be picking on handicapped children. Mike: You mean retards? Oh I have a real good one!! What’s Helen Keller’s dogs name? Man in Crowd: You tasteless bastard. Mike: No but close, Ugrhghh!! You see because she’s retarded!! Man in Crowd: Get him!! Mike was confused. The bead have sweat had turned into a full on thunder shower of the stuff and his underpants? Well don't ask about them! This wasn't the first time he'd caused a riot. But he usually had Adam Dick behind him. However in this dream mode he suddenly realised something. Hate and the Boogeyman weren't his worst nightmare. He had discovered it. Making bad jokes, making fun of fat people, cancer sufferers and retards and thus causing a riot in a comedy club. That was his worst fear. [/Imagination Mode] The dream sequence was over. Mike had gotten a little taste of what his life would be like without the disorganised but legalised chaos that was wrestling. Let me tell you it tasted like some form of gone off cock cheese, not nice at all. Mikes eyes opened, awakening from his imaginatory dream. Dr Herman Sherman was sitting straight again. His legs crossed. He fumbled with his pen, tossing it in and out between the fingers of his left hand, which was aching. A quick flash of his note pad revealled pages of writing. Writing cramp was bad, worst then wankers cramp because there's no pleasure in the end and you have to listen to Mike Wades dreams of being a comedian. There was a silence for a second. The dream sequence had taught Mike not to fear the Hate/ Boogeyman combo. But did he realise that? Dr Sherman knew that Wade should know. But didn't know if Wade knew. The silence was to figure out who knew what........ If you got that! The silence was broken by a relaxed voiced Dr Sherman. Dr Sherman: Well Mike? What did we learn? Mike: Don't slag off fatties cause they could be dying. Dr Sherman: And what else? Mike: And not to......... He had to interrupt there. Because this could go on for a while. There were so many things to learn there. But one really important thing. Dr Sherman: And I'm not referring to that poor sick man or the mentally handicapped kids you 'jeered'. I'm talking about the big lesson. The one about your fear! Mike: Oh yeah!! Eurica! He had it. Mike Wade had figured something out. He knew in his head it was worth the money. Dr Herman Sherman, although his name wasn't Herman Munster was STILL very cool Mike: That standing in a comedy club in Dublin telling jokes that aren't going over must be silimar to what Hate feels every night when he walks through the curtain to no reaction and people wanting him to be dead because he uses a stale over used gimmick and non sensical name! Shrink you're the best! Well it wasn't quite the lesson but whatever. Mike had bolted out of the room, but not before hugging Dr Sherman. Mike never hugged other men. Why would he? They had no tits. Dr Sherman wasn't dejected with his wasted day though. Mike had paid for a 60 minute session. And after 43 gruelling minutes Mike bolted leaving the 'Shrink' with 17 minutes of well earned rest. Mike ran out the door of his Shrinks office and into the waiting arms of little Mickey Moore. Mickey, Mikes ever present heterosexual life-partner, had been waiting for his friend. Mikes shit eating grin said it all. The meeting had been a success. And Mickey knew it. His question anticipated the answered itself. Mickey: You got on well then m'laddie? Mike: That's right. I realised I'm not a pathetic fucking loser but Hate is! Mickey: Ah tis great laddie. Guess what? Mike: What? Mickey: I got ya a little pressie for bein such a good boy. Mikes excitement couldn't take it. He was like a small child. It was 3 weeks to Christmas. In Mikes house no presents were received even 2 months before Christmas. Mike eagerly outstretched his hand. He needed the present or his excitement would over-rule his excrement and thus ruin his pants. Mickey: Here ya go laddie. It has a double meaning! Mickey then presented Mike with a bag of Sand. A 'Sand-Bag' if you will. Mike: What the? Mickey: Turn it round. The message is 'This guy is a sand-bagger!' Mickey giggled. He loved his little effort at being controversial. Even if the joke was only between him and Mike. Mike, now less giddy at the look of his shit present spun the sand bag around to reveal the 'sandbagger'.............................
