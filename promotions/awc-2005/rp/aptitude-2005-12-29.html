Aptitude

29 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/05 Anton smashed his shin into the man's leg, buckling his knees. The other man, a pale man with black hair and wearing red trunks, fell to his knees. He was a leanly muscular man, looking very suited to this kind of quick fighting, but he was seemingly at the mercy of Anton Assault. They were in a four-sided cage of mesh in the California valley, an outdoor deal at night. An illegal tough-man competition. Luis was keen on keeping Anton's skills up. Fires burned all around and a multi-racial group looked on at the action inside the ring. It was a very… communal event, bringing people together. Bloodsport was always like that, since Roman times. Just because swords and shields had been foregone in favor of bare fists didn't change it. People wanted to see combat, to see blood, to see one person victorious in a test of strength. Luis Ferrara, for his part, didn't just want to see one person victorious. He wanted to see Anton victorious. After all, Anton was Luis's meal ticket. Sweat rolled off of the well-built African's dark skin as he took a step towards the fallen fighter. Cheers rose up from the crowd as he sent a low punch rocketing straight to the man's jaw, knocking him into the sand. The man landed stomach-first on the ground with a grunt, clawing at the sand. Anton climbed onto his back and wrapped an arm around his neck, slipping his other under the man's arm. He wrenched back in the modified camel clutch and the man's arms flailed helplessly. Anton was nowhere near a wrestling contract, not yet. It had been three weeks since Anton and Luis's spectacular escape from South America. He had, however, been training a bit in professional wrestling. He did all he could to practice wrestling moves, including more elaborate submissions. He was always a fan of submission fighting and employed it to great effect in his mixed martial arts career. Now that he was going to enter a medium where more complex—and, therefore, painful—submissions were readily accepted, he was eager to use them. The pale man's arms flailed wildly and he beat the sand repeatedly, signaling his submission. Anton scowled at the quick finish, tugging upwards to bring the man to his knees. He still held the submission hold in, however, and he dropped backwards, flinging his opponent overhead in a makeshift suplex. The man landed head-first in the sand before crumpling in a heap. Anton had only had time to try out a wristlock fireman's carry and the camel clutch. Annoying. A wiry-looking man in a Hawaiian-print shirt unlocked the cage door and Anton rose to his feet once again, walking outside. He was met with a dozen or so appreciative spectators, mostly looking like betters who had placed their money on him. Anton gave them all a polite nod before heading over towards Luis. Luis was chatting it up with three women in fluent Spanish, no doubt telling them how much money he had, how luxurious his life was, doing all he could to divert conversation away from Anton himself. Anton got his share of Latin women, though, so he didn't much mind this. As Anton walked towards them, Luis turned and beamed at him. He was wearing a white collared shortsleeved shirt and black shorts, the whole ensemble accompanied by sandles and a goofy looking red beret. Luis smiled and clapped Anton on the shoulder. ''So, Lion,'' Luis said in lazy Spanish; he had been drinking. ''What do you say to a fivesome? Us two and these fine young ladies.'' Anton surveyed them and they all struck poses for the fighter. They were all scantily-clad, something that struck Luis just right. ''If I can see their venereal disease reports,'' Anton said, glancing back at his manager. ''Where did you pick them up?'' The three of them looked slightly offended, but shortly their interests were back on Anton's muscles. They chattered quietly amongst themselves as Anton drew Luis aside. ''When next am I training?'' Luis shoved Anton and gestured towards the Latinas, but Anton shook his head and snapped his fingers. Luis kicked some sand at the Nigerian and snorted. ''Always wanna fuckin' work, huh?'' Luis sneered. ''I wanna fuckin' fuck, understand?'' He glanced over at the women and then jabbed Anton in the chest. ''If you're so insistent, though, it's tomorrow. Afternoon session, says he'll be working you on some technical stuff. You're going right with a specialist group 'cause you already know how to fight, so the guy figures that he doesn't need to spend ages with you on the basics. Which is just as well, 'cause I'm shopping around for a deal and I don't need to be telling them that you'll be ready in so-and-so weeks. Gotta be ready now.'' Anton nodded and took a look over at the Latina women again. His trainer, a heavyset man known as Rude Rand Baker, was a despot. Anton needed that, though. He needed to learn and an accomodating trainer would not point out his mistakes so brusquely. ''I'm taking the one in the middle,'' Anton declared. ''Alone.'' Luis's eyes seemed like they were going to pop right out of his head and he slammed his hand on Anton's shoulder. ''Fuck you, Lion!'' Luis snapped in a harsh whisper. ''I want the one in the middle! You can't fucking do that!'' Anton shrugged. ''They don't want you,'' Anton said calmly. ''So you can take the other two or you can get shit. Your decision.'' Luis ran his hand through his black hair irritably, but he started over to them without saying anything to the contrary. Anton needed to relax after the fight. He always found a woman's attentions to be quite soothing. Anton rose from the ground, rubbing his jaw. It was canvas this time and he was inside, standing at one side of a four-cornered wrestling ring. In a corner opposite from him, a tall, powerful-looking man stood, dark brown hair falling over a clean-shaven face. He rubbed his wrists and waited for Anton to rise. On the apron stood a fat man who looked as if he had four-hundred pounds spread over 6 feet and 5 inches, if not more. The red haired man was Rude Rand and he simply chewed his cigar, arm on the rope and inspecting. As he rose, the other wrestler—a man named Matt Fawkins—started towards him quickly. The only reason that Anton was on the ground at all was that Rand had told him, rather bluntly, to ''cut the kung-fu shit, this is a fuckin' wrestling school!'' Anton had abided, but he had paid for it. He knew far less actual wrestling than had been apparent before. Fawkins neared him and Anton soared upwards, twisting in the air and catching him around the neck before dragging him down in a sloppy snap-mare. Anton had let go of the throw too early, leaving Fawkins to drop on his head; the move looked more like a bastardized ace crusher than the move it should have been. Rand had seen the sloppy manuever and steped through the middle and top ropes, standing in the ring. Anton got to his feet rather quickly and turned to face his trainer. Baker was upon him quickly, though, and his only warning was ''THIS is a proper snap mare!'' Baker threw a rough knee into Anton's stomach and then grabbed him about the neck, hurling him onto the canvas in one smooth motion. Anton landed with a thick smack! and considered his lesson learned. He had to tighten up his game. Rude Rand stood over Anton and put his hands on his hips. ''What the hell are you doing in the ring?'' Rand asked him. He had asked Anton that question several times. Anton did not have too much of an answer, but he made it a habit not to speak anyway. ''On your feet, my friend, I want to see some real wrestling. Use anything you know except for all that Hong Kong bullshit. Obviously snap mares are not your forte.'' Anton climbed to his feet slowly, resting in the corner. No, snap mares were not his forte. He never understood the purpose of snap mares. That wasn't the issue, though. He needed to figure out snap-mares, he needed to master them. He couldn't afford to let any part of his repertoire be weak. Fawkins rolled out of the ring and a recognized veteran amongst their ranks, a stocky Mexican named Lawrence Murra, slipped into the ring. Murra was very well-versed in the basics, making him revered amongst the others. Anton, however, did not partake in idol-worship. Rand slapped the turnbuckle to signal that they should begin and Murra took off. Sensing an opening, Murra wrapped his arms around Anton with a waistlock and hurled him overhead, executing a perfect belly-to-belly suplex. Anton hit the mat and rolled through up to a knee, startling Murra as he turned around. As Lawrence started in, Anton spun around and nailed a quick sweeping drop toe hold. He sprang forward and applied a crossace armbar, wrenching back and setting Murra's legs to kicking frantically. ''Let him go!'' Rand hollered. ''I want to see what you can do, not how fast you can cripple my best wrestler!'' Anton complied with a small frown, rising to his feet and taking three steps back from Lawrence. Murra slowly got to his feet and Anton was on him again, tossing him overhead with a quick Northern Lights suplex. Lawrence's back slapped the canvas and the Lion rushed him, locking him in a full nelson with body scissors. After a few seconds, Rand once again called for the break. ''No more submissions, you fucker!'' Lawrence Murra and Anton Assault got to their feet and locked up. Anton forced the lock-up forward until one arm was slipped around Murra's neck and his other hand was forced up against Murra's chin. Anton stepped past Murra's leg and swept his own backwards, tripping Lawrence up and sending him crashing to the mat with a legsweep. Anton got up and moved away, fists at the ready. The problem with Lawrence Murra and most of the other trainees is that they were not prepared for a fast, high-pace style. They didn't know quite how to deal with it, what to dow ith it, and Anton used this to his advantage. He was relentless, even with all of his restrictions. The other guys were content to lumber around looking for a few big moves in hopes that a couple hundreds would put the other guy away. Anton was completely content to fire off hundreds of twos or threes to keep them down. Murra got to his feet rather slowly, wincing a bit as he moved. Anton rushed him, lifting his feet not more than a foot off the ground and tilting him backwards for a quick spinebuster. He mounted him then, pummeling him with frantic rights and lefts. ''Get off him!'' came the harsh warning from Rude Rand. ''This ain't the fucking UFC, you idiot!'' Anton reluctantly got to his feet, a bit annoyed that Rand wasn't letting him fight Murra the way he wished. Now Rand was simply protecting Murra. Lawrence shakily got to his feet and grasped the top rope, trying to keep himself steady. Anton sped towards him and spun around upon reaching him, gripping him by the neck and throwing him over in a cruel parody of a snap mare: once again, Anton had released too early and let Murra hit the canvas head-first. Scowling, Anton got to his feet, wondering what he was getting wrong. The thumping of heavy boots on the mat instantly caught Anton's attention and the African whirled, only to see Rude Rand charging at him. Anton put his arms up defensively, but Rand was still able to knock him wholesale outside the ring with a running forearm. ''You're a stupid motherfucker,'' Rand called out at him, pointing. Anton had managed to block his face from hitting the apron with his arms and now stood on his feet, a bit shaken from the fall. ''You can do everything but a fuckin' snap mare. You're done for today. Get out of here.'' Anton looked up at Rand, fuming. Anton needed to train, and if his ineptitude at executing a snap-mare wasn't enough proof, Anton didn't know what was. Anton needed a strong teacher, but Rand was just a taskmaster. He wasn't teaching anything at this point. ''Won't come back,'' Anton muttered in thickly-accented English. He turned on his heel and started for the door, snatching up his black duffle bag of gear as he walked out. Luis, who had been watching from a short distance, glanced up disapprovingly at Rand and followed his client out of the building. Rand shook his head and turned back to his other pupils, directing two others to get into the ring. Neither party was concerned with what the other was doing. ''You've got a fight later tonight,'' Luis told him in Spanish. Anton looked back at his manager and shook his head, stepping into the parking lot. He looked for Luis's new red Jaguar, intent on getting out of here as quickly as possible. ''You're ready, eh?'' ''You told me to think about wrestling,'' Anton replied. He spotted the car, walking towards it and adjusting the duffle bag strap on his shoulder. Luis quickened his pace to catch up with Anton. ''Why don't you think about wrestling? Book me in a match. Let's quit these fights, hm?'' Luis scowled, shaking his head. ''No, man, we still need the money,'' Luis said. ''You keep doing fights and I'll get you a few wrestling matches while I talk to some big money people, okay? But for now… yeah, for now, you're fighting. Can't lose those greenbacks, can we?'' Anton shook his head as Luis produced the key, unlocking the car. The muscular African opened the trunk and tossed his black bag inside. He shut the trunk and moved to the passenger's seat, slipping into the car. This was what he had Luis for, after all. He needed someone to manage the financial aspect of everything. Anton still disliked Luis's lack of focus.
