'Coach Idol - The Prequel'... And for your sake I hope it's the end of it as well

7 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th February 2006 So what did you wasters do this week? ... ... Guys? Chuck Norton: Yes... What did you wasters get up to this week? Hash Brown Bridges: Please do not refer to us as wasters. Oh grow up. You're lucky I'm still able to talk. I think I'm hitting my limit. Chuck Norton: Your limit? Yes, my limit. Russ Bridges: What limit? My Universal Narrator Words In A Promo Limit. Chuck Norton: That doesn't exist, have you read some of Adam Dick's work? He must have about ten different narrators on stuff like that. Of course it exists. You simply forget to realise the number of drafts we have already tried before getting this far. Chuck Norton: Oh yeah. So basically at some point I will simply run out of “word credit” and you two will have to go it alone. Russ Bridges: That won’t be for a while, right? Hopefully, but you never know. It could stop at any… … Chuck Norton: Bradley? Russ Bridges: Maybe he hit his limit. Chuck Norton: You think? Russ Bridges: He wouldn’t just stop… Of course he hit the limit. Chuck Norton: Oh dear God. What do we do? Russ Bridges: I don’t know. Go it alone? Chuck Norton: I got it. One of us should go round the back and pretend to be a voice from the heavens and therefore pretend to Bradley or maybe even God. Russ Bridges: Or we could come up with our own idea… Chuck Norton: Such as? … … *snigger* Russ Bridges: How do I get round the back? Chuck Norton: What was that? Russ Bridges: What was what? Chuck Norton: That noise… It sounded like someone sniggering… hehe… Russ Bridges: When? Chuck Norton: There it was again… Russ Bridges: There what was? Chuck Norton: Someone’s laughing at us! Russ Bridges: Who? Chuck Norton: Ssh! Listen… … … … Russ Bridges: There is obviously no-one there. hehe… Chuck Norton: You see there it was again… Russ Bridges: How can I see laughter? HAHAHAHAHA! Russ Bridges: Okay, now I hear it… HAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Chuck Norton: Bradley? HAHAHA- Yes? Chuck Norton: What are you doing? I was playing a joke on you two. Chuck Norton: I didn’t find it funny. Russ did you find it funny? Russ Bridges: No. Chuck Norton: Then clearly it wasn’t a joke as no-one found it funny. I did. Chuck Norton: You don’t count… Okay… Chuck Norton: Now ask your questions, we have things to be doing… Why? What do you have to do? Russ Bridges: We are reviewing the applicants for our new coach. Good. You guys needs a coach. Russ Bridges: *cough* Excuse me? You guys really need a coach. It’ll be good to see you get proper wrestling training. Russ Bridges: *cough* Did you not see who won the Fatal Four-Way last week? I did. Okay, you got lucky. But at least now you will have a proper coach. Chuck Norton: Oooh… I like her! Who? Chuck Norton: The first applicant I’ve just looked at. She’s 23, blonde, very attractive. What kind of qualifications does she have? Chuck Norton: None… But she’d look good on screen. Russ Bridges: Let me see… Well at least you guys have the right idea for the perfect coach. Russ Bridges: How many how applicants did we get? 300? Chuck Norton: Erm, hold on… Chuck reaches down into the bottom of the… Chuck Norton: What are you doing? Narrating. Chuck Norton: Why? It’s what I’m here to do. Chuck Norton: I know, but it’s putting me off. Just be quiet. Will do. Russ Bridges: So how many applicants did we get? Chuck Norton: Seven. Russ Bridges: Seven? Chuck Norton: Just seven. Russ Bridges: Maybe the rest are outside. Chuck Norton: Nope. I checked. We got seven. Russ Bridges: Seven… How many have coaching qualifications? Chuck Norton: One. Russ Bridges: One? Chuck Norton: Yup. Mr. Marshall. Russ Bridges: Mr. Marshall? Chuck Norton: He is a former wrestling coach at a high school. Russ Bridges: Wow. He must be good. Chuck Norton: Yeah, but the other six are models. Very, very, very attractive models. That’s a tough decision you guys have. You have to decide whether getting laid is more important than winning the Alliance Belts and getting even more laid. Chuck Norton: I thought I told you to be quiet. Russ Bridges: But Bradley does make a good point. If we win the Alliance Belts, more ladies will want us. *pondering* *and a little fantasying* Chuck Norton: So you’re saying we should employ this guy Mr. Marshall? Yup. Chuck Norton: Fair enough… Russ Bridges: Wait a minute! I have an idea! Oh god no! Russ Bridges: What if we sell the rights for “Coach Idol” to Fox? We can write the theme tune and loads of the music for the show… Chuck Norton: I like it… I don’t. Russ Bridges: We get loads of exposure and then a record deal. If Clay Aiken can do it, we can! Chuck Norton: Damn right! But what about Celestial Fury? Chuck Norton: Who? You’re opponent on Fresh! Chuck Norton: What about them? You have to face two well-trained wrestlers on Fresh! Russ Bridges: Ssh! You are disturbing our creative juices… But what about the match? You guys need to get a coach now. Chuck Norton: We’ll wing it… Wing it? Russ Bridges: I’m undefeated. How difficult can it be? Famous last words. Russ Bridges: What? Nothing. I’m going to leave you guys to prepare your pitch for Fox Network.
