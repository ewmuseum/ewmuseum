Seems like the Wrath of the Gods got a punch on the nose and it started to flow; I think I might be sinking.

27 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: here Breathe. Breathe. Keep going. Good. Repeat. I imagine this would be more embarrassing if I were actually speaking aloud, but how often does one have to remind themselves to breathe? It’s a motor function. Breathing is something you can control, yet isn’t in constant need of your attention. This aspect as always fascinated me, as any responsibility I have ever had has always turned sour in the end unless I tended to that specifically. To better explain it, I take life one obstacle at a time. If there are too many things to concentrate on, I get frustrated and fall short. Tasks in my care deserve my singular attention, or else bad things happen. Or things don’t happen. So you can imagine the small amount of respect I’d carry for anything that can keep me alive without me having to deal with it. And it’s also at times like these where I thank myself and my brain for its’ ability to ponder over the most insignificant shit. Jesus, sometimes I think A.D.D. is a blessing. In case you can’t catch on, I haven’t had to think about breathing for the past few moments. Though that I reminded myself, now I’ll likely have to keep up at it again. I just keep the wheel turning and I’ll be able to compose myself. Don’t think about the bad, I’ve taught myself. Think about the good. When you first joined me, at the top of the page, I was in the midst of a panic attack. I was having trouble breathing as the shock of the whole day had just hit me. I thought I was trying out for wrestling school, so it’s hard to imagine why I’d be here at this exact time, but when I try to recall the event my mind becomes a blur. It’s the culmination of smoking too much pot, and facing moral dilemmas that compromise your character in turn creating events one would rather not think about. At least, that’s what I figured. Editor’s Note: I have no idea what the fuck he’s talking about. Eddy Estrada tries to snap me in to place. This is a situation I’ve been in over a thousand times; where people talk to me but the blank expression on my face seems to convince them I’m not listening. When in fact, I am listening and examining what is said, while figuring out various other items on my plate for the time being. I get in these trances, and I talk to myself about what has happened, what is happening now, and what I need to do. People tend to fuck my whole process up. Eddy then brings me back in to the now, and almost makes me forget to breathe again. He tells me that we’ve got to get out of wherever the hell we are. He says our mission went sour, that someone fucked something up and we have to leave before we get noticed. I can’t even remember what mission he’s talking about, aside from the fact that I’m looking for a book. I don’t know how many people look for books in evicted Church buildings, but obviously this is a weird situation that I can never fully grasp. I can only hope that the following section from the third-person perspective can clear things up. My mind is too rattled to keep you up-to-date. So, have fun watching… Editor’s Note: The preceding section was placed to evoke emotion in the following piece. Now that the stage has been set, the reader can grasp the subject’s state of mind in entering this rather difficult situation, as well as comprehend the feelings of those he affects. The character is unique, and all emotions that associate with him ascertain that status as well. The Illustrious Face-Eater sits facing Edward, his back against the pew they both hid behind. Eddy was persistent in monitoring their safety, looking over the decrepit benches that lined the floor of Congregation Hall. Night had faded long ago, and the glow of the moon was long since absent in creeping through the holes in the roof. They stood clouded in the large room, fearing any movement could create a stir in the pitch black of sound. This would cause attention, something they didn’t need. ”I really needed that book. I really needed that book.” Eddy says to himself, still not catching the ear of the Face-Eater who sat right next to him. Eddy really couldn’t justify it to himself, how the Face-Eater constantly ignored his comments. It was a slow realization to the point of NOW, but it was here that Eddy figured to connect to Facey through brash statements that actually forced him to face his conflict. Facey was always a person conflict whenever he was at fault. But when a wrong is perpetrated against him, let’s just say it is hard to keep the man quiet. Eddy, in this situation, is a pacifist. So it is easy to see how easily their roles at present can be reversed. It’s all a matter of how long we watch. Editor’s Note: Here, the author makes light of such character-common qualities that will be utilized, possibly in the near future. ”I really wish I would have gotten to know you before, now.” Eddy says, and Facey can do nothing but breathe and stare. He still decided to vent, keeping whatever sharp words he’d thought of behind his tongue for the time being. He’d wait until he had the Face-Eater’s fully pledged attention. “I wish that I knew you were incapable of keeping look-out. I wish that you had the common sense to warn follow your gut instincts. And I really wish that you could keep your fucking-“ Eddy slapped the side of the Face-Eater’s head, continuing his speech. “-fucking HEAD IN THE GAME.” Editor’s Note: Here, the author utilizes a section mentioned in the monologue. A feeling of continuity is established within the piece, as well as aforementioned writings within the character’s library. The impact caused a stir, and quite a kick of dust swirled around. Facey was brought back in the moment and could only look at his future mentor with unforgiving eyes. “Jesus, I CAN fucking hear you man…” he muttered, rubbing the side of his cheek. Eddy peaked over to see the bench again to see if his rambunctious-ness had revealed their position to those looking for them. The church was empty, and after staying hidden for a considerable time, Eddy found it best to leave at this moment. He kicked Facey’s leg, and just as quick darted to his feet, as if to step on the human-form-crutch to get a quicker step. Facey took a moment to catch on, finally falling out of his trance, somewhat frustrated that he didn’t have the chance to finish his thoughts. ”Dude, what the fuck is going on?” he whispers audible enough to get over the loud thump of footsteps that echoed. Eddy merely laughed at his inquisitive nature. To him, it was all to clear and one had to be an idiot or a jackass-making-a-stupid-joke to not understand their plight. He slowed down a minute to give his makeshift partner a chance to catch up, as well as provide a more personal medium to vent his anger. ”Is there something I should know about you? Do you have some aliment that prevents you from using your brain fully?” Facey’s expression stay confused as he kept up his pace, the two darting past the main doors and through the halls that aligned the Congregation Hall, allowing access to all main parts of the large building. The corridors followed all the way around the hall, giving the two plenty of running time to escape whatever fate their captors intended. ”Look, I’m sorry! I just can’t seem to grip this situation fully; what the hell are we running for?” ”We’re running-“ Eddy was quick to cut off Facey in his pre-disposed speech. “-because we don’t want our TESTICLES CHOPPED OFF!” At first, the Illustrious Face-Eater did not know why, and rightfully decided to complain when Eddy had tackled him to the ground without warning, making them both come to a painful stop. Editor’s Note: Damn you, inertia! ”What the HELL is your pro-“ The sound of a gunshot silenced him as he felt a the quickest gust fly by. ”You didn’t hear the first one?” Eddy asked him, using Facey’s chest as support to get up. ”There was a first one?” The Face-Eater replied. This wasn’t rhetorical, there was no time when people were shooting through the thin hallways that separated the church. Eddy pulled Facey down the remaining few feet of the corridor, keeping a low head and pulling him outside past a set of double doors. Once reaching a patch of grass, the Face-Eater found himself at his feet, following Eddy who ran at an accelerated pace. ”How did you know there was going to be a gun shot, Ed?” Facey asked. Eddy slowed in his pace, once again allowing Facey to keep up. Wiping the sweat off his forehead with his shirt, he turned around to see if anyone had been following us down the hall. While no one had caught on, yet, it was only a small matter of time until there was another person hot on their trail with a gun on their hip. ”I didn’t ‘know,’ I just—you didn’t hear the first one? I’m sure there was a first one.” The Face-Eater didn’t know what to say, nor did he have time to respond before seeing a faceless and nameless drone sent after him, catching a good view of the duo and where they were heading. Facey kept looking as he saw the goon relay their coordinates to his friends, and then finally dart in their direction in an attempt to keep up. ”They’re on to us. What should we do?” Facey asked. At this point, Eddy wasn’t exactly struck with ideas, half-baked or not. Instead he decided to increase his speed and leave his pupil in the dust. ”I’ll see you at base-camp, bro. Good luck!” And with that, he must have turned on the negro-muscles in his body because he was out of sight quicker than the fucking Roadrunner. Needless to say, that the Face-Eater wasn’t left with any options. All he had was a hick with gun gaining grown with every step they took, and a group of homos just like him waiting for a piece of whatever this guy couldn’t handle. These are the times where the Illustrious Face-Eater gets struck with that overwhelming sense of panic; the kind that manic-depressive single-mothers get before offing their babies in a bath-tub. Editor’s Note: The Author has no prejudice against manic-depressives, single mothers, or babies. Just manic-depressive single mothers who murder babies. And even that’s sort of on the fly. “Shit.” He thinks. “Shit. I’m fucked.” His pace slows. His steps grow in length. And that’s when he hears, ”Relax, I’m here. ”What the fuck?” He says aloud, many more thoughts with that same theme occurring in his brain simultaneously. That little voice in his head, it says: ”I’ve been here for the better half of this whole escapade. How long has the day been done? Not to mention, the clouds are all out.” He thinks to himself, Well why didn’t she show earlier. ”I thought I’d be nice and let you two play espionage. Besides, this whole plot seems rather ridiculous; enough to the point where I choose to stay until the end. The voice in the Face-Eater’s mind was soft, exactly what he wanted to hear. This whole situation was created out of his own defense mechanism; a culmination of all of Facey’s fears put in to a form of coping. A girlfriend, isn’t that what everyone needs? A girlfriend, however, that catered to every mental anxiety. Now that’s something that can’t be taken with ‘unlimited understanding’. People have limits. Imaginations, however, do not. Editor’s Note: At this point, the author tries to justify the character’s mental aliment with his own situation. It’s almost as if imaginary friends were actually productive. It is at this point, where the Face-Eater’s mind drifted from how tired he was from running, to trying to discover any means of escape. The words, ‘Anything in mind?’ began to echo rather loudly to his girlfriend, who still stay hidden in the recesses of his brain yet spoke very clearly. ”You should head towards the Children’s area.” She says, and Face-Eater finds himself turning his head to face the direction of said area. ”Go between the trees. They’ll have trouble gaining ground on you, if you do.” So the Face-Eater runs his little heart out. Running at his highest capable speed through a grassy patch, he turns to see a man still in pursuit of him, with a larger group of people way behind him. At this point, the group catching up to him seemed implausible, so he didn’t stress himself as much. Their path now cutting through a makeshift forest, the Face-Eater did his best at avoiding every branch that stuck out at him, keeping his head low. It wasn’t long before he came out on the other side of the small patch of trees, now facing a large section of the church that was once designated for kids. The area was centered by a building, pale in comparison to Congregation hall, but large enough to contain the interests of small children. The building was sealed around its’ parameter by a thin fence, which also sectioned off a small playground for the kids. ”Hop this fence, and run through the sand.” the Voice told the Face-Eater. So he obliged, using the most of his strength to slingshot himself over the fence rather quickly. He ran through the sand in the playground, leaving a trail of footsteps behind as he ran along the fence and around the backend of the building, on to a concrete path. ”Follow this around the backside. Head back to the playground. And the Face-Eater did not falter from the stated plan. Following the Voice’s directions, he ran around the building’s backside and again found himself standing at the head of the playground. ”Don’t touch ANY sand. Get on to the playground.” the Face-Eater found himself at the edge of the concrete path that separated the Children’s building from the playground. There was a gap of sand between the concrete and playground, one which Facey had no trouble hopping over. ”Now hide in the slide.” As Facey ran up the stairs of the playground, across the intentionally-rickety bridge, he approached the tube-shaped slide that twisted and turned until it reached the bottom. As he came to, he found that the bearer of the Voice, his girlfriend, was waiting for him inside. the Face-Eater jumped in the slide, not sliding all the way to the bottom and careful to keep all protruding parts on his body within the confines of the slide. The way Face-Eater and his girlfriend lay entwined within the slide, it was an inclination of things to come. ”Boy, I sure am glad to see your face-“ ”Shhh. We don’t want him to hear you.” As she finished her thought, her words were muffled by the sound of quick footsteps and cracking leaves. The grass that crunched under the footsteps did so in quick succession, indicating that whoever was here was in a hurry. There was no way for them to see outside the slide, but the man was falling for what trick they played. He looked through the fence, examining the footsteps that ran through the playground sand. For a moment, he cursed to himself, then talked on the microphone. ”We lost one, repeat, we lost one. He’s out on the streets, over.” A static, muffled reply. ”I didn’t see where the other took off to, but I was in pursuit of the other when he ran through the Children’s area, and there’s no doubt he’s on the public streets on the other side, over.” Once again, there is a static, muffled reply. “Roger that. I’m going to keep after him. Head back to base-camp and I’ll notify you guys if we find the book thief.” It was that last statement that rung through the Face-Eater’s ears that caused his own confusion. Despite the face of Safety rearing its head through Solitude, Facey couldn’t help but think about what book they were talking about as he heard their pursuant take the broken lead, following the edge of the playground to the back and finally out of sight forever. ”What book? Eddy says we didn’t get the book. Eddy says the mission was failed.” Face-Eater can only think to himself. Editor’s Note: Eddy never knew that Face-Eater had the book the whole time, a topic that will be touched on in the near future. Facey begins to panic; doubt begins to swell in his mind and for some reason, the entire situation seems bigger than himself for a second. ”Take a deep breath. You’ve got this down, it isn’t hard. All you’ve got to do… is read.” At first, the Face-Eater doesn’t want to listen to her voice, but it is very compelling. Finally swallowing what ridiculous pride he has, Face-Eater withdraws, very surprisingly, a small leather-bound journal from his back pocket. There is a position, a page, saved in the book by the accompanied red ribbon right in the middle. Face-Eater found the red ribbon a suitable position to pick up reading. And when he opened the page, he didn’t exactly know what to make of it. But beneath all the big words that read out this fucked up situation, the strangest feeling of Déjà vu came over him. The book read: And He who shall compromise the fall of mankind, will be saved by his own blood. The smallest of sacrifices will reveal our character for what it truly is. Bound by immortal words that have yet to pass, the Morning Star shall bring upon the world’s demise through inadvertent actions. Yeah, whatever the hell that means. Editor’s Note: It means that Eddy Estrada did not hear a first gunshot when he saved the Face-Eater’s life. It means that Eddy Estrada knew that bullet was coming the entire time. Now one must truly ask, how can one predict the future?
