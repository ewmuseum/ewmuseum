Date: Aug 14/15 06 Is it possible I hate her already? It's been less than a day. 'Not hungry?' 'No.' I just can't stand to watch you eat. Hah. Oh my God those people are staring at her. STOP EATING LIKE A PIG! I can't believe I just married this girl, that's supposed to be my wedding cake you're making a mess of. I should say that. 'This good cake.' 'Great.' Now please stop making a scene. Those old people are talking about her. 'What're you looking at?' 'Gibber jabber.' There they go with the fucking Russian. I should have left after the show. Oh my dear God I should have left after the show, what the hell was I thinking? The vodka here is fucking crazy. What do I do? How can I get out of this, think Johnny, think. There's got to be a way to ditch her, I mean marriage can only be legally binding in most of the major countries. Mexico wasn't so bad, aside from the heart burn, I actually liked it. Oh no. Don't smile at me, damnit. She is hot, I love those legs. Stop it. No Johnny. Bad Johnny! We have to get out of this. Call Dave. He knows all that legal jargon, until then maybe I can ditch her in the bathroom. I hid from the world for five years I can hide from a crazy Russian chick for a few weeks. I could be on a plane in twenty minutes. Fifteen if I leave right now. What could we possibly have in common? She hardly speaks my language. Mind you that's pretty hot. No. It's bad, she belongs here with her family and her culture, I work on the road, it would be for her own good. 'Is good, you have some.' 'I'm okay, really Talaina.' I just have to find a way to leave this country and never come back without bringing you with me. Oh stop it, stop smiling at me like that, damnit! No! 'You want go fuck?' 'Hell yeah.' I am the Achilles of modern man. Born, bred, and trained to be stronger, faster, smarter. We have rebuilt him. We had the technology, and he is wonderful. I am the lion and this is my savannah. I must fight for it, defend it, with my life. With this power, Johnny, comes great responsability. The responsability to own up on noobs, and to look good doing it. This is your mission, should you choose to accept it, Agent Lexicon. But know that without you we are lost, you are the only man for the job. I ask my fed not who's asses I can kick, but who they think can kick mine. I. Have a dream. 'Hee hee. Stop it.' 'What?' 'Stop looking in mirror.' 'Hey you married me, this mirror may never get to see me again.' 'Haha, come, tell me about your job.' Holy shit my job. Fucking Christ she is warm. Suple. Funny word that suple. 'Johnny, your job.' 'My job. Right. I wrestle.' 'Wrestle?' 'Yeah, I perform on a stage, I fight.' 'I know wrestle. I know Pierce Lavelle.' Everybody knows Pierce Lavelle. 'You know Sentice Vargas?' 'No.' She even pouts sexy. 'You tell me.' 'Alright. I know he's smaller than me, likes the technical side of things.' 'Teckneecale?' 'Yeah, he likes to put on holds and submissions. I know he hasn't won very many matches, this could mean I'm in for either a hell of a fight or a walk in the park. I have to expect that he's hungry for victory and that he's scouted my style. I also know he thinks himself a powerhouse, he's gonna try to wear me down with holds and reversals and then hit me with something high impact.' 'You know much.' 'That's part of my job. Because I know this I know that in order to win I have to go in striking, keep moving and keep it fast. Start working on his arms early, take away his power and I can take away his game entirely. The rest is easy, just going through the motions. You don't understand half of what I'm saying do you?' 'I know you are big, strong, wrestler man.' 'Good enough for me.' 'Keep talking, I like how you talk. Your voice sexy.' 'What should I talk about?' 'Talk teckneecale again, you tell me of Sentice Vargas.' 'Well. Alright his finisher. He's either gonna clean my clock with a super kick or try to go big with a top rope power bomb. Since I'm bigger the top rope bomb isn't gonna come unless he's desperate and I'm worked. The super kick is harder for me to see coming, if he's done his homework he'll know I never quit so.' 'You never quit?' 'I can't. Not in there. When I had nothing I had that. I'm a lousy friend, a horrible father, a conceited lover, but I'm a great wrestler. I've lost family, money, had my company taken away and cheated out by a woman. It's the only thing in this world that I truly have, wrestling is the only thing I've ever been really good at.' 'I know something you very good at.' 'Well that's.' Yeah I know. 'So you are very good wrestler?' The best. 'Better than the average. I fight Vargas tomorrow, you can come and watch.' 'I will watch you fight. Watch you win.' 'That's the plan.' 'Is good plan.' It is a good plan. But after Vargas, then who? This isn't like before, there's always been somebody next. somebodies always stepped to the plate to take me out for whatever reason, but deep down it's because we're all addicted to the challenge. I need to find a challenge, Vargas maybe. I just don't know, I can't pick fights with everybody. Love to smack that Afeaki guy a bunch, Marqez needs me to make him see he's better, just the thought of showing up Lavelle. Ugh. I need to find a direction, something besides sex. Something. Yeah that's the spot. 'Is good plan, no?' 'Hell yeah.' Icarus, to escape captivity was crafted the gift of flight by his father, with restrictions. He was warned that flying too high and too close to the sun would melt the wax of his wings. And flying too low and too close to the ocean would add the weight of moisture to his feathers. Icarus was given a gift but told that to reach the potential of that gift, safety in another land, he was bound by these laws. I have been given a gift of my own, and to reach the potential of that gift I must be diligent, success. If I strike too high too fast I'll fall prey to the veterans pull behind stage. If I dally too low too long I'll become bogged by the lack luster, I am only as good as those that I beat. Unlike Icarus, who couldn't resist the allure of height and melted the wax of his wings and fell to the greed of his human nature. I plan on seeing that other land, that better life. 'Come, hurry, we miss plane!' 'We're not going to miss it.' 'Look, they boarding already.' 'You have your passport?' 'I have passport.' 'Where's the tickets?' 'You have ticket.' 'I thought you had the tickets!' 'You have ticket!' 'Wait, I have the tickets. Alright let's go.' Unlike Icarus, I won't abuse my gift. I just need to stay focused, forget about Jordan and about The Marauder. They're peices of the past, peices this puzzle doesn't mind missing. I have to find a way onto Coast too Coast, get something started with somebody, at least if Jordan was here I'd get to make a show of kicking his ass. Fuckin love to kick his ass, just wish he wasn't so right all the damn time. 'Everything checks out, enjoy your flight mister and missus Lexicon.' 'Thanks.' Mister and the missus. Holy fuck I went to Russia to kick some guys ass and I'm coming back married. My mother would kill me if I ever called her. I should have left after the damn show, I could be back on some beach with good American girls who just want to have sex and spend my money for a couple days. I'm fucking married, this is crazy. 'Johnny.' Why is she whispering? 'You want to join mile high club.' Again? 'Hell yeah.'
