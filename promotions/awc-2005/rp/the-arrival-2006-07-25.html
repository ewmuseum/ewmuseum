The Arrival

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wayne Russell stood at the door steps of Tiara Belle’s house, anxious and nervous at the same time two emotions Wayne had not felt in a long time. He sat there contemplating how he wanted to get the young girl’s attention, a simple knock on the door, you know the old fashion way or would he be better of ringing the door bell, so he could be positive that she had heard him. While Wayne was sitting there pondering which approach would be best Tiara Belle was upstairs watching and waiting. She had been waiting for this night all week long, she had hoped he would not bail on her, only time could tell. Her breath had become more and more indecisive, she at in front of her bedroom window, as soon as the truck had pulled up. There alone with no one else home she sat waiting for him to walk to the door to signify that he was there to pick her up. She sat there for a few moments wondering why he had yet to ring the door bell or even knock on the door. As many people she started to second guess herself, wait maybe it was him that was second guessing himself. She started to ponder and almost band to sweat, but then she had heard it. Wayne pushed his finger tip into the glowing button, he had decided to be more of a modern man, not one from the eighties and knock on the door. As he sat there awaiting the arrival of her, or actually for the truth of it he was sitting there awaiting the arrival of someone. He had hoped like any teenager that the father would not answer, but he was unsure and the moments he sat there waiting made him even more nervous. The palms of his hands started to clam up as he anticipated the arrival of some big guy that was her father figure, but instead he was eased when he saw the adorable face he had fallen for down at the lake. Not only had he fallen for her figure but over the last week or so speaking to her on the phone made him fall for her intellectually as well. “I’m glad you could make it.” Tiara said as she closed the door behind her as if she was trying to get out of the house before a father figure could interfere even though she knew she was the only one home at the time. Wayne started to speak, “ Y….o….u…” With each letter Wayne stumbles a little more showing Tiara how nervous he really was. “Look amazing…” it took Wayne a moment but he finally realized how much of a fool he looked like stumbling each word. “Thank you very much; you can clean up pretty swell, yourself.” Tiara let out a little giggle and looked at him and continued to speak, “That of course is when you want to.” Wayne lets out a little coy smile, not sure if her picking on him was her way of telling him that she liked him and was happy to see him, or if she really was the type of person that is an all out bitch, Of course we all know that years later she is really a bitch but at this point in life Wayne is content with her. “I try to when someone catches my interest.” Wayne is trying to hint towards something but is doing his best to be subtle. “Well if we want to catch that movie we should get going.” Tiara is then escorted by Wayne over to his pick up truck, where he opens the door for the young lady and helps her up into the truck. “Why thank you kind sir.” Tiara trying to sound like eloquent girl as she had just been helped up by and young stud, but in reality she sounded a little retarded, but Wayne paid no attention nodded his head like he was tipping the brim of his cap and walked around to the other side of the truck. Wayne hops in and starts the truck as he starts to back out he turns down the radio so they would be able to talk a little more as he drove them into the movies. Small talked commenced and nothing major really happened, Wayne and Tiara went back and forth with silly things both doing their own way of flirting but also not wanting to show too much interest they showed a kidding side. But with each moment both of them were falling deeper and deeper in touch with each other, it was something like you would hear from a love story, you know teenagers falling in love at such a young age and really being head over heels for each other something that does not happen these days.
