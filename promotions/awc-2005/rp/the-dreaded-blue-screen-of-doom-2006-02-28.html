Date: 28th February 2006 Red Rock is HARDKORE says:So how about it? Emo Screen Name says: How about what? Yes once again we joined Red Rock in his modest 2 bedroom flat, which he shares with a strange reclusive computer programmer named Howard. Today Red Rock isn’t feeling all that great for he has contracted that bastarding sickness bug. It started off with a bit of a tickle in the throat and next thing he knew he was bent over the toilet bowl to get a second look of the sausage sandwich he ate for lunch. Not only has Red Rock becoming infected with this virus he is also nursing the vicious wounds from the brutal ottoman torture chamber match. However in spite of all this Red Rock is still happy to waste a day in front of his computer screen in his bedroom; his illness and injuries are just another excuse not to train for a week. Now some may remember that not so long ago Red Rock took the time to clean his room…well…the piles of shit are slowly crawling back in to shape around his computer desk where he stays up late at night posting on paranormal forums. But at this moment in time Red Rock has already gone on all the forums and has run out of things to do, so he is trying to speak to some people on MSN. Right now he is talking to some emo girl he added from My Space who is from around his area but he’s never met before. Red Rock sits on his uncomfortable wooden computer chair with on leg crossed over the other. He is only typing with one hand today, not because he is wanking but because of the injury he received from Captain Suleimon bruising the bones in this thumb when he used the thumb screw on him. Red Rock is HARDKORE says: We go on a date? Emo Screen Name says: Err…when? Red Rock is HARDKORE says: Tomorrow night? We could go see a band or something? Emo Screen Name says: Um I think I’m busy. Red Rock: I wish they wouldn’t lie to me like that… Red Rock frowns and sits back in to his chair folding him arms and projecting a feeling of disappointment for about 2 or 3 minutes. Emo Screen Name says: U still there? Red Rock sits back forward with a sadden sigh of disappointment to go hand in hand with his expression. Another rejection from another girl, it’s almost as if someone has put a picture of him in the girl’s toilets with a caption that reads “Red Rock: Paedophile you’re children are not safe around this man” Red Rock is HARDKORE says: Oh sorry I have to type with one hand. Emo Screen Name says: Huh? Red Rock is HARDKORE says: I’m typing with one hand Emo Screen Name says: What are you trying to say? Red Rock is HARDKORE says: I can’t type with two hands… Emo Screen name says: u fuckin pervert! Red Rock: What the fuck?! Red Rock’s eyebrows sink toward the centre of his head in confusion at this accusation. Red Rock may be desperate and lonely and hasn’t felt the touch of a woman in a year and half but he isn’t a pervert. And sure he hates women and thinks they’re all heartless soul sucking minions of the devil who has no regard for the feelings of their male counter parts but that by no means says Red Rock is a pervert. Red Rock is HARDKORE says: WFT?! What did I say? Emo Screen Name says: U fink ur goin to get me in bed by telling me ur wankin? Fuck off ur sick! Red Rock is HARDKORE says: WHAT?! I never said I was wanking! Emo screen name is offline. Red Rock: Fucking retard! She was fat anyway. Red Rock leans back in his creaky wooden chair pouting with an aura of annoyance at this girl’s misconception. The last thing he wants is some girl telling all the other girls around that he is a sex pest…that’s what D’avid is around for! With all this in mind though Red Rock quickly thinks past it. It’s back to the drawing board now so Red Rock goes back to www.myspace.com to click on the browse function. He once again tries to look around searching for a possible mate. He types in the search for girls 10 miles within his area and starts to look around again. Red Rock: mmm…Ugly, ugly, fat, ugly, fat, fat, fat, emo, ginger, ugly… God you really do get some really hideous people around this area. As boredom mixed with frustration, loneliness and depression looms Red Rock thinks back a week two to that weird Ren girl who managed to hit Red Rock with an emotional bombshell but in doing so he hasn’t been able to get her out of his head. He found something very luring about her, maybe it was the way she stood up for the littler person? Maybe it’s because Red Rock admired her for being what he fails to be, a real hero. Sure Red Rock gets the cheers from the fans but all the wrong reasons…he is the anti hero. An anti hero is someone who is loved but shouldn’t be loved. He gets cheered because he fails to get anywhere he gets cheered for blowing chances and being the butt of other people’s jokes. Red Rock always wanted to get cheers from the crowd as he all he wanted was to be liked and to be accepted for once in his life. But pity cheering is worse than not being cheered for at all. But of course Red Rock isn’t open about this he will just hide behind his cynical front and sarcastic facial expressions. Red Rock: Mmm, I wonder… Red Rock clicks on the search link in the hope that maybe this Ren girl has a myspace profile he could contact her through. Red Rock simply types the word ‘Ren’ in the text box followed by the enter key. There is a brief moment of hope in the air before a profile is found in the search and low and behold the only result returned is the very same Ren Red Rock met last week! Red Rock’s breath is taken away and he wastes no time in clicking the link. A small dose of joy now flows through Red Rock like the blood in his veins and the air in his lungs. Red Rock: Good gravy! What are the chances? Red Rock waits for the profile to load, unlike many people these days Red Rock isn’t blessed with the gift of broadband internet; instead he has to make do with a 56k modem. Not that Red Rock would notice as he hasn’t experience the blessing that is a 2mb connection. His 7 year old computer is pulling out all the stocks with the over powering sound of the fan spinning. If there was anytime for his computer to pull through it was now! This could be future love this could be the path towards happiness this could be the key to turn around Red Rock’s life. Red Rock: AWW FUCKING BASTARD! The butterflies in Red Rock’s stomachs have died along with any hope he had and the dreaded blue screen of doom stares back at Red Rock from his old sun bleached computer monitor. Red Rock grabs the computer monitor then slaps his hands either side of it. Red Rock: YOU CUNT! You KNEW didn’t you? YOU KNEW! Red Rock shakes the monitor like a British nanny whilst swearing as loud as his lungs will allow him. After about a minute or two of relentless shaking Red Rock realises it isn’t getting him anywhere so he is left with little choice but to lean back in his rickety wooden chair with a face like he was sucking a lemon. Red Rock: My anguish sustains you doesn’t it? You won’t stop until you see me crying, you must loves the taste of human tears don’t you God? Maybe that’s why bad things happen to good people…because it’s human tears that keep you alive! Noah’s Ark, NOAH’S ARK?! Fuck that you just wanted to make people cry YOU BASTARD! Red Rock smacks his head against his computer desk and silence falls upon the area. … Suddenly Red Rock remembers that his new flat mate Howard is a computer programmer, if anyone could save his hopes of tracking down the alluring Red it was him! So with this thought in mind Red Rock springs up from his pit of misery and skips over to his door holding a sense of hope once more. Red Rock scrambles the few feet it is over to Howard’s bedroom door before knocking politely on it. Red Rock: Howard… Red Rock steps back and waits for Howard to invite him in or at least open the door. After a minute of waiting Red Rock knocks again but once again he receives no response. Red Rock: Howard? I can hear you in there answer the door! Red Rock places his hands on his hips as he grows more impatient. He can hear Howard inside and it sounds like he is scurrying franticly, but why? Red Rock: Are you masturbating? Because I can go make a sandwich or something until you’re finished… Red Rock points his thumb over his shoulder towards the kitchen then Howard mutters a response from behind his door sounding exceptionally breathless. Howard: Hold on… Howard opens the door to his bedroom but only enough so he can peek through a small gap at Red Rock. Strangely Howard also seems to have installed additional security locks and a security chain on his bedroom door which pokes and prods at Red Rock’s inquisitive nature. Red Rock: What’s with all the security Howard? I already told you the black people on floor one aren’t criminals and you should just assume they are… Howard: And I already told you I’m not a racist…anymore, I’m simply an exceptionally private individual…what do you want? Red Rock: Have you got a lady in there? YOU Dark horse! Red Rock tries to peer through the gap in the door but Howard moves his body to block Red Rock’s field of vision. Red Rock: Pfftt…OR is it a GUY? Howard: I have no other person or persons in my bedroom. What can I help you with, is there a problem? Red Rock: Yeah, my computers on the fritz I was wondering if you can take a look at it being a computer programmer and all. Howard pauses and it would appear that the one eye visible through the small gap in the door creases with shock. Howard: Err…err…err… sure… have you tried turning it off and on again? Red Rock: Err no. Howard: Try that! Howard slams his door in Red Rock’s face who shrugs off Howard’s strange behaviour. Red Rock exits the field of view for about 5 minutes. Red Rock returns with a disgruntled expression on his face once more as he approaches Howard’s door for the second time. He proceeds to hammer on Howard’s door who answers straight away this time looking equally as worried as before. Red Rock: It didn’t work it keeps giving me a blue screen and it won’t start up properly. Can’t you just come and have a look at it please? Howard: Err…err…sure, ok. Hold on. Howard closes his door leaving Red Rock waiting for a further 2 or 3 minutes. By now Red Rock has been quite patient considering he would usually be bouncing off the walls with fire in his eyes. Perhaps under the circumstances Red Rock has made a special effort to stay calm. Howard is now his only hope at finding Ren and if he snapped at him he would be risking loosing his help. Howard finally steps out of his room; he appears to be unreasonably anxious but Red Rock looks past this anxiety and guides him to his Room. Red Rock: Right o I’ll leave you to it then! Don’t look for my porn! Howard places himself down slowly and nervously in Red Rock’s computer chair and begins to nervously crack and play with his fingers as sweat begins to accumulate around his forehead. Howard: Ok…if you could turn it on for me. Red Rock: I’m sure you can manage that Howard. Howard: Oh no… I insist please do turn it on for me. Red Rock raises a cautious eyebrow at Howard whilst leaning down to press the power button. Red Rock: Right…there you go; I’m going to go watch some TV. Howard: super duper… Red Rock leaves his room with an air of curiosity around him. It seemed a bit strange that Howard was so reluctant to help him with his computer and even more reluctant to switch it on by himself. Red Rock rubs the top of his head as he takes some deep breaths in an attempt to calm himself down when all of a sudden standing in front of him is Dutch sex pest D’avid Bulrat! Red Rock jerks with shock at the unexpected sight of D’avid but quickly calms down when he realises it is he. D’avid: hello to you! D’avid stands up straight in front of Red Rock with his typical over enthusiastic persona beaming from his body. Red Rock: Fucks sake D’avid how did you get in here? D’avid withdraws a key from his pocket and holds it close to Red Rock’s face. D’avid: I had a key cut! Red Rock: I didn’t say you could have a key cut for my flat! D’avid: I knew you’d say no so I didn’t ask! Red Rock: Right… err…don’t do it again! So I noticed some people are moving in to Old Man Mclumpher’s flat? D’avid: YEAH! I know it’s a family and they have a fucking hot daughter! I’d like her to take a piss on me! OooOh! Red Rock: That just doesn’t shock me anymore D’avid…But hot daughter you say, what is she like? D’avid: FUCKING gorgeous! Come over to the window and see! D’avid drags Red Rock over to the window to lurch over the new neighbour’s daughter. A new family and some new faces just across the hall from Red Rock, how exciting! They are called the Darlings; the father was once a CID in the police force but was dismissed after an accusation of gross misconduct. After loosing the job he found it difficult to find a new job and things started to fall apart so they ended up joining the ranks of the poor, jobless and drug addicted; sharing the same block of flats as Red Rock. Red Rock and D’avid stare out the window watching removal men carry various house hold items out of a large removals truck. The family are all standing together all looking quite depressed but trying to maintain their dignity. Red Rock: I don’t see any hot daughter… all I see is a some short fat goth. D’avid: ISN’T SHE GORGEOUS? Red Rock tilts his head at D’avid with a complete look of bemusement imprinted on his face. Red Rock: No! She’s a fucking pig! D’avid: More cushing for the pushion! Red Rock doesn’t bother correcting D’avid he won’t take it in nor will be listen. D’avid presses his face and hands against the glass; licking his lips and thrusting his pelvis against the window with a lustful glint in his eye. Red Rock rolls his eyes and snatches D’avid away from the window. Red Rock: I’d be a terrible friend if I let you anywhere near…that. She’s barley human! How she even walks baffles me I mean look at those canckles! She must be at least 20 stone. D’avid: That’s how I like them! Red Rock: She probably hasn’t seen her burger for years! D’avid: and I intend on finding it! Red Rock: Just think how unclean it will be and the vast amounts of smeg that will be in it! D’avid: There’s nothing wrong with a little extra cheese on the taco! Heh! Red Rock: Ok it’s your funeral, just don’t come screaming for my help when she crushes you! D’avid: OoooOooH! You’re giving me a HUGE errec - * POW * Red Rock: What the fucking crap sandwich was that? Shortly after the sound of a small explosion Howard comes running from Red Rock’s room with his glasses covered with a layer of black dirt. It doesn’t take long for Red Rock to realise what has happened. Red Rock’s facial expression looks like he is shouting at the top of his voice but no sound is coming out, he just stands still sustaining this expression of anger. Howard: I think you’re computer will require replacing Red Rock still holds the same expression. Howard: I…I… Red Rock walks towards his room still holding the same expression; it’s almost as if he is trying his best to keep his rage inside. Red Rock fixes his eyes on his computer monitor only to see a small cloud of smoke trailing through the holes on the top. His computer monitor has in fact blown up thus crushing any hope he had of tracking down a potential girlfriend Ren. D’avid: Howard fucking blew it to shitting pieces! Red Rock takes a huge deep intake of air… Red Rock: NOOOOOOO~! Howard stands behind Red Rock looking very sheepish, Red Rock didn’t mention anything to Howard about Ren but it’s almost as if Howard knows he has crushed some kind of hope Red Rock was clinging on to. Howard: I’m ever so sorry Red! Red Rock sighs and wipes his face with his hands and with a deflated tone of voice he turns around and out walks out of his room Red Rock: Don’t worry about it Howard. Red Rock sluggishly drags himself over to the sofa and silently lowers himself down. He rests his elbow on his knees and rests his chin on his hand. He just looks so disappointed but he doesn’t want to make a big deal out it he just wants to sit by himself. D’avid looks on at Red Rock, from his point of view it is obvious he is upset about something. Red Rock hasn’t mentioned Ren to anyone as he thinks people won’t understand why he is becoming so infatuated by her, or maybe he is still trying to figure it out for himself. But for now it’s looking like he’ll have to try and forget about her.
