Date: 12/17/05 (Yes, I'm American :p) 13th December 2005 So close yet so far. I saw victory snatched right from my hands. I knew there was a reason the racists had signed that match. The only way the could have circumvented my Ottoman dominance was through having one American slug pin the other before I could get a chance to break it up. Cowards. 16th December 2005 I should never have come back to my flat in London. I should have stayed across the Channel and waited there between the show and this infernal Christmas party that Harber is throwing. I know I shouldn't go, and attendance isn't mandatory, but I know that if I did not show up, I would be making a bad impression for my new employers. Why should I care about a cadre of weak, white men who only employ me because they see me as shock value for their boorish, inebriated fans? Right now, they hold the ultimate power over me. Harber, Volkeyva, they decide whether I wrestle for their Club or if I just went back to my meager existance of training worthless worms for peanuts. If they fire me, I cannot hope to attain my ultimate plan for dominance over the entire wrestling world. And in a way, I must humble myself, at least administratively. I must go to a party for a holiday that I personally find repugnant and superficial. I must let these white devils hold dominion over me, until one by one, I destroy them in the ring. Destroy all of them. I have decided that I don't think there is a redeemable soul on the roster. Even those with desirable qualities have others that stain their character. Chainz knows a woman's place is in the kitchen, among other rooms in the house. Yet, I find his pedophilia disturbing to say the least. The Furious Fists of God, their hearts are in the right place, but they are of the wrong religion. I have a great distaste for zealots of my own religion to tell the truth. Pierce Lavelle has a weak will. Adam Dick is not good enough at covering his tracks. Mike Wade is too scared deep down inside. Red Rock, he's a simpleton. I could go on and on, but I do not wish to run out of pages in this journal. I feel I could dismantle this roster piece by piece. But because I've suffered this first setback, I have suffered great indignity. Which brings me back to the original point after great rambling. Since I have come back to my flat, I've received nothing but heckles from people in the street. People, mouth-breathers, they see me in the street and laugh because I said I would destroy the Western slime on the roster, yet lost in my debut match. I know I shouldn't let these things bother me. Circumstances were not on my side. My shoulders weren't pinned. The predatory Damien D'Angelo took the easy road out and pinned the woman to the mat. But their smugness, their attitude towards me, it stings, especially in the face of my confidence, which they have dubbed arrogance. Soon they will be the ones turning their heads away from me on the streets. But for now, indignity. I am stronger than this though. It is my mission to be so.
