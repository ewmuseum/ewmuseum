For the fans...(part one)

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th May 2005 Voice: Hey, look…I’m gonna have to call you back, I’m driving through a tun- As the man is interrupted, he sighs with eventual relief. Once outside of the darkness that remains to be the tunnel, the world is bright again, bright, sunny and jovial. A man is driving a car, a very average looking car down a motorway, and is looking to be very excited. The phone rings once more as the man picks it up. Voice: Y’ello? Yeah, no I can’t. I have to have a meeting, looks like I could be getting a contract with a major fed. The man smirks, bearing some perfect white teeth. He continues to drive the car in one hand, and using the phone in another. The man, with golden blonde hair loose onto his shoulders is named ‘Samuel Livingston’ and is probably better than you. It’s a very big day to advancing his career, despite being the current IwE Heavyweight Champion. Samuel: No. I’m kind of part time with it. You see, their shows are on Mondays, whereas IwE is on Wednesdays, so really, the only major problem I have is getting from Atlanta all the way down to Miami in just two days. It’s harder than you think, as soon as I get home, I practically have to leave. Samuel would have to had made a really tough decision. He could either stay with the IwE, remain as their World Champion, and make a small name for himself, or he could work with the AWC, and build a great reputation, despite not really making a name for himself yet. AWC would be the only way he could advance his career. Samuel looks at his speed, and notices that he is slightly over the limit, so he slows down. Samuel: I have to go, I have to be there within the next few minutes, and I’m still a few miles away, so I’m going to have to speed up, and pay full attention on the road. OK. Cya. Samuel hangs up the phone and throws it in the back seat. Samuel puts his foot to the accelerator and begins to speed up further. He swerves out of the way of a car. All of a sudden, the phone rings, yet Samuel Livingston realises the phone is at the back. The ringing gets annoying, but Samuel tries to avoid listening to it. Eventually, Samuel gives up, he reaches his arm out, and places his arms out on the backseat, trying to grab the phone. Samuel keeps his eyes on the road, but eventually afterwards has to look. As he turns his head around, he hears a beeping sound from the horn of a truck in front of him. As he turns around in shock. The van edges closer. We black out. -- Samuel Livingston: I’ve been having the same dream recently. Ever since the first rumours of my contract signing at the AWC. I’ve been there for a whole day now, and for the past week I’ve been having these dreams. Our new scene is a therapists office. Samuel is laying down on a black leather sofa, looking very comfortable, whilst a female therapist with dark hair is talking to him. Samuel is wearing his navy blue tanktop. He is looking rather muscular, but his eyes are gazing intensely at the therapist. Therapist: So how far from the contract negotiations did these dreams begin to happen? Samuel sticks his index finger on his lips, and thinks for a moment, before coming back with a reply.> Samuel: Near the-the-the ACHOO! Therapist: Bless you. Samuel: Somewhat near the beginning. Samuel closes his eyes. This was a lie, the dreams had begun since before the contracts had been dealt with. The dreams began ever since before Samuel Livingston ever even heard of AWC. This was partly the reason Samuel applied, although he never had actually thought he would be accepted. I guess Samuel was actually better than he gave him credit for. Actually, who am I kidding? Samuel is great Samuel: I have two matches per week. A lot of stress is put on me, do you think this could have any effect on me? Therapist: It’s possible, and rather likely. Wrestling is a high pressure job, especially if one is a champion. I’ve had a few wrestlers as patients, and some of them turned out to be because of their work. I suggest you leave one. Samuel gets up and stays in a seated position. Samuel: I couldn’t do that. The federations mean the whole world to me. I need to advance my career, I need to make sure we don’t get scum like Crimson O’Malleck reaching for the headlines. No. Samuel shakes his head Samuel: I’m staying in both places. For the fans. Our scene comes to a fade with Samuel Livingston leaving the room. The scene slowly fades to black with a therapist muttering something to her breath. Although we cannot hear it, it is a good thing, as it was actually vulgar. View Samuel Livingston 's Biography
