Master V's Theater Presents: Blackman the Pirate (Part 1) Fresh! RP

6 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/06/2006 The camera slowly fades in and the screen is black. All of a sudden static fills the screen. The static slowly begins to change to a blood red color. A red logo appears on the screen as if it is being spray painted on the screen over the static. The logo reads: V-T.V. All of a sudden the logo slowly fades away and the screen once again goes pitch black. Next, a message fades in onto the screen in white lettering as a score of British inspired classical music headlined by thundering french horns begins playing in the background. Basically the opening theme from the old Masterpiece Theater show from back in the day. V-T.V proudly presents...Master V's Theater The camera fades into a small, private, dimly-lit library, where 'The Violence' Vince Jones can be seen seated on an armchair in his usual all- black street gear with a large book laid across his lap. Vince Jones looks up at the camera with a cocky smirk as the classical music finally ceases. Vince Jones: Good evenin', bitches and welcome to a very special show here. Some of you people might know the deal. Some of you people might not. Well tonight, V. Jones and V-T.V are bringin' to you cats the return of the one and only Master V's Theater where we present to you people classically inspired stories and sometimes even some original shit to keep yo heads ringin'. On Master V's Theater V. Jones likes to provide you people with his own twist of ghetto fab on all that ish. Now it turns out that V. Jones got a match this week, some Beneath the Boardwalk shit. (nods to himself.) That's all good. Shit be remindin' V of lookin' around for buried treasure ish or somethin'. And afta some thought V started thinkin' back on a lil story his old grandpa used to be runnin' his gums about when he'd be drunk on a Friday night. The story... Vince Jones raises the book on his lap revealing the title and a picture of Vince Jones himself dressed in a pirate suit wearing a long blackbeard on his face. Vince Jones: ...Captain Blackman the Pirate. Hope you bitches enjoy this shit. The camera slowly fades out and fades into what appears to be a sunny day on a beach on the Atlantic coast of the colonies. Seagulls are flying about all through the sky as a large, black man appears on the scene. It appears to be 'The Violence' Vince Jones and he is dressed in a lush, red pirate suit with sun shining down upon him from his large feathered hat to the buckles on his shiny, black shoes. A narrator with an English accent begins to speak in the background. Narrator: Once long, long ago there was a man by the name of Blackman, Blackman the Pirate... The pirate struts forward and stands in a pose reminiscent to the famous Superman pose with his cape blowing in the wind. He nods his head with a smug expression plastered across his face. Narrator: ...Blackman the Pirate was viewed as one of the most threatening pirates to ever set sail the Seven Seas... Blackman the Pirate: (nods confidently) Hell yeah! Narrator: After only a short time as a buccanneer Blackman the Pirate had plundered twenty ships... Blackman the Pirate: (looks at the camera in confusion) Huh? (in defiance) Fuck that! It was fifty, bitch! Get the facts straight! Captain Blackman flips off the narrator. Narrator: ...commanded one hundred pirates... Blackman the Pirate rolls his eyes and snarls at the thought. Blackman the Pirate: That's three hundred pirates, muthafucka! Narrator: (clears his throat) ...and was one of the wealthiest pirates in the history of bucanneering. Blackman the Pirate kicks some sand and looks up at the sky in fury. Blackman the Pirate: Aiight! This pirate gettin' sick and damn tired of you undercuttin' his resume and all here. Blackman the Pirate was the richest son of a bitch to eva sail the seven seas! Get it? Got it?? Good!! Narrator: Sorry about that. Captain Blackman folds his arms across his chest and shakes his head in disappointment with the narrator's errors...or truthfulness. Blackman the Pirate: Betta be sorry! Narrator: Ahem! But those days were long behind Blackman... The camera slowly fades out as Blackman lowers his head in shame. The camera fades into a small cabin with little to nothing at all. The furnishings are shabby and the cupboards bare. Captain Blackman the pirate can be seen making out with his lovely girlfriend, Yasmina (Jasmine, dressed in a beautiful Spanish styled dress) on a large rocking chair. Narrator: After the mutiny on The Violence, his old sailing ship, Blackman was without his fleet, his treasures, and out of buccaneering for good... Blackman ceases his make out session and glares at the camera and points at in fury. Blackman the Pirate: Now that's where you wrong again, son! Blackman the Pirate saw no mutiny on his damn ship! He just... told them cats he was sick of piratin' things and... he wanted out the game. Narrator: I see. Well, where's your treasure at, sir? Blackman the Pirate: (uneasy) Lost it somewhere! Narrator: And your ship? Blackman the Pirate: The Captain had to pay off some debts now step off! Yasmina grabs a handful of Blackbeard's...blackbeard and begins tugging on it to get his attention. Yasmina: Baby, forget about him and focus on me! Blackman nods his head and begins making out with Yasmina once again. Narrator: Anyways, Blackbeard after a minor setback or two was now retired from buccaneering with little or nothing left to his name, but his beautiful girlfriend, Yasmina who he met on a trip in Spain; but, that was all about to change... Suddenly there is a loud knock at the door. Blackman and Yasmina pretend to hear nothing as they continue making out with one another. The knocking continues, but Blackman and Yasmina continue making out. The knocking continues at the door and Blackman the Pirate finally ceases the makeout session and knocks Yasmina to the floor. Blackman the Pirate: Damnit! Why all the interruptions?? Yasmina: If we ignore whoever it is maybe they'll go away. Now come on. Let's get back to... Voice from outside the door: We know you're in the Blackbeard! Open up! We're not going no matter how quiet you remain! Yasmina shrugs her shoulders and Blackbeard rises to his feet and trudges across the wooden floors of the half empty house muttering to himself. He opens the door and looks out in confusion. Blackman the Pirate: Reno? Reno Blanco? What the hell are you doing here? Reno Banks playing the part of Reno Blanco can be seen standing in Blackman's doorway with a sly grin on his face and some lavish pirate gear. Standing beside him is a blonde haired man wearing a coloinally inspired clothing and a triangle hat upon his powdered wig wearing head. Reno Blanco: Aye sir ! Captain Blackman, its been a long time indeed. Reno Blanco removes his hat and bows before Captain Blackman. Blackman the Pirate: Ya damn right its been a long time! (turns towards the stranger next to Blanco) And who the hell is this? Blanco stands upright once again and replaces the hat on his head. Reno Blanco: A man that is here to turn things around for you, Captain Blackman. The blonde haired stranger steps forward and extends a hand to Blackman in friendship. Blonde stranger: The name's Pearlious, Walter Pearlious to be exact. And yes, like your friend, Mr. Blanco here has already explained I am here to change your current state of being, sir. Blackman looks down at Pearlious' exteneded hand and shoves it aside. Blackman the Pirate: Blackman'll be shakin' no hands here. Walter Pearlious: (nods) Very well. All of a sudden Yasmina comes trotting up behind Blackman and peeps out the door as well. Yasmina: Who are our guests, Captain? Blackman the Pirate: Just an old friend and some otha bitch made! You remember Reno Blanco? Yasmina nods. Walter Pearlious: Might myself and Mr. Blanco here step inside, Captain? Blackman glares at Pearlious and Blanco a bit uneasily and finally walks back inside his house inviting them in as well. Blackman trudges across the floor and sets himself down on a wooden chair which sits behind a large wooden table. Yasmina follows and takes a sit on his lap and begins nibbling at his ear. Blanco and Pearlious both look around the barely furnished house. There is a stove and a few kettles, a small cupboard which is open and bare, and in the far corner of the room is a pile of quilts which may serve as a bed. Blanco shakes his head at the state of Blackman's house. Reno Blanco: 'Tis a shame, you used to have so much. Now... Blackman the Pirate: Shut the hell up! Walter Pearlious grabs a seat at the table as well as Blanco. Walter Pearlious: Well Captain, your friend does have a point. You need this job I have for you more than I thought. Blackman the Pirate slams his fist on the table startling both Pearlious and Blanco. Blackman the Pirate: That's where you two are wrong! Blackman needs nothin' from you two! If you gonna come up in here and insult The Captain's shit then you two can roll the hell on! And that's real! Yasmina tries to calm Blackman down as Pearlious and Blanco try to smooth the situation over once again. Yasmina: Calm down, Captain. Blackman the Pirate: Fuck that! These bitches insultin' The Captain and his house! That shit don't fly! Reno Blanco: Oh, we didn't mean any harm, Captain. Blackman the Pirate: Like hell you two didn't! Walter Pearlious: Will you at the very least hear my proposal? Blackman the Pirate: Hell nah! Get the hell on! Reno Blanco: It involves treasure, lots and lots of treasure for the taking. And you know what that means. There will be lots of gold... Blackman pauses in thought at the sound of the word as images of his old life begin flashing through his head. An anxious grin begins to cross his face. Blackman the Pirate: Now that's the kinda shit The Captain likes to hear... Yasmina: (in definace) My Captain will not be swayed by such empty promises, gentlemen. He will be staying here with me. Blackman hears Yasmina's words and looks at her like she's crazy. Blackman the Pirate: Like hell we're stayin' here, Yasmina! You know how long The Captain's been tryin' to get us up out this hell hole? Fuck Massachussetts! (to Blanco and Pearlious) Sign The Captain up for this shit! Yasmina: (in surprise) WHAT?? Blackman covers her mouth. Blackman the Pirate: Don't mind this wench bitch here! Captain Blackman been out the game long enough. Now Blackman needs a ship if we gonna sail and all. Walter Pearlious: (nods) That has already been taken care of. I am more than prepared to finance this expedition. Blackman the Pirate: (nods) Aiight! Well, what about a crew? If Captain Blackman gonna do his damn thang out there on the sea he can't be sailin' around with just anyone out there. Ya know? Walter Pearlious: (nods) Don't worry, Captain. That has already been taken care of as well. I have assembled the best crew money can buy, more men much like yourself. I just need your handshake, Captain. Are you ready to pursue fame on the seven seas once again? Blackman nods his head. Blackman the Pirate: Ah, hell yeah! Walter Pearlious extends his hand towards Captain Blackman. Blackman rises to his feet and shakes Pearlious' hand. Reno smiles. Reno Blanco: Well, when will the sailing begin, Mr. Pearlious? Pearlious chuckles to himself and makes his way past Blanco towards the door. Walter Pearlious: In the evening, I'll meet you all at the docks before nightfall. Good day sirs. Pearlious exits Captain Blackman's house as Blackman, Yasmina, and Reno Blanco look back at one another in surprise at the short time frame. The camera slowly fades out and fades back into an evening scene. We are now at a small port where a large sailing ships are located. All is calm at the moment as the camera pans around the scene and catches sight of a beautifully dressed, Yasmina, Captain Blackman the Pirate, and Reno Blanco as they make their way past the line of ships. All of a sudden a familiar voice can be heard from one of the ships. The three turn towards the direction of the voice and see Walter Pearlious standing on the most marvelous of all the ships waving at them. Captain Blackman looks at the ship in disbelief. Captain Blackman: Is that... Walter Pearlious: Yes, it is. Its your old ship, The HMS Violence. Captain Blackman: But how the hell did you get a hold of this? Walter Pearlious: Don't trouble yourself with such questions. I thought it would be a pleasant surprise. Captain Blackman: (nodding) Yeah. It is... Yasmina begins tugging at The Captain's arm and looks up at him with worried eyes. Yasmina: (whispering) I've got a funny feeling about all of this, baby. Remember the last time we were on that ship? There was a mutiny and we got shoved off. I don't want that to happen again. I don't want to relive that. And I don't want to ruin my hair! Captain Blackman leans in close to Yasmina and begins whispering back to her. Captain Blackman: Everything'll be fine. Now shut it up and just think about all the good shit The Captain gonna be buyin' for yo ass afta this voyage is ova and we find that buried treasure. Life'll be good, damn good for the both of us Yasmina. Yasmina: (whispering)You better be right, Captain. Walter Pearlious: Well, are you all coming aboard or what? Captain Blackman and Reno Blanco both look at Yasmina who looks back at the two of them a bit uneasily. Blackman, Blanco, and Yasmina walk up the plank and aboard the HMS Violence where Pearlious awaits. Pearlious greets the three of them with a smile. Walter Pearlious: Welcome or should I say welcome back to the HMS Violence? Captain Blackman looks around at his old ship in amazement and begins strutting around the boat confidently. Captain Blackman: Damn! Feels like it was just yesterday... Yasmina: (under her breath) Uh huh. Feels like yesterday that we were getting kicked off this ship. Yeah. Those were the days indeed. Blackman stops in his tracks, whirls around, and glares at Yasmina. Captain Blackman: Hey! That was the past! Now shut the hell up, Yasmina! You tryin' to ruin The Captain's high up in here or somethin'? Captain Blackman whirls around and is surprised by a group of four strangers all standing side-by-side. They appear to be pirates. Captain Blackman looks at the four in disgust and then turns towards Pearlious, pointing back at the four strangers. Captain Blackman: Whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa! Who the hell are these bitches? Walter Pearlious: Who are they? Why they are the crew I assembled for you. How about we go meet them? Blackman nods in agreement. He, Pearlious, Blanco, and Yasmina all stroll over to the four individuals. Captain Blackman looks down at the first individual who is dressed in odd Turkish styled clothing. He has a large handlebar mustache on his face and a red turban on his head. Captain Blackman: Aiight! State yo name, son! Turkish pirate: Zey call me ze Bald Bull. I report to duty, Captain. Captain Blackman: (in confusion) The Bald Bull? Huh? Why the hell they call you the Bald Bull? Bald Bull: Why? Cuz I bald... He lifts his turban revealing a bald head. He then sets the turban back on his head. Bald Bull: You see? And my tongue stings like ze horns of ze bull. Captain Blackman nods. Bald Bull looks back up at him defiantly. Bald Bull: Why do you look at me such? You look at me like you zhink you better, more superior zhan me. You may be me captain, but I still spit on your kind and your colony! You swine! (thrusts his fist in the air) Turks rule! Turks rule! The Bald Bull spits at the feet of Captain Blackman surprising him. Blackman steps up to the next pirate who wears a black and red mask covering his face. Captain Blackman: State yo name! Pirate #2: Arrrrrrgghh!!! Captain Blackman: WHAT?? Pirate #2: Arrrrrrgghh!!! Captain Blackman: Don't you know 'bout respectin' yo captain, bitch?? When yo captain asks what yo damn name is you don't growl you say yo damn name! Now what the hell is yo name?? Pirate #2: I already told ye what my name is! Clean out ye ears! They're full of wax! The name is....Arrrrgghh!!! Captain Blackman: (in confusion) As in...Arrgghh? Pirate #2: Yes! Arrgghh!!! I hate it when people don't catch me name! Matter of fact, I hate your suit...and your hat! I hate your scruffy black beard and I hate you too! I even hate your wench! I hate this ship! I wish I wasn't here, but I need a job! I need money! And that's what I hate the most! Captain Blackman: (nodding) You's one hateful son of a bitch if Captain Blackman has eva seen one! You'll fit in fine! Captain Blackman walks past this man and up to the third who appears to have crazy, red, billowing hair. Captain Blackman: And you are? Pirate #3: Red Sparrow, the bedwetter at ye service, Captain! Look forward to setting sail and not wetting me pants in the process. Captain Blackman: (in disgust) Yeah. Blackman wishin' the same, ya pussy ass bedwettin' bitch! Blackman walks up to the last and least impressive of the pirates. Captain Blackman: And who the hell are you, huh? Pirate #4: Me name's Tanto the Heartless! I'll rip a heart out of any man's chest at any given time. Just say the word, Captain! I'm afraid of no one! Captain Blackman: Hmph! Well, its good to have you aboard, son! Now get the cleanin'! Tanto: What?? Cleaning duty?? But I... Captain Blackman: You heard The Captain! Get the cleanin'! The other pirates roar with laughter as Tanto lowers his head in shame. Tanto: I never get my due respect! Tanto trudges off as Blackman laughs and turns towards Pearlious. Captain Blackman: Aight! Let's get this ship out on the sea! Walter Pearlious: Not so fast! We're missing one last crew member! Shackles! SHACKLES!! All of a sudden the sound of a woman can be heard outside of the ship. They all turn to see a lovely, large breasted wench scolding a brutish looking pirate outside of the ship. The woman: I thought you said you had given up this line of work, Shackles! Shackles: Well I... The woman begins slapping him upside his head in fury. The woman: You lied to me! Shackles: I needed work! I did it for you, Isabella! Isabella: I thought you loved me! Captain Blackman: (peering over the side of the ship) Hey! Get yo ass up on this ship so we can leave, man! Shackles: That's what I'm trying to do here, sir! Captain Blackman: Well c'mon! Hurry yo ass on up, man! Shackles turns towards Isabella and grasps her hands lovingly. Shackles: Look, Isabella this is the last time. I must do this. I don't do this in spite of our love, but for our love. You must believe that, darling. Well, I must go. Isabella: But, you can't leave me all alone. Shackles: But, I must. Isabella: I will not let you go without me! Shackles: Well, how about you come along too? Blackman: What??? Hell nah! Isabella: (thrilled) Oh, it would be an honor, Shackles. Blackman: Hell nah! That bitch ain't sailin' with us! Captain Blackman the Pirate watches in horror as the two lovebirds trot onto his ship. Walter Pearlious: Well, that's your problem to deal with, Captain. And remember our deal. You find that treasure, bring it back to me, and I'll make you a wealthy and famous man. Good day! Pearlious quickly exits the ship as Shackles and his love, Isabella finally join them. Shackles: So, when will we be off? Narrator: And there we have it. Captain Blackman and his band of pirates were now ready to set sail looking for action, adventure, and most importantly buried treasure. Captain Blackman the Pirate shakes his head in disappointment with his situation as the camera slowly fades to black. To Be Continued.... The camera fades back into the small library where Vince Jones or Master V can be seen closing the large book on his lap as he looks up at the camera. Vince Jones: Well bitches, that's it for tonight's edition of Master V's Theater. Tune in next time as we continue our story of our unlikely hero and all Blackman the Pirate. Until then, Goodnight bitches! See you cats again soon! The classical music begins blaring once again as the credits begin to roll and the camera slowly fades to black.
