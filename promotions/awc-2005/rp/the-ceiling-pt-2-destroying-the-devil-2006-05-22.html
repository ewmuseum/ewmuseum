The Ceiling, pt. 2: Destroying the Devil

Date: May 22, 2006 “I’ve been searching for the dolphins in the seaaaaaa…” The radio blared. It roared. It echoed. It did every action that meant a noise coming from a speaker. The air waves were rattled by the music that vibrated into my ear drums as I laid in bed. It was there. It was there. But no type of sound wave really mattered tonight. I was living a life that was nothing but a lie, the horns below my hair jabbing slowly out of my skin, ripping it open little by little. I had found him, the man who really guided me. --------------------------- Tim is in an alley way with another man, a crack head. Tim lunges forward. STAB! In the man’s eyes, Tim sees nothing but a devil looking back. The man drops… --------------------------- For fear alone I laid there in that bed and crept back to my savior, the ceiling. What was once a torture and a strenuous pain was now my only savior. In the ceiling I could lose myself from the world I was now living. There were no devilish Tim Martins or anything of that sort. There were beautiful thing. At this moment I was daydreaming of that time I was helping Liam in 3rd grade. We were making popsicle stick houses and he needed somebody on the hot glue. I was there. I was there. At this moment I was daydreaming of that time I was showing the Lord’s Bible to orphans, and they all just stared at me, their innocent eyes hooked on the gospel. I was there. I was there. At this moment I was daydreaming of that time I was not this sinful beast but rather innocently ignorant of all the wrongdoings. I was there. I was there. Figments are figments are figments though. What was once real is now imaginary and what was once imaginary scare me on every street corner. Instead of a white man I see a devil, standing there and grinning at me as the fires of hell encase him. There are no more niggers…just black men begging for me to stop, to stop haunting their lives. At every step of the way, I can feel Satan’s cold hand on my shoulder. His tricks are clever—for years now he’s guised himself as more saintly than he should ever be. Even though I’ve figured it out now, it’s too late; his grip is firm, as I can feel the coldness he had been hiding for years. So to escape that pain and sorrow I lay in my bed and stare at the ceiling. The ceiling. The FUCKING ceiling. My sanctuary and my torture rack, standing there like a scare crow. It’s nature is enticing and appalling, and while I’m afraid to look, I cannot resist. Soon, I’m pulled into the nightmare. My eyes daze off into dizziness and I can’t tell if I’m sleeping or if this is just reality blacking out. The images start to form, as the flames grow hotter. --------------------------- Boston. The greatest city on Earth. A place where every man, woman, and child aspires to be, or at least visit. So much history has been dug into this soil, that when you gaze upon these lands now, it’s hard not to shed a tear. *BANG! BANG!* The shots whiz by and hit a black man, right in the jaw. As the first one smacks into the bone, you can see shards mixed with blood fly off. Before the man can fully react to this, the second one strikes his temple, exiting out the other side as he goes lifeless. The carcass falls to the ground. *THUMP* Nothing. Dead. The shooter and a friend walk over and drag the body into a nearby alley way. The streets are empty, even though it’s the middle of the day. No one knows why this black man was walking through the Eagle’s Nest during day time. Didn’t he know? “Stupid sum’bitch. Didn’t hear ‘bout the war, did he?” The war. The ways of the Eagle’s Nest escalated into a full-out race war in the streets of Boston. It was underground and yet it was everywhere. The cops were too afraid to stop it, and the citizens were too proud to say anything either. So everyday, black and whites fought in the streets, shooting each other the minute they see them. Hence the empty roads. During the day, or rather anytime it was bright, you never wanted to be out in the streets. There was always a gang of guys of the opposite color looking to gun you down. And when they did, they’d think nothing of it. The body was stripped of all money as the shooter and his friend disappeared into the darkness of the alley. One final thing is heard… “Think the Martins will be please?” The Martins. The leaders of the Eagle’s Nest. After things got out of hand, they were pegged as the big men in the White Lord’s Army, so they took the role. And from their apartment they ran their neighborhood and coordinated attacks, with a lay out of Boston on their kitchen table marked with spots and places that are safe and not so safe. “What about Tony’s? How are they doing? Heard they got fucked?” Tim’s tired eyes intently studied the map. For years he just gazed at it, trying to find a way to stop it all and bring back the peace. But he was stuck now, his ideals sucking him into a world that only meant death. “No, brother, Tony is dead and the place is torched. They killed women and children too.” Liam leaned against the table, puffing on a cigarette. The anger on his face glowed as he reminisced about Tony’s and Steve’s Mart and all the places in the Nest and surrounding areas that have been taken out. All the lives. --------------------------- While the war general lived one life, I was still living another. The problems with the ceiling were now becoming evident, though. It became MORE than just a place to escape. The ceiling was now a portal and I have left it open too long. These images were following me everywhere I went. I tried to escape it for a moment, snapping myself out of the pain and hoping, just hoping, that my ideas of a never ending hallucination that seemed realer than ever wouldn’t follow me around. I started heading out the door for some cigarettes. “Tim, what the fuck you been doing all day in that bed, you lazy son of a bitch.” I heard Liam and saw him out of the corner of my eye. When I turned to see him, he was sitting in his reclining, staring blankly at “Candlepins for Dollars” on TV, the local amateur bowling show. He didn’t make any attempt to establish any sort of eye contact. “Liam, what do you mean? I’ve been busy…” “Busy, fuck that!” The change happened so quick though. Instead of life, he was there, covered in blood. Our living room was drenched in the sunlight that seeped in through the bullet holes in the way. You could see the punctures in his chest; Liam was killed. LIAM! LIAM! But it was too late. My ways, everything about them, brought him down. I have brought him down the path. “Asshole, you listening? HEY, ASSHOLE!” And things were back to the same. The walls were chipping a little; we really need to get on that. “Yeah…sorry brother…I’ve just been, I dunno, aloof…” A moment of silence. Liam’s head turns to the left…as he executes the perfect “I’m on death row 1,000 yard stare into nothing”, he turns back to me with a sly smile. “You getting cigarettes I hope?” The smile on my face is involuntary. He knows me so well. I love my brother. “Yeah.” “Grab me some too.” “Of course.” So I crash out the door and onto the sidewalk. But there it is again. The cars, smashed and burnt, destroyed. The glass blown in and distorted. Three bodies, lying in the road…and who’s fucking attending to them? Nobody. One man still has his head up a little, and when he stares into my eyes, I see that devil again. That devil…fucking laughing in that reflection. But then his head drops and the road loses all that. The bodies disappear and the cars reassemble, with two little girls on cute little pink bicycles ride by. “Mr. Martin! Mr. Martin!” Claudia and Lyndsey. Billy Joseph’s kids. He’s a manager for the Eagle’s Construction company. Nice little girls. “Hey there, girls. What’s your dad doing?” “He’s working in the yard.” “I’m going that way. I’ll go say high. Don’t go too fast!” And of course they don’t listen, but that’s really just part of growing up. So while the sun is still shining at its highest point I start to head off in the direction of the corner bodega, the one right outside the Eagle’s Nest. It’s protected by someone so we don’t touch it, even though the Arabs run it so close. They somehow manage to get business, though—at least enough to stay alive. But I said I’d visit Billy Joseph real quick, and I’m a man of my word. But I’m fucking afraid, no lie. I don’t want to see Billy as he shouldn’t. He’s a family man, a hardworking man. The problem, though, is I know my mind will taint him, as my beliefs already have. --------------------------- “Tim, there he is.” “Who?” “The nigger who was hitting on my baby.” “Well, fuck, here you go.” “Allright man, stop the car.” SCREECH! And I ran. Until finally, I was there. The corner store bodega. I stood in front of it, panting, as the bustling traffic from everyone trying to get wherever they were going shuffled passed me.
