Better late than never

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-2005 The leaves are starting to change color and the grass is not as green as it used to be but for the time being the weather is still pretty nice. When the season's change it brings out the worst in people and sometimes the best in people. For one person it's a time to reflect on his past and concentrate on what's in front of him. For instance Titan here is a simple man and he hasn't wrestled in quite awhile unless it was in a dark and smelly gym or in one of the worst dives this side of Utah. Well times are definitely changing, since the AWC signed Titan up he has had nothing but luck and nothing but a turn of events for him. Take for instance today, the sun is shining and he is out just relaxing not a care in the world right now. Titan: Excellent, a gorgeous day and nothing to ruin it. One thing is for sure is I am excited about my match tommorow night. Now it wasn't what I expected but it's something I can sure have some fun with. Take for instance my opponents, we all are new and fresh talent here in the AWC. I bet their striving to win this match as if it were there last. Well not me. Hell I haven't even read up on these guys, I don't even know if they have said anything about me in the last few days. And it's not because I'm lazy or anything, I just know that with my current mind set I should be fine. Good luck to you other three and well I don't need luck but hey ya never know what could happen. So anyways let's talk specifics. The Atlantic Wrestling Club has decided to take a gamble and sign me on. The past year for me has being horrible to be honest and it didn't look like it was getting any better. But sometimes things do take a turn for the better. Titan puts his sunglasses back and peers off into the distance. As he looks across the beach he notices alot of families out today. He takes another long sip of his soda and than looks back over to the beach. Titan: I've honestly always liked coming here, so many people and so many things going on. And you'd think most people would get a headache from a busy beach. Well standing here actually helps me out when trying to think and concentrate and prepare myself mentally for my match. Watching these people really gets me in the mood for wrestling because I see all their problems and I see all these people getting upset and frustrated and I compare it to what I am doing, which is relaxing by the way, and I go ahead and just laugh about it. It's really a good method and it seems to always help none the less. Titan than sit's up from the hood of his car and tosses the soda can into the garbage. He slides down and than walks over and get's into his car. Of course we follow to see if he has anything else he wants to share. He fires up the engine in his car and pulls out of the parking lot. Titan: When you think about it, it's really funny. I've had my share of bumps and bruises in my career but I've never really done anything interesting to be honest. I've won some titles here and there and I've made some friends and even enemies as well but for some reason I've never really enjoyed myself anywhere else. Now here's the funny part. I look at AWC and I see alot of talent and I see a bright future. But before they signed me on something was missing, there was a little spark than the company needed and I think it was definetly hurting the company. I think, no let's say I know that I am here for something big and something special. AWC didn't sign me on for just another wrestler I think I am that spark that this company is missing. You see, that's the funny part. You may not get it now, but you will later. As we can barely contain our laughter we are reminded once again of Titan's dry and lousy humor but than again he is one of those moods where he is excited and also nervous about his match, who knows exactly what will happen tommorow night. For Titan though, I believe it's more or less just showing up and putting on your best performance. Titan: Now where was I going again? I'm pretty sure I had something important to do... With a puzzled look on his face most are wondering exactly what he is talking about as well. Titan pulls over now into a gas station and pulls up to the pumps. Titan: I almost forgot to fill up before we headed out to Fresno for tommorow night. That would of defintely sucked. Titan begins filling up the tank of gas and just looks around, he also notices the price on the sign at the gas station, his eye's open up wide. Titan: Well not everything can be perfect I guess, you could always hope and wish but sometime you just can't have it all. Thank god it's not any higher though, I had to use the last of my savings to get to Fresno, would of being a shame if anything did happen. Well anyways, I'm out folks. Enjoy the show. Titan gets back into his car after paying the young man the money he owed him for the gas and speeds off headed for Fresh! Hopefully the next words we do hear from Titan will be a little more inspiring.
