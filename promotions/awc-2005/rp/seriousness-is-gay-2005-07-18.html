Seriousness Is Gay

18 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th July Andy M. paced around the middle of a wrestling ring in the gym he had purposefully built at the heart of his Floridian mansion. Whether or not Andy’s deceased oil-tycoon uncle Alfred had intended for his nephew to erect a gym in the middle of the place when he left the house to Andy in his will was questionable, but hey, who gives a damn? The ring itself had suffered some wear and tear, and the sides were lined with badders reading “Andy M. Rules!” and little else. It sat on polished, varnished wooden floor, no padding in sight, at the heart of the large gym, which housed a wide array of exercise machines. Sunlight poured in through the long, tall windows and the skylights, reflecting off of the white walls to make the gym very bright indeed. Today, the AWC newcomer was wearing a white, sleeveless t-shirt with the words “Eating Emo Kids for Breakfast Since 1977” printed on the front in red, a pair of black sport pants and some white Nike trainers. Andy’s hair was as immaculately well-groomed as ever, and he had his hands taped up, as if he were about to begin a work-out session. Andy bounced back-first off of a set of ropes, before boxing with the air a couple of times and grinning… “Never wrestle again? What a crock of shit,” muttered Andy under his breath. This Tuesday evening was to be a monumental occasion for the self-proclaimed Scottish King of Cool; it would be make or break. For this week, on Fresh!, Andy will step into a competitive wrestling match for the first time in roughly 17 months. His problems stem back to the Summer of 2003, however, when Andy suffered a career-threatening injury after being thrown from the top of a steel cage by James “Kannon” Rande and landing awkwardly. Initially, Andy found himself sitting on the shelf for over half a year before battling back to full fitness. Re-entering the sport, Andy continued pretty much where he’d left off, and eventually found himself involved in a World Title bout against Top Dog in indy promotion Online Championship Wrestling. Sadly for Andy, towards the end of the match he felt a familiar “twang” in the small of his back, and so, his last action inside the squared circle was to be pinned 1, 2, 3. Andy was immediately advised against wrestling by his physician; if something went wrong he could end up with permanent spine damage, or worse. But, hell, Andy made his name overcoming the odds, and was determined to do so again. This could prove to be a moment of genius, or a moment of incredible stupidity. Either way, as Andy readied himself for Fresh! and Gabriel, he felt almost rejuvenated. The two mahogany double doors leading into the gym crept own. Andy turned, startled, to notice his Portuguese butler Alvaro, whose legality as a citizen of the United States of America was questionable, shambling into the room. The tanned 32 year-old was dressed in an immaculate tuxedo as always, and carried a platter in one of his hands. Andy addressed him immediately. “What’s this, something to eat?” Andy enquired, “I’m just about to start my routine…” Alvaro glanced up at his boss, continuing to walk slowly, so as not to drop the platter. “Não senhor,” he mumbled under his breath. “Huh, what then?” Out of curiosity, Andy slipped through the middle rope; Alvaro stopped his his tracks before his boss. Alvaro removed the lid from the platter; a cordless telephone receiver was sat in the middle. “Telephone, eet ees Doctor Keaton…” Andy rolled his eyes; “Great,” he uttered, sarcasm dripping from his words. Dr. John Keaton had been Andy’s physician since he had first injured his back a couple of years ago. Keaton had kept in touch with Andy, not because Andy still needed any kind of treatment, but just so that he could be the first to find out about any recurring problems. Andy grabbed the telephone receiving, and with a sigh, pull it to his ear. “Hello?” he said, greeting Keaton. “Andy, what the hell!?” the doctor was quick to snap back. He spoke in a sharp, quick and quite soft-tone of voice, that was about as aurally pleasing as fingernails down a blackboard. “What’s this Atlantic Wrestling Club shit I’m hearing about? Please tell me it’s all just a rumour?” “It’s no rumour; I’m coming back.” Andy replied. However, before he could respond, he was immediately cut off by his furious doctor, who bleeted; “What!?! You can’t do that, what about your back? If you should make one tiny little mistake, you could end up being paralysed! It’s far too dangerous; I think you should pull out immediately.” “Screw it, John, I’ve sat around here for far too long. I’ve got to get back in there!” His physician sighed on the other end of the phone. “Andy, listen to me, you CAN’T do this anymore; I’m sorry, but your days inside a ring are numbered…” “I don’t want to hear it,” Andy replied, he wasn’t buying what his doctor was saying at all. “I’m going back… this is what I live for, it’s what I am, and it’s a risk I’m willing to take! Never wrestle again? Screw that, I’m back, baby!” Keaton attempted to blast out a response, but as the first syllable left his mouth he found himself hung up on by Andy. Shaking his head, Andy placed the phone back down in the centre of the platter, and nodded in acknowledgement of Alvaro. He turned around back towards the ring, ready to get back to work, but as he pulled himself up onto the outside of the canvas, the doors leading into the gymnasium flew open once again. Cayle M., Andy’s younger brother, rushed through with intent in his step, nearly knocking the exiting Alvaro off of his feet. He was shorter than the 6’7” Andy by a good six inches, and his hair was a good deal longer. He wore a red bandana with a plain white Nike t-shirt and some blue jeans. “Andy, quick!” Cayle yelped with the slightest of Scottish accents. “What is it, brother?” “It’s Snowball, she’s gone!” Andy raised an eyebrow. “Gone?” “She’s not in the pen, all the others are there, just not Snowball. I found the cage door wide open, somebody must have let her out…” Snowball was Andy’s prized possession, and the crown jewel of his collection of rare Bolivian badgers, which, after extensive genetic mutation, just happened to breathe fire. Andy realised that unless they located the badger immediately she could probably burn down a good sized portion of the house. Andy leapt down to the floor, recognising the urgency of the situation immediately. “Then we must dash! To the badger pen!” ------------------- Moments later, the M. brothers found themselves outside on this beautiful Floridian afternoon. They walked with haste, knowing that there was no time to lose if this perilous situation was to be resolved! Walking passed the swimming pool, (the tiling on which read “Andy is cooler than you”), Andy began to talk to his slightly more level-headed sibling. “Have you got the keys?” he enquired. “No need,” replied Cayle, “I left the pen door open. The cage is closed though, don’t worry about that…” “Good, ‘cos the last thing we want is another one of those Bolivian mothercluckers running around! It is of the utmost importance that we find Snowball as soon as possible, we don’t want that psychopath reducing my wonderful home to ash…” Soon enough, they reached a wooden shack at the side of the building that looked like it belonged in the Canadian wilderness more than anywhere else. The building was windowless, and the words “Badger Pen, Keep Out!” had been crudely painted into the door, which was slightly ajar. Slipping into the hut, Andy glanced around the cage which took up the vast majority of the floor space. The other badgers seemed to be at peace, and were all either sleeping or munching on some lettuce. Andy paused for contemplation. “The others don’t seem to have noticed,” Cayle pointed out. “They haven’t started burning things yet…” “Yeah, so if you were a genetically mutated Bolivian fire-breathing badger, where the hell would you go, Cayle?” Andy enquired, stroking his chin. “Bolivia? Sheesh, I don’t know, they’re not exactly your average household pet…” Andy grimaced, “Well obviously, they’re far better than a freaking dog or a cat! But whatever, we gotta get moving, brother!” With that, Andy brushed carelessly passed his brother, who followed him outside. Andy took a deep breath, taking in the spectacular surroundings; the red-bricked country house was surrounded by immaculately-trimmed lawns, which were lined with healthy-looking trees. There were plenty of places for a small, woodland creature to seek refuge, but badgers don’t exactly travel at light-speed; Snowball can’t have gone too far. “Okay,” Andy began, “We’ll do a quick scan of the perimeter…” With a nod, Cayle followed his brother, as they began to make their way around the opposite side of the house from which they had come. Feet crunching a-top the gravel surface, Cayle struggled to keep up with the larger strides of his brother, as they passed a couple of wall-climbing plants. “Hmmm, Grob’s door is open, Alvaro must be feeding that badboy…” Andy said, pointing towards a rather grim-looking entrance to the building. The open door seemed to lead down a staircase, which was lit by a couple of hanging bulbs. The wooden braces looked like they were rotting, and the stone walls were grim and black – in fact, it looked a lot like a mine.” Cayle glanced at the open door, and continued to follow his brother. “AIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEE!”… A sudden shriek filled the air, accompanied by a clatter, startling Andy and Cayle. They halted in their tracks, and turned around, as Alvaro sprinted out from the entrance at full-speed. “SON OF A BEEEEEEEETCH!” he yelped. Andy and his brother exchanged perplexed glances. “What the hell?” Cayle questioned. “Maybe the ‘Schnit bit his finger again?” “Hmmm, I’ve never seen Alvaro run quite like THAT! Let’s check it out!” Andy jogged across towards the grimy entrance, flanked by his brother, and made his way down the rickety spiral staircase. Several peculiar noises were being emitted from the bottom, prompting Andy and Cayle to slow down and move with a little more caution. “Okay, this is… interesting…” whispered Cayle. Andy raised his finger to his lips, as if to tell Cayle to keep his mouth shut. At the bottom of the staircase, the place looked more like a medieval dungeon than anything else, its stone walls lit up by flame-torches. There, spurting fire without a care, was the obvious cause of Alvaro’s fright. “Snowball…” Andy muttered quietly, so as not to disturb the fire-breather. Alvaro, you see, had a mortal fear of the badgers, and refused to go anywhere near them. However, the air was filled with far stranger noises than the sound of a fire-breathing badger, believe it or not… “OWWWWWCH! Stopp it u fagit or izz gona kik yur azz!” “Oh no… Not Grobschnit!” Andy whispered, his face full of concern for the Notorious S-C-H-N-I-T. Snowball was busy tormenting Grobschnit, which stood backed into a corner behind bars, severely pissed-off. Looking rather horrible and green, with large black orb-like eyes, the living legend known as Grobschnit was either an alien or a guy from New Jersey with an awesome make-up job (delete as appropriate). An ally of Andy’s for a long time, he had recently been located to Andyville’s dungeon after he attempted to make love with one of many badgers that Andy had kept on the estate. Perhaps, Snowball was out for revenge? It should also be noted that although the ‘Schnit did speak English, it was very, very poor English. “What are we gonna do, Andy?” Cayle asked, “Snowball’s gonna toast Grobber! He’s got nowhere to run!” “Hmmmm…” Andy hummed, “Snowball!” Snowball, startled by Andy’s intervention, turned around and launched fire in the direction of the brothers, who had to leap back to avoid the burst. Snowball snarled before immediately returning here attentions back towards Grobschnit. “JESUS CHRIST!” Cayle yelled. “Okay, out with the easy way… we’re gonna have to take this motherfucker OUT!” Andy proclaimed. “Well, how do you expect us to do that? She’ll just burn us, dude!” “Aha… but although deadly, these creatures do have one slight weakness…” Cayle watched on, as his brother crept down the few remaining stairs towards the cold, hard stone floor. “ANDEE!” Grobschnit yelled rather foolishly, as Snowball turned back towards her owner. However, as she did so, she was immediately met with a swift, hard soccer kick to the jaw, sending her flying across the dungeon, and against a cold, concrete wall. “HOLY CRAP!” Cayle yelled, leaping down towards his brother. “YOU KILLED IT!” “Nah, no way,” Andy began, a grin across his face, “These bastards are as tough as leather boots! She’ll be knocked out for a couple of hours at best…” Cayle approached the down badger, and took a good look; surely the poor little critter’s frame should have been squashed by the impact? But nay, everything appeared to be in order. Meanwhile, Grobschnit was in a state of exuberance. “Haha biatch yoo goht owned!” Andy turned, not very impressed with the whole debacle. “What the hell happened, Grobby?” Andy enquired. “Wel dat butlur uv yoorz woz came wit foode fur da Grob, buht woz attak bye da badger, ‘n she eat da foode and den try to atak mee!” How in the hell Andy managed to understand that was anyone’s guess. “Hmmm, okay Grobby, we shall get to the bottom of this, find the perpetrator that let the damn badger out of the cage, and put them to justice!” he replied. “Heeey Andee kan eye get owt uv da caij fur a while?” Grob enquired, a look of hope filling his black, oily eyeballs. “Can you hell! You tried to mate with one of my badgers dude, you’re staying down here… Now come on Cayle, we must take Snowball back to her pen before she revives, otherwise all hell is going to break lose!” And with that, we left the brothers in a dungeon with a strange green creature called Grobschnit and an unconscious Bolivian dragon-like badger. Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiight.
