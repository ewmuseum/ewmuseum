sick of being tired, tired of being sick. crush me, baby.

25 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: back there I was rollin' around, in my mind it occurred What if God was a her? I know you didn’t have to save me., I say Would I treat her the same? Would I still be runnin' game on her? In what type of ways would I want her? You don’t quite understand our relationship, do you?, she says. She brushes hair out of her eyes, but for some reason can’t keep those strands from falling back in to place. It’ll be a few moments until she interrupts herself once more to do the same thing. Would I want her for her mind or her heavenly body? Couldn't be out gettin' bogus with someone so godly I get it, I say, but for some reason I can’t get past the fact at how lucky I am. If she could do it, she’d lean in and kiss me on the cheek. Then I’d blush, but this doesn’t happen. We cannot make these types of exchanges. If I was wit' her would I still be wantin' my ex? The lies, the greed, the weed, the sex Last time I checked, you aren’t lucky., she says. We sit facing each other, but it never really matters how we sit. You’re haunted. Essentially our communication can’t get any clearer, but sometimes it’s just more comforting to see her face. Wouldn't be ashamed to give her part of my check Wearin' her cross, I mean the heart on my neck It’s going to get harder, I tell her. She knows that we’re going to be taking things with a different approach, now. She knows that she can’t be involved as before. I might lose you. Her I would reflect on the streets of the Chi' Ride wit' her, 'cause I know for me she'd die Cupping her jaw, she blows the renegade strands up and out of her sight. She lightly says to me, We’ve all got to grow up sometime. Through good and bad call on her like I'm chirpin' her Couldn't be jealous 'cause other brothers worship her That’s not exactly what I want to hear, but the way she interprets my body language and gets sappy for forgiveness, its exactly what I’ve come to know and love. Come on, kiddo. She leans in close, shifting her seat next to mine, and says, You know we’ve got a lot of work to do before I’m finished with you. Walk this earth for her, glory, I'm grateful To be in her presence I try to stay faithful And that’s when I take offense. I’m sick of being a pawn, I say. I wish I could have amounted to more sometimes. I suppose that’s why I’ll be doing this sooner than later. But for some reason, a growing and unexplainable restlessness is chilling at the base of my gut. It keeps whispering to me that there are bigger plans coming into play. Something is telling me that I’ll be much more important than I am. And I know that she’s in on it. But now it’s all different. All night she’s been trying to figure something out; like why everything’s so quiet all of a sudden. She didn’t count on it, but now I can control certain aspects of our relationship. Now she can’t read my thoughts anymore unless I choose to let her. Now I can retain somewhat of my sanity that isn’t reserved for a single given day in the month. Now I’m not so crazy, as opposed to being on the EXTREME-end of the spectrum. words by Common (Sense)
