Date: 1st August 2006 Everyone wants to be a hero, everyone has someone who they want to make proud whether it be a mother a father… a family. The shame of letting someone down is sharp and painful like pulling a thousand plasters off your skin. No one wants to be labelled a coward and no one wants to be a disappointment no one wants to feel that pain. If you asked 100 men if they considered themselves a coward 99 of those men will answer no but the truth is we are all cowards. There have all been times where we all have hidden under a blanket of cowardess and there are hundreds of situations where we use brave words but we don’t know how we’d really behave under these dangerous circumstances. But sometimes we do cowardly things not for our own gain, the French in World War 2 for instance. France had suffered very heavy losses in WW1 and was many Frenchmen were willing to pay a high price to avoid a repetition of the carnage. The Great Depression had resulted in France becoming a bitterly divided country. No other well established democracy became as deeply divided as France in the 1930s. When the left-wing Popular Front came to power in 1936, some French conservatives seem to have felt that it was no longer the France that they knew and loved and were willing to fight for. Perhaps it’s was in their best interest to surrender for the benefit of their families. From that day on no one has let France live it down and they had succumb to the shame of cowardess. Charles Darling stands with his hands covering his mouth and bristly moustache thinking of the French during the war, trying to justify the fact that he just dipped Red Rock in hot lava to save his own skin. It would seem that Charles has temporarily shifted the buck on to Red Rock, putting him in the ravaging path of the unhinged cockney dodgy deal Dev Jackson. A man who is willing to explore all the savagery and deep depravity that most men would suppress in the back of their minds. A man would have no one but himself to look out for, there fore everyone else to him is either an insignificant pawn or just plain insignificant. These are things that cascade through Red Rock’s mind like reckless white water rapids. Red Rock paces up and down his kitchen; nervously chewing the skin around his thumb nails whilst fearfully concocting plans to escape from this now very dangerous situation.
