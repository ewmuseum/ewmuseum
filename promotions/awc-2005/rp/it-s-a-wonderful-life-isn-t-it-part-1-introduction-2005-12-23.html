It's a wonderful Life. (Isn't it?) Part 1: Introduction

23 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/12/05 Times were hard for Mike. This was not a good thing. Especially at Christmas time. His plan to gain himself more respect the easy way had backfired just last week sending him straight to a New York city jail. Kidnapping Macys Santa Claus and then holding him hostage in a Reservoir Dogs style situation will always do that for you. Every year Mike Wade enjoyed Christmas like as if he were 8 years old again. Learning the 'awful truth' almost killed him as a child. Some mean little bastard told him when he was 9. Just because he was poor and never got anything his parents told him 'it'. You know the 'it' right? The 'it' that makes Christmas what it is. Not Christ dying on the cross, even though that's what Christmas is. But we're talking about the jolly old fat man here. When young Master Wade found out his deep, dark secret it disgusted him. The disgust he faced and felt that day at the tender age of 9 when he found out Santy wasn't real was something so strong he thought he'd never feel that type of disgust again, how wrong he was. Fast forward 16 years later and what do you know? Mike Wade has achieved his dream of becoming a pro wrestler. And a damn successful one at that. He's held titles in PCW, NTICW, PRIME and is a Double AWC Champion as we speak. But it would take 16 years from being 9 to 25 for Mike Wade to feel the same disgust that he felt that day. The disgust was what AWC was doing to him. First it started when people said he was nothing but second fiddle to Paddy O Shea. Of course Wade scoffed at the pathetic remark. Paddy beats the 'Uber Judge', a homosexual whom its rumoured allowed Paddy to win if he was allowed to rim-job him, then that very week myself and Adam Dick beat Paddy and his husband/ brother/ son (you know gypsies) and won the Alliance titles. However Paddy was a simple cone in Mikes road. A cone which Mike rolled over on last weeks Fresh! of course. And then TFW'd his slut girlfriend. No Paddy was easy pickings for Wade. But of course after Wade defeated Paddy and his sibling for the Alliance titles and of course ran him over. Wade doesn't get his match he's been waiting the last 6 months for. No in fact Paddy O Shea gets placed higher up the card then Wade and even gets a Transatlantic title shot. Paddy O Shea doesn't even deserve a Transexual title shot. On the official AWC website Mike has been rated the #1 contender to Tim Shipleys Frontier title for weeks, months, for what feels like years. Instead a reject from the 'Livewire' title division gets the shot. Red Rock? What the fuck is that? More like Red Ring. You know, that rash you get around your cock when you blow off and don't wipe it properly. If all that didn't seem like Wade was getting the shaft AWC officials booked him against a Kiddie Fiddler. That's right. This week on PPV it's gonna read: AWC Relentless title Cup of Blood Match: Mike Wade (C) vs Gary Glitter Ok it might not read that but it may as well! This week, if it isn't bad enough, that they booked me against a guy modelling his wrestling name on a reject biker gimmick the guy also fucks kids. Michael Jackson and the Glitter Boy (Not Hate) don't have anything on this guy. Well at least I know he won't be able to get his cup of blood from his girlfriends periods. Why? Because 9 year olds don't get em, you sick fucker! Yes it was safe to say Mike Wade wasn't happy. But it may have been worse then that. His friends, the Wade Parade and tag partner Adam Dick, 'The Face' as he liked to be called, had seen a different side to Wade. He had talked about life in AWC not being worth living. Maybe he had a point? He was being wasted there. He agreed to talk about his feelings to his 'shrink' Dr Sherman. =========================================================================================================================== At Dr Shermans =========================================================================================================================== We join everyone's favourite AWC Double Champ, Mike Wade, inside the office of his Shrink. Just like before he lies on 'the couch' that has become a centrepiece of every Shrink office probably in the world. He lies feet facing towards the door, with his gelled head nestled nicely within the confines of the tan leather couch, just like before. It seems like just another visit to the 'shrink'. Mike lies down on the couch and talks about his feelings for an hour while the good doctor tries to make sense out of this muddle of a man. However this time was different. Although he was Irish, Mike didn't usually drink. Now he was by no means a pioneer but he wasn't guzzling back pints and whiskeys as if he were Chainz plying a young child with alcohol. But on this day, on this visit to the shrink Mike was not well. He had been up all night drinking, alone. His own personal issues with his ego were causing him to meltdown. His low tier match on the pay per view against a shit house was doing nothing for his problems. He was right now in the stage between drunk and hung over. The black bags under his eyes revealed a whole world of struggle that is going on inside of him. He had done his best with his appearance this morning but there's no way to cover up the bags. Dr Sherman was looking to delve deep. He too was becoming worried about Mike. Dr Sherman: Mike, what do you think is really wrong with you? It was a harsh question. It was hard to take, but Sherman was wanting to ask that question since cry baby Wade walked into his office all those weeks ago. Mike was taken aback, but attempted to answer the question anyway. Mike: It's all about the wrestling Shrink. Mike continually called him that. Sherman hated it but it was going on too long now, he couldn't get Wade to stop. Dr Sherman: So it's your pprofessional life rather then your personal? Mike: Yeah exactly. My personal life is my own, it's my space. I control it how I wish and don't let anyone in if I don't have to. Dr Sherman: And why not your professional one? Mike: I don't make those bookings Shrink. Dr Sherman: And why are these 'bookings' getting to you? Mike: I'm on an 8 match win streak right? I've got two belts. I've kicked Paddy O Shea around for the last 2 months. But the man I've been beating up and making a fool out of week after week is against the Transatlantic champion at the pay per view and I have to fight Chainz! Dr Sherman: Brian Lee? Mike: No. Dr Sherman: Oh I always thought that..... Mike: Me too, I cant make head nor tail of it. I'm just sick of people getting picked ahead of me shrink you know. I've been told that it's the Relentless title that's been holding me back too. There was a pause. It was mostly from Dr Shermans side, as he thought he may have found the answer. The Relentless title was holding Mike back. Dr Sherman: Well Mike why don't you just lose your Relentless title? Mike: Lose? Please Shrink. I don't lose. And even if I were to lose for one night only, there's no way I'd lose to Crush. Dr Sherman: I thought it was Chainz? Mike: Whatever. There's no way I'd lay down for that! Dr Sherman: Well what about simple laying down the belt, dropping it? This seemed like a completely reasonable suggestion. Mike wouldn't have to lose to anyone. And no more being held back the sky weas the limit! Mike: Na to be honest Shrink I can't go for that. Dr Sherman: Why? Mike: I won this title from Ian English. That bastard hung over me like a shaddow over my career for years, I finally got rid of him by winning this title from him, then I defended it against everyone that was thrown at me. Including 5 other people at Triangles, one of whom is the schmuck I'm against this week, I have become the man you think about about when you hear Relentless championship. I have made this title bigger then it ever could have been, just like I did with the 5 Star title in P**ME. My Relentless title is higher in the card on this PPV then Tim Shipleys Frontier title. My belt has gone from a low tier hardcore belt to a sister belt to the Frontier title and I won't stop until it is recognised as such. And long after I'm gone and AWC and the Relentless title are still hear they will rename it: The Mike Wade is a fucking legend Championship Dr Sherman: I see. What else could you say to that speech? Wade was determined to keep the title but Sherman was determined to get to the end of Wades problems. Dr Sherman: Your mood swings are worrying too though Mike. One day you're in here with me talking and joking and a few days later you run down a man in a Ford Transit van and attempt to kill him and his girlfriend. How do you explain that? Mike: Fuck it he deserved it. He sent me to Mexico in a box. Its called revennge Doc you should try it some time. And as for his 'girlfriend' she was all over me like a cheap suit at the Xmas party. So I snogged her the slut. Dr Sherman: Your behaviour puzzles me a whole lot Mike. These mood swings, the violence, the jealousy the ego problems and then there's the homoerotic nature of your living quarters. Mike: DId you just call me a queer? Dr Sherman: Not at all. I'm questioning the fact that you surround yourself with a group of inferior males, that all live in your house. Some short, some tall, some liars and 2 closet homosexuals. Does this seem...... Mike: HEY!! First of all there are no gays in the Parade. And secondly my Wade Parade are my friends, my fans and my allies. I will not have some shrink like you loking down your nose on us....... I guess these were the mood swings he talked about. Mike probably didn't even realise that he was doing this and he probably wouldn't remember later on. But for now he's kicked off. All the good Doctor good do if he knew what was good for him was sit there and take it. So he did. Mike: ................and another thing I sure as hell am not gay. You sit there in your chair acting all high and mighty and tell me to lose on purpose. I don't know what kinda sick shrink-booker you think you are but I'm outta here. And then he said it. The line that may change his perception on life forever..... Mike: It's fuckers like you who make me wish I just didn't exist! That was it. Mike got up from the infamous couch and headed straight from the door, which he slammed almost immediately upon opening. It was not the Doctors fault. Mike was on edge. His ego was so fragile he couldn't take any critisisms. It was safe to say that this session was over. However it wouldn't be till he got home that the puzzle would really come together. =========================================================================================================================== Back at Wade Manor =========================================================================================================================== Wade Manor. For the first time since perhaps it's inception there was a sound that was never heard there before. There had been many sounds made there before. Moans, groands, screams, snores and whores. Granted whores isn't a sound but Mike was fond of escort girls. All those sounds, but never this. Silence. The Wade Parade was on Christmas vacation and Mike wished he was on his. He longed for the big halls and rooms of the real Wade Manor. The country estate owned by his parents. But it had been years since he'd spent a few minutes with them, let alone an entire Christmas. Mike sat in his single sit leather recliner with a brandy glass in hand. The glass was un-swishable, full to the brim with brandy or some sort of liquor. Not the recomended way to use the glass. But Mike didn't care. He was only usually a semi-social drinker. But since the AWC Christmas party had taken his sober-woes away he couldn;t stop. Was he becomming an alco? It was possible. It ran in his family. However there was a deeper story here. Mike Wade had said that he wished he didn't exist. The only way huanly possible to do that is to die. Mike Wade was trying to do what so many poor unfortunate souls had done before him. Drink himself to death. Mike didn't want to be alive anymore. Or so we're led to believe. His push was non existant despite his talents, he was alone on Christmas, even his Double Gold coudn't comfort him. Mike needed a saviour. And he got it! As Mike sat, his head hunched over in a depressive state as the brandy glass met his awaiting lips a bright light shone there on Wade manor. Mikes attention was caught he jumped from his seat in wonderment. Was it the NYPD coming back to get him after his attempted kidnap on Santa? Suddenly the light broke, and there in Wade Manor stood a man, a man who shouldn't be appearing through rays of light. Not that any man should. It was Adam Dick? Mike: Face what are you doing here? Adam: I am your guardian angel. Mike: You snake in the grass you kept that quiet this whole time! Adam: No Mike I'm not actually Adam Dick. Mike: Jesus you look well like him. Are you his brother? Adam: No. I just took the form of a person you would recognise. Mike: And you couldn't have chose Jenna Jameson? Adam: For if you layed eyes opon my spiritual body you would implode and cease to exist. Mike: That's great and it's actually what I'm looking for. So if you could just show me your tits or poon or something we'll get on out of here. Adam: Mike I'm hear to........ Adam fixates on a wet patch on the crotch of Mike Wade PJ's. Mike: Hey fuck you I didn't piss myself I jumped up when I saw your gay light show and spilled brandy all over myself! Adam: Sure. Anyway I'm here to show you exactly what effect you have on peoples lives and how miserable they'd be without you in em. We need you to exist Mike Wade and I'll show you why!
