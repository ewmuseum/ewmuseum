Vincent Webb p5 (2)

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alice Penn I looked down at the spot where Steven was also looking, seeing that it was true. The tires had a long slash in it, thwarting any chance of using the vehicle for a while. I look away in fear as Steven pounds viciously on the vehicles, showing his frustration. He looks up at me, a drop of sweat going down from his forehead, over the scar that I've noticed so many times. 'Alice,' he says, his voice firm, 'we need to get you out of here. And fast.' I shake my head, noticing that he said that we needed me, not us, away from here. 'No, not without you.' 'Listen to me Alice. They aren't here for you. They are here for me. I don't want to harm you further than I already have,' he says in finality. 'But… Will I ever see you again?' I ask. 'You've saved my life, Steven. I need to repay you somehow.' 'No,' he says, sticking his hand in the air. 'It was my fault from the beginning. If I hadn't gotten you into it, you wouldn't have been involved.' 'If you hadn't gotten me into it, you'd be dead right now and you know it,' I bark back. 'Now you're coming with me one way or another.' Suddenly, I hear a loud thundering noise coming from the hotel. I feel myself being thrown to the ground a split second later, and I feel Steven's body laying over me, protecting me. He jumps in between myself and the hotel and begins to talk to me quietly but firmly. 'Get across the road and hide in those bushes over there,' he says as I peak around my vehicle at the other side, noticing the bushes. I nod. 'Do you hear me?' he says. I could've laughed had the situation not been serious. 'Yes, Steven, I hear you.' 'Go, go RIGHT NOW,' he says, getting to his feet. I follow his actions, but as he nears the hotel, I near the road. As multiple cars pass by, I get an opening and run for the opposite side, making it safely. I hide in the bushes, but peak through to watch Steven's actions. Steven Xandrous I run quickly at the door, knowing that Alice will be safe. I peak inside, seeing no one. Then, sitting in the far back corner of the room is the same man who attempted to kill me. I sigh, seeing that next to him is the woman that runs the hotel, sweating and crying. I pull out my gun and shoot the door open, charging inward. My gun is pointed directly at the man’s head, and his is pointed at mine. “Don’t you remember what I told you?” I say, trying to intimidate him with my voice. “Shut up, damn it. Now either you come with me, or the woman dies,” he says fiercely. “What makes you think I give a damn about this woman? And what makes me think that I even believe you for that matter?” I say. “Because,” he says, smirking, “you’re that kind of guy.” I step nearer. “You don’t know shit about me.” “I know more than you’d think, Steven. Mister Webb has quiet the information. Date of birth, height, weight, street address, fighting tactics… everything,” he says, the smirk still on his face and the gun still steady. “So what’s it going to be, Steven?” I fire quickly, as does he. I hear the man scream as a piercing pain enters my shoulder. Alice Penn I hear two gun shots, and my worries come back as strong as ever. I am, however, very relieved as I see who I believe is Steven stumbling out of the door. That is until I notice that Steven has a gun in his hand, and the other hand his clasped on his shoulder. I can tell by the way he moved that he’d been shot. He looks in my direction, stumbling onto the road. In the one instance, it seems as though everything changed. It seemed as though a huge flash of light had struck, and the next second, Steven was on the ground, lying motionless. I hear and see a black car speed away into the night, leaving their victim to rot. I rush up to him quickly, wondering what had fully happen. Steven lies there conscious and breathing. I use all my strength to pull him up to his feet and put his weight onto mine. I walk him slowly over to my vehicle and open the back door and lay him inside, trying to find a way to get him to the hospital. I look around me. I see one car. Although it is beat up and ugly, it would do the trick. I assume it is the hotel owner’s, so I quickly run into the building. The woman is sitting near the door, sobbing franticly. When she sees me, she points over to the far corner of the room, where a man lie, blood dripping down his neck where there is a dark red hole, at least a centimeter in diameter. I turn toward the woman, trying to talk over her sobs. “Look, give me the keys to your car, please. I need to get my bo—my friend to a hospital,” I say, nearly yelling. She reaches into her pocket with one hand, the other pointing at the corpse. She pulls out a set of keys and hands them to me. “Thank you ma’am…” I rush outside and to the tan-colored car. How could this happen in broad daylight? A murder and a rundown… with no one but me to see it. I quickly jab all the keys into the lock, trying to find the right key to unlock the door. Finally, it opens. I hop in, putting the key into the ignition. I start it up, relieved that it still runs. I run up to my car, grabbing Steven onto my shoulder, and transfer him into the other car, laying him into the backseat. Steven Xandrous I open my eyes, seeing the roof of a car and nothing more. I feel the car moving, but it seems very subtle. Suddenly, for a split second, all I see is white. As the white fades, I see a man who I can only identify to be...myself. My throat has a long, red line across it and my eyes have been blackened. I feel my hands tied behind myself as the life slides away from my grasp, the air coming more slowly each second. I look to my left and I see a man, wearing all black. He turns towards me, seeing my eyes open. I feel something hard smack up against my face as I fall to the floor of the car. I hear the men talking, but I can’t quiet understand them. I feel myself being hurled from the car onto the soggy ground, the mud breaking my fall. Alice Penn I can only watch as the doctors set Steven onto a stretcher, wheeling him into the hospital. The tears begin to come to me as he is pulled away. I wipe the tears away and get back into the car and drive myself home, forgetting about the car’s owner.
