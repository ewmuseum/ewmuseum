Reality slaps and dead bodies

16 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th February 2006 We all know how frustrating it can be when your life feels like you’re running in to a brick wall over and over again. After you’re blood begins to rolls out of the gash on your head from vigorous contact frustration begins to rear its awkward head. But for the more motivationally challenged it takes longer to reach this point. So this person is happy to continue to run in to this hypothetical wall for longer than say someone who knows where they want to be and how to get there. However there comes a time in this idiot’s life when they begin to doubt whether the wall will eventually fall down just by ramming their heads in to it and perhaps maybe they need someone to show them they could simply walk around. Red Rock is a model of idiocy described as above he is far too lazy and stupid to figure out how to lift himself from the wrong side of the tracks. All it would take from him is a bit of hard work and maybe to train once in a while but instead he chooses to spend his time watching repeats of Red Dwarf whilst eating cold custard from the can. And they say you can judge a man by the company he keeps, just take D’avid for example. How desperate must a man become to hang around with a pale Dutch sex pest? He could make a better effort to get involved with his work colleagues but instead of that he has managed to concede from any interaction from any of them. In fact the only interaction he has had with anyone recently landed him in an Ottoman torture chamber match with Captain Suleimon. Red Rock just doesn’t understand other people and it quite often lands him in trouble but now he has got himself involved in a new level of trouble. Hell has no fury like a pissed off Muslim, just look what happened over those pictures of the prophet Mohammed! The Danish embassy is FUCKED! But with this in mind Red Rock still seems to be able to get on with his mundane routine following life style… Senior Citizen: Bah…typical buses always late! Red Rock is at Aldershot bus stations waiting for his bus back to his flat down town. He has just been enjoying a day off in the town centre for a spot of afternoon food shopping…he has come back with a loaf of brown bread and tin of soup. It is one of those typical British days which they all love to complain about, there isn’t a ray of sun light to be seen which encourages everyone to wield their umbrellas. Red Rock sits down on the bus stop bench running his fingers up and down a brown corduroy jacket as an expression of his discomfort towards this man who is trying to start the most cliché of convocations. Senior Citizen: You know they take hours to get here and – Red Rock: YEAH…I get it they all turn up at once! Red Rock rolls his eyes away from this overly bitter senior citizen as he folds his arms and crosses his legs. Senior Citizen: Damn straight! Red Rock avoids eye contact with the old man who tips his flat cap at Red Rock who he believes wants to talk to him. This only enforces Red Rock’s belief that old people seem to have this delusion that everyone wants to talk to them and they’re always right. And despite Red Rock’s barefaced attempt to cut off the convocation the rather fat old man persists. Senior Citizen: I’ll tell you what I blame the council! They’re sodding useless I’m going to sue them I tell you! Red Rock exhumes an obviously loud sigh but it seems this old man wants to vent his frustrations on someone and Red Rock is the poor guy who is next to him. Senior Citizen: I’ll sue them for unfair dismissal and I’ll sue my old lawyers as well! This old man was a former council employee that was fired for falling asleep at the wheel whilst driving a bus and accidentally crashing it in to someone’s house. He got banned from driving and got 6 months in jail... it’s this part he misses out when he gasses on. Red Rock: Look I don’t want to be rude Sir but… Senior Citizen: I’ll get them for this! I lost my wife and kids because of them. Red Rock: I don’t care… Senior Citizen: And they’re cutting my benefits! I’ll loose my council flat! Red Rock: SO let me get this straight? You hate them, yet you’ll life in the house they provide use the bus service they provide and claim benefits they provide? Fuck off! Then for the first time in a long time the Senior citizen ends his old tired rant before the end. Red Rock expresses a stinking frown whilst he glares forward and it is at this point we become aware of his surroundings, his life, and his home. The skyline is ruined by high rise blocks of flats occupied by drop out and drug addicts. The walls surrounding the bus station have been soiled by graffiti. The sound of young teenager in the distance can be heard in the background adding to a sense of discomfort to the good people on their way home. These teenagers with their track suits and hoodies are holding squashed half empty bottles of the cheapest nastiest cider they can get their filthy little hands on. Red Rock pulls his own hood as a gesture to say he doesn’t want to interact with anyone. It is at these times when he shuts himself off where he lets his insecurities and fears attack his previous trail of fears. His main fear of becoming old and realising he hadn’t achieved anything. Or his irrational fear of trying to be someone perhaps his fast rise and swift fall in AWC had crushed his confidence. It all happened for Red Rock in his first week by being award freshman and gaining a main event spot. It was too soon for him he wasn’t ready for that type of exposure and pressure and the impact of the streets of London took away some of that enthusiasm and drive he had when he first joined AWC. Perhaps this is the reason for his constant failures in high profile matches and the reason Sponsors won’t touch him. These fears and insecurities grind at Red Rock and hack away from him like a rusty saw tearing through flesh and crunching through bone. A prolonged agonising feeling like he is being tortured by a dark image of his own self pity. It’s suiting how he faces physical torture at Bloodlust to compliment his mental torture. Voice: Hey! I’m talking to you!! As Red Rock gazed through this window of misery he had managed to block out the outside world enough to render this voice completely inaudible to him. It took this person to physically push Red Rock on his shoulder to gain his attention. Red Rock shudders as if he had been woken from a bad dream and he hastily follows by throwing his hood down. Red Rock: Huh?! Whuh? Oh errr HI? Red Rock veers his head west and in front of him is a young lady who calls her self ‘Ren’. Red Rock analyses her facial expression and is over come by a feeling of confusion because she feels to be quite annoying at Red Rock. Maybe he was stood on her shoe lace or her hand bag. Red Rock surveys the area where his feet are only to discover he wasn’t standing on any of her property so he looks up at this pretty young brown haired girl who has her hands placed on his hips with a disapproving manor. Red Rock: Err… Can I help you? Ren: Yeah don’t you think you were a bit mean to that man? Red Rock: Maybe but I’ve had a bad day I always have to listen to him bitching and whinging about the council! Ren throws holds her hands up and shakes them with a sarcastic tone about her person. Ren: So what you had a bad day! Hip hooray I had a bad one too! But you don’t see me shouting at old people, he’s probably just confused! Red Rock: His piss bag was probably full… Ren’s eyebrows lower and the lines on her forehead become more prominent as she begins to find Red Rock’s lack of respect more and more irksome. Red Rock: Look I’m sure you’re a nice person you look like a nice person but you don’t kn – Ren: I know enough about you that you’re so insecure and paranoid that you take it out on other people, you’re probably so engulfed and wrapped up in your own self pity and you’re so frustrated that you can’t face up to your pathetic life that you’re angry at everyone else! This girl didn’t know who Red Rock was or anything about him but she managed to sum up Red Rock’s life in a couple of sentences. For a brief moment Red Rock felt insulted and angry that this stranger just shouted him down to the ground. And before he could construct a sentence in retaliation he realised that she was right this stranger was right. Ren stood with her arms folded in a similar fashion that Red Rock would do. Her round green eyes winced, which are just visible from under her multi coloured winter hat she was sporting and she pouted her pretty red lips to exclaim her feelings. She stood expectantly waiting to see what Red Rock has to say for himself but for the first time in a long time Red Rock was speechless, he didn’t have much to say for himself h just got SERVED. Ren: Well? Red Rock: I…I… I’m sorry. Ren: It’s no good saying sorry to me! You didn’t try and belittle and bully me! Red Rock now has a feeling of shame linger over his shoulders as his head is hung towards the floor. Red Rock: I’m not really a bad person…I’m just going through a rough time in life but. Ren: Yeah I’m sure… Red Rock scratches his head and tries to get his thoughts around the impact this girl’s words. Suddenly the number 4 bus makes its presence known with its terribly loud diesel engine in conjunction with the high pitch squealing of the brakes. Red Rock: Well…you kinda made me feel a little bit worse… Ren: SO? Red Rock: You pretty much just summed my entire life up in a couple of seconds…that hurts! Ren frowns and begins to feel a little remorseful herself at making Red Rock feel like this. She is quite a righteous soul so she is the type to stand up for one who otherwise would not be able to on their own. However with this in mind she is at the same time a loving and soft personality. She lets out a sigh and slaps Red Rock on the shoulder as she looks at Red Rock with pity in her eyes. Ren: Look I didn’t mean to upset you man I just thought you were a bit out of order. You don’t seem like such a bad guy you just got some issues! It’s quite pathetic really… Red Rock: Pathetic? Oh wow…thanks. Ren: No probs! The sound of the number 4 bus flows through the stale Aldershot air and Ren’s attention is diverted to its direction. Ren: Oh! That’s my bus…see ya around! Ren jogs over to the number 4 and joins the cue to board the dirty waiting bus. Red Rock who is still in a state of shock and confusion shouts over to her before she gets on. Red Rock: HEY! What’s your name? Ren: Ren! Red Rock: I’m Re – Oh… And before Red Rock can give his name the doors shut behind her. Ren looks back at Red Rock through the front windows and shrugs her shoulder. Red Rock stares back at her blankly with the compilation of the engine grumble and the reversing alert beeping from the bus. Red Rock: How rare… ~ Later on in the afternoon ~ Even after an hour Red Rock can’t seen to get this strange girl out of his head, it’s ridiculous that her large personality and in your face attitude contradicted Red Rock’s cynical attitude and pretty much defeated him. Red Rock walks nearer and nearer to his squalid block of flats kicking an empty coca cola bottle along the pavement. His hands are sunk in to this pockets and his face radiates an expression of deep thought. As it happens he has been thinking so much that he hasn’t realised that he left all his shopping on the bus. But he only walks on a further 20 odd yards before he begins to notice his arms feel lighter. Then it sinks in! Red Rock: FUCK IT~! Red Rock grits his teeth and clenches his fist trying to refrain from having a bitch fit and all he can do is walk on. He takes in some deep breaths with a ten count to try and calm him self down and before he knows it he is outside his block. Red Rock: Oh right! Red Rock quickly forgets about the shopping as he has reached his home and after a boring and tiresome day for the lazy young man he is looking forward to nothing more than a sit down on his favourite 20 year old tatty over used but very comfortable old sofa. The thought of slumping down and loosing him self in the lumpy padding makes all the pain go away it might even take his mind off the harsh reality slap from this strange Ren girl. Red Rock withdraws his keys and unlocks the front door then enters the building. Red Rock is greeted by the usual aroma of stale urine and damp. He looks over at the lifts only to see a sign that reads “out of order” in very sloppy hand writing. Red Rock rolls his eyes as he heads for the stairs and hauls him self up slowly, step by step. With each step Red Rock grows more inpatient and more eager to just chill out. His legs are tired and he’s eyes are feeling heavy, his head is spinning from the loud town centre noises but his face lightens up as he reaches the top of the stairs followed by his front door. A sensation of relief flows through Red Rock’s broken soul with the sound of keys jingling and the door creaking open. Red Rock: Ahhh home sweet – WHAT the fuck?! Red Rock’s almost pop out from their sockets upon noticing that his pride and joy, and his comfort zone appears to not be where it was and in it’s place is another sofa. It is different it is a pale green colour with frilly tassels around the base of it. There are large square pads and it is made from velvet. Think coming home and seeing you’re hot girlfriend has turned in to your grand mother or perhaps coming home and discovering someone had killed your child hood pet which was a German Shepard with, say a poodle? This is only some minor comparisons to how Red Rock is feeling at this moment in time. He is like a petrified child as he stands in the door way and after a further 30 seconds of over whelming shock he surveys the rest of his living area. It would seem this bombshell hadn’t quite ended yet; on the walls are framed pictures of birds and other animals. Red Rock: oh my God! What is this?! Howard: Oh hello Steven welcome home. Red Rock: Steven? Who the fucking shit is Steven?! Howard is Red Rock’s brand new flat mate chosen from a selection of paedophiles murderers, transsexuals and God knows what else. Howard is a computer programmer for Norton Anti Virus and Red Rock doesn’t know much about him but so far it isn’t looking good for Red Rock. Red Rock now focuses a dark grimace of rage in Howard’s direct who stares back with his strange crossed eyes behind his thick framed glasses. Red Rock: What the hell have you done, where is my bastarding sofa? Howard: Ah ha ha ha! I can only assume that you are referring to what I referrer as the bacteria factory. Howard scoffs and shows little remorse for being the route of Red Rock’s beloved sofa’s demise. Red Rock: I fucking loved that sofa it was so comfy! And you replaced it with that piece of SHIT? Howard: That happens to be a high sophisticated item of furniture. Red Rock: Fuck that! It looks like something my Nan would have, failing that something that I coughed up! Howard smiles back at Red Rock as he tightens the strings on a navy blue cooking apron. Red Rock attempts to contain his rage, it is infuriating for him that this strange person has entered his flat and changed everything. Red Rock’s eye begins to twitch and the veins in his head swell. He approaches Howard whilst clenching and easing his fist. Howard: I shall be preparing our evening meal shortly. And just like a switch Red Rock’s violent outburst is postponed. Red Rock: Food? Now where the enraged expression once was a huge grin can be found. It looks like there is an upside to Howard if he can cook! Red Rock: What we having? Howard: I shall be cooking roast chicken and bacon melts with barbecue sauce! Red Rock licks his lip at the sound of this rather attractive sounding meal. Red Rock: Well…I guess that sofa might be worth trying. Howard: Super duper I shall begin the cooking process. Howard makes his way over to the kitchen and Red Rock plumps himself down on the new sofa. It feels uncomfortably solid on his arse; this induces an aura of disappointment around Red Rock along with a sorrowful frown. But then again Red Rock hears the clanging of cooking trays and the ripping of food packaging and he slowly begins to except the sacrifice of his favourite sofa. Red Rock shuffles and fidgets on the spot; attempting to recreate an indentation for his bum much like the one on his old one. His face could easily be likened to a face you would be sporting if you were taking one of those poos that just won’t come out! The friction of denim and velvet against one another begin to get slightly louder and the frustration grows more dominant. Then out of nowhere a frantic erratic pounding is being drummed on the front door. Red Rock: What the fuck does D’avid want? Yes that incoherent rhythm can be only one person, it is D’avid no less; Red Rock’s ginger Dutch sex pest friend. Red Rock easily hops off the new sofa much less reluctantly than he would with his old comfy sofa. The loud rapping on the front door doesn’t stop until Red Rock pulls the door open and D’avid spills in to the Red Rock’s flat nearly falling over his own feet. He seems to be very over excited, which raises Red Rock’s level of curiosity. D’avid: Hello to you Red Rock, Hello to you Howard! Red Rock: D’avid, what are you so excited about? D’avid: What am I so excited about? Mr Mclumpher is fucking dead! OH MY GOD! Red Rock’s eyes shoot open as the words leave D’avid’s mouth and attack his ear drum. Red Rock almost can’t believe what D’avid is saying but old man Mclumpher hasn’t yelled or complained in weeks! Red Rock: That’ll explain why he hasn’t shouted ‘queer’ at me at the top of his voice in a while… D’avid grabs Red Rock round the wrist and pulls him in the hall way away from Red Rock’s flat, leaving Howard on his own. D’avid: Come this way thank you please! His flat smells like fucking shit! Heh! OOhhh! Red Rock: Oh okay…I haven’t seen a dead body for a good month or two! Red Rock and D’avid head across the hallway to the flat opposite Red Rock’s. Mr Camfield’s front door is wide open and the pungent smell of week old dead body purges through Red Rock’s senses creating a verse of wretches. Red Rock: ARGH! He smells worse than he did when he was alive! Sitting in an old tatty lazy boy chair is the corpse of Mr Mclumpher. He is holding an empty bottle of whisky in one hand and there is a tobacco pipe on the floor near his other hand, which is now stiff from rigamortis. D’avid: Isn’t it great? A REAL LIVE DEAD BODY! ARGHHHH! D’avid jumps up and down like a child who has just got a puppy for a present! Red Rock: Well…I suppose we best call the police or something… Red Rock looks at the corpse of his old disgruntled neighbour and reaches for his mobile phone. Just before he can dial 999 Howard enters the stale dead atmosphere. Howard: Did I hear you say there was a dead body in here? D’avid: YEAH! He’s over there all wrinkled like a baby monkey! Howard: Err…I used to be a…err…body checker…yeah! Red Rock: A coroner? Howard: Err yeah, Coroner! Let me be alone with the body for about…20 minutes! Red Rock and D’avid look at each other and shrug their shoulders and head back to Red Rock’s flat shutting Mr Mclumpher’s door behind him. D’avid: He’s the fucking shittest person I know! What is he?! Red Rock: I have no idea…but he can cook so I don’t care what he is. D’avid: Ohh I wonder who will have his flat now?
