Chapter 7: Maglie's Reconciliation

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.24.06 Sal Maglie. Darcy Markson awoke with a start. It took him several moments to regain his bearings, and remember precisely where he was; a glance around the area in which he was seated indicated that he was in the passenger cabin of a commercial jet; he wasn’t exactly sure where he was, but wherever it was it was 10,000 or so feet above solid ground. Darcy pulled his cell phone from his pocket and pushed a button to check the time: “9:00 AM, Monday, March 20,” said the alluring womanly voice Darcy had programmed into the phone. A completely useless feature, but one that never failed to make the man chuckle to himself every time. Upon hearing the date, Darcy remembered that today was indeed Monday, and the plane he was on was taking him back to California, the day after the debacle that was his main event match at Sunday night’s Fresh!burst. However, unlike the last time he found himself slightly surprised to be on an airplane…the night after a match in which Adam Dick’s antics caused Darcy to have a hole in his memory where the subsequent twelve hours should have been…the events that transpired last evening came back to Darcy in a matter of moments. The only delay came from Darcy’s somewhat sluggish return from the dream in which he’d had. He somehow recalled that like most of his dreams, this one had been sincerely bizarre. But outside of this fact, he could remember only one other detail his dream – the man whose name was now repeating in his head. Sal Maglie. Darcy looked to his left, noticing his expected travel partner was absent…which was also expected. Because his initial attempt at breaking the ice with one Amy Campbell by taking that road trip with her had gone so well, so well that Amy soon accompanied him on nearly every flight to California since, Darcy looked forward to the long hours they spent together on their transcontinental journeys to and from his East Bay home. Rather than spending the flight in slumber, Darcy enjoyed their repeated in-flight antics. Attempting to annoy each other, purposely picking fights, arguments and debates…all a thinly veiled disguise of two people who, after years of wishing each other rotted and buried, now legitimately enjoyed each other’s company, which continued to astonish Darcy and admittedly, Amy as well. But sometimes things went a bit too far. Sometimes events transpired, events that Darcy’s still-smarting jaw could attest to, that occasionally cancelled the time they spent together hurtling high above the country at several hundred miles an hour. This was how Darcy had found himself waking up from the nap he now had the opportunity to take, and a dream that somehow involved the name that was STILL repeating in his head. Sal Maglie. Alright, this is getting retarded, he now thought to himself, hoping their were no PCers on his flight with telepathic abilities. Why was a journeyman pitcher who played over 50 years ago suddenly at the forefront of Darcy’s mind? He pondered this for a few moments, truly perplexed as to what the answer could be. Was it because he was a fan favorite of the New York Giants, a team that would one day move across the bay from Darcy’s home and become his childhood obsession? Probably not, he guessed. While Darcy happened to know that Maglie was a former Giant, he would be hard-pressed to name even five other players who played for the old New York team. Was it because the flight attendant moving down the aisle with the drink cart had unusually large ears, ears that reminded Darcy of Maglie’s ugly mug that he’d seen when he’d read the groundbreaking Ball Four? Again, possible…but unlikely, he surmised. As far as he could recall he hadn’t seen her when he boarded the plane or any other time until now. Darcy was seconds away of abandoning the enterprise in favor of an issue of People magazine from 2002 that someone had left behind when abruptly the solution to the riddle came to him. Life throws you curveballs, man…your life is full of them. And the curveball was Maglie’s specialty. Just this past week alone, Darcy had seen more than a few of the figurative twists and turns that accompanied the trademark pitch of the man who was nicknamed “the Barber.” (This moniker was due not to an unhealthy affinity for Brutus Beefcake - Maglie never met the man…at least not to anyone’s knowledge - but for his affinity for the curveball; a pitch that would veer towards a hitter’s cheeks as close as a barber’s razor before deftly dropping back down for a perfect strike.) Darcy’s carefully crafted plan for revenge had been the key to it all, and the entire week that led to its culmination he thought himself the master of the mound – after all, he was the one throwing all the curveballs. Definitely one for Jack Murphy by forcing him to team with the girl who played an extremely large hand in his winning two very close matches; one a handicap match against Adam Dick that won Murphy the TransAtlantic Championship with Aimz’ tag-team assistance, the other a main event match against Darcy Crisis featuring Aimz’ distraction combined with an illegal rope-assisted rollup for the cheap victory. But most of the curves were thrown in Amy’s direction. The first curveball came in forcing her to team yet again with “The Bull,” a man she truly despised even more than she hated his nickname. Another came in Darcy’s choice of partner – Ellis Nash, who, if it’s in fact possible, hated even more than Murphy. But the biggest curve of all was the curve that didn’t curve away at all, but kept coming for her like a barber’s razor blade, threatening to cut the deepest. She had to fight me. In making a match that would ensure the two would physically compete against each other, Darcy made it clear that their current relationship and unique history cut her no slack – she was not above confronting in the ring. She’d gone ballistic on learning that she’d have to go through with it, but Darcy never budged from his rationale: They were both employed as professional wrestlers, both in the same singles ranks, both gunning for that same ultimate prize. If she was looking to take him down a peg (and costing him a victory over Murphy certainly qualified as such), he’d make her have to earn it. But on that day, the day that Darcy’s careful concoction at last concluded, it was Amy Campbell who threw the most dastardly curve of all. Her penchant for balls-out, brutal offensive assaults within the squared circle notwithstanding, Darcy Markson was expecting the fight of his life from Amy Campbell simply for everything he had put her through. She spent much of her time as a ticking time bomb as it was – and with Darcy now, to say the least, thesting her limits – he’d expected that it would take everything he had and possibly something extra to keep the bomb from exploding right in front of his face. It nearly had; she’d given him quite a battle. And for his part, Darcy Crisis matched her intensity with his own. Jack Murphy may have been the only participant in the matchup billed as “The Bull,” but everyone who laid eyes upon the epic tag-team affair surely must have been amazed to see what two great bulls of another era of wrestling were capable of when they locked horns, each attempting to assert their dominance. But the one thing, the only thing Darcy should have counted on to transpire last night, he’d neglected to bear in mind: things would go awry from the plan he’d fashioned. And gone awry they certainly had. The match was reaching a climax. Darcy had just hit the Mind Crisis on Jack Murphy – a devastating maneuver that had earned him more than a few victories in the past – but could not put him away on the subsequent pinning combination. And then it happened: One botched spinning heel kick later, Murphy seized the opportunity to target Darcy’s back. The very back that Darcy literally broke…the price he paid in winning Pier Six Wrestling’s ultimate prize. The very back that any jobber worth half the weight of his jock could see the imaginary bulls-eye painted on it. Murphy’s combination of a headlock and leg-scissors across Darcy’s defenseless back left him with nowhere to go. His glorious plan had failed…not only would he not get his revenge, Darcy wouldn’t even escape this match without serious injury. Without thinking his arm shot up, knowing there was only one thing left to do… And it was Amy, Amy…AMY, of all people, who couldn’t take it anymore. With one well-placed kick, she freed Darcy from Murphy’s clutches. It was hard to say who was most astonished by this; her boyfriend, her tag partner, the commentators (even George Cassidy was stunned in silence). But while all parties involved were attempting to sort through what had just transpired, Amy had already left the ring, having had enough of this little game. Her exit, however, caused yet another distraction…a distraction that this time Darcy could this time exploit. One devastating superkick and three slaps of the mat later, the bell had rung. Darcy Crisis, just as he promised, had his revenge. But what he did not promise…what he could nary have predicted…was that Amy would hand it to him on a silver platter. *********************************************************** Darcy did not stop at home immediately after his flight touched down in the all-to-familiar airport outside of East Bay. Johnny Hawk was there again to greet him, and the two went out for an afternoon drink, but the expedition proved uneventful. Having had enough of the affair Darcy was unwilling to discuss the recent turn of events with Johnny, instead electing to shoot the breeze over various topics, all fairly trivial. Darcy soon returned to his place of residence with a clear agenda in mind: unpack, attempt once more to sort through the quagmire of thoughts that the past week had given him to deal with, and then devote himself to several hours of The History Channel. I don’t believe it…I thought my studying days were over, he thought to himself. Why did I think a battle of wits with a Turkish brute was a good idea? His bag slung over his shoulder, Darcy fumbled for the keys that would grant him access to his abode, taking several extra moments to awkwardly retrieve them from his left pocket with his right hand…a method that served practically no purpose. However, just before he could get the small metallic device into the doorframe, he heard a distinctive *CLICK* and watched the door handle slowly pivot. Before he could react, he found a pair of multi-colored eyes staring up at him beneath the most gorgeous ruby-colored hair he’d ever seen. She’d never looked more beautiful…and Darcy Markson had never been more afraid for his own well-being. Several moments passed. No words were spoken. The two simply gazed into each other’s eyes, perhaps waiting to see who would make the first reaction. Then suddenly, without warning, she lunged at him – Darcy made no move, merely closing his eyes, awaiting the assailing he knew he could not avoid. Instead, he found her arms wrapped around him in a loving embrace. For quite awhile, they remained that way. He’d let his bag fall off of his shoulder to return Amy’s embrace. He’d of course seen her just the night before, and practically every night before that for weeks on end. But standing there, locked in each other’s arms, the two felt like they’d been separated for ages. As Amy finally released her grip on him, Darcy was still at a loss for words. He may not have known what the hell to say to her…but he knew he had to try. “Amy…I…” he began. “Fuck you,” she interjected, very softly. “I…what?” “FUCK YOU!” came her reply, this time nowhere near as soft. “Amy…” “How could you do that to me, Darcy? How could you put me through that? How could you make me watch you get your life…your CAREER, Darcy! Your career get squeezed away from you, and put it all on my conscience? Seriously, could you be any more selfish?” Darcy offered no reply. He’d been told many times he had the “gift of gab”…but at the moment, the gift was certainly failing him. '”Say something, for chrissakes!'” “Things...: he stammered, finally. “Things kinda got out of hand. I admit it.” And it was entirely your fault, jackass... he told himself silently. 'And I honestly don't know what to say or do at this point, except to say...thank you.' Amy sighed, “Try again. You tried making me responsible for what happened to you in that match, even if it was by putting me on the other side. You didn't think the fucking CHAMPION wasn't gonna try to cover his own ass? He was determined to win, at any cost. It seems like you forget I'm the only person in AWC who gives a damn about whether or not you're wheelchair bound. I almost wonder if you didn't expect me to save your ass in a pinch - it was a guaranteed win if your partner wanted the victory and half of the opposing team wanted to see you come out of it okay.” “But...” she continued, “I don't honestly think you're that smart when it comes to me. You just wanted to see me squirm there as some kind of sick fucking revenge. You've got problems, especially when it comes to responsibility. You just tried to pull off putting everything on MY chest, and minutes later your lips are smacking saying you really care and thanking me for taking the brunt of your bullshit. I don't get you. Not at all.” Darcy's shoulders slumped, knowing she was completely in the right. He could talk until his voice ran hoarse about how they were both wrestlers, who wrestled, and when their paths would cross their relationship changed nothing. But if their relationship knew boundaries...what was the point? “I got carried away. When I promised that the old Darcy Crisis was gonna start kicking ass like it was 2001, nobody was more excited than me. And then that night, I was seconds away from beating the champ. It was intoxicating, Amy...that's what this business has done to me for so many years. I was THIS close to earning my spot back at the top, THIS close before you took it away from me. I should have known it wasn't quite as malicious as I was making it out to be, but I was flying on adrenaline. I made a bad decision and I was forced to go through with it. And you're right, it hardly even occured to me that I put you in harm's way to satisfy my thirst for glory. I can only thank my lucky stars that you're not quite as sadistic as I am.” Darcy looked on as Amy laid back against the couch and scoffed at him again. “So,” she reacted accordingly. “Enough of the fake thanks. I keep taking the punches here, and even though I've been so close to pullin' out to win, I kept taking your shit. No more. You're playing with my life, trying to recreate your glory days, and I'm not gonna take it for another damned second. The only decisions you make are for your own personal gain, and god forbid you ever admit to caring about me, but I'm gonna put this on YOUR shoulders. You tell me, what the hell is your intent with me? I've put myself on a platter and you've only taken what suits you... so, is that the way it's gonna be, or do you wanna go about things like a grown-up for once?” With that, Amy leaned back against the couch and reclosed her eyes, seemingly with nothing left to add. Darcy moved to join her on the couch. He ran his hands slowly through his long dark locks, taking a few moments to search for precisely the right words to say. “You know, Amy...up until now you haven't exactly made your intentions forthcoming either. But I understand now...you tipped your hand last night. You made your priorities clear as crystal. I owe you the same.” His body began to shake, and while Amy's eyes remained shut she could sense it showing in his voice. He'd been sitting on these emotions for some time...and it was finally time to let them escape. “You have...” he started, but was stopped by another involuntary pause. Jesus Christ, this is heavy... “You've no idea how hard this is for me. But it's time you know. Your selfless act opened my eyes. I put you through hell and you somehow found it in your heart to show me compassion. And in that, I know now who I truly am.” He now turned to gaze directly into her still enclosed eyelids. “My name...my name is Darcy Markson. And I love you, Amy Campbell, madly and stupidly. I love you first....and Darcy Crisis second. That's the way it's going to be....that's the way it HAS to be. And that's the way it's going to stay, until you give me a reason to feel otherwise.” Amy's eyes shot open, then squeezed shut for a moment to comprehend exactly what the hell Darcy had just said. She was trying desperately not to show that her heart was dancing harder than Beyonce, and managed to keep a relative appearance of calm that he most likely saw straight through... but it was worth a try. ''...Took ya long enough. Five damn years, sheesh!'' The only words she had, completely tactless. At least it hadn't changed her. Her response earned her a half-smile from Darcy. He knew her words gave no indication of what she surely must have been feeling inside. Still, he thought it would be nice to hear those feeling spoken aloud. “You know, I totally let my guard down over here...don't you think it might be nice to offer up the same courtesy?” “I said it already, four damn years ago!'' she argued with a slightly sarcastic smirk, but soon relented, ''Fine. Darcy, I...'” Amy chuckled, '”I love vodka. And puppies. You're pretty alright too, I guess.” Darcy sighed almost instantly. '”Oh, come on. I love you. I love you waaaaaaaay too much more than I should. It's unhealthy, honestly, and the only reason I've put up with you for this long. If you're really good, and give me money, I might someday be inclined to have your babies,” she sighed, “And I've loved you since I was eighteen. How's that for having no life?” With that, Darcy broke into a full out grin. And with that, Amy responded with a sheepish smile of her own. This moment had been a long time coming. As they gazed longingly into each other’s eyes, only one thing could happen now after confessing their feelings for each other. I sure wish I had the time, Darcy thought, as he reached for the remote to flip on the television. Amy looked at him like he’s suddenly grown another head. “What are you doing?” “Believe it or not,” Darcy said, as he put an arm around his now admitted love, “I actually have STUDYING to do. Captain Sully really knows his shit. I’ve got from now until the bell rings to cram my head with as much useless knowledge as I possibly can.” Amy rolled her eyes. “Good God…don’t tell me you two are going to have a TALKING match. What is this, the internet?” That last remark actually made Darcy laugh out loud for several moments. Amy leaned into him, taking a small sense of satisfaction in rousing his sense of humor. “All the same…it’s too late to back out now,” Darcy finally said. “And I’m NOT going down in my first pay per view match in five years just because I couldn’t figure out when the second Crimean War took place.” “Nuts to that,” Amy said, rising from the sofa. “It’s time for your first of many study breaks.” Taking Darcy’s hand in hers, she led him back to the bedroom. And this is where the story ends for you…silly perv.
