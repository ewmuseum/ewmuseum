Date: August 15, 2006 this is the open 24 hours, pepsi soaked, morphine-induced life; ripped from the mouths of babies; force fed and choked down after the alphabet. modern tragedy is grief spliced with drugs, alice's mushrooms, black bands around the arms pushed down and tightened around the wrist, cut out of leather studded with pyramids. funerals are held in the parlor of the hilton, maybe three blocks down of saks fifth ave. maybe not. either way, she can still get away with wearing a jacket and wrinkled jeans. 'none of this really matters,' she could say, existential angst is in, goth out -- 'people die all the time,' she could say, 'people die all the time, this doesn't matter, this doesn't matter.' it does/n't. take your choice. it's all the same. it's all the same, except for when the stiff is her child, dead. it's all the same, except she's killed her daughter with too much alcohol and a night out on the ceiling. it's all the same, except when it's her fucking daughter or son. it's not the same then, but to everyone except her. she takes a taxi to jfk, changes in the bathroom and throws the jacket to a homeless man. rips her own copy of romeo and juliet, which would be more accurately titled as: romeo fucking juliet and cutting her heart out with two precise snips of his teeth and then being too stupid to realize what death is by shakespeare bitches her way through the safety issue of bringing toothpaste on an airplane gets on the plane and does not cry. goes to louisiana and does not cry, but goes to the hotel, closes the door and throws the key down the toilet. goes to the hotel, and does not cry, but chugs a bottle of gatorade and locks the door from the inside, and forgets what it's like to feel. goes to the hotel and-- and stays there until the feeling's gone away. she's already forgotten her own voice. it's easy to ignore faded images, easier to ignore yourself, easiest to ignore the living, the dead or maybe both. and cries, stupid in a melodramatic way 'this doesn't matter.' crash, snap, silence, there where? so, 'don't think too much,' you'll get there - 'we all die' someday, if you try hard enough 'to forget and the past will mean nothing, the grave doesn't exist.' and cries. xx her teeth are not sharp like romeo's, but soft and dry under his tongue, lipstick rubbed out by too many pronouncements of love, hair curled for lovers to slide their hands in, feet angled for dancers old enough to remember what beauty lookes like. she is beautiful with the airs of russian heiresses and silk against her breasts and stomach, fair, too fair for fairytales and the eastern sky, delicate enough to wash away with the tides, calcium bones broken with the twist of her eyes; cheekbones sharp enough to cut lips made for tattooing her memories into someone else's skin, someone else's skin so that they will remember, but she will forget, forget her son/daughter forget him forget the darknesses she spilled into the sky with the carelessness of loose thumbs and wine glasses. she, she- she was-- don't lose your footing. xx there are nine planets, seven sins, seven continents, 2 elevators in the building/people in the penthouse, fifteen copies of vogue on her shelf, four freckles on her wrist, five fingers on her hand, eight days on the calendar, 48 floors in the building one coffin with a raised lid in the living room. the lid will not close; her eyes will not close and he/she will not open his own. the wind is so soft, the sun so harsh, the screams so loud (don't lose-- your mind/self/heart/brain/shoes/wallet/feet/words/thoughts-- the world. xx with a hollow tongue and words that hang by blood off the jagged lines of her mouth, she has no room for soft kindnesses this life has fed her dirt and despair her words, too, are empty and the round concave of her cheeks are empty pools of happiness that have long evaporated in dry air she says: go away and leave me here; stop haunting my dreams i am not able to breathe without you, and stop trying to be everything, my inspiration, stop trying to be what i need because then i'll need you and isn't it enough that i want you already, you in the broken tip of my brushes, my paint already longing for your fingers and my colors starting to desire the company of your colors? isn't it enough that i am going to live this life without you and that nobody will remember you for anything but the shadow that darkens my shadow, the white edge that makes me look brighter, the oil on my fingers, the paint in my eyes and what will be your life, you unspeakable endless perfection, you named and beloved angel nothing oh, God, you know this already go now, now, and leave me decaying in the shell of all those dreams that could not be. i'm sorry. i'm sorry because the water is so cold tonight, i'm sorry because you're beautiful, i'm sorry i left you behind. no, you inscrutable harmony, you discordant memory, you, that thing i can't name or remember or let be go now, before you drive me insane, before you become the things i can't let you be. don't take away the last shred of hope i hold on to, this desire to come out of this with as much grace as i can contain, and you are stripping me of all this love, this life, this go, daughter, go, son. don't make me say anymore. xx she thinks that she's good at everything except being afraid.
