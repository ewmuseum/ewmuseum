Date: 25th September 2006 I spy with my little eye something that begins with ‘I’m going to die’ So Kids pick up a knife and throw it in the air. The beats are not complex, I'm just trying to make you kill yourself, And not be perplexed. It doesn't mean any less. Children, in the crowd. If you don't care, then why are you slitting your throat? Children, in the crowd. If you don't care, then why are you slitting your throat? Why are you slitting your throat? And I spy with my little eye something that begins with 'I have just', I have just killed my mom. So put on your favourite song, Forgot the mess and sing aloud, Why don't you sing along? It doesn't mean any less. It goes, Children, in the crowd. If you don't care, then why are you slitting your throat? Children, in the crowd. If you don't care, then why are you slitting your throat? And I can hear you singing, La la la la la la la la la laaa, You're slitting your dads throat, La la la la la la la la la laaa, It doesn't matter that he’s dead, Even though it's not what you’re supposed to do, What you’re supposed to do, What you’re supposed to do, What you’re supposed to do, It doesn't matter that this songs about death, Even though it's not what you’re supposed to do, What you’re supposed to do And I can hear you going? La la la la la la la la la laaa, La la la la la la la la la laaa. ‘How To Get To Sesame Street’ starts to play as a camera pans around the crowd based within a studio. It shows a bunch of children with knifes stabbing away into their parents. Pools of blood are scattered around, as the camera finally comes to a halt and faces the lovable Elmo. He is positioned behind a table full of different shapes and sizes of knifes and guns. The screaming of parents gradually dies down as Elmo begins to address everyone. Elmo: Put the knifes down kids, you’ll poke someone’s eye out. Elmo’s comment is followed by sadistic laughter from the children as he continues. Elmo: We still have a lot more people to torture, so we thought we’d bring in two special guess. Atlantic Wrestling Club very own resident jobbers, FREDROCK~! and Emo Kid. An instrumental version of Europe’s ‘The Final Countdown’ echo’s out as FREDROCK~! and Emo Kid make their way into the studio to a round of applause. They shake the hand of Elmo before joining him behind the table. Elmo: Emo Kid, I can see that your already sweating at the sight of all those knifes. Emo Kid nods his head up and down several times. Elmo: If you could please reframe from slitting yourself for a few minutes and tell everyone your story. Emo Kid: I’ve been a cutting myself for several years now. Emo Kid’s whole body is twitching compulsively as his Panic! At The Disco T-Shirt is drenched in sweat. Elmo: It’s ok, take your time. Emo Kid: I’m here to answer any of your questions on the subject. I’ll also show you some valuable techniques that you can use on yourself later. There’s a chorus of cheers as FREDROCK~! pats Emo Kid on the back. Elmo reaches under the table and pulls up a small piece of paper. Elmo: I know you’ve been waiting for this Emo Kid, I have a letter from five you old Thomas Whitehead. His question is, what is the best way to slit your wrists? Emo Kids eyes light up. Elmo: Now, would you like to demonstrate this for the kids? He scans the table vigorously and reaches down and picks out a knife. Emo Kid: Firstly, its important to find a nice metal implement, preferably sharp, very sharp. Using blunt implements may cause insufficient damage, so remember it must be poke your eye out sharp. If you’re unsure about the sharpness, you can always test it on someone nearby. Emo Kid turns and faces FREDROCK~! before ramming the knife into one of his eyes. He twists it round and heaves it out, it comes apparent that FREDROCKS~! eye has stuck onto the end of the knife, as the crowd laugh. Emo Kid: See, it must be extremely sharp, make a note of that. Remember though, to give the eye back. We do not condole stealing, that’s wrong. They all nod their heads in agreement as Emo Kid passes FREDROCK~! his eye back, who pops it back into its socket. Emo Kid: Now, its important to make a nice swift incision across the wrist. The quicker you do this, the less pain you will feel. Emo Kid slits his wrist as the kids jump up in excitement. Blood squirts out onto the camera lens as he drops to the ground and writhes in pain, screaming like a little schoolgirl. Elmo: A round of applause everyone. The children in the crowd go nuts. They throw their knifes in the air, with several coming back down, piercing them through the skull. Elmo: Now earlier today, we bought FREDROCK~! a cape and he attempted to fly off a building. So take a look at this video, while we bandage Emo Kid up. Elmo and FREDROCK~! are standing on top of a fairly large building singing the Sesame Street theme tune. Elmo: Sunny day, sweeping the clouds away, on my way to where the air is sweet… FREDROCK~!: Can you tell me how to get, how to get to Sesame Street? FREDROCK~! takes a few steps to the edge of the building and looks down. Elmo: Come and play, everything's A-OK, friendly neighbours there, that's where we meet… FREDROCK~!: Can you tell me how to get, how to get to Sesame Street? FREDROCK~! fastens his cape and takes one last look down below. Elmo: It's a magic carpet ride, every door will open wide, to happy people like you, happy people like, what a beautiful… So jump FREDROCK~!, jump! FREDROCK~! sings the final words to the theme tune on his way down. ‘Sunny day, sweeping the clouds away, on my way to where the air is sweet’. He splashes apart after hitting the concrete floor. Elmo: Welcome back kids. As you can see Emo Kid is still alive The crowd give off a sigh of disappointment. Elmo: Sadly, he is a little drowsy because he has just been injected with a lethal dose of morphine. The children erupt into cheers. Elmo: Now, for are next experiment, we need a member of the crowd to volunteer for certain death. Several hands go up in the crowd. After a few seconds, a young boy is selected as he makes his way onto the stage. Elmo: How old are you? Child: Seven! Elmo: Well, would you like to strip down to your underwear and stand against that wall over there. The boy does as he is told. As he leans up against the wall, a hat is placed on top of his head. Elmo: The two guests on today’s show are now going to try and fire bullets towards the kid, and flip the hat off his head. You’ve all seen this done in movies before, so play along at home if you like. FREDROCK~! is shaking as he holds a pistol between his hands. Elmo: Just point and fire. FREDROCK~!: Here it goes. Bang! Elmo: You just killed him you stupid fucker. FREDROCK~!: I didn’t mean to. Elmo: I said aim for the hat. FREDROCK~!: I swear, I did. Elmo: You’ve gone and ruined the rest of the show, you stupid fucking cunt. Someone drag this kid away and put him in the dumpster, with the rest off them. The children begin to boo and throw stuff towards FREDROCK~!. Elmo: Considering this asshole has screwed up the show, I’ll have to tell you what we had planned for are volunteer here. We were going to paint a nice portrait for him to take home, using his own organs. Instead, this spaz pissed it all up. Emo Kid grabs a gun and points it at Elmo as he begins to make a big scene. Emo Kid: Take that back! FREDROCK~! only did what you told him to do. Elmo: Shut up! Emo Kid: Take it back! I mean it, I’ll kill you. Elmo: You don’t have the balls. Emo Kid: I might not have any balls but I have a heart. Emo Kid flips the safety trigger back. Emo Kid: DIE ELMO, DIE! Emo Kid fires the gun as someone drops to the floor. FREDROCK~!: Argh! You shot me in the neck. Blood is gusting out of FREDROCKS~! neck. Elmo: This is a lesson for all you kids out there, don’t take drugs. FREDROCK~! grabs a knife from the table and throws it at Emo Kid. It sticks into his left leg, resulting with Emo Kid stumbling onto his knees. Emo Kid: Why did you do that? FREDROCK~!: You shot me. Emo Kid: I didn’t mean to. The guns fires off again as the bullet hits FREDROCK~! in the leg this time. Emo Kid: But I meant that, bitch! FREDROCK~!: I see the light. Mom, is that you? FREDROCK~! drops down face first into the floor as Emo Kid worryingly crawls over. He lifts him up in his arms, as a tear drops down onto his face. FREDROCK~! unexpectedly comes back round and punches Emo Kid in the face. Emo Kid falls back, sprawled out, as FREDROCK~! mounts him and starts pounding away on his face. Elmo is slightly cheesed off at the two. He has reached under the table and brought up a stick of dynamite, he lights it up. Elmo: Will you two cut it out or I’ll blow this fucking place up, killing us all. The crowd gasp, as Emo Kid and FREDROCK~! gaze up in horror. Elmo: Only kidding, this isn’t real. Wait… Elmo throws the dynamite into the crowd, it explodes as the limbs of the crowd can be seen flying in the air. Elmo: Opps, Sesame Street will be back after this short break. ‘How To Get To Sesame Street’ starts to play again as Emo Kid and FREDROCK~! get to their feet and brush themselves off. Elmo: On your return you’ll be treated to a special surprise, Bert and Ernie will be simulating sexual acts on each other. So pull down your pants and ask your sister or brother to join in. The three of them wave to the camera as it fades to a black. (OOC: I apologise for this, I don’t know what I was thinking when I wrote this but I never do so I take that back. Anyway, the lyrics at the top are based around a song ‘I Spy’ by Get Cape. Wear Cape. Fly, changed ever so slightly.)
