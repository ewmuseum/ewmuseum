The gateless gate...

8 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/06 A monk said to Joshu, 'I have just entered this monastery. Please teach me.' 'Have you eaten your rice porridge today? asked Joshu. 'Yes, I have,' replied the monk. 'Then you had better wash your bowl,' said Joshu. With this the monk gained enlightenment. She stood at the intersection by the curb, exactly one foot away from the pedestrian crossing. A huge Greyhound bus blew by her with a voluminous rush followed by a swirl of hot air and fumes. She looked both ways. The light turned green. She walked. Across the sunlit asphalt, away from a building marked 'Arriving Flights', she gave them not a thought. In the sky above her another large passenger plane descended from the sky. She turned under the jutting slope of the parking building and headed for her car. When one has attained full realization they can become the greatest at whatever they do. Every day is like a test, execpt everyday I pass. Because there is no failing. There is just life. And here I am, back again. I check my level of consciousness. Not the slightest change of breath. The heartbeat is steady. Away for a month, and I am back, and not the least bit excited? That is good. Her steps on the concrete make a tapping sound, lost on everything around her. She shiney brown shoes click their heels when she walks, her long flowered dress flittering at her ankles. She hums a single low note, a sutra. The vibration penetrates the body and the mind. Her self is in deep silence. Walking meditation. Brief flirts with consciousness are met with focus, and handled with conviction. Her walk is solemn. She opens her car door and doesn't care to notice the musty smell. It is noted. The only practical thing to do. The keys are above, by the vanity mirror. There they are... what's this? A program. The staff left it in the car. FRESH! 9th May 2006 Hakon Hall (capacity: 10000), Lillehammer, Norway Names, old and new. Mikey O'Reilly, Aimz, Garbage Bag Johnny, Chainz... memories flood my mind. But that's all they are, memories. Ghosts of the past. Butterfly places the program in the seat beside her, next to the bags the airport staff had delivered to her car. She zips open her gym bag. She tosses the program inside. The corner of her eye catches a scuff mark on one of her wrestling boots. She pulls it out. Time for a new pair. She looks to put the boot back in the bag. In a small corner of sunlight beneath this massive parking lot, a patch of golden rays catch the glint of yellow steel. Her gold belt. She sighs. What is past is past. Time to enter the gateless gate. Butterfly looks up. The vanity mirror stares back at her and her wide, brown eyes. Her eyes slowly blink. She flips up the shde.
