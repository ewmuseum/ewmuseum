Pallas Athena 4: Shedding Cocoons

6 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/6/05 Pallas Athena 4: 11:07 PM Tuesday, 11/29/05 Manchester, England The AWC bus made its' way to the hotel after a successful show in Manchester, but there was an underlying unease to the usual ribbing and bustle of activity. Many of the wrestlers were known party animals and would tear up a new town until morning after most cards. But this was a different kind of town, in a different kind of place. The AWC was back in England for a second week and on a tour of Europe. Road weary was the best way to describe how everyone was feeling. It didn't help that this week The British Bomber made a surprise return from the land of the dead, bringing down heaps of criticism from fans and the press about bad timing and bad taste. The Furious Fists of God had made a lasting mark on this night in England as well, and their antics didn't shine very bright on the reputation of the promotion. Butterfly lamented in the elevator up to her room. She was used to tradition and honor. Men like Jack Murphy and Chainz were good for business but bad for moral. She understood where she stood in all of this, her role in the company. The fact she didn't like it was another story. Tozen 'Cosmic Lion' Fujita had been given every assurance by the AWC of safety and fair treatment. Butterfly would be prepared to watch his back. She did not like having her boyfriend defend her honor, but this is how it had played out. Jack Murphy had gone too far. Chainz had pushed the issue over the edge. Tozen would be challenging Murphy at the next 'FRESH!' in a singles match. A challenge like this was routine for her people. The consequences she could only hope would be better then how she had fared so far. Members of the AWC staff and roster were notorious for staying up long into the night. And even as depressing and dull as Mother Russia was about to make the AWC, it didn't stop some from staying awake, channeling their tension into drinking, dancing, and restaurant hopping. On this night Butterfly and Tozen had by chance ended up sharing the evening with Butch Radder, Jin Osaka, and some of the production staff. Gossip about the night's show was punctuated by loud tales of bravado, furtive flirting, and allot more drinking. 12:38 am Wednesday, 11/29/05 Manchester, England In a small place inside her mind, a focused yet dark place, Megumi waited impatiently for the evening's festivities to quiet down. A few hours after stopping off in the hotel bar for a nightcap the last of the crew headed to their rooms. Butterfly smiled as she closed the door to her room, waving good night to Jin Osaka as she lumbered down the hall to her own bed. When Butterfly turned around to turn on the room light, she saw Tozen had already crashed into bed and was out like a light. Good, she thought. She could not delay any longer. Gathering a small black bag she slipped back out of the room and headed for the exit. Years of practice while living in an all women's dojo had enabled Butterfly to walk with a grace silent and effortless. She would slip by her guardians to go out dancing when she was younger many times. She remembered the few times she had been caught and punished. Butterfly didn't worry as she slipped out of the hotel lobby, right past a drunk and snoring Butch Radder. She was sure the security man was fast asleep and quite intoxicated. She quickly made her way to a waiting cab and they shot off into the night. The driver followed her route, which was dictated turn for turn. When they had reached some predetermined point after a 30 minute drive, Butterfly asked the driver to stop. She got out and slammed the door shut and he sped off down the dark road. Megumi wore tight black nylon pants, and a black stretch turtleneck of the thinnest and finest fabric covered her from the neck down. As she started to walk up a slight hill on the deserted street, she used a rubber band to tie her shortly cropped brown hair in a ponytail. A small black alley cat ran across her path, startled by the curiously silent Megumi, whose footsteps in her special black soft leather boots were as quiet as a mouse. As she reached the corner, out of the corner of her left eye she saw the lights of the mansion. Surrounded by contemporary English early-century architecture stood a blue and white castle circled with palm trees and rose bushes. Lit on every side by spotlights, the house stood out like an eyesore. But this wasn't Madonna's castle or Ringo Star's estate. This abomination of greed gone awry was the dwelling of one infamous English wrestler, Ron Von Dumpling. Megumi felt her skin tingle, and she scratched her tattooed triceps. Even under clothing sometimes Megumi would feel a tingle or a sensation on the inked skin of her left arm. It was like a warning sign, telling of coming danger, or at least that's what it felt like. Megumi cast aside her silly notions and headed toward the shadows and her mission. Walking slowly toward the building as innocently and innocuous as possible, she tried to focus on her job at hand. But pervasive thoughts at at her will like tiny gnawing piranhas on a dead horse carcass. She thought about Jack Murphy and her boyfriend Tozen Fujita and their match coming up next week. As much as she was excited to get to see Tozen wrestle under the Cosmic Lion mask, she was still a little miffed at him for butting into her business. Were all men as stubborn and chivalrous to the point of nausea like Fujita. 'The Bull' was her problem and hers alone. She wouldn't embarrass Tozen by telling him he had dishonored her in some ways, her women trainers would take this as a complete affront to any joshi wrestler. However, she couldn't believe how many men problems she was having. And all for a girl who had never had a boyfriend until a few months ago. With razor-like sharpness she suddenly saw her opening from across the street, and like a cat bounded for a pair of bushes beneath a transplanted palm tree. No one had seen her. There was no sign of guards posted near the large marble door or on the wall, which seemed odd to her. Still she would have to be vigilant and careful. She started working her way around the mansion wall. Quiet as she could, Megumi stalked around a corner toward the back entrance to Von Dumpling's self-made castle. Another man with too much testosterone, she thought. Was she cursed? First Jack Murphy and his 'petition' to clean up the AWC by getting rid of all things untraditional. In his eyes, women didn't belong. Then she had a match at Triangles, the only match she had won since joining the AWC. And Andy M., the returning prodigal son, helped her to win. Two weeks ago in her match against Chainz she was dishonored when he decided to beat the living daylights out of her after the match. And then her boyfriend suddenly decides to get involved with her professional life. On top of it all, she was hired to deliver a message to a Sir Ron Von Dumpling, a professional wrestler and obese, greedy, and devious money-monger who had blemished the name of a highly regarded wrestling agency back in America. She thought about that curse again, the one which would have her dealing with obnoxious, pushy, arrogant, and overbearing men the rest of her life. Bringing herself back to the plan once again, she chided herself for being distracted. Several days of planning had gone into this assignment. By now Megumi knew much about the house she was about to break into. Too much for her liking for she felt almost touched by the evil that permeated within its' walls. Megumi stopped dead in her tracks. She hugged the steep wall when she heard voices talking above her. There were guards around, as she was told. They were probably hired security and not very professional. When the voices had trailed off she decided to keep moving. She had paid for some very special information. Actually it was more like she had bribed a guard to not do his job this night. Big Daddy Taylor had seen to that and had upped his payment for this service. She stood right under the northwest corner, where the man was purported to be stationed. Pulling a small clawed hook from her pocket trailing a very fine strand of rope, she aimed for the ledge. With a quick snap of the wrist she tossed the grappling hook over the ledge, some twenty feet up. It's rubber coated claws dulled the sound of metal hitting rock. With a tug of the rope she could tell it was secure. Five seconds later she was standing on the top of the outer curtain. This wall stood alone except for gates at both the front and back of the estate. Her prey was in the main house. She spied a guest house to the north east and hoped Von Dumpling was not entertaining guests this night. There were guards, of course, and they were walking along small grassy paths between more palm trees, the pool, and the rest of the estate grounds. On the outer curtain she could see the forms of two men making their way slowly around the top of the wall. She knew she had to keep moving. Slithering the rope down the other side of the wall she landed on the grassy grounds without a sound. A quick jerk of the rope and the grappling claw landed in her hands. She tied the rope around her waist and looked out for any approaching problems. The door to the guest house opened to her left, and out came a man with a girl on each arm and drinks in hand. They were heading toward the pool and the already bubbling Jacuzzi. The women were wearing bikinis underneath long flowing robes. The man, in his late forties by the look of his graying temples, also wore a robe, and he shuffled the girls quickly toward the hot frothy water of the bath. The night was cold, too cold for their ensemble, thought Megumi. And this was not Ron Von Dumpling, who probably outweighed the man by two hundred pounds, or so she had been told. Thankfully Von Dumpling was probably still inside somewhere gorging himself or counting his money. The question was if he were in his own house or having fun with the rest of the part in the guest house. If there were people around this mission was all for naught and she would have to wait him out all evening or leave now. She didn't relish the thought of ducking guards for the next six hours. She decided to try the house. She quickly framed a new plan in her head. Quickly shedding her top, she wriggled out of her pants into her black bra and panties. They could easily pass for a bikini. It was cold but she could block out the temperature long enough to get to the house. As the party of three was entering the jacuzzi they placed their drinks on a small glass patio table. Skimming the shadows Megumi made it over to the table where she grabbed one of the girls martinis and then made a bee line for the main estate. 'Hey! Hey you!' Megumi froze with a lump in her throat. She slowly turned to face the man and his two wenches, putting on her best dopey smile. 'Join us, eh? C'mon luv, the water is mighty hot and you look like you're freezing your headlights off. Ha ha ha!' The women giggled and Megumi realized that her bra was suddenly tighter then usual. The cold weather was hardening her nipples. She blushed with honesty but kept her disguised expression. dumb witted and perky was what this group expected, and she would use it to her advantage. 'Thanks, tee hee! But I was sent to be with Mr. Von Dumpling. Do you know if he's in the guest house?' 'Forget that fat bucket of garbage. He left the party about an hour ago to run his fat little fingers through all his money. Forget him, come in. Come in/. The water is nice, baby.' 'Maybe later. Tee hee!' Megumi smiled and waved, then turned toward the estate. The man had given her all the information she needed. *********************************************************** Ron Von Dumpling considered himself a reasonable man, but there were limits to his patience. 'You came here expecting to just walk away with my money?' he growled, brandishing a turkey leg in one hand and a stack of euros in the other. The 'wench' raised an eyebrow, a gesture that was barely perceptible under the deep cover of her brown hair that had fallen before her face. Wearing her bra and panties and still wearing her black leather boots, this thin girl looked rather out of sorts ordering the loose-jowled Englishman. She stared at Von Dumpling through the silken strands, eyes focused and stern. 'Hardly. You made off with the agency's money. You besmirched their name. You have warrants out for your arrest on a number of charges in America. I agreed to make sure you pay what you owe,' Megumi whispered. She reached into her black panty bottoms and pulled out a small card. On it was a number, a figure. She tossed it at Von Dumpling and it landed at his feet. Von Dumpling reached down, licking his lips in anticipation of what was to come. Several months earlier he had been under the employ of CYDONIA Incorporated, a pro wrestling booking office in the United States. For a small percentage they would make arrangements for wrestlers to work different promotions throughout the northeast, as well as overseas. Von Dumpling took the bookings. He also took the money, took advantage of a young women, destroyed hotel rooms, and then left the company high and dry with complaints and law suits. He crossed the briny sea with a suitcase full of money and the satisfaction in knowing he had screwed some Americans. 'That number there must be a joke, eh?' Von Dumpling said. He picked up the card and took a bite out of his turkey leg, eyeing Megumi carefully. He let out a yawn of boredom, Megumi openly showing displeasure at the sight of his mouth full of half-chewed bird meat. 'Better,' Megumi answered. 'It is the one and only way you can settle this matter with the agency without incurring any, shall we say, penalties.' The obese Englishman threw the card on his leopard skin comforter and sat on his bed, chewing his food. Megumi couldn't believe anyone would hire this man as a wrestler on his looks, but had heard that he was a formidable foe who knew how to use his power and girth most effectively. But right now, his girth was squatted on a straining mattress looking at her like she was the other turkey leg. 'Thank you very much for the message, wench. Now get outta here,' he ordered. 'Not until I get what I came for.' 'Like all women, you are a fool. Like all the wrestlers working for CYDONIA, you are an idiot,' Von Dumpling said scornfully. 'You should have gone while you had the chance. I'll deal with this situation with your bosses on my own time. Now get before I sit on you and crush the wind from your lungs, skinny pigeon.' 'I am not like 'all the wrestlers'. And I am not like 'all women',' said Megumi with a snarl. She bent her knees and prepared to fight. A hoot of laughter broke from the fat man, and a hiss from the bed arose as he stood up quickly. His jelly belly heaved to differ. Wearing a confident sneer, he rushed at Megumi. Normally Megumi would sidestep such an attack but she needed to end this quickly and quietly as to not alert any guards. Von Dumpling's contemptuous charge was met by a quick jump and two legs quickly wrapped around his chubby neck. With a tuck and roll Megumi brought the man down with a hurricanrana and suddenly had his head in the vice-like grip of her strong thighs. The triangle choke immediately started to take its toll on the obese man as his face turned from pale to red in seconds. 'Grrr... who are you?' he gasped. 'Butterfly Hamada,' the girl declared firmly. ' I was sent to collect your marker. I was to ask for it first,' she said with a scornful voice. 'Or do you prefer to finish this fight here and now?' She clamped on the triangle choke and Von Dumpling gurgled like a baby. Maybe it was the predicament he was in. Or maybe he seemed to recognize the name. He waved his hand over and over, as if to signal he was giving in. Megumi released the choke on him and quickly got to her feet. 'The money, now.' Von dumpling rose to his feet, fighting the pull of gravity on his potbelly. He waved the turkey leg that was still clasped in his grip at Megumi. 'I have no bloody interest in fighting you.' He walked over to a large wooden oak chest to the right of his enormous bed. He looked at Megumi as he slowly opened the lid, to show he was not doing anything sneaky. He slowly grabbed four large stacks of cash, bundled with rubber bands, and threw them at the door to his bedroom. They landed on the carpeted floor in a heap. Megumi studied him for a moment, her expression dubious. Honor prevented her from giving this man the beating he deserved, but neither did she trust him. 'Go ahead,' he urged. Megumi turned toward the door and bent down to pick up the stacks of money. Ron Von Dumpling could not know about Megumi's fighting abilities or her uncanny 'gifts', and he grinned in triumph at her back as he slowly raised the turkey leg high in the air. His expression said clearer then words that, yes, perhaps the stupid wench could fight, but she was still no match for him. He lunged for her back. Megumi whirled and knocked the turkey leg from his hand in a lightning-quick movement. His jaw hung slack for an astonished moment, then firmed as he closed his eyes and prepared himself for the killing stroke. 'Open your eyes, fat man,' said Megumi. Her command stunned Von Dumpling into compliance. He stepped back warily. 'Why?' he asked simply. 'If you are who you say you are, why not end this and have done with it?' 'Why not indeed?' Megumi said dryly. For a moment she wished CYDONIA Inc. was not quite so picky about certain matters. Old school rules applied. This man deserved no respect, deserved very little in the realm of a fighter's honor. Even the informant, Big Daddy Taylor, had observed that Von Dumpling was a sleazy, lascivious, and evil man. But she had agreed to play by the rules. It was just hard for her to imagine that these men she had encountered over the past two months didn't all deserve the same level of punishment and no respect from her. 'I did not choose this fight,' she told him. 'I came here with a peace offering. You showed you have no honor Ron Von Dumpling. And because of this I will make sure the agency's final orders are meted out. You shall never wrestle again in your life.' She bowed ever so slightly in the sign of respect for another warrior. Then she prepared for battle. Her words held the chilling quality of ritual. Trying to summon a defiant sneer through a face smeared with gravy and turkey juice, the Englishman assumed a defensive posture. Her first kick was low, to the legs, but Von Dumpling's thighs were like tree trunks. His confident grin returned. He swung at her wildly, heaving with every step. A straight kick to the gut halted his rush. So intent was Megumi on the fight that she did not notice the bluish hue gleaning in Von Dumpling's eyes. The man was staring at her left arm, where her traditional Japanese tattoo covered her from shoulder to wrist. His stare was one of wonderment and dread. Little did Megumi notice for at that instant she felt that something was wrong. A moment later she heard the muffled sound of voices and footsteps coming up the stairs. It was time to end the fight. With her next kick she caught Von Dumpling right in the throat. A kick slanted to the left and down broke off his kneecap and with a thunderous roar he fell to the floor. 'Your wrestling days are over, Ron Von Dumpling. CYDONIA sends their regards.' She grabbed his head and with a twist she broke his neck. Grabbing the money off the carpet she headed toward the window of the bedroom. Looking down at the body of Von Dumpling she shook her head and murmured, 'That's how it should have been done all along.' A sudden pang hit her stomach as she thought of other people who deserved the same treatment. Men like Jack Murphy and Chainz should have their careers ended in just the same manner. They didn't deserve to be in the wrestling business. They had no honor. But she was not the person to judge them. She was simply the executioner of punishment, on orders of those who knew better. But even Megumi could see that tonight, a man like Von Dumpling had been served the proper sentence. With that final thought she leapt into the night.
