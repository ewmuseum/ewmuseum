Crisis to be avoided

23 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd August 2005 Eire Og is in a crisis. Those outside the group of Irish wrestlers have no idea how and have had no indication to the truth of the five words that have opened passage but it’s true. Eire Og is in the middle of something that is threatening to completely wreck the foundations that it sits on. Want me to prove it? I know you do. I can see it in the way you are looking at me. You need to see to believe. Okay so I will. Battle of Britain… My point is proved. You don’t believe me? Why so? Let’s consider my reasoning and it will soon become the universal reasoning. Crimson O’Malec, the AWC wrestler’s choice for best wrestler in its first quarter of being in existence… Why is he now? Gone… Missing in Action… Two weeks ago he was locked in a bitter battle with Alexander Strider and now he is gone. See already one third of the much loved organisation is gone. He is out indefinitely which means he won’t be returning anytime soon to pluck the Irishmen out of their rut. Paddy O’Shea, the great Irish wrestler that has been round the block more than once. How has he been in the last few weeks? Terrorised by Hate and his minions for weeks and then put him through the pain of watching his father being kidnapped. Battle of Britain was supposed to be the crowning feather in the flashy new cap of Eire Og. Emerald Isle was supposed to be the crowning moment as Paddy and Crimson combined forces to eliminate everyone on the bus moving around London and then crown themselves as the Transatlantic. Did that happen? Of course it didn’t. Crimson left before the match happened and Paddy got screwed by some English scum. And now on Fresh! Emerald Isle II must defend their duo titles as the group crumbles in the homeland of their enemy… England. Sitting up in a cold sweat, Michael Fitzpatrick-McCarthy awoke in his hotel room, plucking himself from a nightmare, the nightmare that was Battle of Britain. The Farmer’s new scar was refusing to let the Irishman sleep and anytime he did nod off it was only for a few moments and then he was awoken. The once dominant force in AWC was slowly deteriorating into nothingness. The ascension of so many others was threatening to eclipse its very existence and stop the most represented nation in AWC from being symbolised. Sitting up in the humid summer air, Mickey was forced into a grimace of pain. A small dressing covered the fruits of Steven Xandrous’ attacks but with Fresh! less than 24 hours away, and the Alliances Title defence coming with it, The Farmer had been forced to refuse painkillers, the wonders of unfair advantages and all that jazz. Swinging his feet onto the floor, The Farmer sat on the edge of his bed, hunched forward in a position he felt caused less pain on his shoulder. The third degree burns that the branding iron had caused were coupled with an infection from the unclean equipment and the original tattoo leaving the skin weak. Any other moment and The Farmer would have gratefully been placed on the injury list for at least the time being. Sadly this was no other moment. The Farmer held his head in his hands, the moonlight glow shining through the blinds that decorated the Travelodge room. Pain was written all over Mickey’s face as he brushed his fingers through his hair and stared aimlessly out the window, his mind trying to deal with the burns and prepare for his first title defence. Sleep was out of the question. Champion by good fortune was how he had been perceived. Crimson disappeared so the belt was thrown at him. This week is a chance for The Farmer to prove himself but in the light of the weekend’s events he isn’t fancying his chances. As he sat on the edge of the bed, he wanted to scratch at his shoulder but couldn’t. His movement was eliminated as he waited for the burns to heal. Struggling to stand up, The Farmer’s face was contorted with pain and looked to step towards the window. Walking was having an adverse effect on his body. Wrestling was having adverse effect on his mind. Looking out the window, The Farmer wanted a rest. Not a chance to go home and rest, because when eh did that he missed the squared circle but a chance to step back, take a week off from competition and breathe. Each individual battle that Eire Og was fighting on their own fronts had left them wide open to many other attacks and they were slowly losing their power. In a couple of weeks when they go to Ireland things will be improved by the crowd reaction but the hostile environments they received from the Englsih crowds were in no way pleasant. Looking out the Birmingham window, Mickey didn't even know where Paddy was. But... Fresh! is a chance for Eire Og to re-assert themselves. Fresh! is a chance to remain Alliance Champions. Fresh! is an opportunity to prove that Eire Og still have it.
