Keep It on the Down Low

14 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/14/2006 The camera slowly fades into the 31 Union West St. restaurant, Blue Water Grill, one of the best restaurants in all of the city of New York. The time is about 8:35pm and the scene is filled with anxious customers who are dressed to impress while awaiting their meals. Dishes of fresh, steamed shrimp, salmon, lobster and other dishes can be seen being carried around the vicinity by the members of the wait staff. They whip around through the jungle of tables greeting their customers one by one with bright smiles across their faces. The camera pans around the scene and it seems as though all is well with exception to one table in the far corner of the outdoor dining area. The camera zooms in on the lonely black woman and it turns out to be Jasmine who is sitting there awaiting someone to arrive with a slight frown on her face. She is wearing a sleek black evening gown with a shimmering pearl necklace draped around her neck, a pair of pearl earrings dangling from her ears and her long, brown hair elegantly styled. She rests her elbow on the tabletop with her chin in her hands and lets out a slight sigh as her eyes slowly scan the vicinity. Jasmine: (under her breath) Where's V? He said he'd meet me out here about a whole half hour ago. Why's he so late? (shakes her head in disappointment) It figures. The one time he tells me he's gonna spend some time with me and he pulls some crap like this. Well, I guess he'll be here soon. I hope so... A waiter approaches her table and greets her with a bright smile. Waiter: I'm sorry to bother you, Miss, but would do you know if your friend will still be joining you tonight or not? She turns towards the waiter and gazes at him with saddened eyes. Jasmine: Umm...I don't know. I guess we could give him a little more time to show up. I'm sure he'll be here eventually. Waiter: (nods in agreement) Very well then, Miss. Jasmine sheepishly slides her empty water glass across the table towards the waiter. Jasmine: Could you fill up my glass while you're here. Waiter: That will be no problem, Miss. Jasmine: (turns away) Thanks. The waiter snatches up the glass and walks away. Jasmine just shakes her head and wipes a tear from her eye. As the camera slolwy fades out. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ In Another Part of the City... The camera fades into the hallway of an apartment building in New York where 'The Violence' Vince Jones, who is carrying around his Countdown Trophy, can be seen walking down the hall alongside a beautiful black woman who has a large grin on her face at the moment. She seems to be overjoyed with Vince's presence as they stop in front of one of the doorways. She turns, faces Vince, and flashes a great big smile in his face. The woman: I really had a great time with you tonight, baby. You really know how to make a girl feel good. Ya know? Vince Jones: (nods and grins) Yeah. V knows. You ain't gotta tell him, LaShonda. He be gettin' that shit all the time. Ya know what they say right? LaShonda: And what's that? Vince Jones: LLVJ, Ladies Love V. Jones, and that's that real shit right there, girl. LaShonda: Oh, I bet they do. I bet they do. LaShonda looks downward a bit amused as she spots a little boy who is tugging on Vince's jeans. Vince looks down at the little boy in surprise. Vince Jones: What the... Little Boy: You're 'The Violence' Vince Jones! Vince Jones: Yeah! And what it to ya, lil man? Little Boy: You're one of my favorite wrestlers. Can I get your autograph? Vince Jones: Huh? Vince Jones stiff arms the little boy to the floor in anger. Vince Jones: Fuck off, lil man! You interruptin' some grown folks business up in this piece right about now! Now get the steppin' and get yo lil punk ass back in the bed! The little boy hops up to his feet, begins crying and scurries off down the hallway. LaShonda looks at Vince in shock of his actions. LaShonda: Wow! You like that with kids all the time? Vince Jones: Uhhh...Well, V and the lil one (looks down at the trophy in his hand) gotta be out anyways. Been real. (mutters under his breath) Mahfuckin' kids! LaShonda reaches out a hand and places it square on his chest and begins rubbing it up and down in a seductive manner. LaShonda: But why you gotta go so soon (she slides in close and presses her body against his), Big V? Vince Jones: Just gotta. Gotta take care of some business and shit. You know how it be? LaShonda: Oh, don't tell me you're one of those types. (leans in and gently kisses Vince on the cheek) You get a woman all worked up and then just leave her behind wishing she gotten just a lil bit more? LaShonda continues to gently kiss him inching further and further down his neck while caressing his face. A sly grin creeps across Vince's face as he's enjoying every minute of this. Vince Jones: Baby, you really temptin' and shit but V got some places to be right about now. So, we gonna have to pick this back up at another time. LaShonda: (she leans back and begins pouting) But I want you now baby. You've got my fire burning right about now. Someone's gotta put it out. What I gotta do to convince you to stay around a lil while longer? Vince Jones: A whole hell of a lot. Cuz V really gotta get going... LaShonda: (winks and smiles) How about a lil bit of...dessert? You can put that tongue of yours to work and feel me up like a snake; but, if you really gotta go that's fine with me. Vince Jones: (eyes light up) Dessert? LaShonda: Yeah. Got some...ice-cream. Got a lil bit of...whipped cream. (licks her lips) I got some chocolate syrup and some...cherries. You like all that? Vince Jones: (chuckles to himself) Yeah. V likes that shit. (plays along) We usin' bowls and shit? LaShonda: (smiles) I guess you could say that. Vince Jones: (nods his head) V startin' to warm up to this. LaShonda: Oh, I thought you would, baby. Vince Jones: (continues to play along) You got any spoons? LaShonda: (laughs to herself, puts her arms around his neck and looks up into his eyes) Why use bowls when we can use our hands? Well, if you're down the door's open, baby. Just come inside. Vince Jones: (tries to remain composed) Aiight! V'll have to think 'bout that for a second here. LaShonda: Aiight. That's cool, but don't keep me waiting too long. She smiles, unlocks her apartment door, steps inside, and shuts the door behind her. All of a sudden Vince begins nodding his head in satisfaction and laughs to himself. Vince Jones: Yeah boy, we 'bout to put the fan on that ass. 'Bout to lay the hammer down on that shit good and bang it out. Now that's what the fuck V talkin' 'bout here. Vince raises the Countdown Trophy to eye level and gazes at it with a large grin on his face. Vince Jones: V. Jones gotta give you yo props. You and V a match made in heaven and some shit. Things lookin' up... LaShonda (from within the apartment) You coming? Vince Jones: We still thinkin' out here, LaShonda. Give us a moment. LaShonda: (from within the apartment) We? Who's we? Vince Jones: Nevermind that shit! Aiight? Vince shakes his head, wets his lips, and gives the trophy a quick kiss and smiles at it. Vince Jones: Aiight. Its go time! This the gameplan! Let's hit this shit and split the fuck on out! Ya heard? He looks down at the trophy and nods his head as if he's in a huddle with a teammate or something. He then opens the door and slides inside the apartment along with the trophy. The camera slowly fades out. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Back at Blue Water Grill... The camera fades back into Blue Water Grill and the table where Jasmine is seated. She appears to be a bit restless at the moment as she shifts her position in her seat and begins glancing around the vicinity in hopes that Vince shows up. Unfortunately for her she doesn't spot him. She looks at the small wristwatch on her arm and shakes her head in disappointment. Jasmine: Alright. Its 8:50 now. Where's he at? I can't believe this. If he's stood me up than this is some bullshit, some real bullshit! She reaches for her half-empty glass of water and takes a quick sip and sets it back down on the glass table. She reaches down beside her and lifts up a black purse which she sets down on the table in front of her and whips out a small black cellphone. She flips it open, scours through the list of names, comes across Vince's number, and sends him a call. Jasmine: ( Oh, he better answer this damn phone and have a good explanation for this shit! That's all I've got to say about that... She lifts the phone to her ear and patiently waits for an answer as it rings. The phone continues ringing as she begins rapping the table with her fingers. She rolls her eyes as she is directed to his voicemail and hangs up the phone in a haste. Jasmine: What is he doing right now anyways that he can't answer his damn phone?!? She quickly dials his number up again and awaits an answer as the camera slowly fades out. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Back at LaShonda's Apartment... The camera fades in and we are inside of what appears to be her bedroom. Vince is seen sprawled out across her bed with a blindfold over his eyes and a scowl across his face. He is stripped down to nothing but his boxers and his black t-shirt as he patiently awaits LaShonda's arrival. Vince pulls the blindfold off his eyes slightly and looks up towards the empty doorway. Vince Jones: Hey! When the hell you gonna be in here, girl? V can't wait all day and shit! Let's do the damn thing here! LaShonda: (from down the hallway) I'll be there in a few, baby. Don't worry. A woman needs time... Vince Jones: Yeah? Well, time ain't exactly the shit V gotta a whole hell of a lot of right now! So let's get this shit movin'! LaShonda: Chill out, baby! I'm almost ready. And you better have that blindfold on still. I don't want you peeking! Vince Jones: Yeah, yeah, yeah! Vince puts the blindfold back over his eyes and tries to wait as patiently as possible. He suddenly hears his cellphone vibrating from in his pair of black jeans on the floor and quickly lowers the blindfold off of his eyes in frustration. Vince Jones: Who the fuck keeps callin' the damn phone!? Shit! V ain't answerin' it right now! Damn! Vince quickly rolls over, reaches out for his jeans, snatches out the cellphone, quickly opens it and closes it and then puts it on silent mode. Vince Jones: Yeah! Ain't nobody fuckin' this shit up for V right now! LaShonda: (from down the hallway) Aiight. You ready for me, baby? Vince Jones: (under his breath) Ah fuck! Vince quickly shoves the cellphone back in his jean pocket and rolls back over into his ready position. Vince Jones: Been ready for this shit, girl. Come on in. And let's get this shit on! LaShonda: Alright. I'm coming... Vince smiles, rubs his hands together deviously, and puts the blindfold back over his eyes and lays back and tries to appear relaxed and smooth. LaShonda's footsteps can be heard coming closer and closer down the hallway and then they stop. LaShonda: Alright, baby. I'm here. I hope you enjoy this. And I hope you don't mind that I borrowed a lil somethin' somethin' of yours to make this all happen. Vince Jones: (in confusion) Huh? Borrowed somethin'? Vince whips off the blindfold and begins licking his lips in anticipation as he eyes LaShonda from her beautiful face and smile, down to the delicious looking whipped cream bra she sprayed overtop of her heaving breasts, to...his Countdown Trophy which she happened to fill up with ice-cream drenched in chocolate syrup, and topped by whipped cream and a cherry LaShonda: So what do you think? Vince just gazes back at her in utter horror. LaShonda: Wow! Is this your first time doing something like this? (chuckles to herself as she leans against the doorframe) Oh, we're gonna have a great time with all this ice-cream and chocolate syrup, baby. Even brought a lil extra syrup incase you'd like a lil more for some...other things (winks) Vince hops up to his feet in rage and points a finger at her. Vince Jones: What the!? Are you out yo fuckin' mind, bitch!?!? What the fuck were you thinkin', huh!?! LaShonda lowers the trophy by her side in disappointment spilling some of its contents to the floor. LaShonda: But I thought you'd like this, baby? I did this for you... Vince Jones begins snatching up his jeans, pants, and boots, and putting them back on. Vince Jones: Well, you were thinkin' too fuckin' much on that shit! That's where your fuckin' problem was! LaShonda: (frustration mounting) Don't you fuckin' talk to me like that!? Vince marches across the room, looks down at the trophy in her hands, and just shakes his head. Vince Jones: And give me that fuckin' trophy! V out this bitch! LaShonda tries to block his way out the room, but Vince just shoves her to the side and exits. LaShonda turns around begins yelling back at him. LaShonda: Yeah! That's right! Get out! Get the fuck out! Vince Jones: (from down the hall) Don't worry 'bout that shit! V gone and he ain't comin' back! LaShonda: Fuck you! She places a hand upon her face, begins crying, and slaps the wall out of frustration as the camera slowly fades to black. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- In Yet Another Part of the City an Hour Later The camera slowly fades into the hallway of an apartment complex in another part of New York where Jasmine can be seen walking down the hallway like a woman on a mission. Her eyes are focused straight ahead down the hallway as she clutches her purse tightly. She grits her teeth the whole way down the quiet hallway alone. Jasmine: I can't believe he did this! That was some bullshit! I'm out there waiting for his ass to show and he never arrives! I'm a beautiful woman! I don't deserve this kind of shit! I shouldn't have to take this kind of shit! And I won't take that kind of shit off of him! I don't care if he is (in a mocking tone) 'The Violence' Vince fuckin' Jones... She stops in front of one of the door to Vince's apartment, whips out a key, and unlocks the door. Jasmine: ...I'm too good of a woman to have to put up with shit like that! She swings the door open and the lights our out. She looks around the apartment in fury and spots Vince laid out on his black, leather sofa fast asleep and snoring. She glares at him and just shakes her head. Jasmine: V!!! Wake up! Vince rolls over in his sleep, clutching his Countdown Trophy like a teddy bear, and begins muttering to himself. Vince Jones: (in his sleep under his breath) Damnit, LaShonda. V already told yo ass he don't want shit from ya. Now fuck off! V sleepin' over here! Jasmine: LaShonda!? Who the hell is that!? Jasmine flips on the lights marches over to the sofa and begins kicking it. Jasmine: V, wake your ass up right now! We need to talk! Jasmine puts her hands on her hips and glares down at Vince in fury. Vince just rolls over some more and continues muttering. Vince Jones: (in his sleep under his breath) Damnit, LaShonda. Go the fuck away! V tryin' to fuckin' sleep here, babe! Jasmine: (nodding) Oh, you're gonna wake your ass up whether you want to or not! Jasmine begins looking around Vince's apartment and makes her way to the connecting kitchen area. She begins looking through drawer after drawer and finally comes across a hammer. She makes her way back across the room to Vince with purpose. Jasmine: Yeah! I got a little surprise for your ass! Vince Jones: Quit playin', girl. Jasmine reaches down and yanks the Countdown Trophy from Vince's arms. Jasmine: Give me that damn trophy! Vince begins slowly stirring to life as Jasmine sets the trophy on its side on the coffee table in front of the couch, raises the hammer above her head, and looks up at Vince who is now finally coming to his senses. Vince Jones: Hey! What the fuck? That bitch got V's damn trophy again... Vince turns around and his pop open wide with horror as she points the hammer at him. Vince Jones: Whoa!!! What the fuck's gotten into you, huh? Jasmine: (eyes Vince like a hawk) Vince, who the fuck is LaShonda!? That's all I wanna know right now! Now who the fuck is she!? You better tell me! Vince shrugs his shoulders. Jasmine nods her head and looks down at the Countdown Trophy. Jasmine: Wrong answer, V! She raises the hammer ready to strike the trophy and Vince glances at her like she's nuts and throws up his hands. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa, whooooooa! Easy there killa! Chill out with that fuckin' hammer you got there, girl! Jasmine: I should smash the shit out of your damn trophy right now! Vince Jones: (shakes his head quickly) No, no, no, no, no, noooo! You don't wanna do that shit! Now put the hammer down, baby! Jasmine: I will when you tell me who the fuck this LaShonda bitch is! Vince Jones: (shrugs his shoulders) V don't know what you talkin' 'bout! (chuckles nervously) Who the fuck is LaShonda!? V don't know any LaShondas. Jasmine slaps the tabletop hard with her hands. Jasmine: Bullshit! Don't play stupid with me! I heard you talking about her in your sleep when I returned! (nods) Well, you've been so close to this damn trophy ever since you got it! Maybe I should ask your fuckin' trophy what the real deal is! How about that, huh!? Jasmine picks the trophy up off the table and holds it up in front of her and Vince watches her a bit uneasily. Jasmine: So, who is this LaShonda!? Has V been creeping on me with her!? What? You don't wanna speakon that either? Well, in that case... She slams the trophy down on the table and raises the hammer to strike it and Vince throws his hands up again. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Don't do that shit! Jasmine : (glaring at Vince) So, you gonna tell me who this bitch is now!? Vince Jones: V must've misheard you the first time around and shit. So, we talkin' 'bout LaShonda? See, V got this cousin LaShonda... Jasmine: (stares a hole in Vince's head) If you're lying to me right now... She looks down at the trophy and readies the hammer once again ready to strike the trophy with it. Jasmine: ...Oh, I swear I'll smash this trophy in! Vince quickly reaches out and grabs a hold of Jasmine's hand in hopes to calm her down. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa, girl! What the hell is up with you!? How 'bout we chill and calm it down some? Jasmine slams the hammer down on the coffee table and glares at Vince with a wide-eyed look of disbelief of his state of ignorance. Jasmine: What is wrong (places a hand on her breast) with me? You wanna know what is wrong with me? Jasmine begins laughing to herself and Vince begins laughing along with her hoping to calm her down, but her expression quickly turns. Jasmine: Why are you laughing!?!? Vince's laughing ceases. Vince Jones: Yo chill the fuck out, Jasmine. Jasmine: Fuck that! I'm not gonna chill out! So, you mean to tell me that you honestly don't know why I'm pissed right now! Vince Jones: No... Jasmine: (nods her head) Well, let me fill you in on why... I'm pissed off! (points to her hair) You see my hair? Do you know how long I sat in the beauty parlor today getting it done just right for you? Do you even know how long I had to sit while my nails were being painted today? Did you even know that I got a fuckin' Brazilian wax today just for you? Vince nods his head and begins rubbing his hands together. Vince Jones: (grinning from ear-to-ear) Yeah that's that good shit! Gettin' that kitty cat nice and smooth... Jasmine slams the hammer against the table in rage in order to get his attention again and it succeeds. Vince sits up and focuses his eyes on her in silence. Jasmine: Shut the fuck up, V! I'm not finished yet! You ever had a Brazilian wax before, huh!? It hurts! If fuckin' hurts like hell; but, I went through with today for (points the hammer at Vince) you! Vince Jones: Aiight! You makin' V a lil nervous here with that damn hammer, girl! Jasmine: What!? Oh, I'm making you nervous!? Well, if you want to talk about feelings we'll talk about some feelings! How the fuck do you think I felt when I was sitting around for about an hour thinking you were gonna show up and meet me for dinner, huh!? Vince Jones: Oh... Jasmine: (nods her head) Yeah! 'Oh' is fuckin' right cuz I was feeling pretty horrible sitting there alone! I cried Vince and I don't shed tears for many! Then, I come home... Jasmine looks down, notices Vince trying to snatch his trophy back from her and she takes the hammer and tries to smash his hand with it. Vince barely gets his hand away and she just glares back at him. Jasmine: I'll shatter that fucking hand of yours! Vince Jones: No need for that, baby! Jasmine: I should do a whole lot more than that! After being stood up by you tonight I come back here see you sleeping and hear you calling another woman's name! Vince Jones: And V already told you! LaShonda's V's cousin and shit! That's who she is, Jasmine! Jasmine just shakes her head, slams the hammer down on the table, and tosses the Countdown Trophy into Vince's lap in disgust with him. Jasmine: You know what? Forget this! I hope you have fun with that trophy tonight! In fact, I hope that trophy gives good head and turns some tricks for you too! You're gonna need it!! Oh, and don't worry! I'll find out who the fuck this LaShonda is one way or another! Until then, I'm still gonna support you in the ring and all, but that's it! I have no obligations to you other than that! Goodnight! She rises to her feet, and exits the den and goes down the hall. Vince just sits there in momentary silence. He then looks down at his Countdown Trophy. Vince Jones: (laughs nervously) Well, that was some crazy ass shit right there. Thought she was gonna do somethin' drastic. Oh well, she's gone and you're fuckin' safe. (raises the trophy to his face and kisses it) Eh, she'll get over all this shit in a few days. Everything'll be cool as usual. The camera slowly fades to black.
