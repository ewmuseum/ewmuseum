The End's Beginning

5 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug 5/06 'The fuck?' 'Good morning sunshine!' The sun glares balefully down into the front seat of a rather grungy looking car. Jordan White blinks away unconsciousness and immedietly tenses. 'Fuck did I get here?' 'It doesn't matter.' Click. 'All that matters is that you don't make me shoot you. Johnny Lexicon glances past the metallic sheen of a stainless steel snub nosed .38 with a deadly serious expression. 'Alright. Okay. Then, fuck is going on?' 'It's called pay back Jordan, and let me tell you, it's a son of a bitch!' Lexicon suddenly veers the vehicle to the right, Jordan jumps as the car bounces and shakes its way onto a dusty side road. 'Don't you just love the desert?!' Johnny yells over the roar of the trek. Dunes stretch off for miles in every direction, broken only by jagged stone peaks and small, scattered signs of shrub clinging tenaciously to a life best left alone. There was nothing for miles, and in Jordan's widening eyes he started to suspect the horrible truth. 'Look, kid.' He begins but Lexicon interjects, one hand firmly on the wheel and the other still gripping his pistol. 'Nope!' He flashes a toothy smile full of menace. 'We're past that Jordan, I've been listening to you for three years now, I've been haunted by your words for far too long. This is my turn, now you're going to listen to me.' 'Say something good.' 'Trey told me everything.' Lexicon tones flattens as if he's telling Jordan the truth about who shot JFK. 'He felt bad, man, as fucked up as you left that kid he felt bad about what you did to me. He told me about it all, he told me about Eva, the peyote, leaving me to die in Mexico, stealing my company, my money, my home. You took my fucking life!' 'God damn, Trey. Alright, Johnny-' The pistol halts any furthur protest, waving haphazardly in White's direction. 'No man, you took everything I ever wanted in life and then left me to die. And when that didn't work, you flaunted it in my face, you gave me a mask to hide behind, you gave me another lie.' Jordan's clutching the 'holy-shit' handle in a white knuckled grip as Lexicon's steering and compsure slips more from calm into frenzied. 'You walk around with a label for everybody, a come back for every quip. You treat the world as if it's one big joke and you, only you, get the punch line. When Jordan wanted a way to avoid the feds, who did he turn to? You turned to me man, you turned to me and I gave you the means to survive. I didn't tell you to put that fucking mask on, I didn't push you into becoming the Marauder, you put your own head in that leather, and you brought your ass to me!' Johnny slams on the brakes as he cranks the wheel to the left and slams into a ditch. Jordan's head connects with the wind sheild, sending cracks spider webbing out in a perfectly unique pattern around the tiny blot of blood left behind. With a groan Jordan holds his suddenly pounding skull in both hands. Lexicon undoes his seat belt and shifts in his seat to better face his victim, the .38 leveled between them in a steady iron grip. Jordan slumps back and looks through winced eyes at a half grinning, half scowling Lexicon. 'Well. Fuck.' Jordan croaks. 'I know about everything now, I know about you setting me up from the very beginning to take that fall. I know you brought Eva in to get close to me, I know she switched my pills before my match with Trey. I know it was you, Jordan, who orchestrated the collapse of my entire life.' 'Crazy fuckin'-' 'Hey! Focus man!' Johnny throws his door open before lunging for Jordan with his free hand. Jordan tries to pull away but the much larger Lexicon grabs him by the arm and drags him from the car. Throwing White to the ground Johnny aims the pistol at him, the sun at his back forcing Jordan to keep his eyes down against the glare. 'But it wasn't enough to take my money, ruin my career, and steal my company. You had to drug me and then strand me out here in the middle of fucking no-where to die, to disappear. You tried to make me and my name vanish.' Johnny pauses as Jordan starts to stand. 'And you almost got away with it.' 'Almost? Goddamn. Shit. How'd you do it?' Jordan straightens his shoulders and holds a hand to shade his eyes, Johnny's wearing a pair of black jeans and a white shirt left unbuttoned and blowing in the wind. The sun gleams off the barrel of the gun aimed at Jordan's chest and Johnny snorts. 'How did I do what?' 'How the fuck did you survive? Four fucking grams of peyote from me, and you gave yourself that bottle of tequilla. Can't pass that 'I walked out of the desert' bull shit on me.' Jordan points a finger accussingly. 'I dragged you to the most cut-ass, out of the way place I could think of, crashed my fucking car, and let your loaded ass wander around the dunes. Crazy shit happens, yeah, and I'm impressed and all, but I mean, how the fuck you survive that?' 'I sucked the putrid life out of cactuses for five fucking days, baking in the sun and struggeling to bring my mind together from the brink of insanity. And then, then I had to fight dirty little mexican's in dirty little mexican rings for dirty little mexican money. I stole and killed a pig because I thought I was starving to death, but you made it look like an accident, so imagine my reliefe to find out you'd not only survived, but you had made it back home.' Johnny begines to walk as he talks, waving his weapon and hand about to emphasize certain points. 'I was too fucked up still to put two and two together though, you came to me and I asked you for help, and you gave me a mask. You gave me the prison you chose for yourself. But you know what?-' Jordan suddenly grabs at the gun, driving his head forward into Johnny's face. Lexicon reels but holds his ground steady, muscling his shoulder down he over powers Jordan and throws him to the sand, the gun falling a short distance away. Johnny wipes blood from his lip and advances but Jordan's quicker, the gun comes up quickly and Johnny stops short, Jordan getting to his feet hastily. 'Man. Fuck this bullshit. You hid X-rays from me, X-rays that showed me one walk down the aisle away from living in a wheelchair.' 'You stupid shit, that wasn't me! You planted those documents! You planted them to throw the feds off your trail, how could the injured Jordan White be The Marauder?' 'Well. Yeah. So what?' Jordan hesitates, for once Johnny doesn't see the answer for it all on the man's lips. 'You figured it out, dick.' Jordan blinks and Johnny makes his move. A small cloud of dust erupts, sand hitting Jordan's face and eyes he recoils out of instinct. Johnny hits him with a solid punch to the jaw, struggeling to keep the gun aimed out at nothing they fall to the ground. Jordan swings his forehead into Johnny's nose with a crunch, and then repeats for good measure. Lexicon growls and doesn't release his grip, fighting the wave of nausea that rises as his own blood falls from his wounded face Lexicon angles an arm across Jordan's grunting face and holds it at bay. Both men howl and rage and scream as they fight for control of the trigger, the power to end it all. Johnny suddenly surges for a straddeling position and Jordan curls his legs up tight, planting two boots into the big man's chest he launches Johnny a couple of feet to land solidly on his back. So taken by the anger Jordan scrambles to his feet and goes to leap upon his fallen foe. Johnny rolls over to face White and levels the pistol between them. Jordan stops with a gulp. 'Tell me.' Johnny says breathlessly as he starts climbing to his feet, chin and chest soaked in blood. 'Does it look familiar?' Jordan's expression spells confusion first, and then his eyes are inexorably drawn away from the .38 and to the land around them. Just a short ways off a sun soaked beat up old cadillac sits nose first in a ditch, the windows broken and the trunk left open and empty. And just a few steps closer than the car is a small pile of rocks. 'My shallow grave.' Lexicon says behind him, cold sanity replacing the fevered tense. 'Why did you come back here? Why did you come back to build a tombstone for a man you tried to erase? And why didn't you look for me?' Jordan feels the barrel press between his shoulder blades but refuses to raise his arms like some captured criminal or some pitiful nobody pleading for his life. 'I don't know.' 'Don't give me that bull shit!' Johnny screams and pistol whips Jordan across the back of the noggin. White doesn't fall but Johnny shoves him to his knees before the pile of rocks. And there, centered to face Jordan's exact location is a small weather stained plaque with ornate letter engraved upon its surface. Those letters read the name 'Jordan Xavier White'. 'I guess this means I'll be speaking at your funeral.' 'Don't.' Jordan says quietly as Johnny lowers the gun to his head. 'Don't you fucking do it!' He erupts. 'You wouldn't have anything good to say anyway, Mr. I Ended Nine Careers But Still Didn't Expect Jordan to Ditch Me. Fuck, man. You act like life is some fucking chess game, then get pissed when you wander into checkmate. Fuck did you expect me to do? You're a goddamned asshole, Lex, you're a fucking piece of shit, and besides--one of us had to it. Rather it be me doing the stranding, you know.' Jordan stops, reaching down to pull a pack of cigarettes from his pocket. Johnny twitches against the trigger and Jordan forgoes the idea. 'Act like I wanted you fucking dead. I'd have shot you, kid, I'd have killed you. Then again, maybe we'd be a little better off.' 'And the truth shall set you free!' Johnny wails to the cactuses and scorpions. 'Oh how I've dreamed about this since the day Trey told me the truth. Just when I had given up hope on ever seeing you emerge again, when I had started getting my life back in line, started getted ready to wrestle again, lo and behold who scheduled to fight the night I make my return? If it isn't Jordan White in the flesh. I have alot of skeletons in my closet, but only of them walks and talks and lives, spending my career, money earned on my broken back! You see the name on that plaque, don't you.' There's a moment of cold silence and Jordan feels more than hears the hammer of the pistol fall as Lexicon squeezes the trigger. Click. 'We've both lived the hell inside of that mask. Now we both owe each other our lives.' Johnny says calmly, dropping the gun into the sand near Jordan's knees. 'Now you understand what it's like to know that these hills will be your grave, when nature forces her impending will upon you and giving you naught the option of survival. Only then can you realize what I have, only now can you throw aside the fear of everything else.' 'Hahah. Fuckin' Zeus. Fuckin', goddamned, Odin. Motherfucking shithead.' 'This, Jordan.' Johnny says as he steps back, Jordan climbing to his feet with murder in his eyes. 'This makes us even, and now you see, there is nobody to fear, nobody in that ring can hurt you, nobody in there can bring you farther than I have and nobody ever will. Just like nobody can ever take away from me what you took. Because of this, because of each other, we're stronger than ever-' 'So. What?' Jordan interjects closing the gap between them with bravado, the pile of stones looming at his back like a malevolent marker of his past. 'Is this your way of saying you wanna work together? Like we thought we would, like we always should have? We're like negatively charged atoms, you bring us together and all sorts of crazy shit happens right? You wanna rule the roost like the biggest sons of a bitches walkin this earth, you're so fucking arrogant but the worst part about it is that you're right.' Jordan pauses. 'We would rule the roost, and we should work together.' 'But we won't.' Johnny adds. 'We can't.' Jordan finishes. 'You go your way, I'll go mine.' 'That leaves us?' 'Finished. Even. Forever.' Johnny turns his back. 'Have a good life Jordan.' 'Fuck you going?' 'Home.' Johnny starts walking. Jordan squints against the sun and looks around. 'You got a car stashed somewhere?' He asks. 'Johnny?' Jordan picks up the unloaded pistol. 'Johnny! Fuckin leaving me out here? After everything we've been through you don't have anything for me?!' Johnny's hand comes up over his shoulder giving Jordan White the middle finger for the last time. 'That's great.' Jordan's tone quiets. 'Now he's suddenly me.' 'Worse.' Johnny says under his breath with a grin as he puts one foot in front of the other.
