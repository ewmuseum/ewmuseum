A Goddamn...What Exactly?

3 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03.10.06 Josh Marquez has been a very busy man. Since his arrival in the wrestling playground known as the Atlantic Wrestling Club, he’s stepped into the ring/puddle/cruise liner, with some of the biggest names in the company. Pierce Lavelle, Darcy Crisis, Ellis Nash, Gabriel Afeaki and the list goes on. Granted, he hasn’t had a huge amount of success thus far in his fledgling career but given the quality of opposition, he isn’t too disheartened. For even though he has yet to secure that breakout win, the man from Las Vegas has definitely made his mark in the company. Marquez has been the proverbial puppet master, pulling the strings of the forlorn gypsy Paddy O’Shea and elevating himself up the ranks despite having an average at best win-loss record. When he set out along this path, Josh only had one thought in mind - to get on the Coast To Coast card. When that happened though, the gambler in him took over and Marquez made the decision to push it further, to see just how far the ignorant Irishman would pull him up the ladder before the dirty penny finally dropped inside his lucky charm loving head. The answer? Well, Josh isn’t exactly sure yet. O’Shea is still blissfully ignorant, out stealing food and decorating caravans like always, enjoying life again now that he has such a good friend as the Sin City native. It’s got to the point now where Marquez’s confidence is so great that he’s even involving others in what are doubtlessly being denounced by some as ‘nefarious schemes’. Garbage Bag Johnny and the far-too-hot-to-be-a-wrestler Ellis Nash were the first to be drawn into Josh’s web but the chances are high that they won’t be the last. Indeed, the Nevada man was planning just how he would amuse himself at the upcoming edition of Fresh! when he got a phone call. “Jhosh Marquez?” “Yeah?” “You are in a match zis week. Come ready to wrestle. Do not be late or Docteur Drake vil be angry.” That was the whole phone call. No clue about who it was making the call, no mention of his opponent, nothing, just those few words, delivered in an authentic French accent. Josh, being the manipulator he is, instinctively thought that someone was taking the piss, maybe some concerned citizen or roster member attempting to give him a taste of his own medicine. So he checked out the AWC website and, sure enough, there it was displayed in glorious…well, black and white, but the impact was the same as if it had been in high-defintion, super sparkly flashing text. AgentDash vs Bryce Savion vs Leah Adams vs Josh Marquez Four Way Elimination Match Livewire Title Reinstation Tournament, Round One Referee: Joseph Reid Dash came so, so close to beating Garbage Bag Johnny, and if he had, he might find himself contending for a different belt. The Livewire title returns; these first four hopefuls will contest a tough Elimination match to decide the first of the two competitors in the match at Untouchable. Not just any match either, as any beret wearing, garlic loving frog who is planning to be in attendance can see. Another title shot for our Mr Marquez, who is surely well on his way to setting a record for most title opportunities in the shortest space of time. Josh was shocked, and it takes a lot to shock a man who once inadvertently walked into Elton John’s dressing room only to find the notion of concierge service being taken to new levels. But that’s another story for another time. Where were we? Yes - Josh was shocked. Not because he had been booked despite some poor recent performances, the fans clearly loved him - or loved to hate him - definitely one of the two. Either way, he was, as they say in the business ‘money’. Nor was he shocked at the abrupt and ridiculously vague nature of the phone call, the show was coming from France after all. You couldn’t expect a nation of wine drinkers with the military might of a kindergarten to be very organised. No, it wasn’t any of these things that caused him to drop his beer before letting out a stream of expletives that would put a Compton homeboy to shame. Instead, it was a simple, throwaway phrase that adorned the main page of the site. A phrase taken from a statement released, purportedly, from the office of the new ‘commish’, Mike Wade. The Livewire title rewards those hard-working individuals for whom the higher-level championships are too much of a jump. Of course, the piece quickly goes on to say how the newly reinstated title will be more prestigious and that the company will actually spend time focusing on it, but we all know that’s as likely to happen as Emo Kid deciding to wear Abercrombie & Fitch. That he, Josh Marquez, the man who was responsible for one of the defining moments of Coast To Coast, the man who has illuminated AWC since his arrival and a man who has the ear of Paddy O’Shea, is being put in a tournament for this worthless title is an insult, a slight, an unsubtle ‘fuck you’ from the powers that be. There was no way in hell that Josh was going to accept this outrage. He was going to pick up the phone, ring Mike F’ing Wade and tell him to stick his match, Josh would be quite happy orchestrating some backstage shenanigans this week…or was he? For at the end of the day, a title, any title, in this company is something not to be sniffed at. A title puts you among the elite in the company, gives you name value outside the establishment and makes you a nice little lump of money on top of your standard pay. Champions are known and remembered throughout the business, stars without gold quickly fade into obscurity. There has been a buzz about Josh, but it’s been confined to AWC, nobody from PRIME, FUSE or GCW knows a damn thing about him. Not one thing, but you can bet your ass they know all there is to know about Gabriel Afeaki. Not because he is one of the few men in the world who has been defeated by a chimp in an IQ test, but because he is the Relentless champion. Yes, now Josh thinks about it, maybe this little title shot would work out quite nicely. When he wins the belt he can use the strap as proof to Paddy O’Shea that their plan to dominate AWC is working and that Paddy should keep listening to the man from Vegas. If, by some unimaginable twist of fate, Josh doesn’t win the belt at Untouchable, well he can rile up the pikey, demonstrating that it was yet another attempt by ‘the man’ to hold him and his Irish buddy down. So when he picked up the phone, it wasn’t Mike Wade he called. “Allo?” “Room service? Great. I want a steak, some champagne and a nineteen year old virgin delivered to room 327 now.” “But Monsieur, vee do not ‘ave any steak left!” “Salmon then.” “It vill be up soon, Monsieur.”
