Movie Sets, Hot Sex, and Business Managers

30 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-30-06 “OH MY GOD! IT’S COMING RIGHT AT US!” The sounds of a woman’s high-pitched voice is heard as a brunette dressed in a lab coat raises a shaking hand to point at the oversized snake lifting its head up from the floor to hiss and show its fangs. The woman slowly backs away, her hand fumbling over the counter for anything to use as a weapon. She knocks over vials, bottles, and Bunsen Burners, and in the end the only weapon she can find is a syringe filled with some sort of clear liquid. As the snake lifts its head again to attack the lone female scientist the sound of breaking glass is heard as a large figure barrels through a glass door from the left. With the appropriate slow-motion camera affects our hero charges bravely into battle armed with a large automatic gun of some type; what looks like a cross between a standard military-issue M16, the Russian AK-47, and a few novelties from the science fiction genre. As he lifts up his weapon a hail of bullets fly out with a screeching sound. “Get out of here! Go! Now!” The yelling voice of our hero belts out at the female scientist as the large predator twists and coils, hissing and snarling in a mixture of pain and frustration as the high-powered bullets riddle its body. As it retreats our hero walks toward it, constantly holding the trigger down. As the camera zooms in we find the brave slayer to be Scott Taylor, dressed in combat boots, cargo pants, and a black tee covered with a padded vest, his long black hair pulled back in a simple ponytail. The final touch is a headset that fits over his head, the earpiece near his right ear and a small microphone near his mouth. The giant snake crashes into a wall of various scientific gear and then falls to the floor, thrashing and squawking. Scott releases the trigger on his futuristic weapon as the beast finally stops moving; smoke pouring from the barrel of his giant gun. He stares down at the large reptile, bloodied and filled with holes and kicks it for good measure. As he stands victorious over his fallen enemy his moment of glory is interrupted by a shouting voice. “And……CUT!” Scott turns to see a slim man with a goatee, sunglasses, and baseball cap dressed in khakis and a Hawaiian shirt sitting in a chair with a cameraman standing beside him. Others surround these two; a mixture of people holding up boom mikes, mirrors, and other cameras and a few people whose only job seems to be to hold clipboards and jot on them and a few elite who are decked out in Men’s Warehouse and Burlington Coat Factory three piece suits and yakking on cell phones. Scott hands the prop weapon to an assistant and then rips off the headset from his ear, dropping it into his pocket and running a hand over his sweating forehead. As he cools off some animatronics puppeteers carry away the menacing villainous snake to prep it for a second shoot of the action scene. As he stands in the middle of the set he is approached by the beautiful and buxom scientist from earlier. She smiles up at him, running her hands over his muscular arm. “Nice job, Scott. I knew you would have some experience with the action scenes since you’re a professional wrestler and all, but that was really impressive. I think it would be great if you would have hit it with a chair and then…..um….” Scott watches as his co-star, the leading female of this horror/monster film tries to think of the proper technical term for whatever move she is envisioning in her mind. After a twenty second silence she smiles and looks back up at him. “After the chair shot you should hit it with a DBT.” “You mean ‘DDT’.” Scott corrects her on the name of the move and laughs softly as Mr. Hawaiian Shirt makes his way over from his director’s chair and laughs loudly, slapping the wrestler hard on the chest with his hand. “Wonderful, Scott! Wonderful! You’ve been so great in this movie so far! I can really see you following in the line of the other wrestlers who became feature film stars! Picture it with me!” The director turns away from Scott, holding one hand up in the air as he looks off into the distant sky, the other arm wrapped around Scott’s shoulders. Though his sunglasses cover his eyes, his gaping mouth shows the excitement he is feeling. “People will mention the name “Scott Taylor” in the same breath as “The Big Apple” Hank Heegan, “The Stone” Stoney Ravira, and the most famous of them all…. Former WCW World Heavyweight Champion, David Arquette!” Looking slightly less than not enthused at the predictions of his future, Scott looks to the director and tries not to burst out laughing. He removes the bony arm from around his shoulder and then turns back to his co-star; slipping his arm through hers and starting to lead her away from the set. “C’mon, Ashley. Let’s leave our brilliant director to his delusions of grandeur and go find the caterer. Whether it’s dropping power bombs or dropping giant snakes, this stuff is hard work. Besides, you can say you had lunch with me. I’m sure all your girlfriends would just be so pleased to hear that one.” Ashley only lets out a girlish giggle and walks happily away with Scott, arm in arm towards the area of the set where the caterers have set up tables of food for the actors and crew working on this film. As Scott and Ashley fix plates of mostly finger foods and snacks a sign on the back wall reads, “Sci-Fi Pictures Original Studios: Lot B.” While having left the wrestling world earlier this year after a bad deal with a lackluster promotion, The New Hero has found himself doing a campy B-film for The Sci-Fi Channel called “Killer Serpent Attack.” The movie was nothing more than a lame “Sci-Fi Pictures Original” filled with monster movie clichés, herky-jerky acting, and CGI that looked like it was programmed by graduate students from some fancy-shmancy film school. 3 Hours Later: Scott Taylor steps from the bathroom in his hotel room, stepping back into the hotel room with nothing but a towel wrapped around his waist and his towel-dried hair hanging loosely down his back. After the towel falls to the floor Scott picks up some clothes from the bed and gets dressed, slipping on a pair of silky black boxer shorts and a white tee shirt which fits well to his muscular form. As he lies back on the bed and reaches for the remote control nearby the bathroom door opens again and a second figure walks out. “Scott…. you turning in already? I thought we could have some more fun first….” The voice is obviously feminine and has a sultry tone in the words. Scott looks up from putting the channel on the 10 PM news to see Ashley standing before him with her hair pulled up and a slim towel wrapped around her alluring and very feminine form. She smiles sexily as she moves to the bed and climbs onto it, crawling towards Scott. She leans in close next to him and snuggles up to him, rubbing his nose against the side of his neck and giggling. Wrapping his arm around her to pull her closer Scott watches the sports section of the news, the local sports analyst commenting on the winning streak of Scott’s favorite NFL team; the New Orleans Saints led by their star player Reggie Bush. After a few moments of kissing and licking Scott’s neck but not getting any kind of reaction, Ashley pushes herself up and stares at him, eyes narrow. He casually looks over to her with a look of complete non-interest and then turns back to the TV, his attention brought back to the news. Ashley sighs heavily and then crawls over him, grabbing her things from the floor and sloppily getting dressed. “You asshole….” “What?” “You brought me back here after filming was finished and you had sex with me, and now you’re just ignoring me?” “I’m not into commitment. You were cute and we had spent some time together doing the movie. Don’t look anymore into it than that.” “Asshole! You think being some pro wrestler guy makes you better than me?! I bet I did you better than that redneck whore you used to have!” At those words, the former OSW Champion jumps up from the bed and stares angrily across the room at Ashley. His body is tense and his eyes are narrowed, feeling the adrenaline flow through his veins as his heart palpitates. Realizing she has hit a nerve, Ashley folds her arms over her chest as she stands there with her jeans unbuttoned and a shirt in her hands, her wet hair framing her face. She smirks as she turns towards the door, getting dressed on the way. Scott watches her leave and then turns back to the bed, looking at the empty double sized bed. He moves to the door and locks it with the chain and then leans against it, sighing. “Stupid bitch. I had been trying to keep that part of my past out of my mind and she hits me with that bullshit because I wouldn’t screw her again. Stupid bitch…” As Scott slumps against the door he is brought back to reality by the sound of his cell phone ringing. The ring tone blares Megadeth’s “Holy Wars” maybe a little too loudly but then again, when is it okay to NOT have Dave Mustaine’s music playing way too loud for human ears to tolerate? Scott picks up the flip top phone and pushes it to his ear. “Yeah?” The voice on the other end of the phone isn’t heard but it’s obvious from the one-sided conversation that Scott is conversing with his manager or at least someone who works with him. He stands in the now empty hotel room, phone to his ear, and nodding every few moments. “Yeah, the movie is almost done. Maybe two more days of filming and then I can fly back to New Orleans…AWC? Yeah, the name sounds somewhat familiar. I’ve heard them mentioned. What about them?” As Scott runs a hand through his damp hair his eyebrows raise and he tries to hold back a smile. “They did? Really. What was their initial offer? Really…wow, that’s pretty nice considering I’ve been out of action for a while. Yeah, tell them I’ll get in touch with them when I reach New Orleans. We can talk further details then and sign the contract.” There is a long pause as Scott listens closely to the voice on the other end of the phone. He begins to pace the carpeted floor and nods a few times, mumbling “yeah” and “sure” every few breaths. Suddenly he stops in his tracks. “Yes…I knew she was there…No! I haven’t seen her since OSW when she attacked me during the big match. Yes! I remember! She cost me during my big moment! I haven’t forgotten that, Troy! Her brother keeps me updated on what goes on but I’m always in a different part of the country than she is. When we do cross paths, we’ll just have to settle our differences then. Yes, Troy! I know about the damn magazine! That was why I had you look into this AWC thing in the first place.” Scott switches the phone to his other ear as he sits back down on the bed, leaning back slightly. He breathes softly as he listens to his road manager tell him something, another one of the lectures Scott has gotten used to hearing within these past eighteen months. When it’s finally over, Scott speaks up again. “So that’s what the big deal is. I had heard of this thing called “Triangles” and “The Lethal Lottery” but wasn’t sure what they were. So that’s the scoop. Well, with this “Triangles Match” upcoming and all, yeah, I’ll change my flight from New Orleans to fly straight out and talk with them. You’ll meet me there, right? Great. See you then.” Scott pulls the phone away from his ear and flips it shut. He lays the phone on the table by the bed and then leans back on his bed, resting his head against the stack of pillows. He knew that “she” had joined AWC; it was hard to miss what with the news in the wrestling world buzzing about her appearance in the magazine. It had been nearly three years since the last time they had seen the other in person and now they were going to be in the same damn promotion again after all this time. Pulling the covers back and climbing under them, Scott stretched his muscular body out and got comfortable under the warm covers in his hotel room. He had to be up early to head back to the set the next morning to finish up the movie but now he knew that he wouldn’t sleep at all, what with the news that something special and unexpected was awaiting him in AWC once he signed the contract. As Scott rolled over and buried his head under the sheets his mind was filled with the sounds of a steel chair crashing into his head and the roaring boos of wrestling fans as he fell to the mat in a heap…….
