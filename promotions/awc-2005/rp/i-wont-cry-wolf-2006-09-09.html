I Won’t Cry Wolf

9 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/2006 \\\\Prologue Well this is a surprise isn’t it? The man known as Juvian Ramorez has re-appeared from a hole and asked for a try-out in Atlantic Wrestling Club, but the main question is….Juvian Ramorez is now Thirty Three years old, is he passed his prime? Can he really fly like he used to and my god how much ring rust will he have for he hasn’t wrestled in the best part of six months, but the biggest question of them all is just where has he been? End Prologue//// “Hello, my name is Juvian and I am a former drug user” What’s the point in going to these sessions anymore, I mean I don’t suffer from depression anymore, I took time away from the road to get my head straight, oh sorry I forgot to tell you this is Juvian Ramorez here, I am a thirty three year old grown man, who used to be a well known wrestler however I took a wrong turn in life and ended up addicted to painkillers, I nearly took my life but I have to thank who was around me at the time who showed me the way in life. “Welcome Juvian” They all say, but you can here the sniggers from the back of the class, they are all amazed that a person who once had everything complete in life is no better off in life than just another average Joe in the street, but what they don’t know is that I am on the up, for once I have turned to help and I am beating this addiction. “Ladies and Gentlemen please give a huge round of applause as this is Juvian’s last session, he has completed his sixteen month rehab and is now ready to leave, please congratulate Juvian” They cheer and clap, but what for? For all they know I could just walk straight out of this door into a alleyway and shoot up on the bit of heroin that has been passed to me by another user, hell they don’t really care about how well I do on the outside world, there just happy getting that extra amount of money in the back pocket, but ill stay true to my word to them I wont take drugs when I get out of this building, but I never said for how long heh. “Thankyou, your all so kind, but its time for me to go” I place the chair from underneath me to the front of me, with one last blank expression on my face I leave the room, for now I am finally free, but why do these demons still haunt me, as I push aside the door that takes me back to reality; I am greeted with a smell that I have only lingered for, and I have lingered for around sixteen months, the smell so distinctive, channel line four, the trademark perfume from my darling Miss Rayne Lee. “Congratulations on your success” She whispers in my ear with a loving tone of voice, for this woman is special to me, not only have I been with her for going on four years now but she has been there through thick and thin, I mean she even stuck by me when I decided to go to rehab, people like that you just cannot thank enough for, so when I smelt that perfume nothing else in the world mattered to me in that moment of life. As I smile to myself I am met with a slow kiss across my neck which makes all the hairs on my neck stand up, for a chance to feel this maybe I should opt out for rehab every year, not saying my addiction is that bad, just saying at the end of it that the pay off is so special that it’s the one thing that will stick in my head for the rest of my life, so here it is, Juvian Ramorez’s life starts again…or does it? “You waited all this time for me?” I asked her with a nervous tone of voice, because its not just me thinking here, but why would she stay loyal to me, I haven’t seen her in over a year and for her to stay loyal to me, that would be asking a hell of a lot, but Rayne was the loyal kind, I mean she betrayed her own brother, killed her mother and spent five years in prison, I mean I can trust this girl cant I?….not a chance. “You’re the only man for me Juvian, but we need to catch a flight” She said to me, that’s great I thought I’m the only man for her…but what about the women, its also no secrete that she is bisexual so maybe she’s been diving whilst I’ve been away, and what’s this about a flight I mean I don’t even have a god damn passport. “A flight? To where? A one way ticket to hell?! Before I got chance to think anything I was met with some harsh words but some that made me think for a long time. “If you want to go to hell Juvian then get back inside that room, but if you want one more chance at life then get with this place with me, we are going to Portugal!” Hell I am not going in that room again, I don’t care what I have to do to avoid it, me and that place don’t go together anymore, but why Portugal? what is so special over there? I mean apart from the kick-ass beaches and the forever hot weather Portugal isn’t really a place for a man with a headfuck to be going. Ahh I get it now, she wants to slowly appear over me holding a coconut broken into two with a straw popping out of it on a beach right… “I thought I would book you in a wrestling match already” Okay….WHAT?! A match already, hell I’ve been out of rehab less than ten minutes and she has me in a match already?! Does she not know that my mind is all over the place or is she using this as a tactic to make some money off me right away, I know she is my personal business manager as well as my lover but do I actually have to trust her this time?, the only thing I know for certain is that I am never ever going back to rehab. “A Match Already, what about a nice trip to the gym first to get me in somewhat shape?” I said to her, she acknowledged it as a good answer and smiled for a second before tilting her head “The gym it will be then Juvian, follow me” I opened the passenger side door to a black Audi TT which was parked a few steps in front of me as Rayne got in the drivers seat, now I would have offered to drive but one, I am a very bad driver. And secondly I still am high from methadone. So I thought it was in all of our interests if I don’t take a ride behind the wheel. As I buckle up Rayne wastes no time in flooring the pedal, as I lean over to switch the music on I am met with somewhat of a “get off” type of slap on the back of my hand. Rayne then leans over looking down the side of the car door, whilst sweat drips from my forehead as she pay no attention to the road, I can feel my palms getting sweaty as I see bright lights from what appears to be a truck in the distance, I slowly pull on her arm to try to get her attention but to no avail. “RRRR…Rayne….Truck!” Quickly she looks up and turns to the right down a side road closely and narrowly missing the on-coming truck in the other direction, she turns to me an smiles with a cheeky look on her face before picking up the compact disk she was looking for and sliding it into the radio, as the calming riffs of Alice Coopers “No more Mr nice guy” start to play a somewhat relaxed look came across my face, for this is the song that me and Rayne listened and danced to on our first date all those years ago. She smiles to herself also whilst she slowly begins to press down on the break pedal; we slowly pull up outside a building which ironically has a sign outside saying “gym”. As we stopped I un-belted and opened the door stepping out of the car, Rayne walked around the car to meet me before nodding accordingly and walking into the building, allowing me a sufficient amount of time to myself before walking into the building behind her. As I pushed myself through the doors of the gym I could see that it was packed to the brim with local independent wrestlers all slugging their guts out to make sure that when they had a chance to step into the ring they performed to the best of there ability, for a moment it finally clicked, those people training in front of me are no different than myself, this week I have another chance in the ring, I have another opportunity to make sure I can get a contract, for this is my try-out match, and its been a damn long time since I’ve had a try-out match. I saw Rayne talking to a old looking man, I’m guessing he was in my mid sixties maybe even the owner of this joint, and I mean he must be pleased to see a person like Juvian Ramorez in his gym right…or maybe not he seems to have a stern look on his face as he walks up to me with Rayne. “Heck, I never thought we would see a washed up Ramorez in here” He spoke down to me in a Strong Southern accent “Excuse me, do I know you?” I replied trying not to lose my cool “That Depends if you want to, You see Ramorez I watched you wrestle all over in the past but the fact is your old, and I think you don’t have what It takes anymore” He said to me still looking down on me “What the hell do you know, I mean I come out of rehab and straight away your in my face, who in the hell do you think you are?!” I’m really starting to lose my cool now, I mean just who does he think he is? “Ramorez, Son…Stop relying on your past as an excuse, the fact that you’ve stepped into this gym already proves to me that you still have the heart for it, however your past your prime, I’m sure as hell you couldn’t hang with some of the boys here tonight” He said with a optimistic look on his face, did he just challenge the great Juvian Ramorez? As I look around at the guys training I start to feel confident about my chances, I mean some of these are bigger but I have the experience right, I mean I’ve wrestled in over forty different states, over thirteen different country’s hell I’ve won titles left right and center, what in the gods green earth have they won?, nothing apart from a ass kicking from one pissed off Mexican. “You know what old man, I would love to prove you wrong and beat whoever you throw at me” I said with my hands clenched, “Ok, but there is some terms here, if my boy wins then you is banned from here” he said “And if I win?” I Asked. “Well, IF you do then you can train here for free” He said to me once again in a stern accent “Well that seems like a good deal to me, so bring it” I replied. I looked over to Rayne who just stood there with a huge smile on her face, has she planned this all along or has my big mouth landed me in trouble once again, I slowly walked up the steps of the ring and onto the apron, after a few paces I jumped off the apron and walked over to Rayne instead of getting in the ring, I gave her a slow kiss on the forehead before un-buttoning my shirt and taking it off, I handed her the shirt to look after then bent over and took my boots and socks off so I was bare-footed. I then turned around facing the ring and extended my arms out before running to the ring and sliding under the bottom rope before rolling and nipping up in a true Mexican style. Oh feels good to be back in a ring, I run from rope to rope bouncing off them and stretching them as I wait in the ring for my opponent. And a Opponent he is, I guess he is no older than twenty three years old, his physical body is very impressive and he looks to be around two hundred and thirty pounds. As I look around I can see the promoter with a huge smile on his face telling the whole gym to stop what they are doing and come witness this contest, he walks over to do the formal introductions as I stand in my corner ready to go, I know I’ve still got the talent, come on lets get on with it. “Ladies and Gentlemen, and people of ‘Hard Knox’ gym right here in sunny Detroit I give you the main event of this evening, we have two great cruiserweights who are gonna put it all on the line for a personal bet!, on my right weighing a lean two hundred and twenty nine pounds from Orangeville, North Carolina he is the Hard Knox champion Reid Ghana…” Everyone at ringside including Rayne gives a clap and a cheer as young Reid steps forward and bows to the crowd. “And to my right, he is a former Junkie, a Current bum and a Mexican without a job, Juvian Ramorez…” What?!, that’s got to be the crappiest ring announcement I have ever had. Nevertheless this man is mine, I’m going to prove not to everyone in the building that I am still the best, but I will prove it to myself as well. As we circle the ring I hear the bell sound and he immediately charges at me with a lariat taking me right down to the mat. Aww!, Jeez that actually hurt, the little bastard. He walks over to my head and picks me up, he goes for a headlock but I fall backwards countering it into a back suplex, as he goes down hard clutching his head I run towards the ropes and hit a legdrop as I come back off them to the back of his head, I pick him up by the head and take him over my right shoulder with a headlock, as we are both on the floor I can hear him gasping for air as I clench harder around his head and neck. I grab his left arm and work around his body locking in a reverse arm-bar. I look over to Rayne who smiles but then is distracted as you Reid rolls through and slides to the outside of the ring to gather his thoughts, is this all this kid has, man I must still have it as I haven’t broken a sweat and he is almost dying on the outside, as I catch Reid gathering his thoughts I quickly run against the ropes and hit a trademark non handed rope flip taking down Reid and half the audience who happen to be at ringside, upon further inspection I can see that Reid is nicely bleeding from his forehead. I roll you Reid back into the ring, I roll back in and when we reach our feet I kick him with a swift kick to the gut, as he leans over a grab his arm and place it behind my head for a exploder suplex, though everyone knows this is over when I hit my move, I jump backwards hitting a picture perfect Backflip exploder suplex sending young Reid to the mat, as I roll over and cover I hear the crowd count. One… Two… Three… Thankyou ladies and gentlemen but Juvian Ramorez is back, that poor child needs to learn a lot more before he can beat the likes of Juvian Ramorez, Rayne and old Hillbilly get into the ring as Rayne comes over to me Hillbilly checks on Reid who is still out of it and his eyes are all Glazed over. “Was that good enough for you old man?” I said to him with a sarcastic tone of voice “It was good but let’s see if you can do it this week at Fresh!” He said to me “Maybe if you let me get on with training I will do” I said back, knowing in the back of my head that if I didn’t win on fresh then I would truly be doubting myself, for I now know I still have the ability to do it, I know I can do it, I must do it!, come fresh I will do it! ---- FIN ----
