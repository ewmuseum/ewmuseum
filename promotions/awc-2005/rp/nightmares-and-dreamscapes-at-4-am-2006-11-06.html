Nightmares and Dreamscapes at 4 AM

6 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/6/06 The clock on the nightstand by the bed was glowing brightly in the pitch-black room, the blazing red numbers read “3:21”; AM time that was. The room was mostly empty after Ashley had angrily stormed out after realizing she was used for some good loving any nothing else. Scott rolled over in the bed, pushing at the covers that covered his body. He groaned and kicked his legs, pushing the covers back before rolling over to lie on his back. He was obviously dreaming something that wasn’t too pleasant. Though his AWC debut was still a ways off, Scott was finding himself haunted by thoughts of “What if?” and “What will happen?” the more he realized that he would be seeing her again after all this time. His road manager had reminded him of this during their phone call earlier in the night but Scott was too stubborn to really think about it and make a decision over the matter at this point. His face winced as he grunted again, the muscles in his upper body tightening as he partially lifted from the bed. The images in his mind were things he wanted to leave behind in the older days of his career, but they were all too more than happy to resurface now. “Nyyyuuhhh…. No…stay h…here…” Scott’s voice floated softly through the moderately sized hotel room in the darkness as he dreamed. Well, nightmare would be a much, much more appropriate term for what he was doing this night. Though it was a nice seventy-two degrees in the room thanks to the air conditioning, Scott was pouring a sweat. A cold, cold sweat that was. THREE YEARS AGO. AUGUST 24, 2003. NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA. Scott Taylor stood in the center of his private dressing room, decked out his black and green wrestling tights and laced-up black boots. He was wrapping athletic tape around his fingers, getting ready for the main event of tonight’s Old School Wrestling PPV, By Any Means Necessary. Scott was going to get a chance to compete for the World Heavyweight Championship, his second shot at the title in the year since arriving to the company. As he dropped the tape back into his gym bag his green eyes looked over to the other person in his dressing room. The Yet-To-Be Redneck Princess sat on the table in the opposite side of the room, still wearing her wrestling gear, having just finished her match little more than half an hour ago. She sat there, hair pulled back and sipping on a bottle of water. Her match was nothing more than a four corners “OSW Ladies” match where Teresa, on the top of her game after arriving to OSW after leaving PCW, easily proved herself the most dominate female in the company; a sentiment further stated by her on-screen and off-screen romantic relationship with the Number One Contender himself, Mister Scott Adam Taylor. “You ready to go after Boda tonight? That guy may be bigger than you, but you can kick his ass, baby!” Teresa spoke up as she sipped on her bottle of water again and watched Scott get ready for his match. The Number One Contender straightened his elbow pads and reached into his bag for a hair tie, slipping it into his waistband as he moved to the table where Teresa sat. He placed both hands on her hips and leaned in close, his face less than an inch from hers. His emerald green eyes looked right into her baby blue ones. “I’m ready for this. I’ve spent the last eight years working up from wrestling in High School gyms to get here to the big times and now I’m going for the World Heavyweight Championship.” Before anything else could be said Teresa leaned forward and pressed her lips to Scott’s, kissing him softly but very affectionately. He found himself all too eager to return the kiss and their lips locked harder and more passionately. After lingering for several moments Scott finally pulled back and smiled down at her. “Listen, Teresa. I know you won’t like this….” The blonde’s eyebrows arched and she got a wary look on her face, waiting to her what Scott was going to tell her. “I know you’re just as excited about this as I am, but I need you to do something for me. Stay back here tonight. Don’t come out to the ring tonight. We both know our great Champion has been trying every trick in his book to stop me from getting here tonight and I know he’ll try to use you against me if you’re at ringside.” “I can damn well take care of myself, Scott! I don’t need you to tell me what to do!” Came the angry retort from The Top Rope Temptress. Scott sighed as he pulled his hair tie from the waist of his tights and pulled his flowing mane back, tying the black hair in a loose ponytail to keep it out of his way during the match. He sighed heavily again and shook his head, placing his hands on Teresa’s thighs. “I know that. I know you can defend yourself and all, but this is serious. Boda is one big and tough son of a bitch whose going to give even me a hellacious fight to keep that World Title tonight. Please…for both of us, stay in the back during the match. When I beat him tonight, you can come out to celebrate with me. But I need you back here during the course of the match, Teresa.” How could she argue with him? Scott could be just as stubborn as she could and deep down, she knew he was right. This was serious business and the last thing Scott needed was a distraction to keep him form attaining the dream that every wrestler who ever laced up his boots wanted more than anything. She just nodded her head once and then grabbed him by the hair, pulling him towards her again to deliver one more kiss. Scott pulled away from her after another moment of being up in her oh so sweet tender kiss. He smiled as he moved towards the bench, picking up his shimmering blue sequined shirt and slipping it on. He pulled open the door to his locker room and stepped out into the hall, leaving Teresa behind to watch the match on the television from the locker room. The moment had come. Scott stood just behind the curtain under the big screen, psyching himself up for the match. After eight long years, all the work was finally paying off. As his theme music began to blare over the PA in the arena and the lights dimmed, Scott stepped out from behind the curtain and stood with his back to the crowd, arms elevated into the air. As he listened to the ring announcer introduce him his mind was totally focused, only thinking about having the ten pounds of solid gold wrapped around his waist after he defeated the World Heavyweight Champion. The pyro exploded and Scott spun around, throwing his fist into the air and delivering a few kicks to an invincible foe as he moved down the ramp. This match was two months in the making and now it all came down to this, the biggest PPV of the Summer and the main event. “This is all it comes down to, wrestling fans. This match was been in the making for a while. Scott Taylor won a Triple Threat match against “Old School” Dave Gibson and the returning former Champion, Rhett Locke to get this shot. Even when he had to defend his Contender status against Teresa Tomas two weeks ago, Scott didn’t give up. Now it all comes down to this. The main event!” The words were of OSW’s head announcer and play-by-play commentator traveling from his headset into the homes of all the fans watching live, who unfortunately whose name can’t be mentioned since all the licensing fees were used to be able to say “Boda”, “Dave Gibson” and “Rhett Locke” in this writing. Scott climbed into the ring, jumping up onto the apron and flipping himself over the top rope. He ripped off the trademark sequined shirt and tossed it into the crowd as he leapt up onto the second rope, posing and flexing for his fans in attendance and around the world tuning in live. As the defending Champion’s music hit the arena, Scott leapt back down to the mat as the fans began to boo extremely loudly for the hated World Heavyweight Champion. “OH! AND THAT CANNOT FEEL GOOD! THIS MAY BE THE END OF THE MATCH! THE REFEREE IS DOWN BUT THAT POWER BOMB WAS BRUTAL!” The screaming of the color commentator, as Scott Taylor is power bombed into the mat after falling from the seven-foot shoulders of Chris Sloboda. The champion is obviously weak and struggling after all the punishment the two have inflicted in this match. Instead of going for the pin, the big man staggers back into the corner and takes a breather as Scott lays out on the mat, breathing hard but not moving after the fall. As Boda comes out of the corner and moves towards Scott the curtain at the top of the ramp is pulled back as Teresa Tomas steps out. THE American Woman jogs down the ramp and watches as Boda pulls Scott up and takes some weak elbows to the stomach. Scott nails a strong double thrust to the chest of Boda and then Irish Whips him into the ropes. As the big man comes back Scott dives at him, using what energy he has left to nail a huge Spear straight out of the picture book. The fans pop and Teresa begins to slap the mat, screaming loudly for Scott to get up, joining in with the fans that are behind their favorite. “Look alive, people! Boda is DOWN after taking that Spear as Taylor gets his second wind of the match. He has GOT to capitalize on this and put Boda away while he’s stunned!” Scott staggers around the ring, holding his forehead and sweat pours down his entire body. Every muscle he has, and some he didn’t even know existed, are aching and screaming out as he holds the top rope for support. As Boda begins to stir, Scott looks over to see Teresa at ringside, cheering him on and giving her approval. Holding onto the top rope Scott begins to slam his right foot into the mat, giving the cue to the fans worldwide what is coming next. As the crowd pops again the stomps get faster and harder, the THUMP echoing through the arena as The Best Super Kick In Wrestling Today is warmed up. “Here it comes! Taylor is going for “Hit The Lights” and it’s going to be LIGHTS OUT for Chris Sloboda here tonight!” Boda finally pushes himself up groggily and stands at a stupor, his head spinning and body aching after that surprise spear. Taylor runs from the corner and throws up right leg, the sole of his boot connecting with Boda’s chin. The fans in the arena jump to their feet in a huge pop as the move is hit. The World Champion drops again. Still on his feet, Scott begins to give the signal for the end of the match. As he stands above the fallen Boda, getting ready to head to the top rope to end this match the camera suddenly cuts to the ringside to show Teresa Tomas by the announce table grabbing a chair. She folds it up and slides into the ring. Already in the corner with one foot on the second turnbuckle Scott is shocked to see Teresa standing in the ring with the folded chair. He stops climbing the turnbuckle and moves next to her, saying something goes unheard. He doesn’t look pleased at all that she is here and the two are arguing. After a moment of listening Scott nods his head and then moves to Boda, pulling him up, though he can’t even stand on his own after the Spear and Super Kick. “Oh, now this all makes sense! It was Boda who made Scott wrestle Teresa two weeks ago and last week she had to wrestle the Champ himself – though that match never took off as Scott hit the ring and a brawl broke out. She wants her payback!” As Boda stands staggering in the middle of the ring Teresa grabs the chair and lines up her shot. She takes a running start and then – BEELINES TOWARDS TAYLOR, DECKING HIM WITH THE CHAIR! The fans explode in boos as Scott Taylor collapses onto the mat, blood flowing from his forehead as he lays lifeless after the cheap shot! Teresa smiles proudly as she rolls out of the ring and snickers at the fallen Taylor. Finally having his bearing back, Boda grabs Taylor and pulls him up, positioning the near unconscious Number One Contender for a Boda-Bomb. Still at ringside and being booed heavily by the fans for her heel turn Teresa shakes the fallen referee and tries to get him to come to as Boda drills Taylor into the mat. When the man in the striped shirt wakes up he sees the World Champion pinning Taylor and crawls over. ONE! TWO! “NO! GODDAMNIT, NO! THIS ISN’T RIGHT! TAYLOR HAD THIS MATCH WON UNTIL THAT SHE-DEVIL TERESA TOMAS TURNED ON HIM! THIS IS NOT RIGHT!” THREE! A near riot breaks out in the arena as Boda gets the three count and retains his Title. As Taylor lies unconscious in the ring Teresa slides back in and goes to Boda, wrapping her arms around him and kissing him. “AHHHHH!” The scream of Scott Taylor filled the hotel as he was awakened finally from his nightmare. He was drenched in a cold sweat and found himself with a major migraine, the details of the nightmare seeming all too real. He pushes the covers back away from his body and then climbs from the bed, unable to lie down anymore. As his blurry eyes look to the clock by the bed the blazing numbers read “3:57”, just barely 4 o’clock in the morning. He moves across the room to the bathroom, stepping into it. As the icy water fills the sink, Scott began to wash his face, staring into the mirror. He looked into the mirror at his pale face and sighed heavily. It had been three years since the break-up and yet realizing he was going to be in the same place with the traitor was getting to him, making him have these nightmares where that night was replayed over and over again. As he emerged from the bathroom he grabbed his suitcase and tossed it onto the bed, ripping it open. Grabbing the first clothes he saw he hastily got dressed. On the table next to the clock sat his keys and wallet, he grabbed those and crammed them into his pockets. There was no way he could sleep now and hitting the road seemed like a much better idea than staring at four walls until morning. Haphazardly dressed in baggy jeans and a red tee shirt Scott Taylor slammed the door to his hotel room and locked it. He set his sights on the parking lot and moved towards it, planning a long drive.
