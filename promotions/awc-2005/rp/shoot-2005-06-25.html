Shoot.

25 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 june The AWC returns from commercial break and we are shown the backstage area where Maddy Estelle stands with 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict. He wears a cocky grin that compliments his $5000 Outfit. The spark in her eye is a dead giveaway that she is star struck by Benedict's cute boyish looks. He is unphased. James Everett III stands firm in the background holding a piece of paper. Massive heat from the fans... Maddy Estelle: Maddy Estelle here with superstar Nathan Benedict who in a very short while will be competing in an 8 man battle royal Solar Power qualifying match. Back at Fresh, Truth Waters didn't have many nice things to say about you. Do you have anything to say about that? 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:I'm sorry what did he say? I was too busy being robbed of a victory by that idiot Steven Xandrous. Maddy Estelle:Well I believe at one point he called you a Sanctimonious Imbecile. 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:He did... Maddy Estelle:He also said that the utter mention of your name just screams the word 'Asshole' 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:That quite enough I get the point. Paramount is so agitated by this broad that he... 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Fix your skirt you dime store slut. This isnt nacho night at the boom boom room. Let me school you and your little friends on the back about the meaning of ASSHOLE. 1. The AWC, the Atlantic Wrestling Club...the most respectable name in professional wrestling boasts of a Champion that barely comes up to me shoulders! The Fans go into an agitated frenzy! Maddy Estelle:Your referring to the Relentless champion Crimson O' Malec? 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Look I don't know what the guys names is. I have to many IMPORTANT things to do with my time rather than memorize the names of these circus clowns...like reading scripts and throwing banana peels at unsuspecting pedestrians...my time is valuable sweetheart... Maddy Estelle:Sunday, battle royal with some of AWC's toughest stars. Any strategies? Any ideas on how your going to win this week? 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:heh...(Adjusts his Ray Bans) Who are my opponents? Maddy Estelle:Well for one, Steven Xandrous. The man who won the triple threat match you competed in at Fresh. 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Well correct me if I am wrong but I believe I was never pinned in that match. I believe it was Spector who went down for the count...making me UNDEFEATED... Maddy Estelle:Well actually you... 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Moving on...who else Maddy Estelle:Johnny Sake and Void to name a couple more... 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:For the 'sake' of a show 'void' of star power...the AWC sure is lucky to have my name in this matchup. Otherwise viewers may tune out and watch more exciting programming...like hair growth informercials... James Everett chuckle at Benedicts joke. Maddy Estelle looks agrivtaed that this interview has turned into a mockery. 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Whats a matter sweetheart...frowning like that could ruin your complexion...wait too late! Maddy Estelle gives Benedict the evil eyes. 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:Cheer up (slaps her in the ass). After I win on Sunday maybe I'll let you come up to my room for a drink...or three... Maddy Estelle:Really??? 'Paramount' Nathan Benedict:No. The scene fades and we are left to see a dollar bill wave across the screen looking more like the American Flag. 'Paramount' Nathan Benedicts Face replaces that of George Washingtons in the center of the bill.
