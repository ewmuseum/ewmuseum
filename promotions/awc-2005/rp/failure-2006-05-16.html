Failure

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/16/2006 The camera slowly fades into a hospital room where a man who appears to be a slightly younger Vince Jones can be seen laying down. He appears to be unconscious or in a deep sleep. All of a sudden there is a small knock at the door. There is momentary silence and then the sound of the front door creaking open breaks the silence. Some footsteps can be heard on the floor and a shadow creeps across Vince's body. The hand of an old man reaches out and touches Vince. Vince slowly begins to stir to life at the feel of the stranger's touch, slowly opens his eyes, and looks at the stranger in confusion. Vince Jones: Huh? Who the hell are you? Stranger: Someone that was a bit worried about you. That's all... {{End of Flashback}} Vince Jones suddenly opens his eyes and realizes that he was only dreaming. He lifts his head up from the counter of the bar that he fell asleep at and snatches up the half-empty mug of beer resting next to his elbow and gulps it down.The camera rises and catches sight of a middle- aged, African-American bartender with a slightly graying beard on his face who is walking over to Vince at the counter.] Bartender: Ah, so you're back? Well, what's on your mind tonight, son? You were out for a long time there. Vince Jones takes a large gulp from out of the beer mug in his hand and sets it down on the counter. Vince Jones: Nothing... The bartender looks down at him in confusion. Bartender: Nothing? Is that so? Well, I doubt a whole load of nothing is gonna make you come to the bar and drink until you fall asleep on my counter drooling all over the place. Now is it? Vince Jones just shakes his head, sets the glass down, and slides over towards the bartender. Vince Jones: Look, its nothin'. Aiight? That's all that's gonna be said about this shit! Nothin' more, nothin' less. So we can cut the chit chat right here and right now! The bartender snatches up the mug and throws up his hands in surrender. Bartender: Alright. Alright. That's cool. I'm just trying to help. That's all. No harm in that, right? Vince just sits there in silence and folds his arms across his chest. The bartender turns around and sets the beer mug down to the side. Bartender: But if we were talking about your situation I'd say that there's something that's really on your mind. Whatever it is that's on your mind has got you down in the dumps. Whatever it is that's on your mind needs to be released, but you're either too much of a big man do so or too scared of a man to do so. Vince Jones: V. Jones ain't neva scared, man! So, you oughta shut the hell up with that noise! Bartender: I'm just talking. You can take whatever I say however you want, man. An awkward silence passes between the two of them. The bartender turns around and leans against the counter. Bartender: Are you still down about Zero to Hero? Is that it? So, you lost. Its not the end of the world. You'll have other opportunities, man. You'll... Vince reaches in his wallet, snatches out a one hundred dollar bill and slams it down on the counter. Bartender: Whoa, whoa, whoa! What's up? Why you leaving? Vince slowly rises to his feet and the bartender watches him in surprise. Bartender: If it was something that I said, I'm sorry. I'm really sorry, but you're not in a great state right now, man. You really should stick around a little while longer. Vince Jones: V sick and tired of hearin' all this shit! Bartender: C'mon! I'm just tryin' to be of help to ya. You're a regular. Vince Jones: You can't help V with this shit! Bartender: Try me! Vince Jones: (shaking his head) Nah. The only person that can help heal V's pain on this shit is V himself. You don't know how it feels to be a failure like this. Zero to Hero wasn't just another night! Zero to Hero was way more than that and right now V ain't got nothin' to show for that shit! V ain't got nothin' to show him for that shit either! Bartender: (in confusion) Failure? Nothin' to show him? What do you mean? Who is this guy you're talking about? Vince Jones begins walking towards the door. Vince Jones: Its somethin' you wouldn't understand! Somebody you've neva met! Somebody who you neva will meet cuz life just weren't so cool to him! Aiight! 'Nuff said! Bartender: Hey! Wait a sec.. Vince exits the bar and slams the door behind him. Bartender: I was gonna wish you good luck in your match this week. The bartender lets out a sigh as the camera fades to black.
