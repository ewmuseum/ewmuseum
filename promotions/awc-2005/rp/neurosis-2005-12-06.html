Neurosis

6 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/05 Imagine a single beat of a drum. Now repeat that beat: again, and again, and again. A thousand times a minute. Repeat it until it ceases to be individual beats and instead blends into a soundless, mindless, formless cacophony of noise more akin to the buzz of bees at summertime than to any conscious musical creation process undertaken by a human. Imagine such a loathsome, unforgiving, merciless buzz into the head of a human being. Feel how the insane, nonsensical rhythm bounces around inside his skull. See the vibrations emanating from his scalp as the Devil’s music takes root. Watch as his teeth rattle in tune to that single-minded, purposeless drive, planted deeply behind the bloodshot, staring eyes. The fingers move on their own accord: the music wants to come out through them as they haphazardly dance on the hardwood table, their feeble rapping a mere fragment of the pure pandemonium raging inside the bald head. It looks like the Hells are ravaging the body lounging over the said table, half-sitting in the chair pushed underneathe it and half-standing over the table that currently is the center of the world to the body. Oh, the Hells those eyes see right now… Unimaginable. Angelica: ”Seriously, he scares me! He tried to kill me once, for crying out loud! I am really scared, dear. I am so scared I don’t know if I should laugh or cry… Laugh or cry, my dear.” Hate: ”My Angel, Azagtoth is of no consequence to you. If you do not wish to meet him, then merely stay out of his way. If you do wish to meet him, simply speak to him. He will not harm you, for he knows that would raise my wrath upon him. I am a commander, my Angel: and a commander holds the reins over his own troops. What kind of a leader is doubted and questioned by the men he has sworn to govern over? No, my Angel, you are safe. They are my men; they are GOOD men. Azagtoth included.” Oh, what kind of a leader indeed. The kind that finally wishes to escape all the burdens bearing upon his shoulders with the only way he has truly known. Through years of blood and pain, a door has always been held open for the man dubbing himself the Fifth Horseman to run through once he can take no more. It is a door he so wishes to nail shut, but always leaves be, in the back of his mind. It stands there, small, unnoticeable, all but forgotten… Until the day when you happen to pass by and notice it. And remember all those things that lie behind it. The white. The forgiveness. The timelessness. The sharp, sharp ends of the needles… Angelica: ”… seriously, what is it with you and Azagtoth? Every time that PSYCHO attacks someone like the rabid dog he is, you rise up to defend him. I can understand that he is your friend, but there is a limit even to friendship. There is a limit to even the bonds of the Cult, dear…” Hate: ”The relationship between us is far from what you imagine it to be, my Angel. Azagtoth is nothing more to me than, as you put it, ’a rabid dog’. He is my personal attack hound, a trained and honed killer who will put his life to my bidding should I ask him to do so. He is the very essence of the Cult: the elimination of one for the good of the many. Where Azagtoth ceases to exist and think for himself, the Cult begins and starts thinking for him. That is the beauty of it: this savage, unpenetrable, all-knowing hive mind of sorts. We are all a part of it. We can grow even more deeply entwined with it, embracing it’s grasp and letting go of our useless egoes for the nourishment of our collective consciousness. It is a deep thought: but then again, all great thoughts are. No shallow ones would live for long.” The ears, whenever the drumming would stop, snatched at these bits and pieces of conversations that still seemed to linger in the room. A rational mind would have realized that whatever still was there, was merely in the mind itself. The people who had spoken those words were long gone from the abandoned, dusty office, even the one who physically stayed there but was spiritually far gone. They could not stomach the atmosphere: the bleak, grey feeling of decay and entropy. The feeling that if you stayed in the room for too long you could feel your skin peeling off, one flake at a time, until you joined in the fine dust coating the nonfunctioning typewriter and the safe which had been cracked a long time ago. The drumming combated this atmosphere, sent it packing from the head of the man now leaning against the table, breathing laborously. The drumming and the Hells had their hold now: no feeling of eventual death could rip this man away from them now. Not now. Angelica: ”My dear, you… I… I know I am not that old. And I know I speak of a lot of things that I should not be speaking of. But please, my love, PLEASE see what is happening around you! There are signs of rebellion and distrust all around you! Behind your back the jealous speech of those who want to be where you stand. In front of you the open defiance of more and more people who refuse to accept the truth. And besides you… The biggest snake of them all. Azagtoth the Poisoner.” The body’s memory was a bit hazy. What had the girl done after that? The drumming helped, gleefully joining in and giving his brain a good whack which sent him convulsing onto his side, hands grasping the edges of the table with white-knuckled strength. Now the body remembered what the girl had done. She had fallen to the floor, and bled. Bled from her mouth and stained it red, so beautifully deep red… Hate: ”Whore! You truly speak of things you have no understanding of, my Angel. You are lucky, for such slander should be punished with the removal of the offending appendage. Be safe in the knowledge that your tongue holds no interest for me beyond the pleasures of the flesh. But I must warn you: Azagtoth would be all too keen for me to grant him a permission to take it. To him, the flesh means power. And a piece of your flesh would mean power over you. Please, do not force me to share my power over you with anyone. You would not like it. As far as of these omens and signs you speak of, I haven’t seen or heard anything to that effect. True, there are those who dissent, but they complain for the sake of complaining. They lack the fundamental cranial capacity to come up with something to change the way things work. Their way to change is a winding, perilous road of petitions, speeches and protests. My way is so much quicker.” The body winced again, this time at the carnal recollection of the moments before the girl had fallen. How the hand had gone up, instictively. How the fingers had balled up into a fist, scarred and mangled. How the broken knuckles had drilled into the delicate cheekbone of the girl, the full force of the blow blasting through her skull… How that delicate face had swollen up and snapped back at the face of it’s conqueror. The face of the girl had told the body everything. She had been afraid, afraid for her life. And the body had liked it. Hate: ”As far as Azagtoth goes, I have stated it to you that should you not wish to interact with Azagtoth, you should stay away from him. A wolf cannot be blamed for ripping apart a rabbit that comes to the lair of the wolf and acts like it was the rabbit’s own. Just as Azagtoth cannot be blamed for the things committed against you when you instigate such behavior with your acts and words. I have tolerated a lot from you, my Angel, but doubting the person who has so fully committed their life to my cause and my ideals… I cannot stand such nonsense any more. You will cease your slander, my Angel, or I will make it cease. There will be All Quiet On The Western Front, should you force me to take action.” The inhered contradictions in Hate’s words were not completely clear to the corpse now shaking on the table, but neither were they hidden. The interests of Hate and the interests of the Cult: the one and the same? Hate wanted to believe so. He had believed so, and for a while it had been so. But now he was being torn between the anarchy-glorifying means and ends of the group he had so painstakingly put together and a world too alien even for him. A world full of lies where even a master spinner of wondrous tales would get lost. A world where the hits were so hard that in any other line of business they would be considered aggravated assault. But here, they were a part of the job. A job. Being paid for beating up other members of the human race was like a dream come true to a sociopath. But now the sociopath’s mind was wracked with items even more powerful than bloodlust. An entirely different lust. A lust it could not comprehend. Azagtoth: ”My leader, a thousand apologies for interrupting you tonight with my meager problems… But I am grateful that you have gotten the time for me. It is good to see that you are still reachable by the common members of the Cult, and not buried in that dreadful ’wrestling’ of yours, my leader.” Hate: ”Azagtoth, you flatter me. For the Cult, I will always have time. I will always be here. Do not worry about things besides the Cult: they hold no meaning for me.” Hate had seen the realization in Azagtoth’s eyes then, and he saw it now as well, the imprint of the boy burned into his eyes all too clearly. The face, painted white. Black hair cradling the delicate features in it’s embrace, the visage pierced by a pair of sharp eyes. Hunter’s eyes. The eyes that knew that the words spoken by Hate were empty and without meaning, like they had been for some while now. They saw, slowly, as the man they had admired for years grew weaker and more foolish every passing week. They watched the slow dergation of all the values and things they had held dear to themselves. They watched with the powerless stare of one seeing the change but finding no way to stop or reverse it. And then the tongue spoke, and things were different. The white face suddenly painted a miraclous visage of an unbeatable conquerer, one he would follow to the depths of the Earth and back should Hate wish so. It painted such a believable mural of complete obedience that Hate almost forgot his shaking and the buzzing inside his head. Such convincing lies, such tales that tricked the heart and snared the soul… But that ultimately were all in deceit. And neither Azagtoth nor Hate would acknowledge this out loud: and so their trust continued to build upon a foundation of shoddy lies and misinformation. Azagtoth: ”You spoke of it yourself, my leader. Now that the girl’s father wants her back, the Cult is put under jeopardy. A full-scale police investigation, my leader, is something we don’t want. The men would not be too happy about it if we brought an army of pigs at their doorsteps just because of this cheap harlot. My leader, please consider sending her back! She does not belong here, amongst us! She does not believe, nor does she have any want or spirit to believe. She is merely here for cheap kicks, my leader, something we should not lower ourselves to. We are not an organization for every runaway to join just because it’s ’cool’ in their rotten stupid minds. Are they willing to bleed for you? Let me cut her and see if she wants it. Let me TEST her! Let me prove that her façade hides nothing but a cheap whore we won’t accept amongst our ranks!” Hate: ”Azagtoth, your thoughts and words are wise, but alas they are also misguided. You forget the fact that by accepting her, I have extended the protection of the Cult over here. Everyone is entitled to join into this sphere of power: if they are in the Cult, they are under it’s protection. That is the advantage of the many over the one. And Azagtoth, as much as you personally disagree with her, I cannot but protect her like any other member of ours. Yes, this business with her father is unfortunate, but we must bear with it. We will not abandon one of our own, Azagtoth. And that is final.” How many times had he repeated those words? Hell, even he was sick of hearing them! This ”let us save all”-crap he had been pushing on Azagtoth made even him nauseous and untrusting of the true calling of the Cult. It was like the Christian incoherent drivel he had been listening to before: empty, yet cleverly disguised in a sea of fabricated lies and double meanings that was impossible to navigate without catching onto at least something, even the tiniest bit of an imaginary tale. Hate’s trembling hand ran over the smooth surface of a leather satchel. The contents were now the buzz in his head. How he craved for more. Azagtoth: ”Other matters are at hand as well, my leader. We see you less and less each month. You spend time away from us, sometimes not even indulging us on the destination of your lonesome journeys. Then we start hearing rumors, DISTURBING rumors. We hear you play the second fiddle to money corporations on national TV. We hear you clash with Irish pigs and God-preaching misguided and you do not stab their eyes out. You leave their throats intact. In short, they walk away alive, which is not the custom. The enemies of the Cult should be mangled and left in a bloody heap, my leader. That is what you teach us, and that is what you tell us is right. We… No, some of the men are beginning to doubt your example, my leader. How can we trust your words if we can’t trust your actions? Please, my leader, I plead you: forget about the nonsensical theater that has so enamored you. Come back to us. LEAD us like you should.” He had tried excuses, but they all died in his throat. He needed the money, it was fun, there was a deeper meaning to it… None of the explanations made it past his lips. They were sucked down to whatever black abyss they rose from, and rightfully so. Yet it left Hate standing there, facing his lieutenant Azagtoth with no words to combat those the young man had so aggressively thrown his way. So Hate had responded with the only way he knew: and so Azagtoth had fallen to the floor and bled as well. Hate: ”When did I teach you to DOUBT me, Azagtoth? Where in my sermons have you found the tract that gives you the permission to question your superiors? It is the sign of a healthy young mind, I understand, but I also understand that I have made it clear that no such things will be tolerated in the Cult. Questions lead to thought. Thought leads to individuals. Individuals lead into corruption, money, greed, all the vices we fight so bravely to eradicate. How can you pour out the purifying flame if you stop and question ’Why?’ Azatoth, you must clear yourself of these thoughts, for they are impure. Cleanse them, and cleanse yourself: then you shall serve the Cult to your utmost ability.” Cleanse the Impure… The words ran across Hate’s mind. Azagtoth had left, with a mixture of feelings: pride, happiness, brooding, anger. Hate felt them too, even now when his most trusted servant had been gone for hours. The buzz was beginning to subside. His hand caressed the leather satchel as his head laid in rest next to it, eyes half-closed, mind wandering. This was as close to happiness as he had gotten in weeks: it scared that for such a powerful man he needed such weak tricks to attain satisfaction. Hate lounged in the office. Minutes had passed after Azagtoth had left, and yet a feeling was troubling Hate. It was an empty feeling, a void devoid of anything, slowly growing in his chest. As second by second clicked by on the cheap clock mounted on the wall, the void grew. Hate sat down in a chair that had once housed this or that power-hungry executive: it was now empty save for a few measly insects who somehow found a living amongst the polyester and the plastic. The desk in front of him was heavy and very old: his hands clenched into fists as he tried to control himself, tried to control whatever thing was growing inside him. Sweat glistened on his bald forehead: eyes were nailed forward in concentration. Hands clenched into fists. Fists opened into hands. The void would not go away. Slowly, groggy, Hate pulled himself up. His eyes, wandering all over creation, stopped on the leather satchel. It’s cool, smooth black exterior and brass clasp did not betray the true nature of it’s contents. Surely a normal person could walk down the street with a satchel like this under his arm and stop for some coffee at a corner shop. Surely a hard-working dad, living the American Dream, came home with a satchel JUST LIKE IT, filled to the brim with work he would have to work all night for only to get fired the day after. But this satchel did not sell marketing ideas, or job advancement, or pay rise. It sold happiness, followed by sweet death. The clasp came undone… Hate flipped open the satchel. Inside were the tools of this particular trade, all in neat rows, everything in it’s rightful place. Pristine. Perfect. PURE. Hate looked at the marvelous sight, and for a moment thought of the wonders of Creation. But this was something bigger than Creation: indeed, it allowed access to sights and sounds beyond that of the mortal man, maybe even beyond what man was ever meant to see or experience. Right now, it was the only place Hate could go to. He took a black leather strap from the satchel and bound it across his right bicep, pulling it tight with his teeth. The veins he was looking for soon rose to the surface, filled with sickly blue blood like dead maggots. Hate raised a small bottle of brownish liquid to his eye level. If anyone had seen him do this right now, they would have been shocked. That the Fifth Horseman, the Nondivine Juggernaut, would go this far to escape a reality he had built for himself. Escape a reality crafted by him into a reality crafted by the wondrous substance held in his fingers. Slowly, a syringe was filled with the brown liquid. It smelt faintly of sweet decay. It looked like decay too. The maggot-vein was too easy to pierce. This is no ’Fear & Loathing’- There are no Samoan lawyers here, nor a wispy-haired fellow trying to cover a motorbike race. No hotel rooms are thrashed or flooded: and there definitively are no plastic dinosaur tails present. It starts out as a beautiful symphony: the scarred face of Hate spreads out into a smile. All is perfect. All is well. Then, something creeps into it. A small sound of discord. Something right at the edge of the hearing. And then it begins to grow. It sounds like… It sounds like… Well, it is quite hard to explain. Imagine a single beat of a drum. Now repeat that beat: again, and again, and again…
