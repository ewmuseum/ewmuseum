Pallas Athena 3: Hunting Von Dumpling (plus more Origins!)

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/05 More origins of Butterfly Hamada Way in the past: So Megumi Hamada went to train with Tyger Shuri-ken, a friend of her deceased mother and cult wrestling star and trainer in Japan for many years. And she did so for about 6 years. And slowly, Megumi was able to take what her mother had taught her about wrestling, and add that to the veteran training by Tyger Shuri-ken, and become a very good pro wrestler. Tyger Shuri-ken sent her to Mexico for two years to learn lucha libre style from a very infamous rudo from Monterey named Mr. Absolutamente. Women's wrestling is very prevalent in Mexico and Megumi was able to learn allot. In order to train Megumi even harder then all of her peers, Tyger Shuri-ken sent her for one year to America, where she worked for a talent agency. The wrestling agency was a shady place called CYDONIA Enterprises, where they took a big cut of Megumi's pay. But she learned even more touring the United States. They got her gigs in many federations. She did shows with lots of other CYDONIA clients. She was booked against wrestlers like Sai, Shewolf Dachau, Pterodactyl, and a young Japanese wrestler who was also doing a tour, Cosmic Lion Fujita. During this time. Megumi was shaping up to be an incredible wrestler. Tyger Shuri-ken had slowly added to her arsenal a lethal number of holds and techniques. And along the way he helped shape the girl into a veteran herself. One day he even gave her the honor of a tattoo. It was in the shape of a tiger's eye and she wore it on the back of her neck. It was considered a great sign of respect and a tradition to only the greatest Tyger Shuri-ken students. Megumi returned to Japan, and a year later she and Tozen met one day at a wrestling exhibition. They talked about their time back in the states and how much they had learned. They hit it off. Now it goes without saying that in Japanese joshi wrestling, there are three 'no's'. It was forbidden for women to smoke cigarettes, drink alcohol, or speak to the men, but Megumi was so taken by the young man and she was so flattered by his gentleness that their romance was not so easily suppressed. Tozen Fujita went under the mask of Cosmic Lion Fujita. He worked in many of the masked wrestler promotions in Japan. He was a runner for yakuza mobsters from a small child. His family was poor, and the money was good. Yakuza were big financial backers of Japanese pro wrestling, and Tozen went to many matches. He was so enamored with the sport, with the respect that the athletes received, that he secretly began training to become a wrestler. However, his ties to the yakuza could not be so easily broken. Once you were in, it was difficult to get out, especially with a family. So Tozen toed the line between Yakuza mobster and wrestler, and as he grew up and worked out, he became top muscle for the local gang he was with, so his importance and value continued to grow with the mob, even though he longed to get out. Tozen was a humble man. Now this boy meets this girl and everything is different and they think they can make things work. Megumi feels ostracized in Japan, where even though she is a great wrestler, she is still looked at by her peers and by society as a half-breed and half a person. She had no family, only her trainers and their students. But even some of her peers treated her poorly. She dreamed to get out. Megumi and Tozen met in secret, to keep their relationship from her trainers and the public, plus the yakuza. But this didn't last more then a few months before Megumi's trainers found out. They immediately to remove her from the promotion and she would never wrestle again. At the same time, this news carried over to Tozen's promotion, and eventually to the yakuza. The mobsters looked at it as an advantage to have their foot in the door of a rival joshi promotion, which was also being financed by another gang. Tozen was approached, asked for information and inside access. It was then that Tozen realized he was bound for life with the mob and there was no way out. Worse then that, he had dragged an innocent girl into the fray, and not only threatened her career but her very life. One night, Megumi met Tozen after a card. They took a taxi to the airport, got on a flight to New York, and never looked back. This is the time they arrive to the SCW. One month later she signed her deal to wrestle for AWC. It has been one month since entering the promotion. TRIANGLES has come to a pass and Jack 'The Bull' Murphy has become a new problem to 'Butterfly' Hamada, but it is one she relishes more then anything else in the world. She is independent, she is wrestling, and she has Tozen. What more could a girl want? ****************************************** Tuesday, 11/29/05 Liverpool England 'Oh, this is RICH! This is one for my autobiography, that's for damn sure and certain. A pet assassin for the CYDONIA agency coming to me for advice! They send their 'shooter', and I do use that word in the least possible sense, to ME for counsel.' The old man cackled with laughter and delight, clinging to the edge of his writing table as he rocked back and forth in his chair. Caught in the delirium of a wheezing fit of mirth, his pale complexion almost turned a sickly pink. His enjoyment of the situation did not endear him to his guest. Hands clenched at her side, Butterfly Hamada gritted her teeth and waited for the retired British wrestler and promoter to finish laughing. In her opinion, any encounter with a sleazy promoter the likes of Big Daddy Taylor should be handled with a rear naked choke and a broken wrist, not with diplomacy and bargaining. This man was a particularly unsavory character who worked the United Kingdom as a promoter for many years, and was known for being a bastard and a cheat. He was greedy and well known for shorting his workers after an event. Butterfly itched at her tattoos, they seemed to bristle in this man's presence. Besides, his taunt had struck her a little too close to home. The young joshi wrestler had little choice but to deal with this cackling fool. He had information that she needed. She waited calmly, eyeing the man with contempt but trying to hide it. He was fat and bloated, with skinny arms and gray, sallow skin. He almost looked like a fat maggot on his chair, a fitting picture. His office was above a small gymnasium, and she could hear the sounds of a punching bag being knocked around, the smell of sweat reeking through the creaky floorboards. Where the man spent his ill-gotten money was apparent enough as his office was filled top to bottom with souvenirs, books, pictures, and trophies, all wrestling related. In one corner was a huge supply of some sort of exercise supplement and protein shake, all in their boxes. 'Bloke-phlex' or something like that. Finally the old man wound down to a chuckle and wiped his streaming eyes. 'Sit down, please.' He motioned to her. 'Have a seat so we can talk shop, young lady.' Butterfly resented to inference. He had been a wrestler once too, a long time ago, but she was nothing like him. She perched on the edge of the seat offered to her and said calmly with little emotion, 'You received our communication, I presume. I trust you understand the situation.' 'More or less.' The man raised a shaggy eyebrow. 'Something about money owed and promises broken? Seems like a lot of trouble to send someone this far.' 'I was in the neighborhood. And money owed, I would think, is enough for you as a reason to seek someone out.' 'Listen toots, I know all about CYDONIA Enterprises. They are just like me, eh? Bunch of blokes who get a cut of your money, get a cut of the gate, and will pull all their workers from a show if they don't get what they want, right luv? So what is a few measly American dollars to a big talent agency like that?' Butterfly showed zero interest in the details, only in the mission as a whole. 'Man doesn't pay his bills, others start to believe they can do the same. This man broke promises, no-showed cities, and run up a huge credit card bill at numerous hotels under the companies accounts. Plus two possible counts of sexual misconduct and assaulting a police officer in America. But none of this should interest you.' 'So? These things happen. It's the wrestling business.' The man shrugged. His attitude raised Butterfly's temper near the edge. Money was money. But sexual assault was another matter. This business was rife with sexual predators. She had seen enough of it in her country of Japan. What she had seen in her brief tour in AWC made her even more sick. She had come to America looking to escape the sexist garbage that was ingrained in her people back home. In America, it was just as bad but in a different way. Women were just as bad. Women used sex as their own weapon. And the men were flat-out pigs. Jack 'The Bull' Murphy, the AWC's new Livewire Champion, had made it very clear he felt women didn't belong inside the ring. He had his coming, in spades, thought Butterfly. And last week, it was Chainz turn. She had heard that he was a sleazy bastard and a scumbag, an adulterer as well. She almost salivated at the thought of taking him down a peg or two. But he beat her, further flaming the embers of her hatred for all these narrow-minded chauvinist dirtbags. 'Of course, sexual assault is a trivial matter to you,' said Butterfly. 'Maybe not to others with a reputation to protect.' The old veteran grinned and leaned back in his chair. He tapped a jaunty rhythm on the desk with his knotted knuckles. 'Of course. So CYDONIA sends a shooter half way around the world to jolly old England to politely ask this person to pay back his money and return to the United States, face police and sexual assault charges, and maybe stay for a cup of coffee and crumpets. Is that how this is supposed to work?' 'I don't drink coffee,' Butterfly said with a touch of grim humor, 'But you have the basic idea.' 'Aha. Now with the formalities out of the way, why don't you tell me what you are really planning.' 'Payment of moneys owed.' Another chuckle grated from the old man. 'Stubborn wench, aren't you? All right, we'll play it your way. What unlucky bastard stiffed your bosses?' Butterfly resisted for a long moment before answering. There were rumors there was bad blood between this man and her prey. She was advised her informant would relish an opportunity to even the score. Selling out a former comrade seemed inconceivable to her, yet she knew that in the wrestling business it was fairly routine practice. In fact, this man before her looked like he would sell out his own mother for a dollar. Japanese honor in the business didn't seem to extend beyond the Pacific. But she was brought in for a purpose, one she kept hidden from her new friends in the AWC. She was a hooker, a shooter, and she could hurt people... permanently. 'Well?' he prompted. 'Ron Von Dumpling,' she said grudgingly. The British man let out a low whistle. 'Now I see what's what. We used to run together some back when we first started out. If ever a man needed his career ended, it's good old Von Dumpling. Nasty piece of filth. And that's saying allot coming from me,' he said with a perverse smile. The old wrestler reflected for a moment on the pleasant prospect of his former friend's crippling before he concluded with a touch of regret, 'Still and all, I don't suppose breaking Von Dumpling's neck is worth getting your head split open.' 'I don't intend anything of the sort. I have been instructed to get his past accounts settled, no more.' The sarcastic look that the man threw Butterfly clearly stated he didn't believe a word of it. 'Von Dumpling is a well-known pig and a louse. I bet he had allot of fun in America with your bosses money before he left. I remember when we first broke in together in Liverpool throwing drunks out of--' Butterfly raised her hand to cut him off, before he could travel too deeply into the swamp of his past memories. 'You were going to sell me some information.' 'I'll sell anything for the right price.' Butterfly took the cue. She lifted a briefcase up to the desk. In her business suit and tie she looked like a lawyer or accountant on their way to a meeting. Opening the briefcase, she pulled out an envelope stuffed with US currency and tossed it at Big Daddy Taylor. He flipped through it and with incredible accuracy counted the bills. 'This is about half the agreed upon price,' he noted. 'It's exactly half,' she told him. 'You'll get the rest upon the completion of my mission. If you information is good, of course.' 'Completion? You want to go up against Ron Von Dumpling, a little girl like you, and expect me to take the risk that you're up to task?' His head tilted in a sarcastic manner. 'Win or lose, I want my money. Live or die, I want the other half of this envelope when it's all over, regardless if you broke all his fingers or he ate your spleen for breakfast.' 'If I agree, what will stop you from contacting your old mate and warning him?' Butterfly shook her head. 'No, this is the deal. Live with it or I'll go elsewhere.' The man considered this, then shrugged. 'All right. There isn't much call for Ron Von Dumpling around these parts, I'll get what I can for what I know. Let's get down to work.' A map? Butterfly leaned in closer for a better look, taking care to look impassive. Any sign of excitement might jack up the price of the information. She had not expected to see a map. Her secret elation grew as the man talked. She could see why he commanded such high fees. Carefully he detailed the basic foundation of Von Dumpling's estate, his comings and goings, his alarm system, and his security. Von Dumpling, it seemed, had done well in his career. He had a large mansion, well secured. And when he left it, he was protected by bodyguards and security. Only inside his hole could the fat man be approached alone, and even then it would be difficult. As if he read her mind, the informant stopped talking and looked up at her. 'Here's your first big problem. The alarm. Everything is secured, doors, windows. People are covering the gate, walking along the narrow paths of the garden. You'd think Von Dumpling was a Beatle the way he protects himself. Bloody George Harrison or some shit like that. You'd have to be a God damn ninja to break into the place, I'd think. Are you a ninja? I heard a ninja was once eating at a restaurant and a man dropped his spoon. The ninja flipped out and killed the entire town.' He smirked at her. 'No, I am not a ninja,' Butterfly looked at him sternly. 'If I were, I would have cut your head off by now, no?' Big Daddy leaned back in his chair and took a good long look at Butterfly. Twenty six years had not laid a finger on her beauty or her features. She looked sixteen years old at best, with her soft skin and angular feature, with just a hint of baby fat that made the dangerous fighter look as innocent and harmless as a schoolgirl. 'Hmmm. You're a Jap, yes?' he asked. 'I am Japanese, yes,' Butterfly corrected. 'Jap' was a derogatory term that she let slip by, not allowing this evil little man to get under her skin. 'Half-Jap at that, huh? Interesting. Well, half-Jap is better then none at all. I remember a young Japanese boy who came here to train a long time ago. Nasty little scrapper, I'll tell you that. I had my fun with him on more then a few occasions before sending him back to his dojo masters.' The lecherous look on his face made Butterfly squirm. She did not want to know what that meant. 'Got the battle scars to prove it too.' Butterfly hoped her stay in England would disprove her growing concern with British men and their perverse characters. Getting away from this one now was her first mission. 'Maybe after we are done here---' his voice trailed off. Butterfly felt the bile rise in her throat. 'No. I don't think so. And unless you have anything else for me, we are done.' 'How about a name of a security person who may just decide to look the other way the night you decide to ask Mr. Von Dumpling to pay his dues?' he asked lasciviously. 'You are serious?' Butterfly looked at him suspiciously. 'For the right price.' Butterfly hesitated. She had assumed some things were beyond this man's reach. Was it a trap? 'Don't trust me, huh?' He said it with ample agreement. 'Your loss, sweetheart. How DO you plan on getting into this mansion?' 'I'll take the information. One quarter of the agreed upon price then,' Hamada offered. 'One half.' 'One third,' she counteroffered. 'Agreed. I'll make the necessary phone calls, you just need to give me a date and a time an---,' he looked up at a disagreeable face. 'You're right, you're right. I wouldn't tell me, either. I give you a phone number, you make arrangements. I suppose that concludes our business here then, my dear. Unless of course, you would like to stay a while...' He let his words trail off suggestively. He handed her the phone number on a scrap of paper, name included. She didn't even bother playing into his games of suggestion. She noted this could be the name of anyone, any number. The sly look on his face told her he wasn't to be trusted. She shoved the paper in her breast pocket. He thinks he is besting me, thought Butterfly. She gave the man this little triumph. A little bit of detective work would uncover anything suspicious about this man on the inside. And if the information was good, all the better. But she didn't like who she was dealing with, regardless. This man took pride in the information he sold but felt a compulsion to steal from them as well. She was pleased with that. Such a belief would enable him to save face in light of her next move. She pulled out a letter in an envelope and handed it to Big Daddy, who took it with a look of curiosity and distrust. 'This is a letter describing our deal. My associates hold copies. If your information turns out to be wrong or you sell me out, you will pay the price for your treachery, understand?' She tossed the envelope at him. The old vet laughed, albeit uneasily. 'Your people can't touch me. They wouldn't dare.' 'Remember, I'm half-Japanese.' She leaned forward on the desk, looking Big Daddy right in his eyes. Big Daddy became titillated.'And what's the other half?' 'Maybe half-Mexican? Do you know what kind of connections I have in the wrestling business? I have wrestled all over the world. I have made many friends' The threat was a bluff but she hoped the old man would consider it anything but that. He picked up the envelope with the money, held it in his hand as if he were balancing the risk with the reward. In truth, Butterfly wasn't even sure what her other half was. It eventually became a part of her deceptive nature, and she liked that she could play different parts at opportune times. Butterfly had traveled the world wrestling everywhere, and she wasn't bragging when she mentioned it. She had learned three languages, learned different styles, made many friends, and even a few enemies. But as far as her 'connections', Butterfly was a free agent. She had toured with wrestlers from the CYDONIA company in Mexico as well as the West Coast. Feelers were sent out many times to see if she would be interesting in signing a long term contract. But they had never come to her with anything concrete enough to make her want to leave Japan and come to America. Many looked sidelong at Japanese women wrestlers in the west. Could they cope with the rigors of travel? Could they deal with the sexual overtones, the adult content of current pro wrestling trends, the drugs, the road time, the consequences? Most went back home within a year. Most would break way before then. She would be the exception, she felt. And she would do it at her own terms. The informant, Big Daddy, glanced up from his thoughts, seeing a woman before him that was confidant and secure. And this made him believe she would carry out her threat against him. 'Half-Japanese, half-Mexican, eh? That would do wonders for my new product line of sports supplements. We could do commercials for the Japanese and the Latin American market. BLOKE-PHLEX! World famous!' 'If you word is good, you might live long enough to gain some benefit from your... supplements. You look like you need it, Big Daddy. ' Not wanting to cast any ill shadows upon her employer she added, 'If I am caught because of my own mistakes, nothing will happen. You will merely lose your fee. If however your information is wrong and I am betrayed, copies will be sent to promoters across this country and into the states. Wrestlers, promoters, venues, the popcorn salesmen, they will all find out very quickly about you and exactly what you do. Mr. Von Dumpling, I am sure, will be most interested to find out you have details upon details of his life and his home. He will NOT be amused by the little transaction.' Big Daddy Taylor shook his head and chuckled, 'With a devious mind like that, you just might be able to handle old Von Dumpling after all. I must say, it's refreshing to see American blokes developing a devious streak in this business.' 'Their deviousness is equaled by Mr. Von Dumpling's avarice. No more, no less,' Butterfly said firmly. 'Whatever.' He waved a hand in dismissal. 'Don't worry about the information I've given you, missy. It's good. Go along then, and have fun breaking Von Dumpling's thumbs.' Butterfly looked at the man, but did not wish to continue the conversation. He had waved her off, better to leave him with the last word. She gathered herself and left his office with a deep sense of relief that she was gone from the sweaty huckster's lair. There was so much on her mind, she was glad this was over. As she walked around the corner from the man's office and down an alley she thought of her up-coming three-way match against Alexa Kendericks and Ellis Nash. With grim determination she pushed all thoughts of her job and her duty to the back of her mind. One by one, she thought, it would all take care of itself. The AWC would soon find out just how serious she really was, and Ron Von Dumpling would find out why Butterfly Hamada was the best shooter-for-hire in the business. Back indoors, the informant gazed at Megumi's figure long after she had left, chewing on a finger nail and thinking of naughty things that required himself and a half-Japanese, half-Mexican. Is that what she had said she was? It didn't matter. Big Daddy started planning an ad campaign for his Bloke-Phlex using hot women. Her wished Butterfly had taken up his offer. Deep in salacious thought, Big Daddy Taylor gathered the money in the envelope and some papers and reached around his chair for his coat. When he turned back around, a familiar shadow had entered the doorway of his office. The man smiled in anticipation. 'Ah, welcome back, long time no see, ' he said to the approaching figure. 'Changed your mind, did you?' The old man's invitation ended abruptly as two slender hands constricted around his throat. He fought hard to break the hold but a foot the the chest buried his fat frame deep into the leather chair. As his face turned red, he flailed about wildly. Then he turned blue and his bloodshot eyes bulged from their sockets. He slumped over the desk with his swollen tongue hanging from his lips, saliva running down his pale cheek. The intruder slowly pushed the man of the chair and onto the floor. She seated herself at his desk. She went through his paperwork and extracted some important pages of notes. She slid the papers into her jacket pocket then gathered up the rest of the pile of receipts and books and threw them in a heap into a waste basket. A second later a small fire erupted in the trash bin. The shadowy figures feet slowly strutted to the door and walked out silently as the fire began to engulf everything its' flickering fingers could touch.
