An Awesome Beginning

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/05 December 9, 2001 10:47 P.M. Eastern Standard Time “Holy shit! Chris Jericho, no way!” I myself couldn’t believe my eyes, or my ears for that matter. Every single one of the wrestling fans that had gathered at 622 Lake Blvd weren’t betting on this to happen either. In fact, out of the five of us that actually cared, two had said that Rocky would pull through, one claimed Kurt Angle and the other two, including myself, said that Stone Cold would be the first Undisputed Champion in the history of the World Wrestling Federation. But we were all wrong, as something truly surprising had transpired at a WWF, a rare and uncommon occurrence indeed. I just sat there, a bottle of Michelob in my hand, a small smirk on my face unbeknownst to my fellow alcoholics and pot heads. While the fans were vibrantly yelling at each other and the television screen, and the others that were there were either zoned out or completely blitzed, I just sat there, content as I could ever be. It was a good sign, seeing Chris Jericho defeat two of the biggest names in the industry on one night at the Vengeance Pay Per View. It gave me the confidence and the knowledge that the business which had been formerly dominated by giants was changing, and for the better as well. It was comforting to come to this realization only two days before I would be embarking on my first tour of Japan, the first career goal of mine to be fulfilled. In fact, this was the real reason that the gathering had commenced in the first place, to wish me farewell on my month long journey. Well on the top layer it might have seen that way, but I knew better that it was just going to be an excuse for my close friends and people I didn’t give a shit about to get inebriated in one form or another. The Pay Per View was just an added bonus as the guy’s house had an illegal cable box. So as the wrestling came to a close and the loud obnoxious hip hop music took its place, I took a long swig of my beer. An approaching shadow caught the corner of my right eye, and my entire head swung to the side, careful not to spill a drop of the precious amber liquid. The shadow belonged to the body of a girl named Sophie, underage perhaps, and from what I’ve seen and heard, a definite whore. She was an average sized girl, looked to be anorexic or bulimic, and had a really bad fake tan that didn’t look right with her dark complexion. Eyebrows had been tweezed to near perfection, and her eyes were light blue, which was probably the only thing that I liked about her. She stood there, her crotch region a mere five inches away from my hand that was laying prone on the arm rest of the sofa chair. One of the things about Sophie that I had noticed was that whenever she was in the mood for a good banging, a look would come over her face, her mouth would twist up ever so slightly and one of her hands would always rise up to brush away a stray strand of hair, whether it was in the way or not. I suppose it was to be some sort of seduction look, but the fact that she had just taken three massive bong hits made her once beautiful eyes hazy, changing the seduction look to one of slight mental retardation. “So…” she started, clearing her throat and nodding her head as her eyes shot downward. “…I hear you’re going to be umm, going to Japan for a month to wrestle. That’s cool…I always wanted to go to Japan.” I seriously hoped that she wasn’t asking for an invitation or to tag along. Last thing I needed over there was a high school bimbo with an eating disorder following me to arenas and with my luck, becoming a Yakuza slave girl. All I could do was to stare back at her, somewhat lost in where she was going with this, even though I knew that she was just moving down the chain. What I meant by that was that she had already either made out with or got nailed by four of my friends already, and it looked like I was next up to the plate. I watched as her eyes traveled back up and eventually locked onto mine, but I quickly interrupted the unseen pathway by taking another sip of my beer, which was starting to get warm. It gave me an idea. “Hey,” I seemed to startle her with my voice raising up above most of the noise around us, “can you do me a favor? Can you get me another beer from my pack? It’s the twelve of Michelob, in the blue plastic bag in the kitchen.” She smiled, nodding her head a bit as she quickly scampered off, squeezing through the crowded living room and disappearing towards the kitchen. Taking this opportunity, I got up from my seat and went the other way towards the hallway and the den. It was easy pussy, but it was also tainted, and the last thing I wanted during my month long journey was a case of the clap. Besides, most of my friends were in the den. The door was closed for obvious reasons, but I knew that I was allowed inside no matter what the case was. Hell, if they said I wasn’t, I knew enough from my training to put whomever it was to sleep. Guess that was the advantage of being taught guys like Stan Lane and Dean Malenko. There were two random guys talking near the door, but they moved aside when I made a move for the doorknob and opened it tentatively. The smoke hit me like a ton of bricks, slipping through every orifice on my head and causing me to momentarily gag. I squinted in order to protect my eyes from the burning sensation that had already given me a slight headache, and saw about five people in total hanging out in the room. Two of them were Jeff and Dave, my best friends in the place, and the other three was Diana and Chris, a couple who were making out in the midst of sharing a joint, and a girl named Melissa, who might have been the hottest chick in the entire house. She was sitting on the faded brown and green checkered couch against the wall, while my two friends were hovered around the computer, going through the play list on the guy’s computer. Jeff was periodically screaming in a low, pissed off voice. I walked over, placing my hand on the back of the chair and leaning over his shoulder. “What’s your issue?” I asked as Dave took out a cigarette out and offered me one. I declined. “This fucking computer man, there’s nothing good on here. All this hip hop and rap, ‘ooh I’m a gangsta and I’m white!’ Pisses me off, where’s the Floyd man?!” God bless Jeff, who had the exact same taste in music that I did. All rock and roll, from the sixties to the mid nineties, almost none of that new crap. He was wasted though on a twelve of Busch and a few shots of Jagermeister, which only exaggerated his somewhat frequent temper tantrums. He was a high strung guy who got pissed off at the smallest of things, like the time when he thought that someone almost stepped on his shoe. That almost escalated into a bar fight. “Yo man…what the hell does brevity mean?” Dave interrupted, taking a long drag from his cigarette and blowing it towards Melissa’s face, who was near the point of passing out. He started to grin, then looked back at me, giving me a wink, followed by a head nod to the side, and yet another wink. Well, no one had ever mistook him for a master of subtlety. “I think it has to do with a short period of time…I could be wrong though.” Shrugging my shoulders at the thought of going over there and taking advantage of the poor girl. It would be my second opportunity of the night, and much like the first one, I would pass. I actually knew and liked her as a person, she was nice and cool to talk to, which made her even more attractive in my eyes. If she had awakened and become more viral, then perhaps I would pursue. After all, it was my last night in the country for a month or so, then when I got back, I would be staying for only three days before going to work for a Texas based federation. So technically, it would probably be the last time I would see her for the next few months. A commotion from the other room got my attention, and the attention of everyone in the room, sans Melissa on the couch. I could hear loud footsteps, and a voice shouting ‘party’s over’ in a loud, booming tone that could have probably been heard throughout the entire house. Dave was quick to throw his cig away towards the corner and get up from his seat to lock the door. We were in the back of the house, so I couldn’t see if there was a squad car out there or not, but by the sounds of the outside house, the cops had definitely arrived. “The back window, let’s get the fuck out of here.” He was careful not to shout so he wouldn’t draw the attention from the cops. But the smoke that was probably billowing through the bottom of the door would more than likely give it away. So without even having to think about it as if it were almost routine, I quickly moved to the window near the passed out Melissa and lifted it up. Dave was the first to get out, almost pushing me aside as he took his sweet ass time to get through the frame. Jeff hopped out next, quicker and almost diving out onto the grass. The couple consisting of Diana and Chris were the next to go while I woke up Melissa, trying to avoid her from being caught in an empty room filled with a half empty zip lock bag of cocaine and literally a dozen of empty alcohol bottles on the floor. It was an arduous task, and I could hear one of the kids outside shouting “fuck you” back at the approaching cops, which caused me to actually slap the poor girl in the face. That sure as hell got her attention, and the fact that a siren now went off outside was the only thing that had probably prevented her from giving me a good hard one back to the face. She was quick on her feet now as she was assisted through the window by the suddenly chivalrous Dave, and then I followed quickly, making sure to close the window behind me. Outside, Chris and Diana were gone, as Jeff motioned for the line of tall bushes towards the far wall in the backyard. We quickly but quietly sauntered through the uncut lawn, and a glance back to the room had confirmed that the cops had indeed arrived, and were in the process of busting through the door, while Brad, the guy whose house it was, had been dragged in by them. I didn’t like the guy, so I could careless what the consequences he would face were going to be. Dave disappeared first through the bushes and emerged in another backyard, this one being on North Hayden Street, a block all of us knew well enough to go in the right direction. It took me a few moments to realize that Jeff had taken the remains of a bottle of scotch with him. I looked at him as Melissa went through the bushes, but all he could do was shrug. “Hey man, serves that dick right for leaving his booze unguarded.” He retorted, walking past me and onto the sidewalk.” As I joined the group, it seemed as if Melissa was clutching onto Dave’s arm now. Inside, I was thinking that this bitch should be grateful I stayed by to wake her up…she’d be in a cell right now if I hadn’t. But then, I realized that it didn’t matter. None of this really did, because I would be gone to another world in a few days and I would be entirely on my own for the first time in my life. Sure I would be with a group of five other American wrestlers, but I didn’t know any of them really and they were at least five years older than I was. To them, I would probably seem like some green rookie, which at the age of twenty-one, I had convinced myself that I was most definitely not. So this is what I would be leaving behind, the near freezing midnight walks down almost empty streets, avoiding the cops two times a week regularly, with total success so far. I’d miss it in some ways, but I also knew that it was time to grow up. I was a man now, and while this may have just been a wonderful blessed experience for everyone else, for me it was a right into manhood, something that would make or break my career. If I were to be successful there, the sky would be the limit, people would start talking, and I would suddenly be in demand. I knew how it worked, I had heard all the stories. But now it was time to start my story. And as Melissa stopped in her tracks to vomit on a manhole in the middle of the street, I raised my head to the sky, looking up through the fog and catching a glimpse of the stars. I would be one of those someday, a star…Eddie Sanders would become a household name in the world of wrestling. It was time for that part of my life to begin…a smile crept up onto my face just thinking about it. View Eddie Sanders 's Biography
