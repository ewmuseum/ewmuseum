A 'Schnit's Tale (part 1)

25 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th July It’s not so long. Sorry, not been a good week =\ The tale of the creature Grobschnit and how he came into the custody of everyone’s favourite wrestling bozo Andy M. is as eventful as it is unorthodox. It is a tail of many twists and turns, a fascinating journey, and is also, quite frankly, the best damn kid’s story never published. We begin a long, loooooooong time ago. We’re talking hundreds and thousands of millions of years here, okay? Back in a time where dinosaurs roamed the earth, and tiny little single-cell organisms hadn’t even began to think of evolving into human beings. But that’s enough about the planet Earth, because baby, we’re not even on it… The planet Schnitalonia wash a lush and serene place, and was part of a single-planet galaxy orbiting a sun roughly 10,000 billion light-years from Earth. It’s climate was slightly warmer than Earth’s, and at the time the planets two chief races, the ‘Schnits and the Klinks, were far more technologically advanced than our planet is even to this day. Inter-stellar travel wasn’t a problem for these two peculiar races; in fact, it was something that they’d mastered a long, long time ago. ’Schnit Fact #1: They make humans look like dinosaurs. The Klinks, for the most part, were unwanted guests on Schnitalonia. They had landed 12 years previous after their own home planet was swallowed up by its sun, and naturally, the ‘Schnits weren’t too pleased about this. The two rival races had been at war ever since, and despite the superior numbers of the ‘Schnit population, they found themselves more often than not on the losing side of things. The fact of the matter was that the Klinks possessed a far superior size and strength to the ‘Schnits, and had been infamous in their previous galaxy for wiping out anyone that even looked at them funny. They were quick to adapt to the planet of Schnitalonia, and took refuge in it’s many jungles, even capturing a few key cities over time. If things progressed as they had been doing so, the once great civilisation of ‘Schnits would soon be wiped out. Grobschnit grew up in Schnitsville, the capital city of this great planet. He grew up under the watchful eye of his mother Anneschnit, a housewife, his father Bobschnit, a highly successful stock broker, and their adopted son Oogie Boogie; a rather odd 7’ tall big purple fuzzy thing, with the ability to only speak two words; “Oogie” and “Boogie”. As a young ‘Schnit, Grobber had dreams of joining his nation’s proud and numerous army, and putting the smackdown on the Klinks once and for all. A keen and quick learner, he was soon top of his classes at ‘Schnitschool, and became a popular “student” with his “take no shit” attitude. However, the global government of ‘Schnitalonia released at this time that they were fighting a battle that they could not win; they would soon have to flee their homeland for another planet of similar climate and hospitability. So, they handpicked 20 of the planet’s most brilliant students to be blasted into outer space, scout out their assigned planet, and report back to Schnitsville with their findings roughly a week later. Coincidentally, Grobschnit was one of the chosen students. His assignment? Earth. Grobschnit stood at the large steel doors which led into the launch pad; there was no turning back now. If something went wrong, this would be the last time that Grobber would ever see his beloved family again. But he knew what he had to do, and he accepted that; he was carrying out a mission for the greater good of his struggling people. Anneschnit, tears running down here pretty little green face, didn’t want to let her so totally awesome son go. She embraced him like a fat man embraces a bucket of KFC, probably damaging Grob’s bright organe-coloured launch suit in the process. “OMG! Eye wil mizz u soo mutch!” Anneschnit wept. For those who didn’t already know, now would be a good time to point out the fact that the ‘Schnits were incredibly poor speakers of English… or maybe it’s us that speak it badly, considering the fact that the ‘Schnits had been speaking it millions of years before the human race was even created. Oh well. Grobschnit too was close to tears, but a badass motherfucker like him never showed a sign of weakness by crying. Instead, he held his nerve, and comforted his mother. “Izz ohkay mumm, izz gona skout owt da plannette an den I come home an we all be ohkaye agane,” Grobschnit uttered reassuringly. Suddenly, a booming voice can across the small, chrome-plated room in which the family of ‘Schnits had been standing in. “LAST CALL FOR POD 12! GROBSCHNIT, PLEASE REPORT TO THE LAUNCH PAD IMMEDIATELY! WE’RE READY TO GO!” Anneschnit finally released her grip on her son, and stepped backwards towards Oogie Boogie and the tuxedo-clad Bobschnit, who too was struggling not to shed a tear or two. “Tayke kare, sun…” Bobschnit uttered in a dullest tone of voice. Grobschnit nodded… “Eye wihll, daddee” he replied. He soon turned his gaze to his adopted brother and best friend, the big purple lump of awesomeness that is Oogie Boogie. “Oogie, looke aftur da familee my brutha…” “Oogie Boogie…” he replied, sounding not totally unlike the Cookie Monster. ’Schnit Fact #2: The Cookie Monster is an Oogie Boogie ripoff. And so, that was that. Grobschnit pulled his helmet over his green head, and winked one of his black, orb-like eyes at his family, before slipping through the doors, into the launch pad. After a few days of mega-speed interstellar travel, Grobschnit’s pod soon touched down on Earth; in the part of the world now known as “Wales”; oh dear. However, Grobschnit didn’t find what he was expecting… the place was inhabited by big freakin’ dinosaurs. The tiny space-pod came to rest buried nose-first into the soft ground of a “Welsh” forest, the glass panel soon popping open. “Stoopid fagit podd,” Grobschnit muttered under his breath, as he struggled to unleash himself from the extra-tight seatbelt. Finally, he managed, and fell face-first into the soil in which his pod had came to rest. Suffice to say, Grobschnit was not best pleased about the way in which his pod had came to rest. “FAGIT!” he yelled, as he removed his face from the ground, wiping the soil from the front of his helmet. He soon leapt up to his feet, and brushed the dirt from his clothes, before taking a long, deep breath in of the fresh Earth air. ’Schnit’s could breath in any atmosphere; they were blessed with ultimo-badass lungs that also meant they could smoke roughly 1,000 cigarettes a day and not feel any bad effects. However, as Grobschnit looked around himself, he found that he was surrounded – definitely not something he had expected. A group of four Velociraptors had surrounded the Green Creature ‘O Doom, teeth out, and ready to tuck into a ‘Schnit feast. Grobschnit, knew immediately what to do, and rolled up the sleeves on his astronaut suit, clenched his fists, and shouted his war cry… “IZZ GONA KIK UR AZZ, FAGIIIIIIITZ!” A raptor swiped at Grobschnit, but the ‘Schnit was quick enough to duck the swipe, and instead, sunk his teeth into the raptor’s arm. The dinosaur let out a whelp in pain, and was soon met with a swift stiff kick to the jaw, ending it jarring backwards. Seizing the opportunity, Grob swung around, and leapt on the back of the dinosaur, locking an iron grip around its neck. Within a couple of seconds, the raptor slumped to a big, dead heap on the forest floor. “Whoz nexzt, fagitz?” Grob questioned, noticing the group of dinosaurs slowly backing away from him. “I SED WHOZ NEXZT!?” And just like that, the velociraptors all turned heel and fled the scene, knowing that they were dealing with one badass mother’schnitter. ’Schnit Fact #3: Even dinosaurs fear Da Grob. Many people believe that the dinosaurs were wiped out by a meteor strike. Well, I can confidently tell you that that is one of the biggest crocks of crap to ever be fabricated. They were wiped out by something far more powerful than any meteor could ever be… Under a night’s sky, a few years later, we found two male Triceratops’ laying next to a lake, gazing out at the moon’s shimmering reflection. They were communicating in whatever language dinosaurs used to communicate in… thank Christ, then, for dubbing! “Yo, you heard ‘bout ‘dat green creature that be shootin’ up da hood, nig?” one of the massive creatures asked the other. “No, who dat?” “Dis ‘ting, Grobschnit, he be fuckin’ up all da most crunkest dinos, dawg. He been kickin’ da asses of all da biggest playas… he even killed a T-rex, dawg!” The other dinosaurs raised an eyebrow (or whatever triceratops’ had in place of eyebrows), quite worried by the whole situation. “T-Rex!?! Gawd dayum! We gon’ go kill ourselves befo’ dat playa come get us, G…” ’Schnit Fact #4: Grobschnit wiped out the dinosaurs. That’s right; the real reason as to why the dinosaurs left the planet earth is because of the arrival of the infamous Grobschnit. After his pod malfunctioned, and Grob realised he was left stranded on this planet without a way home, he went on a world-wide killing streak, murdering every god damn dinosaur that got in his way. Those that weren’t wiped out by Grobschnit committed suicide, too scared to come across the vengeful wrath of the ‘Schnit. And so, Grob was left all alone on planet Earth, as the great Ice Age set in. His thick ‘Schnit skin helped Grob survive this inhospitable time, as he lived in a large shock constructed from the remains of tyrannosaurus rex bones. If you ever needed conclusive proof that Grobschnit is one badass motherfucker, this is it. In spite of all this, it was not until the birth of humanity that things started to get really interesting.
