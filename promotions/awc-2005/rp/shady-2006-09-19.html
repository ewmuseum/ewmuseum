Shady

19 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/19/06 FADE IN INT. OPERATING ROOM To a top down rotating view of a dimly lit operating room. A garbed up DOCTOR and his NURSES scramble across the floor desperately grabbing tools and the like off of counters and barking orders and updates back and forth to each other. The point of their obsession? A woman in labor, sprawled out on the table in the center of the ring. Unlike what you usually see on the Discovery Channel, though, this woman isn’t screaming in an un earthly pain. She’s unconscious, being forced to breathe by the plastic mask over her nose and mouth. CUT TO: A shaky pan back and forth between speakers. NURSE #1 Her vitals are dropping! DOCTOR Alright, if were going to save the child, we need to give her C-Section. NURSE #2 If we do it now, we can save the child. I don’t know about the mother. DOCTOR What’s the chance for both of them to make it? NURSE #1 30% at best. NURSE #2 If we wait to long, we’ll lose them both! DOCTOR Alright, lets proceed. CUT TO: A wide shot of the OR. Soundless, you see the doctors begin to pass sharp objects back and forth between each other. The eyes of the staff members show a furious will to save the child, but a deep sorrow for the almost inevitable fate of the mother. FADE OUT In the darkness, you hear the healthy screams of a baby and the flat line of a heart monitor blaring in concert. FADE IN INT. BSW STAFF OFFICE - EVENING The camera pans by the office door to see Dimitri sitting at his desk, working diligently on the schools paperwork. CUT TO Close shot of Dimitri from the an angled front view. Just a small desk light shows his pen to the page, and silence has befallen the building, as all the students have left. The sharp tinging of the old school doorbell breaks his concentration. DIMITRI Jesus, now what? He reaches for his cane, a silver rod with a round doorknob shape on the top and his old ring name “TRIGGER” engraved on the side, and props himself out of the chair. INT. BSW GYMNASIUM - EVENING Camera is steady in front of the front door. The bell rings again. Dimitri saunters into the shot and over to the door, the bell rings once more before he finally opens it. Close profile shot of Dimitri’s reaction, his face twisting into a strange mixture of surprise and confusion. DIMITRI There something I can do for you? CUT TO A very tight shot of the visitors lips. They are a dark purple, and his skin pale white. They are pursed, as if he was frustrated. MAN OF QUESTIONABLE MORAL FIBER’S VOICE (gracefully) Who are you? DIMITRI Id ask you the same. MAN OF QUESTIONABLE MORAL FIBER’S VOICE Are you the Clemans boy? DIMITRI What’s it to you? MAN OF QUESTIONABLE MORAL FIBER’S VOICE A lot. CUT TO A POV shot of Dimitri, giving the viewer the first shot of what he’s so confused about. Closest to Dimitri is GLUM, the smaller, pale man, clad in suit and tie. His face is feminine, and his demeanor is graceful. Behind him stands two more men, BASE, a slightly taller man, whose physique lies for all to see, as he seems to have forgotten to put a shirt on. Next to him, ZEIK, another wiry, pale man, with small rimless glasses looks like he was just dragged away from a computer screen, and his face says he was upset by it. They stand in front of a black sedan, parked conspicuously in the center of the parking lot of the school. GLUM We’d be much obliged if you came with us. DIMITRI Listen, kids. it’s a little early for trick or treating. Come back in October. GLUM Don’t make this difficult, sir. DIMITRI Three strangers show up at my door asking me to leave with them. Yea, I’m going to make it difficult. GLUM Have it your way… Glum reaches behind him, as if to grab some sort of weapon. Before he can swing it around the front though, Dimitri taps his cane against the ground, and from the top sprouts a small, spring-loaded blade. He points it at Glum’s face, as if it were a spear. Base and Zeik take a step back, not sure what to do. Glum freezes in place, looking down at the blade with caution. GLUM Now, this isn’t necessary, sir. BASE (gruffly) Don’t do anything you’ll regret, Clemans! DIMITRI What do you want from the Clemans family, shitheads? ZEIK Were just here to collect a debt that you owe my boss. DIMITRI I don’t owe your boss a damn thing. GLUM Its highly impossible that our boss could be mistaken. DIMITRI You bastards have till the count of three to get away from my door, or Skeletor here gets it. BASE Look… DIMITRI One! ZEIK Just listen a se… DIMITRI Two! GLUM Clemans, lets just stop playing games and… DIMITRI THREE! GLUM WAIT, JARRETT, WAIT! Dimitri stops a second… DIMITRI Who? GLUM You are Jarrett Clemans, correct? Zeik inches forward, squinting his eyes to get a better look at Dimitri. Dimitri swings the cane at him, and he hops backwards. ZEIK I think we might be mistaken, Glum GLUM What? ZEIK Boss gave us a profile of Jarrett Clemans, tall, muscular, short hair, strong chin, younger. BASE Now that you think about it… DIMITRI Get out of here now! Dimitri strikes Glum’s knee with the cane. Glum bounces up and down in pain. Base and Zeik pull him back into the sedan. They drive away, frazzled at their own mistake. Dimitri watches them drive away, then taps his cane against the wall on his way in, the blade receding back into the cane. FADE OUT FADE IN: INT. SOME SCOTTISH RESTAURANT - NIGHT Panning shot of a garden courtyard setting. B.O.T. sits across from a beautiful blonde, DALLAS. It appears he’s on a date, as his attire, and his body language both trying equally hard to impress his guest. And by the look on her face, it seems to be working. B.O.T. Yeah, so back in the states I’m sort of important. Kinda like the President. DALLAS (with a Scottish accent, of course) Like David Hasselhoff? B.O.T. Um.. Yeah. Just like David Hasselhoff. DALLAS Really, that’s amazing! What is it you do back in America? B.O.T. Well, plenty of things. I mean, I own a couple businesses, I used to have a radio show. Ever hear of the JBOT Show? DALLAS No… B.O.T. Well, that’s a shame, cuz its only the biggest show to ever debut on American TV ever. I’m serious, its broken like, records and shit. Trust me. DALLAS Well, why are you here then, Mr. American Big shot? B.O.T. Well that depends one where ‘here’ is. I’m in Scotland on tour. I’m here at this fine establishment babbling on about my life to a pretty lady. They share a small laugh. B.O.T. That’s enough about me, what about you? What do you do when your not drinking tea with strange American men? Almost on cue, the cell phone in his pocket blares out an obnoxious ring tone. DALLAS Well I… B.O.T. Hold that thought, sweet heart. B.O.T. swings around facing behind his chair, then draws a small black flip phone from his finely tailored pocket. He flips it open abruptly. B.O.T. Yes? The angry voice on the other end is easily identifiable. DIMITRI (V/O) What the hell have you gotten yourself into!? CUT TO INT. BSW STAFF OFFICE - EVENING Dimitri is pacing back and forth angrily. B.O.T. (V/O) The hell are you talking about? DIMITRI Three strange men just showed up the gym looking for you. B.O.T. (V/O) For what? CUT TO EXT. SCOTTISH RESTAURANT - NIGHT DIMITRI (V/O) I don’t know, something about money you owe them. B.O.T. stands up, outraged. B.O.T. (loudly, without consideration for the diners around him) What!? What money? DIMITRI (V/O) I don’t know, but the guys who came looked suspicious. Not like your usual bill collector. B.O.T. Well how much do I owe them? DIMITRI (V/O) I didn’t ask. I just wanted away from the school. DALLAS Is everything alright? B.O.T. Yes, don’t worry about it dear. CUT TO INT. BSW STAFF OFFICE - EVENING DIMITRI Who’s that? B.O.T. (V/O) A lady I met here in Scotland. I was on a date before you rudely interrupted me. DIMITRI You should be focusing on winning matches, not carousing with Scottish hookers. CUT TO EXT. SCOTTISH RESTAURANT - NIGHT B.O.T. (still loudly) I’ll have you know she is not a hooker! DALLAS What? DIMITRI (V/O) I don’t know that. She could be like one of those smutty chicks like in that movie Hostel. Just waiting to kidnap idiots like you so that some psycho can cut you into little pieces with a steak knife. B.O.T. Firstly, those chicks were Swedish. Secondly, and most importantly, so what if she’s smutty. At least I know that after sitting here listening to her bullshit for the hour & a half I might be getting something out of it! CUT TO INT. BSW STAFF OFFICE - EVENING Very slow rotation around Dimitri. DIMITRI You’re a pig! B.O.T. (V/O) Womanizer. Difference. DIMITRI Whatever. I’m concerned about the visitors I had tonight. B.O.T. (V/O) What did they look like DIMITRI Two smaller guys, one of them a pale gentlemen, the other probably the town nerd. They had some brawn with them, too. B.O.T. (V/O) Dunno… ill make some phone calls, see what’s what. DIMITRI Well, handle this please. I don’t want to be caught in something I can’t get out of. B.O.T. (V/O) Way to be worried about me, partner. DIMITRI Hey, apparently, you got yourself into this mess. And usually id let you get back out on your own, but when people show up and threaten me, I change my mind. CUT TO EXT. SCOTTISH RESTAURANT - NIGHT Tight shot of B.O.T.’s head and shoulders. B.O.T They threatened you? DIMITRI (V/O) Nothing I couldn‘t handle. B.O.T. You use the cane I bought you? DIMITRI (V/O) Yeah. B.O.T. ‘Atta boy? DIMITRI (V/O) Come on, I think this is serious. B.O.T. Well, ill take care of it. Till then, if thy come back, call the cops. I’m going back on my date, so excuse me… DIMITRI (V/O) Jarr... B.O.T. closes the phone, and slides it back in his pocket. CUT TO A wide shot of B.O.T. and his table, now empty. He looks back towards it, looking to resume what he started. Hes stunned when he doesn’t see her there. B.O.T. Shit… He sits back in his seat, angrily sipping what’s left of his tea. FADE OUT FADE IN INT. SEDAN - EVENING The camera gives us a view of the three strange men through the windshield of their black sedan. Base is at the wheel, Zeik in the passenger side, typing something into a small laptop, and Glum in the back, rubbing his knee through his freshly pressed dress pants. GLUM So that was the brother, huh? Close shot on Zeik. ZEIK Yes, Dimtri Clemans, 36. First born of Oletta Rogers and Barbossa Clemans. Fits the description perfectly. GLUM Then he should know where his brother is. ZEIK Indeed. CUT TO: Close eye level shot of Base. BASE Question? GLUM Yes, Base? BASE Couldn’t we just get the money from Dimitri? Cut back to Glum. GLUM Our orders are to bring Jarrett to see the Boss. Nothing about collecting money. BASE But isn’t what this is all about? The sons paying up for the sins of the father? GLUM We’ll leave that up for the boss to decide. Cut to the windshield shot again… ZEIK Guys, I got something… GLUM What is it? Cut to a close up of Zeik’s laptop, which has the AWC homepage featured on it. ZEIK Jarrett Clemans is currently a registered member of the professional wrestling promotion Atlantic Wrestling Club, billed under the alias “B.O.T.” They are currently touring Europe. Cut to Glum. GLUM (Visibly interested) Where in Europe? ZEIK Scotland, sir. GLUM We have to tell the boss right away. Send him a message. ZEIK Yes sir. CUT TO: And outside shot of the sedan speeding down the New Jersey Turnpike. FADE OUT FADE IN INT. PENTHOUSE SUITE - NIGHT Inside the greeting room of a large and fancy penthouse suite of some Scottish hotel, B.O.T. practices a Muay Thai kata. The camera follows his every punch and kick, as the strike imaginary enemies with immense power. DISEMBODIED WOMAN’S VOICE I’m not sure what to tell you, man. The voice permeates the room, the camera cuts to a small speaker phone on an end table, before cutting back to B.O.T. as he throws a series of high roundhouses. B.O.T. Thanks Ai, you’re a pal. AI Hey, you’re the one messing around with shady men. I have no idea what you do in your spare time. B.O.T. That’s the thing, I haven’t borrowed money from anyone! How could I possibly be in someone’s debt? AI Maybe their some sort of mafia, trying to extort us. B.O.T. A wrestling school is an odd target for extortion, don’t you think? AI Maybe, I dunno. Way I see it, the quicker you come back to the states the better. B.O.T. Maybe ill fly back to visit before next Tuesday. I don’t want those guys coming back and Deetch being the only one there again, in case they decide to get more drastic. AI I hear ya. Ill try not to let anyone hang out alone here, too. B.O.T. Yea, good call. AI How’d your first match go? B.O.T. Cake walk, as usual. AI Feeling good this week? B.O.T I’ll keep the Bottitude flowing, but I cant help be distracted. AI Don’t worry about Deetch. He can fight too, you know. B.O.T. I guess. How’s Ivan and Virgil. AI Same old, same old. B.O.T. And you? AI I’m doing alright, I guess. B.O.T. Trouble with the old lady? AI How’d you know? B.O.T. Been having a bit of trouble myself. AI Really? B.O.T. Long story. AI Well, try to stay focused, and win one for me. B.O.T. Ill do what I can. AI Night, Big guy. B.O.T Bye, bye, Bowtie. Close shot of the speaker phone as B.O.T.’s hand reaches across the shot and presses the off button. Cut to a tight eye level of B.O.T., worry on his face. B.O.T. (V/O) Stay focused? Yea right…
