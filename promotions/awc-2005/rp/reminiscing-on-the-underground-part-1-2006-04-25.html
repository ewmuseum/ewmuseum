Reminiscing on the Underground (Part 1)

25 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/25/2006 The scene opens up outside of the crowded Manchester News Arena in Manchester England where a couple thousand Atlantic Wrestling Club fans are all assembled for AWC Fan Appreciation Day that is taking place just a few days prior to the big AWC pay-per-view extravaganza, Zero to Hero on Sunday night As the camera pans around the scene an enormous mass of people can be spotted in front of all the tables where the AWC superstars are signing autographs for their loyal fans. A large cardboard cut out of former TransAtlantic Champ, Jack Murphy is spotted on the scene as a large group of fans take pictures of it in admiration and line up to take snapshots with it as well. A large balloon with Adam Dick and Ellis Nash's faces can be seen floating by overhead. Happy little kids can be seen running about the scene with Red Rock, AIMz, and Chainz action figures in their hands. Popcorn, cotton candy, hot dog, and other various food stands are lined up making insane amounts of sells. There are even a whole lot of t-shirt and AWC merchandise vendors on hand all over the scene hocking their goods to happy customers. All is well and it seems as though AWC's Fan Appreciation Day is a big hit. The camera pans around the scene past all of the smiling faces of the fans and catches sight of the table where “The Violence” Vince Jones is spotted with a black towel over his head covering his face from view as he is signing autographs for fans. The camera zooms on a young, busty brunette girl that is in the front of the line with a huge smile on her face ready to receive an autograph. Brunette girl: Well, hello there, Mr. Jones! A small click of a button is heard. Vince Jones: Prepare to enter my hell, my realm, and my game!! The brunette’s eyes light up with enthusiasm as she steps up to the table. Brunette girl: Don’t worry! I’m always prepared. She pulls down her shirt exposing the top of her monstrous breasts and leans over the table. Brunette girl: Could you sign “To Samantha” right here for me, please? Vince Jones reaches out with a pen and quickly scribbles his autograph down on her chest. She stands up and readjusts her shirt once again with a smile. Samantha: Thanks. You’re so hot. I love you. Oh, I have something for you that you might enjoy. She reaches into her purse, pulls out some paper and a pen, and scribbles something down on it real quickly. Samantha: If you’re lookin’ for a little bit of action later on tonight stop. Here’s the address and my phone number. I hope you come by and see me. She hands the slip of paper to “The Violence” Vince Jones and walks away. A small click of a button is heard once again. Vince Jones: Enter the Violence!!! A young man with a bandana on his head and wearing all-black street gear like Vince Jones steps up to other table. Guy: Yo what da dilly yo, mane? The name’s Tony. I been down with you since the beginnin’, mane. You don’t even know. My boy’s don’t be seein’ this sh*t like I do though. And that’s real. I know you the hottest thing in the game today, but they be callin’ you a busta, mane. Sh*t, I can’t wait for you to get your chance to bust some heads at Zero to Hero! That shit’s gone be hot! Nahmean? I flew all the way out here from Cali- forn-I-A to see you, mane. The small click of a button is heard once again. Vince Jones: You’ve just been silenced, b*tch! The fan nods in agreement. Tony: I know what you mean, mane. You ‘bout to light this joint on fire. And that’s real. So, you ‘bout to hook me up with this autograph or what? The fan reaches in his jacket and pulls out a Louisville Slugger, just like the one “The Violence” Jones is seen carrying around from time to time. Vince Jones takes it from him and quickly scribbles down his autograph on it and hands it back. Tony: Thanks mane. Well, you think you could put me on with these A Dubb Cee people? See, I be wrestlin’ too. I be doin’ the elbow drops, the chair shots , the leg whips , all that good shit, mane. You gotta hook me up. I’m tryin’ to blow up on this A Dubb scene. Nahmean? Tony stands there waiting for a reply and a silence comes over Vince Jones. All of a sudden he gives him a thumbs-up. Tony smiles and nods. Tony: Aiight. Holla at ya boy, mane. I’m out. Tony leaves the table and a little boy with a red cap on his head, black Vince Jones t-shirt on, pair of black jean shorts on, and black shoes steps up to the table with a huge smile on his face. AWC Fan: Uh…hello, Mr. Violence. My name is Timothy Warren and I'm your biggest fan in the whole wide world. I love all your matches. I think you're the greatest. I remember when you joined AWC Wrestling. That was really cool. I like you. I like you a lot and wish I could be just like you when I grow up. A small click of a button is heard. Vince Jones: Prepare to enter my hell, my realm, and my game! Timmy: I love it when you say that. That's one of my favorite lines. You're the coolest, man. I wanna be a wrestler just like you when I grow up. The small click is heard again. The Violence: V. Jones' be A Dubb C's One Man Dynasty! Timmy: I love that line too. Well, could you sign my photo? Timmy hands Vince Jones the photo and he quickly signs it and hands it back. Timmy: Thanks a lot. This is the greatest day of my life. The little boy turns to leave, but he suddenly turns back around. Timmy: Wait a sec. Why you got your face all covered up under that towel? You never have your face covered up. The small click is heard again. Vince Jones: Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Vince Jones starts to bang on something hidden in his sleeve. Vince Jones: Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Vince Jones continues banging whatever is in his sleeve and all of a sudden a small tape recorder which is playing a recording of Vince Jones’ ranting falls out and onto the table. Tape recorder: Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Enter The Violence! Timmy: Wait a sec! You're not The Violence! The little boy jumps up on the table and yanks the towel off The Violence's head to reveal that it's really an imposter. All of a sudden the crowd stops and turns towards the location of all the commotion and the imposter begins to smile nervously.] The Imposter Vince Jones: Hee hee...I think I'll be leaving now. Bye. The imposter Vince Jones quickly whips out an “Out to Lunch” sign, places it on the table he was sitting at and quickly flees the scene as an angry mob of fans come chasing behind him looking to beat him down for his deception. The camera fades out and slowly fades back into the interior of the Manchester News Arena where Jasmine can be seen on her face with a cocky smirk on her face as she walks down the hallways alone. Jasmine: Just a few more days and V will finally be on his way to sitting ontop of the AWC world where he belongs. Just a few short days and we hit up the big pay-per-view, Zero to Hero. (rubs her hands together in anticipation) Oh, I can't wait. Now where is V at anyways? He caused a huge commotion outside with that sit-in he hired to go sign autographs for him. Jasmine stops in her tracks and cups her chin in thought and begins looking all around the scene. Jasmine: Now where is this man hiding? I should know this stuff. Think Jasmine, think! Jasmine's expression brightens. Jasmine: Wait a minute. I know where he's at. There’s only one spot he could be right now. Jasmine makes a 180 turn and goes back down the hallway until she reaches the entrance that leads to what will be the ring area on Sunday night. Jasmine walks through the corridor and enters the large expanse of the arena area. The arena is still being worked on and being prepared for the big event, but at the moment all of the AWC ring crew have left to go on their lunch breaks. Regardless, the scene still impresses Jasmine as she looks around in amazement. Jasmine: Man, to think that we'll be walking down that aisle together at Zero to Hero and that capacity crowd. Jasmine takes in the scene a bit more, turns, nods, and spots Vince far below pacing back and forth in the middle of the ring as if he is in deep thought or maybe worried about something with Reno Banks literally throwing a fit. Jasmine makes her way down the aisle of the arena and calls out to him. Jasmine: Hey V! What are you doing? I've been lookin’ all over for you. You caused a big scene out there with that fake V. Jones signing autographs for you at the Fan Fest. What was that all about? Are you trying to get fired? Reno Banks turns towards Jasmine, folds his arms across his chest, and just shakes his head in disappointment. Reno Banks: I don't know what to say about Mr. Jones these days. Its obvious he's not in the right frame of mind right now. He should be training right now, getting prepared for the big tourney but he prefers to loaf around and pace this ring like a caged animal. What good will that do? None at all. Vince stops in his tracks, glares at Jasmine, and then glares at Reno as well. Vince sneers at the thought of their remarks and turns his back on the both of them. Vince Jones: Screw the damn fans and screw what you think is right for The Violence here, Reno! When you hop in the ring and carry ya own in it then you can holla back! Until then, shut the hell up 'bout V's preparation and shit! Your only concerns with V be the outside the ring shit! (shakes his head in disappointment) Now, you two oughta know damn well by now how V. Jones rolls! You oughta know that shit inside and out. (slowly turns back around and faces them) 'The Violence' Vince Jones been doin' what he wants, when he wants, howeva the hell he wanna for his whole damn career in this fuckin' game and that's real! V. Jones got a whole lot more shit to be worried about right now than fuckin' fans! Right now V want the both of you to leave cuz he gotta clear his head. Reno Banks: (in surprise) Clear your head? Vince Jones: Yeah! V needs some time to clear his fuckin' head around here! You didn't catch that the first time, Reno?? Reno Banks: I don't understand. What's wrong, huh? You've had more than enough time to clear your head, Mr. Jones! Jasmine: (nods) Yeah. What's wrong with you right now that you're talking like this. Vince Jones: This ain’t just any tourney V goin’ into. This a shot at an automatic title shot 'gainst King Bitch himself or whoeva in the hell is champ when the time comes. V. Jones hasn’t seen gold since 360WE shut down and he was holdin’ the damn Eternal title back then. It’s been a long time comin’ and now V already feelin’ that hunga. V wantin' to put that gold back around his waist. Jasmine: Yeah. I see, but... but most people get ready for their matches by training, not pacing the ring. Vince Jones nods in agreement with Jasmine and laughs to himself. Vince Jones: Yeah, but most people ain’t V. You know that. V know that shit and each and every damn bitch made in A Dubb Cee that wishin’ they were in V's position to possibly walk out of Zero to Hero as challenger to the champ know that shit! Now at this time V askin' you two to get the hell on! Reno Banks: But... Vince Jones: Go! Reno Banks: (nods his head in defeat) Very well then, Mr. Jones. Call us if you need us. Jasmine and Reno exit the arena as Vince Jones remains there in silence. 'Its about time those two got the hell on..' He makes his way to the corner of the ring, sits, and closes his eyes. The camera slowly fades out. {{Flashback}} The camera fades in as a slightly younger version of Vince Jones is seen walking down a lonely street at night back in New York close to 10 years ago. He takes a quick look around the vicinity to make sure no one is around. Vince begins walking down the streetlight-lit block in silence. With the exception to a few people that pass him by on the sidewalk here and there he is literally alone. “So, The Violence steps into the ring at Zero to Hero. None of these competitors worryThe Violence at all. They gonna be taken care of, but there’s something 'bout this whole Zero to Hero thing. The Violence just can’t put his finger on it, but something makes V. Jones feel anxious to get back to the ring for this night. When The Violence first said he wanted in there was a feelin’ in the air. Whateva the hell it was it reminded The Violence of things, many things, the old times when V. Jones was tryin' to make a name for himself. Guess you could say V was tryin' to go from Zero to Hero after he finally got released from The Pen and shit…” The camera quickly fades out and then fades back in as The Violence is seen approaching an entranceway that leads down to the subway station of New York. The Violence stops in his tracks, looks around the vicinity to make sure he’s not being followed and then makes his way down the stairway to the bottom. “…Yeah. The old days, the old days in the NYUFC, New York Underground Fight Club. The Violence still seems to remember those days like they were only yesterday. The NYUFC was hell on Earth, but it was The Violence’s hell, his home, nothin’ more, nothin’ less. The Violence owes his life to that place. If it hadn’t been for the NYUFC The Violence may never have been able to get a second chance at life after comin’ out The Pen.” The camera quickly fades out and fades in as we see Vince Jones sitting in the back of one a half empty subway car leaning his head against the window in silence, not making contact with anyone at all. He turns and then looks out the window at the blur of gray that is the subway tunnel. “… Lawyers talk shit, the judges talk the shit, and even the media talks their shit 'bout how when a man comes out of prison he gettin’ a second chance in life! Fiuck that! When The Violence got outta prison he thought he had finished payin’ for his crimes and the life he lived. Hell nah! That was only the beginnin’. Nobody gives a fuck about an ex-con! Nobody was tryin’ to hire a thug that had paid his time and paid his dues and debts to society behind bars…” The camera quickly fades out and fades in as a subway train slowly comes to a stop at a well lit station on the other side of the city. Vince Jones is seen the glass doors of the subway car standing there motionless. The subway train doors part and Vince Jones steps out in silence and begins walking through the station as the thoughts continue to pass through his mind. “…Nobody was tryin’ to give a man that was once part of the biggest nightmare to ever hit the streets of NYC, a second chance. Who in their right mind wanted to see a man like The Violence survive after bein’ released from The Pen? Nobody. They just like the rest of the people that V. Jones hates. They knew so much 'bout V. They think they knew how the man though. These the same people that thought they know more 'bout V. Jones than he knows about his own self. “The Violence’ Vince Jones had about two long years to think about the shit he had gotten himself in. The Violence had finally found the time to tell himself that he was done with the game, but to all of them V. Jones was still a monster that should’ve been sittin’ behind bars like the rest of his crew was. Guess it was society’s way of kickin’ a man while he down, while he can’t fight back, cuz they all knew what would go down if he was stable. They had seen it all before.” The camera quickly fades out and fades in as The Violence reaches a rusted doorway in a dark alley. The Violence slowly slips the door open and looks down to see a dark stairway. He begins to walk down the stairs as the thoughts continue to rush through his head about the past. 'For all they cared The Violence could’ve starved to death and died on the streets that he and the clique once owned and controlled with an iron grip. You hear that shit all the time on t.v. They be talkin’ ‘bout how ex- cons end up gettiin’ released to the streets only to end up behind bars once again; but what the people don’t know is that we get treated like shit on the outside when we tryin’ to be like everyone else. What’s up with the world? We zoo animals or somethin’? Guess society thinks the animals should stay behind bars foreva too, but who the real animals, us or them? Fuck if V knows. Now that V thinks about it, if it hadn’t been for the NYUFC would there be a Vince Jones in the wrestling game now? Would there? Who knows? If it hadn’t been for the NYUFC V. Jones would’ve never met…” Vince Jones is seen stopping in his tracks at that thought. He pauses there in silence for a moment slowly raises his head and looks upward. He lowers his head and continues on his way down the stairs. “The NYUFC days were some of the best days and some of the worst days …” The camera quickly fades out and fades back in as he reaches the bottom of the long, dark stairway. He reaches around above his head and manages to find a small string. He yanks down on the string and a small light flickers on dimly lighting the hallway. All over the walls you can see crude graffiti spray painted everywhere. At the end of the hallway there is a large rusty door. The Violence continues to makes his way down the hall towards the door. He reaches the doorway and takes a seat against the door and just shakes his head. “…Fighting was the name of the game in the NYUFC. If you didn’t get the job done you’d find yourself in a hospital bed the next day. You get put out of commission in the NYUFC you didn’t get paid. You didn’t get paid and you weren’t eatin’. That’s how it was in the NYUFC. The place was 'bout survival and bein’ the top dawg. You’d fight to survive. You’d survive to fight another day and keep the cash flow comin’. Each and every cat knew it. They damn well knew it and if they didn’t they’d learn the hard way. NYUFC was dog eat dog. Nobody gave a fuck 'bout nobody. The more punk asses you took out the game, the fewer guys the “Big Man” would have to pay, so more money’d be goin’ in your pockets for you to live off of. Cripple or be crippled, that’s the way the shit was played. ‘Nuff said!” The Violence is seen sitting against the door holding his head in silence. “But it’s crazy. The Violence can’t believe he’s actually here. The Violence can’t believe he’s back here at his roots, the place that made the man that the people see on t.v today, the man that the people be seein’ adverstisin’ fuckin’ sports drinks and all the other shit he be advertisin’ on t.v. This is where the rise to the top started and it’s been a hell of a long trip for damn sure…. “ Vince Jones stands to his feet and looks at the large rusty door. “It’s been a long time comin’ since V's reminisced on this place... The Violence puts his hand on the door knob, turns it, opens it and steps through the doorway as the camera slowly fades to black. To Be Continued...
