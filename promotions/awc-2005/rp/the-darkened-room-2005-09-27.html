The Darkened Room

27 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/05 9/27/2005 [James von Doom the character of the UWF is shown sitting on a black lounge chair wearing semi-casual black dress clothes. He has a little smirk on his face. He is in a somewhat darkened room.] 'Last time I was here in the AWC, it was a disappointment. I was beaten by Pierce Lavelle.' [James holds his head in shame.] 'I was beaten by a man who is British. (pause) Or Irish. (pause) Or Scottish.' [An even longer pause.] 'Or something like that. But Pierce is nothing more than a vagina...' [James looks up, intently at the camera.] 'A vagina inserted by my penis~!' [James then looks down at the ground again.] 'But the pink taco of doom was too much for my raging Mangina. I underestimated you and now, I am here at the next AWC pay-per-view...in my hometown...wrestling for the very first time in front of my home crowd...' [A long, awkward silence follows this.] 'Against FREDROCK~?' [James looks almost angry, yet confused. He has no idea why he is in a match against Fredrock of all people.] 'Some jobber and then, I'm facing my Head Writer, James von Doom the Handler.' [James smiles deviously.] 'For the first time in wrestling history, James von Doom, the man who has innovated LIKE NO OTHER!...takes on his handler.' [James nods his head.] 'Handler versus character...both having the same name...for the first time EVER in the history of wrestling.' [James brushes his hair back.] 'I know everyone will say that this match will break many barriers. No one has ever done the things that I will do in this match, a Janitor's Closet match.' [He laughs it off like it's nothing. Then, suddenly, he grows intense.] 'But then I get all of these fucking smark fans coming to me and saying that I'm destroying the wrestling business. That I am the reason that the PTC and the AWC and the UWF and wrestling as a whole is going straight down the toilet.' [He shakes his head.] 'Well, it's not me. It's not me...' [James points his finger at the camera.] 'It's YOU. The fans, the wrestlers...nobody wants to have FUN. All everyone wants to do is come on here and be a serious wrestler. The Illustrious Face-Eater tries to have fun, but in reality he's a serious wrestler. Same with the Farmer. Tries to make everyone think he's out there to have fun, but he's a serious wrestler.' [James shakes his head.] 'There's too much seriousness in wrestling. Where the hell are the clowns? Where the hell are the pirates? Where in the hell are the stupid, lame ass gimmicks?' [He motions around with a shocked look on his face.] 'What happened? Where is the bad booking and stupid characters? Where are the lame ass storylines and crappy wrestling? What happened to the wrestling business?' [He motions around again.] 'Wrestling as itself is dead. Wrestling is dead because the fun factor has been eliminated. Who's going to give you people the fun of going to seeing a wrestling show?' [He points to himself.] 'I AM.' [James sits back in his lounge chair and smiles.] 'That's my legacy. I'm going to bring FUN back to wrestling. And that's exactly what I've done. I'm brought fun back to the PTC and to the UWF and now, here to the AWC.' [James smiles.] 'You know, I can just come out here and say anything I want and nobody can do anything about it. Why? Because I'll do it anyways even if they say I can't do it.' [He looks angry.] 'Fuck all these people who tell us what to do and what not to do. Fedheads, bookers, owners, general managers, excetera, fuck them. I'll do whatever the hell I want to do in the wrestling business and do you know why? Because my name is James Fucking von Doom dammit and that stands for FUN.' [He smiles and waves.] 'Hello censors. The out of control von Doom has done it again. I've said it again. So what'ca gonna do? What are you going to do? Censor my entire promo? Tell me not to say that?' [James laughs.] 'I could come out here and take a piss on the AWC logo and you wouldn't do a damn thing. Fucking losers. Anyway, I've got to go. I have more...' [The camera pans back and James is sitting on a black lounge chair with a stripper dancing in front of him. He, of course, has a big smile on his face.] 'Important... *snickers* ...things to attend to.' [The stripper pulls James up and they walk off to one of the back rooms as the scene fades out.] Cut. End Promo.
