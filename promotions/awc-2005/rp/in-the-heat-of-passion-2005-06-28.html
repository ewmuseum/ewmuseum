In the Heat of Passion

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 June In the Heat of Passion written by Brendan S. on 28th June 2005 For AWC’s Solarized Pay-Per-View 1st July 2005 Matches: Alliance Championship Tournament and Relentless Title Gauntlet Match In the Heat of Passion – More than the Sun’s Tale After the Relentless Title match with Christopher Ashton at Fresh! Crimson O’Malec had to return to the hospital for his third visit in three weeks. Doctors and nurses began to know him without looking down at the information chart. “What did you get hit with now?” became a common question of the attractive nurses who came in to do check up. Crimson normally just grinned at his newfound popularity in the hospital in his hometown in sunny Florida, and not surprisingly everywhere else. Crimson almost always went to Florida for his extended periods in the hospital. Become stabilized first and then go home for recovery was his thinking. This is against AWC’s medical standards, but certain allowances have been made for Crimson, because of his mental stability. On the third day after the successful Relentless Title defense, Crimson was set to be released, but he was not told this as the nurses and doctors new that Jessie Marsh had planned on letting it be a surprise that he could come home to Rolling Creek Mental Institution. Crimson, however, knew that he would be going home sometime soon because he could detect his body beginning to feel normal. Normal to most people is not normal to an extreme wrestler like Crimson O’Malec. Crimson sat in his bed and stared out the window. It was raining, but Crimson did not entirely dislike the rain. He had a nurse open the window for him, so that he could smell the fresh air. The nurse said that under normal situations windows in the hospital are meant to remain closed due to the fact that it would allow germs and bacteria to enter the environment. On the contrary, the nurse also said that since it was for Crimson she could bend the rules a little bit. Hearing a sound in the doorway, Crimson broke his trance of watching the rain. He looked to the doorway and saw a familiar face, Jessie Marsh. She stood in the doorway hands behind her back and face down. Crimson could not tell that she was holding something behind her back. She kept her face to the floor; she knew that looking into Crimson’s eyes often made her crack. She wanted to see a look of surprise on his face so she attempted to avoid smiling at him and giving anything away. It worked more that she had hoped. Crimson not seeing her face took the hung head as a symbol of disappointment. He mistook her stance as the preparation for a lecture. “Don’t even start.” Crimson said with a roll of his eyes. “I know exactly what you are going to say. I’ve heard it too many times last week to begin with.” He was irritated. Long restless nights have kept him sullen during the time before his momentous occasion, the first pay-per-view. In his anger he proceeded with an overly exaggerated hardly sarcastic impression. “‘You are taking on too much.’ I don’t want to hear it. I am damn lucky to receiving opportunities like this. I have earned the Relentless Title. In a little less than a week I will most likely have one of the Alliance Titles to go along with it. I am guaranteed a shot at the Frontier Title. I couldn’t ask for more, but certainly you can. Not surprising at all — not for you anyway.” Crimson fumbled with his hands in the hospital bed and quickly finds the remote. He snapped his wrist and turned off the television. Jessie traced the outline of the tiling on the floor with her foot. Her head had still not come up. “You know it really pisses my off that you have the gall to come in here and give me hell over being successful. Everyone else in the world is congratulating me. I guess that’s just too much to ask from you. You can’t be happy for me. I guess since you aren’t getting anything out of me winning match after match that you think I shouldn’t try as hard. Why can’t you just be happy for me like everyone else? You ought to be so happy for me? We should be celebrating instead of bickering over the risks. The reason I am not in any worse condition now than what I am is because I take risks. If I weren’t a risk taker I would be in a body cast. Then maybe you could be upset for me. That would be the time to be sorry for me. I know that you regret my accomplishments as of late, but these times have been some of my proudest moments. Proudest… Do you know how long it’s been since I’ve had some pride? People know who I am. I’m a regular celebrity. My only disappointment is your lack of support.” Crimson picks up the remote again. He aggressively turns back on the television set. He quickly flips through the channels and turns it back off again. When the TV is off Jessie looks up at the blank screen. She presses her lips together and the color flushes out of them leaving them place. She lets out an audible sniffle as her head slowly bows back down towards the ground. “I don’t know what you expect me to do.” Crimson’s tone had changed to more of a plea then protest. I can’t exactly go into Mr. Harber’s office and say, ‘I don’t want you to give me any more opportunities, I think it’s too much for you to ask.’ He already knows my plate is full. Did you read this morning’s wrestling magazines? He already gave one of the drawings to some other brand new wrestler. That drawing was mine, but Harber realized that my stack was full, whatever the hell that is supposed to mean.” Crimson’s voice began to rise. Seeing that a calm tone was not changing the attitude of Jessie, he continued, “Do you see me bitching and griping? No, I understand that I am taking on a little much right now. Otherwise, I would have called AWC offices this morning and demanded that I get put into a fourth title division. I didn’t! You can check the freaking phone bill when I get out of this freaking hospital.” Crimson, again enraged, reached over to his bedside stand and grabbed a white Styrofoam cup. He swiftly swung the cup up to his mouth. He began crunching on the ice with his teeth quite loudly. He slammed the Styrofoam cup back down on the bedside stand and ice flew up out of the cup and onto the floor. Jessie’s attention goes to the ice cubes on the floor. Her face still out of Crimson’s sight, she wiped her eyes and wiped the moisture on her pants leg. “Why don’t you just get off my back? I mean come on now really. It’s my life. I choose what I want to do. I’m a grown man. There is no reason for you to give me your advice. For one, you are not a wrestler. You never have been or even will be a wrestler. So you do not know what it takes to be a wrestler. You don’t have the knowledge to understand what I am and am not capable of. The doctors here and the medical staff of the AWC have enough training to know when I am able to wrestle. I know I went against their recommendations once, but I won’t do it again unless I have to. The other week was probably not the smartest idea. Despite that, I would do it again in a heartbeat. That decision got me into the position I’m in now. One match away from guaranteeing a spot in that match for the Alliance Titles. If Paddy and I win at Fresh!burst then it doesn’t even matter what the result of those four way matches are. He and I will go to the championship match. I promise you though, if we win at Fresh!burst, I will not wrestle in the four way at Solarized. I know that three matches in one night are too much. I was willing to go through it just once to win these tag titles, but if I don’t have to I won’t. Then, if we win this match at Fresh!burst, the championship match will be a piece of cake, because we will have already beat all three teams. It will just be a rematch against a bunch of idiots we already know we can beat.” Crimson cracked his neck by turning his head to the right. He grabbed the bed controls and raised himself to a better seating position. He grabbed the pillows from behind his head and fluffed them up a little bit. He then placed them behind his back. Two bony circles began to protrude from the cheeks of Jessie visibly showing that he jaw was clenched. Her two hands were also clenched into fists and then thrown into her pockets. “Now before you say it, I’m not getting to cocky. I know that this all takes a lot of hard work and concentration. I’m not going to let it get to my head. I understand the workload I am taking on. I also understand that it requires more work and sacrifice then just doing. Preparation and practice are the key to any successful outcome. I have no intention of letting my current success get to my head. It’s a lot to deal with, but its not like I have “nine to five” job on top of this. I have all the time in the world to prepare. As soon as I get out of this damn hospital that is exactly what I intend to do.” Crimson slides his body on the sheet to where his feet are dangling off the hospital bed. His back is to Jessie. She slowly walks over to the chair beside his bed. She takes the gift that she has had behind her back and sets it on the bedside table. She takes a seat. Crimson stares out the window from his bed. “I know what this is about. I know about your little crush on me.” Crimson gets up and out of the bed. He walks over to the window and continues to look out into the dreary day. “I mean, it’s not that I am flattered, it’s just I can not go through that right now. I am too busy with my career and I am unsure of how I would react to dating someone who looks just like Jamie. I could never be sure I was in love with you and it would just be too painful. So you need to stop. I know you are jealous that I am giving more time to wrestling than to you. I know you are upset that my devotion is to wrestling and that I am not devoted to you. And most of all, you are devastated that I have put my heart in to wrestling and that it has not been given to you. But, you need to get over it.” Crimson turns from the window to look at Jessie. She sits in the chair face in her hands sobbing uncontrollably. Crimson realizes how damaging his condescending speech has been. He goes over to her and puts his hand on her shoulder. He stoops down to a knee and looks into her eyes. “Another thing is,” Crimson pauses, either for effect or to gather his own composure. His tone is different yet again. A softer, gentle voice shows Crimson’s concern for Jessie. “You aren’t my mother. You may be the closest thing I have to family, but you are younger than I am. If anything you are more of the inexperienced little sister. I can’t have you try to be something you are not. We are both just going to get hurt in the end if you continue to act like a concerned family member. I realize that you are concerned and believe me I appreciate it more than you can imagine. The truth is however, that I can take care of myself. I am an adult, I know what is best for me. You don’t. Now pick yourself up and dry your tears, because I am not something worth crying about.” Crimson grabs Jessie a tissue. She dries her eyes and stands up. She begins to speak. Her voice is calm and relaxed you would not have guessed she had just been crying. “I came to pick you up and take you home, but I guess that you are grown up enough to call a taxi.” With that Jessie walks out the door. Crimson goes to stop her, but stops himself. He catches a glimpse of the wrapped gift on his bedside stand. He goes over to opponent. Inside is a frames picture. It is a picture of Jessie and Crimson in a bar. Crimson has his Relentless Title in one hand and his other hand is on Jessie’s waist. They are both close together and smiling. Crimson sits back on the bed and stares into the picture. “Oh Jessie…” he says. He begins to talk to himself, now the you starts to seem to be himself and not Jessie. “You can’t ask me to give this up or to give any less than I am now. I feel better than I have in a long, long time. I haven’t felt this good about life in general since before your sister died. Mentally I feel so much better than when I was buried inside myself at Rolling Creek. Maybe physically I’m not as well, but I’m eating, sleeping, doing everything I wasn’t after Jamie died. I even had that courage to go and see her. I could actually sit there at her gravesite and talk to her. I mean I know it sounds a little crazy, but everyone does it. I have finally come to peace with myself. I have finally come to peace with what happened. I don’t think about it everyday anymore. The flashbacks of Dad’s fall, or of Mrs. Jenkin’s funeral, or of Mom or even Jamie’s diagnosis don’t happen everyday. I mean, there bound to occur and bound to be memories stuck inside my head forever, but I don’t need to think about it everyday. So maybe I am burying myself in this work, but burying my self in my work is a thousand times better then burying myself deep inside myself. It took long enough for me to return from the world of silence and if this helps I am not going to give it up. I will not, I can not. Championships, Weapons Matches, Alliance Matches, wrestling period. It’s what keeps me alive. I am going to do everything in my power to continue to do it. I am going to take every step with determination as I go down to the ring. I am going to continue to win and continue to achieve every plateau there is in the AWC. I am going to make everyone I wrestle famous and in return give myself the right to go straight to the top of AWC. The next pay-per-view is Solarized a sun inspired event. Well, AWC’s brightest and most powerful star, similar to the sun, will rise and break daylight over his reign and when the sun sets on Solarized, he will rise again, still victorious. “
