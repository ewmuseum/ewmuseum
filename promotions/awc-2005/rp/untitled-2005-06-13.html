Untitled

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The weathered and tattered Adidas sports bag suspended from his right shoulder (by a frayed black strap) rocked slowly as Layne apathetically climbed the last set of wooden stairs to oppose him that evening. In his right hand he held the key to his newly acquired apartment, which he rolled and twisted between his fingers. --- The overly humid atmosphere was as equal a burden as the unwelcome yet familiar pain that coursed up and down his fatigued body. His mask had become a darker shade of grey since he had received that unexpected harsh right hook to his brow, and unrelenting torrent of knees to his hidden face. The blood that stained his mask also crawled out from beneath it, unafraid of exposing it's self, and descended in thin lines down his veined neck. Blood, which mixed with the profuse leakage of perspiration and drew echoes of satisifaction from the thirsty rabble. 'Kill him!' They chanted in unison. 'Kill him!' --- Reaching the summit of the steep stairway, at long last, Layne examined the corridor now before him. Much like the preceding corridors (it's dull, undecorated siblings), the corridor in which Layne occupied had bare walls and a dust-ridden hardwood floor adorned only by a single unimpressive rug. Layne frowned. --- How ironic it was that the vociferous crowd, whom had unanimously supported Layne only minutes ago as he mounted his floored foe and pummeled mercilessly at their forearm guarded visage, were suddenly encouraging the bloody-faced man they had wanted Layne to murder. Their hearts know no compassion. He had thought as he utilized the criss-crossing mesh to gradually drag up his bruised body. Just like you. Although his vision was impaired and ears assaulted by an aggravating. monotone ring, Layne was well aware that the man they called 'hatchet' approached; a victorious grin hidden behind a veil of caked crimson. 'Kill him!' They chanted in unison. 'Kill him!' --- Having drew a torn piece of paper from the back pocket of his scruffy Levis jeans, Layne confirmed to him self that he was situated outside of the correct room and all that awaited was the quadrant turn of the key he bounced on the palm of his hand. --- Unfortunately for the brute nicknamed 'Hatchet', Layne, as well as anticipating his presence, had expected his reckless attack. Layne rolled against the cage wall to avoid the incoming straight-arm from his enemy, and listened in fulfillment as a scream of suffering tore through gritted teeth upon collision of fist and tight wire. Layne recalled the first and only time he had made the naive mistake of heedlessly sending his own fist at an opponent whose matured intuition had instinctively pulled them out of the way. The wire had sank deeply into his skin and tore it away. His own agonised howl had haunted his dreams for nights thereafter. Fuelled by sudden anger, 'Hatchet' had removed his focus from his lacerated hand and turned to Layne, his eyes widened with malevolent, unrighteous intent. He had released a primal growl prior to pouncing. 'Kill him!' They chanted in unison. 'Kill him!' --- Click. It had taken a slight struggle, but ultimately, the key turned and unlocked the door. Layne did not hesitate for a moment of optimistic anticipation to sink in and postpone the moment for a delightful few seconds. He had taken the apartment without asking to view it simply because he had been through the process of renting a place so many times before, and the end result was always the same. --- Being equally as reckless as he had moments ago, 'Hatchet' charged - a tightly balled fist already reared back. Layne avoided that tightly balled fist by ducking under it. However, that was not his only action. Immediately subsequent to stepping under the forceful hook, Layne directed a perfectly targetted kick at the knee joint, and as the 'Hatchet''s leg buckled underneath him, Layne grasped hold of his bald, sweaty skull and drove it into the wired mesh. The several front-row crowd-members outside of the cage who had watched intently as the visage of 'Hatchet' was propelled into the mesh found themselves splattered with blood as they cringed. Layne imagined, as he released his grip on the head of his opposition, that he had just scarred the man for life, and allowed the bubbling sense of triumph to inflate inside of him. Groaning, James Henry Robinson - known more commonly in the underground as 'Hatchet' - pulled his face from the mesh and guided his taped fingers across the lesions in his countenance. A silent tear fell as he turned and was met promptly with a roundhouse kick to his temple. His head jerked horridly to the side before he unconciouslessly slumped against the cage wall and then collapsed on the blood/sweat-stained floor. Layne fell forward to his knees and failed to hold off the nausea. Whilst the crowd cheered his name, vomit escaped his lips and puddled in front of him. --- Subsequent to twisting the metal handle, Layne shoved open the door. He didn't even acknowledge his new environment as he staggered into the room, dropped his bag to the floor and fell upon the bed. View Void 's Biography
