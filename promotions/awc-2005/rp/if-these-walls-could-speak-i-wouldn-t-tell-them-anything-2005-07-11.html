if these walls could speak, I wouldn't tell them anything

11 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Irrelevent It’s too hard to sit here and listen to Franky rattle on about the qualms of life when I have my own problems to deal with. My old man would say I have no appreciation for the classics, but having a discerning opinion of a man whom many consider a legend is classified to me as preference. “You like Sinatra?” she asked. The way my eyes dart around like a motor function, I noticed her. I actually noticed her noticing my eyes darting between her and the jukebox in the corner. I knew she was going to ask me that. ”I know you know, but sometimes it’s fun to annoy you.” The way she says it, and the smugness of her smile, it’s enough to really chap my hide. My frustration doesn’t come with the fact that she understands every aspect of my life. It comes when she acts like she doesn’t. And tries to make conversation. By annoying me. By interrupting my train-of-thought. ”It just gets so boring sitting here all quiet with you scratching your head all the time.” She says stretching out her legs so much that she has sunken feet below, across from me in her seat. ”What if I just shot my nose off?” I said as quickly as the thought came to me. Her eyes narrowed as if she were worried, and just as quickly ignored the comment through picking up where she left off. ”If you said all the random shit that ran through your head all the time I don’t think I’d be bored, but I’d definitely be freaked out.” It’s at the second bridge of “That’s Life” where my conscious rolls back to my complete loyalty for Dean Martin and I jump to my feet, quickly approaching the jukebox on the far end of the barren bar. “Keep it gangster, @&%$, always in front of a lady.” I whispered to myself, and I heard her let out a slight giggle from behind my back as I stepped away. This whole ‘keeping secrets’—thing, and how I’m not able to do it, is starting to wear me thin. And it’s not like I’m expecting everyone to understand right away, because it’s too hard to. That’s sort of why I’m on the run, right now. But in time, I figure that everyone will realize my course of action, and why I say I can’t keep secrets. I just hope you don’t find me crazy by the end. I scan the library on the jukebox by pressing the arrows on the front pad, scanning over each heap of country, western, southern, bluegrass and whatever bullshit that is rightfully classified in the “Double-Wide Variety”. It’s really hard to find something that, in my eyes, would be considered a ‘classic’, but that’s coming from the guy who thinks Dean-O puts Franky to shame. What it really comes down to is the lady; even though she knows my game-plan from Point A, it’s all about execution. It’s like I live to impress her, or not let her down, or some expectation equally ridiculous. Like clockwork, or some bullshit cleaning job; there’s a new duty on the checklist that must be fulfilled. Her checklist. And now it just got ludicro- OOOOH, the MO’ FUCKING TEMPTATIONS. Ahem, where were we? Oh yeah, the ‘ludicrous’ part. After ditching my last job for three months, ignoring all contractual-slash-legal obligations, and severing contact with any one with whom I shared anything that bares resemblance to a—uh, what do you call those again? Oh, right—relationship, my love has decided it’s time for me to go back in to the spotlight. Selecting E-6 on the jukebox, and E-3 for my second play, I turned back towards the back of the bar and walked to our private booth, which I share with the catalyst of my whirl-wind life. Before the robotic arm even begins to thumb through the catalogue of records, she raises her thumb, and even with her back towards me I can tell she has a smile on her face. Now that my eyes are darting around, classifying the desolate bar as an emotionally secure place for me at the moment, total realization strikes. It’s as if a thought were an itch, and you entertained that thought so much or you scratched that itch to your ultimate satisfaction. In other words, I’ve had an epiphany. This is the exact moment I remember, not being noticed by the two bartenders working frivolously to close the shop (too frivolously to notice me walk across the bar) as I stared them down, where my complete of this acceptance of this woman take place. It’s like when you look at a woman play with children. The kid’s are not hers, to say, but say they are her nieces and nephews. You watch they way they handle themselves, with grace and unconditional care, and it sort of strikes you off guard; that thought of “I’m going to spend the rest of my life with her.” I’m not so much a fag that I get a chub when I see bitches play with little kids, No, I’m not that creepy. This came in regular fashion for me; THINKING TOO GOD DAMNED MUCH. I put my complete faith in this chick at this moment, when this thought occurred- She knows everything about me. She knows what I’m going to say, before I even say it. She can tell me what I’m craving before I’ve even before I get the munchies. She can even steal my good ideas, only because she has them before me. The point is? The fucking point is that she doesn’t do all this. She has the resources to be the ultimate smart ass and the funny thing is, is that it’s in her nature to do so. And there’s no real consent to keep me happy at this point; by now it’s all clear that I may as well be labeled as her property. I try not to think loud, she may here. ”WHEN THE NIGHT!” the jukebox begins to bellow the dry beat of “Stand by Me” by the Temptations. As I sit across from her, catching her head sway from left to right as if one direction were the bass drum, and the other were the snare. Her appreciation for music was what kept me interested, as it has been hard to find someone with that same passion for song. I’ve even tried to write lyrics before. ”But it turned out like crappy teen poetry.” ”Shut up,” I mutter, looking her dead in her ghastly eyes. ”Have you ever tried to not be apart of the scene? I make fun of you for being ‘emo’ because it is so utterly ridiculous and you really need to change your appearance. Especially for this big return we have planned for you, to the business.” Already acting like my manager. Hell, what needs to be changed about my appearance? ”For starters…. Have you ever thought about wearing a mask?” T/B/C///////
