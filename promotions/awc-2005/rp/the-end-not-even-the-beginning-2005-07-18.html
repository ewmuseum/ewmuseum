The End? Not even the Beginning

18 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/07/05 I don't know how much time had passed, or if any time had passed at all. That may sound crazy, but time did in fact seemed to have stopped...I would say I gained this feeling about five minutes ago, but trapped in an experience where every minute feels like a year, I can't really be sure if my timing would be accurate. The smell of death still lingers around me but somehow in the time I have been here, my nose seems to have trained itself to become used to the stench. I'm thankful for that, although I'm not thankful for what occured at around the time I noticed this - The lightbulb in this room had gone out, leaving me in pitch darkness. Not even the shadows could keep me company now....All I had left was my own thoughts. Due to the aforementioned time issue, I don't remember when I started narrating the situation within my own mind, like an author writing out his own autobiography.....maybe I'd always done it but only just noticed. John: You've got to get out of here..... I mumble to myself, as I tilt my head forward and rest it on my knees, which are near my head anyway due to the fact I am sitting on the floor and this keeps every part of my body from touching the surrounding company. Company I like to imagine is not there, a task that is not difficult, thanks to the darkness. For the third time now since I sunk to the floor, I imagine footsteps from the other side of the door behind me. I raise my head from my knees and my eyes open instantly, despite the fact I cannot actually see anything. I realise I have just imagined it again and tilt my head forward again, this time into my hands. The blood has mostly dried, but the wound is still open and still hurting beyond any form of pain I have ever felt before. Now and then it lets out some blood....I feel weak due to the blood loss, but not fatally. At least, I hope not....I begin remembering back to the rooftop. Chris' face as he looked up at my with wide eyes....knowing I will be the last thing he sees as he falls down to the street below. John: NOOOO!! I shout loudly, snapping my head back hard, as for a second I slip too faer into the memory....like I'm reliving the experience, only instead of changing things...I just experience the same response....I snapped back a little too hard, perhaps....as the back of my skull cracks hard against the wood of the door. I groan in a mixture of surprise and pain at the same time. At least it takes my mind off the pain in my hands for a second and had the original desired effect....Taking my mind away from the memories of the begging in Chris' eyes....begging me to save him, even though he knew there was nothing I could do at that moment. I rub the back of my head, again reminding myself of the pain in my hands. I feel some liquid pour from it into my hair, I guess it chose now to release a little more blood. Given all the damage and rotting wood in this place that i have seen so far....I find myself asking how the door can be so solid. I don't have time to ponder this question though.... *Sound of loud footsteps* At first I think it's just me imagining it again and don't even partake in a response this time, but then I hear it again....and again....and again. Getting closer and closer still....Then they stop and I hear some things seemingly getting dropped around various parts of the room....Then the footsteps get further and further away again, until silence returns. I count the seconds now.....reaching the number fifty-seven when I hear the footsteps again, then more things getting dropped against the floor. John: Gabriel? I mumble to myself, wondering if it's him...but then realise that it couldn't possibly be anyone else....I begin to wonder what the hell he is doing out there next, as I hear further items getting dropped onto the floor and in some cases dragged around. Some sound heavy, some sound light....but I have no idea what they are yet. I think I'm about to find out though as the footsteps can be heard again suddenly and sound as though they are heading in my direction....Getting closer and louder with every passing second and then suddenly the door unlocks and gets pulled hard open. I close my eyes, not wanting to see what lies around me....forgetting that I am leaning against the door. It falls away from me and I fall backwards and head my head again, this time on the floor. The force of impact forces me to open my eyes and see Gabriel standing over me. he is now wearing dry clothes, but they look pretty much the same as what he was wearing earlier. His hair is also dry and flows down around his head. He looks ominous, glaring down at me. I start to breath heavy with fear.....he looks like someone whon has some serious business to deal with....as opposed to his blank and calm expressions from earlier. Gabriel: Get up mortal....The time for fun, games and messing about is over. I was right, i think to myself, before noticing the sudden pain in my eyes caused by the sun shining through the hole in the wall. this hits me as i turn onto my stomach and lift myself onto all fours....I push myself to my knees now, as the pain in my hands is unbearable. I use one of them to shield my eyes, looking towards the hole in the wall as my eyes adjust to the sun pouring through it. I see a clear sky, no clouds at all.....I see birds flying around it, but no sounds can be heard....not even cars or people....I wonder how far we are from civilisation....but find myself asking a more pressing question. John: What time is it? I look up towards Gabriel now, still shielding my eyes as I do so....I look into his eyes and for the first time since encountering him, I can see them clearly....I can see pain, suffering within.....except it's not the usual kind, It looks twisted with hate and bitterness. I then notice the scar running through his right eye....I don't care much how he came to get it though, I just want an answer to my question. Gabriel: It is time you got up.... Not the answer i was looking for, but I don't dwell on that as Gabriel grabs my by my top and forces me to my feet, the fabric digging into my skin. I become even more scared now, as Gabriel forces me to stand an inch or so from his face, I force my eyes to stare into his own, even though i juast want to look away....Even the sun, as blinding as it is right now is better to gaze upon than Gabriel's cold, dark eyes....than the signs of life within, or lack thereof. Gabriel: Stop asking pointless questions..... Gabriel speaks loudly into my face, this next sentence before dragging me hard across the room....forcing my legs to move, although they just feel like jell-o and want to give way under the weight of the rest of my body. He then swings his arm, throwing my forward, I turn as he does so and land hard in a seated position on a wooden chair. I don't remember this from last night and assume it was one of the items he bought with him....He just stands there looking at me for a second.... Gabriel: ...and allow everything to finally begin. I get a little worried by the smirk that appears on his face, as I've seen it far too often since this all started....and I am right to be so, as out of nowhere, he junps forward. I leap slightly off the chair in shock and fear. He just pushes me straight back down though and grabs my right hand. He holds it to the arm of the chair, putting pressure on the wound and forcing out more blood....I scream, as I feel him wrapping something around it. I look down and see it's rope...he uses it to tie my to the arm of the chair and then repeats the process with my other hand, before backing away smirking still..... John: Why did you do that? I'm not likely going to go anywhere.....am I? ......What do you want from me? I begin to go back to childhood memories. I don't know why it hits me just then, but I start to feel the most alone and petrified than I have done in a long time. For some reason, even what happened on the rooftop didn't created these feelings. I just want to go home, but I know I never will......I can just feel it. This feeling produces tears in my eyes, only one falls from them though and runs down my cheek. Gabriel grabs my face at the chin, in his right hand and forces it backwards, again forcing me to look at him. Gabriel: You continue to ask pointless questions....You are here because it's your destiny....what more do you need to know? I go to splutter out an answer, but he doesn't allow it....he senses I was about to and tightens his grip on my face, forcing my cheeks into my face. He then continues talking.... Gabriel: Nothing, that's what else you need to know....Nothing, that's all you are in this world....especially compared to higher beings such as myself. I was willing to give you all the answers you seeked and possibly had towards all of this....but you wasted them on useless enquiries. So from now, you only have one question today....and from here on....you only have questions, when I tell you to.....You understand that? Gabriel pulls his hand away from my face hard now, I can't see but I can safely assuming I have red marks on my face where he was squeezing me so tightly, I thought my cheek bones were going to crush under his grip. I wouldn't even be surprised if he left finger marks. I go to answer, but then stop myself...opting instead to answer his own question with a weak nod....he accepts this and turns to his left, my right and walks across the room slowly....I swallow hard and turn my head to watch him, I avoid looking at the burnt body in this room....but still catch a quick glimpse of it. Gabriel stops by a metal barrel, that rests in the corner of the room. He stares into it and i can see he is doing something, but can't see what. Gabriel: So come on, what is your question? Gabriel says, suddenly....catching me by surprise....I think for a second, but the fear inside me allows for no more....I just blurt out the first thing that comes into my mind, as soon as it comes. John: What is all this about? I ask, the second I do...my mind fills with about a million other more important questions in my opinion that I could ask, but I know I can't now and after a slight pause, i carry on this line of questioning....maybe not wise, but I can't help it. If anything, it's stalling him for whatever he has in mind. John: I mean, you're a professional wrestler, I've known of you for years and years.....I remember back when you were in XHW! You were known as Gabriel Reynolds… I notice Gabriel seem to visibly flinch at this.....Clearly something he doesn't like to remember. John: You have a dream life.....You are idolised by millions, I'm sure....You have money. Yet you live here, in a rundown building, You inflict pain on people you don't even know like me and Chris. You seem more like those people you see in horror films and the psychopaths you see on TV that are being hunted by the police, rather than a professional wrestler. Gabriel continues not to look at me, not even after I finish.....He continues to look down at the barrel, silently.....a few seconds pass, probably the longest few seconds of my life. My heart for some reason begins to pound, so much so, I am almost relieved when he speaks and it relaxes slightly. Gabriel: It's not about what I do in the eyes of the public, or where I do these things. It's about who I am.....and it's for that very reason, amongst others that I am doing what I am today....What I did last night. Yes, I am a professional wrestler, yes I do inflict pain on people and get paid for it....but it's nowhere near enough. Not to satisfy the cravings inside me, the lust burning in the pit of my soul....It's not enough to show people the new me and make them realise. To some, I will always be Gabriel Reynolds. The hapless, happy go lucky fool from all those years ago....Now more than ever, that's all people will see me as. Gabriel pauses....I can tell for some reason that is was to smirk slightly. Gabriel: Little to those fools know, I am not the same person I was back then.....I'm not even a person, I realised what I was on a dark and stormy night last year. I am something created in the far recesses of the heavens.....but when I was created, even god himself paled into comparison to his creation...but it was too late, I was complete, I was here....and I could not be stopped. It just took me a while to realise for what purpose I was created for.....To inflict pain, suffering and unimaginable torment on the world, I am the almighty lords biggest regret, I am Lucifer's greatest idol....I am The Angel of Death...... I don't know what to think at hearing this. Apart from the fact I am staring into the back of the head of a madman, one truly insane individual....The feeling of being alone and knowing I am not going to see home again, comes back full force. Another tear rolls down my face and my breathing escalates as I begin to think about all the people i will never see again. Gabriel: ....I am that which cannot be prepared for, I am that which cannot be stopped.....I've already conquered one plain of battle, and now it's onto the next, with many victims to make suffer. New people to enlighten and show the way.....Some of which were lucky enough to escape the First Day of Reckoning. Not this time though, none shall escape me when there time calls for my unmerciful wrath.....That goes for everyone in the world and those in Atlantic Wrestling Club. They don't yet know what's in store for them, right now, they are still underestimating me....Men like Drake Coleman and I'm sure.....The one I face this week, Andy M. Gabriel Reynolds is dead.....All that remains is me, and you are going to help people see that, and i don't just mean Andy.....That, is what this is all about!! Gabriel turns around now, revealing I was right, he does have a smirk spread right across his face. My mind wonders how I could possibly help him, but then I noticed what he pulled from the barrel....a long metal poker. The end of it is bright red, glowing in the sunlight....I look behind him, my breathing getting erratic with fear to see flames and smoke bellowing out of it. He begins walking towards me.....Taking slow and ominous steps.....My eyes grow wide and my breathing speeds up.... Gabriel: But first, let's take care of those wounds..... Gabriel says, as he reaches me and grabs my right hand....forcing the palm open, he holds it as such and begins lowering the hot end of the poker towards it. My breathing at maximum now, I try to struggle but it's pointless. I have no escape.....I only have one thing to do....and that's scream, and scream I do as the heat hits my flesh with a hiss!! Some hours later.......!! The scene opens, fading in from black. Gabriel can be seen finishing the dressing, of the wounds which he inflicted on John. Gabriel then stops and smirks, a long silence follows his words and ends a minute of so later with a loud click.....Gabriel looks up as he hears it. Before turning towards his side....we see as he moves that he is sitting on a black sofa, something rests next to him, which he picks up and holds it in front of his face, revealing it as a newspaper....what is on the front cover, we cannot see.....but it seems to have Gabriel staring at it, not even blinking...before putting the paper down on his lap....as another voice speaks out from opposite him.... John: Is that everything? Gabriel Stands up and starts pacing around the room. He glares over at John and smirks. Gabriel: The End? No…It hasn’t even Begun! The scene the fades to black.
