Come and Av A Go If Ya Think Ya Ard Enuff!

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-05-06 “Hey baby,” came the soft husky voice from a friendly scantily-clad lady wearing way too much make-up as I walked through the streets of Prague. “Vanna have some fun?” “I’m always up for fun,” I reply to the nice lady and with that her lips curl up, she takes my hand and leads me into her apartment. The room is plain and dimly-lit, just about enough to sleep in I guess, it’s a stroke of luck that the nice lady wants me to stay the night though. Not many people would offer a complete stranger a bed for the night. She sways her brunette hair down one side of her head letting it fold over her shoulder and smiles as if something amuses her. “My name is Ivanna Suckuoff,” she says seductively. “’allo Ivanna, my name is Dave Hurst. This is Ducky:” I say as I hold out my plastic yellow duck friend, Ducky, to which Ivanna becomes slightly confused. Her glistening sapphire eyes turn to the doorway and her slightly confused look becomes one of utter bafflement as a long trail of ducks starts waddling into the apartment. “Oh and these are my ducks.” I say assuring her of what they are (in case they don’t get them in the Czech Republic, you know?) “Er… er… D-d-dave… zese will cost extra… A LOT… extra…” she says, finding it difficult to get her words out. I suppose it will be a tight squeeze… Hey hang on! Does she mean money? “Oh sorry love, I don’t have any money.” I assure the Good Samaritan, I’m sure she’ll understand. “VHAT?!” she screams at the top of her voice. “RUBYYYYYY!!!” Ruby…? What the hell is she on about? Answering her screaming call is a lanky, skinny bloke holding a crate of Budvar in one hand, and a can to his face with the other. “Vhat ze hell do you want now Ivanna?” he says. “I vant you to take zis scum sucking peeg away from my room! He takes all zese ducks in vith him and won’t pay!” So this Ruby turns out not to be a short, middle-aged, nasally-voiced New York comedienne, but some scrawny bloke that has had a few cans and thinks he’s superman. Fair enough. “Get the fuck out of ze room!” Ruby screams in drunken fury. At this stage it needs to be pointed out that there is a real reason why these people sound German, it is true that the Czech Republic is not a German-speaking country and in fact has their own language, but in the second world war a large number of German soldiers decided to stay in the country after the war was over and as such the German language filtered into the country to the point that it was almost a secondary language to the people living there. Either that or Dave can’t think of anything else to write and someone on MSN gave him a bunch of Billy Bullshit to write to fill up the roleplay. Judges you mark this paragraph under F for Filler (or B for Bullshit). “Oh yeah! Well what if I don’t want to get out of ze room!” I shout back to the flustered idiot. “Zen I will nut you! For I am a football hooligan! I am a black-belt in the lethal martial art Nut-Yuu-Doe!” He also has a black-belt in Talk-Shit-Zuu… But thinking of something witty to say in response, I could only verbally attack this man in a language that he can understand: “Come and av a go if ya think ya ard enough! Come and av a go if ya think ya ard enough! You’re goin home in a fackin ambulance! You’re goin ‘ome in a fackin ambulance!” “Vhy am I going home in an ambulance? Wouldn’t it be better to take me to ze hospital?” “Yes, I guess that would be a more logical turn of events. DUCKS… ATTACK!” And with that the trail of ducks waddles over to the football fiend. Instead of attacking him with a tirade of violent pecks though, I suddenly realise that this wasn’t the group of evil, rabid ducks that I had ordered and instead was a group of passive ducks that do not believe in violence. They choose not to attack the hooligan, but instead circle him with placards in peaceful protest. “What do we want?” “To stay in the room!” “When do we want it?” “Indefinitely!” “Get away from me you stoopeed ducks! ACHOO!” the hooligan screams. Maybe he is allergic to ducks… “Excuse me, I have a cold.” Damn! We need to come up with something else equally as strange and imaginative that gets Dave away from the scene without losing his front teeth… I really need to stop writing what I’m thinking instead of writing what I’m supposed to be writing. I KNOW! With that I leave the room, and come back with a pram. I climb in the pram, complete with rattle and make baby noises. The ducks start to try and wheel me out of the room to safety. “VAIT A MINUTE!” the football hooligan screams. “I do not tink zat you are a real baby. I vill use ze ultimate are-you-a-baby test… Baby… answer me… vhat is 2 + 2?” Dammit! He had to give me the ultimate are-you-a-baby test… how am I supposed to get out of this pickle? I guess to get out of a pickle you’d just reach the rim of the jar and pull yourself out, which would be a lot easier than answering this flaming question! “Er…” I stutter, not sure what the answer to this question could be. Then it hits me! I accidentally got carried away with the baby act and cracked myself in the head with the rattle! After that I realised the answer. “Goo-goo?” “Correct. You are free to leave.” And with that I was free to leave.
