Gay Cats in Space

18 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th Aug. The Starship Scotchlin cruised across the starry reaches of deep space with a gentle purr. It was a majestically well-crafted spaceship that was at least the length and width of a football pitch and was powered by rocket boosters the size of tennis courts. Imagine Red Dwarf painted in the blue and white of the Scottish Saltire and you're halfway there. 'Captain's log... stardate; blah blah blah, meaningless numbers...' Captain Andy M. sat on the edge of his Sonic the Hedgehog bed-spread in his quarters aboard the great interstellar vessel, speaking into some kind of Dictaphone. Decked out in nothing but black robes, with his hair slicked back, Andy looked very much like an evil bastard. 'The crew of the Starship Scotchlin are beginning to tire after 48 hours solid space travelling. We would have used the hyperspace drive, but unfortunately we had to sell it in order to pay the D.V.L.A... Road tax for this thing is a bastard, I tell you. Never the less, our journey towards the planet AWC has thus far been peaceful and uneventful.' Andy's quarters were slick, if nothing else. A large oval window looked out across the bleak expanse of space, and glass door led out onto a balcony that clearly couldn't be used... you can't breathe outside in space, you know. The chrome walls were lined with photographs from Andy's supposed rise from Star Academy hopeful to full-blown badass evil ruler - speaking of which, a Darth Vader-esque helmet sat on the bed. Andy continued to speak in a faux-deep tone of voice, like that dude that does movie adverts. 'These are the voyages of the Starship Scotchlin... our mission? To boldly go where countless crappy sci-fi parodys have gone before... but with added Scottishness.' With that Andy flicked the Dictaphone off, and clears his throat as he slipped it into the dark expanses of one of the pockets on his robe. 'Deary me, that's tough on the ol' throat,' he mumbled as he got to his feet, very nearly standing on a small, rodent-like robot that was zipping around cleaning the dark blue carpet. Andy took a couple of steps from his bed to turn off the massive intergalactic plasma screen television that had been showing the latest galactic news; the latest on the Yoda rape saga, announcements of a super nova in the Alpha Cheesy Wotsit galaxy, and some mumbo-jumbo about something called Grobschnit being 'hardkorr an frum da streetz'. Andy proceeded to head back over to his bed, pick up his helmet, and pull it over his head. It was pretty much a novelty Dark Vader helmet with a cardboard little 'M' sticky taped to the front, coloured in with red Crayola pens. 'Sweet mother of Jonny Sake, it sure is tough to breath in this thing,' muttered Darth Murrr from behind the mask. He walked across towards the door leading out of his quarters, which was controlled by some super-cool touch screen control panel thing. Instinctively, Darth tapped the screen a couple of times and sure enough, the door slid open. Outside in the corridor, four of Murrr's minions were startled; they had grown tired of waiting for him, and had decided to have a nice little sit. They leapt to their feet, neither of them standing higher than 4ft tall, and immediately saluted their master. Andy snarled behind his mask, annoyed that his loyal followers would be slacking. 'Okay minions, to the bridge!' he commanded. All four of the midgets - clad in identical mini-robes and masks to Darth Murrr - immediately replied in a chirpy voice; 'Yes sir!' The bridge was... well, a bridge. Imagine the bridge from Star Trek: The Next Generation and you've pretty much got the bridge of the Starship Scotchling cracked. However, the Scotchlin's bridge area was packed full of tiny Scottish flags, and all the crew members were decked out in blue and white uniforms and kilts bearing the M. clan tartan - how cool is that? Most of the crew members were hard at work behind consoles packed with loads of buttons and fancy flashing lights, however, two 'men' stood at the giant, triple-glazed window at the very front of the ship, looking out across the stratosphere. These two men, First Mate Cayle and Badass Dude Grobschnit, where the two highest-ranking officers on the ship apart from Darth Murrr, and were generally in charge of making sure everything was kept running smoothly when Murrr wasn't about. It should also be noted that Snowball, of genetically-modified fire-breathing badger fame, was cradled in Cayle's arms, sleeping. 'Boyee, itt shure iz ah bootifull mournin, Crale!' Da' Schnit pointed out in the ghetto-tinged, gravely tone of voice that everyone knows and loves. Cayle raised an eyebrow at this, 'Morning, what the hell are you talking about!?!' He started, 'we're in space, how can you tell its morning... everything looks the same! And for the LAST TIME, my name is CAYLE, not CRALE! GAH!' Grobby scowled at his colleague and folded his arms defensively. 'wtf, stfu.' he responded, as the spacecraft grew closer and closer to the familiar green, white and blue blob that is planet Earth. Cayle thought it best not to respond, just in case he awoke the sleeping Snowball and the badger decided to go on one of its infamous arson streaks. Suddenly, everyone on the bridge was startled as the chrome doors slid open, and the four midget minions of Darth Murrr leapt through. Two at either side of the door, they instantly yelled out 'BOW IN THE PRESENCE OF DARTH MURRR!' in their squeaky little voices, before each pulling out a sparkler from their pockets, and lighting them. Smoke began to appear from underneath the door - Stars In Your Eyes style-e - and the midgets began humming the Star Wars Imperial march tune, as the doors parted once again. 'Dun dun DUN dun da DUN dun da DUN... Dun dun DUN dun da DUN dun da DUN...' Darth Murrr stepped through the door and through the smoke; however, things were not going as planned. Underneath his mask he was coughing violently having inhaled some of the smoke, and was struggling to keep his balance. Eventually, Darth Murrr, blindsided, accidentaly walked right into once of his midget minions, which sent him tumbling to the floor. The crew members all looked up, concerned, as Murrr struggled on the floor. 'AHHH! THE MASK! THE MASK!' he gagged, 'TAKE IT OFF! SMOKE INSIDE! AHHH!' The minions dropped their sparklers and immediately ran to the aid of their fallen leader. 'One... two... three... HEAVE!' Pop! With one almighty pull, the midgets managed to dislodge Darth's crappy helmet. The Scottish EVIL LEADER of Cool coughed a final couple of times, before getting back up to his feet, dusting his robes off. He glanced around the bridge a couple of times, before muttering 'God damn helmet is such a hassle,' under his breath. 'Be seated, minions!' Murrr ordered as his crew members systematically sat back down at their posts. 'How are we all doing today, eh?' The vast majority of the crew members answered back with generic responses like 'alright,' 'good,' 'not bad,' and 'absolutely spiffing, old chap!'. To this, Darth grinned, and said, with a nod; 'Good to hear, my old chipatties! You see, we here on the good ship Scotchlin take care of ALL our brotha's... whether you like sheep-love or not. So, nobody has any qualms then, do they?' Almost immediately, one officer darted up to his feet. He was decked out in the Scotchlin attire, and was also wearing a black baseball cap a-top his head, a mean facial expression making him look one badass dude. He was also wearing a small, red pin-badge, which read; 'I am a huge internet celebrity' on it. At the sight of this, Murrr rolled his eyes. 'By god Terry, you've ALWAYS got some kind of problem!' he proclaimed, which was met with a 'wooo...' from the disgruntled worker. For a brief moment, Darth Murrr turned back to address his other crew members; 'Okay guys, back to work! I'll speak with Terry on my own,' he said, before making his way across to Terry's post. Terry Bitter, it should be noted, was the meanest dude aboard the ship, and would never hesitate to pee in someone's Corn Flakes. However, that said, he was a technical genius; he could do things with a soldering iron that you'd never thought possible... ever seen someone solder a cake, instead of putting it in an oven? That's how cool Terry Bitter was. 'Well, what is it now, officer Bitter!?!' Darth Murrr enquired, as Bitter swivelled around in his office chair. 'Well Murrr, it's my cats,' he began. 'I don't think enough's being done to care for them! I mean, that crazy bastard badger of yours tries to set them on fire at LEAST three times a day, and she ALWAYS steals their food! How are Steven Xandrocat and Kris Kitty supposed to be at peace when they're constantly tormented by a fire-breathing badger!?!' sighed Terry. 'I mean, just because they are homosexual doesn't mean that they deserve to be torched every day, god damn.' Murrr nodded with an inquisitive look across his face, 'Interesting point...' he stated, as he glanced over towards the cat basket, where Steven Xandrocat and Kris Kitty were playfully giving each other a right good rogering. Darth turned away at this, repulsed at the sight of two male cats sharing their love for each other. 'I'll get the 'Schnit on the case as soon as I can, Bitter!' 'Okay, just make sure he does a better job of it than he did testing out my latest program! Three weeks later and I'm STILL picking out errors, that damn incompetent alien fool!' Darth Murrr scowled, and pointing at Terry, said; 'now don't you diss the notorious G-R-O-B! He's gangsta straight-up, you realise how badly he could whoop you, yes?' Terry sighed, 'okay, whatever, just keep my kitties safe!' Taking a mental note of the situation, Andy departed, and wandered across to the front of his ship, where Grobschnit and Cayle were still engaged in a conversation so intelligent that it would make even Stephen Hawking blush. 'Wel, eye fink dat da bezt Popp Tahrtz iz da choklatt unz cuz eeven iff yuo doonot cooke dem tehy ztil tayst gooooder tahn de strawburreez...' Darth Murrr firmly placed his leather glove-clad hand down on the shoulder of Grob, startling the slimey green beast from New Jersey. 'Jeezuz AnDee u skarrin da Grob.' Grobschnit replied, shaking Murrr's grasp from his shoulder. 'Hello chaps! What news from the bridge!?' Darth enquired, exuding a lot more enthusiasm than is expected from the stereotypical evil space lord. 'Absolutely nothing, to tell you the truth,' First Mate Cayle replied almost instantly. 'I did catch a few of the crew members having a Pong tournament earlier, so I sent in Da 'Schnit... needless to say, they all stopped playing when they realised that they could never topple his wicked skills.' Cayle nodded in the direction of Grobber, to acknowledge is gaming prowess. 'Other than that, the popcorn machine is broken; I'll get a couple of the droids to take a look at it later on.' Cayle attempted to continue, but was distracted as his leader's attention began to wander. Darth Murrr was peering out of the 'windscreen' curiously, 'aren't we a little too close to the planet Earth for comfort, Cayle?' he enquired. 'Uhhh, I'm not sure, perhaps we should check the radar?' 'Good plan!' The trio left the main observation deck and hopped across the bridge towards a big flashy sign reading 'Radar & Other Cool Stuff'. Oh, except for Grobschnit, who teleported there, because he's lazy like that. Anyway, after a frantic couple of seconds looking for the radar, it became pretty clear that it wasn't there. 'Jeesh, why the hell are there fish in my radar!?!' Murrr asked, noticing a fine array of tropical fish swimming around before his eyes. 'Oh crap, yeah, I forgot about that,' replied Cayle, 'Lt. Warp put that there, god knows what he did with the radar...' 'Warp!' Darth turned and yelled out. A crew member stood up from his chair, his face covered by a frankly rubbish cut-out of a Klingon's face. ''Ey, mama mia! What are you-a wanteeng!?!' he chriped in a thick Italian accent. 'What the hell did you do to my radar!?!' 'Oh, sorry about that... I needed a couple-a extra bucks, so I sold it-a at a car boot-a sale-a, an' I got those fishys... besides, they are a lot-a more-a pretty to look at than a radar.' Darth Murrr let out a disappointed sigh. 'Remind me, Warp, why I don't kick your ass...' 'Because-a I'd whoop yours, bitch.' 'Yeah, that's it...' BOOM! Suddenly, the lights inside the bridge flickered, as the entire spaceship was rocked with a massive blast, knocking a couple of guys off of their chairs. After steadying himself, Murrr looked around, confused, 'what the bloody hell was that!?!' 'Oh my god! It's HIM!' an anonymous voice yelled out, 'bringing up the big screen...' Sure enough, a massive television screen above the observation deck flickered into life. Everyone inside the ship yelped 'OH NO!' at what they saw; the ghastly, grinning face of Earth's supreme leader. 'OH NO! NOT GEORGE W. MOOSE!' Murrr yelled, cowering behind Grobschnit. The half-man, half-moose, all-retard creature let out a hideous cackle. 'Yeehaw, Darth Murrr, you have violated our air-space again, partna! Turn around, or prepare to be blown to smithereens! MWAHAHAHAHA!' Andy opened his mouth to let out a submissive response, but he was cut off by the unrelenting Grobschnit! 'NEVA FAGIT! U GETT UR AZZ OWT UV MA WAHY ORR IZZ GONA KIK UR AZZ, FAGIT!' Grobschnit shook his fist manically at the screen, unaware of the fact that he was worsening the situation. 'Yippe-ki-yay motherfucker!' Moose shouted, as the screen flickered off. Almost immediately, the Starship Scotchlin was rocked by another blast, and another... and another, bah gawd! Darth Murrr struggled to make it across towards the captain's chair, but breathed a sigh of relief as he finally buckled up his seatbelt. Grobschnit, on the other hand, managed to teleport into his chair... some people have it WAAAAY too easy. 'SHIT! We've lost the thrusters! WE'RE GOING DOWN!' a female voice yelled out. 'AHHHH! BRACE YOURSELVES!' -------------------- And so, after a hideously long sequence involving the Starship Scotchlin almost burning up on entering the Earth's atmosphere, the spectacular ship finally came to an abrupt hault, it's hulk a sizzling mess of twisted metal. Fortunately, much of the bridge was still in-tact, although at this point in time it would have been tough to hazard a guess on the survivor count. Through the smoke and the dust, a small hatch on the outside of the bridge popped open. A familiar head soon popped out; Darth Murrr was coughing again. He climbed up and out onto the ship's hull, trying to catch a better glimpse of his surroundings, and was soon followed out only by Cayle, Grobschnit, Warp, Terry Bitter, Snowball, Steven Xandrocat and Kris Kitty. 'Where the hell!?!' asked Terry, scratching his head in confusion. However, his attention was soon distracted, as his two cats ran up to him. Terry scooped them up, cradling them in his loving arms. 'No idea...' Cayle responded. However, as the dust settled, accented voices could be made out through all the commotion. 'Blinky blonky blimey, guv'na! Come 'ave a butcher's of this!'
