The Bizwrite Saga, Ch 2: The Break-Up

24 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/05 “So…that was an interesting movie.” “Yeah, I guess.” “What’s with you tonight?” “Huh? What do you mean?” “I mean you’ve just been acting exactly like that!” “Exactly like what?” “Like your mom died or something. It’s as if everyone you ever loved in your life just died, that’s how you’re acting.” “It is not, Tim!” “It is too! Now, what’s the problem?” “…” “Liz…” “I dunno…I’ve just been thinking…” “Thinking about what?” “The future. The present. The past. Everything. It’s just been really pulling me down lately.” “What’re you, crazy? This is the time of our lives! We’re going to graduate soon, and we’ll then start working, and then…” “That’s exactly it, Tim.” “What? Graduating? Don’t be nervous about it. It’ll be great!” “No, not that. After graduation.” “What about it?” “It’s just…it’s just that I don’t want to go off and work.” “Whaddya mean? We said we’d both work, start our own business! That was our dream!” “Tim, I don’t want to be stuck here in the Eagle’s Nest for the rest of my life…” “What?! Stuck, in the Eagle’s Nest?! This is the best place to be!” “Tim, be quiet! Let me talk!” “…” “Tim, I’m sick of the Eagle’s Nest. I mean, I love the feeling of safety, of knowing no one here will touch me. I love how we’re all one big family, and I got guys like you to help me. But there’s a world outside of the Nest, Tim.” “Well…what were you thinking about doing, then?” “Wow, you don’t sound too mad. I thought that’d really get your goat.” “Heh, well, it irks me, but I kind of want to hear about what you want to do.” “Actually, I’m thinking about college.” “College? What for?” “Um, because I want to broaden my horizons. I want to meet new people, do new things. I want to make a difference in the WORLD, Tim, not the Eagle’s Nest. And, to be honest, I can’t do that with the things I learned around here.” “Well, um…where were you thinking?” “I don’t want to tell you…” “No, tell me! Please!” “Promise you won’t get mad?” “Promise.” “Princeton. I got in.” “Princeton? In New Jersey?” “Yeah, why?” “That’s, like, six hours away!” “So?” “That’s too far!” “HEY! You promised you wouldn’t get mad?” “…” “Tim?” “I’m sorry. That’s…um…great! No, really! I’m happy for you! What’re you going to study?” “English. I want to be a teacher.” “Heh, like Mr. Carver? That old goat?” “Tim, not every teacher is like Mr. Carver.” “Heh, what’re you going to be, Mrs. Carver?” “Shut up, asshole!” “*sing-song* Liz and Mr. Carver, sitting in a tree! F-u-c-k-i-n-g…” “Hey, fuck head! Fuck you! I knew you’d be a fucking asshole!” “I’m sorry! I’m sorry!” “…” “Liz…I’m sorry…” “…I was thinking about the past, too.” “What about?” “About you. Liam. The rest of the Eagles.” “Oh?” “Yeah. Oh.” “You sound angry…” “I AM angry.” “Why, what’d I do?” “Don’t act like you’ve been a goodie-two-shoes!” “What?” “I know the violence you’ve been part of! The killings! You think I have plugs in these ears? You think I’m retarded, fuck head?” “No, but…” “No-but-nothing! I’ve heard everything! That house you and Liam dismantled! That whole family you killed! And that little boy who was on that tricycle…” “He was saying things…” “HE WAS SIX YEARS OLD! HE WAS SIX YEARS OLD AND YOU SHOT HIM RIGHT IN THE FUCKING HEAD!” “…” “His mother was RIGHT THERE! He was six!...*sniffle*…he was SIX!...” “…” “*sniffle*…and don’t think I don’t know WHY you killed these people…” “They were threatening…” “THEY WERE THREATENING SHIT! YOU’VE BEEN POISONED, TIM!” “Huh?” “Your brain! You think any black, Hispanic, Asian, or whatever who is anywhere NEAR our neighborhood is going to be a threat! You think they’re rodents, that they’re rats who are here to be exterminated…” “That’s cause…” “NO! THEY ARE NOT! *sniffle*” “Liz, where the fuck is this coming from? I thought you were part of the Eagle’s Nest! I thought you prayed to the same White Lord that me, Liam, and the rest of us Eagles did! What are you saying?” “I’m saying FUCK YOU and FUCK the Eagle’s Nest! You guys are so ignorant and self-serving…you think God only loves white people?” “Um, yes! I do!” “WELL YOU’RE WRONG! Every person you’ve killed has some love from God. Every person you’ve put down believes that the Lord will help them. And then here comes you and Liam, oppressing the world like some kind of demons!” “Don’t you dare…” “YOU SHUT THE FUCK UP! You and the rest of the ‘Eagles’ have been saying what you believe for the last 18 years of my life. Guess how many years that means that I’VE said a word, Tim?” “…” “You’re right! Zero! I’ve said NOTHING about what I believe! And now, you’re going to hear it!” “*sigh* Well, Liz, I’ll listen. It’ll be painful, but I’ll listen.” “…” “Go on now. I won’t say a word.” “*deep breath* I believe every man, woman, and child on this earth is protected by God. I believe that everyone can help in one way or another. Those drug dealers are giving the police officers something to do. Diseases give doctors something to do. Criminals give guards something to do. God picks a role for everyone.” “…” “But when you decide to KILL one of these people because of what they look like, that’s when you’re messing with God’s will. You could’ve killed a future doctor that’ll cure AIDs. You could’ve killed a soldier that will save his whole unit. You could’ve killed one of the greatest mothers or fathers alive. And why did you do this, Tim?” “…” “Because they’re black or Hispanic or whatever, and that is BULLSHIT.” “…” “That is all I have to say.” “*sighs* Well…I’m sorry…but you know I’m going to disagree with you…” “…” “But that doesn’t change the fact that I still love you.” “Tim…” “Yes?” “That’s the present.” “Wait, what?” “That’s the present I’ve been thinking about. You and I.” “What about it?” “We’re done.” “What? Why?” “I’ve found someone else.” “Who?” “…” “Please, Liz, tell me who.” “You’ll kill him if I tell you.” “Liz…” “No, you will!” “Liz, I promise you this: I love you. And with that promise means that I only want the best for you. If the best means someone else, then so be it. And no matter WHO that someone else is I will not touch him. Fuck, if someone is messing with him, I’ll help him fight. You’ll always have my protection, you and whoever you love, because I love you.” “…” “Now…who is it?” “Chuck Prowell.” “…” “Tim?” “Chuck Prowell? That n*gger?” “TIM!” “I suggest you leave. I suggest you leave here and never come back.” “Tim, you promised!” “And I will keep that promise. But I cannot promise that anyone else will recognize it. And now that you have betrayed the Nest, I cannot promise your life. To be honest, I could care less if they kill him.” “You promised!” “I did. And if I keep my part of the promise and people are around, then I will get killed.” “Tim, I still want to be your friend!...” “Then you will LISTEN to me and take Chuck away from here! Please, Liz! I’m asking you this because I love you and I don’t want you to be hurt! Please, find somewhere to go and GO!” “Tim!...” “JUST GO!” And that was the last time we had spoken in person.
