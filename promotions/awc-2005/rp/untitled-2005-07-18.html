Untitled

18 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I. He stared at the hardwood floor. Glistening beads of strain-induced sweat streamed down from hairline pores. Some plummeted to the floor while others travelled down his flexing, quivering arms. Laboured veins bulged, like an intricate system of jutting wires beneath his scarred skin. His heart drummed a monotone symphony, crescendoing with every beat, as it pumped blood to oxygen-starved muscles. His nostrils flared with each strained breath while his dry lips were sealed and imperfect teeth gritted. The overwhelming impulses of pain that caused his arms to shake uncontrollably, as they bended and straightened, had overridden the count he had spat through his teeth. He wondered how many more he could perform, and if it was possible to faint from an overdose of pressups. He imagined his biceps and triceps tearing from the bone and his arms buckling and dislocating beneath the weight of his own torso. The wood had become blurred and distorted and discoloured. He collapsed. ### Cold water felt good against his face, although the nauseating faintness would not subside. Hands against grimy, cracked tiles for stability. It shouldn't be this way. Not for him. Why was everything so hard? Her. ### Not even his pillow could bring respite. Each time his increasingly more heavy eyelids closed, another ornament would crash and shatter audibly, or her violated screams would puncture his eardrums. There was no fucking respite.
