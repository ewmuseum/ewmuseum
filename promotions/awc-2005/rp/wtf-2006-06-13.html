Date: 13/06/06 Dark skies surrounded the Hamilton military base in Bermuda. This is normally no surprise to the natives, and Army personnel, but it had an eerie feel to it. It felt like something bad was going to happen, and normally when you get that feeling some bad shit is about to do down. Hey Guys! How’s it going? An old camouflaged 1982 discovery land rover pulled up into the driveway of 3rd battalions living quarters. Inside the car sits a carefree Gadget, who is given a warm welcome from his new tact-team. Yo sarge, what was paki-land like? Well, that person shouting that slightly racist remark would be Kane, the racist of the bunch…even though he himself is Afro-American. Hey, keep the racism to a minimum Kane, at least, until you’re drunk… And it sucked, I got abscesses on my Ass cheeks. Oooh, nice. You bet ya. After the Abscess comment, there was an awkward silence, a slight snigger, and Garrison (The most curious of the bunch) tried looking for lumps on my ass. But I just gave him a slap, and made him do press ups. 20…21…22… Oh, its good to be sergeant</> And after yet another awkward silence, Gadget wandered round to the back of the land rover, took out his rifle, slung his dark green rucksack over his shoulder and headed inside, with a somewhat serious look on his face. *Whispers* Look at my ass, the perverted bunch Inside Gadget swung open the door to his conveniently located office, by conveniently located I mean next to restrooms and the showers, and lay his rifle down on the desk. He made a swift turn, and looked at all the pre-arranged photos of him receiving the medals that decorate his chest, well he hasn’t got them stuck in his chest, I mean they’re decorating the chest of his formal wear, but you probably already knew that, and let out a soft sigh. *quietly* those were the days. After a ten minute staring spree, Gadget finally dropped his gaze from the pictures, turned to look at his bed, and tossed his bag onto the foot end of the bed. BANG Gadget, who was just about to sit on his bed, used his stock reaction to a loud bang, duck and cover. Oh shit, what was that? Gadget, crawled behind the door of his office, and slowly pulled the door open, until there was just a little slit that he could peer through, and saw that the bang was his youngest tact-team member, Eric, had dropped his rifle and put a large whole through the door. ERIC! erm…ye…yeah sarge? Eric begins to panic an tried to hide his gun, and look inconspicuous. Why were you messing with your rifle? Are you a ten your old? Do you have to fiddle with everything? Are you totally retarded? Gadget Pulls himself to his feet, and tries to de-redden his face from the sheer embarrassment of taking cover from a clearly retarded 17 year old tact-team member. N…No…Sergeant Well, why in fucks name were you playing with that rifle soldier? Wh…Who…is Fuck sir? Don’t get cheeky with me Soldier, and why were you playing with that rifle? You could have kill… Sarge, I think you should see this! Not now Garrison, I’m disciplining the young retard here Well ok, but it involves your Car and Kane Garrison puts on a smirk, as he turns round and runs out to the car. WHAT! MY CAR? Gadget, with no hesitation runs outside to check on his prized possession…the land rover. And he has clearly forgotten that Kane is meant to be hurt or something, but come on lets face it, racists are shit compared to land rovers. Gadget shoves the cabin door open with his full weight; a look of sheer anger and disbelief covers his face as he turns his head to look at his land rover, to see it coated with blood, and with 3 large bullet holes in the front of car. … Gadget just stared at the car, completely speechless, he watched the blood drip off of the front of the car. And then, as his gaze reached the floor, he noticed three little plastic capsules on the floor, which had a substance inside of them, a red liquid, which looked exactly the same as the blood on the car. At first, he thought he had just parked on top of them and they had just been dripped on, but when he finally turned to look at Kane, he saw that the supposed-to-be wound on his leg, was not even a wound, there was no bullet hole, where he was meant to be injured, just blood, and again three capsules of the same type were lying just behind him, and covered in the same red substance. Hey Kane, would you mind standing up? Sarge, I cant stand up, I HAVE A FUCKING BULLET THROUGH MY LEG! Well could you please explain why there is no bullet hole, or wound. Why there are three blood capsules behind you, and three under my car? Well You see sir…Funny story… Shut up!, I want this all cleaned off by sunrise tomorrow, and the cost of the hood is coming out of all your paychecks Sir, that’s like twenty dollars worth of damage *with a smirk on his face* Well the army don’t know that now do they… Gadget, leaving the smirk on his face, turned and wandered in side muttering to himself: Its good to be the sarge…oh its good to be the sarge
