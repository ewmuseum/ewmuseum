When You Play With Fire

19 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19.09.06 At Coast To Coast, Josh Marquez’s attempts to gain the first title of his AWC career ended up being like Verne Troyer trying to get on the Hulk coaster – he came up a little short. He couldn’t get to Red Rock before the Fijian animal Afeaki beat the Englishman for the Relentless title. Josh’s failure was compounded when he was pinned before Team East won the match. Of course, just to rub a little more salt in the wounds, the person that pinned him was a girl. Wearing a bikini. Now, after a thoroughly deserved week off (did you see that Full House from the climbing wall?!), Marquez is back in an AWC ring, live on Fresh and looking to pick up some steam in his efforts to climb the ladder, using the gypsy O’Shea as a very versatile rung. His opponent – Ellis Nash. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* 14:57 It had started out as nothing more than a simple pick up game. An ordinary Saturday afternoon where nobody had much to do, saving themselves for that night and the inevitable glut of high stakes games that accompanied the visiting high rollers each and every weekend. Josh had received a call from one of his old school friends around 3 PM, at a time when the temperature was changing from ‘supernova hot’ to merely ‘skin blistering hot’. “Hey man, it’s Tony.” “What’s up?” “Just wondering if you fancy heading down to the court, play a little 2-on-2?” “You, me and who else?” “Eddie and probably Hector, apparently his old lady wants him to do some DIY.” “Remind me never to get married.” “I hear you man, I hear you. So, central courts in 20 minutes?” “See you there.” That was it. That’s how it all began. If Josh had said no, things would have been totally different. If Tony had said 45 minutes instead of 20, things would have been totally different. It was a prime case of that ‘sliding doors’ effect, only without Gwyneth Paltrow. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* 15:21 The court wasn’t empty. Far from it in fact. Most times you get a few kids playing around, kids who would happily move off the court once they realised some adults wanted to play and who would cheer enthusiastically, sounding as if they were watching game 7 of the NBA finals rather than a 2-on-2 game between some friends. Today though, the court was occupied by another groups of guys around the same age as Josh and his friends. That’s where the similarities ended though. For while Josh’s group were a bunch of laid back, easygoing, Hispanic guys, the guys they could see were aggressive, pissed off, Caucasian trailer dwellers. Their bodies were that whiter than white colour and their hair was shaved close to their heads. The slightly sagging midriffs added to the pure trash look, all that was missing was the chewing tobacco. The way they were standing round the side of the court, passing round a bottle of convenience store bourbon, was a pretty clear indication that they had finished their game and were finished. Well, you’d think so anyway. As it turned out, the second Josh, Tony et al stepped onto the court, the trash decided they were going to play again. “Hey ese, what the fuck you think you’re doing?” This was said to Hector, the most chilled and calm of the bunch. So instead of getting pissed at the clear insult to his heritage by ‘Billy Bob’ or whatever this relocated redneck was called, Hector simply laughed it off. “That’s funny dude, real funny. Are you guys done here?” “Do we look like we’re done homes?” Hector exchanged a look with Tony, who looked to Eddie, who in turn looked to Josh, who then responded. “Well yeah, you do.” “Well we ain’t done Jose, so why don’t y’all make your way back down to Mexico and play. Go drink some tequila and go loco!” The rest of the white boys loved this, laughing out loud as Josh and his buddies looked quizzically at them. Had they missed the comedy bit? Was the humour too sophisticated for them to understand? Or was it just that these walking examples of inbreeding were fucking stupid? Josh wasn’t going to listen to this shit, he came here to play basketball. “I’ve got a better idea, why don’t we have a little game - us against you, four-on-four, first to 21 wins?” This got their attention, clearly the chance of embarrassing some Mexicangatos was too good to pass up, even with a bottle of Captain Jack’s $5 Genuine Kentucky Bourbon as an alternative. “Alright Jose, you want it, you got it. Street rules.” Josh nodded in acceptance and both he and ‘Billy Bob’ turned back to their respective groups, discussing strategy before the impromptu game got underway. Then the battle was joined. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* 15:38 It was 20-16 to Josh’s team, Eddie getting 12 of those points and showing exactly why he would have been destined for the NBA if he hadn’t blown out his knee in the penultimate year of high school. Hector was in possession of the ball at the minute, facing the hick called Tiny by his friends. Of course it was one of those ironic names, like calling a morbidly obese guy ‘Slim’. Tiny was easily 6’7” and underneath the soft exterior there was undoubtedly a solid body. Not the smartest though. Hector faked to the left then passed the ball under his leg to the right. If he’d been in a video game, that would have been his signature move, his friends knew it was coming and Tiny should have, given that Hector had already used it twice in this game already. He didn’t though and was left looking like a fool when the ball went straight to Tony he finished the game off with a simple lay-up. The white boys weren’t happy though.. “Bullshit it’s over, we’re playing to 30!” Eddie turned to face ‘Billy Bob’ this time, the two men eye to eye and only a foot apart as Eddie spoke, laying it out in simple terms for the bigger man. “No man, the game’s over. We agreed 21 and we won, now we get the court.” “The fuck you do!” That’s when it all kicked off. ‘Billy Bob’ swung a right at Eddie, knocking him backwards several feet. As Tony and Hector caught him and pulled him up, Josh launched himself at Tiny, taking the larger man down and locking in a rudimentary front face lock, subduing him instantly. The other two rednecks had come to their senses by now and had taken out both Tony and Hector, using their sheer bulk to overwhelm the smaller men. Eddie was still reeling when he caught another right from ‘Billy Bob’, this one sending him to the ground and giving the redneck leader the opportunity to make his way over to his bag lying courtside and pull out a wooden bat. “Hector, behind you!” Josh’s warning came too late, the bat smashing into Hector’s ribs, once, twice, three times, leaving him gasping for air on the concrete court surface as the white boys crowded near ‘Billy Bob’, Tiny making his way unsteadily to his feet after Josh released the face lock to check on his fallen friend. “Fucking dirty Mexicans, you don’t fuck with real Americans. Fuck you, and fuck la raza!” They walked off and got into their car, parked at the side of the court, the four of them piling into the dusty Chevrolet Monte Carlo before ‘Billy Bob’ gunned the engine and drove off. Eddie and Tony joined Josh at Hector’s side, both of them bleeding from the nose and mouth. “Hector, you okay man?” Gasping for breath and in obvious pain, Hector replied. “Hurts…like a…bitch…my chest…” Josh put his ear to Hector’s chest, listening to the wheezing every time his friend drew breath. Then Hector passed out. “Oh fuck! We gotta get him to a hospital now! Tony get your car!” “Should we not just call an ambulance?” “I think he’s got a punctured lung, we need to get him there fast!” ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* As it turned out, Josh’s diagnosis was pretty much spot on. It transpired that one of Hector’s broken ribs had pierced his left lung, leaving him needing emergency surgery. The doctors praised Josh for getting him to the hospital so quickly, the speed with which they had got there had saved Hector’s life. These words meant nothing to Josh though, his mind focused on one thing and one thing only - revenge. As the white thugs had left the scene of the crime, Josh had managed to get a look at their number plate. Not the whole thing, but certainly enough for them to be identified by when coupled with the model of the car. When the time came to give statements though, Josh pleaded ignorance and instructed Eddie and Tony to do likewise. “So you can’t tell me anything about the car?” “No officer, sorry, it all happened so fast.” Three days later though, Josh had them tracked down, using an old friend in the DMV to get him an address for the car’s owner. Turned out that ‘Billy Bob’ was actually called Troy Garvin, formerly a resident of Birmingham, Alabama, who had relocated to Nevada for fear of reprisals after a rape case against him was thrown out on a technicality. Well, old Troy obviously hadn’t learnt his lesson but Josh would be more than happy to be the one to teach him. All it took was one phone call, a phone call to some ‘debt collectors’ who Josh knew, a nominal fee paid in cash to them, and Mr Garvin was taught his lesson. What a lesson it turned out to be. Josh’s instructions to ‘put him in hospital’ left a lot of scope for the ‘debt collectors’ to do whatever they wanted to the unfortunate Troy. Of course, the fact that most of them knew and liked Hector caused them to err a little more to the extreme than usual. The outcome - 3 weeks in hospital for Troy, recovering from innumerable bruises, a broken wrist, a broken leg and a broken jaw which led to him eating through a straw for two months. When you fucked with Josh Marquez, payback really was a bitch.
