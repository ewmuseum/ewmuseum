Deceptions

18 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18-7-05 I think it is about time that I come to tell you the truth. I undoubtedly believe that you are quite offended that I have been lying to you. You see it is too often that we are lied to that we fail to see that sometimes a lie is not always a bad thing. Granted having a belief shattered is not an easy event. I can’t think of such a person who is unfazed by the travesty of the revealing of truth. However, I will say, on my behalf, that it isn’t exactly myself who has lied to you. All the perspectives I have given you are absolute truths. All be it, the facts I have presented are not absolutely true. Still and all, nonetheless, the perspective of such persons, does in good conscience believe that what they think and recall is true. Despite that though, you are still quite admittedly angry that you have been lied to. Lies are a terrible blessing. Lies are deceiving, actually that is the point all together. The question remains “Is deception a bad thing?” I’m not exactly sure I can answer that question. However, I can bring up some very persuading thoughts to ponder. When you go to see a three-dimensional film deception fools the eye into thinking that things are flying at you when really it is a two-dimensional film and two pieces of film that elude your eyes into believing such things can actually approach you. David Coperfield, entertaining, but when you come to look at it, a liar none the less. I think it’s obvious where I’m going with this. Do you remember how great Christmas was when Santa Clause still existed? Or how great it was that the tooth fairy rewarded you for the pain you had to deal with biting into that apple and loosing the loosy-goosy one you’ve been playing with for a week? Do you remember how safe you felt knowing that Tommy whether he was the white or the green ranger was still going to come to the rescue? Now, I know what you are going to say. But what about when you found out the truth? Granted learning that Santa isn’t real can make the average kid cry. However, we can’t exactly say that learning that the “real” world isn’t exactly what you assumed it was is the reason college became a drunken splendor and a rude awakening at the same time. ------------------------------------------------------- Crimson was shocked when he was arrested at Fresh! He did not know that it was even a possibility that Interpol was really there to arrest him. In his mind, he had been an honorable citizen and under no circumstances would violate the law. Crimson was unbeknownst to his past, he had been deceived by himself and his confidences. Crimson stood in the ring in confusion when the International Police had turned in his direction. He was utterly shocked and speechless. The shock turned to confusion, as the motion of being put into handcuffs somehow became a moment of de ja vous. He couldn’t remember ever being put into handcuffs, however, at this moment he could have sworn that this had happened before. He could not think of when he had ever been put in handcuffs. Crimson became frightened like he had never been frightened before. If he had not known where these emotions were coming from or this information about his past, what else did he not know about himself? Not only did Crimson feel he knew the Miranda rights by heart when they were read to him, he felt as if he was not out of place. Fear filled him from his toes; he walked lightly wondering when the world was going to crack and suck him in. He was obviously into something far more serious than he believed he had ever dealt with before. He felt as if he had been thrown into waters he knew by heart, but only forgot how to swim. Crimson’s world grew dark as he was led into the forest of confusion by the hands of another man. Crimson was thrown into the police car outside the arena. His head slammed against the upper car doorframe, which is some accomplishment given his height. His headache brought him back to reality. The Interpol officer in front of him had just entered the car and turned on the ignition when Crimson was determined to find out his situation. “Why have I been arrested?” Crimson asked the driver. “No hablo ingles.” He replied. “Great,” Crimson thought, “I can’t explain any of this to myself and either can the people who put me in this situation.” However, Crimson had no idea how true this statement was. He had no clue that his own friends knew that Crimson had been arrested before, but did not know that he was to be arrested again. Their own faults had gotten him into this situation. It was also a promise between the two of them, never to tell Crimson the truth. ------------------------------------------------------- The truth is Crimson wasn’t exactly put into the mental institution on his own accord like was believed. He was forced to admit himself into the center by another party. Jessie and Jason only tell Crimson that he had admitted himself after Jessie had found him starving and curled in the fetal position in his own family’s house. The truth, would be far worse of a pill to swallow for Crimson. As I’ve stated, deception isn’t always a bad thing. Jessie and Jason aren’t lying to Crimson. They are only reaffirming what he believes to be true. As Jason would put it Crimson’s chooses not to believe or remember certain things about his past to protect him. If Jason or Jessie were to force Crimson to see the truth his body would go into shock. Crimson has chosen to deceive himself than to deal with what happened. Jason knows that the mind and body of a human being are quite more powerful than the average person would guess. Jason knows that the mind and body are built to protect themselves. He knows that Crimson’s mind choosing not to recollect certain times in his past is an unconscious decision by his brain to protect Crimson from mental anguish and possible melt down. Jason remembers from college that the brain often times creates a way to make unwanted memories disappear. One of these ways is to create a completely different personality to store such information. Also known as Dissociative Identity Disorder or DID and sometimes referred to as Multiple Personalities. People afflicted DID or MP are often times victims of trauma during childhood. The pre-adolescent mind often times creates this other personality to remember such traumatic events as rape, witnessing a murder, or even committing a crime. One could say self-deception is only there to guard the deceptor. Jason also knows that the process to awaken people to their own faults is often a very difficult road. Schizophrenics used to be tied to beds and given insulin overdoses before being forced to realize the truth of reality. These measures were taken because often times the body’s physical shock caused convolutions and self-mutilation. Jason having been attached to Crimson could not put him through the anguish of realizing his own actions. Imagine finding out that the biggest lie ever told to you was told by yourself. Jason knew that in Crimson’s case the reaction to such a realization would be life threatening. ------------------------------------------------------- Crimson was brought back to another de ja vous. This time in a jail cell. This time however, he believe he knew where this feeling was derived from. He thought that spending time in his white room with the rubber walls was what reminded him of this situation. Then again, he thought, if I have been arrested, I’ve probably been in jail. Crimson found comfort and discomfort from his current surroundings. Although the silence and dreary atmosphere reminded him of home the silence forced him to wonder about his night. Questions ran through his mind. Questions he knew better of than to ponder. He was a person who knew that asking questions without answers only brought on more questions with answers. He also knew he must not creep back to the inward motion of his emotions that he had once found comfort to. Crimson was brought back to conscienceless when the gate to his cell was opened and another detainee was thrown in. The detainee looks Crimson over as if sizing him up for whatever battle was about to ensue. The man spoke, “Por que, eres aqui?” He said his Spanish slowly and with much deliberation as if choosing unoffending words. Crimson starred at him blankky. “Por Que? Por que, aqui eres?” Crimson positioned his body away from the man. “Eres aqui, si? Por Que?” By this time Crimson’s temper was getting short, “No hablas espanol.” “Well damn, me either.” Said the detainee with a Texan accent. “So what in the hell are you doing in here?” “I don’t know.” Said Crimson, looking down the hallway. “Well, then. You must have been pretty fucked up not to remember.” Said the Texan with no inhibitions. “Something like that I guess.” Crimson said. Crimson was not happy with saying this. He was quite angered at the situation by this point. He had no idea how he got himself into this mess or no idea how to get himself out. He wondered if he had been on some sort of mind-altering substance to cause things like this to happen. This also angered Crimson, he was a man of honor—a man with morals, he would not purposefully get himself into this situation or take drugs period, let alone a substance that would cause a hang over like this mess. ------------------------------------------------------- The truth is Jessie didn’t exactly find Crimson and save him from the turmoil of depression exactly like you think she did. She did, but maybe it’s a little over embellished that what she did was somewhat nonchalant. The truth is Jessie after learning that Crimson was in quite a bind went looking for him. She searched many places until she had finally decided to search his home. She thought it impossible for him to be there, but thought it possible a clue would be there to lead to his location. Working like a red headed Nancy Drew she was quite distraught to have to crawl under police tape to enter the house. She searched the house and thought that the police had taken everything important. Then she went to Crimson’s parents room. She found him under the bed, unconscious and apparently disturbed. She pulled him out from under the bed and tried to wake him. She got him to his feet on her own account and began to get him down the stairs. It is amazing how adrenaline can help you do things normally you wouldn’t try. However, Crimson isn’t that large of a man to begin with. She got Crimson into her car and started driving. She drove all the way out of state before she started thinking better of it. She was unsure of what to do and wished dearly her sister was there to tell her what to do? She had turned to leave Georgia and head for Alabama when instead she made a U-turn. Jessie was assured that the justice system had to work. ------------------------------------------------------- When Jessie went to pick Crimson up you thought the world had ended. Crimson’s questions the night before must have driven him to unsurpassable disgust of not knowing the truth. “What’s going on, Jessie? What do you know about this?” Crimson said as soon as they were out of the police station. “I don’t know, Crimson. I honestly had no idea that leaving the country would get you arrested.” She said solemnly. “Then what do you know? You can’t keep me out of the knowing on stuff like this?” “All I know is that you were arrested for violation of parole, because you left the country.” She said, hoping this would be enough of an answer for him. “And when exactly was I put on parole?” “I guess that when you were admitted to the mental institution certain rules and regulations came along with that.” She said, heart stricken for lying. She turned to Crimson who just nodded in agreement with this answer. The truth was Jessie didn’t feel it was her place to tell him. She didn’t know whose place it was to tell him, she would not be the one to shatter his heart though. Some burdens were just unbearable. I don’t find it my place, as a narrator, to tell you yet either. Crimson will let you know when he is ready to accept the actions which have distraught him for years.
