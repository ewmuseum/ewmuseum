Setting it up.. How young kids fall in Love

11 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10./06 Waiting a week to try and find out if going out on a date with Tiara was just not feasible for Wayne. However she had given him her number, was that maybe a subtle hint to give her a call before hand? Or if he called would it imply he was being too pushy. One thing Wayne didn’t know was that as much as he was thinking about this and worrying, she was thinking about it ten fold. She wasn’t sleeping at night, over what? Some guy she had just met, but she was losing sleep over him… and she had no clue why. After spending two or three days going back and forth, be what you could call wishy washy, meaning he wasn’t sure whether or not he should call her. Finally he made the decision to call her. He picks up the phone and starts dialing. SLAM! The dial tone had barely been heard and Wayne had decided against calling her, he after he made his decision he actually looks down at the phone, wondering if he just did the right thing, but it doesn’t take Wayne long to make the decision to pick the phone up again. Again he dials the number she gave him, this time he allows it to ring about four times before again we here….. SLAM! Once Wayne chickens out, he just doesn’t seem to have enough balls to try and call a girl who actually wanted him to call her. But the story starts to unfold more and more… sweat is pouring down his face. He wipes it once with his left sleeve and then once with his right sleeve. Wayne knows what he has to do… he has to call her. He needs to grow a sac as a bunch of high school boys would say but it is the truth, and he knows it. Once again he tries to call this time it doesn’t even ring four times and she is on the other end wondering who is calling her and hanging up/ “Hello?” Comes from a soft voice on the other end, it sounds like one from a younger girl but one can never tell. “Hello, is Tiara available?” Wayne is looking to win over the parents for his stupid mistake of calling a couple of times and hanging up to no avail. “Speaking, may I ask who is calling?” Tiara in the back of the mind had hoped it could only be Wayne calling, but figured she would soon be disappointed to find it was one of her cousins or something. “Tiara, it is Wayne, you know the boy from the other night at the fire.” As those words came out of the mouth of Wayne his heart dropped, right then and there would probably lead to the whether or not he actually had a chance with this girl. “Oh Wayne, of course I remember you how could I forget such a cute face?” Tiara could tell he was nervous just by the sound of his voice and could only hope that the words she had just uttered made it that much easier for him. “Well… you know you are unforgettable yourself.” Wayne trying anything and everything to win the girl smooth talked her the best he could. “Ah… how sweet,” you know how young girl are easily impressed by such simple lines, man don’t you miss those days? “So how have you been, I was hoping you would give me a call.” “Really,” Wayne shocked and pleased that she wanted to call did not want to seem to excited tried to hide the emotion in his voice but to no avail it was easily detected by her and he could tell by the giggle when she heard him say that. Wayne decides he should go back to the original question before he looks simple, “Not bad and yourself?” Tiara wanted to make him wait a little bit, decided to take a deep breath and slowly exhale before she answered the question. “Very well, if I say so myself,” she knew she had him right where she wanted him. “So, what per se was the reason for the call?” She had him hook line and sinker, she was weary about saying it in that manner, but decided to do it and when she did he hit the ground, literally that is. Wayne who was taken aback by such a question needed to think of something, and think of something quickly. “Well Tiara, the real reason I was calling was to see if you would like to make plans sometime.” She wanted to play it out, she wanted to see how far he would go before she would have him sitting on the edge of everyone one of her words. Little did she know he was already at that state, but she continued to play the game. “That could be a possibility this weekend, what did you have in mind?” Wayne’s heart dropped at that comment, he had hoped she would say that… but now he had to give her an idea of what he actually wanted to do, and that was the tough part. “Well almost anything, if you want we go see a movie, I could take you out to diner, if you like that is.” Wayne seemed a little pushy here… well maybe not pushy but more eager and she could tell. The more and more he pursued the more and more she liked him, his subtle comments, his nervous voice, the stumbling when she asked him a certain question, this all attracted her to him, but for some reason she didn’t know why. “I would enjoy almost any of those things, but I will let you decide and surprise me when you come and pick me up.” Wayne had heard the words he wanted to hear, she had wanted to go out with him after all. “And when would that be?” Again Wayne seemed eager but this time Tiara broke down and showed how eager she actually was. “How about tomorrow night?” as she said it she almost regretted it, she had gotten him to the place where he was like a puppet doing things and saying the things she wanted to hear but this time she he shown weakness. “Sounds great, I will pick you up at seven o’clock.” Wayne had hoped he would get a chance to say that to her, but he never knew if it would happen, but it didn’t and when he said it did it feel ever so good. “See you then.” She knew she had shown weakness and the only way around it was getting away while she could, she never really had a choice but she knew she needed to get away from him at this moment, something she did not want to do. As she hung up the phone, she realized she cut him off before he could say anything, in her eyes she was just out playing him, letting him know she was not waiting on every word he was going to say, instead she got the last words in. Although this is how she felt she did stare at the phone for a little while wondering what had just happened and wondering what the next words out of his mouth might have been. On the other end of the spectrum he stared at the phone, wondering why she had just cut him off. He look at it thinking that she was a bitch but for some reason he really felt intrigued by her, even though she had just done that to him. Neither one of them could wait until tomorrow when they would meet again in person once again. This time in a more personal environment, this time she would have a chance to find the real Wayne not some dude who was just trying to get his friends going, not the boy who was enticed by the other around, she wanted the real Wayne Russell.
