Date: 10.3.06 STRESSED. OUT. Every man has his breaking point. This is no secret to anyone. One is capable of existing in a state of persistent worry for only so long before psychological trauma sets in. The affable are turned into the infuriated; the calm into the livid; the hopeful into the despaired. This was certainly not beyond the reason of one Darcy Markson. In fact, it was this precise, persistent contemplation that tore Darcy away from his East Bay abode. The situation with Amy loomed over him like a deep, dark cloud fighting with every fiber to keep from dumping its contents straight on top of his ill-prepared head. Every minute the conflict went unresolved meant another minute that the cloud gained mass – and the potential for devastating perspiration all the more apparent. Darcy was in desperate need of a figurative umbrella – something that could divert the threatening anxiety harmlessly away from his unprotected scalp, charged with the vital task of guarding his careworn mind. In other words, Darcy needed to blow off some steam… and he knew only one way to do it. The crisp autumn wind took a gust into Darcy’s face as he exited his home. Of course, the briskness was nothing new or out of the ordinary for the East Bay native – not in a city where one could determine the locals from the tourists by who was naïve enough to wear T-shirts and shorts when the bay winds threatened to blow the silly, chilly sightseers all the way to Alcatraz. Yet Darcy exited his home with a frown, noting that the annual summer heat wave had finally come to a close, likely going into hibernation until sometime next spring. Had the Darcy of just three weeks prior had a chance to witness Darcy’s chagrin, he probably would have reached through the time-space continuum to punch his future self in the mouth. But deep down, he knew that the end of the heat wave meant the end of heat of another beautiful California summer. But he couldn’t complain that much. In fact, he couldn’t have asked for more perfect weather. The recently cooled air made his black Quiksilver pullover hoodie far less conspicuous than it would have been during the higher summer temperatures. Yet there was not a cloud to be found in the sky, leaving nothing to block the afternoon sun’s rays from bearing down upon the city. Thus, there was nothing the least bit conspicuous whatsoever about Darcy’s six-foot-plus frame ambling down the street, clad in aforementioned hooded sweatshirt and black, gold-trimmed aviator sunglasses. Indeed, one of Darcy’s family members would have a difficult time recognizing AWC’s Frontier Champion in his excellently executed attempt at going incognito. And it seemed that luck was continuing to smile on Darcy, as it wasn’t long before he found an acceptable target. Actually, acceptable was clearly an understatement – he found exactly what he was looking for. There, right on the sidewalk just outside the Clemons’ Corner Barber Shop, stood a cerulean baby stroller with frosty white wheels, complete with a tiny passenger. Darcy couldn’t believe his good fortune that the stroller appeared unattended. It would undoubtedly be easy pickings… Darcy crept slowly up toward the infant, basking all the unaware in the afternoon sunlight. On closer inspection, he found that the baby was a boy, probably no older than a year. Clutched tightly in his hand was the prize Darcy was after – a grape lollipop, of which the child happily sucked away in his tiny mouth. It was hardly an expensive looking confection; in fact, judging by the small, simple handle and square-shaped candy, the boy likely received it for free in exchange for being well-behaved at the doctor’s office. This was, of course, of no consequence. Darcy had no particular predilection for children’s candy, least of all anything grape-flavored, which to Darcy only tasted like cough medicine no matter what anybody said. What as about to transpire next was not so much for the end prize as it was for the experience of obtaining it. With a quickness rivaled only by that of a jungle cat, Darcy sprang forward and ripped the lollipop from the little boy’s clutches. He was of course no match for the vaunted Darcinator, and the look on his face resembled that of a helpless mouse, positively frightened that his life was about to end at the hands of a hungry, dominant lion. The shock soon gave way to instinct, however, and the child began to bawl. “THAT’S RIGHT, BITCH!” Darcy shouted in the baby’s face, causing it to wail even louder. “YOU JUST GOT RIPPED THE FUCK OFF! YOU BETTER THINK TWICE BEFORE PEEPIN’ THAT SHIT IN FRONT OF THE E.B.E., MOTHAFUCK!” The toddler reached in vain for his sucker, tears pouring out his eyes as Darcy walked away with an extra cock in his walk. He smirked as he tossed the stolen plunder over his shoulder, where it landed at the feet of a pair of horrified onlookers. Darcy caught a glimpse of them out of the corner of his eye, but laughed them off when it became clear that they intended only to point and stare as if they’d just witnessed a twelve car pileup, rather than take any action. Darcy moved along down the street, keeping his eyes peeled for any other small children foolish enough to be carrying candy. It wasn’t long before he discovered another potential target. Unfortunately, unlike the unattended child from before, this little girl was being escorted by a fair-complexioned woman who looked to be in her mid to late twenties. No doubt her mother, Darcy thought to himself. This would be slightly more challenging than robbing a baby all by his lonesome. It is well known in educated circles that human mothers protect their young with a ferocity that does well to match that of lions, tigers and Canadian geese alike. But unlike the latter animals commonly regarded as “wild,” this semi-attractive brunette likely shared the infamous weakness that almost all new mothers shared – they enjoyed prattling on, and on, and ON about their babies, from sunrise to set if given the chance. However, this was merely a backup plan. Darcy had absolutely no intentions of getting trapped into a conversation plagued with diaper genie preferences or the Great Formula vs. Breast Debate. Besides, Darcy knew a much better way to enhance the thrill of this little game…combining thievery with sneakery. Darcy caught yet another break when an elderly woman stopped to admire the brunette’s offspring, leaning in to make “adorable” baby noises in the little girl’s face that in fact made Darcy’s stomach churn. Nevertheless, the child’s protector was now occupied, and even from a distance Darcy could see the baby girl respond to the elder woman’s cooing by reaching out with her tiny hand to slap a large, rainbow-colored lollipop squarely across the bridge of the intruder’s nose. For her part, the grandmother (at least, she was probably a grandmother, judging by how infatuated she was with the little child) was curiously delighted by this, and made another round of cheerful coos in the violent baby’s direction before giving up the affair to engage in conversation with the child’s mother, the subject of which Darcy did not bother to take note. As far as he was concerned, they could be deeply engaged in philosophical debate over the 1917 Bolshevik Revolution – it made no difference. The guards were occupied, the child was isolated, and the ultimate prize was ripe for the taking. After crossing the street as non-chalantly as he could make himself appear, Darcy quickly ducked behind a nearby street bench to better assess the situation. The mother’s back was to him, taking her out of the equation, but a direct approach seemed impossible without the grandmother taking note, even as engaged in asinine baby-related conversation as she was. However, it seemed unlikely that she would take notice if he could zig-zag behind a series of stationary street-side objects that lied in between him and the baby. With the Mission Impossible theme playing loudly though his head, Darcy sprang into action. He moved forward in a crouch, pausing behind a brown trash receptacle. So far, so good, he noted. After determining the coast was again clear, Darcy zagged forward and to his right, stopping to hide behind a large chestnut tree. Still, it appeared that the two were unaware of his presence, allowing him the opportunity to creep even closer. Ever… so… slowly… Darcy slinked toward the stroller, amazed that the two ladies were so engrossed in their discussion that they didn’t notice the shady-looking man inching toward the child they evidently cared so damn much about. Darcy found refuge behind another park bench, which the three ladies crossing three generations stood just a few precious feet in front of. Here, Darcy dropped down to his stomach, intending to make an army crawl under the bench and somehow reach into the stroller, snag the little girl’s candy, and make the quick getaway before the womens’ jaws were finished gaping. Part one of the makeshift plan was flawlessly executed, as Darcy made his way under the antique-looking mahogany wood into grasping range of the child’s lollipop, which she now happily sucked away at with the older women no longer delving into her affairs. But things quickly went awry. Assuming that their conversation was indeed of the variety that would make him want to strike himself repeatedly in the skull with a frying pan if forced to listen to it for very long, Darcy paid little heed to their exchange. And this was a major error on his part; for just as his arm was outstretched toward the child’s confection, the grandmother had chosen that precise moment to take her leave of mother and child…leaving the mother free to look back at her baby, complete with her very own candy thief. “Umm…excuse me?” the young mother inquired of Darcy, who looked to be caught even more red-handed than O.J. at Burger King. It would take some very, very quick thinking to save him now… “Sorry, I- I just-” Darcy stammered. Okay, out of the blocks stumbling like a buffoon. Get it together, Darcy… Just then, Darcy noticed a small tag on the stroller near the right front wheel, simply reading “BABY TOWN.” It wasn’t even a sentence, but it gave Darcy his way out. After a deep breath, he gave a second attempt at explaining his actions. “I couldn’t help but notice this fine line of strollers that you selected,” Darcy said, thanking himself for remembering to wear sunglasses for this operation, saving him from giving himself away with involuntary shifty eyes, something that plagued him every time he tried to pass off a lie. “You see, I work at Baby Town, and I have to say, far and away, my favorite model is…” Uh oh. Talked myself into a dead end. Darcy frantically searched for the brand of this particular stroller, which proved difficult from his position with his stomach on the pavement. His eyes quickly darted around until he saw the word “JEEP” peering out from above the little girl’s head. Seriously? Jeep makes baby strollers? There was no time to question the matter at this juncture, however. Better just take it and run with it… “…this Ice Blue Jeep model here. Really, who better to trust on strollers than an auto company, you know? Kids deserve to ride in style just as much as anybody else,” Darcy finished, pulling himself to his feet. “I was just making sure everything was in order here.” It was a completely lame attempt, and Darcy fully expected the young mother to shoot him a look of disgust. But Lady Luck continued to smile on this candy thief… “Thanks!” the mother responded, her eyes lighting up. “Isn’t this cute little thing just to die for?” At that point she went into an in-depth explanation as to how THIS was the exact, perfect stroller for her little bundle of joy. Or something like that. Darcy was no longer listening, since he’d now broken the last line of defense between him and the sucker-holding little one. Without so much as a second thought, Darcy pounced forward and snatched the lollipop from the tiny girl. It was much larger than the doctor’s office crap he’d lifted off the unattended little boy, and it looked like the little girl was enjoying it far more as such. Well, not anymore… “BAM! YOU JUST GOT SERVED, SKANK-ASS! YEAHHHHH!” Darcy exclaimed, shouting his vociferous words just inches from the girl’s face, who immediately exploded into tears. Her mother looked as if her cerebellum just exploded as she looked on in sheer terror. “WHAT?! WHAT NOW, BITCH?!” Darcy turned to shout in the mother’s face, tearing off his aviators for added effect.. “I just punked her the FUCK out! What the FUCK you got, HUH?!” She had something, alright – Darcy turned his eyes just in time to see it. The pepper spray tore at his face like nothing he’d ever felt before, and he went down like a rock. “AGHpth! FUCK you, bitch! ERAAAAAGHHH!” he screamed in both pain and disgust. “Help! Police!” the mother screamed, desperately trying to attract the attention of a law enforcement officer. “This man is attacking my baby!” “I just wanted her fucking lollipop!” Darcy shouted in return, frenziedly trying to get the mace out of his eyes. “Jesus Christ!” Just then, Darcy heard a voice from the distance answer the mother’s cry of distress. “I’m on it, ma’am!” he shouted. Darcy couldn’t see what was happening, what with the pepper spray practically burning away his retinas, but he assumed that it was a cop, determined to spoil his fun. But what he heard next came completely out of left field… “Release the pumas!” Pumas? He can’t be serious… Yet Darcy’s eyes continued to betray him, leaving him completely unable to see just what the hell was going on. He silently cursed himself for removing his eye protection. He frantically rubbed at his eyes, desperate to restore his vision, and after a few frantic moments he was able to open his right eye, obtaining a blurry version of the scene of events. And it was then that his worst fears were confirmed. Standing right there, only a few paces away, stood a full-grown puma concolor, and he definitely did not look pleased. A small crowd had gathered by now to witness the attacker of the accosted baby, formerly an owner of a delicious lollipop, and his gruesome fate which was about to ensue. The puma’s stomach, just like Darcy’s, was practically touching the pavement of the sidewalk as it was coiled to attack. Darcy could have sworn he heard it growling as he readied himself for the inevitable strike… *** Darcy Markson awoke with a start, with his first sight upon rejoining the waking world being his black felis silvestris catus perched directly on his chest. As it happened, it seemed that the “growl” from the “puma” that Darcy “heard” was in fact only Reaper, doing his best either to encourage Darcy to rouse from his sleep which had lingered into the afternoon…or merely just being obnoxious, as cats are renowned for the world throughout. “Brrow?” came the noise from Reaper’s mouth, which almost seemed like a question judging by the rise in his tone at the end. Darcy was startled at first, both by the unexpected weight lying on his bare chest as well as the sight of Reaper’s face just inches from his own, a face that surely would have been that of a frightful monster to a much smaller, defenseless creature. But it took only several seconds for Darcy to realize where he was before the anxiety dropped. “Reaper, you asshole,” Darcy uttered with a moan, lifting the feline off of his chest and placing him gently to his left. “Aren’t there some mice cavorting around that need their lives ruined?” As if it were is cue, Reaper bounced from Darcy’s side and bolted for the bedroom door, disappearing in a flash.Darcy stretched, yawning as he looked over to the bedside alarm radio clock, which read “1:33” in bright neon-green numerals. Darcy suddenly found himself thanking his frisky feline friend for rousing him from his elongated slumber, as he considered sleeping past noon to be nothing more than a waste of time after he turned twenty-one. Darcy’s first move after pulling himself out of bed was for the dresser, where his cellphone lied resting in its charger. It seemed likely that by sleeping in for so long he’d missed at least a call or two, and his suspicions were confirmed by the giant “MISSED CALL” message eminating from the LG model’s front panel. He prayed to the powers that be that it wasn’t from Amy as he flipped the phone open to solve the mystery. It seemed that some of the luck from earlier in his dream had carried over to the real world, as the call was not from his estranged counterpart. Rather, it was from Johnny, and instantly Darcy knew why. He was supposed to meet his longtime childhood friend for an afternoon of racquetball in precisely fifteen minutes. With the local gym a good ten minute drive from his house, it didn’t appear that Darcy would be able to make it on time, and he dialed Johnny’s number to tell him so. “Hey, Johnny? What’s up, brother?” “Yeah, I got it. I just woke up, actually.” “No, no…I’ll be there. Just a little late, that’s all.” “Really? Right the fuck on. You know me all too well. I’ll catch you in a few.” *** He’d made a game of it, at least. Johnny “Hawk” Harrell spent his entire high school athletic career focusing almost exclusively on racquetball, and their frequent matches in the tiny, nearly completely white multi-purpose arena usually ended with Darcy’s tail between his legs. Darcy had been getting better as of late, and had reduced the gap of the typical 21-2 score to something like 21-12, but it had been years since Darcy had last defeated Johnny in the game of racquetball, back when they both picked up the sport from scratch solely before Darcy elected to focus on ice hockey and Greco-Roman wrestling. Johnny stuck with racquetball, never looked back, and the rest was history. He enjoyed toying with Darcy, as well. The last few points saw Johnny doing his best to make Darcy sprint from one side of the court to the other, laughing all the while as Darcy frantically tried to keep up. Impressively, he had, but when Johnny grew tired of the game he readied for the kill shot. With a remarkable blast, Johnny rocketed the ball off the front wall, where it came back directly toward Darcy’s head. Instinctively Darcy was able to throw up his racket to protect himself, but not well enough to rebound the ball, where it bounced up to the ceiling before falling haphazardly to the ground. Darcy groaned as Johnny doubled over with laughter. He waited for Johnny to finish laughing at his expense, only slightly annoyed that it went on for so long. But Johnny eventually pulled himself up, whereupon Darcy laid down the final challenge. “How bout one more round, best of five?” Darcy requested. “We’ll still change serves where appropriate, but points still count on side outs.” With a simple nod, Johnny indicated that he was more than happy to oblige in another visceral pounding of his longtime friend. “My serve, chump stain,” Johnny told him, with a wink. He received the small rubber ball from Darcy and positioned himself for the serve. “So… ‘No Love Lost’ match, huh?” Johnny inquired as he bounced the ball against the hardwood floor. Darcy nodded. “Yeah…I know it’s kind of lame. I couldn’t really think of a better name though, and it actually works on a couple of levels, if you think about it.” Johnny was evidently in no hurry, as he continued the interrogation. “Why don’t you just break up with her?” “Number one, she deserves what she has coming. And number two, I’ve been waiting for this match my entire career, and now we’ve got the added bonus of it being for all the marbles. Now how ‘bout you drop the inquisition and serve the damn ball?” Johnny shrugged, and soon acquiesced. The serve was low, but Darcy was all over it and returned it with a beautiful volley that stuck squarely in the space between front wall and floor, leaving Johnny with no opportunity for a return. DARCY 1-0 JOHNNY Darcy now had the serve, and as he stepped into position Johnny offered his response. “Deserves what? I hope you’re not talking about the whole Jane thing…I don’t think you can put that whole thing entirely on her shoulders.” Johnny’s words irritated Darcy, causing him to launch a less than stellar serve. Johnny capitalized, slamming the ball well past Darcy’s head. DARCY 1-1 JOHNNY “What do you mean, I can’t put it all on her?” Darcy inquired as he flipped the ball to Johnny. “She basically kidnapped her, and then lied to me about where she was, sending me on a wild goose chase that nearly got me killed!” Now clearly animated, Darcy was more than prepared for Johnny’s serve, slamming the return hard into the front wall. Johnny, being the expert racquetball player that he was, was in position for the return, setting up a series of incredible volleys. It would be Darcy that would emerge with the point, however, with a simply beautiful shot that hit three walls before dropping to the floor, without enough inertia for Johnny to be able to get to it in time. DARCY 2-1 JOHNNY “And another thing,” Darcy added as he took the serve. “In case you hadn’t heard, this whole thing BEGAN when Amy couldn’t handle me and Jane being in the same room together, and she damn near ripped Janey’s throat out. I’m telling you, Amy is unstable, and I can’t handle it anymore.” Darcy nailed the serve right on the head, sending Johnny on his heels for the return. He attempted to play it off the back wall, but his racket got just under the ball, sending it into the ceiling rather than the intended front wall. DARCY 3-1 JOHNNY Darcy smirked, as he not only retained the serve but had Johnny on the ropes. But Johnny was certainly no slouch, and he’d rallied back plenty of times against much higher-quality opponents to take games like these. “Unstable, yeah,” said Johnny, as he waited for Darcy’s serve. “But you still can’t put it all on her. Like I said, you’re an idiot for playing Mr. Vigilante like that. If anything, you should be thanking your lucky stars that Amy cared enough to intervene, or we wouldn’t even be having this conversation.” Once again, Johnny’s words got under Darcy’s skin, causing another subpar serve. Rather than closing in for the slam, Johnny made up for the last point by playing it off the back wall, this time effectively. Once again, the lack of inertia worked against his adversary, and Darcy had to sprint the length of the court to make it in time. He didn’t. DARCY 3-2 JOHNNY “In fact, what the hell’s your deal, anyway? Haven’t you been bitching ever since Jane about how every woman you’ve ever cared about went on to stab you in the back? Look at it this way – Amy might get you into trouble every now and again, but I don’t see any knife wounds in your backside. She legit cares about you, man.” Now it was Johnny’s turn to start things off, and he did so with authority. The serve was ankle high, and made four or five bounces on the return before Darcy could even move for it. DARCY 3-3 JOHNNY “You might have a point, if Amy’s ‘getting me into trouble’ didn’t result in guns being pointed in my fucking face,” Darcy replied. “This is for my own personal safety…it has nothing to do with how we feel about each other.” Johnny just shook his head, smiling. “Would you drop that, man? Once again, nobody forced you to go off on that bone-headed crusade. You can’t put that on anybody but you.” Another low serve came from the hands of Darcy’s friend. This time Darcy was able to get to it, but he was completely out of position for Johnny’s return. DARCY 3-4 JOHNNY “Look,” Johnny said to Darcy, as he positioned himself for what he hoped would be the final serve. I know you don’t like me inserting myself into your affairs, and I know I can’t stop you from going through with this match. But there’s something that needs to be said, and I can’t hold it in any longer…” Johnny bounced the ball a few times as he attempted to collect the right words to convey his message. Unlike Darcy, he wasn’t as gifted as speaking his mind as well as he liked. But he had the all-too-necessary advantage of being able to speak from his heart. After a deep breath, he did just that. “You may be content living your life alone. Always turning your back on the chance to be happy, growing into an old, bitter man. That might work for you, but it doesn’t work for the people that give half a damn about you. And make no mistake, Darcy…there are plenty of people who do. Believe me when I say this…it pains me, just as much as it pains everyone else to see you throw something away when you know, deep down inside, that you love this girl more than you’ve ever loved anybody. You might be able to stand it…but the rest of us can’t.” With that, Johnny followed through with his most powerful serve yet. Desperate not to let the game end like this, Darcy sprinted backward to make the return, which he somehow managed despite swinging the racket backward over his head. Johnny made his return cleanly, as did Darcy on the next attempt. Back and forth it went, one volley after another until Johnny made his final move. Running full speed toward the wall, he feigned a full-throttle slam, only to gently touch the ball with expert control. The ball lost all of its momentum, sliding gently to the ground. DARCY 3-5 JOHNNY Game – Johnny. Argument – Johnny. Completely at a loss – Darcy Markson.
