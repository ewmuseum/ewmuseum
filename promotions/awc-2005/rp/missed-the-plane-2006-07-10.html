Missed the Plane

10 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-10-06 It was Tuesday, July 4th. A clan of NASCAR shirt camouflage hat wearing Copenhagen chewing rednecks clustered at the back entrance of the Richmond Coliseum waiting on their Redneck Princess to emerge from the double doors. With their bellies filled with American beer and hot dogs, their drunken roars exploded when the doors opened. Teresa was tired and her body worn. All she wanted was a cold beer and a hot shower, then a warm bed. Once she stepped out, the crowd parted like the Red Sea and Teresa took a Budweiser handed to her by one of the drunken few, popped the tab open, took a drink and held the can over her head. Even though she had lost her match, in the eyes of her fan, she’s still their winner. Teresa guzzled the beer that resulted in more cheers and drunken roars along with “Teresa Got Screwed” chants. Now she’s had her cold beer, all she needed was a hot shower and a warm bed and maybe another beer. The parted flood of fans closed behind her as she made her way to the secured employee parking when an hand gripped her arm tightly. Thinking it was merely a fan, she jerked away before looking up. “You; I thought you were leaving.” “I’m not gone yet. C’mon, these hicks are drunk and there’s not telling what they’re capable of.” “Scott, I’m perfectly aware of what ‘hick’s’ are capable of.” Scott took Teresa’s arm and led her to her truck. Beside it was parked his black Jeep Cherokee. He opened the passenger side of his Jeep and led her in. “These are Virginia hicks Teresa, not Tennessee rednecks. These people are hell of a lot meaner and more violent. Right now, they are drunk and pissed ‘caused you lost to Vince Jones.” Teresa sighed and climbed into the Jeep. Scott shut the door and climbed in through the driver side. Teresa could see the crowd through the security across the parking lot. They weren’t going home any time soon. “What about my truck?” “After all the wrestler’s leave, security will force everyone else to leave, then the whole place will be locked up. Even the parking lot. See the fence?” Teresa nodded and soon they were back on the road. Teresa didn’t know where they were going. All she was thinking was a hot shower and a bed. She glanced to Scott and other thoughts came to mind, but she pushed them from her mind and stared out the window. “What time is Eric leaving tomarrow?” “Noon, why?” “I need you ready back to Tennessee don’t I?” Teresa sighed and reached inside her front pocket for her smokes. Scott watched her from the corner of his eye and quickly reached a hand and gently placed it over her. “Not now Teresa. We’re almost to my hotel.” No music was playing through the stereo this time. All this could be heard was the engine quietly purring and the raindrops beating against the windows. It had just started to rain. Teresa shot Scott a look but never moved his hand away. “If you think were gonna..” “Get real Teresa. You smell like shit. As soon as we get there, you’re taking a shower.” “Since when did you become my boss?” “Since when did you ever start leaving a show without hitting the showers first?” He was right. Teresa licked her lips and sat back. She always showered before she left the shows. Except tonight. “I got a lot on my mind right now ok? Besides, I can’t stand those bitches backstage. They all are either screwing around in the showers or smoking weed. I just want to be alone.” Nothing else was said for the remainder of the ride. A half hour later, the Jeep pulls into a Hampton Inn. He slides out, walks to the passenger side where Teresa already had her door open and was climbing out. He grabbed her duffel bag and walked her to his room upstairs. Inside the hotel room, or should I say suite, was a large sitting room with a couch, coffee table and a wet bar. A few chairs also were scattered across the carpeted room. A large window to an outdoor swimming pool gave enormous light into the room. To the right was a brass California King bed with a large dresser and a mirror. It was canopied with the thin satin-like red curtains to reveal satin sheets and huge fluffy pillows. Teresa didn’t give the bed a second glance when she found the bathroom to the left and went straight inside locking the door behind her. Scott dropped Teresa’s bag on the floor beside the door and headed for the wet bar. He poured himself a scotch and sipped while he stared at the locked door. An hour had passed and the bathroom door opens. Teresa stepped out wrapped in a towel. Scott was lounging on the bed watching TV. He watched as she walked across the room to get her duffel bag. “If you didn’t lock the door I could’ve left the bag in the bathroom.” Teresa looked up. Scott was lying against a stack of pillows. His well-toned upper body and muscular arms was hard to miss. He was wearing only boxers and looked as relaxed and comfortable as if he really wasn’t thinking of ripping that towel off her. Teresa averted her eyes and cleared her throat feeling similar urges flow through her veins. She grabbed her bag and returned to the bathroom. “Well, I did smell like shit.” “Honestly, you smelled worse. I was being nice. I just wonder how I’m gonna get that stench out of my Jeep.” “Screw you.” “You’d like to wouldn’t you?” Teresa’s face turned red and began to shut the door. Scott slid off the bed and walked to Teresa. His hand stopped the door from shutting completely. He had a serious expression across his face as he looked down on her. “Scott, what you think you’re..” She couldn’t finish because he had reopened the door and jerked her out into the main room. The towel fell to the floor and his lips met hers in a long kiss. It didn’t take long for Teresa to give in and wrap her arms around his neck. He then scoops her up and carries her to the bed. The phone rang. Teresa sat straight up in the bed. The phone rang again. She looked to her left to see Scott lying naked beside her with only a sheet covering him. The phone rangs a third time and she realized it’s her cell phone. She carefully slid out of the bed rushed to her duffel bag still in the bathroom. She grabbed the phone and flipped it open. “Yes?....Oh shit… I’m still in Virginia. There’s no way I could make it in time. Dammit.. I’m sorry Eric. Listen, be careful out there ok? I love you.” She hung up the phone and threw it. It shattered against the wall. She grabbed a set of clothes from her bag and began to dress. “Hey, who was that?” “Go to hell Scott!” Teresa fired as she slipped on her jeans. Scott stood at the door watching Teresa now dressed in his boxers. His long hair was all frayed and every which way. She reached for her t-shirt and slipped it over her head. “Do you know what time it is?” Realization settled into his green eyes and they grew wide. “Ahh hell..eleven o’clock.” “I’m gonna miss Eric leaving. He’s gonna war dammit!” Teresa is nearly in tears now despite her desperate attempts to hold them back. She pushes past him and opened the hotel door. “Where you going?” “My truck what do you think?” “We left it remember?” Scott had slipped a pair of jeans on and was working on his shoes. “Let me take you hope Teresa. You’re in no shape to drive. You could get a taxi to the coliseum but it’s a long drive to Tennessee.” “No! It’s your fault. You promised me!” Teresa had the door open. Scott rushed to her and grabbed her arm. “I didn’t promise you anything. This is your fault too.” He gave her a stern look and she knew he was right. She jerked her arm and he released her keeping her game face. “Damn you. Damn me for being so freaking weak!” Once again, Scott was left standing behind a door and Teresa stormed away. He walked to a window and watched as she used her cell to call a cab. He couldn’t go after her as he could’ve gone after her before, but his pride wouldn’t let him. As Teresa waited for her cab, she looked up and caught a glimpse of Scott standing at the window watching her. She quickly turned her head and the cab arrived. She didn’t give him a second look as the orange car drove off. She hated Scott for returning to her life. She hated him for how he made her feel. She hated him because she may never see her brother again. She hated him she told herself, but she refused to believe the truth.
