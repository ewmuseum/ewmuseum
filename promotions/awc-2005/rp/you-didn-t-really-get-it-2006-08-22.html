You didn't really get it...

22 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: august 22nd THE LUCKIEST ENDINGS... Jane looked at Amy with the most questioning glare ever seen on the planet earth when the redhead stormed through the room and began unlocking the handcuffs that bound her to a hot water pipe. Part of her was inclined to kick Aimz and run, but the disturbed look in her eyes pointed Jane in the other direction. ''Wha--'' Amy interrupted Jane's question, simply stating, ''Just shut up and come with me. This got way more important than you in a hurry.'' She grabbed Darcy's ex-girlfriend by the collar of her shirt and began leading her out, but Jane resisted momentarily. That's when she got re-aquainted with the wall of the warehouse, being slammed up against it with the full force of the other woman's scorn... ''LOOK,'' snarled Amy, shaking Jane and pressing her back against the wall, ''You're n--'' ''Enough of the tough crap! I don't want him back, so stop wasting your breath trying to muscle me away. You two are over when he finds out about this anyway, and that's your fa--'' Amy's fist made a sickening noise when it met Jane's skull. ''Jesus, you can pu--'' began Jane, ''Whoa now...'' She had paused mid-sentence because when she looked up from the ground that Aimz' hand had put her on, the redhead was standing in the exact same place she had been before. Only, this time, she had the business end of a 9mm looking at Jane. ''Scream if you want,'' Amy said shakily, obviously nervous handling a firearm, ''I'm not gonna shoot you, no matter what you do... but I'm banking on the fact that you aren't dumb enough to fuck with someone holding a gun, no matter how good their intentions are. Right?'' There was a nod. ''We can either go as a team and save Darcy's ass, or I can tie you back up and trade you for him,'' she said, ''And don't test me, because you know I'd do it.'' Jane nodded silently, and Amy grinned, ''Good. Then while you're on the fence about what I might do to you right now, let's have a conversation. I'll start; Your ex is currently on his way to the home of a mafia-affilliated gentleman in hopes of getting you,'' while she continued, it was with a VERY raised voice, ''So I think you can see where there's potential for a MAJOR FUCKING SCREW-UP here. But you don't need to worry, because I've been through this scenario before and usually...'' She lifted her shirt slightly, exposing her lower stomach and a scar near her hip, ''I'm the one who gets shot. So you need to trust me on this one, since I've been keeping you safe from those mafioso fucks for the past few days when I easily could've handed you over and spared everyone a whole lot of trouble. I'd have even shed a few tears at your funeral. Unfortunately, we're now looking at Darcy's funeral if we don't do something. He's on his way to some dive club right now, probably armed and ready to maim someone, because he doesn't want the police to find out due to it tarnishing your reputation with whatever governer fuckhead you're working for. He wants to save you from having everything about life taken from you, and he knows what it feels like because you already tried taking it from him once. I'm sure you know more about these guys than I do, but it's a good guess to say that he's not equipped to deal with them, no matter how nice his intentions are... am I right?'' Jane nodded once more, ''Yeah. But...'' ''No 'but's,'' said the redhead, tossing her gun to the ground in partial disgust with herself, but mostly hoping Jane would grow a little more trusting if her captor didn't have a deadly weapon aimed at her, ''Look, Jane... I'm...'' Amy's eyes welled with tears she was trying mercilessly to hold back, but couldn't quite help, ''Jesus... ALRIGHT... Darcy means the world to me. I know you don't love him and that there's nothing between you, and that actually is what's worrying me for once. I know that sounds weird, but you really have no reason to risk yourself trying to save him, especially given your current situation. I need you to care about this. You don't have to help me, but you've got to at least cooperate. I can't... I couldn't possibly imagine what I'd do if something happened to him, especially over this. You're a woman, so you know that I'm not nearly as rough and easy-off as I make myself out to be - I flat out can't live without him at this point. He left once but I'm not letting a god damned thing get between us now, even if I have to catch a bullet with my teeth. You can't trust me as far as you could throw me, and I get that... but I honestly didn't mean for any of this shit to go on, you MUST know that after spending a few hours with Jeph, because his brain just isn't here on earth. This has all been a fucking mess, but I can't let it get any bigger. I don't care if it costs me having him in my life... if he gets hurt or killed, I can't even sleep knowing he's living some sort of happy life somewhere.'' This time Jane blinked with surprise, nervousness gone, ''You really do love him. I thought for a while that he was just territory... like, just another slab of beef for your dating record...'' ''That's why you should never read the internet.'' They both spared a chuckle, feeling far more at ease. Amy had connected to Jane in the fact that they were both human and, not coincidentally, both badly in need of assistance from the other. While Jane didn't share Amy's exact investment in Darcy, she had herself and her family to protect. Seemingly all Aimz had was Darcy, and it was more than obvious that there really was something special between them, not just the convenience of something to sleep and travel with while they worked - which is what Jane had assumed during both incarnations of Darcy and Amy's relationship. ''Now, can we haul ass? I'm about eight steps ahead of Darcy, so I know exactly where he's about to turn up, and I'd like us to arrive before there's gunfire and police. Can we understand one another long enough to keep Darcy from fucking everyone's lives up royally?'' Jane sighed, ''Yes. Let's go.'' The second-hardest part was over with. Once they pulled Darcy's head out of his own ass, the VERY hardest part would be convincing him that Amy actually hadn't kidnapped or hurt Jane. Sure, some people can get away with murder... but it's much harder NOT to blame a jealous girlfriend. THE IN-BETWEEN ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Kindly direct your eyes HERE, to this week's Darcy Crisis installment, for the low-down on what happens between the two points of this story. In case you're lazy, here's a snippet to whet your pallette... Here goes nothing… Darcy brought his thumb down on the pistol’s hammer, with the familiar click of the hammer revealing that the Walther was just as ready as he was. Darcy moved ninety degrees about the concrete stairs to bring his back against the building, the door now just to his left. But as Darcy reached to rap his fist against said door, something came into play that he hadn’t quite counted on. It had not occurred to him in the slightest that not only was he being watched, but he’d been watched for quite some time by the advancing shadowy figure ever since he departed from Johnny’s. And only a fraction of a second before Darcy’s fist made contact with the cold hard steel, the figure made its move, appearing from seemingly out of nowhere to pin Darcy against the club’s back wall. He was so stunned that the Walther slipped out of his hand, spilling downward and coming to rest at his feet. As if it were the theme for the evening, Darcy’s attacker too wore a hoodie, of the black variety with a zipper that was pulled all the way up, and some sort of patch that Darcy could not identify. With the gun out of commission, the assailant used the hand pinning Darcy’s wrist that had previously held the firearm to pull the hood covering her face, revealing a full head of red hair. Mystery solved. ''Amy?! What the fuck?'' She leaned down in the blink of an eye to retrieve the gun he'd dropped, and slipped it under her belt with precision that surprised Darcy as she spoke. ''Jane's with me. I don't think we should hang around here for me to explain, but you don't have to do this.'' ''JANE?! But how?'' Darcy couldn’t help but shout. From around the corner another figure peered into view. First a face, then slowly came the rest of the silhouette into view. The streetlamps were insistent in providing an orange tint to everything, but as the figure slowly approached Darcy could make out her long, blonde hair and familiar slender figure. Instantly a countless amount of questions invaded Darcy’s mind, but he silently echoed Amy’s sentiments. With the hostage evidently already rescued, there was no need for a rescue mission, and the faster they stopped hanging around dangerous criminals who were most assuredly looking for Jane, the better. Darcy nodded, prompting Amy to release her grip. Jane waved them in her direction, but the two made it all of several paces before a new voice entered the scene. ''That’s far enough, ladies.'' The rear door burst open, and three men clad in expensive-looking black shirts adorned with gold chains appeared with the light from the room backing them. True to Darcy’s prediction, they were indeed all packing heat, and with their sights trained on Darcy and Amy it appeared that escape would be impossible. ''I have to say, I didn’t think it would be quite so easy,'' said one of the men, although Darcy was unable to make out exactly which one. ''But I do believe thanks are in order for delivering my prize to me. A token of my esteem, if you will…'' ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- MINUTES LATER... By the time Amy could draw the weapon she had in her belt, two men had her by the shoulders. Jane's attire made it obvious that she wasn't armed in the least, but Amy's baggy hoodie and jeans essentially screamed 'I've got an armory under here' to the wise criminals. One of the men had a hand squeezing the pressure points in the back of her neck to keep her fairly still as the other patted her down, slipping his hands 'accidentally' for a few extra feels, just to be sure. Jane had been dumb enough to try screaming, which got her gagged and knocked out. What really boiled Amy's blood wasn't even the groping at the hands of the sleazy gangster-types, but the moment when one of them knocked Darcy out with a shovel. When the metal connected with his skull, the sickening crack drove the little redhead to wriggle away from the men containing her, giving her just enough time to throw a punch to the eye of the man who'd brought the shovel down upon her boyfriend. Unfortunately, that had been one of those signature moments where Amy's heart got a mile ahead of her brain. The apparent partner of the lacky she'd just downed almost instantly had a pistol to her head, cocking the hammer and getting ready to squeeze the trigger. But, since most anonymous lackies in these types of situations like to get in a witty comment or two before they conduct their business, like you'd expect to see in Miami Vice, the boss was able to speak up before the trigger was pulled. ''Hold on there, Ray!'' he growled, ''Where's the fun in that? Take the broad inside.'' The cocked gun to her temple was enough to make Amy behave. Though she still cursed and commented under her breath, she didn't dare say or do enough to make the last sound she ever heard a gunshot. They dragged Jane inside at the same time, unconscious, and she struggled to see what they were doing with Darcy, but the door shut behind her too soon. When he was out of sight, she nearly threw up and tears began to grow behind her eyes as they slammed her into an office chair, promptly handcuffing both of her arms to the steel frame. She was getting desperate when she started speaking from beyond clenched teeth, ''I swear to God if you're hurting him, I'll fight hard enough that you'll have no CHOICE but to shoot me first. You're already pushing me to the ed--'' Within a split second, she was spitting blood in the aftermath of a stiff cuff to the cheek. ''Come on,'' she groaned, ''Just don't fuck him up. Cut my legs off if you want. Hell, rape me...'' The two lackies surrounding her and their boss all laughed aloud at the same time when the leader spoke up, ''I think we'll pass on that one. I've got a daughter your age... can't be any more than 17.'' ''Twenty three.'' ''... Jesus, doesn't that fucking fruitbasket throw you a bone once in a while or what? You're asking a bunch of fat Italians t--'' ''Seriously, can you just shoot me now or something? And if Darcy ends up dead, make sure you torture the blonde whore before you take me out.'' They scoffed, ''Pfft. Women.'' That's when two men brought a drowsy-looking Darcy inside. Jane was coming-to, and Amy was on the edge of getting shot for her mouth. Her mind blanked with the onset of a panic attack but by the time she blinked, her mouth was just closing and Darcy already had a gun in his hands. She could tell by the look in his eyes that it was an infamous mindfuck, where he was forced to choose which people walked away unscathed. The room now had only three of the strange men; The leader was next to Darcy with a smug grin, and each girl had one henchman behind her. Thankfully, she and Markson knew one another almost through and through... they both had the same idea in mind, and Amy glanced to the man beside Jane to confirm it with Darcy's slight nod 'Yes'. Jane was the weakest of them, and they were unbound at that point - most likely for the fun of the mob boss to see one of them writhing on the floor in death momentarily - so she was the least likely to be able to fight her way out of the room. That's why Darcy's gun was aimed at her. She was still too groggy to even know where she was, but it wasn't a scenario of natural selection where the weakest was picked off. The gun fired, and the man next to Jane dropped. In that moment, Amy crushed the groin of the man beside her with a heavy elbow sideways and grabbed the gun he immediately dropped. When it came to weaponry, however, she was passive. While Darcy gladly shot the kneecap of the boss as he drew a gun of his own, Amy hesitated and instead stomped into the face of the man beside her, knocking him out long enough for her to kick the chubby Italian leader in the nose so Darcy could take his gun. When they ran, Darcy had to sternly growl at Amy to retrieve Jane, who she was hoping he'd forget about long enough to be 'accidentally' left at the scene. Thankfully, Jane was already up and running on her own. They pressed through to the front of the empty club, foregoing the back door where they knew at least two extra men had been waiting. The sound of sirens grew blessedly close, when one sound silenced everything. A simple blast. Everybody seemed alright as they kept running, but Darcy turned when he heard Jane scream. It wasn't for herself, though - he looked downward to see Amy on the ground. The day began with Darcy being chastised for putting himself and Jane at fatal risk - a risk he was begrudgingly able to accept, given the circumstances. But never once did Darcy dream that Amy would get caught up in all of this. This was a risk he was completely unable to accept, and he likely never would have set foot anywhere near this club if he knew that he'd see what lay before his eyes in the moment. Everything seemed to move in slow motion as he rushed to his her aid. Suddenly, the man who was wary to harm even a fly outside the ring was firing wildly in the direction of her assailants. So overcome with rage, it was impossible to tell if he was actually hitting the gunmen he was aiming for as he dragged Amy to relative safety. ''AMY! Oh God, Amy! Come on, baby, show me some life,'' he pleaded with her, frantically checking for a pulse. Just as he felt her hear, a policeman ran around the corner and tackled the man who he'd watched shoot the redhead. Police cars pulled just feet from Amy, Darcy and a fairly concerned Jane, who was pleading with three officers to stop aiming their weapons in Darcy's direction. While Darcy refused to stand up or leave Amy's side at all, he gladly relinquished his gun to an officer. ''Alright,'' said one of the officers, putting a hand on Darcy's shoulder, ''Let's get this cleaned up.'' A SLIGHT RETURN... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- An IV, painkiller-induced double-vision, a searing pain in her side... Amy blinked her eyes halfway open, darting them around the room once before she locked them on Darcy. He was half-asleep, head laying beside her shoulder as he slouched from the chair next to her bed. A few years before, this was the exact scene between Amy Campbell and Brandon Roche - her thenandneveragain boyfriend at the time. She groaned loudly, ''God, not this again...'' ''I'll bet it hurts like a bitch,'' Darcy began, ''But you're lucky... it only grazed you with fragments. We'll get this IV outta you, and you out of the hospital by tomorrow if you're nice to the nurses.'' ...Thank God for mob henchmen having aim as bad as Stormtroopers.
