A great uncle I only saw a few times a year, I can hardly grieve for. My great aunt died a couple of weeks ago, and I didn't cry... but my surrogate uncle - a friend of my father's - passed on from a heart attack two weeks prior, and I was devastated. You know that dirty little feeling that you really SHOULD be caring more than you do? You a young kid, on your way to a funeral or getting dressed for it or whatever, and you take a moment to pause. It's a little shimmer of self-doubt. Why aren't you more upset? Did this person not deserve the same affection you'd give someone you might've called twice a week? It's perverse and 'wrong' to act like you aren't in complete mourning, so you bury that little sense of wonder until the next funeral, when you get to push it down again and again and again until someday you're to the point I'm at. Most of the time, you're travelling with little more than a memory of the time you built the tire swing in your cousin's yard and the adults were gathered on the other side of the pool for a cookout. What was Uncle Whatshisface wearing that day? You've got no clue, all you remember is the tire swing... because that's what mattered then. Now, you're having to go back and add extra meaning to already half-emptied memories because someone in them who you didn't even remember being present ANYWAY isn't gonna be there to make any more with you. The smoke makes her a little lightheaded and even a bit cheerier, but she's relentless in her journey to slay the Mad Lady. With a renewed viciousness, she forces the war horse to pound the ashen pathway in the name of (self)righteousness and all that is good. Nothing will stop her now. With her sights set on the cemetary, our hero rears her mount and proceeds full-speed, even past the Wraiths and Skeletons. She's quick enough for their attention to have gone in another direction even as they notice her. The holiest of Paladins tears her plate helm off to reveal sweat-soaked streaks of red hair, stinging her eyes with their moisture. It doesn't phase her, she's almost there. That's when she sees it; The four corners of the Mady Lady's home. She must dismount and shoo the horse away before the fight, and does so before she charges in with nothing left to protect her skull - completely oblivious to the danger after a thousand deaths died along the way to this moment. Mad Lady Ellise says: 'You can't do it! My sash is my own! None will champion this belt!' Our hero could say any number of things. She's stored up a million retorts after hearing the Mad Lady shouting and groaning threats from miles away, but the moment they're in is far too important for her to be wasting time with negative talk or sinister remarks. She's better than that. The Mad Lady strikes first, clawing at our hero's face with a gnarled hand that once clung obsessively to a pipe. This woman's youth was far from kind to her, but our hero still recalls her own enough to reconfirm to herself that she's got what it takes to win this fight, both mentally and physically. As the Mad Lady casts a sarcastic spell in an attempt to distract our hero, she persists with a mental fortitude rarely seen. Instead of letting the witch Ellise distract her, she lets thoughts of her husband D'Arcy The Marksman consume her - keeping her pushing on in the battle. He won't sleep in an empty bed again tonight. With one fell swoop, our hero slashes the ashen heart of the Mad Lady Ellise, causing the ghost witch to moan and cry with the sorrow of all of her lost vitality. It ends there - she falls to the ground in a pile of bones, and the golden belt that had lashed her hips to her spine shines out among the remains. Our glorious hero is a moment from possessing the sash she needs to reach the next mantle of life. She removes one leather glove and reaches for it triumphantly... ''Unnnngh...'' Amy's crusted eyes crept open in the light of 8 AM, peeking through the window to warm her face. The computer was viciously beeping at her and, when she lifted her head, the majority of the keyboard was etched into her cheek... not to mention the fact that the space bar was sticky with drool. She questioned her sobriety at first, but then looked up. World of Warcraft was open, right along with a 'Disconnected From Server' notice. In that moment, she remembered playing well past Darcy going to bed with Dave Gibson. They'd been indulging their nerdiness and going on some mad adventure within the game when she'd gotten a little too sleepy. Her sleeping schedule leading up to one of the fights of her life had interrupted her favorite hobby. It was a battle between wrestling and geekdom, and wrestling had won... for once. Darcy was apparently still in bed, or already gone to the gym for his morning warm-up. She looked behind her groggily, staring away from the PC and straight to the kitchen area - the empty kitchen area. He didn't even leave her breakfast? ''Asshole,'' she groaned, stretching out her shoulders, ''Probably took pictures of me laying there, too...'' Regardless, it was time to start her training day... ... And this time she wouldn't have a sword, horse or any magical powers - despite how helpful that combination would be when it came down to getting Ellis Nash's belt. Follow Me -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Present DAY! Amy had followed Chainz onto the train, then off of it. He'd stopped in a washroom for a moment, so she ducked into the ladies' bathroom in an attempt not to be seen. She busied herself, washing her hands for no real reason - aside from the dirty feeling being within yards of that man left with anybody - and realized, when she looked into the mirror, that her attempt not to be seen had failed. Miserably. Michael Sloan laughed, ''This is cute, two young kids alone in a train station bathroom during their crazy adventures through Europe...'' In a panic, Aimz looked around only to notice that he'd closed the door and they were very much alone. He knew that she had far too much pride to scream like a normal woman, and took advantage of that silence by taking a moment to speak before he pummeled her appropriately. ''You look worried, baby,'' he sneered, ''Don't be embarassed, I saw you following me all the way back at the airport. You think they don't have reflective surfaces in Norway or something? You're the first, but also worst fucking stalker I've ever had.'' Chainz smiled, ''But don't worry, you'll get better with time. Besides, stalking really gets me off. It's so... forbidden, you know?'' ''Don't try to be intellectual. I know you're fucking trash, so stop pretending.'' ''You know, it takes guts to say that to me. But it's not guts, it's your hair... my mother had red hair. And that dirty-talking mouth... I know it's done more than just idle chit-chat...'' She tried to stay brave, but a no-holds-barred encounter with this man hadn't been what she'd intended to do to keep him out of her match. By the time she could blink, he was choking her - making sure to press a weakened portion of her back against the sink's edge by adding pressure to her midrift with his groin. He was making it very clear that things could end in rape just as easily as violence. ''Why were you following me? Hoping I'd rent a car so you could cut the brakes? Or maybe you just wanted to see where I was staying... bring a hooker or two up to my room for some fun I'm sure that asshole with a girl's name who you fuck couldn't even imagine.'' Amy glared at him, almost uncaring that her air supply was running low. Whatever he was going to do, she didn't really want to be awake for. As basic and animal as Michael was, he wasn't stupid like she assumed. He knew her purpose with one look in her eyes. ''You don't want me getting involved in that match, do ya?'' he chuckled, ''You knew getting my attention before going for a title wasn't a smart thing to do, but I understand that you couldn't resist. Now, though, you want me out of the equation...'' Sloan continued, ''It breaks my heart, baby. Really does.'' He relented his grip on her throat slightly, allowing for the tiny redhead to breathe. Even he was surprised that she hadn't already retaliated, but she'd learned at the Fan Festival that any normal beating would have no effect on him. He continued, ''But there'll be plenty of time for you and me after you win that title. It just ups the stakes a little, doesn't it?'' That man was the one and only in AWC, or anywhere in recent memory, who had made her truly afraid. Nothing was normal about him. That's why she didn't fight back... the time wasn't right. She couldn't win this encounter. It could be considered a victory if she got out of it without bleeding. To her surprise, Michael let go of her completely, tossing her against the tiled wall. He spit near her feet in disappointment before he finished his words... ''Just wait for me when you take Nash out, or I'll catch up real fast. I might just let you wear that little title backwards while I give you a celebratory anal fisting in your locker shower.'' Before he left the room, Amy spoke a simple ''Fuck you'' between gasps for breathe. His only reply was ''You will.''. Base, at best... but at least he was leaving. That was the first time in recent years that Amy had honestly feared for herself. That man wasn't just a bad actor playing a character, he seemed to live it. Right down to flipping through a hardcore porn magazine in the airport, in plain sight of children. He was the first foe she believed could easily do to her the things he promised, and that was no small order. Following him, planning anything... that had been the worst idea. It was a mistake she couldn't make again. She underestimated the creature who'd at first seemed like little more than glorified trailer trash. Now, he was a very real threat. When she finished with Nash, she truly believed he'd be waiting... and, honestly? Even with Darcy, she'd have trouble stomaching another encounter with Michael Sloan, in or out of a ring. With or without characters and fourth walls and all of those insider boundaries. He didn't live within those rules, and now neither could she. It was a war she couldn't win. At least, though, she'd be fighting it with a golden shield around her waist. If Adam Dick could melt his title into a crown, maybe she could get a chastity belt worked out of the Frontier title -one that only Darcy could hold the key to. Hopefully Chainz doesn't know how to pick locks. She leaned against the wall and shuddered, but soon stood and tried washing the redness away from her throat. Darcy would be waiting at the hotel, maybe she could feel cleaner then. She suddenly felt like she needed that honest male presence, since her worst enemies amounted to a pothead chick and a guy who probably sold his testicles just to watch a horse fuck a woman. Even if she did win that title, and things seemed to look up... Amy knew she'd accidentally unlocked a real beast, and Sloan would be waiting in the gutter for her regardless of how great things got. She stood up shakily and made sure to look over her shoulder every second after she left that room to go to the hotel. From rapist murderers to girls who pretended to be indifferent for the sake of not showing any real emotion or weakness, life was getting REALLY interesting. Even winning a title wouldn't bring the riot down. ... And that was honestly the most disappointing thing since Tom Cruise not dying in the end of Mission: Impossible.
