Vincent Webb - Revolation

23 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/23/05 I look at her face, her mouth is left agape. I guess that it’s better for both of us now that the story’s out. I look at the clock. Nearly one forty-five. I’ve been droning on and on about this for about a half an hour… I look back at Alice. She simply stares back at me. I assume she’s astonished, but I can’t quiet tell. I clear my throat. “Alice?” “Wait. If you’re Xandrous’ son, then… the only person I can think of that would try to kill you would be Webb. Vincent Webb. Is that who is doing all this?” she asks. Dead on. All I do is simply nod. “So, you’re saying that one of the most dangerous men in the mafia are after YOU?” I nod, but then I stop dead. “What? Mafia?” I ask, confused. “Well, yeah. Webb runs one of the most malicious mafias that’s been around in years. Somehow they got connections with the police, so they can get away with... anything. That’s how they got you out, Steven,” she says, seemingly piecing together my mystery. “But why would they want me out?” I ask, unable to make a connection. She shrugs. “Oh…” I say, quietly. “What?” she says, hearing me. “When I got out, I got a phone call from him about a month later, which would be a little less than a week ago. He tried to bring me in with him. He was going to use me…” I say, surprised by the revelation. Everything becomes so clear. Why he gave me the money, how he got the photos… and most of all, who did this to me. “He was the one who organized my attempted murder. And when they found out I wasn’t dead, they were going to try again. Then they heard that my father was dead and that I was on the run. So they tried to bring me in.” “But how did they figure out that you had amnesia?” she asks. “You said they had deep connections, right?” She nods. “Maybe it was one of those doctors or something. But when I turned my back on them, they decided they had to finish me.” Now it makes sense… ‘Oh, how the pedigree crumbles…’ I look up at her. “Alice, what did my family do to the Webb family?” She looks at me, astonished. “You mean you haven’t found out yet?” I shake my head. “Well, there was a guy named Eric Xandrous, known only to the media at that time as Eric X of course, who would always sign his murder scenes as ‘Eric X’. He made the mistakes of killing a Webb. It was like our own little World War Three. Murders left and right. Eric, by that time, had developed his own mafia, which he called the X Pedigree. Strange name, I know. But he didn’t want his last name getting out. He didn’t want the Xandrous ‘legacy’, I’d guess you’d call it, to carry on to his true pedigree. You. Your father. But Vincent Webb had uncovered that secret, and that propelled the war even further.” “So, how do I end it?” I ask, worried. “Where did it start?” she asks, cryptically telling me the answer. “Vincent Webb…” I say to myself. “Yes?” that familiar voice says, nearing the room, happiness seething through. Speak of the devil. Alice lets out a quiet cry and quickly walks to the back of the room. Vincent laughs at the site. “Don’t worry, my dear. I’m not here to kill you. And I’m not here to kill Steven. I think he did that on his own. Nearly talked himself to death.” I double-take, confused. “Yeah, I was listening. I see that you figured every piece of the puzzle.” He walks nearer to me, and sits at the edge of the bed. “But, you never know kid. You can always change the puzzle around. If you join with me, you, and your little girlfriend, will be safe and sound. No harm done. Well, no more harm done,” he says, looking around the room. He looks at me, smiling. “So what do you say, Steve? Going to change you mind?” I look straight at him, my emotion unchanging. “Fuck you.” He simply nods. “Alright. Take the hard road, that’s all fine and good. But here, you might need this.” He pulls a handgun from his pocket and tosses it onto my bed. He walks out of the room, stopping at the door. “Just… think about it kid. And when you do, just give me a call.” He winks at me and walks off. Smart ass motherfucker… I turn towards Alice, my worries growing. She shrugs. “Alice?” I ask, not sure what to do. She steps toward me. “Steven, I think you know what you got to do. He’s playing you. You’re a mere pawn in his sick little game,” she says, as through it were a written speech. “If he has you, he controls everything. He can use you to kill people that used to be your friends or family. And you wouldn’t even know. Don’t let him get control…” I look down, but she gently pulls me by my cheek to face her. “Do what is right.” I nod. “I will. I promise you that,” I say with sincerity. But what IS the right thing? “I’m going to give you some time to think I guess. Just… call me up, or get someone to call me up, later. I’ll leave my number with your attendant,” she says, smiling. I nod grimly. What is the right thing?
