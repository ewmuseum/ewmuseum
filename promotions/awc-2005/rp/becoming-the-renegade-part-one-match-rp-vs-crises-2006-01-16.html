Becoming The Renegade [Part One - Match RP Vs. Crises]

16 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/16 What's she doing here? He'd arrived at AWC's Christmas Eve Party Fashionably late. He didn't like being on time, he liked to operate from his own schedule. He hadn't been greeted, or even seen. He prided himself on being stealth, especially when he didn't want to be noticed. He was at the party for ONE reason, not to mingle, not to further his career, but to work out a few details on his newely signed contract. Heading through the dim hallway he saw her, enjoying some punch or another sort of drink. Teresa Thomas. Having just left HSW himself, Jason Warr was pretty familar with the Redneck Princess. In fact, he'd been curious as to why she'd cancelled her appearence to face him only a few nights prior. He'd given up assuming she'd come down with the flu or a cold, and walked out himself. Now there she stood, not 20 Feet away, laughing and talking it up with a couple friends. Had he not been keeping a low profile, he might have made a scene. Publicly adressed her, something like that. However, he didn't want the press to know of his joining AWC until his first match. He didn't feel like answering any questions, giving interviews, signing autographs.. after years of pro wrestling he'd come to value his privacy. He didn't want to be a huge media sell out, probably why he didn't go to a lot of autograph signings or photoshoots. Also the reason he didn't have any merchandise sold. The fans would see him when he was good and ready. He was nearly in the arena's main office to do what he'd come for, when he had a change of heart. ---------- Pete Sanders never missed AWC when it came into town. He proclaimed himself to be AWC's greatest fan, coming to every show he could, in the best seats he could afford. For the Christmas Show, he was right up front. Camera at the ready, he was currently enjoying Teresa Thomas and her arguement with the choir. 'I'll have to put these and some sound bytes on my fansite' he uttered to himself, snapping some pics and enjoying every minute of the event Out of nowhere, a giant gift was lowered from backstage to the ring. Sanders was ready for whatever it might be. He certainly didn't expect HSW's Jason Warr to pop out, and Chase Thomas to the back. 'Holy Crap' was the only thing he could say, before snapping ten or so photographs, pulling out his cell phone, and calling the nearest Pro Wrestling news source. ---------- He'd chased her for a while before she managed to give him the slip. He'd pretty much screwed himself, all those flashbulbs exploding in the arena, people would have pics of him in an AWC ring, and the press would be all over him in a matter of hours. Or so he thought. Pushing his way out the back door, he was greeted my several rogue photographers, news camera's, fans. He slammed the door shut and ducked into the close by mens room, pulling the cell phone from his pocket and locking the door. After some hasty dialing he began to speak calmy. 'Angela, it's me ..It doesn't matter where, but I need you to do something. Just pack me a bag, throw in some money and clothes, and get me a plane ticket to Nashville. Put the bag in a deposit box, try for #10, and leave the key at will call ..I'll be fine, I just need to take care of something ..No, I'll go alone. I need to lay low for a while, and I have someone to find. Just fly back to Manhatten, call if you need me ..I should be back in time for the new year, but it depends. I don't have much time, they'll find me soon, I love you babe, bye'. He folded the phone and placed it back in his jacket. Nearing the door, he pressed it open. Only a few of the people he'd been avoiding remained, the rest probably took off deeper into the arena. Without much thought he made a run for the door. He was noticed immediately, but his athletic body carried him away fast enough. ---------- She'd betrayed HSW by leaving the way she did. But that's not the part that bothered me. She'd avoided me, skipped out on our match. I became obsessed, not sure if I could beat her or not. My usual confidence was scarce at the time, I had something to prove. Outing myself to all those smarks was probably a bad idea, I know. So I had my Fiance pack me a bag, I was going to find Teresa Thomas if I had to personally question each and every family member she had. She wouldn't avoid me, again. My flight to Nashville was uneventful, and upon landing I began to look around. I only knew a few things, One She lived in the area, and two it was on some farm. Right, So I didn't know anything. That could be changed, in due time. ---------- He stood in the Parking Lot of Big Willy's World of Liquor. Not a subtle name, not a subtle venue, yet in blended nicely with the surroundings. Gravel parking lot, wooden building. Warr stood, gravel crunching beneath the soles of his sneakers, wearing cargo pants and pullover Sweatshirt. One small bag slung over his shoulder, he'd sat for a couple nights and observed the outskirts of Nashville. Checked into a local hotel under an assumed name, he'd come to find out this was -THE- place to buy booze in the county. Not so much because of it's presentation, but because 'Willy' made real Tennessee Moonshine in the basement. He had tons of customers. Warr watched from the woods across the street, as cars constantly came in and out of the shop, between the hours of 10 and 11 PM. The rest of the time, it was completely empty, bar the odd customer looking to fill his tank with gas. It was 8 In the morning, the shop had been open for a few hours. The same man was behind the counter all the time, a large mountain man he assumed was Big Willy. Stepping through the doors, he approached the man, staring right in his direction. Big Willy put down his newspaper. 'Can ah help yew son?' he said, his gritty accent breaking the ominous silence as Warr furtherly approached. 'I'm here to ask you a few questions,' he said. The boy wasn't from around here, thought Big Willy. His appearence was completely out of the norm, from baggy clothes to blue hair. He wasn't scared, nervous.. he lacked any sort of emotion. Big Willy didn't need to waste his time, probably another activist onto his shady 'business' dealings. 'Go Fuck yerself, Hoss' he said lifting his paper. With the news obstruction blocking his vision, he didn't see as Warr launched his right hand in Willy's direction. Grasping Willy's left hand, he pulled his arm behind his back and slammed him gut first onto the cold table, applying pressure to Willys left arm. 'ARGHH WHUT THE FUHCK YEW SONOF-' he was cut off as Warr wrenched his elbow. 'AHRGHRHHHH,' he screamed. Warr remained still, twisting Willy's arm into a pretzel, not saying a word. 'Stop! Please! Argh Yew a cop?'. 'No, I'm not a cop,' Warr said, completely calm during the entire assault. 'I just need some questions answered.' 'Let mah arm Go- ARGH!' he said, only for Warr to twist harder. 'Where's Teresa Thomas?' 'Who?' said Willy, his breathing still labored. 'You know exactly who I'm talking about, and if you don't know where she is, I'm sure you know some of her friends, or family.' he said, maintaining his grip. Big Willy was as tough as they came around these parts, but even he couldn't take this much pain. 'The Thomas Farm is in the next town, it'a about 10 Miles north of here.' Defeated, Big Willy let out a breath, only to notice the hold hadn't been released. 'That your truck?' said Warr, pointing to the outside window at a beat up Ford in the parking lot'. 'Yah it is.' 'Where are the keys to to it?' 'Oh FUCK YEW, beatin' me up and now yer stealin' mah ford?' said Willy, beads of sweat forming on his brow. 'Of course not, you're giving it to me,' he said. Before giving Willy's arm a HUGE pull, causing the big man to scream loudly. 'YOU'RE' twist. 'GOING' twist. 'TO LET ME HAVE IT.' pull. 'OUT OF THE KINDESS' More Wrenching. 'OF YOUR FUCKING HEART' Warr shouted, tired of this conversation. Big Willy motioned to a key chain on the table, which Warr picked up, and took off towards the door. He knew he wouldn't have much time before the giant redneck pulled out his gun and started firing, so he threw the piece of crap into gear and took off down the road. ---------- Several Hours after the incident at Willy's, he stood in the forest outside of a ranch. Through the Magnified view of Warr's binoculars we see the front door reads 'The Thomas's.' He'd been surveying the property since he'd arrived that morning, and he planned to make his move soon. From his vantage point he'd seen both an old woman and large man arrive home with Groceries. Tucking his spy thing into his bag, he made his way onto the property, and as opposed to using a covert method of entry, he simply knocked on the door. Nothing. Not one for patience, after about 30 seconds Warr slammed his shoulder into the door, breaking the deadbolt and throwing it open. He knew he'd have to be careful, and that he'd been heard, so he stood in the doorway preparing himself for the fight. 'What the Heck was that?' he heard coming from the Living Room. He assumed it was the young man, and as Teresa's brother Eric entered Warr's view, Jason threw a punch that connected with Eric's head. Falling the the ground of the entryway, Eric Thomas laid dazed and nearly out. The trained and precise Punch at taken a lot out of the ex Military enlistee. It was about that time Mrs. Thomas found the two, now engaged in a full on Brawl. The larger Eric Thomas lifted Warr right off his feet, throwing him through onto the ground. Sweeping out his legs, Warr regained his advantage, throwing trained kicks at Eric's head. After a few, Eric laid on the ground, unconcious as Mrs. Thomas screamed. 'I'm sorry if I lack the characteristics of a Southern Gentleman, but will you shut the fuck up?' Warr said, shocking the older woman. She could do nothing as Warr lifted Eric into a chair grabbing rope from his bag and tying his hands behind it. Mrs. Thomas could do nothing but watch. 'Go Get me a Glass of Water, Now' Said Warr yelling at Mrs. Thomas, who quickly scurried into the kitchen. The sound of water running was heard as Warr finished tying up Eric. As Mrs. Thomas returned, Warr grabbed the Water. 'Listen,' he said 'I don't want any trouble here. I wish I wouldn't have had to do what I did, but I did. I just want to ask your son a few questions, have a seat, don't make it difficult for me'. He finished, tossing the Water onto the face of Eric Thomas. As Teresa Thomas's brother came to, blood running down his black right eye, the real part of the investigation began. Coughing, Eric began to tell his tale.. -TO BE CONTINUED-
