On Your Feet

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/25/06 Anton wraps the gauze up around his wrist, snaking it around his thumb and encasing the joint tightly. He sits alone, the dark oppressing the little light that is present. He feels naked but he isn't—he's dressed in his black fighting shorts and a loose black tanktop. His eyes are glued to his wrist, ostensibly focused on the task at hand. There was no way he could really focus, though. Everything that had happened, everything that continued to happen, plagued him. He continued to wind upwards. He was filled with rage, with the desire to wound Luis. The man was a lunatic. He was everything evil in the world—greed, lust, violence, addiction—all rolled up into a greasy little freak with a heavy accent. Luis was nothing but a ball of human sludge, a cesspool of decadence that only warranted being cleansed by… well, by more of what he wallowed in. The only thing that would keep him at bay was his addiction. The only thing that would sedate him was his lust. The only thing that would occupy him was his greed. And the only thing that could eliminate him was violence. Anton missed with his gauze and sighed as he began the careful process of unwinding it again. Of course, he couldn't just storm in and beat Luis to death. He would be just as bad as the man. Besides, despite all that Luis had been looking out for himself, Luis had also looked out for Anton. Luis had made it possible for him to be in the Atlantic Wrestling Club, he had put the pieces together that had led him to the title shots he had gotten, to eventually winning the Frontier title. In a way, Anton relied on Luis more than he wanted to admit. Anton desperately wanted to think that what Luis had done to Amber was just a lapse. It was a nasty lapse, yes, and Luis had gotten just what he deserved, but did he deserve more? Did he deserve to be crushed beyond recognition, to be whittled down to the barest strand and left alive only to suffer? Anton didn't think anybody really deserved that, no matter how much people might talk. He had to think clearly about this. Luis had committed a crime, but Anton couldn't rectify it by committing another. Anton began to wind back up again, the white gauze pulled tight around his wrist. And why shouldn't he? The man had assaulted his girlfriend! The man never learned, not ever. Even when Anton thrashed him, even when Anton beat him within an inch of his life, he never learned. Even now, Luis was simply breathing in all that air to come back again. He lay in his hospital bed recovering to the point where he could misstep again and Anton could come so close to snapping his neck once again, just to go back and repeat the cycle. Yet, what would happen if Anton finally did snap Luis's neck? If Anton dispatched Luis for good, either verbally or physically. Would Luis learn then? Would Anton even care if he did? Anton shouldn't care. He knew he shouldn't. Luis wasn't worth worrying about. He wasn’t worth this mental debate or the hassle that would come from wasting him just so far again. The obvious solution would be to end him. Cold logic was what Anton wanted and that was exactly what it told him. Destroy Luis. The gauze caught around Anton's thumb and he grunted, beginning to unwind again. He couldn't take this lightly. He wanted to go at it full-tilt, to make his decision, to attack right now, but he couldn't. Nothing went well that way. He had to breathe, to think things through. He had to make sure he was making the right decision because fucking Luis up beyond all recognition might not be the right one. Cold logic told him that it was, but cold logic wasn't always right. Sometimes he needed to figure things out so they made sense to his emotion-wracked brain. Amber wanted him to get rid of Luis in any way possible but Amber wasn't always right. She had been right enough that Luis was a terrible, horrible person, but she hadn't feared him before. Now she was afraid of the Venezuelan and that obviously clouded her judgment. Cold logic told him to destroy Luis, but Amber did, too, and he didn't trust that Amber was level-headed enough to make a clear decision on this. Could cold logic be biased towards someone? Anton wasn't going to assume that it couldn't be. Wound up. Anton wanted to smash his fist into Luis's face, to show him blood, to yank mercy away from him and show him exactly why touching Amber was a bad idea. Wound down. Anton had to think things through, to make sure they made sense. To make sure they were right. Nothing could be done rashly. Wound up. Anton was going to gut Luis with his bare knuckles. Wound down. Anton needed to take deeper breaths and calm down. Wound up. Maim. Wound down. Think. Wound up. Kill. Wound down. Breathe. breathe… Anton felt hands on his shoulders and he glanced upwards. Amber stood behind him, her hair falling down over her face and covering one of her eyes. She was dressed in a sheer nightshift. Anton didn't take the time to admire her curves. She began to knead her fingertips and thumbs into his shoulders, closing her eyes and murmuring softly. Anton let his head slump forward a bit. His hand dropped off of the gauze, letting the long end dangle from his wrist. Relaxation spread throughout his back and arms. ''What do you want?'' Anton asked Amber after a bit of letting her simply massage him. Her eyebrow perked up, but Anton didn't rescind the question. ''I want you to settle down,'' Amber said after a light sigh. ''That's all. You're so worked up, hun, and you're just going to train. You don't need to be so tense.'' She leaned over his shoulder, her cheek next to his temple. He felt her breath on the side of his face. He didn't have to look at her to know that she was giving him a sympathetic smile. Anton shook his head. ''You do not have to lie to me,'' he told her shortly. ''You would not disturb me now if you did not want something. Tell me.'' He twisted to look at her as he felt her hands slipping away from him. She folded her arms under her breasts, pursing her lips a bit. ''Okay,'' Amber conceded. Anton turned a bit more, slinging his arm over the back of the couch and waiting for her to speak. ''Okay. I was gonna say that… you know… you should go see Luis.'' ''I should?'' Anton asked curiously. Amber nodded. ''Yeah. And you should tell him to go fuck himself. Tell him… I don't know. But you know you have to throw him away, don't you? You know you have to get rid of him.'' Anton lowered his head a bit, tightening his lips. He should have known she would come at him with that, that she would force his hand just as he was agonizing over this very question. She always did. Anton wanted to wind the tape back up his fist, to wind it down, to think this through. He wanted to weigh his options himself. Of course, these two had already done it for him. It was Luis or Amber. Anton had made his choice. ''I will go.'' ''Thank you.'' She sounded genuinely grateful. Surprises came all the time. The gauze was wrapped halfway up his wrist with the end dangling. Anton pushed through the door of the hospital room, shrugging off the nurse as he stepped inside the flawless environment. The walls were a sterile white, completely unmarked, and the tiled floor just beige enough that Anton noticed the walls didn't just go forever in every direction. A tall, metal thing like a dresser drawer was pushed up against a wall and shelves with casters on the bottom were situated around the room for easy access. No matter how the room's contents were set up, though, they all focused on one thing; all were outlying planets to the central sun that was the bed. Laying in the bed, covered with snow white blankets, was a battered and bruised Luis Ferrara. Anton forced his face to remain smooth. Luis let his head roll backwards and turn to the right so that he could see Anton as the tall man closed the door behind him. Luis gave a small, rather contemptuous chuckle. Anton shrugged it off for now. There was no use trying to ask Luis to be anything but himself. Anton would have liked a little civility from the man, but then again, he hadn't exactly come to share tea and crumpets. What was quite clear was that Luis knew why Anton was here and he wasn't impressed in the slightest. Anton folded his arms over his chest. Luis blinked and sighed. ''Whaddyou want, Lion?'' Luis asked impatiently in Spanish. ''Come to beat a broken man in his bed?'' ''Shut up, Luis,'' Anton responded. ''Do you think I'm just cruel for no reason? That's you, if you remember correctly.'' Luis trembled with anger, shaking his head. ''I gotta reason, you dumb fuck,'' Luis snapped. ''I told you the reason. I had a reason the whole time, but you're just too blind to see it's a damn good reason. You're just too stupid to—'' ''Once in your life, you should try to step out of your body,'' Anton cut in savagely. ''And just observe yourself talking. Gibberish! Rubbish! All of it. You can talk shit all day, but that's all it is in the end. It means nothing.'' Luis curled his upper lip. ''You're the one who's brushing your teeth and cleaning your ears with shit. Like a goddamn crackwhore, swallowing the white jizz of the lies that Amber's feedin' you, and she's feedin' you like a baby, man. Guess that's what you are inside. When I was around, I was leadin' you by the hand 'cause you didn't know where to go, and now she's forcefeedin' you to believe what she wants you to believe 'cause you ain't got shit for shit to believe in yourself. Don't gimme that 'I read' bullshit, neither. You're a fuckin' idiot, a lost cause, man.'' ''I'm an idiot, Luis?'' Anton growled. ''Because of what, I decided it would be a good idea not to grievously injure my girlfriend?'' ''Eck-fuckin'-zactly.'' Anton took two long strides to the bed, lifting his hand and holding it in the air above Luis. It was cupped as if the Venezuelan's neck belonged in it, as if that was what was supposed to fit, as if Anton's hand served no other purpose but to squeeze the life out of Luis. Luis barely looked at it before sniffing contemptuously. ''Puss-ACK!'' Anton's hand zoomed down and squeezed around Luis's throat. ''Listen, Luis,'' Anton snarled as he choked the smaller man. Luis kicked his legs violently, his hands grabbing at Anton's wrist and forearm in vain. The only sounds Luis could make were violent hackings as he tried to work his windpipe. ''You are garbage. Do you understand me? Fucking garbage. I have no use for you now because, really, what was the last thing that you did for me? What did you do that I can appreciate, that would convince me to let you stay around? To let you stay alive? All I want you to do is to stay away from Amber and to get the fuck out of my—'' Anton was cut off as a heavy blow struck the back of his head. He lurched forward and then spun around, bringing his arms up and by chance blocking a second one. He quickly regained his lucidity and saw who was attacking him. He knew the wild hair instantly. He swung wildly, simply to get her to duck, and then he shot a jab forward. He felt her knee shoot up into his ribs and he grunted in surprise. She threw her elbow up into his jaw and he leaned back against the bed. He had hoped he wouldn't actually have to fight her, but if she was going to press it, he would. He gave her a sharp jab to the nose and she stumbled backwards, shaking her head. The door was already open, so she stepped out there. Anton glanced back at Luis for a split second, seeing that the man was laying still as he drew breath back into his lungs, before rushing after Nneka. He planted his feet and struck out with another jab, but Nneka caught his wrist and dragged him out, pushing his back against the wall. She kicked at his side, but this time Anton caught her leg, twisting and slamming his elbow into her jaw. The force of the blow spun her smaller body around in a barrel roll and he released her leg, letting her topple to the cold tile. Anton stepped forward and thrust his foot out at her head, knocking the rising Nneka back onto her stomach. Nneka managed to drag herself away just in time to avoid another strike from her older brother. She rose as she could and ran, stumbling at first but quickly finding her footing, down the hall and around the corner. Anton quickly gave chase, heading off at full speed after his younger sibling. He didn't know what kind of plot she and Luis had set him up for, but if she was going to strike him, she was going to pay for it. Nneka practically flew down the stairs and Anton hurtled after her, each of them keeping impeccable balance as the charged past the throng of doctors, nurses, patients and visitors, weaving around vending machines and carts, letting shouted insults and reprimands slide off of them like condensation. They were both driven. Nneka was driven by fear. She was trained well, but she had realized too late that she could not overmatch Anton. Anton himself was driven by a need to discipline her and a blind hatred. She was not just going to get away from him. Nneka tore down another stairwell and Anton followed, arms pumping as he propelled himself with gazelle-like strides. She turned and ran through another doorway. Anton lowered his head, speeding up and rounding the corner swiftly. He found his shoes crunching on grass and he slowed to a stop, looking around him curiously. Here was a green place. Serenity in the crisis center that was the hospital. There was no ceiling or roof over the place so that the sun's rays could bear down benevolently on this square patch of land. A tree was planted in a back corner, giving a bit of shade. It was a sanctuary, almost holy. It was peace. Anton felt it was a shame that he had to break that peace like he was going to. Nneka was backed up against the far wall, fists raised as if she could fight. Anton walked towards her purposefully; he didn't approach in a fighter's stance. He came at her with arms down and eyes blazing. She withered at the sight of her brother like that. She knew what was going to happen. She knew he was going to beat her severely. He knew she knew these things. She could never defend herself against him, though. It was a mental flaw she had, he supposed. Maybe an emotional one. She'd pay for it. Anton looked at his sister for a long while with his fists balled. She looked straight back at him like a lost puppy, waiting to endure whatever it had to to be taken back. Anton tilted his head to the side as he studied her. She'd fallen in so deep with Luis that he didn't know if he could extract her. She had let the man infect him. She had become his accomplice. She had become his weapon against Anton and Anton could not abide that. He lifted his hand, spreading his feet so that he could put more power behind the strike. Nneka didn't cringe. He lifted it higher, ready to hit her. A wheezing from behind Anton made him whirl around. Luis stood at the entrance of the courtyard, leaning up against the doorframe because that was the only way he could remain upright. He hacked mightily, fighting a battle against his liquified insides to keep air inside his lungs. Anton lowered his hand slowly as he looked at Luis. He had done this to the man. He had wounded him like this. And yet, the man came down here to stop him from… whatever it was he was going to do. Was it because of Nneka? Was it because he didn't want Anton to become a monster? Both equally unlikely reasons, but that just made them equally plausible. He didn't know why the man had come down here. What Anton did know was what he could see. What he could see was that Luis was very likely dying on his feet. Anton had to help him. Amber thumbed the sticky green deeper into the bowl. Her mind drifted as it had so often recently. She worried about Anton. He wouldn't handle this right. She knew he wouldn't. He would go back to Luis's side once the man had a word with him. For some reason, the greasy little idiot was able to get past Anton's usually-invulnerable armor, to chip at his brain. Amber was starting to put chinks in that armor that only she could get to, but it was slow work and Anton was resistant. She envied Luis just as she hated him. He had to get away, by his own choice or otherwise. The problem was that she didn't know how to do it. She couldn't depend on Anton alone and she couldn't go with him, not after Luis attacked her like that. She flicked on her lighter. Goddammit if this wasn't stressful. She brought the lighter flame down to the bowl and wrapped her lips around the mouthpiece. Crackle. Burn. Inhale.
