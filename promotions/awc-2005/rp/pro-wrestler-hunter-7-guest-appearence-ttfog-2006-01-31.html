Pro Wrestler Hunter #7 (guest appearence: TTFoG)

31 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-31-06 {{ All previous Butterfly Hamada rp's shall be renamed 'Pro Wrestler Hunter', changed from the opriginally titled 'Pallas Athena'. }} Megumi spun around to face the accusing masked man. 'I did not kill that man,' she said. 'Well, I certainly didn't leave his body in the back alleyway of some street in the trash, now did I?' retorted The Green Grappler. 'That to me kinda adds up, doesn't it? How do you explain that?' 'I can't. You saw him, he just... died. As if he was programmed to die if he revealed the person who gave him the silver cigarette case.' 'Yes, well, now THERE is an explanation.' Megumi had no time to listen to the foolish man's ramblings. A few hours before the show, she had caught a man following her and green fool, but when she confronted him, he mysteriously died in her arms when questioned who he worked for. Just blocks from their hotel they left the body behind some large trash cans and high-tailed it back to their rooms and then off to the arena. Butterfly had kept an eye on this newest AWC wrestler the whole night. He had seen what had happened. He could be a witness if she was ever questioned about that body in the alley. Suddenly, he became someone she had to keep track of, and she was getting tired of it all. She was going to make absolutely sure all her bases were covered from here on out. 'Why don't I just call the authorities and tell them where the body is and what happened. The man was obviously a vagrant and a common thug. There has to be an explanation. Let them figure it out,' said Grapps. 'No!' Her vehemence seemed to surprise the veteran wrestler. 'Well, if you didn't kill him, what do you have to worry about?' he asked reasonably? Plenty, thought Megumi. The last thing she needed was to leave another body in her wake. Her past invited speculation. Sooner or later someone would put the pieces together and label her as the Pro Wrestler Hunter. That day seemed close at hand for the news of Tozen Fujita's accident and subsequent paralysis was spreading far too quickly throughout the wrestling community. And Ron Von Dumpling making a surprise appearance in the United States doubled the chances she would be fingered as this lunatic shooter who ended careers by attacking from the shadows. 'Come on,' she said abruptly. She tucked the silver cigarette case in her training bag and set a brisk pace toward the door. The Green Grappler fell in beside her. 'Where are we going?' 'Parking lot.' 'Oh? Good. I need to freshen up, take a shower, change masks...' Megumi was in no mood to banter. She slipped an arm underneath Grapps bicep and grabbed a hook of his left wrist, clamping on a joint lock. 'No showers. No time. In and out in five minutes.' 'Um, hello? That's my tennis hand? My serve is going to go to heck if you break it.' he admonished her. Megumi looked at his vague smile, wondering for a moment if the man truly was a simpleton. 'You're coming with me.' 'Yes, so it would appear. ha ha!' “You don’t get it, do you? You’re my hostage.” Megumi swung open the doors to the parking lot entrance and they headed toward a line of parked cars. 'Just keep walking.' She cinched on the wrist hold to push him along. 'Well, really now,' he huffed. 'There's no need to be so cloak and dagger about the whole thing, I'm a willing victim,' he said, looking her over and smiling. His calm acceptance of being her hostage disconcerted Megumi for a moment. Grapps smirked at the bewildered expression on her face. 'Don't look so surprised, my dear girl. I will admit, a wrist lock is a new approach, but many a lady has wanted to ride on The Mean Green Machine. Know what I mean?' “We’re here for a car, Grappler-san. Not for a backseat encounter. And stop talking so much. Hostages tend to need to stay quiet.” Megumi pointed to a green corvette at the end of the parking garage. “That is your car, I take it? You ARE The Green Grappler, right?” “How did you ever guess?” he said, breaking out in a smile. “It’s a classic 196---“ “We’ll take that one.” Megumi cut him off. She headed toward a small sports car, when suddenly....! 'That WENCH has tempted me for the last time!' 'Brother, she will pay for her sins, I garauntee you.' 'I do not believe this @#$%!', hissed Hamada under her breath. 'Jar wing foo? What does that mean?' Grapps asked before being pushed behind a pillar. 'I am tired of dealing with these idiots.' Hamada turned to Grapps and gave him the be quiet sign. 'Don't say a word. If they find you OR me here I am going to end up kicking the crap out of all three of you.' The Furious Fists head toward a red pick-up truck. 'Wait. Hmph, this is perfect. Come with me.' Hamada grunted. 'Oh fudge, I think I know what you have in mind.' As Tim and Liam enter their truck, Hamada pushes Grapps in front of engine and then sneaks around the large cargo trunk. 'Hey!' yells Liam from the passenger seat of the pick-up. 'Whose that green person!? Shouldn't he be wearing white?!' 'I saw that bastard earlier, hanging outside our door. What is he doing in the parking garage? Near our truck? Let's go ASK him, shall we brother?' Tim and Liam jump out of their doors, but Liam doesn't notice the person standing behind him. Then Tim hears a sickening thud, and turns to see Butterfly hammering his brother in the head with a pair of brass knuckles. The big six footer goes down to the ground after the fifth shot to cranium. Hamada then rams the truck door as hard as she possibly can into Liam Martin's head. 'That's for the beating you gave me, %$#@!' 'Behind you, Tim!' 'What? Who?' Tim Martin whips around to see who said that, only to be fooled by The Green Grappler's ventriloquism tricks once again. The Grappler kicks the door and it slams into Tim Martin's back, and he cries out and reaches for his shoulder. 'Get in the truck, now!' Butterfly orders sharply. The Grappler slams Tim Martin's head into the side of the truck, throws him to the ground, and then jumps into the driver's seat. 'What have you gotten me into?!' the masked man screams like a woman. 'Drive the truck! Now!' Hamada says as she presses on the gas herself and the truck screeches in reverse. 'HEATHENS! YOU'RE STEALING OUR TRUCK!' Tim launches himself at the truck but it's too late. He and his brother are left laying in the dust as the truck takes off. 'YOU CAR-JACKING HEATHENS!' The red pick-up made a screeching donut and then zoomed out of the parking garage, smoke blazing out of the tail pipe. They drove the truck hard, but Megumi could not tell that they were being followed. Dark clouds scuttled across the full moon as they made their way from Kentucky into Indiana. The signs said Bedford, but to Megumi they could have said anything, to her it made no difference. She was on the road and on the move. If there was a place and time to lure out the Pro Wrestler Hunter, this was it. Finally they sighted a small rest stop off the side of the highway and Hamada told her masked hostage to pull over. He parked in front of a 24 hour store, and the wind gusted and shook the pick-up truck in the cold dark January night. The Green Grappler shivered at the steering wheel. “Do you think now would be a good time for me to change into my street clothes? The cape and tights are fun and games but it doesn’t look like we’re having any fun and I don’t even know what kind of game we are playing, don’t you know.” Megumi realized that all this time the masked wrestler was wearing nothing but his gear and had kept his green flowing cape wrapped around his bare chest and back. “Give me the keys. Hot tea and some food for both of us, and then we are leaving.” She scooted out the door and headed for the store. “Do make mine a capuccino , will you dear… I mean, if they have that here in,” he looked out of the window of the truck and nothing but sky and dirt, “wherever we are.” Megumi exited the convenience store with some beverages and food a few minutes later and Grappler met her outside the front door of the store. He was now dressed in jeans and a white shirt, very conservative; but then there was that mask. “Do you never take it off?” Megumi asked Grappler as she handed him his cup of coffee. “The mask? What if someone in public saw me? No, I will never reveal my true identity until someone beats me for it.” Grappler spoke it like a challenge into the night. “The Green Grappler is a mysterious figure, an enigma wrapped in a puzzle!” “I worked in Mexico for a year. I studied with a great luchadore or two.” Megumi stated. “That is very interesting. Maybe we know some of the same people.” Grappler stated innocently. Megumi thought about her trainer in Mexico and what he might think of The Green Grappler as a wrestler. “I doubt it.” “But wait! Everyone will know us both once we get to New York!” Megumi looked confused at his words. “The Green Grappler takes The Big Apple! I’ll be on Regis! Letterman will be calling! Jon Stewart! And I’m going to have this incredible story about how I was kidnapped by a Japanese girl after she beat up some bible-thumping rednecks…” “We aren’t going to New York,” Megumi stated matter-of-factly. She headed back toward the pick-up truck. “But that man with the silver cigarette case,” Grappler said and started to follow her. “He said he got it in New York from someone, right?” “No, we’re going to Indianapolis, where we’re wrestling next week. Or at least where I will be wrestling.” She kept throwing little insults at The Green Grappler but he seemed oblivious and she couldn’t believe he was that dim-witted. “I am positive that I am being expected in New York. Better to stay clear and see what pops up.” “We are going to spend an entire week in Indianapolis? How dreadful! And I don’t have more then a change of clothing in my bag.” He said, his voice trailing off. “The bag,” Megumi pointed to it on the passenger seat. “What is inside of it.” “Clothing. Jewelry. Mask. Cape. Boots. Bottle Rockets. My green jammies. Sparklers. Roman candles. Silly String. A rubber chicken, you know.“ He concluded. “The essentials.” “All that?” Megumi eyed the gym bag skeptically. It looked pretty small to hold much more then some wrestling gear. “Empty it.” True to his word, Grappler reached into his bag and produced masks, fireworks, capes, false teeth, flies stuck in ice cubes, a wooden dummy— “That’s enough!” Megumi finally insisted. “I’m almost done,” he said, rummaging in the bottom of the bag. “Best for last, and all that. Ah! Here it is.” He fished out a long round silver missle shaped object and waved it triumphantly. Megumi couldn’t believe her eyes. The green fool had produced… a bazooka!
