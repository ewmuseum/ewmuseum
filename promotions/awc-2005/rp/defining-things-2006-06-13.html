Defining Things...

13 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/13/06 aim-less adj. Devoid of direction or purpose. I have to keep this brief, you understand, as a lot of company business remains unfinished. Despite my accomplished amateur background, the likes of which should have been enough to warrant immediate inclusion in the main event picture, I was brought into the AWC without even so much as a press conference. No promo videos. No entrance music. Hell, I didn't even have a spot on the damn roster. The AWC brain trust took my years of expertise and stuck it coaching a couple of no-talent stiffs without a damn clue what it takes to be a real wrestler. So for months I merely treaded water in your beautiful Atlantic. Even after those two country jackasses rode off into the sunset, it was two steps forward and two steps right back. There was nothing I could do, when you have this much God-given talent you can't just walk away, no matter how deep they try to bury you. Because I knew, beyond a shadow of a doubt, that if I ever came across even a hint of opportunity, I would run roughshod over anyone in my path. I'm a god-damned amateur icon. I wrestled at the number one college wrestling program in the country and was never even pinned. Let that sink in for a moment... I WAS NEVER FUCKING PINNED! But something was holding me back. Something I coudln't control. Until now. in-ten-tion n. 1. A course of action that one intends to follow. 2. An aim that guides action; an objective. Oh, how they've changed. But I'm not really at liberty to get into that right now. All in due time, of course. Although I find this entire change of direction absolutely electrifying, I can certainly understand your hesitation. After all, if I'm allowed to unleash the entire wrestling arsenal at my disposal, what chance does this roster of hacks have when they step into the ring? Correction... HER RING.
