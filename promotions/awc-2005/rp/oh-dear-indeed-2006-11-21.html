oh dear indeed

21 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.21.06 ’’WHO THE FUCK DO YOU THINK YOU ARE?! ’’ Darcy’s face turned abruptly to the side as he felt his left cheek sting with powerful force. He couldn’t tell for sure, what with being blindfolded and all, but it sure as hell felt like he’d been slapped. Backhanded, to be precise, if he had to wager a guess. It seemed to carry a bit more momentum than your normal run of the mill slap palm strike. He elected to give no reply, judging by the fact that it didn’t seem as if this man had any wish to engage in witty repartee. The longer he kept his mouth shut, the sooner this guy would stop wailing on him. Not at this juncture, however. “HUH?! I ASKED YOU A QUESTION!”the man responded, continuing his interrogation. He followed up the query with a shot to Darcy’s jaw, this time from the right. He packed quite the wallop, so much so that Darcy wasn’t sure how he remained upright in the chair, handcuffs aside. As he recoiled from the blow Darcy flexed his jaw, checking to see if it had been broken from the blow. The fact that he could still move it from right to left, then back again seemed to suggest it wasn’t; nevertheless, his attacker had certainly left a mark. After a moment or two Darcy could feel his mouth filling up with something, of which he spit out in front of his blinded eyes. He couldn’t say for sure whether or not it was blood. Sure tasted like it, though. ’’Aww….what’s the matter, Darcy my boy? Don’t care for the taste of blood in your mouth? Well I’m afraid that that’s just TOO DAMN BAD!’’ Another rocket sailed in toward Darcy’s jaw as the man switched back to attacking the aft side of his face. It hurt like hell, of course…but the pain paled in comparison to the rage that burned ever brighter with each passing strike the man took at his defenseless body. If only I could get one goddamned hand free! he thought silently, gripping his bound fists so tight that they surely must have been white as bone. He’d been rocked around enough by this point to piss off even the most tranquil of hostages. And to make matters worse, the man had targeted Darcy’s face; and nothing in the world made him angrier. Not being able to hear Ellis Nash whisper about “Pretty Boy Darcy” when he walked past, evidently unaware that his hearing was just good enough to catch her subdued sentiments, was disappointing in and of itself. But there was something about taking a blow to the face that made him see white. As a child, he and his sister Marie got in their fair share of fisticuffs. As the older brother he always held a sizeable advantage, and he tried not to abuse the power too much. But if Marie had the audacity to slap him across the face…well, God help her. And God help this asshole, too. Unfortunately for Darcy, it appeared that He had. An outright assault in response to this unmitigated violence was out of the question – even if he could move, which he couldn’t in the slightest, the cloth wrapped tightly around his eyes prevented any hope of providing much-needed accuracy for his vengeance. Still, with the pain mounting, it took every ounce of his being to keep himself from shouting every curse word he could think of in this vile creature's direction. If he couldn't fight back physically, why not verbally tear him a new asshole? The idea was incredibly enticing, and Darcy's brain was practically begging for the opportunity. But it simply wasn't worth it. This guy obviously was taking some sort of sick pleasure in beating Darcy to a pulp; making him angry would likely only increase the anguish that came with every swing of his fist. If he could hold mum, maybe he would grow bored of punishing Darcy's body, and he could start wallowing in his own misery all the sooner. Darcy gritted his teeth, refusing to give him the satisfaction of appearing afflicted in the slightest. Grasping desperately for assistance in this endeavor, he tried hard to picture the scene from the original Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles movie, where Master Splinter was able to hold his tongue despite the nefarious assault from the Shredder and his henchmen. If a giant mutated rat could stave off the overwhelming temptation to petty retaliation...then By George, so could he. ’’Come on, there has to be something you'd like to share with the class, isn't there? Don't tell me the cat's got your tongue... ’’ Darcy let his head droop, offering no response to his abductor's queries. If he could keep nothing else...he still had his dignity. Not to mention what little college experience he had directed in the expertise of psychological study and maneuverability, which strangely came into play in this most unlikely of situations. Clearly, the man was demented – perhaps even emotionally retarded, giving in to temptations that typically can be held in check for those who have matured past the age of a first grader. By refusing to indulge his negative behavior, it stood to reason that this negative reinforcement of his abhorrent conduct could create a psychological aversion... He couldn't help himself any longer. Looking up in the direction that he was fairly sure he heard the voice coming from, he offered only one word...one that would set this man's temper ablaze, no doubt - ’’Meow. ’’ Darcy heard the man utter something just under his breath, and with that came a sharp blow to the stomach, with which Darcy couldn't help but groan as the wind all too rapidly escaped his lungs. While Darcy cursed himself for failing to keep his big mouth shut, he was also incredibly thankful that his wife-to-be wasn't here to share in his predicament. Of course, having to witness her getting physically abused would be tragic enough – but mostly, Darcy was glad that she wasn't here because of what had just transpired. If one little cat call was enough to warrant this level of maltreatment...the very notion made him shudder when he considered what might have happened if Amy and her big mouth were here to make things assuredly worse by an exponential degree. ’’Enough...I suddenly find you incredibly boring, ’’ came the voice again. Darcy wished like hell that he could get at least a peek at this guy...there was still a chance that this was all an incredibly vicious, drunken prank that Johnny might be pulling on him. He thought back to a time when he and Johnny weren't quite fifteen, a time when Johnny was quite fond of insisting that he would one day make Darcy “cry like a bitch.” Maybe he was just following through on an age-old threat... Somehow, he doubted it. Even in a drunken stupor, Johnny's conscience wouldn't allow him to go through with what happened next. “It sounds like you're getting a little cranky, little baby Crisis head,” the voice spoke again, with Darcy's ear detecting his movement from the front of the room to the back. ’’Maybe it's time you had a nap... ’’ Darcy's mind raced, sensing the man's words were probably foreshadowing something less than pleasant. But if there was any fortune to be found in this unfortunate predicament, at least Darcy could take solace in that he didn't have to ponder his impending doom for very long. He heard something lifted off the ground from behind. The auditory disturbance yielded no clues as to what the object might be...and neither did the sharp blow to the back of his next. With one last thought before consciousness flooded from his brain, he prayed that it wasn't something too heavy. *** I have two selves. I've spent the last ten years of my life traveling the world, chasing a dream I never even knew I had until I finally up and left home. Whether I was earning my stripes in the depths of Canada, running up and down the Western Hemisphere carrying the banner for Pier Six Wrestling, or flying back and forth across the Atlantic Ocean for the appropriately titled Atlantic Wrestling Club, there practically isn't a corner of this green earth I haven't been where there are enough people to gather together for the viewing pleasure of human combat. But for most of that time, I spent it bouncing around from one ship to another, going where ever somebody could write me a check in payment of the craft I loved to perform. Since I was hopping from one place to the next, one promotion to the next and one lonesome post-3:00 AM hotel bar to another, I could never really count on sticking around any one place for any real length of time. Before I had the pleasure of Amy Campbell entering my life, it was a fairly lonely existence. So when I wasn't nose-deep in a Daniel Quinn novel, running for miles in place at the gym or trying to drink myself under a table, I kept myself busy doing a lot of people watching. Maybe this isn't news to you or anything, but I often lose sight of the fact that everywhere I go, anytime I see a crowd of people going about their everyday lives...I forget that each one of those people has a story to tell. Each a unique individual, with unique experiences shaped to mold a philosophy that explains the purpose of their existence and everyone else's, in this great big bustling universe of ours. And I'm blown away every time. Typically, those philosophies stem from one of the big religions floating around our world. Personally, I've taken a good look at all the big ones...and I have to say I'm not terribly impressed. Jews, Christians, Hindus, Muslims...they've all got some good ideas. But if there aren't glaring flaws in the facts they refuse to yield on (Jesus was white?), they're plagued by some unnecessary exclusionary clause that holds their religion to be correct, and all others to be worshipping crap, knock-off ''Great-Value'' brand gods. I'm loathe to pick a favorite, but if you're going to go ahead and put a gun to my head for a choice...I have to say I'm impressed by Daoism. Its story is simple enough, and doesn't require years of Sunday School to digest; Legend has it that while passing through the hills of China, a bridge keeper asked a wise man named Lao-Tzu to write a book containing his thoughts and beliefs. This yielded the Tao Te Ching, Daoism's “Bible,” if you will. But instead of a gi-normous encyclopedia filled with archaic rules designed to cast out the non-desirables, the Tao Te Ching is practically just a pocketbook of poetry. Best of all, the poems make sense. With words that preach the utility of flowing like a leaf on the river of life, and asserting that the softest branch shall never break, it seems difficult to argue against a religion that values compromise far more than rigidity. I told you all that so I could tell you this – Daoism also holds true a certain “duality” of existence. If you've ever wondered where that Yin-Yang symbol you've seen everywhere comes from, here's your answer. Ancient Chinese metaphysics assert that two complimentary forces, Yin and Yang, can be found in all things of our known universe. Yin is the black element; it is sad, passive, dark, feminine, downward seeking, and corresponds to the night. Yang, oppositely, is the brighter element; it is happy, active, light, masculine, upward-seeking and corresponds to the day. Yin is water, while Yang is fire, or wind. Maybe you don't believe in any of this crap, and that's fine. But for yours truly, it somehow resonates within me like a tuning fork raking my skeleton. For I am no stranger to the duality of my own existence...I live simultaneously as both Darcy Markson and Darcy Crisis. Version M is my Yin; the more practical, reasonable, humbler version of my being – probably the one people enjoy being around better. But the “D to the C”...that's my Yang. That's the bold, brash glory-hound willing to sever his spinal cord if it means that no one can truthfully say they did it better than Darcy Crisis. Crisis and Markson are two unique, separate spheres of my life...they cross over, surely, just as a piece of Yin exists in Yang, and vice-versa, as the Taijitu symbol we're all so familiar with would suggest. But ultimately, as I have discovered in my years worth of travels...neither can survive without the other. But the duality doesn't end there, friends. Within each sphere, I lead a dual existence. With Darcy Markson, there is the version I believe myself to be; my own life, as narrated by me, and the company I have kept watching my own life transpire, every second of every waking hour for the past twenty-eight years. Then, there is the version that I project to the world. It mostly consists of who I am, but is also interposed with what I want others to see when they think of me. A good picture, but never fully accurate; and even then, it depends on who you ask. The ones who have stake in my well-being and affection tend to highlight the good qualities, while minimizing the flaws. Those who stand to gain from my complete and total failure see an opposite, uglier looking image in the place of the former. I remember an old Baz Luhrmann song that suggested the key to life was only remembering the compliments people paid you, while forgetting the insults…but like he said, if anyone is able to succeed in doing this, I’d like to know how. The duality of Darcy Crisis, which I do believe has come to the forefront of my life as of recent, breaks uniquely into separate spheres all its own. On the one side, there is the Darcy Crisis I have become. For many, many years, I traveled down a road that every day I begged for another chance to go back in the direction I came, if only for one opportunity to correct the mistakes I had made. Leaving Amy, naively allowing myself to ascend a thirty-foot cage when I knew full well the owner of said cage had it out for me…take your pick. I spent a lot of sleepless nights knowing that opportunity would never be realized. But here I am. Rock me like a hurricane, if you so desperately desire, but just like the whirlwind tropical disaster, you’ll find the only things that can stop me from tearing up your city are a whole lot of land, and a whole lot of prayer. Okay, that was cheesy, I know, but it couldn’t be helped. I honestly feel like I’ve recaptured my old form, the one that saw me rise to dominance in PSW. The past several months have been a whirlwind that saw me capture my first championship since my back injury, finally earning the one-on-one match against Amy that had eluded me my entire career, and a series of grueling title defenses that saw me prevail time and time again. They’re calling me unstoppable…and God help me, I’m starting to believe it. Then, aside from the Darcy Crisis I have become…there is the Darcy Crisis I can become. One challenge after another has been sent perilously in my vicinity, hoping that I won’t have what it takes to meet and overcome them. I’ve met them all; and in the interest of always growing, and always reaching up for something more, it’s time I went looking for challenges of my own. I reached the AWC at the very bottom of the ladder, and I’m proud enough of what I’ve accomplished to admit, without a trace of arrogance, that I’ve climbed quite a few of the rungs. But when I look up, I still see rungs, and I see the vaunted, esteemed, downright legendary competitors I must pass if I intend to reach the top. The Darcy Crisis I have become will be symbolically represented in the Gauntlet final against Jack Murphy. Murph is a hell of a talent, and I think the world of him…I only hope he knows that I simply do not plan on losing the last match of a gauntlet I pushed so hard to begin and see through all the way to the end. Just like my glory days of Pier Six, I have an opportunity to stand proud as a double champion…and just like in Pier Six, I fully intend on fulfilling that end. The Darcy Crisis I can become will, like me, make his debut in the evening’s main event. I have to admit, I’ve never competed in a match quite like this one at all. Even before the magnificent prizes, even before the “golden triangle” which will surely hold the best and brightest the wrestling world can offer this side of the Atlantic…I will be locked within the confines of my own, personal triangular cage where my destiny awaits me. Truth be told, I was doing Adam Trimm a hell of a favor by calling one Pierce Lavelle the new Trimm. Trimm couldn’t hold Lavelle’s jock, and never will. I’ve faced off against men in every corner of this earth, some twice my size, some thrice my tenacity and mean streak…yet none, none period, were so purely good at what they did as what I’ve seen from Pierce Lavelle. You can say that I’m just blowing smoke up his ass, and maybe you’re right…all I know for sure is that if I have to retire knowing that I didn’t rise my game high enough to take Lavelle down in Triangles 2006…I’ll have to retire knowing that I didn’t accomplish all the things I ever set out to do. *** One shot after another, Amy was working her way to a buzz in the kitchen of her parents' home. She and Darcy had been left alone like teenaged children, warned not to 'make a mess' while the elder Campbells were gone to visit family overnight. That's when Aimz busted out her prizes from an early-morning trip to the liquor store, and Darcy persisted to turn the heat up as high as it would go in the house, clearly not used to Canadian autumns. Amy stirred shots with her pinky fingers, sucking the remaining alcohol off the minute they left the glasses, and seemed to be making a display of her beverage menu when Darcy entered the room after retrieving a sweater from his luggage. She was making sure that all of the bottles were on the table, clearly opened, and that she lined at least three shots in front of herself at a time. Something was amiss to Darcy, because he'd spent the last year of his life watching this very same girl avoid drinking more than two or three ounces in an entire evening due to past alcoholism. He didn't speak up, though. She was the one who initiated conversation, trying to make her efforts to get drunk even more blatant... ''Want a drink? I remember whenever my folks went away, I'd bust out the booze. And you know what they say, when in Rome... do whatever the hell you want.'' Darcy pondered the question for all of about two nanoseconds before reaching an answer. ’’Sure thing, Amy, ’’ he said, not even missing a beat. ’’Got any rum in storage there? My stomach is in knots... ’’ The redhead quickly shot him down, ''No rum. Can't handle rum anymore. Baaaad times...'' she winced, ''And are you THAT afraid of my dad killing you in your sleep? He was just kidding!'' ''Better make it tequila then...'' he responded, sounding slightly disappointed to hear that his liver poison of choice was out of stock, probably in thanks to a deal Amy made whilst praying to the Porcelain Gods. ''...or as I like to call it after a healthy dose, 'kterkula!' And no, I'm not worried about Daddy Campbell. Worse comes to worse, I figure I could take him...I'm worried about the guys I might not be able to.” ''So am I,'' was the response Amy offered after taking Darcy's shot for herself, ''Do you ever think about what happened in Pier Six? This is ten times as bad, Darcy... and your spine honestly isn't getting my vote of confidence.'' Oh dear. Darcy wasn't sure what instance bothered him more – the fact that Amy was on a quest to get positively tanked, whether he could keep up or not...or that she was playing the ''broken back'' card again. It was something that most of the time, he couldn't bear to give more than a few seconds' thought before re-burying it deep within his consciousness where it was powerless to inflict torture on his psyche any longer. Doing a complete 180, Darcy turned to argue his case. ''As I recall,'' he told the redhead, ''the last time I had to go through half a roster to get what I wanted, I came out on top. Granted, Steven Wright and Adam Trimm are no Jack Murphy or Pierce Lavelle...but then again, Darcy Crisis is no Darcy Crisis....if that makes sense, at all.'' Amy's upper lip curled slightly as she produced a full-sized glass from across the table and began pouring tequila straight into it. The scene could've been part of Alanis Morrissette's not so very ironic 'Ironic' music video, ''Did you ever stop to think of what it might feel like to watch someone start to destroy themselves? Maybe you should put down your wrestling tapes and start researching the torture of seeing someone you love repeat destructive past behaviors just to get a buzz that'll only make them suffer.'' With that, she put the glass to her lips and made a half pint of tequila disappear in under two seconds. If Darcy hadn't been so incensed that he didn't have Amy's support in his time of doubt, he might have noticed what was going on here. Hard to say, because he didn't even have the smallest clue. ''I work in a destructive business, Amy,'' he said, doing his best to keep his cool. ''And last time I checked, so do you. I'm not going to spend the rest of my life afraid to take a risk or two just because some asshole threw me off a cage practically a lifetime ago. And if I told you to slow down, you'd tell me to shove it up my ass. So........I'm not telling you to shove it up your ass.......but I think you get the idea.'' Amy hiccuped and her stomach audibly growled. Clearly, she was starting to feel the effects of her methods, and feared she wouldn't be conscious long enough to get her point across to Darcy. That's why she spoke up so soon, and made sure her words bit hard, '' 'Some asshole' nearly took away your ability to stand on two feet and lead your life. I definitely get the idea, Darcy... I just happen to think you're missing it by a mile.'' Darcy sighed. He couldn't shake the notion that both of them were equally correct – her with a legitimate concern for his well-being, and him for adding perspective to the supposed ''risk'' that Amy was doing her best to blow out of proportion. It was remarkably easier for Darcy to navigate the treacherous argument waters when he knew he was right, or could at least convince himself of as much. This scenario was a little trickier. ''I appreciate the concern, Amy...really, I do. But more than that, I would really appreciate it if I had your support. Putting myself through this God-forsaken gauntlet and hopping ships to a main-event that, judging by the fact that I can't make heads or tails of how it's supposed to work, will probably be pretty hellacious...well, it definitely isn't the smartest decision I've ever made. But neither was agreeing to devote my life to a career where guys have full license to scramble my brains with a steel chair without worrying about the resulting lawsuit.'' He turned away from her, folding his arms. He continued, speaking softly. ''But just like that decision, I'm locked into this one. Too late to back out, which means we're well past the point where nagging doubts are going to do me any good. So why don't you do yourself a favor and save your breath, if you don't have anything positive to offer?’’ A beat passed. After the silence matched the tension in its unbearablity, Amy opened her mouth to speak, but Darcy cut her off after the first syllable. Either she’d relented, or he’d incited her enough that she was about to say something completely uncalled for. In either case, it likely wasn’t something that would make him feel better, so he elected to end the conversation there and then. ‘’Hey-‘’ he interjected, still speaking barely above a whisper. ‘’I’m sorry. I didn’t mean to lash out at you like that. I suppose I could stand to take it down a couple notches. I’m gonna go take a walk or something…’’ Darcy turned inward, looking to embrace the girl he loved enough to dump his concentrated flaws on with little to no shame. As he moved to give her a kiss, he was suddenly taken aback by the redhead’s breath, which by this point had grown strong enough to knock an elephant unconscious. ‘’Jesus, Amy…’’ he remarked, scrunching his nose. ‘’How much did you…’’ Darcy turned to gaze at the bottle of Jose Cuervo Gold, which didn’t look so gold any longer now that its contents had been fully consumed. He turned his eyes back to Amy once more, who suddenly had the faraway stare in her eyes normally seen in escaped mental patients. Following a burp, Amy echoed the sentiments Darcy had shared with her last Thanksgiving. ‘’More kterkulaaa, please!’’ she exclaimed, before slumping over in Darcy’s arms. *** A bucketful of water splashing powerfully against Darcy’s face roused him from his slumber. He awoke to find himself still bound to the increasingly-uncomfortable chair, now sporting a soaked shirt and dripping locks, with an ungodly-splitting headache to match. He expelled the water that had found its way into his mouth like a well-trained orca whale, surprised to find that no taste of blood accompanied the banished fluids. With his mouth clear, Darcy shook the hydrolyzed hair matted against his eyes, hoping to get a grip on his bearings. As his vision was restored, he found that he was no longer blindfolded, and could see a portly, pear-shaped man sitting in a backward-faced chair before him. ‘’Rise and shine, Darcy Markson,’’ the man said, with a cackle. Instantly, Darcy recognized it to be the voice that had tormented him with blindfolded blows across his face and chest shortly before he went dead to the world. He stared into the eyes of this man, noting first and foremost that he was an ugly motherfucker. Normally Darcy wasn’t one to succumb to physical stereotypes, but even here he had to admit that this guy, with beady eyes peering out from his oversized glasses on a potato-shaped head, probably would have wagered to be a Jeffrey Dahmer-type. The fact that he was locked up in this guy’s basement only confirmed the unfortunate suspicion. ‘’I’d love to oblige, buddy…’’ Darcy responded, coughing as his sore chest took exception to his using his voice. ‘’…but you’ve made it a little difficult for me to do any rising.’’ The beady-eyed fatso cackled again, evidently taking great pleasure in Darcy’s physical discomfort. ‘’Never mind that part,’’ he answered, rising from his own chair as if to mock his victim’s predicament. ‘’Just stick to the waking up part.’’ Darcy shivered from the water clinging to his hair and clothes, which only caused more pain. ‘’Duly noted,’’ he said, adding a muffled groan. ‘’Now would you mind explaining what all this about?’’ ‘’That, my friend, is among many things of which we must discuss…’’ TO BE CONTINUED…
