Shortest rp ever. Includes Photoshoot, Beer, and a Phone

9 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-9-00 “That will be all Teresa. Thank you for your cooperation.” A slender dark-haired photographer horridly began to pack up his gear while his assistant tossed Teresa a towel. “It’s not like I had a choice.” The Redneck princess murmured as she wrapped the large towel over her bare skin. The photo shoot lasted only half hour but to her it felt like eternity. Never had she been so humiliated. The worse was knowledge that the whole world will soon see her in a way no one has ever seen her before, save a couple. “You should’ve read your contract. Trust me, I didn’t like this shoot no more than you. You should really do something about that face. If you looked half of what you did in your indy days, I’d ….” “SHUT THE HELL UP!” Teresa shot the photographer a stern glare and stepped behind the curtain to change. “…All you would have to do is put a bag over her head and she’d be a perfect. Just think, all that raw energy and you know she hasn’t been screwed in a long time.” “You didn’t have to take her pictures. I’m going to have nightmares about that for months.” “Come on Pete. She don’t look that bad.” Pete, the photographer stared at his assistant Ted for a long moment and zipped his bag. From behind the curtain, Teresa was listening. Prepared to bust a couple of heads, she stepped from behind the curtain only to find the two men had just left. “Damn you Harber. Sick sonofabitch.” --------------------------------------- The cell phone was neglected once again inside the duffel bag. It probably was on his last minutes of battery life and still it rang. Teresa unlocked the door to her cheap hotel room carrying a paper sack full of cheap crackers; off brand Vienna sausages, Bronco cigarettes and 64 ounce bottle of beer. Her worn out sneaker stepped on a piece of paper that obviously had been slid under the door. Looking down, she noticed it was a white envelope with her name printed in black ink. Whispered curses slip from her pink lips. She sets the paper sack on the small round table beside the door and retrieves the large bottle of beer. After unscrewing the lid, she takes a long swig and picks up the envelope and sits down on the bed. Dear Teresa I am back home in the states on a two week leave. I visited Mama. What the hell is your problem? First you beat the shit out of her nurse right in front of her two young kids and Mama, and now after purchasing the newest AWC magazine, I see you nearly nude? I’m worried about you sis. Scott contacted me and told me what happened between you two. Here’s a pre-stamped envelope to my base in Iraq. Please write me. If not me, find a doctor or something. Your brother, Private Eric Tomas A single tear rolled down her cheek. She carefully folded the letter and returned it into its envelope. Eric didn’t know everything. He didn’t know she knew his father wasn’t her father. He didn’t know why he attacked the nurse. He didn’t know why she took Scott from her life. He wouldn’t understand. No one would. Teresa took another swig of her cheep beer, winced from the taste and cursed Harber for making her buy such cheep shit. The cell phone made another attempt to grab Teresa’s attention. She looks toward her duffel bag as the phone continued to ring. A sigh exited her chest and she reached down into the bag. The phone quit ringing but she had it in her grasp. Just as her eyes fell upon the screen to catch the number, the screen went blank. The light went out. The battery finally gave up and died. What her eyes did catch was all she needed to know. Jack….. “Aww hell, what that dumb son’bitch want?” She mumbled to herself before she dug into the paper sack for her smokes. Teresa figured it was that damn magazine that brought this piece of her past to surface. It was only a matter of time anyway before he’d come looking her up.
