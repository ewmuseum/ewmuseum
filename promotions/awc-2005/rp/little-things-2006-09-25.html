When Amy Campbell stormed into Chainz' locker room on September 19th, she left the Fresh! camera crew locked outside. Naturally, it was a ploy to be mysterious... but only the most creative of minds could imagine what happened inside the room with no lenses in sight. When Chainz greeted Amy, she'd pulled him inside... only to pin him against a wall. ''Oooh,'' purred Sloan, ''Forceful. I like it.'' ''If you say ONE WORD, I'm gonna put you through the fucking wall.'' ''One word.'' Amy let go of the monster, instead choosing to keep her distance and simply smack him in the cheek, ''I'm serious. And this visit isn't at ALL what you, let alone the people watching through the cameras beyond that door are gonna think it is. Want a little irony, Michael? I'm using your presence to play Darcy, and I'm playing Darcy because AWC's paying me to show our relationship off a little more. Boosts ratings, y'know... and since he's avoiding me at home, I might as well confront him at work.'' Sloan smirked. He was much smarter than she thought, and obviously knew that the redhead was trying to appear as knowledgable and imposing as possible, ''They'll find a few thousand ways to cut your contract if you don't play along with what sells t-shirts. Am I getting warm? Speaking of warm... it's a little hot in h--'' ''Fuck you.'' ''Well, you COULD, but now it looks like the company finally realized how much it could benefit from your cockteasing skills!'' he laughed, ''Way to go, Red - you're a corporate whore, and you're taking that so-called lover faggot of yours along for the ride! How does it feel to be one of the highest paid hookers I've ever met?'' She had no comeback. In a roundabout way, what he said was entirely true. She was doing exactly what she'd always spoken against - playing an angle to benefit her employer. It just so happened that the playbook included getting attention from Darcy, which was the only part convincing her. That, and the fact that she'd be back in the gutter - probably quickly getting herself addicted to coke once more - without AWC giving her a leg to stand on. Amy Campbell had gotten her rubber arm twisted into quite a messy situation, and the worst part? Darcy couldn't know about any of it. He had to think she was genuine in her desire to publicize their relationship, or he'd make sure both of their tenures with Atlantic Wrestling Club ended very abruptly, and most likely with broken windows or an arson charge. ''Just in case you missed it... I, Michael CHAINZ Sloan, alleged rapist and all-star chauvanist slob, just called YOU the highest paid slut I know. That's an important title coming from me.'' ''Please, Sloan...'' Amy sighed, ''It's not a good day. Not that you give a shit, but I'm not feeling up to any witty banter.'' Sloan chuckled again, ''Feelin' karma's sting, eh? Can't say I know how ya feel,'' he said, and began to unbuckle his belt, ''Are we gettin' it on or what?'' ''Can you PLEASE listen to me and just go in a corner? I'll fucking sit in the closet if I have to... just until the cameras are gone from the doorway.'' Chainz smiled and opened the door, which Amy quickly ducked away from. He took it upon himself to launch a hand down his pants and announce 'Shittiest blowjob I've had in a while...'. When the redhead heard that, and he laughed 'Aww, that sucks. Nobody's around to hear it.', she punched him in the kidney and ran out the door under his arm. One more minute with that scumbag and she'd have snapped... or hung herself. His way of checking the hall was unique, to say the least, but it worked... and he seemed too concussed that night to give her too hard a time. The plane ride home would prove to be barren, but thoughtful... and that was basically the only thing she could truly be thankful for. A whore blessed with insight still unzips the John, though... and eith that phrase went her moment of contentment. Chapter 2... ===================================================================== Not many people know the feeling when you hit the mat. I think a lot of fans would liken it to some experience they had falling off of their bike as a kid. You know, when the wind's knocked out of you - you get that faint pain in your chest, almost like you're about to get a cold but all bunched into one tenth of a single second. You try to process what just happened, then you get back up and try riding back home to show mom your scrape. The key is that you get back on, even out of necessity. When you're pinned on a mat for three seconds with no air or thought of what will hurt where the next day, you don't have the chance to get back on... not for a long time. It's like falling off of your bike when you're five miles from home and watching the bike float up in front of you, then speed off on its own. You're not catching up unless you bust your ass, and it's gonna take a while to catch a magical bike like that. As much as I'm supposed to be a star, I don't win too often... and I hate appearing in front of cameras on shows unless I'm in the ring and far away from a mic. Unfortunately, after spending years trying to catch the elusive bike speeding ahead of me, I've come to a point where certain things are necessary - just par for the course. Promotion is a good example... but my personal relationships weren't ever something I imagined would be for sale. It pays, though. If I help AWC, AWC helps me... mainly by keeping me employed. If I don't get Darcy back to earth somehow, losing my ability or welcome to wrestle would spell certain death. In a way, doing what they want by putting my relationship kind of public DOES make Darcy a little more accountable for his actions. I guess in some sick way, I'm keeping tabs on him by having our interactions on tape and by keeping him on his toes, on the spot constantly being given decisions about 'us'. But who am I kidding? I'm fucking myself royally here. AWC has me backed into a corner, and they're poking me to pull the man I love into the same corner. That might not be such a bad thing if, y'know, our relationship didn't currently resemble the aftermath of an atomic bomb being dropped in George W. Bush's lap. Instead of pushing things in a positive way, I'm just airing the dirtiest, most grossly stained laundry I own. I don't think it's possible for me to make a decision that doesn't bite me in the ass here. I refuse, I lose my job and might never get Darcy back. I keep going with it and I might never get Darcy back, but I'll have a home and enough cash to eat. Plus... I'll get to wrestle. That's fighting out my emotions, and legally! If I can come out of this without getting punched by Darcy, or at least not completely ditched by him, I'm doing pretty well - no matter how many people are wearing shirts with my name on the front. This isn't to say that the shots I'm taking from this tequila bottle are necessary... but they're sure helping keep my priorities confused enough to slightly excuse what I'm doing to Darcy on TV each week. I'm probably the runner up for 'Worst girlfriend on the planet' right now, and it's sad that the only thing I can even imagine being proud of is waking up tomorrow and winning the title for 'Most hungover on a Tuesday morning'. Chapter 3... ===================================================================== She wasn't sleeping. She couldn't eat, and the normally hygenic young lady was even getting too lazy to bathe. After Darcy had been absent the past two weeks, Amy didn't at all expect what was in store for her when she opened the front door... Darcy, on the couch and absolutely NOT expecting her. ''Wh... I...'' She couldn't find words. After what she'd pulled on Fresh!, she didn't expect him to speak either. For once, Aimz was stunned stupid. ''Yeah, I live here, thanks.'' Darcy said, his words cutting sharp. ''What's YOUR excuse?'' ''I... I live here, too,'' she sighed, ''Alright, let's just skip to the fight... I honestly don't have the energy to do this...'' Darcy folded his arms. Skipping past the obvious barrier wouldn't be easy. ''One of the provisions of your living here, as my girlfriend, is that you aren't allowed to sleep with other guys. Believe me, I've no interest in screaming at you either, but at the very least, you need find the energy to convince me that you aren't in breach of said housing contract.'' She shivered and set her bags down, already looking on the verge of tears. ''Darcy,'' began the redhead, ''You know me. God, by NOW... you've got to know me. Do you honestly think I'd-- actually, no -- I'm not even acknowledging the idea. I don't HAVE to prove it to you, because you already know I'm not that low. I do a lot of things, but cheating isn't on the list.'' ''Well there's something else I thought you were above too, Amy.'' Darcy snapped back. ''That stunt you pulled on TV. Seriously Amy, what were you trying to accomplish? And if you didn't fuck that bastard Sloan, just what the hell WERE you doing?'' ''Think about it for a second. I didn't seem like myself, did I? And I didn't, because I wasn't myself... and I can't tell you exactly why without both of us ending up in the line to cash unemployment cheques.'' Darcy raised a brow. What? He quickly shook his head, though. It didn't really matter what motivated her to do it. Amy had recklessly and selfishly drawn a line in the sand, hoping to God that he would blink. He didn't. And now they were here. ''Alright, Amy...'' he started. ''I can think of...thirteen dirty words I could call you off the top of my head. But a liar isn't one of them. If you say you didn't do what I think you did, I believe you. But that still doesn't give you the right to put our problems under a spotlight for everyone to see. This isn't some 'Nick and Jessica' bullshit publicity-starved relationship, this is the real fucking deal.'' Amy couldn't believe that he didn't understand even slightly. Telling him what the company was telling HER would cause him to go into a tailspin and have them both out of work, washed up Shit Creek without a paddle, ring or dime. Not being direct hurt her, and she was already far too ashamed of her actions, but her silence would benefit them in the long run... by keeping a roof over their heads. ''Do you need glasses? Seriously... do I look at ALL happy to you?'' She didn't. It looked like she hadn't slept in a month. And Darcy guessed that in her prolonged insomnia, the smiles had come few and far between. This was normally the part of the fight where Darcy would relent, Amy would eventually start lowering her guard, and the two would realize they loved each other far more than whatever it was keeping them at odds. Indeed, Darcy felt the pangs in his stomach, trying to remind him that he loved the unhappy girl that stood before him. But he wouldn't let himself relent. He couldn't. The past few weeks saw both of them cheat death, Darcy's ex-girlfriend being kidnapped by Amy's friend, and a touch of domestic violence that Darcy still couldn't believe came out of him. To top it all off, Amy had decided to throw gasoline on the fire by putting it on camera. The rollercoaster ride of ''Fight-makeup-fight,'' as entertaining as it had been, had finally caused one too many jostles to the head. ''No, you don't,'' Darcy admitted. ''I hope because you realize that you've done nothing but make my life a living hell for the last month or so.'' ''And mine's been a smooth ride, right?'' Amy growled, ''Get over yourself and look at the big picture... if I could CHOOSE what happened on and off camera, I'd be a fucking champion with the sweetest little personality on the planet.'' ''Are you trying to tell me that you pulled that little stunt against your will?'' ''I'm not telling you anything directly. If you've got a feeling that someone else is cashing in on it... you might be right. Either way, it's not gonna stop,'' she said, ''We're both accountable for what we do, and we're not in a line of work that leans toward privacy.'' Darcy glared at her. This didn't make a lick of sense...not to somebody out of the loop, anyway. ''Amy, I'm going to ask you a question. And what ever answer you give me, I'll believe you. But be straight with me...'' Darcy took a deep breath, completely incredulous that he was about to ask her the words that came next. ''Is somebody gambling on our relationship?'' ... At least he wasn't onto the obvious. ''No.'' And thankfully, she didn't have to lie to convince him. Chapter 4... ===================================================================== Every whisper Of every waking hour I'm choosing my confessions Trying to keep an eye on you Like a hurt lost and blinded fool Oh no, I've said too much ... I've set it up. Every teenager will hate themselves at some point. By my age, you're supposed to be over that... but I can't seem to get past it. If you're doing something you know is wrong, you HAVE to hate yourself - or dislike who you are even slightly. I'm driving my boyfriend up the wall deliberately and, sadly, it's technically to save both of our jobs. Darcy knowing what the company currently expects of me would send him into a tailspin of epic proportions and if he didn't quit, he'd end up being fired... so I'm working between two scenarios here; Tell him and possibly have a relationship, but no jobs or keep the charade up, possibly lose our relationship and most likely still have jobs doing what we love. I know one thing for sure, and it's that he'd never forgive me if he left wrestling over something like this - so option two is my only real choice here. I play puppet to AWC's ratings machine and I get the opportunity to smack the shit out of somebody once a week as a means of coping, but I haven't felt that it's worth it. Not for a moment. There was a moment, though, where I'd thought that Darcy wouldn't bother going home this week. He hadn't chosen to show his face anywhere around me, and I assumed this week would be just like the two that passed before it - uneventful in my so-called love life. Instead of finding our cat lovingly pawing at my socks when I walked in, I saw Darcy out of the corner of my eye and my heart skipped a thousand beats. In a way, I wanted to say 'How dare you...' - but he could say the same to me, and basically did. This time, our fight was weird because... well... it wasn't much of a fight. Really, we should have been tearing one another apart, but I was oddly passive and let him dance around calling me a whore for what seemed like ages before I even bothered denying what I'd allegedly done with Chainz. If anyone with half a mind could understand the sort of pain that stems from being deceptive and feeling like a bigger sellout than an American Idol contestant from a rock band, I'd talk to them about it. Believe me, I would... but all I have is myself. That's always the way it's been, and it doesn't seem to be changing anytime soon. I can't go back to Canada, because I'm ashamed and far too embarassed to let my parents know how badly I'm screwing myself over out here. I can't go on TV and be myself, because I hate lights and microphones so much that 'me' is a fairly quiet individual for two hours every week. I sure as hell can't take comfort laying next to Darcy, who'd probably shove me through a wall if I even tried. All of my tears are gone, after last week I'm not even sure that I'm still capable of crying, and my complete loss of appetite has me so weak that I can't stand properly, but still so sick at the sight or thought of food that I can't imagine sitting down to a meal. The only upside is that I'm not stupid enough to spend the money this sham is gaining me on drugs. Booze, sure... but I'd have to put the muzzle of a rifle down my throat if I even dreamed of buying cocaine, let alone with money this tainted. I guess this is me trying to convince myself that I can't go any farther down into the pit I built the shovel to dig, but there's no way that this is the end of the misery for anyone in the situation. Except for Sloan, maybe - just because he seems to take pleasure in me realizing what a pathetic excuse for a human being I sometimes am. The AWC office staff seem to think it's cute enough, though - I keep getting praise, praise, praise! Great job last week, Amy! We love watching you rip out Darcy's heart like that... after all, you can't care THAT much - you're Aimz! You don't need men! Blahblahblahblahblahiwishiwasdead. I'm not at all happy to be a part of a company that promotes such horrible conduct in a relationship... but I seem to be content living off of a company that promotes horribly bloody violence and putting people through the roofs of motor vehicles. That's sad enough to be an Alanis Morrissette song. And yet, without hesitating (much), I'm gonna do it all again in a few days. I'm going to hate myself even more in a week, and within a month I'll have cracked hard enough to outright tell Darcy and have us both standing in line for unemployment! Yesterday, a hobo told me that life is beautiful because of the little things, and I wonder if he's including things like sick deception. I guess it would make sense, because you wouldn't know beauty without seeing ugly first, and that's why people like Michael Sloan still exist in today's society. It's also probably why there is such a thing as a fat stripper. My sweet little life gets to press on, though... and probably get worse. With any luck, I'll lose again this week and be back to an even crappier card slot the week after. If I get myself low enough in the company, I might be able to inch by without worry of selling some stupid god damned t-shirts. That, or I'll just post my pictures on the internet to a point that people get so tired of me, I won't even have to speak to get a reaction. It's 'the little things', like a webcam, that make life so much easier sometimes! Having opted to stay in a hotel after her altercation with Darcy at home, Amy had somehow managed to start getting even less sleep than she'd been getting with her life in complete turmoil. It added that sort of Kate Moss, punk/heroin-addict flair that photographers seemed to think was all the rage in advertising. That's why she was posed under a bridge in Northern California, wearing a painfully high thong and an 'Aimz' t-shirt with a cutesy little Anarchy symbol replacing the A in her name. It was a shirt she had absolutely no hand in designing - despite her artistic background - and a thong she definitely didn't select or desire to wear in public. ''Angrier. You've got to look angsty - your female fans want to relate to you, and that thong's got your male friends wanting to bang your brains out!'' As he swerved around her with a bulky camera in hand, Amy looked at the little man up and down, ''Can you do me a favor and just... not talk?'' He laughed aloud and put the camera to one side for a moment, ''I call the shots here! I'm trying to work my magic - after all, you aren't exactly the world's pretti--'' ''Finish that and I'll break this ungodly heel off in your as--'' she began, then looked at the 'AWC' logo on his van. After a moment of pondering the silliness of having a wannabe-French-arteeeest type as a wrestling photographer, the little redhead bowed her head. She had two options, but still no real choice but to do what she was told. So she continued, ''Err, sorry... bad day, that's all. I know these photoshoots are part of the job and all, I'm just not used to... errr... being quite so bare...'' Looking like a whore, and knowing that you're not even important enough for the company to pay for a decent - perhaps beachside or at all appealing - locale? Come on. It was the worst day, but only one of many more to come. ''It's all art in the end, Amelia! You are paid, the company has something for their website... everyone goes home happy!'' With each passing second, her dignity slipped out of grasp. With each passing second, AWC made another dollar... but, with each passing second, a redhead was losing something far MORE than dignity - a shot at happiness. She continued to cooperate with the photographer, if not only because he was acting too gay to be creepy. Besides, he was just a pawn in the bigger game that AWC was playing... Come to think of it... if he's a pawn, what does that make Aimz? Consider this the hint of the century. Consider this the slip that brought me to my knees.
