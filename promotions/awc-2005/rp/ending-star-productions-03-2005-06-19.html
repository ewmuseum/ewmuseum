Date: 06/19/05 OOC: I apoligize for this roleplay. I had a better one, but I had to reboot and lost my saved data. Sorry for not giving you a better match, Brenden. -------------- …The following is a presentation… …Of… …Ending Star Productions… Copywrite 2004-2005. _______________ It’s never-ending. This entire cycle the Ashtons are cursed with. The slow downfall into obscurity that managed to hit every single one of my brethren is now working its way toward me. And its working toward me in the exact same way it worked toward my brothers. The viral infection of the Ashton family, the curse that has been around for what seems to be generations is now finally affecting me. And the strange thing? I’m welcoming it with open arms, and for whatever reason, it’s not hitting me as hard as it hit my brothers. If it was alive, you could have said it was scared to make a move seeing as it has never been met with such apathy, but it’s not alive. If I had met this problem early last year I could have brushed it off, because I wasn’t past my prime. But then the curse hit Alex, the second youngest of my brothers, who wasn’t even close to his prime. Alex then promptly proceeded to vanish off of the face of the Earth. There is one constant. Everyone began to lose their wrestling “spark” right before their first big title shot. From Aaron, who brought the Ashton name into the main-stream; to Andrew, who made the name of Ashton what it is today. To even Alex who succeeded in beginning the burial of the name with his obvious disrespect for the sport. And now to me, who continues what Alex started, with my slow degeneration of the Atlantic Wrestling Club. Has my big break come? Is Tuesday night my big break? Is Tuesday night the passing of the Ashton torch? Is Tuesday night really my breakout, is it the night my name replaces Andrew’s at the top of the Ashton kingdom? It’s something I’ve dreading all of my life. What is easily my worst fear could be coming to pass. And what better time to do it than right now, what better time than the most hectic one of my entire life? I guess the universe is out to screw me. Unlike Andrew, unlike Aaron, unlike Timothy; none of them had anything to worry about before they won their breakout matches. Sure, they all said they had problems all of them tried to pass their daily lives off as a huge spectacle, except maybe Aaron, who was the original nonchalant member of my family… at least in my generation. And after they won their breakouts, they passed their toughest victories off as second-nature chores. I hated that so much. Every single time we would meet at my mother’s manor; they would use, what they would call “Solid Snake tactics,” and turn the subject of the dinner conversation to what they happened to accomplish in their “home feds.” Even Corey had some tales of grandeur to share, while Alex and I had nothing. We achieved a little in Indy feds and I was a legend in Japan. But it wasn’t enough to impress anyone, we didn’t even attempt to delude ourselves. Even when Timothy retired and I thought the bravado was finished, from at least him, he brings home his cockslut, Morgana, and gets my mother’s attention again. For the longest time, I couldn’t bear these “pleasant family reunions.” Every time I got a call from one of my brothers, someone went to the hospital in my next match… it was never me. And now comes my breakout. Now I have the chance to become what my brothers once were. Now I have the chance to become the very thing I despise so deeply. Now I have the chance to receive the attention my brothers received from our parents. Because, let’s face it, that’s the only reason the 6 of us became what we are today, that’s the only reason the six of us entered this profession. When you look back on it; Eric, who we always considered to be the oddball of our family, was really the one who was normal. While we were all trying to outdo one-another, he dropped out of the race, and pursued different professions; i.e. those that would give him an equal amount of pleasure, instead of being a mindless rodent running desperately through a maze trying to find the respect of our parents. Something I hated… and yet yearned so much for. I hated thinking about when I would become my family’s poster-boy. I hated to think that I would become a clone of one of my three eldest brothers. I hated to think that I would be an egotistical jackass, who would never even stop to consider the feelings of others. I hated to think that I would only amount to a man that lives in his own shadow, not opening up to anyone, living in fear of something… or someone. But what I dreaded most was becoming Andrew Ashton. I dreaded becoming the brother that created his own legacy through pure hard work and perseverance in the way that Andrew did. I couldn’t stand the thought of becoming a replaceable tool, something you could just throw away after it had run its course. I couldn’t bear to become something anyone could use to elevate themselves through the ranks. I couldn’t become a mindless workaholic. It just wasn’t an option; not for me, at least. And yet I’m the worst Ashton yet. I’ve become exactly what I’ve hated. I’ve taken all of the traits that I hated from Aaron, Tim, and Andrew and put them all together. I’ve become the epitome of hate and worry. I’ve made sure that my life has been cushioned in every way possible. I live like a goddamn rock star, I have friends in high places, and I can basically get a job any place I please. I’ve become Aaron. And yet, I’ve closed myself off from everything; refusing to do any sort of promotional activity, other than wrestling, and even that isn’t full-time. I’ve become completely unreliable, and share my thoughts and worries with no one, but myself. I’ve made sure that everyone worries about me, and I take pleasure in them knowing I’m gone, and wondering whether I’ll come back or not. I’ve become Timothy. And I have become what I’ve dreaded most. I’ve become the tool, and it was proven in Neon Wrestling, it was proven when I aligned myself with the people that used me to elevate their own worth. I’ve become the very mindless workaholic I’ve strived not to be. And even now, when I couldn’t care less about what happens in the AWC, I still find myself thinking about it… and it scares me. I’ve become Andrew. But in some strange way, I’ve been yearning for this. I’ve been yearning to become what I hate. I’ve been yearning to become the egotistical, closed-off workaholic. And I’ve been yearning for it because of one reason. In every federation they joined they became king. Whether it was Aaron as the king of Yahoo Wrestling, or Timothy as the king of Xtreme World Wrestling, or Andrew as the true king of New Era Wrestling; they were the greatest. They were the ones to beat; they received the immortal status so many people in this profession yearn for. They received everything they wanted. From recognition from people who didn’t even know them to world-wide respect, they received it. And now, it’s my turn. The Atlantic Wrestling Club has no order, it has no laws, and it’s under martial law. Those who want to amount to anything have to go against the so-called “figures of authority” to even receive recognition. It’s time to set up a rule… my rule. My turn has finally come. I’ve beaten one of your best, Pearl. I made your most sadistic bastard bleed. And now, it’s the dwarf’s turn. Now I go from the challenge of a monster to the challenge of a man named Crimson O’Malec, a literal dwarf. Apparently, you’re my breakout opponent. Consider yourself lucky to be in the ring with the King. And Crimson, when the night closes, I’ll show you why I’m King. Game Over, motherfuckers. The King has come. ___________ June 19, 2005 2:05 P.M. Narration: Alyson Rivers Location: Seattle, Washington “Ye… no… we…” I manage to spurt into the receiver, getting cut off every time I try. The man on the other end of the phone is a completely obnoxious employee of the Atlantic Wrestling Club, asking random questions about every imaginable subject, ever. And as if that wasn’t enough, the little bastard cuts me off when I start a sentence. “Hello? Are you there?” the annoyingly high voice of the man comes out of the receiver. “Ye…” I begin again. “I want to know whether Christopher Ashton will be at “Fresh!” this Tuesday.” And here we go. That’s how it’s been this past week, all of it obviously stemming from Chris’s victory over Hate. “Well, uh, yeah,” I stutter. “That’s great, tell Mr. Ashton that Pearl would like to see him in his office before the taping,” the annoying employee says. Oh, sweet Jesus. “Um, sure,” I say, the insecurity overrunning my voice. I was desperately waiting for the ‘click’ of the phone, signifying that the man had hung up, but it didn’t come. “I would love an authentic Ashton autograph…” is what came instead. … “…so if you could…” he begins, but is cut off by the tone of the line. I smile while shaking my head as I drop the phone. Yeah, I’m not even sure where Chris is, let alone all of his brothers. Thing is, Chris hasn’t called me since that night where he claimed he was at the airport and promised to be back soon. Well, it has been about three weeks and I haven’t heard from him at all. And by now, I’m worried. I know I saw him on “Fresh!” and I thought he’d come back and see me afterward, but nothing. I’m starting to get really scared for him right now. “Chris…” I let myself say in a worried whisper. I swivel my chair around and ace the skyline of the financial district of Seattle, and sigh while taking in the view. How could I just let him go? I should have known he would lie. I should have known he would go back on his promise. I should have gone after him. And then again, hindsight is 20/20. Maybe I’m just overreacting. Maybe I just miss him. I don’t even know anymore. I get up and walk to the water cooler near my office door. I grab a cup and pour myself some water. I just don’t understand why he wouldn’t tell me if he was doing something hazardous… and then again, that might just be the reason he wouldn’t tell me. “Oh, I just don’t know…” I leave off in a quiet yell. I take my final gulp of water and crush the paper cup in my trembling hand. I slam the cup down into the trash can and go plop myself down on the leather couch. This is ridiculous; I shouldn’t be worrying so much. Chris is fully able to take care of himself…at least I hope he is. I sniffle and rub my eyes before standing up and walking over to the view of the skyline. I move close to it and look down at the crowd below, the crowd of miniscule people, all the hustle and bustle of the business section of one of America’s biggest cities… and, strangely, I feel completely at peace. “Please, Chris, come back to me.” To Be Continued…
