Label it anyway you want, but you cannot deny, I am the master of it. You see, there are many layers to the way we as human beings perceive what we see. There are many different methods of accepting what you see, ignoring what you can’t, and still remaining alert. For the past few weeks, I’ve been digging at the heels of Tim Shipley. Of course I’ve indulged you in the tale of how I cam to be, where my motivations find route, and why I had to don the mask; but it has all been adding to pieces of this puzzle. Now that it is formed, and my attacks on Tim Shipley have relented, another series of events has progressed. This has resulted in a subsequent equation: Pierce Lavelle is guilty by association. But Pierce Lavelle also embodies the mountain I must climb. And then Adam Masters comes along. The Educator. He extends the torment I must put Tim Shipley through. I could have ended it at the Battle of Britain. I’d never have had to win that Frontier Title from Andy M. It would have been around my waste from the beginning. But the Educator. When Hate beat me, it was marginal. I’m not forgetting that loss and it will return paid in full. Even when Tim Shipley defeated me, I was able to take a step back and bite my tongue… for now. But the Educator. It was an instant feeling, an unshakeable weight on my shoulders that wouldn’t leave with smoking a joint. I couldn’t relax. Something was bothering me and I couldn’t figure it out. Is it my ego? Knowing that I’m better someone, but losing to them, it can get to you. But again I ask, am I too cocky to accept the fact that someone outclassed me? On a rare occasion? Yes. I knew it, my instincts told me. And then the Prometheus Serum rains from the sky. I had a high amount of disrespect for Core after I left. The truth of said Serum came out in the form of Kassidy Drake, an interviewer who turned out to be Smiley… The man who beat me for Pride Contender-ship. The man that “gave” me the Classic Title. All because of a supplement. But the Educator. This wasn’t for a chance at greater things. A chance for a chance for a chance at greatness. This was the main stage. Tim Shipley and the Illustrious Face-Eater. But the Educator won, not the Illustrious Face-Eater. And why? All because of a supplement. I’m not going down like a bitch. I’m not letting Adam Masters walk away gently because he was destroyed by Hate, oh no. If you know anything about me, you know that physical pain isn’t enough to satisfy me, watching someone experience this. I’m going to torture Adam Masters to a similar vein that I tortured Tim Shipley. I want to hold no responsibility in the pain he experiences; I merely want to show him the door outside that he’s been hiding this whole time. I want the Educator to make the choice in taking his own life. I want him to realize he is better use dead than alive. And I’ll be damned if Pierce Lavelle or David Harber is going to take that chance from me, merely for the sake of morality. Morality is dead. So is chivalry. And so is respect. It’s all about staying ahead of the game. Having control. Having power. That may mean a few underhanded tactics. Like, placing moles. Sabotaging emotion. Maybe even fucking them up in the ring. I’ll see Pierce Lavelle on this road to get to where I need to be, there’s no doubt. In fact, I wouldn’t doubt that I’ll need his presence coming up, but it’s just whether he knows I’m watching. And if that fucking bitch does her job right, Pierce will have no idea who beats him to the punch… Until I take his Transatlantic Title. ... You guys cannot see me, but I am smiling profusely at this prospect. So how’s work? Shut up. So how’s work? ...it was okay. Is he back, yet? I don’t know when he’ll be back. He hasn’t come in this past week. The show’s been over for days, what could he be doing? Are you sure this is going to work? Of course I’m fucking sure! He fucking lives in Harrisburg on the weekends… I thought. You thought? Shut the fuck up. Now pay attention, because he’s crafty. A real P.I. type of guy, so he may question your motives a bit. What exactly am I supposed to do? We already talked about this! I know, but this game is getting a bit to complicated. I don’t want this—to become my life, y’know? Listen, keep your trap shut and do as I say, and it will be finished soon, believe me? I won’t put you in a situation you don’t like for longer than I have to, and I just want this to be over so I can get you back by my side. Really? Of course. Now be alert. Because when Pierce comes in, you have to catch his attention. Adam? ...yes, Kelli? I love you I... love... you, too-click. I had no leads at this point. No clue as to knowing where to go, when it came to Adam Masters and his where abouts. Because really, that’s all I want. None of this Prometheus Serum garbage concerns me, unless someone has to jack themselves up to beat me again. All I care about is finding the Educator and eating his eye-balls, and I can’t do that. Because Pierce Lavelle is a pansy and likes to dick about. So I go to Harrisburg. The way I look at it, there are probably some loose ends I need to tie up. Kelli Stoner: Kelli is an idiot. A sack of a good pair of tits and a loose vagina who constantly pledges herself to me. Why I’ve taken advantage of this girl for as long as I have is beyond me, but what’s worse is why she puts herself up to this abuse. I don’t take advantage of her sexually, that’s not my style. But I can’t count how many times I have turned down her advances. I know I drive her away and I know it kills her, but I can’t watch her loving me. She makes me feel creepy, like teenage boy looking for love in a daycare center. I don’t feel right with her. So do I enjoy making her heel unhappy? No, but I feel I have to. I feel I need to get her attention focused on something else, something productive. So I let her manage me. In Core Wrestling, we had an alright relationship. She kept her sights on the main focus, my career. As long as there were no emotional attachment, she could stick around. And then, Zach came into the picture. You remember Shaunna? The girl who “haunted” me, however you wanted to put it? Well that was not a random occurrence. Earlier this year, I was involved in a fatal car-accident that involved Shaunna Prather, a teenage girl who crossed the street at the wrong time. Wasn’t my fault, but that didn’t mean I couldn’t help anyone out. Here was some kid, who had just watched his girlfriend cross when he told her not to. He said, “Don’t cross.” And she told him it was okay. And now she’s dead, because I killed her. And this kid? He watched it all happen. He watched me kill her. How could I not help the kid out? I brought Zach along as an insurance policy. Someone who would thank me for the chance I gave him, and use his potential. But instead, he never let go of that grudge of me killing his girlfriend. Fine, that’s okay. But he didn’t have to prove he was a terrible human being by taking advantage of me; getting fucked up with me over the days off between shows, and sometimes at the shows themselves. He didn’t have to fuck, Kelli, either, and create this twisted relationship we all have going now. So now, back in Harrisburg, I’m waiting for the first stage of my plan to be executed. Zach and I wait in the motel room I rented for he and Kelli, while we try our best to make up small conversation. He probably resents me for ditching him at Core Wrestling, and not telling him or Kelli about my time in the AWC. But I couldn’t give a shit. He’s my employee and I’ll make him do as I please. And I tell him, I need Kelli to do something. For me. He can’t object. Kelli calls me, and lets me know. “Pierce is in town, just like you said he’d be.” I told her his plain touched down yesterday. He most likely wanted to stop at home before catching up with his regular routine. “Just play coy, right?” I tell her, yes. Be friendly. Be memorable. Make him want you. And Zach doesn’t hear a word of this. I get off the phone with her. “I’ll make you proud.” She tells me over the hustle and bustle of restaurant’s kitchen. And then I turn to Zach. I smile wide. Phase two is about to begin, and no one even knows what it is. Kelli has a surprise coming, Pierce has an even bigger one. But I figure, after this... Zach Midas will be the Federal Court System’s problem, not mine. The air had cooled immensely, but I’m warm. I’m too fucking warm! The fucking fleece on this collar keeps rubbing my neck the wrong way. The way I sweat, I’ve got to have a fucking rash from it. “Dude, are you fucking hot?!” “I’m in a god-damned windbreaker and its forty-fucking-degrees outside; I’M FUCKING COLD.” Zach snapped back at me. “Stop bitching and drink your Old English.” I wish I had his Old English. Malt liquor can be tasty when you’re already waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaasted. Maybe he’ll give me a sip. If I look long enough, he’ll probably offer it to me. Fucking fleece, my neck hurts. So, my eyes. They’re screaming for a chance to drink that OE, I’m looking at the bottle so hard my eyes begin to peel back the label. The soggy label pulls itself from the bottle, finding its true form as a butterfly in the wind, on a path of mystery right into the ground. Zach littered, and I wish I could tell him how wrong he is for doing so. I mean, why should he fucking litter? What’d this city do to him, huh? Uh… are we allowed to drink in public? I wish I could put my lips on that bottle. I wish I could drink his Old English. My body longs for malt-goodness, and I’ve had enough. I have to ask, or else I’m just gonna kill him and take it. Who would notice? I could go in that alley over there, and no one would see. AH! I can even take his windbreaker. Bet that will keep me cooler than this fucking- gah, I hate fleece. “Hey, a.... let’s *hick-up* over thurr...” I mumble. And point. Towards the alley. He’d better know what I mean, I know what I meant. “Whu-huh? What’re you-“ “GIVE ME A DRINK!” Everyone across the street looks at me. Fuckin’ go call cops, see if I get arrested!
