Trials and the Consequence - The Authority

11 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/06 Why is it that one word inspires so much desire and greed amongst the population? Why is it so that the word creates an aura of fear and respect, but rarely both? Why do we all crave it, then suffer once we have it? Authority is a nice offshoot of power in that it stems from a supposed legitimate source. It commands credibility and officialty like no other. It demands that you recognise your opinions and wisdom on the subjects. It is an incredibly potent position of power to both behold and wield. Yet few on this earth truly achieve it and even fewer manage to mount a stranglehold upon it. There’s a simple reason for it and it equals anything you’ve ever heard about someone obsessing over power; they will do anything in their own power to achieve more. Unsurprisingly the natural foil for authority is rebellion. It is in stark contrast to the pompous, official rumblings from the authoritarian. Instead the rebellious among us shirk from such pretensions and attempt to break new ground, to embark on a journey that will upend the authority and change it forever. Sadly what so many people forget is that the end result of this journey (if successful) is that they become the authority. There is no choice in the matter and then the cycle begins anew. This can be seen as a necessary element of evolution. This can be seen as a necessary evil on the path of a megalomaniac who isn’t quite all there. And then it can be seen as something that, if it isn’t done, will threaten to halt the cycle which will only lead to ruination. So it follows that at every turn a rebel shall emerge to wreak havoc with the establishment, make his daring ploy to snatch the authority for himself and then rinse and repeat the steps anew. Ignatius Lisieux is one such rebel. I smiled grimly when I saw the man enter the AWC ring at Coast to Coast, shaking the hand of one Dr. Drake. I could see it from the beginning what way things were going to go down. It didn’t matter that he was signing the contract for his own personal reasons. All that mattered was the fire in his eyes. You knew right from the beginning that he wouldn’t be kept on a leash by anybody irrespective of the authority involved. And Dr. Drake is one powerful man. Without a care in the world Ignatius knocked the owner of AWC to the canvas out cold though. It was almost an afterthought. The person wasn’t important, the symbol was. It is the statement of the rebel. To invoke the masses one must enervate them, drum up the support from within. To do this there must be a symbolic act which people can attest to and agree upon. The denouncement of authority is the oldest, and most powerful statement one can make. It is also the most dangerous. You are laying your claim out in front of you from the start, putting your cards out for all to see. Not exactly the way to open your proceedings but a gambit that has worked in the past. Lisieux is no slouch either. All that need be said about the man is his status upon leaving PRIME. I don’t even need to mention it because everyone knows it. That’s all there is to it. His stature and presence have seen to it that his statement has been visualised by everyone involved. It is the power of the rebel. With those two in place Lisieux has entrenched himself in the midst of AWC with this one gesture, a gesture which I take slight offence to. This is problem that lies within the statement of the rebel. This is the crux of why I feel that such a symbol can go beyond what is necessary. In doing such an outward and blatant attack on the authority, he brings the wrath of two separate parties upon himself, the latter of the two being totally random and undetermined by the rebel. He cannot expect to know this party because it can be anyone. Simply put, he pisses off the other rebels. Wrestling has changed a lot over the past twenty years and beyond. From the inception of the nationalisation of the WWF, to the Monday night wars, to the current glut of federations, one and all fighting for the right to be called as the number one product in the world. So many fantastic wrestlers making their way from continent to continent, town to town to prove that they are the best that can be found in the world. The arguments roll over and over across borders without ever a definitive answer simply because of perspective. One thing that has no changed however are gambits like those of Lisieux’s. All wrestlers dream of the authority and how they can shape matters in their own favour. So they all try to climb the mountain and enact the change that they wish to have. Each wrestler does it in their own way. Having been around for a good fifteen plus years now, I have some sort of idea of how this works. So what is it that pisses me off? What is it that annoys me about this? He’s an outsider, he’s a rebellious upstart who’s barely been in the industry six years and is already commanding power far beyond his years. He’s claiming the respect that is only wrought through years of toil and grit. Toil that I have ploughed through with the determination of the bull. It makes me sick to see him jumping through those hoops without a care. I don’t care for upstarts because reputation accounts to nothing once you try and abuse it yourself. Sure let others use that reputation as a way to be fooled, but never let yourself be the one to hawk your wares as a market trader would on the street. Lisieux is doing nothing but this. Furthermore, he’s just treading on my fucking territory. And I can’t have that. *** ‘Jack?’ ‘Yeah…who is this?’ ‘It’s Sylvia. We need to meet as soon as possible. It seems things have advanced far quicker than I imagined.’ ‘Alright, I’ll meet you at my gym?’ ‘No, that would be too obvious. Head for the Burger King just near the exit onto the highway. Be there in twenty minutes.’ A solid click was heard from her end of the phone and then silence. I palmed the cell phone closed and jammed it into my pocket. The silence of the house left a subtle undertone of malevolence in my mind, the very air seeping into me with menace. Jessica had been away for the past two months now on scouting trips for my gym. While I had grown more and more despondent by the blockade imposed by Fr. Pearson, Jessica had pressed onwards and upwards, looking for the best the world had to offer, and to try and convince them to come and work under my tutelage. It was nearly seven o’clock in the evening by now and it would take almost fifteen minutes to make it to the restaurant. Best get going so. *** You know, I wonder if the McDonald’s brothers ever dreamt that their products would be shilled to you by a clown and people who were more than happy to take your order? Or the fact that you can’t go more than ten blocks in most cities without encountering them. I doubt somehow that that was their vision when they opened up the chain. They were never much to look at, just a mixture of soothing colours and tones that were supposed to create an atmosphere of calm and serenity. I didn’t care much for the overall appearance and didn’t take much time to admire it. Instead I had business to attend to. ‘Would you like fries with that?’ ‘Yeah sure, and a coke.’ I did my best to hurry it along but there was no fear of something taking longer than it should regarding McDonald’s. I got my cheeseburger and greasy fries within the minute, along with the half drank, full of ice coke to complement it. Smiling like I was twelve I moved away, prodding the cheeseburger to make sure it was dead. Sylvia Carter was sitting off in a corner, playing with a nugget of chicken like it was something about to be culled. Her gaze flickered from side to side, a little anxious but nothing more. She was dressed a lot more provocatively this time, discarding the out of sorts business uniform for a more fashionable dress. It was figure hugging and revealed a little bit more than intentioned. Not that I was going to complain, it’d just make things far more complicated. ‘Punctual, I like it.’ ‘Well, you made it sound like it was urgent so why are you surprised?’ ‘True. And it’s true that it was slightly urgent.’ ‘Oh?’ I slurped on the vile coke to wet my parched throat. It was like bile going the wrong way but I sucked it up nonetheless. She had me intrigued. ‘Do you remember that Fr. Pearson likes to travel every month or so around the state to do a number of evangelistic like sermons to the people?’ ‘How can I forget? The last one around was the demonic inclinations of professional wrestlers. He made it sound like we were sucking at the teat of the devil and then spraying his seed like whores.’ ‘That was…well that wasn’t what I expected you to answer with.’ ‘Sorry, it just really bothered me.’ ‘No problem.’ A quick pat on my hand before she continued didn’t escape my consciousness. She smiled briefly with it and then reverted her face to normal, as if it hadn’t happened. ‘Anyway, it turns out that there’s one particular destination that he hasn’t failed to go each and every time. He rotates all the other towns that he travels to, but this one he makes sure to visit. I think it’s worth investigating purely because I reckon that he’s been doing a little more than simply evangelising if you get me.’ ‘That’s a serious allegation Sylvia, do you know anything for sure?’ ‘No, but I need to go there to see if I can…if we can find anything out.’ ‘Wait, you want me to go with you?’ ‘Yes, because with the both of us there we’ll be able to cover more ground.’ ‘I’ll be noticed though…which should make it easier for you to get around unnoticed?’ ‘Exactly.’ Huh, the girl was smart. I’ll give her that. ‘When shall we go then?’ ‘Tonight Jack.’ ‘I figured as much. Ready to go then?’ ‘Yeah, I’m not eating this crap anyway.’ ‘Me neither.’ Without a word we left the restaurant, heading for my car. She instinctively knew which it was, something I was surprised about but then again it is a recognisable car. She on the other hand had no car which puzzled me. I let it hang in the back of my mind though and returned to the task at hand. ‘So what’s the town we’re heading to?’ ‘Albany.’ ‘Albany it is.’ *** Authority is something that can’t be ignored. It can be attacked and assaulted, but whether or not it emerges triumphant authority always gets its say in. It’s just the way things work. Now on the way to Albany I remembered that once again authority was going to be assaulted, this time the authority of the town. One cannot forget the path we must take once we embark. The end result is one becomes authority. That has always been the goal for myself; to be that authority. It was never going to be any other way, whether it was in or outside the ring. That’s why I just can’t let Lisieux slip on by past me. It is my authority to reinvent, not his. Fresh! is the perfect opportunity to halt him before he can embark on his own journey. I have toiled for too long to let him put a spanner in the works. It is not that I don’t admire him for having the same goals, he’s just going to be getting in my way. Authority must be exerted by those who can. At Fresh! I was going to do that. *** As I looked out the window of his car the sun began to set for the day. Soon it would be night and the beginning of the plan. Things would work out how they were supposed to, least that’s how Dr. Drake said they would. I was sceptical but Murphy didn’t realise any of it yet. It would be too late before he did. The authority on the issue would have his way no doubt. So begins the road to Golgotha.
