Episode One: Bar Room Brawl

19 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th August 2005 SCENE 1 INT. SAM'S BIKER HEAVEN, NIGHT SAM'S IS A RUN-DOWN HOLE OF A BAR RIGHT OUT IN THE MIDDLE OF ROUTE 41, SURROUNDED BY DESERT. ITS CLIENTELE CONSISTS ALMOST ENTIRELY OF A GROUP OF HELL'S ANGELS. THEY SIT BY THE STAGE, WATCHING A MIDDLE-AGED STRIPPER PUT ON A HALF-HEARTED SHOW. OUR HEROES SIT AWAY FROM THIS GROUP AT A SMALL TABLE NEAR THE BAR, NURSING A PAIR OF BOTTLES OF BUDWEISER. V.O.: And now, dearest fans, we present to you number one situation comedy! It am make you laughing long time! In first episode – SUPER AMERICAN Gakuto and MR EVIL Tamura cruise for some hotties! Fo' sho'! *musical interlude* TAMURA: ここにしている私達はである何Shinji... か? GAKUTO: [sighing] I told you, daddy-o – we're cruising for chicks! TAMURA: [pausing to survey the room before taking a sip for his beer and turning to his friend] …Shinji に... すべてのこれらの女の子顔毛がある. LAUGH TRACK: Hahaha! GAKUTO: That's it, brother – I'm not gonna have you rain on my parade! From now on, you keep your filthy mouth shut! [Both sigh and look in opposite directions.] TAMURA: 私は単一の思考との殺すことができる知っている。 GAKUTO: You best quit your jibber-jabber, Darth. TAMURA: [jumping to his feet] 私、ディックの部分がほしいと思うか。 GAKUTO: Maybe I DO want a piece of you, 'MISTERRRRRRRRRR EVIL!' [With that, Shinji launches himself at Gakuto, landing punch after punch upside his head. Before long, Gakuto gets the upper hand and body-slams his friend on the bar, before breaking a bottle and holding it to his face] GAKUTO: WHO'S EVIL NOW?! MAN: A-hem. [Camera swings around to show a burly officer of the law, replete with nightstick and flanked by a pair of equally-large bikers] GAKUTO: …mommy? [COMMERCIAL BREAK] SCENE 2 INT. POLICE STATION (CELLBLOCK), DAY OUR HEROES SIT HANDCUFFED IN SEPARATE BUT ADJACENT CELLS. BOTH WEAR BANDAGES AROUND THEIR HEADS. GAKUTO: This is all YOUR fault, punk. TAMURA: 私か?! 私は寿司にだれでも表面試みなかったし、回さなかった! GAKUTO: Yadda, yadda, yadda, quit your whining won't you? I didn't even cut ya! TAMURA: 従って平均ある場合もある。[starts to cry] GAKUTO: Again with the tears… WOMAN: Yes, officer, I'm sorry – it won't happen again. OFFICER: Make sure it doesn't – they don't like outsiders in these parts. [The woman and the police officer walk into shot. We see the woman from the Team's point of view…] G&T: Ahh…Summer… [The picture becomes rose-tinted and sentimental violin music plays in the background as the Officer unlocks the cells and uncuffs the boys, who run into Summer's outstretched arms.] SUMMER: Ahh…come on boys…let's get you home. [The lads nod as Summer tenderly directs them outside…] Scene 3 EXT. POLICE STATION, DAY THE SCENE TAKES PLACE ON A WIDE OPEN PLAZA OUTSIDE THE SAME POLICE STATION AS IN THE PREVIOUS SCENE. THE VIDEO IS STILL ROSE-TINTED AND THE VIOLIN MUSIC CONTINUES TO PLAY… GAKUTO: Summer, thank you so much for bailing us out, babydoll. [As soon as they're out of sight of the police station, Summer's smile falls. Her eyes narrow and the violin music stops with a sound like a needle scratching across vinyl.] SUMMER: WHAT D'YOU THINK I AM? HAVE YOU ANY IDEA HOW EMBARRASSING THIS IS, YOU TURKEYS?! [she slaps Gakuto across the back of the head. She goes to strike Tamura too, but he shies away. She relents.] Ahh hell, Tam – I can't stay mad at you. But YOU, [points at Gakuto] you little sucka – what the hells were you thinkin' of? You KNOW Tam doesn't mean anything he says. TAMURA: …[gestures for Gakuto to translate] GAKUTO: [shouting] SHE SAYS YOU'RE A LOSER WITH A B.O. PROBLEM! TAMURA: [cries] SUMMER: [fixing Gakuto with a glare whilst taking Tamura into her arms] You just bought yourself some time in the pen, mister. GAKUTO: [grinning from ear to ear] It was worth it. BOOYAH! GIMME FIVE MAN! [he tries to high five Tamura. Tamura looks at Gakuto's hand then buries his face into Summer's shoulder] SUMMER: Shush, shug, it's okay…Summer's here. C'mon. Let's go home. Scene 4 INT. TSE'S APARTMENT, EVENING THE CAMERA IS FOCUSSED ON THE FRONT DOOR. IT BURSTS OPEN AS GAKUTO FLIES THROUGH IT. SUMMER: IF YOU EVER TOUCH ME THERE AGAIN… GAKUTO: I know! Damn woman! [Tamura follows timidly behind.] SUMMER: Come on, Tam, let's go watch Wheel Of Fortune. TAMURA: YESS! WHEEE OFF FORTUNOOOO! SUMMER: …right. [Summer takes Tam's hand and they walk into the living area, out of shot. Gakuto gets up off the floor, rubbing his shoulder, and notices a letter on the mat. We switch to a shot from his POV – we see the orange AWC logo stamped in the top-left corner. Switching back to a wide shot, we watch him tear the envelope open and read the letter. Switching back to a POV shot we see the words 'match'…'England'...and the signature 'David 'Pearl' Harber'.] [We switch to a shot of Summer and Tamura watching TV in the living area, when we hear Gakuto from out of shot.] GAKUTO: [getting gradually more excited before shouting out…] YAHOOOOOOO! TAM! TAAAAAAAMURAAAAAAAA! MATCH! [At the sound of that word, match, Tamura stiffens. He holds himself a little taller and cocks his head at an angle. He slowly, methodically, gets to his feet. As Gakuto enters the room, Tam stares him down.] TAMURA: … GAKUTO: It's a battle royal for number one contention for the Alliance Championships in AWC… [Tamura nods] GAKUTO: We have to pack…it's Sunday night in England. [Tamura nods again] GAKUTO: …well come on, man! [Tamura raises his hand.] GAKUTO: What? TAMURA: [slowly] 悪の力と私達は私達の敵を征服する- のために私達が暗い主の驚くばかりの強さを利用するときだけ私達の夢をなる現実に缶詰にしなさい。時間は立ち、戦うことを来た。私達は私達自身の名前を作るか、または試みることを死ぬ. [Upon finishing his speech, Tamura leaves to pack.] SUMMER: What did he just say? GAKUTO: 'With the power of evil we will vanquish our enemies - for only when we harness the awesome strength of the Dark Lord can our dreams become reality. The time has come to stand and fight. We shall make a name for ourselves or die trying.' SUMMER: …why can't I hook up with NORMAL guys? NEXT TIME…AN UNEXPECTED VISITOR! OOC NOTE: I realise this is a little late coming, but the style is radically different to what I'm used to writing and this entire piece has taken me around six hours to complete (from conception to final draft). I've tried to make it as movie-script like as possible. Feedback is not only welcome but begged for heh. Cheers, Kit
