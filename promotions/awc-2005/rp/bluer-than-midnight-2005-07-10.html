Bluer Than Midnight

10 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/10/05 Things were beginning to be unbearable. As I walked the streets people were scurrying away, covering their faces and making looks towards me that were putrid. I realized that it wasn’t the usual crap, that it wasn’t the whole “Holy fuck, you’re hideous!” shtick. This happened everyone in a while, and it was always my fault, one of the many things that I blamed myself for. I forgot to change my bandages. I had problem gone months with these on, the dirt starting to come to life on them. I must’ve just been used to the smell, because that’s what I was around all the time. It makes sense, though, that they’d smell so putrid. In the past months I have passed out in alley ways, been shoved into the dirt, and overall made a mess of myself. I couldn’t clean this bandages because they’d just get ruined. I didn’t really need to shower anyways, and in fact I’ve only showered my skin twice since the accident (which is an experience that I would imagine would be worse than all the torture in the world). I rented a room in a motel somewhere in the dark part of the city, needing access to a bathroom. This room was small and dark, the lights too dim for any kind of reading or writing in the main part of the room. The bed was filthy and so was the carpet, stained with only God knows what. The TV pretty much epitomized the place with a giant hole in the center, the little shards of glass still around it. I wasn’t here for a good night’s sleep, though, I was only here for business. The bathroom was well lit, which was quite a shame because I really didn’t need to see the facilities I was using. The toilet was covered in old dry shit as was the shower, all over the walls and the utilities. The sink was stained yellow like the water that flowed from it, and the mirror reflected this with a big crack right down the center of it. It wasn’t much, but it was all I needed. Changing my bandages is one of the most painful things I do. The reason that I don’t do it all that often is that I can’t stand the pain that comes from it. Its necessary, though, and that’s the only reason why I do it. Otherwise, I’d lose all the little human contact I have right now. I stood in front of the mirror and stared at myself. This was, by far, the dirtiest I had ever been. It was almost as if I was brown with little specs of off white underneath everything. I could see the dirt just oozing all over me, the greases and the stain and all that shit. It was pretty bad. I put my bag on the counter top and took out a few things. First, my trust knife, which I have used on anything that has needed it. Next, a couple rolls of bandages. They weren’t the brightest things on Earth, but they looked brand new to me. And, finally, a piece of rubber. I looked at my self one more time before I started it, shaking my head. “Here it goes…” I said to myself as I grabbed the knife. Slowly and cautiously I slit across the first layer of bandages on me, peeling them off. I started to unravel and, as I got closer to the skin, you could see something dark. Pausing for a moment, I placed the rubber between my teeth, biting down. Now that I was ready to continue, I continued to unravel until I got down to the final layer. Staring at it as the tears formed in my eyes, I bit down as hard as I could and started ripping the bandages off. You could hear bits and pieces of my burnt skin flying off with the bandages, the pain incomprehensible. I screamed as I clenched my teeth, finally removing that last bit from my forearm and throwing that discarded bandage to the side. The tears were now flowing down my face, my muttered whelps sounding pitiful. A 60 year old man weeping like a 4 year old boy…how pitiful. But it was a price I paid for being alive, and slowly and horribly I continued to change the bandages on my body. --------------------------------------------------------------------- As the hours passed and my body grew numb from pain, I was finally able to finish the job. I was weak and weary and dizzy, though, all the screaming and crying and bleeding I did was quite draining. I stumbled into the main room and looked around, my vision blurry and my thoughts slowly deteriorating. Things were spinning and my blood was rushing. I had to get to something, my body was not going to take it anymore. Without hesitation I rushed myself to that disgusting bed and flung myself onto it, passing out in a moment’s notice… --------------------------------------------------------------------- (In the middle of the jungle, there is chaos as soldiers are taking cover and firing into the distance. Explosions are occurring while bullets whiz by, the destruction and events overwhelming.) “OH MY GOD!” One soldier says, “OH MY GOD! WE’RE GOING TO DIE!” The soldier rocks back and forth, curled up in a ball, crying. “Fuck, they’re everywhere!” One man screams, “We need to get artillery!” (The chaos ensues as the camera draws a gun and starts firing into the jungle. The camera, apparently, is actually a person, most likely Drake.) “Private Coleman!” One man screams, “Get over here!” (The camera runs over through the thick brush and dives next to the man, staring right at him.) “Call in the coordinates for the Artillery attack! Tell them to get all around us! Hurry!” (The camera grabs a radio communicator and starts rifling off names, only understandable if you know what they’re saying.) “TAKE COVER! SARGE, WATCH OUT!” (In a split second, the camera looks behind the man barking orders at it to see an ensuing explosion shooting its way. The camera sees the face of the man dying in the fire while it covers its own view up with its hands, the flames engulfing it in noise…) --------------------------------------------------------------------- I shot awake at that very instant. Everything was so realistic about that, everything so scary. It was exactly how it happened, how the accident occurred. This wasn’t the first time this has happened, though. Ever since the accident happened in that jungle I’ve had that dream. Sleep has never occurred for more then 5 or 10 minutes…I mean, how could you stay asleep after that nightmare? I decided that I needed to get out of that room as it only haunted me and headed off into the city. It was dark outside and not many people would be out where I was going…rather, not many of the people that I detested. The only folk that were out and about at this time of night were the type that had problems of their own, some bigger than mine. They looked at me and saw something normal and, in some cases, saw something they could envy. It was peaceful in a sense but, like everything else, it was lonely. The place I decided to head into was called, quite simply, “Steve’s Diner”. It was a little run down place with a few people who looked like regulars solely in that they had that dead look to them. The place wasn’t exactly filthy, but I definitely wouldn’t use the five second rule if my food fell onto the floor here. As I sat down at the bar I grabbed a menu and looked it over, even though I already knew what I wanted. “What can I get you?” The man behind the counter wasn’t exactly chipper, but he was the most up-beat guy I’ve spoken to in a while. He had a sad smile that told you that this diner was all that he had going in his life, which meant that he really wasn’t anything much at all. “Cheeseburger with onions, no fries, no nothing, just the burger.” I tried to flash a smile but it felt awkward, so instead I just made eye contact, something I usually didn’t do. You could tell he was taken back by my looks but that he was trying to hide it. He looked at me funny while not making any sort of eye contact at all, kind of shying away. As he grabbed my menu and placed it back on the counter he headed back into the kitchen, cooking my burger with the finest of attention. The other patrons of the restaurant…well, apparently, the other PATRON in the restaurant was one of the saddest looking people ever. He was grotesquely overweight, his fat belly hanging out of his ragged black t-shirt. There was no way he had money, so it absolutely bewildered me that he was obese. He stared down at his food, slowly lifting it to his mouth with caution, placing the morsels between his teeth. With every chew you could see him getting fatter, his eyes glazing over as this must’ve been the most activity he’s had all day. By looking at him you could see the loneliness all over his body. Nobody but a business man would want him, and even the business men would leave his poor ass on the curb. I was now sick with pity, so I did the normal thing I do in any restaurant and stare down at the counter. I looked at the counter below me blankly, thinking about the pain that ensued earlier. I was drawing empty when suddenly, a plate slid in front of me. “Here you go, son. Would you like a drink as well?” His smile was begging me to buy something else from him “No thanks.” I shook my head and stuck out my head, immediately digging in, when, without reason, he interrupted me. “Are you sure? Not even a Coke? Sprite? No water?” It was officially annoying, and I needed to make this cease to exist. “Look,” I said, “First off, don’t call me son, I’m probably older than you, if not barely younger. Second, when a man says NO, he means NO! God damn it, can you take a hint? The only reason I came in this here shit hole was because I thought it’d be the one place where I could not have conversation…but I guess I’m fucking wrong!” Where that came from, I don’t know, but the second I finished I regretted it. I looked at him and saw the lower lip trembling with innocence, his eyes growing deep and empty. He slowly turned and headed back into the kitchen. I could hear those silent tears from the counter, and it hurt. Losing my hunger, I left the restaurant and headed back onto the street. I forgot which way my hotel room was…didn’t matter though. At this point, the hotel was the least of my worries… -----([([([END FEED])])])----- --------------------------------------------------------------------- “The harder I try, I can’t never, ever, ever find peace in this life…” --The The
