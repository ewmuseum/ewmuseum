The Paranoia Continues to Build.

30 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/30/2006 {{Flashback}} The camera slowly fades into what appears to be a dark, dank, arena that is literally packed with onlookers. The location and current time is unknown; but, the intensity of the crowd on hand is apparent. The sound of hundreds maybe a little over a thousand screaming, screeching, and yelling maniacs can be heard piercing and echoing throughout the arena air. At this time it appears a battle is taking place down below on the arena floor and all the onlookers are literally salivating at the carnage. The camera catches an angled sky view of the two combatants battling it out way down below and quickly zooms in. As the camera catches a better look at the two pitfighters, one of them appears to be a black man with a black bandana on his head, a well-trimmed and now bloodied up goatee on his face, a black tanktop t-shirt that is drawn very tight around his muscular frame, and a pair of black jeans on. He is seen draped almost lifelessly against the black barricade that separates the combatants from the screaming masses in the stands and is now the support base for his nearly deadweight. The pain and agony is deeply etched across the warrior’s face as blood drips from a small gash on his forehead coloring his face a crimson red. The man that we see is none other than “The Violence” Vince Jones, the man that the wrestling world would learn to fear in the distant future; but, at this moment in time he is being pummeled into submission by his opponent, a rather massive man in a white t-shirt and black denim overalls with a dog collar around his neck and a bearded face that is soaked by his own blood that is slowly streaming down. As Vince Jones tries to summon the strength to stand up to his own two feet his opponent gazes at him and lets out a loud, booming laugh and just shakes his head in disappointment with Vince. Bearded Man: Look at you! Just look at you! You supposed to be the man that’s rising to the top of this dump!?!? The behemoth of a man reaches out and slaps his victim across the face making Vince drool his own blood on his shirt. Beareded Man: All I see right now is a soon-to-be dead piece of New Yawk prison yard crap! The monstrous, bearded man’s chest heaves up and down with laughter as he spits in the face of Vince Jones. This sends the crowd into a frenzy. Vince Jones looks up at the bearded man with defiant eyes and snarls back. Vince Jones: You think you gonna finish me off tonight, HUH?? Then do the damn thing, bitch! C’mon on wit it! The bearded man chuckles to himself as he reaches down on the littered arena floor and grabs a lead pipe. Bearded Man: Don’t mind if I do! The bearded man raises the lead pipe high over his head and begins inching towards Vince ready to strike. Bearded Man: Lights out, b… All of a sudden Vince Jones springs forward and nails the bearded man square in the gut with a swift kick out of desperation that leaves the big man stunned and makes him drop the lead pipe to the floor with an more than audible cling clang sound. Vince Jones drops to his knees but somehow manages to muster enough strength to lunge forward with a powerful right hook to the big man’s kidneys that sends the man reeling backwards clutching his side in pain. Vince slowly rises to his feet, grabs the man by the back of his head and slings him face first into the barricade as the whole arena erupts and becomes amped by the sudden turnaround. He quickly whips the big man around and begins laying into him with vicious lefts and rights to the face over and over and over again in succession. Suddenly, the large, bearded man manages to get a hand up and block one of Vince’s oncoming punches and swiftly nails Jones with a low blow that sends Vince teetering to the arena floor in agony. Vince Jones grabs himself and begins rolling around on the floor in pain as a loud “Ooooooooooooooooooh” comes from the collective voice of the blood thirsty audience. The bearded man slowly reaches down and snatches up the lead pipe once again and looks down at Vince Jones in satisfaction as he is prone on the arena floor. The big man belches out a roar of laughter as he raises the lead pipe high above his head. Bearded Man: Its over for you, boy!! The beastly combatant begins nailing Vince Jones in the ribs with shot after shot from the leadpipe he is wielding. The crowd goes nuts at the ruthless agression in the arena as the bearded man continues to beat Vince Jones into submission and begins taunting him as well. Bearded Man: How you like that?? What?? I can’t hear you, boy! How the hell you like that, huh?? The bearded man begins nailing Vince Jones in the kidney area as well over and over again making Vince Jones start to cough up his own blood on the floor. Bearded Man: Look at you! You’re garbage! And to think they call you The Violence of the NYUFC! Well, I’m done playing with you! Time to finish your scrawny ass off for good! The bearded man raises the lead pipe high above his head and measures up for the final deathblow. Bearded Man: Lights out, boy!!! With a quick swing, the bearded man strikes Vince Jones across the skull. Vince instantly rolls over on his back and just lies there literally motionless in a heap on the floor to the satisfaction of the boisterous crowd. The large, bearded man immediately drops the lead pipe to the arena floor, brushes off his hands and shoulders, and raises his beefy arms high in the air as he rejoices in his victory. All of a sudden a loud, booming, low, deep voice blares out over the speakers throughout the arena. Announcer: Your winner of tonight’s NYUFC Friday Night Prize Fight is… “The Dog of Death” Drew Winthrop!!!! Drew Winthrop begins circling Vince Jones in victory and pounding his massive chest. He then looks down at Vince Jones and gloats. Drew Winthrop: Time for me to go collect my paycheck, bitch! Looks like you gonna be a lil hungry this week! I’m sure the dogs’ll spare you a few scraps to eat out their dumpsters this week. The big man roars with laughter and just walks away. All of a sudden a well-built man appears with long blonde hair, a black leather jacket with the words “The Hellraiser” written on the front in crimson, and a pair of black leather jeans on. Vince Jones’ expression intensifies as the man smirks at Vince’s state of defeat and just chuckles to himself. The man is none other than Vince Jones’ longtime nemesis in the NYUFC, Jack ‘The Hellraiser’ Wright who is prepared to wallow in Vince’s defeat. Jack Wright: Well, well, well. Would ya look at this. I like this. I’m really likin’ this. With all the shit that’s been spewin’ out your mouth recently I guess you never woulda thought you’d find yourself laying on your back looking up at the ceiling. Did ya, punk?? (chuckles to himself) One question for ya though, how’s the view from the floor?? Jack Wright lets out an evil laugh and just shakes his head. Jack Wright: It’s a shame. It’s a damn shame. I’ve been hearing a whole rack of people talking as if you’re supposed to be the next big thing around NYUFC. People actually come up to me (leans over Vince and points to himself) and have the gall to allow your name to roll off their tongues like I’m supposed to give a fuck! Vince Jones coughs and some more blood spurts from out of his mouth. Jack Wright appears a bit outraged by this and swiftly kicks Vince in his side. Vince clutches his ribs in agony as the blonde-haired man’s expression intensifies a bit. Jack Wright: HAVE SOME RESPECT WHEN YOUR CHAMPION IS TALKING TO YOU!!! (slowly begins breathing in and out trying to regain his composure.) Now, look at you! You’re a bloody mess! And to think that you’re supposed to be “The Violence of NYUFC”! People think you could be the most dangerous man to ever set foot in this place. Is that right?? Well, I don’t give a damn what the people might think!! The way I see it, you’re still nothing but V.J in my eyes, the NYC prison yard trash that wandered his way down here to the NYUFC hoping to make a name for himself. Jack Wright shakes his head and just laughs to himself. Jack Wright: Well, you don’t look so dangerous to me right now. In fact, until you can call yourself the champion of the NYUFC, you and the rest of these jackasses that wish to one day get on my level are nothing but dirt beneath my feet. Jack Wright raises his arms and circles around Vince once more. Jack Wright: You see all of this? This the NYUFC! This my house! This my world! This is my hell! Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright runs this! And you?? Jack Wright we looks down, at Vince and just chuckles. Jack Wright: You just exist here because in my world because I allow it! No ifs, ands, or buts about it, V.J! You got that?? You only exist here because I haven’t decided to wipe your sorry carcass off the face of this map yet! One of these days though you’re gonna overstay your welcome and when that day comes, I’ll be more than happy to end your career around here, for good! Jack Wright kneels down beside Vince and Vince keeps his eyes steadily locked in him with an intense glare. Jack Wright: In the meantime, you oughta do yourself a favor and just leave this place! You’ve lost! You’re down and out! It’s over for you! You’re done! Jack Wright stands to his feet and turns his back on Vince. Jack Wright: Hey! Someone get over here and peel this human blood stain off my arena floor before he starts stinkin’ up the joint! I’m gettin’ sick and damn tired of lookin’ at him! The camera slowly fades to black as the words of Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright continue to echo through the air. {{End of Flashback}} ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “You’ve lost… …You’re down and out… …It’s over for you… …You’re done… You’re down and out… …You’re done… …You’ve lost… …It’s over for you…” The camera fades in and we see 'The Violence' Vince Jones draped across a bench in the middle of Huntington Beach Mall in California clutching his Countdown Trophy close to him as if it’s a teddy bear and tossing and turning as if he’s having a nightmare. He is wearing a black New York Yankees baseball cap turned around backwards on his head, his trademark dark shades, a black Rocawear fleece jacket with a white t-shirt and a chain underneath. He is also seen in a pair of baggy black jeans and a pair of black Timbaland boots on as well. The words that haunt Vince the most continue to ring in his head. “You’ve lost… …You’re down and out… …It’s over for you… …You’re done… You’re down and out… …You’re done… …You’ve lost… …It’s over for you…” The time is about 6:35p.m. Shoppers pass by left and right occupied by their individual shopping needs while taking in the sights around them from stores like EB Games to Victoria's Secret. Every once in awhile the occasional excited wrestling fan passes by and notices Vince’s presence. All of a sudden Vince’s eyes pop wide open and he sits up in fright. His eyes dart about the scene in panic. He finally comes to his senses and regains his composure as he looks down at the Countdown Trophy in his arms and begins cradling it. Vince Jones: Fuck! That shit was only a dream. Thank God! Still got V’s buddy and still livin’ it up. Vince Jones appears totally oblivious to the people that pass him by. He takes a quick glance at his wristwatch and just shakes his head in disappointment as he begins muttering to himself. Vince Jones: Now, where the hell this chick at, huh? Jasmine said she'd be out in a few. Damnit! Every time we go to the damn mall she be pullin' this crap! How much shit is there to be lookin' at in Bath and fuckin’ Body Works! All of a sudden Vince’s cellphone rings. Vince Jones: What the fuck now? Shit! Vince whips out the cellphone and answers. Vince Jones: Yo! (his gaze hardens as he hears the voice on the other end) Fuck! Its you again! Didn’t V tell yo ass to stop callin’ and shit! Damn, Reno! (pauses and nods) Yeah, V knows about this fuckin’ match this week! (pauses and nods) Shut the fuck about that shit! So what if V spit in Lavelle’s face! V’ll do that shit again if he has the chance to! (pauses and shakes his head) Oh, so you mean to tell V that you would’ve made sure shit like this wouldn’t have happened, huh? Fuck that! V livin’ it up without yo punk ass holdin’ him down right now and that shit’s final! You might as well give that shit up, Reno! V owes you jack! (shakes his head) And that’s why yo bitch ass ain’t workin’ for V no more and V ain’t gotta worry ‘bout seein’ yo sorry ass around A Dubb Cee no more! Vince pulls the phone away from his ears and snarls. Vince Jones: Conversation’s over, bitch! Vince hangs up the phone, stuffs it back into his jeans pocket, and folds his arms across his chest in frustration as the camera fades out. A LITTLE WHILE LATER... The camera fades back in and we see Vince Jones still sitting on the same bench with his Timbaland booted foot propped up on it now and an even angrier scowl across his face. Vince lets out a grunt of frustration as he checks the time on his watch once again. Vince Jones: Aiight! C'mon Jasmine! V ain't got all damn day here! Hurry up and waltz yo ass on out! Camera fades out once again EVEN LATER... The camera fades back in and we see Vince Jones pacing around back in forth in front of the bench about ready to blow up with rage over his long wait for Jasmine to return. Vince quickly glances down at his watch once again. Vince Jones: Aiight! This shit gettin' on V’s fuckin’ nerves here! V been out here foreva and a day now! Where the hell she at? V ain't gonna be standin' around outside this joint foreva and shit! All of a sudden a young teenager with a Vince Jones t-shirt, blue jeans with a hole in one knee, and a black baseball cap turned backwards on his head passes by and spots Vince Jones. Fan: Hey! Its 'The Violence' Vince Jones! Sweet! Can I get your autograph, man? The fan shoves a pen and a Vince Jones AWC trading card in Vince's face. Vince stops in his tracks and glares down at the fan in fury. Vince Jones: WHAT?? Vince snatches the pen and the trading card out of the fan's hands and rips the two apart before his eyes. Vince Jones: Get the hell on kid!! The teenager runs off in fear as Vince Jones turns around and kicks the bench in fury. Some onlookers stop and watch Vince Jones with astonished looks on their faces. Vince finally takes notice of all the people watching his outburst and turns towards the crowd. Vince Jones: Hey! What?? You people ain't seen a man a lil pissed off before?? Quit starin' and get the movin' before V. Jones really goes off! Ain't nothin' to see here! The onlookers immediately lower their heads and try to go back to their normal shopping as Vince Jones just shakes his head and mutters to himself. Vince Jones: Shit! Can't get no kinda sense of damn privacy these days without some nosy bitch mades all up in yo Kool-Aid and shit! Vince Jones turns around and sees, Jasmine, standing in front of him with a pair of shopping bags on either arm and her arms folded across her chest. Jasmine: V, what was all of that about? Why you makin' such a scene out here? Vince Jones folds his arms across his chest and looks down at her in disappointment. Vince Jones: What you mean what was that all about? What you think that shit was all about, huh? V obviously was gettin' frustrated with waitin' around for you to come out that fuckin’ store and shit! Jasmine leans back and rolls her eyes. Jasmine: Excuse me?? I was in there forever? Are you kidding me? I was only in there for about fifteen minutes, V, just fifteen minutes! That's it! Vince glances down at his watch which now reads 6:50p.m. He glances back at Jasmine in anger at the fact that she is right. Vince Jones: Yeah! Well, ya know somethin'? That was fifteen minutes too long! Jasmine just shakes her head in disappointment at Vince's cop out of a reply. Jasmine: Uh...yeah whatever, V. You know that I'm right. I didn't have you waiting that long at all. You just don't want to admit it when you're wrong. That's okay though! Fifteen minutes is a lot shorter of a wait than the hour or so you had me waiting around while you were probably off screwing that LaShonda! She begins walking off without Vince and he is livid at her response. Vince Jones: There is no fuckin’ LaShonda, Jasmine! How many fuckin’ times does V gotta tell you this shit, huh? Jasmine stops in her tracks and whirls around to face him as he approaches. Jasmine: Until you tell me the truth, V! Vince hesitates for a moment. Vince Jones: But V ain’t hidin’ shit! Jasmine: (narrows her eyes in suspicion) Then tell me why you had to stop and think about that one, huh? Why? What was up with the hesitation? (shakes her head and folds her arms across her chest) I’m not stupid, V! I’ve known you forever! If you want to keep hiding the truth from me you can try, but its all gonna come out eventually! Vince grabs Jasmine’s hands and looks her deep in the eyes. Vince Jones: But V ain’t fuckin’ lyin’ to you! He been tellin’ it like it is over and over and over again, but you just don’t get it! You don’t listen! Jasmine: Oh, I’m listening. I’m listening to every word you say, V. Whether I believe you or not is another thing. Don’t underestimate a woman’s intuition. That’s the last thing you ever want to do. Vince Jones: (in confusion) Intu-what? Shit! Whateva the fuck that is don’t matter! The only thing that matters is that you oughta give V some credit on this shit! Jasmine: You know I’d love to do that, but when you catch your man with his hands all over another woman’s breasts it makes you wonder even more. It starts to put things into perspective just a little bit. Vince Jones: Let V put shit into perspective for you one more time here. V never cheated on you with this LaShonda bitch! Jasmine: Well, that better be true because if I find that you’ve looked me square in the eyes and lied to me then you and me are through and that’s all there is to it! I hope I put everything in perspective for you as well! Vince clutches Countdown Trophy tight. Jasmine notices his changing demeanor and shakes her head. Jasmine: (focuses her eyes intently on Vince) But if you’re innocent than you have nothing to worry about so there’s no need to get all tense, V. If you have been lying to me well I guess you and that trophy of yours will be spending a lot of lonely nights together… Jasmine just shakes her head in marches on in frustration with her shopping bags in hand. Vince stands there with his Countdown Trophy and looks down at rather nervously after hearing Jasmine’s ultimatum. Vince Jones: Well, you heard that shit! Not a word about that night to anyone! We in this shit together, man. V goes down and he takin’ you with him… The camera slowly fades to black as Vince makes his way through the mall and tries to catch up with Jasmine.
