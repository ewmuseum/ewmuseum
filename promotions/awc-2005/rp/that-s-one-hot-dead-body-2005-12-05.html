That's one hot dead body!

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th December 2005 Todays adventure unfolds in the form of a local greasy spoon Café calls Les & Susan's. Upon entry to the café there is already an instant negative feeling. There are 5 tables with blue and white checkered patterns on the table clothes. They all have various condiments with a crusty scabs over the tops of the nosel. The large pain glass window at the front of the café has a small thin layer of grease and grime on it somewhat tinting the window but you can still see the view of the children's nursery opposite the road. The lace curtain, which covers from the bottom to the middle of the window is covered in brown water stains and obviously hasn't been cleaned for years. So we get the impression the hygiene in the place seems to be substandard and business isn't what it used to be as we meet the owners, Les and Susan Hollis. Les appears behind the filthy counter wearing a 1980's Sheffield United football shirt. He twitches thick mustache and places his hands down on the counter whilst puckering his lips in a manor of frustration forming an expression of disarray. He wipes his brow and sighs before lighting up a cigarette. He begins to ponder on the mess of his life as he flicks the ash from his cigarette on the floor. What had become of his marriage and what happened to the beautiful woman he married. The dreams they shared to run their own business seemed to be burning away like the cigarette he was sucking on. Suddenly his trail of thought it demolished by the light tingling of the bell above the door. Les chokes and tosses his cigarette on the floor and stands up straight. Les: Hello mate what Can I do you for? The man that steps in is a rather famished and wet Red Rock. The hard rain poured down relentlessly not so long ago and Red Rock got caught in the thick of it. His brand new £25 parker jacket from Asda is now soaked through and his hair is sticking to his face. But now the sun is beam through the thinning gray clouds and it is time for lunch. Red Rock unzips his jacket and observes the menu briefly whilst rubbing his chin indecisively. Red Rock: Can I have a full English all day breakfast please? Les smiles and nods before showing Red Rock to a table. Red Rock looks around and frowns at the state of the place whilst wondering why there is nobody in here at a time where a café should be busy. Red Rock takes a seat against the back wall just under a cork notice board, which has a few old notices from 1995. Les: That'll be about 10 minutes mate! Les creepily steps backwards away from Red Rock still maintain a grin across his plump alcohol fueled red face with its broken blood vessels and other ugly blemishes. Red Rock rests his head on his hand and begins to flick through a local newspaper that someone seems to have left behind whilst muttering to himself. Red Rock: Bloody hell... Red Rock idly flicks through the paper skimming over pointless stories like protests over some mobile phone mast or some stupid woman complaining about some shit. Red Rock finally reaches the personal ad's. Red Rock always says that you know it's time to put your head in the oven when it gets bad enough to answer a personal ad, but with this in mind Red Rock does like to check them...just to laugh at them...obviously...he can get a date any day he wants....seriously! Red Rock: Albino she/he...seeks similar...HA HA good luck! Red Rock skips a few before another one catches his eye. Red Rock: former famous heel announcer for in the country for a few days seeks no strings fun contact Jeff... Red Rock raises an eyebrow and grins as he deviously begins to tear the advert out to pull some kind of prank on who it obviously is. Suddenly the hollowing screech of a woman can be heard from behind the counter. This will be Les' Wife Susan. Her voice sounds haggard from years of smoking and alcohol, it gives off the impression that she is uneducated and not very well spoken. The sound of her voice sends a chill down Red Rock's spine and over behind the counter Les' face appears to be turning a darker more irksome shade of red. He slams both his hand down on the counter as he tries to repress his rage, clenching his fists and breathing heavily through his nose. Susan: You're bloody useless Les! Bloody Useless! Susan comes out to the shop floor from through a doorway with red white and blue ribbon strips over it. She is sporting a thick woolen pink turtle neck jumper, popular in the early nineties and her make up is over the top and applied very thick. Round her neck is a few large tacky gold chains and she has several tacky gold rings on each hand making her seem less and less elegant. She resumes her verbal assault on Les completely unaware of Red Rock quietly awaiting his meal and trying to hide behind the newspaper. Susan: You can't do anything right you're a bloody waste of – OH you didn't say we had a customer! Susan puts on a smiling front to try and hide the shambles of her marriage to Les and all of a suddenly is speaking calmly with a hint of class in her voice. Red Rock slowly puts the news paper higher to completely cover his head as he was pretending he can't hear the couple arguing. Susan: Don't worry petal we'll be with you in a bit! Red Rock sheepishly lowers the news paper to utter a response. Red Rock: Thank you... Susan and Les just stand still grinning at Red Rock. Red Rock nervously looks out the window and begins to contemplate his escape. Susan: We'll be with you in a minute...if Les doesn't Screw it up again! Les: Oh for Christ sake Susan leave it out. Susan: Why Les? You're a worthless husband it's you bloody fault I drink it's your fault I'm putting on weight! You give me hives when you do things wrong all the time I'm always having to clean up after you ruin everything. I'm sick of it Les! Les shakes his head and rolls his eyes and Susan runs over to Red Rock and grabs his T-Shirt. Susan: You have to save me take me away with you I can't stand him he makes me unhappy the doctor gave me anti-depressants but they only mask the pain PLEASE HELP ME! Susan begins to sob on Red Rock should who at this point is trying to get up from his seat and head for the the. But Les runs over and grabs Red Rock by his other sleeve in desperation. Les: She doesn't cook, she doesn't clean! Shes an alcoholic. She's no good, no good at all! HELP ME SON! Red Rock: Err.. Susan: He can't satisfy me in bed anymore! I need a REAL man! Les lets go of Red Rock and all of a sudden explodes at Susan. Susan backs off from Red Rock as well and it appears that the troubled couple are about to fight. Susan: I don't love you anymore Les! I Can't love a fat disgusting pig like you...you make me sick. Susan looks Les up and down with a disgusted look on her face. Red Rock slowly tries to slip away out of his seat but it spotted by less. Les: SIT DOWN YOU! Red Rock darts back in to his seat as Les scourers at his wife. Les: I've had it with you, I'm out of here! Les removes his apron and slams his to the floor whilst aggressively glaring at Susan before turning his back to make his way to the door. But before he can put his hand on the handle Susan throws one more blow at Les. Susan: I want a divorce. Red Rock's jaw drops at the sentence and Les stops in his tracks and turns around looking rather distressed at Susan's words. Susan stands up proud with her arms folded with a smug aura around her. Susan: That's right Les I want a divorce, you can keep the business and the house. But I'm taking our kev! Les sluggishly turns away from the door and sigh as he approaches Susan, who is still maintaining her smugness. Les: You can't do that Susan. You can't take our Kev. Susan: Why not? I'm going to do it, I'm going to take him to Spain and I'm going to get a real husband! Les: Our Kev died 10 years ago Susan. When are you going let go? Les and Susan stop and stare at each other for a little while, Susan still tries to keep up her proud from but she begins to burst in to tears and falls in to the arms of Les who begins to comfort her. Susan: I only went down stairs for a poo...how was I supposed to know he'd drown in the bath? At this point Red Rock is looking very awkward, the expression on his face exclaims a feeling unease. It's almost like the deeply emotionally troubled couple of forgotten he was there. Red Rock tries to make his presence known by raising his hand but to no avail. Red Rock slumps back in to his chair and folds his arms. Les: It's alright Susan...It's alright we can try for another one. Not to replace our Kev of course....you friggin' drowned him! C'mon love. Susan and Les both disappear through the door leading to the flat above the Café leaving Red Rock by himself in silence...still waiting for his food. Red Rock: I only wanted some breakfast...Jesus Christ! Red Rock slumps down in his chair and is becoming greatly annoyed. He only wanted something to eat no become witness to some domestic fuck up. Red Rock grabs his coat shaking his head as he heads for the door. It would seem that recently Red Rock's luck only seems to be in the ring after recently beating Race Alexander a man who was highly tipped after first showing at Fresh 2 weeks ago but he pulled through and got his 6th win in AWC. Red Rock has 2 things on his mind this week: finally ending this stupid feud with The British Bomber and proving himself in GTT5. That's assuming he can make it out of this café alive. Just as Red Rock puts his hand on the door to leave and look else wear for food two huge thugs barge through the door sending Red Rock stumbling back against a table. Both men are wearing black leather jackets and each of them have menacing looking skin heads. Red Rock's face of annoyance instantly has turned pale with fear at the sight of these two men. Red Rock: DON'T BUM ME! Red Rock cowers under the table covers his head with his hand. The two thugs names Ross and Steve look at each other before Ross the taller of the two throws the table Red Rock is hiding under across the room, which makes a loud crash and they begin to bellow at Red Rock in their thick northern accent. Ross: Get up you prick! Red Rock slowly gets to his feet holding his hands in the air as if he was surrendering. Red Rock: Err...are you going to kill me? Steve grabs Red Rock by his shirt and drags his face to his own in an act of intimidation. Judging by these two mean character it would seem that they are two goons working for some kind of gangster. Steve grunts in Red Rocks face before Ross barrages him with questions. Ross: Where is LES. Red Rock: Who's Les? Ross: The prick who owes my boss 15'000 quid, the useless prick who runs this sorry place! WHERE IS HE? Red Rock: Well...I don't know him personally I'm not from around here! Red Rock's face is trembling and he squints his eyes in here. At this point Red Rock would be forgiven if his bowels entered a somewhat weakened state as he observes the crow bar in the left hand of Ross. Ross: Don't fucking bull shit me! Steve: I think he's telling the truth, listen to his posh voice! Red Rock: I'm not posh, I'll think you'll find this is what things sound like when they're pronounced properly. Ross: Did you just fucking jip me? Red Rock: Err...no? Ross raises his fist and is about to lamp Red Rock with his large fist but Steve grabs Ross' arm. Steve: Leave it out he ain't worth it! Red Rock grins and pokes his tongue out at Ross Steve drags Red Rock to the middle of the room and sits him down in a chair. By now all the noise has raises suspicions upstairs and from behind the counter Les appears only this time he is wearing a towel round his waist due to the fact him and his wife has been running a romantic bath in the room above the café floor. Les: whats going on here? Whats all this shouting? Les sees Steve and Ross and instantly realizes who they are, but he knows that they don't know what he looks like so he quickly gathers his composure. Les: Oh Hello... Ross glares over at Les and Steve walks over to him and grabs him round the neck. Steve: Are you Les? Red Rock: YEAH! That's him beat him up! Ross: Shut up you! Les: I'm not Les...* COUGH * ... that's Les over there! Red Rock: WHAT?! He's lying! Steve looks over at Red Rock who is feeling a mixture of rage and fear. Steve marches over to Red Rock who holds up a chair to fend him off like a lion tamer at the circus. Red Rock: PLEASE Don't Hurt me! He's Les he's a liar. Les: Don't listen to him this is his café! Steve: Then why are you naked? Les: Les over there spilled my breakfast all over me...and he let me have a bath! Ross: Then why do you stink of shit? Les: I haven't had it yet... Ross and Steve nod and both look over to Red Rock. Red Rock: He's lying! Come on you two aren't stupid enough to believe that are you? Steve: Huh? I'm not stupid! Ross and Steve turn around at Les and then again at Red Rock, needless to say both Les and Red Rock are crapping their pants. Ross: Right if none of you are going to own up to who Les is, we're going to have to cut both your thumbs off! Red Rock's face melts and turns white as snow and his eyes swell and turn red with an over whelming sensation of anxiety. Red Rock: M..mm...my thumbs? But...I need my thumbs... Ross: That's the point. Steve: DUH! Ross grabs Les and Steve grabs Red Rock then they forces them both down in a seat each. Steve withdraws some blue nylon rope and begins to tie Red Rock and Les back to back. Red Rock by now is close to tears and he kisses his thumbs, possibly for the last time. Les however is already crying and begging for his life. Steve finishes tying the pair of blubbering grown men up and walks over to the windows and shuts the blinds. Red Rock: PLEASE I'm NOT Les, my names Red Rock I'm a wrestler on AWC Steve: OH I like AWC Red Rock's face lights up it would seem his luck has turned. Steve: But I don't have a fucking clue who Red Rock is. Red Rock: God damn it! Les leans over to Red Rock and whispers to him. Les: Sorry mate, I'm not going down on my own. Red Rock: If I get out of this...I'm going to burn your house down. Ross slips out a pair of garden sheers from his pocket and grins the two. Ross: Who wants to go first? Les: HIM! KILL HIM FIRST! Ross: Well done you just volunteered. Red Rock: HA blown out rasclart! Ross advances at Les who bursts in to tears and starts to kick and scream. Red Rock: Bloody hell...have some dignity if your going to die! Suddenly a loud creaking is audible above Ross and Steve's head. The pair ignore it and put it down to the crappy structural work to the building, but Red Rock looks up and notices the ceiling is seeming a lot more curved in than it was before and a small crack is beginning to form. Ross grabs Les' hand and puts the sheers around Les' right thumb. Red Rock is still looking up at the ceiling just as another loud creak fills is heard and reverberates around. Red Rock grins as he notices a large amount of moisture gathering on the ceiling. Steve: What are you so happy about? Red Rock grins and looks sharply at the ceiling drawing Steve's attention to the increasing growing crack in the ceiling which is now surround by moisture. A couple of drips of water drops on the centre of Steve's head. Steve: SHIT! Another loud creak screams through the 4 men's ear drums and Steve's jaw drops and at that instant the ceiling falls through with a shower of water and rubble from the ceiling follow by a large white bath tub which lands on Steve crushing. Red Rock and Les' eye spring open as Ross panics and flees the scene. The sheers fall on Les' lap and he swiftly uses the to free himself he run over to the bath tub and what he seems shocks him deeply. He covers his mouth and a cold silence fills the air. Les: Susan? Les puts his hand on Susan's shoulder who appears floating face down in the large bath tub. Les: Susan? She's...dead....shes killed her self. In the background Red Rock is struggling out of his blue nylon hand trap. Les falls to his knees and takes Susan by the hand and starts begins sobbing. Les: I'm so sorry Susan! Red Rock finally escapes the ropes and walks over to Les and puts his hand on his back and shakes his head with a concerned look on his face.. Red Rock: Well Les, I would say I'm sorry...BUT you nearly had my thumbs cut off so fuck you! Red Rock walks sobbing widower and walks out, but not before slapping him across the back once again. Red Rock: By the way, that's a hot dead body!
