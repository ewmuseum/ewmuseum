I Need Research

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 17, 2005 [It’s a pretty normal raining British day. The Battle of Britain is only a few days away and one of the matches on most peoples minds is the Giant Haystacks Invitational. There are a total of fifteen men in the match that will take place in a barnyard. Some of the men have been recently spotlighted in Atlantic Wrestling Club’s shows. Although there are a few that have not been seen in a year or so. They seem to have disappeared off the face of the Earth.] [One of those men is ‘Sensational’ Sean White. White has been living a quiet and elegant life back in Omaha, Nebraska. He has not spoken to anyone involved in the wrestling business for almost a year. For some unknown reason Sean has accepted an invitation to be at Atlantic Wrestling Club’s Battle of Britain extravaganza. Sean is currently in Britain, outside looking at the gray colored sky with a baseball hat over his head. He is wearing a black hooded sweatshirt and khaki pants. Sean is smiling.] Sean White: I think I hate Britain. I don’t remember. [Sean looks around at his surroundings. He is in the middle of a roundabout. Sean laughs.] Sean White: Why did I accept this invitation? These Brits are crazy. [Cars zoom by and begin honking. Sean laughs.] Sean White: Yes that’s right. I know you people missed me. I’m ‘Sensational’ Sean White. I’m a changed man. I hate everything that doesn’t have to do with me. Yes it might be egocentric but that’s just who I am now. After I fired those loser bodyguards and broke up with that stupid girl I decided to change the way I looked at life. Don’t let anyone into my life unless I know they can be trusted. I can’t trust anyone. [Sean smirks as he steps out into the street and crosses before almost getting hit by a honking car.] Sean White: Yes this is my time to shine. This Giant Haystacks Invitational is my time to shine. To get back into the spotlight that I once had. The people loved me. Thing is I don’t love them anymore. Or did I ever love them? Probably not. [Sean begins looking at the roundabout and all the cars passing through.] Sean White: God that makes me dizzy. Anyway though about this Giant Haystacks Invitational thing. I don’t know any of the people in it. That’s why I have come up with a plan. [Sean reaches into his right pocket and pulls out a piece of folded up paper. He unfolds the piece of paper and looks over at a large shopping area close to the roundabout.] Sean White: This is a list of everyone in the Giant Haystacks Invitational. I’m going to find out a little about all of them. Just to see if these people in Britain actually know what is going on this weekend in their country. Let’s go over to that shopping center and find out what’s going on. [Sean White has the piece of paper in hand and walks towards the outdoor shopping center. There are many people walking around with bags in hands. Sean walks into the middle of the center and looks around at the people walking by. Sean stops a fairly old man. He has a slouch in his posture while wearing a bright red trucker cap. He has on a blue jacket and light blue jeans.] Sean White: Sir excuse me, if I could ask you a couple of questions it would be much appreciated. Old Man: Ok but hurry up sonny! I’ve got a couple of errands that need to be ran. Sean White: What is your first name? Old Man: Robert. Sean White: Hello Robert, my name is Sean. I’m a professional wrestler and I have a show coming up in your great country. I’m going to ask you about three of my fifteen opponents. Do you think you could help me? Robert: I’ll try. I don’t know wrestling that well. Sean White: What about the Farmer? Robert: I guess he farms. Sean White: Really? Never would have guessed. Anyway how about Pact? Robert: Never heard of him. Sean White: Me either. Finally, Grobschnit? Robert: I’m sorry but I don’t know him either. Sean White: Grobschnit? That sounds like a disease I got that weekend I was in Las Vegas! Thank you for your help, even though it wasn’t much. [The old man, Robert walks away. Sean looks around at the shopping center and sees a bright smiling young teenager walking by. Sean grabs him and pulls him back.] Teen: Yo, I didn’t do anything! Sean: Listen kid, you watch wrestling? Probably. I’m just going to ask you some questions about my opponents this week. Teen: Whatever dude. Sean White: Since I hope you have a better memory than my last interviewee I will rattle off three names and you will tell me a little about them. James von Doom, Kris CarMichael and ‘Insane’ Chris West. Teen: Well von Doom is like a God or something. I don’t know that well but everyone seems to know his name. CarMichael is a champion for some other fed and Chris West, well he’s insane like his name says. Sean White: Thanks kid. [Sean pushes the teenager away from him without getting much help from him either. Sean hits another quick glance and spots a little girl holding hands with her mother. Sean walks up to her.] Sean White: Hello little girl, do you watch wrestling? Little Girl: Wrestling? Yes I guess. Sean White: Do you know about William Curr, Eliminator, or Al Ambrose? Little Girl: One is in Core...I can’t ‘member who. I don’t know the other two guys you said though. Sean White: Well forget them. Thank you for your help. [Sean White turns around as the little girl begins picking her nose. He turns around to her again looks at her and shakes his head. He then realizes that the mother didn’t even pay attention to her child as he just walked up to her and began talking. Sean walks out into the middle of the shopping center and accidentally bumps into someone. He turns around to see who ran into him. It is a very good looking blonde haired woman. Sean looks down to check out her body and spots her wearing an AWC logo on her t-shirt. Sean smiles.] Sean White: Hello, I’m looking to ask you some questions about AWC. Just about who some of the guys are. What’s your name? Woman: I’m Sabrina. I can help you for sure. Sean White: Alright these guys are in the Giant Haystacks Invitational. Sean Aspinall and Ian English? Sabrina: Aspinall is hardcore I guess. Ian English is a PRIME Hall of Famer. Both guys are great athletes. Sean White: Hmmm, what about The Real Deal, Giant Sperm, and Chris Metal? Sabrina: The Real Deal lives up to his name, nothing more. Giant Sperm is different I guess. I’ve never really seen him wrestler. Chris Metal is a legend. He’s been around before and it seems like this is a one time thing. Oh yeah and I believe you also have to face Steven Xandrous. Relentless Champion, which means he’s good for something. Pretty decent striker as well. Sean White: Ok, final guy. Sean White? [Sean smiles at Sabrina while she thinks.] Sabrina: Uh...I think he was in Neon. I’m not too sure. Don’t really like him. [Sean just stares at the girl. His smile turns into a frown very quickly. The girl looks confused. Sean jumps up and down while pointing at the girl.] Sean: PROSTITUTE! SHE TRIED HAVING SEX WITH ME FOR MONEY! [Sean smiles while Sabrina looks shocked. British police run over to her and grab her by the arms. Sean walks away from the woman.] Sean White: I may not be in the best shape. I may not be the favorite and I may not be remembered for my accomplishments but I sure as hell with go in to this farm like I did on my grandparents in Nebraska and raise some hell. These Brits don’t deserve to see me out in the public again. I’m going to hide in my hotel without having to stare at some of the hideous faces. [Sean exits the scene where he set up Sabrina and walks back towards the roundabout disappearing off in the distance.]
