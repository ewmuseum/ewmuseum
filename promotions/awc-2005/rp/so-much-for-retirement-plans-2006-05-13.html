So Much For Retirement Plans...

13 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th May 2006 Prologue “Fame lost its appeal for me when I went into a public restroom and an autograph seeker handed me a pen and paper under the stall door.” – Marlo Thomas Fame is a strange thing. More strange is the way that fame affects people. Some people thrive on in, making themselves a spectacle as much as possible and exposing themselves as much as they can to the public eye. Some people desperately seek it, attempting all kinds of things that news reporters and paparazzi yearn for, just to get another 5 minutes in the spotlight. Others deny its existence and make relentless attempts to hide from it, shrouding themselves from the spotlight and avoiding exposure at all costs. Exceptional Dave Hurst is one of those in the latter of the examples given above, not a celebrity by any means, but has gathered enough popularity in the wrestling world to be pestered relentlessly by fans, wrestlers, and promoters alike. Hurst gained respect amongst the wrestling population of Primetime Central in 2004 and 2005 by his competing in the GTT tournament that they hold each year (of which Rich ‘Renegade’ Rollins has recently picked up the latest crown). Hurst defeated previous GTT finalist Jay Phoenix and the defending champion (and before undefeated in tournament competition) Angelo Deville en route to the final of that competition, before failing horribly at the final stage and losing to the far superior wrestling ability of Doctor Curiosity. Unfortunately, the attention gained from defeating individuals from this tournament, and especially the one Angelo Deville, didn’t come without a price. A number of bitter wrestlers, either defeated, or seeing themselves as the person who should have first defeated Angelo Deville in the tournament and seeing Dave Hurst as undeserving of the moniker decided to make a point of ridiculing the match. They publicly stated their dismay at the ‘dismal’ refereeing of the contest and came to the conclusion that the refereeing in the tournament was either incompetent, or biased, and some even claimed that the tournament was corrupt and that Dave Hurst was the instigator of the whole thing. Those people were then taken to court by Primetime Central CEO Argyle on charges of slander and were charged a substantial fee in lost revenue. Hurst was not only angered by the allegations, but as a result of the incidents (and the repeated harassment by bitter fans at his apartment suite) decided to hang up his boots at the incredibly early age of 25, with the wealth gained from his wrestling career as his retirement package, and set up a duck conservation centre in East Sussex, England for rare and endangered species of ducks, and has not competed since. -----=====|=====----- Chapter 1: So Much for Retirement Plans Don’t even dare… You make one more step and I swear to God I’m going to rip you limb from limb… I stand in a wide-legged posture, leaning forward with my hand out in anticipation. My adversary stares straight back at me, not moving an inch, the sound of crickets chirping and the splashing of the water in the lake beside us the only sounds stopping this moment from being in absolute silence. We both stand there, our eyes staring at each other, bloodshot with rage, waiting in eager anticipation for the other to make the first move. Waiting for an excuse; a signal; for the moment… to pounce. Don’t even dare… you haven’t got a chance… “Edward Duck… Get back in your pond!” Today my adversary is not that of Jack Murphy, or Adam Dick, or Chainz or any other wrestler, my adversary is none other than the notorious Edward the Duck in his latest attempt to escape from the Lewes Sanctuary of Rare and Endangered Ducks. He skips off to the right of me. I turn swiftly in a quarter-circle and move forward in an attempt to grab the duck as it flaps ferociously past me, but my flailing arms clutch only thin air as the foul fowl (you see what I did there?) shoots right past me and makes a run for the gate. He’s not going to escape this time, no way. I turn on my heels and press as hard as I can into the grass, propelling myself into a last-ditch sprint to get the duck before it gets free. I leap full stretch in front of me, trying to grab the rebellious animal as I land, but once again am outfoxed and outrun, missing the catch by mere inches and landing face-first into a muddy puddle. As I bring my head out of the sludgy dirt pool, shaking lumps of muddy slime out of my hair, I can only watch on in vain as the renegade duck reaches the gate, its escape almost complete, but it is not free yet. One more thing stands in its way, and unfortunately for the rebel this one thing just happens to be Guard Ducky, the greatest duck guard there ever was. The plastic yellow bath toy dressed up as a guard, with a black cap on his head with ‘GUARD’ written on the front in bold yellow letters stands before the escapee as he approaches the gate and his exit of the sanctuary. Edward stops dead in his tracks. Just the simple glare of Guard Ducky was enough to freeze him on the spot in absolute fear, as I drag myself up out of the mud it becomes clear the Ducky has things covered. No words are exchanged, but Edward turns on his heels slowly and then waddles solemnly back to the pond, it was close, but we put that escape artist in his place. -----=====|=====----- Phew, that was close, another close call. That renegade duck seems to get closer and closer to escaping each and every day. It certainly keeps you on your toes. Who needs professional wrestling? I get all the challenge and physical exercise I want and need here in this duck conservation centre. It’s just as fun. Yeah, it’s just as good, I’m glad I gave it all up. I guess I’ll go and slap the kettle on after all that running about. A bit of a rest before feeding time I guess. I take a walk into the kitchen, the black and white tile flooring and plain pastel blue walls illuminated by the glorious sun creeping out from the clouds, the rain long gone and summer finally arriving to these parts. Tea and two sugars it is, perfect liquid refreshment on a day like this from the tiresome efforts of apprehending the escapee earlier. Ducky is happy in the living room splashing around in a saucer of water watching the documentary channel, he’s not one for tea anyway. I finish the cuppa with a quick stir of the spoon and the familiar ting as I tap it on the side of the cup. I take my beverage and give it a quick sip as I look out into a back garden that I thought I’d never see. The sun beaming down on a huge blanket of lush green grass, an almost lake-sized pond stretching across with sparkling clear-blue water and a gaggle of ducks drifting across it in a staggered formation… That landscape gardener’s done a flipping great job! A red van appears on the cobbled road to the side of my magnificent view. That’ll be the new Chinese post lady, Mei Ling. Taking a slow stroll over to the front door, I open and greet the nice lady, way too young to be in a job like this. It makes you wonder whether she really, really loves posting letters, or whether this was someone taking cosplay a bit too far. “Hiya Mei,” I greet the sprightly post lady as she leaps out of her van with a bundle under her arms. “Hey Dave!” she replies, almost over-excitedly. “Here ya go!” She hands me the bundle of letters and gets back on her merry way. Climbing back into her van and making her way back up the road to the rest of her round. Walking back to the house, I shuffle the letters around one by one inspecting them for clues of their contents. Bills, bills, bills, ooh a free mobile phone… oh wait, it’s free except you have to pay, letter from the bank, what the hell’s an AWC? Bills and one of those “You’ve won a holiday!” scams. I walk back into the living room and bury myself in the couch next to Ducky. “What you watching, little mate?” I ask as I pull out the AWC letter, it looks like a documentary on the American Civil War; Ducky can’t get enough of those documentaries. I rip the seal from the envelope and pull it out expecting another advert of some description, AWC being some Arctic War Commission or something, or Antique Candle Wax or something, but it appears to be an offer of a wrestling contract. I haven’t received one of these things for a long time. It’s a little out of place really, seeing as I haven’t wrestled in over a year and have made clear my intentions that I don’t want to wrestle anymore, but still this letter arrives. Either way I’m retired now, so I screw up the letter and throw it across the room to the waste paper basket, it went straight in and never even touched the rim – swish! So what does the bank have to say I wonder? Dear Sir, You are being contacted because it has been brought to our attention that the overdraft of your account (ac no: 23452346) has been exceeded. After the large loan that you have received from the bank and with your credit card also being maxed out last month it leaves us with no choice than to take this matter into legal dispute. We are currently owed the sum of £215,250.00, you must pay this amount by June 30th or we will be forced to gain the equivalent value in repossessed items. We are aware that the value of one item of your possession, the Lewes Sanctuary of Rare and Endangered Ducks is approximately £250,000.00 and are willing to settle all the debts that we are owed in exchange for this item. If you have any queries regarding this matter, please feel free to contact me with one of the contact details shown below. Thanks in advance, Fred Turner Debt Collection Administrator Newhaven Branch – Pratwest PLC. Tel: [detail withheld] Fax: [detail withheld] Email: [email removed] Stoopid Pratwest! How the hell did I manage to work up debts of £215,250? In a frantic search for answers, I rip open the envelope of the first of the many bill letters and look for answers… Gas: £85,500.00 Ooooh… I guess that under-soil heating was a pretty expensive accessory to keep hold of after all… Electricity: £107,850.00 Ouch! I probably shouldn’t have kept that set of floodlights going every night… but I thought those ducks were scared of the dark! Water: £18,750.00 Okay, I guess that’s probably how those debts got worked up… damn I never realised keeping one of these sanctuaries was that expensive. Maybe I should have gotten that lottery funding in the end. I knew there was a reason why they were offering that much! Oh dear… what are we going to do? I need money fast, and I need somewhere else to stay now that I’m going to lose my home along with this flaming sanctuary from that stoopid Pratwest! My attention suddenly draws to the waste paper basket, and more specifically the wrestling contract offer lying inside of it. Now I’m not a Nobel prize winner for my advances in brain surgery, I’m not a slick-haired shiny-teethed Hollywood actor and I’m not a shrewd market-savvy business executive, but if there’s anything I can do that will solve all of these problems it’s professional wrestling. You know what? What the heck! What’s the worst that can happen eh? I walk over to the waste paper basket, unfold the crumpled letter and search it for a phone number. Thankfully, the telephone bill hasn’t come in yet, so the phones haven’t been cut off. Here’s a number: David Harber… Hang on… what am I going to do with all these ducks?
