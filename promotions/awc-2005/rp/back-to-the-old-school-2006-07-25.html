Back to the Old School

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/25/06 As the Atlantic Wrestling Club’s two newest signees sit sprawled on worn black leather recliners, helping themselves to a seemingly endless supply of adult beverages from a gigantic cooler situated between them, the movie Old School rolls on a nearby television set. Beanie: 'Gentlemen, we're discussing a brand-new way to look at a fraternity.' That pretty much sums things up for the two founding fathers of Delta Upsilon Iota. One, the walking personification of every meathead frat guy you’ve ever had the misfortune of running into late on a Friday night. While some value academic degrees or annual salaries, he instead measures his worth by counting the empty beer cans on his floor and multiplying that by the notches on his bedpost. But there’s something to be said for this sophomoric nature, because deep down don’t we all need someone like this in our lives? Someone who can make us look good around our girlfriend one day and take our mind off that same girlfriend the next. Beanie: 'In other words, forget all the normal rules that apply to both college and society, because this is a very big idea, my friends.' The other, a good old county boy, who still believes in holding doors for women, Sunday mornings in church, and Copenhagen tobacco. Blue collar in every sense of the word, this North Carolinian sporadically rubs his sore right knee, the result of a career-ending football injury that consequently set in motion his circuitous route to the AWC. What the Atlantic Wrestling Club yet holds in store for these two blue chippers, however, remains very much a mystery. Beanie: 'We are talking about a non-exclusive, egalitarian brotherhood where community status and more importantly, age, have no bearing whatsoever.' Colby Korver, a 24-year old who only recently completed his 5th semester…of his junior year. Measuring 6’2” and weighing in at a stout 232 pounds, Korver has obviously spent a great deal more time during college in the weight room than the classroom. While an impressive physical specimen, Korver is certainly overshadowed by his mountain of a tag team partner Hank Cobb. But at 6’8” tall and 348 pounds, Cobb towers over pretty much everybody. Korver: 'Alright brah, well there's still about an hour left in the movie and we're down to just one case. You want to pause it and make a beer run?” Cobb: “Aw hell man, I’m fixin’ to settle right back into this lazy boy for a nice, little nap.” “Cobb! I told you I invited those freshman girls from Friday night over to barbeque tonight. I need my wingman. I need the big guy.” “That’s fine, just wake me up five minutes before they get here so I can throw on some Old Spice and a fresh t-shirt and we’ll be good to go.” “You are absolutely killing me. This is only the first half of our big afternoon. You’re clearly forgetting we still have PCU to go. But instead of manning up, now you’re demanding a freaking catnap? What happened to you? “Oh come on now. I already have a nice little buzz goin’ and just wanna rest my eyes. For cryin’ out loud, I was up until 4am last night luggin’ your drunk ass home from the dorms. Dammit Korver, I’m tired. And my feet hurt.” 'But it's Saturday. Tomorrow's the day of rest, you should know that.' 'Well, when I'm up all night babysittin' you, it changes to today.' 'Babysitting? Last night was kickass. I don't know about you, but I had an awesome time.' 'I know you had an awesome time, the whole town knows you had an awesome time.' 'But so did you, admit it. I saw you talking to that hot little blonde number on the porch. Now don't you dare sit here and tell me that was just another example of your babysitting abilities.' 'No, you're right. I mean, it was great. Her aunt actually grew up going to high school with my 2nd cous...' “Cobb, I don't need the boring details. I need Hank the Tank.” “Ok, ok......I’ll do one.”
