Loose Ends

12 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12, 2005 August 12, 2005 Los Angeles, California So, at this point I was bouncing between promotions. I was hoping to make a splash with Online Championship Wrestling, but it folded before I could legitimize my Hardcore Championship reign. I had signed on with an upstart promotion created by Andy M. while he's competing in Atlantic Championship Wrestling, Squared Circle Wrestling. I'd never thought I'd be making a stop here, at Atlantic Wrestling Club, in between, but I was offered a great deal, a chance at gold. Plus, I'd be free of BUFF, I'd be free of Robert Lange, and I'd be free of all the so-called 'legends' holding me down. But first, I had one more issue to take care of: Joseph Rosenberg. So, I thought I'd take a little trip down to the offices of the man himself, with the intentions that I didn't want to have anything to do with him anymore, that I was going to make my own name, by myself, and cast him aside. He'd be understanding. Right? RIGHT?!?! 'YOU WANT DO TO WHAT?!?!' he shouted at the top of his lungs. 'Don't even claim you helped me in any way, you just got all the riff-raff away from me. I was the one who pinned Rob Torborg to win the Hardcore Championship. I was the one who was this close to beating 'Perfect' Paul Paras. And when I needed your help, where were you? The only time you came to my aid, I was on my way to beating a neutered Alexander Mary.' The surroundings of this quarrel weren't that lush. Just some bare wooden walls, a few certificates showing Rosenberg's qualifications to be an agent in countless professional sports leagues, a desk containing 2 or 3 computers armed with cable connections, all the best pornography cable can download, and a mean game of Windows Solitaire. There were quite a few telephones also situated in the office, and Rosenberg usually has to contend with a few calls at a time, but during the spat the lines were suddenly silent. 'SO?!?! You can't go back on my dream, to manage a World Champion wrestle, be it in Online Championship Wrestling, Atlantic Wrestling Club, Squared Circle Wrestling, what have you! I gave you endorsement deals! I gave you a popular tag team with Robert Lange! Hell, I got you into a Relentless Championship at the Battle of Britain!' Delving into my limitless stockpile of wit, I cleverly replied, 'Yeah, at least it's in Britain, so I only have to suffer immense jet lag and not have to learn another language.' He second-guessed his original statement. 'Well, the way they talk, it's almost like another language. Half the people in Britain are overly proper and the other half are chavs. I can't tell what's worse.' I thought about it, again, and since I'm not the type of guy who'll disrespect a company by taking its title and throwing it in the trash, I'll take it, and I'll run with it, and I'll cut myself a deal with Atlantic to be a contracted superstar while defending the title. On the flip side, I'm already in a company called Squared Circle Wrestling, and they're pretty similar to Atlantic Wrestling Club, with their international schedules. What if I have a title defense in Germany on Friday and I have to make it to Canada by Sunday to have a match on Strife? It'll almost make me want to throw the match. And it made me hate Rosenberg more. He talked to me more, half-pleading with me to get out of the fucking building, and half-pleading with me to stay. I couldn't tell which was more pathetic. I almost thought he was going to attack one of his computers with a shotgun and call me a dickhead, his intensity was unparalleled. Nothing like the stupid things we used to do to get ready for matches in Online Championship Wrestling, like go on a hiking trip, or go out for an endorsement deal and get kidnapped by the PAAWN. I'd like to say I staged that attack, because it was so absurd, but that actually happened. I bet you'd like to know why Joseph Rosenberg and I hate each other so much. Let's use the Wayback Machine! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- August 5, 2005 Nashville, Tennessee As you might know, from looking at the date, is that Online Championship Wrestling is long dead, dead until Dean comes up with the assets and the give-a-damn to start it up again, which might be the beginning of next year, or the beginning of the next century. Things between my agent and I were going well, but today was the beginning of the end. I was back home, in Nashville, Tennessee, recovering from all the nagging injuries I suffered from being in Online Championship Wrestling. At least I still had a souvenir from that run, even if it didn't go the way I wanted. I was the OCW Hardcore Champion at the time, and when OCW closed down, Mark Kelley, acting President, let me have the belt because 'Dean was probably going to redesign it, anyway.' I still kept up a training regimen of running and going to the gym, which I did in case anyone wanted to hire me again. I was outside, running, to stay conditioned and to become lighter, as I wanted to keep my shape. Who wants to look like Tatanka or Road Warrior Animal, anyway? The only things I take on my run are a bottle of water, so I don't become dehydrated in the scorching Tennessee sun, and a cell phone, so that I can take care of matters as they come up. I wouldn't want to miss a call from World Wrestling Entertainment if they ever voiced a desire to sign me. Absurd? Yes. Impossible? No. So, I was on my way, running like a madman, just me, the road, and the trees. Suddenly, though, sounds were emanating from my pocket as if a traditional phone were in there. But, it was coming from my cell phone. Some people want to be flashy and play the latest 50 Cent bullshit on their phone, or the other newest hit touting 'bling-bling' and screwing whores. I know of nerds whose ringtone is the Super Mario Brothers theme. Me? Keep it simple, stupid. I flip the phone open and see that the call is from 'Rosenberg'. I answer it. 'It's me, Rosenberg.' Well, DUH. 'I think you better stop what you're doing, because I have a GREAT opportunity for you. Some promotion called the Atlantic Wrestling Club wants you to come in and work a match for them on their August 19 pay-per-view show, The Battle of Berlin.' I calmly replied, 'Sure. Any other details.' 'Well, for starters the match is going to be a big battle-royal style match, and it's going to involve guests and contracted AWC workers. And one more thing, this match is for the AWC Relentless Championship.' At this point, I think I almost did a spit-take. 'It's for WHAT?!?! You can't be serious!', I blurted out. 'There's gotta be some kind of catch, some crazy gimmick, right?' 'Not that I know of,' Rosenberg conceded. 'Is it OK if I fly out to your office and use one of your computers to find out more about the match and my competitors?' 'Sure, but there's one I already know of, and one you need to keep your eye on: Steven Xandrous, the champ.' 'Alright, Rosenberg, I'll come by later,' and I ended the call. I could swear I ran twice as fast that day. Motivation, indeed. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- August 8, 2005 Los Angeles, California I arrived in Los Angeles, California, this morning, and if you've never been before, unlike me, it's a real shithole. There are thugs and smog, and I couldn't tell you which is worse. At least I never get bothered by thugs. Gangs are for people who want to be politicians but have no time for laws, taxes, or the economy. Only war. At least that's what I'd like to think. There are some bad characters in Los Angeles, and you won't find people who pretend they're bad by being on the grass when they're not allowed to. So, I'm out on the sidewalk, with a suitcase, trying to hail a taxi, because I needed to get places. I had a confident swagger, and my clothes exuded confidence as well, a black button-up shirt, black slacks, and black shoes. Some people dress in all black because they want to be goth, I dress in black because it goes well with everything. One rolled to a stop, and I looked at the driver. He was Middle Eastern, and spoke with an accent, but I don't partake in such silly games as racial profiling. 'You know where Joseph Rosenberg's office is?' The man replied with, 'yes', but it sounded so foreign, I could barely understand it. I got into the car and it was a mundane trip, which brings me to something else. I hate regularity in my life, which is probably why I became a wrestler, because no two promotions, wrestlers, or storylines are the same. Even though things might be revisited, they are kept fresh with a spin. Having a regular job would be depressing for me, plus I hate layman's work anyway. But, anyway, back to the story at hand. I arrive there a few minutes later, and give the driver his money, and we went our seperate ways. I walked into his office, and took a seat. 'Hey,' I tentatively said, but it looked like Rosenberg was a bit busy, minding the call of someone else who needed his attention. Apparently he was trying to get more money, but he already took a contract extension, so he couldn't get a raise. Sucks to be him. Rosenberg says 'Bye,' and the call ends. He hangs the phone up, and turns towards me. I tell him, 'I'm here because I'm trying to get a leg up on my competitors for the Relentless Championship match at the Battle of Berlin.' Rosenberg frowns, then tells me, 'I'm sorry, but I looked at a tape of the August 2 Fresh!, and I learned that it is to be a Haystacks Match, in the memory of the late Giant Haystacks. It is to be set in a farmyard, and the only way to eliminate an opponent is to pin a wrestler under a haystack for 3 seconds.' I looked at him, dumbstruck, and could only think of one thing. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- June 2002 Total Demolition II It was my first ever Championship Match, but I could only wonder, 'Why did I sign up for this?' I was in the ring with Tiger Sid, Roz Blayze having been eliminated. It was a Goldfish and Baby Turtle match. I had the advantage on him, the entire time, but I didn't remember what I did before I threw Tiger Sid into the tank, it was a haze. All I could remember past that point was me being announced as the winner and the new OCW Internet Champion, and taking the belt. But before I could leave, a rookie by the name of James Slade said something about wanting to challenge for my belt. Then, I got to the backstage area. As soon as I got to the backstage area, and could end the facade of being 'Eliminator', I ran, trying to find The Big Bifford, president of OCW at the time. He wasn't that hard to find, being over 500 pounds and all. So I found him. He slowly turned to me, and said, 'Congratulations on winning the OCW Internet Championship. I'm glad you ran to find me, I didn't want to have to walk all this way. Anyways, I'd like to tell you that your match against James Slade on Saturday Night's Main Event is a pillowfight match!' 'A what? I wanted the Internet Championship match because I thought it would be the last one contested under such silly rules!' He laughed, and said 'Oh, no. Silly is the standard for Internet Championship matches! Trust me, it'll only get sillier from here! Just wait until the pay-per-view!' I didn't want to have to deal with him, but I didn't want to get fired, and lose a paycheck, so I took it in stride. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- August 8, 2002 Los Angeles, California Giant Haystacks reminded me of The Big Bifford, and this match reminded me of his OCW Internet Championship matches. I hate The Big Bifford, and I hate the OCW Internet Championship. I don't have my replica of it anymore, because I gave it to some bum on the street. He probably melted it and made it into a necklace. Good for him. So, I stood up, and looked at Rosenberg. 'Well, you know what? I signed a deal with Squared Circle Wrestling. And even though I'm going to hate being in this match, I'm going to win it, because everyone thinks Squared Circle Wrestling is a pissant upstart fed, and I'm going to make them look good, dammit! I'm going to make Atlantic Wrestling Club look like chumps!' Rosenberg was irate, because to him, it was illegal. 'You can't do that without contacting me first! I have to approve all contracts for you!' I laughed at him, because I'm not a dumbass, I knew what the contract entailed. 'You were so sure OCW would stick around, that the deal only applies to OCW! It doesn't apply to any other promotions! I'm a free man!' Rosenberg cried, 'No, that can't be!' I told him to look through it, and lo and behold, he found a section that stated the contract only applied if Eliminator was in OCW. 'Goodbye, asshole!' I kindly told him, as I exited the room. 'You can't leave me!' he pathetically exclaimed. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- August 12, 2002 Los Angeles, California I decided that while I was in Los Angeles, I might as well develop a tan. So, I stayed there for a week, hoping to be free from Rosenberg. But, he called me. He was angry with me, but wanted me to come back, because I was money to him. We're back at the office, and my hatred of Rosenberg rivaled my hatred of The Big Bifford. I was tired of his antics. 'You can't leave me!', he pleaded again. 'Watch me,' I calmly replied. I left.
