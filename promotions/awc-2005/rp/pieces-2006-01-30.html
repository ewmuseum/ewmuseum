Date: 1/30/06 ''You can go sleep with him,'' Anton said gruffly towards his sister in Igbo. She stood at the threshold of his room, her belongings in hand. She had not been here for more than a week and, of course, she had decided to stay in her brother's room. However, Anton had decided upon something else. Her conduct wasn't going to allow him to let her stay in the room. There was no way. ''Not with him; in his room.'' Nneka lifted her chin defiantly, taking a step backwards. She locked eyes with her brother and dropped her things, clenching and unclenching her fists, slowly. She did not move for a long time, simply eyeing him. Anton knew she was mad. Her furrowed brow and set jaw told him that much. He assumed no stance. No matter how much of a traitor he thought she was, she was still his sister. He was obligated to return her to their parents in one piece. She started towards him, leaping across the room. Her hand swung out, clearly to give him a reprimanding slap across the cheek, but he easily blocked it away with a forearm. With a yelp, she stepped backwards and clutched her wrist. Nneka looked up at Anton and made a disgusted sound, turning away from the room. ''Luis can protect me better than you can,'' she accused. She cast a final glance at him and he responded by folding his arms authoritatively over his broad chest. She snatched up her things and slipped away to find the Venezuelan. Anton turned and walked towards the wall, pulling open the curtains. He blinked as sunlight shone through, rubbing his eyes. He turned away, focusing on getting ready for the day ahead. There was training to be done, conditioning. He had to remain in top form. He found himself standing in the middle of his hygenic activities, however, holding only a dry toothbrush in hand. He shook slowly, gritting his teeth. Before he could stop himself, his fist slammed a dent into the wall. He felt the anger soak out of him, just a little bit, and he turned towards the exit of the room. No one had better be in the bathroom. Luis shut his eyes as he heard Anton's howls reverberate through the house. The man was a fucking idiot! Couldn't keep the noise level down. It was the morning! Luis laid back in his bed, slowly opening his eyes. He watched as Nneka busied herself with settling into his room. He smiled at her whenever she looked at him, but past that, he barely took notice that she was in the room. He took a deep breath, thinking that he might just go back to sleep. The last few days had been complete turmoil. Luis had been taking the girl out, showing her places, people, things. Things that she didn't experience in France, things that she didn't experience in Nigeria. Now she had the chance. Luis rubbed his temples and plugged his ears with his thumbs, trying to block out Anton's latest scream. Thinking was difficult in the mornings now. ''What are we doing today?'' Nneka asked in courteous English, laying down at the foot of the bed. She stretched out, taking the time to rest after moving all her things into the room. She looked at him, propping herself up on her side. Her eyes searched him curiously and he shrugged, giving her a faint smile. ''Do we hadda do something?'' Luis asked. Nneka frowned at him and shoved his knee playfully. He smirked and shook his head. ''We should stay in, yeah?'' Nneka crawled over to him, laying her head against his stomach. He brought his hand up, running it through her hair. ''Come on, Luis,'' Nneka protested quietly. ''There is so much to see. So much to do! This is Los Angeles, isn't it?'' Luis nodded his assent. ''We have to do something.'' Luis nodded, knowing that he was not going to win this fight and not feeling like putting the effort into doing it. ''What, then?'' Luis asked her. She looked up at him, puzzled. ''I'm all ears, 'Eka. What do you wanna do?'' She blinked, her face scrunched up thoughtfully. Finally her eyes lit up and she tapped him on the lips. ''I know just the thing,'' Nneka said. Luis arched an eyebrow and looked towards the window as she told him the plans excitedly. Luis nodded his assent at all the right places. Anton forced himself up off the ground, keeping himself perfectly balanced; iron weight plates were set on his back. He went down again and came up, a short grunt marking the number. His eyes were closed, all functions turned inward. His only marking of time was the action of pushing himself up again. Everything, every muscle, every tissue, every nerve, was numb. He was almost floating in nothingness. Umph. Umph. Umph. He didn't even feel the weights. He had a problem that he reflected on. His sister. He didn't know how to protect her. He knew he had to, that much was clear. He couldn’t always be there. She was too stupid to know how to operate here in L.A. She was either shut in at the university or shut in at home in Abuja. She didn't know how the world was because his father and mother had money to withhold the knowledge. She had seen things on television, but television was hardly the same as the real world. Anton didn't know where Luis and Nneka had gone. He didn't want to know. All he wanted was the assurance that Nneka was safe. At this point, Anton made no distinction between wanting Nneka safe for her own sake or for his parents' sake. As long as she stayed alive and unbroken, unsullied—past the point that she had already been, at least—he would be relieved. He just wanted to know. With Luis, he would never have that assurance. Luis made a face at a woman passing him by, causing her to look away sharply and try and leave his presence quickly. Nneka gripped his arm, her head laid against his shoulder. Luis blinked as he led them through the crowd, darting towards small passes. She insisted on staying so close, however; he wondered how she could keep on the grip for so long. Luis ducked into a small space between two buildings and flipped out his wallet, thumbing through the bills. Nneka looked over at bills, watching. Fucking scalpers. This was Disneyland, a place for children, a place where fun was supposed to reign, and there were fuckin' scalpers. Luis sighed and folded the wallet, slipping it into his back pocket. He looked over at Nneka and patted her on the shoulder. ''Is something the matter?'' Nneka asked in English. Luis shook his head. ''Dun worry yaself about it,'' Luis reassured her. Nneka smiled at him. ''So, any… uhh… rides you wanna go on?'' Luis wasn't all that familiar with amusement park rides. He'd been on a few in his time, but not too many. He wasn't exactly keen on the idea, either, but if she was going to make him, he guessed he could go on the rides. ''First…'' she said, gripping him by the wrist now. She tugged him towards the nearby restrooms, her smile not fading a millimeter. Luis had noticed that she had really become wild over the course of the few days he had known her. Perhaps she had simply had all this pent up inside her and she had finally found a venue to get them out: him. Frankly, it was taxing. He liked to fuck, but she was a nag. ''Whaddyou want?'' Luis snapped, taken off guard. She pushed the door of the men's room open and crept inside, tugging him along behind her. ''One of my friends,'' she said softly. Luis rolled his eyes. Always one of her friends. ''She said she would love to do it in Disneyland. I want to make her jealous.'' Luis smiled as she turned around, quickly getting to taking off her shirt. A nag with a nice body. She pulled him towards her and locked him in a kiss. His hands fumbled with his shirt buttons as he leaned into her. Suddenly, her eyes widened. Before he could ask her what was up, a heavy metal object slammed on the back of his head. She was jolted backwards and Luis felt himself falling to the tiled ground. A hand ripped him off the ground quickly and he found himself beaten across the face. Luis's eyes flickered open as he hung against the sink, coughing. A thick-muscled man had his hand clamped over Nneka's mouth. Luis turned back to the others, watching for an escape route. The metal object from before—a red-painted wrench—slammed up under his chin and Luis's legs lifted off the ground. He fell to the ground before, once again, he was yanked up. A hand rummaged near his ass, but before he could make a typical quip, a knee drove into his face. His head was whipped back and it hit the sink. He looked up, seeing a black-haired man opening his wallet and grinning. A heavy boot met his ribs before they left him alone, leaving him to lay on the ground. Nneka was pulled screaming into one of the stalls, forced inside and followed by the man who had held her before. Luis tried to pull himself up and crawl towards the exit, but there was no way he could get up. He was too weak. Fuckin' Disneyland. Anton sat on the couch in the apartment, letting himself relax. He read a book, an evolutionary spiel by Richard Dawkins. Although he had not attended college, he still read what he could. Only a dim light behind him was lit, just allowing him to concentrate on reading. His entire day had been spent trying to take his attention away from the situation at hand with Luis and Nneka. It was an undesirable something, a something that he didn't particularly want to deal with right now. A frantic knocking came to the door, followed by a heavy thud. Anton slapped the book down on the chair's arm and leapt up, streaking towards the door. He pulled it open and found himself squatting a bit to catch his sister. She sagged in his arms and he looked her over incredulously, his eyebrows seeming as if they would raise off of his face. He scooped her up and carried her over to the couch, laying her down. She had gone out that day in a black tubetop and a miniskirt. The skirt was cut neatly up the backside and her panties were gone. Her tubetop was extremely rumpled, scrunched up just over her breasts. Purple lipstick was smeared over her back in what was supposed to be words, but the characters had been smudged out by rough handling. He kneeled next to her, lost for words. She turned her head to him, glossy eyed. A faint smile adorned her lips. Drunk. He could smell the alcohol wafting off of her. ''They said… they said they would take care of me, brother,'' she confessed to him in shaky Igbo. ''That's all. They took care of me. It's… it's nothing.'' Anton watched her eyes as she told him this. He didn't dare to touch her hair for fear of what might be lurking inside. ''Who?'' Anton asked. ''Luis?'' It dawned on Anton that Luis was not a 'they,' but he could have been involved. He had to have been involved. Nneka shook her head. ''I don't know them,'' she told him. ''One of them was named… um… Roos, I think. And then another was named… I don't know. But they took care of me, didn't they? Look, I am…'' She laughed, burying her face in the couch. ''I am…'' ''Filthy,'' he finished for her. He stood up and turned towards the door, intending to close it so he could further tend to her. She was a mess. As he walked towards it, however, a familiar form slammed into the doorframe, coughing. Crimson blood spewed from his lips and onto the front of his shirt as he stood there, heaving for breath. Anton's eyes blazed with fury and his legs took him fleetingly towards Luis. The Venezuelan put his hands up protectively, but Anton's fist rocketed towards Luis's stomach. ''Bastard!'' he snapped in Spanish. Luis fell to a knee, groaning. ''Who did you sell my sister off to? Who did you whore her off to?'' Anton went to punch Luis in the face, but he craftily dropped to a prone position, leaving Anton's fist to crash into the door frame. ''Who!?'' ''Fuck that bitch!'' Luis shouted finally. He dragged himself fully into the apartment, , coughing. ''Fuck her! She got it easy! Fuckin' got fucked and whatever else. She fuckin' coughin' up blood, Lion!? Did she just get beat the fuck up, have to hitchhike home and make people see I ain't a fuckin' hobo!? You have any idea how long it took me to get back here? Fuck that bitch!'' Anton slammed his foot against Luis's neck and the small man gurgled, kicking his neck. ''Where were you?'' Anton asked, his voice cold, slow, and tense. Luis managed to squirm away, grabbing a low stand and hurling it towards the Lion. Anton moved easily and advanced on him. ''Yeah, I was there,'' Luis admitted. ''I was the one gettin' hit with a wrench. Maybe her pussy's opened up a little bit more, but other than that? Who gives a shit!?'' Anton roared, reaching down and grabbing Luis by the hair. He lifted him up and glared into his eyes. Luis looked straight back at him, fear radiating off of his form. Finally, Anton simply let him go. Luis collapsed, falling into a nearby table. He wheezed, muttering under his breath. Anton ran his hand over his head, gritting his teeth angrily. They were so irresponsible. How could this even happen? He wasn't there, that was how. He wasn't there. Now he had to pick up these pieces of people. He had a burden to bear and he did not like it. He shied from crosses for a reason. He was not going to be their martyr.
