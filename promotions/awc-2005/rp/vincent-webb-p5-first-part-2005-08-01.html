Vincent Webb p5 (first part)

Date: 8/1/05 Quickly, Alice and I run out of the room. I can’t believe I screwed up so badly. It was probably how they got here, but even if it wasn’t, the mistake had still been made. We quickly run outside to the parking lot where Alice’s car is parked. I see small areas of fogginess, and I assume that it was from those men checking inside the car to assure their next, or hopefully next, victim. These guys must be some amateurs… Alice unlocks the vehicle and I rush to the driver’s door, stopping her from getting in. “Please,” I say quickly, “let me drive. I’ll get us away from here.” She nods solemnly and runs around the S.U.V. and jumps into the back seat. I turn towards her, confused. “Well,” she says, sensing my confusion, “if there’s a chase, I can tell you where they are.” “Good thinking,” I say, assuming it will work out well. I put the keys that Alice hands me into the ignition and start the car. I put it in reverse and attempt to back up, but a force slows us down. I look backwards, seeing nothing, so I try it again. Then the noise I hear tells me what it is. “Shit...” I say under my breath. “What?” Alice asks, her voice somewhat panicky. “They slashed our tires,” I say quietly. “WHAT?!” Alice yells, her anger and fear rising. “So we are stuck out here?” I whip the sweat from my face using my shirt. We both step out of the car and I bend down, looking at the now-flat tires.
