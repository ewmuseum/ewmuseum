Sex Isn't

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/06 Luis's head was sunken forward and the motions of two-stepping were just that: motions. Boris's Amplifier Worship roared on the speakers, but that was just background noise. The slick, slight Venezuelan was dressed in nothing but a pair of baggy jeans that hung off his hips and a white wifebeater with a salsa stain on the stomach. Usually, he'd be flying on a white rush, but cocaine wasn't fueling his arm-flailing dance this time. Adrenaline was; the pure drug of the body, stimulated by stress. By thought. He knew that this Amber chick was no good. He knew it in his mind and soul. His nuts thought she was good for the whole aesthetic, though, and that was what bothered him. Despite the fact that he desperately wanted to get her away, he desperately wanted to fuck her. Nneka was nice. No, great. No, no; Nneka was astounding. That didn't prevent Luis from lusting, though, and that was the problem. Sasha Volkyeva might be a looker, but she had a vice-grip on him and the Lion that Luis didn't like. Add to the fact that she wasn't here every day like Amber was and suddenly the third girl he wanted wasn't so viable an option. So what was he to do about Amber? He couldn't puzzle the girl out. She hung on the Lion's arm like a loveburr, but Luis could see it was more like a leech. It had to be. Lion's didn't love, it just didn't happen. Luis knew that the Lion was human, but it wasn't in the Lion's nature to love someone. Other than his sister, of course, and that was purely platonic. And to assume this chick loved the Lion back? He couldn't see a woman loving a man that spoke so little. Well, come to think of it, he did make the effort to speak a lot more when he was around Amber. The familiar sound of the door being pushed open yanked Luis out of his epinephrine trance. He didn't look behind him; a simple lift of the head was all he committed to. He didn't want to seem startled. After all, this was his house. A clunky thud announced that the door was closed again and Luis balled his fists. Nneka and the Lion were out training. It wasn't likely that either was back so soon. Luis breathed. It was her, then. ''What are you doing?'' came Amber's soft voice from behind him in her native English. Luis glanced over his shoulder at her; the girl was dressed like the whore she was, in a thin and immodestly short green miniskirt, a lighter shade of green for her tube top, and a breezy white jacket. Luis licked his lips and turned back around. He wasn't gonna let the jade get to him, not unless he could get to her first. ''None of your business,'' Luis growled. ''Where's the Lion?'' ''You act like I left with him,'' Amber said. He could hear her approaching, smell her approaching; that was a heavy scent she had on. Luis blinked and realized that he'd stopped two-stepping some time ago and was just standing there with his back to her, looking like an idiot. He turned to face her almost reluctantly. ''Whatchoo doin' back?'' Luis asked. Amber shook her head, folding her arms under her breasts. Luis resisted the urge to let his jaw drop. ''I live here now, Luis,'' Amber said with a slight scowl. ''You know I do.'' ''Why you bothering me?'' ''You're impossible.'' Luis flashed her a grin and began to two-step, resuming the motions gradually. Amber sniffed, a slight curving coming to the corners of her lips. ''Training to be a clown?'' ''Trainin' to fuck you in the ass, bitch.'' Amber arched an eyebrow. Luis's grin didn't leave his face. Making sense wasn't really Luis's game. ''I guess I'll leave you to whatever it is you're doing, then.'' She turned slowly, letting an arm fall to her side as she started back to her—the Lion's—room. ''Wait,'' Luis said, pushing the harshness out of his voice. He stopped again, taking a step towards her. ''Yes?'' Amber gave him half a glance and no more. Luis's eyebrows knitted together. ''Whaddyou want with the Lion?'' Luis asked. ''Why you hangin' 'round here?'' ''I can't care for a man?'' Amber accused. She rounded on him, fists planted on her hips. Luis found it an effort to draw his eyes away from her chest. ''Why do you think I'm using him?'' ''I never said--'' ''Your girl is pretty chatty, Luis. She told me what you think of me.'' Luis grunted and Amber took a step forward. ''But I'm not using him, Luis. You are.'' She wore a confident smile on her face. A smile that Luis quickly slapped off. Amber cried out, toppling into a chair. Luis sniffed as he stepped over her, reaching down to grab her by the hair. Not the skin, not any part of it. If he touched her, he didn't know when he'd stop. He needed control. ''Don't talk to me like that, y'understand?'' Luis snapped. Amber looked up at him, her eyes meeting his. He didn't notice any fear. ''I ain't using the Lion. You're doin' that. I don't know how, but you are. Didn't think he was the type to get screwed up by a ho.'' Amber didn't try to slip away from Luis's grip, instead giving the Venezuelan a small smile. Luis's frown deepened. How could she remain so calm? ''Did you ever consider it might be love?'' ''Yeah, and then I got to thinking about realistic shit, like pigs flyin' or Garbage Bag winnin' the world title.'' Amber hastily bit back a giggle. ''Sex ain't love.'' ''No, it's not,'' Amber agreed. ''For instance, I don't love you.'' Amber let her fingers slide slowly over Luis's forearm and Luis drew a deep breath. She was a whore. Luis's hands were all over her. ''Knees, Nneka,'' Anton barked in rapid Igbo. ''You forget you have knees and sometimes you forget you have feet. Use them.'' Anton moved over to the side, arms folded over his chest. He was dressed as he normally was for fighting or training, stripped above the waist with his short black fighting shorts and tall boots. He wasn't sweating overmuch, though. It was his sister, clothed in an almost-annoying bright yellow, who was the main perspirator today. Nneka hunched her shoulders in the way she usually did when Anton criticized her form before launching back into the heavy bag. Anton would have to try an eradicate that. Anything like that, any slight nuances, shifts in form, kinks in the wall of defense, had to be eradicated. Anton knew he had to be victim of some small tics like that, some little quirks, but he had done his best to minimize them. Looking on, he could pick out all the seemingly-trivial faults in his sister's form. He only wished he had that same insight on his own form. Watching yourself on video wasn't the same as watching someone else. ''Elbows, Nneka. You have elbows.'' ''I wish you'd forget you had a mouth,'' Nneka grumbled thickly around her mouthguard, but she complied with his wishes by driving her elbow hard into the swinging red heavy bag. The gym was small and cramped, as usual, sporting only a single weight bench crammed into the corner, a simple circle for sparring, and a few bags along the near wall. Anton didn't see any point in spending money on renting a lavish exercise room if all that was going to be done was working with bags and a little sparring. ''Don't speak,'' Anton warned. ''You break your concentration, divide it. You can't win that way.'' ''Shut up!'' Nneka snapped, thrusting her fist into the bag and stalking away. Anton followed her slowly, eyebrow cocked in surprise. ''What?'' Anton asked. ''You!'' Nneka spun around on her heel and spat her mouthguard into her hand. ''Stop being such a prick!'' ''I didn't do anything,'' Anton protested. Nneka dipped her head and turned around, rubbing her arm. Anton blinked. He didn't know what had caused this, but… he laid a hand on her shoulder, a hand that she promptly knocked away. ''Is something the matter?'' ''Something's always the matter. Something's always going to be the matter as long as you keep that slut around.'' ''Why won't either of you leave Amber alone?'' Anton snapped. ''Why does she bother you so much?'' ''Because!'' Nneka stamped her foot. ''Because she's ruining everything.'' ''Stop telling me what Luis tells you. I can speak to Luis on my own. All you say is what Luis says.'' ''That is what I think! Why don't you accept that?'' Anton set his jaw, shaking his head. ''You're being a foolish little girl,'' Anton said darkly. ''Perhaps I should be arranging to send you back to mama.'' Nneka turned around, her eyes as wide as planets. The shock waned quickly, though, and her eyes became dark black holes. ''You are always calling me a foolish little girl,'' Nneka said. ''Well, you said it. I have knees. I have feet. I have elbows. I have fists. Perhaps I should help you remember that I have them, too?'' Anton stared at his sister levelly. Emotion couldn't be allowed to bubble through, but it wanted to. Shock, the same shock that had passed over his sister now passed over him. Anger. ''Are you… threatening me?'' Nneka's eyes didn't waver. In the space of the blink of an eye, Anton had moved. Nneka cried out; Anton's palm hung his entire armspan away and Nneka was on the floor, blood gushing from her nose. Nneka rolled over onto her side, coughing and rubbing at her throat. Anton snarled. ''Threatening me?'' he growled, flexing his fingers. ''Get up, then, little girl, and show me you have fists, if that is what you want.'' ''Brother…'' Nneka groaned, coming up to all fours. Anton drove his shin into her side. A short grunt passed from her as she was knocked into the wall. Anton hovered over her like a vulture, face bleak. ''Brother, stop. I didn't mean it. Please.'' Anton spat. ''Now you didn't mean it. Now that someone shows you that you are what you are.'' He kicked her stomach. Nneka curled up, trying to protect herself. ''A foolish, foolish little girl.'' Anton brought his foot up, intending on driving it down on her side, but the sound of her whimper stayed him. He took a step backwards, looking down at her. No remorse crossed his face, not as it usually would have. She was a foolish girl who didn't know a thing about what she was talking about. A girl who spat everything that Luis told her back at him. A fool. He had tried to understand. He had. He closed his eyes and took a deep breath. Foolish. Luis looked down on the black-haired woman, a thin smile on his lips. Amber eyed him curiously. She looked good with her hair splayed out like that. Sort of like Nneka's. God, he loved Nneka's hair. Amber gripped his forearms. They were done now and all there was was to rise. Luis's eyes bored into her brain. He didn't intend to get up just yet. ''Sex isn't love,'' she reminded him, a bit of odd worry creasing her forehead. ''What are you doing with the Lion?'' Luis demanded. She laughed, trying to slip away from him. He pinned her down by the shoulders. ''Just because we just did it doesn't mean I'm pliable,'' Amber said stonily. ''All I want is what's best for Anton.'' ''Bullshit.'' ''That's all I want,'' she reiterated, touching Luis's lips. ''And I mean to see to it that he gets what's best for him.'' Luis let his hands away from her shoulders and she snuck out, standing up and gathering her clothes up before hurrying to her—the Lion's, dammit!—room. Luis watched her go. Well, he watched her ass, but it was near the same thing. She was using him, no matter what words she used to hide it. How was she using him, though? Sex wasn't it. It couldn't be. She'd been good, but not that good, not mind-erasingly good. Could it be love? Luis grunted. He couldn't find himself ever loving a chick like that, but he had never had a lion's tastes.
