Mike Carman: Just Another A Dubb C Punk Ass To Be Silenced

12 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/12/2006 The camera fades into the New York City penthouse apartment of “The Violence” Vince Jones where he can be seen pacing back and forth across the floor in thought. He can be seen wearing his usual all black street gear. His valet, the lovely Jasmine can be seen sitting on the couch intently watching a soap opera on t.v as Vince begins to rant about his upcoming match at AWC Fresh Burst Vince Jones: Well, it appears The Violence is booked for this upcoming Fresh Burst; but a match against a Mike Carman?? C'mon! What the hell kinda shit is this, huh? Yeah. V somewhat new to the company and all, but this shit is ridiculous! This like some damn wrestlin' for dummies shit or somethin' here! How does a man like V. Jones go from takin' on superstars to takin' on a bitch made that wouldn't even be worthy enough to lick V's damn toilet bowl clean after takin' a dump? Vince Jones shakes his head in disappointment. Vince Jones: Guess its whateva. Gotta get ahead somehow. What betta way to make a statement around A Dubb C than to put the clamp on this Mike Carman cat. Ya know? V. Jones has had to silence many in his career. He’s silenced b*tches, hos, chumps, jack asses, pimps, playboys, boy toys, suckas, giants, lil guys that thought they was giants, monstas, skitzos, freaks, ninjas, hobos, the list goes on and on, Jasmine. But ya know somethin’? Oh well. Just think of the headlines though, Jas. V. Jones can see it all now… Vince Jones looks up at the sky as if he’s envisioning a flashing billboard or something. Vince Jones: …The Violence knocks off… Jasmine: NOOOOOOOOO!!!! Don’t do it, Sandra! He’s not worth it! Vince Jones turns and looks at Jasmine in fury as she ruins his moment. The camera turns and catches sight of Jasmine clutching onto the remote and leaning forward watching the t.v screen. Jasmine: NOOOOOOO!!!!! Sandra, if you’re really gonna become engaged to him you’re insane! He cheated on you! He cheated on you with that prissy little slut, Stacy! I remember this from last week’s episode on Friday! Can’t you see?? Isn’t it obvious, Sandra? He’s not going to work every Friday! He’s going to that slut Stacy’s house! How can you be so ignorant??? Vince just watches her in confusion. Vince Jones: What the…? Jasmine: He barely returns your calls, Sandra! That’s sign number one that he’s no good for you! He’s always late! He barely ever kisses you! Sandra, what’s the big deal?? Why is this so hard for you to figure out, huh?? Vince Jones: Hey! Jasmine quickly turns around and motions for Vince to be quiet for a moment. Jasmine: I can’t miss this part, V. I know you're having one of your 'I Am God' moments right now and all; but, I’ve been watching this show for weeks just to see this. John wants to get engaged to Sandra, but it turns out that he’s been fooling around on her behind her back with Stacy who is the girlfriend of his brother. Vince Jones: And The Violence is supposed to give a damn about this sappy ass shit because? Jasmine: Shhhhh!! Here it is!!! Jasmine clutches the remote even tighter and a large smile crosses her face as she watches even more intently than before. Vince Jones just shakes his head and watches the screen in disgust. The camera switches to the t.v screen where a guy and a girl can be seen standing face-to- face staring lovingly into each other’s eyes. John: Sandra will you… All of a sudden the message of doom, “To Be Continued” , flashes up on the screen as the scene fades and the credits begin to roll. The narrator begins to speak. Narrator: Well, that pretty much wraps another episode of “As the Years Go By”. Tune in next as we hear the rest of John’s message to his beloved Sandra and maybe a little bit more. Until next time… The camera switches back to Jasmine who is nearly in tears with excitement. Jasmine: I, I just can’t believe this. Oh, I can’t wait for next week’s episode. Sandra better not say yes to that fool. Vince Jones just shakes his head in disappointment. Vince Jones: Enough of that bullshit! Turn that crap off! That sappy shit makin’ V wanna puke or somethin’! Jasmine turns off the t.v and turns around looking up at Vince. Jasmine: Oh, is that so? What’s wrong with my soaps, huh? Vince Jones: That shit you watchin’ ain’t nothin’ but fairy tales for adults. That ain't real life. That shit might go down in over in Jersey, but not in NYC. Jasmine: Fairy tales for adults? What? Vince Jones: You heard V. That ain’t nothin’ but fairy tales for adults. That crap ain’t real. Jasmine: Oh? Well, what about your little episode last week, huh? Looked just like one of my soap opera scenarios to me and that’s real. Vince looks at her in confusion. Vince Jones: Huh? What episode that The Violence had? Jasmine: You know what I’m talking about. I came by sometime last week to see that you had nearly demolished this whole apartment. Stuff was tossed around everywhere. I see a broken t.v, shattered glass on the floor, ripped up paper lying around all over. Then I stopped by your room to talk to you and you had the door locked. You wouldn’t even step out. You just yelled at me and told me to leave. Vince Jones reminisces on the events from last week and just shakes his head. Jasmine: Now that I think about it. You never told me what was wrong with you that day. Vince nods in agreement. Vince Jones: Yeah. That’s right. V said jack and we’ll leave it at that. That shit ain’t important right now. What’s important right now is the present. What’s important right now is focusin’ on shovin' a boot up Mike Carman's punk ass! All of a sudden a large grin forms on Jasmine's face as she tries to motion for Vince to be quiet. Jasmine: Ooo!! oooo!! oooo!!! Its Days of our Lives!! I love this show, V!!! Shhhhh!!! Its about to start!! Vince Jones rolls his eyes at the thought and heads towards the door Vince Jones: We’ll talk some more later. V ain't watchin' any more of that sh*t! The camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones reaches the door and exits.
