Hurt So Good

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-7-06 “Hey, you ready to leave?” A large 6-foot tall figure sporting a black Stetson cowboy hat stepped into the hospital room. Teresa brought her long sandy blonde hair into a ponytail and nodded. A loose Tennessee Titan t-shirt covered her bandaged ribs. She had been brought to the hospital shortly after Fresh and now she’s been released with orders for plenty of rest and consider a rehabilitation program for her drinking problem. “Thanks for picking me up Jack. I don’t know if I could handle the clutch on my truck.” A smile escapes Jacks lips. He was standing outside the arena when the EMS took her away on a stretcher. He also knew she had no one else to call when it was time for her discharge. He approached her and took her take-home bag containing nick-nacks provided by the hospital. “I’m here for you girl. You know that. I’ll always be here for you.” He stared down at her face long enough to let his words sink in. He had seen how Scott had taken advantage of her intoxicated state and won their match. With that man completely out of her life, Teresa would be very vulnerable. No one knew this more than Jack Slayter. Her baby blues left his dark blue eyes when she felt his large hand gently squeeze her arm. This man has been trying to get close to her ever since he followed her to Spain. He has clung onto her like a leech and she just can’t seem to pull him off. “Listen Jack, I appreciate all this, but don’t take me calling you this morning the wrong way. I just need a ride to my hotel” Suddenly a cell phone rang. Both Teresa and Jack give each other a glance and look down. It was Teresa’s phone. The cell was in her take-home bag that Jack was carrying. Teresa reached down to get it but winced in pain. Her broken ribs couldn’t take the strain. She looked up at Jack who was watching her. He took the cell phone from the bag. He resisted the temptation to sneak a peak at the ID and handed it to her. “Hello?” She stood there with her phone to her ear listening. Jack watched curiously as the expression on her face turn hard. Finally she hung up and threw the phone in the bag before snatching it from Jack’s grip. “Son of a freaking bitch!” Teresa mumbled to herself before storming out of the hospital room. Jack followed behind without a word. He had the keys. She wasn’t going far without him. Ramada Inn served as Teresa’s next temporary sleeping quarter until the next show. It was a moderately priced hotel with the basic Queen-sized bed, large dresser with equally large mirror, night stands on both sides of the bed with wall-mounted lamps, and a small round table with two chair opposite of the small bathroom. The colors held an Italian feel to accommodate the Italian restaurant downstairs. Teresa used her key card to open the door to her room. Placed on the small round table was a bouquet of red rose buds. Teresa looked over her shoulder and gave Jack a suspicious glare. He returned with a sheepish grin and pointed toward the bed. “Oh my god Jack. You didn’t….” The door shut and automatically locked behind them. Jack rested his back against the door and crossed his large arms over his half-barreled chest. He watched with self pride as the delighted redneck accepts her gift. The gift was simply two cases of Miller beer, and a large bottle of Jack Daniels Whiskey. Teresa sat on the edge of the bed and immediately opened the bottle of Jack. She took a long swing relishing the burning sensation racing down her throat. Soon she’ll forget how sore her broken ribs are. “Easy now. Don’t want to over do it. It’s too early to be passing out.” Jack sat down beside her, grabbed a bottle of beer and placed the two cases on the floor. He watched as Teresa kicked off her sneaker and scooted back onto the bed so that her back is resting against the pillows at the head board. He took his first drink then scooted beside her. They sat in silence for a few moments listening to rushing cars speeding by on the interstate while rain splattered madly against the window. Teresa closes her eyes and leans her head back against the wall. “You just don’t know how relaxed I feel right now Jack. I got my favorite drink in my hand, a soft warm bed to sleep in tonight and this rain makes it all the better.” “I have an idea. I’m pretty relaxed too.” Jack reached to touch her and was surprised when she didn’t jerk her hand away. All the memories he had of her all those years ago came rushing back like a tidal wave reminding him of what they had and how that punk Scott Taylor took his jewel away from him. “Teresa?” “Yeah?” Her bottle was nearly half gone and resting on the small night stand beside her. She was lying on her back staring at the wall with her eyes closed. No matter how much she drank, she couldn’t drown the thoughts of the night before. The slightest hope she had left that maybe Scott had forgiven her for what she done had been crushed the night before. She was in no shape to wrestle. Something was really wrong with her but he didn’t care. All Scott wanted was revenge and the bandages wrapped around her ribs proved it. “I know who you’re thinking about. Forget him. He’s an asshole. I know you don’t want to hear that, but it’s the truth and it’s about time you accepted the truth. Look what he’s done to you?” A single tear escapes down the corner of Teresa’s eye. She felt his thumb gently wipe it away. Jack had rolled over to his side to face her. She turned her head to find him watching her. “I’m being forced to wrestle on the next show. Dammit, I’m injured. Don’t anyone care? Jack couldn’t help but feel sorry for her. He could see it on her face that her attempt to change the subject doesn’t help her feel any better. He bravely took her hand and brought it to his lips for a soft kiss. “You know I’m right Teresa. I can see it in your eyes. Your ribs aren’t the only part of you hurting. Let me back in and I promise to let all that pain go away.” Teresa didn’t move. The whiskey had relaxed her body to the point, she didn’t want to move. Jack’s promising words felt soothing to her ears, but she knew better than that. She didn’t want Jack lying beside her. What she did want she couldn’t have. What she did want hated her and she hated him for being so heartless. Before she could speak a word, she felt Jack’s soft lips press against hers. His fingers carefully brushed strands of blonde hair from her eyes. Her alcohol driven body told her to relax and allow him to satisfy its urges. Her mind, full of anger, frustration, and rage screamed to release these emotions. Jack breathed hard against her ear and began kissing her neck. “Jack…..” She whispered. Her hands grasp tightly into his black-shirt. “Shhhhhh” He replied in a harsh whisper as he carefully slipped off her shirt, then his own. His Stetson fell to the floor. “Just relax. You deserve it” “Ohhhhhhh” Teresa moaned. She couldn’t fight him. He was much too strong and dammit, his touch felt too damn good.
