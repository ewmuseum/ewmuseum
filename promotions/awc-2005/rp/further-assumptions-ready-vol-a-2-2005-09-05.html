Further Assumptions (reAdy Vol a:2

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a love letter. That was apparent, but on more detailed clarification, it was more a note of admiration than anything. Nothing like a work of prose composed on the melodic heartstrings or anything like that; just a simple declaration of a few emotions printed neatly on a small slip of white paper folded. It was held in delicate hands, soft looking to touch, and dainty in its grasp. The girl holding the letter didn’t want anything to happen to it, cradling the single leaflet as she read it. ”I do not want to sound forward with you, but I have developed a bit of a liking to you. I hope this isn’t any disrespect to you in any way, but I hope that we could sometime go out and do something, sort of like a date. I know you’re not like other girls, but I’m willing to do more or less whatever you want on this date. Please let me know.” Title: “Further Assumptions 2/3 (estimated)” Subtitle: “Lies and Truth = True and False” Kirima Nagi walked slowly towards the next destination on her path, the spot near the mall. The same spot where she had her fight with Yagami Kyosuke, there had been little activity since then. Excluding the Jue Hibiki incident, the city had been more or less quiet, at least in Nagi’s jurisdiction. But because there was a bit of peace and quiet didn’t mean that Nagi had to get lazy. That only meant that she needed to make sure that things stayed the way they were now, and not turn into another dramatic incident of violence and death. There was something vigilant about her, despite her bad girl reputation. After clearing her name of the Hibiki murder rather promptly, she was getting a little less hassle from the other students as far as looks and whispers. Respect and order had returned to the campus, and Nagi wouldn’t have it any other way. There was still something about Nagi that put everyone on edge, however. With the uncharacteristic show of justice within Nagi shared with Kyosuke that night, she was still keeping her bad girl image. There wasn’t a day that didn’t pass without someone feeling the wrath of the First and Last. Some unlucky soul would have bruises by the end of the day… that’s just how things go. It was either she or the other guy; and the way she saw it, she’d prefer it to be the other guy. Remember that. Nagi approached the fountain and stood before it, watching the water cascade down into the pool. The water was a cycle, coming up from below through the waterworks back down from the fountain nozzles. It almost reminded her of other things in her life the way it would continue to circle back and forth, in and out, up and down. It was almost similar to reincarnation, if she believed in that sort of thing. No, she was reminded more of a battle. It would go back and forth, around and around, without gain or advantage on either party. Much like the battle of life and death, where one dies, another is born and the neutrality is restored. Of course, Nagi didn’t have enough time to continue her thoughts. She was knocked back into reality suddenly. That… was a literal statement. Someone had actually knocked into her… hard. The two figures hit the waters with a huge splash, Nagi already thrashing herself about trying to pry the opponent off of her. This particular opponent seemed to have a bit of skill, and knew where Nagi kept her weapon of choice. All it took was a hand to slip into her jacket and flip the switch on her stun gun to end the fight quickly. Nagi gasped as electricity jolted through her body, as well as the entire fountain. After a moment, the stun gun shorted out from the overcharge, and left a sizzling vat where Nagi lay on her side, her torso facing up with eyes closed. The thing about electricity and water, its well known that they don’t mix, and it can be said that if you tackle someone into a pool of water and electrocute her, there’s little to no time for the attacker to escape as well. The female figure lay next to Nagi, arms wrapped around her waist holding her tight from the struggle. In theory, there was no winner or loser in this skirmish. Both were unconscious from the electrocution, and it was now a standstill. Rather neutral… much like the fountain itself. The insignia of her school crest was on her blazer… -=-=-=-=-=- The same school crest was on the breast of Chibi’s blazer, standing out like a coat of arms worn by knights of old. He walked to school slowly, carrying his bag over his shoulder. It seemed like his mind was elsewhere, thinking on other things besides preparation for school. His mind was so much distracted that he barely heard his name called out. “Damn, what’s up with the pint-size?” Jinnai muttered, arm crooked up holding the handle of his school bag over his shoulder. Kawada, Ishikawa, and Kawaii all turned and watched as Chibi walked right past the four of them right on down the sidewalk, despite Jinnai having called for him a few times. Ishikawa shrugged her shoulders, soccer ball cradled under her arm. “Who knows, who cares?” Right away Kawaii leaned forward in front of Ishikawa and cupped her hands around her mouth. “CHIIIIIIIII-BIIIIIIII-CHAN!!” Ishikawa jumped gritting her teeth as the high-pitched girl squealed out Chibi’s nickname. With no way to ignore that, Chibi turned around with a distant expression, looking back as if he were awaken from a dream to find those four behind him. “Oh,” he said drowsily. “Morning.” His hand gave a wave to them, a half smile. Kawaii was the chipper one to wave back, adding a bow to the greeting. “Good morning, Chi-bi-chan!” “You know how he reacts to that name,” Kawada said, walking ahead of the rest. “So why persist?” “’Cause its sooooo CUTE!” she chimed back, clasping her hands together as she followed with the rest of them. “And if there’s anything I know, it’s about what’s cute.” “Cutie Kawaii,” Jinnai muttered, nudging her in the arm as he walked ahead of her. She smiled and nodded, giving him a ‘thumbs up’ sign. “That’s right!” Moving on, “Shiganori,” Kawada stood by Chibi as the 5 of them walked to school together. “What was on your mind so early in the morning? Having trouble with the homework?” He nodded, a sheepish smile. “Well yeah I always have a little bit of trouble with the material, but that’s not what I was worried about really.” Ishikawa took her turn to be nosy. “What’s up then?” the tone in her voice said that she really didn’t care, but she wanted to know what was going on. “Well,” Chibi began, “usually I walk to school with my friend Ikari. You know Ikari right?” Kawada nodded slowly. “Yeah, she’s in your class. I know.” “Oh yeah, the cute lil’ babe with the big—“ Ishikawa cut off Jinnai with a slicing kick into the back of his knee. He yelped as he dropped down to one knee, glaring up at her as he picked himself back up. “—heart,” he continued, sticking his tongue out at her. Chibi nodded, half chuckling at Jinnai. “Yeah, she’s a really cool person. We grew up together, went to grade school and middle school together.” “So what’s wrong with her?” Kawaii asked curiously. “Well I went to her house to meet her like I’ve done for years, and well… her mom said that she was already gone.” “So?” Jinnai shrugged dismissing his reasons. “Maybe she felt like going it alone today? I can understand that… sometimes I gotta get away from the babes and have a little me time, ya know?” Ishikawa was ready for him here. “So what, you close your dirty magazines and put them under your pillow to achieve that?” Before Jinnai and Ishikawa could start so early in the morning, Kawada cut them off with another question to Chibi. “So… why would she do something like that?” “I dunno. She’s never left without me all these years. That’s why I’m a bit concerned.” Kawada rubbed her thumb against her forefinger as she thought about the situation. “Well,” she said finally, “all you can do now is ask her when you see her at school.” He nodded, giving Kawada an appreciative look. “You’re right. That’s exactly what I’m gonna do before the first bell rings.” -=-=-=-=-=- It was a love letter. Well… not really, but you get the point by now. After having read it, the girl’s hands were shown folding back in half along the crease already folded. Then she slipped it back into the envelope, long fingers tucking it neatly inside. Tucking the flap inside the envelope, she held it out towards someone. “Thanks… but no thanks.” The low voice of Kirima Nagi couldn’t be colder. -=-=-=-=-=- The lunch bell rang, and all the students piled out into the quad to make the most of their break time. Chibi walked out of the building… alone, and despaired. More so than before school had started. His eyes scanned over the quad quickly before catching onto someone. Then he moved through the yard towards them, coming to stand before Kawada, Jinnai, Kawaii, and Ishikawa with short breaths. The four upperclassmen were standing around talking when the year younger sophomore approached rather briskly. The look on his face told Kawada that everything wasn’t settled since that morning. “So I told the gal,” Jinnai was telling his friends as Chibi joined the group. “ ‘Look, if you don’t wanna go out that’s fine, but you don’t have to slap me!’” Kawada shrugged, sighing at the end of that story. “Well, that’s what happens when you try and hit on people at a funeral. Especially with lines like, ‘Hey, I know you and Jue Hibiki were close… so since she’s gone let’s see if we can get closer, huh baby?’” Kawaii nodded slowly, as if she actually leaned something from such an idiotic story. “So true…” “Well?” Ishikawa asked, footing her soccer ball around with the toes of her shoes as she turned her attention to Chibi. “What’s up now?” “Ikari didn’t come to school today.” He snapped back urgently. “I don’t know what’s going on.” Kawada’s thumb went to rubbing her forefinger as she frowned. “Hmm.” “Hmm?” Kawaii heard the gears moving in Kawada’s head... in contrast to the absence of those running within her own. “What do you think, Kawada?” “Well, there might be something going around, that’s what I think.” Chibi’s eyes showed that he didn’t like the sound of that. “Something going around? Like what?? Like something going around snatching people?!” “Holy crap,” Jinnai rolled his eyes as he plopped down on the grass. “Cool your jets, kiddo. She didn’t say anything like that at all.” But the seeds were already planted in Kawaii. The girl leaned in close to Kawada, a scared look in her eyes. “But… there ISN’T anything going around taking up cute girls like me… is there??” “No,” Ishikawa shook her head. “I don’t think Jinnai has the balls to do anything like that anyway.” He growled at the soccer girl as Kawada shook her head. “No, no that’s not what I meant at all. I meant there must be like a bug or something moving around the school, because I noticed that Saotome wasn’t here today either.” “He wasn’t?” Ishikawa asked, scratching the back of her head as she thought back. “As a matter of fact, he wasn’t. I didn’t really notice, surprisingly. I’ve grown accustomed to ignoring that jerk at this point.” Chibi’s brow rose. “Saotome was gone? Maru was as well.” “Heh looks like the two took a lover’s holiday.” Jinnai grinned perversely. “Lucky bastard.” “What’s Ikari not coming to school have to do with Saotome and Maru skipping?” Chibi asked exasperated. Kawaii put her finger to her chin as she looked up in thought. “Hmm, I could’ve sworn there was someone else gone as well… but I can’t remember who it was…” Jinnai looked around the yard before calling out to a passerby. “Yo, Tachibana!” The longhaired girl stopped in her tracks at the sound of her name. She was rotating through flash cards, making the most of her free time to pick her grades up. “Yes?” “You know who else is absent today?” She looked up to think about it in fashion of Kawaii, but retracted an answer a bit quicker than the airheaded girl. “Um, Kirima Nagi. Kirima Nagi didn’t show up for school today.” Chibi heard that, and his face went grim. “K-K-Kirima Nagi… is gone?” he turned around slowly to face Tachibana with a bleak expression. She nodded. “Yep. That’s not something that doesn’t go unnoticed. You think the child doesn’t sleep easier knowing the monster’s not under his bed?” “She’s got a point,” Ishikawa added in. “It’s been pretty lackadaisical around here. Must be because Nagi’s not here putting everyone on pins and needles.” Chibi still wasn’t over the fact that Nagi, along with Saotome and Maru, were absent. “I gotta go!” The 5 juniors watched as Chibi ran off into the building, Tachibana turning back to look at them with a quizzical face. “What’s with him?” Jinnai shrugged. “Drama queen?” Ishikawa rolled her eyes as Kawada kept on thinking. “Hmm. Nagi, Ikari, Saotome, and Maru… all missing… Chibi seems to be worried about those names together. I wonder why?” “Aw hell,” Jinnai signed, leaning down and placing his hands on his knees. “Please tell me we’re not gonna Scooby this up and try and figure out what the hell’s going on, right?” The next thing he knew, he was standing there in the library with the rest of them with a cynical look on his face and arms folded. Ishikawa nudged him in the side and smirked. “Cheer up Shaggy,” she whispered. Jinnai would have strangled her there and then if not for Ms. Ozaki standing across from them behind her counter thinking over the information Kawada told her. With a copy of HP Lovecraft closed with her finger holding place, she processed what she was told as much as she could. “Well…” she started off slowly, her fingernail scratching against the paper of the book lightly. “I can see the connection between Saotome and Maru being absent. Nothing but a lover’s holiday between students; but it’s the fact that Ikari and Nagi are also gone that muddles things.” “And Chibi seemed to worry a bit more after he heard that Nagi was gone.” Kawada relayed that detail to the librarian. “Like he was afraid of what she could do to Ikari, or even to Saotome or Maru.” Ozaki smiled a bit with a shake of her head. “Come on, you treat that girl like she’s the Devil herself.” “She’s pretty close to it I say.” Ishikawa responded. “I heard she killed her father. If that doesn’t say psychologically unstable, nothing does.” “That’s just myth,” Ozaki corrected her. “If Nagi had done anything like that I’m sure she wouldn’t be in school right now. She’d be in some correctional facility somewhere, or asylum getting help.” Ishikawa didn’t seem to assured by her reasoning. “Well… either way there’s something totally wrong with that girl. I’ve never seen anyone with such an evil presence as her…” “I’m even scared of her, and I’m not scared of any chick!” Jinnai agreed, much to the eye rolling of Ishikawa. Kawaii chimed in as well with her own ‘SCA-RY’ comment. Despite all that was going on, Kawada had grown a bit silent over the period of time. Ozaki looked at her, cocking her head to the side to meet her eyes. “Something wrong, Kawada?” She looked up slowly, and then her eyes went back down to the counter. “Well… back when Yagami-sempai was looking into Hibiki’s death… Chibi came into the lab to speak with us.” Ozaki nodded. “Yes I remember.” “He told us… that he saw Nagi at the scene of Hibiki’s murder sometime around the time that she died. He heard her scream, and then saw Nagi walk out of the factory right afterwards.” Kawaii’s hands went over her gasping mouth. “NAGI’S GONNA KILL IKARI TO GET BACK AT CHIBI RATTING HER OUT!!” Everyone looked at her, startled at her outburst. Jinnai gave Kawaii a cross look for saying such a stupid thing before stepping back and shaking his head, pacing back in forth in a straight line. Ishikawa patted Kawaii on the back to ease her. “Okay… Kawaii? Let’s calm down here. Don’t jump ahead of the story here.” Kawada shook her head anyways. “Yagami-sempai said that she spoke with Nagi himself, and she said that she didn’t do it. If he believes her, then I think she’s innocent.” Ozaki folded her arms over her white blouse and looked down at the book. “Well… what else can be said? I guess we’ll just have to wait until Chibi comes back tomorrow and get whatever explanation from him we can.” “If he comes back, that is,” Kawaii uttered frightened. Ishikawa sighed, Jinnai in the back throwing up his hands with a shake of his head at Kawaii. Just then Ozaki slapped herself in the forehead lightly. “Oops. How could I forget?” Everyone laid eyes on her. “What?” perhaps she had stumbled upon some answer that they had overlooked. But she only pointed down to the book she was reading and smiled softly. “I lost my page.” -=-=-=-=-=- Knocks came to the door in rapid succession, knuckles hitting wood with urgency. Finally the front door opened, and an older woman wearing a white apron and a blue dress looked at her guest. “Oh, Shiganori!” she smoothed back her short black hair and smiled with a strange look. She found it rather surprising to see her daughter’s friend standing at her door… with school still in session. “Is there something wrong?” “Ikari didn’t come to school today!” Chibi blurted out dramatically, concern etched on his face. “We have to do something!” Naturally in time like this a parent’s natural instinct kicked in, and the parent turned into an unstoppable force to be reckoned with, allowing nothing to stand between them and their offspring. However, Ikari’s mother’s instinct brought her to giggle. “Oh, Yuna!” she smiled, her hand to her mouth holding back her laugh. “Yes, she came back home sometime after you saying she wasn’t feeling good. I’m so sorry you were worried about her all day!” Chibi stared at his friend’s mom in disbelief. There he was, worrying sick about his best friend, and she was at home all day in bed instead of being dead or raped somewhere by a vicious killer. Shows what an overactive imagination can do for a young man these days. “She’s…” he repeated slowly, trying to process everything, “been home sick all day?” She nodded. “Yep. I figured she’d have run into you on her way back home, but… I guess not. Sorry!” Exhaling deeply, Chibi looked down at the ground with slumped shoulders. Then he looked back up with a smile, placing his arms behind his head. “Well then, hahahahaha! Sorry to worry you like that! But if it’s not too much trouble, can I speak with her for a second? You know, give her today’s homework and whatever?” The smile fluttered from her face slightly. “Oh, I’m sorry. She stepped out not too long ago. She said she felt like a bit of fresh air would do her good, so she went for a walk.” “Oh.” Chibi’s arms went down to his sides. “Well, I’ll just run back to school now. I was just so worried about her.” “Aw, aren’t you a sweet boy!” she patted him on the head as a mother would their child, which kind of brought a reddish hue of embarrassment to Chibi’s face. “I’ll let her know you dropped by!” Chibi thanked her and began walking back to class. As he ran back to school with only a few minutes left of lunch remaining, he tried to make some sense of things in his head. “Ikari’s fine… but that still doesn’t explain what happened to Nagi, or Saotome. Could it have something to do with… that day?” He ran past a line of foliage, trees and bushes placed near the sidewalk at the edge of the park. As he turned the corner towards school, someone stepped out of the bushes with their eyes cast in his direction. Ikari looked down at her hand, watching it tremble. Squeezing it into a fist, she looked back up after Chibi before walking in the opposite direction, back towards her home. The way her hand was shaking… it looked like she was suffering from the after effects of an electric shock… -=-=-=-=-=- As punishment for leaving school grounds without permission, Chibi was made to stand outside his classroom with a placard hung around his neck. I am a student that does not understand how to follow rules and guidelines, and am destined to become a plague on society. Obviously, he was not pleased. But he didn’t seem that upset with things. He just wanted to make sure that Ikari was okay. Seeing that she was not in any danger is worth the embarrassment of having to wear the placard. Chewing bubble gum, he drolly tried to pay attention to class behind the closed door at his back, but his mind began to wander. Giving a ‘Screw this’ to standing there, Chibi placed the sign down and began to wander the hallways. Thinking he could sneak into the library and get on the internet in its lab, he headed towards the western portion of the building. On his way, he passed the infirmary, but stopped. Something caught his eye that made him walk back in front of the door and turn the knob. He entered, and everything was silent in the school medical room. There was a bed against the wall, along with cabinets of medical equipment, and jars and canisters of cotton balls, tongue depressors, and lollipops for those checkups every so often. A large white curtain hung across the room on a metal bar with steel hoops, a divider for those times when there’s inter-gender cases and a student must disrobe. Chibi stood at the door for a moment before someone spoke to him. “Um… is someone there?” Chibi knew that voice. “Maru?” Her head came poking out from past the curtain, looking at him with a polite smile, though it looked fatigued. “Chibi? What’s wrong? Why are you here?” He thumbed over his shoulder and smirked. “I got placed out of class for leaving school during lunch. But why are you here? Is everything all right?” She nodded quickly, her eyes darting at the door. “Um, yes… I fainted earlier today, and I’ve been resting here since then.” “Oh, I see.” Just then, Maru winced. Her eyes closed slightly as she staggered forward a bit. She gasped, a lightheaded look on her face as she clung to the sheet divider. Chibi stepped forward to help her. “Are you okay?” “I’m fine!” she whined, eyes narrowing on him. “I’m… just fine… heh, so don’t… worry…” Chibi’s brow rose. “Maru… do you know where Saotome is today? He didn’t come to class.” She closed her eyes, thinking as she bit her lip. “Mmmmm… I don’t know. I think he had some things to do, so he’s probably… pounding it out… right about now… heh.” “Oh, okay,” Chibi accepted that as an answer, though it was shady the way she responded. “Well there’s been some strange things going on around here lately. Saotome and Nagi didn’t come to school today, and neither did my friend Ikagi. I thought something was going on, and he was behind it.” “Oh is he ever.” Maru giggled as she leaned on the curtain and exhaled deeply. “What’s that supposed to mean?” Chibi asked? “Oh god!” She exclaimed, closing her eyes. “You know what it means… Damn… you were there, when that beast Nagi beat you all up without reason. Oh… Saotome has it all worked out… when we see Nagi… we’ll teach her a… les… less…lesson!” Things were strange with Maru. “Saotome told you she beat us up without reason? That’s now it happened at all.” Maru looked up with her head cocked to the side. “What?” “Saotome somehow got into his mind that if he could have Nagi as his girlfriend, he could rule the school with an iron fist. But when Nagi refused… Saotome thought he could take her by force…” -=-=-=-=-=- “Well Nagi… fancy running into you here.” Saotome said with a cocky smirk. Nagi stared at him, uninterested in him or the people with him. “What is it? I’m busy.” “I was hoping that maybe you reconsidered the offer I gave you a few days ago.” She shook her head. “Not really. I have no desire to become acquaintances with you, let alone look at you honestly.” That didn’t bode well with Saotome, his cocky smile turned into a dissatisfied frown. “Too bad…we’ll just have to do things the hard way then.” Just then Chibi threw himself between Nagi and Saotome. “No! I won’t let you do it!” Looking back, Chibi told Nagi to get out of there. As he turned back around, a fist was there to greet him, and it knocked him onto the ground, rolling into the grass on his back. Nagi watched Chibi roll into the grass without expression, looking back up as the other three boys looked on eagerly. “Surround her.” And on Saotome’s order, the three of them took a position facing her. Three on one… those odds were great, Saotome thought. But of course, he was assuming that she was just like every other girl… that he had a chance to beat her if the numbers were over her. Height, weight, strength… if it was tripled, he might have a chance… He was assuming that she wasn’t a demon… -=-=-=-=-=- “No… no… no…” Maru repeated. “You’re lying… my Saotome wouldn’t lie to me like that… mmmmmmmNO.” Chibi knew something was going on then. Maru’s breath had grown terribly short since he began that story. Like she couldn’t take the truth, and was having a nervous breakdown. Chibi rushed over to her and grabbed her by the shoulders to calm her down. “Maru! Maru calm dow…” And with the veil pulled from his eyes, he saw everything clearly. Only thing was… the veil was more like a white curtain pushed aside enough for him to see. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] What was even stranger… was that Jinnai was peering in from the window behind them. They had obviously picked up a second unwanted spectator in Chibi, though he was the only one to make himself known. Saotome looked at Chibi with cold eyes. “Do… you… mind?” Chibi glanced from Maru looking down to the cold eyes of Chibi… and even to the lecherous ones of Jinnai who was shooing Chibi away mime-like from behind the window. He nodded slowly, and pulled the curtain back closed before leaving the infirmary. Walking back along the hall slowly, he felt at ease at least. “Well… Saotome and Maru are clear,” he muttered to himself. “So I guess Nagi’s taking a skip day or something… So I guess I have nothing to worry abou…” He stopped, because there was, of course… something to worry about. There was always something to worry about, especially for the students of… well the name of the school isn’t important right now. What Chibi had remembered however, was. -=-=-=-=-=- It was a love letter… … and it was in the hands of Kirima Nagi. She was handing it back to the person that had given it to her, with words of rejection. The hand took back the note they had worked on, Nagi walking away from them back up the path towards the quad. The person she walked away from, most would expect it to be Saotome given the story that Chibi told Maru. Instead, it was Chibi himself that was shot down. He held the note in his hands; head slumped in defeat. He turned around to sulk off in shame and embarrassment, but stopped in his tracks. Someone else was there to bear witness to his expression of love for the terror called the First and Last, the girl Kirima Nagi. Someone else was there to share his pain of rejection… and he had assumed that they were alone… Shiganori ‘Chibi’ Hyotaru met eyes with Ikari Yuna… … his best friend… who was also in love with him… … and he assumed they were just friends…
