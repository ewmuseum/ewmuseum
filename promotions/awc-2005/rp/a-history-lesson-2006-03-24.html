A History Lesson

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/06 The Internet buzz, the dirtsheets, the fan polls, every single soundbite and quote, it all indicated that in the Trivial Punishment match at Twilight of the Gods, Captain Suleimon was the underdog against Darcy Crisis. Clearly, unequivocably... in the court of public opinion, this match was Crisis' to lose. Now, in the history of modern man, the Turks had not been the underdogs that much. Seriously, the Ottomans were a finely tuned war machine in their heyday. Just ask the Greeks and the Bulgarians. I think the entire city of Sofia collectively pissed its pants when the Ottoman army marched on it. In fact, if you wanted to go all the way back to when the Turks were last the underdogs... ...well, let's take it all the way back to the Middle Ages, into this conversation by two Turks. Turk #1 So yeah, I heard there was this bunch of white guys riding over the hills and stuff. Turk #2 O rly? Turk #1 Ys rly. They look pissed. Turk #2 Shit. This isn't because we sacked Jerusalem, is it? Turk #1 Yeah, I think it is man. Turk #2 Shit, damn, it's probably because Achmed took a whiz on Golgotha. I knew he shouldn't have done that. Turk #1 Well, I don't know, I don't think they knew about that. Or about my cousin who took a dump in the Holy Grail and served it to this monk and passed it off as coffee. Turk #2 Oh man, that was classic. But seriously man if they didn't hear about that, what the hell could they be coming for? Turk #1 Well we sacked their holiest city. Turk #2 Oh yeah. I guess that pissed them off bigtime. Turk #1 Yeah. I mean, there's like... I don't know, like thousands of them. I don't know exactly. I sent Sharif to take a rough headcount and they shot him through the heart with an arrow. Turk #2 That's a hurt piece. Turk #1 Yeah. Turk #2 So what are we gonna do? Turk #1 Well, I think they have more guys than us. Like they just brought like everyone from all their countries and shit. But they all came from like far away, so they're probably all beat as shit. Turk #2 Yeah man, I can't go from Istanbul to Ankara without getting a little gassed. They came all the way from... shit, where do you think they came from? Turk #1 I don't know. I mean, I was gonna have Sharif ask them where they came from, and well you know... the whole ARROW'd! thing. Turk #2 Yeah... yeah. So yeah, so we just like, wait for them to get tired? Turk #1 Well yeah. I figure if we get a good night's sleep we can at least, you know... outlast them. Turk #2 Well, what if they all just take a giant nap before the battle? Turk #1 What? Turk #2 You know, if they just take a nap, you know, with some sleeping bags and fluffy pillows. Some chamomile tea and warm goat's milk they heated up in the microwave. Turk #1 Dude, the microwave isn't going to be invented for like another eight hundred years or so. Turk #2 Oh yeah. I forgot. But still. Turk #1 Well, let's just hypothetically say that they all take one giant nap before battle, and the Sultan becomes a retard for the time being and decides not to attack them while they're sleeping, and all of a sudden, they're not absolutely exhausted from riding on their horses over thousands of miles across rivers and swamps and forests and all that other shit, well, we'll just have to use the terrain to our advantage and outsmart them. Turk #2 That's deep man. Deep. Hey, you wanna go smoke from a hookah before we all go to fight to our deaths over the Holy Land? Turk #1 Fuck yeah. I'm down for that shit. The Turks, a calm, cool, collected people. And really ever since that day, they were pretty much the favorites in any war they fought in. Notice after that, the West really didn't pick many fights with them. I mean, after nine Crusades of getting the shit kicked out of them by Ottoman warriors, you think you'd learn your lesson. So thusly, the Ottoman Empire kicked ass and took names for all those years, all as the favorite in whatever war they were in. Like I said, this group of folks didn't quite know what it was like to be the underdog. Especially not Captain Suleimon. Nope, in his mind, he's still the favorite he is as the last carrier of the mighty Pasha Dynasty. Some would call it arrogant. Some just overconfident. Some would call it pathetic. Still others might call it being cocksure or whatever. But still, one might look at another chapter in Turkish history, a little more recent than the first exchange. Turk #1 Dude, World War I just started. Turk #2 Shit man, what side we on? The Allies? The Krauts? Please, tell me we picked a side. I don't wanna be all neutral like those Swiss fags. Please, please... Turk #1 Relax man, we picked a side, and I think we picked a winner. We're gonna be on the Germans' side. Turk #2 Oh yeah! I never liked those limey bastards anyway. Plus the Germans have really good sausage. So what are we gonna do to prepare this time? Turk #1 Nothing. We kicked their asses in the Crusades. We'll do it again. Turk #2 But what if they take another nap and... Turk #1 Dude, they're not taking any naps. They'll be well-rested. But still, we rule and they drool. And as we all know, the Ottoman Empire was dissolved after they were humiliatingly dismantled in World War I. Nothing like latching yourself onto the losing side in a bout of overconfidence. So you had Suleimon who at times displayed the arrogance of the Ottomans in WWI, but was an underdog, like his Turkish Crusaders of old. Which side would prove right? Well, while history may repeat itself... it's not the be-all end-all. And thusly, Captain Suleimon was training for his match, the Trivial Punishment match, against Darcy Crisis. 'Unh... Queen Elizabeth the First.' Suleimon touched the tips of his elbows on the tips of his knees, then fell flat on his back, ready to repeat his exercise for the 127th time. Dogan had to be quick with the questions and have one ready for Suleimon to answer upon the height of his situp or else he'd face chiding. 'The last queen of Hawaii.' 'Unh... Queen Lydia Liliokalani...' One-hundred twenty-eight questions, all answered correctly. Suleimon was as sharp on his history as he was on his wrestling. Still, something hadn't set right with Dogan. His friend Suleimon was bordering on maniacal arrogance, despite the fact that Darcy had been keen to him at every turn so far. To Dogan, the Captain was fighting a losing war, and he didn't even know it. 'What was Henry Hudson looking for in his explorations?' 'Unh... the Northwest Passage.' While in his heart he wanted to see Suleimon fail again to knock him down a peg, Dogan still felt uneasy that Suleimon might have been walking into his defeat. Schadenfreude wasn't something that he could feel easily. He let took his hands off Suleimon's feet, and the Captain broke his concentration. 'What the hell did you do that for?' Suleimon was pissed. 'Why do you try so hard?' Suleimon looked annoyed now. 'Answer me... why do you try so hard?' 'Don't you take that tone with me. And I don't know why you'd ask such a question.' 'Don't be stupid, Suleimon. You know that you're running scared from Darcy and that he's had the answer for you this whole time. Why try so hard to fight a losing battle.' A losing battle? Suleimon sneered and remained silent, out of protest. 'You know it... you know you're losing. Ha, that's the first step, admitting you have a problem. Maybe now you can change...' 'Silence.' DOgan was going to open his mouth again. 'SILENCE!' Now he shut up. 'You think I train this hard because I think I'm going to lose? Because I'm scared? Because I think Darcy has my number?' Dogan went to open his mouth again, but cut off. 'You think that because Darcy Crisis answered a few questions and because I haven't been able to get over on him that he's automatically going to win. You're just so ready to be just like all those other message board posters who are willing to write both me and your own people off. Well, I'll tell you something. Just because I know I'm going to win this match doesn't mean I don't have to prepare for it. 'The Ottoman Empire has always prided itself on knowing the plan of action, and I am following through. If you think it's panic, then you're no better than fools writing me off.' Dogan looked on in momentary silence. He cleared his throat. 'Fine... there's no way to change your mind then. Back to the training.' Suleimon's face was still stone cold as he went back to his situp training. Whether he was confident or arrogant the world wouldn't know until after Twilight. But there was no denying that Suleimon knew he had to work. But could he?
