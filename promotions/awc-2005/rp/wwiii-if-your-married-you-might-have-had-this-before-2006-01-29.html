WWIII, If your married you might have had this before

29 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/29/06 A strong melody from Garth Brooks’ song I’ve Got Friends in Low Places is heard bellowing through out the country and western bar. The expected crowd is in attendance, the cowboy hats, the flannel shirts, big belt buckles, and of course the beer. The place is filled with a good amount of people, most sitting at the bar drinking a beer, no not a pint or a lager for you foreigners it is a beer in a mason jar, the only kind we have ever seen in America, Budweiser. The crowd is dancing along with the music having a good time enjoying music beer and each others company. Meanwhile Outside A nice looking Black Chevrolet Silverado pulls into the parking lot. Nothing really abnormal for the parking lot as most of the people inside are good Americans and buy only American made products such as Chevy, Ford, and sadly some of them drive Dodge, but some day they will learn. The truck pulls up to a parking spot, we can see inside the truck, and we see what seems to be a angry woman in the passenger seat. As we take a closer look we see it is none other then Tiara Belle and Wayne Russell, the newest members to AWC. But what could they be bickering about this time, anything is the obvious answer to that, their married. “What are we doing here?” An obviously irritated and also confused Mrs. Russell shows her concern to her husband. While she is talking Wayne unbuckles and reaches into the backseat of the Crew Cab Chevy and brings out his black leather cowboy hat. “What do you think? Line dancing and having some fun.” “Are you seri……Where did you get that hat?” More and more concern is imminent in the face of Tiara Belle. “I went and bought it the other day, don’t worry I got you one.” Wayne seems to be very happy about his recent purchases, he looks forward to this new surroundings. “Oh, no you did not. This is not my type of thing you know that.” Tiara is showing her disgruntlement but Wayne is not paying any attention. “Well maybe it is time for you to look into some new ways of having fun then. Maybe that is why you don’t like that other tag team in AWC, what are their names?” “Norton and Bridges? Those guys are assholes, and to top it off they can’t even sing.” “I never said they were going to be the next Garth Brooks, Alan Jackson, or hell even Brooks and Dunn, but you need to start somewhere” Wayne is all excited about going line dancing and experiencing something new, but you can tell his wife is not sharing in the enjoyment. “Are you telling me, those two buffoons put you up to this?” The vein in the forehead of Tiara is starting to throb. “No, not at all, you think I would actually have a conversation with them?” Tiara has the look of death in her eyes; she thinks her husband has just betrayed her for some other people that he knows she does not like. “Then why would you pull a stunt like this?” “Stop being like this Tiara, there is no stunt, I was just trying to expose us to new things, and this will be a good time. Now grab your cowboy hat and come on inside with me.” Wayne continues to open the door after plopping the new cowboy hat on the legs of Tiara Belle. She is stunned as the leather hat hits here jeans and looks up with a glare in her eyes as if she doesn’t approve of Wayne’s newest hobby. “Come on babe, it will be a good time, we can dance along with a couple of songs, and hell we can even have a couple of drinks.” Wayne is ear to ear with a smile and continues to get out of the truck. Tiara Belle tries to interject but Wayne just keeps walking, “But….But….” Before long he is at the passenger side door and opens it trying to pull his wife out of the vehicle. Finally she comes along, willingly but it’s obvious she is not enthusiastic about it. “You will have a good time, trust me.” Wayne is smiling again as he now has her out of the car and heading over to the entrance with her cowboy hat on, they are arm and arm. Wayne opens the door for his wife and allows her in first like a gentleman, this is the man she knew but most people did not, the soft, gentle, and loving man. Their Entrance… The two walk into the bar and many of the regulars look their way when the first enter, they stick out like sore thumbs not wearing the complete cowboy outfit, but it is pretty obvious that they meant to be here and are not just looking for directions by their hats. “Shall we get a drink?” Wayne looks over to Tiara Belle and hopes she is going to nod yes as he really could use one by this time… I mean hell it was already 8:30 pm and he usually starts drinking around 5-5:30 pm, those three hours make a big difference to someone who drinks as much as he does. “I think I’m going to need it for me to stay here.” Tiara looks around with shifty eyes not to sure, she is going to enjoy this or not. Wayne leads the way over to the bar and orders a good ole American beer. The regulars think nothing of it, until Tiara Belle orders. She tries to order some kind of fancy drink or some shit and people start looking her way like she is an alien. Wayne quickly whispers to her, “Babe, I think you should try something not so elegant.” Tiara Belle nods and changes her order to a good ole fashion beer, not her choice drink but when it comes to it and she has to drink it she will. They both sit down at the bar and eat popcorn from the bowl that is placed in front of them and they slowly sip their drinks. As the beer goes down and the conversations get more interesting, it’s noticeable that Tiara Belle might actually like this new hobby of Wayne’s. The previous song by the Charlie Daniels Band finishes, then we hear the opening riffs of “I love this bar” by Toby Keith come on. Every single person in the bar gets up or starts singing as they feel it is like their theme song, it is almost like Cheers only not everyone knows your name. Not wanting to feel left out Wayne grabs Tiara Belle’s hand and escorts her to the dance floor, where they both have a little fun with a nice little slow dance, Wayne singing along during the chorus since he had recently purchased the CD so he could at least connect with these people on a certain degree. The two enjoy a dance and you actually get a smile out of Tiara as she sees happiness in the face of her husband again. Since the last time they wrestled in AWC he has been down a little bit, seeing as the way they entered was not as they both had hoped. He wasn’t the only one not happy with their entrance, but it wasn’t the loss that bothered her, it was the treatment from some of the other tag teams, she was hoping for a warm welcome, instead she was getting shunned much like women did before the second World War. World War III…. Or the first signs of divorce you tell me… Well we are about to see World War Three once Wayne tells Tiara why he really brought her out, but he figures he will wait till the song ends. Well for Wayne the song isn’t long enough and it ends shortly and they make their way back to the bar. Wayne orders them both another round of beers, as Tiara takes her seat at the bar, the looks they were getting from the regulars have slowly stopped with each person getting more accustom to these strangers in their bar, but they notice they are just out for a good time and mean no harm. Wayne takes his seat next to his wife, he smiles at her as he takes his first sip of his beer, and she does the same. The cold beverage is satisfying to their thirst that they got from dancing. Wayne slowly puts down his beer and places his hand on the blue jeans of Tiara. He looks her in the eyes and he slowly starts to speak, although he is very hesitant. “Well babe…. I wanted to try something different but there is another reason I took you out tonight.” Tiara knew something like this was going to be happening but she was hoping there wasn’t an alternative reason for him bringing her out. “Ok…. Well you can tell me anything you know that. What happened?” “Well it’s not really what happened it is more of what is going to happen.” As his final sentence starts to trail off, he reaches into his shirt pocket and pulls out an envelope. “We got another letter today.” “From who? What does it have to do with? You know you can tell me anything.” Tiara is on the verge of starting to bite her nails like anyone does when they are nervous, the way Wayne is presenting this makes it a lot worse then it seems. “I knew it we were fired after our first loss. Damn it!” Wayne is quick to get a response in before she thinks they are really getting fired; there is something drastic in this envelope but not that drastic. “No…No… they have sent us the next Fresh! Card.” “Let me see it!” The excitement was in her face, but then she quickly remembered how Wayne brought it up to her. She grabs the envelope out of the hands of Wayne. Quick to take the piece of paper out of the already ripped open envelope, she is quick to unfold it and the top has the monogrammed symbol of AWC. She quickly skims the letter for their name and place on the next card. Wayne sits there taking another drink of his beer knowing what is going to be coming. “What is this!?!?” Tiara is quick and very forceful about her question looking for an answer. But not allowing time for one she poses another question, “Did you request this?” “No… not at all babe, I opened it and was looking for the right moment to tell you.” “So how did this happen?” “I don’t know… we can ask when we get there on Tuesday.” Wayne is doing anything he can to try and calm down the usually very quaint and subtle woman down, however he is unsuccessful. “I knew you joined forces with those two rambling morons didn’t you? Didn’t you!? Fine don’t answer me.” Not like she gave him anytime too actually answer, but Wayne knows better then to try and talk and she is probably about go into another rambling monologue, well she wants a conversation but he knows better then speak when she is. “So what you expect me to just be a manager now? You don’t want to wrestle beside me? You want to be the king pin and get all the credit although both of us went through the blood sweat and tears?” This was the point where Wayne needed to intervene and he did, “Darling I had nothing to do with this, I think they just took a tag team partner from each team to try and see who is who in the division, you should be supporting me and expecting me to take it all and if I do it helps us get closer to the Unfuckables. I mean we are the next champions!” Tiara looks at him like he is crazy but at the same time like he has a point. She still doesn’t look like she is too happy. “Pay the bill, and lets go we have had a great time but we must talk about this elsewhere.” Wayne reaches into his pocket and pulls out money and leaves it on the bar as he leaves a half full bottle and full bottle of beer on the bar. He waves good bye to the barkeep and follows his wife out the door knowing he is in for a long night ahead of him.
