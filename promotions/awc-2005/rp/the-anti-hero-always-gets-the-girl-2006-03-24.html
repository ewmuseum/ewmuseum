The anti hero always gets the girl.

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March 2006 No shame no gain are the words repeated on the breath of Red Rock as he paces down his hall way; returning to his flat. No shame no gain are the words he mutters to himself because two weeks ago he found himself in the most uncharacteristic and bizarre situation he has ever been in. Red Rock decided to let his hair down for one night only and have a drink or two and the consequences ended him up in a sordid threesome with his friend and manager D’avid Bulrat and his new neighbour’s fat goth daughter Sarah Darling. But the predicament is this; Red Rock’s has found a new love interest in Sarah Darling’s best friend named Ren. She is a beautiful girl, just a couple of years younger than Red Rock but wise beyond her years. Now what would she think of Red Rock if she was to find out what kind of things he gets up to? She would think he was some kind of man slut, which Red Rock is defiantly not. No shame no gain indeed because he is going to have to muster up the courage to try and convince Sarah not to say anything to Ren, but is two weeks going to be too late? Yes quite a problem for Red Rock, but in spite of this he has managed to spend some of his bonus for winning the relentless title. Red Rock walks down the stale stinking hall way sporting an expensive new hair cut! No longer does he bare the long greasy unwashed badly groomed locks, now he has a shorter sleeker more confident tidy hair style. He flicks his trendy new fringe out of his eye before reaching in to his pocket for his key. As he pushes the door open he smells the attractive scent of some of Howard’s gorgeous home cooking sweeping through the atmosphere. Red Rock inhales the alluring odour of steak and ale pie with home made pastry and thanks his lucky stars for Howard. Red Rock: Howard, that pie smells hot! Howard doesn’t appear to hear Red Rock’s compliment as he is knelt down taking the freshly baked pie from the oven. Howard turns around and unexpectedly see Red Rock occupying his field of vision. And what follows is a strange reaction… Howard: NO! NOO! I BURIED YOU IN THE WOODS! Howard drops the pie; splattering it all over the floor and up the bottom of the cupboards. Howard recoils back and cowers up against the breakfast bar with a face like he has just seen a ghost. Red Rock closes the door behind him with a concerned expression looming. Red Rock: Howard, it’s me Red! Howard instantly recognises Red Rock’s voice and lowers his guard with a sigh of relief . Howard: Oh Red it’s only you, I’m sorry about that you startled me. Red Rock: Yeah ok. Who did you think I was? Howard: Oh…you just reminded me of someone I knew. Red Rock nods and smiles at Howard with curious thoughts running through his mind about what Howard meant about the woods? However the thoughts don’t linger for very long because he has much greater things on his mind. Now is a good time to venture across the hall to talk to Sarah about what happened. If he had any chance of stopping himself from wrecking any chance with Ren before anything even happened he has to address this issue. On that note Red Rock slowly backs away towards his front door; leaving Howard alone to clean up the splattered pie. In the last two weeks Red Rock has maintained a level of communication with Ren via the magic of text messaging, which has built the foundations of a potential relationship but he still hasn’t seen her in person since the day of that party two weeks ago. He still hasn’t seen Sarah either so as he stands before her front door he takes a few moments to compose himself before ringing the door bell. The theme tune from the hit ITV show ‘the bill’ is the tune that plays when Red Rock presses his finger on the button. This could be something to do with the fact that Charles Darling is a shamed former CID in the police force, who can’t quite let go. The tune from the door bell fades out and Red Rock takes a step back just as the front door opens. Sarah who is no taller than 5’3 stands before Red Rock in an over sized Children of Bodom t-shirt. Instantly there is a thick painful air of awkwardness between the two as they awkwardly fumble around with their words. Sarah: Oh…err…HI… Red Rock: Heh…um…hello… There is a brief awkward silence before an invitation is delivered. Sarah: Would you…like to come in? Red Rock: Ok… Red Rock slips his hands in his pockets and steps in to Sarah’s flat. The awkwardness is so immense that she doesn’t even notice Red Rock’s brilliant new hair cut. But that is the least of Red Rock worries at the moment. Red Rock follows Sarah in to the living room where she is watching Kerrang TV, which is playing Marilyn Mason’s tainted love for the 7th time in the hour. Red Rock: I fucking hate that song! He spoilt the original! Sarah: That’s my favourite song. Red Rock: Oh right…sorry. Anyway! Not important…I’m here… Sarah: I know…you want to talk about the… Red Rock: Don’t say it! Red Rock resists the urge to cringe in front of Sarah in fear of offending her then they both take a seat on the sofa and engage in convocation. Red Rock: Right…we know what we did so I’m going to be blunt. You see you’re friend Ren… well I kind of…well… Red Rock begins to turn red in the face with embarrassment whilst looking at the floor and dragging the tip of his foot around in circles on the carpet.. Red Rock: well…I sort of like her…and I was just wondering if…you could kind of…not say anything to her about…you know… Sarah sits in silence; simply staring at the floor with her hands cupped on her thighs. This body language is making Red Rock slightly nervous because it suggesting that there is something she isn’t telling him. Sarah: Of course I haven’t told her…how could I? I’ve felt so guilty about it ever since it happened. It would crush her if she knew about it. Red Rock: Oh right…why would you feel guilty? Sarah swings her head towards Red Rock so their eyes lock. Sarah: Because she likes you, you IDIOT! The impact of those words smack Red Rock in the face harder than a baseball bat with a nail in it but at the same time they cover his body in good bumps and a powerful feeling that Red Rock hasn’t felt for a long time joy. Winning the relentless championship holds no flame to what Red Rock has just been told and he had NO idea at all. Red Rock: Ren…likes me? Sarah: Yes Red, she does she only came to the party because I told her that the guy she told off at the bus stop sounded a little bit like my new neighbour because all I hear is you complaining at D’avid. Red Rock: SO let’s get this clear. René Lodge likes me. Sarah: Yes. 10 minutes later Red Rock: So just to confirm this right, Ren likes me? Sarah: YES! And if you ask again I’ll punch you in the fucking balls! Red Rock gulps and finally decides to stop asking. At this point he is feeling more joy than he has felt in the last 5 years of his declining life. But there is one question in Red Rock’s mind on the subject of the threesome, which he needs to get off his chest. Red Rock: err… just one question about the…you know… did you tell anyone? Just as the question has been put on the table the sudden bellowing voice of Charles Darling, Sarah’s father comes bursting through the kitchen door. Charles: YOU! YOU!. Red Rock veers his head at Charles in shock then back towards Sarah in utter bemusement. Red Rock: You told your dad? What the fuck?! Charles Darling approaches Red Rock pointing his finger at him like a gun in his face with an emotional and tearful tone about his voice. Charles: Don’t you ‘what the fuck’ me you oity toity little bastard! You and that ginger Dutch BASTARD defecated… my beautiful, my pride and joy… my gorgeous daughter. Charles looks at Sarah with a tear running down his cheek as Red Rock is frozen in his stance in fear of moving. Charles looks over at Red Rock now sporting a rather unhinged grimace. He then waves his finger again at Red Rock who swallows; keeping his eye on the renegade finger. Charles: I thought you were a good lad…a boy like you could of made CID but no… no not now… not after you and that ginger BASTARD took advantage of my daughter in the most sordid sex act! Red Rock: I’m sorry I didn’t know what I was doing… I was drunk! Charles now aims his aggressive finger towards his front door; dismissing Red Rock from his property. Charles: Get out of my sight. Red Rock opens his mouth to defend himself, but in that moment he realises he should just stand down, Charles is obviously not emotionally stable and to challenge him may not be in Red Rock’s best interest. Red Rock slowly but cautiously backs away as Charles sinks in to his sofa; burying his head in his hands and silently weeping. Red Rocks 180 degrees towards the door and all of a sudden his face drops. Ren: A threesome? Red Rock: REN?! Ren stands in the doorway of the Darling’s living room baring a feeling of huge disappointment. With all the shouting and aggressive pointing Ren, who had come to visit her friend had gone unnoticed standing silently in the door way. She heard everything, everything about the threesome with her best friend. Red Rock could feel her disappointment because over the last few months it is a feeling that he has becoming accustomed to. Ren’s bottom lip quiver but at the same time she tries to build a brave front. Ren: How could you take advantage of her? Sarah: I’m sorry Ren! It wasn’t my fault! Red Rock: Hey shut up! You’re just as much to blame as me! Ren: Don’t blame her! You’re just a sick pervert; I thought you were a nice guy! A little but mental but a nice guy! Ren’s brave front drops as tears gather in her eyes just as she begins to shield them from Red Rock with her hand. Red Rock is now filled with a different kind of disappointment as it has now become very clear. Red Rock’s greatest fear over the last few weeks has been ruining his chances with Ren and now it is something that he is going to have to face up to. All it took was one night of careless drinking to spoil anything and despite the fact it was Ren who instigated Red Rock’s drinking that night he is in no position to question the kind of feelings Ren must be feeling now. Red Rock: I’m…I’m sorry… Ren turns her back to Red Rock with her arm across her stomach in a defensive gesture. A single tear escapes her tear duct and rolls down her check; spoiling her make up and it is at this point Red Rock leaves with his head hung in shame and a terrible feeling of guilt eating away in the pit of his stomach. Red Rock exits the Darling’s flat after looking over his shoulder one last time to see Sarah comforting Ren. An hour or so later Red Rock now finds himself sat down on his uncomfortable sofa with a grey cloud hanging over his head and feeling sorry for him self. He is taking comfort in watching his most haunted series two box set, which he also bought with his bonus from winning the relentless title. He watches as Derek Acorah tries to comprehend the evil spirits in the London detention centre whilst think about how Derek is feeling lost and confused because he is feeling something he has never felt before. At the moment Red Rock relates to what he is watching, sure he has had one or two girlfriends but nothing that lasted any longer than a month. He feels confused because Ren isn’t even his girlfriend but he is feeling all these strange feelings of guilt knowing that he has upset her, on top of that he is feeling a huge loss. It is like finding something that you have been seeking for years and when you finally find it you drop it and it shatters in to thousands of pieces rendering it irreparable. His trail of thought is bluntly cut when a thudding become audible in his flat. Instantly Red Rock jumps out from his seat. After watching a box set of most haunted, he has become slightly more on edge when it comes to strange thuds. Red Rock: What the fuck was that? Red Rock surveys the area surrounding him looking for a natural explanation. There is another thud but this time there is a groan. Red Rock’s eyes spring open and his stomach does a back flip, especially because the sounds seem to be coming from his own bedroom. The hairs on the back of Red Rock’s neck stand on end and he begins to feel cold with fear. He is living in an old converted Victorian building, many people have lived and worked in this building it could easily be a ghost! Red Rock: Argh bugger what the fuck is that!? Another loud crash followed by a rhythmic banging on the wall is heard and the groans become more and more prominent. Red Rock reluctantly approaches his bedroom door with his guard up. The banging gets faster and louder and the groans become more violent. Red Rock: Who’s there? Red Rock’s voice becomes nervous and quivery but he still approaches his door. He places his hand on the handle and slowly turns it. He pushes the door open slowly whilst wincing slightly and peers through… Red Rock: D’AVID? Oh for GOD sake, don’t tell me you’re masturbating in my room? D’avid is leant back against Red Rock’s wall on his bed, hunch double with shaft in hand all without Red Rock’s knowledge. Red Rock cringes with disgust as D’avid’s ginger pale body is in its full ginger pale glory. D’avid releases his grip on his penis and lets it flop on the bed. D’avid: heh… ok I won’t tell you! Red Rock: Why do you always do these things in my room? In the corner of Red Rock’s eye he notices something on his TV that D’avid was beating off over. Red Rock: Hold on what is that? I recognise that scenery. Red Rock walks over to his TV and presses play and what he sees sickens him. Red Rock: D’avid you didn’t! D’avid: Heh… Red Rock: YOU FILMED IT! You BASTARD! You filmed the threesome! AWWW! That’s sick! Red Rock, although he is disgusted he is also curious so he doesn’t slam the stop button immediately and allowed the tape to roll. Red Rock: Hold on…If that’s you giving Sarah a pounding, where am I? Red Rock watches on and watches D’avid bending Sarah over whilst humping her like a horny Jack Russell. But Red Rock still can’t seem to find himself anywhere in the shot. D’avid: err… Red Rock: Did I do the filming or something? You know considering I was drunk I still had a pretty steady hand, it’s almost as if I was completely still! D’avid: errm… Red Rock is about to his the stop button when all of a sudden he sees himself on the video walking through the bedroom door. Hey you guys are having SEX! Bitchin’ wooooooo! And after Red Rock’s drunken slur on the video he passes out on to his bed as D’avid and Sarah continue their rampant sex session. Red Rock: OH MY GOD! D’avid watches Red Rock’s face and the fear of God runs cold through his veins. All this time D’avid knew that there was no threesome because he has been wanking to the video of that faithful night for the last two weeks. Red Rock: D’avid, you KNEW! You are fucking a CUNT! I just fucked things up with Ren, because she thinks I’m some kind of sex pervert like you! Ren is still over Sarah’s house all pissed off at me because of you! So we’re going to march over there now and you are going to TELL THE TRUTH! D’avid: But then Sarah will hate me! Red Rock: Fuck that D’avid you only like Sarah because you get off on sweaty minges but I actually REALLY like Ren and from what I understand she likes me too so if there is any chance of us getting together we are going to go over there with this tape and show them the truth! D’avid: fine… Red Rock: And for God sake, put some clothes on! D’avid shuffles across Red Rock’s bed and puts his clothes back on whilst Red Rock stands watching him like an angry parent. D’avid finally gets dressed and the time comes to once again save his chances with this infatuating young woman… …Red Rock and D’avid now find themselves outside the Darling’s flat; Red Rock has the video evidence in his hand whilst D’avid is looking very sorry for himself. Red Rock rings the door bell to the tune of ‘The Bill’ once again and Charles Darling answers the door. Red Rock: Hel – Charles: YOU AGAIN? Haven’t you done enough damage? Taking advantage of my daughter and nearly coming between two best friends! You have some nerve! AND YOU! You sick ginger bastard I ought to give you a good hiding too! D’avid: Heh! Really? OooOOooOh! Red Rock: Not now D’avid! Mr Darling I have here in my hand conclusive evidence that I had no part in any threesome! Charles: Oh yeah? What kind of evidence? Red Rock: Video evidence! Charles scours at Red Rock but stands aside to let him in. Red Rock walks in to the living room where Sarah and Ren are sat on the couch. Ren looks at Red Rock and tires to hide her panda eyes from the tears. She is nursing a glass of lemonade, her favourite drink whilst watching Bridget Jones’ diary; a DVD for any woman who just got screwed over by a man. Red Rock: Ren! I didn’t do it! There was no threesome and this video will prove it! Red Rock shoves the video in the tape player but before pressing play he gathers Charles and Sarah around the TV. The setting is almost like that point in Scooby Doo when they unmask the villain. Red Rock: This will prove to you Mr Darling and more importantly to you Ren that I’m not a weird sex pest…LIKE SOME OF US! D’avid: Heh… Red Rock punches the play button and the images of D’avid with Sarah bent over plays. Ren shields her eyes in disgust and curls up on the sofa. Ren: AWWW RED! THAT’S SICK! Sarah: YOU FILMED US? Everyone in the room is disgusted and in shock. Mr Darling stares at the TV with his head tilted to one side; he is a very protective man over his daughter and for any father seeing this type of image would have a devastating impact but Mr Darling simply stares through the TV. His face turns pale and he almost looks like a dying man then he turns around and exits the room without saying a word. Then a face of pride and relief replaces the face of anguish and sorrow up on Red Rock’s face Red Rock: See Ren! I’m not the pervert you think I am and I know you like me and I feel the same way about you! I was thinking that we could maybe go out? Ren: No…no, Red I won’t… And just like that Red Rock’s pride and relief is squashed. Ren: You just showed me a video tape of D’avid banging my best friend and you expect me to run in to your arms? Why don’t you go get fucked Red! Red Rock: But… But I didn’t do anything wrong. Ren: I did like you but you’re just a weirdo, just leave me alone! Red Rock: But…AH DAMN IT! Red Rock turns around and storms out of the room with a cloud of defeat following him. No matter what Red Rock does always ends up in disaster, anti heroes don’t get the girl that easy. It is a curse to fail and everything seems to be an uphill struggle, you can’t just put in a video tape of you’re best friend boning the girls best friend and expect to get the girl. Things don’t always work out the way the do in films, real life is more complicated than that and this is another harsh lesson in this subject Red Rock is learning. With Boolie his old friend leaving on the plane to New York shattered his expectations and once again Red Rock’s childish expectations have been ruined. A man of 20 has a lot of growing up to do only he will learn it the hardest way.
