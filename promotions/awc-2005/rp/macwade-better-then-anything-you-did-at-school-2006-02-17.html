Turning his back on him and tossing him off a 50 foot cage, beating him and his brother for the Alliance titles. He thought about these things, and then he looked at that marquee: FIRST BLOOD. Then there was an incident Tuesday Night. A ruckus occurred after Freshburst at the hotel they were staying in. The ruckus was obviously caused by Paddy’s insane jealousy towards Mike Wade and his history making career in AWC so far. First Blood is now out the window, and as Mike sits today in his house thinking about this feud ending rubber match he sees: Main Event: Paddy O Shea vs Mike Wade- Loser Leaves Town The prospect of Mikes career being on the line had started taking it’s toll. Mike sat, stuck fast in his chair, his head cocked in astonishment and enjoyment with what he was seeing on the screen. As the immortal words and riffs of Joe Esposito played in the background, Mike watched as Daniel LaRusso, his ever present trainer Mr Miyagi by his side gets into crane position and kicks the evil Johnny Lawrence right under the chin and the place goes wild! “That’s it!” Mike leaped from the chair, himself emulating a crane. He had found a secret weapon, something Paddy would never see coming. There he stood in his living room, a grown man of 26 years, emulating the Karate Kid. His arms were raised in typical fashion and his right leg bent upwards towards his body leaving his standing leg be his left. Mike was after all left footed.
