Without You.

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd January 2006 By now Red Rock is slipping further and further towards that place where no one really wants to go, it is dark probably cold and very depressing...it's name is rock bottom. Red Rock is being dragged in the red with his spiraling debt and rent arrears, he is running out of people to ask for help and is running out of options fast. His best friend in the world Boolie finally reached the point where he could take no more just at the end of Red Rock's run in GTT5 over an argument about a hot meal and left to return to his family home in New York. This fatal blow was probably going to finish Red Rock once and for all. Now he didn't have anyone to split the bills with, he is defiantly fucked. So it's no surprise that Red Rock is just around the corner from a mental break down. But for now he isn't quite there in fact at this moment in time we find Red Rock in a park named Osbourne park in a town named Farnborough, which is a 10 minute bus ride from Red Rock's home town of Aldershot. Red Rock is currently on one of his night time strolls. A night time stroll something that he often goes on just to collect his thoughts and clear his head, he usually ponders about what to do next or how to avoid the debt collector. In the day it is a place where children come to play football, and their little child games. This park is somewhere Red Rock used to come in his early teens to play a bit of football with his friends himself but now it seems to have become a criminal hot spot. Since Red Rock left it has seen scenes of relentless muggings, unprovoked beatings and even savage rape. Darkness fell over Osbourne park quite a few hours ago now so the temperature has slipped ever so slightly bellow freezing. Red Rock is perched on a slimy broken wooden bench with a thin layer of glittering frost covering it. Perhaps Red Rock should of worn some more suitable shoes than his thin blue converse all stars. He wraps his arms around himself in an attempt to keep in the warmth but despite his thick green winter jacket and large woolly scarf he still finds him self shivering under the icy grip of this Monday evening. Red Rock: It's fucking cold out here... Red Rock brings his knees up in to his chest as well as pulling his fluffy hood over his head in another move to collect some warmth. With Red Rock's restricted vision from the large hood he surveys the area he used to hang around and observes the corrugated iron fencing, which surrounds the park. After the time after Red Rock's teen years ended it seems that a number of kids have made their mark in spray paint over the fencing. Osbourne park is now a very different place to how he remembers it, it now resembles that of a ghetto. As Red Rock fixes his gaze on the trashed up play area it makes him reflect on his own life. Things are changing for Red Rock and he doesn't know what will happen next. In retrospect Red Rock is nothing more than a confused little boy who was released in to the real world too early for him to cope. He is an emotional young man who lets his personal concerns block the tasks in life that you need to do to get by and for this reason he is forcing himself in to a fit of depression. He feels trapped as if he can't go anywhere, the feeling he gets in his stomach is a sickening feeling that drags him down to the ground with a gray cloud over his head. Someone needs to help this boy. Red Rock: Suppose I better go home now... Red Rock realizes the time as he tilts his wrist to look at his watch and decides it would be best if he made his way home. The time now is 1 am so there isn't many people out now there is a cold silence that lingers over the whole park. The only sounds that Red Rock can hear is the wind wisping through the leaves. He slowly paces up a cracked old concrete path with his hands in his pocket as well as his head facing the the ground. He kicks lumps of moss which grow from the cracks as he makes his way to the gates, replaying the last time he saw Boolie in his head over and over again. It was the day before his final GTT5 match against Core's William Gwynn. It was a race against time to try and stop Boolie from getting on that plane, almost like something out of some crap romance movie. That faithful day, 10th January 2005 In the distance the sound of a grumbling loud car quickly fades in to the open space where people are exiting cars. This is the front gate of Heathrow airport a place where thousands of people use to fly every day, and like every other day the place is heaving. There are rows of cars and taxis dropping holiday makers with their over packed suitcases, and people making business trips swinging around their travel bags as their irked expressions bring down the mood of the people with in a 3 meter radius. But going back to that strange loud car sound it seems to be becoming more and more dominant to the point where it is drowning out the sound of the convocation between the crowd. Then out of no where a blue Peugeot 205 comes darting in to the pictures with its engine roaring and exhaust dragging along the ground. In the driver seat is Red Rock's mad Dutch neighbor, D'avid and next to him in the passenger seat is a frantic looking Red Rock. Red Rock: RUN D'AVID! D'avid: But my exhaust has fallen off and I'm illegally parked! Red Rock: NO TIME! Red Rock throws the passenger door open with great force in his action. The sound of metal against a child's face follows, which shocks the crowd of people in the form of a collective gasp. The angry mother stands with her jaw dropped to the floor in, which only worsens as she watches Red Rock and D'avid sprinting for the lives without any concern for the child's bloody nose. Red Rock and D'avid disappear through the set of automatic doors in to the main area of the airport. Red Rock flaps about like a decapitated bird desperately reading signs which may direct him to his desired location. Red Rock: WHERE IS TERMINAL 3! D'avid: STOP SHOUTING! Red Rock: Right! Sorry, we only have 10 minutes to stop Boolie flying out of our lives forever! D'avid: If we don't make it, will you come live with me? D'avid throws a rather suggestive grimace in Red Rock's direction to which Red Rock retorts with a look of disgust with an equally disgusted tone of voice about Red Rock. Red Rock: Oh... GOD no! Anyway! Stop talking and run! I think we have to go this way! Red Rock and D'avid continue to run through the airport towards where they think terminal 3 is. It is now only a matter of minutes before Boolie is gone from Red Rock forever. The pair dart and weave through crowds of people. Red Rock's face appears to be becoming more and more strained the closer they get to the boarding area. Red Rock: Look over there! That's where Boolie must have boarded! Red Rock how ever doesn't realize that D'avid isn't running next to him. Red Rock stops in his tracks to investigate as to why so he turns around 180 degrees to see D'avid standing still staring at a public information sign. Red Rock approaches D'avid with a storming pace and an irritated face plastered on him. Red Rock: D'avid! Why have you stopped running? D'avid: This sign says stop running. Red Rock: Oh fucking ignore the sign it doesn't mean anything! Red Rock grabs D'avid by the arm but just as he does another man holding something roses and a present speeds past the two. Obviously it would seem that he too is also trying to stop someone from boarding a flight. This man has an emotional aura about him and a huge sense of urgency. Red Rock: OH NO! D'avid: What? Red Rock: That guy! D'avid: What about him? Red Rock: He's running towards terminal 3 with roses and a present! D'avid: ehhhh...so? Red Rock slaps himself across the forehead in frustration, although it is not entirely obvious what Red Rock is going on about he still expects D'avid to know what he is thinking. Red Rock takes an erratic stance with his legs widely spread whilst pointing in the direction of the man. Red Rock: If he gets to the boarding area before me they won't let me on the plane to stop Boolie from leaving, they'll let him on first! They can't let TWO desperate people who are trying to foil someones plans escape from a shit life in the same flight! D'avid: HE MUST BE DESTORY'ED! Red Rock and D'avid lock their sights on the strange mustached romantic man, he has already gained a safe lead over Red Rock and D'avid. It was now time for Red Rock to put his wrestling training and stamina in to action! Red Rock with D'avid by his side begin to burn rubber after the Romantic. Red Rock can still make out the distant figure of the man through the ocean of people so Red Rock recklessly shoves and barges his way through an elderly tour group. The sounds of bones popping out of sockets can be faintly heard in the back ground as Red Rock and D'avid leave their frail bodies to wither to the floor like sacks of jelly. Red Rock and his ginger side kick begin to close the gap on the Romantic as they manage to negotiate their way through the thicker part of the crowd. The boarding gates are now visible in the distance as Red Rock catches up with the Romantic. Red Rock: STOP RIGHT THERE! You mustached bastard! If there is going to be some dramatic loser delaying this flight it's going to be me! Red Rock sports a competitive grimace as he slaps his own chest as he says the word “me”. The Romantic raises his right eyebrow at Red Rock giving him an unsure expression. He can't quite make out if Red Rock is serious of some punk who is taking the mick out of him. Romantic: Huh? What you talk about mate? Red Rock: I have a good friend on that plane and he I can't let him go! Romantic: Look pal! The woman I love is getting on that plane, I need to tell her I love her before it's too late! Red Rock: Love this, you rasclart! The Romantic's face creases up, forming a line between his eyebrows and its not long before Red Rock slaps the back of his around the Romantic's face. This makes the Romantic loose his balance and he veers towards a group of Japanese students. In one swift moment the Romantic collides with the students creating an explosion of expensive digital cameras and roses. Red Rock looks back over his shoulder as D'avid jogs by adding insult to injury by kicking the Romantic in the stomach to slow him down further. D'avid: Heh! Take that you bastard you are shit! HA, AAAHH! The Romantic is left further and further in to the distance, but you can still make out the tears rolling down his face as he realizes this crazy long haired but rather fast young man and strange ginger Dutchman has ruined his final chance to win back his one true love. Red Rock: UNLUCKY DICK HEAD! The boarding gates are now in front of Red Rock, with a few minutes to spare before it is too late. There is still a short line of people preparing to board the flight, but it is clear that Boolie isn't in the line...a 7'7 man isn't very difficult to miss! Red Rock: NO! He's not in the line, this complicates things... D'avid: They won't let you on the plane you know... Red Rock: I've seen enough movies that say other wise D'avid! Red Rock makes the final dash towards the line of people, he pushes his way past the holiday makers and businessmen receiving a hell of a lot of abuse from passengers on his way. He fumbles past the last few who throw half eaten bagels and other items of food, to which he responds with some stuttering nervous apologies. Red Rock: Sorry! I'm trying to stop my friend from leaving on that plane... Red Rock by now is immensely out of breath. He reaches the desk at the boarding gates where there is an airport attendant standing checking the tickets and boarding passes. Red Rock throws himself over the table as he struggles to breathe and franticly gasps for air. His face has turned red and his heart is pumping 10 times faster than it should be pumping but nether-the-less he manages to muster the strength to stand face to face with the airport attendant to expel an wheezy request. Red Rock: You have to let me on the plane, there is someone very important I can't let fly away on that plane. Red Rock looks sincerely in to the eyes of the tall brown haired attendant. She tilts her head with a sympathetic smile across her face, which raises Red Rock's hopes. Attendant: Oh Honey that's so sweet. Red Rock: So I can go? Attendant: Of course you can't sweetie. Red Rock: Why the fuck not? The Attendant sighs before patting Red Rock on the head leaving Red Rock with the feeling that he is being patronized, which backed up by the Attendants typical attendant condescending tone of voice. Attendant: Huh? This isn't a movie we can't just let you on the plane without a ticket or a boarding pass, you could be a terrorist! D'avid: Does he look Asian to you? Red Rock: D'avid, for fucks sake...shut up! Please Miss you you don't understand! Attendant: We get a lot of people like you every day, we simply can't just let people on. Red Rock: I fucking hate reality...can't you please just make an exception? Red Rock clutches the attendants blue uniform blazer to plead to her kind will. Red Rock: Please! Let me on! Man sitting on the floor: Oh for God sake let the bloke stop his boy friend from flying already! Red Rock: Yeah let me stop my - hold on. HEY! Red Rock turns around to clear up this persons misconception by waving his index finger in the direction of the man. Red Rock: He's not my boyfriend! Boolie: RED?! Red Rock: BOOLIE?! Red Rock hadn't notices Boolie sat down on this floor at the front of the line, this would explain why he couldn't see Boolie from a distance in the line, he was sat down! Red Rock's stomach does a back flip as it comes clear that it's not too late to try and talk him round. Red Rock throws his arms around his good friends neck like a child would to his father who has just returned from a long trip. Red Rock: Oh Boolie! I'm sorry we had a fight, I'm sorry I let things get this bad! Boolie: I'm sorry we had a fight too... Red Rock: Please don't get on that plane! D'avid: You know...heh...this is getting a bit sexual. Red Rock: D'avid, fuck off. Red Rock slaps D'avid across the face, who gets some kind of sexual thrill out of it. Red Rock: Boolie you're my best friend I can't do this with out you. Boolie sighs with a few pounds of anguish in his tone. This is something that has plagued Boolie's thoughts since Red Rock joined AWC. Boolie stayed loyal to Red Rock through it all, but it was the letter and tickets Boolie received at Christmas from his parents begging him to come home that finally swayed his decision to leave Red Rock and leave the wrestling business. Boolie: Red, I'll never forget all the times we had. But the game is over for me, I can't handle this anymore I just want a quiet life now with my family...I'm sorry. Red Rock's eyes begin to swell and become bloodshot as the lump in his throat begins to choke him from holding back the tears. Red Rock: Please...don't go. I'll do anything to make things better, I'll train harder! I'll win some championships I promise! This time in 3 months I'll be in the main event we'll be living in a posh house in London and have a hot meal every day just please don't go! Boolie bites his quivering bottom lip as he too begins to fight back his own tears. Red Rock and Boolie's friendship is a unique friendship in a way where two people only need each other and no one else to get by. Material possessions didn't matter, Red Rock and Boolie never wanted big cars and a big house, they were to be added bonuses to a comfortable carefree bachelor life style they always dreamed of since they started training together all those years ago. All they wanted to do was have a lot of fun...but the fun never kicked off. D'avid: If you love him...let him go. Red Rock: I though I told you fuck off! D'avid: Heh... Boolie: Sorry Red, but its just not turned out the way we talked about I can't go another day wondering where the next meal is going to come from. This just isn't how I want to spend my life. Red Rock releases his hold around Boolie's neck just after the reality of Boolie's decision engulfs his thoughts. Small streams of tears now roll down both mens cheeks. Red Rock sniffs and wipes his eyes on his sleeves before taking a few steps back to allow Boolie to get to his feet. Red Rock: Fair enough Boolie... You're right you don't deserve to live like this, you're a really good person. But I will tell you this I will become the Wrestler I said I would be and I will do it for you I will make something of myself in AWC and you're always welcome to join me again once I've achieved that. Boolie smiles and simply nods back at Red Rock. The two men share one more tear felt hug before Boolie lifts up his hand luggage. Red Rock stands aside and waves him away as he disappears through the boarding gates and out of Red Rock's life. Back to now Red Rock has now walked a large proportion of the way home, he walks down a long strectch of road calls queens road. The time is now 3am and he has been out walking for 3 hours and is now deathly cold. The only thing distracting him from the bitter freeze is the desperate thoughts of the promises he made to Boolie on that day. Perhaps he did seem a little bit over dramatic at the boarding gates, but what he said he meant. He wants to better his life he just needs to make the chances. Fate has dealt Red Rock a cruel hand. We now watch as Red Rock wonders off in to the night slouched over with his hands in his pockets.
