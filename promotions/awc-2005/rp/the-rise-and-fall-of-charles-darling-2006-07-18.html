The rise and fall of Charles Darling

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th July 2006 The modern man these days wants nothing more than the high life they want to climb that ladder to fame and collect that title “celebrity”. With television like X-Factor and Big Brother it only encourages these oblivious dreams of fame to the point where the very word celebrity has been rendered redundant. People will do almost anything to reach this status, some would publicly humiliate themselves on nationally television, leaving themselves open for a 50 year old guy with a high waist band to rip flesh from bone and other will risk mass public hatred for the rest of their lives just to be on the cover of heat magazine. There was once a time when the word celebrity meant something, when it was a status to be proud of with people like Marilyn Monroe or Frank Sinatra, there was once a time where the modern man wanted nothing more than to build a home with a wife and children. It was seen as a thing to be proud of to be able to sit down around the table in the morning with your children before work and sit down in the evening for dinner. Theses days its fast food fast cars and fast lives and there was a time when respect out weighed fear in society. Red Rock’s neighbour Mr Charles Darling was once a man of respect, honour and a pillar of society like he aimed to be. He wore his police uniform with his moustache trimmed to perfection and his hair swept to the side with his head held up high. He was so proud when he finally made Chief Superintendent in the summer of 1986 and it wasn’t long after he was made proud once more with the arrival of his daughter Sarah later that year. He then had a lot of responsibility with his new job and his new baby girl he wanted nothing more than to build a future for his family. Four years later in to the job he was flying high the respect from his peers was building up like grease on a spotty teenagers face. There was a time where he couldn’t walk down the hall at the station with out people shaking his hand and congratulating him on his latest successes. His daughter Sarah was growing up, she was getting a bit fatter but she was running around growing curiouser and asking more questions like a child of that age would. Then one day he was assigned a case involving one of the most notorious gangsters in the east end of London his name was Paul Day. It was the case that would suck the air from an officer’s lungs and the colour from their faces, but not Charles Darling he was going to take this head on. But upon accepting this case he didn’t take the time to realise that this vicious gangster was the same man who drove his predecessor from the job… but why? He organized a meeting of his officers straight away with piles of enthusiasm about him. This was his chance to make his name in the police game world wide and he was wasting little time. Maybe this was the beginning of his down fall, in his haste he missed out various little facts and the biggest one being he was now a target not just him but the one thing he was most proud of… his family. The clever thing though about this gangster is he didn’t just kidnap or beat people up, no he was more calculating than that he was a very intelligent educated man. He was well spoken, very smart and a smooth character. This doesn’t mean to say he didn’t kidnap or beat people up, he would just use those key ingredients to manipulate people to his advantage rather than simply scaring them. This is why he was so successful and Mr Charles Darling was going to find this out soon enough. Charles had used every lead and every contact he had and he was finally gathering enough evidence and enough ground to finally make an arrest on Paul Day. He led himself to believe that he had him that he had Paul Day running scared so he crept in to the wood work waiting for the day to come he was being very quite, perhaps too quiet? September 15th 1989 arrived where he was to come home to his old Tudor manor house only to find his wife standing in the front door trembling erratically with tears streaming down her and a face white as a ghost. Charles ran up to his wife instantly thinking the worst, call it intuition but he knew it was involved Paul Day and his daughter. His worst nightmare had come true Paul Day had taken his daughter and made the most venomous threats if he didn’t get what he wanted. He was not to call the police because this meant instant death to Sarah his now 3 year old daughter. The date was set for the arrest of Paul Day and it was this information that Paul Day needed, he wanted to spring his own counter attack on the police that plague his dealings. This was the cruellest of Ultimatums but Charles loved his family more than anything so with a heavy heart he did Paul Day’s bidding. The day came the under cover policemen arrived Paul Day was expecting and 3 officers were killed that day. From this day on Charles Darling slipped in to depression blaming him self for those deaths. Thinking it was all over now and his daughter was safe he began neglecting this case and that is when people started to ask questions. This was the man who was the front of this case he was pressing his officers in to over drive with over powering aggression towards putting this man away but now he had become overwhelmed by fear. It was only a matter of time before he was removed from the case all together much to his secret relief. He was so happy it was all over and he could get on with his work but he would never be the same he would always have that failure lingering around him and those 3 young officers haunting him in his sleep. Stilling thinking it was all over he tried to go about his daily business until a sports case appeared at the station. In side was hundreds of thousands of pounds with a note to Charles Darling from Paul Day thanking him for his tip-off. It was one final kick in the teeth from Paul Day he had set it up to look like Charles Darling had turned corrupt and sold his officers out. Charles Darling former Chief superintendent would walk out of that court room as tears rolled relentlessly down his face with the flash from the paparazzi camera beam off them. He could only look over his shoulder at his broken down wife and screaming daughter before being placed in to the police van not to see the light of day for 15 years. February 1st 2006 When you come to a point in your life when you’re feeling down nothing beats the warm arm of a good friend. In this case poor old Charles at this point could do no better than a pint of cider for a friend. With his hair thinning at a simultaneous rate as turning grey he sits with arm propping his half conscious body with alcohol running through his veins. There are now only a handful of people at the bar in the King’s Tavern’s illegal lock in; Charles, a rather shifty looking chap in a pair of smart jeans and a stylish shirt looking suspiciously sober and of course the barman. The sounds of music and drunken shouting pound through Charles’ confused and vulnerable mind as he pushes him self up from the bar before conjuring a bassy belch. He wipes his face with his right hand, hoping some of his vision would return but to no avail. He feels a hand on his shoulder, which brings him back to the stained fabric of his bar stool. Dev: Oi mate, can’t ‘elp but notice you been ‘aving some money troubles. The heavy over the top cockney accent of a man named Dev Jackson breathes in to Charles’ incoherent ears. Charles being a forming Chief super knew who this man was, he is one of the dodgiest dealers in illegal Pirate DVD’s CD’s and other various bootlegged items in the area so Charles is not interested. Charles attempts to shrug Dev off but Dev persists. Dev: Don’t get up so quick son shine, cos I happen to know that you ain’t got no where to live and you ain’t got no work. Charles: Piss off you git! I don’t care what you got to say you’re scum Dev Jackson. It’s almost as if Dev Jackson just hangs out in places like this just to take advantage of those who reach rock bottom. He is the devil tempting Adam with the apple like the snake that he is. Dev: You ain’t got a choice mate, you don’t want to go ‘ome to your lovely family and disappoint them? The thing with these people is they are persuasive and manipulative, despite the shame that Charles has endured he still wants nothing more than to protect his family and with the image of his 16 year old daughter in his head he descends and listens to what he has to say. Dev: I got a little place in a block of flats in Aldershot I just acquired I need it to store some gear. I need someone to stay in there and guard it. Charles stares blankly in to the eyes of this criminal and remembers there was once a time where he spat at scum like this and here he was putting serious though to becoming a pawn in their game. Dev: You want to live with your wife’s fuckin’ parents for the rest of you fuckin’ life or do you want to give your pretty wife some good fuckin’ news for once? Charles: I Don’t… Dev shoves a piece of paper with a number written on is in to Charles’ hand looking coldly in to his eyes and baring his teeth. Charles recoils slightly with intimidation across his face as he slips the paper in to his tweed jacket pocket. Dev: Think about it Darling you’ll be on my books you have the place rent free you just tell the wife you’re working for my internet business she’s not going to ask questions. Tell her you’re working for an old mate. You ain’t got nothin’ to worry about you’re just looking after stuff for me. It’ll be chusty mate, trust! Charles’s bald spot turns red as it produces small patches of sweat, Charles swiftly dabs the moisture from his head as he reaches out for his long vinyl jacket with his hand trembling in fear. He barges and jostles through the crowds of pub patrons before the sound of music shouting and glasses clinking are replaced by the cold sounds of wind cutting through the air and traffic whooshing past. Charles is now by his self, alone with the proposal battling his righteous frame of mind. He thrusts his hand in to his pocket to withdraw the piece of paper with Dev’s number written on it. He walks as he stares at the paper before the skies open up on him. This would be the toughest decision Mr Charles Darling would ever have to make; obviously a family man like Charles would make the right choice for his wife and child. The Present date A tired man, rough around the edges now sits alone in his study surrounded by piles of cardboard boxes. These boxes are stacked around the small chip board desk all the way up to the ceiling and inside them are thousands of bootlegged DVDs, jeans, cigarettes anything that you’d expect to find at a dodgy London market. Charles sits with piles of paper in front of him with bold red writing across them, you don’t need to read the words to understand what they mean. Charles flips a pen back and forth through his fingers whilst desperately searching for a solution but it’s not long before his frustration is heightened by the noise his daughter talking loudly on the phone to her best friend Ren. Charles: SARAH, BLOODY SHUT UP! Sarah’s angsty response is projected down the hall way with a barrage of foul words but Charles is too tired to argue back. It seems as if Charles’ life is becoming more and more meaningless he has been reduced to baby sitting a criminal’s illegal merchandise behind his families back. He is a liar and a criminal everything he never wanted to be but surely he is doing the right thing, at least his family are happy and feel stable. Charles returns to his accounts as he slides his hands across a pile of paper; looking for the next bill to take care of when suddenly the vibrations of his mobile grumble on the surface of the table. He picks up phone in his hand and the display reads “Dev”. Charles rolls his eyes as he throws himself back in to his rickety plastic computer chair before answering. Charles: What now, Jackson? Dev: Awite mate, I got some more stuff for to look after! Charles shoots to his feet as he looks around the room for anywhere else he could possibly put another box. Charles: Dev, you said this was everything I haven’t got any more room! Dev: Well bloody well make room this is important! It’s… lets just say a new project so you’ll have to take extra care of it. Someone will be over around now with the package. Sorry for the short notice! Charles grits his teeth before banging his fist on the table causing the cheap chip board wood to bend. Charles: Listen here Jackson! Dev: No you listen Charles, I own that building you’re living in and I can have you out of it and I can tell your little wife and daughter what you’ve been doing! So I suggest you do as I say! Dev Jackson swiftly hangs up the phone just before the front door is rapped upon by an unknown stranger. Charles’ ears prick up as he slaps his phone on the desk whilst dashing for the door. Charles: I’ll get it! It’s for me its work! Charles speeds down the hall and straight for the door, grabbing the handle and throwing it open only to see Ren standing in front of him. Charles: Oh it’s only you… Ren: Hello Mr Darling, some strange looking man in a long black jacket gave me this to give to you? It’s pretty heavy! Charles opens his arms and accepts the unknown package it isn’t too large it’s a box about a 30 inches by 30 inches. Charles holds the box curiously before finding a note stuck to the bottom attached with a piece of sellotape. He throws the package under his arm, hoping his wife and daughter don’t see him. He returns to his study like a rat returning to its nest with a small morsel of food. He then unfolds the note and reads it in his head. Charles: Hello Charles, make sure you keep this safe it’s a new project that’ll make me a lot of money! Judging by the size and weight of this package it doesn’t take long for the former police chief superintendent to figure out what is inside this new box Charles: Drugs…DRUGS?! Charles instantly begins to panic, there is no way he could keep it here not only did he have any space for it but it was too dangerous. If he got caught with this it would mean the end for him once and for all. Meanwhile across the hall… Red Rock: I still can’t believe I fell for that… Who would have believed it? Ashton Kutcher pretending to live here for all that time, no wonder we never saw Howard! Ren had already darted upstairs to leave Red Rock and D’avid to follow shortly after locking the car up. They had just recently returned from their mammoth road trip after we last left our anti hero falling victim to cruel prank orchestrated by D’avid Ren and Punk’d’s Ashton Kutcher. Red Rock is sporting the very same pair of filthy jeans he has been wearing for the last 3 days but he is however sporting a new Punk’d T-shirt. D’avid: And I still can’t believe how quickly your mood changed once you found out you were going to get a free Punk’d T-Shirt! Red Rock faces down at his new Punk'd T-shirt to admire the fine stitching. Red Rock: I know! It’s not cheap either and they gave me this sexy hat! I nearly feel bad about caving Ashton Kutcher’s face in with a shovel! D’avid: Heh… well it was only a matter of time before he Punk’d a paranoid short tempered person such as your self! Red Rock: I am not bad tempered! D’avid: You have a worse temper than Gordon Ramsey in the kitchen of a little chef! Red Rock huffs at D’avid shaking his head in a disapproving manor; he then slots the key in to the door and enters. Red Rock inhales the stagnant yet warningly welcoming smell of home. He proceeds to kick his shoes off across the room where ever they will land before stumbling over to the space where the couch once was. Neglecting this vital fact Red Rock falls smack bam to the floor face first thus creating a loud thud as his skull meets the carpet. Red Rock: OUCH! I forgot we no longer own a sofa…aww gay! Red Rock rolls himself over to a seated position then D’avid joins him on the dirty space where the sofa once sat. Red Rock: D’avid… have you painted a pair of regular ear phones white with tip-ex in a poor attempt to make people think you own an I-Pod? D’avid: Heh…errr yes. Red Rock: D’avid, pretending to own an I-Pod is worse than actually owning one! Have you no shame? D’avid removes his fake I-Pod ear phones before taking a fake I-Pod made from a discarded cigarette packet. D’avid: Heh…David Beckham has an I-Pod… Red Rock: For shame! Just before Red Rock can kick in with his self righteous I-Pod rant an erratic knock at the door pounds through Red Rock’s head. Red Rock: Oh bollocks! It’s probably Mr Camfield the fucking shit face land lord! Red Rock nervously arises to his feet desperately trying to think of a way to talk him self out of any potential problems. Red Rock’s teeth grind back and forth in fear along with the sound of his front door receiving more reckless poundings. Red Rock: Oh fuck me we’re ruined! Red Rock decides at this point to bite the bullet and open the door. He slowly reaches out with a trembling hand and despite the fact his land lord just exceeds 5’0 he is a fiery tempered man and can be very scary indeed. So it is to Red Rock’s relief that upon opening the front door Charles Darling from across the hall stands. His relief is hastily replaced by curiosity as he can’t help but notice this box at his feet. Red Rock looks at Charles with slight bemusement about him. The last time he spoke to Mr Darling was the time he showed him a video of D’avid and his daughter Sarah having rampant sex in Red Rock’s bedroom. Red Rock: Muh…Meh…Mr Darling?! What do I owe this pleasure? Ahem…err. Charles: Don’t panic mate I’m not here to harm – Just before Charles can finish his sentence D’avid comes bursting out of nowhere with handfuls of women’s underwear! D’avid: HAVE THEM! HAVE THEM ALL I’M SO SORRY! D’avid hurls pairs of underwear at Charles who is now sharing Red Rock’s bemusement. D’avid: I DON’T KNOW WHY I DO IT I JUST FUCKING LOVE TO SNIFF YOUR WIFE’S… Charles picks an item of lingre off his face. Charles: MY WIFE’S?! Oh it doesn’t matter now… I need a favour from you son… Red Rock, now with a tense bead of sweat rolling off his fore head enquires. Red Rock: Oh err…sure… what do you need? Charles: I need you to look after this box for me! I don’t want you to open it under any circumstances, but I can’t keep it at home. Red Rock: Why not? Charles: It’s… private. Red Rock covers his cheeky smile whilst nudging and winking at Charles. Red Rock: Oh I get it…it’s a stonking great porn collection… okay we won’t look at your vast collection of smut. Charles: IT’S NOT SMUT! Blast you! It’s just private so just trust me and don’t open it! Please. Red Rock: Alright don’t get a hot spicy one! Just stick it over there in the corner. Red Rock points in the general direction of the corner by where his dining table is, which is at this point completely covered in crap such as old newspapers, junk mail and late bills. Charles shunts the box across the wood laminate flooring of the kitchen which joins to the dining area. Charles: Thanks…I’ll come back for it later! Red Rock: Why do you look so worried? Charles: WORRIED?! No I’m fine…must be my heart…damn heart attacks! Red Rock raises an eyebrow with a suspicious glance whilst Charles nervously tugs on his collar. Charles: Well…I’ll be going now! Charles waves and grins at Red Rock and D’avid whilst quickly backing up toward Red Rock’s front door. Charles escapes in to the hall way and instantly buries his face in his hands as he slumps against the wall, obviously feeling a tremendous amount of guilt. Charles: OH GOD what have I done? I’ve dropped him in it now, he’s only a lad a young lad he’s got his whole life in front of him! If he gets caught with that he’ll get done for possession with intent to supply and it’ll all be my fault! What have I become? I lied to my family and I’m a pawn to a criminal I might as well just end it all! Charles sighs and removes his hands from his face. Charles: ARGH! How much did you hear? Ren: Pretty much everything, mainly the bits about your dropping Red in shit… By now Ren has established her self as the type of person who would have a lot to say to someone who is causing anguish to another, so it’s no surprise Ren stares at Charles with a hand on her hip. Ren: Now Red may be a bit stupid and he may have NO social skills what so ever, but if I know one thing it is he doesn’t deserve to go to jail because of you! Now you go back in there and you get your damn box of drugs and you give it back to who ever gave it to you! Charles: You’re right what I am doing? I’ve become too obsessed with my own well being that I forgot how much I was hurting the people around me! Charles with tears swelling in his eyes turns around and pushes Red Rock’s door back open. Charles: Red… I’ve come for my – OH MY GOD! Charles’ skeleton nearly jump through his mouth when in front of him he sees D’avid with his face buried in the large box of cocaine with an insane dribbled giggles escaping him. Charles: YOU GINGER! At this point the sound of the toilet flushing becomes audible and is shortly followed by Red Rock exiting the bathroom. Red Rock: D’AVID WHAT ARE YOU DOING?! Charles: THE DRUGS! Red Rock: THE DRUGS?! D’avid: The druuuuugggs…. Heh! Charles is sweating with the word panic written all over his face, Red Rock is blinded with rage towards Charles and D’avid is rolled over on his back, wiping his nose. Charles: You have £100 worth of cocaine in your stupid fucking ginger beard! Red Rock: YOU LEFT A BOX OF COCAINE IN MY HOUSE! YOU CUNT! Charles holds his arms open towards Red Rock in a submissive gesture, hoping it may calm him down. Charles: Let me explain…sit down Red. Red Rock sighs and allows Charles to explain, he figures that a former pillar of society like Charles would turn to this there must be a good reason. Charles pulls up one of Red Rock’s dining room chairs and pulls it up in front of Ren, Red Rock and D’avid who is licking his top lip, feeling the rush of the cocaine. Charles: Basically since I got kicked out of the force things turn to shit, this bloke call Dev gave me a chance to have the flat over the hall in exchange for me looking after his illegal gear… It started off as DVD, CD’s the usual crap… but now he’s moved in to the drug dealing business. Red Rock: Dev Jackson? I heard of him! Charles: He owns the whole building… its almost as if he preys on people who hit rock bottom, I’m not the only one… the white Rastafarian couple on the top floor are in the same boat… he’s a bully Red he’s a manipulator and there is nothing I can do. You must understand that my wife and Sarah must never know about this. Then Charles’ mobile phone unexpectedly beings to ring and to Charles’ horror the name on the display is Dev Jackson. And with a sick feeling in his stomach he holds the phone to the ear then after 3 seconds the convocation is over and a look of brutal horror is etched upon Charles’ pale face. Red Rock: What? What was that? Why are you pale? Charles: Dev just got a call from his boss… he’s coming back for the cocaine. D’avid: NO! IT’S MINE! Red Rock: So this means… Ren: You guys are fucked! I’m going home! Red Rock: Thanks Ren… see you if I don’t get my legs broken because of CHARLES! Charles: Don’t worry! We can blag it! I got an idea! Ren rolls her eyes as she walks out of Red Rock’s front door. Charles then gathers together a sober Red Rock and a drugged up D’avid. Charles: We just top it back up with flour and hope he doesn’t notice! Red Rock winces, creating a look of disgust at Charles poor effort of a plan before delivering a sarcastic applause. Charles: Well he’s going to be here in 3 minutes so we don’t have a choice! Now go get some flour! Red Rock sighs as he steps over D’avid who is rolling around on the floor dribbling. He makes his way to the kitchen cupboard and is lucky to find a bag of flour. Charles then snatches the bag and scurries over to the open box. Charles: This is wheat flour…its brown! Red Rock: Well it’s all I got so take it or leave it! Charles: ARGH! Fine! Charles realises that he hasn’t any time left as he pours the brown flour in with the white powder. He makes a vane attempt to mix it up but it appears he has only made things worse for himself. Red Rock looks down at the ruined cocaine and shakes his head. Red Rock: Know if he wasn’t going to kick your arse before he will now! No one is going to want to buy cocaine mixed with wheat flour! Charles: I’M RUINED! Then a sound of a scary familiar nut case cockney can be heard from the hall way. Red Rock: Oooh sounds like he’s here! Red Rock picks up the box and shoves it in Charles’ shaky arms whilst ushering him out of his flat. Red Rock: Good luck! And with one last slap across the back Red Rock shows Charles the door. Red Rock curiously stands by the door and listens in to the convocation from the safety of his own home. He can make out many words because it sounds like the two men are whispering so he can only make out the enraged parts of the convocation from Dev Jackson. Dev: FLOUR?! YOU IDIOT! … Dev: YOU GOT 1 HOUR TO GET ME THE MONEY OR YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD! … Dev: OKAY THEN! WELL HE HAS 1 HOUR TO GET THE MONEY OR HE’S DEAD! THUMP The sound of fist connecting with face is heard followed by the sound of head hitting floor followed by the sound of foot steps walking away. Red Rock wastes little time and opens his door to discover the outcome of this convocation. He looks to the floor to see Charles with a bloodied nose. Red Rock looks down on Charles with a smug expression on his face. Red Rock: YOU shouldn’t of got involved with drugs? Charles manages to pull himself up to his feet, pinching the bridge of his nose to try and stop the bleeding. Charles: Yeah I’m not scared of him! Red Rock: Why not? He said he gave you an hour to live… Charles: Well…I kind of… Red Rock: NO YOU DIDN’T! Charles: I’m sorry Red I panicked I thought he was going to kill me! I had to blame you! Red Rock’s face drops and falls a thousand shades paler so it would seem that Red Rock has been dragged in to Charles’ spiralling decline in his life. A man who was once a man of hour has cowardly dropped Red Rock’s life in danger. How low will a man sink just to protect his own family? TBC…
