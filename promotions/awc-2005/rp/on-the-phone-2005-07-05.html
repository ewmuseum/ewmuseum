On the phone

5 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th July I awoke next to my luchious girlfriend as my chrome Sony alarm set off and I sat up to turn the Madison avenue sound I programmed myself off. It reminds me of New York and my childhood of going up and down there on my skateboard before I got these growth spurts. I turned to look at Tanya and watched how beautiful she looked while she was sleeping. Then after I spent a while looking at the radiant beauty I clambered from underneath my empire state building bed sheets and walked out of my baby blue painted bedroom. I stepped out onto the landing and flinched as I turned the light on, as my eyes had not yet adjusted to the light. I walked out further and turned onto the stairs, I fell down the first one as the colored spots appeared in my eyes and took up most of my vision. But I persevered down the stairs and entered the kitchen and flipped the switch for the light, although I still flinched as my eyes had still not fully adjusted. I Switched on the coffee pot, just as the phone rang. I ran into the sitting room and picked up the phone, I looked at the caller ID saw The Educator was ringing me. We haven’t always seen eye to eye and barley knew each other. But I still answered. Jonny Sake: Erm….hello? The Educator:Is this the number of a certain Mr.Jonny Sake? It’s The Educator. Jonny Sake: I gathered. The Educator: What do you have caller ID or something? Jonny Sake: Obviously, The Educator: (Shamefully) I hang around with Kris too much don’t I? I hate the pisspot, But anyway, You know you have a match with Kris don’t you? Jonny Sake:I do now. The Educator: Well I want you to knock some sense into him, so he can spell more than Wombat Juice. I think it is rubbing off on me. Jonny Sake: Erm ok…..but he is my mentor……but he is dumb…….erm…….ok then. The Educator put the phone down suspiciously after I said ok, and sent me a fax shortly after I finally got that coffee. It read….. Dear Mr. Sake, I would like you to accept the proposal I have, put on offer and beat the crap out of Kris, He was keeping me down, or how would you say it? Holding me back. So if you beat him into a coma maybe, I would break free from his dumb, ignoramus and stalking like clutches and let me reach the potential full he kept me from reaching. I am not sorry that I hung up, because I am not too fond of you, but I had to go and teach my class. Yours Sincerely, The Educator
