Be Aimz and Amy, just not like that. No, not now. Tomorrow. Don't do THAT! Don't you wrestlers know this?

19 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: before the weird changed deadline Don't touch me. Never touch me, and I'll pretend like you never have. There's some kind of sick joke in everything that happens to me. Sometimes, I have one of those huge emotional breakdowns where everything is promblematic and oh so horribly distressing. I guess when I'm NOT having one of those breakdowns, you'd call me happy. It's kind of a relative term, where the concept of sadness is completely relative to how long you've been happy. Needless to say, I don't ever stay happy for very long. By now, most people know what my deal is - I'm completely incapable of what's seen as normal human interaction, and instead choose to put an emphasis on the little things. You know, passing glances and 'almost' touches. It gets me into a lot of trouble with men, and most women write me off completely for it. In fact, if Darcy was going to hurt me most in this match, it WOULD be with Ellis Nash. Well, it could've been Karina Wolfenden... but that was long over. He knows better than anyone that I don't get along with women at all, unless they're acting like guys. Chicks tend to be cruel, tactical and malicious bitches. Like me, but not me... so, by default, they suck. Back to those massive emotional guttings, though. I'm currently having one... in a way. Am I pissed? Yeah. Hurt? Sure. Betrayed? Yup. Hell bent on showing Darcy exactly how he needs to stop messing with my head, and why? Eh, I'm on the fence about that one. I'll probably spend the majority of my time pounding on Ellis Nash for even daring to get into the middle of his deliberate and overly dramatic lowblow to our relationship. And that 'relationship' word, that's frightening. Most people don't see things the way I do, and they think I'm completely fucked up because of it. It's not exactly how I live my life that seems to bother them, it's the way I deal with people. I can drink and do whatever the hell I want, but it seems to be that as soon as I speak a word to another human being, it's dramatic. I don't and won't change how I speak to people, and I can't help their god damned reactions to it. Where does someone get off calling me too dramatic when I'm living my life, and I'm happy with my end of it? People don't seem to see that playing it safe or constantly bitching about other people being dramatic tends to just mean that they haven't realized what living at your fullest is. I don't believe in holding back. How do you experience everything you can without craning your neck towards the world? Either way, I'm not in East Bay this week. I took a little layover in a tiny town called Boston, and it looks like I won't be seeing Canada again this week. I'm staying with a tattoo artist friend of mine who runs a shop just off of the financial district. I generally love Boston, especially the pubs, and while the shop's open and my friend's tattooing, I've got no plans of sitting around watching him ink people all day. People recognize me, they ask the usual 'I like that tattoo, what's it mean?' bullshit. When I looked up from sketching in the parlor lobby, some kid was staring my way. ''Hey. Uh, how much would this be?'' he extended a piece of flash from the wall into my face, ''About, umm, this big... on my hip?'' ''Sorry cat, I don't work here.'' ''You sure?'' I was trying to be nice, but that's just ignorant. ''Yeah, very sure. The receptionist just went out for a smoke break, and Jay's tattooing - but in a minute someone'll be around to help you.'' He stuck his neck out, ''Whatcha' drawin'?'' ''It's, um...'' I looked down for a second to the pad of paper in my lap, and realized exactly what it was I'd been sketching out. A joint Darcy Crisis and Aimz logo, along with a lame ass test sketch of a fist for no apparent reason. ''DC... A?'' the kid squirmed a little, he probably thinks I work for a skate shoe company... that little Volcom tattoo on his wrist pretty much speaks it, ''What do you do, if you don't work here?'' He seemed to be putting two and two together, but I can't help fucking with people's heads. I looked him dead in the eye, ''I kill people, then draw cartoons in my spare time.'' ''Are those contacts?'' he pointed at my eyes, ''The blue one, is it a...'' ''JASON! COME UP FRONT BEFORE I OFF ANOTHER CUSTOMER!'' The kid backed away and stated that he'd come back when someone could help him. Between the buzz of the tattoo gun, I could hear Jason chuckling out back and beginning a story with 'That's Ames, she...', which meant I'd end up having to engage in small talk with the customer after their tattoo as a sort of revenge for running one out. Maybe this wasn't such a good idea. Time to hit up one of those pubs... HOURS LATER... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Three pints and an odd reunion. The day kept getting funnier. ''One question, Amy,'' Mikey asked, scratching his head, ''Does this Murphy do dropkicks?'' ''They kinda... uhh... you actually live here and haven't ever heard of the Dropkick Murphys?'' she raised an eyebrow with the question, ''You've never heard 'Skinhead On The MBTA', 'Barroom Hero'...'' O'Reilly's gaze still looked blank. Apparently he hadn't heard of the Boston locals, a world famous Irish punk band. ''...Seriously, Mike. You're Irish. You're living in Boston... and you don't know anything about the Dropkick Murphys? Punk rock band, emphasis on rock? Boston heroes? Union boys? They do miracles for the dockworkers.'' Mikey searched his mind. He'd lived in Boston for six years but none of them seemed familiar. ''No,'' he said. ''Can't say that I have. But I have heard of Biohazard, Hatebreed, and other hardcore bands. You know, hardcore? the underground equivalent to punk rock?'' ''I know we're trying to be drinkin' buddies now that we've run into one another here, and I'm still all emo and pissed about my boyfriend - but don't start with me about hardcore. Comeback Kid? Terror? Gorilla Biscuits? Throwdown? Black Flag? I could go on forever. Seriously, let's not go there right now... but yeah, you know of some alright bands,'' grinned Amy, ''I'm talking about DKM, though. You HAVE to see them! We'll go, have some drinks, see the Murphys and hang out with 'em later in the night. I've known Kid for years, and the asshole owes me a hundred bucks anyway.'' She ended up having a great night with O'Reilly, teaching him Dropkick Murphys songs and then singing along to the live show. They got happy, got drunk and she got him autographs from all of the band members. It was a memorable evening, and something that caused them both to forget that they hated one another professionally. That's the way it should have been with Darcy, in her eyes. It always had been. Maybe O'Reilly was just too drunk to notice, but he seemed to have the right idea when it came to living a life outside of the dramatic and dagger-driving world of wrestling. There was only one way to get even farther from that universe. Home. BACKUP VOCALS----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Having a few beer with Mikey O'Reilly really brought back memories of how good it felt just to knock the shit outta someone and not have to go into an emo spiel about it. I liked that freedom, and even the loss of control that was necessary to achieve it. It reminded me again why I like the male mindset a lot more than I convince myself of - I beat the snot out of him a few days ago, and then we were laughing over a pitcher of Guiness and wailing along to a Dropkick Murphys rendition of 'Wild Rover'. It brought me back to how Darcy used to be, how he should've stayed. It was on my mind the entire flight home. Back to the empty apartment. I'm not even subletting this shithouse yet, I haven't bothered moving everything to East Bay. I've still got that pathetic excuse for a weight bench, a 20 inch TV and some boxes of winter clothes I have no use for in California. Usually when I've got a weight in my hand, I sit around thinking about how hard I can punch and how impressive it is. It's funny, because throughout my life I've had nightmares where I have this one golden opportunity to take out someone I truly hate - whoever it is at the time - and I go to throw this knockout punch with all of my might, but end up having it be the softest blow in recorded history. It barely even grazes the hairs of their face. I seem to be littered with delusions of lost opportunities and weak finishes. Most of the time, the dreams are about small girls - like the ones I loathed in high school. Here comes another one of those insights that eventually relates back to Darcy. I can feel it creeping up my spine, into the back of my neck and ultimately latching onto my psyche just to remind me that I still have to deal with him at some point this week. Anyway... despite being entirely integrated into the 'popular' crowd, I couldn't stand girly girls or anyone who took part in the bullshit drama that we ladies seem to bathe in. I've never heard a conversation at a table of men that starts with 'Did you SEE her sandals? We've so gotta get them to break up...', and that's usually what draws me to them much more frequently than girls. I can be instantly confrontational, which is my nature, with a guy and not have it be an issue where he's obligated to hate me and make fun of my hair for the next ten years. I love men because I hate women, and I guess in my own special way, I wanna show them how they should be treated. Sadly, I usually end up inadvertantly treating guys like crap - especially my male friends, who I keep in the 'friend zone' and tease mercilessly until they end up chasing my tail and getting punched, or getting laid and dumped two weeks later when I come around and realize the reason I didn't want a relationship with them in the first place. I'm a mess, when it comes down to it. Half the time, I can't remember exactly how old I am and the other half of the time, I'm so sure of myself that every mistake I'm speeding towards seems like a fleck on the windshield. It's a weird way to live, always throwing myself at things completely; It tends to get me hurt more often than not. But without pain, there can be no joy. How fucking lame did that sound? It's true, though. To know unbridled joy means you have to have had enough pain to contrast and compare the emotions. I'll never know what absolute bliss is, and neither will any jerkoff who claims to know it already or be on some dumbass spiritual quest to discover it. I don't give a damn what religion, color or belief system you're from - you'll never quite know the ultimate level of any feeling, because the concept of emotion is almost entirely limitless. It's man-made, like time... just without any linear form of measurement. I mean, there's love, hate, like, dislike... but where the fuck is the measuring tape that tells me exactly what emotional trigger indicates that I'm in any of those states? Where's the 12 o'clock of hatred? What about love? The idea of measuring such things with no need for comparison is complete bullshit, no matter what God or Goddess or king or queen or president is telling you so. Simply put, there isn't metric for love. There's no formula for hate, unless we can count 'Aimz + boyfriend / other girl = H.A.T.E' in the textbooks... and there's certainly no certainty to happiness. I'm only even thinking this way because of the discomfort these censoring 'that's too dramatic' assholes throw my way, I guess. Ellis Nash thinks living to the fullest requires too much drama. It's back to that contrast and comparison argument - how can you find peace without first experiencing chaos? Obviously, you can't CREATE peace without understanding drama or chaos... so why should I throw attempts at contentment the same empty punches I toss in my nightmares? There's a cute phrase for my relationship. This is a crazed state of mind, and I know that because I know myself and my past. Drugs used to take me to a place in my head where I felt content and sure of the things around me, even if I was only sure that everything was insane and constantly changing. They had a desired effect. They easily forced my head one way or the other. I took two Percoset about 45 minutes ago, and they're just now making me get a little nappy. The desired effect in this case wasn't to numb any physical pain, but to help slow my head down so I could sleep without waking up feeling hungover with thoughts of my boyfriend tossing thoughts of another girl - a wrestler, at that - around in his head while he sleeps alone. Nash would NEVER let Darcy act on any feelings, and I doubt entirely that feelings even exist with him in her case... but it's that uncertainty about everything in life that always kills me. I wish there was a switch to flick that meant we'd be together forever - then at least one thing was sure. The only things I can currently count on are that I'll eventually die, and getting struck hurts. Getting struck down emotionally is probably the worst. Heh, there goes another cute, gothic little phrase. I'm not sure why I have such an obsession with jealousy. I can't honestly say it's just because a lot of guys hurt me. I have it in for women - we're bitches, plain and simple. We'll plot to take everything from someone else if it suits us. We usually could give a damn less about someone who can't give us something. I know these things so surely, simply because I know myself. I've got ovaries, and I can't say that I've never done any of those things - or sat in huge groups of girls who plot to do those things. Part of it is some societal need - this huge 'girl power' movement that makes us feel as a gender that we have something to prove to the world, and before we can top the guys we have to get to the top of our own food chain. Thus, the constant cat-fighting and backstabbing that's so common with women in the world and workplace. I need to be better than you if you have tits, and you need to be better than me. Otherwise, we're just 'there'; There's nothing significant about either of us if one can't stake a claim over the head of the other. Anyone who wouldn't know or agree with my thoughts on the subject obviously never set foot outside of their house, or heard their mom on the phone mentioning how she saw so and so's sister's mother-in-law at the grocery store wearing... gasp... track pants! Yeah, moms do that. Even the kindest women are still women, and they're prone to being bitches. Man, I should be cutting my wrists - not lifting weights and watching infomercials! Whoa... here comes one of those insecure moments. It's kind of like gas cramps, but a little more stomach-churning... I seem to be trying hard to convince myself that I know who I am, despite my mental diatribe about everything being uncertain without something solid to compare it to. I don't know what I'd compare myself to, because I don't have a normal sense of good and evil or anything in between. The only thing I really think I know right now is that I was pretty damned good and important to Darcy, and I didn't even expect something in return - I just wanted peace between us for once. We hate each other, and absolutely love it. We've spent half a decade pissing one another off and completely getting off on it. It's a little unhealthy, but the way we get along is so unique that it can't be called anything short of complete, albeit mismatched, love. I keep closing my eyes and trying to sleep, but my heart skips a beat and my head turns to panic the second his face shows up behind my eyelids. I don't know why I'm so worried about any of this. There are these little moments where I'm calm and things make sense, where I think that all of it's just for ratings and it'll be done once I step out of the ring. Problem is, a lot's been said, and even more will be done on the mat. It's going to complicate matters probably beyond repair - whatever that is for he and I. I have no idea how to get on the same page, or planet, with Jack Murphy, and the thought of Darcy speaking to Nash more than me to map their strategy puts a knot in my stomach, even though I'm the one blocking his calls. When it comes down to it and you really look over things, I tend to make myself suffer more than anyone else. Maybe that's why people like Ellis treat me as if I'm some kind of moron. Everyone else sees the things coming that I can't seem to glance at in enough time to avoid. I like it that way, though. Without pain, there is no joy... and I'll repeat that until I believe it. Sleeplessness and painkillers are making me flip-flop almost beyond repair. I keep lifting these stupid weights up and down on this Wal-Mart bench just because it's one of the only things left in my apartment that I didn't already ship to East Bay. It's freezing outside, and the heat in here is shitty this time of year... plus, even the tiniest lifting makes me feel a little more alive inside. The whole concept of lifting weights makes someone feel powerful, whether they're lifting two pounds or two hundred. I max out at about 100 for a single press, which isn't too bad... for a girl. Though I'm sure there's some chick in PTC who'll say she can bench twice that while baking a cake, taking a shower and eating a protein bar. People take these little shots at me in hopes that it'll get my attention. Honestly, it does... and it bugs me a little, usually just before bed. I guess they like that, it gives them some kind of satisfaction to be the types who act like they're above or cooler than me when really, they're nothing better or special at all. Normal people playing abnormal roles when life isn't throwing them anything... that's retarded, to me. I get weird shit tossed at me on a daily basis, which is pretty much just my luck, and usually end up having an excuse to half-ass things... but I don't. I get mad, I make sense and I don't fall too far into an abstract concept of cool or 'in'. Fuck pot smokers and college kids, living outside of the cliche of what you're 'supposed' to be doing at a certain age or in a certain situation is the only way to experience as much as you can. If someone's got some feigned shot or sarcastic observation, but they're doing it to achieve a certain image, it goes in one ear and out the other on my end. People like to fuck with anyone they can in pursuit of glory and greatness. It's the shits, but it's also the way it is. While mental monologues might not be cool, currently in or anything different from the norm, my thoughts - as opposed to some diary with flowers on the cover - provide a minor solace and sense of certainty. They're mine. I'm sick of this stoner shit, and I'm really sick of this Percoset's attempt to numb me up. Opium-based painkillers... what was I thinking? My lack of a cohesive approach to anything really gets under my skin, and apparently bugs more people than I thought. Then again, anyone who complains is most likely caught up in their own ridiculous attempts to prod and mock everything that they see as possibly superior or, at the very least, more put-together than what they can achieve at the time. This is Darcy Markson's entire approach to life. He's creeping in on me way too much. I'm starting to get tired, just staring at the TV in hopes of something coming on that might make me feel more productive. Nothing happens, because I disconnected all but three-channel basic cable when I decided to start moving out. I look at my cell phone, and nothing happens. No new calls. He must have given up. He should have given up. After all, he'd made so many attempts and nothing happened. I'll sleep, then I'll call him. I'll never be the one to touch first, ever... but if I touch because you reached out, it makes a difference in my pride. Ignoring his extended hand makes me feel needed, like I could care less whether I lived with or without him when really, I'd probably be eating myself alive moreso than I already am if he left my life again. But I'll sleep, then I'll call him. I'll reach out just far enough to touch his fingers, and he'll know I'm still here to repair once we're completely broken. HOW TO DEAL------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ In between shows, you always get this sense of either accomplishment or complete disdain for yourself. I love the 'it's just business' excuse that most people use to treat others like shit in the world of wrestling. Someone can break your neck and say that it was just business, payback from the boss for that stiff bump you gave his golden boy the week before. Whoops. Here's a match with your boyfriend, because you didn't have enough problems already. 'Don't worry, baby, it's just business!'. Thinking of the match is a sour spot, but it's all I've been left to do this week. I'm not near him by my own choices. I'm not near a ring. I'm far, far away... but it won't leave me. All I can think about are the mistakes that everyone in this thing are about to make. Darcy's got the right idea, but Nash doesn't. She'll get in there and try to beat me by mocking my style. She'll parody it and try to make me look bad. It's good for a cheap laugh, but the only way to TRULY defeat someone isn't to mimic them, but to be brave enough to bring your own real style up to the table and put it full-tilt against their's. In a world of politics and bullshit, too many people forget that simple rule of a fight. Always be yourself, and mockery does not an individual make. Nobody will ever really get a leg up making fun of someone else - they need to best that person in their own way, not the easiest way. Any chimp with half a brain can be controversial. They can be weird, they can make no sense, they can be 'different' and detached in the same way people like Ellis Nash try so hard to be. Sure, that'll sell a few t-shirts with some ridiculous little catchphrase on the chest, but it's not going to make them memorable as anything more than someone who once got into an argument over the talent or methods of someone else. I haven't had to feel that insignificance since I came to PTC. Even when I was nearly raped in a ring by a goth jackass and saved by a skateboarding freak with a monkey, I had more dignity than the people who spend too much time trying to think of a good burn on me and too little time finding out how to be better than me. If I don't have a clue about life, they're FAR beyond a bad situation. I don't think any publicity stunt could pull me back to the sickening low that those people still live. Don't worry, baby... it's just backstabbing. But if it's on-camera, that means you have another dimension of bullshit within which you can get away from being accountable for your actions. I took a flight home from Boston to escape the 'HEY, AIMZ!' shit. I don't want to be all business when I'm trying to live my life. I wanna be able to be Amy Campbell without having to wonder how the booking of next week's show will affect that. I want to be able to trust who I'm with, without worrying about some trick coming up on him and proving her superiority by beating me in some halfway pre-determined match. I don't want to worry about being paid to take the fall for someone who can't rightfully earn their own push. ''Truthfully,'' I grumbled to Jeph, my best friend of many years now, ''I'm not gonna be able to look at him after this.'' I'd voiced the majority of my thoughts to him. Well, to his voicemail during my flight home... but the sense of how detached from the wrestling business he was kept me hanging around with him. Jeph Howe, that is. I never had to worry about him betraying my trust once I got a title belt or a good push. Of course, I also never worried about him understanding a damned thing I said. ''Why?'' ''You can't have a night where you're only trying to hurt a certain person, then smile and have a beer with them.'' ''You hung out with that Irishman in Boston, though.'' ''That was different,'' I growled, ''He was already too drunk to really know who I was.'' ''Maybe Darcy's drunk with love!'' ''... This is useless, isn't it?'' ''Look, I don't pretend to know much about wrestling... but shouldn't 'true love' and all that shit prevail over what happens in some fake ass ring?'' Part of him could have been right, but it absolutely wasn't. It was partially my fault that Darcy had brought wrestling so deep between us - I was the first to strike back when he let his feelings get in the way of my matches, after all. But that was rightfully done... now things were just out of hand and at the mercy of some kind of vicious cycle of supposedly playful revenge. I couldn't pretend to understand it. Two cities. Two countries. Two cultures, four days. I could've gone to see the Pope himself, and it still wouldn't teach me how to deal with this sick contrast. I can't live entirely on camera, but I can't escape that person when I'm off camera. Not even a gallon of vodka could teach someone how to deal with this kind of thing. And trust me, love won't help you one bit in this situation.
