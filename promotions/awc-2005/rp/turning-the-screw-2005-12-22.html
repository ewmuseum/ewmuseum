Turning the Screw

22 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/12/05 ‘Would you just get out of my way!’ I roared the statement randomly around me, flailing arms in the general direction of whoever, not even checking to see. Not that my sight would help. Cascading waves of blurriness washed over my vision, tossing everything from side to side and distorting them all in an interesting and psychedelic way. Oh how very appropriate. It was Amsterdam after all. The colours all melded in my brain, dirty shades of brown and grey, black and blue, all contriving to create a collage of sludge. Every now and then a pink would arrive to brighten the landscape, heralding some person’s head, arm, leg or torso coming in close vicinity to me. This would soon be followed by the body part moving swiftly out of view. I couldn’t blame them. I’m walking down the banks of Amsterdam having left the hotel and my head is awash with mixed emotions, twisting and turning through my brain, leaving nothing settled and for sure. It was strange and something I hadn’t experienced in a long time. I generally just don’t get drunk, it’s not in my mind frame to do something like that. But tonight… ‘Fuck off!’ A brutish (or what looked like a brute, how the fuck do I know? I’m drunk! It could’ve been a dog!) male shouted at me and carelessly I barrelled into him, elbowing him in the head unintentionally but at the same time with purpose. I feigned sorrow at the man who was now lying on the ground in a heap and cursing loudly but with a flick of my hand I left, stumbling off. Anyway, where was I? Oh yeah, tonight. So many things happened and it just doesn’t make any sense to me at all… First of all Jessica, having taken care of me earlier in the week at my lowest point (I was not going back there) had been ignoring me for the entirety of the evening it seemed. She was too busy powdering her makeup, or eyeing up the other women and telling me how good they looked. She looked fucking fantastic though and the thoughts… But I didn’t get a chance to even contemplate what the fuck to do about that when Sasha came over. And she looked damn good as well. And Jessica had wandered off, so who was I to pass up the opportunity because when I’m with Sasha there’s something…tense about the situation. Yeah that’s what I’d call it, tension. No matter where we are, there’s always something on the edge, something tight and nervy and I really enjoy those moments. Sitting and sharing a drink it seemed like she was melting a little to me, becoming a bit more open and free to talk but…I didn’t get the chance to do anything because of that stupid spin the bottle. ‘Sir, would you please stop dancing in the middle of the road it’s very dangerous.’ ‘Piss off you blaggard!’ I swung wildly and I connected with something. Bringing my hand close to my face it turned out that there was blood on it, who’s I didn’t fucking care. This was venting time. I looked around and it seemed people were watching me, eyeing me up from top to bottom and it was only now I realised I was speaking quite loudly, exasperating my thoughts to the whole wide world. ‘Well fuck yas! I can say whatever the bloody hell that I want!’ With a twist of my body I spun on my heels and managed to hit the cobbled street hard on my face. Ow. I slowly pushed myself off the pavement and steadied myself on the cobbles, thinking as to where I should head now. Neon lights lit the path up ahead. There really was only one choice. So anyway, this bloody spin the bottle. I’m sitting there and wondering could this get any worse, the two women that I think I could potentially sleep with at some point and the other woman who won’t shut the fuck up about honour and retribution and all this other stupid crap that she hasn’t got even a concept of. Honour? Talk to me in twenty years when all your friends are either dead or at your back, waiting to twist the knife and leave you lying. Wrestling is a business, not a time for bonding. I don’t know why I joined in, maybe it’s the sick and twisted side of me that wants me to crash and burn, to die in front of everybody and see my embarrassment. Spin the bottle… Spin the fucking bottle… Of course I got Sasha. Couldn’t tell you why I ran off after, the spanner that I am. Don’t know how I’m going to salvage that one. And then talking to Jessica….she’ll get over it I expect. It’s not like I was pissed off at her…well, not directly anyway. Two women and not any way of deciding which one to journey after. I really hadn’t got a clue. Bright blue and green lights hummed right in front of my face and as the blurriness faded slightly I could make out two letters. XX. What the hell is XX? Where’s the door and I’ll check this shit out. The inside of the building (wherever the hell I was) was a lot darker. There was some sort of pounding music going on in the background, only serving to highlight the headache that was throbbing through me and crashing from side to side in my brain. Despite the possibility of it being a rave party I knew that wouldn’t be happening right now. For a start there were poles in the room…and seats. ‘Hey, you need something to relax you?’ ‘All I need is a bloody clue as to where the fuck I am.’ ‘It’s ok baby, just chill here and I’ll let you understand.’ Someone took my hand and I acquiesced, letting them usher me to some seat and then their body brushing against mine. It was distinctly oily and slick. Long hair rushed past my face and then the definite feeling of breasts coming into view. Well, least it wasn’t a gay bar. The rest of it was a muddle of neon clothing falling by the wayside as the girl did some sort of dance to the music. To say that I was following it would be one blatant lie. I was slightly aroused…but not because of her. I was too locked on the thought of Volkyeva in that dress. Mmmhmmm….but then I got Jessica too and that only worsened the problem. I audibly groaned as the two melded together. ‘You like that boy? Mmm, seems you’ve got the tools anyway.’ She carelessly grabbed my erection, feigning interest. I couldn’t give a shit what she thought, I knew how I marked up in the general estimation of people. I pushed her away and stood, heading towards the door. Well I think it was the door. In the background I could hear her catcalls for money but I wasn’t listening anymore. The cool air finally blew into my face as I reached outside again and I headed for the street. I could hear people calling from either side of the street, some offering untold pleasures of the flesh for a low-low price, others espousing interesting more kinky alternatives, and the first drink was free. Fuck that, I needed to sober up even if I was still horny. Jessica or Sasha? Jessica or Sasha? Jessica or Sasha? Alright stop thinking about them then brain, just forget about them, just place your mind for the task ahead this week, after all Jessica had landed you in a match at Winter Warfare…can’t even remember the name of it now but it was a final end to this stupid debate. A one-sided debate too. Women cannot wrestle. And I will prove it. So how had she won the last time huh? Oh don’t give me that crap, that was smoke and mirrors, she didn’t use her talent to try and stop me, she used a bloody big screen to distract me. I have no problem with beating the crap out of her one last time, putting this whole matter to bed. I could so use a bed right now… The walls of the street were closing in now and the width of the street was narrowed to only allow one person head either way. But the remarkable thing about it was the numerous windows all the way along. With lots and lots of semi-naked women. But what about last week? How come you lost to Lavelle? Maybe you’re starting to lose it… Shut up! I’m just saying… Shut up! ‘Can I help you babe?’ ‘Well what does it look like I want?’ I pointed down and I could see her lick her lips. She had the sweetest ass and a pretty smile. That’s all I needed. ‘It’s twenty for the first half hour and then we can talk from there ok?’ ‘Sure…’ I heard the door click behind me. Fuck me I haven’t had sex in so long…
