Date: 12/12/05 FUCK IT FUCK IT FUCK IT FUCK IT FUCK IT FUCK IT Mike Carman looked down at the sheet of paper, which had been poorly ripped down the center. As seen above, the note was a fairly easy read; all you had to know was two words. As he studied it with a cup of coffee, never did he realize that this was in fact written by him. Mike placed his hands over is face, as a pain began to bounce around in his head. Dazed and defiantly confused, he eyeballed the cup of coffee as thin strings of stream slowly faded as they rose from the scolding surface. ”Fuck…it,” He proposed to himself, pushing the cup away from sight before he lost himself within the deep silence and thoughts of having only a cup of coffee to communicate with. And if they made any sense at all, Mike would congratulate you with a big hug. Boredom. Silence. Hunger-MARIJUANA!? Mike shot up from the table and dashed across the kitchen towards the ‘bread box’. The wooden device sat in its corner as Mike glanced at it with a pair of bloodshot eyes. He placed his hand on the knob and lifted, watching as the sliding door revealed a brown paper bag. His heavy breathing would soon lead to his demise, his one chance to put the unbearable down buzz aside and bring happiness forth. ”Don’t you EVEN!” Mike shut the contraption and turned his attention to Kyle, who watched on with a glare in his eyes. ”Want to smoke,” Mike added, acting like the suave gangster he was. ”Uh, why not,” Kyle responded with a fat juicy J already rolled and ready to spark. The scene fades to a grayish screen of smoke as the room begins to fill with the goodness that god blessed earth with.
