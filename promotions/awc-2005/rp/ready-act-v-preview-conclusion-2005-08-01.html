-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- R/E/A/D/Yearnings -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The desires of life are nothing but trivial in the grand scheme of things. We want everything we can possibly desire to make our lives easier, or to give into our wantonness. But of course some things are rather useless. It’s up to you whether you want these things, or need these things… Success, love, happiness, pleasure, money… Must you be a star? Must you be the best? No… you can’t be THE best… not when the Masonna’s closing in. Kawaii sat on the train on her way home flipping through a fashion magazine carefree. You could look at her and tell without a doubt where her desires lay. She was tanned, blonde, face caked in makeup in Kogal or Ganguro street fashion. She even wore a little fake yellow flower in her hair because of her school uniform. As she turned page after page, her desire echoed through her head… “I want this… and those sandals… and that color lipstick… and OOOH!! I want that skirt! And I NEED that sundress! And…” She just goes on with her wish list as the scene continues onto a new scene, and a new person. Back at the high school, Ishikawa was walking into the locker room coated with a thin layer of sweat. Her skin glistened under the fluorescent lights, standing there in short black shorts and a white tank top leaning against the door. She reached down and started to peel off her cleats, eyes lowered to the ground with fatigue and thought. “I need to do better. I can’t be the best if I don’t push myself. Get with the program, Jun!” The inner torment of a girl’s own pressuring… it’s what motivates her, as well as looms over her, throwing her into her soccer playing. She wiped her brow before padding across the tiling to change… and the scene moves on. Jinnai, the male of the group… it was obvious why any one boy would make friends with three girls. Odds in his mind stated that one out of three girls would put out… so let’s get three of them, and eventually it’ll happen! “Arrgh!” Jinnai sat there on that park bench in his uniform leaning back, watching the waves of girls pass by on their way home from school. Babe watching was one of his top after school activities, though he highly doubted that he’d get any points for it on his college entrance application. As a rather nubile pair of girls in short school skirts walked by, his eyes scanned over each and every inch of them in the whole minute it took for them to pass. Inside, his mind screamed for release. “I-wanna-FUCK!! Arrrgh!” The school bag sitting across his lap started to slant at a downward angle towards the ground. The scene changes before the youth’s crotch explodes, this time entering the home of the final member of the quartet of friends, Kawada. She sat at her desk with the lights out except for the desk lamp itself, stacks of books on both sides of her workspace. Underneath the lamp itself a clutter of printed papers were spread out, handwriting jotted between the line spaces, or even over the print itself. Kawada was definitely diligent in her studies; that could be seen by far. She leaned back with a heavy exhale, tugging at the collar of her blue T-shirt to ventilate the rest of her body. Her eyes stayed on the papers below, however, and her lips were moving slowly reciting the information. The sound itself was very low, and in her mind the words she said were loud and clear. But amidst the stream of information another voice urged her on. “I have to raise my grades. I don’t know what I’ll do if I don’t get into college… I must keep studying.” Now another scene flashes into vision, a sunny day on a bridged walkway above a street. It was just one of many ways the students walked to and from school, but definitely one of the more trafficked paths. Walking across the bridge was the pompous Saotome, and clinging to his limb for dear life, the cute Maru. A trendy girl with bracelets and accessories to accent her school uniform, she seemed happy alongside her love, though Saotome himself seemed to care less if she existed at all. He just stared ahead on the path he was on, the girl with her eyes closed and a sleepy smile on her face. “I only need him…” the thought ran through her head backed by complete faith and without true understanding. But in such a fragile young state, they only wanted what they thought they needed… but some things are bad for you completely. Saotome glanced down at the girl, and faintly a smile cracked through his lips. “I don’t need her… but I took her. Now she’s mines…” They passed by a short young man leaning against the railing of the bridge, and they left the scene as the focus turned to the young boy. That young man with scruffy auburn color hair and a black sling pack across his chest. He seemed to have his eyes set on something below, a lovesick look upon his face. Below, the tough called Nagi leaned against a wall, this time in the alternate attire. Her street clothes were dismissed for a white dress shirt with the top two buttons undone, and a gray skirt hiked up some past her knees with dark blue knee socks and a pair of white, gray, and red sneaker flats. She seemed to be asleep at her post, head tilted back with her eyes closed. Girls would walk by on their toes not to awake the sleeping beast, whispering and giving short looks on their way by. A few boys started to pass by slowly as well, their stares lasted longer however. Nagi’s left eye flipped open as they moved past her, and one of them stuttered in his step. The two boys started to walk on ahead, quickly, the third boy with a look confused by the other two’s actions. Nagi’s eye closed, her arms crossing over her chest as she remained docile. The boy on the bridge smiled after watching that scene. “You protected me from those bullies, and now I’m forever in your debt. If it takes until the ends of time, I’ll make you mines… Kirima Nagi.” “Oi, Chi-bi-chan!!” The boy turned around to see a younger girl with reddish brown hair in pigtails over her shoulders smile at him. “You gonna stand there and daydream before the day begins?” “I thought I told you,” scratching the back of his head with a bothered look, “not to call me Chibi-chan. I hate that name.” She gave a playful though apologetic look before walking up to him and patting him on the shoulder. As she neared him, it could be seen that they were the same height, if not the girl an inch taller. “Aw, its ok, Hyo. You won’t be small all your life.” “Gee, thanks,” he gave a weak smile before walking with her to class. Below, Nagi had her own thoughts going through her mind, though they weren’t as trivial as the others. “When will this end… why won’t it stop? All I want… is the end… of this madness…” Sighing heavily, she slipped her foot into the noose of her bag, kicking it up in front of her before snatching it out of the air with her left hand. With eyes still closed she brushed a bit of hair back behind her ear, her piercing gaze focusing ahead before heading to class herself. Inside the high school, time seemed to move in a circle. As this tale of events progressed from one point, it now comes back to end. That breezeway… windowed on both sides… and just as in the beginning, Tachibana stood before the darkened portion of the hallway. She seemed to be mustering the nerve to step into the light, the sounds of students coming to school in the background. Footsteps were heard in the distance, most cluttered, but one pair of heavier steps began to grow closer. With a nod, she started to walk forward, this time not as slowly, but a gradual pace that brought her into the center of the hallway at the same moment someone else did. They stopped ahead of each other a few steps, Tachibana standing there with her eyes cast at the floor. The person before her was wearing a black suit and tie, his own hair slicked back down the nape of his neck. He was a relatively young looking teacher, and seemed to act in same fashion, briefcase slung over his shoulder with a cigarette tucked behind his ear. He looked down at her, expressionless, as the girl did the same. Finally she looked up and smiled politely at him. “Good morning, sensei.” His blank look broke into a wide, carefree smile. “Morning, Tachibana!” They both nodded, and after standing there for a few lingering seconds they both moved in different directions. But the paths their thoughts were moving in seemed to be nearly identical. ”I only want him to love me… nothing more…” Tachibana’s heart yearned for him. The smile stayed on her face the whole way to class. Her teacher, however, didn’t seem to keep his smile for as long. It faded away, and a pondering look swept over him. “What is this… love… or lust?” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- R/E/A/D/Y -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Life has its twists and turns, and ups and downs. And most of them were right under our noses, hidden by those around us. Each and every one of us wants to be the star of the show, have our own personal tale to tell… and in fact at some points… we do. Life is a story itself, and we ARE the main character. Only… on this person’s case, she has two. Voice seemed to resonate from the school, a frenzy of thoughts each of them with their own voice, each with their own yearning. It’s the same with everyone… ‘I want… I need… I desire… I yearn…’ One person’s desire may very well be the end of everything. Those voices carried off into the air towards the clouds. They all seemed to quiet though, as one girl sat crouched on the edge of the school’s rooftop. This pretty young thing… with flowing jet black hair that fluttered in the breeze, she stared out at the landscape of the city listening carefully. So many people… so many desires… they were all different, yet they all shared one common thing. “Everyone… will suffer…” Her voice was a light pitch that seemed to whisper eerily, eyes narrowed into a sick look of malice. In her mind there was silence, no thought except for that one desire. Well… that… and the sound of a woman’s maniacal laughter echoing through every bit of her brain… She was Juri Hyobanshi… She was the Masonna.
