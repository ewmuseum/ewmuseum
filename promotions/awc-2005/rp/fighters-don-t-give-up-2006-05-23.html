Fighters Don't Give Up!

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/23/2006 The camera slowly fades into a hospital room where a younger version of 'The Violence' Vince Jones can be seen laying in the hospital bed fast asleep. All of a sudden the door of the room slowly creeps open and a large bodyguard wearing dark shades steps inside. He makes his way over towards the bed of Vince and looks down at him and just shakes his head. He begins tapping him on the cheek to wake him up. Bodyguard: Hey! V. J, wake up! C'mon! Wake up, sunshine! Vince Jones slowly begins to stir to life and he opens up his eyes and begins looking around the room in confusion. He holds his head in pain as he turns towards the bodyguard. Vince Jones: Fuck! What the hell, happened? Bodyguard: (shrugs his shoulders) I wish I could tell you. Vince Jones: Shit! And you don't know a damn thing 'bout this? Bodyguard: (shakes his head) No, not a damn thing. Vince Jones: (snarls) Fuck this shit! Wheneva the hell V. Jones gets out this damn hospital bed he huntin' some bitch mades down! Somebody payin' for this shit! Bodyguard: Yeah. Okay. Anyways, I'm not here for chit chat. I'm here to deliver you a message from the boss. The body guard reaches in his pocket and whips out a letter and hands it to Vince. Vince looks at it in confusion. Vince Jones: What the hell is this, huh? Bodyguard: I don't know. I'm just the messenger. Anyways...get well soon. Vince Jones: Yeah! Whateva... The bodyguard makes his way towards the door and exits the room. Vince immediately rips open the letter, whips it out, and begins reading through it. He glares at the piece of paper in anger, balls it up, and chucks it across the room into a trashcan in the far corner of the room. Vince Jones: Fuck that! Man, can't believe that Big Lex puttin' it down like that! They got me stuck in the hospital... Vince Jones tries to pull himself out of bed, but the pain is too great even for him and he falls backwards. He slaps the bed in frustration and just lowers his head. Vince Jones: Fuck this! Gotta get out this fuckin' bed! Man, V can't get cut off like this! Fuck that! This ain't even cool! This ain't even fair! Vince Jones holds his head and sounds as if he is starting to cry to himself. Vince Jones: Is this it? Damn! It can't end like this! A voice: What can't end like this? Vince stops, quickly whips his eyes, and looks up at the source of the voice in confusion. Vince Jones: Huh? Who the hell are you? The Voice of the stranger: Who am I? I guess you could consider me a friend? Vince Jones: (confused) Friend? What the fuck? Man, we've never met! How the hell we supposed to be friends? The Voice of the stranger: Oh, we've met before you were just unconscious. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Don't tell me you the one that did this shit to me, old man! The voice of the stranger: No, I had nothing to do with that. I'm the man the man that found you out on the streets after what must've been an attack and had them bring you in. Vince Jones: (clutches his head) Look, you got V's fuckin' head spinnin' right now, man! Do you at least know who the hell did this shit? The voice of the stranger: I'm sorry. I wish I could say. Vince Jones: Damnit! The voice of the stranger: So, what did you mean by the end? Vince lowers his head in shame. Vince Jones: This could be it for V. V bein' asked to take care of a lil bit of business and shit and he can't get out of bed. Too much pain. No tellin' what kinda shit's wrong with V right now. The voice of the stranger: This isn't like you. Vince Jones: (confused) Huh? What the hell you mean, old man? You don't even know V. The voice of the stranger: I know more about you then you think and I know that you're not the type of man that'll just sit there, rot in bed, and begin thinking about the end. You're the type that's gonna stand up and keep on going. Vince Jones: Nah. That was before, man. This shit's different. The voice of the stranger: Is it really that different? Is the situation really that bad or are you just telling yourself that? Well, maybe I should've left you out on the streets to rot away rather than have you brought in. That would've saved people a whole lot of time to actually care for a guy that thinks he's gonna get up and move on. Vince lowers his head in shame. The voice of the stranger: So, when you get over your self-pity... A card is tossed in the direction of Vince Jones. The voice of the stranger: Why don't you call me? Until then, get your head straight. You're a fighter and fighters don't give up.... The camera fades out and fades back in to the present where Vince Jones is seen sitting on the bench in a small locker room with his head hung low. He slowly lifts his head. Vince Jones: V's a fighter. Fighters don't give up just like he said back in the day. V we got get back up and take charge once again. Its time... The camera slowly fades to black.
