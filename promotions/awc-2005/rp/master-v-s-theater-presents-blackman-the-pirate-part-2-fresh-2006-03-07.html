Master V's Theater Presents: Blackman the Pirate (Part 2) Fresh! RP

7 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/07/2006 The camera slowly fades in and the screen is black. All of a sudden static fills the screen. The static slowly begins to change to a blood red color. A red logo appears on the screen as if it is being spray painted on the screen over the static. The logo reads: V-T.V. All of a sudden the logo slowly fades away and the screen once again goes pitch black. Next, a message fades in onto the screen in white lettering as a score of British inspired classical music headlined by thundering french horns begins playing in the background. Basically the opening theme from the old Masterpiece Theater show from back in the day. V-T.V proudly presents...Master V's Theater The camera fades into the small, private, dimly-lit library once again, where 'The Violence' Vince Jones can be seen seated on an armchair in his usual all-black street gear with a large book laid across his lap. Vince Jones looks up at the camera with a cocky smirk as the classical music finally ceases. Vince Jones: Good evenin' bitches and welcome back once again to Master V's Theater. Last time we were here Master V introduced all you cats to the story of Blackman the Pirate. Now let's get back in the mix. Enjoy the story. The camera slowly fades out and fades back into the decks of The HMS Violence where Captain Blackman, Reno Blanco, Yasmina, Isabella, and the pirates Arrghhh, Red Sparrow, Shackles, and The Bald Bull are all assembled. Tanto can be seen swabbing the decks with a rather sour expression on his face as he mutters to himself. The voice of the Captain Blackman can be heard speaking in the background as the camera pans around the group. 'Ya know somethin'? It was good bein' back on the seas again. Eva since the shit that took place in The Captain's previous life as a pirate and shit Captain Blackman had been waitin' for this moment, waitin' to get back on the waters and smell that salty air and all. That's the kinda shit Captain Blackman lived for and all. Life was finally good again; but, if there was one thing Captain Blackman didn't miss while he was out the whole buccaneer game and all it was havin' to deal with bitch mades like the crew that thatWalter Pearlious cat had assembled for The Captain, but what you gonna do? You gotta make due with what you given in circumstances like these...' Captain Blackman looks at his crew with an air of confidence about him. Captain Blackman: Aiight bitches! You cats know why you here and all... Tanto: No, why are we here, Captain? Most importantly, why am I here? Captain Blackman turns towards Tanto as does the rest of the crew. Tanto is now standing there looking back at him with an expression of defiance on his face. Blackman shakes his head in disappointment. Captain Blackman: And why the hell you speakin' up and shit, man? Aren't you supposed to be swabbin' the damn decks of this ship?? That's arms no mouth, bitch! Tanto: Because I want to know why am I here? Why? I am a pirate and I should be treated as such! I deserve my due respect as such, Captain! Captain Blackman: Well look, man. Its like this here. Each and everyone on this ship got they own special job and spot they fill. Got that? You just happen to be the Ship Bitch. 'Nuff said! Tanto: But we've got women aboard this ship, Captain. Captain Blackman: And? Tanto: Why doesn't someone like...(points at Yasmina who is busily doing her nails and tending to her make-up.) your Yasmina swab the decks? She's not doing anything of importance. Blackman pulls Yasmina close and she looks up at Blackman and smiles brightly. Captain Blackman: Oh, she got a special duty on this ship, a very important one at that. She the one that keeps The Captain happy and shit if you catch Blackman's drift. Yasmina: And I do a good job too. Yasmina turns towards Tanto and sticks her tongue out. Tanto: But you don't understand. I have skills too. I do not appreciate being condemned to the status of a lowly ship hand by you! Like I said, we have women aboard this ship to do the duties which you assign to me! So, I demand a reassignment right this instant! Captain Blackman just shakes his head and folds his arms across his chest and slowly begins pacing back and forth. Captain Blackman: So, let The Captain get this shit straight here. You of people gonna be tossin' out demands at Captain Blackman? Is you out yo damn mind here? Last time The Captain checked he was wearin' the captain's hat and shit, not you. With that said you best be shuttin' the hell up and get back to the swabbin' before The Captain has yo punk ass swimmin' with the sharks! Tanto: (in defeat) Yes Captain! The Bald Bull turns towards Tanto and spits on the deck in his direction. The Bald Bull: Lazy good for nothin' Colonial! That's why your colony sucks unlike me homeland and me peoples! (thrusts fist in the air) Turks rule! Captain Blackman: (to his crew) Anyways, we all gathered here togetha on this damn ship to do a job for The Captain's boss, Walter Pearlious and shit. Basically he got us on this boat searchin' for some buried treasure and all... Red Sparrow raises his hand with an anxious look in his eyes. Captain Blackman points in his direction. Captain Blackman: Yeah? What the hell is it? The Captain tryin' to explain the deal to you bitches. Red Sparrow: Had a question. Where is this treasure? Captain Blackman reaches in his pocket and whips out a small map with a large X displayed on it and flashes it in front of the group. Shackles looks at the map with large astonished eyes. Shackles: Wow! Its a treasure map! (nudges Isabella) Look sweetheart, its a real treasure map. I have not seen one of those in years. Blackman turns and glares in Shackles and Isabella's direction. Captain Blackman: You two done ova there, huh? You two finished? Blackman tryin' to take of business here and answer the man's question. Now shut it up! The Bald Bull sneers at the two of them and spits on the floor in front of them disgusting Isabella. Captain Blackman: (to the crew) Anyways... it turns out that the treasure we lookin' for is down around Port Royal in Jamaica. Red Sparrow raises his hand once again. Captain Blackman: Yes? Red Sparrow: (clears his throat) Now is this treasure...buried? Captain Blackman looks at Red Sparrow and begins laughing as he saunters across the deck towards him. Captain Blackman: Is the treasure buried? Is the treasure buried? Blackman stops in front of Red Sparrow and slowly rolls up the treasure map in his hand. He then begins beating Red Sparrow across the face with the map like a madman to the amusement of the rest of the crew. Captain Blackman: What the hell do you think?!?!?? We fuckin' pirates here! You think we just get treasure handed to our asses, huh!?!?! NO! We fuckin' dig for that shit, man! Damn! Blackman backs off of Red Sparrow and tries to regain his composure as Arrrgh folds his arms across his chest. Arrrgh: I hate stupid questions like that! Captain Blackman: (slowly) Aiight! Do you cats got any otha questions 'bout this whole pirate operation we got goin' on here? No hands raise. Captain Blackman: Any at all? Red Sparrow hesitates but slowly raises his hand once again. Captain Blackman glares at Red Sparrow in frustration. Captain Blackman: What is it now? Red Sparrow: How long will the voyage be? Captain Blackman: As long as it got to be, son! Red Sparrow: How long is that? Captain Blackman: How the hell should Blackman know, huh? We just started this damn voyage, shit! Red Sparrow raises his hand one more time. Captain Blackman: (grunting) WHAT?? Red Sparrow: May I be excused, Captain? Captain Blackman: (frowns) I think I may have just soiled me pantaloons. Blackman slaps himself across the forehead as he dismisses him and The Bald Bull spits on the deck at the feet of Red Sparrow in fury. Arrrgh: I hate pantaloon wetters! The camera fades out and fades back in. We are now in the bare cabin of Captain Blackman as he can be seen sitting at a small table with a quill in hand as he writes in his 'Captain's Log'. The voice of Captain Blackman can be heard speaking in the background as he writes the words down simultaneously. 'Now Captain Blackman wasn't too fond of these bitches that he had to deal with in the beginnin' but bein' locked up on a damn ship with cats for a long enough time got a funny way of givin' you a new appreciation for people you wouldn't be appreciatin' on the regular and all. You spend enough time with people and you start learnin' shit 'bout them and all. You get to see the good, the bad, and sometimes the ugly in people. Take for instance the man Blackman knows simply as...Arrgh. ' All of a sudden there is a knock at The Captain's door. He looks up from his writing and answers. Captain Blackman: Come in! The door slowly opens and Arrrgh steps inside. Captain Blackman: Yeah? What is it, Arrrgh? Arrrgh: Captain, I come to report that the weather is looking find today. We are in for smooth sailing by the looks of things. And you know how I hate it when the sailing isn't smooth. Captain Blackman: (nodding) Yeah. You seem to hate a whole lot of shit, man. Arrrgh: Aye. That is correct, sir. I hate when you're right. Captain Blackman: Kinda figured that much; but, I got a quick question for ya, man. Arrrgh: Yah? Captain Blackman: Is there anything that you don't hate? Arrrgh cups his chin in thought. Arrrgh: Hmmmm...I have never thought about that. The thought has never crossed me mind, Captain. I hate having to think about that; but, now that you mention it there is one thing I don't hate. Captain Blackman: And what's that? Arrrgh: Me mission in life. Captain Blackman: (laughing) You have a mission in life, Arrrgh? Arrrgh: (nodding) Yes and I believe it to be an important one. Why? Because people speak too much of peace, love, and happiness these days. BAH! What about hate, I say? Well, what about it? We are restricting the people of this fine world to think about only happy things. That, sir, is discrimination. I will not stand for such. If I want to be angry and hateful then I should be allowed to do so as I please... The Captain nods and looks at Arrrgh like he's nuts. Arrrgh: ...Captain, I speak for the masses. My one wish is to one day lead a nation, a great nation full of hate and haters. I'm talking about hater men and women, hater children, hater husbands and wives. They'll live in their hater houses and live their hater lives. They'll all one day pledge of allegiance in a united nation of hate.... Blackman raises a hand signalling for Arrrgh to stop. Captain Blackman: That'll be enough, man Arrrgh: You won't allow me to finish my story. Captain Blackman: No, we don't need no more, man. You got passion though. 'And passion that bitch have for that damn dream of his. Afta that talk Blackman learned that no matta how hateful that cat seemed on the surface he had a love for his vision that was surpassed by nothin'. Then, there was The Bald Bull. Now this cat was interesting no matta what it was this cat had a way of makin' it into an international contest with his country and shit....' The camera fades out and fades onto the deck where Captain Blackman can be seen looking over the railing at the sea. All of a sudden The Bald Bull approaches Captain Blackman and lays a hand on his shoulder. The two of them look out at the beautiful blue sea together on this particular sunny day. The Bald Bull: (takes in a deep breath) Ahhhhhhh! Ze sight is beautiful, no? Blackman nods his head in agreement. Captain Blackman: Hell yeah it is! Kinda missed this whole view while bein' out the game and all, Bull. The Bald Bull: Ahhhhh yes! It is beautiful, but you know what? Captain Blackman: What, man? The Bald Bull: This view is nothing but a pile of dung from a donkey's ass in comparison to zhe view from the waters near my country. Captain Blackman: WHAT?? The Bald Bull: (nodding) That is correct, Captain. I spit on your ugly Atlantic like so! The Bald Bull spits in the sea and walks away as The Captain just watches in him in confusion. The scene switches to the galley of the ship where the crew is all assembled for dinner. Tanto, who is wearing a frilly apron, can be seen spooning slop onto the plates of all the crew members and they look at their meals in disgust. Tanto spoons a large helping of the slop onto Captain Blackman's plate and he holds his nose. Captain Blackman: Hey! What the hell is this stuff, Tanto? Tanto: (smiling) Its my nightly special. Eat up. Its a fine dish if I day say so myself. Arrrgh: I hate nightly specials especially those cooked by you Tanto! This looks like the same crap you've subjected us to for the past three nights! Yasmina frowns at her unappetizing plate. Yasmina: Yeah. He makes a fine point there. Your cooking leaves a lot to be desired, Tanto. I'm not eating this mess. You're trying to kill us! All the rest of the pirates and crew members begin grumbling amongst their selves in anger with their dinners. The Bald Bull looks around at the rest of the crew and just shakes his head. The Bald Bull: (slamming his fist on the table in outrage) My friends, if we were in my homeland right now we would not be subjected to a meal such as zhis! We would be feasting on the finest meats, drinking the finest of drinks, and eating marvelous pastries! Instead, we are gathered here around zhis table ready to fill our bellies with the loose feces of swines that had their privates chopped off and devoured by the fishes! That is why your colonies are inferior and...TURKS RULE! TURKS RULE!! Crickets begin chirping at the table as Red Sparrow appears a bit intrigued by the slop on his plate. He begins scarfing it down. Suddenly he falls out on the floor unconscious to the surprise of the rest of the pirates. The next scene is of a wild storm that took place one night at sea. The crew members can be seen scrambling every which way trying to keep the ship afloat as giant waves wash aboard the ship. The wind is howling and the sky is a dark gray. Captain Blackman is seen at the helm yelling at the top of his lungs at the weather they're experiencing. Captain Blackman: FUCK YOU! FUCK YOU YOU SON OF A BITCH! YOU CAN BLOW THE CAPTAIN AND THIS FUCKIN' SHIP TO HELL, BUT WE AIN'T EVA GONNA STOP! THIS SHIP AIN'T SINKIN'! Red Sparrow can be seen struggling to make his way towards his captain. He finally reaches him as a giant wave leaps up and smacks them both in the face. Captain Blackman turns towards Red Sparrow. Captain Blackman: AND WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT, SPARROW??? Red Sparrow: CAPTAIN, I HAVE A QUESTION...FOR YOU! Captain Blackman: SHOOT, BITCH! Red Sparrow: IF YOU WERE AN ANIMAL, WHAT TYPE OF ANIMAL WOULD YOU BE, CAPTAIN?? Captain Blackman flares up in rage. Captain Blackman: A SHARK SO THE CAPTAIN COULD KILL YO ASS FOR ASKIN' DUMB SHIT IN THE MIDDLE OF A DAMN STORM! Captain Blackman lunges at Red Sparrow and tries to choke the life out of him while The Bald Bull can be seen swinging about hanging onto a ship sail for dear life. The Bald Bull: YOU CALL ZHIS A STORM! WE HAVE REAL STORMS IN MY COUNTRY! I SPIT ON YOUR WEAK STORM LIKE SO! The Bald Bull spits into the wind, but his spit is redirected and nails him in the eye. The Bald Bull: BAAAAAH!!! I CAN'T SEE, CAPTAIN!!! The scene switches back to the Captain's quarters where he can be seen still writing in his log book. 'Shackles, was anotha interestin' cat that took a lil time gettin' used to as well. He and Isabella sure kept us going. Especially Isabella...' The scene switches to the deck where all the pirates can all be seen taking a break from their work. All of a sudden Isabella can be seen strutting across the deck with Shackles following behind her like a little lap dog. The other pirates as well as Captain Blackman and Reno Blanco can all be seen drooling as she walks by in her dress which fits rather tightly around her large bosom. Captain Blackman: Ya know somethin'? That Shackles is one crazy ass lovesick son on of a bitch. Just look at that bitch followin' behind her like he got a leash around his neck or somethin'. If The Captain could start this damn voyage all ova again he'd leave his sorry ass behind; but, that Isabella could still ride. The Pirates: (nodding in unison) Mmmmmm hmmmm!! Yasmina: (snarls at the thought of Isabella) I think she's a good for nothing, toothless wench Captain Blackman: Yeah? Well, The Captain thinkin' she's got the goods! The Pirates: (nodding in unison) Mmmmmm hmmmm!! The scene switches to the Captain's quarters at night. Yasmina can be seen tossing and turning and bed beside Captain Blackman. All of a sudden she slowly opens her eyes, turns around, and sees a pair of eyes staring back at her through the point window along with a rather pleased grin. Yasmina suddenly begins shrieking at the top of her lungs waking up The Captain. Captain Blackman: Ah shit! What's goin' on here?? Yasmina points at the port window in fear. Captain Blackman: What the hell kinda shit is this? All of a sudden Isabella can be heard yelling from the deck. Isabella: SHACKLES!!! Shackles: (from outside the window) Huh? Isabella: Are you peeping through windows again?? How many times have I told you about that already? No more peeping. You're supposed to be a changed man! Now get back to bed right this instance. Shackles : (from outside the window in defeat) Yes, madam! The eyes disappear from the window as Shackles pulls himself back up the side of the ship. 'Then there was Tanto. Yes, Tanto...' The camera switches to the galley where Captain Blackman meets up with Tanto who is peeling potatoes with a knife. Captain Blackman: What the hell you doing, Tanto? Tanto: Peeling potatoes, Captain. Captain Blackman: Well, you ain't doin' a good job with that shit! Peel those damn potatoes faster, bitch! Tanto: Aye aye! The scene switches to the deck on a sunny day where Tanto can be seen scrubbing the decks. Captain Blackman walks up on Tanto and kneels down beside him. Captain Blackman: What are you doing, Tanto? Tanto: Scrubbbing the decks, Captain! Am I doing a good job? Captain Blackman: (shaking his head) Hell nah! You scrub decks like you scrubbin' yo grandma's wrinkled up puss! Scrub those decks faster, bitch! Tanto: Aye aye! The scene switches back to the Captain's quarters. Where Blackman continues to write in his log. 'And then there was Red Sparrow who constantly reminded The Captain of long this voyage really was...' There is a knock at the Captain's door and he answers. Captain Blackman: Come in! In steps Red Sparrow. Captain Blackman: Yes? Red Sparrow: Captain, I have a question for you? Captain Blackman: The Captain has no clue where babies come from, Sparrow! We discussed that shit already. Red Sparrow: No, a different one. We can save that question for another day. Captain Blackman: Aiight then! Shoot! Red Sparrow: Are we there yet? Captain Blackman: No! Red Sparrow: Are we there yet? Captain Blackman: No! Red Sparrow: Are we there yet? Captain Blackman: (shakes his head) No! Red Sparrow: Are we there yet? Captain Blackman: NO! Red Sparrow: Are we there yet? Blackman leaps to his feet and gets in Red Sparrow's face. Captain Blackman: If you ask that damn question one more time... All of a sudden there is a loud, sickening thud, as the ship suddenly jerks as if has ran into something. A voice from outside: LAND HO! Captain Blackman: What?? Red Sparrow: I guess we made it. Blackman and Sparrow both scurry out the quarters and meet up with the rest of the crew on the deck. Captain Blackman looks up at Shackles. Captain Blackman: Hey idiot! Shackles points to himself and Blackman nods. Captain Blackman: Yeah! You! You supposed to say that shit before we hit land! Shackles: Sorry, Captain. Its been a long time. Blackman shakes his head in disappointment and looks at the land before his ship with a smile on his face. Captain Blackman: Well, we're finally here. You know what that means. Get our shit off this boat so we can set up camp for the night. To Be Continued.... The camera slowly fades out and we go back to the private library of Master V (Vince Jones) where he can be seen closing the large book once again. Vince Jones: Well bitches. That's it for our show tonight. Tune in next time when we bring you the final portion of our story here. Until then have a goodnight bitches! The camera slowly fades out as the classical theme music begins to blare out once again and the credits roll.
