.44 Caliber Love Letter | Ein

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.05 08.30.05 | Kyoto, Japan | Evening After he drank his coffee, Jacob stood up and made his way towards his bedroom window. Another win, another mind-numbing headache that got on his nerves. Winning was all that it was cracked up to be, after all. The match with Grady wasn't so painful; at least nowhere near as taxing as the Best of British match had been. If Jacob were to be put in another match of that sort, even he couldn't say the result would be the same. How could it be? 'I got lucky, that's all.' But on the day following Labor Day, Jacob would have to do it again. Two big wins in the beginning of his AWC career gave him a name and a face for everyone watching. The fans were beginning to know who he was, and that would lead to a bigger expectation of him to win matches- something he wasn't sure he was ready for. 'Eh, why am I thinking so hard about this? Wrestling shouldn't be so important.. I have other things to worry about over who I face next in the ring.' After a minute or so of staring out the window and into the streets, Jacob found no other alternative other than the decision that he perhaps a bit of a walk was in order. -- And there he was, walking the streets of Kyoto as the moon had settled into its universal spot in the sky. It's shine was enough of a comfort; at least to Jacob, to whom it was more of a relief than the sun would ever be, for reasons more obvious than one would tend to think. Sure, Jacob was a wrestler. Sure, Jacob was an American man living in Kyoto as he represented the country of Wales on Pay Per View television. He was weird, yes, but you couldn't simply know that Jacob had such bad luck that he wouldn't be able to go into sunlight ever again- even if he sort of looked like a vampire with all the black clothing he wore. Either way, that was not the first thing on Jacob's mind. Instead, he was too busy feeling bad that wrestling popped into his head before his missing sister. Even though he was indeed worried about having a rematch with Andy M., Mike Wade and the British Bomber, it wasn't something he cared about. So what if he won? It didn't change much. And it didn't change the fact that the person who tried to kill him had been in Japan, and was possibly still there. 08.03.05 | Toms River, New Jersey | Afternoon 'For fuck's sake,' Pete exclaimed to his little brother, 'I don't want you heading up there with me. I'm going to be getting drunk as shit and if you come along and get fucked up, too then mom's gonna kill me.' Alex scoffed in defeat as he and his brother left the Seven Eleven on the corner, walking leisurely down the street as he sipped his soda. 'Mom would kill you for going at all, why would it matter if I went up to Toronto with you?' 'Why would you want to go?' Pete sighed. 'You wouldn't be able to do anything, you young stack of pancakes, you.' Alex blinked. 'Did you just call me a stack of pancakes?' 'Shut up.' Alex stopped in his tracks from the abrupt insult, but he soon realized that his brother had his attention focused on something other than bothering him; and once he saw who Pete had his eye on, Alex understood why. 'You know, if there was one reason I wanted to leave high school, it was to get away from you ass pillagers,' Pete sighed, approaching the red haired man with a sarcastic smirk. It was one of the few times the middle child of the Moreno family had noticed Ein donning any other facial expression aside from a smile. Not that Pete paid enough attention to care. 'What's the matter?' Pete continued, careless of how the other man was feeling. 'Upset that you have no one to blow now that my bro ran away?' Ein tilted his head just a little bit, enough to show interest in what Pete had said. A reaction. Just what Pete asked for. 'Hah! That's what you're so mopey about, isn't it?' Pete questioned. Alex, on the other hand, seemed quite put off by the conversation. Not that he had sympathy for the person Pete was making fun of, but because of the simple fact that he'd heard the same insults over and over, and it was becoming quite tiresome. 'Dude,' Pete's little brother grumbled, 'let's just head home. He's not worth bothering every single day, for Chrissake.' Ein sighed at the bickering of the two brothers, before he attempted meekly to talk over them. 'Jacob... ran away?' For all he knew, Jacob died one month ago; he was certain of it. And yet, he hadn't stuck around that night to find out what happened. No one did. Still, he was a bit surprised at the news of Jacob running away. At the same time, however, he also remained surprised that his own brothers thought that Jacob had actually gone out with Ein, which couldn't have been farther from the truth. That was also the reason why Ein never brought it up; he hated to admit that he one person he adored didn't care for him in reutrn; not in the same way, at least. 'He was always the confusing one,' Ein recalled to himself, 'if he didn't like me.. why was he so interested in being friends with me?' Jacob was the type of person who would take a bullet for a friend in need- literally. With the sickness Jacob suffered from, there was always an urge in his heart to make himself presentable to the public rather than pushing people away. In a way, Ein and Jacob were the same; they both felt they were freaks, and neither one could have their mind changed about that. It was sad, but unfortunately very true. It simply made Ein even more sad when he and Jacob lost touch with eachother as time passed. As Ein continued to reminisce, Pete stated dumbly a reply to Ein's question. 'He went to Japan or some shit,' he said, and in such a manner that it almost made Ein second guess Pete's statement and how much truth was behind it. 'And just why would he do that?' 'How the hell should I know?' Ein shook his head. 'You're his brother?' Pete looked back at Ein, almost as if to say he was the craziest man on the planet. 'That doesn't mean I hibernate up his ass all the time, you colon cake.' That was all Ein needed to hear. Knowing he'd be lucky to get any more information out of his annoying acquaintance, Ein brushed past the two boys with little regard. 'Good day, boys.' Little did Ein know just how lucky he'd get from a measly trip to Kyoto. He hadn't expected that he'd find Jacob so easily, but then again, a lot of thing happened in Ein's life that he didn't expect. Needless to say, Jacob hadn't expected it, either. And so when Jacob had actually bumped into Ein on that fateful night in Kyoto, Jacob did the only thing he could think of doing. He ran. As childish as it was, he ran and nothing more. And the more he thought about it, the more it pissed him off that he did. 'No wonder I'm thinking about wrestling.' On Tuesday, there would be nowhere for him to run to if things went bad. And the sooner Jacob got that through his head, the sooner he'd find his sister. All he needed was a little hope, and a lot of luck. The walk he would take then would be much more painful than the one he was taking at that moment, that was for sure. 'Dammit, can't my life just be simple for once?' Jacob knew the answer to the question, it was just too bad that it was an answer he didn't want any part of knowing.
