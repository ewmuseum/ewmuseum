Back to School (Match RP)

24 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera switches to the studio where the show is being taped. A man with brown hair and wearing a brown suit is seen sitting in a leather chair looking at the camera and talking. In front of him is a small, black, glass table, and across from him is nice black, leather sofa. The audience in the studio can be heard cheering wildly as the show begins.] Host: Welcome back to AWCI, Atlantic Wrestling Club Insider. I’m your host, Terry Nickels, and I hope you all enjoy the show. Tonight we bring to you all a very special guest. At this time, I would like to welcome “The Violence” Vince Jones who will be in a 'Back to School' match facing off against Red Rock. [“Thug Luv” begins to play in the background and Vince Jones appears on the scene. At the sight of Vince Jones all the audience begins to boo. He slowly saunters his way across the stage towards the leather sofa as Terry Nickels rises to his feet to greet him. Terry extends his hand out towards Vince and Vince looks down at his hand in disgust. Terry leans over and whispers to Vince.] Terry Nickels: I know what you’re probably thinking, Mr. Jones, but atleast shake my hand for the sake of the show. It’s just for the looks, ya know? [Vince Jones sneers and then nods his head in agreement and gives him a nice hearty handshake. He begins to squeeze the life out of Terry’s hand and Terry begins to wince in pain at his vice-like grip. Terry looks at Vince uneasily and begins to whisper again.] Terry Nickels: Ahhhh….what are you doing?? You’re hurting me, Vince. [Vince Jones laughs to himself and replies.] Vince Jones: It’s for the looks, son. [Vince releases the handshake from hell and pats Terry on the back as Terry holds his hand in pain.] Terry Nickels: Please…have a seat. [Terry Nickels tries to slap some feeling back into his hand as they both take their respective seats.] Terry Nickels: Well Vince, it’s good to have you here on the show. Vince Jones: Yeah…whateva. [Terry Nickels finally manages to regain his composure and smiles.] Terry Nickels: So, my first question for you Vince is why? [Vince folds his arms across his chest, leans, back on the sofa, and just looks at Terry in confusion.] Vince Jones: Why what? Terry Nickels: Why a 'Back to School' match at Twilight of the Gods? It just doesn't seem like your type of fight. Vince Jones: (shakes his head) Its hardcore. If its hardcore its V. Jones game. V knows hardcore unlike Red Rock. It don't matter where the hell the fight is. V. Jones comin' out on top with that shit, man. That's just the way it is. Terry Nickels: But its a match in a school. How do you go about getting ready for somethin' like this. Vince Jones: What you tryin' to say, huh? V. Jones may not have been the type of cat that wanted to be up in school, neva have and neva will. First chance he got though he picked up his GED, Good Enough Diploma and shit. While gettin' that shit V. Jones learned that you gotta research and all if you tryin' to figga stuff out. Terry Nickels: But how you plannin' on going about preparing for this? Vince Jones: How the fuck else you think he did? V went to school! [Vince and Terry both appear a bit surprised by the sudden outburst.] Vince Jones: What the? Voice: Hey!! You heard me over here?? [Vince Jones overhears the yell, lowers his dark shades, and turns around to catch sight of a guy in the audience wearing a Red Rock for President t-shirt and who is standing up in the middle of the audience.] Vince Jones: What the hell do you want, huh?? The guy: I just wanted to tell everybody something real quick, if you don’t mind. Vince Jones: WHAT?? This ain’t yo damn interview we havin’ right now! No one gives a damn about you! This V. Jones’ time, b*tch! [Vince tries to regain his composure and takes a deep breath in and then out.] Vince Jones: Aight, aight, aight. If you got somethin’ to say, make it quick so V can move on here. Ya heard? [The guy nods in agreement.] The Red Rock Fan: Alright. Here it goes. [clears his throat] Red Rock RULES!!! YEAH!!!! [Vince Jones raises an eyebrow in confusion and just shakes his head as the guy’s buddy jumps up and gives him a high five.] The Red Rock Fan: Red Rock RULES ALL!!!!! YEAH!!!! HE DA MAN!!! HE DA MAN!!! [The guy rips off his shirt and starts going crazy. All of a sudden Vince Jones becomes a little enfuriated by the commotion and stands to his feet.] Vince Jones: HEY!!! [The two audience members stop jumping up and down and going wild to hear what Vince Jones has to say.] Vince Jones: You two bitch mades sit yo punk asses down! V ain’t tryin’ to hear jack ‘bout yo lil Red Rock on V’s time! Get it? Got it? Good! Come back when that b*tch made hero of yours is gettin’ interviewed! Aiight?? [The two guys slowly regain their composure and take their respective seats. Vince Jones just rolls his eyes and puts the dark shades back in place on his face as he takes his seat as well.] Terry Nickels: Alright. Now, where we? Ah yes. Tell us a little more about this school visit of yours. Vince Jones: Well, its like this. Reno, tossed the idea to V. He got V interested in this whole deal and decided to hop on board. That's what got V into going into school to do this. Voice from the audience: Give me an“R” ! Give me an “E” ! Give me a “D” ! [Vince and Terry overhear the commotion from the audience and turn around to spot a bunch of cheerleaders wearing outfits with Red Rock's face in the middle of each of them.] The cheerleaders: Give me an “R“ ! Give me an “O” ! Give me a 'C'! Give me a 'K'And what does that spell girls?? Red Rock!!! Wooooooooo!!!! [All of a sudden the two Red Rock fans from earlier hop up once again and begin yelling and screaming at the top of their lungs.] The Red Rock fans: RED ROCK RULES!!!! YEAH!!!! THEY KNOW!! THEY KNOW!!! HE DA MAN!!! HE DA MAN!!!!! Vince Jones: HEY!!! [All of them stop their cheering and look at Vince in surprise and fear.] Vince Jones: All you bitch mades shut the hell up ‘fore V. Jones has those damn pom poms shoved up each and everyone of yo asses!!! [A blonde-haired cheerleader in the group manages to muster up enough confidence to reply as the rest of her group stands there in silence.] Blonde cheerleader: But…we’re just supporting Red Rock, Mr. Violence. We’re apart of his fan club. Vince Jones: Look here, ya damn Valley Girl! You think V could give a damn about yo Red Rock fan club, huh?? Now sit yo asses down! This V’s time right now, not Red Rock’s! [They all nod their heads in agreement and take their seats once again. The cheerleader begins crying and her friends console her. Vince turns towards Terry and just shakes his head and points in the direction of the audience.] Vince Jones: Y’all don’t screen these idiots that come waltzin’ they asses up in here?? [Terry shakes his head.] Terry Nickels: No, I’m sorry, Mr. Jones. Its against our policy to do so. [Vince slaps his own forehead and just shakes his head in disappointment.] Vince Jones: WHAT?? C’mon now. Damn! Any cat could come rollin’ up in this joint. Damn Bin Laden or somebody could just be chillin’ up in here right now to blow some shit up’! Ya know? [Vince turns towards the audience and just happens to catch sight of a guy that looks just like Osama Bin Laden sitting in the front row who puts a finger up to his lips signaling for him to be quiet and not blow his cover. Vince Jones turns back towards Terry.] Vince Jones: But yeah, a whole lot of stuff went down when V was doin’ that whole schoolhouse visit gig. A whole hell of a lot of stuff went down. Terry Nickels: I see. Mind sharing your story with us? Vince Jones: Yeah. V can tell you all a lil somethin’ somethin’ ‘bout that. Terry Nickels: That’s great. [Terry Nickels turns towards the camera and smiles.] Terry Nickels: Well, you heard The Violence. He’s gonna share with us all his experiences from his visit to high school. At this time let’s go to a commercial break and we’ll be right back with some more Atlantic Wrestling Club Insider. [The crowd applauds as the camera zooms out and slowly fades to black.] To Be Continued… **Commercial Break** [The camera fades in and “The Violence” Vince Jones can be seen driving down the streets of East Hartford Conneticut in his red Ferrari in the morning as his voice can be heard narrating in the background.] Narrator Vince: Aiight. It’s like this. It was ‘bout Thursday mornin' and all. If the details seem a bit off and shit its cuz V ain't much of a mornin' person and all. Reno Banks had gotten V on this goin' this goin' to school gig and all and V was hyped and ready to go. Anyways, it’s early in the mornin’ and V cruisin’ down the block tryin’ to find this damn high school and all… [Vince Jones continues cruisin’ down the block and he sees three scantily clad women standing on a street corner.] Narrator Vince: So, V decided he’d stop on this street corna and ask for some directions. Yeah. They was some hos, but V was thinkin’ they’d know their way around, cuz...well, they asses been around. [Vince Jones can be seen whippin’ the ferarri up alongside the curb and turn towards the three women as they come walking up to Vince’s ride with big smiles on their faces.] Vince Jones: Hey! Look here, hos. V. Jones tryin’ to find… [All of a sudden the ho in the middle lunges forward, grabs Vince, and begins trying to make out with him. Vince struggles free from her grasp, wipes his mouth dry, and glares at her in disgust.] Vince Jones: You betta slow yo roll, bitch! V got business to take care of today. He ain’t tryin’ to get caught up in jack with yo STD havin’ ass! Ya heard?? V ain’t tryin’ to get hemned up and burned here! [The hos look at their ho comrade and just shake their heads at her in disappointment and laugh.] Ho #1: Don’t mind her. She’s a little horny right now. She hasn’t had any in about two whole days. She gets a little antsy whenever she’s a on a drought like that. When she hits this state there’s almost no controlling her at all. [The desperate ho begins salivating at the mouth wanting a taste of Vince. Vince notices her desperation and just shakes his head at her as well. The three hos look at Vince Jones in delight and lean in close giving him some suggestive gazes..] Ho#2: So, you looking for a good time or something, big man? For the right price all of us could be doing whatever you like all night long. [She begins caressing Vince’s arm, but he doesn’t really pay much attention to her advances and suggestions at all.] Vince Jones: V ain’t lookin’ for none of that from you hos right now. See, V tryin’ to find this East Hartford High School. You cats know where the hell that is? [The two normal hos shake their heads and appear a bit disappointed in their business failure. All of a sudden the desperate ho’s eyes grow wide and she looks at Vince in excitement.] Crazy Ho: Oooooo oooo ooo! I know! I know where it is! I know where it is! I was supposed to be going there to meet my boyfriend. He's a senior. [She folds her arms across her well-endowed chest in frustration.] Crazy Ho: So, you need directions? We can have sex on the way? How about some sex?? C'mon! You know want a piece of this. [Vince pulls his hand away from her in disgust as she slaps her booty.] Vince Jones: Get off me! Why don't you do yo damn high school senior boyfriend or somethin' [The crazy ho lowers her head in disappointment.] Crazy Ho: His parents say he's too young to have sex. Oh, I can't wait until Tyler turns 18. When he does I'm gonna give him a nice blow... Vince Jones: Whoa! Whoa! Whoooooooa! Enough 'bout that shit! V don't wanna hear all that! V. Jones just tryin' to find out where the hell this damn high school is! Now where the hell is this High School at, huh?? Crazy Ho: You mind driving me over there? I can give you the directions myself. [Vince looks at the crazy ho uneasily and nods.] Vince Jones: Aiight, but it goes like this. Don’t you try to pull anything. You do and that’s yo ass! Get it? Got it? Good! [The crazy ho nods her head in agreement, runs around the car and hops inside. Vince pulls off down the street and Vince and Terry’s voices can be heard in the background as Vince and the ho go riding down the street of Conneticut.] Terry Nickels: You mean to tell me you actually picked up that sex crazed, hooker, Vince? What if she had infested your seats with something lethal. There’s no telling what she might’ve had. Narrator Vince: Hey. V had to do what he had to do. He had no clue where that damn school was. She knew so V went with the flow. Terry Nickels: Well, how did the drive over there go with that sex crazed hooker in the car along with you. Must’ve been rough with her desperation for sex and all. Narrator Vince: Yeah. It was a bit rough... [Vince Jones is seen still focusing on the road as he drives down the busy streets of Conneticut. The crazy ho turns around, looks at Vince, and then her eyes slowly drift downward. A frontal view of Vince driving down the streets of Hartford is shown and all of a sudden the sound of a zipper unzipping is heard and the ho’s head drops out of view.] Vince Jones: (in surprise) Hey! What the… [Vince quickly swerves the car to the left and swerves back on course nearly missing another car that was coming towards his. Vince looks down and begins struggling to get the ho to let go.] Vince Jones: What the hell you doin’ down there?? [All of a sudden a relaxed expression crosses Vince’s face and he sinks down in the driver’s seat a bit.] Terry Nickels: You didn’t let her get away with that, did you? Narrator Vince: Huh? Ah hell nah!! What you take V. Jones for, huh? [Vince can be seen reaching down and viciously smacking the ho in the back of the head and she sits back up on the car and wipes off her lips. Vince looks at her in fury and zips his pants back up.] Vince Jones: You try and pull that crap again and you out this car! Understood?? [The crazy ho nods in agreement.] Terry Nickels: Is that what really happened or are you just saying this to keep yourself from getting into trouble? Narrator Vince: Whoa, whoa, whoa! That’s how it really went down. What? You think V would just randomly be bangin’ some crazy ass ho or somethin’? That ain't how V. Jones rolls, man. [The camera fades out and fades back into small alley in the city where Vince’s red Ferrari is parked and can be seen rocking back and forth wildly. The loud moaning of a woman can be heard inside and then all of a sudden she begins yelling.] Crazy Ho: Ooooooh yes!!! Yes!!!! Harder! HARDER!!! GIVE IT TO ME, BABY!!! GIVE IT TO ME ROUGH LIKE YOU DO IN THE RING!!! [The car continues rocking violently and then it slowly comes to a stop. Vince Jones sits up, puts his dark shades back in place, gives a thumbs up to the camera with a cocky smirk on his face, backs the car out of the alley, and pulls off down the street. The camera slowly fades to black.] Narrator Vince: Hell nah! That’s V’s story and he stickin’ to it. Well, after drivin’ around for a bit V and the ho finally reached the high school. Shit was interestin' from the time he set foot in those damn doors... [The camera fades in as Vince Jones is seen with a rather uncharacteristic large, goofy grin on his face as he pushes open the front doors of the high school and looks around the scene. The camera pans around as your typical high school teenagers are seen walking around all about the hallways every which way.] Vince Jones: Damn, high school! Got fuckin' teenagers roamin' around all over the damn place! Don't even know whether they comin' or goin'! How the hell V gonna go through with all this shit? A group of high school girls pass by Vince Jones and look him up and down with smiles on their faces. Vince catches their glances, but doesn't give them the time of day. Group of high school girls: (in unison) Heeeeeeey sexy! The begin giggling amongst themselves as Vince turns and shakes his head at them in disappointment. He shudders at the thought of them. Vince Jones: Damn young ass fucks! Hell to the nah! Vince reaches in his pocket and whips out a small notebook and begins taking notes. Vince Jones: Note to self, jail bait all over the damn place up in high school... A busty, blonde girl who appears to look twenty-one or older walks past Vince Jones and smiles at him as well. Vince Jones: ...some of that jail bait looks fine as hell though. Be careful. The boy looks up at Vince Jones in relief as Vince Jones looks down and just sneers at him. The nerd quickly hops to his feet and looks up at Vince. Narrator Vince: ...even made a few friends up in that joint... Terry Nickels: So, you helped out that kid? How nice of you. Narrator Vince: Uhhh...yeah whateva, man. V taught his ass a lesson on life and shit... Nerdy kid: Vince Jones?? Oh, you've gotta help me, man! This is perfect timing, a prayer in disguise! These bullies are trying to steal my lunch money.
