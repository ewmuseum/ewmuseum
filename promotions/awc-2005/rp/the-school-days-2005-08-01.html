The School Days

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday 8th January 2001 Date: Monday 8th January 2001 Bluntside South School had seen it's fair share of new students over the years since it has opened in 1982 as a fresh, new school for the suburban areas of Los Angeles. Students from all over the city had joined the school late, attracted by the school's modern facilities, good educational record and respectable reputuation in the area. Some had just moved to the city, and chosen the school, ending up lucky enough to get a space at the in-demand establishment. Some had also came from other countries to the school, such as Canada or the United Kingdom, but none had ever emerged from a country that most students would class as 'just enough place on the map'. The reputuation of the school, however, couldn't of put a perception of the school across that was further from the truth. The school may have achieved good results thanks to the intelligent students choosing the school ahead of others in the area, and it may have automatic doors leading to the reception desk, but it would be no lie to say the school was a cruel, corrupt society. Teachers were playing fools to a senior staff which would do anything to get what they want, be it funding, a pay-rise or a bit of press coverage for a school which, in reality, was just like the others in the area. Bullying would go on without anyone doing anything substantial about it, students would often show little respect for people not in their 'gang', and teachers would often show a mutual dislike for each other outside of lessons. Despite incidents of reasonable publicity involving assaults and staff mis-conduct, the school had still somehow maintained a high reputuation. And this would no doubt be why the thirteen-year old Amélia Silveira was going to attend Bluntside South, starting on the first day back after the Christmas holiday having just moved to the country from her native Portugal. Her fluent understanding of English and the reasonable wealth of her father had been enough to get her in to the school, although most would predict that her nationality would cause her some problems 'fitting in' at her new school. She had been popular back home, with lots of friends, but some would be concerned that she'd be a misfit without her looks. The Monday which she would start on would be a typical Monday, for January, with the weather cold and the wind containing a bitter freeze which would make anything outdoors be unpleasant. She'd arrived at the school at around 8:20 PM, which was about the same time that the earliest arrivers would make it to the school. Getting to the school by taxi, she had managed to remember where the reception was from her only visit in December, and made her way to the automatic doors leading to them. Passing through them she walked past a tall, brown-haired teacher, probably in his early-30s, whose immediate impression was to look her up and down. As he walked on to do whatever he had to do before the students began to arrive in bulk, Amélia couldn't tell whether he was looking at her in the perverted sense, or whether he was checking her uniform. The school uniform Amy had been given was one of the things she was most nervous about. With the 'help' of her older, sixteen-year old sister Catarina, she had bought the uniform that she might of deemed to be too 'small' for her. The uniform was thankfully fairly modern, with a white polo shirt and navy blue jumper. That part was fine, but with her sister insisting on there being no restrictions on skirt length, she was wearing a skirt a decent bit above the knees, which was the required length in Portugal (and thus, she had always worn tight-fitting trousers, to the boy's approval), and she was concerned about getting into trouble. Her sister was wearing a skirt of about the same length, though, which made it less of a problem. The rest of the uniform consisted of a pair of black tights, and black shoes, which again was fair enough with her. She just felt more 'exposed' than normal. Even though no-one was around, Amélia felt everyone was watching her, but regardless she made her way over to the office window on the left of a thin corridor. Knocking on the window lightly, an ageing woman with curly, grey hair turned in her desk chair. Upon seeing the tanned Amélia, she signalled to wait a moment, and after a swig of what appeared to be mineral water she made her way to the window, opening it and querying what the young woman wanted. Office Lady: Yes, what can I do for you? Amélia Silveira: Emm.. I'm Amélia, I'm supposed to be starting here today.. emm.. The semi-nervousness in Amélia's voice was clear, and the office woman interrupted her, noticing this, and laughing a little at the same time even though nothing funny had been said. Office Lady: Ok, ok.. I know why you're here. Take a seat over there. The woman pointed to five red seats opposite the office window. Amélia nodded, walking over and sitting down in the seats, brushing the back of her navy blue skirt down first, so that it didn't end up shorter than it was already. Amélia looked around, watching a few girls arriving in the distance, and worrying that they were really different to her. The woman in the office had disappeared for a moment, re-appearing with a walkie-talkie in her hand, talking into it. After a moment she disappeared again, and Amélia sat waiting for a while, thinking that people might have forgot about her. Looking at her silver wristwatch, the time showed it as being twenty-five to nine, and Amélia suddenly felt a sense of concern that she might of been forgotten about. The nerves of fitting in kicked in, and as she began to feel a little restless, shivering ever so slightly due to the temperature, a man appeared standing between the doors. Man In The Doors: Er, Amélia Silveira? The man looked around for some reason, even though the dead end only had one person in it. Amélia looked up, and he spotted her, looking her up and down as the other staff member had earlier. Amélia Silveira: That's me. Amélia smiled faintly, standing up. The man motioned over to her, reaching his hand out. As she reached her hand out for the handshake, she got a chance to examine her new 'head of year', as the school called it. With the school spanning students from ages 11 to 18, each year needed someone to run it, and this man was obviously the one running it. She'd never met him before, as he was away when she visited, and so this first encounter was an important one. Amélia looked him up and down without his noticing, and from it she could tell he was a young man, in his late-20s. His brown stubble annoyed her slightly, but she could tell that to students he'd actually be deemed remotely attractive. After the handshake, Amélia stepped back, and a moment passed before the teacher grinned scarily at her. Head Of Year: Welcome to Bluntside South, Amélia! Amélia Silveira: Er.. Thanks. Head Of Year: My name is Mr. Bebbington, unless you didn't know, and I'm your head of year. I'd normally take you around the school now, but I'm quite busy and I hear you've visited before, yes? Amélia Silveira: Emm, yeah.. before Christmas. Mr. Bebbington: Ok. Well, I'll just take you straight to your class, then. This school isn't as stereotypical as most US schools, so you may need to get used to it, as it's also quite different to how things were in Portugal, I imagine. Amélia Silveira: Er, yes, I guess it is.. Amélia gave the teacher short, simple answers in order to try and avoid putting her nervousness across. She was also surprised he hadn't commented about the exposure of her upper leg, but with his age, she wasn't surprised. Mr. Bebbington began to make his way toward the automatic doors, before looking back and grinning again. Mr. Bebbington: Come on, then. Amélia nodded, brushing herself down again and following him. The two made their way to the bitter cold of the outside, walking past one or two older students who greeted him, before making their way through an open fire door with a notice on it that appeared to be in Japanese. The fire door led into a large hall, with the door at the other end of the room leading to some stairs. Mr. Bebbington: Ok, this is the staff stairs, as you may know. These are a shortcut for staff to get to the upper levels of this block, and so obviously you shouldn't use them. At the top of these stairs is your room. The first period this morning is in there, and then you'll be onto your timetable. Mr. Bebbington took out a timetable, handing it to Amélia, who had a glance at it before looking for a pocket. Noticing that she didn't have one, she put it in the black bag slung over her shoulder. The head of year grinned again, leading Amélia up the stairs. By now the time was 8:45 PM, the time that students were required to turn up by. As of such, when Mr. Bebbington looked through the window, the room was full of the students Amélia would be sharing the next seventy minutes with. Upon seeing Bebbington, the blonde-haired female teacher in the room made her way across to the door, leaving the room for a moment. She, like the other teachers, looked Amélia up and down before smiling at the new student, aware of her identity. Mr. Bebbington: This is Amélia, who as you know will be in your class from now on. I've gave her the lesson timetable we assigned her, and now I'll leave it up to you for her to fit in. The blonde-haired teacher nodded a little. Mr. Bebbington: She's quite intelligent. Amélia smiled a little at the intelligent comment, before standing up a little straighter. The head of year opened the door, poking his head through the door to sudden silence, which surprised her as he seemed rather incompetent at his job. Mr. Bebbington: Robert, I need to see you now. A general 'Ooh' from some of the girls in the class emerged, and Mr. Bebbington made his way around a corner, followed by an acne-covered, bulky boy, who Amélia assumed was Robert. Upon spotting the Portuguese girl, he looked her up and down, smiling at her a little. Amélia didn't know how to react, so she just returned his smile in a cute smile, before getting the temptation to roll her eyes after he passed by. Blonde-Haired Teacher: That's Robert. He's the loose nut in the class. I'm Mrs. Bradley, and I'm the other loose nut in the class. Amélia laughed a little at the rather poor joke, smiling at the same time, before following Mrs. Bradley into the classroom. She very light blonde hair was long and curly, and like Mr. Bebbington she seemed to rather young, and probably in her late twenties. Amélia wondered if this was always the case, as she'd been used to balding, greying men with a 'shoe polish head'. Mrs. Bradley clapped her hands loudly, making the general chatter stop for the best part, turning the focus to her and the new student. A lot of the focus came to be on Amélia, which she would expect to be the case for quite a lot of the day. Regardless, Mrs. Bradley clapped her hands twice again to garner the rest of the attention, before smiling, looking at Amélia, and smiling again. Mrs. Bradley: This is Amélia, who'll be joining us from.. now. Amélia didn't know how to react, and the nerves suddenly kicked in on a large scale. She just smiled, scanning the room and looking at the various different people scattered around the room. As it was a Science room, all of the people were seated on stools at large, brown, wooden desks, and there was therefore a less formal layout in the room than she was used to. Mrs. Bradley: I expect you'll help her to fit in here. She's just moved to the city, and her sister is a couple of grades above her. Some of the girls in the middle of the room had begun talking again, and had turned their backs to the teacher. Mrs. Bradley ignored this, continuing to speak, whilst a few of the boys looked Amélia up and down, nudging each other in random acts of approval. Mrs. Bradley: Katie..? One of the girls who had turned her back slowly turned around to look at the teacher. Katie: Yes? Mrs. Bradley: I want you to help Amélia through her first day at school. Mrs. Bradley was clearly having trouble pronouncing 'Am-eel-e-a', pronouncing it as 'Am-el-i-ya'. Amélia tried to ignore this. Katie, meanwhile, just nodded a little, turning her head and rolling her eyes to the laughter of the two friends she was sitting with. The teacher motioned Amélia to go and sit on the vacant seat next to one of Katie's friends, before walking back to the front of the room. Mrs. Bradley: Ok, now that we're back at school, let's get the register out of the way..
