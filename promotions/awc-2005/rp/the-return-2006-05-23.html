The Return

23 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/06 New England this time of year is usually one of the most beautiful seasons, but in 2006 there has been a small problem. This year we seem to have an abundance of rain, a large abundance actually causing problems. Coastal flooding, unemployment rating going through the roof due to people not working, and even worse more pissed off people on the roads, the one thing Massachusetts and all of New England doesn’t need. However in their cozy little home in Newton, Ma newlyweds Tiara Belle and Wayne Russell are sitting in their living room. Wayne is comfortably sitting on the recliner with his feet up, Wayne is sipping on a gin and tonic while his wife Tiara is on the computer drinking a glass of wine. “Dear?” Tiara looks over and Wayne to get his attention, once she has received it she continues on, “It seems we have received all of our information from AWC higher ups.” Wayne stops drinking from his glass and gives her a look of interest, she then continues on speaking, “ Yeah we have been let back in, we are now back on the roster of AWC. Aren’t you excited?” Wayne in a mocking voice, “Oh yes dear.. WOOT WOOT!” Tiara looks like she is not pleased by any means. Wayne picks up on the attitude, “Sorry babe, yes… yes I am really excited. When do we start again?” “Well that’s where the problem is….” Tiara starts to stall as she reads the screen in front of her. “We are expected to be in the Czech Republic by next Tuesday night seeing as we are on Fresh!” Wayne is paying full attention, before he was just pretending so he could try and stop his wife from bitching, I know it’s hard to believe Tiara Belle bitching, never. “Do we have a warm up match like on the under card or something?” Tiara reads the screen that is in front of her, and the anticipation builds for Wayne, but he sits patiently until she finally reveals the information. “We are the sub main event, and we are fighting for a chance to fight Celestial Fury for the belts.” “Those two still have the belts?” “So it seems.” Tiara responds to Wayne rhetorical question as if he really wanted one. “Those two have had the belts since we left? What was that 2-3 months ago? No wonder they wanted us back so bad.” Wayne feels his ego start to swell a little bit, knowing that AWC and David Harbor went looking for the pair only a couple of months after they were let go with Nash and Bridges. The two go back to doing what they were doing respectively, Wayne drinking and Tiara reading about the recent happenings with AWC. It’s not long before Wayne is interrupted once again by Tiara. “Honey, it looks like those two goofballs Nash and Bridges are gone as well.” “Damn it!” “What?” “I actually liked their singing; it was a form of comic relief, what was that song they wrote about you?” Wayne lets a little chuckle out knowing that soon enough there would be a problem with his statement and then it came. “How dare you? I can’t believe you actually thought they were funny. They were degrading and disrespectful to women, most importantly to your wife!” “Yes dear,” Wayne says it like he knew he was wrong saying but he really did mean it. “Yeah, those goofs are gone; most of the teams are gone. Soon the Furious Fists of God should return but for now they are out. Soon enough, soon enough will we have our own, Wayne?” “That’s right dear, we will get ours.” Wayne in a mocking voice hopes Tiara catches on and is no longer going to play this game. Seemingly it worked and the two go back to peace and quiet for now, until Tiara decides to speak again.
