Date: 23.10.06 I am shit. I’m not the shit, I’m just shit. It’s been some ridiculous amount of time since I’ve won a match. The gypsy is leaving. I’m wrestling two no names. Whatever happened to the high profile matches - Lavelle, Crisis, Nash. Where did they go? Oh wait, I know. Down the toilet. Why? I am shit. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* The confidence that was normally exuded from the pores of Las Vegas’ own Josh Marquez was missing this week as made his way through downtown Miami. Why is he in Miami, Fresh isn’t coming from there this week? Well, basically, he is getting trashed. Legless, annihilated, battered, smashed, inebriated, hammered, they all describe the state he is hoping to be in by the end of tonight. Why is he putting himself through this fun, if liver-threatening night? Ironically, it’s to detox. Not to rid his body of toxins mind, no, rather to purge his memory of recent events that have occurred around him in AWC. What events, what has happened that is so bad? Duh…everything. After a great showing at Coast To Coast, nothing has gone right. His performances on Fresh have been mediocre and his showing at Untouchable was just embarrassing. The king prawn played a bigger role in the match than he did. He was number one in the rankings though, he had Paddy O’Shea wrapped round his finger, that must be a good thing? Well, obviously it is, but even that has gone to shit now. The pikey has decided he needs a break, leaving Josh having wasted two months with dick all to show for it. Without Paddy he’s just another guy with a bad record, suitable only for midcard mediocrity. That’s not very good, is it? No it fucking isn’t. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* That tequila was really not a good idea. Where the fuck did Tony go? And Eddie? Was Hector even here? I need a pick me up. Vodka bar? I’m there. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* So now he’s stumbling along the beachfront, his Armani shirt spotted with various colours, the sources of the stains unknown to him, all he knows is that it was someone else who spilt those drinks over him. To passers-by, he is nothing more than another well-dressed lush bringing down the tone of the place. Tonight, that suits him just fine. Since he made his debut in AWC, he’s been getting recognised more and more by people as he goes about his everyday life. Even in a bustling tourist trap like Las Vegas he isn’t safe. He’ll be sitting at the bar having a casual drink when an English family will come up to him, brandishing pens and paper, demanding an autograph. Or he’ll lose a hand at the poker tables and a vacationing Aussie will try to start something. It really was getting to be a pain in the ass for him, so the ‘who-gives-a-fuck’ attitude he was experiencing down here on South Beach was just perfect. If he hadn’t been too wasted to realise. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* I don’t like this vodka very much. Sambuca, that’ll do it. How much?! Fuck sake…No, of course I’ll pay it. Fucking rip-off. Shit that tastes like tar! You call this rum?! Sambuca? I didn’t order sambuca… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Actually, he wasn’t totally wasted. He still had the awareness to know that only one thing would finish this night off in style. Some grade-A, suntanned, beach fit ass. There was no shortage of hotties here and despite his level of intoxication he was sure that he could do the business and provide the goods if required. So off he wandered from the vodka bar, shot glass still in hand, the bartender too afraid to remind him of the fact. Hell, it was a fifty cent glass, definitely not worth getting his ass kicked over. The guy was plastered. There was somewhere Josh remembered from earlier, a bouncer who he’d recognised from some as yet undetermined place guarding the entrance, offering him a booth, free drinks, the whole nine. Now if only Josh could find it… ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Here it is, hi Jerry! Yes you are, Jerry, big Jerry, it’s me, Josh! I am not, why you being like this Jerry?! Fucking say that to me! I’ll… Okay, calm down, I was just playing. Fat fucker. Oh shit! ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* For a guy who’d been knocking them back throughout the evening, as well as a large part of the afternoon, Josh moved pretty fast. Only problem was, he moved quickly to the ground, tripping and falling, ending up spread eagled on the sidewalk, looking at the stars. This was one of those situations that not even the old Marquez charm could turn around. That sucked. As he was helped up, Josh made a pact with himself that as fun as it may be to get absolutely destroyed, pussy came first. Hey, I’m not going to sugar coat it. Then, as if by magic, the path of the night suddenly became good again. I’m talking 5’5”, blonde, 32DD good. Why she was willing to go anywhere near Josh in his current state was a mystery but he sure as hell didn’t mind. When she offered to take him home, clean him up and look after him, he looked like he’d won the lottery. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* I am the fucking man. Yes you are and you’re going to get laid. I’m a legend. Ruin her man, just ruin her. I plan to. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* And he did. None of that penile dysfunction for Josh Marquez. No sir, if anything, the alcohol helped him, made him concentrate more, made her enjoy it more and made that special moment so much better. After that of course, he fell asleep, he wasn’t Superman after all. It was a satisfied sleep though, in a warm bed instead of a piss soaked gutter. Awesome. Well, until he woke up that was. For lying beside him in what felt like a single bed despite in actuality being a king size was unequivocally not the woman he had come home with last night. Or at least not the woman he had seen through the beer, vodka, whiskey, rum, sambuca, tequila and brandy glasses. Far from it actually. Instead of 5’5” think 6’1”. Rather than blonde think dyed redhead. Add 14 inches to the bra size and you’re getting close to the true picture. Yes, Josh Marquez, the hottest man in AWC, wanted by women throughout the country, slept with a whale. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Oh. My. Good. Fuck. I’m never living this one down. Probably best to slip out quietly. Shit, don’t wake it! I mean her! This is a new low. Fuck it. Today is a new day. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* Exactly.
