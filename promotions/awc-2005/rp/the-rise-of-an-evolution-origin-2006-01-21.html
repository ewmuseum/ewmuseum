The Rise Of An Evolution: Origin

21 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/1/06 Nine years ago I lived in a sweet little house in Trenton, New Jersey. I had a father whose name was Jeff H. and my mother, Stephanie Carter Harris. It wasn’t until three years ago when I discovered that my father didn’t really get up and leave me like my mother said, my mother lied to me and since that day I hadn’t spoken to her very much. For years, nine to be exact, I had hateful feelings toward my dad, I wished death upon him when I was in my early teens, the thought had crossed my mind that the wish finally came true, but once I met him again, once we both realised it wasn’t his fault that’s when I jumped ship, I loved my father more than my mother. It wasn’t because he was richer or a better provider, after all my mother did everything she could to keep me going when I was younger, but it was because he never lied to me and damn, he was cool. My mother also felt that I betrayed her, after she told Jeff to leave us that was when the money was starting to run out. With my dad on the road we didn’t get much money from him and the little we did get was from his wrestling appearances. He kept just enough money for himself then gave most of it to us but it wasn’t enough unfortunately, we had to move to a cheaper house in Brooklyn, New York, now I’m sure there were better area’s in Brooklyn but the part we lived in was bad, it was worse than bad. I only became apart of the “big dogs” around there because I stopped someone from getting killed, they made sure I was alright where on the other hand my mother started seeing another man, a man called Nick Jones, he seemed alright at first but then he turned into an abusive bastard. My mother was too scared to leave him in case he came after her, I tried to get her to leave, but she wouldn’t listen and when Nick found out I tried to get her to leave him he came after me, I was only 14 at the time, and I was no match for him. But God knows I tried to fight back, it got to the point where he started hitting me with various objects, and I was in hospital more times than I could count. When the police asked what had happened, I told them the truth but my mother being the scared bitch she was, she always said I was lying, she told the police I just didn’t like him which was indeed true, but she also said I fell out of trees and fell down stairs. I left, “mah main homie” X Fiddy let me move in with him, I had to choice but to leave my mother to it, it was great news when I heard that Jeff had beat the hell out of him a few years ago, that was the decider that I would go with him. I was in the house when my father and mother were fighting about how much time my dad spent wrestler rather than being at home, I knew even at that young age that he was only trying to earn a living but Stephanie didn’t understand that, I guess in some way she missed him but in other ways… she was a real bitch. Trenton, New Jersey, July 1995 A view of the morning sky opens up the scene as we see the sun on its descent high into the sky, the birds are already awake as they begin to sing, it was very hot outside as it looked that another great day was upon us. A Street is then shown, showing some nice houses in a row along a road, the street name it self couldn’t be seen but it seemed like a very pleasant neighbourhood. It was also there that a future wrestling superstar known to many millions around the world, Jeffrey James Harrison the third, he lived with his wife Stephanie and his little boy, Shawn. Some may have thought that Jeff was an extremely sadistic guy but once you got to know the man you just knew he was just acting, he became a wrestler when he was much younger and had trained very hard but the 29 year old man found it difficult to reach the fame he had hoped for and at 29 years of age, he began thinking that it was beginning to be too late for him to make a real success of himself. He was thought a very talented wrestler, one of the best around but he never made it into big time, something which had boggled everyone that saw him wrestle. He had wrestled a world champion from a big promotion one time and came very close to winning if it wasn’t for the fact that his shoulder dislocated and the match had to be stopped, as Jeff nursed himself back to health he tried at it again but no one would book him because they didn’t want to hire someone who was injury prone, although that was his first injury in about five years. He wrestled for a small company called Jersey Professional Championship Wrestling or JPCW for short, a place that travelled around Jersey entertaining hundreds of fans, Jeff was too good for that place and everyone knew it but he kept at it, he was the man that was pulling in the fans, Over the years that he was away from home travelling around the state, his wife grew bitter, more and more every day and kept it locked away deep down, she knew that one day she would explode and tear him a new one even if he didn’t deserve it, she didn’t like being alone though. She had no job, with Jeff being away, she had to look after Shawn and when Shawn was at school she had no one to speak to. Sure she had friends but they all had jobs, something that she wanted dearly. As the scene goes to one particular house it was obvious by the name on the letter box that it was the Harris residence, a huge step down from the mansion he bought in Las Vegas shortly after he made it big. Jeff had just finished his tour dates with the company and had returned home after being away for three weeks, as he entered his house looking a lot different than he did a few months ago by having long black hair rather than the short spiked blonde hair, he dropped his suitcase on the floor, almost immediately Shawn came running at him and hugged him, obviously pleased to see his father, Jeff picked up Shawn by the leg and dangled him upside down as he walked around the house looking for Stephanie. Being hung upside down was something that Shawn loved, it was something only his father would do, and it was their special thing. Stephanie was in the kitchen making dinner, she had expected him home two hours ago but there was a lot of traffic getting home, Stephanie still felt bitter inside but on the outside she seemed sweet as pie. She turned to him and allowed a smile to touch the edges of her lips; Jeff put down Shawn and she walked up to him and kissed him passionately. She then hugged him then stood back. “You’re late.” Stephanie says. “Ah, I know. It was the traffic, I meant to call but I didn’t think there’d be much point; there was nothing I could do about it. Besides I wanted to wait until I heard your voice for when I could see you in person, I’ve missed you.” Jeff says, he hugs his wife. “I’ve missed you too. You should spend more time at home you know, being home for a week isn’t good.” Stephanie replies as the two separate. “I’m sorry, I love wrestling though Steph, plus it pays the bills.” Jeff says. “You know if you could a proper job, closer to home then I could get a job and help pay the bills also.” Stephanie says, Jeff didn’t like that, for someone to say wrestling wasn’t a proper job was quite ignorant, wrestling was the best job in the world in Jeff’s eyes. Plus if they both got jobs they’d be earning less money than Jeff does by wrestling, Stephanie and Jeff knew this but he knew Stephanie just wanted him to come home. “Well, I don’t want to stop wrestling, not yet anyway, besides, I have the next month off.” Jeff says. “You do?” Stephanie asks while looking happy. “Yeah! The company is sending some of their talent over to New Orleans for a few weeks, they begged me to go but I knew you wouldn’t like it, plus I really wanted to come home and see the evolution in the family!” Jeff says smiling. Jeff stared at Shawn and smiled at him, he crouched down to him and pulled something out of a bag over his shoulder, and Shawn smiled and opened the bag then pulled out a wrestling title belt. Shawn looked surprised for a moment then hugged his father; Shawn was one of the biggest fans of wrestling, he tried to go to every one of Jeff’s shows, he got to go backstage also, he was a hit with some of the other wrestlers backstage, he was their mascot. Shawn ran off up stairs with the belt in tow, Jeff stood tall and looked at Stephanie. “You bought him a belt?” Stephanie asks. “Nah, I won it, the Primetime Championship, nearly broke my damned neck winning that thing.” Jeff replies while sounding proud of him. “Aw, congratulations honey.” Stephanie says back, she then gives him a kiss on the cheek and a hug. The Primetime Championship was their version of the Heavyweight title, he beat a wrestler named Sean Storm for it, the two were good friends and would later win tag team gold together, as Shawn was upstairs, Stephanie thought it would be a good time to try and convince Jeff to stay off the road for longer than a month, they had nearly enough money to keep them going for about six months, she thought it’d be best if she didn’t try and shout at him as that could just make things worse and drive him away for longer. Jeff loved his wife and he loved his son, but he also loved wrestling, it was something he wanted to do since he was Shawn’s age, but getting married and having a child wasn’t on the top of his list, he had accidentally gotten Stephanie pregnant and she demanded to be married before the child was born, Jeff never ever thought of Shawn as an accident though, he was proud of his boy, he thought of calling him Jeffrey Harrison the fourth but he wanted to break away from that tradition of calling all the boys Jeff, so he called him Shawn Jeffrey Harrison instead. Jeff sat at the kitchen table as Stephanie got him a beer, dinner was still cooking, what they were having Jeff didn’t know. He didn’t particularly think that her cooking was anything special, far from it in fact. But he ate it because he knew she tried hard at it. Stephanie hands him the beer then sits opposite him and twirls her long curly blonde hair. “So I was thinking.” Stephanie says. “Again?” Jeff smirks. “Hey!” Stephanie shouts with a smile on her face. “Ha-ha, sorry love. Go on.” Jeff says, laughing a little. “I was thinking that maybe you could spend a little more than three weeks at home, I know you love the wrestling but seriously Jeff, you need to spend time with your son and especially with me, I love you and I miss you greatly. We have more than enough money to last us for about half a year.” Stephanie says, sounding a little false. “I don’t wrestle for the money Steph; I wrestle because it’s my life.” Jeff says. “Wrestling shouldn’t be your life anymore; you have a wife and kid.” Stephanie says back, believing that he should be getting paid a lot more than he should if he was away from his family this much. “I have a wife and kid yeah, but wrestling has always been my dream, I don’t see any need to give it up you know? I have had a great life living here and you’re the reason why I’m not out there in different parts of the world trying to make my self a bigger superstar, that one wrestling promotion is the only thing that can feed my dream, I stay in Jersey because I love you and Shawn, I don’t like being away from the two of you either but I also hoped that you understood my passion for the business, whether you like it or not, it is a real job. It’s as good as any sport there is.” Jeff says. “But I get lonely just sitting alone in the house all day, all my friends have work until the late afternoon, I want to work Jeff, but because you’re not here I have to look after Shawn. You know, parenting isn’t all about the mother looking after the child, it does take the both of us, and you looking after him for that week in a month when you’re around is not what I call good parenting.” Stephanie replies in a somewhat hard tone. Slowly but surely their voices began to raise, this wasn’t the homecoming that Jeff expected it to be, he has always felt that if someone had a problem with him then they should bring it up straight away, usually because when someone kept their anger bottled up… It usually resulted in Jeff getting a headache. “I am trying to give Shawn everything that I had and more when I was his age, my father was a wrestler and his father was a wrestling manager, so I’m away for a few weeks every month, he’s always happy to see me when I get back.” Jeff says. “Happy to see you when you get back? I’m surprised he even knows you exist, you are a terrible father Jeff and it’s pissing me off.” Stephanie says with her back to him. Jeff could feel the headache coming along, Shawn could hear his parents fighting, and he hated it when they did that but he couldn’t do anything to stop it. He was too young to hear language like he was hearing from his mother; Shawn didn’t think Jeff was a bad father, he did miss him but he loved it when he returned because when he did return it was Jeff and Shawn every day of the week. Jeff loved his son more than anything but he also loved to wrestle, if he had it his way, he’d take his son with him if it wasn’t for the fact that Shawn had school to attend. “Stephanie, just calm…” Jeff says is a quiet tone, he didn’t want Shawn to know the two were fighting although it was too late. “The hell with calming, you can either quit the wrestling business or you can fuck off in my opinion.” Stephanie shouts. “You want me to quit my dream?” Jeff asks confused. “Wrestling shouldn’t be a dream, it’s just a hobby.” Stephanie says in an angry tone. “Wrestling is my life, I can’t give it up.” Jeff answers. To Jeff, wrestling was not a hobby; it wasn’t a hobby to any wrestler. “You either give it up or you can get out.” Stephanie shouts. “…Fine.” Jeff replies. Jeff really didn’t want to quit but if it meant seeing his son, he would have to, but Stephanie had made up her mind. “In fact no Jeff, because even if you do quit you’ll still think about wrestling won’t you?” Stephanie asks, knowing that wrestling was Jeff’s first love. “Of course I will, but I’m willing to quit for you, what more do you fucking want?” Jeff says as the stress starts to get to him. “I want you to not think about wrestling and just think about your family.” Stephanie orders. “You can’t expect me to give up my dream and not think about it afterward, you should stop being such a controlling bitch and just calm the fuck down.” Jeff shouts wrestling was Jeff’s life; there was nothing else he wanted to do. Stephanie stood up angrily then marched off up stairs, Jeff just sat back looking more upset than pissed off, he sipped at his beer as he listened to the banging from upstairs, he couldn’t believe what just went off, it all started off great but because of his wife’s selfish views on parenthood he had to give up his dream, or at least nearly did, he knew what was coming, he was being thrown out, it was just a matter of time before she marched back down stairs with his suitcase and kicked him out the door. That moment wasn’t too far off either as almost immediately she came back down stairs; she threw the suitcase down first and waited for it to crash against the front door. Stephanie went back into the kitchen, Jeff stood up and took out a cigarette from a packet from his shirt pocket, and he stands up and faces her. “I guess… I’ll be leaving then.” Jeff says, hoping she would change her mind so the two could make up. “I think that’s best.” “I’ll uh, just go and say goodbye to Shawn then.” Jeff says shocked. “No you won’t, I want you to leave now.” Stephanie says while scowling, Jeff couldn’t believe the way things were happening. “You can’t stop me from saying bye to him.” “Get the fuck out, now!” Stephanie shouts, Jeff put his beer on the table then walked out of the kitchen, he thought about going upstairs but he didn’t want to get physical. “Just tell him that I love him.” Jeff says in a respectful tone. Jeff then picks up his luggage and exits the door; he had been home for less than half an hour, he couldn’t believe it, he walked to his car and put his stuff in the back of his car. He took one last look at Stephanie who then slammed the door, Jeff shook his head. Shawn runs down the stairs and hears a car driving off in the distance, he finds his mother in the kitchen fuming with anger. “Mom? Where’s dad gone?” Shawn asks. “He’s left Shawn, he left us because he wanted to wrestle rather than be with his family.” Stephanie says trying to hold back the anger. “Daddy left? But he just got here.” Shawn says, looking hurt. “He only cared about him self and only came home to tell me he was leaving.” Stephanie replies with anger in her tone. At that moment, Shawn’s eyes filled with a hurt look, tears formed at his eyes and he ran off back upstairs. The scene fades to black as Stephanie begins crying also. It was at that moment that I began to gain hatred for my father, I didn’t know how long we have left in that house after he left, I couldn’t conceive the prospect that she was lying, I was too young and gullible, I really wanted Jeff to stay right where he was, when Jeff gave me that title belt to look at which I still have to this day, that was the last time I saw him for around seven years, he missed me growing up in my teenage years and it wasn’t his fault, he offered to quit his dream just to spend time with the family but that bitch of a mother made up her mind. I thought Jeff didn’t deserve that and it must have eaten him away for years on end. Ironically it was only until she threw him out when he started making it big. He got out of the independent wrestling companies and got into a major promotion, two years later just as I got my first girlfriend, Jeff started feuding with Neu Generation, the beginning of Jeff’s ultimate demise. It sickens me that Neu Generation still lives it sickens me more that Jeff does also, it also sickens me that Stephanie made up all those lies just because she was pissed off with my dad. Just because he was trying to earn a living for his whole family and she didn’t like not seeing him she kicked him out, yeah that made a lot of sense. The minute my mother admitted lying to me I left her in a disgust, Jeff had invited her to life at his mansion and she did for a few days but then took off somewhere, I didn’t see her until my dads so called funeral. I could never forgive my mother for what she did to Jeff, I now hate her, and it’s because of her that we lived for all those years poor, just having our heads above the water. She wanted me to go with her but I wasn’t going to, I was going to become a great superstar just like my dad and with the ultimate demise of Bloodbox and a new career just about to begin in the AWC, my career is about to reach the next level.
