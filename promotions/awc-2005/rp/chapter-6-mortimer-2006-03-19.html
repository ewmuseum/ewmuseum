Chapter 6: Mortimer

19 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.19.06 Pinned squarely on his back, there was no place for Darcy Markson to go. The man had made a career out of somehow always seeming to find a way to escape these sorts of predicaments. In fact, Darcy always felt that the greatest assets he brought to the wrestling ring were not quickness, or strength, or death-defying courage, but rather his meticulous strategy and ring psychology. Whether he was seconds away from crashing to the canvas from a vicious powerslam, or hemlocked in a seemingly inescapable submission hold, Darcy was always working to stay two or three steps ahead of his opponent, always crafting an escape route, always calculating the quickest way to bring the momentum back to his side. But strategy wasn’t going to help Darcy here. With nearly 200 pounds working with gravity to push directly onto his chest, pulling a shoulder free was out of the question. There were no ropes nearby to latch onto to earn liberation from his predicament via referee mandate. And with Amy still steamed at him over his arranging of a match that would pit the two on opposite sides, Darcy sincerely doubted he could count on her to show up and rescue him if things somehow got any uglier. Actually, he had asked her to do just that, but apparently she wasn’t finished not speaking to him. Darcy was flying this one solo. Truth be told, Darcy knew he wasn’t going anywhere. And as he stared up at the toothy grin beaming down on him, he got the sense that his adversary was aware of the same fact. There’s only one way out of this, Darcy thought. Sheer, brute strength. No pain, no gain, D-Train… With that, Darcy garnered all the strength he could muster and pushed the barbell off of his chest. He exhaled sharply as he brought it back down, well aware that the ensuing lift would be far more difficult. Yet he pressed on. Just one more set, Darcy…he thought, again staring up at the killer clown face he had mounted to the ceiling to help motivate him during his bench pressing, looking intently at the name “MORTIMER” carved into his forehead, dripping with blood. Certainly gratuitous and obscenely violent, but surprisingly effective at inspiring a struggling lifter. You’re not going to let Mortimer get the best of you, are you? “2…3…4…” Darcy whispered as he completed each rep. He could feel his strength beginning to leave him though, and decided that Mortimer alone would not help him finish the task at hand. He pictured his upcoming match on Fresh!burst, standing across the ring yet again from Jack Murphy, with a chance at redemption after his dubious defeat at the hands of the TransAtlantic Champion a few weeks prior. “5…6…7…” Darcy continued as he imagined trading blows with the appropriately named “Bull” of a man, eventually gaining the upper hand and leveling Murphy with a shot to the jaw. He could almost feel the electricity from the crowd cheering him on as he stared over the fallen champion, his revenge now had. “8…9…” Just one rep away, he thought. But then another image entered his mind. Once again, he found himself standing tall over his defeated opponent, the raucous crowd screaming his name. But it wasn’t Murphy lying before him this time…it was Amy. Battered, bruised and beaten, she looked practically lifeless as he stared at her crumpled form. The crowd was loving it…but Darcy felt like there was a gaping hole in his chest. And the hole was too much for him to bear. Halfway through the last rep, Darcy mustered what little he had left to place the barbell back on the rack above him. He sighed, lying on the bench for a few moments with his hands pushed against his face, contemplating both Amy’s fate and his own in the coming days. Was this really what it’s going to come to? Darcy surmised the situation. Amy had told him that she wasn’t a wrestler – she was a fighter. The only way she knew how to go was full tilt; caring about victory first and the long-term physical effects on her opponent second…or possibly as far down the list as fifth, behind her place in the title hunt, the weather, and Canadian governmental affairs. While Darcy didn’t think himself quite as ruthless, he was also determined not to let Amy get the best of him. He was fully aware of what she was capable of in the ring, witnessing her compete numerous times in what would be more accurately described as wars rather than matches. But Darcy Crisis was no slouch himself, having come out on top in Buried Alive matches, gauntlets, and most notoriously, the Pier Six World Championship match that had seen his fall from atop a 50 foot cage. But he had survived that match…and no matter what Amy brought when the two squared off in their tag team affair, he knew he would survive that as well. But at what cost? As Darcy reached for a towel to wipe himself off, the image of a broken Aimz, broken at his own hands continued to haunt him. He was beginning to see Amy’s point – at any given moment, their match could cease being a match and in a flash become an all out war…a war that neither of them planned on losing. After all, this wasn’t their first time… Practically five years ago to the day, Darcy Crisis and Aimz faced off in another tag team match, in the confines of the California-based Pier Six Wrestling. And while the current AWC tag team affair was obviously laden with underlying drama beyond the spirit of competition, THIS match, five years prior, would practically set the standard for the animosity that existed between the two. In those days a powerful group known as The Elite were running roughshod over the company. At first the group consisted only of the Pier Six owner, Eddie “Impact” Osbourne, and Aimz, his then fiancée. But in looking for an enforcer to back him up, Osbourne saw promise in an up-and-coming superstar named Darcy Crisis, and took the man under his wing. The group was eventually rounded out with the addition of Darcy’s then-girlfriend Kaley “Kalypso” Martin. For months the foursome threw their weight around, making life miserable for anyone who stood in the way of their nefarious ambitions. But as time wore on, Darcy and Kaley found they couldn’t keep up with the sheer vileness of the owner and his soon-to-be bride, and the latter found themselves confronted by Darcy and Kaley, challenging them for supremacy of the company. So set the stage for the epic tag team match; Kalypso and Darcy Crisis against Osbourne and Aimz, who in those days worked together as the legendary tag team Kruel & Kind. In fact, the two had broken into the business together, and their high-flying, death-defying tactics were what first brought their names to the top of the marquee. On that day, Kruel & Kind proved to be too much for Darcy and Kaley, and surprisingly most of the match had been between Eddie and Darcy, with only a few punches and arm drags traded between Aimz and the Darcinator. Aimz technically had come out on top in their first real encounter, but Darcy’s first stand against her and Eddie would spark his run as a true superstar in the world of wrestling. A lot had changed in the ensuing five years. Kaley vanished from the scene a couple months after she stood with Darcy against The Elite, and Osbourne had not been heard from in years – the last time Darcy had seen him was the night he and Amy walked out on the company, hand in hand. Indeed, much had changed…but five years later, Darcy once again found himself standing against Aimz. Once again, Darcy had decided he’d had enough of her antics and was prepared to physically put an end to it. And once again, it was Darcy Crisis who had the most to prove, the most on the line as he was perhaps biting off much more than he could chew in taking on his girlfriend, of all people, as well as the TransAtlantic Champion. And that’s the key, Darcy thought, the crucial difference. She’s not just some bitch standing in my way. She’s my – the l- Darcy was purely petrified of the “l-word,” a menace that existed for him long before the groundbreaking television series revolted Darcy by allowing Mia Kirshner to continue acting. But was there really any other way to describe how he felt about Amy? And was he blowing everything he worked for by putting both Amy as well as himself in harm’s way, all over a few mind games the two had played on each other? Darcy silently cursed himself for coming up with these potential problems long after he boisterously went ahead with plans that always seemed so foolproof at the time. He could almost hear his mother again accusing him of “flying by the seat of his pants,” whatever the HELL that literally meant. But it was too late to focus on such matters. He knew he couldn’t count on Amy to show him the least bit of mercy, which left him no room for any on his part. If there was to be a war on their hands, he would have to be prepared to do whatever it took to survive, no matter who got hurt along the way. Sure, he knew he may not be able to live with himself if anything terrible happened to Amy, ESPECIALLY if it was at his own hands…but if it wasn’t her, it could be him. Am I prepared to make that kind of sacrifice? Just remember who you are, brother. Darcy…Mother…FUCKING…CRISIS. You used to be the champ. You made a statement to the world that the Darcy of old is back, and he gave Jack Murphy a run for his goddamn money. You had his ass BEAT, if it wasn’t for Amy showing her face at the wrong time, and the wrong place. This place is full of ass clowns and mid-card curtain jerkers who would love nothing more than to take you down a peg, just like Amy tried to do. NOTHING can stand in the way of the D-Masta…you can’t forget that. And when you beat her and Murphy…WHEN, this isn’t a matter of if, brother…the world won’t forget it either. Sighing, Darcy left the small workout room he’d put together in his apartment. He thought himself pretty good at coming up with pep talks for himself, and occasionally even others, having often been asked back to his old high school to inspire the kids with words of encouragement. He always thought he was simply eschewing utter nonsense, but the gymnasium was always ready to follow his speeches with thunderous applause. Perhaps they were far more inspired by what he’d done with his life rather than anything he had to say, but it nevertheless stroked his constantly attention-starved ego all the same. But finding himself just as troubled about going blow for blow with Amy, he began to believe that maybe he wasn’t quite the brilliant orator he’d been led to believe. Can things really stay the same? Will things really go back to normal once this is settled, once it’s over? Will it REALLY be over? Darcy sighed again. Maybe I’ve really screwed up this time… The voice in his head again tried to get him to focus, to get through the match had deal with these more pressing matters when he could afford to…but it was no use. He would likely be consumed with these thoughts even as his entrance music accompanied him to the ring, where he would lock eyes with his once and future adversary. Unfortunately, there was no way to get around it – two people who cared very deeply about each other might very well tear each other limb from limb. And in spite of it all, that damned Mortimer still had a shit-eating grin on his face.
