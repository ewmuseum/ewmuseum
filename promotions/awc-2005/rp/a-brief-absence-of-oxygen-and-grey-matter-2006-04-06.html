A Brief Absence of Oxygen and Grey Matter

6 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6, 2006 I had been huffing some insecticide I found in the bathroom, you know, to see if the whole “Naked Lunch” thing was true. When I finally came to, I was still a bit disoriented. My head burned like a congested dragon. Also, it was pitch black because there was a cloth bag tied around my head. And my hands were tied behind my back. But I felt something. It was plush. Nice. “Hey! Somebody help me!” I yelled, trying to attract attention, but I was shushed by someone very near to me, someone who smelled like he was important. It’s a clean smell with a slight hint of blood. “There’s no need to yell. I’m not going to hurt you.” His voice was calm, but authoritative. He continued quickly, “That would be counterproductive towards my purpose.” “Who the hell are you?” I spat out from beneath the bag. “I can’t disclose that information. If I wanted you to know who I was, I wouldn’t have put the bag over your head now, would I have?” He brought up a good point, but I guess I didn’t really care too much who he was anyway. All I knew was that I was in desperate need of a cigarette. The other man was a straight shooter, though. He got right to the point, not hesitating at all after his rhetorical question. “Right now, we are circling the block in my limo. You and I are the only ones here. The driver can’t hear us because that glass is completely soundproof. I have a loaded magnum pointed at your chest. I mean you no harm, but in the case that you do not cooperate, I’ll be forced to use it. Now, do you agree to cooperate?” “Sure,” I shot back urgently. I really didn’t seem to have much of a choice in the matter anyway. This guy was full of fucking rhetorical questions. “Good. You’re a smart man, Johnny,” he said, in a way that made me know that he didn’t think that I was a smart man at all. “So you know that ant problem your house has had recently?” “What ant problem?” “The ant problem! You’ve got fucking ants all over the place. It’s disgusting.” “Sorry, I hadn’t noticed.” “Well, why do you think you had ant killer?” I shrugged. By now, I was growing impatient and beginning to lose focus. I could only concentrate on how the cloth bag made the corners of my nose itch, and how badly I wanted to scratch it. The guy, though, sounded like he was getting a bit irked. I let him continue. I’d just feed into his game while secretly untying myself. I’d trace the location that his voice was coming from, and I’d catch him by surprise. I’d clap my hands on his temples as hard as I could, pull off the mask, take the gun, and steal his wallet. I’d use his credit cards to buy a plane ticket to Monaco where I would change my name to Giuseppe Bonfranc, a corporate officer in the investment banking business. I’d drive an expensive red coupe and I’d even get a briefcase with my monogram on it in gold leaf. It would be the perfect crime. “Never mind. The bottom line is that I did a little bit of field research. You’re a fighter, Johnny. You have few moral qualms, and you’re usually too drugged out of your mind to feel pain. I snuck into your house when you left to visit your dealer and replaced the insecticide with a general anesthetic. I knew you’d eventually try to huff the ant killer when you found it, hell, you’d huff anything, but that insecticide would have probably killed you, you fucking moron.” I stayed quiet. If he wanted me alive, maybe he was bluffing with the gun. The ropes were taut. I wasn’t making much progress. He got quieter, and his voice grew more ominous, more secretive, and more vindictive. “Now here’s what I need from you, and this information is top secret. If anyone finds out about this, you’ll be a dead man. Got it?” I nodded my hooded head. “I’ve been watching you at your new career, and I’ve got to say, I’m impressed. This is why I chose you. I’ve noticed you have a unique ability to fight and defeat women. Most men have some reserve, even if it’s subconscious, about attacking women. You lack that reserve. In society, a myth has been propagated that hitting a woman makes a man less of a man. It completely weakened the male species.” “The male species?” I inquired. I knew what he was talking about. I had seen the movie, I just didn’t think it was true. I always hated those kids who thought the Matrix was real after it came out. They’d wear trench coats or sunglasses because they have some complex which makes each one of them think that he’s Neo. I can’t stand those mother fuckers. “Yes. Males and females are two separate species. The females were far more advanced technologically. Once, Earth was full of males, roaming naked and free throughout the plentiful fields, but not in a gay way or anything. Then, Earth was invaded by an alien species. Man was overpowered and enslaved. Sex is a device created by women to control men. Men develop a craving for it; one that’s insatiable. But sexual reproduction is woman’s way of assimilating mankind into her own species.” I was more than a bit confused. What about all that shit they taught you in sex ed class when you were growing up? What about the diagrams? “How did the species of man sustain itself without reproducing?” “We reproduced asexually.” “Asexually?” “Yes. Unfortunately, as our species evolved, and we began reproducing through coitus, we lost unnecessary traits and gained new ones, like external genitalia.” I still wasn’t following, and I had found out that I was a pretty lackluster escape artist. My wrists were getting tired, and the ropes still seemed to tightly bind my hands. I had no choice but to keep listening. He could sense my confusion, even through the bag, so he went on. “Pre-corrupted man had no genitalia. Instead, the extra mass that is now the penis was attached to the brainstem and was a part of the brain. An oblong shape jutted out of the back of the bottom of man’s head. It allowed us to communicate telepathically, allowing all of mankind to share a universal mind bank. It was the real enlightenment, Johnny, a peaceful, functional, unimaginable society.” I had begun to try to figure out who this was, and whether or not this was some joke. The voice sounded vaguely familiar, but I couldn’t place it. Although, there was a strong chance that it was Tom Cruise. I wanted to tell him that he was great in Vanilla Sky, but on second thought, I realized that it probably wasn’t Tom Cruise at all. “The scent of a woman, her pheromones, degrades the bonds within the nervous system. We believe that the exterior portion of the brain slid down the spinal cord and manifested itself into the reproductive organs we have today. At first, of course, they were much smaller, much less developed. However, through generations of breeding, they’ve become larger and larger as the brain continues to corrode. Eventually, the penis will reach its maximum girth, and the mind of man will be stripped to its most basic functions. In short, man will simply be a sperm factory, solely used as a device for breeding.” “Okay.” I was confused. “Now what is it exactly that you want from me?” “Johnny, you see, the only way man can regain paradise is to rid women from our planet, and you’re the man for the job. Do you think women resist you because you’re a filthy, wretched man? Because you look like the kind of guy who would jump out of the bushes and rape someone?” “I’d say so. Yes.” “You think that’s clever? That’s not funny. You’re a jerk.” “Sorry.” “Women avoid you because you’re immune to them. And that’s dangerous. You’re worthless for breeding because you’ve smoked yourself sterile. I had some of the tissues from your garbage analyzed at the lab.” I felt a little bit awkward already, and now I was feeling completely uncomfortable about being in a car with the only other man on the planet who has handled tissues I’ve masturbated upon. “Johnny, do you have a small penis?” “No. Well, it’s average sized. Okay. Yes. But my balls are pretty big.” “I thought so,” he said, doing nothing to rebuild my self-confidence. “Your brain hasn’t degenerated as much as the rest of the species. That’s why you’re immune to the controlling powers of women. You’re the only one who can get close enough to reclaim Earth from them.” “That’d be nearly impossible. That’s over three and a half billion women to rid. And I’m only one man; one very lazy man.” “It’s not that simple, Johnny. It’s not that simple, but it’s not that complex either. You don’t have to remove all three and a half billion women. You just have to find the head. Once you find and destroy the leader, the remaining cells will die.” It was a B-Movie copout if I had ever heard one, but I decided to go along with it, because it would take up a lot less time, and I’d probably be back smoking my bong in front of the television before Walker was on anyway. “The only way to destroy the leader is to hit her with the vagina punch.” “Yeah, that’s starting to make sense,” I agreed, “but it could be anyone. Am I supposed to just roam the streets, punching every woman I see in the ovaries?” There was a short pause, and then he answered. “Yes.” He was a bit startled that I figured out his plan. Perhaps I was, in fact, “The One.” I heard the fuzz of an intercom system. “Driver, stop the limo, and then take this man home.” The limo stopped, and I heard the door open. “Now turn around. I’m going to untie you.” I shuffled around and stuck my hands out behind me. I felt the ropes drop off of my wrists. I quickly took the mask off, but I was the only one there. Then, I noticed that I wasn’t in a limo. I was in my driveway, in the back seat of my roommate’s Kia. The bottle of insecticide was dangling in my hand from my arm; hanging down to the floor. I heard the front door open, so I sat up. Hasone was a bit startled. “What the hell are you doing in my car?” “I was, uh, just taking a nap.” “Have you been huffing the ant spray?” he asked. The observant bastard caught a glimpse of the can in my hand. There was no way to talk myself out of this one, so I just looked at the can and probably mumbled something. It’s all a little hazy now. “We needed that to kill all the fucking ants, you idiot.” Hasone grabbed the empty can out of my hands and tossed it towards the garbage bins. He missed, but got in the driver’s side door anyway. I exited the vehicle and circled around to the passenger’s seat. “What are you doing? I’m going to work.” “Drop me off at the emergency room.” Hasone sighed and started the car. He began to back out of the crooked driveway, when I started patting down my own pockets. “Hold on. Let me get my cigarettes.”
