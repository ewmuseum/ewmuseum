Blessing in Disguise

11 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th July 2006 His chesty cough echoed off the wall less than six feet away. Curled up on the motel bed; his jaw resting on his knees, Brett Jackson was battling with the amount of the dust in the dingy, basic room. He had felt alone in his life before. He was an only child to a single mom; it was a fact of life that sometimes he was alone. But not since his wrestling training had begun had he felt this alone. Every day of his training and travelling he was surrounded by family. He was surrounded by those that loved him. He had been surrounded by the important people in his life, and tonight he missed them. Staring blankly at the cheap cream-paint-covered wall, Brett let out a deep, disappointed sigh. He was nervous, not an uncommon feeling for wrestlers before their matches but he had no-one to support him. His mom, dad and half-brother were all on the other side of the world. The eastern Atlantic Wrestling Club roster included Brett Jackson, yet no other Jackson-surnamed employee. He was completely on his own; which is strange when you consider he is a tag-team wrestler. Still in complete silence, Brett began to reminisce in his head hoping to comfort himself. “Just through these doors boys” encouraged their father. Greg “The Hammer” Jackson was a moderately successful wrestler. He was well-known and well-liked on the circuits he performed on, but he always lacked that spark, that something extra that would have made him one of the big boys. But to his two boys, he was as big as Hulk Hogan. Looking down at them Greg felt so proud to see their beaming faces staring directly back up. Their pale, young faces were decorated with smiles from cheek-to-cheek as they followed their father towards his gym. “Will anyone else be here?” asked the 6 year-old Brett, hopeful that he would get to see someone like The Undertaker in the gym, regardless of the fact that Greg had nothing to do with the WWF. Greg smiled. “Maybe there will, but not many. After the show last night I’d say a few guys are resting up today.” Greg directed the boys through the gym doors in the musty foyer, before sending them into the main training area. The room looked very empty with about three guys working out and training. Two guys were doing weights and one guy was toying with a punch bag, but that was enough for the two boys. Brett Chambers stood wide-eyed as he peered around the room. The three men were just as muscled as his father and that was more than enough for him. This made everything real and the icing on the cake was the wrestling ring in the centre of the room. Brett couldn’t move. He was overcome with emotion and awe as he watched his father walk towards the other men in the room. “Hey Greg,” greeted the man that was half-heartedly punching the bag as he saw “The Hammer” approaching. “Hey Tony,” nodded Greg as he turned to make sure his two sons were following him. “You not training today?” asked the Italian-American making reference to Greg’s attire, which was a pair of cream trousers and a sky-blue shirt. “No, no…” Greg shook his head and turned to Brett pulling him into view. “I’m spending time with my sons.” “Sons?!? I only thought you had one boy.” Tony stopped his dodgy punches and held the bag in his arms as he laid eyes on Brett Jackson. 'So which one are you? Stephen or his little brother?' 'This is his half-brother, Brett,' smiled Greg, gently pushing Brett in the back, forcing him forward to stand alone, looking dough-eyed at his dad's wrestler friend Tony. 'I'm not quite sure where Stephen is...' shrugged Greg as he swung his head around, trying to find his first son. 'Knowing him,' smiled Tony, 'he's probably climbing on the ring ropes.' And surely enough when Greg and Tony turned to look at the ring Stephen Lawrence was standing on the second rope in the corner with his arms in the air, celebrating an imaginery victory. The two men chuckled to themselves as Brett stood nervously at the feet of his father, lacking any confidence in himself to going run around the gym. 'He does enjoy winning,' smiled Greg as he headed towards the ring with Tony and Brett following behind. 'Who are this week?' smiled Greg. 'You,' smiled Stephen as he hopped out of the turnbuckle and climbed through the ropes, 'and I just beat Tony with a Piledriver!' 'Really?' smiled Greg,simply proud of the fact his son was dreaming of being him. 'Doesn't sound realistic to me,' smirked Tony, 'that boy really does have an over-active imagination.' The two wrestling friends shared a laugh as Brett simply stood in silence and watched his brother hop to the floor. 'Have you been here before?' asked Brett as Stephen approached. 'Of course,' shrugged Stephen, 'everytime dad is in town I come down here. Come on... I want to show you something...' At the time, Brett had been upset about his brother having been there more, but looking back it made sense. Stephen was older and Brett's mum was apprehensive about his relationship with his father so now he understood why Stphen knew the wrestlers better but still that was the moment, even at only 6 years old that he knew he wanted to be a wrestler. The size of the men, and even the size of his dad was something to behold. It was where he wanted to be. It was what he wanted to do. And that was where he was. Granted, he was more alone that he would have ideally liked but he knew that his family would be watching the show when he performed. 'Hey guys, this is my little brother, Brett!' Stephen had introduced him to everyone at his wrestling school as his little brother. It had felt weird to hear him call that, considering they weren't that close. And it felt even weirder when they started to perform together. Infact, Brett was known to everyone as Stephen Lawrence's little brother. That was until they both agreed to tag up. And then it hit Brett like a brick wall. All of his life he had been known as the 2nd member of the Jackson brothers. To everyone he had came second behind his half-brother, until now. The eastern Atlantic Wrestling Club would meet Brett Jackson before they met Stephen Jackson. Originally he had felt forced to be alone. But the more he thought about it, it seemed like a good idea.
