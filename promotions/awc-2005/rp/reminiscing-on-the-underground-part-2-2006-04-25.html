Reminiscing on the Underground (Part 2)

25 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/25/2006 The camera slowly fades in as a door swings open and a slightly younger version of Vince Jones slowly steps in through the doorway with a black baseball cap turned around backwards on his head. He must be about the age of 21 or 22. He is wearing a black tank top that fits him snug a pair of blue jeans and black Nike sneakers on his feet. Some loud underground hip-hop beats can be heard bumping through throughout the air. The camera zooms in on Vince Jones as he slowly looks around the vicinity and gets acquainted with his surroundings. He nods after he finally adjusts to the sights around him. Vince Jones: So, this the place I been hearin’ so much about? Damn. They didn’t tell me about all the other shit that be goin’ down here. This must’ve been K-9’s lil secret hangout spot back in the day. The camera pans around the dark and smoky pit that’s full of action at every corner. The whole floor is crawling with thugs, punks, ex-cons, terrorists, the homeless, freaks, and all the other underground scum of the city. In one corner of the room a man can be seen trying to sell some of his “hottest” items, like TVs, car stereos, jewelry, etc. to anyone that’s willing to name the right price. The camera continues to pan and catches sight of a bunch of street thugs standing in front of a table with what looks to be illegal firearms and other assorted weaponry. The camera continues to pan as a man is seen beating another man’s head into the ground in fury and jacking him of his money and his jewelry. Hanging from the ceiling you can see t.v screens that are displaying some gruesome highlights from some recent pit fighting matches. On one screen a large Hispanic man can be seen laying flat on the ground struggling with all his might to try and keep his neck from being slit by his opponent that almost has him overpowered at the moment. One the screen beside that one a large, beefy metal head can be seen Jackknife powerbombing his opponent onto the concrete floor leaving him unconscious and spitting on him in fury as a crowd of hundreds of screaming maniacs go nuts. The camera continues to pan around the vicinity and a few businessmen can be seen standing in front of some betting booths placing their bets on the prize fights of the night. The camera continues to pan and posters of different prize fights can be seen littered all about the walls covering up some the graffiti that is covers all the walls like wallpaper. The camera continues to pan a little more and a pimp in a bright purple suit and purple hat with a large feather in it can be seen putting his horde of hoes to work and disciplining others with b*tchslaps across their faces that nearly give them whiplash. The camera continues to pan and catches sight of Vince Jones who is looking around at the place in amazement. All of a large breasted Hispanic girl approaches Vince Jones and begins caressing his chest. Hispanic ho: Aye papi! You caught my eye from across the room. What’s your name? You look like you really know how to play rough. Vince Jones: Vince, Vince Jones, but they used to call me V.J back in the day when I was with the clique. Hispanic ho: (in confusion) The clique? What clique are you talkin’ about? This is New York everyone around here got some kinda clique they claimin’. Vince Jones flashes the gang symbol of his old New York City gang and the ho’s eyes quickly light up with enthusiasm and shock. Vince Jones: V.J don’t roll with just anybody, baby. He only rolled with the best of ‘em and that's real Hispanic ho: You were down with them? But, I thought all of their group was locked up behind bars. How the..? That’s impossible. Vince Jones: Hey! I got lucky. Good behavior’ll drive a man crazy, but when you want out The Pen ya gotta do what ya gotta do. That’s the way it is in this game. You gotta find away around the system one way or anotha. Ya know? Just got out the pen ‘bout a month or so ago. Life is hell bein’ on the outside once you been on the inside. Things just ain't the same. The ho nods in agreement and begins to smile in Vince’s face as she puts her arms around his neck and looks up at him with a seductive gaze Hispanic ho: I see. Life is hell all the way around, but you know what’ll make everything better? The ho begins to lean in as if she’s going in for a kiss. Hispanic ho: A woman, a woman like me. You got money on ya right now? If you got enough you and I can have a little bit of fun together. I’m sure I can show you a good time, papi, if you let me. We can do this shit any way you like it. How about that? Vince Jones looks at her in disgust and takes her arms from around his neck and just shakes his head. Vince Jones: Maybe lata, baby. Right now V.J gotta find the man that runs this joint. V.J plannin' on holla’n at this cat and seein’ what’s what around here. The Hispanic prostitute watches Vince Jones walk away in disappointment. Vince Jones doesn’t even take five steps before a young pickpocket slips behind him and tries to steal Vince’s wallet. All of a sudden Vince quickly reaches around behind him and snags the culprit by his wrist. Vince turns around and lifts the young boy up off his feet by his wrist and sneers at him. Vince Jones: What the hell you think you doin’, son? The boy looks at Vince’s eyes in fear. The boy: Don’t hurt me!!! I was only playin’!! Vince Jones: I know the game, kid! I know it inside and out! Fuck around with a man like me again and you gonna be short one hand! Get it!? Got it!? Good! The boy quickly nods his head in agreement. The boy: Yes sir! Vince Jones: Now roll the fuck on, kid before I have your little b*tch ass floatin’ down a stream or somethin’! Vince Jones releases his tight grip on the boy’s arm and drops him on the ground in a heap. The boy quickly gets up to his feet and scurries away. Vince Jones just shakes his head and looks around the vicinity to see if he can find someone that can help him out. He turns towards a man in a leather jacket, ripped jeans, and Mohawk. Vince puts an arm on his shoulder and the man whips around and pulls out a knife on Vince. Vince Jones takes a step back and looks down at the brandished knife. Vince Jones: Put that shit away before I have to rip that shit off ya and cut you a second smile! I ain’t lookin’ for a fight here! I need to find some shit out! The punk slowly slips the knife away and looks at Vince Jones uneasily. Punk: What are you trying to find out? Vince Jones: Who the hell runs all this shit? I’m tryin’ to get hooked up. The punk shrugs his shoulders. Punk: How the hell should I know that? The punk turn and walk away. Vince Jones look around the vicinity some more and turns and catches sight of a bearded man that is at a small table selling stolen gun parts, guns, bullets, scopes, and other accessories. Vince Jones slowly pushes his way through the crowded floor towards the man at the table. He finally reaches the table and the man looks up at him with a stern look on his face. Bearded man: So, you see somethin’ you like here or what? Vince Jones: No Bearded man: Well, If you don’t I suggest you back the fuck away from this table so I can get some more customers! Got it!? You cuttin' in on my business, young blood! Now get going! Vince Jones’ eyes light up in fury and he seizes the man by the collar of his shirt in fury startling the man. Vince Jones: Bitch!? I got your bitch right here! I’m about to bitchslap the taste out your damn mouth! Now call me a bitch one more time and see what’s up! I wish you would! Vince Jones releases him and the man looks at him with a startled expression on his face. Vince Jones: Now shut the hell up and listen! I’m here lookin’ for the man that runs all this! Where’s he at!? I gotta talk business with the man! The bearded vendor regains his composure and clears his throat as he straightens up his t-shirt. He then glares at Vince Jones uneasily. Bearded man: So, you wanna know who runs the NYUFC, huh? How you figure I’m gonna tell you jack shit after what you just did to me, punk? You think runnin' shit or somethin', huh? I'm runnin' things now! Vince Jones begins to flare up in rage again, but he tries to calm himself down and act civil for a change. Bearded man: Hmmm..Well, if you wanna know the deal it’s gonna cost ya. I’m a business man here. I do business. That’s how I make my living. Vince Jones: You sell guns and gun parts bitch! How the fuck is all that gonna help me out? Bearded man: I also sell information. So, what’s this bit of info worth to ya? Vince Jones: This ridiculous, man! Bearded man: Hey! You the one that wanted to be all gung ho and militant and shit! Well, if you want this info I got you gonna have to pay up. Now what's it gonna be? Vince Jones rolls his eyes and reaches in his pockets. He pulls out a crumpled up five- dollar bill and tosses it in front of the vendor. He looks down at the bill and frowns at it and then at Vince. Bearded man: Come on, man. You gonna try to bribe me with chump change like this? Well, I guess it’s worth something. (cups his chin) Let's see... The bearded man picks up the bill and stuffs it in his pocket and grins a gap-toothed grin. Bearded man: The man you’re looking for is named Big Lex. Vince stands there and gazes at the man impatiently. Vince Jones: And? So, his name is Big Lex. Where the hell can I find the man? That don't help me. The vendor looks at Vince Jones and laughs outloud. Bearded man: Oh, so now you need a little more info? Is that what you want? Well, that’s gonna cost ya some more. This info don’t come for free. Vince Jones looks at the vendor with irritated expression on his face. He reaches in his pocket and whips out a crumpled up twenty-dollar bill. He slams it down on the table and the vendor grins back at him. Bearded man: Well, now we’re starting to get somewhere here. If you tryin’ to talk to Big Lex. You’re gonna wanna go down that hallway over there. The bearded man points to a hallway across the large room in a corner past the large crowd of hoodlums and assorted bad characters of New York City. There are two large men in black business suits with dark shades on their faces and slicked back hair standing in front of the entrance to the small hallway watching everyone intently to make sure no intruders come through and get to their boss. Vince Jones turns in the direction of the hallway and then turns back to the bearded vendor and nods. Vince Jones: Just over there? Okay. That’s cool. Well, I’m out. Good deal. Vince Jones turns and is about to walk away, but the bearded man grabs his hand and clears his throat to get his attention. Vince turns back towards the bearded man and looks down at him impatiently. Vince Jones: What the hell do you want now, huh!? The bearded vendor chuckles to himself and just shakes his head. Bearded man: Now let’s be real here. You think those two just gonna let anyone waltz into their boss’ office uninvited with all the illegal shit that’s goin’ on down here? How the hell they know you ain’t with the feds? If you expectin’ to talk to Big Lex you gonna have to give them the secret code word to pass through. Vince Jones rolls his eyes at the vendor. Vince Jones: Okay. What the hell is the uckin’ code word??? Oh, you gonna charge me some shit for that info too, b*tch?? The bearded vendor chuckles to himself. Bearded man: Well, I was gonna give it to ya for free, but now that you mention it, that’s gonna cost ya too. I mean this some prime info I've got here. Come on, buddy. Fork over the cash. Vince Jones quickly reaches in his pocket and whips out another crumpled up twenty and slams it down on the table in fury Vince Jones: There! Is that good enough!? The bearded gun vendor grins and nods in agreement as he grabs the twenty from off the table. Bearded man: That’ll do just fine. Now, when you go over there make sure you tell them Troy sent you. Got it? Vince nods and looks at the bearded vendor uneasily he then turns and walks away as the bearded man calls out to him and laughs. Bearded man: Nice doing business with ya. Vince Jones pushes his way through the crowd and makes his way to the hallway across the large room and in the far corner. The two guards see Vince Jones approaching from a far and they stand firmly in front of the entrance of the hallway with their massive arms folded across their large chests. Both men look as though they stand nearly seven tall each. Both of the man look down at Vince Jones with stern expressions on their faces and Vince Jones looks up and returns the gaze. The guard on the left begins to speak. Left guard: So, what brings you here, huh? Who sent you here? Vince Jones: Troy sent me. Right guard: Troy? I see. Well, go right on through. The two guards step to the side and Vince Jones makes his way down the lighted hallway that’s plastered with NYUFC event posters from past and present. He takes a right and make his way down the end of the hallway and reaches a door marked “Big Lex”. Vince Jones reaches out, turns the knob, and opens the door. He steps in the doorway and take a look around the fully-furnished office. In front of him sits a desk that’s loaded with paperwork. On his right sits a small filing cabinet. Behind the desk sits the big man himself, Big Lex, who is looking back at Vince Jones with a pleased expression on his face. He is wearing a gray pin-striped business suit and has a shiny bald head with a well-trimmed goatee on his brown-skinned face. Vince Jones steps forward and looks down at Big Lex. Vince Jones: So, you’re Big Lex? The big man nods in agreement and grins at Vince as he leans back in his desk chair.] Big Lex: In the flesh. And you must be V.J. Vince raises an eyebrow in confusion. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa. How the hell do you know who I am? Big Lex laughs to himself and stands to his feet and looks Vince dead in the eyes. Big Lex: I’m connected. I run this city from the ground up, V. Consider me the heart of New York. I’m the man that puts the sleeplessness in the Big Apple, been doing so for a long, long time. Vince Jones: Hold up, Biggie! That’s where ya wrong! My clique and I was runnin’ this game longer than you can imagine! Big Lex shakes his head and chuckles to himself as Vince Jones flashes the gang symbol of his old gang. Big Lex: I like you. You really amuse me. You mean to tell me that you believe that you and the rest of that little rag tag gang of K-9's was ever in charge of New York City? Son, don’t you get it? Have you gotten the chance to take a good look around the place yet? I’m runnin’ an empire here. That clique of yours existed and made its name on the streets of NYC only because I allowed it to be that way. If it wasn't for me there would no EDW. Vince Jones flares up in rage at his comments and slams his fist on the desk. Vince Jones: BULLSHIT!!!! Big Lex laughs to himself at Vince Jones’ reaction to his statements. Big Lex: Yeah. I figured that would’ve been a little hard for you to chew, but it’s all good. Calm yourself. You’re in Big Lex’s house right now. Vince Jones: Well, if you were the reason behind our EDW and allowed our clique to exist and you got all this power and shit then why the fuck did K-9 and the rest of us get locked up in the first place, huh? How you explain that? It ain't wastin' away behind bars, mah fucka! Big Lex: (nods) That is true. Well, as for you and the rest of the EDW someone in your clique fucked up! That's why you all ended up getting locked up. Its as simple as that. Don't get caught and there are no consequences. See, I had given K-9 a lil assignment for you all to take care of. Vince Jones looks at him in confusion. Vince Jones: Assignment? Big Lex: Yes, an assignment, V.J. Remember that little bank heist? Well, the idea was for you all to break that bank, get the money, and have it brought to me. Then, I would've taken it upon myself to give you all a generous cut of the booty. We hit a little snag though. Turns out there was a snitch in your crew. And that's how you all landed behind bars. I mean, I can only do so much in a case like this. I'm a businessman,like I said and business has to keep moving. I can't stop for losses. (shakes his head) Now, enough about this sh*t. What can I do for you? You lookin’ for a way to make some cash? You lookin’ for a job in the NYUFC? I mean, I guess its the least I could do to make up for your hardships. Vince Jones: (confused) A job? How’d you know? Big Lex: V.J, you’re one of us. You’re just like each and everyone of us down here. You’ve come out The Pen, been invited back on the outside, but the people on the outside don’t wanna see people like you and me succeed. They wanna see us starve to death. They wanna see us dead. They wanna see us rot in the flames of hell. Our kind gotta stick together. No matter who they are or where they come from they all end up here, in my house lookin’ for employment. So, you down or not? I can give you all that you’ve been denied since being released. I can give you an opportunity to make it if you allow me to. All you gotta do is say that you’re down and it’s a done deal. So, are you down? Big Lex extends his large hand towards Vince Jones. Vince Jones looks down at Big Lex’s hand and then looks up at Big Lex rather uneasily. Vince finally shakes his hand in agreement. Vince Jones: I’m down with this sh*t! Whateva it takes to make some cash. Big Lex: That’s what I like to hear. All of a sudden the door of the office swings open and a large man comes walking in with a leather jacket, blue jeans, and shoulder length brown hair. He stops in his tracks and looks at Vince Jones in fury. The man: Big Lex, who the hell is this b*tch!? Big Lex looks past Vince and catches sight of one of his employees standing in the door looking back at him in frustration. Big Lex grins at the man and beckons for him to come closer. The big man comes walking over towards the desk and Vince Jones and the big man face each other and glare at each eye-to-eye. Big Lex: Jack, meet V.J. He’s the newest man to join the empire. You two may get to know each other real well in due time in The Lion’s Den. Jack looks at Vince Jones in disgust. Jack: This piece of shit? Hmmph! Vince Jones looks Jack up and down in anger. Vince Jones: Who you callin' a piece of shit, huh? Jack: Look here…V.J? When a man like me addresses street trash like you you just shut the hell up and listen! Now, you stay out of my damn way in the NYUFC and you just might survive around here long enough to be somethin’! Despite your lil outburst I still believe that me being the champ around here ought to extend my hand and welcome you. Its the least I can do. Now if you lookin’ to learn a little somethin’ come on out to my next match, son! Next lesson’s free of charge, junior! I’m dedicating this match to you, sunshine! Everything that happens to that chump out there tonight could happen to you if you're not smart! Jack turns and storms out the room and slams the door behind himself. Vince Jones shakes his head and turns back towards Big Lex. Big Lex: That was our champ. He’s the main attraction around here. He came here to me asking for a job just about a year or so ago in the same position you’re in right now and now look at him. He’s the man in NYUFC right now. Well, you heard the man yourself. You’ve been invited to see the champ in action. Let’s go and enjoy the show. Vince Jones nods his head in agreement, but is still boiling with anger over his meeting with Jack. Vince Jones: Yeah. I wanna see what this bitch is made of in there tonight. Big Lex: Calm down, V.J. Calm down there. If you get off to a good start in the NYUFC and get the job done when you need to you’ll be facing him in due time and have your shot at glory; but right now it’s time to just sit back, relax, and observe. Vince Jones cracks his knuckles in fury and the two of them exit the room as the camera slowly fades out to black.The camera fades back in and we are in a small arena where hundreds of screaming maniacs are seen on their feet going wild and crazy in anticipation for the next NYUFC match. Loud hip-hop beats bump through out the air of the large arena from every corner. A large jumbotron can be seen hanging on the back wall above a small entryway. Crates, chains, baseball bats, and other assorted weaponry can be seen littered about the large, ringless arena floor. All of a sudden a man with black jeans, boots, a white t- shirt, sunglasses on his face and long black hair appears through the entranceway and makes his way to the middle of the arena floor with a mic in hand to announce the next match. Announcer: Heeey yooooooooooo! You all know what time it is here in The Lion’s Den of the NYUFC! That’s right! It’s time for the main-event death match! All the people in the arena stand to their feet and begin yelling at the top of their lungs. Announcer: The winner of this match gonna be leavin’ with all the loot while the loser could be leavin’ in a stretcher. Anything’s fair game in The Lion’s Den. The camera switches to the inside of a special luxury box where Big Lex and The Violence can be seen standing side-by-side watching the activity on the floor. Big Lex looks down at the arena’s floor in amusement and turns towards Vince Jones. Big Lex: So, what are your first impressions of your new home, V.J? You liking it so far? You anxious and ready to get into your first prize fight here in the NYUFC. Vince Jones just stands there in silence and watches the arena floor intently as the challenger, who is a large black haired man wearing a dog collar around his neck, black jeans, and boots appears and raises his arms in the air as the crowd watches him in awe. Vince Jones: Place looks nice, but a guy like V.J ain’t here for the looks, he’s here to see some action and get paid. Big Lex chuckles to himself and puts a hand on Vince Jones’ shoulder. Big Lex: Oh, don’t worry. You will… The camera switches back to the floor of the arena where the lights slowly are dimmed. Announcer: And now here comes your NYUFC reigning champion, standing in at 6’9” and weighing in at 285lbs, JACK “ THE HELLRAISER” WRIGHT!!! The whole arena erupts with enthusiasm as video of flames begins to play on the large jumbotron. The camera zooms in on the entranceway as the man we saw before with long, brown hair appears with black jeans and is shirtless exposing his heavily tattooed and red body-painted figure. He stands there with his eyes closed breathing heavily as if he’s getting pumped, psyched, and ready to seek and destroy. All of a sudden his eyes open wide with fury and he comes charging at his opponent as the lights come back on. His opponent tries to fend himself but he ends up getting speared to the ground and Jack begins pounding the crap out of him with blow after blow to blow to the head. He quickly lifts the man up to his feet and whips him into the barricade. He follows him in with a clothesline that makes the crowd go “OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHH!!” Jack Wright begins backhand chopping his now bleeding opponent in the head like a man possessed and leaves him drapped against the barricade. The camera switches back to the luxury box where Big Lex and Vince Jones can be seen watching the action down on the floor still. Big Lex: So, what do you think of our champion so far? Vince nods and creeps closer to the glass and watches from above as Jack Wright is totally dismantling his opponent with ease down below. Vince Jones just stands there in silence, fury, and hatred for the champ. The camera switches back to the floor where Jack Wright is seen now choking his opponent with a large chain that he has drapped around the man’s neck now. He throws him to the floor and begins beating him across the back as the man yells out in pain and agony at the top of his lungs. Each lashing by the chain opens a new, fresh wound on the man’s already battered body. Jack Wright begins laughing at the top of his lungs at the carnage he’s created in the middle of The Lion’s Den tonight. He begins kicking the man who is writhing on the concrete floor in a whole new world of pain right now. Jack Wright: Get up! Get up you worthless piece of crap! I ain’t finished with ya yet! Get up bitch! The man tries to get up to his feet and Jack begins slapping him in the back of the head to the amusement of the crowd. The man suddenly lunges and tries to tackle Jack to the floor, but Jack Wright just sidesteps him and laughs. Jack Wright: You piece of crap! You call this a fight!? You’re not even on my level! This is my house! You hear me? My house! All of a sudden the screaming maniacs in the arena begin to chant “KILL HIM!!!” over and over again to the amusement of Jack Wright. Jack Wright grabs his battered and beaten opponent by his hair and lifts him up to his feet. He immediately kicks him in the gut and nails him with a jumping double-arm underhook DDT on the concrete floor that leaves the man unconscious in a bloody heap. The crowd watches in awe and Jack Wright begins stomping away at his bloodied up opponent in fury. He then spits on the man as the crowd goes wild at the beating their champion delivered. The camera switches back to the luxury box where Vince Jones and Big Lex can still be seen watching the floor. Big Lex laughs outloud. Big Lex: Ahhh, another victory for the champ. Damn! That man’s been rakin’ in the dough for me. Big Lex points down at Jack Wright who can be seen walking around the arena floor in celebration of yet another victory in the NYUFC. Big Lex: Take a good look, V.J. That man down there is the heart and soul of the Lion’s Den. Many men have come to me looking for employment, but none of them have ever been able to stack up to The Hellraiser. That man is NYUFC. Vince Jones looks down and comes close to the window. The camera switches to the arena floor where Jack Wright stops in his tracks turns around and looks dead on at Vince Jones who is watching him from above in fury. Jack Wright looks up at him with the same gaze of fury and hatred. The camera switches to the luxury box window where Vince Jones is seen watching as Jack Wright does a slow and steady cutthroat motion and points up at him. Vince Jones smacks the glass of the window in fury and Big Lex chuckles to himself as he tries to calm him down. Big Lex: Calm down there. Calm down. You’ll get your chance. You’ll get your chance to fight the man in due time. Vince Jones: Fuck this sh*t!!! Fuck it! I want him now! The Violence smacks the glass window in fury and he suddenly snaps back to reality and looks around in confusion. All is silent. 'Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright was the man to beat back in the NYUFC and V. Jones wanted more than anything to be the man to topple him. From that day forward V was determined that he'd do whateva the hell it would take to get that opportunity. And that shit was real!' The camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones sits and remains in the corner of the ring in silent thought. To Be Continued...
