Date: 7th March 2006 Transitional Champion: def : Of or relating to characterised by transition. To bridge the gap from one wrestlers reign to another. This is a transitional champion. Being a transitional champion and going down in history as one for your career isn’t such a terrible thing. Many famous ones have passed through this business we call pro wrestling. Outside even of our fair haven of AWC exist some of the best. Many times it’ll be said if it wasn’t for the Iron Sheik there would’ve been no Hulkamania. And without Hulkamania there would probably be no business for us to be working in. Even in the hallowed halls of AWC history we’ve had important transitional champions. Without Ian English who knows what would’ve happened to Mike Wade and the Relentless title. Ian English was a stopgap between former champion Steven Xandrous and the man who made the Relentless title, Mike Wade. English was a champion, yes, but only to be fed to Wade. Above that, the big one, the Transatlantic title. Many people have said it that the man who Transitioned first was the Educator. He was a stopgap between Pierce Lavelle’s first reign and his famous second reign. His 11 day laughable reign as champion was only to be a transitional champ to lead on to a second Lavelle domination. Those people though are wrong. By very definition a transitional champion bridges a gap between two different men. To usher in a new era to the sport. The Educator beat Pierce Lavelle only to lose it straight back, therefore he is not a transitional champ, but a short term one. A shit one, but a champion all the same. The cheekier amongst us have even claimed that my partner and heterosexual life partner, Adam Dick, is the latest AWC transitional champion. They say he was put there to only hold the belt up between Lavelles reign of pure dominance and bridge the gap to Jack Murphy’s era. However those people are wrong too. The Lavelle era of dominance was supposed to be followed by the Dick era of dominance and destruction. However Jack Murphy somehow fluked his way into the picture. Yes by definition this makes Adam Dick a transitional champion, maybe. Why maybe? Because Tuesday night the 7th of March 2006 Mike Wade can save the blushes of Adam Dick. Maybe Jack Murphy is the transitional champion. In fact not maybe, he is! Adam Dick will go down in history as the man who ended Pierce Lavelle’s reign and etched himself a place in history as King Dick. However Jack Murphy, he will go down as the man who bridged the gap between King Dick and the unstoppable force known as Mike Wade. Jack Murphy is a man who shouldn’t have the belt in the first place, but it won’t matter, because Tuesday night Murphy isn’t a wrestler, Murphy is a belt rack for the man who will etch himself a place in the AWC Hall of Fame by becoming the first ever Grand Slam champion. That man is Mike Wade. He’s come a long way baby! ***The Story of Mike Wade*** Note: Some names of wrestlers and promotions have been changed so Mike couldn’t get sued. Two words come to mind when you think of Mike Wade though, His Swerviness!” Mikes face took a grimace and without a second thought we spat straight into the camera lens. As his saliva dripped down the screen he stood straight. His back straight as as arrow and his voice raised, he was ready. He’s swerved us all again. “Don’t have sympathy for me. I let you look into what my life was for a second, to show you what this title means to me. This is the culmination of almost 7 years work. I care about one thing tonight, not the roar of the crowd, not my body, certainly not Jack Murphy. The one thing that matters is that tonight I come out as Transatlantic champion. My plight was not to have the crowd cheer me as I complete my boyhood dream. My plight was to show you how much I wanted it. I showed you that. Now watch one and all as I take my place as #1. Nobody will help Jack Murphy not his dead trainer, his dead dog or his pet goldfish. Tonight is his curtain call and tonight I’m standing tall. 2 Irish men battling till the end. A civil war of sorts. A great Irishman, poet WB Yeats wrote: Romantic Ireland’s dead and gone, it’s with O Leary in the Grave, He wrote it in 1915. But tonight I reverse Yeats words: Romantic Ireland’s back and bright, they’re watching Mike Wade win tonight! See you tonight Jack, see you tonight. -From the mind of Mike Wade.
