Mid-Career Crisis

13 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/2006 MID CAREER CRISIS FADE IN: INT. THE BACK OF A CHRYSLER 300 - DAY the rear-view mirror revels the hidden eyes of JARRETT CLEMANS, from here on out known as B.O.T. He’s obviously a made man, his sunglasses probably the same price as one of the rims on the car he‘s in. The purple tint does little to hide the beams of annoyance radiating from his eyes. A small bounce reveals a staunch snarl on his squared jaw… CLOSE NEXT TO B.O.T. from his left, coming from the seat up. His mighty left arm, dressed in a light grey blazer is wrapped around a small woman, SASHENKA, who rests her beautiful head on his chest as she slumbers through his impatience… CHASE SHOT from the left side of the car. The camera pans down the side slowly, the deep purple paint almost hypnotizing, and is another sure sign of our B.O.T.’s wealth. As the car makes a sharp right, the camera stays at the top of the street, watching the 300 disappear into what is revealed to be tattered streets. The camera slowly rises until the blocks in view are seen in full. Not a house is standing. Most lots, just disheveled stacks of wood and debris, the street next to them barely visible under the rampant trash. NEW ORLEANS - 2 WEEKS AGO EXT. FRENCH QUARTER - DAY B.O.T. and Sashenka stand before an older man and women, BILL and IRENE, who are reciving a white envolope from out visitors. BILL You don’t understand how much this means to us, Sash. SASHENKA Don’t thank me, it was his money. BILL Ill thank everyone involved openly. 1.5 million is a godsend. B.O.T. Its nothing, really. B.O.T. sticks his hands in his pockets, Sashenka shakes Bill and Irene’s hands. Before the pair can do the same to B.O.T., he takes a step back. B.O.T. If you could excuse us, we need to hurry to the airport, I’ve got to be someplace. IRENE Are you sure you won’t stay for lunch? B.O.T. (hastily) Already ate, thanks. He grabs Sashenka by the arm and drags her with him. BILL (as B.O.T. and Sash walk back to the car) Well, thanks again Sash! SASHENKA Your very welcome! See you later! After a sly nod, the driver opens the backdoors of the Chrysler 300 and in slides the couple. As the door slams shut, so does Sashenka’s fist against B.O.T.’s arm. SASHENKA What the hell was that, jackass!? B.O.T. (rubbing his arm) Look, I told you id help you, not that id like it. SASHENKA And I thank you for it, but try not to be so damn grumpy about it. B.O.T. Im sorry… Shady dealings in the middle wastelands aren’t my idea of a picnic. SASHENKA It was charity, for gods sake! B.O.T. Charity for who? Ray Nagin and company? The guys that put this city in its position to begin with? SASHENKA Ill have you know, Bill and Irene are legit humanitarians. B.O.T. I bet… SASHENKA Look, im not getting into a political argument with you right now. You should feel good about yourself. You did the right thing for a change. B.O.T. Pssh… The front door shuts, and the DRIVER sits in his seat. DRIVER Sir? B.O.T. Jackson Square, please. Think I need a nap. FADE OUT. Darkness. The sound of a shower running. B.O.T. (V/O) Sometimes, I wonder why the hell I think some of the things I do. Riding through the city streets of the place, when your Geraldo Riveras and Anderson Coopers were visably and vocally upset about the lack of haste when it came to saving these victims lives that faithful day, I couldn’t concur. I’m sure whenever anyone looks out to the wreckage, they feel sorrow and remorse. Me? I can’t help but be a little jealous. FADE IN: INT. BATHROOM OF A HOTEL SWEET A low shot of the wall from floor level. The shower head squeaks to a halt,, and out steps B.O.T., though only his feet can be seen. The camera follows his feet to the sink. B.O.T. (V/O) Jealous of her. Katrina. That she can strike so much fear into so many people. Leave such a devastating mark in the minds of so many victims. TOP DOWN SHOT Of B.O.T. shaving in front of the mirror. B.O.T. (V/O) If I wish I could annihilate on such a grand scale. I want to be feared and respected like a Category 3 Hurricane.. He nicks his impeccable chin with the razor. B.O.T. (V/O) When I think things like that, I start to figure its just a case of me being hit in the head too many times. That or I’m finally going insane, like all my old bosses used to tell me. I don’t doubt the latter… RIGHT PROFILE SHOT Of B.O.T. washing what’s left of the shaving cream off of his face. CUT TO : A MID LEVEL FOLLOW SHOT Of B.O.T. walking down the hallway towards the camera. B.O.T. (V/O) Truth is, I don’t need to be a hurricane to be destructive. I am destructive. Hell, 13 years now I’ve struck fear into the hearts of whoever was unfortunate enough to be announced as my opponent. I’ve beaten down and flooded out wannabe’s dreams like they were their own little Cities of Delusion. I’m B.O.T, I’m not jealous of anyone. I’m unable to be jealous of anyone. Why? Because I’m fucking better than everyone. CLOSE SHOT: B.O.T. (V/O) Richer… Sexier… and just damn better! B.O.T. walks into the shot, and the scene FADES OUT. FADE IN This time, with a little soundtrack. “The Payback” by James Brown reverberates as the camera chases B.O.T.’s well dressed legs through what seems to be some sort of gymnasium. A WIDE SHOT laters reveals our hero’s location. INT. THE BROTHERS SCHOOL OF WRESTLING - DAY ONE WEEK AGO B.O.T. is trotting though the wrestling school, past large, drabby wrestling rings, where the sweat blood and tears of young hopefuls pour like Viagra at the country club. Heavy bags, weight equipment, and the culprit stereo system pumping out the James Brown jams spot the floor in aesthetic locals. B.O.T. slides his wool tweed coat off of his shoulders and gives a nod one of his favorite trainers. The camera follows his view towards a beautiful, short, Asian woman. She waves, and the camera shifts back to the focus, a humble little office off in a corner. B.O.T. pops the door open. INT. BSW STAFF OFFICE - DAY Close shot of a giant stack of folders. B.O.T. (V/O) What the hell is that? Mid Shot of a older dreadlocked man, B.O.T.’s brother and best friend DIMITRI studying a pair of papers. DIMITRI You asked me to use my connections and find you some companies of interest, right? B.O.T. strolls into the shot, hanging his jacket on the back of a leather office chair. He descends into the seat, and rolls up into a modest, K-Mart grade aluminum file cabinet/desk combo. He glides his fingers over the folders and begins to case the pile. B.O.T. In what country is a company called ‘SEX’ “of interest.” DIMITRI I don’t know. I’m a big fan of SEX myself. B.O.T. Something named SEX that involves touching sweaty men is something I’m compelled to pass on. DIMITRI I didn’t actually look at the folders, now. I just grabbed them from our talent placement archives. I figured you’d look through them and make your own decisions. B.O.T. I can’t believe we archive a place like this. Look, its not even a creative name. B.O.T. tosses the folder on to Dimitri’s desk. CUT TO Close shot of the front of the folder. It reads: “SUPER EXTREME XTREME” DIMITRI (V/O) “Super Extreme Xtreme” what? B.O.T. (V/O) How the hell should I know? CUT TO: Midshot of both Dimitri and B.O.T. working at their respective desks. B.O.T. tosses folders aside one after the other, becoming more and more visibly frustrated with every passing document. B.O.T. No… No… No… DIMITRI Keep it down, eh? B.O.T. Wouldn’t have to be so loud if you had done your job like I asked you to. DIMITRI (in a stereotypical southern slave accent) Oh massa, issa so sarry. B.O.T. Real cute, ass hole. DIMITRI What the hell are you so upset about? B.O.T. I’m getting old, man… 33 is a risky number in this business, and I feel my career slowly starting to end. I need to find a place where I can make a good impact, and maybe stake the claim that I deserve in this industry before I hang up the boots. DIMITRI Firstly, 33 isn’t old. I’m 36, and if I could I jump back into the ring in a heart beat. But I can’t, meaning you have to carry the legacy, my friend. Secondly, find your own damn fed. A place I think might fit someone may not be the place said someone can fit into, you dig? B.O.T. I’m trying to, but all we’ve got in our files are companies just begging to shut down in a month or two. DIMITRI Then get new files. B.O.T. From where? DIMITRI Are you really gonna make me hold your hand ad walk you through this? B.O.T. Its looking like that. DIMITRI Well, I don’t know what your expecting me to do. I can’t just make up great companies on the fly. B.O.T. A guy with as many wrestling connections as you must know somebody somewhere. DIMITRI I told you what I know already. B.O.T. Come on! Please? DIMITRI Look, if all you plan to do is bump your gums back here, than take a hike. I’ve got real work to do. B.O.T. Psh… fine… B.O.T. stands up and stomps out. CUT TO: INT. BSW GYMNASIUM - NIGHT The room is dark, only a dim moonlight illuminating the gym through a large window. In front of the window is B.O.T., dripping in sweat and smoking a cigar. From the stereo blasts “Last Remaining Light.” CUT TO: Close shot of a side profile of B.O.T.’s face… Curl like smoke and breath again Down your throat, inside your ribs Through your spine, and in every nerve Where I watch, and I wait and yield to the hurt. He lets the smoke billow around him like an aura, hiding barely his obviously un-jovial mood. He leans back in the frail folding chair he’s employed, the creek it gives off lets us assume its far past his prime. CUT TO: Mid Shot of B.O.T. sitting in the chair on the right side of the screen. In the distance a large gray door creaks open. Out of it walks AI KAWASHIMI, one of BSW’s teachers. She trots slowly towards him, her heels barely audible in the midst of the music. B.O.T. pays no mind. Seven moons and seven suns Heaven waits for those who run Down your winter and Underneath your waves Where you watch and you wait And pray for the day AI Audioslave, huh? Must be one of those nights. B.O.T. Its late, go home. AI Na, Ill hang out. Looks like you need company anyway. B.O.T. No I don’t… Ai smirks and strolls up around the chair and plops onto his lap. He wraps around his arm around her. AI Asa no kougan, yuube no hakkou. B.O.T. I hope you plan to make that make sense. AI “Rosy cheeks in the morning; White bones in the evening.” B.O.T. Live today, die tomarrow. AI Yeah. B.O.T. What’s that for? AI Your concerned about the future of your career. That’s why your timid when it comes to jumping into the AWC. B.O.T. How did you know about the AWC. AI I’m nosey. B.O.T. I knew you were Japanese, but I didn’t know you were a ninja, too. AI I do what I can. B.O.T. And I understand that I should live everyday to the fullest, but the days I can live in doing what I want to do to the fullest are numbered. Deetch is wrong. The peak of my career is almost over. AI Then don’t wrestle at the peak of you career. B.O.T. I refuse to Ric Flair it. I’ve got too much pride and shame for that. AI You don’t always have to be the best, you know. B.O.T. Yes, yes I do. AI That’s your problem. You set your standards to high, too early. Now when people are getting to the peaks of their careers, your no longer content with the mediocre. That’s a bad mindset to be in when you do actually get old. B.O.T. How do you figure? AI You think the average 60 year olds life is full of fun and excitement? They don’t get to see new cities and countries every day. Don’t get to go out in front of thousands and perform. Don’t get to compete in the art of combat week after week. You know what they do? They sit in their house, watch re-runs on the Game Show Network, and wonder why their children don’t call them. ‘Old Man’ Status is far away from the man who’s lap I’m sitting on. B.O.T. You always know what to say. AI Yeah. So will you take me with you on tour? B.O.T. Is that what this is about? AI I’m just saying. Why go all over Europe if your not gonna bring the chick that talked you into it? If it weren’t for me, you wouldn’t be going. B.O.T. I still haven’t decided to go yet. The contracts in my desk, just not sure I’m gonna sign it. AI Come on, you know you want to. Rumor has it, Aspinall’s there. B.O.T. That’s only rumor. I tried to look him up, but there’s no trace of him. AI Just do it. What else are you gonna do. Stay here and get yelled at by Deetch? B.O.T. Guess I see your point. He can run this place for a couple weeks without me. AI Yep. B.O.T. But im not taking you. AI Aw, come on. B.O.T. I need you here. You’re my best trainer. Who else is gonna whip those dickhead students into shape. AI You always know what to say. FADE OUT FADE IN: EXT. BSW - MORNING B.O.T. stands in front of a rental car parked outside of the shabby brick exteriors of the Brothers School of Wrestling. Joining him to bid a short live farewell is Dimitri, Ai, and the remain two head trainers, VIRGIL WHITE and IVAN KANNON. They’ve just finished loading the rest of his bags into the trunk. IVAN Lisbola, eh mate? B.O.T. Yea, never been. IVAN No shit? It’s a nice place. B.O.T. So I hear. DIMITRI Go ahead and win, don’t embarrass me. B.O.T. Shut up. AI Take care. B.O.T. You too. IVAN Take it easy, bloke. B.O.T. takes a seat inside the car. He drives off screen. CUT TO: Bird’s Eye chase of B.O.T. driving over the Tacony/Palmyra Bridge, on his way to Philadelphia Airport. B.O.T. (V/O) To Portugal. I’ve got an international legacy to start.
