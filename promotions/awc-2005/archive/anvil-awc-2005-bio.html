Anvil Biography Real Name Ryan Hayes Nicknames Rye, Big Rye, Anvil, A Hometown Pittsburgh, PA Height 6'2'' Weight 282 Biography With Hammer being the high-flyer Anvil certainly fits the mold of the powerhouse member of Tempered Steel at 6'2' and 283 pounds. Anvil sports a full, black beard that is a bit scraggly and 'all over the place' in appearance. Like Hammer, Anvil frequently wears a Pittsburgh Pirates baseball cap outside the ring but has shoulder-length black hair. Anvil is extremely thick but his body is not hyper-defined which helps him pull off a lot of belly-to-belly & belly-to-back suplexes, powerbombs, and other high-impact power moves with a smooth style. Just from watching Tempered Steel once even a novice wrestling fan would notice that Anvil is the more talented of the two team members. As far as attire, both members of Tempered Steel wear long, loose-fitting black karate pants. Anvil has the words 'Tempered ' and 'Steel' stitched vertically downwards in white block letters, one word on each pant leg. Anvil also wears black wrestling boots, has deep, brown eyes and frequently has black and gold tape wrapped around his wrists. Anvil is the member of Tempered Steel more frequently seen wearing protective gear such as knee or elbow pads. Anvil is also just what his name is, the solid rock upon which a lot of double-team moves are built. Anvil comes in to wear the other duos out. He is the 'slow and steady' of the group and is usually the setup man for Hammer who delivers the finishing blow.
