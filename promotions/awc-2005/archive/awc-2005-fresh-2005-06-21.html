AWC’s controversial new acquisitions Sam and Pat, known collectively as Rainbow Flag, contested a somewhat bland non-televised match with Thomas and David Forthright, an independent father-and-son duo hired on an ad-hoc basis, before the show went on air. Rainbow Flag picked up the victory after a brainbuster by Sam led to a pinfall on the younger Forthright, David. Tonight’s footage begins with the image of a chalkboard. On this chalkboard are two stick figures: One labeled FR~! and the other labeled EN, labeled in what one would usually consider chicken scrawl. As the camera zooms out from the chalkboard, the beloved FREDROCK~! comes into view with a large pop from the audience. FREDROCK~!, sporting the ever-flattering blue tights, clutches a broom to his chest. FREDROCK~!: My name is FREDROCK~! and today, I will be teaching you the etiquette of janitor... ing!” FREDROCK~! bangs his broom against the tile floor, feeling liberated, a large smile dancing across his lips. The camera then pans from the janitor and his chalkboard to Ellis Nash, sitting on a chair before the chalkboard and staring at FREDROCK~! like he is growing a tail. The camera zooms out once again so that focus is railed to the both of them. FREDROCK~!: There are many an important thing you must know about being a janitor, Ellis Nash. First things first, you need your own... FREDROCK~! pauses for dramatic effect, thrusting his fist into the air above him. FREDROCK~!: Janitor UNIFORM! Ellis leans back in her seat, crossing a leg over the other, regarding FREDROCK~! with a perturbed gaze. Ellis Nash: A janitor uniform? FREDROCK~!: Yes, Ellis Nash! A janitor UNIFORM! And, what a COINCIDENCE~! I have an extra! With disturbing enthusiasm, FREDROCK~! waddles over to his janitor closet and throws the door open to reveal, dun DUN dun DUN, a janitor uniform! FREDROCK~! snatches the uniform by its collar and carefully inspects it, lips pursed in a contemplative manner, humming a showtune underneath his breath. After he probes the suit, FREDROCK~! brightens and tosses it towards Nash, who simply blinks at the uniform laying across her lap. FREDROCK~!: Put it on! Ellis, noticing a pizza sauce stain on the front left pocket, shakes her head. Ellis Nash: Oh, hell no. There is no way I’m putting this sh – FREDROCK~!: PUT IT ON!!!!~! The brunette, surprised by FREDROCK~!’s acute snap, stands with a sigh and begins to shimmy the uniform over her current outfit, the navy blue of the uniform deftly contrasting with her white tanktop and blue jeans. FREDROCK~! rubs his hands together evilly. FREDROCK~!: Now that you have your janitor uniform~!, FREDROCK~! will teach you the fundamentals of janitoring! FREDROCK~! whips his broom down so that the end of it is nearest to his head and smacks the board with it. Nash jumps at the abrupt noise. FREDROCK~!: This is me, FREDROCK~! FREDROCK~! points to the stick figure labeled FR~! with the end of his broom. FREDROCK~!: This is you, Ellis Na – wait! Now that you’re a janitor, you must have a janitor name! ELLISROCK~! Nash’s eyes immediately widen. Ellis Nash: FREDROCK~!, I really don’t think that’s necessary. FREDROCK~! smacks the chalkboard with his broom again. FREDROCK~!: Of course it’s necessary, ELLISROCK~! You aren’t a janitor if you don’t have a janitor name~! Ellis Nash: But – FREDROCK~!: No buts, ELLISROCK~! Now as I was saying, before I was so RUDELY interrupted! FREDROCK~! scoffs. FREDROCK~!: To be a successful janitor, ELLISROCK~! must abide by the three janitoring rules. These janitor rules are accepted by every janitor all over the wor – FREDROCK~! is interrupted by Ellis Nash throwing her hand up in the air. He sighs, obviously agitated with Ellis’ second interruption of the lesson. FREDROCK~!: Yes, ELLISROCK~! ? Ellis Nash: Is janitoring a word? FREDROCK~! rolls his eyes in anxiety. FREDROCK~!: Of course it’s a word, ELLISROCK~! Would FREDROCK~! use a word that doesn’t exist!?!! Ellis Nash: Well, technically – FREDROCK~! smacks the board with his broom again. FREDROCK~!: Never mind technically, ELLISROCK~! If you want to be assistant janitor, you must follow the three rules of janitoring! Nash crosses her arms, annoyed. FREDROCK~! remains blissfully ignorant to this fact. FREDROCK~!: Rule 1: ELLISROCK~! must obey FREDROCK~! at all times unless otherwise noted! The crowd react with quiet murmurs and anticipation, perhaps realising O’Malec may be finished. Ashton climbs to the top rope and surveys the crowd. They respond with resounding boos. Ashton looks down at O’Malec, who is unmoving, and dives off with a breathtaking standing moonsault! Truth Waters: Ashton goes for the moonsault… but O’Malec moves out of the way at the last second!!! The crowd erupts. O’Malec rolls out of the way before contact can be made, Ashton hitting the mat hard and fast. Ashton holds his knee, and he seems to have damaged it in the impact. O’Malec starts to get to his feet despite the pain he is experiencing, realising it may be his only chance to retain his championship. Ashton, on the other hand, is in no position to move anywhere. Ashton points to his knee, catching the referee’s attention. George Cassidy: Get that man a doctor! Truth Waters: A doctor?! Ashton needs a psychiatrist, Cassidy! He’s a psychotic freak inside that ring. George Cassidy: Christopher Ashton is not psychotic! He’s a gifted competitor and he wants to leave here tonight with the Relentless championship in tow. Truth Waters: Not if O’Malec has anything to do with it. Larsson calls for Jin Osaka and Matt Matthews to come down to aid Ashton. O’Malec meanwhile rolls out of the ring and checks underneath the ring for anything he could use as a weapon against Ashton. O’Malec’s face lights up suddenly as he removes a chain from beneath the ring. The crowd go into a frenzy as O’Malec dives into the ring just as Ashton is about to be worked on by Osaka and Matthews. Truth Waters: O’Malec’s out for revenge now! George Cassidy: Somebody stop him! There’s a madman on the loose! O’Malec swings the chain wildly, almost catching trainee nurse Matthews on the head in his fury. The nursing staff quickly flee the ring, leaving the two competitors there by themselves. Ashton looks genuinely frightened of O’Malec and backs off like a scared puppy dog. O’Malec gives him no breathing space, however, and chokes him out with the chain. Larsson intervenes, but the determined O’Malec doesn’t let up easily. Eventually O’Malec backs away to cheers from the crowd as Ashton desperately tries to breathe normally again. Truth Waters: Christopher Ashton, dare I say, has never looked more scared in the ring! O’Malec has him where he wants him now, Cassidy. All he has to do is finish him off. George Cassidy: It’s never as simple as that when you’re dealing with an athlete of the calibre of this man. Truth Waters: Yeah, O’Malec’s one hell of an athlete. George Cassidy: (annoyed) I meant Ashton, you dimwit. Truth Waters: I knew you meant Ashton. I was trying to p*ss you off. George Cassidy: Watch your language. O’Malec lifts Ashton to his feet, kicking him hard in the midsection. O’Malec takes Ashton by the arm and acrobatically jumps onto the top rope, still holding on. O’Malec then dives off, catching Ashton in a breathtaking hurricanrana into a pin cover. Lars Larsson is slightly slow to go down for the count. ONE! TWO! Ashton kicks out, but a cold sweat has started to develop on Ashton’s forehead, reflecting his current condition. O’Malec shakes his head in frustration, but smiles as he realises Ashton is in no position to fight back. Truth Waters: I’ve never seen Lars Larsson get down to make a count so slowly before. Could it be that he was in awe of O’Malec’s hurricanrana? George Cassidy: His pills must not be working today. Typical… Ashton, still backing away and attempting to escape the ring, leans on his good knee and uses the ropes to stand as best he possibly can. O’Malec approaches Ashton to knock him out of the ring, but Ashton cleverly dodges O’Malec and throws O’Malec between the ropes to the outside. O’Malec lands hard on the mat. Suddenly Ashton stands up and the crowd buzz with boos and jeers as they finally see that Ashton was faking the knee injury the entire time. There is shock all around the arena as Ashton picks up the chain O’Malec used on him and swings it dangerously, ready to pounce. George Cassidy: Like a lion eyeing up his prey, Ashton is about to eat O’Malec for dinner. Truth Waters: Ashton makes his run-up… Ashton bounces off the ropes and dives over the top rope, meeting O’Malec with a suicide dive. Both men instantly crash to the mat below, exhausted. Larsson stands by in the ring. Truth Waters: Turning point in the match here. George Cassidy: It’s already turned in Ashton’s favour, Truth. Ashton is first to his feet, laying into O’Malec with right hands and knees to the ribs. O’Malec, clearly in a lot of pain, rests against the commentator’s table, but Ashton quickly pulls him away and rams him face first into the steel ring steps. Ashton rolls O’Malec, who is now bleeding from his chest and mouth, back into the ring and makes the cover, using the ropes for leverage. ONE! TWO! The champion manages to shoot his shoulder up off the canvas as blood rolls down onto the mat from his face. O’Malec, bruised and battered after Ashton’s recent onslaught, tries to get back to his feet, but the challenger is quick to continue his attack. Ashton locks on the deadly STF submission hold, grinning devilishly as he does so. O’Malec begins to feel the pressure of the hold, as he reaches out for the ropes. Truth Waters: O’Malec is in a tight spot here. The STF is by far one of the most painful submission holds in wrestling. Period. It’ll take all his will and determination to get to the ropes. George Cassidy: Or Ashton could just yank O’Malec back into the middle of the ring and prolong the agony. The blood runs down O’Malec’s face as he reaches out for the ropes, a gruesome scene. Ashton doesn’t relent, constantly increasing the pressure on O’Malec’s knee and neck. O’Malec screams, but it is not in pain. O’Malec’s strength gets the better of Ashton, and by some miracle, O’Malec shoves Ashton off of him. O’Malec turns around to face Ashton, who appears like he has just seen a ghost. George Cassidy: Um, what is up with O’Malec? He looks like Hannibal Lecter. Truth Waters: I’m not sure, but he’s got Ashton’s demise on his mind. O’Malec slowly walks towards Ashton, who holds his hands up in defense as he backs up. O’Malec dives at Ashton, mounting him and delivering massive and powerful right hands to the head. Ashton quickly becomes busted open and screams in pain as O’Malec violently continues the punishment. O’Malec then lifts Ashton up to his feet and whips him off the ropes. Ashton hangs on and feigns a short-arm clothesline, then grins at O’Malec as he gathers up all his strength and delivers Reality Calling! George Cassidy: Reality Calling! That’s the end for O’Malec, Truth. When Ashton hits that move out of nowhere, you just have to applaud him! Truth Waters: Indeed, a powerful finishing move by Ashton. Listen to these fans getting behind O’Malec, though. George Cassidy: These fans are wimps. They know as well as you and I that O’Malec is on cloud cuckoo right now. Ashton yells to the crowd, telling them it’s all over. The crowd respond with boos, but Ashton is unfazed. Instead of covering O’Malec, Ashton finds a ladder and lays it down in the middle of the ring. Ashton constantly checks on O’Malec as he does this to ensure he doesn’t get up. O’Malec shows some signs of life after Reality Calling, but Ashton signals he is ready to end it. Truth Waters: Ashton is planning to finish O’Malec off in style here, but what has he got in mind? George Cassidy: I would assume it involves that ladder. Ashton drags O’Malec over to the corner and roughly lifts him to his feet. Ashton places the weak O’Malec onto the top turnbuckle and points to the sky, as if to tell God himself “This is how it’s done”. Ashton locks O’Malec up for the Fisherman’s suplex, but in a burst of energy, O’Malec reverses into a roll-up just before the two men land on the mat. ONE! TWO! Truth Waters: Great reversal by O’Malec… could it be?! THREE! George Cassidy: Argh, how could it be?! Truth Waters: This crowd absolutely explodes! Crimson O’Malec is still Relentless champion, and on a performance like that, you couldn’t take it away from him. George Cassidy: (frustrated) How could – but what – this is impossible! Truth Waters: Impossible or not, you have just witnessed what was arguably one of the bravest performances in AWC history. Seems to be what O’Malec specialises in. George Cassidy: Yeah, whatever. I’m going to be in a bad mood all week now. O’Malec lies on the mat, though despite the blood running down his face, it is clear he is elated with the victory. Larsson hands the beaten O’Malec his Relentless championship as the medical team head down to the ring to survey the damage. Ashton smacks the mat in fury, only now realising he has lost the match. O’Malec doesn’t bat an eyelid at Ashton’s annoyance; he simply enjoys the moment. George Cassidy: There lies a resilient man, Truth. But his luck will eventually run out, trust me. Truth Waters: It will take a hell of a challenger to take that title away from Crimson O’Malec, Cassidy. A hell of a challenger. George Cassidy: And we've actually over-run our time slot here on Fresh!... Truth Waters: What about a Fresh!man? George Cassidy: There's no time! The copyright notice pops up on the screen, hurrying the announcers along. Truth Waters: Guess not. Adios! See you all on Sunday for Fresh!burst!
