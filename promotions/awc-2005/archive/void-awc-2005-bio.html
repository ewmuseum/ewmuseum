Void Biography Real Name Layne Sorensen Nicknames the Miscreant; the Marauder. Hometown Undisclosed. Birthday 1980 Height 6' Weight 229 Fed Titles None, as of yet. Fed Awards None, as of yet. Wrestling Style An unorthodox, unpredictable and malignant offence consisting primarily of brawling and wrestling innovation. Most effective with his nefarious employment of foreign objects. Merciless in his intent to floor and triumph over his opponents; cares not for any single wrestler he opposes and will happily endeavour to hurt them. Has a penchant for ‘breakers’ - especially those of the spinal column-decimating variety. Finisher Move the Inquality Finisher Description Layne clasps both of his arms around his opponent’s waist and elevates them from the canvas, clasping their legs under either arm. With his opponent straddled, Layne releases his opponent’s right leg, allowing it to dangle. Subsequently, Sorensen uses his now free right arm to hook his victim’s neck/head. He then proceeds to fall backward, collapsing to his back on the canvas at a rapid speed, and viciously drills his opponent’s skull into the mat. A more lethal, truculent version of a fisherman’s brain buster. Finisher Setup Move Soundless Requiem. Setup Description A reverse DDT with a bit more 'umph'.
