Alexander Strider Biography Nicknames 'The Mongoose' Hometown Pittsburgh, PA, USA Birthday December 25th, 1978 Height 6'00' Weight 225 Fed Titles AWC Transatlantic Champion Fed Awards Winner: 1st Annual Zero To Hero Tournament Biography History: Alexander Strider is a man who enjoys the wrestling world because of all that it offers him. The chance to use violence when it suits him, the opportunity to use people as he sees fit, and the fame and adoration of the mindless throng that he hates so much. Thinking himself better than the everyman, Alex chooses to forge a legacy of violence and supremacy in the world of professional wrestling. Strider is a manipulative liar, a player of a cerebral game in which he refuses to be the pawn. True to his almost obsessive personality, Alex has abstained from all drinking and drugs, and concentrates on keeping his body and mind in perfect condition. Adding to his list of personality quirks is his tendency to womanize. Alex finds sport in almost every aspect of his life, and sees women as little more than a cursory diversion. Alexander flew to Japan after a short stint as a collegiate wrestler where he learned the 'Mongoose' form of mat wrestling. After three years in the orient, Strider returned home after an invitation to the Total Championship Wrestling Federation, and later became Superior Championship Wrestling's Superior Champion the very next year. Currently Alex is being interviewed by a national sports magazine for a top story on the developing world of professional wrestling. *************** Appearance: Alex Strider has the body of a greek god, the face of a leading man, and was smart enough to have taken law at Harvard. But he chose to pursue professional wrestling because it was what fit him best. Deep blue eyes framed by short curly blonde hair, Alex Strider knows he's gorgeous, and he knows he's the best there is. Alex Strider wears full black ring tights with gold lightning streaking up and down both thighs. The only clothing that covers his torso as he wrestles are short black armbands that are placed right above his bicep, beneath the shoulder on both arms. He wears matching gold boots and gold fingerless ring gloves. Strider has a perfect smile that he loves to show off behind lips that look good enough to kiss, but beware, as he is capable of spitting his venom out at the unsuspecting and the suspecting alike. Strider is so vain that has abstained from alcohol and drugs his entire life, and he obsesses over his physique. No scars, no tattoos, Strider does not hide his contempt for those that willfully destroy their own body through bad living or sloth. Hidden inside this vain exterior is a perfectionist that has driven himself time and again to wrestling championships and a legacy that belies his age and youthful looks.
