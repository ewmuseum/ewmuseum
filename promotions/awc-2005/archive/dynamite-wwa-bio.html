Dynamite Biography Nicknames D Hometown Mexico City, Mexico Height 5'11'' Weight 233 Biography Like his tag-team partner TNT, much of Dynamite's personal background continues to be a mystery. Dynamite came to the attention of Jim Surge and TNT as a highly recommended young high-flyer by a prominent Mexican promoter when the pair was searching for a young risk-taker to pair up with TNT's power and practicality. And Dynamite did not disappoint. Without even knowing Surge and TNT were in attendance scouting him Dynamite put on a magnificent aerial display, dazzling the audience and cementing his place as one-half of the Detonators. The rumor mill has churned out that the choice of Dynamite, as tag partner, might not be as coincidental as it seems. Some of Los Bambitos think Dynamite used to work for TNT during the big man's border-running days as a professional coyote. There is also a rumor from Dynamite's past that he can never seem to shake – the rumor being that Dynamite lives his life in fear of the revenge of a father whose daughter drowned while in Dynamite's care. The girl was supposedly Dynamite's girlfriend at the time and died in a tragic fishing accident. General Appearance Physical Appearance: Dynamite's well-defined body matches his hyperactive attitude. Like TNT it is impossible to know exactly what he looks like because the only time Dynamite removes his mask is to shower (his skin is allergic to the rayon in the mask's cotton blend) and when he is in the shower he is generally alone. Ring Attire: Always wearing his dark green luchadore-style wrestling mask. Wears black, loose-fitting karate-style full-leg pants with the word 'Detonators' running vertically down, stitched into the outside of both legs in stark white letters. Always shined black wrestling boots with black laces. From time to time when TNT puts a small Mexican flag emblem on his boots Dynamite does the same thing to his boots. Additional Information Dynamite is the more high-strung member of the Detonators and his mouth has the speed to match his hyperactive personality. While Dynamite may be quick to react he doesn’t always think of the future consequences of his actions. Dynamite seems to adhere very closely to a “be-in-the-moment” sort of life philosophy. Dynamite's ad-libs in the ring are usually the only rare occasions that the Detonators deviate from manager Jim Surge’s gameplan. And while some of those moments put the Dets in a precarious position, some of those moments are also when the Detonators are at their creative and athletic best. “D,” as he’s sometimes referred to, is usually thought to be the younger of the two Detonators but as yet actual ages for either Detonator are unconfirmed. A guess from seeing them in action is that TNT is 26-28 and Dynamite is 22-24. When the Detonators first appeared in the WWA Dynamite often found himself as the odd-man-out during disputes with manager Jim Surge as TNT frequently took the cowboy's side and it began to frustrate him. But Surge’s unyielding dedication to the Detonators’ cause and Dynamite’s innate drive to succeed seem to have been enough common ground to forge a temporary truce in the camp. Despite this encouraging sign Dynamite has never expressed his personal gratitude to Surge for Surge's assistance in getting Dynamite into the United States legally. Dynamite is the classic lowlife heel and more closely aligns himself with Los Bambitos, the fanatical Detonators fanclub that follows the tag-team everywhere they go than with his partner or manager.
