Adalia Delorian Biography Real Name Jacob Moreno Nicknames Absolute Zero, The Faithful Vampire, Chino (to family/friends) Hometown The Jersey Shore Birthday December 23, 1983 Height 6' Weight 201 Biography Jacob Moreno's birth was quite possibly the most celebrated day in his family's life. He was the oldest of three sons (Jacob 21, Pete 18, Alex 13), each of which sharing good times and bad with their parents. However, Jacob was very well the most unlucky of the three sons, hit with a terrible disease in which he cannot go out into the sun without risking his well being. One fateful day, a teenage girl walked up to the doorstep of the Moreno household, alone and with no place to go. Her name: Adalia. From what she explained, her parents left her behind on their way home in Japan. The family and the girl attempted to get in touch with her mother and father, but no response had ever come. Seeing no other alternative, Jacob's parents took her in, and for approximately fifteen months, she lived with the Moreno family. It wasn't at all difficult for the 16 year old to become a part of the family, but after Jacob's father was paralyzed in a life threatening car accident, Jacob had to take on the responsibility of working to take care of the family over himself. He soon became the person everyone had went to if things gone wrong, and thus, at become one to blame for most of the family's shortcomings. One night, Jacob gave Adalia a lift to a rather late concert she wanted to see, in fear of his parents yelling at him for her not being able to go; and being the only one who would go at such a late hour, he had no option of asking someone else to go instead. On the way home from that concert, an event that Jacob never thought he'd see in other than in movies- much less take an actual part in- had left the 21 year old out in the street, bleeding from a fatal gunshot wound. Adalia promised to take care of Jacob and get him home, and the next morning... Jacob woke up in his own bed. His wound was no longer there. Furthermore, Adalia was no longer in the house. On a hunch, Jacob goes to Japan in hopes to find Adalia's real parents. In turn, he also finds the AWC, a way that he can relenquish his emotions of regret, sadness, and anger. Filling out his contract application, however, he decided that the only way to truly feel better about his actions, would be to mourn his sister's disappearance under her name. Thus, Adalia Delorian was born again. Wrestling Style Striker/Adaptive (Adalia's strength is not his wrestling ability, but his way to keep from being predictable; he'll go into one match displaying power, the next he'll be a high flyer. The only thing he excels at truly is simple fighting with his fists and feet. Finisher Move Delorian Driver Finisher Description Starts out in the setup of a Michinoku Driver, as Adalia lifts his opponent onto his left shoulder. From there, he then snap mares his opponent, flipping them over. While his opponent is in the air, he remains latched onto his/her head, driving them into the mat with an inverted DDT. In some cases, if he hates his opponent enough or they already kicked out of this move once, he'll hold onto the head and lock in a dragon sleeper. Finisher Setup Move Enfilade Setup Description Adalia grabs his opponent as if going for a reverse neckbreaker, before suddenly flipping them over by their throat and into an X-Factor. He follows that right up with a standing moonsault splash. Other Moves STRIKES The basis of Adalia's moveset. These are his bread and butter attacks, and he'll stick to these strikes more than the regular professional wrestler would. He's surprisingly lethal with these basic moves, as well; the strength behind his relatively small stature can frighten and/or catch opponents off guard. Also noted: Adalia is a southpaw, so most of his power comes from his left hand.
