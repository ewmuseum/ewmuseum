Shawn Singleton Biography Real Name Shawn Singleton Nicknames 'The Tactical Assassin Hometown Oakland, California Birthday September 19th, 1977 Height 6'1 Weight 238 Other Feds 4WW Other Fed Awards 4WW North Wind Champion, 4WW West Wind Champion, 4WW South Wind Champion Wrestling Style Technical brawler, suplex specialist Finisher Move The Next Wave Finisher Description Shawn spins his opponent around, locking him in a half nelson. He uses his off-hand to wrap around his opponent's waist, and completes the modified german suplex. Finisher Setup Move The Broken Promise Setup Description Vertical brainbuster suplex Other Moves Signature Moves: Retribution - Whirl Neckbreaker (Shawn holds the opponent on his shoulders like a samoan drop. He flips him over like a death valley driver, but drops to one knee, catching the back of the opponents' head and neck with the knee) Vindication - Inverted Release Powerbomb (Shawn has his opponent in a standing headscissors and lifts him up for a snap powerbomb, but continues the momentum while falling back, flipping his opponent onto his face.) The Predator (used less frequently) - Shawn signals towards his manager, The Hunter, before kicking his opponent in the waist, doubling him over. He double underhooks both arms, and drives his opponent's face into the mat with the double underhook faceslam (Pedigree).
