House Show 9/4/06 Captain Suleimon vs. Aimz With the crowd nicely settled in for this light bout of entertainment, AWC continues its tour around the country promoting its upcoming Zero 2 Hero tournament. Entertainment Managers Sasha Volkyeva and Pearl Harber have been hard at work, setting up workshops and opportunities events in the hope that they can find someone with the spark to make it AWC. Little is known how successful they've been right now. But, that is for another time, right now we've got action in the ring as part of this expo for AWC. The tightly packed crowd are baying as the Ottoman Emperor Captain Suleimon enters the small building. The blood is boiling in the house already and Suleimon hasn't even done anything yet. His grim features make for the perfect heel complement. He's just so easy to hate. The complete reverse reaction is heralded for the arrival of the Red Raver Aimz. She has the smug grin on her face, reminding the Captain of all that she's done to him. The crowd starts up chants, mostly unfavourable to how Suleimon. His outrage builds, waiting to explode for the match up. Ryan rings the bell and they're off, both workers tying up and testing each other. Suleimon gets a cheap shot in to take the advantage which he holds for several minutes, taking every opportunity to attack Aimz's legs and stop her from mounting any sort of aerial offence. Aimz meanwhile does her best to avoid Suleimon but with each blow her mobility is tested. The exclamation point of this beating is second rope legdrop to the knee of Aimz. The cries are stifled by the crowd who is working hard to keep here in the match. The Captain is cocky though and slips up, trying to be too smart with a back body drop. It's easily telegraphed and Aimz comes in with a swinging neckbreaker. This is followed by the ubiquitous kip-up which ruins any form of psychology this match had, just like a certain Heartbreak Kid does all the time. Suleimon struggles to his feet to try and stem the flow but Aimz is at the forefront now, swinging left and right, clubbing the Captain to the ground. After that, it's a simple matter of the Dead Aim and there's your match to start off the expo. As Aimz wanders to the back, slapping hands as she goes, Suleimon looks on from the ring with a vicious look to him. If you witnessed over a month ago, Teresa Tomas scored her first victory against Victor Cage. Finally, Teresa Tomas and Garbage Bag Johnny met in the center of the ring to lock in a collar and elbow tie-up. She went for a quick cover, only to get a one count. He counted three and called for the bell. Sgt. Jacobs vs. Orangebeard vs. Killian O'Leary This bout started with none of the three men rushing in hastily to try and gain an advantage. When both O’Leary and Jacobs could wait no longer and both made lunges for Orangebeard, the pirate wanted none of it and scampered out of the ring to momentary safety. Jacobs then took advantage of the distraction, taking O’Leary’s head and executing a violent neck-breaker. Jacobs then looked to take advantage, but after ducking a clothesline and coming in with a flying forearm, Killian seemed to have Gadget out cold. The count went 1… 2… and on the three was broken up by the crafty pirate, getting to grips with proceedings in his first ever wrestling contest. Killian wasn’t a happy camper, and OB decided it would probably be better if he’d retreat to the outside of the ring once more, but this time stay there. Only Killian had other ideas and chased the lumpy pirate around the ring, eventually running into a clothesline from Jacobs. The fight was brought back into the ring (with OB realising his home is outside the ring) with Jacobs taking it to O’Leary, executing a back suplex followed by a single leg Boston crab. O’Leary fought to the ropes to break the count. Orangebeard was bored so decided to get back in the ring and take advantage of both men being on the ground, but was surprised when an outstretched hand by O’Leary caught him by the throat. Getting to his feet Killian O’Leary landed numerous devastating shots to OB’s head eventually landing one right on the button, flooring him. O’Leary made the pin 1… 2… and it was broken up by Sgt. Jacobs with a double axe handle to the back of the head. Jacobs pulled O’Leary to his feet, grabbed his wrist and launched him into the referee. The referee was flattened, but O’Leary still stood, but as he was about to turn around and face his opponent was met with a swift kick to the groin! The referee didn’t see it and Gadget took full advantage and grabbed Killian around the waist ready for the M203! He was about to lift him, but O’Leary swung an arm back in desperation and caught Jacobs flush in the face with his elbow! Jacobs stumbled back trying to regain his balance, and O’Leary rushed straight to the ropes, coming back to hit Jacobs with The Harvest! But Jacobs ducked and O’Leary continued straight into the ropes. Jacobs ran over to O’Leary and grabbed him by the waist with one more attempt at the M203, but Killian wasn’t going to let him and clutched the ropes, holding on for dear life! Orangebeard walked over from outside the ring, taking his pet parrot Olly who was screaming a number of obscenities and held him up to the two gentlemen. Olly then pecked ‘The Sarge’ in the left eye, to the blind side of the referee! Gadget stumbled backwards clutching his eye and O’Leary took full advantage running to the nearest ropes and smashing his arm straight into the unaware face of Gadget with The Harvest! Killian then hooked a leg for the 1… 2… and 3 winning the match in triumphant fashion (as most people that are triumphant usually do).
