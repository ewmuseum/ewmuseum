Victor Cage Biography Real Name Victor Cage Hometown The Southern Coast Birthday 03/30/1980 Height 6'3 Weight 225 Biography Often refered to as 'Cage', new comer to the world of wrestling. He's not of superhuman size but he does have heroic stamina and determination. He is a man of tough nature and has proved in his past to take a good amount of pain and continue to fight on. Cage was raised in a faithful Catholic family. His mother taught him to be strong in the Catholic faith and shows it in his life. He is an ideal Catholic, he is a heavy drinker, a womanizer, and never misses a sunday at church. Cage doesn't trust many people, the people he does trust, he trusts with his life. His father was in the military and moved around quite a bit. This is probably why Cage doesn't have the best people skills. He grew up most of his life in the South. After high school he enlisted in the Marine Corps. Here he gained his knowledge of fighting. With his developed skills, Cage qualified for the Marine Scout Sniper Program and was deemed an official sniper by the United States. He fought in the Iraqi war on two different deployments. He suffered a wound from grenade shrapnel on his second deployment. Spending more than a year in an intense war zone, Cage constantly has flashbacks. After a his first match in AWC, Cage meets with Guffey, the hobo who winds up becoming his manager and his mentor. Wrestling Style Extreme Brawler/submission/MCMAP Finisher Move Kill Zone or Snipershot Finisher Description Kill Zone is a Pedigree or Snipershot is a variation of the sharpshooter where he wrenches down on the opponents legs and leans back to pull up on there face. Finisher Setup Move One Shot Setup Description Rock Bottom Other Moves Cage is brand new to the wrestling world. He's inexperienced in the ring and will probably show it. He did no wrestling in high school and his on mat technical fighting is non-existant. What he lacks in inexperience, he makes up for in toughness. He's not the biggest guy in the club or the smallest. He uses what size he can to his advantage and knows that weaking one particular body part will work rather than a battle of brute strength. Cage is much stronger than he looks. He is quick to an all out fury of strikes of knees, elbows, fists, and feet. He can take punishment to extreme levels. In the same breath, he can deliver punishment in extreme levels. His training with the Marine Corps Martial Arts Program gives him excellent martial arts skills. It is a mixture of seven different types of martial arts. His technique is perfected. One shot is the typical set up move for Snipershot. Kill Zone is generally followed by a pin. Suplexes, multitude of creative, painful suplexes. Knees to the stomach. Russian DDT (Variation of DDT, falling straight down on the throat instead of on top of the head.) Lots of elbows and knee strikes. Flying elbows. Blood Choke holds. Swinging neck breaker. Continuous strikes to opponents knees.
