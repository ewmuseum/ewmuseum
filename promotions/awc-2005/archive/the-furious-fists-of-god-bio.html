The Furious Fists Of God Biography Fed Titles Alliance Champions x2 Fed Awards Fresh!man x3, top ten various times Finisher Name The Fist of God Finisher Description Tim or Liam grab opponent and irish whip him or her right towards either Tim or Liam (whoever isn't whipping). The one who is being whipped to spins around real fast and, as opponent comes flying towards either Tim or Liam, they throw a furious fist right into the opponent's face. Setup Move Usually anything to make the opponent stunned/groggy and standing straight Move Set Alternative Team Finisher: The Book of Revelations Liam or Tim have opponent in powerbomb position. Whoever isn't powerboming is kneeling there with a knee out. Tim or Liam then throw opponent down with Jack knife powerbomb, sending opponent's head straight into the fucking knee. This isn't a finisher you get up for, so save this for big matches. The Fist of God will be used MUCH more, but The Book of Revelations is the one to totally push the Fists. (Note: Revelations is the last chapter in the bible...so, like, it's the end...it's a figurative name :P) IMPORTANT: Tim uses cheap tactics, Liam doesn't. Tim is more of a thinker, Liam is a killer. Doesn't mean Tim can't kill or Liam can't be cheap...those moments are just more for the other. Look at the descriptions in each wrestler's little thing below. -+Tim Martin+- How Tim Fights: Beginning of matches- If has upper hand: will do a lot of small moves, not really any lifts, like clotheslines or european uppercuts. A lot of irish whips into ropes, usually leading into moves where the opponent can be dropped without lifting them. Will really work the opponent in an efficient manner. If Opponent gets upperhand: will desperately try to muscle way out of horrible situation. This will usually lead to getting Tim the upper hand or just fucking wearing him out, leading to opponent further domination of Tim. Also, Tim isn't afraid of a cheap manuver. In Middle- If has upper hand: Will now start the high impact moves. Many of the signature moves are done around here. This is an attempt to try and end the opponent before giving him any sort of comeback oppurtunity. If doesn't have upper hand: This is where Tim starts getting cheap. He'll do low blows, eye pokes, anything he can to try to level shit out without wasting much energy. If it calls for it, he'll try to level out the ref in some way in order for cheaper bullshit. At the End- If Tim has upper hand: Look for the Lord's Wish, big time (obviously). If isn't going for the devestating finisher at the time, will either do more signature and big impact moves or just straight up punch, depending how tired. If ref is out, will start brining in weapons. If doesn't have upper hand: Desperation, desperation, desperation. Big swings, big jumps, big bumps, big everything. Cheap shots, jump off turnbuckle...Tim will do anything. If the oppurtunity arises, he'll go for the Lord's Wish. Tim's Moves Finisher- The Lord's Wish: Tim gets opponent and brings him up to shoulder. Tim then lifts opponent up and holds them in the air (like Undertaker's Last Ride), but then steps forwards and drops to his butt with his opponent, landing back of opponents head on Tim's shoulder, as if landing a reverse stunner or a neckbreaker. The move is much more devestating then a regular neck breaker, though, as the opponent is dropped from much higher up. Signature Moves- Eagle Splash: A diving full body splash off the top rope. Lands body on body. White Light of God: Opponent runs at Tim, Tim picks him up for the spine buster straight into the air, but instead drops down to his knees and drops opponent chin on his shoulder. Ear Pull: Opponent is sitting up on the ground, and Tim gets behind him and starts pulling on his ears until ref breaks it up or opponent gets out. 6 Feet Under: Tim is behind opponent and grabs their waist, lifting them straight into the air. While their still in the air in front of Tim, Tim grabs shoulders and slams him straight into the ground right onto his ass. Vice Crab: Opponent's head is between Tim's leg, as if the beginning of a powerbomb. Instead, Tim grab's opponent's legs and starts pulling on them, closing his legs hard around opponent, commiting a sort of floating Boston Crab. Eagle's Claw: Tim palm's opponent's face and then picks them up with the other hand, spine busting opponent and going for immediate pin. Confessional: Opponent starts out with head between legs, like a powerbomb. Instead, Tim flings his opponent over his shoulder, sending him flying and landing in a heap. Open palm choke to kick to head: Not really a move, but an antic. Opponent is down and Tim begins choking them with his palm. Once the ref lifts him up, Tim begins arguing with the ref. With his back facing his opponent, Tim smoothly kicks his opponent's head with his heel. -+Liam Martin+- How Liam Fights In the Begininng If Liam has upperhand- Big moves real early. Liam likes to show off that's he's a fucking beast. He'll throw his opponent around, and if the opponent is as big or bigger, he'll try to throw him around as good as he can (Liam is strong, though...mother fucker is 300 lbs!). You might catch him to a To The Gates early, but that'll inevitably fail (fucking rules of wrestling). If Liam doesn't have the upperhand- Then he's pretty much just trying to coast. It'll take something big to get him off his game, so he'll be dazed. He comes out like a fucking fire in the beginning. In the Middle If Liam has upperhand- He'll slow down here, but don't be surprised to see some signature moves. This is where he'll do a big man submission, like some kind of camel clutch or chin lock. Ultimately, Liam loses the adrenaline. If Liam didn't have the upperhand- If Liam was dominated in the first part, this is where he goes crazy. Now he'll start the big tosses, big drops, big punches, all that shit. He comes flying like a bull here. In the End If Liam has upperhand- Usually, Liam is exhausted. You won't find any move that won't either set up To The Gates or actually is To The Gates. Really, Liam gets fucking tired from going crazy. If Liam doesn't have upperhand- Will try anything to get To The Gates off. The finisher is a killer and can be easily done (all the opponent has to be is on the shoulders, then thrown off). The end of matches are pretty much meant for Liam to do To The Gates. Finisher- To The Gates: Liam lifts opponent and rests him on his shoulder (like Lesnar's F5). Liam then lifts them and drops them foward in front of him, putting his knee out and dropping their face right on his knee. Signature Moves- Hail Mary: Liam whips opponent into ropes, as opponent comes back Liam picks him up and holds them in the air before dropping back and landing his opponent's neck on the ropes. The Lord's Anger: Liam grabs opponent's head, clenching his with his hands. He then throws his opponent down as hard as he can, smashing opponent's face into the ground. The 7th Day: Liam is standing near ropes with his back facing it, and lifts opponent in the air with a suplex. Holding him there, he then flings the opponent behind him and out of the ring. The Devil's Temptation: Opponent is standing against turnbuckle groggy. Liam is standing at opposite turnbuckle and starts running towards opponent. He then jumps and flips, flying upside down and front flipping and smashes straight into his opponent. Biblical Bomb: Picks opponent up for powerbomb, spins around and executes a sitdown powerbomb into immediate pin. Throat Punch: Opponent is flat on the ground and Liam stands above him. Liam then strikes down fist a hard fist into the opponent's throat. Liam walks away, grinning evilly. Eagle-D-T: Liam grabs opponent and execute a one armed DDT, immediately following it with a punch to the back of the opponent's head, potentially grinding his face into the mat. Heaven's Elbow: Opponent is down. Liam kneels over their head and begins praying to himself. At the end, he opens his eyes and drives his elbow into the opponents face or throat (usually depends if the ref has already warned Liam about punching opponent in the throat or not). ---------------- Basic moves are prety much big-man brawler moves. ---------------- Antics when being taken down: Usually if Liam or Tim are groggy, they will swing blindly, hoping to land a punch. This can be used for their opponents in many, creative ways. In other situations, they could just as well land the punch, which usually results in a dramatic 10 count...though this rarely ha ppens. Entrance Music 'Hallelujah', the popular church choir song.
