When man became a farmer, and thus dependent upon daily and seasonal changes of weather, he often turned to worship the great force that regulated these changes – the light and heat of the sun. The worship of the sun, although not peculiar to any one time or place, received its greatest prominence in ancient Egypt. There, the daily birth, journey, and death of the sun was the dominating feature of life. One of the most important gods of Egyptian religion was Ra, the sun-god, who was considered the first king of Egypt. The pharaoh, said to be the son of Ra, was the sun-god's representative on earth. In later Egyptian religion, under the rule of Ikhnaton, the sun-god Aton gained complete supremacy in what was Egypt's only monotheistic period. In Mesopotamia, where sun worship was also very important, the sun-god Shamash was a major deity and was equated with justice. In Greece there were two sun deities, Apollo and Helios, although there was no institutionalized form of sun worship. The influence of the sun in religious belief also appears in Zoroastrianism, Mithraism, Roman religion, Hinduism, Buddhism, and among the Druids of England, the Aztecs of Mexico, the Incas of Peru, and many Native Americans. George Cassidy: I’m pretty sure Crimson O’Malec’s face is ready to bust wide open. Pact picks up a ladder lying outside the ring. He sets it up, it’s about twelve foot tall. He picks Cyris up and lays him on ladder half way up. He grabs his whip and climbs the other side of the ladder. George Cassidy: What the hell is this? Pact is TYING O’MALECS HANDS TO THE RUNGS OF THE LADDER!!! Truth Waters: O’Malec is getting his senses about him and is not too happy about the predicament he’s finding himself in. Pact climbs down the ladder and goes to the other side. Pact grabs the feet of O’Malec and pulls them off the rung, leaving O’Malec hanging by the barbed wire around his wrists and O’Malec screams in agony. George Cassidy: PACT IS ATTEMPTING TO CRUCIFY CRIMSON O’MALEC HERE IN THE ARENA. Truth Waters: This has just gone way too far… Crimson grabs onto the rung on the ladder above his hands and pulls himself up, screaming the entire way. He gains some momentum and kicks Pact in the jaw. George Cassidy: I don’t understand it… You could send both Pact and Crimson down the Niagara Falls without a barrel and they’d both act like you sent them down the kiddy slide at the public pool. Pact pulls down on the feet of Crimson digging the barbed wire back into his wrists. Crimson screams in agony again. Truth Waters: Somebody stop this match. Someone is going to die! Crimson pulls himself up to get a foot on a rung. Crimson pushes both feet into the air and they land on Pact’s shoulders. This applies pressure to Crimson’s wrists and he again screams bloody hell, but determination makes him begin kicking sideways into the face of Pact. Crimson is screaming the entire time. Truth Waters: I truly do believe Crimson has turned into a wild man. George Cassidy: Loss of blood will do things to you. Horrible things... Kris Krimzon comes to in the corner of the barricade and charges after Pact. He spears Pact into the ladder and the ladder comes tumbling down on all three of them. Crimson O’Malec on top, Kris Krimzon sandwiched and Pact on the bottom. It is literally a car wreck inside the ring. The fans, meanwhile, are chanting "Holy s*it!" after what they have just witnessed take place. Truth Waters: There’s no way to describe what we just witnessed. It’s - it’s carnage in that ring. George Cassidy: Who’s going to be the first to escape and possibly get the victory? Remember, the Relentless championship is still hanging in the balance. Pact crawls out from underneath the ladder, though he is clearly worst for ware. O’Malec also manages to escape, rolling onto his back and staring up to the lights. Fans of both O’Malec and Pact will them on, but the crowd is divided between the two. Krimzon claws his way over to Pact, but the veteran fends him off with stomps to the chest and head. O’Malec sets Pact up for a german suplex from behind, and hits the move perfectly. O’Malec goes for the cover afterward. ONE! TWO! Truth Waters: So close, O’Malec… George Cassidy: O’Malec is giving these fans false hope here. They cheer for him, but deep down he knows he can’t win. O’Malec gets to his feet again, lifting Pact up after him. Krimzon uses the ropes to lift himself up, but he can’t seem to stay standing. Krimzon falters, collapsing against the ropes. O’Malec is taken by surprise as Pact delivers The Downfall! The high impact diamond cutter leaves O’Malec and Pact lying on the mat. Pact just manages to lay a hand across O’Malec’s heaving chest for the cover. ONE! Truth Waters: The Downfall could have done it! That’s surely it for O’Malec! George Cassidy: Perhaps… TWO! But O’Malec gets the shoulder up at the last moment. Pact pulls his own hair in disbelief. Krimzon uses his last ounce of strength to drag O’Malec to the corner. Krimzon stomps away on O’Malec. Krimzon pulls O’Malec roughly to his feet, hitting a DDT on the smaller man. Krimzon then rests against the ropes. Pact approaches Krimzon from behind, rolling him up into a small package! Truth Waters: Pact surprises Krimzon!!! ONE! TWO! Krimzon barely escapes before the three count. As the pin cover takes place, O’Malec somehow recovers from the DDT and scales the top turnbuckle. The fans are on their feet, ready to see what the inventive O’Malec has in mind. O’Malec dives off, nailing both Krimzon and Pact with a dropkick. O’Malec builds momentum, running off the ropes and delivering a hurricanrana to Krimzon and a running DDT to Pact! O’Malec roars to the crowd, who respond in kind. The fired up O’Malec measures Krimzon, ready to finish him off. Krimzon turns, and O’Malec, using the ladder which lies in the middle of the ring, takes the legs from under Krimzon with a vicious shot to the kneecaps. Krimzon goes down like a shot, as if he has just been knee-capped. George Cassidy: What kind of a freak is O’Malec?! Truth Waters: He’s a freak who desperately wants the Relentless championship! With Krimzon out of the equation, O’Malec focuses his attention on the hardcore legend, Pact. O’Malec seizes Pact from behind, but Pact low blows O’Malec, bringing him down to his knees. Pact quickly heads outside the ring, checking underneath the ring for any weapon he could make use of. Pact’s eyes light up when he spots a kendo stick. Pact takes the kendo stick, enters the ring, and measures O’Malec for what could be the knock out shot. Truth Waters: If Pact hits O’Malec with that, you can bet your bottom dollar that O’Malec is done for. George Cassidy: Well, he’s old, but I have to hand it to him, he knows how to finish off his opponents. Hit them hard and fast! Pact cocks the kendo stick back, and lets rip on O’Malec, but there’s no-one there! O’Malec quickly ducks the shot, retaliating with a dropkick to the knee. Pact drops the kendo stick, and O’Malec takes it before Pact can retrieve it. O’Malec bounces off the ropes and hits a home run with the kendo stick on Pact’s head! The crowd erupts as Pact’s lights are knocked out, and he collapses to the mat face first. Truth Waters: Listen to this crowd, ladies and gentlemen! George Cassidy: Why can’t they keep it down?! Truth Waters: Because O’Malec is about to win this match! O’Malec, clearly out of energy, falls on top of Pact to make the cover, though he seems unaware of his surroundings. ONE! TWO! THREE! Truth Waters: YES!!! Crimson O’Malec is the very first Relentless champion here in AWC! And there’s no doubt that he deserves it! The crowd erupts into cheers as O’Malec is handed the Relentless championship by James Brunt, who congratulates him as he hands it to him. O’Malec pats Pact on the chest, showing his respect for his opponent. Outside the ring, Krimzon curses himself. O'Malec can't help but smile as the fans show their appreciation for his efforts. Fatigued, he briefly speaks with James Brunt. Brunt motions for a stretcher for O'Malec. George Cassidy: Aww, the baby needs a stretcher. Truth Waters: The man has been through hell tonight, Cassidy. If you were in that situation, you'd be torn apart limb from limb. O’Malec savours the cheers from the fans, as Pact and Krimzon enter the ring. There is a brief moment of tension, but both men shake hands with the fallen O’Malec, showing their respect for the new champion’s efforts. Truth Waters: Magnificent main event, it has to be said. These men couldn’t have given us any more than they did. George Cassidy: I suppose I’ll give the devil his due. Well done, Crimson O’Malec. Truth Waters: Take it in, Crimson. Take it all in. You are the very first Relentless champion! AWC medical staff Jin Osaka and Matt Matthews are now in the ring, setting up the stretcher and checking on O'Malec's condition. O'Malec nods that he is fine, but they continue with their diagnosis. Eventually, O'Malec is layed on the stretcher and brought out of the arena to a huge round of applause from the fans. All the while O'Malec is holding his newly won championship in the air. Osaka and Matthews congratulate him as they head backstage. Kris, spent and tired from his match, walks out of the AWC building towards his clearly marked vehicle. He gets to his car, but is interrupted by yelling. Kris looks up in the direction of the yelling to see someone he notices to be familiar to him somehow. He then sees what the man is carrying, which is a sign reading “Dogs Are People Too!” Kris sighs as the man nears. Man: Kris, you have got SOME nerve using that dog in the match up. Kris Krimzon: Just shove it man. I lost already, get over it. Man: No man, YOU shove it. You’re a f***ing idiot and a danger to society! Kris Krimzon: Man, have you even SEEN that sign you holding? Man: F*** you man! F*** YOU! Kris, obviously worn from his difficult night delivers a thunderous right hand to the jaw of the protester. The protester is thrown backwards and knocked off angle by Kris’s vehicle. The sign the protester was holding scrapes the back of Kris’s car and Kris notices it. Kris then jumps onto the protester, who has fallen to the ground by this point, delivering numerous blows his face. The protester lets out a high pitched shrill, alerting Bruno Hague to the scene. Bruno pulls the angered Krimzon off of the protester, shoving Kris against his car. Bruno Hague: Kris! Are you crazy!? Butch Radder: Uh oh… Bruno looks up at Taz who is kneeling by the hurt protester. Butch Radder: I’m sorry Kris, I didn’t know it was you… Bruno Hague: What did you do!? Butch Radder I saw the fight so I called the police. Bruno mumbles a curse word under his breathe. Bruno Hague: Kris, we’re going to have to arrest you… Kris Krimzon: Son of a bitch… Hague puts handcuffs on Krimzon’s hands and releases Krimzon. Kris jumps up onto the back of his vehicle and spits. Kris Krimzon: Thanks a lot Butch, you taddle tail… Kris remains quiet as Bruno and Butch help the protester to his feet. The police arrive shortly after, and Kris is put into the police vehicle as the cops question the protester. “Bohemian Like You” explodes into ferocious feel-good guitar, David Harber coming out to the ring with considerably less applause than earlier from the tired and satiated crowd. Truth Waters: It’s time for our Fresh!man announcement, and really, who can look past Crimson O’Malec’s determined and gutsy performance tonight to become the first AWC Relentless champion? George Cassidy: I can. Fresh!man is Strider’s tonight. He carried that handicap match. James Brunt, in preparation for the drawing of a number, takes off his top hat and empties a small jar of little pieces of paper into it. Smiling, he shakes the hat around, mixing up the numbers, as Pearl steps into the ring. Pearl: First of all, I must say it’s been quite an evening tonight. Quite possibly the best Fresh! yet. What do you think, ladies and gentlemen? The fans cheer, showing their appreciation. Pearl: As such, this decision has been a tough one for me. I’ve seen a number of exciting and impressive performances tonight. Ellis Nash looked great in her Three Way Fury match... Truth Waters: Seconded, but that’s because she’s hot. Pearl: ...and Strider and Lavelle, despite some problems, managed to work together admirably for at least part of their match. But I don’t think there is any doubt in anyone’s mind as to the most spectacular performance of the night, and this man came through a gruelling, entertaining and sometimes shocking Weapon of Choice match to deservedly become the first Relentless champion. Tonight’s Fresh!man is Crimson O’Malec. An appreciative ripple of applause runs through the crowd. Pearl: O’Malec put his heart and soul into the match – to the unfortunate extent that he has now been rushed to hospital awaiting a blood transfusion. He experienced a huge amount of blood loss during the match, and we can only pray for Crimson’s recovery and continued health. George Cassidy: That is a shock. Truth Waters: Here’s hoping he recovers quickly... I’m sure he will. He’s a brave guy. Pearl: And as an added incentive for Crimson when he returns – I will now draw his prize! Remember, the number 1 entitles him to entry into a match for the Livewire title; the number 2 means he gets to go for the Frontier title; and the number 3 grants him a shot at Alex Strider. Harber delves his hand into Brunt’s top hat. Truth Waters: What’ll it be for O’Malec... Without looking at the number, Pearl hands the piece of paper to James Brunt.
