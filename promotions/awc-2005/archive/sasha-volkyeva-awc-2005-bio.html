Sasha Volkyeva Biography Birthday 26th February 1958 Place of Birth A "small village" in Russia Biography Confident, dominant, imposing and beautiful, Ms Volkyeva - or "Mother Russia" as workers sometimes nickname her backstage, but never to her face - came into AWC in November 2005 after scares (following two deaths, one of which was later found to have been staged, the bringing of a bomb into the ring on a live show, and a number of big injuries) over Harber's suitability for the job of Entertainment Manager. She immediately made her mark by imposing new rules on live broadcasts. The tall blonde's power is nominally acquired through the finance of her rich husband (despite their separation), yet Volkyeva inspires such awe as she goes about her dealings that one gets the feeling that without the monetary backing she would have made her way to the top anyway. Her expensive attire and expert make-up hide the fact that she is in her late forties, and fan polls frequently place her above many of AWC's younger females in the "Prettiest" stakes. In the beginning she was never seen without her personal bodyguard, but lately she has appeared more and more frequently without her minder, indicating a growing confidence in her stature that could spell doom or glory for AWC, all depending on her intentions - which remain unknown.
