The Q. Biography Real Name Nicholas [name withheld] Nicknames Shadowlines, The Mirror Hometown Sequester Camp (A primarily outdoor facility for the mentally fringed.) Height 6'7' Weight 298 Biography Soon. Other Feds PRIME, 5SW, GCW Wrestling Style Power/Aggressive - His main goal, in the ring, is to let his opponent know that he's the real deal. From hardcore tactics, to risky rop-rope moves, he'll do absolutely anything. Finisher Move Intervention of the Machines Finisher Description Puts opponent into the same position one would do if they intended on executing a powerbomb. He flips the lifeless body onto his shoulders/upper back and, in snap like fashion, flips him back over so that his stomach and face make absolute contact with the ring canvas. Finisher Setup Move Anything that'll allow enough time to perfect the finisher. Setup Description Burp Other Moves Trademark Shaming of the True: (Opponent has to be knocked out/dazed and flat on the mat) The Q climbs to the top rope and and jumps backwards. Midair, Q pokes his elbows outward, and inevitably lands on the opponent, clocking his ribs as well. Surprise, I broke you in half: A leaping gore after a hard whip into a turnbuckle. The impact the opponent makes against the corner is enough to repel him back towards Q, who then gets a running start and dives, head first, towards his opponent. The Scars of Insincerity: Sit-down powerbomb. Comparable to the Pearl River Plunge that Ahmed Johnson brought to life back in the day. The insincerity unfolds after the move is executed. Q spits at his opponent's face. Watch The Gap: Simply said, Q picks up his opponent after a severe move and slams his bicep/forearm into the middle of the opponent's chest. He tends to stray away from this move because of an incident at a promotional event in Thailand while in PRIME. Without going into too much detail, it involved a young boy who didn't like clowns. Q insisted that he was a 'friendly' clown who served no harm whatsoever. The young boy's father gets involved. Words are thrown around. Whamo, 'Watch The Gap' like an aluminum bat into the father's heart shell. Q wound up in jail. General Does DDT's obsessively. His theory is that it only takes a couple blows to the head for a human being to feel legitimate effects of an ass beating. On occasion, you may see Q do a headlock or sleeper. You will literally never see him doing anything truly submissive. Whether it's the fact that he doesn't like to do them, or doesn't know how is another story. Put the fuck up, or shut the fuck up Q's brutal. He wants to hurt whoever he's matched up with because, in all honesty, wins mean much more than people say they do. Q understands this, and thus, beats the snot out of his opponents. Whether he loses or wins, he'll always leave some kind of mark. He has a way of turning even the straightest of matches into a no-holds-barred, hardcore bloodfest. He's also very twisted and seven or eighteen tools short of a whole box, which helps. Entrance: Opening acoustic guitar seems to fall like snowflakes from the arena ceiling as all the lights stay shut off. The video screen sends those in attendance on a storybook of colors. Colors in all different shapes and sizes sporadically mold together and turn into something completely different. Then, with a blast of drums, guitars, and a bass line so severe, a white screen of light blows up. Comparable to a nuclear explosion, the light slowly starts to dim, which allows enough room for a shadow to trot down the ramp. The Q, now revealed and prepared for anything, marches down the ramp and into the ring. Entrance Music Welcome Home' by Coheed and Cambria
