Kris CarMichael Biography Real Name Kristopher David CarMicheal Nicknames Kris, KC, 'The Defect', 'The Rebel' Hometown Detroit, Michigan Birthday September 9, 1980 Height 6'3' Weight 210 Biography Ring Entrance: The lights of the entire arena disappear leaving no light whatsoever. Screams and yells are heard from everyone. 'A wraith with an angel's body A demon with a smile of gold You soulsucker I won't become like you A killer with the perfect weapons, crystal eyes, and a heart of coal You soulsucker I won't lose myself in you' ‘Bleeding Mascara’ by Atreyu kicks on the PA system now as the lights flicker. They turn on completely showing Kris CarMicheal standing on the top of the stage. Over his right shoulder rests his FnX championship. He wears his signature underOATH tee-shirt and black pants adorned with numerous chains and foreign objects. His Mohawk stands tall in the center of his head. Kris begins on his walk down the ramp. Kris looks around at all the norms and laughs to himself. 'Look how pretty she is when she falls down Now there is no beauty in bleeding mascara Lip are quivering like a withering rose She's back again' Kris slides under the bottom rope and gets to his feet. He throws his title down on the mat and jumps to the back turnbuckle. He crosses his arms in an ‘X’ revealing his black finger-paint. He smiles at the boos he hears and waits for his latest challenge. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ History: The day Kristopher David CarMicheal was born was supposed to be a happy day. A day where his parents were supposed to rejoice. They didn’t. At least, his mother didn’t. Kris’s father had left when he heard they were going to have a baby. He just couldn’t do it at that time. When Kris came into the world, his mother looked at him and sighed. She knew that the road ahead was going to be tough. A road that she really didn’t want to travel on. Kris began to grow up and his mother tried to help him. She was poor though. Kris found himself with very little food and only one toy. Life was truly hard. He lived in a small run-down house not too much bigger than a shack. There was one floor, one bathroom, and one bedroom. Kris didn’t know anything at that point so it didn’t bother him. His mother on the other hand, she knew that this couldn’t go on. She took him to an orphanage to try and give him a better life. Kris lived in the orphanage for two years until he was five years old. At that time, he was adopted by a new family. This was a family that wanted to pass on their legacy to a son. The father was a big name in the car industry and the mother was a lawyer. They had tight rules to follow. Kris made his way into school. He was overjoyed at first. The thought of seeing others like him was a dream come true. That dream fell to the floor and shattered when he found kids were nasty to him. His haircut that he had found likable (A Mohawk) was a joke to most kids. As he moved up in grades, he found himself even more unaccepted. No one seemed to like him or anything associated with him. Kris lived through these years of hell and moved on to a young gentleman. He left his adopted family at age seventeen and looked to make it into the world of wrestling. He wanted to pay back the world for what they did to him. Kris found luck with a trainer. The trainer went by the name ‘Picture Perfect’ Pat Sweeny. Pat trained Kris in the fields of lucha libre and various other branch off styles. That wasn’t what he enjoyed most though. He wanted to truly hurt others. When he made his debut in HSW, he made it with Jamie. Jamie was his girlfriend and manager. The two worked together magnificently. On his journey he ran across a boy named Odis. Odis came in from an abusive father and traveled with Kris. Odis left the journey when he wanted to stay with a special Sensei they met. He taught Kris the ways of PAIN and endurance. Kris continued to wrestle with his changing styles. He went from wishing to disengage the simulator to his badass attitude. With the FnX title around his waist, he has learned that PAIN for him is only pleasure. He finds fascination in hurting others. Jamie saw this and found herself scared. Kris was unstable. Jamie left Kris alone. He sat with nothing to keep him sane. That twisted unstable side he shows is now nearly permanent. He sees things. Hears things. He's not close to being right in the mind.
