The Annoying Neighbours Biography Biography They are friends of James von Doom who live next door and mooch food off him and try to get him and all of his friends involved in their lame, stupid schemes to make money. He trained them to be wrestlers so they'd stop annoying him, but that hasn't stopped TAN from doing what they do best. Be annoying. They got their stupid nicknames from James just for that. Joey is called HIV Joey because he thought HIV had some to do with an IV pole, so the name stuck. He also said that STDs were TBS's and LSD was LDS. These stupid displays make him look even more like an idiot than he actually is...not really though, but I'm trying to be nice. Lamar is called the Kebbler Elf because of his small stature and pudginess. He looks like Savio Vega meets Harvey Whippleman or something. Weird guy. He's Joey's sidekick, but, in reality, they are just James von Doom's newest trainees and are his bitches in every way, shape, or form. To see that this is true, they once agreed to jump off a bridge if he'd jump. James told them he was joking and they thought he was lying so they went to jump off the bridge anyway, but luckily, James talked them out of it and they've been stalking him ever since.
