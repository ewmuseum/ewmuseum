Steven Xandrous Biography Real Name Steven Xandrous Birthday August 17th, 1977 Height 6'3' Weight 220 Biography He is in his mid-thirties, and he wakes up in the middle of nowhere, doesn't know who he is or anything. But for some reason, he has all these flashbacks to his childhood and he keeps seeing the same man over and over laying in a pool of blood in flashbacks. He's stuck in the middle of nowhere wearing only jean shorts and he finds this guy trying to fix his car. Then somehow Steven pulls out a knife, and he doesn't know how he did and he's flipping out and the guy asks him for help. Steven slits the man's throat, leaving him to die. Steven escapes the 'nowhere' but when he sees the man dead, he has that flashback again. He looks in the mirror and he sees himself, realizing that it was he who was in the pool of blood.He's left to walk all the way back to civilization to try to remember his past. He has extremely light blue eyes which looks more white than blue. He has two permanent scars on his face, one about three inches long, starting from his eye, and the other by his nose. He hair is pitch black and ruffled, and he has sideburns extending into a goatee. His body is medium build, and he has lots of scratches and scars on his body. In the ring, he wears gloves and tights, both pitch black, and white boots. Wrestling Style Meticulous Striker, Submission Finisher Move Homicidal Wreckage Finisher Description Xandrous picks his opponent up onto his shoulders and tosses the body upwards and slips his arm around their neck which a Diamond Cutter-like move.
