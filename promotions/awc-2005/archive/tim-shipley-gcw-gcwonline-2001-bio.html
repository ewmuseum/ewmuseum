I. Character Background Tim Shipley is just an ordinary college guy, struggling to make ends meet so he can continue to study Mathematics at the respected Harbrook College in Albany. The shy computerite also happens to possess a love of wrestling, so it only seems natural for him to attempt to get a job in pro wrestling to cover him through college and, if he has extreme success, potentially set up a future permanent career path. With an IQ of 143, much of the mental side of the game goes into Shipley's grappling style. Although Shipley enjoys wrestling very much, the game really takes a backseat role to his struggles with normal life. Shyness around girls, struggling to get college assignments finished in time and failure to get invited to the best frat parties are all part-and-parcel of the Tim Shipley experience. He has a thin, wiry frame with little visible muscle and a flat but not toned stomach. He has a very light skin tone. Tim Shipley has thin, brittle, straw-coloured hair which is spiked up at odd angles as if he has not touched it since waking up - this is often the case. Tim's face is narrow in shape; he has thin, almost colourless lips and minimalist eyebrows. His eyes are a light grey-blue. Tim hasn't the best skin in the world - he will normally be seen with some acne spots on his chin and perhaps a few others around his face, particularly on the nose and forehead. He is always seen with a quizzical expression on his face. A. Important Life Notes -His run in AWC is strictly summer-only, as college is his main priority in life. -Has an IQ of 143; this intelligence is often evident in his match tactics. -His parents had Tim very late; as such, he was brought up in a different way to most children and left to his own devices more. His parents didn't socialise at all with the parents of other kids at school, which didn't help Tim make friends, so young Tim became a loner, devoting his time to his schoolwork and the computer; both playing games and developing skills such as web design and programming. -Has always been poor, and so is very prudent with money. His two luxuries: a laptop, for college, and an iPod because he loves music. Will often resist the urge to speak up if in the presence of someone who doesn't know the value of money and fritters it away. Much of his income is sent to his parents, who are living off practically nothing in their box of a flat. Both Tim and his parents have better financial security since Tim's six-month run in Core Wrestling. B. Athletic History Tim always hated team sports, but still endeavoured to keep himself fit all the way through high school, perhaps because he had little else better to do. ‘A healthy mind in a healthy body', as they say. C. Professional Wrestling Core was Tim's first wrestling promotion. He originally went for xCw tryouts, but trainer Six Knots was so impressed with Tim's natural ability that he spoke with cW owner Matthew Lanier about bringing him straight in to the Core roster. Lanier agreed, and although Tim was hesitant at first to accept the spotlight, he is growing into his role. Reaching the semi-finals of Ringmaster really boosted Tim's confidence, and success in the feud against Jake Summers coupled with a teaming with friend Tony Aliso (culminating in capturing the Tag Team titles) pushed this further. However, Tony's untimely assassination at the hands of Knucks deeply shocked Tim, and so his return to wrestling in AWC will understandably be fraught with nerves and hesitation. D. Soft Spots and Weaknesses -Tim is very careful regarding ascending to the top rope. He has a fear of heights and is worried that with the pressure of thousands of fans watching him, the giddy heights of the top rope may be too much for him and the risk is too high. He will, however, happily mount the second turnbuckle for his trademark Chi-Squared Drop, and the top rope is a viable option when desperate or very confident. -Tim doesn't like to cheat. He will NEVER do so unless desperate. II. Wrestling Style Generally a mat technician, using waist-locks, leg takedowns, basic submissions; but Tim's slight frame means that in desperation he can pull out a high-risk top-rope move with relative ease. Tim will not normally resort to brawling, but if so it will be in the form of a flurry of weak punches. Tim is an intelligent worker and will usually come to the ring with a gameplan, often choosing to target a specific area of the body. He will thrive on an opponent's known weakness, or if his opponent has had injury problems in a specific area, Tim will make sure to work on this fragile area of the body. III. Forbidden Maneuvers Eye rake, low blow, usually any weapon attacks, any form of cheating, super kick, any top rope manoeuvre involving a lot of athleticism (a somersault is about the most he can manage by way of athletic manoeuvring; no moonsaults, 450 splashes, phoenix splashes etc.).
