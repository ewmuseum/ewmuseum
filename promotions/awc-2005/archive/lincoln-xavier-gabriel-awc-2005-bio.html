Lincoln Xavier Gabriel Biography Real Name Lincoln Xavier Gabriel Nicknames The Extraordinary Gentleman, The Saint, Every Man, Link Hometown Philadelphia, Pennyslvania Birthday July 4, 1985 Height 6' 1' Weight 211 Biography Born and raised in Philadelphia, LXG started out his life as a normal child in a normal neighborhood doing normal things. He was never popular, but he was never one of the outcasts either. He was... for all intents and purposes... normal. He could easily be missed in a crowd, but he was always seen. It’s very hard to explain, but that’s just the way he was. Even when it came to academics, he was average. Never above, never below. Just average. It was like someone had taken the definition of normal... personified it... and turned it into Lincoln. The normalcy ended for Lincoln in the fifth grade, though, when something terrible happened. He died. It was an accident, everyone said. He was never supposed to be in the car alone. The parking brake was faulty. He changed the gears himself. All sorts of rumors surrounded the death of this child, but none of them ever seemed to make sense. None of the theories ever came full circle. That’s because none of them were true. In fact, his death wasn’t even true. As a child that wouldn’t be missed, Lincoln was taken from his family, friends and life to become the guinea pig for a government experiment: Project Prodigy. Project Prodigy was the study of a wonder drug that was supposed to increase the mental capacity and capability of the brain. It was supposed to turn normal men and women into geniuses. It was supposed to revolutionize and change the world. Two out of three isn’t that bad, right? Known within the program as One, Lincoln was studied and watched closely for the next three years. He was like a lab rat, as they didn’t even have the decency to give him the guise of a normal life. All he knew were doctors, experiments, needles and pain. Puberty was when the drug seemed to finally work. It clicked out of the blue, like an X-Gene activation of Marvel fame. At age 14, he became a sponge for knowledge and one of the fastest learners on the planet. It really was a miracle drug. Project Prodigy tested his limits as much as possible... but he seemed to have none. He become adept at Martial Arts within a month. He was doing advanced trigonometry and calculus, perfectly, in a week. It was like... they had recreated Einstein, only better. Which is what they wanted. Anything they taught him, stuck in his brain like it had been there his entire life. He seemed to have no limits. He seemed to be the perfect mortal. Because medically, there was no difference between him and anyone else. This last fact is what interested the military. General Marcus Ambrose was the man that led the operation. He wanted Project Prodigy’s formula and he didn’t want to have to deal with the red tape to get it. So he led a team of operatives on a search and retrieval mission to the labs to get it. An inside tip alerted One’s personal doctor to the raid and she did the only thing she could think of to do. She put the formula in One’s head, sent him off into the world... then killed herself. They found her in her car the next morning. One was 18 when he was thrust out into the world on his own. No family, no money, nothing to his name but the clothes on his back. All he had were artificial memories and a bar code on his right shoulder. It was like forced amnesia or something. He wandered the streets for a good two weeks before he stumbled across Meredith Gabriel, MD. She was stranded, he car stopped working right in the middle of a rain storm. It was horrible. The only reason One was out there was that he had no shelter for the night. He offered assistance, she accepted. She was amazed at how he managed to fix the car in less time than it took for her to finish thanking him. In an effort to repay him for his help, she took him to the nearest shelter, making a promise to come by in the morning to see how he was doing. That visit sparked a friendship that would last from then on. Soon she realized that the man was suffering from amnesia, but somehow managed to retain an intelligence level beyond any she’s heard of. Eventually, she took him in and he’s become an honorary member of her family, by taking on the name of her father, Lincoln Xavier Gabriel. While it may seem like he has a solid grip on his life, there are still many questions that need to be answered. Will his past come back to haunt him? Will the nightmares ever stop? Will he ever find his true family? What does the future hold for a man that will, apparently, never stop learning? Wrestling. Duh. Wrestling Style Martial Arts / High Flyer Finisher Move Second Chance Finisher Description Leaving a groggy opponent within range, LXG runs to the ropes and jumps onto the top one. Pushing off, he slingshots himself back towards the opponent to perform a flying thrust kick to their face. Finisher Setup Move Memory Loss Setup Description This is a move that is never done the same way twice. Sort of like a personified version of his amnesia, Memory Loss tends to change from match to match. It consists of a quick series of punches and kicks, put together in classic Martial Arts combo style. These aren’t random, though, as they’re more focused on pressure points of the body. The end result tends to be leaving a stunned or groggy opponent standing in the ring... waiting to be finished. Other Moves Trademark Moves: - Mercy: This is a move that hints at a dark side for LXG. This is usually performed when he’s angry to the point of being incapable of rational thought. Designed as a counter to a punch or another move that will allow him to catch the arm of an opponent and lock it beneath his arm. He’ll then bring his other arm around and simply hammer away at his opponent’s face until he’s stopped, they fall unconscious or beg for mercy.
