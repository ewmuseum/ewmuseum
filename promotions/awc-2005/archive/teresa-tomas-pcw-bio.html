Teresa Tomas Biography Real Name Sonya Nicknames Tope Rope Temptress, THE American Woman, Redneck Princess Hometown Nashville, Tennessee Birthday 6-9-78 Height 5'7 Weight 145 Biography Daughter of a well respected US Navy Captain; Teresa was what you would call a Navy brat. Growing up, she always hung out with the boys. She found the girls were boring and too wimpy. This of course labeled her as a ‘tomboy’ and eventually, she did become ‘one of the boys’ so to speak. She is known to be tough and stubborn with a temper that can become violent at times. Only the few closest to her has seen her more softer, gentler side as well as what the public sees her as. Due to past experiences in the business field, she knows the rules of the game and takes nothing from anybody, thinking it best to work for what she wants. Besides her wrestling career, she also sponsors her own monster truck with a life long friend and ex as her driver. The two people she calls family are her brother Eric and her mother who both live in her home state of Tennessee just outside of Nashville. Now at the age of 27, she knows her life isn’t even half over and expects to live it to its fullest. Other Feds LTL, PWA, RSW, NTICW, PCW, RPW, OSE, GWA,HSW Other Fed Awards GWA Women's Title, PCW TV Title Wrestling Style High Flyer/ Technical Finisher Move Southern Comfort Finisher Description Teresa dropkicks the person's knees causing them to go down. While they are down Teresa grabs their right arm, twists it and turns so they are on their stomach with their arm twisted. Then she sits down so she's kneeling facing away form them, grabs their leg and pulls it back. Finisher Setup Move Flying Squirrel Setup Description Jump toward the opponent from the top rope either toward the head or the back of the knee forcing her opponent to the mat. Other Moves Signature Moves: Flying head kick off the top rope sometimes done with a back flip in the air before contact is made. Top rope moonsault. Top rope hurricanrana Teresa uses various acrobatic moves to maneuver her way around her apponent and uses her usual lesser weight to her advanage with quickness. Entrance Music 'Big Guns' by AC/DC
