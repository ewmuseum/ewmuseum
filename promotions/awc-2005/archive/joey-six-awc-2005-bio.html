Joey Six Biography Real Name Josiah Farnsworth Nicknames J-6, Five-And-One, J-Limit, and tons of other stupid ones he'll make up from time to time Hometown Jacksonville, FL Birthday June 25, 1987 Height 6'2' Weight 189 Wrestling Style Spotmonkey / Bad Finisher Move Six Second Hangover Finisher Description Top Rope Corkscrew Moonsault Finisher Setup Move Kick-Snap STO or Superkick Setup Description ...no. Other Moves He only knows, like, six. Six real wrestling moves. It's how he got his nickname. Here they are: Six Second Hangover Kick-Snap STO Tornado DDT (He's actually good with this one. He does it a variety of different ways, most commonly out of a spinning headscissors, more commonly called a Deja Vu) Hurricarana Small Package Superkick (Yeah, I know it barely counts. He was almost Joey Five.) In all honesty, this is his most commonly used move: Getting The Shit Kicked Out Of Him Joey's got loads of natural acrobatic talent, he can flip and stuff. Since this is the case, he'll try to do it a lot. This will undoubtably frustrate his partner. And that's about it. He's still learning. Oh, and his chops and punches look weak, but he kicks real well. It's probably the one thing keeping Jiro from giving up on him entirely. On a barely related side note, he'll make a hand signal for Collision Course that's supposed to look like twin C's, but actually looks more like he's a reject from the Thriller video. Try it, and you'll see how ridiculous it looks. Entrance Music 'Feel Good, Inc.' by Gorillaz
