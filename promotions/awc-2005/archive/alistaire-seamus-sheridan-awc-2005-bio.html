Alistaire Seamus Sheridan Biography Real Name Alistaire Seamus Sheridan Nicknames ASS, Asshole, the Blacklion, Four-Leaf Phenomenon Hometown Blacklion, Ireland Birthday April 10th, 1969 (36 y/o) Height 6'9' Weight 388 ½ Biography Description of Appearance: Alistaire Seamus Sheridan looks like your stereotypical Irishman, only with an added twist. He stands 6’9” tall and weighs an enormous 388 ½ lbs. Most of his weight comes from his ripped physique, but he does have the beer belly to go along with it. He has the natural ghastly white complexion that accompanies every true Irishman. For his attire, think stereotypical Irishman/leprechaun. Here’s a link of what I want, pretty much: http://www.funfolly.com/g/mascots/ci257mud.jpg. However, take note to one change: I want the green shamrocks on the undershirt to actually be *orange four-leaf clovers*. As for the rest of the attire provided in the link, it’s pretty much accurate. The green jacket and pants, white socks and gloves, and black boots. As for the aforementioned added twist, it is the simple fact that he dons a mask to hide his entire head. Here’s a reference link: http://i20.ebayimg.com/03/i/05/e9/5e/3b_1_sbl.JPG. The linked four-leaf clover is what is emblazoned on the front of his mask *without* the leprechaun, the black lines on the inside of the cloverleaf, and the green flames surrounding the cloverleaf. Just the four-leaf clover and its colors (although the green and orange should be more neon looking). The rest of the mask is the same color of white as the middle stripe of the cloverleaf. The mask also has that shiny, squeaky clean affect. History: 'He's the sweetest ninja ever.' -- a random, openly homosexual pirate. Wrestling Style Blacklion ASS Beating. As a former bare-knuckle boxer, busting balls and hymens on a regular basis is what he enjoys. With his incredible strength for untrained and intoxicated powerhouse wrestling, he loves to gloat and show-off his colossal power by literally throwing and destroying his foes with ease. At times, when he chooses to tip the bottle a little more than usual, he may even unleash his ultra rare drunken kung-fu fighting style. However, for the most part, he sticks to dismantling every limb from its body in sight. Finisher Move Holy Christ on a Cross! Finisher Description A crucifix neckbreaker. While applying a standing headscissors onto the opponent, the attacker lifts them into the air before suspending them above and behind his head in the crucifix powerbomb position. Rather than actually executing a crucifix powerbomb, the attacker powers them over his head while kneeling to one knee. As the opponent descends, the attacker spikes the back of their neck into his knee. Thus it is called a crucifix neckbreaker. With the amount of power behind this maneuver, this is the end-all be-all of ASS' arsenal and usually causes a serious neck injury to his opponent. He will more than likely sustain a minor knee injury from this precarious move as well. Note: This is his ULTIMATE finishing maneuver. Therefore, there is no kicking out from this move. The opponent is rendered useless and more than likely unconscious. He only uses this maneuver when it is absolutely necessary because he usually defeats opponents by other means. Please ask for my permission before using this move. Finisher Setup Move ASS PUNCH~! Setup Description Well, let's see here. There's an ASS, and then there's an ass. Picture the ASS lifting his opponent into the air with a left-handed choke lift and delivering a devastating one-hitter quitter (with a force equal to 100 one-hitter quitters) into the opponent's ass. Yes, the crowd loves it. Yes, it's pretty fucking bizarre. And yes, this is the newfound antidote for colon cancer (and testicular cancer in some cases). Other Moves Canadian rack powerbomb. Celtic Cross: While executing a crossed-leg Boston Crab, the attacker makes sure to sit down on top of the opponent's back. With ASS' size and weight, it only adds more pain to the hyperextension of the opponent's spine. Devastating sit-out jawbreaker. Kick in the ol' bollocks. Top o' the Mourning: Vicious snap crossed-arm powerbomb into the turnbuckle, which is usually exposed beforehand. Two-handed chokehold into sit-out chokeslam.
