Amy Silveira Biography Real Name Amélia Constancia Silveira Hometown Lisbon, Portugal Birthday July 1st, 1987 Height 5'7' Weight 116 Fed Awards Fresh!man (19/07/05) Biography Amy Silveira was born Amélia Constancia Silveira on July 1st, 1987. A native of Lisbon, Portugal, she was the second child to Hugo Cristiano Davi Silveira and Cecilia Kristiansen Silveira. Her older sister, Catarina Silveira, was born on September 16th, 1984. Amy grew up in Portugal and, from a young age, got interested in gymnastics and kickboxing. She would continue with these whilst growing up, with the enthusiasm of her father driving her forward. The family moved to Los Angeles, USA in late 2000 when Amy was 13. She had always used her native name of Amélia up until the move, but at her new school her friends began to call her Amy, and it stuck. She and her sister had no problem fitting in Stateside. Hugo Silveira, Amy's father, had been a successful businessman for many years. He had also, however, been an unsuccessful manager in the wrestling industry. He had always dreamed of being involved in some sort of wrestling success story, but his many failures left him with only one choice - His youngest daughter. Amy had never even thought of wrestling as a career, but it was her fathers dream, and after years of grooming her without anyone suspecting at all, AWC provided him the chance to have his last try at success. And Amy reluctantly agreed. After all, it was her father, and she didn't have much say in the situation. Other Feds None (for the character, as a handler several) Wrestling Style High-flying and martial arts Finisher Move Torcendo Moonsault Finisher Description A corkscrew moonsault. Finisher Setup Move White Lightning Setup Description A powerful spinning heel kick to the chest area. Other Moves As Silveira practiced gymnastics from a young age in Portugal, she has high agility and thus can do some of the most complex high-flying moves. Her finisher is one such move. Her wrestling, however, also incorporates and often features kicks and other moves related to martial arts and kickboxing. Her weakness would be any powerful moves, and actual technical wrestling manouvers. The latter she would be unlikely to carry out, due to a lack of actual in-ring training and experience. Silveira's signature moves are her set-up move and finisher. She also performs the super springboard dropkick as a quick, unexpected aerial/martial arts attack, sometimes as a set-up move or even counter attack. Silveira calls this move 'Repentio Morte'. This move is: ------------------------------------------------------------ Amy quickly jumps onto the turnbuckle, spins around and performs a flying dropkick, either to the outside or into the ring. ------------------------------------------------------------ Another signature move, often used as a second finisher with powerful effect, is the shoot kick. If using the 'Torcendo Moonsault' is not possible, Silveira will use the shoot kick, which she calls the 'Thunder kick'. Performed quickly and often with little expectation of the move, it is: ------------------------------------------------------------ A kickboxing-style kick with the shin, striking an opponent's face or chest. ------------------------------------------------------------ She also occasionally uses the 'Sunset flip'. A description of the move is: ------------------------------------------------------------ This is a pinning move where a wrestler and his/her opponent face each other, with the attacker on higher ground (such as the top turnbuckle). The attacker dives over the victim, catches him/her in a waistlock from behind, and rolls into a sitting position as he hits the mat. As the attacker rolls over, he/she pulls the victim over backwards so that he/she lands on his/her back. ------------------------------------------------------------ Among her other moves are the springboard dropkick (like 'Repentio Morte' only off the top rope), the Lionsault, the split legged moonsault ('A moonsault variation in which the performer jumps to the top rope, but before moonsaulting drops down, so that his/her legs split, and flips back using his/her thighs.') and the split legged splash (same as previous, only a splash, not a moonsault). Some of Silveira's most common kicks are the roundhouse kick, sidekick, spinning heel kick (aka 'White Lightning', her set up move) and occasionally the scissors kick. Silveira uses some power-orientated moves, although few are classed as her key moves. She may often use the kesagiri chop, which is a diagonal chop to the opponents neck, and she occasionally uses the clothesline and flying clothelines. Other power moves are used to more effect against other female wrestlers and cruiserweights, but Silveira knows that power moves will have limited effect against male heavyweights. Silveira rarely uses submission moves, but should the oppurtunity occur can perform the sharpshooter and several leg locks. OVERVIEW - Finishers Torcendo Moonsault (Corkscrew moonsault, dominant finisher) Thunder Kick (Shoot kick, see above for description of move) - Trademark Moves White Lightning (Spinning heel kick) Repentio Morte (Super springboard dropkick, see above for description of move) - Submission Moves Doesn't use any regularly. Most commonly Sharpshooter and various leg locks. Entrance Music 'Girls' by Prodigy (Hedley 4 AWC Edit)
