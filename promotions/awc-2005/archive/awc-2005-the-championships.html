The Championships

TRANSATLANTIC CHAMPION: Garbage Bag Johnny CONTENDERS: 1 Ellis Nash 2 Pierce Lavelle 3 Darcy Crisis The Transatlantic championship is nominally bestowed on the individual capable of the greatest in-ring output in AWC. This is by far the best respected of the title belts AWC has to offer, and represents the crowning accomplishment of an athlete's career. FRONTIER CHAMPION: Darcy Crisis CONTENDERS: 1 Aimz 2 Jack Murphy 3 AgentDash The idea behind the Frontier championship is to reward those promising athletes who look set to break into the main event scene sooner rather than later. AWC staff plan to associate this title belt with high-quality, fast-paced matches. ALLIANCE CHAMPIONS: The Coalition CONTENDERS: 1 - 2 - 3 - The Alliance championship was introduced a month after AWC's inception, with an innovative points-based league deciding the first champions. The Alliance division is for duos only, with the emphasis on testing teamwork just as much as individual merit. RELENTLESS CHAMPION: Jack Murphy CONTENDERS: 1 Aimz 2 Red Rock 3 Captain Suleimon The Relentless championship rewards those wrestlers who can overcome every obstacle to prove their superiority in the ring. The champion will have shown his ability to prevail under a number of different circumstances, usually in no-disqualification situations. LIVEWIRE CHAMPION: Johnny Lexicon CONTENDERS: 1 Josh Marquez 2 Blue Ruin 3 Tyson DeBough Athletic, energetic, keen and willing to learn. These are the attributes of a good Livewire champion. This title belt is aimed at the lower- to mid-carders of the AWC, and acts as a pathway to stardom. The championship division was disbanded in December 2005 and resumed in September 2006. COUNTDOWN TROPHY HOLDER: (title disbanded) A temporary accolade introduced during the summer 2006 roster split to ensure cross-Atlantic opportunities for midcarders. Always defended under special rules: a five-minute time limit and then sudden-death (one-second pinfalls) until a winner was found, and had to be defended at every show before Coast To Coast. Paddy O'Shea was rewarded for being the last holder with a Transatlantic title shot. TRANSATLANTIC CHAMPION (CROWN): (title disbanded) When Adam Dick won the Transatlantic championship he melted down the original belt to form a Transatlantic crown which he wore for the duration of his reign. It was later discovered that he had tricked Sasha Volkyeva into sanctioning the crown as an official championship separate from the original Transatlantic title, which is now represented by a new belt. Henceforth the belt and crown were treated as separate accolades until Coast To Coast 2006 when Garbage Bag Johnny reunified them.
