B.O.T. Biography Real Name Jarrett Clemans Nicknames The Notorious B.O.T., The Bishop of Bottitude, Tenacious B Hometown Moorestown, NJ Birthday May 17, 1973 Height 6'8' Weight 296 Biography -Long Term Supporting Cast- Name: Dimitri Clemans Profession: Wrestling Trainer (Technique Specialty), Retired Pro-Wrestler Once a world wide legend, Dimitri 'Trigger' Clemans passed up the fame and fortune, handing the torch over to his younger brother, Jarrett 'B.O.T.' Clemans, andcashing in on a subtle life of the day to day grind. Not being able to stay away from the game for too long, though, he opened a wrestling school in 2003 with B.O.T., and would become its bookkeeper/Co-owner. He has beaten a passive form of lung cancer recently. Name: Ai Kawashimi Profession: Wrestling Trainer (Striking Specialty), Semi-Retired Pro-Wrestler, Ex-Yakuza Breaking away from her dangerous affiliations with the Suzuki Family Yakuza based in Tokyo, sheused her natural athletic and highly trained martial arts abilities to enter the women's wrestling circut in Japan. The mob wouldnt let her out so easily, though, demanding she pay them a large percentage of her checks per match. In 2004, while touring in the land of the Rising Sun, B.O.T. met Ai, and took her out on a date. He recognized her skills, and asked her to work at his school to train his students. She refused because of her mob ties, but B.O.T. wouldn't give up so easily. He offered the Yakuza boss a large lump sum to forget she ever exisited. He agreeed without hesitation. Shes been one of his top trainers of both men and women, and one of his closest friends ever since. Name: Ivan Kannon Profession: Wrestling Trainer (High Spots Specialty), Semi-Employed Pro-Wrestler, Degenerate A native of Brisbane, Australia, Ivan was raised in a lower class faimly. Always fighting throughout his teens, he had a reputation of being nothing more than a thug or troublemaker. It was 'suggested' to him by his father that he 'straighten up' and get a 'real' job. Taking that to heart, he moved to the United States in search of something to make a career out of. Nothing suited him, until he saw a wrestling match from the ICW promotion, and watched as its main eventer B.O.T. (Then known as JBOT) beat the sotout of some unsuspecting punk. As much as B.O.T. dislikes people like Ivan, (loud, un-educated, etc.) but he couldnt seem to get away from this one. Ivan, along with Virgil White, had followed B.O.T. from venue to venue for six months straight in the summer of 2001 in hopes to be his understudies. B.O.T. consistantly declined them, but would eventually take them under his wing, even if only to run errands for him. He would soon begin to train them, however, and book them in small time matches. In 2003, he hired the pair to become trainers in his wrestlign school. Name: Virgil White Profession: Wrestling Trainer (Ground Game Specialist), Semi-Employed Pro-Wrestler, Fallen Star. A humble, amazing athelete throughout his highschool years, NFL scouters all across the country wanted to make this young stud from Atlanta, Georgia the next big thing. Off the field, Virgial was an all-around gentlemen, always giving to charities and helping when needed. Though, an unfortunate accident with propane and alcohol would make him an afterthought. He burned his vocal chords out, making him mute for the rest of his life. The barrier of communication that was errected there after was a big factor in Virgil's change of attitude. He became frustrated with the amount of effort it took to get simple ideas across to his friends and family. He also grew angered over the leauges lack of intrest in him, now that he could no longer speak for commercials and interviews. To vent his frutrations he found a new hobby, fighting. Frequenting MMA bouts across the country, he wouldn't break into the pro wrestling scene until he met Ivan Kannon, another deliquient with a will and a way. Name: Dr. Emily Sparks Profession: Psychiatrist, Hopeless romantic B.O.T.s personal shrink, because face it, he's crazy. Whenever the limelight (or any light, for that matter) makes our hero feel as if hes not himself, she pulls him together, countering his prideful, arrogant logic with professional medical analysis. Though an air of sexual tension has arisen between the two, there's no real drive from either side to make anything of it. Diviorced, she hopes to find her true love one day... -Recent Supporting Cast- Names: Bass, Glum, and Zeik Professions: Henchmen The hands of the Society's agenda, charged with the task of delivering the message to B.O.T. that he owes their boss money. They dress the part well, looking like they may very well be a dangerous trio, but when it comes to execution, well, they should probably watch more Pacino. Name: Sashenka Spence Profession: FBI Agent, Heartbreaker An old friend of the Clemans boys, she grew up with them in East Paterson during their school years. And then, almost like that, she picked up and left for what was rumored to be Europe to meet her biological parents. She would return a few months ago, for what seemed to be a rekindling of a romance with B.O.T., but would instead be revealed to be her attempts to bring down a dangerous international organized crime syndicate called The Society. Name: 'Lady Harken' Profession: Crime Boss, Mystery Lady Little is known about the women who calls herself 'Lady Harken,' except that she and Dimitri know each other, and she has some unfinished business with B.O.T., business he knows nothing about. More to come... Other Feds XWF, IWF, ICW, WWA, REW, XPW, Federation E, XCW, HWF, OWO, NOW, and a small assortment of independant work. Other Fed Awards IWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP X3, IWF HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP X1, IWF INTERNET CHAMPIONSHIP X2, IWF INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP X2, ICW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP X1, ICW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP X2, ICW INTERNET CHAMPIONSHIP X1, ICW UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP X1, FEDERATION E WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP X1, XCW NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP X1, XCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP X2, OWO TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP X1 Wrestling Style MMA, Submission, Technical, with a sprinkle of raw power. Finisher Move B.O.T. Drop 01' to Murder, He Wrote Finisher Description The deadly combination of B.O.T.'s 'B.O.T. Drop '01' (Emerald Fusion) and 'Murder, He Wrote' (Triangle Choke w/ Keylock) Finisher Setup Move Dragon Sleeper Setup Description a sleeper applied from reverse DDT position Other Moves Submissionist- A practiced martial artist, B.O.T. takes pride in his wide knowlage of submission holds learn in his studies. Keylock Armbar Leglace Kneelock Posdad to headlock (judo throw to headlock) STF Rolling Butterfly Lock Standing front facelock Standing Crossface Abdominal Stretch to backbreaker Indian Deathlock Camel Clutch Bearhug MURDER, HE WROTE - Signiture Submission where B.O.T. wraps his legs around the opponents head, neck, and a single arm, locking his foot around the back of his knee forming a triangle shaped collar around the opponent. Pulling back on the trapped arm adds pressure to the parts in question, suffocating the opponent.(Triangle Choke) B.O.T. adds to the fun by keylocking the trapped arm. Technical- B.O.T. has spent years making sure his knowlage of the basics is unparralled. His dedication to technique has allowed him to make even the most basic and widely known moves his own by adding his own little flair. This also includes devistating punches and kick combinations. Stalling Suplex Belly to Belly Suplex Wrist Clutch Scoop Slam Capture Suplex Hiptoss Backbreaker Chickenwing Clutch Pin Armdrag B.O.T. DROP 01' - Signature Technical move, a modest variation of the popular Emerald Fusion, which is a side piledriver of sorts. Named after the first time he used it back in 2001, when it was formerly called the 'JBOT Drop' Powerful- Being 6'8 and near 300 lbs has its advantages, including emense power. Its been known to help out all aspects of his combat, or take over when they dont work. Gorrilla Press Slam Running Powerslam Sit Out Spinebuster Samoan Drop Double handed chokeslam MEGALOMANIA - Signature power manuver. After whipping the opponent into the ropes, B.O.T. catches him on the rebound, and whips them around his body, flattening him on the ground after a full revolution. (Black hole slam) Misc- For when B.O.T. gets 'creative.' Though hes rather unpredictable, heres some moves hes been known to frequent. Running Clothlesline Fist Drop Enziguri (in moderation, of course.) Flying Knee Big Boot RIDE THE LIGHTNING - Signature strike, during a sprint, B.O.T. executes a choking leg trip takedown (Running STO) Entrance Music 'Cowboys' by Portishead
