If Lavelle was to lose, his Transatlantic title would be desanctioned, rendering worthless months of hard work. Hamada grief-stricken 11th December 2005 Butterfly Hamada and the AWC received bad news one night after Fresh! from Manchester, England. Hospital reports at last released to us have revealed that Tozen Fujita, better known as "Cosmic Lion" in AWC, has suffered a broken neck and two crushed vertebrae after a barbarous attack Tuesday night by an unknown assailant. He is in critical but stable condition at a local hospital where doctors believe he will make a slow but steady recovery if all goes well. Surgery is slated for Monday morning. Hamada and Fujita came to AWC from Japan where they hoped to continue their careers as professional wrestlers. Hamada is shaken up very badly after this assault on her boyfriend. There are no leads or suspects in the attack, but word has leaked out that a calling card was left during the vicious assault that may lead to more questions. No further information is being released at this time except that Fujita was branded with the word "CYDONIA" on his left shoulder. No AWC wrestlers have been labeled as suspects at this time. Fujita was preparing for his premier match in AWC against Jack "The Bull" Murphy before he was discovered by a masseuse in his dressing room. EMTs were rushed in to take the wrestler to the hospital. Later that night, Hamada faced Murphy in the match as a replacement for Fujita. Hamada blamed Murphy for the attack but there is no evidence linking the Irish-born Murphy to the crime. Authorities are considering leaving the entire incident in the hands of the AWC front office, as they consider this a "wrestling issue" and claim that in this business people tend to keep secrets from outsiders and take care of problems "in house". Butterfly Hamada has had no comment about the incident to anyone outside of the AWC. But authorities in Britain say they are more than ready to come in to press legal charges if anyone is forthcoming with information in the attack. This week's edition of Fresh! has been confirmed as the last live show prior to AWC's upcoming major event Winter Warfare on December 23rd, with Fresh!burst on the 18th given over in its entirety to screening of the AWC Christmas Party, live from Amsterdam. A local children's choir and controversial British rock band Babyshambles, fronted by drug addict Pete Doherty, have been confirmed as live entertainment for the Party, though probably not at the same time. It is now confirmed that the big guy himself, Santa Claus, will be making an appearance on Fresh! this week. The executives and wrestlers are very excited, as they feel that Santa's appearance on the show will bring a cheerful presence to the backstage area, as well as brighten up the show, the card for which has now been released - and once more, David Harber has got past his colleague Sasha Volkyeva's strict rules to book a mouthwatering match between PCW alumni Andy M., Paddy O'Shea and Red Rock, in what is billed a "PCW match" - pipes, chains and wrenches! To win, a wrestler must chain both his opponents to ringposts - and to complicate matters further, Aimz, whose anti-PCW agenda has provoked a negative response from fans of AWC, is injected as guest referee.
