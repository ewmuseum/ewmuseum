Do you have any idea how cotton-mouthy somebody can get from wrestling as hard as Alex Strider did? It’s not like he gets to sit on his ass all night and sip expensive bottled water like somebody I know. Truth Waters: What’s that supposed to mean? You better be talking about your estranged wife, and not me, because I will slap you, Cassidy. Alex Strider: Secondly, as you all may know… there was a little… altercation, after the main event. You’ve all been wondering… ‘What did Strider do to deserve such attention?’ Well, I’d love to set the record straight. What you saw last week, was simply a misunderstanding. Truth Waters: Here he goes again. George Cassidy: You are so biased. Like our audience can actually sit there and take what you say objectively? You HIT this man with a steel chair, completely unprovoked! Maybe you should just hold your judgment until after he’s done speaking. Alex Strider: The police of Florida were there… NOT to arrest me… but to PROTECT me! The audience boos loudly at this obvious lie, not taking a word he says for truth. Strider smiles and nods Alex Strider: Oh, I can tell that you don’t like that. But the truth is… as the FIRST Atlantic Wrestling Club Transatlantic champion I would draw a lot of negative attention. You understand, disgruntled losers might want to harm my person, being jealous of not possessing the talent and skill that took me to world renown in one night’s time. The boos become even louder, and Strider raises his hands to ask for quiet, pausing as the audience calms themselves down and smiling again after they have. Truth Waters: Folks, that is a barefaced lie. I myself watched as he was placed in the back of a patrol car, HANDCUFFED! Alex Strider: The police were there to escort me, and make sure that my place of honor among the elite of society would not be tarnished. And, now that you know the TRUTH… Alex pauses and casts a glaring eye towards the ringside announcer of the same name. George Cassidy: Looks like you’ve earned a little bit of attention, there, Truth. How’s it feel to know the whole world’s looking at you? Truth Waters: It feels fine. This guy doesn’t have nothing on me. Alex Strider: …Now that you know the truth of the matter, I hope you’ll all be able to sleep fine at night, knowing full well that your Transatlantic champion, “The Mongoose”, is safe and sound, and ready to show you exactly the kind of man I am going to be here in the AWC. With that, I’d like to ask Pearl to come on out, and make this thing official. "Bohemian Like You" by the Dandy Warhols plays in the background. The lights converge on the stage, heralding the entrance of AWC's Entertainment Manager, David “Pearl” Harber. Pearl holds in his hand the prize that Strider has earned, the Transatlantic championship belt. James Brunt: Making his way to the ring, the AWC Entertainment Manager… DAVID “PEARL” HARBER! A huge pop greets his arrival. Pearl heads to the ring, shaking the occasional fan’s hand, but he is a bit more determined than his usual demeanour would show. Pearl eyes Strider with an untrusting eye. Truth Waters: The fans love to see this charismatic man. By the looks of it, he hasn’t been fooled by Strider’s excuses. George Cassidy: I think what they love is that Alex Strider is FINALLY going to get the belt he earned at Zero To Hero! Truth Waters: He’s already had the belt. He just didn’t get to keep it when he went to jail, Cassidy. Quiet down now, the man’s about to speak. Pearl: Thank you for the invitation, Strider. I’m… pleased you were finally able to make it tonight. With all the trouble you’ve been having I wasn’t sure what status we would find your job here. The fact of the matter is this, the fans came to see an AWC show, and we’re going to give them the best. The crowd cheers Harber, as he nods appreciatively. Pearl walks around the ring as he speaks, the AWC Transatlantic Title in his off hand. Pearl: Alex, I don’t think you changed a single person’s mind with your speech. But I’ve come down here for a reason. Two reasons, actually. First off, I haven’t known you long, but what I do know is this: you’re scandalous, you’re a liar; and you’re a cheat. Alex shakes his head vigorously at the Entertainment Manager as the fans cheer loudly. Alex raises the mic to speak, but is cut off quickly. Pearl: But in spite of all that, you still proved to be the best at Zero To Hero. Barring any further martial interruptions… Pearl raises an eyebrow, getting some laughs from the crowd. Pearl: I am here to present you… with the AWC Transatlantic championship belt! Alex Strider beams, and holds out his hand to accept the belt. The crowd disapproves, and Harber pauses, and almost begrudgingly hands the belt over into Strider’s eager clutches. Truth Waters: Well, I didn’t expect him to not give the belt over, but I don’t think I like the idea of Strider representing what it takes to be a true champion. George Cassidy: Get over it! He worked his ass off. Give the man SOME credit, will you? Strider raises the championship belt high in the air. Running over to the corner, he jumps up, and holds it over his waist in a show of triumph as “Will You Smile Again?”, his theme song, begins to play. Pearl makes a motion with his hand to cut the music, interrupting Strider’s victory lap. Strider pauses and looks angrily at Pearl. Alex Strider: What’s the idea, Harber? Pearl: Just one more thing, Strider. Remember, I said I came down here to do two things? One thing, was reunite you with that belt. The second thing… is for the FANS. You should be happy you’re the champion. And next week, on Fresh!, we’ll get to see just how great of a champion you’re going to be… when you DEFEND the Transatlantic championship for the very first time… The crowd pops very loudly. Strider frowns. Truth Waters: The title will be defended next week on Fresh! This is great! Alex Strider: You know what, Pearl? That’s just fine with me. I can’t wait, because you’re going to get exactly what you ask for: A GREAT DISPLAY from AWC’s Transatlantic champion. You had all better pay close attention, because you don’t know it, because you’re about to watch a star go supernova... Pearl: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Hold off the catchphrases. Don’t you want to know who you’re facing? Strider just stares at him as the fans quieten down. Pearl: Now, earlier in the night I told you that AWC’s wrestlers would really have to work for their title shots, and I’m staying true to my word. Every week on Fresh!, I will elect one person for that show who has taken it to the next level. A most valuable player. A leading light. A ‘man of the match’, as they would say in the UK. But I needed a name... so of course, I came up with something cheesy, and named this honour the AWC Fresh!man! The crowd pops and laughs in equal measure. George Cassidy: The Fresh!man?! So... it’s a college first-year...? Eh? Truth Waters: He means that the MVP each week won’t be called the MVP, he’ll be called that week’s Fresh!man. Idiot. Pearl: Now, it’s not just about the honour of becoming Fresh!man that entices workers to do their utmost every single week. No, no. James, if you will? Beaming, the purple-suited, top-hatted James Brunt comes into the ring and takes off his top hat. Fishing into his pockets, he throws a smattering of small pieces of paper into the hat. George Cassidy: What in God’s name is going on... Pearl: (beaming) Every week on Fresh!, I will announce that show’s Fresh!man at the end of the show. The Fresh!man will then choose a number from James Brunt’s hat... Brunt, smiling, shakes the top hat around, mixing up the dozens of small pieces of paper inside. Pearl: If the Fresh!man picks the number 1... he is entered into a three-way match for the Livewire title, which will take place when three participants have been decided in this way. If he picks the number 2, he’ll be entered into a similar match for the Frontier title. And if he picks the number 3... he’ll receive an automatic shot at the Transatlantic title. Oh – and there is also the wild-card; if he picks that, his prize will be at my discretion... Pearl winks. George Cassidy: This is no way to allocate title shots! Bandying them around for drawing a number from a hat! I thought he said people would have to work hard for their shots! Truth Waters: And so they will. Remember, only the Fresh!man – the MVP for a particular night – gets to pick a number. It’s a great idea! Pearl: So without further ado, I will announce this week’s Fresh!man. If he picks the number 3, he gets a shot at Alex Strider next week – Alex Strider: So if he picks a different number, I keep my belt? Harber laughs apologetically. Pearl: Eh, not quite. You see... tonight, the picking of a number is more of a formality. You will defend your title next week... and the title defence will be against tonight’s Fresh!man... so to save a little time, I asked James earlier on to remove all other possibilities. Strider narrows his eyes. Alex Strider: So the pieces of paper in there all have the number 3 on? Pearl laughs. Pearl: That’s right. Alex Strider: (angrily) That means this charade is an entire waste of time! He takes a step forward and knocks the hat out of James Brunt’s hands. The bits of paper scatter everywhere, fluttering to the canvas. Pearl: (coolly) That’s right. I just wanted everyone to become familiar with the concept. But since you won’t allow me that privilege, I’ll simply announce right now who you’ll be facing next week for your title. A silence falls across the Silver Spurs Arena. Pearl: This week’s Fresh!man is... Truth Waters: Who could it be? This person is getting a shot at the Transatlantic title! Pearl: PIERCE LAVELLE! The arena erupts, but more with released tension than anything, some sectors of the crowd still not having warmed to the raw rookie that is Pierce Lavelle. George Cassidy: Lavelle! Pierce Lavelle takes on Alexander Strider next week for the Transatlantic title! Truth Waters: And Strider is furious! Alex Strider: Hold on a minute! I took on Pierce Lavelle this past Friday at Zero To Hero and beat him! What sense is there in making me face him again? He doesn’t deserve another chance! Lavelle screwed it up first time around! Pearl shakes his head. Pearl: Oh, Strider, you don’t want to piss me off. Just shut your mouth for one second and listen to some sense. Pierce Lavelle put in a sterling display tonight to see of the challenge of Zack Johnson – Alex Strider: Who was DEBUTING! It’s not like it was a challenge! Pearl: The point – oh, it doesn’t matter what you think, Strider! Next week you face Lavelle with the Transatlantic title on the line and THAT’S FINAL! Oh... and your favourite referee, Michael Ryan, is going to be in charge... David Harber flashes a grin at “The Mongoose” as “Bohemian Like You” hits the speakers and the Entertainment Manager makes his way down the ramp. Truth Waters: Well that’s it folks! Strider and Lavelle next week with the title on the line! And Michael Ryan will be refereeing! George Cassidy: But that presents no problem for Strider. He told us earlier he’d already sorted things out with the Senior Referee – Truth Waters: Oh, don’t tell me you believe a word of that! Look at Strider. He’s white as a sheet! He knows that he does not want to be getting in the ring with either of Pierce Lavelle or Michael Ryan just yet... George Cassidy: Which is a blessing, because he doesn’t have to just yet. Not until next week when we’ll see you live from Atlanta, Georgia! Truth Waters: That’s all we have time for! Next week is a must-see show! Be sure to tune into Fresh! from the Olympic Arena!
