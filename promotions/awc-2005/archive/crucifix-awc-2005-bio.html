Crucifix Biography Real Name Aiden Nicknames 'Fallen Angel' Hometown O.C. California Birthday 01/22/1979 Height 6'0' Weight 210 Fed Titles None as of yet. Fed Awards None as of yet. Biography The team, Deliverance, consists of the sly and cunning Crucifix and his behemoth of a brother Ghost. These two men have been through what most would call a disrupted and disturbed life. Growing up in the suburbs of O.C. California Crucifix who then went by the name Aiden and his his brother Ghost who went by Colin distanced themselves from their parents. Ghost was always the standoffish one of the two as he sat back and watched his brother do the walking and talking. As time went on the two witnessed their mother and father being slain by a burglar. Crucifix battled the burglar but at the age of twelve could only do so much. Receiving a knife blade acrossed his chest where now he dawns his cross tattoo Crucifix fell lifeless to the floor. Becoming enraged Ghost charged at the man delivering many massive blows to the head of the man with a nearby pendulum. When the police arrived they placed Ghost 'Colin' under arrest and Crucifix was sent to a safehouse or adoption center you may call it. Following the trial as close as he could for a young male Crucifix became aware that his brother was being sentenced to prison for manslaughter and would serve ten years behind maximum security prison walls. Finishing his years of childhood moving house to house in the adoption agency Crucifix finally came of age and left. Moving to San Jose he visited his brother frequently, something the adoption agency wouldn't allow him to do. Not able to hold a job Crucifix turned to the only thing he knew how to do now, a most valuable skill he obtained through his rough life...fighting. Crucifix originally was a cage fighter and a damn good one at that. Making his way up the rough world of cage fighting he was approached with an offer...the AWC was looking for new recruits and to find Crucifix they must've been looking hard. So Crucifix agreed to sign with AWC leaving his life of cage fighting behind on one condition, they had to sign his convicted felon brother, GHOST. Hence forming the team Deliverance. Ghost was parolled from jail and served his time well in jail by lifting weights and doing calistinecs he had blown up. Entering prison with a weight of 180 pounds and leaving at a massive weight of 240 pounds. Mostly muscle Ghost was an entity to be dealt with now but prison messed with his head. Ghost no longer responded to people, he no longer spoke a known language, and no one could understand him....except Crucifix. So here we are now...one outcast orphan...one convicted felon...and one hell of a tag team, DELIVERANCE!
