Pact Biography Real Name Christopher [name withheld] Nicknames The Nightstalker, The Ultraviolent Icon Hometown Yonkers, NY Birthday 15th October 1957 Height 6'3' Weight 234 Fed Awards First Consecutive Two-Time Fresh!man Biography Born in Yonkers, NY on October 15th, 1957, Pact had a happy upbringing. Almost too happy - American as apple pie. In 1970, at age thirteen, the height of rebellious hippie drug culture, he went off the rails, becoming involved with the New York arm of the Mafia. Under the guidance of Toto Carluccio, he became the most infamous - and youngest - hitman in the underworld. At age 16, his world fell apart. One of his marks had survived and tracked him down to his home in Yonkers, killing his mother, father and sister and seriously injuring him. Pact woke up in the emergency room asking himself what he'd done with his life up til this point and could come up with no appropriate answer. It all amounted to nothing, and he'd lost the one thing he truly cared about - his family. He vowed to never let anyone get close to him again and to do his utmost to protect those around him. With that in mind, he enrolled in the Army and was shipped off to Vietnam. At age 19, on his second tour of duty in what should have been the closing days of the war, he was captured by the VC. After spending an incalcuable amount of time bound and gagged on a cold steel floor, fed through a drip, he was sold to Yakuza boss Keitaro Kitsune. Mr. Kitsune, impressed by Pact's size, decided to imprison him in his 'Den of Death' - an underground Gladatorial battle arena, featuring some of the most brutal deathmatches the world has ever seen, many often resulting in death. Pact managed to find the balance between fighting for his life and helping those around him - he used the rudimentary medical training the army had provided him with to bandage the wounds of those men he destroyed in the ring. Pact had a blistering 4-year unbeaten run, claiming the undisputed King of the Deathmatch crown in his 325th consecutive victory from Yuki Ohayo. The Den of Death was shut down one year later when Interpol finally tracked him down. The staff at the den were arrested and incarcerated - Keitaro Kitsune escaped and is still at large today. Filled with resentment toward a Government he felt had made no effort to rescue him from the hands of almost certain death, Pact turned his back on the army and became a lone traveller, roaming the streets of America, wrestling to make money for a bed and food. At age 48, he made the step up to the big time by joining AWC in the hope that the recognition his sacrifices deserve would follow in due course. After a string of victories that saw him become the first two-time Fresh!man, Pact was pinned by Crimson O'Malec in a Weapon Of Choice match. The match itself went down as one of the defining moments of the early days of AWC but the real story happened outside the ring. Later on that evening, Pact was attacked in his own home by a gang of masked men. The injuries he sustained saw him spend several days in intensive care. He discharged himself on the morning of the 14th June and made his way to the AWC Fresh! taping to announce his retirement. Before he had the chance to make it final, the one man whose very existence Pact had all but written off, Japanese gangland boss Keitaro Kitsune, made his first appearance in AWC, threatening death to members of Pact's newfound 'family' if he did not continue to wrestle. Since that night, Pact has been on a relentless tare through the AWC roster. It is clear that he will stop at nothing to find the peace he seeks, as evidenced by his evermore death-defying wrestling style. AWC wrestlers are advised to think carefully before agreeing to any match with the Ultraviolent Icon - after all, the most dangerous man is the one with nothing left to lose.
