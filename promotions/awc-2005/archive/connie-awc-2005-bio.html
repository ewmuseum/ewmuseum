Connie Biography Real Name Constance Mary [name withheld] Nicknames Connie Hometown South Philadelphia Height 5'6 Weight 125 Biography Connie is truly a veteran of the wrestling game. She has been in the wrestling industry since 1989, working as a manager and valet in various independent circuits in the United States and Canada. In 1993, she spotted a fantastic young talent in Ottawa Wrestling Association by the name of Race Alexander. She took one look at him and new he had all the tools. He was handsome, had incredible in ring skills and oozed with charisma. She knew that he had what it took to make it to the big time, and with her guidance, that he... they... could be there in no time. Little did she know that it would them close to 13 years to make it the Big Credit. To her credit, she stuck with her wrestler, who shortly after hooking up professional, hooked up romantically. Despite setbacks, injuries, grudges, bad press and everything else, she has stood by Race Alexander the Wrestler and Race Alexander the man. Now that they have made it to the big time, she will do everything in her power to make sure that they make it to the top. Her viligance, however, has come at a price. Still an incredibly attractive woman with plenty of curves, lines have begun to creep onto her face; lines covered through the use of heavy makeup. Her large breasts have began to droop, which she combats through the use of a push up bra. While she knows that Race still has plenty of good years left him, she knows that the shelf-life on a valet is limited. Its now or never for her professionally, and perhaps even personally with Race, who if successful, may begin to look to upgrade her with a newer modle both at ringside and in the bedroom... ...and she will do whatever it takes to make sure neither happens.
