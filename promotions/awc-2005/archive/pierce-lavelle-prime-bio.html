Pierce Lavelle Biography Real Name Pierce Robert Lavelle Hometown Harrisburg, PA Birthday 12th of August 1982 Height 6 ft 2 Weight 235 Fed Titles Transatlantic Champion (Current) Fed Awards Transatlantic Champion x3 / Runner up in the Zero To Hero Tournament / Voted best wrestler / Voted Peoples Choice wrestler / Voted Most wins Biography Pierce Lavelle Pierce Lavelle was born in Harrisburg in 1983, where he lived with his mother Marie and father Brad. Pierce’s life was one suppressed by a controlling and demanding father. Pierce spent a lot of his youth playing sports, wrestling and ice hockey. Off the ice and mats, he would have to hit the books to study for a medicine degree. Pierce grew up with two of his best friends, Robert Green and Lois Wood. Lois Wood became Pierce’s teenage romance and the first person he ever loved. When they departed after high school, Pierce didn’t think he would see her again, until Robert Green began dating her in the first year of College. Pierce found it difficult, but was glad to have his best friends together again. In college, Pierce studied medicine with astronomy and mathematics. It was something that wouldn’t seem interesting to him in High School, but after attending a few lectures he became hooked on physics and math problems, becoming the real nerd! In his second year of College Pierce began to take his music skills to a heavy metal level and joined a small campus band called, Gods of War. With his skills developing and his love for AC/DC and Black Sabbath, Pierce soon became lead guitar and singer and took the band to a new level, selling out tickets at different dances and flings. This growing taste for Rock N’ Roll, caused Pierce to spend more than sufficient amounts of his fathers money, his tuition money and when Pierce finished his physics and maths degree, his band celebrated with a large party in the girls dorms. Pierce soon felt the burden of having miscreant band members and watched the drink and women take over their lives. Before Pierce could finish his last semester in Medicine, he soon found out he had been set-up by his own band members, to which Dean Winters did not believe. Pierce faced expulsion with only a degree in Maths and Physics. His trip home wasn’t one of high expectations; Pierce’s father had wanted one thing for Pierce, for him to become a doctor. Without Pierce having the full PHD and with him being expelled, he soon felt his father’s punishment, but this time, he stood up for himself. Packing what little he had and saying goodbye to his sister and mother, Pierce took a road trip to Albany New York with his friend Robert Green. In New York, they hoped for normality and maybe a better chance at life. Robert set about getting a career out of his Accounting and Science degrees, while waiting tables with Pierce in a restaurant. Pierce finally managed to make something of his own life, by joining the up-and-coming wrestling company called AWC. Pierce maintained a steady income and soon found himself reunited with his teenage sweetheart, Lois Wood. As their relationship once again developed, Pierce got sucked into the mafia gang world after becoming friends with Lilly Connelly, a young girl in the witness protection program. Pierce managed to save her, but after the mafia boss Marvin Harrington ordered for them to be smoked out of the building, Pierce put Lois first and rescued her; Lilly however never made it out and died in the blaze. Pierce and Lois began to rebuild their relationship, falling in love once more. Unfortunately, although things were going well for Pierce Lavelle in the AWC - he became the Transatlantic Champion and had his career on the uppermost peak, but back in Albany, the previous incident didn’t dissipate. Harrington soon broke out of prison, kidnapping Lois Wood. Pierce had no choice but to once again move into a life he didn’t want and with the help of Agent Tony Rush, they rescued Lois Wood and it resulted in the death of Harrington. However, Lois Wood soon left without a goodbye, wishing to finish off her Law degree and start a proper life in Harrisburg. With Pierce now single and once again living the quiet life, he took to regaining the transatlantic title after his defeat at the Battle of Britain. He regained the title, and became the first ever two time champion. This would soon mark the long run, otherwise known as the Lavelle era by some. His championship reign would last 140 days, with a mix of more drama produced by non other than Adam “King” Dick. The series of mind games and trouble, caused by Adam Dick, would lead to Pierce’s banishment from wrestling for a year and a troubled present with Dick, Sasha and the DA office. Pierce soon became the victim of the troublesome miscreant, Dick. After being framed for drugs use, Pierce is now spending his days as a non-wrestler, working close to David “Pearl” Harber, waiting out his ban… Eight months and he soon found himself being contacted by Adam Dick – the very man that set him up. Dick looking for help with a murder charged, contacted Pierce, in return for his help, Dick wrote an affidavit to help lift Pierce’s ban. Facing the District Attorney court, now, with the help of his lawyer - Lois Wood. Pierce brought his case to trial, hoping for an outcome that would allow a return to wrestling. The surprise lay in an unsuspecting corrupt cop, John Ramsfield. With Ramsfield’s testimony and Adam Dick’s affidavit, along with various questions and rebuttal, Pierce was found “not-guilty” by the Jury and the charges dropped. Returning with no record to the AWC, Pierce now realised he had a choice to make with the show being split it two – West and East. During a Fresh! West show, it was revealed that Pierce chose to join Sasha’s show for two main reasons – Sarah Kennedy and Michael “Chainz” Sloan. He faces his first return match this week on Fresh! A few seconds later, the Atlantic Tron produces an image on the screen, an image of a Celtic cross. In the foreground, a series of words appear on the screen, each flashing in succession in bold, red letters... PIERCE...LAVELLE...IS...BACK! Then, the driving metal of 'Happy?' by Mudvayne fills the arena, as an explosion is heard in the arena, illuminating the arena once more. Standing on the stage, with his head down, is Pierce Lavelle, and the crowd is going absolutely crazy. Slowly raising his head, Pierce Lavelle looks out into the crowd, showing off his new look: shaved head, Van Dyke beard, and new wrestling attire - short tights, boots, elbow and kneepads. The AWC Legend walks down to the ring, as Chad Gray’s vocals reverberate throughout the arena. In this hole, that is me, the dead are rolling over. In this hole, thickening, dirt shoveled over shoulders. Tear meat from the bone, tear me from myself, are you feeling happy now? Pierce Lavelle jumps off the turnbuckle, turning to look at his opponent, his eyes locked as the lyrics slowly come to an end. The crowd quietens as the metal words course through their eyes… Are you fuckin’ happy, Now that I’ve lost everything… Wrestling Style: Pierce Lavelle is very methodical and quick about the way he approaches wrestling. He focuses mainly on technical submission moves and power. Using high-flying for entertainment value and also desperation when an opponent regains momentum outside the ring, or underneath him. At the beginning of a match, he’ll scout out his opponents movements – weakness’s and strength’s – he’ll play it easy at the beginning, working on the softer areas of wrestling. Middle of the match is where the fun will begin – he’ll begin to use his technical and submission moves to out manoeuvre his opponent, playing games with his opponents mind. Keeping a quick speed momentum throughout, tiring his opponent while working an area of weakness. If the opponent begins to cop-on to the wrestling style, he’ll switch immediately to Power, to hurt, weaken and confuse his opponent. If the opponent regains momentum and soon takes over the offensive, Pierce will use desperation moves – high flying, quick tosses and power moves that allow for a quick upper hand to allow him to turn the tables once more. Nearing the end of the match, he’ll remain focused on the weaker points, usually an arm, neck or shoulder – allows him to apply a stronger move effective hold with his finishing move. He’ll set his opponent up and then connect the Whiplash II, usually causing his opponent to tap-out or be suffocated.
