Sgt. Jacobs Biography Real Name Andre Martin Jacobs Nicknames Gadget Hometown Newark, NJ Birthday 22nd March 1980 Height 6' Weight 221 Biography Jacobs is the oldest child (He has two younger brothers, and three sisters), of a widowed mother who just can’t keep a job down in Newark. In his adolescence Andre was curious about everything, everything he saw he had to touch or fiddle with, which made him prone to getting into trouble a hell of a lot. Of all his siblings, many people believe he is responsible for his father’s early death and his mothers gray hairs. In his life there are 3 things he loves more that anything, Booze – Food and Women (In that order). But the only things that stop him getting drunk every night are his two other loves – His computer and his car. Plus Iraqi beer tastes like piss. He joined the army with the hopes of learning self-defence, and Computer Science, but sadly he got shipped out to Iraq within a month of joining and he has been switched between platoons and services so many times he hasn’t ever had time to get to the education center. He is here straight off his tour of Iraq with the 181st Airborne (His final military switch) but when he injured his right knee and could not be in active service, the only way he thought he could carry on using the self defence techniques he learned in the Marines (his second switch) was to do something which involved fighting. So in short Gadget is your man if you want anything fixed, or anything taken care of (if you catch my drift). He’s your friend with a foul mouth, and a bad ass sense of humor.
