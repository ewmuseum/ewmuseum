Ken Kaze Biography Real Name Kennedy Kenneth [name withheld] Nicknames AWC's Resident Moron, Elite of GEORGE-jitsu, Five-Leaf Extraordinaire, Hardcore Hero, Hero of Hardcore Hometown Raleigh, North Carolina Height 5'9' Weight 177 Biography Don't fuck with this deranged man. Seriously. He'll eat your babies. He'll even gargle them to a boil to dilute the methamphetamine from your bloodstream. Yes, I'm talking about those babies. No, he doesn't give dome or anything like that, he'll just rip your nuts off . . . with his teeth! Because he's raw and stuff like that. He'll even chop off your wee-wee, bake it in some lemon juice, and feed it to you. Matter of fact, you'll ask him to do this. I promise. He's a crazed moron. His best friend is a nail-studded aluminum trashcan encased in barbed wire. He calls it George the Trashcan. He also believes he's 100% Irish. Although he is of Irish descent, he was born and raised in Raleigh, North Carolina, USA. A place not even remotely close to Ireland. Other Feds Greatest Wrestling Alliance, Primetime Wrestling Academy, New True Internet Championship Wrestling, Primetime Championship Wrestling, NOWrestling, Jolt Wrestling Other Fed Awards GWA Hardcore Champion (2x), GWA European Champion (2x), GWA Tag Team Champion (as The Federation w/ Ron “The Thrill” Hill), GWA Intercontinental Champion (2x), GWA 2001 Ruler of the Ring Winner, PWA Graduate, NTICW inaugural Extreme Champion (2x), NOW inaugural King Of Kage Champion, 07/09/04 PTC Top 10 Rankings (appeared as an honorable mention), 07/31/04 PTC Top 10 Rankings (appeared as #3 on the rankings), Jolt Ambassador Champion Wrestling Style GEORGE-jitsu. Hybrid fusion of the hardcore, innovative cruiserweight, and submission wrestling styles . . . with George the Trashcan. Essentially, he just beats the living hell out of his opponents with George. A natural spot-monkey who is not afraid to incorporate his nail-studded aluminum friend encased in barbed wire. Finisher Move [1] Celtic Crossface Extraordinaire [2] Emerald Isle Bomb [3] Five-Leaf Clover Press Finisher Description [1] cobra clutch/crossface crippler hybrid [2] wrist-clutch fisherman brainbuster (with pin 80% of the time) [3] five-star frog splash, motherfuckers Finisher Setup Move [1] Burning Shamrock [2] Shining Leprechaun Setup Description [1] kickwhamDIAMONDCUTTER (alt. finisher) [2] decapitating running step-up enzuigiri Other Moves Avalanche/super cobra clutch suplex. Avalanche is from the top turnbuckle. Super is from the middle turnbuckle. Cobra Clutch Bomb: Sit-out cobra clutch piledriver. With the opponent in the cobra clutch, they are lifted up into the air. The opponent's head is forced forward and downward as their feet leave the ground. Using their momentum against them, they are slammed onto their head and/or neck region in between the attacker's legs. Corkscrew 450 splash. Figure Four around the ringpost. Fireman's carry into double-knee gutbuster. With the opponent in a fireman's carry, the attacker forces the opponent off of his shoulders, but still maintains his grasp on the opponent. The attacker leaps into the air driving his knees into the opponent's midsection. As they both crash onto the canvas, the attacker's knees are further lodged into the opponent's midsection for the true effect of this maneuver. Reverse gory special piledriver. Reverse triangle chokehold. It is simply executed from the opponent's backside to avoid any possible escape. Reverse triangle chokehold hurricanrana/frankensteiner. See 'Triangle chokehold hurricanrana/frankensteiner' for explanation. Note: The maneuver is performed the exact same way except for the fact that it is executed from the opponent's backside. Series of three hammerlock Northern Lights suplexes resulting in a pin. The opponent's arm is hammerlocked before the suplex. Adding this feature allows the attacker to single out an opponent's arm for a subsequent submission hold. St. Patrick's Spinal Tap: It's essentially an elevated modified cloverleaf leglock, better known as the Texas Cloverleaf. With the cloverleaf leglock applied, he crouches slightly whilst making sure to position his knee directly into his opponent's spinal column to apply the maximum amount of pressure possible. It's impossible to escape from this maneuver once it's locked in. Triangle chokehold hurricanrana/frankensteiner. Catching the opponent with a hurricanrana or frankensteiner with one leg wrapped around either arm and the other leg around the head, the attacker applies an armbar in mid-rotation. Once both are grounded, the attacker maintains the armbar with a grounded triangle chokehold.
