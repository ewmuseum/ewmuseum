Kris Krimzon Biography Real Name Kris Krimzon Nicknames 618 Hometown St. Louis, Missouri Birthday 1/17/79 Height 6'4' Weight 230 Biography A leg injury in August of 2005 has had him sidelined for months on end. 'Kris? Kris was a trouble child, leading a troubled life. He had lots of friends, and was cheerful at school, but when he arrived back home, it was a different story. Up until when he was around thirteen, he would go home to a drunken father and a mother hard at work. He would just walk into his room and read his books, which he loved. He couldn't get enough books. He'd read Dr. Seuss all the way to the Holy Bible. Reading, to him, was like a sanctuary from what surrounded him. You see, outside his closed door were two people who couldn't stand each other. His mother, always working hard around the house, hated that his father would flop down onto the couch, unbuckle, and gulp down alcohol. His father was a big alcoholic, and he drank from two to six beers a day and the alcohol made him flip at everything. His mother asked him to toss away his beer, and then he punched her, right in the jaw, leaving a bad bruise. Then when young Kris came out to get a drink, his father saw him and did the same thing to Kris. When the school nurse asked him the next day what was happened, he said he fell. The nurse was going to send him home, but he begged the nurse to call me instead. Of course, this all ended when his first teenage holiday. On holidays, his father stopped drinking, and one night they went out for presents. They were crossing the street when a semi-truck driver fell asleep at the wheel. They died upon impact. It took a few months, but his life got back on track, and he wasn't just cheerful at school. He was always cheerful, and everyone couldn't get enough of it. He was the most popular kid in high school. He did basketball, baseball, track, and wrestling. His favorite was always wrestling, though. I asked him why one day, and he told me, 'This way, I can inflict pain on those who've never had to deal with the things I have.' That's when I knew that he could make it. He was driven by something. Most kids his age just wrestled to just wrestle, or to get popular. Kris was different… He had a fuel. Everyone else was running on empty tanks. Kris had the energy to make it though, and he did.' - Kris Krimzon's Uncle, Ben.
