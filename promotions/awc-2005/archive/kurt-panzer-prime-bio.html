Kurt Panzer Biography Real Name Kurt Panzer Nicknames The German Warmachine Hometown Berlin, Germany Birthday 17/9/ Height 6'9 Weight 295 Biography Back Story: The key word when thinking of Panzer is. Volatile. The very nature of his existence is one in which he was chosen from a sample of 2000 children to take part in a government program, a program which was never revealed to the public. Panzer along with four others were chosen to be the new face of Germany, a new face for an old ideal, something which had been lost, but not forgotten in time. Panzer was the perfect German even from birth. Blond hair, blue eyes he was the perfect arian male. Panzer was to lead the new generation, a new type of soldier to bring in the masses through fear and terror. Subjected to numerous tortures, to desensitize himself from the real world, pain is just another word, fear is just another word. The only time he faces true fear is the flashbacks in his life he suffers. Further details are classified... Ring Characteristics: In the ring he can snap in an instant this is his main weakness, losing sense of himself often leading to disqualification, injuries to himself and other parties...or worse. Other Moves Finisher #1: Howitzer:- Sit out choke powerbomb. This is Panzer's primary finisher and most regularly used in regular matches. Finisher #2: Blitzkrieg:-Charging Spear. Whilst the Spear is being more and more commonly seen in the wrestling world, the reason for it is it's effectiveness, especially with a man packing 295lbs behind him. Not used as often as Howitzer but still can be used, mostly against significantly smaller opponents, due to them being sent like a rag doll. Finisher #3: The Final Solution:- The last gasp attempt to win a match, this is a desperation move which is saved for extremely important matches or ppv's. The move itself is essentially a top rope version of the Howitzer, risking himself and his opponent, the move could quite literally be, do or die.
