Michael 'Chainz' Sloan Biography Real Name Michael Sloan Nicknames Chainz Hometown Birmingham, AL Birthday 3/19/81 Height 6'5' Weight 295 Fed Awards Countdown trophy Biography Biography: Born on March 19, 1981 to an abusive father and negligent mother, Michael Sloan was dealt a raw hand from the start. His early years saw his father and mother abusing drugs, alcohol, and sex ( not always with one another ). Mike developed quickly, intellectually and physically, but was constantly at the mercy of his father, Donald Sloan. His mother, Joyce Sloan, was too high to notice the abuse or do anything about it. On April 12, 1993 Mike came home to a site of murder and carnage, as he walked in on his father bludgening his mother to death. Knowing he was next, Mike was quick to act as he grabbed a knife from the kitchen and proceeded to stab his father over 40 times. When the police arrived they were shocked to find the horrific slayings. Mike was taken into custody and evaluated. He was found to be highly intelligent, highly disturbed, and highly traumatized. Mike was taken into a mental asylum where he spent a year before being released to a special care home. The police concluded that Mike was temporary insane and was acting out of fear and anger over the abuse he had endured during his childhood. The next few years saw Mike educating himself and training himself physically. Everything was going fine, until his sadistic and twisted nature took root once more and he was arrested for rape and assault and battery. The evidence was weak and the girl had been sexually active that day, so Mike pleaded guilty and received a deal of 8 years, which he served until he turned 24. His stay in jail allowed him to reflect on his life, educate himself some more, and continue his training. He also meet Tracy through a pen pal system and turned his life around. On his release he meet with AWC staff, with Tracy's help, and now wrestles alongside so many great wrestlers. Other Feds DWF, 360 Other Fed Awards DWF Peoples, Hardcore, Heavyweight, and Dream champion.
