Damian D'Angelo Biography Real Name Damian D'Angelo Hometown Philadelphia, PA Birthday 06/13/78 Height 6'4' Weight 247 Biography Born to a life of misery, Damian was the victim of an abusive, drunk father figure whose purpose in life was to make Damian's a living hell. For years Damian endured the vicious onslaught of drunken rage, before finally turning to a small cult like atmosphere of friends, who happily 'took care' of his parental problem. With the removal of any real father figure in his life, he continued on happily in his cult life, and appeared to be getting stranger and stranger. He began to speak less and less to the point where he'd often go days without saying anything, trapped within the confines of his own mind. He began to feel the need to experiment with the ability of the human mind, in which he conducted several experiments on himself, such as sewing his mouth shut and sitting at the bottom of a water well for three days. Damian considers himself fairly misunderstood, a little eccentric, but nonetheless another normal productive member of society. People generally judge him from his bizarre appearance and amount of tattoos, and instantly label him as an outcast. Damian's secret agenda is to truly gain acceptance in this cruel world.
