Zero 2 Hero 2006, the preview

28 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
zero 2 hero. manchester evening news arena, england. One year ago Atlantic Wrestling Club launched itself upon the receptive world with the eight-man, one-night singles tournament that would decide the new promotion's first top champion. The show was titled Zero To Hero, and its winner, AWC's first Transatlantic champion, was "The Mongoose" Alexander Strider. One year on and Strider's life has tragically lost his life. AWC has risen to the top of the wrestling world but all the while controversy has dogged that title belt first held by The Mongoose. All but one of the Transatlantic champions to date have found themselves entwined in the dark and complex tale of the Prometheus Serum, a drug first used by Core Wrestling's Smiley in order to boost his ring powers. Prometheus is illegal in pro wrestling yet it has managed to taint an entire year of championship competition. Strider's death was linked to use of the drug. The Educator, who won three title belts including the Transatlantic strap in one night at The Battle Of Britain, was found to have accomplished this feat only because he had been using Prometheus. Pierce Lavelle, a two-time champion and AWC's only Legend, is currently serving a 12-month ban for use of the Serum. Adam Dick, also now a two-time champion and the current holder of the belt, is alleged to have been the catalyst behind the engineering of rival Lavelle's suspension. Jack Murphy, a current foe of Dick, is the only previous champion not to have found himself mixed up with the deadly Serum... yet. Zero 2 Hero could not come at a better time. With the devious Dick again at the pinnacle of AWC despite the best efforts of AWC's hardworking roster, it is time for a new challenger to step up and bring to the Transatlantic title the prestige it deserves. The prize on the line tonight is a Transatlantic championship match at August's Coast To Coast. Eight "zeroes" will fight out the most gruelling tournament in pro wrestling today, each and every one desperate to have the chance to become the one that takes the crown from "King" Dick's head. It is clear that AWC cannot hope to challenge the global enterprise of PRIME further without a controversy-free upper echelon. David "Pearl" Harber anxiously awaits the deliverance of a saviour. A man who can wrestle honourably and successfully. A future champion to be proud of. But are the contestants singing from the same hymn sheet? Zero 2 Hero competitors are looking out for themselves and themselves alone. It's none of their business what Pearl wants from the Hero of 2006. All that occupies them is a desire, an urgency, a pressing need to win Zero 2 Hero and propel themselves to the top of the wrestling business, where inevitably all believe they belong. Our eight "zeroes" want to win this title shot by any means necessary. And three hours from the first bell of the night... somebody will.
