Filipe Alvaro Biography Real Name Filipe Álvaro Nicknames The Portuguese Poser Hometown Agadao, Distrito de Aveiro, Portugal Birthday 14/2/1983 Height 5'9' Weight 256 Fed Titles None as of yet Fed Awards None as of yet Biography A regular Romeo. Filipe thinks he’s God’s gift to women, but with his national language being Portuguese; and the fact that he struggles with English a little he’s constantly making mistakes and saying the wrong things to the ladies. This usually results in him being slapped across the face. It never stops him trying though, partly due to the fact that he doesn’t even realise what he’s done wrong. Filipe is known to be a bit of a cheater at times, both in and out the ring. One of his main assets is the fact that he can easily get himself out of a situation with his excellent charm. He’s not a huge weapon user but it’s something he definitely wouldn’t overlook. Filipe grew up in a middle class state. His father, Rui Álvaro was a professional wrestler himself. Following in his footsteps, by the age of 18 Filipe was already a pro over in Portugal. Being one for the fame and fortune, he quickly realised that it wasn’t his home country that would get him it. He moved to the U.S in search of popularity. Wrestling Style Technical, Aerial, Cheater Finisher Move Portuguese Power Finisher Description Double Arm Piledriver Finisher Setup Move Boa Noite!
