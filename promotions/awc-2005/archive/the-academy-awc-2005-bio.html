The Academy Biography Biography TIM SHIPLEY WRITTEN SEPTEMBER 2004. UPDATE COMING SOON! Tim Shipley is just an ordinary college guy, struggling to make ends meet so he can continue to study Mathematics at the respected Harbrook College in Albany. The shy computerite also happens to possess a love of wrestling, so it only seems natural for him to attempt to get a job in pro wrestling to cover him through college and, if he has extreme success, potentially set up a future permanent career path. With an IQ of 143, much of the mental side of the game goes into Shipley's grappling style. He had signed a pre-contract agreement with the Albany-based Neon Wrestling, but with the unexpected closure of that promotion due to a number of factors, he has had to look further afield for jobs, before finally settling on this rolling monthly contract offer from Core Wrestling. Although Shipley enjoys wrestling very much, the game really takes a backseat role to his struggles with normal life. Shyness around girls, struggling to get college assignments finished in time and failure to get invited to the best frat parties are all part-and-parcel of the Tim Shipley experience. PIERCE LAVELLE Born in Harrisburg PA 1983 - he studied for 3 years in the Thompson Medical College, until he was expelled. Pierce had spent all of his parents money on his band, drink and gigs. His parents felt betrayed - Pierce soon lost his friends that he knew in the private college, but also his band 'Gods of War'. Pierce's father had always wanted Pierce to become a surgeon, like he did and his father before him; Pierce's grandfather. However this being the case, Pierce was never taught the basics by his parents; he never had the dreams and hopes a child should have. For most of his teenager life, he was inside studying to get into medical college. His parents wishes! Soon Pierce realised everything he had been taught, was everything his father wanted. Not Pierce himself. He soon began to rebel against his family, thus leading to his expulsion. With nothing more than $ 3000 dollars left from his savings account, Pierce decided it was time to run - to leave Harrisburg and his control freaks for parents. He got on the earliest bus and took a trip to Albany, New York. With nothing to do, and no where to go. Pierce decided to rent a dingy apartment near to a small college and a Pizza Hut. He decided to apply for a job, to pay the monthly rent. Pierce now works 10 hour shifts to pay for rent and one day a new college tuition. So he can go to college and do what he wants to do. One day while chatting to his friend Robert Green - a huge wrestling fan. He found out that a company called the AWC was coming into town - looking for wrestlers. Pierce decided it was time to put the college wrestling team behind him and try out for the AWC. After trying out for the company, and leaving all his details. Pierce received a call later in the week. Pierce now wrestles in the AWC - while continuing with his normal life in New York.
