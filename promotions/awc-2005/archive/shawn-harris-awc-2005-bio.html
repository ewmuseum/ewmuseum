Shawn Harris Biography Real Name Shawn Jeffrey Harrison Nicknames The New F'n Evolution, The Natural Selection Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada / Clearwater, Florida - He interchanges between the two. Birthday 20/6/85 Height 6'1 Weight 220 Other Feds GCW, BloodBox Other Fed Awards GCW Survivor Match Winner, Bloodbox Belt of Excellence Champion Wrestling Style High Risk, Technical - Since being off with injury, Shawn has been concentrating more on his technical skills and has decided to only go high risk when it absolutely calls for it. Finisher Move The Evolutionizer Finisher Description Triple Jump Moonsault Legdrop - Much like the triple jump moonsault used by Sabu except he hits the opponent with a legdrop not a body splash) Natural Causes (The RKO - Shawn waits for the opponent to stand, as he does, Shawn jumps up facing away from him, grabs his head and falls forward, slamming the opponents jaw into Shawn's shoulder) Finisher Setup Move Evolutionized Dropkick Setup Description DropSault (Shawn hits his opponent with a dropkick, as he hits his opponent he flips back and lands on his feet) Other Moves Other Moves: Signature/Tribute Moves - Thunderclap (Gorilla Press Slam to a Back Breaker - Only to people of similar size or weight, taught to him by the former GCW World Champion 'The Thunder from Down Under' Jimmy Thunder.) - Bitter End (Reverse Gory Special Piledriver, taught to him by former GCW World Champion Adam Masterson.) - The Chain Reaction (Crippler Crossface, taught to him by his father, Jeffrey James Harrison.) - The Generator (Lifts opponent onto shoulder then hits a front face slam, taught to him by his step uncle Neu Generation.) Other moves: - The Immobilizer (Vertebreaker, uses it as a tribute to Theodoric Solomon, one of Jeff's greatest friends and one of Shawn's greatest inspirations.) - The Generizer (Jacknife Powerbomb (On Smaller Opponents)) - Reckless Abandon (Rolling Boston Crab) - Fateless Hope (Fameasser) - Shadow Kick (Superkick) - Hardkore Bomb (Running release powerbomb) - Standing Shooting Star Press - The 450 - The XTF (Modified STF) - Arm Wrench Hip Throw - Front Face Lock - Shooting Star Press - Leg Sweep - Top Rope Spin Kick - Various Spin Kick Moves - Belly to Back Suplex - Belly to Back Flip - Crossface Chicken Wing - Springboard Dropkick - Springboard Moonsault (Lionsault) - Lion Tamer (Walls of Jericho) - Karate Kick - T-Bone Suplex Powerslam - Unprettier - Senton Bomb - German Suplex - Frankensteiner (Toprope Hurricanrana) - Hurricanrana - Twist of Fate - Superplex - Half Belly To Belly - Reverse Arm Bar - Side Suplex - Snap Suplex - T-Bone Suplex - Wrist Clutch Suplex - Snap Mare - Split Legged Moonsault - Drop Suplex - Piledriver - Swinging Neckbreaker - Even flow DDT - Dragon Screw - Dragon Suplex - Tiger Driver - Tiger Suplex - Butterfly Suplex - Body Press Bomb - Chokeslam - Double Powerbomb - Falling Powerslam - High Angle Spinebuster - Samoan Neckbreaker - Torture Rack - Flipping Armbar - Sambo Suplex - Scissors Kick - Powerbomb - Big Boot (Only to cruiserweights or people of the same height) - Dropkick - Diving Moonsault - Frog Splash - Missile Dropkick - Diving Spear - Diving Fate Of Doom - Sharpshooter - Boston Crab - STF - Ankle Lock - Deathlock w/ Bridge Entrance Music 'Trip With Jesus' by The Union Underground
