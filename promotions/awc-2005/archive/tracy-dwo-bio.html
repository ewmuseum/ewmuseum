Tracy Biography Real Name Tracy Stanton Nicknames Tracy Hometown New York, NY Birthday 7/5/84 Height 5'7' Weight 102 Biography Tracy Stanton was born into a good life, not rich, but by no means poor. Upper middle class would be the most appropriate term to describe her family and upbringing. She was a good student throughout school, though not as good as she could have been had she applied herself completely. Her looks however attracted the opposite gender and she spent many hours partying, being a cheerleader, and having the type of fun only hot popular girls can have. One fateful night during a wild party she ran into some trouble. She was drunk and high and was about to be taken advantage of by some of her supposed male friends. Fortunately, or unfortunately, for her Michael Sloan was invited to the party and had had his eyes on Tracy for awhile. When he saw her about to be taken advantage of he defended her from the other males. He walked her to his apartment and proceeded to beat her silly and teach her that her way of life was wrong and that if she didn't change it she'd be in the ground. Armed with this new information Tracy reapplied herself to he studies, to fitness, and to her new love interest Michael Sloan. For some reason the beating he gave her knocked some sense into her and from that day on there was only one man she desired, that being Mike. Only problem, Mike was a sick, sadistic, violent, extremely troubled teen at the time who was shortly put into jail. She stuck with him throughout his sentence and worked to get him into wrestling on his release. She continued shaping her body in the most appealing way she could think of. On Mike Sloan's release her measurements were 32B-24-32. She loved the way she looked except for her breasts, which she soon balooned up to a 37DD bra size. Mike Sloan didn't complain. With her new 37DD-24-32 figure on a 5'7' and 110 pound frame she could melt any man with a glance his way, but she had eyes for Mike Sloan and Mike Sloan alone. He soon fell head over heels for her too and the rest is history. Tracy helped Mike Sloan get into the AWC under the ringname of Chainz. She still struggles trying to help Chainz out of his horrible way with women and his violent and extremely disturbed world view. Together, they can go a long way.
