A moment of silence dominates, then, backed by “Way Away” by Yellowcard, images of AWC’s superstars appear, one after the other. The guitar plays softly first, then goes straight into a strong, powerful riff. I think I’m breaking out I’m gonna leave you now There’s nothing for me here It’s all the same Transatlantic Champion Alexander Strider proudly holds his newly won championship up for the masses to see at Zero To Hero, as he is being escorted out of the building by police officers. And even though I know That everything might go Go downhill from here I’m not afraid A quick collection of images flash across the screen as the song moves into its chorus. Hate sending Kris Krimzon flying through a row of empty chairs; James Brunt opening his suit to reveal the Frontier, Relentless and Livewire title belts; Ralph Richards emerging from Maddy Estelle’s chest. These images collide suddenly, and the song bursts into its lively chorus, as the AWC logo whizzes across the screen. Way away, away from here I’ll be Away, away, away, so you can see How it feels to be alone and not believe Feels to be alone and not believe Anything The pyros explode into the sky, and AWC Fresh! is on the air. The thousands packed into the Cumberland Crown Coliseum scream in unison as “Way Away” plays with a fury. The camera twirls around the ring and focuses in on the AWC logo in the centre of the ring. We pan over to ringside, while the fans continue cheering, to ring announcers Truth Waters and George Cassidy. George Cassidy: We are live right here and now, and this, ladies and gentlemen, is AWC Fresh! Truth Waters: And what a week it was last week here on Fresh! What kind of twists in the plot could there be here tonight? Might we see more rivalry developing between Strider and Lavelle? The AWC Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider’s appearance on-screen elicits an immediate onslaught of boos from the crowd. He is seen walking down a corridor – striding, even – with a grimace on his face. The weeks of wear are getting to Strider, and he is still visibly healing from the brutal attack he suffered at last week’s Fresh!. Coming up to a door, he pauses for a second then swings it open. As it comes slowly back, having hit the wall on the other side, the sign “DAVID HARBER” can be read on the door. Pearl: (checking his watch) You’re late. Entertainment Manager David “Pearl” Harber is lounging comfortably on a leather sofa, chewing on a cereal bar and taking occasional glances at a muted monitor showing the action. Seeing himself on that screen, he smiles and stands up to look coolly at the champion, who hotly begins to air his grievances. Alex Strider: Did you not notice the movement going on out there? There are hundreds of people picketing me! They’re practically burning me in effigy! People are still pushing the “Killer” envelope, even though I’ve been cleared of all charges, Harber. It’s slander! What are you going to do about that? Pearl: This is America, Alex. They have the right to “peaceably assemble”. If you can’t keep order… Pearl: Don’t interrupt me, Strider. I’ve got something very important to say. Last time I checked, I was the one who’s in charge. You will listen. Alex Strider: What a joke. Slow to bring up a guard, Krimzon is susceptible to the brief onslaught, but after the first few blows he uses his head and manages to duck behind Pact, who swivels quickly to face him, arms raised. Both men nod and take a step back. Truth Waters: They’re both satisfied with that exchange, Pact and Krimzon showing mutual respect with the nod. George Cassidy: Are you sure? From my angle it just looks like each thinks he has the other sussed. Again the instigator, Pact drops his guard and lowers his head to charge in at Krimzon with an attack of strength, using his legs to propel himself onward into the chest of the slightly lighter man. Krimzon absorbs the force well, though, and rather than going down, reaches over Pact’s back to grab hold of his tights. But Pact is insistent, and finally Krimzon gives way to the weight pressing against his torso to relinquish his hold on Pact’s tights and fall to the mat messily. The sudden absence of resistance sends Pact stumbling forwards, and he steps over Krimzon’s body to right himself by leaning on the ropes. George Cassidy: Pact was eager to prove his superior strength there, but Kris Krimzon put up more of a fight than was expected. Truth Waters: Krimzon at 230 pounds is not the lightweight people seem to take him as. You can’t quite say Pact’s show of power backfired on him, but it certainly took more out of the 48-year-old than he expected. Krimzon, now up, takes the initiative by sending Pact across the ring with an Irish whip. “The Nightstalker” evades Krimzon’s attempted drop toe hold by jumping skilfully over and coming back off the opposite set of ropes; Krimzon gets back to standing just in time to receive a rollicking clothesline to throw him unceremoniously onto his posterior. George Cassidy: Venomous clothesline. Once again, Pact’s stamina is going to be in question as he is exerting himself in the opening stages... Pact takes hold of Krimzon’s head from behind and delivers some right hands as Krimzon is unable to move from his sitting position. Dazed, Krimzon slumps to the side, and Pact attempts to sit over his torso but once more Krimzon sits up, and with handholds behind each of Pact’s knees, pulls backwards roughly to effect a loss of balance. Truth Waters: Krimzon works his way out of a hole, and we’ve now got both men just sitting up on the canvas. In unison, the two competitors get to their feet. Pact initiates a tie-up, and the two wrench away at it for a few seconds. George Cassidy: Little test of strength here. Could be interesting. Truth Waters: You’d expect Pact to take it – George Cassidy: Except he’s old, and old men are weak. Pact works his way behind Kris Krimzon, but Krimzon manages to duck out of his grasp and into a waistlock from behind himself. He now grabs Pact’s wrist and twists into a hammerlock, before sweeping Pact’s legs out from under him. Both men fall to the mat. Truth Waters: Hammerlock and a leg sweep from Krimzon, showing some in-ring know-how. Krimzon rolls away at once, leaving Pact to lick his wounds as he backs up against the ropes. Pact now begins to push himself up, and Krimzon, with perfect timing, runs at him and executes a bulldog. George Cassidy: Bulldog! Truth Waters: And our first cover. ONE! TWO! Pact kicks out on Michael Ryan’s second count. George Cassidy: A two count. Respectable... Krimzon is up in a flash, not wasting any time as he drags Pact up and sets him against the turnbuckle. Numerous downward chops to the chest later, Pact is gritting his teeth. Truth Waters: The sting of those slaps is taking its toll on Pact. Look at the colour of his chest! Krimzon now elects to whip his adversary to the opposite corner, but Pact is able to reverse the motion and send Krimzon towards the turnbuckle. George Cassidy: Irish whip reversed. Krimzon, however, manages to put the brakes on before crashing into the corner-post, to cheers from the crowd. Pact, annoyed at this development, follows up, but Krimzon pivots and shoots out a leg which catches Pact in the chin. Pact is caught off-guard and takes a step back; Krimzon takes hold of him and flips himself up onto Pact’s shoulders looking for a hurricanrana. He falls back into position, but Pact is holding tight! Truth Waters: Hurricanrana – no, Krimzon can’t bring him down! With immense strength, Krimzon pulls his body back up into a seated position on Pact’s shoulders, and looks to rock back once again to execute the hurricanrana. Again, however, Pact manages to negate the momentum running in Krimzon’s direction. George Cassidy: This is extraordinary. Krimzon just can’t deliver this hurricanrana. Truth Waters: Inhuman strength by Pact to hold on with Krimzon’s entire body weight dragging him down. Pact now sets Krimzon back up on his shoulders and locks his arms around his torso. He turns slowly around, and a buzz possesses the arena. Truth Waters: Pact is now looking to counter this – YES! Pact slams Krimzon to the mat with a straitjacket powerbomb! George Cassidy: Powerbomb! Of the straitjacket variety! What a reversal. Truth Waters: That’s taken Krimzon’s breath away. Pact could have this match already. Pact hooks the leg: ONE! TWO! Krimzon kicks out! Truth Waters: Kickout! Krimzon’s still got some fight in him after taking that straitjacket powerbomb! Angry, Pact pulls Kris Krimzon up by his blue hair. Wincing with the pain, Krimzon is powerless to resist a punishing knee lift into his abdomen; this is followed by a swinging clothesline. George Cassidy: Pact swung his arm like a sledgehammer across the chest of Kris Krimzon. Pact again brings Krimzon to his feet, and slams his back to the mat with a scoop slam. Pact now drops his leg across his opponent’s collarbone. Truth Waters: Body slam followed by a leg drop. Pact is in control here. Pact now takes a moment to look out at the crowd, some of whom are chanting his name. Louder, however, are the chants for Kris Krimzon to regain a foothold in this match. Angry at this, Pact turns back to Krimzon and aims a vicious kick at his head. Michael Ryan now intercepts Pact and warns him to cool off a little. George Cassidy: The blood is rushing to Pact’s head here. Truth Waters: He needs to tone it down a little. I think that’s what Michael Ryan is telling him. That was a stiff kick into Krimzon’s face right there. Krimzon now staggers up and Pact is on him at once with a snap kick to the midsection. Pact now loops Krimzon’s arms over his own and hits a double-arm DDT! Truth Waters: Double-arm DDT! And Pact looks for the pin once more. ONE! TWO! Krimzon shoots an arm out to grab the bottom rope! George Cassidy: He’s got the ropes! Truth Waters: Yes, Mike Ryan spotted that! Ryan stops counting, waving his arms at Pact to signal that the fall has been broken. Frowning, Pact stands up, but resists the urge to argue with the referee’s decision, instead running a hand through his long hair. Krimzon begins to claw himself up using the ropes; Pact takes hold of his hair and marches him over to the turnbuckle. George Cassidy: Pact just will not let Kris Krimzon get his bearings. Pact slams Krimzon’s head into the turnbuckle and turns him around, forcing him into the corner with a barrage of kicks to the abdomen. Truth Waters: Pact’s stomping the hell out of Krimzon in that corner, with the aid of the top rope! Referee, stop that! Michael Ryan has also spotted Pact’s indiscretion in holding the top rope to improve his balance, and he ushers Pact away from the corner, reminding him of the rules. Pact nods furiously, pushing the referee aside to try and get to Krimzon, but Krimzon takes him by surprise by suddenly running at him and leaping up onto Pact’s shoulders, swinging through with a twisting hurricanrana! The fans explode with joy. Truth Waters: Hurricanrana! Out of nowhere! Krimzon makes the cover: ONE! TWO! Pact kicks out. George Cassidy: Kickout by Pact! Krimzon just took him totally by surprise there. Truth Waters: That’s right, Cassidy, and all of a sudden the crowd are loving this! “DOUBLE K! DOUBLE K!” Krimzon gets up, taking a bow to his fans, but Pact recovers quickly to roll Krimzon back and over for the pin! George Cassidy: Pact’s rolled him up! ONE! TWO! Truth Waters: No! Krimzon counters! Kris Krimzon manages to swing the momentum his way, converting the pinning predicament into one of his own. ONE! TWO! Pact kicks out violently! Truth Waters: Kickout! We were on the edge of our seats there! George Cassidy: Pact suddenly rolled Krimzon up from behind, but somehow Kris Krimzon managed to reverse that pinfall into one of his own! Truth Waters: Neither man, however, achieved a three-count. Pact and Krimzon both get to their feet. Krimzon initiates the next movement, as he rapidly moves in and delivers a snap suplex to send Pact heavily to the mat. Krimzon turns through to make yet another cover: ONE! TWO! On the second count, Pact throws Krimzon off. George Cassidy: Krimzon looked for the fall once more after that snap suplex, but it wasn’t enough to keep “The Nightstalker” down. A little frustrated with the lack of progress in proceedings, Krimzon brings Pact to his feet and almost lazily ties up. He inclines his body, looking to take Pact over with a fireman’s carry, but Pact suddenly spins 180º and hits The Downfall! Truth Waters: THE DOWNFALL BY PACT! George Cassidy: Krimzon was in control but Pact somehow managed to wriggle his way out of the fireman’s carry and hit a surprise Downfall! Truth Waters: But can he capitalise? Both men are down! Michael Ryan begins the count as Krimzon and Pact both lie on the canvas. ONE! TWO! THREE! Pact now struggles over to Krimzon’s body and hooks the leg: Truth Waters: Here we have it! ONE! TWO! George Cassidy: NO! Krimzon kicks out! Kris Krimzon manages to kick out at the last. Truth Waters: This match really could go either way, and it nearly has on so many occasions. Both men now up, Krimzon runs towards Pact, but Pact halts him in his tracks by extending his arms and grabbing hold of Krimzon’s throat with both hands! George Cassidy: What is this? Pact just took Krimzon by the throat! Michael Ryan’s immediate protests are waved off as Pact manages to lift the turning-purple Krimzon into the air, turn partway round and throw him roughly to the mat. Pact himself falls to the mat, exhausted with the effort. Truth Waters: Some kind of double chokehold throw there! But it seems to have taken just as much out of Pact as it has out of Krimzon. George Cassidy: See, Pact fights like a heavyweight, but he’s really only average in that department at two three four. It makes no sense to try and lift someone of your own weight by the throat – the energy wasted in proportion to the damage delivered is just too much. Truth Waters: Although he does appear to have done a considerable amount of damage... Krimzon, now on all fours, is gasping and massaging his throat as Pact now scrambles back up. The older man lays into Krimzon with thrust kicks to the ribs, forcing Krimzon flat. Pact now backs away to the corner and starts to ascend the turnbuckle! George Cassidy: Pact’s climbing the ropes... oh dear. This led to his downfall against Alex Strider at Zero To Hero. He attempted an Asai corkscrew moonsault, of all things, and missed completely. After that, it was a struggle for Pact to get medical clearance to continue to compete in AWC. Don’t tell me he’s going to try it again...! Truth Waters: Well he can’t, because Krimzon is up! Indeed, Kris Krimzon is getting to his feet. He now begins to stagger around, dazed. Pact glances behind him from his perch on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd, and, seeing Krimzon on his feet, launches himself sideways. With inch-perfect precision, he now bounces off the top rope and throws his spread body towards his opponent, engulfing Kris Krimzon with a cross body block! George Cassidy: WHAT A MOVE! Truth Waters: Perfect execution! Springboard cross body but WITH A DIFFERENCE! George Cassidy: And now the two have fallen to the mat in perfect pinning position! Michael Ryan drops to the canvas to make the count. ONE! George Cassidy: This could be it right here! TWO! THREE! Truth Waters: It is! What a surprise! A quick and sudden victory for the 48-year-old against Kris Krimzon in our main event. Pact, showing little emotion, gets back up and is congratulated by the ring announcer. James Brunt: The winner... PACT! In the aftermath of what was an impressive main event, the fans are cheering and full of life. Suddenly, "Bohemian Like You" by the Dandy Warhols plays all around the arena, heralding the entrance of AWC's Entertainment Manager, David "Pearl" Harber. Pearl makes his way down to the ring with a huge smile on his face, while Pact gets ready to leave. The chants for Pearl are deafening as he enters the ring and hurriedly intercepts Pact before he leaves. “PEARL! PEARL! PEARL!” echoes throughout the arena as the Entertainment Manager grins, enjoying the adoration from the AWC fans a great deal. Pearl: That’s right, you’ve guessed it! It is indeed time to announce this week’s Fresh!man! George Cassidy: (sarcastically) Whoop dee doo! Another worthless Fresh!man. Truth Waters: Can’t you ever show some enthusiasm? I mean, are you capable of doing that? The fans cheer, ready to find out just who has earned the honour of being this week’s Fresh!man. Pearl: So, allow me to introduce to you all, the Fresh!man for this week… PACT! Truth Waters: Pact won it?! That’s the second week in a row Pact has been Fresh!man. What an honour. George Cassidy: Is this supposed to be some kind of lame joke? Giving the award to that old man again? I mean, it’s - it’s preposterous! Pact, still at ringside after his win over Kris Krimzon, almost recoils in shock that he is the Fresh!man for the second week in a row. Pact extends his hand and Pearl takes it gladly. Pact, in absolute disbelief, looks out into the sea of people all supporting him, even after his match is over. Pact gives them a charming wink, slightly uncharacteristic of the cold, calculating ring veteran, and prepares to pick the number from the hat. Truth Waters: Pact will now have two title shots under his belt. What a start for the veteran in AWC. George Cassidy: What’s with all the praise? I fail to see why you, or anyone, would actually like this guy. Pearl: Okay, take your pick! Pact inserts his hand into the hat, finding the paper he wants and removing it. Pact lifts it high into the air, then views it himself in disbelief. The camera catches a glance of the number - it is the number 1. Pearl: Number one guarantees you contendership for the Livewire championship, Pact. Congratulations are due, of course, you being the first person to become Fresh!man two weeks in a row. Nice one, Pact. The fans erupt into applause as Pact stands proudly in the middle of the ring, his eyes telling his story of sacrifice and pain. Pearl pats Pact on the back in congratulations and heads out of the ring as “The Dehumanizing Process” hits once again. The fans around the arena instantaneously come to their feet, giving the veteran a standing ovation. Truth Waters: Can anyone stop this guy? I mean, just take a good look at him. That there is a man who has been through the wars, Cassidy. You talk about your divorce like a baby, while Pact could brave, say, twenty divorces and come out smelling like roses. George Cassidy: Watch your mouth, Truth. Anyway, ladies and gentlemen, if you'll avert your eyes from the screen now and think about our next show in two weeks time. That's right, Fresh! is taking a break because of certain, erm, arena difficulties. I thought we could trust Pearl with anything, Truth. Truth Waters: Clearly it wasn't his fault. I can - George Cassidy: Save us the blabber, Truth. Join us again on June 7th, when we emanate from Uncasville, Connecticut! Truth Waters: Emanate? Er, sure. We've got a big match lined up for you next show, but Pearl won't let me give any details. I'll just give this cryptic clue: Pact has won two title contenderships through becoming Fresh!man twice in a row, but he's actually in for THREE title belts... Good night, everyone!
