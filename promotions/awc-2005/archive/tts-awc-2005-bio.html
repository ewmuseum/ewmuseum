T.T.S. Biography Biography AWC had a chance to catch up with some of the TTS Entourage at a trendy Jazz cafe in Waterloo, London. John:... so I says to him, I says 'That's a Merc!' *A ripple of giggling comes from the leather beanbag perched TTS entourage and they sip their Decaf Doublemochalattefrappacino drinks* AWC: First of all we would just like to fill in a few minor biographical details, surnames, age... Harry: I'm Harry, just Harry; He's just John, and we're all Twenty-Something. AWC: O...kay, how did you get involved in wrestling? John: I'm very accomplished in the field of Judo. Daddy had me start when I was Something-Teen... Harry: We played a lot of Rugby too, London Oratory to begin with, then London Scottish, then at University level. John: Our transition into wrestling for us was like a double espresso AWC: I'm sorry what? John: Quick, strong and impactful Harry: But it we're not black and it didn't cost £3 *Awkward hysteria ensues from the TTS entourage* AWC: I dunno if we can put that in the interview Harry: It's okay, I'm not racist. I have a black friend called Dwayne at the gym. *John recieves a text message, his phone is loud, as though he wants everyone to know when he is called or contacted* John: Yah, I'm sorry we have to fly - we're going to The Banazeez Club, it's very exlclusive, you won't get in. Ciao. Finisher Name Doublemochalattefrappacino Decaf!
