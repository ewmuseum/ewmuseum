Blue Ruin Biography Real Name Michael Lee Spicoli Nicknames Spicoli Hometown WhacKyWorld Birthday July 12th, 1978 Height 6'3' Weight 223 Biography As opposed to the mindless morons, Ruin does his damage in the shadows. His tendencies to be vulgar, foul, and vicious are masked by his understanding of the situation. Ruin knows where to commit crimes under the sheets and below the radar of those scanning the roads, and that's exactly what makes him dangerous but completely normal at the same time. He's an incredibly deranged man, who enjoys being a part of the seedy midnight swarm. He likes the feeling of risking his life around legitimate criminals. I say that because Ruin's never killed anyone. That said, he's paid a lot of money to see and do things that most barely can retain. He, himself, isn't grimey but he enjoys to be a 'part' of the grime. The inner city characters that dwell the streets throughout the night and hide, like rats to light, from the police at guard are his family. Whether they accept him or not is a completely different story. He gets off by scaring people. He forces people to flirt with disaster by cramming it down their throats and letting them trip on it for the day. He lets them off, they barely know what just happened, and goes out to find someone else to disturb. He doesn't date anyone, and it's mainly because of his theory. According to Ruin, women force commitment out of you and commitment's like heroin. That said, he doesnt date. He's found that life's much easier when it's rode alone. With all of that behind us, the question lights up like neon at night: 'What's Lawrence's family like?' The family's a little different. They all live in their own little worlds. ----- Andrea Spicoli - Martin's mother. Has a serious ciggarette addiction; at fifty-nine, she's been smoking since she was eighteen years old. It has since caught up to her, to the point where she's forced to use inhalers. She's also bipolar, and her moods swing like a giant pendulum. She works for the department of motor vehicle; a teller for almost twenty-five years. She's in the whirlwind that is her mid-life crisis. Edward Spicoli - Martin's father. Fifty eight years old, and has been a Trekkie for nearly twenty years of that time. There's even an additional bedroom in their house that holds the thousands of pieces of Star Trek memorabilia. This, unfortunately, has gotten in the way of his marriage with Andrea on many occasions. There were times where Edward would go off, for weeks at a time, to a spree of conventions. He loves big bass fishing, was in the Vietnam War, and worked for the department of transportation for 28 years. Gabriela Spicoli - The sister. A lesbian, who obsesses over The Who, vynyl, and eats ectasy like cheerios. Her and Martin have always been tight siblings, but have grown somewhat distant since she started experimenting with women. At 27, she still lives with the parents. She doesn't intend on leaving until they tell her to. She's had one job; a cashier at Rite Mart, but prefers to sell OTC pills to middle schoolers that don't know any better not to buy them. Grady McMason - Martin's uncle. He had a nervous breakdown after he found his wife cheating on him with their mailman... in their bathtub. He tried to kill himself on several occasions, but never succeeded. Andrea decided to let him live at the house, just because she was afraid he'd try and kill himself again. This is also one of Martin's best friends, as they share identical personalities, at times. That said, Martin, one time, broke Grady's jaw. That story's for another time, though. Doesn't work, but loves to gamble with play money on Party Poker. He's also prescribed to lithium and lexapro. He takes both of them twice a day. If he doesn't, 'bad things' happen. ------ As I said, they live in WhackyWorld. This means that it's completely unknown as to where the Spicoli's live. There will never be enough evidence to ever answer the question, but this is what you should see if you ever think you saw their home; a light blue, three-floor home in the middle of nowhere. There aren't any neighboring homes, stores, or people. Just a lake to the left corner of their backyard and an abandoned farmhouse to their right. A large cherry tree sits in front of the blue house, its surroundings decorated by lawn gnomes and dried-out flowerbeds. Outside of the house, it's still. The weather never seems to change; rain seems to be nonexistent, along with snow. Inside, a zoo of delusion paints the open spaces. There's a dirt path, stretching for miles, that seems to seperare two divisions of land. Across the dirt path, is WhackyWorld. To the Spicoli's, it's but only a hop, skip, and a jump away. To everyone else, it's a galaxy beyond Arcturus. Wrestling Style Technically brutal Finisher Move ShockTrendKill Finisher Description Ruin puts opponent in powerbomb position. He flips him over, and then allows the opponent's weight to rest on his back while he wraps his arms around their neck and executes a kneeling guillotine. Finisher Setup Move Nothing. The opponent must be dazed enough for Ruin to get him into position, though. Other Moves Entrance 'Hell's Kitchen' by Dream Theater starts to play throughout the arena. The volume slowly rises until it can get everyone's attention. At the stage, lights are beaming dark blue colors. Ruin comes out quickly, but doesn't continue walking down the ramp. Instead, he stands at the center of the stage with his down. Eyes closed, he waits for Mike Portnoy, drummer of Dream Theater, to bring us into the adventurous instrumental vortex that is the song. When he does, the regular colored house lights spark on. He jumps up and down two times, smacks his chest with his fist, and sprints down the ramp without ever acknowledging the crowd around him. Rolling under the bottom rope, 'Hell's Kitchen' comes to a close and Ruin awaits the bell. Moves: Let's go a completely different route, here. I'm not going to jot down a list of moves, because that's been overdone and I find that people look up the 'best' moves on the internet and make a gigantic, meaningless list. For the 'move list', I'm going to use Ruin's in-ring personality and the styles he's grown accustomed to while wrestling. For starters, Ruin doesn't believe in 'warming' the match with holds, locks, and grapples. He likes to do things as dirty as possible, and looks for ways to hurt his opponent. In a way, he looks like he's drastically rushing during his matches, but that's just a part of his tactic. He's always the first guy to jump on the fight, and will never back down until the referee decides to pull him off. He likes to study his opponents before the bell rings, mainly by staring coldly into their eyes. He comes into each match with the mentality that he's fighting a complete stranger in some inner-city back alley. He doesn't want to know his opponent in any way, as it may cause a disturbance in his intentions. He likes to bleed, and doesn't mind taking nasty bumps. He'll grow through tables, get splattered to pieces by steel chairs, and take everything anyone's got. He likes to indulge in giving pain, and can allow a disturbing amount of recieved pain without a wince of displeasure. In fact, there's time where he'll grin during a grueling and bloody battle. He'll never back down, physically, and will try to overpower even the biggest guys on the roster. He loves to use the turnbuckle, especially the top rope, and will attempt to execute devastingly powerful moves. Suplexes, Swinging DDTs, Supremo backdrops, you name it. He'll do absolutely whatever it takes for a victory. Entrance Music 'Hell's Kitchen' by Dream Theater
