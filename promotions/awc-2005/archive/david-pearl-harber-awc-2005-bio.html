David 'Pearl' Harber Biography Real Name David Harber Birthday 17th July 1969 Place of Birth Malibu, California, USA Biography David Harber is, simply put, the ideal man to run a highly competitive firm with copious in-work aggression, employees with more money than sense and a substantial element of danger. The 6'2" former surfer has the kind of chiselled body one might expect from an active wrestler, but in fact "Pearl", as he chooses to be nicknamed, has never stepped inside the squared circle competitively. Although you'd never guess it from his boyish enthusiasm, Pearl had never harboured - no pun intended - an interest in wrestling (aside from keeping up-to-date with friend Michael Ryan's progress) before the AWC job came up. The firm's chairman, however, admired David Harber's attitude so much that he gave him the job of Entertainment Manager on the spot, and ever since, Pearl has done his best to get involved in the sport as a whole, usually attending two or three wrestling shows a week in the run-up to AWC's big launch. Pearl exerts such a confident and pleasant aura that it is difficult to imagine the company going a single step in the wrong direction under his watchful eye. Job Entertainment Co-Manager
