Gabriel Afeaki Biography Real Name Formerly Umaga Reihana Nicknames Gab, Gabi, Gabs, GA Hometown Savu Savu, Fiji Birthday 1980 Height 6'3' Weight 285 Fed Titles Relentless Title (Won at Coast To Coast, 2006) Biography born under starred skies life, death and life again, friend his faith, extinguished Other Feds Flatlined fighting, the Asylum Other Fed Awards Undefeated in Flatlined fighting and in any singles cage competition. Combined record 22-0-0). Wrestling Style MMA; Gracie Jiu-Jitsu // Judo // Savate Finisher Move Fijian Leg Lock Finisher Description Heel Hook Finisher Setup Move the Fi.Ji.T (fee.jee.tee) Setup Description Heel Pick Other Moves Elements of the Character: Gabriel can most likely draw comparisons to wrestlers like Kurt Angle (for intensity), Brock Lesnar (for raw power) and Ken Shamrock (submissions). However, these are only rough comparisons. GA is much more likely to resort to his MMA gameplan than attempt to engage in spot fest matches. A master of Gracie Jiu-Jitsu, Judo and Savate, Gabs is an expert in his field: an undefeated mixed martial artist, his skills are second to none. Gabriel is a very fluid and agile fighter. His quickness and strength are his biggest weapons, however his immense technique is a facet often overlooked due to his sheer physicality and in ring presence. Gabriel is very, very intense in the ring. An intimidating figure due to his often external portrayal of calmness, during combat this composure is carried over. Ring Style: GA rarely ever uses the turnbuckles, he is not concerned with aerial assaults. Although agile, his game plan lies as far away from using aerial manoeuvres as possible. Due to his noble sense of competition he will also rarely cheat to gain an upper hand or resort to underhand tactics. Relying on his power, speed and technique, Afeaki will look to use any of his strengths to take his opponent apart piece by piece. Strengths:Gabi is an amazingly powerful man and will look to impose his physical will on an opponent from the offset. A superb striker in the mould of a Chuck Liddell, anyone willing to stand toe to toe with Gabi will more than likely meet an early end. However, this is not Gab's uppermost intention: he has a plethora of takedowns, suplexes, throws and trips all at his disposal. His intention is to physically batter the opponent by continually wearing them down with a modified 'ground and pound' tactic. Looking to suplex, throw, slam his opponent into a weakened position to start his submission work. He can outwrestle the majority of people he encounters, and he is blessed with great speed and quickness for someone so big. His power is frightening. His intensity, also frightening. However... Weaknesses: No man is without his flaws and Gabs in the ring has weaknesses to exploit. He is often too explosive for his own good, and can sometimes face a lack of stamina as a match progresses. He is also very green in terms of his wrestling career. He can fight, but wrestling is a different matter. The Move List Gracie Jiu Jitsu: Gracie Jiu-Jitsu places emphasis on fighting techniques found on the ground with submission holds involving joint-locks and chokeholds mixed in. GA is a 9th degree black in GJJ and consequently he holds a solid red belt. GJJ Techniques - Kimura Shoulder Lock, Rolling leg lock to Knee Bar, Lift Throw, Rear Naked Choke. Many of the submissions listed further down are variants of GJJ holds. Judo: Judo forms the major part of Gabriel's takedown offence. Using sweeps, throws and trips Gabriel likes to slam his opponent to the ground. GA is a 9th grade Black Belt, and is well versed in all Judo styles. He uses some throws as a basis for submissions, while others work merely as a method to inflict pain. GA is nefarious in his delivery. Judo Techniques - Ashi Guruma (Leg Wheel) , Daki Wakare (High Lift and Separate: used to set up head attacks or arm submissions.), Hane Goshi (Spring Hip Throw), Harai Goshi (Sweeping Hip Throw), Harai Tsurikimo Miashi (Lifting Pulling Foot Sweep), Ippon Seoinage (One Arm Shoulder Throw), Kibishi Gaeshi (Heel Trip Reversal), Kosoto Gari (Small Outside Reap), O Guruma (Large Wheel), Osoto Gari (Large Outer Reaping), Sukui Nage (Scoop Throw), Sumi Gaeshi (Corner Reversal), Tomoe Mage (Circle Throw), Ura Nage (Rear Throw), Yama Arashi (Mountain Storm), Yoko Gake (Side Hook). Savate: Savate is a form of French Kickboxing and employs several striking techniques, as well as various takedown. Gabs is a silver glove practicioner of Savate. Savate Techniques - Kicks: Fouette (whip kick), Chasse (piston-action kick), Revers (thrust kick), Coup de Pied Bas (sweeping kick). Punches: Direct Bras Avant (jab, lead hand), Direct Bras Arriere (cross, rear hand), Crochet (hook, bent arm). General MMA: Although GA is heavily influenced by his backgrounds in his various art forms, cage fighting required him to learn other basic techniques to employ. These ranged from takedowns to strikes. MMA Techniques - Double Leg Takedown (GA opts for a double leg takedown that resembles a Batista spinebuster.), Single Leg Takedown (Best described as a one leg Rugby tackle.), Gabi also can act as a stand up fighter and aside from his Savate will throw boxing punches and some Muay Thai knee strikes. Submissions: You know how people have a thing about cars? Or bikes... or you know... a thing... well Gabi's thing is submission holds. Don't ask. They just are. Achilles Lock*, Anaconda Choke, Bicep Slicer, Guillotine Choke*, Knee Bar (Variations), Arm Bar (Variations)*, Triangle Hold. The Wrestling: Gabi is new to the wrestling game, but has found himself accustomed to use certain moves. Suplexes - Using similar skills found in Judo to lift and throw an opponent, Gabi has taken several suplexes and incorporated them into his Martial Arts style. [Descriptions to be found by clicking the suplex link] Belly-to-back Cradle Suplex, Millennium Suplex, Tiger Suplex, Belly-to-back Flipping Throw Suplex, Tiger Suplex '85, Taz-mission Suplex, Belly-to-back Hammerlock Suplex, German Suplex, Belly-to-belly Suplex, Head-and-arm Suplex, Belly-to-belly Overhead Release Suplex, Cradle Suplex, Leg Capture Suplex, Snap Suplex, Soviet Judo Suplex. Other Moves - Charging Clothesline (a favourite spot where GA will explode from the ropes with a hellacious clothesline), Fireman's Carry Takedown, Arm Drag, Dragon Screw. Ring Entrance: The arena plummets into darkness, leaving the unsuspecting crowd wondering what is going to happen next as multiple flashbulbs partially illuminate ringside. A few moments later, the Atlantic Tron flickers to life, showing only a black, empty screen. Immediately, there are two sets of silver points a few inches away from each other drawing outlines of the human eye. When the drawing is complete, the outlines open, revealing a pair of red eyes with white irises and no pupils. Evil laughter fills the arena as the orange eyes slowly transform into lion eyes: a pale yellow-green base with feline irises. Underneath the eyes, these words in a blood red color appear... THE FIJIAN ANIMAL HAS ARRIVED... The lights flicker back on as the evil, demonic introduction of Thorr's Hammer Troll begins to play. From the curtain emerges Gabriel Afeaki, dressed in black hoodie and Relentless Title Belt, slowly walks down to the ring as the fans watch in awe, as the lyrics of the Haka play over the harrowing sound of Troll fill the arena. The Fijian steps off the ramp way and looks out into the fans, relishing the fear he's bringing to each and everyone one of them. He slowly makes his ascent up the ring steps. James Brunt: Making his way to the ring, from Fiji, weighing 285 pounds... he is G to the A, GABRIEL AFEAKI! Gabriel Afeaki enters the ring, looks over at ring announcer James Brunt, and the assigned referee of the match, and walks over to his corner, jumps onto the top turnbuckle and sits there patiently, waiting for his victim as the song slowly dies down and the lyrics finish... the music dies down as an acappela Haka war chant slowly finishes... It is death, it is death... it is life... The Maori voice peters out as Afeaki sits on the turnbuckle waiting for the match to commence. Entrance Music 'Troll' - Thorr's Hammer
