Killian O'Leary Biography Real Name Killian O'Leary Hometown Dowra, Co. Leitrim Birthday 2nd October 1984 Height 6' 4 Weight 245 Biography Born in Ireland. Moved to America when he was 5. Grew up to become a wrestler. A pretty sweet wrestler at that. Wrestling Style All-Rounder Finisher Move O'Leary 'Plex '06 Finisher Description T-Bone Suplex Finisher Setup Move Usually an Irish Whip, but it's best hit out of nowhere Setup Description What? You still here? Other Moves Killian O'Leary Height: 6' 4 Weight: 245 lbs. Wrestling Style: Killian is a very good all-rounder. In-Ring: O'Leary is very competent at grappling moves; throws, tosses, suplexs etc. His finisher is a suplex, so it sort of comes with the terriorty. He is very comfortable taking to the air. He can springboard off the ropes, springboards off the apron. Lots of planchas, lots of high risk. The Next Top Star's mat work is okay at best. He's no Chris Benoit by any stretch of the imagination, so will shy away from this if possible. If he were to come up against Benoit in the ring, he'd lose. Badly. Killian will try very few HUGE power moves. Heis a strong guy, but is more about endurance in matches. He will happily last a guy out, rather break his neck. Killian delievers a mean right hook. His left hand isn't so strong and usually used to draw a block, leaving his opponent open for his right hand. His kicks are good, strong kicks. Not amazingly stiff, but you'd know you'd been kicked by him. Killian will play to the crowd as much as possible. If he gets a slight advantage, he'll try to piss the crowd off. At times, he may be all business, like against major enemies such as Jackson Snow but for now have him play to the fans. Get them to hate him. Killian is a clever wrestler. He knows his way around the ring. He also knows about working parts of the body. Mostly he likes to work the neck, as his finisher focuses on that, but if the wrestler has a known injury he'll go for that. He is an old schol heel. Play that up. I apoligise for this next part in advance. Seth gave me a website with moves on it. So I went wild. Common Moves: Killian will attempt and usually pull off 80-100% of these in a normal match Spear (Delievers quite a good spear. Not amazing, but good.) Spinebuster (Done the cool way; with the rotation and stuff (Arn Anderson stylee, not The Rock stylee) Dropkick Shuffle side kick Triangle hold Sleeper hold Choke hold Sit-out DDT Running DDT Missile dropkick Stinger splash Five star splash Top rope elbow drop Top rope leg drop Lots of release suplexes; Nothing too similar to his finisher. Boston Crab Rare Moves: Killian will attempt between 0-40% of these in a match. These don't have specific names. Moonsault - That's right, he can do one. It's pretty good. Swining Neckbreaker - Chris Harris' Catatonic (More showboating and possibly more rotation so that KKK finishes on top of his opponent, screaming in their face (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves4/catatonic.wmv)) Front face slam (Holds opponent in the air to pose for the crowd. (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves3/SupernaturalDriver.wmv)) Modified Swinging Neckbreaker (More chance to pose to the crowd before hitting the move (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves3/ModifiedSwingingNeckbreaker.wmv)) Top Rope Double Underhook Somersault Bomb - Crazy J's The Suicide ((http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves4/TheSuicide.wmv) This just looks cool so I want it) Rob Conway's Ego Trip (It looks kind of cool and would be a useful corner move (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/egotrip.wmv)) Turnbuckle Bulldog - Spike Dudley's Dudley Dog (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves/aciddrop.WMV) Rib Breaker - Scoot Andrews' Force of Nature (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves/ForceOfNature.wmv) Although not as fast into the move. Hold the guy up there and walk round the ring if possible. Signature Moves: Signify Killian looking to wrap up a match, so lead towards WhiteWash. Will roughly attempt 40-60% of these in any one match. They have specific names. Star Bright Swing - Alex Shelley's Shellshock (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/shellshock.wmv) Give his opponent shit after doing it. Idol Take Down - Doug Basham's Last Impression (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/LastImpression.wmv) Not used to go into a pin, but a chance to stick his face into his opponent's face and give them shit. Dropping Star - Jun Akiyama's Sternness Dust Alpha (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/sternessdust.wmv) but much more stalling at the top of the move. Have him play to the crowd while holding the opponent up in the air, unless it's someone big like Sylo. Cracking at the Top - Blue Panther's El Nudo Lagunero (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/ElNudoLagunero.wmv) Killian will talk crap at his opponent while doing this, or simply pose for the crowd. Curbstomp - Simon Dean's Curbstomp (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/curbstompdean.wmv) Almost always done in a match Irish Suplex - Tiger Mask IV's Millennium Suplex (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/MSuplex.wmv) It's just an awesome move Finisher: O'Leary Suplex '06 - Tucked T-Bone Suplex (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves/tbone.wmv) Exactly how Shelton does it. Again, I blame Seth for all the links. I need help with the names of the moves though. If something else isn't covered for his in-ring mentality, you can come up with it.
