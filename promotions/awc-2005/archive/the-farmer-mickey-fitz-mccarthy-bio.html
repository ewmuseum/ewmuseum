'The Farmer' Mickey Fitz-McCarthy Biography Real Name Michael Fitzpatrick-McCarthy Nicknames 'The Farmer' 'Farmboy' Hometown Dowra, Co. Leitrim Birthday 21 August 1981 Height 6' 4 Weight 233 Biography Michale Fitzpatrick-McCarthy needs money to save his farm. Wrestlers get paid. See the connection? Mickey did. Appearance: Messy, red hair and blue eyes with chiselled good looks masked by a large rough beard. Mickey has a naturally strong looking upper body. He wears a plain white t-shirt. He has white tape on his wrists. He has very ripped muscles. He has a small green shamrock tattoo on his left shoulder. On his lower body he wears dusty blue jeans and black boots with a thick black belt. Wrestling Style Brawler Finisher Move Searing DDT Finisher Description Death Valley Driver set-up dropped into a sit-out DDT Other Moves Michael Fitzpatrick-McCarthy is a brawler. Nothing more, nothing less. He has very little wrestling training. He is naturally strong and very well built. His centre of gravity is very high and is knocked off balance quite easily. Has had a few technical wrestling training sessions. His punches are hard and stiff. Drunken Irish brawls train everyone to fight in some manner. He doesn’t kick. EVER. His poor balance means he generally stumbles when he does kick. He doesn’t go to the top rope. EVER. He will do the 10 punch thing in the corner, but nothing more. Mickey likes the idea of weapons and blood. He will bleed in a match and has no problems with it. Big hardcore spots are welcomed. No submission moves. Mickey can’t do any and can’t survive against any. Even a simple armbar takes it out of the Irishman. He will tap to any submission finisher, and will have trouble battling against simple submission holds. Common Moves: Lots of punches. Mickey likes his opponents in front of him, going backwards, ie being forced backwards by his punches. A few DDT’s nothing innovative. Basic DDT, Reverse DDT, high angle DDT, Raven Effect DDT. A few suplexes. Again, nothing innovative. Body slam and power slam. 10 punch combo, in the corner and standing (when standing opponent is knocked back into the ropes) Signature Moves: MASSIVE Spear Stalling brainbuster with a huge impact on the neck The Cull - T-Bone Suplex Irish Pride – Flapjack with opponent landing on Mickey's shoulder The Harvest – Clothesline from Hell (used to finish matches) Finisher: Searing DDT - Death Valley Driver set-up dropped into a sit-out DDT Entrance Music “Not Far Away” by Pennywise
