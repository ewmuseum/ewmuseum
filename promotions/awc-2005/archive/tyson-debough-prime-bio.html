Tyson DeBough Biography Real Name Tyson DeBough Nicknames 'Dice' Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday September 19, 1983 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Biography Tyson is a gambler at heart, and the epitome of the “High Roller” Las Vegas lifestyle. He started a career as a model, because he was brought up being told that’s all he’d amount to, was his looks. The older he got, the more he loathed it, and decided to get into fighting. At 17, he started training in anything he could get his hands on, and ultimately landed himself a career in the wrestling industry. He’s 23 years old now, and has been in most every fighting scenario possible. A rookie to the AWC maybe, but a rookie in the ring this man is not. Tyson has a habit of carrying a pair of dice in his pockets, hence the tag “Dice”. He’s extremely cocky, so when he’s got his opponent in precarious situation, he sometimes will roll his dice to determine their “fate” if you will. A cruel taunt, but has become a trademark to him, and a crowd favorite. His mother is the sole survivor of a horrible marriage, his father long gone, and basically dead to him. Both his mother, and himself have very large incomes so he's backed by in large by himself financially, with some insurance from his mother. He's a cocky sonofabitch, and takes this job as fun, not serious.
