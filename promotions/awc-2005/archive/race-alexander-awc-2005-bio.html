Race Alexander Biography Real Name Race Alexander Hometown Toronto, Canada Birthday November 12, 1972 Height 6'2 Weight 233 Biography Race Alexander has always possessed all the tools to not only compete in the world of professional wrestling, but to become one of the industry's biggest stars. He has the look. He has the in-ring skill. He has the mic skills. What he hasn't had is his big break. Race has spent his 13 years in the industry, being a main man in several regional independent circuits, but has never been able to hook on with a national promotion. Thats not to say that big promotions didn't come calling on him in the past... PCW had a contract typed with Race's name on it, but a jealous and ambitious Basher Kingston purposely blew out Race's knee during Race's farewell match in the Ottawa Wrestling Association. The Dream Wrestling Federation came calling too. Unfortunately, it turned out that a couple of the guys on the roster had grudges against him from their days in the Catskill Wrestling Federation years earlier. A whisper here, and backroom deal there, and the opportunity disappeared as quickly as it presented itself. When he injured his shoulder while wrestling in Japan, it took him 12 months of rehab, and another two years to shake the lable as 'damaged goods.' A DUI arrest in 1998 after his sister's wedding sent whispers throughout the industry that he had a drinking problem. Despite being just .02 above the legal limit and strictly a 'special occasions drinker', he ended up doing a stint in alcohol rehab in an attempt to repair his damaged reputation. Now, along with his longtime valet/fiance, Connie Battaglia, who has been by his side for 12 years, he finally has received his big shot from the AWC. The contract is signed. Now Race wants the titles, money, fame and endorsements he believes are due to him and will do whatever it takes to make it to the top. He will use every option and tactic available to him to achieve his goals. It took Race 13 years to get to the 'big time,' and nothing is going to stop him from rising to the top. Nothing. No one.
