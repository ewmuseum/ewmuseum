Juri Hyobanshi Biography Real Name ‘I’ve been called many things in my time, but for now my given name is as you’ve heard, Juri Hyobanshi.' Nicknames Masonna (Masochistic Girl), Hakkyouko (Insanity Child), Enzeru Tenma (Angelic Demon) Hometown The Shibuya (of Tokyo, Japan) Birthday 'I'm 16, if it makes any difference. The details of the date aren't important... it changes from time to time.' Height 5'3 Weight 121 Other Feds UEF Other Fed Awards Last ever UEF International champion, youngest UEF International champion Wrestling Style There's only one word for her style... Anarchy. Finisher Move Eien Shifuku / Rapture Eternal Finisher Description Locked in a blink of an eye, the Rapture Eternal is the kill switch of Juri Hyobanshi. The opponent can never anticipate it’s coming, nor prepare against it; Juri is too fast and too lethal with this technique, flawless execution for maximum suffering. At the most unlikely time during a fight, the opponent will find hands upon him, one clutching his throat and the other shoved in his mouth with a double application of a Tongan death grip (front throat nerve clutch) and mandible claw. The painful shock going through the neck, so close to the brain stem and spinal cord, emits a total shock of the nervous system from the neck down. The brain detects so much pain pulsing through the body’s system and panics, where logic most likely sends an immediate command to tap the hell out or risk possible long-term damage. Immediate damage is guaranteed as Juri’s sharp nails can dig into the skin or the soft tissues of the mouth and draw blood, causing the victim to cough up blood if the hold is applied with force and held longer than average (3 seconds) time. Finisher Setup Move Nothing... Setup Description ...can warn or prepare you for the Rapture Eternal. Other Moves Uzumaki: Her failsafe move, should something prevent the Rapture Eternal from being applied. A moonsault Swanton Bomb, where she flips back in a moonsault, and instead of landing chest first she kicks forward for her back to hit instead. One Winged Angel: Running up any surface presented at her (including other opponents or tag teammates) she’ll spring off with a corkscrew moonsault, hitting the opponent behind her with a hard axe kick to the nose and landing in a crouched position. The Ring: Juri turns her back to the opponent as she hits a hard haymaker to the nose and back flips, kicking them in the throat with her first foot and then in the nose with the second close after. Then she quickly nips up after landing on her shoulders, jumping and twisting around and kicking the opponent in the nose again with a right heel spin roundhouse, and then into the jaw with a left spin roundhouse. It’s often for Juri to draw blood from the nose, and if she focuses break/fracture it, considering that between the first roundhouse and the second she backfists the nose with her right hand followed by a left hook so quickly it looks like she’s merely swinging her arms with the kick. Blind Woman Curse: A super fast thrust kick to the eyes with lingering damage. The Flower of Carnage: Juri somersaults (from running or springing) at the opponent and grabs their head for an X-Factor. Her legs come down on top of the opponent’s arms and after impact are hooked behind her knees. She locks in a front face lock as she leans in on the downed opponent, but her hands are so small that the official cannot see the thumb jabbed into the side of the opponent’s neck. The front face lock is left out sometimes, as it’s only added when Juri’s bloodlust is increased. Juri jumps at the victim with a shuffle leg lariat to knock them down. Then she bounces off the ropes and comes back at the rising opponent with a jumping leg lariat to the chin, putting them back down on the mat. As they come back up for a third time, Juri bounces off the ropes and jumps into the air. Her left leg comes first, but instead of making contact, she switches up (since she’s been using the left leg the whole time) and nails a springboard shuffle leg lariat that puts them back on the mat once more. Before Juri hits the mat herself she twists around and drops her left knee into the throat… since she was using the left leg after all… Nailing a Twist of Fate, Juri will fold up so her knees hit the opponent in the back of the skull on the way down. Then she’ll roll around until she’s mounting the opponent’s head and with one hand pulling the head off the mat (by the hair if applicable) Juri will smash fists into the temple of the opponent. Juri jumps and twists, nailing the victim in the face with the back of her left elbow as she follows with a right Roaring Elbow and a flying knee to the jaw close after. When whipped, Juri will hold onto the arm and curl into their reach, nailing a well-placed back elbow into the solar plexus. Then she’ll hop up, wrapping her legs around the opponent’s head and pull them down into a piledriver. Rushing in, Juri will put a solid knee into the solar plexus of the opponent, stunning them and knocking the breath out of their lungs. During that couple of seconds Juri will stare coldly into the opponent’s wide eyes before nailing a hard back elbow to the base of the skull, knocking them onto all fours. Bell clap into a stiff double palm face strike In the dark, baggy garb of Juri, if there is a call for no rules/no DQ, then her legs, feet, hands, and forearms will be lined with steel plates for a more lethal impact. Of course secretly Juri may load one special body part to strike with during a standard match underhandedly. Often catching the opponent by surprise, Juri will snap the back of her hand across the opponent’s larynx with a knife hand chop, cutting off the flow of air briefly. With loss of breath and shock the body tends to panic and doesn’t respond correctly, leaving them defenseless to Juri. Grabbing a kick, Juri will snap a two-point kick swiftly from the attacking knee to the grounded leg’s knee, sending them down to the mat. Unruly matches will allow Juri to proceed with a hard axe kick to the groin, but if rules are in place she’ll lock in a standing reverse figure four and bend back with a deathlock vice on the head and neck. Locking the arms in a double chickenwing with her legs from behind, Juri will roll the opponent over to leave them face up, prone and helpless as she pries open the mouth and shoves in any elongated object she can force into the opponent’s mouth. The idea is to strangle them on this object until they submit or pass out from choking, or until they can’t take the forceful shoving from Juri continuing to ram the entire object down their throats. With the opponent running at her, Juri will catch them with a Lou Thehz press of her own, except she’ll gouge both eyes on impact to push the head back, uneven the opponent’s balance, and put them on their back where they belong. On the ground Juri will continue to pry at the eyes trying to pull them out. A running lariat where Juri dives over the top rope with her arm connecting across the opponent’s collar. Locking around the arm, she uses her weight and momentum to pull them over the top rope, where she’ll drive them on top of their head in a inverted Emerald Fusion. Crossing her right over her left, Juri will twist backwards, uppercutting the opponent in the jaw with her foot. Then she’ll twist around to land on the right foot facing the opponent.
