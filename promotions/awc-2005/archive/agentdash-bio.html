AgentDash Biography Real Name Cedric [name withheld] (formerly Charles Kensrue) Nicknames Dash, The Agent Hometown Berkeley, California Height 5'10' Weight 190 Fed Awards #2 Nov7/06 Biography Patience, friend. Other Feds Core Wrestling Other Fed Awards Core Wrestling Classic Title Wrestling Style High Flyer Finisher Move Star Struck Finisher Description Springboard Leaping Sit-Down Facebuster Finisher Setup Move Agent Kick Setup Description Jumping Thrust Kick to Shoulder (sends opponent stumbling into ropes) Other Moves Wrestling Style & Strategy AgentDash has been a high flyer his entire career and will remain so until he retires. After being employed in the CIA, he also likes to add an element of skilled fighting to his attack - this rare combination allows him to hold his own when he is outsized, something very common in his matches. He also likes a fast-paced match to benefit his style and is quick in all areas of the ring and outside of the intended wrestling area; that being said, he believes that when the match moves to ringside he gains the advantage with more obstacles and objects to use to catapult himself off of, using the momentum to follow-through on strong aerial kicks or moves. One of Dash's strongest areas is his ability to reverse moves, dodge attacks, and move swiftly away from dangerous situations - if the momentum is against him in a match, he will usually try and turn it around using this approach. If the situation turns from bad to worst, Dash will scramble out of the ring and challenge his opponent to an outside battle. This small wrestler is agile as well as having a high endurance - he runs off of adrenaline, almost a requirement when trying to outwrestle larger opponent. Dash is certainly capable of beating larger opponents by tiring them out using his speed and administering a very quick match-pace. Dash is certainly weak in areas of wrestling, however - his strength and size being the highest disadvantage to his attack. He avoids grapples at all times; he rarely begins a match in a grapple and instead tries to slide out of a grapple challenge or throw in a quick blow with a leg or arm; sometimes it works for him, sometimes against him. His submission skills are certainly not finely tuned, but developed enough to keep him close to the level of advanced submissionists. If he begins to feel the match slow down or turn into a technical battle, he forces risky moves of the springboard and general aerial variety. Big risks are also known to effect his game - though he has matured and gained enough experience to know when a dangerous move will result in his demise. Sometimes, however, aderenaline gets the best of him and makes him execute moves that don't always end up well. Move Set Finishing Move Star Struck - Folling the Agent Kick, Kensrue elevates himself using his opponent's shoulder, lands on the top-rope, springboards off and plants his opponent with a Sit-Down Facebuster. Set-Up Move Agent Kick - Jumping thrust kick to the shoulder that sends opponent stumbling into ropes. Signature Move Set The Strike - Dash's own version of the Ace Crusher, with less impact. Mission Accomplished - A Headscissors Takedown executed from the top rope (to the outside or inside of ring). Death From Above 2006 - a Shooting Star Press finished as a Head-Butt. Very rare, dangerous move which is performed only at PPVs and very special matches. Basic Move Set - Many of these manuevers can be performed in many different situations.
