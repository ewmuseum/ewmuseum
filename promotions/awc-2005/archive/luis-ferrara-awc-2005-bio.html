Luis Ferrara Biography Real Name Luis Ferrara Hometown San Carlos, Venezuela Birthday November 3, 1978 Height 5'9' Weight 158 Biography |PERSONAL INFORMATION| Heritage/Nationality: Venezuelan Physical Description: Luis has light tan skin and is clearly of Hispanic descent. He has jet black hair that comes down to the nape of his neck, typically greased back to give him a slick look, and he cares for his short, devilish goatee meticulously. He has no tattoos or any markings of the sort on his body. He's short and rather lean, not athletic at all. Attire: Luis comes down to the ring in a suit, most notably a white zoot suit, but he has been known to change from time to time to a more contemporary business suit. Across his chest is generally seen a slight bulge where he keeps his tonfa, but when anyone asks about it, he generally responds 'That's just low long my dick is.' Personality: Ferrara is a slick, well-spoken individual with a deliberate accent and a hot temper. He is quick to step in on anything Anton might be about to say and handles the press with ease and vigilance. He often carries brass knuckles around with him to 'help' Anton. Easily seduced. Management Style Ruthless; interferes heavily Finisher Move Yes Finishing Move Description Tonfa (hidden in suit) Finisher Setup Move Yes Other Tactics |WRESTLING STYLE| Luis prefers to let Anton fight. That is his main tactic; get behind Anton. When he is forced to fight for himself, he portrays himself as a coward, typically performing the classic 'Flair Flop' with a twist: Luis has a penchant to do a REGIONS~! shot rather than poke the eyes. He sneaks around until he can get a decisive upper hand, whereupon he attacks with finishing manuever. |FINISHERS| TASTE THE KNIFE|●●▬▬▬ An overhyped name for the move, Taste the Knife basically consists of Luis kicking his opponent in the crotch, then grabbing their head and dropping down with a knee smash, driving their face into his knee. He generally uses this when the ref is distracted and he manages to slip into the ring. This move is not used when he is fighting for himself, only when he sees an opportunity to aid Anton. INTRAVENOUS INJECTION|●●●▬▬ 2Count-20% 2.5-20% 2.9-30% Pin-30% Generally shortened to 'Injection,' the Intravenous Injection begins with Luis locking his opponent in an inverted facelock. He then grabs their tights and lifts up shortly, bending backwards and drilling his opponent with a high-impact DDT. This is Luis's actual finisher, used when he must fend for himself, which is rare. Because he is largely untrained, it is sloppy and incredibly stiff. Entrance Music 'Born on Your Knees' by Napalm Death
