Alcaeus Biography Real Name Anacletus Alcaeus Nicknames Alcaeus Hometown Appolonia Birthday Unknown...by a different calander Height 6'9 Weight 285 Biography Anacletus Alcaeus believes himself to be a 2500 year old vampire. There are no records of his birth, and all ID's he has (Driving License, Passport) only date back 4 years. Everything about this strange individual is shrouded in mystery. He walks in public, dressed extravegantly, applying ash and dust to his face for a more 'human' look. Alcaeus claims to have been a warrior in the Ancient Greek army's Appolonian Guard. Standing at 6'9, he is a hulk of an individual, and as his story goes...he was a legend among his bretheren. But then, one day, while sacking a village during an invasion of Persia...he came upon an individual who could stand against him in battle...the first of any men who could claim to do so. The supposed man had uncanny speed, and balance, and managed to disarm Alcaeus in a matter of minutes. The creature though, took pity on Alcaeus, and made him into a Vampire. The man's name was Cyrus...and he led his life in public as a rich merchant. His reasons became apparant for making Alcaeus one of his kind, when he revealed his history to Anacletus. His maker had been a travelling merchant, when he had been struck upon by a band of thieves. They stole everything, and left him for dead...until a rogue vampire found him, and turned him to the side of the preternatural. The rogue was mad, and left Cyrus on his own, with no teaching, and no information. He spent his first day in agony, the light of the sun almost blinding him, causing a reaction in his blood, which caused him to become unusually warm. The myths though, of Vampires burning in the Sun, Alcaeus states are untrue. Cyrus yearned for someone to share his immortal curse with, and he chose Alcaeus, a man of sizable stature and personality, to be his ally and friend. The two travelled the world together for countless years...until one day Cyrus simply disappeared. It was during the time of the Great Roman Empire that Cyrus disappeared into nothingness...and Alcaeus never set eyes on his old maker again. Every now and then, he heard stories of Cyrus...a travelling Merchant who's eyes could seduce any woman, and frighten any man. Though these could be coincidence, Alcaeus chooses to believe they are more than that. Over the proceding years, Alcaeus created for himself homes in Rome, Constantinople, Athens, The Ural Mountains, Persia, Florence, Venice, Paris, Rome once more, London, and finally Chicago. On an eternal search for truths in existence, he has come to a stage in his life where he feels he cannot go on in the secret shadows of vampiric silence. The 21st Century, to Alcaeus, presents the perfect time to come into the open...to declare himself. He has tried this before, with varying success...but now he inserts himself into a world of recognition...wrestling...and looks to make a name for himself, where he can be heralded and understood.
