You have Russell, who's from Pleasure & Pain, and a total amateur, Heroic Henry, taking each other. This is Henry's first match in years, and it showed. Russell was basically spot-on for most of the match and was tearing Henry apart for the first couple minutes of the match; severe elbows to the face, a reverse piledriver, and Russell was finding an obsession with the ring corners. He would Irish-whip Henry into the corner and them keep him there for the duration while kneeing him in the stomach and continuing the vicious elbows. It wasn't long until Henry showed first blood, with a slash above his right elbow from an apparent face slam into the ring post. The evidence in front of everyone's eyes was enough to show Henry's inexperience, and the physical torment he was going through almost forced the ref, on two occasions, to stop the match. After ten minutes of absolute punishment, Henry found a second wind after rolling away from a leg drop. Crawling to the ropes, he pulls himself up and charges after Russell, screaming bloody gore and slapping the side of his head.
