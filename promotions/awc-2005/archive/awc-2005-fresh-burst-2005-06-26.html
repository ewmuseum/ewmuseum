Ashton is irate outside the ring, pulling FREDROCK~!’s other leg off in frustration. Pact grins, but turns around only to receive a boot to the gut from Sake. James Brunt: FREDROCK~! and Christopher Ashton have been eliminated! Truth Waters: Well, there goes your lover boy and his dance partner. George Cassidy: He is not my lover boy! I was simply supporting him in this match. Besides, my money’s on Nathan Benedict to win this one, Truth. Sake delivers a wicked belly-to-belly suplex on Pact, who lands awkwardly on his leg. Xandrous, who has been largely quiet in the match thus far, nails Void with a neck breaker from behind, then scales the top turnbuckle. Before he can land any high risk move, though, Benedict pushes him off the top rope and to the floor outside. Xandrous lands hard on his back, but seems to be unharmed. Benedict gives the thumbs up to his manager, James Everett III. Everett applauds his client with a grin plastered across his face. James Brunt: Steven Xandrous has been eliminated! Truth Waters: Last week’s Fresh!man has lost his chance. What a surprise that is! George Cassidy: Being Fresh!man isn’t everything, you know. ASS does some damage to Void and Sake, with head butts to both men. ASS then attempts a big boot on Benedict, but Benedict ducks it. Benedict whips the big man off the ropes and drops him with a back elbow. Truth Waters: Paddy O’Shea’s back-up man, ASS, showing his brute strength. George Cassidy: Brute strength he may have, but I see no ability in that big oaf at all. Pact takes Void up after ASS’s head butt and delivers one of his most unforgiving and painful moves, The Betrayal. Pact kicks Void right in the REGIONS~!, and Pact easily dumps Void over the top rope. Pact quickly checks out the final men in the ring - Jonny Sake, ASS and Nathan Benedict. Sake rushes at Benedict to try to surprise him and knock him out, but Benedict sees him coming and tosses Sake over the top rope to the outside. James Brunt: Void and Jonny Sake have been eliminated! Truth Waters: Well, we’re down to three now! It’s going to be either Pact, Nathan Benedict or ASS who will decide the order in the competitors will enter in the Solar Power match. George Cassidy: It has to Benedict. This guy has such an intricate mind; he’ll have fun choosing the fate of his opponents. ASS attempts to fend off the double team from Pact and Benedict, but is unable to do so. Pact and Benedict whip the big man off the ropes and easily eliminate him with a double shoulder tackle. ASS lands, interestingly enough, on his ass outside the ring. Everett laughs at ASS, mocking the bodyguard. ASS reacts with anger, chasing Everett up the ramp way and backstage, leaving Benedict without his manager at ringside. Pact and Benedict locks eyes, and the crowd are instantly behind the veteran, Pact. James Brunt: Alistaire Seamus Sheridan has been eliminated! George Cassidy: Meaning we’re down to my man Nathan Benedict and… Pact. How unfortunate. I was hoping Benedict would have a challenge. Truth Waters: Oh, I can assure you that Pact will put up a hell of a fight. He wants the Relentless championship as much as Benedict does. Pact and Benedict tie-up, with Benedict gaining the advantage. Not holding back, Benedict knees Pact in the sternum and delivers a vertical suplex to the veteran. Pact, however, reverses it in midair and lands on his feet. Pact gives Benedict a knee to the gut in retaliation, and plants Benedict with a snap DDT. The crowd let out a collective “Oh!” as Benedict’s head smacks the canvas. Truth Waters: Whoa! What impact in that DDT! George Cassidy: The old man’s still got some wind in his sails. Pact is unable to cover Benedict under the battle royal rules, and so lifts his opponent up to try and dump over the top rope and out of the ring. Benedict stops Pact in his tracks, however, hitting an elbow to the midsection. Benedict takes Pact down with a hip toss, then follows it up with a leg drop across the neck. The veteran rolls away as Benedict takes time to play to the crowd. George Cassidy: (in disbelief) They’re booing? Truth Waters: What do you expect? Benedict is a sly, conniving, little wimp. Benedict lifts Pact to his feet just as Everett comes back down to ringside. Benedict gives him the nod, but Benedict is distracted and Pact takes advantage, locking on Forever Blackeyed. Pact drags Benedict down to the mat with him and continues to apply pressure on the neck. Benedict can hardly breathe. To escape the move, Benedict has no choice but to poke Pact right in the eyes. Pact relinquishes the hold at last, but the damage has been done to Benedict. Truth Waters: Nathan Benedict had to resort to petty tactics like that to escape the hold. Man, what a wimp. George Cassidy: And you say I’m biased? Pact, in a burst of adrenaline, rushes at Benedict to eliminate him, but Benedict ducks Pact’s clothesline and Pact goes flying over the top rope. Luckily, he manages to hang on and clumsily rolls himself back into the ring. Benedict stomps on Pact with a vengeance, then chokes the life out of the veteran. Benedict lifts Pact to his feet and sets him up for a death valley driver. Truth Waters: This can’t be good for the veteran, Pact! George Cassidy: Benedict is going to dump Pact out of the ring! Benedict goes to deliver the death valley driver to eliminate Pact, but somehow Pact lands on the apron, albeit slightly off balance. Pact hangs on, fighting off Benedict with jabs to the chin. Pact propels himself back into the ring, dropping Benedict with a springboard dropkick to the face! The crowd come to life and cheer for the veteran’s efforts. Truth Waters: Amazing show of skill by Pact there. For a man of fifty, that isn’t too bad. George Cassidy: Yeah, yeah, sing his praises now. Just wait until he breaks his leg doing that one day. Then we’ll see who the skilful one is. Pact and Benedict both get back to their feet, Pact gaining the advantage straight away with a boot to the gut of Benedict, followed by a stunning orange crush suplex. Pact takes Benedict by the head and runs to the ropes with him, hoping to dump him out. Benedict goes flying over the top rope, but brings Pact with him. Neither men’s feet hit the floor, however, both hanging off the top rope precariously. Truth Waters: What a tense situation we have here! Who has the will to hang on? George Cassidy: Obviously Nathan Benedict! The two competitors manage to use their strength to pull themselves back onto the ring apron. They trade shots, with Benedict managing to re-enter the ring first. Benedict attempts to knock Pact out, but the veteran claws his way out of predicament with a thumb to the eye. Pact scales the top turnbuckle, and the crowd go into a frenzy of cheers, but Everett intervenes, taking Pact’s legs from under him and crotching him on the top rope. The fans boo Everett with fury, but he manages to make himself scarce before he can be spotted. Truth Waters: What a slimy creature that Everett is! He could have just screwed Pact out of the victory. George Cassidy: Here comes Nathan Benedict, taking advantage of his manager’s genius work! Benedict goes to the top turnbuckle and attempts a superplex on Pact, but Pact resists, pushing Benedict down to the mat below. Pact looks around at the fans and shrugs his shoulders, as if to say “What the hell”. Pact dives off with an asai corkscrew moonsault and hits it perfectly. The crowd are in shock at the move, and the “Holy s*it!” chants soon follow. Pact rolls onto his back after the high impact move, but Benedict looks to be out of it completely. George Cassidy: How can that old man perform a move like that? HOW?! Truth Waters: Because Pact is, quite simply, a legend in this sport. I’ve told you time and time again that he is capable of amazing things. That was one of them. Pact slowly gets back to his feet and drags Benedict to his feet, too. Benedict can barely stand after the moonsault. Everett shouts orders to Benedict, but his client is in dreamland. Pact grins at Benedict, and then signals to the fans that it’s all over. Pact runs towards the ropes and delivers a wicked King Killer to Benedict, then lifts him up and easily dunks him over the top rope for the victory. Truth Waters: That’s it! Pact has proven once again that he deserves to be in the top ranks of AWC and the world of wrestling with an impressive win in the battle royal. George Cassidy: So that means Pact decides the order of entrants in the Solar Power match, eh? Disaster… The crowd come to their feet, jeering Benedict and applauding Pact at the same time. Pact gives the crowd a theatrical boo, but the response he gets is phenomenal. Pact’s hand is raised by Lars Larsson. James Brunt: Here is your winner… PACT!!! An eruption of cheers from the fans in attendance as Pact heads to the top rope to take in the reception. Truth Waters: It’s been a great night, ladies and gentlemen! Don’t miss Solarized, AWC’s upcoming pay-per-view on July 1st! Order now and enjoy a night of action you will never forget! George Cassidy: Who knows, folks? You may see this man, Pact, break his leg. Now wouldn’t that be worth the price alone? Truth Waters: From Truth Waters and George Cassidy, it’s good night! George Cassidy: And good riddance… A video package begins, showing the cream of the crop in the Atlantic Wrestling Club in short bursts. Each superstar’s highlights are shown - Alexander Strider’s Transatlantic championship win, Crimson O’Malec winning the Relentless championship, Pact becoming the first ever two-time Fresh!man among others. All these images freeze on the screen one by one, as “Blood” by Editors begins to play in the background. A pulse is heard, beating rhythmically along with the music as the opening chords are played. Then, the Sun takes over the screen and the words “AWC Presents Solarized” appear in huge black letters. Brief images of the four teams involved in the Alliance championship league are shown, then the table as it stands. The drums kick in as if on cue, and the teams various wins and losses are shown. Emerald Isle stand triumphant in the ring after their victory on the 21st June edition of Fresh! The Full House are shown folding their arms, looking menacingly at the camera. The Annoying Neighbours are shown together, apparently psyching each other up. And finally, Krimzon Karnage Inc. are shown together, with the Educator explaining some tactics to Krimzon. Narrator: Solarized will see the very first Alliance champions of AWC crowned. Four teams are vying for that honour - which of them will have what it takes? David “Pearl” Harber is shown in the ring on Fresh! on the 21st June, explaining the prospect of the Solar Power match. Pearl smiles and the scene switches to the reigning Relentless champion, Crimson O’Malec. His stance is confident; his face, determined and wise. Narrator: A man with determination steps into the ring for a gauntlet match against nine men… His mission? To survive. The words “AWC Solarized” are whispered with “Blood” still playing, sounding very ominous. A ring of fire is shown, with two unknown competitors lying outside the ring, burns and cuts all over their bodies. The screen splits, with the Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider on one side, and the challenger Pierce Lavelle on the other. The images fuse together, and the rivals stare at each other with nothing but hate and fury in their eyes. Narrator: In their ultimate test, two men will fight for the AWC Transatlantic championship… Lavelle and Strider are shown trading blows in their previous encounters. Narrator: But this is no ordinary environment. The flames take over the screen suddenly, and Pearl’s announcement of the Inferno match is shown. Lavelle and Strider’s expressions are absolute shock. Narrator: One thing is for certain - one man will come out champion, but who will pay the price? A huge collage of images of AWC superstars take over the screen, with Strider and Lavelle at the core of it all. The Sun is then superimposed onto this image, making Strider and Lavelle’s faces the core of the Sun. “Blood” comes to an abrupt end, and these words come across the screen. AWC Presents Solarized - 1st July, 2005, only on pay-per-view!
