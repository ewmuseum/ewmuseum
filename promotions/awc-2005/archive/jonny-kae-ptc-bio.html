Jonny Kae Biography Real Name Jonathan Alexander Spencer Kae Nicknames 'The Most Perfectly Attuned Body in Professional Wrestling' Hometown London, England Birthday 19th August 1970 Height 6'7 Weight 268 Biography Jonny Kae was born to an aristocratic family in Basingstoke, England. He was raised with a silver spoon in his mouth, given all he ever asked for by his dolting parents. Kae was sent off to boarding school at an early age, where he learnt not only his curricular studies, but the usefulness of manipulation via his fellow students and teachers. When he was of age, Kae was sent to Eton, where his already instilled arrogance was deepened by the rhetoric fed to the pupils. Kae was educated in Economics and Accounting, as well as Mathematics and English, and given a clean slate to choose any higher education institution of his choice. Choosing against Oxford and Cambridge, Kae instead went to Harvard, in the USA. He found he had an art for leadership there, and in no time was captain of the soccer team, American Football team, but more importantly for present purposes, the wrestling team. His glowing resume would serve Kae well. During his time at University, Jonny Kae was approached by a modelling agency by the name of Profile. Having seen his self-made portfolio (created more out of arrogance than contemplation of any pursuit of modelling) the company was eager to sign him to their books. Kae agreed to work under their banner, going to castings all over, appearing in campaigns for such designers as Diesel and Full Circle. But it was in commercials that Kae found his niche. Having done a few minor parts, Kae became the flagship face of 'Drogo Pens' in 1991, at the tender age of 21. His charismatic approach and well-bred good looks made for a lethal combination in the world of fashion and commerce. Kae was soon recognised for his abilities, and given a small part in a movie, a Napoleonic Drama based on the life of Nelson. He was soon given the second lead role, when his natural talents were recognised. Kae used his father's money to help fund the programming and production of the low budget film, and many suggested this was the reason for his sudden elevation to supporting lead. The movie, unsurprisingly with the arrogant Kae up-showing the lead, flopped. But his acting skills were recognised and enjoyed by a different audience, that of proffessional wrestling. In 1994, Kae was given his first offer of professional wrestling. Having graduated from Harvard with a Masters, Kae decided to stick in America, and accepted. He made various appearances for the large promotion throughout the states, but his work ethic and arrogance backstage soon led to his contract being cancelled. Dejected, Kae returned to the UK, to explain to his domineering father why he'd stayed in the USA. To his luck, perhaps, his father 'miraculously' passed away within 1 day of Jonny Kae coming back. There was a news fiasco, as William Spencer Kae had not signed his will. However, Jonny had managed to turn the entire estate his way (many believe through political influence and intrigue). Jonny thus inherrited his Father's umbrella corporation, Kae Enterprises, all his assets and estates in the UK and offices in the USA. Due to his financial assets and corporate ties, Kae was granted an unconditional US Citizenship (much as his father had been) and had a mansion built on the outskirts of Chicago. Kae's natural facility for entrepreneurship furthered his father's company. Within 2 years, Kae had turned his father's European empire into a worldwide conglomerate multi-national. Extending into Sports broadcasting, and all forms of entertainment, as well as taking advantage of the new phenomena of the internet with huge scale investment paid due dividends. However, Kae could not help but feel he had failed in one aspect of his life. In 1997, Jonny Kae returned, now at the age of 27, to the ring, with a small federation called the World Championship Cyber Wrestling Corporation, or WCCW. This time, with money and power behind him, Kae could survive as his own, arrogant and usurping/manipulative personality. The rest is history. However, in early 2002, Kae hung up his boots, believing his time with wrestling was finished. Having been a 9-time world champion, and owner of a wrestling federation, Kae decided to return all his attentions to his corporate interests. Kae Enterprises flourished with Jonny Kae's astute mind. They infiltrated further markets, and had a hand in the East Asian Growth spurts, with communications and IT sciences. The company also, however, made powerful friends and influences. There were rumbles of underworld involvement in Kae Enterprise operations, with some of Kae's critics going missing, and then turning up in bizarre situations which led to their credibility being stricken. The pressure on Kae was large, too, taking its toll on his happiness. In 2005, Kae was contacted by a friend and former colleague in the ICWF, Vincent Noble, who offered him a place in the Viking Wrestling Federation. Kae at first dismissed the offer, but after subsequent offers, he had second thoughts. Kae returned to the ring in January 2005, winning his first world title for 3 years, and bringing a smile back to the face of the man who's entire life revolved around power and domination. He juggled his corporate responsibility with his love for wrestling, and soon managed to cope magnificently. In March 2005, Kae found his first position in the Prime Time Central Umbrella of wrestling organisations, with the Headstrong Wrestling federation. Jonny entered as a rookie, unknown in this facet of the industry, but still wearing the VWF Heavyweight Title around his waist. Soon, he was propelled to the main event in HSW, however, winning the Evolution Title vacated by the record holder, El Nutso. Kae went on to remain unbeaten in HSW competition for over 6 months, losing only 2 matches otherwise (1 to Deszaras at Super Series 1, the other to Mindy Mercer in a Global-HSW interfed matchup, due to the interference of The Resident Evil, Deszaras). Jonny Kae's legacy to the business world is great, but his mark on wrestling is perhaps just as bold. He now comes to the Atlantic Wrestling Club, wiser, but perhaps not as young as he once was. Many pundits claim that Mr Kae will buckle under the pressure of a new commitment, and that at the age of 36, he simply doesn't have what it takes to rebuild a great name. Only time will tell...
