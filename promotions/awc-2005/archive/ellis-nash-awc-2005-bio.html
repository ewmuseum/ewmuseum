Ellis Nash Biography Real Name Eleanor Elisabeth Nash Nicknames ER~!, Chuck Palahniuk Hometown Cortland, New York Birthday July 12, 1979: 27 Height 5'8' Weight 126 Fed Titles Frontier Champion (03/24/2006-06/23/2006) Fed Awards Q1: Best Comedy Segment; Q3-4: Most Wins, Best Winning Streak, Best High-Flyer; Top 10 #1 (x6) Wrestling Style Strikeopalooza amalgamated with light-footed aerial and springboarding. Finisher Move Decree Finisher Description Spike** Inverted Facelock DDT. Finisher Setup Move any Setup Description thing. Other Moves most often used -- Strikes: Forearm Uppercuts, Step Jabs, Backhand Chops, Front Kick, Bicycle Kick, Spinning Wheel Kick, Spinning Roundhouse, Jumping Heel Kick, Shining Wizard, Dragon Whip, Front Dropkick, Single Leg Running Dropkick, Baseball Slide, Flying Clothesline, Crossbody. Grapple/Suplex: Drop Toe Hold, Snap Vertical, Flying Head Scissors, Underhook Face Breaker, Sitout Jawbreaker, Swinging DDT. Ground: Mounted Punches, Knee Drop Bulldog, Twisting Leg Drop, Foot Chokes. Aerial: Split-Legged Moonsault, Frog Splash. Springboarding: She's lanky. Use your imagination. etc. information that may or may not be important: 1) is not below cheating, will resort to it whenever the fuck she feels like it and is notorious for region (tildes are so yesterday) shots 2) utilizes strikes more often than actual wrestling moves because that's where she is well-versed in and it shows 3) shrewdly isolates parts of her opponent's bodies ie: if they're a flyer, will relentlessly target the leg 4) cocky, arrogant, pompous, whatever; will, with a grin, endlessly mock both her opponent and the fans that supply her paycheck day in and day out 5) does not go out of her way to be a spot monkey necessarily, but whenever the opportunity strikes (or she needs it) she'll go up 6) i don't watch wrestling, i hope this is evident 7) goodbye Entrance Music 'Oblivion' by Aimee Allen
