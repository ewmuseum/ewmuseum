Jayden Gi-Kyon Biography Real Name Jayden Gi-Kyon Hometown Seattle, WA Birthday July 9th, 1984. Height 5'9 Weight 181 Biography Jayden Gi-Kyon is a household name in many circles, save for the World of Wrestling and he has a strong desire to change that. In the realm of martial arts he is known far and wide, most of his fame coming as the 2004 Olympic Gold medalist in the art of Taekwondo, representing the United States in the light heavyweight division. While this may be his most famous accolade it is far from being his only mark on the sport. The 4th degree blackbelt has carved a legacy that will be long untouched in the great sport of Taekwondo, being the only man to be voted both WTF (World Taekwondo Federation) and ITF (International Taekwondo Federation) athlete of the year. A competitor on the national circuit since he was fresh faced 15 year old, the Gold medalist has garnered many accolades. 2004 Gold medal (Light heavyweight division) Member of 2004 Olympic Taekwondo team. (Lightweight division) Jayden, a proud resident of Seattle, met and trained with Global legend 'American Youth' Josh M. in the Gi-Kyon family Dojang, owned and operated by Jayden's father, a martial arts master and Hollywood fight choreographer. Jayden, the superior martial artist helped American Youth to step his game up and in return, the longest reigning World Champion in Global Wrestling history took Jayden to the canvas and showed him all of the Wrestling essentials and much more, declaring Jayden as the first student of the infamous Puro-Pop Wrestling style. Wrestling Style 'Puro-pop'. An exciting technical style with numerous high-flying maneuvers and stiff martial arts kicks. Finisher Move # 1 - Threads of time. # 2 - Kakoriaphobia. Finisher Description # 1 - (Threads of time) Grabbing the opponent by the wrist Jayden rushes to the ropes, leaps onto the top rope, kicks off and drops clutching his opponents head and driving him down with a spinning DDT. # - 2 (Kakoriaphobia) Springboard flipping neckbreaker. Finisher Setup Move Running tornado leg lariat. Entrance Music 'I caught fire' by The Used.
