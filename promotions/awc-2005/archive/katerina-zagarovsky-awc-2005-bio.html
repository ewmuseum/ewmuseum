Katerina Zagarovsky Biography Real Name Katerina Zagarovsky Nicknames The Angel of Death, The Black Hammer Hometown Moscow, Russia Birthday 1980/08/02 Height 5'7 Weight 125 Biography Katerina Zagarovsky was born in Kiev, Ukraine before moving to the States with her family. Raised in a very religious family, faith and Christian doctrines had been a major part of her life. Enough so that she had taken time to concentrate on theology and ancient religions as part of her studies. On the more physical side, Katerina also had been always active and trained under the tutoring of her uncle, a former circus acrobat, since her young age. The young Katerina had also been quite a tomboy and had a good reputation of being patient and kind enough until she would knock the boys who teased friends and herself for too long. These little incidents had brought her an interest in learning more ways to be able to keep her peace. She took martial arts (Muay Thai and Tae-Kwon-Do) and is rumoured to continue her training on a daily basis. A close family friend and wrestling instructor having seen her in different street fights contacted(she was in her last year of High school) and offered her a training in wrestling. Interested, Katerina accepted and in time, had participated in a few indy matches to the awe and surprise of all. On her second year of College, this came to a stop when she had (for studies and other reasons) decided to stop everything to move to Rome. Katerina had then vanished completely until recently... Other Feds AGWF, XWF, JUW, CORE, DRWF Other Fed Awards AGWF First Lady, XWF Best New Comer Award, XWF RPer of the Year (2004), XWF X-Light Laureate, DRWF writer of the month, DRWF FTW champion, DRWF Double Crowned champion, DRWF Sovereign champion. Wrestling Style High flyer/martial arts/Hardcore Finisher Move 1- 'Exorcised Absolution' 2-'Knock to Heaven' Finisher Description 1-Pedigree except Katerina jumps with her opponent higher in the air and ends with both her knees hitting the back of her opponent's head. 2-corner Asai DDT (aka Marafuji' Shiranui) Finisher Setup Move 'Faith Call' Setup Description Flying Spinning Roundhouse Kick Other Moves Trademark Moves: 'Heaven's Bliss' Handspring Back Elbow Smash 'Prayer's End' Fireman's Carry Face First Powerbomb 'Salvation Strike' Horizontal Flip Scissors Kick but will lands on her feet Martial moves: Thrust kick Axe kick Sidekick Jumping kick to the head Open palm thrust Sweep kick Spin kick Wrestling moves: Knee smash Baseball slide kick Arm-breaker Forearm Smash Lou Thesz Press Clotheline Northern Lights Suplex Aerial Moves: Backflip Splash Rounding Moonsault Splash Off Top Rope Legdrop Catapult Crossbody Sucida Double Springboard Moonsault Splash Submission moves: Figure Four Leglock Cross face Scorpion Deathlock Cougar Clutch Tiger's Suplex Hold Katerina is a young woman with a good agility and speed but seeing her weight, she takes this to the best of her advantage. She is also aggressive without having to resort to cheating (holding the ropes, using weapons when not necessary or interference from 'future' friends.). Her heat will come often from the fact she will continue to attack/subdue her opponents even after the bell signaled an end to a match. Katerina is a strong competitor and as such will have a strong competitive spirit. Anyone who had not been either respectful or shown their value to her in and out of the ring will be met with a harsher version of her own self. Anyone attempting or making any gestures/cheating will understand why she had chosen a killer in gimmick. The young Russian woman is also very persistant and has enough stamina (due to her background) where she can receive a lot of punishment and still be able to strike back. She is not the kind of athlete who will show off too much and is one to go for the precise and continuous hits until her opponent is brought down. Psychological games played before and during a match will only bring more fuel to the fire, hence bringing her to another level of the dormant violence inside her. She nonetheless will try to remain calm and focus at the start of the match until her opponent will click her off. ********* Fighting Style: Katerina had been given an extensive training by a circus acrobat and thus will use this ability to the maximum of its potential. She is also a street fighter having fought since she was a kid and it shows in most of her matches. Thankfully, a good level of discipline had been brought to her while studying: Rukopashnii Boi: The much harder and direct Systema fighting techniques (based on Systema, a Russian martial art) used by the Spetsnaz, is a realistic combat style of fighting with real-life, rather than sport, application. It involves a unique, high speed, elliptical open-hand striking system, kicking, and defense on the ground. Unlike many Asian styles, Systema has no predetermined fighting stances, but teaches you to defend yourself against attackers from any position. In addition, students are trained in the use and disarming of weapons, improvised weapons, as well as combat in non-traditional situations such as fighting from a chair or while in a car. Katerina had become a skilled and lethal expert in this style. Sambo: A combat grappling style often said to be a mix of jiu-jitsu and greco-roman wrestling. Katerina is quite good at this but not to the extent she has reached with Rukopashnii Boi systema. Luchadore style wrestling: Katerina has taken her aerial moves from this as well as combining it with her already great acrobacy level. She uses this much more on special occasions only since she sees this as a way to show off her skills. No matter, she will adapt and use the Mexican wrestling style IF her opponent is known to be sharing that same style. ********* Favourite specialty matches: Extreme and dangerously precise, Katerina will enjoy any kind of matches but excels at TLC kind of matches. Favourite weapons: Unless it is the end of a match/non DQ, she will not use any weapons during the match as she sees the use of it as non competitive. Will she use any after? How did the opponent acted during the match? Is it someone she dislikes? If so...A chair or something else will better be far from her reach.
