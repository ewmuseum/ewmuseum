Hammer Biography Real Name Andrew [name withheld] Nicknames Andy, Hammer, H Hometown Pittsburgh, PA Height 5'11'' Weight 233 Biography Hammer is the more slight member of Tempered Steel at 5'11' and 232 pounds. To make the most of his physical attributes Hammer has become pretty good off of the top turnbuckles (and ring ropes in general) and his flexibility and balance clearly reflects that. Hammer is never cleanly shaven but he doesn't sport a beard. Out of the ring he frequently wears a Pittsburgh Pirates baseball cap and his dirty blonde hair is long enough to be seen out of the back of his cap but does not extend all the way to his shoulders. Hammer has much more of a chip on his shoulders than his partner Anvil and therefore has really worked hard to make his body look extremely well-defined. If ever he gets injured or takes a break there is no doubt his body would tighten up and he'd be extremely stiff in the ring. As far as attire, both members of Tempered Steel wear long, loose-fitting black karate pants. Hammer has the words 'Tempered ' and 'Steel' stitched vertically downwards in white block letters, one word on each pant leg. Hammer also wears black wrestling boots, has hazel eyes and frequently has black and gold tape wrapped around his wrists. Hammer is supposed to be just what his name is, the hammer of the team. He has sporadic bursts of extremely high energy and frequently is the one to get the pinfall in tag matches. Hammer, however, does more often than not find himself being the one trapped in the ring absorbing a lot of punishment and straining to tag in Anvil. As a result, he has unusually strong stamina for someone his size.
