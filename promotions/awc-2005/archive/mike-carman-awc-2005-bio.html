Mike Carman Biography Real Name Michael Christopher Carman Nicknames Cock Block... The Carmanator Hometown New York City Birthday 5/10/80 Height 6'1' Weight 218 Biography He grew up in a household with only a mother, and all of those years weren't the best of times. Mike had to work while he took care of his sick and dying mother. He had no one else to turn to, so while his mother lied dying in bed, Mike would cook and do what he could to make money. It wasn't too long before his mother died, though. After this event Mike walked to a foster home, they happily accepted the poor little boy. It was there where he made a very good friend; he went by the, Kyle Phillips. Mike's years went slow in that home but when the day came, Mike had his chance to leave, so Kyle and Mike took off in pursue of a new like. One day, while roaming the streets of Cleveland, OH, Kyle and Mike entered a building to get out of the cold. It was there they found an interest in this, so it seemed appropriate to try to learn and then find a job in wrestling field. They trained and worked with the gym that same gym and now look to join a fed. They come to AWC, and try to further their ability in the ring. Mike Carman will be the one who wrestle's, Kyle at this point, will only be by Mike's side. They soon will form a tag team, though, but until then Mike is the only competitor out of the two. His gimmick is to just act himself, but add a bit of flavor with mean promo's and a top notch in ring performance.
