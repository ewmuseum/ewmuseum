Red Rock Biography Real Name Josh Y. Nicknames The Anti Hero Hometown Aldershot - England Birthday 5th August 1983 Height 5'10 Weight 197 Fed Titles LiveWire Champion x1, Relentless Champion x1 Fed Awards Fresh!Man x 3, Longest Reigning non cheating Livewire champion, better than Angelo Deville, People's Choice for Q3-4A Biography Red Rock is a guy who was born in Frimley park hospital in surrey England and grew up just around the corner in a small army town called Aldershot. He is shaping up to be another of the great British losers being put next to great British losers such as Ricky Jervais or Hugh Grant. Living in Aldershot town Red Rock saw and heard stories of street beatings mainly by the drunken louts in the streets of the town centre. His fighting background came from fighting against the English human disease known as 'the chav' being the type of out spoken person he was often found himself in fights round every corner. After a while he discovered trying to talk his way out the fights just made things worse so he decided he would have to learn to defend himself or die... Being a fan of the bigger wrestling federations and being the young idiot he was thought he could copy wrestling moves on these people. This resulted in further beatings and humiliation...to his disappointment. Red Rock a persistent young man didn't give up there so he decided he would learn a martial art. He went to several classes and picked up one or two things, but he decided it wasn't quite for him. His reasons for leaving and I Quote 'I looked like a gay boy in those crap pajamas'. So Red Rock looked for other means to train himself to fight. In his local comic book shop he say an advert for a wrestling school run by a former wrestler of one of the great wrestling federations. He decided that this would be a fun way to learn to defend himself he thought 'Hey! no one will beat me up if they knew I was a wrestler' So he paid the fee and started to learn and train and he persisted and sweated and became fitter and faster. Starting now to feel a little more confident in his abilities to fight he went out for a night on the town looking for a fight. When a guy shouted across the street at him 'oi queer get a hair cut!' Red Rock told him to piss off and called him a cunt. The bloke chased Red Rock down the street as did his 20 friends. It seemed that Red Rock's confidence went out the window at this point. In The hospital Red Rock was visited by his coach, they had a convocation and his trainer explained that its a bit different in the ring than it is on the street. Red Rock's confidence was nothing as he laid there after feeling the wrath of 20 men beating him near to death but his trainers visit wasn't all bad news. He still had some contacts in a federation over in the USA and he pulled some strings and his trainer offered Red Rock to be one of his students to go over there and wrestle to real crowd. This brought a face to Red Rock's bruised and cut up face. So after some weeks Red Rock recovered from his attack and got back on the horse with all the enthusiasm and heart in the world. He trained and trained until the day where he would leave for the USA came. He had made it in to the USA wrestling circuit a place where he would be seen and a chance to prove himself and to this day is still fucking stuff up making a dick out of himself with the help of his ever loyal side kick Boolie! ...Boolie later ditched Red Rock and the vacancy for whacky sidekick was handed down to sex pest and reknowned ginger, D'avid Bulrat. Typical Red Rock Style moves: Boot Scrape - The victim is on their back. The attacker puts their foot on the victim's face. The attacker puts more weight on the victim's face and they spin their body around on the foot which is on the victim's Eye Rake, Double Boot Scrape - The victim is on their back on the mat. The attacker jumps up and puts both boots over the victim's eyes and scrapes them with the sole of their feet as the attacker's feet slide to the mat. Low Blow - Any assortment of blows to the groin, of which the male writer of this list wishes not to have to explain. Playing Possom: Red Rock lies on the floor pretend he is injured or knocked out, then when the opponent comes close we will deliver, a thumb to the eye a low blow or tackle them to the ground and attack. Standard Moves: armbar submission - Opponent face-down, grab a single arm and wrench back on it. bulldog headlock - slap the opponent in a headlock and run forward, dropping down and slamming their face into the mat. dragon screw - known also as a legdrag, leg whip, etc. Holding opponent's foot, fall down with a sideways-sorta twist, using your free arm to wrench them down by their knee dragon sleeper - hook on an upside-down facelock (reverse/inverted DDT setup), reaching under the opponent's far arm. The attacker lifts up one of the victim's arms straight up in a wrist lock. The attacker then punches the victim in the chest. Heart Kick - The attacker is facing the victim. The attacker lifts up one of the victim's arms straight up in a wrist lock. The attacker then kicks the victim in the chest. Head Butt - a british sytle nutting someone a savage headbutt to the face Body Press - The attacker jumps at victim and hits the victim at an angle, knocking them down to the mat. Drop Kick - The attacker jumps in the air and kicks the victim with both their feet. The attack can be focuses on the victim's head, chest, stomach, back or legs. Flying Head Scissors - The attacker jumps in the air catching the victim's head/neck between their legs and sealing the lock by crossing their ankles. The attacker falls the the mat, they pull down the victim to the mat. Forearm Smash - The attacker strikes the victim with their forearm. Can be a clubbing overhand forearm or straight forearm jab. Kick, Back - The attacker is facing the victim, the attack spins 180 degrees and strikes the victim in the stomach with the sole of their foot. Kick, Spinning Roundhouse - The attacker performs a 360 degree turn and kicks the victim with the top of their foot.
