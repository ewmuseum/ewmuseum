Zero To Hero 2005, 6 May 2005

6 May 2005

Thousands of fans scream their lungs out in collective excitement as AWC Zero To Hero comes on air. The arena is full to the brim with people, who are extremely lively, the waiting for AWC finally over. Several fans hold up signs such as "AWC is the bomb!" and "Holding Out For A Hero”. The fans continue to cheer in anticipation as we pan down to our commentators, Truth Waters and George Cassidy. Waters appears the more eager of the two to see this event begin. Cassidy sports a sullen frown, reflecting his personality. Truth Waters: We're here! The world is watching, or we would hope they are, as Atlantic Wrestling Club gets off the ground tonight! Personally, I cannot wait to see what's gonna happen tonight! Potentially, we could be in for anything. George Cassidy: One thing is for sure, Truth; we will crown a Transatlantic champion tonight after this long and arduous singles tournament ends. Truth Waters: For those participating, it'll be long and arduous; for us, it'll be enjoyable. Lighten up a bit, Cassidy... George Cassidy: Look, don't even waste your time. Let's just get started with this: I don't like you, and you don't like me. Can we agree on that? Truth Waters: I'll get back to you on that one. Send me an e-mail or something. My address is Truth dot Waters at... Oh wait, I’d better not give this out on live television! George Cassidy: And that's why you'll do what I say as long as we're working together, Truth. I think we're about to start. In the ring, announcer James Brunt stands. Dressed in a stylish yet unorthodox purple suit, Brunt holds the microphone to his lips, fully aware that each and every person in the arena is focusing their attention on him. Brunt grins, ready to do what he loves. James Brunt: Ladies and gentlemen, you are all very welcome to Atlantic Wrestling Club's début event, ZERO TO HERO! The crowd erupt into a flurry of cheers, random chants for Brunt and applause. Brunt reacts with another smile, as he flattens out his purple jacket, part of his unusual ensemble. James Brunt: Now, it's time to begin the show. The fans are ecstatic upon hearing this news, what they have been waiting for. James Brunt: So, without further ado, please welcome AWC's Entertainment Manager... DAVID “PEARL” HARBER! The lights diverge on the stage, heralding the entrance of AWC's Entertainment Manager, David Harber, or "Pearl", as he prefers to be called. Pearl's face tells a story; he is absolutely overjoyed with the reaction from the fans, who are present in their thousands. "Bohemian Like You" by the Dandy Warhols plays in the background, as the fans try to slap Pearl's hands on his way down the ramp-way. It seems Pearl is intentionally moving slowly down the ramp, taking in the overwhelming reaction as if it were his last moment on Earth. After a few moments, Pearl reaches the ring, heading up the steps and entering the ring to a rousing applause from the fans. Brunt hands his boss the mic and leaves the ring, leaving Pearl alone. Pearl tampers with the microphone, teasing the fans that he is about to speak. Pearl: Good evening, Tampa! A huge pop from the crowd on the mention of their hometown. The reaction is frightening, in fact. Pearl smiles in appreciation as he strolls up and down the ring. Pearl: Atlantic Wrestling Club has officially begun, ladies and gentlemen. Tonight – yes, tonight – Tampa, Florida will witness the very first show of AWC live and in person! Again, the fans come to their feet and pop for Pearl's words. Pearl: Now, as the Entertainment Manager, I will be responsible for your entertainment! I promise to bring the best action, the best superstars, the best matches; the best of everything rolled up in a bundle. And the best part is, it's all for you fans! Truth Waters: Man, Pearl has these fans on their feet! Just listen to them chanting his name already! George Cassidy: Typical... Indeed, the fans are chanting "PEARL! PEARL! PEARL!", all of them as rowdy as before. Harber does a three-sixty degree turn in the ring, once more embracing the atmosphere in the arena. However, judging from his demeanour, he is not quite done yet. Pearl: It all begins tonight with our Zero To Hero tournament, pitting eight of the best wrestlers in the world against each other in singles competition, with the winner becoming our first Transatlantic champion. The person who holds this championship will be top dog and deservedly so after you witness what is in store for these athletes tonight. We're looking at a simple singles tournament, ladies and gentlemen, but along the way, you'll be wowed, shocked, awestruck and most of all, entertained! At the end of the day, you see, I stand for your best interests. I believe you like to be entertained, am I right?! The fans respond with an emphatic "yes", if the massive pop is anything to go by. Pearl: Good. Very good. You don't realise how truly proud I am to be standing here in this ring in front of you all, only minutes away from Zero To Hero, an event that will justify the hype! Trust me on that one. I only have one more thing to say, fans, and that is the following: the future looks bright for the AWC. Let's make sure we make it happen! So come out of your seats, be loud and be proud, and enjoy the show! Pearl pauses momentarily, basking in the adoration from the fans. The reaction he is getting is absolutely unbelievable. Pearl: You're in for one hell of a ride! Pearl raises his arms into the air, an action which only makes the fans cheer even more. The noise level in the arena is out of the control as Pearl hands the microphone back to James Brunt and takes his leave. His confident stride up the ramp says it all – he couldn't be more proud of how his brainchild, AWC, has begun. Pearl gives one last wave to the fans, who applaud him. Truth Waters: Ready to kick this show off, Cassidy? George Cassidy: Save your enthusiasm, Truth. I expect big things from this show, so I'll be hoping it delivers. Now, the shot changes to that of a training gym, where AWC’s contracted wrestlers have engaged in preparatory exercises for the past few weeks under the guidance of esteemed head trainer Six Knots. Most faces are as yet unknown to us, but the former Neon duo The Old School Connection of Zack Johnson and Joshua Curtis is visible, as is the hardcore veteran Pact. The ring now fills the screen again – it is now fully set up, with the AWC apron draped in place. The arena moves as if on fast-forward – a feed from just minutes ago as people milled into the Sun Dome to find their seats shows a buzz of activity as little blips otherwise known as people zip around the auditorium at twenty times the normal speed. But in the ring, all is taking place in real time, as the eight contestants in tonight’s Zero To Hero stand straight, positioned systematically about the ring, looking out at what must have been an empty arena when this portion was taped. The camera now slowly pans around the ring, affording us a good look at each wrestler, and subtitles fade into view telling us the name of each wrestler. They are: Pact, Alexander Strider, Danny Polar, Hate, Christopher Ashton, Kris Krimzon, Sektor and Pierce Lavelle. We now commence zooming slowly away, and the word ‘ZERO’ practically obliterates the ring from view. ‘TO’ fades into place over the top as ‘ZERO’ neatly disappears, and then the word ‘HERO’ floats onto the screen just below it. The scene fades to black. Snarling, Alex Strider now grabs hold of the steel chair he brought into the ring a while ago. Instantly, the crowd is abuzz. Truth Waters: AND THAT IS AN EVEN WORSE IDEA! George Cassidy: The lack of respect displayed by our employees tonight is simply disgusting. Truth Waters: Rest assured, he will pay for that! The booing of the crowd turns to whistling and cheering as they see the tall, strong announcer wanting to get involved. Michael Ryan has now turned away and slumped to the mat as Strider advances and CRACKS THE CHAIR OVER THE SKULL OF PIERCE LAVELLE! George Cassidy: GOD ALMIGHTY! What a shot! Truth, get back here! George Cassidy: STRIDER JUST GOT A MOUTHFUL OF STEEL! Truth tosses the chair nonchalantly to ringside, shrugging off the heavy praise of the fans as he slides out of the ring and returns to his seat. George Cassidy: What the hell – Truth Waters: You’re welcome. Now shut the hell up and let’s call the action. But there is in fact no action to call, as all three men in the ring are laid out: Lavelle having taken a colossal crack across the head with the flat of the chair; Strider having taken most of the force of a shot he attempted to block with his arms; Ryan having been thrown to the mat by Alex Strider. George Cassidy: This main event has degenerated into a mess! No competitors... no referee! Truth Waters: And really both competitors should have now been disqualified... Strider for the chair shot – George Cassidy: And spitting at Michael Ryan! Truth Waters: And the spitting, yeah. And Lavelle because I came in and did his work for him! After ten seconds or so, Michael Ryan finally rises, not really hurt, but shocked more than anything at the audacity of “The Mongoose” Alexander Strider. Taking his bearings, he notes that both wrestlers are now fallen, and starts to belatedly count them out. ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Lavelle brings his head off the mat for a second, and a camera close-up reveals that it is now streaked with blood from a wound to the eyebrow. Truth Waters: Oh, that does not look good. He’ll need that seen to. George Cassidy: Strider’s chair shot has caused a nasty cut to Pierce Lavelle’s eyebrow. Truth Waters: No, the chair pierced it. Besides, how could a cut pierce something? George Cassidy: Oh dear, it seems the match writer has gone mad. Truth Waters: Yep. Bringing terrible circumstantial jokes that you would use at school into a match for a pretend wrestling promotion isn’t really the best of ideas. FIVE! SIX! Strider and Lavelle both now begin to crawl to opposite corners of the ring. SEVEN! George Cassidy: Good, it looks as if this match will be continuing. Truth Waters: You mean you’re enjoying it? You’re actually enjoying a match? George Cassidy: I – uh – it’s not bad... EIGHT! Strider now straightens up fully. He glances warily at Lavelle, who himself recovers to a vertical position in the opposite corner and meets his eye. Dazed, both men assume fighting stances and begin to advance slowly on each other. Truth Waters: It’s just like the beginning all over again... George Cassidy: Except this time Lavelle has blood streaming down his face. Truth Waters: Yes, that is true. Hesitance goes out of the window as the two combatants collide in a ferocious grapple, Lavelle winning out and twisting Alex Strider’s right arm around. Strider bends as far forward as possible to ease the strain. This leaves his side exposed, and Lavelle exploits this with a quick shot of the left-elbow into his ribs. Strider staggers away and falls to his hands and knees. Lavelle moves towards him, but Strider suddenly shoots his arm up between Lavelle’s legs. Truth Waters: REGIONS~! shot! George Cassidy: He sure knows how to switch a match around, does “The Mongoose”. Lavelle’s eyes water as he turns away, clutching at his vulnerable parts. Strider comes behind him and runs him into the ropes, following him in and springing backwards as Lavelle does the same, the result being that Strider rolls Lavelle into a pinning predicament. George Cassidy: Agile move by Alex Strider... and we could see a near fall here. ONE! TWO! Lavelle kicks out with half a second to spare. Truth Waters: He’s not quite there yet. Strider just needs one big move... then the title belt’s his. Not that he deserves it... George Cassidy: Disrespectful scumbag. Unfazed, Strider gets up quickly, taking Pierce Lavelle with him, and sends him into the ropes. Strider bends down for a back body drop, his arms ready to latch onto Lavelle’s sides to give him extra power, but Lavelle sees it earlier and leaps him athletically, flipping over and locking arms with Strider on the way. As he lands, the momentum pulls Alex Strider up onto Lavelle’s back in the crucifix position. Truth Waters: What a move! I’ve never seen such an innovative counter to a back body drop... George Cassidy: I’m not sure how much of it was intended; can he even make something of it? Lavelle staggers back a couple of steps, but he knows what he has to do, and do it he does, launching Strider forward over his head with a huge burst of energy to land the crucifix powerbomb! Truth Waters: CRUCIFIX POWERBOMB! George Cassidy: Now that is one to knock the breath right out of you. Lavelle could have him here! Finishing in the sitting position, with one of Strider’s legs either side of his head, Pierce Lavelle is already pinning his adversary, a situation which the referee notices at once. ONE! TWO! Strider kicks out! Truth Waters: THR – TWO! Strider kicked out at THE last possible millisecond! I really thought Pierce had him! George Cassidy: Lavelle has been taking punishment for much of this match, but with one big move like that it was more than conceivable that he could have captured the Transatlantic championship right there. Lavelle sits up, somewhat annoyed that his big move has not afforded him the victory. Truth Waters: Pierce Lavelle’s face is still streaked with blood. George Cassidy: Luckily the worst of the bleeding seems to have stopped. He gets to his feet and drops immediately into a bulldog on the rising Strider; Lavelle latches comfortably onto his opponent’s head to bring him back to the mat. George Cassidy: Bulldog by Lavelle, continuing the offence. He stands Strider upright and looks out at the packed arena before laying into Strider with a series of right hands, sending “The Mongoose” progressively backwards. Truth Waters: He’s backed him up against the ropes with some tough right hands. Lavelle now backs away before running at Strider, but Strider leans back using the top rope to help him and brings a leg up into Lavelle’s face. Lavelle groans and turns away with the stiff impact to his cheekbone; it would have served him better to remain standing straight as Strider comes round to his front to lock his arms up above his head and launch him into a double-underhook suplex! George Cassidy: Underhook suplex! Alex Strider takes the fight back to Lavelle. Truth Waters: The marginal cruiserweight did well to lift the heavier man there. Double-underhook lifts are tough, you know. Alexander Strider hooks the leg somewhat belatedly. Ryan nevertheless makes the count swiftly: ONE! TWO! Lavelle kicks out easily on the second count. Truth Waters: Lavelle’s little spell on top has rejuvenated him. Simple kickout. George Cassidy: Alex Strider has got to return to the strategy that was working so well for him earlier in the match. Lavelle has a hot head... so outwit him! Truth Waters: It isn’t quite that simple Cassidy. Lavelle stands shakily, and Strider takes advantage of his guard being down to nip in and execute a quick side backbreaker. Pierce Lavelle falls to the mat again, his hand shooting to his aching spine, but Strider shows no mercy and continues the attack with an elbow drop. Unwilling to take further damage, Lavelle rolls away to the apron. Strider backs off with a grin. Truth Waters: Lavelle goes out to the apron to recuperate. George Cassidy: I’m not sure that’s the wisest move. Truth Waters: Indeed, Alex Strider looks happy as hell about it! Lavelle stands up on the apron, but before he can make any move to re-enter the ring Strider is on him with a hard right hand to the face followed by a boot thrust into his mid-section. In a flash, Strider slides outside the ring and nips up on the apron alongside Pierce Lavelle. Truth Waters: Strider gets on the apron next to Lavelle... what could he be up to here? George Cassidy: I think I know... OH! The crowd reacts with a synchronised ‘OOH!’ as Strider sacrifices his own body in order to dump Lavelle down onto the matting at ringside with a powerful back suplex before his opponent has even registered Strider’s presence alongside him. Truth Waters: Back suplex! And Lavelle was in dreamland! George Cassidy: He had no idea of his bearings there. He probably doesn’t know a hell of a lot more about them now... ONE! TWO! George Cassidy: Both men are down outside the ring here. Truth Waters: Normally at this point, you’d hear the fans chanting for their favourites, but the reality is they haven’t taken a shine to either of these guys. George Cassidy: Strider was going the right way about winning their respect – that is, until he spit in Michael Ryan’s face... Truth Waters: Whatever punishment AWC gives him for that, the AWC fans hating him is punishment enough for Strider. He will live to regret that move. THREE! FOUR! Strider now stands unsteadily, and makes his way over to the announcers’ table. Truth Waters: You know, if he wants to level things with me after what happened earlier, I’m all for it... George Cassidy: No! Sit down. FIVE! SIX! Truth Waters: Fine... Strider doesn’t seem to have any interest in Truth Waters, however. He in fact heads for ring announcer James Brunt, who is resplendent in his neatly-pressed purple suit. Breathlessly, Strider motions for Brunt to hand him the Transatlantic title belt which he is currently cradling on his lap. SEVEN! George Cassidy: Strider wants the belt! Truth Waters: No! Don’t give it to him, Brunt... George Cassidy: Too late. Strider grabs the belt roughly from the unresisting hands of James Brunt and turns towards Lavelle – who is already up and rushes at him with a punishing clothesline! Truth Waters: Clothesline! Alex Strider goes down like a sack of potatoes. George Cassidy: His body jack-knifed in mid-air, such was the power of that clothesline! EIGHT! Lavelle now rolls back into the ring and stands, awaiting Alexander Strider’s recovery. NINE! Truth Waters: He’d better make it quick... Just in time, Strider staggers to his feet and rolls into the ring, disoriented. Pierce Lavelle meets him with a number of kicks to the upper back, not allowing Strider to get up. Lavelle holds the top rope to assist his search for more power, but Michael Ryan spots this quickly and makes Lavelle break it off. George Cassidy: Lavelle was using the ropes to get some better power there. Truth Waters: Mike Ryan did well to spot that so fast. That’s why he’s Senior Referee. Annoyed, Lavelle still backs off, and Strider stands before rushing at Lavelle with an attempted takedown of some sort. Having none of it, Lavelle stands his ground and performs a double axe-handle strike to the back of Strider’s head, knocking him flat. Pierce Lavelle runs to the ropes and comes back, expecting Strider to still be down, but “The Mongoose” has risen quickly and looks for some kind of lift or back drop. Reacting quickly, Lavelle channels his momentum and adjusts his body position into a running DDT! Truth Waters: DDT! George Cassidy: Great shift of momentum by Lavelle to avoid that back body drop. He wasn’t aware that Strider would be ready for him, but those were some great reflexes. ONE! TWO! Strider kicks out just in time. Truth Waters: Just a two count. The match continues. No champ just yet. George Cassidy: So who’s your pick, Truth? Truth Waters: Hmm, it’s tough. But I’ll say Lavelle... he hasn’t offended me quite so much. George Cassidy: I suppose you aren’t quite the impartial observer anymore after you gave Alex Strider that chair shot! Lavelle guides Strider back up, and with a grip on “The Mongoose”’s curly hair, brings him over to the turnbuckle. He slams his head face-first against the top turnbuckle, and now pushes Strider front-first into the corner before hoisting him up onto the top, so he is in a sitting position staring out at the crowd. Truth Waters: Lavelle’s setting Strider up on top. George Cassidy: And backwards, too... I feel a high-impact move coming on. Lavelle now mounts the ropes himself, and, standing on the second turnbuckle, locks his arms around Strider’s waist. Sure of a firm grasp, he launches himself backwards, swinging Alexander Strider’s lighter body overhead in a devastating German superplex! Truth Waters: German suplex! George Cassidy: The belly-to-back from the top! Sweet heavens... Truth Waters: Strider landed high on his back and neck... that could really have done some damage. George Cassidy: It looks like that’s taken its toll on Lavelle, too... Strider lies prone, dead-centre, having been released on impact by Pierce Lavelle, who now crawls desperately across the match towards his opponent. Stretching out an arm, he covers his body. ONE! Truth Waters: Lavelle wasted some time there... TWO! Kickout by Strider! Truth Waters: Yeah, and as I thought, Strider got out of it. George Cassidy: Only just, though. This match is on its last legs. Truth Waters: As is Alexander Strider. George Cassidy: As is Pierce Lavelle, even. Truth Waters: Fair point. These two men have been through so much tonight – and it isn’t just this gruelling contest they’ve had to see out. Both of them are currently competing for the third time tonight! The stamina they’ve both shown is frankly astonishing. George Cassidy: There is nothing Lavelle and Strider want more than a hot shower and bed right now. Truth Waters: How about the Transatlantic title? George Cassidy: Alright, maybe one thing. The two warriors up, they begin to trade epic blows in the centre of the ring. Respecting the ability of these two men to keep going, the crowd pop loudly. Truth Waters: They’re just slugging it out now. That’s what I like to see. A good old brawl. George Cassidy: They just haven’t got the energy to innovate anymore. One blow from Alex Strider now sends Lavelle sprawling. He is up at once, but the moment of freedom was enough for Strider to catapult himself off the ring ropes and now he uses that momentum to launch his tired body into the air, lacing his legs around the head of Pierce Lavelle for an incredible rendition of the flying head scissors! Truth Waters: Flying head scissors! My, that came out of nowhere! George Cassidy: Alex Strider calling on every last drop of energy left in his body now! What an astounding achievement... to be able to come out and do that after three tough matches of constant wrestling action. The exhausted “Mongoose” now throws himself onto Pierce Lavelle for the pin, hardly bothering to hook the leg. The crowd counts with Michael Ryan, confident that this epic match is finally over. ONE! TWO! Truth Waters: NO! Lavelle kicked out! George Cassidy: How on earth did he manage that? Truth Waters: And, ladies and gentlemen, the show must go on! Stunned, Alex Strider shoves the rising Lavelle back to the mat and makes the cover again, this time using proper technique. Ryan shrugs and makes the count for the second time. Truth Waters: He’s hooking the leg this time. He’s got him. ONE! TWO! George Cassidy: It’s another kickout! Strider has lost his chance! Truth Waters: Lavelle kicked out with just a fraction of a second to spare! George Cassidy: I’m tearing my hair out here! Truth Waters: Well, what’s left of it. Lavelle again tries to stand, and the exhausted Strider can now offer no resistance. Lavelle doggedly ties up with his adversary, now moving round behind him and executing a slow inverted suplex. Truth Waters: Reverse suplex by Lavelle. Textbook execution. George Cassidy: True. Strider’s body rotated about his head like... like a pendulum. Truth nods vigorously. Meanwhile, Lavelle has rolled Strider over from his grounded position and pulled the far leg over to approximate to a rough cover. ONE! TWO! Strider kicks out. George Cassidy: Two count. It’s clearly going to take a superhuman effort to win this match. Truth Waters: Every extra second these men go on fighting for just adds to the plaudits the winner of this match will get. Truth Waters: These two men have come so far together in this match, but is Lavelle going to be able to hold on? James Brunt: The winner... and new AWC Transatlantic champion... “The Mongoose” ALEX STRIDER! Truth Waters: All four men sharing words inside the ring now. I hope this situation ends quickly, because this could get very ugly if cool heads don’t prevail. George Cassidy: We’ll see you on Tuesday for the inaugural Fresh!... that is, if the police haven’t shut us down for good by then...
