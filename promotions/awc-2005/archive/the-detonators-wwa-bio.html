The Detonators Biography Biography The Detonators have the advantage of having spent nearly every minute of the last 3 years together so they know each other extremely well and it shows with quick tags and an extremely trusting attitude. They never sell even a hint of being upset with each other while in the ring and their masks obscure any facial gestures that may be made in private. They often use hand or head signals to alert the other member as to what they plan to attempt. TNT generally starts off matches for The Detonators and then they go to a style of tagging in and out to match the body type of the opponent - TNT for a more muscular opponent and Dynamite for lighter weight foes. With their masked appearance and identical ring attire they have mastered the no-tag swap out and use it whenever it is advantageous to do so. Move Set SIGNATURE MOVES Time Bomb: TNT lifts opponent up into a powerbomb position and slams him down to Dynamite who catches him in a shoulder neckbreaker. The timing of the move needs to be just exactly perfect and the Dets make it look silky. Instruments of Destruction: Dynamite hits a superkick to the chin of an opponent that TNT holds in a belly-to-back suplex position. TNT uses the momentum from the kick to bridge back into a bridging German suplex and pinfall attempt.
