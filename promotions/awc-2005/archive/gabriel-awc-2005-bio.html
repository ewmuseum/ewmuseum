Gabriel Biography Real Name Gabriel Nicknames 'The Angel of Death' Hometown Brooklynn New York Birthday 03/29/78 Height 6'1 Weight 235 Biography As a child his parents believed the devil was inside them. They tortured him in many cruel ways to beat the devil from him...to no avail. After 3 long years of pain and punishment he was put in a juvenile detention centre for the murder of his parents. Here he suffered a new kind of torture...as the darkess of his soul overcame him sending him into a state of pyschological illness. He was released as teenager and taken in by a wrestling family who where good enough to foster him. After a few months they couldnt bare any more of his mental problems and filed to have him institianalised but he ran away and came across the wrestling business. A place where he can unleash his deamons and fury.
