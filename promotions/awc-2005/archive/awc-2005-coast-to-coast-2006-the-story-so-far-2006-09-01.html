Coast To Coast 2006, The Story So Far

1 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Story So Far On 29th November 2005 in Manchester, England, AWC changed forever. After a series of undesirable incidents, including the tragic death of first Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider, and Tony Aliso smuggling a bomb into a Fresh! broadcast in the very case that Pearl was due to present to Triangles mystery briefcase winner Tim Shipley, David Harber's grip on his job as AWC Entertainment Manager was looking shaky. A mysterious elegant Russian blonde was repeatedly meeting with majority shareholder Sam Newman with no explanation given to the fans, the employees, the Entertainment Manager. Then that night in Manchester, Sasha Volkyeva told us "Atlantic Wrestling Club is in crisis!" Without "Mother Russia", who knows where AWC was going? To scale the highest peak, or plunge to the bed of its own ocean, disappearing without a trace? Her regime was imposed at once. Safer rings. High security fences. Movement of the commentary desk. Extra vigilance. Five-second count-outs. AWC was losing its identity. Working together was out of the question. Harber and Volkyeva were at odds right from the moment Pearl had his new colleague "Get the hell out of here!" Frostiness prevailed until the AWC Christmas Party, when, attired in a spectacular burgundy dress, Sasha revealed that there was indeed a person beneath that cold exterior, jovially offering Harber a "Pretzel?" But Pearl freakin' hated pretzels. He ate one anyway. Sasha threw herself into an intricate web of half-baked dalliances with the big men of AWC; Jack Murphy, Chainz and Anton Assault have all served their time as lackeys of Mother Russia. Meanwhile, Harber reasserted himself with increasing confidence over the real meat and potatoes of AWC; Volkyeva's lack of expertise was becoming painfully obvious and Pearl assumed more and more responsibilities until Sasha's authority existed nowhere but on paper. Then on 12th May 2006, AWC changed forever... again. Dr. Kasidy Drake, inextricably tied up in AWC's past as the first known user of the Prometheus Serum (abused by The Educator and Alex Strider; used to frame Pierce Lavelle), bought a huge amount of stock in the Club and became Chief Financial Advisor. While his influence has largely been off-screen, it was Drake who announced that AWC would cut back down to just one Entertainment Manager - putting both Harber and Volkyeva in sudden jeopardy. The roster divide was designed so that Drake could separately assess the influence and ability of both; AWC-West and AWC-East were formed, and for the first time there was a genuine structure to AWC's cross-Atlantic organisation. Now we come to Coast To Coast - the biggest event of the calendar, at which both rosters will meet, bringing concurrent live action from at least three confirmed locations - Wembley Arena, London; Continental Airlines Arena, East Rutherford; and the good ship Jewel Of The Seas, midway across the Atlantic. The build-up has seen its good times and bad, and there's no question that Sasha Volkyeva's position is by far the weaker of the two Entertainment Managers going into the event at which one will finally be selected, but anything could happen at Coast To Coast. And knowing AWC, anything will happen.
