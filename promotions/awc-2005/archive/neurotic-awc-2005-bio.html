Neurotic Biography Real Name Jamie Paige Hometown Hazard, Ky Birthday 08--11-1980 Height 6'4 Weight 265 Biography Jamie was born and raised in Hazard, Kentucky. He lived with both parents and a younger brother until the age of 18 when he decided it was more then time for him to settle into his own place. Growing up all he could dream of was leaving Hazard. Going to a big city, and hitting the big times, it didn't really matter which. But all that changed. Upon moving in to his own place, Jamie found the workforce in Hazard was gradually declining. Unless you wanted to be a coal miner you were stuck pumping gas for an old lady or slaping a hamburger on a bun for some snot nosed punk. jamie grew to resent people as he refused to die of 'Black Lung' by becoming a coal miner. He became distant, depressed, and anti-social. Everyday, he would roll out of bed, and search for a new job. Jamie had in fact conformed, and began slinging burgers. While at work one day, his high school crush had come into the fast food joint where he was doing his time. He noticed her and immediately headed for the back, but it was no use. The manager had this to do, had that to do, and neded Jamie to man the counter. Knowing he had to pay his rent, he did so with much reluctance. Upon seeing Jamie his high school crush, waved and smiled slightly as she placed her order. Could it be that people didn't really look down upon those in the fast food industry as jamie had thought? As Jamie had done himself. He hands her the order she had placed, and as she leaves she turns around and looks at him one last time before saying these few words, he will never forget... 'Get a real job you loser.' This crushed Jamie. It crushed any compassion he would ever feel for anyone, crushed his pride, crushed his emotion. This day changed Jamie Paige forever. Jamie heads to the back takes off his work attire and tosses it in a heap by the time clock before walking ou the door. On his way home Jamie makes a stop by a friends house, to pick up some 'party pills' as he called them. When he arrived home that night, Jamie took the pills...all of them. He laid backin bed, fading into darkness knowing full well that his pain would now be over forever. Jamie was wrong. He was committed do to his failed suicide attempt. Failed in the worst way posible. His mom had come over to tell him some news of his father's promotion at work and opening up a spot there if he was interested. She found him unconscious, and immeiately dialed 911. Knowing thathe would just try again, mothers intuition I guess, and also knowing they could not be around him all the time, his parents decided to have him commited. Or abandon him, as Jamie saw it. Everyday in the hell hole he had known to become his new home, he would toss back random pills they would give him, and drink his little dixie cup of water to wash it back. But every chance he would get, Jamie would again, attempt to kill himself. To end his pain. Hanging, stabbing, pills, starvation, thirst, Jamie had tried it all. The world would not release it's grasp on him. Jamie finally felt that no matter what he tried, he could not die. That he was almost invincible. He owuld test this theory by cutting himself, with sharp rocks, scissors left lying around, broken glass, anything he could find. Just to see how long it took to heal. His doctors labled him 'Neurotic', which just seemed to stick. After convincing his doctors time and time again, over a matter of several years, that he did in fact enjoy hurting himself, he was no longer obsessed with trying to take his life, they finally had no reason to hold him any longer. He was not insane, and they could not prove it. He had simply become a glutton for pain. Jamie was released. Upon his release, Jamie noticed a flyer for tryouts in a small independent wrestling fed. He decided, this may be for him, with his tolerance and pleasure in pain. Jamie went. At the tryouts, he was spotted by a wrestler in AWC who saw what he had to offer, and the pain he could endure, without so much as grimacing and was encouraged to submit a resume to AWC for an opening card slot. Meet Jamie Paige....aka Neurotic.
