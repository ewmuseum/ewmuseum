Sage Biography Real Name Cori [name withheld] Hometown Salem, MA Height 5'8' Weight 180 Biography Story: Cori was a regular teenager full of angst. The common suburbia case… A regular house and family, yet Cori, the middle child, displayed the popular depression and sadness of teens today. In her attempts to save herself from what her family felt was no biggie, she became interested in magic. In her attempts to learn about the ancient Celts she acquired a mentor, Norm, owner of the local bookstore where Cori bought her materials. As her interests in ancient religions flourished she began to learn a simple form of martial arts related to Tai Chi. These studies taught her to use her enemies’ energy against them to reverse attacks. As she became dedicated to Paganism she participated in a Seeker ceremony that required a renaming ritual. With her new name, Sage, her mentor Norm, and her new fighting skills she wishes to proceed into competition. Appearance: Sage is 5’8” and has the build of a slender male. Muscle definition is evident but not dominating, think teenage male model. She has the ripples of abs and defined arms and her shoulders are broad. Her haircut is short in the back and on the sides, but the top is grown out a bit. Just slightly longer than you could spike it. A few pieces of her dark, black hair fall onto her face, as all her hair is shaken forward because of her haircut. Her blue-ish green eyes shine off her matte, pale skin. She usually wears a solid forest green t-shirt and jeans with a slate gray zip-up hoody. When competing she wears baggy black cargo pants with a tight sage green tank top. To the ring she wears a black mesh shirt over top her outfit. Additional Info: Sage is a character that’s cool and collected 95% of the time. She’s sometimes quiet and reserved and lets her actions speak, but most of the time she casually responds with sordid casual responses. She’s the type to warn you about something… but do it in such a cryptic way that you’re not actually sure what you should be concerned about. This mystic aura propels her into story lines under the assumption that she knows something that others may not, about an odd happening or person, or their history. Her cryptic knowledge is what keeps her mystique. Some fans aren’t sure if they can trust her, but some love her. She is truly neutral. Other Feds PCW, PWA Other Fed Awards 2 time PWA Graduate Wrestling Style Martial Arts/Technical/Lucha Libre Finisher Move The Offering Finisher Description After slightly stunning the opposition Sage will pull them towards the corner, run up the turnbuckles and out the rope a few steps then jump off while kicking her legs up on the persons shoulders and performing a hurancanrana. Finisher Setup Move see move list Other Moves Setup Move #1: Drawing Down the Moon Sage runs to the ropes jumps on them and propels herself into a moonsault on top of her victim. Setup Move #2:Invocation A double handed choke slam. Sage lifts up the victim holds them for a second while looking to the sky then throws them into the mat in front of her. Common Moves: Dropkick Round House Kick Body Slam DDT Suplex Common Holds: Half Boston Crab Full Boston Crab Sleeper Armbar However almost any martial arts based or technical moves are used.
