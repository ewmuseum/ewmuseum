Trent Marshall Biography Real Name Brent Allen Marshall Nicknames 'Mr. Marshall' Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday 02/27/76 Height 6'1' Weight 251 Biography Marshall was signed to an official AWC contract in early February of 2006. Match #1: Fresh (02/28/06) (Mr. Marshall vs. Neurotic) Marshall defeated Neurotic via tapout to Marshall Law. Match #2: Fresh (03/06/06) (Mr. Marshall vs. Mikey O'Reilly vs. Jason Ortiz) Marshall was pinned by Mikey O'Reilly in a Three Way Fury match Match #3: Fresh (03/13/06) (Mr. Marshall/Agent Dash vs. Garbage Bag Johnny/Sgt. Jacobs) Marshall was pinned by Garbage Bag Johnny.
