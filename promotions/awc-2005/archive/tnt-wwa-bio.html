TNT Biography Nicknames T Hometown Habilito Village, Veracruz, Mexico Height 6'2'' Weight 282 Biography TNT has a very murky background. His real name is still unknown even to his manager Jim Surge. Rumors have been trailing TNT that he worked the Mexican border for many years as a pollero or coyote, smuggling immigrants into the United States and that is the reason he has to remain as a masked wrestler with no identity. There are also some theories among the Detonators' rabid fan-club Los Bambitos that TNT isn't even Mexican, and that's why he sometimes makes a big show of displaying the Mexican flag. Jim Surge 'discovered' TNT in a literal barroom brawl where TNT just beat the heck out of a couple of young hecklers making fun of his green mask. TNT didn't reveal his real name but instead told Surge his initials were 'TNT, like dynamite.' TNT told Surge he had recently been competing in some independent Mexican wrestling federations and would love the chance to wrestle in the United States. Surge took TNT on and flew him to England where the cowboy assisted his former teachers by helping teach TNT the very basics of the classical Cumberland and Westmoreland styles and a thorough review of the classic King's Road style. Opponents always seem surprised to see a masked Mexican break out the King's Road style, even when they know it's coming. Armed with his new training, TNT & Surge returned to Mexico to find the perfect partner for TNT and they did so in a luchadore league - another masked wrestler with the appropriate moniker of 'Dynamite.' Los Bambitos are the fanatical band of 30 traveling supporters in 6 Volkswagen busses that follow the Detonators everywhere they go. While TNT generally tries to avoid them outside the ring, Dynamite is friendly with several of them and always provides Los Bambitos with plenty of cash so that the nutjobs can buy up a majority of ringside seats. This is so that whenever an opponent is thrown to the outside of the ring they can interfere on behalf of the Detonators as de facto lumberjacks. Manager Jim Surge is a rancher and ex-wrestler and native of Tombstone, Arizona, USA. Before he retired Surge was one-half of Redemption Song, a cowboy and Indian team that were undefeated CHZ tag-team champions which eventually landed them a contract with PCW. After Surge's half-brother Ben Nighthorse Campbell retired due to injury, an uninspired Surge returned to his Arizona ranch to contemplate his future. With a lot of resources at his disposal due to his successful US cattle business but with himself woefully out of shape, Surge decided the quickest way back into wrestling for him would be as a manager, especially manager of a tag-team given his experience as part of a successful team. But Surge keeps a distant eye on a return to action as a singles competitor, actually joining TNT & Dynamite in their weight room sessions from time to time lately. General Appearance Physical Appearance: TNT is fairly tall but he's certainly as thick as a tank, physically speaking. A powerful but not hyper-defined body helps him with his quickness and agility in the ring. His actual facial appearance is unknown since he has never been seen without his wrestling mask on. Out of the ring he wears his mask (sometimes with a Mexican flag bandana around the mask's forehead), jeans, a white t-shirt, black leather jacket and black biker boots. Ring Attire: Always wearing his dark green luchadore-style wrestling mask (even to shower as was established during his WWA tryout). Wears black, loose-fitting karate-style full-leg pants with the word 'Detonators' running vertically down, stitched into the outside of both legs. Always shined black wrestling boots with black laces. From time to time TNT puts a small Mexican flag emblem on his boots but if he does, Dynamite does the same thing to his boots. Additional Information Despite the obvious weight difference, the Detonators have mastered the art of no-tag swap out when in trouble. This is because whenever either of the Detonators seem badly beaten and they may need to pull a swap they attempt to get on their sides in the fetal position with their back to the ref. With this constantly-obscured view it is impossible to tell a swap has occured until it is too late. Manager Jim Surge usually gets involved distracting the ref or the opponent during swap outs. From time to time Los Bambitos will do something to cover an illegal swap out. Personality: Despite being the powerhouse, or perhaps because of it, TNT is the more laid-back member of the Detonators (if such a thing is possible with a team named after a device which causes explosions). It is more likely the product of his hometown of Veracruz, a much less hectic place than Dynamite's hometown of Mexico City. He also feels very fortunate to be teamed up with manager Jim Surge who has already added a classic King's Road style to the Mexican's natural powerhouse ability. Is the member of the team that never seems to lose his head despite the enormity or unexpectedness of a situation. TNT is not preachy, though, so don't expect any of his cool nature to rub off on Dynamite.
