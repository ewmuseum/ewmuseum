Garbage Bag Johnny is the white adopted child of two black parents. 2. Garbage Bag Johnny's alignment is now face because although he behaves in typical heel fashion, a large portion of the crowd regards him as a cult superstar because his name is fucking Garbage Bag Johnny, and he has the best entrance music in the AWC. Furthermore, he's kicking ass and taking names all over the place while half the time he's too fucked up to even know where he is and what he's doing. Bottom line is, GBJ is the fucking man. 3. Garbage Bag Johnny was declared legally dead in an Indiana hospital one time. Ever since, he has had an avid distaste of the state of Indiana, and only crosses the border to buy fireworks or cigarettes. 4. Garbage Bag Johnny spent a year riding the rails just because he felt like it. 5. If Garbage Bag Johnny could eat lunch with any person, dead or alive, he would eat lunch with Garbage Bag Johnny. Wrestling Style Aerial Brawler Finisher Move The Tragically Hipbuster Finisher Description Fireman's Carry into Falling Forward Sideways DDT Setup Description The Tragically Hipbuster can come out of anywhere and everywhere Other Moves ENTRANCE: Theme Music - 'Garbage Bag Johnny Will Win Zero 2 Hero' by Garbage Bag Johnny and Primus. Pyro - No explosions or flashing lights, but lots of smoke. Walk - A slow meander to the ring...usually Garbage Bag Johnny is on something, and his black lung really forces him to conserve his energy. FAN REACTION: Garbage Bag Johnny is a heel by behavior, but depending on his recent backstage alliances and feuds, he will get a strong reaction either negatively or positively from the crowd. GBJ can be almost 100 percent liked by the crowd, but he cannot be nearly unanimously hated because he has a lot of cult fans who like him no matter what he does, simply because they like the way he carries himself. CURRENT FAN REACTION: After winning the TA Title at Coast 2 Coast, GBJ has become a huge fan favorite- his alliance with Adam Dick broken, allowing him to emerge as the lovable tramp (in a Charlie Chaplin-esque way) whose off the wall antics entertain rather than infuriate. MATCH STRATEGY: Garbage Bag Johnny isn't the strongest, fastest, or most athletic guy in AWC, but what he lacks in physical ability, he makes up for in mental ability. Though he doesn't have much in terms of technical know-how, he knows how to fight and can handle punishment. He likes to use his dirty maneuvers to humiliate his opponent or to quickly turn the tides. Also, his wild temperment and bouts of paranoia sometimes bring GBJ into rampage mode. His ego will oftentimes bring him to take unnecessary risks in lieu of a flashier, more entertaining style. Also, due to his black lung, Garbage Bag Johnny will sometimes accept his opponent's attacks rather than exerting to counter, avoid, or escape them in order to use his energy more efficiently.
