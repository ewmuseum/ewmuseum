Zero 2 Hero Fan Festival Prelude A dusty, cold morning over the Manchester Evening News Arena. It’s pretty early, probably about half five in the morning. The surrounding area is quiet, but directly in and around the arena is chock full of activity. You see today is Thursday the 27th of April. Nothing too spectacular to the average person but then again, this event isn’t for the average person. It’s for the wrestling fan, the different breed who’s despised and mocked equally. Foul, loudmouthed, redneck idiots. That’s what you might call some of them but then again, an awful lot of them are far from it. Wrestling has come a long way since the old days when it was in bars and nightclubs to keep it going. No, now it’s the big time. Because in about four hours from now the doors to the arena are going to open and lay host to hordes of AWC fans as the Zero 2 Hero Fan Festival kicks off. The Entertainment Managers of AWC decided to really push this, their biggest show to date, to run a nationwide tour to rustle up hype and excitement for the pay-per-view event. The Fan Festival here today is the culmination of this. Tomorrow, is Zero 2 Hero. Why so early to start though? Well, there are a couple of notable people here already… A black polished Mercedes rolls up right outside the arena and stops softly. The tinted windows reveal nothing but it would be pretty hard regardless in the rising dawn. Despite this the door opens promptly and two figures exit from the car. A man and a woman. They playfully joke with one another and you can see them hugging, holding. Soft touches between them adds to this light atmosphere and you can they are at home with another. Finally some might say. The man speaks first, complimenting the woman and smiling gently. She responds in kind and they both blush. “You know…I’ve got a good feeling about this Sasha,” lilted David ‘Pearl’ Harber, feeling so free and alive for the first time in a long time. “Me too…don’t forget I still have a surprise for you David,” purred Sasha. “Really?” “Really. It is…a Siberian treat for you. One I hope you will be most pleased with.” Sasha smiled, not coldly at all but freshly. She too was looking and feeling better. “Well, if it’s from you…it’s bound to be good. Shall we?” He opened his palm towards the entrance and at the same time put his other hand towards hers. She looked at it and beamed. “But of course.” The two made their way into the arena by the door which was open. Workers passed by the two, some raising eyebrows, but most of them too busy getting things ready for the day. They only had a couple more hours as it stood. One man in particular though had been waiting for the two to appear. As he approached David and Sasha loosened their respective grips and straightened themselves up. “Yes John?” drawled Pearl who seemed less than enthused by the stocky, short man’s inevitable appearance. “David there are a couple of things which need your attention…both your attention.” John looked to the two entertainment managers and pulled out some documents for them to look at. “Now if you’ll see on page…” It was then that Sasha cut him off and took the papers, stuffing them back in the briefcase with which they were produced from. She looked at him and pressed a finger to her lips. “Sssh.” The only word, if you could call it that, that Sasha uttered. Without another she moved onwards into the arena, gazing at all the different aspects which would be soon open to the public. Her eyes in particular locked on the Zero 2 Hero stage which would be host to the new and old superstars tomorrow night. Meanwhile Harber looked at John and shrugged before giving him his own papers and telling John later. “But! The Security!” “Bruno will take care of it John,” drawled Pearl once more before he disappeared. “But…he’s sick…” John replied as he trailed off and sighed. The crowd is baying at the outburst from the self proclaimed King of AWC. Some of the more important onlookers (such as the entertainment managers Sasha Volkyeva and Pearl Harber) are looking nervously at the situation as now more than ever the complete understaffing of sceurity for the event is coming to full bear. There isn't a single fagot on the roster who can deal with the shit-storm we will put them in if they even try to oppose us. Adam Dick: That's right. You see this crown? Power baby. An exultant choir. The crowd immediately burst in a mixed evocation of emotion, unable to contain its excitement. Over in the corner where the two entertainment managers sat Sasha Volkyeva beamed. Pearl turned to her in a state of shock, taking her by the hand. Pearl Harber: You didn't... Sasha Volkyeva: Just enjoy it comrade. The two turned back towards the stage where now there was a commotion. The stage had begun to be lit by dark red and gold lights as the song continued to play out. The crescendo of cymbals and blaring horns amounted to the pomp and circumstance that was so fitting. In a very smart and dignified way, two lines of men marched out onto the stage, forming a protective barrier on the stage. They all were smartly dressed in full military uniform, decked out in their respective regalia fit for a display. They all held firm, waiting for a signal. And then they notice more of these "soldiers" taking up space in the crowd. They held large, intimidating rifles. The scene was set, and everything was beginning to look similar to a war zone. Then, the bright red lights begin to encompass everything. Banners begin falling. The music gets louder, and it is recognizable. It's the orchestrated Anthem of the Soviet Union. With the militarized soldiers closely surrounding Ellis and Adam now, they get a good look at the insignias on their uniforms, as they match the large displays on each banner; the banners now drapping over every piece of AWC property. The insignia, very simply, leads everyone to believe that the Bear has come home. The insignia? The Sickle & The Hammer. Over the PA, a voice begins speaking. The accent is similar to that of Sasha Volkyeva's; Russian. Voice: Respect, Comrades, is not passed about like a cheap bottle of vodka on a night of celebration such as this. Respect IS DEMANDED by reputation and presence. By now, everything is becoming more blatantly and blatantly obvious to those who know their wrestling trivia. The crowd who is old enough to have watched the ever more obvious memory of the incumbent begin to roar in approval. As the anthem plays out the soldiers marching through the crowd being to force their rifles into the air in time with the beat and it becomes a formality where this is headed. Pearl Harber is in a state of disbelief, so happy at the prospect of who is about to march out into the arena and into the lives of AWC. Voice: Do you wield respect, Comrade? He's short, he's skinny, and he doesn't have the trademark beak anatomy classifies as a nose. It is hard to see the man's face, being that he hides it with a general-fashioned military cap and the elongated collar of his jacket propped up. The crowd's collective jaw drops as Mike Wade walks like a cocksure gunslinger down the aisle, confident that nobody is going to touch him with the beef he's got with him.
