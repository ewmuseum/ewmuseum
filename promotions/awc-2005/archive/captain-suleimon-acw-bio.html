Captain Suleimon Biography Real Name Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon Nicknames The Sultan of Smackdown, the Pride of the Ottoman Empire Hometown Istanbul, Turkey Birthday November 1st, 1975 Height 5'10' Weight 198 Fed Awards Q3-Q4 AWC Best Talker Award Biography Image: Curly matted black hair, beady brown eyes, light brown skin, black pushbroom moustache, skinny in build, has a defined musculature, but nothing overly bulky Ring Attire: Comes to the ring wearing a khaki petticoat with military decorations and a red fez cap with a golden tassel. Wrestles in khaki pants, black boots, crimson 'wifebeater' tank top, white wrist tape and crimson elbow pads Style: Used to be a spot machine to the nth degree, much like the Great Sasuke, Jeff Hardy or the ECW Cruisers. Has gravitated to a ground-based style over the years due to the toll the high-risk style has taken on his body, but will still prefer to air it out. History: Abdul Wahad Suleimon has never been a fan of the West. He sees Western Europe and especially the United States as the bastion of decadence and softness. While most like-minded peers of Suleimon wrapped themselves in fanatical Islam, Suleimon himself plunged himself into the history books. He fell in love with a time when Turkey, his homeland, was a world power. He studied the Ottoman Empire with great fanatacism. He became so immersed in it that he thought he was an heir to the Pasha line. Oddly enough, his other love, professional wrestling, was a totally American dominated profession. He watched it on TV, rooting for anyone who would bring the American establishment down... Iron Sheikh, Nikolai Volkoff... and he watched swearing that one day, he'd show those decadent American swine how brutality is really supposed to be dished out. Suleimon trained and caught the attention of Malik Ismed Rahman, a local wrestling legend with connections in America. After a few years of wrestling in Turkey, Suleimon was ready for the much more vibrant and competitve scene in America. However, he wouldn't enter as just any Turk, some random foreigner with a whitebread personality. He would storm the American scene as Captain Suleimon, with General Rahman as his manager, the last bastions of the Ottoman Empire. He surveyed the indie scene until he got a contract to compete in A1E. There, he made a small splash, competing against the likes of Big Dog, Gladiator and Drifter, but injuries set his career back. He competed in UXW as Sully Man!, but he doesn't even acknowledge that part of his career anymore. It's a black mark on his resumé, and he likes to leave it at that. Suleimon is intentionally gimmicky, but he is also a very cerebral competitor. He will debate history. And he will spew rhetoric about the decadence of the West. He, however, is not a jihadist, nor is he a 'cheap-heat' character. Although he is a Muslim, he doesn't do anything for the glory of Allah or any of that crap. Matchwriting Aide: Suleimon has been described as the Turkish Jeff Hardy at times, eschewing ring psychology for mind-blowing high spots earlier in his career. This style has gotten him injured several times, including a painful compacted tailbone early in 2001 which he didn't fully recover from until quite recently. So he's scaled back on his spot-o-rama style and added in some more ground based holds and even some submissions. He still doesn't use much psychology and will rarely ever work a body part outside of the neck occasionally (although sometimes this is by default because many of his moves target the head/neck area), but he's a lot more sound on the ground, and he's always learning new holds and moves. Aerially though, he's still as deadly as he was years ago, although he's a lot more careful of the spots he chooses to fly. He's eliminated the SSP from his repertoire altogether, partially because everyone else does it and partially because it was overkill, and the Leap is only used in the biggest situations (blood feuds and Championship scenarios... think ACW Spirit or World or AWC Frontier or Transatlantic), but he's added a few springboard and out-of-ring moves, like the lionsault and the Space Flying Tiger Drop. As for match finishers go, he will most likely build towards the Dervish unless in a huge situation where he'd break out the Leap, or when the rules allow, the Arabian Facebuster. When the Dervish has been scouted and/or countered, Suleimon will go to the air or improvise... when all else fails, he'll fall back on using the dangerous variant of his Tiger suplex to finish (a page out of Mike Randalls' book) Entrance Music 'The Turkish March' Mozart
