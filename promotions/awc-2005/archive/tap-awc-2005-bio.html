T.A.P. Biography Real Name TAP Hometown Schenectady, NY Birthday September 7, 1986 Height 6' 4' Weight 255 Biography T.A.P. was born September 7, 1986 into the middle class home of NY state policeman and a Waffle House waitress. Like his father before him T.A.P. was a natural athlete. However while his father excelled at baseball, T.A.P. chose the road to a wrestling mat. However with time comes change and T.A.P. fell in with the wrong crowd. Like many youth across the country, he experimented with alcohol, which later led to drugs. Needless to say a young man that could've obtained a wrestling scholarship with any school in the control wasted what collegiate future he had. T.A.P. started skipping school and eventually let his vices get the best of him. Eventually he started dealing drugs and became part of a huge FBI sting that led the prosecution of one of NY state's biggest drug dealers. Through a plea bargain T.A.P. was able to escape jail time, by turning states evidence against the dealer. As result of dealer lord's arrest, he swore revenge on young T.A.P., whom he thought was the sole reason he was convicted. Nevertheless six months later, T.A.P.'s dad was found floating in the local resevoir. At that moment, what little of a childhood he still had grasp on was lost forver. Immediately T.A.P. and his mother was placed into the witness protection program and shipped as far as the law could get them from NY state. Hoping to get both out of harms way, the FBI transplanted them from to the town of Jamestown, KY. Needless to say it was quite the culture change for T.A.P. One would've thought T.A.P. would've learned his lesson from the whole saga, but his father's death drove him deep into depression. One of which, he tried to supress at the bottom of a Everclear. T.A.P. tried to wash away the misery of his father's death with whatever bottle he could find. Consequently the night of his 17th birthday, in a drunken rage T.A.P. stole his mom's car and went on a warpath through Jamestown, ending the evening in a traffic accident. Whether it was divine intervene or ironic coincidence, the driver of the other car was local legend, Darren James. Mr. James, once known as the man of a 1000 nicknames, was the owner and propietier of Jamestown Massacre Dojo. The dojo was basically a Y.M.C.A. type facility where Darren served as mentor to many of the area's trouble teens. The school also served as a training center for amateur wrestling. An interesting note is that one of the most famous students of Jamestown Massacre is that of former NWC and CORE superstar, 'Golden Boy' Jake Summers, whom James trained in the professional style. While living life as hard as one could and injuries from Darren's own time in the ring never grounded him, an accident he couldn't escape did. As result of the youth's car crashing into the Darren's motorcycle, the former hardcore superstar, found himself confined to a wheel-chair. Instead of pressing charges, Darren convinced the court to let him take Tommy under his wing, as a way to work off the kid's 36 months of community service. With time the two men formed a lasting friendship, although they still find themselves at odds. They may not always see eye to eye, but over the past twenty-four months the two have formed a mutual respect. Darren sees a lot of promise in his new protege, thinking perhaps he's got another 'Golden Boy' on his hands. T.A.P. enters A.W.C. as Darren's second prized pupil and someone Darren wishes the best for. With his wheel chaired mentor by his side, T.A.P. beckons for all challenges.
