Bruce Lomond Biography Real Name Bruce Robert Lomond Nicknames 'The Hatred' '100% Reality' Hometown Glasgow, Scotland Birthday 12/08/79 Height 6'4 Weight 238 Biography Bruce grew up in the large industrial city of Glasgow, Scotland. His father and mother split up when he was four, leaving Bruce with one parent when his father moved to Austria. For five years, Bruce and his mother struggled through life before his mother began to move on. In a staggering turn of events, she married a restraunt owner when Bruce was 13. Bruce resented his mother's love interest, and made his feelings heard. He constantly argued with his step-father and at age 16 had enough. He bid farewell to his mother by sending his father to intensive care. Bruce roamed Europe until he was 20, when he moved to Miami, Florida, with funding from his mother, who paid Bruce through life until this point using her husband's money. However, four months into America, the step-father found out about Bruce's money, and Bruce was left without cash. He decided to try and earn an honest wage, but could not find a job with his basic qualifications, thanks to his distractions as a teenager. He resorted to underground fighting, and from it earned enough to get by, however, this career was cut short when Bruce suffered a horrendous beating. He went through a further year without a decent occupation, before putting his now established skills into the wrestling industry.
