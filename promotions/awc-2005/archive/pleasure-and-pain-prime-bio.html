Pleasure And Pain Biography Fed Awards Top Ten Apperances: 6.6.06 #7 Biography Tiara and Wayne are mixed gender tag team that has been married just under a year. They both attended the same high school but were not overally friendly. After high school they each went their seperate ways in which sooner or later lead to them meeting at wrestling school and falling in love. They both completed wrestling school and are now looking to break into the industry as a tag team so they can share their love for each other and their profession together. They are going through the normal things a newlywed couple would go through and it is as exciting as it could ever be and they want to share it with the rest of the world. They have lovers quarrels but at the sametime they are in what you might call puppy love with each other.... Finisher Name InDenial Finisher Description Wayne tags in Tiara Belle and then throws the opponent off the ropes. When they bounce back he picks them up like he is goin for a back body drop but as he starts to drop the opponent, Tiara Belle leaps from the top rope landing a leg drop on the falling opponent, she then makes a cover.
