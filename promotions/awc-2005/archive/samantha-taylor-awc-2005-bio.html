Samantha Taylor Biography Real Name Samantha Taylor Nicknames Princess Samantha, Disney's Diva Hometown Orlando, Florida. The most magical place on Earth! Birthday 3/3/81 Height 5'8' Weight 132 Fed Titles None, yet. Fed Awards None. Except maybe most cheerful superstar? Biography Samantha Taylor is what seems to be a rarity in wrestling; a genuinely happy female competitor. There is no abuse in her past, nor forseeable problems in her future. A proud graduate of Florida State University, she found work after graduation in the world famous Magic Kingdom Theme Park, where she plays Cinderella for various character appearances. And now, she's here. Yay! Gimmick: Fan favorite female competitor, with a case of Disneyphilia. Generally happy, someone the fans can feel good about cheering for. Also can be seen as something of a sex symbol, though in a more innocent, PG-13 Disneyish way. Think Lindsay Lohanish before the sluttery kicked in. Character Traits: - Disneyphilia. Samantha LOVES Disney and all of its movies, hence her occassionally being known by her fans as 'Princess Samantha'. - Very happy and cheery. Not to the point of being obnoxious, but she's a genuinely good person without many cares in the world. -Fanservicey. She likes wearing somewhat revealing costumes to the ring, but in an innocent sort of way. Cute, but not sluttish. On her way down to the ring, she'll often kiss one of her male fans on the cheek. - Gymnastic Background. Hyper agile and flexible, which helps her greatly in the ring. She uses a combination of technical and aerial moves, with a smattering of basic strikes and submissions. - Heroine. Likes standing up for the abused and the downtrodden. Other Feds None. AWC's her first professional endeavor in wrestling. Other Fed Awards 'Princess of the Month' at Walt Disney World for two years running. Hey, she's a great Cinderella, what can I say? Wrestling Style Agile, graceful highflying. Finisher Move A Whole New World or Wish Upon A Star (rarer) Finisher Description Five-Star Frog Splash or Shooting Star Legdrop Finisher Setup Move The Magic Teacup Setup Description Tornado DDT Other Moves Samantha Taylor's background in dance, ice skating, and gymnastics defines her offensive and defense style in the ring. 'Princess Samantha' has remarkably strong legs, so she uses these when striking more often then not. She also is very capable of twisting and flipping through the air, even without height to start from, so she is a difficult competitor to catch. Of course, if you do catch her, she's likely in trouble because of her slight size, and the fact that she's not yet used to being thrown around so much. Strikes: Knife-edge chops, basic kicks (ambidextrous with these, she can throw hard with both legs, leg lariat, spinning wheel kick. Grapples: Basic moves here, really. DDTs, jawbreakers, anything she can get off with her slight size. Some examples are below. DDT, sitdown jawbreaker, kneeling jawbreaker, Russian legsweep, Moves Off Top Rope: The main way the princess damages her opponents. Her slight size means she obviously can't suplex people, so she simply throws her 132 pounds at them again and again, hoping to drive the air out of them enough for a pin. Diving Hurricanrana, Diving Moonsault, Diving Legdrop/Elbowdrop/Kneedrop (the basics), 450 Splash, Shooting Star Press, Diving Double Stomp, Moonsault Double Stomp, Phoenix Splash, DragonRana Dives: Against Princess Samantha, nowhere is safe, especially not the outside. Her agility allows her to execute a variety of jaw-dropping aerial moves that fling her body onto opponents on the floor. Tope Suicida, somersault Tope Suicida, Tope con Hilo, Asai Moonsault, Springboard 180 Moonsault Suicida, Handspring to Moonsault Suicida, Top Rope Somersault Plancha Entrance Music “Beauty and the Beast” by Alan Menken & Howard Ashman
