Rustin Slade Biography Real Name Rustin Slade Nicknames 'Rain Man' Hometown Belle Harbor, NY Birthday July 24, 1976 Height 6'4' Weight 216 Biography PRECEDING ARRIVAL TO 4WW: Rustin Slade was born outside of Belle Harbor, NY on July 24, 1976. Most of his life was spent pursuing higher education. This was mostly due to part of his mother and father’s wishes. Both Margo and Edward Slade wanted Rustin to become a lawyer, an occupation that passed down from generation to generation. Although Slade excelled at school work, so much for him to get an interview for Harvard Law School, Slade had another passion, professional wrestling. Seeking an alternative to his fated life, Rustin skipped his interview at Harvard Law School to catch an open tryout in New York City for 4 Winds Wrestling. The scouts for 4WW were so impressed by Rustin’s technical skills, and aptitude for creating new and unique wrestling maneuvers, that he was quickly signed. The rest would become history. BEGINNING OF 4WW CAREER: Rustin debuted with an explosion against “The Done Deal” Paul Williams. Although Rustin had lost his match, it is generally accepted that the match could have gone either way. The match allowed Paul to get eventual Tempest gold, but Rustin was not concerned so much with the belt, as he was with another matter. Rustin’s eye became focused upon East Wind and West Wind Division Referee, Chastity Spence. His intensity to gain her attention became so apparent, that Rustin’s career had suffered a slight downfall with some continual losses. It would not be until Maelstrom VI: New York Nightmare, that Rustin would finally get his life back on track. Entering into the 2nd Annual 4 Winds Wrestling Tournament, Rustin surprised himself and his fans by beating 3 other opponents to get into the finals. A well placed Silencer from YTO Rob Givens had ended Rustin’s chances of becoming the 2nd ever 4 Winds Champion. Rustin did not walk out empty handed from the pay-per-view, however, as this was the first hug he shared with Chastity. The hug was unrequited, however, and Rustin suffered a knee to the groin for his troubles. Secondly, he gained a sense of respect for Rob Givens. RUSTIN'S NEXT SEASON: Rustin, coming out of the loss from Maelstrom VI, had now set his sights back onto wrestling for the most part. His efforts of pursuing Chastity had also begun to pay off. She finally started paying more attention to him. Unfortunately for Rustin, Chastity’s old army boyfriend, Lincoln Frink was also paying attention to Rustin. Trying to get Chastity back for himself also, Lincoln had set off some violent encounters with Rustin, sending him to the hospital on several different occasions, and even going so far as to throw Rustin off the side of a bridge in Europe. Rustin, however, was not discouraged in his career. He formed an allegiance with former rival Paul Williams, YTO Rob Givens, “Severed” Kristian Kross, and “Dark Angel” Darek DeAngelo. He became West Wind Champion, in what many consider to be Rustin’s best match of his career. In a Coal Miner’s Glove Lumberjack Match, Rustin had trumped a victory over Zane Ozz, debuting the vicious Riverside Rockabye. Rustin’s title reign was short-lived, however, losing the title to Draven Lox in a Hardcore Chain Rope Deathmatch. Rustin’s career had once again began a losing streak, in part due to cryptic warnings to stay away from Chastity from a Master of the Mystical, Dr. Henry Voodoo. These warnings were avoided, however. Later in the season, Rustin regained some hope, being a fan favorite in the race for the third spot at the main event of Maelstrom VII: All The King’s Men. His chance was squelched by “The Dark Horse” Syckle, in what many consider to be another great career highlight of Rustin Slade’s. With continuous interference and after match mayhem from Lincoln Frink, Rustin finally accepted to a match at Maelstrom VII where Chastity’s love would be on the line. To add further humiliation, the loser would have to be put into a dress and photographed for the upcoming issue of the 4WW Magazine. RUSTIN SLADE VS. LINCOLN FRINK AT MAELSTROM VII: Rustin went into the match, aside from further warnings from Dr. Henry Voodoo. The match was mainly in Rustin’s favor, until everyone thought he had the match won with the Fairy Tale Ending. Chastity refused to count Frink out for the three, turning her back on Rustin. Rustin confronted Chastity, only to be viciously piledriven by Frink for the three count. In the aftermath, he was photographed by Frink and Chastity in a dress which was violently put on him by Frink. He did not end the match there, however. Frink rolled Slade into a casket, and lit it afire, while Slade was inside. Rustin was saved from imminent death by Dr. Henry Voodoo in a spectacular display of magic. Henry and a group of Rain Men had extinguished the fire, cementing Rustin into a state of debt to his new found friend, Dr. Henry Voodoo. RUSTIN SLADE'S FEUD WITH SHAWN SINGLETON: After his loss at the Maelstrom VII PPV, Rustin continued his quest for Chastity, now with the help of Dr. Henry Voodoo. He enlisted the aid of Dr. Voodoo to help him regain Chastity's love. This would involve a bizarre magic ritual that took a month to prepare. At the climax of the ritual, when Rustin thought he would get his dream girl back, he was interrupted by Shawn Singleton's mangager, Hunter, and a new wrestler named Martyr. They ruined the ritual, destroying Rustin's hopes and putting Henry out of commission. Rustin, now angered beyond belief struck back at Shawn Singleton, calling him out, blaming him for the loss of Chastity. Singleton put Slade through many tests. These included a one on one cage match with Martyr, and a special referee match against former West Wind Champion Draven Lox, with none other than Chastity Spence, who now was in Singleton's corner after a break-up with Frink. After Slade emerged victorious, he earned the shot at the West Wind Title of Shawn Singleton's at Maelstrom VIII: Down and Dirty. RUSTIN SLADE VS. SHAWN SINGLETON: Perhaps the greatest match in Rustin's career was decided at Maelstrom VIII when he faced his nemesis Shawn Singleton. The 'Ladder of Success' match turned out to be a grueling test of endurance for both men. When the match had seemed to be all but over as Rustin reached for the West Wind Title above the stage, his former girlfriend, Chastity came onto the stage, begging Rustin's forgiveness. Rustin decided to get the title rather than his lost love. In a startling move, Chastity pushed the ladder over, causing Rustin to crash through the stage, breaking his heart yet again. Rustin lost the title as Singleton climbed the ladder to retrieve the belt. After the match, Singleton pulled a double cross on Chastity and suplexed her into the gaping hole along with Rustin. AFTERMATH OF THE FEUD/CLOSING OF THE 4WW/AWC: A broken-hearted, and bruised Rustin retreated into himself. He became distant and went on another losing streak. Chastity attempted to get close to him, but Rustin still remained distant, snapping at Chastity. In a startling turn of events, Rustin joined his nemesis, Shawn Singleton in his group, 'The Triumvirate,' consisting of Shawn, Rustin, and Martyr, managed by the dubious Hunter. Rustin began getting his edge back, winning matches again. Without the hassle of Chastity, he remained focused and dangerous in the ring. After the announcement of the closing of the 4WW, Rustin was ready to cash in his chips and retire, but Singleton convinced Rustin for one more run. There was a new fed that called to the both of them: The Atlantic Wrestling Club; AWC. With renewed hope and vigor, Rustin signed on along with Shawn to have one more reign of terror.
