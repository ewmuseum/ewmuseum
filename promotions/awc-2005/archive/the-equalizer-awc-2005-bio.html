The Equalizer Biography Real Name John Smith Hometown Washington D.C. Birthday 07/04/1976 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Fed Titles None so far Fed Awards None so far Biography Wrestling apparel: A red-white-and-blue mask with the Stars-and-Stripes being the general theme. Similarly themed wrestling tights. Black wrestling boots. Blue elbow pads with small imprints of the US flag on the elbow. History: The Equalizer is a very patriotic man who has been inside the wrestling rings for almost a decade now. His father and grandfather were wrestlers as well, and he sees it as a truly American sport which everyone should be able to enjoy. Inspired by his forebears he works diligently to root out those who would exploit the sport, trying his best to bring troublemakers and cheaters to justice. Entrance: Lynyrd Skynyrd's 'Freebird' starts playing over the PA, and a pair of waving US flags show up on the video screens. The Equalizer strolls out, waving at his fans and carrying an American flag with him. He trades high-fives with front-row fans before heading to the ring, where he shakes hands with the referee and then stretches until the match is ready to begin. (If the show is being held outside of the States, The Equalizer leaves the flag backstage and tries to avoid raising any antipathy in any way he can) Wrestling Style All-arounder, leaning towards brawler Finisher Move The American Dream Finisher Description Camel Clutch/Cobra Clutch Combo Finisher Setup Move Equalizing DDT Setup Description DDT Other Moves Style: The Equalizer is a jack-of-all-trades, but he finds brawling comes to him the easiest. Equalizer likes to keep things simple, preferring hard, crowd-pleasing slam moves and methodical yet simplistic submissions. He will never, ever cheat or act in an unsporting manner. He doesn't tolerate cheating in matches in general, but will try to get revenge within the limits set by the rules: he will not go out to break them. Even if accidental, The Equalizer will all but go limp from terror if he realizes what he has done. The Equalizer always tries to shake hands with his opponent before and after match, no matter what has gone down in the ring or in the past. Strategy: The Equalizer prefers to start match out with a slow feeling-out process, utilizing standing chain wrestling and tests of strength to gauge what would be the best course of action. If the opponent is physically outmatched, The Equalizer will try to overpower them and throw them around the ring. If they're larger, he will either brawl or take them down to the mat to be stretched out to size. After the beginning, The Equalizer tries to work his way up to different suplexes and slams before finally going for the Equalizing DDT/The American Dream-combo. Moves utilized by The Equalizer: Takedown moves: Bodyslam, drop toe hold, arm wringer, arm drag, leg tackle, snapmare, belly-to-belly suplex, fireman's carry slam, headlock takedown Standing moves: Dropkick, punch combos, clothesline, bionic elbow, running forearm, chops across the chest, slap, Three Point Charge High-impact moves: Vertical suplex, stalling vertical suplex, powerslam, tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, slingshot suplex, slow-motion backdrop, double underhook powerbomb, Uncle Sam Slam, Equalizing DDT Submission moves: Sleeperhold, chinlock, basic chain wrestling (hammerlock, side headlock, full nelson), airplane spin, chickenwing facelock, The American Dream, Golden Gate Bridge Signature & finisher moves: Three Point Charge: The Equalizer takes the Three Point stance, usually behind a groggy opponent. Then when the opponent turns around, Equalizer charges in and shouldertackles them down. Uncle Sam Slam: A runniing powerslam with authority. The Equalizer holds his opponent over his shoulder for a few seconds to generate suspense and then takes a short sprint, slamming the opponent down hard afterwards. Equalizing DDT: A basic DDT, but The Equalizer performs a salute before he hits the move. The American Dream: Equalizer's main finisher. With the opponent lying face down on the mat, The Equalizer sits on their back like in the camel clutch and hooks their left arm behind his left knee. He then uses the opponent's right arm to apply a cobra clutch and then pulls back in the combination submission, putting extreme tension on the neck and back of his opponent. Golden Gate Bridge: Rotating torture rack. The Equalizer gets his opponent in a torture rack and then starts spinning around as fast as possible without falling over, usually resulting in a nice crowd reaction. Entrance Music 'Freebird' by Lynyrd Skynyrd
