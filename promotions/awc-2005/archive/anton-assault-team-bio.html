Anton Assault Biography Real Name Antony Nnamdi Ozubike Nicknames The Lion, Mr. CKD, Double A Hometown Paris, France Birthday August 16, 1980 Height 6'1' Weight 229 Fed Titles Frontier title [6/23/06- ] Fed Awards Fresh!man [1] [Most Recent: 6/13/06]; Biography |PERSONAL INFORMATION| Heritage/Nationality: Nigerian (despite being born in France) Physical Description: Anton is a well-built individual, very muscular despite his rather lean frame. He has dark, brown skin that marks him an African and a slightly round face with a broad jaw. He is prone to frowning, so his eyebrows are often seen drawn together. He has a tattoo of a lion's head dominating his back, although it is tough to see against his skin. His hair is black and he generally keeps it cropped close to his head. He never keeps facial hair; the most that grows is stubble before he shaves it down. Attire: Anton wrestles in black short trunks with green trim in typical UFC/MMA style, coupled with black boots with white and green laces and black knuckle gloves. He enters the ring wearing a dashiki that he takes off before fighting. Even when jumped, when he gets the chance he removes his dashiki before continuing. Personality: Anton is a severe individual, his concern primarily laying in the act of combat. Outside of that, he tries to distance himself from drama as much as possible, letting his manager handle all of that. He speaks very little because his English is not good, although when presented with someone that speaks Spanish or French, he is more prone to opening up since he is fluent in those languages. He is slightly arrogant about his abilities, but he is rarely condescending. In the ring, he is intense and extremely focused. Relations: Nneka Maria Ozubike, sister Ugochukwu (Ugo) Edward Ozubike, father Nina Ozubike, mother Entrance: “WAAAAAARRRLOOOOOOOOOOORRRRRRRRD!!!” The guttural, almost desperate cry from Steven Rathbone announces Lair of the Minotaur’s “Warlord.” A long guitar slide sounds and then the song enters a fierce rampage, accompanied by flashing red lights and an image of a lion leaping into a pit of men and tearing into them wholesale, sending limbs flying and terrified bodies everywhere. Amid the crushing riffage, a form dressed in a multicolored dashiki steps out onto the stage: the one and only Anton Assault. Of course, right behind him is his manager, Luis Ferrara, dry washing his hands and casting an arrogant glance up onto the crowd. |BIOGRAPHY| Anton was born in Paris, France to Ugochukwu Edward Ozubike, the ambassador to France from Nigeria, and his wife Nina. He spent a cozy life in France, learning to speak French as well as his native Igbo. When Anton was seven, his father moved the family back to Nigeria, settling near Lagos and in the Igbo southeastern region of the country for a long while. Anton led a life regarded as priviledged, giving him the opportunity to explore things that many of his fellows could not. He was encouraged to take these opportunities and, immediately following his eighteenth birthday, his father sent him back to France for a college education. Anton had never had any intent on attending college, however. He was a reasonably bright person and his aversion to university was on a philosophical basis: he did not like anything that would attempt to structure his progress further than he had already been structured. He took the money that his father gave him and drifted about for nearly a year, not looking for employment or anything else. Over the course of his stay in France, he began to explore combat sports. He began to take an increasing interest in them, attending whenever he could and cheering on his favorites. Eventually, he came to start studying martial arts, taking lessons night and day with his father's money. He began to learn as was intended for his return to France, but he did not fill his head with academia. He mastered the arts surprisingly quickly due to his relentlessness; most people simply took hour-a-day classes because of their schedules. Eventually, Anton began to train heavily to become a mixed martial arts competitor. At the time, he still fought as Antony Ozubike, but he was quickly given his trademark moniker: the Lion. At twenty, Anton had become something of an underground sensation in France and other neighboring European countries. He became the Light Heavyweight champion of Franco-German Fights in a dominant show and won several titles in Britain. However, because the regions were small, he found himself travelling a lot to defend and challenge for different belts. Eventually, Anton decided that the best way to improve his game would be to bulk up and increase his submission focus. He entered as a heavyweight while twenty-two, showing considerable improvement in his holding. He became one of the most successful underground fighters, but several attempts to break into big name success failed. Anton attributed it to the form of the style, a style which he was still adapting to. In lieu of becoming a big star, Anton decided to travel to South America and compete there. In South America, there were not so many organizations: most of the big fame came from illegal fights for big money, fought in cages with--almost literally--no holds barred. Anton first arrived in Brazil, but he moved to Venezuela not long after. He began to achieve some small fame in Venezuela when he was approached by a former small-time coke dealer named Luis Ferrara. Luis had gotten out of dealing coke to focus his attentions on the combat sports. Luis managed to convince Anton into letting Luis manage him and Anton agreed. Luis immediately got to work cutting deals with Luis for bigger cards with more well-known fighters. Anton proved himself worthy by demolishing his opponents, using the new, faster-paced system of fighting to his advantage. He was undeniably faster than most of the other wrestlers, managing to strike fast and hard. When he couldn't, however, he locked his opponents down with brutal, debiilitating submissions. His 'stock' shot up and Anton became one of the biggest things in the illegal fight circuit. When the Venezuelan government broke up an event that Anton and Luis were attending--Anton had not fought yet, as he was in the main event--Anton decided to return to Nigeria for a while and let all the heat die down. They did just that and Anton began to take all comers there, keeping it relatively low-profile. Eventually, his family found out of how he was spending his time--he had not kept in contact with them--through a video tape seen at a store. They attempted to reach him, but he was never where he was the night before. Eventually, Anton and Luis returned to South America to continue finding thir fortune. It was several years of moving around before Anton got fed up with the deal there. A shootout with some of Luis's coke dealers, to whom he owed money, convinced Luis and the two of them booked a flight to the States. Luis had begun to scout out potential opportunities and he saw that professional wrestling was very lucrative. This began Anton's short training in pro wrestling--Anton was quick to learn, having previous experience in combat sports. Luis's wheeling and dealing brought Anton a quick contract for AWC, where we join him now... Wrestling Style Shoot/Intense Martial Arts/Submission Finisher Move Varied Finisher Description Depends on situation (explained in Moves) Finisher Setup Move Varied Setup Description Depends on situation Other Moves |WRESTLING STYLE| Anton Assault is a shoot fighter at heart, using incredibly powerful strikes to his advantage. In the ring, he has eschewed the typical takedown MMA style for a more stand-up, martial arts one and he typically favors kicks, knees, and elbow strikes because of wrestling's regulations against closed fists. He is fond of striking combinations, especially hits to the jaw. He is also an extremely dangerous submission wrestler. His match style consists of him concentrating on one part of the opponent's body—either the legs, arms, or neck—and continuously working on that area, generally leading to a tap out. His throws are increasing in use and power as well, Anton being able to seamlessly weave suplexes and the like into his form. He is comfortable with many paces of fighting, making it difficult for people to outpace him in order to gain victory. His weaknesses are mainly that he is vulnerable to top rope and extreme aerial techniques, having never studied them thoroughly. When whipped into the ropes or when his opponent goes into the ropes, he does his best to stop the situation; he is quick and agile, but when sent on a one-way charge, he is not comfortable. Also, he is not good whenever he is taken off his feet. Leaving the ground involuntarily could result in destruction for him. He doesn't often leap off the top rope for aerial moves much, either. Dangerous Points: His right cross: An extremely dangerous punch to be on the receiving end of. His legs: He has ended more than one match with a brutal kick and they often do well stopping opponents cold. Also, his repeated knees are a favorite maneuver of his. His speed: Although he dislikes flying and being shot into the ropes, Anton is very fast. Opponents who believe that his submission style is indicative of a lack of speed will be cruelly surprised. |STRATEGY| Main Strategy: Anton chooses a part of his opponent and focuses on it. He wears down on it. He executes submissions on it, he strikes it, he beats it into the ground. Then when he feels ready, he puts them in a submission targeting that point, preferrably one of his finishers. Match. Set. Game. Beginning of the Match: Anton begins with a heavy striking game, just pummeling his opponent with combinations and such. He leads them all over the ring as he tries to figure out where he wants to target. If he has some idea of a weakness, he typically goes after that. Middle of the Match: Anton starts in with the submissions, looking to change it to more of a grounded game. He will execute strikes when he feels they will aid him, but he typically looks to keep his opponent down while he wrenches the hell out of his chosen spot. Ending of the Match: Anton breaks out his big guns here, going all out to lock on his chosen finishing hold. When Facing Power/Larger Wrestlers: Anton does his best to tire them out. He typically prolongs the striking portion of his game, leading them around and making them work. After he feels they are sufficiently winded, he surprises them with a succession of submissions. When Facing Smaller Wrestlers: Here, his strategy is similarly to prolong his striking game, but for another reason. Smaller wrestlers generally have much more endurance than larger ones, so Anton looks to hit them with the most punishing strikes and combinations he can. He seeks to hurt them before they can take off. |SITUATIONAL NOTES| When Shot into the Ropes: Anton's first reaction is the grab the ropes and try and stop his motion. If he can't do that, he tries to simply stop mid-run. If tht fails, he leaps at his opponent, looking for any kind of strike (or, possibly, a Thesz press). He does NOT want to be in a position where he is tossed into the ropes and he does anything to stop it. Likewise, he doesn't shoot his opponents into the ropes. When Set Upon the Top: Anton's strategy here is to knock his opponent away and simply drop off the buckle. He doesn't do any flying moves, so on the top is generally just a bad position. When on the Mat: Anton tries to get up and face his opponent first and foremost. If his opponent is close, however, he may try and ground them as well, looking for a submission. When on the Outside: Strikes. No submissions on the outside, because there's no time. He goes for a huge strike to the head or knees and then tries to get his opponent back in. If it's a match that eliminates count-out, however, it's business as usual. He typically doesn't use weapons, no matter the match. |MOVESET| |GENERAL MOVES*| Takedowns Running shoulder tackle Spear Short spinebuster Lou Thesz Press (desperation, when shot into ropes) Submissions Armbar (quick, not serious) Leg grapevine (quick, not serious) Nelson (full/half/three-quarter) Hammerlock (quick) Texas cloverleaf Abdominal stretch/Octopus hold Dragon sleeper Beast choker (dragon sleeper with bodyscissors) STF Elevated Boston crab Triangle choke Katahajime -- Anton despises the Sharpshooter/Scorpion Deathlock. -- Anton also does not use a traditional sleeper, instead opting towards the nelsons, the dragon sleeper, or the Marine Throttle (described below). KATAHAJIME SUPLEX|●●●▬▬ Anton stands behind his opponent and slips one arm around their neck in a bicep choke/sleeper. His other arm loops under theirs and locks in a half nelson. From here, he lifts backwards with a suplex, either holding it in and smashing them into the mat headfirst or releasing near the end and letting them land flat on their faces. He never throws them very far, wanting them near for more punishment. SHEER-DROP KATAHAJIME SUPLEX|●●●●▬ It is simply what it says. Anton hooks his opponent up for the katahajime suplex and lifts them into the air without arching backwards, holding them upside down for a split second so all of their body weight is focused straight down. He then drops right to the ground, smashing them into the mat. This puts extreme damage on their neck. This move is also known as the katahajime driver. URANAGE CRUSH|●●●●▬ Anton slips his arm over his opponent's chest and shoulder as if looking for a typical uranage* (alternatively, he locks his opponent in a Marine Throttle). He then twists his hips and hurls his opponent over in a judo-style toss, but when they're practically vertical, he throws his other arm out and falls prone to the mat, drilling them into the canvas with a modified uranage. Finishing Moves NIGERIAN ARMBAR|●●●●● Target: Arms|Escape-10% Ropes-2% Tap-88% Anton gets his opponent on the ground facedown. He grabs one of their arms and straddles it, facing away from the opponent. He then yanks their arm backwards, hooking it under his and stretching it over his knee, but not hyperextending it. He crosses one of his legs over their arm but behind his other ankle, standing almost daintily on his toes. He then drops down to his knees without warning, hyperextending their arm dangerously and almost invariably leading to a tap-out. TIGER TRAP LEG SCISSORS|●●●●● Target: Legs|Escape-5% Ropes-10% Tap-85% Anton gets his opponent on the ground face-up. He grabs one of their legs, lifting it up and bending at the knee while facing them. He then steps through with his same leg (i.e., when he lifts their left leg, he steps through with his left leg) and leans forward, pushing their leg back slightly. Then he lifts his other leg up, crosses it over and drops down, bringing his lifted leg down onto their ankle. This compresses their leg backwards, twisting it in a way a knee certainly was never meant to twist. STRAPPADO|!!!!! Target: Arms|Illegal One of Anton's few top rope moves. He sets his opponent up backwards on the top turnbuckle. He climbs up to the top as well, but he slings his legs over the outside and plants his feet on the second rope. He tugs his opponent's arm behind their back in a hammerlock. He then lifts up and hurls them overhead in German suplex fashion, slightly towards the side opposite of the hammerlocked arm. He quickly locks his feet on the top rope and tenaciously holds onto the hammerlock. This has the effect of leaving them nearly suspended in air, their arm being wrenched backwards agonizingly. Since the move is illegal, he often leaves this on for three or so seconds—more than enough in any case—and unhooks his legs, quickly flipping backwards and landing on his opponent's back. From here, he either turns his opponent over for the Nigerian Armbar or gets up, planting his knee on the small of their back and pushing forward with the hammerlock in the same fashion that it had just been applied. TEETH OF LIONS RULE THE DIVINE|●●●●● 2Count-00% 2.5-00% 2.9-00% Pinfall-100% Anton hooks his opponent in a front facelock with his left arm and lifts his right arm up, curling his fingers inward but not into a complete fist; his palm heel remains exposed. He then releases the front facelock and spins around, slamming his palm heel as hard as he can into their occipital ridge--where the skull meets the spine. This is a brain shake technique and if done correctly, knocks his opponent straight out; Anton does this correctly 19 times out of 20.
