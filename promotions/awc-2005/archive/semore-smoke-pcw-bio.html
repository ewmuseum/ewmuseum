Semore Smoke Biography Real Name Simon 'Semore' Smoke Nicknames The Manipulator, The Magician Hometown Wilson, North Carolina Birthday April 5, 1970 Height 6'2'' Weight 220 Biography Simon 'Semore' Smoke was born on April 5, 1970 in the small town of Smithfield, North Carolina. His family as a joke for the pun that it created gave Simon at an early age the name “Semore”. The name was horribly misspelled from the 'Seymour' it should have been to the 'Semore' that Smoke has been known throughout the years due to the family’s inability to spell. At an early age Semore found his life very tough as he grew up on that proverbial 'bad side of the tracks' in town. His family wasn’t the best off in town and the desperation showed at an early age as Semore quickly learned the streets to try and make a living. This of course led to several problems in his early years as Semore was arrested on numerous occasions and before the age of eighteen Semore had already accumulated a slew of juvenile sentences in his life. During the first Gulf War, Semore was arrested for burglary and possession plus assault with a deadly weapon. The charges resulted in a very unorthodox sentence by the judge who had become accustomed to sending Smoke to juvenile detention and jail. Instead of jail, the judge suggested that Semore either accept a 4-year enlistment in the armed services or a life sentence in prison for his multiple offenses. Semore of course took the 4-year service in the army and came back seemingly a changed man after he received his discharge. Semore soon sought to straighten out his life and looked for an outlet of his military training that would also help him make a living at the same time. It was at this time that Semore stumbled upon a wrestling school by the name of PWA (Primetime Wrestling Academy). At the time of application the academy fed into a farm federation known as PCW2 (Primetime Championship Wrestling 2), PCW2 in turn then fed into PCW (Primetime Championship Wrestling), which was one of the more respected wrestling organizations of the time. Semore found that he had quite a knack for professional wrestling and after a short stint within the academy Semore soon found himself called up into the newly acquired NTICW (New True Internet Championship Wrestling) where he soon became World Champion. Not long after that Semore would find his way to the big time, PCW, where he enjoyed a quick win-streak coming out of the box that lasted roughly three months before being ended by none other than PCW legend, Clyde. Semore went onto win the PCW Rising Star Championship and PCW Extreme Championship before having to step out of the federation when his demons started to get back to him. Semore had long had an addiction to marijuana that continued to follow him throughout his life. That addiction combined with the amount of money that Semore was making as a professional wrestler led to stronger drugs like Cocaine and Heroine. Semore found himself spending all of his wrestling money on drugs and not on the necessities he should have been and it became quite evident that Semore was quickly reverting back to his old self. With those problems became the onset of the end of his career. Semore soon decided to retire away from the wrestling industry to try and combat those demons and now, three years later at the age of thirty-six he's returned to try and recapture that glory that he misses dearly.
