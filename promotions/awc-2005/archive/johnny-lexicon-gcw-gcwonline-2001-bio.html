Johnny Lexicon Biography Real Name Johnathon William Lexicon Hometown Calgary, Alberta Birthday July 26, 1977 Height 6'5' Weight 267 Other Feds 3W, GCW Other Fed Awards 3W = Skyweight, Hardcore, World GCW = Hardcore Wrestling Style All Around, Brawler Finisher Move Pattern Perfect Finisher Description Okay it is a sit out double under hook power bomb without the sit out and into a sunset flip. Pedigree position into a jacknife and Johnny holds his legs and flips over to roll him up and pin. Finisher Setup Move Anything Setup Description That's the beauty of it. No set up = no warning. Other Moves Signature Moves: Power Clothesline, Johnny doesn't need any running room, mastered the art of using his own body weight to turn momentum into a decapitating clothesline that has changed the pace of main event match ups. Reversals: This is where Johnny shines, his broad knowledge of wrestling moves and executions gives him a great advantage here, spotting his opponents and quickly devising counter strategies has always been Johnny's claim to wrestling fame. Instead of a move list of generic moves and such, I'm going to give you instead an impression of how the Lexicon in my mind takes it to the ring. Start of by saying Johnny is an extremely confident wrestler, he is very conscious of what's going on inside and around the ring, not likely to be caught unaware unless specifically plotted to do so. He's also not a dirty fighter, he doesn't like using weapons to help him win, believing any victory worth claiming is worth holding without the tarnish of cheating. Johnny's fan appeal comes from pure heart, he's very unlikely to tap out to a submission move unless it is that particular opponents finishing maneuver, other wise it simply isn't enough to put Lexicon down. Never say die is his motto, if he had a motto. As far as style goes, Johnny is an all arounder to the max. He started mat wrestling in grade ten, won provincials two years and Nationals in grade twelve. So he knows technical. He spent five years in Japan learning Kung Fu from his ex-wife's grand father. When ditched by Jordan in Mexico he was forced to wrestle Lucha Libre's for months and then forced to emulate their style to portray The Marauder for six months in the states. Johnny's got a wealth of knowledge and experience inside the ring, possibly more that most people on the roster. And he's not shy about using every tactic available to him to win a match. He WILL go to the top rope for a diving elbow to the outside, he WILL drop a toe hold and go for an ankle lock, he WILL trade blows with the biggest mutha fucka to climb through those ropes. Johnny is also a smart opponent, always scouting his opposition to be better prepared. For technical fighters he'll open with strong striking, keeping the pace fast and working on a leg, or a shoulder to take out the technical strength. When fighting a high flier he'll open technical, always targeting a leg he'll try to slow the pace down and keep his opponent grounded, wearing him down for one of Johnny's various power moves. When fighting somebody stronger and bigger than himself Johnny will open with quick strikes, always working on knees and coming off the ropes for lots of heavy impact moves, and as the pace slows he'll move into technical submission or sleepers to finish it off. His finishing move should only be used as that, a finishing move. There's two chances to reverse, in the pedigree position or at the peak of the power bomb. But if the move hits and Johnny locks the sunset flip, then that should be it. No kickout unless we've decided before hand that it's needed for angle progression. Style: Johnny is a Leo(Zodiac) Snake (Chinese Astrology). Like the Leo he is powerful, majestic, and territorial. Extremely competitive and always striving for the spot light, his moves are flashy his dare devil feats performed with a flourish. Like the Snake he is savvy, seductive, and disarmly smart, hard working and possessed of keen follow through. He is always fashionable and his grin always infectious.
