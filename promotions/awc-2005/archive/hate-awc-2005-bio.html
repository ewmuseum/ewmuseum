Hate Biography Nicknames The Fifth Horseman, The Nondivine Juggernaut Hometown Philadelphia, PA Height 6'2 Weight 225 Fed Titles None so far Fed Awards Q1 Best Feud (w/ Paddy O'Shea), Q1 Best Segment (The Public Execution), Q2 Best Heel, Q2 Best Fighter, Q2 Best Moment Biography Appearance: Hate is very well toned man from top to bottom. He doesn't have bodybuilder-style bulging muscles, but possesses a very powerful frame. His head is completely shaved bald, and there are several spike-like piercings on the bridge of his nose. Hate has scars on his chest, shoulders and back, as well as a large amount of scar tissue on his left arm from a failed attempt at OD'ing himself. Ring gear: In the ring, Hate wears faded blue jeans and a pair of black combat boots. Also for matches, he paints his entire head blood-red, creating a rather eerie effect. When Hate enters the ring, he wears a long, black trenchcoat and a WWII-era gas mask. The trenchcoat has dates, names and places written all over it, all relating to different historical massacres, murderers and crimes. On the back, between the shoulder blades, stand words that have been done in a bit heavier writing than the others, reading 'What fickle angels are we...' Entrance: Without any warning, Marduk's 'Dracul Va Domni Din Nou In Transilvania' begins to grind away at the speakers, filling the audio system with doom and darkness. Hate appears in the entranceway, with a black gas mask covering his head and wearing a long, black trenchcoat. As he walks to the ringside, Hate shouts verbal abuse at the fans through his mask before removing it at the ringside, revealing his painted head. Hate slides into the ring and takes off his trenchcoat before putting both the coat and the mask away, utterly concentrated on the match ahead. Entrance themes used: Agoraphobic Nosebleed - North American Corpse Desecration (Zero To Hero - PTC Super Series) Marduk - Dracul Va Domni Din Nou In Transilvania (PTC Super Series - 28/03/06) ----- Bio: Society: a community having common traditions, institutions, and collective interests. These collective interests might be to allow individuals better themselves through free competition. Or have individuals work for the society, thereby improving the society and all it’s individuals as a whole. Or control everything and make sure every individual is equal. To attain these ends, society tries to raise new crops of humans that can act within the laws and the norms set by the society. Most of the time, society works more or less right: and despite minor defects, new individuals are happily introduced to the grind. But sometimes, society goes wrong. Due to one unfortunate circumstance or another, a growing individual is disturbed so that nothing the society can offer can bring them back. These men are known as criminals, renegades and rogues: they are the ones whom mail anthrax letters to public officials, whom take up guns and shoot up a schoolyard full of playing children, whom assault and rape college girls in their beds. These people come in all forms and sizes: whatever twists them inside, does not carry on to the outside. One of these is a man. Six foot two. Bald. With piercings on the bridge of his nose. And a hell of a lot of anger in him. The earliest thing he can remember is being led away by an elderly lady at the foster home, impatient with the confused child. His mother, sporting a black eye and fresh bruises on her face, cries after him while the father lights up another cigarette and yells for the “bitch to quit her fucking bawling”. This would be the last he would see of his parents in years. Then, a foster home outside of Philadelphia. White, middle-class family. Classic suburban Americans. An accountant father whom bowls every Thursday. Schoolteacher mother who bakes apple pie for every football game and plays a game of bridge with the neighborhood ladies every once in a while. Two kids, the perfect all-American twins, playing soccer and baseball in their junior varsity teams. Outwards, they welcomed the quiet boy with open arms: outwards, they seemed like the ideal place for a kid to grow up at. Except that they were not a part of the American Dream: they were a part of the American Cover-up. For every night of bowling, the father would have a night of whoring and drinking. Every apple pie was accompanied with shouting and slaps all around, kid and father alike. Every now and then the slaps were interrupted by a punch and a slammed door: later, both would continue to purge themselves by further abusing the children of the house. In turn, the twins would blow their frustrations on the youngest, adopted son, not fearing retaliation from their parents because of what the youngest might or might not tell. The twins grew up and left for college, and Mom and Dad were left with their adopted son and each other. They went to church every Sunday: and almost always, the priest would notice the bruises on the son’s face. “Oh, he slipped and fell”, they would tell him, laughing at their son’s clumsiness and being generally merry for everyone. And the priest would laugh, alongside the disgustingly obese neighborhood ladies, who had ballooned out of age and stupidity. And never would the son dispute the claims, and everyone thought him as clumsy as his parents claimed him to be. The night he turned 15, he wasn’t clumsy. The police dispatched to the scene were greeted by the mother outside of the house, holding a bleeding forearm. “Quick, quick, he’s killing Herb!” Inside, chaos. Everything lay in ruins: cupboards of trophies were smashed, family pictures ripped, report cards on the kitchen door stapled together and covered in written insults. In the living room, working with a rhythm not unlike a stonecutter, the birthday hero lifted the prized slugger of the now-gone twins up and down and up and down on his father’s body. Herb would never recover. The initial smash from the bat to the back of the head disabled him completely; leaving him paralyzed for the rest of his miserably short life. The boy was sentenced for aggravated assault, amongst others, and was sent to a juvenile prison. While there, he beat up two more people before suffering his sentence in silence. At the age of 17, he was transferred over to a regular state prison. While psychotherapists protested that the boy, now a man, had not changed, merely built up another reservoir of violence, the standardized tests showed otherwise. He no longer checked the “I would like to kill my parents”-box on the papers, and gave his full name instead of “Spawn of Satan, Prince of Darkness”. Two hours after being released from the prison, he had traded his worldly possessions in for a haircut, a copy of “Mein Kampf” and a pair of brass knuckles. He became a neo-Nazi. He became a communist. He became an anarchist. He wore pictures of Hitler, Lenin, Guevara and Bundy on his shirts. He listened to music that only served as a springboard for extremist group propaganda. He beat people up, for money or for fun, and became the single most feared man in any moshpit around the country. He spent many nights in heated arguments over politics, religion, government and economics, dismissing everything as useless shit. Truly nihilistic, it was claimed that by this time he had become addicted to fights, not unlike a businessman to his morning coffee. A day just didn’t feel the same without a bloodied fist. Ultimately, the grim outlook he had on life overwhelmed him and he tried to overdose on heroin. The failed attempt scarred his right arm and psyche forever, and when he emerged from the hospital, there suddenly was a new purpose for his life. He began to study with an increased fervor, devouring textbooks both known and unknown in his search for answers. For many sleepless nights, he raged away at himself in this or that basement, trying to formulate his ultimatum to the whole world. And on Sunday, when the church bells awoke him in a cold pool of bile and vomit, he understood what he had to do. He took his trenchcoat, already decorated with dates of massacres and names of dictators, and added his gospel at the top: ”What fickle angels are we…” He systematically eradicated any trace of his original name. In Boston, MA, an old couple was beaten savagely in their home when they stumbled upon what looked like a burglar during the night. The surprise family reunion was broken as the betrayed child took to his arms the first thing he could reach, a lamp, and proceeded to cave in his father’s sternum despite his mother’s screams for mercy. The man had to be taken to the hospital: the woman survived with mere stitches. All the thief seemed to have taken were a birth certificate and the woman’s sanity. Days later, a foster home/adoption agency nearby burned down under mysterious conditions. The man returned to Philadelphia, head painted red. He was now the Fifth Horseman, Hate: the root from which Famine, Pestilence, War and Death sprang. To finalize this process of severing ties, Hate led several of his weak-willed disciples into what was unofficially known as “The Reich”, an apartment complex occupied by neo-Nazi activists. In twenty minutes, Hate had made clear that he was not a supporter of fascist policies or their Final Solution: his Final Solution rested in his fists, and was meant for everyone. Spending a few months in prison for inducing a riot, Hate then returned to Philadelphia, where he began to root himself in and gather a sort of a doomsday cult around himself. Everyone would die sooner or later: the Cult of the Fifth Horseman was merely there to help things along. To fund his cult and its activities, Hate began to sell his fists for whoever wanted them. Surprisingly, people running mixed martial arts competitions were interested in him, and through them several wrestling promoters got a sniff of a man who had the potential to be the heel of a century. Embracing these new businesses as a chance to beat up people legally, Hate gladly accepted the offerings, and stormed through gyms and schoolbuildings, leaving wrestlers and locales alike completely destroyed. Now, Hate is in the Atlantic Wrestling Club. Sleep and fly away… You’ll be tortured forever. Other Feds Several small independent federations Other Fed Awards None to mention Wrestling Style Aggressive brawler/powerhouse Finisher Move Eleventh Commandment Finisher Description Double Underhook Piledriver Finisher Setup Move White Man's Burden Setup Description Air Raid Crash AKA Kryptonice Krunch Other Moves Strikes: elbows, kneelifts, headbutts, basically utilizes his whole body as a weapon in the most effective way possible. Especially fond of running forearm smashes, mounted elbowsmashes and headbutts in the corner. Grappling moves: Hate favors simple suplexes and power moves, such as Belly to Belly Suplexes, Powerslams, Piledrivers and the like. However, when the match starts to drag on, Hate will become frustrated and go for more high-impact moves or one of his signature moves. Submissions: Hate's reserve of submission moves consists of several versions of the Camel Clutch and the Full Nelson, as well as a wide array of chokeholds of every imaginable kind. The Abomination Hold is the crowning jewel of his arsenal. Signature Moves: Death From Above (Top Rope Double Foot Stomp) Abomination Hold (Gory Guerrero Special, where Hate leans as far forwards as he can for maximum effect) Kamikaze Suplex (Top Rope Northern Lights Suplex where Hate comes crashing down with the opponent) Hatebred (Powerful Clothesline) Entrance Music ''Dracul Va Domni Din Nou In Transilvania' by Marduk
