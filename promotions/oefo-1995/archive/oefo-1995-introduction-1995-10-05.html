"[OEFO] Introduction"

5 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** OUTLAW EXTREME FIGHTING ORGANIZATION *** (heavily based on "Street Fighter: The Storytelling Game" by White Wolf Game Studio. Some written material is blatantly plagerized from the game book; but hell, I'm advertizing their game) - INTRODUCTION - From the alleys of New York to the hills of Malibu, from the frigid wastelands of Siberia to the humid jungles of Brazil, Outlaw Extreme Fighters gather to prove their fighting prowess. Driven by revenge, greed, glory, honor, or desperation, these men and women pit their combat skills against one another to see who is the best in the world. - SETTING - Most matches take place in the pyramid-shaped, OEFO Arena; on the mighty Mississippi River in Memphis, TN. So far, there are ten sanctioned fight areas in the OEFO Arena: Square Ring Octagonal Ring Cage (Square Ring) Cage (Octagonal Ring) Ultra-Cage (no ring) Alley/Street Scaffolding The Web Ground Zero The Explosion All of these will be explained, in detail, in a later post. any special requests by fighters will always be considered and are very welcome. Events outside the OEFO Arena are rare, but can happen. The only sanctioned fight that has taken place outside of the arena was the "Ultimate Survivor" match. This will also be explained, in detail, in a later post. - CHARACTER CREATION - This will be explained in letters to any interested players. If you are interested, mail [detail withheld] for an application. - GAME SYSTEM - Matches are played out using a modified "Street Fighter: The Storytelling Game" combat rules system. Regretfully, players will have little control over fighting the matches; but will have complete control over what their fighters say, who they fight, where they fight, and when they fight. Sneak attacks are allowed, but both combatants must completely agree for a sanctioned match can occur; except in rare cases where I (the gamemaster) decide that a match will take place. The gamemaster is always in complete control of the game, and all decisions are final. - CLOSING - We're going to have fun with this. That is what gaming is all about. On with it!!!!
