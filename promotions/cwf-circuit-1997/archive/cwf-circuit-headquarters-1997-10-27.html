*************************** CWF HEADQUARTERS ************************** ******************** I. Introduction from President II. Vital Information of CWF Operations III. Titles of the CWF IV. Venues of the CWF V. CWF Application I. <U>Presidents Address</U> Let me first thank you for your interest in the CWF. I formed the CWF in disgust of what has gone on to long in Fantasy Wrestling...biased Fed heads, and Fed heads who werent cut out to do the job. Myself, i'm sick of being told theirs gonna be a card out on Saturday, and when its Tuesday they finally email ya saying it's gonna be a little late! Here in the CWF, THAT WONT HAPPEN. It's my responsibility to bring you the best in online wrestling, and i'm gonna do just that. II. <U> Vital Information of CWF Operations</U> Roleplays: You must Roleplay to win! I expect, perhaps, 2 Roleplays a Week. When Roleplaying, please dont use this kind of a role Play: (Exp.) Wrestler: John Doe, i'm gonna kick your ass. That was a stirring roleplay, i know:) All i ask for is give a thought provoking, entertaining Roleplay...its the only way to go, if you want to reach the top. Closed Flashing League: For those of you who are not aware of what this is, this is when you send me your roleplays, and i send them out to the other handlers. That will keep us all from waking up in the morning, and having 100 emails. Their will be 2 days that i send the flashs out...most likely Sunday Night, before 8 pm Michigan time, and Wed. Night, before 8pm Michigan time. Wrestling Events: Their will be 2 live cards per week. Monday Night War- 5 live matchs, 3 dark matchs. Thursday Night Fights- 5 live matchs, 3 dark matchs. Saturday Showcase- 7 Dark Matchs III. <U>Titles of the CWF</U> The Most important part of any league, the GOLD! CWF World Title CWF U.S. Title CWF European Title CWF T.V. Title CWF World Tag Team Title <U>Venues of the CWF</U> This is where the cards will take place: Monday Nights: Detroit, Michigan Thursday Nights: Houston, Texas Saturday Nights: Seattle, Washington V.
