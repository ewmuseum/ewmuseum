BorderWars '97, Night One (part three)

26 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BORDER WARS '97 NIGHT ONE, PART THREE....... [We come back from the advertisements to a shot of "The Pro" Mike Woods sitting in his "cell", which appears to be somewhere at the back of the arena. Woods is sitting watching TV on a monitor which has been pulled up beside the cell. When he sees the monitor come onto a shot of himself, he turns and smiles, putting one finger in the air for "We're Number One". He yells out "We're coming for ya Dangerous Alliance!!".] BF: Mike Woods sure looks ready to take on all comers in The Gauntlet. I'm sure he's as eager to take part in that great event as we are to watch it. LVK: It must be hell having to sit there all alone and think about what's coming up. The anticipation must be killing these guys. [Then the shot changes to "Wildfire" Tommy Rage in his cell. Rage is sitting reading Pro Wrestling Illustrated, and doesn't even notice the cameraman.] SW: Mr Rage is obviously catching up on what those no-good journalists are saying about him. LVK: No-good journalists? This coming from a guy whose country's press hounded Princess Diana right until her death. SW: Uh....uh...... [Next up is Gary Albright, who is sitting back in a chair cracking his knuckles. He notices the cameraman and smiles, before going back to a serious look.] BF: Gary looks like a determined man as well....gee, that cell can hardly hold the big man. I wouldn't be surprised...... [Frankhauser is cut off in mid sentence as Albright spins around in surprise, and looks to the left of the camera. Then we see a huge dark figure loom out of the left of the shot.] BF: ......what the??? LVK: Who? [We now recognise that it's Scimitar.] LVK: Scimitar!! SW: Wonderful! [Scimitar has a long stick with him and he pokes it through the bars....] BF: Scimitar has some sort of stick.....is he going to poke Gary Albright to death? Ha ha!! [Albright grabs the stick, but then rears back in pain. A sharp "snap" is heard.] LVK: Huh?? OH MY GOD.......It's an electric cow prod!! I knew I'd seen it somewhere before!! He just shocked Gary!!! SW: This is marvellous! [Scimitar rams the prod into Albright several times, shocking him each time. Albright jumps about in pain.] BF: Absolutely disgusting!! Someone help the poor man. [We see security come running out of the right of the screen, and Scimitar is gone. Albright is rubbing his ribs, obviously sore from the shocks.] LVK: Gary's hurt.......what's wrong with that sicko? I've seen some deranged wrestlers in my time, but this takes the cake!! BF: Luckily security were able to prevent Scimitar from doing more damage....but who knows what damage those shocks have done to the big man from Montana. [The shot changes to the crowd in the arena, who are at fever pitch. We see signs saying "Are you ready......to enter the World of Scimitar?", "Sir William is a poof", and "Forget Tiger Woods.....Mike Woods is the real deal". We still hear the commentators, but the camera shot stays panning the crowd.] BF: I really am disgusted by what just happened - I hope Scimitar isn't left to get away with this scotch free. I would hope the NeCW management would take some action. But right now it's time for our next match, which is a "Survivor Rules" match. It will be The Brotherhood going up against Strange Company. Guys they met last week on Superstars in one od the oddest matches in NeCW history. The Brotherhood just didn't look good that night, Larry. LVK: No they didn't. As it has been reported lately in Rumor Central, there seems to be some internal problems in the Brotherhood. Whether this is true or not isn't clear, but Heep and Villa do not seem to agree as of late. SW: Problems or not, Strange Company is going to dominate them. This is just their excuse before they even get in the ring. It's sad. BF: I don't think that is the case. They haven't even responded to whether or not that rumor is true. Either way here they are, The Brotherhood! ==============================Border Wars '97======= ==Team elimination match== ==30 minute time limit== The Brotherhood (The Unknown, Uriah Heep and Jose "el Cuervo" Villa, w/ Ray MacDonald) vs Strange Company (Jack Strange, Velvet Taco and Phoenix "Firestorm" Huston) ==written by McGill================================== ("Hatred" by Man o War starts playing as Villa, Heep, and the Unknown enter the arena along with Ray MacDonald, their manager. Mixed Face/Heel Pop.) [Uriah Heep, the tallest of the three at 6'6", is wearing his usual black tights, while Villa is wearing all black tights, and a white mask with a drawing of a raven on each side. The smallest of the three, 5'10" The Unknown, is also wearing all black tights, with black boots, and a black mask that covers his entire face except his mouth.] LVK: Look at Villa shaking his head at the Unknown. Is he unhappy that Heep's music played or what. Heep hasn't acknowledged that motion, and I doubt he saw it. Now here is Strange Company! (The crowd decidely begins to boo as "Head Like a Hole" by Nine Inch Nails begins to blare out in the arena. Jack Strange leads his group down the aisle.) [Strange has on his now customary black tights, a leather left arm sleave that straps around his shoulders. His famous red and purple hair gleams in the lights. Huston, the tallest of his team, has on a black body suit, with the arms uncovered, and a black flack vest. He has his black hair in a flat top cut, and is very built. Last out is the smallest of the group, 6' Taco, who is wearing his purple velvet tights, and a Matador hat, but no mask, which he dispensed of long ago.] SW: Here come the real men in this match. BF: Strange with Phoenix Huston and Velvet Taco. This is going to be a good one. The Brotherhood can't be happy after getting doused with Hot Sauce and locked in the cells. LVK: Right you are, Bob. They really humiliated The Brotherhood last time out. One can only wonder what they have in store tonight. What is this? Heep seems to be having words with Villa. BF: Larry it looks like they are heated words too. Viila stays in the ring to start and Heep steps to the apron and onto the ground? There's the bell, this match has started and Heep is walking away! LVK: Is this an elimination or what? Heep looks at them half way down the aisle and then walks out of the arena! See if we can get Kevin Kash or someone to interview him backstage. I would like to know what is going on. SW: He has seen the light! He saw the beating ahead and left to save himself. BF: I doubt it. Villa has already begun against Huston. He is getting knocked around in the Strange Company corner. He got distracted by Heep leaving. You gotta wonder where this leaves The Brotherhood now that they are one short. Now the tag to Taco. LVK: Taco comes in and begins to work on the legs of Villa. He wraps up a spinning toe hold. Villa is grimacing in pain. Now it is a whip into the corner and Taco jumps. ..Villa ducks! Taco ends up stradeling the steel corner post. That's gotta hurt. SW: Taco need to gaurd his family jewels. He may want to have children someday. BF: Villa tags the Unknown and he comes in with about two chops in a row. Now a flurry of kicks to the stomach and lower back of Taco. Now a roundhouse to the head. What an impact. Taco almost did an entire flip before he landed. Taco manages to get out of the ring for safety. He is met by Straneg and Huston for what look like a strategy meeting. LVK: Taco back in the ring. The count was up to around 8. Taco immediately tags out to Strange. Strange and Taco both grab the Known and whip him into the ropes. They miss and Unknown hits them with a double clothesline. SW: Taco does need to leave or he will hurt his team. BF: True, Sir William. The count is going now, the ref has reached 7...8...9...Taco lunges, but not in time. He is out. Taco has been eliminated because he was in the ring too long after tagging out. The Unknown immediately goes after Strange, who was arguing the call with the ref. LVK: You would think a ring veteran like Strange would know to keep his focus on the men he is wrestling. Oh my, an eye gouge by Strange and a DDT! SW: Now that ingrate will get what is coming to him. BF: Strabge and Huston double team Unknown in their corner. Villa is barely standing. Look at this! (Ray MacDonald has taken his jacket off and rolled up his sleeves. He has stepped to the mat and is standing next to Villa.) LVK: Is the ref going to allow this? It looks like MacDonald has taken Heep's place! The ref is letting it go. Now Strange holds Unknown while Huston kicks at his mid section. Tag to Strange. BF: Strange misses with a lariat and gets hit with a legsweep. He flips up and goes after the Unknown. He catches him in a side suplex! Here is the Spinning Powerbomb! SW: He won't get up from that. LVK: You're right. There is the three count and the Unknown has been eliminated. Now Villa steps into the ring. Strange catches him in an inside cradle. He pulls the tights! 1...2...thre..NO! Villa manages to kick out. SW: Lucky chap. He won't last long. BF: Strange picks him up and a Running Powerslam! Villa is really absorbing some punishment now, Larry. LVK: Yes he is. Now a tag to Huston. Here is a side Suplex by Huston. And another. Now a vertical suplex. Oh my, did he get him up high or what? BF: That had to hurt. Now he has him in "The Operative" (Submission hold). MacDonald has entered the ring to break it up, but gets cut off by the referee. Strange comes in without a tag. The referee didn't see it. LVK: He asked about it and Strange nodded that they did tag. A tag to Huston, who climbs the turnbuckle. Strange sets him up for a spike piledriver. Huston jumps and DOWN GOES VILLA! What a devastating move. Here is the cover... (Referee counts to three and Ray MacDonald enters the ring.) SW: Now all they got is a Bruce Lee wannabe that can't even act like a good manager. This is going be easy. BF: MacDonald hits Huston with a roundhouse kick! He used to be a professional kickboxer, Sir William. Did you know that? SW: No, I wasn't aware of that fact. It makes no difference. It is two against one now. MacDonald hasn't got a prayer. LVK: Now Huston has a hold of Ray. Here comes Strange off the tope rope! Ray moved and Strange hit Huston! Now a series of kicks by MacDonald on Strange. Both men are down! Huston rushes at him and Ray ducks! BF: That sends Huston over the top rope and onto the floor. Here comes a moonsault! What a move! He landed it. 1..2..3!!! Ray MacDonald just pinned Strange! Here comes Huston and Ray rolls him up with a backslide! LVK: 1..2..3!!! It's over! Ray MacDonald wins it for The Brotherhood! Here comes Villa and Unknown from the back to celebrate. Wait they have something! (Villa and Unknown have Hot Sauce and are spraying it on Strange and Huston as they try to leave the ring.) SW: This is terrible. Do we have to watch this? BF: I don't see why not. This is just payback for last week on Superstars. haven't you ever heard of Karma, Sir William? LVK: Kama....some second rate wrestler in another federation isn't he? SW: Do be quiet Mr Van Keel....what you were saying Mr Frankhauser - I don't think this is what they had in mind when they made that word up. BF: Sure it is. What goes around, comes around. Reap the whirlwind Strange Company. LVK: <chuckles> Well they do deserve it. (Strange Company get backstage in a hurry and they are followed by security and by The Brotherhood, who are also followed by security to keep them apart.) ===================================================== [Camera changes to the three commentators at the broadcast booth.] BF: I'm sure The Unknown and Jose Villa are heading straight to look for Uriah Heep as we speak.....they'll want some answers. LVK: It looks like the rumour was true alright - Heep obviously doesn't want to be a part of that group anymore. I never did see him as a team player anyway. SW: You're right there....Uriah Heep doesn't belong in a group......unless it's a Psychiatric class that is. BF: Cut it out! Wait! What's that music I hear? LVK: No! It couldn't be! They didn't let him do it on a Pay-Per-View? SW: You bet they did! It's "Playboy" Ronnie D! ["I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said Fred starts up and "Playboy" Ronnie D walks out from behind the curtains. He stops and poses a bit. He's wearing a tuxedo since it IS after all a big occasion. He also has some round sun-glasses on. After posing for a little while, he dances and prances his way down the aisle. He stops, looks at a girl, and shakes his head and walks away! He hops up into the ring and has the ring announcer toss him the mic. There's a small thud from the mic as "Playboy" catches it. "Playboy" is greeted by his usual chorus of boos.] Playboy: All right! You can cut the music! ["I'm Too Sexy" cuts out.] LVK: How DOES this jerk get away with this? Playboy: I want you all to BUTTON UP! [The crowd starts booing louder.] Playboy: Since I'm obviously not going to get you to shut up, I'll just talk louder than you'll ever scream. I'm going to talk to you motor-head idiots about something very important. Me. Yes, about me and how I am going to simply DESTROY a certain Mexx tonight. Of course, I'm also going to have to talk to you about the current state of affairs in NeCW. So why don't I just get on with it now? [The Detroit crowd is really booing.] BF: "Playboy" is certainly getting a reaction here in Detroit. This crowd is really into BorderWars! Playboy: First off, I'll talk about the goings on around here. I like to save the best for last. So, some pea-brain calling himself "Terrence McGill" comes around here with his dead federation, the Zodiac Wrestling Federation -- known for it's wife-abusers, deadbeat drug addicts and other pleasant characters -- and expects us all to take him seriously and embrace this turn for the worse. I'm going to tell you nimrods once, and only once, that mergers never work. I can see it now: In six months, all of us will be sitting at our homes saying, "Whatever happened to that NeCW federation? Oh yes, now I remember!" And what will we remember? Terrence McGill, THAT'S what! I have never seen such a pitiful excuse for a loser as him. He makes ME work on a Thursday, when I will NEVER work, just so him and his buddy Clint... er... Fletch can get more money! As far as I'm concerned, Terrence McGill is a good-for-nothing low-life punk and he will be the END of NeCW. [The crowd boos some more.] LVK: Why I never -- SW: Ssssh! There's more! Playboy: But on to more important issues, like this rumor page I've been told about. Yeah, a friend of mine that's "wired" told me about it. So I logged on to the internet and entered "NeCW Rumorville." First of all, it's R-U-M-O-U-R, NOT R-U-M-O-R like some dimwits like to spell it. Real men use the letter U. But my real comment: What a piece of utter TRASH! I have never seen drekk like this. I've read super-market tabloids once or twice, but this is TEN TIMES WORSE! This numbskull has the nerve to print filth like, "Ronnie D is saying that he has been guaranteed the World title," and "Ronnie D hates Joe Williams." If he had any balls at all, he'd check his story with me first. That nerdy gimp asked me to do an interview, and I obliged, but rest assured that I NEVER would have had I known what kind of a dirty little weasel he is. I won't tell you who Rumor Guy is, but I know, and if that punk doesn't shape up -- I'll tell the world who he is and lose him his job! [The crowd starts booing again.] BF: I always thought Rumor Guy was doing an admirable job, but to each his own. Playboy: And now on to the subject of Mexx. This little coward hides behind his manager Darius and expects us to all respect and fear him. He can kick all the metal posts he wants, he can have that punk manager talk all he wants, but when it comes down to it -- I'm the superior being. I'm smarter than him, I'm more cultured than him, I'm richer than him and I'm better than him and any of you screwballs! He can practice Taekwondo or Judo or Karate or whatever that fighting style is, but he won't hang on to that strap for very long. The only way he beat Kain was by fluke, because Kain didn't give a damn about Mexx. Any old idiot can see that. Kain was in the ring thinking, "Why am I not facing 'Playboy' Ronnie D, who is ten times better than this jabronie?" Then, he got rolled up. 1... 2... 3. THAT is how Mexx became the interim champion until he inevitably has to hand the belt over to ME. [The crowd boos.] LVK: What arrogance! SW: I know! Isn't it wonderful? Playboy: You can consider me the champion as of RIGHT NOW! Me facing Mexx is just a formality. Now excuse me, I've had enough of looking at you animals! Hit the damn music. ["I'm Too Sexy" starts up.] BF: Well, that was certa -- Playboy: Oh, and I almost forgot! Mexx, Terrence, Fletch and all of you idiot fans can SUCK MINE! [The crowd explodes with boos.] BF: OH MY! That was UNCALLED FOR! ["Playboy" hops out of the ring. As he walks by the announcers' table, "Playboy" stops and thrusts his groin at Larry Van Keel.] LVK: That was just vulgar! ["Playboy" continues on his merry way, taunting fans and the like until he reaches the back. He stops, turns around, and thrusts his groin at the crowd. The crowd [Not surprisingly] starts booing even louder. The boos are coming so loud that you can't even hear the music as "Playboy" walks back through the curtains.] SW: Didn't you love it? BF: I certainly did not! And on that note we're going to throw to a commercial break.....don't go away.....
