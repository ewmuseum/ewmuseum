Closing our doors

15 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens on a conference room in a Detroit, Michigan hotel. There is a podium set up with a mass of microphones, and rows and rows of seats facing the podium. There are reporters, photographers and cameramen everywhere, and the noise from their talking forms a babble of sounds. On the wall behind the podium is the NECW logo.] [Into the room walks NECW President Fletch, who is wearing a plain black suit and red tie. He walks in behind the podium, and the gathered people quieten down.] Fletch: Thank you all for being here today, on a day which I must say has been the hardest of my life. As you will all have heard, NECW has closed, after a year in operation. The reason for this is problems I have had with my health. I recently had a bad turn due to recently-diagnosed diabetes, and as a result the doctors have ordered me to cut back on my duties and relax a bit more. That has forced me to close NECW as of this week, a situation I had hoped would never happen. [Fletch looks very upset, and wipes a tear from his left eye.] Fletch: I had the horrible task of telling all of the NECW employees and wrestlers the news this morning, and understandably there are some disappointed people. However, nearly all have reluctantly supported this move, and have shown great support for me in this day of darkness. I am supremely confident that most of the former NECW wrestlers will be successful wherever they go, and I have cherished the time spent working with them all. There are too many names to mention individually, but I wish them all the best of luck. I'll take some questions now..... [Hands fly into the air and reporters start calling out. Fletch points at one reporter, who stands up.] Reporter: Jenny McMahon, Wrestling Today. Mr Fletch, what will become of the proposed merger with the AeWF? Fletch: Well, unfortunately, that deal is no longer possible with the NECW closing. However, AeWF President [name withheld] has offered all former NECW wrestlers a place in his federation. Reporter 2: Joe Pideco, Detroit Daily News. Is this the last we will see of you in wrestling? Fletch: No way. While I'm being forced to step back from the daily grind of running a wrestling federation, I will still stay involved in the industry. Myself and [name withheld] will be running the World Cup of Wrestling, with events every month, and I will also be helping several up-and-coming wrestlers in terms of guiding their careers. Also, I hope to facilitate the odd independent wrestling card. Reporter 3: Mike Smith, Wrestling This Week. Do you expect other wrestling organisations to snap up the former NECW competitors? Fletch: They'd be fools if they don't. Guys like Kain, Chris Hopper, Harley Robinson, Simon McQuaid, Chris K, Jim Lewis jnr, Vampyro, and many others, could be champions in any organisation, in my opinion. I'd be surprised if they're not offered contracts by NECW's former rivals. Reporter 1: What do you say to those people who will see NECW closing as a sign of your failure? Fletch: I do feel very sad that NECW is closing, and I do feel that I am failing the wrestlers. However, the support and well wishes I have received from them in the last 24 hours has been amazing. And to anyone who says that I've failed, I say this - I ran a worldwide wrestling organisation for 12 months. I attracted some of wrestling's most talented competitors. My staff and I put out over 70 wrestling television shows, and nine hugely successful pay-per-view events, as well as many other shows and events. We provided the platform that launched the careers of men like Jinsei Shakanuzi and "Wildcat" Jim Lewis jnr, men who were unknown here in the US before they became a part of our "family". Does that sound like failure to you? It sure doesn't to me. Reporter 4: Tom Landel, ESPN. Can we expect to see you running a wrestling organisation anytime in the near future? Fletch: [smiles] who knows? It won't be in the next few months, but if my health improves, I might open another wrestling federation. It'd be much smaller than NECW, but it's a possibility. Well folks, I'm afraid I have to cut this short right now, because I'm still not feeling 100% healthwise. Thank you all for coming. [Fletch walks out of the room as we fade to black] ---------------------------------------- You're the man Fletch........ You are the man. -"The Machine" Chris K ---------------------------------------- Agreed. Godspeed and looking forward to seeing you back in the ring again, Fletch!
