NeCW now recruiting: a sample card

22 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi wrestling fanatics! Fletch here, president of the NeCW (National e-mail Championship Wrestling). We're currently recruiting - made-up wrestlers only, handled by people with plenty of time to put into their efforts. We're a mixture of booked and simmed, and have great action! Attached below is an example of one of our cards, and if you like what you see, go to our website at: http://www.angelfire.com/ma/Clunk/NeCW.html and fill out an application form. We take anyone! Anyway, here's the sample card: ### # ######### # # # # # # @@@@@@@@ # # # # # # # @ @ # # # # # # # @@@@@@@@ # # # # # # # @ # # # # # # # @@@@@@@@ ######### ########### THE NEW BREED IN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING ///////////////////////////////////////////////////////////// .....................FRIDAY FRACAS........................... \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\ [The screen fills with images of NeCW stars in action, as "The Final Countdown" by Europe plays. We see Gary Albright delivering the full-nelson suplex, "Franchise" Jim Meedman posing for the crowd, "Wildfire" Tommy Rage swinging punches at an opponent, and many other scenes, before the screen stops on The Executioner holding up the National title belt after his cage match victory over The Judge. Then the screen changes to a wide-angle shot of a packed house in an arena, with fans going wild. The words "Earlier this evening in Cincinatti, Ohio" appear at the bottom of the screen. The final screen cut shows us a studio, with Bob Frankhauser and "Living Legend" Al Rich.] BF - Hello, everyone, and welcome once again to Friday Fracas....and yes, that shot you just saw was a packed out arena in Cincinatti.....the NeCW is on the road. AR - Yeah, Booby...we're taking our action to the people....not that I care about the people....but yes we're going all around this country to show the fans what real wrestling is. Forget that other crap you see on television, with stripping champions, washed up old guys and plenty to talk but no action.....this here's where the real thing's at! BF - Yes indeed! And have we got a show for you tonight! We've got "Ice Cold" Joe Williams in action, along with Gary Albright, The Punisher, the Megapowers and a whole host of other stars. But that's not the end of it - our main event....falls count anywhere.....the ominous Psycho Dudes up against the Canadian Rockers - Justin Finley, and Lance Hart, who makes his return from injury. AR - Yeah, injury at the hands of the Psychos! That Hart's a dumb Canadian fool - why would he want to get back in the ring with the two behemoths who put him in hospital in the first place? BF - Because he wants revenge, and to help him exact that revenge he has brought in fellow Canadian high-flyer "Jumping" Justin Finley....to reform their old tagteam the Canadian Rockers. These guys were Canadian champs Al, and they're primed to give the Psycho Dudes are good run for their money! AR - Phooey! It's gonna be like Thanksgiving at my place - the Psychos'll be carving them up like turkey, and breaking bones like there ain't no tomorrow! BF - And like I said, that match will be a falls-count-anywhere match, which means pinfalls or submissions can be done anywhere in the arena. Now, the other singles matches have an important bearing on the overall championship picture here at NeCW, because on Monday, live in Nashville, Tennessee, the top three contenders for the National belt will fight it out in a triangle match. Whoever wins that match will get a shot at National champ The Executioner at April Avalanche, coming tomorrow week... AR - Oh yeah! The Executioner sure was impressive in winning that title, and I think he's in for a long reign. Look who he's got to contend with - Tommy Rage? Ha! Gary Albright? Creampuff! Jim Meedman? Give me a break! BF - Despite what you say Al, I think The Executioner is going to have a tough time holding onto that strap - there are any number of wrestlers here who could beat him for the title. Anyway let's check out the results of our two preliminary matches: * "Maximum Karnage" Harvey Kristopher bt Masked Knight by pinfall in 3:47. * The Superpowers bt Joe Franks/Phil the Maniac in 6:34 when Kent Powers pinned Franks. Okay, now let's talk about our first main match of the night....pitting big Gary Albright against The Punisher.. AR - I've made it clear I like the Punisher, whereas that Albright's a big girl! BF - We're going to join the action with the match only a couple of minutes old...... [cut to tape] ********************************************************* Regular match, one fall only. Gary Albright vs The Punisher (w/ JJ Styles) BF - Gary Albright with a belly-to-belly suplex, here's the count....one, two, shoulder up!! Albright now whips The Punisher into the ropes, but The Punisher executes a flying clothesline! AR - Take that Albright, you big wuss! BF - Punisher now nails him with a short clothesline, and runs into the ropes, but Albright sends him airborne courtesy of a big backdrop. Punisher's writhing in pain, holding his back. Albright's going for the pin....... one, two, kickout. AR - Hey, who's this coming down the aisle. BF - The fans have reacted to somebody.....it's "Maximum Karnage" Harvey Kristopher!!!! AR - All right! Harv' my man! He's coming to keep an eye on Not-bright no doubt! BF - Yes, it's been clear that Kristopher doesn't like Albright - he resents the fact Albright beat him in his first NeCW match... AR - Albright cheated! Everyone saw it! BF - He did not.....stop lying Al! [Kristopher approaches the broadcast table, and high-fives the Living Legend. He then pulls up a chair and sits watching the match.] Kristopher is just pulling up a seat....he's keeping an eye on the action in the ring. But I doubt he'll be sitting there for the whole match.....I've got a feeling he's going to be in the thick of the action before long. AR - No way, Harv's just here scouting his number one rival, just like any good wrestler should do. BF - Meanwhile, in the ring, Albright is taking charge. He catches Punisher in a belly-to-belly suplex! He's going for the cover.....one, two......NO! Albright now sees Kristopher sitting at ringside, and he's leaning over the ropes questioning Maximum Karnage... AR - Lookout! Punisher rolls him up...he he! BF - The cover........one........two.......thr....NO! AR - Another case of a slow count there Frankfurter! BF - The Punisher throws Gary Albright into the ropes......LOOKOUT! Kristopher just pulled down the top rope, and Albright has gone crashing out of the ring! AR - The best thing is the ref didn't see it! Ha! BF - They're brawling on the floor.....OW! Albright just flung The Punisher head-first into the ringsteps!! Did you hear the clang as his head hit? Wait......JJ Styles is up on the ring-apron, distracting the referee......Kristopher comes over with that chair.....BANG! He nailed Albright with the chair!! AR - Goodnight Not-bright! BF - Punisher and Kristopher hurl Albright back into the ring, and the ref's back on the job now......here's the cover....one.....two.....thr....NO! The Punisher now with a fistdrop to the forehead. AR - Just a matter of time baby! Albright's done for now! It's alright being big and strong, but when you've got nothing left in the fuel tank, goodnight! BF - WAIT! Punisher just went for a legsweep faceslam, but Gary Albright countered it with a faceslam of his own. Albright's going for a half Boston, a move popular in shoot wrestling. But no, the Punisher manages to reach the ropes. Albright picks up Punisher and flings him into the ropes.....belly-to-belly suplex!!! AR - No, no!! BF - Stan Lee-Smith counts....one.....two......thr....wait a minute! JJ Styles broke the count by pulling his man out under the ropes!! The ref's going over to speak to Styles. HEEEEY! Kristopher's on the top rope, where the ref can't see....HE'S GOT THE CHAIR!!! AR - Do it, do it baby!! Finish him for once and for all!! BF - No, the ref's turning around, and he's seen Kristopher. He's warning him not to do it....Kristopher jumps!! He's just jumped off the top strand, and brought that chair across the head of the unsuspecting Gary Albright!! Albright's down in a big way!! [bell sounds DING DING DING] BF - Punisher is now joining Kristopher in beating Albright, as the bell sounds.....NO! Kristopher with that chair down across the throat of Albright. The ref tries to stop it, but he gets a big punch from JJ Styles, which sends him sprawling. This is absolutely disgusting!! AR - Ah, shut up weener! This is exactly what Albright deserves. Go Harv', go Punisher!! BF - I can't believe you're condoning this! [crowd pops big time as some more wrestlers run to the ring] BF - LOOKOUT! Here comes TNT and the Superpowers to break things up.....The Punisher and Harvey Kristopher are fleeing back to the dressingrooms. Albright's hurt....this is something nobody wanted to see... AR - I did! BF - Would you be quiet! TNT and the Powers brothers are helping the fallen Albright back to the lockerrooms....he seems alright, but he sure is groggy.. Ring announcer - your winner.....in a time of 6:44........as the result of a disqualification......Gary Albright! [some cheers, but mainly boos from the crowd, who are upset at what just went on] [cut back to studio] ************************************************** BF - Yes indeed....a very sorry sight there, and let me say before you say anything Living Legend, that was disgusting, and don't you try and condone it! AR - Whatever you say Booby! He he he! Poor widdle Albwight, got beat up by da bad men! He he he he he he! BF - Let me say folks, I think things are starting to get out of control in this federation, and unless president Fletch starts fining or suspending these hooligans, things aren't about to get any better! But enough about that, because it's time to move on to our next match-up, pitting two very different men against each other. On the one hand we have "Playboy" Tony Flint, who is new to NeCW, but has won many titles in his time, and on the other hand we have the hottest young sensation in NeCW at the moment...."Wildfire" Tommy Rage. What'ya say Al, two very different wrestlers there? AR - Of course they're different! Rage is a pandering fan-lover wuss, and Flint is a man of class. I'll give Rage credit in that he moves well for a guy his size, but Tony Flint is a legend, and that Jessica......man! BF - Don't get started on that! We're going to join the match between Tony Flint and Tommy Rage about seven minutes into the contest..... [cuts to tape] ***************************************************** Regular match, one fall only. "Playboy" Tony Flint (w/ Jessica) vs "Wildfire" Tommy Rage (w/ "Perfect" Paul Richman and Jason Havok) BF - Flint and Rage are outside the ring, trading punches.....neither man is giving an inch......no Rage's punches seem to be having more effect. AR - Things could get interesting out here. BF - Rage knocks Tony Flint into the ringsteps, but Flint nails him with a kneelift, and throws him back into the ring. Flint now with a crucifix-like move......one, kickout. Flint comes down hard with an elbowsmash! AR - You'll notice Flint doesn't let up for a minute - when he knows he's got the advantage he really goes in for the kill! BF - Flint throws Tommy Rage out of the ring. Stan Lee-Smith counts: one, two, Tommy Rage reenters the ring. Tommy Rage hits Tony Flint with an elbow! He places Flint on the turnbuckle.......superplex!!! Tommy Rage whips Tony Flint into the turnbuckle....Flint comes back, but is met with an elbow! Rage now takes him down with a short clothesline. AR - Look at this....Rage is acknowledging the fans instead of going for the cover......this shows that idiot's lack of experience. BF - Rage now with a knee to Flint's leg, and now he's clamping on a step-over-toe-hold.........Flint escapes to the ropes. AR - Rage is obviously concentrating on that right leg, and it looks like the Playboy is hobbling! BF - Tommy Rage goes for a snap suplex, but NO!......... Flint counters it with a vertical suplex! Flint misses with a clothesline.......he's obviously not comfortable on that leg.......Rage is hoisting him up!!! Gorilla Press!!!! One, two, in the ropes... AR - Oh no, it's not looking good for Tony, his leg is really hurting now! BF - Rage whips him into the ropes, and executes a legsweep, taking Flint to the mat. You're right Al, Flint is almost ready to give this one away because of that leg! Rage picks him up and brings Flint's knee down across his own knee.......ouch, that's gotta be hurting something bad! AR - Damn! Come on Jessica honey, do something. Don't just stand there and look pretty! BF - Rage is moving in for the kill, he knows he's got Flint hurt! He's applying some sort of double-underhook........DOUBLE UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER!!!!! Rage picks up the prone Flint........he's going for the Firecracker!!! There it is, his inverted DDT!! The cover.......one......two.....three!!! [huge pop from crowd. Rage gets to his feet and thrusts both arms in the air, to a roaring ovation] AR - No way! Tony Flint has obviously hurt his leg big time, and that punk Rage didn't have the common courtesy to stop the match so he could receive medical assistance! BF - He was just winning the match, and you can't tell me you would have ever stopped a match because an opponent was hurting! AR - I would have! BF - [sarcastically] Oh, okay. [serious] Let's go to the ring announcer...... Ring announcer - The winner.......by pinfall in 10:53........Tommy Rage!!! [big pop from crowd] [cuts back to studio] *************************************************** BF - Another win for Tommy Rage keeps his unbeaten streak going, and basically assures him of a spot in the triangle contenders match on Monday night. AR - Rage is a punk, that's all I'll say! BF - At April Avalanche next Saturday night, Rage will be joining his Perfect Alliance stablemates in a War Games match against Death Runs Cold. Al, there's no love lost between these two groups. AR - Why don't you just say they hate each other. These guys want to tear each other apart, and at April Avalanche they'll get the chance to in a thunderdome cage. I can't wait to see the Perfect Alliance taking a whipping! BF - Now, as of yet Death Runs Cold haven't got a fourth member for that match, because The Executioner will be defending his National title later in the night. Any ideas who they might be bringing in? AR - I know who they're bringing in, but I'm not going to tell you Booby, or the morons watching tonight. You'll all just have to wait and see, but I'll say this - it'll be a major surprise, no doubt about that! BF - I don't believe you really know, but it'll be a wild match anyway. Earlier tonight we got a sneak preview of that match when the Megapowers, Jim Meedman and John Crowe, took on the Masters of Mayhem Mash and Smash... AR - Yeah, this was a wild one... BF - We join the action with the match a little over eight minutes old, and Jim Meedman and Mash in the ring. Take a look at this... [cuts to tape] ********************************************************* Regular tagteam match, one fall only Megapowers ("Franchise" Jim Meedman and "Iceman" John Crowe, w/ "Perfect" Paul Richman and Jason Havok) vs Masters of Mayhem (Mash and Smash, w/ Mr Cremation) BF - Meedman places Mash on the turnbuckle, and catches him in a double underhook superplex. Stan Lee-Smith counts: One, two, shoulder up. Meedman is now going for a spinebuster slam....no, Mash counters it with a swinging neckbreaker. AR - Mash and Smash are really going to work on the necks and shoulders of the Megapowers, I think that's a big part of their game plan. BF - Could well be...Mash with a chop, and another...both slapping across Meedman's chest. Mash throws Meedman out of the ring now, and goes out after him. He nails him with a neck snap and goes for a flying lariat, but Meedman ducks and counters it with a fist to the midsection. Meedman back into the ring and Mash follows him back in. AR - Meedman really needs to tag if his team has any chance. BF - Mash flings Meedman into the ropes...he goes for a clothesline...Meedman ducks.....Meedman executes a Hotshot! Both men are stunned......can Meedman make it to his corner....he's trying..he made it! The Iceman's really going to town now.....he nails Mash...he nails Smash....bodyslams for both.....a double clothesline....Smash is sent flying to the outside. AR - Come on Mash and Smash - kill 'em!! BF - Crowe whips Mash into the ropes, but Mash reverses it. Mash misses with a shoulderblock, and both men are hit with a double clothesline. Both men to their feet now....Crowe runs into the ropes, but hey! Mr Cremation just tripped John Crowe! Here comes Jason Havok around to Mr Cremation's side of the ring.....Mr Cremation runs, but no Paul Richman is coming from the other side....they've got him! AR - Leave him alone! Come on ref, disqualify them! BF - Smash comes running over to help his manager....but the Franchise has got him, and they're brawling at ringside. Havok has hold of Mr Cremation, and Paul Richman just gave him a big roundhouse right......Mr Cremation's out to it!! AR - That's not fair - two on one! BF - Death Runs Cold haven't been playing fair ever since they joined NeCW, I guess the Perfect Alliance have just decided to fight fire with fire! Meanwhile, in the ring the Iceman is dominating Mash. He whips Mash into the corner, and comes crashing in with an avalanche. Crowe whips Mash into the ropes, but misses with a clothesline. NO! He nailed it on the second attempt! AR - Ha! Smash just laid out Meedman with a ringside table.....the Franchise just became roadkill! BF - Mr Cremation is continuing to fight with Paul Richman, and the ref's coming over to try and gain some control. Meanwhile, Smash has climbed back into the ring.....he just nailed an unsuspecting Iceman with a clothesline to the back of the head! AR - Yeah! Do it, do it! BF - Smash has Crowe up in the powerbomb position........they're going for the Last Rites! AR - Yes, yes, yes!! BF - Mash comes off the top rope...he spikes the Last Rites powerbomb! The ref's turned back around......Jason Havok is trying to get in the ring to break it up.....the cover.....one......two......THREEEEE!!!! Havok arrived too late! The Masters of Mayhem have won it! [stunned silence in the crowd, and then booing starts] AR - This is great! Old French-fry is having a snooze at ringside, while his partner get's his Last Rites in the ring! Megapowers? Pah! BF - Jason Havok is clearing out Mash and Smash, he looks angry......Mr Cremation looks particularly ruffled as he joins his men and leaves. Once again the Megapowers have been denied....but once again a match between the Perfect Alliance and Death Runs Cold has turned into utter chaos! It look s like the only real way to settle this feud is in the thunderdome cage at April Avalanche. Ring announcer - your winners.....in 12:35.......Mash and Smash....the Masters of Mayhem! [loud booing] AR - Death Runs Cold once again prove they are better than the Imperfect Alliance! The War Games match at April Avalanche will go much the same as tonight Booby, with Mr Cremation and the boys coming away with the win! BF - We'll see! [cuts back to studio] ******************************************************** BF - So yes, more chaos in a Perfect Alliance/Death Runs Cold matchup! Is there a more intense feud in the NeCW right now! AR - I doubt there is - this isn't a feud, it's a WAR! BF - Okay folks, don't go away, because we'll be right back after these messages......
