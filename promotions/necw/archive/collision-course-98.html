Umpala attacked from the beginning and never let up. He was so relentless that he literally had to be pulled off of Gemini by the referee. Umpala nailed a monster frankensteiner and picked up a quick pin. "The Devastator" Kurt Castle -vs- Big Ed Jenkins Winner: Castle by pinfall Time: 1:34 Highlights: Big Ed got knocked off of his feet early and was never able to stand again. Castle nailed a missile dropkick and then began to use mat techniques to keep the larger opponent down. The last chance that Big Ed had to stand up was snapped by a small package that ended the match. Great win for Castle. Quality of Life -vs- Law & Order Winner: Quality of Life by submission Time: 6:19 Highlights: This one was a picture of how good the tag scene will be getting in the near future. Quality of Life worked well together and managed to get this win in handy fashion. Whole Life knocked Law out of the ring and followed him out. His partner, Universal Life, then clamped an ankle lock on Order and gained a submission victory. This was impressive because it showed that they don't always need the "Death Certificate" move to win a match. Look for these two to rise up the ranks in the coming weeks! Jimmie Trent -vs- Keith "Rockshade" Schiano Winner: Schiano by pinfall Time: 7:06 Highlights: This was a decent match despite Jimmie Trent's faulty flying moves. Trent missed with more than one top rope dive and even missed a dropkick on the outside of the ring. Trent didn't give up easily as he did nail a few of those high risk moves he is so fond of. Unfortunately, Schiano was ready for most of them and had a counter. He powerbombed Trent after a failed head scissor move. Then he set him up and nailed his infamous "Schiano Suplex" to get the pin. The Spellbinder -vs- "The Machine" Chris K Winner: Spellbinder by countout Time: 8:48 Highlights: Spellbinder really went to work on the Machine in what turned out to be a nasty match. During the match, they went to the outside floor area. They were brawling when Chris K began to nail Spellbinder with a chair. Spellbinder returned fire--literally! Spellbinder turned and threw a fireball in Chris' face and then climbed into the ring. Bahner had been distracting the referee and now was ranting around the ring about how Spellbinder is "coming for Mexx." The referee counted to ten and the match was ended as paramedics came out to assist the young wrestler. Chris was released with no major damage. He was very lucky about the distance and angle that the fire was thrown at him. After the match, Interim president McGill expressed orders that Spellbinder was not to be in the building from that point on because he is a danger to every wrestler in NeCW.
