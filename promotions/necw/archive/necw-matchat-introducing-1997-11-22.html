Already we've seen top names like Vampyro, "The Player" Brian Fury, Jacob Skar and Don Ford, and Brandon Charm sign to wrestle in NeCW. Tell me about this first person - "The Machine" Chris K.... F: Sure. Chris has been plying his trade in the smaller regional organisations for a while now, and I saw him wrestle a few weeks ago. I was immediately impressed, and offered him an NeCW contract. He jumped at the chance to come and take on "the big boys". KK: Let's hear from "The Machine"..... Brokiro-san, come out please! [Brokiro Harakili comes out of the man WC and goes over to the tabel. He asks for four beers. He sits up and begins to talk.] Brokiro: Hi everybody. I am Harakili. I am sure that many superstars will beat me, but I want to try it, so I am waiting for challenges. is there anyone who would like to fight me in my debut match? I will fight everyone. Jinsei: Hey, nobody kicked my a[bleep]s. and I think that I am able to defend myself, against anyone, pro-wrestling or not, I am a FIGHTER. This interview is over.
