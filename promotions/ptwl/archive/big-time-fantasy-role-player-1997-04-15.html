^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v United States Pro Wrestling ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v Hello everyone. I'm [name withheld] < [detail withheld] >, and recently, I have decided to return to the realm of leading and running my own E-Mail Wrestling League. The league will not be your run of the mill e-wrestling league, as most leagues tend to be league mixed with a great amount of different guys, each handling several different wrestlers and having some wrestlers be on a totally different level than others in terms of where they belong and so forth, so to try to make a good amount of people on the same level and provide a playing field that seems to be equal and closer knit, I provide the NEW alternative to the doldrums of your everyday e-wrestling league. With this, I give you... United States Pro Wrestling or USPW. I'm going to try to keep this league up to par with matches and cards I can keep up with and attempt to get out on time, but remember, this is ALL supposed to be fun and a game, so have fun with it, and try not to take things too seriously. Thanks for checking this out, and I hope you'll check United States Pro Wrestling out too! ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v In ThE BeGiNiNg (Its gets sorta confusin`.) ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v After Dukex22!!! abrupt departure from the Prime Time Wrestling League, and his throwing of ALL of his stocks, his bonds, his recent money and funds gained from the purchase of the ULTIMATE!!! Wrestling Alliance and approximately 50% of the PTWL, Shiloh J. Mount, former head of ULTIMATE!!! and scapegoat used as an excuse for the PTWL heads for the actions of Dukex22!!!, has attempted to bring exciting, action packed, fun filled wrestling action to the fans, but with a little less funding than he had previously with the ULTIMATE!!! organization, but with the progress and growingness of the league that will definitaly change. Shiloh has now entrusted part of the federation's backing to friend and announcing partner, Dean Wright and long time close friend, Taylor Phillips. Now, with the resources of the three collectively pooled together, they have a chance to produce a nationally oriented product with action NOT Only for the Hard-core fans, but for small children as well. After some ridicule as to the morals behind ULTIMATE!!! and its former stars, Mount and Company have quickly formulated that ALL shows will by TV M14, for Mature Audiences of 14 or older, unless supervised with an adult. This is just in case things get out of hand or go above and beyond the limitations that were expected or emotions run too highly. The action will try to remain at a TV PG. rating, however. ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v The Skinny ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v United States Pro Wrestling. Wrestling that will LIKELY take place around in the United States, and sometimes travel across the border to the eastern side of Canada. The federation started in Milford, Pennsylvania, a small town near the New Jersey/New York/ Pennsylvania Tri-State border. As an independent organization, the USPW hopes to stick to smaller arenas to try to produce sell out crowds from that atmosphere before even THINKING of any type of expansion. We want to try to make sure EVERYONE is involved, first, with roleplays. I want at (League MINIMUM) LEAST one a week, hopefully you guys will give me a WHOLE lot more. "Strats" or "Strategies" of how your wrestler will wrestle, or perhaps attack another are also something we will need. Strats are basically the way we get angles to start working in e-wrestling, and I hope you all will remain constant in informing me of the types of angles you will be playing and so forth. Strats can usually (HINT, HINT) be accompanied by some speaking from your wrestler(s) out to the crowd and so forth. Interviews for the card are _ALSO_ very important. Sometimes, these can make or BREAK you in terms of decisions on the matches. Interviews can be sometime BEFORE the match, pre-"taped" interviews before the card is scheduled to go "on the air," or even an In Ring Interview. All interviews will be conducted by Dean Wright (a smart ass, though intelligent in broadcast journalism) or Taylor Phillips (who is more of a gutsy type, and is NOT to be pushed around). This is all in good fun, and will hopefully add dimension to the league, and your characters around, and guys, DON'T push the announcers around in interviews, okay?!? ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v Belts (You know, Championships) ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v In an effort to make sure the belts that are SUPPOSED to be the most important, REMAIN most important, we will introduce championships AS we need them. The first title will be the USPW Heavyweight Championship, to be defended once every other week, if not every week. The title is supposed to be the MOST prestigious of all championships in United States Pro Wrestling, and the focus will for it to remain that way. If tag teams tend to arise and show up, and we have a fairly competitive division of maybe seven or eight, we will add USPW Heavyweight Tag Team Championship Belts to our fold, but only when that becomes necessary, so I encourage you to enter a team into United States Pro Wrestling. ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v Cards (You know, the match things) ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v United States Pro Wrestling will be holding ONE card a week to start off, USPW Stampede, and it will occur on Sunday Nights. The first card will have THREE matches, and we'll continue to have three matches on every single card from then on, unless we need some extra matches or there is something special to take place on the card. As USPW grows in prominence, we'll not only be adding matches to the cards, but hopefully extra cards as well. I might even start to send out some information on "House Cards" where we will be seeing some matches that took place, but were not televised on the USPW`s weekly broadcast. These cards will hopefully be action packed and WELL worth the week between to watch (or read, for that matter)! Hopefully, after we get things going, the feedback will be positive and we can expand the cards a little bit. ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v "Booking" ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v As mentioned above, ALL matches will depend on _LEAGUE PARTICIPATION_, basically the guy who seems the most dedicated and works to hardest by putting out the _BEST_ and most _ORIGINAL_ stuff. Monotony is boring, and it drags, so be original. In order to win, you have to be different, because LIFE is different... so this is ROLEPLAY based along with some other participation.
