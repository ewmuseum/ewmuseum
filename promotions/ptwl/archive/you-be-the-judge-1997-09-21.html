You be the judge! I was jobbed unfairly yet again!

21 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You be the judge. Which RP is better? The first one or the second one? Keep in mind that the handler that wrote the 2nd RP is good friends with the organization's president. Read on and give me honest feedback. ============================================================ [Fade in.] [Clinton, NY. 8:31 PM. Camera swirls around a few times and slowly zooms in on RCW professional wrestler, [name withheld], standing behind a concession table. A long banner hangs from the table that reads "Robert Championship Wrestling" and hefty looking cardboard boxes are stacked on the table. Brigano slides a few boxes out of the way to give the camera a better view of him. He wears a loose off white flannel shirt along with a pair of loose fitting black jeans and a leather belt. He grins at the camera and begins speaking.] [name withheld]: Eric Cooper, my friend, you are a fool. I put on your interview in the corner of my personal training center for a little motivation. And motivation is just what it gave me. You made a complete and utter ass out of yourself, Coop, and if you beleive the words you spoke, I honestly feel bad for you. You said something about never giving up your title. That's a crock of shit. You know as well as I do that when the Pay Per View rolls around, your ass is mine and so is your title. Andru Edwards, you're involved, too. But you're the least of my concerns, now. Edwards, Cooper, you two are so over rated that it sickens me. I'm the most under-rated wrestler in the sport and that's the God's honest truth. I'm the man that came out of knowhere with very little experience and took the RCW by storm. I'm the man who defeated Submarine in his 3rd professional wrestling matchup, and I'm also the man with the perfect combination of brains and brawn to knock you right out of your cheap O'Neal boots. [Brigano smiles.] And I also find it ironic that none of you RCW wrestler have your own personality. You're all emulations of big time wrestling stars. Well I've only got one thing to say about that ... it makes me sick. Anybody that can't act the way they are deserves to be beat and deserves to be left in a pile of their own blood, piss and puke. [Brigano reaches into the box in front of him and pulls out an action figure. It appears to be Rex Watson, he tosses it acrossed the empty hallway. He pulls out a handfull of other figures and weeds out three. Andru Edwards, Eric Cooper and himself.] [name withheld]: Now if I can have your attention for another minute or so, fans, I'd like to give you a little prelude to the Pay Per View. [Brigano grabs the Andru Edwards figure, rips it out of its package and slams it down onto the table.] First on my list, "the Franchise" Andru Edwards. [Brigano twists the arm a few times until it snaps off like a twig.] Not too much effort here. [He bangs the action figure on the table about three or four times, it breaks into peices.] See, Andu Edwards is a plastic wrestler. He's fairly breakable but unfortunatly for his wife, he has no warantee, unlike the action figure. Just like I did to his miniature likeness, I will break Andru Edwards, leaving only peices of him behind. He's not too much trouble. [Brigano rips the Eric Cooper figure out of it's package and hits a button on the back.] Action Figure: I'm gonna kick your ass! I'm the RCW champ! I'm gonna kick your ass! I'm the RCW champ! [Brigano grabs the figure and slams it on the table a few times, and whips it at the wall as hard as he can, it breaks in a couple peices. Slowly, it stops it's talking as the camera zooms down at it. It's destroyed.] [name withheld]: Just like the real Coop, this one's all talk, too. I broke him, and in just a week I'll break the real thing. And then ... [Brigano pulls his likeness out of it's package and straps on the included rubber RCW title belt.] this is what you'll see standing in the center of the ring. [name withheld] and his RCW title. And that's a promise ... bank on it. I'll see you soon, Coop. I'll see you soon Edwards, and I trust I'll see the title as well. [Fade out.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------- [Camera fades in on a big mansion off of the coast of the New Jersey Shore. The camera is just sitting outside of a big gate that says COOPER ESTATE in nice italic letters. The camera then rolls up the drive way. It goes for about a mile until coming up to the big house. The house is HUGE! The camera then goes into the backyard and shows all of the features in the backyard, such as an Oylmpic Sized Swimming Pool, a tennis court, and a basketball Court. The camera then continues on its journey on the Cooper Estate and comes to a little house about a half of a mile away from the mansion. On the outside it says PRIVATE GYM! The camera man then knocks on the door. Then you can here a voice scream COME IN!!! The camera then goes in and sees a man running on a treadmill. The man is seen to be the RCW World Champion, ERIC COOPER!!! He is dressed in a pair of Adidas warm up pants, and he has no shirt on. There is sweat dripping down his pech fuzz shaven forehead. He then turns it off and walks to a refrigator and grabs a Poland Spring Water. He then walks over to a table and sits down.] Eric Cooper: You know something, we are just over 3 days away from the big RCW Pay Per View One Night Stand. The night, when I put my RCW World Title on the line against [name withheld] and The "Franchise" Andru Edwards. Fellas, we are the 3 biggest stars in the RCW so Barnes the idiot that he is thinks to himself, "Put them in the Main Event, nothing but money and ratings!" Well Barnes, did it ever cross your mind that I CAN KICK THE ASS OF EITHER ONE OF YOU POTHETIC LOSERS!!! All you guys do is knock on the door of Barnes, and beg for title shots, and cry to him. Well Edwards, your the biggest cry baby between the two of you. I am sick and tired of your whining to Barnes. Edwards, when I want something I dont whine about it. I TAKE IT!!! Just ask Barnes about that little COOPER CUTTER!!! Edwards, at One Night Stand I am not only going to end all of your whining I am going to end your career YOU STUPID PIECE OF SHIT!!! Now [name withheld]. Briggs, me and you have NEVER had the chance to meet in the ring. As a matter of fact, the first time I met you was when you _BEGGED_ me to join PTWL, or CHAOS!!! or whatever! Brigano, I have some respect for you now. And I am a very hard person to get respect out of. But I am telling you this, in the match this coming weekend. You play fair and square like a good little boy, then you still got my respect! And I dont really care if you respect me or not because either way, I am still going to kick your ass. So Brigano, save yourself the humiliation. And dont bother messing with Cooper, because who knows when Ill snap next! [He gets up and begins walking around.] Eric Cooper: I wonder how Robert Barnes is doing after that unfornuate incident last night in MSG. I sure his neck isnt broken or nothing. [He then walks into a door that says on the outside TRAINING ROOM. He then walks in and a man is seen standing there in a pair of jeans and a T-Shirt.] Eric Cooper: You see this man? This man tought me every single wrestling move I know! He taught me to be what I am today. And I am proud to say that this man is my FATHER, Thomas Cooper! He was once a pro wrestler but didnt really have the luck I have had so far. But he is still the man I dedicate my career to. And this Saturday night, he is going to accompany me to the ring. And I will retain this title for you dad. Not for all the millions of fans, you dad. Thomas Cooper: Eric, you have to remember. Dont get unfocused by the fans, Edwards, or ANYTHING!!! Eric Cooper: I promise dad. RCW, face it whether you like it or not Im gonna still be champ, AND THATS THAT!!!
