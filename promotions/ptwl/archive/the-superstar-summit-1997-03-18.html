The Superstar Summit?!?

18 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Superstar Summit was originally created by [name withheld], the handler of the infamous 'Super' Scott, and former FASW Fed Head. The first Superstar Summit was between the FASW & EWA. The winner of the most interfed title and regular matches would keep their name, and unify with the other, losing fed. The EWA ended up winning it. To read the GREAT card, to go ewa.ml.org/summit/summit.htm . This is the webpage that also has the lineup for this year's summit. You can't get in the Superstar Summit, not now, it is to late, I believe. But you could get in it if you were a member of the EWA/PCW/DPW/MLWO/UEW/WAeW/PIW/IIWF/PTWL and I think maybe a few more feds .. maybe. The bulk of hte matches are with the EWA/PCW/DPW/MLWO. There is a short history lesson on the Superstar Summit. :) Kent
