Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Prime Time Wrestling League (PTWL) introduced itself to the newsgroup on 6 January 1997 as “the place to be in '97,” aiming, in its own words, to compete with “the BIG TIMES sutch as FASW, EWA, CHAOS!!!, PCW, DPW.” An application form followed within weeks, alongside a standing call for writers: a booked league built around original wrestlers, promising “a small 1-2 match a week writing job” to anyone willing to take it on.

PTWL ran a sustained recruitment campaign through the first nine months of 1997 without ever posting a card of its own. Its update notices pushed roleplaying quality over quantity — “BIG roleplays will win matches” — and in March its own president backed a separate sixteen-man tournament, run for a three-dollar entry and a thirty-dollar prize, describing himself in the same post as the operator of two further promotions and a freelance writer across four others besides.

That same spring, a departing PTWL figure's own account, written for an unrelated new promotion, recorded a partial sale: the purchase of a promotion called the ULTIMATE!!! Wrestling Alliance together with roughly half of PTWL itself, and the departure of PTWL's own prior operator over the money involved. The account does not say what became of PTWL's own half once the sale went through.

By September, PTWL was hosting rather than merely competing: its “Wrestlegeddon '97” letter proposed a joint pay-per-view bringing together as many as sixteen promotions from across the newsgroup, America Online and other networks alike, and a follow-up the next day counted fourteen confirmed, among them Superstars of Wrestling, Network Online Wrestling, Psychotic Championship Wrestling, Championship Mexican Lucha Libre and the Extreme Wrestling Association.

No card, roster, or result belonging to PTWL survives anywhere in its own record, from the January founding through the September pay-per-view push. Its last dated document is from 21 September 1997; a later advertisement for an unrelated promotion describes the same operator's PTWL role only in the past tense, without stating when or how it ended.
