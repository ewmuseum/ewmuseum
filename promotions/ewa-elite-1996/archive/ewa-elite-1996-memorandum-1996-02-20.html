Specs Sheet on your Wrestler _____________________________ Wrestler Name: Height: Weight: Gimmick / Background: (! MOST IMPORTANT !) What ONE wrestler he resembles MOST: Special Move with description: - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - (Assign a point value to each category. 16 - point MAX) Skill: (moves / holds / smarts) Power: (strength) Agility: (quickness / ariel skills) Rampage: (ability to go NUTS!) Dirty: (cheat shots / cheating) _____________________________ END _ OF _ FORM ___ NOTE: These two areas on the Specs Sheet will help me make your wrestler as realistic as possible. II. More information about E.W.A. A. E.W.A. Events / cards / Pay - Per - Views E.W.A. wrestling events will, right now, only be held on SATURDAYS. If the league turns out to be what I hope and YOU, the wrestlers, want more the demands will be met. PPV information will be available as soon as E.W.A. has started. B. Rankings and Record information This information will be available after EVERY event. Unconditionally! C. TYPES OF MATCHES All types of matches will be welcomed. Current list is as followed: - Cage - Ladder - Scaffold - Triangle - Bedlam (only concerns tag-teams : no tags in/out, everyone fights) - Thunderdome (Special PPV's only) - No DQ's - No Count Out's D. Misc. - Final matches for E.W.A. events will be announced on Thursday before - Consent from both parties will be necessary to sign a match III. Closing Please send this back to me completely filled out ASAP! I really want to get things underway as I'm sure you do! Before the first E.W.A. event I will email everyone in the league a copy of all the wrestlers BIO's. Thank you, [name withheld]
