WHAT THE EWA IS ALL ABOUT The EWA is all about e-wrestling, role-playing, stats, rankings, "who's the best", and "who's got the gold". EWA is fantasy e-wrestling. Its consistent, accurate, up-to-date, and exciting. I started the EWA because I was tired of wrestling in other e-wrestling federations for only a week or so, then moving on because the league runner got bored or over-worked. EWA is here to stay! The EWA only accepts the highest quality wrestlers. That is what the EWA is all about. II. HOW DOES E-WRESTLING WORK? (Let's use myself as an example for this question.) Some guy (or gal) starts some e-wrestling league. This guy re-cruets some people that like wrestling to play the roles of the wrestlers in his league. The people he re-crueted tell the e-wrestling league runner how to create his or her wrestler (ex: height, weight, finishing move, etc.). The league runner takes these wrestlers that were created by the re-crueted people and enters them into a SIMULATOR. When the league runner has a e-wrestling event, he uses the SIMULATOR to wrestle the actual matches between the wrestlers by taking the wrestlers various attributes (skill, strength, stamina, etc.) and comparing them to one-another to reach a result. The role of the league runner is to report the results of the simulated wrestling event. Event after event, the people who created the wrestlers for the league engage in setting up matches, friends / foes, "jumps", and just plain old TRASH TALK. A. WRESTLING LEAGUE SIMULATORS There are a few e-wrestling simulators out there. WLS5.1, RAMPAGE, and TNM are the big names. These are the only ones I know of, but I'm sure there are more out there. You can get any of these e-wrestling simulators off of the Internet for free. The authors of the software just ask of you that if you use the software frequently then send in a donation. B. WHERE TO GET A SIMULATOR WLS5.1 at : http://www.axess.com/badboy/sims/ RAMPAGE at : http://users.aol.com/T725/Rampage.html TNM at : http://www.fh-landshut.de/~lyger/ C. HOW DO THEY WORK? Simulators work by assigning numeric values to various attributes, moves, and probabilities of moves. The simulator takes those numeric values and applies them to various mathematical formulas to reach a result for each move during a match. Every time a move is executed, health points are taken away. Simulators do not always use the same formulas for every move. So this means that just because you lost to someone once before, doesn't mean you'll always lose to them. III. TRASH TALK "Trash Talk" is one the MOST important things involved in being a fantasy wrestler. Since no one can see you or your wrestler, it is important to let everyone get to know you through your "Trash Talk". "Trash Talk" is just like the stuff you see on the WWF and WCW wrestling shows, there INTERVIEWS and COMMENTS that your wrestler should make about: - What's going on in the league - An upcoming match - Other wrestlers - Yourself - ANY VIEWS on the league from your WRESTLER'S standpoint This is the part of EWA I take seriously. To be a wrestler in the EWA, you've gotta make your presence known to the rest of us. C. HOW TO GET A MATCH Now If you remember, "Sunday Night SLAM" (and PPV's) are the nights that you need to get yourself a match. To get a match you must decide first who you want to wrestle, then go from there. Once you've decided who you want to wrestle, the next step is to let me know by writing me an email message saying simply: Dear Tom, [your wrestler's name] would like a match with [wrestler's name you want to fight] this Sunday Night SLAM. [Your Name] That's IT!! After I receive your message I'll send out the official EWA MATCH CONTRACTS. When both wrestlers sign the contract and return it to me, you'll get the match. V. CREATING ANGLES FOR YOURSELF A. WHAT IS AN ANGLE? An angle is a kind of like a "story-line" that you involve your wrestler in. An example of an angle, that was used in the WWF in the 80's, was the MACHO MAN, ELIZEBETH, & HULK HOGAN angle. ELIZEBETH was with MACHO MAN, but he didn't treat her so good, so HOGAN SAVED HER, thus setting up the MACHO MAN vs. HULK HOGAN feuds. Another example is the time RIC FLAIR and the FOUR HORSEMAN broke STING's leg after a match. They "JUMPED" him after the match and beat him up, thus setting up RIC FLAIR vs. STING. I'm sure you all know what I'm talking about. Angles are very important to e-wrestling as well. They make it a lot more interesting for yourself and the group involved. VI.
