"NEW EWA Sunday Night SLAM(#3) Card"

24 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************* E.W.A. : Sunday Night SLAM (episode#3) ************************************************* JB: Hello, and welcome again to another addition of "EWA Sunday Night SLAM"! GW: And I've gotta say that tonight Jim, we might only have three matches lined-up, but THEY ARE GOOD ONES!! JB: I'm also gonna add the fact that I've been hearing a lot of RUMORS this week! RUMORS about what's gonna happen tonight! TONIGHT IS GONNA BE BIG!! I can't wait! GW: I do know one thing, and it's a FACT. The MERCENARY is HERE TONIGHT! SECURITY is searching for him because he's not, legally, allowed to be here tonight. JB: That's right Gary. The MERCENARY didn't sign a match for this SUNDAY NIGHT SLAM. None the less though, he still is gonna try and seek his revenge on the TOXIC TWIN. GW: Well let's take a look at TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: (match#1) Donny the Bull vs. Big Bad Boris (match#2) Rick Roberts (TV) vs. The Raven - TV Title Match (match#3) Toxic Twin (W) vs. VAMPYRE - World Title Match JB: Donny the Bull is just coming off of his WORLD TITLE LOSS to the Toxic Twin, who is also fighting tonight. I'm expecting something to happen between these two as well before the night is over. GW: Not to change the subject, but were also gonna have a TV Title defense tonight. Rick Roberts will defend his TV Title against the Raven. Both of these guys have put in their share of time here in EWA, and I'm sure the best man will win. JB: Well let's get to the first match! First up is DONNY THE BULL vs. BIG BAD BORIS in singles competition. Let's get down to the action! (* bell sounds *) GW: Donny the Bull and Big Bad Boris are standing toe-to-toe in the center of the ring! Donny makes a gesture to shake hands with Boris! Donny and Boris are shaking hands when all of a sudden Donny tells Boris that VAMPYRE is standing behind him! Boris turns his back to look, but when he does Donny pulls Boris into him by his arm and executes a clothesline! What a CHEAP SHOT! Boris is quickly back up to his feet, but Donny gets the initiative and applies a bear-hug on Boris! Boris reaches for a handful of Donny's hair, but the referee warns him for illegal tactics! Boris, in fear of being DQ'ed, changes his mind and decides to throw a forearm into the side of the Bull's head! Boris breaks the bear-hug, and goes for an Irish-whip into the turnbuckle! Donny reverses it on Boris! Boris goes flying into the corner! Boris is stunned, and is standing in the corner! Donny climbs to the outside onto the apron, and is going for a guillotine drop on Big Bad Boris! Boris escapes the attempt and applies a hammer-lock on the Bull! Donny falls off the apron, BUT Boris still had a hold of Donny's right arm! Donnny's right arm is injured! Boris goes out onto the arena floor after the Bull! Boris hits Donny with a haymaker! The Bull recovers from the blow quickly and starts to charge at Boris! Boris counters with an arm-drag! EXCELLENT MOVE! Donny hits the cement hard! Boris quickly takes advantage of the situation and delivers 4 stomps right in Donny's stomach! Boris picks up Donny and throws him back into the ring! Donny is still down in the ring! Boris pulls him up by his hair and hits him with 2 forearm smashes! Donny is stunned! Boris is going for an atomic drop on the 480lbs. Donny the Bull! Donny's weight is too much for Boris! Boris falls backward with Donny the Bull on top! Donny goes for the cover! 1…2… Boris kicks out! Boris got the wind knocked out of him! Donny pulls Boris up by his hair and locks-up with him! Donny delivers a quick knee to Big Bad Boris's groin! The referee didn't see it! Boris is DOWN! What a CHEAP SHOT! Donny is going for the cover! 1…2…3! Big Bad Boris is PINNED after 18:05! Donny the Bull is Victorious!! ******** END OF MATCH ******** JB: WHAT A CHEAP WAY TO WIN!! Donny the Bull must have re-evaluated his priorities since he lost his COVETED WORLD TITLE against the TOXIC TWIN last week. GW: A win is a win in my book… Whatever it takes, is always enough. Anyway, GOOD for Donny, I'm glad to see that he's actually beginning to realize that WINNING and the MONEY is the only thing that TALKS in this professional wrestling league! JB: Gary, you make me sick, and you know that's NOT TRUE!! GW: ANYWAY, our next match is the current E.W.A TV Champion, RICK ROBERTS, vs. THE RAVEN. Rick Roberts will, once again, defend his prized TV Title. JB: I've gotta say that this will be a GREAT match-up. There's no BAD BLOOD between these two, and it should be a clean, technical match-up. JB: Well I've got some more DIRT for you EWA fans out there. DONNY THE BULL isn't going back to the LOCKER ROOM! He's staying at RINGSIDE for this next match! We all know that Rick Roberts and the Bull are on good terms, but the Bull and the Raven? Who knows what's gonna happen! GW: Looks like the BULL is making one of the ringside OFFICIALS get up, so he can have a seat! (Gary White smiles and starts laughing) (* Donny the Bull is sitting in a CHAIR, in the far corner of the ring area, near the far barricade *) GW: Well it looks like everything has calmed down, and the officials look like there ready to start with the next match. Let's get down to the act… (* Gary stops in the middle of his sentence because he's interrupted by the CAMERA MAN. The camera man hands Gary White a piece of paper, then goes back behind the scene. Gary quickly READS the piece of paper and then looks up at the camera. *) GW: I've just been informed that SECURITY HAS JUST FOUND the MERCENARY, and you'll LOVE THIS!! The MERCENARY is back in the LOCKER ROOM AREA RAISING HELL!! Almost the entire SECURITY staff is back there keeping the MERCENARY and TOXIC TWIN from ripping each other's heads off!! JB: Well, the REFEREE wants to move on with the next match. We'll keep up UPDATED on the LOCKER ROOM SITUATION. GW: RICK ROBERTS and the RAVEN are all set to go! Let's get to the ACTION!! (* bell sounds *) JB: The bell rings, but the Raven is still complaining about DONNY THE BULL being present at ringside. Rick Roberts is waiting over in his corner still. The Referee assures the Raven that he will not allow the BULL to interfere with the match. The raven goes back to his corner and locks his eyes up with Rick Roberts. The Referee gives DONNY THE BULL a quick warning that he'd better not interfere. DONNY THE BULL gives a quick nod. The Referee turns and gives Rick Roberts and the Raven the signal to start wrestling. Rick Roberts and the Raven start towards one another. Raven pokes Roberts in the eyes! The Referee gives the Raven a warning for his use of illegal tactics! The Raven slaps Roberts in a side head-lock, and takes him over to the ropes! Raven gives Roberts a rope burn! As soon as the Raven breaks the hold, Roberts tries to execute a backbreaker! Roberts gets him up, but the Raven was ready for him! The Raven throws him weight into Roberts and knocks him over! Raven is on top and going for the cover! 1…2. Roberts kicks out! Raven is to his feet first! The raven pulls Roberts up to his feet, and is going for his special move the INVERTED POWERSLAM! WHAM!! Raven executes the move! Raven goes for the cover! 1…2… Roberts kicks out! The Raven is off to a GREAT START!! Both men are getting to their feet! The Raven quickly slaps on the chicken wing and tries to get Roberts to submit! Roberts breaks the hold, and get his distance from the Raven! Both men stare down one another! The Raven and Rick Roberts lunge into a lock-up! Roberts turns the tables, and turns it into a drop-toe hold! Roberts stays on top of the downed Raven, and applies a leglock! The Raven quickly escapes, and both men lock back up! The Raven backs Roberts up into the ropes! The Raven gets the initiative and attempts an arm-whip into the ropes, BUT Roberts reverses it and arm-whips the Raven into the CORNER!! The Raven hits hard! Roberts grabs a hold of the Raven and throws him into the ropes! The Raven comes back off the ropes, AN UPSET, the Raven executes a flying head scissors on Rick Roberts! WOW, WHAT A MOVE!! The Raven pulls Roberts up to his feet again, and executes a backbreaker! Roberts is in PAIN!! The Raven grabs Rick Roberts by his hair and pulls him back up to his feet again! Roberts gets a quick burst of energy and takes a giant swing at the Raven! The Raven ducts under Roberts telegraph punch! Roberts back is turned to the Raven! The Raven scoops Rick Roberts back up and completes another INVERTED POWERSLAM!! Raven goes for the cover! 1…2… Rick Roberts kick out! HE'S GOTTA BE ALMOST OUTTA GAS!! The Raven gets up and waits over by the ropes for Roberts to get to his feet! The Raven is sizing him up for a big clothesline! As Rick Roberts slowly gets to his feet, the Raven starts a flat out run at him! Rick Roberts SEES the Raven in time AND COUNTERS! Roberts sunset flips the Raven! The Raven is down and stunned by the move! Roberts quickly gets turns and drops a knee on the Raven's head! Roberts picks the Raven up by his hair, and gets behind him to set him up for his special move the BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX-PIN! Roberts does it! The Referee starts the count! 1…2… The Raven kicks out! Roberts gets up to his feet first, and sets the raven up for a snap-suplex! Roberts executes it! Raven is lying on his back near the corner turnbuckle! Rick Roberts climbs up to the second rope and executes a front-elbow drop on the Raven! Roberts goes for the pin! 1…2… The Raven kick out again! WHAT A TURN OF EVENTS!! Once again Roberts picks up the Raven, AND… OUCH!! THAT MUST OF HURT!! As soon as Roberts had the raven to his feet, the Raven mustarded up enough energy to KICK Roberts RIGHT IN THE GROIN THEN NAIL Roberts WITH A VICIOUS CLOTHESLINE!! Raven picks up Rick Roberts head off the canvas, SLAMS in down into the canvas! The Raven goes for the PIN! 1…2… Roberts kicks out! WOW!! Raven picks Roberts up by the hair, BUT Roberts gets the initiative and jabs the Raven in his right eye with his thumb! Roberts quickly grabs the Raven around the waist and executes a gut wrench suplex! Roberts gets up and moves behind Raven while he's on the ground! Roberts reaches down and grabs the back of the Raven's hair, picking him up to his feet! Roberts executes his BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX-PIN maneuver! The Referee starts the count! 1…2… The Raven kicks out, BUT Roberts keeps his hands locked around the raven's waist! Roberts executes his BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX-PIN maneuver AGAIN! The Referee starts the count! 1…2…3! The Raven is PINNED after 23:09! Rick Roberts is Victorious, and retains his TV Title!! ******** END OF MATCH ******** GW: WOW, WHAT A MATCH!! I was surprised to see DONNY THE BULL not interfere during the course of the match. Maybe DONNY isn't so bad after all… JB: RICK ROBERTS and the RAVEN are still in the ring. The Referee has just handed RICK ROBERTS his TV Title BELT back. The RAVEN seems a little MAD. (* RICK ROBERTS looks over at the RAVEN, and then walks over to him. ROBERTS is standing in front of the RAVEN, who is down on one knee. Both men stare at each other. ROBERTS extends his hand down to the RAVEN and says, "Good match…". The RAVEN looks up at ROBERTS for a second, then takes his hand and says, "Yeah… Good match.". When the two men get done shaking hands RICK ROBERTS leaves the ring first. DONNY THE BULL, who is still at ringside, is surprised by this. RICK ROBERTS left the BULL at ringside. *) GW: It looks like DONNY THE BULL and RICK ROBERTS might not be as good of friends as we thought. DONNY looks pretty mad! (* As the RAVEN begins to get out of the ring and head back to the locker room, DONNY THE BULL sneaks up behind him and RAMS him! The RAVEN goes down onto the concrete HARD! The RAVEN is lying on the floor, and is stunned by the BULL 's actions! DONNY THE BULL lets out a huge YELL as he grabs the ANNOUNCERS TABLE and sets it up in the aisle! The ringside announcer is screaming for security, but there all back in the LOCKER ROOM with the MERCENARY and the TOXIC TWIN! The BULL then goes and grabs the RAVEN up by his hair, puts his other hand around the back of his neck, and starts SLAMING the RAVEN 's FACE of the ANNOUNCERS TABLE! He finally stops after 4 GOOD WACKS (and slightly cracking the table)! The RAVEN 's FACE is BLEEDING!! DONNY THE BULL then puts his hand on the RAVEN 's throat and CHOKESLAMS the RAVEN THROUGH THE TABLE!! DONNY takes one last look at the RAVEN, then turns and heads back to the locker room. *) JB: DONNY THE BULL is running WILD tonight! WHY is he doing this?!?! GW: No one knows, but at least its finally over! WOW!! This SUNDAY NIGHT SLAM is incredible so far! JB: The MEDICS are attending to the RAVEN now. They have him up on his feet, and he looks like he should be "OK". Their taking him back to his dressing room. GW: It also seems that the LOCKER ROOM SITUATION we had earlier with the MERCENARY and the TOXIC TWIN is over as well. SECURITY has removed the MERCENARY from the building. No one was hurt, and TOXIC TWIN will still be wrestling in the next match. JB: All right then, let's get to our main event! Our MAIN EVENT tonight is a our current WORLD Champion, TOXIC TWIN, will be defending his title against one of the leagues DARKEST wrestlers, the VAMPYRE. GW: This should be a great match. Both men have, some what, similar wrestling styles. JB: Both men are down in the ring, and the Referee wants to begin the match. Let's get to the ACTION!! (* bell sounds *) GW: Toxic Twin and the VAMPYRE are both in the ring, waiting at their corners. VAMPYRE turns and is giving his manager, Scarlet, his robe. Toxic Twin sees that VAMPYRE has his back turned and jumps him from behind applying his fatal special move the SKULL CRUSH! VAMPYRE breaks the hold! Toxic Twin slaps VAMPYRE right across the face! VAMPYRE is stunned by his actions! Toxic Twin grabs VAMPYRE and goes to Irish-whip him into the corner! VAMPYRE reverses it and throws Toxic Twin into the corner! Toxic hits that top turnbuckle hard with his shoulder! Toxic Twin's right shoulder is injured! Toxic is grabbing his shoulder in pain! VAMPYRE goes for a clothesline on the injured Toxic Twin, but Toxic quickly drops to the canvas and executes a step over toehold! OUCH! VAMPYRE came right down on his right knee cap! His leg is injured! Both men are slow to there feet. VAMPYRE challenges Toxic Twin to a test of strength! Toxic Twin, with an injured shoulder, locks-up with VAMPYRE, and his injured leg! VAMPYRE wins the test of strength, but Toxic gets the initiative with a solid kick to the gut! VAMPYRE buckles over, giving Toxic a chance to nail VAMPYRE with some quick jabs! Toxic goes for the big fist, but VAMPYRE ducts under it and goes for a neck-breaker! VAMPYRE executes it! Toxic is down and stunned! VAMPYRE goes for the cover! 1…2. Toxic Twin kicks out! Both men lock-up! Toxic Twin delivers a hard knee to VAMPYRE's stomach, and then nails VAMPYRE with a forearm to the back! VAMPYRE is down (back facing up)! Toxic Twin knee-drops VAMPYRE right in the center of his lower back! Toxic Twin then executes 2 hard stomps to VAMPYRE's lower back! Toxic pulls VAMPYRE to his feet! VAMPYRE is stunned! Toxic lays a fierce side-kick into the side of the VAMPYRE's right leg! OUCH! VAMPYRE doesn't go down though! Toxic Twin grabs VAMPYRE by his hair, and then sweeps the VAMPYRE's legs out from under him! Toxic Twin takes a break from the action and walks over to the ringside camera! TT: YOU SEE THIS, ALL YOU MISERABLE EXCUSES FOR WRESTLERS?!?! THIS IS WHAT I DO TO PEOPLE THAT I DON'T LIKE!! JUST WAIT TILL YOU SEE WHAT I CAN DO TO PEOPLE I HATE!!! JB: WOW!! He's MAD! But that was a mistake on Toxic's part! VAMPYRE is standing right behind him! Toxic Twin turns back to VAMPYRE, and is greeted with a boot to the gut! VAMPYRE arm-whips Toxic Twin in to the corner! Toxic re-injures his arm! VAMPYRE applies a hammer-lock to Toxic's weak arm! Toxic quickly reacts and pokes VAMPYRE in the eyes! The Referee warns Toxic Twin for his tactics! VAMPYRE recovers quickly, and while the Referee is reprimanding Toxic Twin, VAMPYRE sneaks outside of the ring and is on the apron behind Toxic Twin! Toxic turns around to see the VAMPYRE! VAMPYRE grabs the top rope and springs over, doing a front flip (MOONSAULT)! VAMPYRE hits Toxic Twin right in the face with his back! Toxic is DOWN! WHAT A SPECTATULAR MOVE! VAMPYRE is going for the cover! 1…2… Toxic Twin kicks out! WOW!! Both men get up! Toxic Twin comes up with a drop-kick and nails VAMPYRE in the chest! VAMPYRE goes flying backwards and hits his head on the bottom turnbuckle in the corner! He looks HURT! This could be OVER! Toxic Twin goes for the cover! 1…2… VAMPYRE kicks out! HOLY-COW! THESE TWO ARE RELENTLESS!! Both men get back up to their feet! VAMPYRE gets the initiative, and goes for an Irish-whip into the ropes! Toxic REVERSES it! WHOA!! VAMPYRE REVERSES IT ON Toxic Twin! Toxic goes flying into the ropes! VAMPYRE nails Toxic with an acrobatic kick off the ropes! Toxic is down! VAMPYRE pulls Toxic up to his feet! Toxic gets the initiative and goes for a piledriver! Toxic can't get him up! VAMPYRE reverses it and executes the piledriver on the Toxic Twin! Toxic Twin is almost unconscious, and lying on his back near on of the corners! VAMPYRE is climbing to the top rope! He's going for his special move, the MOONSAULT! VAMPYRE jumps, but Toxic rolls out of the way!! VAMPYRE lands right on his back and is stunned! Toxic rolls on top of the fallen VAMPYRE and starting choking him! The Referee breaks the choke after an 8 count! HOW VICIOUS!! Both men are absolutely EXHAUSTED! VAMPYRE is the first one to his feet! VAMPYRE pulls Toxic up to his feet, then throws him into the ropes! On the way back, VAMPYRE's manager decides to involve herself! Scarlet grabs a hold of the Toxic Twin's feet as he bounces into the ropes! Toxic goes down hard, and right onto the hurt shoulder! THAT MUST OF STARTED TO TAKE ITS TOLL BY NOW!! VAMPYRE executes double AX handle on Toxic Twin! The VAMPYRE climbs onto Toxic Twin's back and applies a hammerlock! Toxic slowly works his way up to his feet, but the VAMPYRE still has the hammerlock applied! Toxic backs VAMPYRE into the ropes! The Referee breaks the hold! The VAMPYRE doesn't let up though! VAMPYRE quickly grabs Toxic Twin and throws him into the ropes! VAMPYRE attempts a big fist, but Toxic ducts under it and keeps running! Toxic Twin gets the initiative back and goes for a flying shoulder tackle! VAMPYRE makes a big mistake, and jumps straight up in the air to perform a leap frog! DISASTER!! Both men KNOCKED HEADS!! Both men are DOWN! The Referee begins the count! 1…2…3…4…5…6… Toxic rolls over on top of the VAMPYRE! Toxic Twin is going for the cover! 1…2.. VAMPYRE kicks out! WHO IS THIS GUY?!?! Toxic gets to his feet, he sits VAMPYRE up and APPLIES HIS SPECIAL MOVE, the SKULL CRUSH!!! Will VAMPYRE SUBMIT?!?! The Referee picks up VAMPYRE's hand once… It falls back down! The Referee picks up VAMPYRE's hand for the second time… It falls AGAIN! The Referee picks up VAMPYRE's hand for the THIRD TIME… IT FALLS AGAIN!!! The VAMPYRE submits after 26:51 of action! Toxic Twin is Victorious and retains his WORLD TITLE!! ******** END OF MATCH ******** GW: That's a deadly submission hold that the TOXIC TWIN has there Gary. I wouldn't want to have that on me! JB: Your not kidding! WAIT A MINUTE! Why is SCARLET, VAMPYRE 's manager, rushing herself and the VAMPYRE out of here so fast?? (* TOXIC TWIN is still in the ring, celebrating his win over the VAMPYRE. SCARLET and the VAMPYRE are already heading back to the locker rooms, and at a pretty fast rate. *) GW: Something's WRONG. Why are they in such a rush to get out of here?? (* SUDDENLY the LIGHTS go out in the entire arena. Leaving just the RED ENERGENCY LIGHTS to provide a very dim lighting. *) JB: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!?! (* TOXIC TWIN stops celebrating and starts looking around! He doesn't know what's going on! All of a sudden TOXIC TWIN gets nailed from behind, and goes face down to the canvas! The crowd is panicking! After a few seconds the lights come back on and the crowd calms down, BUT then everyone takes notice at the ring! TOXIC TWIN is still lying face down in the ring, but in a PUDDLE OF HIS OWN BLOOD!! Women in the stands are SCREAMING!! There's a BENT PIPE on the mat next to TOXIC TWIN. TOXIC TWIN was hit hard in the back of the head with that PIPE! *) GW: OH MY GOD!! GET THE MEDICS DOWN THEIR NOW!!! (* The medics race down to the ring and start to tend to the injured TOXIC TWIN. TOXIC TWIN is unconscious and BLEEDING FROM THE BACK OF HIS HEAD. *) JB: WHO DID THIS?!?! I didn't see anyone at ringside?!?! GW: Was it the MERCENARY, or THE BULL?!?! JB: It couldn't of been! We would've SEEN them!! HEY WAIT A MINUTE, TOXIC TWIN 's TITLE BELT IS MISSING!! SOMEONE STOLE THE WORLD TITLE!! GW: I bet whoever STOLE the title, also SMASHED TOXIC TWIN with that PIPE!! THIS IS HORRIBLE!! JB: We're out of time EVERYBODY! We'll keep you UPDATED! (* camera fades out *)
