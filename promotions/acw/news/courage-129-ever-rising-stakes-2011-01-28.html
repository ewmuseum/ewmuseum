COURAGE 129: Ever-Rising Stakes.

28 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It may be the season of merryment, but in ACW... everybody's getting ready to throw down and put a lid on a year's worth of frustrations. The opportunity of a lifetime awaits at END GAME 2010, but the battle between Jeremy Hunt and SilverHAWK is engulfing everything else. Yet, there was still so much mayhem that was unleashed on COURAGE 129, many viewers thought it was a super-sized episode. Nope. Just a normal day at the office for ACW's brave warriors. + Khristain Keller found a way to get all up in Keith Scott Zimmerman's business, as the two men continue on their collision course for the ACW World Title. + While the Man Without An Alias tries to figure out what's up with Fejona Min, the Rogue Slayer had to carve out an opportunity to get herself back in Z's good graces. + Speaking of that hooligan, Z continued his countdown to midnight and opened a whole new can of worms along the way. + Ryan Billows swore that he would rid ACW of Jonny Yankee once and for all, and also sound out a warning to Midnight Cowboy that the latter's time was also coming to an end. + Three teams took to the ring for the right to vie for the ACW Tag Team Titles, in another Four Ways To Glory Qualifier, and the result was... well, some incredible in-ring action. + And finally, 'The Darwinist' Dean Denton ensured that he would become Italy's public enemy number one for the next 10 years (at least), following his shocking assault of Rud Albion last week with another blockbuster this week. That's right. All that and so much more went down on COURAGE 129. No wonder it garnered a total of 36.65 million viewers. Where else can you get drama and action of THAT nature, eh? Nowhere, I tells ya. Nowhere. Only at ACW. Only on SlySports. Recognise, bitches.
