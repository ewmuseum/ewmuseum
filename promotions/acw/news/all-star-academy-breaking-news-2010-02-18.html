All-Star Academy: Breaking News!

18 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In October of last year, ACW decided that it was doing well enough to have a spin-off federation dedicated at honing the skills and the confidence levels of the students in their training facility. Thus, the All-Star Academy was developed and operated as an actual entity. While designed to be separate from ACW itself, there would indeed be some crossover as some of the students in the Academy had proven themselves worthy of hanging with the bigger superstars. The first season of SHOWCASE episodes -- all nine of them -- proved to be a big hit for the live audience and the release of these episodes on DVD are highly anticipated. Plans for the second season of SHOWCASE episodes seemed academic, and everyone was eager to find out when this second season would land. For a while, it appeared as if that logistical and resource problems would delay the project indefinitely. But through a series of fortunate and blessed circumstances, the All-Star Academy is now proud to announce that it's now just a matter of weeks before the second season premieres. And while the first season did not have the luxury of a television deal to appeal to the masses, latest developments for the second season are wildly constrasting. In fact, it is with great pleasure that we reveal the following breaking news: US pay-cable telvision channel Starz has reached an agreement with ACW management of unprecedented proportions. Starz, which of December 2008 is available to 17 million subscribers in the United States alone, has decided to feature the programming of the All-Star Academy's second season of SHOWCASE after being given a DVD sampler of the first season of SHOWCASE episodes as well as many videos spanning ACW's entire run of events since 2002. Considering that Starz currently broadcasts what they feel is a mega-hit, Spartacus: Blood and Sand, it was no surprise that the network representatives were not at all turned off by the sometimes overly violent shenanigans of ACW's wrestlers over the past six years. An immediate rapport was struck with SilverHAWK, current General Manager of ACW and the rest of his associates. The rest, as they say, is history. Stay tuned for more news, as we at ACW and the All-Star Academy finalise the details for the second season of SHOWCASE. A subsite for the Academy will also be going up here on the website soon, complete with transcripts of all nine episodes of the first season of SHOWCASE. It's going to be a great year for ACW and the Academy, and the real fun is about to begin. We at ACW hope that you're ready for all the awesomeness we're about to unleash!
