COURAGE 122: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

28 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
COURAGE 122 will be beamed live from the PAOK Sports Arena tomorrow, and with emotions running high after last week's show, everybody expects COURAGE 122 to be a powder-keg waiting for the spark to explode. * Jade Argent came within an eyelash of beating Keith Scott Zimmerman for the ACW World Title last week, with Trey Vincent and the members of the cabal watching on intently. It was eventually Khristain Keller who had the final say in the matter, blasting Argent with a chair just as it seemed KSZ would tap out. One wonders what Keller's agenda is, and who is his real target. Will Keller reveal why he did what he did? Will Jade Argent take action? * Going back to KSZ; the Only Wrestler That Matters will surely be out for blood after Trey Vincent orchestrated a massive beatdown on him during last week's broadcast. The question is, will Keith go right after Trey or will the Cabalites involved in the attack be the first to suffer KSZ's wrath? * And on that note, it's with some surprise that we announce COURAGE 122's opening match: Kati, an integral member of the cabal, will square off against Allison Lindum-Zimmerman. That's correct, Keith's paramour and manager! Whether this is the doing of General Manager SilverHAWK or Jeremy 'The Man From SlySports' Hunt, one thing's for sure: this will be a match to keep an eye on! * Speaking of Jeremy Hunt, his unknown agenda against SilverHAWK and ACW will definitely continue this week, but in what way? The official of the Huntsmen has everybody except Z and his cabal on edge. Will there be another addition to the SlySports band of wrestlers? Considering Hunt's powers of persuasion, don't count that out. * One particular Huntsmen has been involved in a war of words with Z over the last week; that's right, Rud Albion! Albion and the Caped Crusader have traded scathing remarks on the ACW's website's Twitter-esque function, off the heels of their intense confrontation last week. There are still question marks over what exactly Z has on red-headed referee Paige Allen; we can only hope that there'll be new developments regarding this whole scenario this week. * As reported earlier in the week, Trey Vincent will have his first match ever since losing the ACW World Title this week on COURAGE 122. His opponent? None other than 'The Two' Booster Reid. Booster, the adopted son of respected broadcast man and former ACW World Champion Jimmy Reid, has been described as being over the moon at contesting his professional wrestling debut against an All-Star of Trey Vincent's magnitude. Will there be a fairytale beginning to Booster's ACW career, or will the might of Trey Vincent mean a comeback victory for the Only Sports Entertainer That Matters? * Another individual making his debut on COURAGE 122? Fight Man. He likes to fight, in case you didn't know. His opponent? ACW's biggest loser, Rory Hayes. Will there be another defeat in Rory's future? The odds say 'yes'. One man who hopes he won't lose is John Sarsgaard, as he defends his Spirit Of ACW Championship against MIS, one of the recent call-ups from the All-Star Academy. Sarsgaard stated last week that he knew what to do to make things right to the fans and Midnight Cowboy. It'll be interesting to see what John does. * And finally, the one's everybody been waiting for. Omega. Fejona Min. ACW [Black] Scorpion Championship. And get this: the two storied rivals shall come together once more in a Scorpion Cage XXX Battle. This showdown has been a long time coming, and the fans simply cannot wait to witness just who will come out of this the victor. With all of that going on, you might mistake COURAGE 122 for a pay-per-view. ... That's ACW for you. See you at COURAGE 122!
