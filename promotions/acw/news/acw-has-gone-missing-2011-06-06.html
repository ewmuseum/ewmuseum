ACW HAS GONE MISSING?!

6 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You might have wondered were your weekly helpings of COURAGE have gone in the month of March? Short answer, ACW has gone missing. Jeremy Hunt, ACW's new CEO and Owner in full of the company as of January 1st, 2011 is said to have cancelled every planned South American show leading up to the supposed LEGENDS V show in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Rumor has it that this was only the latest step that the former SlySports television executive has taken in distancing himself from the UK-based media conglomerate since taking control of ACW from SilverHAWK. SlySports representatives could not be reached for comment on this manner. Then again... Jeremy Hunt couldn't be reached for a comment either. On either the controversy of ACW's possible separation from SlySports or when AND where the next COURAGE would be held. BREAKING NEWS UPDATED 03/17/11: Word has been given to those on the ACW roster that a COURAGE will soon be held in Vancouver, British Columbia... that's right the ACW is COMING HOME for the LEAD-UP to LEGENDS V! More news on this all, as it arrives.
