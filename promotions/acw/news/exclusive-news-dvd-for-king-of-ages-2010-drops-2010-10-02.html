EXCLUSIVE NEWS: DVD for KING OF AGES 2010 drops!

2 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With COURAGE 122 airing last night, General Manager SilverHAWK today held a press conference in the lobby of the Holiday Inn Thessaloniki, several hours before his commute to Athens -- the location of COURAGE 123. The nature of this press conference? The unveiling of the KING OF AGES 2010 DVD! "Yes, it's out. And it looks incredible!" HAWK gushed as he held up his copy of the DVD for all to see. "And to commemerate the release of the DVD, I have some very exciting news. For those fans that weren't able to catch the pay-per-view, they had to make do with reading the transcript. Of course, there was this mysterious wizard that stole the transcripts of TWO of the matches; the one involving Arkady, and also the one involving Greg Miles & Orlando Grant. Well, I'm happy to report that we caught that dastardly wizard, and he's duly restored the transcripts of the two aforementioned matches! So, go ahead and log on to the ACW website. You'll be able to read the COMPLETE show, without any missing portions!" On that note, a brief digression: * Click here to read all about Arkady's thoroughly epic showdown with his former team-mates, The Supernatural SWAT. It included, as those who watched the pay-per-view live will tell you, a very special cameo appearance by a famous former Cruiserweight Champion from FWO! * Click here to read the transcript of the other match stolen by the transcript-stealing wizard, featuring Orlando Grant and 'Vicious' Greg Miles! And now, back to business. The reporters that had gathered, while happy that the transcript-stealing wizard had finally been caught, were underwhelmed by the General Manager's news. HAWK grinned cheekily, suggesting that he had something else to share. He most certainly did. "The buyrate numbers for the pay-per-view are coming through now. I don't have the official figures with me, but I can say with absolution that KING OF AGES 2010 has exceeded all expectations. The numbers have even surpassed the buyrate for LEGENDS IV, which is a complete surprise. FOR QUEEN & COUNTRY 2010 did excellent numbers back in February, considering it was ou first pay-per-view in nearly four years. LEGENDS IV raised the bar to a whole another level. FIGHT NIGHT 2010, while a critical success, didn't actually do blockbuster numbers. But that can be attributed to the fact that the pay-per-view was one giant fight, and also COURAGE COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER the night before featuring a completely loaded card. It's awesome to see that KING OF AGES 2010, however, has blown all of that out of the water. I'm a pragmatic man, and I know that the good times won't stay good forever. But this is truly a cause for celebration!" With that, SilverHAWK handed out a copy of the KING OF AGES 2010 DVD to all the reporters in attendance. Of course, they had to *pay* for it; Joe Bishop standing by the exit with a cash register and six bodyguards made sure of that fact. But still, a nice gesture from the General Manager nonetheless. It'll be interesting to see how IN ACROPOLIS 2010 performs, at the end of October... and of course, hype will be running wild for END GAME 2010 in December. As it is, the financial position of ACW has never been stronger, and for better or for worse, ACW has SlySports to thank for their superb run thus far this year.
