Courage 106: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

14 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* At the pay-per-view, Trevor Wilson survived his biggest challenge and retained the Spirit of ACW Title against all the odds (and thanks to a slight assist of sorts from Jade Argent). What's next for the seemingly unstoppable Trevor Wilson? Will the fans' favourite Midnight Cowboy come hunting for Wilson once more? * Speaking of Jade Argent, he seems to be officially back in the ACW fold and has managed to make enemies out of both Khristain Keller and Jesse Ramey at the pay-per-view. What sort of backlash can Argent expect from Keller? What sort of action will Argent take against Ramey after their backstage pow-wow? * Omega survived the Gauntlet Hell Challenge to retain his Scorpion Fighting Title, and has indeed solidified his stranglehold on the division. Who will step up next to try and take on the Craziest Bastard In Professional Wrestling? Is anybody stupid enough to even try? * After successfully turning back the challenge of The SOBs at the pay-per-view, Ed and Klein -- the Pop Culture Phenoms -- are rumoured to have some sort of celebratory speech lined up for Courage 106. What exactly will happen? Are there any other tag teams out there that want a crack at the Phenoms and the ACW Tag Team Titles? * Keith Scott Zimmerman lost to Trey Vincent. Shawn Jessica Hart lost to Alias. How will KSZ and SJH react on Courage 106? What does the new #1 Contender, Trey Vincent, have lined up for Alias? How much gas does the Original Pulp Hero have left in his tank after retaining his World Title in a classic match at the pay-per-view? * Iceman. Z. Buddy Saxon. Vince Jacobs. Chris Moliano. All are rumoured to be attending Courage 106, as ACW washes up on the shores of Spain for the first time ever. What other madness will go down? TUNE IN TO COURAGE 106 TO FIND OUT, AS THE ROAD TO LEGENDS IV ROLLS ON! ... Only on SlySports, bitches.
