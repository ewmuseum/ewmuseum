On The Road #3 by Jenna McMullen - Z.

JENNA: Hi, Z! It's good to have the chance to speak with you. Anyways, first question; how's it been in Ukraine so far, days away from COURAGE 111? Z: Ukraine's actually a nice place. People make it out to be some shithole devoid of women with bangable qualities, but there are actually quite a number of sweethearts out there. I look forward to having sex with each and every one of them. JENNA: ... Ooookay. What did you think of LEGENDS IV, overall? Z: It was fine. I would have liked it better had I beaten Christopher Moliano, but I think I proved myself to everyone watching; I can indeed wrestle. Congratulations to Trey Vincent, I knew you could do it. JENNA: I had a feeling he would. Who's your favourite wrestler in ACW now? Why? Z: Khristain Keller. JENNA: Really? You're not joking? Z: Of course I am. Keller's rubbish. My answer is Vince Jacobs. He rules. JENNA: Don't tease me like that! At least, not until this thing is over... ALRIGHTY, THEN. Still nobody has Keller down as their favourite wrestler. Who's the wrestler you hate most in ACW now? Why? Z: Nobody, to be honest. I haven't gotten around to making anybody my sworn enemy yet. JENNA: Let me know when you do. Okay, since you can clearly see my nipples jutting out like sore thumbs, I'd like to ask what you think of them. Also, is this Ukrainian weather too cold for you? Z: Well, I wasn't going to say anything but those are some delectable nipples. And nope, the weather's just fine. Of course, I've been in colder places so it's not a problem for me. May I suggest wearing a bra and a couple of sweaters, to keep yourself warm? JENNA: Why, thank you times two! I didn't bring any bras along, sadly, but I do have a lot of sweaters. Next question; if you knew me growing up, would you have asked me to prom? Z: Sure, I suppose I would. You're a sweet-enough girl. Your prom was what, just two years ago? JENNA: You sly dog, you. But thank you again! I think we should have drinks after this. Aaaaand finally, what can your fans expect from you in the months to come? Z: I have fans? Oh, okay. All I'll say is, The Summer of Z will be immense.
