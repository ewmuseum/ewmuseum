On The Road #7 by Jenna McMullen - Sepiroth Du Luc.

15 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Hi, Sepiroth Du Luc! It's good to have the chance to speak with you. Anyways, first question; how's it been in Ukraine so far, days away from COURAGE 111? SDL: Ukraine is definitely different. If you would have told me last month that I'd be in Ukraine wrestling again, I'd laugh in your face and tell you you're crazy. JENNA: Hehehe. That happens to me quite often. What did you think of LEGENDS IV, overall? SDL: LEGENDS IV was an amazing event from what I saw. A lot of these guys have been in the business for a long time; it's good to see them still wrestling... doesn't make me feel as old. *laughs* JENNA: That's nice! Who's your favourite wrestler in ACW now? Why? SDL: The old SDL probably would have given you some kind of sarcastic response, but those days are over. I'm just glad to be back. To answer your question, though, I've always enjoyed Alias and SVJ. JENNA: They are mortal enemies, you know. And yet, still nobody has Keller down as their favourite. This is getting ridiculous. Oh well. Who's the wrestler you hate most in ACW now? Why? SDL: Probably most of the rookies here in ACW. I'm not of fan of having to teach a young guy how to replace me. JENNA: You're paranoid! Okay, since you can clearly see my nipples jutting out like sore thumbs, I'd like to ask what you think of them. Also, is this Ukrainian weather too cold for you? SDL: Huh? I'm uses to the cold weather, it's nothing new to me. I'm no diva like some of these so-called superstars here in ACW, who bitch and complain about not getting a 5 star hotel or cab service when they need it. JENNA: Nothing about my nipples? Two in a row. Sigh. If you knew me growing up, would you have asked me to prom? SDL: Next question. JENNA: ... I'll take that as a yes, but I am quite depressed now. Thanks for that. Finally, what can your fans expect from you in the months to come? SDL: Hopefully some good entertaining wrestling. Getting back to my roots of entertaining everyone. I've been away from the sport for awhile so it's taking me a while to get adjusted, but some big things will be happening in the next couple of weeks.
