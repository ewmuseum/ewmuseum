Shareholders VOTE on this week's main event!

28 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With two days to go until the next ACW Courage show at Montreal's ACW Arena, the Shareholder Committee is proud to announce the first major vote by the Shareholder's since the One-Off show! For the main event of Courage on April 30th, shareholders are not only getting the chance to vote for the FOUR opponents participating in the match, but also for the CHAMPION who will defending his title. With a Scorpion Title Match already booked between the Fighting Champion Alias and the infamous Rejection, which leaves either the Spirit of ACW or the World Heavyweight title as possibly being on the line in two days time! Will the shareholders vote for their proud ACW World Champion Seymour Almasy, who has almost been pushed off the recent shows by the machinations of Violence Jack? Or will the shareholders vote to turn the tables on the conspicuously inactive "Spirit" of ACW Steve Knox? Which FOUR all-stars will the shareholders choose to face them? The answer to these questions and more, Thursday at Courage!
