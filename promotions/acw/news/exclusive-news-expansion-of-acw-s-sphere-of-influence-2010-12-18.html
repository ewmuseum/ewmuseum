EXCLUSIVE NEWS: Expansion of ACW's sphere of influence.

18 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ever since All-Star Championship Wrestling returned in March 2009, it has been quietly rising through the ranks to become a global phenomenon. In all its years of existence, ACW has never been considered anything more than a national wrestling promotion that has always been able to pump out amazing events while also being somewhat afflicted with a variety of problems that'd blow your mind if you think too hard about 'em. But as of January 2010, ACW's stock and status rose several notches when ACW entered into a long-lasting partnership with SlySports Entertainment LLC. For the first time in a long time, ACW had an immense amount of money and resources at its disposal... and even though the partnership with SlySports has taken a twisted turn thanks to the machinations of one Jeremy Hunt, nobody can deny the positive influences of the union. For one, ACW was able to transform its long-time training school -- the All-Star Academy -- into an actual promotional entity. With ACW touring the European continent, the All-Star Academy has grown to be a representation of the ACW brand across North America; while playing host to a variety of burgeoning personalities and superstars of the past looking for a new lease of life, the All-Star Academy has become more than a launching pad for future ACW stalwarts. It has its own television show (on premium cable network Showtime) and developed an entire sub-universe of its own. Remarkable. Now, with ACW moving on to Italy and counting down to END GAME 2010, the next step in the organisation's evolution has arrived. ACW and SlySports Entertainment LLC have, effective of November 2010, announced the acquisition of the majority stock of New York's Finest which will serve as a feeder company of sorts, similar in manner to how the All-Star Academy operates. New York's Finest (NYF) is an independent professional wrestling promotion in the great state of New York. Owned and operated by one Clint 'Slugger' Matheson since July 2005, NYF prides itself on being the home to some of the most promising up-and-comers in the industry, whether they're just starting out or re-inventing themselves. A number of past and current ACW's All-Stars have ties with NYF; most notable of these individuals are Mach 2 and Orlando Grant (who's currently back in NYF). With this newest move, it appears that NYF's future will be inexplicably tied to the global phenomenon that is ACW, and many feel that this latest development will only ensure good things for the men and women of the NYF roster. Already, NYF have picked up the tag team Children Of Hellas, who have been featured at recent ACW events during the organisation's tour of Greece. Now with two developmental organisations under its wing, one wonders how far can ACW soar with SlySports Entertainment LLC at the wheel. I may be biased, but I feel there's no limit to how high we can go!
