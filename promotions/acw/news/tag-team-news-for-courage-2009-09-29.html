Tag Team News for Courage!

29 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Heirs of Wrestling will not only be in action, but will also put their newly won tag team title belts on the line on the next edition of Courage! Just weeks after winning the belts in an elimination chase that lasted for months, the Heirs of Wrestling will be defending their titles against The Misfit Toys. The team of Jeremy Stevenson and Kati may front for Trevor Wilson, but they'll gladly welcome the opportunity to add more gold to the Minutemen's now empty collection. No word yet on if the Heirs asked for a title match, or are being forced to defend their titles.
