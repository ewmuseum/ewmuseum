COURAGE 132: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

24 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In just two days, COURAGE 132 will take place. And it's already shaping up to be a gigantic show. Sheesh, when is an edition of COURAGE ever going to be low-key, when so much is at stake all the time? To start off with, we have news that God's Forgotten Son and Billows -- two Huntsmen -- will NOT be in attendance. The reasons are unknown, but fans of these two individuals will surely be disappointed. Wait, who am I kidding? Nobody likes GFS and the Risen Lion. Good news, then. Other things that are confirmed to be happening: * 'The Two' Booster Reid. 'The Black Plague' Ruben Ross. Oh, there will be fireworks. I mean, come on; it's Ruben EFFING Ross. Enough said! * Andy Sharp, your *new* Spirit Of ACW Champion, will be in attendance. To gloat. And possibly defend his title. We'll have to see what happens following the aforementioned gloating. * Since there's a new Spirit Of ACW Champion, there are plans afoot to find a new #1 Contender. To that effect, a mini-tournament of sorts. The first of two matches will see Lutawicka square off against... Rud Albion! That should be gangbusters? The second match? No details regarding that have emerged yet, but many seem to think it'll involve a Huntsmen. * Spike Saunders will be joining Renaud Cardinal at the Broadcast Booth for the first hour of the show, and will then roll up his sleeves for a epic clash with Omega later in the show. Omega, if you remember, eliminated Saunders from the 2010 End Game Match. * It appears as if ACW's Finest, the new ACW Tag Team Champions, will be having their first title defense on 132. Their opponents, however, are yet-to-be unknown. But that means KSZ will be in... two matches? Man. * Yes, your LEGENDS V main event will go down on COURAGE 132. Keith Scott Zimmerman. Your New God, Z. 2/3 Falls Match for the ACW World Championship. Holy shit, that's gigantic. That got you hyped up? It should. Looks like the Hunt era is getting off to a great start, entertainment-wise. So you'd best catch COURAGE 132, one way or another. If you miss it, you might just be the stupidest person alive. Yeah, I went there. What of it?
