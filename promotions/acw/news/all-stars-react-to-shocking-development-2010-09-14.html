All-Stars react to shocking development!

14 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACW's KING OF AGES 2010 pay-per-view was a monster hit with fans in Sweden (except for their hometown hero getting the shit kicked out of him), fans all over the world and everybody at SlySports. Yet, on a pay-per-view which was loaded with monumental moments, one stood out: Vince Jacobs ditching the colours of ACW, and signing with a rival promotion poised to make its comeback in a matter of weeks. That's right. Legacy of Champions. Here are the initial reactions from several of the All-Stars we spoke to today -- Rory Hayes: Bah. I think they tried to recruit me. I told them to fuck off. What? It's entirely possible that they wanted to sign me. Buddy Saxon: ... call... my Uncle Nigel... tell him... I'm... going to... *vomits and then passes out* Kati: Yeah, right. LoC tried to recruit Rory Hayes. I'm going to believe that. Oh, my reaction to Vince Jacobs joining 'em? Eh. So what? Fejona Min: Does it look like I give two tugs of a dead dog's cock about Vince Jacobs joining LoC? Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. I'm new. Iceman: Good riddance to the COCKSUCKER~! Jack Harris: Bleeding 'ell, Hayes got a contract offer from LoC? I don't facking believe that for one twatting second! The Amazing Gabriel: Doesn't matter what place it is; LoC, ACW... wherever The Night Life goes IS the 'A' show! Fever Pitch: LoC? The guy that sang Wild Thing? He's making a comeback? Pfft, fuck him. Zip Zap: I go wherever Gabriel goes. All hail Fearless Leader! Elixr: Love me some Aria Murphy... but she's here now. So eff them. Kaus: Every has-been and wash-up who can't let go of the spotlight goes to LoC... Legion of Old Cunts. Tyson XL: ... *waits for The Amazing Gabriel to stop talking* Keith Scott Zimmerman: I didn't realize it was called Legacy of Washed-Up Former Champions. Jade Argent: Job done~! Who's next to have their ACW career destroyed? Form an orderly line behind Pro Wrestling's Crybaby. Alias: ... No comment. Jesse Ramey: As much as any of these other fuckers like to sit back and make snide comments to try and make themselves look good; this is a huge loss for A-C-W. Will we continue to be at the top of the game? Yeah, one superstar doesn't make or break a company. But this is truly a saddening loss, considering Vince has been one of my favorite superstars working in this business for a long time. Honestly? Best of luck, Jacobs, and hopefully our paths will cross again one day. Khristain Keller: Jesse Ramey just proved to me that he sucks balls... just like Legacy of Cuntflaps do. Z: Ramey sucks balls. What? Keller said the same thing? Christ, maybe I was wrong about him. Oh, you want my opinion about Jacobs. Hh. Stay tuned to COURAGE 121. Or maybe COURAGE 122. I'm a man of many schemes, which means I have a packed schedule. I do have one thing to add: RAMEY. SUCKS. BALLS.
