For Queen & Country 2010: Pre-Show Updates!

6 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* Vince Jacobs' feud with Jesse Ramey has been well documented over the past couple of months. And after their altercation on Courage 105 which brought about the official announcement of their pay-per-view confrontation, scores of fans have been wondering if a simple 2/3 Falls Match will resolve the issues lingering between both men. SilverHAWK has decided to spice things up, because he values the fans' feedback and wants to make sure that you all get an epic match. So, check it out: first fall, submission rules; second fall, anything goes (a'la Scorpion Fighting); and the third fall, if need be, would be contested under standard wrestling rules where only a pinfall will do the trick. * Iceman had a plan. He planned to cash in his rematch for the Scorpion Fighting Title at the LEGENDS IV pay-per-view, so that he could regain the title belt on the grandest stage of all. However, on Courage 105, he was invited to compete on the upcoming pay-per-view in the highly anticipated Gauntlet Hell. Iceman has revealed that he now has a new plan; win the Scorpion Fighting Title at FOR QUEEN & COUNTRY 2010, defeat every challenger that comes at him, defend the title at LEGENDS IV, gain the respect of everyone... and then, shock the world with his future plans for the Scorpion Fighting Title. We'll see exactly how Iceman fares with his new plan. Oh, and upon hearing that neither Keith Scott Zimmerman nor Trevor Wilson considering wanting to take him on at LEGENDS IV, Iceman only one word to describe both men: it starts with a 'c' and ends with a 'r'. Take a wild guess. * After his less-than-straightforward debut victory on Courage 105, many would have thought that Z would have gone out partying in the streets of Manchester. He did, and in the process, apparently has re-injured himself. Z is stated to be unfit to wrestle for the next three weeks, which has drawn the ire of not only SilverHAWK but the masked enigma's burgeoning rival, Chris Moliano. Chris Moliano is currently not scheduled for a match at the pay-per-view, but many are saying that Potential Personified will be addressing the events of Courage 105 and his defeat to Z. Not too long away from the pay-per-view now, ladies and gentlemen. Are you ready, as the road to LEGENDS IV starts to take shape? ... If not, you better get ready. Because we sure as hell are!
