All-Star Academy: Updates Galore.

19 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been some time since news of the second season of the All-Star Academy's SHOWCASE was announced, and in that time, the tapings of several episodes have come and gone. Starz, the US pay-cable television channel, has revealed that airing of these episodes will take place towards the end of March... but that doesn't mean we can't give all of you who didn't manage to attend the tapings some spoiler-ish details. So, if you weren't in attendance in Detroit's famous Joe Louis Arena at the tail-end of February and you want to know what happened, continue to read on. If you happen to be one of those fans who doesn't like spoilers? That's cool, check with your local cable operator to see if you're subscribed to Starz and wait on official word for when you can begin to enjoy some Academy goodness. Ready? 5. 4. 3. 2. 1. ### SEASON TWO | EPISODE ONE 20 February 2010 Selected results and moments of note * ICU embraced his role as new Chief of the Academy and declared an unofficial tournament of sorts that would crown the new United States Champion. Yes, as the first holder of that title belt, ICU decided to bring back the United States Title into play. * Malk Al-Haq def. JP Boudreau to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Orlando Grant def. John 'The Hammer' Smith to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Rory Hayes def. Ulf German to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * The Kole Brothers (former competing tag team from 2003) def. Abilities (newcomers) to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Kristinia Kage (making a guest appearance) and Haley Mulligan def. The SOBs to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Peter Phoenix def. Art Ajax (newcomer) to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Also of note: former fighter from theAsylum, Eddie Scott Poser, rode into the arena with a horse and revealed he'd been hired as colour commentator. He then proceeded to fall asleep at ringside and awoke a while later during the start of the Orlando/Smith match. -- SEASON TWO | EPISODE TWO 21 February 2010 Selected results and moments of note * Andrew Kole def. Kristinia Kage to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Peter Phoenix def. Rory Hayes to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Haley Mulligan def. Joseph Kole to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Orlando Grant def. Malk Al-Haq to advance in the US Title 'Tournament'. * Also of note: a young chap by the name of Vince Jacobs Junior made his debut, claiming to be a superfan of 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs, to the point of christening himself as the rightful heir to the Jacobs' wrestling legacy. Unfortunately, VJJ lost in his debut match to a man simply named X (perhaps because of the large 'X' scar across his face). Whether he has any relation to Z is unknown, but rumour has it that X maybe used to compete in theAsylum way back when. * Also of note, part two: Mary-Lynn Mayweather of fWo fame made an appearance, revealing that she'd signed up to compete in the Academy and expressed displeasure at not being included in the US Title 'Tournament'. -- SEASON TWO | EPISODE TWO 28 February 2010 Selected results and moments of note * Peter Phoenix def. Haley Mulligan to advance to the finals of the US Title 'Tournament'. * Orlando Grant def. Andrew Kole to advance to the finals of the US Title 'Tournament'. * Jack Harris def. Peter Phoenix and Orlando Grant in a Ladder Match in the finals of the US Title 'Tournament', becomes the NEW United States Champion. * What happened: in a shocking turn of events, Jack Harris showed up just as Orlando Grant and Peter Phoenix, tired survivors of the US Title 'Tournament', were about to do battle for the US Title. Long story short, Harris managed to convince ICU how him being involved would be good for business. Harris got his wish, and his size plus his relative freshness saw him claim the United States Title. ICU was not the most popular of figures after this decision. ### And there you have it. A lot more went down, but we can't go on and tell you EVERYTHING, can we? That'll really ruin the joy of watching it unfurl very soon on Starz. Check back soon for more news regarding All-Star Academy, as its continues to gain momentum!
