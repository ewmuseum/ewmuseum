EXCLUSIVE NEWS: SilverHAWK disappointed, books match for LEGENDS IV.

21 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SilverHAWK, ACW's General Manager, expressed extreme disapproval of Ed’s tirade during COURAGE 110. The brasher half of the Pop Culture Phenoms hijacked the broadcast and made claim of hanging the tag title belts from the rafters and having an unscheduled match at any time he pleased. As COURAGE 110's main event occured, Ed was called away from the monitors in the back to HAWK’s office, where reports say Ed was chewed out for his brash and unplanned action. An unnamed source is said to have overheard SilverHAWK tell Ed he would never be booked in a singles contest for the duration of his contract, supposedly to expire early next year. Ed seemed livid, but calmed when HAWK duly announced a Tag Team Titles bout for LEGENDS IV. You've read it here first: Pop Culture Phenoms would defend the title belts in a traditional Four-Corners Match against England’s Best, Irish Spring & The Entourage. The Entourage made their debut after weeks of vignettes, claiming they were here to save ACW. The brutally assaulted the Phenoms and strung up numerous other wrestlers. Just recently on COURAGE 110, they assaulted the newcomers Irish Spring, leading to an unsanctioned parking lot brawl. And yet just two weeks ago, the recently formed England’s Best got the best of the Entourage, sending them packing from the ring. SilverHAWK hopes that the Four-Corners Match for the ACW Tag Team Titles can help resolve the issues that have been brewing between all eight individuals.
