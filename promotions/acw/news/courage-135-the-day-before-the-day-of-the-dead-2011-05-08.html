COURAGE 135: The Day Before The Day of the Dead

8 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last time we met on COURAGE 134, something unexpected happened. Every active title was up for grabs. And not only that, but the Champions did something that they had yet to do while on tour in Mexico. Every Champion retained. Would this same adage hold true for the final Courage before DIA DE LOS MUERTOS 2010? This week, only the Tag Team titles are up for grabs, but that doesn't mean they're the only thing at stake. Mexico has proven wild, not only for every Champion within ACW, but those who have worked for the company. Equipment has gone missing, security officials and production staff have been hurt by warring drug cartels. Just earlier in the month, COURAGE 134 had scheduling difficulties due to pieces of the ring being stolen. Not to mention the sudden disappearance of the nation's own Oso Bannon, rumored to be a son of a very unsavory gentlemen within this embattled country. The road to LEGENDS V has certainly not been kind. To say good-bye to Mexico, before the farewell pay-per-view on this beleaguered tour, COURAGE 135 is 100% sweet parejas action! As you will see in the transcript each match is contested in a tag team set-up, including the special trios match-up announced on last week's show for the ACW's own Campeonatos de Parejas. Speaking of odd couples... Forcibly friendly rivals in the fine art of ACW-wide terrorism, ACW owner Jeremy Hunt and ACW World Champion Z will no doubt be speaking about the state of ACW and there sect's plans for DIA DE LOS MUERTOS!
