COURAGE 124: Jesse Ramey versus Ryan Billows? Oh yeah!

24 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the 124th edition (kind of) of COURAHE just days away from airing, news of a HUGE match has been announced by the office of SilverHAWK. It seems that after his planned match against former World Champion Trey Vincent did not end as planned, with Alias stepping in for a 'missing' Jesse Ramey... the 2010 King Of Ages has now been given a concrete shot at becoming the Spirit Of ACW Champion. After putting on a fourth-quarter entry for COURAGE Match of The Year, Ryan Billlows will have to shake free the bruises to face what would arguably be his toughest challenge to date in the Anti-Star. And how will Alias react to the announcement of Billows/Ramey? The Original Pulp Hero might have lost last week against Trey Vincent, but not for lack of trying. Instead, it was because of the undue involvement of Keith Scott Zimmerman, a man who just might have a more storied history with Alias then any other current ACW superstar. Will Alias look to involve himself in another of what is sure to be a sweet sports entertainment invention, courtesy of The Risen Lion? Will Jesse Ramey prove once and for all that there is NOTHING the Pulp Hero can take from him? Will Billows continue being so damn awesome? Well... probably, maybe and fuckin' A.
