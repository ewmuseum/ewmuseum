EXCLUSIVE NEWS: Shane Robertson in extremely critical condition.

28 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As many loyal All-Star Championship Wrestling viewers may have seen on the latest edition of COURAGE, one of the All-Star Academy’s new up-and-comers was given a chance to truly shine, only to be shot down horribly. Shane Robertson, whose hook was that he was a man on a mission from the Vatican to cleanse the professional wrestling world of all evil, surely couldn’t have expected what transpired on COURAGE 112. Robertson, 31, who was in no way or shape a young pup, had finally been given the opportunity to feature on actual ACW programming thanks to the help of his mentor and trainer Jesse Ramey. However, it seemed as though something had snapped with Ramey this past week on COURAGE 112 during Shane’s inaugural appearance on COURAGE… and the result was tragedy of the highest order. The beating the new star was given by his trainer has left Robertson in a coma and a vegetative state. As it goes with most professional wrestlers, Robertson did not have any immediate family at the time to comment on his condition. But we are being told that after seeing what damage he had done, Jesse Ramey is going to be footing the hospital bill for his former student. Also at this time, there is no word of Robertson being transported from the Ukraine back to the United States because of the severity of his condition. The doctors we were able to speak with had stated that there was no brain function from Robertson as of yet and that if there are no signs of life in the coming months they will be forced to just ‘pull the plug’. This is truly a sad day in the eyes of the ACW management team. But with the conditions and stipulations being set in place for this leg of ACW’s tour, punishment for Ramey’s actions will not happen. A comment from ACW’s General Manager SilverHAWK was as quoted: “Jesse was following the rules that Jeremy Hunt set forth for this tour. I cannot validate punishing him for only doing what the rules said and abiding by them. Jesse is going to have to live with his actions for the rest of his life, and hopefully this will be a sign to Hunt as well.”
