On The Road #5 by Jenna McMullen - Ja Gi Kyung-Moon.

15 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Hi, Ja Gi Kyung-Moon, and welcome to ACW! It's good to have the chance to speak with you and I hope that a newcomer such as yourself will feature more prominently on the shows. Anyways, first question; how's it been in Ukraine so far, days away from COURAGE 111? JAGI: Fortunately my wonderful agent and manager Amelia Greatbridge has arranged for us to stay at the Victoria Hotel in Donest'k, Ukraine. It's a luxury hotel, so I just stay there. I ventured out of town just once and I've not wanted to go out since. There's nothing to do in this town. Even VirtualTourist lists most of the things to engage in Donest'k involve just walking around aimlessly or standing and thinking. If I can do that in the comfort of 5 star accommodations why on earth would I spend my time dodging the construction sites that are EVERYWHERE in this city. Metropolitan urban center, fifth largest city in Ukraine... this place runs on coal mining. We might as well be wrestling in West Virginia. JENNA: West Viriginia's a nice place, though! What did you think of LEGENDS IV, overall? JAGI: The whatnow? JENNA: Um, you know, the pay-per-view we just had? Never-the-mind; who's your favourite wrestler in ACW now? Why? JAGI: SJH. I've watched him since EPW and I like the cut of his jib. Also I have no idea who these other people are. JENNA: Fair enough. Yet another one who hasn't picked Keller. Who's the wrestler you hate most in ACW now? Why? JAGI: Trent Savage. That rat bastard. JENNA: Um, Trent's actually a referee. But that's alright, you're new. Okay, since you can clearly see my nipples jutting out like sore thumbs, I'd like to ask what you think of them. Also, is this Ukrainian weather too cold for you? JAGI: In anticipation of your nipples, my manager has asked that I wear this blindfold. So I don't know what to think of them; however, I've heard nice things. As for the weather, I grew up in the state of Michigan, on the coast. It's got three seasons -- dry, wet, and Day After Tomorrow. Ukraine can't throw anything at me I haven't already dealt with. JENNA: I loved that movie! If you knew me growing up, would you have asked me to prom? JAGI: As an adult I have more discretionary income and don't mind throwing down the C-notes, but as a teen I'd have had to know somebody before I committed to spending hundreds of dollars on them for a date. JENNA: Hmmm. I'll take that as a yes. Aaaaand finally, what can your fans expect from you in the months to come? JAGI: Wrestling matches, I presume. Listen, I have a massage scheduled and then dinner. You want in? JENNA: Wow! Okay, sure. Leave the blindfold on, though!
