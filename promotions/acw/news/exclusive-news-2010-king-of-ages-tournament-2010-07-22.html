EXCLUSIVE NEWS: 2010 King Of Ages Tournament.

22 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a six-year hiatus, the King Of Ages Tournament is back in full swing! And following the monumental Preliminary Qualifying Battle Royale on COURAGE 116, the participants for the next phase of the 2010 King Of Ages Tournament are known to everybody; with Z, the winner of aforementioned Battle Royale, getting a bye all the way to the Semi-Finals. Yet, Z will still be involved in the next phase of the Tournament. And now, to tell you all about that exciting next phase. It's all about Matchplay, baby. We can't reveal too much about the dynamics about Matchplay just yet, but we promise the beans shall be spilt in due time. For now, perhaps you'd like to see the line-up of matches arranged for COURAGE 117? Yes? Ask and ye shall receive, fans and critics alike! MATCHPLAY 1.1 - Rud Albion vs. Buddy Saxon MATCHPLAY 1.2 - Jade Argent vs. Z MATCHPLAY 1.3 - 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs vs. Khristain Keller MATCHPLAY 1.4 - Kellen 'Machine' Masters vs. Sepiroth Du Luc MATCHPLAY 1.5 - Jesse Ramey vs. Keith Scott Zimmerman Pretty awesome, huh? You'll just have to tune in on COURAGE 117 and find out how the above-listed matches will tie back to the impending Quarter-Finals of the 2010 King Of Ages Tournament! Remember, whoever wins the Tournament... gets the King's Choice. The power to get anything the heart desires. Yes, we know. We rock.
