ACW - Under The Spotlight

14 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keith Scott Zimmerman 1) Tell us a little about KSZ for those who don't know him? I'm the Best Wrestler Alive today, and I'm going to prove it in short order. Action? Made it flourished, two titles with & beating Hall Of Famers, then when they tried to WCW me I helped lead a massive walk out that crippled the joint. Squared Circle? Won another belt in one of the best, most-horrifying matches of all-time, and got my lovely wife around me. Oh, Coral. I should really send him a fruit basket or something. Went to the fWo, won the Internet Championship three times, went undefeated for six months, beat like seven former fWo World Champions on my way to holding it myself. And the ACW World Heavyweight Championship is next on the list. 2) What brought you to ACW this time around? Were you not paying attention three seconds ago? THE ACW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! After that? All the rest of the titles, even the damned Scorpion. Nobody's ever accomplished the Grand Slam here, and when I do THAT? Nobody will ever be able to doubt my skillset ever again. 3) What are your memories of your last stint in ACW? People were cheering me. I'm still not exactly sure why. 4) Your favourite wrestler and why? I don't have a favorite wrestler, so would that make Keller the default? How does that work? 5) If you could fight anyone at Legends, who would it be? Alias for the Championship. Obviously.
