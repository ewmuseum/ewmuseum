END GAME 2010: One door closes, and another door opens.

26 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the weeks following COURAGE 130, the hype for END GAME 2010 has grown tenfold. And why shouldn't it? With so many matches and so much at stake, it was slated to be ACW's biggest pay-per-view of all time. The most important event in the federation's rich existence. Since that fateful day that SilverHAWK got kicked in the head, life has gone on. Everybody geared up for END GAME 2010, and with it the demanding End Game Match that had evolved to be a game-changer on so many levels. WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 005 was meant to provide everyone with a teaser of what was to come. It did, but not in its intended format. Nonetheless, anticipation reached an all-time high in light of grim news that while everybody's favourite General Manager was alright... his condition hadn't improved all that much. He was still in a coma, and a question mark was raised over who would represent him in ACW's last stand against Jeremy Hunt and God's Forgotten Son. The days passed. Press conferences were held to promote END GAME 2010, and what lay ahead for ACW thereafter. Mainly, the end of the tour of the European continent. Where was ACW going next, in January and February of 2011? Mexico. A brand new market, ripe with die-hard fans ready for ACW's hybrid mix of hard-hitting wrestling and dramatic entertainment. And then, it happened. It was time for END GAME 2010. My name is Archibald [name withheld]. People call me Archie all the time. I've been the webmaster for the ACW web-portal for a long time now, despite my young age. I haven't always been timely with updates or quick to polish up sections of the portal as they should be. But I've had a good excuse; I'm so in love with what ACW brings to the table with each show, I'm often very distracted. Tonight, as we're officially into 2011, I have to sit down and confess something to you all. No spoilers here, but you can probably take a stab in the dark: it's not going to be the same anymore. And that terrifies the shit out of me. And out of everyone who watched END GAME 2010 last night. Still, there is hope. And I, along with the millions of ACW fans, will cling on to that great hope. For it is all that we have right now. END GAME 2010 was a roller-coaster of emotion, but one thing's for sure. Even in these uncertain times, there's always room for a little heroism. Onward to 2011, then. Onward to a brave new world. Because that's just it. We all have to be brave now. Even myself. ... Even if it's going to be the hardest thing in the world to do.
