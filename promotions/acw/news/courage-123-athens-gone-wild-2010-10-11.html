COURAGE 123: Athens Gone Wild.

11 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Athens. One of the world's oldest and most historic cities. All-Star Championship Wrestling. One of the world's most recognisable sports entertainment (i.e. professional wrestling) organisations. Combine the two. What do you get? Simple; the 123rd (kind of) episode of COURAGE. And after last week's adventure, there was every chance that COURAGE 123 would serve up even more twists and turns. Guess what? It did. + Keith Scott Zimmerman decided to be proactive in his war against Trey Vincent. + Khristain Keller was given some help in HIS vendetta against his rival Jade Argent. + 'The Two' Booster Reid tried to recover from his humiliating debut performance last week. + Ryan Billows decided to show the world just why he's so effin good at what he does. + Alias and Jesse Ramey found new and creative ways to irritate each other. And so much more. Do yourselves a favour: get your favourite beverage, get comfortable and dig into the transcript. Be warned, though. The transcript might make you hate ACW for being so awesome. It's a risk we're willing to take, though. LOL JK GTFO.
