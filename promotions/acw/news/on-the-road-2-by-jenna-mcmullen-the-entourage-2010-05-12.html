On The Road #2 by Jenna McMullen - The Entourage.

12 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Hi, guys! It's really good to have the chance to speak with you. Anyways, first question; how's it been in Ukraine so far, days away from COURAGE 111? SAUNDERS: Unlike the rest employed under the All-Star banner, we have a choice to not be here; and thankfully so, earn up to it. If it weren't for SilverHAWK requesting our presence today, you wouldn't see us until COURAGE 111? CALLIE: Do you know how many business meetings we had to cancel and reschedule to make it to this god-forsaken place today? JENNA: Tell me about it, I had a really important pedicure appointment that I had to reschedule. Bummer. So, what did you think of LEGENDS IV overall? Good or bad? CALLIE: We were screwed. SAUNDERS: Well, we can finally possibly quite practically have a World division based on talent and not money thanks to Trey. At least I hope so. Can't say I'm surprised that Rej lost; he never could keep up with either of us in the past. As for England's Best, they can have their moment... for now. They won... CALLIE: Yet we were screwed! JENNA: Okay, who's your favourite wrestler in ACW now? Why? Pick one each, or I'll get confused. CALLIE: You expect everyone to say someone else other than themselves, right? The Entourage should be everyone's favorites. We're simply better at this than every single person on the bankroll combined. SAUNDERS: Also Trey, to answer your question. JENNA: Okay, so none for Keller yet. Ha! Who's the wrestler you hate most in ACW now? Why? SAUNDERS: Hate, isn't a word we would choose to use. CALLIE: Between mine and Spike's combined history, we have little doubt that we irk each person in some way or have had some encounter in the past. But the one person we don't hate is you Jenna. JENNA: Yay! Okay, since you can clearly see my nipples jutting out like sore thumbs, I'd like to ask what you think of them. Also, is this Ukrainian weather too cold for you? SAUNDERS: If you choose to appear like this, great... your choice. The wrong one, but yours. CALLIE: What he means is they are great. But seriously Jenna, sweetie, why don't you drop the ditz routine and work with us on the road? Better benefits, big pay increase, and no need to subsidize yourself to the moronic denizens. SAUNDERS: Plus the perks. JENNA: Yeah, they ARE pretty perky! And awww, thank you, Callie! I'll definitely think about the offer, too. Now, next question which is especially for Spike; if you knew me growing up, would you have asked me to prom? SAUNDERS: ... JENNA: I'll take that as you would! Aaaaand finally, what can your fans expect from you in the months to come? SAUNDERS: We're on a mission, and that mission has yet to be accomplished. CALLIE: Until ACW is cleansed -- saved from the corrupted pests -- The Entourage will continue to upstage everyone else in the ring.
