Max Danger to return at End Game?!?!?!?

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For the past, let's say month or so, maybe less, we've been bombarded with emails, text messages, picture messages, video clips, and even actual, hand-written letters, about ACW's year-end supershow extravaganza, End Game. They were not from fans. They were not from hopeful participants from around the globe for the End Game match itself. No, instead, all communication came from one man. A self-proclaimed king. Sorry, King. Capital "K". He left ACW with nary a word said, just that he was going where it wasn't so cold anymore. But now he's coming back. Coming back to, as he said, "regain what is rightfully mine", meaning the ACW World Championship. And to do this, he's entered his name into the End Game Match. He won the ACW World Championship in a battle royale and End Game is also a game of survival. Is the ultimate chicken shit capable of, against all odds, coming out on top again? "Yes," he said. We'll see at End Game.
