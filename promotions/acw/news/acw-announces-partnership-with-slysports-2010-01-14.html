ACW Announces Partnership With SlySports

14 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACW, Stephen and Nathan Roy are very glad to announce that the rights to the ACW brand have been purchased by SlySports. The deal, which will bring in the company and the brothers an estimated $100m finally allows the company to produce live television events for the first time in a number of years. It also means that ACW will for the time being be moving continents and touring around Europe, where SlySports is majorly subscriped to all over the Main Lands. We managed to get word with both Nathan and Stephen Roy to gauge how this would change ACW as a whole. "We are ecstatic to announce the deal we have made with SlySports as it will change ACW as we know it." Stephen Roy said. "Since we relaunched the brand last year, the fans have been tremendous around Canada, and it is because of them that we now have this opportunity to take our product across the World and into Europe. We realised that North America is blessed with an abundance of wrestling companies and their products so we and SlySports decided to take our show overseas, and what better place to begin than the United Kingdom in just a matter of weeks?" "As a company we have worked hard to build the reputation up from what it was," said Nathan Roy. "However this deal shows what our good work and the work of everyone involved has gotten us. Here we now have ACW, with a regular timeslot and now a captive audience to entertain. We will go bigger, brighter and better into the decade and we hope to have a number of top class athletes showing you just what ACW is all about." SlySports did issue a statement saying it plans to show ACW Shows at around a 10pm slot so that it will miss the watershed and the product will not be diluted. This is indeed a big step for the company as a whole, and we hope to see all of you Europeans snapping up the first tickets for shows when they go on sale tomorrow. We would like to thank everyone that made this possible, and we hope 2010 is the year ACW becomes your favourite product to watch.
