COURAGE 126: Here we go. Lock and load.

18 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The edition of COURAGE after a pay-per-view almost always promises a bit of upheaval and a ton of major surprises. It makes it appear as if that particular edition of COURAGE is like a televised pay-per-view of some sort. COURAGE 126 -- which just so happens to be the first ACW live event to take place in Italy in over a decade and a half (dating back to when ACW didn't even have a television show) -- exceeded all expectations. And while no titles changed hands, there were enough shocking moments to ensure massive creaming of pants. So, whatcha waiting for? Go ahead and check out the transcript of COURAGE 126. Find out which former ACW World Champion left ACW forever. Find out who's the new #1 Contender to the World Title. Find out... aahhhh, you know what? Just, go and read it. Now, preferably. You absolutely will not regret it.
