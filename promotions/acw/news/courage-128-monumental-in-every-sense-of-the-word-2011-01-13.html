COURAGE 128: Monumental, in every sense of the word.

13 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been an extremely hectic period for all at ACW lately... but, we promise; it'll only get better from here on out! Not to mention absolutely unpredictable. Can't forget about that. Before we get to the matter at hand, a quick recap. Yes? Yes! + After the groundbreaking IN ACROPOLIS 2010 pay-per-view, COURAGE 126 marked the first show of ACW's tour of Italy, and it ended with a veritable game-changer: Jeremy Hunt and God's Forgotten Son revealed just who they really are, sending shockwaves throughout the entire organisation! + Shortly after that, it was announced that ACW was expanding its sphere of influence by acquiring majority stock of a little regional promotion in the United States: New York's Finest! NYF would thereby be christened as ACW's first independent developmental territory, solidifying plans to propel ACW to the top of the professional wrestling mountain. + On the heels of the shocking ending of COURAGE 126, Jeremy Hunt commissioned the airing of an exclusive special telecast that delved into the rich and incredible history of the Spirit Of ACW Championship. Be warned, however; The Life, Death And Rebirth Of... The Spirit Of ACW is a broadcast that breaks kayfabe at numerous points. Consider that an outright disclaimer, if that sort of thing isn't your cup of tea. + And as if one developmental territory wasn't enough, ACW went ahead to announce the acquisition of the majority stock of another regional promotion in the United States: West Coast Championship Wrestling! A surprising but bold move, summing up the turbulent partnership ACW and SlySports Entertainment LLC have gone through ever since their union at the start of the 2010 calendar year. + What was next? COURAGE 127, of course! With the lines in the sand drawn, The Huntsmen and SilverHAWK's band of warriors began laying out a plan for resolution, that'll culminate at END GAME 2010 with extremely high stakes. The problem was, as the night drew to its conclusion, everybody forgot all about The Masked Enigma and his tribe of terrorist rebels. And Z proceeded to make everybody pay, standing tall amongst the carnage as the broadcast drew to a close. Now, what else could we possibly have to offer? Oh, I don't know. How about a transcript to the most watched programme in the history of SlySports Entertainment LLC? Yeah, that sounds good, doesn't it? 31.20 million viewers on its original airing on SlySports' SPORTS-1 channel. So many good matches. So many bombs dropped. The road to END GAME 2010 may be nearing, but COURAGE 128 opens up so many questions... you'll be left wondering two things. 1. How does ACW plan to wrap everything up by END GAME 2010? 2. How does ACW keep on doing these spectacular shows? ... You'll just have to keep watching, natch.
