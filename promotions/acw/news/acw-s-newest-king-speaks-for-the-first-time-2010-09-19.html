ACW's newest King speaks for the first time.

19 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Weeks ago, ACW had a summer blockbuster of a pay-per-view. The man that walked away from Stockholm with all the plaudits? None other than ACW's newest King -- Jesse Ramey. And as the All-Stars touched down in Greece in preparation for COURAGE 121 and the next arc of events, the lovely Jenna McMullen managed to snag an unofficial interview with Jesse. No doubt, the Anti-Star will have more to say on COURAGE 121 regarding what happened at the pay-per-view. But for now? Why not kick back and check out the banter between Jenna and Jesse? -- Jenna: It’s my pleasure to get to sit down and talk with you once again, Jesse. The biggest question everyone has on their minds is how does it feel being the 2010 King Of Ages? Jesse: For ten long months, I’ve struggled to get over that slump, Jenna; my less-than-fantastic re-debut at END GAME 2009, 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs, Khristain Keller & Jade Argent, and then Khristain Keller in singles competition. Four big match situations and the chance to finally shine were just outside of my grasp. The entire 2010 King Of Ages Tournament will all go down in my record book as the defining moment of my career. I’ve been wrestling for nineteen years, and I’m thirty-four years old. That doesn’t sound old to many people, but in this business people look at that as being over the hill. You’re too old to be doing this kind of thing any more, and you can’t compete with the young bucks that are in their 18s or even in their early 20s. On the 26th of August 2010, I proved my worth to this company, and I didn’t need any help getting to the top. I didn’t need to rely on anything other than the talent I’ve been putting out my entire career. I told people that it was going to be my time to shine, and it was far overdue, but I’ve finally proven my critics wrong. I’ve finally put the nail in that coffin that’s been haunting me for months. My first big match win, and it has to be the sweetest big match win anyone could have ever expected. The only people I can really thank for this moment Jenna are the loyal followers of the Dark Ages, my court helped me to this moment and I owe to them all of the thanks. Jenna: That’s all nice and stuff, Jesse. I guess. As far as I can tell you really got things rolling going into the pay-per-view as well, finally staking claim to a win over Khristain Keller. That must have been a sweet moment for you too, huh? Jesse: Khristain Keller. Obviously people, especially Khristain himself, seem to forget that this wasn’t the first time I pinned his shoulders to the mat. Let me give you all a little history lesson on the past ten months. COURAGE 108, I beat Khristain Keller. LEGENDS IV, Jade Argent may have gone on to beat me in the heart-pounding climax of The World Series, but I was the man who eliminated Khristain from that match-up. COURAGE 115, Khristain Keller’s team defeated mine in a hell of a 10-men tag team free-for-all. COURAGE COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER, Khristain Keller defeated me in the first-ever Four Walls Battle. And on COURAGE 120, I defeated Khristain Keller in what many would call our rubber match. The score is set at three-to-two, Keller; but as far as I’m concerned going man to man with you Keller our record is still in my favor two to one. The battle between you and I is far from over, and it wouldn’t surprise me that as long as the two of us are still competing if this thing doesn’t see a hundred matches culminate within the squared circle of All-Star Championship Wrestling. Jenna: Yeah, once again that’s nice and all. Sometimes I don’t quite understand your way of explaining things. Jesse, can you leak us any information as to what is next for you and what your King’s Choice may entail? Jesse: Until you have proven yourself loyal to stand amongst the rest of the members of the court of the Dark Ages Jenna, you will be in the dark just as much as anyone else. I do not have to tell my loyal subjects what my King’s Choice decision is going to be; they already know the answer to that question. And none of them will question that decision as they know it will be the best decision for the kingdom. I know what I am going to do with my King’s Choice, as for the rest of ACW. They will all find out in due time. Jenna: And to conclude this interview Jesse, something that has been attracting a lot of buzz over the past couple of months is the resurgence of the wrestling promotion Legacy of Champions. The majority of your fans know that you were a staple of this promotion for the longest time; what are the chances of you jumping ship to work in your former stomping grounds? Jesse: Let’s veer off the record for a moment here, Jenna; Legacy of Champions and Black Tom Williams had their chance to secure me to a contract before the last time the promotion went under. He didn’t act on my offer to keep me an exclusive talent in LoC and that’s their loss. All-Star Championship Wrestling, SilverHAWK, and even Jeremy Hunt have done leaps and bounds more for my career than Tom Williams ever thought about doing. Why anyone would want to jump back to that place is beyond me. Actually, no, it isn’t beyond me; either you just couldn’t cut it in any other promotion running or you just like being smacked around like a little bitch. Well, you can all have fun being driven into the ground in a backstage atmosphere that is surrounded by nothing, but politics. My home is All-Star Championship Wrestling, and that’s not coming from 'The Anti-Star' Jesse Ramey. That’s coming from Jesse Ramey the human being. I’ll continue to work in this promotion until the day I die, and although my blood once ran the colors of the Legacy of Champions, I’ve had a very successful blood transfusion of the All-Star Championship Wrestling. Jenna: Well Jesse, thanks for another intriguing and quite odd interview. At least the website seems to get pretty good hits from the stranger crowd when we do these things with you.
