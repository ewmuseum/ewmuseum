LEGENDS IV BUILD: SilverHAWK gets the ball officially rolling.

22 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fresh off the news report that revealed the General Manager's booking of the Four-Corners Match for the ACW Tag Team Titles, SilverHAWK held a press conference earlier today to announce that it was time to start the hype machine for LEGENDS IV. He began by stating that in lieu of the unique mechanics of the main event for the pay-per-view, both Alias and Trey Vincent would be handing over the ACW World Champion and the $1,000,000 respectively during the event itself. SilverHAWK also touched on the growing feud between Sven Avsbern and Kristinia Kage. Two broadcasts of COURAGE, two Scorpion Fighting Rules bouts, and nothing had been resolved. HAWK officially announced a rubber match-up for LEGENDS IV, but in a surprise twist, revealed that the contest would be fought under Standard Rules. Funnily enough, HAWK's phone rang just seconds after that; it was Sven Avsbern, who promptly called the General Manager a cunt before hanging up. SilverHAWK explained that he wanted to see Sven and Kristinia, both of whom were more at home with the stylings of the Scorpion Fighting division, resolve their differences in a more traditional way. SilverHAWK went on to comment on the situation involving Khristain Keller, Jesse Ramey and Jade Argent. With the World Series being tied up with each wrestler having one victory to his name, HAWK hinted that there would be a resolution planned... but chose not to spell out the details. Once again, the General Manager's phone rang. It was Khristain Keller, who was more elaborate in his cursing-out of HAWK than Sven Avsbern was moments earlier. After ending the call, HAWK justified his inability to make a decision on what the finale of the World Series would entail by ensuring he had something good in store. The press conference ended with SilverHAWK promising that a promotional video for LEGENDS IV would be launched on the ACW website within the next few days, and that the video would be making the rounds on SlySports in the form of a commercial.
