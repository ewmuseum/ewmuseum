COURAGE 124: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

24 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
COURAGE 124 is days away, and there's a lot to be excited about. * As confirmed earlier in the week, Jesse Ramey will be challenging Ryan Billows for the Spirit Of ACW Championship. One must wonder how Alias will figure into this, seeing how he messed with Ramey last week. * After a couple of wins under his belt, Fight Man will be given the chance to challenge for the [Black] Scorpion Title. Omega's going to have his work cut out for him. After all, Fight Man likes to fight! * Two matches, two defeats for 'The Two' Booster Reid. This week, the young man has the chance to rectify matters but he'll have to do it against Fejona Min. This will mark the first match for the Rogue Slayer this year contested under Standard Rules; how will she fare? * The Night Life have made quite an impact ever since debuting. They will have the opportunity to make further waves, when the tribe is involved in a 12-Person Tag Team Match. Their opponents? Irish Spring, Abilities and The Entourage. Oh yeah, baby, ACW's tag team division just keeps on getting better. * Speaking of which, newbz The Payne Gang will take on Ulf German and Der Deutscher Soldat in the opening match of the night. Will the two burly Germans finally pick up a win, or will the new kids on the block clinch their first televised victory? * Having settled his travel issues, Arkady Rasputin will be gracing the ACW squared circle for the first time since the KING OF AGES 2010 pay-per-view. And the Russian Wolf will go one-on-one with Orlando Grant. Orlando very impressively beat 'The Two' Booster Reid last week, and will be hoping to nab another win to build up some much-needed momentum. * And finally, the big one. Keith Scott Zimmerman and Trey Vincent will do battle... but in tag team action. Yes, the World Champion and his fiercest rival will have to pick two partners for a Six-Man Tag Team Match. Who will each man choose? We'll only find out when the show gets underway. All of that, and so much more. COURAGE 124 is shaping up to be awesome. ... Did you honestly expect anything else from us? :-)
