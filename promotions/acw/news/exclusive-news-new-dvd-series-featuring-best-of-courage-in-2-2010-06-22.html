EXCLUSIVE NEWS: NEW DVD SERIES FEATURING BEST OF COURAGE IN 2010 ON THE WAY.

22 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As ACW touched down in Moscow for COURAGE 115 coming up in a few days, General Manager SilverHAWK took time out to conduct a press conference regarding an exciting new DVD project. "Basically, we've had some stellar matches on COURAGE over the past six months. And thus, we're going to release a Best Of COURAGE In 2010 compilation, featuring the matches that the fans themselves have deemed as the best!" HAWK explained, looking a little worse for wear and also scowling more than usual. "Volume 1 of the series will cover matches from COURAGE 101 to COURAGE 105, and that will be hitting stores from July onwards. Of course, as we're in Europe now and SlySports... being based in the UK, this means that the Europeans will get their chance to purchase this DVD first. I expect that fans back in the States might have to wait until August or possibly even September. Still, I think it'll be worth the wait, and extremely good value for money." SilverHAWK also revealed that there were plenty more DVD releases in the pipeline, to boost up ACW's DVD library. He mentioned that the DVD releases in 2010 were part of a brand new initiative to target a more international fanbase, which has quadrupuled ever since ACW began their tour of Europe in January earlier in the year. Already, we have a listing of matches that are confirmed to be on the DVD: COURAGE 101 Chris Moliano vs. Trevor Wilson Alias & Shawn Jessica Hart vs. Keith Scott Zimmerman & Trey Vincent COURAGE 102 Omega vs. Sharc vs. Kati vs. Rory Hayes vs. El Hombre Sin Nombre vs. Iceman vs. Landon Stevens Trey Vincent vs. Alias COURAGE 103 Sharc vs. Iceman Heirs Of Wrestling vs. Vox Nihili COURAGE 104 Iceman vs. Kristinia Kage 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs & Trey Vincent vs. Jesse Ramey & Shawn Jessica Hart Omega vs. Alias COURAGE 105 Rud Albion vs. Buddy Saxon Z vs. Chris Moliano Scott Riktor vs. Alias
