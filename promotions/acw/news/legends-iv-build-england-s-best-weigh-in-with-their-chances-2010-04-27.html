LEGENDS IV BUILD: England's Best weigh in with their chances of victory.

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After SilverHAWK announced that the ACW Tag Team Titles would be defended in a Four-Corners Match, speculation has been rife with what each team has to say about that bit of news. The Entourage and Irish Spring weren't available for comment. Ditto for the Tag Team Champs, the Pop Culture Phenoms. The newest team on the block, however, chimed in with their thoughts of what they felt was going to be one heck of a showdown at LEGENDS IV. Buddy Saxon, one-half of England's Best, was giddy at the opportunity to challenge for the Tag Team Titles. "I mean, Rud and I knew after our win over The SOBs on COURAGE 110 would put us in contention. And sure, maybe having two other teams in there will be tricky business! But hey, this is LEGENDS IV~! I'm sure SilverHAWK thought of this Four-Corners Match to have maximum enjoyment, and I'm really looking forward to it! Rud and I didn't initially anticipate having Irish Spring thrown into the mix along with those rascals, Spike and Callie. However! I did say some time ago that I was hoping to be involved in a legendary showcase at LEGENDS IV, and this Four-Corners Match for the ACW Tag Team Titles is as legendary as it can get! Yay!" Rud Albion was more strategic in his views. "Indeed, having four teams battling it out with the first person to get a pinfall or submission bringing an end to the match means there are a lot of variables to consider. England's Best nonetheless are up for the challenge and we will assist to demonstrate why All-Star Championship Wrestling has one of the fiercest tag team divisions in the business. At the same time, Buddy and myself will be looking to walk out of Barcelona with the Tag Team Titles after what is sure to be a tooth-and-nail fight, purely because both of us have come to relish these sort of big-match environments. It will not be easy, but then again, nothing in this business was ever meant to be a cakewalk. England's Best will bring forth its mightiest challenge that will ensure nobody ever thinks we are nothing more than an empty vessel blowing out hot air." While the views of the other three teams remained to be seen, it is clear that everybody involved in this mammoth match-up will be primed for what is guaranteed to be the most legendary showcase of the tag team division in the history of ACW!
