Dear Shareholder

10 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Ladies, gentlemen, and children of all ages, I welcome you to the Danger Zone as soon as we get the quote/unquote 'water pipes' fixed for a historic night of wrestling! On this night I'm going to prove to Brian Spaes and his little revolution that the sorry, pathetic, and extremely unattractively pale fans of ACW do have the control. Sounds exciting, yes?" "On Thursday, August 13th, provided these quote/unquote 'water pipes' are, like previously mentioned, fixed, I will be defending the ACW World Championship, the most prestigious and shiny and golden belt in the whole world (and Canada) against whomever the shareholders vote as their top choice to attempt to dethrone the King -- which is quite unlikely, but I"m not the type of person to quench one's dreams." "I will personally pick up your ballots as the show unfolds and announce the winner before that night's main event, which will be the match in question. So, please, vote for your Andy Sharps, your Kelly Masterssss, your Ikan Jobtayoos, or, heck, even your Brian Spaeseses if you truly believe in the work he's doing to save Christmas or puppies or whatever his revolution is supposed to be doing, besides being an annoyance to everyone." The following message was paid for by, well, nobody. Max Danger just sent it in for submission.
