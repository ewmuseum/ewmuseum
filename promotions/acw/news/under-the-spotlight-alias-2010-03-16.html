Under The Spotlight - ALIAS

16 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1) You have just defeated SJH at ACWs first PPV in 3 and a half years, tell us how you are feeling? On edge. Now I wasn't there for the final ACW PPV, so this is my first ACW PPV in closer to four years. The experiences I've had on ACW supershow's though, whether it was Legends III, The Wake or End Games, prepared me better then I could have ever imagined for FQ&C... and shit, I made it out alive. 2) What is your thoughts on the current roster that ACW has? I want to say this now, so that everyone knows it... we are the strongest we have ever been. Ever. ACW's early years in the 90's are called the Golden Era by some folks, and those weeks after the first Legends had spirits running high, hell, even the shows after the One-Off that wasn't just that, had a lot of people excited about ACW's future... but what we have in place right now? With those coming out of the Academy, with the spirit of this European tour... this current roster has me on edge, as the top dog. I got no idea what'll happen next. 3) Speaking of which... when will KWolf join ACW? When she wants to, and not a moment sooner then that. Never count out Vox Nihili, though. 4) Can you name your top five favourite people to work with? Ever? Well shit. [laughter] Starbuck, natch. I'm not going to count Vince, because that goes without saying as much as counting Wolfenden would. So the five besides, I'm gonna have to say this ain't no 'top' list and'll be more off the top of my head when it comes to favourite folks... Osyrus, who always felt like the anti-Alias at least in the those first years back in ACW. He pushed me to be a bigger star. High Flyer is one hardest working men in this business, and if it takes another hundred Courage's until our next match... then so be it, I'll wait. Jesse Ramey is a new favourite of mine. People better not be surprised about that pick either, after seeing the fight he gave Vince at the PPV. Kelly Masters was someone that gave me such an unexpected run for my money in such a short time, and was the type of real takin' bastard that I wish were around more often. One of the people that I really enjoyed wrestling against, but who's career ended before he could reach his greatest potential is Quinton May. Don't know what happened to the sumbitch but he certainly had more in him. 5) Do you have anything left in ACW to do? Long as I'm livin' and breathin' I'll have something left to do in ACW. I am honored to be the only active Hall of Famer in ACW... Thing is, if you wanna qualify 'anything left to do' in terms of winning... I still haven't won End Game, or King of Ages either. And of all the titles I've won in ACW, including the golden strap that's around my waist at the moment, their's still one title left that I haven't won. Yeah, just one. And it's been in and around ACW longer then I have. I'm not one to hype, so I won't get much into which title I'm talkin' about. Suffice to say I'd be taking Wilson up on his offer at Legends, if we were able too. Except... Vincent'd complicate that, cause he's a son of a bitch. 6) Going to LEGENDS... and to Trey Vincent, how will you defeat him? I'm not going to factor in what happened at End Game, even if I did beat him then... cause the past ain't nothing but a series of semi-educational footnotes. But if I make it to Legends, and I will because I don't want to pull a Keller and lose the World title a week before the big show, then I'm going to bring the fight cause that's all I know how to do. One fist in front of the other, until TV is tapping out to the Anarchy's Lullaby. Again.
