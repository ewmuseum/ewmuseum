ON THE ROAD - Greece #1.

22 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Oh, Greece! I'm so happy we're here, because I've got a brand new suitcase of bikinis that I can definitely showcase and finally land a hot guy deserving of my high standards. But anyways; hello to you, Tyson XL! I'm Jenna McMullen, ACW's finest backstage interviewer amongst other things. You had a great debut on COURAGE 121. Tell me more about the fight with Rory Hayes. TYSON: Well, Jenna, it’s hardly a fight when the guy standing across from you is the equivalent of ACW’s welcome mat. The Amazing Gabriel wasn’t about to soil his hands on the Rory Hayes’ of the world, but I’m more than happy to snuff out the riff-raff for him. JENNA: Okay, interesting. So, have you ever been to Greece? I'm loving it so far. I fell in love with Xanthi because of all the culture and stuff, but I can't wait until we hit the other locations, especially ones with beaches. Are you a beach guy? TYSON: Mmmmm… bouncing slow-motion titties… JENNA: Yikes, okay. Ummmm, let's change the topic. What's the deal with this whole Night Life thing? Can you tell me anything about The Amazing Gabriel, your ringleader? Liiiike, for example; is he seeing anybody? TYSON: Like he said a few weeks ago, The Night Life is here to give ACW something that they can be entertained by. We don’t need to go where the party is because we ARE the party. And you might have to fight Elixr for a spot The Amazing Gabriel’s spot arm… actually, she might just like that… JENNA: Alright, then. Duly noted. What can we expect from YOU specifically in the months to come? TYSON: Whenever The Amazing Gabriel and the Night Life bring out the party, there’s always some drunken idiot fans, not to mention the various buzzkills that make up the ACW roster. My job is simple, Jenna… keep the riff-raff out. Keep them off our asses by any means necessary. JENNA: That sounds interesting. Anybody on the roster currently that you'd fancy doing battle with? Alternatively, is there anybody you DO NOT want to step into the ring with. TYSON: Anybody with the gold. The ACW titles are being held by a bunch of pretenders and paper champs. You’ve seen FP and ZZ get it done last week, so those tag titles are on our minds, maybe other titles. You’ll just have to wait and find out like everybody else. JENNA: I see. Finally, Tyson XL, I would like to ask how you move so quickly considering... well, you know. Your size. It isn't an attack! I'm just curious at how quick you are on your feet, and all. What's with that? TYSON: …You know that I could crush you, right? JENNA: Okay, so that's my cue to wrap this up. See you around!
