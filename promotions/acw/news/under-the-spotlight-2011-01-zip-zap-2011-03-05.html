UNDER THE SPOTLIGHT 2011/01: Zip Zap!

5 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hours after the climax of END GAME 2010, the ACW bandwagon readies itself for a couple of weeks of well-deserved rest before moving forward to Mexico. But one man never rests; that man is Renaud Cardinal, ACW's premiere play-by-play broadcaster/commentator. In his free time, Renaud also conducts interviews with noteworthy All-Stars. On this fair morning, the first of 2011, Renaud Cardinal runs into the one and only Zip Zap at the airport. Hilarity ensues. Well, not really. But read on anyways. RC: Well, it's 9:00am on 01 January 2011 and we're all waiting for the first plane out of Rome. Not surprising running into you here, Zip Zap! It's a good time as any to conduct an interview, don't you think? ZZ: I guess. RC: Right, so. My first question is simple; the last tweet on ACW's Chatterverse for the year 2010 was The Amazing Gabriel threatening to beat you upon returning to the hotel last night. Did he really do it? ZZ: Oh, no. *wincing as he sits down* That would never happen. RC: What’s that black eye, then? ZZ: Oh, this? Oh, The Amazing Gabriel was excited, he was gesturing and accidentally hit me in the face… six times… RC: I see. All in all, END GAME 2010 wasn't a very good night for you. Insulted by Gabriel earlier on, didn't get to represent The Night Life in the Four Ways To Glory Match and then swiftly eliminated in the End Game Match. It must have been scant comfort, then, that you got to at least hit your finishing move on Keith Scott Zimmerman? ZZ: Oh, yeah, that’s going on my highlight reel! I can’t help but feel I might have let the gang down, but Kaus was running an errand so they stuck me out there to take his place, last minute. I was in there for a whole minute, though. That was awesome. RC: What's your thoughts on Jeremy Hunt becoming ACW's new be-all and end-all? ZZ: That Jeremy Hunt guy just really needs to take it easy. He’s so high-strung. He needs to relax, chill out, smoke a bud, man. I’ve got some good stuff for the right price. RC: And how about... Z, becoming the new ACW World Champion? Any thoughts on that? ZZ: That guy scares me. He’s a guy in a mask. People with facial things… piercings… masks… they scare me and I’m not sure how to deal with that! RC: ACW moves on to Mexico next, but we won't be there for another 19 days for COURAGE 131. What will you and the rest of The Night Life be up to during the intervening period? ZZ: The Amazing Gabriel and the rest of the guys have all it charted out, but I’ve been given an important task. I’ve been designated The Amazing Gabriel’s personal baggage handler. I’ll be staying at the hotel and making sure nothing happens to his bags. You have no idea how daunting this task is, but it’s one I take with pride! RC: Any plans to pursue a singles career down the road, or do you see yourself in The Night Life forever? ZZ: I am Night Life, through and through! Gabriel says for me to go out there and win, I do it… well, except for at END GAME, but we won the Four Ways To Glory! Gabriel says to attack somebody, I do it! Gabriel says Night Life needs to take a break, I can do that! ... maybe… wait, can I get a do-over on that question? RC: No. There was some definite friction between Gabriel and Tyson all throughout the show last night. Care to expand on that? ZZ: Ty’s a good guy and The Amazing Gabriel is The Amazing Gabriel, so I’m sure they’ve got everything handled. RC: Okay, then. That's all I have. I'm due to board my plane to Paris in a few minutes. Thanks for your time, Zip Zap. ZZ: No problems!
