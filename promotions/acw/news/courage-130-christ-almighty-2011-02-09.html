COURAGE 130: Christ Almighty.

9 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Um. There's not much we can say about this show. Actually, there's a lot that we can say. Mainly because it was such a loaded show. A ton of guest surprises -- some of whom most ACW fans thought they'd never see again. Curveballs nobody would have expected. And an ending that left many an ACW faithful with a sickening lump in their throat. At the end of the day, COURAGE 130 broke all sorts of records; 46.53 million viewers on SlySports SPORTS-1 channel. An all-time record for SlySports. As far as everybody at ACW (who isn't aligned with the forces of evil) is concerned, however, all that came at a heavy price. Want to know what we're talking about? Go ahead and check out the transcript of COURAGE 130. You have been forewarned of the double dose of awesomeness and tragedy. Only at ACW. Only on SlySports. Recognise, bitches.
