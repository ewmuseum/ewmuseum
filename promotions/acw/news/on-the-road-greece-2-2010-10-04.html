ON THE ROAD - Greece #2.

4 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Ahhh, this is the life. After a hard night's work... at COURAGE 122, I finally get a chance to beach it up. After this? We go to Athens! I'm so excited. But first, I, the finest backstage interviewer in ACW history amongst other things, have the pleasure of talking to one sexy lady. Hello, Aria Murphy! It's me, Jenna McMullen. I haven't seen you -- or Arkady, for that matter -- since the KING OF AGES pay-per-view. What's up with that? Where's Arkady? ARIA: My beloved has been having visa issues due to some prior indescretions in this country. Seems he got himself in some trouble before joining ACW and they may have banned him from ever entering the country again. However, we found a way for him to still compete and he will be back on COURAGE 124. Thank God for good Jewish lawyers. JENNA: Okay, interesting. So, Kroenen and Vago are totally gone, huh? Will we see that X3 guy, again? He's a former FWO Cruiserweight Champion, isn't he? What's his relationship to Arkady, for those of us not in the know? ARIA: The wretched creatures you all know as the Supernatural SWAT had their contracts torn to shreds and lit on fire. Arkady beat the holy hell out of that piece of sh*t Kroenen and even if they stayed in ACW, they'd probably be hiding in a corner knee-deep in their own excrement, fearing that my beloved would pummel them again. As for my sensai and former FWO cruiserweight champion, Xin Xin Xiong; he is like a playful wind. He comes and goes as he pleases without a trace or worry. It's hard to tell when or where he will show up again. It's a roll of the dice. Arkady and he have no real connection outside of me. His appearance at the pay-per-view was my doing. I was not going to let the SS once again play the numbers game and cost someone a victory, especially not my beloved. JENNA: I see. Alright then. Aria, I wanted to ask; I understand you used to be an in-ring competitor yourself, and a pretty good one at that. Will we be seeing you compete here in ACW, or will you remain Arkady's... errr, consultant? ARIA: I am currently in training. While I may have some past accomplishments in the ring, I want to refine my skills. Xiong and Arkady are teaching me a more brutal and stiff offense. Too many females in this business run around and do the whole flippy thing due to their lack of size. They twist, spin, flip, and race around like a gymnast on speed. When I am done with my training, the only time my feet will leave the canvas is when I am driving them through someone's chest. JENNA: Gotcha. If I may be candid for a while, is there anything non-business going on between you and Arkady? ARIA: Did the whole 'my beloved' thing go right over your head, you worthless piece of eye candy? Or maybe the whole me kissing him before each match was too complex for you? Who did you blow to get this job? JENNA: Sorry, I had to ask! I thought it was superficial. And to answer your question; nobody... errr, that I remember. Uhhh, next question. How have you been enjoying the Greece tour so far? Get a chance to beach it up yet? ARIA: Actually, this is our first night here. We have been in Siberia, training during this time off. My beloved likes to practice his Muay Thai there. JENNA: Hmmm. That's nice. Finally, I would like to know if you had any thoughts on what went down on COURAGE 122. You did watch it, right? Omega winning the [Black] Scorpion Title, Ryan Billows winning the Spirit Of ACW, the war between Jade Argent and Khristain Keller, the other war between Z and Rud Albion; any of it catch your eye, for any reason? ARIA: Omega better hope he doesn't cross my path again. I should have stabbed him in the eye with switchblade when he tried to prey upon me. Arkady saved him from that fate, as he is psychotic and my beloved doesn't believe in fighting injured or damaged people. Billows, on the other hand, is on my short list of people who I want Arkady to kick into oblivion. What is with so many people in this place becoming whiny bitches? Ramey and then, Billows. No one cares if you're all moody and dark now. Shut the f*ck up and fight. Argent and Keller together in the ring? Sounds like Argent got his dream date. Next up, Lance Bass! The final pairing should be a no-brainer when it comes to my feelings. Albion is one of the few allies in this promotion and I loathe Z entirely. I hope Rud puts him in a coma and saves the world from anymore of Z's stupidity. Personally, I'd start by skinning him alive with a potato peeler. Then, I wo-- JENNA: --Okay, so that's my cue to wrap this up. See you around!
