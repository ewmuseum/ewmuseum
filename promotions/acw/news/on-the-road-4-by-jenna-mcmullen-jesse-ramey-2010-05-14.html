On The Road #4 by Jenna McMullen - Jesse Ramey.

14 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JENNA: Hi, Jesse! It's good to have the chance to speak with you. Anyways, first question; how's it been in Ukraine so far, days away from COURAGE 111? RAMEY: The first time I had a chance to wrestle in this area was a couple of years after the Cold War had ended. It's sad to see how many people are still poverty stricken in this nation, but it's also nice to see how far they have come along since then. JENNA: That's neat.. What did you think of LEGENDS IV, overall? RAMEY: Despite being disappointed in some of the outcomes, this show was action-packed. JENNA: You got that right. And I looked FABULOUS! So, who's your favourite wrestler in ACW now? Why? RAMEY: Chris Sheffield, Alias, is always going to be my favorite wrestler until the day he decides to retire. And it's because he and I see so many things on the same page. Although, I am beginning to like what I'm seeing out of Keith Scott Zimmerman with each passing week, and I can tell why he's been so popular in the past. JENNA: Shame about him being with that Allison girl. Sigh. Who's the wrestler you hate most in ACW now? Why? RAMEY: I wouldn't call it hate, as much as I despise Khristian Keller with a passion. I can't honestly say that I would have beaten Argent at LEGENDS IV if Keller hadn't came back to the ring. All I do know is that I would have had a better shot at it. JENNA: I thought so too. I was rooting for you. This also means nobody yet has Keller down as their favourite wrestler. Okay, since you can clearly see my nipples jutting out like sore thumbs, I'd like to ask what you think of them. Also, is this Ukrainian weather too cold for you? RAMEY: Is that really the kind of question you should be asking? As far as the cold here in the Ukraine; I'm used to the cold, so it doesn't really bother me much. JENNA: It's a fair question to ask! But I see your point. I think. Next question; if you knew me growing up, would you have asked me to prom? RAMEY: I didn't go to prom, so probably not. JENNA: Awwww. That's sad, in more ways than one. Aaaaand finally, what can your fans expect from you in the months to come? RAMEY: The same old action-packed wrestling they've come to know from me.
