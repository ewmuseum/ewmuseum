Courage 105: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

23 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* After spending most of Courage 104 sharing cookies and trading stories, new friends Buddy Saxon and Rud Albion will be in action against one another on Courage 105! Supposedly, it was Buddy who requested for the opportunity to take on The Bronze Lion. Rud, while somewhat surprised, readily accepted the challenge as a means of continuing to show the world just what he's capable of. This exhibition match between two friends of similar sizes should be an interesting one to watch. * Z's the masked enigma. Chris Moliano is Potential Personified. And on Courage 105, both men will get it on! This will be Z's in-ring debut for ACW, injured ever since he signed back in January. For the former Spirit of ACW Champion, it'll be the first time he's lacing up those boots for a match after the vicious attack suffered at the hands of Trevor Wilson on Courage 101. With these two men not seeing eye-to-eye, what exactly will happen during their match? * High Flyer showed up on Courage 104, introduced everybody to his new charges -- the Pop Culture Phenoms, fresh out of the All-Star Academy -- and basically insisted that the Tag Team Titles were going to be around the waists of his favourite two students in no time at all. The question that Alias will have to ask himself is: has his partner, K-Wolf, signed a ACW contract yet? If not, there is trouble ahead for The Original Pulp Hero. * Speaking of Alias, the ACW World Champion must be getting sick of Trey Vincent. Especially since the latter has raised the bounty on the head of Alias to $300,000. And going by the ominous message delivered by The Sports Entertainment Icon at the end of Courage 104, everybody is in for a massive surprise on 105. Can Alias hang on to the coveted gold, and go on to defend the title against #1 Contender Shawn Jessica Hart at the upcoming pay-per-view? * Keith Scott Zimmerman. He's a bad guy. But he's getting cheered rather frequently in ACW. That's got to be odd, right? After sending Frank Pierce packing on Courage 104, many are asking the question -- what's next, for The Only Wrestler That Matters? Will we find out Keith's next target on Courage 105? Omega. Trevor Wilson. Iceman. Khristain Keller. Vince Jacobs. Midnight Cowboy. Jesse Ramey. Sharc. They will also be in Manchester for Courage 105, along with a couple of special guests that we can't quite reveal here. Manchester, are you ready for ACW?
