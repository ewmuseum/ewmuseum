The Wake

11 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Wake is the second Supershow of the new generation of ACW... and it's sure to be a hot one. Coming off the tails of Legends III, which was now three months ago, ACW has changed a lot since then with many people coming and going. Given that this will be ACWs final show at the ACW-Arena before they begin a Nationwide tour of Canada, expect blood, sweat and probably some tears from the All-Stars as they try to steal and show and make sure they are top of the nights highlight reel. Rumours are flying around the eWrestling universe about just who will be turning up and just who will be competing on the night. We can exclusively tell you to expect a number of surprises for the show, and also that the ACW AV staff have been very very busy over the past two weeks. But what we are all interested in, is just who will be walking into the squared circle. Here is the card for the Wake; ACW Scorpion Championship Five Stages of Grief Match Alias (c) vs. Khristain Keller Spirit of ACW #1 Contender Match Chris Moliano vs. (Participants yet to be announced) ACW Tag Team #1 Contenders Match The Kingdom (Jamie and Danny King) vs. The Sex Symbols vs. PITS vs. Milehigh Club vs. Misfit Toys For a Scorpion Fighting Championship Title Shot Scorpion Scramble - Most Pinfalls Wins (30 mins) Iceman vs. Landon Stevens vs. Jack Johnson vs. Jack Harris vs. Rory Hayes ACW Tag Team Championship Match Heirs of Wrestling vs. Unknown Elements ACW World Championship Match Max Danger (c) vs. Brian Spaes vs. Andrew Sharp So there you have it. Six epic matches, and also remember that the Scorpion Championship match will be FIVE matches itself!! It's sure to be one hell of a show.
