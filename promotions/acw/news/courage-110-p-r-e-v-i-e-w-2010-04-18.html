COURAGE 110: P-R-E-V-I-E-W.

18 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
COURAGE 110 represents the last stop on the road to LEGENDS IV, and we’ve got an exclusive preview of what is sure to be yet another blockbuster broadcast. * Alias is still the ACW World Champion after his war with Keith Scott Zimmerman, but the match ended in a shambles thanks to one Trey Vincent. Neither Alias nor Zimmerman have commented on the situation specificially, but if you know the history between the two men… then you’d know that both men would be outraged at not being able to have a conclusive ending to their match. And when you throw in Shawn Jessica Hart and his insane attack on senior referee Henry Irwonsen into the equation, one can only speculate what will happen on COURAGE 110. Alias and Trey Vincent have a date at LEGENDS IV, but what about KSZ and SJH? One assumes they will be having some words on COURAGE 110, but what it’ll lead to is anybody’s guess. * Having been given the chance to skip COURAGE 109 to rest up, the Pop Culture Phenoms return to take on Manuel et la Bull; the pairing of Manuel and Bull impressed against Irish Spring a couple of weeks ago, and have been granted a non-titles match-up against the Tag Team Champions. Speaking of Irish Spring, they will be in attendance on COURAGE 110 and it is said that they have been following the developments in the tag team division very closely. * On that note, it is confirmed: Buddy Saxon and Rud Albion, England’s Best, will be squaring off against The SOBs. One reckons the winners of the match will have a genuine case for a showdown with the Champions at LEGENDS IV. However, The Entourage — victorious over the tandem of William T. Rex and Abraham Swift on COURAGE 109 — will certainly be staking their claim for an opportunity to challenge for the Tag Team Titles at LEGENDS IV. How exactly will the issue of which team gets the right to face the Pop Culture Phenoms at the biggest pay-per-view be solved? * Way back on COURAGE 108, SilverHAWK announced a World Series as a method to keep the likes of Jade Argent, Jesse Ramey and Khristain Keller in line. The reward? A shot at the ACW World Title. Match #1 was won by Jesse Ramey. Match #2 was won by Jade Argent. On COURAGE 1110, Jade Argent will face off against Khristain Keller. In the event that Argent wins, it’s game over for Keller and Ramey. Judging by how these three men have it out for each other, though, one can almost guarantee that the Argent/Keller showdown will have Jesse Ramey involved in some capacity. The first-ever World Series in ACW’s history might very well reach its climax on COURAGE 110, and it’d not be good if you didn’t tune in to check it out. * Kristinia Kage and Sven Avsbern had a heck of a fight on COURAGE 109, but it seemed that Sven Avsbern didn’t quite stick to the script. Both Kage and Sven agreed to a friendly contest during their Scorpion Fighting Rules encounter last week, but Avsbern turned it up several notches midway through the match and ended up destroying Kristinia. Kristinia reportedly spent the day after COURAGE 109 cooped up in a hospital, and was released with a clean bill of health. That inspired her to demand for a rematch with Sven. When pressed for comment, Sven Avsbern revealed that he decided to forego with the formalities during his fight with Kristinia because he felt sickened by how little quality Kristinia seemed to lack. The Swedish War Machine emphasised his goal to be the top dog in ACW’s most violent division, and stated that his rise to the top would be paved with performances against Kristinia Kage. Whatever the story is, we’ll see Kage/Avsbern revisited on COURAGE 110. Many more conflicts are swirling about as ACW heads into Barcelona for the last show before LEGENDS IV… but it’s time to attend another one of Trey Vincent’s lavish parties. See, Trey Vincent has it in his mind that he’s definitely going to win the ACW World Title at LEGENDS IV and as such, is conducting a series of parties counting down to the actual day itself. So, yeah, it’s time to go. Free food, free booze and a lot of pretty ladies; it’s hard to turn down, despite how slimy Trey Vincent is. But yes, do tune in to COURAGE 110 to catch up with the rest of the ACW gang. All that and more, only on… SLYSPORTS~!
