EXCLUSIVE NEWS: FIGHT NIGHT 2010 TO BE PRECEDED BY SPECIAL EDITION OF COURAGE!

22 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Days away from COURAGE 115, the man currently ruling ACW with his iron fists decided to have a press conference of his own. That's right; Jeremy Hunt, the bald-headed Programming Executive from SlySports that has usurped SilverHAWK's position as master and ruler of everything in ACW -- powers granted to him by the Owners (the Roy Boys) -- took time out of his busy schedule to talk about what was on the horizon for ACW. "After we get done with COURAGE 115, we're going to have the second edition of WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE and that'll be in Belarus!" Hunt announced. "SilverHAWK did a great thing coming up with the concept for WRESTLECIDE, and the fans in Belarus really loved our product. The decision to do this really made sense. From there, we roll towards... no, not FIGHT NIGHT 2010. Not just yet. You see, the FIGHT NIGHT pay-per-view will only feature one gigantic brawl: the Scorpion Fight Night Challenge, the second of its kind. How about all the other rivalries? How about the other title matches? These are the questions that have been posed not only by you, the media, but also by the wrestlers. I'm happy to therefore reveal that one night before FIGHT NIGHT 2010, we're going to have a special super-sized edition of COURAGE. It shall be called COURAGE COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER. The disaster being, of course, the absolute insanity we're going to witness at the FIGHT NIGHT pay-per-view. COURAGE COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER will not be a pay-per-view. Like I said, it's a super-sized edition of COURAGE and it'll feature many matches. For one, the Chain Match between Mark Weiler and Orlando Grant? Yeah, that'll happen at COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER. All the titles sans the Scorpion Fighting Title will be defended at COUNTDOWN TO DISASTER. It'll be awesome, it'll get the best ratings ever, and it'll be awesome. Yes, I said it twice. Deal with it." Jeremy Hunt had nothing else to say about the matter, and ended his press conference there after receiving an urgent call. Perhaps it was his bosses back at SlySports headquarters in jolly old England. Stay tuned for more developments on COURAGE 115!
