Exclusive News: Zimmerman Calls Out Vincent!

30 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a string of victories following his ACW PPV return and defeat, Keith Scott Zimmerman made it plainly evident to ACW's web team what the future holds if he has his way. "I've been telling everybody who listened that I've got a two-man Kiddo list, and I'm set to drive the katana through them both. It's nothing personal with Chris -- he has the ACW World Championship, the biggest prize in the game, and I want it. I want to get a shot at him one-on-one to prove that my legacy, my work in that ring, is better than it's ever been and that I deserve to go down in wrestling history as a legend in my own right." Suddenly, Zimmerman's voice got lower, and he cracked his neck before issuing the following: "The problem is this one annoying roadblock that's popped up in front of me. That roadblock? Trey Vincent has the UNEARNED #1 contendership, and I can't seem to find a way through to get another chance at it. I had Trey beat at For Queen until he mulekicked me, and that opened the door for him to win the match. However, had the official seen the low-blow -- had I gotten Irwonsen in there or something along those lines -- TV would've been DQed and I would've gotten the match vs. Chris at Legends IV. It's where I belong. Trey had his opportunity and blew it...hell, he tapped out, the most humiliating and self-defacing act a wrestler can do to their legacy in that ring. Say what you will about me; my record's not perfect, but I've never given up in my life and goddamn sure not when a World Title -- let alone The Only World Title That Matters -- hung in the balance. But Trey did. And now he and his magical backdoor contract get the shot at Legends? I realize Hawk's running around like a madman with the Fight Night that's going to happen and his attention's obviously diverted to that and keeping ACW running day-to-day up to Legends and launching our web service after it. The Board keeps deadlocking on this. I'm cutting out the middleman. I'm done trying to play the white hat to get Corporate to go along with me. Simply put? Next week is your ass, Trey Vincent. You're going to agree to meet me in that ring, go one-on-one with me again, and put a shot at Alias up with it. Otherwise, you're not going to make it to Legends. You saw how I ran off Pierce. You saw how I threw around Iceman. And you haven't spent one millisecond in the ring since I fucked you up at the last pay-per-view while I've continued my undefeated Courage run. DAYS, Trey. Hours. You better make it so I find you, because you don't want me to go looking for you. I put on clinics in that ring, and I retire people on a whim. I don't need secret contracts or eighteenth-rate Rock promos to do it, either. That's how simple it all really is. Come this Courage, I'm going to get what I want because it's what's fair, it's what's owed to me, and hell, it's because I'm Keith Scott Zimmerman. Otherwise Fight Night II will start next week with the last moments of your ACW career, a blubbering, mutilated carcass with their final scream as you pass out in a pool of your own blood. You can try to find out if I'm bluffing, Trey. But I wouldn't chance it."
