The Wright Man - 5

7 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 April 2011 “Hello.” There was audible breathing on the other side of the phone but nobody answered. “Hello?” It continued. “Is this someone playing funny buggers?” Finally, a loud cackle erupted: “You’re too much.” Keegan’s intensity lowered considerably when he heard the Texan accent: “You’re a bastard, you know that? I thought someone was playing with me. How the fuck are you doing anyway, Hank?” “I’m good. How’s life treating you?” “Not bad. Good to hear from you. To what do I owe the pleasure? It’s been too long.” Wright agreed with his buddy’s assessment: “Yeah. It has. Guess where I got a job?” “McDonalds?” Although Special K couldn’t see his former ‘sparring partner’ he could sense a shake of the head and he’d have been right: “A C W.” “OH!” “Yep.” The Englishman smiled: “How did that come about? Great news. Really, I’m happy for you. I’ve never heard of the place…” “I bet you haven’t,” Hank smirked. “Seriously chuffed for you Hank. When do you start?” “January. They’ve got a promo video of me on a show in Italy but guess where I’m making my debut?” “Where?” Hank revelled in telling his ex-MSLL co-worker: “Me-ki-co.” Keegan couldn’t believe it: “You’re kidding? Aw. What a fucken’ result that is. What a start. It’s a dream, isn’t it? How do you feel about goin’ back?” “Excited, really excited. They want me to come in as a face.” If you thought Keegan was surprised a moment ago: “What?! Are they nuts?!” “They’ve got an idea. We’re going to play up to my history there and build me up as a heel but when I cut my first promo, I’m going to lead them down the garden path and then kiss their asses. Without overdoing it.” “Mm. Reckon it’ll work?” Hank rotated his neck and his eyes squinted: “I don’t know Keeg. What do you think?” “It might. They know you down there and might have forgiven you by now. Either way, as we’ve always said, it doesn’t matter whether they love you or hate you as long as they react to you and they will.” “Think?” Special K shook his head: “Howay Hank. You know they will. That's guaranteed. I don’t know whether you being face down there will be a success or not but what I do know, what I’m one hundred percent of, is that the place will be buzzin’ when you enter.” “Thanks Keeg.” “What for?” Hank was quiet for a second: “Don’t get all mushy on me! What the fuck’s goin’ on with you?” “Well, I was trying to get a certain person a job with nbW. Never mind. Just shows you shouldn’t have any friends in this business. They all, every single one of them, burn you, toss you aside and shit on you once they’ve taken all your money and got you into bother with the law more times than…” Like a typical heel, Hank cut Keegan off on his comeback: “Fuck off. You weren’t going to get me a job there!” “I was! I got you a great deal an’all: Two pairs of boots, a Big Mac, large fries, a strawberry sundae and some tacos.” Hank had no answer. Keegan continued: “I’m proud of you Hank. I am. Honestly, I’m really happy you’re going there. At least one of us is going back, eh?” “Yeah. It’s about time, isn’t it?” “It is that, aye.” Hank’s tone lowered: “Want me to get you in?” “Thanks Hank, but no. I’ll be done with nbW soon and then I’m going home for a bit. It’s for the best.” “You know ACW are recruiting people for EWL, their European fed? Give the office a call. See if you can get in there. You’d be perfect for there. Like me in Mexico.” Keegan was receptive: “Think?” Hank was adamant: “You know you are. It’s the…” “WAIT. The Wright man in the Wright place at the Wright time?” Hank howled: “I wasn’t gonna say that…” Special K was sceptical: “No, of course you weren’t…” “But, yeah.” “I’ll see Hank. It does sound like a dream but my dreams don’t come true these days. Am I okay to mention you?” “Goes without saying. Give them a call. Promise me?” “Okay Hank…” Wright pushed: “Promise?” “I will Hank. Promise.” The Texan said: “I’m gonna get off here in a minute but make me another promise.” “What’s that?” “I’ve spoken to John, who helped get me into ACW. I’m gonna pay the boys a visit down there. Come down and let’s shoot the shit. One last time. Who knows when we’ll ever get the chance again, especially in Mexico?” It sounded good: “Aye, I will. It would be rude not to, wouldn’t it?” Wright beamed: “That’s my boy!” “Thanks for calling Hank. Knock the cunts for six down there, eh? I’ll be thinking of you.” “Cheers Keeg. Take it easy. Give my best to your folks. How are they doin’?” “Better now bonny lad. Much better but it’s still not easy, you know? How’s ya lad? Getting big, like his old man?” Hank’s voice was solemn for once: “He is. I still haven’t told Yvonne about Mexico.” Keegan questioned that: “You should. Going to?” …. “Hank?” “Yeah, I am. Now. Thanks Keegan.” “Anytime Hanky Tonk. Anytime. See you in Me-Ki-Co.” Hank had things to look forward to: His ACW debut, a MSLL reunion and a piss-up with his old acquaintance, both in the ring and in the bar, Keegan. In the meantime, he had something he had to do that he was dreading… Tell his son that Daddy won't be around much in 2011…
