Hound The Brute, The Dog of War Iron Bars Location: Unknown Date: June 17, 2005 Number (Archive): 37 Number (Wrestler): 1 I chose you, Marcus Rafael Brown, to be my new protector for a reason. You have an unquenchable fire that burns within you but no way to utilize it. Right now, that fire is equivalent to the dying spark on a wick shortly before the winds carry it into oblivion. What you need is a fan. Let that fan carry the flames and I assure you that ACW will be reduced to ashes beneath your feet. Father Shanahan gave me an opportunity to grow. For years, I helped other people get what they wanted. I was a starmaker, but never did anything for myself. My talent was thought to be mediocre at best. I was a better teacher than I was a student for whatever the reason. In fact, a rumor had caught my ear that a former student of mine now runs around ACW. He’ll be dealt with in due time. They aren’t the point. Do you know why you’re about to be locked inside this stronghold for a day, my Hound? Can you fathom the mental and physical torture you are about to endure? Seymour Almasy is a wily individual. You cannot let him out of your sight for a split second otherwise the jackal will snap right back at you. Consider this to be your awakening, Marcus. You put a dent in him, but the match that should have been mine fell into Almasy’s lap because you didn’t put him out for good. You need to become stronger, Hound. I figure a reprieve from all the troubles of outside world would do you some good. Faithfully, I listened to Father. When I was lost in a torrent of alcohol and drugs, he showed me the way. He showed me the light of Cthulhu Mythos and now my soul holds protection for the rest of my days while I swear fealty to him. Now I sit in this room. Solitude had never scared me before in my life. But…now I don’t know what to make of my time. This darkness blankets me and I can’t help but shake a little. The air is wintry and pungent. The only source of heat I can muster is that of my own breath. Conjure your negative thoughts, Hound. Focus them into a single epicenter and make that your fuel. Unleash that hatred for an abysmal life and combine it with your yearning to succeed. Then and only then will the world stare in awe of the fury and strength that you potentially wield at your fingertips. While I take in my twenty-four hour detention, my mind drifts through the past three years. Drinking. Nearly O.D.ing on pills. Going into the army. Fighting in Iraq. Nearly getting killed by some worthless fucking towelhead with a rifle. Falling off a balcony and barely surviving. Returning to the United States due to severe injury. Telling Uncle Sam to fuck off by recovering, then going AWOL and getting dishonorably discharged. Barely holding down a shitty job at my brother’s wrestling school, training wastes of space and time. Now. Before, your life had not much more meaning than the minute bugs you step on as you walk down the street. I’m bestowing upon you the opportunity to metamorphose and furnish you with a new destiny. You have been given the remarkable gift of the blessings of The Old Ones. Do NOT squander it. Sure, I’m in the limelight now after assaulting Seymour Almasy. I took his blood in my hands. Nothing I’ve never done before. I’ve been in a wrestling family. It bothers me slightly, still. Seymour had never done anything to me. But…he’s the enemy. He denounces the teachings of Cthulhu Mythos and for that, he is against me. He wants to hinder my ascension to unparalleled immensity. As much solemnity as I feel, I cannot afford to squander this opportunity. It’s my last chance. Seymour Almasy. On Courage, I don’t want your title. I don’t want your End Game spot. I just want you to hurt. … Forgive me.
