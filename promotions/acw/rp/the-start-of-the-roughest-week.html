The Start of the Roughest Week

3 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alias The Original Pulp, The Tin Angel, The Spirit of 2004, Tyr, Bigby (explained below) The Start of the Roughest Week Location: Outside the Dickens Athletic Center Date: May 03, 2005 Number (Archive): 32 Number (Wrestler): 1 One week before Courage 75, the night of Courage 74. The Original Pulp ex-Hero walked out of the back entrance of the Dickens Athletic Center, and his bones creaked from under him. The reason wasn’t even because he was tired, oh no, he hadn’t even wrestled a match tonight… hell, he had been in a stationary place of contemplation most of the evening. Just sitting. You know though, thinking back, it was probably the fact that he just sat there… stationary, that made his bones creek. Atrophy and all that, you know? These thoughts weren’t on the forefront of Alias’s mind though, there was always a reason he his bones cracked and creaked for some reason. An old salt in a thirty-year olds body. He had this next week, from the show finally moving out of Chicago, to Windsor… and he’d use it like he used the week before… to track down Keller. Alias was almost the last one out of this arena, tonight. He was in a current haze of contemplation, right now. Find a way to Keller, find a way to his baby girl. Then the hell he would send him to… “Great fucking show, you lazy fucker!” Alias had almost made it to his car in the back lot, and peaked his head around to see the two men behind him… some twenty-feet. Alias’s baby tensed under the cold night air of the Windy City, this was the last thing he needed, some prick getting on his nerves… possibly looking to be a big man and throw a punch at him. “These places need security gates…” Alias muttered to himself. Yeah, to keep the goons in. “What happened, Pulp Zero, you OD on painkillers and whiskey and just decide you leave your little girl with Keller?!” Jimmy Weiliary, a fan of ACW since the 1993, had come all the way from Philly for this… well not THIS, this was just a fun end to a pretty damn good show. Being such a mark of the fed for so long he had ridden the waves of disappointment through each closure, and been incredibly excited about each opening. Now, as he scratched his bald head while walking towards Alias, this was his chance to vent out against Alias, all his frustrations towards a guy he had followed for to long, it seemed. Like the closures, the openings, Alias had shown so much hope in who he had become since the 90’s… and now? What now? It had been three years since Jimmy first saw the Original Pulp Hero… and three years later, what, no hero? It was bullshit. Jimmy’s friend stopped him for a moment, grabbing his shoulder. “Hey man, you, you sure this is a good idea?” Jimmy just looked at his friend, a disgusted look on his face, as if he didn’t trust that they’d be all right. “These guys can’t rampage man, and this Alias fucker would be in even deeper shit if he tried anything. Right?” “Yeah, yeah I guess.” Replied Jimmy’s shorter and stockier friend. Alias just stood there, in the exact same place where he had stopped when he had first been yelled at. What had he come to… hell what had the world come to, when he had to deal with this shit. These days… though he shouldn’t feel the need… he was to proud to walk away. Since what happened with Andy Sharp, he couldn’t talk to the fans, he couldn’t talk to a lot of people… but that was alright because they weren’t talking to him. Until now. Jimmy and his friend walked up to the Pulp Original, Jimmy on the left and his friend shaking on the right. Alias glared, sneering at the taller of the two men, the one that had been yelling at him. Maybe the man was drunk, himself, on overly-expensive arena beer. “You got anything to say? Or are you going to get going on your way, and not get hurt?” There was a pause, in the moment. The wind even seemed to still itself, in the Windy City. Then… Jimmy Weiliary, fan of ACW for 12 years, spit in Alias’s face. After that it was all a blur. For Alias. Cause you see, Jimmy blacked out after the first punch. His friend ran away, for help, after the second. The third strike wasn’t a punch at all, because after the first two right hands in this man’s mug, Alias swept out his feet from under him with a shin kick coming from the side. The man fell roughly to the ground, hitting his head sickingly on the cement after let go of his collier with his left hand, once he had started falling back from the kick. Alias mounted the man, this now defenseless man, and looked at his now slightly swollen face. Jimmy wasn’t Jimmy anymore, this bald-headed man… was Khristian Keller. The root of Alias’s current problems. The problem. So… that’s the explanation on why he wasn’t about to show any more restraint then he had before. Sorry. He reigned down shots. Two lefts. A right. Keller’s head snapping back and forth each time. The second right was coming… except for… Except for the figure behind him dragging him away from the body. The body wasn’t lifeless, oh no, Alias had left him unconscious… but he knew how to leave him alive too. Was the man who had grabbed him, SilverHAWK? An old enemy and an even older friend? One of the good guys? Sharp? Almasy? No. No luck, on that front. It was a police officer. Already even, he had Alias in hand-cuffs. So of course, what was those next words to the man who didn’t seem to talk to anyone anymore? “You have the right to remain silent…” Alias didn’t hear the rest. Keller would be in the hospital for the next little while.
