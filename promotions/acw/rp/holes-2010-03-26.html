Holes

Date: March 26, 2010 "Do not trust your memory; it is a net full of holes; the most beautiful prizes slip through it." -- Georges Duhamel To be brutally honest, I don't know where this story truly began. That's the beauty of alcohol. You can drown old memories. It's simple. Kind of like tossing children into an ocean before they learn to swim. I also can't be sure of every single turn this tale has taken, but I know how it will end and when, but not why. There's a lot of what I consider ancient history (pre-2004 mostly) that's pretty easy to remember with the aid of video sharing sites. True, these matches and interviews provide only a glimpse, but these little snapshots resuscitated comatose memories that I thought I'd successfully vanquished. Bigger stuff is harder to forget. It'd be pretty hard to forget about Chelsea, the mother of my first child, Barbara. My father always said I'd never be man enough to father a boy. At the time, I won't lie, I wondered if he said it because he was clairvoyant or just a giant dick. I bet (smart) money on the latter. Barbara will be nine in August. Shit, I'm getting old. But she's a tale for another day. After Chelsea came Lisa. We met in PIW and started fucking. It was good. Just brutal, emotionless, do whatever you want to each other. It didn't matter if I liked what she did to me, or if she liked what I did to her. It was all about power. Imposing my will on her, she imposing her will on me. She also got knocked up and gave birth in June '03. Lisa insisted on calling him Richard. At least he doesn't have my last name. Maybe he'll be spared from whatever curse I was born into. John Lennon famously had himself a "lost weekend." My weekend lasted about four years. After Action! Wrestling imploded, so did my life. Quoting myself from 2008: "You see, I realized what this business was transforming me into. One of YOU people. All you people want are material objects to give your empty lives meaning. I wanted the crap that people shoved down my throat. Want a HDTV? Sure, I've got the money. Want a car? How about three? Sure, I've got the money. Alcohol? I could drink a year's supply in a week if I had wanted to. I could have had coastline property. I could have had anything I wanted. "It was pure hell. Because I grew up believing that money, and having things, cured all. And finally, when I made it, I could have everything, and I was more miserable than before because everything I thought I knew was a lie. And it nearly destroyed me. "Now it's all gone. I sold my house and everything inside for a bus ticket south. I sold my car for a bottle of whiskey. All my priceless Sharc wrestling merchandise, all my personal videos of all my great battles, all the mementos of my life, the pictures of my family – gone. The only thing I didn't sell was my life. I am my own master. "I lived on the streets. I lived on floors of roach-infested motel rooms with some of the most disgusting human creatures you could even imagine.. I've survived. Alone. No money. No home. No transportation. No ID. Nothing. It took me getting thrown into jail for a few hours for beating up some human waste to remember how great it is to beat up people, televised, and without consequence. "I haven't followed wrestling for years. I didn't have the luxury of a paycheck, regular meals, friends, family, or anything you people all take for granted. You're soft. Have you really lived? Have you really struggled? Do any of you people even realize how weak you are, living in this country where you can have anything you want? I challenge you to bitch about your life after living without your precious AC, television, computer, telephone, food, or running water." Sharc is just a name. Sharc is just a wrestling persona. However, at times I couldn't even distinguish between myself and my persona. Really, there isn't that much difference. Sharc is me. He lives somewhere in my brain. Lately, however, he's been trying to escape from death row. It's a place where many pieces of me live. Memories. Insanity. Hope. Love. All because of these pills. Lately, it feels like pieces are escaping. Like there are…holes in my brain. Like there was this giant prison riot and the inmates are slaughtering the guards one by one. Are these pills rotting my brain? Not sure where this is all leading. But I don't think it's anywhere good.
