Date: April 05, 2010 It had been a long time since the 6’8” 280 pound red headed beast of a man had opened his closet to retrieve such items like, his wrestling boots, tights, and his pain killers. He hadn’t been seen around the wrestling scene for years his stomach filled with not food, but butterfly’s. “I don’t even know if these still fit.” Was said by the tall man while slapping dust off of his boots. The voice was recognizable, but it had been so long since this voice has been heard it’s hard to make it out of who it was. The man shuffled through his drawers until he finally pulled out his passport and a cell phone charger. The man rubbed his eyes that you could tell were lacking sleep, and continued with his hands through his long red hair. “What am I doing?” Questioned the man while grabbing his hair. You could tell that this wasn’t a relaxed man at this point, but more a stressed out worried man. “What if I screw something up? What if I miss a spot and ruin the match.” The man started to breath a little bit heavier now. “Or what if I injury someone?” Cue pacing back and forth. “What if the fans don’t remember me?” Started breathing quicker now. “Shit! Shit! Shit! What am I doing?!?!” He now started to pace faster around his small room which was no bigger then a crappy motel 6 room. Cheap bed, with a small cheap TV sitting on a old wooden TV stand. The TV barely got any reception. Whatever life this guy was currently living was nothing to be proud of. On the wall hung pictures from his so called glory days. Pictures with such names as LLB, Krow, and Sean Cylear. The man grabbed one of the pictures off of the wall and then proceeded to sit down on his bed as the springs decompressed they make a high pitched screeching noise. “Man I wish these days were around.” The guy glanced over at a window in his room and started to gaze off into the sky appearing to calm a little bit down. “I had it all, fast cars, women, and money. Now I have nothing, nothing but this shitty studio that I can barely afford. On top of that I am forced to fly overseas to work! Somewhere I have never even been to. I didn’t even know these people liked wrestling. There is no way they will even know who I am.” The guy had calmed down till he started thinking about walking out to a sold out arena and hearing absolutely nothing, no cheers, no boo’s, just silence....a wrestlers worse nightmare. riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiinnng The phone startled the man as he almost flew off his bed. He shook his head and now had an angry look on his face as his phone while staring the phone down like it was a mortal enemy. He leaned over picked up the phone and put the receiver up to his ear “Yeah?” “Uhh hello this is Jerry for Downtown Taxi.....I’m looking for a Sep....Saproth......uhhhhh Dew.....Luck?” The guy had an amazed look on his face. Not only will the oversea fans not know who he is, but people in his own country don’t even remember him. “Yeah asshole, I’ll be out in one minute. Don’t go anywhere. By the way, the name is Sepiroth Du Luc.” SDL slammed the phone and threw all of his belongings into one sack and tossed it over his shoulder. “Well here goes nothing.”
