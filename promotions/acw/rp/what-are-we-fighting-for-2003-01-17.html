What are we fighting for? Written: 1/17/03 A beautiful, African-American woman wearing nothing but a red skirt, a red thong coming up above the skirt, just a lil bit to give you a peak at what you want but will probably never get, walks her sexy self into A*Dubbs view. Allen is decked out, wearing snugged designer Roccawear blue jeans, a turtle-necked light brown Rocca Wear sweater, and light brown Timberlands. His ice is blinding, as his smile is pearly white. He smirks, showing the woman he’s interested, as he continues to have a great time at the new hot spot, Klub-187. He loved it there. It was a place where the music was always off the chains, and the ladies, they were off the map, and out of Allen’s league. He may of knew it, but he showed no intentions of letting it slow him down on his hunt for this fine ass female. Walking after the fine woman Allen tried to talk to her in the sexiest voice he could get out of his mouth. “Whasup baby? You wanna come back to mi casa for some R&R?” The woman stopped, and looked back at Allen. She thought to herself, ‘He’s cute, but damn, the pick-up line was sorry.’ She keeps walking, obviously not being sold by Allens sweet-talk. “Baby I know you hear me talkin’ to you, what’s up wit it?” The woman stops, not wanting to be too rude by completely putting Allen off with no response at all. “You gotta try a lil harder if you want any of this..” Allen looks up at her, “….What? Whatchu want from me youngin’, you want a drink?” The woman smiled. She was no ordinary girl, she was a strong, sophisticated woman, not looking for some young boy, in which she could tell Allen obviously was. She didn’t really think there would be any harm in getting a drink with Allen, so she decided, why not? Allen on the other hand, was captivated by the woman’s perfectly sized c-cup breast, and the rest of her curves, they weren’t too shabby either. “Two martini’s, nucka!” The bartender fixed them their drinks, as Allen slipped him a twenty. “So what’s a fine ass hoe like you doin’ walkin alone? Where’s yo pimp?” “What?” Allen tried to elaborate. “You know, yo pimp-daddy, tha one you goto to give yo money after you let some nigga you don’t know hit it?” The woman took no hesitation in slapping the taste out of Allen’s mouth. Allen felt it, in agony. He thought to himself, “OW!” but didn’t let the woman get the best of him by hearing Allen give a hint of pain. He remembered what Mama Willis taught him back at home. “Boy, don’t you ever hit a lady!” Allen thought to himself, ‘Damn.’ He then remembered some more words of wisdom, from Papa Willis. “Boy, don’t you ever hit a lady….unless you pay for her drink at tha club! Dem’ drinks is expensive! Slap da bitch if she ain’t puttin out!” Allen was given the go. “What the FUCK?! That shit hurt you bitch!” As Allen went to raise his left hand not knowing much better, another dark, broader one stopped Allen from proceeding with the left hand to her face. The woman looked up, at the mans huge frame. Allen looked to see what had stopped his hand from connecting with the bitches face. “Don’t go hitting ladies.” It was Osyrus. “Mind ya bidness parnah.” “For the next I don’t know how long we are each other business.” Osyrus, looking towards the fine looking broads direction implied, “Gods speed bitch.” The woman, being disgusted walked away, not caring for either men she had just had the ‘pleasure’ of meeting with. Osyrus took her seat, with nothing but a business look on his face. “Allen. You must not understand what’s about to go down in the next few weeks.” Allen wasn’t even paying attention, too busy staring at the chica’s on the dance floor. “I understand I need to be hoppin’ on some MAD shorties before I bump up out of dis’ joint tonight NUCKA! Naw mean?!” Allen goes to bump fists with O, but remembering just exactly who Osyrus was he stopped right before he went to make the motion. He sits back into his seat. “Nucka what are you talking about?” “I’m talking about the Owners. Ethan doesn’t want ICU leaving on a stretcher for nothing. He is looking to be the key factor in the war against Dunn and Boyd. If we take him out, we got their heart. You read?” “Yah, whatever Osyrus. But what I’m failing to see is…” Allen looks back into the crowd, as O hangs on his last words. “What’s that?” Allen looks straight into Osyrus’ eyes. “Where is the part where I am supposed to give a fuck? Why does it matter to me if Dunn and Boyd win, and why tha fuck does it matter if Winters wins? No matter who gets the W in the end, the outcome is always tha same. A white bred runnin’ the company, just a matter of whether there’s gonna be two white men, or one white man. No matter what O, we get the shaft.” “Are you trying to make this a race issue?” “I don’t really give a damn. All I know is, I’m not sure why I should side with anyone just yet. I’m with Winters, and I’m paid, but if Winters is to all of a sudden lose his power that don’t mean my contract will be terminated….I’m an asset, and so are you. Before yo’ ass even go into this war O, don’t you need to know why you’re fighting it?” With that, Allen pats Osyrus on the back, before leaving him to check out the fine ass hunni’ in the white tryin’ to hurt somethin on the dance floor.
