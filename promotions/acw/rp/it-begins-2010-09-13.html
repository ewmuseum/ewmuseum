It begins.

13 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 13, 2010 Night falls over Botham City, but not everyone is sleeping soundly! We go to a quick shot of the Botham City Bank, where the safe's vault has been blasted wide open and the despicable Jokah-san is directing a heist! A fallen night officer escapes attention and crawls towards the silent alarm, alerting Botham City's esteemed Police Department before crumbling in a heap. Meanwhile at Police Department's HQ, Commissioner Jorden and Captain O'Hara assess the situation. Jorden: It's Jokah-san, and he's robbing Botham City Bank! O'Hara: Shall I light up the Cat-signal, Commish? Jorden: No, I'm sick and tired of having Jokah-san escape prison every other month; let's use... the other signal. O'Hara: *gasp* You don't mean-- Jorden: --I'm afraid so, old friend. Fire up the Fight-signal! A heavily taped fist logo spins towards the screen and back! When we return, both Jorden and O'Hara are standing on the rooftop of the HQ, staring up into the stars above as a skylight shines brightly besides them. Jorden: ... a glorious fist, punching up in the sky! O'Hara: I'm still not sure if this was a good idea. Suddenly, a silhouette emerges from the shadows; the masked figure of Fight Man! Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. O'Hara: Mother McCree, so glad you're here Fight Man, you see-- Fight Man punches O'Hara's lights out. Interesting. Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. Jorden: Fight Man, Jokah-san is on a rampage and must be stopped! He's been last seen at Botham City Bank, and police cars are converging fast but we don't know what kind of weapons Jokah-san has on himself. You know what you have to do! Fight Man: I sure do. Fight Man proceeds to punch Commissioner's Jorden's lights out. Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. He then hails a cab, kicks the door open, punches out the driver and throws him out, cartwheels his way in, headbutts the steering wheel and stomps the pedal. Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. Also, I wish I had my own car. Really. Spinning taped fist twirls in and out! Meanwhile, at Botham City Bank, Fight Man makes a crashing entrance; that is, crashing through the doors and driving into the bank! He steps out of his car, sees a henchman, and starts to fight the henchman. The henchman is joined by other henchmen who get beated up by Fight Man like all the other henchmen. Any onomatopea you would expect from this fight have been replaced by the the type of attack executed, so instead of a "KA-POW!" you hear "PUNCH!" and instead of "BOFF!" you hear "KICK!" and there's a lot of colorful expressions used as well such as "IN THE JIMMIES!". But just as Fight Man was clearing the room of the small fry, Jokah-san appears, brandishing a stack of dynamite over his head as he calls forth to the masked pugilist. Jokah-san: Not another move, Fight Man, for if you do it'll be your last! I have just enough dynamite here to blow us all and a few innocent bystanders into kingdom come! So I suggest you just stop with the punching and let me go with all the loot, or else you'll have your death and quite a few others on your conscience! Fight Man: I'm Fight Man; also, I always have a contingency plan for cases such as this. Jokah-san: Like what? Fight Man punches Jokah-san's lights out, then grabs the dynamite before it's armed. Then he runs over and punts one of the goons in the ribs before stomping his back repeatedly. The day is saved, somehow or rather, and Fight Man goes outside to greet the cheers of the confused public. They don't know why they're cheering. Commissioner Jorden is waiting outside and accosts Fight Man. Jorden: Great job, Fight Man! I bet we won't be hearing from him or his troops for a long time now! Fight Man: Yeah, well... I just hope that Jokah-san and his goons learned their lesson. Jorden: And that would be? Fight Man: That violence doesn't solve anything. Commissioner Jorden and Fight Man both share a jolly old laugh at Jokah-san's expense as he is carried out into the paddy wagon by burly security officers. Fight Man: I'm Fight Man. END!
