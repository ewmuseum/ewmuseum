Hound The Brute, The Dog of War Touched (One) Location: Journal Entry Date: July 12, 2005 Number (Archive): 44 Number (Wrestler): 2 June 4th, 2005 Father Shanahan says that my training has improved greatly over these past few months. I’ve studied his work closely. Watched the tapes. His fights with various stars of this world have been very informative. I watched a fight. King of the Indies 2004. His final match with Sonny Gabriel Silver in which the master was victorious was a beacon of hope for our kind. Father spread the word of Cthulhu Mythos into every last non-believer Cthulhu’s will has to be spread in order to grow as a person myself. Watching him destroy that misbegotten ego-driven madman that night gave me a sense of hope. Father knows what he’s doing and when he chose me to become his new protector, I was honored. My life had meaning again. For months on end, I studied every last scripture that he could give me. My mind absorbed words that I hadn’t thought possible and new ideals were bestowed upon me every day. The life I left behind was disappearing. I forgot about the names of all those around me. Elijah, begone. Gabriel, fare thee well. They found their own paths to success. Now I was left to carve my own niche. I lost my way in the Army. Uncle Sam and his money were NEVER kind to me, so I waved good-bye to them, too. He found me after a local indy show for some federation. I pulled in some gas money, but that was it. Then when the man known to man as Horace Tully came to me, he promised me opportunities beyond my wildest imagination and all I would have to do is watch his every move. Be his shield and keep the outside world from getting in and breaching our ideals. I did so. My training excelled rapidly, according to Father. So well, he personally summoned me to assist in his conquest of ACW. But gave me this new moniker. I was to be his Hound. Watching his back and having me sic someone the second they spurned our beliefs or just chose to try and make a name for themselves at the expense of us. Not just that. He chose a herald for me. Her name was Marion Sterling.
