Chinks in an Already Weakened Wall

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Simian Kade 'Kade, New York's Finest, The Fallen Angel' Chinks in an Already Weakened Wall Location: The Deepest Depths of Temptation Date: July 23, 2004 Number (Archive): 9 Number (Wrestler): 1 I hurt myself today To see if I still feel Coping has become extremely hard in the past few days. I focus on the pain The only thing that's real Trying to restrain myself. I attempt to look at the bigger picture, and the benefits. But my mind always trails away. The needle tears a hole The old familiar sting Bury me in the dirt; relieve me of my pain. Try to kill it all away But I remember everything The first sip, the final sip, and all the sips in between: chinks in an already weakened wall. What have I become My sweetest friend My reflection stares back at me through the mirror. It hates me. I hate me. Everyone I know goes away In the end Death shall bring down all of us. Nobody is safe, nobody may evade destiny. And you could have it all My empire of dirt To the victor go the spoils. In some people’s minds, it is the spoils. In my mind however, if this is what the winners get, god help those poor bastards who are losing. I will let you down I will make you hurt Forget what your parents tell you. Forget their attempts to turn you away. It’s sweeter then a thousand drops of nectar. But it’s more sinister then Hitler could have ever hoped to be. I wear this crown of thorns Upon my liar's chair Your parents haven’t experienced it. You haven’t experienced it. I have experienced it. And I pray to God no one ever has to see the things I’ve seen. Full of broken thoughts I cannot repair I have memories of men, men darker then the deepest parts of Hell. I have memories of souls, broken beyond repair. Beneath the stains of time The feelings disappear No matter what they say, words cannot describe it. I ache for it, I long for it, every day of my life. And I would kill for it, if only it were that simple. You are someone else I am still right here … For murder is basic. Yet, so are tears. What have I become My sweetest friend You can take a man’s life and not think twice. But the most complex and strenuous task known to man, is to say ‘no’. Everyone I know goes away In the end I had it all. I have it all. And you could have it all My empire of dirt I had nothing. I have nothing. I will let you down I will make you hurt Except for the profound sting that runs through my veins. If I could start again A million miles away I hold out. Waiting for the day. When you are, once again, my number one priority. And we can be together, again. I would keep myself I would find a way Unfortunately… that day is not today.
