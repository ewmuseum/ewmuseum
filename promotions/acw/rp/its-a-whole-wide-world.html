It's a whole wide world. Written: Today, do-dah! He grunted, a momentous grunts bounced and echoed from the glass walls of his small makeshift home in he ward. He had a small bed with an extremely thin mattress with nothing else. Not even a toilet. Urine soaks a corner that in his mind Scott has labelled “Toilet”. Scott stops, his arms forming a diamond shape which disappears behind his head. He wears nothing on his head. No Christmas hat any more. Confiscated by a guard who had a bad day. That made Scott mad at the time. By now he had forgotten Christmas even existed, in his small existence in this transparent glass globe he lived in, he thought this was life, he thought this was all it was. He felt let down some how but couldn’t understand how. Scott saw the camera which had been spying on him for some time now. He decided to make a statement or two… “A camera…” he mumbled. He remembered the name but knew not the use of it. “You’re a camera.” He said as his naked, cold body pressed against the glass. “Oh camera, what magical wonders to work… The inspiring tapestry you weave for all the impressionable youths out there in my universe. How beautiful you are.” Scott’s hand pressed the glass hard, white crept across the hand as the blood was cut off a small amount. “A… C… W…” he read out loud as he saw the logo on the side of the black piece of technical equipment. “Do you work in my world?” No answer. “Do you work out there… in the universe…?” He stumbled out. Again, no answer. “No.” A look of shock covered his face. “I work… for you… Out there… in the universe. I remember now. I remember… I work out there… in the universe… only it isn’t the universe to everyone else, only to me, this is my world you see, but out there… Out there is a whole world, a large, vast world capable of destroying itself. We can… We will… Destroy ourselves.” *** Stop it now. Keep the peace.
