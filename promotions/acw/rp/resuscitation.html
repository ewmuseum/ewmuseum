Resuscitation.

15 May 2005

LLB The Law Resuscitation. Location: Empty arena. Date: May 15, 2005 Number (Archive): 34 Number (Wrestler): 2 Sweat poured out of him like a running water fountain as the back of his body stuck hard to the top and middle ropes, before he shot off them, the feeling in his back pulsating throughout his ribs as if they were just nailed with a sledgehammer. LLB shot across the canvas floor at quickened speed… measuring the body bag before he leapt off his feet… crushing it together as the top met the bottom, while it fell back into the ropes, and out of the ring. LLB rested on his arms as he caught his breath on the mat. Things were getting harder for him now. He was not even the same wrestler from two years ago, let alone the same person. The times had changed LLB. The times had slowed his body down. The times had made him mentally weaker. Training to get back into the ring was harder than he thought. It had also taken much longer too. But only a few days from now, it would all begin again. LLB would be (one of two) guest referees in the ACW Championship Title Match between ‘Superstar’ Vince Jacobs and Khristain Keller. LLB would return to the ring. ‘The Law’ slowly rose from the wrestling mat. In a split-second his mind had taken him back to the feeling of standing in front of a packed crowd. But today, as LLB looked through the empty seats, he barely saw the sunlight beam in through the outside doors of the area. It had been such a long time; the name LLB was more than likely already forgotten… or at least just a distant memory to most fans. Wrestling was not the same as it was two years ago. The popularity had dropped. The competition was much smaller. Wrestlers were coming in, making an impact, and then vanishing again. Federations were gaining support… and then closing right away. The strong times through 2000-02 were long since depleted. Names like Kenny Rock, Neo, the Boston Strangler and Jim Johnson, just to name a few, were still and always would be legends in their own right, but amongst today’s new fans, and the coming of tomorrow’s, they would not hold the same prestige… the same name value… as they once had on those who actually experienced them. LLB’s name… had also fallen into that category. Roland Erman sighed. That day would come for every wrestler. Every football player. Every baseball star and so on. The new fans of today would find their own Joe Montana’s, Cal Ripken’s and Scott Slugger’s. They wouldn’t even have to be that great to be considered in the same realm, just a different time, as those that have now lived on through their sport, only in memories. It would happen to everyone… but the sorrow of thought continued to play in LLB’s mind… that his legacy could now end in any type of scenario. In his early 30’s, ‘The Law’ was still young… but in wrestling years he was not only getting on in age, but also in style. One wrong move, one wrong hit… and his career could be done. Let alone his career with the wrestling crowd. Would the ACW fans support ‘The Law’s’ comeback? Would the ACW fans appreciate what ‘The Law’ has done in his wrestling career? Only those select few are able to come back with such an impact. LLB had the charisma of Shawn Michaels to be as successful as him. He had the mic work of The Rock to get everyone riled up in an instant of arrival. He had the wrestling skills of ‘Stone Cold’… not great… but he could blind the fans into thinking he was. LLB had all the tools for a decent comeback… The only thing left to conclude… was if LLB would be able to do it now. If LLB could be given a second chance. He left the ring, knowing his body could barely handle another few years. It was now or never. His return was finally inevitable. … So were the three ice packs waiting for him when he got back to his locker room.
