In my pocket

12 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#33) by Phoenix Rose, dated 12 June 2004, from the fed's rebuilt site on acw.ewmania.com (its 2004 edition) as captured in the Wayback on 12 July 2004. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Phoenix Rose Title: In my pocket Date Made: 12 June 2004 Location: I am the man that loves you You are the man that trusts me I am the man that will end you You are the man that uses me I am the man that idolises you You are the man that respects me I am the man that will betray you You are the man that will be destroyed by me. Who are you? Who are you? …Who am I? Some people think that this is all about respect, well not me. This is all about the money, always has been– always will be. I help set up the ring before shows for that extra bit in my pay packet, some people think that it is because I am dedicated to ACW, well not me. This is all about the money, always has been- always will be. Sometimes I ‘forget’ to pay you for that soda, some people think that it is because I am forgetful, well not me. This is all about the money, always has been- always will be. I am all about the money, you are nothing but a dollar bill and you’re right. In. My. Pocket.
