II

6 April 2010

Date: April 06, 2010 The back doors to the cab drivers car slammed shut as Sepiroth Du Luc waved goodbye to his crappy apartment. Ok so he didn’t really wave goodbye to his apartment because then he would be waving to nobody, and SDL isn’t a sappy little bitch. He was mentally waving goodbye to the past life that he lived, goodbye to the local grocery mart that he went to every week to purchase his groceries and other necessities, goodbye to the red light that he got stuck at every morning before heading to his 9-5 job that he was working before being signed to probably one of his smallest contracts ever by ACW. The money was no more then what he was currently making being a paper pusher it was actually less. It wasn’t even close to what he was making a couple years back in his golden years. Then again people would pay $60.00 and order whatever pay-per-view that he was wrestling at. SDL took this opportunity because wrestlers like him don’t wrestle for the money, they wrestle for the adrenaline rush and the fame. For some reason SDL attracted fans, he wasn’t that talented on the microphone, he didn’t have a flashy gimmick. Hell to be honest his least favorite part of his job was having to be a puppet to a wrestling organization that only craved 2 things, blood and money. Go here, do this, do that, sign this, sign that. It’s what previously drove SDL out of the field of entertainment. Back in the day SDL could handle the hardcore matches that then top promoter Jim Johnson was throwing his way. He could take multiple chair shots to the head a night, and then proceed to get thrown through a TV. Now though? SDL questioned his bodies strength and durability. He wouldn’t know if he could handle that until it actually happened. Lucky for him though he wasn’t expecting to be part of the upcoming scorpion division that was currently taking the ranks of ACW by storm. He wasn’t quite sure what his role was going to be. All that he was told was be ready to work a match against a young kid at the next Courage. The cab came to a screeching halt as SDL’s head flew forward as he wasn’t ready for the stop. SDL threw the cab driver a nasty glare through the rear view mirror. The cab driver in fear of losing a portion of his tip threw back a fake smile at SDL. “$42.50” Announced the cab driver. SDL reached into his back pocket and pulled out his wallet. All that he had left was two fifty dollar bills. Du Luc pulled out one of the fifties and handed it to the cab driver. SDL notified the driver that he could keep the change and the cab driver graciously accepted the tip. Sepiroth swung open the back door to the car and proceeded to make his way to the back of the cab as the trunk was already popped a waiting for SDL to open it up and grab his bag that was back there. SDL grabbed his bag and made his way to the airports front doors. This is it.
