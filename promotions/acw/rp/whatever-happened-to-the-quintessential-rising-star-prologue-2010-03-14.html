Whatever Happened... To The Quintessential Rising Star? - Prologue; Chapter 0, Part 7

14 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 14, 2010 ACW COURAGE: THE BRIDGE TO 2005 09 DECEMBER 2004 Quinton May looked at the big door right in front of him. The last time he was at work for ACW, he'd lost to a young upstart. The last time he was at work, period, was a couple of weeks ago at The Squared Circle's TNW 34. Fresh off his inaugural address as the new Evolutionary Champion, Quincy turned back the challenge of one Phil Atken to retain his title, cleared the air and patched things up with Atken... and then found out that the man that had been caring for him since 2002, ol' Bruno, had suffered a heart attack. And ever since that fateful night weeks ago, Quinton had been at Bruno's side. Until now. Until this moment. Because, Quinton had decided what he needed to do. He had made his mind up. He was going to the source. Firstly, Quinton May had to make sure he left on the right note. Without knocking, Quincy opened the door, locked eyes with a preoccupied SilverHAWK, and cleared his throat. "I have to go." SilverHAWK looked up from the stack of documents he was poring over. Had something to do with ratings and buyrates and stuff for the first broadcast in Courage in slightly more than a month, which was about 25 minutes away. ACW was in a right mess of epic proportions, and this Courage broadcast -- entitled Courage: The Bridge to 2005 -- was meant to have a dual purpose of washing out the year that was and bridging into the year that would be. Network problems and other such plagues that the company was battling made SilverHAWK's life one giant headache. And that was before all the messiness with certain members of the roster. Now locking eyes with Quinton May, who was standing at the entrance of the big man's office, HAWK sensed that something was very amiss. Quinton May hadn't really appeared to be right. Ever since losing to Seymour Almasy on the last episode of Courage a month ago, in fact. "Sorry, could you repeat that, stranger?" SilverHAWK asked. He wasn't quite sure what he'd heard, what with being engrossed with his documents and all. "You have to do what?" Quinton, shoulders sagged and eyes narrowed, sighed. "I have to go. Like, leave. The company. The business itself, in fact. It's not an easy thing for me to do... and it's probably coming at a very baaaad time for you now. And I know I ran out right after my match on the last show like as if I was running away for good. But this is it, for real. Long story short -- I have to leave ACW. Tonight." SilverHAWK's jaw dropped ever so slightly, and his eyes fluttered for a couple of seconds. If one didn't know better, one would think that he was about to have a heart attack or something. The Canadian Gladiator sighed once more as he walked over to HAWK's table and put his hands on the table. SilverHAWK now stood, wanting to be literally eye to eye with Quincy Mama to try and find out just what was going on. As far as SilverHAWK was concerned, Quinton was the future. Behind Alias, Quinton was the man most fans came to see when they bought a ticket for the ACW show. Quinton May brought the ACW Television Title to a whole new level. And HAWK was kind of hoping Quincy coming back after a month off would be a permanent return, because the big man needed some positive news for a change. "So, Quinton, it's definitely good to have you back here in the ACW. You worked very hard for the company last year, no question about it, and you were one of four people I wanted to bring back. I can't tell you -- and this is no joke -- how proud I am to have you on my roster. Truly, you did great things last year." The words of William Laguna, from ACW's very first show back on the air earlier in the year. Yes, it was coincidentally on April Fool's Day. No, nobody named April was fooled. Yes, I just made that up. Anyways, the words of William Laguna now resonated in the forefront of HAWK's mind. He felt exactly the same way. SilverHAWK began to speak, but found that no words came out of his mouth. Quinton raised his head and cleared his throat. "Look, I'm sorry. I didn't really plan for this to happen." "I... why? Why the sudden decision?" SilverHAWK finally found the words to articulate his exact thoughts. "I mean, I know you're waging this war with this Alexander Pembridge person and whatnot -- but what does it have to do with you leaving?" Quinton removed his hands off of the surface of the table and folded them. He looked at SilverHAWK, the man behind the machine of ACW. Many people had come and gone as the one running the show. But whenever anybody thought of ACW and the captain of the ship, only one name would ever spring to mind. Not Alias. Not Vince Jacobs. Not Osyrus. Not Ethan Winters. Not William Laguna. SilverHAWK was the name. He was the f'in MAN. And to Quinton, HAWK was all that and more. Quinton looked at the weathered face of SilverHAWK, still thinking about how to respond to the big man's question. The Canadian Gladiator started to wonder if there was ever a book big enough to contain all the stories he'd heard about the legend standing just an arm's length away from him. That thought morphed into a realisation of how much like a father figure HAWK was to Quinton, and May almost lost it. His face scrunched up in an effort to hold back tears, and May had to think of a tiger brutally mauling Osyrus just to get the sappy feelings out of his head. "I... it's not just you and ACW. I'm actually going to do the same with The Squared Circle, which is technically on the shelf courtesy of Harvard Avalon's... well, you know. I skip one show because I wanted to be at Bruno's side, and all that happens. Anyway, what I'm about to say may appear a bit... whatever to you, but I need to say it." Quinton began, shifting his line of sight away from SilverHAWK's steely gaze and focusing instead on the contents of the big man's table. Quinton tried to open his mouth to continue, but it was now his turn to be at a loss for words. The muscles in his larynx simply refused to budge. SilverHAWK noticed. Oh, he noticed. "It's okay, son." What the hell do you think Quinton did? A tear fell down his right cheek, and the urge to hold back just withered away. Despite that, Quinton refused to let himself degenerate into a mess while he still had stuff to say. "It sucks. This whole thing sucks. Leaving the Squared Circle bloody sucks because I just secured the biggest win of my career there, beating Ketih Scott Zimmerman and winning the Evolutionary Title!" the Castaway ranted, his emotions continuing to spiral out of control as yet another tear escaped from its hiding place. "Nobody had been able to do it, but I did! I beat him! And then Phil Atken, that cheeky joker, came calling. He wanted to gatecrash the party. He didn't know it, but Phil wanted to take away the only thing that was keeping me in this business after the last few months I've had. Somehow, while Phil didn't manage to beat me, I still feel that the damage was fully done and that him coming sooo fricking close to taking away the last bit of *me* I have left in this business was the straw that broke the camel's back. Because truth be told, there were times in that match that I looked at Phil dead in his eyes. And all I saw... ... all I saw was Alexander Pembridge. Laughing at me. Taunting me. Teasing me. Preparing to rain down what he called my blackest night on me. And even though Phil and I left things on a good note, I end up having to face the reality of my life when Bruno goes down with his heart attack. I start looking at people, and think they're out to get me. It gets even worse when I simply think of anybody on the roster here in AC-dub. That's why I ran out in a huff after that night I lost to Seymour. I looked at the young chap, and I saw a deranged psychopath out to make a life a living hell. Only, I knew it was not true. Made things worse. And when he beat me? Christ, when he pinned my mat to the shoulders and got to three, my mind went blank. I... can't handle that anymore. I have to take action. I have to find out what all this is about. Because it's not just him claiming who he says he is. It's everything. All the weird and downright crazy things that have been happening to me? All connected. And that sickens me. It's larger than life, and it's connected, and it's in control of me. My destiny. The only one who should be controlling my destiny -- my life -- should be *me*. I need answers. I need to find out the truth. And while I do that, I need to make sure the people I care about and the things I care about stop being involved in the puppet show is my life. That's why, I need to leave." Maaaaaaaan, talk about information overload. Talk about baggage, yo. Quinton's got issues. SilverHAWK blinked, a little bit slowly than he usually blinked. As if he was had attained control, just for a second, over the usually subconscious activity of blinking. It was indeed a lot to take in. HAWK didn't always get the trials and tribulations Quincy endured. But he understood nonetheless the Canadian's desire to put an end to all the mayhem that seemed to follow him no matter where he went. There was only one response that sat on the tip of HAWK's tongue. "Okay, I hear you." Quinton looked up again at SilverHAWK as the fight against his waterworks ended with only two lonely drops of tears having escaped. A teeny bit surprised at HAWK's reaction, he was. SilverHAWK seemed to take a bit to absorb everything Quincy had to say, but he appeared to understand the situation. "Hey, it's not like I'm not completely gutted." HAWK stated as he stole a glance at the wall clock. 20 minutes to show time. "But, whoever Alexander Pembridge is doesn't have a contract and after the last we spoke, our security's all on guard for him. He's never going to come back here until something happens. So, you're going to go and take the fight to him." "Y-Yeah, I guess that'd be the less roundabout and rambling way of saying it!" Quinton shot back after a thought, smiling wryly at SilverHAWK's ability to cut through the bullshit. The big man chuckled, proud at managing to get the flimsiest of positive reactions from the troubled Canadian Gladiator. Having reached a mutual understanding, SilverHAWK abandoned any notions of trying to talk Quincy out of his decision. And so, he extended his hand out to Quinton May. Former two-time Television Champion. Former two-time Scorpion Fighting Champion. The quintessential rising star. And generally, one of the most exciting and promising individual that ACW -- in any era -- ever had in a very long time. Quinton wasted no time in grabbing HAWK's extended hand and shook it firmly. Perhaps for the last time, in a long time. "You think you'll be back soon?" HAWK asked after both men were done with the handshake, and the big man sat down back in his chair. "Like after everything's sorted? The door's always open, you know... as long as I can keep this place open, ha!" May grinned. ACW going under had been a running gag for a while. "I honestly don't know. But yeah, ACW's been a home to me for the longest time. Just not showing up the last month regardless of the fact that there have been no shows has been weird enough." Quinton ran his hand through his hair, thinking of all the good times. Andd despite all the chaos that had been piled not only on him but on the federation as a whole, there were plenty of memories Quincy was going to be taking with him to wherever he had to go to next. SilverHAWK leaned back in his eat, looking at the man that in his first appearance on an ACW television show, was seen sitting alone in his locker room monitoring the rest of the roster with the aid of five close-circuit television sets. HAWK's trip down memory lane continued with the memory of how Quinton hired lookalikes to mess with Dane Rivers way back in the early months of 2003. How (somewhat) ironic that it would be a so-called clone of Quinton by the name of Gerald Parker that caused the Canadian Gladiator so much trouble earlier in the year. Heh. SilverHAWK then flashed to the moment that he knew the Castaway would become a rising star. The pay-per-view that was Pain Or Pleasure 2003: elbow drop from the top of a steel cage down onto 108192, one of the toughest and fiercest fighters ACW had ever seen at the time. A man who was never the same after Quinton defeated him, following weeks of intense battle. And it was all because of that majestic elbow drop, which was still featured on the highlight reel of Courage's opening sequence. "So, I'm going to go now." Quinton suddenly quipped, snapping SilverHAWK back to reality. "I just have one last thing. Well, three things." SilverHAWK looked up at Quinton, who had one foot out the door. "What three things?" May folded his arms once more. This next part wasn't going to be particularly easy, but it needed to be done. No way he could leave without clearing things up and setting the record straight, Quinton thought. He owed it to himself, and he owed it to SilverHAWK. Clearing his throat, the Canadian decided to get on with it. "Well, I wanted to say sorry to you. A couple of weeks ago, on a TSC show, I made some comments about you and a donkey or some sort of farm animal. Fact of the matter is, I was pissed and venting, but that wasn't an excuse. You're an awesome man, and I'm pretty sure you could still take most people in the ring and do so with style!" "Heh. I did in fact hear about that. And don't worry, I've heard worse. What's next?" SilverHAWK shot back, amused at Quinton's need to clear the air. "AH, well, I need you to say sorry to Seymour for me!" Quinton sheepisly continued. "He beat me fair and square on the last episode of Courage, and I just blew him off in the end. Like I said, I went insane in the brain and saw him as Alexander. Seymour's going to be a big frigging deal here in no time at all, and I'm sure you can count on him to deliver the goods. The guy's got the tools to be a World Champion, HAWK. Hold on to him, you hear?" SilverHAWK nodded. He agreed with Quinton's assessment. And now, Quinton's expression become more solemn. "And finally, I need you to say sorry to Christopher for me. I've been acting like a big jerk to him, when all he was trying to do was be a friend. He's earned his spot, and as Alias, he's been the shining knight of ACW. Tell him I'm truly sorry, that I'll make it up to him, and that he's actually been the second most inspiring person for me in ACW." Quinton shifted around in his spot a bit, looking relieved that he'd gotten it out. SilverHAWK smacked his lips together, feeling proud that the Canadian Gladiator had noticed he had to right his wrongs. Still, with all the confessing, a sliver of curiousity started to bark out at the big man. "Will do, Quinton. I will repeat word for word what you've just said to Christopher." HAWK stated, realising that this could very well be the last few seconds of conversation with the Rising Star. "But hey, I'm curious. He's the second most inspiring? Who then has been the ultimate inspiration to you?" Quinton immediately laughed, and wiped the dried-up tears from earlier from his face. The answer was simple. It consisted of one word. And it was the god's honest truth. "You." SilverHAWK blinked, and a bewildering smile crept onto his face. He genuinely hadn't expected that. Quinton winked cheekily, and nodded at the big man for possibly the last time in a long time. HAWK nodded back, his heart swelling up with emotions. Once Quinton was out of sight, the man many consider to be the heart and soul of ACW bit down on his lower lip, wondering why it had to come to all this. Why a good man had to leave, when he still had a lot to offer. Shaking his head, SilverHAWK decided he only had one thing left to say. It wasn't really to anybody, and it was certainly too late to Quincy. In the end, it was more like him wishing to himself. "Go get 'em, Quincy. Give 'em hell." And that was that; the end of Quinton May in ACW, in TSC. ... the end of Quinton May, period.
