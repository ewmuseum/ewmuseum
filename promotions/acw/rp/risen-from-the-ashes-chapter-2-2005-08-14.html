Gacy King Kill Risen From The Ashes - Chapter 2 Location: Obscurity Date: August 14, 2005 Number (Archive): 5 Number (Wrestler): 2 He cast his line into the pond, and waited with nervous anticipation. After hours of discussion and rebuttal, only one fish took the bait. The network czar and merchandise king found the product too susceptible for failure, and an unlikely money maker. This didn't hurt Gacy's feelings though, because when all was said and done, he had a drug tycoon in his back pocket. Mr. Montuori also found the project risky, but couldn't forget that dangerous blueprints make for the most uninhibited successes. At the conclusion of the meeting, he handed Gacy his personal business card, and indicated for him to arrange a future meeting documenting where the money would need to be spent. Gacy accepted with limited enthusiasm, and the corrupt alliance was formed. At first he was sceptical, what with Montuori's reputation and all. But then he got to thinking ... who better to start an underground revolution with than a man who spat on the face of morality? There would be no stop signs, and nobody telling him to hold anything back. Not only was Lajon's involvement acceptable, but a necessary genesis for the dynasty Gacy was about to build. Plus, Montuori knew nothing of the business, and would leave all the decision making to Gacy himself. Dust under his boot scattered as the sole graced the cold concrete below. Scanning the room, he could only make out the silhouette of a hollowed enclosure, devoid of any sign of human attention. He could here the clicking of a generator as his real estate agent flipped on the interior lights. Like a tidal wave in slow motion, the fluorescent bulbs across the ceiling deliberately gave substance to the corridor. Gacy's eyes focused to the light as the salesman approached him from behind. 'Beautiful aint it?' Not even a hint of doubt in the agent's mouth as he continued his pitch. 'Oh yeah, like a car crash.' The man formerly known as King Kill wasn't the kind to be easily sold, but the building he now inhabited had a special aura about it. It was dingy, and had the stench of gasoline and heavy machinery covering every square inch. Apparently it was once the home of an auto shop, but now the only resemblance were oil stains painting the floors and walls. Off in the distance he noticed two small offices, that with a few minor touch ups would make an excellent base of operations. To its right were duel restrooms, which if they still had running water, would be more than enough to satisfy the needs of a few hundred people. 'Ya see that door in the corner? That leads to a 900 square foot storage room.' Dressing room, that'll be more than enough. Gacy began to envision the 18x18 foot ring, surrounded by hoards of rabid fans. Their cheering enveloped his mind, and he became lost in the greatness he knew this place could omit. Seeing the content look on his client's face, and wanting to seal the deal, the realtor chimed in. 'You mentioned you're a sports entertainment company. What is that like fake wrestling or something?' This was a question that always irritated Gacy. Especially when it came from some over pampered tampon that probably has never broken a bone in his life. But no matter, Gacy was feeling far too good to give a shit about this rat fuck's opinion. 'Yeah, somethin' like that.' 'Wow. So you gonna make this place famous like that fWo or what?' A smile than lined Gacy's face as he appreciated the building one last time. 'Famous? ... Doubtful ... Infamous? ... Inevitable.'
