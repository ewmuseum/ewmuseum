Mr. Irrelevant

28 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 28, 2010 The first anybody ever heard of "Sharc" was in January 1999. The Xtreme Wrestling League was so proud of me that they buried mention of me at the very bottom of a three-line note: "Three more wrestlers have joined XWL. Fox and Wolverine, former tag champs in other organizations, have been signed, as has Sharc, a sadistic wrestler if there ever was one. Still getting info about these guys, who have mainly wrestled in Japan." Now, XWL's original web site is long gone, but I'm pretty fucking sure that the same week that I signed, a whole bunch of other people signed as well. XWL had been in business for just over six months when I signed. Their roster was filled with a bunch of aging cunts who were so desperate to get back on TV that they were willing to put themselves through hell. With a solid fanbase and decent ratings, they decided to go out and sign everybody they could as part of a "youth movement" of sorts, with the hope of becoming THE dominant U.S. hardcore wrestling federation. Veterans elevating newbies. You know, how the wrestling business used to work for ages before aging cunts had to hold onto their precious main event spots. And I'm still bitter about that stupid news blip. First off, Fox and Wolverine blew. If they ever won the tag titles, it was completely forgettable. As for me, Japan? I never fucking wrestled in Japan! It wouldn't bug the shit out of me if that had ended up being part of my gimmick or anything, but it was just fucking lazy shit by that Mack "The Knife" Hunter waste of flesh. I was a rookie. Japan? Fuck you. I should've stabbed him with a knife, really made that asshole live his gimmick. And who did XWL jerk off to back then as part of their "youth movement"? The top two were guys known as Coldblooded and Crowbar. This Crowbar wasn't that jerkoff who hung out with that screaming goth chick and David Flair later on. PS Fuck you, WCW. Now, honestly, those two tore shit up. They had some "legendary" (relative term, given the hardcore genre and the number of people who actually witnessed them was quite small) fights, but where are they now? They aren't in ACW. They aren't on TV. They're gone. Forgotten. History. Fox and Wolverine? And two black dudes who were given some stereotypical voodoo shit to work with. Mexican Blacks = hardcore? So who was Sharc? "Sadistic." He was also Mr. Irrelevant. Right from the beginning. Now, here it is, 11 years later. Outside, the sky has transformed from dark blue to black. My company is a bottle of wine. Sarah was supposed to be here, drinking this swill with me, and I was supposed to pretend that I liked it and I was normal for a few minutes. She was one of the few people on this planet who has ever had that ability, to make me forget everything -- the past, the nagging pain in my body parts, loss, loneliness, failures. Sadly, I can count the number of people who have had that ability on one hand. And all of them are out of my life forever. The truth is I don't matter for who I am. The machine that is ACW would move on, whether I'm here or not. The Scorpion Division would roll with Omega, Iceman, and the rest of the boys (and girls). Hell, Trey Vincent wouldn't even notice I'd gone. He hasn't spoken a word to me in weeks. Liar. Big talker. Big nothing. If I were to go out, where would I go? My money's a bit tied up at the moment. Had to set up a little something for Barbara and Richard for down the line before it's too late. I'm surrounded by strangers in a foreign land. There's only one person I can call. A guy who works for ACW who promises that he can get me anything I need. A guy with "connections." There's only one thing I need tonight. "It's me," I tell the man with the connections. "I need a whore." "No problem," he tells me. "What do you fancy this lovely evening?" I paused. "Long brown hair. Plain-looking. I don't want a whore whore. All the usual extras." "Domme?" "Yeah," I said with a sigh. Not that he even gives a shit. Although, I'm sure he could write a scary fucking tell-all book for all the things he gets for the boys. "I'll call you back shortly with the info." "I'll--" "I know your good for it, dude, no worries." "Later." Several floors below me, a pool sits empty. The pool is me. I serve a potential function. I exist. People walk by me, maybe even take notice of me for a moment. But ultimately, tonight, they decided to move on to something else. Leaving me still. Useless. Empty. Alone. I exist in the world, but am not part of it. The whore won't be any better off for spending the next couple hours here. If it wasn't me, it'd be somebody else. I won't be any better off for her spending that time here. But at least I'll be able to sleep tonight. Whores and wrestlers are kindred spirits. They sell their bodies for money, just as we do. We're lost, broken, alone, addicted, whatever. But still, on some level, we get off on some aspect that keeps us doing it. I get off on saying, "Fuck you, Coldblooded. Fuck you Crowbar. Fuck all of you guys who are now gone from the business. I'm still here. Better than you ever were." Even if I was just an afterthought in the beginning. That could be me. One day soon, in the end, I will also be just an afterthought. I'll be forgotten. The wrestling business will go on. And just like the billions and billions of people who have walked this earth and died before me, none of what I have done will ultimately matter. Because, just like them, we are all irrelevant. I live for now. Because what the fuck else is there to do?
