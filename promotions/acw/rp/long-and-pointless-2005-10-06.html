Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster, The Shillin' Villain, The Choice of a New Generation, The Czar of Cashflow Long and pointless Location: LDC-HQ in NYC, NY Date: October 06, 2005 Number (Archive): 10 Number (Wrestler): 7 It was close to five at the Lowell Dot Com headquarters on Madison Avenue in New York City, New York. Receptionist Jill sat reading a romance novel and clacking her gum. Lowell exited the conference room and walked over to the front desk where Jill was seated. He stood whistling in an attempt to get her attention. When it was obvious she wasn't going to quit ignoring him Lowell slammed his palm down on the counter to make a loud bang. 'Jill, hey, how's it going? Good? Yeaah, I don't know if you saw, but I bought a new Camaro yesterday. Cobalt Blue. Worth a pretty penny. Actually it's worth many pretty pennies, but you know what I'm mean.' Lowell gave off a fake laugh and reached over to rub Jill's head in a buddy-buddy fashion. 'Ohhh Jilly Jill Jill, what would I do without you here to keep me sane?' Jill glanced up from her book and kind of half-smiled. Her flowing red hair was tied back in a ponytail. 'I don't know,' she said with a patronizing smirk. 'So I was thinking...what would you say if I asked you on a date. Nothing too fancy! Ho hooo... Don't get ahead of yourself! We're not engaged! This isn't our 63rd wedding anniversary! I was thinking maybe a nice, romantic dinner at a modestly priced restaurant. As friends. We can discuss movies--do you like movies? I LOVE movies. Mortal Combat? Johnny Cage? I've been told I people people alot of Johnny C. Maybe it's my perfectly symmetrical face.' 'Heh, I don't know, maybe...but I really better be going.' 'What's the rush! The Commie and I were planning to order a pizza and play Dreamcast. If you wanted, I could ditch him and you and I could order the pizza and play Dreamcast. Maybe play naked--I don't know! No shame here!' Jill stood up from her seat and grabbed her things. She turned to find Lowell leaning forward. His eyes were closed and his nostrils flarred as if he had been smelling her. 'You smell like the carnival. Mmmm...cotton candy.' 'You...smell like vodka and...' her expression was that of disgust, 'gasoline.' 'Gasoliiina! God, no one hates dirty Mexican hip-hop more than I do. Well, maybe Jimmy, but he just plain hates all minorities in general. You should have heard what he said about the Jews early today!' Lowell and Jimmy Cain stood next to an watercooler filled with Pepsi. 'God, I hate Jews. What's their deal anyway? Fucking Woody Allen. That guy iS a homo, I swear.' 'But--but--HITLER!' 'Yeah. Pretty sure he was one too, or at the very least a cross dresser.' 'HIIITLER!' 'Shut up Lowell, you're going to wake up the Commie.' The Commie stumbles out of his room further down the hall. 'I had a bad dream...' He padded down the hall in his footy PJs. Carebears. 'I dreamt that I was stuck in the Gap and these pairs of Gap sweatpants kept walking up to me and asking--very politely--that I rub their crotch. So I did. I'm not one to reject a pair of horny Gap sweatpants. And so it's going pretty good. I feel a little bit of moisture, and I think, 'GO COMMIE GO!' I'd never satisfied an article of clotching before... or a human being, but that's okay cause I'm pretty sure I'm meant to reproduce with some kind of intergalactic superhuman THING that travels here through a time rift. Time traveling aliens. Could you IMAGINE? I'd be set.' Lowell nodded. 'Ah yes, the sweatpants dream. I think we've all had that one before. One thing, though...when I took off the sweatpants there was a candid picture of Bob Dylan, naked and sitting Indian-style with a miniature cowboy hat sitting atop his head. Weird.' Jimmy raised an eyebrow to both of them. 'That's just fucked up, guys. I dream of sex and torture. And Ghetto Stomping a bunch of unsuspecting ducks. Boy, oh boy, was that a good one! I thought it was funny that they made a cartoonish popping sound and all their feathers went everywhere.' 'I dream of sex--with yo' momma!' Kenjamin, the guy from the temp agency, just happened to have shown up. He was this skinny guy who wears white button up shirts that are atleast three sizes too big and dress pants that have cat fur clinging to the ass. Kenjamin was a red-head which meant he was always yelling. Think a smaller, squeakier, early-twenties Will Ferrell. Kenjamin walked past the Commie, bumping him into the wall, and held out his fist for Jimmy. He made the Pouty Face. 'Feeest bump?' Jimmy rolled his eyes and obliged. Fist bump it is. 'Noice! Boys, I was just thinking, what if sometime soon we hosted an office party with hookers and that guy who can make people levetate! I'll bring the soda--Pepsi, o'course, right boyeee!' He snapped his fingers and pointed at Lowell. Lowell liked Kenjamin. He thought he was funny and that he picked up the lingo fast. Way faster than Commie. What a waste of a custom Lowell tie. Oh well. Kenjamin put his arm around Commie and pulled him in close to give him a noogie. 'Commie here can bring the fudge, won't ya Commie? Hahaha! That was a JOKE! I was implying that Commie's gay! Commie you big faggot, you're alright! You're really not, but what-ev! We're all buds here! Jiiiiiimbo, wanna' go pick up some tram later tanight!' Just to point out--Kenjamin idolizes the Jimmy. He thinks he's the coolest cat ridin' sub on the far lane. Or something. Jimmy, who's usually off-the-wall and nuts, drunk or stoned or both half the time, could not get into Kenjamin's sense of humour. He was just a Frat Boy with stupid red hair and a shedding cat. Fuck his cat. Note to self: Kill Kenjamin's cat. Maim it. Put it through the paper shredder on Jill's desk. Stuff what's left of it in Commie's shoes. 'Yeah, I don't think so.' 'Cool, cool! Maybe some other time then! Lowell, you up for some fur burgers! Commie? Wanna' get your dick wet tonight?' He knelt down besides Commie with his face next to the Commie's crotch like he planned on 'speaking' to it. He quickly stood up and fanned his nose. 'Dude--a clenched fist is NOT a vagina!' 'What the fuck were you doing with your face stuffed in his crotch in the first place?' Kenjamin was all like 'Huh?' before changing the subject. 'Did anyone watch NHL last night? Fuck man, I love me some hockey! I love the fights! If I were on the ice with Domi or some other enforcer guy I'd fucking shank 'em good! One quick swipe across the ribs and you've got yourself inked in the record books for sure!' He pretended as if his Bic pen was a knife and waved it infront of the Commie's face, backing him into the wall. 'Just joshin' ya! I wouldn't really stab ya!' 'Why?' Kenjamin turned around and found Jimmy Cain standing, arms crossed over his chest with blank expression on his face. 'If you're going to make like you're going to stab someone, stab 'em. Take a pound of flesh or whatever it is. Merchant of Venice motherfucker. Don't pussy out. Commie, lift up your shirt.' The Commie swallowed hard but did as he was instructed. Jimmy grabbed a switch blade from his pocket and held it infront of the Commie's eyes. The blade popped up. He lowered it to his gut. 'Oooooh jeeeeeze, oooooh jeeeeeze.' Thunk! The Commie fainted. A smile crossed over the Jimmy's face. A sick and twisted one. He put away the knife and turned to look Kenjamin in the eye. 'Always keep them guessing. You'll never have to question a person's loyalty that way.' Kenjamin nodded. They fist bumped again and then he took off down the hall to grab his sport jacket and head out to the bars. He was thinking he might go see if his friend Sean wanted to play Mannequin with him tonight. Basically, you stand there and stare into the window of someone's house, acting like mannequins. Neat, huh? Lowell glanced over to Jimmy and asked, 'You weren't really going to do anything to the Commie, were you?' 'I didn't have to.' 'Yeah,' Lowell said scratching his head, 'but that's not really what I asked. If Commie hadn't fainted like a bitch, would you have...you know...cut him?' 'Oh yeah! I've been close before. I almost bludgeoned him to death with a chair while he slept last Tuesday. I know, I know, but I was messed up on Angel Dust and thought he was a possessed.' 'Ah, I getcha. Guess what? I interviewed this totally hot chick the other day. Totally smokes Jill. We're talkin' Jill if I brought her to PC Medic and outfitted her with a new motherboard!' 'Kay--what?' 'She had big boobs.' 'Oh okay.' 'But she couldn't type and happened to forget to fill in her name and address on the application sheet. I've just been calling her Cadence Cristo for the last couple of days. Thought it sounded cool. Ca-dence Criiiiiiis-to. Hehe. Noice!' 'I'm going home,' Jimmy stated frankly as he turned to leave. 'I thought we agreed THIS was your home? I've got twenty-somethin' bedrooms and a sauna to boot! A sauna, Jimbo! We could Jamaican Rain Hot Box my sauna and watch Hollywood blockbusters solely for the simulated sex scenes!' Jimmy shook his head. He pointed in the direction he was headed. 'As great as that sounds--and I'm not being sarcastic, it does sound fucking awesome--I really have to go home and beat up that homeless guy I abducted for the sole purpose of using him as my own personal human punching bag. I feed him oats and the occasional Poweraid and leave him strung up in my weight room. I'm -this- close to having broke every single one of his ribs!' Lowell laughed. Oh Jimmy, what a joker. He was joking, right? Lowell didn't care either way. The homeless aren't people--they're living, breathing ghosts of people whose only real use happens to be if you have no change for coffee and don't want to break a bill. Just reach your hand in their begging cup. They're usually too fatigued from sleeping in the cold to catch run after you. That is unless you happen upon one that's 'fresh on the streets'--still got that spring in their legs and some food in their belly. They're dangerous. Usually disgruntled. Bitter. Carrying a stapler that they stole from the job they were fired from. You've got to watch out for those ones. Wow. That flashback seemed to drag on and on, didn't it? 'Wait! You interviewed some bimbo for my position? You asshole! I made the Commie a man and this is how you repay me! I'm sorry the stress involved with working here is giving me wrinkles and that I can't afford implants on the nothing salary you pay me, but have some god damn FUCKING respect!' Lowell, you have been shamed! His head hung forward. Then he realized he has no conscious and looked up, smiling. 'The Commie's been asking about cha, babe. What should I tell him?' Wink. Wink. 'I'm leaving. I've got to get home. I have a life outside this alternate universe you call an office. I need to get out of here.' 'Fine. Go. Jill, wait. Wait wait wait!--' cringe. The door slammed shut. Lowell was all alone inside his obscenely large headquarters slash mansion. Lowell pursed his lips together and narrowed his eyes. Parents are out--Tom Cruise in Risky Business. Oh wait. 'Loweeeeell! The Dreamcast won't turn on!' 'Just a second! Don't peel off the tape, it's the only thing that's holding it together!'
