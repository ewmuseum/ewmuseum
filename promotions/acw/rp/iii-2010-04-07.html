III

7 April 2010

Date: April 07, 2010 Sepiroth walked up to the his airlines customer service area and was presented with 2 options, one being a self check in and a second one being service from a customer service representative. SDL hadn’t flown in years, he had no need to, but mainly no money to. There were many places that SDL would love to see which is another big reason why SDL accepted his contract offer from ACW. They were in the middle of a huge european tour which would take SDL to places that he could only imagine, like his first stop Madrid, Spain. SDL had been to Madison Square Garden enough times to be a regular, he frequented the American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida often. But, never had he ever been to any place in Europe. SDL approached the customer service rep area only to be instructed to move to the self check out. “Bitch.” SDL muffled under his voice as he walked over to this little computer that didn’t even come up to his waist, but was very intimidating to Sepiroth who wasn’t very computer savvy. SDL started to answer the questions on the screen as they presented themselves to him. Everything was going smooth until a message appeared on the screen notifying Sepiroth that at this time they were unable to process his request and that a customer service representative would be over shortly to help him out. Once over by SDL the same customer service representative that originally instructed SDL to proceed over to the self check out now instructed him to follow her back to where they first met. SDL couldn’t believe it. What the hell happened? SDL thought to himself. He use to fly all the time...never coach like he was this time but that didn’t matter. He had never had this kind of problems, he was use to having his boarding pass handed to him or mailed to him directly. Now he’s having to go to some stupid computer only to tell him that it can’t give him what he wanted. This is exactly why SDL never trusts technology. “So I just have a few simple questions to ask you then we can proceed with getting your boarding pass ok?” Instructed the customer service rep. “Sure whatever it takes to get this process going” Replied SDL. “First of all I need to see your passport and a valid drivers license or a Visa/Mastercard” Sepiroth was taken back by this. “Are you questioning who I am?” Asked SDL. “Just standard airport security questions sir.” Replied the customer service rep. “I have never had this problem before!” SDL reached into his bag and pulled out his passport and his wallet and continued to hand the requested items to the customer service rep. As he handed them over she thanked him and glanced at them and started to make notations on her computer. “So are you flying for business or pleasure?” Asked the lady. “Business. If you would have asked me a couple of years ago I would have said pleasure though.” SDL replied back. The lady made a couple of more notations before grabbing her chin while thinking. “One moment please.” The lady left SDL at the counter and returned with another lady. Sepiroth normally didn’t care to be out numbered by the opposite sex, but in this situation he knew it couldn’t be good. “Sorry for your troubles sir, how are you doing today?”  Asked the newer customer service representative. “I’m fine what in the hell is going on?” Asked a frustrated SDL. “Well it’s been quite sometime since you’ve flown, and before that you use to fly quite frequent and since you have flown so much prior to 9/11 the government has complied a list of certain people we need to take extra safety measures to make sure aren’t getting on an American plane.” “Don’t you know who I am?” This wasn’t like SDL to start getting cocky and to attempt to use his name and his old fame in his favor. But, at this point in the day he was fed up with waiting for his boarding pass.  “I am Sepiroth Du Luc I use to be on TV at least twice, sometimes three times a week. I’m sure you’ve heard of me. Anyways I am flying over to Spain to reignite my wrestling career.” Both of the customer service representatives looked at SDL puzzled and confused. “Sir at this time we are going to have to ask you to come with us.”
