Introducing Wrestling's Biggest Bully

29 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Colossus Nicknames? He generally does the name calling Introducing Wrestling's Biggest Bully Location: Joey T's luxury penthouse, Beverly Hills Date: June 29, 2005 Number (Archive): 39 Number (Wrestler): 1 'Gimmie your dinner money!' Colossus chuckled to himself, drool running down his chin, as he shook an invisible kid around by the ankles. He was sound asleep, dreaming of the glory days. Back when he ruled the school one may think. But no. Just dreaming about yesterday while queuing in MacDonald's. Our masked fat man here, Colossus, is what could be described no better than as a bully. A dim witted, overweight and cruel bully. He effectively never grew out of this stage, never grew up, just grew out if his belly is anything to go by. It was at this point that our chubby bully was awoken by the loud growl of his gut; MacDonald's entering his mind inducing such a reaction. 'I need food.' grunted Colossus as he rolled off the sofa, cracking his arm on a coffee table on his way down - not that he noticed. 'Hey baby! Watch yourself! That's a thousand dollar table!' Introducing Joey T, Colossus' agent. Joey T was by all accounts, a geek. A scrawny little runt, but with an IQ of 142, an incredible talent for making money, and thus, a supermodel girlfriend and million dollar penthouse in Beverly Hills. So just why is this guy associating himself with an overweight, dim witted buffoon such as our Colossus here? 'Come on man, get up!' yelped Joey, as he snatched the TV remote from Colossus' chubby mit. 'We've got publicity shoots to do, baby! We've got people to see! We've gotta get you out there, we've gotta start the wagon rolling baby! You're gonna be a star!' Just how could this guy be a star you're wondering. Over the years, Joey T's taken many a risk in this business, and all you need to do is glance at his beau's 34DD chest to realise that he knows full well what he's doing. Particularly when there are more motives than just simply making more money involved. You see, Joey T and Colossus, aka Harry, were school buddies. Taking a trip down memory lane for a moment, Joey T remembers the days that he'd be surrendering his lunch money to this buffoon, doing his homework for him, and remembering how his slender frame kicked off Harry's obsession with hitting the punching bag in the gym. Now it was time for Harry to repay Joey, not that Harry had any idea he was doing this. As far as he knew, by being plonked in a wrestling ring, it effectively gave him a license to bully and hurt people. Joey handled all the tricky stuff, like contracts, finances, publicity... Talking of publicity... 'Marvellous!' beamed Joey as he proudly looked at the local newspaper. His first publicity shoot had worked like a charm. Refuse to feed a 465lb man for a morning, dress him up in his wrestling gear, and take him to a fast food restaurant, neglecting to bring any money for food with you, and hey presto, you get the aforementioned MacDonald's incident, as documented in the local press. 'ACW Wrestler In Fast Food Assault.' read Joey proudly. His first publicity 'shot' going better than anticipated. 'Only got a small mention, but we'll be cooking in no time!' 'Cooking?' grunted Collosus. 'In a minute.' Joey replied dismissively, as he carefully cut the article from the newspaper before filing it away in the top drawer of his desk. 'You hungry big boy?' Joey asked with a wry smile, with his next publicity “shot” in mind.
