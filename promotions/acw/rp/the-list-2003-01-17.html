The List Written: 01 - 17 - 03 Dane Rivers stared silently out the window. Sitting in the white, old fashioned taxi, Dane glanced blankly at the objects he stood passing by. It was odd for him to be in a taxi, let alone being out and about, for that matter, but it was a pleasant day, or atleast for now it was, and it was time to visit his old friend, Raitt Berketh. While Dane daydreamed, the cab came to a stop infront of the cemetary, Dane didn't move until the voice of the cab driver came through the pane glass window. '10.00, Sir.' Dane finally stepped back into reality, and pulled a twenty dollar bill from his jacket pocket. 'Give me ten minutes...' Dane stepped out the cab and walked slowly, down the gravel path toward the grave sites. He would've walked at a quicker pace, but he knew where the stone was, and he liked strolling here, so peaceful and serene. He spotted the grave and followed the straight toward the spot. He set foot upon the grass infront of the tombstone, and read the text. Raitt Berketh 1948 - 2002 'How have you been, old friend.' There was a long pause between the two, if you could call it that. 'Me, I've been good, whole new start...I can't stay long, but you know what I want to stay...I miss you.' Dane turned around, walking the path he used to get here. 'I almost forgot.'He walked to the stone, and reaching in his pocket, he pulled out another piece of paper. It had a name written on the front. 'Julian Kang.' He lifted up a rock on the stone and slid the paper under it... 'More will come,' he sighed... 'More will come.'
