Chances

28 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 28, 2009 The sun set so pretty in the sky behind the ACW Arena, with it’s oranges and yellows integrating the blue to make a purple haze resplendent in the horizon. Ikan found his eyes meeting its picturesque beauty and he almost didn’t hear the cab driver state the trip cost eleven dollars. He stuffed his hand in his pocket and handed over the note. The cabbie put it in the ash-tray, never giving Ikan a second look. “My change?” Ikan queried, pushing his hand to the front. Curiously, the cab driver looked in the rear vision mirror, retinas boiling with pith and vinegar, shaking his head at the very notion of forking out change. “I just assumed, eh? Most folk I drop here tell me to keep it, eh?” he muttered, belligerently shoving the change in the awaiting paw of Jobtayoo. Ikan pocketed it and pushed the door open. “Need a lift back?” Jobtayoo shook his head, almost hypnotised in the hue the sun and sky provided. It was almost as if God were painting an image of his future before him, presenting him with a glimmer into the future. Perhaps teasing him that things might actually work out in his favour. He drew breath, allowing his shoulders to rise and fall dramatically and for his own entertainment. ‘Vell, Ike. Here vee are,’ he thought to himself. ‘Zis is it!’ With his bag in hand, Jobtayoo straightened his tie, never noticing the cab driver pull back out onto the road and seamlessly rejoin the rest of the world rushing by. Ikan, cautiously stepped across the parking lot, nerves growing all at once. His stomach bubbled and garbled with the tension, indigestion lapping viciously at the peak of his stomach; threatening to rush his oesophagus. Ikan stepped on toward his future within All-Star Championship Wrestling. ＼(＾▽＾*) i k a n (*＾▽＾)／ “Well, Ikan,” silverHAWK said, pushing the contract across the desk, “I must admit we need a little more enhancement talent and given your history… I think you’ll fit right in.” HAWK gave the European a smile and shuffled a pen across to rest on top of the contract. Within Ikan, his heart sank like a villain in quicksand in an Indian Jones film. His face screamed “dejected” at the man offering the contract and he sigh heavily. His hand picked up the pen but his heart was screaming “WALK AWAY!” For the first time in Ikan’s life, he listened to his gut instinct and pushed the contract back silverHAWK, creating a baffled grunting noise from the ACW honcho. “What? Is there something wrong?” HAWK asked quizzically. Ikan nodded. “Yah. Zis is not vhat I vant. I’m sorry. I thought zis time things could be different for me,” Ikan admitted, rising from his seat. His rise was thwarted by the open palms of HAWK who wanted to talk things through, he seemed a little amused all the same at the situation. “Wait a minute… you… how do I put this… you think you can offer us more?” “Yah, as matters of fact. I think I can offer zee ACW brand a WHOLE lot more, Mr. HAWK,” Ikan rebelliously informed. silverHAWK shrugged, bemused by it all. His arms folded across his chest as if he were waiting Ikan to explain himself. “For years, Mr. HAWK, I have pushed people. Been stepping stones. Vell, I no longer vant to be zee man people take for jokes. I think I am better zan zat,” he proudly stated. “I have been jobber for many promotion, Mr. HAWK. I think zis time I should be given chance.” “A chance for what, Ikan?” Jobtayoo raised his chin, stared HAWK dead in the eye and with his whole heart behind him, resonating with the pride Ikan had stood up for himself for the first time in so long, he answered HAWK with his dream. “To be a champion.” HAWK attempted to stifle his laugh unsuccessfully. “You? A champion? Ikan, I’m sorry but you do KNOW who we have on our list? We’ve got Andy Sharp. Max Danger. Rejection. Brian Spaes. Alias, for fuck’s sake, kid. You think you deserve the chance to go over people like that?” He tried not to fathom the idea, his was in fact the most selfless man almost in existence but his dreams were flowing today. They pressured his mind and ate at his consciousness. The need to do this justified the means and proudly, Jobtayoo nodded at silverHAWK. “Yah. I do. I deserve chance. Vee all deserve chance to prove ourselves.” HAWK reached across and glanced over the contract, looking through it like the impervious administrator he was. He pressed it back to the desk and the whole section on enhancing the roster was crossed out. He spun the contract around and showed it to Ikan, who gingerly sat in his seat in awe of the offering. Holding a pen before his eyes, silverHAWK couldn’t help but smile and feel some warmth. It was overwhelming to see the way in which Ikan was elated to be given his opportunity. His moment in the sun. Plucking the silver fountain pen from HAWK’s grasp, Ikan signed his name along the dotted line. Ikan Gritsenko. He pushed the contract back to HAWK, shaking like the proverbial leaf and he fell back into the chair, awestruck by the moment. “There,” HAWK said, “you’ve got your chance.”
