Forever Infamous - Part Four

3 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 October 2010 “October!” the deep, recognizable voice of Oso Bannon shouts as a car door slams. “And still this hot! I feel like I’m back in Mexico City.” A yellow taxi speeds away, almost like it really wanted to get as far from him as quickly as possible. Standing still for a moment, Oso takes in the view. A condominium, pristine and bright white under the intense sun, with a perfect lawn. When he moved to Ohio he immediately found mowing his adopted parents’ immense lawn therapeutic and for a second he was brought back there. He snaps out of it only when he sees a SOLD sign near the end of the driveway. “Cheap bastard... I told him I didn’t want to live in the valley.” He begins walking towards the house, speaking in his native tongue to a gardner near the porch as he passes. Twisting the knob of the door, he takes one last, long look at his surroundings. Home? The house is bare except for a few brown boxes strewn across the floor. “Wallace?” Oso shouts, his voice reverberating through the walls of the bare condo. The chipper, Southern drawl of Chester B. Wallace echoes back. “I’m in the office!” Oso walks the small staircase to the second floor and immediately sees the grandiose scene in his peripherals. He steps into the office and faces the gaudiness of the room, along with the gaudiness of Chester’s smile, head on. There is more gold -- some real, some fake -- than any person should have in one room. The walls, end tables, and desk showcase many pictures of Chester with prominent professional athletes and officials, but the walls are covered in framed portraits of 1950s pin-up porn. The air smells of nothing but his cologne. “Nice to see you’re all settled, Wallace.” Oso spits. “I thought I specifically said no valley?! The way I see it I’m the one who’s important to this operation. Shouldn’t I have a say so?” “Win a championship and then we can discuss the living arrangement, young Bear!” Chester replies happily. “Such a diva! Perhaps we should start calling you cub! Besides, I got this place for a steal! It was foreclosed upon and...” Wallace stops and seems to think for a second, then sneers angrily. “I don’t think I need to explain anything else to you, Bannon. All you are is a body. Your bail, your training, this house...all of it bought with my money. Suppose I could just add it to your tab...” A scream interrupted. “I'VE TOLD YOU ONE TIME TOO MANY THAT I DON’T OWE YOU ANYTHING!” “And we’ve concluded one time too many that your debt isn’t what’s keeping you here.” Wallace speaks back calmly. “I’m the only shot you’ve got. Don’t concern yourself with what I’m doing. Think about wrestling and only wrestling. That’s why you’re here and that’s all. You’re going to get us both back on top, Oso.” “And what if I don’t?!” Bannon shouts back. “I don’t think I have to spell it out for you. You may have been a hard ass on the field in Ohio, but you wouldn’t last a day back in Mexico City. Maybe not even in the real world. All you know is competition and the things that motivate you to compete. If you leave, you may as well just go blow your brains out.” Oso Bannon’s eyes erupted with anger. His body began to lightly spasm. He wanted to mangle Wallace right then and there. “Talk to me like that again and I’ll blow your brains out. On God, Wallace. On God.” And just like that he was gone. He’d walk until he found a place to clear his head. Then he’d call another yellow taxi to take him back to the dark, dingy room...
