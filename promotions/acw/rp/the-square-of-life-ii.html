The Square of Life: II.

11 March 2005

Iceman The Ice Master; Cold Man; Mr. Freeze; Iceman (oh, wait, that's his name LOL); Icey; The Man Who is Cold; The Opposite of Fire; Father Winter; Mother Nature (wait, no); Ice Ice Baby... and so on. The Square of Life: II. Location: Iceman's Locker Room. Date: March 11, 2005 Number (Archive): 29 Number (Wrestler): 2 He awoke in a gasp. Cold sweat poured out from his body... as if he had been laying there for sometime now. The surface below him was cold... ice cold... as he tried to move... but found himself crimped up in even more pain, as more parts of his body met the unforgiving cement. He finally collected his thoughts... or some of them anyway. Iceman laid still, in the silence of his locker room... covered in a cold sweat which felt to him as if his body was awake way before his mind. Either it be on purpose or not, he didn’t think about that too much. Iceman tried to move again... this time fighting the uncomfortable pain in his body as he was able to sit up. An icepack rolled off the top of his forehead and into his lap. He spat across the locker room floor in sight of it. It was another embarrassing moment for him. And though it was just number two in an ACW-count... it played on him more, and it played on him harder than those embarrassing moments he would be put through in the PIW. He had Blast From the Past for those. He had no skill for those. But most importantly... he had no desire to have skill for those. These days were different. Iceman tossed the icepack to the corner of his locker room... before struggling to stand up on both feet. His head pounded in agony... and then it came to him as to why he was here in the first place. There Iceman stood... in the center of the ring... rage pumping so easily throughout his body he could’ve been a bloody outlet for it... The Loser stood across from him. His stupid grin. His ignorant style. His annoying gob of drool... that constantly dripped out of his mouth. It was EVERYTHING Iceman was in the past. And it was EVERYTHING Iceman didn’t want to be in the future. The critics thought he was still a joke... and the best way to change their opinion... was to take out the real one. Iceman charged at The Loser. But he failed to realize TL’s plan. He failed to see The Loser grab the top rope. And he failed to think it didn’t matter; that Iceman would find a way to hit The Loser regardless of what he was trying to do. SMACK. Again Iceman did it. He embarrassed himself. Landing face-first (basically -- the announcers made it seem more intense than it actually was) for the second straight week... Iceman could already see the headlines coming straight for him. It was over. This was it for him. If they didn’t accept him last week... they WERE NOT going to accept this week. So Iceman did the next best thing. He temporarily escaped his problem. He knocked himself out. And there, Iceman rested, as he leaned back against the cold brick wall. Sure, the wall was freezing... but it actually had a softer feel than the cement floor he had spent at least the last three hours on.
