Slowy killing a legend Pt. 3

Slowy killing a legend Pt. 3 Written: 10.24.02 AHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!! The violent screams of a man in pain. Oh, how I enjoyed that sound! That sound pierced the ears of everyone that was lucky enough to hear it. The crackling of bones; the thumping of metal hitting flesh, then flesh hitting flesh made them cringe in their seats. But there was nothing they could do, except watch the horror that ensued by my hands. Thousands of witnesses in attendace to this crime. Those blues, browns and green eyes just locked on my every movement... My every evil intention that I had for this half beaten man, that laid in front of me. It intrigued me even more to continue the punishment, as mothers young and old, pulled their children away from the view of the ring. The warm blood spilled from the gashes throughout the body of this ACW superstar; the blue canvas of the ring half way covered in red. My weapon of choice; the steel pipe laying in the distance, drenched in blood. He undoubtly was trying to fight to stay out of that white light one sees before they die. His eyes rolled in the back of his head... Pupils gone from sight. You know who I am talking about, or do you? He tries to get back to his feet... 'Where in the hell do you think you're going,' I yell down to him, clocking him in the back of his neck with a thuderous right hand that puts him back onto the mat. I turn around quickly, that's where I envision my manager, Ryojin and Ethan slowly motioning and yelling for me to hit him again. I can see Ethan moving in slow motion, micking punches being thrown by me, and landing on the spine of the ACW legend. That's when my rage takes over. An emotion that starts to overwhelm me in the heat of battle, as I slowly lift up my right leg... An eternal fire that burns deep down in my soul to claim vengenace for an adversary, whoever was stupid enough to cross me. My leg moving faster amd closer to my target... This true feeling that no man could understand. My leg rocked him as his crimson mask laid face down in the canvas. It felt like I had unlimited amount of power at my disposal as I stomped him with lefts and rights. Leaving foot prints all over his body. The crowd continue to jeer, but their hatred only fueled my hatred for him. Their reaction made me want to make him suffer more... And he would suffer. But what was going on here? Shouldn't I be in pain also? I rubbed my left hand against my rib cage, stopping my vicious attack. No pain shot from that region of my body surprisingly. I grew more confused by the second. Wasn't it just a couple of days ago when I had trouble getting out of bed; now beating a man half to death with no problem... Something just isn't right. I slowly kneeled down to him; placing my left knee in a small pool of blood, as I got closer to the man that I had been attacking for the past serveral minutes. I started to tug on his left arm, flipping him over onto his back... No suprise. It was still SilverHawk, but something was different about him. This wasn't the same SilverHawk that I loathed each and every week... This was just too easy for me, as I could see small breath of air entering and exiting his chest. Where was I? Is this a dream? SilverHawk's eyes opened... The cagey veteran quickly lunged toward me. But a tight grasp to his throat instantly stopped him as I violently choked him down to the mat. I could feel his breaths getting shorter and shotter as he fought back less and less. He laid still on the canvas for the final time, I slowly removed my hand from his throat... WHOA! I had awaken from this dream; white wifebeater and black boxers replaced the wrestling attire I had previous been wearing. I was in my bed, covered from head to toe with blankets as I slowly clutched the cold side underneath my pillow. Rolling onto my back; looking at the ceiling above, I couldn't help but to smirk. Because I knew this wasn't a dream... This was the near future I was seeing. In a matter of days, the entire world could look foward to seeing the end of SilverHawk in ACW. I could look forward to seeing the end of the legend.
