Therapy.3 Written: 14 JAN 2003 Having survived my ACW debut, I trudged back to New York for my third therapy session. Truth be told, I didn't really care for the therapy as much as I cared about seeing Natalie again. And she did look absolutely smashing, if not a little tired. And my two best friends.. well, they looked happy to see me. Of course, the temperature of the room probably was the reason behind that. But hell, after having lusted after men for so long, aren't I allowed to have a crush on a beautiful woman? One who's smart and friendly as well? However, I have NOT forgotten about our last therapy session. How did she know I was stabbed with a knife, when I merely said I was killed? Or did I actually say I was stabbed? My mind, which is filled with thoughts of me dancing in a field with Natalie, naturally thinks that I did indeed say it but just forgot it. But my heart.. the same heart that told me I would make it out of Isla Murderque Grona alive. So, as I walked into her office earlier, I found myself in a dilemma. She opened up the session with the same question she asked previously; how was I feeling. Well, I was doing alright. I made a good impression backstage last Sunday, on my ACW debut. Made friends with the crew-girl at the Washington Dulles International's McDonalds. And I didn't have any scary visions or whatever. Although I just HAD to bring up the fact that I had made a new friend in Janet, the McDonalds crew girl whom I met when I arrived at Washington just a couple of days ago. Met her again when I returned to the airport, to catch a flight back to New York. Hell, she said she had actually been at the MCI Center to catch Courage in the flesh. And she saw me! Whoo! Natalie was happy for me, but she was concerned as to why I looked so tired. 'Just touched down in New York last night; it's a tight schedule!' I replied. 'Understandable.' Ironic, eh? I noticed she looked a little tired, and she noticed the exact same thing about me. Heh. So, she began to ask if I had experienced any panic attacks since last week. I hadn't, and I made it a point to thank her for the advice and comfort she provided over the phone. Then, a whole slew of questions started raining down on me. 'How was your wrestling debut, in detail?' Wasn't too bad, to be truthful. Had a lot of fun after I had the displeasure of meeting one Dane Rivers. He seemed kinda stuck up and moody and all. But hey, that's his deal. 'Do you have a match on this week's show?' No idea, really. Right now, there's some sort of power struggle going on in the company. The situation is bad; so much so that I don't even know where this week's Courage will be held. However, I have been told to show up. I guess it's mandatory to show your face even if you don't end up doing anything. She went on to probe a bit about the wrestling industry, as she was somewhat fascinated and curious about it. I had no real knowledge of the industry as well, but whatever I knew, I relayed it to Natalie. And then, I realised something. This was her style. It didn't feel like a therapy session. It felt like two friends sitting down, at a Starbucks, sipping coffee.. talking about whatever the hell came to mind. This whole thing felt incredibly cool. The first session was awkward, the second was interestingly fun except for the last part.. but this session was just incredible. 'So, this next question is somewhat tricky; how good a fighter were you when you were.. you know, a fighter?' she asked as leaned back after having taken a sip of water, her eyes trying to judge my immediate reaction. Wow. She was right; it was tricky. And suddenly, everything started to feel a wee bit uncomfortable. But I told her the truth; I was pretty decent. I mean, my size and inexperience really made it difficult but I didn't view it that way. I saw the whole experience as a battle for survival, and a chance to win something so amazing. One that could eventually pave the way for me getting the answers I so desperately want. Alas, every chance you have in M15 has to be taken. Because once you lose four straight or eight in total, you're set for the guillotine. 'Excuse me for a second; M15? Four straight or eight total?' In the spirit of the moment, I had let it slip. I started to panic, but I reassured myself. Bruno had promised me that whatever was said in this room would never leave the room. It would not even be picked up by any listening devices. He made sure of that himself, he told me. 'Um.. yeah. The organisation. I'm sorry, I said a little too much there.. but I'm not really sure I'm ready to talk about THAT just yet,' I stammered in response. Half-expecting Natalie to sulk or something. 'It's perfectly fine!' And she said it so cheerily. For the remainder of the session, she did some word association; nothing too heavy. However, when it was time to leave, something happened. She led me out of her office, and as I reached the elevator, I extended my hand out to her. Least I could do to show my appreciation, really. When her hand clasped mine, however, I had a vision. A rather long but painless one. Natalie Quinston has secrets. And now, I know them. ALL OF THEM.
