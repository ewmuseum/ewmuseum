hallucinations [1] Written: ... …By now Scott has put on a pair of old, paint splattered jeans. Back in his apartment he felt safe, but as he walked across his bedroom and entered the sitting area of the small apartment the feeling of safety subsided. Scott seemed to see something. Scott dove across the wall and landed in a seated position against the corner of the room. Sweat poured down his face as he looked up at what he thought was there. “T…T-T-Tiger…” Scott fumbled out in fear. There was nothing in the apartment. Nothing was there… Nothing. How did Scott see something that didn’t exist? This wasn’t Scott Perry’s first hallucination, far from it. Scott had been experiencing hallucinations on and off for the past 5 years, although none quite like this one. Scott’s hallucinations had consisted of imagining objects changing from one thing to another, much like last weeks incident at his job in the local restaurant. This hallucination was different; this one was vivid and dangerous. This hallucination threatened Scott’s life in his eyes. Scott was scared; he was as scared of this hallucination as a small child was of the dark. As Scott continued to cower in the corner his imaginary zoo animal licked its lips. It was mealtime for the unfed animal and Scott Perry was going to be its lunch. Scott began to weep; he covered his watery eyes with his hands and bawled at the top of his lungs. Seconds passed as if to be minutes, minutes passed as if to be hours and finally, after what seemed to be days of bellowing and sobbing Scott looked up to see nothing. Nothing. No Tiger, no danger, no fear, nothing. Scott leant his head back against the wall, tears flooded down his face forming what were similar to streams down his cheeks. He had a funny taste in his mouth; the salty tears had dribbled in to his mouth, some had dried and formed a crispy layer on his face. He wiped away the encrusted tears and took a deep breath. He sighed. “Thank God.” He whispered to himself, “Thank… God…”
