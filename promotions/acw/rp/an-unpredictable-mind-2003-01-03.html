An Unpredictable Mind

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#115) by Quinton May, dated 3 January 2003, from the fed's first site, acw.ewmania.com (its acw2 edition, 2002–04) as captured in the Wayback on 30 May 2003. An Unpredictable Mind Written: 03 JAN 2003 I awaken with a jolt, gasping for breath and drenched in my own sweat. At least, I think it's sweat.. Earlier today, I had my first therapy session. My friend and landlord, Bruno, recommended that I go for therapy. Seeing how since I returned from what only be described as utter hell, I've only interacted with Bruno and his two girlfriends. I came home, and immediately napped. And I had a dream. Well, more of a recollection.. Of what did I dream? An exact re-enactment my first therapy session. I mean, it felt so real.. it felt as if I was going through some kind of time loop or something. Factoring in all the weird things that have gone in my life, I wouldn't be surprised if it WAS a time loop. 'Just a dream,' I mutter to myself whilst finally catching my breath. Turning my head to the right, I see that it's already night time. Weird. I felt as if I had been asleep for about twenty or so minutes. I rest my head on my lumpy pillow and close my eyes. Long day tomorrow; gotta help Bruno fix the truck and.. Uh oh. I feel it. A brain-flash. A vision. Whatever; the pain resembles the bully of your school kicking you in your privates.. repeatedly. But as suddenly as it comes, it vanishes. Once again, I'm left breathless. I mean, if it was because I was having sex, I wouldn't mind.. I shake my head, in an attempt to get rid of the proverbial cobwebs, before I start thinking about my most recent flashback of the past. Ever since I.. well.. I don't know how old I am, actually. But for as long as I can remember, I've been blessed or cursed with these brain flashes. My link to my past. The only thing in the world I want, really. To know what I was and who I was. Anyway, rolling out of this small bed of mine, I drag my feet over to the dresser, and pick up my glass of water. After gulping it down, I close my eyes and try to remember.. What is it this time? Instantly, I find out that I'm in a different place. A sunny place. San Diego? Los Angeles? Rio de Janeiro? No, judging by the way the people around me are.. I'm guessing Miami. Gay paradise of the US. 'Makes sense,' I whisper to myself whilst in my advanced state of recollection. I am, after all, gay. Well, was. I thank God for getting me off the island and safely back in civilization. I am one of his unwanted children, being gay and all. But he took the time to save my pitiful life. And now, I'm owe him. I am now not a child, but his disciple. That is why I've decided to give up my gay beliefs. Anyway, I see myself in Miami. A beachside. I'm walking, and walking.. and suddenly, I'm in a dark alley. With a knife in my hand. And a youthful looking Latin punk standing in front of me. Smiling sadistically at me. He kicks the knife out of my hand. I'm surprised, shocked, and inevitably.. pissed. He continues grinning at me. I lash out at him with my left fist, but he blocks it and retaliates by smashing his left forearm in my face. Staggering back, I fall down.. but once I realise my mouth is cut, I lose control of my mind. Jumping up, I again attempt a left hook. The Latin guy's fast; he ducks and attempts to catch me with an elbow-smash. This time, I show how quick I am by ducking and connecting with a sidekick to his gut. He falls to the ground and with those beady eyes of his, leers at me. With all the hate in the world. He couldn't have been more than seventeen, really. I try to see if I can feel myself, in an attempt to gauge my age. Before I remember this a sequence I have no control over. I'm just taking a trip down memory lane.. a very vivid trip. 'You wanna die the hard way, fuckhead?!' I find myself screaming. Weird. I never EVER say FUCKHEAD. 'You wanna dance.. let's dance!' he responds, before rolling back to his feet. Instinctively, I go for a right overhand. He sidesteps and plants his right foot in my gut. Yelling out in pain, I slump to my knees which gives the Latin guy a chance to score with a stiff sidekick to the back of my head. I can't even scream out in agony, it hurts so much. I lay on the ground for a while, before I muster the energy to rise. I turn to face him, ready to finish him off. 'You asked for this.' I snigger and rush at him. He drives the knife into my gut. Seconds later, I'm on the ground, the life being sucked out of me.. and before I know it, the sequence ends and I open my eyes, back in real-time. 'I was murdered?!' I spit out incredulously. How am I alive then? How? Why? // note // the murderer's perspective can be found here: http://iow.ewplanet.net/iow/_main/vignettes/140.shtml
