Ghosts [Past] Written: 12/22/02 The following Experience took place off-camera, at the End Game PPV on December 22nd, between SilverHAWK’s “Butterflies” segment and the “Branson Blair/Steve Bridge?” match. Now, since all that’s cleared up… enjoy. 'There you go HAWK...' With that John, the nicest little technician you could ever meet, left SilverHAWK’s side, in a hurry to get back to work. John simply nodded to the obscured figure that quietly replaced him at HAWK’s side. HAWK peered his head out of a small curtain more or less all of the arena was viewable... 'Fuck me...this is gonna be one hell of a night.' As the curtain closed, HAWK went to walk back to his locker room. Only to be caught off guard by the man who now stood in his path. “You better believe it’s gonna be one hell of a night… Aaron.” SilverHAWK had been caught off guard for the first time in a long time. This instance however, it was a pleasant surprise because he now stood face to face with a man who he had shared some interesting history with. Chris Sheffield. The Original Pulp Hero. Alias. “You’ve been doing great lately, old man, nice to see you with that big ol’ strap finally around your waist.” Alias said jokingly, giving Hawk a small jab on the shoulder. To people who had been watching ACW since October of 2002 this might be a bit of a surprise… for long time fans however… this reunion was a long time coming. “You can start calling me old when I get more then a decade on you.” Hawk said with a sly grin. “Hell, you aren’t looking too young either anymore.” “Let’s just say I’ve been through far far too much shit since ‘97…” Alias shook his head. If Hawk only knew the kind of things he had been up to since he first disappeared. “It show’s Sheff, you wouldn’t believe the heart attack Dunny almost had when Winters said he had signed you on during breakOUT. Of all the people for Winters to bring back… never thought it would be you.” Alias laughed to himself a bit, thinking about the present irony of that fateful night. “You’ve toned down a hell of a lot since I last saw ya, though… damn, it was right before we came into New York, too, wasn’t it?” Nothing like bringing up some good memories of the past, thought Alias, and there where damn few of those left. The two superstars leaned up against the cool concrete wall of the corridor, Alias shook a cigarette from his latest pack and lit it up. The embers sparked in the deeply shadowed location. He offered the pack to SilverHAWK. Hawk shook his head ‘no’, so Alias dropped the crumpled pack in his leather jacket pocket and took the cig from his mouth. “Yeah I remember the last time we talked, we where in The Pyramid, man did that place just light up when ACW came to town, and it was just after our US title Ladder Match…” “Your just lucky I didn’t rip you apart Sheff.” “Yeah that’d be just your luck, heh. That was one fucking match though.” Alias and SilverHAWK almost instinctively rubbed there respective lingering injuries, before catching each other, and quickly stopping. “For ACW’s first Ladder Match I think we did pretty damn good, hey I even here ol’ Vince got some ‘inspiration’, after he saw what we did, and put together that Micheals/Ramon match, trying to upstage us… You know my knee is still fucked up from that final spot?” “I’m sure the people in the first couple of rows are still fucked up from that final spot.” Alias said to SilverHAWK with an amused grin. “You’ll never know how much that match, that damn U.S. title, hell especially what we’d been going through a couple of months before, helped me grow up. I’m just glad you took me under your wing, Aaron, you where like a big brother you know.” The sudden emergence of Chris Sheffield’s lighter side hit SilverHAWK by surprise, inside he appreciated the corniness of it all. “Don’t get all fucking sentimental on me, Sheff, we both have a big night ahead of us. Bigger then back in Memphis and you know that. Better get ready for the expected, huh?” “Nothing ought to be unexpected as long as you trust the people that you know have your back… good luck and when you get the chance tell Jimmy to get better… he doesn’t deserve to be out… none of us will ever deserve to be out.” SilverHAWK nodded solemnly in agreement at Alias, and after shaking hands with him, Hawk was once again alone with his thoughts as he drew the curtain back one more time to watch the now converging MSG crowd.
