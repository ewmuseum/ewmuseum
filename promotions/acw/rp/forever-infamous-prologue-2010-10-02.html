Forever Infamous - Prologue

2 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 02, 2010 The sun is rising in Los Angeles and Chester B. Wallace still hasn’t slept a wink. His three television spread that sits before him is set to ESPN, ESPN2, and ESPNu. If you were to watch these channels all at once -- as he is -- you would see his face twice in one hour on each. He can plead every night to the gods, but his troubles aren’t going away anytime soon. A television whispered faintly and a sidebar photo of Wallace appears, “This is Erin Andrews, from ESPN’s new Los Angeles office, with a special report.” “Chester B. Wallace has officially been fired from his position as head of sports management for powerhouse firm Endeavor, a branch of the company that he himself helped build. This comes in addition to the five year ban from being an active agent he received from a federal judge last week.” A clip showing a dimly lit courtroom and a disgusted looking judge appears. “Mr. Wallace, I’ve seen a lot of unscrupulous professionals come through these doors and you are by far one of the most terrible. You’ve taken advantage of inner city youth and their families all across this country and I hope that you’re charged in those places as well as here. You are sucking the life out of something that is supposed to be pure and exciting and fun. And not even for the love of competition or sport, but just for personal gain. If it were up to me, you’d never work in this town again, but as it’s your first tampering offense a five year ban will have to do.” Chester buries his head in his hands and sighs. Reaching across his desk for his security bottle of scotch, his eyes come upon the sports section of the Los Angeles Times where the headline reads: THE RISE AND FALL OF OSO BANNON “Oso, you dumb son of a bitch...” Wallace mutters before taking a big rip from the bottle, “At least I’m not the only one going down in flames. Hell, I only got five years. You’re done for good.” He takes another slug from the bottle as his eyes move to a little blurb about All-Star Championship Wrestling’s current tour of Europe. After a moment of thought, those same eyes grow wide. Wallace reaches for his cellphone. “Hello, Southwest?” he says. “Put me on the soonest flight to Cleveland, Ohio you’ve got.”
