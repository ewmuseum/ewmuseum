New Beginnings Written: 12/31/02 At Endgame heroes fell. At Endgame villains rose to heights unimagined. At Endgame the federation fell deeper into its darkest hour. But at Endgame there was still celebration for one man. One man who seized his first ACW title in his second month of working with the company. One man celebrated having won both of his matches and the ACW United States Championship in the Madison Square Garden arena, not far from his hometown of Blackwood, New Jersey. No one could take away his high. He was the ACW United States champion. He is Geo Vacton. Vacton zipped up his backpack and swung it around his back. He picked up a leather strap from the nightstand of the hotel and gazed at it as he had done several times over the holiday. He thought of how he had come so far in ACW... It wasn't the World Championship, but it meant the World to him, and so, it could just as well have been. He lost himself in thoughts of the crowd cheering him on, they really liked him. He was becoming an idol for them, a role model... Whether that was a good thing or not, he didn't know. He just knew he was going to take the ball and run with it, because it's the only thing he could do. He put the belt over his shoulder and took a deep breath. Ever though ACW was taking time off, Vacton was still on the road. He didn't have a home to go home to... The road was his life, he had been a nomad for as long as he could remember. 'Hey, skippy, lets go!' The door opened behind Vacton. Startled, Vacton quickly turned around to be met with the jolly face of Magick Man. 'Guess what? I got the Card for Courage on the 12th.. You're not going to believe this.. As of right now, you're main eventing. Isn't that wild?' Vacton grins and shakes his head. 'That's amazing... Jeez, Magick, you're amazing. You've done a lot for me these last two or so weeks. I don't know if you've been working someone's strings, but I've been doing a lot better in and out of the ring...' This wasn't a spur-of-the-moment speech Vacton was making, he had planned it since the moment he won the title. 'I know you got a lot goin' on with 21w.. But I was wondering if you'd be my permanent manager. Y'know? It'd be the way it is now, just more official.' Magick Man grins and thinks for a brief moment. 'Geo, I'd love to be your manager. And hey, I did it before, handling you and Haunt, right? Sure, it's two different promotions now, but Hey, they don't call me Magick Man for nothing.' There's a very tender moment between Vacton and Magick Man as the two shake hands settling the agreement. 'But, Haunt is my number one priority... So, you gotta know that. But every chance I get that I can come out and see you live, I will. Even if I'm not down here with you, I'll be on top of your situation. I'll steer the boat, you power it. Haha.' Magick pulls out a sheet of paper and hands it to Vacton. 'Here, this is the rough card for Courage.' Vacon took the paper from Magick. 'Thanks.. I don't know how you get this stuff...' Vacton scans the sheet and then puts it into his pocket. 'Yeah... Well hey, how about we catch a few drinks at the airport bar? It's New Years Eve, and there has to be midnight somewhere... We can grab a few brews before we hop on our different planes.' Vacton grinned. 'Sure thing, Magick.' 'Ok, let's get goin' then...' Magick leaves the room and enters the hallway. Vacton stays back for a moment, looking at his title and thinking about the year to come. 'They're gonna see a lot more of me... in Two-Thousand and Three.' Vacton grined as he took off his backpack to put the gold belt inside. Vacton walked out of the room, locked the door, and looked forward to drinking a few brews with his new manager.
