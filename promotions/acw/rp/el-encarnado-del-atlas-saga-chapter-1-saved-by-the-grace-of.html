Yuri Yates The One Man Military Force, the Absolute Behemoth, the Paramount Colossus, the Superhuman, El Encarnado del Atlas El Encarnado del Atlas’ Saga: Chapter 1 – Saved by the Grace of . . . Location: Unknown Date: August 19, 2004 Number (Archive): 12 Number (Wrestler): 1 11:59 am. Tuesday. April 30, 2002. I remember that date so clearly. It was the day I was born. It was not the date of my birth, however it was the date of the beginning of my life. All the way down onto the exact time. It was a blistering afternoon. The ultra violet rays produced by our solar system’s single source of radiance scorched my bruised and battered body. My clothes were torn and rugged. I only wore shredded jeans that exposed my underwear, as well as one sock with a hole on the tip that revealed my big toe. I found myself laying in a narrow alley situated in between two dumpsters, shielding myself from the passersby’s vision. I had awaken with sweat pouring not only from my face, but from all over my body. I had slept in a pool of my own perspiration. Drenched, I stood up to absorb the environment of which I was contained. My chest was a beaten, bright red that resembled the shade of a stop sign. I did not recognize my milieu for it was very alien to me. I began my journey toward the nearest scene of civilization – the busy sidewalk of which many people sauntered through. However, just as quickly as I had taken a step towards life, life had stepped on me. In other words, reality sank in. My right leg quickly collapsed as I dropped to the pavement in a heap. A sharp pain jolted through my right thighbone – apparently, my right femur bone had been broken. But how? I pondered on that thought for a second. Then, a few seconds. Next, a minute. After that, a few minutes. I could not think of a plausible reason as to why my right leg had been broken. Come to think of it, I could not think of anything at all. My memory had failed me. I did not even have a memory. Am I dreaming? I queried myself. Am I still sleeping? Why can’t I think of anything? I must have been suffering from amnesia because not a single thought could be traced through my mind. Reality once again came back into play as I clutched my right thigh in agony, once more realizing the injury I had obviously recently obtained. “Help!” I screamed out at the top of my lungs, hoping one of the passersby would hear my cry. “Somebody! Anybody!” I relinquished the tight grasp on my wound and cupped my face with the palm of my hands, before laying back onto the sweltering pavement once more. I knew for sure I would be stuck here for quite a while. I might as well and try to acquire some rest before I manage up enough energy to crawl towards the sidewalk. “Hello?” a masculine, yet not fully matured voice inquired. “Is anybody back there?” The voice echoed throughout the alley. I sprang up onto my buttocks instantly, looking toward the beginning of the alley. My cry for help had been answered. But how? I pondered again. No one could hear me. I thought about it for a couple of seconds more. It was obvious to me now. God. God had answered my cry. I had been saved by the grace of God. “Hey!” the voice resounded once more. “Who’s back there? Who needs some help?”
