Gacy King Kill Risen From The Ashes - Chapter 1 Location: Obscurity Date: August 14, 2005 Number (Archive): 4 Number (Wrestler): 1 The grimace upon his face indicated the distaste within his mouth for the attire he wore. A firmly pressed, recently purchased three piece suit. Black in color, painted with gray pin stripes. His under shirt was the same shade of night, accentuating the silver power tie he adorned on this particular Spring afternoon. His shoulder length blond hair was neatly trimmed, and his face clean shaven. He had become that which he spent the last 5 years of his life revolting against; a businessman. Not through choice, but by necessity. Recent events had taken from him the only way he knew how to make money, and soon enough his nest egg would be gone as well. If this didn't work, he would become no more than a derelict. A worthless addition to society with no real purpose. Scavaging the land for a meal, coating the world with a dilapitated carcass. He would become the shell of a man who had such great potential that was tossed away by his own selfish desires. Survivial would only be achieved by the changing of his goals, which he reluctantly did. 'This is it, don't fuck up.' The mammoth of a man took a deep breath, and turned from the large mirror. His footsteps echoing across the tile floor, he approached the portal to his livelihood. Behind that door sat 3 men, 3 pocket books, and 3 answers to his problems. All he had to do was convince one that he was worth listening to, and just maybe he wouldn't have to spend the rest of his days in an infested alley. Opening the large oak mass, he made his way through the hallway he saw as his purgatory. It was the one place that divided his eternal bliss, and the literal shit hole of life. Although brief, his time in this corridor calmed him before the intellectual storm. He was able to regain a small bit of faith in his own product that was just enough to convince himself that this wasn't a feeble attempt. It came just in time. A weak willed man would be fed to the wolves that inhabited this new chamber. Gazing around the room, he saw the stern gazes of these attainable producers. Each had achieved a wealthy living in their own particular field, which was a fact this presenter was well aware of. The first was Miles Thompson, an Executive Producer for a local television network that specialized in extreme sports. He had quickly become well known for his success in the entertainment industry, and was on his way to achieving national exposure. The second was an older man by the name of George Mason. He had recieved his fortune after releasing a controversial line of apparel that appealed to a young athlete demographic. Any person in the sport entertainment business knew the lucrative influence of merchandise in their business, and Mason would stand to do quite well if he signed on. The third was the black sheep of the group. Lajon Montuori, infamous for his involvement in 1/2 of the Rocky Mountain region's drug network. As a front, Montuori also owned 10 pipe and tobacco shops across the Front Range. Although unconventional, he did have money and that was all that our hopeful entrepreneur gave a shit about. They all turned their attention to the new inhabitant as he walked around the large centered table. His eyes remained focused on his destination as he circled the room, and finally came to his place at the head of the table. Clearing his throat, he began the speech that would influence his entire life. 'Good afternoon gentlemen, and thank you for meeting me here on such short notice. As I mentioned to you over the phone, my name is J.W. Gacy, and I am about to present to you all, a very lucrative proposition.'
