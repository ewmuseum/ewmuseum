Training Written: 17 JAN 2003 '20 minutes break! Then, back to work. You're gonna have to be at the airport by six!' Bruno yells out to me as he tosses me a black towel. I grunt in response, but by then, he's already slammed the door of this mini-gym behind him. Sweating profusely, I dab the towel all over my face and try to stop panting long enough to get myself in a sitting position. Yesterday, training with Bruno began. He vowed he was gonna bulk me up and teach me how to fight better than I've ever fought. I reminded him I was in the wrestling business now.. and he laughed at me. 'Wrestling is just fighting with fancy costumes, son! Hell, boxing is fighting with gloves. In the end, it's all the same shit! Now, get rid of those purple shorts, for the love of god!' ... I like purple. In any case, Bruno simply made me work on my stamina yesterday. Push-ups, bench pressing, jogging, skipping, sit-ups; a pretty easy regiment, I had to admit. And I was liking it. Today was a whole different ball-game, however. Today, Bruno went straight to the really physical stuff. Sparring, scenarios, and the like; we had started at seven in the morning. SEVEN IN THE MORNING. Who the hell starts training at seven in the morning? I believe some people actually have animalistic sex at seven in the morning. But train? So, we began with the simple stuff. Stretching, bending, sniffing armpits and the like. And then, Bruno starts hitting me. I was confused, so I just let him hit me, thinking he noticed me sniffing his ass. Bad move. He knocked me out for about.. ten minutes. 'Man, you're weak! I don't believe you were a fighter all those months!' Bruno screamed at me. That got me angry. I mean, sure.. I may not look like it. But who has the right to judge and ridicule my valid claims of having fought some of the world's toughest, and on occasion, emerged the victor? Right at that moment, Bruno was proverbially spitting at what I had gone through all those months. So, I got to my feet, and began to fight. I must admit, it felt good. We fought, no holds barred, for thirty minutes. Maybe longer. I poured forth every single thing I picked up throughout my life. Memories of Isla Murderque Grona came flooding back. My first fight. The fight in which I won my first reward. It was great. Alas, I came out on the losing end.. my stamina let me down, and allowed Bruno to finish me off. And now, here I was. Just a few minutes past nine o' clock, flat on my back. Beaten. Dejected. Deep down, however, I knew Bruno was somewhat proud. And I appreciate what he's doing for me. But this Sunday, should I have a match, I'm going in there with a wrestling mentality, and coming out with a wrestling mentality. I know I can fight. But can I wrestle? This second chance at life was given to me, and the deal was to chance. It's been hard trying to be straight. Hell, just an hour or so ago, I sniffed Bruno's ass. I had semi-lewd thoughts while doing so. But I know I'm doing well as far as repenting goes. Hell, a few years ago, I wouldn't have given any girl the time of the day. Now, I have four; Natalie Quinston, Janet, and Bruno's two girlfriends. 'You ready to continue?' Bruno asks as he trudges back into the gym, carrying a bottle of Gatorade in his right hand. I turned my head and looked at him, 'Yeah, I am.' Even though I want to wrestle, something deep within me tells me I'll be doing some fighting real soon. Now, if I could get a vision to back that up, I'd be more at ease. Or would I? Last vision I had.. WAS NOT GOOD AT ALL.
