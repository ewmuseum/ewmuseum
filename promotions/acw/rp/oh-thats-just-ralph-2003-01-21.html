Oh, That's Just Ralph!

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#145) by Jay Smash, dated 21 January 2003, from the fed's first site, acw.ewmania.com (its acw2 edition, 2002–04) as captured in the Wayback on 26 July 2003. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Oh, That's Just Ralph! Written: 1/21/03 'That's right folks, and if you don't like the first 7 minutes in your work out, we will mail you the extra minute, absolutely free!' 'We are now back to the show...' 'Oh, That's Just Ralph!' **Applause** 'Oh well that's just me and well that's just you. That's a plate of bat poo-poo. That's ok, eat it anyway, hahaha, Oh, That's Just Ralph!' 'Today's episode, did you just break your mother's nose again?' 'Oh honey, dinner is ready.' Bam! A football flies across a living room on the set of Oh, That's Just Ralph. The football knocks the woman calling for Ralph down to the ground and causes blood to shoot out of her nose like crazy. 'Oops. Did I just break my mother's nose again?' The audience then replies with, 'Oh, That's Just Ralph!' **Applause** The next shot begins on the set of Oh, That's Just Ralph, with Jee Smacks, opening a door as a bunch of people begin to do their cheering kind of thing when popular sitcom actors walk into a scene. Jee Smacks runs right in and sits down on a red couch. 'Hey everybody, how is it goin' today?' 'Oh, everything is fine. Ralph just broke my nose again.' 'Oh Ralph. Hahahahaha. Say, what is this I hear about you going to a Taco Bell and eating all the Tacos?' 'Well you know me, I love my Tacos!' 'Oh, That's Just Ralph!' **Applause** Suddenly, the shot of the show breaks away and a late breaking news story is then shown on the screen with a man in a nice suit sitting behind a large desk. 'Hello everyone, I am Very Large, with a late breaking news story. Mother Rhino, the mother you all know and love as the mother of Ralph in Oh, That's Just Ralph was found in her 5-story apartment building, raped, and brutally murdered. Suspects have not yet been named but are believed to still be on the loose, armed and very dangerous. We now bring you back to your original broadcast, Oh, That's Just Ralph.' 'Hey, whose that mother with the broken nose?' 'It's me, it's me.' 'I said whose that mother with a broken nose?' 'It's me, it's me.' 'Hukuwaka, Hukuwaka, Hukuwaka, wam!' The show once again, is cut off with late breaking news coverage. 'We are sorry to interrupt your broadcast once again, but we have now been told that that their are two suspects in the Mother Rhino murder. One, by the name of Roy Rogerson, who played Ralph, in the Great show, Oh, that's Just Ralph, and the suspected partner, Suckit Nicely, the actor who plays a man by the name of Jee Smacks in the very same show. We will have more details later this evening. Now back to the show, Oh, That's Just Ralph.' **Applause** 'Oh boy, here comes Braxton Blude and Gao Vacum. Those guys are bad.' 'Now honey, don't be scarred, you have your nice friend Jee Smacks to help you out.' 'You're right!' Knock, knock, knock! Braxton Blude and Gao Vacum knock on the door then enter, into the living room on the set. Jee Smacks stands up and begins to talk when the show is cut off once again with late breaking news. 'Ladies and gentlemen, it is a sad, sad day for us all. I now bring you live with Lilo and Stitch, both reporters, out on the streets, getting more information about the murder, of Mother Rhino, also the 65 year old women from New York City.' 'Hi Very, Stitch and I have been out here all night trying to find more information, and we think we have found more information, as the police discover a body just two blocks away from the scene of the crime, in a dumpster in the back of an ally. The body matches actor Joe Ajacks, who played the roll of Braxton Blude in the show Oh, That's Just Ralph. We are not sure if this is linked to the same crime as Mother Rhino, then again, we are not that stupid, and we realize that both bodies were characters from the great show, Oh, That's Just Ralph. Back to you Very.' 'Thank you, and we will have more later on this evening, and once again we are sorry for the interruption. Now back to the show, Oh, That's Just Ralph.' 'Oh, That's Just Ralph!' **Applause** 'I think we learned a valuable lesson today. We should knock and wait for someone to answer the door next time, instead of opening the door to a person's house. Thank you Jee Smacks for opening our eyes and teaching us a lesson.' 'No problem Braxton Blude and Gao Vacum. I am so glad I could help you realize that, it's not your fault that you don't know what is right and what is wrong, you just need to be taught my a man that understands, and a man that can finally end what people like you have started. You are very welcome, and I hope I can help again some time.' 'Good bye Jee Smacks, good bye Mother Rhino, and Oh...Good bye Ralph.' 'Huh? Oh...' Ralph is seen eating some Tacos while Braxton Blude and Gao Vacum head out the door. 'Oh, That's Just Ralph!' **Applause** More late breaking news 'Once again, we are terribly sorry for interrupting the show, but it has come down to this. Mother Rhino, Braxton Blude, and Gao Vacum, by their character names from the TV show, Oh, That's Just Ralph, have all been found murdered in different parts of this street tonight, and still, there is no sign of Jee Smacks or the mysterious Ralph. If you know these men, and know any information about their where....abouts, please contact the police. Now we go back to our original broadcast of...Oh, That's Just Ralph.' 'So we sing this song, And we sing it great. It's time to say so long, See you next week. We all love you, and you love us. Remember, to eat you Tacos, and Oh...That's Just Ralph!' 'See you next time, on Oh, That's Just Ralph. Next weeks episode. Jee Smacks gets a new car, Ralph drops a taco in it, and Braxton Blude laughs! Next week on, Oh, That's Just Ralph.' The show ends, and finally we see the real Jay Smash and the really real Ralph, standing up, watching the television as they laugh. 'Wow, that was a great episode.' 'yeah Jay, those guys really know how to teach them bullies some lessons. I just hated all those un necessary interruptions. I didn't get those at all. Who would be stupid enough to add things like that to make a show seem longer?' 'Yeah really, mind idiot came up with that idea. Some real dumb asses out there, I'll tell ya. Anyways, that show almost reminded me of something....' 'Hmmm, yeah it did. I just can’t get my finger on it though.' 'Oh well, it doesn't matter. I just know that this thing between Geo Vacton and I, are cooling down for now, because I got my eyes on someone else. It's time I go one by one, paying back those who started something with me. First on my list...Brandon Blade....'
