Ernie French Remember [1] Location: Date: July 10, 2005 Number (Archive): 43 Number (Wrestler): 2 Eight months, encased within the same four walls. Imagine the horror of living in one room, painted Brilliant White, for eight months. Eight months, restrained by your own mental state. Imagine the frustration of being unable to remember your name for eight months. This was the life I lead. I sat on the floor of my cell with nothing to keep me company but my own inept mind and the daily struggle as I fought to remember. To remember my name, my past, my… My anything. Until that is, around six days ago. Sitting in my cell, legs crossed in the centre of the room, a knock on the door brought me back to full consciousness for the first time in around 36 hours. “Visitor.” The call from the orderly came bellowing through the small air holes in my cell door. Moments later the door slowly creaked open, sunlight blinding my vision. As I squinted my eyes and looked toward the opening in my cell a shadowy figure loomed over me, clutching something in his right hand. I raised my hand to act as a shade, to no avail. “Stay where you are.” The voice said bluntly. Not that I had much choice I stayed seated, my eyes aching from the first view of light in the last 12 hours. Feebly I asked, “W-who? W-what?” And the man began to tell a tale that I found difficult to believe. The memories I thought to be lost forever were held inside a folded piece of cardboard that he clutched in his strong right hand. That was the day that I was promised the safe return of my memories. Unfortunately they were only to be returned under his strictest of conditions. I’ll do anything to remember. The pain this emptiness causes is just too much to burden any longer.
