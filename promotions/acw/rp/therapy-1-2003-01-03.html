Therapy.1 Written: 03 JAN 2003 Therapy.1 - 03 JAN 2003 Pacing about in the waiting room, I was nervous. Aside from Bruno and two of his girlfriends, I've not interacted with anybody since my return to civilization. This therapy thing wa Bruno's idea; he figured it would be a good outlet for me to talk until I was blue in the face. When Bruno found me, I felt almost obliged to tell him every single thing that went on. He would have none of it; he simply pitied me and took me in. Now, I realise that I can't go around talking to any old fool about what happened. Secrecy is of the essence. The door to Natalie Quinston's office opened, and out stepped the psychiatrist herself. She looked very young, possibly twenty-four or so. Long auburn hair, and the body.. well, if I had come across her years back, I would have never become gay. 'Mr Quinton May?' she asked as her eyes set on me. I simply nodded, still somewhat stunned by her beauty. Plus, her fashion sense.. very good. 'Come in,' she said whilst smiling. This was it. My session was about to start. For two weeks, I had been imagining how this first session would pan out. I was quite excited but at the same time, sceptical. As I stepped into her office, my heart almost stopped; it felt so cosy. Natalie sat herself down on a blue couch, and motioned for me to sit on the other blue couch across from her, with only a round glass table seperating us. I smiled nervously as I eased into the chair, and looked around the office. Natalie, meanwhile, picked up a folder off the glass table. I presume it was about me. 'Um, Bruno did tell that whatever I say in this room stays in this room, right? I mean, the stuff I have to say; it's heavy and it could get me into trouble..' I spoke up. Assuringly, I got a nod from Natalie as a response. Well, it was going well so far. So far. 'So, Mr Quinton.. how are you today?' 'Good. I'm nervous about this whole thing.. but I'm good.' 'Yes, your friend Bruno told me you might be nervous. Tell me, how did you come to meet Bruno?' Wow, she was fast. I thought she might ask me questions about what I had for lunch or what porn video did I watch the previous night. I remained silent for a while, not sure of how to answer. She didn't look impatient though. Of course, probably part of the job. 'Well, after escaping the island, I swam and swam until I just gave up. I let myself drift over the seas.. then, I hit land. I remember once I was on solid ground again, I passed out. For what seemed like a couple of days. Turns out it was an abandoned beach or something like that. I got up, and walked. Walked for miles. Then, this truck came by.. and well, Bruno was in it. He almost knocked me down..' I babbled almost excitedly, before I noticed how strangely she was looking at me. Almost as if.. As if she didn't believe what I was saying. 'Go on,' Natalie remarked as she crossed her legs. 'Well, he brought me to his home for the night, and he pitied me enough to let me stay there for, like, ever!' She nodded her head slightly, before looking down at the opened folder on her lap. I had a lewd thought for a moment; couldn't help myself. 'This island you spoke about.. what is it, exactly?' Oh crap, I was afraid she was gonna ask this question. But at the same time, I was prepared. 'To begin with, it's an island where the ultimate dream can be acheived if you're smart enough. You don't have to be strong or whatever. It's all about survival, and you have control over whether you live or die. But.. it was hell. It was absolute hell. And if I go into the specifics, I fear they will find me and kill me...' I replied softly. And I knew right then that Natalie Quinston wasn't believing a word I said. 'They?' 'The people who work for.. for the organisation that owns the island. I don't want to die. Not without finding out what and who I really am,' I shot back quickly, totally on edge now. She was looking very solemnly at me now, and I noticed just how hyptonic those eyes of hers were. 'How come you don't know much about yourself?' 'I don't know. I was brainwashed, probably. I think.' Silence. Awkward silence. If I were her, I'd shoot me. I was sounding like an absolute moron. Bruno did say I whined a lot. 'Do you know your age?' Natalie asked, and I sensed concern in her tone, which was weird to me. I shook my head, to indicate I didn't. 'I have no idea how old I am. Bruno said I looked like a retarded four year old, his girlfriends said I seemed like a thirty year old guy.' Perplexed, Natalie took another glance at the folder, and sighed slightly. I knew a tough question was coming up next; probably about me being gay? 'Okay. You say that you often get brain flashes.. visions.. of your past. Like a big jigsaw puzzle. Why do you think so?' she queried, her eyes narrowing and looking really baffled by what she was asking. Not the question I was expecting, especially considering I was kinda sensitive about my link to my past. The way she phrased it felt like she was mocking the whole idea of it. Biting my lower lip, I struggled to reply. At this point, I really experienced discomfort. I wanted out. 'Quinton?' 'SCREW THIS!' I screamed like a girl. I had enough. I couldn't take it, so I got up and stomped out of the office. I was halfway down the hall that led to the garage, before I froze. What had I done? Why had I done it? I turned around after a moment's thought and peered inside the office, as I had left the door slightly opened. I could see that Natalie was somewhat displeased. But not at me. At herself. 'I-I'm sorry..' I commented as I stepped back into the office, catching Natalie; who had just buried her face in her hands; by surprise. She smiled meekly and gave me those comfort eyes. 'I guess I'm still uncomfortable with the whole idea of talking to someone other than Bruno.. but I certainly believe all I need is some time. Maybe I come back next session and we try again?' I murmured, suddenly ashamed of how I acted moments earlier. She stood up and closed the folder. 'I'd recommend that, yes.' And her smile was the seal of approval I needed to tell myself it was all gonna be okay.
