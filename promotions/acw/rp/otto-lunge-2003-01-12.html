Otto Lunge Written: January 12, 2003 Otto Lunge is a 53 year old, overweight, moron. He's not retarded. He's not slow. He has the ability to learn, he's just incredibly dense. Sure, you're inclined to feel sorry for him.. But don't. He's not worth the time. He's a freeloader who never tried to do anything on his own. For the last few years he's clung to Geo Vacton, milking him for everything he can. He's cost Vacton thousands of dollars in expenses from food, necessities, accessarys and bills from his rampages in food markets and especially bakery's. Geo Vacton thought he left him behind in 21w, but he was wrong. Otto is back, and in some ways he's worse than before. Geo isn't cruel. He wont let him live on the streets, so he has no choice but to provide for him once more, and take him in. It's hell all over again. Otto Lunge, a burden or a blessing in disguise? No.. Definitely a burden.
