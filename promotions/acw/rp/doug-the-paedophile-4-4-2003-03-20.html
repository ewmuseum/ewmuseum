Doug the paedophile [4/4] Written: 20th march And I said what about Breakfast at tiffanies She said I, Think I, Remember the film I, Yes I recall I think… -Breakfast at tiffanies --- 7:29 am, Scott Perry took his seat at the round dark wood breakfast table, a place mat sat on the table in front of him with a bowl of cereal on top. “Help yourself to milk Scott” said Gail in a cheery voice. Scott has never been, nor never will be a morning person. Mornings make him ill. , and Gail’s voice sliced through his mind like a freshly sharpened knife. “Thanks.” He groaned, attempting, yet failing, to sound friendly. The door opened and closed, and as Scott looked up he saw a bright, cheerful husband, Doug. “Good morning Scott, feeling better!” Doug yelled. “Yeah.” Scott grunted back quietly, looking down at his cereal whilst grabbing the milk and soaking the cereal with it. “You don’t look to good…” Doug said as he sat down at his dining room table opposite Scott. “You okay buddy?” he continued. Scott dropped his spoon. “I have to leave.” He got to his feet, making a scene in the centre of the room and leaving, slamming the door behind him. As Scott packed his bag Doug and his wife talked through the problems Scott has faced and what may be wrong at this time with his mental state, Doug took advantage of the situation attempting to make Scott seem crazy. Scott entered the room, still tired but he was fully dressed now and his bag slung over his shoulder. Doug raced to his feet; Scott pushed him out of the way. Doug almost sat on the table as Scott opened the back door and left. Doug left his home and followed Scott to the bottom of the driveway. Scott stopped. “Scott, please!” He pivoted to face Doug. “Scott, I know you saw me, but… But it’s just the way I am.” ”Doug, I’m leaving. If you follow me, I will report you, if you leave me be, it can be our little secret. Understand?” An ultimatum if Doug had ever heard one. Doug moved only back towards his house. Scott…? Well Scott walked out of their lives forever.
