Brandon Thomas Title: Seeking the Truth: Part Two Date Made: March 15, 2004 Location: 'Detective' Kraig Carter's Office To be married and having a child, comes responsibilities. These responsibilities would include, providing and caring for the family, spending quality time with the family, and most importantly, not cheating on your wife. For Brandon Thomas, it would be hard to accept responsibilities such as these. In the past, Brandon had been known as ‘The Ladies Man,’ before marriage corrupted his life. His wife, Elicia Thomas knew of this, she’d caught him plenty of times during his man whoring days, but she loved him and always gave him second chances. Though, she swore to it that this time, would be the last, and Brandon promised her it would never happen again. But she grew weary of his words, she HAD to know for sure, she HAD to know if her husband was cheating on her with any fans, staff personal, or some of those beautiful managers over at the ACW. Her heart wouldn’t be satisfied until she found out the truth. “Yes, I need to speak with Detective Kraig Carter please.” And there was only one way to seek out this truth; there was only one man good enough for this type of job, Detective Kraig Carter. Elicia stood at a reception desk in an unfamiliar location. She wore a pair of big, oval black sunglasses with a long, grayish brown overcoat that stood an inch or two above the ground. Her shoulders were tucked in, with her hands inserted into the pockets. The receptionist looked at Elicia oddly as if she was some type of weirdo, “And you are?” “The woman who called earlier looking to speak with Mr. Carter and you said just come on by.” “Oh yes, just go through that door,” she pointed to the door that stood beside the desk, “And go straight down the hall, it’s the last door on the right, you can’t miss it.” Elicia nodded, “Thank you.” As she proceeded through the door and into the hall, thoughts of why she was doing this would cross through her mind. But she kept telling herself it was for the ‘truth.’ Was her husband’s word not good enough? She guessed not and that’s why she kept proceeding down the hallway. She came upon the last door on the right and a name written in black was on a gold plate. ‘Detective Kraig Carter’ it said. She knocked. “Come in.” spewed a deep voice from inside the room. Elicia turned the rusty doorknob and slightly gave it a little shove. She peaked her head in first and seen a very husky man sitting behind an oak wooden desk. After peaking her head in, she stepped one foot in and slowly entered the room. “Mr. Carter?” she asked, not realizing the man she searched for was an African-American. “I am he and you must be Mrs. Thomas?” Elicia nodded, “Yes.” she walked over and sat down in a red-seated chair, “And you know why I’m here?” “I was told because you think your husband is cheating on you?” She took off the oval sunglasses and ran her hand through her thick black, head of hair, “Yes.” “Well I’ve never did any cases like this Mrs. Thomas, I’m sorry but-” Elicia immediately cut in, “He is a professional wrestler.” Detective Carter’s face lit up. Elicia had been doing a little research of her own, she had known about Carter’s wrestling career in the OSW and Carter was quiet impressed. “Whom is he working for?” “The ACW, or so he says. Since his suspension in the LoC he’s not been in the greatest of moods, but now that ACW gave him a job, he’s lightened up, but I’m afraid that he might be having another affair again or if he isn’t now, he will be sometime in the future. I just need you to spy on him, go undercover or something.” The big, husky black fellow leaned forward on his desk with a slight image of concern dashing across his face. He placed his elbows on the table and linked his hands together, “Ok Mrs. Thomas, I’ll see what I can do. I have some friends who’d be willing to give me access into the ACW.” “Do you think my husband will recognize you?” Carter smiled, “No, not at all.” “Good, because I don’t want him knowing anything about this. Not a word.” “As long as the price is right miss, no one will find out.” “I have plenty of money, you just do the job right, and you’ll get your money.” “Ok then, I’ll start tomorrow. I’ll get in contact with my friend, get me a job on the ACW staff as some type of custodian, and do some research on your husband for a few months. After work, I’ll follow him, and see if he is messing around on you. We’ll meet back here in one month, by then I should have at least some details to give you.” Elicia tossed on her sunglasses and stood up, “I’ll be waiting then.” she said, turning around and quickly exiting through the door. Detective Carter picked up the phone and dialed a number, “Hey, do you remember that favor you owed me?” “…” “Well, I need it now.”
