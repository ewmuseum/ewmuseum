Rome the Vile Title: Breaking the Habit #2 Date Made: April 11th, 2004 Location: Ft. Launderdale, Florida It was a swift kick to the balls of Rome's ego when he realized that all those warnings about his extreme style of fighting shortening his career were legit. When he was 25, it didn't really occur to him that top rope leg drops, and moonsaults to the arena floor every night have severe reprocussions on your body. As he looked back at his former life, it became very apparent to him that the cocaine and alcohol was killing the pain his peers had warned him about. Well, the cocaine anyway. He still loved to drink, but when he would come home after hours of training now, no beer could make him forget about the punishment he just took. But the end result was worth it. Joshua had made up his mind, and a bad back wasn't going to stop him from returning to the mountain top. He would once again grace the main event of a prominent national promotion, and the Sunshine State Wrestling School was just his starting block. He had to swallow his pride, and put away the ego he was once notorious for, but he did it. 4 nights a week he walked through the doors of that dusty old wherehouse, and re-learned the basics alongside teenagers who once looked up to him. This gave him the opportunity to reinvent himself. He cast aside his tired old movelist, and began developing a style that was more to his liking. Vicious striking blows, vile head drops. This man had anger within. With the influx of the Japanese strong style of wrestling in the states now, Joshua saw his time to strike. He got word that the once successful All-Star Championship Wrestling was opening up their doors once again, and was looking for talents of all shapes and sizes. There would be some returning superstars, but ACW brass wanted new blood, especially since they got word that a number of their main eventers would not be returning. Joshua saw opportunity and fortune in ACW, and began assembling a tape. He included matches and accomplishments of old, as well as a few matches he had worked recently. Once a finished product was completed, he sent the package off to ACW Headquarters. It was just a matter of time before his phone was ringing. It took 2 weeks, but the phone call finally came. An ACW talent scout invited Joshua Powell to attend their April 15th show in Cleveland. He didn't guarantee Joshua anything, and made clear to inform him that this was just a chance for Joshua to mingle with the staff, and see how well the two meshed. Joshua had heard this before, and it had always gone well. He knew that this ocassion would be no different, but remained humble on the phone. As the two hung up their respective phones, Joshua sat back in his rugged recliner. In the back of his mind, flashbacks of his famed life ran through his head. He could feel the shiver down his spine, and the hairs on the back of his neck. A slight grin lined his face, as he spoke quietly to himself. 'I will make them love me.'
