Souls of the Damned pt. 1 Written: dunno Greed.... The surroundings were white, clear and accurate, it was a park surrounded by many trees and a foresty area. A little boy played with his father as the wrestled in the deep snow, they kept at eachother and I just sat there with complete greed within my hunger and thoughts. I never had this, my father died when I was 7, and I've never heard from him since. I hate my father, he played with my emotions and let me go like yesturday's garbage. I felt like I am nothing and nobody would care wether I am alive or not, however, nobody does, but that's aside from the matter. I never had him, he manipulated me and threw me away, he broke my heart into a million pieces and I never got it back. Nothing can bring him back, I would hate to see the day because nothing would be stopping me from getting to him and putting him down forever. His voice still kicks in my head and my dreams and when I think about it, I know nothing about him. I lost him at such a young age and nothing could hurt more. My little heart was torn to shreds and nothing could fix it. I watched them play and I thought about everything that was not right, everything that was un-holy and I never really understood why god played with me, I never understood why it had to be me, I forever summon god for that, he is a soul of the damned and I hate him forever, nothing he does can change my life, I am not going to waste my time praying to him. The boy got up from his father, he was laughing hard, and he began to run towards the deep, mystical part, my surroundings. The boy ran right into me, right into the forest and his father lost sight, he heard a sharp scream, he ran towards us but could not find him fast enough. He finally found his son, aggressivly bleeding from his neck, and I had given out everything I wanted. I did not want this boy alive, I had sadistic, unthinkable thoughts running through my head. There stood a man infront of the father, a very tall man with a scar on his left cheek, who stood with blood surpassing his mouth. The father fell into a heart attack, and I... ooops, he just walked away....
