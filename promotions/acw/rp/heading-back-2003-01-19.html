Heading Back

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#142) by Jay Smash, dated 19 January 2003, from the fed's first site, acw.ewmania.com (its acw2 edition, 2002–04) as captured in the Wayback on 23 July 2003. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Heading Back Written: 1/19/03 Back on the road, Jay Smash still looking tired from an early drive back, and Ralph, now wearing a pair of jeans and a sweatshirt, instead of his ski suit, begin to talk as they head off from the ski resort and make their way to the arena for the Courage event. 'Hey look what I found at the resort.' 'Put it in.' 'Well ok then' Ralph opens up the case to a cd, which he slides into the CD player. He then pushes play and turns up the volume. The music begins to play: 'Is this the real life? Is this just fantasy? Caught in a landslide No escape from reality Open your eyes Look up to the skies and see I'm just a poor boy, I need no sympathy Because I'm easy come, easy go A little high, little low Anyway the wind blows, doesn't really matter to me, to me Mama, just killed a man Put a gun against his head Pulled my trigger, now he's dead Mama, life had just begun But now I've gone and thrown it all away Mama, ooo-ooo-ooo Didn't mean to make you cry If I'm not back again this time tomorrow Carry on, carry on, as if nothing really matters Too late, my time has come Sends shivers down my spine Body's aching all the time Goodbye everybody - I've got to go Gotta leave you all behind and face the truth Mama, ooo-ooo-ooo -(anyway the wind blows) I don't want to die I sometimes wish I'd never been born at all...' Jay Smash and Ralph are both seen moving there heads and singing to the music...then they stop and their heads slowly nod up and down to the beat. As the music goes on, they split the lyrics and do a Wayne’s World impression, if you have ever seen that movie.... 'I see a little silhouetto of a man Scaramouch, scaramouch will you do the fandango Thunderbolt and lightning - very very frightening me Gallileo, Gallileo, Gallileo, Gallileo, Gallileo Figaro - magnifico But I'm just a poor boy and nobody loves me He's just a poor boy from a poor family Spare him his life from this monstrosity Easy come easy go - will you let me go Bismillah! No - we will not let you go - let him go Bismillah! We will not let you go - let him go Bismillah! We will not let you go - let me go Will not let you go - let me go (never) Never let you go - let me go Never let me go - ooo No, no, no, no, no, no, no - Oh mama mia, mama mia, mama mia let me go Beelzebub has a devil put aside for me for me for me!!!!!' Jay Smash and Ralph shoot their heads back and sends them forward, as they start rockin' to the music. 'So you think you can stone me and spit in my eye So you think you can love me and leave me to die Oh baby - can't do this to me baby Just gotta get out - just gotta get right outta here After the music finally slows back down again, Jay Smash and Ralph have the same impression as they did from the beginning of the song. 'Ooh yeah, ooh yeah Nothing really matters Anyone can see Nothing really matters - nothing really matters to me Anyway the wind blows...' Ralph lowers the volume and both men smile and laugh after the song is over. Jay Smash continues to drive as they continue to talk. 'Great song.' 'Well of course, one of the best dude. I can definitely see that you are ready to enter that ring tonight.' 'Oh you can finally tell? Yeah I feel like myself again. No more doctors, forget it. If anyone is getting injured tonight, it's gonna be Geo Vacton, because I'm finished with the injuries.' 'Oh man, I need to take a crap.' 'We need to get to the arena first, we will go when we get there.' 'I don't know man, I'm not feeling to good now. I think there was something wrong with that chicken this morning.' 'What, the chicken we had last night?' 'Yeah, I had some this morning.' 'You idiot! You shouldn't eat chicken if it's been out on a table all night, anything can happen to it.' 'Yeah, well we need to go somewhere because I'm not feeling good at all.' 'You're gonna have to wait, I don't see anywhere we can stop.' Jay Smash and Ralph are still on their way to the arena while Ralph tries to hold his bladder for another hour...
