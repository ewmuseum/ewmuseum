Neo Xealot Akuma, Djinn, Akira Neophyte part 1 - What's in a name? Location: Los Angeles, CA Date: September 19, 2004 Number (Archive): 15 Number (Wrestler): 1 Kinjite (Kin-Jeet-Eh) sat next to Kazuya on the bed, helping him pack his gear. While folding the trademark hooded jacket which he donned in Flatlined and more recently the Delta, she couldn’t help but notice the ‘Neo Xealot’ insignia upon the neck. “Neo Xealot? You still call yourself that?” Kazuya smiled and lightly caressed his younger sister’s cheek. He then converted his caress to a squeeze and proceeded to ruffle her hair a bit. “The name was given to me.” He took the jacket and traced his fingers along the insignia’s letters, “I didn’t just make it up because it sounded cool or whatever.” She punched him playfully in the chest. “You know, you’ve never told me that story.” “I think you’re a little old for that.” She was 16 years old, 6 years his junior. “Tell me you asshole!” Kinjite tried to tickle him under his arms. Uncharacteristically ticklish, he eventually gave in. “Okay!” He wheezed, “I’ll tell you. Jesus Kin.” “Cool!” She leaped into a cross legged position and gazed at her big brother in genuine wonder. Kazuya was well taken by her fullness of life and energy. “Now speak young grasshopper!” He laughed, “Whatever Kin.” He cleared his throat and unsuccessfully pulled his long fringe out of his face. “You know,” Kinjite mused. “You should tie that up.” “What? First you hate my name and now you don’t like my hair?” He made a puppy-like frown. “Is there anything about me that you like?” “I love you Kaz! I just think that you try to rip off too many videogames.” “I honestly have no idea what you’re talking about.” He dropped the jacket to the bed, “Anyway, you want to hear the story or what?” “Why would you say something like that?” Her tone was jokingly pathetic. “Well it seems to me that you’d rather make snide remarks.” “Nevermind that!” She tickled him again, “Now begin!” “Okay! Okay! Fuck!” He paused for a moment. “The name derived from name given to me by your grandfather, the year you were born. He called me ‘Young Neophyte’ which means…” “…beginner.” She interrupted. ”Yes. But it also means apprentice which is what I was at that time.” “And ‘Xealot?’” “I’m getting to that! Now gimme some room!” Kaz pushed her away, “To be a zealot means…” “…to be a fanatic, a devotee, a believer, a...” She interrupted again. “Shut up!” He said with another laugh “He gave me the name zealot because I truly believed in the power of our family martial art. “Okay!” She giggled cutely, “It’s corny if you ask me!” “Well grandpa wasn’t a funny man,” He digressed, “So he essentially called me young neophyte and young zealot for most of my stay. Present day: I combined the words, shortened the Neophyte, added the X for visual effect and voila!” “Yeah…” Kinite paused, “But it still sucks and your real name is better.” “It may ‘sucks’ little sister. But it’s mine and I hate it!” She giggled again, “I love you bro!” “I love you too little sis’.”
