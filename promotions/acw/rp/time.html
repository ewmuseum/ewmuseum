LLB The Law Time. Location: A locker room location. Date: February 17, 2005 Number (Archive): 26 Number (Wrestler): 1 It had been a while before he laced up his boots. The PIW journey was another short stint that ended quite suddenly. For almost a year now LLB sat on the backbenches... watching his youth slowly slip away from him. He was now twenty-seven... another one of the best years of his life... lost in the realm of time. And soon time would catch up to the once known lawyer. Soon time would damage his skills... and LLB would fall out of the category that he was once on top of. There was a time... there were many days... in which ‘The Law’ was on top of the world. He reigned supreme in both PIW and Action Wrestling for years on end. The flag at the top of the mountain never looked like it was going to be replaced. It just drifted in the air... showing off its brilliance to all whom watched from below. One can get lost in that realm of glory. And LLB did. Through fame and good fortune, ‘The Law’ took what he had for granted. He assumed his reign among the league’s best would seemingly last forever. That, in all walks of life, is never the case. And where did LLB stand now? Because of complications... some being his fault, while others did not fall directly upon his shoulders, ‘The Law’ stood on his knees... at the bottom of that mountain. The mountain, which once saw his name and his flag... fly solo on top of a climb only few men could say they had reached. He had never thought his legacy could bring him back down. Not like this. But as ‘The Law’ struggled to pull on his second wrestling boot... he realized his hands trembled. Maybe it was the steroids he had taken in the past. Maybe it was the wreck his body had gone through before. Or maybe it was his age catching up with him. He had health concerns now. Throughout life he had learned that not all of them came when you were older. Three years ago he had suffered a stroke. A stroke which kept him from competing in Action Wrestling for several months. He was suffering from Crohn’s Disease, a digestive disorder diagnosed to him two months ago. He had ongoing muscle strains from past matches, replicating in his back every morning he woke up. He suffered from bilateral clubfeet. The bones in his feet were now much weaker... through all the wear and tear they took on improper surfaces such as Action Wrestling’s ring (Reed Young was cheap, he didn’t pay for the best padded mats) and Asylum’s cage structure (Joe Campbell was crazy, but you knew that). However LLB suffered from something much stronger as well. The battle against time. Soon there would be that time when LLB couldn’t wrestle anymore. Soon the closing of federations would not be the only thing truly holding him back. Soon it would be his health. LLB’s name would lose its meaning after it was all said and done. He had made a mark so the legacy of the “lawyer” would not be forgotten... but in time, it would come, when the word LLB didn’t mean that much anymore. If it didn’t already. ‘The Law’ finished lacing his boot, as he stood and grabbed the towel behind him. In time he would fade into nothing. But as LLB looked at the ACW logo on the towel... his time was not over just yet.
