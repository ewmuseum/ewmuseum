Memories, the First Part Of Written: 28th October two-thousand-and-two 'Aaron, you have to do it sometime, and now might as well be the best time to do it...' She was always hassling me...but hey, she is my children's mother, and I love her more than anything on this planet. I met her back in my hey day, if I ever had a hey day that is, let me just say she was an avid fan of the WWF when I was the curtain jerker back in the late 80s. I opened up a house show with KoKo B Ware, and she caught my eye. She was attractive back then, and she has got even better looking in my opinion as the years have taken a toll on her, but in a nice way. What was she talking about? Well Bill Goldberg has done it... Kurt Angle has done it... Dwayne Johnson has done it... Got It Yet? ... Mick Foley? That's right; she wants me to write a book. Not an actual book, but a series of diaries that I could give to my children, to give to their children and their children's children, you get the picture. I had been pondering on it for a while now, but to be honest, I didn't think anyone would really be interested in my story, but since you are reading this now, I guess there will be some interest. The problem was...when I took the biro in my hand, and rested it against the white pad, the word 'blank' came to mind, as that was what my mind began when I tried to start. Where to begin? I guess I will start it soon, who knows really...I have so much to write about that I don't know what to write...does that make sense? 'You'll feel better for it' she would say every time I picked up that pen...too bad I could never feel the urge to do it...one day...one day...
