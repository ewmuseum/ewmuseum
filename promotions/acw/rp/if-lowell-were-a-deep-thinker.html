If Lowell Were A Deep Thinker.

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster If Lowell Were A Deep Thinker. Location: In my deviant mind. Date: January 31, 2005 Number (Archive): 24 Number (Wrestler): 1 So yeah, Lowell’s a deep thinker now. :-) He watches Indie films and then sits down with a group of his closest friends and discusses what the writer/director was trying to ‘say’ through his work. He dresses like a Scene kid – ie. tight black jeans, a thrift store T-shirt, and a pair of dope Converse’s. He laughs, but not often, and when he does, it’s usually in response to some thought-provoking Haiku sent to him via. e-mail. He secretly enjoys Emo, but will trash the genre publically, just ‘cause. And he’ll write music with emotional subtext, but it’s not Emo – oh no, it’s “Lyrical Poetry”. Sometimes he cries when he thinks of all the starving children in the world. There’s so many. Why, God, must you overpopulate planet Earth! Can’t you just zap some of those horny Asian chicks’ wombs so that our population can thin out a bit? But wait – an apiphany! Lowell’s come to the conclusion that perhaps there is no God! Thus, he’ll rebel and form his own ideas and beliefs! He’ll become a vegan, an athiest, a hypocrit! Protests will follow! Sit-ins, sit-outs! Eat-ins, eat-outs! They’ll travel to Rome and slap the Pope with the Glove of Self-Righteousness. But it won’t stop there! You see, after organized religion has been snuffed out, Lowell and his dynamic group of Metrosexual followers will engage the White House and the Secret Service, forcing them to listen to their lame U2 records until they surrender. The ‘hippes-that-don’t-want-to-be-called-hippes-but-totally-are’ will legalize marijuana, and for a short while, everything’ll be cool. That is until they realize that smoking a J in front of a bunch of cops is no longer considered rebellious. Thus, marijuana is ousted from its spot as the ‘Nah, I’m not a stoner, I just like to mellow out sometimes’ drug, and is replaced by coke, acid, PCP, LSD, and heroin. Then the needles come out and everyone’s getting high all the FUCKING time! Nothing gets done! Everyone’s at home watching day-time TV, stoned! The economy flat lines. No one can afford anything. Christmas gets cancelled, and children everywhere are pissed and get sent to bed with a black eye and bloody nose for mouthing off to Daddy while he’s shooting up. “Fucking little shit, this’ll teach ya’! COME ‘ERE, JOHNNY! I SAID COME HERE! OH, YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD! YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD, YOU HEAR ME! WHERE’D MY ELASTIC BAND GO!? IF I FIND IT HIDDEN IN YOUR ROOM I’M GONNA’ MAKE YOU DISAPPEAR, JOHNNY! I’M GONNA’- I’M GONNA’ TEAR YOUR HEAD OFF AND I’M GONNA’ TEAR YOUR FUCKING ARMS OFF! AND WE’LL JUST SEE IF YOU CAN POINT THE BRUISES OUT TO CHILD SERVICES WITH NO FUCKING ARMS, JOHNNY! HOH HOH, WE’LL SEE!” This is about the time when they all realise that they are no longer anarchists. They have established their own form of government. They are they authority now. And with so many people getting high there is a shortage on coke and heroin and other formerly illegal substances, and people start to go through withdrawals. Everyone’s sweaty and paranoid and bedridden. Can’t sleep. Can’t eat. There is a revolt against the revolt! The death toll, MASSIVE. Lowell is the only human being left on the planet. His last words before hanging himself from the flag pole in Lowell Dot Com Square (in Washington, DC): “Guess I screwed up. Meh.” CRINNNNNNK. Rope tightens around his neck. He chokes, twitches, shits his pants, and dies. … Okay, scratch that! Lowell’s no longer a deep thinker. He’s still the same superficial, self-absorbed, egomaniac he’s always been! See what could have happen if Lowell were to focus his abilities on something other than shilling and acting like a jackass? I know, it scared me to have to write it.
