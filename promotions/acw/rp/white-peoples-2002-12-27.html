White Peoples. Written: 12/27/02 As Al set his bags down on the ACW gymnasium floors, he found two big men wrestling in the ring together, along with many trainers teaching their wrestlers the proper way to punch, kick, and things of the sort. Al smirked. He motioned to go into the locker room to get his gear on, but thought to himself, and walked straight into the ring. Noticing the two were names he hadn’t heard of in some time, he wondered to these two men were. Prospects. Al walked in the center of the ring, oddly enough right in the middle of their match. “You two wouldn’t mind if we make this a triple threat, would you?” He echoed slyly. The six four, three-hundred pounder on the left looked at Allen’s size, and without hesitation replied, “No problem, I need the spots.” Allen pulled off a devilish smirk off the remark. Still wearing his sweat pants and tank-top, Al looked at the man on the right, who was nodding in agreement with the other man’s answer, to Allen’s nonchalant looking challenge. Allen grunted to himself, “White boys”. He took to his corner, and waited for one of the oversized wrestling trainers to ring the bell. They did so, and off to the races Allen took, running straight to the man who so confidently answered his challenge. Before the man could even react, he was laid out with a double-arm DDT. The wrestler on the other end came charging, and like a sixth sense, Allen knew it was coming. Being in the corner, he leapt off the second turnbuckle, flipping onto his opponents body, shifting his weight so that Al’s feet were on the ground now. Allen waited, before dropping the man on his head, with a piledriver. The other man got up. Al watched him, backing up into the opposite corner. As the man backed into his corner, they both rushed at each other in a screaming nature. The wrestler now next to Al as he came running towards the bigger man held Allens leg, stopping him from running. Allen, foolishly taking his attention off the charging opposite, begins stomping on him. Bad Idea. The rushing barbarian clothesline’s Allen, knocking Allen from his feet, and from his senses. Allen’s pride was hit, but not hard. A few seconds past, and Allen slowly regain the use of his feet. He hopped up, not even being noticed by the amatuers as they were off fighting each other on the floor, Al took to the top rope, almost perfectly diagonal to where they stood. He leapt off of the top rope, his time in the air almost like poetry in motion. In fact, time slowed down, just to see the grace, and elegance of Allen while he was in the air. He prepared for the landing, as time sped right back up, hitting both men and laying them both out. One of the wrestlers trainers spoke out, not being able to contain himself. 'Beat his ass Joey!' Allen, not wanting to be picky, quickly picked one man up, and brought him back into the ring. Not waiting one second so that the man could gain a breath of fresh air and have a chance of being on the offense, Allen brought the man right back down with Tha Electrifyer, used by Diamond Dallas Page only with another name for the move. The amateur was down, and out. Al, not thinking it would be enough starts for the top rope, maybe to finish it off with some Pimp Juice, or the 420 Splash. Instead, he shrugs it off, pinning the amateur wrestler in the center of the ring. A trainer of one of the amateurs goes into the ring, counting the: 1…………………..2……………………3. Allen raises up, smirking, and leaving the ring, picking up his bag. He felt he had got the exercise, or rather the “touch-up” he needed to stay sharp. But an unexpected hand graced his shoulder before he could do so. “Hey man…” Allen did not stop. “HEY!” Allen turned around, staring down the small, pudgy white man who was looking at Allen in a puzzled way. The same pudgy white man that made the pin-count for Al to claim his victory. “Hey, HEY YOU’RE AL WILLIS FROM THE eWo! I knew I saw that flying somewhere else! What…are you signed with the ACW? I thought the Asylum got you!” “…..”Allen thinks about even responding, about walking out in a fashionably bad-ass way, but when he thought about his manners, when he thought about all the people who had broke his heart and done him wrong, he thought twice. “Nah, they didn’t get me. The offer was better over here in tha ACW. And call me A*Dubbs playa, you don’t know me.” The trainer puts out his right hand. “Let me train you.” Allen looks at him in a puzzled way, “Umm…why would I need your training?” “Because I need cash..” Allen turns and begins to walk out the door. White people.
