Through The Looking Glass Mirror

Andy Sharp The Canadian Cavalier Through The Looking Glass Mirror Location: The inner psyche Date: December 09, 2004 Number (Archive): 23 Number (Wrestler): 1 Well, the paperwork had been all but finished yesterday. As of yesterday evening, 6:32 P.M. in a meeting with All-Star Championship Wrestling officials, I got inked to my first-ever major professional wrestling deal. Granted, ACW didn’t have the broad scope of your fWos and WWEs, but it was the perfect place to start. I had slept well that night, dreaming of what was to be in a place like ACW. I’m only 21 years of age and I have my whole life ahead of me. I’m living the very dream I sought out my whole life. I have the chance to place the name of Andy Sharp in the annals of time. I may never reach the heights that the likes of Andre the Giant, Hulk Hogan, Randy Savage, Stone Cold and The Rock touched. But ACW? It was a nice start. Waking up from my soft, warm bed was an exhilarating feeling. The smile on my face still hadn’t left and that feeling of euphoria flowing through my veins still present. I sat up, looking down at my tattered blue jeans. Fuck, I need better taste in pajamas. Maybe I could buy some of those big pajamas at the big and tall store. Us 6’6” bastards need clothing, too. Shrug. Wait, did I just think the word, “shrug?” What the fuck am I, in an AIM conversation now? There I go, trailing off again. Well, anyway, I sat up from the bed and made my way over to the closet mirror, making sure to comb my hair. I’d only have one week to spend with Uncle Rich as long as I was here in Pikeville. Need to have some fun with him before I headed out on the road, so I think me and Rich are going to go see the Incredibles. Yeah, nothing like bumbling superheroes to add more liveliness to a kick-ass day. I set the comb down on the dresser to look at the body in front. Congrats, Andy. You deserved it. All those extra hours spent with Eli Brown at his school paid off. Sure, you’ve been training and spending just about every off hour you have, learning the business from a WBG Hall of Famer, but is all that training going to pay off when the time comes for those lights to shine down on me? I mean, those fans out there. Those venues that have seen the best compete in front of them. Are you really ready for all that, Andy? Are you absolutely, positively ready for what lies ahead? Maybe. Maybe not. I sure as hell knew that I wasn’t going to let all the hard work I’ve put into this mind, body, and soul go to waste inside the cubicle of a 9-to-5 job. Not many have the chance to go where I’m about to go, but I’m going. And nothing’s going to stop me. Nothing at all. OW! …Well, maybe I’ll actually learn to NOT throw my shoes in the middle of the floor where I can trip on them. Hmm? Is that a bag of Barbeque Ruffles underneath the bed? Score! EW! There’s MOLD in here! GAG! Jeez. Times like this I hate being a Mysophobe. And note to self: Firebomb this place. Firebomb it for the good of mankind. If I die in the ring, I don’t want a moldy bag and an unkempt room being a testament to what Andy Sharp once was. I’m getting out of here before these damn germs regroup. Filthy bastards. I threw on a white t-shirt from the closet and headed down the stairs, looking towards tomorrow and praying for success in the days to come. … … … …Man, that was gay. Hey, visit my psyche another time when I’m not overwhelmed with stupid, uber-articulated thoughts, ‘Kay?
