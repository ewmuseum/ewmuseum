Getting into the gym... Written: 12/27/02 A young, determined Al Willis walked into the building, holding a bag with “eWo” on it, along with freshly braided cornrolls, a no-sleeved black shirt, and sweat pants. His all white Air Max Plus’ were almost perfectly new, except for a small mark left from Allen’s pit-bull called “Faith”. Allen stops, to admire the signs, the “End Game” poster-board still hung up on the walls following a hellacious pay-per-view. The gym Al was walking into was not a regular one, it was one that was hosted by the ACW. A dynasty made years before Al even stepped into the scene. He sighed, and started his pace. He walked up to the secretary in her desk in front of the entrance to the gym. “…” He is slow to make a speech, being taken away by what would soon be his new work-out spot, the place where he would train to eventually become as skilled, as acknowledged as the ACW World Heavyweight Champion. He remember what he had to do. “Hi. I’m A*Dubbs.” Not even knowing what he’s doing (From no experience with professional wrestling gyms), but still wanting to be polite and not show his Brooklyn accent, he continues, “I’ve come to work out…” The secretary replies with not the utmost kindness, but Al did not mind. “Oh, that way, and for further reference Mr. Willis, you can walk right in without speaking to me.” Allen thinks about responding. “What tha fuck did you say hoe? You wanna sit there and speak to me like I’m some lil chump? BITCH WE COULD SCRAP RIGHT HERRE! WHATCHU WANT?” It stayed in his head. He politely resolved a statement and spoke, “Uh, thanks.” Al walked off, past the secretary, walking into the ACW Gymnasium for a routine work-out.
