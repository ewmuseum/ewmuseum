The Wright Man - 7

5 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 May 2011 Henry Irwonsen had played a huge role in recruiting Hank Wright to ACW. Both parties were keen but they hadn’t sealed the deal just yet. Henry had one final thing to do – meet the man himself first. Hank was asked to fly out to Italy first in order to get a production video out in time for End Game 2011, one of the promotion’s most famous shows held in Italy’s national arena, Stadio Olimpico, home to AS Roma and SS Lazio. Wright was asked to meet Henry at Roma Termini, the capital’s bustling and principal metro station, at the Via Marsala exit. As Hank stood there with his hands in his jacket pockets, he almost felt at home. A sea of humanity swarmed through the Eternal City, commuters flooding the area, they were everywhere, left, right and centre. Taxi drivers hounded The Tank asking him if he needed to go anywhere, a certain hotel, but he repeatedly rejected their requests, hoping Henry had that sorted and that he’d bloody hurry up! A few minutes later, Henry approached Hank, smiling as he extended his hand: “Hank, I’m sorry I’m late.” Wright’s concerns washed away straightaway. Now, it was down to business: “Nice to see you Henry. Don’t worry about it. You’re here now.” “Need to eat?” “I do.” Henry gestured to his right with his thumb: “I take it you don’t want to go there?” (He was pointing at McDonalds, which is situated directly outside of Roma Termini and in Via Marsala itself. It was also one of the reasons for their meeting place. McDonalds is distinctive!) Hank laughed but Henry cut him off: “Only kidding. They’d have my ass if I took new talent there. Ever heard of Piazza Navona?” The Tank shook his head: “No, sir.” Irwonsen knew what he was doing. It’s surprising how Piazza Navona goes ignored, given that it’s a beautiful square in its own right and a fantastic place to eat, though not if you’ve only got a few euros in your pocket, and in the evening buskers and street entertainers give it a lovely vibe, one that you’re lucky to be sitting in such a romantic spot even if you are paying through the nose to do so. This was daytime, however, and Henry and Hank weren’t gooey-eyed lovers. Plus, it was December. They were both about business. “Hank, we’ve come up with an angle. We want you to work with The Reids.” Wright’s guard came down. There was a smile on his face: “Are you being serious?” Henry was unflinching: “Yes. Booster, Jimmy’s son, is a talented boy and there’s history there.” Wright nodded: “Yeah, there sure is. It’s just that this angle should’ve happened earlier, shouldn’t it?” Henry was in agreement: “But there’s nothing we can do about that now. We can still do something with it and it makes perfect sense. We think it’ll be a great way, the perfect way, to bring you in and Booster will benefit by having an angle with someone of your experience. It gets Jimmy involved in a storyline too, something for him to get his teeth into…” “How’s he doin’?” “He’s great. He’s getting a kick out of seeing Booster in the ring and this stuff with The Cabal. He’s not ready to leave wrestling just yet.” “That’s good to hear. Is he down for this?” “He loves it. He thinks it’s perfect and he’s actually said the same as you. It should’ve happened earlier.” Hank grinned: “That’s nice to hear. So…” Henry imitated Wright: “So, you’d work with them for approximately ten shows. We can take you in another direction after that, whether it be in singles or tags. Would you be okay with tags?” “I’d be receptive to anything these days. Tags are fine too. You mentioned the Scorpion Fighting division too?” “I did. It’s a great place for you to show what you can do. Your style is well-suited to that. I’ve been watching some of the brawls you had in Mexico and your earlier stuff in Texas, man, you were wild! This division, if you can still do it and you want to do it, is custom-made for your talents. We’ve got some exciting names in there too so it’s not a step down for you at all, Hank. Look at it as a place to start. You’d have a lot of fun in there and your opponents would have a lot of fun with you too.” Before responding, Hank took a sip from his glass of water and put his hand across the table: “When do you want me to start?” Irwonsen was slightly surprised but Hank repeated the gesture and nodded: “I’m in. Tell me where and when you want me to begin. I’m in.” The pair shook hands and Henry chanced his arm: “Tomorrow?” “Tomorrow, it is. Where am I staying?” “Don’t worry about that. We’ll sort something out for you. Meet me outside of Termini again tomorrow at the same time and I’ll show you something we’ve already got in the works for you.” “Pleasure Henry. Can’t wait.” Not that the pair were done there… They still had dessert to polish off in the enchanting Piazza Navona. The eating wasn’t quite finished but Hank’s ACW adventure was about to begin… All over again.
