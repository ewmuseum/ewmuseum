The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Flashback: Written: “Get him!!!!” The eerie call of a tall muscular child can be heard as a heard of children run towards another child like a pack of raging wolves. Crowding around one child the group stare on as the child cornered by the other children cowers into the school railings and begins to carelessly flop onto the black tarmac school playground. “ I’ve done nothing wrong! Why me? Leave me alone! Please!” The grounded child begins to blubber these words out as the rest of the children look on fruitfully. One child with a callous look spread across his gaping spot covered face begins to approach the child on the floor. “ Your fat and ugly that’s why!” The words echo around the playground as more children begin to surround the group. As commotion around the child is loud, teachers scurry towards the scene as the other children disperse. A tall blonde teacher approaches the large child now sat up on the floor crying softly and wiping his snot infested nose onto his sleeve. “ What’s wrong dear?” The women has intense interest in the problem which seems to have arisen. She tilts her head with a sense of acknowledgement which shows the care she is showing towards this child’s downfall. “Nothing I’m fine” Sniffles the child as he begins to pull himself to his feet slowly. “Then why was you on the floor and why are you crying?” The women continues her interest with another question towards the child’s position when she arrived at the scene. “ I fell over that’s all! I fell over! That’s it!’ The child begins to come conscious of the Teachers suspicion and increases his tone of voice denying the truth out of his mind. “Well are you ok? Do you need some help?” The women taken back by the child’s tone of expression still shows care in his situation and offers help. “I’m fine ok!” The words are spoken by a Large child who brushes past the teacher and makes an advance through the playground. ****************************************************************** Hot, Sweating and shaking Ron Williams scuttles to a upright position as he is awoke by something. “Dammit for fuck sake! Not another Flashback! I cant cope with this much longer” Realising he’s been dreaming again of the past Ron becomes frustrated with his own situation as he clambers out of his linen bed and carefully guides himself into the bathroom. “I cant cope! I cant go through this again! Not in dreams, not in reality, Not anywhere!” Trembling as he washes his flushed hot patchy face, Ron begins to advise himself and tell himself the truth. “ You cant do this to me! You cannot do this to me! Not another fucking flashback memory is going to come from you!” Ron stares into the Mirror for a split second before turning around and slamming the wooden bathroom door shut. He walks into the clammy room and begins to cuddle the rubber chicken known as Barry like a child. Barry is alive? BUT….ICU…?!?
