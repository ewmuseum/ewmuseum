Hound The Brute, The Dog of War Touched (Two) Location: Journal Entry Date: July 18, 2005 Number (Archive): 45 Number (Wrestler): 3 June 7th, 2005 The training continues. The time is drawing near when I feel I can be at the ready whenever Father Shanahan requests my assistance. To capture every aspect of the Cthulhu Mythos religion is tiring. My mind is numb, my body weary and my hunger growing. I’ve been virtually starved for the past two days trying to get these scriptures down. Good thing Marion is here to help me along the way. It intrigues me. She’s…different from the rest of my brothers and sisters with the exception of Ethan. He’s but a child, though. It is to be expected of him. Father. Horace. Perdition. Krauser. Just a few of the men I’ve met. Every one of them is like me. Cold. A chilling aura that surrounds us all and keeps us numb to the world of emotion. But she…Marion…Hemlocke. If it weren’t for her tutelage then I surely would’ve squandered the golden opportunity presented to me by Father. She kept my mind afloat when it was about to sink into this world of new ideals. She taught me how to pace myself with this training in order to assure maximum efficiency. I see a lot of emotions in her. She’s open with them. To me, at least. She swears total fealty to Father Shanahan, but she has her moments. She’s almost like…I don’t know how to describe it accurately. Like she is the antithesis of everybody here. Marion treats me well. Better than any woman has ever treated me in some time. But enough about her. Right now, I figure I should end this. I need to continue my workout. My muscles are already coursing with power but if I can continue to push the threshold then I will do so. I end this on a good note. Things seem to finally be looking up for me since Father found me. I even hear rumors that once my training is complete, Father was to give me an assignment right out of the box. An opportunity to get back into wrestling, even.
