How The Young Become Old: Tales From Hell (Part 2)

Jesse Ramey Title: How The Young Become Old: Tales From Hell (Part 2) Date Made: 4-01-04 Location: Boot Camp That day, though it started out boring, was going to become one of the most challenging days that I had ever faced in my life. The words emerging from Major Alexander’s mouth were piercing to say the least as not only was I about to be sent to the most feared place by anyone that had set foot onto that camp, but Major Alexander was going to be the one accompanying me to The Hole. I could only lower my head in shame at what I had done, but because I was the youngest and I was the smallest I would have to endure this pain for a long time to come; or at least that is how I felt to begin with. Major continued to hover over me with that stern look on his face, the one that never left. He didn’t seem like he had ever been happy before in his life or ever known how to show emotion unless it was the kind that struck fear into the hearts of anyone that took a second glance at you. I sucked it all up though, and I was going to take this like a man. At least maybe then it would show Major that I wasn’t going to be one of the young cadets that would crumple up and cry, although that was most likely the only thing I was going to be able to do in that “place” for the next couple of hours. “Cadet Ramey!” Major shouted once more into the air with that booming voice, “Follow me.” “Sir, yes, sir!” I shouted as well, with a bit of a squeak, though I kept myself from being very noticeable. Major turned his way around and began to walk forward through and past the rest of the cadets that were standing up straight making no sounds or faces as he passed them, but when it came to my turn they did the exact opposite. They all wanted to see me crack, but I tried not to let it get to me. If anything they only fueled my anger for the way they treated me. Why the resented me so much, I have no idea, but they did me worse than anyone that had came through the camp before, or at least I had been told this. Major continued his marching, passing by the bunks and nearing a small little metal looking object in the distance. It wasn’t very big looking, but it looked to have a handle at the top for opening. From that position it only seemed to get smaller, but it looked as though it opened downward, could it possible get longer? The time to find out was near, and I was almost shaking in my boots at this point in time. I knew not what to do, because of the stories that I had already heard about this “pit”. I was more afraid of how long I was going to be left in that thing than I was about going into it. Major stood over it and lowered his hand to the handle; pulling back on it very slowly the image within began to become more in sight to me. It didn’t seem to be very scary at all, from where I was standing at least. It looked to be maybe five and a half feet down, any of the taller boys would have had to crawl up into a ball to fit into it, but at least it was long enough to stretch your feet out in some. What was so bad about this thing anyway? Sometimes questions like that hurt us in the end though as Major Alexander looked me in the eyes, continuing to glare at me with his cold, heartless eyes, “Hopefully when you come out of here you’ll begin to train harder, Cadet Ramey. Once you come out of here you’ll never want to be the last in line for drills ever again. Do you understand?” I nearly got myself into more trouble as I continued to analyze The Hole, almost forgetting everything that Major had said, but I was able to get off a, “Sir, yes, sir!” No more words materialized from either of our mouths, only Major motioning for me to come into The Hole; and I did. I made my way into that little box very snuggly actually; it was almost a perfect fit. There was just enough room at the top for me to have a little bit of neck space and then he closed The Hole shut. As the door lowered all of the light in the box began to vanish, very slowly, and soon my sanity would be the other thing within the box that would vanish. I was locked in The Hole now and I was all alone, in the darkness, with only my thoughts. A normal person can only go so long like this without losing their composure though and for a ten year old things were going to move even quicker. I was beginning to hear things for the first time in my life that weren’t real within three hours of being in that hell. A ten year old shouldn’t have that much on their mind though, to trouble them so much that they would begin to hear the voices of death surrounding them, but when you have this much pressure placed upon you and have are separated from your family anything can happen. Maybe that was the real reason that I had lost it so young. The fact that my parents were never really around for me to begin with or maybe it was just the fact that I was made to carry such a load at a very young age. Either way, it didn’t matter at that point in time, because the only thing I could think about was how I was going to survive if I was ever placed back into that hell? If I was ever placed back into it? How was I supposed to survive in it now? I wanted so badly to begin to dig my nails into the dirt surrounding me to try and tunnel my way out, but it would have been of no help, because there was no way I could get a decent channel going the way I was positioned. It was hopeless and my thoughts were continuing to get to me more and more. My eyes were beginning to sink into the back of my head as well. I could barely stand to keep them open in the darkness and soon images began to flash through my head as well. Death was the only thing that I could think of, and soon I would be dead. But I couldn’t let this get to me. I had to fight on, no matter how hard things would get. If I let this first encounter dominate me then who is to say that from that point on everyone would dominate me? I didn’t know the duration that I would be left in that miserable place, but I was certainly going to do my best to look as though nothing had happened to me while I was in it. I knew it was time to get serious with things and begin to show my dominance in this place, but how would I escape this hell? And even if I did would I be able to flee the torment of this voice now that lingered in my mind? That sweet, soothing voice that told me to do so many evil things and dominated me for so long in my life. I knew it was true though, and soon he would help me. My time was coming.
