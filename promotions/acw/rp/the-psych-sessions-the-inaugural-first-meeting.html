The Psych Sessions // The inaugural first meeting

12 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
God's Forgotten Son 'GFS' and 'The Master of his own destiny' The Psych Sessions // The inaugural first meeting Location: Undisclosed Date: July 12, 2004 Number (Archive): 6 Number (Wrestler): 1 “It all started when I was five years old; going on six while growing up in a providence of Canada and the one thing that made me feel like I truly belonged, was professional wrestling. I would watch Stampede wrestling all day on the weekends, because it was the only thing on television that I would dare to view. It was either staying at home on the couch; in front of the warm glow of Bret Hart’s excellence, Brain Pillman’s unpredictability and the grace that Owen Hart displayed as he moved around the squared circle...Or going out shopping with my mother. I rather not choose the latter.” Another voice emerged from behind the reclined; black leather lounge chair, where the first voice emitted as he lit his cigarette and blew the nicotine into the air conditioned atmosphere. The second mystery individual scribbled away on his notepad; editing his mistakes quickly with his red ink pen, while he examined the body language of his patient. “...So how did that make you feel?” The assumed psychiatrist jotted down another note to self. The patient set his ignited cigarette onto the table to his left, stretched out before he continued nonchalantly. In reality; the mystifying lounge chair occupant said nothing at all; as he stared into the white ceiling dreamingly. The psychiatrist waited patiently for his response, clearing his throat which must have been the cue that he was ready to write. “I guess you’re ready huh doc? Ready to listen to my life story; why I am the way that I am...and what chain of events has placed this loathing individual on your uncomfortable sofa.” “If it’s uncomfortable for you, we can fix your arrangements.” The doctor reached for some pillows, but returned his attention to where it belonged. “It’s fine, let us just hurry this up.” The patient said coldly. “Having my mother not understand my incredible dream, made me hurt deep inside. Of everyone that was around me; the butlers or maids, the exquisite cooks that were brought in to prepare my meals...she was the only person who couldn’t understand my vision, which is destined to happen.” The psychiatrist tried to interrupt politely, but he was cut off by his patient’s harsh tone change. “...But first I have to get through this, isn’t that correct?” “Yes, you do unfortunately.” The doctor chimed in confidently. “Before I can sign your release papers, you must go through a psychological evaluation, so we can understand where your mind is at. And once you pass these tests with flying colors...off to All Star Championship Wrestling you will go. That is where you want to start your professional wrestling career, isn’t that right Mr. ---“ “Don’t you even dare challenge me; just because my mother setup this little charade, doesn’t mean that you are in control. You’re just another fucking puppet in her little show...dangling around by your nimble strings, until she decides that your usefulness is completed. All I want to do is move on with my life, and if I have to put up with these psych games to evolve...THAN SO BE IT! This isn’t the worst thing that has happened in my life, so I can easily survive mere parlor tricks, which my stupid mother has dreamed up. And from this point on, you will to refer to me as Seth. Nothing more, nothing less.” “I’m sorry that I set you off Seth, I didn’t mean to. Your mother only wants what’s best for you and your well being...you know that better than anyone. But enough about her for now continue where you left off.” The doctor scrawled more writing in his journal, before setting it down to sip from his glass of water. “Unlike my dreadful mother dearest, my father nurtured my dreams and aspirations. He would tell me to draw pictures of my favorite wrestlers; like the Dynamite kid and Davey Boy Smith. Collectively they were known as the British Bulldogs, I also drew Andre the Giant...including Hulk Hogan. I used so much red and yellow on Hulk’s drawing, that my father always replaced my box of crayons...no questions asked. I had five boxes of almost new crayons, but only those two colors were missing. Since my father found out Stampede Wrestling’s fan mailing address; he would mail my letters and drawings to Dynamite and Smith, to make sure they knew how much I idolized them. Although my mother thought it was a waste of time...and I may never hear a response, she thought I shouldn’t get my hopes up.” “Did you listen to her; did you believe what she said?” The doctor moved closer with his tape recorder firmly in his grasp, capturing Seth’s sigh of resentment for the woman that should been the most important thing in his life, like normal children. “At first I didn’t want to believe her or my father, decisions of that magnitude were hard when you’re five. I almost lost hope all together until my father came back from his business trip early; which was coincidently around the time of my sixth birthday...and boy did he surprise me.” Scribbling down in his notebook once again; releasing a smirk within the curvatures of his face, the doctor simply replied. ”So what did he do for your birthday Seth?” He could hear his patient chuckling to himself as he was hidden behind the sofa, another gesture quickly noted. A happy memory must have risen to the surface amongst all the angst ridden hate that boiled in his patient’s soul. The longer he could keep Seth in high spirits; it could be possible that more information could be retrieved, but you could always expect the unexpected with loose cannons. And Seth ranked at the very top of that list. “For my birthday, if it’s really any of your business, my father took me to my very first wrestling match. I was so excited when he showed me the tickets...I wanted to play with them every day until the event. But he was afraid I would lose them, it was understandable. My mother being the bitch that she normally was, told my father not to take me. In her words; seeing that nonsense up close could rot my mind. Never the less; we went to the show and my father placed me on top of his shoulders, as I held up my stick figure British bulldog sign, so the real bulldogs could see from the ring.” “Did anything else memorable happen during said event?” Dropping his glass onto of the brown coaster, as the doctor seemed some what intrigued just when his smile widen. “Of course, I saw the Dynamite kid climb all the way to the top turnbuckle; everyone in the building cheered him on as he flew off, connecting with his trademark diving head butt onto his adversary. It seemed like everything was moving in slow motion at that very moment, the planets had aligned in perfect formation...I was destined to be in that place, where history would be made. As Davey reentered the ring, making the final pin...I could feel the love the fans showered their heroes with. I always wanted to be loved like that by my peers; and everyone around me. The only way that could happen is if I became a wrestler too.” “You remember that memory very well, which shows that you are serious in becoming a wrestler...even through the hardships you’ve had in the past. But let’s stop for today on this good note, and we’ll continue next time. Same time next week Seth?” The doctor closed his notebook and pressed the stop button on his recorder as his patient rose to his feet. “Like I even have a choice doc...Like I even have a choice.”
