Lancett The Megastar, Kid Frost, Rook' When we look back. (1/3) Location: Back in the day. Date: September 02, 2004 Number (Archive): 13 Number (Wrestler): 1 I look back at my days when I use to cheer the men whom I despise nowadays. The men who respect the company look down at me in distrust. I disrespect everything I come by for the past year or so, but why? I couldn’t ever figure that out. Randy took his lettermen jacket off from his broad shoulders. “How’s are star safety? What was it last year, three interceptions per game?” An elderly man sarcastically muttered. Who limped to his right onto a wooden cane that he has been using for years, proof was within the worn wooden floors indented with his cane steps, instead of footsteps. This is one man who I would always look up to. This man I’d run to and show him I got a “C” on a paper and he’d smile with his toothless grin and pat me on the back, this man was my father. He always made my day a bit more cheerful: “How where those girls? Where they standing outside your class room like they always do, today?” “Of course pop.” I said to him with a smile on my face. He always wanted to know more though, “How’s the quarter back, what’s his name?” ”Duke Linton. He is doing pretty well, coach is getting our second string’s Quarter Back up and ready, he looks promising.” I had a neighbor, Marcus Andrews, and he was the nerd of the school. Most of the time he’d give me the answers to homework and tests. I would jump threw hoops to get the classes with him and to sit by him. If it weren’t for him, I’d probably still be in Washington Middle School. We meet when where were three and he moved in. He was the only man I knew who tried to push me to do stuff I didn’t want to. Out from the left side of the yard a lanky young man strolled into the yard carrying a backpack. Son of Lewis Andrews, who was a very wealthy businessman whom helped pay for the jerseys and the high school supplies, owner of Andrews Furniture, and daughter of a beautiful Ophelia Lewis, who worked at Andrews Furniture also but as a clerk and designer came up to me: “Hey you going to come over to study for Global History?” He’s been trying to get me to study for about freshmen year. It was beginning to get very old. “Hey, I gotta’ practice some ball carrying plus I have a date with Megan tonight.” I slipped and he caught it. “Megan Holmes?” I sighed as I took the football, which just so happen to be thrown to me during the conversation about studying, and tightened my grip on it. “Yes.” I said softly, “But we aren’t going to do anything, so don’t get your panties in a bunch.” He has had a crush on Megan Holmes sense the fourth grade, it was really kind of sad. “You do know you’ll fail Glob Hist (short for Global History) and you will not be able to graduate on time, right?” And fate took it’s toll…
