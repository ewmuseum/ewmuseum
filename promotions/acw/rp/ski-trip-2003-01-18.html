Ski Trip

18 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#139) by Jay Smash, dated 18 January 2003, from the fed's first site, acw.ewmania.com (its acw2 edition, 2002–04) as captured in the Wayback on 22 July 2003. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Ski Trip Written: 1/18/03 It's around midday, and the day looks brighter than ever. The snow is on the ground and people from all over are on their way to ski. A silver BMW Z8 makes its way into the parking lot of the ski resort and slowly parks on the icy pavement. Jay Smash walks out wearing a black leather jacket, a pair of black leather boots, a pair of loose fit, dark blue denim jeans, his hair slicked back and tied behind him, and a pair of sun glasses. As he shuts the door, the passenger side door opens and Ralph walks out with a purple ski suit on. As his right foot steps out of the car, he is seen wearing a ski already on his foot. He takes his left foot out of the car next, having a hard time getting out of the car and onto the icy ground. When both men are out of the car and the doors are shut, they make their way to the entrance of the resort. 'You know, their are places you can suit up in side. You didn't have to put everything on yet.' 'Oh really? Nah, I usually get everything on and start right away. I never came over here before though. Me and my friends usually go to the mountain over here.' 'That's not a mountain. That’s a small hill leading back onto the highway.' 'Never saw the difference. I mean, why pay to get in the resort when you got one right here! You just don't know what you are missing.' 'Right... Well you can go ahead and have fun on your hill. The rest of the sane people are going inside the resort to ski up on the mountain.' 'Hello ladies, what's goin on?' Ralph takes a look at two ladies walking towards the resort when they turn to hear Ralph, and turn away laughing.' 'Ok, ok, come on Jay, those ladies seem to know what they are doing. Hey look at that, they are going into the resort where the real men.... and women go. Are you man enough to go in their Jay?' '...Gee, I don't know... Might be a bit tough.' 'Well come on Jay, suck it up.' 'I was kidding you ass! I'm ready to go, lets go! I told you before too, I'm also here to prove to you that I am ready for Sunday. If I can ski down this mountain with this injured leg and feel fine, then I'll be just fine for my match against that mother f...' 'Woah! Calm down! There are ladies in our presence.' Ralph points to the two ladies he saw before as they walk into the resort and head to the top of the mountain. When they get to the top, Jay enters the lodge to suit up. At this time Ralph is alone, getting his goggles onto his face and practicing his stances for going down the mountain. Jay Smash comes back about 10 minutes later in his ski suit, ready to go. 'What they hell are you doing?' 'I'm getting ready to go down the mountain. I'm doing my stances.' 'Stances? You look like you're gonna have a seizure. Stop shaking.' 'I'm cold. It's cold out here!' 'It's a fake mountain. It's not real snow! They made the snow themselves! Any real mountain is too far from the arena so we had to come here. And it's not that cold at all.' 'Well it is if you don't eat anything all day, drink alot of cold water and try to ski down a huge mountain!' 'So are you saying you are hungry?' 'Just a little.' 'Fine, we will get something to eat.' 'Hey, looks...isn't that Jay Smash?' 'Who? The wimp that said hi to us before?' 'No silly, the sexy one with the strong body.' 'Oh, yeah I've been staring at him since I walked in here.' The two ladies begin to talk to each other about Jay Smash, as Jay listens, smiling, and getting ready to head back to the lodge to get something to eat with Ralph. 'Oh look, the wimp is hungry, lets go follow him. Maybe we can get rid of that loser he is with and have him ride with us.' 'Oh Kelly you are so bad.' 'I know.' Jay Smash and Ralph Lemmburg head into the Lodge where they sit down at a table to get something to eat. As they sit down, the two hot ladies enter the lodge and sit at the bar. They both turn over to Jay Smash and wave as Jay Smash looks up to wave back. One of the ladies raises her hand, calling Jay over. 'Would you excuse me for a second Ralph, I'm just gonna see what's at the bar.' 'Ok no prob, I'll go with you.' 'No, no, you see you need to sit here and wait for the waiter so you can order the food. You are hungry right? So just sit here and ummm, read this menu.' 'I can do that. You go ahead and get some drinks. We might need them when we are going down a mountain that big. Especially when it's cold.... Oh right, it's fake...sorry.' 'Right...' Jay stands up and heads over to the bar while Ralph sits down looking at the menu. The two ladies turn to Jay as he walk over and order a drink. 'Hey, aren't you Jay Smash?' 'Yeah, like that wrestler.' 'Yes I am. Well, not much of a wrestler right now. I have an injured leg.' 'Oh you poor baby. Maybe someone should take care of that for you.' 'Actually it's not so bad anymore.... but... a little more help wouldn't hurt.' 'My name is Jessica, and this is my very good friend Kelly. We will be in room 4 here at the resort tonight. We can help that leg out for you.' While Jay Smash is having his conversation with the two ladies, Ralph is seen looking at the menu, until a waiter walk over to take the order. 'Hi my name is Dan, what can I get for you?' 'Hi Dan, can I get a double cheese burger, and my pal Jay Smash, you might know him from the wrestling federation ACW, well he will get some Buffalo Wings, as spicy as you can make them.' 'Would you like anything else?' 'No, I'm fine.' 'Ok we'll have that right out to you as soon as we can.' 'Sounds good. Thank you.... Hey Jay the food is comin!' Jay Smash is seen once again with the ladies as they all smile, until Ralph yells to him. Jay Smash looks down and then back up, a little upset at Ralph. 'That's my friend Ralph is you were wondering. He is my manager in ACW. You watch right?' 'Oh we don't watch anymore, but we used to watch you all the time. Hey, how about you ditch the loser and come with us now.' 'Wait...loser?' 'Yeah, ditch the ass.' 'Wait just a second. Ok, he might be kinda slow at times, and seem kind of unorganized but he is a good guy. Don't call him that. He is my friend.' 'Aww well isn't that sweet. You 'friend' isn't gonna get you any with two hot chicks like us. You know you want us.' 'Yes...yes I do. But I can here to ski with a friend, and that's what I am gonna do. I'm sorry ladies, have a nice day.' 'Oh, whatever. Loser.' Jay Smash heads back over to the table where he sits down with Ralph and waits for the order...
