Trevor and Company - 3

8 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 08, 2009 Or "Falling In" July 8 Brian Spaes sat on the hood of a car in the lot. If it was a fan's no one knew. He was leaned back on his elbows with his unlaced combat boots resting on the bumper in front of him. Trevor Wilson, the Sentinel of the Minutemen, walked toward him. In tow were the newest soldiers in the Minutemen and Trevor's hand picked allies, Jeremy Stevenson and Kati. The first thing that Brian noticed, and it was hard not to, was how out of place they were with Brian's current state. While he was dressed for combat, Jeremy and Kati were dressed for a party. The only thing Brian had to go on was Trevor's word. Right now, that was all he needed. "Welcome, one and all, my Minutemen." "Brian, allow me to introduce, Jeremy Stevenson, and Kati." "He can probably figure out which one of us is which dude. Aside from the rack, Kati's the only one here with green hair." "Shut up, Jeremy. My rack doesn't need a back up excuse. It's enough of a reason on its own." Brian reached to his ear and slid out a cigarette from behind it. He twirled it in his fingers for a moment and then brought it to his lips. "Are they always like this, Trevor?" "As far as I can tell. Like I said, we haven't all been together in quite a while." "Trevor got a contract with Legacy of Champions and forgot we existed." "That's not exactly how things went down." "Sure it was. Know the funny part, Brian? His first night there he won a tag team belt. He leaves us behind to form a tag team. Ain't that some shit? And his partner was a guy who's ass I used to kick back in the day." "He already knows all that, Jeremy. He was in Legacy at the same time. You were an alcoholic and a drug addict back then though weren't you, Brian? Did you even know you were working there when Trevor did? Has he mentioned this to you at all?" The Bic lighter went off and Brian inhaled deeply. And then again. The sweet relief could not come fast enough. "I know that, but I wasn't there long enough for us to work together. You used to wrestle under the name Shodan right?" "Yes, sir." Jeremy nodded and hearing his old pseudonym put a smile on his face. "I wasn't done talking about him being in a tag team though." "Okay...continue." "He was tag team champion for a while and then lost the belt. But then he was going to form another tag team with a guy named Fanatic. He's a masked superhero by the way. That's two tag teams this guy was involved in or planned to be involved in and yet he never once picked up a phone to call one of us." "Wait a minute." Brian inhaled and kept the smoke in while he spoke. "You were going to work with Fanatic?" "I was. He was a former Underground champ and I was rising pretty fast in the ranks. Plans changed after a couple weeks." "When Trevor says plans he means they wanted to give him the Underground championship and he'd slob anyone's knob to get a title belt and be in main events." Brian took a puff and peered at Trevor who stood shaking his head. "Listen, the both of you obviously have some talent if Trevor is vouching for you. That's exactly why I want you here. We need manpower, and we need skilled manpower at that. I don't mind you guys being talkative and wanting to tell stories and all, but there comes a time when I'm going to need you to shut up and fight, okay?" "We're down with the idea, and we're down with getting some work done. Until the marching orders come down from Trevor then we're just background noise, comrade. And I speak for my Lite Brite friend over here." Kati stared at Brian as he inhaled once more and flicked his ash to the ground. "You know, Brian, you should really start smoking herbal cigarette's. Jeremy does and he's even had sex a few times. I even trust him to dye my hair." Trevor let out a sigh and looked at Brian with questioning eyes. At the moment he was wondering if he was about to be kicked from the Minutemen. "I honestly don't give a shit what you do away from here. All I need to know is that you believe in the cause and like swinging chairs. Well do you?" "She does. Sometimes at me and sometimes at other people. And before you ask no it's not foreplay before we have sex because we haven't had sex." "Forget it. Let's get inside the arena so you can see what we're working with." "Lead the way, captain." Kati quickly saluted Brian and then turned to face the arena with Jeremy doing the same. Trevor shrugged at Brian as he hopped off the car. The Minutemen took a glance at the All-Star arena, and then descended upon their playground.
