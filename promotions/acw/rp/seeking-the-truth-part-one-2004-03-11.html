Brandon Thomas Title: Seeking the Truth: Part One Date Made: March 11, 2004 Location: Las Vegas, Nevada (In a House) The day was warm, the birds chirped, and the streets were buzzing with motored vehicles. Today was the day Brandon Thomas would make a long journey towards Sweden, where the ACW was now located. His wife didn’t like the fact that he’d be hundred and thousands of miles away from home. She didn’t want him being so far away from his family, though his family wasn’t at the top of his favorite’s list. Wrestling was. Thomas was packing his underwear, shirts, pants, socks, and a few other utensils in his suitcase for the trip as Elicia entered the bedroom dressed in a white gown that dragged across the floor as she walked. She was beautiful, nonetheless, but a girl with such beauty would never hold Thomas back from his destiny. “Brandon, isn’t there somewhere else you could go. Sweden is a long ways away, too far away.” She plopped down on the bed, beside the suitcase, “Brandon I love you, Eli loves you to, why can’t you just stay home with us and just be a family.” Brandon stopped for a moment with his hands on the bed, he turned his head and looked at Elicia, “You want me to just throw my career away like that when big things are waiting for me. I’m going to be a star, a huge star, a star so great that the entire world is going to know who I am and with that fame, comes money, money we don’t have right now, money that we need right now. We can barely pay the rent, much less the other bills that we have cluttering our table. We need this.” “I know we need this Brandon! That’s why I’m working two jobs, that’s why you need to get a job closer so that we can both take care of Eli and spend quality time with him.” Brandon sighed, “I wish my parents were still around.” “Baby, they weren’t always going to be around for you and you knew that. We’ve spent all of the money we’ve inherited from them, its time to provide for ourselves-” “And that’s why I’m going to Sweden, to make us a living.” he shouted back. “Why is it you don’t want me or Eli going with you then? I want to know.” Brandon turned his head and began packing the rest of his things. For some reason, he didn’t want to answer that question. In his mind, he knew exactly why he wanted to go alone, but it wasn’t what she had been thinking. He wasn’t going to Sweden to have anymore affairs as before, he was going because he loved the profession of wrestling. And he wanted to make something of himself. She just couldn’t understand; typical woman, “I know what you’re thinking. Your thinking that I’m going up there to get some ass on the side, right?” she looked down at the mattress, “Well I’m not Elicia, whether you think I am, I’m not. I’ve told you the reasons for me going, you know what I want.” “Fine, just go.” she said jumping up out of her place and storming out of the room. Brandon only sighed, knowing that things would be better in the near future. He knew that ACW would turn him into something special. A superstar.
