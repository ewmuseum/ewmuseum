The Psych Sessions // A childhood revisited

22 July 2004

God's Forgotten Son 'GFS' and 'The Master of his own destiny' The Psych Sessions // A childhood revisited Location: Undisclosed Date: July 22, 2004 Number (Archive): 7 Number (Wrestler): 2 The music within the psychiatrist’s calm office began with simple piano-laden intro and non-verbal chanting, before a negligible howl slowly eased into the soothing lyrics, which promptly followed. In his brown leather circular chair; where he could normally be found, the doctor analyzed his patient’s body language…stiff as a board while eyes closed shut in a trance like state, lost in the harmonious track. Seth’s face pointed straight upward, letting his mind drift thousands of light years away…yet amazingly in tune with the environment around him. I jumped in the river and what did I see? black-eyed angels swimming with me a moon full of stars and astral cars all the figures i used to see all my lovers were there with me all my past and futures and we all went to heaven in a little row boat there was nothing to fear and nothing to doubt The quiet room was awaken by an uncanny telepathy; two voices united as one at that very moment, as Thom Yorke was imitated beautifully in the disturbed young man’s interpretation of “the Pyramid song”. Young but painfully wise; even the doctor felt every note that wisped pass Seth’s cherry red lips as he continued to sing…the psychiatrist toke this opportunity to inscribe more personal experiences in his black journal with his red pen. While the music slowly faded out; the doctor closed his note book and placed on the coffee table near by, politely applauding as Seth remained trapped inside his own imagination. “I thought the music would be a good change of pace; especially since you like the artist I played for you. You’re a good singer Seth, have you ever thought of starting a band?” the doctor turned the volume up on his tape recorder as his young troubled patient sighed loudly. “…Yeah, I’ve already done that and it didn’t turn out so well. Enough of the small talk doc, let’s move on.” Reaching to his left, Seth picked up a glass of water, drank from it as he returned the container back to its location on the table. The doctor reopened his log to jot down something he forgot, before turning back to previous outlined pages, finally finding what he was looking for. “Okay Seth, I agree with you about not wasting anytime in our sessions. Let’s talk about a new topic shall we, and I think we should go back to your early childhood. We touched on a little bit about some fond memories you had…but we need to delve deeper into what events transpired, to transform you into the man you are today. So Seth, can you tell me more about your up-brining?” Contemplating while rummaging his nimble fingers through his jumbled black and blonde hair; it was as if Seth was searching for some hidden infinite intelligence to answer the doc’s inquiry…or maybe he was looking for another cigarette to smoke, similar to the one the individual known as GFS smoked upon first arriving. “Why are you trying to put me through these mind games; you probably already know what I am going to say, before the question is even uttered as it is read from your stupid note book. Who is going to force me to endure these pointless interview sessions, if I choose not to come here anymore huh? Can you answer that unscripted question doc? Because when it’s all set and done…nothing is going to prevent me from achieving my destiny.” Seth knocked his water off of the table in aggravation as the psychiatrist scribbled away; shaking his head in disbelief, whilst his secretary must of heard the commotion, running in to clean up the mess. “Now Seth, there are no games being played here. As much as you like to disagree; thinking that everyone is against you, deep down in the pit of your soul…you realize that our meetings are for your own benefit. We want you to achieve your goals, see your dreams come to fruition as you envision them…but first, we need to complete our time together. However; if you feel that you do not want to finish the allotted time, you’ll be forced to return regardless against your will. Now let’s continue without anymore interruptions.” “Fine fucker. When I was a kid just old enough to comprehend; our family did a lot of traveling around the world, mostly done in the United States, sometimes we went over seas to London or Paris. Since my mother and father were business people, we never stayed in one place very long, but let me correct myself…they never stayed in one location before I was born. However; my mother was very fond of New York City because of its ambience and the city’s historical roots. In the year 1981, within Manhattan’s Upper East Side; I was born on April 5th during a home birth.” “…When the doctor wiped the remaining birth residue from my flesh; my mother cradled me in her arms, slightly whispering in my ear so only I could hear, she said that I was the most beautiful baby she had ever seen. It’s unlikely that statement is still true today, seeing as we don’t even speak to one another.” Seth’s parched throat was re-hydrated with his replacement water’; the doctor hurried to capture every emotion he witnessed in his journal, while Seth’s motor mouth spoke at 90 mph. “You said that you don’t speak with your mother, was that the case during your childhood?” the psychiatrist knew what buttons to push, prodding with his mental acumen while his patient adjusted his position in the sofa. “No, not at all to tell you the truth. She used to act like a normal mother, wife should be. Accepting, nurturing and all the other personality traits you would expect…but the more my father traveled around the world without her; frequent visits only to leave again a few weeks later, made my mother grow cold on the inside. When that started to happen, I knew I had to distance myself from her before it was too late. But I was in too deep before I knew it.” “Was there anytime in your life, where you knew that your mother loved you?” the psychiatrist checked the time on his watch while listening intently to how his patient would respond. Everything meant something; a twitch of a nose, a lift in an eyebrow…and even a violent outburst. Those subtle clues would be the pieces of puzzle to figure out Seth as best as the certified therapist could. Clearing his throat; the enigma stopped, slightly leaving the doc hanging by a thread as he stopped writing before he continued. “One time when I was in the first grade; school was over and I waited for my bodyguard to pick me up from school, so I decided to waste time and get on the swing set. It seemed like hours had passed, yet no one was in sight as my elevation grew; suddenly appearing from the distance I saw the silhouette of mother. I knew it was her by the large individual who sauntered across the playground by her side. Excited because my mother never picked me up; I leapt from the swing in mid forward movement and as I lost control, smashing my face into the concrete ground.” “Were you embarrassed?” the doctor ferocious scrawling within the notebook; his red pen running out of ink, while the sounds of transcription echoed in the office amidst the story telling. “No, I was in too much pain as my mother ran to my rescue; wiping the blood from my broken nose with her expensive clothing apparel, which I still know nothing about to this day. As my bodyguard held me in his arms as we sped off to the nearest hospital…that was the only moment where I knew my mother really loved me. Is that good enough for you? I’m done talking about the past for today.” Reaching forward as he pressed the stop button the recorder, “Yes Seth, that’s good enough for me. Another excellent session, but we’ll continue this next week, where we left off today.” Placing his belongings in his briefcase, the doctor lead his patient toward the exit as the phone rang. “…I knew that it would be you. Are you sitting down? I have a lot to inform you about.”
