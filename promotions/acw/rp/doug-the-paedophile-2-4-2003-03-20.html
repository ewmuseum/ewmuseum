Doug the paedophile [2/4] Written: 20th march So I was sitting in my cubical today And I realised Ever since I started working, erm Every single day of my life has been worse that the day before it Which means that every time you see me That’s the worse day of my life What about today? Is today the worst day of your life? - Apology Rejected --- Twenty minutes of Scott Perry’s life were wasted sitting on the bed of his small room. This, paled comparison to the weeks he had wasted inside his solitary confinement mental health cubical and yet, in the same breath somehow managed to feel as if it had lasted much longer than his stay in the ward. A knock on the door, light, yet firm. “Come on in.” Scott said in a polite manner. Enter Doug. Scott sat up. He had been laying with his face looking up at the white ceiling. As Scott looked across the tiny room Doug filled the door. He was a small man, about 5 feet 5, extremely thin. But the room was so small that even Doug managed to fill the door. Behind him… “Scott,” Doug said, taking a sidestep and placing his hand on the back of his small daughters blonde head. “Scott, this is Jenny. She’s my daughter.” “Oh hello Jenny!” Scott said in as cheery a voice as he could summon. “Your daddy has told me all about you.” She looked up at her father. She was uneasy around Scott. “This is Scott Jenny. You remember me telling you about the man I looked after? Well this is him.” She still looked uneasy. “Don’t worry darling, he’s all better now.” Doug reassured his daughter. “Yeah jenny, your daddy made me all better. Daddy is my best friend in the whole world.” And then came a shout. Mother called out the regular call. “Dinner guys!” her voice muffled from the bottom of the stairs. Jenny ran off. She still was ill at ease about the new visitor, Scott Perry. Scott’s face sank. He felt like a monster. “It’ll be okay buddy. She will warm to you, like I did, remember?” “Yeah…” Scott sighed. “C’mon buddy.” He said as he scooped Scott to his feet with his trusting hand on the former television champion’s back. “Lets get some grub in ya.” - ‘He was my best friend at the time. I didn’t know. I really didn’t. If only I had known… If only…’
