Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster, The Shillin' Villain, The Choice of a New Generation, The Czar of Cashflow Business as usual Location: The LDC-HQ, where else? Date: October 25, 2005 Number (Archive): 12 Number (Wrestler): 8 Unorthodox is the only word to describe Lowell Dot Com’s wrestling style. Well, I guess nonsensical would be another, but whatever. It throws people off. Just when you think you have him figured out he does the exact opposite. Wrestling genius? No. No, he simply lacks focus and the ability to strategize. A hundred million things flood his mind and he just goes with the first one that registers. See: Him climbing up onto the announce table and jumping off with a front dropkick. Without any consideration as how he might land, which would result from leaping forward off of an elevated base and kicking his legs out infront of him. But it seems to get the job done, so can anyone really advise him to rethink his gameplan, or lack there of? He’s been Scorpio Champion since End Game and has defended it on nearly every show, despite being a quote un-quote weasily heel champion. However, he has had help from Jimmy Cain, and by help, I mean that Jimmy is pretty much Scorpio Champion and that Lowell just wears the belt around because it looks nice. Joined in progress is Lowell Dot Com abusing himself in the Champaigne Room to photos of Jill, his receptionist. Most are of her staring directly at the camera with an annoyed look on her face, with one actually of her giving it the finger. Lowell Dot Com made with the Aww Skeet Skeet and left to wash his hand, having broken one of the cardinal rules of home ownery: No sex in the Champaigne Room. Does the Squishy Squishy count as sex? How bout if you draw vaginal lips on your hand and talk in a girl’s voice? No? Well, then I guess Lowell’s never had-- …cake. Shut up. Moving on… Walking out into the lobby, he finds Jimmy Cain standing with a foot pinning the Commie to the floor. Oh, and there’s lots of loud, unpleasantness going on, too! LDC jogged over and stood beside Jimmy for a second, assessing the situation. Jimmy’s foot was on the Commie’s throat and the Commie’s face was turning purple from lack of oxygen. Lowell, after scratching his head in a puzzled manner, walked casually over to the front desk where Jill sits all day, typing up reports -- actually she just photocopies pages out of a Game Genie booklet and lays them on his desk at the end of the day… …as one might expect, nothing has ever come of it. LDC tapped the counter with his index finger and propped his head up with his hand. “Hey, yeah, um, mind filling me in as to what that is?” he asked, before turning and pointing to the Jimmy. Cain was in the middle of killing the sweat pants wearing fatty. “Might wanna’ make it quick, too.” “…Yes. Um, I don’t know. I saw what’s-his-face tumble down the stairs, land on his neck, get up and then start running for the door. Jimmy eventually caught up with him when he threw his shoe and hit him in the back of the head. I think that brings you pretty much up to speed.” Jill went back to reading the Cosmo she had laid out infront of her. Lowell walked back over and rested his hand on the Jimmy’s shoulder. “Jimbo,” Lowell started, “what seems to be the problem?” “Teeeeeell………… heeeeeeeem………….” gasped the Commie, eyeing Cain. Jimmy Cain took his foot off of Commie’s throat and patted the wrinkles from his designer shirt. THEN he popped that collar. And Jill had an orgasm… but that’s because she happened to be masturbating to one of those “Sex Tips” articles and all the naughty imagery contained within. It seems all anyone does at the LDC-HQ is fight and masturbate. Well, ‘cept for the Jimmy. He spends much of his free time of fantasizing about ultra-violence and destruction, as well as ending the lives of people named Coral Avalon, whom he’s scheduled to face at the next Courage. But don’t tell Lowell. He’d fuh-lip! The Jimmy didn’t say anything at first. Lowell crossed his arms and looked scornfully at him. “You know Commie’s suppose to be scraping down the dumpster right now. What could be so important that you would deprive him of this simple pleasure and throw your SHOE at him? You know I don’t like being the voice of reason! I’m the voice of anti-reason!! GAWD, YOU’RE GIVING ME A MIGRAINE!! FUCK!” He held his hand over his forehead and sat down cross-legged on the floor. “I…tink…he…broke…my…THINK BOX!” The Commie felt around for blood and sure enough there was. “Oowwwwchies!” The Jimmy, boring through the Commie with a death glare, answered with: “That’ll teach you to say “Hi” to me! What did I FUCKING tell you about that?!” Lowell: “EVERYBODY STOP WITH THE MOTHERFUCKING SHOUTING!!!!oneonethisisfun!1” The Commie looked freaked out for a second, then reached over, grabbing Cain’s shoe and smelling the inside of it. It smelled like heaven. “Mmmmmmm.” “--I’m gonna pretend I didn’t see that, Commie- I’m gonna pretend that what I saw was you apologizing and offering to give me a lubed up handy for being such a stupid fucking faggot back there. You’re lucky I’m saving up my rage points to go all WARJIMMY!! on the next episode of Courage, or else you’d be lying underneath my crawlspace nexext to my effeminate mailman.” The Jimmy stomped him in the groin and walked off out of sight. Lowell took his fingers out of his ears and looked at the Commie- fetal position with both hands buried in his crotch. “Two hander, ehh? That’s INNOVATIVE. YOUS THA X-DIVISION OF MA CREWWWWHUUUUUUUU!! Hehehe. LET’S GO LEFTY! LET’S GO RIGHTY!! Hehehehe… fun. I’m out. Finish up here and then get back to scraping out the dumpster filth. Don’t want the Health Commission shutting down our “Cheap Transient Housing” business- that’s brings in a good chunk of change… and by change I really do mean change. Last month we made four quarters, a bent nickel, and a USED syringe!!”
