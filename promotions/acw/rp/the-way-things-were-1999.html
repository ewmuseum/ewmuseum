Scott Rojas 'Salvador's Fat Son', 'Hijo de la Lanza', 'That Goofy Motherfucker from Defiance' The Way Things Were // 1999 Location: Laredo, Texas Date: July 23, 2005 Number (Archive): 46 Number (Wrestler): 1 'Otra vez' 'Otra vez' 'Otra vez' Salvador Rojas, the famed La Lanza, was schooling his son in the ring on the tactic of the arm drag. Alejandro, only 15, already resembling the man he would become. Chubby, a bit of a show boat and not very serious. His arm drags were loose and not very precise. Actually, he was more slinging his training partner around the ring. This was a good day for Alejandro actually, and he was borderline horrible. Any way that's what had Salvador angry 'HIJO! You are sloppy! Sloppy! Put more care into it! STOP!' Salvador called both teenagers over to him. For someone in his fifties, he was in immaculate shape and the silver hair suited him well, he looked distinguished in his khakis and orange shirt. The Rojas family was one of legend in the Southwest United States and Northern Mexico. Salvador's father, Santiago, was the first one to wear the mask of La Lanza and did so gloriously for the better part of thirty years. That was a part of the mystique of La Lanza, Santiago and Salvador seamlessly transitioned the hood from father to son. The two had the same build and size and wrestled so similiar that fans thought that La Lanza had eternal life, being able to wrestle for nearly 50 years uninterrupted. That was a different time that today. La Lanza dissapeared before the mid nineties as ones like Rey Mysterio Jr, Juventud Guerrera and Eddy Guerrero started to reach the American stage. Salvador wanted his son to carry on the legacy of his grandfather and take La Lanza into the new millenium. But with how sloppy he wrestled, how fat he was and how un serious he was in the ring, there was a better chance of Cop Rock coming back. 'Manuel, toma una rotura. Bueno?' 'Esta bien profesor.' Manuel, who later would wrestle for three years and almost get the great title reign he was destined for but would succumb to heroin, left. 'Hijo, listen to me when I say this. You have been given more than alot of men in this industry. Herencia. Legacy. You must carry on our name with honor. And you can't doing that being an idiot in the ring and not taking us seriously. If your grandfather was here, he would break down crying after seeing what you are doing with La Lanza's name.' Salvador reached in a bag and pulled out a green and white mask. The mask of La Lanza. 'This is what we stand for, Hijo. La máscara. Two generations of Rojas have fought under this very mask. When you were born almost 16 years ago, mi padre looked at you and smiled knowing that La Lanza would continue.' Santiago told Salvador
