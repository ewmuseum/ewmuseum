Chapter Two - [ Answers Bearing Questions ] The Dunn household lay motionless, with only the gentle sigh of the intake and outtake of breath harming it's peacefulness. Charles, and Margaret Dunn lay in the master bedroom, as their young son, Matthew curled up to his Finding Nemo bedspread, just a wall apart from his parents. It's was the suburban dream, literally, until a minor bump caused Margaret to regain consciousness. After living in a house for so long, you get used to the noises that it creates in the night. Be it the ticking of a clock, or the rattling of an electricity meter, or even the crooked shakes of the wind. But this was an unfamiliar noise. A noise which spread fear into Margaret's heart. "...charlie..." she whispered, as well as shaking her husband gently. "...charlie...I think someone is in the house." Charlie Dunn's eye's slowly lifted, as his senses came back up his spine as his body parts began to become controllable again. He gazed over at his wife, immediately sensing that something was wrong. "I think someone's in the house...listen." ... ... ... ... *bump* "See!?" Charlie sharply sat up, and twisted his legs over the side of the bed, before pulling out a baseball bat from underneath his bed's framework. Margaret looked in shock, as it was something she didn't even know existed up until now....but was glad of her husbands smart thinking. "Call the police, and then go into Matts' room you hear me?" "Yes." Before she could say another word, Charlie was away to investigate, as he slowly made his way to the open bedroom door, before checking either side of the hallway for anything suspicious. His first line of thought was his son, and he quickly checked the room directly left of his own, where his son lay peacefully, much to his delight. *bump* The sound was coming from downstairs, but Charlie knew his house from top to bottom, and he knew, that going down the stairs would have been as much of a signal to any intruder than a foghorn to their ear, and therefore, he stood, parched as the top of the staircase, waiting for anyone whom would dare to come up. This floor was clear of any such evil, and therefore Charlie had decided that whomever was in his house could do anything he wished downstairs, but not up. His heartbeat raced, the sound of which to him, was just as loud as walking down the stairs would have been, and then suddenly he sensed movement. The corner of his eye clocked a black figure shifted from the kitchen to the living room area, before it stopped. Charlie, frozen to the top of the stairs prayed under his breath for a retreat from the intruder, but his prayers were not answered in any way whatsoever. The black figure made movements towards the stairs before looking up the staircase, as he was also clearly hesitant to how much noise the wooden steps would create, but before he could land a sole on the wood, Charlie leapt to his feet. "Don't you dare come up here whoever you are!" It was an act of adrenaline which then placed the two figures at a standstill. The intruder stood silently. "The police are on their way pal, just leave..." But the black figure did not move, as Charlie didn't know his next move. He had no idea how long the police would take to get here...nor did he know if the thief was armed. Dunn lifted the black baseball bat into sight, making sure the mystery figure knew that he was carrying, but once again, there was no movement. Panic began to set into Dunn's senses, before he quickly turned around. But it was too late. Another figure pushed Dunn toppling down the stairs, the noise of which was enough to wake what would seem the neighbors. The masked man at the bottom of the stairs looked down at Dunn, before looking up at his accomplice in anger. "What the fuck have you done?!" The attacker slowly made his way down the stairs, stuffing something into his back pocket as he did so. "We got what we came for, so let's get out of here." He continued down the stairs and out the door, leaving the initial intruder standing over Charlie Dunn's motionless body, before catching movement at the top of the stairs, as Dunn's wife looked down in horror, before finally screaming at the top of her lungs for help. The masked man quickly escaped, and wondered off into the night as Margaret quickly paced the steps and dove to her husband, who lay on his stomach, his mouth trickling blood. She looked at her soul mate as tears ran down her cheeks, before catching the culprit to his state. The knife which was etched in his spine. The knife which had ended Charles Dunn's life. But why?
