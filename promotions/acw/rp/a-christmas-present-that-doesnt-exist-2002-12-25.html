A Christmas Present: That doesn't exist. Written: 12/25/02 It was Christmas time. A time to spend with the family, giving love and cherishing each and every one of them as you give them a prized something that they truly wish for. Ivan was back at his home for the Christmas holiday after the major event that had taken place and many could say is jump starting his major push to the main event. Yet there was one person who believed other wise and that being his son. Ivan sat in his lounge chair watching his son rip into his presents hoping for the one thing that he truly desired, but the fact is Ivan nor his mother could find that present. He wasn’t going to get it because it didn’t exist. It only existed in his son’s eyes and yet how was he going to explain that to his son. His son was down to his last present. “I bet I know what this is.” His son said in an overwhelmed look on his face as he just ripped the present open in a rush, but the smile on his face quickly turned upside down as he realized he didn’t get what he wanted most. “I hate you!” He screamed in dissatisfaction for his family, but more to his father. The reason was because he knew his father was never there in the last two years, he knew that his dad had beat him for no apparent reason, and now his dad had beat down the one person that he looked up to. It wasn’t his dad that he looked up to. Why would he look up to his dad who beat him and who was never there. He looked up to Osyrus. A man that showed power and strength but Ivan knew better. Osyrus was nothing but an unbarring asshole that only thought about him. He didn’t care for his son liking him, nor did he even want a fan or a person who looked up to him. All he wanted to do was hurt and brutally beat each and every person that lived on the face of the earth. So he had power, and that’s all he cared about. If you had power then he would side on your side. Just like he was with Ethan. Ethan had most of the power in ACW and that’s what you see Osyrus on his side, because he can’t fend for himself. His power lies in the people he hangs around with, and Ivan knew that. He knew Osyrus didn’t have any power and it was only a matter of time before he proved just that.
