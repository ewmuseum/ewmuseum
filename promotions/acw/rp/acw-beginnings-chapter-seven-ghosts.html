What this is Chapter Seven of “ACW Beginnings”, the serial the fed published before it reopened (per its own history page), as captured from the rebuilt site in the Wayback on 15 July 2004. Only chapters Two and Seven were captured. The chapter menu is not reproduced.
Chapter Seven - [ Ghosts. ] "Ladies and Gentleman, we shall now commence the auction of Brian Carter's assets." SilverHAWK and Jimmy Gonz took to their seats at the back of the auction hall, HAWK quickly scanned the hall for anyone that he could recognize. SilverHAWK had made the journey from Munich, where ACW was currently on the last part of their European tour, before the British leg. The first few auctions passed, with their prospective buyers rather happy about their new purchases, but it was clear that many of the assembled had come for the businesses which Carter had a large state in. SilverHAWK flicked through the auction booklet, and seen that ACW was next. "The next item is number 435-432, and it is majority stake in the wrestling federation, All-Star Championship Wrestling. This has been going on and off for 14 years now, and we will begin the bid, for Mr. Carter's shares at $2 million." As the room sat silent, a board was lifted in the air and the auctioneer responded by nodding his head. "We have a bid of $2million." SilverHAWK moved his head, so that he could have a view of the bidder, it was a woman, but before he could make a comment to Gonz, another board was lifted, and this time it was someone he did know. "$2.5million." William Laguna. The man whom HAWK had only met at Charlie Dunn's funeral, was now bidding for the federation. Aaron was slightly taken a back by the situation, and as he looked at Gonz, the woman made another bid. "$3million." Laguna gave a dry smile in direction of the lady, before nodding at the auctioneer once again. "$3.5million from that gentleman over there." The lady turned her head and shot Laguna a blank stare, before continuing the bidding war, but taking it to another level. "$5million." The room gasped, as did the auctioneer, as his collar was suddenly digging into his Adam's apple, as it seemed that the eyes of the room were all placed on Laguna. And Laguna responded. "$6million." Jimmy Gonz looked down the aisle, the ladies face ringed a bell, but to be fair, there wasn't a female face in America that didn't ring a bell somewhere for Jimmy Gonz. SilverHAWK kept his eyes locked on Laguna, watching him as he looked smugly at anyone who seemed to catch his eye. It seemed that a large number in the hall didn't want anything to do with a wrestling federation, they wanted a profitable business, and wrestling was dying...right? "The bid is $6million, does anyone want to make an advance on six?" The female bidder pondered for a moment, which seemed like an hour to everyone else in the room, but not before long, she made contact with the auctioneer. And shook her head. "Going once." Laguna shook hands with his two associates either side of him. "Going twice." SilverHAWK looked at yet another, new boss. "Sold, for $6million to that gentlemen in a blue suit" Only moment later, a staff member from the auction house gave Laguna a sheet of paper, which contained the information needed to finish the deal, and then everything would be set. As the next lot started, Laguna began to make his way to the door, but as he did, he locked eyes with HAWK, who sat two rows over from him. Neither man budged, as Laguna swiftly moved out of the auction house, as Gonz kept his eye on the lady bidder, and overheard her speak. "Oh well Alana, there is always next time..." The lady nodded, as Gonz clenched his fists as the face finally fitted a name. "Winters." SilverHAWK turned around quizzically. "What did you say?" "Winters, Alana Winters, I knew I had seen her somewhere." HAWK shook his head, trying to dust off the mental cobwebs. "Seen who?" Gonz pointed. "The bidder...that's Alana Winters, you know, the daughter of..." SilverHAWK cut him off. "Ethan Winters."
