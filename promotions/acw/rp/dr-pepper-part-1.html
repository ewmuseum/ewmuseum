Dr Pepper!? Part 1 Written: ‘Who could of thought? Who could of thought this would all return? All the dreams and aspirations we have known of have returned. Well we have to thank someone. Yes Dr Pepper.’ The scene now suddenly opens with the 400lb + Ron Williams walking into the picture clutching a large can of Dr Pepper in his clammy mitts. He is wearing a Santa Clause hat and is singing and dancing. He excitedly enters the entrance of a large hospital where he approaches the Reception desk with huge anticipation. ‘ Is he here!? Is Dr Pepper here!? ,Ron clambers nearly onto the desk, spit flaring from his large gaping mouth. ‘Is who here!?’ Impatiently and showered in spit the women quite agitated gives a solemn response to the overly eager Ron. ‘Dr Pepper. THE MAN WHO FIXED MY BARRY!!!!’ Ron pulls the rubber chicken from his pocket which seems to have had its head stitched on. ‘What…what is that!?’ The women points at Barry half stunned half terrified. Surely she doesn’t think its real?. Ron stares behind him almost oblivious that she is actually making reference to Barry. ‘NOOO….THAT!!!!’ The women touches the end of Barry’s nose and jumps back almost like she is expecting the texture of his body to be different. ‘Ohhhh its Barry!!!! Don’t touch what you cant afford you silly women! Ner ner ner ner ner!!! I have a chicken and you dooooooooooooont!’ Ron teases the women childlike who seems unimpressed with the large mans presence. ‘Can I please tell you to give me the information you need because I have work I need to take care of.’ The women is highly impatient now and seems ready to crack under the obvious pressure from the annoying Ron Williams. ‘I want Dr Pepper. The guy who made my best friend Barry better you willy head!’ Ron points at the women’s head and pretends to make a penis on it. Commotion grows surrounding them. Some people laugh loudly which turns the women a crimson red colour. Others try and stop Ron from making a mockery of this defenceless women. ‘We don’t have a Dr Pepper working here! Now please leave before I call security.’ The women is now half in tears as she screams vocally towards the comedian known as Ron. ‘Ok lady calm them passions. You got your knickers on the wrong way round today? I bet you wear them Grandma knickers don’t you!? You know them big ones like grocery bags? But I think ill go now. Tell Dr Pepper I will come later. Your Security guard has a bigger head than someone from Theme Hospital and I don’t want him to use it on me! BLOATED HEAD BABY!!’ Ron walks out of the hospital imitating someone with a large head as the scene fades to black…
