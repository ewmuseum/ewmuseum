Neophyte part 3 - Defiance

19 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Neo Xealot Akuma, Djinn, Akira Neophyte part 3 - Defiance Location: Los Angeles, CA Date: September 19, 2004 Number (Archive): 17 Number (Wrestler): 3 “Can you tell me about our mother again Kaz?” “Sure. I remember her being a quiet woman and very beautiful. I never met my father.” “Why not?” “He died a couple of days before I was born.” “Sorry…” “Yeah… Anyway, my mom was Chinese born but brought up in the ways of Bushido. During her time in America she became a Christian and practised in secret upon her return. She gave me both Western and Eastern names in remembrance of my heritage. Kazuya was the name I was called, but she also named me after an angel.” “Raziel, right? What does that mean anyway?” “Secret of God.” “Oh.” “…You look just like her you know.” “Really?” “Yeah.” “Then she must have been beautiful!” “True… and to add to that, grandfather says that our mother had strange powers.” “What sort of powers?” “He said that she would ‘talk to the walls’ as a baby and when she got older, she could see the ‘true beauty’ of those she happened to look upon. I noticed as a boy that she never ever used the word ‘if’ in her time and it was as though everything she said about the future was a certainty to her.” “Did she say anything about me?” “It was on her deathbed. She told me that one day I would find great power and then moments before she died with you in her arms, she told me that when you are in trouble I must use my power to protect you… Kin… what’s wrong?” “I’m crying you fool! That’s the most beautiful story I ever heard.” “Oh… sorry.” “Don’t be sorry Kaz! Did you meet my father?” “Yes. He was our grandfather’s right-hand man and I spoke to him many times before you were born. He, however, also disappeared before his child – you - was born and that fulfilled the Prophecy…” “Prophecy?” “You mean, you don’t know about the Prophecy?” “Nope.” “Did grandpa not teach you anything?” “…” “When he was a little boy, an old woman told him that his daughter would give birth to two children… a Child of the West which represents chaos and a Child of the East which represents order. She said that together the two children would form an unstoppable force. Because my father Michael Leonard was American, grandpa believes that I am the Child of the West. Your father was Chinese so you are thereby the Child of the East.” “You’re making this up right.” “Swear to God. Grandpa believes that you and I form the circle because we are so different yet so close. From the day you were born, we were both raised to create this culmination – this circle and union.” “I’m not with you.” “Like it or not Kinjite… you are with me. There is a reason why you cannot tolerate my gravitation toward chaos. A reason why I have become your guardian. Everything has a reason.” “Well I don’t like the reason.” “Neither do I, Kin… And that is why I do what I do.” … “You really hate grandpa don’t you?” “I know how you feel about him, Kinjite. But he raised you infinitely better than he ever raised me. Look what I have become. I have an IQ of 182 and unlimited athletic potential. What am I because of him? I’m a weapon, Kin, a killing machine. Nothing else.” “I don’t see you that way.” “Well you’re the only one who fucking doesn’t.” “Shouldn’t that matter to you?” “Why? Why should that fucking matter to me? Tell me.” “Because I care, Kaz.” “I know you do and I’m sorry but that’s not enough.” “What’s enough? When does it end?” “When grandpa is dead.” “…Dead?” “Dead.”
