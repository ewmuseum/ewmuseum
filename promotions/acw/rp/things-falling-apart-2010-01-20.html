Things Falling Apart

20 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 20, 2010 The feeling of euphoria that had consumed Wilson since hearing the bell ring and having the title handed to him wasn't quite gone. Instead he had slowly become tired. Worn out from the night and the tension leading up the match. Then watching End Game to scope out the competition and see who was coming for his title. Midnight Cowboy and Khristain Keller. Interesting names in an awkward situation, but Wilson already had a plan for how that would end. "There's something we need to discuss." Wilson ran a hand through his hair and was unsatisfied that he was still sweating a bit. "I know we've been hitting peaks ever since I brought you two in, but we're in a different place now. I never thought Spaes would fall like that, and I should've been smarter about who I trusted. That's different now." Stevenson and Kati sat cross-legged next to each other on the locker room floor while their leader sat on the bench. "Have you tried finding him?" Wilson chuckled and shook his head. "No need. The man was like a brother to me once. Now he's a black sheep at best. I want you to know that once we hit Europe we're going to continue this path we're on. I'm seeing things five moves ahead for the first time in a long time and it feels good. Now that I've got this we can begin our slow climb to the top." Wilson pulled the title belt close to him and spread it over his lap. He smiled and knocked the main plate of the belt. "That's actually a problem. You're talking about plans for "us" but you're assuming there's going to be three of us. There isn't." The smile faded on Wilson's face and he looked up to what he hoped was a joke, but instead saw Stevenson standing up. "I'm out Trevor. This has been great and all, but I'm not jumping across the pond." "It's just new ground for us to capitalize on. This doesn't need to be a big thing." "It is, and I know what you're doing. Stop feeling guilty. You owed us last winter, but now we're square. Any debt you may have felt hanging over your head is long gone brother. That's for the both of us. It's time to move on, Trevor." "You're leaving too?" Kati looked at both her partner and her leader before shrugging. "No. I'm coming along for the ride. I knew he was going to leave, but I was always going to stay. This is for the best." Wilson stood up and still had a face of disbelief. He dropped the title to the bench and paid no attention to the clanking noise it made when it slipped and hit the floor. "That's it then." "Yeah. That's it. Give 'em hell in Europe."
