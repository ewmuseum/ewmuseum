Almost Famous - Part One

2 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 02, 2010 Oso Bannon had been in the same holding cell for thirty six hours and still the only question he could ask himself was how he’d managed to get himself there. The looks of adoration and inquisitions about past games coming from the guards who were keeping watch over him were enough to reaffirm his feeling that he wasn’t meant for this. He was a superstar. Cheered by basically a whole state collectively for three, almost four years. They weren’t worried about him taking drugs when he was giving them five sacks a game. They didn’t care that he needed painkillers just to get out of bed as long as he was showing up on Saturdays. Then a routine traffic stop led to him getting busted and all of a sudden everyone had a problem with the man the television and magazines referred to triumphantly as Bear. Everyone except for these guards. “Man, I’ll never forget when you got to Stanza four times last year against Iowa.” one exclaimed excitedly in Oso’s direction, “And then you got him a fifth and he couldn’t even come back in the game!” The other guard chimed in, “Just ruthless!” The first guard continued, “Trust me, Bannon. I deal with countless gorillas with bad attitudes in here everyday. We do plenty of things, some that might be frowned upon, to get one over on them. If I were in charge of things I’d let you pump yourself full of whatever drugs you wanted. No fucks given if you’re helping the Bucks win a national championship!” “Well...” Oso finally spoke, his deep voice calm. Almost too calm for a man who was under arrest and couldn’t afford to post bail. “...I wish the federal government felt the same way you do." “Damn country’s going down the toilet!” The guard rages. “Terrorists and gangbangers running free everywhere and we’re locking up the only guys who help us find our only solaces. Ass backwards, if you ask me!” The other guard mimics, “Ass backwards!” A loud, annoying buzzer sounds throughout the holding cells and a dull voice comes over the loud speakers. “VISITOR FOR OSO BANNON. PREPARE FOR ENTRY.” The clicking and clacking of doors and locks. Footsteps, faint at first, but getting louder by the second. Another “celebrity” enters and faces the imprisoned Bear. A jovial voice... “Hello, Oso. Remember me?”
