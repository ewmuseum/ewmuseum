Played. Written: 1/3/03 In the background stood a dance-club by the name of Klub-187, a hot new joint for teens 21 and older to dance, and straight out chill. Allen stared down at his golden Rolex watch-piece. It read 8:10 PM. Allen thought to himself in a regrettable manner, “Dayum, if this hoe wasn’t so fine I would of hopped into my Escalade and been out here before this piece said 8:01. What a brotha will do to get some ass.” He looked at the crowd of people, screaming like barbarians trying to get into this hot new location. He smirked, but remembered his agitated state, looking straight back out to the road. Being bored with time, he remembered back to the moment where he met this mysterious but fine-looking diva. He remembered back to dinner two nights ago, at Hyde Park, an expensive, high-class restaurant where only the luxurious would go to dine. Allen sat by himself, preparing to receive a T-Bone steak he had ordered almost 15 minutes prior to his entrance. Allen licked his lips, while almost like prey, he set his eyes on a female, sitting two tables down from his own. Allen stalked her with his eyes, almost making sure she could see his fly status. He licked his lips slowly and seductively, not missing a single spot, and not even caring if he were to. She looked at him, without being able to help herself from a smile. Allen smirked in a cocky manner, while his food was served. Almost halfway through his dinner, it didn’t take very long for Allen to get another peak across the restaurant at the extravagant looking woman. She couldn’t handle it any more, so she excused herself from her table, walking towards the ladies restroom, but taking Allen’s route. She passed by him, letting him smell the Vicky Secret coming from her radiant glow. He closed his eyes, knowing his prey had just been caught, but the kill was yet to come. He opened back up his two brown eyes before excusing himself from a table of one, to go into the men’s room, ironically right across the hall from the woman’s. He walked in, defying any respect for woman anywhere. The woman was not doing anything, but waiting for his arrival. There, Allen got a close up glimpse. She was beautiful. Caramel skin, a red-dress just so low that she wouldn’t be considered a whore based on appearance, and a knock-out body. Allen bit his finger, before walking up to this woman. At this point, he would be a fool not to. “Yo yo YO! Whasup baby? You goin’ let a nigga hit or what? I gots to admit though, you had a nigga trippin wit that lil walk you busted out.” The woman looked at him as if he were a 18-year-old cocky child trying to spit game. “Are you serious?” Allen looked at her as if she were crazy. “Bitch I’m as serious as a fuggin’ heart attack, I knew you was feelin this nigga right here, so as I was sayin’, when you bout’ to let a brotha hit?” She sighed him off as if he were a little boy asking for a pony in his back-yard for his birthday. “Who are you calling a bitch first of all?” Allen was stunned that she wasn’t taking his constant shouts as compliments. “I said YOU FLY! I ain’t even MEAN it like dat kid! You is a DYME! DYME STATUS BABY! I ain’t even mean to cause no harm, I’m just tryin to get on!” Allen could see that she wasn’t buying into one word he tried to sell. “Aiight then, AIIGHT. Lemme get dem’ digits, and I’ll call, so YO’ ass can have tha time of yo life some other night!” The woman smirked. “You’re cute. So I’ll tell you what.” She walked up to Allen, in her own smooth, seductive manner. “Go ahead, and in two days, meet me at the place next to Klub-187, at about 7:30. I got tha place for you.” Allen looked at her as if he was a pimp. “I gotchu girl, couldn’t get off of this dick anyways. I gotchu. Two days? It’s on then. I’ll holla.” Allen walks out of the bathroom, looking at some overweight chick just walking in. “Do ya bidness girl, cuz I JUST did mines.” Allen continues his pimp strides. “Caramel”, still being inside the bathroom smiles, before walking out herself to get back to her own date. Allen snaps out of his lil remembrance. “DAYUM, shorty was fine. If she was somebody else I’d beeen gone now, but for dat phat ass I saw at ‘park, I’ll freeze my ass off in tha cold for a few more minut…..” Before Allen can continue, his brain alerts him of the remembrance. “…meet me at the place next to Klub-187…” Allen snaps his head over about 20 yards from where the hip-hop, outrageous Klub-187 is, to see a familiar place, one where Allen used to go for parties. “Dayum.” Allen walked away to get back into his Caddy Escalade, ignoring his invitation to go into Chucky Cheese.
