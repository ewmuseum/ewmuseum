Whatever Happened... To The Quintessential Rising Star? - Prologue; Chapter 0, Part 3

27 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 27, 2010 ACW COURAGE 04 NOVEMBER 2004 “A-C-W! A-C-W! A-C-W!” If you, like most of the fans in the arena, are trying to figure out WTF just happened, allow me to explain. Seymour Almasy leapt backwards off the top rope for his Super Gagazet Driver. In mid-air, Quinton May managed to kick his legs free, causing his body to rotate downwards, land reasonably vertically, and Thunderfire Powerbomb Seymour to the canvas in one fluent motion. Got it? Don’t worry, neither do I. The one thing that all of the fans COULD agree on, however, was that Seymour Almasy was DONE. FINISHED. TOE TAGGED. If Quinton May could cover, he could get a one-hundred count. It’s a funny word though, that ‘if’. Two letters, but such a very big word indeed. Quinton was in no position to cover. Hell, Quinton was in no position to do pretty much anything. He was crumpled in a heap next to Seymour, motionless. The crowd couldn’t believe it. All they could do now was support their favorite, and do their best to help him block out the pain, help him will himself to his feet. The referee looked on in shock, as the crowd now joined the battle, dueling chants rising above the din. “SEY-MOUR!” “QUIN-TON!” “SEY-MOUR!” “QUIN-TON!” “SEY-MOUR!” “QUIN-TON!” And as fate would have it, it was “QUIN-TON!” who managed to move first, and gracelessly threw an arm over Almasy’s broken body. The Quinton supporters let out a sigh of relief. The Seymour supporters hoped for a miracle. ONE! TWO! No one would blame Almasy for not being able to get up. He’d fought for twenty minutes against one of the best ACW had to offer, and had taken a horrific beating, while giving one out in return. No one, except himself. … KICKOUT?! The referee couldn’t believe it. The fans couldn’t believe it. Quinton May couldn’t believe it. Hell, even Seymour himself couldn’t believe it. For his fans, the season of miracles had come early. May once again was forced to rack his brain. Not able to come up with anything substantive immediately, he went to Irish whip Seymour to the ropes, but to his shock, the battered and bruised Final Fantasy reversed the whip. Seymour crouched, summoning up his power, whatever he had left, to try and take Quinton’s head off with one final blow. LARIAT… MISSED! Ducking under the clothesline, Quinton leaped up to the second rope, and twisted in mid-air, coming back to strike Almasy with a clothesline of his own. Finally, inspiration struck May, as he laid on the mat face down after the move. Finally, he had the weapon with which he would end the contest, and dispel his doubts. Wearily, Quinton picked Almasy up, pushing the Final Fantasy’s head between his legs, and double underhooked Seymour. May’s hands reached forward, grabbing the back of Almasy’s armor-like tights, preparing to put Almasy away. HIDEAWAY~! … Or not. May found Almasy frantically pulling back in an effort to block the move, stomping his feet, shaking his arms, and generally doing anything he could to disrupt Quinton’s move. Somehow, Seymour managed to wriggle his arms free, and swept out both of May’s legs. This was most likely Almasy’s last chance. He leaped over most of May’s body, grabbing the legs as he did so, and landed hard across the shoulders and chest of the Rising Star. Almasy pulled May’s legs over his shoulders as he did so, the pinning predicament looking much like the end of a rana rollup as Seymour pulled as much of his and May’s weight as he could over Quinton’s shoulders. The official dropped to count, as May frantically kicked, trying to extricate himself from the predicament. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! … Unfortunately for Quinton, it was a kickout that was too late. The official’s hand had slapped the mat for the three fractions of a second before May kicked free. And as the referee called for the bell, Quinton May knew that he had lost. Again. *DING DING DING!* "THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH... SEYYYYYYMOOOOOOOURRRR ALMASSSSSY!" the ring announcer boomed, he himself a fair bit exhausted just by watching what had transpired in the ring. For his own part, Seymour pumped his fist in the air, before collapsing to the mat from exhaustion. Both men lay still, trying to catch their breath after the incredible contest, as the crowd rained cheers down on both. Slowly, Almasy surged up to one knee, allowing the official to grab his left wrist, and raise the arm in the air, signaling him as the winner of the contest. Seymour couldn’t believe it. Last week, he had suffered one of the most humiliating defeats of his career. This week, he managed to come through with his biggest win to date. It was amazing how wrestling worked sometimes. If Seymour was elated, Quinton May was absolutely disgusted. Losing sucked. Losing after twenty-five minutes when your opponent countered your finisher sucked even more. He’d been outsmarted, and that thought tore away at him as he tried to stand. But considering the looking on Quinton's face, there was something else that troubled the Canadian Gladiator. By the time he made his way to his feet, he saw Seymour standing, looking at him, hand extended in a show of respect. May paused for a moment, snorted derisively, and turned away, rolling out of the ring with his head hung low. Almasy was somewhat upset, but shrugged it off, and wearily ascended the turnbuckles, arms raised in the air as the crowd cheered. Now, all Seymour had to do was keep winning, and the gold that he sought would eventually be his. Of course, in ACW, that’s easier said than done. *** TSC TUESDAY NIGHT WRESTLING #32 09 NOVEMBER 2004 Ever feel like you just want to give up? Like, you really can't be arsed anymore? That was how Quinton May felt, as he continued to stare at the floor. Having gone through absolute hell and back for the majority of the last couple of months, Quincy Mama now felt that fighting the good fight was not worth it anymore. And this, from a man who had just saved himself from spiralling further into the darkness that was slowly engulfing him. Boooo. Know what's worse than a man who's bitter and develops a meanstreak to unleash his frustrations? A man who just can't seem to care anymore. And that was how Quinton was at the moment. There was no happiness in him. Neither was there any anger. What was in abundance, though, was... emptiness. A huge void, stinging as much as a howling abyss. And have you ever heard an abyss howl? I haven't, but Charles Gunn has, and if he says it's bad... then I'm inclined to believe that badass. Anyways, as stated, Quinton wasn't doing anything except staring at the floor. Observing the specks of dust slowly but surely settling on the floor. Exciting stuff, innit? Yes indeedy, nothing more enthralling than watching dust float around in the air. He wasn't alone on this night, though. Oh, no. Ever since the love of his life came back into the fold, Quincy has had another strong pillar of support to lean on. Some would say the ORIGINAL pillar of support, since the saying is -- behind every successful man is a successful woman. And, hey, Linda raising baby Dylan all on her own, in this day of age? I'd say that's pretty successful. "Do you want something to drink, Quinton?" Linda asked softly, snapping Quinton out of his trance. Linda was mighty concerned about Quincy's broodiness over the last nine days, and hoped he would get over this funk he had found himself in. Non-talky Quinton isn't a real treat to hang around with. May looked up at Linda and smiled thinly. "No, it's alright. Thanks anyway." And then? The bastard resumed his oh-so-fun observation of the floor. Linda simply nodded and shuffled out of the room, leaving Quincy Mama to his own devices. If he wasn't going to talk, then Linda figured that there was no use trying to coax him to do otherwise, or any other thing. Guess he just needs... more time to himself, Linda pondered to herself. Closing the door of the room behind her, Linda sighed and folded her arms as she leaned herself against a wall. For the last nine days, ever since S&S, Quinton hadn't said much. He was seemingly haunted by the loss to Santos Salvatore, although for the life of her, Linda couldn't understand why. Someone else who didn't get it was Bruno. Yes, he too was present on this night. And like Linda, who were told by Quincy not to accompany him down to the ring & stay there at S&S, Bruno had no clue as to why Quinton was so shaken up over this defeat. Yeah, it's a bitter pill to swallow... but the Canadian was acting as if the loss to Santos had some major impact. This was probably because Quinton didn't tell either Linda or Bruno about what Santos had said to the Rising Star once the match was done and over with. You know, about JULY 20. Oh, and maybe the so-called son of his most hated rival (who was presumed dead) coming back to seek revenge? That probably had something to do with Quinton's funky mood. Quinton had not told anybody the beating he'd received at Alexander Pembridge's hands a while back. No sir, he kept it all to himself. And as such, there was a lot resting on his shoulders. Quinton wondered whether it was a good idea being in both ACW and TSC. Not only was it exhausting, but wherever Quincy went... trouble seemed to follow. Anywho, Bruno had returned from... wherever the fuck he was, and like Linda, had a concerned look on his face. Oh, and a piece of paper in his hand. Linda heard Bruno approaching and promptly peeled herself off of the wall. The two of 'em had been discussing over what had Quincy so down in the dumps, and as expected, neither could derive at a sensible outcome. "Hey. Our gloomy guy talking yet?" Bruno asked of Linda. Who shook her head dejectedly, pretty much summing it allll up. Bruno sighed as he screeched to a halt. This was bad. "I did ask him if he wanted something to drink, and he did actually respond. Instead of, you know, ignoring us... like he did the other day when you asked him if he wanted a massage. Of course, you could chalk THAT down to him probably wanting a woman's touch in that area." Linda shot a playful smile to Bruno, who grinned back sheepishly. Truth be told, Bruno was glad that Linda and Quinton had reconciled their differences and their animosities once and for all. The two former lovers were now starting over, as friends, and in Bruno's opinion, it was the right call on both Quincy's and Linda's part. Absolutely. Thing was, with this latest hiccup in Quinton retreating into his own private shell and acting as if there was nothing to care about, Bruno figured that this whole thing could possibly end up wrecking Linda's and Quinton's relationship. Again. And Bruno knew how much the wacky Canadian cared for Ms Connolly. If this funk May was experiencing ended up damaging his relationship with Linda once more... ... Bruno was certain that Quinton would probably snap and become a demented, blubbering maniac. Or something close to it. No way in hell did Bruno want matters to spiral out of control that much. "Least he's talking now. The first few days there, it was scary. He would just tremble for no reason, and keep his mouth shut. As if he was hiding a diamond inside there. Ahh, I think he's just really taking this to heart. It's been a tough couple of months for him, see. Guess the loss to Santos has more or less made him worried." Bruno analysed stoically. "Worried?" Linda echoed, a little puzzled. "What has he got to be worried about? That his career is going to go down the drain just because of one loss? I'm very confused over the whole thing, as you can imagine. Have no idea about the ins and outs of wrestling. I just... watch it sometimes. Mainly because of Dylan. He likes to see his fath... Quinton in action. Other than that, though, I can't claim to know why one defeat would affect him so adversely. Him not talking doesn't help me understand." Bruno nodded, able to feel the waves of desperation enemating from Linda. She didn't really know much about the Alexander Pembridge stuff. Bruno figured the less she knew, the better it was. "I know, sweetheart. And trust me, it's all very complicated. Quinton's a complicated man. Let's just say, he thinks that with this loss... there's reason to worry over his place in this world. Because every time everything seems to be a-okay, somebody with a grudge appears and takes him out. It's happening both here and in ACW. He's always managed to fend off those enemies. One way or another. Not Santos, though. Hence, the worry. Although, really, he shouldn't be acting out like this. I only fear that the contents of this piece of paper... could only make matters worse." "Why's that? What's the paper say?" Linda immediately queried, her curiousity peaked. Like the lady said, she didn't know a hell of a lot about wrestling, but Bruno was doing a damn good job explaining everything to her. Everything she needed to know, at least. And if the paper could prove a further detriment, then Linda definitely needed to know what was up. Fast. Bruno, with one last apprehensive glance at the paper, cleared this throat. "Quincy has a match tonight. Apparently, he's been drafted into this King of Submission tournament that consists of 16 of the wrestlers currently on the roster, and May's one of them. Trouble lies with who his opponent is. It's... well, somebody that Quinton isn't too fond of. I am not sure why he doesn't like Christopher, but Quint did tell me that he really dislikes how Christopher... gets allll the limelight. This is going to be very bad, I think." Linda wanted to open her mouth to ask something, but she stopped herself. Christopher. The name sounded familiar enough to her, and with good reason. She knew about May's dislike about a man named Christopher; it had been that way for welll over a year now. The Canadian simply chose not to divulge it to anyone. "Oh, dear." Linda muttered, as she finally remembered. "I hope I'm wrong here, but by Christopher, do you mean the other competitor who works for both the Squared Circle and ACW? Christopher as in... Alias?" BINGO. Bruno was a bit surprised that Linda knew, but he nodded his head. Just as the door of the room swung open and startled both Bruno and Linda. Quinton, tight-lipped and all, had a slightly agitated scowl on his face now. Improvement from there being no hint of expression at all, just a few minutes ago. But, ya know why he was scowling? Quincy has quite the heightened sense of hearing. And although Bruno and Linda were practically whispering to each other, Quinton had heard what Bruno had just told Linda, about the Rising Star's match for the night. "I can get you out of the match, Quint. On my way to do that just now!" Bruno quickly offered, but was halted by Quincy Mama raising his hand up in the air. And as if things couldn't potentially become worse, May just had to say it. "No. I'll fight the supposed Original Pulp Hero tonight. It'll be good therapy after the last few weeks I've had. Also? It's been a long time coming, him and me. A very long time coming. Had to happen sooner or later, I guess." Folks? If I were you... I'd seek shelter somewhere far away. Because when Quincy steps into the ring tonight with Alias, there will only be one thing on May's mind: ending the legacy of Christopher Declan Sheffield. No two ways about it. *** ACW COURAGE 04 NOVEMBER 2004 "Quinton! Hey, Quinton!" SilverHAWK shouted, watching as the Canadian Gladiator walked down the hallway quickly. "Wait up, will you? We need to talk!" May had his bag and his belongings in his grasp as he turned a corner. SilverHAWK silently cursed the former Television Champion for being such a fast walker. HAWK had wanted to ask if Quinton was alright, considering he hadn't really looked like his usual self the whole day and the reaction at the end of the Seymour/Quinton match earlier was not one HAWK had seen very often from the young superstar. So, imagine the big man's surprise when he rounded the same corner Quinton did earlier and found that May had breezed through the exit of the arena. In fact, HAWK could see Quincy enter a taxi cab. HAWK was confused. "He's leaving? Running away?" the big man muttered to himself. His legs tried to move, but SilverHAWK remained rooted to the spot. His mind told him to run after Quinton, but the legs just wouldn't move. And away Quinton May went, as the taxi-cab drove away hurriedly. All that was left to SilverHAWK was questions. One of them being whether Quinton would return, or if he was gone for good. The other one being -- why and just what the heck was going on? *** TSC TUESDAY NIGHT WRESTLING #32 09 NOVEMBER 2004 On a night of utter mayhem thus far, we were about to witness... an interview! Nikki was in position, microphone and all. Giant smile? You said it. The person she was about to interview? It was the same man that scared the bejesus out of her on TNW 31, but Nikki had no clue that Quinton May was the scary-looking green freak that she encountered on that fateful night. Of course, May was not in the mood to tell her, anyways. And hey, look who was standing next to Quincy. NO ONE. That's right, the Canadian Gladiator told Bruno (manager) and Linda (love interest and now a friend) to stay in the locker-room. Simply because Quincy was going to expose a side of himself many had never seen. "Hello, one and all!" Nikki exclaimed, beaming from ear to ear. Crazy bitch. "I'm standing next to Quinton May, who is now in the midst of a winless streak which was further compounded by his loss to Santos Salvatore, at SINNERS & SAINTS. Tonight, Quinton has the chance to make ammends by competing in the KING OF SUBMISSIONS 204 tournament! And his opponent just so happens to be ALIAS! YAY! I like Alias. I think he's cool. But anywho, it'll be the battle of two men who also compete in ACW, and have done so for over a year and a half now. Amazing that they've never fought before, huh? Such an honour to host their virgin standoff here, in tSC. Before we touch on the upcoming KOS Preliminary between yourself and Alias, though, I'd like to ask you something Quinton. It is rumoured that Santos said something to you after winning the match nine days ago. What did he say that has gotten you in such a loop, and do you intend to once again challenge the only man who has beaten you TWICE?" The Canadian Gladiator, standing to the left of Nikki, had his arms folded and a nasty scowl on his face. Bruno and Linda? I already said. Not by his side; Quincy had requested for them to stay behind in the locker-room. To shield them. From what? The impending verbal assault he was going to spew. "Forget about Santos for a second, Nikki. I don't want to talk about him. I'd rather address something you just said a few moments ago, which caught my interest. You say you think Alias is cool, huh? Know what I think, Nikki? I think... Alias is absolutely FULL OF SHIT." Oh, ho. Hoooo. Snap? Nikki and the rest of the watching world were completely flabbergasted. One particular Alias fan, who was a five-year old boy that had been diagnosed with 'Cryalot syndrome' just a few years back' started sobbing his pathetic heart out. Quinton? He just smirked. It wasn't a cool smirk; not cool enough to warrant a tilde and stuff, but you get the feeling that the Canadian Gladiator was pleased with saying what he just said. Nikki finally composed herself and looked a wee bit angry. "Why would you say a thing like that? Alias is cool! He was our first champion, and defended that title with honour & valour! He is the embodiement for everything tSC. And, hey, wasn't he the one who saved ACW in that LEGENDS 2003 Main Event, huh? Without him, would there even be an ACW?" "First of all, get your fucking facts straight, Nikki." Quinton snapped back, ready to lose it. "Sheffield did NOT save ACW from the grip of Ethan Winters. Had Winters not committed suicide, it wouldn't have mattered if a monkey carrying the Ebola virus had ended up winning the Main Event at LEGENDS 2003. And, the embodiement for everything tSC? My GOD, what a load of bullshit. You are delusional. Everybody out there is fucking delusional. Everywhere I go, I have to hear about Alias. Alias, this. Alias, that. Alias is such a good wrestler. Alias is such a good role model for the kids... even despite being a god-damn chain smoker. Alias is a good lover. YEAH, and how many bastard kids of his are littered around the world, huh? Doesn't one of our referees, Monet whatever, actually have a kid that was fathered by Sheffield? And you condone that, Nikki? Regardless of what a bed-hopper he is, you still think he's cool? OF COURSE YOU DO! Because, he was our first-ever Champion. A day after regaining the ACW World Title. For which, he defeated... uh, SilverHAWK? You're kidding me, right? Aaron J. -- that's SilverHAWK for you clueless ones -- couldn't beat a turkey if his life depended on it. Okay, he did beat Osyrus to win the title this year to begin with, but Osyrus is a piece of garbage, anyways." Quinton stopped and chuckled at the outbreak of jeering. Yes, it was THAT audible. Don't usually associate Quinton May and jeering, but hey, Alias will always be the perennial crowd favourite, right? It didn't matter to the fans that the Canadian also ripped on Osyrus there, but it REALLY didn't matter to Quincy Mama. He knew what he was saying was alllll right. And thus, he continued. "So, okay. The second time he won the ACW World Title, he beat some old man for it. The first time? Ooooh, it was that Best of Seven nonsense with his gal-pal Jason Kain. While the two of them were busy trying to outdo each other for the sake of RATINGS, I went through absolute hell. I had to fight a man who had absolutely nothing to lose. I went to war with Vincent fucking Pembridge, and I nearly... lost all my friends because of him. Turns out, I eventually did, heh. But that was before I slayed the monster. I beat Pembridge at his own game, at KING OF AGES 2003. But did anybody care? NO. They were too busy congratualing Sheffield and Kain for their successful Best of Seven bullshit, aimed to jack up ratings in ACW. They were too busy congratulating Sheffield on winning the ACW World Title, and Kain for having the balls to propose marriage. Whoooop-DEE-dooooo. I almost died beating Pembridge, and they get all the respect? Yeah, okay, that sounds fair. Extremely. Yep. ... Maybe it's just me, but I like to look at every side of the story. Didn't Sheffield land into some trouble with the fWo because of some overdosing issue? But, hey, nobody cares because it's.... dun dun dun, ALIAS! Right? If it was some other fucker, everybody would be calling for his/her head and trying to force him/her out of wrestling. Sheffield, though, gets the special red-carpet treatment. I'm sick of it. I'm sick of people calling Alias the Spirit of 2004, because really, what he has done? Not all that special. Look at how Youngblood easily took Sheffield down and made him look like a rookie. Look at how easily Vince Jacobs beat him for the title at KOA 2004. Spirit of 2004? Original Pulp HERO? Okay, sure. Whatever. Tonight, I'm going to go out there and make sure Christopher leaves the arena a broken man in our KOS Preliminary. I don't care what he has in store for me, or if he's even listening to me. Tonight, I'm going to show that Alias is not all that great. He's not the Spirit of 2004, by any strech of deluded and hoakey imagination. And he sure as hell isn't the embodiement of anything... ... except proof of how we need to use contraceptives more often, for the sake of mankind." Having said his piece and made his point, Quinton snarled at Nikki and left the scene, ready to embark on his mission of beating Alias, snapping his winless streak, and advancing in the King of Submission tournament. Nikki was definitely shocked at all the harsh things that May had to say. Turning around, though, Nikki saw someone in the shadows. Didn't think long for that someone to step out of there and into the spectrum of light. ALIAS it was, smoking a cigarette, and having a totally puzzled look on his face. Guess he *was* listening to everything Quinton had to say about him, eh? Needless to say, Alias was not the least bit happy. With a snort, the former Champion threw his cigarette down onto the floor and crushed it with the heel of his boot, prior to rushing off in the direction opposite of which Quinton headed in. All the while, Nikki just stood there, not quite sure what to make of the whole situation. All she knew was -- Quinton May had gone so completely dark, it's as if he was a different person altogether.
