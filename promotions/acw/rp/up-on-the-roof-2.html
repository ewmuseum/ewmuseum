Up on the roof >2< Written: 12/21/20 Other parts >1< --- A stone fell from the elderly, crumbling rooftop of Scott Perry’s apartment block, his naked cold body stood peering over the edge of the building, his toes no longer touched the floor, he felt weightless as he outreached his arms in a crucifix position. He felt as free as a bird. “Yellow.” He stated numbly, he continued. “I am so yellow.” Scott stepped back from the edge, a tear dripped from his tear duct down his cheek. The tear felt warm on his cold skin, he closed his eyes and more warm liquid seeped from the gaps. Scott had a funny taste in his mouth as the tears dribbled on to his tongue. Scott hugged himself a he turned toward the door. Scott winced in pain as blood trailed behind him. As he looked down at his foot he realised that he had been treading through glass, he limped inside the apartment block and dragged his feet down a flight of stairs, he crossed the hall and entered his apartment, the door was wide open, he did not close it behind him. Scott sat down on his crucifix which was leant up slightly against the back wall, he crossed one leg on to the other and looked at the base of it, glass stuck out from the open wounds which seeped out blood on to the wooden floorboards of his ‘sitting room’. Scott dropped his foot to the floor before pulling his legs on to the crucifix and lying down, Scott put his hands in the air and saw how they shook vigorously from the cold and the pain seething through his body. Scott thought back to last Saturday… --- “I polished the cutlery slowly, precisely, being sure not to leave any crap on them. Carol sure did hate it when I left crap behind on the cutlery. She would whine and bitch about it for hours if I did leave any on.” “As I dried the forks from the big blue tray my eyes began to go blurry, that wasn’t any big deal I guess, I mean that happens a lot with me, my eyes have inflammations on the inside which cause puss to occasionally ooze in to my view, hence the blurring. However, when I blinked hard the blurring didn’t disappear as it usually would. I blinked again and again and again. It got to the point where I dropped the fork and the towel to the floor and began rubbing my eyes and slapping my face in an attempt to get my vision back. I gave up, and, as I did, it returned. I looked down at what was a crate full of wet forks and they were knives. I looked down at the fork that I had dropped. A… Knife…” “What is wrong with me? Why do my eyes deceive me like this? Are these knives of are they forks? I still can’t decide, and so I pick one up. I feel it, turn it, tilt it. It looks and feels like a knife, but moments ago it was a fork, I swear it… It was a fork…” “The towel is a gone, the chef and the other staff are gone, I walk out to where the tables filled with customers where and they were empty, clean an bare. I look at my watch.” “7:26am” “That can’t be… I… I only just started work… It was 8pm only moments ago… How?” “How…?” --- Scott had fallen asleep by now, he was exhausted and there wasn’t long until endgame, Scott knew that he was fighting Geo Vacton who couldn’t possibly be at one-hundred percent, but still, Scott knew that he neither would be at one-hundred-percent. Scott slept hoping to recuperate in time for endGAME, he slept and dreamt dreams that only he could dream… Goodnight.
