'Superstar' Vince Jacobs YOUR GOD, The Reason there is a Show, The Ratings Grabber, Pro Wrestling's Phenomenon Reminiscing – Part 1 Location: The past Date: October 22, 2004 Number (Archive): 20 Number (Wrestler): 1 May 5, 1992 Young Vince Jacobs and young Chris Sheffield was both getting there start in All-Star Championship Wrestling and both impressed the management. Chris had that raw energy that was going to take him far in this sport and Vince had the charisma that you couldn’t teach. He was born with it. Both men wanted to make it big in this sport and they knew that ACW was going to be that place. They had watched guys like SilverHAWK and Joe Bishop tear up the ring and wanted that chance to prove that they to could some day do what these guys had done. It was a long tough journey for both men until that faithful day when they would find out if they would still be working dark matches or on the main roster on television. Both men had been separated by management so that they could rely the news to them along with others who wanted that chance. Management told Vince Jacobs first what his situation would be. “Vince, we are very impressed with your ring ability and the way you handle yourself on the microphone. We would like you to get better in the aerial department but that is something that you will learn in the ring each night when you face ACW’s top talent.” Vince smiled. “You mean..” “Yes Vince, welcome to ACW. You are now a member of the main roster.” Vince shook hands with everyone. He was excited at the news and he wanted to tell everybody. But he also thought about if Chris made it. He knew deep down that Chris had the talent to make it but it could go both ways with management. The men left Vince and went next door to Chris’ room to give him the news. “Chris, you my friend possess something that a lot of athletes don’t have. You have raw talent and heart. You go out there and give it your all and that’s something that will make you a big superstar in years to come. So the management team here at ACW would like to welcome you to the main roster.” Chris couldn’t believe it. His dream had come true. He was going to the big time. “Thank you guys. I am going to work my ass off to make you guys not regret this decision.” “We know you will Chris. Also we would like for you and Vince Jacobs to form a tag team to help out our struggling division. I know this will excite a lot of people. ” The men left Chris’ room as Chris sat down still in shock. Suddenly a knock was heard on the door. In walked Vince Jacobs. “So what’s the news Chris?” Vince spoke “I’m in Vince. Plus they also want us to form a tag team. I think we can win the titles.” Chris responded “I am sure of it.” Vince said extending his hand to Chris. Both men shook hands as the newest members of the ACW roster formed a bond that could not be broken. RIGHT?
