Harvest (2)

9 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 09, 2009 The small two door sedan lurched to a halt on the gravel shoulder. Smoke and dust rose from the earth and enveloped the underside of the vehicle as the door swung open. 'UNGH.' A hollow grunt. Dan Felton braced himself against the frame of the open door, pulling his large frame out of the car. His white polo shirt, sopped with sweat, was stretched over his stomach and crudely tucked into his khaki trousers. He reached in and grabbed a marigold notepad from the passenger seat and quietly shut the door. He looked at his notes, then at the building, then at the immediate surroundings. He had spent hours driving around rural Texas looking for the antiquated gymnasium that sagged nearby. Finally, he had arrived. Dan Felton was here on a hunch. He was accustomed to the crazies that left hotline messages at Pro Wrestling Insider. But the voice of an old man lead him here. There was a genuine sincerity in that voice. His voice suggested a secret, a secret that he had been bursting at the seams to let out. Felton saw the empty gymnasium parking lot, and whimpered as he looked at his watch. He was late, but surely the house show wasn't over in Nacogdoches. He took a long swig of his Red Bull and tossed the can aside as the carbonated caffeine dribbled out and lightly sizzled on the tan surface below. He looked back at his rent-a-car, the clown vehicle that sardined him for over 400 miles. He looked a the parking lot, which was closer, then back at his vehicle. Surely, he couldn't park like this, but he scoffed at the prospect of re-entering that simmering leather cage. He opened the driver's side door, and popped the hood. He turned on his emergency lights and walked to the front of his vehicle, propping open the hood. Then, looking both ways before crossing, Dan Felton started towards the gymnasium, and the secret that was held inside.
