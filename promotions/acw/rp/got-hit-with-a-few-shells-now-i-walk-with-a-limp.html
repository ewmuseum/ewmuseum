Got Hit With A Few Shells Now I Walk With A Limp.

23 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster, The Shillin' Villain, The Choice of a New Generation, The Czar of Cashflow Got Hit With A Few Shells Now I Walk With A Limp. Location: A hot steamy sauna in the LDC-HQ. Date: June 23, 2005 Number (Archive): 38 Number (Wrestler): 4 The steam filtered up into the vent as Lowell Dot Com, the subject of these sometimes short, sometimes needlessly long “experiences”, sat with a towel wrapped around his waist thumbing through a stack of papers. Resumes. Applications. Foreclosure notices. Things of that nature. You know, the usual. As the Czar of Cashflow tossed the useless ‘financial papers’ threatening to take back all the neat little do-dads he’d bought on E-Bay and proceeded not to pay for, he came across a resume titled “Jimmy Cain, Pool Cleaner(man whore)”. Now, Lowell was pretty sure the three pools he owned (none of which he actually uses) had a “cleaning mechanism” in them that made them clean themselves… although such a thing was never included in the bill, or even offered for that matter. So a pool cleaner he did not need. A man whore? His eyebrow raised, a quizzical look formed. Lowell’s not gay. You are. Fag. Anyway, let’s just say Lowell didn’t pay the “man whore” part any attention and proceeded to jerk off looking at a ‘Strictly Women, No Dudes’ magazine. With that taken care of, Lowell went into his “chin pinch and squint a lot” hunched over pose that signaled the struggle for thought had begun. The rat falls off it’s wheel, into it’s water dish, and DIES~! A new, better rat is brought in, “jacked up” on steriods usually found in Khristain Keller’s blood/urine sample, and smart as a whip. He does some quick mental math and comes to the conclusion that Max Danger is a member of the Nazi Party, and also… a closet homo. I mean, COME ON, who else would think that a shaved head looks good? The “pic” in his bio makes him look like he just orgasmed to LLB’s “Judge This” shorts… Look- women around the world have been “Judging This” since you had it printed on your tights- and know what, foolio? 5 and ¾’s ain’t somethin’ one should brag about. Rather, you should change it to “Accept This- It’s All I Got :*-(“. ANYWHOO (I love pointless tangents, they’re fun)- Lowell pressed the “-->” on his I-Pod Shuffle. “Victims of Hardstyle” went all: “Are you ready?” in that deep booming voice of its, and Lowell – who currently uses it as his alt. entrance theme when tagging (though they never actually HAVE) with Sars – stood up and did the “head-bump hop-strut” across the floor of the sauna, to the intercom on the far wall. He hit the custom-made button called “DROP THEM PHAT BEATS” and a female voice answered, “Yes Mr. Dot Com, what do the the clouds remind you of today?” Oh- there’s a sunroof in the sauna. Lowell likes to lay and watch the clouds. Believe or not, he can find, and even provide a decent enough argument, that every cloud he sees looks JUST like him. Vane? NO SIR-EE-BOB! The Notorious LDC shook his head and smiled. “No, Judith, today it POURS. The clouds are off panty-banging, and the lucky ones are FULL-OUT banging- making all kinds of baby clouds the daddy clouds will abandon.” “…OK, then what is it? I’m not telling you anything about my sex life- we JUST had one of your ‘Q&A’’s the other day.” “No, Judith, I want you to ring up a mister JIMMY CAIN. I see here on his app that he’s got “mad skillz”- skillz that could be useful to me in my future endeavors in the… A… C… Oh jeeze- how does that go again?” “W- it stands for “wrestling”, something close to what you do I guess.” Lowell snapped his fingers, and replied, “Ah yes, that silly ol’ thing. :) Well Judith, I’m in the mood to shake it and I think Ima’ shake it like a polaroid, beh-beh!” “And that means what exactly?” “I’m Scorpio Champ now… I’ve probably made a few enemies just by winning the thing at Mend Game. Well, just like the name of the show implies, me coppin’ some gold went a long way to “mending” the “game”.” Long silence. “Mr. Dot Com..are you high? What is it? Coke, acid, -are you back in your old habits again, sir?” “Yes, of course I am! Habits are made for a reason! I’ll probably be so “Hollywood High” by five you’ll need to blend up my steak and tater tots and feed ‘em to me through a straw! Drugs are so much fun! I wonder though… where does Keith Richards blood go after it’s been taken out of him? Do they just drain it into some Ethiopian water reserve or can the public…like… BUY it?” “Lowell.” “FIRST NAME, WHORE~! THAT’S A NO, NO, NO! That’s three no’s. Next time don’t assume just cause I’m high that you can just break the rules. Tell me…when I get all coked up do you steal my diamonds? Do you strip the gold lining from my walls? I’m not lying when I see this- the LDC-HQ opperates under the laws and practices of the Middle East. We fly planes into those we oppose- We cover up our various illegal and quote un-quote “inhumane” practices- And if you steal from the bossman, aka ME, I will- I repeat, I fucking will… cut off your hand. Don’t test me, Judith..or I’ll have your hand as an ash tray on my desk.” “Sorry, Mr. Dot Com, I- I should’ve known better…” Lowell nodded, forgivingly. “It’s OK, just don’t let it happen again. Oh! And get on task! I want Jimmy Cain and his ‘wrestling trained’ behind in my office within the hour, and get the legal guys to draw up a contract. I’m imagining something along the lines of “Lowell owns your soul and may use you has a human shield if he ever has to shoot his way out of a building surrounded by the po-po’s.” –That, or something LIKE that. Okay? Kay.” Judith tapped the keys on her keyboard, and replied, “Okay, will do. You have a good evening, sir.” “No, Judith, YOU have a good evening. Ha, y’know, I kind of feel like a jerk for telling social services that you sexually molested your son all those times, and ruining your marriage and all… but atleast now you don’t have all those pesky “motherly” duties to keep you from working 23 straight hours without a break. I heart you, Ju Ju.” A loud sigh came from the intercom. “Heart you too, Mr. Dot Com. Goodbye.” “Ciao!!” Lowell took his finger off the intercom button and returned to his place on the bench across the room to soak up more healthy steam. He turned, opening a cooler. He pulled out a bag of ice and pressed it to his knee. Why? Because Lowell believes in continuity. Yep. He got trounced at End Game despite winning the Scorpion title, so why shouldn’t he be feeling the effects now? He’s not super human. His body doesn’t heal at the snap of his fingers… although that WOULD be cool. Really cool actually. But no, a snap of his fingers merely gathers his many “escorts” for a good fuck session- no special healing powers to speak of. One quick thing though… Did Lowell’s leg even GET injured? Meh. Oh well. Continuity’s continuity, and it’s surprising the guy’s even TRYING. Lowell gets an A for Effort, as this is probably the extent of what he’ll do to ‘make things work’ story-line wise in ACW. I mean come on, he didn’t even know what the fucking W stood for.
