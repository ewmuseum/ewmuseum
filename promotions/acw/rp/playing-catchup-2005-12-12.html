Playing Catchup

Coral Avalon The Kleptomaniac Playing Catchup Location: Hotel Room, post-Courage 83. Date: December 12, 2005 Number (Archive): 15 Number (Wrestler): 1 Many events have befallen Coral Avalon in the last year. At the start of the year, after Coral had recovered from several injuries that had amounted toward the end of the Squared Circle, he took his girlfriend (Annabelle Summers) to Japan for a lengthy stay. This trip served two purposes that have had far-reaching consequences that has profoundly affected Coral's eventual stay in All-Star Championship Wrestling. The first consequence was that it has further endeared Annabelle to Coral, as Coral knew how much Annabelle loved Japan, and also knew that Annabelle (a fluent Japanese speaker) would be a good person to have along the way. The second and more important consequence was that Coral had developed his wrestling style even more and now had become one of ACW's most prominent stars even after a three-show stay. In spite of the fact that he only had one victory to his credit, Coral had proven that he had the abilities to be a top star, particularly with his debut showing against Khristain Keller that nearly ended with Coral winning the ACW World Title in his debut. Coral was now blending a more evolved submissions game with the power of his body-breaking maneuvers to create a style that has thoroughly affected anyone he's encountered since coming back to the States. But that didn't really matter at this moment in time, because Coral Avalon was back in his hotel room, following his narrow loss to Seymour Almasy. Coral had expressed mild disappointment at the loss, as it simply came down to Almasy being, as Scott Falk would've put it if he were around at this moment in time, one lucky motherfucking bastard. The only thing that really stopped Coral from calling it a day was the cute, raven-haired girl in front of him. Annabelle Summers hadn't truly changed all that much since I last decided to write about her, which was probably a few aeons ago, and holy crap I am breaking the fourth wall like I'm some sort of Seth. Let me get back into character. Annabelle Summers hadn't changed much. One of the few things about her that had was that she hadn't cut her hair in a year (aside from dealing with split ends and the like), which meant that her hair now reached the middle of her back. Appearance-wise aside from that, she hadn't changed. She was still short (a half a foot shorter than Coral), had a meek stature as if she was still unsure of herself, and was still absolutely adorable. Emotionally, she had changed a bit to where she didn't cry every fifteen seconds, a direct result of being with a man she really loved. She still had her crying fits every now and again, and her feelings were still easilly hurt. However, the crying had become much less frequent... always a good sign. Annabelle looked positively seraphic in her white dress and dress shoes, and stood in front of Coral. Under normal circumstances, Coral probably would've collapsed into the nearest bed and slept for a week... but he had promised to his beloved girlfriend that he would do something of the dating variety with her. And, needless to say, Coral wasn't about to disappoint Annabelle. One could say that Coral now served two mistresses in his life, and he should thank some sort of God that one of them, Annabelle, is willing to work around the issue of the other mistress, wrestling, and be with him through thick and thin. Coral wrapped his arms around Annabelle and hugged her gently. He was glad to have someone as understanding as she was: a trait that he had found lacking in his last two girlfriend, Allison Lindum and Patricia Grayson. That was something he hadn't found much of in life, having dealt with a eccentric and controlling rich family and the difficulties of breaking into the business of wrestling. She was similarly glad, she hadn't made very many friends in her life due to her incredibly shy nature. Coral helped her break out of her shell, and she subsequently became familiar with Coral's small circle of friends as well. Granted, she still felt guilty about being partially responsible for the end of Coral's relationship with Patricia Grayson, but that feeling had diminished after a year of simple dating. Probably most importantly, Annabelle had given stability in Coral Avalon's love life. Prior to the eventual start of their relationship, Coral's love life had endured much turmoil and hardship, and that reflected in his abilities to win. He lost many matches in the Squared Circle he might have otherwise won if he had just improved his focus... something he didn't get to do until his aforementioned stay in Japan. Now, the two were a couple that were seemingly inseperable. And we'll give them room to enjoy their date.
