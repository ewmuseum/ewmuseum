Suite 16 (Intro)

Ecks Darkside of Wrestling, Rising Demon, formally know as Xavier and Blood'Weaver. Suite 16 (Intro) Location: O'Brien Field Date: July 23, 2004 Number (Archive): 8 Number (Wrestler): 1 The cheers of men came from a skybox over a picture perfect baseball field. The air was stained of undercooked hotdogs, alcohol, and people. The Swing of the Quad City struck out at the bottom of the sixth inning to the Peoria Chiefs. The men did their routine high fives and punches in excitement as their dates talked into the back to each other laughing at their boyfriends. “I need a new boyfriend,” was heard from the group of ladies in the back gathered around the bar. The rest of them, the kind of women you can’t tell which one of them is the most beautiful over the others, giggled in their own adorable way. Delayed cheers, laughs, and sobs came from the suite next to them, but one man laughed over them all; Alexander Burace, Ecks. “Haha, I’m going to Disney Land!” He celebrated over everyone’s warcries. The man sitting next to Alex pulls his billfold out and pulls another one-dollar bill out. “The one guy who doesn’t need the money gets it, starting to think that they know we are playing mound ball and that Alex has the money.” A man who was drinking a Samuel Adams snapped back at the man who complained about Alex winning, “You know everyone doesn’t like Ecks to win if he was wealthy or not. Everyone just hates him winning period, because he has the most bogus job.” Another man interrupted this discussion, “For one, he doesn’t have a bogus job.” The man stood up and it was clear he was a good friend of Alex’s. “He has a very respectable job. For two, James and Keith need to get real jobs that match-up to your ages. Remember you work for me.” He corrected himself, “To think of it you have jobs that match your maturity level.” They all chuckled in agreement expect for the two taking the burn. Ecks spoke up, “It is okay guys. You two are the two best pizza chefs I know.” They all handed their one-dollar bills to Alex. “Just like old times guys… Thanks for inviting the posse Raver.” The Darkside of Wrestling put his hand on the Raver’s, the man who stock up for him, shoulder. “Come on guys lets get back to mound ball.” Another man walked in as he listened. “Where were you Neil?” Keith asked. “Walked the wife and kid to the car.” Raver butted in again, “You mean got bitched out by the wife and told not to go to any tity bars with us.” Neil agreed, “Yeah like I said took the wife to the car.” Neil took the seat behind the two pizza chefs. A pop fly hits above the neighborma suite next to them and bounces into their skybox. More whimpers come from suite 16.
