Hello.

11 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11, 2009 Hello. Let me introduce myself. My name is Iceman. I am an ACW wrestler. I have no past history you that need to know about. Consider me a newcomer to this sport. Let me make one thing perfectly clear… You will respect me. If you don’t already, and you should… you will. I will go down in the history of the new ACW era as the best wrestler to walk into the ring. FUCK Andy Sharp. FUCK Max Danger. FUCK Seymour Almasy. Just because they have a history here, you will see them put over me. You will see them stand out in the crowd. You will see them in the main events. The fans will cheer them. The fans will boo them. The fans will give them respect. But mine will come. I promise you. Just because I have no back story, no history, no respect, they will get the spotlight. I will be shunned. I say fuck that. Fuck you too, for not taking anything I said seriously. I will show Andy Sharp. I will show Max Danger. I will show ACW. My name is Iceman. You’ll learn to fucking know it. Even if I have to force you.
