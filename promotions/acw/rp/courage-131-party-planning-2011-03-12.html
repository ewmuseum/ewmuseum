Courage 131 - Party Planning

12 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 March 2011 THE FOLLOWING SEGMENT WAS CUT DUE TO SHOW LENGTH. THIS WAS A PRE-MATCH SEGMENT INVOLVING THE AMAZING GABRIEL, FEVER PITCH, AND THE DEBUT OF EL HOMBRE SIN UN NOMBRE. THANK YOU. “Oh, no, no, no, no!” In one of the locker rooms, the camera caught up with ACW Tag Team Champion and THE FIRST-EVER WINNAR of Four Ways To Glory, The Amazing Gabriel. But right now, he didn’t look or sound like somebody who had gone through hell to become $100,000 richer. In fact, he sounded quite the opposite. “Dude, what the fuck do you mean you can’t make it?” He asked to a voice on the other side of his iPhone. “No… No, no, listen, listen. Don’t tell you didn’t get out of the country. Don’t feed me excuses. You forget about Today. You owe ME, mofo. It wasn’t my place to ask questions why you needed that stuff… Man, fuck you, too. Your head’s gotten way too big for the rest of your scrawny, wrinkled body! Screw you, Chuck. Blow it out your ass.” Shutting off his phone, he hissed as he nearly brought his fist into the wall backstage. “Pssh. I didn’t ask questions when I got him that tiger blood in the first place.” The viewing audience couldn’t help but chuckle at TAG as he turned around. He wasn’t in the mood for this crap; he had a party to plan tonight. “PITCH! WHAT UP, NEGRO!” The Amazing Gabriel turned around and patted his tag team partner on the shoulder for a job well done in the Four Ways To Glory match. “Dude, good news,” FP told him, adjusting his signature Night Life bandana. “We got him, man. Took a couple of favors and a couple more calls to Tito, but I got him...” “Tito?” “Yeah, I had to… pull a couple favors.” He quickly coughed. “GETSOMEGUYSOVERTHEBORDER…” “Huh?” “Nothing, fighting a cold. But we found him, man. He has the good shit that you wanted. Top shelf, high-quality. During my last trip to Mexico, I bought a quarter… dude, I woke up three days later in the Bronx, woke up in a canoe going up the Hudson river.” “Oh?” Gabriel raised an eyebrow. “I was in TIAJUANA when I bought it, hommes!” Fever Pitch screamed. “But I got more ass than a toilet seat in the process. We’re gonna have a good time tonight.” He looked both ways before he lowered his voice. “Just uh… one thing, oh, Amazing One.” “What’s that?” “This guy’s a… how do I put this delicately? He’s a little out there, man. People are afraid of this guy. I mean, I dealt with him before through one of his contacts, so this is the first time I’ve actually met him. They call him El Hombre Sin Un Nombre, dude.” Gabriel raised his eyebrow, looking a little panicked… “PFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFT.” Charmingly, he blew a farting nose into the palm of his hand. “Whatev, dude, whatev. Man Without A Name, riding on the desert on a Horse With No Name, too. Big deal. Dude could eat babies, murder orphans, be the cause of AIDS or worse… be a scientologist. Long as he ain’t trying to get me on the spaceship and he’s gettin’ me my shit, he’s my brother from another mother. Where is he?” A quick, rapid knock at the door answered his question. “Oh. Nice timing.” TAG and Fever Pitch opened the door to a rather odd sight. That of a man standing in the doorway, looking every bit as terrifying as his name suggested. The man had a clean-shaved head with rather dark, intimidating eyes with a ski mask covering the bottom half of his face. A knee-length peacoat over a charcoal suit rounded out his haunting attire as Fever Pitch gulped for air. “Uh… El Nombre, si?” He nodded, not saying anything as he stepped into the locker room. FP shut the door behind him as TAG looked up at the mystery man. “El Nombre… this is The Amazing Gabriel, he’ll be in need of your services. We…” “BLAHBLAHBLAH, YAKETY-SMACKETY!” Gabriel brushed him off before looking up at the man in black. “Look, man, I have lots of money. MONEY. All on the company’s dollar, too.” He dapped a fist with Fever Pitch and giggled before turning back to El Hombre. “I need some good stuff. Good stuff. We’re planning a little shindig in the ring. And we’ve got quite the party tonight, friend! What do you got?” Saying nothing, the men pulled a plastic bag out of his peacoat and dropped it on the table. TAG searched around the bag’s contents. Unmarked bottles, some sheets, and small wrapped plastic bags made him smile. “Sir… uh… Nombre?” He extended a hand to him and smiled. The masked stranger said nothing and briefly shook hands before nodding. “Let me clear one thing up, though…” TAG scratched his mustache. “You don’t have a spaceship, do you?” El Hombre El Sin Nombre simply released his grip and left the room, not letting out anything more than a single breath. Fever Pitch exchanged a glance at TAG, not sure about what they’d gotten themselves into. “Dude… he didn’t answer my question.”
