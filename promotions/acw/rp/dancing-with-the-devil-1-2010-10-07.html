dancing with the devil [1]

7 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07 October 2010 10 July 2010 London Heathrow Airport. Jack Harris wolfed down the remnants of his double-stacked steak sandwich he'd been gnawing at for several minutes now, drawing a couple of curious glares from nearby bystanders as he did so. A low growl from the Pikey Madman was enough to scare everyone who was looking at him, and Jack leaned back in his seat. His flight to Finland wouldn't be for another hour or so. There was still plenty of time for the Pikey Madman to conduct some personal business. Of course, for Jackie boy to do so, the man walking towards him had to pick up the pace and not get distracted by every piece of tail in a short skirt breezing past him. "Oi. Hurry up, ye wanker!" Jack muttered under his breath. Eventually, the short man with crooked eyes did join Jack at his table, seating down with a goofy grin plastered on his face and a manilla folder shoved under his left airport. "What are yer smiling at, Tommy?" Jack asked of his visitor. Tommy shrugged. "I reckon the broad over there, with the pink skirt, ain't got any underwear on! Incredible!" Harris rolled his eyes and snapped his fingers. Tommy removed the manila folder from under his armpit and placed it down on the table, using the index finger of his left hand to slide it across to the Unit. Jack hastily opened up the folder and scanned the contents. "So it's all been banked in then?" Tommy nodded, looking around most likely for more women to ogle at. Instead, Tommy found himself staring at an oddity. And not a female one, too. The Pikey Madman didn't notice Tommy's sudden shift of demeanour. Instead, he continued to pick apart at the documentation within the folder. It, after all, was a summary of money going into his bank accounts. Profits, from shenanigans involving the recently-concluded 2010 World Cup Finals. Satisfied, Jack closed the folder and placed it down on the table. "Alright, Tommy. Off ye go, then. Sod off. I'll call ye when I need ye again!" Jack commanded, reaching out for his cup of coffee. Tommy blinked and met Jack's steely gaze. "Oh. Oh, aye, gotcha. Cheers then." Grabbing the folder, Tommy bolted to his feet and walked off. Jack narrowed his eyes at the sight of Tommy rushing away, as his hot beverage made its way down his throat. It was only when Tommy peered back over his shoulder nervously did The Unit put two and two together. He was about to be joined by another visitor. Possibly a rival gang leader? "Hello, Jack." Harris stood to his feet and slowly turned around, fists clenched. Not the best of settings to get into a fight, but shit happens sometimes. Imagine Jack's surprise when he saw just who he was face-to-mask with. "... Bloody hell. What are YOU doing here?" Z smirked. "We have business of our own to discuss, of course. Sit down. It's going to be a good one." tbc
