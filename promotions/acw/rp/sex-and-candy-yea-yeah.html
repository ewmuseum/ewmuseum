Sex and Candy, Yea Yeah.

30 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gabriel Malik Walczak Gabe, G-M-Dub, New School Rookie Sex and Candy, Yea Yeah. Location: His Heart. Date: July 30, 2004 Number (Archive): 10 Number (Wrestler): 1 She was beautiful. The way in which she moved almost looked like she belonged on a stage. Her flowing brown hair, her big blue eyes, and the dimples in her cheeks. Just to look at her drove him wild. He wanted her, not in a sexual way, but in an emotional way. He couldn't control his temptation, he wanted her to be his. But she couldn't be, and she wouldn't be. He didn't know how to handle the rejection, was he that bad looking or was it merely something he didn't quite understand? This girl, rather woman, was what he had always dreamed of. He would have done anything to be with her. For just one night, the two of them alone he would have given the world. Honestly, he would have given it for just one word with her. He didn't even know how to approach her or the situation, which could be half the reason she slipped right through his fingertips. Was it another man, that she went to? Of Course. Should he attack, and just make her see the weakness that her new man possesed, or would that make him look week? He didn't know the answer's to his own questions, but he knew that he had to do whatever it would take to make this girl his own. And so, as the first day of high school began; so did his quest.
