Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster Lowell, Anastasia, and The House They Will Share. Location: NYC, NY Date: March 17, 2005 Number (Archive): 30 Number (Wrestler): 2 The two-story building was in good shape. No cracks in the foundation, no graffiti, no broken windows. Plus, the door was blue. He liked that. A blue door means no human being has ever been murdered inside; it’s those red doors you have to watch out for. Oh, they’ll get ya! Lowell ventured up the steps, running the tips of his fingers along the bricks. He smiled and gave a nod. “Those are some good bricks. Gotta’ have good bricks incase of a hurricane or tornado.” The real estate agent turned, raising an eyebrow. “In New York, sir?” “Or a plane! You never know when one of those rag heads are going to fly one smack dab in the middle of your office when you’re under your desk fucking your secretary!” replied Lowell, as he followed the real estate agent inside. “Okay.” Ben, the real estate agent, flicked on the lights to reveal a so-so looking foyer. The interior was plastered with dated wall paper and the whole place had a musky smell to it. A layer of dust covered everything. “Are those naked ladies?” Lowell asked, pointing to the walls. “Eh…yes.” “She’s kind of a cute. Have her number?” “Uh, no, sir… She’s a cartoon.” Lowell replied, “So? All cartoons are based on someone.” “That’s not true.” “Listen, you either know how I can get in contact with her or you don’t. Don’t give me the run-around.” “The run-around?” “Yeah, the run-around. You know, the ‘do unto others as you would like them to do unto you’. It’s from that old childrens tale about the cowardly lion and the elephant that was slower than the rest of his peers so he got sent away to a third world country to sew designer clothing and ended up hanging himself to escape the cruel world of sweat shop labor.” Ben nodded, though he didn’t have a clue what Lowell was talking about. “Ah yeah, I getcha. No I don’t have her number.” “Too bad,” said Lowell, “Question: what is with all the statues in this joint resembling penises?” And sure enough, about two dozen tiny statues were sitting around the room, most on the front desk. “This…” he said, gesturing with his hands to illustrate that he was talking about the building, “was a brothel in the early 70’s.” “A brothel, eh? So, like, this is where a guy would go if he wanted sex - no questions asked?” Ben nodded. “Yep. Until the police came and shut ‘er down. They say a rookie cop accidently shot the most beautiful prostitute this city’s ever seen. Anastasia.” He pointed to a picture mounted on the wall. “That’s her picture right over there.” Lowell wandered over. He stared at the woman in the picture. She was sitting in an antique chair, leaning forward just enough to show off her cleavage. “Anastasia,” he sounded out the name. “I likey.” With his hands in his pockets, Ben paced the floor, continuing on with the story. “The custodian brought in to look after this place reported strange sightings.” The Human Advertising Machine was intrigued. He turned, and asked, “Sightings... Like what kind of sightings? Like of Anastasia? Were they… sexual?” “Oooooh yeah, they were sexual alright. The person who told me the story also told me that after the custodian returned home, fucking his wife was never the same. It bored him. They experimented with everything. Roleplaying, S&M, EVERYTHING, but nothing ever satisfied him, again. Apparently, the pleasure he experienced that night was something… not of this world.” Lowell was all: mouth agape, eyes as wide as saucers. “That’s incredible!” “Yep. If it’s indeed true, that is.” “Oh, it’s true! I bet Anastasia is just itching for you to leave so she can jump my bones right here, right now! I bet she’s a lady of the street but a freak in the bed! Like all proper and stuff when she floats down to admire me while I eat, but when the clothes come off, OH MAN! Get the girl a leash, y’know? Y’know?!” Ben looked sideways. “Sure… Whatever.” “That’s it! I’ll take it!” “Well, you should probably know-“ “I said I’ll take it, you stupid Joey!” “My name is Ben. It says so on my name tag.” “I know.” Both exchange a narrowed-eyed look, as if not quite on the same page. “Riiight… I should go get the paperwork then so we can finalize this before you change your mind.” “Change my mind? Are you nuts! I get a foyer with porn for wall paper and a ghost that’s a nympho; why would I change my mind? This is pretty much just the greatest deal EVER!” Lowell was giddy. “Okay, then.” Ben left to fetch the papers from his car, while Lowell hopped up on the front desk. He turned to see a small four-inch long penis stature sitting to him. “Hehehe. Mine’s bigger. P0WN3D~!” Lowell thought he heard something creek and swivelled his head around quickly. “Anastasia, is that you?” Nothing. “I’m Lowell, the new owner! *Bats eyelashes* I’m going to turn this place into my company’s headquarters. Don’t worry though, I’m sure we can work something out so that you can stay rent free!” He chuckled to himself. “I mean, I’m sure you don’t want to be seen as a free loader. So just… do whatever it is that you did to that smelly old janitor… to me. Heh. Heh. That cool?” Nothing. “You just think about that, okay? Okay.”
