One Day Away :: Courageless in a Sea of Faces

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alias The Original Pulp, The Tin Angel, The Spirit of 2004, Tyr, Bigby (explained below) One Day Away :: Courageless in a Sea of Faces Location: Seattle, Washington Date: August 14, 2005 Number (Archive): 3 Number (Wrestler): 2 Courage 79 is tonight… 7:49 AM Chris Sheffield shifted on the sheet of his mattress, the cool morning air chilling his body to the bone. With all the cuts and bruises and bandages recently, he had taken to sleeping without the sheets. It’s okay people, we’re not watching him sleep naked or anything either, he’s a boxers in bed kinda guy. Some people can’t stand that deep cold feeling, of the morning, but for Alias… it was more then welcome. It kept his body wrapped in something akin to an ice bath, as opposed to being wrapped in a warm cocoon of blankets… and in his shape, that fit. He cracked one drossy eye open, and then the other. Another new day… and another show on the horizon. Not just any show, though, he was at home with Courage. Which really just meant another scar, most likely. Alias grumbled at this thought, though hell it was what he did, and even where he slept held it’s own scars. Scott Perry, in 2003, had shown up here on behalf of Winters Inc, and had trashed the place during he and Alias’s war over the TV title. Suffice to say then, Chris was at his smallest place, an apartment in downtown Seattle, somewhere away from his other place New York, and far enough for him, away from his home in San Fran… where he could just crash, while on the road. Speaking of which, he had to move that burnt pile of Armani suits off the front lawn of his place in San Fran… buuuut that was another story. Sheffield stumbled out of bed and stretched his back and arms out, yawning, but also for a minute showing all the fresh looking scars along his back, and the now slightly green-ish shoulder bruise, in something of a Christ pose. All symbolism was lost as he stumbled into the bathroom to brush his teeth and get the taste of the morning, overnight lingering of tobacco and smoke, out of his mouth. Soon after he’d hop into the shower, twist it to as hot as he could stand, and boil his rough and cold muscles to life. Because that’s when his day could truly begin… 9:20 AM Scratch that. After a man, that smokes to much for what he does, quits drinking, he had quit after his last binge before End Game showed him the danger of how others could manipulate him in that position, he still needs another vice to somewhat replace the alcohol in his life. That is where caffine comes in. That sweet sweet coffee bean, and all of it’s life givingness. Alias exited Starbucks, and took a gulp of his black coffee. No, it wasn’t mocha’d, late’d or frappe’d… or anything inbetween. It was black and it was coffee, and it was just what the Pulp Original needed. He hadn’t given a second thought about all the hardships of his battered and bruised body, but then again by now, that was second nature to him. Though of course, as he made his way back to his apartment, he couldn’t be lost in thought… on any simple subject either. All he could think about was… the Spirit. But what exactly was this Spirit of ACW? Past the man with the mask that had caught his attention, stirred memories, stirred him to MAKE SVJ REMEMBER. The reasoning being that Jacobs always thought to much of himself to ever hold weight in anything, while Alias had always been so much… emotion. Chris Sheffield shook his head and smirked. Him and his emotions always getting the better. The tag match, too, he know… that’d probably bite him in the ass too. However, he did what he did to even the odds, help the good guy… save face. Let’s see if that paid off… 5:12 PM He was antsy to get to work, to start things off. Get a lay of the land, and just generally check things out. This was his second rebirth within ACW, so this time he was going to be himself, he was going to be ready. Ready for anything. That’s why he had shown up at the Royal Brougham Pavilion two hours early. Hell, and the worst thing that could happen is that Hawk would just send him home early and he wouldn’t get caught up in the insanity of the show. Wouldn’t have to run into Miles or Silver… he’d get a chance to plan things through, for Jacobs, and even get a chance to heal these wounds. Sure, Alias’d be angry that he wasn’t fighting… but there’s even an upside to anger. 7:04 PM “Oh, bullshit!” Of course the downside to anger, is the ever present possibility of an argument coming from all lines of discussion. Hawk had been busy with other things, admittedly, trying to find out more about the odd man with the mask, from the last show… and thus had shown up at the start of the show with everyone else. Alias had waited two hours, but at least knew every corner of the arena by then. Then again, the wait had also made him pretty edgy, over analyzing a number of things. Which led the Pulp Original and SilverHAWK to there current conversation. Hawk leaning against the door frame of his make-shift office for the night, and Alias pacing the hall in front of him. Neither of them where looking forward to this conversation, but they both knew it was coming after Alias broke what some lightly called backstage, Hawk’s retraining order to end all restrainers. Alias had just swore, and Hawk was going to reason with him… for the last time. “You came out, you came out and you attacked SVJ. You didn’t even go after that Darkness guy, you attacked SVJ. Now I know you did the right thing, ending that 2-on-1, and I know Firebrand asked you to be his partner in the first place, but you STILL broke the rules I gave you and Jacobs. After what happened with the… Spirit… you guys, you guys need some time completely separate before you kill each other.” Hawk finished saying his piece, and kept his eyes on Alias as the Pulp Hero continued to pace. Alias stopped and looked at Hawk, he knew he wasn’t going to win this fight. Hawk wasn’t exactly his best friend anymore, but he was giving him a break with this, because he knew where Alias was coming from. He helped someone… he didn’t go all out to attack Jacobs. If Alias kept pushing things though, whatever punishment Hawk decided to eventually give, it would be an even bigger kick in the teeth. “Alright. So what’s the punishment?” Unflinchingly, Hawk replied. He had already figured it out in his head. “You’re suspended for this show. Without pay. I know you have a place in town, so just turn around and go on home. You’ll have the option to come back for Courage 80… but you’re being fined a nights pay, as well, so you won’t be making any money that night either. After that… we’re back to normal.” Alias tensed up for a moment. God. Damn. Jacobs. Then untensed and let out a sigh, he turned to walk away from Hawk, but something came to mind, going over Hawk’s words in his head, and he spoke back at the owner of ACW on instinct. “When any of us ever get a normal life, we’ll either be dead in the ground or retired. And I’d rather be dead, then sitting on my ass at home.” 7:32 PM Sure I was just about to leave… though admittedly I had stopped and talked to a few people, grabbed a smoke with a people of the tech people. Then I saw THEM. The Sect. It surprised me that what Hemlocke had said was still ingrained in the front of my mind. Then again, they reminded me of every wrong thing I’ve ever encountered in ACW. The megalomanic bald asshole. First there was Osyrus… and now, now Violence Jack. Jack seems worse then O, even, because atleast O never flocked himself with a cult, he only believed he could. Oh fuck, even with VJ, Tully, Hound and Hemlocke all in one clusterfuck… I’m still going to go over there and give ‘em a kick in the teeth. Better be worth putting this cig out, half way through, too. You know, I have a feeling it will be damn well worth it. Admittedly I’m a bit worked up, but I’m not on the show as it is, so I might as well get a little enjoyment. “Shanahan, you better be keeping that bitch on a leash. Next time she feels like speaking enough bullshit about your cause or cult, I’ll be in the right mind to laugh in all of your damned faces. Don’t think that any gang warfare of yours, of theres, is ever going to accomplish Jack, in his house. We’ve done it before, and again, and you’ll get fucked royally in the ass, as a result. Packed up and shipped out. You might be tough, and you might have done some impressive things, against impressive people… but in the end, there’ll always be me and this company.” I see him grin at me, and I tense. Is he going to sic his dog on me now? I keep on going… and let him continue just taking this all in. Let him continue sizing me up. He hasn’t been around for the last couple o’ weeks… so he’s going to hear what he needs to. “You might think you have the numbers, or whatever god gives you your power. The truth is, you’re not bigger then these walls, none of you will ever mean more then what they mean. You might think you can tear at ACW, in the name of whatever Black Wisdom…” The eyes of Violence Jack never look away from me, either, so I stare right at him… and he stares right back. He’s probably got plans, but like hell they’ll effect ACW. “You’re wrong.” 7:37 PM Yes, Alias had just walked away from the Sect. Whether they let him, or they had too, evidently they all had bigger fish to fry. The Pulp Hero was finally heading out of the Pavilion, soon to be back home with his feet up. Away from his lack of anything to do in this damned place, at the moment, and away from another possible run in with the numerous other dangerous verbal confrontations. Craig Miles. Sonny Silver. They where both somewhere in this same building. So maybe, just maybe, it was better that Alias wasn’t around… what with the property damage of possibility of a lost contract in NFW, that would await. He growled for a moment, his shoulder acting up again. The second time that it dislocated, honestly wasn’t the fucking charm, at all. He rubbed at it, but it only acted gingerly. Alias stepped out the doors, into the Seattle night… but that’s when a hand grabbed at his shoulder. The night wasn’t over. He spun around, balling his fist. Then stopped. Kelly Flawless, the United States Champion. A man he helped train, and a guy he’d be fighting for that very same title one of these days soon… took his hand back away from Alias. Flawless not only looked a little worried about something, but he also had a twinge of anger in his eye… and that anger was directed at Alias. “We need to talk.” 8:47 PM Bad day. Stared bad, stayed that way. At least Alias was back home, back at his apartment. Feet kicked onto the coffee table that lay in front of the couch, as he watched the start of the Danger and Silver match on the TV that sat at the other end of the one-room. Yep, I tell you, you can take the man out of ACW, but you cannot take the ACW out of the man. He had kicked off his shoes and socks not to long ago, and was down to just the blue jeans and the white shirt he had started the day with. He ran his hand through his hair for a moment, slowly reclining into the couch, and generally lost in thought. *knock* *knock* Some one was at his door. He hadn’t ordered any take out, invited anyone over, or anything of the sort. As far as he knew, his address to this place hadn’t been revealed those couple of years ago with Perry either, so it couldn’t be some fan boy either. The Pulp Original got up and made his way to the door, looking out the peephole. No one was there. Alias quietly mumbled to himself, and started walking back over to the couch. *knock* *knock* “What the hell..” Alias, walked back and unlocked the door, opening it. No one was there. He… was going to need a damn cigarette if this kept up. The Spirit of 2004 shifted back into the room, starting to swing the door shut behind him… when someone came barreling through, cracking him in the back of the head with a forearm shot. Alias hit the ground but rolled to his feet, spinning around to fight his attacker. Horance Tully. “Are you insane?” Alias sneered, at the sunglass wearing right-hand of Father Shanahan. Speaking of whom. Tully rushed at Alias, knocking him off balance, but getting a straight jab to the right eye, breaking his sunglasses, for his trouble… and Violence Jack swept in the room, flapjack in his taped hands. Violence Jack, long black overcoat sweeping in the air that rushed across him, brought the flapjack down across Alias’s face before he realized the true danger of his current situation. The blow was as hard as it was fast, and Alias crumpled to the wood floor of his apartment… almost out cold. Almost. Looking up, at the spinning room around him, he closed one heavy eyelid, fighting to keep conscious. Before he closed the other, and his world went to black, Tully stepped over him, taking off his sunglasses and throwing them on his chest. Then Violence Jack came into view, a hazed view. He had been planning something. The sick grin on his face clinched that fact. And his voice was the last sound that Alias would hear… until sometime later. “You are a great warrior, Mr. Sheffield… and you will serve the Sect well…”
