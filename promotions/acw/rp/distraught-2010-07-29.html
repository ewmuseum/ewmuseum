Date: July 29, 2010 01 January 1989 It was my birthday yesterday. And instead of being at home, celebrating with my family, I was a great distance away. Not by choice. I look down at my feet, fighting back my tears. Thinking back to what happened yesterday. I couldn't help myself. Like a statue I stood, frozen to the spot. There was not a single part of my body that moved, besides my thumping heart and racing pulse. Beads of sweat formed on my palms and neck. My breathing thickened and I found it hard to swallow. There was not yet any reason for such reactions, but in my dreamlike state, I did not question such things. I watched the foggy gloom, swirling in a mist under the bridge and my eyes widened as the mist parted and a figure emerged. The figure I had been waiting for, the source of my fears. As he closed the distance between us, fear consumed me like a dark shadow, spreading from my toes, all the way up into my clenched throat. Still, the figure walked on, coming closer with each step, but never seeming to reach me. His eyes flashed open and bore into mine; they were cold and filled with hatred. As our gaze held, the coldness seemed to seep into me, as if sharing deep dark secrets that no one wanted to know. He was two steps away when the screaming started. I have begun to realise that my disdain for my stepfather will only escalate following the events of the past six days. The policeman that assured me to great effect only moments earlier was now on the phone, making arrangements that would no doubt see me returning home. Nothing will ever be the same again. I felt in my bones. Why did it have to happen this way? Mother will try to comfort me. It shall be of no use. My siblings, Magnus and Tindra, will be ever so relieved to see me again. I have missed them the most. Even at my young age, I know for a fact that life isn't supposed to be this difficult. Will somebody save me?
