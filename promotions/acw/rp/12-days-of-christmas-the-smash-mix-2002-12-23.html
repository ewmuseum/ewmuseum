12 Days Of Christmas (The Smash Mix)

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#103) by Jay Smash, dated 23 December 2002, from the fed's first site, acw.ewmania.com (its acw2 edition, 2002–04) as captured in the Wayback on 26 May 2003. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
12 Days Of Christmas (The Smash Mix) Written: 12/23/02 'The following you are about to see may not be suitable for those under the age of two and a half seconds old. I not at age requirment, we do ask you please change the channel. Please welcome, here on the public access channel of Smashville, Jay Smash' (Jay Smash is seen on screen wearing a red santa clause suit with hat and beard. He is not moving, but just standing on the screen as millions watch around the globe. Finally, he begins to speak) Jay Smash: Is this thing on? Oh, well, if it is... Hello world! Yep, it's me, Jay Smash. I guess I should go ahead an mention this now... I'm really not growing a beard out. It's fake. And the red suit, just more props for what I am about to show you. No, really, so if your children think it's Santa, change the channel, because I'm not. I won't go ho ho ho... I mean... HO HO HO! In a deep voice like that. I'm not gonna go flying around the world in a sleigh and a bunch of reindeer. And I'm not really fat, it's just alot of cotton. So why are you even watching? Go do something else. It's getting late. Why are you even awake? Don't you know that Christmas is not that far away? Don't you know that at this time that I am standing here making this video, that it is almost a day until Christmas? In just a half an hour, it will be Christmas Eve. And you know what that means. Get the presents ready, have your tree lit with wonderful lights, and ordiments. And don't wanna forget your star or angel at the top, or what ever you prefer sticking on your tree is you have one. So... anyways, good night, have a save holiday, and don;t you forget, children, leave Santa lots of milk and cookies so you can learn to give fat people food when they get older. But remember what your parents say, if the fat guy is sitting on a street corner with old clothes on, and can barely move, he is probably homeless, not Santa. So you shouldn't give them food. I mean, what's the point? We are all a bunch of ass holes anyway right? Sitting around, watching tv when it's almost midnight, thinking, hey this guy is a jerk, or maybe, hey this guy has a point. Yeah, well you know what I think? I think it's time you go ahead and change the channel if you don't think I'm entertaining. Go ahead, change the channel. I guess I can;t be entertaining unless I win my matches right? I guess one big loss is ACW ruins everything? Well... change the channel if you think so, but if not, stick around and maybe you can here what else I have to say.... (Jay Smash waits another few minutes, scratching his beard, playing with his hat, blowing his nose, and whistling. Finally, he stops everything he is doing and continues to speak) Jay Smash: Ok cool, just making sure I got all the jackasses out of the picture that think I suck, but seeing that those guys taping the show are not telling me that ratings have dropped, I guess that means many of you are interested. That is why, I have come up with a SECIAL SONG, just for you. I call this song, The 12 Smashing days of Christmas, that have nothing to do with 12 christmas days, but to make it sound good, I put what I have to say about the PPV in a song. 12 SMASHING DAYS OF CHRISTMAS: 'On the first smashing Christmas, what happened to me? I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the second smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the third smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the fourth smashing Christmas what happened to me? 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the fifth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the sixth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the seventh smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the eigth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 8 count by the ref, 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the ninth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 9 stiches went into my head, 8 count by the ref, 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the tenth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 10,000 + cheering fans for me, 9 stiches went into my head, 8 count by the ref, 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the eleventh smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 11 hours in the hospital, 10,000 + cheering fans for me, 9 stiches went into my head, 8 count by the ref, 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3. On the twelfth smashing Christmas, what happened to me? 12 reasons why I hate Brandon Blade came to mind, 11 hours in the hospital, 10,000 + cheering fans for me, 9 stiches went into my head, 8 count by the ref, 7 training days before the match, 6 guys threw out presents, 5.... pin.... attempts.... 4 gay guys talking to me, 3 chair shots to the leg, 2 bad injuries, and I got pinned for the 1-2-3.' Jay Smash: I sure hope some of this made sense, but if not, allow me to discuss each of these things. Well, as you know, I lost at the PPV in the US title match and I felt kind of bad, mostly for the fact that I spent 11 hours at a hospital getting check ups, including 9 stitches in my head because of Brandon Blades chair shot, and the three to my leg, as 10,000 plus fans cheered me on the get up and fight, including the 3 gay guys and Ralph, as I still had some close calls in the match, and still standing after the brutal attack on me, hence the part with the 8 count by the ref, and afterwards I had two injuries, my leg and head, but still kept a promist in giving the fans a surprise while 5 friends of mine and I passed out presents to the fans. I sure hope that clears alot up. Oh right, and I do think Geo Vacton put up a fight, and it's not him that I am after, and right now, that US title is not on my mind. Instead, I have my mind set on kicking Brandon Blade's ass after he decided to ruin the match and attack me, costing me a big part of that match. My chances in winning were much greater than they were before he interfered, and that is why it will soon be your turn Brandon Blade, to enter Smashville. Why? Cause it's my time, it's smashing time! Have a Merry Christmas for now...mother fucker. (And with that said, the scene cuts off, and Jay Smash leaves, pulling off the Santa costume.) Sorry if anything is mispelled or anything like that, but I am not using my computer at home, and the one I am using has something wsrong with Microsoft word for some reason. Hopefully it's not that bad for anyone.
