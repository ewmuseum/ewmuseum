Letting Go ::: Threshold of Reflection

Neo Xealot Akuma, Djinn, Akira Letting Go ::: Threshold of Reflection Location: Londo, England Date: September 24, 2004 Number (Archive): 19 Number (Wrestler): 4 Fade Away by Seether ================ Forget-Me-Nots in one hand, a bottle of saki in the other, Kazuya found himself at the threshold of reflection again. The cold English air beat his body with a familiar feeling of inner cold and outer warmth and he tightened his scarf like a noose. He felt the same way a year ago. He stood over Her gravestone and let the epitaph pierce him. Here lies Harmony Diane Blair. She was loved by all and will be sorely missed Simple and true – the message was written by those who chose to forget the tragedy. Kazuya could not, despite a promise to himself that if he could not die for her then he would never ever die. He placed the flowers at the foot of her gravestone and reflected. … It was exactly a year since his beloved Harmony was ravaged and slain. Since then the man known as Neo Xealot spiralled into a new insanity. His insatiable lust for revenge proved vain. His vulnerable state unlocked a place in his mind where the darkness was knocking and the demonic presence of Akuma stole its way in and fuelled a desire to achieve absolution above satisfaction. And the voice seduced and comforted. It was a substitute for the love which his other once provided. He welcomed it, bleeding, sweating and crying arctic water. Only the abyss heard his vow to become the enemy of evil men and the most powerful combatant in existence. And he loved it… Until Akuma grew restless and the migraines began to agitate with every bellowed drone. “Akira… find the sword… become Akira.” Over and over again, the billion voices blasted the command from the pupil of his mind’s eye. He began to give in to the torture heading east to the forge from which he sprang, finding and bonding with his symbiotic weapon Hyperion. The blade, now blood stained and brimming with souls unworthy of oblivion completed his transition from man to unholy conduit. But the fiend who killed his beloved evaded him. And Kazuya wavered. He wavered from his vengeance and eventually gave up. But he never forgot the temporary peace which Harmony had brought him. Eternal love would always bring him back to her in life or death. It even brought him back on this, the first anniversary of their collective damnation. … The bottle tinkled against the chain attached to his trousers amidst the silence. He set it down on the dirt underneath him and looked up the angel statue in Harmony’s likeness and spoke to it softly. “Hi… Harmony.” He looked away. The angel’s gaze was fixed upon him as his ice cold tears hit the grey sand where he knelt. “I’m so sorry that I wasn’t there for you… I beat myself up everyday.” He longed for the marble figure to take life and touch him to take the pain away. Hopefully, she could conceal his hardship within the unbreakable shell and keep it from world. But he was the only miracle present there. Morning dew slid down the cheeks of the angel as if it was restrained. Kazuya smiled at the symbolism. “I went on a date the other day … I know it’s what you would have wanted but it’s just not the same and I know you wouldn’t have liked her… I love you so much, Harmony. I will never ever stop loving you. I remember how much you loved your wine. I brought you some of Japan’s finest because I know you always wanted to taste it. Remember how we used to drink wine after we made love? There’s nothing I want more then to make love to you again. One day we will. Can you wait for me?” He touched the bottle’s cork and clenched it. “Do you like the flowers? I sort of stole them from your parents yesterday. They’re you’re favourite aren’t they? I like them too – the name reminds me of eternity. Your father still blames me for… what happened. For your sake I forgave him because he’s my only living link you. But I am partially to blame aren’t I?” He paused to wipe his face, wet with liquid tragedy. “I’m still hearing voices. They’re much clearer now though. But I don’t want you to be part of that world my love. That world is mine. This world is ours.” Kazuya found enough courage to look at the angel again. The tears coursed down his face this time. “The real reason I’m here is to say that I won’t be back next year.” He smiled and laughed as if responding to a joke from his beloved Harmony, “It’s not your fault. I just love you too much to let you see me like this again.” He sighed, the release stuttering in a melancholy context. “What you gave to me cannot be taken away and I hope you can find it in you to see through my evils. In return I shall dedicate all the good I do in life to the memory of you.” With a twist, he removed the cork from the steaming bottle and holding the neck he toasted the angel with all his being. “Bottom’s up sweetheart.” He took a short swig from the bottle and emptied the rest of its contents on the top the gravestone. “I will always love you.” He stood up and kissed his right hand before placing it on the lips of the angel. …and I you, my love… ================ ~ fin ~
