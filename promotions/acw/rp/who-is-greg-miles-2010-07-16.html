Who is Greg Miles?

16 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16, 2010 The year was 2010. The economy had gone to shit and families suffered the consequences. One family in particular, the Miles family struggled daily. They strived to survive off below average income and hoped one day a better job opportunity would approach them for the sake of their ten month old baby girl. Greg Miles was a man of faith who prayed to God in high hopes something more promising would grasp him and his family by the arms and deliver them from the temptations of evil. Greg hated sin, but at times he found himself bathing in sin because of the people he conversed with daily. That wasn’t the life he wanted, not for him, not for his wife, and most certainly not for his little girl. Greg debated long and hard on what he should do next in his life, for the sake of his family. The thought of going back to college glided through his head, but soon drifted away. He was already in debt due to credit cards and a previous student loan which impaired his ability for an education. His current job was working in a factory, which included several illegal Mexicans with mini-vans and Volvos. He was tired of clocking in and out every day, going through the motions just for a lousy paycheck at the end of the week. Eventually, it would not be enough; eventually his little girl would grow up needing money for cars, prom dresses, and most importantly, college. Growing up he was not fortunate enough to experience the luxuries of these things. His mother was a stripper who was never home. At the age of eight, he found himself roaming the streets at night fighting alongside his childhood friends before moving in with his father at the age of twelve. There, his step-mother and three brothers were verbally abusive and sometimes physically while his father worked on the farm for ten or more hours a day and once a day’s work were fulfilled, he would drive to a small redneck bar, and drink until his mind was lost. It was a rough life, but he stayed strong and never lost faith. And now he finds himself in a similar situation as before, but this time he will take control and lead his family to a better life than what he had to experience. He would fight for their future. Growing up, he was always a fighter, and enjoyed watching the UFC. However, he would never make it into the UFC without a background to define him. He would have to make a name for himself somehow, someway, and he took the first opportunity available. The ACW Scorpion Division
