Max Danger The Danger Man, The King of Submission The First Shot Location: Tokyo, Japan Date: May 12, 2005 Number (Archive): 33 Number (Wrestler): 1 The King of Submission was back at his second home in Japan, but it wasn't to visit with Coral Avalon and Annabelle Summers -- who were currently occupying the house -- it was to promote a wrestling event. His wrestling event. Yes, under the SPIRIT banner -- which he owned with Bradley Duncan and others -- Max Danger was holding himself a show inbetween his ACW dates. That was why he sat behind a microphone in his brand-new dojo surrounded by several members of the Japanese wrestling media. Truth be told, it wasn't a huge deal. Sure, Danger was known to the Japanese people. He'd spent a good percentage of his wrestling career working in Japan. He'd regular work tours of the country inbetween his other commitments. Now he was looking to run a few of his own shows a year, the first time he'd done such a thing since helming gfwa OMEGA several years back. It was no great surprise that he was doing it, since he considered the fans in Japan to be the greatest in all the world. Of course, that begged the question: why was he working for All-Star Championship Wrestling in the United States? Nice timing, because he had just been asked that very question by one member of the press. 'They were the only ones to give me a call back,' Max replied with that patented playful smirk of his, inciting chuckles from those in the room. 'Seriously, though, I knew I couldn't pass up the opportunity. You guys know how much I love it here -- I do own a home here afterall -- but I wanted one more chance in the States. ACW just happened to be the only company there that was willing to take a risk on me. 'I guess a few were scared of my past; broken neck, broken back. Can't really blame them.' Max shrugged, then that smirk of his returned. 'Besides, the wife was getting a little homesick for Maine. So you guys know how that goes.' Once again they started to chuckle. The talk shifted to the show the Danger Man was running, which was simply being called SPIRIT - THE RISING. He reeled of several of the competitors on the show, and was then asked about anyone he couldn't get. With one of those 'well, it sucks, but whatcha gonna do?' faces plastered all over his mug, Danger answered, 'There was only one person we couldn't work out a deal with that I really wanted.' After a pause... 'Kenjiro Ito.' The media members knew him well. The native of Osaka was currently plying his trade in America with Jolt Wrestling, enjoying a reign as the first ever Ambassador Champion. But he wasn't just taking on challengers from his home company. Nope, he was branching out, willing to defend against anyone, anywhere. But apparently not at Tokyo, Japan for SPIRIT. No, he was too good for them. Or was he? Let's let Danger answer, shall we? 'Talks didn't begin until recently. It wasn't until he competed against and beat Alias on the last edition of ACW's Courage that I wanted him on the show -- admittedly, I'd seen very little of his work previously. And I'll be honest here, the kid was good. REAL good.' Max held out his hands, wearing a similar dejected look on his face. 'He's only been doing this professionally for two years, too. That's quite amazing. He almost reminds me of myself after I'd had my first tour through these parts, coming back the second time. Has a very bright future ahead of him.' One of the reporters raised his hand and asked, 'Were you looking to face him yourself?' It should be mentioned, this entire press conference? Spoken in Japanese. Oh yes, the Danger Man was quite fluent. A sheepish grin etched across Danger's face. He didn't even have to verbally respond. A buzz grew in the room about the prospects of such a match. Sadly, it wasn't to be. 'Not sure if I could beat him at this point -- definitely wouldn't have asked for a title shot -- but I think I could've given a hell of a run. I mean, I'm not back in complete form yet. I've had a couple of strong showings, the most recent being the hour-long draw against Seymour Almasy, but I'm just not there yet. 'But Ito would have had a battle on his hands.' Once again Danger shrugged, 'But he wasn't interested according to his reps.' A feeling of disappointment filled the room. Oh how they would have loved to see that match. Sensing this, with that smirk firmly implanted on his face, Danger mused... 'Maybe I'll just 'invade' Jolt and petition him face-to-face.'
