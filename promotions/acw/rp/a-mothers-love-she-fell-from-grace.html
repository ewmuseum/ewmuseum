Simian Kade The Son of Sin A Mother's Love; She Fell From Grace Location: New York City Date: December 08, 2004 Number (Archive): 22 Number (Wrestler): 2 I watched from a distance as the casket began its decent into the floor of the Earth. To most standing around the rather large hole in the ground this was a dismal sight. I wasn’t particularly happy about her passing, I was indifferent I guess. Apathetic. I was more shocked that I found myself standing here watching her final mortal-lead symbolic precession into the afterlife than the fact she was actually gone. She hadn’t existed to me in years, so to see my relatives weeping and sobbing felt… Odd. Albeit I hadn’t seem them in years it was still a little surprising that with me standing no further than fifty yards away not one of them recognized me. The stubble on my face hid my identity a little you might say… Shaving doesn’t become a big priority when you find yourself held down by the responsibility of visiting two funerals in the span of two weeks. And three in a few recent months. The ties that were attached to the coffin were pulled back above the ground as one of the members of the burial crew was passing around a shovel with a portion of dirt on it. Some of my closer relatives took handfuls of the brown substance off of the shovel and tossed it upon the coffin as they wept. I stood there a little dumbfounded… No, don’t get me wrong, I wasn’t exactly feeling bad about any of this… I was shocked so many people were upset about her passing. In recent years this woman had become more evil than even I could have imagined, or for that matter, ever dreamed to be. Maybe her passing was a glimmer of hope. Or maybe this is another cruel blessing from an all too unforgiving World. She fell from grace, much like I, and to tell you the truth, if it was today that I was to meet my fate, I would welcome it with open arms.
