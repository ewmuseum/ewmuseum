Trevor Norman The End Chapter I Location: Hotel Room in San Antonio, Texas. Date: September 23, 2004 Number (Archive): 18 Number (Wrestler): 1 Monday afternoon, he slept on the hotel sofa. His head was pounding, and his mouth was very dry. The taste of old vomit lingered in his mouth, as he rolled over on the sofa. He gentle opened up his left eye and scanned the room, to check the time. It was almost 2:30am, as He made a grunt noise before making an attempted to get off the sofa. As he stood up, his legs both went limp and he fell flat on his ass. The sounds of him falling echoed threw the hotel room. The night prior to this morning, he had drunk for almost 20 hours straight. He started to crawl towards the mini fridge; he had remembered he bought a bottle of water before he came back to the hotel. He opened up the fridge slowly, and reached in to find the bottle of water he had bought. He pounded the whole bottle of water, and then lay back onto the floor. Tonight, he was post to meet with the LoC owners, to discuss a few matters, but he knew it wasn’t anything good. He and his brother had disappeared from the LoC awhile ago, for no reason at all. LoC was furious with both of them, and Trevor could picture the events that were about to happen. He laid there for a few more moments before his cell phone went off. He reached around trying to remember were he last left it. On the fourth ring felt it vibrating in his pocket and pulled it out. “Sup?” He listen too the person on the other end of the phone, as his face expression changed. “You serious Don Man, that’s a bit shitty, I guess?” He kept on listening, until the other side hanged up on him. Trevor turned off his cell phone and laided it on the floor, as he looked up at the ceiling. “So LoC closed its doors today, fuck never seen that coming.” Trevor mumbled to himself, as he got up and walked over and crashed on the unused bed. It was gone, and there contracts were ripped up. Too some it would be a sad day, but as Trevor seen it, The Normans were on the free agency market. A Fresh Start Chapter II coming soon.
