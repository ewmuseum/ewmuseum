How Rejection Met Alias

26 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 26, 2009 July 3, 2006 - Legacy of Champions: Underground 7 Alias...calling himself the Sixth Man at the time...attacking members of the Legacy of Champions roster. "Where the... what the fuck happened to me?" This was supposed to be a slow night. "Hey... keep a cool head, a'right? You ain't in the best shape to be moving 'round all dat much." A man sat, propped up against the base of his locker. It's wooden base was broken, and if he was lucky, it wasn't his head that had broken it. The benches in the locker room had been thrown everywhere, one of them had been lying across the prone body of a bloody and banged up individual when Tony Suits and Brute had arrived. This did not relax either of them, this appearance, for the apparent reasons. Needless to say that Brute removed the bench as soon as possible and thrown in aside with the others. It didn't take him an enormous amount of effort sure, but then again he was quite a big guy... and even thought he had seen alot of things... he hadn't seen something like this before. Neither had Tony Suits. What took them by surprise the most, however was the fact that the man who had been attacked, in a vicious and unforgiving way like KKK and Latrell Samuel before him, seemingly, was still... conscious. Rejection. He was down for the moment, but not out. The blood had clotted and dried down the right side of his head, maybe where it had hit the television screen, across the locker room, which was smashed and sputtering bits of smoke even still. Now, Rejection was still conscious sure... but he wasn't completely lucid, not yet. He still had to shake the cobwebs from his brain. He looked to push himself higher up into a seated position, out of his slouch against the broken and splintered wood behind him, but that same damn pain shot up his arm (except this time even more) and Rejection slumped back down. A grimes on his face, teeth gritted. "I said keep ya'self still, man. We're gonna get ya some help... the last thing ya need to do is injure ya'self, like that kid on Violence last week, trying to look as tough as we know you is." Tony Suits kept talking to Rejection, in as soft a way as a guy with a thick Italian-Queens like accent, can be. Rejection just droned him out, and hell, the ringing in his ear helped. He had gotten himself into quite a fight, and he didn't quite know what the fuck for. Things all over the room had been used as a weapon on him, including himself, at one point, as a battering ram into that TV set... he looked down at this fists though, untaped as they where and red as hell, and saw the blood on them. Blood that wasn't his. Not fully, I mean his knuckles where cut but... bloody knuckles don't bleed that much. He cut Suits off from talking, he had to talk through the events that had just happened out loud, at the moment, to really remember them, to really make sense of them to Suits and Brute... and himself. "I was in the locker room alone, alright. I guy had come and given me," He shot a look at Tony Suits, Brutes was looking around the room now, and then thought to himself it wasn't his business, "Given me something, then left. I had finished taking the tape off fists." "Then what's all dis?" Tony Suits said, in a grim bit of pointing out the situation. "I was getting to that, okay?" Rejection replied with a bit of a hiss, gaining more bearing the longer he continued. "Well my back was to the door, and I tossed the wad of tape to the side, I don't think it hit the trash can but it's not like you can tell in this room, anyway. And the minute my hands where empty, this lit cigarette hit me in the back of the damn neck." "One guy?" Brute said, picking up a dented steel chair in the corner nearest Rejection. "Yeah, one bastard." Rejection continued as he pushed himself to a seated position and took a deep rustling breath before coughing out 'a bit of the pain' not to mention a bit of built up blood and phlegm in his mouth. "Brute, find that cig stub, a'ight?" Suits said, looking over to Brute, who threw down the chair." "So I spin around and this guy was standing there in the doorway, I couldn't see who it was of course... just an outline of him, you know? He was my height, even a bit shorter I think. I didn't jump him because I wasn't sure what the fuck was happening... and I was waiting to see if I could recognize who this bastard was, once my eyes adjusted to the little amount of light in this room, before I ripped into him. Then he closed the door behind him and I could hear this rough and gravely voice of his saying to me... 'Might as well make this a fair fight.' Yeah, fuck 'im, a fair fight... the bastard still jumped me in this locker room before he even finished that sentence." Rejection shock his head, and now looked to get to one knee. Brute grabbed his arm to help him up, but Rejection gave him a little push, he was getting himself up, and with the help of what remained of the locker behind him, pushed himself to his feet. He checked to see the state of the bottle of pills at the top shelf of the locker, it had been knocked down but was in one piece, and then Rejection turned to Suits and Brute. "Still... he got the best of me tonight, yeah. Whoever that bastard was." What was written across his chest was now fully apparent, it had been obscured in the debris that Rejection had found himself in. Suits and Brute immediately noticed the numbers, but since Rejection looked like he wanted to vent one more thing they waited. Rejection raised his bloody fist to eye level to show Suits. "I got a peice of him, though. I took his blood, just like he took mine. And once I find out who he is... there'll be more to come." Tony Suits nodded in agreement. "Yeah. Yeah, there sure will." Suits motioned his head to the door. "First though, let's git ya looked at." Suits walked to the door, kicking debris to the side, to clear a path. Rejection looked to follow him, but his leg gave on him a bit. And to be better safe then sorry till they could get it looked at, Brute steadied him by putting his arm over his shoulder. They made it to the door, when Suits stopped Brute and looked at Rejection. In his hand he had a towel, which he had grabbed from one of the lockers as he was leaving the room, and pointed at Rejection's chest with an arched eye and an upturned lip, a mix of disgust and matter of fact. "You wanna get rid a that?" 'Course he was talking about. 1 2 3 4 5 6 The numbers down the front of Rejection, from his throat to his waist. Rejection grimaced, knowing this was his blood. "Fuck." He took the towel and smeared it down his torso, smudging out the countdown of blood. The inevitable questions still hung in the air, however, as Suits and Brute took Rejection to get some help... he still had a night ahead of him. The Sixth Man had crossed to the Underground side of things again, and now he hadn't just taken out a man reviled by all, he had attacked an Underground contender, a daaangerouns man in this environment... and a man who had quite the interesting... and not to positive relationship with The Faction. Who was the Sixth Man? Where... was the Sixth Man? How many people... were going to be looking for this guy when all was said and done? Like the man in black who stood in the darkness at the end of the hall, opposite from Rejection, watching him walk away before rounding the corner. There was a glint of silver, from the visage of the man in black, as he walked into the dim light and picked up another ciggerate butt, like the one that evidently hit Rejection in the back of the neck. He looked at it before pocketing it... "Hrmmm." Was the grumbling thoughtful sound that came from his mouth and like that... he was gone, and Underground continued.
