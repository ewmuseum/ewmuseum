Meeting with an old friend, Emilie Larae

God's Forgotten Son 'The Past, the Present, the Future', 'The Malignant Archangel', 'The Demon of Despair' Meeting with an old friend, Emilie Larae Location: Scripps Hospital, late evening Date: May 26, 2005 Number (Archive): 35 Number (Wrestler): 3 The thinly cracked, grayish colored cement was unpleasantly cold underneath the soles of his feet, as the youngster came to a complete stop. He stood silently on the pathway before the young man stared forward into the reflective glass that was a few feet away. Hospitals had always made his skin crawl as he continued onward as blue suited orderlies passed him through the sliding door. The entrance/exit doors slid to a close behind him, as he looked back cautiously. Even in his most self-assured state, his personal demons created this paranoia, which he could not rid himself of. But maybe this person he would reintroduce himself to on this day, would have the answers he searched for since his adolescence. But before he would find said person, he would have to know where they were hiding. The clad in black youngster approached the reception’s wood oak desk, he leaned forward as his black hood concealed the upper part of his facial features. However, that did not prevent the mysterious stranger from gazing upon the reception’s fifty-year-old bosom as he read her gold plated badge. “Rebecca Taylor, is it? That’s a beautiful name darling,” The youngster said as with his English accent, lips curled up in a pleasant and inviting smile…before signing his signature on the dotted line of the sign in sheet. But underneath his simulated warm exterior, there was something truly bitter that bothered the Scripps Hospital employee, although she replied kindheartedly. “Thank you and how can I help you, sir?” Mrs. Taylor cut right to the chase. “Where is Emilie Larae?” Bluntly, the visitor replied showing his true identity as the receptionist checked her roster promptly. After a few moments, Rebecca knew exactly where to send the bizarre outsider, as she pointed in the direction of the wide corridor on her left hand side. The young man, who looked to be in his mid twenties nodded wordlessly and off he disappeared down the hallway. Mrs. Taylor lowered her head behind her fancy desk, where she returned back to the plied high paperwork that she had to carefully review. But as the experienced hospital worker furiously tried to go through her assignment(s) as quickly as humanly possible, without any inaccuracies…her thoughts were still on the individual whom she just met. “Who was he?” Something Rebecca pondered, as her work had came to a momentary halt while she extended her left arm, capturing the clipboard within her grasp. “…That patient hads not received a guess in almost a year, so who was this person that arrived from out of the blue.” Mrs. Taylor’s curiosity had gotten the best of her and this impulse for answers would not alleviate, until her aged eyesight behind her rectangular glasses saw his signature on the sheet of legal documentation. Once her bluish-gray pupils reached the bottom of the paper, where she should find the young man’s John Hancock, Rebecca was in for quite the surprise. “Oh no, I cannot believe this,” She paused tentatively. “…There’s no way, this could be him.”
