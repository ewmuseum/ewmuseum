Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster, The Shillin' Villain, The Choice of a New Generation, The Czar of Cashflow Somewhere. Location: We Ride Together; We Drive Together... That's About The Jist of It - I Date: September 15, 2005 Number (Archive): 8 Number (Wrestler): 5 Lowell Dot Com knew of one thing, and one thing for certain- that Jimmy Cain would always have his back. Through thick and thin, through the deep and the grimy and murky and swampy, Jimmy Cain would be there, riding sidecar as he returned fire against all those that might try and conspire against them. He knew this, because that’s what best friends do. They put themselves in potentially life threatening situations. Like that time Lowell decided to take part in a high steaks poker game put on by the mob, despite having never played or heard of poker before in his life. Once he’d lost all his money, and not won a single hand, he figured 'Hey now! I may not know the rules of this so-called 'poker' but I do know that Lowell Dot Com always gets his in the end! Rack ‘em up, boys!” He’d go on to lose another three hands. He leaned across the table and punched the mob boss in the mouth. Out came the sub-machine guns, but Lowell was prepared for this. He threw up his hands, and said, 'What!!? Ya think I don’t carry? Ya think I didn’t come STRAPPED!? Fools!!' Reaching into his pocket, he produced the Noisy Cricket from Men in Black and pointed it at the mob boss. He beat his chest, and grasped it with two hands. Booming voice said: 'Somebody’s gonna die tonight, bitches, and if that’s gotta be me, well then I’ll just take yall down with me!!” Enter six more heavily armed guards, each bigger than the one who preceeded him. Lowell dropped Noisy Cricket and 'walked that shit off'. He laughed nervously. Sweat jewelled on his brow. “HAAAAAA~! I WAS FOOLIN’ YALL! HAAAAAAAAAAAA! THEMS WUZ JOKES, YA SEE!” He swallowed loudly. Loudly enough so that everyone in the room could hear him, and the mod boss, who was seated at the table of course, snapped his fingers. 'Take him out back and beat him across the head with something hard- preferably unclean and at one time used to clean toilets or urinals.' One of the guards nodded and started towards Lowell. Lowell fell to his knees and grabbed a hold of his pantleg. He began screaming, “NOOOOOOOO! I WAS KIDDING! I’M NOT THAT BLACK, DAMNIT! I SHOP AT THE GAP!” CRACKALACK! Introduced to the mighty ungloved pimp hand of dangerous smite you were, Mister Lowell. LDC’s held cheek: 'Ooooooooow! *Pouty face*'. 'Bastards,' he said beneath his breath. His took notice of everyone in the room. He looked each in the eye and damned them all to hell with a look that said 'This isn’t right. Not here; not now. I’m barely clothed, I have a used condom covered in shit stuffed in my pocket. What if they find it? What if they find out my dirty little secret? What if they connect me to the All-American Rejects video?? Noooooo!!' The tail end “ooooooooooo!!” was yelled aloud as Lowell jumped to his feet and booked it for the only exit. The two biggest henchmen – Kevin Nash-sized dudes – linked arms with Lowell as he passed by, or tried to rather, and Lowell recoiled back several feet into the arms of (oops!) an even bigger, more 'pro. street, ya feel it?' guard whose name was Alex. Alex was known for his penchant for breaking fingers and having sex with the wives of his victims. But Lowell doesn’t have a wife, or a girlfriend for that matter, so who…? Commie? MADISON AVENUE, NY LOWELL DOT COM ESTATE The Commie sat at his pink vanity table drying his hair and trying on varying shades of lipstick. He pursed his lips together and smiled, cutely. OH, and the New Kids on the Block happened to be playing in the background. 'I’m WANTED… for looking fab-U-LUSS, HEE HEE!!' UN-MADISON AVENUE, NY LOWELL DOT COM ESTATE Lowell wept quietly on the floor, balled up in the fetal position. There were so many things he wanted to accomplish. Write a novel (about exploding zombies), live in a far off South African village (while writing a novel about exploding zombies), discover the cure for cancer… and destroy it while standing in a cancer ward and laugh, 'BUUAUUUAHHH!' and 'MUAHAHAHAHA~!!' Just like that. Yup. That would be sweet. And lastly and most importantly, discover the cure for exploding zombies. He looked up into the vengeful eyes of the mob boss and said, 'Listen, I-I-I know me hauling off on your face wasn’t probably the smartest thing to do, but with hindsight being 20/20 as we all know, I feel as though I’ve matured since then and am now a better person in here and in HERE. If you could let me off just this once I promise not to mention any of the crazy shit I saw when I peaked through the blindfold back at your house. The underage hookers? WHAT UNDERAGE HOOKERS? I DIDN’T SEE ANY UNDERAGE HOOKERS- HOW BOUT YOU ALEX?!' He turned, crazy eyed, and pulled on Alex’s custom-made Italian suit. He pulled him in close. His pupils trembled. Desperation. Lowell turned once again to face the boss. 'ALEX DIDN’T SEE SHIT EITHER. Everything’s flush as full, baby! I’ll fill up an eyedropper with vinegar when I get home and punish myself for three straight days! Anything, just… please. Let me go.' He put his hands together. He was officially begging, now. 'Think of it as a sign of good faith. Never know, someday your family might need y family, whether it be, oh I dunno, sub-par web hosting or a free shill on a television show not many people watch! Anything you want; I can it make it happen.' The crime boss smiled…then shook his head 'no'. Lowell heard the mechanical click of a gun behind him…louded…and the engine of a Porsche. They must have been 20 stories up, there’s no way a-SMASHHHHHHHH!! The giant window that substituted as the fourth wall on the far side of the room exploded in about five million tiny shards, spraying into the eyes of everyone standing- sans Lowell :-D Jimmy Cainl had driven Lowell’s red Porsche off the roof of the parking garage across the way and landed it in the Hilton penthouse suite. Lowell opened his eyes. He looked to his right- guys laid out and bleeding. Then to his left- more guys laid out and bleeding. Finally, he focused his eyes straight forward- Jimmy Cain exiting the car with a pistol in one hand and a sword in the other. (???) He walked over to Lowell and offered him a hand. He helped him to his feet. 'Sorry I’m late. The Jag wouldn’t start.' “Fucking Jag. Always gives me shit.' 'So…what now?” SIRENS. “…Yeah, I’m pretty sure this is a no parking zone, we MIGHt wanna make tracks,' the Jimmy said finally. 'Agreed.' Jimmy turned, picking Lowell up in a fireman’s carry and running toward the gaping hole where glass use to be. He jumped. His chute opened and he guided them safely across the street to a park. Once landed, Jimmy and Lowell ran a good five minutes to find there getaway parked next to a bike rack with half a dozen 12-year-olds standing there pointing sadly to their bikes. Jimmy pulled the car out of park, and Lowell got out to give one of the more expensive looking bikes a kick. Just to scuff it up a bit. He then got back in the car and they drove off as the sun began to rise over the herizon.
