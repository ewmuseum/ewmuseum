I may be an asshole...

6 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The ACW site's own Experience board post (#7) by Khristain Keller, dated 6 September 2005, from the fed's site at theacw.net (its 2005 edition) as captured in the Wayback on 3 November 2005. The site's own page furniture (the menu and the link back to the character's biography) is not reproduced.
Khristain Keller K2, Your Favourite Wrestlers Favourite Wrestler I may be an asshole... Location: random location #101 Date: September 06, 2005 Number (Archive): 7 Number (Wrestler): 2 ...but I'm not a fucking asshole. You know what's funny. Ideas always work out better in your head. For instance; the idea that I'd sit on my ass for a month until Almasy finished his little running battles with Andy Sharp. Good idea in my head. Bad idea in practice. Being the epitome of the word 'asshole', it is very unlikely that I shall say that I am actually wrong about something, but hey, there is a first for everything, and with this being, officially, the first time I have ever been wrong, then I can say it quite easily. However, as with all things in life, there are two sides to every story, and it seems the old fable of the boy who cried wolf has come back and literally bit me on the ass. Almasy, for the past ten days, has spent his time hooked up to a vitamin drip, as the poor boy is so damn tired that he couldn’t go on any longer. But isn’t it coincidence that he does a daisy right when Mr. Keller enters his life? Yeah, I know what you’re thinking. “That bastard Keller had something to do with this.” It all adds up doesn’t it. The ex-wife coming back to fight Almasy, fucking with his head a little. The matches all lasting so long, leaving Almasy physically drained. Plus the evidence that I am spiking his mineral water with all the chemicals under the sun. What? What’s that? You don’t have the evidence? Oh. Didn’t fucking think so. If I did all this stuff I’m supposedly guilty of, don’t you think I’d shout it from the rooftops that I fucked over Manga boy? At the end of the day he’s just another name on a list to me. A big fuck off list that is. But I’m not that shady. Honestly, I’m not. … I’m going to surprise everyone soon. This little thing called professional pride is starting to take me over. Something must be wrong with me. Khristain Keller
