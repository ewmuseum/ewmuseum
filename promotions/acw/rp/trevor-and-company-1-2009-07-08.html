Trevor and Company - 1

8 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 08, 2009 Or "Wonderfullness" July 6 "What exactly do you call this look you have?" Jeremy Stevenson, the man with the look in question, laughed and ran a hand through his long hair. It looked greasy but there was really no way to be sure. "I call it sleaze. These indy chicks love it. Might even get a few piercings just to complete the image. She's got enough metal for both of us." He jerked a thumb in Kati's direction but she ignored him, or couldn't hear him, and kept cracking her knuckles. Whatever was playing on her iPod was currently more important than the conversation these two were having.. The man that sat before Trevor Wilson, with the "indy" hairstyle and the girl with the toxic green hair and piercings were some of the oldest allies Trevor had. Long before Trevor Wilson met Brian Spaes, these three were watching each others backs. "So, Rejection is it?" "Come on, man. Don't give me that. Tell me you haven't read about one show, or searched and downloaded one of our dvd's. We've been friends for years man." Jeremy ran a hand through his hair again but this time it appeared he did so because it was part of his thinking look. "The three of us, yeah, but we didn't all get the same break you did. Of course, she and I probably book more shows in a week than you do in a month." Now Trevor sat and pondered the words. He took a glance at Kati. When she noticed, she smiled at him and winked. Then she gave him the finger. She kept her earbuds in place. "Did you look over the offer I had sent your way?" "Yes, sir. Looked it over and signed it. If I had an envelope I'd even mail it but we've been busy." "You don't have any questions at all? Did you even read the thing?" "Skimmed mostly. She gave it a good once over though and if she's down then so am I." From across the room Jeremy retrieved what appeared to be a signed contract. One section for himself, and one for Kati. The contract was indeed simply waiting to be mailed, or handed to the higher ups directly at this point. "You guys are really okay with this?" Jeremy ran his hand through his hair again but this time just to get it out of his face. Behind the strands he pushes away were eyes that brimmed with fire and somberness. "We don't owe you anything, Trevor. No matter what you ask us to do though we're with you. We're your soldiers. No one else." "That's good to hear, but I hope that doesn't mean you'll give Brian any shit." Kati started cackling before Trevor could finish his sentence. "God that's hilarious. Bill Cosby is the man. Good idea taking those records and converting them for me." She pulled out her ear buds and laid them on the table. "So when do we leave for Montreal? I've been coming up with Canadian jokes all week."
