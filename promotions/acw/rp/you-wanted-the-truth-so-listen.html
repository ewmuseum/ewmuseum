You wanted the truth, So listen.

15 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trevor Norman You wanted the truth, So listen. Location: Unknown Date: November 15, 2004 Number (Archive): 21 Number (Wrestler): 2 I’m the One. I have proven myself to all those that had doubt it me. I am a king. The team of Gacy and Oswald has fallen to these hands, but this was even better. Maybe there was a little thought that I wouldn’t walk out on top. Leaving the ring with the well earned victory, which I have so long deserved. You came at us motherfuckers, with all you had, but was it worth it. Tell me now, was it worth the defeat you suffered. Thinking about it now, I never really had a doubt before it went down. Shit, they didn’t even have talent, they were jokes, and they don’t belong in a place like this. Go back were you need no talent to earn a victory, go back were hopeless people admired you for nothing, go back to were you could at least give each other hope. The fact is splink is no match for me, and no match for theNormans. You want a round two, don’t waste our time. There is only one. That’s how it will be. Admire me, Because I have told the truth.
