The Long Kiss Goodnight

Max Danger the Danger Man, the King of Submission The Long Kiss Goodnight Location: Los Angeles, California @ Staples Center Date: October 25, 2005 Number (Archive): 11 Number (Wrestler): 2 The Long Kiss Goodnight Starring the lovely Max Danger and his manish wife Jessica Dated: 10/22/05 Location: Staples Center in LA The only people still left in the Staples Center were crew members dsassembling the set, random front office employees, and those needing some sort of medical treatment for their many wounds. One such person was Max Danger. He'd gone through a war against Sars the Clown and Lowell Dot Com. He bled buckets. All in hopes of winning the Scorpio Championship. He had to ask himself, 'Was it worth it?' Well, actually, the doctor asked that very question, while he was applying a bandage to Danger's chest. His face was already covered like Marv's from Sin City. When he heard the question, he simply stared at the good doctor. Yeah, he'd lost. In yet another big-time, important match, he'd come up short. It seemed, at this point, he was simply one of those great competitors that simply couldn't reach the highest plateau and become what he craved so desperately to become: CHAMPION. 'Every last drop of blood,' the King of Submission said matter-of-factly. The doctor couldn't look away from Danger's icy glare quick enough. But you know, he says that, but deep down, he can't help but think that there needs to be a change. He keeps coming so close, but at the end of the day, he's still going home empty-handed. He didn't scratch and claw his way through rehab from a broken back just to come back to this. He came back for championships. And right now, that's just not happening for him. So, yeah, sure, he'd do it all over again. There's no doubt in his mind. But there was something deep down that was gnawing at him. He shouldn't be losing these matches. Hell, he made a living in Action! Wrestling winning exactly these types of matches. He couldn't be stopped from collecting titles as he charged up the card. Of course, he never was really the same again after losing the Bantam Championship to Keith Scott Zimmerman two years ago inside that Steel Cage. Not that he could explain to you why that was. Danger rubbed the back of his neck. To say it was sore would be an understatement. He was driven right on top of his head with one of the most vile and vicious moves every invented: the Pepsi Plunge. And he took it in a manner not befitting a healthy neck. It was fortunate it wasn't broken or worse, had killed him. Jessica started to massage her husband's neck. 'I'm sorry,' she said. Max went to peak over his shoulder, but cringe at the pain doing so caused. Through the grimace he asked, 'For what?' Shaking her head and shrugged her shoulders. Honestly, she didn't know what to say, and she wasn't sure why she said she was sorry. Max turned around in the metal folding chair, away from the doctor trying to patch him up. 'Take a break,' the Danger Man told him. Sighing, the doctor stood up and walked into the supply closet. He needed to steal some tongue depressors anyhow. Danger stared up into his wife's crystal-blue eyes. He placed his hand on her face, rubbing her cheek with his thumb. 'This is nothing, Jess. For five years now, you've been with me through thick and thin. Through the highest of highs and the lowest of lows. I love you. There hasn't been anything or anyone that has meant as much to me as you have.' Max sighed heavily, continuing to stare directly into Jessica's eyes. 'I'm sorry.' Jessica was perplexed. What on earth did he have to be sorry for? For losing a couple of matches? Big deal. That they can deal with. They've dealt with far worse. 'It's been five wonderful years that you've traveled at my side. You've given me the strength to obtain goals that I didn't think I would be able to. You've helped me along when it seemed I was done for. But now... I don't think you can help me. Not anymore.' Jessica's jaw would have dropped had she not been too shocked. Her eyes began to well up, as she just stared blankly at her husband. She couldn't understand what she was hearing. 'What I need to win these matches, to win the belts, is not going to come from you any longer. I have to search within myself to find that strength.' Danger held Jessica's hands in his own and stood up. He was looking down on his wife. 'And to do this, to achieve my goals, I can't be worrying about you. I can't keep you safe any longer. And you can't take care of yourself...' 'But...' '...no matter how much you think otherwise. You know it's true, Jess. I wish it wasn't so, trust me. I wish more than anything that you continue on at my side, but I simply can't take the risk anymore. Knowing what these people can do -- what they are willing to do... it's too dangerous.' Tears were rolling down Jessica's cheeks. Max wiped several streams away before placing his hand under his wife's chin, lifting it. He placed the hand on the back of her head softly. He kissed his wife. Long and passionately. When he finally pulled away, he didn't say another word. He moved towards the door. Jessica's eyes re-opened, and she jumped out of her seat. 'Xand?' she called after him. But he didn't look back at his wife. He just kept walking towards the door. 'Xand?' He slipped through the door, disappearing from sight. 'Xander!?'
