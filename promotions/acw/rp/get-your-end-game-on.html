Lowell Dot Com LDC, The Human Advertising Machine, This-Space-For-Rent, The Walking Billboard, The Endorser, That Blue Haired Sellout, The Webmaster, The Shillin' Villain Get Your (End) Game On! Location: The LDCHQ. Date: May 30, 2005 Number (Archive): 36 Number (Wrestler): 3 His bags were packed(plane tickets in the side pouch), and Lowell peeked through the curtains, eyeing the driver of the cab parked in his driveway. He looked like Osama, which Lowell thought could mean a potential kidnapping, so he had Benny and Huss go rough 'em up a bit and steal whatever 'weapons of mass destruction' he may have hiding underneath the seat. When they returned, they promptly reported that no such weapons were found, and that the driver was actually just a really tanned Canadian(is there even such a thing?), thus putting Lowell's mind at ease. 'Thanks fellas, I thought we might have had an incident there, like the last one only, like, ten million times worse because it'd be happening to me and not those dudes in the towers,' Lowell said with a smile, stuffing a crisp five dol- oops! Lowell retracted the five dollar bill and instead dug out a wallet-sized photo of himself and placed it in Benny's shirt pocket. 'Don't breathe too deeply or you'll bend it!' 'OK, boss, is this to your liking?' Benny inhaled then exhaled softly. 'Fantastic!' exlaimed the Blue Haired Sellout, before turning to Huss. 'Huss, buddy, I'm sorry, but I don't have anything for you.' Huss squeezed a man tear from the corner of his eye. 'Jeeeeeze, boss...' 'Hey, hey! Now what did I tell you about the using that word in my presence?' Lowell asked sternly. 'That it sounds too much like JC's first name and that you're the only person or thing us lackeys should worship?' Lowell replied, 'EXactly.' He snapped his fingers and his two 'Ninja Cops' as he likes to call them, as they look like they're straight out of Double Dragon with one wearing an all blue track suit with the sleeves torn off and the other wearing a matching red one, grabbed his bags. Oh, and they're both former Karate World Champions...atleast, according to Lowell. 'Ninja Cops...' Turns to Benny. 'Benny...' Nod. Turns to Huss. 'Huss...' Nod. 'I'm going to miss you two most of all. A whole lot more than that jerk that keeps the books around here. I really do hate that guy.' The Human Advertising Machine then looked behind him to see Chris, the 'jerk that keeps the books', standing there with a pad of paper in his hand. 'Ah... Chris, what did I tell you about walking around quietly in the LDCHQ?' Chris frowned, and gave a deep sigh. 'That you could be talking about us behind our backs, and that what's the point of you talking about us behind our backs if we're just going to eavesdrop.' 'EXXXXXXACTLY. PEOPLE it's all in the handbook I gave you. I shouldn't have to ask you to recite the Code of Conduct on the spot like this to get you to obey the rules.' All three hung their head. Lowell nodded. 'AHHH, I can't stay mad at you guys! Group hug!' Benny, Huss, and Chris went to hug Lowell, but were stopped when the boss' hand went up. Lowell said, 'Damnit! That was a TEST! Chapter 11, page 385: ALL GROUP HUGS INNIATED BY LOWELL ARE TO INVOLVE EVERY BUT HIM AND WILL CONTINUE ON UNTIL LOWELL CRIES OUT, 'MORALE IS THROUGH THE ROOF! EVERYONE MAY NOW GO EAT/POOP/SLEEP AND WATCH VIDEOS OF LOWELL! You people are LACKEYS. I'm the 'bossman' - the head honcho - the guy whose feet you would lick clean just to get a ‘Hello’ from... I don't 'hug' you; you hug each other.' Huss raised his hand, and Lowell called on him: 'Yes, Benny, what is it?' 'Sir, isn't that a tad... not heterosexual?' 'Yeah, like... What if one of us likes it a little too much?' asked Benny. 'I don't care. You three have no sexual preference. You are the worms that irrigate the soil of my EMPIRE! You're A sexual.' 'Oh.' 'Now grab my bags, minions. Huss, you've got my PS2 and X-Box. Benny - my clothes. And Chris… just go stand in the corner and punch yourself.' Chris then went and did just that. And as Lowell and his 'Ninja Cops' exitted the still-haunted(no, I haven't forgotten about that, I'll get to that someday) LDCHQ, a loud THUD was heard. Huss stopped for a second, as if concerned, but Lowell waved him on. 'Pssh. He's faking. That guy couldn't punch his way through Kleenex. Did you see his noodle arms? By LOWELL, I hate that guy! Everything about him makes me so mad!' 'Plus, he's a Jew. Whiny little bastard,' added Benny. 'Benny, that was entirely out of line. We may discriminate against a person's their physical appearance and choice of gaming console, but we DO NOT discriminate against a person's race or religion. I mean, just because Hindus are WRONG doesn't mean they need to be told they are. And just because fags are gay doesn't mean they need to be TOLD they are. And just because Mexicans are both lazy AND smelly, well- OK, I guess there is something to be gained by telling those people what's up. Maybe they could take a bath once and a while.' Nods all around. Lowell then got in the cab and waved bye to his droogs. 'Now remember, NO PARTIES! One of you is a snitch and I'll KNOW! CHOW!' The cab pulled out of the drive way and disappeared down the street. Lowell was on his way to End Game to wrestle for both the Scorpion and TV titles.
