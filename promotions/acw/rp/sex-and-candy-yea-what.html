Sex And Candy, Yea What?

Gabriel Malik Walczak Gabe, G-M-Dub, New School Rookie Sex And Candy, Yea What? Location: His Heart Date: August 05, 2004 Number (Archive): 11 Number (Wrestler): 2 The first day was finally here. They had a senior orientation early that morning, and then they were given their schedules. This was the same thing he'd went through four years in a row now. Nothing changed, this school it stayed the same, this town, it to stayed the same. It was like a dream world that he lived in. Nothing ever changed. His schedule for the year wasn't hard either. He was on the four block plan. First Hour: Advanced Biology Home Room Second Hour: Math Topics Third Hour A: American Problems Lunch Third Hour B: American Problems Fourth Hour: Physical Education It was a pretty easy schedule, the only thing that posed any threat was Advanced Biology, but with his grades in the previous years, it didn't matter if he passed or failed that class. Honestly, he could have graduated early, but he just finished out the year for something to do. Then she walked by. That was his something to do. He made being with her his single most important goal this first semester in High School. He'd described her in his mind, so many times before. Her beautiful hair, her amazing body, and her eyes. They could pierce into your mind, like a single glowing star in the sky. He knew that she was his object of obsession, but was obsession such a bad thing. Then the bell rang, and first hour was set to begin. 'Another day.' Gabriel thought to himself sighing, 'An-, Another Day.'
