Doug the paedophile [1/4] Written: 20th march That boy needs therapy Psycho Somatic He’s a nut He’s as crazy as a coconut That boy needs therapy Psycho Somatic Hes as white as a sheet And he also makes false teeth… The Avalanches – Frontier Psychiatrist --- Scott Perry had begun what seemed to be an amazing return to mental and physical health with the help of only one man. His former guard and new best friend, Doug. Today Chronicle one of the Scott slash Doug friendship begins. They return home, to Doug’s home. The following happened between the minutes of now and whenever you finish reading: “Welcome home!” Doug announced as he swung inward his white front door. The hallway was beautifully decorated in peach shade wallpaper, a pine table with a small draw was up against the right hand wall with a clear vase on top filled with beautiful, aromatic yellow flowers. Scott dropped his bag and walked slowly, in a trance, in to the home of Doug. “Honey?” Came a bellow from the upper floor of the home. Scott winced backwards as a pretty 37 year old woman came halfway down the stairs in front of him. “Don’t worry Scott,” Doug said with a reassuring shove in the back. “This is my wife, Gail. Gail, this is Scott, he’s gunna be living with us for a while, aren’t you Scott?” Scott gave a nod as if he were a nervous, shy child, not that he was. Scott Perry was an international wrestling phenomenon. Looking at him now, you would never have guessed that fact. “But Doug.” “Not now Gail. Okay?” “Okay Doug. Later.” She said, looking her husband directly in his brown eyes. “Shall we show Scott to his room?” “Gail motioned for Scott to head up the stairs. He grabbed his bag and followed willingly. Gail opened the door at the end of the upstairs hallway. The room was about 2 metres by 3 metres and had a small single bed and one door wardrobe in it. Scott dropped his bag on his bed. “I’ll let you settle in.” Gail said, closing the door. . ‘Settle in’ I thought to myself. ‘This shouldn’t take long. Settle in to a cupboard. I mean, there wasn’t really much to settle in to. But what came next would prove to make my stay even more uneasy…’
