Europa Wrestling Ligue (EWL) Europa Wrestling Ligue is a professional wrestling organisation set up by Mr. William Laguna, with one goal in mind; to provide the segregrated citizens of Europe a reason to come together in unity. A form of entertainment that could be shared across borders, and indugle themselves in. Having had experience in the professional wrestling business, Mr. William Laguna feels he's completely qualified to steer the Europa Wrestling Ligue towards becoming the talk of the European continent. Owned and operated wholly by Mr. William Laguna, the Europa Wrestling Ligue also serves as an official development territory to All-Star Championship Wrestling, which is in turn a subsidiary of SlySports Entertainment LLC. 20 JAN 2011: Finally, after months of hard work, Mr. William Laguna's dream has seen the light of day. ONE NIGHT IN AMSTERDAM -- EWL's inaugural event -- marked the start of a new era in European professional wrestling, and with the talent that's flocking to the Europa Wrestling Ligue, anticipation for the next event is already at an all-time high. ONE NIGHT IN RIGA is currently scheduled to take place on 22 February 2011, and there are already rumours of a couple of huge announcements lined up that'll set the stage for some incredible matches in the months to come. New York’s Finest (NYF) New York's Finest (NYF) is an independent professional wrestling promotion in the great state of New York. Owned and operated by one Clint 'Slugger' Matheson, NYF prides itself on being the home to some of the most promising up-and-comers in the industry, whether they're just starting out or re-inventing themselves. As of November 2010, NYF will serve as a feeder federation of sorts to All-Star Championship Wrestling. A number of past and current ACW's All-Stars have ties with NYF; most notable of these individuals are Mach 2 and Orlando Grant (who's currently back in NYF). With this newest move, it appears that NYF's future will be inexplicably tied to the global phenomenon that is ACW, and many feel that this latest development will only ensure good things for the men and women of the NYF roster. The NYF roster: Ravi Pandey-James, Orlando Grant, The Incredible Ari, Wyman, Clancy Catastrophe, Kimberly Akiro, Art Ajax, George 'The Enemy' McMenemy, Thomas Gunn, Murdah Mansfield, Galen Hellas, Temperance Hellas, Hiraldo 'The Avenger' Pacheco, Jabarri, Diallo, Tornado Tex, Red Rock Reuben, 'Six Shooter' Deacon Fox, 'DQ King' Mickey Gordon, Franklyn 3.0, 'Ballistic' Bobby Lienhard, 'Showstoppah' Jason Slade, 'Fabulous' Ben Aspinall, Kieran V. King, Hooch Manly Manson, Garrity Jones Jr., Nicole Gan, Karsonator, Junichi Sayago, Boone 'Big Time' Hammond. West Coast Championship Wrestling (WCCW) West Coast Championship Wrestling (WCCW) is an independent professional wrestling promotion based in the great state of California. Owned and operated by one J.D. Fowler, WCCW has been entertaining wrestling fans in the West Coast of the United States since 2005, and more so since they had a weekly live event since January 2008. As of November 2010, WCCW will serve as a development territory of sorts to global wrestling promotion All-Star Championship Wrestling. One of WCCW's former superstars, Saleem Nagi, is in fact in the final stages of contract negotiations that'll see him land an exclusive and highly coveted spot amongst ACW's elite All-Stars. With the depth of talent in WCCW and this latest link-up with ACW, surely it can't be long before we see more of WCCW's present superstars get the opportunity to ply their trade on a worldwide platform. Some of WCCW's top stars include: - 'Savvy' Steve Savoy, the current Golden Champion (having been so since January 2008) - Noah Spencer, the current Silver Champion (and having actually been contracted to ACW for some months) - DJ Marks & JD Raines, the current Tag Team Champions (managed by the extremely bodacious Michelle Rowe) - Archer 'Bullseye' Arrows, the current Cruiserweight Champion - The Thunderer - High-Commander My-moon - Jocko Abernathy Greene - Jacqueline Monteiro - 'The Unabashed Racist' Hiram Jones Some of WCCW's past stars include: - The White Canary (a former Cruiserweight, Silver and Golden Champion) - Saleem Nagi (a former four-time Silver Champion and ACW's newest recruit) - Monte Young & Carl Kimball (longest-reigning former Tag Team Champions)
