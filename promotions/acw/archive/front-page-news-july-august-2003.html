ACW — News

Front page news, July–August 2003

circa 10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed's own front-page newsdesk on its first site: short announcements for the Courage episodes of 13 July to 10 August 2003, a preview notice and a fan poll, as captured in the Wayback on 12 August 2003. The site's menu is not reproduced.
------------------------------------------------------------------------- Holy Sh*t! Holy Sh*t! Holy Sh*t! A Courage Preview! A Courage Preview! A Courage Preview! *FAINT* Preview For Courage ------------------------------------------------------------------------- After a Short Delay... I give you Courage. Remember to leave your PAYPAL details before you read, so we can take the $5 off your account. Thank You. [Courage | July 27th 2003] ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yee-HAW Courage is up fellas, and it's a grand-dime-flicking-penny-licker of a show, so go and read up now, before the big bogey man get's you. Seriously? Courage is up. Sorry. [Courage | July 20th 2003] ------------------------------------------------------------------------- On a Wednesday? Get Real. The first Courage after the momentous GLORY has taken place. Hold on to your chair...this is gonna be a rocky road to King of Ages. [Courage | July 13th 2003] ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Courage...on a Wednesday? Just a small note for you fans that this week, Courage has been moved to Wednesday by TNN/Spike. Although the show will go down on Sunday night, the network has moved the screening of it to Wednesday. The show, which is coming from Phoenix, Arizona has been rumoured to be a stellar show, as the King of Ages 1st round is due to take place. However this will only be a one week deal, and Courage will resume, LiVE! next Sunday. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Date - August 10th 2003 Location - Houston, Texas As the days until King of Ages count down, the ACW World Title is nearing finding a new home, as the tournament upon which the PPV's name was created, is nearing completion. [Courage | August 10th 2003] Who Will Be The King Of Ages?
