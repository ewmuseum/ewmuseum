High Flyer Biography Real Name Jack Harmen Nicknames Friendly Neighborhood Lunatic Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday 12/17/1975 Place of Birth Bethlehem, PA Height 6'0 Weight 224 Blood Type AB- Eye Color Blue Biography Jack Harmen spent the first eight years of his life idolizing his father. He spent the next 8 years fearing the man he would become, and miserable with life. He spent the next 8 years following his dream, finding "true love", and figured out how to have both. The following 8 years, High Flyer thrived, flourished, and then threw it all away. Now. Who knows what the next 8 years have in store. But one thing can be certain. It will be the last 8 years with any importance to the Lunatic. Because as anyone knows, life ends at 40. And shotguns make no whimpers. -Currently under the ongoing psychiatric care of Clarissa Cambridge. Notes provided from Journal document contained within black suede pad book, dated 8/20/07 He is one of the ACW shareholders, buying a ticket for ACW's first show in 2009.
