In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Controlled Power; Hound is not the same person he once was in his first ACW run. The uncontrolled beast with a lack of ring psychology returns with a much more focused offense and a nearly superhuman tolerance to pain. The rudimentary moves he used to employ have been nearly replaced with more surgical high-impact moves (backbreakers, powerbombs, etc.). Trainer: Elijah Brown, Sonny Silver, Rene D'Artagnan, Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan. A recent excursion to Japan saw him training under an unknown individual. Again, nothing worthy of note Career History Glory. I've wanted it all my life. Many people have promised it to me and they've all failed. Violence Jack promised me this glory. I became his Hound and his Dog of War seeking only to destroy those that got in his way for a sip of the Holy Grail of Success. Things were going good at first. Then, he abandoned me in my time of need. Fuck him. I was supposed to be the Scourge of ACW. I was supposed to be the man that would usher in a new era of chaos that could not be paralleled. Instead of going out with a bang, I went out with a whimper. I was exiled from ACW not because of Coral Avalon, but because of my own greed. I fled to Japan. What happened there was a journey that I, myself, still cannot believe. Now, I'm back. With Father and Brother looking out for me now, I aim to succeed and make the Brown dynasty proud. There's only one thing stronger than my family bond now. The will to succeed.
