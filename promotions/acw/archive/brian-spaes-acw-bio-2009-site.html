Brian Spaes Real Name: Brian Michael Spaes Nickname: "The Man Who History Forgot" Also known as: The former 'Impulse' Brian James Born: July 1st, 1977 Place of Birth: Fort Worth, Texas Hometown: Flying S Ranch, Weatherford, Texas (actually lives currently in Brighton Beach, Coney Island, New York) Height: 6'3 Weight: 242 pounds Blood type: B + Eye color: Grey Team: The Minutemen Trainer: Jim Schwartz, but mostly Self Trained Theme song: Ecstasy of Gold as preformed by Metallica Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Vale Tudo (Anything goes) Signature moves: Nishamura Style European Uppercuts 'Blong Clutch" youtube link Dragon Suplex Busiaku Knee Kick youtube link Uranage youtube link Gotch-Style Piledriver Finisher: 1) Spinning Back Fist youtube link 2) Damascus Head-Leg Lock youtube link 24 seconds in 3) Vertical Drop Brainbuster youtube link Finisher name: 1) Uraken 2) D-Lock 3) The Firestarter Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: X Wrestling, 21 Century Wrestling, Action! Wrestling, the Squared Circle, High Noon, LEGION Other/Previous teams: Wrestling 101 (Action! Version), The A-Team (TCW) Other/Previous managers: Denise Carruthers, Joe Black Other Wrestling titles won: Several Cruiserweight Titles, 21w United Champion (Last Champion, Last Match), X Wrestling World Champion (Twice) Other Wrestling awards won: X Wrestling Hall of Fame, Has Won Two Cruiser Tourneys Biography You know that guy in your past that you randomly meet on the street and shocks you. You walk away from him, and as soon as you are out of earshot, you call up your wife, your best friend, your family. "I saw so-and-so. My god, they look horrible." "I don't know what happened to them." "Thank Christ I didn't end up like him." I am him. I am fighting destiny. I am trying my best to keep my head above water. I am not the man you thought I was. I will be much much more.
