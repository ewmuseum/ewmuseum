'Agent' Krystal McKenzie Real Name: krystal Nicole McKenzie Birthdate: May 17, 1981 Hometown: Chicago, Illinois Height: 5'6 Weight: 133 lbs. Nicknames: 'Agent' 'Secret Agent' Status: Face Manager(s): None Current Teams: None Entrance Music: lovefurypassionenergy by Boy Hits Car Wrestling Style: High Flying Signature Moves: Dropkick, Hurricanrana, Twist of Fate, DDT, Diamond Dust Set Up Move: Blessing By Pain (Krystal lays out the opponent with a superkick. ) Finisher: Paradise Lost (After laying out the opponent with her 'Blessing by Pain.' Krystal climbs to the top ropes and dives off nailing the opponent with her version of the Swanton Bomb.) ACW Title History: None ACW Award History: None Other Federations: CWF,HWF Other Federation Awards: CWF Internet Champion, CWF Roleplayer of the month, HWF Superstar of the month Handler Information Handler Name: Meagan A. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Biography Not much is know at the moment, about Krystal McKenzie because of her short term memory loss. It was said that she was born on May 17, 1981 in Chicago Illinois. Krystal grew up as any normal girl playing with dolls and barbies. But there was something different about her. She enjoyed watching wrestling, something that most girls growing up didn't normally like to do. She didn't know why she liked it so much, it was just something that fascinated her. Growing up Krystal didn't pay much attention to what she wanted to do. She would be okay, if she had to work at Walmart. A few years past, and Krystal was finally starting to feel her female side. Yes, she still liked wrestling but not as much as she used to. She also wore dresses, along with makeup, tight pants and other types of sexy clothing now. Well, you might think her life is perfect. But it isn't. This is the turning point of her life. The bad point of Krystal's life came as she had just turned 17. A few months after her 17th birthday, a dangerous flood came to Chicago, Illinois. Many people were killed and lots of things were destroyed. Krystal was one of the victims to this flood. After the flood Krystal was found face down in the mud far away from home. She was breathing but unconscious and was in a coma. She must have been submerged under water for a long time and luckily for her she survived. The doctors did all they to keep Krystal alive. She finally woke up from her coma a few months later. In result to the damage Krystal was left with what the doctors think is a short term memory. She can't remember who her family is, when she was born or even where she was born for that matter. The only thing she does know is that her name is Krystal and she is a women. Krystal grew up the next few years alone. She needed to make a living for herself. So for the next few years she went training in many karate dojos learing the fine arts of Tae Kwon doe, Mixed Martial Arts, Jiu Jitsu, and Kung Fu. She still loved wrestling like before and couldn't miss an event. Krystal skills were noticed, and she became a secret agent a few years ago. That is where she works now along with her wrestling career. As an agent she stays undercover disguising herself in many different outfits to keep her identity a secret. This woman agent has helped out her private agency a lot by bringing many wrong doers to justice. As an agent Krystal promises to help out people in need, and one day hopefully her memory will come back to her, and she will finally know just who she really is.
