Alexandria Malone Biography Real Name Juliet Alexandria Malone Nicknames The Princess Hometown Phoenix, AZ Birthday 20 (born May 9) Place of Birth Phoenix, AZ Height 5'5 Weight 120 Blood Type O- Eye Color Green Biography Alexandria Malone. Student and little sister to IWO and A!W Hall of Famer Joey Malone. Ex-girlfriend of former Spirit of ACW Champion and all-round douchebag Steve Knox. Now, she's here to live up to the legacy set before her by her brother and prove to the world she's more than just a pretty face who can be treated like priceless China. ... So what the fuck is she doing with a pair of fucking douchebags like the Heirs?!?! Other Feds tSC, NFW, Legion Other Fed Titles tSC Heavyweight Championship, tSC Evolutionary Championship Other Fed Awards See above Past Managers The Blue Rogues, Steve Knox Team The Heirs of Wrestling w/Frank Prince and Ryan Gallway Past Teams & Groups The Blue Rogues, unofficial member of the Steve Knox Posse Wrestling Style High Flyer with shades of kicking ability Finisher Move Any of the above Finisher Description Any of the above Special Moves Sunlight Slingshot (Asai Phoenix Splash) Alexandria springboards into the second rope and rebounds back with a corkscrew 450 splash. Think the Lionsault on steroids. Sun Shower (Standing corkscrew shooting star press) A flashy, showy move. Won't get the pin, but it'll look really cool. Sunstroke (Reverse hurricanrana) A brutal move that sees Alexandria basically dump an opponent onto their head. Can be done off the top rope, in which case, it's just called the Super Sunstroke. It could get the pin if it needs to, but Alex mostly uses it to set up the Sunspot or the Sunlight Press. SUNSPOT (Superkick) Alexandria has a particularly potent superkick despite her size, and while she can't always hit an opponent in the face, she can bring them to their knees and then hit them with it if she needs to. It might not always get the pin, but it WILL knock an opponent down just from the sheet force alone. EMERALD AVALANCHE (Shiranui / Sliced Bread #2) This is Alexandria's favorite finisher, she'll usually look to put an opponent away with this. Much like the move's inventor (Naomichi Marufuji), she may look to set this up with a superkick (or in Alex's case, the Sunspot) and then catch the opponent with it in a devastating one-two punch. But she also would be fond of just catching this move out of nowhere. SUNLIGHT PRESS (High elevation shooting star press) The difference between the Sunlight Press and most of Alex's other aerial moves (particularly of those that require a shooting star) that are similar to this is sheer impact. Alex gets an amazingly high elevation off of her shooting star press, enough so that it's almost akin to what the Five Star Frog Splash would be in shooting star form. So, the impact is actually large enough to hurt Alex herself at times. This is Alex's best finisher, but as she tends to hurt herself when she does it, she likes it the least.
