A C W YOU GOTTA FIGHT COURAGE 114 ¤ 03 JUNE 2010 GDYNIA SPORTS ARENA, GDYNIA, POLAND ready, set, go! Ahhh, Poland. COURAGE 114, being ACW's first stop ever in Poland, began in the most unusual way possible. Not in the ring, not in SilverHAWK's office and definitely not in Jeremy Hunt's office. Where, then? Oh, in a makeshift office designated to the bodyguard of the SlySports Programming Executive that had been given free reign to do whatever he damn well pleased for the night. Yes, indeed. WAR was, for all intents and purposes, God for the evening. "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen." WAR smiled thinly as he leaned back in his seat, his hands clasped together on the table. With the fans cheering loudly, WAR decided to dispense with his speech. "Tonight, I have been bestowed with opportunity to shape the way COURAGE unfolds. I aim to bring the best level of entertainment, and therefore, I have designed matches that I believe all of you will enjoy and that will be considered classics. Earlier on in the day, I give both Jesse Ramey and Khristain Keller the chance to pick opponents and match types for each other. This is, how you say, a Pick Your Poison special feature. Both men have been at loggerheads for long time now, and I decide to allow them to play 'booker' for one evening. Therefore, I am proud to announce two blockbuster matches!" Another round of excited cheers. The fans were liking what they were hearing so far. WAR nodded his head, pressing on. "The first... is a Handicapped Fight. Khristain Keller will fight JP Boudreau and new United States Champion Malk Al-Haq. In order for Keller to win, he must only defeat one man by pinfall. This is what Jesse Ramey wants, and this will be what Jesse Ramey gets. The second... is what is called a Blindfolded Ballad. Jesse Ramey will be blindfolded and taking on JAGI. Khristain Keller feels that this will be a big challenge for Jesse Ramey, so we shall see how a blindfolded man can survive in a Scorpion Fighting Rules environment! So far so good, yes?" The response WAR got? A roar so loud, the roof of the building almost flew right off. We also have a fight which has been weeks in the making!" WAR resumed, clearly relishing the role he was in. "Kati and Kristinia Kage will meet in the ring to settle differences; but with a twist. Upon request, the special guest referee for this fight will be none other than Sharc! That is not all. Over last few weeks, everybody has seen a group of three individuals coming into the arena and attacking people for fun of it. These individuals are called The Supernatural SWAT, and as of today are officially part of ACW roster. Tonight, they will have their debut in a unique Polish Gamble. What is Polish Gamble? Simple; it is Tables Match under Tornado Tag Scorpion Fighting Rules, whereby both members of the team must be driven through tables for the other team to win. The Supernatural SWAT will fight against the team of... Avo Chavez and Sepiroth Du Luc!" Talk about pulling out the awesome card. WAR certainly knew what the Polish fans wanted to see, and it appeared as if on this night they wished to bear witness to people being slammed through tables. Leaning forward, WAR cleared his throat and decided to wrap things up. "There are many other matches in store. For example, last week Orlando Grant and John Sarsgaard requested SilverHAWK for a match. Mr. Hunt has taken suggestion under advisement and given me power to go ahead to decide. So, tonight, we will see Orlando Grant and John Sarsgaard fight Jack Harris and 'The Alpha Dog' Mark Weiler. But there is a twist. I am adding additional members to each team. Orlando Grant and John Sarsgaard? Your partner is Chris Moliano. Jack Harris and Mark Weiler? Your partner... is Z!" Oh ho ho snaaaaap. WAR had just turned the oven up several notches, and the fans responded with a massive roar of approval. Chris Moliano and Z, facing off for the first time since their epic encounter at LEGENDS IV? Incredible. WAR afforded himself another thin smirk at the reception of the fans, but continued. "That is not all, ladies and gentlemen. I have many other matches in store. A tag team battle royale to determine new #1 contenders for the Tag Team Titles. England's Best defending those very Tag Team Titles against a tag team from another organisation. Not forgetting a fight that has been anticipated for a very long time; Fejona Min versus Omega, for the Scorpion Fighting Title. And finally, for main event, I have decided to have Keith Scott Zimmerman compete against 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs... and that fight will feature lumberjacks at ringside, with their identities to be revealed later. I am sure that with line-up that is in store, every one of you watching at home and every one in attendance will remember COURAGE 114 for very long time. And you will also remember that it is I that provided you with all of this. I look forward for rest of the evening. Enjoy." With those final words, WAR leaned back in his chair and nodded at the camera. The stage had been set for an absolutely massive show, probably the biggest in the SlySports era of COURAGE. And as the cameras faded away from WAR's makeshift office, many people were beginning to ask a collective question. What else could happen on a night like this? TV... Champion!
