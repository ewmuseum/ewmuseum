ACW // End Game Posted by Zezu on June 08, 2005 at 16:03:55: LINK - http://www.theacw.net/television/archive/2005/endgame.shtml Background - More or less a month in the making out of character, almost 3 years of the making in character, this PPV is the culmination of what has gone before, what is going on now and what is to pass in the future. Opinions will differ, maybe widely maybe not at all, but from a personal standpoint, I've never been as proud as I am right now.
