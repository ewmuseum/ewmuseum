Oh, how they loathed the Shillin' Villian. Lowell turned gingerly and glared, also gingerly, at the Danger Man, who stared right back at him, arms crossed wearing a very proud smirk on his face. Jessica, too, was doing the same. But it appeared that SilverHAWK wasn't quite finished. "And one last thing. The lone blemish on Max's record since End Game is a match against your friend Lowell, Jimmy Cain..." Smiling smugly was Mr. Dot Com. This, too, was done gingerly so as not to hurt himself anymore than he already was. "...which wasn't entirely on the up and up. So, next week, a rematch will be held. Jimmy Cain versus Max Danger. And Lowell..." The Scorpion Champ turned to look up at HAWK (again, yes, gingerly and with a noticable limp), "...yourself and your other lackey, the Lowell Dot Commie? They are barred from ringside." Through the cheers, HAWK finished with, "Have a nice day" and exited. Standing in the ring were Lowell Dot Com and Max Danger (and Jessica, but ya know). Lowell hung his head, mouth open, mic lingering just below his chin. He looked up and toward where HAWK had just been standing . . . and for some strange reason, began smiling (although it, LITERALLY pained him to do so, that burn on his face was in exactly the wrong place when he smiled). "Thank you, Mr. And Mrs. Silver COCK, sir, madam -- do you HONESTLY think I'm worried for the Jimmy? Huh? NO WAY! The Jimmy is a TRU WARRIORAHHHHHHH!" he turned, eyeing Max, " -- he beat you once Max and he'll do it again, that much I'm sure of. Get ready for a shitkicking the likes of which you've NEEEVVER experienced before! And as for you and I at Ready or Snot: Yeah, sure, what-ev." He tapped the Pepsi sticker on the Scorpio title. Hold for wincing... and continue. "Choice of a New Generation," he gestured to himself, then to the Danger Man, "HAS-BEEN! Think I'm scared? I'm not. You think I think you're gonna' take my precious Scorpio gold? NUH-UH! NO WAY! You must be confused or something -- have you been forgotting where you left your car keys lately? -- DO YOU HAVE ERECTILE DIFFICULTIES? -- Listen . . . Max . . . I'm better than you. Always have been, always will be. So you can suck Silver COCK off and get yourself a title shot, do you think that makes you GOOD? Is that TALENT? HEY! CAN WE GET THE MEXICAN STREET WHORE OUT HERE~!? I'M SURE SHE'S JUST AS SKILLED IN THE NECESSARY AREAS WARRANTING A TITLE SHOT!!" The Human Advertising Machine's face turned beat red. He ran his hand over it... very slowly. Arm hurtage and all that. "I hate you, Max, really . . . I do. I don't know what I'm going to do yet but you better be watching your back these next few weeks because I *will* be making a call to some very shady individuals. Or maybe I won't. Maybe I'll just come up from behind and beat you down with a lead pipe! We'll see . . . we'll see . . . It's been nice SCHOOLING you on the mic, but yeah, I've got more important shit to tend to . . ." And with that, Lowell turned, very gingerly, and left to the back walking very much like a mummy. All the while, Max and Jessica stood there, smirks on their faces. They'd gotten what they wanted; a title shot. Lowell could talk all the trash he wanted, because it didn't matter. It was simply talk, and Max had done more than he had ever wanted to. Now, now it was time for what he did best: wrestling. There really was only one more thing Danger wanted to say, and he said it in a low, creepy whisper into the microphone, "Ready or not, here I come." Cheesy, yes, but that's what you've got to expect from the Danger Man from time to time. "Born of a Broken Man" played once more and Max took part in celebration of not only his victory over Sonny Silver but also of getting a title shot against Lowell Dot Com at Ready Or Not. As he stood on the middle ropes, looking out into the crowd, his body covered in his own dried up blood, there was something apart from a title shot on his mind: Sars the Clown. //fade//
