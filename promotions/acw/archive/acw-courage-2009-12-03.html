ARC IV - Change December 2009 Recorded LIVE! from The Corral at Stampede Park, Calgary Card subject to change without notice INTRODUCTION It's been a weird year for ACW and as that year draws to a close with End Game not too far away the final Courage of the year is sure to draw some battle lines. But what side will you be on? The Saga Continues... The latest edition of Courage began where the last one left off. The ring was in a state of utter chaos. A recap video revisited last week's events in black and white film, with tints of red (think "Sin City") in Sharc's hair and upon the faces of ACW wrestlers. Khristian Keller was knocked out on the floor (courtesy of Trey Vincent bashing him in the head with the ACW World Title and a microphone); Andy Sharp was knocked out in the ring (courtesy of a ACW World Title-aided knee drop to the skull); and Alias, both of his wrists handcuffed to the top rope, was coming out of the fog, when he suddenly realized what had happened to him, and he began kicking wildly, but Sharc avoided the worst of it and nailed Alias in the jaw with a forearm. Vincent handed Sharc the microphone. "Alias," Sharc grunted. "Next week. You versus me." "I'll kill you!" Alias roared. Sharc didn't even flinch at the threat. "A long time ago in a federation far, far away, Sharc and Alias met under some unfortunate circumstances. Unfortunately for me, I had a bullet in my leg. So, Alias... When we meet next week. How good do you think you'll do with a bullet in your leg?" The crowd was dead silent. Sharc lifted up his sweatshirt to reveal a gun in the waistband of his jeans. Slowly, he removed the weapon from his waistline, and like something out of a nightmare, he put it to Alias' right leg. His right index finger on the trigger of the revolver, Sharc cocked the hammer of the gun with his thumb. Alias eyed the gun, then Sharc's empty eyes. Click was the sound of the cocking of the gun hammer. Alias' eyes closed, in preparation of the searing pain to come from his leg as bullet tore through flesh. Click was the sound of Sharc pulling the trigger. Alias' eyes remained closed, but there was no accompanying "boom." Silence Slowly, Alias, opened his eyes, curious as to what had happened. Had the gun misfired? Or was his life somehow over, and he was in some sort of soundless purgatory? Nope. Everything was the same. The fans were silent. And all Alias saw before his face was the smiling face of Sharc, a man who was begging for a one-way ticket to a world of Deadmotherfuckerdom. Sharc, no doubt, was happy in the complete mind fuck he had just pulled off on Alias, a man who had seen a hell of a lot of mind fucks in his time in this business. But then, for Alias, it all went to white and blurry. Quickly and violently, Sharc struck, bashing the gun barrel into the side of Alias' face, sending a white lightning shock of pain through Alias' eyes and skull. The only thing keeping Alias somewhat vertical were the handcuffs that had imprisoned him in the ropes. The fans booed as Sharc stuck his empty revolver into his black sweatshirt, and he and his business partner Trey Vincent stood tall. Victorious and conquering. One plastic cup half-full of beer flew into the ring. Then two more plastic cups. Followed within moments by a barrage of garbage. Vincent and Sharc raised their arms high in the air, celebrating their dirty deeds, even as they were pelted by trash. That was last week. Tonight, Khristian Keller was scheduled to get his shot at revenge on Trey Vincent, and Alias and Sharc were also set to go to battle. Some "war games" one show before End Game. Shall we play? Aggravated Sexual Assault? "Chilliwack? That has to be the most retarded name for a Canadian hellhole Trey Vincent has heard in at least two days!" Yes, Trey Vincent was just randomly insulting a Canadian town tonight for no obvious reason. The fans in the arena couldn't even hear his insults at the moment. After all, he was backstage in an empty hallway. And in Calgary, a city nine hours away from the object of his verbal assaults, no less. "Chilliwack? What do you scrubs do around here for fun? Jack off in the chili, then scarf down each other's semen-flavored chili? It's the sequel to 2 Girls, 1 Cup. Two Tards, 1 Bowl of Chili!" OK…now I'm really confused. Perhaps ACW's (self-proclaimed) Franchise Player was practicing his insults for an in-ring promo at a future ACW event? Or maybe he's really drunk and somebody ribbed him and told him he's in some other town? Whatever the case, his flow was interrupted when a blonde woman ran into him and collapsed to the floor from the impact. TV looked down and realized she was crying. "Oh, come on, the impact wasn't THAT hard." She sniffed loudly, as Vincent extended a hand to help her up. Sheepishly, she grabbed his large hand, but she kept frequently looking back from where she had come from. That's when Trey realized he had just been thrust into the middle of a situation of some sort. "You OK, honey?" Trey noticed that her white long-sleeved shirt had a large tear down the front, which she was desperately trying to keep shut with her left hand. Despite the eye makeup that was running down her cheeks, the woman was quite attractive, with shoulder-length blonde hair, and a face that vaguely resembled Sarah Michelle-Gellar. "He…he…" Trey cocked his head to the side and looked in the direction where she kept looking. "Let's get you to a safe place." The blonde jerked her hand away, as her eyes went wide in fear. She apparently wasn't in a trusting mood at the moment. "It's fine. My dressing room is right there. Whoever...did whatever to you won't dare come into my dressing room. Trey Vincent is the biggest star in ACW. Trey Vincent could rip the head off a bear and then take a dump down its blood spewing neck hole before the bear's heart even stops beating. No one fucks with me." There was a loud bang in the direction from where the blonde had just run from. That was enough to send her over the edge and to trust Vincent. "I called the cops. You'll keep me safe from him until they get here, eh?" "Sure, honey. Come on." With that, Trey took the woman's hand and led her into his private dressing room. Inside, it was dark, so Trey flipped on a light and had a quick look around. "It's safe. It even has a lock on the door. What happened? Did somebody attack you? You are quite bangable, let's be honest." After a couple of deep breaths, she launched into her tale: "Well, my name's Sarah Summers. I work for the Stampede Corral. I was minding my own business, doing some paperwork in an office backstage for my boss, you know, when all of a sudden he was there. At first, I thought he was lost, but then he told me he had been watching me earlier. I told him I wasn't interested, but he's all talking aboot fucking and shitting on a mountain, and I'm just looking for an exit. He was sooo hideous. So bald and ugly and crazed. I think I might vomit." "Bald? Ugly?" Trey snapped his fingers. "Keller!" "Who's that now, eh?" "He's always talking about fucking mountains full of shit. Dude is craaaazy. You have no clue how lucky you are. Stay here. You'll be safe. Nobody can get in." That's when the door opened and Sharc strolled in. "Ahhh!" the blonde screamed. "Yeah, I have that effect on women," Sharc deadpanned. "Sharc, perfect. Keller just sexually assaulted this woman. I need to go talk to SilverHAWK before we have a Benoit-situation on our hands." Sharc looked around. "I don't see any electrical cords." "Just STFU and quit making jokes, jerk! I'm serious." "Ah, you must be. You just went to first-person." Sharc sighed. "Fine. You're the boss. I'll protect her with my life. And this gun," he said, digging out the weapon he had used one week earlier against Alias. He lightly petted the revolver with his right hand. "Are there any NORMAL people in ACW? Jesus," the blonde said. "Next time you guys are in town, I'm calling in sick." "You must be talking about Omega, because Trey Vincent is totally normal." "Aside from the whole talking in third person thing, eh?" "Fine," Trey said. "Just lock the door and don't open it except for me." Trey paused, looking at Sharc with the gun. Quickly, Trey yoinked the gun away from Sharc and handed it to the blonde. "Maybe YOU should have this to keep him honest." "Good call," she said. The camera followed Trey as he exited the room and went looking for SilverHAWK. After just a few moments, Trey found the boss near the "gorilla" position. Or, perhaps the "Hawk" position? "HAWK! You've got to toss Keller out of this building, and hell, the whole COMPANY while you're at it. Do you have any clue what he's done?" The camera cut to the opposite side of the building. There, four police officers were walking toward Khristian Keller, who was at a soda vending machine. The camera cut back to TV and HAWK. "This is all your fault, HAWK! What are you gonna do about it?" "Wait, how is this my fault? And WHAT is my fault? You do realize you haven't told me one damn thing yet?" "Oh, right. You missed it all. Well..." The cameras cut back to Keller and the cops. "Mr. Keller, you are under arrest for aggravated sexual assault." "I'm WHAT?!" Keller screamed. "Do you understand?" "No I don't fucking understand, asshole!" "You have the right to retain and instruct counsel without delay. We will provide you with a toll-free telephone lawyer referral service, if you do not have your own lawyer. Anything you say can be used in court as evidence. Do you understand? Would you like to speak to a lawyer." "What language are you speaking right now?" Keller said. "I obviously can't understand a god damn thing you just said because I don't speak Bullshitese! The only person I plan to assault tonight is Trey Vincent!" That was when the cops demeanor changed to something much more serious. Two of the officers removed their Glocks from their holsters. Keller, quite obviously pissed but not wanting to risk an incident, dropped his soda and turned around to let the officers cuff him. "This is crap." The camera cut back to Trey and SilverHAWK. "And THAT is what happened." Trey pulled out his cell phone to check the time. "Actually, by this point, I guess you won't have to kick him out. But in a bit, you may have to bail him out. Because that bald bastard is on his way to jail!" SilverHAWK, who was completely disoriented by the entire tale TV had spun in the last several seconds about blondes, baldies, and Benoits, started to say something, but Vincent was already walking away.
