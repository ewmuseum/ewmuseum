Breaking Kayfabe — by Jason Kain

What this is A column written in the voice of the character Jason Kain on the fed's first site, after the King of Ages 2003 card, as captured in the Wayback on 10 October 2003.
Breaking Kayfabe By Jason Kain Holy McFuck! I never expected to see an outcome like that. The first ever Jason Kain PPV Main Event, and I still even surprised myself. From the beginning, everything was strange… a “storyline” as you will. They wanted to book Chris and I for the main event, but didn’t know how to do it… Hell, WE didn’t even know how we were going to pull this off without either killing each other, or running it into the ground. Then we came up with a beautiful statement. Ratings Instead of trying to make up a reason for one of us to hate the other, we just went and kicked ass. Sure, Jade leaving me wasn’t exactly a planned situation. Hell, I love her beyond this federation… and it seemed as if Chris was the only person to understand that. Not even I figured that out. Not until I was willing to give up did I fully understand the situation I was in. At first I blew up destroying everything, just because I couldn’t have her. Then I realized, I COULDN’T have her… and Chris finally realized that she felt the same way. And boom. Here we are. I’m an engaged man. Chris is ACW’s world champion. How thing change in a matter of days. But I do have to say the people backstage are amazing with everyone. It doesn’t seem that the federation has the same type of anger towards each other backstage. It’s like we’re a family. It may sound really stupid, but it was proven at the end of King of Ages. Heh… I even watched the taping of the show, and I swear Dante Inferno, who has been a proven asshole, was lifting his hand in salute. But, I digress. This federation couldn’t be without people like him. Though I did notice a few things that made me get a little bit pissed. After the shows, everyone congratulates each other on a job well done. Sure, there’s sometimes a bit of bad blood between feud partners, due to someone taking a little more leeway in the match than they should have… or possibly the unseen rivalry, due to someone getting a bigger push of the sorts… but all in all, everyone usually congratulates the winner AND loser of a match. And after King Of Ages, I was in for the strange treat of EVERYONE being there. Everyone, except for two people. SilverHAWK wasn’t at the show… so he makes sense. But the other? Vincent Pembridge. If anyone has been dragging himself away from the backstage and singling himself out to be the biggest dick back there, it’s gotta be Mr. Pembridge. He pulls off really shitty plans with his cronies, then goes through with them and actually injures and kills in the ring… or at least beside it. Death is not a funny thing, folks. Sure we hated him, but when Ethan Winters died we still mourned the loss of a great man. Same thing with Knickerson, he should have never died from that stunt. But it seemed to be played off as an “accident.” Sometimes I truly disagree. NO ONE deserves to have their life ended. NO ONE. But as we saw, we do have situations we can’t control. Maybe one day people will see that we do this as a fun way to entertain… not just a place to fight endlessly and cause a ruckus. Quinton was destroyed backstage… a fair storyline had been ruined by Pembridge, who had taken the whole thing too seriously. I know Jade had talked to May, and so had I and Alias… Quinton only stated that it was HIS TURN. And I think we all understood what that meant. Look, we here at ACW know that there’s risks, and at times, those risks become worse when we act stupid. Sometimes, I think we should just cut the losses, and fire Pembridge… but then there’s that side of me that knows I’ve been there myself… I was jailed for breaking someone’s knee. Back then, I figured it was justified by my jealousy. But in all reality, I was a dick. I should have been proud. I learned my lesson. And maybe the youngins in this business, AND the people who should know better, will learn theirs. Until that day, we’ll sit here and entertain… and the risks will be taken, hoping we put on a good show for you. Now, if you don’t mind, I need to get back to my fiancée. I believe the word might be… um… horny. I’m Jason Kain, and this was Breaking Kayfabe.
