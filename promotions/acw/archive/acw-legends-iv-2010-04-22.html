The subtle voices of various All-Stars voiced over a montage of the previous months worth of wrestling. ACW. LEGENDS IV. Take a breath. Take a moment. This is a moment to look around and realise just how lucky you are to be here; right here and right now. This is a moment that will live in your memory forever as being the greatest single wrestling experience of your life. This is a moment to know deep in your heart that this will out-perform anything you have ever seen. This is the moment to realise that not everything will go as planned. This is the moment to know that someone will show up that you didn't expect. But. This isn’t a moment for nostalgia. This isn’t a moment for looking back at the previous installments of the biggest pay-per-view and supershow events that ACW has ever had with a tear in your eye, thinking back at how cool it was when such and such was around. This isn’t a moment when you take note that your nearest rivals are dead in the water or they flat out don't exist. This isn’t a moment to rest on your laurels. This isn't a moment to stop going upwards and onwards. This isn't a moment to think you have made it. This isn’t a moment. This is ACW. As the SlySports Entertainment LLC logo vanished from all visible screens it seemed that a toggle had been moved somewhere in a command centre of sound because the noise that soon greeted every household viewer not lucky enough to be in Spain went to a level that could only be described as ‘holy shit’. Panning the Nou Camp in Barcelona, home of the football champions of not only Europe but of the World, F.C. Barcelona. The viewer bore witness to a sea of bodies holding their signs and their flags and screaming at the top of their lungs. They all pointed their heads in on single direction. The top of the rampway. Standing under a gigantic version of the SlyTron at the beginning of this ramp was SilverHAWK. ACW's General Manager and bona fide ‘Legend’ himself was dressed to the nines in a smart grey suit and brown shoes, his body bursting at its seams as he looked around at the crowd with a huge smile on his face and a skip in his step. He looked down at the ring, the Spanish sunshine gracing it with its presence for the meantime. Keeping it warm until the All-Stars came out and gave it a good seeing to and bathed it with their blood. It was something out of this Wworld looking around him. He soaked in the adulation for a while before placing the microphone to his lips as the Spaniards hushed their mouths. “... welcome to LEGENDS FUCKING FOUR!” In a throwback to last year’s event, SilverHAWK opened in a rather unusual style for a GM... but he didn’t care. But I have some business to attend to tonight, and what better way to get it done that come out here and talk to you amazing fans, because not even a fucking volcano can stop ACW!” The Spanish crowd roared once again as the whole world had come to a standstill because of a volcano in Iceland that nobody could pronounce. “Tonight is going to be a busy night, that’s a fact, so I will get straight to the point with this. Argent. Keller. Ramey.” The Spanish fans booed until they heard the name of Jesse Ramey… all three men were in a situation that needed to be resolved. “My plan for this situation was to have it solved neatly by now, but hey; that backfired. You get pinned or made to submit… you are out until only one man is standing.” The fans cheered and raised their signs as another match had been officially signed for what was going to be an epic card. “I also want to stress that this will be a long show, ladies and gentlemen.
