God's Forgotten Son Biography Real Name Ethan Winters Jr. Nicknames The Forgotten King, GFS Hometown New York City, New York Birthday March 27th, 1981 (29) Place of Birth Seattle, Washington Height 6'2" Weight 247 Blood Type B negative Eye Color Piercing and revengeful, Chestnut brown Biography For most of human history we have searched for our place in the cosmos. Who are we? What are we? We find that we inhabit an insignificant planet of a hum-drum star lost in a galaxy tucked away in some forgotten corner of a universe in which there are far more galaxies than people. We make our world significant by the courage of our questions, and by the depth of our answers. - Carl Sagan 'Tis better to be silent and be thought a fool, than to speak and remove all doubt. - Mark Twain I never thought i always knew I’d look back on my tears and laugh, but i never knew I’d look back on my laughter and cry. - Unknown The graveyards are full of indispensable men. - Charles de Gaulle Be not afraid of greatness. Some are born great, Some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them. - William Shakespeare Once you eliminate the impossible, whatever remains, no matter how improbable, must be the truth. - Sherlock Holmes (by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle) Nothing can stop the man with the right mental attitude from achieving his goal; nothing on earth can help the man with the wrong mental attitude. - Thomas Jefferson Human beings, who are almost unique in having the ability to learn from the experience of others, are also remarkable for their apparent disinclination to do so. - Douglas Adams Other Feds ACW, LoC, Legion Other Fed Titles ACW United States Championship Other Fed Awards 2004 ACW King of Ages (defeated Quinton May in the finals) Team The Huntsmen (w/Hunt, Khristain Keller, WAR and Abilities) Wrestling Style The key to God's Forgotten Son's success is Vale Tudo influence, where he blended all his techniques and skills as an indestructible base (fierce striking, beautiful submissions, brilliant grappling) to attack his victim's entire body with unpredictability and the highest proficiency. G.F.S. pinpoints the weaknesses in his victim like a surgeon, due to his tremendous in-ring intelligence/confidence and strong focus on psychology. However, it is unknown as of yet how Ethan has progressed with other fighting techniques during his 2 year absence from in-ring competition. Finisher Move This variation sees God's Forgotten Son first lock an victim in the pumphandle hold before then using the hold to raise the victim up over G.F.S.'s own shoulder. From here God's Forgotten Son slams the victim down into an unfathomably stiff sit-down piledriver. The force of the move is transmitted directly down onto the victim's spine, compressing vertebrae and torquing the victim's neck between the legs of the G.F.S. Finisher Description 'A CHANGE OF FAITH' (Primary Finisher) Special Moves Planchas: Like those before him, G.F.S. occasionally uses some type of death-defying flying move on the outside (only during important match-ups). These are different than his other moves since he can do them any time he feels like it which is very rare he would do them at all. Over the top rope no-hands moonsault plancha, top rope moonsault plancha, top rope somersault plancha High-Level Impact moves: Late in the match impact moves. At this point, G.F.S. is getting frustrated to the point where he could outright end a match if the opportunity presents itself. All variations of suplexes (regal-plex, top rope head and arm suplex, release northern lights suplex, wheelbarrow suplex, chimera-plex) Krush rush (Running fireman's carry throw onto the top turnbuckle), stiff Wild bomb, brainbuster onto the knee, - High-level Gutbusters (Gorilla press, Fireman's carry w/ both knees, super fireman's carry*, super fireman's carry w/ both knees) - (face-oriented) Mirko Crocop like roundhouse kick to the temple, (Max Danger style) shining wizard, Dangerous III, SHI NO UTSU (running soccer-style kick to the face) High-Level Submission holds. Late in the match submission moves; unlike the mid-level ones, these can all pick up a victory for G.F.S. - Back (Gorilla Clutch Hold, Figure Four Deathlock) - Legs (Dragon Clutch/STF variant, Cross kneelock) - Arms (Dragon wing, Iron Octopus, Anaconda Cross) - Neck (Border City Stretch, Dragon clutch w/ leg scissors) While all these moves G.F.S. can do below, he is not limited to just these. He can do virtually anything you can think of, but of course as he uses common sense to wear down his opponent to build to the end of the match with unexpected offense. Alternate Finishers 'SACRIFICAL RETRIBUTION' (High-Risk Finisher) Also been known as the Imploding Star Press or Flaming Star Press, this version is a double rotation 450 in which God's Forgotten Son gets on the top turnbuckle' jumps backwards and executes a 450 splash inwards (facing the turnbuckle) onto a downed victim laying on the mat. G.F.S. curls into a tight cannonball as he rotates, stretching out his body only a split-second before hitting the victim. By tucking his body in as close as possible to his center of rotation, God's Forgotten Son increases his velocity and the massive body-to-body impact, due to his heavyweight size. This version of 450 splash is considered a very dangerous maneuver. Both to execute and to receive in professional wrestling, thus God's Forgotten Son is one of the few wrestlers in the world who can consistently perform this maneuver. As G.F.S.'s aerial finisher, which is VERY RARE and he will use this when he feels his opponent is KNOCKED THE FUCK OUT. Moving out of the way or placing something between G.F.S. and his target is the best way of avoiding full on contact. 'A WILLING SACRIFICE' (Primary Submission) The victim is forced to the ground, while God's Forgotten Son lies on their back. At a 90-degree angle to the victim, G.F.S. puts some or all of his weight on the victim to prevent them from moving. The victim's arms are then hooked and pulled back into their body in a full nelson; stretching the arms, shoulders, neck and pectoral muscles. Once fully locked on, G.F.S. places his head in between the victim's shoulder blades and performs a front flip for extra leverage, which leaves the victim in an unnatural position, causing immense pressure and lack of leverage to counter while God's Forgotten Son immobilizes him. This excruciating Grounded Full Nelson hold with bridge, is nearly impossible to escape so once it's locked on, it almost ALWAYS gains the win by submission. ANOTHER G.F.S. ORIGINAL G.F.S.'s top submission and it deserves to be his finisher, based on the way he viciously attacks the body so when it is locked on, the pain becomes too much for the victim to handle. If someone is creative enough to counter the move or reverse it into a pinning predicament, they could score an upset victory. 'THE FALL OF MEN' (Ultimate Finisher) An avalanche variation of 'A Change of Faith' has G.F.S. lift the victim up onto his shoulders in the fisherman suplex, very similar to the high leg cradle suplex. From there, G.F.S. swings them around into the sitout piledriver as both crash into the canvas below. Both versions of the maneuver drop the victim down on their head/upper back. Needless to say, this is rather effective. If this alone wasn't effective enough, G.F.S.'s entire offense basically gears around hitting and kicking his victim in the head as hard as he can. Thus victim is often powerless to resist the 'Change of Faith' or 'The Fall of Men' and it usually seals the match.
