'Astonishing' Andy Sharp The Astonishing One, The Superduperfly, The Highlight of the Night II, The Whole F'N Channel, Mr. Whatever Night Courage Comes On, The Lord of the Skies, Man of 1,000 Planchas, Dictator of Dives, Fuhrer of Flying, H311A 1337 Real Name: Andrew Justin Sharp Birthdate: 3/17/83 Hometown: Pikeville, KY (Born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada) Height: 6'5' Weight: 242 lbs. Entrance Music: 'Vertigo' by U2 Medical Information Natural Hair Color: blonde Eye Color: green Blood Type: O+ Body Fat Percentile: 12% Favored Workout: Weight lifting, aerobics, acrobatic-like shit, I'd wager. Past Illnesses: Chicken Pox, influenza Past Injuries: Twenty-two stitches placed in forehead as a result of the events of Courage 72 in which Alias flapjacked him into steel steps. In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Mother Canucker. Yes, that's what he's calling it now. Essentially, his own Sports Entertainment brand of wrestling. Exhibits traits of brawling, technical/amateur, slight comedy offense and a few high-impact moves. Basic at everything with the exception of a heavy emphasis on flying. Trainer: Elijah Brown and the Everlasting School of Excellence Status: Face Set-Up Move(s): The 1337 F337 STIFF Owen Hart-like Enziguri; comes in many variations. Finishing Move: The Sharper Image/The Unnecessary Risk The Cradle Shock (in lamen's terms, Cross-Legged Fireman's Carry Michinoku Driver)/High-Impact 450 splash Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: None ACW Awards: Television Champion (Current) Other Federations: None. ACW is his very first professional endeavor Other Federations Awards: None. Career History MATCH WRITING AID: Unique Traits about Mr. Sharp: -Wise for his age. Slightly immature, however. -Impulsive -Germaphobe -Loves women -Big fan of wrestlers of the high-flying/luchador persuasion -Canadian and damn proud of it. A practitioner of the same Americanized Lucha Libre that mentor Eli Brown brought to the table, it’s only made more dangerous by the size of Andy Sharp. He’s about the size of Edge with a younger, more vibrant energy about him, making for a dangerous combination. From his high school days, he also does possess a rudimentary background in amateur wrestling so he can at least build from there in his matches in order to go for the bigger moves and high spots. While he does have some good basic ground ability, he absolutely loves taking to the sky in order to dish out damage, no matter the size of his opponents. He also has an affinity for kicking his opponent into jelly. He’s incredibly fast for a man of his size and body frame and he takes full advantage of this by executing a lightning-fast form of offense from the time the bell rings. He can dabble in just about any style you can think of, but by FAR, he isn’t he best at any of them with the sole exception of the flying. And now, the WEAKNESSES: -He only has a year experience underneath his belt, meaning that he is a rookie and that he is prone to making your typical rookie mistakes. -His explosive offense and personality in the ring mean that he’s hitting you hard and fast. The problem with that? He has to expend a lot of energy, thus meaning that his stamina isn’t the best at times. He could probably wrestle a good twenty minutes at most before tiring the fuck out. -He can dabble in any style, but he isn’t the best at everything. Alias would break him down in a contest of mat wrestling. Kelly Flawless’ power would dominate him. Khristian Keller would probably beat the piss out of him, knowing his aggressiveness in the ring. -Submissions are not his friend, either. He’ll simply try to use his tall body to try and power out of the opponent’s submission and fight like hell to keep himself from being in one. He also doesn’t have a lot of know-how to dabble in submissions himself. -Doesn't drop people on their heads, EVER. Most notably, piledriver variations of any kind. This is a product of his lucha training. MOVELIST! HOLY. SHIT. SPOT. EVER. (Running Springboard 450 Suicida) UNIQUE TRAITS: Smart. Big fan of fellow wrestlers. Tends to get emotional in his matches. Funny. Smart-ass. Won't back down from anyone. BIOGRAPHY: Eli Brown, a multiple-time world champion in a place called the Wrestling Battle Ground, he retired about five years ago due to a crippling knee injury, but kept the “Everlasting School of Excellence,” his own training gym, open. Eli Brown, a practitioner of Americanized Lucha Libre, has taught some of the finest students of the game out there. He taught people that the art of flying is just as important in the everyday wrestling world as your mat technicians and hardcore specialists. Then Andy Sharp came along. Andy Sharp graduated with honors from a local high school in Toronto, Ontario, Canada and went to the States, seeking a career in wrestling. He, like just about every other wrestler out there, sought to achieve this destiny by being a long-time fan of the sport. Mainly the daredevils. Jimmy Snuka. Sabu. Rob Van Dam. Just to name a few. He loved their ability to grab the crowd with death-defying maneuvers and natural charisma. He heard of the ESE located in Pikeville, KY and knowing a relative there, he stayed with them to pursue such a journey. Easily the finest student that Eli Brown has had the honor of teaching, Andy caught on quick. He had a massive body, about 6’6”, close to 240 pounds. His tall frame and powerful leg strength gave him an excellent natural balance, giving him an edge over even the smaller students. Eventually when Andy felt he was ready to pursue the world of professional wrestling, he traveled local indy circuits while earning some cash. Soon after, Eli found him a job. Enter: ACW. And that’s the crappy, summarized story. Learn it. Love it. Or don’t love it. Like I give a shit. :-P Quotes "Well...ain't that some shit.
