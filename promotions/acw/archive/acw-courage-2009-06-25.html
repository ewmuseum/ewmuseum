Who Killed Seymour Almasy: Andy Sharp It was a rainy evening in Montreal, a hell of a way to start his particular Courage, but Mr. All-Star felt confident about the night. It was counter to how his night last week ended, crashing in flames against the notoriously harsh competition in Brian Spaes. "Wanted to ask you a few questions about Almasy," but before the start of anything brand new, Sharp felt the reach of the specter of his early ACW career, in Christopher Sheffield, as the Original Pulp Hero held the back door of the ACW Arena open for ACW’s Golden Boy to enter. "How could it not be, man?!” Sharp’s fists were now whitened, as he clenched them in frustration and slow burning anger, “Look, you might want to act like this is different then the last time you had a mad on against Keller. He looked up, “No, I think I’m free, buuuuuuuuut...” The Danger Man stood up off of Marcus and looked directly into the fierce eyes of Brian Spaes. “I don’t think,” Max looked at his phone again, “no, no, I won’t be defending this belt,” Danger tapped it with his finger, after which Marcus sprayed and wiped it down, “so soon after this first defense.” “. . .” On one hand Spaes was completely dumbfounded and frustrated. He also knew he wasn’t going to let the ACW World Champion escape this situation unscathed. His face turned scarlet and Marcus gulped. “I don’t care if you want to defend it or not. I want. To. Fight. You. Book the match Danger Man. You and me next week. I won’t let you go another week out of my hands.” “Aww, that’s sweet, Brian,” Danger said, patting Spaes on the cheek with Armsy. Probably not the wisest of moves, considering how on the brink the Man Who History Forgot of destroying him he was. Max pulled Armsy back and put it to his chin, thinking. “Hmm.” That was the sound he made when he was thinking. “I got it,” the Danger Man said, lifting Armsy up into the air in an ‘ah ha!’ moment. Max, with a smirk on his face, said to Brian. “Even though we all know it’ll be your little buddy Trevor, let’s have ourselves a little tag match. You can have Trevor or whoever else you want -- like you could get someone else to team with you,” Danger scoffed at the notion, “and I’ll get me a partner. How’s that sound?” ACW’s Devil nodded intently at the King of ACW. His disposition improved a bit as he wasn’t Threat Level Midnight anymore. He went face to face for a second and then stepped away and had a sly smile himself. ”Fine. Tag match. Find someone and I’ll see you outside. You know what? I’ll enjoy beating you twice. After I win, I’ll knock you out another time with the Uraken and take that strap. See you outside Danger.” “Toodles,” said the World Champion, waving with Armsy, before he hopped out of the ring with Marcus and climbed back into his throne on which he was carried to the back, leaving Speas in the middle of the ring.
