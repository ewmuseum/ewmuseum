Iceman Real Name: Colt Slinton Nickname: "Snap Case" Born: 12/24/1977 Place of Birth: Fontana, CA Hometown: None given. Height: 6'4 Weight: 220 lbs Blood type: O Negative Eye color: Ice blue Team: None. Manager: None. Trainer: Unknown. Theme song: “First it Giveth” Queens of the Stone Age Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Technical Signature moves: Standing Missile Dropkick; 'The Edge' (Hotshot into a Front Russian Legsweep). Finisher: 1.) Pearl River Plunge; 2.) Camel Clutch Finisher name: 1.) Respect; 2.) The Icecap Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: PIW. Other/Previous teams: PIW: Dynamic Duo (w/ Blast From the Past). Other/Previous managers: None. Other Wrestling titles won: PIW Tag Team Champions w/ Blast From the Past (3); PIW Television Champion. Biography Iceman’s a pretty common alias. Trust us, he’s not a pretty common guy. Four quick things you need to know: 1.) He’s extremely competitive. 2.) He has a SERIOUS temper. 3.) Yes, he used to be in PIW, and yes, he was a big time jobber. 4.) Pretend #3 never existed. Please revert back to points #1 and 2. For your own sake. --- You might remember Iceman, you might not. Hopefully for your sake, you don’t. That’s not because Iceman used to be PIW’s biggest jobber. It’s not because his former teammate, Blast From the Past, was a three-hundred-pound, out of shape, joke of a wrestler. It’s not even because Iceman was once as big of a joke as anyone else. It’s because these days, Iceman has a very serious temper. Gone are the times of being laughed at… and making himself look stupid just to get on TV. It’s taken years and years of hard work and determination, but Iceman has become a pretty decent wrestler. Enough of one that he doesn’t have to put on an act anymore. Enough of one that the ACW officials decided to give him a tryout, and were unanimous in choosing to add him to the roster. They also disregarded his past run in this federation. Yes, he still uses the nickname Iceman. And although it’s a common and overused alias, Iceman wants to clear his name. He’s no joke. He’s no jobber. And he sure as hell isn’t going about this run lightly. Please, for your sake, it’s best not to talk about his past. Remind yourself of his tempter. And if you try him... You’ll probably be sorry.
