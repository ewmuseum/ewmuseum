Remember what I said to you at Legends? I guess these last two weeks… this is the world's end." "Nah," Masters, the Machine, shook his head with a grin, "Not yet." Then Masters walked away from the War Horse, and rolled from the ring. Then something hit Alias, as he rushed to the side of the ring, and yelled out over a hushing crowd, "Sympathy for the Devil" playing in the background. "You never said yes to joining the Minutemen!" Now the crowd really hushed. It was seemingly the inverse of the questions left in the chaos of Alias joining the revolution just one week ago. Masters, looked out to the balcony for a moment, to a mixture of cheering and jeering. His granite etched face hid any emotion in that moment. "I'll join you. Yes. But, all politics aside… not the Mintuemen." With that, the night ended… with a Ronin seemingly now within the Revolution. WINNER: Alias via pinfall (A-Bomb)
