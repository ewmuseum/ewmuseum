"For weeks now jennifer, me and you have been playing a peaceful game of cat and mouse, and well tonight, looking at that cage right there, reminds me of a game I used to play as a youth. Mousetrap. You see, I've now pushed the button, and all those little balls and leavers have been pushed, pulled and prodded, and now in the middle of the ring, stands the mouse, and that big silver thing around you in the trap." Keller chuckled. "Let me digest." The crowd boo'd as they guessed this match wouldn't be starting for a while. "You see, what I find most odd, if that the fact you would put those TWO people in danger over all of this..." Keller pointed, and the ARENA took note. "Ladies and gentleman, meet Monet and Izzy, Alias' family." Like a caged pitbull, Alias jumped up against the cage and began to bark at Alias, motioning to the referee to open the door. "Calm down dear..." Keller walked around to the opposite side of the cage. "I'm merely saying chief, just seems very weird given my wild nature to put those two anywhere near me." Keller peered into Monets eyes, she raised a small smile. SMASH Keller smashed his fist against the hard steel cage as Izzy's eyes began to well, but then all of a sudden, Keller began to smile as he motioned for the referee to open the cage door, and ushered Alias to the other side. "I just wanted to fuck with your head Alias...no malice." His right foot sat ontop of the ring steps. "But." Then it came off. "You know rusty, I've been thinking a little before the match and that, and well, I came to the conclusion that, neither of us would really get anything out of this would we? OK, we've pissed each other about for the last couple of weeks, but what in the end would us beating each other to a pulp in that cage lead to?" The crowd gave their answers. "Don't listen to those muppets." The crowd gave their abuse. "I don't want to split you open from ear to ear right in front of your own kin...that's not my style, I'm far more subtle than that, as you have probably concluded by now ladies and gentleman." Alias came to the edge of the cage. "Keller, where the fuck are you going with this?" He laughed. "Straight to the top baby. You see, for weeks now the ACW World Championship has been vacant, and for all it's pretenses, and for all the vileness that seems from the pores of this company, it is the one true thing that keeps me within it's five walls. By the looks of it, my shot may have come and gone, only to be ruined by either you, or by Jacobs...and I've yet to get my revenge." Alias looked over at his family...beads of sweat dropping from his brow. As Keller went off on another tangent as his voice grew deeper. "Vince Jacobs, former CWL and ACW Champion, current fWo superstar and then Alias, former ACW and Squared Circle Champion, former, fWo superstar...sounds good doesn't it, rolls off the tongue." Keller smiled at Alias. "Khristain...Keller." He was smiling no more. "I might not have the title Christopher...but I have it up here," he prodded at his temple. "You see, I'm the brains of this bitch...fuck, I'm the Nostradamus of this beast! Fuck...I'm even better than that, Nostradamus predicted to the future...I dictate it." Keller paced around the cage, in-rant his arms flared in all directions. "I've took everyone for the ride Alias, you, Jacobs, Duncan, HAWK, fuck...even Laguna and Joe Campbell have been assistants to where I currently stand...at the turning of the tide." Keller was now at the door of the cage. "You and Jacobs...you both have weaknesses, and it's a weakness that only the powerful dawn." Keller pushed the referee in the face as he toppled off the ring steps. "You have everything to lose...as I have everything to gain." Keller dropped the microphone as the cage door was sealed shut from the outside, as Alias rushed to the door, Keller launched the keys into the crowd. "Good luck...fuckhead." Then, as quick as lightning, Keller jumped backwards off of the ring steps and clutched Alias' young daughter in his mitts. "NNNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO..." Alias dropped to his knees as he looked at the stone cold face of Khristain Keller whizzing past him and up the ramp towards the backstage. Monet, now raising from her position after being tossed off her chair in the exchange, screamed at the top of her lungs... "HE TOOK HER...HE TOOK OUR BABY CHRIS!" As Izzy cried, Keller walked up the ramp as the tron came to life, it's sound booming and rebounding off every surface in the arena...a scarce camera crew follows a clambering Vince Jacobs back to his locker room finally, only to see his wife bound and gagged with the rest of the room demolished and smashed into a million pieces. As she yelped through the material, which was then loosened, she uttered, "she's gone..." Alias, climbing the steel cage watched on as the picture flicked to Keller, a screaming Izzy in his hand as she stretched out for her father, who was nowhere to be seen. They quickly passed open corridors as an escape route was obviously planned out, and outside sat a running vehicle at the back of the arena. The picture then changed for all in the arena as Keller placed Izzy into the backseat, Jacobs' daughter already in the passenger side children seat, screaming also, and as Keller ran over to the driver-side, he got into the car and put it in gear, turning back only to see Jacobs and Alias chasing him in the distance. "Everything to gain huh...and this is all the leverage I'm gonna ever need." And as the car sped off, Jacobs and Alias coming up the rear, the two former champions stopped as the ongoing car turned the corner and was quickly out of sight...Alias dropping to his knees once more and sobbing into the concrete below him, Jacobs on the other hand, looking around for the nearest vehicle, but as not one could be found, he cursed and turned away...
