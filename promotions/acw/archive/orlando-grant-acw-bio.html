Orlando Grant Biography Real Name Orlando James Grant Nicknames The OG of ACW, Air Force One, The Quarantine Boy, The Ziploc Boy, Original Gangsta Hometown Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn, NY Birthday 4/23/1986 Place of Birth Yonkers, New York Height Five foot and eleven inches Weight 226 Blood Type O+ Eye Color Brown Biography One of the very first class of the brand new All-Star Academy, Orlando Grant was given a number of good references as an up-and-comer by the likes of his former tag team partner, Mach 2; along with former Spirit Of ACW and ACW World Champion Andy Sharp. Now looking to entertain crowds up with his exciting blend of over-the-top antics as well as putting his body on the line to nab total victory, OG will be one to keep an eye on in the months to come. He's already proven himself in the All-Star Academy against a myriad of opponents, even toppling the seemingly unstoppable Jack Harris to become the United States Champ. In ACW itself, Orlando's made many heads turn with his frenzied in-ring showing, and has come very close several times to claiming the Scorpion Fighting Championship. Recent times haven't been too kind to this young man, but Orlando's resilience will no doubt see him rise to the top within no time at all. Question is, can you handle what the OG of ACW will throw at ya? ... You better be able to. ############ Influences: Andy Sharp, Seymour Almasy, Quinton May, Shawn Michaels.
