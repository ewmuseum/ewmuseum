In a bad bit of news for Ramey, as he soared backward through the air, he landed awkwardly on the injured right leg. Upon landing, he curled up, cradling his leg, while Vincent hopped out of the ring and retrieved his skull cane. He lifted the weapon high in the air, which got a mixed reaction (Vincent bad! Weapon good!) from the fans. Back inside, Vincent didn't hesitate in pummeling Ramey's injured leg with the cane, until it split from the repeated blows. Vincent powered Ramey up, lifted him onto his shoulders, and charged toward the ropes, and chucked the challenger over the top rope to the floor, grabbing himself an extremely cheap victory. And the fans didn't like this "loophole" one bit. Sure, it was in the rules, but Vincent had basically just cheated them out of a good fight with a decisive winner rather than a cheapo two-man battle royal style victory. And with that, cue "I'm Bad" by Last Vegas. The sound of Trey Vincent being announced as STILL the ACW World Heavyweight Champion was buried in an avalanche of curses and boos. Yeah. Like Trey gave a shit. He took the ACW World Championship greedily, focused on one thing and only one thing: making Keith Scott Zimmerman revel in his latest triumph--make this idiot realize who was the big dog running the kennel and who the little bitch was. "HEY, CHARLIE CHAPLIN!" gleefully scorned Trey, rubbing the ACW's crown jewel to a higher shine. "HOW ABOUT YOU GET A GOOD LOOK AT--" The chair was gone. Keith Scott Zimmerman was gone with it. Trey's voice dropped fourteen stories. "WHAAAT the..." He saw the crowd, jeering, then the crowd jeering, then OH SHIT CHAIR ... No. Not really. Keith Scott Zimmerman'd merely snuck up on him and feinted swinging the chair but it was enough to show everybody around the world the Franchise Player's underbelly, and Zimmerman loomed over Minnesota's Finest, who was on his back holding out his hands in compliance towards Keith. "Hey, come on, man." tried Trey to change his entire tone. Keith Scott Zimmerman's face changed, and the chair went from over his head, to at his shoulders, to down by his side. The crowd booed as Trey offered up a small smile, and got himself back to his feet. "You--" was as far as Trey Vincent got before the chair was suddenly up at Zimmerman's shoulders again, then cutting through the air to smack Trey in the face. What the throw of the weapon lacked in grace it made up for in accuracy, and Vincent spun around from the force of the impact. And just when you think the crowd could ovate no louder? Trey Vincent? How's about you HIT MY MUSIC! And just that quickly, Trey Vincent was on his back, and Keith Scott Zimmerman stood over his fallen body like a feudal landlord over a drunken peasant. The ACW World Championship glittered behind Trey's head, but Keith moved to get the microphone. Once he did, he had a message. He laid down on the mat, and the microphone picked up Trey groaning in pain as Zimmerman's cold voice rang out over the PA. "You're going to pay me what I'm owed Trey. You're going to give me a shot at the World Title. Because if I don't get what I'm owed, I PROMISE YOU, Trey--you'll make history all right. You'll be the first person to DIE while ACW World Heavyweight Champion." Silence. Creepy silence. And then...uh...Soundgarden's "My Wave" as Keith dusted himself off and got to his feet. No pose. No smile. Not even a smirk. Just a man with his hated rival and the thing he wanted most in the world laid bare at his feet. Uh...yay? WINNER: Trey Vincent via RING-OUT; retains ACW World Title! WAR. What is it good for? We're about to find out... So as the final credits for another violence filled edition of COURAGE rolled, viewers normally would switch to another channel. Who could blame them? At the end of the day who wants to see who produced the fucking lights unless your name is Christain Bale? This night. This one night it seemed ACW had a special little surprise up their sleeve. Well not ACW, but Jeremy Hunt. As the final set of credits rolled, those who would normally expect the ACW logo would suddenly taken back to somewhere in the arena as Jeremy Hunt sat behind his desk. A stash of papers scattered all over the place and a little product placement for Apple as his iPad currently displayed the New York Times. "Don't go just yet, people. This voyage to Eastern Europe has been filled with various surprises that some ACW fans have loved, and I'm sure that some have hated. I put those classified in the 'hate' category down to various backstage individuals currently designing the shows." Somewhere SilverHAWK was in a drunken rage. "Therefore next week, my friend, colleague and guardian will be booking the show." As WAR stepped out from the shadows behind Hunt, not many viewers knew exactly what this would mean but every ACW All-Star knew that next week would be hell on Earth. If SilverHAWK wasn't pissed before... he sure as fuck was now.
