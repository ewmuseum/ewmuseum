JD Raines Biography Real Name Jeremy Donovan Raines Hometown Kent, WA Birthday December 6th, 1987 Place of Birth Olympia, WA Height 6'6 Weight 245 Blood Type AB+ Eye Color Blue Biography Alignment and Personality: Much like his tag team partner, a total shallow, sometimes ignorant, and chauvinistic jackass. Proud of his physique and looks down on what he considers to be lesser beings. Description: Especially tall. Somewhat lean, but still some good musculature. Long black hair he keeps in a ponytail, clean-shaven face and blue eyes. Wears a pair of blue pants-length trunks opposite his partner with white boots and the same bad tribal patterns. They don’t know what any of them mean, they just looked cool when they had their tights designed.
