AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Biography Everyone has been born and raised to a family of various sizes. Some have been raised with no dad; some have endured the lifestyle of being a single kid. We all have are own childhood and stories, and they seem to change in all of us. Some maybe the same, but others change throughout the world. My story is a grueling one which you might cry for, or you might fear, but when your feelings come, just remember it’s just feelings because you never endured the feeling for real. The date was April 27, 1976 when my life had begun. A day that I wish would have never came which you will notice why in a few short minutes. I grew up in a low class family as only child. I was only 8 years of age when my behavior was caught on by many adults and kids. I was known as the smelly kid in school because my hygiene was so abnormally gross no one would dare come close to my rotting carcass. In turn it has lead to me being known as loner. I would sit by myself at the lunch table being as silent as can be. Not peeping a tiny word no matter what they called me. Yeah I was called names to. Not faggot like some would be called but ‘FuckHead’. Yeah that was my name in elementary class and has stuck with me ever since. Reason will shortly come. Since I was born though scars and bruises were seen from my face and body. These sparking the way people see me. No matter what they think of me or see me as my father is the one to blame. He beat me like a bloody pulp. Yet it was his fault but it was in the genes. His family heritage had nothing but fathers who beat there children. Turns out that I was the only child so I got spoiled with being beaten like a rag doll from one part of the house to the next and that lead to the aggression and bloody reputation that I so desire now. When I turned 10 I was sick of the shit he had done to me and my mother. Yes his beatings didn’t just stop with me; they lead to my mother as well. ‘You dam hooker’ he would say smacking her crossed the face. The beatings had to stop and it was going to stop on August 3, 1976. He went to hit my mother once more and I had the courage to grab his arm and stop his motion. He turned to me and asked ‘What Fuck are you doing?!’ I looked him square in the eyes and bam! I punch him straight in the nose. Bleeding profusely from the nose he ran out of the house to never be seen again. My aggression and courage started there but didn’t end. A year later I was taken to where my mom worked. She told me she worked as a nurse and worked on sick patients in the bad parts of the city. I can picture this day like it was just yesterday. I had just gotten out of the car and a man, with a building full of muscles, as he stood there asking my mother about things. He then shined some cash in front of her face as if that was to pay her. She then took the money and told me to wait by the car as she took him into the neighboring building. So I sat there on the curb looking down the street, seeing trash fly around and wondering what this world has come to, to let trash just float around like it is. Then I heard gun shots coming from a nearby street as a car screeched from the corner smoking its wheels as it flew by me. After 30 minutes of waiting my mother came out with the man who now had a big smile on his face. I asked her ‘Well how is he?’ She responded with a ‘He wasn’t all that bad, he wasn’t great though.’ She made it sound like she was a real doctor but as he walked away he heard what she said and was angered by it. Coming back he grabbed her and ripped her around. Getting into her face and cursing at her. My courage shortly dropped with that because there was nothing for me to do. The man was fighter and built like a horse. I could only watch him beat my mother down in the middle of the street. She began to cry and I felt sorry for her, but my anger still was present at the time. Shortly there after I found out she was a slut. Selling her body for money but I new she was doing it to put food on my plate. I respected her for that but has time wore on it got to me more and more. When I entered high school at, Osage City High School I was known as a silent loner, but as the kids teased me more and more I thought it wouldn’t get to me but it did in due time. My family tradition got to me as well, as I became a bully. I beat kids down if they called me ‘Fuckhead’ or if they called my mother a ‘Slut’. Many now knew me as a bully and thought as me as only that. They didn’t realize what I had gone through, and I had no clue of why I had changed into this, but the simple fact was I was tired of being called names, and I was tired of being beaten. I was pushed over the edge and now the tradition has come out in me. My story continues when I became married at the age of 21. Sure a person who would marry me is delirious but she did and she loved me. Actually I got her pregnant and was forced to, but as the kid grew older and became more involved in my family heritage. As much as I disliked it beating on my son became a daily thing. Yet I had to end it before it got out of hand. I knew just the thing to do it to. I need an aggression, and I need blood to rush and bruises to appear. That’s when I joined revenge. A wrestling in a cage that would keep me cage in. After a month of fighting and clawing my way I was dam close to becoming the champion, until they sent me to Russia where the main place was held. As soon as I got there I was given word that revenge in America was finished, over as they call it. I was left without a home, stranded in Russia and the only thing I could do was fight my way. So I kept fighting in Russia with revenge but that shortly closed as well. So I headed back to the states to find something much like it. I found ACW another wrestling style business but without a cage. So here I stand in America once again.
