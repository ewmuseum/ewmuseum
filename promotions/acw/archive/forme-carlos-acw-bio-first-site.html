What this is The fed's own biography page for the character Forme Carlos, from the fed's first site (acw.ewmania.com, the 2002 to 2004 edition), as captured in the Wayback on 27 September 2003. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Forme Carlos Real Name: Forme Carlos Birthdate: 7/14/68 Hometown: Mexico city, Mexico Height: 6'4' Weight: 230 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Neutral Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Ever and a day'- AFI Wrestling Style: Submissionist/Kung Fu artist Signature Moves: Arm bar takeover, Hip toss, Piiledriver, DDT, Cross ankle lock, head lock, indian death lock, powerful kicks, powerful blows Set Up Move: The Loco hat dance (He sets his opponet up for what looks like a powerbomb but as they go up he slams them back down onto their neck/shoulder area. ) Finisher: Rompa el molde (Break the mold) (He grabs onto his opponet and drags them to the corner he stands them up as he climbs to the second rope, he locks their head in and spins off holding onto the opponet heads sending the back of the head crashing into the mat) ACW Title History: ACW Award History: Other Federations: as forme- CCW, PWA/ as other- AW, NGW, DWO, LOW, PWE, NTW, XAWF, and many more Other Federation Awards: As forme- None/As other- AW/NGW/XAWF hall of fame, many title reigns Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: n/a Biography He was born in Mexico City, Mexico he grew up there in a poor shack with his 4 brothers and sisters as he thrived to find a better life for himself. While in Mexico from the age of 10 to 15 he was trained into being a fighting machine as he needed to provide for himself he entered the world of pit fighting and became well known until he was tired of Mexico and it’s people. At the age of 18 a man approached him with a way to the promise land, the U.S.A. He took the man up on his offer as the man gave him the plan and the map. With that Forme Carlos set out on his trip to the promise land with an unsure future, as he arrived into the United States of America in Arizona. He ran into the international drug lord, Albert Cono (Albert cone). As he was given money and drugs he was not bad at it until when he lost some of the drugs by messing up, for his mistakes the drug lord shot him in the side of the face and left him for dead, a women found him and called 911. After a long road to recovery he got out of the hospital and with no job and a hungry stomach, at the age of 20 he re-entered the underground pit fit world, as he climbed high in the ranks. Everything was going well. Life for him was finally looking up, until he was in his final match with the money from this he could break even, pay off the hospital and send for his brothers and sisters, but in this match he was physically destroyed he was back in the hospital. After recovering he became a trainer to fighters teaching them Kung Fu and some submissions that he made up and the lesson that he never forgets, Never turn your back on an enemy and never underestimate the competition. After the training business slowly died down to nothing but what he called ’punk street kids trying to get a name for them self’. Now at the age of 35 he wants to see if he still has what it takes to be what he use to be.
