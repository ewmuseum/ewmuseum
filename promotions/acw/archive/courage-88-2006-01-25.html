"But I think I liked it more when I didn't have so much to atone for." I nodded. "Ignorance is bliss." "Mmhmm. And..." She searched for more words. "I don't know. It just seemed so easier before. All I had to do is stay away from the speed, watch how much a drink. It's all matter of discipline, you know?" I coughed. "Yeah." "Now...it's like; I'm dirty or something. The priest told me that I'm struggling now because of living a whole lifetime's worth of sins." "Okay..." -I didn't quite know how to respond to that. Maybe that's because I partially agreed with it. "Doesn't that seem dishonest to you, though? I mean, they put you in recovery and you're supposed to keep yourself from one little vice. But they spend the entire time pushing God on you and telling you that you were weak from the start because you didn't read the bible enough." "Maybe that's true..." -It made sense at the time. My life choices weren't made in the light of God. "And you know what? Maybe it is. But...well...let's take you for example. You're a wrestler, right?" "Right..." "Who made you into what you are?" I stopped. Something inside of me wanted to say: "My father." --But what if I had my pop's blessing? Would I still be Brandon Youngblood? Would I still be a wrestler? Yes. "I did." "And sure, you may not be happy with yourself right now. But you have a history. A history of overcoming life's tribulations without God. They could be small or they could be big- but they are still there. Don't you think that's unfair?" "How so?" "Well, it's like they're saying 'Well since you can't live without some kind of addiction; here's another one, here's a bible.' -It's like they don't understand that al ot of these people- they haven't simply given up on everything that could be good about life. They've just given up on themselves. They've lost faith in who they are and what they can do to make a difference in their own lives. So when something else happens that they can't cope with or their faith in God can't explain- then they're back at square one. Suddenly, that bottle or 'checking up' on 'those old friends' doesn't seem like such a bad idea." Then it suddenly made sense. All of this turmoil summed up into a simple revelation: I had merely lost faith in myself. "Well. This is my car." I just stood there as she opened the door. Staring at her. She was so warm. So wise. The very tone of her voice: An offer of knowledge. "Hey, wait!" I said. I walked up to her slowly, because I didn't wanna scare her. I just... "Listen. Um..." I finished around for a piece of paper. "Do you got a pen?" She did. I wrote my cell phone number and the city I was going to be in next week on the back of a recept. "Listen, I don't normally do this. But, al ot of what you said made sense and you know...if you ever need someone to talk to or something...or...I mean, maybe if I ever needed to talk to you? You could call, or..." Then, giggling, she leaned in and kissed me on the cheek. "I'm sorry. But you are just too cute." I was speachless again. "Uh..." "I'll call you." She said. I just stood there blank. "I promise." "Cool." I was content with that. "Do you need a ride to your hotel or something, I could drop you off..." I looked around..."Uh, you know what...nah that's alright. I was kinda planning on joggin' back." "Alright...well...it was good meeting you Brandon." "You too, Alissa." I stood in that parking lot long after she had pulled off. It was cold outside, everything surrounding the church seemed so bleak, made double desolate by winter. But inside; there was a new summer surrounding my heart.
