AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Biography Hillary Small was born on 4th June 1980.….her birth certificate states her parents to be Mr and Mrs Alexander Small. She was abnormally heavy when she was born, and it was for these reasons that her mother died hours after her birth in the most traumatic of circumstances….she passed away holding on to Hillary’s hand. A week later Hillary was in a home as her father had decided she was to blame for the death of his sweetheart - and so he left her on the doorstep of the children’s home. At the age of 8, still living in the home, Hillary was 5’7 and was diagnosed with growth and hormonal problems. At primary school she was bullied - but had not yet gained the courage to fight back. When she was 12 she started secondary school….and rid her memory of her days as being on the receiving end of bullying. She was intimidated by the older children, the first time in her life she had really known anyone taller and bigger than herself - although she was nearing 6 ft by this stage of her life. She took the initiative….and slowly (but surely) became a bully. Her first victim was Laura. Laura was a kind, caring small girl. Small being the key word. She was mentally weak and lacked self respect to stick up for her self. She never told anyone about Hillary bullying her…… Her second victim was a boy! His name was Tom - and he had gone to her primary school and was one of the children who had persisted in taunting her. He needed teaching a lesson……but what he got was a stern beating at the hands of Hillary. Three years later and her bullying toll was at an astronomical 20. And finally - one could not hold it in for much longer. His name was John Dawson - and he was the reason Hillarys school career ended on a rather premature note. Yes - she was kicked out of school at the age of 14. She had never been Einstein anyway….and didn’t miss it. She spent the next few years mostly locked away in her room….as her social skills waned - her education became nothing but a memory and her personality was sharpened. At the age of 20 - three years after leaving the home and becoming a (very poor) discomforting nurse she undertook a task that would shape the rest of her life. She joined a wrestling school. A scout from a nearby federation recognised her undeniable physical attributes…..her muscles….her power and the accuracy that many woman wrestlers simply could not give federations like the WWE these days. She was seen as a new breed of femininity - a completely new style of wrestler. What she lacked in charisma and brainpower she made up with her ability. And then…at the age of twenty three she signed for her first wrestling promotion. ACW.
