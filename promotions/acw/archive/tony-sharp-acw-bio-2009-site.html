Tony Sharp Real Name: Anthony Steven Sharp Nickname: "The Thunder Bay Blade" Also known as: The Gray Wolf Born: 2/4/1979 Place of Birth: Montreal, Quebec, Canada Hometown: Montreal, Quebec, Canada Height: 6'6 Weight: 264 lbs. Blood type: O+ Eye color: Blue Team: None Manager: None Trainer: The Thunder Bay Dungeon Theme song: 'One Man Army' by Our Lady Peace Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Calculating Technician Signature moves: -Exploder Suplex and variants (Top Rope, Off apron, Multiple, etc.) -German Suplex and variants (Multiple, Release, Bridge, Power Hoist, etc.) -Running Knee Strike (a la William Regal) -Snap Swinging Neckbreaker (sometimes to suspended opponent) -Charging Double Knee Strike -Complete Shot The Rest: -Elbow Strikes (Standing, Running, to trapped opponent, etc.) -Knife-Edge Chops -Running Forearm Shot -Gutwrench Suplex -Short-Arm Lariat -Snap Scoop Powerslam -Powerbomb -Pendulum Backbreaker -Rolling Fireman’s Carry Slam -Kick to head ala Austin Aries -Belly to Belly Suplex variants (side, overhead, top rope) -Jumping Neckbreaker Slam -Any sort of submission you can think of. Finisher: 1. The Sharpshooter 2. Fireman's Carry DDT Finisher name: He's not in the habit of naming moves, they are as they are. Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: Thunder Bay Wrestling Other/Previous teams: None Other/Previous managers: None Other Wrestling titles won: TBW World Champion x7, TBW Tag Champion x3 Other Wrestling awards won: Most likely to not crack a smile Biography -Older brother of ACW wrestler Andy Sharp and son of NHL Legend Evan Sharp. Growing up traveling with their father, the professional sport of hockey always appealed to Tony. Travelling the road with his father practically up until his retirement, a career in hockey seemed to be promising, even with some far as to say he was a shoe-in to join the illustrious Olympiques Gatineau. Brother Andy’s interest waned in hockey, which some speculate led to the falling out between the two for Andy not feeling the same about the family business. -A knee injury had cut his career short. Bummed out and seeing what strides younger brother Andy had made in professional wrestling, Tony decided to take his size and strength and apply his trade as well. Rather than seek out the glitz and glamour that Americanized professional wrestling extolled upon its pupils, Tony sought out something that would push him to limits far beyond anything experienced. Enter The Thunder Bay School. -After spending a little over a year enduring gruesome beatings and being stretched by some of the best, Sharp applied his newfound skills to the circuit and quickly became a standout athlete, dominating many indy feds in Canada with a combination of size, strength, and technical cunning. -Taking some time competing all over the world, including Japan, the UK, Puerto Rico, and Italy, Tony returns home to assert his dominance and prove who the better brother is.
