LIVE! on Sunday 27th October From The Staples Centre, Los Angeles, California Introduction If one word could label last week's version of Courage, it would be revenge. After not being heard from for over 2 weeks after the beating he received at the hands of Winters Inc., SilverHAWK returned last week in controversial circumstances as he was the driver so the Inc's limo. Tonight will we see SilverHAWK get more revenge? or are Winters Inc. going to come back stronger than ever? There Is My Fate I felt the warm locker room air brushing my body as I walked back into my locker room from my meeting with Mr. Winters. I walked back over to my locker with the large dent in it and gave it a gentle rub. I gazed upon my bandaged hand and rubbed my fingers over it lightly and gave it a kiss to make it feel better. I take my bandaged hand and open up my locker and look inside the door and see a reflection of myself in the dirty mirror. "I look so different." I said to myself brushing my blonde hair out of my eyes. I noticed something was wrong this show in ACW, and I knew what it was. "My mask." I have yet to put on my mask from when I threw it back in my backpack. I turn away from the mirror and dig through my backpack to reveal my mask, still wrinkled and smelly from last week's Courage. I slowly put the mask back on and begin to tie it when I hear this voice in the room. "Andy.. Andy.." This voice echoed to me. "Who's there?" I begged, but it didn't answer my question. "Your mask hides your true identity Andy, let yourself be free." That damn voice exclaimed, talking like he knew me. "It hides nothing. Its apart of me." I said as I slammed my locker shut. I knew this was no voice in my head, because I've heard voices before, and it was nothing like this. "Its a shame you waste such time putting on a mask you like, when you don't like who you are when wearing it." That comment bothered me a lot, and I wasn't pleased to hear it. What he said was true though, I didn't like how I was being treated in ACW. There were lots of talk about me being jobbed even more than I was to push the new and upcoming talent because I was too big for everyone now. "That's bullshit, and you know it." I yell back at him in a angry tone. "I know more than you think." The voice chuckles back. "Please, tell me who you are." I ask him one more time. "Call me Fate." The voice commanded. "Fate, what do you want from me?" I questioned, but was followed with no response. "Fate.. Fate.... FFFFFAAAAATTTTEEEEEE!!!!!!" I scream but nothing was to be heard. Fate left. Winner: KaGe by Count-out Over Last week one man put everything he had on the line. He put his honor, his dignity and his future on the line. He took the risk of stepping in to a professional wrestling ring because of a deal he made with the devil himself. Ash Wild took on a deal that he knew he stood no chance of winning but he went for it anyways. The offer seemed too good to be true so he got greedy and went for it. What was the deal? Pretty simple really, if he won his match against the mystery opponent he got to advertise and recruit for his "movie" outside of the ACW arenas.
