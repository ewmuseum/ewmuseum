Captain Suleimon The Sultan of Smackdown, the Pride of the Ottoman Empire Real Name: Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon Birthdate: November 1st, 1975 Hometown: Istanbul, Turkey Height: 5'10' Weight: 198 lbs. Entrance Music: 'The Turkish March' Mozart Medical Information Natural Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: AB Body Fat Percentile: 3.9% Favored Workout: Medicine ball Past Illnesses: common cold, severe Anglophobia (not entirely cured) Past Injuries: broken leg, compacted tailbone In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Grounded pseudo-technician who takes to the air occasionally. Used to be a spot machine, but years of high-flying action have taken a toll on his body Trainer: General Rahman Status: Heel Aerial Moves: Front dropkick, top rope cross-body, hudaconrana, tope suicida, missile dropkick, moonsault, lionsault, flying cross chop, Arabian facebuster* Counter Moves: Enziguiri, flip-over counter to back body drops and German, Tiger and other back thrusting suplexes Favorite Moves: Tiger suplex, reverse Tiger suplex, running DDT, arm dragon screw, favored weapons include Cat O'Nine Tails and steel chair Set-Up Move(s): Sultanbreaker Reverse full nelson chinbreaker Finishing Move: (a) The Whirling Dervish, (b) The Leap Across Continents (a) Tornado reverse DDT, (b) Starts out on the top ropes, back to the ring ala moonsault. He then jumps back and does a sort of reverse swanton bomb. Rarely does this move anymore because of the damage he does to himself while doing it. Special occasions only. Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: ACW Awards: n/a Other Federations: AWC, A1E, MBE Cup, UXW Other Federations Awards: n/a Career History Moveset *denotes move may also be used as a finisher Submissions:Seated full nelson, armbar, hammerlock (usually segues into a back body drop or a vertical suplex), The Turkish Tiger Tamer (kinda a cross between Cattle Mutilation and the Hennig special... opponent is seated on the canvas, and Suleimon hooks his arms in a rear double chickenwing [ala Tiger suplex]. Suleimon then nestles his head in the back of the victim's neck and flips his body over the victim in a bridge) Aerial moves: Front dropkick, top rope cross-body, hudaconrana, tope suicida, missile dropkick, moonsault, lionsault, flying cross chop, Space Flying Tiger Drop Grapple moves: Tiger suplex (*dangerous Tiger suplex), reverse Tiger suplex, arm dragon screw, running DDT, Hennig Special Hardcore moves: *Arabian Facebuster, Cat O' Nine Tails, chairshot Tariq Abdul Wahad Suleimon was born in Turkey to a fanatical Islamic family. Like many other fanatical Muslims, Suleimon hated the West, especially America. But paradoxically, he loved professional wrestling. Couldn't get enough of it growing up. He was weened on watching WWF broadcasts, but instead of rooting for Hulk Hogan and Sergeant Slaughter, it was the Iron Sheik who was his hero. He also got a hold of many Japanese tapes, where he became infatuated with Tiger Mask and those crazy jumping Japanese high-spotters. So Suleimon trained to be a pro wrestler, much to the disliking of his strict family. He also delved himself into the history books, to a time when Turkey ruled the world... or at the very least, the Middle East and Balkan Europe. So enamored with this, he began to disown his own family and take on the bloodline of the ruthless Pasha dynasty. Thus, Captain Suleimon was born. Under the training of General Rahman, an old, grizzled local wrestler, Suleimon learned the basics. He developed a mean streak like no other. He often injured sparring partners, a practice that Rahman surprisingly condoned. Rahman also brought in high flyers from the Far East to teach Suleimon how to throw his body like a missile. The key was controlled flying... no recklessness. But Suleimon didn't really learn that well when it came to restraint. His talents got him noticed by several English and American indie feds (Indie wrestling isn't that big in Turkey, although it's rumored that Suleimon wrestled bears and tigers and word of that spread to the West... those rumors are unfounded to this day). He eventually signed with Liberty Bell Wrestling, where he was noticed by A1E, a national promotion. Suleimon's stay in A1E was forgettable. He feuded with Gladiator, but his reckless style sent him back to the injured list repeatedly. He came back for one shot deals here and there, but mainly stayed backstage as a road agent until he finally was completely healthy once more. Now, the campaign for Ottoman dominance has been begun again in ACW. Matchwriting Aide: Suleimon has been described as the Turkish Jeff Hardy at times, eschewing ring psychology for mind-blowing high spots earlier in his career. This style has gotten him injured several times, including a painful compacted tailbone early in 2001 which he didn't fully recover from until quite recently. So he's scaled back on his spot-o-rama style and added in some more ground based holds and even some submissions. He still doesn't use much psychology and will rarely ever work a body part outside of the neck occasionally (although sometimes this is by default because many of his moves target the head/neck area), but he's a lot more sound on the ground, and he's always learning new holds and moves. Aerially though, he's still as deadly as he was years ago, although he's a lot more careful of the spots he chooses to fly. He's eliminated the SSP from his repertoire altogether, partially because everyone else does it and partially because it was overkill, and the Leap is only used in the biggest situations (blood feuds and Championship scenarios... think ACW Spirit and World or AWC Frontier or Transatlantic), but he's added a few springboard and out-of-ring moves, like the lionsault and the Space Flying Tiger Drop. As for match finishers go, he will most likely build towards the Dervish unless in a huge situation where he'd break out the Leap, or when the rules allow, the Arabian Facebuster.
