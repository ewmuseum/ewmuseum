ARC III - Requiem 23rd July 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice INTRODUCTION For many ACW superstars... the time is now. The time has come for them to stand up and be counted as the company prepares for all out war. Table of Contents Introduction - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Pi in the Milehigh Sky. Mr. Wrestling Pi carefully did up his wrist tape. It was hard to see through his red and gold “PI” mask, but it looked as though he was not in a very happy mood. Depressed would probably be the feeling. Time was running out on the chance to crown the first ACW Tag Team Champions, and the deft wrestler from Tellin, Montana knew he didn’t have the talent or fanbase to become a strong part of the singles competition. Tag team wrestling is all that he knew. Pi in the Sky were 0-4, still looking for their first win. But he knew it would come soon, and he hoped it would come tonight. They only needed one to get on a roll, Mr. Wrestling was sure of it. Just at that moment, Avis Flyfield zoomed his way into the room, a smile as wide as possible. It was as if he never had a bad day. “Hey, avenger!” Flyfield said with a grin. He knew his pal was down in the dumps… only two weeks ago they had lost for the forth time, to an incoming tag team called The Misfit Toys. This was not what Pi in the Sky needed. New teams could not enter and just vault their way over them. Then the dreams of tag team gold would never, ever happen. “Cheer up, chum!” Flyfield said as he sat down beside Mr. Wrestling. “I promise you we’ll win one. We’ll win one real soon!” Mr. Wrestling looked up and forced a smile across his face. But looking into his teammates eyes… so full of happiness, hope and overall joy, he couldn’t help but grow a real smile across his own face. “Okay.” He said in sign-language. “You got it.” Just then, however, a gigantic figure loomed over both of them. The crowd booed. His shirt read “POON FOR BREAKFAST, LUNCH & DINNER”, depicting a meal of each, with a woman’s open legs on top of the main course. He was The Nookie Monster. “Da Monster in da house!” He shouted, with no real need to raise his monotonic voice. “Hey!” Flyfield said in excitement. “Are you ready to go 2-0 tonight!?” Mr. Wrestling Pi moved his eyes back to the ground. He forgot. It was ‘The Milehigh Club’s’ turn tonight. ‘Pi in the Sky’ would have to wait until next week. “Nookie ready.” The Monster said, saliva freely dripping from his mouth. “Da Monster is always ready.” Avis stood up and rubbed Mr. Wrestling on the back before heading towards the Nookie Monster, and tapping him on the shoulder. “Nice shirt.” Flyfield commented as they left the locker room. “Can we grab some dinner afterwards?” “Nookie?” Nookie Monster asked as their voices and images carried out of sight. Mr. Wrestling Pi just continued to stare at the floor… once again a depressed look upon his face. That Time Again... “Time for a challenge. Tonight’s the night Chris.” The sound of “Cocky” by Kid Rock sounded through the arena as Chris Moliano stepped out from behind the curtain and was met with a fairly warm reception. He slapped the outstretched hands of some of the fans as he walked the aisle before hitting the ring. Microphone in hand, the ACW faithful knew by now what was coming. Chris signalled to have his music cut and raised the mike to his lips as he stood in the centre of the ring in full wrestling attire. “So it should come as no surprise to you fine people by now that I am out here, standing in this ring with a purpose. You see time after time I’ve walked that aisle and I’ve styled and profiled and I’ve climbed into the ring and promised a wrestling clinic. Tonight is no exception, but with one very big difference. Tonight marks my first ever victory and the biggest step along my path towards glory. Ladies and gentleman...the era of pure Moliano magic is about to begin!” Chris pumped his fist in the air, drawing a mixed response from the crowd. “When you fine people go home tonight you will remember this moment as a landmark event in the beloved sport that is wrestling. The day that magic came alive. You will all grow old and tell your children that you were there the night Chris Moliano arrived in this business. The night that my star began to shine, before I ballooned to the biggest superstar the business has ever seen. In the past I’ve been accused of being arrogant and egotistical and as you hear my words some of you may even be inclined to agree. Thing is, I will live up to own sky high expectations and set the bar for all those who follow me inside the wrestling ring. I’m no longer speaking as a cocky young man – Chris Moliano speaks the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth.” Moliano nodded his head, psyched up by his own words and visibly ready for a fight. “So once more I ask each individual in the back to stand forward if they dare – Christopher Antonio Moliano is about to put on a show.” Moliano stared down the ramp awaiting a response. He was confident of victory and there was nobody in ACW that was going to defeat him. Not this time. Not on this night. Cue "Ecstasy of Gold" by Metallica. Brian Spaes. Suddenly Chris Moliano did not look quite so confident. Moliano Challenge The Minuteman himself Brian Spaes emerged from the back with eyes locked firmly on the ring and its occupant, Chris Moliano. Spaes walked towards the ring as Moliano tried to psych himself up for this challenge. The number one contender to the World title up against a man without a single victory to his name. The phrase “one way traffic” may have come into many a mind inside the ACW arena. Spaes entered the ring as Moliano extended his arm, offering Spaes the microphone and a chance to verbally respond to the challenge. Spaes took a look at the microphone in Moliano’s hand before swatting it aside. Tonight he was strictly business, and that did not bode well for “Potential Personified”. Ding Ding! The two combatants stared at one another from opposite corners of the ring as the bell rung. Showtime. Spaes stepped forward purposefully toward Moliano with an intense look on his face. Moliano himself seemed eager to impress, his determination to succeed showed as he began to step forward towards Brian who circled the ring plotting his first move. Suddenly Spaes leapt into action and swung away with a right hand but Moliano countered surprisingly easily as he blocked the punch and then took the number one contender down hard with an impressive looking arm drag into an arm bar. Chris pulled back to apply additional pressure to the right arm as he pulled him up to his feet and turned the arm bar into a hammerlock. Spaes was in pain though he tried to hide that fact, and attempted to escape by swinging his left elbow back to try and strike Moliano in the head, but Chris once again had the move scouted. Chris was able to duck underneath the elbow before picking up Spaes and sending him down hard onto his knee with an atomic drop. This time Spaes yelled out unable to hide his pain, but stayed on his feet as he walked away gingerly. He recovered enough to spin round but again Moliano wag once more the first to react as he scooped him up and slammed him down to the mat once again with a perfect scoop slam. Moliano jumped up into the air as Spaes arched over on the mat to feel his lower back as a shooting pain shot through his upper body. This was not what anybody could have expected. “Who’s the Daddy?!” Chris screamed out overjoyed and playing to the crowd who roared a slight cheer of approval as Moliano began to get himself worked up by doing two or three standing star jumps to the delight of sections of the crowd, and the total surprise of everyone else. As Spaes got back to his feet Moliano changed from a star jump to a beautiful looking dropkick striking Brian down. It was safe to say that Brian Spaes had certainly not expected Moliano to come flying out of the blocks in this manner. Perhaps this would finally be the wrestling clinic he had promised for so long. As soon as his body hit the deck he began to roll out of the ring under the bottom rope in an attempt to break up the considerable momentum Moliano was building up in the early stages of the match. A wise and veteran-like manoeuvre indeed. Spaes regained his breath on the outside as Moliano really started playing to the crowd inside the ring to try and get them firmly behind him. His head may have been ringing but one thought ran prominently through Brian’s head. What the hell was happening? He was the man behind the revolution of ACW, and here he was being outclassed by a young guy who was yet to muster a single win inside the squared circle. He shook his head to clear it once and for all – it was time for him to get going. He had a message to deliver. Smack! Focusing too much on the thoughts in his head as he pulled himself back up to his feet with the aid of the ring apron, Spaes had not seen the baseball slide coming. The sliding black boots of Moliano crashed into the side of his face and sent him sprawling into the barricade. Chris pointed to himself with pride as he gazed at the crowd cheering him on - Moliano was competing with a smile on his face, and the results were simply stunning. Chris strode confidently to the outside and began to fire away with some hard right hands into the face of Spaes who was down on his knees. One… Two… MOL-EEE-ARNO! Three… The referee began to exercise his ten count as Moliano took a step back before unleashing a picturesque Superkick which knocked Spaes down – and perhaps even out. Four… MOL-EEE-ARNO! Five… Moliano slid inside the ring with Spaes still down as the referee continued his count… Six… Brian was barely moving on the outside and Moliano could sense that his first win was well on its way…as could the crowd who could barely believe the incredible display Moliano was putting on going to school on the man who could well be the next World champion as they continued to chant his name. MOL-EEE-ARNO! Seven… “Here comes eight”, Moliano spoke with a smile. Holding up his own fingers counting along with the ref. Eight… With Brian very much still down this was it. Moliano scaled the turnbuckle in preparation of a victory salute and started gesturing to the crowd in celebration. The roar from the fans rung through his ears, all that was left now was for him to stand on that turnbuckle with the crowd loving it almost as much as he did. All that remained were the final two counts as Moliano listened hard to the referees voice. His frustration at not winning was about to be lifted. MOL-EEE-ARNO! Nine… The count combined with the chants coming from the crowd were like therapy. His moment had arrived. He waited ready to erupt with a roar of triumph, cupping his ear to hear the final count. Ten. Ten? TEN? Thud. The sickening sound of Moliano’s body being tossed from high up on the turnbuckle down to the mat brought a gasp from the crowd in unison. Spaes had of course been biding his time, utilising the referee’s count to gain a maximum amount of energy back. As soon as he heard the count of nine he jumped up like a flash and slid under the bottom rope. The sight of Moliano up high on the opposite turnbuckle with his back turned was an opportunity he could ill afford to miss as he threw his body down to the canvas with a sickening sound. Now it was Moliano’s turn to lay motionless, as he landed awkwardly on the hard mat. His mind still full of thoughts of winning and success, he had not even realised exactly what had happened as he lay flat out on the ACW canvas. “Who’s the Daddy? Time to show you what I’m about.” Spaes spoke aloud as he regained some more strength and shook his head to try and relieve the ringing in his ears from the stiff shots he had taken on the outside just moments earlier. The wind had been taken very much out of Moliano, and Spaes knew that as of now, he was in complete control. And boy was Chris going to suffer. Spaes trod slowly over to Moliano, who was yet to make any significant movement, with a look of intent on his face as his stalked his prey. It was time for some serious pain. Brian kicked gently at the head of Moliano with his right boot, playing with his lifeless body whilst grinning sadistically. Finally he grabbed Moliano by the hair and pulled him to his feet, taking a moment to stare into his eyes before reaching back and striking him hard in the face with a stiff right hand, sending Moliano back down to the mat with a single punch. The way his body landed after being tossed off the turnbuckle had left Moliano in considerable pain and with no real energy left in his body. He was breathless, without energy and worst of all at the complete mercy of the sinister Brian Spaes. Seeing Moliano in this condition brought joy to Spaes as he again pulled Moliano up by his hair and this time set him up for the Deadlift Dragon Suplex. Connected. The crowd ‘ahhed’ as Spaes hit the big move and Moliano’s head bounced off the canvas with considerable force. It was enough to knock most men out cold outright, somehow Moliano moved an arm but it was no more than a token gesture. His lights had been turned out. Spaes casually made the cover which the referee was quickly down to count. One… Two… Oh no. Spaes shook his head and raised his opponent up off the mat eager to inflict more damage. He picked up Moliano again, looking to finish him off... URAKEN! The devastating spinning back fist connected resoundingly, if Moliano wasn’t quite out from the last move – he was now. Spaes did not even have to hook the leg, simply leaning over Moliano and resting a hand on his chest as the referee made the count, which in truth was a mere formality. One… Two… Three. Mercifully it was over. The bell rung as Brian Spaes had his hand raised in triumph. Sure he had been given a scare and perhaps underestimated his opponent early doors, but at the end of the day he had won and that was all that mattered, as the crowd cheered him as he raised his hand aloft. Despite taking the early initiative his lack of ring presence had cost him dearly once more and so Moliano’s wait for victory continued. Brian had delivered a message and racked up another for the win column. Not even Max Danger would be able to get up after that kind of offence. That was of course, exactly what Brian was counting on. WINNER: Brian Spaes via pinfall Crashing Into The Ground The beat to “Ricochet” by Shiny Toy Guns enveloped the All-Star arena. Trevor’s Soldiers, the Arms of the Sentinel, the Minutemen, whatever particular nick name fancied them most was one the fans would throw around. They preferred The Misfit Toys. After that, Stevenson rolled under the bottom rope. He joined his partner in the center of the ring after she acquired a microphone. Kati attempted to twirl the microphone in her hand, but it proved to be difficult so she stopped trying. The continued absence of a fan address started to wear on the crowd. Jeremy bent down to whisper something into her ear and it seemed to click. “The Minutemen rule!” The crowd was a split with some booing the group, and others cheering. Throughout the crowd the Sons of Liberty were supporting the Minutemen from their respective sections. “We’ve got half the gold, and just as soon as we win the tag titles, we’ll have a larger fraction of gold. Then our recovering drug addict leader will win the world title and we’ll have all the gold!” The Sons found it difficult to cheer and keep the momentum up when they were cheering statements that painted Brian Spaes in a bad light, but Brian was still proud of his ability to move on from his checkered past. “Like a huge drug addict. I guess he used to zone out for entire events until Jeff Garvin smacked him around enough. Oh, and before I forget, you Kingdom motherfuckers are gonna get blindsided sometime very soon!” Jeremy smiled and put a hand on Kati’s shoulder. This main claiming to be a Captain is nothing more than a psychopath running rampant in this quoteunquoute fan-run company. Kati looked poised to reveal more, but Jeremy took a turn with the microphone. “We’ve got a simple request. Avis, you can come out here alone, or you can bring a friend. Whatever you choose is fine by us. We’d just like you to come on down, step into the ring, and get some of your limbs broken. We’ll even settle for some spilt blood.” No reply came at first, but it didn’t take long for The Wing Commander to come out. And he definitely wasn’t alone. “Danger Zone” by Kenny Loggins played over the PA to a pretty good pop. Out walked Flyfield, propeller hat and all. He even held his Amelia Earhart figurine from Night at the Museum II. And the crowd cheered even more as they saw it was Mr. Wrestling Pi who accompanied Avis to the ring, completing the tag team the crowd actually liked: Pi in the Sky. Avis and Pi walked down to the ring, however through his mask, Mr. Wrestling definitely looked a little dejected. Meanwhile Avis just made sure his uniform was all together, and he was ready to go. He jumped on the apron, to even more cheers. Then spinning his propeller around, he did the same as Kati and Jeremy just looked on… completely unimpressed. “Broken bones.” Kait said to Jeremy. She didn’t want to settle for blood any more. But as Flyfield entered the ring, he turned and pointed to the back… for everyone’s worst nightmare. “Nookie”. Limp Bizkit. “BBBBBBBBOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” 240 Pounds of wrestling garbage walked out, trying to soak in whatever reaction he got. The “worst nightmare” had nothing to do with the Nookie Monster being an imposing figure. He sucked big time. In the one month he was in ACW, the Monster had already made it to everyone’s backlist. This. Guy. Sucks. Do not wrestle. Avis cheered him on like crazy, though. He was Nookie Monster’s biggest fan. WINNER: The Milehigh Club Mood Changer “Damnit to hell!” Choosing only one word to describe the mood of Chris Moliano was difficult at this time. Angry or frustrated would be the main choices as he cursed backstage and threw his gym bag down to the floor below. Another challenge had received another answer which had in turn led to only another defeat. One thing for sure was that he was tired of tapping out or having his shoulders pinned to the mat at the conclusion of a match. He was a born winner, but the way things had gone inside the squared circle he had been reduced to nothing more than a big Loser. That’s right, with a capital L. “Hey don’t beat yourself up over there...” Chris recognised the sweet caring female voice instantly. ACW referee Paige Allen stepped into shot and strode over to Moliano who turned his head to face her. “...leave that to the rest of the roster.” Paige smiled at her joke very much at Chris’ expense. Anyone else making such a comment would undoubtedly have set him off and led to an argument but for some reason Chris found he could only smile and force a slight laugh at her joke. “I’m sorry” Paige started, “I was just kidding Chris. You put up a good fight again, you’ll get your win soon enough.” Before she realised what she was doing Paige found herself smiling and placing a comforting hand on Chris’ shoulder. Moliano’s eyes were quick to respond to this as he looked at her soft hand and then back into her eyes. Paige quickly withdrew her arm and brushed her red hair from in front of her eyes before taking a small step backwards. “Anyway...see you around.” Paige turned and walked back down the corridor. Chris opened his mouth as if to speak but instead for once decided against it. He smiled himself as he picked up the bag he had thrown to the ground in frustration and slung it over his shoulder. Maybe things were making a turn for the better after all. Choosing a single word to describe his mood was suddenly a lot easier. Happy. After shedding the suits to reveal matching silver tights, the high-flyer tandem made their way down the ramp. “Next, at a combined weight of 417 pounds… the team of Andy Sharp and Mach 2 … THE FIRST AIRBOUR…” He was forced to stop in his tracks when out from the crowd came a pair of rowdy fans in matching black hoodies. A large man and a much smaller man attacked both Andy and Mach 2 from behind, driving them both to the ground just outside the ring. The Elements and the Sons both looked on at the scene transpiring near ringside when they realized what was happening. These strangers, pummeling Andy and Mach 2 with a rain of endless fists and kicks, were not just any two bodies. The familiar smug grins were barely visible underneath their disguises, but the second the hoods went flying back out… BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! The Heirs of Wrestling were seen, pumping their fists for the crowd in delight of their handiwork. Both men removed the hoodies and removed their sweatpants to reveal their incredibly gaudy royal ring gear before discarding the clothing amidst a sea of rabid, angry Canadian ready to strike them down. Both members of the 1st Airbourne crawled around lifelessly, wondering what had just hit them. “WE RUN THIS SHITE NOW!” Pierce screamed, heavily accentuating the second-to-last word with a horrid English accent. “THIS IS WHY THE EVOLVED SURVIVE!” Gallway added. “BECAUSE GOOD ARE UNEVOLVED AMOEBAS… AND THEY’RE RETARDED!” Once they were through with the Airbourne, Gallway and Pierce slid into the ring and a huge BRAWL ensued between the remaining six competitors! DING DING! Butch paired off with both members of the Heirs of Wrestling while the Elements picked their spot and cornered Mack Brody on the opposite side of the ring. Frank Pierce used his power to keep the big man restrained while Gallway ran at him and planted both feet in Butch’s face with a nasty Spinning Wheel Kick that saw the momentum taking him to the apron. Pierce followed suit with a stiff Forearm Smash to the face that rocked the big man and sent him crawling to the outside. Meanwhile, in another corner, Mack was doing his best to fend off both members of the Unknown Elements, a team the Sons of Anarchy had bad blood with in the last few weeks. Mack blasted Shawn Arrows in the face with a stiff Headbutt. Lopez came to his partner’s rescue and wanted to atone for his loss last week. He pummeled him about the head with a series of nasty Forearm Smashes, but a swift knee from the big man rocked him long enough to be tossed to the outside. Mack, living up to his finisher’s moniker, came barreling at Lopez and blasted him with a nasty Clothesline on the floor! Before Mack knew what hit him, the suicidal half of the Unknown Elements flew right over the ropes and connected with a nasty Tope that sent he and the bigger half of the Sons crashing to the canvas. Not ones to be outdone by the likes of any other team, the Heirs of Wrestling both slide back into the ring after disposing of Butch Brody against the ring steps with a vicious “THUD!” Gallway measured up Mack, Shawn, and Carlos on the outside and with the help of Pierce pulling down the ropes, manages to FLY over the top and crash onto all three bodies with a Spaceman Plancha! Roaring to life, the Montreal crowd started to actually give some love to the otherwise hated teams. Frank Pierce bounced off the ropes and FLEW over, carrying all 268 pounds over the ropes with a No-Hands Suicide Dive that took down all parties! “A-C-W! A-C-W! A-C-W! A-C-W! A-C-W!” The first one to move out of the entire ruckus was Pierce himself, managing to soften his handling using all four other bodies. The Baron of Ballistics rolled underneath the ropes and stood in the ring once again, laughing his ass off at the plight of all other parties. Ryan Gallway was next to enter the ring as both men threw their fists in the air and high-fived one another. The Dynastic Duo absorbed the heated reaction from the Montreal faithful and ignored it, but what they should not have ignored was Butch Brody and a SICK Double Clothesline that turned both men inside out! Beating on his chest, he let out a chest-bellowing roar to the crowd before picking Gallway for the first cover of the match. ONE! TWO.. SAVE FROM FRANK! Barely a two-count, Frank managed to elbow the big man in the back of the head. Butch had the aid of the ropes to make it back to his feet, but a violent series of right hands rocked the younger Brody Brother. Pierce flew off the ropes and bounced back into Butch, who caught him in mid Cross Body and WHIPPED him across the ring with a Fallaway Slam! Before Butch could have any time to celebrate, a bell-ringing Jumping Leg Lariat from the smaller Gallway caught him across the temple, but only served to bring him down to one knee. Seeing this, Gallway flew off the second rope and connected with a Springboard Dropkick that didn’t complete get him down, but managed to teeter him for the moment. Looking on shocked at Butch’s tenacity, Gallway ran off the ropes looking for a third move, but Butch was at the ready, catching the much smaller man in a Fireman’s Carry! Luckily, Pierce had found his second wind long enough to take out his legs from under him with a Chop Block, finally bringing Butch to the canvas. Ryan Gallway breathed a sigh of relief as Pierce took hold of Butch in a Camel Clutch position. Seeing a golden opportunity, the Prince of Precision lived up to his moniker and popped off a picture-perfect Front Dropkick right to the nose of Brody! Once again taking a moment to celebrate their utter dominance as a team thus far, The Heirs high-fived one another and laughed boisterously. However, the crowd became deafening. Fever-pitch, even. Both could still be considered to be rookies, but the Heirs were not stupid. Both men turned around… STEREO DROPKICKS! The First Airbourne were back in the game and the crowd celebrated with both Andy Sharp and Mach 2 as they took hold of Gallway. Begging for mercy, Ryan Gallway would find that Andy had none as he lifted him up and drove him across the knee with a sick Gutbuster. Holding him in place, Mach 2 flew off the ropes and landed a Somersault Swinging Neckbreaker! Pierce was not exempt from the ire of the First Airbourne, either, finding himself the victim of a barrage of both Knife-Edge Chops and Shoot Kicks in the corner. Andy and Mach double Irish Whipped Pierce cross-corner and Andy followed him in with a Stinger Splash! Kneeling in front of him, he invited Mach 2 to do the same and his pupil kindly obliged. Leaping off Andy Sharp’s back, Mach 2 landed a nasty Leg Lariat across the jaw of Pierce, with the momentum also carrying him to the apron like Gallway had done earlier in the match. Andy picked Frank up and drove him near the ropes with a Scoop Slam just as Mach 2 leaped to the top rope and crashed all his body weight on top of Pierce with a Back Senton Splash! “LET’S GO, AIRBOURNE! Clap clap clapclapclap! LET’S GO, AIRBOURNE! Clap clap clapclapclap! LET’S GO, AIRBOURNE! Clap clap clapclapclap! LET’S GO, AIRBOURNE! Clap clap clapclapclap! Andy Sharp and Mach 2 took both Pierce and Gallway respectively and tossed the out on opposite ends of the ring. Sharp and Mach nodded at one another before both slingshotted themselves to the outside with Stereo Corkscrew Pescados to the floor! By that point, the entire arena had erupted in support of their hometown boy as well as his pupil who they treated like one of their own. As that ruckus cleared out, Mack Brody was back in the ring with Carlos Lopez, putting the boots to the Tar Heel. Picking him up in the corner, the Mack Truck continued his sheer dominance of his opposition by burying a succession of hard back elbow strikes. Backing up a few steps once he was sure to have him stunned, he ran forward, but Lopez had the wherewithal to connect with a Dropkick to the knee in mid-run. Mack fell forward and collided with the second turnbuckle. Arrows made his reappearance, sliding underneath the ropes and getting his bearings with his tag team partner. Mack pulled himself up, but found himself on the bad end of a Seated Jawbreaker from Carlos Lopez. As Mack staggered backwards holding his jaw, Arrows was there waiting for him as he SNAPPED him down with a vicious Hangman’s Neckbreaker! Butch Brody came in to try and aid his brother, looking for another surprise Double Clothesline. This didn’t end well as Shawn Arrows ran up the ropes and flew back at the big man, snapping him down with a sick 450 Lariat that he’d debuted a couple weeks earlier! After Arrows took a moment to recover from the landing, both men carried him to the nearest corner before letting him have it with a NASTY Running Facewash from Lopez/Dropkick from Arrows! Dragging the big man to the center of the ring, Arrows hooked both legs. ONE! TWO! NO! Just barely, Butch had gotten the shoulder up. Arrows ran off the ropes and tried for another move, but this time, Andy Sharp was there to cut him off at the pass, holding down the ropes so the ring veteran found himself dumped to the outside! Just as Andy made his way into the ring, Lopez was there to cut him off at the pass with a sharp (pun clearly intended) kick to the head. He took his left boot and raked Andy’s face across the side, stunning him. A trifecta of painful kicks to the temple had brought Mr. All-Star down to one knee. Andy tried to fight back with a pair of hard rights to the stomach of Lopez, but the crafty technician booted him in the temple to halt his progress. Striking while the iron was hot, Lopez got himself a full head of steam off the ropes, but had the unfortunate luck to run into a sick Corkscrew Flying Elbow Smash from Andy! Kipping up to his feet, Andy started to position himself in the middle of the ring looking for the match-ending Leet Feet v.2 that netted him big victories in the past. As Lopez clutched his jaw and made his way to his feet, the Superkick came… DUCKED… BRIDGING GERMAN SUPLEX! Lopez dumped the Highlight of the Night on the back of his head with the vicious suplex, going for the cover. ONE! TWO THR…SAVE BY MACH 2! In the nick of time, Mach 2 had saved the match for the Airbourne, not wanting to be the first team out of this hard-fought chase. Lopez found the source of the pinfall break and elbowed Mach 2 in the side of the head for his troubles. Before he could whip the youngster off the ropes, Mach 2 reversed and slid under Lopez’s legs! He ran up the ropes and off the rebound, BLASTED him across the face with a sick Flying Enzuigiri across the back of the head! Lopez went crumbling to the canvas with a dull thud. Mach 2 was back in, but not for long as Mack Brody was back in the game. Turning around, Mach 2 walked right into a STIFF Big Boot! After getting floored, Mach 2 got fired across the ring. Mack 2 wished to survey his dominance, but would have a hard time doing so. A NASTY Flying Shoulder Block from Frank Pierce staggered him into the ropes. Off the rebound, Pierce grabbed him by the waist and somehow managed to drive down the biggest man in the match with a Belly-to-Belly Side Suplex! Now standing over him in the center of the ring, Frank smiled and raised a pinky finger in the sky, taking in a lot of jeers. The fans knew what was about to come next. “YE OLDE ROYAL ELBOW!” Bouncing off both sets of ropes, Pierce came to stop in the center of the ring before planting a firm elbow right into the forehead of Mack Brody! ONE! TWO! TH… KICKOUT WITH AUTHORITY! Pierce, despite being close to the 270 weight class, was pitched off an irate Mack as he got around to his feet. Pierce ran off the ropes again and could’ve possibly went looking for the Royal Road, but found a hand wrapped around his throat. Shawn Arrows slid into the ring and tried to catch Mack off-guard, but found himself trapped in the other hand of Mack. With both men firmly goozled for the moment, he tried to lift them both up, only to have a pair of boots meet him in a rather questionable region. At that point, Shawn Arrows held Mack up for a moment as Pierce got himself all the speed he could muster… ROYAL ROAD ON MACK BRODY! Despite having a nice size advantage over even Pierce, the Margrave Digger packed a lot of power into his own frame, toppling him with the Shoulder Block! After their temporary alliance, Shawn Arrows let him know it was off by pummeling him about the face with a nasty series of right hands. After stunning Frank with a Facecrusher Knee Strike in the middle of the ring, Shawn turned to get some speed off the ropes, but Butch Brody was there to catch him with another Fireman’s Carry! Pierce, shaking out the cobwebs, ran to his aid, only to find himself ALSO in the grip of Butch Brody. The younger member of the Sons of Anarchy grunted slightly, but showed AMAZING strength as a DOUBLE Samoan Drop brought down both Arrows and Pierce at the same time! Applauding the very impressive feat of strength from the big man, Butch waved both hands in the air to get the crowd to make as much noise as humanly possible. However, the time for fun and celebration was ended rather quickly when another pair of stereo dropkicks – this time from Mach 2 and Ryan Gallway – teetered him backwards. Butch went sailing back to the ropes, but another set of dual dropkicks from the cruiserweights of the match finally was enough to send him careening through the ropes and out to the floor. The alliance was quickly broken when a sharp kick met Mach 2 in the temple. Kicking him a few more times, Mach 2 ran across the ropes and tries for a headscissors, but Mach suddenly put the brakes on the hold and SLAMMED him hard with a Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker! After he bounced across the knee, Mach 2 followed it up with another cover. ONE! TWO! THR… KICK OUT! Seeing the opportunity to put an end to this match, Ryan Gallway climbed out to the apron and scanned the sea of fans roaring for him to complete his very next move. The move that would put the nail in the coffin. SPEED OF SOUND! The Springboard Shooting Star Press had landed perfectly! After Mach 2 crashed atop the body of his hated adversary, he didn’t have time to follow up after Shawn Arrows came back into the fray and cut off his momentum with a boot to the jaw. He then tossed the youngster into the ropes… ARROW-SHOT DDT! Arrows had driven the New York native damn near through the canvas with his finishing maneuver. He then climbed atop the body of Mach 2 and cradled his body looking for the cover. ONE! TWO! THR… SAVE BY ANDY SHARP! The fans let out a sigh of relief as Mr. All-Star proved to be in the right place at the right time, saving his partner and their team from early elimination in this Chase to the Wake. Andy exchanged fists with Arrows in the center of the ring and it seemed that neither man was going to stop until Lopez blasted Andy from behind with a nasty elbow. Both men turned Mr. All-Star around and shoved him to the top rope as Lopez kept him up there. Fighting back, Andy kept the fists flying doing his damn best to make sure Lopez wasn’t able to follow up with either the Superplex or Brainbuster off the top he planned on using. Lopez fired back with several chops, but a nasty headbutt of all things finally knocked him off the top rope. Quickly, the former Spirit of ACW and World Champion dove… DOUBLE FLYING CLOTHESLINE! Both members of the Unknown Elements found themselves on the receiving end of Andy’s spectacular high-flying maneuver, but Andy would barely have any time to recuperate as Butch Brody locked his sights on the former champion and buried a knee into his gut. Scooping him up for something resembling a Bossman Slam, Andy flipped out… LEET FEET V.2! Like a gigantic sack of bricks, Butch Brody paid for his interference in spades with the big slam. The hometown crowd for the Highlight of the Night came to life as he attempted the pin… BOOOOOOOOOOOO! But BOTH Heirs of Wrestling grabbed Andy Sharp and casually tossed him over the top rope, sending him crashing to the floor. Quickly moving into action, Butch Brody found himself in the clutches of Pierce as Gallway set his sights on the big man… CORONATION! The Flatliner/Enzuigiri strike brought the big man down as Gallway climbed atop the big man and hoped this would be enough. ONE! Mack started to climb through the ropes as he watched the prone body of his brother. He needed to get there fast. TWO! The Mack Truck was halfway into the ring when a stiff Running Knee Strike from Pierce cut him off at the pass… THREE! Alas, it was too late. Mack, coming within inches of saving his brother, slumped down to the floor as “Champion” by Pastor Troy exploded through the speaker system. The fans were up in arms that the potential victory had been stolen from their very own hometown son. “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNERS OF THE MATCH… THE HEIRS OF WRESTLING!” Tommy Vale bellowed. “THE SONS OF ANARCHY HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED FROM THE CHASE!” With each of those last words burning, Mack went to check on his fallen brother while the Heirs headed for higher ground. Both the Unknown Elements as well as the First Airbourne stared with unparalleled hatred for the team that dared to steal the win in this match just to prove a point. Meanwhile on the ramp, Alexandria Malone was out there to greet her partners in crime, high-fiving each one and exchanging hugs. Clashing fists as a sign of a job well done, the crowd remained standing to give those fuckface Heirs of Wrestliung the verbal beatdowns they deserved. Divide and conquer worked just fine in eliminating the biggest threats to the ACW Tag Title hunt in the Sons of Anarchy, but with the looks on the faces of both the Elements and the Airbourne glaring icy daggers through the Heirs of Wrestling, it was clear they had painted a bigger bulls-eye on their chest for next week.
