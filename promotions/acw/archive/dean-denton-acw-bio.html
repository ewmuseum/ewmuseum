Dean Denton. Biography Real Name Dean Lee Denton. Nicknames The Darwinist, The Lizard, The Living Example, The Evolutionary Revolutionary, The Gene Pool's Lifeguard, The Atheist Over-Fiend, The Bible-Burner. Hometown No constant habitat. Birthday August the 2nd, 1982. Place of Birth Dagenham, London, England. Height 5'10 Weight 215 Blood Type Cold. Eye Color Green-yellow. Biography You can't really judge him. Since the advent of human self-awareness there has been religion. Beliefs, typically unfounded, reinforced through tribal ritual, social transmission and happy ignorance. It is the nature of humans, part of their hereditary psychological make-up, to imagine, create, deify and worship; to fill the gaps in their knowledge with theory, rhetoric, supposition and superstition. It makes them happy and whole. No more questions, no more confusion. A simple life with simple rules. Dean Denton was born with yellow eyes. Well, not strictly yellow, more of a yellow-green, the colour of bleach thick against kitchen tiles. But yellow enough. He grew up in one of the poorest districts of London with a father absent in body and a mother absent in mind. With those eyes, that sickly pallor and those tiny sharp teeth, he never had a chance. From his first day at school to his last romantic rejection, he was always The Lizard. And for a long time, Dean Denton believed in nothing. All he saw in the world was pointlessness and randomness, chaos and amorality. And he was correct, but in his rectitude he could find no solace. Then nature took over, and those religious instincts found something to cling on to. Denton discovered the works of godless philosophers such as Friedrich Nietzsche and the Marquis de Sade who taught him that compassion and kindness are superficialities with no purpose other than to manipulate others and that discarding one's morally imposed limitations would lead to a more fulfilling life. He discovered the work of the great naturalist Charles Darwin, whose theory of natural selection and of self-interest being the dominant directional force in the lives of all creatures inspired him with a new perspective on human behaviour. He read Richard Dawkins and admired him for his fervent promotion of Darwin's beliefs. He studied these authors alongside a copy of the Unabomber's Manifesto, which he found, quite disturbingly, to be an excellent read. From this, he created an entire world-view, a new mentality. He would become the most callous, aggressive, ruthless creature ever witnessed by humankind. He would transcend human limitations, finely tune his mind and body to near perfection. He would become the apex, the zenith, the alpha male, and in the race for survival, he would undoubtedly be the fittest. An endeavour at least somewhat prompted by massive insecurity. But nonetheless, Denton spent years transforming himself into a hard-bodied fighting machine, studying Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai and Judo, as well as further contemplating the theory that would come to dominate his life. Dean Denton felt whole for the first time, but at the expense of all of the characteristics considered desirable in a human being. Every day, he grows more and more like his namesake, his motivations simplified down to the frighteningly efficient mantra of hunt, kill, feed and mate. Every day, more like a lizard. But you can't really judge him. Not that that'll save you from him. Past Managers Arturo DeLillo. Team The Huntsmen. Wrestling Style MMA-influenced striker / submissionist.
