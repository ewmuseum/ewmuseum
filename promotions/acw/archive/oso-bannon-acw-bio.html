Oso Bannon Biography Real Name Oso Francisco Ibarra Bannon Nicknames The Bear, The Liberator Hometown Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio Birthday June 6th, 1988 Place of Birth Mexico City, Mexico Height 6'5 Weight 259 Blood Type O+ Eye Color Dark Brown Biography What can be said about Oso Bannon that hasn’t already been said in the papers, in the blogs, and on the television? His name was synonymous with dominance on college football’s national stage from the moment he laced up his cleats as a freshman for The Ohio State Buckeyes. Adopted into a wealthy, football-centric family from the impoverished, drug addled ghetto of Mexico City, he also became a symbol of the American Dream and an inspiration to anyone who wanted something bigger and better out of life. He was a lock for the Chuck Bednarik Award, given to the best defensive player in college football annually, his senior season and was considered by most, if not all, analysts to go number one in the NFL draft. Then, one fateful night in early November 2009, he was pulled over traveling from his parents home in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio back to Columbus with enough steroids to juice up a whole team and enough painkillers to make sure none of them ever felt a thing. Add a little cocaine to stay awake for good measure and you’ve got a marquee name, a superstar athlete, reduced to infamous rubble. Exiled from football, estranged from his adopted family, and not behind bars simply because he named names to the feds, Oso soon found himself aligned with another infamous name: Chester B. Wallace. A man virtually responsible for making sports representation what it is today, Wallace found himself under his own federal investigations and eventually banned from all conventional, professional sports. Yet, he had never been the most conventional man and so he refused to lay down, choosing instead to take his newest and only client to All-Star Championship Wrestling. With torrid reputations proceeding them everywhere they go and a roster full of wrestlers who could care less, only time will tell what’s in story for the pair together out of convenience.
