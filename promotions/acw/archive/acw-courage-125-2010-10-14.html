IN THE DARK #1 Having returned to active competition several weeks ago, Bobby Minio found that the landscape of ACW had changed dramatically since his beating at the hands of Iceman before LEGENDS IV. In fact, Bobby Minio also had to come to terms with his current status -- an unwilling member of the cabal, the super-evil group headed up by both Trey Vincent and Z. On this night, Bobby would have his second match as a member of the cabal. His opponent? ACW's resident eccentric drug-abuser in Antwone MacDaniels. Antwone kicked off the match in sensational fashion: by running around the ring sluggishly and then asking Minio if he had any PCP. Minio's response was to drive his knee upwards into Antwone's sternum, which was swiftly followed by a overhead belly-to-belly throw! And if that wasn't punishment enough for Antwone, Bobby Minio went on to spectacularly whoop his drug-infused arse. The fans, despite not being very high on Minio considering his status as Z's latest recruit, were very impressed with the style of the beating dished out. Antwone could barely string together two offensive moves, and perhaps it was a good thing that Minio finally decided to bust out his New Religion finisher as it put MacDaniels out of his misery. After the match, Bobby Minio looked like he had something to get off his chest, documented by him grabbing a microphone. The appearance of Kesavan and Kati, his colleagues in the cabal, put an end to that. IN THE DARK #2 Tag team wrestling in ACW had undergone a renaissance of sorts in 2010, stemming from when the Heirs Of Wrestling seemed to be running amok without any challengers in sight. That was then (January), this was now: and the now featured many active teams in the division in addition to a number of burgeoning tandems in the shadows and also the All-Star Academy. Two of these teams debuted several weeks ago to losing starts. Tonight, they did battle against each other: the odd pairing of Rokko Strange and his ex-girlfriend Aya Sumiko who had wrestled together in Europe and Asia for many years, against the Greek brother/sister duo of Galen and Temperance Hellas (the Children Of Hellas). Rokko and Galen got the match started, with Rokko using his vast experience to great effect. Temperance appeared extremely agitated at her brother's inability to match up against Rokko, and it was therefore no surprise when she pounced on the opportunity for a sneaky blind tag. Rokko was tickled at Temperance climbing into the ring for a crack at him; clearly, he didn't think much of her. He would be stunned when Temperance proceeded not only to match Rokko move for move, but Temperance was able to take down the experienced Rokko with a springboard hurricarana! It became apparent to Rokko that Temperance was highly-skilled and quick-footed; the latter of which he couldn't claim to possess. Hence, Rokko tagged his ex-girlfriend Aya into the match. That development naturally had the fans very excited. Two women in the ring, raring to tear the house down? And in semi-skimpy outfits? Yes, please. Of course, the action that followed didn't last all that long. The moment Aya appeared to have gained an advantage, Temperance resorted to an eye gouge, before rushing over to the opposing corner and spitting in Rokko's face! Aghast, Rokko stormed into the ring to get his hands on Temperance. But with the referee doing his best to keep Rokko at bay, Temperance took advantage of the situation and ordered her brother to ascend to the top. One fancy double-team move later (suplex / frog splash combination), and the Children Of Hellas had racked up a tainted win. Aya Sumiko was pissed, as was Rokko Strange. Temperance? Beaming from ear to ear, despite her younger brother's insistence that they'd won the match via cheating. Clearly, Temperance wasn't bothered with the 'how'. A win was a win, was a win.
