Three elbows from a desperate Almasy sent LLB sprawling backwards, off the second rope. He scrambled back to his feet, but by then, Almasy was airborne. MOONSAULT BODYBLOCK! CONNECTS! Seymour clutched at his back as he rolled off The Law, injured, but still battling. He leant against the ropes for support, taking a deep breath as the Law got back up. Seeing that, Seymour threw his body forward, and reared back his right hand. DEATHTOUCH! COVER! ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! LLB’s shoulder shot up off the canvas at two. Effective as Seymour’s charging palm strike was, it wasn’t enough to put The Law down for the count of three. Seymour dragged LLB back up to a vertical base, popping him with a pair of elbow strikes to the jaw. The Law reeled back, and Seymour followed with two more, before burying a hard right kick to the abdomen. Front facelock. DDT. No. To Seymour’s surprise, LLB put the brakes on, managing to stop Seymour’s DDT, and instead throwing Seymour up and over! NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX INTO THE TURNBUCKLES! The Final Fantasy crashed head over heels into the corner, slumping to the mat in a heap. LLB, meanwhile, was inwardly exultant. Finally, it seemed, he’d cleared himself out of this funk he was in. Dragging the Final Fantasy away from the corner, he covered, being sure to hook the leg. ONE! TWO! THR--NO! Seymour Almasy was still alive. Still kicking. But LLB sensed that his time was soon. Almasy was ripe for the picking. Ready to be put away. Grabbing him by the wrist, he pulled Almasy up, shot him into the ropes, and declared his findings once more. ERRONEOUS CONCLUSION! … MISSES! DOUBLE STOMP TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD?! Yup, Seymour jumped over the spearing LLB, driving both feet into the back of his skull. With LLB dazed, Seymour saw a chance, twisting The Law into the time-honored MAGISTRAL CRADLE! ONE! TWO! THR--NOOO! LLB managed to wiggle free with pure determination. The desire to halt his losing streak and vault to the top of the industry guided him as he pulled himself off the canvas once again. He scored with a sharp, hard European uppercut, leaving Seymour staggered, and waiting for the end. Here it was, Roland Erman thought. The chance he needed. For Seymour Almasy had erred. And in court, when opposing counsel erred, there was only one response. “OBJECTION!” SUPERKI---CAUGHT! TURNED TO THE ANKLELOCK! Truth be told, Seymour didn’t apply an ankle lock too much. But for smarks in the crowd, the move had significance. It was the move he’d won the Scorpion Fighting and Television Championships with back at breakOUT. Almasy cranked down awkwardly on his rediscovered submission, but LLB was larger than Fejona Min, plus, LLB’s ankle hadn’t been damaged in the course of this match. Those two factors allowed The Law to get to the ring ropes, thus forcing Seymour’s clean break. The Final Fantasy was growing slightly frustrated as well. He hadn’t wrestled as solid a match as he usually did. For some reason, LLB was doing extraordinarily well against him. But Seymour didn’t let frustration get to him too much. He picked LLB back up, and Irish whipped him into the opposite corner. Collecting himself, Seymour charged, going airborne for a Stinger Splash. NOBODY HOME! Almasy crashed into the corner, tottering backwards out of it, and The Law was there to capitalize. SCHOOLBOY ROLLUP! ONE! Being a veteran, LLB recognized his ring positioning. He was so very close to the ropes. Why, all he’d have to do was reach one or both feet back, and he could put them on the ring ropes, giving him lots of leverage over his 40 pounds lighter opponent. If he did that, victory would be nearly assured. His foot crept back slowly, in the second between the referee’s count. Ready to throw himself away for a win. Ready to become ACW World Champion. TWO! But… No. THR--KICKOUT! He couldn’t do it. Just simply, flat-out, couldn’t do it. Ramming Almasy into the post was one thing. Cheating him out of everything he’d worked so hard to obtain was another. No, he was going to do this right. The way it should be done. Conviction swirled through him again as he fired Seymour into the ropes. And to that damned voice in his head that told him he’d regret it later, he had but one thing to say. “OBJECTION!” ON THE BUTTON! Down LLB fell, smoothly hooking Seymour’s leg on the way down. As he settled in for the cover, he said but one thing, loud enough, he thought, for no one else to hear. “I rest my case.” ONE! TWO! THREEEEEEE~! It was over. He’d done it. LLB had become the ACW World Heavyweight Champion. Then why the fuck was the referee holding up two fingers? LLB looked at Monet Samuel in utter confusion and disbelief, until he saw Seymour’s right boot resting on the bottom ring rope. It hadn’t been three. It had been only two. So close. So very, very close. But not enough. Now, LLB looked down at Almasy himself, at the World Champion’s battered body. “Not yet,” Almasy insisted, in response to the Law’s earlier statement. LLB was, to put it mildly, not pleased. “Stay down!” he practically screamed, getting up and firing stomps into the Final Fantasy’s head. Seymour covered up as best he could, the crowd chanting to inspire him to get back up. “SEY-MOUR! SEY-MOUR! SEY-MOUR!” Somehow, The Law noted darkly, there were no chants now of “LLB! LLB! LLB!”. But that was okay, because this match was about to end. LLB picked Seymour up, setting him up for a powerbomb. If he hit this, it was gonna be over. Up Seymour went. But things didn’t go quite according to plan. SUNSET FLIP COUNTER! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! And just like that, it was over. Before LLB really knew what had happened, his shoulders had been pinned to the mat for three seconds, and he had lost, again. Seymour sat up groggily, offering his hand to LLB as the dumbfounded Law sat up in turn. He blinked a few times, unable to believe his latest misfortune. But he shook Seymour’s hand. He wasn’t pleased, but he did it anyway. Rolling from the ring, LLB walked back up the ramp, head hung, not looking back as Seymour was presented his championship. Something, The Law knew, had to change. Winner > Almasy via pinfall; retains World Title. CREDITS.
