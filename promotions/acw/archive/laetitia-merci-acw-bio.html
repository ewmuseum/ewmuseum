Laetitia Merci Biography Real Name Laetitia Merci Nicknames The Scarlet Merci Hometown Cannes, France Birthday October 18th, 1992 Place of Birth Paris, France Height 5'4 Weight 121 Eye Color Blue Biography Laetitia is Claire's stunningly gorgeous little sister. She's 17, but frequently lies about her age and says she's 18 in order to wrestle. She is really close to Claire, primarilly because Claire is her only remaining family. Laetitia relies almost completely on Claire, almost to the point that some might consider Laetitia to be an albatross around her neck in the business. However, none can deny the heart she puts into the business, either. Before becoming a professional wrestler, Laetitia was a popular teen fashion model in France. Her move to professional wrestling was a bit of a surprise to everyone close to her. Even in America, she's sometimes followed by paparazzi, photographers, and reporters. She has poor-to-nonexistent English skills and relies on Claire to be her translator. Her words get lost in translation because of Claire's very rude personality, as Laetitia is extremely kind and quite modest.
