Geo Vacton He's T-N-T, He's Dy-No-Mite Real Name: Geo Vacton Date of Birth: May 20, 1976 Hometown: Blackwood, New Jersey Height: 6'2'' Weight: 235 lbs. Entrance Music: 'TNT' by AC/DC Alignment Information Fan Dispostion: Neutral Current Teams: N/A Manager(s): Magick Man Allies: Enemies: Move-List Information Wrestling Style: Well rounded Signature Moves: The Basics (regular moves): TNT DDT (Standard DDT) Super kick Suplex (variations: snap, german, vertical, t-bone, fisherman's, rolling, reverse, half belly, released Belly-to-Belly) DDT (tornado, double arm, reverse, top rope) Rocker Dropper (fameasser, from anywhere) Swinging Neckbreaker Sidewalk slam Oops! (Atomic Drop, followed by pleas that it was an accident) Powerbomb (variations: gut wrentch, crucifix, running, tiger, sit out) Lariat (variations: flying, backbrain, dragon screw, head-hold) In the Air: Tilt-a-whirl slam (top rope, anywhere) Moonsault (variations: asai, corkscrew) Hurricanrana (from anywhere) Spinning Heel Kick (in the air, rope, anywhere) Elbow drop (variations: top rope, on the mat) Splash (variations: phoenix, firebird, frog) Head scissors take down (top rope or standing) Set Up Move: - Finisher: Dynamite Drop - Reverse DDT into a Cross Cutter Historic Information ACW Title History: US Championship ACW Award History: Other Federations: zTw, tHw, 21w Other Federation Awards: Biography As a commentator once said, “Of all we’ve seen, we only know… a Fraction of Vacton.” That wasn’t the last time that phrase would be heard, in fact it was going to become the name of the Official Geo Vacton website. Some say they know a lot about Geo Vacton, and some might know a little bit more. But even as much as his very own father knows, it’s still but only “a Fraction of Vacton.” And the chances of you learning any more then that by reading this right now, is very slim. Not much is known about his early life. In fact, with the exception of what was learnt from the interviews with Geo Vacton’s father in zTw(2), nothing is known. And even what his father said leaves a lot to the imagination. We do know that Geo has a brother who is also a wrestler, but has never made it past the indy’s. His name, Jeff Vacton. Geo & Jeff started wrestling each other in the basement of their fathers house in Blackwood, New Jersey. After hearing a loud bang in the basement, their father came rushing down the stairs only to see Geo and Jeff high fiving each other after pulling off an excellent hurricanrana. Seeing some talent and desire in his two boys, after grounding them for two weeks, decided to enroll them into wrestling school. Their trainer, Nick Steel, or as he was known in the business, “Reel Steel.” Steel was well known in the indys, and was sort of doing old Nat, Geo’s father, favor by training his two boys. Geo caught on much quicker to most of the moves, and was much more agile in the air then Jeff. Jeff didn’t put in as much as Geo, so of course Geo soared way ahead of him in his wrestling ability. I wish I could say that Jeff buried himself in school work and that kind of stuff, but that isn’t true. Jeff got involved in some rough crowds, but luckily got himself on the right track after high school. Geo entered the indys as “Beefcake Brad” in the EHW: Extreme Hardcore Wrestling which was under the management of Brian Nelson. EHW didn’t last very long. Jeff had also entered this federation under the name of “Jayhawk.” Surprisingly, or well, very surprisingly, Jeff become the World Champion of this small federation, and even led a stable, which was popular in some small towns where they traveled frequently. Geo didn’t stop though. He knew he’d get the chance to wrestle in the main event, and show everyone what he was made of. It was here that Geo met Otto. Otto became Geo’s “Go get it” boy, even though Otto was substantially older then him. Otto grew into Geo’s lackey very quickly, and as anyone will notice, Otto isn’t very quick when it comes to brainpower; and that’s pretty much all we know about Otto. As stated before, EHW didn’t last long. EHW went under, only to reopen again as “HEW: Hardcore Extreme Wrestling.” It was here that Geo would be aloud to wrestle under his real name, of Geo Vacton. Geo did several main event angles, although he never became World Champion, although he did come close many times. HEW didn’t last long either, and Geo felt his luck was running dry. Very dry. Geo joined the “HWA: Hardcore Wrestling Alliance.” As you may of noticed, Geo started off very hardcore in his rookie years. Which is what probably got him used to take major bumps and falls in the ring. HWA was great for Geo. Here he captured his first title, the “Hardcore” title. He won it from “Happy Pete” by pulling out a naked picture of Bea Author from his pocket, causing Pete to pass-out. After months of problems backstage, Geo was fired. But being fired wasn’t a bad thing… It was a GOOD thing! Because the next morning, Geo got the call that would change his life. A call from no other then Dr. Kenneth Zyber. Zyber owned the zTw, ZyberTech Wrestling and was interested in Geo Vacton, he wanted him as part of the “team.” After asking if perhaps he wanted the number of his brother, Jeff Vacton who was still wrestling in the HWA, (HWA would be the last federation that Jeff wrestled in) Geo said yes. The next day he arrived in the zTw, he was at home. Everything seemed great, and things couldn’t be going any better then they were. As zTw closed, Geo was well on his way to becoming a possible Steel champion, and gaining a lot of heat wrestling the likes of Mr. Haunt and Quizbo. In mid-July of 2000, zTw closed its doors. Left with no where to go to, Geo was asked to apply for the tHw: Totally Hardcore Wrestling by his fellow zTw’er, Lionheart. Geo stepped into tHw feeling confident that he could make it. At this point, Geo had a small following of fans and was able to pump the crowd up during every match. If nothing else, Geo could make it look good. Bringing in his wacky antics, and combining them with extreme stunts, Geo was making it. Coming towards the end of the tHw era, Geo was wrestling such well known wrestlers as Arch Angle and Tommy Dreamer. Infact, Geo wrestled Tommy Dreamer at the very first “Tuesday Morning Uproar” in the main event. Geo lost, but not without gaining a little more respect from not only the crowd, but as well the people in the back. Totally Hardcore Wrestling closed just before it’s annual PPV, “ToTTR: Totally Over The Top Rumble.” Geo was a contender to win this match, and very well could have been on his way to face the tHw World Champion. When tHw closed, Geo stopped wrestling for a very long time. He helped out a few places, wrestled a few matches and made a few appearances here and there. But nothing spectacular. Then one day in late summer of 2001… Geo got a phone call. Another call which would change his life, another call from the same man who did it before… Dr. Kenneth Zyber. Zyber was reopening zTw. He was going to make a run of it, and was choosing 6 past zTw/WcF/ZWF wrestlers to wrestle in his new company. Geo Vacton was chosen. ZTW2 seemed very promising, and it had a lot of support. But it failed. It made no impact on the (e)Wrestling world whatsoever, although it did give a few superstars a chance to show what they could do when more fans were watching; if anyone benefited at all from zTw, it was Geo Vacton. He won the Aluminum Championship in a match with Maverick, the Aluminum Championship was the equivalent of the Hardcore championship… finally Geo had a REAL title to call his own. But zTw was still gone, and Geo still had no job. Geo looked to the one place he’d been match on TV, the one place that interested him whatsoever. No, not Asylum. No, not fWo. No not any of them. 21w. Geo had followed 21w ever since it was invaded by the Asylum. After hearing that 21w was looking into a few of the zTw wrestlers, Geo made a phone call to Mike Powell, and entered 21w. Geo entered the arena his very first night, knowing he’d be seeing the 21w logo a lot more now, and surprised the fans with a bang. Using the initials of “GV” in his tron video, he fooled the fans into thinking he was Graphic Violence, only to come bursting in with “TNT” by ACDC. Vacton stayed with 21w for a short while and was involved in a few classic 21w bouts including his infamous RAGE match with his arch nemesis, Mr. Haunt. His time in 21w was short lived, however, as he soon dropped out of 21w to pursue a career in ACW. A few months before ACW lost its TV deal, Geo quit ACW and decided to retire from wrestling because he had decided that no place would ever be like ACW. Vacton couldn't stand not wrestling though, so he soon came crawling back to ACW to be welcomed with open arms.
