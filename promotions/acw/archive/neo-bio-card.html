Neo Xealot Akuma, Djinn, Akira Real Name: Kazuya Raziel Leonard Birthdate: 2nd August 1981 Hometown: Born in Hong Kong / Raised in Osaka, Japan / Hails from Los Angeles, CA Height: 5'11' Weight: 209 lbs Entrance Music: 'Megalomaniac' by Incubus Medical Information Natural Hair Color: Dark Brown Eye Color: Grey Blood Type: O Body Fat Percentile: 5.71% Favored Workout: Martial Arts-based practical (meditation, kata, punching bag) Past Illnesses: Minor cases of influenza Past Injuries: Various back, neck and head injuries In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Unmatched Martial Artist/Aerial Hardcore Unorthodox Trainer: Unknown Status: Neutral Set-Up Move(s): Judgment Moon Backflip kick to the chin which sets up Paradigm Shift Finishing Move: (1) Paradigm Shift and (2) The Hush (1) After his victim stumbles, Kaz dashes toward his stunned opponent and rises so that one of his sides faces opponent and with the hand on the side of his opponent, hook-uppercuts his victim in the chin extremely hard while jumping very high. The impact launches the guy across the ring high and far! Xealot can incorporate a much faster and direct version of this move into combos or as the ending part of a sweep kick, if he misses he can often get a thrust kick in while landing. Think of videogames like Street Fighter or Tekken as inspirations for this move. (2) Xealot runs up the front of his victim and backflip kicks him in the face, ascending perpindicularly and eventually landing upon his opponent body-first. Basically it's a run-up backflip body splash and its extremely dangerous to both Xealot and his victim. Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: None ACW Awards: None Other Federations: Delta, Flatlined Other Federations Awards: None Career History Wrestling Style: Xealot is not a wrestler and he does not try to be a wrestler either despite being in a wrestling environment. Like he has always done, Neo Xealot practices a unique art he calls Akumate (Demon Fist) – a lethal interpretation of the martial art used by his mother’s family. Experts claim that it is based upon Shotokan Karate, Ninjitsu and Win Chun and that it borrows many dangerous aspects from the external Martial Arts disciplines of Hapkido (Korea), Kenpo (Hawaii) and Muay Thai (Thailand). Xealot’s interpretation of the martial art is mixed in with “the breaking technique” of tameshiwari (the same technique used to break stuff with strikes strengthened through meditation) and can employ it instantaneously in his much feared power punches. He has also dabbled in the caged-fighting art of Vale Tudo and is well versed in mounting and able to do so from any position, subsequently pounding his victims into non-existence. In a wrestling ring, his expertise in unarmed combat makes him ideally suited to an extreme unorthodox style and he seamlessly combines his unmatched martial arts repertoire with high flying and hardcore antics by (for example) adding impetus to strikes by leaping off the top rope or spin kicking a chair into his victim’s face. Nevertheless his wrestling style is decisively stiff and toe-to-toe, few parallels can be drawn with his amazing kicks and punches and the grappling technique of what is considered to be acceptable wrestling. Akumate’s main trait is that it relies on the force of inner rage over the forces of inner peace. It is a paradox of martial arts that frowns upon fighting styles being intended for self-defensive purposes only – the opposite, in fact, is true of Akumate and it is intended for attack and tailor-made for both ring-based warfare and systematic domination of all opponents. Xealot relies on being unbelievably quick, agile, flexible and graceful. His muscular strength is formidable and his astounding ability with regard to an art called Aiki (to use ones strength against him, to ‘harmonize’ one’s own strength and the strength of his opponents) and his exclusive knowledge of many powerful secrets make him a complete combatant and a tricky opponent with which to deal. He does not suffer the same weaknesses that most in his weight class suffer and is more than a match for most opponents. He has pure fighter’s intuition and his ability to evade, dodge and parry makes it seem as though he can tell the future – because you can’t beat, what you can’t catch. Coupled is a single-minded perseverance unheralded in the fighting world and the young warrior can take a lot of punishment around the bodily region. It has been noted that severe blows to the head work well against Neo Xealot. Every opponent that has beaten him thus far has employed a tactic involving aggravation of head trauma in one way or another. This may well be his only weakness. He is a genius of his trade and his art is so refined that it is believed that there is no fighting style that he cannot match with his own. As a fighter he has mastered the maximisation of every strike’s potential – making each attack an utter art to behold from the sidelines. He is regarded by many to be the best short-ranged striker in the business, putting unprecedented power behind his trademark straight punches and lightning speed and accuracy behind his rapier-like kicks and knees. It is the subtle nature of his game that is the most attractive however… Brutal yet beautiful, his movements flow between moves and techniques and defence so fluidly… as if it came to him as naturally as walking. He frustrates opponents by unnoticeably changing his style and strategy during matches. Xealot is also a melee weapons specialist. In ring competition, tonfa sticks wielded in each hand and long wooden staffs remain weapons of choice for the young fighter. He is also a superb swordsman, one of the best in existence because he is able to wield the weapon practically unlike most martial artists. Beware - his diminutive size and pretty boy looks do well to hide his rage which is just a couple of notches short of all out homicidal. He welcomes this state of rage to his arsenal though, seeks to control it, so that he can break out in every match and destroy with irrational powers. The fuse of this rage is short – it can be set off by even the most subtle of insults. Opponents would do well not to piss him off. It is believed that in terms of technique, he is probably the greatest fighter in the world… The jury, however, is out on whether or not Neo Xealot will ever live up to his mercurial promise. Inconsistency and complacency have plagued him for his entire career and he can sometimes over elaborate in the ring – employing over-the-top manoeuvres when simpler ones would have sufficed. On his day however, he is unmatchable and very have been able to compensate for his artisan when he’s on-song. BASE MOVES -“Distance Strike” - Striking moves requiring a great level of mental and spiritual control in order to knock opponents across distances. His favourite being the full force power punch named Viral Conquest. -“Wind Walker” – Kick combos of two, three or four hitting consecutively in unbelievably high mid-air -“Dragon Tamer”- Underarm punches to the ribs that are meant to preferably fracture or break. Most of the time they stun the victim. -“Angel Hunter”- A deadly modification of the Dragon Tamer where Kaz continuously uppercuts the victim’s jaw. -“Dance of the Devil”- A modification of a Muay Thai tendancy, Kaz secures a grip behind his victim’s head/neck, kneeing him in the face, abdomen and kidneys (when exposed) for a random amount of times to wear the victim out. He then forces his victim into a corner and knees the living shit out of him before turning him around and kneeing him some more, powering him to another corner and then repeating the cycle over and over – usually finishing with a spectacular finishing effort. -“Punches” – Xealot loves breaking through an opponent’s defence to unleash his hard-hitting yet lightning quick punch flurries. -“Kicks” – Xealot loves to kick the shit out of opponents, his vicious shin kicks to the thighs and lower-legs and knee sides are a particularly good method of wearing down tall opponents. High snapping roundhouse kicks frequently catch victims off guard for an unexpected knockout. Signature Moves/Holds (Potential Match Enders): -“Viral Conquest” – Full force power punch to the chest area which sends the victim tumbling backward ala Paul Phoenix from the Tekken videogame. - “Reaper Fire” – Sprinting sideways power punches to chest using both fists ala Evil Jet Li in The One (knocks opponent back hard). - “Plague Dominion” - Pushes opponent face-first into the turnbuckle and then throws his trademark punch into the small of his victim’s back. He then follows up with a high roundhouse kick to the back of his victim’s head. -“Vindicator Reject” - As his victim punches or extends his arm in some way, Neo Xealot grabs the arm in reversal with an alternating hand and pulls his victim down to the ground beside him with the momentum, throwing a knockout punch to the temple as his opponent descends -“My Final Heaven” - Three alternating Dragon Tamers followed by an alternating Angel Hunter and then a double-legged back flip kick. Usually executed during ‘Dance of the Devil’ -“Pseudo Infinity” - Sprint, Dragon Tamer punch or ‘Dance of the Devil’ setup to jumping upper-cutting elbow to the jaw/chin from a crouched position. -“Demigod’s Rampage” - A super fast flurry of punches and palm strikes to various points on the victim’s body followed up by a upper cutting right hook through the face. -“Demon Wind Dance” – Sprint to jumping corkscrew kick* with unbelievable height, hang time and speed (*three kicks that hit consecutively in mid-air but with alternating feet and the body spinning in a corkscrew motion. The attacker lands right at the moment of the third kick hitting.) -“Demon Pillar Splitter” – Xealot lifts his victim from behind and brings him down tail-bone first upon his knee. Can augment the move with countless varying strikes. -“Demon Steel Wing” – Right hook to the midsection that stuns the victim COMBOS -Various sweep kick to power punch/elbow combos -Various dangerous Standard/Spinning/Roundhouse Punch/Elbow strikes/combos performed without mercy or the intention of letting the victim escape. -Various deadly Standard/Spinning/Roundhouse Kick/Knee strikes/combos hitting to places where they are meant to injure -Various merciless mixed combos usually executed after powering the victim into a corner -Submission combos executed after a standing tackle to the abdomen THROWS AND TACKLES -Judo Wheels, Hip throws and Shoulder throws -Low Tackle leads to other moves, mostly submission combo and mounted punches -Grappling kicks and punches -Punch/Kick Reversals performed when he grabs attempted kicks or punches from his opponents (Usually uses opponent’s momentum against them in some way to land various throws, choke submissions or even pulling the victim down into devastating knockout punches) -Known to tackle victims into the cage and then destroy them with combos MISC: -Backward/Forward flawless Somersault/Cartwheel/Flip/etc Kicks -Martial Arts submissions and chokeholds (mostly armbars with heel kicks, legbars with heel kicks, scissor locks and ankle shoots Biography: I’ve scoured the underground for almost a decade. There I basked in the dust as it finally settled upon my hardened flesh and the ground all around me where the innocents lay. Every one of those soulless entities has touched me in a way that no prophet ever could. I became pure and earned the divine right to purge the world of its demons. Under the guise of a professional head hunter, I sought out and destroyed notoriety manifest. The demons came in different forms - from pimps to drug lords to mafia bosses and they were all judged in the same way – my blade saw to that. When the phone stopped ringing and the demand for spillage of evil blood died down, I stepped into the subterranean arenas of the world and crushed all who came forth to oppose me. I thusly earned my keep in a world that I loved. And I, the infamous Bladewind, the great demon hunter under the concrete skies of this Promised Land, was as free as I had ever wanted to be. I found pleasure in the ill-begotten pleas for mercy rambling from the throats of the ones I judged and I was like an Angel – beautiful, majestic and untouchable. The forgotten world was my kingdom; its misplaced denizens my subjects. I continued, empowered by my newfound godhood, to wade through the blood of a thousand enemies and I was living the American dream in my own unique style. Then I met Harmony… And I loved her and she loved me and all my undertakings in the underground that once seemed so necessary, became inconsequential when compared to her and the feelings that she brought to my heart. I realised that I was not just an instrument of hate. She took me to the surface and I utilised my talents of destruction in a legitimate world where nobody knew of my origins and my past. I thrived again. In my naivety, however, I failed to see the consequences of my actions in my previous life. In that life I had no family or friends. I thusly had no weaknesses and I had nothing to lose. But in my union with Harmony, I had had ironically gained the virus that I had eluded. Somebody raped her and killed her to get to me. I hunted the perpetrator for a year and turned up with nothing but eternal heartache… and a revelation. He came to me in nightmares at first, but when I started to gain insight into my birthright, he came to me in dreams. Akuma was his name and he taught me how to fight like the masters of old. Together we engineered the Demon Art of Akumate – a martial art devoid of all weaknesses. Thus empowered, he came to me again and led me to Japan and the wayward place of my birth. He told me of a chamber beneath my grandfather’s home, where the final piece of the puzzle rested and waited for me to claim it. I stole inside and claimed the prize. It was a sword forged from the finest steel and embossed in the most precious jewels. Akuma dubbed the blade… Hyperion. And it has become My blade; My right hand… My Hyperion. The edge runs red with the blood of the wicked. The crimson falls emanate a thousand echoes and all are riddled with agony, guilt and defeat. I take no small pleasure in the unified acoustic personification of evil pain. It is like music to my ears, an endless chain of the sweetest sensations, a melancholy choir of those who have been slain… The rhapsody that results has no equal in my wake and it fills me to the brim… It fills me like the ambrosia cup and makes me whole. I can still hear the past as if my own brain was reciting it to me in a studious drone. The clarity is unreal. The clarity surpasses memory. I hear a billion naives begging for their wretched lives. I hear a billion questions that I have asked myself my entire life. And I laugh at myself and the demon and its blade laugh along too. Because those billion questions that were once blown into the aerial abyss and scattered among the earthly ruins in fallout… …have become bound together like the shrieking pleas of uncountable enemies… …and one question has resulted. …and the answer to that question is Hyperion. The answer to ALL questions is Hyperion. For we are the same - the blade, the demon and I. Look at me. Despair. I have cried the tears of eternal lifetimes. Spilt enough blood to drown the earth… and for what? … nothing… I was created with one singular purpose… To become a conduit and a temple; to stage the reunion of the demon warrior Akuma and his symbiotic weapon Hyperion! … And now that we have fused, our essences have become intertwined in a ritualistic dance that marks the deaths of tainted men. Let them flee into the night, let them pray like pagans in vain ceremony… For we will come for them. We will come for them all. And our wills combined and manifest will judge the world. … None can hide from Demon, Flesh and Metal. We are cannot be beaten for we are many.
