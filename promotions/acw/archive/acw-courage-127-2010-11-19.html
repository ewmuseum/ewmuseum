IN THE DARK #1: Saleem Nagi vs. JP Boudreau The fans of Ancona were in for a treat tonight, even before the live broadcast of COURAGE was set to take place. In one of All-Star Championship Wrestling's biggest minor league signings, Saleem Nagi -- a former West Coast Championship Wrestling Silver Champion and one of the most dominant competitors in the promotion's history bar none -- was getting his chance to shine before the live audience. It had become a tend, recently, to put these newbs through a string of IN THE DARK matches so that the live audience wouldn't become accustom to them if anything were to happen to them in a short amount of time. And considering the number of bad guys on the roster? People disappearing was a pretty often occurence. Saleem's opponent for the evening would be none other than ACW mainstay JP Boudreau. This was nothing to snuff you nose at either, as many others had done in the past. Boudreau was a true professional in the ring, and could easily have you winded quick enough to get a quick roll-up pin all victory within a matter of minutes. Making an impression, was not going to be an easy thing to do. And just as anticipated, the match did begin hot. Boudreau took advantage quickly taking away Saleem's legs with a quick drop toe hold, into a springboard dropkick as Saleem began to stand up, and then continued the pain by locking Saleem into a single leg grapevine. It took several elbow shots to the skull of JP to break the hold, but Saleem slowly found his way back to his feet. It would be a clothesline attempt that would begin to see some offense from Saleem. As JP attempted the clothesline, Saleem dropped holding onto the top rope that had helped him find his way to his feet. The move sent Boudreau flying to the outside of the ring, and it was all downhill for him after there. That one fatal flaw would seal his demise, as JP had tried to crawl back into the ring he was met with a running straight knee to the face. A move that Saleem had given the perfect name to, The Downfall, because once he hit it there would be no coming back from it. On this night Saleem would pick up his first pinfall victory in ACW, but from the looks of his move, and the length of time that it took JP Boudreau to get back to his feet. This kid could very well have a future in the [Black] Scorpion Championship ranks of this company. But that is neither here nor there, Saleem would first need to make his way through the ranks of the already established members of what had come to be known as the IN THE DARK crowd.
