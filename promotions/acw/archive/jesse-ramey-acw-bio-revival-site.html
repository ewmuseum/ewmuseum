Jesse Ramey 'Bad Attitude', 'Mr. Action!', 'THE All-Star Championship Wrestler' Real Name: Jesse Tyler Ramey Date of Birth: January 6th, 1976 Hometown: Harts, West Virginia Height: 5'11' Weight: 205 lbs. Entrance Music: 'Going Down In Flames' by 3 Doors Down Alignment Information Fan Dispostion: Face Current Teams: none Manager(s): none Allies: Blackheart (UWL), Fubu (UWL,EWF,CWA,JEW), Kurt Ragnarock (JEW), Cianan (CCW), Asa Fountain (FSW) Enemies: Blayze (CCW), Cianan (CCW), Sebastian Toomes (OSW), Steven Lee (PURE), Chad Villain (Action!) Move-List Information Wrestling Style: All Round (Focuses mostly on high flying and submission; with a passion for hardcore) Signature Moves: Touching the Sky (Split-Legged Moonsault), Touchdown (Rolling Thunder), Extreme Measures (Jump Spinning Heel Kick to the Face), Culture Development (Asai Moonsault). Set Up Move: The Hangtime - Tornado DDT from any position Finisher: The Attitude Adjustment - Elevated Boston Crab w/ Right Knee In Opponent's Back Historic Information ACW Title History: none ACW Award History: none Other Federations: UWL, EWF, CWA, JEW, CCW, OSW, FSW, PURE, PWC, tA, thReat, RaGE, Action! Wrestling, WWC Other Federation Awards: UWL Hall of Fame Inductee, UWL Tag Team Champion (w/ Blackheart), UWL Television Champion (First to win and last to hold) (x 2 and Longest Championship Reign), First EWF Hardcore Champion, CWA Tag Team Champion (w/ Klown), CWA Hardcore Champion (x 2), CWA World Heavyweight Champion, JEW Cruiserweight Champion, JEW USA Champion, CCW Independent Champion, CCW Lightweight Champion, FSW Tag Team Champion (w/ Asa Fountain), First and Only PURE Heavyweight Champion. Trained By: (Brawling, Psychology, Stealth, and Stamina Training) July, 1986-December, 1990: Juvenile Military Boot Camp, Drill Instructor Major Charles Alexander III; (Basic Professional Wrestling Training) January, 1991- March, 1992: Killer Kowalski's Pro Wrestling School; (Martial Arts Training) April, 1992- March 1993: Tiger Mask in Japan while Jesse worked for AJPW; (Submission and Technical Training) April, 1993- July, 1994: Stu Hart in the Dungeon, with the help of Bret and Owen on Technical training occasionally. Biography One man can only live one life, or at least that is what you have been told all of your life, but the truth be told this is the tale of a man that has been made to live many in a very short time. From his humble begins as a child on into his haunting present. His story is not for the weak or faint at heart. Rittled with memories of the death of his parents, women, drugs, money, the people who he thought he could once call friend, and the only real love of his life taken from him. A career in professional wrestling that has been no accomplishment to him, as close to five of his twelve year career was devoted to having the management of whatever federation he was in holding him down. The only places he was safe was overseas and even there he had his enemies in the community itself. His decision rested upon one of two things, coming back to the states and hoping to find a promotion that wasn’t ran by underhanded monopoly or to stay overseas and risk his life wrestling in communities that were nothing short of anti-American. Fate, is something that comes swiftly, and now, the fate of this still young man’s career rests in the hands of ACW. Match History Courage 1: Jesse Ramey v CJ Trenton Loss via CJ with clean pin.
