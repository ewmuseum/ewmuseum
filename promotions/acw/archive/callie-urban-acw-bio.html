Callie Urban Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Chinatown, LA, CA Birthday 07 April 1982 Place of Birth Xi'an, People's Republic of China Height 5'4 Weight 125 Blood Type O- Eye Color Grey Biography Adopted by Chinese parents, Callie was pushed to excel at an early age. Between martial arts, language classes, and a host of other extracurricular activities, it shouldn't be a surprise that she graduated at the top of her class and was accepted into Stanford. It also shouldn't be a surprise that she graduated with honors and an MBA. While she was going through school, a friend, who was watching her compete in a martial arts tournament suggested she give professional wrestling a try. Deciding it was a nice distraction from the rest of her life, she met John Stevenson who then became her trainer. A year later and she debuted with no brand Wrestling under a street rat persona. She stayed with nbW with brief forays into other promotions. Taking some time off to start her own investment firm, she returned to nbW and was poised to win the world title until a fan-related incident left her with a broken neck and a painful year of rehabilitation. Again taking time off to focus on her business she returned to nbW for a few brief stints before Spike Saunders contacted her with an offer to come to fWo. A few months later the street smart persona dropped and her real personality came to the fore. Now she's set to take on anyone in the organization who gets in her way of what she defines as a reality check for the performers and fans alike. Had enough of a nice little sheet bio? Here's the deal. She's in the middle of a nasty divorce with a crazy woman, running five directions every week with her own business and still dealing with all the crazy that goes on in fWo and RR. There are only fumes left in the tank, but it's only a matter of time before someone tosses a match in... And that match is named Trevor Wilson. Other Feds nbW, LoC, eDw, fWo, RR Other Fed Titles nbW Dynasty, nbW Keystone, fWo Cruiserweight, fWo Tag Team Wrestling Style Precision strikes and submissions with a high-flying move thrown in Finisher Move Step-up Shining Wizard Finisher Description Gentrification Special Moves Sleeping Dragon is a standing Shiranui DDT, Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon is a reverse DDT, Omniscience is a backflip kick.
