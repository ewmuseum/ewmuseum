ARC IV - Change 1st October 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Halifax Metro Centre, Halifax, Nova Scotia Card subject to change without notice INTRODUCTION The first show of the ACW Tour! Three Kings ACW World Champion? Check. ACW Tag Team Champions? Check. Beautiful, young manager? Check. Nerdy smark fanboy? Check. What’s not to love about that? Well, if you were the fans piled into Halifax Metro Centre, the answer would be LOTS. And the boos were loud and clear when Champion by Pastor Troy blasted through the speakers. That was the cue for the brand new ACW World Tag Team Champions to emerge from backstage. But the first thing through was a golf cart driven by Marcus the Smark, who was wearing the biggest grin the world had ever seen, and sitting shotgun was the hot and sexy manager of the Heirs of Wrestling, Alexandria Malone. Coming behind them, being pulled by the golf cart, were Frank Pierce and Ryan Galway, encased in two bullet-proof bubbles. They waved to the fans, who booed them mercilessly. Both Frank and Ryan held hands up to their ears as if they couldn’t hear the jeers, which caused them to grow louder. The music transformed into Amazing by Kanye West and being pulled in behind the Heirs was none other than that piece of shit, Max Danger, in a slightly higher-elevated Pope Mobile, sitting upon a posh throne, waving to the people like he was Ms. Winner of the Local County Fair Pageant in the local parade. The boos for the Heirs? They were nothing compared to the ire the Danger Man drew from the fans. Plastic cups, foam fingers, insults, middle fingers, EVERYTHING was thrown at Danger and they all just bounced off the glass bubble surrounding him. A lot of extra ringside space was evident this week, it was fortunate that they had left ACW Arena, ‘cause this massive vehicle wouldn’t have made it inside. But as it were, Marcus drove the thing around the ring to get it in position to drive right out when it was time for them to go. And for the fans, that time was right now. They would rather not have to here any of these men speak. But one-by-one they all emerged from their protective shells and climbed into the ring. Standing side by side, the three men stood, displaying their championship belts around their waist, as Alexandria and Marcus clapped behind them. The fans just shouted, “YOU SUCK!” and variations on that. Marcus then held a microphone and into it he said, “Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce to you the greatest collection of wrestling awesomeness ever assembled in one ring, The Three Kings!” Boos, again, rang out as all three men bowed for the masses. Handing the mic over to “The Baron of Ballistics” Frank Pierce, the bigger half of the Heirs of Wrestling tandem surveys the crowd, looking out to a sea of jeering Canadians. Scoffing at their hatred, Pierce chuckles as he stands on an apron. “ART THOU READY?!” BOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! The take on the famous phrase sent the crowd into a fit, but not half as bad as what was going to happen next. “NO! I SAYETH, ART. THOU. READY?!” The hatred came even harder as Pierce smiled and handed the mic to Ryan Gallway. The self-proclaimed Descendant of the Heaven and Stars looked out to the crowd and laughed, dressed in a very gaudy European shirt along with some weird jeans he may have picked up over in Japan. The eurotrash gestured towards his… uh… unmentionables and laughed. “You silly Francophone fucks!” he began. “What you see before you is the greatest tandem of talent that’s ever assembled under one burning sun! Why would I EVER accept Brian “Just One More Needle” Spaes as my trainer when this man…” He gestured to Max, who was still adoring his reflection in the ACW Championship. “…Our Lord and Savior, took us under his wing to show us what REAL greatness is? We are not just heirs apparent to a throne that none of you stupid pretenders deserve! After we ran The Unknown Elements and those Sons of Anarchy Brody Brother dicks right on out of here, we showed the First Airbourne that they can’t win when it counts… story of Andy Sharp’s life.” BOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Handing the mic to Alexandria Malone, the catcalls came out in full force. The former two-time tSC Champion let off a sarcastic laugh under her breath before she began. “Opportunities. That’s really what this business comes down to. By aligning with Max Danger, what we’ve done is not only create a way to get our own names out there, but to show you people what true talent is! I promise to you, ACW won’t be the only place out there that knows what we Heirs of Wrestling can do. Rest assured as not only ACW AND tSC Tag Team Champions, we’ll show each and every one of you who the greatest team of all time is. Hollywood Wrecking Crew, Blitzkrieg Funk, Vox Nihili, Legion of Guys Named After Milk… WE are the greatest tandem of all time. Max, take us home!” Marcus the Smark walked in front of the Danger Man and dropped to one knee while lowering his head and holding his arms up to hand Max the microphone. Danger took it from the fanboy and spoke into it, ignoring the boos. “Thank you for people nearly as amazing as I am. It’s truly an honor to be in this ring with actual peers.” Danger rubbed under his eye as if he were wiping away a tear. “YOU’RE FULL OF SHIT!” one man yelled from his seat somewhere out in the dark abyss that was the audience. “Sigh,” was all the Danger Man could say, ‘cause really, “What is there left to say.” He said that too. “My comrades and I have bested every challenge laid out before us. We know what they’ve done, but I, myself, have sent Kelly Masters to find some other venture he can quit halfway through, Frank Dylan James, I dunno, he may have to forgotten to come back up here or had his passport revoked. I put an end to the quote/unquote revolution and broke poor Andy’s neck -- “AN-DY! AN-DY” -- and now I think he’s going to team up with his brother as the Neckbraces or Team Cripple or something like that.” BOOOOOOOOO! “So, who’s left out there to challenge me? Alias? He’s so gullible, I could probably talk him into being our court jester.” The fans booed and chanted for Alias. Max ignored it. “So, I ask you fair people, who’s left? Trevor Wilson? HA! Maybe he has a title shot due him, but he couldn’t even properly defend the Spirt of ACW where he got to make up the rules! Hey, here’s an idea for you Trev, you should have made the match immediately after the number one contender’s match. You NEVER give your opponents a chance to recover! Amateur.” Danger scoffed and shook his head. The Danger Man moved around the ring a little bit, taking a moment to wink at Alexandria, who rolled her eyes. See, back in Action! Wrestling, Alexandria came around a couple of shows and had a bit of a thing for the Amazing One. That little crush was long gone but Max? He thought she still dug him. But at any rate, Danger stopped and leaned against the ropes, looking towards the entrance. “Now, I know the Heirs have a tag title match tonight against Trevor’s lackeys, but that’s not anything I’m even remotely worried about, and neither are they, so I seem to have some free time on my hands. SO, if there’s anyone back there that feels they can actually give me a challenge, then I’m willing to hear your offer.” Danger stood there a moment. “Anyone? Bueller? Bueller?” GROUND CONTROL, WE HAVE LIFT-OFF IN FIVE… FOUR…. THREE… TWO… ONE… At just that moment, Touch The Sky by Kanye West blasted through the speakers, and to the delight of everyone, Mach 2 emerged from backstage. The Danger Man mouthed, “seriously!?” as he turned around and chatted with the Heirs. “MAX!” Decked out in his wrestling attire, Mach 2 pointed a finger out to the ring. The Supersonic Kid hadn’t forgotten one iota about how the ACW Tag Team Titles slipped through their grasp and it appeared he would remind them. Danger didn’t looked too concerned, but Mach 2 wasn’t here to impress. “Man, when we opened this show up, I didn’t know we’d have another flood. You Three Kings? Let’s call a spade a spade. You’re nothing more than Three Bitches… I’m sorry, four. HI, ALEXANDRIA!” The crowd roared in applause as Alexandria turned to her compatriots and mouthed, “I’m not a bitch, am I?” before going back to Mach 2. Danger didn’t appreciate these rude comments one bit. “You listen he…” “NO, YOU SHUT THE FUCK UP!” The fans were loving every second of this. This was a backbone that Mach 2 had not shown off since he’d been to ACW. He didn’t care that he was only a 5’7” cruiserweight mouthing off against an entire stable, he would be heard. “I got news for you guys… Andy’s doctors said that despite taking that sick drop on the outside, he only suffered a stinger. He’s at home tonight, BUT… he’ll be back NEXT WEEK and he’s challenging that stupid ass, Frank Pierce, to a match!” Nodding that he’d take him on, Pierce waved both the ACW and tSC Tag Team Titles for all to see, but Mach 2 continued. The crowd lost it as he turned his attention to Danger. “As for you… you want competition? Consider it done. Maybe after you decided to come out and steal our Tag Team Titles… maybe I’ll just take your title tonight, Max!” “OOOH!” Danger chortled underneath his breath. “Silly kid. It’s non-title, of course. You’ve done nothing to garner a shot at this belt, but I’ll humor you and take your challenge. Andy… if he can even pick himself up by next week… will suffer the same fate that you will tonight. I accept. Now, play my BETTER Kanye West music, production monkeys!” Amazing by Kanye West blasted for all to hear as Mach 2 stared on with intent before disappearing behind the curtain Despite the fact that he was indeed alone this evening, he had a HUGE opportunity for payback tonight and if he had his way, he'd make the most of it. ACW Debut Match Rory Hayes vs. John Sarsgaard Already in the ring, perennial Scorpion Title contender and all-around asshole, Rory Hayes, sat in the nearest corner, not moving. To say that he’d had a very rocky start in All-Star Championship Wrestling would be nothing short of an understatement. He’d heard one of the very first All-Star Academy students would be joining ACW on the road as they begin their tour and the rumor was he wanted in on that action. “For Boston” by the Dropkick Murphys was blasting overhead, signifying his appearance in the ring. “Ladies and gentlemen,” began Tommy Vale as the crowd started a light round of jeering. “The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Already in the ring, from Boston, Mass, weighing in at two-hundred fifty-one pounds… RORY HAYES!!” Getting little more from the crowd than a round of jeers for his trouble, The Boston Mangler paid them no mind as his music faded out. “Point To Prove” by Theory of a Deadman. Parting the curtains was a rocker-looking brown-haired Caucasian male. With brown hair that ran to his shoulders, sporting various facial piercings as well as a black sleeveless shirt, and jean shorts for ring attire, the newcomer to All-Star Championship Wrestling smiled out to the crowd. He handed out some high-fived some fans, taking in a nice ovation from the crowd. “And his opponent, from Chicago, Illinois, weighing in at two-hundred fifty pounds, making his All-Star Championship Wrestling debut… JOHN SARSGAARD! The very first student of the new All-Star Academy, it was clear that Sarsgaard was going to be a force to reckoned with in the realm of ACW. Six-foot-five and just about two and a half bills, he was a big guy. A three-year pro before he headed down to the ring, John made the crossover to wrestling just over a year ago and had been working the independent scene for some time. A former practitioner of both amateur wrestling and amateur boxing at an early age, John wanted to make a good first impression and he’d have his chance to set the pace for he and his fellow students. Both combatants circled up in the middle of the ring. While John clearly had the size advantage, Rory Hayes weighed about the same and packed a little more power in his more compact frame. This would be an interesting fight. DING DING! The two men locked up immediately, and Sarsgaard went to work by rolling the veteran with a big arm drag! Taken aback by the quick moves of the big student, Hayes fumed to himself silently before running for another assault. Johnw as quick to take the Scorpion wrestler down with a big drop toe hold, then turning it right into a headlock. Hayes got back to his feet and shoved Sarsgaard hard into the ropes. Hayes, not taking kindly to this insult, quickly bum rushed Sarsgaard and sent the rookie flying into the buckles. Hayes finished it off with a big knee to the chest before turning his attention to the jeering crowd. “THIS IS WHAT YOU THINK OF THE NEW GUY, HUH?!” Spitting a gob of loogie at Sarsgaard’s feet, Hayes snickered, but he’d taken his eye off the ball too long when a DEVASTATING right caught him flush across the jaw, sending Hayes scrambling down to the mat. Hayes stumbled up in a daze and found himself on the bad end of several more jabs to the temple before getting whipped into the corner by the timekeeper’s table. As Paige Allen kept her count up, Hayes rolled in and out of the ring to reset it so he could prolong the rookie’s punishment.
