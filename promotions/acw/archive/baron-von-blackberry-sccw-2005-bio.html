Baron Von Blackberry Biography Real Name A 'real' name, you ask? Fool! The Baron partakes in FAR too much science to worry about a 'real' name! And trust me when I tell YOU that my real name is a closely guarded secret! At least, my first name is. But just for you, I will let you in on this secret for scientific reasons. My REAL name is... George. Yes. Okay, not really. Infidels. You'll believe anything I tell you, won't you? Nicknames The Devil Fruit Hometown The Scenic, Yet DIABOLICAL Nation of Fruitsylvania Birthday He was born at a time when men were men, women made sandwiches, and robots killed you for even BLINKING! Place of Birth The Nationally Diabolical Hospital (and Racketball Court) of Fruitsylvania Height 6' Weight 224 Blood Type Blood means NOTHING in Fruitsylvania! The only thing that matters is the oil you put in your robots and how much deathly your robots' death beams are! Eye Color Blue Biography The 'diabolical' dictator who rules over all he surveys with a iron, yet fruity fist. He comes from the highlands of Fruitsylvania, claiming to rule an entire empire with science. He doesn't, really. But don't tell him that. His feelings are delicate and fragile, even if he does claim to be the 'most diabolical fruit of them all'. Either way, if you don't consider the man who refers to himself as "the Devil Fruit" as completely insane, you yourself may be nuts. Making his debut in Sin City Championship Wrestling in both blackberry-themed mask and labcoat, Baron von Blackberry became infamous for his insane antics and immaculate skill in the ring - he won two championships with the promotion before succumbing to injury at the hands of Terrence Kingsley. Blackberry is as insane as he is talented, however, and one could consider him to be a "loose cannon" amongst the ranks of the Brotherhood of Awesome. Can he be trusted to get the job done when it matters the most? Other Feds SCCW Other Fed Titles SCCW Gateway Champion, SCCW Livewire Champion Other Fed Awards He stole a Wrestler of the Year award from the future once. Does that count? Past Managers The Baron has no need for such trifling things as a 'manager'! To accept help of that kind is to admit that he is not perfect! And we can't have that in the Blackberry regime of the future, now can we? Team The Brotherhood of Awesome Wrestling Style Fruitsylvanian Scientific Strong Style (All-arounder) Finisher Move 1) Blackberry Storm, 2) Blackberry Curve, 3) Blackberry Bold Finisher Description 1) Legsweep DDT, 2) Horns of Aries/Last Chancery, 3) Frog Splash Special Moves - Retcon Punch (a punch that, should it connect, has any variety of utterly insane and reality-distorting effects - his most legendary and dreaded move!) - Royal Butterfly Stretch (double underhook with bodyscissors) - Fruitsylvanian Decree (Inverted stomp facebreaker; Chuck Taylor's Sole Food) European uppercut, superkick, belly-to-belly suplex, leg lariat, flying knee strike, blind springboard hurricanrana, run-up super hurricanrana, swinging neckbreaker while the opponent is trapped between the top and second ropes, running double knee in the corner followed by a monkey flip, corner handstand into a mule kick, STO, pendulum backbreaker, running dropkick in the corner, anklelock, Cattle Mutilation, seated surfboard with headscissors, Mexican Surfboard Entrance Music 'Moskau' by Dschinghis Khan Trainer The Baron would have you believe that he is ENTIRELY self-taught. And by that, he means that he actually went back in time to train himself, thus creating a horrible time-loop. See what happens when you mess with time travel, kids? Manager Miranda O'Reily
