World.Wide.Wrestlecide 005, 16 December 2010

A C W THERE WILL BE BLOOD WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 005 ¤ 16 DECEMBER 2010 VAILLANT PALANCE, GENOA, ITALY Once the cue was given, the cameras started rolling and WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 005 began beaming to thousands of fans online! The pyrotechnics were of the highest order as about 5,200 fans welcomed the ACW roster to Genoa, one week removed from the epic COURAGE 130. The citizens of Genoa served up a hell of an atmosphere in the Vaillant Palace, simply salivating at the line-up of matches that lay ahead in the evening. # - And the first match of the evening would be a real doozy for the fans. By doozy, I meant the opposite, because none other than Aleczander was involved. The fans groaned as The Ultimate Survivor himself strolled out from the back, ranting and raving at everyone in sight. He hadn't been spotted since being disassembled like a toy by Omega on COURAGE 129, but he was back and raring to go. His opponent? Selby O'Brien, of The SOBs. Aleczander stopped annoying the masses and celebrated. It was Selby's brother, Sheamus O'Brien, that was the man that The Ultimate Survivor beat to win his debut match. No wonder Aleczander was such in a jovial mood. But there was a difference now: The SOBs had recently become Cabalites, and even if Sheamus wasn't all too hot about that career move, it was proving to be quite the smart decision. Selby was particulary excited by the new chapter in his life, and he really took it to Aleczander in the opening moments of this match. With Sheamus watching on intently on the outside, Aleczander and Selby went to town on each other. Both men appeared evenly matched, which didn't suit the fans since they really didn't care for either combatant. It came to pass that the fans started chanting for referee Paige Allen (not at all surprising seeing how she was bending over a lot), which made Aleczander mad. That only helped Selby O'Brien to build an advantage over Aleczander. The Anglo-American Idol was able to, however, counter a suplex attempt into a tossing flapjack, positioning Aleczander on the verge of victory. Unfortunately for Aleczander, Selby O'Brien was able to recover and counter his opponent's Big Brother Bomb finisher into a victory roll... and a handful of tights for good measure! One, two, three; Selby stole the win, his cheatery not detected at all by referee Paige Allen. Aleczander was beside himself and demanded for an immediate rematch. What he got was Sheamus -- impressed by his brother's sneakiness in earning the win -- rushing into the squared circle and kicking The Anglo-American Idol in the nuts. Selby didn't appear all too glad at that, but he had to be content with the victory. A nice addition to their scrapbook especially in terms of following up from the previous edition of WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE, where The SOBs had defeated Big E. Smalls. In contrast, it was most definitely not a good night for Aleczander. One had to think what lay ahead for Aleczander... but the sight of him writhing on the canvas, cupping his crotch, accomplished something: it made the fans very happy. Imagine that. Backstage, a gentleman wearing a funny-looking suit was wandering around listlessly. He also wore a brandless cap to go with the suit, for whatever reason. Upon noticing that the camera-man was filming him, the man suddenly shaped up and removed his cap. "Hello, all! I'm Dirk Dickwood and I shall be representing somebody in ACW very soon, for I am an astounding agent!" the man known as Dirk Dickwood announced, before rather abruptly getting down and busting out a breakdancing routine. Unfortunately for Dirk, he wasn't very good at it and garnered a lot of stares from the crew members that had gathered to watch. Snorting in anger at the non-appreciation, Dickwood got up and dusted himself off, prior to storming off in a fit of rage. Nobody watching on the SlyTron quite knew what to make of that, so they just jeered a little and waiting for something else to happen. # - That something else would turn out to be a match. Valley Baptiste was already in the ring for the night's second contest, and he was warming up for what was sure to be an incredibly tough battle. After all, his opponent was the Goddess Of Fight. She who had competed on all four previous editions of WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE and had yet to lose. Yes, it was the one and only Fejona Min. Min walked out briskly to the squared circle, probably thinking of her ongoing emotional war with Christopher Sheffield. Still, it didn't hinder her ability to evade Valley charging at her with a flying cross body block. On the contrary; that was the jumpstart the Rogue Slayer needed to bring herself to the present, and she unleashed a barrage of kickity kicks that she was famed for. Valley was able to recover nicely and surprise Fejona with a couple of flash pin attempts, giving fans hope that a major upset was on the cards. Unfortunately, Valley Baptiste's resolute fight was quickly dashed when Fejona Min busted out a sunset flip powerbomb upon being whipped into the corner turnbuckle with authority. Immediately thereafter, Fejona subjected Valley to her deadly ankle lock submission. Valley tapped out quickly, to nobody's surprise and to great dismay from the fans. The Rogue Slayer smirked at the crowd giving her grief for the victory, and she wasted no time in exiting ringside. Fejona went so far as to confiscate a poster of Christopher Sheffield from a young fan and rip it to shreds, sending the aforementioned young fan into a crying fit. Needless to say, Min's vendetta with the Original Pulp Hero was far from over... but when would it end? Outside the arena, a certain Caped Crusader was loitering about with one hand clutching a lit cigar and the other hand fussing about with his infamous BlackBerry mobile phone. Indeed, it was Z -- the man with a plan. An individual who was running riot over ACW with his doomsday clock that many figured now had something to do with the ACW World Title considering his obsession with one John Sarsgaard and the latter's shot at the World Championship that he'd earned in the All-Star Academy one year ago. Z was extremely invested with whatever the text message he was composing, but he immediately responded to Andy Sharp stepping out of the shadows and approaching him. Are they all here?" Z asked, before taking a drag of his cigar. Sharp zipped up his jacket all the way to the neck and nodded once. "Yes. I can't believe you pulled it off without Bishop or SilverHAWK finding out anything. A tip of the angler's cap to you, good man." Mr. All-Star and the Masked Enigma shared a hearty laugh, prior to the two men walking through the back door of the building. Nobody had a clue what Andy Sharp was referring to, but since Z was involved... there wasn't much optimism that he was up to anything honourable. # - Last week, Malk Al-Haq and Iceman saw their opportunity at qualifying for the 2010 End Game Match AND earn a shot at the ACW [Black] Scorpion Title go up in smoke thanks to Oso 'Bear' Bannon. Tonight, they would get one last chance to right a wrong, in this tag team match. The winners of the match would go on to duel in a special IN THE DARK~! match-up right before END GAME 2010, to determine just which individual would advance to the 2010 End Game Match. A lot was on the line here. Saleem Nagi made up the first half of the opposing team. Malk and Iceman, already in the ring, laughed at the newcomer. There was no way anybody who would end up being Saleem's partner would prove to be a threat. Until, of course, the Colossus himself walked out much to everybody's surprise. And everybody included Saleem Nagi himself. Who was the Colossus? None other than... Spike Saunders. Y'know, of The Entourage? Former ACW Tag Team Champion, amongst other plaudits. Yes, Spike Saunders had returned to ACW after losing at IN ACROPOLIS 2010, and both Malk & Iceman began to bicker over the fact that a supposedly easy task had turned out to be a mammoth exercise. Some pun intended there. Saunders shook Saleem's hand and engaged the newcomer with some strategy, before charging into the ring. Saleem was not far behind, and in an instant, the ring was filled with all four combatants going crazy. Limbs were flying and tempers were flaring. Shockingly, Malk had actually taken Spike out of the ring with a fallaway slam, leaving Saleem and Iceman alone in the ring. But when the Babylonian Overlord exited the ring to pile on the punishment, Spike Saunders overwhelmed Malk with a pop-up forearm smash. And followed up by lifting the Babylonian Overlord over his head and tossing him over the security barricade, into the laps of some beleagured fans! Iceman, who had up until that point been controlling the battle with Saleem, was outraged at his partner being taken out in such a shocking manner. That allowed Saleem Nagi to recover and surprise Iceman with a STO legsweep out of nowhere! Saleem then followed that up with his extremely brutal Downfall finisher. The three-count came next and just like that, it was all over. Saleem Nagi and Spike Saunders had won. Post-match, the newcomer and the returning Colossus shook hands once more. The first step had been accomplished; now, the two would meet in a few short weeks for a spot in the 2010 End Game Match. Saunders saluted the fans and left Nagi to prepare for a gigantic challenge. What of Iceman and Malk? They hurriedly hobbled from ringside through the crowd, barking at each other at what awaited them once they were due to meet up with their leader Z. Backstage, decked out in his most casual wear topped off with a swank leather jacket, 'The One' Jimmy Reid was seen barking at random crew members. All he got back in return were disgusted grunts. Noticing that the cameras were on him, Jimmy decided to indugled everybody in a bit of exposition. "Yes, it's true. I'm in cahoots with Z. SHOCKING. Not really, actually... considering that I've been pulling the strings for this whole operation since ACW's MAGNIFICIENT return to prominence in March 2009. Bah, humbug! Soon, the night of anguish will envelope you all, and Z will make everybody kneel down in absolute terror! Just you all wait." With a haughty chortle, Jimmy Reid flipped the ol' double one-finger salute and kicked over a trash can. Clearly, he was in some sort of mood. Or maybe, this was just him being him, and therefore all new to us modern fans not privy to The One's incredible and historic body of work back in the 1990s? Whatever the case was, Jimmy Reid had just dropped a bombshell: he had just laid claim to being the man behind Z's iron curtain. Wow. Talk about immense. # - Once the ringside area had been cleared and touched up (in record time), all that beckoned were the combatants in this netx tag team match to make their presence felt. Imagine the reaction of the fans when Vance Bootlier and Dolf Biggler walked out together. Vance and Dolf were both relatively recent hires to ACW, but that didn't stop both men from establishing themselves as rising douchebags. Dolf because of the annoying way he did everything, especially talk. Vance because of his controversial views. Perhaps this was a union made in heaven. Or perhaps that was the next tandem: the crowd simply exploded for Carrachio Salfuego and Quintana Rogerio Santiago! The latter had earned an opportunity to compete in the forthcoming 2010 End Game Match, while the former was preparing himself for a showdown with his rival in 'The Two' Booster Reid. Both men then saw this match as a warm-up of sorts, and capitalised on their opponents not being able to co-exist by springing a double dropkick! The bell had not even rung, and already Carrachio and Quintana -- who have teamed together a handful of times in the past -- were standing tall. Once order was restored, Vance took charge for his tandem and brought the fight to Carrachio. The Madrid Boy had seen himself become an unexpected hit with the fans over the months, and showed just why he was proving to be a commodity, easily dominating Vance Bootler and frustrating the Controversial Loudmouth to no end. Even when Bootlier was able to string a number of offensive moves together, it was a blind tag from his partner Dolf that proved to be his undoing; another round of bickering between Vance and Dolf was interrupted by Carrachio sneaking up from behind and dropsaulting both men into oblivion! The tag was then made to Quintana Rogerio Santiago, who relished the chance to get his hands dirty and made quick work of the still-reeling Dolf Biggler. Biggler was able to prevent himself from being completely overrun, via an eye-rake here and a closed fist there. It was all legal in the eyes of referee Roderick Lynch, who probably saw a bit of his old self in Biggler. In any case, Biggler's luck would run out. Just as he'd gained an advantage over QRS, the masked luchador would suddenly leapfrog over Dolf and make the tag to Carrachio, who then hit a pinpoint missle dropkick off the top of the turnbuckle! Scintillating. Vance Bootlier, by that time, was protesting loudly to get back into the ring. Carrachio had a better idea. He tagged his partner back in, before rushing over and knocking Vance off his perch on the apron. Quintana Rogerio Santiago gathered his breath and laid Biggler out with his bridging dragon suplex finisher. One, two, three; the win was secured, and the fans were left celebrating happily. Backstage, Joe Bishop -- ACW's Production Manager and confirmed Hall Of Famer -- was standing by with a pre-prepared statement. He addressed the fans on SilverHAWK's condition, stating for the first time that the General Manager was doing somewhat better but nonetheless still deep within a medically-induced coma. HAWK would out for a long while, Bishop confirmed, due to the heinous acts inflicted upon him by first Z and then God's Forgotten Son at COURAGE 130... but the Architect would eventually be on the road to recovery. Bishop then turned feral, threatening God's Forgotten Son with bodily harm if SilverHAWK's condition worsened. On that note, Bishop segued into his next point. "It seems as if we have one more remaining slot to give away in the upcoming 2010 End Game Match, since one of our intended surprise guests has declined participants!" Bishop mused, calming down just a bit. ACW's Inside-Out Man proceeded to announce that at END GAME 2010, Aleczander would take on The SOBs in a 2-On-1 Handicapped Match. The winner would earn entry into the 2010 End Game Match. And if The SOBs won, it'd be the brother that secured the pinfall that would be eligible to compete in the End Game Match. Pretty big announcement by Bishop there. At that very moment, Aleczander hobbled by in what appeared to be a medical dressing grown, and swore on the grave of his old Playstation 2 console that he would be victorious on all counts at END GAME 2010. Satisfied with his own self-hype, Aleczander hobbled away, leaving Joe Bishop to shake his head in despair. "Why did we ever hire that guy? Oh, wait, Hunt did. Makes sense now!" Bishop lamented to nobody in particular. # - The fans had seen quite the spectacle so far with some of ACW's future best and brightest. But the next match was going to be a battle of old versus new, so to speak. The German National Anthem played none other than Ulf German to the ring, the beast getting something of a tepid response. One who was once a feared prodigy of the squared circle had been exposed, humbled, and humiliated by 'The Darwinist' Dean Denton and had his own partner, Der Deutscher Soldat, turn against him for promises of power and fame within the cabal. Further to that, Ulf was destroyed by Omega and his boundless rage on the most recent episode of COURAGE just seven days prior. But now was time to regroup and he could start with a win tonight. Glory by Sugar Ray played over the arena's speakers next, making room for the debut of one of ACW's newest hopefuls. A masked man (!) saluted the crowd with many thumbs up, grinning the entire way. His name was D.I.M. -- or rather, the Dude In Mask. Yes, rather unique name, wasn't it? Gaining a nice reception as he was announced from Italy, he rolled into the ring and ran laps around Ulf German, preening and posturing for the crowd. It appeared he was having a grand old time, but this would be a good test for the masked man. D.I.M. hit the ground running as the bell rang, dizzying Ulf with a series of absurdly fast arm-drags and headscissors takeovers. Ulf was unable to really get out of the starting blocks as D.I.M. took the fight to him, firing off a dropkick that sent German to the outside. D.I.M. measured him up from the top rope, moonsaulting from the top rope to the floor! G etting a great reaction, the Dude In Mask rallied the fans as he threw more fists at Ulf. Ulf reversed a dive off the apron and sent his hopeful opponent crashing into the barricade! What happened next was three of the most boring minutes anybody had seen. Ulf -- still not really himself -- took to D.I.M. with some rather plodding, but effective offense, bodyslamming him a few times and Reverse DDTing him halfway to China. A close fall off the DDT and another off a powerbomb had Ulf upset. He got him in a fireman's carry, but D.I.M. slipped out the back, going back to the high-risk offense. He caught him with a Sweet-Ass Dropkick and followed up with Dude, That Hurt (i.e. a Running Pump Kick) for a near-fall. D.I.M. went up top and tried for a move, but Ulf cut him off at the pass. He tried for a fireman's carry again, looking for the Effeiciency off the top, but D.I.M. slipped out and BIT Ulf on the forehead, sending him down to the ground. With a military salute to the crowd, he soared off the top like a missile and crashed down with the Air-to-Dude Missile (a spectacular Soaring Frog Splash)! A quick three-count later, and D.I.M. was victorious in his very first outing. As for Ulf German, he had to suck his thumbs and come to terms with yet another crushing blow to his morale. It wasn't often that people saw Jenna McMullen, ACW's premiere backstage interviewer, dressed in her casual-wear. But there she was, strolling backstage aimlessly, fiddling about with her iPhone. Out of nowhere, three rather portly gentlemen appeared and scared her panties-less. Jenna shrieked and dropped her iPhone... only for one of the three portly gentlemen to rescue it dramaticallyDonatello, Michaelangelo and Leonardo. Formerly the Feared Ninja Assassins back in '04, who had returned on COURAGE 130 thanks to their eventul meeting with Jeremy Hunt. Which didn't turn out so well for them after Jenna realised that, and more about the former Feared Ninja Assassins. While Jenna was relieved at the rescue of her iPhone, she was appalled at the sight of the three men she beat up a week prior. Leonardo quickly clarified the situation, claiming no ill will towards the lovely blonde for her attack last week. "We probably deserved it. Sorry for piling on bad memories on what was already a tough day for you!" Leonardo apologised. Donatello and Michaelangelo nodded, affirming Leonardo's words. Jenna accepted the apology, while still being wary of the three visitors. Donatello added that since they had no specific roles upon being signed by Jeremy Hunt and that the Italian crowd had taken to ACW programming really well, there was therefore the formation of a team of Italian Broadcast Correspondents. "Which is all us three!" Michaelangelo beamed, proud of the new role. Jenna appeared at ease at this news, which made Leonardo propose something: a dinner, paid for by himself and his two brothers. Jenna, being Jenna and having nothing to do, accepted. Needless to say, the former Feared Ninja Assassins were delighted at making thighs right with the blonde bombshell. # - Next up was a big ol' Six-Men Tag Team Match. The first team was captained by Fight Man, seconded by JP Boudreau and rounded up by 'Zen-Zational' Zhang Tao. The fans went wild for The Hardcore Hero, hooted and hollered respectably for the Montreal Frog and were nonplussed at The Beijing Zen Master standing around looking lost. All of that turned into jeering when the next team, aptly made up of three members of the cabal rushed out and attacked their opponents without warning. Seeing how the assigned referee for this match was Mark Shields, it was no surprise that Kati and Jack Harris and Kesavan were given cart blanche to pound their three opponents into kingdom come. Fight Man was at least able to mount something of a comeback against Kati, but The Pikey Madman and the Mongolian Witch-doctor Of Ultimate Doom were on hand to scramble Fight Man's brains with a double chokeslam on the outstide. Once they were done, Kati motioned for Harris and Kesavan to get their opponents into the ring while also commanding for bastard referee Mark Shields to ring the bell. One minute after, Kati scooped up the plaudtis of victory and put an end to a highway robbery. The four Cabalites stood tall in the middle of the ring, towering over the pile of broken bodies and the rubbish that had made its way into the ring. Kati stared out scornfully at the rioting masses, while Kesavan procured a microphone and said something that shook everybody to their core. "Z's coming into this ring later. And he's got a surprise for everybody." Clearly, it wasn't a good surprise. Or was it? No, probably not. # - The night's penultimate match had been advertised to the fans in the arena mere moments after they had began streaming into the building. No wonder the jeering remained an all-time high (a holdover from what transpired just five minutes prior with the cabal) when Abraham Swift and William T. Rex -- collectively known as Abilities or Hunt's Cunts in some quarters -- came strolling out from the back. They were flanked by Jeremy Hunt's bodyguard and the biggest man on the roster in WAR... as well as the #1 Contender to the ACW World Title, Khristain Keller. As expected, Keller still had the World Title in his possession. That jerk. K2 wasn't quite done being a mega-douche, to the surprise of nobody except his team-mates. He didn't even step into the ring for this Eight-Men Tag Team Match. No, Keller went right over to the Broadcast Booth and set himself down there. WAR watched on in clear disbelief at his colleague's decision not to join in the match, but there was no time for questioning. The fans going wild the moment Corrosion Of Conformity's Deliverance blared over the speakers heralded the arrival of KC Payne and Kassidy Payne; i.e. The Payne Gang! With them were Monte Young and Carl Kimball who were better known was Union O' MontCarl. Y'know, ACW's first-ever same sex married team? Yeah. Neither Kimball nor Young were in a mood to show each other a hint of affection, however; KC and Kassidy had filled the two newcomers on the evil deeds of the Huntsmen in the ring, and that meant a frantic opening to the match. Even as his compatriots were being beaten back by The Payne Gang and Union O' MontCarl, Khristain Keller refused to budge. It was probably a good thing for WAR and Abilities, then, that referee Lucas Nuckallin was finally able to take control of the match. WAR and Kassidy Payne were left to square off, but Kassidy displayed no fear in the face of WAR's wrath. Still, WAR was able to dominate Kassidy until he noticed Keller falling asleep at the Broadcast Booth. Kassidy was able to take advantage, and tag out to KC. That set off a chain reaction of hit-and-run attacks, involving every member of the 'good guys' team. Everybody got a chance to lay a beating on WAR, who tried to rally back and tag out for a breather but to no avail. Perhaps he was still feeling the effects of Maddox St. James' ONG BAK KNEES OF DEATH~!!! on COURAGE 130? If that was indeed the case, Khristain Keller didn't appear too concerned. In fact, he had completely fallen asleep. Abraham Swift and William T. Rex, on the other hand, were livid. Leave it to Abe to come up with a momentum changer, though; he drove a knee into the spine of Monte Young as the latter bounced off the ropes in a bid to take WAR down with a cross body splash. The rogue knee-strike went undetected, and WAR was able to destroy Monte with a power-slam before tagging out to Abe. Abraham very nearly paid for his cockiness as he entered the ring, for Monte caught him in a small package roll-up pin. Abe squirmed out at the death, but was unable to prevent Monte Young from tagging out to Kassidy Payne. Despite having been on a high due to his part in the unruly way COURAGE 130 concluded, Abraham Swift actually appeared a tiny bit fearful of Kassidy. Kassidy fed on the cheers of the fans and unloaded a can of whoop-arse on The Uncouth Senior Citizen... but Abe was able to save himself from getting overrun by an eye rake. With that momentary distraction, Abe was able to tag out to William T. Rex. The World Eater finally got his first official taste of action in the match; unfortunately, it turned out to be anti-climactic. Kassidy ducked underneath William's high kick attempt, and both men ended up clotheslining each other. With both combatants for each team down, it was a race to find out who would tag out first. It turned out to be a really close call... until Khristain Keller suddenly awoke at the Broadcast Booth and began ranting about 'kicking your skinny arse into oblivion'. One had to assume he was probably having a dream about Keith Scott Zimmerman; whatever the case was, that momentary distraction drew the referee's attention, and he was thusly unable to ascertain who tagged out first. That sparked off another round of chaos, which took place all over the ring and even at ringside. When the two legal men -- WAR and Carl Kimball -- finally got back into the ring, what followed was a rather unique turn of events. WAR ran roughshod over Kimball, and had the plucky trooper in his sights. An attempt at a double-handed choke-bomb went sour, though, when Kimball smartly drove his knee into WAR's injured chest! At that point, Khristain Keller quickly sprinted to the ring and hopped onto the apron, blind-tagging himself in. Carl Kimball's follow-up DDT attempt was countered into a release Northern Lights suplex, and Keller promptly stepped into the squared circle. Needless to say, WAR was not pleased at this turn of events. Keller laughed, shoo-ed WAR out of the ring and then proceeded to lay Kimball out with his PainKilla finisher. The three-count was academic, despite Monte Young's last-ditch effort to break up the count. WAR fumed on the apron at Keller hijacking the match at the last second after having spending virtually the entire match as a spectator. Once K2 realised there was nothing left to do, the Bastard King Of Ages wheeled out of the area with his head held high. WAR stuck around, barking about to nobody in particular in his native tongue, while eyeballing Monte Young checking up on his fallen partner/husband. What of Abilities and The Payne Gang? The two teams continued brawling relentlessly all over ringside, even after the bell rang for the end of the match. The bell only served as a signal for Abraham Swift to procure a lead pipe from under the ring and go to town on both Payne brothers with it! The shot to KC Payne wasn't clean, but it was enough to allow William T. Rex to follow up with a sickening blow in the form of a pulling piledriver on the exposed concrete. Abraham and his pipe showed Kassidy no mercy, and by the time Rex Silver and his team stepped in... it was much too late. Kassidy Payne lay in a pool of his own blood, his face mangled beyond repair. KC Payne lay comatose on the concrete just as EMTs came running out with stretchers. Abilities stood tall, arms raised and basking in the approval of WAR. A troubling sight for the fans, and the rest of the teams in the Four Ways To Glory Match upcoming at END GAME 2010. Backstage, an amublance had arrived on scene. Streams of EMTs were buzzing about, and Joe Bishop looked on rather bitterly as KC and Kassidy Payne were hurriedly escorted from the ringside area towards where the ambulance was parked. Once both Payne brothers were loaded into the back of the ambulance, Joe Bishop could be heard remarking that he now needed to find a replacement team for the Four Ways To Glory Match. At that moment, a random crew member walked up to Joe Bishop and informed the Production Manager that there was a situation that needed his attention. "What now?" the weary Inside-Out Man asked, clearly having a struggle temporarily filling in for his friend SilverHAWK. "I have no idea. Some lanky British guy told me to tell you to come out to the ring later, after the main event finishes. Said it had something to do with the All-Star Academy!" the random crew member explained. Joe Bishop shook his head, eyes widened. "Christ. This can't be good." # - It was finally time for the night's main event. And it would prove to be an interesting one; despite the so-called understanding between Jeremy Hunt's band of warriors (i.e. The Huntsmen) and Z's tribe of terrorist rebels (i.e. the cabals) at the conclusion of COURAGE 130, two individuals from each villainous faction would do battle. Much to the crowd's chagrin, of course. God's Forgotten Son was out first, to an unhealthy amount of jeers. The man responsible for sending SilverHAWK to the hospital had to be escorted out by Dean Denton's Atheist Militia, but there was no trace of emotion on his face. GFS rolled into the ring and hoisted the Spirit Of ACW Championship up above his head, only to get pelted by a ton of trash. A fitting reception. Without warning, GFS's opponent for the night came marching out sans any theme music; it was Der Deutscher Soldat, the burly German who had joined Z's gang not too long ago. Not that he had a choice in that matter, for those who remembered just how those sequence of events unfolded. As it was, Soldat swiftly rolled into the ring and quickly charged at GFS, taking down the Spirit Of ACW. God's Forgotten Son may have been a touch pre-occupied with thoughts of just who he was going to face at END GAME 2010 for the fate of ACW, and thus Soldat was able to take him to school with his arsenal of power moves. However, GFS wasn't a former United States Champion and the 2004 King Of Ages for nothing; rolling out of the ring to grab a breather drew Der Deutscher Soldat to give chase. Just as God's Forgotten Son intended. GFS was able to catch Soldat off-guard with a drop-toe-hold. Soldat's face bounced off the protective mats, and the reigning Spirit Of ACW followed up with a springboard knee-drop off of the ring apron. Thereafter, GFS tossed Der Deutscher Soldat back into the ring and knocked him out with a running DDT. The three-count was never in doubt. Oh, wait. Actually, it was. Thanks to Soldat managing to get his foot on the ropes. The reigning Spirit Of ACW was flustered for a moment, but shook it off to go right back to work. With every offensive move that GFS threw at Soldat after that, however, it appeared to many that DDS was soaking it up and dishing out punishment of his own. Pretty soon, any advantage that God's Forgotten Son thought he had had evaporated and the two men were brawling in the middle of the ring. The fans were mesmerised by the tough fight that was unfurling in the ring, despite their hatred for GFS and their relative indifference to DDS. Surprisingly enough, Soldat's unwavering resistance and eventual turning of the tide frustrated God's Forgotten Son greatly. Even to the point of the reigning Spirit Of ACW resorting to a mule kick to get out of a rear waistlock. That caught the eye of referee Li Mu Bai, who admonished GFS with a stern warning. Two minutes later: the bell rung, and referee Li Mu Bai awarded the disqualification victory to Der Deutscher Soldat. Why? After sliding out of a full nelson attempt by GFS, Soldat pushed the 2004 King Of Ages into the ropes and demolished him with a sickening lariat. Soldat's resultant pick-up attempt saw him get struck in the crown jewels again. The decision was easy for referee Li Mu Bai to make. God's Forgotten Son was enraged, but before he could do anything; the likes of Kati, Jack Harris and Kesavan jumped out from the crowd and stormed the ring. In response, Abilities came barrelling down the ramp and entered the squared circle just as Kati tended to Soldat. Two of the four teams in the Four Ways To Glory Match stared down each other, while GFS and Kati locked eyes as well. Truly, the understanding between Hunt's brood and Z's tribe was an uneasy one as far as the worker bees in each group were concerned. ... but the night was far from over. The appeareance of Kati, Jack Harris and Kesavan was merely the precursor, to the arrival of the Caped Crusader himself. That's right, Z was in the house, and he strolled down to ringside armed with a microphone and the rest of his tribe. Yes, even Blackie Hornberger and the drugged-up Pete Strombo. Yes, even Simon Blythe, the Agent To The Stars who had not been seen too much on-camera lately. No sight of referee Trent Savage though, surprisingly. Z quickly took his place in the ring while his charges surrounded the squared circle. Once the Huntsmen were allowed to leave the ringside area without incident, The Masked Enigma got right down to business and invited Joe Bishop to come out on stage. Bishop complied almost immediately, and demanded to know what Z was up to, especially since the Inside-Out Man was told that the All-Star Academy was a possible topic. Z laughed lustily. "Oh, Joe. You silly man; the All-Star Academy IS the topic. Or rather, the demise of it. Hh. Perhaps I should go back a bit, innit?" With Bishop suitably stunned and frightened, Z proceeded to tell a story. A story of how nobody in ACW, including SilverHAWK and Joe Bishop, had been wise to what had been happening in the Academy since the middle of September. The reason for that, as some knew, was because the All-Star Academy's chief correspondent -- Sally Broxton -- had been taken captive by Fejona Min during the Rogue Slayer's rivalry with Kristinia Kage. Since then, a replacement had not been hired. Z further clarified, stating that he had a team of IT geeks making sure no form of communication crossed the Atlantic Ocean ever since Sally Broxton was taken out of commission. Bishop asked what Z was driving at. Never one to turn down a moment to shock the fans, Z snapped his fingers and a horde of people emerged from the fans. Most found them unrecognisble, but Bishop and several other crew members that had come out to the stage were stunned. About 13 individuals in total, one of whom was Shade... Z's top lieutenant in the Academy and also apparently the new United States Champion, seeing how he was carrying the title belt. Z made it clearer for everyone. "The fans over in Yankland already know this, but it's time for you and the rest of this machine to find out: the All-Star Academy is no more. ICU, the Chief of the Academy, is now under my payroll. So is the rest of the organisation. Effective from the first of January 2011, I am the new OWNER of the brand-new All-Star Association! That's right, you heard correctly. No longer will these individuals, many of whom earmarked for future success in ACW, be considered as 'students' toiling away in an Academy. Which was obviously not deemed important enough by ACW management, seeing how you guys took your time trying to find a replacement for your correspondent position. I took that pussyfooting as my invitation to execute the final phase of my plan. All the donations I've made to the Academy since the beginning of the year? Seeding money. I care about these individuals. These men and women, whom you guys draft to make up the numbers to sustain the television deal that you have in place. I won't treat them like that. Starting next year, the All-Star Association -- the very inaugural property of he newly-formed ZCorp Industries -- will embark on its destiny on becoming the greatest independent professional wrestling promotion in North America. And by North America, I mean Hawaii. Imagine that. At any rate, you guys took your future for granted. Now, you HAVE no future. For all intents and purposes, the All-Star Academy is finished. The so-called dream ended last night, as a matter of fact. Yes, impressive how I got everybody out here across the pond in 24 hours, innit? Even more impressive was you idiots being so oblivious to what was happening, not even caring when news about the Academy stopped coming through on a daily basis. You shrugged it off and thought nothing of it. It's only the Academy after all, right? ICU and his team have it covered, right? Nothing can go wrong, right? And even if something does go wrong -- SO WHAT, right? WRONG. Say goodbye to your supply line of future All-Stars. Say goodbye to the All-Star brand as a whole. I've taken the Academy and made it my own. In almost no time at all, ACW itself will be reshaped in *my* image. And you will all only have yourselves to blame. You brought this on yourselves, with your ignorance. I merely did what I do best; capitalise on an opening. Tch. You fools have stepped it in now. Oh, and by the way, this is a good a time as any: One minute." With that blockbuster announcement, WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 005 ended with Z in the ring, surrounded by the cabal which included 13 individuals from the now-supposedly defunct All-Star Academy, staring defiantly at Joe Bishop. Who, as it was, had slumped to his knees and wore a mask of abject woe. He, like many others, was crestfallen to hear what had happened to the All-Star Academy. And they were also extremely terrified, because Z's doomsday clock had now inched ever closer to midnight. With all the mayhem swirling around in ACW, many were horrified at the prospect of what the Caped Crusader had in store for his grand reveal. Knowing Z, it would be suitably legendary.
