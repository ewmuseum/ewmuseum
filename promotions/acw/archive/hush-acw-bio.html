Hush Biography Real Name Alex Hughes (formerly Jeffrey Stone) Hometown Sherbrooke, Quebec, Canada Birthday 27th November 1980 Place of Birth Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Height 5'10 Weight 195 Blood Type A- Eye Color Olive green Biography The only thing that Jeffrey Stone ever wanted to do was wrestle. He grew up idolising such grapplers and Dusty Rhodes, deciding from a very early age that following in their footsteps was the only thing he wanted to do in life. So he drifted his way through school, shunning academic achievements in favour of athletic pursuits, and left with a less than impressive set of qualifications. His uncle, "Big" John Jacobs, played an instrumental role in Jeffrey's development as a professional wrestler. As the head booker and chief operator of Pittsburgh's Steel City Wrestling, John bled his son into the business slowly and steadily. He hooked him up with one of the region's top training academies and the contacts he'd gained from over three decades in the game helped secure the young Stone with a dearth of bookings across the country. With a quickly-expandingskill set and a growing reputation on the North American indy scene, Jeffrey Stone was well on his way to hitting the top. Unfortunately for Stone, things would soon take a turn for the worse. It was to be the biggest night of his career. He was set to take on elder cousin, Sid Jacobs, in an epic sixty minute iron man feud blow-off match. All 600 tickets had sold out weeks beforehand, and rumour had it that talent scouts from two of the country's biggest promotions were in the house. It was his moment. Little over twenty minutes had happened when it happened. Jeffrey lifted his cousin in the air, but he botched the powerbomb. Jacobs's neck snapped on impact and he was rushed to hospital immediately. He couldn't hold out. Three hours later, Sidney Jacobs was pronounced dead. A direct result of his cousin's botchedpowerbomb. Devasted by the death of his son, John Jacobs fired Jeffrey immediately and did his best to make sure that Stone would never wrestle on the East Coast again. He was infuriated; dead set on waging a war on Jeffrey Stone's career to avenge the premature death of his own son. But Jacobs wasn't the only one hurting; Sid Jacobs left behind a wife and three young children, left on their own to survive without an obvious source of income. Guilt-stricken, Jeffrey pledged to donate a sum of $4,000 to SelenaJacobs's bank account to make up for the catastrophic loss that he had inflicted on the family. At the cost of her pride, Sid's widow took the sum, knowing that she would not survive long without it. Effectively black-listed from every major independent fed in America, Jeffrey relocated to Japan. He needed to earn to provide for himself and to keep up with his payments to the former Mrs. Jacobs, and with his lack of qualifications and set of skills wrestling seemed like the only option. Over the years he developed his abilities and grew as a fighter, but it wasn't long before John Jacobs tracked Jeffrey down and spread news of his discrepancies around the Japanese scene. With only a few minuscule Japanese promotions willing to offer him work, Jeffrey Stone was running out of options. So he headed back to the United States to plead with Jacobs, but his efforts were rebuffed. So Jeffrey Stone became Alexander Hughes. He legally changed his name and relocated to Canada, hoping that John Jacobs wouldn't be able to find him. But that wasn't enough. Wrestling under a changed moniker would not suffice; the moment he showed his face inside a ring, Jacobs would find out about it. So he pulled on a mask. Jeffrey Stone became Alexander Hughes. Alexander Hughes became Hush. Now, competing under an alias and a mask, the former Stone eeks out a living as he struggles to keep up with his responsibilities so Sidney Jacobs' bereaved family and provide for himself in the process. With the threat of John Jacobs looming, his clouded past haunting every movement and his paycheck getting smaller and smaller, Alex Hughes's former dream job has turned into a living nightmare.
