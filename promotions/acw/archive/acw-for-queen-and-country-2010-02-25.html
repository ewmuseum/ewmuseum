Shawn Jessica Hart took it quickly with a grin. Reaching inside of the robe he pulled out something that brought jeers from London’s ACW faithful. The Legacy title. The last Legacy Champion pointed at the LoC with a wink, before swinging back, his eye on the back of the Original Pulp Hero’s head! The glint of the gold caught Alias’s wildly blinking eye however and he ducked out of the way, before rolling to the outside. Not happy with it ending just there and wanting to leave three letters printed across Alias’s forehead, Hart gave chase. The Pulp Hero roared out to the timekeeper, who held the ACW World Championship on his lap to throw him the belt. Without pause because, let’s admit he had a slight fear for his safety, he tossed Alias the World title. The Pulp Hero spun around as Hart was once again about to brain him with the Legacy title. Alias pushed the ACW World Championship out at the LoC Legacy title. The two golden titles clashed. SPARK The crowd gasped as a surprisingly sharp burst of sparks shot out from the polished metal on metal impact. The impact also caused both men to drop their titles in surprise. Equally surprised was Irwonsen, who spun around wondering what had caused the sound and light to burst behind him. Nothing but both men looking down at two titles, Alias grinning at a large scratch across the ACW of the World title as he shook his arm… and SJH gasping in horror as the face plate of the Legacy title seemed to have jarred loose from the belt. Bending over to save his baby, SJH was quickly admonished by Irwonsen from the ring before he could touch it. Felicia soon scampered over to pick it up (making sure to bend from the waist) and Irwonsen made sure to tell her and JKJ that if they tried anything else close to being funny… they’d both be tossed from ringside. Then he began to count-out the World Champion and his contender, the End Game winner. Both men ducked back into the ring. Hart… with a surprisingly fevered anger. Alias… to his feet first. However, the fact that our neighborhood Pulp Hero was heading significantly slower than before, as well as the fact that SJH was able to spot the attack attempt, led the Phenom to duck the lariat, and almost in one swift movement, connected with a side kick to the back of Alias’s knee. Alias sank to his knees, and SJH grabbed him by his throat, grounded chokeslamming Alias sideways onto his knee, creating a grounded bow and arrow-type of submission… while taking time out of this busy schedule to also punch at Alias’s shoulder in random intervals. Hart continued to wrap around the Pulp Champion's throat, and began to squeeze, prompting tremendous boos from the crowd. Once again Henry Irwonsen began a disqualification count, letting SJH know that after this he’d be out of a World title no questions asked, and at four, Hart let go, releasing Alias, who collapsed onto the mat, grabbing his further aggravated arm in agony while also feeling the pain in his shoulder and back. SJH lifted Alias up and put his head between his legs in a standing head scissors position. The fans exploded with jeers as SJH hooked his arms around the Champ’s waist and attempted to lifted him up, but exploded with cheers as Alias sank to his knees. Hart hurriedly released Alias from his standing head scissors position and quickly rammed the heel of his right boot into the back of Alias’s knee several times, causing Alias more pain to deal with before he drove an elbow down on the World Champion’s injured shoulder. SJH quickly put Alias into the standing head scissors position, and managed this time to flip Alias into the air. Hart spun around, rammed Alias's back into the ropes, and slingshotted off, completing a slingshot powerbomb pin, with the obvious intention of trying further to destroy Alias's back, to go with the arms, in the process. ONE! TWO! NO! SJH put his hands on his head, annoyed that he had not gotten the win then and there. Biting his lower lip, he mounted the Original Pulp Hero and rained down a plethora of hard-hitting shots to the face of his opponent, but Alias put his dukes up and managed to block a fair number of punches from connecting. This just made Shawn Hart more frustrated. The Phenom decided to get off of Alias and pull him up at the same time, before taking control of his arm and wrenching it inside out. Alias tightly closed his eyes and growled, but SJH kept up the pressure, applying a wristlock to further aggravate the injured arm of the World Champion. Aggravate him, he did. For when SJH went ahead and wringed the Pulp Hero's arm one more time, the latter snapped and directed a kick at Shawn's shin, before using his free arm to pick the Phenom up and spin him up & over, DRIVING him down onto the canvas with a mammoth modified powerslam. The ring shook from the thunderous impact, as the Wembley Arena crazies roared in approval. Picking himself up, Alias wiped the sweat from his forehead, before rumbling off the ropes and pulling off a sliding knee to the side of Hart’s head almost making the End Game winner bleed in the process, but hey; the greater good, right? With the turnaround complete, Alias hooked the legs and the referee did his job. UNO. DOS. TRE - NA NA NAAAAA. So close, yet so far. Awwww. The Pulp Her brought SJH to his feet with an angry sneer and Irish whipped him towards the ropes, only to have it reversed. Alias came off the ropes with a Lou Thesz press. The fans cheered in unison as the Pulp Hero took Hart down once more, finally taking the direction of the match fully to his advantage. He peppered Hart’s forehead with right hands and then hooked both legs for the cover. Hart powered out of it at two, before he tried to get up, Alias took him down by the left leg and started hammering away at it with kicks to the inside quad. SJH pushed him away with the other leg and managed to get to a vertical base. He threw a kick at Alias’s midsection, but Alias caught it and hit a dragon-screw leg whip. The Phenom fell to the floor in pain as Alias stomped away at his left knee. Alias brought Hart back to his vertical base, and then began to slowly circle him, and no sooner was Shawn fully upright than the Championship journeyman dove in and took him back to the mat with a successful double leg takedown. Alias jumped back to his feet, Hart’s knees still in tow, and he dragged his opponent to the center of the ring, where he grapevined Hart’s legs around his arms and attempted a Texas cloverleaf hold. Hart reacted quickly, sending a thumb into Alias’s good eye, to jeers from the crowd, when Alias attempted to turn him over, causing Alias to release the hold and stagger backwards. The Phenom got to his feet to continued jeers from the crowd and approached Alias, who immediately pulled the The Phenom into a collar-and-elbow tie-up. After jockeying for an advantage, Hart looked to break the hold, which gave Alias the opportunity to duck behind him and apply a rear waistlock. Hart executed a standing switch, leaving himself behind Alias and the Pulp Hero slightly off-balance. SJH looked to capitalize, attempting a German suplex, but Alias somehow managed to block the hold by sitting out and making his entire body dead weight. Hart struggled to pull Alias back up, and at the peak of his haul, Alias used his legs to power both men backwards. SJH struggled to remain on his feet, due in some part to the attack on his knee that Alias had started, which allowed the Original Pulp the opportunity for a quick go behind. With a momentary advantage he was determined not to squander, Alias immediately applied a full nelson. With Alias now focusing on Shawn’s shoulders, most likely in an attempt to neutralize his arms and keep him from utilizing much strength in his finishers not to mention to counteract the beating his own arms had take to give Alias a latter chance for the Anarchy’s Lullaby, and equalizing the pain that the Pulp Hero’s arm was in, Hart knew that he needed to escape and fast. However, when he yanked his arms outward to break the hold, Alias merely stepped forward, wrapping his right leg around Shawn Jessica Hart’s left and tripping him. Hart’s face bounced off of the canvas, opening a wound on his lip, but that was the least of his concerns, as he found Alias had freed his leg and moved into a seated position over his back. Effectively trapped, Hart could only struggle in the hold in hopes of loosening Alias’s fingers. When he reached backwards to help the process along, Alias simply headbutted his hands, sandwiching them between Alias’s own hands and his head and sending a powerful jolt of whiplash through Hart’s neck. Hearing SJH cry out in pain, Alias arched his back even further, putting even more pressure on Harts’s shoulders and back, while keeping the growing pressure off of his own pained shoulder. Just as suddenly, however, Alias stopped. Moving with speed and precision so as not to lose his hold, Alias pulled his right arm free of Hart’s and quickly drove it hard into the back of SJH’s neck before locking in an amateur move of all things, an awkward and painful looking half nelson. While wrenching backwards with his left arm, Alias bore down with his right arm on the back of Hart’s neck, leaving the Phenom in an extremely unenviable position. Unfortunately for Alias, however, Hart wasn’t completely without options, as he had been moments earlier. He was now able to turn into the half nelson and use his free arm to fire an elbow into the side of Alias’s head. Two more successive elbows and the hold was released. Alias jumped back up and began stomping wildly at the neck of his opponent, obviously intending to wear it down and hard. Dragging Hart closer to the ropes, Alias then proceeded to climb to the second turnbuckle, before bringing a hard Hart-style elbow drop onto the neck and shoulder area of Hart, bringing visual pain and discomfort to the Phenom. Visibly feeling the stress of the sudden attack on his neck, Hart held it in agony. Like a shark that smelt blood, Alias slung Hart over the ropes onto his neck, before bouncing off the ropes and sandwiching his opponent’s neck between the stiff ropes and his leg. Writhing in agony as a result of the viscous attack on his neck, SJH tried desperately to crawl out of the ring, or at least to call to Jon Katz Jr, only for Alias to cut him off with a stamp to his fingers. Adding a customary boot to the side of the head for good measures. Grabbing SJH’s right leg, Alias dragged him away from the ropes and locked him into a Camel Clutch, right in the centre of the ring. Holding onto the submission, he wrenched back on it for a few moments before finally deciding to release it. Felecia was screaming at Henry Irwonsen in the ring, something about Alias’ inability to fuck her and otherwise deciding to milk her for free oooor something else about that cow from last week, while Alias lifted up Hart back to his feet. Hart, still somewhat out of it due to the damage that he had most recently sustained by Alias on his second (fourth?) wind, tried to break free. However, Alias, hooked his own leg around Hart's, and faced the opposite direction of SJH.
