Mach 2 Biography Real Name Mark James Bishop II Nicknames The Supersonic Kid, The Speed Demon Hometown New York, NY Birthday 23 (born January 15th, 1988) Place of Birth Yonkers, NY Height 5'8 Weight 184 Blood Type AB- Eye Color Green Biography Alignment and Personality: Usually a very well-mannered, polite wrestler. Friends with lots of the boys backstage and lends a helping hand to those in need. Loved by fans for his incredible high-risk offensive style. Sometimes displays a sarcastic side. Does have something of a temper sometimes and it does come out in extreme circumstances. Protective of those close to him. Description: Straight black hair, blue eyes, little bit of stubble on his face. Lean, prototypical cruiserweight build (think Matt Sydal/Evan Bourne). Wears a pair of black pants-length tights with black kickers highlighted with a neon blue design along the edges. Tights have all kinds of crazy spiral and jet patterns across the sides. -Mach 2 is a good friend of both former ACW standouts and former ACW Heavyweight Champions Seymour Almasy and Andy Sharp. Andy liked what he saw with the kid and got him a spot training in Almasy’s now-defunct Almasy Office. From there, he learned the finer points of “throwing yourself at people” while bolstering a fairly decent counter game. -After wowing audiences for several months within ACW as part of the First Airbourne with Andy Sharp, took time off following a brutal beatdown by the Heirs of Wrestling and Max Danger and did a few tours with Japanese promotion, Kyoto PRO. He added some striking and ground wrestling ability to his game to become a more well-rounded opponent. -During a recent tour with New York’s Finest, the fed that Mach 2 once called home, he met Big E. Stark, an older, but imposing rookie that showed some heart and lots of promise. Giving him the opportunity that Andy Sharp once gave him, Mach 2 made an offer to become Big E.’s tag partner and he accepted. Working under a temporary 90-day trial contract, Big E. Stark and Mach 2, along with Mach 2’s girlfriend, Lettie Rios, have vowed to take ACW by storm and whoop a lot of ass doing it, fulfilling what the First Airbourne could not do. Win the ACW Tag Team Championships. As of the new year, Big E. Smalls impressed the ACW brass enough to have been signed on full-time. Other Feds New York's Finest Other Fed Titles NYF Tag Team Champion x4, Kyoto Pro Tag Team Champion x1 Other Fed Awards Voted most likely to hit the ceiling with his next high spot. Past Managers Andy Sharp Team Big E. Smalls w/Big E. Stark, Lettie Rios Past Teams & Groups The Sounders (with Davis Rogers) Wrestling Style American independent mixed with something of Japanese light heavyweight. Despite being a pretty young age, he’s wrestled since he was sixteen. Excels with a mix of striking, high-flying and evasion/counter wrestling. Knows a hell of a lot of creative flash pins and is pretty much the OMG WORKRATE guy of the team. Finisher Move 1. Corkscrew Shooting Star Press 2. Swinging 3/4 Neckbreaker off turnbuckles (Spike Dudley's Acid Drop) Finisher Description 1. The Speed of Sound 2.
