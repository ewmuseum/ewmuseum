Sunday 29th September | Live on FOX From The Pepsi Center, Denver, Colorado Introduction As a new federation, ACW has been very much of an experiment from the word "go" and tonight it is still no different, to be honest, nothing in ACW's future will be confirmed until the first PPV at least has been put under Winter's a Co's belts. However, last week with in a meeting with his TV bosses, Winters created a few problems for himself, one of which could result in no Courage after Relentless, because they might not have a TV Deal! Tonight many a question will be answered, and more importantly many matches will be made for next week's superCARD, the very first in ACW's existence. With the roster still trying to find their feet, the PPV's quality is in certain doubt, as Winter's tried to sell his product to the highest bidder, or the one which he wants to join, which seems to be TNN. Tonight is going to be a clear indication where ACW will be going by this time in two weeks. Tonight feuds with be decided in the only way known, PPV matches and ACW's TV future will be decided... or will it? Deep Shit It had taken Winter's a full week to realise it, but he now truly understood what he had put himself in; deep shit. As he paced through the backstage area of the Pepsi Center, he only had one targeted destination, and that was to the ring. As he brushed through the curtain to no music, no pyro, just the silence of a capacity crowd. The ACW fan's had still to make their mind up about Winters, was he truly trying to bring the product to the next level? or was he just a money hungry Bishoff wannabe? Probably the later. "Tonight's going to be a VERY important night, so I'm going to make this quick and painless for you guys. Last week I made a decision, a pretty huge decision as ACW goes, and that was to break the contract with the very TV station we are being shown on at this current moment in time, FOX. In two weeks time, we could have no show being broadcasted to the world, and that smells trouble for ACW in general. I NEED YOUR HELP DENVER!" The capacity crowd was stifled, what the hell was he talking about? "Tonight I have 5 senior members of TNN coming to watch the show, and they are due to arrive in around 20 minutes, and then take their seats in that empty box over there and what I need you guys to do is...be really enthusiastic about the matches and all the competitors tonight. What I'm saying is... cheer for the good guys and boo the bad ones OK? Pop at every big spot! Make a "ooohhhhhh" at a nice piece of skill. Call people assholes. DO ANYTHING, I don't care what, but make it so that ACW's fans are the best fans in the world because if you do do that, then ACW will have a better chance of staying on TV, do you get me people?" The fans looked at one another as they wondered if Winter's had been smoking something before he came on, he wanted the fans to kid on they are into the show? If they wanted to do that they could have went to a WWE event. "COME ON GUYS, ARE YOU WITH ME?" The noise level in the arena slowly grew until almost all of the arena were on their feet cheering for Winters, he was delighted by this, and shot his arms in the air as his battle cry was heard, but it wasn't what he thought. Stood behind him, was one pissed off HAWK! HAWK swiped the microphone from Winter's grasp and pulled him back as he tried to exit the ring, obviously not wanting to be anywhere near the man whom he tried to destroy. "Winter's, fuck FOX but what the fuck is going on in this booking?" *POP FROM THE FANS* "A tag team match? No wonder you're ACW stock has plummeted further than your two-bob whore of a mother. Surely you can come up with something better than a crappy tag team match, I want ICU, that is true, but I don't wanna tag with this red-haired guy, I don't need him." Winter's nodded and asked for HAWK's mic, but HAWK laughed at him and pointed to get his own. "HAWK, I have no reason to mess you about anymore, I know that these fans love you, and to be frank, you can make me more money than anyone on the roster, but trust me on this one, why will people buy a PPV, if they seen the Main Event the week before on Courage?" For the first time in HAWK's mind, Winter's was making sense. "Main Event huh?" Winters' eyes widened as he realized what he had just promised HAWK. "That means that I should get a decent take of the PPV buy rates...good shit Winters. As for next Sunday, ICU Vs. SilverHAWK for the US Title, it will be 1 for 1 for HAWK, as in his first try, he wins his first ACW Title on the first try." As HAWK's music hit and he was about to leave the ring, Winter's was this time pulling him back from the ropes, as the music came to a halt. "It's...not that simple HAWK. You see the production team needed this match to have...a tingle about it, and I decided that the belt, would infact be right above your head next week, in a LADDER MATCH!" As the crowd popped in the arena, HAWK's ego shrunk incredibly, he was a 15 year veteran, and anyone whom has wrestled that amount of time will know the damage that it does to your sticks, or to be precise your knees. Winters must have known about this, HAWK could feel it, and as Winters waved good-bye and walked through the ropes, he high fived fans either side of the barrier, as he knew that it was 1-0 to him.
