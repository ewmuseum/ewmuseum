Lindsay Troy Biography Real Name Birth name: Lindsay Strozzi, was legally changed by her father Nicknames the Queen of the Ring, the Chairwoman, the Sovereign of the Swerve, Dis, Sonny Silver's Worst Nightmare Hometown Tampa, Florida (announced from there); also resides in Cambridge, Massachusetts Birthday August 30th Place of Birth Tampa, Florida Height 6'3 Weight 170ish pounds Eye Color Hazel Biography There's a not-so-little girl, Who has a lot of curls, Cascading down her shoulders from her head. And when she is good, She is very....very good. But when she is bad... She's just better. Nemo me impune lacessit: No one provokes me with impunity. Other Feds PRIME (current), EPW, fWo, A1E Other Fed Titles PRIME Universal Champ x2, PRIME Tag Team Champ x2, PRIME Intense Champ x1 (current), PTC Unified Champ (retired the belt), EPW World Heavyweight Champ (longest reigning), TEAM Champion of Champions, EPW Tag Team Champ, fWo Hardcore Champ, fWo Tag Team Champ, A1E Tag Team Champ, A1E Cyber Champ, A1E Triple Star Champ Other Fed Awards PRIME: Hall of Famer, Wrestler of the Week (bunch of times), Colossus Curse Breaker, **Has never lost at Colossus**, 2007 Roulette Winner, 2007 Face/Tag Team/Feud of the Year; OTHERS: FWI/ENN #2 Wrestler of the Year (2003), FWI/ENN Diva/Manager of the Year (2004), ENN Match/Moment/Wrestler of the Year (2005), MARK Awards Tag Team of the Year (2005), MARK Tournament Winner (2006, beat >=) ), Holzerblog Wrestler of the Month x 2 (June & August '07) Past Managers Dan Ryan Past Teams & Groups Silver and GOLD, The Inner Circle Wrestling Style About as well-rounded as one can be without turning into a circle. Also has a penchant for beating you silly and making it look easy (see MWA) Finisher Move Aerial: Top-rope Corkscrew Moonsault into Tornado DDT; Submission: Bridging Sharpshooter with a Crossface; Power: The Amazing Red's 'Code Red' (seen Finisher Description Aerial (Ultimate): The Crowning Glory; Submission (Ultimate): Dynastic Cycle; Power (Ultimate, ONLY on cruisers 220lb or less...NO EXCEPTIONS!): By Royal Decree Special Moves COMMON SPOTS: martial arts strikes/punches/kicks/throws (will work super-stiff if provoked...and it doesn't take much. Oh, and none of those bullshit sissy "girlie forearm shots" either), front-flip legdrop running off the ropes, Enziguri, spinning roundhouse heel kick (landing on feet), top turnbuckle armbreaker, baseball slide flying headscissors (opponent on outside), reverse underhook DDT (or any DDT variation), underhook face breaker, Cobra Clutch legsweep, stiff knife-edge chops, running backflip DDT (using ropes as a springboard). SIGNATURE MOVES (also Alternative Finishers): 360* Top Rope Hurricanrana into Pin ('All Hail the Queen'), Double Underhook Front-Face Plant ('Final Judgment'), Crossface with Optional Barbed Wire ('Thy Kingdom Come'), Koji Clutch (leg-sling grounded crossface; "Divine Right") **NOTE:** For a full MWA, kindly visit LT's PRIME bio (click here), and then click on the "Matchwriting Aids" button.
