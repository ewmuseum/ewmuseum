Chester B. Wallace Biography Real Name Chester Brand Wallace Nicknames The Infamous Hometown Los Angeles, California Birthday 03/19/1960 Place of Birth Tuscaloosa, Alabama Height 5'11 Weight 238 Blood Type A+ Eye Color Brown Biography Chester Wallace’s story, much like the wrestler's he manages, is well documented. Born to a well to do Baptist family in Tuscaloosa, AL, young Chester studied his charismatic pastor every Sunday and quickly realized he himself had a gift for gab and persuasion.  As life rolled on, Chester found the south a little too slow and small time for his taste, so he packed up and headed west. By the year 2000 he was one of the premier sports agents in the country, co-founding entertainment giant Endeavor’s sports representation department and making a lot of people, including himself, a lot of money. Six years later, after eleven separate charges of making illegal, unsanctioned contributions to college athletes and their families, Chester was exiled from a business that he helped build. A business that he made relevant, glamorous, and important. A business that he made famous! One could easily say that Chester B. Wallace’s presence in the ACW is attributed only to the fact that professional wrestling, in all its obsession over ratings and controversy, might be the only organized sport that would do business with him. But if you know or have looked into him, one could also easily say that his motive for being here would never be that cut and dry. His relationship with Oso Bannon, outside of its obvious business connotation, is unknown. Many industry insiders feel the duo will simply be a flash in the pan and that if Wallace was serious about wrestling he’d manage someone with actual in-ring experience and not just some juiced up, pilled out, gorilla with a well known name. When faced with that question, Wallace said on live television, “You say tomato. I say to-mah-to. Mark my words: Oso Bannon will be a star in this business...I don’t care if he hasn’t trained a day. Take that bet to Vegas.” Guess we’ll all see just how lucky we’re getting in the future. Other Feds The NFL, the NBA, the MLB, and regrettably the NCAA. Past Managers The Endeavor Agency Wrestling Style Dirty, Cheating Manager Finisher Move Constant Manipulation Finisher Description Everyday Life Special Moves Salt to eyes, canes to heads, referee distractions. You get the picture! Aside from a few dirty tactics and a gift for gab and negotiation he's useless. Entrance Music "Power" by Kanye West Trainer Irving Azoff Manager Chester B. Wallace
