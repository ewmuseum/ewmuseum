Alexi Volstein Real Name: Birthdate: 8/3/75 Hometown: Erfurt, Germany Height: 6'3' Weight: 241 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Heel Manager(s): n/a Current Teams: n/a Entrance Music: 'The End of All Things to Come' - Mudvayne Wrestling Style: Brawler with a little martial arts experience Signature Moves: n/a Set Up Move: Arm hook and back kick (Arm hook and back kick ) Finisher: STF (STF) ACW Title History: n/a ACW Award History: n/a Other Federations: n/a Other Federation Awards: n/a Handler Information Handler Name: Sean AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: n/a Biography Alexi and Christoph were born to Bruno and Arabella Volstein in the late 1970’s. They were born and raised in the town of Erfurt, Germany until their twenties. Bruno, their strict, ultra-nationalistic father, decided to home school the boys, as it would be best for them. Bruno strongly believed in the ideas and methods of Adolf Hitler. Mein Kampf became Bruno’s bible and he made Alexi and Christoph read it in their early teens. Their father was the owner and president of a successful factory so the boys had a good life. The factory was so successful that Bruno hired a translator to teach Alexi English. Alexi learned fluent English by the age of 18 and had plenty of knowledge pertaining to dictatorship and Hitler. To pay for Christoph’s English lessons, Bruno ran an illegal fighting circuit in his factory on the weekends. It was successful, but not successful enough to teach Christoph good English. Alexi would go and watch every weekend soaking up all the techniques...just in case. Alexi saw the brutal, animalistic fighting as an art. As they began reading up on American and Western European history in their late teens, both Volstein brothers developed a distaste for mankind. When Alexi was 22 and Christoph was 18, they were sent to China to join a violent cult their father had been in contact with for a few months. The cult was anti-human, anti-society, and anti-civilization organization. Not surprisingly, Alexi and Christoph both went willingly. The brothers spent a few years in the army/cult before leaving for America. While in the cult they beat, killed, bombed, shot, and did a slew of other things to the Chinese people of business. At the ages of 26 and 22 respectively, Alexi and Christoph migrated to America. They felt they could “spawn” their own army and bring chaos to Americana. With a loathing fire in their hearts, their following began with just a few zealots. One of the first member’s occupations happened to be professional wrestling. He informed Alexi and Christoph that professional wrestling could be a great way to get their message heard nationally. He said if fans and the organization thought it was only a gimmick, they wouldn’t think twice about it. It was, at that moment, decided. Alexi and Christoph were going to become wrestlers. They found a German-born trainer near their new hometown of Olympus, Washington and took in his services. They told the trainer about their plans, and he told them he would do all he could to mold them into great wrestlers. They trained for two years before trying their hand in an actual ring, in front of actual fans. The rest...is history...
