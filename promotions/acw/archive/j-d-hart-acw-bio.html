J.D. Hart Biography Real Name Jacob Davis Hart Nicknames The Luchadorible Hometown The Sunset Strip Birthday Aug. 12, 1983 Place of Birth San Diego, CA Height 5'11" Weight 220 Blood Type B- Eye Color Brown Biography A former high school All-American wrestler, who couldn't afford to go to college (Fresno State) changed his life and started dancing at the Sunset Strip's 100% Pure Gold. He stopped that after learning of an opening at White Mountain Wrestling and began training to become a professional wrestler.
