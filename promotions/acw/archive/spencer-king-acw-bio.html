Spencer King Biography Nicknames The Guv'nor · The Chancellor of Excellence Hometown London, England. Birthday 18th January 1985. Height 6'1 · 1.85m Weight 232lbs · 105kgs Blood Type O Eye Color Green. Biography Spencer King was born into a wrestling dynasty; destined for greatness from the moment he stepped foot in a wrestling ring. A true artist of wrestling, Spencer's style is a thing of technical mastery. The Chancellor of Excellence would now be looking to show ACW exactly why he is so renowned across the globe. By hook or by crook he would be remembered. Other Feds UKIW · NJPW · AMW Other Fed Titles UKIW (UKIW World Champion) · NJPW (NJPW Champion) · AMW (AMW World Champion) Team The Kingdom Wrestling Style The Guv'nor uses a devastating blend of technical excellence and athletic prowess to defeat his opponents. A silky smooth wrestler, his classic wrestling style has drawn praise from many corners. Finisher Move Hope'n'Glory Driver The Royal Flush Camden Lock [Click for video(s)]. Special Moves The Royal Decree (Hammerlock Sweep DDT) The King Sting (Cradle DDT) Fawlty Tower Drop (Leg Capture Backbreaker) The London Eye (Catatonic Backbreaker) The Crown Jewels (Double Underhooked Neckbreaker) BRITplex [Different Variations] (T-Bone BRITplex, Bearhug Exploder BRITplex, Tiger BRITplex, Belly-to-Belly BRITplex, Lord-Nelson BRITplex, Release Regal BRITplex) [Click bracketed description for video].
