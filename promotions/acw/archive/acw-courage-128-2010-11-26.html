A C W THE EMPIRE STRIKES BACK COURAGE 128 ¤ 26 NOVEMBER 2010 TORINO OVAL LINGOTTO, TURIN, ITALY INTRODUCTION PRE-SHOW ¦ HOUR ONE ¦ HOUR TWO Introduction At the start of last week's broadcast, Jeremy Hunt and his band of Huntsmen made a bold statement. Towards the end of that segment, the devout fans of ACW found themselves believing that good would triumph. It wasn't that Hunt and his goons weren't convincing in their promise to reshape ACW. No, it was because SilverHAWK and his son GoldenHAWK were ready to fight back. And they had begun to accumulate some talent to back it up. No, not Christopher Sheffield and Keith Scott Zimmerman. They were already known to be for ACW, although there's still major disbelief that these two men are actually co-existing. No, the beast known was Omega stepped up to the plate and essentially pledged his allegiance to SilverHAWK. Midway through the broadcast, Jeremy Hunt's bodyguard WAR -- the biggest man in ACW -- and Omega did battle in the first of a series that would determine ACW's eventual fate. WAR prevailed, but only via disqualification. The ACW faithful and SilverHAWK still had faith. The end of the night came, and the fans watched as another epic match unfolded: Sheffield and Zimmerman, against Keller and God's Forgotten Son. The momentum shifted numerous times, and the fans watched on with bated breath. Alas, the fans also watched... as Z and his tribe of terrorist rebels invaded the scene. Laying waste to everyone. Sparing nobody at all. At the end of the night, there was nothing but doom and gloom. One can only hope, tonight -- especially during the planned showdown between WAR and Keith Scott Zimmerman -- doesn't end the same way. Hope is in very short supply, though... Next: PRE-SHOW. ---- IN THE DARK~! IN THE DARK #1: Saleem Nagi vs. Antwone MacDaniels In the second of ACW's newly-signed 'developmental promotions' -- the West Coast Championship Wrestling -- Saleem Nagi had quietly built up a reputation for being completely and utterly awesome. He'd also been heralded as one of WCCW's greatest, so when ACW had the chance to snap him up... many members of the staffing committee were pleased. Saleem himself was pleased to be out there, getting another opportunity to mix it up with ACW's burgeoning stars. Alas, his opponent for the night was none other than Antwone MacDaniels. He who was always on the look-out for some sort of drug (usually either PCP or crack). Tyrone Biggums, he wasn't. MacDaniels did some sort of hip-hop routine en route to the ring, and once in there... proceeded to offer to perform fellatio on Saleem if the latter could procure some crack for him. Nagi's response? To unleash one of the most spectacular of ass-whoopings that the fans packed inside the Torino Oval Lingotto had ever seen. And it all lasted about sixty two seconds. That's when Saleem put the cherry on top of the sundae and re-arranged Antwone's face with The Downfall: a running knee to the face. Three seconds later, it was two wins out of two for Saleem. Impressive stuff from what many were calling ACW's next big thing. IN THE DARK #2: Fight Man & JP Boudreau vs. Carl Kimball & Monte Young Speaking of WCCW, Carl Kimball and Monte Young had previously featured as the longest reigning WCCW Tag Team Champions. They had been a revelation in the tag team division... until they decided to try to betray their leader , 'Savvy' Steve Savoy. That didn't turn out too well for Carl and Monte. A short while after that incident, Carl and Monte lost their Tag Team Titles. In a bid to retain them, the two childhood friends had to do something utterly absurd. They had to marry each other. Seriously. Many months later and fresh out of WCCW, Monte and Carl found themselves picked up by ACW. Their debut match as the newest tandem on the block was against two hardworking individuals. The ever-affable JP Boudreau... and the man who loved to fight: Fight Man. Fight Man seemed confused by all the buzz surrounding Carl Kimball and Monte Young, until JP Boudreau explained it to Fight Man. The Hardcore Hero couldn't stop laughing after that. "How do these Italians know we're a gay married couple?!" Monte demanded angrily. Carl Kimball could only respond with a shrug and a peck to the cheek of the (surprised) Monte. Fans of WCCW had seen that a lot, but ACW fans and the Italian crowd were stunned by that. So was Fight Man, who almost paid for it when Carl Kimball rolled him up. Thankfully, Fight Man recovered and the match continued. All four individuals had distinctively differing styles, but in Kimball and Young's case... it wasn't a problem. Fight Man and JP Boudreau, on the other hand, found it a tad difficult to gel with each other. That meant good things for the newest signings to the ACW roster. Alas, after seven minutes of back-and-forth action, JP Boudreau surprised the technically sound Monte Young with a Figure Four Leglock transitioning out of a belly-to-back suplex. Fight Man kept the equally adept bruiser Kimball at bay long enough for Boudreau to draw the submission out of Monte. After the match, all four men shook hands in a good show of sportsmanship. Kimball wanted his partner (professionally and personally) to hug him as a manner of comfort, but Young was clearly not cool with that. Kimball initiated a hug anyway, drawing 'awwws' from the crowd. Monte shook his head, clearly regretting that he and Kimball didn't have to resort to get married way back in WCCW in order to get another crack at the WCCW Tag Team Titles... one that didn't result in victory, to add insult to injury. IN THE DARK #3: Aleczander vs. Kati And finally, it was time for top-billed IN THE DARK match-up. Pretty Boy Swag by Soulja Boy and Gucci Mane hit the speakers, letting all of the rabid Italian fans know their (least) favorite reality TV star (douchebag) was making his way to the ring in a foul (somewhat tipsy?) mood. In his search for a manager since being left to the wayside by SlySports Entertainment LLC, the muscled Mancunian had managed to bungle a series of losses over the last several weeks. He wanted a big win to turn things around and what better than by putting out an open challenge. Ricochet by Shiny Toy Guns quickly answered said challenge, bringing out none other than Kati herself. Kati had been in dire straits with the likes of her cohorts in the cabal, namely Z -- her leader. Feeling her on the bad end of an ACW Tag Team Titles match-up a few weeks ago, she needed a win to quickly win her way back into Z’s good graces. Aleczander hit on the toxic-haired siren by quickly throwing a couple of sly pick-up lines (as well as a pelvic thrust) in her general direction. How did Kati respond? Well, a couple of hard kicks to the legs might have done it. The former right hand to two-time Spirit Of ACW Champion Trevor Wilson slapped Aleczander around a little more, kicking him at the legs to bring him down before ringing his bell with an enzuigiri for a quick two-count. Kati continued her attack, running off the ropes and dizzying the muscular Mancunian with a headscissors into the turnbuckle. He turned around and ate another running dropkick to the corner, doubling him over, which allowed Kati to slingshot into another dropkick, taking him down. The Ultimate Survivor weathered the storm as he ducked another run off the ropes and turner her nearly inside out with a clothesline for two. A scoop slam got two, as well as a vertical suplex and even a headbutt drop to the rib cage. It seemed the Amazing Ace had been adding moves to his repertoire. The thought was quickly dashed when he went for another suplex and got another two-count. Kati couldn’t take much more punishment, so when he went for a second scoop slam, she ducked underneath and took out a leg. Watching intently from the ringside area seemed to be none other than the Night Life’s siren, Elixr, looking on intently for a few moments before disappearing from sight. A couple more kicks brought him down as well as a Tornado DDT, which lead to Postmarked From Heaven (Springboard Moonsault) for a close two. Kati went up top and tried for another move, but Aleczander greeted her up top, hitting her in the back. He tried for a suplex, but Kati went to the eyes, raking them with her nails and shooting him off the top rope… From there, it was a quick SDMR (The Moonsault Double Foot Stomp) to the chest that got her a three-count and a hard-fought win. As for Aleczander? The losses continued and the search for a manager was going to roll on. Next: HOUR ONE. ---- weirdo meets egomaniac. Another week, another episode of COURAGE. And this week, things had to end up much better than last week. At least that was the way one Ryan Billows was looking at things. He walked down the hallway, already decked out in his wrestling gear, with a scowl on his face. Last week hadn't been a good week for Billows; no, it had actually been a decidedly bad week. “...fucking Cowboys and goddamn toy soldier idiots...” Billows had one thing on his mind: his recent loss to Jonny Yankee. And he had every intention of going out to his show, The Lion's Den, and rectifying that loss tonight. He, however, stopped in his tracks. There, in front of him, several feet down the hall, was one of the nicest asses he'd ever seen. Two handfuls of perfect ripe plumpness. He cocked his head to the side and managed a brief smile, “Well, well, well, maybe tonight could turn out to be a BUNderful night...” But suddenly, out of nowhere, somebody popped up right in front of the Risen Lion. "I'm Fight Man." announced the one and only Hardcore Hero, as he put his hands on his hips and locked eyes with Billows. Billows was slightly taken aback, naturally. He was actually more concerned with the random chick with the bangable behind, but after stepping to the right to catch another glimpse of her, she was gone. Disappeared into thin air. And that made Billows mad as all hell. "You're a fucking roadblock is what you are, get out of my--" "--I have something of utmost importance to tell you!" was the boisterous interruption from the most fightingest man in all of ACW. This brought a scowl to the face of the former Spirit Of ACW Champion. But seeing how the piece of eye candy he was checking out had vanished, Billows stepped back and folded his arms across his chest. All the while mentally preparing himself to unleash a spectacular beatdown of Fight Man. Given the all-clear to speak again, Fight Man nodded his head and smiled. "I... like to fight." he whispered. The fans watching this unfold on the SlyTron? They laughed. Oh yes, they did. Billows was none too happy. "Christ almighty, what the hell did I do to have to deal with these idiots?" he muttered to himself through clenched teeth. Fight Man merely stuck his chest out and looked extremely proud of himself. Before the Risen Lion could speak, though, Fight Man raised his hands. "I heard what you said about me last week. That I'm better than Ric Flair. I am honoured, but there is no one better than the Nature Boy. Perhaps, Mick Foley before he got extremely overweight. He is, after all, one of my influences. He likes to fight too. Nevertheless, I feel I should inform you to stop besmirching the good name of Ric Flair. Now, onto the second matter on the agenda. I have something of a proposal for you. Don't fear, it's not a proposal of marriage." Billows shook his head. What was the moron in front of him, donned in a mask obviously a rip-off from Captain America, babbling about? Why was Billows even humoring this...this masked cuntflap? Was ACW putting something in the water to turn out all this retards? All these “One Flew Over The Cuckoo's Nest” rejects? Did Billows need to be careful about drinking from the faucet? And why, was it, that he seemed to attract all the attention from the bonkers boys of ACW? “Look, pal, I don't know what your deal is. Perhaps your mother was standing on a hard surface when you bungee-jumped out. Maybe ACW is all about hiring crackheads now. Or, all things considered, maybe I really am in some sort of Hell. Whatever the case...take your game, and find somebody else to play with you. I've got better things to do with my time. They're getting set up for me in the ring, The Lion's Den, heard of it? It's the hot little show that you'll never be a guest on, what do you think of that?” Fight Man titled his head to the right and appeared to chew over Billows' words. His answer? "Irrelevant. Now, shush. It's time for Fight Man to do what he does best." And then, he cleared his throat. Billows was losing his patience, and balled up his fists. Billows sneered...was this fucktard really about to come at him? It would be a walk in the proverbial park, but still, The Watcher didn't feel like doing that song and dance with this guy. A lightbulb lit above Billows' head. “Wait!” Fight Man dropped his fists, looking on dubiously as Billows tried to go. “Iceman told me to use that Ric Flair line. He also said Mick Foley smells like he bathed in anchovies, and Hulk Hogan looks like a piece of shit, and not just normal poo, but the orange kind you get after eating spicy food from Wendy's. So, man, I think you're betting on the wrong horse coming after me...the real name on your agenda should be Iceman. Oh, he also said, and I quote, 'Fight Man...what a dumb name...now Iceman, that's a fucking man's man's name.' Yes, that's what he said!” Billows watched on and almost smiled as Fight Man thought about this words and begin quaking with anger. “You...are a good human being to fill me in on these ridiculousosities. I will find Iceman, and we... will... FIGHT!” With that, Fight Man bounded off down the hallway, leaving Billows to shake his head. “I swear to God...half this place needs to be lobotomized...” Welcome to another week, Billows. Our Hopes and Expectations ACW was in turmoil. Following IN ACROPOLIS 2010, the lines had been drawn in the proverbial sand with the memory of one Jesse Ramey, presumed missing and speculated to be d-e-a-d. On one side stood Jeremy Hunt and his Huntsmen, while the other served as a safe haven for a resistance that grew stronger by the minute. Waiting in the wings? None other than the cabal, plotting something of their own like the terrorist rebels they were. Week by week, with each episode of COURAGE, the Huntsmen and the good guys of ACW drew closer to the middle and the violent confrontation that surely awaited them there. Total destruction was possible for several at any moment. Oso Bannon sat pensively in his assigned dressing room, oblivious. His arrival to the organization had come at a time where joining one of these alliances should have been easy and welcome, but surprisingly neither had much interest in acquiring The Bear’s services. His agent, Chester Wallace, maintained hope that they would find their place in the high profile battle that raged around them, but his dismal performance in his first match the week before certainly wouldn’t change any minds about the Associated Press’ 2008 Defensive Player of the Year’s worth in a professional wrestling federation. And so, for the third week straight, he would remain the vanity hire. Someone that even the most despicable personalities in the locker room felt didn’t have the right to be there. “Ay, Bear! How goes it mate?” All except for one? The Amazing Gabriel stood in the dressing room’s doorway, lighting up a cigarette. His eyes darted around the room, surveying it, their moves shielded by his always present sunglasses. It was only seconds before he spoke again. “Ugh. Christ, guy. I’ve been hearing all the talk, but it didn’t sink in for me until I saw the condition of this changing room. I’ve seen caskets with more space and whores with less filth. Everyone really does hate you.” Now it was The Bear whose eyes wandered around the room. It certainly was a far cry from the facilities he had gotten used to at Ohio State. Wallace had sold him on the idea of a clean slate. A fresh start for both of them. Gone were the perks associated with their old ways. Most of them anyway. He patted his left pants pocket and felt comforted, if only for a brief moment. “Ahem.” Oso had been drifting off as he so often tended to do in recent months, but The Amazing Gabriel was not someone who enjoyed being ignored. His time was precious, just ask him yourself. Bannon considered saying something, but couldn’t find the words. Something deep within him told him that even if he could, “The Amazing One” shouldn’t be his first choice to vent to. And so he remained sitting in silence, a silence that didn’t stand a chance as long as the leader of The Night Life was around. “Here I am, a lone extender of the olive branch in a sea full of ravenous sharks, and you can’t even do me the justice of paying attention! No wonder you’re finding it so hard to make friends, champ! Maybe if you weren’t constantly zonked out on downers...” His words were laced with hypocrisy, as The Amazing Gabriel and the group he championed were well known to frequent rave parties all around the world. It’s not like people get high on life at those sorts of events, but again, if you asked “The Amazing One” he’d surely tell you that there’s a huge difference between his style of self-medicating and Oso’s. “...Now, I’d say that I’d give you something to perk you up, but I’m hearing Ancona has phenomenal Night Life (ZING!) and I’m not sacrificing my good time to help you. Nothing personal. Instead, I’ve a gift that’s even better! Wrestling advice from one of ACW’s premier superstars... ME!!! Isn’t that amazing?!” It was rare for The Bear to get excited about anything anymore and even more rare for him to speak to anyone other than Chester Wallace in spite of his disdain for doing so. But he had been beaten down last week. He still hated to lose and burned with the desire to win. If he wanted to melt Iceman, he was going to need all the help he could get. He replied softly: “Thank you.” The Amazing Gabriel grinned broadly, patting Oso Bannon’s head as if to say “it’s alright”. He loved it when best laid plans began to come together. He reached for the door and pulled it closed slowly. “There, there Bear. I assure you there will be no one making you look like a cub tonight...” And the door slammed shut. The Sound of Silence in The Lion's Den The set-up in the ring was a familiar one, although the crowd hadn't seen it in a handful of weeks...it was still recognizable. Two lion-emblazoned flags on either side of a big red flag with the face of the man (fashioned to resemble Che Guevara) who'd soon be making his way out. The host of the festivities, so to speak. The simple stool on one side of the ring. The massive throne sitting atop a golden platform on the other side. Above the ring, dangling on chains, the elegantly-framed ridiculously-large flatscreen television. And if you didn't yet realize what segment was in store for you, the music that hit the PA system might make something click in that sluggishly slow brain of your's. “How could you abandon me, I thought you'd always be, There for me, standing tall, one and one for all, My support modeling, rolling as a team But instead on our own, enemies of old” “April Suits” by Taproot. And then there he was, the one and only Risen Lion. Billows He would usually be decked it in the fanciest of fashions, but tonight, he wore his wrestling tights and a sleeveless red t-shirt with alternating blue and yellow grimy text reading “Love me or hate me, and we both know you hate me...”, the back of the shirt finished the statement, “...I hate you more.” Underneath of that endearing statement was a lion, it's tail fashioned to resemble a key. It was a pretty sexy shirt... but, regardless... The Key of Keystone State didn't look altogether too happy, and when we recalled the events that had transpired last week, it was easy to understand why. Last week, due to a distraction from The Smooth Operator, Midnight Cowboy, Billows had found himself on the losing end of a match with Cowboy's friend, the former Action Wrestling superstar, Jonny Yankee. And it had been Yankee's first match in nearly 8 years, at that. So, yeah...Billows sauntered down to the ring with a frown on his face, and climbed the ringsteps with a frown on his face, and then he snatched the microphone up off the throne before collapsing into it with a frown on his face. And as his music died down?--He wore a frown on his face. The fans were still booing him, the fair share of them hadn't forgotten about his oscar-worthy act two weeks ago where he'd tugged at their every emotion, feigning that he'd re-injured his back, working them back into his corner only to shit all over their emotions. No, that wasn't the kind of thing folks were apt to forget any time soon. “You know, people, I'm really not in the mood to hear it tonight.” Billows' words would only make the crowd turn it up to 11. He leaned back in the chair, staring at the ceiling with a raised eyebrows, sighing audibly into the microphone. “Right. I forgot...I'm dealing with Italy tonight. Fat imbeciles with greasy mustaches with decent Tuscan wines being the only worthwhile things this shithole has to offer...and funny nintendo mascots. Other than that though, you all are about as worthless a meth-head in a skincare commercial.” The words would be translated for the crowd on the SlyTron, and they would proceed to boo once again. Billows rolled his eyes and shot to his feet. “Look, like I said, I don't feel like hearing it tonight. Last week, I got screwed over by the supposed hero of ACW. Seriously, Dudley-Do-Right fucked me right up the proverbial ass last week,” the crowd roared, Billows bit a lip and continued, “And you know what? I call bullshit. Really, I do. Because here I am, a guy who can't even take a leak without having officials making sure I'm not cheating...but Yankee gets Cowboy to distract me AND grabs a handful of tights? And everybody is fucking chipper as can be about it?!” Billows looked out the crowd, as if he expected them to throw him a bone or something. Fat chance, Lion, they thought, you've spent the last handful of months making your bed, now you can sleep in it...alone. They cheered. Billows' bottom jaw jutted out in a somewhat humorous expression of his sincere frustration, “Right...Italians...bottom line is. Yankee's win last week? A FUCKING FLUKE!” Billows screamed, and the crowd booed, obviously disagreeing with The Risen Lion's assessment of Yankee's exciting career-revitalizing win last week. “Screw all of you, it was a fluke, and that's all there is to it. I've got one of the best win-loss records in the sport right now, and I'm not letting some crusty cuntflap like Jonny Yankeedoodle ruin it. That's why, tonight, being the man of action that I am...I'm issuing an open challenge!” The crowd actually cheered at that, it was, indeed, an exciting prospect. “We all know that everybody and their grandmother wants to get their hands on the former Spirit of ACW, so, you dingleberries in the back, tonight is your opportunity to do so. Who's it gonna be? Who's got a pair big enough to step up and inevitably suffer the walk of shame tonight after I hand them their ass?...” Billows would go on for a few moments, leaving the crowd to ask the same question. Who would be the man to answer the challenge? Sheffield? Albion? KSZ?!!! Or shit, what if Fejona Min decided to come out here and hand Billows his ass? UNDEAD!!! “Undead” by Hollywood Undead answered the question, and the crowd stood up, more than a bit surprised. This was a man that hadn't had any run-ins or interaction with Billows yet, and so one had to wonder why he was answering the challenge. But then again, as one saw the woman accompanying him, things became more clear. Maddox St. James, The Silent Spartan, stepped out from the back being accompanied by the beautifully devilish Aria Murphy. And while Maddox had yet to deal with Billows, it was common knowledge that, once upon a time, Billows and Aria Murphy had been very very close friends. Combine that history with the fact that a win over Billows would look pretty damn good on Maddox's resume...and...well...you couldn't blame the guy for taking advantage of the open challenge. The two Pack-members strutted down to the ring, Maddox making sure to slap as many hands as he could as Aria stepped out in front of him and faced him, backing down the ramp as she signed some words to him. Maddox nodded as Billows watched on in amused fascination. As The Sub Zero Soldier and The Ice Queen slid into the ring, the music dying down, Billows looked from one to the other and back again, before holding the microphone up, “...Really? Seriously?” Aria didn't need to sign the words for The Silent Spartan, he could read lips well enough to read those six syllables just fine. Maddox St. James offered a sly smirk as his only response. Here was a man who was the epitome of the adage: 'actions speak louder than words.' Billows shook his head before walking over to the stool and picking the microphone up off of it and turning around, starting to pass it to Maddox before dramatically frowning, “Ohhh...you probably don't need this, do you, Helen Keller?” Billows laughed at his own joke as the crowd booed. Aria sneered and signed to Maddox. Captain Canada didn't lose his cool, immediately signing a response to Aria who snatched the microphone from Billows' grasp and smiled as she spoke for Maddox St. James. “He says, he may be deaf and mute, but the fact that he sees an asshole in front of him confirms that he isn't blind and lord knows your wretched stench can be smelled for miles away. He didn't really say that last part, but you know I just had to chime in.” Aria translated. Seriously, Aria, did you put him up to this?” Aria came back at Billows, “Actually, I wanted to march Arkady down to the ring but he mentioned something about being busy with more important things like taking a dump than deal with the man of 1000 nicknames and zero talent. Maddox stepped up as he has a love of obliterating the vile much like I do and believe you me, not many are worse than you in ACW.” Aria turned to Maddox to catch him up, but Maddox shook his head and signed to her, calm and collected. Aria nodded and turned back to Billows. “Maddox says that you truly are quite the dumbass for trying to get into a war of words with a man who doesn't talk,” the crowd cheered at this, “He says, if you're quite finished hearing yourself speak, he's more than ready to show you how he gets his points across.” Powerful statement from The Silent Spartan. Billows cinched his eyebrows together, “Well, fine then, you give me ten or fifteen minutes to warm up...and we'll let them clear my set out of the ring so as to not spill your blood on my throne...and then...I'm gonna put you on the map, kid, for all the wrong reasons.” With that, Billows threw the mic to the ground and slid out of the ring, stomping up the ramp. Aria and Maddox exchanged a look as they exited the ring... it seemed to convey the knowledge that it was going to be a hard road, but...if they took the right route, it would be well worth it. Tonight, before the end of the hour... THE RISEN LION VS. THE SILENT SPARTAN! R & R, English style. It was finally time for the Brummie Bulldog to breath easy. After months littered with thoughts of revenge for old friends, and looking over his shoulder not only because of what the cabal might do to him but also because of the Huntsmen wanted to do to him… Rud Albion felt like he was finally able to breath easy. It might be why, for the first time since his debut, the Kingdom’s Keeper was appearing on a Courage camera in his civilian clothes. Yes, Albion didn’t plan to wrestle tonight and that’s why he wore a dark grey pinstripe suit with a silk blue shirt underneath it, his Union Jack tie slung around his neck. Sure he was in his civilian clothes, but remember it was still Rud Albion… so naturally that didn’t mean at all that he’d be in jeans and a t-shirt. No, the sharply dressed Bronze Lion was quite alright with the looks that he was getting from those backstage as he navigated the usual hustle of COURAGE as it made it’s way across Italy, towards END GAME 2010, and as Albion thought of his possible World Championship title shot and smiled, he almost ran into a certain newcomer for a second possible week. This time it wasn’t so coincidental though, as Dean Denton had something he wanted to tell England’s Own multi-racial all-star. “What you said last week, I wanted to thank you.” Albion was taken aback by the sudden gratitude from the immediately controversial all-star. “So I take it you aren’t planning on going out to the ring to start another riot tonight, aye?” Albion replied with a smile that hid the arms length wariness he still had for the Darwinist. “Ha, no... management have taken that little matter out of my hands anyway. They've decided to keep me off TV for a week while I 're-think my presentation'.” Denton rubbed the back of his neck and laughed nervously, a laugh that Rud quietly shared. He knew all too well what it was like to be repackaged by those hipster marketing types. “But that's for the best. I've been thinking all week about how I'm going to move forward. How I'm going to be myself again. At first, it was frightening, I'll admit... but now I'm starting to feel a lot more comfortable in my own skin. That's thanks to you.” Albion nodded, a hardened wisdom discernible through his rough visage. “You're welcome, mate. I've been in your position and I know that when you're out on your own with nobody to talk to, mistakes are made.” Denton smiled in response, then leaned in closer, as if he needed to say something away from prying ears. “Now the next problem I've got to work on, and it sounds really pathetic, I know... but... I'm homesick,” he blushed. “I never thought I'd say it, but I'm missing Ol' Blighty. Missing the people, the cool English air, the old Working Men's Pubs, Full English Breakfasts...” “Full English! Hash browns...” Albion said. “Baked beans...” “Fried eggs...” “Black pudding,” added Denton. “Like how my mum used to make it.” “Aye,” Albion said, looking somewhat wistful. “Travelling the world is one of the reasons we do this, but I can't help but agree with you, youth. You know I think England has its charms, or else I wouldn't be going out there in the Union Jack every night. I wouldn't be wearing this bloody shocking tie.” Denton chuckled at the joke. Albion regarded him with caution. There seemed to be something different about Denton now. His voice seemed pandering, almost vulnerable; his face was softer and there was a strange warmth in his yellow-eyed gaze. He was far from the cold-hearted psychopath that had torn Ulf German to shreds last week. Maybe that was genuinely just his act, his... gimmick.
