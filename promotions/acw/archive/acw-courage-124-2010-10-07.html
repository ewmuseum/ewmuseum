IN THE DARK #1 It had been a tough couple of months for Sheamus and Selby O’Brien. The duo had come close, time and time again, to capturing the ACW Tag Team Titles, including a hotly-contested match-up against current champions, The Entourage. However, fate didn’t have it in the cards. Sheamus O’Brien came out, looking all business while his brother, Selby, looked on with interest. Sheamus vowed to turn their luck around by playing spoiler to the opponent. And God, when Pretty Boy Swag by Soulja Boy and Gucci Mane hit the speakers, the loyal Grecians prayed to their numerous deities that he’d shut his opponent the fuck up. The man who was touted for a couple weeks known as the newest prize in the SlySports conglomerate, then disappeared (thanks to a beating at the hands of Jack Harris)… well, had reappeared. Self-proclaimed “Celebu Cum Laude” Aleczander made his way out blowing kisses to fans, telling people within earshot that he fucked up “Operation: Dance Floor” and tapped the ass of Danielle Lloyd during “I’m A Celebrity and I Can’t Get Up!” The former reality show had been in training the last several months, ready to make a proper debut and no doubt with one eye on a path of vengeance with Jack Harris. The match started fast on the part of Sheamus, taking the inexperienced Aleczander to task with numerous grappling holds to keep him off-base. After spending the first couple minutes putting on a clinic, Sheamus took to the sky, hitting a flying forearm, then a dropkick in rapid succession. The elder SOB tried going high, but Aleczander found an opening and kicked the rope out from under him, sending him crashing down. The Amazing Ace worked over Sheamus in the corner with some shoulder blocks, then took him to task with a sloppy, but still effective Flapjack. A two-count was close, so Aleczander repeated the task, hitting a back body drop. A pair of Clotheslines knocked Sheamus silly, letting Aleczander almost get the win. With Selby cheering on his twin brother, Aleczander slapped on a pretty basic armlock, keeping him trapped. Moments later, Sheamus fought back to his feet and hit a pair of forearm shots, followed by a swank Springboard Dropkick that netted another two-count. Sheamus measured him up for a facebuster, but Aleczander had just enough ring sense to backdrop him right over the ropes and out to the floor. Selby went to check on Sheamus while the referee was distracted with Aleczander, pleading with his brother to do the old twin switcheroo. Selby was baffled, yelling at his brother for wanting to commit such an atrocity. Aleczander wasn’t about to wait for the sibling rivalry to brew, so he pushed the brothers into one another. Rolling Sheamus back into the ring, Aleczander spiked the elder SOB (by a couple minutes, mind you) into the mat with a spinebuster/chokeslam-style move he called The Big Brother Bomb. One. Two. Three. And that about did it. Aleczander was victorious in his not-ready-for-primetime debut. Meanwhile, Sheamus and Selby fought all the way to the back, seemingly falling further and further into rotten luck. IN THE DARK #2 Having found himself blackmailed into the scheming arms of Z, Bobby Minio would embark on his first official match since his horrific beating months ago. The fans were abuzz with rumours aplenty; the nature of the information Z had over Minio, the talked-about-but-unseen conflict resolution between Minio and Iceman (both now Cabalites), et cetera. Valley Baptiste, the young man who'd impressed in the All-Star Academy months ago, didn't seem to care about any of that. All Valley wanted was a chance to wrestle a good match and possibly win. Minio was inclined to acquiesce, and controlled the early stages of the match with his no-nonsense style. Baptiste appeared beleagured initially, but after a desperate leg-sweep saw Bobby land on the back of his head and stay down for longer than he should have, Valley realised he'd carved out a perfect opportunity. Baptiste slowly clawed his way back to contention, with Bobby Minio decided to ditch his innovative high-flying techniques for a more ground-based offense. Truthfully, though, after the sixth vertical suplex from Valley that had dropped Minio on the back of his head, the newly-turned Cabalite was seriously considering an extreme form of defense. Alas for the enterprising Valley Baptiste, Bobby Minio was an experienced athlete. Countering a sleeper hold into a belly-to-back suplex, Bobby Minio proceeded to measure Valley up and timed his springboard backflip into a dropkick perfectly. A New Religion, for those not in the know. Minio didn't quite connect cleanly, but it was jusssssst enough to get the three-count and the win. After the match, both Valley and Minio shared a look of respect and acknowledgement for the tough battle they were in... but Iceman of all people came storming out and viciously attacked Valley Baptiste! Minio was stunned, and was itching to peel Iceman off of Baptiste, but the appearance of Kati seconds later put an end to any such notion. Clearly, life in the cabal would prove to be an interesting ride for the reluctant Bobby Minio. IN THE DARK #3 Greg Miles was a man on a mission. Ever since his defeat to Fejona Min on COURAGE 121, the Baytown Badass has found that he needed to ascend the ladder of contendership starting from the very bottom. Last week, he took out Der Deutscher Soldat impressively. This week? MIS, the stout Japanese bruiser who'd impressed in the All-Star Academy months ago. The stage was set for Greg Miles to rack up another win and possibly line up another shot at the title belt he now demanded was rightfully his; the [Black] Scorpion Title.
