Klein Biography Real Name Joshua Bruno Klein Hometown Philadelphia, PA Birthday March 25th, 1981 Place of Birth Nuremburg, Germany Height 5'11 Weight 195 Eye Color Green Biography Klein and Ed went through three different versions of the Jeremy Odesa's "Dungeon" training academy, which was run by part ACW Owner High Flyer. They made their debut in an independent league GWA, with Klein wrestling singles matches and winning a few titles before Klein being introduced as his tag team partner. They then entered IML3, as the Disposable Heroes, nicknamed Food and Drink. They would garner success, winning the IML3 championship. In 2006, they joined LoC, gaining their tag team championship belts in their first match before the promotion shut down it's doors. Since, Edwards has been trying his hand at the movie business, starring in various B-Movie comedy and horror pictures. Klein has found steady work as a referee, for both the Squared Circle and eventually the Fans Wrestling Organization. Now they return to the ring, and to All-Star Championship Wrestling. Tag Team Notes Relationship to Each Other: Klein is very loyal to Ed, no matter how bad Ed treats Klein(which he does very much so). Think Of Mice and Men when dealing with their relationship. Ed will rag on Klein, but ultimately it's to make him stronger. Ed will even lose his temper and attack Klein. Say, if Klein were to cost them the tag team championship in a big match, Edwards may attack Klein and seem as if the team split. But the next week, Klein will be back at Ed's side as if nothing had happened. No matter what Ed says or does, Klein will stay by his side. Tag Team Finisher: SMACK-OW~!(Ed hits a spinning wheel kick, Klein nails a leg sweep, aka total elimination) Music Depending on the week, their music will change to reflect popular culture. Suggestions include... "Pants on the Ground," "Don't Stop Believing" by the Glee Cast, but their current go to song is "Rabbia e Tarantella" by Ennio Morricone starting at :58 seconds in. Manager A large cardboard box Klein carries with him a large box everywhere he goes. It's his safety blanket. Whenever he gets in a state of extreme panic, he will sit Indian style and place the box over him. Fans will grow to call it "Foxy Boxy" over time. Appearance: Klein has long red hair that he hides his face behind. He constantly has an expression of bewilderment. He wears red pant length tights with the flash insignia on the right leg, just above the knee. The word "Klein" is on his ass. He has bright blue eyes. Favorite Quote: “Just you wait until the day machines destroy us all!” GIMMICK: Technophobe Klein hates anything invented in the 20th century. In fact, he is the closest professional Amish wrestler. He is scared of pyro, cameras, cell phones, video games, flat screens, anything. You prod him with a pager he'll run screaming for the hillsides. Klein, however, is the nicest, most sincere, honest individual you will ever meet. As such, he can be easily manipulated to serve another's outcome.
