HIROSHIMA BITCHSLAP (which is to say)… A-Bomb x2! Alias hooked both legs hard and folded the bigger man up, to the outpouring cheers from the crowd! One! TWO! … … No? THREE! Yes! Sympathy for the Devil kicked in, Alias had kept his prize, successfully defending his belt once more, and in doing so… kept that place atop of All-Star Championship Wrestling, and the wrestling world. It was icing on the cake that Harris wasn’t getting a half a million dollar pay day and Trey Vincent would be waiting another day yet again, to pay up for his World Championship. Irwonsen helped the Pulp Hero to his feet and raised his hand to the cheering Spanish Pulpa faithful. Alias could barely stand, taking a knee in the corner, completely spent in falling to the power game, if not the finesse game of Jack Harris. Jack Harris rolled from the ring, angrily clutching his neck… ready to ruin someone… another day. The timekeeper at ringside went to grab the golden Championship belt to hand to Irwonsen… BUT someone from out of the crowd had another idea. SHAWN JESSICA HART! The other half of For Queen & Country’s main event snatched the World Championship from the timekeeper, and looked at it with that infamous Hart smirk for a moment. Henry Irwonsen was fast to get to the ropes to yell at SJH, even if Alias was too beat-up from hardly defeating Jack Harris to do likewise. See, that’s what Looord Gaga wanted, an Irwonsen that was too brave for his own good. As the senior referee ducked half of his boby between the top and middle rope, he chastised the End Game 2009 winner, demanding that he hand over the World Championship belt and walk away. Thing was, Hart wasn’t hear for the World title, not tonight, it was just being used for what would happen next. HANGMAN’S HART ON! ON THE APRON! The Phenom jumped to the apron, confronting the former Captain Ironsides head on, and yanked him half way to the outside before jacking his head to the apron, watching as his body then tumbled to the mats outside to the jeers of the Spanish crowd. Alias rushed forward to stop SJH, but collapsed to his knees his body betraying him after tonight’s main event. SJH quietly hopped down to ringside, not even bothering to step inside the ring, and instead stood over the prone body of the once great Spirit of ACW and now most senior of ACW refs. Hart looked at the World title for a moment before tossing it on Irwonsen’s crumpled body. He could only shake his head in anger, at this moment in time, with the final moments of For Queen & Country replaying in his mind. The Pulp Hero growled at Shawn Jessica Hart from the ring. And somewhere in Madrid, all Trey Vincent could do was laugh at this wonderful continued destruction, if not a lost opportunity to spend half a million dollars… with just six weeks till Legends IV. WINNER: Alias via pinfall; STILL ACW World Heavyweight Champion
