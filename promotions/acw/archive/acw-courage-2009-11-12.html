Vincent tossed Keller to the floor, so Sharc and Vincent could focus on sending Sharp out of this arena on a stretcher. TITANIC POP! ALIAS! The Original Pulp Hero stomped his way down the aisle. Sharc stared at nothing else, forgetting the rest of the world as he saw Alias approach step by step. Sharc lowered his hood, revealing his long blood red hair, his face revealing pure rage, and Alias returned the look of pure hatred. And within seconds, it was on. Sharc and Alias, mid-ring, just unloading with skullcrushing punches to each other, with neither wanting to even show a hint of injury, despite the ridiculously violent impact each of the human concussion machines were having on each other. CRACK! Forgot about Trey. Vincent from behind, chair in hand, blasted Alias in the back, leading to an opening for Sharc, who hit Alias with the Sharc Attack, his version of the Ace crusher. Vincent shouted some sort of instruction to his partner in crime, which was inaudible due to the deafening boos in the arena. Sharc dug into the pockets of his sweatshirt and pulled out…two pairs of handcuffs. Vincent dragged Alias toward the ropes, as Sharc held up the cuffs so the live audience could see what was about to happen. Not wanting to give Alias any shot at blocking this, Sharc moved in and snapped the cuffs around the top rope, and then around Alias' right wrist. The second pair went around Alias' left wrist, and then to the top rope as well. Alias could not defend himself. Vincent, looking to maintain the status quo, ran out to the floor, grabbed a microphone and… THUMP! …smashed Keller in the head with it to make sure he was staying down. Sharp was still flat on his back, but Vincent stomped him once for good luck anyway. Alias, coming out of the fog, suddenly realized what had happened to him, and he began kicking wildly, but Sharc avoided the worst of it and nailed Alias in the jaw with a forearm. Vincent handed Sharc the microphone. "Alias," Sharc grunted. "Next week. You versus me." "I'll kill you!" Alias roared. Sharc didn't even flinch at the threat. "A long time ago in a federation far, far away, Sharc and Alias met under some unfortunate circumstances. Unfortunately for me, I had a bullet in my leg. So, Alias... When we meet next week. How good do you think you'll do with a bullet in your leg?" The crowd was dead silent. Sharc lifted up his sweatshirt to reveal a gun in the waistband of his jeans. Slowly, he removed the weapon from his waistline, and like something out of a nightmare, he put it to Alias' right leg. His right index finger on the trigger of the revolver, Sharc cocked the hammer of the gun with his thumb. Alias eyed the gun, then Sharc's empty eyes. Black. For several seconds, viewers saw nothing but a black screen, somewhat akin to the closing moments of "The Sopranos" series finale from a couple years back. Then, the ACW copyright briefly showed at the bottom of the screen. Leaving you wondering... What the hell happened to Alias?
