Jay Smash Real Name: Jay Samati Birthdate: February 16, 1974 Hometown: Summerville, New Jersey Height: 6'8'' Weight: 295 lbs. Nicknames: Smash or Big Jay Status: Face Manager(s): Ralph Current Teams: No Team Entrance Music: Raise Up-Saliva Wrestling Style: Hardcore-Highflying style Signature Moves: 1.Smash Attack=Swinging Jaw Breaker from top turnbuckle 2.Springboard Frogsplash Set Up Move: Vitamin Jay (grabs opponent from behind with his right forearm around his opponent's neck. He grabs his opponent by the left side with his left arm, and swings opponent around from the right, dropping the opponent foward into a bulldog like position. ) Finisher: Smash Landing (Faces opponent, and gets him into a Pedigree position. He then raises his opponent by the arms, with his opponents legs right over Jay Smash's head. Jay Smash drops down with his opponent, slamming his opponent to the ground face first. (Also a modified version of a facebuster)) ACW Title History: No Titles(yet) ACW Award History: No Awards(yet) Other Federations: OPW, XOW, XIW, POW, ICW, IcW, XPW, WCW, AOW, NeWave, NeWave:2, NeWave: 3, CWFW, WCWF, WWF Fight Club, WOHM, and XWE Other Federation Awards: OPW-World/Hardcore, XOW-World/Hardcore/Tag Team, XIW-European/Tag Team, IcW-Extreme/IC, HWF-Extreme/US, NeWave-Continental, NeWave:2-US, WCWF-Tag Team/TV, WWF Fight Club-IC/TV, WOHM-World Handler Information Handler Name: Jay Smash AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: no MSN Biography My life is not simple to tell. But it is the only story I love to tell, because it's the only one I am unable to find the ending too. This story does not end until I am gone, and even then, I hope to leave a legacy behind. To begin, I was born on February 16, 1974 in Secaucus, New Jersey. To my knowledge, I was raised my my mother Anne V. Samati, and father James V. Samati, until I was 2 years old, when my mother had another child, killed with my father during a horrible blizzard, while driving in our beat up truck. I lived with my mother until I was ten-years-old, and at that point, my mother had been diagnosed with cancer. She died before my eleventh birthday, and I was left on my own. Of course, with the great education I had up to that point, I knew my way around, and learned everything I could from my mom before she passed away. I took off trying to find someone I could trust to take care of me, and a job that would pay a child that was only eleven. I worked at a Holiday Inn. where I spent my days cleaning dishes in the kitchen. A few years later, I met a man by the name of Brad Zymberman who stayed at the hotel for a month on business. He was a wrestling trainer, staying at the hotel for free, payed by the company he was working for. Later that year, around my 15th birthday, Zymberman was finished with his business at the hotel, and since we became such good friends, I took a shot at asking him if I could go with him. It took a while to get an answer out of him, but finally, when the word 'yes' came out of his mouth, I knew that I would be starting a whole new life, again. I lived in the guest house at Zymberman's home and became the towel boy for the wrestlers he trained. He told me that wrestling would be too dangerous, every time I asked to try out. But his answers to my pleading never changed. That's when I entered the ring anyway at the age of 16, given the chance to be a referee in a live match in front of hundreds of people in the small arena. I took a chair shot in that match, just being the referee, and I still stood up, with bloo dripping down my face and finished the match up for the two men facing eachother. The chair shot was later told to have been set up to hit me, after one of Zymberman's superstars, who did not like me very much came up with the plan. Zymberman saw how I got up from the chair shot and thought, maybe I can take being in dangerous situations. I got a match against the ass who made the plan to hit me with the chair, and I beat him with my first finishing move, the springboard frog splash, that brought money signs rolling in front of Zymberman's eyes. He became my manager for a while as I heading towards my pro wrestling career. That's when Zymberman left, without saying good-bye, and no reason of why he had left. So I felt once again, as if I were alone. I took my contract to be in my first pro wrestling federation, and I became known as Demon, the hell raiser in XIW and XOW. I was 20 at this time, so I had much to learn about wrestling, and this gothic thing wasn't working out. So I took a different approach, as the man known as Cobra, in XOW, until I left, to join OCW as the same character. I made my self known in the OCW for 4 years, and at 24, I took a break for a while, then returned as Jay Samati in WCWF. The federation closed, bringing me to WWF Fight Club, doing great, until I turned 26, and that federation closed down as well. It was a tough time in the wrestling business. Alot of people had enough superstars, or were getting ready to close down for good. That was until I joined CWFW, which was not doing so good, and took a jump into AOW, with the same owner and most of the same superstars from CWFW. When I turned 27, I found myself in both the AOW and HWF, trying to pull off being in both federations at one time. Time went by and HWF closed down, and AOW took a smart move, trying to stay alive by joining forces with another feeration known as ICW, forming the federation known as NeWave. NeWave had it's reign for a good while, and at this point I became known as Jay Smash, after a a big change in character. I became the man that everyone knows and loves today, the hell raiser, pain giver, high flyer, that lays the smashdown on all his opponents. NeWave was not doing so good, and their was trouble with getting AOW back, so instead, ICW re opened and the superstars that were left, including me took a chance in ICW with some new faces as well. The closing of ICW came so quick that nobody had enough time to get much done, and I was once again at the point of taking a break. During that break, I got married to Katie Smash, who I was dating for quite some time. After we had our baby, Jay Smash Jr., I went back into the wrestling business, joining NeWave: 2nd Coming. That was going well, until once again, the guys up stairs could not keep things running. Jay Smash could not handle moving from a different federation to another, and he was going mad. It was as if I was becoming the man he always told himself never to be. It took alot of work to get him back as himself again, and he joined XWE and WOHM as he was getting better until he could finally find a federation that he could stay in longer than any other fed in his life time. He is now at the age of 28, and his career in ACW has so far been a great ride, and hopefully not even close to over.
