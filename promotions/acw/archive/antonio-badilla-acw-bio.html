Antonio Badilla Biography Real Name Joseph Antonio Badilla Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday 6/26/83 Place of Birth Brooklyn, New York Height 5'11 Weight 248 Eye Color Hazel Biography "So the people wanna know somethin' 'bout Tony Badilla? Wanna know that he grew up rough in the ghettos of bushwick? Everybody wanna know somethin' right? Lemme ask you, if it mattered so much, where were you then, where were you when I was bustin' ass in the gym, and where the hell were you when I was making my paper in the dimly light independant New York circuit. That's right, you weren't there and you couldn't be bothered to be there, so Tony Badilla? He ain't got time to spend a minute explaining himself to the likes of you. na'mean?"
