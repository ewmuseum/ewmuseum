Darius Daniel Biography Real Name Chase [name withheld] Nicknames Dark Spirit; The Manhattan Emperor; Feral Hulk Hometown New York City, New York Birthday March 12, 1980 Place of Birth New York City, New York Height 6'7 Weight 311 Blood Type O- Eye Color Brown Biography The newest addition to the Sect of Black Wisdom, Darius Daniel teamed extensively with Horace Tully in Japan and takes Ethan Knight's place as the standard partner of 'The Adamant' in All-Star Championship Wrestling. How Darius was 'turned' by the diabolical Bruce Shanahan and his cloak-and-dagger organization to their foul cause isn't clear. Obviously VJ preyed on some inadequacy or doubt lurking in the psyche of the man called 'Dark Spirit' and managed to bring him into the fold. Daniel has made little known about his past except that he grew up in a privileged area of Manhattan in New York, growing up as a wrestling fan who wanted a part in the business. Always a stand-out athlete, the transition was natural for Darius Daniel and he soon went overseas to learn his lessons 'the hard way', in the rings and dojos of Japan. Team The Sect of Black Wisdom Past Teams & Groups CONCUSSION (w/ Horace Tully in Japan) Wrestling Style Puroresu-styler power grappler. Share some of the stylings of Horace Tully in that he likes spirited exchanges of strikes, but the bread-and-butter of his offense is his power game, slamming and generaly throwing around opponents which are generally much smaller than him. Moves and fights like Hernandez of LAX, moving with uncanny agility and balance for a man of his size though with a tendency to remain on his feet and keep the match grounded. One of the most fearsome aspects of Darius Daniel is the feral rage that can consume him in considerably stressful situations, particularly if he's cut open and sees his own blood. In these cases he descends into a rampaging state, like that of a wounded and corned animal, seeing red and using every ounce of his considerable strength to lay waste to everyone he views as an adversary. His strikes are stronger and he's dangerous to be in the ring with if you value your career and well-being. The sight of his blood is a major catalyst for this and opponents will go out of their way to keep from shedding Darius' blood, lest his lariats take on a new level of ferocity and his already vicious backdrop driver becomes outright life-threatening. His already stiff wrestling style is elevated to greater heights and he will be like a rabid animal, impossible to rein in until the adrenaline wears off, at which time he may be exhausted from the energy expended during the outburst. He is by no means 'invincible' during these periods but his pain threshold is significantly higher and avoiding his wrath is advised until he winds down, at which time he's most vulnerable to his opponents. This crazed, animalistic state is what led to the moniker of 'Feral Hulk'. Finisher Move Backdrop driver. Brutal belly-to-back suplex that drops the opponent on their neck/shoulders. Typically done in Takeshi Morishima fashion and approaching more from the side as opposed to directly belly-to-back, but if in the midst of a fiery rampage or the desperation stage, Daniel will drop the unfortunate recipient directly on the crown of their head ala 'Dr. Death' Steve Williams' infamous Homicide Backdrops in mid '90's Japan. No one but the most resilient of the world's best superstars will ever escape that. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uWOdguvyNs Finisher Description Backdrop Driver Special Moves ---Signature Maneuvers--- Top-rope layout senton splash Dead-lift German Suplex. Daniel will lift the opponent off the ground and leave them suspended for a moment or two, then hoist them up further and fall back into a German suplex. He sometimes holds the bridge for a pin attempt, but predominantly releases on impact. 0:22 mark of http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lAu5SuC0A20 Sky High Crash -- Big waist-lifted slam. Daniel will bend over and wrap one arm around the victim's waist or even thighs, hoisting them high up in the air and slamming them viciously to the mat. Also seen as Takeshi Rikio's Musou. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqJQs65U-8w Spiral Bomb - Hoists opponent into a powerbomb, rotates around several times, and sits down with a crushing powerbomb. Break And Ride - Darius grasps the would-be victim in a cobra clutch, hoists them into the air, and brings them down over one knee in a backbreaker. He usually finishes by maintaining the hold, spinning around a few times, and whipping the opponent most of the way across the ring in a display of power. Curbstomp - With the opponent facedown on the mat, Darius grasps their arms and pulls up so that the opponent's head is suspended several inches off the mat. He then takes one big foot and stomps their head into the mat. Hell-evator - Darius hoists his opponent into a powerbomb position, then grabs the opponent's head and tucks it into their chest. He then falls back, throwing them overhead so that they land on their upper back/neck or head/neck. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oi_1BccV5GY Lariat - Like Tully, Darius will often attempt to polish off tired and battered opposition with a swift and powerful lariat. In the midst of his notorious rampages, bellowing like a bear, Darius may utterly level his opponents with decapitating short-arm lariats that are almost difficult to watch, to say nothing of what happens if he gets a running start....
