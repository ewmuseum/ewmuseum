Big E. Stark Biography Real Name Tyrone Edward Stark Nicknames Big E. Hometown Yonkers, NY Birthday December 10th, 1980 (age 30) Place of Birth Manhasset, NY Height 6'2" Weight 287 Blood Type A+ Eye Color Brown Biography Alignment and Personality: Despite being about seven years older than Mach 2, he’s the rookie of the duo, having only logged about two years, about to enter his third as of this writing. Formerly a defensive lineman for his college football team, went down with a knee injury that curbed his career. After some hard times, he found wrestling as a new outlet. Soft-spoken, generally fun-loving type guy that’s also protective of his friends, hard to make angry. Loves having a good time, quick with a joke. Description: African-American, some muscle, but mostly a portly guy. Think a slightly younger Mick Foley for body type when he was in his prime. Wears a black wrestling singlet with “BIG E.” on the front in blue and “STARK” on the back, same color. Black wrist tape, black boots. When he gets fired up, the straps come down. -Mach 2 is a good friend of both former ACW standouts and former ACW Heavyweight Champions Seymour Almasy and Andy Sharp. Andy liked what he saw with the kid and got him a spot training in Almasy’s now-defunct Almasy Office. From there, he learned the finer points of “throwing yourself at people” while bolstering a fairly decent counter game. -After wowing audiences for several months within ACW as part of the First Airbourne with Andy Sharp, took time off following a brutal beatdown by the Heirs of Wrestling and Max Danger and did a few tours with Japanese promotion, Kyoto PRO. He added some striking and ground wrestling ability to his game to become a more well-rounded opponent. -During a recent tour with New York’s Finest, the fed that Mach 2 once called home, he met Big E. Stark, an older, but imposing rookie that showed some heart and lots of promise. Giving him the opportunity that Andy Sharp once gave him, Mach 2 made an offer to become Big E.’s tag partner and he accepted. Working under a temporary 90-day trial contract, Big E. Stark and Mach 2, along with Mach 2’s girlfriend, Lettie Rios, have vowed to take ACW by storm and whoop a lot of ass doing it, fulfilling what the First Airbourne could not do. Win the ACW Tag Team Championships. As of the new year, Big E. Smalls impressed the ACW brass enough to have been signed on full-time. Other Feds New York's Finest Other Fed Titles New York's Finest Heavyweight Champion x1 Team Big E. Smalls w/Mach 2, Lettie Rios Wrestling Style As something of a rookie in the business, he’s at least gotten all the basics down. As the power half of the team, his specialty is coming into the ring and playing clean-up duty, playing the Hernandez to Mach 2’s Homicide of LAX fame. He’s like a mini-tank during his more dominant spells. Due to his former athletic background, he can surprise some people with aerial moves from time to time.
