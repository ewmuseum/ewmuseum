End Game Match The large-form battle royale of ACW, whereby the first and last entrants are decided with a very special The Beginning of the End Match to open the night. The winner of said match becomes the last entrant in the End Game Match and the loser becomes the first entrant. The fourth place finisher procures a shot at the ACW Scorpion Fighting Title, while the third and second place finishers face off in a one-on-one match to decide a number one contender for the Spirit Of ACW Title. The winner traditionally gets a shot at the ACW World Title, at the biggest stage of them all -- LEGENDS! 2005 Winner: Seymour Almasy Runner-Ups: Alias (2nd), Rook Black (3rd), 'Cocky' Craig Miles (4th) 2009 Winner: Shawn Jessica Hart Runner-Ups: Khristain Keller (2nd), Midnight Cowboy (3rd), Omega (4th) 2010 Winner: Keith Scott Zimmerman Runner-Ups: Andy Sharp (2nd), Alias (3rd), Fejona Min (4th) King of Ages Tournament The marquee tournament of ACW, it is an event that has made notable wrestlers into ACW’s top superstars. The tournament has propelled its winners to the next level of the company and has existed as both a 16 and 20 person tournament. Starting from 2010, the winner will gain a reward called the King's Choice: it can literally be used for any purpose. It's up to the beholder to decide. 2003 Winner: Khristain Keller Beaten Finalist: 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs Beaten Semi-Finalists: Janitor Morris, Dante Inferno Prize: #1 Contendership to the ACW World Title 2004 Winner: God's Forgotten Son Beaten Finalist: Quinton May Beaten Semi-Finalists: SilverHAWK, El Gato Negro Prize: ACW United States Title + #1 Contendership to the ACW World Title 2010 Winner: Jesse Ramey Beaten Finalists: Z, Keith Scott Zimmerman Beaten Semi-Finalists: Kellen 'Machine' Masters, Buddy Saxon Prize: King's Choice Scorpion Fight Night Challenge Absolutely anything goes. Falls count anywhere, literally. Involving EVERYONE on the roster, the Scorpion Fight Night Challenge lasts for two straight hours, and is the showcase event of the Scorpion Fighting division with the Scorpion Fighting Championship on the line. Every ten minutes, a target’s name will be randomly generated and those targets are aptly named because a fall on them will gain their competitor two points. The flipside of being hunted as the target is that you will gain two points if you procure a fall. It will be interesting what violent and unpredictable wrinkles are added in the future. 2009 Winner: Alias (11 points) Runner-Ups: Brian Spaes & Max Danger (tied for 2nd), Violence Jack (4th) 2010 Winner: Omega (14 points) Runner-Ups: Fejona Min (2nd), 'The Alpha Dog' Mark Weiler (3rd), Jesse Ramey (4th)
