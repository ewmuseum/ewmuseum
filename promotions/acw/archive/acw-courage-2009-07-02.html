AFTER LAST WEEK’S COURAGE. Brian Spaes and Trevor Wilson stood in the wrestler’s parking lot. There were only a few random cars out, but most of the workers already went back to their home/hotel for the night. There was a thin mist out, exceedingly humid and hazy given the late night in Montreal. Trevor was hanging back, leaning up against Brian’s silver truck. Brian lit up a cigarette and took a few cursory puffs. He took off his baseball hat and scratched his black and gray hair as he was composing his thoughts. In the darkness, a shadow imposed by the few security lights shone on the asphalt. “It’s you isn’t it.” The shadow nodded affirmatively. "But you're--" Wilson started, surprised by this sudden cloak and dagger meeting. "I know," said the shadows. “Good. I never thought I would come and make a deal with you, but I appreciate you hearing me out. I know you feel the same way about me. It’s awkward doing this like we are, but it’s probably for the best so we don’t have them interrupting, the self appointed sheriffs and them.” Brian’s fingers trembled as he held up his Camel to his mouth. The revolution though? It had very much arrived. Sadly the bag and the belt had already been taken to the car. “Oh, snagglefrass.” “It’s really no secret that you would hold every title, if you could.” Alias continued to ignore Danger, as he began to stride closer and closer to the Amazing One, “You’re a greedy man, and greed is good... even if it means a little blood on your hands. Like Marcus out there, like Seymour at Legends... Isn’t that right, Danger?” “You seem to know a lot about a man that you’ve never gotten a chance to know.” Danger said coyly, as Alias forebodingly got closer to him, and he didn’t even twitch. The two drunk meat shields on either side of his helped his confidence. It was true; both men had been on a million minutes of television, within the same shows as each other. Now in ACW and FWO, as well. Yet still, if you asked either what the name of the daughter was of the man in front of them, they’d be at a loss. “I don’t know a lot of things about you.” Alias agreed, “But if it’s one thing I do... it’s that you’re capable of what was done to Almasy.” “Yeah, I am.” Danger said with a grin, as he had all the reason to have put Almasy away and that reason was all gold. “But you didn’t do it.” Alias finished the thought, knowing full well that as much as he wasn’t a fan of Max Danger... he wouldn’t have done, what had been done. “No... no I didn’t.” The King of ACW said, a shrug of his shoulder, Armsy shrugging it’s wrist, “Not that I’m upset, though, cause it certainly shot me right to the top of ACW with minimal effort.” “You hold onto that title... while you can.” Alias said, shifting away from the door, before Danger went to run out into the rain towards his car, “Because I have a Scorpion on my side that says I’ll be coming for it faster then you think.” Max Danger('s Assistant) & Khristain Keller vs. Brian Spaes “Seems to be doing his job quite well.” Stephen Roy said, a smile on his face. He wasn’t quite familiar with the day to day politics of this federation which he and his brother were the main shareholders of, and he liked it that way. Still, as Keller brought Spaes down with a belly-to-back suplex with a splash into the canvas, he found it entertaining. In fact the whole night had been nothing short of spectacular. “This is what we brought him in for, to fight Spaes?”, shot in Boyd with a certain amount of surprise at this revelation. “And anyone else who might try to stir things up. Like I used to do.” SilverHAWK replied with a tinge of nostalgia as the wrestling match continued in the rain. “Keller is… immoralistic.” “Immoralistic?” Nathan Roy asked with a grin. English might not be his first language, but he knew this wasn’t a word. Keller had tried to keep the back synch locked in, and follow it up with a few choice elbows to the back of the head of Spaes, but Brian shot out a heavy hand that cracked Keller across the side of the head and stumbled him away. The crowd was eating it all up. “He prides himself on not having much in the way of moral standards. Says he gets in the way of getting shit done. Frankly, sometimes, I have to agree.” SilverHAWK replied to the gathered governing board of shareholders around him. “Aaron…” James Boyd replied back to him, surprised once again. Even against Ethan Winters they wouldn’t have brought in a sociopath to do the dirty jobs. “James, I know what you’re going to say.” Hawk noted, addressing this moment with seriousness, “But if we’re going to throw someone into the fire, into something they might not walk away from unscarred, better Keller then Andy… or Alias.” Hawk, the Roys, Bishop and Boyd thought for a moment underneath their large tented area, and this was true. The crowd roared as Keller and Spaes locked up in the middle of the ring. “You’ve got a point.” The Devil of ACW and the Bastard King of Ages. Who do they cheer, when they cheer? Or do they cheer for the fight in ACW, match itself more then the men in it? Or do they cheer for the man seemingly fighting for ACW, as opposed to the man fighting for an ideal? The rain soaked thousands in attendance barely heard the music at first. DJ Champion had to pack up his equipment after the introduction of Brian Spaes… he assumed that was the final man in the match, and that he was fighting alone tonight, as Trevor Wilson nursed his wounds and cheered him on from within the scrum of Minutemen fans. But no, there was one more entrance it seemed, and the Sam Roberts Band were doing the honors, all the way over on the right side of the Festival… with speakers cranked to eleven. Keller forced Spaes into the corner, and shoving an elbow into his throat, Keller seethed, “Too bad you’re alone tonight, you’re boyfriend doesn’t seem to want to save you.” But Spaes held a defiant look on his face, “Luckily… the Minutemen… are more then two.” Who was this mystery man? The same man now running through the crowd and towards the ring? It was the same man who had met with Spaes and Wilson a week before, after last weeks Courage. The same man that SilverHAWK watched with shock, as he hopped the barrier. The crowd roared for a moment, hardly believing what they were seeing, as well. Then all began falling into place. The fight that brought mutual admiration. The shared outlook for the feds responsibilities for itself. The growing weight of the bureaucracy around them, which once welcomed at least one of them with open arms. This man was a Champion. He was the Heart and Soul of ACW. And he was Brian Spaes partner for tonight. “Please, allow me to introduce myself…” A L I A S had joined the Minutemen. Or so it seemed. At first the crowd cheered, not knowing which of his enemies he’d go after. But Keller, oh he knew. Separating from Spaes in the corner, Keller quickly rolled from the ring, and allowed Alias to roll in… the crowd was at a moment confused and disappointed. They wanted the match to continue without a hitch. Keller though, wouldn’t let Alias get his fight… and he was also smart enough to avoid a 2-on-1. As the crowd reaction slowly shifted to one of uncertainty, quiet anger and surprise… Keller looked at Alias, then toward Hawk’s position deep in the ring-side crowd, then smiled and shook his head. “You get in this ring, you son of a bitch!” Alias roared, once again having a face-to-face match with Keller kept from him. It’s flawed.” "I was brought back to save ACW, do you even realize that you ignorant fuck?!”, continued Keller with an incredulous tone, “Protect what works! I was supposed to save you…” The last sentence was suddenly dropped in a hushed, frustrated tone. Keller hadn’t even meant to say it. Luckily… “Speak up, son.”, Alias didn’t hear Keller, and growled back condescendedly through gritted teeth. Keller shook his head, and began to walk away, “I said FUCK YOU.” The lightning struck, illuminating the area, and etching harsh light on the faces of both. Their war would continue, but not tonight. Both he and Spaes grabbed one of Alias’s wrists. Before raising them, as the thunder roared over their heads along with the crowd in a confused, bitter, surprised and jubilant mixture of cheering and jeering. The Minutemen were one stronger. It had arrived with a screaming roar from the Original Pulp Hero, who was soon after joined by Brian Spaes and Trevor Wilson. Even as the papers they held slowly soaked in the rain, their mission didn’t seem to slow. SilverHAWK, Joe Bishop, Steven and Nathan Roy, they stood in shock. James Boyd softly grinned to himself. How was this happening… how did it become so big to stop from happening, so suddenly? Were where the signs of the revolution? Certainly now, they were all through the ACW Montreal Wrestling Festival. Brian Spaes leaned over the top rope and took a flier from Michel at ringside, the crowd were and raucous, uproarious, but... some... some were loving this. It was the start of something very big for ACW. How big, was something that had to be waited for to be seen… in terms of who would fight back, when there supposed savior in Alias wasn’t on the side of ‘right’ to save ACW from itself again. Though maybe he was. Wilson stood firmly in the center of the ring, Spirit of ACW around his waist, as he truly held the spirit of the moment. Both Spaes and Alias looked at the red paper, the white text, the blue marker. Water dropping on it with every second, but not washing away it’s potential. "It's a manifesto, sure," Spaes said to Sheffield, "But..." "It says everything it needs to say." The Heart and Soul of ACW finished his sentence.
