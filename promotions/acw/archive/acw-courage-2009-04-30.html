He was on his way then, back to tip-toeing down the hallway, even though they were the first two people he’d encountered since he’d started fifteen minutes ago. Finally, however, he had reached his destination. He put his ear to the door, listening for any kind of movement inside. Luckily, the target of this mission was Kelly Masters, and he was in the ring doing battle for the Spirit of ACW. Which, come to think of it, annoyed Max. Why’d he get a title shot? “Grumble, grumble,” went Danger. He took a deep breath, though, ‘cause he had a mission to complete. He had to rescue Armsy McDanger from the evil clutches of The Machine. Open the door with just enough run for him to slide inside, Max squeezed himself in and presed himself against the wall, quietly shutting the door behind him. Next, well, he just picked up a chair and slammed it into it. Not very ninja-like, but desperate times called for desperate measures. But maybe it wasn’t the wisest move, because he heard the door opening then and had to think fast. So he hid in the locker right next to Kelly’s. Through the slots, Max could see who entered: Johnny Long in a towel. “Pfft! It’s just him.” said the Danger Man out loud. Long jumped up off the bench and looked around frantically, “Who’s in here!?” Then a small smile crept onto his face, He grabbed onto his towel like he could make it drop in a second if need be. “Is it one of my fans? It’s okay, baby, you can come out. I won’t bite... hard.” But no one emerged, making Mr. All Night Long frown. He’d been hit in the head a lot since joining up with ACW, so maybe he was just hearing things. He shrugged before walking over to his locker. The one Max was in. Thinking fast, well, okay, Danger actually was just looking at his iPhone and opened the door. It just happened to be the exact moment Johnny was standing in front of it. WHAM! The door smacked Long right in the face. He remained standing for a moment before ever-so-slowly falling over like a stiff board. Hey lay on the ground at Max’s feet, who looked up from the DangerPhone, then went right back to it. He, however, couldn’t resist himself from placing a foot on Johnny’s chest and mouthing, “One, two, three.” In his mind, he had defeated him four times in one night, could Kelly Masters say that? “NO HE COULDN’T!” shouted the King of Submission out loud for whatever reason, then he kicked the locker that he thought was Kelly’s. He was able to open it then, but all he found was clothes hanging inside. That’s when an angry Danger yanked the towel off an unconcious Johnny Long and did this: Max stood back and took a picture with his DangerPhone. He then said, “You’re not going to get away with this, Kelly Masters.” 5-Man Spirit of ACW Championship Elimination Match (Cont.) Now, back to your regular scheduled programming. Update, Steve Knox still has yet to show his face. So much for the fans bringing him to justice. Inside the ring, Jimmy Cain continued on his malicious run. He slammed the ‘Ginsu Shredder’ into Sharp’s midsection (luckily Andy wore a singlet) and rocked him with a crisp, high angle DDT. Cain, his appetite for violence not quite satisfied, stepped over the prone body and locked on a camel clutch, of course, he also shook and cursed at Sharp. Or not because Sharp rotated with the ‘plex and landed on his feet. What can I say, he’s an athletic freak. Andy hit the ropes, and met the then standing Masters with a leaping head kick that landed squarely. Mr. All-Star dropped onto Masters for the cover. One! Two! THREE! Jimmy didn’t like flips or cartwheels. “Knock that flippy, monkey shit off!” he screamed at Andy, who was quite pleased with himself. By now, Masters was back up to his feet and the showdown commenced. The three men all exchanged quick looks. Hey, who knows, maybe they’d form some alliances. Ha, wrong. Jimmy moved in toward Sharp, and Kelly moved at Jimmy. With Cain and Sharp locked into a collar and elbow tie-up, Masters burst between the two. Right hand for Sharp, left hand for Cain.
