Geo Vacton Real Name: Geoff Vacton Birthdate: May 20, 1976 Hometown: Blackwood, New Jersey, USA Height: 6'2 Weight: 235 lbs. Nicknames: Geo Status: Neutral Manager(s): Magick Man Current Teams: Team Resitance Entrance Music: 'TNT' by AC/DC Wrestling Style: Hardcore/High Risk Signature Moves: “The Vactonation” (Knock-out suplex into a dragon sleeper) Set Up Move: ( ) Finisher: Geocide (Twist of Fate / what some who are misinformed would call the Spine Doctor, but they would be wrong because the Spin Doctor is not the same as the Twist of Fate. It's very different.) ACW Title History: United States Championship (End Game 12/22/02) ACW Award History: Other Federations: zTw (1), tHw, zTw (2), 21w, ukw Other Federation Awards: zTw Aluminum Championship Handler Information Handler Name: George AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Biography (Before Pro) Not much is really known about Geo before he became pro. It's known that his father noticed his intrest in wrestling, and provided him with confidence to do his best in the ring. He even paid to have a trainer come to their house and train him with mat skills, and tought him how to take falls on their mats in the basement. When Geo got older, he started seriously training in the gyms, working on the mats with other students and instructors. He took it pretty seriously, but was known to crack jokes about everything. 'Eh, I don't much like that possition. What else do we have the on the (ah!)?' He was quoted to say when learning how to execute moves in the 'Tree of woe.' In 1999 he got his first break into the wrestling industry wrestling in small indy feds, but he would find his really success story to begin when he was approached by a man who called him Dr. Zyber. (Career History) 'zTw, zTw, zTw.' It was all the rage, chant the name of the federation while the wrestlers were preforming high risk and dangerous moves. And fighting in matches like the infamous 'Inferno Match' with Mr. Haunt was certainly filled with 'dangerous moves.' Moves that could prove fatal even. As Geo climbed to the top of zTw, he would be stalled by the likes of Kid Xtreme, who was on his own drive and took things much further than they ever needed to be by stabbing Geo with a sword in one match, luckily not leaving much of a scar on his right arm. Geo would him back, however, when he called for the help of 'Quizbo 'the gay clown'' to drop a sang bad from on top of the arena onto Kid Xtreme. But at the height of his success in zTw, the company closed down. As it usualy happens, birds of the same type fly together. This was no different, although it didn't exactly work out. Lionheart, one of zTw's biggest names, came to Geo and told him about a company called 'tHw: totally HARDCORE wrestling.' This sounded good to Geo, and him and Lionheart both sent in applications to tHw. Geo and Lionheart were both accepted, but Lionheart was subjected to injury, and so Geo flew solo throughout all of his tHw career. There isn't much to scream about in Geo's career in tHw anyway, but he did find his way staggering to the top, like it happens so often, just as the company was reaching it's downfall. Geo wrestled the mainevent of tHw's new show 'Tuesday Morning Uproar' with Tommy Dreamer, a moment Geo would remember forever as the biggest moment in his career. Geo lost, but that didn't matter. He had wrestled tHw's very best, and that's all that did. He would go on to wrestle the likes of ArchAngel and more before tHw would hold it's last event. But tHw became only a bridge to further Geo's career. Geo got word that zTw was coming back, and so he waited. And waited, and waited. But finaly it came, monthes later he was contacted and showed up to wrestle in zTw's first show. He was told that he was chosen to be one of the 7 former zTw wrestlers to be brought back in for it's opening, including the likes of Quizbo and Maverick. It was here that Geo captured his first piece of gold, the zTw 'Sturdy Aluminum Championship.' Even if the title was something of a joke, Geo was very proud to hold it. But he could not enjoy his championship reign, since the company folded once more that very night. But Geo didn't die. No, he went on. Infact, he continued to move up. He was approached by members of the 21w organization and given a contract to wrestle. Geo wrestled several matches, and won nearly half of them. He wasn't taking a ladder to the top, but he was getting there facing the likes of 'Golden Glen Miller' and having a historical 'Rage Match' with Mr. Haunt, a match that would be considered a dream match by any zTw fan. It would be nice to report that Geo left 21w at good terms, but sadly that isn't true. Geo was fierd after a backstage arguement with 21w owner, exactly what was said to make him get fierd is unknown, but it left Geo with nowhere to go. Geo sort of started climbing down after 21w, finding the only place that would offer him work was the suffering ukw. Geo wrestled a few matches, and found some success with the crowd, and probably going to go on to achieve futher success since everyone seemed to be really into him, but backstage Geo knew that ukw was not for him. Going from 21w, a place where Geo didn't have to work hard to push a show because the company already had such talented workers working in it, to ukw where he had to give it absolutely everything to put a move over just to be able to wrestle the next week and hopefuly get better ratings, wasn't working for him. So he quit, the first time he had ever quit in his life. After ukw, Geo stopped wrestling. He decided he should take it easy, just sit back and watch what everyone else is doing. But he couldn't stay put forever and he isn't made for anything but wrestling and entertaining. So after a bit of a hiatus, Vacton returned to the ring to wrestle in ACW, and that's where his story waits to be futher written. Experience Archives Mama Magick One more day... The terrible 53s?
