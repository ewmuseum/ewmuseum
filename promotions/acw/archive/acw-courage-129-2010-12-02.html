A C W THE EMPIRE STRIKES BACK COURAGE 129 ¤ 02 DECEMBER 2010 NELSON MANDELA FORUM, FLORENCE, ITALY INTRODUCTION PRE-SHOW ¦ HOUR ONE ¦ HOUR TWO Introduction END GAME 2010 is on the horizon, and everybody's getting ready for the battle of a lifetime. For two men, this battle will see one of them become the supreme ruler of everything that ACW stands for. Jeremy Hunt's got his forces all riled up, and ready to fight to the death. SilverHAWK may be a tiny bit behind, but he has wisdom and experience of past wars on his side. The man's a survivor, and he'll fight until his very last breath. All it'll take for ultimate victory, perhaps... is one mistake. But as the sound of drums get louder and the storm clouds gathering, do both Jeremy Hunt and SilverHAWK have their contigencies planned out? Can Hunt's avatars -- Khristain Keller and God's Forgotten Son -- stay on the same page long enough to stick it to HAWK? Can SilverHAWK's avatars -- Keith Scott Zimmerman and Christopher Sheffield -- continue to rock it and plant the seeds for triumph? Last but not least: can GoldenHAWK step up to the plate and attain victory for his dad? Only time... will tell. Next: PRE-SHOW. ---- IN THE DARK~! IN THE DARK #1: The SOBs vs. Union O' MonteCarl Having seen their renaissance thwarted by squabbles and inability to capitalise, The SOBs kicked off the night's festivities by storming down to the ring and showing mean faces to the capacity crowd. In turn, a majority of the fans jeered the ever-living crap out of the brothers from Belfast. But they went crazy for one of the newest acquisitions in ACW: the tandem of Monte Young & Carl Kimball (a.ka. the Union O' MonteCarl), who had been on the free market for a while after their departure from WCCW (which just so happened to be the latest additions to ACW's development territory family). The unique thing about Young & Kimball was that they were a legally married couple, and Kimball was not ashamed of that fact -- especially judging by the 'I'm Married To Him! ' shirt he was wearing as he strode down to the ring on Monte's right hand side. The fans laughed and cheered, even as Monte pled with his partner to remove the shirt. Sheamus didn't find the bantering and the friendly bickering between Carl and Monte a single bit endearing, and thus engineered to kick off the match with a ferocious double clothesline once his opponents set foot into the ring! And while the referee was able to garner control of the ruckus that developed shortly after, Monte and Carl never quite recovered from that. Selby and Sheamus put their differences aside to impose their will on the match, constantly exchanging tags and eventually isolating Carl Kimball. By that stage, Monte Young had only spent a grand total of 75 seconds as the legal man for his team, and was dying for a tag to get his mitts on either O'Brien brother. It almost didn't happen, especially after Selby destroyed Carl with a spinebuster slam. Kimball kicked out at the death, however, and Selby decided to tag his brother in. Sheamus got too cocky, thinking he was moments away from victory... and within a flash, Sheamus found himself in the clutches of a sleeper hold! Carl Kimball had somehow managed to slip out of a suplex attempt and slapped on the submission move, only to let go of Sheamus a while later. Why? Simple. Sheamus had been weakened long enough for Kimball to make the tag. Like the proverbial house of fire, Monte Young kicked arse upon charging into the ring and got the fans on their feet. He very nearly got the victory after a snap running DDT on Selby, but Sheamus made the save. Monte continued, nevertheless, his assault on Selby... but Sheamus had one last trick up his sleeve. A blind tag as his brother came off the ropes proved vital, for Selby fell victim to Monte's powerslam right after. The resulting pinfall cover didn't count, to Monte's bewilderment. Sheamus? He snuck into the ring, got the jump on Young and held on to his surprise victory roll long enough for the win. A solid win for The SOBs, and a tough loss for the married duo Young and Kimball. The latter tried to console his reluctant spouse after the match. Monte resisted at first, but relented after much urging by the fans who exploded in cheers when Monte and Carl hugged it out. Aww, how sweet. IN THE DARK #2: Fight Man vs. Vance Bootlier Two weeks ago, Vance Bootlier made his ACW debut and promptly proceeded to make himself supremely hated. He sang the praises of Dean Denton and insulted Keith Scott Zimmerman. Clearly, Vance -- who went on to get torn apart by Quintana Rogerio Santiago -- was a fan of the bad things and bad guys in professional wrestling. And clearly, Vance hadn't learned anything from that fateful night two weeks ago. Vance went off on a rant before his match begun, which had the fans massively outraged. Amongst other things, Vance spoke about the following; 1. Christopher Sheffield being overrated. 2. Jeremy Hunt being very handsome. 3. Ryan Billows being the best pound-for-pound performer in ACW. And finally... 4. Fight Man being the stupidest thing in ACW in its entire history. It was fitting, then, that Fight Man himself came out next. The Hardcore Hero received a standing ovation, which made Vance Bootlier extremely furious. Things got worse for Vance when it was announced that the match that was about to take place was to be contested under Scorpion Fighting Rules. Before Bootlier could even process that information, Fight Man took control of the match -- by way of smashing a steel chair into the back of Vance's head! Bootlier did fight back, scoring a number of crunching blows to the Hardcore Hero's face. Fight Man hulked up after each blow, however, and it didn't take long for him to have Vance Bootlier tapping out. How did that happen? Simple! Fight Man somehow conjured up a leather belt and choked Vance with it. Oh, how the fans loved that. Fight Man celebrated with the fans after the match, leaving Bootlier to gasp for air in the ring with another defeat to suck on. Two straight defeats, both by tapping out. Oh dear. IN THE DARK #3: MIS vs. Carrachio Salfuego And the night's final IN THE DARK~! match-up featured -- much to the crowd's delight -- Carrachio Salfuego! Having come up just a little bit short in his match the week before, Salfuego was hoping to get right back on track. Just as Salfuego was able to turn the tables on MIS with a punishing flapjack four minutes into the match, none other than Booster Reid himself popped up over the security barricade. Booster was dressed in street clothes and immediately set about to catch Carrachio's attention. The Madrid Boy tried to ignore his new rival and concentrated on the matter at hand, but MIS was able to very nearly pull off the upset by catching Carrachio in a small package! Salfuego survived, and went back to work on attaining control of the match. Which he eventually did, after systematically absorbing all of MIS's offense and throwing back some of his own. Booster, happy with his dirty work, high-tailed out of ringside with the fans jeering him roundly. Z folded his arms across his chest and set his eyes on his group, seated down in front of him. "Smoke and mirrors. Illusions. Misdirection is a big part of who I am and what I'm trying to do here. "And I have faith that tonight, both of them will fare equally well in their respective missions. Go on, you two. Do me proud. Do yourselves proud. Do the cabal proud. And raise some hell along the way!" The new fearsome twosome bumped fists and duly went on their way, amidst some hushed murmuring from the rest of the tribe. Once Sharp and Kati exited the room, the smile on Z's face evaporated, and he began scanning the rest of the group with his calculating and mysterious eyes. "Fejona Min. Stand up." Uh oh. Even though you had ties with Jeremy 'El Fucko' Hunt, I knew I could convince you that working with me was the better choice. And you've done well so far. That's just it, though. Or else, there will be dire consequences. And you, of all people, will know that I'm not kidding. Not when we're so close... to the end of phase one." A blanket silence fell over the crowd in the room. Fejona Min swallowed her pride and nodded her head, thereby noting her leader's criticism. Z waved his hand dismissively a few seconds later, prompting Fejona to shuffle out of the room. She knew what she had to do now. Z's words stung, but it was fact. And Min didn't like being reminded of her failures. The Caped Crusader knew Fejona would find a way to make things right. But he had other people to pick at first. "Okay. That's done. Malk; truly abysmal performance last week. You'd better improve this week, or I'll unleash a drunk Kesavan on your ass. Iceman; you lost to a f**king rookie last week. And we need to continue to strike from the shadows. SilverHAWK and Hunt can continue their pissing match all they want, while trying to figure out what *I* have in store. As long as we have the element of unpredictability, we're in a strong position. But as long as you guys keep losing, our reputation takes a dive. Do you understand what's at stake here? Do you get it?" A defiant glare from every single person remaining in the room was all Z needed to know that his message had gotten through. And with that, the Dastardly Mastermind could breathe a little easier. "Gravy. Now, let's make this a night to remember!" Z shouted, fists all clenched up. The rest of the tribe shot to their feet and in the spirit of the moment, bumped fists with each other. The Caped Crusader nodded, before motioning for one Mark Shields to come over to him. As the gang filed out of the room, ready to remedy last week's so-so affair and set off on their respective tasks, Z and Mark Shields sat down in one corner of the room, with the door left slightly ajar and Jack Harris standing guard, his hulking frame ready to scare the bejesus out of anybody who didn't have an invite. In the biggest victory he'd managed in years, and in the afterglow of the moment "Keller..." grumbled a voice. Khristian turned to face Keith Scott Zimmerman who was slumped in the corner, microphone in hand. But when his eyes met Keller's -- Keith smiled. "You stupid, stupid man. What in the hell made you think...that the man SilverHAWK chose..." SMIRK~! (only at .7 but even so) "...was ME?" Keller looked at Keith like an arm had grown out of his forehead but that look on his face wouldn't be on it long. R U M B L E Khristain Keller whipped around, as the familiar chords of Link Wray’s guitar stylings literally rumbled through the arena along with the roars of the Italian crowd in attendance. Keith Scott Zimmerman might have been cheated out of saving ACW by himself but hell… it showed how much the kid had grown, when he could think on his feet enough that it didn’t really matter… just as long as ACW was saved from the likes of Jeremy Hunt and the likes of the King Shit of Fuck Mountain. Christopher Sheffield, the Original Pulp Hero, stepped out from backstage and cracked his knuckles. Keller’s face was one that Sheff’s fists where familiar with. Just as recently as COURAGE 127 they had battled, and tonight… in what was turning out to be the fourth match IN the BATTLE FOR ACW… SilverHAWK didn’t have to worry about the machinations of Z getting in the way, right? Hell, this was the Pulp Hero who had returned to ACW to save the clutches from Ethan Winters in 2003! As he began his walk towards Keller, the #1 Contender still frozen in a fighting position while the World Champion Keith Scott Zimmerman waited within the ring with mic in his hand and his wife by his side, Sheffield cracked his knuckles and began to say something. Before he was cut off by KSZ. “Sheff! Watch out!” The Only Wrestler That Matters' voice reveberated over the speakers upon the sight of Fejona Min run out from the backstage area, causing the Original Pulp Hero to spin and face the Cambodian Head-Kicking Machine (new one!). “The fuck is this, Min?!” Sheffield growled, immediately, his attention taken away from the battered Keller, and squarely on Min before she could reach the Pulp Hero. “Don’t tell me you’re *still* trying to tear me apart because I lasted longer in the Battle Royale then you did on COURAGE 126! Don’t tell me you’re just following orders sent from the ZED-head! I saved your life in that damned Stockholm Syndrome Match, at KING OF AGES 2010! You coulda been burnt to a crisp, but I took the brunt of the flames!” THOCK! Before Sheffield could say anymore, Keller had rushed up behind him and cracked him across the back with his forearm. KSZ, YOUR World Champ, had slipped from the ring as fast as possible though… the damage he had taken from his match with Keller not withstanding and looked to immediately go after Keller. Min took the chance to start her attack on the Pulp Hero, as he stumbled towards her. With a kick to the ribs, Sheffield roared back up and pushed the first official [Black] Scorpio Champion away from him with disgust. “And this is what I get in return?!” Sheffield was livid, and not just because HIS match to save ACW hadn’t even started yet. “Sneak attacks, stitches and a fist full of my own teeth! Get the hell away from me ‘til you’re ready to look me eye to eye and tell me the truth! Otherwise, go crawl back to where you came from!” Fejona advanced on Sheffield, as Keller and KSZ battled behind them… Allsion Lindum-Zimmerman could be seen looking around the Nelson Mandela Forum, wondering just when the rest of the cabal would get involved, when the rest of the Huntsmen would join in on the fight… or if this was much more personal then the all-out gang warfare that had embroiled ACW. “If I did that, you’d be seeing a lot more of me…” Fejona hissed. “Because we come from the same place, Sheffield…” Sheffield seethed, as he cocked back his scared right fist. “Don’t try to play me, I know exactly where I come from.” “I don’t question that for a second.” Fejona replied, tensed and ready to explode as the Italian crowd cheered for the battles surrounding them. “But I know for certain that you don’t know anything about me… or as much as you’d like to, about that photo I gave you!” Fejona went to kick Sheffield in the ribs again, and the Pulp Hero took the brunt of force of the kick, his knees almost buckling from the pain… but he stood strong and held on to Min’s leg. “The photo! Why did you have the photo?!” Sheff threw a punch, and his fist was parried and locked up by the cabal’s de facto lieutenant commander. “Because my mother was in it! Nora Min.” Fejona Min fired back in return, and Sheffield’s eyes narrowed, thinking about the woman he couldn’t quite place in the back of the photo’s crowded celebration. “I might have been born in Cambodia, Christopher, but I’m a legacy baby just like you.” Just like you. Sheffield roared and picked Fejona up in something of a capture suplex, bringing the woman up and over and almost slamming her to the ground, if not for her ability to roll under the force of the Pulp Hero’s slam… leaving her atop the Pulp Hero. “You’re nothing like me!” Sheff balked, eyes widened in rage. Fejona began to reign down punches on the Pulp Hero, as the Man Without an Alias returned them from his prone position. The World Champion KSZ and his #1 Contender Khristain Keller had returned to ringside and were battling around it like a couple of wild dogs, sending the fans into a feeding frenzy. It would seem that no more matches would be happening tonight. Only chaos, disguised as COURAGE. ... With an END GAME in sight. F I N.
