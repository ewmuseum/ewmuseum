Zezu — 1 August 2011 - We have been going on and off since Sept 2002. - We have put out around or just below 200 shows. - We try to keep things fresh, we have a number of tournaments, big ass FIGHT matches and other gimmicky type things that keep us and the handlers going such as;
END GAME MATCH The large-form battle royale of ACW, whereby the first and last entrants are decided with a very special
The Beginning of the End Match to open the night. The winner of said match becomes the last entrant in the End Game Match and the loser becomes the first entrant. The fourth place finisher procures a shot at the ACW Scorpion Fighting Title, while the third and second place finishers face off in a one-on-one match to decide a number one contender for the Spirit Of ACW Title. The winner traditionally gets a shot at the ACW World Title, at the biggest stage of them all -- LEGENDS!
