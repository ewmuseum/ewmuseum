Arkady Biography Real Name Arkady Rasputin Nicknames The Russian Wolf, The Lethal Lycan Hometown Now resides in Berlin, Germany Birthday 8/24/80 Place of Birth Siberia Height 6'2 Weight 243 Blood Type O+ Eye Color Blue Biography Arkady Rasputin, the self-proclaimed "One Man Wolfpack", was born and raised in a small shack deep in Siberia. His uncle, a former KGB agent, withdrew from the big city, upset with how the world was changing. Seeing a rage in Arkady, his uncle trained him in various forms of combat. From hunting to hand to hand combat to survivalism, Arkady was taught all his uncle knew, but his rage was still uncontrollable. The time finally came when he struck out on his own and found himself in trouble immediately. Within just days, he was put in jail for assault due to inciting a fight in a bar. Finding no real place, he found himself homeless. He was offered a spot in an underground fighting ring, after being seen by a "scout" while defending himself from some police officers. He rose through the ranks, quickly becoming the top fighter in the entire ring. That's where Kroenen found him. He was in Moscow, looking for talent and happen to catch a fight involving Arkady and three former Russian soldiers. Watching him make short work of the trio, Kroenen offered Arkady a chance to show the world what a true warrior is and be able to vent his rage. At first, Arkady turned down the request for reasons that are forbidden to be discussed. However, after much prodding and a wolf-themed variation of the traditional Japanese tiger mask to hide his identity, Arkady agreed. No longer a lone wolf, Arkady has found himself a pack and is willing to do whatever is needed to help them remain on the top of the food chain.
