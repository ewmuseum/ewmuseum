Tony Sharp vs. Kelly Masters A match made in Bruiser Heaven – actually, it was backstage at Courage and reaffirmed during the main event of that evening. Tonight, they’d settle the score. The Machine and the Thunder Bay Blade – expect blood, bruises, and sheer brutality. Buckle the fuck up. The rumblings from the All-Star crowd erupted into chaotic and adrenaline driven roars. Tony Sharp swatted the black curtain away with ‘One Man Army’ by Our Lady Peace accompanying him in the background. Sharp looked determined, and the Montreal crowd stood firmly behind the hometown boy. Beating the hell out of Rejection would have to wait a week – Kelly Masters would receive that distinction this week. Sharp popped through the ropes into the ring, an aura of toughness followed Andy’s big brother wherever he went, and this was no different. “Get that fuck out here, rook,” Sharp growled toward Trent Savage, ACW’s shortest tenured referee. Say no more and wait no longer. One by one I deal with them, Take them by the neck Drag them around kicking and screaming, Showing the law of cause and effect Fans didn’t need Nonpoint to know who was coming out next, but Kelly Masters’ entrance music only reaffirmed their thoughts. The rigid, muscular figure of Masters (who, by the way, looked ten to fifteen good pounds heavier from when he debuted) stormed through the pitch black curtain, and his eyes instantly locked onto the Gray Wolf. Inside the ring, a fight awaited him, his tense muscles and cold, emerald eyes fit the stage that’d been set. I’ll spare the overly descriptive details of his journey to ringside – all you need to know is that it was robotic and as unattached as every other week. Masters climbed the steps and entered the ring through the ropes. As if they were magnetic, Sharp and Masters quickly met each other dead center in the squared circle. The skinny was that Tony had a slim, but noticeable height advantage; while, strength and build would go to Masters, although Sharp was no slouch himself, not by a longshot. Trent Savage, playing his strong side, attempted to part the two, but failed miserably at being Moses and the Red Sea. Instead, the fists, forearms, elbows, and headbutts started flying. Savage backpedaled, wisely I might add, and motioned for the bell. DING! DING! DING! Right, now the match can start. Oh, they already started? Well, apparently it’s ‘Fuck the Bell Night’ – don’t forget to grab your free shirt. Masters deflected a straight right with his guard, and replied with a stiff elbow shot that caught all temple. Sharp stumbled backward, slightly dazed, and brought up a guard of his own. Wise choice as an advancing Masters soon let his fists go, but the majority were deflected by a plus guard from the Thunder Bay Blade. Masters dropped into a wrestling stance, and drove Sharp back first into the turnbuckles. Tony axe smashed Masters’ lower back, and received a shoulder thrust into his midsection for his troubles. The give and take continued, with Masters shoulder thrusts eventually taking the advantage. With the Montreal native and ACW fan favorite slumped in the corner, Masters stepped back and unloaded on Sharp with knife-edge chops. CHOP! WOOOOOOO! CHOP! WOOOOOOO! Sharp, a scowl firmly planted on his face, turned the tables and switched Masters into the corner. Yeah, obviously Tony Sharp had something nice in store for Masters. CHOP! WOOOOOOO! CHOP! WOOOOOOO! CHOP! WOOOOOOO! CHOP! WOOOOOOO! CHOP! WOOOOOOO! The Thunder Bay Blade threw some nasty ass chops, let me tell you. The expression on Masters’ face told the story, but there didn’t appear to be any skin broken, only red welts. Grabbing hold of Masters’ right arm, Tony began to Irish whip his foe corner to corner, only to stop Masters, and reel him back in. Short-arm Clothesline! Nobody home! Masters ducked behind Sharp, and latched on with a waistlock. Tony fired back elbows, all which were narrowly avoided by The Machine. Masters popped his hips bringing Sharp off the ground, and then launched the Montreal native toward the turnbuckle with a German. THUD. “OOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHH!” Tony Sharp’s head and neck impacted against the top turnbuckle before the ex-hockey player crumbled face down onto the canvas. Savage stood wide-eyed before checking on Sharp, but the rookie referee was soon shoved away by Masters, who rolled Sharp over for the cover. One! TWO! THREE! Not a fucking chance. Tony Sharp was one tough bastard, and even a sick German suplex onto a turnbuckle wouldn’t put him down so early. Masters immediately went back to work, setting Sharp’s left ankle on the bottom rope only to drop a knee across the defenseless ankle. Tony rolled away in pain, and received a few stomps to the aforementioned ankle. The ACW faithful let out a collective groan of disapproval, and Masters pulled the big bruiser to his feet. A quick outside single nabbed Sharp’s left leg for Masters, and a crisp Dragon Screw Legwhip. MKM (Mr. Kelly Masters) retained the leg lock, and transformed his position into a Half Crab. Sharp, aggravated and working with a hitch in his giddy up, easily managed to crawl forward and grab a handful of ring cable. “Rope break, Masters,” Savage kindly informed ‘The Machine’, but quickly backed off when Kelly met him with a harsh glare. Masters did release the hold, just not in the timely fashion that Savage had expected. With the hold ceased, the Thunder Bay Blade climbed to his feet with ring cable aid. Masters closed the distance with a short burst, but quickly flew over the top rope, thanks to Tony Sharp hitting the canvas and pulling the top rope with him. Sharp stood, tapped his forefinger against his temple several times, and cut through the cables to join Kelly Masters on the outside. Sharp helped Masters up to his feet (what a gentleman), and then promptly smashed his head into the ring apron (I take back the last part). Of course, no head smashing is complete without running their face across the apron, and that’s exactly what Tony had decided to do. Masters stumbled away from the apron, and eventually came to rest against the barricade. Sharp pursued, ducked a snappy back elbow, and, with a waistlock, backdropped Masters onto the cold concrete behind them. “Eight!” shouted Trent Savage, who continued his count. Sharp pulled Mr. Kelly Masters to his feet as Savage’s count reached nine, and then hurriedly rolled him back into the ring to break the rookie official’s count. Sharp rejoined the opposition, and greeted him with a boot scrape across the forehead. The Thunder Bay Dungeon graduate applied a front facelock, which allowed Masters to power to his feet. Masters ducked underneath the lock, and into an arm wringer, which was then forced into a hammerlock. Sharp fell forward, placed a foot behind Masters’ own, and spun behind the Virginia Beach native after a drop toe hold. A chinlock and a knee on the small of the back was Sharp’s next move. Painful, yes. Able to finish a match – unlikely. Masters proved that thought correct with a quick sit-out, alleviating the pain from his back, but still trapped in Sharp’s chinlock. The former MMA star again flipped his hips, this time finding himself facing head-on with Sharp, who had transitioned his chinlock into a front facelock of his own. Masters dropped to a knee, but quickly exploded up – with Tony Sharp’s legs firmly gripped in a double leg takedown. No worries, as Sharp dropped his two hundred-sixty four pound frame toward the canvas, which made for a successful counter. The Thunder Bay Blade wrenched on his facelock, but once again found himself suspended in air from another power lift. There would be no reversal this time as Sharp landed back first on the canvas, and the impact jarred his facelock, allowing Masters to slip out into a full mount position. ELBOW! ELBOW! LEFT! ANOTHER LEFT! With an improved guard from Tony, Masters spun back toward Sharp’s legs. The All-Star Arena instantly sparked to life. ACHILLES’ HEEL! The Machine had scissored Sharp’s injured wheel, and looked to get the ankle secured. TAP! TAP! TAP! So much for that. Although much anticipated and exciting, it was over. Oh, by the way, that tapping was not Sharp’s hand meeting the mat. No sir, it was actually Tony Sharp’s foot being driven in Kelly Masters’ hands, chest, and face. I changed the sound for certain entertainment purposes, ‘thwack’ or ‘thud’ would have made more sense. The kicks jarred Masters, and allowed the Gray Wolf to spin to his feet, and charge in at Masters. Double Knee Strike! “OOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHH!” Post-impact, Tony slid on his knees and reached back to throw an arm across Kelly Masters’ chest. One! TWO! THREE! Yes! No! Yes? Savage clearly showed two fingers to Sharp, and then raised his fingers higher for the All-Star audience to see. Sharp may have been dismayed, his scowl would back that, but he wasted no time whining. Using Mr. Kelly Masters’ short hair, Tony assisted the youngster to his feet and sent him into near cables. Masters ducked a clothesline, bounced back from the opposite side, and was lifted up before being slammed back via ring shaking spinebuster. The impact bounced Sharp upward and allowed him to slap on a side headlock. The homegrown, Montreal product wrenched on the headlock, but a bridge and rolling takeover soon put Sharp’s shoulders flat with the canvas. One! TWO! Kickout! The Thunder Bay Blade rolled through with his kickout, and had Masters back to where he started, locked into a side headlock. Despite the headlock, Masters pushed up to his feet. Tony spun behind Masters into a hammerlock and pushed the slightly heavier Masters into the corner. Sharp wasn’t through, and elbow smashes meeting Masters’ thick skull clearly portrayed that. Sharp dropped his shoulder, and slammed it into the small of Masters’ back. Tony latched around Kelly’s waist and hoisted him into a German suplex. Complete with bridge, folks. One! TWO! THREE! Close, but no see-gar. Laying down a plethora of stomps seemed to be Tony’s next objective; an objective that proved to be prudent and painful, the best you can get in wrestling. The Gray Wolf, quite the fitting nickname given his current predatory status, hoisted Masters up and landed a pair of forearm shots to the Machine’s jaw. Sharp latched around Kelly’s waist, and hoisted him into a side carry. Pendulum Backbreaker! The Thunder Bay Blade maintained the hold, pressing a hand down on Masters’ throat and the other down on Masters’ legs. Savage crouched down closely to the action, looking for a submission from Masters, which appeared high unlikely. Sharp, with a snarl, realized this and pulled the Virginia Beach native up before bringing him right back down onto the same knee. The Canadian bruiser rolled Masters off his knee, but once again pulled Masters back up. Sharp shoved Masters into the ropes and then Irish whipped him across the ring into the parallel cables. Running Dropkick! Mr. Kelly Masters’ dropkick was delivered with precision to Sharp’s right kneecap, instantly flooring Andy Sharp’s older brother. The Machine, his back clearly bothering him, rose to his feet and followed with a trio of stomps to Sharp’s knee. The strategy was effective, and even more so after he applied a spinning toe hold. Kelly took a firm hold before reefing back and forth on the leg. Guess who decided to grace ACW with his supreme presence? The Main Attraction of Augusta, Maine (just made that up), the Danger Man, the King of Submission. Roll out the red carpet … Max Danger! Danger strolled down to ringside, but his attention averted from the action in the ring to the ring itself. He pulled up the ring apron; with his other hand, he produced his iPhone, which he used as a miniature flashlight. Soon, Danger disappeared underneath the ring; Well, actually, his feet were still sticking out. The search for Armsy resumed, but what were the chances the arm was underneath the ring? He soon reappeared (in full), stood up, and, in frustration, kicked the imaginary dust at his feet. Danger turned toward Masters and commented, “Well played, Masters. Well played, indeed.” With Danger looking in, Masters released the toe hold, fell backward, and spun into a spinning back fist that landed perfectly. Apparently awaiting a reply, the Danger Man continued staring, but Masters never heard the quiet comment and was fully immersed in the action. Finally, Max turned away and headed towards the back. And now, back to what’s important. Which, by the way (FWO), is wrestling. Masters hoisted the Thunder Bay Blade back to his feet and drilled a knee into Sharp’s abdomen. The Machine locked on a standing side cradle, lifted Sharp into the air, and fell backward into a bridge. One! TWO! THREE! Ha, ha. No. “Zere is one minute remaining before ze time limit expires!” Yeah, that’s the time limit. Sharp wasn’t ready to cash in. The Thunder Bay Blade fought back with fists to Masters’ gut. With Masters hunkered over, Sharp backed off and came back in with a running knee strike. Masters popped up, narrowly avoiding the knee, but was met with a vicious turn-around clothesline from Tony Sharp. Sharp made the cover. One! TWO! THREE! Not true, I mean close, but not close enough. Tony lifted Masters up and onto his shoulder. How does a FIREMAN’S CARRY DDT sound? DDT! Not the one you expected, as Masters shifted off Tony Sharp’s shoulder and caught him with a falling DDT. There would be no fall, however, because Masters maintained the facelock and vice gripped his legs around Sharp’s body. The guillotine choke was locked on snugly, and Sharp’s struggles were fruitless. Savage slid in next to the two men, who were smack dab in the ring’s center, and watched intently as the time limit approached expiration. FIVE SECONDS! FOUR! Savage raised Sharp’s right arm! THREE! It dropped! TWO! It dropped again! ONE! Savage raises the arm again! IT DROPPED! DING! DING! DING! With the bell rung, Masters held onto the submission a little longer before letting the Thunder Bay Blade drop to the canvas. The Machine got back to his feet, and took a long look down at Sharp. Next step? Lobbing a gob of spit toward the fallen opposition, of course. Masters slipped through the cables, and, for the first time in ACW, heard a rumble of boos at his closing actions toward Sharp. Fuck it, right? Right.
