Claire Merci Biography Real Name Claire Merci Nicknames The Golden Merci Hometown Cannes, France Birthday April 2nd, 1984 Place of Birth Paris, France Height 6'1 Weight 170 Eye Color Blue Biography Claire is the older of the sisters, and is actually considered to be Laetitia's legal guardian. She's been a professional wrestler since she was fifteen, so she's actually a ten year veteran who's spent most of her time in Europe. She's also wrestled on tours for America and Britian, and she's become a fluent speaker of English as a result. She can even speak with an American accent. She's not too fond of Laetitia's desire to become a professional wrestler, though she has a soft spot for her and doesn't like to see her upset or hurt. This often affects her judgement, she'll actually jump in and get Laetitia disqualified in singles matches rather than see her get crippled. Claire is genuinely unpleasant to be around, and is nowhere near as kind as Laetitia is. She tends to distort Laetitia's words on occasion when translating for her, sometimes on purpose and sometimes by accident. She almost blurs the line between face and heel, but since she adores her little sister, she'll be considered a face. She then places one of her legs across the victim's body and her other leg is wrapped around the opponent's head. While pulling the arms, Claire pushes with her leg, creating incredible pressure on the opponent's neck and back. 3) Double underhook lock wtih body scissors, often done while the opponent is sitting and Claire is in front of them for the maximum amount of pressure. 4) Claire applies a flying headscissors, but stops short of taking down an opponent and instead grabs an arm and hooks in a cross armbreaker on the opponent. In addition, she also tries to wrap her long legs around the opponent's head/neck as well. She hangs from the opponent's arm until they collapse or tap out. The Six Seconds Magic from the WWF No Mercy game from the N64, in other words.
