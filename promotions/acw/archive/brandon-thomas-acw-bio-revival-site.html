Biography Heel, arrogant, overwhelmingly confident. Yeah, he has to be one of the cockiest pricks in the wrestling business to date. You, your nothing to him, nothing but a peon, peasant, or a jobber. The only thing he cares about is himself. If you think your better than him, he’ll prove you wrong, rather if its in the ring, or on the microphone, he‘ll put you to shame. He is one of the youngest in the business, but by God, he is the best. Brandon Thomas is wrestling and without out him, your entertainment will be lost.
