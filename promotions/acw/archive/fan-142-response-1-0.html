Response 1.0 Posted by Lewis on September 27, 2004 at 12:15:42: Who was your favorite writer? Kamlesh - I'm just amazed at the quantity of quality material he can submit for shows, each-and-every week. He's deserved of recognition. I'm also a fan of the majority of his characters, which makes his writings that much better to read. Who was your favorite character? Gacy and Oswald; Fall of Adam - Simply intrigued by the characters and Josh's work with 'em. What angles are you interested in? ACW Vs. Carter/Keller/Jericho/Inferno Quinton May Vs. Joseph McMillan Fall of Adam God's Forgotten Son Favorite Match? Enjoyed Fall of Adam Vs. Fejona Min/Natalie Quinston and SilverHAWK Vs. GFS. Worst Match? Not worst, but least appealing; Azrael Asesino Vs. Jamar Gordo. Was relatively short, and I really need to do some research into the characters before I can enjoy their matches. Funniest SEegment? Oz's Beautiful People segments were cool; enjoyed 'em. Best Segment? "You're going to DIE" - Enjoyable read. Worst Segment? Not worst, but least favourite; The Era of the Beautiful People. Short. Wrestler with the most momentum? Can't really choose a specific wrassler. Funniest line of the night? “You see I’ve started up something I like to call… the ACW Beautiful People’s Club, sponsored by me of course,” Kelly paused with a look of complete idiocy on his face to see whether or not Jamar was looking at him yet; he was not. “And well, you see Gordo, you’re not in the club.” - I guess. What did you like most about this weeks COURAGE? The majority of the content. What did you not like about this weeks COURAGE? Prrffft. Dunno.
