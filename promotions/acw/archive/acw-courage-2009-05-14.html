Sometimes things just have to be bluntly put into place. Here is Courage. Read it. Blood & Oil I The rumbling of thunder would lead-in to Courage, which was less than an hour away from starting. Only there was no thunder, and that was quickly apparent as a motorcycle, its deafening pipes firing with life, rolled into the ACW parking lot. The rider was Kelly Masters, recognizable enough considering he wore no helmet – no surprise there. Masters pulled into a space further than necessary, and rested the 1990 Harley-Davidson FLSTF Fat Boy’s kickstand against the pavement. He was early, per usual, but certainly was not the only All-Star to arrive early. Another roll of rumbling thunder rang out from behind him. The sound, this time, came from under the hood of a '70 Challenger. The black, and slightly beaten, beast of a muscle car flicked it's high beams at Masters, causing the man who had defeated Montreal's own Tony Sharp in the opener last week, to squint for a moment before looking away. The Challenger then pulled up beside Masters and his Harley, and out stepped the man who oh so fittingly owned a car such as this. Alias. The Scorpion Fighting Champion grinned, and ran his fingers through his goatee, having stepped from the cab of his muscle car. "Not to open up every conversation I have with you, with a recommendation, but I hear motorcycles are good enough killin' machines with helmets." “Then I guess they know not to harm one of their own,” Masters deadpanned, grabbing a hunter green, shoulder bag off the back of his bike. “I have to admit, that’s a damn fine car you’ve got there – a classic. That bad boy’s seen good days and bad, little worse for wear, but damn if that big ol’ engine is gonna quit,” Masters continued, his eyes scanning the Dodge Challenger that Alias stood beside. The Pulp Hero, took a black leather gym bag from the back of the car and then slammed the door shut, replying, "Yeah, I taught her everything she knows." Alias slung the bag over his shoulder and leaned on the top of the Challenger, his eyes drifting towards the back of the ol' Opera House now known as ACW Arena. Without looking back at Kelly he asked a natural question for these two warriors, "So who're you fighting tonight?" “Night off,” Masters grunted, in no way, shape, or form sounding enthused. "Huh," was Alias's only reply for a moment. Masters gave him a bit of a sour look. "No nothing wrong with that, I guess. Course when I started I wrestled... what was it? Thirty-five matches on my first thirty-three shows. I think. Course, I took a week off sometime during that too. So that's alright. It's not like you needed to keep up with me." Alias finished with a devil-ish grin, before beginning to walk off to the back entrance of the Arena. Mr. Kelly Masters’ followed, robotic pace in full effect, although perhaps quickened a bit more than usual. “Thirty-five, you say? Not bad,” Masters said with a shrug. “Seems to me somebody is always lookin’ to get slapped around,” Masters resumed with a grin. “Chances are I’ll end up fighting somebody tonight – just ask Tony Sharp how easy that is. Hell, if I figured I’d be inactive, I wouldn’t be here.” "Isn't that the truth. Even when you don't plan to fight, the fight always ends up finding you... especially if you've got a little gold or silver around your waist," Alias said referring to Championship in his bag and the man he would be fighting tonight in a cage match, "That was a hell of a fight against Tony Sharp last week, by the way. Haven't got a chance to meet the man yet, but I hope he's less of a pain in the ass then his little brother. Sure Andy tries to be a good guy... but that's just the thing, he tries." “Would a fighter want it any other way? Better than sitting around grab-assin’,” Masters replied. “I don’t have any time for those Sharp boys, and, if you ask me, they’re both morons. I wouldn’t mind takin’ another shot at the both of ‘em,” the Machine finished with a scowl. "Far as I'm concerned, another shot inevitable. Just as me and Andy having a rematch... or Tony and I meeting in a ring for the first time, you know? It's like you said, someone is always lookin' to get slapped around." The two men entered through the large double door to the ACW Arena. "So here's to the future of the fight. Maybe Montreal will be standing when we're done." Masters nodded. “Should still be after tonight – you are locked in a cage, after all.” Alias nodded in return, tonight would be... a hell of a fight. "Should be."
