Sunday September 15th, 2002 | Live on FOX BJCC Arena, Birmingham, Alabama Introduction Every new federation must have a first show on TV, and more times than not, that first show is the stepping stone to something even bigger. Rule #1 in federation creation; Make the first show rock! Last week, ACW had it's first show, and it ended on a very low note, with internal bickering between the three owners ending with the show being cut, just as the climax to the US title match was going to be made. It was not just the three owners that where bickering, as there are already fights in the locker room. With Shabazz's clear intent to annoy every white man, ICU decided to step in, with a confrontation looming in the near future. The comical and athletic talents of eXistenZ have been brought into ACW, by one of the most unlikely sources, Wombat Guerrera. Last of all was SilverHAWK. As an essential cog in the ACW machine for the last 6 or so years, Winters doesn't seem to like the look of him, and he was the initial starting point to the management feud. A federations foundation should be strong. Strong Management. Strong Roster. Strong TV links. Strong Production. ACW's biggest problem lies not with SilverHAWK or anyone else, but whether they can pull together and get this to work, or is that the fun of it? Watching utter mayhem commence every Sunday on FOX! EARLIER TODAY! The three senior members of the ACW staff stood side by side with one another, inside the arena hallway. It was still a good three hours until show time, but you could still hear the sound of drilling and hammering as the road crew where erecting the ring and set. The trio stood, Winters, Boyd and Dunn, in that order, Boyd obviously trying to be the referee between the two men who caused the show to come to a sudden halt last week, to the delight of the FOX executives, yeah. "Listen Dunn," Winters started the meeting, "I realise now that I can't do this alone, well with Boyd. Last week was very foolish of me, as I thought that this was just any normal production, but this is nothing like your run of the mill TV show is it." Both Dunn and Boyd shook their head in the agreement, as Winters continued on with his cry. "Therefore, I would like to apologize with all my heart, as we are in this as a trio, not a duo, or anyone going along. A trio." "UNITE US!" Boyd yelled as he threw a clenched fist in the middle of the warring ACW staff. Dunn placed his hand on Boyd's and reluctantly, Winters placed his on the heap. "I appreciate that Ethan, I had to take a stand last week, I'm just sorry that the product had to suffer. If I could have gotten through to you any other way, I would have, but it just wasn't going to happen." Winters understood, one of his main...scrub that, his only fault was that he was a little egotistical. He had been told that before, he has even admitted it openly, he thought that was what was special about him, he never dithered in self-pity, he always thought the best of himself. Both Dunn and Winters shook hands as they agreed to set their differences aside for tonight, as this was as big a show as last week's was. Through the whole of ACW's existence, they had not ever had a world champion, as it was merely a road show. Tonight, there would be a champion for the very first time, and it was between two men, Arcayin and Osyrus. Two men who have only been ACW employee's for merely a month, and it hurt people like... Swooping From The Depths "SILVERHAWK! SILVERHAWK! SILVERHAWK! SILVERHAWK! SILVERHAWK! SILVERHAWK!" It was a cry that he had heard so many times that he couldn't remember, but he wouldn't give it up for one minute. For years he had traveled the world with numerous wrestling federation. Japan, Europe, South and North America, but no matter where he went, he could never find a home. ACW was Home. However, it seemed like Ethan Winters had become his Mum's new boyfriend, as he thought that this man was out, to break his home up, even though that wasn't in Winters' game plan. As SilverHAWK entered the arena, on a dusky September night, he could only think of the happy times in his wrestling career. Being undefeated in any federation ring for over 13months. Winning the first ever Triple Cage Match which was put into practice. For surviving a barbed wire match in Japan in 1995, something that he would never forget, and never re-attempt. His win over Stone Cold Steve Austin, back when he was just "The Ringmaster". HAWK had done a lot in his "career" but it seemed as if the biggest hurdle that he had to overcome was going to be the new owner of his home, Ethan Winters. The grudge had come from nowhere, even though Winters could be used by ACW, it seemed that it was never going to happen. "Sup HAWK?" It was Jimmy Gonz. Gonz and HAWK had known each other for a long time, even they never knew how long their friendship had lasted, but it had lasted through the good, and through the bad. Gonz, being just as old as HAWK, was told that he was not needed in a wrestling capacity at ACW anymore, but they would have an opportunity for him. He was now helping with the interviews at ACW, that was all. Winters had turned one of the most under-rated wrestlers on the planet at one time, into an assistant for interviews. HAWK was scared what position he would be offered. Ring Crew? Fluffer? Doorman? It wouldn't come down to that...would it? "Gonz, how did it come to this huh?" Gonz shook his head in despair and put a supporting arm around HAWK as they walked to his locker room. "Man, it used to be you and me who seen all the old guys walking in, asking for a little bit of give from the young'uns, now we are the old dogs just looking for a friggin' bone. Listen man, all us old guns are still routing for you man, you gotta take a stand about this. OK none of us are gonna take over the fed or anything extreme as that, but the way Winters has just pushed us aside has put a bad taste in my mouth, it's disrespect from a little cotton nose candy fucker like him." "Gonz man, it's gonna change, I'm going to see Dunn later, and then things will be changing, for the better." Both Gonz and HAWK shook hands as they stood outside a door which had HAWK's name on it. "Don't get me wrong HAWK, these young guys are good, real good, but even they know how we are being treated." HAWK opened the door to reveal possibly the smallest dressing room in the building, Gonz patted HAWK in the back once again and left chuckling to himself as HAWK stormed off, looking for his pray. Guerrera and eXi had met only last week, but they seemed to have bonded in some unusual way, as they where both "special" characters. A pop from the fans showed that this match was going to be pretty well received. eXi and Wombat posed to the fans as they took a few photo's as they both circled the ring, eXi almost bumping into the referee. As both men looked to lock up once again, a kick in the gut by Wombat gave him the initiative in this match. Wombat repeated the kicks and pushed eXi to the corner of the ring. eXi was thrust into the corner as Wombat hit eXi with a few punches to the jaw and then whipped eXi to the corner. As eXi bulleted to the corner, he planted his foot on the bottom turnbuckle and then turned around as Wombat ran towards him, eXi hit Wombat with a drop toe hold which left Wombat smashing his face off the bottom turnbuckle. Wombat popped up to his feet and was thrust to the mat with a snap suplex as he bounced off the mat and held his back in pain. As he sat up eXi bounced off the ropes and drop kicked Wombat to the back of the head, a move which could have caused a serious injury if done with enough power. eXi pulled Wombat's mask and pulled him to his feet, and then whipped him to the ropes. Leap frog by eXi and then he looked to monkey flip Wombat, but Wombat rolled through it, on eXi's back and jumped on the second rope to hit eXi with an Asai Moonsault! "One...Two!" eXi jolted his right side into the air as he kicked out of an amazing move by Wombat, who picked up eXi by his red straw like hair and put him back to the mat with a power slam, before climbing up to the top rope, but as he got to the top eXi was already up, and he knocked Wombat's legs from beneath him and then hit in him the face with a few forearms. eXi himself climbed to the top rope and tried to hit Wombat back to the mat with a super-plex, but Wombat blocked it, and through eXi off the top rope, onto the mat outside! eXi stumbled to hit feet as Wombat came off the top rope and to further eXi's pain, hit him with a cross body. "Holy Shit! Holy Shit! Holy Shit! Holy Shit! Holy Shit!" As the fans mocked an old ECW chant as eXistenZ and Wombat tried to up the ante a little more. Wombat too eXi and lay him over the barrier before climbing onto the ring apron himself, Wombat then jumped off the ring apron and kicked eXi in the back, causing him to squash up against the barrier and then bounce back off it of as he lay on the ground holding his chin. Wombat rolled into the ring and then rolled back out as he broke the referee's count up, to the referee's annoyance. "Get him back in here Wombat!" Was all the referee could muster as Wombat hit eXi with a few right hands as he mounted him on the mat, the referee starting another count. Wombat then pulled eXi to his feet and threw him back in the ring, then jumped up the apron, awaiting eXi's return to his feet. As eXi did get to his feet, Wombat sprung himself to the top rope and went for another cross body, only to be stopped as eXi drop kicked him in mid-air! "SOMEONE IS GONNA DIE TONIGHT!" was shouted by the nearby ring announcer as both men lay on the mat, the referee beginning his count and the fans where on their feet as they applauded the wrestlers' efforts so far. Both men got to the their feet at the same time and began to exchange right hands as the fans still stood up, chanting away. Both men connected with two right hands each before Wombat went for a clothesline but missed as eXi ducked, eXi then turned around and attempted to kick Wombat in the stomach, only for Wombat to catch his foot, eXi's only way out was to somersault backwards but as he landed, Wombat planted him with a double-footed dropkick to the chest which nearly sent eXistenZ out of the ring! Wombat lifted himself to his knees and then sprang up, as he then moved over to eXi, who was lying in the fettle position in the corner of the ring, holding his chest. Wombat grabbed his leg and pulled him to the middle of the ring, and then singled that he was going up top again. He waved for eXi to rise to his feet, which eXi did after a matter of seconds, but as Wombat launched himself off of the top rope, eXi was clearly aware of what he was doing, as he almost caught Wombat in mid air and rolled him up into a small package type move, it didn't matter what it was called, the shoulders where pinned. "One...Two...Three!!" A crowd exploded as eXistenZ's hand was raised in victory as he was paraded to the fans and then climbed the top rope and through his fist in the air. "I RAWK!" As eXi was on the top rope he looked down at Wombat, who was going through the move in his head it looked like. As Wombat was about to leave the ring, eXi pulled him back as both men shared a truly magic moment in the middle of the ring, as the sell-out ground was on their feet, giving these two men the props that they deserve. The came in the ring as mutual friends, but now that is what they really were, friends, as they left the ring together. Arcayin In all it's history, ACW had never had a World Title, as it's main purpose was a touring wrestling road show, there was no competition, there was just a group of men trying to put on the best show possible, and that is what they did for the number of years that they where doing it, until a certain Mr. Winters came into the federation. With his introduction, he brung the elements needed to take the ACW Road show, to a whole new level, and right now, the head of this new promotion would be chosen. The two men who where going to go for this title, where Osyrus and Arcayin. One of which has been around a decent amount of time and have enough respected federations on his resume' to take him very seriously, and the other doesn't have a resume'. These two men have probably not met in their life, but tonight they will meet for the biggest prize in the ACW, the Champion will go home a Legend, the loser a forgotten entity. "Born with Nothing, Die with Everything" by Papa Roach played throughout the arena as the fans got to their feet to boo and jeer the oncoming wrestler, Osyrus. "From San Diego, CA, weighing 285lbs, OSYRUS!" As he walked down the ramp way, fans either side of the barrier lobbed abuse at him, but were petrified when the abuse was lobbed back, as Osyrus snarled at fans either side of him before sliding into the ring and not even bothering posing for a fan picture in the middle of the ring, he just stood, motionless, as the arena went blood red. As the drums and guitar riffs of “My Plague (New abuse mix)” by Slipknot began to play, the fans exploded for a monster that had never even said a word yet. The fans did not know anything about him, or what he wanted in the ACW, but they liked his power, his strength, his stamina and not least his ability to destroy careers. Arcayin strolled down to the ring before jumping on onto the apron and climbing into the ring, where Osyrus stood, not budging an inch for his opponent. Both men eye-balled each other in the middle of the ring as the bell rang, but both men just stood nose-to-nose, in an attempt to build the intensity between the two. Osyrus was the first to move, as he hit Arcayin with a right hand, which had no affect what so ever. Osyrus looked at his fist and pulled back for another right hand, but Arcayin ducked the shot and stood behind Osyrus, signaling for him to "Just Bring It". The fans exploded as both men began to exchange right hands, Arcayin winning the minor battle of the fists as he drove Osyrus into the corner and then smashed his knee into Osyrus' abdominal muscles and then threw him to the opposite turnbuckle and rammed into him with a body splash ala Billy Gunn, but a lot more powerful as Osyrus bounced off the turnbuckle and slapped himself against the mat. Arcayin roared a battle cry as he picked up Osyrus and whipped him to the ropes, monkey flipping him in the air and then clotheslining him three times as Osyrus kept coming up for more and more. Osyrus had crawled to the middle of the ropes, as Arcayin moved over to him and stuck his boot in a few times before sliding out of the ring and pulling Osyrus out with him, by the legs. Arcayin looked to whip Osyrus into the ring steps, but Osyrus put the breaks on just in time and instead, whipped Arcayin into the crowd, as Arcayin flipped over the barricade on impact, squashing a group of fans at ring side who were loving it. Osyrus lay on the mat while he got his bearing before pulling Arcayin by the hair and slinging him back over the barricade and then back into the ring. Osyrus walked over to the announcers table and grabbed a steel chair and then slid back into the ring, he was about to clean Arcayin's clock with it before the referee got in the way. "You going to DQ me?" Osyrus shouted as the referee put his arms in the air trying to persuade Osyrus not to go down this road, but he didn't have the chance to, as Arcayin wrestled the chair out of Osyrus's hands and then kicked it out the ring, both men standing off once again, clearly Arcayin wanted to see which man was the better, no weapons, just talent. Osyrus nodded with Arcayin's gesture, as they began to circle each other and then locked up, Osyrus putting his knee to Arcayin's face, causing the Monster to reel back to the ropes. Osyrus whipped Arcayin to the other ropes and then hit him with a hard knee, which caused Arcayin to almost flip in mid-air. Osyrus picked him up once again and hit him with a German suplex to the mat and the repeated the action, going for the first pin of the contest. 1 2 Shoulder was up. Arcayin wasn't going to give up that easily. Osyrus got up, whacking LLB in the side of the head with another boot, and exiting the ring, he then snatched another chair from ringside, the true man contest didn't last long did it? He slid back into the ring where a stuttering Arcayin stood against the ropes, as he tried to get himself up, but as Osyrus swung with the chair, their was only going to be one result, and that was Arcayin falling out of the ring as the chair connected with his skull. The referee could do nothing, this was a historic event, he could DQ someone and then have the match the next week, he had to let this go, thank God Arcayin fell out of the ring, or else the referee would have been in a whole lot of trouble. The ref didn't even make the ten count as the two men where out of the ring. Osyrus lifted the chair in the air, like he had just slain a dragon with Excalibur, but he had the slain part right, as Arcayin was lying on the outside, blood oozing out of his head, just above the eyebrow. Osyrus rolled back outside as he picked apart the head of Arcayin, trying to seize that wound open even further. Arcayin's wound was now a giant gash above his eye, as the blood poured into his eye, giving him a half crimson mask. He was rolled back into the ring by Osyrus who then took the liberty to celebrate before covering Arcayin, a very lackadaisical cover at that. 1 2 3! No! Arcayin kicked out as blood dripped on the mat as he lay on his side, Osyrus sat up and smiled, but it wasn't a nice smile, it was a sick smirk that graced his face as he got up and waited, as Arcayin rose to his feet, very slowly. Osyrus looked to smash Arcayin's face with a big boot, instead Arcayin had the awareness to duck it, and then turn around and hit Osyrus with a belly to belly suplex which bought him some time. As both men got to their feet, Osyrus held that steel chair in his hands once again and cracked Arcayin over the back with it and then out him into the sleeper hold on the middle of the ring. Arcayin struggled, but he was slowly fading as the referee was right beside him, but Arcayin was not going to let out, as he rose to his feet, Osyrus still clutching to his chin. Osyrus pushed Arcayin into the corner and then whipped him to the opposite corner following it up with a clothesline. Arcayin shook himself off as Osyrus hit him with a right hand, no to affect! The fans rose to their feet as Arcayin let Osyrus pound his face in with right hands, but they where doing nothing to the red Monster. Osyrus looked to bounce off of the ropes and then clothesline Arcayin but Arcayin hit him with a huge spear! Arcayin covers Osyrus! 1 2 3! NO! Osyrus kicked out right at the three count, the referee even counted the three, but Osyrus got up just in time. A groan from the fans clearly showed who they wanted to win the match. Arcayin shoved Osyrus into the corner and then looked to whip Osyrus into the same corner as the motioned to whip him across the ring and then pull him back, but the referee positioned himself so that he would be caught in the middle, which he was, squashed by the oncoming Osyrus. As the referee slowly got up to his feet, Arcayin moved over to Osyrus and went down to pin him, but Osyrus smashed him in the head with the steel pipe!!
