What this is The fed's own applications page as its rebuilt 2004 site carried it (the same house rules, with the owner now taking free-form e-mails instead of a form), as captured in the Wayback on 13 June 2004. The site's menu is not reproduced.
Applications -> We are an angle based federation, but our system is a bit of a mix, being that winners of feuds etc will either be determined, or if neither handler can decide on an eventual winner to the feud, the winner will be decided by ACW staff, on whom has worked the hardest throughout the arc. -> Your application will be judged on many things, the overall effort put into your application, the overall quality of writing on your application (your history, sample rp, general character stats) Think of it this way, would you go into a workplace and give them a half assed CV? NO, so don't give us an application with bits missing out, and if they are missing there must be a good reason, as to us it will be tossed if you have missed bits out. -> You do not need to be exclusive, but we will not accept people who are in say another 3 feds, plus ours, because it simply isn't logical that you can give your all at ACW with three other feds on the burner. -> ACW requires a lot of work to be done behind the scenes, that means you must create and organize your own angles, writing most of your own matches, along with rp'ing and putting in segments for the show. We are not slave drivers, either you do it or you don't, but the majority of people that do the work, are doing the best in ACW so far. We don't think of it as, do the work and you'll get a title, we think of ACW as a team that tells a story...if you wish to be part of telling that story, good. -> All applications will be read, but we will not reply to your application. Yeah, we know that's a little shitty, but we are afraid that due to time constraints and outside work i.e. college etc, we just don't find the time to reply to every single application. Usually if you haven't been told you are accepted within a week or two then your application has been unsuccessful. However, we will be keeping all applications, because there may be a time were we need a character or a handler like you, and if they happens, we will give you a call. -> The last thing I have to say is that anyone who comes here looking to win belts or achieve the mini-fame which goes along with being in an eFed will not belong in ACW. We are a group of guys/and girl whom just want to have fun, and by that, we have fun by making ACW one of the most enjoyable federations on the net. ->We are here to tell a story for people to read, that is all. On the way, if you do well and are feuding with the right people, someone may drop a belt to you, but it doesn't mean your anything special, or superior to the next guy or girl. I'm trying something now... Send me an email to... [detail withheld], [detail withheld] It can contain whatever you want...a full bio, an angle idea anything which you think I would want to see as far as the owner of this fed is concerned and I guess if I get a load of crap for the first few apps, I'll put up an actual application form. :) Have fun. AND REMEMBER, WE DO NOT REPLY TO APPLICATIONS...
