JAGI Biography Real Name Ja Gi Kyung-Moon Nicknames The All-American Korean American, Ja, Verona's Finest Hometown San Diego, CA Birthday August 14, 1986 Place of Birth Verona Township, Michigan Height 5'11 Weight 194 Blood Type B- Eye Color Brown Biography Ja Gi Kyung-Moon isn't anything special, looking at his background. Normal family life, normal schooling, normal young man who got out of school in a recession with little life ambition and a ton of student debt to his name. Nobody wants to hear about the guy who got into professional wrestling on a whim to give Sallie Mae her money. Yet it doesn't end there, or we wouldn't be seeing him today. No, JAGI realized that he was actually fairly decent at this whole "wrestling" thing. But fairly decent, and five dollars, won't get you a cup of coffee in this sport. One needs a name, a marketable reputation, ring experience, and a hook. JAGI played it smartly -- starting internationally against a collection of Asian and U.S. stars taken in for a tour of Mongolia. After that, he came back to the United States to start in San Diego. After some time in that state, JAGI moved out to Germany for an extended tour, now heading back to the United States. Known as something of a journeyman, a federation that can provide JAGI with a place to hang his hat and let him be himself would stand a good chance of keeping JAGI for the long-term. Other Feds 4CW, TGW, MONGOL!, other places you've never heard of Other Fed Titles Not yet. Other Fed Awards Sexiest Asian man in the history of pro wrestling Past Managers Self-managed Team Has a clan in Modern Warfare 2 Past Teams & Groups Nope. Wrestling Style Hapkido with Lucha influence Finisher Move Northern Lights Bomb (primary) / Blood Chokes (desperation) Finisher Description Shotgun Wedding / What Dreams May Come Special Moves Ja-Gi-T: A Jumping DDT, often chained from another attack. Ars Nova: A Crucifix Driver from special positions. Moon Over Verona: A high-flying Senton Bomb, can also be a secondary finisher against enemies that Ja Gi can't lift for the Shotgun Wedding.
