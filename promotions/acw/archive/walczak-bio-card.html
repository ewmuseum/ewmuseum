Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: None ACW Awards: None Other Federations: None Other Federations Awards: None Career History For Gabriel, Life had always been a street fight. It didn't matter wether he was playing soccer on the concrete in Red Hook Park, punching out some punk who made fun of his heritage, battering bodies on his high school football fiend, or brawling in the stands at his old stomping grounds, Darkside Wrestling (DSW). Nobody ever gave Gabriel a free pass to anything. He walked into All-Star Wrestling Championship expecting no favors and quickly realized he wasn't going to get any. Despite his fame, his enviable contract, and the fact that he's affilated with the premier league in wrestling, Gabriel wakes up every day with the same bad attitude he had in the old days in Dannemora. Dannemora had always been an out-of-the-way place, avoided by New York high soceity, and relegated to those forced to make a living with their hands, loading and unloading the ships in New York Harbor. By the time Gabriel was a kid the shipping industy had abandoned the neighborhood. Fortunate resides made an exodus to places with better opportunities. Those who remanied had to fight for the few scraps left behind. Gabriel might not have been the biggest kid in Dannemora, but he was certainly one of the toughest. It was not a reputation he'd earned simply becuase he knew how to use his fists. Gabriel cultivated his fighting skills by studing judo. It was a discipline he'd never abandon. To this day, he believes his deadly 'Flatline' suplex can be attributed to his martial arts traning. At Dannemora Union Free High School Gabriel continued building his name. He was a nose guard and offensive guard for the school's football team and, more notably, the only student in the history of Union Free to be named captain of the squad three years in a row. Gabriel's entree into the sport of kings came courtesy of World Pro Wrestling Hall Of Famer Dominic Samartino. Samartino was never a superstar, but his opponents recall that, pound for pound, he was one of the most dangerous men in the companys history. Fortunatly for Gabriel, Samartino happened to live in Dannemora, and -- after meeting the studen on a cold winder day on Dalton Avenue -- taught the young man to never take anything for granted. Once you give your opponent a moment to catch his breath, Samartino reasoned, you're a dead man. There were no million-dollar contracts waiting for Gabriel when his wrestling traning was complete. He worked in high school gymnasiums and at county fairs, driving home from matches with his teeth loose and gauze sticking to his bloody forehead. But his riotous styled earned himt he attention of small independent promoters. Eventually, he became a bright star in the industry, a guy who never stopped trying, even when it appeared that he was ready for the ambulance or the morgue. As a result, he was recruited into DSW. His tenure in the group wasn't suppose to last long. He was brought in as an opponent for Andy Hartley, one of the organization's rising stars. Instead of lasting a few dates in DSW, Gabriel remained there for nearly five years, eventually winning the group's championship belt. On many occasions he was battered so badly that his career appeared to be over. In 2002, he was momentairly paralyzed when Andy Hartley and Rick Styles punished him with a spike piledriver. Gabriel was out of action for nearly 7 months, and experts predicted his retirement. Instead, he reutrned to meet out revenge. Today, as he walks through the dressing room of the All-Star Championship Wrestling, he's gained the respect of many, and he often finds himself in a huddle with the more experienced wrestlers, hoping to feed off of their energy. Gabriel realizes that the game is a snake pit full of menaces waiting to take a bit out of him at ever chance, but he has to find his way before he's lost in the shuffle. Working with the older guys, will give him exactly what he needs, and that's the guidance to succeed in a company filled with much more ring wise competitors.
