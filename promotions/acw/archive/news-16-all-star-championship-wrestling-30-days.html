All-Star Championship Wrestling 30 Days...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-Star Championship Wrestling 30 Days... Gabriel Malik Walczak has been suspended from active competition for thirty days and will be removed from the active roster. Once every 30 days, every ACW superstar is drug tested. Gabriel Malik Walczak hadn't even completed one month under ACW contract, but due to the date he signed his contract, he was up for the testing late last week. Gabriel failed his urine and blood test for various illegal substances, ranging from Cocain to Cannibus. The following is a brief answer he gave as to the drug usage, and his future plans. 'I failed the test, what more can I say. I haven't touched any cannibus recently, but it stays in your system for 45 days. Infact I haven't smoked any cannibus since signing with ACW. As far as the cocain goes, I do admit I used it recently. It wasn't my intent to do so, but as I returned home from one of the events, I found a celliphane package stashed away under the lamp in my house. Picked it up, tasted it and one thing led to another. So I failed. Now that I've been taken off of the roster, I'm seeking help. I've enrolled myself willing into a rehabilitation center known as 'Our House' In Charleston Illinois, and hopefully will come out of this clean and a better person. When this is all over with, I'd love to remain in ACW. Just wait, I'll be back.' Gabriel spoke of his plans, and seems to have a positive outlook on his venture into the drug rehabilitation world. We wish him the best in his journey, and are looking foward to his work once he's a clean man. If we get any more information, I'll keep you posted.
