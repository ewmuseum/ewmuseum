Shef Boiadee's Stew

24 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shef Boiadee's Stew Posted by Shef Boiadee on January 24, 2005 at 10:56:34: Hiya, My name is Shef Boiadee. Yes, I am a great cook, but what I am best at is evaluating wrestling shows. ACW has caught my eye with their unique style of presenting their show. I have evaluated each part of the show and cooked up what I thought of the show. The Oddest Couple Of All- Indeed an odd couple! Alias really layed down the law and certainly was an awkward situation for Alias. Up The Creek without a Paddle- Sounds like a title of a horrible movie with Scott EEEEEEEEvil in it. Silverhawk really told the woman named Flawless in this part of the show. Flawless getting a little case of the SARS...if you know what I mean ;) Listen up for Once- The Normans came to the ring. Gloating over last week's victory and Trevor really underestimating the Sharpness of Andy Sharp. Also threatened to leave if ACW didn't give them better opponents. Makes me want to cook up a recipe of Whoopdatarse on them. Superstar, Meet the SuperChumps- The world champ made his prescene as he layed out an open challenge for the world title. I wonder who will put their name on that paper? Andy Sharp Vs. Trevor Norman- Finally! a match! Lowell Dot Com should be shot! Cost Andy Sharp the match! I hope Sharp puts Lowell Dot Com in his place! Taking It's Toll- Silverhawk contemplating what matches he could cook up. A Celebration for Trevor- The stupid Norman's celebrated a tainted victory :( Thine Gauntlet is Puteth Down Before Thee- Sounds like a knight is coming. TMM and Slapnutz challenge the sorry Normans for their titles. Go TMM and Slapnutz~! Alias vs. Seymour Almasy- Alias once again defeating another opponent...what a shocker =/ The past fortels the future- The ACW staff is still contemplating a match. Make your decision already! A Magnificent Debut- A new face to ACW. Got to hate guys like this who disrespect the federation and take lightly of the place. Furthermore, he was very sarcastic throughout the whole segment and really angered the fans. Once again another stupid open challenge that needs to be accepted to shut his mouth. Kelly Flawless Vs. Fejona Min for the TV title- Yikes! The Flawless one in a match!? Going for the TV title!? and Could she win!? Yes, Yes and No =( Hate 2 Break it 2 You- The stupid Normans declined the challenge. That figures that you'd accept a challenge. That would be stupid to accept a challenge you'd lose! The untitled (Part 2)- God's Forgotten Son has finally arrived. He's awesome! Hostility between Almasy and GFS in this part of the show. Vince Jacobs Vs. Slapnutz for the world title- Well slap my nutz and call my Charlie, Slapnutz accepted the open challenge! Would he be victorious and become the new world champion? No. But it was nice to dream about it for a second there. Laying Down The Law- The world title match for breakOUT was made as SVJ will have to defend vs. Keller, Alias and the grand ole mighty GFS. Good show overall. Courage 69 should be interesting.
