Dante Inferno Real Name: Dante Dokic Birthdate: 24/12/1975 Hometown: Atlanta, Georgia Height: 6’7’’ Weight: 302 lbs. Nicknames: Hellraiser Status: Neutral Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Did My Time' By KoRn Wrestling Style: Powerhouse Signature Moves: Obviously this can’t be done to all wrestlers, as some may be too heavy. For others moves such as various DDT’s (Flowing, snap, tornado) may be used. Snap Powerbomb, jackhammer, big boot, German suplex. Set Up Move: ( ) Finisher: Heaven/Hell. (As Dante’s opponent comes from the ropes, or runs in his direction, Dante pushes him up in the air, and then puts his arm around the man/women’s neck before they both plummet to the canvas. In a Stone Cold Stunner fashion.) ACW Title History: ACW United States Champion ACW Award History: None Other Federations: reVenge Other Federation Awards: None Handler Information Handler Name: - - - - - - - - - - - - AIM Name: - - - - - - - - - - - - MSN Name: - - - - - - - - - - - - Biography Dante Dokic was born on Christmas Eve of the year 1975, but not without any complications. His birth was the main cause of his mothers death, as she had a major hemorrhage after he was born. He and his mother only spent precious seconds together, but he still believes to this day that he knows her better than any individual on this planet ever did. With no mother and a father who had fled as he found out he would have another mouth to provide for, Dante was taken to an Orphans Home, where he would spend his first year of life. After his first infant year had past, we was given to a loving family, James and Betty Johnson, who would care for him for the next 18 years. The Johnson's where a very religious family, and that had to be passed on to their new foster son. Dante attended many hours at church, helped with varies activities the local parish provided. He even read numerous books about the teachings of the bible in his spare time. As he bore into his teenage years however, things began to come into a different light for him. As he was told about his original family, and the complications of his birth, he began to change his thoughts and feeling on everything which his life was structure upon. Everything that he believed in, the teaching of the Bible, had taught him that if he acted in a proper manner, he would be praised for it in later life. He couldn't understand why such a thing would happen at his birth, why would he be punished if he had not even taken a breathe in this world, never mind acted in sin? He began to question the teachings, questions that not many people could answer to him without decorating their words with lies and fiction. His questions where questions that the living cannot answer. Dante was so driven by these questions, that he more or less had taken for granted all his life, that he needed answers. Nobody living could give him those answers, so he would have to ask on the other side. He believed in heaven. He also believed that there was no reason why he shouldn't go their, so, he attempted suicide by slitting his wrists and leaving himself to bleed to death. He didn't die. He was found by his foster father who more or less found him in a conscious state, even though he had lost a lot of blood. While in the hospital the doctors said that he should have died, but they didn't know why. Dante tried to kill himself on a number of occasions after that attempt, but somehow they went wrong. --- 12/8/1984 - He tried to stab himself in the heart, but he missed the major artery and survived. 22/10/1985 - After collecting a gun from his fathers cabinet, he tried to shoot himself in the head, but the trigger had seized up, and the gun began unable to use. --- There where more of these occurrences, where his attempt at death failed in one way or another. In his darkest hour however, he seen the light. On Christmas Eve of 1993, Dante's 18th Birthday, after a suicide attempt where he swallowed a full tub of aspirin, he received a vision. Of his mother. This vision changed his life for the better, it conveyed a message to him that his mothers death was not in vain, but it was so that this very moment could concur. Dante was here for a purpose, it was just up to him to see what that purpose was. Dante was brought up well. Even though he had put his parent through a lot in his short stay on this earth, they still loved him as much as they did the first day they placed their eyes on his infant years. Dante left home soon after the vision, trying to find his purpose in the World that he had wanted to leave only a short time ago. Being mugged and beat up for money was never a good thing, especially when you where in a foreign place. With his home leagues away, no money, and little clothes on his back, he had to take refuge in a homeless shelter in New York City, his current state of residence. NY was where he initially learned how to fight, for money, in an underground boxing league which gave everyday 9 till 5 people a chance to let it all out. The business men paid to either watch a fight, or paid to join it. Being a monster of a man, Dante was acquired to be one of the fighters in it. Dante was never injured. Dante was never beaten. He amazed many people with his natural talent when it came to fighting, it seemed as though the mugging was another challenge that fate had had planned for some time. After making enough money to settle some differences with a few people, he then left NY looking to gather his thoughts again on the task. To find his purpose. Only time will tell if he ever does find it, or will fate have another stumbling block in store? Experience Archives test Demons Testing The Limit's Of One's Faith Hell.
