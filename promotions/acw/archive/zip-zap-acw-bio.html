Zip Zap Biography Real Name Bobby Daniel [name withheld] Nicknames The Fastest Man Alive Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday 7-17-84 Place of Birth Chelsea, England Height 5'7 Weight 175 Blood Type A+ Eye Color Green Biography Like his name suggests, incredibly energetic and hyper. Constantly seen bouncing around as if he were hopped up on sugar, he maintains a constant daredevil persona in and out of the ring. Appearing to seek constant attention and approval from Gabriel, he’s often the first to put his body on the line for the group and for his cause. CURRENTLY: One sixth of the current ACW Tag Team Championships. By executive order, Freebird Rules is in effect, any two members of the Night Life may defend the championship. Team The Night Life Wrestling Style Lucha libre/American independent style. Favors constant hit-and-run attacks, will chop down opponents by attacking the legs, can also attack opponents from all angles. Treats the ring as a playground. Finisher Move 630 Splash Finisher Description The Zapper Special Moves Catapault into corner Dropkick, Corkscrew Plancha, Various Double Knee Strikes, Running Knee to cornered opponent, Roundhouse Kick, Various outside dives, Diving Double Foot Stomp (sometimes with flips), Moonsault Side Slam, Various standing splashes (anything from moonsault to standing corkscrew SSP), Shocking Elbow (http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UN3YVMDQaRI&feature=related) Entrance Music 'Slam' by Pendulum Trainer The Amazing Gabriel
