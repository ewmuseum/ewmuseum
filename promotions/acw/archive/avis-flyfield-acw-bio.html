Avis Flyfield Biography Real Name David Jonathon Flyfield Nicknames The Wing Commander, the Bird of Prey, The Captain Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday Age 26 (March 21st, 1982) Place of Birth Seattle, Washington Height 6'3 Weight 223 Blood Type B+ Eye Color Brown Biography -A Seattle native coming from a long line of wealthy industrialists who helped build airline manufacturer, Boeing, from the ground up. Spent most of his life pampered and spoiled rotten. -Father cut him off when he realized the lazy bum was doing absolutely nothing with his life. As a result, kicked the poor boy on his ass. -Longtime friend Aaron Fetzer convinced him that he was in shape enough to get into wrestling, so with the remainder of his money, visited Elijah Brown in Kentucky with hopes of learning to wrestle. -Traveling around from indy fed to indy fed while gaining a cult following. Though mostly job fodder, is going to put up a goddamn fight every step of the way. -Believe it or not, holds a victory over Seymour Almasy. Granted, like, twelve dudes helped him win, but he still did it. Personality: -Happy-go-lucky personality in the squared circle. Once an annoying heel in ACW’s last run, Avis Flyfield is now a slightly more confident, charismatic babyface the fans love to get behind. -He’s obsessed with planes, jets, and makes lots of horrible puns having to do with flying, but people have looked past that and have grown to respect him for putting on some decent performances over the last couple years. Other Feds Ringside Wrestling (current), The Squared Circle (current) Other Fed Titles Ringside Wrestling Fellowship Champion, The Squared Circle Tag Team Champion Other Fed Awards 8-time Golden Wings winner (some award show he hosts from his basement once a week) Team Pi in the Sky w/Mr. Wrestling Pi Past Teams & Groups Still stuck with Fetzer and Adam Nowell Wrestling Style Sports Entertainment/Bump Taker Finisher Move The Afterburn Finisher Description Double-Knee Backbreaker Special Moves Forearm Smashes, Punches and Kicks (of the flashy Sports Entertainment kind), Scoop Slam, Enzuigiri, Running Back Elbow/Forearm, DDT, Missile Dropkick, Springboard Clothesline, Fireman's Carry into Flapjack, Snapmare followed by dropkick/Soccer kicks. Loop The Loop (Tornado DDT) The 747 (Flying Headbutt) Entrance Music 'Highway to the Danger Zone' by Kenny Loggins Trainer The Everlasting School of Excellence Manager 'Cadet' Aaron Fetzer
