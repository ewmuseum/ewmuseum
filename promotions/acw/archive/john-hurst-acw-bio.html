John Hurst Biography Hometown Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada Birthday September 26, 1980 Place of Birth Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada Height 6'1 Weight 243 Blood Type B- Eye Color Brown Biography John Hurst wrestled in Japan, man. Japan. For five fucking years. They take wrestling seriously in Japan. Did you ever wrestle in Japan? No. You fucking didn't. That's why John Hurst is better than you. Wrestling Style Suplexes and Strikes Finisher Move Leghook backdrop driver Finisher Description Violence Special Moves Black Pawn's Gambit [European uppercut >> side kick to the knee >> rising knee to the face.] All done in quick succession. Black Knight's Gambit [Punch rush >> fireman's carry >> mountain bomb.] The opponent's arm is trapped during/after the fireman's carry and used to turn them around and bring them to their feet for the mountain bomb. Bishop Eats Queen [Thai clinch >> rising knee to the face (release Thai clinch) >> side kick to the stomach >> reverse roundhouse axe kick to the back of the head.] The axe kick knocks his opponent to the mat. Pawn Eats Queen [European uppercut >> Thai clinch >> rising knee to the face (release Thai clinch) >> side kick to the stomach >> reverse roundhouse axe kick to the back of the head.] Exploder 270 [Exploder with a 270° rightward spin.] Entrance Music "Top of the Line" by M.O.P.
