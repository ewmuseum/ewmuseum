Jade Real Name: Jade Greene Birthdate: March 19th 1978 Hometown: Ontario, Canada Height: 5' 11' Weight: 135 lbs. Nicknames: The Jaded One Status: Neutral Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'One Step Closer' by Linkin Park Wrestling Style: High Flyer/Mat Technition Signature Moves: Hurricanrana, Dropkick, Closeline Set Up Move: ( ) Finisher: Jaded Skies (A frontflip off the turnbuckle onto the shoulders of her opponent, pulling out a hurricanrana, tossing her opponent to the mat or outside the ring over the top rope.) AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Biography Jade was raised in Ontario Canada with her older brother Brian and her two younger sisters Brigit and Jessie. The four of them lived with their father after their mother died in a horrific car accident. After the accident, Jade's father felt like he was responsible, and began to drink heavily. Jade began to spend less and less time at home, and more time working out with the guys from her local wrestling team in the gym after school. The guys finally convinced Jade to join the team, and she dominated the league. After coming in second place for the Provincials, Jade won a scholarship to UCLA for wrestling and graduated two years early. Jade only had one good friend in high school, Drew Wilkie. He and her were two of a kind, and could always be seen pulling pranks on the principal or their teachers. If one was in the office, often the other wasn't far behind. Leaving her family and her only friend Drew behind, Jade went away to college following her wrestling career. She was dormed in the co-ed housing units, and was roomed with a guy who went by the initials AB. AB (standing for the American Badass) and Jade became the closest of friends, and were inseperable throughout their entire college experience. Winning wrestling competitions together, and taking home the most gold two students of UCLA have ever dreamed of, Jade and AB thought they had it made. When they finally finished their few years, Jade and AB split directions and followed their own paths to wrestling. Jade was accepted in a local federation, the World Class Wrestling Alliance and AB was accepted to one across the country. A few months later AB was back in Jade's life, but this time hired as a bodyguard for Jade's little sister Brigit (aka Opium). Brigit had located Jade after she abandoned her family three years earlier, and Jade was concerned with the amount of hatred going on within the fed and between herself and one other named 'The Rob'. Hiring AB to protect Brigit, him and Jade kept in touch, by moving into the same house together. Although never romantically involved, AB and Jade were often told they should just give up and get married. In Jade's first few years of college she met a man, who she thought she fell in love with. Eric 'Highlight' Reil was another wrestler she met and they decided to start dating. The two of them were always seen together, and decided to get engaged. The rocky relationship ended after Eric split from the WCWA after college, leaving Jade and everything else behind without a word. After Eric, Jade didn't trust a guy for a very long time. She then met Bonz and Justin Tyme of the Prime Time Players. Her and Bonz hit it off right away, and began to date, taking things slowly at first, but then getting quite involved. Jade and the Prime Time Players came across a stable of individules who called themselves 'The Industry'. Rein, Skye and DDW were the three main wrestlers, who made most of the decisions. Automatically Jade and Rein developed a firey red passion of hatred for each other. Their two attitudes just clashed, and they hated each other in every way possible, thus starting the fued of the PTP'ers and The Industry! Jade couldn't hate every member of the Industry, as DDW was her best friend 'Dangerous' Drew Wilkie, from high school. The most anticipated duel through all of Jade's wrestling career, was that of her best friend and her boyfriend. The feud that never happened, DDW vs. Bonz will always be the angle which never was! Wrestling was still the main priority in both lives of Jade and Bonz, and Bonz ended up accepting a deal with another federation, therefore leaving Jade behind. He could not stand by and watch Jade still be friends with DDW, after the horrific temperment and feud they had with Rein and Skye. When Bonz left, Jade thought her luck was up, and gave up on looking for a relationship. Rein and Skye had long deserted DDW and wrestling altogether, so it was okay for him to start living with Jade, just as friends. In moved 'La Loca Wop' Gino Dagino, her sister Brigit as well as AB, and they had formed their one little happy family, naming themselves, 'The Revolution'!!! When the WCWA closed, it turned into new management and was reformed as the AWW. A short lived life as AWW was ended with the sudden desertion of the CEO's, and everyone was out of a job. A few of them moved to Steele Wrestling, but again their careers were cut short, due to the idiocy of the management. DDW had decided after this last closing, he wanted to retire and left AB and Jade alone to find their futures. Jade joined the uKw after leaving SW. She didn't really fit in well until meeting up with a man named Curtis Reile. They started to date for a while, but because of his former manager, Stacey Powers, Jade screwed him over, joining up with his rival and former friend, Jason Kain. The two of them have been dating, and winning gold all over the federation since. Jade is on hiatis now, waiting to hear back from a few federations, but is also hoping to be reunited with Jason in the All-Star Championship Wrestling Federation as soon as possible.
