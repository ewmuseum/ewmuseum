A C W BLACKEST NIGHT WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 006 ¤ 28 JANUARY 2011 ARENA VFG, GUADALARAJA, MEXICO Once the cue was given, the cameras started rolling and WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 006 began beaming to thousands of fans online! The pyrotechnics were of the highest order as about 6,500 fans welcomed back ACW to the Arena VFG, many of whom were still reeling from the events of COURAGE 132 the night before. The citizens of Guadalaraja served up a hell of an atmosphere nonetheless, simply salivating at the line-up of confirmed matches that lay ahead in the evening. SINGLES MATCH - SPIRIT OF ACW TITLE: Jonnie Morningsocks vs. Andy Sharp © But rather disappointingly for the fans, it was Andy Sharp's title defense which would be the first match of the show. Which meant a whole bunch of Cabalites would be accompanying him, as had been the case the night before. Sharp's plucky challenger? Jonnie Morningsocks, an independent talent on the Mexican wrestling scene. Sharp recoiled in disgust as he stepped into the ring; young Jonnie wore no proper footwear. Nope, all he wore... were in fact very thick, multi-coloured socks. Yes, it was highly bizarre. That aside, Jonnie appeared to be very physically imposing, and had a couple of inches of height over Sharp. Mr. All-Star had the power of Cabalite Rules, though, which meant Jonnie never stood a chance to begin with. It took Sharp -- and his bag of tricks and his fellow Cabalites -- all of two minutes to dispose of Jonnie Morningsocks, courtesy of The All-Star Break. Once the match was done and over with, Andy Sharp motioned for his fellow Cabalites to teach Jonnie a lesson. Iceman stepped up to the plate, insisting that Kati and Jack Harris and Kesavan return to the back to prepare for their upcoming match. Iceman -- still being massively reviled for his part in last night's World Title Match -- proceeded to de-socks Jonnie Morningsocks and stuff said socks into Jonnie's mouth. Yikes. Evidently, Iceman was having fun being a Cabalite goon, and left the ringside area proclaiming all the fans 'cocksuckers'. Junior Cabalites State Their Intent As 2010 reached its conclusion, three individuals that were also adjunct roster members of the All-Star Academy had found themselves in an absolute rut. Thankfully for them, they found a reprieve in the form of acceptance into an extension of the cabal that was running rampant in the Academy. And since then, these three individuals have found themselves in the shadows, waiting for an opportunity to rise up. MIS, Koji Takada and 'The Natural' Nathan Adams found themselves joined by Olivia McMullen; ACW's newest backstage interviewer and Jenna's sister. It seemed that the trio had been granted a shot at the ACW Tag Team Titles. The holders of which happened to be their fellow Cabalites, who were higher-up in the gang's hierachy. MIS, speaking in his capacity as team captain, told Olivia McMullen that this match had been arranged by Z, as a way to test the mettle of the young Cabalites. MIS swore that while he and Nathan and Koji respected the current Tag Team Champs... there would be no holding back. Koji chimed in, stating that he hoped the Tag Team Champions were ready for a battle of a lifetime. Nathan Adams finished off by insisting that it was time ACW embraced new blood, for the age of 'over-exposed dinosaurs' was coming to an end. SINGLES MATCH - STANDARD RULES: Remi De Rozario vs. Fejona Min Remi De Rozario had a fruitful if ultimately unsuccessful debut on COURAGE 131. He would have another chance to kick-start his ACW career on this night. The Costa Rican Bullet was full of bubbling enthusiasm in the ring, spurred on by the fans giving him a modest reaction. All of that went south when Remi's opponent made herself known: Fejona Min, The Rogue Slayer. This was to be Fejona's first match since pulling double-duty at END GAME 2010, and the Goddess Of Fight -- complete with a black duffel bag with unidentified contents -- appeared to be at peace with the ferocity of the crowd's hate. With a wicked smile fixed on her flawless face, Min entered the squared circle eager to add another victim to her unbeaten WRESTLECIDE streak. Remi, to his credit, fought valiantly and didn't prove to be a pushover. But against a Cantonese Killer Queen in an especially vicious mood, the Costa Rican Bullet had absolutely no chance whatsoever of gaining victory, eventually falling to Min's Elegy finisher. After Remi limped out of the ringside area, Fejona Min asked for the undivided attention of the fans. She didn't get it. Obviously. Fejona didn't mind; she instead delved into the black duffel bag she'd brought out and produced... a title belt? An old one, at that. Min went on to explain that in her hands, was a relic from the past: theAsylum's Women's Championship. How utterly random. At any rate, the Goddess Of Fight remarked that she had just achieved six wins out of six matches across all six editions of WRESTLECIDE, which she felt deserved a reward. A reward in the form of a newly-christened title belt; the ACW Internet Championship, seeing how WRESTLECIDE was a Web-exclusive show. The fans shat all over that, outraged that Fejona would be go egotistical to pull such a stunt. On forums all across the Internet, a mighty furore was raised. Probably. Anyways, Fejona stated that if anybody could win five matches on WRESTLECIDE in a row decisively, they would get a crack at the Internet Championship. Alternatively, if there was anybody who wrestled Fejona in the months to come and could get her to submit, Min would personally hand over the Internet Title to them. With a bow and a middle finger, the Cantonese Killer Queen took her leave, rendering the capacity crowd inside the Arena VFG speechless and furious and debating over the legality over this supposed Internet Championship. The Return Of The Ohh Gee Another trip backstage yielded a surprising result; the return of Orlando Grant! The OG of ACW was joined by a personal friend of his, Wayne Skillet. Wayne spoke on Orlando's behalf, stating that The Ziploc Boy wasn't able to speak because he was deep in preparation mode for the night's main event. Wayne, however, told Olivia McMullen that Orlando was finally glad to be in action, having fully recovered from his beating at the hands of Ryan Billows way back on COURAGE 130. Orlando suddenly snatched the microphone out of his friend's hands and spit the truth: his first agenda was to snap his losing streak, and to do so by avenging a one-year-old grudge against Shawn Jessica Hart. His second agenda? Taking down Z and the rest of his Cabalites, in order to save his friend in John Sarsgaard! With that, Orlando tossed the microphone aside and stormed off, leaving Wayne Skillet to tell Olivia McMullen one simple fact. "OG's back, yo! And he's mad as all hell! Word!" TRIOS TAG TEAM MATCH - ACW TAG TEAM TITLES: MIS, Koji Takada & 'The Natural' Nathan Adams vs. The Cabalites KJK © Back in the ring, two sets of Cabalites were ready to dance. To the trio of Kati, Jack Harris and Kesavan... this was nothing more than an exhibition defense of their newly-won Tag Team Titles. But unknown to them, the junior Cabalites had every intention of going all the way. MIS and Kati started things off, with MIS being the captain of his team. And right from the start, Kati and Harris and Kesavan (assigned captain for his team) found themselves surprised by the fact that their junior Cabalites weren't holding back at all. MIS and Koji exchanged frequent tags, isolating Kati from her two giant team-mates. Every opportunity she had of rolling out of the ring and thereby activating a tag was thwarted by Nathan Adams, who was relishing this massive opportunity. It would be because of Nathan Adams that Kati would, rather shockingly, be the first to be eliminated. After managing to counter Koji's powerbomb attempt into a hurricarana, Kati was well on her way to her corner turnbuckle. Nathan Adams, in an inspired show of not-giving-a-fuck, smacked Harris and Kesavan in the back of their knees with a steel chair! Koji was able to tag in MIS, who made short work of the toxic-haired siren and eliminated her from the match following a sit-out powerbomb! Unfortunately for the junior Cabalites, the advantage wouldn't last all too long. Nathan Adams continued to wail on Jack Harris and Kesavan at ringside, drawing the ire of referee Tomaz Heinreich who had let Nathan's initial attack on the two giants of the cabal with a steel chair slide. Yet, Nathan Adams continued to push his luck (in more ways than one) and even busted The Pikey Madman open with a vicious swing of the chair! Well, that was it for 'The Natural' Nathan Adams. He got disqualified by Tomaz Heinreich; some would deem it a controversial call, but the fans didn't care for any of the six Cabalites, so it didn't matter to them. Nathan ranted and raved as he was being ejected from the ringside area, but he did serve up Harris on a platter to MIS and Koji Takada. One tandem front suplex later, and Jack Harris found himself out of the match. That just left Kesavan, also badly beaten, left for his team. Suddenly, it appeared as if a shocking upset was about to come to pass. But if MIS and Koji Takada think that the Mongolian Shaman Of Black Arts was going to just throw in the towel? Neh eh. Kesavan shook off the attacks by Nathan Adams and his steel chair and went to town on MIS and Koji. In the space of three minutes, he'd demolished Koji with a tremendous chokeslam and then put MIS down with a crucifix powerbomb. The Cabalities KJK had managed to retain the Tag Team Titles, but just barely. Jack Harris and Kati joined Kesavan with their muted celebrations in the ring, with the junior Cabalites on the outside rueing what could have been. And also, probably some backlash for the unexpected ferocity and fight in their challenge. SINGLES MATCH - STANDARD RULES: Valley Baptiste vs. Thugson Valley Baptiste had found himself a recipient of an unwanted losing streak. However, after last night's shenanigans involving Bobby Minio, Valley marched out to the ring with vim and vigour not seen for a while. His opponent in this exhibition match? New hire Thugson, who was revealed to be Overlord Jeremy Hunt's latest acquisition and replacement for Turncoat Keller in his Huntsmen stable. Thugson, as expected, looked like three-day old trash. Smelled like it, too. Nonetheless, Valley Baptiste was more than ready for this one, adamant to turn around his luck and get back on victory road. And by all accounts, Valley was well on his way towards accomplishing his goal, with Thugson bamboozled by Valley's aerial ability. Thugson was able to slow his opponent down with a squelching clothesline two minutes in, but wasted the follow-up: "Cylons! I see Cylons! I KNEW IT!" Thugson ranted, confusing the ever-living heck of out of everybody. Valley took advantage, rolling to his feet and catching Thugson with a falling neckbreaker. Wasn't enough for the win, but it did swing the match back into his favour. Thugson, however, was a very competent brawler (seeing how he'd been trained by two of the toughest brawlers on the roster) and was once again able to claw his way back into contention. The fans were riveted, surprised by the roller-coaster nature of this seemingly straightforward match. It wasn't until the appearance of Bobby Minio, of all people, that the match took a turn. Valley Baptiste didn't notice Minio at first, until he had Thugson primed for a sleeper hold. Minio chose that moment to hop onto the ring apron, garnering Valley's attention. Thugson took advantage, nailing Valley with a knee-lift to the gut followed by an elbow strike to the back of the head. Six seconds later, Thugson was perched atop one of the corner turnbuckles. "FOR YOU, ADAMA!" he screamed, right before busting out a flying headbutt splash. There was no kicking out from that, and Thugson was victorious in his second match ever. Post-match, Bobby Minio entered the ring and watched as Valley slowly pulled himself together, pissed that he'd lost another match. A match he was on the cusp of winning, too. Before the young European prodigy could even ask Minio why he'd come out, the reluctant Cabalite connected with a thrust kick to the face. Bobby Minio went on to brutally attack Valley Baptiste, drawing blood even. Security were eventually able to break it up, but it was far too late. What was up with Bobby Minio? We didn't want to know, to be honest. TAG TEAM MATCH - STANDARD LUCHA RULES: Quintana Rogerio Santiago & Carrachio Salfuego vs. The Locksmiths Next up, was what many fans were waiting for all night. A tag team match involving four individuals that could definitely bring it. Sure, The Locksmiths were rather new to ACW but just by the look of the two brothers walking down to the ring (sans theme music), everybody knew that Teddy and Alastair could bring the goods and that they meant business. The next team? Why, it was the pairing of Quintana Rogerio Santiago and Carrachio Salfuego! Both men had found themselves on the fringes of the roster, seeing how crowded the roster had gotten over the last month and a half. Still, that didn't bother Quintana and Carrachio a single bit. They instead relished in the fact that they had an opportunity to team up with each other, and engage their fellow upstarts in a match in a packed-to-the-rafters arena. Quintana and Teddy, captains for their respective teams, started the match. And it didn't take long for all hell -- the good variety -- to break loose. Constant tags, double-team moves, high-flying antics from Quintana and Carrachio, technical groundwork from Teddy and Alastair; this match had it all. The fans were on the edge of their seats, watching on with great interest. Carrachio Salfuego even dared to bust out a frog splash that didn't pay dividends, which allowed Teddy Locke to lock in his favoured STF submission hold. Thankfully for Carrachio, he was close to the ropes. Not-so-thankfully, Teddy was able to drag Carrachio back to the middle of the ring just as the latter had reached 'em. With no choice, Carrachio tapped out. Teddy felt that he and his brother were on the verge of victory. But QRS had other ideas. First, he took Alastiar out by leapfrogging Teddy's charging shoulder block and transitioning into a cartwheel dropkick. Secondly, he was able to hold off Teddy's spirited onslaught and pulled out his bridging dragon suplex finisher out of nowhere! Alastiar, having taken some time to recover on the outside, was too late in getting into the ring. Quintana had held on for the 3, and secured the victory by virtue of pinning the opposing team's captain! An excellent tag team match, and all four men shared looks of acknowledgedment after the bell, knowing that they'd just put on a mini-classic and that they would perhaps lock horns down the road again. SINGLES MATCH - ACW GATEWAY TITLE: Dolf Biggler vs. Billows © Dolf Biggler hates masked men. He made that entirely clear 24 hours prior. Unfortunately, Dolf was unable to harness his disdain into a victory over ACW's latest masked luchadore, DIM a.k.a. Dude In Mask. Yet, it appeared as if Overlord Jeremy Hunt saw something in Biggler he liked, and awarded Dolf with a shot at ACW's newest title belt. Held currently, in case you forgot, by Billows. No Ryan, just Billows. Dolf Biggler's eyes shimmered as he watched Billows slowly saunter down to the ring. Surprisingly absent from the broadcast the night before, Billows appeared to be having trouble concentrating on the fact that he had a match. Looking as if he'd just a returned from a week's worth of hardcore partying, Billows paid no attention to Biggler in the ring, instead choosing to sneer at the fans in the front rows. That's Billows for you: he loves to stick it to the fans! When Billows finally got into the squared circle, Dolf Biggler decided to take the bull by the horns and unleashed a barrage of high-impact offense. The Risen Lion was not to be outdone; he was, after all, Overlord Jeremy Hunt's new golden boy, and for good reason. That only served to add to the incredibly competitive nature of the match, Dolf Biggler surprisingly showing that he could hang with the legendary Billows and earning some cheers in the process. Unfortunately, Billows isn't considering one of the most lethal finishers in the business today for a reason. Countering a cross body from Biggler into a powerslam, Billows went on to down Biggler with his Trend-Ender finisher. First title defense in the books? Check. Fans irate? Check. All that was left for Billows to do? Annoy the fans even more by raising the Gateway Title high above his head, while in the background Biggler had to be carted out of the ring by EMTs. In this sort of form, Billows = virtually unbeatable. And that wasn't a comforting fact for everybody wanting to challenge for the Gateway Championship. TAG TEAM MATCH - STANDARD LUCHA RULES: Elias Morales & Raul Campos vs. DysFunction Several months ago, the tandem of Rokko Strange and Aya Sumiko -- ex-lovers who'd learned how to live together despite their storied past together -- were signed to ACW on part-time contracts. Ever since then, a whole slew of tag teams have joined the organisation, making ACW's tag team division something to watch out for. Strange and Sumiko (DysFunction) came out for this match, probably hoping that a win over their opponents would push them up the congested contendership ladder, which in turn would mean full-time contracts for them. Said opponents were new hires: Mexico's own Elias Morales and Cuban stalwart Raul Campos. Accompanying them, was their manager Mahmood. Before the match began, Mahmood decided to do some impromptu vocalising: he introduced himself and his charges, and explained to Rokko and Aya that this match was more than a match. More than sorting out who moves up the ladder and who moves down. "This match is a treat for the fans! My guys are great. I'm sure you guys can bring it. Let's tear the house down. Let's give these fans something to remember!" Mahmud proclaimed boisterously. Naturally, the fans liked the sound of that. Rokko and Aya (the assigned team captain for her team) exchanged looks and nodded. They wanted nothing more than to please the fans, and if the victory fell into their laps, it would be a bonus. Unfortunately, Mahmud's words were merely a distraction. Elias and Raul (the assigned team captain for his team) clocked their opponents from behind and savagely beat both of them down before the bell could be rung. Even at the insistence of referee Tomaz Heinreich, Elias and Raul would not let up... until Mahmud barked at his charges to stop. From there, Raul picked Rokko Strange up and dumped him out of the ring, while Elias dragged Aya to her corner of the ring and dumped her there. With order restored, referee Tomaz Heinreich had no choice but to start the match. Aya was easy pickings for Raul, who dragged Aya back towards the middle of the ring and downed her with a sit-out powerbomb. The three-count was academic, and the match was over since Aya was the captain of her team. On the outside, Rokko Strange had come to and was hankering for revenge. Alas, Elias Morales was present with a chair. SMACK! SMACK! Two shots to the side of the head, and Rokko was down again. The fans had been duped and conned, as had DysFunction. Morales and Campos retreated with heads held high, and their manager Mahmud smirking it up like a supervillain. Surely, there would be consequences for this heinous act. The Rise Of Hank Wright - 1 With more matches coming up, the HuntTron sizzled to life and the lights in the Arena VFG dimmed dramatically. It was obvious that the fans were about to witness some sort of promotional package. If only they knew. DISCLAIMER: The following video presentation breaks kayfabe on occassion. If you do not know what 'kayfabe' refers to, please proceed to the snack counter. Enjoy, and don't say you haven't been warned. An un-named and intimate arena containing four athletes was shown... complete with 2,000 fans. The last participant, who had just arrived inside the squared circle, received a massive pop as he climbed up onto the top turnbuckle and waved to the fans, who were eating out the palm of his hand. As he came back down to earth, a caption revealed the masked man’s name was ‘Magica’ and another graphic told the viewer they were watching a 4-Way Dance between: Magica, Aurelio Espinoza, The Kamikaze Kid and El Principe. A bunch of names that meant absolutely nothing in the United States or in All-Star Championship Wrestling. Antonio Martinez, the booker of Mexican wrestling promotion MSLL, said: “John (Stein) saw Hank wrestle as ‘The Hurricane’ and loved that personality. Hank really was that in and out of the ring. So, we decided to do videos where we would give our fans weather warnings." One of Hank Wright’s old hype packages is shown: “A Hurricane is predicted for the first of October and is set to hit the Gulf of Mexico. Experts have predicted it’ll be the biggest in the country for more than a century.” Martinez continued: “We’d show videos of hurricanes damaging places around the world and the reasons why they occur. Metaphorically, it was perfect because we actually believed backstage our federation needed someone to come in and cause controversy. When John told me Hank’s name, I loved it. It worked.” Suddenly, at the beginning of a Four-Way Dance pitting the diminutive and beloved Magica against El Principe, Japanese junior The Kamikaze Kid and the hotly-tipped youngster, Aurelio Espinoza... an overwhelming wind effect -- thankfully only in terms of its sound and not force -- echoed around the arena before a pyrotechnic explosion frightened half of the audience. A voice told the fans to brace themselves for a Hurricane as predicted. John Stein laughed boisteriously: “A few actually believed there was going to be a hurricane! Most sane people knew it was all part of the hype but in a matter of weeks it became apparent Hank Wright was the real deal. He ran straight through their heroes and shook wrestling up from day one and his debut was well-crafted. We handled it really, really well and I’m proud of it. I know Hank is too.” ‘The storm’ paved the way for peaceful classical music. That in turn was replaced by a laugh and Hinder’s All-American Nightmare. “LET’S GO!” Stein smiled: “Just before Hank debuted, he wanted to change his name to The All-American Nightmare and we were reluctant because I loved the Hurricane name, gimmick and we’d hyped it up for weeks and weeks. Within two minutes of talking, he had me convinced otherwise and I’m glad he did. He’d have been huge but the name, the entrance music and the way he terrorized Mexicans and their country... he really was the right man in the right place in the right time. He was gold.” Hank stormed to the ring as the four cruiserweights and everyone in the arena watched the six foot six brick shithouse waltzed straight down the aisle with his eyes fixed on everyone, no one in particular. And as the song reached a crescendo, Hank stepped over the top rope and socked Espinoza straight in the jaw, thereafter elevating him effortlessly into the air with a Gorilla-Press Slam and dumping him to the outside. Espinoza literally sailed over the ropes and landed on the concrete with a thud and a groan. The crowd were stunned and so were the other competitors but Hank wasn’t done there. The Kamikaze Kid was like a rabbit caught in the headlights when Wright grabbed him by his long locks and stared down at him. The Asian high-flyer stamped the canvas but his feet were no longer there seconds later as Hank picked the hapless import up and drilled him with a devastating Dominator. El Principe had seen enough and wanted no part of Wright, holding his hands up and then extending his hand to the big man. Hank stared a hole through him and accepted the gesture and as he shook the hand of Principe, one of the best bad guys in the organization, Wright brought his fellow heel chest-to-chest with him and ushered in a boot to the midsection that winded the luchadore. If that wasn’t enough to deprive him of his oxygen, the subsequent delayed Powerbomb succeeded in draining any reserve of air he had left in his body. It left one man… The 5’5", 175-pounder Magica. As Hank Wright turned to face the little legend, who’d never attack anyone from behind, there was an eerie hush amongst the masses. Hank outweighed the Mexican mainstay by some 120lbs and towered over the incredible performer. You could only see Magica’s eyes through his blue and black mask but they were all you needed to see. He was worried, anxious, shitting his tights and ready to give birth to a litter full of kittens… Not that you’d know as he ran straight into the future Tank and unloaded with three shots and a roundhouse kick that had absolutely no effect on the Texan. No, none whatsoever. Hank ushered in a simple knee smash to the stomach that took Magica off his feet immediately and then dragged the dynamic warrior back up by his mask. Hank choked the cruiserweight and picked him up by his throat, dumping him into a neutral corner where he let the pocket-sized superstar have it with four Machine Guns. Fans were wincing at what they saw. Hank pulled Magica out of the corner and then whipped him into the ropes, flattening the masked crusader with a big boot, somewhere in the size 15 region, as he rebounded back into his path. He wasn’t done there though. As he cast a glance over the crowd, the fans began to finally respond to this intruder by booing the bastard for all they were worth. They still didn’t know who he was, though they would soon enough and once they did they’d hate him more than anyone they had in a long time, but he hadn’t left them with a positive first impression and this disrespectful, disgusting treatment of one of their heroes was the rubberstamp. “We weren’t going to use Magica but he insisted on doing it to put Hank over. We were worried because he was our second biggest babyface. Magica told us it wouldn’t affect him and that people would have sympathy for him if Hank destroyed him. Of course, the little guy was right. He was just as popular as ever with the fans and it helped establish Hank as a badass. It turned out to be a win-win situation.” As Magica was recovering, Wright suddenly ran the ropes and then sealed his place among their disaffections with a sickening Sliding D that extracted an arena-wide gasp. Instant notoriety. Hank wasn’t done there though as he barked angrily at the ring announcer to ‘fucking give him a microphone now’ and when he got one, he was roundly booed before he opened his mouth: “Yo no hablo Espanol…but I believe you understand what I said anyway." The jeers only intensified. Twentyfold. “If a picture tells a thousand words, I’ve just written a whole damn novel and you don’t even know who I am. But you will. The hurricane warnings were just the start. By the time I’m done, you’ll be wishing you’d been hit by a real one because I’m going to turn this godforsaken country upside down and drag it into the twenty-first century. Be careful what you wish for, people. I’m your All-American Nightmare.” And, as quickly as he’d come… He was gone again. SINGLES MATCH - SCORPION FIGHTING RULES: Ulf German vs. Der Deutscher Soldat Ulf German had been unceremoniously pulled out of the ring during his Triple Threat IN THE DARK~! Match the night before. The man responsible? None other than his former tag team partner, Der Deutscher Soldat. Nobody knew if they'd see Ulf after that, considering his role in attempting to thwart Z's evil scheme at END GAME. Yet, Ulf German was seen late during the broadcast of COURAGE 132, seemingly about to meet his end once and for all. Despite the doom and gloom of the situation, Ulf demanded only one thing: an opportunity to settle the score with Soldat. It was Ulf, after all, who decided to pluck Soldat out of the All-Star Academy so many months ago and mentor him towards becoming a formidabble force in the ranks of the tag team division in ACW. Now, granted; that didn't happen. Nonetheless, Soldat's ultimate betrayal of Ulf back on COURAGE 128 was a sore point Ulf couldn't quite over, and he was adamant for the chance for personal vindication. Der Deutscher Soldat agreed to the challenge, out of respect, and it appeared to be a done deal when Soldat's fellow Cabalite got the match approved by Overlord Jeremy Hunt. There was none of that when the match -- contested under Scorpion Fighting Rules -- kicked off. Ulf, despite being wounded rather badly the night before, turned back the clock in a major way and took the fight to Soldat. Many fans feared that Soldat's fellow Cabalites would hit the ringside area at any moment, but that was not to be the case. Sadly enough for Ulf, his inability to put Soldat away in the early stages of the match proved to be his undoing. DDS recovered and introduced Ulf's head to the steel ring-steps. Repeatedly. Try as he valiantly could, Ulf couldn't find a way to battle back against his former partner. It came to pass that Soldat was able to put Ulf away with a full-nelson slam on the ramp and gain the victory. After the match, Soldat's fellow Cabalites finally appeared; Bobby Minio and Lutawicka had with them chains and a body-bag. Ulf had his final shot at revenge and failed. Soldat watched on without emotion as Minio and Lutawicka dragged a groggy Ulf German out of sight, and probably out of everybody's mind for the very last time. SINGLES MATCH - STANDARD RULES: Alejandro Herrera vs. Malk Al-Haq Alejandro Herrera, the Nearly Man of Mexico's professional wrestling landscape, had done pretty well for himself the night before in his quest to gate-crash Andy Sharp's party. Alas, Mr. All-Star had with him the power of the Cabalite Rules handbook and handily defended his Spirit Of ACW Championship. Malk Al-Haq was one of the many Cabalites utilised by Sharp, in his sham of a title defense. And on this night, Herrera -- given a temporary contract that would last until the end of February -- chose Malk as his punching bag. Malk, always up for a fight, was game and spent the first couple of minutes of the match controlling proceedings. Alejandro's advanced years allowed Malk to set his own pace and dominate the elder statesmen. However, the Babylonian Overlord got cocky and incorrectly assumed that he had Alejandro on the run. As such, when Malk when for his Babylonian Drop finisher, Alejandro Herrera was able to squirm out of Malk's grasp and connecting with a spinning dropkick! Malk found himself tumbling out of the ring and his head made impact with the security barricade, to the delight of the fans. Thereafter, Malk was doomed. Already having to deal with a sickening headache, Malk had no answer for Alejandro's relentless assault, who fed on the roars of the fans packed inside the Arena VFG and the memory of last night's showdown with Andy Sharp. Even with Malk pulling out every little trick in his book, the Nearly Man of Mexico's professional wrestling landscape emerged triumphant after a kickstand double-arm DDT. You betcha sweet hiney that the fans went completely ballistic for that result. Alejandro's victory was an emotional one, and it showed in the way he celebrated with the fans after, leaving Malk to lick his wounds in the ring, with a hell of a lot of 'splaining to do. SINGLES MATCH - STANDARD RULES: Orlando Grant vs. Shawn Jessica Hart Exactly one year ago, on COURAGE 103, Shawn Jessica Hart and Orlando Grant had an epic match. Epic, because SJH absolutely crushed Orlando Grant. Blink, and the match was done. It was not one of Orlando's best moments, although he would bounce back from it. Shawn Jessica Hart would use that momentum to eventually challenge the ACW World Title at FOR QUEEN & COUNTRY 2010, but he came up short. With that little historical piece out of the way, the fans were rightly pumped for this match. This was Orlando's first match since last December, and after having heard from him earlier in the night, the fans were excited to see Orlando in the flesh. Despite not being a top-tier star, the OG of ACW received a tremendous pop upon his entrance, and Grant seemed truly overwhelmed by the response he'd received. Still, Orlando was able to re-align his focus and entered the ring with a determined look etched on his face. This match was more than an intent to get back into the swing of things; it would be his chance to right a one-year-old wrong. Now, Shawn Jessica Hart was known around the world for being a flamboyant son of a bitch. But surely nobody could have expected the Prime Minister Of Gettin' Sinister to have made his entrance to the tune of... David Bowie's Magic Dance. Further to that, Shawn strutted out dressed exactly like David Bowie's Goblin King character from the Labyrinth movie. The fans were stunned. Orlando Grant was repulsed. SJH, however, was having a ball of a time; so much so that his entrance lasted all of seven and a half minutes! When the Prime Minister Of Gettin' Sinister finally got to the ring, there was an evident shift in his attitude. Almost as if he'd slipped into match-mode, and discarded the flamboyance previously displayed. That only served to whet Orlando's appetite for this match. From there, the Hardest Working Man In Pro-Wrestling Today and the OG of ACW went on to tear the house down. Orlando looked just a tiny bit rusty, especially when matched up against Shawn Jessica Hart's unique offense, but he was at least hanging in there and doing a good job of frustating SJH. Orlando, at one point, even resorted to six different kind of flash pins; all in the space of one minute, making the fans giddy with excitement. Shawn survived all the near-falls, and after three previous attempts at his Hart On finisher failed, he managed to land it on the fourth time of asking. Instead of going for the pin, SJH decided to pick Orlando Grant -- who'd only just moments earlier very nearly put Shawn away with his Big Shit Poppin' finisher -- up and slapped him across the face twice. Orlando was livid at first. And then, he was unconscious... having fallen victim to SJH's rendition of Billows' Trend-Ender finisher! Yes, Shawn Jessica Hart had blatantly stolen his former LoC-er's finishing move, which was more than enough for the victory! The fans, thrilled by the engrossing encounter, gave both men a standing ovation at the end of it all, with the Hardest Working Man In Pro-Wrestling Today slipping back into Bowie-mode. However, with one subtle difference. No, not his dismissal of a beaten Orlando Grant, who rolled out of the ring enraged that he'd lost yet again. With the show coming to an end, SJH looked into the nearest camera and smiled widely. "I'm comin' for the Gateway, old friend! WHOOOOOOO~!" Shawn proclaimed, following up with the 'belt-around-my-waist' gesture. Declaration of intent? With the history Hart and Billows have, we definitely hope so.
