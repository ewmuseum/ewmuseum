Frank Pierce Biography Real Name Franklin Robert Pierce Nicknames The F-Bomb Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday 12/19/1985 aka The Greatest Day of Human Existence next to the day internet porn was invented Place of Birth Federal Way, Washington Height 6'3 Weight 268 Blood Type AB- Eye Color Blue Biography Frank Pierce received his training from wrestling legend and Hall of Famer Sonny Silver. As Sonny’s very own godson and the closest thing he’s ever had to a father, Prince believes the wrestling world is his for the taking and people should bow down to his very whims. Having such an incredibly cocky and self-aggrandizing demeanor, the only person who can stand to be around him is Ryan Gallway, who lives in his own world of delusions. Once upon a time, he was commonly seen backstage acting as royalty and anyone who doesn’t comply with his wishes are often shunned. To make himself sound either smarter or more regal, he uses a variety of badly-spoken foreign accents, which really make him sound ignorant and boorish. If aggravated, he reverts to his normal American speech pattern, using long strings of slurs (usually “whores” and “douchenozzles.”) UPDATE: During the month of November 2009, Frank Pierce came into a crazy-ass inheritance that has left him -- for lack of a better term -- rich, biatch! As such, he can now be seen flaunting his weatlh in people's faces and using his money for his own sick amusement. He's completely dropped the royal schtick, now walking to the ring in some of the gaudiest attire that money can buy. Frank believes with his newfound (unearned?) weatlh, that he isn't just some rich punk that's the future of the business... he IS the business. Everything he does practically screams "CHAMPION" and by not respecting him, you're against him. PERSONALITY: Dismissive, coldly arrogant, and seemingly has a short fuse. A completely demanding douchebag, Frank Pierce will almost never actually make eye contact with anybody he’s speaking with (outside of the Heirs), seemingly staring off into his own world.
