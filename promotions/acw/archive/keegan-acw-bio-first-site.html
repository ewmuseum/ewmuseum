Keegan Real Name: Keegan Carrahar Birthdate: 24th October 1976 Hometown: Newcastle, England. Height: 6'5 Weight: 282 lbs. Nicknames: The Yardstick, Special K, The Geordie Genius, The Prince of Palermo, The Height of Humanity, The Essence of Extreme. Status: Neutral Manager(s): N/A Current Teams: N/A Entrance Music: Prizefighter - Bush Wrestling Style: Basic Brawling and Technical Wrestling. Leg/back attacks: Chop-block, Ankle Lock, Texas Cloverleaf, Scissors Kick to the spine, Flying Elbow to the back, Half Boston Crab, Backbreaker + Sidewalk Slam (includes Twilt-a-Whirl variations,) Torture Rack, Spinebuster, Head/Neck: Russian Legsweep, Front-face Russian Legsweep, Death Valley Driver, Vertical Suplex, Dragon Sleeper, Running Reverse DDT (Scorpion Death Drop) Swinging Neckbreaker, Triple Powerbomb, Belly-to-Back Suplex, Side Headlock, Powerslam, Head scissors (on the ground,) Rabbit Punch, Bulldog, Front-Face Powerbomb, German Suplex, Pump Handle Slam, Arm/Hands: Figure Four Armlock, Wristlock, Hammerlock, Hammerlock Slam, Armbar, Armdrag. General submissions: Surfboard, STF and Cobra Clutch. ======================================================================================================================== Biography: For those of you that may care, my name is Keegan Carrahar. Named after a famous Footballer, Soccer to you Yanks, I was born in 1976 and raised in Newcastle, England. Living proof that there is life outside of London, Newcastle is a superb place that is blessed with a lively nightlife and gorgeous girls to go with it. Well I have to say that don’t I? It’s where I lived. I had a decent enough education. While I did do well enough to attain entry to get into College I know that if I’d not acted the bollocks I could have done better. Oh well. Not to worry. You can wipe your arse with O-Levels once you get you’re A-Levels and that’s what I did. After that, I thought I’d go to University but I soon realised that I really should travel for a bit first and decided to go the place that I’d always dreamed of – Italy. What was meant to be a few months away enjoying money that had been in the bank for years turned into a four-year stay that changed me as a person and my outlook on things forever. It is where I grew up, physically and mentally, and for that reason I will never ever forget it. Palermo is where I ended up. I knew it was notorious but so is every major city in the world in one way or another. It’s a case of steering clear of it. Typically, I didn’t and ended up smack-bang in the middle of it when I battered a bloke for giving me grief. Amazingly, the leader of a Mob was on hand with his mates and liked what he saw. The rest, as they say, is history. I started off acting as a bodyguard for his ‘missus.’ She was called Annalise and epitomized why I left England to go to Italy. Simply put, she made a believer out of me that love at first sight does exist. Like everything in life, I had to wait and pay my dues. Whether we’d admit it to anyone else or between ourselves, we were in love all along, even if it was wrong, and brought the best out in each other. We made each other happy and that’s what love is all about isn’t it? Dino couldn’t stand it. He never loved Annalise. He couldn’t have. He had a woman for every night. But that didn’t stop him from being gutted that he’d lost his ‘fallback’ and a lady who would do anything for him, including acting as a ‘trophy’ on his arm for the important occasions. Plus, it had been done all under his nose. I don’t like to go into what happened next but all will be revealed in the book. Let’s just leave it at Dino died as a result of the stress it caused and we lived happily ever after. Well, that’s not strictly true. Wishful thinking on my behalf. Michael D’Alessandro, who was also there when Dino(the leader) first spotted me, couldn’t stand it. He’d never trusted me. He saw me as an outcast and someone who would cause trouble, which means he was right, though that was never my intention. How can I be blamed for falling in love? You can’t choose to do that and I certainly wouldn’t have at the age of seventeen. Yet, I did and by breaking Dino and Annalise up, I destroyed the Boss and enraged Mickey. Big deal. I had what I wanted didn’t I? Yes. Until that bastard ruined it all… After she died, I couldn’t stay in Italy any longer. I regretted having to move. I had made so many friends there, people I considered as family, but it had to be done if I was to move on. I had left Newcastle as a boy and felt that it would always be my home. Four years on, I was thinking the same about Palermo but I also knew that if it happened once then it could happen again. Still, I’ve been back there a handful of occasions since and will continue to because of the people who made me what I am today and who I think about all of the time even though some of them are no longer with us. The only other place I’d ever really wanted to go, and by really I mean had to, was New York. Therefore, after heading back to England for over a year and getting my head sorted out, I left for the Big Apple unaware and indifferent to how long I would stay there. With the money I’d made in Italy, I wasn’t taking such a big risk anymore and I didn’t have any commitments. I could get up and leave after ten days or ten years. It didn’t really matter to me. I reunited with an old school friend of mine, Lharn Huscroft, in Beverly Hills. He had left England at approximately the same time I did and while we had remained in touch through a few letters here and there, it was the first time I’d seen him in five years and I received quite a surprise when I found out that he’d been employed to run a Wrestling organization, something he’d always dreamed of, and the fact that he was in his early twenties too just meant it was a phenomenal feat. As a favour, and for little too, I worked as an Interviewer for the company for six weeks but being on the road meant had unsettled me when it should have been the forum for fantastic times and I had to turn around and say ‘thanks, but no thanks.’ He seemed to be disappointed but he understood what I was going through. After all, he now had a Wife, Kelly, and said she was the only thing he missed about being a Wrestler. If it wasn’t for her, he would have also slept in the ring he put up and entered every evening. I’m not sure if that’s sad or sweet but I’ll get back to you on that one. Through Lharn, I met Adele, a friend of one of his friends, and we hit it off immediately. She was everything I wanted, sharing a lot in common with Annalise, but she wasn’t an exact replica and that’s a good thing as individuality is obviously essential. I loved her, I really did, but I cheated on her and blew it big style. It wasn’t her fault. It was mine and I should have known better but I was under a lot of strain at the time trying to juggle my person and professional workloads. The only consolation is that the ‘other’ woman meant the world to me, Joanna, and she became pregnant. I blew that too and now I don’t know whether I have a son or daughter at all. That just goes to show how silly I can be and it’s not that I didn’t want children because I do. I was ready for them. I just don’t think she thought I was ready for them – or to commit to her. It’s a shame and one of the few regrets that I have because I never did tell her otherwise. In amongst all of this, after spending time competing in a number of various Fight Clubs, word spread around the streets of New York that a rich man by the name of Salvatore Di Maggio was going to open one that would entertain hundreds, sometimes thousands, of people each week but still remain discreet somehow. I was intrigued and went along to see him in August two thousand and one. He gave me a tryout against a Wrestler called Steve Hayden, who I heard earned himself a cracking deal with a Wrestling organization some time after. Anyway, I won and not only earned myself a new job and a packet but a cult following in the process. It may not have been much to those who get their posteriors cleaned for them but it touched me. That a millionaire like myself could strike a chord with thousands of ordinary person. I discovered myself again. For all of the links to the Mafia and the millions sitting there in the bank, Keegan Carrahar is still an ordinary individual who enjoys ordinary things even though I’ve had extraordinary experiences. In my third month there, I won The Fighting Zone trophy. However, I had to defend the title against Pat Walsh after just winning it and that meant my first defeat on American soil in this brand of violence. It worked a treat though as it spurred me on to regain it, which I did in the match I still regard as my finest hour, literally, over here. In an Iron Man Match, and behind at two falls to one with twenty minutes left and losing brain cells by the second, I rallied back to take the tie three-two with a last-gasp victory and a special celebration thereafter. I’ll never forget that night even though some of it is a blur. The reception at the end was remarkable though and I’d like to think that those who were there that night know how much it meant to me, not only in terms of regaining the Championship but their support and applause at the end. It was an emotional evening to say the least. I held the trophy for five months and while that sounds superb and I’m pleased with how long I kept it for it was anything but brilliant if I’m brutally honest. I rode my luck at times as the personal and professional pressures started to combine and kick in. Out of those five months, I participated in a few fantastic fights but didn’t win any of them as I had at Angelic Death against Walsh. My victories in that time were either one-sided or lucky. I eventually lost to John C. Willis, a mountain of a man, who also turned out to be my half brother. Although portrayed as being a thick prick, Willis surrounded himself with a group of people, including D’Alessandro, as well as convincing Lharn to turn his back on me. It was also the same night Adele left me. So, just as the start of my second run as Champion had been the pinnacle of my career, the termination of it was by far and away the worst. Yet, while it hurt immensely and led to a two-month hiatus on my behalf to get my brain sorted, it only served to make me stronger. The winner of the King of The Underground Tournament would automatically earn a shot at the Championship. In a scheme devised by Salvatore, who hated Willis, he entered me in it under the moniker of Prince, a supposed newcomer. I won it and then helped the big bastard to retain the title against Uncensored, who had originally assisted Willis in winning the trophy from me, which set up a showdown in Italy. The stage was set for me to join the elite and become the first man to win the Championship on three separate occasions since the company reopened. Sadly, it never happened. Before we could get to Palermo, my adopted home, The Fighting Zone took a break for reasons unknown to me. I was disappointed to be deprived of the opportunity to take on my number one nemesis in front of people I loved and make history. I know for a fact that had I done it, and I was extremely confident I could, it would have topped what happened in California and probably ensured anything afterwards would be small in comparison. I suppose it just wasn’t to be. Joe Campbell, the owner of Asylum, called. Well it wasn’t him directly. But he needed someone. A group to oppose The Stranglehold, who had him by the bollocks at the time. Along with Willis, Tapestry, Splink and The Freak – The Fighting Zone’s finest – we set out to overcome this threat and in an Empty Arena match, where I nearly broke my neck, we managed to beat them while Joe got rid of their leader, Chris Universal, and everything was right again. I felt fabulous. It couldn’t or wouldn’t last. I can’t explain it. I wish I could. But, in Asylum, I wasn’t the Fighter I used to be. Many people aren’t! Seriously though, a series of injuries harmed my chance of making it there and although I shut Jeff Garvin up and took his Submission Title in a Ladder match, another magnificent moment, I couldn’t establish any consistency even though I was now working with Willis and not against him. Still, even after breaking my foot and keeping me in the company by sending Tapestry, a lovely lady and a dear friend of mine packing, the man known as Thanh was one bridge too far. And, the stipulation that one of us would have to leave if John lost meant it was extremely important and unfair in my opinion. We were the first ones to admit we hadn’t done as well as we would have liked. Anyway, to avoid moaning about the hand I was dealt, a coin toss ruled it was me who would go if John didn’t win and he didn’t. I wasn’t annoyed at him. I couldn’t be. I was angry at Campbell. After all, I didn’t even have the chance to defend my job. So here I am, courtesy of him, and back in the Wrestling world. I don’t know if I should be grateful or ungrateful that I’ve got a job and I’m privileged enough to still have my handsome bracket shown on television every week. There’s no point in looking back though I have a point to prove to everyone. It may be a daunting task but it’s one I’m ready for. I don’t care if you love or loathe me anymore. I can’t promise you will. But I’ll make one guarantee… You will take notice of me. Experience Archives A Reminder of Myself Gallery Small Face Photo Front Face Photo Body Photo Quotes
