ACW Chatter archive (2009 site)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed's own "ACW Chatter" archive from its 2009 site (acw.ewmania.com, the relaunch edition): dated one-line posts by its roster characters, each followed by the character's name and the date, 15 February to 8 April 2009, newest first, as captured in the Wayback on 14 April 2009. The site's own menus are not reproduced.
