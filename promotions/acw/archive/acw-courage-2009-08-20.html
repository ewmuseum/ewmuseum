ARC III - Requiem 20th August 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice INTRODUCTION With the companies position in Montreal now becoming weaker by the day, ACW is looking for a change. And as fueds and champions build up to the Wake there are many questions on the lips of the fans. But ACW always has something up it's sleeve... and it will soon be unveiled. The Last Stand - Part One "Get all of those people closer to ring-side!" Joe Bishop was not a happy man as he watched the small numbers coming through the entrance doors and into their seats. Over the past couple of weeks ACW had seen it's fair share of problems. From leaks of pipes to leaks of information, causing unrest and disharmony in the group. And this...this wasn't going to help. "Fuck..." Bishop turned around to see his long time friend Aaron Jones, known to many as SilverHAWK, scanning the arena. He rubbed his beard as he heard the whimper of a noise from the crowd... and the colour of the empty seats. "HAWK... I'm trying to get the people a little closer together, more compact. So that if we have any photos being taken it will at least look a little fuller. HAWK though wasn't listening. "What are we gonna do Joe... this place is half empty." "Don't worry about it, the Roy Boys have got a plan isn't that true?" In an instant HAWK flipped. "What the fuck do you mean they have a plan? They don't run this place... I do Joe! I do! It's me who has to sort this out and I need to sort it out now." "What the..." And as the former ACW star walked away from his long time friend, tail between his legs, Bishop knew that HAWK was wrong about one this. He wasn't the one to sort this out. He didn't know how. Nothing to See Here FrostbytE1! Jeremy Stevenson tossed a seemingly empty black duffel bag about the air. While the bag seemed quite roomy, it also appeared to be empty with the ease at which Jeremy flung the bag a foot or so ahead of him as he walked. Kati didn't seem to mind that her partner was enjoying himself. She was busy bobbing her head to the left and right without any real sense of purpose. They were aimless. They always were. Unless they were given an order by their leader. Ahead of them Jack Johnson, a brand new hire seemed to be enjoying himself as well but for completely different reasons. He stood decked out in his ring gear and an ACW shirt since it was a pipe dream to believe he'd have merchandise in his own name. The thought of climbing his way through most of eastern Canada to get some small ounce of fame paid off when he finally got the call by the Roy Boys. Now here he was. Lower on the totem pole than a midget eating fat ass. Signing to a place with the likes of Max Danger, Kelly Flawless, and Alias would make you realize that you'd need to enjoy the taste of shit for a while until you finally pulled out that big win you needed. The Misfit Toys would not be that win, but they sure didn't mind meeting a new person. "Hey, guy. Are you part of the Kingdom?" Johnson didn't seem to notice the duo as they were walking, but he heard the sound of the duffel bag bouncing and was met by Kati's words. She also stood about an inch away staring into his eyes. "Me? Not a chance. I think you need to be British and a great athlete for that. I'm kind of working my way up there if you know what I mean." "No, I don't know what you mean." Jeremy thought to toss the bag again, but crumpled it in his hands instead. "Just consider yourself lucky that you don't fly a Union Jack." "Yeah because I'd have to tie you to a car hood and burn you alive if you were." Kati's words came out as she slowly grew into a smile. We're practically best friends with Alias and he'll kill you if we ask." She gestured in a wink that was very drawn out so that he'd notice. There wasn't much of a reaction from Johnson other than a smile, but he figured she was just being nice. "I'm not just being nice to you James." "Jack." "Keep pushing it cherry lips. I'd stay and chit chat but we have business in the ring soon." Kati smiled again, but then flipped Johnson off and walked away. Jeremy just kind of shrugged and walked away. Johnson just kind of stood not knowing if he should write off the situation or put some stock into it. He wrote it off. "Hey, what's in the bag?" Stevenson continued to walk but spoke up so that Johnson would hear him. "It's a secret, so don't let anyone know I told you it's athletic tape. For the boss." Johnson thought he understood, but in a way hoped that he was wrong about what was going on. He just didn't want to bump into Flawless next. As the noise continued to grow exponentially, Mach 2 gritted his teeth and tried to weather the technical storm he’d been put through in the last several minutes. It was with nothing less than sheer desperation that Mach 2 resorted to throwing a sharp and flexible kick upward, nailing Pierce right between the eyes. Another blow, and soon another landed him in the eye. Pierce, stunned for the moment, let go of the hold as he shielded his eye while Mach 2 rolled to his feet. Attempting to chop the much bigger man down, a plethora of Shoot Kicks landed directly in the leg. A few more stiff Forearms from his good arm allowed Mach 2 to try and negotiate a Springboard maneuver off the second rope, but all he did was fly into the arms of Pierce… PAUPER POPPER! The Sit-out Spinebuster rattled the ring from such a vicious impact as Mach 2 quickly twitched about, looking like he had little to nothing left in the tank. Pierce followed through and put both hands on his chest looking for the win. ONE! TWO! THRE… NO! It was closer to two and eleven-twelfths, but according to the two fingers held up by Irwonsen, this match would continue. Pierce, who was just about tired of this nonsense, hooked the Supersonic Kid by the hair and pummeled him in the face with right hand after right hand. Heeding the referee’s second or third warning in this match about a DQ, Pierce let up and dragged Mach 2’s prone form to the center of the ring. Sensing defeat was at hand, Pierce tagged in Ryan Gallway and positioned himself on the second rope. Gallway used his partner as a launching pad to fly from even greater heights with a spectacular Frog Splash. Everything was perfect. The precision. The leap. The flight… … The landing, not so good. RRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH! Montreal and its fans were back in this match as Mach 2 just narrowly avoided the impact, sending Gallway plummeting to the canvas from a great elevation. Rolling around on the mat and kicking his legs, Alexandria stared both the Heirs dead on and beat the ring apron, trying to rally The World Warrior back to his feet. MACH 2! MACH 2! MACH 2! MACH 2! Roaring and cheering from the All-Star Arena didn’t fall upon deaf ears. Mach 2 heard each and every cry as he used the aid of the ropes to make it back to his feet. Gallway made his way up and ran at his nemesis with a Sunset Flip. Mach 2 rolled through and upon making it back to his feet, fired of a STIFF Roundhouse Kick to the head of Gallway, rattling his brains. Clutching his arm in pain and leaving it slightly to the side, he egged on the space-themed athlete to get back to his feet. Ryan obliged and tried for a clothesline, but a hard forearm caught him in the temple. As he came back to his feet, it was a Spinning Wheel Kick that knocked him back on his ass! Whether he was just a glutton for punishment or Gallway was just brave, he somehow got back to his feet in a daze, but he’d regret that decision when a hard “CRACK!” echoed throughout the All-Star Arena, courtesy of a Pele kick from Mach 2! Grabbing his arm, he took a couple moments to compose himself before hooking the leg. ONE! TWO! ELBOW FROM PIERCE… MISSED! Mach 2 saw the big man charge into the ring and moved out of harm’s way long enough for him to elbow his own partner. Making it back to his feet, Mach 2 disposed of him with a 540 Kick! After Pierce had his head nearly kicked off into the next zip code, Mach 2 pointed a finger at the sky as he slapped his chest with his good arm. He knew his arm wasn’t going to have the strength necessary to pull off the Speed of Sound, but he could still do it from the much more stable ground of the top rope, so that’s where he went. Now firmly perched on the ropes while Gallway was still down and out for the moment, he flew… SPEED. OF. SOUND…. MISSED. The fans who were left in the All-Star Arena groaned with a mix of disappointment and agony. The distraction from Pierce and his interference gave Gallway enough time to roll out of the way, letting Mach 2 take the full brunt of the mat’s impact. Seeing this as a golden opportunity, Gallway fell backwards to the ropes and made the tag to Frank Pierce. The Baron of Ballistics – nursing a throbbing jaw from where Mach 2’s boot connected – was incensed to get this match done and over so he lifted The Supersonic Kid up into the Electric Chair position, precisely where he didn’t want to be. Gallway was on the top rope when he flew off… DOOMSDAY CUTTER! Once Mach 2 hit the ground off a sickening angle from the Doomsday Cutter that the Heirs of Wrestling had dubbed “The Ascension” Pierce had easy pickings. As confident as can be, he hooked both legs emphatically and cradled up the body of the 1st Airbourne member. The fans prayed for that one last kickout. ONE. TWO. THREE. But it never came. Just as soon as the victory music of "Champion" played, all three members of the Heirs celebrated in the ring. Holding each other’s hands, the sweet sound of Tommy Vale announcing the Heirs of Wrestling as the winners to this contest made their night. Max Danger may not have been here, but tonight, the Heirs of Wrestling showed that their focus was solely on the Wake. After delivering a devastating blow to their rivals, the First Airbourne, it was clear that this war would be far from over. As Pierce delivered a sick boot to the arm of Mach 2 for good measure, Gallway and Alexandria Malone smiled to each other and hugged in celebration. Ryan tried to get a hand to that ass, but that failed miserably as Malone pulled away, sneering at him. The festivities aside, this was a gruesome sight to end the night on. Andy Sharp was gone and would be some time before he figured out this was just a ruse from the Heirs of Wrestling to divide and conquer. Brian Spaes was nowhere to be found and it would also be some time before he realized what was done. Mach 2 was an island alone, trying to fight off the world. But tonight, the weight of the world had come crashing down on him. WINNER: The Heirs of Wrestling via pinfall (Pierce over Mach 2)
