Honor, Glory and a Random Opponent. Honor & Glory was ready to kick off. Usually on PPVs, the World Champion doesn’t come out until the main event. Tonight, though, was different. With Survival of the Fittest, six qualifying matches were needed for the main event. And even the World Champion needed to qualify. So, sound monkeys in the truck, cue “Terra in Black”, please? Thanks. With his entrance music playing, all that was left was for the AYE SEE DUBYA WORLD CHAMPION to come out. And, seconds later, he did. CHEEEER~! Seymour raised the championship high over head, walking to the end of the stage area, just before the ramp, where he dropped to his knees, belt still raised. That, of course, is the cue for pyro. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! With said pyro exploded, Seymour rested the richest prize in ACW over his shoulder, continuing his trip to the ring by slapping hands with fans the whole way. Stepping between the ropes, Seymour looked out at the psyched crowd, asking for a microphone, which he soon received. “Hey everyone! Welcome to Honor & Glory!” CHEER~! Hey, he’s the World Champion. And he’s popular, so the people like him. Hence the cheering. “As you all know, tonight we’re having Survival of the Fittest. Six matches, with the winners moving on to the main event of the evening, for this.” Here, he hoisted the ACW World Championship in the air, showing it off for all to see. “And we’ve got a lot of great matches,” he continued. “Unfortunately, no one’s told me yet who I’m facing, so, I’m assuming I don’t have an opponent. Therefore…” Slowly, Seymour laid the ACW World Championship down on the mat, shiny side up. “If you don’t have a match tonight, and want a shot at this World Title…come on down, and we’re going to kick off Honor & Glory with an ACW WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH!” As it turned out, of course, someone did come down. “Sovereign” by Peace Warriors began to play, as a newcomer made his way down to the ring. The fans didn’t quite know how to react, as the masked man stepped in the ropes, looking at the ACW World Champion, nodding his head. “And you are,” the Final Fantasy asked, holding the microphone out for the leopard-masked man to respond. “Machaveli St. Romani,” came the reply from the man, who began limbering up for the contest at hand. “Good enough for me.” SotF Qualifier / ACW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP Seymour Almasy© versus Machaveli St. Romani Seymour Almasy stood in one corner, the ACW World Heavyweight Champion. The man, as it were, in ACW. In the other corner stood a man that the commentators were scrambling to find out information on. Thus far, all we knew was his name, given by his own word, Machiaveli St. Romani. Wearing the mask of a leopard. Making his ACW debut. Yet with a chance to take hold of the richest prize in the wrestling world. Monet Samuel stood in the center of the ring, raising the ACW World Championship high overhead, refereeing her first World Title match of the Almasy era. She went to show the belt to Seymour, but he waved her off. He’d been there before. Let the newcomer have a look at the prize, see what he was fighting for. Conviction burned in the masked man’s eyes as he looked down at the championship, well aware of what it meant. Finally, he nodded, and Monet handed the belt to the time keeper. The ACW World Title was on the line. And here. We. Go. First, though, the challenger for the World Championship extended his hand. Seymour had seen this ploy before, used by some of ACW’s more nefarious characters. But he was a good guy, and as such, he shook hands with his opponent. No attack came. St. Romani was ALSO a good guy. And with pleasantries exchanged, the match. Romani and Almasy tied up, jostling for position, until St. Romani grabbed Seymour’s right arm, executing a full arm drag and twist. A simplistic move, really, but something the technically deficient Seymour was ill-equipped to deal with. Machiaveli drove an elbow into the locked arm, prompting a groan of pain from his adversary. Finally, Seymour hit upon the counter, rolling to the mat, then kipping back up, using the momentum to apply the arm drag and twist himself. Being bigger than Seymour, St. Romani simply pulled himself away, sizing up the situation. The World Champion found himself rapidly growing impatient. He wasn’t a big fan of this technical, slow beginning to matches. He liked to throw himself at opponents, and overwhelm them with quick, confusing attacks. So that was what he tried to do, rolling towards Apostle Romani, and coming up for a clothesline. That was about when everyone stopped looking at the man in the leopard mask as a rookie, because he caught Seymour by the arm, flowing into a single arm DDT, before applying a hammerlock. No rookie, this one. Obviously, Machiaveli St. Romani had been trained, and had practical in-ring experience. It showed in the little things he did, the way he held the hammerlock tight for maximum effect, the ring positioning designed to prevent Seymour from reaching the ropes. It spoke of classical, practical training. And it spoke of a man with the technical tools to rein in the spotty, aerial attacking World Champion. Still, Seymour managed to make it to the ropes. Romani relinquished the hold immediately, a refreshing change from a lot of the chicanery that seemed to define ACW nowadays. Almasy was definitely glad for an opponent who wasn’t trying to bash his brains in with a steel chair. Back to a vertical base they went, Seymour once again taking the initiative. This time, though, his attack worked, catching Romani in the face with a well-thrown dropkick. The leopard-masked competitor rose to his feet, only to eat a second. Almasy pounced, charging again, baseball sliding into Romani, a move that sent him to the floor. Saint Romani used the guardrail to pull himself back up, slightly disoriented. He saw the crowd gesturing behind him, and turned, wondering what they were looking at. About then, he saw the form of the Final Fantasy sailing at him. TEKMISSILE! Ever play Final Fantasy VI? If so, you’ll know that’s the name of a stupidly powerful Magitek Armor ability you get to use in the beginning of the game. It owns. So of course, Seymour named his suicide dive after it. Both men lay on the mats following the Tekmissile, Romani obviously in a bit worse shape than the Final Fantasy. As soon as he was able, Seymour rolled Romani back into the ring, and climbed back up to the apron. SLINGSHOT ELBOW DROP! Using the ropes, Seymour threw himself back inside, and drove an elbow into the chest cavity of the challenger. Wasting no time, and knowing that the more damage he took here, the tougher Survival of the Fittest could be, he made the cover. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! Romani, it seemed, also had heart. Seymour picked Romani up, scoring with a knifeedge chop across the chest, followed by another. He attempted to whip Machiaveli into the ropes, but the Saint countered, shooting Seymour into the strands instead. Almasy came back with a high cross body, but Romani deftly caught Seymour. For a few minutes, it seemed that he was going to do a rib breaker, or a fall away slam, but the Saint thought outside the box, tossing the Final Fantasy up to his shoulders before executing a Samoan Drop. Almost as if saying he wasn’t a simply pure submissionist, Romani hit the far ropes hard, rolling forward on the ground, before leaping into a twisting, flipping senton splash. Landing hard across the smaller man, he grabbed a leg, holding it for a cover of his own. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! On the kickout, though, as Seymour threw his shoulder up, the resourceful Machiaveli grabbed hold of that arm, cinching in a top wristlock that held the shoulders back to the mat. ONE! TWO! LEFT SHOULDER UP! Cranking down on the hold more, though, forced Seymour back to the canvas. ONE! TWO! BRIDGE! Showing off his neck strength, Seymour bridged up, fighting off the top wristlock, fighting his way back up to a vertical base. He kicked Romani in the gut, and stepped back, looking to throw a shot to keep the Apostle off-balance. DEATHTOUCH! CAUGHT BY ROMANI! Machiaveli then ran for the nearby ropes, leaping over the top rope, and driving Seymour’s arm down across the top rope! Seymour fell to the canvas, clutching at the limb, while Romani ascended to the top turnbuckle, taking careful, precision aim, before jumping. DIVING KNEEDROP! TO THE SHOULDER! Fans gasped, as they saw the World Champion rolling on the canvas in pain. ACW PPVs were known for surprises, sure. But could a veritable unknown really be taking the fight to the company’s World Champion? Better believe it. Romani picked Seymour up, still clinging to the right arm, and SNAPPING it over with a dragon screw to the arm. Almasy ducked this time, stepping behind the Saint to finally get off a real offensive blow. LEFT-HANDED DEATHTOUCH! Still favoring the worked on right arm, Seymour opted instead to nail Romani in the back of the skull with a shotei. Machiaveli pitched forward, into the ropes, and Seymour took advantage, wrapping both arms around St. Romani’s waist, and taking him over. GERMAN SUPLEX! WITH THE BRIDGE! ONE! THINGS FALL APART! Booting the rising Romani in the midsection, Seymour hooked a front facelock, and lifted St. Romani up, getting ready to spike him on his skull. LEVEL FIVE BRAIN-- NO! APOSTLE ROMANI SLIPS DOWN… INTO THE FUJIWARA ARMBAR! Almasy was STUNNED. The fans were STUNNED. But not the Saint, who had so expertly maneuvered himself into this position, cranking back on the armbar, prompting screams of agony. The Final Fantasy was in trouble. Big trouble. With the desire to win fully fledged in his mind, Seymour began his crawl for the ropes. St. Romani, meanwhile, positioned his body to prevent Seymour from getting there, intent on capturing the World Championship. It was now down to WILL, pure and simple. The smaller Seymour crawled, the newcomer Romani tried to block. Tried, tried, tried… Yet ultimately failed. Seymour managed to drape a foot over the bottom rope. If Romani was disappointed, the mask prevented everyone from seeing it. Again, he immediately relinquished his submission, showing the values of sportsmanship that he seemed to covet. St. Romani shot the World Champion into the ropes again, attempting to catch Almasy in the face with a round kick. Seymour, though, managed to duck underneath, coming back with the JECHT SHOT! Shaking it gratefully, Seymour raised his opponent’s hand high for all to see, in a show of mutual respect. This PPV was called Honor & Glory.
