LIVE! on Sunday 1st December From Turner Field, Atlanta, Georgia PREVIOUSLY... After the ACW Majority Owner announced mid-week House Shows that were to start this week, the wrestlers have become even more unsettled, as they are warned to work the shows or face the consequences...what will they be? Can Dunn and Boyd convince the roster to work the shows? and how did the actual house show go? ACW is turning into a roller coaster ride...and we haven't even reached the summit yet. To The Point.
