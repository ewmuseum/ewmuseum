What this is The fed's own biography card for the character Alias, from the fed's 2009 site (acw.ewmania.com, the relaunch edition), as captured in the Wayback on 15 April 2009. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Alias Real Name: Christopher Declan Sheffield Nickname: "The Original Pulp Hero, Pulp, The Hero, Bigby, Sheff" Also known as: "The Tin Angel" Chris Phoenix (early/mid-90's) Born: April 2nd, 1975 Place of Birth: Bartlett, Tennessee Hometown: Information Not Available Height: 6'4" Weight: 242 lbs Blood type: O Eye color: Blue on the left, White on the right Trainer: Kyle Morris Theme song: "Sympathy for the Devil" by The Rolling Stones Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Muay Thai Striker, High Impact Grappler Signature moves: + Welcome to ACW, Motherf***er (A hammerlock recoiled out, into a hard clotheline) + Click Boom Tiger Crush (A headbutt to the nose, a knee (elbow) to the mid-section, and then an elbow to the top of the head (Jumping Knee Strike to the face); moves in parenthesis for when the move is performed later in the match) + Bullets (Fast and hard right elbows to the back on the neck, while at the same time shooting his right knee up into the opponents face... stopping at around the ninth or tenth simultaneous shot... on a good day) + Pulped II (The Codebreaker) + Glasgow Kiss (Swandive Headbutt) + The Big Fat Kill (Belly-to-Belly Suplex off the top rope; though thanks to what ACW representatives note as a "reoccurring injury to his core", it's only pulled out for bigger moments) DDT, Hart-style Missile Dropkick, High-angle Powerbomb (with pin, if it's hit near the end of the match), Diamond Dust (if the situation leads to the correct positioning) Finisher: 1) A-Bomb, 2) Anarchy's Lullaby Finisher name: 1) Tilt-a-Whirl, 2)Modified Buffalo Sleeper Hold Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: ACW, tSC, fWo, tA, A!, 21w, WWRPG, IWO Other/Previous teams: Invictus (w/ 'The Wolf' Mike Randalls and Rook Black; tSC), Team Resistance (w/ SilverHAWK, Jason Kain and Geo Vacton; ACW) Other/Previous managers: Monet Samuel (as Freefall; ACW '92-'94) Other Wrestling titles won: tSC Champion, ACW World Heavyweight Champion (x2), ACW Television Champion, ACW United States Champion (x2), WWRPG 'I Quit' Champion, PTW World Champion, The Real Carnage World Champion, fEar Fury Title Other Wrestling awards won: ACW Immortal (Hall of Fame Inductee), ACW co-Superstar of the Year, ACW Face of the Year, ACW Match and Moment of the Year (2003), Russel David's The Mark Awards - Wrestler of the Year, Best Feud - w/Sonny Silver; tSC (2004) Biography The son of a pseudo-legend. The would-be golden boy of All-Star Championship Wrestling. The lost potential. The unrecognizable pulp hero that returned to the world, after eight years underground. The savior of some federations, and the final nail in the coffin of others. The World Champion of ACW. The Champion of tSC. The legend swept away in bad contract signings, controversy and nearly forgotten. The inevitable. The man who would bring the flood to the asylum. The quest for redemption, for respect. The scorpion. The Spirit. The heart. The soul. The will. The man in black. The big bad wolf. The question is... what's your Alias?
