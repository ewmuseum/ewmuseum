ARC II - The Road to Legends III 16th April 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice introduction As the curtain closes on ARC I, ACW leaps forward with a new goal circulating it's waters; keep it coming. The first month of activity had been a successful one, but the novelty of return will not last much longer as competing beasts of the wrestling world now hover over ACW in it's infant stage. The eyes of the world await the first mistake, the first poor show. However the drive and passion of the engine room makes sure that the federation will never bare off it's tracks. For ACW is back. Fully charged and ready to knock anyone out it's path. Welcome to All-Star Championship Wrestling ARC II...and this time. We mean fucking business. Uh, Perhaps Later Would Be Better? No? Well, Okay. Unlike previous installments, SilverHAWK wasn't going to be gracing the ACW faithful with his presence at the top of the ramp. Instead, music came blarring out of the speakers. "Amazing" by Kanye West. The boos were immediate, save for a small pocket up in the balcony. Through the curtain walked the King of Submission himself, Max Danger, dressed to kill in a black suit with royal blue stripes (with matching vest and pants) and a royal blue dress shirt underneath (the right arm seemed a little thicker, maybe it was swollen?). The only thing strange about his appearance inside the All-Star Arena was the fact that he was here so early. He was notorious for arriving well after things had gotten started. But no matter, the Danger Man, took... his... sweet... time getting to the ring. With him, as always, was what he dubbed "Armsy McDanger" the prosthetic arm he had stolen from Brock Shepherd five months prior. Max, with that smirk of his plastered across his face, made a lap around the ring, soaking in the jeers. "You suck!" screamed a fan several rows back. "Who said that?" Danger demanded immediately. He scanned the general area looking for the culprit, even pointing at several people with the fake limb and asking "was it you?" but no one admitted to it. With that, Max procurred himself a microphone. He wasn't going to stand for such malicious words. Climbing up onto the apron, Danger continued to look out towards the section where he thought the words could have come from. "So, that's how it's going to be? You're going to hide out amongst the masses and shout mean and hurtful things at me? Maybe you think you're being cool, I don't know, but what I do know is that I'm hurt. Right here," Danger pointed, with the prosthetic, at where his heart should be (it was still up for debate whether he had one or not). "Physically, I can't actually cry, but on the inside... on the inside..." He sniffed. The fans began to ride him then, "CRY BABY! CRY BABY! CRY BABY!" Danger shook the fake arm defiantely at the people and stepped inside the ropes. He twirled around, pointing the hand at everyone inside the building. "You think it's funny, don't you? Making fun of someone, telling them they suck, well... you suck! How does that make you feel? Huh? Yeah, that's what I thought." In Max's mind, everyone was just as hurt as he was. Sadly, they weren't. They continued to spew their hatred at the King of Submission. Danger took a deep breath while closing his eyes. He tried blocking out the people and their awful words. That's when his iPhone started to ring. Opening his eyes, he reached into his suit and pulled the DangerPhone from it's inside pocket. "If you'll excuse me," he said to everyone inside All-Star Arena. They immediately began to shout and boo and make all sorts of racket. Max walked over to a corner and bent over slightly, putting one hand into his right ear while placing the iPhone to his left. "...last I saw it was in the back of the car." Danger listened for a moment. "Honey, I can barely hear... hang on." Max put the iPhone to his chest to cover the mouth piece and spoke into the microphone again. "Seriously, guys, not cool. I'm on the phone here, care to keep it down please?" "NO!" shouted the fans in unison with a few "fuck you" and "you suck" and "I love you" mix in. Yeah, he had a few people who loved him, what of it? Danger sneered as the people clearly couldn't be reasoned with. He brought the iPhone back to his face, "I'm going to have to call you back. Love you, too." With his head lowered, and a look of disgust on his face, Max placed the DangerPhone back into his suit pocket. Slowly, he raised his head and shaking it, he said, "You all should be ashamed of yourselves. You really should." The audience continued to give him shit as he paced around the ring. After several seconds of silence on Max's part, he lifted the mic back to his lips and began to talk, "The reason I'm out here so early tonight is because I have a bone to pick. Several actually." The Danger Man took a few more moments for himself before continuing. "Firstly, this new incarnation of ACW has crowned all three of its Champions but with one HUGE mistake -- I'm not one of them!" Max threw the prosthetic arm outward in a "what the frick?" manner. "What's up with that? I mean, c'mon, the King of Submission, the Danger Man, Max Danger, former Evolutionary Champion in the Squared Circle, former Action! Wrestling Dyad, Carnage, and Bantam Champion. I was ACW Champion, for crying out loud!" For 72 hours, but still, he was champion. Danger shook his head. "Seymour Almasy. Steve Knox. Alias. Those are your champions." The fans cheered for Seymour and Alias, but booed Knox. "Alias'll probably bleed to death before he even makes it to his next match and Seymour has, I'm pretty sure, a very large collection of anime and manga porn, right ladies?" The Party in the front row shouted a few unkind words towards the King of Submission, but he only give them a wink. "And don't even get me started on Steve Knox. You'd think he was allergic to his belt the way he avoids it so much, and it's only been a week! Seriously, if you don't want it, I'll take it. I'll even give you money for it. How's that sound?" And he was serious. He wasn't above paying for a championship. He'd thought on several of occasions of just creating his own, but feared it wouldn't be recognized as a real title. "But beyond that whole situation, I have a beef with one Kelly Masters." The fans cheered. Kelly Masters was a no-nonsense kind of guy who just got in there and whipped your ass. They liked that. He wasn't afraid to mix it up with anyone. "On two separate occasions, this guy who once claimed to be a better submissionist that me, and I'll give you all a couple of minutes to let that soak in and laugh like I have." Danger paused for a few moments, but instead of laughing, the fans just started shouting "NEW KING! NEW KING!" Max went "huh" before continuing, "Back on point, twice now he has snuck up on me and trapped me in that submission hold of his." "YOU TAPPED OUT! YOU TAPPED OUT!" "If it were possible, I'd give every single one of you the middle finger," Danger said, indicating the prosthetic. "But alas, yes, I did submit to his little submission, but it wasn't because it hurt. Nope. Not at all. Look." Max began jumping up and down and only winced slightly. "Doesn't hurt a lick. Sometimes in life it's good to give. I'm a giver and I wanted to make the kid feel good. He hasn't been doing this very long, he made a bold claim back in Just Wrestling, and I didn't have the heart to tell him that he just simply wasn't good enough to hang with me." The fans were onto Danger's bullshit, of course. I mean, Kelly did submit him in their first encounter effectively eliminating him a chance at either the Spirit of ACW or World Championships, and then during Fight Night, he made him tap out a second time preventing him from winning the Scorpion Fighting Championship. "Unfortunately, I believe my charity has given young Kelly Masters the wrong impression, that's why I'm here tonight -- to issue a challenge. I'm sorry, kiddo, but it's time to quash your heightened sense of abilities. I am the King of Submission, and it's about time you learned that." Just then, Nonpoint's "There's Gonna Be a War" fired up. Everyone inside All-Star Arena was on their feet (okay, they already kind of were, but now they were really up, ya know?). Out from the back walked Kelly Masters, with a very focused look on his face. That's how he always looked, sure, but this was more focused, if that was possible. He knew making Max tap wasn't a fluke and he was more than willing to prove it again. Of course, he was still banged up from two straight weeks of hellacious battles. During Fight Night, he did all sorts of damage, both to his opponents and himself, then just last week, he fought in two matches, and although he came away empty handed, he sure did impress. Unfortunately, he had the bruises to show for his troubles. "Um, I didn't mean so much now," said the Danger Man to deaf ears. "Perhaps... I mean, I'm still in my suit, and... you're seriously not going to stop that robotic march down the aisle, are you?" Kelly Masters continued the trek down the aisle as the King of Submission was getting a little jittery inside the ring. "The Machine" hopped up onto the ring apron when suddenly a very loud air horn went off from the balcony. WHAM! The Dangerous III roaring elbow slammed into Kelly's nose, sending a shower of blood everywhere, and Masters to the floor, flat on his back. The split second Kelly shifted his eyes was just the opening Max needed. And while he had connected with the blow, Danger wasn't too pleased. "Dude, you got blood all over my suit!" Max Danger vs. Kelly Masters Boos rained down on the Danger Man, as he continued to look down at his suit, disgusted by all the blood splattered on it. It was a new suit, too. This enraged Max more than you could ever know. Maybe moreso than making him tap out two weeks in a row. ...NAH! But he didn't pounce on Kelly Masters right away. No, he began to slowly remove his suit (he took out his iPhone and placed it in the corner with his prosthetic). First came the jacket, which he threw into the front row, because what was he going to do with a blood-stained jacket? Let someone sell it on eBay. Danger took off his tie and dress shirt next, and underneath, on his right arm, was he extra-large elbow pad. Yes, Max had this particular shirt and suit sized to fit around the elbow pad. That's just the kind of kind he is. Danger then began taking off his pants, getting a few hoots and hollers from the ladies (and a couple of guys). But underneath wasn't his boxers or his penis, but rather his wrestling trucks. He also had his boots and shinguards and all that goodness on underneath. Finally, once he'd removed everything he needed to, the Danger Man climbed out of the ring and grabbed Kelly Masters by the hair. Meanwhile, Mark Shields was casually making his way to the ring, smoking a cigarette along the way. "Damn it, Danger! I was enjoying a smoke and you have to start this bullshit and I have to come down and officiate it!" shouted an annoyed Mark Shields. Max slammed the back of Kelly's head against the floor before walking over to Mark. He grabbed the cigarette from his lips and stomped it out on the ground. "You'll thank me when you don't have cancer." "It's not likely," grumbled Shields as he pulled out a pack of smokes from his pocket. "You're just lucky I have more." Danger went back to Kelly, he was still on dream street thanks to the devastating roaring elbow, and pulled him to his feet. Mark climbed back into the ring and called for the bell to officially start the match (ding ding ding). Meanwhile, on the outside, the King of Submission rammed Kelly head first into the ring post. He slammed Masters' face against the ring apron then took his head and dragged it along the canvas, leaving a smear of blood all along the side. You think he was still upset about getting blood on his suit? Taking his head in his hands, the Danger Man pummeled Kelly's face with alternating knee strikes. A final one, knocked him back against the apron. With the ring apron the only thing keeping Masters upright, Max smirked at his handiwork. A fan behind him shouted, "FUCK YOU, QUEER!" Danger spun around in the blink of an eye and looked around for the speaker. He was shocked to see it was a ten-year-old little bastard. Max glowered at the punk and was about to say "I oughta give you a spanking like your parents should have the moment you were born" but that mouthful of a comeback was halted thanks to a lunging punch to his upper back. Danger staggered forward to where he was inches from that foul-mouthed kid. Spit came flying from the kid's mouth like a shotgun, but Max was able to dodge the goober, and it popped Kelly right in the center of his chest. He would hit men and women alike, but a ten-year-old? Maybe the thought crossed his mind, maybe it didn't. Max didn't really give him a lot of time to consider it, drilling him in the side of the head with a Yakuza Kick! Kelly fell to the floor as Max turned to the kid, "Thanks, man!" He gave him the thumbs up before pullling "The Machine" back to his feet. Mark Shields was finally going to show some authority and said, "Get his ass in the ring, Danger!" "I'm happy to oblige," remarked the King of Submission as he rolled Kelly into the ring for the first time this match. Masters was crawling on all fours when Max rolled into the ring after him. He got a running start and punted Kelly square in the ribs. Masters rolled over onto his back, clutching his side, leaving him open for a vicious knee drop right onto his bloodied nose! ONE. TWO. KICKOUT. That definitely wasn't going to be enough to keep the Virginia Beach native down for a three count. Max wasn't deterred however, grabbing the back of Kelly's head and slamming his massive elbow pad into his face! WHAM! WHAM! WHAM! The three shots kept Masters on the ground long enough to Max to come off the ropes with another knee drop -- NOPE. Kelly dodged out of the way at the last second, and quickly pivoted on the ground and grabbed ahold of Danger's foot. Everyone rose to their feets, exstatic. Max, however, yanked his leg free of Kelly's grasp before he could do anything with it. Danger pulled himself under the bottom rope and to the floor, a look of absolutely terror on his face. As he kneeled on the outside the fans ripped into him again. "YOU'RE A PUSSY! YOU'RE A PUSSY!" Weren't Canadians supposed to be friendlier? Apparently not. Max stood up on the outside, looking out at the crowd appalled. He should have been paying attention to inside the ring, as Kelly reached through the ropes and grabbed him by his shaggy hair. Max turned around but instead of being pulled back into the ring, he poked Kelly in the eye and pulled his throat down across the middle rope. Masters flopped back onto the mat and Danger immediately slid into the ring. He slammed an elbow down onto Kelly's chest. Max stood and delivered a second elbow, then a third. A fourth would have followed but Danger waved that idea off and just stomped on Kelly's face. STOMP. STOMP. STOMP. STOMP. A fifth one was on it's way, but Max wiselyl thought better of it. Despite the pain he was in, Kelly snapped as Danger's leg, only he had pulled it away just in the nick of time, and dropped an elbow onto the side of Kelly's neck. ONE. TWO. Another kickout from "The Machine". Danger peeled Kelly up off the mat and sent him hard into the turnbuckles. Max didn't charge in, he simply walked in and kicked Masters right in the stomach. Turning him around, the Danger Man took Kelly's by the hair and slammed his face into the top turnbuckle! Despite their hatred of Max Danger, the fans couldn't refuse to count along. "ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! T---" Just because he could, Max stopped at nine and with a smirk turned Kelly around in the corner and chopped him hard across the chest ("WOO!" -- they'd gotten over him stopping before ten). He did it a second time ("WOO!") before taking him by the arm, and after an arm wringer, whipped Masters across the ring. He slammed into the opposite turnbuckles hard and Max came flying in after him with a Corner Shining Wizard! The momentum sent Danger over the top rope, but he held onto the top rope and kept himself on the apron. Kelly fell onto all fours as the King of Submission ascended the turnbuckles. He wasn't a high flyer like earlier in his career, so when he did, he made sure to make it count. DOUBLE FOOT STOMP!!! The fans held their collective breaths as Danger jumped off the top rope and delivered a double foot stomp to the mat! Kelly Masters just did move his head out of the way at the last possible second! "You're one lucky son of a bitch," Mark Shields muttered in the general direction of Kelly Masters. Missing the move didn't hurt Danger at all, as he did a somersault on the mat and was right back on his feet. Masters had pulled himself up in the corner and just barely got out of the way of a charging Max and his clothesline. With Max now in the corner, Kelly began to unload with a series of right and left hands, peppering Danger square on the jaw. Max wasn't quite sure how to defend himself, the southpaw was throwing him off just enough to keep him off-balance. Kelly took Max by the arm and went to a whip across the ring, but nope, instead he pulled Danger back towards him looking for a massive clothesline. Danger, however, ducked and locked Masters around the waist from behind. The two men began to battle over the waistlock, with Kelly managing to put his leg behind Danger's to block any sort of suplex or takedown attempt, and began to pry his hands apart. He was quite stronger than Max and was able to break the hold and lifted Danger onto his shoulders. The Danger Man squirmed free of whatever Kelly had in mind, and quickly rolled him up with a small package! ONE! TWO! THR-- NO! "The Machine" powered out of the roll-up attempt and quickly got back onto his feet. He wiped blood from his nose before reaching out and catching Danger's kick attempt. An enziguri was tried by the King of Submission, but Kelly ducked, but without hesitation, Max went into a log roll to get away from Masters before he could even think about attempting the Achille's Heel. Danger was out on the apron when he got back onto his feet. When Kelly approached him, Danger slammed his shoulder into his gut, doubling Masters over. Max slingshot himself back into the ring and pulled Kelly over with him for a rollup. Only, he didn't. Kelly blocked the attempt, but when he went to punch Max in the face, Danger jerked his head out of the way, causing Kelly to punch the mat. Max kicked him square in the ass from his position on the ground, sending him into the ropes. Danger got back onto his feet but was up-ended with a double leg takedown and Masters started to unload on Max with a flurry of punches. The fans were going crazy, chanting, "KELLY! KELLY! KELLY!" Mark Shields was at least attempted to be a referee and telling Masters to stop with the face punching, but Kelly wasn't really listening to him. Mark really wanted to just shrug, say "fuck it", and go back and have a smoke, but he actually kind of needed the job, otherwise why would he have come all the way to Canadian to do this (I mean, aside from being able to get younger chicks drunk legally)? So, he began his five count. But the King of Submission knew he'd started it too late and needed to get himself out of the very painful and uncomfortable position. So, he flicked Kelly in the nose. It was a small thing, but when you had a potential broken nose, getting flicked just right was going to hurt, especially since you didn't expect it. And to top it off, Danger dug both his thumbs into Kelly's eyes and began to bite that very nose he'd just flicked. Masters screamed out in pain because, really, who expects to get flicked in the nose, have thumbs jammed in their eyes, and get their nose bitten? Not very many people. Danger managed to reverse the positions and now he was on top of Kelly. He began to rain down upon Masters with several visious elbow smashes, with the added benefit of having the back of Kelly's head bounce off the canvas with each blow. "Dude, you're going to have to stop that," said Mark Shields before he started his five count. And at five, Max threw his arms out to his sides. He never released anything until the referee said five, 'cause in his mind, it was ON the count of five, not before the count of five that you had to break a hold. The former ACW World Champion got to his feet and shook the cobwebs free from his noggin. Kelly Masters pulled himself up into a seated position... THWACK! ...only to get kicked right in the chest. Danger reared back and delivered another bruising blow to his chest. Max then nailed him in the back with a kick before coming back around front with another ki-- CAUGHT! Max began hopping up and down on his free leg and Kelly rose from the mat holding onto Danger's other leg. Danger was kinda fearful of what might happen, his mind racing with what to do. Earlier, an enziguri was ducked, and he couldn't reach out and poke Kelly in the eye. So, he kinda just had to wait and react to whatever Masters decided to do -- which scared the hell out of the Danger Man. Kelly decided to throw Max's leg away, sending him into a spin. Masters wrapped his arms around Danger's waist and started to hoist him for a German Suplex. Max, however, blocked by putting his foot behind Kelly's leg just as Masters had done earlier to him. Max threw two elbows backwards into the side of Kelly's head, but neither loosened his grip. So, Max slammed the back of his head into Masters' nose, which released a small whimper from Kelly, and loosened his hands just enough. Danger dropped down to his knees while holding onto Kelly's hands and quickly scooted backwards between Master's legs. Danger yanked on Kelly's arms, flipping him over onto his back. Still holding onto one of Kelly's arms, Max slid around on his butt, and got into position and locked in it. What? Oh yeah! DangerLock. Dead in the center of the ring, the Danger Man had Kelly trapped in the anaconda vice submission. The fans began to scream and cheer on "The Machine". Danger was holding the submission with all his strength and in his mind screaming, "TAP! TAP! TAAAAAAAAP!" Then he said it out loud, "TAP! TAP! TAAAAAAAAAAAAAP!!" And he did. Max's eyes went wide. Why? Because he thought he was a damn Jedi, willing Kelly to tap out with his mind, even though it was his devastating submission hold. Well, okay, he knew it was that, too, but the idea of having Jedi Mind Powers? Awesome. "Amazing" played once more as Danger released the hold and got to his feet. He stumbled back against the ropes and looked down at Kelly Masters, who lay on the mat in pain, looking up at the lights. Mark Shields way of trying to check on him was tapping him in the ribs with his boot and asking, "You dead?" Kelly wasn't. Just in pain and pissed. The Danger Man smirked as he stood there and he said, loud enough for Kelly to hear, "And THAT'S why I'm the King of Submission." And with those words, Danger collected "Armsy McDanger" and his DangerPhone, and headed to the back, leaving Kelly to lay around and think about things. Suffice to say they roared in approval of the violence in front of them. Cain went to swing another heavy elbow into the side of Alias's bloodied face, only for the Pulp Hero to duck under him. His stomach bleeding heavily into the bandages, he was no were near ready for a suplex, so Alias decided to pull something out for the second time in ACW. Something that Karina Wolfenden had taught him. PULPED II Cain was bent the wrong way, as Alias rocked him with the Lungblower. Alias rocked Cain with several forearms before bringing him to his feet, and locking him into a hammerlock. The Pulp Hero was sure that Cain wasn't even quite sure how to pull off one of these hammerlocks. Nah, kidding. But not as tight as Alias's hammerlock at least. Whiskey Jack then recoiled it into a harsh clotheline onto the concrete steps. The crowd outside roared out the name of the move, and even though Jimmy Cain was no stranger to them. "Welcome to ACW, Motherfucker!" Cain kicked out the legs of Alias, and slowly, crawled back into the ACW Arena to the jeers of all those outside. As Alias followed after him he scrambled through the doors and up the stairs to the balcony. Jimmy was scrambling for a plan, for a-OOOPH! The Pulp Hero had grabbed by the feet and yanked into onto his face, as they had made it halfway up the stairs. Jimmy kicked Alias in the face, catching him on the side of his blinded eye. Whiskey Jack growled, as Cain turned to face him. No more runnin' for Jimmy! Jimmy gonna FIGHT! The American Psycho roared bloody murder, and jumped towards Alias. Alias looked tensed and ready to collide with Cain, and by which I mean, Alias jumped out of Cain's way at the last moment and let the former Scorpion Fighting Champion tumble halfway down the stairs on his own accord. Alias, bloodied and bruised, grabbed the likewise Jimmy Cain and hauled his now half conscious body the rest of the way back to the balcony. The fans of the balcony now seeing the two fighters live, as opposed to on the ACWtron, roared in approval. Cain suddenly low-blowed the Original Pulp Hero once more. He was going to be the UNANIMOUS Fighting Champion, if it killed him. Or hey, if he had to kill Alias. Either way. He kneed Alias in the ribs once more with his barb-wire knee pad and then let Alias slink to all fours once again. Sizing him up, just like he had at ringside, Cain prepared to punt the Pulp Hero in the head once more. Only Alias popped up, and hip-tossed Cain. With the luck of Cain himself, at this moment, the hip-toss sent the Dow Jones Deathmachine tumbling down another set of stairs. This time to the foot of the balcony. Alias stumbled after him, and as he lost his footing, a fan kept him standing. The Pulp Hero gave the man a quiet nod as the crowd cheered them on. Once he was at the front of the balcony, Alias brought Cain to his feet. Cain headbutted Alias however, his eyes filled with seething insanity and awkwardly hefted the Pulp Hero up in a fireman's carry. The crowd screamed and was otherwise silenced, as Alias was dangled over the edge of the balcony. Was Alias going to be tossed into the Ginsu Shredder and off the balcony? Was Jimmy Cain that crazy? WARJIMMY~! Probably. But we weren't going to find out. Alias started raining in elbows and knees into either side of Cain's face. At first, it caused Cain to stumble and then fall down onto the balcony. KNEE. ELBOW. Alias wasn't about to let-up however. KNEE. ELBOW. The Pulp Hero was filling Cain with Bullets. A battered, puffy face Cain gurgled out a mouth full of blood and spit. Alias wasn't waiting any longer, he had done enough damage. He had done enough. Almost. The Pulp Hero locked in the Anarchy's Lullaby, to the extreme displeasure of the angered Jimmy Cain. TS was there to check on Cain. If he would either pass out or quit. TS's friend Strombo cheered the ref on from the balcony's fifth row. tap.tap.tap. The bell rang almost quietly, all the way down at ringside, below the roar of the crowd. As far as in-ring matches went, it wasn't. It had lasted in the ring all of seven seconds. From then on in, it was fighting at it's truest. The Original Pulp Hero stood to his feet, and looked from the battered body of the bastard that was Jimmy Cain to the roaring and cheering crowd around him and the people in the hundreds below him. He could only roar back in return, in a way almost addressing the ACW faithful. At this moment he was Arthur Pendragon of Camelot. He was Julius Caesar of Rome. He was King Leonidas of *ahem* SPAAARTA~! At the moment, he was the man on the top of his division. All in the name of the fight. For ACW. For the ACW Scorpion Fighting Championship. The World Champion Seymour Almasy and the Spirit of ACW Steve Knox, were still above him on the food chain, but right now Alias lifted his title belt in the air, having just been given it by TS the temp. A good percentage of the ACW Arena once again roared loudly. The road to Legends III had only begun. WINNER: Alias via submission (Anarchy's Lullaby); STILL Scorpion Fighting Champion
