Rome the Vile Has been, That guy from that promotion, Worthless piece of shit Real Name: Joshua Powell Date of Birth: August 4th, 1975 Hometown: Ft. Lauderdale, Florida, USA Height: 5'11' Weight: 235 lbs. Entrance Music: 'Judith' by A Perfect Circle Alignment Information Fan Dispostion: Face Current Teams: None Manager(s): Rome now sees any individual at ringside a burden, and has strictly forbidden ACW from slapping any pair of tits on his arm. Allies: Everyone else Enemies: I can trust my mother ... I think. Move-List Information Wrestling Style: Stepping away from his former high flying style, Rome will only take to the air on few ocassions, and reverts mostly now to a more strong style approach. He will use predominently hard striking blows, and various head drop maneuvers, looking to wear down every part of his opponent's body. Signature Moves: '3 Libras' - Combination Mongolian Chop/2 Forearms to Face/Running Yakuza Kick to opponents face, 'Myndphukd' Tiger Bomb into top turnbuckle, Springboard Twisting Shooting Star Press to outside standing opponent(generally a group of opponents), 'Tree of Rome' - While opponent is in tree of woe, Rome runs from the opposite corner and slides into opponent's face ... knee first. 1.) Forearms(every part of body), 2.) Falcon Arrow/Brainbuster Combo, 3.) Boot Scrapes, 4.) Violent Spinning Heel Kick, 5.) Yakuza Kick to kneeling opponent, 6.) Chop Frenzy, 7.) Rear Naked Choke, 8.) Corkscrew Backbreaker, 9.) Second Rope Stomp to opponents back(Generally following C.S. Backbreaker), 10.) ACW Award History: Mr. Congeniality? Other Federations: NAWA, oWa, and various indies and tournaments. Other Federation Awards: NAWA World Heavyweight Champion(x3) Biography Ask the man born as Joshua Powell about his life before wrestling, and he will have little to stay. Not because he is trying to hide anything, but because he can't remember the majority of it. He recalls his pre-fame life as rather normal. As many other pro wrestlers, he did the whole high school jock, date the head cheerleader thing. He holds his mother in high regard, and to this day considers her his greatest(and only) idol. When he attended his first pro wrestling class, was when the man now known as Rome the Vile deems himself reborn. It was an unknown school in a suburb of Denver, Colorado. The head trainer was a rather obese man, who had very brief fame over a decade earlier in Mexico. The only thing notable of the man was that he was the first to acknowledge the talent that Joshua had. He reccomended the moniker of 'Rome' to Joshua, in reference to the young student's love for Greek and Roman mythology. To this day, it is the only name Joshua has heard while entering an arena. After graduating from the school, Rome then worked indy shows predominently in the Rocky Mountain region, impressing small crowds, but never getting any national attention due to the lack of interest in indy wrestling in Colorado. After realizing that pro wrestling was the business he belonged in, he dropped out of the University of Colorado in his Junior year, and moved back to the east coast where the sky was the limit. From there, the blue chipper easily found work after trying out for various indy promotions in the Florida area. Finally, his name was getting mentioned in various wrestling magazines, and bookings began coming from cities farther north. Still considered a Junior Heavyweight at the time, Rome was invited to attend a handful of national tournaments, and even won the New England Junior Challenge in 1998. It was this victory that got him noticed by a man by the name of Jonathan Paris. Paris was the owner of the quickly booming North American Wrestling Alliance, and saw Rome as a great babyface for his midcard. Little did Paris know, but with the addition of a sharp tongue, and a flashy attitude ... the 'God of War' would become quite lucrative for the NAWA. After adding more mass to his body, Rome found his way into the Heavyweight Division, and won his very first Heavyweight Title by winning the NAWA's annual Iron Man Challenge. In 2000, Rome made his way into PWI's top 10, and was at the top of his game. The next year, thing's began to go downhill as Joshua developed an addiction to cocaine, and was stripped of the NAWA Heavyweight Title, and temporarily removed from the roster. It took months of rehab for Joshua to sober up, and a very unlikely visitor to get him back in the ring. The man who came to see Joshua was former NAWA Heavyweight Champion, and leader of the Circle of Chaos, NAPALM. The massive NAPALM had never been very friendly with Joshua, but amazingly came to him with a great offer. The COC had recently lost a member, and was in need of someone to compete in the NAWA Cruiserweight Division. Rome would no longer be a headliner, but at least he would get his job back. So Rome became the 'Warrior of Chaos', and took on a new gothic gimmick, which suprisingly took on very well. He was now seen as a strong midcard heel threat, but there was something very odd with the relationship formed between he and NAPALM. The ladder mentioned was known for performing strange rituals outside of the wrestling ring, and had been rumored to be able to brainwash certain individuals with spells. The fragile mind of Joshua would succumb to this, and in his worst moment, nearly took another life of another NAWA performer, by attempting to run his motorcycle off the road in a car. Rome was again released from his NAWA duties, and faced an attempted murder charge in front of a Massachusetts judge. His lawyer got Joshua off on a temporary insanity plea, and the former NAWA Heavyweight Champion spent a full year in a mental institution. After his release, Joshua made his way back to Florida, only able to find work as a security guard after hours at a local mall. His house had to be sold, and his fortunes earned were all gone. He now had to start over, with no direction. After months of working as a civilian again, Joshua refound his love for pro wrestling, and with the help of a promoter, began training again free of charge in a wherehouse owned by the Sunshine State Wrestling School. It was here that he developed a japanese strong style of wrestling, adding much more dangerous head drop and striking maneuvers to his move list. He put together a tape of matches he had worked with other members of the SSWS, as well as a list of credentials, and sent it to the headquarters of Total Impact Wrestling, where a few former NAWA workers were now working. Asking a shockingly low amount to work again, and with his name alone, TIW brass agreed immediately to signing a deal with Joshua Powell. They saw great potential in the history he has with a number of their current talent ... a very violent history.
