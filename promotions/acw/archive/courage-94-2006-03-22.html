Seymour was a practical sitting duck for the Rogues now. Scott Falk was ready to get in a lick, but Andrew held him back. No, Seymour was his now. Jill screamed in the corner, trying to get free of Robert, but Scott just held her by the head and forced her to look on at this catastrophe. Andrew kissed his knuckles and waited for Seymour to rise, only to PUNCH him with one of the hardest blows ever seen in ACW history. A trickle of blood ran down the face of Seymour Almasy as he tried to stand again, but Sharp pulled out some white workout tape and started to wrap his fist up with it. Readying his fists for some medieval asskicking, he delivered a jaw-crushing European Uppercut that sent Seymour flying into a low orbit before skittering across the ring. The blood flew even more as Andrew now grabbed him by the hair. Blow after blow rained down on his head, opening up the gaping wound on his forehead even more. Andrew motioned for the ACW World Heavyweight Title Seymour dropped as Codemaster handed it to him. He shoved the belt right into his face without words, but Seymour in a daze, got the message. Andrew was telling him to kiss the title goodbye. But since Seymour wasn’t into kissing belts, The Spirit of ACW decided to make the belt kiss him…well, kiss his forehead. And by ‘kiss,’ I mean ‘knock him the fuck out.’ Almasy was knocked very close to unconsciousness after the blow. This was just sick. Fans kept chanting the name of their hero in a vain attempt to will him back to his feet and fight the odds. But stupid fans. The bad guy always wins. Sharp grabbed him by the head and pants and HURLED him over the top rope with relative ease. Andrew posed for the camera and blew a kiss before walking to the outside. While Seymour was incapacitated, Sharp measured up Seymour as he continued to stagger, now complete with CRIMSON MASK. Up was left. Down was northeast. Just a way of telling you that Seymour had no idea where the hell he was at this point. But when he felt the hand of Andrew snap him up by the hair, he knew it wasn’t going to be good. He eyed some fans in the row who couldn’t believe just what the hell they were seeing. They were seeing a man utterly destroy his former best friend without ANY show of remorse. This vain motherfucker had the gall to call himself the innocent party in all this? Sharp sneered at a mother who was keeping her crying child from watching this. This was too much. Andrew, with a look of pure glee in his eyes, HURLED him face-first into the steel steps! He sickeningly bounced back and crumbled to the mat at The Spirit of ACW’s feet as more fans laid down the boos. But again…Andrew STILL wasn’t done. Tossing Seymour back into the ring, he climbed through the ropes as the Rogues now began to pick off the Champion, one by one, with Andrew directing traffic. A HUGE right hand from The Codemaster. A sharp roundhouse kick from the tiny Scott Falk. A vicious Polish Hammer from Robert Falk. The punishment was getting out of hand. Andrew was sitting there, enjoying this. He’d been corrupted and it seemed that the poisoned mind of Andrew Sharp was beyond the salvation that Seymour had hoped for. Finally, Andrew measured him up as Codemaster once again dragged the 183 pounds of dead weight back to his feet. He held his hands out like a video camera, proud of his destruction. Now, it was time to cement Andrew’s latest victory. He took the blood-stained tape and smacked him in the forehead one more time before applying the standing headscissors. For the last time tonight, Andrew would deliver a move that was already despised in the wrestling world. He double underhooked. He jumped. FACE-OFF! Seymour was limp and a pool of blood was starting to form right where his head was laying. Entranced by the sight of the damage, an almost euphoric look crossed the face of The Spirit of ACW as he now stood tall over his Zenith opponent. He placed a boot right on the small of Seymour’s back as Mega Job continued to film. A grim picture was soon taken as Trish Grayson handed both the Spirit and ACW World Titles to Andrew. With two arms, he raised both titles into the sky and posed for the camera. Jill cried out from where she was as Robert Falk continued to restrain her. Sharp didn’t take too kindly to this annoyance of the girl crying, so he shut her up GOOD with a BELT SHOT across the forehead! The ditzy interviewer collapsed right next to Seymour while the Rogues just laughed at her plight. She’d been knocked right out from the shot and this fact only made Andrew smile as he went back to his posing. Ladies and gentlemen, we now leave you with three gruesome images. One: Seymour lying in a pool of his own blood. Two: Jill KO’ed on the ground with a cut above her right eye. Three: Andrew Sharp holding both titles as a chilling sign of a future that may very well come true. What has Andrew Sharp done? -fin- CREDITS.
