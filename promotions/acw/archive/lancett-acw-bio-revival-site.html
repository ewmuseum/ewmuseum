Lancett The Megastar, Kid Frost, Rook', Peoria Pirate, The Weasel Real Name: Joshua Gary Date of Birth: April 6, 1983 Hometown: Peoria, Illinois Height: 6'4' Weight: 249 lbs. Entrance Music: Static X's ''The Only'' Alignment Information Fan Dispostion: Neutral Current Teams: Manager(s): N/A Allies: Enemies: Move-List Information Wrestling Style: Technical Spotfest, aims to damage the head and back of the victim. Signature Moves: Headlock take down, drop toe hold, clothesline, dropkick, belly-to-belly, double underhook overhead belly-to-belly, bulldog, tope rope flying elbowdrop*, and moonsault. Mega Stardome – Phoenix Splash.** The Pinnacle -Styles Clash.* Kiss on the Cheek – Shining Wizard. Real Reason there is a Show - Asai Moonsault into a Inverted DDT. The Uprising – Burning Hammer.** Lancett Lock – Ankle Lock.* Megastar Struck – Tilt-a-whirl Backbreaker. 3 Strikes – Three STO’s one forward on to Lancett’s knee, one backwards onto his knee again, then one normal one. Six Star Struck – Elevated Double Underhook Facebuster (Christopher Daniels’ Angel’s Wings). Best Damn Driver (In the business) – Any piledriver.* Legacy Ender – Any DDT he hits besides “Real Reason there is a Show.” Dishonor in the 1st Degree – Elevated Boston Crab. Disgrace – Perry Saturn’s Rings of Saturn as he trash talks them when he has them locked.* Reign – Texas Cloverleaf. Hex Helix – Hurricane’s Eye of the Hurricane onto his knee.*** * - They are use to finish a match. ** - They are saved for a PPV match. Set Up Move: Look Above. - * Finisher: Shut the Fuck up! (STFU) - Shining Wizard Historic Information ACW Title History: ACW Award History: Other Federations: hWo, Carnage Other Federation Awards: hWo Rising Star Championship Biography With all the shit, gilded times, and drama Lancett had to deal with day in and day out he has probably matured the most in the last couple of years. He has meet people in the back and he has hated them from the start. He has meet people outside of wrestling and hated them too. This loner persona has become custom for the days at hand. He’d tour with the federations and come home to his girlfriend, Amber, and faceless grace. He joined hWo and followed “Superstar” Vince Jacobs, the man who trained him. He followed his footsteps, and he followed his handprints. He acted as if Vince was a resurrected Christ that walked this earth and Lancett was his disciple. Fuck that! Lancett took matters in his own hands. He turned on Vince and he became a cheered man. People praised the ground where he walked. It amazed him how stupid people could be. He played along, and still is playing along in the game called hWo. Now, he is in ACW with new fans, new people to praise him, new people to hate him, a new owner to kiss ass to, or maybe not . . . Disrespect? Dishonor? Distrust? All of the above.
