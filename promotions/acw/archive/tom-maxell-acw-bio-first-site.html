What this is The fed's own biography page for the character Tom Maxell, from the fed's first site (acw.ewmania.com, the 2002 to 2004 edition), as captured in the Wayback on 30 September 2003. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Tom Maxell Real Name: Tom Maxell Birthdate: 10th June 1976 Hometown: Los Angeles Height: 6ft6 Weight: 276 lbs. Nicknames: Maxell, Volatile Content Status: Heel Manager(s): Tom has no manager Current Teams: Tom isn't part of any team Entrance Music: 'The Red' by Chevelle Wrestling Style: Technically Gifted Brawler/Very Hardcore Orientated Signature Moves: Airplane Spin, Arm Breaker, Arm Wringer, Atomic Drop, Brain buster, Corner Body Press, Drop Toe Hold, Elbow Smash, Flap Jack, Forearm Smash, Heart Punch, Heart Kick, Jawbreaker, Knee Breaker Drop, Knee Lift, Neck breaker, Neck Snap, Pile driver, Power Slam, Short Arm Clothesline. Trademark Move: Aversion (Tom raises his opponent up in a suplex position and then hangs them over the top rope. Keeping his opponent in the head lock in the hold, he suddenly drops down to the mat spiking his opponent's head into the mat with devastating DDT) Set Up Move: TMKO (Singapore Cane shot which more often than not knocks his opponent straight out. This will often be used behind the referee’s back and the V.W.H will follow, forcing the Ref to call a knock out for the match ) Finisher: View With Horror [V.W.H] (Tom hooks his opponent’s arms up from behind in a full nelson position while on the ground. He then lifts his knees into his opponent’s back and digs them in hard, forcing his opponent to submit.) ACW Title History: Tom has held no titles in ACW thus far ACW Award History: Tom has won no awards in ACW thus far Other Federations: UFS California, UFS Nevada Other Federation Awards: UFS C Champion, UFS N Champion Handler Information Handler Name: Tom AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Biography Since his desire to control and discriminate people started, Tom had been a proud member of the Underground Fighting Syndicate. The UFS was a place where hundreds of people from all over the country took part in, with several states having its own “promotion” within the syndicate. Tom entered the UFS at the age of 19 as part of the UFS California group and he remained there for 3 years. The California part of the UFS was considered by many who followed as one of the most brutal entities of the whole movement and Tom was one of the main players in the country. In Tom’s last ever match within the UFS California; he defeated the main champion in the promotion. He defeated the most profitable and egotistical men in the region to become the UFS California Champion. Tom was proud of himself, but he didn’t know how to react. It was the first time in his life where he had won something to be proud of and the first thing to be congratulated on and he didn’t know how to react to it. The next week, Tom left the promotion resigning the championship. For the next few months Tom lived in a caravan living off the prize money that he had won over the three years. He did nothing but sleep, eat and drink. His trusted Singapore Cane stood in the corner of the caravan… clean, not being used. He had to think… he had to do something. The pressure of being respected and congratulated and his not knowing how to react to the kindness was his downfall. He was a fighter… and he was a champion… but not in his own mind. Tired of getting bugged by his coked up, alcoholic parents whenever they stopped gambling long enough to ask for money, Tom decided to leave LA and head onto a different state. Speculation started that after a break from the cage he was going back… a fresh start in another region of the UFS. At first it wasn’t in his mind, but the more and more he heard the speculation, the more and more he thought after fighting again. It was what he wanted to do… he loved the hurt and pain… he loved smashing a Singapore Cane over someone’s head knocking them out and seeing the blood gushing from their forehead. He eventually gave in and joined the Nevada branch of UFS. Right from the start Tom Maxell was the name on everybody’s lips. People talked about him… some praised him, those were the people Tom hated… others despised him; those were the people Tom liked. He stayed in UFS Nevada for 4 years. The Nevada region UFS was slightly different from what Tom was used to. Where as California was all out brutal fighting in a cage, Nevada was also in a cage it was slightly more restricted. Fighters were required to add to their performances by spending time talking on a microphone… something that Tom wasn’t accustomed to. Tom was used to being the quiet boy in the corner who said little and had little confidence to speak out. Over the first two years Tom spent in Nevada he learnt to loosen up and become confident and successful on the microphone. He become learnt to take the role as a heel to a new level in fighting and because he was killer at working up the crowd, the prospect of being a recognised and respected champion was less daunting for him. Though he is still weary he now takes everything in his stride and he has more confidence in him to handle the pressure. This showed, as Tom Maxell became the Nevada champion towards the summer of 2001, signalling the three-year anniversary of his run in the Nevada branch. Unlike last time… Tom was recognised as a hate figure of the fans… and he loved every second of it. He handled the pressure and he stuck in and learnt to be a successful champion. Tom was the UFS Nevada champion for little over a year without changing face, without going stale and keeping on the ball. Tom was a success and did everything that was needed of him… until the end of his 4-year contract loomed. Tom expressed a wish to move on again in his career and do something new. Towards the end of his contract Tom put over a new champion by dropping the title and he moved away from Nevada on good terms. Tom had a 2-month rest to let his body heal before signing with a small wrestling promotion based out of Arizona. Tom spent 7 months wrestling for this promotion as one of the hardcore division mainstays. His run ended as the hardcore division was disbanded and Tom was released from his contract and left to do his own thing. Wanting to stay with Wrestling as opposed to Fighting, for the past 2 months Tom has searched high and low to find a place that will accept his hardcore brawler style of wrestling and give him the chance to express himself the way he wants to, but so far to no avail.
