KC Payne Biography Real Name Kevin Charles [name withheld] Hometown Pikeville, KY Birthday April 23th, 1979 (age 31) Place of Birth Pikeville, KY Height 5'11 Weight 244 Blood Type AB- Eye Color Brown Biography Alignment and Personality: Rough-and-tumble Face. The older of the two brothers, Kind of a wiseass and a dick to those he doesn’t know. Doesn’t take too much seriously, but does love to put on a good show for the fans. He’s more of the instigator of the duo. Description: Short, but very thick and stocky. Long, stringy black hair, heavy goatee. Left arm caked with multiple tattoos. Crimson trunks with “PAYNE GANG” on the back in Rockwell Extra Bold Font. Crimson kneepads and matching boots. Other Feds ELW Other Fed Titles ELW Tag Team Champion x9 Team The Payne Gang w/Kassidy Payne Wrestling Style All-rounder with a solid power base. Has a pretty well-rounded arsenal out of the two and whereas Kassidy Payne is the more powerful of the two. Having more technical skills than his brother, KC likes to start the matches for his team and is just as comfortable on the mat as he is taking to the skies or punching people in the face. Is 70% face-in-peril and as the hot tag, he explodes with suplexes and the like.
