He shrugged it off, and as Lancett got to his feet, Alias wrapped the shirt around his throat. At four, Alias released the hold, and the referee admonished him for the choke. Alias said back to the ref that the kid had to be fought with his own set of rules if he wanted to fight dirty. Well… ol’ Al had a point. Lancett huffed and puffed on the canvas, before springing to his feet and charging Alias. Alias ducked underneath the clothesline attempt, and Lancett was soon to return off the other side. Alias used his Doc Brass t-shirt to clothesline him down, choking him to the canvas before tossing the shirt into the audience. With Lancett down, gasping for air, Alias pinned down his shoulders, hooking a leg. One! Two! Lancett kicked out so Alias stood back up and kicked him three times in the side, softening up his ribs to follow with Kid Frost’s battered shoulder and neck. Alias lifted Lancett off the mat, ready to hit him with a signature move… Pulped, but that's when Lancett retaliated, low blowing the Original Pulp Hero to his knees once more. Lancett returned to his own feet, placed Alias between his legs, and drove him deep into the heart of the canvas with a double underhook powerbomb. Alias's back bounced off the mat as he rolled to his side and then onto his stomach completely to avoid the pinfall. Lancett was forced to roll Alias completely over and cover the leg. One! TW-NO! That little roll gave Alias enough time to recover and kick out in the middle of two. Lancett stood up for a moment, contemplating his next move. Lancett walked towards Alias once again and as he bent over, Alias grabbed at Lancett rolling his up and ending up on top of him. As opposed to pinning him, Alias let loose another barrage of solid lefts and rights. After finishing up on the brawling end of things, Alias grabbed Lancett’s arm and cinched it up… falling into a fujiwara armbar of his own. Lancett held his face in pain, and observed the blood that collected on the canvas as his back, pain also shooting up and down his arm as Alias pulled back on it. Finally releasing the armbar, Alias stood back to his feet… waiting for Lancett. Lancett stood and swung with a wild right, but Alias evaded contact and caught him with a stiff European uppercut. Another one backed Lancett into the ropes, whereupon Alias tried to whip him across. But Lancett spun around and tried to short arm clothesline Alias, but Alias ducked and pulled Lancett backwards into a rollup! One! TWO! THRE-KICKOUT! Lancett was slow getting up, and this allowed Alias to hook him up and pop him back into the mat with a double arm DDT. The fans exploded upon the connection of this maneuver, which layed out Lancett quasi-cold on the mat. Still he had enough wits about him to roll to the outside… half awake. Lancett tried to use the outside to take a quick breather, but that breather was about the span of a breath, because Alias ran off of the ropes opposite of that to Lancett and exploded through the ropes. Suicida elbow smash, flying through the second and third ropes and connecting with Lancett! It took Alias a few moments to recover before he pulled Lancett, who was much worse off, up to his feet and tossed him into the ring again. A Bret Hart-style elbow drop from the corner later, and Alias quickly went for the cover on Lancett. One! TWO! THREE-NO! Lancett kicked out with the ref's hand just inches from the canvas, and the crowd gasped in shock. So, Alias decided that now would be a good time to end this match. His wounds had, thankfully, sealed up on their own and his adrenaline was keeping his standing through this week of hell, as he pulled Lancett back to his knees now. Alias looked to hook his arms in. Anarchy’s Lullaby. Denied. Lancett fought against it before it was hooked in. Alias grabbed Lancett’s head and rammed the back of it into his knee, sending the younger star almost cold on his knees. Alias took the opening that he needed, and took it as quick as his body would carry his to do it. Anarchy’s Lullaby! Lancett didn’t even squirm… because after all he’d been through, with this match being just one of the factors… he just didn’t have fresh enough legs to keep him going. The referee checked for a response from Lancett, a sign of life…. knowing that Lancett was the kind of wrestler, the kind of inert fighter that wouldn’t tap out or say I quit. The ref lifted his arm once. No response. The crowd kept on cheering… chanting Alias’s name. The hand was raised… and it dropped for a second time. The buzz within the crowd grew larger, the electricity within the arena made the place buckle. There Pulp Hero had returned… set on the fact that he would fight for his right within this company, still fight for this company. The hand was raised… It dropped. Lancett was out. The Ohio faithful blew the roof off the place. Alias released the hold and fell back, for now… for just a moment he could rest. He heard his music, the cheers… and he smiled. Lancett had tried, even with his injuries… he had put up a fight. Alias however, was back in form in ACW… pulling no punches. … heh, and they say you can never go back home. When it came to Alias… well… SilverHAWK wanted to make sure of that. Andrew Jackson once said that, one man with courage… is a majority. Alias wondered what he had gotten himself into, for his own beliefs, his own martyr complex, as Courage… faded to black. WINNER : Alias
