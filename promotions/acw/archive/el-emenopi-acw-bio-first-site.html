What this is The fed's own biography page for the character El Emenopi, from the fed's first site (acw.ewmania.com, the 2002 to 2004 edition), as captured in the Wayback on 27 September 2003. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
El Emenopi Real Name: Elian Ramirez-De La Fuente Birthdate: 4/7/77 Hometown: New York City by way of Zapopan, Mexico Height: 5' 5' Weight: 147 lbs. Nicknames: Mexican Super-Job, Idiot, Alpha-Beta Disasterpiece, El Mastero De Disasterio... Status: Neutral Manager(s): n/a Current Teams: None... Entrance Music: 'Loser (Alpha-Beta Remix' by Beck and El Emenopi Wrestling Style: Luchador... but isn't exactly 'talented' Signature Moves: Finding any perfect way to hurt himself... on accident... and ruin the match for himself... somehow. Set Up Move: F-Minus (In homage to his new found screw-up hero, Brock Lesnar... El has designed his finisher after the F-Five, but only as Brock screwed it up, dropping the opponent on his back as he falls down and lets them land on him... ) Finisher: The QR STar (Brock Lesnar's shooting star 'break-your-own-neck' head butt...) ACW Title History: BWAHAHAHAH! ACW Award History: Unofficial ACW Super-Jobber Other Federations: ukw, McDonalds Other Federation Awards: BWAHAHAHAH!!! Handler Information Handler Name: Jae AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Biography Elian Ramirez-De La Fuente is a former Mexican baseball legend... in his own mind. At the same time, his long-lasting tenure as World Champion of Mickey D's Wrestling (known also as 'beating up rats near the dumpster with a broom') made him a wrestling legend... in his own mind. He ran around in independante federations causing havoc dressed as a luchador... but he was never truly hired in any of the places. All he would do is do a run-in, and mess up the match... all the while, usually injuring himself and suing the fed for all they had. He flew to England in hopes that he could get a job in one of their 'corny' federations... but couldn't even land a spot there, catching only a janitorial job in, Harringey based, United Kingdom Wrestling. He would force himself into matches there, getting himself into trouble mainly, and lose every single one. Leaving him with a lifetime record of zero wins, senty-eight losses... When the company folded, they didn't even give him a pink slip, since they forgot he even worked there. He finally made it back home, climbing into the wheel-well of a international flight to New York... Now a homeless man trying to find his way around, he showed up in ACW, with camera at hand to bring his new porno series... 'Females Went Freaky-Deaky'... to life. The idea AND his porn career flopped. And he, once again, has turned to wrestling... He's not truly hired in ACW, but his constant hacking of ACW technology might help him get somewhere... maybe.
