ARC II - The Road to Legends III 4th June 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice introduction Our deepest fear is not that we are inadequate. Our deepest fear is that we are powerful beyond measure. It is our light, not our darkness that most frightens us. We ask ourselves, Who am I to be brilliant, gorgeous, talented, fabulous? Actually, who are you not to be? You are a child of God. Your playing small does not serve the world. There is nothing enlightened about shrinking so that other people won't feel insecure around you. We are all meant to shine, as children do. We were born to make manifest the glory of God that is within us. It is not just in some of us; it is in everyone. And as we let our own light shine, we unconsciously give other people permission to do the same. As we are liberated from our own fear, our presence automatically liberates others. As some things change, some remain the same. LEGENDS III will change everything. Public Service Announcement - HAWK Style! "Welcome to Legends fucking three!" A roar from the ACW faithful greeted SilverHAWK as he pushed his way through the backstage curtain and onto the top of the ramp where he now positioned himself under the bright spotlight. Dressed for the occasion in a smart suit-trouser and jumper combo, he looked around at the sell-out crowd and took a deep intake of oxygen to calm his nerves. The past few weeks had been far from smooth, and if truth me told, HAWK wanted this night over and done with. "I remember telling you all I would stop doing this but tonight is a special occasion... I'm wearing shoes goddamnit, I want to show them off!" The sarcastic roar from the crowd was met by a smile by HAWK as he watched the ring technicians making their final preparation to the second ring of the evening. "What a show we have in store for you tonight Montreal...what a show! I'm sure you are all aware of the card being the true ACW fans that you are?" A slight response from the fans prompted HAWK to run down the card. "In a matter of moments Max Danger and Kelly Masters will be starting their Ultimate Submission Match!" You could guess what reaction either men got from the crowd. "Followed by Tengu again Chris Moliano! With Iceman squaring up to Preston Baxter after that." HAWK began to pace as he caught a number of cameras flashing on his right hand side. "Then, the Spirit of ACW title will be on the line as Rejection faces up to both Sharp brothers! Man, we aren't even done. It's Alias up next (huge cheer) as he takes on Brian Spaes for the Scorpion fighting title in an ACW first, an Empty Arena Match." HAWK paused for a moment as he let that sink in. "Please ladies and gentleman, stay calm during that part of the show to get you all safely out of the building, as trust me, this isn't going to be the easiest thing in the world to execute! We have screens erected outside so that you won’t miss a thing, so please, don’t panic, you will not miss anything. And lastly...our main event." This is what they had been waiting for. "I want to say a few things first about that. ACW to me, is like one big family...not everyone gets on, but we sure as hell don't piss on the couch no matter how mad we are." In the world of fucked up analogies, HAWK had won. "In my opinion...some people have pissed on the couch as of late, and trust me when I say this won't be continuing. Tonight my hand has been forced...tonight’s main event will be Seymour Almasy going up against Violence Jack." The announcement was a shock to some, and more like common knowledge to others. "But let me tell you this faithful...no matter who wins tonight, they will be defending the belt on Courage next week...and the week after that...and the motherfucking week after that until someone who deserves the gold has it around their fucking waist!" An almighty roar raised the roof as HAWK nearly burst a blood vessel with the anger in his voice. "Enjoy Legends everyone...I'm sure you won't be disappointed!" Kelly Masters vs. Max Danger Legends was officially ready to begin. However, the action would not be inside the All-Star Arena, no sir. Out in the parking lot, a ring had been constructed hours before show time, and the lot was filled with ACW fans. 2500 customers also sat inside the arena – anxiously waiting to watch the Ultimate Submissions match on the large video screens. Outside, the skies were clear, although the air had started cooling, but no red-blood, wrestling fan cared about the weather. The temperature was easing lower and lower, but the action was just heating up. Mark Shields, cigarette hanging from his mouth, stood inside the ring, but had mixed feelings about his duties. For once, he could smoke without getting hassled. On the other hand, he’d be forced to officiate for an hour straight. One by one I deal with them, Take them by the neck Drag them around kicking and screaming, Showing the law of cause and effect The specially rigged sound system exploded ‘There’s Gonna Be a War’ by Nonpoint through the speakers throughout the parking lot. Masters parted the ACW crowd like Moses did the Red Sea. His white trunks had been replaced with royal purple and gold trunks, but aside from the slight wardrobe change, Masters was unchanged. Occasionally, a fan would slap Masters on the back, but the Minneapolis native paid no attention to them. He slid into the ring, jumped to see his feet, and began his pre-match muscle loosening (arms, neck, and legs). ‘Amazing’ overtook the crowd’s rumbling as Max Danger followed the path Masters had taken to the ring. To prevent the fans from touching him, Danger’s three beer buddies from weeks earlier flanked him, and Marcus (the smark) followed behind the Danger Man all the while recording the entrance on his own version of the DangerPhone. Max sported black and blue tights – always a new color scheme for Danger, who finally reached the ring and slid in. Shields motioned the two men into the center of the outdoor ring, and quickly jumped into the rules. “Sixty minutes,” Shields informed with a roll of his eyes, “three ropes breaks each, submissions and inability to answer three arm raises will be a point each. The man with the most points at the end wins.” Of course, as Shields finished his rules, Danger pointed to himself as the “man with the most points”. The two submissions rivals separated, and the reserve ACW timekeeper (Marcus) rung the bell. DING! DING! DING! In exactly sixty minutes, the King of Submission would be crowned (not literally – there’s a cup, not a crown). Danger, brimming with confidence, circled around the ring, and the less seasoned Masters did the same. Normally, Masters would have started with a furious pace, but Danger was as good a wrestler as any and wrestling for sixty straight minutes was a whole different beast than ten or fifteen. The slow start wasn’t surprising, but with nearly half a minute burnt up, Masters shot in for a double leg. Danger was evasive enough to avoid the takedown, and Masters simply shot through back to his feet. They began circling each other again. Danger seemed hesitant to lock up with Masters, who outweighed Danger by a good fifty pounds. Whether he wanted to or not, that’s exactly where the Danger Man found himself. Masters pushed Danger back into the corner. Instinctively, Danger reached for the ropes before quickly realizing his mistake and releasing them. “Doesn’t count!” Max exclaimed, enough to muster a shrug from Shields. What did count was the forearm shot that landed exactly where those words had come from, and Danger was none too pleased to meet the second consecutive forearm either. The third seemed downright unnecessary, but Max wasn’t about to speak up. Masters, a good chunk of Danger’s hair in hand, pulled Max toward him then utilized another forearm shot, this one hitting Danger clean in the Adam’s apple. The illusion of choking sent Danger’s hands around his throat and Masters seized the open opportunity with a ring rattling double leg. Danger deflected the hammer blows that followed as best he could, however, he did take a few good smacks to the face and neck. When Masters postured up to hit Danger again, Max got his legs between Masters and himself. He scrambled to his feet during the brief opening, and a drop toe hold on the approaching Masters put the Augusta born wrestler in control. Max moved up on Kelly, slapping a chinlock on Masters. His attempts to scissor Masters’ arm were fruitless as the Machine slipped onto his knees and peeled Danger’s chinlock loose. With both men on their knees and facing each other, Masters lunged out locking Max into a front headlock. Masters immediately pushed into Danger, looking to cut his airway off with his forearm across Danger’s neck. A quick, inside sit-out from Danger ended the choke, and was allowed him to apply a hammerlock from behind Masters. The cat and mouse wrestling game seemed to favor the more experienced and pure wrestler, Danger. With a hint of annoyance at the excellent counter from Danger, Masters powered up to his feet, bringing both Max and the hammerlock with him. A snap back elbow missed narrowly, and Danger took advantage by moving around Masters’ side to deliver a gut-busting knee. The Machine bent over in pain, which made Max smile as he applied a choke around Masters’ throat with his free arm (the other kept the hammerlock in place). The hold was not complete as Danger then leapt and wrapped his legs around Masters’ midsection. Body Vice (and Hammerlock) Guillotine Choke! Floating down Shit Creek without a paddle would explain Masters’ current predicament as the lulled crowd quickly found their energy. Masters struggled with Danger’s weight at first, but eventually succumbed to the 220 odd pounds that hang onto him like a leech. Danger laid his head and body back, increasing the pressure around the neck. “I’m the greatest!” he shouted, and in the background you could hear Marcus reaffirm Max’s statement with “He actually is the greatest”. If you were to ask Masters at that moment, I’d doubt he would agree. Strength could certainly come in handy from time to time, and when Masters powered into a tripod (two legs and his head), that was one of those moments. When he picked Danger, eyes wider than a caffeine junkie, up with only one useable arm (should have brought Armsy), you could once again give strength credit. Running full speed, and slamming all 270 plus pounds of him into Danger, who then collided with the turnbuckle? Priceless and effective in escaping the first serious submission in the match. Upon being released, Masters, out of breath, dropped to a knee next to Danger, who was nursing his newly tender back. After a few moments passed, both submission wrestlers had managed to get up. It was Masters that grabbed and pulled a tuft of Max’s hairdo backward. The knee that was placed oh-so precisely at the small of Danger’s back put him back down on his knees. Those thick, iron clubs that Masters called forearms dropped Max face down after two of them consecutively smashed against the King of Submission’s neck. Stomping on Danger’s back proved to be the next piece of the puzzle, and Masters did so with much pleasure. Next step? Pain. Masters knelt down (by that, I mean perpendicularly on Danger’s back), locked on paw around Danger’s chin and the other onto his right ankle. Of course, once you have that all locked up, the goal is to have a face-to-face between the head and foot. Not impossible, but much easier if the Machine could just snap Max’s spine in half (no big deal). So, it was after a short torturous period that Masters released the hold. Max and Marcus were both relieved, and Shields seemed to be jubilant that there were no broken spines, yet. With both men back up (Masters aiding Danger), the Machine put a boot into Danger’s midsection and flipped him over with a gut-wrench suplex. Kelly propped himself back up, and immediately dropped an elbow across the Danger Man’s throat. “GAH!” escaped the Danger Man’s mouth as he clutched his throat with his hands as he rolled over onto his stomach. Kelly stood, straddling Max, and slapped him in the ribs, causing Danger to remove his hands from his throat to his sides. Masters took Max’s arms and put them over his knees as he kneeled down and applied a camel clutch. Not something you typical... WHAM! WHAM! WHAM! ...ah, there we go. Clubbing forearms across the sides of Danger’s head. That was more like the Machine. Mark Shields, from his standing position, asked Max if he wanted to give it up. “Maybe?” he asked. “I dunno, man, falling down into an early hole...” replied Mark. “I vote no, then,” said the (for the time being anyway) King of Submission. Since he wasn’t going to submit, Kelly stood and pulled Danger up with him by the undersides of his arms. Masters wrapped his arms around the waist of the Danger Man, but Max had the good sense to put his leg behind Kelly’s and block any thought of a German suplex. Instead, he got three elbow smashes right in the back of his skull, knocking his head forward violently with each blow. Masters hooked Max again in the waistlock and powered him up for the su... NO! Danger hooked his feet behind Kelly’s waist and countered with a forward roll! Max was back on his feet with Kelly flat on his back, Danger had his legs in his hand, put his leg through Kelly’s, and then turned him over into with the sharpshooter! Or not. Viciously, Masters kicked at Danger’s head, knocking him backwards and against the ropes. Kelly, from a crouched position, dove forward look for another double leg takedown, and he got it, but Max wrapped his arms around Master’s neck and applied the guillotine choke! The impact of being slammed into the mat still did not cause the King of Submission to release the hold. Max was actually able to even wrap his legs around Kelly’s body, adding more pressure to the submission hold! The fans outside, while some were on the side of Danger (a handful, really), the others began to cheer on Kelly Masters. Max had the hold cinched in tightly, but Kelly had the distinct height (three inches), weight (fifty pounds), and overall strength advantage on the current King of Submission. Masters placed both palms flat against the mat and had his knees under him, with Danger hanging onto him like a tree sloth. Kelly took a couple of breaths to steady himself then managed to get his feet under him, pushing up off the canvas, and into a kneeling position, still with Max clinging to him like he was a baby in one of those chest baby carriers. “I think you should really tap out,” said the Danger Man, squeezing his arms around Kelly’s neck and throat even tighter. Masters answered only in a grunt as he powered up into a standing position, reached behind him to try and pry Max’s legs apart. “Hey, what do you think--” but Danger’s words were cut off as Kelly unwrapped himself from Max’s body vice, hoisted him up, and with a mighty heave, tossed him out of the submission and up and over his head. Danger landed with a thud on the mat behind Masters, but quickly rolled to his feet, only to get smacked across the mouth with a stiff right-hand. Kelly followed up with a hard left hook than knocked Max sideways and against the ropes. Masters grabbed and Max’s head and pulled him from the ropes. Still clutching Danger’s head, Kelly unloaded with a series of knee strikes that Max was able to mostly block with his hands, but a few hit him in the ribs, and a couple got him right on the nose. Out of this position, Kelly hooked up the Danger Man and hit him with a snap suplex, slamming his back against the canvas. He floated over but, obviously, didn’t go for a cover, instead he attempted to transition into the jujigatame! Danger, however, was blocking by locking his hands together, preventing Kelly from getting his lone arm free. He tried slamming the back of his knee down against Max’s face, but that still didn’t do the trick. Frustrated, Masters released Danger’s arm and picked him up by his shaggy hair. Max, in desperation, thumbed Kelly right in the eye! He followed up with a quick koppou kick, catching Masters in the chest and knocking him back a couple of steps. Danger raced in and grabbed Masters’ head, looking for the Codebreaker, and smashing Kelly’s face against his knees. But the Machine caught Max as he jumped up. He lifted him up onto his shoulders, but before he could do anything, the Danger Man slipped out behind him. He grabbed him in a waistlock, but Masters planted his feet and began to pull apart his arms. He held onto one of Max’s arms as he pulled away then yanked Danger towards him. Massive clothesline? Ducked. Kelly turned right into... SMACK! DANGEROUS III! The roaring elbow connected with the butt of Kelly’s chin, allowed Danger to go in and apply the DangerLock!! The crowds inside and out were going bonkers. Hey, what Max lacked in size and strength against Kelly Masters, he made up for with experience. He wasn’t the King of Submission for nothing. Kelly Masters now was in a predicament. He was groggy from the Dangerous III and this was quite the deadly submission hold, but he was still stubborn enough to think he could get out of it. Did he want to try and power out and risk injury? Did he want to use up a rope break this early into the contest? Even if he tapped out now, he still had over forty minutes to not only tie the score but also take the lead and win. What was he going to do? “C’MON!” shouted the Danger Man. “You know what you have to do!” Mark Shields was down in position, waiting for either a tap out or Kelly submitting verbally (which, let’s be serious, he was NEVER going to do). “Tap! Tap! Tap!” shouted Danger’s drunken buddies and Marcus the Smark. Everyone else? “KELL-Y! KELL-Y! KELL-Y!” What did he do? Tap. Tap. Tap. --- SCORE: Danger 1, Masters 0 --- “Oh yeah!” screamed Danger as he released the submission and jumped up onto his feet. He was elated, after all, he was now up 1-nil. Max paraded around the ring like he had just won the match. He was about to high-five Marcus the Smark but then thought better of it. He’d probably “pleased” himself during that submission, so... yeah. He’d pulled out the big guns early, netting him the lead, but, you see, here’s the thing. There was still now just over forty minutes to go in the contest. As he stood on the middle turnbuckle looking out over the sea of fans in the parking lot of All-Star Arena, that realization struck the King of Submission. “Aaah, fudge!” He turned around and saw that Kelly Masters, on the opposite side of the ring, was climbing to his feet. Snarling. Frothing. Yeah, he was a skosh upset. He did what he had to do, he preserved his body to continue fighting for another forty-odd minutes. He didn’t like that he had to submit and concede the early lead, but there was plenty of time. Then again, he was still having to shake loose the cobwebs from Danger’s hellacious spinning elbow smash and the squeezing pressure of the anaconda vice, and Max? Well, he was a wily veteran. There was no telling what he had up his sleeve. But Kelly didn’t give a shit. He just wanted to pummel the Danger Man, make him cry uncle, and take everything from him that he held dear. After all, this night and this match was why he came to ACW in the first place. And he wasn’t leaving until he’d come here to do: make Max Danger his bitch. Max could see all this in his bright, green eyes. “Gulp.” And with that, Danger hopped right out of the ring and backed up to the barricade that had been put up just a few feet from the ring to keep the fans at a safe distance. But there was much less room around ring side out here than there was inside the arena. Kelly Masters cracked his neck from side to side then began stalking forward. As he neared the ropes, Max smiled nervously, then said, “See ya!” and dove underneath the ring. Seriously. Mark Shields chuckled causing him to get a wicked look from Kelly. Shields just shrugged at him and lit up another cigarette. Max Danger had the lead now. Thus began the game of cat and mouse. Our Every Move is a New Tradition “So, Jon Crisp versus Malik Roland.” “I'm going with Malik. Bigger and more of a brawler.” “Man, I'm sticking with Crisp. You can't stop an NCAA champion on the mat. This is the new tradition.” Tengu vs. Chris Moliano The lights in the arena went out and smoke began to filter onto the stage as red lights began to glow in and out at different times, illuminating the smoke red. The chilling sound of “Der Golem” by Fantômas began to play over the speakers as the masked Tengu emerged from the back, staring down towards the ACW ring. His walk to the squared circle was slow and menacing as if he were stalking the ring, only stopping occasionally to snappily turn his head and stare at some of the fans. As he slid under the bottom rope and into the ring he ventured towards a corner and slowly mounted the top rope where he perched, as if ready to pounce. If he were to have his way, Chris Moliano was in for a long and hard night. “Cocky” by Kid Rock. The lights came back on as “Perfection Personified” Chris Moliano came out from backstage and walked down the aisle. His trademark beaming smile was absent, instead replaced by an intense focused look as he climbed through the ropes and into the ring, sizing up his smaller opponent. The majority of the crowd was evidently in Tengu’s favor, but this was lost on both men who had only victory in mind. DING DING! Moliano left his corner and raised his arm, challenging Tengu forward for an old-fashioned test of strength. Tengu was quick to accept and stepped forward, locking hands with his challenger as the two men began to battle for supremacy. Moliano was visibly giving it his all and managed to take the upper hand over the smaller Tengu in the lock up, before shoving Tengu down hard to the canvas with all his might. “I win! That’s why I’m the best you ugly cretin!” Moliano again raised his arm aloft, this time with an air of triumph at winning the first mini-battle of the match. The crowd did not respond too kindly, causing Moliano to lean over the ropes and exchange insults with a teenager wearing a baseball cap sat in one of the rows near the front. “MOWIANOOO!” Tengu sprung up off the mat and charged towards the distracted Moliano. As Chris turned around he was met by a stiff kick to his right calf, followed rapidly by an even stiffer kick to his left. Moliano yelled out in pain as he hopped away into the corner but Tengu was right behind him and slammed him face first into the top turnbuckle. Tengu fired away with knife edge chops in the corner, followed by a karate elbow strike causing Moliano to grab his jaw in pain as the crowd roared their approval. Tengu backed off for a second before flying back into Moliano with an impressive handspring back elbow smash that took Moliano down. Chris instinctively bounced back up only for Tengu to again fire away with some ferocious stiff kicks working the legs of Moliano who was struggling to protect himself. As a last resort he battled out with a thumb to the eye that brought a quick lecture from referee Paige Allen who was not going to stand for such cowardice. Tengu cleared his vision and hit an impressive roundhouse kick on Moliano who went down to the mat hard. A solid opening to the match from the Hiroshima Hell-Hound, and one that the fans in attendance seemed to appreciate thus far. Tengu grabbed Moliano by the head to pull him back up to his feet, before firing away this time with a variety of chops to the chest. Backhand, openhand, and finally a crushing overhead chop to the head which sent Moliano tumbling again. Moliano was all over the place and Tengu again pulled him back up, this time connecting with an expertly executed Bridging German Suplex for an early pinfall attempt: One… Two… Moliano only just managed to power out using the strength advantage he held over his smaller opponent to full effect. Tengu was immediately back on the offensive and not giving the Personified One any time to recuperate. The beast whipped Moliano into the ropes, as he bounced back off them Tengu rolled through and hit a bicycle kick sending Moliano down. Tengu was perched over Moliano like a bird stalking its prey. Chris was just about able to climb back up unassisted and saw Tengu looking ready to pounce on him at any second. As Tengu did move forward to pounce Moliano was ready for him and hit him hard with a back elbow shot, followed by a kick to the midsection. Tengu doubled over grabbing his rib area as Moliano positioned him for a powerbomb. Powerbomb…REVERSED! Tengu countered the powerbomb attempt and planted Moliano head first with a flip-over DDT to the delight of the crowd. He rolled over into a cover. One… Two… Thr…KICKOUT at two. Barely. Moliano had been unable to get any of his patented ‘Moliano Magic’ going at all, and Tengu was clearly on top and dominating the bout. The Hell-Hound was already thinking of the best way to put this one to bed and score the victory. He wanted to send a statement throughout ACW, that he was a force to be reckoned with. An animal to be feared. Tengu was back on the attack with more harsh and unforgiving kicks, this time focusing on the legs of Moliano and wearing them down. A foe unable to stand is a weak one. A sweeping kick took Moliano’s legs out from under him completely and he hit the mat. Tengu did not hesitate in dropping an elbow on the inside of his left leg, continuing to wear him down from the base. Grabbing Moliano by the hair Tengu helped Moliano up, Moliano’s legs nearly giving out underneath him as he did so. SMACK! Another knife edge chop to the raw chest of Moliano, this one the loudest of the lot. Gutwrench suplex! Moliano was flat out as the gutwrench suplex had its intended effect. Tengu stood up immediately poised and ready to strike, before looking over to the far turnbuckle and making his move towards it. No sooner had the thought entered his twisted mind than was he atop the top rope with the crowd awaiting his next move. Tengu the Bird of Prey took flight majestically. Senton Bomb! Connected! Moliano was unable to move out of the way as the full weight of Tengu’s body came crashing down on him. Opting against going for another cover Tengu instead climbed back to his feet and ran towards the ropes... Asai Moonsault! Connecting again! This time Tengu nonchalantly went for the cover. He did not bother hooking a leg or even putting much pressure on the opponent, in his mind this was over and Moliano had no escape. One... Two... Thr...KICKOUT! Kickout at two! Tengu could not believe that the match was not over and bounced up getting into the face of Paige Allen who was quick to stamp her authority over him. Some members of the crowd seemed surprised by Tengu’s negative reaction, and even more surprised that Moliano had even been able to get a shoulder off the mat after such an impressive aerial assault. Tengu was seething – not many would have predicted that arrogance on his behalf rather than Moliano’s would cost him a potential victory. Failing to press his advantage and go for a more convincing cover had given Christopher Antonio Moliano the merest chance to raise a shoulder and escape, a chance he had just taken. Tengu stared a hole through Paige Allen before grabbing Moliano by his dark hair once more and then whipping him into the corner. Tengu followed him over and worked himself into position and locking in a Boston Crab hanging over the ropes! Moliano screamed in agony and shook his head as Tengu had a Tarantula locked in. Tengu snapped. Quite who this female referee thought she was he did not know. First delivering the slowest pinfall count known to man thus giving Moliano the opportunity to kick out, and now daring to lay her grubby little hands on him in the middle of one of his signature moves. Tengu stepped forward aggressively and got right in Paige Allen’s face, to her credit she did not back down even though faced by the intimidating sight of the Hiroshima Hell-Hound. The crowd started to turn against the Japanese star, not that he gave a damn. Victory was everything. Tengu turned around and this time locked a Boston Crab on Moliano, making use of the earlier damage he had dealt to his legs. Chris began to scream, only this time a small portion of the crowd began to cheer for him, willing him towards the ropes. Tengu’s actions may have turned the crowd against him, but he was still in complete control. “Give up Mowiano! With one strong effort he leapt forward off his arms and grabbed hold of the bottom rope as the crowd cheered. Tengu again focused on the female referee, almost stalking her in the way he does an opponent. Paige Allen was brave, but being faced with the threat of the freakish Hell-Hound was something else. Tengu prodded the downed Moliano with his foot as he contemplated his next move.
