The Scorpion Column

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A column from the ACW site's own superstar-columns section, signed “-VP”, written in the voice of a character as the fed's first site stood in the Wayback capture of 10 July 2003. The site's menu is not reproduced.
So, this bloody Internet column thing sounds like sodding fun. Figured I might as well give it a try. Would be a way of showing my face around these parts without actually showing up for any events, me feels. Pity, that thReat & ACW events are on the same night. But really, I don't see a bleeding difference between the two. Neither organization has any real talent. Nobody whom can even ruffle me feathers, really. ACW was the bigger disappointment. Some cunt named Nature answered my challenge a few weeks back, and I decimated the poor sod. Now, he's out on the streets, selling his arse for a quid. Shameless bastard. Lot of them running around in ACW. That pillock, SilverHAWK, for one. What? Yes, I'm talking about that bastard, SilverHAWK. Acting as if he's the saviour of everyone. Bah, humbug. He's old. Overrated. Past his bloody prime. He's apparently the boss around these parts now, but do I bloody care? Not a sodding bit, thank you very much. I could easily beat SilverHAWK any given day of the week, but it's simply a matter of respecting his wishes to not pass away too early in his life. Speaking of which, this Quinton May and his band of sodding cunts seem to be absolutely lifeless. You twats, I don't appreciate you lot digging up my past. What, you didn't think I'd bloody find out? I'm the bloody Scorpion Of Manchester, I invented backstabbing and all that bollocks. This goes out to the child, especially. 006.392 or Joe Cole or whatever the hell your bleeding name is. I will TEAR YOU APART if you insist to carry on. I'm oh-so-sorry if I had to interrupt your friends at the Legends show, wasting MY time beating them sods up, simply so that I could announce my wonderful return, but it had to be done. I was in the back, pondering over what shite your friends, the janitors, were babbling about. And for the sake of everyone's sanity, I decided to do them a favour and cease the mindless tirade. What do I get in return? Nothing. Oh wait, no. I'm mistaken. I get a bunch of misfits who wish to hunt me down and kick my arse simply because I tred on some toes. You would think that after the beating I handed to you, CHILD, you would learn your lesson. Doesn't seem that way. But you know what? I'm tired of the bloody games. I've got my own business to take care of in thReat, but I will have a surprise for you soon enough, child. Something a little more suited for you. That's all I'll bleeding say about that. Now, with that out of the way, I was asked by an interviewer to give my first impressions on some of the personalities on the ACW roster. Frankly, I feel it's a bloody waste of time. But after some thinking, I realise it would be quite enjoyable. So, here we go. SilverHAWK: Cunt. Osyrus: Sodding cunt, but has talent. A*Dubbs: A bigger twat than Michael Owen. Geo Vacton: No idea who this bugger is, but he's probably a big a twat as Dwight Yorke, me thinks. Scott Perry: Meh, I hear Thierry Henry's queerness fails in comparison to this pillock. Quinton May: Queer. Everybody else in ACW: Bloody pieces of shite. This rant has turned out to be quite enjoyable, actually. And you know what? I'll be back with another column soon. I've got millions of ideas running through me head, and it's only fair that I grace the Internet with my infinite wisdom. Until the next time I log on to the bleeding Internet, cheerio. And oh yes, SilverHAWK... You, sir, are absolutely fucked. Have a good 'un. -VP
