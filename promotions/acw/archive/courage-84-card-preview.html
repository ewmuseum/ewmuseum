COURAGE 84 Indianapolis, Indiana - Conseco Fieldhouse Friday, December 16th, 2005 SINGLES MATCH Alias vs. Coral Avalon Courage 84 will see ACW Legend Alias take on the Kleptomaniac, himself, Coral Avalon, in what should make for a competitive, hard-fought match. Alias has done it all in ACW, and is considered the corner stone of the promotion by many. Alias is a former two-time ACW World Heavyweight Champion, US Champion, and TV Champion, while Coral is currently one-half of the fWo World Tag Team Champions, and a former Action! Bantam Champion. We at TheACW.net asked Play-By-Play announcer Jim Reid for his thoughts on the match. "Alias is going to have to overwhelm Coral, and take him off his game. He has lethal Muay Thai strikes -- some of the best knees and elbows I've seen utilized by a pro wrestler -- and he is going to have to use those to keep Coral from putting together a string of offense. Coral has been in there with some of the best. He's beaten Garvin. He's beaten Zimmerman. He's beaten Malone. And he came with in eyelash of beating OUR World Champ! "Coral's backbreakers and subs are near impossible to weather. You cannot allow for him to get "rolling" and put together that kind of offense. Alias is the bigger man, he is the better striker of the two, and he has the reach. He should work this like an MMA fight. Striker versus wrestler. And that's not to say Alias can't work the catch-as-catch-can style and submit people as well -- he can, and he has! However, Coral is just a little bit better in those areas, and the Original Pulp Hero would be ill advised to try and play Avalon's game. Like I said, Alias just needs to keep his distance, punish Coral with his strikes, then move in close enough to work the clinch and knee the FUCK out of his mid-section. Then, and only then, should Alias attempt to hit some of his more high impact moves." QUASI-GRUDGE MATCH Scott Rojas vs. Calypso Little history lesson: Rojas faced Calypso a little while back, Calypso(then Sars the Clown) won the match by nefarious means. Rojas called up the ACW Offices and asked that this match be booked. Rojas is sick and tired of being on the losing end of things here in ACW, or worse, not being booked at all! -- and is hoping that a win here will put him on the right track as far as ascending the ACW ladder is concerned. Calypso, on the other hand... well, we're not too sure. Can ANYONE predict what is going through that guy's head? Surely we'll see a Gypsy something-or-other, and Calypso will probably say alot of really nasty things, but other than that, it's entirely up in the air! General Show Information The following are also expected to make an appearance and/or compete; the number-one contender for the ACW World Heavyweight Championship, Seymour Almasy; the King of Submission, Max Danger; Scorpio Champion, Lowell Dot Com; the ever psychotic and unpredictable Jimmy Cain; God's Forgotten Son; and hopefully Keller.
