The Mexican crowd loved the Analogue Man, and not just because he was a refreshing respite from the techo-beats of the Night Life. “You’ve got your rematch.” These were the first four words that came out of the words of the Pulp Hero’s mouth, to the surprise and the joy of all of those in attendance tonight. Sheffield certainly wasn’t wasting any time, as he paced the entrance way and stared down the Amazing Gabriel. We weren’t born with that glass ceiling at our feet, as ya mentioned… shit, we had to get past our own setbacks to break through it just the same as everyone else. By being tougher, meaner and simply enough… damned better then anyone around us.” Sheffield stopped pacing and pointed at Tyson XL. “Kinda like how Tyson XL did. From one Scorpion alum to the next, you had a hell of a run, man.” Tyson XL let out the slightest of grins, but all TAG could do was shoot daggers in the direction of the Original Pulp Hero. Sheffield continued, because if Gabriel was going to challenge him to a rematch at Muertos, then he’d pick the stipulation. “I didn’t get where I am by not thinking ahead though, either. Which is why I’ve already got a stipulation in mind… the numbers advantage that you hold dear, square in the nuts.” “Oh yeah?” Gabriel shot back, “And what’s that?” “You ever hear of…” Sheffield titled his head, and grinned… “The Torneo Cibernetico?” The Mexican crowd knew EXACTLY what Sheffield was talking about, and roared in approval. “Seven-on-seven. Line-ups pre-determined. Elimination rules. The promise of one man left standing… which means not only are the Tag Team titles on the line, but shit, since it’s MUERTOS, we may as well wager something as well.” The crowd went buck-WILD at that announcement. Finally, the possibility of the Night Life not being able to use their tremendous numbers advantage they’d exercised since they set foot within ACW. The Amazing Gabriel looked at his group... “YOU’RE ON!” The crowd went even MORE crazy. Hope that you’ve got yourself a team rounded up.” “Oh, you don’t have to worry about that.” Sheffield grinned as he raised his half of the ACW tag team titles over his shoulder. “Now, seeing as at this Pay-Per-View, Dios Des Los Muertos… we’ve both got to put something on the line of ours. You, Amazing Gabriel, have been running around here in a cloudy haze, thinking that you and your group of raver nutjobs can reign supreme. Well, think again. The winner of the Cibernetico… the winner gets YOUR leadership of the Night Life to do what they see fit with it.” And with that announcement, TAG was stopped in his tracks. He looked at his group, who all had worried looks on their faces as he shook his head in disbelief. Finally, TAG turned around to face an eager Sheffield, waiting for a reply from The Amazing One. “You got a deal…” He raised another finger. “I know that my Night Life will help their fearless leader win, so I don’t worry about any of them. BUT… in putting up the TOP SPOT in the world’s GREATEST PARTY… I want something from you, Sheffield.” TAG continued, grinning at the Pulp Hero. “And what’s that?” Serious as a heart attack, The Amazing Gabriel clinched the top rope, staring down the Pulp Hero. “I want the World Championship.” Sheffield opened his mouth for a moment, but didn’t say anything, he was confused. “You do realize that I’m the TAG TEAM Champion, and not the World Champion as well, after beating the cabal last week? You aren’t that high, are you?” “NO! I want a shot at the World title, and not just any old shot… I want one of yours. No one has won that prize piece of gold more then you, in ACW. And yet, you haven’t had more then a few matches for the World title since you lost in at Legends IV last year. I wonder why that is, Sheff? You afraid you’ll never win it again?” Gabriel chuckled for a moment, before resting his hand on Fever Pitch’s shoulder. “See, I’m not stupid, Sheff. You’ve got a title shot sitting back there somewhere. A rematch. Lemme take your worries away like your own personal puff the magic dragon. Get yourself a World title shot, so that it can belong to me.” Sheffield could only growl back at the Amazing Gabriel. “See what I can do.” “Then it’s settled…” TAG fumed. “The winner of the Cibernetico – ME, might I add – will get the ACW Tag Team titles for their side, my Night Life, and what will be MY title shot. You better get that title shot, Sheff, or deal's off.” “Count on it, fuckhead.” “Rumble” by Link Wray played over the speakers as Sheffield grimaced, staring down the members of the Night Life for several moments before he disappeared behind the curtains, leaving the Night Life alone in the ring. Inside, Tyson XL had his fists taped up as he prepared for his pending match-up with God’s Forgotten Son. He remained inside the ring as Aleczander, Fever Pitch, Zip Zap, Kaus, and Elixr exited. TAG stayed behind and mouthed a final parting shot to his enforcer. “Remember what I told you… if you lose, you’re GONE.” Tyson only nodded as he shook his head. TAG walked up the ramp as he and his posse decided to leave from sight while a referee walked past them, heading to the ring for COURAJE’s opening match. After these commercials, Tyson XL vs. God’s Forgotten Son! Stay tuned! Give Me Patience, Or Give Me Denton When you’ve been locked out of a place you’ve called home, and been forced to follow behind through the whiles a Mexico, a location that had cost the ACW crew a few cew members, several wrestlers and a good amount of stolen equipment already, it takes something out of you. And yet, Rud Albion didn’t actually look all that worse for wear. Save the red that still clung around his eyes from the police pepper spray last week, Albion held certain strength and resolve that underlined a very dangerous presence that had boiled over within the newly coined English Axe Murderer. Rex Silver worried about THAT, more then the head of ACW’s security worried about the state that Albion had found himself in, in those early hours after End Game. In the Courage’s since, what he had become in the journey to tear down Dean Denton, well if you were paying attention… it had taken Denton off his guard… So you can only imagine what it had done to the Guardsman of ACW, someone who had always been friendly with the Kingdom’s Keeper… the man who was now turning into the Kingdom’s Killer. Albion took a gym bag from Rex Silver. In the chaos that had embroiled when Albion had been told about the restraining order put out against him, by Denton, he had forgotten his gym bag. The former Tag Team Champion lifted it ever so slightly to check the weight. “Thanks Rex. Is this everything?” “Yeah, it’s everything.” Silver replied, his thumbs resting at the top of his black slacks, “Look I’m sorry that security wasn’t able to help you last week, I mean the amount of fires we have to put out on any given show…” “Forget it, just getting…” the growl in Rud’s voice paused for a moment, as he looked in the bag, then up at Silver, “… my things, is enough.” Silver nodded, and then put held his hands out by his side, he wasn’t going to go without trying to help out a man who he had respected for keeping order up until now. I could help you handle things, put in a good work for you if you need it.” Albion put up a hand to stop the big security head, and forced a grin in return. “I’ll keep that in mind. You know, I really do appreciate this.” Albion finished as he held up the bag. The amount of bottles I’ve taken to the back of the head protecting that jackass from an over zealous fan or two… but I just wanted to make sure you know what you’re doin’.” As he responded Rud Albion didn’t look away from Rex Silver, he didn’t even blink. “If I knew myself anymore, I’d be in there… and no out here. The ACW Champion continued his assault by grabbing the leg and twisting it around into a Standing Leglock. “Hh.” Z didn’t count on meeting this much resistance from DIM as he tried to fight his way up, so he shrugged his shoulders and just cranked back on the hold even further. DIM wasn’t about to go down fighting. He could make history by scoring one of the biggest upsets of all time and win the ACW World Heavyweight Championship. He took the other leg and threw it up, connecting with a couple solid blows to Z’s head. Rather than let go, Z returned fire by stomping at the leg a couple more times, then let go to simply deliver a couple blows to the head of Dude In Mask. Resuming his leg attack, he pulled him back to his feet and clobbered him with some more fists, but DIM returned fire with a couple stiff blows of his own, making him dizzy. DIM was back to a vertical base again, whipping Z into the corner. He hobbled, but still went for an attack. Z, however, threw a hard European Uppercut into the chin of DIM, sending him back a step. Having the advantage on his side again, Z ran forward… FIREMAN’S CARRY FLAPJACK! DIM held onto his knee in pain, but bought himself a little time as he turned back to Z, who was grimacing in pain. DIM cracked him in the side of the head with a kick, then caught him with a couple of Forearm Smashes, then finally ended the combination of moves with a Step-over Spinning Heel Kick. While Z was down, DIM slowly got back to his feet and beat on his chest as he looked out to the sea of fans to get them riled up. “SAAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY WHHHHHHHHHHHHHAAAAAATTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!” And the fans cheers as DIM turned his attention back to the Dastardly Mastermind. With Z left in a complete fog, DIM hobbled to the second rope and flew off… SWEET-ASS DROPKICK! Nailed right in the temple, DIM connected with the kick. He grabbed onto his leg in pain, unable to follow up immediately, but then shut out the pain and went for a lateral press. ONE! TWO! TH… KICK OUT! Z had kicked out again, but this time, was showing a little bit of fatigue. Dude In Mask started to clap repeatedly, getting the fans into the match as he cold-cocked Z with an elbow to the head. He climbed to the top rope and waited for Z to stumble back, flying with a Diving Cross Body. One of the best Diving Cross Body moves ever seen. The grace. The agility. The form. …and missed. Completely. Z ducked out of the way, leaving DIM to crash badly on the canvas, holding onto the leg again. Z slashed a thumb across his throat as he ran at DIM and caught him with a Running Knee Lift underneath the chin, knocking him nearly out cold. And with it, he fell right into a cover, making sure to grab onto the bad leg. ONE! TWO! SHOULDER UP! DIM appeared to be just a little smarter than the average bear, using a shoulder rather than the leg to kick out. Z had to applaud that a little bit, then suddenly remembered this masked asshole who was NOT Z was trying to take his title. So he waited for DIM to get back up and ran to the ropes. He had a wicked Lariat in mind, possibly, but DIM ducked and grabbed him from behind with an Inverted Facelock… SOMETHING ELSE CLEVER THAT HAS THE WORD “DUDE!” Connecting with the Spinning Headlock Elbow Drop perfectly, he fell right into a cover and grabbed a far leg. ONE! TWO! NO! Z kicked out again as DIM was in disbelief, thinking he had the match won right there. DIM wasn’t deterred in his quest to win the ACW Championship, though, and tried for another whip, but Z said “fuck that noise” to that and sent DIM flying into the corner. DIM got a back elbow and caught him in the face. DIM tried leaping to the second turnbuckle again, but Z grabbed onto the leg and pulled him back, sending him crashing onto the ground again! Z wasted no time scooping him up and DRIVING him into the canvas violently with a Fisherman’s Swinging Neckbreaker! This was going to be it! ONE! TWO! THR… NO, NO, NO! That should’ve been the finish, but it wasn’t. DIM was putting up way more of a fight than Z initially thought. DIM was on the ropes, though, bleary as all get-out. An irate Z was all “fuck this shit” and decided to put this way the best way he knew how. HIT. Z’S. NOPE! DIM shoved him off and back into the ropes. Z came flying back as DIM doubled him over with a kick to the chest, looking for his own finisher. DUDE, THAT HURT A….NO! The Single Underhook DDT didn’t get it, either as Z shoved him off into the ropes and as he returned, caught him right in the leg with a Dropkick! DIM tumbled over and held his leg in pain as Z grimaced. He had enough of this. He waited for the limping DIM to make it back to his feet… he wasted no motion. A couple Forearm Smashes to the face, then a kick to the bad knee for good measure again… THE DYING BREATH! The very same move of the former 21w/fWo Immortals Champion Kellen Kinkade… the Dying Breath. The VERY same move that he used to defeat Keith Scott Zimmerman a couple weeks ago on COURAJE 132. And with that, Z hooked the leg and pulled back into a cover. ONE! TWO! THREE! And that was that. Another successful defense for the Dastardly Mastermind. He had one game plan in the masked “Z” underneath the ring, but when he kept up the attacks on the leg, eventually it paid dividends and gave Z the victory. “THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND STILL ACW CHAMPION… Z!” While Do the Evolution played over the arena’s speakers loudly, Z raised the ACW World Heavyweight Championship high overhead. He’d been given much more resistance than he thought he would against the newcomer, but he had won the match and kept the title, much to the EXTREME hatred of the crowd. Sliding underneath the ring, came the masked “Z” right on the assault, picking the bones of the downed DIM like a vulture. “All yours, scamp,” Z chuckled underneath his breath as he walked up the ramp. Meanwhile, now back into the ring, the fans jeered and hissed at the masked “Z” as he fumed and boiled with rage, waiting for the masked DIM to get back to his feet. “YOU STUPID…. COCKSUCKER~!” he screamed as he threw off his own mask to reveal none other than fellow Cabalite, Iceman. He grabbed DIM by his mask and tights, then SHOT him violently into the turnbuckle, shoulder-first. DIM slumped over as a jeering, hate-filled crowd let Iceman know what they thought of him. “NO, YOU IDIOTS! YOU’RE DOING IT ALL WRONG! I’M THE FUCKING HERO! ICE-MAN! ICE-MAN! ICE-MAN! ICE-MAN!” Being set off by the roaring crowd, he picked up DIM again and tossed him right through the ropes and out to the floor. Having utterly SNAPPED, the man known as the Snap Case rammed a never-ending barrage of fists into the head of DIM as he yelled at him. “YOU WANT TO SHOW ME UP?! YOU THINK YOU’RE HOT SHIT, YOU PIECE OF MASKED SHIT?!” Admist the jeers, the hate, and the disdain being showed for him, he continued to kick and punch away at DIM, who’d been winded and picked apart by Z. He was simply finishing the job and this was all because of taking a loss last week and being defeated by the newcomer, DIM. As Renaud Cardinal and Tony Millar both protested these heinous actions by the loose cannon in Iceman, he continued to ram DIM’s head into the ring steps several times before turning his attention over to the American announce table. “YOU FUCKS! GET THE FUCK OUT OF THE WAY OR YOUR ASSES ARE GRASS!” The Italian and Pakistani announcers were at least happy it wasn’t their table, but Millar and Renaud got out of the line of fire as Iceman picked up the groggy DIM and snarled as he pursed his lips together. “YOU’RE GONNA PAY! YOU’RE GONNA PAY AND YOU’RE GONNA FUCKING PAY HARD, COCKSUCKER!” Renaud and Tony Millar got the hell out of there as Iceman launched DIM like a missile, THROWING him up and over the announce table, landing with a dull thud against the barricade and crashed to the floor. Rubbing his hands together as if he’d taken out the trash, Iceman smirked with an evil grin as he walked back up the ramp, happy with the damage he’d caused. Trainers rushed out to ringside to check up on DIM, who had no idea what the hell hit him. Meanwhile, we were left with the parting shot of Z and Iceman, both men standing at the top of the ramp. Iceman licking his chops, satisfied with the complete Pearl Harbor job he’d commited on this night. And the more important picture? Z. STILL holding onto the ACW World Heavyweight Championship for at least one week longer. FIN. WINNER: Z via pinfall (still ACW World Heavweight Champion)
