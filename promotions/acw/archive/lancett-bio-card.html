Lancett The Megastar, Kid Frost, Rook' Real Name: Joshua Gary Birthdate: April 6, 1983 Hometown: Peoria, Illinois Height: 6'1'' Weight: 249 lbs Entrance Music: Static-X's ''The Only'' Medical Information Natural Hair Color: Brown Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: O Body Fat Percentile: 7.03% Favored Workout: Jumprope. Past Illnesses: I'm perfect. Past Injuries: I'm unbreakable. In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Lancett, trained by “Superstar” Vince Jacobs who is a world class all around athlete and wrestler, uses some of the moves he was taught from his trainer but not as much as he use to. Lancett has developed his own style over the days he has wrestled. He normally targets the head/neck/upper back and legs to weaken them for his two finishers. He’d weaken head/neck/upper back with jabs, submissions, head/neck bumps, and kicks to the face/neck/upper body/spine tingling kicks to the spine. He’d target the legs with submissions, kicks, chop blocks, and dragon screws. His history in hWo gave him a lot of hardcore experience, and he will not hesitate to put a chair up your ass. Trainer: “Superstar” Vince Jacobs Status: Heel Set-Up Move(s): Lancett Lock The victim is on their back, Lancett grabs the one of the victim's legs and twists the victim's ankle. Lancett goes to the mat and applies a scissor lock to the leg. Six Star Struck Lancett executes a standing head scissors and a hooks both the victim's arms. Lancett jumps up and drives the victim's face into the mat. Legacy From The Heavens Lancett is on the tope rope as the victim is lying down in the ring. Lancett does a back flip landing belly first on the victim and pinning if the shoulders are touching the mat. Best Damn Piledriver In the Business Lancett lifts the victim up as if for a bodyslam. Lancett puts the victim's head down between Lancett's legs so the victim is up side down and belly to belly with the vicitm. Lancett can drop down to their knees or sit down to the mat. Legacy Ender Lancett applies a front face lock on the victim. Lancett falls straight down or backwards, driving the victim's head into the mat. In Your Face When the victim is sitting with his head against the second turnbuckle in the corner. Lancett goes a running start from a connecting turnbuckle and lays a lethal dropkick to the face of the victim. Lancett normally has to land his feet on the apron his body through the ropes. Sometimes Lancett will replace the dropkick with a knee to the face. Finishing Move: Lancett-ation Lancett stands behind the victim facing towards them. Lancett hooks both the victim's arms and places their head inbetween the victim's shoulderblades. Lancett then turns around in a motion so they are now facing the opposite direction and still holds onto the victim's arms, as a result the victim is now in a position similar to that Lancett was just in, their arms hooked and their head bent in the middle of Lancett’s shoulder blades. Lancett then drops down driving the victim's head into the mat. It can be noted that the turn around is very similar to the set up to a face to face backslide. The move can also be set up with the victim bent down attacker facing away from them and hooking both Lancett's arms behind their back and dropping down. Born to Reign The victim is on their back, Lancett grabs both the victim's legs and locks them into a figure four with their arms, placeing the victim's straight leg into their arm pit. Lancett steps over the victim rolling them on their stomach as if for a boston crab and sits back. Inverted Lancett-ation The victim is behind Lancett. Lancett places the victim's head between their legs and hooks both their arms. Lancett turns around so the victim is now in front of Lancett facing the same direction, at the same time, the victim ducks down and sticks their head between the victim's legs and stands up while still having the arms hooked. The victim is lifted up so they are now upside down, Lancett suspends the victim by keeping their arms trapped. Lancett holds the victim's arms and grabs the victim's legs. Lancett sits down, dropping the victim on their head/back of the neck. Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: ACW Awards: Other Federations: hWo and Carnage Other Federations Awards: hWo's Rising Star Championship Career History With all the shit, gilded times, and drama Lancett had to deal with day in and day out he has probably matured the most in the last couple of years. He has meet people in the back and he has hated them from the start. He has meet people outside of wrestling and hated them too. This loner persona has become custom for the days at hand. He’d tour with the federations and then go home. He joined hWo and followed “Superstar” Vince Jacobs, the man who trained him. He followed his footsteps, and he followed his handprints. He acted as if SVJ was a resurrected Christ that walked this earth and Lancett was his disciple. Fuck that! Lancett took matters in his own hands. He turned on SVJ and he became a cheered man. People praised the ground where he walked. It amazed him how stupid people could be. He played along, and still is playing along in the game called hWo. Everyone saw it coming: Frost and Lancett where at each other’s throat as much as Trevor was after Jade’s panties. It all ended that Frost and Lancett where going to go one on one retirement match. Lancett lost due to Trevor Echo getting involved, which was a shocker. Once he retired from hWo one federation picked him up with their revival: ACW: All-Star Championship Wrestling. Now, he is in ACW with new fans, new people to praise him, new people to hate him, a new owner to kiss ass to, or maybe not . . . Lancett has taken ACW more lightly than hWo. In hWo he debuted and won a championship belt. ACW he debuted and won, but just a match against Phil Atken. From then on the best moments where probably him getting a TV Title shot, controlling a show, and Main Eventing against Alias, and to Lancett he never lost against Alias. Much more is to come in the legacy of Lancett. Now his destiny brings him to EdW... (more to come as time goes on) Disrespect? Dishonor? Distrust? All of the above.
