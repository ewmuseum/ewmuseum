Reid: "He's rolled Brandon onto the table...I think he's up to something..." Coral rolled back into the ring and far to the opposite side of where Brandon lay prone. Then he charged. Lipton: "HE'S JUMPED TO THE TOP ROPE!" Reid: "SPRINGBOARD FROG SPL-OH!" It would've been a springboard frog splash... ...if Brandon hadn't said "Fuck that." and moved out of the way. Instead Coral frog splashed himself through a table. HOLY SHIT HOLY SHIT HOLY SHIT Brandon went for the cover- table rubble and all. 1. 2. 3. NO. KICKOUT. Lipton: "I can't believe it!" Reid: "Habeeb it! Coral is UP. What heart!" Brandon was the only person not cheering. Instead he just rolled Coral back into the ring with a pissed off look on his face. Youngblood followed and wasted no time in pulling Coral to his feet and drilling him with belly-to-belly suplex. Brandon pulled him again and then threw him head first threw the ropes into the post behind the turnbuckle. Lipton: "Youngblood is definitely not giving Coral another inch. No room for counters or hell, even breathing." Reid: "So it seems." The guy was systematic. He pulled Coral out from the turnbuckle right into a Reverse DVD-position on top of his shoulders. Brandon spun around, walked to the center of the ring. BURNING HAMMER. Brandon didn't go for the pin and instead followed that up by throwing him headfirst into the turnbuckle post on the opposite end of the ring. This time Brandon left his body just hanging there with his head up against the side of the post. Then Youngblood went to the outside where Coral's undefended upper torso hung over the second rope/turnbuckle. Brandon took out the time to lay into his opponent some more with some fists, pulled his head under to the lowest turnbuckle- and then made sure that Coral's head would line up with the metal post again. Reid: "Oh christ, what now?" Brandon leapt over the barrier into the crowd, telling security to clear them out of his way. Youngblood soaked in the crowd reaction for a second. These people loved every minute of this and Youngblood was going to give them a show. Lipton: "He's taken off---" From a full-speed sprint, Brandon leapt from the barrier with a flying yakuza kick!!! And if Coral's head had been there that would've been the end of the match. But Avalon moved at the last second leaving Brandon to drop to the floor with a pathetic thud. Reid: "Oh man! I can't believe he *missed*." Lipton: "Coral just lucked out and saved himself from defeat." Coral rolled to the outside and pulled Brandon to his feet- lifting him into a body slam but holding him horizontally with his back exposed. The Coral began to run Youngblood's spine into the post with a ruthlessness rarely seen in him. Again and Again and Again. And then? Fall away slam fake out into BACKBREAKER! Avalon was ready to wrap this up and immediately rolled Brandon into the ring. He made a b-line for the barbwire bat and set it down on the canvas. Then he pulled Brandon to his feet and dragged over where his doom awaited. Reid: "Coral's sitting Brandon up here......OH MY GOD!" Lipton: "Damn! Flatliner onto the BARBWIRE BAT." Reid: "Coral's back up and the crowd is on their feet....he's signaling to the top turnbuckle." But before Coral made his way up to the top rope- he snatched up that old steel chair. Lipton: "He's going to end this with a frog splash!" Reid: "A frog splash with a chair, Greg! You don't get up from that. This match is over!!" Coral was poised at the top rope- chair in hand- ready to land a devastating weapon-assisted five star frog splash. SCHMAK!! And now Coral goes flying off the top rope to the floor. Hilarious. Lipton: "What the hell was that!? Where'd that steel chair come from?" Reid: "It came from the Apron! Jesus that was loud! Max Danger chuckled and then jumped down only to roll Coral back into the ring. Lipton: "I wouldn't be surprised if Max just K.O'd Coral. Avalon didn't see that at all." Now both men were on the ground. After sufficiently booing the hell of Danger, the crowd began to stomp and clap, pleading for any one of them to get up and finish this match. Ref: "5...6....7...8....9" CORAL WAS UP! Coral was up...swinging at the air. Now he's stumbling all over the place. Reid: "The kid is out of it." Lipton: "I don't even think Coral knows where he is." Reid: "Thanks alot, Max Danger!" Youngblood got to one knee trying to shake the cobwebs out of his head... ...Then Coral came punch-drunkenly barreling right at him. Youngblood weaved out of the way of a loose armed punch and then locked in: The Gridlock. Then he fell to the ground applying the leg scissors... And Coral quickly tapped, fearing to lose anymore oxygen to his brain than he already has. Reid: "Coral tapped!!! Coral tapped!!!" Lipton: "Only because of Max Danger!" Reid: "It was back and forth between these two, who knows what would've happened had Max not gotten involved. But one this is for sure, Brandon Youngblood deserves this lucky victory here tonight and the fans just love it! What a way to kick off our Canadian Tour!" Winner > Youngblood via submission; new Scorpion Fighting Champion It's a trap! Youngblood stood there burning with pride under the white lights, being carried by the cheers of the crowd. Brandon fought hard, fought fairly and they did not hate him for beating Coral Avalon. Danger was to blame for Coral's loss. Not Brandon. And though, it wasn't exactly a clean victory- Brandon was happy to take it. He had come so far in a short time tha he had to put it in perspective. The new Scorpion Champion kissed belt before turning to look to his moon and stars; a woman who deserved to bask in the celebration every much as he did. "Alissa!" Brandon yelled, waving her in. She was smiling, clapping and blowing kisses to him- motioning for him to stay in the ring by himself. This was his moment. Youngblood then jumped to the turnbuckle nearest to Alissa and held the title to the sky to more cheers. Alissa in turn, snapped photos- the flashes going off with the rest. It was amazing to him to have captured ACW gold in such a short amount of time. And to have this wonderful woman stick by him through one of the worst times in his life. This moment alone validated all of it. Brandon winked at Alissa and walked over to the other side of the ring to another turnbuckle. Jumping on it he pointed at the Scorpion title then back at himself. The crowd's approval- still thick- seemed to die down a bit. The cheers were made empty as all the eyes in the front row went immediately to the center of the ring. Brandon didn't understand. 'What's going on?' Alissa, whose face was now down turned in hideous anger, pulled something from under the ring. A steel chair. The audience gasped and tried to warn Youngblood, but the girl was quick and by the time Brandon even thought to turn around... Smack. The sting of betrayal. He knew it was her. Before he could grab his back in pain or turn around and seek an explanation another chair shot lit up his spine with a sickening slap. Smack. Then another; and Youngblood- title and all fell into a heap on the canvas. Brandon shielded his face from another shot with the chair, but was opened back up when Alissa jabbed the edge deeply into his gut. It was then- with his head exposed that she cracked him over the face. Flashes of light were no longer pictures of victory and glory but instead portraits of pain and agony. Every chair shot was a complete and utter maiming of his emotional and physical self. It was an attack from which there could be no defense. The announcers, along with everyone else in attendence were horrified as Alissa rubbed her lipstick off on her wrist and spat red processed whale fat onto Brandon's injured body. Brandon rolled over onto his stomach and tried desperately to get away, but the spike from a high heel met his shoulder blade, punctured his skin and caused him to scream out in terror. It was then that she look them off and- using the ropes as support- began to repeatedly kick Brandon in the face. A violent repitition of kicks and chair shots lit the newly crowned champion's face on fire- blood now cooling on his upper lip. She then pulled off her sweater- exposing a bra that held no breasts. Instead there were plastic implants that he kept affixed to his chest with flesh colored putty. The literal 'breast plate' dropped to the mat like a flimsy- rubbery piece of meat. In his fury he had apparently bitten his lip, red and white foam leaked out of the corner of his mouth as he mussed up his hair. The announcers and the crowd were on their feet- they couldn't believe it. This beautiful, modelesque woman transformed into skinny and taut man right before their very eyes. "What. the. fuck........." was all Lipton could stutter. Reid just wanted to know who this was. The face seemed familiar as he got out of the ring and walked over to the time keep- punching him in the face and taking the ring bell. And just as fast as he rolled out- he rolled back in where Brandon was dumbstruck and punch-drunk. On one knee one second and stumbling around the next. Brandon just got one eye opening glimpse and he knew who it was immediately. "You-??" DING. Back down to the mat. The assailant threw the bell down and traded up for 200-some odd pounds of wrestling machinery. Obviously straining and short of breath...he tied Youngblood's arms into the ropes...which left Brandon with an exposed front. The strange cross dressing man, picked up the already dented steel chair and without drama or warning he drove it into the champion's face with enough force to shatter a skull. "That's it! Get someone out there! This is ridiculous!!" Ried screamed. Everyone could hear him except Youngblood who was now out cold. Smack Oooohhhhh!! Smack Oooooooohhhhhhhh!! The attack continued on as brutal as it was unprovoked. Viscous streams of crimson cascaded down the victim's face as his eyes remained deathly shut. Whoever this was- he was out to destroy Youngblood right in this very ring. In a deceptive act of mercy, the attacker detangled Youngblood's arms out of the ropes and actually caught him before he fell. The man strained heavily, gritted and nearly screamed at he lifted Brandon from a front suplex and hung him out "to dry" on the top rope. By this time the awe had warn off and the everyone regained the present of mind long enough to jeer. This didn't seem to bother the man as he picked up the chair and took a few steps back into the center of the ring. He patted his weapon against the canvas- once, twice, three times- then his face lit up with a bloodly and sociopathic little grin. With a running charge, he dropped the edge of the chair under...then swept upwards in a golf swing arc... ...the top portion meeting Brandon's face with such a force that he flipped completely backwards out of the ring! If this was an actual match that would've called for a "Holy Shit" chant. But this was nothing more than a brazen massacre made more chaotic by it's perplexing nature. Brandon's valet....was a man? He then picked him up and outstretched his arms in a double underhook. Ried: "Izzat....is that...Garvin!?!?" Then the attacker let out a sudden shrill and girlish scream. Followed by insane laughter. Then twisted both of their bodies and drove Youngblood's skull into the unforgiving tilefloor. "Nope." Lipton said shaking his head like a man who took a gamble and lost.
