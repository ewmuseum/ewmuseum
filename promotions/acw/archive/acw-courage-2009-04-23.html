ARC II - The Road to Legends III 23rd April 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice introduction The so the train keeps a rolling but with a tunnel in close site one thing is still on everyones mind. Will ACW come through the other side? Forked Tongue, Hand of Vengeance "Ph'nglui mglw'nafh Cthulhu R'lyeh wgah'nagl fhtagn!" It wasn't a phrase that made any sense in the English language, or any other human tongue for that matter. But the alien phrase was a warcry of sorts for the deranged head of the Sect of Black Wisdom, and to hear it in the ring was to know suffering was in your near future. But for now, other business needed tending. His own brand of welcome to the arriving fans of ACW. "Come forth, children of the forbidden realms, and know of your origins and destiny! Embrace the ways of the Old Ones and you shall find solace that no false savior can ever offer!" Bruce Shanahan, wielding a horned staff not unlike the mysterious druids of old myth, stood amidst the people that he sought to bring to his side, the myriad and multicultural fans of ACW, roving through throngs of tailgaters across the parking lot as a fiery evangelist of the blasphemous and demonic. Many had already chosen to follow him in his time, their insecurities manipulated and allegiances swayed to his cause; those who defied his gospel shirked away from causing him harm now, as his many disciples tended his flanks as makeshift security. They carried monstrous effigies of things that would bring horror to any man of sane mind, and spoke in harsh strings of monosyllables to the beer-drinking crowds that sought to shout them down, drawing their attention away from the action on the big screen and what was happening inside the arena. Most knew it was madness, the hollow diatribe of a maniac, and to give it credence was to invite despair and destruction. But many listened to the mad prophet, and of that many, some found consolation and empathy in his venomous promises. With every night that the curtains parted in Montreal for All-Star Championship Wrestling, more sheep joined the flock of the damned and the wicked. Regardless of the legion of bitter enemies and boisterous detractors that strove to run them out forever, the Pariah Saint's cultists were nearly overwhelmed with requests for guidance and the faction's penned propaganda, all to the ceaseless call of the high priest as he sounded his promises for all to hear. The lost and the misguided found a voice in the cult, and an alarming majority found strength in its ways. It was another fine day for the Sect. Though their ACW endeavors had yet to be as successful as they'd have liked, they had never been at a loss to replace the few losses the Sect had suffered in manpower, and for every small collective that defected, droves waited to take their place amongst the faithful and learn of their masters lurking in the dark corners of the cosmos. "I have seen what lies at the heart of the great kingdom beyond, my friends, and it is redemption the likes of which the Vatican can only imagine! And to partake of its nectar you need not give your souls to a false Christ or follow the teachings of any eastern oracles! Follow me, and join the ranks of the Sect of Black Wisdom, for we will prepare you for the spoils of eternity! He paused as a colossal shape plowed its way through the crowd, breathless, his dark skin pallid by comparison. Something wavered in Darius Daniel's clutches, and it immediately caught the eye of Shanahan. "Darius..." Shanahan paused, pursing his lips. "You bring news, and it is grave." The towering subordinate nodded, pushing ropy dreadlocks out of his face and offering a better view of his shellshocked expression. "You perceive well, Father Shanahan." "It's not difficult to piece together. You look like Hell just opened and swallowed your firstborn. What's so urgent, brother?" Darius held up a necklace, his giant hand quivering as he gripped the cord in his fist. A primal carving of dark stone, it was a circular icon printed with the head of Great Cthulhu himself rising above drowned R'lyeh. To most it was simply a sinister symbol, but to the studied it was an unholy image known as the Seal of R'lyeh. "His medallion? Brother Tully left his medallion unattended?" The man called Violence Jack grunted as he ripped the emblem from Daniel's grasp, gazing on it with a fervent stare that could burn through steel. "What treachery is this, Darius?!" The larger man seemed hesitant to speak further, glancing about nervously at the crowd that had gathered to watch the exchange, and Shanahan's vehemence only swelled as he held the item aloft. "For one to leave their Seal unattended for any period of time is to desecrate the names of the Old Ones, Brother Daniel. Why do you bear to me Brother Tully's symbol of the pact?!" "Father.....it gives me pain, but....Horace Tully is a brother to us no more. He has denounced the Old Ones, and condemned the Sect of Black Wisdom." One would expect a loose cannon like Shanahan to snap at such a revelation and erupt into a violent tirade. But perhaps none in the wrestling industry are like the psychopathic mastermind, and his reaction was a simple sigh. Some of the followers, however, visibly began to give their high priest space, fearing the repercussions of being in his path if his mental stability were compromised in that moment. "I see...." Darius shrugged off one of the disciples that had laid an arm on his shoulder. "He attacked our attendants in the arena and stormed out, voicing his discontent as he left. I believe.....I believe it's has been our recent failures to rise dominant atop ACW. He's grown restless, and with multiple attempts to gain power foiled in one evening, I fear it pushed him over the edge. His competitive spirit couldn't cope with playing second fiddle to Knox and his entourage, and watching weak men like Almasy and Sharp battle for the title seemed to take a toll on him." "A pity. Truly a tragic defection and ones that pains me deeply. But even a man of Tully's promise isn't free from the punishment that goes with deserting the gods who have shaped his mortal flesh and empowered him. They are vengeful gods, and on this physical plane, we are their right arm of vengeance." A composed smile passed across Shanahan's face as he handed his staff to one of the disciples at his side. "Assemble some of our more....'strongarm' members. When we get our hands on our traitorous brethren, he'll be praying for the wrath brought upon Gomorrha to save him from ours." "As you wish.... The crowd parted, and like Moses through the Red Sea, Darius Daniel passed untouched through the waves of murmuring fans and stormed back into the ACW Arena to gather the Sect's faithful. Violence Jack's eyes fell to the seal as the giant disappeared from view, and he shook his head, forlorn with loss. The man he had chosen as his successor had turned from the gospel, and even for a man as dark and vile in nature as Boston's Bastard Son, it brought sadness. "Tully....what have you done?......" Introducing... The sound of “Cocky” by Kid Rock hit the arena and the crowd looked in unison to see who would emerge from the back. Chris Moliano. Moliano was a virtual unknown in the wrestling industry with only a failed stint in the now defunct 21Wrestling and a losing effort in fWo Survivor 4 to his name. He hadn’t even been seen anywhere of note in the years since then, not that you’d know it as he walked to the ring with all the distinctive swagger of a returning champion. He seemed in the relative lukewarm reception as if it were the hailing of the fed's hero. Chris slid under the bottom rope and applauded the crowd mouthing “thank you” for a rapturous ovation that existed only in his own mind. The man formerly dubbed “Potential Personified” called for and received a microphone as his music faded out. “Now correct me if I’m wrong...” Moliano paused for a second to flash an enormous smile highlighted by teeth as white as fresh fallen snow. The smile popped even more perfectly against his well-tanned skin. “...but did you people pay to see a wrestling show tonight?” A slight cheer rose from the crowd who were still unsure what to make of the brash individual in the centre of the ring. “Well you no longer have need to panic or fear – because a man dripping with talent is finally here”. This time the crowd did not respond quite so well to his arrogant tone and a few boos were clearly audible in the arena. Moliano, oblivious to any negative reaction posed and used his free hand to direct the crowds gaze towards his incredibly well defined abs. “My name is Christopher Antonio Moliano, and I stand before you as a champion in waiting. I have all the tools to succeed and become unquestionably the greatest wrestler ever to grace a wrestling ring with his presence. Lord knows I have already succeeded in being undeniably crowned as the best looking”. Now the vast majority of the crowd took exception to his cockiness and made their disapproval heard. “Hey guys don’t be jealous, if you’re unsure whether or not that last statement was true – ask your girlfriends, just as soon as they’ve stopped staring at me”. Moliano smiled, and this time embraced the boos from the crowd. “I do however understand that proving my initial statement of greatness to be true, I will have to demonstrate my vast skill set inside this very ring. So you lucky people are about to witness the very first edition of Chris’ Wrestling Clinic. In this premiere I am prepared to issue an open challenge to anybody in the back with the ‘courage’...” Moliano winked to the crowd, evidently pleased to have worked the name of the show into his interview. “...to go hold for hold with Perfection Personified, and be the first to fall victim to some first class patented Moliano magic”. With that Chris threw the microphone outside the ring and began to limber up in preparation for his debut bout, when some familiar music hit to the sheer delight of all in the ACW arena. All except Chris Moliano. "Sympathy for the Devil" by the Rolling Stones Alias - The Original Pulp Hero himself appeared to be answering the challenge. Moliano swallowed hard barely able to believe what was happening as Alias stepped out from the back, microphone in hand. The music faded as Alias stood atop the rampway and began to speak. “Hello again Chris”. This was not the first time these two had met. Alias and Moliano had squared off in 21w, twice, and it seemed as though Alias remembered. From the terrified look on the previously beaming face of Moliano, he remembered too. “So the last thing I remember you saying to me, Chris, was... I quit. Certainly you had all the reason too, because I had you bent backwards across a ring-post with a bow and arrow submission. A baseball bat across your neck. Really you had all the best reasons to say the words. So it's real nice to see you try this Perfection Personified shtick on again, and challenge the best in ACW... six years later." Moliano’s expression changed between one of fear and rage with each breath he took. “You see Chris unfortunately for you I remember you a little too well. I can vividly recall how not only did you lose to me back in 21w, but you lost to every single guy you ever faced." Moliano shook his head in denial and attempted to respond. He had walked right back into possible notoriety again and met a ghost of his past, in the heart and soul of ACW. “That was a long time ago, and a lot of things have chan...” “Hush” Alias interrupted. “You challenge anyone out in the back, that means you challenge me. I'm going to tear you down too... if you want to step in the House of All-Stars, of Champions... of Wrestling. I'm going to make you wrestle... and I know you ain't ready, but let's see how far you've come, Chris." Alias started approaching the ring, but as he did the lights in the arena flickered. The Scorpion Fighting Champ slowed his pace and then stopped before he reached the ring. Moliano breathed a noticeable sigh of relief then covered it by instead urging Alias towards the ring, his bravado suddenly returning. Sheffield simply displayed a wry smile before continuing. That's when a flash of something in the crowd caught the Pulp Hero's eye. Sheffield paused. The Sect? Alias's bandaged fists tightened. But more movement started in the crowd, and it was from multiple places. Alias didn't know what was happening, but he was thrown... and he looked at the man in the ring, that he hadn't seen for six years and didn't care for. He brought the mic to his mouth for a moment. An uncertainty hung in the air, even the crowd was on edge. “Chris – good luck”. With those words the lights in the arena suddenly went out. A few seconds later a smoke bomb went off from inside the ring, the initial flash from the bomb illuminating a figure suddenly standing in the ring... and the lights came back on in an instant. There, in the centre of the ring within the smoke stood Tengu, the unsettling Hell-Hound of Hiroshima. He peeked his head from side to side, as if like a vulture. Moliano looked around and saw that Alias had gone, presumably now on his way to the bar... what he and 'Perfection Personified' had to say, was said six years ago. Not that his whereabouts mattered to Chris at that moment in time. From the look of the masked beast in front of him, he had a big problem on his hands inside the ring. Chris Moliano vs Tengu (Open Challenge Match) Moliano stared at the beast that stood before him doing his best to maintain the confidence that he had felt prior to the appearance of Alias, and then the emergence of the frightening unknown quantity that was Tengu. Ding ding. The bell had rung and of two combatants standing on opposite sides of the ring, only one looked ready to fight. Moliano seemed taken aback by the ringing of the bell, and unsure how to even approach the matchup ahead. There was something about Tengu, something that positively terrified the brash Moliano. Tengu was poised looking ready to explode at any second. Finally Moliano summoned the belief to attack, and charged toward Tengu looking to take him down with a clothesline. He was however far too quick for that, and instead sent Chris down to the canvas with an impressive roundhouse kick to the delight of the crowd. Chris bounced back up immediately only to be greeted by a hard series of chops from the mysterious Tengu, the man known as “The Hiroshima Hell-Hound”. With the chest of Moliano red raw from the impact of the harsh chops Tengu followed up with an acrobatic corkscrew dropkick, sending his opponent down to the canvas and tumbling right out of the ring to the outside. The crowd certainly appeared to like what they saw from the masked debutant as Chris struggled to regain his feet with the aid of the barrier. Tengu paused, taking his focus off Moliano and recognising his surroundings and the crowd for the first time. The “Bird of Prey” had arrived in ACW. He turned back to face Chris just as he rolled back into the ring and waited for Chris to stand up before hitting him first with a thrust kick and then with a perfectly executed Bridging German Suplex for the early pinfall attempt. One... Two... Moliano was only just able to roll out of the pin. As both men stood back up Tengu wrapped his arms around Moliano before hitting an overhead belly to belly suplex that sent Chris flying across the ring. Tengu showed strength that defied his size, standing only 5’9” but tossing the 6’4” Moliano across the ring fairly easily. Moliano sat up and yelled out in pain whilst Tengu looked on at his fallen opponent. He walked menacingly toward Moliano, but this time Chris saw him coming and was first to the punch as he hit a big right uppercut out of desperation, which knocked Tengu backwards. Chris saw his first chance to take control of the match and used the moment to hit one of the best looking dropkicks you’re ever likely to see, sending Tengu down for the first time. “That’s what I’m talking about ACW!” Moliano was obviously delighted with the dropkick and yelled toward the crowd. His back was still in pain from the two suplexes he had taken but the adrenaline rush had taken over after his first offense of the match. Now was the time to put on the “wrestling clinic” he had promised. He would show Alias. He would show Tengu. He would show ACW. He would show his talent to the entire world. ...Or not. Chris had taken too long yelling and tending to his back and Tengu had already regained a vertical base. Chris attempted to Irish Whip Tengu into the corner turnbuckle but the move was reversed, instead sending Moliano into the corner with authority. Tengu charged towards the corner with intent but was greeted by the raised boot of Moliano, and as Tengu doubled over he placed him in position for a powerbomb, and lifted him up... ...Reversal! A thunderous Flip-over DDT from Tengu sent Chris head first into the mat and delighted the crowd. Tengu was poised to pounce once again and waiting for Moliano to stir. Chris’ head was spinning from the impact, and what awaited him as rose to his knees attempting to get up was only going to make that worse. A high speed roundhouse kick to the head nearly decapitated Moliano who lay motionless on the mat. Instead of going for a cover Tengu was up on the top rope in an instant. Displaying great speed and agility he leapt through the air with a picturesque inverted shooting star press that bought crowd to their feet. This one was as good as over. Tengu had been determined to make a statement since he appeared from behind the cloud of smoke. Again opting against going for the pin he waited on Moliano once more, perhaps wanting to end the match in style with a finishing manoeuvre. The anticipation was in the air as everyone in the arena waited on what move Tengu would deliver next as Chris finally began to stir and attempt to get up using the ropes for the assist. ...but instead he rolled outside the ring. Still clutching his head he staggered back up the aisle toward the dressing rooms. This was not his night, and he knew it. Chris disappeared behind the curtain, pausing only to yell at some members of the crowd who jeered him along the way. The count was academic and the referee soon finished his ten count and the match was over. The ‘wrestling clinic’ Moliano had promised had not gone at all to plan, and he had instead lost in short order. The cursed spirit that was Tengu had ruined Chris Moliano’s debut. By not allowing him a pinfall victory Chris had perhaps tainted his too. This upset the demi-God - and that was not good for anyone in ACW. Especially Chris Moliano.
