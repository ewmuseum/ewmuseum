LIVE! on Sunday 20th October From The Fleet Centre, Boston, Massachusetts Introduction Life and the world of wrestling federations seem to have a lot in common, these days especially. As soon as you think things cannot possibly get any worse...they do. It seemed as if Ethan Winters was the King of the World last week, as he looked on at yet another new addition to his stable, in X Wrestling's star KaGe. This week Winters has put on a special "Tryout" match, in which the winner would be given a place into his federation, under his wing. All of this goes on as the main story of last week was the no-show of SilverHAWK, who had only been seen leaving a hotel room late last week, to travel home to his wife and child. Although this week Winters may see his Try-Out match and the wrestling debut of KaGe as the big news on the show, only one man will take the headlines tomorrow morning... "You ready for your match kid?" was the question Ash Wild kept hearing all day long. Ash had arrived at the arena bright and early to get prepared for his first ever wrestling match. Ash had no idea what he had got himself in to. At the time when he made the deal with Mr. Winters he wasn't thinking clearly. He wanted to be able to promote his movie and find some talent so badly that he would've agreed to damn near anything at that point. Once he made the deal with the devil Ash realized what exactly he had got himself in to. Ash also realized that Mr. Winters never bothered to mention who his opponent for the match was. Was it going to be a monster like ICU? Was it going to be the world champion Osyrus? Ash had been worried all day long and he still had no clue. Just then he snapped back to reality as he felt a hand on his shoulder. Ash quickly looked up to see Mr. Winters standing there. "It's almost time kid. You sure you want to do this?" said Mr. Winters confidentially. "I'm positive." said Ash as he stood up to stare Winters right in the face. "Good. Because you're the first match. So get your shit together and head up to the ramp and get ready to be electrified. Good luck kid." said Winters as he turned around and started to walk away. "Wait...I have a question." said Ash, it made Winters stop in his tracks and turn back around to the face the kid. "Yeah, what is it? I don't have all the time in the world and neither do you." said Winters smugly. "Well...who is my opponent?" said Ash with a concerned look on his face. Winters stood there for a second then a smile spread across his face. He took his shades off and walked back over to Ash. He put his hand on Ash's shoulder and stood there for another second looking at the kid. "That is a surprise. You'll see once you get out there. Good luck kid." said Winters as he took his hand off Ash and walked away. Leaving Ash standing there pondering his decision. It was time to get it on and Ash was as scared as he had ever been. It was too late to back out now. Time to go show the world what Ash Wild was capable of. Ash grabbed his elbow pads off the chair and headed to the ring as the camera faded to commercial. World War II, constant shoot-outs between police and suspects, Columbine, even fights going on in your own neighbourhood. The battle brews in ACW for the first time.
