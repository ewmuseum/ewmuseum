What this is The fed's own biography page for the character Jason Kain, from the fed's first site (acw.ewmania.com, the 2002 to 2004 edition), as captured in the Wayback on 29 September 2003. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Jason Kain Real Name: Jason Evans Birthdate: February 15, 1980 Hometown: Asbury Park, NJ Height: 5' 9' Weight: 236 lbs. lbs. Nicknames: Hottwire, Epitome of Innovation Status: Neutral Manager(s): Jade Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Quick Death' by Transplants Wrestling Style: Technical/Hardcore Signature Moves: All Suplex Variations Set Up Move: Electric Slide (Full-nelson bulldog ) Finisher: Overload** (Done like a buffalo sleep except the choking arm is pulled all the way through so that the armbar is done with the same arm. The other arm clenches down on the wrist of the armbar and pushes at the back of the head. **Sometimes down with the leg scissors if desperate.) ACW Title History: None ACW Award History: None Other Federations: ukw, EYE, tW, XwwA, XWA, ACWF Other Federation Awards: ukw European Champ, ukw 2x Tag Champ, EYE Steel Champion, tW #1 Contender, XwwA 3x Hardcore Champ, XWA 2x Hardcore Champ, XWA World Champ, ACWF 2x Tag Team Champ, ACWF Hardcore Champ, ACWF Rookie of the Year (1996) Handler Information Handler Name: Jason P. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: n/a Biography (Patented Moves) Shocking Experience = Buffalo Sleeper with Leg Scissors. (After Glory, Kain stopped using this as his finisher for a more brutal, more excessive version) White Lightning = A leg forced Spear, done from anywhere, but usually catching the opponent with it as a reversal or off guard. Wire Cutter = A Dudley Dog-type maneuvre, but from off the ropes instead of the turnbuckle. Circuit Breaker = A stunner. Usually done to break a combination... but sometimes done to set up for the Power Surge. Power Surge = Kain's only top rope move. A money shot style splash, but Kain does a 180 flat spin, so when he lands his head faces the turnbuckle. (Common Moves) All Suplex variations, with preferences to snap suplexes and snap DDT's/Brainbusters. (Cruiserweight Spots) Uses mainly the harder suplexes due to their lightweight structure. Also uses White Lightning (spear) as a rope toss reversal. (Heavyweight Spots) The weaker faster suplexes are used more often against guys heavier than him. But uses the Electric Slide and Wire Cutter (Dudley Dog/Stratusfaction... basically a Dudley dog off the center of the ropes...) as setups for his finisher. (SuperHW Spots) Suplexes only when the opponent is tired. But goes for the Wire Cutter and Electric Slide as used before. Tries more speed based moves and stuff that will take the opponent down quick: Drop Toe Hold, Leg Trip, body tackle, and Lou Thesz Press. (Hardcore Spots) One of the more spectacular parts of Kain's move set. Any time a chair is in the ring, makes the big attempts to hit his suplex or DDT into the chair. Also goes for drop toe holds to it. -------------------------- Biography: As a child he had been brought up with a loving family, a great group of friends, and a wonderful suburban setting. His life could have never been better. Up until his Junior Year he was getting straight A's in school, was one of the top students at the Sean Hayes IPW Academy of wrestling, and was in the school football team playing as a star defenseman. One day it all changed. His family suddenly decided to move. His school grades flopped to failing. His girlfriend dumped him at the prom. His job framed him for stealing money and fired him. Everything fell apart. His parents packed up everything and were ready to go... but Jason was nowhere to be found. He woke up on the polluted beach of Asbury Park, his face scarred from some sort of beating he received, his clothes torn, his wallet gone. In need of money he walked the streets of Asbury only to find an illegal cage fighting circuit... he joined the group and made his meals off of his winnings... and sometimes he was brought in by a prostitute who watched the fights, but only for her to help his wounds and give him the couch for the night. He tried his luck in small federations, little jobs here and there as a masked wrestler, others as a premier heel... He started to get on his own feet. He found a job in England, working for ukw as one of their most loyal stars. He even found courage in God, by mysterious notes left on his door teaching him of the bible. Things were looking up for him again. But what goes up most certainly comes down. Kain suddenly was out of a job when the fed went belly up. He was a multiple champion, but it didn't mean anything to any federations... they wanted something more. He finally snapped, beating up random people and running from the cops on numerous occasions. The money he had earned and his popular face were the only things keeping him out of jail. But the money was running low... and the popular face had long since been scarred. Somethig that could only make him be worse off than before... With nothing left but a promo tape, his old mask crudely sewn back together, a resumee, and a clenched fist, he banged on the door of the ACW headquarters...
