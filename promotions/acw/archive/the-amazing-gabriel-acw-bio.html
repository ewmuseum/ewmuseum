The Amazing Gabriel Biography Real Name Rumored to be Steven Gabriel [name withheld] Nicknames The Amazing One, TAG Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday December 5th, 1979 Place of Birth Sacremento, CA Height 6' Weight 219 Blood Type AB+ Eye Color Brown Biography Total showman in every sense of the word. A rather eccentric individual who can be seen wearing bizarre colors in ring and is something of a total cigarette-smoking sleaze, complete with ugly porn moustache. The ringleader of the group, the brains, and the mouthpiece for the Night Life. Appearing to be a master manipulator, he’s managed to collect a various assortment of social outcasts and lured them in with promises of fame and fortune. It’s unclear how he maintains contracts over this group, but we at least know his end game: Gettin' that money. CURRENTLY: One sixth of the current ACW Tag Team Championships. By executive order, Freebird Rules is in effect, any two members of the Night Life may defend the championship. Other Feds Various independent federations in different countries Other Fed Titles Here and there Other Fed Awards He's probably logged a few Team The Night Life Wrestling Style Tactical High Flyer. A master of no particular style, but with expertise in several. Angers opponents with flips, cartwheels, reversals, runs in circles until he gets the advantage, then lays down a hurt. Finisher Move A badly named and superawesome shooting star press; can also be done from the Springboard position. Also complete with “point-to-the-heavens” Macho Man taunt when done regularly / Dragon Sleeper with body scissors Finisher Description The Amazing Star Press / Euphoria Special Moves The Pill Popper (Springboard kick to the face ala Low Ki) The Amazing Gabrielbow (Austin Aries' Pendulum/Powerdrive elbow) Blu-ray (TAG's take on a Death Valley Driver. Great go-to move he can hit on people up to around... let's say 270-ish. Can also hit this to opponents running into the turnbuckle ala Icarus.) Hanging neckbreaker (done Rick Rude-style, complete with unnecessary gyrations and/or pelvic thrusts) The Flipside (standing moonsault side slam) Brewski Boot (jump spinning roundhouse kick) The LED (blue thunder bomb) Hakken (running knee smash to seated opponent) Celestial Phenomenon (springboard moonsault off the top cable) Other Moves: Lots of reversals and convoluted counters like backflips, cartwheels, etc., multiple kick variations (rolling koppou kick, enziguri, jumping high kick, dropkicks, etc.), discus elbow smash, tope con hilo, springboard back elbow, ranhei roll-up pin, rolling neck snap, flying european uppercut, double stomps (mainly off second rope), michinoku driver, forward russian legsweep, satellite headscissors, dragon screw (to legs and neck), slingshot legdrop, spinning bootscrape, diving high-angle body press, running body press to cornered opponent, senton back splash (standing or off second/top rope, be creative), various submission holds with intricate shit. Entrance Music 'Slam' by Pendulum Trainer Self-taught (rumored to have studied in multiple countries)
