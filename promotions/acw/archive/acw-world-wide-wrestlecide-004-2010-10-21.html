World.Wide.Wrestlecide 004, 21 October 2010

21 October 2010

A C W THERE WILL BE BLOOD WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 004 ¤ 21 OCTOBER 2010 GOUDI OLYMPIC HALL, ATHENS, GREECE Once the cue was given, the cameras started rolling and WORLD.WIDE.WRESTLECIDE 004 began beaming to thousands of fans online! The pyrotechnics were of the highest order as about 4,500 fans welcomed the ACW roster back to Athens, one week before the highly-anticipated IN ACROPOLIS 2010 pay-per-view. The citizens of Athens served up a hell of an atmosphere in the Goudi Olympic Hall, simply salivating at the line-up of matches that lay ahead in the evening. # - The first match of the night featured two men who had been embroiled in a personal rivalry... except, none of it had ever been featured on COURAGE programming. But even though the Grecians in attendance had little knowledge of the happenings in the All-Star Academy, the sight of Malk Al-Haq -- who was adorned in a sleeveless tanktop with the words 'fear the cabal' embossed in the front -- was enough to make them jeer their hearts out. In contrast, Malk's opponent got a cheer not only because he was reported as being Malk's biggest rival in the All-Star Academy, but because this man was the current reigning United States Champion. That's right, for the first time, the behemoth Italian known as Palio graced the hallowed squared circle that was the ACW ring and got a taste of what life outside of the All-Star Academy was all about. The fans were given a very quick primer of the bad blood brewing between the two individuals, and the rules for the resultant match that was deemed Malk's final chance at the United States Championship; 10-minute time limit, title belt could change hands on a disqualification, the Champion would have unlimited rope-breaks while the Challenger would only have to settle for three rope-breaks. With that, the stage had been set, and the battle was underway. Neither man moved a muscle nearly thirty seconds after the bell had rung, but Malk soon realised that wasting time was not in his favour. Palio, touted by many industry experts that have been following the All-Star Academy as one for the future, was not intimidated in the slightest by the fact that this was his first non-Academy match-up. In fact, Palio absorbed the initial wave of punishment Malk dished out like a true Champion and rattled the Babylonian with a gorilla press slam that had the unintended effect of sending Malk crashing through the Pakistani Broadcast Booth! Barely two minutes registered on the clock, and there were already massive 'holy shit' chants reveberating throughout the arena. Naturally, the appointed referee for the match in Leon Hurst was at a loss on how to proceed. Especially with Akbar and Feroz, the Pakistani Broadcast Correspondents, furiously lamenting the destruction of their sanctuary. Palio appeared to be on edge, thinking he was on the verge of a disqualification. Surprisingly, Malk Al-Haq recovered and quickly (as fast as he could in his state) got back into the ring. The Babylonian begged referee Leon Hurst not to stop the match or to award him the disqualification victory; Malk desperately wanted to beat Palio, fair and square. Hurst admonished Palio for going overboard, and then did two things: to extend the time-limit to 15 minutes and to award Malk one more rope-break. Palio was thankful for the referee's consideration of the situation... and then proceeded to trap the still-recovering Malk in one of his many feared submission moves; the Kokina Clutch! Right in the middle of the ring, too! The situation looked grim for Malk, but despite having just gone through a table a minute or so earlier, the Babylonian was able to fall back on his determination to triumph and somehow managed to reach the ropes. Just as quickly as he'd gained an additonal rope-break, Malk had lost the advantage. Better than losing the match, though. Palio relented, a giant smile plastered on his face. Thirty seconds later, the reason behind the smile was clear. Malk going through a table and nearly getting choked out took a lot out of him. He was no match for Palio's next feat of strength and audacity; being downed by his own Babylonian Drop finisher. The three-count was academic, and Palio basked in laying to rest his rival's challenge for the United States Title once and for all. # - Backstage, the lovely Jenna McMullen was standing by with the clean-cut Carrachio Salfuego who hadn't been seen since his famous match-winning pinfall in the main event of COURAGE 124. As alluded to earlier in the week on the ACW website, the story was that Carrachio was attacked in his hotel room hours before COURAGE 125; and the Madrid-born grappler went on to tell Jenna and the rest of the Grecians in attendance that his assailant was none other than the man he pinned on COURAGE 124 -- Jack Harris! Carrachio, with a band-aid on above his right eyebrow to add to the nose-guard that he was still sporting thanks to Ryan Billows' attack a month or so earlier, expressed his outrage at the Pikey Madman's actions. Jenna asked what Carrachio was going to do about it, and Salfuego responded with news of a match later on in the night that had been booked by SilverHAWK hours earlier. That's right, Carrachio Salfuego would do battle with The Pikey Madman! While the fans cheered at the prospect of that showdown, Carrachio found himself being suddenly joined by an individual who had become a semi-contraversial figure as of late. It was the Brummie Bulldog, Rud Albion. Rud thanked the punch drunk rookie sensation for joining himself and KSZ on 124, and also congratulated him on scoring the deciding pinfall. He was pleased to see that the Madridian was taking the fight back to Harris, and the cabal, not becoming intimidated by the former United States Champion. Albion let Carrachio know that he had an ally in the Kingdom's Keeper, because an enemy of his enemy's friend was most definitely his friend. Salfuego thanked Albion, though didn't quite understand him due to the inherent language barrier, before assuring Harris that his days were numbered. Albion then noted the same for Bobby Minio, in their upcoming match. Jenna McMullen thanked both men for their time and wished them luck in their respective battles with Cabalites later on in the night. # - It was time for some red-hot tag team action, as only ACW could provide. Jump Around by House Of Pain played out The SOBs known better and individually as Selby O'Brien and Sheamus O'Brien; the latter having turned into a giant-sized SOB over the course of the last few weeks. After scoring a victory over Irish Spring on COURAGE 125, the two had a date against Abilities and The Payne Gang at the upcoming PPV and looked for some much-needed momentum heading into the big show. Selby and Sheamus discussed strategy as their opponents made their way through the curtains. Being accompanied by a lovely young Latina, Leticia Rios (known affectionately as Lettie or She With The Spankable Be-hind), the team of Big E. Stark and former First Airbourne member Mach 2 made their way out to the ring and got the crowd going with a great pre-match dance routine. Mach 2 had taken a year off ACW to apply his craft in New York's Finest back in the States where he'd won their newly-formed Junior Heavyweight Championship. Along with Big E. Stark, the two comprised ACW's newest tag team appropriately named Big E. Smalls. Stark, a 300-pound brick shithouse, was with Mach 2 under a temporary 90-day contract with intent of turning it to a full-time contract with some good performances. Mach 2 started off fast and furious with Selby O'Brien, the two spending a few moments exchanging holds. Mach 2 ran circles around the usually fast high-flyer, taking him down with a series of leg takeovers. Selby got the advantage and tried to monkey flip him to no avail. Selby and him got into a series of pinning exchanges that had the crowd on their feet. The two shook hands, which only infuriated Sheamus. After a moment of discussing strategy, Selby grounded Mach 2 for moments before Mach 2 found an opening and tagged in Big E. Stark. The big man went to work immediately, mowing right through Selby and tossing him around the ring with ease. Sheamus interfered on his brother's behalf, only to get hurled outside the ring alongside his brother. Mach 2 climbed atop the back of Stark and leaped to the outside, taking both men down with a shooting star suicida! Back inside, Selby fell victim to a near-fall. Mach 2 tried a top rope move, but Sheamus crept behind him and pushed him off the 'buckles, much to Selby's chagrin. Selby tagged him in and Sheamus immediately grounded the former tag partner of Andy Sharp with several elbows. After a few grinding headlocks to the head, Sheamus went airborne and caught him with a springboard dropkick for two. A bridging German also got two. Sheamus and Selby made a couple quick tags, blitzing the youngster from New York with some impressive tandem moves, including a backbreaker/stomp off the middle turnbuckle that nearly got the win, had it not been for a save by Big E. Stark. The distraction let Sheamus work over Mach 2 behind the refere's back, ramming him into the steel ring-post. Sheamus worked the arm with an impressive Cross-Arm Breaker submission. Mach 2 barely fought to the ropes, and slipped between a double-team attempt with Sheamus using Selby like a battering ram, only to make his twin brother eat turnbuckle. And then? TAG TO BIG E. STARK! The fans were incredibly pumped at the drama that was unfolding, and erupted with cheers when the big man charged nto the ring. Big E. Stark was a freight train, knocking down both Sheamus and Selby, sending both men flying into one another before hitting a Butt-Butt in the corner, crushing both. In an INCREDIBLE show of power, Big E. lifted BOTH twins on his shoulders in a firemans carry, dropping both with a tandem samoan drop that almost got three, had it not been for a weak shoulder by legal man Selby. Sheamus jumped back into the fray, only to have been neutralized by Mach 2. Lettie Rios looked on, cheering her man as he attempted a dive to the outside on Sheamus, only for Sheamus to make it out of harm's way. A referee Leon Hurst got lost in the confusion from the outside, Big E. Stark got back to his feet, but didn't see the switcheroo as Sheamus pushed Selby out of the ring. Stark went to pick him up, but a rake of the eyes followed by a Shitfaced Busaiku Knee Kick scored him the victory despite not being the legal man. All in all, a very impressive outing by the newest tag team on the scene, but the storybook first win for the team had been stolen from their grip by Sheamus O'Brien and his scheming ways. The SOBs had definitely come a long way over the past 12 months, and Sheamus especially looked hell-bent on making sure that he and his brother would walk out of the upcoming pay-per-view with all the momentum needed to make a play for the ACW Tag Team Titles! # - Backstage, Kristinia Kage and Orlando Grant met up to discuss what was coming up later in the show; the tag team match from COURAGE 125 that never materialised, because of Fejona Min's attack on her. Orlando re-assured Kristinia that he was still up to be her partner, and that he'd be by her side up until it was time to head to the ring. Kristinia thanked Orlando for being there for her, and told the OG of ACW that while she was still suffering from last week's ambush... she was looking forward to getting her hands on the two female members of the cabal. The Ziploc Boy then quipped that he had an idea for something that could happen AFTER the match, win or lose. But before he could spill, Orlando motioned for the camera-man to leave the room. # - In the ring, there was a bit of a commotion going on. Akbar and Feroz -- the Pakistani Broadcast Correspondents who were unable to do their jobs because of what happened to their Broadcast Booth earlier in the show -- had stormed the ring and began expressing their grievances at their Broadcast Booth continually being disrespected. "First on COURAGE 123, and now tonight!" cried Akbar, much to the crowd's displeasure. Feroz went on to demand that a proper representative of ACW come out to apologise on behalf of the entire organisation for the lack of respect accorded to them. A whole minute passed before, rather surprisingly, the call was answered by two very surly gentlemen. Abraham Swift and William T. Rex; better known as Abilities. Abraham declared that he and his partner William, having been contracted on a semi-regular basis to act as enforcers for Jeremy Hunt's band of merry men (i.e. The Huntsmen), were ideal representatives of ACW. Akbar and Feroz objected to the presence of Abilities, right before there was to be another twist; Abraham revealed that he knew Akbar and Feroz used to be wrestlers several years ago, and therefore challenged them to an impromptu match. Akbar vehemently turned down the offer, only for Swift to state that a victory for the Pakistanis over himself and William would go a long way to making everybody respect them. A quick huddle involving Akbar and Feroz resulted in an altogether surprising development: they accepted the challenge. That would prove to be the stupidest decision ever made on live ACW programming. Once the bell rang (nobody quite knew how referee Roderick Lynch popped up at ringside so quickly), Abraham and William proceeded to whoop the living daylights out of Akbar and Feroz. The slaughter lasted all of two minutes, as two sit-out chokeslams by Abraham on both Akbar and Feroz was followed with William T. Rex memorably scoring with a flying headbutt from the top of the corner turnbuckle on poor Feroz. Despite their status as big-time jerks, Abilities were given a somewhat resounding round of cheers from the crowd following the successful pinfall. As for the Pakistanis? Lesson learnt for them. We hope, for their sake. # - Backstage, ACW's resident masked mastermind Z was speaking candidly to his giant Mongolian bodyguard Kesavan about how the cabal was slowly but surely gaining steam. Kesavan asked Z if he'd spoken to Trey Vincent regarding the latter's upcoming battle for the ACW World Championship at IN ACROPOLIS 2010. Z confirmed that he had, and also stated that Trey was busy preparing for the fight of his life. Kesavan noted that the ACW World Title was critical to Z's plans, but the Caped Crusader merely laughed. Their conversation was interrupted by a knock at the door; it was Malk Al-Haq, who was looking like hell after his failed attempt to best Palio earlier in the show. Z's demeanour changed, and he invited Malk to sit down. The door was closed shut and nobody knew what happened after that... but it was clear that Z was none too happy about Malk's failure to reclaim the United States Title. # - Rud Albion had been a busy bee on COURAGE 125, although what he ended up doing was not something he chose. Had he a say in the matter, Rud would have taken out his frustrations on his rival Z one week ago, instead of waiting for the upcoming pay-per-view. Alas, Z had sent his then-newest recruit of the cabal to attack Albion. The result was Rud Albion and Bobby Minio battling it out like savage beasts in the boiler-room for nearly thirty minutes before security broke it up. Tonight was an opportunity for both men to lock horns officially, inside of the ACW squared circle. Albion got the match started chewing on the mixed reception he was garnering. Half of the crowd supported his war against Z, but the other half despised his affiliation with Jeremy Hunt. Bobby Minio, a reluctant member of the cabal had considerably less to worry about as far as the fans were concerned, and concentrated much of his early attack on Albion's legs. Rud found it difficult to maintain an upright position even as he mounted various comebacks against Bobby's relentless assault, but was eventually able to hiptoss Minio out of the ring. Thankfully for the reluctant Cabalite, he was able to land on the outside relatively unscathed. Not so thankfully, that set Albion up nicely for the execution of his tope suicada that had both men winded on the outside for a while. As a matter of fact, both Albion and Minio nearly got counted out but they were able to return to the ring in the nick of time. Rud tried to capitalise on his newfound momentum with his Red Coat finisher... but Bobby was able to counter and pulled off his trademark Shotgun DDT. At the very death, Albion got his shoulder up, surprising many in the audience. Minio was frustrated, but piled on the pressure immensely in the build-up to his New Religion finisher. Unfortunately, The Kingdom's Keeper was able to evade Minio's dropkick off of a springboard backflip and instantly laid the reluctant Cabalite with his Stacked Albion finisher. After nearly 10 minutes, Rud Albion triumphed over Bobby Minio and limped out of the ring to bask in the afterglow of victory. Bobby Minio threw a fit in the ring, furious that he'd been outwitted by the Bronze Lion... and the rage in his eyes suggested perhaps he wasn't quite done with Rud, who could now fully focus his energies on Z at IN ACROPOLIS 2010. # - Backstage, 'Vicious' Greg Miles -- bandaged and generally looking like a bus had run him over sixteen times -- was ranting away at Joe Bishop, ACW's hardworking but semi-perverted Production Manager. Greg demanded another chance to prove himself in the ring, claiming that his defeat to Omega a week prior was a fluke. Bishop told Greg that not only had he been defeated, Greg was almost destroyed at Omega's hands and was not cleared to wrestle. The Baytown Badass got pissed and kicked over a trash-can. Tough. Bishop sighed and then caved; he promised Greg that he'd find him an opponent for the pay-per-view, as long as Greg could get into fightin' shape by then. The Vicious One sneered at Joe, boasting that broken bones and almost slipping into a concussed state six times over the last week wasn't going to stand in the way of his destiny. # - On COURAGE 124, Carrachio Salfuego stepped up big time by volunteering to take the missing Jade Argent's place in the explosive main event. Already having solidified himself as a burgeoning warrior with his run-ins with Ryan Billows, Salfuego shocked everyone by getting the decisivie pinfall over Jack Harris. In retaliation, Jack Harris ambushed the young Salfuego in his hotel room hours before COURAGE 125. And thus, here we were; The Pikey Madman better known as Jack Harris against the talented rookie from Madrid that had a heart of gold. Naturally, the fans were extremely excited to see this grudge match unfold. And right from the start, Carrachio took the fight to Jack Harris. The Unit had barely stepped into the ring when Carrachio flung himself at arguably Z's top goon, knocking them both out of the ring. That meant that referee Roderick Lynch had to exit the ring too and try to put an end to the ringside brawling. Easier said than done, especially after Harris sent Carrachio crashing into the steel ring-steps. Once Harris and Salfuego finally got back into the ring, it was the latter surprising once more with a armbar takedown into an attempted crossface submission. Harris got free and nearly beheaded Salfuego with a massive kick to the face! From there, the Chancellor of Excellence took over and did so by taking his time to hammer Carrachio with a variety of hard-hitting moves. The second that Jack Harris went for his Execution finisher, though? Carrachio roared back to life; he chopped Harris in the neck and connected with a spinning heel kick to the ribs. That was swiftly followed up with a spinning neckbreaker and a springboard moonsault, which earned the young Madridian a near-fall. Carrachio didn't let up, despite Jack pulling himself together and using his size advantage to good measure. The fans were riveted by the sheer intensity of the match and how the advantage swung from one man to the other like a pendulum. Finally, Jack Harris just about managed to put an end to Salfuego's bravado with a fallaway slam from the top of the turnbuckle, and inevitably followed up with his Execution finisher. Despite having won out over Carrachio, the fans nonetheless gave Jack Harris grief and the Pikey Madman wheeled away from the ring somewhat in shock over Carrachio's resistance throughout the course of the match. It was then no wonder that the crowd served up a standing ovation to the young Madridian when he finally came to. A valiant but ultimately futile effort for Carrachio, but he had absolutely no reason to feel ashamed of the defeat. # - Backstage, Z had just stepped out of his room following his meeting with Malk Al-Haq. Malk, who was escorted down the hallway by Kesavan, looked a little bit shellshocked. Must have been one hell of a meeting with Z. The Masked Enigma turned in the opposite direction and whistled to himself, only to run into referee Trent Savage. Trent blasted Z for having manipulated his friend Pete Strombo, referencing the elaborate scam that Z pulled on Pete which culminated at COURAGE 125. Z, maintaining his smug-as-all-hell composure, preached that whatever happened to Strombo was solely the fault of Rud Albion; a sentiment he first declared on COURAGE 125. Savage was beside himself and called Z a monster, demanding to know where Strombo was. "He's undergoing brainwashing training. He'll become my new weapon of mass destruction, but it'll take time." was Z's callous reply. Trent Savage was left speechless, and Z laughed as he exited the area. Shocking. # - During ACW's travels across Europe, several new talent specific to that location have been brought on-board and given extended contracts. In Spain, ACW picked up the team of Manuel Et La Bull (Manuel and Bull). And when in Ukraine, ACW came across the tandem of Pukazho and Jeinmenner (who have recently settled on a team name - Dinamo Ilyich). The two teams have had mixed fortunes since debuting, racking up more losses than victory. On this night, both teams would have the chance to rectify that but not against each other. No, they would be joined by Kaus and Tyson XL of The Night Life. Triple-threat tag team match, with the first pinfall registered to settle things. The fans were naturally hyped. Tyson XL, having earned himself a chance to contest for the [Black] Scorpion Championship at the upcoming pay-per-view, got the ball rolling with Manuel and Jeinmenner. Despite being a big man, Tyson was able to run circles around both of 'em, but soon found himself beaten down when Manuel and Jeinmenner decided to team up. And from then on, it became a story of The Night Life's representives being targeted by their opponents. Manuel and Jeinmenner made constant tags to their respective partners, keeping Tyson isolated in the ring and away from the eager Kaus, but Tyson was always able to stand tall despite the double-teaming. However, when Bull was able to slip out of Tyson's clutches and connect with a shoulder barge leading to Pukazho picking Tyson up and slamming him down with a death valley driver... fans figured Tyson was done. Alas, both Pukazho and Bull went for the cover. Neither man wanted to shirk away from claiming the win and thus Tyson was able to slink away. Unfortunately for Tyson, Bull was able to catch him before he tagged out to Kaus. But even with Manuel and Jeinmenner being tagged back in, Tyson XL remained -- despite the double-team -- alive. And after manipulating his opponents to turn on each other, Tyson was able to tag out to Kaus. Needless to say, Kaus cleaned house in a major way. Tyson assisted Kaus in the search for victory by distracting the referee, and seconds later, Kaus had laid out everybody out with his Temporal Shift finisher. Highly impressive stuff from Kaus, who went on to pin Manuel for the victory. After the match, Tyson and Kaus celebrated a very hard-fought victory, while Dinamo Ilyich and Manuel Et La Bull argued with each other on the outside. Another defeat for both teams, flushing down all their hard work during the match. Pity. # - Backstage, 'The One' Jimmy Reid was standing by with his adopted son 'The Two' Booster Reid. Jimmy cleared his throat and turned to the camera, addressing every single wrestler on the ACW roster rather solemnly. The former ACW World Champion expressed regret at his son's inability to win a single match so far. Booster tried to speak up, but Jimmy curtly cut him off and ordered his purported heir to the Reid wrestling legacy to keep quiet. Jimmy went on to state that perhaps a mistake had been made in bringing Booster into the fold so quickly after his training, but warned that he was tired of all the jokes and the teasing the Reid family had been getting. Jimmy began to get feral, demanding that he wasn't going to stand for Booster being made the butt of all jokes... and even went so far as to state that it was HIS job as Booster's father to admonish Booster for being such a loser. Talk about tough love, huh? # - In another part of the backstage area, Jenna McMullen was armed with a microphone and an unusually sad expression over her face. She went on to reveal some very disturbing news. "Fans, on COURAGE 125... Jade Argent's pre-recorded interview with Renaud Cardinal had promised a special feature presentation of what was being hyped as Jade Argent's very last professional wrestling match against a mystery opponent. This match was supposed to have taken place yesterday, American time. But unfortunately, we regret to inform you that Jade Argent never showed up. Imagine how all those fans back in The Key Arena felt. They bought tickets for a single wrestling match, and both of the competitors never showed! Sheesh." Amid some scattering jeering and general shock, Jenna also stated that Jade Argent's whereabouts were unknown and that nobody in ACW knew if he was going to show up at the upcoming pay-per-view, where the Australian Opportunist is actually scheduled to face the ever-(un)popular Bastard King Of Ages. Suddenly, Khristain Keller stumbled onto the scene! Keller, looking completely shit-faced, shoved Jenna out of the way and joyously ranted about how he'd once and for all silenced Argent. "The cuntbag's gone forever!" Khristain chortled, to much displeasure from the fans. Was it for real? Had Keller really gotten rid of Jade Argent? We'd find out, definitively, in seven days time. # - Back at ringside, the fans readied themselves for a match that should have transpired seven days ago and also for all intents and purposes... the main event of the night. Fejona Min and Kati were out to a huge chorus of jeers. That was expected. Orlando Grant came out, super-hyper and raring to kick some arse. He got a good reception from the fans. But when Kristinia came out? The crowd went ballistic for the hometown girl. Kristinia Kage raced down to the ring and attacked Fejona Min to kickstart the match, with Orlando Grant taking Kati out of the ring to do battle. That left referee Paige Allen with a very difficult task in trying to rein in all the four competitors. When order was finally restored, Kristinia and Kati were deemed the legal competitors. Kristinia -- the victim of perhaps one too many sneak attacks over the last several months -- was immediately on the ropes with Kati wailing on her vindictively, in a bid to avenge her loss to the hometown girl on COURAGE 123. Unfortunately for Kati, Kristinia was able to move out of the way of an attempted jumping thrust kick and rolled across the ring to tag in Orlando Grant. Kati got bamboozled by Orlando's energetic offense, and each try to tag out to Fejona was thwarted by the OG of ACW, who gained near-fall after near-fall. Kati was in further trouble when Orlando and Kristinia exchanged four tags in the space of 90 seconds; the fans watched delightedly as Orlando and Kristinia executed a series of hit-and-run attacks, leaving Fejona absolutely fuming on the ring apron. It was then a good thing that Kati was able to counter Orlando's Big Shit Poppin' finisher and blasted him in the face with a jumping knee-strike. Kati proceeded to knock Kristinia Kage off the ring apron and made it to her corner to tag in Fejona Min. Unquestionably the most dominant female performer in ACW's history, Fejona went to town on Orlando Grant; the two had faced off against each other twice, and the score was tied at one apiece. Orlando tried valiantly to put up a defensive wall and make the tag out to Kristinia, but the Rogue Slayer was unwavering in her attacks. Once she'd slapped on a triangle choke out of nowhere on Orlando Grant, the fans feared for the worst. They didn't have to, because Kristinia Kage pulled off a very sneaky trick seconds prior to that; getting a blind tag off of her partner Orlando as he was launched into the ropes by Fejona. Kristinia ascended to the top of the turnbuckle and dropped a stunning elbow drop down onto Fejona, drawing a huge chorus of cheers from the fans! Fejona kicked out of the resulting cover, but many felt the advantage in the match was no longer in the favour of the two female Cabalites. Fejona would prove everybody wrong, slipping out of Kristinia's grasp just as the hometown girl was going for a brainbuster and connecting with a desperate neckbreaker. Both women, slated to square off against each other in seven days, were stranded in the middle of the ring and completely spent. Luckily for everyone, both Fejona and Kristinia tagged out to their respective partners... before proceeding to brawl with each other at ringside! In the ring, Kati and Orlando Grant went balls to the walls, with the fans thoroughly excited by what was unfolding. On the outside, Kristinia was seemingly in trouble as Fejona had lined the hometown girl up for her trademark hurricane kick. Except for the part where Kristinia rolled out of the way, and Fejona's foot made unprotected contact with the steel ring-post. Ouch! In the ring, it was a different story altogether. The OG of ACW once more went for his Big Shit Poppin' finisher, but Kati was able to escape and surprise Grant with a roll-up out of nowhere. Annnd, Kati held on long enough to get the three. No, seriously. A bit of a shocking finish, considering the non-stop action, and Orlando was left stunned. On the outside, a hurt Fejona Min was pleased that Kati secured the win, but she didn't get a chance to savour the victory. Why? Oh, because of Kristinia Kage and a steel chair. SMACK! SMACK! SMACK! Three insane chair-shots to the head, and Kristinia Kage stood tall over Fejona Min, semi-triumphant. Hell of a way to send a message to Min ahead of their showdown in seven days, and certainly a frentic finale to what had been a roller-coaster show. RESULTS RECAP * Palio def. Malk Al-Haq via PINFALL (5:02) to retain the ACW United States Title. * The SOBs def. Big E. Smalls via PINFALL (12:13). * Abilities def. The Pakistani Broadcast Correspondents via PINFALL (2:01). * Rud Albion def. Bobby Minio via PINFALL (9:47). * Jack Harris def. Carrachio Salfuego via PINFALL (6:22). * Kaus & Tyson XL def. Manuel Et La Bull and Dinamo Ilyich via PINFALL (6:39). * Kati & Fejona Min def. Kristinia Kage & Orlando Grant via PINFALL (11:40) F I N
