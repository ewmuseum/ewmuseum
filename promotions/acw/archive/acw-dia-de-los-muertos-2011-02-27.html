DIO DE LOS MUERTOS EDITION MATCH #1: Lutawicka vs. Remi De Rozario Just an hour or show before the actual start time Dio De Los Muertos, the fans who arrived to the show early were going to be treated to some ACW action. The first of two IN THE DARK~! Matches would see Cabalite member Lutawicka making his way to the ring to the sounds of “Here To Stay” by KoRn. He sneered out to the sea of thousands in attendance before the Angry Young Man entered the ring, ready for action. “Seize The Day” by Avenged Sevenfold played and the fans gave a polite round of applause for the Costa Rican Bullet, Remi De Rozario. The seven-year pro entered the ring. In a rematch from their quick, but hard-fought confrontation on Courage 131 (both men’s debut match), they were both eager to get on the board with a win. The action started out just like the wrestling fans of Mexico were accustomed to, fast-paced. For the first couple minutes, Lutawicka and Remi exchanged holds in a rapid-fire frenzy, ending in standoff after no less than TEN pinfall counters. The action got even more fast and furious (5, in theatres now!) when Rozario reversed a Headscissors and sent Lutawicka to the outside, then flew at him with a Tope Con Giro! Back inside the ring, a Slingshot Senton from Rozario almost got a win, but a kickout from Lutawicka told him he’d have to earn his victory. RDR went to work again, taking out the leg of The Angry Young Man and kept up some leg screws, but an attempt at a Spinning Toehold was reversed into an inside cradle for two. Lutawicka took control with a thumb to the eye and an excellent dropkick. From there, he utilized several different headlock variations aimed at the head, no doubt to soften him up for his REZ Lock. When RDR freed himself from a hold, he was shot off to the ropes, but Lutawicka’s second dropkick to the knee almost got a nearfall again. An attempt at a Tornado DDT off the ropes was countered into a Northern Lights for two. RDR then hit a Snap Suplex, then a Bridging German, both two-counts a piece. He tried for his Cartago Sling finisher, but Lutawicka flipped out and behind him, trying for the REZ Lock… which got countered into a modified O’Connor roll! ONE! TWO! THREE! And just like that, Remi De Rozario beat Lutawicka and got a measure of revenge from several weeks ago! The fans cheered as RDR celebrated with some of the fans while Lutawicka kicked at the ring ropes, pulling his hair out that victory had been snatched from what should’ve been Remi De Rozario’s defeat. MATCH #2: The SOBs vs. The Menendez Bros vs. The Locksmiths The second and final match-up before the PPV went to air was an end result of three matches that took place on COURAJE 135. The horribly-named Menendez Bros, Irish twins the SOBs, and The Locksmiths all lost out on a chance to compete in the show’s Triangle Tag Team Ladder Match to compete for an ACW Tag Title shot at Legends V. So tonight, the three teams would have to settle for competing in IN THE DARK~!, where a victory would be absolutely necessary for one of these teams to regain some momentum. And lastly, the Menendez Bros walked to the ring, getting a somewhat positive reaction (for they were neither Cabalites or sourpuss assholes). Eventually once Alastair tried for a cover, he was there to break it up. The three men battled for several more moments, trading nearfalls until Smith got kicked to the outside by a Dropkick from Harvey, then Selby caught him with one of his own, then dove out onto both guys with a springboard moonsault! Now Bertram, Sheamus, and Teddy Locke were the legal men, going out in full force as they came to blows.
