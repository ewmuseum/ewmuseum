The cheers ran for Kain now… INN-O-VATE INN-O-VATE INN-O-VATE But Kain was taken completely by surprise, causing Alias to catch him in the center of the ring! Kain kicked toward the roped, but to no avail! Kain writhed and twitched… but finally the sorrow reached his face… as he tapped. Jason Kain - 4 Alias - 4 The ref puts up four fingers on both hands as the crowd explodes. Three minutes left… and the score is tied at 4-4! Alias throws Kain forward, then tries to get to his feet… but the stamina had been sapped trying to keep the hold. Alias wobbled back… Kain rushed in, tossing in a kick… but suddenly the kick was caught! Alias tossed the leg up wrapping Kain in a horrendous lock, spinning around him and driving Kain to the mat with the Capture Suplex! The crowd watched as suddenly Alias smiled and pointed to the sky. One minute left. He climbed to the top rope… and set in motion for his shooting star… the Whiteout… But Kain rolled out of the way, leaving Alias nothing but mat!!! Kain stalled for a moment, gaining his composure as Alias laid motionless… then headed for his own turnbuckle!!! He stood up and looked at the clock! 10!!! 9!!! 8!!! Kain jumped!!! 7!!! He spun in mid air, landing a picture perfect Power Surge!!! 6!!! But he rolled off from the impact… 5!!! 4!!! 3!!! He rolled back on for the pinfall… The clock: 2!!! The ref: 1!!! The clock: 1!!! The ref: 2!!! The clock: BUZZER!!! The ref called the match a draw!!! Kain’s eyes stayed wide open, the crowd exploded, Alias sighed relief, and the ref stood completely amazed. Suddenly… it happened. HO-LEE SHIT HO-LEE SHIT HO-LEE SHIT Winner: ? The Tie Breaker Kain looked on. His final pin attempt was stopped by the bell. Kain could have just been the world heavyweight champion! And it slipped from his fingers. Kain looked over at Alias… but instead of going insane like expected, he crawled over to Alias, and slapped his chest, with a gesture definitely not expected. Kain gave him the thumbs up before flopping onto his back. Both men laid on the mat, staring at the sky. The hour was over, the match over… and still… no victor was crowned. The crowd continued it’s “Holy Shit!” chant through the arena, staring down at both men. Kain thought he had that match, but Alias slung the arm over at the second seizing the tying score, completely baffling the crowd and Kain himself. They were too well matched. Alias knew it as he laid there with his arm still over Kain’s almost unconscious body. Kain’s head lolled to the side, and looked at Alias’s battered face. Alias just sighed, wondering what would happen now that the federation had gone an entire three months without a champion. Alias roused first, though, pushing himself to his knees. The cheers were amazing, this was not your normal everyday match. This was a match of a lifetime. Alias stayed kneeling next to Kain, realizing that he had just screwed up ACW’s superior plan to have a champion. Kain made a breathless smile, then looked up at the lights. After what seemed like an eternity, Kain rolled over… pushing the debris from the table away from himself. He also got to his knees as Alias stood up and put out a hand to pull Kain to his feet. Both stood there, staring at each other for a moment, then gave a brotherly hug as the crowd exploded with cheers… but something happened within those mere moments of gladness… Music. Not any music… “Courage” by Alien Ant Farm. Joe Bishop’s music. Bishop stepped out, looking to the sky with an upset face. Bishop stood there, slowly moving the microphone in his other hand up to his mouth. He paused, still looking at the air above the two men. Slowly… his mouth opened. “You two are not leaving that ring.” Bishop sneered, lowering his head nonchalantly. “I told you that if you even THOUGHT of trying to screw us out of a champion… just because you’re FRIENDS…” The word friends came out with sarcasm and disdain. “I had said we had something planned for it.” He looked down toward the ring, his eyes shifting from man to man. Kain looked up with a sneer of his own while Alias just stared on with a slack jaw. “Above you is a cable. Hung twenty feet high. Attached to that cable is your All-Star Championship Wrestling World Title belt. Under the ring you will find a ladder. You two will finish out the contract you signed earlier. This pay-per-view WILL end with a champion. ACW WILL have a representative.” Bishop smirked as Kain just glared down the ramp at him. Alias turned away from the ramp with his hands on his hips, obviously disappointed. The ref stopped by the ring and pulled out the ladder. Kain turned to Alias, and Alias looked back. The federation WILL have a champion. DING DING DING!!! All-Star Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship Decider Iron Man Match Alias Vs. Jason Kain Kain glared up the ramp at Bishop who just wagged his finger and pointed to the sky, as the rope was lowered. The belt loomed overhead as Alias climbed out of the ring screaming his objection to Bishop, who just laughed, then pointed at Kain… as the man had climbed out of the ring and was grabbing the ladder. Alias looked at Kain as he mouthed one word… “Ratings.” Then smiled. Alias returned the grin though the dried blood around his mouth and slid under the ropes as Kain tossed the ladder over. The crowd roared as Kain hopped to the apron and pointed to the air. With a hard slingshot, Kain flung himself chest first into Alias, sending him to the mat hard. Kain stood up pulling Alias with him, pulling his head into a DDT, and driving him back to the mat. Kain stood up, obviously showing the amount of pain hadn’t totally vanished. He limped over to the ladder, and pulled it upright. Alias pushed himself to a knee and watched Kain set the ladder in the ropes. Alias stood up, using up his energy shoving Kain face first into the ladder. Alias wobbled around, then walked up to Kain back and pulled his legs up, leaving Kain’s head in the ladder rungs. Alias watched as Kain struggled slightly, but Alias dropped back, shooting the back of Kain’s head right into the rung above it. Bishop watched laughing his head off as his two “trained seals” worked their ass off in the ring. But a commotion came from behind him, as Jade stepped out and protested the match as well… But Bishop grabbed her as she ran past, pulling her to him and holding her back from the ring. Kain watched weakly through the rungs of the ladder as Alias jumped out of the ring, yelling in protest again. This time Kain didn’t stop him as he walked straight into Bishop’s face. Bishop let Jade go and pointed back at the ring. “Get back in there, Chris. This isn’t about YOU anymore. This is for the federation.” Joe looked down the ramp toward Kain, as Jade ran up to him… She didn’t say a word, but climbed into the ring and pulled Kain out of the rungs, and pulled the ladder upright. She opened it up and pulled Kain to his feet, she pointed to the ladder, telling him to climb it. But he shook his head as he pulled away from her shoulder. She looked at him wondering why. “I’m not winning it like this.” Was all Kain said, shoving the ladder back to the mat, then waved Alias back to the ring. Jade slid out of the ring, with a look on her face that showed more emotion than she had expected. Alias climbed back into the ring and looked at Kain… Kain just twisted, laying a hard wild right to Alias’ head. Alias stood shocked. Kain had struck him, even in his condition. This was going to the end and he knew it. Kain suddenly exploded with more than just a fist… a hard shoulder announced that the White Lightning had hit with tremendous force. For a man that had just endured an entire hour of a beating, he had bounced back for another shot very quickly. Alias hit the mat hard. Kain pulled the ladder out of the corner, and stood it up. He started climbing, but the spear he handed to Alias must have taken whatever fight he had left as his climbing was almost non-existent. Alias started to his feet as Kain worked his way to the top. But Kain’s ascent made no headway as Alias shoved the ladder sideways in one full swoop. Kain crotched himself on the ropes, then fell into the ring. Alias pushed himself to his feet again, grabbing the ladder for support, but Kain shook the ropes, chinning Alias with the ladder… putting both men on the mat again. The crowd exploded, both men were back to square one, with no power, and no way of truly finding a decent way to end this match-up. They couldn’t walk out. They couldn’t rest. It’s was one on one, until the bitter… or bright ending. As both men lay, they stared up at the belt. Kain noticed that a pouch was taped to the inside of the belt. What is that? Alias looked up at the belt as well. He wanted the prize… and now both men wanted to know more than anything, what was inside the pouch. Both men starts rousing at the same time, and the crowd started up again. One half screaming out “P-U-L-P!” and the other chanting “MIS-TER KAIN!” Kain got to his feet first, though… grabbing the ladder and shoving it into Alias’ midsection. He followed it up by tossing the ladder to Alias, and driving a hard hitting dropkick into the ladder slamming it further into Alias’ body. Alias fell back into the ropes, as Kain used them as a springboard to steal an attack from Alias’ book, nailing a hard roundhouse to the back of the head. Alias fell forward, clutching his head, as Kain rushed toward the ladder again. He started to set it up as Alias stood up behind Kain. Kain turned around… but got a hard kick to the stomach before the elbow hit the back of his head… quickly a knee came up, stopping him from falling to the mat… As Alias hit his mark with all of the Bullets, Jade looked on enthralled by the match. The ladder fell from all of the movement, and Alias took this into a note-to-self, as he spun to have his back face it. He grabbed Kain in a reverse half-nelson… pulling him belly-to-belly, and lifting him high over his head… PULPED!!! Into the open ladder!!! Kain lay on the ladder, virtually shattered. The ladder seemed dented, but hardly useless as Alias rolled Kain off of it. He set it up himself and started toward the top… Jade started screaming… “GET UP KAIN!!!!” which started a chant. Kain took this to heart as Alias reached an arm up to the belt… suddenly the arm was wrapped and Alias had no clue as to what was happening… but the crowd knew. The full nelson bulldog, Electric Slide, from the top of the ladder to the mat, made the entire world know. As both men lay on the mat again, the crowd’s chanting grew louder… “Holy Shit” from one corner. “Innovate” from another. “Alias” from yet another… But above everything, they heard the one thing that made them get up again. Laughter. On their knees they looked up the ramp. Joe Bishop was laughing at them. One word came to both their minds. Ratings. Suddenly Kain jumped to his feet and started for the upright ladder… Alias shortly behind him… Kain got to the top, reaching for the ladder, but suddenly from under him the ladder seemed to slip away… but in fact it was Kain. Alias had him up on his shoulders… virtually standing. HIGH ANGLE POWERBOMB!!! Kain twitched as Alias started up the ladder toward the belt. He looked down at Kain, who wasn’t really moving at all. Kain could only look up, a blurred Alias standing at the top of the ladder. Alias wasn’t grabbing for the belt… but as Kain tried moving… and couldn’t… he moved the one thing that mattered to Alias. A thumbs up. Alias grabbed the belt off the ring and held it into the air as the announcer roared loudly… “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE IS YOUR WINNER AND NEW ALL-STAR CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING WORLD CHAMPION…. “ALIAS!!!” All Good Things… The crowd was on it’s feet as Alias stood atop the ladder holding HIS world championship. He smiled and looked at the belt… then looked at the pouch on the inside. He had put it there for a reason, after all. He looked down at Jason Kain who was slowly getting to his feet. And the smile grew bigger. Alias climbed down the ladder to where Jade was looking over Kain’s injuries. She looked more worried than upset about the loss he had just endured. Kain didn’t seem to mind much though. He looked over to Alias, and gave him a brotherly hug. When they let go Alias was still holding his hand. Into it he gave the pouch. Kain looked at him puzzled. “Open it.” Alias winked. Though the black eye made the wink a little less noticeable. Kain pulled back the string and out popped the engagement ring he had thrown away six weeks ago. He looked at it in amazement, then looked back at Alias completely confused. “You wanted hope, right? Well, I got mine…” Alias smirked, then looked at Jade who hadn’t seen what they were doing. He called for a microphone and handed it to Kain. “Go get yours.” Kain turned around and looked at Jade. The crowd hushed waiting for Kain’s speech. “I never thought I could start a speech without my classic tag line, Jade.” Kain smirked… still breathing heavy. “But I don’t want you to suck on this. I want you to just answer me fully and truthfully.” He looked down at his hand. The ring glistened in the lights and the crowd stayed quiet. “Two years ago, I would have said I would have never tried this again. Two years ago, I would have thought that the stupid games we play in this ring had no meaning. Nothing to hope for. Alias… Chris… He helped me realize that there’s always something to hope for. It took him a long time to fully make me respect that hope… but he finally did it. One word we used for it was… ratings. I didn’t understand it at first… SilverHAWK had faith in us. Even Joe Bishop had faith in us. He had hope that we could bring this federation it’s glory and honor. I thought it was all bullshit. But now I stand here in front of you with hope. Hope that what I had gone through with you over the past year wasn’t in vain. Hope that I could go on in this world finally happy. I realized that this hope wasn’t the same hope everyone else has.” He turned to Alias and smiled. “That belt is yours, man. Congratulations. I’m glad that what you hoped for is finally yours. You told me to go get mine… and here I am.” He looked at Jade then suddenly knelt down. The crowd got completely hushed, as many of the female audience squealed and aw-ed. Jason Kain looked up at Jade Green and sincerely spoke the words. “Jade Greene. You came into my life as a joke. Jokes were fun. But they are short-lived. When you outlived the joke, I realized that we weren’t a joke. We were the real thing. I had planned for you to come here and we could be together again… but I faltered in that, realizing my hope had been dashed. I was depressed… and stupid. But you came through. Then there was a situation that caused YOU to lose that hope. And when it turned out incorrect, we were still hopeless. I found my hope, Jade. I found you again. And now I have a new hope… “That maybe, just maybe, you can hope for a future… with me.” The tears from Jade’s eyes were profuse. “Jade Greene, since the day we came together, I’ve loved you more than even I had thought… and now that we’re apart… I realized I loved you even more than that. I have one simple question to ask of you…” The crowd was silent to the point of a pin-drop. “Will you marry me?” Kain’s face showed such a face of trusting that he didn’t look like the tough guy that just finished and hour and a half of hell. This face was the face of Jason Evans. And that face was staring directly at Jade’s… who’s lip was quivering. She choked on her words. The seconds passed as hours. The crowd as silent as the dead. Alias stood behind his friend, with the belt on his shoulder, proudly. Not only as a champion, but as a friend. The entire roster of ACW had stepped out onto the entryway. If this wasn’t breaking Kayfabe, nothing was. No fighting. Just a simple question. … … … “Yes.” The roar wasn’t only the crowd. It was the wrestlers. The ring crew. The people at home. Everyone. Dante Inferno raised a fist to the air. Alias stood clapping his hands. People who had been in the audience were on their feet. Old enemies in the crowd, Curtis Reile and Stacy Powers stood cheering like mad. This was the culmination of a true year in ACW history… maybe not the biggest event in history… but now one of the most memorable. Jason Kain and Jade Greene stood in the middle of the ring. Alias jumped to a turnbuckle throwing his title to the air, and the crowd grew even louder. The federation was complete, that night. A world champion. A King Of Ages. An expulsion. An engagement. A new owner. One thing was for sure. All-Star Championship Wrestling would NEVER be the same...
