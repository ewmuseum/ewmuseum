A widely acclaimed star, as in movies or sports, who has great popular appeal. 2. One that is extremely popular or prominent or that is a major attraction. This part of the ACW site houses the online biographies of the contract superstars. In these biographies are stories of their own personal paths and histories, and small insights into the present and the future. Also included with their biographies is their latest promo photos, so you can see the man, or woman behind the story. All of the biographies are created by the handlers themselves and are regularly updated with information such as match stats and also their latest going-on in other federations out width ACW. If you are indeed interested in the works and the writings of any said ACW superstar, you would be advised to also check out their Experience folder, which is on all of the biographies also.
