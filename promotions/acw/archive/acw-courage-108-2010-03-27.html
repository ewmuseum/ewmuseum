Meet the reason you exist: "I didn't have to come to the ACW to become a superstar...I brought my spotlight with me" It was the sound of the ever-familiar voice of the Icon echoing throughout the arena. There were jeers, until the usual visual eye candy danced across the ominous trinity of super screens. A select bevy of monikers appeared at random, exploding on the ACWtron one after the other. Pro Wrestling's Phenomenon - The Icon - The Living Legend – The Ratings Grabber After the last moniker appeared on the screen, the all too familiar “Ring Superstar” reverberated throughout the arena speakers. The bank account's thick and his pockets are fat Peep the smirk on his face when he watching you tap A three-count or submission, which steez you wanna go? 'Cause this muthafucka right here is a reason there's a show! Vince Jacobs came out onto the stage looking straight ahead with a smirk on his face. He slowly made his way down the ramp, ready to face Jade Argent in this opening match. Vince smirked and pointed to a "Vince Not In HOF = We Burn Your House Down " sign. He stood in front of the ring before making his way around to the steps. The Superstar stood on the steps with arms out in a cross before climbing into the ring. The multi-time World Champion sneered at a cameraman who got a little too close, then headed to his corner. I HAVE ARRIVED AND THIS TIME YOU SHOULD B-B-BELIEVE THE HYPE! I LISTENED TO EVERYONE AND NOW I KNOW NONE OF THEM WERE RIGHT! I’LL BE THERE FOR YOU AS LONG AS IT WORKS FOR ME! I PLAY A GAME, ITS CALLED IN~SIN~CERITY! Silver sparks flooded the entrance stage as the familiar voice of Trent Reznor screamed. The small drum and bass beat from the beginning of “Starfuckers, Inc” then rumbled through. GOOD MORNING MR. ARGENT, WOULD YOU LIKE SOME K? BOOOO! As the exclusive remix of “Starfuckers, Inc” by Nine Inch Nails got into full flow, the stage started to show signs of activity. I bet you think this song is about you, don’t you? Don’t you? Don’t you? At the entrance way stood a man with his back to the crowd, his head bowed. His arms outstretched. A championship belt in his hand. He wore baggy silver pants, a matching elbow pad on his right arm, and shoulder length bleached blond hair a little longer on one side than the other. AND WHEN I SUCK YOU OFF NOT A DROP WILL GO TO WASTE. ITS REALLY NOT THAT BAD, Y’KNOW, ONCE YOU GET PAST THE TASTE. ASSKISSER, STARFUCKER!! And with that, Jade Argent turned to face the crowd, a savage grin plastered on his face. Argent strutted arrogantly towards the ring, as the his two mammoth valets, Rhyme & Reason, trailed a few steps behind. Bringing up the rear was Argent's agent, Ira Cohn, a nondescript Jewish looking man from New Jersey with short brown hair and a business like suit that suggested he made decent money doing what ever it was that he did. The Australian Opportunist eyed Jacobs every step of the way, even as he stepped through the ropes, leaving his posse on the floor. After the bell sounded, Argent and Jacobs circled each other cautiously, playing over their previous contest that had occurred eight months ago on another continent. After the circling came the mid-ring tie-up. Argent gained the upper hand first, slipping on a standing side headlock. Not that it lasted long, as SVJ easily shoved Jade toward the ropes. Argent rebounded. SUPERSTAR KICK! Denied! Argent spun 180 degrees and strutted toward the ropes, pointing at his head, as Rhyme and Reason applauded his genius. Jade is so SMRT! Jacobs rushed toward the Aussie, but Argent was ready, and the Superstar walked right into an abdominal stretch. Again, this didn't last long, as the wily veteran easily adjusted his position and hiptossed his way free. As Jacobs went to grab Argent, he was greeted by two feet to the face. The Icon landed flat on his back, as Argent scrambled to get vertical and go on offense. It wasn't meant to be, though, as Jacobs slapped Argent's legs out from under him. An elbow drop by Jacobs missed. Then a leg drop attempt by Argent missed. Jacobs charged, but Argent was ready, and took the Superstar down with a drop toe hold. Make that, tried to take Jacobs down. To Argent's surprise, Jacobs was still standing, having slipped free of the attempt, and quickly hooked Argent for the Four Star, Jacobs standing figure four leglock! Counter by Argent into a small package by Argent! One, two, kickout by Jacobs. Both men rolled to their feet and charged toward each other. Argent shot an elbow toward the Superstar's face, but Jacobs simply ducked and waited to catch Argent with a hangman's neckbreaker. As the Aussie cradled his jolted spine, Jacobs was already back on the offense, yanking Argent up for a suplex. In mid-air, however, Argent was able to slip free and land on his feet behind Jacobs. Russian leg sweep! Cover. One! Two! Kick out by Jacobs! Showing his quickness, Argent got vertical first and charged toward the future Hall of Famer, smashing him in the mouth with a spinning heel kick. And then Argent showed off his tremendous technical skills with a choke. Once referee Paige Allen counted to four, Argent released the illegal maneuver, allowing Jacobs to cough and hack as he tried to suck in oxygen. After grabbing a handful of hair, Argent then poked Jacobs in the right eye. After a couple of disrespectful kicks to Jacobs' head, Argent yanked up the former ACW World Champion and nailed him with an inverted atomic drop. Off the ropes, but Jacobs blasted Argent with a clothesline! Argent got up, only to be taken down by a second straight clothesline. Jacobs attempted a third straight clothesline, but this time the Aussie was ready and caught the Icon. Exploder suplex! Cover by Argent. One! Two! SVJ kicked out. The Australian Opportunist quickly skittered toward the apron and waited on Jacobs to get up. After a few moments, Argent sprung into action and lunged at Jacobs, connecting with a flying crossbody block! One! Two! Thr-kickout! Looking to gamble on some more high-risk offense, Argent this time opted to climb the corner buckles as he waited on the Icon to stand. Jacobs, however, used his peripheral vision to figure out what Argent was up to and feigned grogginess, stumbling in Argent's direction. As Argent dove, he didn't have a clue what was in store for him in less than a second. A STAR IS BORN SPINEBUSTER~!! One! Two! Th-KICKOUT! The high-impact spinebuster damn near put Argent through the mat (except, ACW doesn't have to lower itself to such cheap stunts in 2010, hiyoooo!). As Argent struggled to rise, Jacobs was off and running. The Reason There Is A Show coming up! NO! Argent swung wildly and blindly overhead, and was able to break free of the Asai moonsault/inverted DDT attempt as fists connected with Jacobs face, staggering him. Argent quickly spun around. Enzuigiri, missed! With Argent scrambling to get vertical once again, Jacobs charged to the ropes, hoping to hit The Reason There Is A Show this time. "OHHHH!" roared the crowd, as Argent followed Jacobs step for step sneakily, then shoved SVJ face-first over the top rope. In mid-air, Jacobs rotated and was broke the fall to an extent using his right hand on the apron, but his landing ended up quite bad, as the Superstar stumbled and smashed his left arm into the guardrail. As Jacobs regained his bearings and footing, the Australian Opportunist was about to fly. SUICIDE SENTON! The crowd roared, as Argent's body crushed SVJ beneath his own after the successful running dive over the top rope. Argent was about to get up and celebrate his triumph, but he felt a hard tugging at his long hair. A right fist blasted him on the left cheek. Then another. And another and another and another, as Jacobs tried to rearrange Argent's facial features from flat on his backside. Jacobs shoved Argent off of himself and shakily pulled himself up, as Argent yanked himself up with the aid of the ring skirt. By the count of six, Jacobs was back inside. By seven, Argent was slamming his shoulder into Jacobs' midsection. Sunset flip! But Jacobs held onto the top cable so Argent couldn’t pull him over, then Jacobs sat down. One! Both of Jacobs hands grabbed the top rope for added leverage and cheaterage! Two! Jacobs was holding on with every ounce of strength. Three! Didn't come, as referee Paige Allen caught sight of Jacobs cheating in the pinfall attempt and told him to let go and let Argent up. Argent shoved Jacobs. Jacobs punched Argent, a blow that took Argent down to one knee. Jacobs off the ropes. STAR GAZER! The shining wizard blasted the side of Argent's face! One! Two! Thre-KICKOUT!! Jacobs had enough. He signaled for the end, and he was ready to deliver. After dragging Argent to his feet, he put him in suplex position. STAR STRUCK! NO! Argent spun free. SPIT STRUCK! Yes, Argent countered Jacobs spinning suplex DDT with a wad of spit to the face. Jacobs was stunned by the tactic. He wiped the spittle off his face and flicked it at referee Paige Allen, which was enough to distract her so Jacobs could punt Argent's testicles deep into his pelvis. ROLL-UP BY JACOBS! ONE! TWO! (A handful of tights so blatant that Argent inadvertently mooned one side of the audience!) THREE! (Unfortunately for Argent, his ass was on the opposite side of his shoulders, so Allen never saw it.) The bell sounded. Argent was shocked by Vince's blatant cheating. His entire posse protested the disgusting, low down tactics, as Argent rolled out of the ring to release some of his frustration by kicking various objects (the guardrail, some furniture, a ringside photographer...). But tonight, the Icon had gotten the better of Argent, and he was announced the victor. WINNER: 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs via PINFALL. There Will Be Blood His match with Jade Argent was over. But 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs' night had just begun. Within seconds of hearing his name announced as the winner, his role changed from victor to victim. He felt a hand grab his arm, and SVJ assumed it was the referee about to raise his hand. Except, it wasn't the referee's hand raising his arm. Instead, he was spun 180 degrees and came face to face with himself? *SMASH!* Well, actually it was a 20"x48" mirror being smashed to pieces on his skull! Jacobs collapsed to his knees upon impact, more stunned than hurt for the moment, his senses dulled. He didn't hear the fans let out a loud "Ohhhh!" upon seeing the impact, or even understand why he felt like this. All he saw was a pair of black legs before him, one of which was rapidly moving toward his face. KNEETOTHEFACE! Flat on his back now, Jacobs saw the blurry ring lights above his head. Then, a shadowy figure standing over him. It took a few moments before his attacker's identity sunk in. Sharc! Psychopathy Red sat on SVJ's chest and blasted him with right-handed punches to keep the Superstar stunned and flat on his back. Sharc's taped fists were quickly turning pink as Jacobs' blood sunk into the material. Jacobs was a bloody mess. The crowd looked on, a bit sickened by the sight of the Icon's blood spraying off his forehead onto the mat with every furious punch. Sharc grabbed two handfuls of hair and pulled SVJ up into a sitting position, then slowly spun him around, presenting his lacerated face to every side of the audience. Many women had to turn away. Men's eyes were wide, their jaws agape. An eerie silence came over the arena. Once the bloody artwork had been shown off, Sharc violently shoved SVJ's head toward the canvas once again and headed to the floor. He headed under the ring and found a table. Back inside the ring, Sharc rolled SVJ's still body toward the edge of the ring before sliding back out to the floor. Sharc laid SVJ in position on the table. Before going back in the ring, Sharc paused and stared at the bloody stream pouring over SVJ's face. Sharc looked at his bloodstained hands, opened them up, then put both palms down onto SVJ's wounds. After a couple seconds, Sharc raised his blood-soaked hands to his face and wiped Jacobs' blood on his own cheeks, bathing in the blood of his enemy. Re-energized suddenly, Sharc climbed back inside the ring, grabbed hold of the top rope with both hands, and launched himself. CRUNCH!! Suicide senton through the table!! Sharc casually laid on top of Jacobs' bloody and bruised body, having successfully driven him through the table, which broke in such a way that it looked like it exploded like shrapnel. He stared everywhere, yet nowhere. Face blank. Eyes unfocused. Yet, content. Why was this happening? Was this all payback for Jacobs' actions over the past two episodes of Courage? Two weeks ago, the Superstar had walked Sharc's valet, Sarah, into a doorknob and tossed her out of a locker room wearing nothing but a towel. One week later, as she looked for some small amount of payback, Jacobs nailed her with a thunderous Superstar Kick. Sharc ran in to her rescue, then got kicked in the head as well. Referee Paige Allen wasn't concerned with the "why" as she pleaded with Sharc on the floor to go to the back and end this insanity. And she was no longer alone, as she was joined by fellow referees Trent Savage, Tomasz Heinreich, and a pair of suited ACW security guards. Sharc heard all their pleas. "It's over, Sharc." "You don't need to do this." "You're better than this." "Let us help him." They tried every tactic. From his seat on top of Jacobs, Sharc eyed each ACW official one by one. Their pleas went silent as they saw the vacant look in his eyes staring into their own. It was like staring into the eyes of an animal off a fresh kill. Extremely dangerous. They all knew they had to be cautious. Beneath Sharc, SVJ twitched. He stood up, the crowd staring on. Jacobs was no hero to any ACW viewers, but Sharc wasn't doing anything worth cheering about at the moment either. Slowly, Sharc turned his head around to the right and stared down at Jacobs face. The officials all saw it. The snarl. Suddenly, Sharc was back inside the ring, crawling, and looking for… Oh dear god. Broken glass! He found the biggest sliver that remained from the shattered mirror and quickly rolled back out. However, in the few seconds it took for him to find his deadly weapon, the entire officiating crew (and even more security guards) had formed a wall and pulled SVJ out of the wreckage and a bit further away from the broken glass-wielding maniac. "No! No! NO!" they all shouted from their knees, arms extended like crossing guards trying to stop a speeding car heading for an injured child in the crosswalk. Sharc's foot didn't hit the brakes, however. He stepped closer toward the officials. "You do it and you're DONE here, Sharc!" Heinreich warned. "He will NOT hurt her again!" Sharc shouted, as he clutched the shard of glass as if it were a knife. "Wow," a voice suddenly boomed over the speakers. "Ho. Lee. Shit." The fans all turned their eyes toward the entryway as the self-proclaimed Sports Entertainment Icon Trey Vincent walked down the aisle toward the officials and medics attending to the bloody and injured Jacobs. Vincent looked through the wall of striped shirts, getting an eyeful of the bloodied face of the future Hall of Famer, as if it were a wreck on the side of the highway he was passing by. Without much caution for his own safety, Vincent put himself between Sharc and all the officials. "Sharc, buddy, that is ENTERTAINMENT right there, my old friend. How've you been?" No response. Vincent whistled to get Sharc's attention. "Yoo-hoo! Over here, Richie." Sharc's sadistic eyes turned toward TV at last. "You know that's seven years bad luck, yeah?" Vincent said with a smile. After the attempt at breaking the thick tension in the building, Sharc looked down at his right hand. It almost looked like he didn't understand how that glass got in his hand. Letting out a hiss of pain, he dropped the glass onto the protective floor mat and shook a spray of his own blood out. "Sharc, you just impressed the hell out of Trey Vincent. You just obliterated a former World Champion. You just obliterated an Icon. You just obliterated a future Hall of Famer. How'd you like to do that again later tonight?" A quizzical expression came over Sharc's face. The fans, coming out of their shock, booed as they realized exactly where Vincent was taking this. "You see, Sharc…step into my office, why don't you?" Vincent said, pulling himself onto the apron and sitting on the middle ropes, offering Sharc an entrance into the ring. Without much hesitation, Sharc followed TV into the ring and surveyed the blood and broken glass littering canvas. Meanwhile, out on the floor, the medical crew was busy placing a neck brace around Jacobs' head, in preparation of putting him onto a stretcher to get him the hell out of this war zone. And in the ring, Vincent began to sell. "Once upon a time, there was a great and mighty warrior who got screwed over by a one-eyed king. So, naturally, he put out a bounty on the king. It started with one hundred thousand dollars. Then two hundred thousand. Week by week, the stakes increased. The jackpot increased. Eventually, the great and mighty warrior got to tonight. Tonight, we're not playing for pocket change anymore. Tonight, the prize for that king's head? SEVEN HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS!" The camera zoomed in on Sharc's blood-stained face. "Can you imagine such a perfect scenario, Sharc. A few weeks back, you were screwed out of this opportunity. But now, we just pulp fictioned our way back to where it all began. Tonight. Sharc. Versus. ALIAS. ACW World Championship!" And there it was: the smile. "I'm paying you hundreds of thousands of dollars to brutalize a man you despise! All you have to do to collect is win that title and make sure Alias doesn't get to Legends! Then, YOU could be the man to main event the biggest show of the year. You'll write your ticket to the Hall of Fame and immortality. What do you say, Sharc? And holy hell, I'd extend my hand, but you're bleeding like a stuck pig. What do you say, Sharc? Do you accept? Will you fight Alias? Will you DESTROY Alias? Will you be the next ACW World Champion and go on to main event Legends IV against Trey Vincent?" "Yes." The crowd roared, but that's only because the growl of acceptance wasn't Sharc's at all... instead, the growl came from the Original Pulp Hero himself. Glistening gold of the World Championship on his shoulder, heavy bandages around his waist, Alias sneered at the violent display of bloodletting in and around the ring. The one-eyed king walked definitely towards the bloody-fisted wild man in the ring, and the man who would pay him to do to Alias what he had just done to SVJ. As Alias rumbled half way towards the ring, Spanish fans leaning as far over the barriers as possible to touch the Pulp hero, SVJ was escorted past his long-time former nemesis. Alias paused for a moment, looking at SVJ, but he didn't show his hand in terms of emotion... when it came to the carnage done to the Superstar. Picking up pace again, referee Paige Allen who had been trailing behind the medical staff with SVJ, stepped in front of the World Champion. "You don't have to go in there..." The Original Pulp Hero said nothing to Chris Moliano's better half, simply brushing past her and walking to the ringside steps. As he stood on the apron Trey Vincent could only grin a miiile wide, watching as Alias stepped into Sharc's line of sight, having already agreed to fight Sharc. And that wasn't ALL he was going to say. "You will fight me, Sharc." The Pulp Hero growled, not flinching in eyes of the man who already had blood on his hands, "But you will not destroy me. You will not defeat me. You will not take me title... so you can go on to Legends to kick Trey Vincent's ass." TV couldn't help but scowl at that last part. When Sharc beat Alias... he knew who was the prostitute and who was the pimp... right? Right. I know you want to tear into me, Sharc." Alias roared, stepping closer to the one-man bloodbath, "But I don't think you can... because I'm not Vince. I know the bulls-eye that lays on my head, not just because of the money that TV has put on me since January, but because the way I fight and the damned dirty people I fight against." Those last words were almost barked at Trey Vincent, but Alias centered his focus on Sharc what was said next was crystal clear and laced with napalm. Alias took the World title off of his shoulder with his free hand and held it in front of Sharc's face. "Ya don't even have to say yes, Sharc, because your actions already put the wheels in motion for this... before Trey Vincent could say a thing." Sharc had been on the losing end of every one-on-one match during their career, and tonight the stakes would be even higher than usual. Would this be the night Sharc ends the dominance? VICTORY ROLL! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! "YEAHHH!!!" roared the crowd! This time there was no kickout. Just the sounds of insanely happy ACW fans screaming their lungs out as the bell sounded and "Sympathy for the Devil" kicked into gear to signal yet another victory for the man who will likely go down as the greatest ACW World Champion of all-time. This didn't make Trey Vincent happy. It made him so unhappy, in fact, that the Sports Entertainment Icon charged down the aisle, slid into the ring, and began stomping on Alias before he could even hear his name announced as the winner of the match. P.S. Cut that Rolling Stones music. And then came the boos. Sharc was back up, scowling, as the realization sunk in. He had just lost to Alias. Again. Sharc clawed his way over to Alias and began choking the World Champion while Vincent put the boots to him. The two on one assault continued for about thirty seconds before the crowd absolutely erupted. KEITH SCOTT ZIMMERMAN HAD ARRIVED! The Only Wrestler That Matters hit the ring. Sharc was first to see him and blindly charged right into: HIT MY MUSIC~! Upon impact, Zimmerman gritted his teeth and grabbed at his knee, which Vincent had battered not too long ago. Vincent's head spun around, as he realized KSZ had arrived. Before KSZ could even get back up to his feet, Vincent bailed out of the ring. Zimmerman pulled himself up with the aid of the ropes, as he stared a hole through TV. Vincent and Zimmerman shouted at each other and did some finger wagging, but whatever they said was drowned out by the crowd. Meanwhile, Sharc rolled to the floor, leaving KSZ as the last man standing in the ring. That is, until the Original Pulp Hero's hand shot up and grabbed the middle rope. Then the top rope. KSZ spun around. Alias. Keith Scott Zimmerman. Tonight, KSZ had saved Alias from a beating. The second time tonight that he had saved a respected member of the ACW community. But in just one week, Keith Scott Zimmerman would be the next man to try and dethrone the ACW World Champion and collect on the bounty. If history was any guide, the bounty would jump another $100,000, which would mean KSZ could walk away with the ACW Title and $800,000! However, Trey Vincent would be the special guest referee. "Next week," KSZ mouthed in Alias' direction, before pointing at the gold championship that sat at the Original Pulp Hero's feet. As it was, they had seen each other again tonight after all. With Trey Vincent looking on from the side, Alias and Zimmerman's staredown continued. "Next week," Alias said with a nod, before wiping a handful of blood of his face and snatching the title off the mat. Clutching the ACW World Championship with his right hand, he launched the gold title into the air, which made the crowd roar. The fans couldn't wait for next week to come. Because next week, two of the greatest champions in the history of this business will collide on Courage 109. Don't you dare miss it! WINNER: Alias via PINFALL; retains ACW World Title.
