Kassidy Payne Biography Real Name Kassidy Michael [name withheld] Hometown Pikeville, KY Birthday July 30th, 1981(age 29) Place of Birth Pikeville, KY Height 6'2 Weight 270 Blood Type AB- Eye Color Brown Biography Alignment and Personality: Gruff-and-Tumble face. A generally good-natured man until you piss him off. One of the boys as it were. While he’s the younger of the two, he’s stronger and often times smarter. He’ll often play along with KC’s plans and general bullshit. Absolutely loves a good fight every now and then. Description: Built like a tank. Long dirty brown/blonde hair. Strong nose. Small brown eyes. Red box trunks with white lining on the outside, “PAYNE GANG” on the back in Rockwell Extra Bold font. Crimson kneepads and boots. Red thick left elbowpad with white backing. Other Feds ELW Other Fed Titles ELW Tag Team Champions x9 Other Fed Awards ELW Tag Team of the Year 2007, 2008 Team The Payne Gang w/KC Payne Wrestling Style Kinetic brawler. At times, his wrestling style could actually be likened to The Ultimate Warrior. Not so much the crazy, face-painted spaceship-toting, queer-hating aspect, but the fact that he is a goddamn freight train, almost always in motion. Because of this, he’s usually the hot tag. His style is a lot less meticulous and more simple than KC, but he can and will fuck you up with sheer strength alone. During double teams, he’s usually a perch or the set-up man for KC’s flying moves. Finisher Move The Styles Clash (belly to back inverted mat slam, if you're into that technical sort of thing) Finisher Description The Great Payne Special Moves Snap Scoop Powerslam Various stalling suplexes (nothing too complicated, vertical, back, so forth) Punches to face and midsection (southpaw) Short-arm Clothesline Sidewalk Slam Running Shoulder Block Big Splash Body Avalanche Military Press Slam (can press weight in excess of 300 pounds. SCARY shit)\ Running Corner Back Elbow Classic Airplane Spin Bionic Elbow Three Point Tackle 2-5 Signature Moves: Kentucky Stampede (yup, just a renamed Oklahoma Stampede) STO Overhead Double Choke Suplex Bearhug Entrance Music 'Deliverance' by Corrosion of Conformity Trainer The Everlasting School of Excellence; Eli Brown
