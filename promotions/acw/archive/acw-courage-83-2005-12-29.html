And as he wriggled desperately in Coral’s arms, trying to escape, Seymour knew what was coming. Avalon knew it too. Seymour had erred, and now, he would make the number one contender pay for that error, shifting Seymour in his arms so that he could land the basic, entry-level backbreaker, driving his knee into the small of Seymour’s back. Of course, considering it was Coral executing it, Seymour’s body broke into convulsions from impact, kicking and screaming on the canvas. The proverbial levee had now burst. The deluge was imminent, as when Coral Avalon was involved, one backbreaker had a propensity to become many. As if to prove my silly analogy correct, he grabbed Seymour in an inverted facelock, getting ready to drop the Final Fantasy with a Dragon Backbreaker. Seymour, on the other hand, wasn’t quite so willing to let his back take another devastating blow. He threw a series of arcing hammer fists up at Coral’s head, finally forcing a break of the inverted facelock. Avalon was met with a quick back kick to the midsection, and Seymour took advantage, spinning around to hook Coral’s head and drive him to the canvas with a lightning fast DDT. It was Seymour’s biggest offensive move of the canvas, and he elected to cover after it. ONE! TW--KICKOUT! Almasy barely got one and a half out of the DDT, as Coral simply kicked out. He hadn’t taken as much abuse as Seymour had so far, not by a long shot. Of course, that was a state that Seymour hoped to rectify. Coral was the recipient of a pair of chops to the chest, followed by Seymour trying an Irish whip. Avalon reversed, however, and Seymour was sent helplessly into the ropes, bouncing back into Coral’s waiting arms. Avalon promptly snatched Seymour spinning him through the air before driving him down across the knee. Quebradora con Hilo! Or, for the non-Spanish inclined, TILT-A-WHIRL BACKBREAKER! The Final Fantasy let out an agonized gasp, but Coral wasn’t finished. He wanted to drive the rest of the air out of Seymour, and for that, a slightly different tactic was required. Coral scooped Seymour up over his shoulder in a fireman’s carry, and simply pushed forward, falling to his back and jamming both knees into Seymour’s abdomen with a modified gut buster. With Seymour gasping like a fish on dry land, Coral figured it was a pretty good time to cover. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! In spite of Seymour’s lack of air, he was able to shoot a shoulder up off the canvas. “CO-RAL! CO-RAL! CO-RAL!” The Kleptomaniac’s supporters came out in full force, but Avalon was too focused to acknowledge them save a small fist pump. Coral picked Seymour up, and clapped on a quick front facelock, before taking Seymour over and snap-suplexing him out of his boots. Working quickly, Avalon rolled the smaller man over to his stomach, locking in a camel clutch. Seymour desperately looked for a way out, as Coral expertly pulled back on Seymour’s chin, wrenching away at the neck and the back. Seymour, thankfully, managed to retain his arms, having evaded Coral’s attempt to bind them, and pushed up to hands and knees. Using all of the force that he could muster, Almasy surged upwards, carrying Coral Avalon on his back for several steps before driving him back into a corner, breaking the clutch. Almasy fired off a few back elbows, in an effort to keep Avalon off of him. Seymour turned, popping Coral with forearm smashes to the face, before boosting him up to a seat on the top rope. Seymour ascended in turn, looking out at the fans as he stood on the top rope, but he couldn’t waste time with waving to them now, or else Avalon would likely hurt him. Top rope Frankensteiner! Coral found himself snapped off the top rope head over heels, landing hard on his back in the middle of the ring. Seymour didn’t follow up with a cover, though. No, he knew that one move, as impressive as it was, would not be enough to take down Coral Avalon. He needed…another move. With THAT thought firmly in mind, Seymour moved to the outside, climbing up to the top rope as quickly as he could. He measured Coral carefully, before flipping off the top, stretching himself out so he would land full force on Avalon’s abdomen. Somersault Senton! From there, Seymour simply grabbed a leg, hooking it back and waiting for the count. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! Seymour sighed at the sight of Coral’s shoulder shooting off the canvas. It meant that Avalon was far, FAR from being done. The Final Fantasy decided that he was NOT letting his foot off the gas pedal, pouncing on Avalon as he rolled over to his stomach. Seymour grabbed an arm, scissoring it, and then cupped both hands under the chin, pulling back with his variant of the Crippler Crossface. It must be noted that Seymour was no submissionist. Thus, his method of pulling back on the chin to increase torque was suspect, a fact that was not lost on the Kleptomaniac. “OWWWWW!” Seymour let out a scream as Coral bent back one of his fingers, forcing Almasy to release the crossface. It was a perfectly legal technique, of course, but something that Seymour had never thought of. That was why it worked. The right arm of Coral was still scissored, but Avalon corrected this with a series of left handed chops to the temple. He surged to his feet quickly, gut wrenching Seymour up over his shoulder, before dropping quickly to the canvas with a Seated Canadian Backbreaker! COVER BY AVALON! ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! “SEY-MOUR! SEY-MOUR! SEY-MOUR!” With the pressure being poured on, the Almasy fans in attendance let their presence be felt, cheering on the #1 contender. Coral picked Seymour back up off the canvas, choosing to Irish whip Seymour into the ropes rather than follow with another backbreaker. Avalon ROCKED Seymour with another hard European uppercut. Almasy’s head snapped back from the impact, but Coral grabbed it and pulled it right back into the path of another devastating uppercut. This time, Seymour’s body spun 180 degrees from the force, exposing his weakened back to Coral. The Kleptomaniac damn near salivated. LUNGBLOWER! Coral fell back, driving both knees into Almasy’s back. Seymour recoiled, staggering face-first into the corner, one hand reached behind him to try and ascertain the damage. Avalon gave him no time, hooking Seymour up for a belly to back suplex, before shifting mid-move to DRIVE Seymour over his knee with a Back Suplex Backbreaker! With Almasy writhing on the canvas, Coral covered, hooking the near leg. ONE! TWO! THR--FOOT ON THE ROPE! At roughly two-and-a-half, Seymour managed to get his foot on the ropes. Too late, Avalon realized the mild error he had made in not dragging Seymour away from the ropes. Wasn’t like Coral was TERRIBLY concerned with this problem, however. He was still in firm command of the match, and Seymour was hurting from his back’s erstwhile love affair with Avalon’s knee. The Final Fantasy slowly fought his way back up, as Avalon circled at a distance, waiting for the right time to strike. Once Seymour pushed himself up to unsteady feet, Mr. Avalon decided that now was as good of a time as any. Running Enzugiri! Ducked by the #1 Contender! Moments after Coral landed on his face, Seymour was already pulling the former Action! Bantam Champion back up. He wasn’t quite sure what he’d do when he got Coral there, but it seemed like a good idea. Fortunately for the Final Fantasy, an idea came to him. He hooked both arms in a double chickenwing, gritted his teeth, and prepared for extreme pain. TIGER SUPLEX! WITH THE BRIDGE! ONE! … Quick quiz for you ACW fans. How are Seymour Almasy and London similar? I’ll give you time. Okay, I lied. Your answer is.. They both have bridges that fell down. Seymour lay on the mat, disgusted with himself that he couldn’t grit the pain out for two more seconds, or at least long enough to force Avalon to expend energy kicking out of the pinning predicament. Coral, for his part, grabbed his neck. Tiger Suplexes weren’t fun things to take, after all, but as much pain as his neck was giving him right now, he knew Seymour’s back was in far worse shape. It was only a matter of time now, really. The longer this contest went, the more it favored Coral Avalon. The newcomer to ACW moved towards a kneeling, battered Seymour, intent on finishing him off, but Almasy would not say die. Chop! Chop! Chop! Three knife-edge chops, delivered from Seymour’s knees, were enough to stagger Coral. Almasy lunged forward, driving a makeshift head butt into Avalon’s abdomen, doubling him over long enough for Seymour to struggle to his feet, double-underhook Avalon’s arms, and connect with a legsweep DDT. ILLUMINA! Roaring out pain through clenched teeth, Seymour dragged himself to Coral, flipped the bigger man over, and through himself into a cover. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT! The Final Fantasy grimaced. Only a two, for one of his best tactics. He knew deep down that he needed to end this thing quickly. His spine couldn’t take too many more backbreakers. And so Seymour picked Coral back up. He made sure to let his legs do most of the heavy lifting as he scooped Avalon up over his shoulders. Deciding not to put any more pressure on his back by running, he simply hooked Coral, sending him to the canvas with the GAGAZET DRIVER! Adrenaline poured through the Final Fantasy’s body as he picked Avalon back up one more time. It was time for the coup de grace, something he could use to finish off Coral. Had he known Coral better, he would have understood why it seemed to be an ESPECIALLY good choice. The brainbuster and Coral Avalon were not good friends. He’d lost three matches to the damned thing in the space of two months once. And Seymour had a pretty damn good brainbuster, as they went. LEVEL! FIVE! BRAINBUSTER! … CORAL FALLS ON TOP! ONE! TWO! THRE--NOOO! Que? Well, just as Seymour managed to get Avalon vertical for head-spiking goodness, his back gave out, and Avalon fell on top, damn near winning the match right there. Seymour fans looked on with eyes half closed. It was unlikely things were going to go well from here. Victory was in sight for the Kleptomaniac. All he had to do now was reach out and take it. And as he picked Seymour Almasy to his feet, it seemed that victory was one move away. HALF-NELSON BACKBREAKER! Ah, that should do nicely. Coral covered, cinching a leg, ready to hear the sweet sound of palm on canvas. Once. Twice. Thri--no. To put it mildly, Avalon was in shock. Seymour’s shoulder had squeaked off the canvas a fraction of a second before the official could make palm meet mat one more time. But still, he didn’t waste time with frivolous activities like complaining to “Average” Joe Hill, or pounding the mat. Instead, he continued trying to *gasp*…WIN THE MATCH. Seymour winced as Coral picked him up, trying an Irish whip. Somehow, Seymour reversed, throwing himself into the opposite ropes. There was one shot left, he figured, and he had to take it. As Avalon came back from the Irish whip, Seymour threw himself into a somersault, heel aimed for Coral’s head. ETHER STRIKE! The devastating koppou kick, newly added to Seymour’s arsenal, caught Coral Avalon between the eyes. Almasy crawled, desperately, managing to throw himself across Coral and hook the leg, for what could well be his last attempt at victory. ONE! TWO! THR--NOOO! KICKOUT! So close, and yet, so far. Seymour felt like crying as he saw Avalon’s shoulder shoot off the canvas. His body was racked with pain, and he didn’t know how much more he had left. He slowly wobbled to his feet, staggering around like a drunken man, trying to get his bearings. Avalon may have had the imprint of a heel in his forehead, and he may have been knocked goofy, but he still saw Seymour ripe for the picking. Showing the resolve f a champion, he simply grabbed Seymour’s ankles, pulling them out from under Seymour and making the #1 contender land flat on his face. From there, Coral simply picked Seymour up in a wheelbarrow position, wrapping both arms around his waist. It didn’t take a rocket scientist to know that Seymour was in serious trouble. WHEELBARROW SUPLEX! Coral, however, didn’t release. Using all of the force that he could muster after the draining contest, he rolled through the suplex, pulling himself and the Final Fantasy back up. Face turning purple with exertion, he managed to hoist Almasy in the air again. SECOND WHEELBARROW SUPLEX! The veins in Avalon’s neck sprung forth as he WILLED Almasy back up, one more time Seymour, however, managed to grab onto the middle rope with both hands, his deathgrip preventing Coral from executing a third. Finally, he had no choice but to throw Almasy’s lower body down. Before he could react, though, Seymour jammed a mule kick to Coral’s abdomen. He recognized that he’d used similar counters, mainly because Avalon’s style entailed striking from behind. No time to think about that, though. He had a match to win. Spinning sitout double-underhook facebuster. THE WINGS OF VALMAR One problem, though. Seymour was done. He flopped to his back after hitting the move, rolling to his stomach in an effort to stave off any attempt by Coral to cover him. Of course, escaping Coral Avalon wasn’t quite that easy. Avalon eyed the top turnbuckle, seeing in it the way to put Almasy away. Seymour was barely mobile, after all. This was Avalon’s time, his chance. Pulling a barely coherent Seymour back up, Coral placed him on the top turnbuckle, beginning an ascent up the turnbuckle…backwards. The reason for this was immediately clear, as he began to pull Seymour up into a fireman’s carry, in preparation for the devastating super gut buster off the top. Almasy attempted to block this with elbows to the head, but these had little, if any impact. Avalon steadied himself, pushing Seymour up in the air, as he threw both himself and the Final Fantasy off the top. Seymour flew aimlessly for a few seconds, before attempting his one, final gambit, Twisting desperately, he managed to get a hand on the back of Coral’s head, and then a second, pushing Avalon to a similar, horizontal position as he was now. And then, gravity continued taking effect. Both men crashed, face-first, but Avalon flipped over onto his back from the impact of the fall. Seymour landed about a foot away, and even in utter agony, he could see his downed opponent. With the last of his energy, Seymour threw an arm across the Kleptomaniac. ONE! TWO! THREEE!!!! Mercifully for Seymour, it was over. But even as Joe Hill raised Seymour’s arm in the air, he knew that he’d been lucky as Hell. It had been a lucky bounce that allowed Seymour to get the pinfall, a desperate move made seconds away from being finished off. Sure, Seymour had won. But considering the way the match had played itself out, the #1 contender knew that a silver bullet had been dodged. Winner > Seymour Almasy via pinfall
