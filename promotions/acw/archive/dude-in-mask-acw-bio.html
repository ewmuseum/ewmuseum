Dude In Mask Biography Real Name Unknown, has been known to answer sometimes to “Dude. Nicknames THE Dude, Just Some Dude, Duder, Dude-A-Mania!, The Dudester, Some Dude in a Mask, other horrible variations of such a name Hometown Because he is a proud man who allegedly forgets where he hails from, he’s kind of just rolled along with wherever he happens to wrestle for that cheap pop. So he’s announced from the same city/town/country where the show takes place. Birthday Unknown, rumored to be mid-to-upper twenties. Place of Birth Also unknown Height 6'3 Weight 231 and 7/16th pounds Blood Type Might as well by B+ He seems to always be positive. Har har har. Eye Color Brown (wears contacts with question marks in them) Biography He is a guy. In a mask. He isn’t somebody that’s hiding under a mask moniker because of some secret shame, he isn’t some guy that’s hideous or got crippled or his parents died at a young age, he is simply a guy. A dude, if you will. With a serious bout of Adult ADD at times. But what he IS is fun to watch, unpredictable, and sometimes crazy to the point where it may involve multiple officials to restrain him and/or possibly causing property damage. Basically, he is ACW’s answer to masked warriors of the ring like ROH’s El Generico or CHIKARA’s Delirious, Frightmare, or Hallowicked (or really, half of CHIKARA’s colorfully masked roster) He has spent a vast majority of time perfecting his craft across several different independent promotions, based out of Mexico, Germany, Japan, Italy, the US, among others. Honing his craft all over the world, he has now found his home in All-Star Championship Wrestling, looking to wow a much bigger audience than what he’s been accustomed to. Personality: Happy-go-lucky, carefree personality. Not much of a talker beyond simple speech patterns where it seems he’s not listening (“Huh?” “What?” “Zuh?” and that annoying grunting sound people make that sounds like “I dunno” without actually saying it.) and almost has a code of silence that he won't break. Will do just about anything to get a laugh or reaction from the crowd. Total showboat in the ring, it’s very clear he loves what he does, not worrying too much about championships, wins/losses, and political wrestling BS. A true wrestling fan at heart. Other Feds Various organizations overseas. Competed in the US, Mexico, Japan, Italy, Germany, among other places Other Fed Titles Various other titles not worth mentioning. Past Managers Midget in Mask (He's living somewhere in a masked midget retirement home) Wrestling Style A blend of several high-flying/grappling influences. Specializes in Americanized Lucha Libre/independent style and Llave, a Puro/Lucha combination submission-based mat wrestling style popularized in Toryumon. If nothing else, he flies around like it’s going out of fucking style. Finisher Move 1) Dude, That Hurt A LOT 2) The Dude to Air Missile Finisher Description 1) Lifting Single Underhook DDT 2) ing Splash, extends his body out like a missile before impacting with the splash Special Moves Rapid-fire pinfall/wrestling hold sequences Liger Series (Mexican Surfboard, Romero Chinlock, Mexican Surfboard/Dragon Sleeper combo) Rolling Elbow Smash Flying Forearm Lungblower Sweet-ass Dropkick (yep, it’s really called that, has a really good snap, can also do top rope Sweet-ass Dropkicks) Snap Suplex Springboard Back Elbow Rounding Asai Moonsault (like HBK) Enzuigiri (either leg feed or no-hands) Diving Crossbody Top Rope Spinning Wheel Kick RVD Stepover Spin Kick High Angle Belly-to-Back Suplex Fireman’s Carry Flapjack Trademark: Dude, That Hurt! (Running Pump Kick, good closefall maneuver) Dude, That's Some Jukin' And Jivin' (crowd-popping move, three left jabs followed by a split and a discus punch to the face) The Dude-driver (Pumphandle Face-first driver) Dude’s Tied Up In Knots (Octopus Stretch/submission finisher) Something Else Clever That Has The Word “Dude” (Spinning Headlock Elbow Drop) Dude, That Move Really Pulled The Room Together (Reverse Tornado DDT out of the corner, only used on opponents same size or more) Entrance Music 'Glory' by Sugar Ray Trainer Unknown
