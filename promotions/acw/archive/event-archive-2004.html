All-Star Championship Wrestling 2004 EVENTS In 2004, ACW was resurrected and brought back to television screens all over the world, promising a mix of the glory days and new talent. A degree of success was achieved, but almost as if destiny was against the company, more troubles with the network arose. Hard work and solid participation wasn't quite enough in the end, as ACW barely survived in the turbulent environment that was mainstream programming. The future is still uncertain, that is for sure.
