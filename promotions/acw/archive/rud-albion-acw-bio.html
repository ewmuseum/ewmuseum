Rud Albion Biography Real Name Rudyard Okafor (named Rudyard after 'The Jungle Book' author Rudyard Kipling) Nicknames England's Own, The Bronze Lion, The Brummie Bulldog, The Kingdom's Keeper, The One, The Only Hometown Birmingham, England Birthday August 28th, 1982 Place of Birth Birmingham, England Height 6'0 Weight 223 Biography The son of a Nigerian doctor and a former Bollywood starlet, Rud's road to fame in the British wrestling federation OWW wasn't the straightest line to superstardom. Beginning as the masked Indian wrestler Rajput Pabuji, Rud was given a chance to wrestle not only as himself but also for his home country, after two things happened. He had inexplicably gained popularity in the Middle East, and the stalwart superstar for Great Britain, one of it's biggest names, "Britain's Best" James West was injured. Though some controversy initially followed, a group of promoters supported Rud's 'debut'... and by the summer of 2001, he was flying high. Especially in the UK, reaching across cultural barriers. Sadly, after September of 2001, the United States soured to Albion. For the first half of 2002 he was even replaced by the "Sloane Ranger" Daniel Smythe, the man who had been James West's injury replacement in the past. The tables had seemingly returned on Albion, as he had won the card position over Smythe, after the retirement of "Britain's Best". It actually took until mid-2004, after regaining his spot within OWW in the latter half of 2002, and constant threats away from the UK, to turn heel. Eventually the naturally charismatic "Bronze Lion" was once again accepted world-wide as one of Britain's most reliable bastions of truth. All he's trying to do, is hold true to himself and where he came from. Albion is the warrior, the seemingly everyman underdog representing The Kingdom. As long as they let him, at least. Other Feds ACW, OWW Other Fed Titles ACW Tag Team Championship (w/ Buddy Saxson), OWW World Champion Past Teams & Groups England's Best (w/ Buddy Saxson); The Huntsmen (w/Khristain Keller, God's Forgotten Son, WAR and Jesse Ramey) Special Moves Stacked Albion - (Straightjacket Piledriver) Red Coat - (Double-underhook facebuster) Lion's Roar - (Butterfly hold before locking his legs around the opponents waist) Dead Yampy - Topé Suicida The One And Only PUNCH - Marks an X and then puts a line down the middle for the Union Jack before coming off the rope with a top-rope fist drop Entrance Music "Shot By Both Sides" by Magazine
