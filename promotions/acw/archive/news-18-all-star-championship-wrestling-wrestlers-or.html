All-Star Championship Wrestling Wrestlers Ordered To Pose

All-Star Championship Wrestling Wrestlers Ordered To Pose As part of Hillary Duncan's plan to get ACW more 'out-there' to the public, yesterday she ordered all ACW staff into the Headquarters for some new promo shots, and it seems the first to come out with new shots is Tyson Osario, one of the new stars in ACW. After being asked what his first promo shooting was like, T.O. had this to say; 'Why they pick me? Look at me.' T.O. smiles and continues, 'Ain't no person up in A C Dub that can touch me, in the ring, or out of it. I'm just too cute.' When questioned about the shoot, Tyson furthered his comment, 'Everything went really well…they made me tie my…you know… behind my… anyways… apparently it was causing the lens to blur early? Hell… I knew I was hung, but daaaamn…' 'This one chick, she couldn't stop staring. Think her name was Hilary? Yeah, she liked the Rising Star.' When questioned about his relationship with billionaire heir Winston Smith Junior Tyson retorted… 'It was something I regret, and something I got to do to pay my dues. Hell, I wish I'd have taken the other choice, carting about this foo'.' I tried to interrogate further… 'It was some shit I did at a time of desperation, and was given two choices… one was have Winston as my manager… the other… the other, something else.' Obviously hitting a nerve, I sidetracked slightly, asking him how he felt he was settling into ACW, and being touted as the 'Rising Star', 'You know… AC Dub's about to watch something very special, ya see… I'm confident in my abilty, and myself… watch my stock rise…watch me rise… and watch this nigga become a someone. Now can you dig that? Su….' Tyson smirked into the camera, 'Feel that, my brother,' he finally beamed as Mr. Osario declined to comment any further. Draw your own conclusions ladies and gentlemen. Will ACW push Tyson Osario? Will Tyson Osario push Tyson Osario? What is his relationship with Winston Smith? How big is it really? And will the 'Rising Start' of ACW, rise to the big occasion? We shall soon see.
