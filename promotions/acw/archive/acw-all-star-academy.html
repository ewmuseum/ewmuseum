The academy of tomorrow. The Tale of the Tape ACW, being in the business of developing new stars, has always had some sort of training facility since its inception way back when, and especially ever since the modern era kicked off in 2002. Through the organisation's ups and downs, there has always been hope in the development of future superstars. In 2009, after a hiatus of two years, ACW returned from dormancy. And with it, came the All-Star Academy with a ton of promise and a masterplan to boot. After months of work, ACW decided to consider the All-Star Academy something of a branch-off organisation and treated it as its own entity. Thereby, in October of 2009, the All-Star Academy added a new element to the mix: an event called SHOWCASE. Each episode would be treated as a testing ground for the development of the students in various roles, packaged together with the enhancement of the in-ring ability and microphone skills being the top priority. After all, the All-Star Academy is not called the academy of tomorrow for fun. ACW considers the All-Star Academy to be a serious investment, and it shows with the amount of work that's dedicated to each episode of SHOWCASE. So, they might not be polished superstars or wizards of the microphones -- but the warriors of the All-Star Academy have a point to prove. They belong, and they want to entertain the heck out of you! In his new capacity as Chief of the Academy, ICU resurrected the United States Title during the first episode of the second season of SHOWCASE. ICU, who was the very first holder of the title belt when it was initially introduced during the beginning of ACW's television age (way back in 2002), decreed that the United States Title would serve as the main title belt of the All-Star Academy and hopefully produce Champions that would go on and do great things. As of 28 February 2010, the holder of the United States Title is none other than Jack Harris. BACKSTORY: Dick Zhirkov is an experienced referee who has been plying his trade in some of the top independent promotions around the world. Impartial and firm, Dick has been charged with upholding the law in the Academy Of Tomorrow, and he plans to make sure that every match under his control gets called right down the middle. Dick is a consummate professional, who will surely be an asset to the All-Star Academy. BACKSTORY: American-born Chinese who tossed aside tradition for all the Western pop culture he could get his hands on, Kevin Chang is what you call a die-hard wrestling fan. He has embraced his fandom of the sport to the point of becoming a referee, just so that he could have a front-view pass to his favourite past-time in the whole wide world. Belinda Decroix; REFEREE DATE OF BIRTH: 28 October 1987 BIRTHPLACE: Montreal, Quebec, Canada RESEIDENCE: Montreal, Quebec, Canada VITAL STATS: Five foot and seven inches; 117 lbs. BACKSTORY: Daughter of a man who had wrestled all over the world for 20 years, Belinda Decroix found herself immersed in the industry from a very young age. After deciding she didn't have any other passion, Belinda followed in the family tradition and ended up becoming a referee. She's young, doing part-time studies, a bit naive in the ways of the world, and a stunning knock-out that'll have your pants a little bit tighter every time you see her. Belinda Decroix may only have her mind on her career and her studies, but every wrestler she comes across will have both eyes on her arse. Hammond Jones; REFEREE DATE OF BIRTH: 22 December 1969 BIRTHPLACE: Detroit, Michigan, USA RESEIDENCE: Detroit, Michigan, USA VITAL STATS: Six foot and one inch; 220 lbs. BACKSTORY: Former wrestler and superstar of Malboro State Wrestling from 1993 to 1999, Hammond Jones endured one too many injuries that saw his wrestling career end in 2004. Since then, he has dedicated his life to training young upstarts and also contributed back to the industry by becoming a referee. Still as uncouth as when he was a wrestler, however, Hammond has a natural affinity for the burgeoning bad guys, and is not above giving them little nudges in the right direction. SHOWCASE Season One ran nine episodes, from 14 October to 27 December 2009; Season Two five, from 20 February to 14 March 2010.
