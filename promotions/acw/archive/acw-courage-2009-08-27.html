ARC III - Requiem 27th August 2009 Recorded LIVE! from Montreal, Quebec, Canada Card subject to change without notice INTRODUCTION There was an eerie silence backstage in the ACW-Arena, as the collection of the whole roster stood in the loading bay. Unbeknownst to them they were all here for a reason, and that reason now stood in front of them. “Listen up…” SilverHAWK. “I’ve called you all here today for one reason and for one reason only.” HAWK scanned the floor and seen his roster. They were all here, and that made his stomach flicker… but he couldn’t show it. HAWK the Boss was now standing in front of them. But wait… “Where the fuck is Danger?” Noises could be heard from around the group… people looking around their shoulder for the ACW World Champion until the group parted in the middle, Marcus the Smark motioning forward through the crowd. “Um… he is going to be a little late tonight Mr. HAWK, but I’ll make sure he hears all of this.” Marcus then pulled a Dictaphone from his pocket and purposely showed everyone how he was pressing the record button… and then smiled as he ushered his way back into the middle of the group. “I’ll deal with him later.” HAWK looked to his left and right as Joe Bishop and Jimmy Gonz stood at his side, he felt stronger with them in his mind… with them in his life. “ACW right now is lagging far behind every other promotion you all know about… and that’s my fault.” A murmur from the group was probably an agreement to that statement. “My head hasn’t been in the game… it’s been other places and for that I’m sorry but I can’t do anything about that now. What is done is done. But what I can do is take my fed back.” HAWK grew from each spoken word… like it was feeding him energy. “That’s right… this is my fed. The Roy Boys have no doubt done a lot for us here, no doubt, and they will continue to be smart and assured to make sure their investment is not a wasted one. But as long as they pick the destination… I’ll be the one steering the ship.” HAWK once again looked around at the wealth of talent at his disposal… and also of the talent that has left. “That’s right we have had a few walk because they didn’t agree with what was going on, and I wish them all the best for the future but sooner rather than later I want them to be asking themselves if they made the right choice. Did they leave a wasting away federation… or did they leave just as we started to aim for the peak? You all have a job to do here… you all get to do what you love and what you do to have fun in that ring and around here, and I’m sure very soon the Roy Boys will be making an announcement about our future but I want you all to know one thing. ACW will not die. It will never die. Fuck the reason it came back is because people were asking about it again. The people want this place. The people respect this place for what it has achieved. We aren’t the richest. We aren’t the flashiest. And we sure as hell don’t have the best photographers.” HAWK took a deep breath. “But what we do have is the core. We have a core like no-other in this business and we have guys who are soon going to be in that very same core. I’ve no doubt that we are going to see some changes… but they will all be for the better. After the Wake things are going to change… some of you may like it, some of you may not. All I can ask from you all. Every single one of you out there… is to give us a chance.” HAWK looked around at his men… all of whom now had their fists balled and were pumped and ready to go, and in the final, symbolic moment SilverHAWK raised his right fist into the air. “Now lets give these fuckers something to think about!” I Bid You Farewell It had been awhile since SilverHAWK opened Courage. But not that long that he didn't remember what to do. "Let me hear you Montreal!" His attempts at giving the crowd an injection of cheer fell on a number of deaf ears as the half empty arena stood on its feet... mostly. "There have been a number of rumours floating around ACW in recent weeks... and frankly a lot of bullshit has been spoken about this place." HAWK was stern in his words as he paced the top of the walkway. "Number one, ACW is not closing." Even though it was a half empty arena, it still blew the shaky roof off as HAWK calmed the fears of many of the shareholders and its fans. "Number two, we have lost a number of superstars over the past couple of weeks, this is true." Names such as Kelly Masters, Frank Dylan James and a few more had left. "But that doesn't mean that we won't be replacing them with even better superstars!" Rumours of fWo stars and a former PIW Legends were swirling around the arena the whole night. "But the biggest news of all... Montreal. Is that we here at ACW, will be embarking on a Canadian tour after the Wake." A mixed reaction from the crowd told HAWK all he needed to know. "Now let me get this straight right now... this isn't because of you guys sitting here. Hell no. You guys are the reason we are able to do this! But nobody, myself or the Roys ever imagined that the ACW relaunch would go as well as it has done. Nobody. And with that means that we can show our talent all over the Country... and maybe, just one day, all over North America. After the Wake we will be hitting places like Nova Scotia, Saskatchewan and many more... but in December we will be returning right here for the very next ACW Supershow!" So what I want you all do to is make as much noise as you can tonight for these guys... as they will be giving it their all. And at the Wake... we go off with a motherfucking bang!" HAWK passed the microphone to the nearest staff member and then lifted his hands and dropped them in a 'we're not worthy' type motion, before heading off to the back to sort out the rest of the show. Little did he know, his night was soon going to go from bad... to worse. Rise to the Throne At the ringside area, the fans were on their feet as the camera cut to ring announcer Tommy Vale. Next to him, was a silver podium with the brand-spanking new ACW Tag Team Titles which, after a very grueling series of matches that saw it narrowed down from a mass selection of tag teams down to two, The Heirs of Wrestling and The First Airbourne would contest for. With the titles resting comfortably, Vale nodded to the crowd before he began, looking happy as ever. “Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome to the ring at this time, one half of the ACW Tag Team Title match at the Wake and one of Montreal’s own…” He had to pause. The crowd reaction was about to send the roof flying. “…The First Airbourne… Mach 2 and ANDY SHARP!” GROUND CONTROL, WE HAVE LIFT-OFF IN FIVE… FOUR… THREE… TWO… ONE… “Touch the Sky” by Kanye West. A pair of spotlights shined over the All-Star Arena as bright flashes of red and blue lights flashed in strobes over the entryway. Out form the curtains, both decked in street clothes, both members of the First Airbourne were almost taken aback by the sounds that only 2500 people could make, feeling more like twenty-five thousand at least. Andy and Mach 2 took some time to high-five each of the audience on the way down to the aisle. Taking in this tremendous reception, Andy actually climbed over the barricade and into the crowd, hugging and high-fiving some fans near the area. This had been his home for the last six months and now that it was announced that ACW was going to go on tour across Canada for an extended period of time, this was a bittersweet homecoming for him. Mach 2 rolled into the ring and let his mentor have his day amongst the crowd. After he was through, he shook some more hands and hugged more children in the crowd before climbing back to the ringside area and back into the squared circle. The music may have finally died down, but the deafening response from the Montreal faithful wasn’t that much quieter. Andy smiled to himself as he and Mach 2 had a few words with one another away from the microphones. “THANK YOU, ANDY! Clap clap clapclapclap! THANK YOU, ANDY! Clap clap clapclapclap! THANK YOU, ANDY! Clap clap clapclapclap! THANK YOU, ANDY! Clap clap clapclapclap!” Raising the microphone to his face, he tried to get out his first words, but they were all for naught. Andy couldn’t shake the feeling as the reaction continued to send a shiver up his spine. “…I ain’t left yet!” Was all he said with a playful shrug before the crowd roared yet again. Finally, Andy Sharp patted one of the tag title belts across the silver surface before he pressed on. “The ACW Tag Team Titles… lots of work has been put into this division. We saw four very great teams compete for a shot at these belts and when it was all said and done, we narrowed it down to two. On one side, you have Ryan Gallway and Frank Pierce, the Heirs of Wrestling…” BOOOOOOOOOOOOO! “I know, I know, boohissdie evil assholes!” HE chuckled before he continued. “Now, after the Wake, this is going to be the last you see of us as we go on tour across Canada, but for tonight… AND for the Wake, we’re going to put on the absolute best goddamn show we can and kick some royal ass while doing that!” Mach 2 clapped in agreement while Andy let the fans go. “Now, I’ve also got the biggest night of my career lined up. I have a chance for double-gold because not only am I teaming up with Mach 2 to face the Heirs… I’ve got Brian Spaes and Max Danger in Triple Threat for the ACW World Championship!” Whatever remnants of the roof were left prior to their introduction? Yup. Gone. “People have been asking me for the last two weeks since I almost won the ACW Title in two weeks. What’s gonna happen? Are you gonna up and leave Mach 2 for singles gold? Are you going to have a substitute wrestle for you? Aren’t you worried your attention will be divided? Well, I say this to you. Thus far in ACW, I have made history as the only person in this federation to have held BOTH the Spirit of ACW and ACW Championships on either shoulder at the same time. It has yet to be repeated. But not only that… but I WILL make history as the only man in this business to have won BOTH the ACW World Title AND the newly-formed ACW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP! And I have a score to settle…” Sharp turned his attention to the entrance. “Pierce… Gallway…Malone…” Sharp began as he paced around the ring. “You three thought it was pretty hilarious walking around here, thinking you guys were hot shit. You guys pretty much stayed on the other side of the finals through the grace of God. And yet, you had the AUDACITY… to bring my father’s name into all of this. Faking an accident… Mach 2 clued me in on what you fucks did… you three are going to fucking PAY for what you’ve done! I know later tonight, I have to team with that bottom-feeder Max Danger, but that’s later. This is now. I say we don’t wait until the WAKE! WE’RE DOING THIS RIGHT FUCKING NOW!” Whipping his shirt off and hurtling it as far as he possibly can into the sea of rabid Canadians, Andy clutched the ropes with one tightened hand. “HEIRS… GET YOUR ASSES OUT HERE NOW!” “Champion” by Pastor Troy. Ask and ye shall receive. The crowd got heated quickly as the Dynastic Duo themselves walked out. Both men decked in fancy brown suits, complete with red and blue capes and matching microcrowns, the Heirs chuckled amongst themselves. Flanking them was none other than Alexandria Malone. Dressed to the nines in a tight pair of brown dress pants and a tanned turtleneck that she wore rather gracefully, both Heirs pulled out microphones and motioned for their music to be cut. But they were not without hardware. Around both their waists rest a pair of title belts. In their off-time, the Heirs had added some impressive gold around their waists in the form of the tSC Tag Title belts. Pierce smirked as he beamed first. “Sirs Andy of Sharp and Mach of Twain… do’est thou retards not understand whoeth you’re spewing your semen at?!” He inquired, much to the mix of hatred and confusion of everybody. “We, The Heirs of Wrestling, hath just conquered a team that dares call themselves legendary in Team VIAGRA and proved our royal greatness by reducing them to mere flaccid stumps. And now, we looketh towards what the future holds. ACW Tag Team Gold… those silver-clad wonders will look fantastic wrapped around our gorgeous, title-magnetized waists! …Oh, your father doth say Hi.” Ready to jump out, Andy was about ready to climb out of the ring when Mach 2 did everything he could to restrain him. After taking a breather, The Airbourne continued to stare down their challengers as Ryan Gallway continued. “And Mach 2?” Gallway began as he chuckled. “Sir, I’ll have you know that my ears did not go without burning this last week. In the galaxies I’ve seen and the places I’ve been, we take open challenges from insignificant little gnats like you and use them to wipe our royal asses with! Son, I run and slay bitches on Orion’s belt and I’ve bedded some of the most beautiful women this side of the Milky Way Galaxy! One thing that I’m thankful for is that in all the parts of Canada we’ve been, the stars are now directing us towards getting the fuck out of this smelly shithole!” BOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Laughing amongst themselves, it was Alexandria’s turn to speak as Pierce handed her the microphone. “Mach 2, just to humor you, we will accept your little challenge. Tonight, we’ve decided you’ve got to deal with the Baron of Ballistics… The Thursday Night Knight… The Sovereign Superman… Frank Pierce!” Snatching the mic back, Pierce now stuck a finger out, continuing his venom. “And Mach, tonight, after I’m through destroying thine under-developed teenage boy body around, there shan’t be enough of you to even fit at the Wake in a matchbox! BOW BEFORE YOUR KINGS!” With that having been said, “Champion” played once again as all three members of the Heirs of Wrestling stuck their hands in the air, signifying they had intent on becoming the very first ACW Tag Team Champions of the new era. Andy Sharp and Mach 2 just continued to glare. With their own plates full tonight, as much they wanted to, they couldn’t risk significant injury heading to the most important matches of their respective careers. But tonight… big things would happen. STICK AROUND! SINGLES MATCH Mach 2 vs. Frank Pierce It was just mere days away. The Wake. More importantly for the Heirs of Wrestling and for the First Airbourne, it would be the race for the Tag Team Championships. But that was still a few nights away. Tonight, momentum was the name of the game. With Andy Sharp in action and not forgetting the actions of the two-on-one handicap match, Mach 2 challenged either member of the Heirs of Wrestling to a match-up and Frank Pierce answered the call. It was truly going to be a match of lightning-fast speed versus sheer power and that was going to happen right now. “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL!” Tommy Vale bellowed, stirring up the entire All-Star Arena. “GROUND CONTROL, WE HAVE LIFT-OFF IN FIVE… FOUR… THREE… TWO… ONE!” “Touch the Sky” by Kanye West. And with the roar of the music came the cheers from the crowd as one of the most exciting members of the ACW roster made an appearance. Running to the ring at a break-neck pace, Mach 2 was fueled by the energy from the crowd as he not only slid underneath the bottom rope, but rolled forward and kipped to his feet just to show off his great athleticism. “First, in the ring, from New York City weighing in at one-hundred seventy-six pounds… MACH 2!” After backflipping off the turnbuckle, Mach 2 hopped the ropes and pointed fingers to the fans in the audience before his music cut. Gold spotlights? Check. Mini pyro shower at the entrance that ACW probably shouldn’t do with its budget, but doing it anyway? Check. Booing Canadians? Oh, you bet your ass, check. “Requiem For A Dream (Remix)” by Clint Mansell. As the curtains parted, there was no gigantic vehicle that became synonymous with each entrance the Heirs of Wrestling made. There was no pomp and circumstance for once this evening. Parting the curtains with matching serious looks on their faces were Ryan Gallway, Alexandria Malone and Mach 2’s opponent… “And his opponent, from Places More Royal Than Any Canadian Can Afford On Looneys…” BOOOOOOOOO! Yeah, Montreal, blame Ryan Gallway who may or may not have had something to do with changing around his card introductions. “Weighing in at two-hundred sixty five pounds… “The Baron of Ballistics” Frank Pierce!” Entering the ring, Pierce immediately discarded his gigantic gaudy red robe and smiled as he looked down at the much smaller Mach 2. With both Gallway and Malone watching closely on the outside making sure that his job was done, Pierce cracked his neck and his knuckles as Mach 2 did his own pre-match stretches in the corner. DING DING! A meaty SMACK from Mach 2 caught Pierce immediately off-guard. Like a frenzied bull, The Thursday Night Knight charged in for a killing punch, but Mach 2 ducked and returned fire by peppering the lightning-quick kid with several kicks to the leg to try and tie up his hamstrings. Frank swung again and tried to catch him with a boot to the face, but Mach 2 caught the leg and caught him with a back heel trip. Mach 2 readied himself for any number of high-flying moves, but Pierce quickly slid out of the ring, much to the chagrin of the fans who were left in the All-Star Arena. Heading for higher ground, Pierce tried to brush him off while the Supersonic Kid didn’t waste any time. Running off the ropes, Mach 2 headed for a big flip, but suddenly shifted gears and skinned the cat, putting himself back in the ring. Taking a mock bow, Mach 2 was enjoying the attention from the crowd while an irate Pierce kicked at the bottom steps, gritting his teeth. Sliding back in the ring, he tried to attack Mach yet again, but the youngster ducked and hopped off his shoulders, popping off an AMAZING Tilt-A-Whirl Headscissors on the big man! Sending Pierce scurrying to the corner, the smaller half of the Airbourne ran at him attempting something in a way of a Monkey Flip, but Pierce managed to catch him in his tracks and put him on the top rope. He tried to catch the youngster with a punch, but Mach 2 managed to block it and fire back with a stiff kick to the head of his own. With his brains effectively rattled, Mach dove off and SPIKED Pierce head-first into the mat with a sickening Flying Tornado DDT! The fans went crazy as he scurried over and hooked the leg. ONE! TWO! NO! Almost three this early into the match, Mach 2 stayed the course as he peppered the big man with a series of forearm shots. Getting himself a running start off the ropes, Mach 2 went ot work and tried for a second headscissors, only for Pierce to block it and drive him emphatically into the mat with the Sit-Out Spinebuster he effectively called the Pauper Popper! After clutching the back of his head in pain, off the nasty Tornado DDT he had to endure, Pierce took a second to catch his breath. Rather than follow up with the cover, Pierce went right to work, pummeling the high flyer about the head with several vicious Clubbing Forearm shots. Whipping him into the ropes, Pierce followed him in, only for Mach 2 to slide between the side ropes and try for the slingshot kick that he’d utilized on occasions like his trainer, Andy Sharp. Frank had the move well-scouted, actually grabbing him by the legs in the Wheelbarrow position. Rather than opt for the suplex, he merely SLAMMED him down stomach-first across the ropes before sliding back down. “Get him, Frank!” Alexandria yelled to her charge as she balled a fist in anticipation. “Crush the infidel under the might of your boot, fellow evolved being!” That would be Gallway’s weird contribution. After Alexandria stared at him funny, she went back to cheering on Pierce, who was now throwing the boots to the winded Mach 2. Grabbing him in a Cravate-like submission, he shook him vehemently almost in an attempt to pop Mach 2’s head from his body like the cork off a bottle. Rather than follow through with any sort of submission attempt, Pierce made it outright deadly by driving a series of upward knees aimed right for the head! After the series of assimilating blows, The Margrave Digger rolled him into a Snapmare and waylaid the cruiserweight with several vicious Crossface Punches! Once he was through with about four or five massive blows, he put Mach 2 on the mat and hooked a leg. ONE! TWO! TH… NO! Mach 2 didn’t even kick out, he merely fired a shoulder off the mat to prevent an early loss. Pierce took his sweet time letting the Supersonic Kid get back to his feet, scraping his face with the side of his boot. After clutching Mach 2 in the gutwrench position, Frank took the opportunity to show off his power by completely flipping him up and over, getting him about seven feet in the air in the process before crashing helplessly to the mat! Standing in amazement of his power, the fans jeered Pierce as he took time to give the fans a mocking bow while his cohorts in crime rallied him on. As Mach 2 tried to get back to a vertical base, a straightforward kick to the face sent him rolling across the canvas, landing him near the ropes. Mach 2 got nothing in the way of a break as The Baron of Ballistics was already on him, pressing a knee firmly into his back, choking him in the process. Referee Paige Allen started her count which Pierce held out until four and a half. He let Mach 2 rest for mere seconds before pressing him back into the ropes just a little more to try and take the air out of his sails. “YOU’VE BESMIRCHED MINE AWESOMENESS FOR THE LAST TIME, YOU SHITRAG!” Pierce screamed in that completely bogus and condescending manner that made sound stupid, but was ultimately a staple of being one of ACW’s more hated villains. Scooping Mach 2 by two handfuls of his black, curly hair, Mach 2 found an opening and tied him up with a modified Inside Cradle! ONE! TWO! NO! As the fans let out a cheer for his attempting comeback, a Flying Roundhouse caught Pierce flush in the face, sending him bouncing back to the ropes. As he bounced back, a Low Roundhouse caught him in the stomach and turned him over as Mach 2 gained some speed, hopping up and over Pierce with a Leaping Rocker Dropper! He used his entire strength to get the big man over into a pin. ONE! TWO! KICKOUT WITH AUTHORITY! Mach 2 went flying off his opponent, the strength of nearly 270 pounds coming into play. Mach 2 got himself another running start, but what was waiting for him was a SICK Clothesline that turned him inside out and back again! Somewhere in the neighborhood of about eight stomps to the chest greeted Mach up on his very unceremonious landing before Pierce got up and drove a sick Bionic-like elbow to the head. Now taking him to task, He hoisted him up before DRILLING him in the center of the ring with a Pendulum Backbreaker! As Mach arched his back in pain and cradled it with a hand, Pierce extended the pinky to the crowd. Oh, Lord, here it was. “YE OLDE ROYALE ELBOW!” The bounce off the ropes was the prelude to one of the more hated and blasphemous moves in ACW. Moreso than a Khristian Keller Tranquilizer, but less so than a Kelly Flawless Battle Rap. The Ye Olde Royale Elbow dropped right across the spine of the kid as Pierce rolled him over and went for a rather lax cover. ONE! TW… Mach 2 managed to brush off the mere forearm that Pierce used for the cover, but that turned out to be a quick mistake as seconds later, he was lifted off the mat and driven violently into the ground yet again, this time in the form of a Standing Powerbomb. At this point, it appeared that Mach 2 was going to have absolutely nowhere to go and that was probably an astute guess as several vicious punches to the face caught Mach 2. Lifting him up, he Military Pressed him high over his head before sending him crashing back down with a Gutbuster, letting him roll lifelessly. Making sure this was absolutely it, he rolled through and hooked a leg. ONE! TWO! HAND UNDER THE ROPES! It was a great call by Paige Allen, but not one that Pierce really cared much for. So much, in fact, he DROVE a hasty knee into the small of Mach 2’s back and began pulling at him in a version of a Modified Camel Clutch. It wasn’t applied with the expert skill that Iceman had used in his past matches, claiming victims with his Icecap, but Pierce was doing a fine job of keeping pressure on the back and neck. Mach 2 screamed in pain and held out a hand, considering giving the match up for a few seconds. The ACW Tag Team Title match was mere days away and he knew he had to keep himself fresh, but there was a big part of him that knew he needed to stay in the game. “Mach, do you give?” Paige Allen asked him, also spotting any signs of a tapout. “NO!” he screamed. This just seemed to set Pierce off a little more, who released the hold for a moment, only to lift him up and drive a foot in the stomach. He ran off the ropes and tried to decapitate him with some sort of Clothesline, but Mach 2 was at the ready and rolled up AND over into a Sunset Flip! ONE! TWO! NO! Pierce scrambled back up and tried to once again stop the Supersonic Kid from getting the speed that made him so dangerous. This turned out to be a worthless endeavor as a flurry of kicks caught him before a big Flying Knee from Mach 2 put him on his ass! Frank took a moment to wipe a trickle of blood from his lip as Mach fell back against the ropes, also being slowed down by his throbbing back. After letting out a shout to the crowd, who reciprocated in kind, Mach 2 ran at Pierce and rolled over the ropes with a Spinning Wheel Kick, carrying BOTH men to the floor! While Gallway and Alexandria looked on with concern from the other side, Mach 2 rolled Pierce back underneath the ropes and perched himself on the top rope. Egging on the big man, Mach 2 flew and nailed him right in the face with a Double Flying Knee Strike, Ryan Gallway’s very own Tea Bag move! “THAT STUPID PIECE OF PRIMORDIAL OOZE!” he shouted in a craze. “THAT’S MY MOVE! I HOPPED MANY A STAR IN THE ALPHA CENTAURI GALAXY TO GET THOSE MAD HOPS!” Winking at Ryan Gallway, he stayed on top of Pierce’s body and went for the cover. ONE! TWO! THR… KICK OUT! Even though Pierce powered out of the move, the fans could feel the momentum completely turn in his favor! On the verge up an upset in the making, Mach 2 took hold of Pierce’s head and ran up the turnbuckles… SHOCKW…NO! Frank grounded himself and threw Mach 2 halfway across the ring, blocking his finishing blow in the Shockwave. The Sovereign Superman slapped his shoulders, letting the fans know exactly what was coming next. The same move that Pierce had used to knock Andy Sharp out of his boots with weeks ago, The Royal Road would be his end… ROYAL RO… SCHOOLBOY! ONE! TWO! THREE…KICKOUT... ONE SECOND TOO LATE! Mach 2 got himself the hell out of the ring and landed on the outside. Frank Pierce couldn’t believe it! Ryan Gallway and Alexandria Malone, both white as ghosts, were in shock. Even the fans were in shock. But Tommy Vale told them what they had just seen… “LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, YOUR WINNER OF THE MATCH… MACH 2! If that wasn’t enough, Mach 2 came to life when “Touch the Sky” started to blast over the PA. Pumping a fist in the air, a mile-wide grin etched itself across the face of the Supersonic Kid, who had just scored what some could call a major upset in singles competition! Inside the ring, The Baron of Ballistics was frothing at the mouth like a rabid dog ready to bite his next victim. Kicking the ropes in a complete frenzy, Ryan Gallway and Alexandria Malone did their best to keep him restrained. Meanwhile, Mach 2 was free with the crowd to celebrate what was the biggest victory in his ACW tenure thus far. Last week, the Heirs and Max Danger had put one over on both Spaes and the First Airbourne. This time, it was Mach 2 turning the tables on the much bigger Frank Pierce and getting some much-needed momentum heading into the Wake. While the Baron of Ballistics fumed and pointed at Mach 2 to get back in the ring to take an ass-kicking like a man, it was Mach 2 putting his hands around his waists to let him know something very important. The Wake. First Airbourne. First ACW Tag Team Champions. Wilson spoke to Shields in hushed tones, gesturing at the Spirt of ACW belt on the time keeper’s table, and doing all sorts of other movements with his hands as he talked. Finally, Mark walked over to the announce table and began relaying the message to Renaud Cardinal, who picked up the house mic and said, “Ladies and gentlemen, I have been informed that tonight’s main event is now to be contested for the Spirit of ACW Championship!” There was a smattering of applause, but the building was less-than-full so it wasn’t the usual fervor everyone was accustomed to. But nevertheless, the announcement sure did alter the landscape of the match. Now whoever pinned Trevor Wilson would walk out of the All-Star Arena with a brand new shiny belt around their waist. Danger’s eyes lit up like a kid on Christmas morning tearing open the wrapping paper and seeing the Nintendo he’d always wanted. Andy Sharp, too, was intrigued as it was he who lost out on the chance at Legends III to become the Spirt of ACW once again. The two rivals had been working quite well together thus far despite the fact that they would be doing battle in two weeks at The Wake for Danger’s ACW World Championship. But now? Oh, all bets were off. Wilson grabbed Mark again and whispered something else into his ear, who relayed the message to Cardinal, who... “The match is still Max Danger and Andy Sharp versus The Minutemen... EVERY member of the Minutemen.” Odds were stacked decided against Danger and Sharp now. But Wilson wasn’t finished. He had one more thing to add to the festivities. Renaud let everyone know... “And they are all legal.” That was decided unfair to those opposing the Minutemen, but Trevor Wilson wasn’t going to take any chances. He’d gotten one-up and wasn’t going to take to chance losing not only the match but also his championship. Wilson and Brian Spaes remained on the outside, both looking ever more confident, especially since Jeremy Stevenson and Kati were heading down the aisle behind Danger and Sharp. Spirit of ACW Championship Max Danger and Andy Sharp look at one another then look towards Brian Spaes and Trevor Wilson pulling themselves up onto the ring apron. Danger and Sharp scoot towards the center of the ring when they turn and see Kati and Jeremy on the other side, hopping up onto the apron. They were surrounded. The two men in the center of the ring stood back to back. Danger facing Spaes and Wilson and Andy eyeing down Kati and Jeremy. “What do we do?” “Well, personally, I kind of want to slay the dragon. Let’s get to work.” The Angel quote was completely unheard by Sharp as he had exited the ring in a flash just as Max threw himself at the original Minutemen in the match. He staggered them with elbow smashes and turned to see how Andy was fairing. All he saw was the Misfit Toys coming at him and Sharp, grinning from ear to ear, waving goodbye to him as he backed up the ramp. “You son of a--” Danger was engulfed by the Minutemen, but not wanting their leader to be destroyed, Marcus the Smark and the Drunk Toga Dudes (mostly ‘cause they were drunk and always up for a fight) climbed into the ring. They were quite easily dispatched because 1) Marcus was a wimp and could pretty much be knocked out by the wind, and 2) the drunks, while good in a bar fight, were no match for the more experienced fighters in the ring. But this did allow the Danger Man to retrieve Armsy McDanger which he used to whallop Jeremy Stevenson in the back of the head, knocking him to his knees. A wicked kick to the back of the head knocked him flat on his face. Spaes attacked Danger next, but Max ducked underneath his clothesline, smacking him in the belly on the way by with the prosthetic. Danger continued forward and clocked Wilson in the face, knocking him sideways. Max turned around as Brian was readying to attack again. Danger tossed the fake limb at Spaes, who caught it, allowing Danger to hit a wicked Dangerous III on one of his opponents at The Wake! When he turned around, Danger was face to face with Kati. Trevor Wilson, holding his cheek, was not pleased with Max’s refusal to just lay down. It was odd, usually it was Max who escaped at the first sight of harm, but tonight Andy had been one step ahead of him, and Danger was fighting. Truly standing up for himself and against this revolution. “I’m not going to hit you,” Danger told Kati, who slapped him hard across the face. Ever since his daughter was born four years ago, Danger refused to get physical with a girl. He wouldn’t hit them. Anyone who’s seen his matches with Michelle Masters in FWO could attest to that, and, hey, he still managed to win the Internet Title against her. But she kept slapping him but Danger wouldn’t hit back. While she did this, “Sympathy for the Devil” by the Rolling Stones came across the speaker system, filling All-Star Arena, and through the curtain walked the fifth and final member of the Minutemen, Alias. The Original Pulp Hero climbed into the ring and grabbed Kati by the shoulder, pulling her out of the way. “That’s enough,” Alias said calmly. Danger spit onto the mat before he looked up and said, “Why, if it isn’t the knight in shining armor, The Face of ACW himself. Wow, what have I done to be so honored with your presence? The true king. Should I kiss your feet?” Alias said nothing, he just looked down at the ACW World Champion. Brian Spaes had recovered from the roaring elbow and walked over to Alias’ side, as did Trevor Wilson. The Misft Toys lingered in the background near a corner. “I’ve been waiting a long time to get my hands on you, Danger,” spat the Man Who History Forgot. “Well, here I am. Even you couldn’t screw it up now,” Max replied with a smirk and a wink. He got punched in the face for his troubles. “Pretty clever, huh, Max?” The Apex Predator was quite proud of himself, altering ther stipulations of the match. It’d worked out better than he’d thought, as he never thought Andy Sharp to bail on Danger, leaving the “King” of ACW and it’s World Champion all alone. Spaes punched Max again, knocking him sideways and onto one hand. Danger shook off the pain and righted himself back on his knees. He just shook his head. “If you’re expected me to kiss your feet, join your cult, perform fellatio, or whatever, I’m sorry to disappoint you guys, ‘cause no matter what you expect of me, I’m not going to do it. Not willingly anyway.” Both Brian and Trevor reached for Max, but Alias put a hand in each man’s chest, holding them up. Alias looked into the eyes of both men, who weren’t that happy with what just happeend, but straightened up anyhow. “My savior.” said the Amazing One, dripping with sarcasm. Alias allowed, even helped, Max back onto his feet. But just as calmly as Alias had let Danger get to his feet, he just as calmly put him down with Pulped II, smashing Max’s face down across his knees! Alias covered Danger, as Mark Shields was just baffled by everything that had transpired. But he made the count at any rate. ONE! TWO! THREE! And just like that the ACW World Champion had been defeated and Trevor Wilson had retained the Spirt of ACW Championship, his plan working to a T. All five members of the Minutemen stood over Max Danger, their arms raised into the air. All except for Alias, who’s eyes remained on the fallen Danger. WINNER: the Minutemen by pinfall (Alias pins Danger with Pulped II; Wilson retains Spirt of ACW Championship)
