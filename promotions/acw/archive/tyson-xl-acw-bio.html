Tyson XL Biography Real Name Gregory Tyson [name withheld] Nicknames Heavy T Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday 9-12-83 Place of Birth San Diego, CA Height 6'3 Weight 330 Blood Type B- Eye Color Brown Biography A rotund man that can move with great speed, Tyson XL was constantly ostracized all his life for his weight. Seeing potential within him, Gabriel took him in and made him the enforcer for the group. Gabriel points and Tyson obeys silently, taking out the Night Life’s enemies with brute flab… er, force. CURRENTLY: One sixth of the current ACW Tag Team Championships. By executive order, Freebird Rules is in effect, any two members of the Night Life may defend the championship. Also as new [Black] Scorpion Champion, this also makes him a double champ SUCKA!!!11! Team The Night Life Wrestling Style Athletic big guy like Vader, Bam Bam Bigelow, Takeshi Morishima, and Hugh Morrus. Deceptively quick for a big man, capable of crazy agile feats. Finisher Move Moonsault/Falling gutwrench powerbomb Finisher Description Crush Groove/XL Bomb Special Moves -Forearm shots -Scooping powerslam -Ura-nage -Dropkick (doesn’t get too much air, but it works) -Body splash in the corner (also in standing and rarely, top rope flavors) -Running hip attack/The Butt-Butt -Springboard clothesline (more in the vein of Amish Roadkill, climbing the ropes) -Open palmed chops (sometimes, doing chop/punch combos in the corner) -Cartwheel splash/hip attack to cornerned opponent (crowd-popping move, no matter what his alignment) -Running twisting spear (weird, I know, Rasche Brown does it) -Bossman/swinging side slam -Senton back splash -Corner slingshot elbow drop -Discus lariat -Modified shining wizard (sloppy, yet effective like Ryota Hama, another wrestling fat guy) Entrance Music 'Slam' by Pendulum Trainer The Amazing Gabriel
