Sharp came to a stop right in front of the Heirs of Wrestling. Shaking his head, Andy stood and saw who was directly in front of him. Without hesitation, Sharp spit on Ryan and punched Frank right in the mouth! Andy spun and kick Ryan in the chest before throwing a back elbow into Pierce’s throat! He probably should have left well enough alone though, as flying through the ropes was the Danger Man, who rammed his massive right elbow pad right into the back of his head! Sharp sprawled over the announce table as the Heirs, pissed as all hell, helped Danger to his feet. Max turned Sharp over and mounted the announce table, where he slammed his elbow repeatedly into Andy’s face. While he was doing this, Frank and Ryan pulled up the protective mats surrounding the ring. The crowd was going frantic now. Screaming at Danger not to do what he was probably going to do. But Sharp had enough sense in him to headbutt Danger and knee him in the gut. He jumped up onto the apron, quickly hopped onto the middle rope and springboarded off with a moonsault press! Danger caught him! The Heirs helped Max not fall over from the weight landing down on him, and that allowed the King of ACW to get Andy in position and... TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER ON THE CONCRETE!!!! The crowd sat in stunned silence as the Danger Man left the limp body of Andy Sharp fall over onto his back. Max, breathing heavily, smirked an evil smirk, as Frank Pierce and Ryan Gallway applauded their King. Back in the ring, Brian Spaes was recovering. Medics were running down to ringside as Danger climbed back onto the ring apron. The match wasn’t over. He still needed to finish this and take home a title he never intended to lose. Spaes was on all fours as Danger hopped up onto the top turnbuckle Sharp-style. He leapt. KISS. MY. FEET! The double foot stomp connected with the back of Brian’s head, driving it down into the mat! Danger crawled back over to Spaes and turned him over onto his back. ONE! TWO! THREE! And just like that, the King of ACW was still the ACW World Champion. Amazing began to play again as the Heirs of Wresling climbed into the ring, followed by Marcus the Smark and the ACW World Championship belt. The Heirs helped Max to his feet as Marcus strapped the belt back around his waist. As Brian Spaes, leader of the revolution, lay, unconscious at his feet, and Andy Sharp was being fitted for a neck brace and loaded onto a gurney, Max Danger stood, center of the ring, hands raised high into the air with Frank Pierce to his right and Ryan Gallway to his left (and Marcus behind them, smiling the brightest smile of all). WINNER: Danger by pinfall (KISS MY FEET on Spaes); retains ACW World Championship So Long, Suckers But the Danger Man wasn’t finished. He demanded a microphone and Marcus the Smark provided it for him. Standing in the center of the ring, with the Heirs of Wrestling behind him and on each side, the King of ACW brought the microphone to his lips. “It is with a heavy heart that I stand before you all right now, the ACW faithful, the people who helped bring this great company back to life and supported it through thick and thin,” Danger lowered his head in respect, and because he could feel the tears welling up in his eyes. “Despite the way you all feel about me, I just want each and every one of you to know, that I love you, from the deepest depths of my heart. Truly and honestly. Forever and always.” The tears were coming and Danger sniffed. Marcus brought him a tissue, which Max attached to Armsy and blew his nose into. “I’m sorry. It’s just... I get emotional.” Danger held up a hand to tell the people to give him a moment. He was really baring his soul now. “Ladies and gentlemen, next week we begin our grand tour of the Great White North, leaving the comfort of the All-Star Arena.” Max took a deep breath. Frank and Ryan squeezed his shoulders. “Thank you, thank you,” Danger said to them. Taking another deep breath, Max knew he needed to finish and finish quickly. “So, on the behalf of every person who works for this company, I would like to say, Montreal... All-Star Arena... ACWites...” Danger paused as a smirk appeared across his face. “Good riddance!” The boos rained down upon Max Danger, Frank Pierce, and Ryan Gallway as they made a hasty exit, leaving Marcus the Smark to fend for himself as the fans threw whatever trash (and some chairs) they could get their hands on.
