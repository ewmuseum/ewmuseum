Horace Tully 'The Adamant'; 'Mr Wrestling 2005' (self-proclaimed) Real Name: Nathaniel Tully Birthdate: July 18, 1978 Hometown: Dover, Delaware Height: 6'1 Weight: 221 Entrance Music: 'Dead Skin Mask' by Slayer Medical Information Natural Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: O+ Body Fat Percentile: 6.5% Favored Workout: Upper body and stamina training Past Illnesses: Flu, common colds, chicken pox....pretty much every common ailment Past Injuries: Dislocated elbow; concussion In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Tully shows a variety of wrestling disciplines in his ringwork. Most notably is his pure technical wrestling ability, chaining together moves with ease and fluidity (especially in the early stages of a contest). He can also support his groundwork with any number of stiff strikes, notably a barrage of forearms and elbows. Tully's matches tend to start with back-and-forth chain-wrestling exchanges in the feeling-out stages, wearing down the opposition and gradually progressing to more high impact maneuvers as the bout continues. He tends to win with one out of his impressive array of submissions, but he has won many a match with his ultra-stiff Lariat and The Horror Out Of Dover maneuvers. Trainer: Raymond Beckett; Hirohito Ryuji; brief training under Yuji Nagata Status: Heel Aerial Moves: Barring the occasional plancha when throwing caution to the wind, Tully stays on the mat. Counter Moves: Enziguri; dragon screw leg whip; STO; sitout jawbreaker Favorite Moves: Swinging neckbreaker; dropkick; pendulum backbreaker/chestbreaker; T-bone/Exploder suplex; STO (running and standing); single arm DDT Set-Up Move(s): Demon-Slayer; It Came From Yuggoth; Lariat; The Horror Out of Dover; Unholy Roller; The Cardinal Sin 1) Tully lifts his opponent into a vertical suplex, corkscrews around, and sits-out, dropping his opponent face and chest-first onto the mat 2) Cross-armed Northern Lights Bomb 3) Aggressive, attacking clothesline in the vein of a Stan Hansen, Samoe Joe or Homicide, and the type that has all the potential in the world to end a match in the late stages and even KO the recipient. Before using this move Tully will telegraph it by removing the elbow pad on the arm he's going to use before hitting the ropes 4) Pumphandle lift into an inverted Michinoku Driver-style faceplant, or even headdrop in some situations 5) Tully grabs the opponent in a rear waistlock, runs toward the far corner, and rolls them backwards. Instead of stopping in a rollup, he continues to roll through and uses the momentum to deliver a high-impact German suplex 6) Wrist-clutch Death Valley Driver Finishing Move: The Everlasting; Jujigatame; Anaconda Vice 1) Tully maneuvers behind his opponent and locks both arms in a double chickenwing, as if to execute a Tiger suplex. He instead instead forces his opponent facedown on the mat, and then flips over in a bridging full nelson submission. As a variation, he'll lock the chickenwing on an already downed opponent and apply the move 2) Cross-armbreaker.
