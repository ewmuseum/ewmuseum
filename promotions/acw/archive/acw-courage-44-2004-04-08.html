Unable to believe what he'd just accomplished, Quincy Mama swung his leg over the top of the ladder, now straddled at the top platform. The Alburquackan crowd were chanting Quinton's name with gusto, toasting the remnants of their drinks to him, as he raised the TV Title above his head. Once again, he'd proven just far he'd go to win. And when it was all said and done... ... Quinton May was truly a Rising Star quickly marching towards the top prize. WINNER : Quinton May, new ACW Television Champion.
