Kroenen Biography Real Name K.R. Kroenen Nicknames Anarchy's Angel Hometown Berlin, Germany Birthday 1/22/76 Place of Birth Berlin, Germany Height 6'1" Weight 223 Blood Type AB+ Eye Color Blue Biography The second generation wrestler K.R. Kroenen is a former babyface superstar in Europe. His good looks and dazzling moves got him over with the people in no time when he hit the European wrestling scene. However, time and numbers eventually caught up to him. During a fatal four-way match, he had a fireball thrown into his face. He missed several months and came back wearing a modified air filtration mask along with a new attitude. His claims that his face was damaged beyond repair from the fireball are still just his word, as no one has ever seen him without his mask. His psyche cracked from the incident and he became a dark brooding young man bent on creating havoc and anarchy throughout the wrestling world. Now, with his two associates, he looks to bring destroy all that stand before them and dismantle the machine.
