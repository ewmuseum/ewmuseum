Horace Tully Biography Real Name Nathaniel Tully Nicknames The Adamant; Reverend; Heart of Burning Hometown Dover, Delaware Birthday July 18, 1978 Place of Birth Baltimore, Maryland Height 6'2 Weight 236 Blood Type B+ Eye Color Green Biography Horace Tully remains the right-hand man of the infamous Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan, diabolical mastermind of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Many believe he is currently being groomed by VJ as his successor in the wrestling world come the day of his inevitable retirement, and if Tully's in-ring skill is any indication, Shanahan has chosen his man wisely. Whereas VJ dominates all around him with his dark persona and inhuman stamina and Darius Daniel provides the raw strength and brutality of an old-time enforcer, Tully is the consummate wrestler. He's been praised as one of the most overlooked up-and-comers by many of the most trusted judges of talent in the business and this acclaim hasn't gone unnoticed by Tully himself. He dubbed himself 'Mr Wrestling' and intends on using the new ACW as a platform to prove he's not full of hot air, vowing to squelch whatever his stints in RPW and PRIME may have shown American audiences. Other Feds RPW; PRIME; tSC Team The Sect of Black Wisdom Past Teams & Groups The Covenant (tag team w/Ethan Knight) Wrestling Style Compliments his masterful technical knowledge with an offense heavy in stiff elbow and forearm strikes. He's backed off of his submission-heavy game in recent times in deference to an attack that focuses on pummeling his opposition with strikes and high-impact moves before finishing them with one of his handful of top-tier match-enders. Finisher Move 1) Lariat -- Aggressive, attacking clothesline in the vein of a Stan Hansen or Nigel McGuinness, and the type that has all the potential in the world to end a match in the late stages and even KO the recipient. Before using this move Tully will usually telegraph it by removing the elbow pad on the arm he's going to use before hitting the ropes and throwing every bit of force he has into it. Also executes short-arm versions, though they don't win him matches with thr frequency of the running version. A primary finisher. 2) Emerald Flowsion. Pick-up into an over-the-shoulder position and ending in a sit-out slam, dropping the opponent on either the upper back or head/neck. Tully often adds a corkscrew or pivot for added effect. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NpnAsbqJan4 3) Wrist-clutch Death Valley Driver. Won't see use unless the opponent is feisty enough to have escaped defeat at the hands of the lariat and The Horror Out Of Dover. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rAzQVMYoMyE Finisher Description 1)Lariat 2)The Horror Out Of Dover 3)The Cardinal Sin Special Moves --Move Set--- Swinging neckbreaker Crossed arms sitout powerbomb Dropkick Pendulum backbreaker/chestbreaker T-bone/exploder suplex STO (standing and running varieties) Dragon screw leg whip Single arm DDT (used with exceeding frequency in matches where he targets the shoulder, on par with most of the WWE's roster using scoop slams) Enziguri (standing and step-up) Sitout jawbreaker Fujiwara armbar submission Fireman's carry into a shoulder breaker Fireman's carry into a single arm DDT Shoulder armbreaker Knife hand chops Stiff forearm/elbow shots. This is most of his standing offense in any given match. Jujigatame (cross-armbreaker). Potential match-ender if the captured arm has been worked extensively. Variety of takedowns, armlocks, leglocks and submissions, both simple and obscure. ---Signature moves--- The Everlasting -- Tully maneuvers behind his opponent and locks both arms in a double chickenwing, as if to execute a Tiger suplex. He instead instead forces his opponent facedown on the mat, and then flips over in a bridging full nelson submission. As a variation, he'll lock the chickenwing on an already downed opponent and apply the move. This move has been put on the backburner as part of his offense, Tully having moved to a strike-oriented arsenal with submission-wrestling as his secondary approach, but may be brought out again if the circumstances dictate it to be useful. Shinjuku Explosion -- Newest maneuver in his arsenal picked up on a recent Japanese tour. Double underhook lift into a powerbomb, ending in a sitout side facebuster. Demon-Slayer -- Tully lifts his opponent into a vertical suplex, corkscrews around, and sits-out, dropping his opponent face and chest-first onto the mat. It Came From Yuggoth -- Crossed-arms Northern Lights Bomb. Unholy Roller -- Tully grabs the opponent in a rear waistlock and rolls them backwards. Instead of stopping in a rollup, he continues to roll through and uses the momentum to deliver a high-impact German suplex. He'll hold the bridge for the pin about 25% of the time. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08yA_ZHPmSA Entrance Music 'Night Eternal' -- Moonspell
