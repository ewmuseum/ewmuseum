DarkStar Biography Real Name Gavin Drazail Nicknames The Forgotten Legend, The Prince of Anarchy, The King of Chaos Hometown Parts Unknown Birthday January 21, 1980 Place of Birth Philadelphia, PA Height 6'2 Weight 228 Blood Type Type AB Eye Color Dark Blue Biography From an insane asylum in Philadelphia, to sold out arenas across the world, to incomprehensible planes of existence between the very strings of reality, to high school gymnasiums just to make a living, to today. It's been a strange journey to ACW. And we all know it's just going to get stranger.
