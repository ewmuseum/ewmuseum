Medical Information Natural Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Piercing and revengeful, Chestnut brown Blood Type: B negative Body Fat Percentile: 3% Favored Workout: Past Illnesses: Cocaine addiction, steroid abuse and severe depression Past Injuries: Undisclosed In-Ring Information Wrestling Style: Although almost all of his wrestling history is unavailable; the individual known as God’s Forgotten Son’s wrestling style is all arounder. GFS is able to wrestle under most styles and mimic the others, which was a valuable skill taught to him during his adolescence by the industries’ best professors of professional wrestling. So essentially, GFS can do any maneuver his heart desires. But having so much of a broad arsenal at his disposal; GFS likes to rely on his vast knowledge of ring psychology, which was made popular in Canada and Japan, before making its way into the United States. So it is common that you will see GFS use a Mat based submission style; to keep his opponents grounded, in addition to a high speed aerial display of acrobatics…which will help against slower but stronger adversaries as he tries to drive the air out of their lungs. Severe fatigue is stand follow-up. While in the ring; GFS likes to focus on maneuvers that will send jolts through the spinal cord like neck/back breakers and suplexes…He’ll use different variations of submissions to contort the lower back, causing spasms as he slowly builds up to the high impact finisher or favorite ground submission. GFS is passionate about ring psychology; and if done correctly, he understands that no adversary will be able to escape the jaws of defeat. God's Forgotten Son is facing his opponent. GFS bends his opponent down and hooks both of their arms so they are behind his opponent's back. He links his hands together and lifts his opponent up so they are upside down, their head near the side of his body. GFS then sits down, dropping his opponent on their head. “Meteoric Suicide” (Alternate Finisher) A 270-degree back flip from the top rope or top turnbuckle, that ends in a leg drop instead of a body press. Also known as the Shooting Star Press Guillotine Leg Drop. GFS will rarely use this move, which is why it is an alternative finisher. But if he connects with this death defying move from the top turnbuckle or somewhere high up, it will guarantee the finish to the match, and a possible end to his opponent’s career. “Pleasure from your Pain” (Primary Submission) An arm-capture/Crossface combination of two submissions moves. GFS laces his right arm under the throat of his opponent (similar to a undetected choke), using all his leverage to hyperextend the elbow via barring of the arm, and also strains the neck to a great degree from the head being pulled back as well. *A tap out by the opponent usually is standard, because GFS doesn't let go until they submit.* “Selfless Punishment ” (Ultimate Finisher) Another variation from an elevated position on the second ropes; GFS leaps backwards from the ring ropes, to plant his adversary head first into the mat. Death and defeat will immediantely follow. This is another GFS rarity, mostly saved for hated adversary that won’t stay down. Due to speed, elevation and impact of this maneuver, an opponent could be rendered unconscious or paralyzed with all the force being taken by their neck and spine.
