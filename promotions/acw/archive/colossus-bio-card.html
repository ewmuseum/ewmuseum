Historical Information Past Team Associations: Past Faction Associations: ACW Awards: Other Federations: Other Federations Awards: Career History Let me tell you a bit about Colossus... He's not the smartest guy in the world. He may not know the capital of France. He may not know his P's from his Q's. He may not know right from wrong. But what he does know is how to fight. Colossus, as his name suggests, is colossal. He's huge. He's a monster. He doesn't have morals because he doesn't know what the word means. He's your school bully, skipping classes, not very bright, itching for attention, and picking on people smaller than him (and at 465lbs, that's near enough everyone he meets). I've known the guy since school. I used to do his homework. He used to eat my lunch - not surprising when you look at the size of him. Who would've thought that 20 years down the line I'd be his agent, getting him a lucrative contract at the ACW, and eating lunch out of HIS pocket? I'm not at all bitter, I handle all his financial dealings nowadays, he doesn't know any different, the big oaf. All he knows is how to bully people, pick on people, hurt people, and make their life a misery, and putting that monster in a wrestling ring effectively gives the guy a licence to do so. Drop him on his head, he won't feel anything. He doesn't feel pain, he doesn't even realise he's hurt, he's too stupid to. All he wants to do is hurt people. And all I want to do is continue lapping it up in my penthouse with my supermodel girlfriend, and watch the cheques roll in as that big oaf kicks, punches and bullies his way to the top. Oh, and by the way, the name's Joey T. Nice to meet you.
