Hank Wright Biography Real Name Hank Wright Nicknames The Tank, The Governor. Hometown Fort Worth, Texas. Place of Birth Fort Worth, Texas. Height 6'6 Weight 278 Blood Type Bad Eye Color Blue Biography "I'm the WRIGHT man in the WRIGHT place at the WRIGHT time." "Everything's big in Texas - but nothing is bigger than Hank Wright." "(Insert insult here.) You oughtta try it sometime." "I'm big, strong and bad to the bone." Hank Wright grew up in Texas idolising big men from his home state and nearby Oklahoma. The stars Wright watched were not only over in the United States but also in Japan. As a teenager, he couldn't have possibly known that he'd also achieve similar notoriety in the world's two richest countries and, crucially, the most important wrestling nations. Wright debuted in his late teens and was groomed to become the sport's next big thing at the tender age of 21. A national organization bought out the local Texas territory he performed in and as a result, he was crowned their final champion. This meant he had automatic entry into a tournament to declare the new national heavyweight champion, a tournament he was tipped to win. He didn't and he never crashed through the glass ceiling either. Hank returned to his native Texas and worked for various companies. He held several heavyweight titles but it took him years to get settled in a decent company. By the time he did, he'd become a selfish, egotistical main-eventer who cared only about himself and was a promoter's nightmare. As the internet gained popularity, fans' knowledge of Wright's misdemeanors grew and Hank's standing amongst the faitfhful dipped. He had no other option but to turn heel. Oh, how he thrived. Hank's act worked because it was real. It reinvigorated his character and he was able to keep his 'spot.' For a while. Humiliated and hounded out of Texas when he tried to hold the company up for more money, Wright moves south to Mexico to try and revive the fortunes of an ailing organization as well as resurrecting his reputation. He succeeded at doing both. Turning up his heelish ways to 11, Hank terrorized anyone and anything Mexican and became a 2-time World Heavyweight Champion in just 2 and a half years. He spent more than half of his tenure there with gold around his waist. With his reputation out of character restored, Wright returned to the US to be nearer to his young son from a failed relationship. He works part-time dates and while he's not a rich man, he's done well enough to wind down now that he's in his early 40s... Ha. Those of you know Hank Wright know that's not The Tank's style. In or out of the ring. Other Feds The Tank only looks forward. Other Fed Titles Loads. Wrestling Style He was called The Hurricane and not for nothing. When he's in full flow, it's difficult to live with Hank. He lives life in the fast lane, both in and out of the ring. Stiffer than a Brit's upper lip. The Wrong Foot: Hank scores with a Bicycle Kick using his left foot. The Lone Star: A Suicide Dive/Dragon Rocket that forces his opponent back into the barricade. Texan Legsweep: Russian Legsweep into a Lariat. Texan Uppercut: Elbow Uppercut. The Wright Hand: Seemingly going for a Flying Body Guillotine, Hank runs full pelt but actually slides out of the ring and catches his unsuspecting opponent with a big-time right. This can sometimes be followed up by a Slingshot Splash. The T: Three Point Shoulder Tackle. Combos/Spots: Texas hold 'em: The Tank hoists his opponent up into a Gorilla-Press position and threatens to throw them over 3-4 sides of the ring, pulling the brakes on each time. Eventually, he dumps them behind him in the centre of the ring, forcing them to land on their ribs. Hokey Pokey: Hank puts his (W)right leg in, kicking his opponent repeatedly. Then, he turns around and comes back at them with a hard knee to the face while they're crouched down in the corner. A WRIGHT Beating: With his opponent backed into the corner, Hank shows his 'Hurricane' moniker off: European Uppercut, Machine Gun, European Uppercut, Machine Gun (alternating hands) left knee to the stomach, right knee to the stomach, two punches to the head, two kicks to the chest and then a Yakuza Kick to the face. Heart of Texas: Preceded by an Alabama Slam or Rib Breaker, Hank's version of the Flashing Elbow is buried into his opponent's chest. TSE: Hank hits a Yakuza Kick in the corner. As the opponent stumbles out, Hank hooks the arms and executes a Backdrop Driver. Complete with pin in the latter stages of a match. Unnamed Spots: 1. Hank holds his opponent up in a Chokebomb position but drops them onto his knee with an inverted atomic drop instead. Thereafter, he rebounds off the ropes with a Shotei. 2. Wright's Irish Whip is followed up by a Clothesline. He then goes to do the same in the opposite corner but slams on the brakes and sends him back to where he came from and follows up with a running shoulderblock into the gut. Thereafter, he'll look to complete the sequence with a superplex. 3. Similar to number 2, Hank will use two Irish Whips but in the second instance, the poor bugger will take a front turnbuckle instead, leading to an Avalance Belly-to-Back Suplex as opposed to a Superplex. 4. Oklahoma Stampede followed by a pin. When the opponent kicks out, Hank will keep the pressure on with a running legdrop to the head and follow that up with a second pinfall attempt. 5. Showing off his strength, Wright will plant his rival with a one-arm Bodyslam. As they try to get to their feet, he'll repeatedly pound them with axe handles to the back and follow up with three teaspoons of elbows to the spine. 6. Double Powerbomb will lead to one of two things. Neither option is appealing: A) Texas Cloverleaf. B) Turnbuckle Powerbomb. 7. After completing the diabolical Dragon Screw Neck Whip, Wright will follow it up with the point of the elbow to the neck or throat, depending on which body part they're favouring. 8. Leapfrog Body Guillotine. With the opponent still draped over the second rope, Hank exits, stands on the apron and executes a Standing Legdrop. 9. On the outside of the ring, Wright lifts his opponent up in the Fallaway Slam position and runs the twat into the ringpost. Then, he dumps him at the altar with a bodyslam to the floor. If he's a heel or in a bad mood, he'll think on and expose the concrete floor first. 10. Another spot on the outside: The Hurricane whips his opponent into the steel barricade and then, full head of steam, charges at them and deposits them into the crowd with a big boot or lariat to the face. 11. Hank puts on a 'resthold' (smirks) in the form an Abdominal Stretch for about five seconds. During that time, he also ushers in 3-4 elbows to the ribs and then reveals the real reason behind the 'resthold': Kentucky Bomb. 12) Repeated knees to the side of the head, he rebounds off the ropes and scores with a running kneelift. 13) An Irish Whip that sends his rival into the opposing turnbuckle with such velocity it turns them upside down and inside out (a la Michaels/Flair.) As they're up on the apron, Hank comes storming in and knocks them onto the steel barricade from the apron with a running Elbow Smash or big boot. 14) Snap Mare - Wright-off.
