What this is The fed's own biography page for the character Seymour Almasy, from the fed's 2009 site (acw.ewmania.com, the relaunch edition), as captured in the Wayback on 15 April 2009. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Seymour Almasy Real Name: Jason Seymour Wilson Nickname: "The Final Fantasy, The Protagonist" Born: June 12, 1981 Place of Birth: Long Island, NY Hometown: Yasoinaba, Japan (actually residing in Montreal, Quebec, Canada) Height: 5'8 Weight: 192 lbs. Blood type: O+ Eye color: Green Manager: None. Trainer: Toshiro Mifune's School of Everlasting Excellence (2005). Before that, the in-house training facilities of the UWF and Global Wrestling. Wrestler Style Wrestling style: Aerial artistry combined with striking and sports entertainment flair. Signature moves: Jecht Shot (Jump spinning roundhouse kick) Jecht Shot II (Running layout single-leg dropkick) Ether Strike (Rolling koppou kick) Deathtouch (Running shotei to opponent in the corner) Operation Babe Hunt! (Somersault tope con hilo, with theatrics) The Dirge of Cerberus (Inverted facelock swung into Ace crusher) Level 5 Brainbuster (Stiff vertical drop brainbuster) Illumina (Double-Arm Legsweep DDT) Princess Guard (Full-nelson knee facebuster) Top rope moonsault press Somersault bodyblock off the apron/top rope Wrist-Clutch inside cradle German suplex hold Hammerlock Northern Lights suplex hold Anklelock submission Finisher: Springboard gamengiri, aka Kaval's Tidal Wave. Finisher name: The Sublimely Magnificent Jecht Shot Mark III (Jecht Shot Mark III for short) Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: UWF, tSC, Global, Other/Previous teams: RPQ~! (w/ Max Queue), The Dark Ages (w/ Clyde), Dies Irae (w/ Dawn McFarlane, Lia Ambrosi, Vivica J. Valentine, & Virgil Ambrosi), The Youngbloods (w/ Lew Spectre, Kenny Mercury, & Jamie Shock), the Knightmare Brigade (w/ many members of the McClancy family) Other/Previous managers: Laura Winters (his ex-wife), Other Wrestling titles won: ACW World Champion, ACW TV Champion, ACW Scorpion Fighting Champion, tSC Championship, tSC Tag Team Championship, PTC Global Champion (3x), UWF World Champion (2x) Other Wrestling awards won: Second man to hold every championship in the UWF, Winner of the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, Winner of the Primetime Invitational Tournament, PTC Wrestler of the Year (2004), Winner of the 2005 ACW Endgame Match Biography Seymour Almasy came to All-Star Championship Wrestling in 2005 with the aim of finding himself. One could say he succeeded too well, capturing the ACW Television and Scorpion Fighting Championships in a match against Fejona Min and Kelly Flawless at breakOUT, winning the endGAME match in the same year by outlasting the legendary Alias, and becoming ACW World Champion at Legends II in a triple threat match against Khristian Keller and the champion, Max Danger. By the end of his tenure, though, it looked as if Almasy was on his way out of the sport. After losing the ACW World Title to Andy Sharp on Courage 100, Almasy addressed the criticism levied at him by many on the roster, and declared that he had done everything that he came to do. Of course, his exhortation for the void to be filled was somewhat empty, considering that Lord Lowell Dot Com closed the promotion soon after. Three years later, however, and ACW has risen from the ashes. So too has Seymour Almasy, who has been one of the leading faces of the promotion's comeback. Almasy himself states the reason for his return to the promotion is the fan-inclusive concept. A fan favorite for most of his career, and a competitor who thrives off the support of the fans, one must believe that Seymour wonders what he can do in a setting with a few thousand loyal, rabid fans at his back. One could imagine that regaining the ACW World Championship would be on his mind, as might winning the Spirit of ACW to complete a sweep of active ACW titles. Ask Almasy himself, however, and his goal is simple: Have fun.
