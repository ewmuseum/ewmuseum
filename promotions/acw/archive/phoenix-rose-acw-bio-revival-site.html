What this is The fed's own biography page for the character Phoenix Rose, from the fed's second site (acw.ewmania.com, titled "ACW: The Revival", 2004), as captured in the Wayback on 16 July 2004. The site's own menus and gallery images are not reproduced.
Phoenix Rose Rosey, Rose, Marc Real Name: Marcus Steiner Date of Birth: 26th September 1979 Hometown: Wigan, near Manchester, England Height: 5 feet 5 Weight: 220 lbs. Entrance Music: Private Eye - Alkaline Trio Alignment Information Fan Dispostion: Face Current Teams: None Manager(s): None Allies: The Mammoth & Marshall Enemies: Geo Vacton Move-List Information Wrestling Style: All-rounder Signature Moves: : implant DDT, snap suplex, release German suplex, hurricaranna, double under-hook suplex, gut wrench power bomb, short arm clothesline, super kick, spear, diving head butt, jumping roundhouse kick, full nelson slam, Samoan drop, diving elbow drop (from the top rope), death valley driver, belly to belly suplex, pile driver, sharpshooter, single leg Boston crab, Moonsault dropkick (in the corner). Set Up Move: Ashes to Ashes - elevated inverted atomic drop Finisher: Dust to Dust - Phoenix climbs to the top rope and faces out towards the audience before jumping backwards in to the ring, spinning around 180° and hitting a frog splash to his downed opponent. Historic Information ACW Title History: None ACW Award History: None Other Federations: OWL(v1, 5), ACW (v1), EYE (v1, v2), DWF, RUSH, FWF, ZDW, SE, LOW, NGW, hWo, Core Wrestling Other Federation Awards: OWL Outright champion x2, EYE Gold champion (x2), LOW Tag Team Champion, Core Wrestling Continental champion (x1) Biography These days’ rotten childhoods are very fashionable in the world of wrestling. People tell you that they’ve had it tough, they try to make out like their life has been so much harder than anyone else’s. Marcus Steiner, or Phoenix Rose as he is more commonly known is not one of these people. Marc hasn’t had the worst life imaginable, he hasn’t had to fight to get to where he is today, as a matter of fact things seem to have dropped right in to his lap. Sure, he’s had a few mishaps in his life that have sidetracked him, but who hasn’t? Marc’s life began in a small town called Wigan, near Manchester, England. He came from a working class family, which meant that he did not have all of the material things a child in this day and age ‘need’ to get on in life, but he coped. He didn’t get bullied, he didn’t get shunned from his peers, and he managed to get quite a few close friends throughout his teenage years. This is where the previously mentioned sidetracking begun. You see, Marc’s friends weren’t the most innocent group of people you had ever come across, in fact they were scumbags, in to drugs and drink, but for years Marc managed to stay clean. After a few years of close friendship with what he saw as his best friend, Daniel Lenegan, Daniel asked Marc to take a ride with him whilst he ran some errands. Little did Marc know that the errands were actually drug runs, and little did Daniel know that he was under surveillance. Both men were arrested and charged with drug dealing. Marc ended up serving a three year long stretch in ‘strangeways’, a notorious prison in Northern England. His new cellmate, Brad Nemain, taught Marc a few valuable lessons about the prison system. “Rule number one: Make an impact. You can’t just sit around like some pussy and expect to get through your stretch without getting in to a fight or two. Fuck a few people up bad early in your sentence and people will know you mean business.” Brad told Marc. From there he would explain the other rules. “Rule number two: Always… Always have a stash.” Drugs ran that prison; all the big guys dealt drugs to the inmates. This kept the cellmates happy with the dealers and kept people off their back. It also ensured the dealers safety once they got a crew together to protect the head dealer. Marc set to work on making an impression, roughing a few people up to make an impression, and what an impression he made. People feared him for the three years that he served, but as his sentence came to an end Marc soon realised that once he got back in to society he had to clean up his act and get back to work on being an upstanding citizen once again. As Marc left the prison he realised that in the three years he had been inside nobody had even visited him. He hadn’t heard from his friends or family in three full years. Realising this he set off to find his parents and his sister. The home that once had been the Steiner residence was now the home of one Mrs. Linden, a nice middle-aged woman who was no help to Marc in his quest to find his family. In fact, nobody was any help – no matter where he looked, no matter whom he asked, nobody seemed to know anything, and if they did know they sure weren’t sharing. Marc also came to the realisation that he needed a job to afford the rent on a flat in the local vicinity. He managed to get a job working in a local gym six days a week. He spent quite a lot of his time in there after hours working out just as a hobby. Once in a while he would go to power lifting shows to support the members of his gym that took part in the competitions. At one of these events he met a wrestling promoter who mentioned that Marc had a certain look to him. He wrote down a telephone number and told Marc to ring him the next day. The next day came and Marc called the promoter, learning that his name was Anthony Davis and that he ran a small wrestling promotion named North-England Wrestling Federation (NEWF) Here would be the place that Marc came up with his Phoenix Rose persona that is so well known these days. This promotion put Marc on the long road to success in professional wrestling. From there he would go on to become a world-renowned competitor in the wrestling world.
