Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A recruitment post from 17 May 1997 dates the promotion's own run at that point to just over six months, calling itself Classic Wrestling Federation and pitching itself plainly: “For over 6 monthes, the CWF is where the big dawgs of e-wrestling have played. We are a booked e-fed, where your roleplays determine who wins the gold.” It billed real wrestlers as its own roster rather than invented characters, naming champions running “from eddie gilbert to bret hart” and advertising DDP and the Steiners as still open to sign.

By 27 August 1997 the promotion was casting for a roleplayer to inherit “the Hart Foundation of the CWF” whole — Junior Heavyweight Champion Bret Hart, Chris Benoit, and Owen Hart — after the stable's previous writer had been “relieved of contract” for reasons the post doesn't name, with the group already booked into a triple, six-man, three-team WarGames match at the next pay-per-view. Applicants were asked to roleplay at least five times a week, the league's stated minimum, and the same post claims a run of “over ten months” and “many accolades” won along the way.

No card or result survives despite that claimed run. Whether the Hart Foundation found a new writer, or how the WarGames match came out, isn't in the surviving record.
