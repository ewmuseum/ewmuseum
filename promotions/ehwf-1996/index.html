A July 1996 e-fed whose surviving broadcast books a United States Title tournament through an all-real-wrestler roster and announce team. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The sole surviving broadcast for this promotion, “Wednesday Night War” on 31 July 1996, opened with an announce team of Vince McMahon, Tony Schiavone, and Joey Styles hyping an eight-man United States Championship tournament and a Television Championship defense by Tommy Dreamer against Buh-Buh Ray Dudley.

The tournament ran a full bracket. Yokozuna beat Sid with the Banzai Drop, Bam Bam Bigelow beat The Giant, and The Undertaker and Gary Albright each won their own quarterfinals to reach the semifinals, where Bam Bam Bigelow beat Yokozuna and Albright beat the Undertaker.

The final never happened as a match. The broadcast announced that “due to an injury, the docters will not allow Bam Bam Bigelow to wrestle tonight,” and declared Gary Albright the tournament winner, and the promotion's first United States Champion, by default.

Buh-Buh Ray Dudley took the Television Championship from Dreamer the same night, and the broadcast closed promising a sixteen-team World Tag Team Championship tournament that Sunday.

No further document survives. Whether that tag tournament, or any card after it, ever ran isn't in the surviving record.
