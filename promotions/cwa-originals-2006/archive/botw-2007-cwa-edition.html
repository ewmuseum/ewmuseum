January 13th, 2007 CWA version hosted at Prospera Center in Chilliwack, BC "Pain" by Three Days Grace plays as we see images of Vesuvius, Scavenger, Noah Skanes, Arizona, Sam

Night Dweller vs. Gravity "Mother" by Danzig plays as Night Dweller makes his way towards the ring. He receives a mixed reaction from the crowd as he is followed by Black Panther on his way to ringside. He then has a few words with Black Panther and then pulls himself onto the apron before he steps through the ropes into the ring.

Gravity pays them no mind as he makes his way down the ramp to the ring and then pulls himself onto the apron. Gravity then looks around at the crowd and simply shakes his head before he steps into the ring and referee Mike
Wagner calls for the bell.

Gravity then stands Night Dweller up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a vicious clothesline to the mat. Gravity follows that up with a leg drop across the throat and goes for another pin attempt. 1...2... kick out by Night Dweller.

Gravity stands Night Dweller up, whips him off the ropes and Night Dweller ducks a clothesline and then gives Gravity a reverse neck breaker to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gravity. Night Dweller then stands Gravity up and whips him through the ropes and out to the floor. Night Dweller then drops and rolls himself out of the ring to the floor as well. Dweller then gives Gravity a German suplex to the floor. Dweller then stands Gravity up and rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring as well. Night Dweller then stands Gravity up and then gives him a vertical suplex to the mat and then goes for the pin.
1...2... kick out by Gravity. Sammy Jo Johnson - This tournament features sixteen men and any of them could go all the way tonight. Steve Blake - Night Dweller is a multiple time CWA champ and Gravity is one of the strongest wrestlers I have ever seen.
