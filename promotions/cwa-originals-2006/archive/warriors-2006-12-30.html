December 30th, 2006 "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath plays as Arizona comes out onto the stage with a smile on his face, already looking smug before the last show of 2006 has even started. The crowd starts an "Arizona sucks" chant while he stands there and simply nods his head, looking around at the fans who apparently dislike him as much as ever. Arizona then makes his way down the ramp towards the ring before he rolls himself under the bottom rope and then quickly gets to his feet, a microphone already in hand.
