CWA (Originals, 2006) — Card

CWA Warriors, 17 February 2007

17 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 17th, 2007 There is no opening sequence this week as Shannon Skanes and Bruce Miller storm down the ramp and roll themselves into the ring. Shannon paces and seems to be out of control, so it's Bruce that speaks into the microphone first.
Bruce Miller - Last week, the Sensational Seven took the night off! See, we figured that with the kid facing
Vesuvius, we had a clean match on our hands. Scum like Samson and
Sam Grogan had the night off, so we figured this would be a good night for the CWA. I guess that goes to show what we know, even after twenty plus years in this business! Now, Vesuvius, you think you got one over on us! You think you have that title now and everyone has to kiss your fat ass! I got news for you, Vesuvius, the
Sensational Seven are about to make your life a living hell. That means that your little henchmen like Kindred, Orlock, and that turncoat son of a bitch Scavenger are going to suffer!
Shannon then grabs the microphone from Bruce. Shannon Skanes - Vesuvius, you really crossed the line last week. You crossed it and set fire to it! Your complete lack of respect for the CWA title and the turn made by Scavenger is proof that the CWA is on a downward spiral. Fans, I can tell you one thing. Anyone who messes with my son like we saw last week.... they pay the ultimate price! Vesuvius and his boys and about to feel a lot of pain, the kind that will affect the rest of their pathetic careers. I expected more from you, V-Man. I expected more from you,
Scav. From here on, I have my eyes wide open and you will wish you had left it alone and played nice. The Sensational Seven are going to do a lot of damage tonight.Shannon tosses the microphone out of the ring to ring announcer Jan Hawkins and then he starts towards the back. We then finally hear "Coming
Undone" by Korn as the opening credits roll, images of Vesuvius,
Scavenger, Bruce Miller, Shannon Skanes, Blair Drake, Orlock, Steve
Hawkins and Kelly Buchanan flashing across the screen before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms and Steve Blake are ready to call tonight's action.
VS. "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays as Chastity and Ophelia step out onto the stage where the crowd gives them a mixed response. The two of them then talk on their way down the ramp towards the ring and then high five one another befopre Ophelia takes her place in their corner and Chastity pulls herself onto the apron before stepping through the ropes into the ring. Their music then fades and we hear "Tainted Love" by Marilyn Manson plays as Julie and Neriah
Sweet make their way onto the stage. The rookie sisters make their way down the ramp quickly and Neriah slides under thew bottom rope into the ring where Casey Braun calls for the bell as Chastity puts the boots to Neriah. Chastity then stands Neriah up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a dropkick to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Great start to the night as two tag teams prove that the CWA women's division is top notch.
Steve Blake - No offense to these ladies, but we are all wondering just how ugly tonight will get with the Sensational Seven on the war path.Ricky Simms - I will agree with you there. The violence could be pretty extreme.
Chastity pulls Neriah to her corner and tags in Ophelia. Ophelia and Chastity then set Neriah for and deliver a double vertical suplex on her. Ophelia then stands Neriah up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline as Neriah ducks it and then she gives Ophelia a series of knife edge chops that back her up to the ropes. Neriah then whips Ophelia into her corner where Julie is waiting with a boot out and she kicks Ophelia in the jaw with it.
Neriah then tags in Julie and then the two of them whip her off the ropes and give her a double backdrop to the mat. Julie then stands
Ophelia up while Neriah exits the ring. Julie then whips Ophelia into a neutral corner before she then gives her a monkey flip to the middle of the ring.Ricky Simms - The Sweet
Sisters have impressed early on in their CWA careers. Steve Blake - They have held their own against veteran divas in this company.Julie stands Ophelia up, whips her off the ropes and Ophelia takes a shoulder block from Julie and doesn't budge. Ophelia then thumbs Julie in the eye and then picks her up by the hair and slams her face first to the mat before tagging in Chastity. Ophelia then picks Julie Sweet up and then
Chastity launches herself off the top and gives Julie a wicked kick to the chin, sending her to the mat. Chastity then hooks the leg for the pin. 1...2...3! The Glamour Girls score the win in a close one over the Sweets.Ricky Simms - Strong showing here by the Sweet Sisters, but Chastity and Ophelia get the win.Steve Blake - Good match, further proof that the ladies that work for this company play second fiddle to no one.We then look to the beach where we see ocean waves crashing in and the sun setting on the horizon. We then see Brandon Caldwell entering the picture, a smile on his face.
Brandon Caldwell - This is Brandon Caldwell coming to you from sunny and warm California. This week, let's discuss the large shadow
I live in. You see, Steve Caldwell was my father and, when I started wrestling, the comparisons of me to my father were made right away.
I felt the need to be someone I wasn't. I felt the need to become a legend overnight and we see where that got me. I have since realized that I need to be me.Brandon sits down on a blanket in the sand and then grabs a bottle of champagne from a bucket of ice next to him. He then pours himself a glass of it and takes a sip before he speaks to the camera again.
Brandon Caldwell - When I saw what happened last week with Vesuvius and Scavenger showing a complete lack of ethics, I realized that is not the life for me. Soon, you will see me inside a CWA ring again. I will not be a man trying to be Steve Caldwell version two.
I will be the man who is set to make a name for himself. I will stand tall, look every man in the face who I oppose and I will become the champion I was meant to be.He then raises his glass towards the camera and we fade out and then go back to the ring.
VS. "Hate Me" by Blue October plays as the Grogan Twins come out to a loud chorus of boos from the fans. They then make their way down the ramp and then jump onto the apron at the same time, the only difference in their appearance being their names on the leg of their tights. It's Ben who steps through the ropes into the ring to start the match. Their music then fades and we hear "Bonecracker" by
Shocore and Blair Drake and Steve Hawkins step out onto the stage to a loud ovation from the crowd. Both men act shocked by such a positive reaction and then they both slowly make their way down the ramp to the ring. Blair and Steve then play a game of paper, rock, scissors to decide who gets to start the match and Blair wins that right while Hawkins heads to their corner. Blair steps through the ropes into the ring and referee Mark Davis calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - This is out first chance to see the Grogans in a tag team match.Steve Blake - Blair and
Steve are one of the best teams out there, so they should be smart enough to smell a switch if and when it happens.
Ben and Blair stand nose to nose and have a few unkind words. Blair then slaps Ben across the face and then ducks a right hook from Ben before giving him an atomic drop and then gives him a side
Russian leg sweep to the mat. Blair then goes for the quick pin.
1...2... kick out by Ben Grogan. Blair stands Ben up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a backdrop to the mat. Blair then stands Ben up and whips him through the ropes and out to the floor.
Blair then drops and rolls out to the floor after Ben and then starts putting the boots to him while nearby fans cheer.
Ricky Simms - The fans love watching the Grogan kids take a beating.Steve Blake - They're weasels and the fans know it.Blair stands Ben up and whips him towards the ring steps. Instead, Ben jumps onto the ring steps, turns and gives Blair a clothesline to the floor. Ben then stands Blair up and slams his head into the ring apron and then rolls him into the ring before rolling himself into the ring. Ben then stands Blair up and whips him into his corner where Sam gives him a punch to the face. Ben then tags Sam in and the two of them set Blair for and deliver a double vertical suplex on him. Sam then stands Blair up while Ben leaves the ring. Sam whips Blair into a neutral corner and then gives him a sidewalk slam to the mat from there and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Blair Drake. Sam then stands Blair up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a dropkick to the mat. Sam then goes to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He poses there for the booing fans and then he leaps off, missing with a frog splash when Blair rolls out of the way. Blair then rolls to his corner and tags in Steve Hawkins.
Ricky Simms - I smell a momentum shift. Steve Blake - I would agree there.Hawkins enters the ring and gives Sam a clothesline and then gives a charging Ben a backdrop to the mat. Hawkins then picks Ben up and slams him to the mat and then puts the boots to him as Ben rolls out of the ring. Hawkins then turns and ducks a right hook from Sam
Grogan before giving him an inverted atomic drop. Hawkins then gives
Sam a double knife edge chop that sends him down to the mat. Hawkins then gives Sam a knee drop to the forehead and goes for the pin.
1...2... Ben pulls Hawkins off of his brother. Ricky Simms - Ben saved the day there as they were on their way to a three count loss.Steve Blake - There is a really good chance that was going to be the case, yes.
Hawkins gets into a brawl with Ben on the floor. Hawkins then turns and rolls himself back into the ring. While the referee has a few words with Ben Grogan about his tactics, Sam Grogan drills
Hawkins between the eyes with brass knuckles and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The Grogans win as Blair fails to make the save in time. Sam Grogan bids a hasty retreat from the ring as Blair pleads to the referee for a reversal of the decision, but it stands and
Blair screams at the Grogan brothers, letting them know that this isn't over by a long shot.Ricky Simms -
Another slimy win for the weasels.Steve Blake - They're going to be a royal pain to the tag team division in the CWA.As the victorious brothers continue to celebrate, we look to the parking lot where Tough Eagle is sitting in the rain, his head down. He is approached by Fixxxer.
Tough Eagle - It's been a tough week. Fixxxer - I am certain my brother had his reasons. Tough Eagle - Twenty years of wrestling at his side and, for the first time, I don't know who he is.Fixxxer
- Scavenger is my brother. I know how you feel, but... Tough Eagle - I have to be the one to hold him accountable. Fixxxer - I would just steer clear for now. Tough Eagle - I need to make sure he sees that this is the wrong path. Vesuvius is a very bad man.Fixxxer -
He proved that last week, yes, but I don't think... Tough Eagle - I have come a long way, created a great legacy and
I created it with the help of Scavenger. Now, that is all tainted and I need to stand up and fight for the right things in this world.
Fixxxer - He's not himself. I don't want to see bloodshed between you two.Tough Eagle - Sometimes, blood has to be spilled. Sometimes, that is just how it has to be.
Tough Eagle wanders off and Fixxxer is left shaking his head. We head back to the ring.We then look to the ring where we see Lauren making her way to the ring, shaking her head as she does so. As she steps through the ropes, we hear "My
Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative and we see Scavenger coming out to loud boos from the crowd. Scavenger runs down the ramp and slides into the ring where Lauren already has a microphone.
Lauren - Jake, you didn't have to come out here. Scavenger - Lauren, this is an issue between you and me. Lauren - Jake, I cannot stand by what you did. You are my husband and I love you, but last week was wrong.
Scavenger - Last week was business baby. Lauren - No, Scav, last week was wrong. You were wrong. You sent that kid to the hospital and handed Vesuvius the world title.
Scavenger - Look, I have been wrestling for twenty years for these fans and I have nothing to show for it. I'm expected to be comic relief and I am sick of it. I am a legend inside this ring and
I'll be damned if I am going to watch some kid hold the CWA title because of who his father is.Lauren -
Jake... what happened to you? This isn't you talking. Scavenger - The hell it isn't! I am done being the nice guy and talking to a damned chair for these idiots! I am going to be a champion and you, as my wife, will do as I say!
Lauren - Pardon?Scavenger - I am the husband. You raise my kid, cook my meals and, if you're lucky, I stay with you!Lauren slaps Scavenger across the face. He then grabs her wrist and squeezes it. Lauren's eyes fill with tears and she falls to her knees.
Scavenger - Hit me again! Come on! Hit me again! Let's go! Lauren pleads with her husband while the fans boo. We then see
Danny Dixon and Jimmy Jack charging into the ring. They pull
Scavenger off his wife and then gives him a double clothesline over the top rope and out to the floor. They then stand on either side of
Lauren and dare Scavenger to come back into the ring. Scavenger yells and hits the apron and then starts making his way up the ramp towards the back.Ricky Simms - Thank God they came out.Steve Blake - Scavenger has snapped. He has gone to the dark side.Danny and Jimmy help Lauren out of the ring and help her towards the back while Tony Parizi steps out onto the stage and looks a little upset by what he has seen. He tells the camera that a real man deals with other men, that they don't pick on their wives. Parizi then goes to
Dixon and Jack and has a few words with them before asking if Lauren is all right.
VS. "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J plays as Jon Shocker makes his way out onto the stage, marking his return to the active roster to the delight of the fans. He stands on the stage for a few moments and beats his chest to the beat of the music. He then starts down the ramp only to be attacked by Samson. Samson gives Shocker a double axe handle smash to the back and then gives him a fall away slam to the steel ramp. Samson then stands Shocker up and drags him down to ringside and then slams his forehead into the steel ring steps.
Samson then rolls Shocker into the ring before rolling himself into the ring where referee Joe Davidson then calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Samson is making his usual start to the match. Steve Blake - It's a rude welcome for Jon Shocker, a man who was nearly the CWA champion on more than one occasion.
Samson then stands Shocker up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a shoulder tackle to the mat before going for the pin.
1...2... kick out by Shocker. Samson then stands Shocker up, whips him into the corner buckle and then misses with a shoulder block when Shocker steps out of the way. Shocker then gives Samson a hip toss to the mat and then follow that up with an abdominal stretch on him. Samson struggles against the hold and refuses to submit when asked by the referee.Ricky Simms- Shocker is working through ring rust issues early here.
Steve Blake - Shocker, as I said, was a great wrestler in the past and I believe he can be that again.
Samson reaches for the ropes and manages to grab the top rope which forces the break of the hold. Shocker then gives Samson a series of kicks to the ribs and then whips him off the ropes and then gives him a backdrop to the mat before giving him an elbow smash top the throat and then he goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Samson.
Shocker then stands Samson up, whips him off the ropes and goes for a shoulder block, but Samson stands tall and then gives Shocker an elbow to the jaw and then a series of knife edge chops to the chest that send Shocker into the corner. Samson then knees Shocker un the gut and then gives him a double under hook back breaker before he goes for the pin. 1...2... Shocker gets a leg on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Shocker is hanging in there. Steve Blake - Samson is one of the best in the CWA and he's not walking over Shocker at all.Samson stands
Shocker up and whips him through the ropes and out to the floor. Samson then has a few words with the referee while we see Goliath emerging from the fans at ringside. He blows salt into the eyes of
Jon Shocker and then retreats back into the crowd with a smile on his face. Samson then drops and rolls himself out to the floor and then gives a blinded Shocker a clothesline to the floor before standing him up and rolling him into the ring. Referee Joe Davidson then takes a close look at Jon Shocker and calls for the bell. He then starts screaming at Samson who claims he doesn't know what's happening.Ricky Simms - Samson has been disqualified after his father made an appearance to try and secure him the win!Steve Blake - Good call by Joe there, very impressed with the DQ.Samson argues while Joe Davidson rolls Shocker out of the ring and then starts helping him to the ramp. Samson just stands in the ring and shakes his head before slumping his shoulders and then he drops and rolls himself out of the ring, resigned to the loss. We then look backstage where Lauren is being looked at by a doctor when Fixxxer and Tough Eagle enter the room.Tough Eagle
- We just heard... Are you all right?Lauren - I'll be fine.Fixxxer - I don't know what happened to make my brother snap like this. Lauren - He's never been like this.Tough Eagle - I'll talk to him.Lauren - You can't. He is beyond that.Tough Eagle - Look, you may be his wife, but you are my niece. No one touches you. No one.Fixxxer - We have your back if you need any help.Lauren - Thanks guys. I'll be fine. It's a mild sprain to my wrist and no big deal.
Tough Eagle - Oh, it's a big deal. Scavenger is a dead man. Eagle and Fixxxer then storm out of the room while Lauren breaks down and starts crying, then we head back to the ring.
VS. "Buttons" by Pussycat Dolls plays as a tuxedo-clad Corina makes her way onto the stage where the crowd gives her a mixed reaction.
She has a band aid on her forehead as a memento from last week's match. She quickly makes her way down the ramp and then rolls herself into the ring before quickly getting to her feet. She then tosses her diva title belt over the top rope and out to the timekeeper and then she paces inside the ring while her music fades.
We then hear "Stinkfist" by Tool and Kelly Buchanan comes out to an equally mixed reaction. She has a large bandage on her forehead and she isn't smiling as she slowly makes her way down the ramp, wearing a tuxedo as per the agreement before the match. Kelly stops at ringside and looks around at the fans and then pulls herself up onto the ring apron before stepping through the ropes into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Twice now these women have nearly killed one another for the right to hold this title and tonight is part three.
Steve Blake - Kelly has never looked this focused. Corina could be in deep trouble tonight.As Kelly and
Corina have a few words in the middle of the ring, the diva lumberjacks make their way down the ramp to take their places at ringside. Referee Casey Braun then calls for the bell and Corina and
Kelly start trading punches to the delight of the fans. Kelly then thumbs Corina in the eyes, whips her into the corner buckle and then lands a series of shoulder blocks to the gut on Corina before she then gives her a belly to belly overhead suplex to the mat. Kelly then quickly gets to her feet and starts putting the boots to
Corina. Kelly then stands Corina and whips her right over the top rope and into the arms of the waiting lumberjacks. Kathy Smith and
Julie Sweet catch Corina and then roll her back into the ring where
Kelly then applies a boot choke on Corina, using the top rope for added leverage.Ricky Simms - Kelly is again showing that vicious streak.Steve Blake -
Kelly is a well rounded competitor inside the ring and she apparently wants to hurt Corina tonight.
Kelly then stands Corina up, whips her off the ropes and both women clothesline each other to the mat, causing referee Casey Braun to start counting them both down. On the five count, Corina kips up to her feet. On the six count, Kelly does the same only to take a super kick to the jaw from Corina. Corina then whips Kelly through the ropes and into the pack of lumberjacks. She is caught by Chastity,
Ophelia and Mary Sanderson. The three of them then give Kelly a power slam to the floor. Mary then stands Kelly up and rolls her into the ring, a smile on Mary's face while Chastity and Ophelia high five one another.Ricky Simms - The diva lumberjacks are trying to make a difference here.
Steve Blake - Looks like Ophelia and Chastity have picked sides between their former partners. Mary's just sadistic and it wouldn't have mattered who she could take a cheap shot at.
Corina stands Kelly up, whips her into the corner buckle and then gives her a knee to the gut followed by a tilt a whirl back breaker to the mat. Corina then pulls Kelly's jacket off and then starts putting the boots to her while tossing the jacket out to the lumberjacks who, in turn, toss it into the crowd. Corina then tears at the shirt, ripping it to pieces as she tears it off of Kelly and then she starts to choke Kelly with it. Kelly answers with an eye gouge on Corina and then whips her off the ropes before giving her a round house kick that sends her to the mat. Kelly then starts ramming Corina's face into the mat repeatedly while the referee threatens to disqualify Kelly Buchanan from this match. Kelly then looks to ringside at Ophelia and Chastity and has a few unkind words for them. Kelly then pulls off Corina's jacket and tosses it from the ring and then she stands Corina up and slams her forehead into the corner buckle. Kelly then picks Corina up and gives her a scoop slam to the mat.Ricky Simms - It looks like
Corina is bleeding again.Steve Blake - Yeah, this is turning ugly as we expected that it would. Kelly gives Corina a knee to the forehead and then tears off her tuxedo shirt. Kelly then stands Corina up, whips her into the corner buckle and then misses with a shoulder block in the corner when
Corina steps out of the way and Corina then gives Kelly a knee to the ribs. Corina then gives Kelly a German suplex to the mat, stands her up and gives her another. Corina then gives Kelly a series of forearm smashes to the forehead, also busting Kelly open again.
Corina then stands Kelly up and tangles her arms between the top and middle rope. Corina then grabs Kelly pants and pulls them off, tearing them from Kelly and then Corina doesn't stop there. Even as the referee has rung the bell and declared Corina the winner, Corina starts biting the forehead of Kelly Buchanan.
Ricky Simms - Corina keeps the title yet again, but she has completely gone off the deep end!Steve
Blake - This is sick.Corina drops and rolls herself out to the floor, the lumberjacks backing off while some of them head up the ramp towards the back. Corina reaches under the ring and pulls out a baseball bat. Kathy Smith sees this and makes her way around the ring to try and talk some sense into Corina.
Corina gives her a kick to the gut and then a clothesline with the baseball bat while the referee frees Kelly Buchanan from the ropes.
Kelly rolls herself out to the floor where Neriah and Julie Sweet help her make her way to the ramp. The bat wielding Corina rolls herself into the ring and looks stunned to find Kelly isn't here.
Corina then throws the bat towards the retreating Kelly with the
Sweet Sisters. Corina then grabs her belt from the mat and holds it high while the fans boo her. Corina spits blood on the mat and smiles as she gazes at her title.Ricky
Simms - This woman is completely off her rocker. Steve Blake - Kelly just couldn't match her crazed intensity in repeated attempts. She got close in the ladder match, but Corina is still the champion.While Corina finally leaves the ring with the belt over her shoulder, we see Lauren leaving the arena when she is headed off by Steve Hawkins.
Lauren - Not now, Steve.Steve Hawkins - I just wanted to see that you were all right. Lauren - I'm fine. The doctor gave me a soft cast to hold it in place and I'll be fine.Steve Hawkins -
Where are you staying tonight?Lauren - I am going to stay with my uncle.Steve Hawkins - I don't know what has got into Jake.Lauren - I already told you, not now.Steve Hawkins - Yes, so you did. Look, if you need anything... Lauren - I just want to get out of here and forget tonight ever happened.Lauren exits through the back door and Steve looks on, clearly a little rattled with what has happened here tonight. We then return to the ring.
VS. "My Girlfriend's Girlfriend" by Type O Negative plays as Scavenger and Vesuvius step out onto the stage to the loudest boos of the night. Vesuvius holds the CWA title high and then the two of them make their way down the ramp, Vesuvius tell the camera that he isn't scared of anyone. The two of them then pull themselves up onto the apron and then each steps through the ropes into the ring while their music fades. We then hear "The Hand That Feeds" by Nine Inch
Nails and Shannon Skanes and Bruce Miller make their way onto the stage to what is the loudest ovation of the night. They then race down the ramp and slide under the bottom rope into the ring and then they start brawling with Vesuvius and Scavenger as referee Mark
Davis calls for the bell.Ricky Simms - A great main event tonight as these forces collide.
Steve Blake - I am a little surprised that Scavenger and Vesuvius have the courage to be out here right now, considering everything that happened this week and last.Bruce gives Vesuvius a clothesline over the top rope to the floor and
Shannon does the same to Scavenger. Shannon and Bruce then high five one another and motions Scavenger and Vesuvius to get back into the ring. No sooner have they done this than Orlock, Kindred and Everest are inside the ring and then they attack Shannon and Bruce. Once the referee calls for the bell and disqualifies Vesuvius and Scavenger, they are back inside the ring and jumping in to make the beat down a five on two.Ricky Simms - It was a set up!
Steve Blake - Anarchy Inc. set up the two leaders of the Sensational Seven to take a beating tonight! The beat down continues as the fans boo. We then see Danny Dixon,
Jimmy Jack, Trevor Lang and Cody Anderson making their way down the ramp and all four of them have chairs in hand. The crowd cheers as they chase off Anarchy Inc. and then they stand inside the ring and motions them back inside. Vesuvius smiles and tells them that this is just the beginning and that Anarchy will rule the company.
Ricky Simms - He might be as crazy as Corina. Steve Blake - Yes, but we expect that from Vesuvius. The rescuers attend to Bruce and Shannon who are both bleeding from the forehead. Medics rush into the ring while Cody Anderson gets the microphone tossed to him by ring announcer Jan Hawkins.
Cody Anderson - So this is how you bastards want to play? Here's a simple suggestion. Next week, this ring will witness an old fashioned CWA rumble and you had better find two friends. The
Sensational Seven have hereby challenged you to a single elimination rumble! Vesuvius, you want to be the big man? You want to run the company? Take on its best, big man and bring that wife beater
Scavenger with you.Cody tosses the microphone out of the ring and we see Vesuvius nodding and smiling. Ricky Simms - It would appear that Vesuvius has accepted this proposal.Steve Blake - Next week, we will see some real violence. This should be a really great clash between two of the most powerful factions that this company has ever known.
Ricky Simms - That's the end for tonight, fans. Tune in next week for that huge main event and more as the stars of the CWA show you why we are really the very best in the world!
We fade to black as Shannon Skanes and Bruce Miller get to their feet inside the ring and wave off the medics. They will be back next week and apparently, so will Noah Skanes.
