CWA (Originals, 2006) — Card

CWA Warriors, 10 February 2007

10 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 10th, 2007 The show opens as "Coming Undone" by Korn plays and we see images of Vesuvius, Noah Skanes,
Corina, Kelly Buchanan, Bruce Miller, Arizona, Steve Hawkins, Stan
Smith and Xandra before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms and
Steve Blake are all set to call the action for tonight.
VS. "Beautiful Stranger" by Madonna plays as Zack McCracken comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He then makes his way down the ramp and rolls himself into the ring and then calls for the microphone from ring announcer Jen Hawkins.
Zack McCracken - Tonight I win back my fair love. Tonight Felicity will see that I am a champion when I square off for the title against Steve Hawkins and I become the new title holder. Yes, it will be a grand evening. No one can stop me from becoming a champion after all these years of toiling about in front of you ingrate fans.
Zack tosses the microphone down to the mat and then we hear "Space Lord" by Monster Magnet and Steve Hawkins comes out to a loud ovation from the crowd. Hawkins has the title belt over his shoulder as he marches down the ramp and then makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron. Steve then hands off his title belt to the referee who passes it on to the timekeeper while Hawkins enters the ring. Referee Mark Davis then calls for the bell as the two men lock up.
Ricky Simms - I don't understand why Zack tries. Hawkins is going to kill him someday if this continues.
Steve Blake - Zack is in his own world. It's a very deluded place. Hawkins shoves Zack into the corner buckle and then gives him a clothesline in the corner. Hawkins then rains down punches to the forehead on Zack while the fans count them. After ten punches, Steve then gives Zack a vertical suplex to the middle of the ring. Steve then stands over Zack and nods towards the crowd. He then stands Zack up, sets for a DDT and jumps to the second rope before falling back and delivering the DDT on the challenger. Steve then rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Steve Hawkins wins the match and retains his title.
Ricky Simms - We didn't see that coming. Steve Blake - Yeah, that surprised the hell out of me... Hawkins rolls himself out of the ring and starts making his way up the ramp while we go to footage of a man we see only in shadow. He speaks, but his voice is altered as he does so.
Unknown Man - You see, the CWA is a friendly place. The established stars are safe. The rookies are having their time to learn the game. The thing is, sometimes someone needs to change the rules. Sometimes someone needs to change the way everyone views the business. Soon, I will change how you all see wrestling.
Soon, I will make my presence felt inside that ring and I will take no prisoners. I will destroy everyone in my path. I won't build a stable. I won't make friends. I will leave a trail of victims. I will be your new CWA champion. You have been warned.
The scene then turns to snow for a few moments before we return to ringside. Ricky Simms - Who the hell is that? Steve Blake - I have no clue. "Place For My Head" by Linkin Park plays as the five ladies who will battle it out for the Women's title stand inside the ring. The referee for the match, Myst, then tells them which two will be starting the match after a random draw backstage.
Kathy Smith and Shawna Miller stay inside the ring while Mary Sanderson, Sammy Jo Johnson and Xandra take their places in three corners of the ring. Referee Myst then calls for the bell and the match is underway. Kathy locks up with Shawna, then the two of them shove each other into the ropes. Shawna steps back and then lands a vicious forearm to the chin on Kathy before she then gives her a vertical suplex to the middle of the ring. Shawna then quickly gets to her feet and puts the boots to Kathy.
Ricky Simms - These two have been battling each other since the dawn of time.Steve Blake - It does seem fitting that these two would start this match.
Shawna stands Kathy up, whips her off the ropes and then gives her a backdrop to the mat and then goes for a leg drop across the throat only to have Kathy roll out of the way and Smith then quickly gets to her feet. Kathy then whips Shawna off the ropes and gives her a dropkick to the mat. Kathy then goes to the one neutral corner and then lands a falling head butt to the chest on Shawna before she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Shawna Miller. Kathy then stands Shawna up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline when Shawna ducks it only to take a thumb to the eye from Sammy Jo Johnson. A now blinded Shawna turns and takes a super kick to the jaw from Kathy who again goes for the pin. 1...2...3!
Kathy Smith eliminates Shawna Miller from the match. Ricky Simms - Thanks to an aid from Sammy Jo, Shawna is out. Steve Blake - The rivalry between Sammy Jo and Shawna goes way back as well.Myst then motions Mary
Sanderson into the ring as the next name drawn backstage in a match where all five women start out at the ring, but there will be no tagging in or out. Mary and Kathy start trading punches in the middle of the ring while the fans cheer. Mary then rakes the eyes of
Kathy and then picks her up and tosses her over the top rope and down to the floor. Mary then goes to one of the neutral corners and climbs to the top rope. She leaps off and lands a double axe handle smash across the back on Kathy. Mary then stands Kathy up and rolls her into the ring before rolling herself into the ring and then she goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kathy Smith and Mary then has a few words for Myst on the speed of her count.
Ricky Simms - Mary had better not piss the referee off. Steve Blake - Mary isn't one who is known for keeping her mouth shut.Mary then stands Kathy up, whips her into a neutral corner and then runs in for a shoulder block only to find no one home as Kathy dodges her and then rolls her into a school boy for the pin attempt. 1...2...3! Mary Sanderson has been eliminated. Mary then quickly gets to her feet and tells Myst that count was too fast. She takes a swing at Myst, but Myst ducks it and then gives Mary an atomic drop which sends her right out through the ropes to the floor. Myst then motions to Sammy Jo Johnson that she is next to enter the ring. Sammy Jo quickly enters the ring and ducks a right hook from Kathy and then gives her a Rock Bottom to the mat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kathy Smith.
Ricky Simms - Despite months up here calling matches, Sammy Jo looks solid in that ring.Steve Blake -
Sammy is always in shape. She is an underrated talent. Sammy stands Kathy up, whips her into a neutral corner and then gives her a fireman's carry that she turns into a Samoan Drop to the mat. Sammy Jo again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kathy who refuses to allow herself to be pinned tonight. Sammy Jo then stands
Kathy up, whips her off the ropes and then gives her a backdrop to the mat. Sammy Jo then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Kathy Smith before hooking the leg for the pin.
1...2...3! Sammy Jo Johnson scores a mild upset by taking Kathy
Smith out of the match.Ricky Simms - The crowd's in shock with that one.Steve Blake
- I really figured Kathy would win outright here once Mary Sanderson was out.Sammy Jo is barely to her feet when Xandra gives her a series of knife edge chops to the chest. Xandra then whips her into the corner buckle before giving her a monkey flip to the middle of the ring. Xandra springs to her feet and then lands an elbow across the throat on Sammy Jo before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Sammy Jo Johnson. Xandra then stands Sammy
Jo up, whips her off the ropes and both women clothesline each other to the mat. Myst starts counting them both down. On the three count,
Xandra kips up to her feet. On the four count, Sammy Jo does the same and then thumbs Xandra in the eye before whipping her through the ropes and out to the floor. Sammy Jo then drops and rolls herself out to the floor as well. Sammy Jo slams Xandra's face into the steel guard railing. Sammy Jo then whips Xandra towards the steel corner post only to have Xandra reverse it and she sends Sammy
Jo face first into the corner post instead. Xandra then rolls Sammy
Jo into the ring before rolling herself into the ring where she then goes for the pin. 1...2... Sammy Jo gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Great ring sense there by Sammy Jo. Steve Blake - Well, she has a lot of experience inside the ring, though many would have no idea.Xandra stands Sammy Jo up, whips her off the ropes and ducks her head for a backdrop only to take a kick to the face from Sammy Jo who then gives Xandra a swinging neck breaker to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Sammy Jo Johnson is the new women's champion!
Ricky Simms - One week removed from sitting here, Sammy Jo is now the title holder!Steve Blake - This represents hard work on her part. I am really happy for her.
Myst grabs the title belt and hands it to Sammy Jo. Sammy Jo then poses on the second rope, title belt in hand, while the crowd cheers her on. We then look outside the arena where Goliath is seen pacing in the parking lot. The camera zooms in on the CWA legend.
Goliath - You know, I have been watching my son's actions over the past couple of months. People keep asking me how I feel about
Samson cheating and taking shortcuts. People keep asking me why I don't step in there and try to stop him. People keep asking me when
I will start to make a difference in the CWA again. Fans, I am done watching what Samson does. Next week, I will step inside the ring once again and I will prove to everyone where I stand in regards to my son's recent actions. Samson, Daddy is going to give you exactly what you deserve in seven days.
VS. "Who Made Who" by AC/DC plays as Caleb Knox steps out onto the stage. He is only there for a few moments when we see Dan Jalldoff rushing out to start brawling with Caleb Knox. The two of them trade blows on the stage and then Jalldoff levels Knox with a clothesline to the stage. Jalldoff then drags Knox by the feet down the ramp while the fans cheer him on.Ricky Simms -
This battle has been going on for months and now they have a chance to settle it one on one.Steve Blake - This won't be a technical match, for sure.
Jalldoff stands Knox up and then whips him shoulder first into the steel ring steps. Jalldoff then rolls Knox into the ring before rolling himself into the ring and then referee Mark Davis calls for the bell to get the action officially underway. Jalldoff stands Knox up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a dropkick to the mat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Caleb Knox. Jalldoff then stands Knox up, whips him into the corner buckle and then lands a shoulder block on Knox before giving him a belly to belly overhead suplex to the middle of the ring. Knox again goes for the pin as we see Gravity making his way down the ramp. 1...2... kick out again by
Knox. Jalldoff then spots Gravity and tells the referee to get rid of him. Mark Davis shrugs and explains Gravity hasn't done anything to cause him to be sent away.Ricky Simms -
This doesn't look good for Jalldoff.Steve Blake - I'll agree with you there.Jalldoff has a few words for Gravity and then turns his attention back to
Caleb. Caleb dives and takes Jalldoff's knee out with a chop block and then pulls Dan to the middle of the ring where he applies an ankle lock submission on Jalldoff. Jalldoff refuses to submit while
Gravity looks on from ringside with a smile on his face. That fades when he sees Syckle marching down the ramp and looking like he means business. Jalldoff rolls onto his back and then kicks Caleb Knox into the corner buckle. He then gets to his feet before Caleb then gives him another chop block to the mat. Caleb then turns Jalldoff over and applies a Boston Crab on Dan while the referee then checks for a submission. Syckle then starts chasing Gravity around the ring. Gravity then slides into the ring and then rolls out another side with Syckle close behind, causing referee Mark Davis to become distracted and start shouting at Syckle and Gravity. We then see another man rolling himself into the ring with a chair in hand. He clubs Caleb over the head with the chair before he rolls himself back out and Jalldoff goes for the pin. 1...2...3!
Ricky Simms - Dan Jalldoff scores the win tonight as Ghostly Figure interfered!Steve Blake - The masked menace is back in the CWA!Gravity spots Ghostly Figure and then he turns and exits through the crowd while a downed Caleb lies motionless on the mat. Jalldoff exits the ring and extends his hand towards The Ghostly Figure. Figure then clotheslines Dan Jalldoff to the floor and then heads around the ring where he sees Syckle and the two nod towards each other.
Ricky Simms - It looks like Figure did this to get back at Gravity here and not to help Dan Jalldoff. Steve Blake - The name Jalldoff makes you unpopular right away. As Ghostly Figure and Syckle head up the ramp, we look to footage taken earlier in the day, Shannon Skanes standing in front of his home. He has a black suit on and sunglasses as the unseasonably warm weather continues for another week.Shannon
Skanes - I got to where I am because I am the best at what I do. Stan Smith got to where he is because his sister is good at what she does. Stan, we will meet inside the ring again very soon. You won't have Terminator or Killer to back you up. I am seeing to that, willing to make them an offer that they cannot refuse. I will then see you inside a steel cage. I will take you from pillar to post one last time, Stan. I will do it because I am man enough to do it and because I am sick of your complete lack of respect for people in this game. I won't tolerate you any more. This time, it won't be triple threat. This time, it's just me and you. This time, I will get my final win over you and then I can move along with my career.
I have better things to do than beat you every week until I retire. Shannon then heads into his home and we head back to the ring VS. "Buttons" by Pussycat Dolls plays as Corina steps out onto the stage, ready to settle who the real Diva champion is tonight. She makes her way down the ramp, the title belt not over her shoulder for the first time in a month. She then rolls herself under the bottom rope and into the ring. She then paces inside the ring while her music fades and then we hear "Stinkfist" by Tool and we see
Kelly Buchanan making her way towards the ring.
Ricky Simms - These women both held the title at the end of a brutal and revealing match last week. This week they settle the score.Steve Blake - We have no clue what tonight's stipulation is.As Kelly steps up onto the ramp, she asks for the microphone from ring announcer Jen
Hawkins. She then steps through the ropes into the ring.
Kelly Buchanan - Last week, you and I proved we will do whatever it takes to be the Diva champ. This week, let's keep it simple. The reason I suggested we wear street clothes to the ring is because I want a match with you that doesn't mean wrestling as much as battling and deciding who the superior woman is tonight. I challenge you to an I Quit match.Ricky Simms - The fans are cheering. They approve.Corina nods her head and referee Francis Clarke calls for the bell. Kelly
Buchanan then drills Corina between the eyes with the microphone and then whips her through the ropes and out to the floor. Kelly then drops and rolls herself out to the floor and then starts putting the boots to Corina. Kelly then grabs a nearby audio cable and then starts whipping Corina across the side with it. Corina is bleeding from the forehead as Kelly then wraps the audio cable around her throat and tries to choke her out with it.
Ricky Simms - Kelly is vicious tonight. Steve Blake - Yeah, the fans don't like this at all. The idea of mtutual respect has quickly dissolved in this feud.
Kelly releases the choke and then pulls Corina's T-shirt off. She then picks up the audio cable and starts whipping Corina across the back with it. Kelly then stands Corina up and slams her face into the apron and then rams her face into the steel ring steps twice before she then gives her a side Russian leg sweep to the mat.
Corina is spitting blood on the floor as Kelly shouts at the referee to ask her. Corina refuses to give up when asked, so Kelly stands her up and then rolls her into the ring. Kelly then reaches under the apron and pulls out a table. She slides the table into the ring before rolling herself into the ring.Ricky
Simms - I have never seen a diva busted open like this. Corina is a mess.Steve Blake - Kelly is on a mission.
She wants to destroy Corina and take that title belt. Kelly sets up the table and then turns her attention back to Corina. She eye gouges Corina and then places her on the table. Kelly then goes to the nearest corner and climbs to the top rope. Kelly then leaps off the top and goes through the table as Kelly rolls herself off in time to avoid disaster and a certain loss.
Corina then rolls herself out of the ring, staggering around as she tries to collect herself. Kelly then rolls herself out to the floor, clutching at her back as she does so. Corina reaches under the ring and pulls out a fire extinguisher. She sprays Kelly in the face with it and then uses it as a weapon, drilling Kelly's forehead with it and splitting her open as well. Corina then slams it into the gut of
Kelly before throwing it aside. Corina then puts the boots to Kelly before standing her up and whipping her shoulder first into the steel corner post. Corina then applies an abdominal stretch on the floor and the referee checks for a submission, but Kelly is not willing to give up.Ricky Simms - This is one bloody fight.Steve Blake - It's intense and these women are really starting to hate one another.
Kelly fights her way out of Corina's hold, giving her a hip toss to the floor. Kelly then lands a leg drop across the throat on
Corina and then places her in a sleeper hold. Again, Corina refuses to submit and gets to her feet and then rams Kelly's back into the steel guard railing twice to break free of the sleeper before she then gives Kelly a reverse head butt. Corina then turns and eye gouges Kelly and then places her in a headlock and gives her a DDT to the floor. Corina then stands over Kelly who is motionless on the floor. Francis Clarke checks on Kelly and then motions towards the back for medics while she calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Brutal end and Kelly never did say that she quit. Steve Blake - The girl is out cold. She kicked it up a notch and
Corina answered the call.Corina stands
Kelly up and rolls her into the ring. The referee tries to stop her, so Corina gives Francis Clarke a low blow before she then rolls herself into the ring. Corina then unbuttons Kelly's jeans and pulls them off, leaving her in her black panties while blood pours from her forehead. Corina then wraps the jeans around the top rope and then she pulls off Kelly's T-shirt. She ties the shirt to the jeans while medics look to enter the ring and aid the still motionless
Kelly. Corina then wraps the T-shirt which is now in the shape of a noose, around Kelly's neck while the fans boo.
Ricky Simms - Both of these girls crossed the line tonight and the fans are not impressed.Steve Blake -
They both snapped. That title belt made them both snap. Corina hangs Kelly from the top rope and then Corina drops and rolls herself out to the floor. She then gives Kelly a series of punches to the stomach while Kelly shows no signs of coming to.
Corina then heads to the ramp, the time keeper handing her the Diva title. Corina holds the belt high which draws the boos of the fans.
She just shakes her head and then starts towards the backstage area while we look to the GM office where Ray Dees is in Mark Johnston's face about what we just saw.Ray Dees -
Mark, that match was brutal. That can't be the end of this feud. Mark Johnston - We have two women who are clearly growing to hate each other more every week. I have a simply proposal. Next week, they have one last match. Next week, Corina faces Kelly Buchanan in a match where they battle each other for the right to be the Diva champ.Ray Dees - Are you adding a
stipulation?Mark Johnston - Yes I am. Last week, they showed they will wrestle nude if they have to. This week, they proved that they will bleed if they have to. It's that kind of dedication that causes me to propose the following. They meet inside the ring in a Lumberjack match. The thing is, the people at ringside will be women. They will make certain the match stays inside the ring and maintain order.Ray Dees - How many women will be there to contain them?Mark
Johnston - I will allow any woman on our roster to be there. The other stipulation is simple. They will each wear tuxedos and the winner is the one to strip the other out of it.
Ray Dees - But these girls will stop at nothing to be champion. Mark Johnston - Exactly. You see, the ratings for these matches have been through the roof. I would be a fool to not book this fight next week.Ray Dees - It should make for quite the battle.We then head back to the ring.
VS. "Mine Teil" by Rammstein plays as Vesuvius steps out onto the stage where the crowd gives him a standing ovation. He tells the camera that the title is coming back home tonight and then he makes his way down the ramp towards the ring with his arms raised and indicating that this will be his pose in a few minutes when he gets his chance to become the new CWA champion. He then rolls himself into the ring before quickly getting to his feet and then he poses in the corner buckle before his music starts to fade. We then hear
"My Way" by Limp Bizkit and CWA champion Noah Skanes steps out onto the stage where he gets the loudest ovation of the night.
Ricky Simms - This is the big title shot for Vesuvius! Steve Blake - The man worked to get this chance and now here it is. Let's see if he makes good on it.Noah makes his way down the ramp and then hands his title belt to the time keeper. He then makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron and then steps through the ropes into the ring. He then tests the ropes while referee Joe Davidson then calls for the bell. Noah and
Vesuvius lock up. Vesuvius shoves Noah into the corner buckle and then gives him a series of kicks to the ribs before he then gives him a double under hook suplex to the mat. Vesuvius then places Noah in a headlock and then gives him a bulldog to the mat before rolling him over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Noah Skanes. Vesuvius then stands Noah up, whips him off the ropes and Noah ducks a clothesline and gives Vesuvius a reverse neck breaker to the mat before he goes for the quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Vesuvius.
Ricky Simms - These men are dreaming if they think they can win this match so early.Steve Blake - They have had many battles and most go on for the entire show. It could happen again.Noah stands Vesuvius up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a knee to the gut and then a hip toss back to the middle of the ring where he then headlocks
Vesuvius and then sets for and delivers a vertical suplex to the mat. Noah then stands Vesuvius up and gives him a second vertical suplex to the mat and then goes for the pin again. 1...2... kick out by Vesuvius. Noah then stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and Vesuvius manages to hold the ropes while Noah goes for the dropkick and only connects with air before falling to the mat.
Vesuvius then puts the boots to the fallen champion as momentum takes another shift.Ricky Simms - This match is going back and forth as we expected.
Steve Blake - They are very evenly matched and they always have been. Vesuvius then stands Noah up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a shoulder block to the mat. He then gives Noah an elbow smash across the chest before hooking the leg for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Noah Skanes.
Vesuvius then stands Noah up, whips him into the corner buckle and then gives him a snap suplex from there back to the middle of the ring. Vesuvius then perches himself on the second rope and waits for
Noah to stand. Vesuvius then launches himself and lands a clothesline to the mat on the champion and goes for the pin.
1...2... Noah rolls a shoulder out and Vesuvius shakes his head. Ricky Simms - Vesuvius thought he had the three count on that one.Steve Blake - It was close, but Noah is the toughest wrestler in this company and won't stay down.
Vesuvius then stands Noah up and whips him through the ropes and out to the floor. We then see Orlock and Kindred making their way down the ramp and the fans actually boo them. Noah gets to his feet on the floor and spots Orlock and Kindred. He has a few words for them and then calls to the referee regarding them being here.
Referee Joe Davidson then has a few words for them while someone else jumps the guard railing. It's Scavenger. He shoves Noah face first into the steel corner post and then gives him a DDT to the floor. Scavenger then stands Noah up and delivers a spear that sends him to the floor again before rolling him into the ring.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is Scavenger doing? Steve Blake - Looks like the crazy one has turned to the dark side.Vesuvius climbs to the top rope as
Orlock and Kindred retreat towards the back. Vesuvius then lands a splash off the top rope onto the fallen Noah Skanes and then goes for the pin. Unaware of Scavenger's interference, the referee counts the pin. 1...2...3! Vesuvius wins and is the new CWA champion!
Ricky Simms - Vesuvius knew that the Sensational Seven weren't here tonight. Vesuvius somehow gets Scavenger to join Anarchy Inc.
and now he is the champion.Steve Blake - I don't like it either. I guarantee you we have not seen the last of this feud.Vesuvius holds his newly won belt high as the fans litter the ring with garbage and chant "Bullshit"
over and over. Vesuvius bows before them and then steps out onto the apron before dropping to the floor where Orlock, Kindred and
Scavenger are waiting to congratulate him on becoming champion. The four of them then start up the ramp while the referee checks on the fallen Noah Skanes.Ricky Simms - Well, fans, that's it for another edition of Warriors. See you again next time.Steve Blake - I think the heat just keeps getting cranked up.We fade to black as medics check on the now former champion inside the ring and Joe
Davidson looks around confused, aware that something else must have happened here tonight that he didn't see.
