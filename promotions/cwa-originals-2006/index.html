A fantasy federation told as events only on a tenant directory of its own, a weekly Warriors from 9 December 2006 and a Battle of the Warriors 2007 CWA Edition, with Overdrive cards in March 2007, and a CWA World Heavyweight, Inter-Continental and tag title history that begins in January 1984 and is given as running to 2009. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWA Originals is the owner's own home page for the CWA as a story, events only, begun, it says, in 1984 and not a roleplay fed; its index lists a weekly Warriors from 9 December 2006, a Battle of the Warriors 2007 CWA Edition, Warriors through February and Overdrive on 3 and 10 March 2007, mostly from Chilliwack, British Columbia.

Its title history gives a CWA World Heavyweight line from 1984 to a reign it dates to May 2009 and calls current, with Inter-Continental and tag lines beside it. Its champions are not those of the CWA of 2003 and 2004, nor of the 2008 CWA, so this is kept as its own wing beside them.
