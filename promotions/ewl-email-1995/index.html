Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The E-Mail Wrestling League's whole surviving record is two calls made after it had already closed. On 22 August 2001, a post from someone describing himself as the league's own owner from 4 November 1996 to 4 August 1999, and the handler of its wrestler Paul Blair, asked whether anyone from the newsgroup community had ever been part of “Email Wrestling League that closed in 1999,” floating a reunion card he was considering putting on later that year.

Seven weeks later, on 10 October 2001, a second call went out over the same signature line as the league's own founder and president, setting a firmer plan: a reunion show in mid November, open to anyone who had been part of the league during what this second call dates as its full run, November 1995 to September 1999.

Nothing else survives. No show, roster or title from any point of the run either call describes is on record, and whether the reunion the second call announced ever actually took place is not known either.
