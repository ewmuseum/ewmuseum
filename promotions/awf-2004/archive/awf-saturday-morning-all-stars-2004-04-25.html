AWF Presents.. All-Star Wrestling April 24, 1985 LIVE (taped) From the Baltimore Arena, Baltimore, Maryland ------------------------ [The opening sequence begins. Some generic sythesizer music plays as we see footage of professional wrestlers. Graphics of stars fly about the screen, and the voice of an announcer speaks.]
"Welcome, everyone, to American Wrestling Federation Saturday Morning All-Stars!" [The camera cuts to the Baltimore Arena. As it pans across the crowd, the adults and children wave and yell for the camera. Cut away to the commentators, who don't seem to be at the actual arena, but the crowd's behind them--- so who are you to judge? The graphics identify the two commentators. "Diamond" Jimmy Cage. He is dressed in a Hawaiian shirt, dark sunglasses, and covered in silver jewelry.
DK: Folks, welcome to the first edition of AWF All-Stars! I'm Dave
Kelso, and with me is "Diamond" Jimmy Cage! We got alot of action coming to you today, so let's not waste any time and get down to the ring!
