Application -Singles -Tag Team --About-- The ICW is a booked e-fed (cards are written by the staff, not by a simulator), which concentrates mainly on storylines and angles... Handlers are encouraged to start new angles, whether it be in their roleplays or by e-mailing the staff.
Roleplaying is done on a message board, cards are sent through e-mail and posted on the website (
http://wgm.8m.com ). --Rules-- - No complaining if you lose a match, if you lose it's either because you : a) Didn't roleplay enough, b) Your roleplays stunk or c) It's for your own good (you might need to lose for a storyline or something)
- No 'hardcore' wrestlers, it's an excuse for crap roleplaying. - Original Wrestlers only, and don't make them rip offs of current wrestlers. (Rock Cold Stephen Austin etc) - Have fun, don't get too wrapped up in E-Feds. If you can't roleplay, I'll take any excuse, even if you just didn't feel like roleplaying. - Maximum of 4 wrestlers for each member. (2 singles and a Tag Team, 1 Single, 1 Tag Team, whatever combo you like.) - Send in ideas for storylines, send in ideas for matches, even if it doesn't involve your wrestler. We're open to ideas! --Roleplay Rules-- - Members must roleplay twice a week, at the least. - No excess swearing. If you can't say it on TV, you can't say it in ICW... - No Homosexual and no Racist insults thanks. It's just not cool. - Keep your roleplays longer than 10 lines, and keep them interesting! Don't just stay in the ring, or your locker room, have your wrestler go out into bars, or into a zoo, anything!
--Championships-- There are 6 belts in ICW. Apart from the Chairshot Invitational Trophy, belts must be defended at least once every 30 days. ICW International Title: ICW's equivalent of the World Title, this is the strap every ICW Superstar is after.
ICW Television Title: The ICW equivalent of a U.S or Intercontinental
Title, this is the title that propels ICW wrestlers to superstardom!
ICW Cruiserweight Title: This belt holds the same prestige as the
International Title, but is only available to wrestlers under 230 pounds.
ICW Tag Team Titles: Want these belts? Grab a partner and go for the gold. ICW Womens Title: Women can kick ass too :) Chairshot Invitational Trophy: Defended every 2 months in a 6-Man
Tournament on ICW Saturday Rumble, this trophy is sometimes regarded as more prestigious than the World Title.
--Cards-- The ICW holds 2 cards a week: ICW Saturday Rumble: The flagship show. Interviews, matches and behind the scenes action make Rumble 'Can't Miss TV'!
ICW Charged: Hosted by James Helton and Remy Powers, ICW Charged is the recap of the weeks events. ICW Charged is where the upcoming cards will be announced, and where most interviews will take place.
And we can't forget the Pay Per Views! Each ICW PPV has a different twist to it: January: Pain: The first Pay Per View of the new year is home to the Pain Bowl. 15
ICW Superstars in a Battle Royal, if you are eliminated, you fight other eliminated superstars outside the ring! If you pin one of these eliminated superstars, you are allowed to re-enter the ring, and have a second chance of winning the
Pain Bowl, and an International Title shot at Hit The Road,  if you are the last man standing in the ring!
February: Destruction 101: The loser of the Destruction 101 main event doesn't just lose his shot at the World Title, he is also hancuffed on both legs and arms, and beaten on by every single ICW superstar, who have been ordered to teach him a lesson in Destruction 101!!
March: Hit The Road: The ICW PPV where the main event isn't held inside the confines of the arena, but held anywhere outside! The Main Event could be in the forest, on a highway, even in your own frontyard!!!
April: Caged Warriors: 2 Main Event Cage Matches at once!! If you're lucky enough to win one match, why not interfere in the other?
May: MAYhem: ICW's main PPV!! This is where the grudges are settled, in No DQ, No
Count-Out matches! There MUST be a winner, in each match!
June: 'Rasslin No gimmicks, no specialty matches, just plain old 'Rasslin! If you want to see 2 guys beating the hell out of each other, this is the event for you!
July: Random Enemies: 10 Team Tag Team elimination match! Chosen randomly from the ICW superstars, the winning team has the choice of getting a
Tag Team Title shot, or beating the hell out of each other in a Hardcore rules match! August: Sky High: Who will survive the 12 foot high scaffold match? Considering there's tables all around the bottom of the scaffold, NOBODY!!
September: Sin: 10 Man Hell In A Cell match!! The winner receives an International
Title shot at the Graveyard Shift!
October: Graveyard Shift: An open Grave match! 6 empty graves, each with a different gimmick applied to it! A variation on the casket match at the start, put your opponent into one of the graves, and then, fight another match according to the gimmick on the Tombstone of the grave you, or your opponent were placed in! Confusing? Yes. Painful? HELL YEAH!!
November: Thanksgiving Ass Kicking 2 unlucky superstars will be forced to fight their match, inside a house, during Thanksgiving Dinner! WWF has In Your House, but this is taking it too far!!!
December: X-(Treme)Mas! Each and every match on the card, is a Weapons match! Tables, Chairs,
Ball Bats, flame, everything!

Chocolate. :) Story Line Staff Commissioner Wilson: A 48 year old, retired wrestler, Wilson has wrestled in the NWA, SMW,
UWF and was even in the WWF for a short period of time.
He now uses his wrestling experience to deliver justice and order to the ICW. Push him around, expect to be hurt.
Paul Shift: (Face Play by Play) A fan of wrestling for 20 years, Shift always roots for the 'good guy'. Paul once tried his luck as a wrestler in the now defunct wMo, but in his one and only match was powerbombed through a table, and lost the match in a record 17 seconds. Paul has learned from his mistakes, and has vowed never to strap on the boots again. Regarded as the 'voice of ICW'.
James Helton (Heel Play by Play) A former promoter, Helton quit his job after embellishing more than two hundred thousand dollars from his former fed. By sticking him firmly behind the mic at the ICW announcer's table,
Helton will (hopefully) never repeat history with ICW.
Co-hosts ICW Charged with Remy Powers Remy Powers: (Interviewer) Although his job is one of the most dangerous in the ICW, Powers always manages to get on the good side of the wrestlers he is interviewing, whether they are heel or face. Co-host of ICW Charged with James Helton, he never stops digging at wrestlers to get the full story.
Scott Loben: (Head Referee) Although he is the youngest member of the ICW Team, Loben and his form of justice have made him one of the most respected, and injured, referees in wrestling. If you push Loben around, he won't push back, he'll disqualify you, and if you've got a belt, take that off you too.
Michael Howle, Jeremy Jedd, Nathan Cove: (Referee Team): More lenient than head referee Loben, these 3 refs don't mind getting pushed around, and most of the time will let the match continue.
