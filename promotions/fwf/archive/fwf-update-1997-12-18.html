http://fwf.wrestleworld.com -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ____LATEST NEWS & RUMORS____ We have gotten a SLEW of new wrestlers and members. The competition should be heating up even more here in the FWF with new tag teams, cruiserweights, and Extreme competitors coming in from all over the world. Its December, and THAT MEANS its our birthday! The FWF turns Two years old this month, and with that comes the Pay Per View, WinteRumble III. The card (so far) for that event can be seen below...we are STILL ACCEPTING challenges for that card...send all challenges to [detail withheld] ! CHALLENGE FOR THE CARD The FWF Slammy Awards Nomination Ballot is out! Remember to fill it in as soon as possible, and remember you dont HAVE to pick four men for each category, this is just a nomination ballot...anything you can do is fine. The main ballot will be sent out on the 26th of December. The Cruiserweight Title battle royal is shaping up to be a great match, and we are going to be importing international talent for this match..but we need more FWF members to join it as well!! If you have a cruiserweight, and want the gold..email me his name, and he will be added to the match. It seems that there is a certain titleholder here in the FWF that is refusing to defend his belt! Although he won the title in a sister league, he says it isnt his style to wrestle on FWF television, and may take losses by countout or disqualification...or ever forfeit his belt! FWF is going to be seeing some GOLDEN days again... The damage caused by Scorpio in the FWF has been amazing...then he brought in Thanatos, and he has been on a TEAR as of late...what would happen if they decided to ADD to their stable...? At Winter Rumble III We have Reports from every wrestling hotline and the Internet that Superst.. ooops.. Sensational Frankie Starz is rumoured to have a BIG Surprise for the FWF and if he gets his wishes, For The Franchise Andru Edwards. It's also rumoured that he may have a New Manager...and it's been said he may be considering joining a certain faction that alrerady exists in the FWF, Whats Starz connection with Hades? It is now obvious what "Sensational" Frankie Starz returned to the FWF for..it is apparent that he wants more FWF Gold...but he has kept it no secret that he wants ANDRU EDWARDS! The match has been signed for the big pay per view, with the winner being deemed #1 contender to the world title...but rumors are flying that this may become a THREE WAY DANCE!!! "The Franchise" Andru Edwards has found his man...or woman shall we say...the man that brought such big names into this sport as Sensuous Samantha and Erotic Erin is about to do it again...this time with what might be the greatest protege yet! It is confirmed that she will debut with him at WinteRumble III, but we may HEAR from her before then..and trust me...this isnt a name you have heard before... Please use the email addresses below, and refer to them when sending correspondance to the Fantasy Wrestling Federation...as there are still a few of you sending things to the wrong people... *Any idea, tell me!!!
