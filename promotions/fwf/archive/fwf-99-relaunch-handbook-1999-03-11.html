FANTASY WRESTLING FEDERATION '99 Whats up everyone..If your receiving this letter right now then odds are you are the best of the best on the roleplaying scene. I am the new President/Owner of the Fantasy Wrestling Federation '99. If you have never heard about the FWF let me break it down for you guys in the best way that I can. The FWF was one of the best federations on the internet..{angle,and quality wise}..A federation that lasted well over a year and was on the verge of making it onto the main frontburner of the E-fed industry..but as things do,something went wrong..the president of the federation Cgil decided that it was personal to him so he started screwing people out of matches they earned and also started giving himself titles that he didn't deserve. Well now I'm going to take the FWF to the heights that it should have reached the first time..The only difference between the old FWF and FWF '99 is that there will be NO REAL WRESTLERS , in my eyes thats the easiest way to roleplay because of the fact that you pretty much have a script of what your charactor says and does, and that takes out alot of the creativity. The FWF '99 will have the best angles..the best quality..and the best roleplayers. The cards will be full and in detail..the big matches will happen, and the federation will grow to be one of the best that the internet has ever seen.Thats my promise to each and every person that joins the
FWF family.

---{Heavyweight World Title}--- The Heavyweight World Title is the highest and most sought after title in the
FWF '99. It will be held by only the best and defended against only the best..if you hold this title it means that you are THE MAN, but you are also the man that everyones gunning for..The title comes with its perks, but it also comes with a commitment so if you think that your good enough to stand the hype, then this is the title that you'll be going after.
----{United States Title}--- This is the second highest title in the FWF '99..Its held by the number one contender for the Heavyweight World title and can be used as a stepping stone into the spotlight of the FWF '99. Many people have jumped from nonames to superstar Via this title..once again I repeat the title comes with perks..but also with a commitment to defend against the best around.
{Hardcore Division} The neXt division in the FWF '99 is the Hardcore Division..The champion of this is known as the Hardcore Champion and holds the Hardcore title..This division with have tons of wild matches designed for only the strongest and most durable athletes in the FWF '99..I REPEAT this division is not for the weak of heart of the sensitive..each hardcore match will be filles with gimmiks and very, very eXtreme matches..only sign up for this division if your sure your wrestler has what it takes.
{Cruiserweight Division} This is where the little big men of the FWF '99 get there swerve on in all kinds of high flying eXitement filled matches.The weight limit is 215 and under..The Cruiserweight Title will be awarded to the best cruiserweight who comes out and takes care of business on a weekly basis.. Again the weak of heart and not recomended for this one..or pretty much anyone.
