Saturday Sacrifice (4 September 1999) Card

26 August 1999

FROM CEDAR POINT AMUSEMENT PARK IN SAN DUSKY, OHIO, IT'S SATURDAY SACRIFICE!!! "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam takes on "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz! Al Snow with Head steps into the ring, psychosis verses psychosis, against
Goldust! Former World Tag Team Champions, The Dark @lliance begins their climb back up the ladder against "The People's Champ" The Rock, and "The Hardcore Icon"
The Sandman! The Man Called Sting steps into the ring with one half of the current FWF
World Tag Team Champions as he takes on Raven!
The other half of the World Tag Team Champions steps into the ring against
"The Paragon of Virtue" as Perry Saturn takes on "Lionheart" Chris Jericho!
"The Franchise" Shane Douglas with Francine goes on a little "Trip" as he comes face to face with "Up in Smoke" Owen Hart with Stympy T. Hyena!
And In The Main EVENT!!!! The Match Rick Rude Tried but could not Stop!!! For the World Heavyweight Title!! "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash defends his title against "The Man from The Darkside" THE UNDERTAKER!!!
