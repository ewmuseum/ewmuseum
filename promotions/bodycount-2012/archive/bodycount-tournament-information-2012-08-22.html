J The Ripper — Aug 22, 2012 What is The Warrior Grand Prix? The Warrior Grand Prix is an event held by BodyCount Fighting. It's a non-exclusive open invitational tournament that brings the best from the wrestling world, fighting world, and even off the streets together in one massive tournament to crown The Warrior Grand Prix Champion. There are no weight limits and very little rules. All skills and fighting styles are welcome. This tournament proves once and for all who the best fighter in the world currently is no matter where they come from. There is no second place and the winner will receive the following: Guaranteed: - Five Million Dollars - The Warrior Grand Prix Trophy - The Warrior Grand Prix Title belt. - The Title of The Best Fighter in the World Optional: - A lucrative BodyCount Fighting Contract. - A future title shot to be determined at a later date. What are the requirements? Sign up. How will we RP? There will only be a select number let it, once that number is reached apps are cut off. In one to two weeks the tournament will take place at a rapid pace to crown a champion.
