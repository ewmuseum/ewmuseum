**ROUND 1 RESULTS** + Golden Tickets & Updated Bracket.

15 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J The Ripper — Sep 15, 2012 Ladies and Gentleman this is the Warrior Grand Prix. The most prestigious prize in all of MMA. Fighters from around the world have come to bleed and win all for one cup, one title, and one belt with the option of a future title shot and a lucrative BC contract on top of FIVE MILLION DOLLARS! Our first fight of the night would be Umaga Reihana who’s established himself as an accomplished fighter and on the other side would stand a wrestler with a lot to prove -- Castonovo. “Machine gun” by Portishead hit as strobe lights illuminated Umaga rising from the stage. A look of pure determination was on his face as he made his way down the ramp with his trainers and into the octagon. 'All of the Lights' by Kanye West f/Kid Cudi and Rihanna would follow as Castonovo made his way up from the stage. He extended both arms before walking with a certain swagger to the octagon. These two would waste no time as the referee read off the rules. Neither touched gloves and went to their corner until the bell rang and the fight started. It was an explosion with Umaga throwing bombs but Castonovo was able to dodge a few of them. One staggered him though as he flew against the cage. Umaga charged only to be dropped to the mat where Castonovo began dropping hammer fists in an open mount. He quickly transitioned to side mount throwing sharp knees into Umaga’s side. Umage slipped his hips around and got Castonovo into a full guard and shoved him away. Both men jumped to their feet and Castonovo shot for Umaga’s leg. Umaga blocked and began throwing hooks to catch Castonovo in the jaw. Castonovo let go and jumped back barely dodging a deadly right hook. Umaga just grinned as a bit of blood ran out of his nose. Umaga pushed the fight forward backing Castonovo up but Castonovo exploded with a superman punch that caught Umaga off guard. The big man was rocked and moving back as Castonovo threw a flying head kick that also landed! Soon Castonovo had Umaga against the cage. He’d obviously been training as he grabbed Umaga by the neck and locked him in a Muay Thai clinch and began throwing knees. Umaga knew he was in trouble and used his power to shove Castonova back. Castonva couldn’t move in time to block the incoming flying knee of Umaga followed by the barrage of fists he began to throw. He covered up and shoved Umaga back but Umaga charged forward, maybe a little too anxiously, and got rocked with a hard right that sent him down to the mat. Castonova swarmed dropping bombs as Umaga covered. Castonova grabbed Umaga’s arm and tried to yank back and lock in an armbar as Umaga fought with all his strength. 10 seconds. Castonova roared and he pulled back harder, his veins popping out, Umaga roared as well before losing his grip and found himself in an arm bar. He yelled out in pain and looked over at the time. The bell rang signaling the end of round one and Castonova got to his feet, obviously frustrated. Both men took their corners as their trainers gave advice. Both men nodded, put their mouth piece back in, and the bell rang. Both exploded from their corners again, each man looking for an opening. Castonova led with a few jabs but the explosive Umaga swatted them away and rocked him with a bomb that sprawled Castonova to the ground. Umaga pounced but Castonova pulled a perfect guard. Umaga began trying to work his way into a more advantages position and finally threw his hips into side mount. Umaga fell back, grabbing Castonova’s ankle, and locked the ankle in as best as he could. Castonova roared in pain but used his free leg to nail Umaga on the shoulder and weaken it enough to just slide free. Both men were back on their feet and Umaga smelt blood but it was Castonova’s turn to bring the pain and quickly nailed three quick jabs and a strong left and a right. He ended the combo with an uppercut that sent Umaga against the cage. Castonova charged but Umaga was ready and picked him up slamming him hard onto the mat. Umaga jumped into full mount and began raining down hammer like fists and the referee was about to call it but the bell rang AGAIN. Umaga stood up disgusted. Normally a BC fight would last five rounds but for the tournament it was only a three round fight. Castonova and Umaga took their corner, got their advice, and went into the third and final round. Umaga was tired of the games and pushed the fight to the corner shoving Castonova back before throwing more lefts and rights including an ear shattering leg kick. It was just enough to catch Castonova off guard. SPINNING ELBOW! Umaga nailed the spinning back elbow and Castonova never saw it coming. He hit the mat and Umaga swarmed again before the referee swarmed calling for the end. Umaga Reihana via TKO 1 minute in the 3rd round. - 3 - 0 ---- Our next fight would feature Mike Sodo vs. Aleczander the Great. Aleczander was already in the octagon as Mike Sodo rose from the stage to the sounds of “Warrior” by Lloyd Banks. Mike nodded his head to the music but was ready to fight. Every inch of his body showed that as he entered the octagon. The rules were read and the bell sounded. Aleczander rushed in, big mistake, the highly trained Sodo caught Aleczander with a flying armbar taking both men down to the mat. CRACK! Aleczander tapped as his arm was broken in under 30 seconds. Sodo stood up and scoffed as he exited the octagon. He didn’t need the results, he just made a wrestler look like a chump. As he headed up the ramp he yelled to the fans “Bring me a real fighter!” before disappearing to the back and the medics checked on Aleczander. Winner - Mike Sodo via Submission in 30 seconds of the very first round - 3-0 --- Victor Reise made his way as “Reise Reise” By Rammstein played. The German public went nuts for their hometown hero as he walked into the octagon. The mammoth man jumped a couple of times before the ring announcer was handed a note. “Ladies and Gentleman we were just informed that Mr. Alexander has been deported back to the United States at this time.” Reise spit in disgust as everyone booed. Reise won by a technicality and didn’t like it one bit. One thing was for sure, his round two opponent had better bring it. Winner - Reise via No Contest - 3-0 --- Pat Gordon Jr. vs. Michael Vayne Michael Vayne rose through the smoke and pyros to “Crazy Train” by Ozzy Osbourne. He made his way to the octagon and was soon followed by Pat Gordon Jr who entered the octagon and sprinted around, ready to fight for the first time in the BC octagon. The bell sounded and both men circled one another. Gordon moved forward but was caught with a hard right hand followed by a left but he was able to catch the leg and trip Vayne. Gordon began to stomp on the face of Vayne but Vayne rolled and caught Gordon’s ankle locking it in as Gordon hit the ground. Gordon was crafty though and slid through jumping to his feet and soccer punting Vayne straight in the skull! Vayne rolled to the side but Gordon didn’t go for the kill holding both hands up like he’d already won. Vayne shook the cobwebs loose and latched his hands around Gordon’s hips and slammed him overhead. Gordon landed on his neck hard as Vayne quickly tried to get to top mount but Gordon slid his hips into a guard holding off Vayne. Vayne pushed off and the bell for round one went off. Both men took their corners, got their advice, and came out. They circled looking for the opening and Gordon went to throw a high kick, Vayne swatted it away and landed a left, a right, and finally a hellacious uppercut. Vayne went to swarm but the referee dove over Gordon calling for the end. Vayne jumped up on the cage holding up both arms as he was announced the victor. Michael Vayne via KO - 2 minutes of Round 2. - 3 - 0 ---- Dave Hendrix vs. John St. Abraham. Dave Hendrix made his way to the octagon. He loosened up as he waiting for Abraham and Abraham appeared, mic in hand. “The lord almighty has instructed me not to fight! Boo if you like but you’re all sinners and I refuse to inflict punishment on this pathetic sinner!” Abraham threw the microphone down leaving everyone confused as he left. Hendrix yelled “Pussy!” as Abraham walked to the back. From backstage the yells of a man could be heard, almost like he was being drug away, and they sounded almost like Abraham’s screams... Winner: Dave Hndrix via No Contest - 3-0 Fight of the Night: Umaga Reihana - participant wins 50,000. Submission of the Night: Mike Sodo - participant wins 50,000. Knockout of the Night: Michael Vayne - participant wins 50,000. --- Golden Ticket(s): A new bracket has been made to give those who rp'ed but no opponent and those who did but lost a fair chance at this thing. So the Golden Tickets are being awarded to Johannes and Gordon Jr. Another round has been added since these people have RP'ed and the ones that rp'ed have moved on to the Semi-Finals.
