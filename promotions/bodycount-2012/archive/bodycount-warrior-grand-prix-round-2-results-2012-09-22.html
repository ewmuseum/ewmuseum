**ROUND 2 RESULTS!**

22 September 2012

J The Ripper — Sep 22, 2012 WARRIOR GRAND PRIX ROUND 2 Welcome one and all to round two of the Warrior Grand Prix brought to you live from Munich, Germany. 16 were trimmed down to 10 with two men receiving a golden pass, a chance at redemption, while two men with impressive victories, Umaga and Vayne, moved on to the semi-finals and now await the winners of tonight and next weekend to enter the octagon again. Tonight, we find out which four men will advance to face one another next week and who will just be another body added to the count. --- Gordon Jr. vs. Hendrix --- The blood lust was ever present as the fans were at a fever pitch to see these men enter the octagon and do battle. They would enter men, they would fight as Gladiators, and they would leave as God’s to the fans in attendance and around the world. Gordon Jr. had already made his way to the octagon with his trainers. His music died down and he took his corner getting a few final stretches in. “Voodoo Child” by Jimi Hendrix played over the PA as the strobes and fog danced over the stage. Hendrix rose and stepped forward jumping a little to loosen up. He stared at Gordon as he began to mentally strategize how to take Hendrix down and advance. Meanwhile Gordon Jr was doing the same as Hendrix entered the octagon and jogged around it as his name was announced. He took his corner, did his last minute stretches, and then both men moved to the center of the octagon where the referee explained the rules. Both men nodded that they understood and touched gloves. Ding Ding... Gordon Jr and Hendrix circled one another, Hendrix tested Gordon Jr with a few jabs. Gordon Jr. would test Hendrix with some inside leg kicks which Hendrix easily dodged and bounced back in. Gordon Jr. decided to push the fight first and came in swinging in which Hendrix dodged most of the strong punches and just ate the jabs. Gordon Jr. tried to move in for some dirty boxing but Hendrix pushed off him and threw a strong kick that caught Gordon Jr. off guard. He was only half able to block the kick which staggered him. Hendrix struck moving in with hard lefts and rights but it would be the uppercut that rocked Gordon Jr. He hit the mat and Hendrix swarmed on him throwing a few bombs before jumping on his back and wrapping his arms around his neck. He got the hooks in and fell back with Gordon Jr. in a choke and it was locked in perfectly. Not wanting to risk his career or, especially, his life Gordon Jr. did the smart thing and tapped. Winner: Hendrix -- 4:05 of the 1st round -- via Submission. VOTE: 3 - 0 --- Stark vs. Johannes --- Johannes made his way to the ring, ignoring the German fans as he scowled. He entered the octagon and held the cage, putting his head against the padding with his back to the entrance ramp. “Devil’s Reject” by Mad Child kicked up and small pyros went off as Stark rose from the stage. He was ready for this, he’d been blowing social media up as of late wanting to get in the octagon and prove himself. Tonight he would have his chance and he looked pumped. Stark paid respect to the German crowd and sent love back home to the camera. As it was with every fight, each man was introduced, called to the center of the ring, were told the rules, asked if they understood, and were told if they wanted to touch gloves now. They understood but neither man touched gloves, they were too focused, and backed to their corner. Ding Ding! Both came out with a head of steam. Johannes seemed eager to put his “Golden Ticket” to use and tested Stark with various kicks and punches, all were either swatted away or dodged as Stark took a methodical approach. He lifted his knee a few times, feigning a front kick to see how Johannes would react. Johannes jumped back but came back in aggressive causing Stark to jump back and swing around. Stark threw a hard thigh kick that echoed through the BodyCount Arena and made the fans “Ohhh”. Johannes obviously had a stinger on his thigh as he shook his leg trying to move back in. He stepped forward putting most of his weight on it and that was a mistake. The methodical Stark saw it coming and as soon as the weight went on that leg Johannes buckled forward right into a straight front kick to the face. Johannes crumpled to the mat and the referee dove over him before Stark could dive in. Stark pulled his mouth piece out and ran, jumping up on the cage throwing both arms out letting a savage roar out. He slapped hands with his trainers as he jumped off the cage and was declared the winner. It was also announced he had the second fastest win of the tournament with 45 seconds. Winner: Stark -- :45 seconds of the 1st round -- via KO. VOTE: 3-0 --- Reise vs. Dean --- Dean was already in the octagon as the lights dim and the colors of the German flag swirled, dancing in the fog as the biggest man in the tournament and the pride of the German people rose from the stage to the sounds of “Reise Reise” by Rammstein. Viktor Reise would FINALLY fight this week and the German people were going crazy for their hometown hero. Reise entered the octagon with pure determination etched on his face as he didn’t even stretch and just walked to the center of the ring. Dean made his way over and everything was went over again. Dean extended a hand but Reise just backed to his corner. Ding Ding. Reise didn’t move fast at all, in fact, he kept his hands down. Dean, on the other hand, came in trying to use speed to his advantage. He threw kicks at Reise but his tree trunk like legs were barely affected. Reise grabbed Dean by the throat and threw him back against the cage sending boulder-esque fists into Dean’s stomach. The lack of oxygen and the feeling of his sternum and ribs cracking set his nerves on fire sending multiple pain receptors to the brain. Reise tossed Dean to the mat and just stood, stoic, as the German people cheered. Dean wobbled to his feet only to find the bear paw of a hand pushing him against the cage. More shots to the ribs from Reise followed by one of the hardest rights that had been seen in the tournament. CRACK The sound of Dean’s jaw breaking was sickening. Reise didn’t let up and hit him just as hard with the left. Another right and the orbital socket was broken. Dean crumpled to the mat as Reise started stomping on his face over and over until the referee dove in. The German people exploded with cheers as Reise just spit and walked out of the Octagon. He saluted his German people but that was all from the Mammoth man. Meanwhile EMT’s loaded Dean up and took him to the back. Winner: Reise -- 1:50 of the 1st round -- via TKO. VOTE: 3 - 0 --- Alexander vs. Sodo --- It was time for the final match of the night and possibly one of the biggest. It had been a social media war of words up to this point. Alexander would take on Sodo who held the quickest win in the octagon at 30 seconds. The past didn’t matter now though because it was go time. “It’s time for war” - LL Cool J. Jakob Alexander rose from the stage as pyros and strobes went off at the same time. The strobes continued to go to the beat of the music as Alexander nodded his head, flanked by his trainers, shadow boxing a little as he made his way to the octagon. Alexander pulled off his “Team Weapon X” shirt and stretched taking his corner. “Bad Company” - Five Finger Death Punch The German fans cheered for Mike Sodo after the exciting 30 second armbar victory in round one of The Warrior Grand Prix. Two fireballs launched in the air as Sodo rose from the stage through the fog rolling his neck letting the bones in his neck crack. He stared Alexander down who stared right back. Sodo entered the octagon throwing his custom Hitman “Torch” shirt to his trainers. Sodo made a lap around the Octagon, staring at Alexander as he went by and finally took his corner. The fighters were announced, the rules were explained, both understood, and neither touched gloves. They were quick to their corners as the tension in the BodyCount Arena began to rise. ROUND 1: Ding Ding. Both men came out, both men tested the other with kicks and punches, and both were looking for an opening. Sodo feigned a right hook which Alexander bit just enough on for Sodo to shoot in for the takedown. Alexander bounced on one leg until his back hit the cage. Sodo quickly locked his arms around Alexander’s hips and slammed him taking side control. Alexander’s ground defense was good though. Sodo was only able to get a couple of knees to the ribs in before Alexander popped his hips and got Sodo in a half guard. Sodo mounted up and drove a fist down at the right time catching Alexander, a bit of blood quickly came running out of his nose as Sodo went for another but Alexander caught the arm and used momentum to roll into an armbar. Sodo used his free hand to keep Alexander from completely locking the armbar in. Sodo displayed his agility, managing to get to his feet and drive his left hand down rocking Alexander enough to loosen his grip. CLACK CLACK - 10 SECONDS LEFT! Sodo threw caution to the wind having the dominate position and started raining lefts and rights down nailing Alexander with some quality punches but it wouldn’t be enough as the bell rang and the referee made them separate. Both men got up, the announcers speculated that Sodo won that round but it was still anyone's fight. With two rounds left both men got advice from their corners, Alexander got the blood wiped from his nose and the cut over his eye taken care of. Both men stood and round 2 was about to be under way. ROUND 2: Ding Ding. Both men met in the center of the octagon and Sodo, confident in his take down, shot in but Alexander caught both his arms hooking them and threw a hard knee that sent Sodo sprawling. Alexander went in but Sodo held him back with kicks from his back. Alexander grabbed one ankle but couldn’t quite get the other as he looked for an opening. Alexander knew the referee would stand them up soon anyway so he jumped back letting Sodo back up to his feet. Sodo was now sporting a nasty cut on the bridge of his nose as well. Alexander threw a few leg kicks but Sodo just ate them and threw a few jabs that moved Alexander back. Sodo came in close for some dirty boxing but Alexander caught him in the clinch and jerked Sodo hard to the right, breaking his defense, and followed with a quick knee that landed hard against Sodo’s nose. Sodo didn’t let it keep him down though and started throwing uppercuts. Alexander knew he couldn’t take many of those so he quickly released the hold but grabbed Sodo’s waist and slid behind Sodo taking him down. Sodo landed hard on his neck but was able to get his guard up. Alexander looked for an opening while keeping an eye out for Sodo going for a submission attempt. Sodo locked his legs around Alexander’s waist and Alexander went to drop the hammer but Sodo was able to roll and take full mount! Alexander covered up as Sodo tried to rain down bombs. Alexander wasn’t done though and he was quick. He was able to slip out into a half mount, Sodo went to try to take side control but Alexander quickly flipped Sodo over. Clack Clack - 10 SECONDS LEFT! Alexander flipped himself in a top north south position and was able to lock Sodo in a choke hold. It wasn’t the best position he wanted for a choke but he had to try something. He Sodo could feel the air leaving him and the lights going out but just before the lights went out completely... The bell rang and the referee separated both men. Both men took their corners again. This time Sodo’s cuts had to be tended to. Both men were getting tired but with one round left neither man wanted it to go to decision. They got their advice, a quick drink of water, and both were back on their feet. The referee asked both men if they were ready, they nodded, and they came out to cheers as round three started. ROUND 3 (final round): DING DING! BOTH men threw caution to the wind and began standing and banging with each other. Sodo hit a beautiful left, left, right combination but Alexander was impressive himself throwing a right leg kick, a right hook, a right body kick, and a straight left. Sodo bounced back wiping the blood away. Sodo came in pushing the fight taking Alexander to the cage. Alexander blocked the take down as Sodo fought for a better grip. Sodo went for the takedown again but Alexander hooked the leg, tripping Sodo and ended up on top. Both men quickly fought for position and Sodo managed to get his guard up just in time. Alexander postured up and at the right time threw a perfect right straight down nailing Sodo right in the eye. Sodo wouldn’t let it him rock him though as Alexander went for another but Sodo caught the arm looking for the armbar as Sodo and Alexander rolled. Alexander managed to slip out though and beat Sodo in position taking side control. Sodo’s defense was as good as Alexander’s though and quickly Sodo slipped his hips free. Sodo worked back into full guard and pushed off on Alexander. Both men quickly got to their feet. Sodo came in a little too aggressive and Alexander took advantage throwing a kick that was aimed straight for the side of Sodo’s head. Sodo was quick though and the kick caught his forearm though leaving quite a stinger that echoed across the arena. Another “Ohhhh” was heard from the fans as Sodo shook his arm but moved back in. Clack Clack -- 10 SECONDS LEFT! Both men had their ups and downs and wasn’t sure who was leading in points. They both came in just throwing on each other, both taking punch after punch. Both men had a swollen eye, multiple cuts, and a busted nose as they each tried to get the (T)KO. There was one last flurry by both men as the bell sounded. The referee broke them up as both men look pissed off. Each man took their corner and waited for the decision. They took the center of the Octagon as the referee held both men by the wrist. The announcer followed. “Round one - 40/39 Sodo!” Sodo pumped his free hand. “Round two- 40/39 Alexander!” Alexander held a hand up, bouncing a little. “Round Three 40/39 and your winner...JAAAAAAAAAAAKOB ALEEEEEEEEXAAAAAAAAAAANDER!” Sodo just shook his head as the referee held up Alexander’s hand. Winner: Alexander -- FULL 3 ROUNDS -- via DECISION. Vote: 2-1 --- All four winners were called on stage as the announcer informed everyone that next week it would be Stark vs. Alexander and Reise vs. Hendrix. Hendrix took a standard fight pose with Reise who just stood with his chest out towering over Hendrix. Meanwhile Alexander and Stark both were talking shit to each other, apparently another feud was brewing. That would be the closing scene for Round 2 of the Warrior Grand Prix. KNOCKOUT OF THE NIGHT - STARK - 50,000.00 PAYOUT SUBMISSION OF THE NIGHT - HENDRIX - 50,000.00 PAYOUT FIGHT OF THE NIGHT - SODO VS. ALEXANDER - 50,000.00 PAYOUT TO BOTH MEN.
