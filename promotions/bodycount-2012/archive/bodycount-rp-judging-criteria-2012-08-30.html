Warrior Grand Prix: How RP's will be judged.

30 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J The Ripper — Aug 30, 2012 A lot of people like to know how they'll be judged, especially in a tournament, therefore I'll be laying that out here in this thread: 1. Consistency - Your story should be consistent as well as make sense. Remember, you'll be in Munich, Germany during the entire tournament. 2. Story - While it is important to tie your opponent in to your rp's you'll be judged on the story you're telling. A good story makes for a good read. 3. Spelling - Yes, it sounds stupid, but in this day and age with all the spell checkers that are built into word editing software as well as browsers there's no reason to not have your work spell checked. 4. Meeting deadline - Meeting your deadline will help A LOT. 5. Originality - The more original your story and rp's are the more they'll be noticed and considered. 6. Quality over Quantity - This old rule stands true today. A better written roleplay will always win over a longer written roleplay. Take pride in the quality of your work and if it happens to have to go long then so be it but a shorter roleplay that's rich with quality will always win over quantity. 7. Being the first out of the gate - Pretty much having the guts to post your roleplay before seeing what your opponent does/says tends to give you a bump. It takes guts to "go first" because you have no idea what your opponent has in store for you. For now that's it but keep checking back because this will be updated as we think of more.
