**ROUND 3 RESULTS!**

30 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J The Ripper — Sep 30, 2012 OOC: First off it took 5 different people to judge both matches. That’s how close they both were. I’m extremely impressed by everyone this round. I wanted to make sure we had these matches right and both fights came down to a tiebreaker. Great job to everyone involved. REMEMBER, DUE TO THE DELAY, RP'ING WILL BE EXTENDED TO MIDNIGHT EASTERN TIME FRIDAY NIGHT! --- Round three would decide which two men out of four would head to the semi-finals where Umaga and Vayne sat in the wings. Tonight four men would step inside the ominous octagon of BodyCount and decide their own fates. No scripts, no plans, just two men transformed into new age gladiators fighting for what’s theirs to take. With so much on the table who would rise up to the challenge and who would fall from the ranks of the chosen? Tonight we would see the underdog Hendrix step up against the biggest man in the tournament. Can David really slay goliath or will Viktor “Reise” his way to the semi-finals? Finally, in a match where there will be nothing but war, “Weapon X” Jakob Alexander takes on Jonathan Stark in what’s turned into a social media blow out. The two started exchanging words on the BodyCount stage and continued all week. Tonight, the talking ends, and we find out who has what it takes. This is BodyCount. Welcome to Round 3 of the Warrior Grand Prix. Viktor Reise vs. Dave Hendrix --- “Rock 'n Roll” by Motorhead began to play. Pyro’s from the stage lit up the arena as Hendrix rose from the stage. He tapped his face a few times psyching himself up and made his way to the octagon. Hendrix put his head against his corner, mentally going over his game plan as the colors changed to that of the German Nation as “Reise Reise” by Rammstein began to play. The German crowd roared as Viktor Reise rose from the stage, stoic and statuesque, before staring at the octagon making his way to the Octagon. The two men were escorted to the center of the octagon and told their instructions. The referee asked if they understood and both men nodded heading back to their corner and not touching gloves as the fans were in a frenzy for the bell. DING DING! Hendrix paced himself as Reise just charged in looking to end him as he did Dean a week prior. Hendrix had obviously studied the tape and ducked under a giant boulder fist and worked the ribs before dancing back outside. It was obvious that Hendrix wanted to stick and move this fight and knew one shot from Reise could turn the lights off. Reise snarled, frustrated, and swung again only for Hendrix to duck and nail a few solid shots to the ribs and then land a kick to the back of Reise’s knee. It was a good strategy to work the back of the knee. It didn’t matter how big Reise was, if the back of the leg was caught just right, even the biggest man could hit the ground. Reise was obviously frustrated as he pushed forward on Hendrix and feigned the right this time which Hendrix bit on and was only able to move just enough to only be grazed by Reise’s left. Even though it was just a graze it still shook Hendrix up a little bit. He shook his head but before he could fully shake it off Reise was coming forward, this time slamming a fist in Hendrix’s gut, lifting him a little off the ground. CLACK CLACK -- 10 SECONDS LEFT! Reise looked at the clock and growled as he moved over to Hendrix. He went to throw that deadly right again but Hendrix moved and landed two more sickening kicks to the back of Reise’s knee. They made a loud popping sound, as Reise’s natural reaction was to grab at the knee. END OF ROUND ONE! It was amazing. Someone had lasted a whole round with Reise but you could tell Hendrix was hurting as his trainers checked him and gave him more advice. Reise sat down, actually a little winded, and caught his breath. He shoved a trainer away as the bell was about to sound. DING DING ROUND 2! Reise came out, an expression of anger etched on his face, which was unusual for the always stoic Reise. Reise shoved the trainers away again and came out limping. Hendrix barely knew where he was as Reise came forward but Hendrix sidestepped the bull of a man and slammed another kick into Reise’s knee. Hendrix finally, with everything he had left drove himself shoulder first into the back of Reise’s knee. It finally buckled and the big man went down. Hendrix dropped down and wrapped himself around the tree truck like leg of Reise as Reise tried to kick him off. Reise roared as Hendrix held on trying to clamp in the leg lock and torque the knee. Reise threw fists backwards but they didn’t nearly have the same effect plus Reise was getting tired and winded. Hendrix looked to the sky, at the hot lights, heard the screams of blood lust from the German fans, and scanned his thoughts of the past, of why he was here, and why he had to do this. With that Hendrix found his grip and locked the leg lock in. Reise roared in pain and even though he was huge, he was not stupid. He knew if he stayed in the lock it could mean his whole career. There would be another battle, another fight, this was just pure dumb luck. And he tapped. DING DING! Hendrix wobbled up to his feet as Reise stood as well looking down at Hendrix. “One day we will meet again.” The giant of a man said as he limped out of the octagon. Hendrix’s hand was held high as he still couldn’t get over the fact that David had done it. Goliath was slain. For now... Winner: Hendrix -- via submission -- 3:52 of the 3rd and final round. Voting: It was VERY close but came down to a 3-2 decision. --- It was time for the last match of the evening and this one was going to be another war. Jakob Alexander vs. Jonathan Stark --- The talking was over as “Mutant X” by Twiztid began to play. Various colors of pyro and strobes swirled as Stark rose from the stage. He put his arms up in an X and then slid a thumb across his throat. This would be Mutant X going against Weapon X. Stark made his way into the octagon flanked by his trainers and pulled off his custom “Mutant X” shirt made by Xyience. Next up was Mutant X himself. The lights dimmed as strobes began to pulse to the beat of LL Cool J’s “It’s time for war” until pyros lit up the stage and Alexander rose from the stage. He snarled at Stark as he headed down the ramp toward the octagon. He pulled off his custom “Weapon X” shirt by SilverStar and THREW IT at Stark. Stark started to come forward but the referee warned him he would throw the match out. The crowd was in a frenzy as the two stared each other down, jawing at one another as the referee told them the rules. They both nodded they understood and didn’t even bother touching gloves as they backed to their corners. DING DING! ROUND 1! And it was on! Both men came rushing from the corner like a bull and collided in a hailstorm of fists. Both men wouldn’t back down until Stark finally shoved Alexander back and went for the front kick that put him in round three but Alexander caught it and took Stark to the mat! Stark held guard but Weapon X would not be denied as he moved to side mount and began raining down elbows into Stark’s ribs. He quickly slide himself into a north/south position and started slamming well placed shots into Stark’s ribs on both sides. Alexander laughed as he easily slid into side mount again and mounted up grabbing Stark’s arm. Clack Clack! 10 SECONDS LEFT! Alexander grabbed the arm and applied the arm bar but couldn’t quite get it completely locked in. Stark blocked him from locking it in and held on for ten seconds. DING DING Alexander stood up cursing as he went to his corner. Stark shook his arm as he moved to his. There wouldn’t be a first round KO tonight. Both men got their instructions and then the bell sounded. DING DING! ROUND 2! Both men began the same way, exploding on each other but Stark got the upper hand catching Alexander on the jaw. Alexander fell back but didn’t go down. Stark charged and jumped with a flying head kick but Alexander ducked and threw with his south paw and nailed Stark right above the eye busting the skin open as blood began to ooze. Stark fell from momentum and Alexander swarmed on him mounting up on him. Stark was in trouble and he knew it as he saw an opening and reversed Alexander taking top mount and holding it for himself. Now it was his turn. He went for the already injured eye of Alexander and as soon as he had the opening, he busted the wound open again. Alexander tried to see through the blood and the stars but instead found two well-placed hammer fists right in the mouth. “Not so pretty now, are you pretty boy?” Stark roared slamming more fists where he could find an opening. Alexander was at his mercy, he couldn’t see the clock, but he could still hear. CLACK CLACK! Alexander grabbed both of Stark’s fists to keep him from punching. Stark tried to pull away but Alexander wouldn’t let go. Stark was ONE punch away from ending the fight and he was about to get that chance as his right hand came free. DING DING! Stark swore as he stood up, blood gushing down his face. Alexander was no better. Both corners worked on stopping the bleeding. Alexander even spit out what looked like a tooth. They got their instructions, cleaned up, and their cuts were not running at the moment. Both men knew it came down to this, the third and final round and neither wanted to go to the judges. DING DING! ROUND 3! Both men came out a little slower this time. Stark tested Alexander with some jabs and Alexander would back Stark off with inside leg kicks when he looked as if he was moving in to strike. Alexander threw a leg kick and Stark bounced back just in time only to find Alexander coming at him. Alexander feigned a punch which made Stark sway away. As soon as Stark came back in and Alexander landed Alexander caught Stark with a spinning back elbow. Stark landed against the cage as the crowd was getting more into it. Alexander charged forward working the body as Stark turtled up. Stark finally shoved Alexander back and flew forward, this time the head kick landed! Alexander was rocked and Stark went in for the kill going to shoot in and finish with some ground and pound but Alexander threw a knee and Stark went down. Alexander leapt without thinking, just out of pure instinct, and landed on top of Stark. CLACK CLACK! 10 SECONDS LEFT! No. It could not end like this. Alexander threw all his strength into his punches and everyone of them landed. The referee finally dove over Stark and Alexander jumped up and ran, jumping on the cage and roared with his arms extended before making an X. Stark was arguing with the referee telling him he was covering and there was no reason to call for the fight. It wouldn’t matter since the decision was final. In the war of words, it was “Weapon X” Jacob Alexander that would be moving on to the semi-finals. It had been a war, as promised, and both men were met with standing cheers from the crowd. Stark left the octagon still pissed off. Winner: Alexander -- via TKO -- 4:55 of the 3rd and final round. Voting: This was EVEN CLOSER than the previous fight. Judges were conflicted on both sides. 3-2 After Alexander was cleaned up he threw on his shirt and went to the top of the stage. Alexander put the X up as Umaga just stared him down. Vayne and Hendrix both made a fighting pose. Ladies and Gentleman -- your SEMI-FINALISTS! With that, round 3 was in the books. Submission of the Night: Hendrix -- 50,000.00 Knockout of the Night: Alexander -- 50,000.00 Fight of the Night: Stark/Alexander -- 50,000.00 to be paid to both men.
