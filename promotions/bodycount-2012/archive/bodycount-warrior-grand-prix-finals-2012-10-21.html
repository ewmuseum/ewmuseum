BodyCount Presents: The Warrior Grand Prix finals!

21 October 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J The Ripper — Oct 21, 2012 THE WARRIOR GRAND PRIX FINALS JAKOB ALEXANDER VS. VIKTOR REISE After weeks upon weeks of fighting and men from around the world giving their blood, sweat, and tears to be named The Best Fighter in the World as well as be the first ever Warrior Grand Prix Champion, it all came down to two men: Jakob Alexander who was virtually unknown came in and blew through opponents and gave the fans some of the best matches in the tournament. He showed that he was more than just a flash in the pan and now he stood ready to fight in the finals and claim what he felt was his. Standing in his way was the behemoth of the tournament, Viktor Reise. Viktor had literally beaten a man to the point there was still speculation if he could ever compete in any type of venue ever again. After Reise fell from the tournament it was discovered that Hendrix had failed a substance abuse test which put Reise right in the finals after his original opponent, Stark, was unable to make it back to Germany. The BodyCount Arena was packed. Fans from all around the world stood screaming at the top of their lungs because after all the fan fair of the day it was time. It was time to decide who was the champion. The sold out arena was rocking the place as a familiar chant started to play with the sound of seagulls in the background. The lights dimmed as fog covered the stage and the strobe lights played off the chrome of the stage. Rising from the stage itself was none other than the national hero himself; Viktor Reise. The fans went ballistic as “Reise Reise” by Rammstein played. Reise was flanked by his trainers as the mammoth of a man gave a wave to his fellow Germans and entered the octagon. The lights dimmed once more and strobes danced around as LL Cool J’s “It's Time for War” began to blast through the arena. Jakob Alexander rose from the stage and made an X with his arms as pyros shot off. He was met with a mixed reaction as he sneered and continued to the octagon throwing off his custom “Weapon X” shirt as he entered. He jogged around the octagon and as he passed Reise sneered “Even a redwood can be chopped down, you freak.” Reise just watched him with his cold, calculating eyes. It probably wasn’t the best choice to poke the proverbial bear with a stick but Jakob Alexander was riding a high. The two were called to the center of the octagon and as they had all tournament, were recited the rules. Neither man touched gloves as they returned to their corners. ROUND 1 DING DING Both men came out at a steady pace. Reise struck first throwing a giant right but Alexander ducked and took Hendrix’s strategy as he worked the ribs and the back of the knee on Reise. Jakob went to dance outside but Reise grabbed his arm and threw an elbow into his sternum possibly breaking the collar bone. Alexander got away from Reise and held his sternum for a moment but Reise was coming forward until the referee stepped in and held Reise back, calling for the doctor. Jakob Alexander objected and said he was fine. The doctor looked at his chest and nodded as the fight resumed. Reise took a moment to shake the pain from his leg and moved back forward. Reise came in again for more of his devastating strikes but Alexander shifted all the way around to Reise’s back and jumped on, locking in a choke but the biggest man in the tournament was still at a vertical base and quickly slammed his back and Jakob Alexander into the cage. Alexander, showing heart, refused to let go even though it felt like his collar bone would rib through his chest at any moment and wrenched tighter. Reise showed signs of being choked as he slammed Alexander faster into the cage but Alexander wouldn’t let go. Finally the big man reached behind him and with pure strength threw Alexander to the mat with a sickening thud. Reise went to drop a huge knee right on the injured collar bone but Alexander rolled right in time and jumped up throwing a kick right to the back of Reise’s skull. THUD! The fans echoed with “ohhhh” as it was like a gunshot going off. Alexander didn’t let up as Reise didn’t go down and kept throwing shots to the back of the head until the sound for the end of the round signaled. “CLACK CLACK! 10 SECONDS LEFT!” Alexander became frantic throwing more kicks to the head of Reise. Reise finally hit the mat and Alexander swarmed trying to get the choke in and managed to lock it in perfect with Reise flat on his stomach. DING DING! END OF ROUND 1! Alexander roared out of frustration as Reise slowly pushed himself up, wobbling a little to his corner. Both men were checked on, given advice, and then the bell sounded for round two. ROUND 2! DING DING! Alexander came out like a lightning bolt ducking around Reise and throwing precise shots. So far Alexander had been the one in control which was impressive considering the size of Reise. Reise, though, was seeing double from the kicks. He swung but missed by an inch. Alexander slammed more fists and kicks into the ribs, shifted around and went to the back of the knee throwing his shoulder into it. Reise went down to one knee and Alexander went to throw another devastating kick but as he threw it Reise caught the leg. Alexander looked surprised as the giant stood and drove a right hand straight into Alexander’s nose, cartilage exploded, and blood began to pour from Alexander’s nose. Rage and adrenaline took over for Alexander though and started to actually do the one thing no one had done all tournament and stand and bang with Reise. He ducked punches and drove his own, busting Reise’s nose, lip, and opening a cut above his eye. Alexander went to throw the death punch but Reise caught his fist! Reise snarled and threw a knee to Alexander’s ribs and you could hear the snap. Reise snarled as he limped forward pushing Alexander toward the cage. Alexander felt the back of his head hit the cage and Reise dropped the atomic bomb. One hard left followed by a devastating right and Alexander crumpled to the ground – out cold. The referee called for the bell. “LADIES AND GENTLEMAN, YOUR WINNER AND FIRST EVER WARRIOR GRAND PRIX CHAMPION – VIKTOR RESISE!” The place exploded as confetti the color of the German flag fell from the sky. The medical crew checked on Jakob Alexander as Reise was presented with the Warrior Grand Prix title, trophy, and an even more lucrative contract than he had before. He also had won a future title shot whenever he chose. On top of all that he won 10 Million dollars which was presented to him as well as the title of “The Best Fighter in The World”. After all the blood it was done. Just like that Jakob Alexander fell short by a hair and Viktor Reise was the first ever Warrior Grand Prix Champion! Reise held the trophy high with the title over his shoulder as an interviewer held a microphone up. All Reise had to say was this, translated from German: “FOR GERMANY AND ITS PEOPLE! THE GREATEST COUNTRY IN THE WORLD!” Reise was met with thunderous cheers and applause as the first ever Warrior Grand Prix came to an end. Reise via KO -- 4:42 of Round 2. http://www.bcfighting.com/viktorwgp.png
