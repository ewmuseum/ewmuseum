The "No Show" rule.

31 August 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J The Ripper — Aug 31, 2012 Since this tournament is being limited to only 16 individuals it's very important that everyone shows up. Anyone that doesn't show up (and trust me if you check the forums, the site (http://www.bcfighting.com) the BC twitter (@BCFighting), and a lot of your big "wrestling news" sites (ex: http://www.ewrestler.net) you should have plenty of warning in advance on everything) this is what will go down: I have sworn that no matter the outcome of a match (as long as both participants competed) NO ONE will be injured in this tournament so your wrestler/fighter is completely safe but if for some reason you no show for no reason and waste a potential spot someone else may have wanted your character could end up on a very bad side of things. I just want to be up front and honest about how serious I am about this. I'll be taking the time to write results so make sure you show up ;). J The Ripper — Aug 31, 2012 I'm like at least 30% sure this is just a rumor! http://ewrestler.net/demo/rumors.php?idrum=15 :-P Justin — Sep 1, 2012 I have no issue with this rule.
