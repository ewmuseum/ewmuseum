A Chronicle of the Adriatic Gargoyle

13 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spike Saunders — Sep 13, 2012 "Fifteen years ago I chose to chronicle the life of one individual that had made a name for himself by putting his life on the line every day of the week. This specimen was a highlight attraction in all the underground fighting arena's in the Eastern Hemisphere. A lean, mean, killing machine -- as the saying goes. He caught my attention and I have followed him ever since. This is a Chronicle of his life. His name was Johannes Antonious de Castonovo. A six foot one, two hundred twenty four pound behemoth, born on 1985's 24th day of October in Venice, Italy. A fighter from the day he was born. Growing up, always the bully, never the bullied. Unlike others he took no pleasure in beating the weak down. His goal and ultimate destination was always on finding somebody that could give him a fight for his life. Even at an early age as a young tyke Johannes would participate in street fights with the hooligans looking to cause trouble. Always the same result would come to pass. A mess of blood on the stone and his stature standing tall. This was who he was. This was the nature of the man that would come to be known as the Adriatic Gargoyle. From his early teens into mid twenties, anytime there was a fight going on in Europe or surrounding countries -- expect his presence, is what the folks would say. The underground fighting scene was filled with hopeful's in his eyes. Men and women that gave it their all but failed to give him that challenge... that rush... that he was looking for. Even the barbaric electrified cage fights and other monstrosities the demon promoters would put together failed to provide him what he sought. His most common destination was an underground, below sea-level establishment called 'The Deep'. It was here that his string of victories with an unprecedented undefeated streak coined him the monicker of the Adriatic Gargoyle. His fights would eventually lead him to joining the MMA Fighting training camp called 'Golden Glory' in the Netherlands, in effort of honing his own style to take with him into the cage. In hopes of finding somebody else with a similar purpose in life. He would train with all he was worth for a full year before taking to the Black Market in London. Yet another training ground with the best of the best. Few even took up his challenges for a fight and were presently surprised by the result, win or loss. It would be in 2007 that Johannes would finally join a true fighting promotion. The Fight Festival. The first step in his journey to where this Chronicle started. He only fought six fights in the course of two years, racking up four impressive victories and two defeats that made him reconsider his goals. The caged arena was a spectacle he had grown use to for a decade, but his travels still failed to bring about a worthy opponent. Someone that would risk their lives alongside his own when in the center of that ring. Somebody with the same skill and momentum. Somebody that was a true killer. This Chronicle will go on to include Johannes Antonious de Castonovo's introduction into the professional wrestling circuit, by way of the 4 Corner's Wrestling developmental territory of NBW, and ultimately joining the roster of the NBW. His timing could not have been any better while they had just announced the revival and partnership with The Fighting Zone. A venue that Johannes had always wanted to be a part of but was too young at the time to participate. His destination would see him take on the last TFZ Champion, William Arthur Reagan, a fellow kill or be killed fighter. A warrior he put on the shelf while winning the TFZ Championship. His own reign would last sometime before his defeat at the hands of another great fighter in Keegan. Keegan and WAR would face each other in a final match with a fatal outcome, and the end of the TFZ that he enjoyed. His goal to be the best was finished. There were no other places to hone his fighting skills aside from returning to the underground. That lifestyle is one he chose to avoid returning to and instead sought out a different breed of fighters and chose a life of professional wrestling. Joining the Family Keeling alongside Aleczander, they would win the Dynasty Tag Team Titles at NBW's Legacy pay per view event. And then it happened. Heaven was knocking on his door. The promotion BodyCount came to existence and with it the announcement of the Warrior Grand Prix. A tournament with the best fighters in the world. Some old names like himself, and even some new faces. They all had one thing in common and that was the thirst for the fight. A fight for their lives. A goal to be the very best in the world and have the clout to back it up. A place where they could put their lives on the line to obtain that goal. Succeed as the victor, or wish for death as the defeated. My name is Civial Percie, and this is the Chronicle of the 'Adriatic Gargoyle' Johannes Antonious de Castonovo's life before his toughest fight to date -- Versus Umaga Reihana." Elsewhere in Germany, standing next to a ruined old building stood the Adriatic Gargoyle with a bottle of Jack in hand. His attention focused on the surveillance camera fastened high above to his right. "Monaco di Baviera, in Germania. Come ho perso questo piscio buco." (Munich, Germany. How I have missed this piss hole.) He took a long swig from the bottle in his hand. "Wrestling in tutto il mondo e di evitare buche come la Germania. Poi Body Count del Gran Premio del torneo è venuto a vita." (Wrestling across the world and avoiding pits like Germany. Then Body Count's Grand Prix tournament came to life.) He took another gulp and looked out at the desolate scenery of the part of town he was in. "Il mio sangue è in combattimento. La mia passione è quello di combattere." (My blood is in fighting. My passion is to fight.) The rest of the bottle found itself drain down his throat before he idly shook the bottle in his hand. "Poiché io sono un combattente." ( For I am a Fighter.) A close up shot of the Adriatic Gargoyle filled the screen only to be replaced by the incoming bottle chucked at the camera. Shattering the bottle and lens and ending the feed.
