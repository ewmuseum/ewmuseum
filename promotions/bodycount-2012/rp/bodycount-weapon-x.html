BodyCount — Roleplay

Weapon X

22-23 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jakob Alexander — Sep 22, 2012 Now, you realize that Weapon X AKA Jakob Alexander, is the real deal. Went to WAR with Mike Sodo for fifteen minutes, neither man backing down. They thought that a whole tournament of dominant mixed martial artistry isn't who I am. I went out and did what I had to do. Munich, Germany. Warrior Grand Prix? That is MY house. The scene flashes to show Jakob Alexander surrounded by his fellow mixed martial artists in his gym located in Boston. .:Jakob Alexander:. It's not about tommorow, fellas. Tonight is the night that you show the world just who in the world you are. What are you gonna bring to the damn table? Are you gonna bust somebody in their mouth? Are you gonna do your god damn job? Are you gonna breathe from your chest? Are you gonna fight from your heart and give everything damn thing you've got? Cuz I'm gonna do that. I'm gonna give you all everything thing I've got, every fight. That dedication, that drive. I need that tonight! Scene flashes back. .:Jakob Alexander:. if I can make you think twice, I'm in your head. I've already got one added to the BodyCount, Anybody wanna join Mike Sodo? CaptainBananas — Sep 23, 2012 (FADEIN: Mike Sodo looks dead ahead in his street clothes. His face is a bit swollen from the Round 2 scuffle from the night before.) Sodo: Jakob... Weapon X... Da Pitbull... whatever your f[BLEEP]ckin' name is this week... you're gonna be seein' a lot of me. It doesn't matter if you want to or not. This isn't over. (FADEOUT)
