Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

KOMBAT-PRO's own founding coverage, posted to a Portuguese wrestling forum between 31 December 2008 and 9 January 2009, narrates a fictional promotion founded by a fictional president, Hidetasu Sugiura, and stocked entirely with real New Japan, All Japan and Pro Wrestling NOAH talent, portrayed under their own names in an invented storyline. Sugiura's own contracted roster split into two rival factions carried over from real New Japan continuity and renamed for the occasion — RISE became the Dark Ikemen Entity, and Great Bash Heel became Team F*ck The World! — while three masked late signings unmasked as Kaz Hayashi, Shuji Kondo and Satoshi Kojima.

Its debut show, 27 January 2009 at the Tokyo Ryogoku Kokugikan, drew a reported “Super No Vacancy” house of 9,584. Before the bell, Sugiura announced that every one of KOMBAT-PRO's championships would be decided over the promotion's first two tours, with a further surprise held back for the second. The show closed on a five-on-five, two-out-of-three-falls war between the Dark Ikemen Entity and Team FTW: Shinsuke Nakamura took the first fall over Tomohiro Ishii, Togi Makabe answered for Team FTW over Prince Devitt, and Hirooki Goto won the deciding fall over Jado to hand the Dark Ikemen Entity the main event.

By its own “Desperado” tour that June and July, KOMBAT-PRO carried four championships: a KP World Heavyweight title held by Nakamura, a Jr. Heavyweight title KENTA defended twice (including a 24-minute win over Shuji Kondo at the promotion's own debut in Tokyo's JCB Hall), a Tag Team title held by Masato Tanaka and Tatsuhito Takaiwa, and a Trios title the No Remorse Corps (Roderick Strong, Davey Richards and Rocky Romero) defended twice against a Team FTW trio. A third faction, the GURENTAI, entered the rivalry across the same tour, and Naomichi Marufuji spent it working off a handicap-match loss against two Dark Ikemen Entity members before returning the favor with a ringside chair.

No closing statement survives in what was read here, and the promotion's own life was never bounded by this forum's own coverage of it in the first place: a different member replying to the thread in March 2009 states directly that the booking had already run for over a year on other forums before this one, and continued running on those other forums alongside it.
