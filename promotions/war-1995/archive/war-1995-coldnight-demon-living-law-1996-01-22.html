WAR! Wrestling Federation — Roleplay

"Coldnight Demon" Kahl Kahlan & "The Living Law" Brutus Judd

22 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Night, as usual,is dark. A helicopter view of a mountainous and snwy area pans around with a flashlight. Eventually they find a man climbing up the mountain half-naked. When he reaches the top, the helicopter crew and a reporter are waiting.
Interviewer: Mr.Kahlan, is it true that you are entering LAW! wrestling. Kahlan: Yes. Interviewer: Why? Kahlan: For money to feed my relatives back home in the Ukraine, for recognition, for competition, for glory. Interviewer: Why are you called the COldnight Demon? Kahlan: Excuse me, my English is not perfect....I am Coldnight Demon, because I can withstand the coldest nights, as I am now. It is a nickname from my homeland.
Interviewer: And what are you going to do in LAW? Kahlan: Show the world how the ex-Soviet Unions best wrestler fights. Now, I must go. And with that, he continues climbing viciously up the steep mountain side. ___________________________________________________ "The Living Law" Brutus Judd, in an interview segment: I haven't got much to say, except that it must be known that I am the Living Law here in law. If you break the rules, or go to far, you'll be hearing from me. And the biggest lawbreaker I can see right now is Big Bob Rem is guilty as sin, and Captain Konran, or whatever his name is, shall be hearing from me soon. After the Jurys Verdict, there can only be one punishment. My "Death Penalty" Power bomb.
