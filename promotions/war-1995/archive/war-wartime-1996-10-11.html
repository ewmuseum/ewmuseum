Gib: Great, thanx Bob, but I`m not returning back to the ring in a hurry. I`m enjoying laying my posterior here in this commentary seat.
And it`s a pleasure to work alongside such a charming man. Bob: (Beaming smile). Thanks Suna. By the way, there`s no chance of getting shaved, is there?
Gib: No way Bob. The beard stays. It`s a total love magnet. Bob: Negative or positive attraction? Haha, just kidding Suna. Anyway folks, we`ve got a great show in store for you. T.J. "THUNDER" DECAN & SETH BLITZSPEAR get it on while War!Lord champion ROBSKI battles AMERICAN PATRIOT & DAMIEN LEGEND tonight. SID "STRAY CAT"
STARR returns to the WAR! ring after a four month absence to take on
WAR! newcomer "Mr.FUNK" JULIUS JACKSON Jnr. Also returning after a lengthy absence is TYLER STEVENS who battles the returning JASON
MIDNIGHT. Suna: Two lovelies there. Bob: I`m sure they`re pleased to hear that. Some more big news. THE
CHAOS KNIGHTS have had to pull out of the last minute of their match
against APOCALYPSE because of flight delays. Replacing them is KALIFORNIA HEAT. We`ve got a whole lot more tonight as well folks, so keep watching. Also watch out for the Nuclear War!Fare report with the charming Susan Andrex...sorry...Andrews. Now let`s get to
Robski`s first match of the evening,against the American Patriot. ****************************************** Non-Title AMERICAN PATRIOT ( 8 ) Vs. ROBSKI (7) ****************************************** Patriot took out all the stops in this brutal trans-atlantic battle. Both men are patriots, but of different countries. It all came to a head here. Patriot began the assault, jumping Robski in the ring, then flooring him, working him over with elbow drops and knee drops. Robski battled back only to be levelled again but continued to kick out of Patriots pin attempts. Robski succeeded in reversing a whip to the ropes and threw Patriot to the outside. Robski followed and began clobbering Patriot with chairs and stairs. Then Robski pulled Patriot back into the ring before throwing Patriot out again, over the top rope and onto the timekeepers table. Robski resumed the assault outside the ring, but when Patriot reversed a whip into the metal post, Robski was knocked cold. Patriot pulled Patriot into the ring and went for the pin. Somehow, Robski got his hand on the bottom rope. Patriot was amazed, and set up Robski for the Patriot missile.
Robski got caught and Patriot covered for the three. Result: Pinfall by American Patriot MR: 7 MD: 8.46
****************************************** SID "STRAY CAT" STARR (8)Vs. "MR.FUNK" JULIUS JACKSON Jnr. (6) ***************************************** Sid Starr paraded to the ring with a horde of beauties in tow. They had to be ushered back by WAR! security. "Mr.Funk" responded in kind, dancing down the aisle with 70`s chicks. Starr kept cool and the match progressed tenatively for the first couple of minutes, neither really making any dramatic impact.
Suddenly Starr executed a perfect drop kick and the match totally went under his control. Over the next couple of minutes, Jackson got a few second winds, but no real gusts that blew Starr down.
Starr finished Jackson Jnr. off with a top rope DDT. Result: Pinfall by Starr MR: 7 MD: 9.35 *************************************** MUTILATOR (8) Vs. DARKNESS (8) *************************************** Two huge men squared off. Mutilator received plenty of boos while
Darkness got all the cheers as he entered to the Crossroads musics.
Neither of the two mammoths were prepared to give an inch, and the first couple of minutes was a stalemate as the two matched power for power. Eventually it was Mutilator who was on the retreat as Darkness slowly got the better of Mutilator. A piledriver clearly put the match in the favour of Darkness. Mutilator wasn`t out of tricks though and smashed Darkness with an upper-cut out of nowhere. Both fell to the floor, but Mutilator got up first and executed a neck-breaker, setting Darkness up for the Mutilator Bomb. Darkness resisted and then displayed a rare moment of skill by pulling Mutilator over into a small-package. Mutilator kicked out, whipped Darkness to the rope and caught him in a massive belly to belly suplex, over into a pin.
Result: Pinfall by Mutilator MR: 8 MD: 11.59 ********************************** BANE (8) Vs. BOUNTY HUNTER (7) ********************************** Bane entered the ring like a masked storm and proceeded to level Bounty Hunter with severe body blows and body drops. But a mistake by
Bane in the fifth minute gave Bounty Hunter the chance to get back in the match. Bane had executed a gutbreaker on Hunter and went to the second rope for a big splash. Hunter got his knees up and winded
Bane. Hunter, showing more technical expertise than in the past, kept
Bane on the ground and worked him over for several minutes. Then, Bane kicked out of a pin attempt and exploded back into action, levelling
Hunter with lefts and rights. When he thought the time was right he put Hunter between his legs and went for the DeathDrop. However, before he could, Hunter had enough presence of mind to block and then lift Bane over the top rope and to the outside. Bane fell bad, Hunter collapsed inside the ring and got a countout victory. Result: Bounty
Hunter by countout. MR: 8 MD: 12.52
********************* APOCALYPSE (3) Vs. KALIFORNIA HEAT(9) ********************** Danny Delane and Matt Powers took note of Bane`s tactics and assaulted the ring with a vengeance, taking the unprepared Apocalypse with full venom. Apocalypse tried to counter, but within ninety-seconds it was all over after Delane`s high flying assault and Powers reverse bulldog on Chaos. Result: KH by pinfall MR: 4/8 MD: 1.32
**************************************** JASON MIDNIGHT (9) Vs. TYLER STEVENS (9) **************************************** Match of the evening. MidNights blend of martial artistary ad wrestling skills against Stevens high-flying, knowledgeable moves provided a memorable contest that see-sawed back and forth. The match promised much for the future as both showed off their wares. MidNights great relationship with the fans - a relationship that Stevens once had but has now forsaken - looked as though it would prove the decisive factor between the two evenly-matched competitors as he rallied from every beating Stevens dished out. Ultimately it was
Venom, Steven`s aide, who was the factor that influenced the outcome. MidNight had just executed the DreamLand and covered Stevens for a sure pin. With the referee concentrating on counting, Venom pulled
MidNight off. MidNight responded furiously, complaining to the ref, who saw nothing. In the confusion, Stevens rolled MidNight up and got a sour victory following a very sweet match. Result: Pinfall by
Stevens. MR: 9 MD: 35.05 ******************************* ANNHILATORS (8) Vs. INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION (9) ******************************* Annihilators were up against very tough opposition in Industrial Revolution but passed the test in another exciting WAR! match-up. Both teams displayed an impressive array of high-impact moves. Even though it was Industrial Revolution, especially Hakeem the Machine, who were the better of the teams in terms of pure wrestling, it was the Annihilators to use illegal methods that got the better of IR in the end. Stretching the refs count for double teams, choking
MegaDiesel with corner ropes and distracting the ref to let the other work on their opponents worked wonders for a very promising team. Ultimately, it was the fast-moving, high flying 500lb+ giant Venom who was too much for IR and managed to get Hakeem with his devastating "Injection"
moonsault. Result: Annihilators by pinfall MR: 9 MD: 19.45 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ NUCLEAR WAR!FARE - THE HOLOCAUST report. Take it away, guys! ************************* Non-Title STORMSTALKER (6) Vs. NOMAD (8) ************************* The masked wrestler, Nomad stretched Liberty Champion StormStalker in this non-title match. Nomad matched StormStalkers pace, and showed a little more ring savvy. Several times Nomad seemingly had StormStalker beat, but StormStalker had the resolve - and the fans - to get back into the fight. StormStalker slowed the pace, and began to control the match more to his liking. Nomad, however, reversed an arm bar and threw StormStalker to the ropes. Nomad followed through and clotheslined StormStalker to the outside before he even had a chance to bounce off the ropes. Nomad climbed to the top rope and kneedropped the prone StormStalker from the outside. Then he dragged
StormStalker back into the ring and executed the Caravan Superplex from the top turnbuckle, resulting in victory. Perhaps if the title had been on the line StormStalker would have had more fight in him, but not this night. Result: Nomad by pinfall MR: 8 MD: 13.48
************************* SETH BLITZSPEAR (6) Vs. T.J. " THUNDER" DECAN (8) ************************* A total grudge match. Seth fought for the fans, angry at Decan`s turn against them. But if Decan had turned a corner, he found on the new street a very sadistic streak, administering a first class beating to Seth in this encounter. Seth was having big trouble with Decan`s mass, which Decan was using very effectively. The ex-world War!
champion was not going down without a fight and began one of his infamous "Blitzes", ignoring the punishment, or perhaps storing it and turning it back on Decan. Seth turned to his trusty clothesline and rocked Decan from one corner to the next. But Decan wouldn`t go down.
Seth got desperate and climbed the top rope and leaped onto Decan`s back with a tight sleeper. Decan slowly went down, staggered into the centre of the ring then fell back, aiming to crush Seth. It succeeded. Seth was winded. Decan went to work on Seth`s back, executing vertical splashes and big elbow drops onto his spine. Seth tried to blitz again, but Decan hammered him down. Then he picked
Seth up into a torture rack which he held for several minutes. Seth was too stupid to submit, and eventually the ref decided to stop the match to prevent serious harm. Result: Decan by submission. MR: 7 MD:
20.56 *************************************** WORLD WAR TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH THE AGONIZERS (6) Vs. LOST BOYS (6) ************************************** General Leo and Matt Grappler tried their best to make this chance be the match that got them the tag belts, but the match just wouldn`t stay in the ring as the two teams continually ended up fighting outside the ring. Eventually, and unsuprisingly, both teams were counted out. Result: Double Count-out MR: 5 MD: 3.25
*************************************** CAGE MATCH. Non-Title DAMIEN LEGEND (8) Vs. ROBSKI (8) *************************************** This totally bloody, savage encounter between ex-partners was a brutal experience to watch. There wasn`t much in the way of wrestling. Just simple bashing each other into the side of the cage. Neither of the two competitors made any real effort to escape, because it would be to admit defeat, even though it meant victory. Highlights of the event were Robski slamming the cage door on Legends face and Legened swinging Robski by the feet into the cage. Oh, and the superplex by
Legend on Robski from the top of the cage (which left them both out cold for a couple of minutes). Robski recovered first, but Legend was hot on his tail and executed a belly to back suplex on Robski as he climbed the cage wall. Legend then made a move himself to escape and almost managed to escape Robski grabbed his foot. Robski yanked him down. However, Legend fell
bad and hurt his knee on the fall. Robski escaped, got the victory and returned to the ring to work on the injury which Robski noticed and proceeded
to injure the knee with chairs, T.V`s and anything else. Then he undid the cage wall bracketing and let it fall on Legend. Legend needed treatment, and after threats by War! Officials, Robski left allowing Legend to be treated for his injuries.
Result: Robski first to the floor. MR: 8 MD: 24.38 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (c) WAR! Wrestling Federation 1996
