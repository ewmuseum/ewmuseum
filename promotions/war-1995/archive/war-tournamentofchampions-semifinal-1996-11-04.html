WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

Tournament of Champions — Semi-Final

4 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Posted on behalf of [name withheld]. Dave Scott: Welcome back. Tonight we've got two big matches... Tonnage meets Damien Legend and Seth Blitzspear goes up against TJ "Thunder"
Decan. Booster Dunne: Tonnage and Legend have never exactly been close pals. Tonnage wants to regain the World WAR! title and Legend has wanted to win a title here in WAR! for a long time. It should be a good match.
DS: Let's go to our ring announcer! Announcer: This is the first of two fourth round matches in the WAR! Tournament of Champions! It is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, a former World WAR! Champion from Albany, New York, weighing in at 565 pounds..... Here is... TONNAGE!!!!
(Split reaction from the crowd) A: And his opponent, from Seattle, Washington, weighing 323 pounds... "THE RECKONER" DAMIEN LEGEND!!! (Another split reaction from the crowd, although a few more cheers than for
Tonnage) Tonnage and Legend stared each other down in the middle of the ring. Tonnage had a seven inch and 242 pound advantage over his opponent. Neither was able to take an advantage for the first few minutes. They traded blows until
Legend's leg sweep brought Tonnage down. Legend kept Tonnage on the mat and tried to wear him down. Tonnage dodged an elbow drop and locked in an armbreaker, showing some technical proficiency. He whipped Legend to the corner and splashed him. Tonnage continued to use high-impact moves on
Damien, utilizing his weight advantage. Legend was pummelled with bodyslams, suplexes, and powerbombs. Tonnage looked to have the match well in hand, but
Damien pulled a DDT out of nowhere. They both laid on the mat for awhile before Legend rolled over on top of Tonnage. The big man raised his shoulder before the three count. Damien slowly pulled Tonnage to his feet and stunned him with a series of European uppercuts.
DS: Legend now taking control with those uppercuts... Damien throws Tonnage to the ropes... huge flying clothesline! Tonnage is in trouble! Damien covers him ...1....2... Tonnage's shoulder is up.
BD: Legend isn't letting up. You've got to keep on Tonnage if you want to beat him. DS: Legend off the ropes, drops a knee. He pulls up Tonnage again, fires him into the corner and follows him in. Tonnage catches him with a back elbow to the face! Legend goes down!
BD: Tonnage goes down on one knee also. He's not able to follow up on that counter. At this point of the match, he could be running out of stamina. Maybe he needs to hit the stairmaster more.
DS: Tonnage, now moving very slowly, picks up Legend. Tonnage with a sideslam! He goes for the cover...1...2... Legend kicks out. Tonnage is up again and takes Legend to the corner. He drives his shoulder into Legend. BD: Tonnage looks tired. He's just leaning on Legend in the corner. That might be wearing Damien down a bit, but I think it's just a tactic by the big guy to get a little rest. Retirement must be looking pretty good to him now.
DS: I hope Tonnage doesn't hear you saying that, Booster. He's starting to move now... He whips Legend to the opposite corner, runs in, and rams another shoulder into him! That just about tore Legend in two!
BD: One Legend is enough for me. DS: Tonnage backs up to the other corner.... runs in full speed... splash... Legend moved out of the way! Tonnage was hurt by that! BD: Legend's down, too. DS: Tonnage struggles to get over to the prone Damien Legend. He tries to pick him up... inside cradle by Legend!! ...1...2...thr.. NO! BD: Legend was playing possum. I hate it when they do that. I can't believe Tonnage fell for it! DS: Legend is going to try to suplex Tonnage! He's got the big man up... no, he can't hold him up. Tonnage reverses it. He has Legend in the air... brainbuster! BD: That was a great move! Legend was knocked senseless. DS: Tonnage struggling to follow it up... Legend rolls him up again! But Tonnage rolls on top of him! ...1...2...3! Tonnage got him! BD: And he did it wrestling like a small 400-pounder! DS: Nice effort from Damien Legend, but Tonnage was a little too much for him. Members of Armageddon have come down to ringside to congratulate Tonnage. He needs one more win if he wants to regain the title, though.
BD: Notice who isn't in the ring, though. TJ Decan hasn't come out to congratulate Tonnage. DS: Decan has to prepare for his match in a few moments against perhaps the greatest superstar WAR! has ever known - Seth Blitzspear! BD: There are a lot of people in WAR! who would disagree with that statement, including me. Seth is okay, but he's no Blue and Gold. DS: Seth and Decan at one time were friends, but Decan was upset over Blitzspear's decision to relinquish the title and he joined Armageddon, which was a shock to just about everyone. BD: I saw it coming. DS: They've squared off a few times recently, but this may be their most important match-up. The winner will face Tonnage for the World WAR! Championship, and the loser goes home after a great tournament run. BD: It would be very interesting if Decan wins and has to face Tonnage, and we don't need that kind of interest here in WAR!, so I'm going with Blitzspear in this match.
DS: Wouldn't another Blitzspear-Tonnage match also be very interesting? BD: Kinda. DS: Okay... Let's go to our ring announcer... Announcer: This contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, coming to the ring with his manager, Pat Mills, from parts unknown, weighing 306 pounds.... Here is SETH BLITZSPEAR!!! (Huge crowd pop as Seth runs to the ring) A: His opponent, a member of Armageddon, weighs in tonight at 560 pounds even.... T.J. "THUNDER" DECAN!!! (Crowd boos) There was no love lost between these two. Decan and Blitzspear went right after each other in their typical brawling fashion. Blitzspear quickly opened up a cut over Decan's left eye. That didn't slow Thunder down any, as he came back and rammed Seth face-first into the ringpost. Both seemed oblivious to pain and the referee's count. There had to be a winner, so the referee waved off a couple of double countouts and let the match continue. Back in the ring, Blitzspear got the upper hand. Seth used all his scientific wrestling knowledge to bring Decan down with a choke. He finally let him go when the ref threatened disqualification. Seth continued to stomp and punch on Decan. Armageddon members came to ringside to cheer on Thunder. Decan caught a distracted Blitzspear with a lariat, turning the tables in the match. Decan picked him up and delivered a gutbuster. Decan methodically pounded on the former World WAR! champion to the approval of his stablemates at ringside.
DS: T.J. whips Seth to the corner hard! Seth bounces out of the corner and gets driven to the mat by a huge Decan clothesline. Everything's going Decan's way now. BD: The thing with Seth is you never know when he'll get that second or third wind. He's got a lot of guts, probably to make up for the lack of brains. Pat Mills can only do so much thinking for him.
It's time for Seth to get that second wind before it's too late. DS: Decan is trying to finish it now! A huge powerslam by Thunder....1 ...2... not quite! Blitzspear kicked out. Thunder is quickly up and off the ropes... a big splash on Blitzspear! And another cover
...1....2... Blitzspear gets his shoulder up again.
BD: Seth has taken a beating in this match, but so has Decan. I'd say Seth is a much better conditioned athlete than Decan is, but you wouldn't know it by looking at them now. Decan looks very strong.
DS: He does look strong, and he's showing that strength now with a suplex, no, he's placing Seth on the top turnbuckle. He's going for a superplex! This is unbelievable! But Seth pushes him off the second rope... and Blitzspear leaps in the air and delivers a clothesline from the top rope! Decan is down!
BD: Seth still has a lot of fight left in him. DS: Seth unleashes a flurry of fists on Decan! The crowd is getting into it! Blood is covering Decan's face... BD: And that might be an improvement! DS: Decan is up in the center of the ring but Blitzspear has stunned him with this attack! Seth continues to hammer away! Decan's starting to wobble! BD: He's out on his feet. The ref better get in there and stop those closed fists Seth's using! DS: Blitzspear off the ropes... He's tripped! Biff McGriff reached in and grabbed his foot! Decan drops his weight on the back of Seth's head, driving his face into the mat!
BD: That's going to leave a mark. Not to mention a stain. DS: Wait! Coming down to ringside, it's Nemesis! He's attacking Armageddon! BD: Now that isn't smart. He must be a close relative of Blitzspear's. DS: Nemesis attacks McGriff... Felicia McGraw hits him in the back with a thrust kick... Nemesis goes down... Biff with a Full Metal Jacket! Meanwhile, in the ring, Decan drives Seth's head to the mat again with a DDT! BD: Nemesis is getting killed down there by Armageddon. Here comes the cavalry! It's Jerry Gordon, Stein Berg, and Jack Frost. They start to mix it up with Biff and Tonnage and the Lost Boys and
Apocalypse... DS: Decan hits a second DDT on Seth! Seth is out cold! BD: Now Bane's running down to ringside, too. He attacks Gordon. Felicia is kicking Nemesis, who's out of it. The Lost Boys and
Apocalypse are taking care of the Cold North.
DS: Decan has Seth set up for a third DDT... he hits it! Decan rolls him over...1...2...3! Unbelievable! Decan pinned Blitzspear! BD: Here come more re-enforcements.... Helel, Jason Midnight, Jim Bondo... DS: Just about everyone in WAR! is down there! I don't think the fans even realize yet that the match is over. And here come more guys... American Patriot...Raven...Kalifornia Heat... Officials are trying to break it up. BD: Nemesis is hurt. Gordon and Frost aren't in good shape. Patriot just decked McGriff... Raven jumps Helel... Insurance rates just went through the roof. Helmroy's going to be pissed.
DS: Well, as some order is starting to be restored at ringside, we'll take this opportunity to end our telecast tonight. At WAR!Mongers, we'll see the final of the Tournament of Champions - the tournament that just wouldn't end *will* end there. Tonnage and TJ "Thunder"
Decan are the participants... that is, if they get out of here in one piece! Until then, good night everyone!
BD: Later! (c) 1996 WAR!
