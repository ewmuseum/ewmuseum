War!Zone — Battlefield: New York

WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presents WAR!ZONE - BATTLEFIELD: NEW YORK From New York, New York On Feb 18 1997 With your mild hosts "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all to an evening of excitement with WAR! Tonight you will be witness to ELEVEN amazing matches, all out in the open in New York`s Central Park.
BF: And not a cloud in the sky. My good relations with Michael J.Fox helped me secure this wonderful venue, and we`ve got a big crowd here tonight.
Bob: One of the biggest Eric. And surely they must all be here for tonight main event, the World War Title Match between reiging champion Lord Byron, and the challenger, Seth Blitzspear.
BF: The question being asked is can Seth snap out of his funk and beat the man who seems unbeatable - Lord Byron. The answer has to be no. Seth has been mentally and emotionally damaged by all the betrayls around him, and there is no way he has recovered.
Bob: I don`t know whether I`m sure about that. He has been getting some moral support, but as yet, it has not been disclosed who from. Meanwhile,
Seth`s team for Cow 3 appears to be assembled, and it appears to be very mysterious. Seth & Stevens have been confirmed, but joining him will be two mystery partners.
BF: CoW is always full of suprises, but that ain`t going to save Blitzspears backside. Bob: Stevens is in action tonight, defending his Liberty title for the first time against Doug Simpkins.
BF: This must be their millionth match, and Stevens only won the last encounter. It`s been all Doug and that`s the way it`ll remain. He`ll have that belt back tonight.
Bob: One man who certainly stands a strong chance of getting a title tonight is Bane, challenging Charles Martel for the Unified Title. Martel has to beat Bane, in any possible way, to keep that title, and it has to be wondered whether he can do it against such an imposing force.
BF: Martel has the ability, but I must share in your doubts about his ability to cope with such an overwhelming force.
Bob: And tonight, we have a fourth title match. Kalifornia Heat lost their International War! Tag Team titles to The All-Americans last week, but they have the opportunity to bounce back immediately to take the World War
Tag Team titles for the team whose name is synonymous with the main tag belts, The Lost Boys.
BF: The Heat are too screwed up from their defeat to their All-Americans. There is a lot of self-doubt in their camp right now, and it is unlikely they`ll have the bottle.
Bob: One other highlight for tonight is the debut of Endou Dunne, who claims to be the great-great-great-great-great grandson of WAR! legend,
Booster Dunne.
BF: How can that be? Bob: He claims to be from the year 2173, I think. BF: Cripes. KRAKEN (9) Vs. DEATHJESTER (6) A far better performance from Kraken, who is improving by the week since his association with The AC. Following a back suplex, he executed "The Greatest
DDT", which knocked DeathJester out and got Kraken the victory.
MR: 7 MD: 4.51 Kraken by pinfall NUTTAGE (4) Vs. "MASKED MANIAC" JARRED TOKAREK w/Sunny B. (8) The 6`ll Tokarek was totally destroying the tiny Nuttage, so he started to undo the turnbuckle pad in an act of desperation. When the ref turned around, having been in a debate with Sunny B, he looked at Nuttage who pointed at Tokarek. The referee, Alan Kirby, stupidly believed Nuttage and
DQ`d Tokarek.
MR: 6 MD: 6.23 Nuttage by DQ REIGN OF TERROR (6) Vs. ELEMENTAL FORCE (8) All the cheating in the world couldn`t save them from the onslaught produced by the gigantic team of Elemental Force, but it did save their points tally.
Even though their resort to dirty tactics kept them in the match for longer than what was fair, the lawof averages caught up with them, and referee
Jamie Cassidy caught Pierre choking Maelstrom with the tag rope for the third time. For this, they were DQ`d.
MR: 6 MD: 16.44 Elemental Force by DQ "AGONIZER" BRENT GRAPPLER (8) Vs. "WILD-ONE" JACK MCARTHY (7) A fairly close match seperated only by Grapplers determination, which proved ultimately telling, gaining the pinfall victory with a reverseroll-up.
MR: 8 MD: 12.10 Grappler by pinfall ENDOU DUNNE made his first WAR! appearance (unless he was in WAR 2173!!!) and really got the crowd going, and pinned "Wee" Willy Winkle following an elbow drop off the top rope.
"JAMAICAN SUPERSTAR" ARRON IRON (8) Vs. NOMAD w/Flea (8) Fast flowing match with plenty of near pinfalls and excitement. The victroy was given to Nomad when Iron had flea by the neck on the ring apron, and
Nomad executed a neck-breaker from behind, and then covered Iron, hooking the tights to secure the victory.
MR: 9 MD: 23.12 Nomad by pinfall WINDY CITY WARRIORS (7) Vs. CANADIAN CARNAGE (8) Occassonally this match reached a level just above a brawl, but only occassionaly. When it did though, Carnage got the victory, with Canuck pinning Malcolm Todd with a cradle suplex.
MR: 8 MD: 8.55 Carnage by pinfall "HEAVY WEAPONS" MAX DOOM (7) Vs. "SIDEWINDER" BRUTUS NOIR (9) Noir was in a total frenzy and must have been seeing Max Doom as Randy
Blake, because he was so brutal, and so vicious, that Max Doom simply could not cope with the brutality. He tried to get away, but Noir wouldn`t let him. Noir applied the Marseille Strangler and got the victory.
MR: 8 MD: 7.15 Noir by submission UNIFIED TITLE MATCH CHARLES MARTEL w/Venom (8) Vs. BANE w/Flea (9) This match went pretty much as expected, with Bane squashing Martel and
Martel responding at various stages throughout the match by getting Bane on the mat, by out-smarting and out-wrestling him. Then at 14.41 this happened...
Bob: Martel has Flea by the neck. BF: Flea has been causing Martel problems all night. Bob: Bane has got up from that bulldog by Martel. he lumbers after him...but Venom hits the ring and blindsides Bane. Alan Kirby does not know what to do.
BF: Disqualify somebody. Bob: Flea was interfering. Bane should be DQ`d. He was beaten with that bulldog. But now Bane & Venom are going full-force on each other...and Venom slams Bane! Woah! That`s a sight and a half. Martel suplexes Flea into the ring, and now he goes to help Bane.
<Ding ding ding> Bob: The ref has called for the bell, but what is the decision. BF: EZ has the mic. He`s going to give us the result. EZ: Ladies and gentleman, the result of the match, as a result of a disqualification...and NEW UNIFIED CHAMPION...sorry, I mean, AND STILL
UNIFIED CHAMPION, CHARLES MARTEL! Bob: Well, I`m confused. BF: Me too. MR: 8 MD: 15.50 Martel by DQ WORLD WAR TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH THE LOST BOYS (6) Vs. KALIFORNIA HEAT (9) The Heat opened the match like a house on fire, hitting The Lost Boys hard and fast with some (slightly illegal) double teaming. After the first five minutes, the Lost Boys were disorientated, and regrouped, slowing the pace down much closer to their level They began to hurt the Heat with some high-impact moves, but they couldn`t keep the Heat down...
Bob: Delane with a dropkick on Greg. Frank enters but Powers powerlines
Frank out of the ring. Powers follows out. Delane whips Grewg to the rope...shoulderblock...the cover...
1! 2! Bob: Not quite a three. BF: Frank has levelled Powers with a chair outside the ring...and he ain`t stopping. Bob: Delane with a slam on Greg. Now an elbow drop...he covers once more... 1! 2! Bob: Frank breaks the count...and I think he has broken Delane`s head. BF: Look at the dent on the steel chair where Frank bashed Delane on the head. Bob: This is without a doubt going to be a disqualification victory for
Kalifornia Heat. Frank & Greg are both up and....spike piledriver...
BF: Look at the big blotch of blood left by that piledriver. Bob: Delane is bleeding profusly. He`s going to need some help. (Crowd heel pop) BF: Could this be the kind of help they want? It`s the British Empire... Bob: They`ve got hold of Powers outside the ring...Lord Savage locks Powers into a boston crab...Lord Wylde climbs to the top turnbuckle...OH MY GOD!
KNEE DROP from the top turnbuckle to the outside and onto Powers spine - while he was in the Boston Crab. He`s going to need some serious medical attention. BF: And so is Delane. A second spike brainbuster by The Lost Boys. Bob: This is horrific. BF: No, this is fun. This is WAR! MR: 8 MD: 16.29 Kalifornia Heat by DQ LIBERTY TITLE MATCH "HBK" TYLER STEVENS w/Big Duke (6) Vs. "DIRTY" DOUG SIMPKINS (8) Tyler Stevens could not have been prepared for the intensity and the brutality with which Simpkins fought. He was clearly out to injure the newly crowned Liberty Champion, working away at Stevens knee. With Big Duke and the crowd rallying him, Stevens found his way fighting back into the match, but the damage that Simpkins had done to his knee prevented any real momentum going. The Stevens did this...
Bob: Stevens with a face-rake! That`s not like him. BF: Maybe not, but it`s got him out of trouble. Doug was getting him ready for the DeathDrop.
Bob: Stevens goes for a back-suplex...but his knee gives way! Simpkins falls onto him... 1! 2! Bob: Stevens kicks out. He tries to get up, but his knee is in serious trouble. He`s having to hold onto the ropes to keep himself up.
BF: Doug is up now, and he is like a hawk. He sees his prey, defenceless... Bob: And he dropkicks Stevens out of the ring....and then he follows him to the outside...and he gets the steel steps and drops them on Stevens again and again. Big Duke is coming to Stevens aid, but Simpkins sees him coming...and he pushes Duke into the barriers and over into the crowd.
Simpkins slips back inside....and he gets a countout victory. BF: He could have had a championship victory, but tonight, he seemed more intent on injuring Stevens.
Bob: Simpkins. Who knows what goes on in his mind. BF: Vengeance Bob, vengeance. MR: 7 MD: 10.14 Simpkins by countout WORLD WAR TITLE MATCH LORD BYRON Vs. SETH BLITZSPEAR Byron totally had Seth in this match, working over his arms and necks. Seth was no way near his normal self, and found himself consistently in submission holds. But he wouldn`t give in, so Byron reverted to high impact moves such as neckbreakers, suplexes and the like. After several very near three counts, Jerry Gordon came to ringside, and grabbed the mic as Seth continued to be pulled around like a doll by Byron.
JG: Seth....you`re pathetic...you`re washed up. Just look at yourself. It just proves you were nothing without me. You may have been worthy of being a champ once upon a time, but no more. You couldn`t even beat Suna Gib.
Hope you have time to fit some of these in... general tactics (Goon throws the mic down and leaves) Bob: What business has Gordon got coming down to ringside like that? BF: He`s in the business of humiliating Seth, that`s what! Bob: Seth...elbows his way out of a sleeper...off the ropes...clothesline..Byron down...Byron up...clothesline again...Byron down.
Seth has suddenly reignited. The fans are going ballistic, just as Seth is. _THIS_ is Seth Blitzspear.
BF: Quickly, take a look at the aisle.... Bob: The Punisher...the 7`4" Punisher has made his way down the aisle and is confronting Jerry Gordon!
BF: Why? Bob: I hear he is pledging himself to Seth Blitzspear and the defeat of
Ground Zero. We`ll come back to that, because in the ring, Seth Blitzspear has Lord Byron in a military press and he drops him hard. Off the ropes...bodysplash. Now he throws Byron into the ropes...FLYING TIME!
Byron is out of the ring and out cold. Now Seth leaves the ring and he chases after Gordon who has been chased away by The Punisher.
BF: This has been mayhem. Bob: Tox has Pat and Seth backstage. [Tox] Pat Seth, you had the title in your hands.. youthe title you covet so much.. what went on [Pat] Tox, Seth has been getting trained by an old .. friend. Someone who has tried to refire Seth, but so far failed... Now what Jerry said has re-sparked it.. Seth will not accept any belts or title matches until he has destroyed Decan, Gordan and Bane. Starting at CoW. where our trainer will aid Seth, from within the ring!
Bob: Woah, a big announcement there. Not much of a suprise, but it looks as though the old Seth may be back.
BF: Well, he may be, but he`s still stupid. He had Byron beat, he could have had the World title, but he ran after Gordon.
Bob: He`ll be pleased to have the 7`4" Punisher by his side. BF: Slowly but surely people are siding with Seth - Punisher, Doom, Stevens...but they`re not going to stop Ground Zero from totally dominating. Bob: I hear Stevens will be fit for next week, but Kalifornia Heat will be out for two weeks following the injuries received earlier this evening.
They will not be participating at WAR!STARS. BF: Lost Boys, and the British Empire, have caused The Heat a loss of chance for glory. They won`t be happy.
Bob: And Adouro looks a prospect and a half. And Doom got beat tonight for the first time. But the big news still is the double count-out between
Seth & Byron. Ladies and gentleman we`ll be back on Thursday with WAR!CORE featuring a gigantic match between Decan & DeatBringer for the War!Lord title. Until then, for Bullfrog, this is Avocado Bob saying goodnight and
