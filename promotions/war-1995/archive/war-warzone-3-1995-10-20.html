WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WarZone 3

20 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm afraid you're all going to have to excuse me today. This is going to be a well below average card because I've got a lot of work to do and I've just done the RSPWF top 75 thing (see it on the newsgroup) so I haven't got too much time. Service will be resumed to normal. And a reminder to all that, since War! has expanded so much, the more significant your match, the more the amount of reporting/commentary. So start getting your matches hyped up now!
WRESTLING UNLIMITED PRESENTS............... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION Presenting WarZone 3 Live from the Warena, Berlin, Germany With your so-so hosts, "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies and gentlemen, to WAR! wrestling, live from the Warena in Berlin, Germany. Tonight we shall see the first round of the battles for the International War Singles and Tag-Championships.
Bfrog: Well, they've been a long time coming, but they're finally here. And so are the new influx of wrestlers we've been waiting for.
And what a mean looking bunch they are...just the way I like them. Keep your eye out for Tonnage. He's Seven Foot tall and 565lbs. IF that doesn't intimidate Jerry Gordon , nothing will.
Bob: How about Reverand J.R. Death, another bigman. 7'6, 470lbs. He must be the biggest competitor in war, and by all accounts, he's going to be a problem for Seth Blitzspear, who hasn't won since he's been back in War.
Bfrog: What you've failed to mention is that with me being vice-president, I've managed to bring in some of the best talent around. Most notably the current CWWW North American & Internet
Champion Sid"StrayCat" Starr. Bob: Ah, yes, Mr.Arrogance himself. Well, we'll see how well he does when he faces The Mandarin. "The Master" Akira Hashimata had to pull out due to a leg-strain, so StrayCat, the high flying man the fans don't like is to replace him.
Bfrog: It may be fate. It may be time for StrayCat to get another belt under his..er...belt.
Bob: And we musn't forget the tag-team matches either. Divine Alliance are backand they want their titles back. Will the Chaos Knights stop them? Will Bombsquad not get injured for once? And how reslistic is it for the Spirits of 76 to expect to win a title.
We'll know after tonight. Lets get it underway. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The numbers in brackets represent percentage of domination in the match) International War Singles Championship. Round 1 Booster Dunne (62) Vs. Star Spangled Butt Kicker (38) Dunne just completely dominates this match. SSBK is a large man at
6`3 and 345lbs, but Dunne, with Randy Blake at ringside, manages to keep the match at his own pace. Then, about five minutes into the match...
Bob: SSBK's manager, Doug, is shouting support to him...Blake doesn't like it...and he's just nailed Doug from behind with a chair..SSBK sees whats happened...he's not happy...but Dunne catches him from behind wih a knee tothe back...then a neck breaker...Dunne..top rope..flying elbow drop..and theres the three. Booster Dunne proceeds to the next round. Oh, and look, there's the Spirits of 76 coming to ringside.
Americus picks up the mic and says to the fallen SSBK "Star Spangled Butt Licker, you pitiful excuse for an American. You are an example of everything wrong with my great country. Look at you crying like a baby, where is your pride? A fat, slob that is too lazy to work for a living. You are an embarassment to everything good in America, just like those Aderondac Brothers!"
They then spit on SSBK and leave. Match end: 5.32 Match rating: 6/10 SETH BLITZSPEAR(27) Vs. REVERAND J.R.DEATH (73) The Reverand is totally dominant, and the fans still aren't behind Seth BLitzspear, the former World War! champion. The match is over quickly when The Reverand makes Seth submit to a torture rack.
Matchend: 4.37 Match rating 6/10 OG GARY TRENT (42) Vs. "FRENCH FURY" JEAN DE MORT (58) Trent initially gives the newcomer from VWF something to worry about, nearly scoring a pin in the first minute with a cradle suplex.
French Fury recovered, responded, and at the seven minute mark of this well fought match, used his own "Paris Drop" cradle suplex to get the win over OG Gary Trent
Match Rating: 7/10 "EUROPEAN HAVOC" JEAN VAN BASTEN (48) Vs. RUMBLEFISH (52) Van Basten, so muscular and powerful, was able to brawl back well against Rumblefish. It was Rumblefish's willingness to bend the rules that gave him the edge over Van Basten. Eye gouges and face rakes whenever Van Basten got any momentum served to allow
Rumblefish to keep on pounding. He eventually decided it was Rumbleation time, but Van Basten was feigning and quickly got up and superplexed Rumblefish from the top rope to get the pin at the 9.18 mark.
Match Rating: 5/10 "THE MANDARIN" (54) Vs. SID "STRAYCAT" STARR (46) The match was fast, furious and exciting. Each wrestler gave his all in an amazing display of scientific skills, but eventually it turned into a shameless brawl. The Mandarin got the better of Starr in the brawl, but when Starr managed to reverse an Irishwhip, he executed his "StrayCat Killer Kick" - a running dropkick - which was enough to get the pin at the Seven minute mark. Match Rating: 8/10
JOEY THE CLOWN (50) Vs. (NICE MR.) NEXUS The match never bacame anything more than a brawl really, but it was
Clown who got the victory when Nexus was disqualified for trying to use brass-knuckles. Match end: 4.59 Match Rating 3/10.
TONNAGE Vs. JERRY "GOON" GORDON Bfrog: Tonight we see the end of Jerry Gordans career. It's going to be Jerry "flat" Gordon after tonight. He's about eight inches shorter and 300lbs lighter than Tonnage. There ain't no way that
Gordon is going to win. Bob: You know I hate agreeing with you, but 'm afraid I'm going to have to. Gordon is just being flung about here.
Tonnage...slam..splash..and another...and another...Gordon must be out of it...he's coughing up blood...within the first minute...he must have internal injuries...powerslam...referee, stop this match...vicious whip intothe corner...look at the blood on the mat...Tonnage coming in with an Avalanche...Goon moves...Tonnage has winded himself...Gordon...top rope..flying bulldog...and he's done it...he's pinned tonnage...just within two minutes...this is amazing...but Tonnage isn't happy..Little Louie, Tonnage's manager, has delivered a devastating chop to the throat..the medics are tring to help, but Louie is keeping them away with a steel chair...and Tonnage has delivered five consecutive splashes. Gordon could be out of wresltling permanently after this....
Match rating 7/10 BRUTUS "SIDEWINDER" NOIR(63) Vs. KID ENIGMA(37) As much as Enigma tried to keep the match technical, the now 280lb Noirs innate ability to brawl was the theme of the match and with the aid of the
"Marseille Strangler" it was enough to get the pin at 6.20.
Match rating 5/10 INTERNATIONAL WAR TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP TOURNEY ROUND 1 DIVINE ALLIANCE (53) Vs. CHAOS KNIGHTS (37) The Divine Alliance showed no signs of ring-rust, and Zeus over-bearing power was enough to swing the match, getting the pin over Mayhem following a Thunderpress at 8.35
Match Rating 6/10 CELTIC CONNECTION (39) Vs. SPIRITS OF 76 (61) The Spirits had the match going all their own way, especially Americus who put on a supreme performance against a wary Celtic Connection. However, in the fifteenth minute, The Green Man executed a thunderous piledriver on Independence. Greenman thought it was all over, went for the pin, but Independence got his foot on the rope.
Green Man tagged Shamrock Hero who came running in, only to be met by a clothesline from Americus. With that, he got the pin.
Match Rating: 6/10 HIGH VOLTAGE (45) Vs. APOCALYPSE (55) A fairly fought, clean ,match, but Chaos's "Psychoslam" - a face first powerslam - was the move that got Apocalypse the victory at
19.35. Match rating 8/10 ADERONDAC BROTHERS (57)Vs. HOT STUFF (33) The Aderondac Brothers were pumped and ready to go in this match, levelling the much smaller Hot Stuff at every oppurtunity with decisive powermoves. Their was no doubt aboutthe result. Bombsquad with the A-bomb on inferno.
Match end: 5.29 Match rating 6/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ In the other mathces, Ghetto Boyz defended their titles successfully against Canadian Carnage whilst Painkiller was counted out against
