SAW 8 Cntd... ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~~~ “THE CARDINAL” ANDREA MANCINI (2) w/o Acolytes Vs. STEIN “ICE” BERG (10) Mancini turned up without his Acolytes. No-one knew the reason why, but they knew that without them Mancini had no chance against the ex-world War! Champ
“Ice”. The match lasted 30 seconds. A whip to the ropes reversed, a boot to the face and a chokeslam followed by a splash finished Mancini off quickly.

[Wendy] Whoever. **fade out** ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~ LOST BOYS w/Wendy (9) Vs. GHETTO BOYZ (9) A totally cracking match. Wendy was at ringside wearing her “Terrible” Terry James T-Shirt (available via telesales for only $45), blue jeans like they`d been spray painted on, and some very high heel shoes with an
“Apocalypse Now” baseball cap. Great match commentary, huh? Figured deatail was important. The Losts Boys came in to some punishment from BDV to begin with, and it looked like it was all over when BDV pinned LB1 following a backbody drop.
Eventually FCF came in and the Lost Boys managed to get a go at the Ghetto
Boyz after Wendy distracted FCF. LB1 worked over the knee with knee breakers
and elbow drops on the knee. LB2 tried to get a sharpshooter on, but FCF kicked out. He tried to tag BDV back in, but Wendy held on to his leg. The ref went to complain at Wendy, and as he did, Wendy passed LB 1&2 brass-knuckles, and they levelled BDV and FCF for the victory.
MR - * * * ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~ LORD BYRON w/ King Benny Vs. “THE ECONOMIST” RANDY BLAKE w/Ms.Paula Many see Blake getting old. Many see Booster Dunne as an heir to this great wrestlers throne. But a new line of thinkers see it as perhaps the excellent tech-wrestler, Lord Byron. Byron the second coming of Blake? Possibly. In the match, Byron was all the things Blake is. Focused, determined, methodical, not giving an inch.
Blake, typically, was the same. The fans witnessed a superb match as the old king of tech-wrestling and the young pretender to the throne locked up.
Byron worked on the legs of Blake after having worn him down with a chokehold that managed to fool the referee. Blake wouldn`t give in. Blake fought back. Blake almost got Byron with a backslide. We join the commentary...
Bob - Blake with an abdominal stretch. Byron reverses. Blake gets the rope. Byron lets go. Byron with a slam. Snapmare! Now he gets in a sleeperhold with Blake down.
Rick - Nowhere for Blake to go. Bob - Blake delivers a sneaky rake to the eyes. He gets up and boots Byron in his pretty face. He doesn`t like that. He charges. Blake throws him to the corner...follows through...Byron gets his foot up. Blake goes down.
Byron with the cover.... 1 2 Bob - Foot on the ropes. Byron with a DDT now. Another cover. 1 2 Bob - Blake kicks out again. Byron goes for a cardleplex. Blake blocks. Reverses. Cradleplex. Perfectly done! 1 2 Bob - 3! No! Almost! That was close. Byron shakes it off. Locks up with
Blake. Gets him in position for a german suplex...and he does it into a pin.
I haven`t seen that since....well, since Blake last did it a long time ago. Cover! 1 2 Bob - 3! And Lord Byron wins it. He`s ecstatic about it. A good win for him, but Blake is dissatisfied with his performance. Rick - Just watch out for this hot new blue blood. He`s going places. MR - * * * * * ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~~~ DEATHJESTER (8) Vs. RICK SHOWBOAT (7) NON-TITLE MATCH A non-title bout, and it showed. DJ was below par, but Rick Showboat was still trying his best to prove he could beat a War! champion. His best chance came when DJ rammed himself into a post when trying to shouldercharge
RS in the corner, but RS moved yet failed to take advantage. DJ recovered and used his ‘DeathBounce’ to defeat Rick Showboat, who, to all intents and purposes, put on a promising display.
MR - * * * ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~ CROCMASTER (7) Vs. THE AMERICAN PATRIOT (8) FOR THE WAR!LORD TITLE T.A.P raised his game for this match. He was not only defending his interfed title but he was fighting for the War!Lord title. TAP Has hungered for a War!
belt for a long time. Within the first five minutes, it became clear that tonight was going to be his night.
Rick - CrocMaster of the BrotherHood is totally crushing The American Geek. Ow! What a roundhouse kick by CrocMaster! Bob - Where did he learn to do that? Rick - In the swamps of course. He calls it the “Tail-lash”. Bob - Well, it has stunned Patriot. He`s down. CrocMaster covers. 1 2 Bob - 3 almost, but not quite. CrocMaster goes for a CrocBomb. 1 2 Bob - And again, Patriot survives. It shows you the resilience he has. CrocMaster is getting a bit angry now. Airplane spin by CrocMaster now! And that is no mean feat on a guy like Patriot. Patriot giddy. CrocMaster
charges him...but Patriot catches him into a hiptoss. CrocMaster gets up, Patriot comes off the ropes.....PATRIOT MISSILE! CrocMaster goes down. He hooks the leg. 1 2 Bob - 3! Oh, almost. not quite a three. CrocMaster shows stamina. Patriot gets up again, he`s really trying to put CorcMaster away. What a suplex!
What an elbow drop! Antoher cover! 1 2 Bob: CrocMaster still has some energy left in him. Patriot pulls CrocMaster up, CrocMaster grabs TAP`s legs....he pulls TAP over, he`s got TAP prone...catapult into the corner...TAP hit his head on the corner post. He staggers back...CrocMaster with a clothesline from behind...he goes for the
Crocbomb...TAP elevates him over the ropes....CrocMaster outside...TAP inside...the ref is counting....and CrocMaster is counted out.
Rick: The American Patriot won`t be happy with that, but as you heard earlier, he wants the big prize anyhow. If he`d given 100% this match, he may have just snuck it.
MR - * * * ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~ WORLD WAR! TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH ADERONDAC BROTHERS (9) Vs. APOCALYPSE (8) Chaos crushed Bladerunner, Mayhem rocked Bombsquad. Mayhems antics proved a difficult package for the Ad.Bros, but they used their experience and started to fight back. Chaos came back in and started to control the match until
Bombsquad got his act together and nearly finished the match with a devastating bigfoot on Chaos.
Wendy, manager of the Lost Boys came to ring and passed them brass knuckles into the ring. They didn`t pick them up, but the ref cited this as interference on behalf of Apocalypse, and the Ad.Bros won by DQ.
Wendy started having a go at the Ad.Bros, and they turned on her, but Lost
Boys 1 & 2 came to ringside with baseball bats and started assaulting the
Ad.Bros. It turned into a massive brawl between Ad.Bros and Lost Boys, and eventually they were seperated.
MR - * * * ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~~~ INTERNATIONAL WAR! CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH BOOSTER DUNNE (10) Vs. SID “STRAYCAT” STARR (9) This was a dazzling encounter. Starr was at his best in this high paced match up which was laced with some calming and perfect technical moves. Starr showed off some new moves...on Ms.Paula, and she was almost charmed, but his flirting almost cost him the match, because Dunne kneed him to the outside, but to be fair, helped him back in. Starr showed no such decorum and threw
Dunne out at first oppurtunity and went for a flying ax-handle but Dunne punched Starr in the gut midway down. Dunne got back in the ring and so did
Starr. Dunne then brought the match to his pace and slowly mastered Starr and the match. Dunne knew that he had won five minutes before he finished
Starr off with his Booston Crab. Another exciting War! match and another demonstration of Dunnes maturing into one of the premier wrestlers in War!
However, while Sid Starr was on the way back to the dressing room, Rick
Showboat attack him from behind and executed a piledriver on Sid Starr on the concrete floor, then smashed his head against the wall. Starr had to go for treatment.
