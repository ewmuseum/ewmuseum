------------------------------------------------ WAR! FEDERATION proudly presents WAR!ZONE 5 with your hosts
Avocado Bob & Dr.Nathaniel Darkness Live from the War!datorium --------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: : Ladies & gentlemen, welcome one and welcome all to War!Zone 5. This is the twelfth War! card and it is graced by many debuts & 21 matches. Debuting today are two new tag-teams - The Nightbreed (Jacob Demonchilde & Grizzly) & The StormLords (Thunder & Lightening). We`ve also got a legend wrestler join War! in the shape of that ol` cowboy Brewster Cogburn (the first winner of the SlickRic tournament). His style of music will differ greatly from that of Classico`s, his opponent tonight. Another debutant match today is..
Dr.Nathaniel Darkness: Are you going to let me get a word in? Avocado Bob: I was just introducing the card. Doc: Did you ever think that I would like to do it. Bob: Not really. But go ahead. Doc: Okay. Right. Well, er...oh, where`s the prompter..hmm..aha..yes, "The
Economist" Randy Blake faces the ridiculous "Joey the Clown".
Bob: We all know what happened to a certain federation when a clown entered there. Doc: Well, this one might have some talent, you never know. Speaking of talent, they don`t come much more talented than the man from NCW (as well as
China) The Mandarin. That`ll be a juicy match. I`ve seen him before and despite his small size he can defeat just about anyone. Also debuting tonight will be "The Highlander" Angus McBaine. I`ve finished now Bob. Get that
Avocado out of your nose and tell the people about what`s been happening. Bob (removing Avocado from nose): Well, soem late developments have obviously meant a change in the card. We`ve seen how the debut matches have altered the card, but the card had already been drastically changed at War!Report 5 with Seth Blitzspear taking The Brander to a hospital and used The Brander as material for skingrafts to get rid of his scars - at least that`s the story on the street. A series of events followed, resulting in a challenge between the
Seth Blitzspear and the MoW. Seth will defend his belt against the five active members of MoW..
Doc: Have you ever realised how MoW looks the same upside down. Bob: Except for the o. Doc: Oh...yeah. Bob: Are you feeling okay? Doc: Yeah...fine I suppose. Look, just carry on will you? Bob: The MoW will all fight Seth Blitzspear for the worldwar title tonight, and if they don`t beat him, they will disband.
Doc: It`s a risk they`re taking because they know they`ll do it. Bob: Well, Seth beat about six guys at Nuclear War - one after the other. I think he can do it. Oh, I`ve got to stop there...the first match is about to get underway.... ---------------------------------------------------------- Joey the Clown Vs. "The Economist" Randy Blake Joey outsizes Blake, but Blakes skillful planning keeps the highhflying Joey on the mat, and using the minimum of moves to weaken Joey, Blake gets the pin with his brainbuster/cradleplex combination at 3.47
---------------------------------------------------------- Classico Vs. Brewster Cogburn Cogbrun, the veteran, outbrawls the rapidly worsening Classico in a slugfest. His ruggedness and resistance prove enough to get a pin at 5.22 ------------------------------------------------------------- The Nightbreed Vs. The StormLords Divine Alliance watching at ringside Highlights: Bob: And there is Thunder with a piledriver on Jacob Demonchilde...He goes for the pin..Grizzly interferes at the 2 count..and Lightning has knocked the
7`6" grizzly over over with his "Lightening bolt" top-rope dropkick..it`s chaos in the ring...Thunder holds Demonchilde up for Lightening`s dropkick...here comes Lightening...Demonchilde moves..Thunders knocked down...Demonchilde gets the 3 count. Matchend 14.23
---------------------------------------------------------------- Fabrice "lightening Claws" Duvall Vs. The Mandarin Highlights: Bob: Mandarin just isn`t coping with Duvalls high flying antics...theres another flying clothesline....is Duvall about to do a moonsault...yes he is...and he`s connected..he`s goes for the pin..it`s a 2 count..he goes for the figure four leglock...but Mandarin has grabbed the French high flyer....hes wrapped him up into a small package....the Mandarin has won!
Doc: A great victory for short-ass`s around the world. Matchend: 7.02 ------------------------------------------------------------------- BombSquad Vs. "The Highlander" Angus McBain Highlights: Bob: Pretty impressive debut for the Scotsman..he throws Bombsquad to the ropes..but Bombsquad telegraphs the move...he kicks McBain in the gut..McBain bends over...ohhhhhh and Bombsquads set McBain up for the A-Bomb..and there it is..the inverted powerbomb into a boston crab..what a devastating move and what an impressive win.
Doc: I don`t think anyone can recover form that move. Matchend: 10.05 -------------------------------------------------------------- "Booster" Dunne Vs. Nexus Nexus, coughing throughout the match seems to get sympathy from the handsome "Booster" Dunne, but Nexus gives none in return, keeping Dunne locked in an abdominal stretch for 4 minutes. Dunne eventually escapes and the match spills outside where both men are counted out at 8.32
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ClanDemon Vs. Light & Darkness With Divine Alliance watching Highlights: Doc: All this thunder & lightening in War! I`m surprised it hasnt rained. Bob: Keep your eyes on the match. Doc: Look, ClanDemon are about to set Darkness up for apiledriver off the top rope... Bob: But Darkness has managed to get his legs round IDW`s neck - oh my God, he`s flipped him off...and Darkness has sprung onto the turnbuckle...and there`s a flying sunset flip onto DeadPunk...and thats a victory for L&D at
8.50 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Johnny Thunder Vs. DeathThreat w/WarMaster Highlights: Bob: I see WarMaster is looking after DeathThreats gun.....And DeathThreat has just slammed Thunder to the outside...DeathThreatis distracting the referee...
Doc:...and WarMaster is beating Thunder on the head with the butt of DT`s gun. hahahahaa. He ain`t waking up..and WarMaster is kind enough to throw
Thunder back into the ring. Bob: And there is DeathThreats special move - "The Finisher" Bulldog. And that is the end for Thunder, the ex-international war champion. Is there no justice inthis sport?
Matchend: 4.40 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Masters of War (The Fire & BadTimes Billy) Vs. The Youngbloods (Shaft & Badrock) with Kang, Divine Alliance, Trailboss Mike Dallas, Mr.Fixit & Bombsquad watching Highlights: Doc: What is Kang doing at ringside? Bob: It isnt just Mr.Kang, there is Trailboss & Mr.Fixit as well...from FWA I think...and Bombsquad is at ringside as well.
Doc: This just isn`t fair on the Masters of War. They`re outnumbered. Bob: And the match is underway...hey, BadTimes better stop shouting at Kang...oh..too late..Shaft has rolled BadTimes up...and its a 3 count..he pinned BadTimes just 10 seconds into the match...and I think that`s
Youngbloods first win..and everybody is celebrating in the ring..Look at this, it`s turned into a melee...the Masters of War have entered the fray...it`s chaos in the ring..the other Masters of War have cleared the ring of
Bombsquad, Trailboss, Mr.Fixit , The Youngbloods & The Divine Alliance with baseball bats...they`re keeping them out as well..oh my God...Bludgeon has just Bludgeonbombed Mr.Kang..but the others get back in the ring..the Masters of War run off..
------------------------------------------------------------------- Mountain Top Madman Vs. Bludgeon Bludgeon doesn`t show, MTM wins by default. -------------------------------------------------------------------- Brutus Noir Vs. XL-9 Brutus Noir, a tough brawler, is outbrawled by the methodical XL-9, the impervious XL-9. But Noir manages to apply "The Marseille Strangler" from which no one returns...a pin victory for "The Sidewinder" at 7.35
------------------------------------------------------------------ Freedom Title Championship Match The Maltese Falcon Vs. InnerCity Firmer ICF starts with some devastating clotheslined, but The Falcon gains momentum and uses his highflying-hard hitting moves to gain an advantage.
Late in the match, with Falcon perched and ready to get the victory with his Blackbird splash, DeathThreat enters the ring, assaulting Falcon. ICF gets DQ`d at 9.47. Seth Blitzspear runs to Falcons rescues but Falcon had cleared
DeathThreat off by then.
---------------------------------------------------------------- WorldWar Tag TeamChampionship The Divine Alliance (Amon Ra & Zeus) Vs. The BloodStreet Brother Highlights: Doc: Bob, five minutes have gone and the BSB`s haven`t drawn blood yet. Bob: Probably because Zeus is overpowering them...look there is another suplex...no it isn`t...Chad shifts his weight...he falls onto Zeus..but Zeus kicks out...Marlon runs in...He clotheslines Marlon to the floor...He picks
Chad up for the "Thunderpress"...oooohhhhh..and Zeus has thrown Chad onto Marlon...and he`s pinned them both....another impressive title defense by the
Divine Alliance.
MatchEnd: 5.59 ------------------------------------------------------ International War Championship "The Amercian" Butch Williams Vs. "Trailboss" Mike Dallas Highlights: Bob: And Trailboss has jsut reversed a piledriver...Butch dodges the elbow drop..Butch slams Dallas..and another slam..and another slam..3 big slams on one big guy..and now Butch is trying for the "Superpower" Slam...and he`s got
Dallas across his shoulders...but Dallas grabs the ropes and blocks the move...he`s turned it into a guerilla press...but Williams moves out of it..he`s slid onto Dallas` back...and he`s turned it into a stationery sunsetflip...he`s got the victory...what a truly champion performance by Williams.
Matchend: 13.24 ---------------------------------------------------------------------- World War championship Match 1 Seth Blitzspear w/ Johnny Thunder Vs. 20,000 volts Match Highlights: Bob: It`s nice of Johnny Thunder to be in Seth`s corner to give him support. Doc: And that support seems to have worked well...Seth is like a madman in there Bob: Can you blame him..his belts on the line...and he`s disposed of 20,000 volts already with a side-slam at 3.34.
Doc: Next! Bob: And it`s BadTimes Billy Doc: And Billy isn`t getting momnet to rest. Seth is...Blitzing him with Forearms...BadTimes is dazed...and badTimes is out of there...the second time today he has lost in under a minute..and you can see WarMaster is angry with him.
Next Match Vs. The Fire Seth again holds his own but shows signs of tiredness. A powerslam at 8.44 is enough for the win though.
Seth Blitzspear Vs. DeathThreat Doc: And as you can see, DeathThreat has slowed down the whole pace of the game..he`s been tiring Seth with chinlocks and sleeperholds and now this
Boston Crab Bob: But Seth has managed to get to the ropes. DeathThreat has to break the hold...but he isn`t..he`s been DQ`d but he isn`t letting go...thank God Johnny
Thunder is there to clothesline DeathThreat off. But now it`s time for Seth`s toughest challenge...BLUDGEON. I don`t think Seth can stand, let alone wrestle...his knee seems to have given way.
Doc: And straight away Bludgeon is working on that knee...Seth is in agony ( Seth rallies with the cheers of the crowd and makes a comeback ) Bob: What is Seth doing? Why is he going for a figurefour leglock? This is madness...he can`t put up with that kind of pain..not the state his knee is in......
(Later) Doc: Just how long have they both been in this figure four leglock? Bob: I make it five minutes now...they can`t keep it up for much longer Doc: But they both have to. If Bludgeon loses, the Masters of War must disband. If Seth loses, there goes his hard fought belt.
Bob:....the referee is talking to them both...and there`s the bell. Now what`s happened? The ring announcer is about to speak.....
Ring Announcer: And the result of this match at 23.51 is..................... .....a double submission. Both men submit! ----------------------------------------------------------------- Bob: Can you beleive that? Doc: Quite frankly, no. I`ve never seen anything quite like it before. It`s a fair result I suppose, but I take my hat off to Seth. Time & time again he has proved he`s a champion. This man, and I hate to say it, is a star.
Bob: Doc, I think you`ve truly shocked me with that comment. But at the end of the day, nothings really changed. See you all at CivilWar 1 and then at
"InsanitySiege", the first FORTRESS supercard.
