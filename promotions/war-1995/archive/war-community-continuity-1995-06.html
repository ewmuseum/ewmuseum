WAR! Wrestling Federation — Board

“Let's keep it going”

29 June - 4 July 1995 (five posts)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Five community replies from rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, 29 June - 4 July 1995, after WAR!'s own founder went quiet for a stretch mid-run. Quoted material repeating an earlier post in this same thread has been trimmed rather than re-served twice; each poster's own new remarks stand in full.
---------------------------------------------------------------------- [29 Jun 1995 — [name withheld]] Hi, Well, Jake is gone for a while, but that doesn't neccessarily mean that WAR! should fall by the wayside. As the handler of the current International WAR! champion and also the newly-crowned FWAR! interfederational champion, I'm certainly interested in defending my belts!
Any suck-- er, takers? Having run a fed for a few months, I can offer some advice and perhaps even help out a bit.
— [name withheld], Digital Arcana ([detail withheld] / [detail withheld] fax) ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [30 Jun 1995 — [name withheld]] Well... The FWAR! Universal Championship will be fought like a normal belt in the FWA (since Cogburn joined) so whoever decides to take over
WAR!, please consult with me so we can talk about the interfed belt and possible turning it into the FWAR!-UCWA Universal Belt ([name withheld]'s league). At the end I lost contact with Jake and didn't know the belt exchanged hands until last week. If I was in WAR!, I'd offer to help keep it going, but since I'm not =(... Unless... Naa... Nevermind.
— [name withheld] ([detail withheld]) ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [30 Jun 1995 — "Pat"] Hey, Ok....who is the FWAR! Champ?? According to the FWA it's me, according to WAR! it's him...so.....WHO IS IT!?
Pat ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [30 Jun 1995 — [name withheld]] It's Brewster Cogburn. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [30 Jun 1995 — "Stu"] As the Handler of the World War Title holder I'd be on for keeping it alive!! Stu. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [2 Jul 1995 — [name withheld]] Hey! What about "the Economist" Randy Blake? He beat Brewster Cogburn with a sleeper at the last WAR card, Casualties of WAR! All titles were on the line in the Survivor Series-type matches, meaning Blake captured at least the WAR International title, if not also the FWAR title by beating then-champion Cogburn (who won that title from Big Poppa earlier in the card). Blake later was eliminated by double-countout, so he did not lose these titles. As Jake gave the stipulations for Casualities of WAR! in his
WAR!Zone VII: ">Doc: I`d just like to take this brief moment to give a newsflash about >Casualties of War. ALL War! titles will be on the line. So if one half of >the WorldWar Tag team champions gets pinned by another wrestler, they concede >the belts. The same applies to the singles. >Bob: Well, that`s going to make Casualties of War a lot more interesting..." There you have it. Blake was the last man to hold those titles, at least the WAR International title for sure. He is now competing in the newly- formed WFW...
— [name withheld] ([detail withheld]) ---------------------------------------------------------------------- [4 Jul 1995 — [name withheld]] I agree (reluctantly!) that Randy Blake became the International WAR! Champ when he put Cogburn to sleep in the last card. However, I believe that the FWAR! belt can only be defended across the federations (WAR! and FWA), so it couldn't have been up for grabs.
Somebody offered to take over WAR! I would be glad to answer any questions about how to run the fed.
— [name withheld], Digital Arcana ([detail withheld] / [detail withheld] fax)
