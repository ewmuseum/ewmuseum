WARATHON I — The Anniversary Bash

With 19 matches to write up, I’m sure you lot will understand some reports being brief. Well, here we go.
The opening card of WAR! Season 4 and the card celebrating 1 year of WAR! WRESTLING UNLIMITED presents.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION presenting WARATHON I - THE ANNIVERSARY BASH LIVE FROM THE WARDATORIUM, SOMEWHERE With your deicidal hosts ‘Avocado’ Bob Whitely & Rick ‘Ricky’ Soal ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The camera opens to a man standing on the in Toxic Pit set. He is wearing the
WAR! broadcaster's usuals and a glow-in-the-dark green gasmask. The pit in the middle of the set is spewing out a greenish mist and the sound of something boiling and hissing issues forth from that same pit. Finally the man rips the gasmask off and takes a deep breath of the fumes from the pit, grinning widely.
[Toxic Shock] AAAAAH! Nothin' like the smell of the pit to wake ya up in the morning! Hey, everyone out there? Enjoying the show so far?
The Crowd pops bigtime. [Toxic] Weeeell? Aren't ya glad WAR! is back on track? HUH? Even BIGGER pop. [Toxic] Aaaaaw. Ya really care about us. It's enough to bring a tear to ol' Toxic's eye. It really is. Oh, the overwhelming emotions.....
Toxic grins slightly and continues. [Toxic] Well, ya showed us how ya feel with this big turnout today. However, my guest here in the pit now is a man I've never seen display any kind of emotion.
Give a big welcome to the current WORLD WAR! CHAMPION. The man who will face the terror that is Tonnage for that very title tonight..... STEIN... ICE....
BERG! The crowd pops big again as Berg walks onto the set with his manager, James
North. Berg is a huge caucasian man. He stands over seven feet tall and has a massive bodybuilder's physique. His hair is white and cut in a short, military crewcut style. Across his massive chest the word ICECOLD is tattooed in big, black gothic letters. He is wearing his white wrestling trunks and white boots, and the expression on his face is as emotionless as ever. He also carries the World WAR! Championship belt over his shoulder. Next to Berg is his manager, James North. North is a distinguished and robust looking gentleman in his fifties. He is wearing a white suit.
[Toxic] Welcome to the pit, champ. Berg just nods. [Toxic] Okay, let's get down to business, shall we? Tonight you face once again a man feared all over WAR!.... Tonnage. And it's not the first time, is it. Hey,
Mr. Director, let's roll the tape.

[Toxic] Okay, the first time you two met it looked like you weren't really prepared for the big man....
The screen changes to show clips of Tonnage and Berg's first meeting, with
Berg really never putting up a meaningfull offense and Tonnage getting the 3 count.
[Toxic] Now, what happened there? [Berg] One thing happened there. I wasn't prepared for how overwhelming Tonnage's weight really is. It took me by surprise, and I never recovered from the initial onslaught after that, so he won.
[Toxic] I see. Well, you met again some time ago, and the outcome was NOT the same. The screen changes to show the second meeting, with Berg slamming Tonnage and getting the three count.
[Toxic] I take it you took into account Tonnage's great bulk this time? [Berg] Yes. I did, although my back was sore for days after the match. [Toxic] Which brings us to tonight. Third time. Do you feel ready to take on the huge monster that is Tonnage? And what about the Horsem... I mean Armageddon?
[Berg] I am ready. I am always ready. Fighting someone like Tonnage will always be difficult....even dangerous.... That does not matter. I have a goal in WAR! A goal which consists of disposing of all the scum who may think they can break the rules at will. Tonnage is just one of many, but he is one of the most important links in the chain. If he can be broken then that shows the rest that so can they.... As for Armageddon. They do not worry me. I think Tonnage may be trying to prove a point whenever he faces me. And to prove that point he has to beat me by himself. No, I do not worry about the rest of Armageddon showing their faces... And even if they do I still have WAR!Force to take care of them while I am busy in the ring.
[Toxic] Well, looks like you have the champ ready, Mr. North. Are you as confident as he seems? [North] Oh, most certainly. Tonnage, you may think Ice to be afraid of you since he isn't bragging about how he's going to show everyone that he's the baddest, nastiest, most horrible wrestler in WAR! by destroying you... Well, the reason for that is very simple. Only a fool brags, and Ice is no fool.
[Toxic] Well, thanks for coming into the pit guys, but I'm afraid we're outta time, so without any further ado.. Let's get ON with the SHOW!
The fumes from the pit grow thicker and soon obscures everyone on the set before the camera cuts to Bob & Rick...

Finally, Tonnage gets his big chance to beat Stein “Ice” Berg for the World War Championship. Bob: The match has been a long time coming. Match Rating: * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BRUTUS NOIR (6) Vs. THE AMERICAN PATRIOT (9) Noir had recently renounced his Sidewinder title, and that element of his psyche seemed to be lacking certainly. The American Patriot showed exactly what he was made of and why he is a fast rising star in the world of wrestling by dominating the match from start to finish, wearing Noir down,and putting Noir down with not one but three Patriot Missiles after Noir refused to submit to a Revolution Sharpshooter, clearly revenge for when Noir cost
Patriot a title shot. Interesting point of note was Johnny “The Sidewinder”
Missiles appearance at ringside.
Match Rating: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “HOLLYWOOD” JOHNNY DREAMER (7) Vs. “SUPERSTAR” GOLDEN PAGE This match was above average, but not outstanding. Dreamer and Page were evenly match and remained so until Dreamer caught Page with a superb dropkick. The match was Dreamers from there, and he took it, making
Superstar submit to a figure-four leglock. Match Rating: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ GENERAL LEO (6) Vs. SABIN FIGARO (6) The match was short. Leo attacked Figaro with a rare ferocity, Figaro fought back almost equally, but not enough. the match went outside the ring, down the aisle and into the car park, out of the carpark, down the highway, into the mall, into the carpark of the mall, into the back of a family saloon, back to the family house, into their living room (which was promptly smashed up) and eventually they stopped, and the police arrested them, but were released the next day without charge. The match was a double count-out by the way. A lumberjack match has been agreed between the two to take place at the next card. These guys need to be kept in the ring!
(After) Match Rating: * * * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ THEATRE OF PAIN (9) Vs. STARSMASHERS (9) This was a high quality tag team match, and it entertained the fans immensely. TOP were the slightly larger side, but not by much. Appollo
Friday was on the end of a lot of punishment from TOP, but it almost worked to their advantage, because Henry Rome came in quite fresh and turned the battle. Conquer, however, managed to execute a perfect moonsault on Friday when he tagged back in, and it got TOP the victory.
MR: * * * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ APOCALYPSE (8) Vs. THE ADERONDAC BROTHERS (8) Mayhem was the odd-one out in this match, being the smallest by over a foot and under six feet tall. It played an important role in the match result as well because he just couldn`t cope with Bombsquad and BladeRunner. Chaos stood more of a chance, and indeed got the better of both the Aderondac
Brothers. Chaos couldn`t keep up the pace to finsh them off and had to tag in Mayhem who, despite a good start against the Brothers, was subjected to
“The Blade” - a piledriver onto the top rope. Needless to say, Mayhem didn`t get up, and the Brothers got the victory. Chaos cleared the ring afterwards though to help his teammate from further punishment. A good, 20 minute long match.

The fans were being spoiled with a match of the highest quality for the first five minutes. Blake was really suprised that Trent was fighting so well, but he kept his cool, and didn`t give way. Both were inflicting a lot of serious damage, but Trent, after twenty minutes, was showing the edge. Then, just as he was about to have a sure pin on Blake following three consecutive
‘drive-by’ ghostbusters, everyones attention turned toa massively brightlight shining through the murky glass roof of the War!Datorium and a low humming sound. Then, the light and noise vanished. When everyone turned back to the ring, there was a fourth man standing in the ring. ‘ I am SICHS’ said the fourth man. With one punch he knocked Trent out of the ring. He looked about seven foot tall, and 400lbs of pure muscle. Blake tried to get away,
but couldn`t. Sichs sinched in an excruciating Bearhug that started turning Blake blue. Ms.Paula returned with Booster Dunne, but even Booster couldn`t stop the bearhug. Blake passed out, and when he did, he was dropped. The chalky, grey skinned Sichs walked walked away, and nobody got in his way.

Duke went to aid Benny, but CrocMaster got up, caught Duke with a neckbreaker then a CrocBomb, and got the 1-2-3,as well as the War!Lord title. Duke`s first major loss in War! and it was at a devastating price. CrocMaster, leader of the Brotherhood, took away the Rich Boys only title.
