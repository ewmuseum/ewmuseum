WAR-REPORT 2 The "INTERNATIONAL WAR CHAMPIONSHIP" TOURNAMENT Round 1 With Avocado Bob & Toxic Shock Avocado Bob: Welcome Ladies & Gentlemen to the International War Championship tournament. 16 of the best single wrestlers in WAR! today will be pitting their strengths and wits against each other in an amazing contest
ToxicShock: That`s right Bob, 16 top wrestlers in one top event. We`re going to see some superb wrestling tonight, and time is already running short.
Avocado Bob: That`s sadly true Bob, for there is so much action here, that I can`t talk enough! But I would like to say before the start is that we may have, within the next couple of days, have some BIG info on a great new future for this fed. Something BIG could well be happening, and it`s currently top secret.
ToxicShock: If it`s what I think it is, it`s going to change the future of wrestling forever.
Avocado Bob: I don`t think you`re wring there Tox, but something just as important is this tournament tonight, which is getting underway right now.
--------------------------------------------------------- Round one 30 minute time limit one fall Bludgeon Vs. Morgue Bludgeon, powerhouse that he is, brawls with the mighty morgue, and eventually gaining the advantage during the match, piledrives Morgue for a clean pin at 28.34
Massive Man Vs. BadTimes Outclassed, outsized, overpowered, over-rated. BadTimes could not keep up with the Massiveone, and after a rare body splash by the big-guy, BadTimes was leaving the stadium to try and have some good times.
ArchitisMan Vs. Classico Architisman, arguably the holder of WAR!`s wooden spoon, rises to the occasion and matches Classico blow for blow. Using his extra pace, Architis tires
Classico and follows up with the "BackSideBLast" - A flying sunset flip from the top-rope. Classico doesn`t go down, and sits on Architis for a pin, but
Architis manages to reverse it and somehow pins Classico at 21.54. Shock victory
Johnny Thunder Vs. The Fire Thunder`s "rain" of victories continue with a substantial win against The
Fire, following The Fires DQ for using his "Heartburner" on the referee "MAD"
Mike Domin at 6.43 Seth Blitzspear Vs. WarMaster Debut match for the Masters of War manager WarMaster. This old veteran shuddered at the youngsters power,and it was only a matter of time before Seth used his "Flying Time" backdrop to send the part-time wrestler, full time manager into the crowd
FunkyMonkey Vs. Jack "scratch it" McCain Two faces squared off in FunkyMonkeys debut, and Funky proved exceptionallt popular, using arm-drag takedowns, half nelsons,leg scissors, moonsaults and every other high flying move in an impressive display of FunkyMonkeys speed, agility and ring balance. A peoples champion was born with his victory over
Scratch it, and Monkey celebrated with the crowd for a 5minutes after his 5 minute victory
20000 volts Vs. Roc Hardy These fan-favourites, both making up ShockTactics, brawled hard and drew blood, even from fans. Friendship seemed to be lost in their desire for a title, but in the end, both were DQ`d for electrocuting a fan at 7.50. A sad turn of events for a very popular tag-team
DeathThreat Vs. "Sunny" Sam Sefton DeathThreat, with Seth BLitzspear on his hit-list, was determined to make an example of Sefton, and at the end of the day, the rookie couldn`t cope with
DeathThreats amazing execution of his moves, abd was counted out of the ring at 12.20 after DeathThreat copied BLitzspears "Flying time" Move.
------------------------------------------------ Round 2 One Fall 45 minute time limit ------------------------------------------------ Bludgeon Vs. MassiveMan Bludgeon, obviously aware of being outsized and outpowered, sits back for a lot of the match and tries to tire Massiveman. Bludgeon, after regaining some of the advantage, throws Massiveman into the ref "Soft" Tom Chewy.
With the ref knocked out, Masters of War storm the ring, clobber Massive man with a couple of chairs and tables and run off, only to be confronted by the
FourSeasons down the aisle. A brawl ensues, but in the ring, "Soft" Tom Chewy recovers, and with BLudgeon pinning Massiveman, gives the victory to Bludgeon at 12.47
ArchitisMan Vs. Johnny Thunder Architis dominates the battle, with Thunder not really getting a hit. But during the match, he catches Architsman with a suprise leg-lariat. He then throws Architis to the ropes and applies a crucifix pin, gaining the victory at 9.36
Seth Blitzspear Vs. FunkyMonkey FunkyMonkey & Seth both enter to rapturous applause from the fans, but it is
FunkyMonkey who starts winnig the batle, dizzying Seth with his pace. Then suddenly, FunkyMonkey is caught with a lariat by Seth and the match turnbs around. Applying powermove after powermove, Seth sets up Funky to show him what it is really like to fly with his flying time move. But out of nowhere
DeathThreat arrives, clobbers Seth from behind with the butt of his gun while
Bludgeon keeps the ref & FM distracted and runs off. With SB out of it, FM climbs to the top rope and delivers a flying elboy drop, getting a victory at
16.34 DeathThreat gets a bye ------------------------------------- Round Three - The semi-finals 1 fall 60 minute time limit ----------------------------------- Bludgeon Vs. Johnny Thunder Avocado Bob: And where is Johnny Thunder? This is the second time in a week that one of Bludgeons opponents hasn`t turned up for his match.
Wait....wait..yes, we have some footage from the locker room...my God, that`s the other masters of war, and they`re with some heavies, the heavies are keeping the others from helping Thunder, Thunders being dragged off into a car..where are they going.
ToxicShock: I`ve got a feeling Thunder IS NOT turning up. Victory, by default, to Bludgeon FunkyMonkey Vs. DeathThreat DeathThreat, from the outset, tries to brawl with monkey outside the ring, eventually suceeding and got both eliminated by a double-count out. As a result, the winner, and now International Champion of War is "BLUDGEON".
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: By fair means or foul Tox, by fairmeans or foul Tox: Definitely a case of foul here. Bludgeon has used every dirty cheap trick in the book to win. But then again to win is one thing, but he can`t keep on cheating forever, and one day he`ll face someone who is going to beat him fairly. Either that, or it `s going to have to go to "The Pit".
Avocado Bob: ANd nobody wants that. You`re lucky to come out alice. Tox: Lucks got nothing to do with it. But tonight, we`ve seen the crowning of a new champion. May his reign be short.
Avovado Bob: Well Tox, I`ve got a feeling, a BIG feeling, that if the BIG thing happens, it`s going to be a very very short reign.
Tox: Very. Avocado Bob: SO for me and the `shock, goodnight from the WAR!DATORIUM ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Top Five Wrestlers: IWC: Bludgeon Death Threat XL-9 Johnny Thunder Seth Blitzspear Top 3 Most Popular Wrestlers : FunkyMonkey Seth Blitzspear Morgue 3 Most Hated Wrestlers : Bludgeon DeathThreat XL-9 ------------------------------------------------------------ At WAR!ZONE 2 - Champion Of War title is up for grabs.
