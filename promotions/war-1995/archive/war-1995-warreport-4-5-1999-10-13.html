The crowd gave a good ovation to the lucky thirteen wrestlers. This was to be a very quick battle royale though. Seth Blitzspear immediately eliminated Jerry Gordon, and would have gone after him if Zanders and Goat hadn`t gone after him, but he eliminated them as well. They tried holding him an arm each, but he used his raw strength to throw them over, then got into a punch-up with Floyd. The next two to go were Kenny B. and The Golden Boy, both clotheslined over the top rope by Corporal Nobbs. 8 men were left, all would go through to the Room of Doom match - Corporal Nobbs, Machinehead, Seth Blitzspear, Nomad, 'Hard' Harry Who, Dain Jerrus, Brian Floyd and Who went next, kicked in the face and over the top rope by Machinehead. Floyd was finally eliminated by Seth Blitzspear via a military press. Nomad blinded Jerrus with sand to the face, and eliminated him thusly, but Nomad was knocked out shortly afterwards by Corporal Nobbs, who blocked the sand and headbutted Nomad over. Machinehead disposed of Brian Holland with a slam over the top rope, leaving Machinehead, Blitzspear and Corporal Nobbs in the ring, getting immense cheers from the crowd. Neither of these three wanted to lose, and it turned into an exciting three-way fight for the next several minutes, until Blitzspear eliminated Machinehead with the 'Flying Time'. He tried to eliminate Nobbs in the same way, but Nobbs stopped short of the back drop and hair-slammed Seth face-first into the mat. The two fought for several minutes longer, until Seth threw Nobbs into the corner, charged in with a clothesline, but Nobbs bent down and backdropped Seth over the top rope and to the outside, winning the Battle Royale as a result.
