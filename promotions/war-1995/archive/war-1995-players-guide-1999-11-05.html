WAR! PLAYERS GUIDE V2.4 WAR! Wrestling Federation (WAR! for short) was established in April 1995 by Jacob Kane and held it`s first card on 2 May 1995. Since then, WAR! grew from being a small, quantity not quality fed into one of the largest, most respected feds in the world of e-wrestling that produces quality and fun. As the saying goes, old feds are good feds because they stand the test of time... But events led to Jacob Kane`s departure...and then return, establishing a new WAR wrestling federation -WAR:WRESTLING FEDERATION. With it`s opening card on August 17, it looked set to continue on the success of it`s predecessor. And it did...sorta. Shortly after the formation of War: Wrestling Federation, War! Wrestling Federation was merged into War: Wrestling Federation, as did the titles. The International Belt was scrapped in favour of the National War! championship, but beyond that, it was business as usual... And then, in June 1998, Jacob Kane had to close War! Wrestling Federation to pursue life. But the bug has bitten him again. He has new ideas, new dreams...the WAR! must continue. Contents 0. The Fedhead 1. What is WAR! ? 2. A brief history of WAR! 3. What is WAR! ? war n. & v. - a specific conflict or the period of time during which such conflict exists; the suspension of international law during this period; hostility or contention between people, groups, etc; an e-wrestling federation of that name. That is, of course, the dictionary`s definition. Here is mine... It is, like all other e-feds, a federation where you enter characters and try to guide them to success. Success, of course, depends on what your goals are. Some may call WAR! a `booked' e-wrestling fed, but in fact, it uses the Jacob Kane Judgement System, which will be described later. WAR! is as much about the events beyond the ring as it is inside it. In a way, it is very much a soap-opera that depends on your ability to become your character. It is the emphasis on the characterisation of each wrestler that distinguishes WAR! from other feds (or at least it used to!). War! is a unique and quirky fed, that exists in a world of it`s own, and is all the better for it. Most importantly though, WAR! is exciting, fun and addictive... A Brief History of WAR!... Since its establishment in April 1995, many great stars of e-wrestling have passed through the talent roster of WAR! including RSPWF Hall of Famer, Brewster Cogburn. But WAR! has also been the home to many great names such as Tonnage, Bane, Lord Byron, Intrepid, Southern Man and countless others. WAR! is where many of the greats of e-wrestling started and stayed. The first season of WAR! involved mainly the feud between the Masters of War! and Seth Blitzspear...The Masters of War! defeated the Four Seasons, but it was Seth, the World War! champ who overcame the stable known as the Masters of War!... In June 1995, WAR! came to an abrupt end following the tragic and dramatic death of WAR!`s President, General Andropov, assasinated during a motorcade in Dallas, Texas...WAR! was then forced to shut down following financial irregularities... October 1995 saw WAR! explode onto the scene again, with"Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy taking over the role of vice-President...or President of Vice as some called him...Seth Blitzspear won the big title at the start of season 2, but "The Economist" Randy Blake managed to beat Seth on three consecutive occasions by count-out or DQ, meaning that he won the belt...Season 2 also saw a new generation of wrestlers enter the WAR! scene such as Sid "Stray Cat" Starr, The Reverand J.R.Death and Tonnage...A new dark age dawned with the formation of the Horsemen of the Apocalypse, led by The Reverand J.R. Death, who eventually won the big title...December saw Jacob Kane pass the reins of power over to [name withheld]...but by Feburary, [name withheld], [name withheld] & Jacob Kane formed a new lineup for fedheading in WAR!...the champion J.R.Death had gone missing, [name withheld] was presumed dead... But by now, the third age of WAR! had occured, with a big influx of talent such as Tyler Stevens, Rick Showboat, Bane, Big Duke & Lord Byron...At a Royal Rumble, WAR! veteran Booster Dunne won the International War! title that had been vacant for nearly two months and would hold it for a record four months...and by March, The Horsemen had become Armageddon, and they would dominate the WAR! scene, feuding bitterly with other stables such as War!Force, The Clique, The Rich Boys & Street Level...and they were lead by Tonnage, who had emerged as the domineering icon of WAR! while Seth struggled to find form... [name withheld], with others, established an exciting WAR! spinoff fed called RAW at this time...with the mysterious departure of cofedhead [name withheld] , [name withheld] joined Jacob Kane as cofedhead, doing a damn fine job of it as well...bitter feuds and battles filled the third age of War!... But with Steinar`s temporary departure at the end of May 1996, [name withheld] filled the gap, heralding in the fourth age of WAR!, and promised to be the greatest yet, as WXW merged with WAR! brining its finest talent. Other talent also joining were many NCW stars such as The Jammer, "Iceman" Dean Diamond and Marc Destructo and also rising stars T.J. "Thunder" Decan and Robski...the fourth season, starting in June 1996 and comprising of the three fedheads [name withheld], [name withheld] & Jacob Kane appeared very exciting. June was filled with the Countdown to Extinction tournament, eventually won by T.J. "Thunder" Decan...By this time, Seth had won back the World War! Title and the Lost Boys began a long, briefly interrupted reign as tag team champs and Bane had beaten the amazing Lord Byron for the International War! Title...Armageddon continued to dominate the War! horizon over the summer of 96, but the biggest news was Seth giving up the World War! title which prompted the very large and very popular Decan to side with Tonnage... October 96 began with the return of Jacob Kane & [name withheld], forming a five man fedhead committee for War!...October saw the continuation of the Tournament of Champions, the arrival of the New Dawn Army, a bursting tag scene, the return and arrival of many great stars as well as a definitive points system. It just kept on getting better... This era of WAR! was particularly violent, and with T.J.Decan having joined Armageddon, things looked to be going all wrong for the rest of WAR! But eventually Decan turned on Armageddon and that spelled the beginning of the end for the super-stable. When Tonnage retired and McGriff was suspended for almost killing Jason MidNight at MegaWar I - The BloodBrawl, Armageddon fell. Jason MidNight was beginning a popular reign as champion, and Lord Byron had returned involved in a deadly feud with Bane. But after a three week christmas break, and with MegaWar II just around the corner, Jason MidNight left WAR! due to his injuries. The World War Title was vacated and Lord Byron and the rising superstar Chris Deugau fought an epic battle resulting in Lord Byron becoming champion. At this point, the AC was becoming established, and Ground Zero, a stable under the management of Flea, was gaining momentum with the intention of destroying Seth Blitzspear. They almost succeeded with T.J Decan (a suprising member after the terrible acts by Ground Zero of burning down his horse stables) and long term friend Jerry Gordon on their team. The WAR! continued. For the next several months, everything was chaos, with only Doug Simpkins lengthy reign as Liberty champion providing any stability. At Casualties of War IV, Lord Byron lost the World Title to Bane and then Bane to McGriff all in the same evening. Tyler Stevens had won the Liberty title by now, and Chris Deugau was International War! champion. In the War!Stars trilogy, three round robin tournaments, WAR! saw some great action, and it underlined the ability of Doug Simpkins, who would go on to win the World title off Biff McGriff. Tonnage returned and teamed with Frank The Tank, and Armageddon was back in action. Ground Zero accomplished an amazing feat though. Even though Flea had died, the four remaining members - Sid Starr, Nomad and The Windy City Warriors, all held titles. Now, on the eve of the Summer `97 season of WAR!, new talent has been signed by the new yet mysterious WAR! President Mike Hunt. Some say it is the strongest selection of wrestlers ever. Some even say WAR! possesses the toughest roster in e-wrestling. The summer is always hot, but it has never been this hot... Unfortunately, it was hot in the wrong way. Things went a bit haywire, to put it briefly, and WAR! went from the handling of Jake to Homer and then to Allan. Jake returned early August 1997, but he felt to much had changed. He had to start a new WAR. He did, but it quickly became the old War!, which is never a bad thing... From August onwards, War! showed a maturity and sense of humour in perfect balance, which was helped to be brought about by three men who would be significant figures in the rebirth of War! - Southern Man ([name withheld]), Juggernaut (Milhouse) and Intrepid (Ryan Howard). Juggernaut would go on, following Southern Man`s initial success, to hold the World championship for an astounding five months, before Sid Starr finally claimed the gold... Time saw the rise of Wolfgang to claim the gold at an amazing Extinction `98 event. But then WAR! closed. But, recently it had opened back up. But, since Jacob Kane was losing interest, he and Si decided to close it down. To no prevail though, [name withheld], a member of WAR!, stepped up and has now taken over WAR! The ROLE is your character, not you, so be like your character, and play it to it`s upmost. Now let`s look at the PLAYING bit. WAR! is fun, because it`s a game and because, with the Open Flash System, it tries to create the kind of atmosphere of thirty odd people sitting round a table, after a few drinks, hamming it up, basically having a laugh and being good mates. If you don`t want to be friends, don`t be in WAR! The most important thing is to have a target and GO FOR IT. Let it be your driving purpose. Without an objective, your wrestler will not have direction and will just flounder. Whether the goal is to win the World title or to injure as many people as possible, or something more imaginative, it will be a lot more interesting for you. And it can be a measure of success for you. Without a target, you won`t have direction. And without direction, you are going nowhere. Fast. Have short term goals such as winning a feud or earning a certain amount of money in a set amount of time. Perhaps you could pick another wrestler and aim to be higher than him. Have long term goals. What titles do you want? Where do you want your character to go. As I said earlier, this is a roleplaying fed. This means you play the role of your wrestler and you immerse yourself in the world of WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION. In WAR! you develop their character, their attitude, their whole approach. Often, you will be required to respond to a claim or an accusation against your wrestler, or you may be making that claim. The important thing to remember though is that your character isn`t just a fighting machine. The character is supposed to have a personality and a life as well, which is created by relationships with other characters, whether negative or positive. It may also require you to be devious. WAR! has always been about the development of story lines as well. You play an important role in those stories, either byinitiating them or participating in them or reacting to them. Think of an angle, an approach or just something you can do that might make an interesting story or plot. This is what makes War! entertaining, and it is supposed to be primarily entertainment. It can also make things a whole lot more interesting. These plans etc. may require you to team up or plan with other players. Don`t be afraid to do so. And don`t be afraid to create alliances with people who aren`t of the same face/heel alignment as you. Why? Because the world ain`t so black & white. The truth is, there is no good or evil, just what things people are for or against, and what their position is on things. Face/Heel simply refers to relationship with fans. Its very plausible for two wrestlers to be great friends despite being opposites in terms of fan affections. And its also why people of the same fan alignement will feud. Remember, face/heel is almost irrelevant. If you stick to that as a basis for your character, you`re going to get nowhere. For example, other areas of tension may be pro/anti America, pro/anti wrestler X, pro/anti brawling etc. Of course, if you`re a face and you thrive on fan support, you need to be careful who you ally yourself with. Also, if someone makes claims, or does something that has repercussions on your character, you don`t just have to say `no'. Instead, play along with it, develop it, make something happen out of it. The more history there is to your character that there is, the more you develop the personality, the more real they become. Those of you who have done this will know what I mean... Questions to ask about your wrestler. Why did he become a wrestler? What is he like? What does he like? What does he do in his spare time? Who are his friends? Where is he from and what is his relationship to that place? What are his motives? Who is he outisde of the wrestling ring? What is his life like outside of wrestling? Married, single, kids? What? You should be able to reach the stage where you actually believe this character to be real! Magazine Articles The one big change this year in WAR! to all previous year is an emphasis on everyone doing Magazine Style articles. This is not to say that flashes and other formats must be abandoned, but Articles are basically going to be compulsory. I don`t care if you don`t flash for a month, but you MUST write an article at least every two weeks WAR!Magazine. Why the emphasis on Articles? Basically, it offers so much more than a simple flash. You can still use the text you`d use in a flash, but instead of leaving it at that, or filling it with description, you can use the third persons view to analyse, to refer to history, to comment, to get other opinions, to speculate, to look at possible underlying feelings and motivations... Basically, Articles should give an insight into the wrestler and the angles they are involved. Yes, it`s going to require more thought, but the benefits are massive. Articles are more interesting to read than flashes. Other players will take more interest in your character. These Articles will be put together and create a great format to chart the history of WAR! What I`m aiming to do is give characters more depth. This Article approach is what has tempted me back to do WAR! more than anything else, and I feel very strongly about it. But it doesn`t mean you have to stop flashing through good ol` Wrestlevision. Flashes/Interviews Flashes are the dialogue sent out by your wrestler, in character. Flashes are the half of this game that the players, and not the fedhead do. WAR! is now a DUAL FORMAT FLASH SYSTEM. It is both closed and open flash. Open Flash System means each message gets sent out by yourself to everyone else on the distribution list. This may mean a lot of flashes. Some like it, some don`t. Simple as that. But O.F.S means that your message will definitely get out. To send a 'flash' or 'roleplay' out to the fed, use the most current 'Flash/Roleplaying' list that I sent, and send it to the whole group of people. WHY FLASHING IS IMPORTANT Flashes are important in several ways. First of all, they`re entertaining and informative. Secondly, they`re very important in creating hype. Hype is very important because it draws more attention to a match. Also, a hyped up match is more likely to get better commentary. Furthermore, it will bring more attention to your wrestler, and they may be more in the minds of fedheads for titleshots than the quieter wrestlers. In 'real' wrestling, wrestlers that are pushed tend to get more air-time or are involved in plots etc. The decisions made in 'real' wrestling about who gets a push comes from the fedhead. In e-wrestling, it is up to you. You have to make your own air time (by flashes, interviews etc), you have to set up your own angles and create your own heat. The players that do this succeed, because they are bringing entertainment and interest. Players that provide entertainment are considered valuable by fedheads and as thus, they are rewarded with victories and belts to keep them in the fed. So basically, if you don`t participate, you`re not going anywhere but down. I feel it is important to note, after noticing the debates on RSPWF in late October 1996, that WAR! is not a federation where the person who flashes the most wins. WAR! kinda uses the Human Judgement System, and therefore takes into account many more factors than simply flashing. However, flashing is an integral part of WAR! and can certainly influence whether you win or not (regular, quality flashes will get you wins, no flashes or bad flashes will get you defeats) Below are some of the things I give thumbs up to when people do flashes. They may just be guidelines, but I do like to see people follow them. In the subject header put in a relevant subject header. Don`t just do "Re: War!Card 5". Try instead " X comments on War!Card". Putting in a subject header really helps things out. When possible, try to summarise a flash you are responding to, rather than including it, because this takes up more mailspace. Understandably, some people may prefer to comment on each line, but when possible, try and summarise. It will also grab the interest of non-related readers more. However, I would like to note that it really irritates me when people include large lumps of another persons text for no reason. Furthermore, flashes which just include previous flashes, commenting on them paragraph by paragraph I tend to find are, to be honest, not interesting to read. Some people can do it well, and are funny with it. Others however.... When typing out the flash, try and get in character. Be the character, not yourself. Think the characters thoughts, motivations and feelings. Also, consider the surroundings etc. Be descriptive, set the scene, if it is necessary. Some of the best flashes occur when people have been thinking about what they`re going to say before they type it. So when you`re walking around, away from the computers, try and think of something to say in advance. Trust me, this helps create some excellent flashes. Face2Face interviews. E-mail the person you`re feuding with and construct a head to head flash, and then send it to everyone else. This is a really effective form of flash. If you see someone do a good flash, feel free to compliment them (one on one) because it`s always nice to know you`re doing well : ) Another way to do this is to write the whole dialogue between you and the other character and then send it to the other player. They can edit it, and send it back to you, unless they like it and then they can send it out. If you like what they have sent to you, send it out. If you don`t, edit it and send it to them for approval...and so on... Or do it on IRC! Things I give the thumbs down to.... Slagging off other players. This is a total NO-NO! If you have any problems with another player, go to the fedhead first. Overuse of strong language. Swearing is acceptable to a degree, but try and keep it down. The fact is, most people who resort to swearing just can`t think of an original way to insult someone. Swearing in flashes, I dare say, can come across as very childish unless it`s really in character. Commenting on peoples writing/spelling in a negative manner. This is basically bad netiquette anyhow. Saying someones spelling is bad is like laughing at someones speach impediment. Its basically rude, and the important thing is that they are participating. Negative comments just deter people from playing, and that is a big NO-NO! However, this is no excuse for players to have bad grammar. Sentences should begin with capitals and end in full stops. The text should be broken up into proper paragraphs as requiree as well. Rascism. Whether in character or not, rascism is despicable. Flash Format Although you are welcome to do flashes in any format you like, the general format I have seen that looks the most impressive is... [Descriptive text in square brackets] Words from the character [Fade out] Although, if you want to make it more complex (please do), after the words from the character, do more descriptive text, such as a change in the characters face etc. and then make some more comments. Repeat until satisfied. As for length of flashes, make sure that they are of appropriate length. If you have a lot of important or interesting stuff to say, a long (5k plus) flash would be apt, but on the other hand, a five line flash could be equally as apt. The important this is that (a) You flash - to show that you are active and to give your wrestler a better chance (b) Don`t be boring (c) Don`t be repetitive (d) Say something that needs to be said. Furthermore, consider alternate ways of getting your message across. Perhaps a different format instead of somebody standing there. Some suggestions are: Newspaper/Magazine article; TV report; Fly-on-the-wall documentary etc. Cards WAR! used to be a two big cards a week fed. It used to have five fedheads. This led to chaos and delayed cards. Since March 1997, WAR! had one 'televised' card a week, with approximately 8 matches on it. This was called War!Zone, and I`ve got to say, it was the first and original War!Zone, having been establised back in May 1995. Currently I`m not sure exactly how much I can handle, but my feeling at the moment is that there will be a monthly WAR!ZONE supercard, with full play by play and commentary, and for the rest of the time War!Shockwave will be used, every Sunday, featuring 8-12 matches. On each card, you can do just about anything. All you have to do is tell the fedhead what you want to do, whether it is tactics, or jumping someone or an interview or whatever. Do whatever it takes to be entertaining. Priority of tactics is given to either the first in or best strats, although most of the time I try to blend them in to get everyone a little bit of what they want. I want exciting strats. They make exciting cards. Warning: Make sure strats and interviews are sent in A.S.A.P because I only check my mail about once, maybe twice a day. Because WAR! sometimes uses CWMR2 to help with the match-structure (not the results, just the way the match would flow) it is not necessary to send in long, detailed strats detailing how your wrestler will fight, unless markedly different, because the CWMR2 simulates the style in which your wrestler would fight. This helps you give more time to write those entertaining flashes and interviews. SUPERCARDS Where would we be without supercards? Some call them PPV`s, but I prefer supercards. There has never been regular supercards, until now... Well, there will be a War!Zone supercard every month. The Rankings System These determine how you are ranked. It is based on your win-loss record, to keep it simple for me. .The WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION ranking system is split into the belt catergories - World, Manhattan, Liberty, War!Lord and World Tag. Match Resolution I used to proclaim that WAR! used the Human Judgement System, trying to determine rationally who won. But who the hell was I kidding? All along it was the JKJS - The Jacob Kane Judgement System. It`s not a bias system. It`s a fair, booked system. If we think you`ve worked harder than your opponent, you should get the victory (assuming nothing else happens!) but at the end of the day, it is the fedheads call, so you`re just going to have to accept the verdict. We try our best to get the right results, but on occassions, your opinion will differ from the fedhead. If you lose, you have to accept it. The measure of the man is whether he can come back fighting from defeat. The fact is, sometimes it is too close to call in WAR! because so many of the players produce equally high standards of work. There is no favoritism though, there are only what we hope are fair results. We can do no more than that. Fedheads WAR! has experimented with co-fedhead sysytems, but now there is always but one supremo in charge. He may have an assistant, but it is unlikely. However, one fedhead cannot keep everything running alone, so there is always need for people who would like to help write matches. If you`d like to help, e-mail the fedhead. Without help, it isn`t always possible to produce the desired quality. People & Places & Things in War! These are just some of the people & places in the War! universe.
