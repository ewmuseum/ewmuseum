War!Fare — 20 June 1996 (Part 1)

Date sent: Thu, 20 Jun 1996 09:25:42 GMT-3:30 From: [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Send reply to: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: WAR!Fare part 1 *************************************************************************** ** WAR!FARE *************************************************************************** ** [Scenes of WAR! superstars in action...] Tonnage with a Giganton Crush on American Patriot...Lord Byron with an
Aristoclutch on Booster Dunne...The Lost Boys with Wendy...Storm Stalker and
Jason Midnight locking up...Og Gary Trent with a "Drive-by"...Suna Gib rubbing Bane...Brutus Noir covered in blood...Sabin Figaro with a "Bum Rush" on
Tyler Stevens...Seth Blitzspear holding the World WAR! belt high over his head after beating Tonnage...WAR! logo (tm)
Dave Scott: Good evening, fans! We've got another jampacked lineup on tap for today. Seth Blitzspear will defend his title against "Magnum"
Biff McGriff. OG Gary Trent defends the Liberty belt against Johnny Missile. And Lord Byron has a title defense as well. John Christopher: But it's not going to be against who he thought it was. Tony J got this comment from Booster Dunne's manager, Ms.Paula, earlier today... ---------- Tony J: Ms.Paula, where is Booster Dunne? I've been told he won't make it to his match tonight against Lord Byron.
Paula: That's true. Mr.Dunne has left the country. After his recent injuries, he felt it would be in everyone's best interest if he took some time off. Currently, he is in Japan resting and training for a comeback. He will hold a title in WAR! again. That I can promise you.
TJ: I've also heard he will be fined for not showing up to this scheduled match. Paula: Yes, a fine has been levied. Mr.Dunne can easily afford anything WAR! tries to throw at him. Mr.Blake has made sure of that. Speaking of my other employer, Mr.Blake offered to take the match with Byron tonight, but WAR! officials refused. They said the challenger would need to be someone in the top ten. What a disgrace to WAR! it is that a former International and World WAR! Champion like Mr.Blake doesn't qualify for a title shot against an overrated talent like
Lord Byron. Whoever they dig up to wrestle Byron will be greatly inferior to Mr.Blake, I'm sure.
TJ: Well, thanks for your time, Ms.Paula... --------- DS: As Ms.Paula said in that interview, Byron will have to face a wrestler from the top ten as decided on by WAR! officials only moments before the International title match later.
JC: That puts the champion at an obvious disadvantage. Here he was training for Booster Dunne, but now he has to wrestle somebody else. I'd file a lawsuit. DS: I know you would. Let's get on to some action... Craig JayHawk w/his Punx vs Brutus "Sidewinder" Noir There were no surprises in this match. JayHawk and Noir went right after each other, using everything but the kitchen sink. Noir was cut open two minutes into the match, but that only seemed to spur him on. JayHawk for his part tried to keep the match in the ring as much as possible, but Noir preferred the outside area. Noir dragged JayHawk to the timekeeper's table and slammed him on top of the timekeeper's bell. JayHawk rallied and suplexed Noir into the second row of the crowd. The referee completely lost control of the match, and with the help of a fast ten-count, declared the match a draw.
Result: Double countout Rating: *** Bane vs Suna Gib Bane demanded this match to finally get revenge on Suna Gib, who has been harassing him for some time. Suna surprised Bane by actually wanting to fight this time. Gib gained control of the match with a series of chops to the throat and a reverse atomic drop. With Bane hurt, Gib decided to have some fun. Gib produced a roll of tape which he used to tape up the eye holes in
Bane's mask. Bane went ballisitic, attacking Gib blindly. One lucky swing dropped Gib. Bane jumped on top of him and pummeled him. Suna was no match for the enraged Bane and was finished by Bane's Death Drop.
Result: Bane by pinfall Rating: ** 1/2 Reverend J.R. Death w/the Preacher vs T.J. Thunder Decan This was a battle of two big powerhouses. Death had the height advantage at 7'6, but Decan had the weight advantage at 560 pounds. Death looked to have developed a little ring rust in his absence. However, he showed he is still a force to be reckoned with. Decan got an early pop from the fans when he slammed the 470-pound Reverend. Death fought back, concentrating his attacks on Decan's back. He tried to get Thunder in the Torture Rack, but Decan proved a little too big. Decan surprised the reverend with a boot to the head. T.J. then went on the attack with punches, kicks, and clotheslines.
Death survived the onslaught, then fired back with his own. Eventually, the two big men brawled to the outside and continued to fight up the aisle back to their dressing rooms.
Result: Double countout Rating: ** "Iceman" Dean Diamond vs Colossus Colossus jumped the Iceman before the bell rang and went to work on the newcomer. Diamond was whipped into the corner hard, flipping completely over the top turnbuckle and landing in a heap on the outside. Colossus threw him back in the ring and began to toy with him, rubbing his face in the mat.
Just when Diamond looked like he was through, he managed to get Colossus rolled up in a small package and almost got a three count. That threw
Colossus off his game. Diamond got Colossus down on the mat and worked on his legs. After a couple of near pins, Diamond got brave and went to the top turnbuckle. Colossus wasn't ready to be put away, though. He stopped
Diamond
with a shot to the gut and followed it up with a superplex. A quick cover got Colossus the hard-fought victory. "Dirty" Doug Simpkins came half way down the aisle to "scout" Dean Diamond during the match, but somehow managed not to get involved.
Winner: Colossus by pinfall Rating: ** 1/2 Total Destruction (Theater of Pain) w/The Dark One vs Agonizers w/Colt McGraw, Terra Branford, and Celes Chere This was a big match for both teams. A win would put either team in a good position for a title shot. Neither team was going to go down without a fight.
Vanquish and Conquer took it to General Leo early on, using frequent tags to stay fresh. Matt Grapler made the save for his partner, and was eventually tagged in. "Mr. Wrestling" tried to turn it into a wrestling match, but the
TOP were not going to let him. They brawled all over the ring and soon all four fought outside. McGraw held Vanquish for Grapler, but Mr.Wrestling hit his manager by mistake when Vanquish moved out of the way. Meanwhile,
Conquer had gained the advantage over Leo. Conquer slammed him into the ringpost, rolled him back in the ring, and got the pin while putting his feet on the ropes for leverage.
Winners: Total Destruction by pinfall Rating: *** Storm Stalker vs "The Blade" Larry Brown A rematch from the last WAR!Fare, Storm Stalker and Larry Brown couldn't wait to get at each other. It went back and forth for the first ten minutes...
DS: Brown suplexes Storm Stalker. A cover, but a quick kick out by Stalker. The Blade whips Stalker into the ropes...clothesline, but Stalker ducks...Stalker back with a dropkick! Legdrop by Storm Stalker. JC: The Blade's playing it smart. He rolls outside to clear his head. Have you ever seen anyone look so lovingly at a hockey stick? DS: Can't say that I have. Brown now finally gets back in the ring. He and Stalker exchange punches. They're going at it like a couple of heavyweight boxers! Brown ends that brawl with an eye poke. The
Blade bodyslams his opponent...a kick to the face...elbowdrop...and now a choke! Get in there, ref!
JC: He's got a five-count to break the hold! DS: Brown drags Stalker to the ropes to choke him again. He's risking a disqualification! Brown drapes him over the bottom rope, runs off the opposite ropes, and jumps on his back! Brown tries it again...but
Storm Stalker moves out of the way! That had to hurt. Stalker is up ...knee lift...a punch to the midsection bends Brown over...and there's a DDT! Just like that Larry Brown is out of it!
JC: Yeah, but Storm Stalker is not pinning him. He's going to the top rope. You can't give a veteran like the Blade time to recover like that. DS: Someone's coming out of the crowd! It's Doug Simpkins! He shakes the rope...Storm Stalker falls awkwardly into the ring! Brown rolls him up, grabs the tights...1...2...3!
JC: No upset win for Storm Stalker today! That's a great ring veteran, "The Blade" Larry Brown in there, not some loser nobody like Seth Blitzspear. DS: "Dirty" Doug lives up to his name and helps the equally dirty Larry Brown get a big win. Winner: Larry Brown by pinfall Rating: ** 1/2 Reckoner vs Robski Robski got a loud reception from the crowd, but he didn't seem to mind the boos. The fans were squarely behind the Reckoner. He used that energy to launch his attack on Robski. Reckoner went for a quick win with a
gut-wrench suplex followed by a bulldog. Robski managed to kick out, but the Reckoner went right back on the offensive, whipping Robski into the ropes. He ducked his head for a backdrop attempt, but Robski caught him and delivered a pedigree. Robski was slow to make the cover, however, allowing Reckoner to kick out of a pin attempt. Robski dominated the match from there...
DS: Robski has Reckoner over his shoulder! Will it be a powerslam? Maybe a shoulderbreaker? No, he just dumps him right over the top rope! And Robski is not letting up - he's going right out after him. Reckoner gets whipped into the steel railing! Robski slams his head into the ring apron and rolls him back in the ring. Robski has the
Reckoner again...belly-to-back suplex! Reckoner is just about out of it, and Robski is looking pretty confident which the fans don't appreciate.
JC: Robski is going to be a force to "reckon" with in WAR!, don't you think? DS: Robski with a backbreaker...and a headbutt...and another headbutt to the face...he's slapping Reckoner around now...Come on, just pin him already, you don't have to humilate the man!
JC: He doesn't *have* to, but he likes to give the fans a little something extra. He should be commended for that, not booed like these fans are doing. DS: Robski sets Reckoner up for his finisher, the Jack-Knife Powerbomb... but Reckoner with a back body drop to escape it... Robski hangs on for a sunset flip... but he can't get him over... the Reckoner hooks
Robski's legs and leans forward....1...2...3! Reckoner got him! That's an incredible victory for the Reckoner! And a devastating loss for Robski! JC: Robski's still in the ring complaining to the referee. That was obviously a fast count. His... Uh oh, here's trouble! DS: Butch Williams has run down to the ring! Robski hasn't seen him yet. Williams attacks Robski! He's wrapping him in the American flag and now he's lighting the flag on fire! Robski rolls out of the ring and runs to the exit.
JC: Williams is asking to get killed, isn't he? Last time on WAR!Fare, Tonnage, Bane, and Lord Byron beat him senseless, so now he's back this week making more enemies. I would not want to be "the
American" Butch Williams right now. DS: I wouldn't want to be Robski right now. He ran out of here like he was in pain, although I don't think he was on fire long enough for any serious injuries. Let's see if our next match can top that!
"Dirty" Doug Simpkins vs Jason Midnight Both wrestlers had prepared a long time for this match. Midnight ran to the ring and attacked Simpkins. "Dirty" Doug tried to slow the pace down to his liking, but Midnight wasn't about to let him. Midnight used his speed and martial arts abilities to keep Simpkins on the defensive.
DS: Midnight has Simpkins...whips him into the ropes...big back bodydrop! Simpkins hasn't gotten any offense in. JC: Now what's that punk think he's doing? DS: Midnight is locking in a Boston crab right in the center of the ring! The fans are on their feet as they'd love to see Simpkins title reign come to an end right here with a submission!
JC: No! Simpkins will never give up! DS: The ref is asking him if he quits. He's shaking his head no. Simpkins is edging towards the ropes, but he's got a long ways to go! You can see the pain on his face.
JC: And look at Midnight! He's got a crazed look on his face. He's pulling back on Simpkins' legs for all he's worth! DS: Simpkins keeps inching toward the ropes. He won't give up! Midnight can't believe Simpkins hasn't quit yet! JC: Just a little more... Yes! Simpkins grabs the bottom rope. DS: Midnight is reluctant to release the hold, but he does just before the five-count. Simpkins tries to go outside, but Midnight grabs him by the tights and hauls him back in. Jason starts kicking the champion's back. He seems to have a solid gameplan - go after Simpkins' back.
JC: Maybe that's Simpkins' plan. He's setting Midnight up for something. DS: Midnight is continuing his assault on Simpkins. There's a bodyslam. He's climbing up to the top turnbuckle again. He leaps for a double axe-handle... Simpkins dodges it and plants his fist in Midnight's stomach! Midnight keels over in pain! Simpkins grabs his head...DDT!
He laid Midnight out! JG: Just like that the veteran Simpkins is in control. I knew it all along. DS: He's unable to follow up on that DDT, however. He's taken such a beating at the hands of Midnight, he's just lying there beside Midnight. JG: He's just taking a little rest. He can pin Midnight anytime he wants. DS: Simpkins starts to get to his feet... and so does Midnight! Simpkins with a roundhouse...blocked! Midnight fires back with a right. Doug tries another roundhouse...blocked again! Midnight drops him with an uppercut! Midnight with a scoop and a slam. Midnight waits for
Simpkins to get to his feet... dropkick! Simpkins flies through the middle ropes. JG: Watch out, Doug! Midnight's going to the top turnbuckle! DS: Midnight leaps to the outside... nobody home! Simpkins moved out of the way! Midnight landed hard on the floor. Simpkins picks up Jason Midnight... what's he doing? Oh no! He gave him a stungun on the steel railing! JG: Ha ha! I'd like to see Midnight get up from that. DS: Well, I don't think you're going to see that. Midnight is out cold. Simpkins gets back in the ring. The referee continues to count Jason out...5...6...7...8...9...10! Simpkins will get a hard-fought victory this time. JG: It was easier than it looked. Simpkins had a gameplan and he stuck to it. Midnight made one too many mistakes. Winner: "Dirty" Doug Simpkins by CO Rating: **** (continued...)
