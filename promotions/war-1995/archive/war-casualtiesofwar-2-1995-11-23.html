Casualties of War 2 — The Unforgotten War

SUBJECT: Casualties of War 2 - "The Unforgotten War" DATE: Thu, 23 Nov 95 15:33:44 GMT WRESTLING UNLIMITED Presents.... In association with Bobs Drive-by-Diner ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION presents CASUALTIES OF WAR 2 "THE UNFORGOTTEN WAR" Live from the WARENA, Sao Paulo, Brazil With your Supercard Superhosts, "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies and gentlemen, to the second annual Casualties of War this year.
Bfrog: Huh? You can't have two annual events in one year! Bob: We can. Just as Casualties of War marked the end of the Summer Season last time, it now marks the end of the Autumn Season. But fear not, for we carry straight on to the Winter Season without a break.
Look out for the replacement show for Masters of War, which has now sadly finished. Replacing it will be WARRIORS. Just the same, but a different name. Enough about that though, lets talk about tonight.
Bfrog: Good idea. What I'd like to talk about first is Brutus Noir. Bob: Yes indeed. An interesting development. With Brutus Noir winning the World War championship, it gives all the members of
Tonnages team to win it. Remeber, at Casualties of War, all titles are on the line, as long as you are either pinned or submit. Otherwise, the rules for the rest of the night are simple - Don't be a casualty.
Teams of four battle each other. Once you have been lost a fall, you're out of there, leaving your team a man down. Once all the members of one team have been eliminated, the remaining members of the other team proceed to the "CivilWar" - A battleroyale with all the survivors.
In the civil war battle royale though, you can lose by all methods, including being thrown over the top. The last man standing is awarded the prestigious "Immortal Soldier" Trophy, and a shot at the world
champion - if it isn't the world champion. Bfrog: How about the Seth Blitzspear match? Bob: Excitement all the way Bullfrog. It's a special "Mask" match where Seth must remove the mask of his opponent to win. This is the climaz of a series of events that have cost Seth his world title, and for a while, his fans. A conspiracy was established, but Seth
Blitzspear will try to overcome it tonight. The good news for Joey the Clown fans is that he will be captaining his team today, but a suprise replacement has been called for Jerry
Gordans team. He is, as yet, unannounced. Bfrog: And don't forget Blake big match tonight against the ghost from his past. No word yet as to who it is, but it is known the Hot Stuff -
Cinder & Inferno, have been called up to that team. The other two remain a mystrey. Bob: Without any further hesitation, lets get to the first matchup which is Cardinals Crew versus The American Heroes.

Non-Casualty: American Patriot ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: And now we go to a comment from the three time world champion Brutus Noir... ( The Camera homes in on Brutus Noir. He has the World War! Championship belt round his waist and the viewers can see he has a new tattoo comemorating his victory. It depicts Randy Blake in the
Marseille strangler with Ms Paula begging for mercy.) BN: So, at last I was given the title shot I deserved and I won back what is rightfully mine. - For the THIRD TIME - beat that Shitspear. I am just disappointed that my old friend ICF wasn't there to see it. Well I suppose the hospital just wouldn't let him out and seeing as he couldn't move his neck anymore he couldn't watch the TV. HA HA HA.
It seems that some overgrown mutants have designs on this belt at Caualties of WAR! Well let me tell them that although I may be on a team of losers I do not intend to lose. I have beaten bigger and better men than you and this time you will feel the true power of the Sidewinder. How will it feel to be humiliated again deMort.
J.R. Death you seem scared to face me. You had to grovel to the referee to get the match stopped last time we met just because you were bleeding a little bit. Some hard man. This time there will be no mercy. As for you Tonnage, how is it going to feel when you have no manager after this bout. Its gonna have to be a mighty small coffin to fit Louie in.
You lot had better watch out as I waited a long time to get this beauty back and I will defend it with my life.
(Camera fades out) Bob: He seems determined, but not as determined as the next team facing each other, led by Sid "StrayCat" Starr. Let's hear what they have to say..
The background is black with the Casualties of War logo across it. Sid Starr is standing there facing the camera, grinning arrogantly. He is a handsome, young man with short blonde hair. He is approximately 6'2"
and about 220 lbs. He is wearing black shades, a black leather jacket and black wrestlingtights and boots.
SID: Casualties of War! Matches with 8 men in the same ring! A recipe for mayhem and destruction! And ya know what, wrestling fans? I LOVE IT!
Sid laughs out loud, then calms down and speaks again. SID: Casualties of War will be the end of you, Bozo the Clown! You and your fellow circus rejects have some serious pain and suffering heading your way! Let me introduce to you the men who will utterly DESTROY Team
Clown! A collection of the FINEST technical wrestling talent in this or any other federation! First the man who I fought in my first match in
WAR. A True master of oriental wrestling! The Mandarin! The Mandarin is a trim man with black hair. He stands about 5'9", 225 lbs. He is wearing a button-down shirt and a pair of khaki slacks, matched with
Vuarnet sunglasses. He sneers at the camera, then speaks: MANDARIN: Team Clown...how fitting to have your name match your essential essence. There isn't a wrestler among you who could stand up to the rigors of a true fight. One would almost think that we could underestimate you, that your pathetic collection of wrestling rabble might actually win. But the truth is that you will meet your fate in that ring. None of you could withstand the
*least* of us. I live to see the expression on your sad painted faces when the last of you is beaten to a thin paste on the mat. SID: Next we have another master of the oriental style of wrestling, and when I say master here I mean it literally. Ladies and
Gentlemen...."The Master" Akira Hashimata! (Hashimata somersaults into view, does some karate show off stuff and then bows to his team members)
SID: Next on the list we find a very nice and decent guy, and a true ringtechnician. Nice Mr. Nexus!
Nexus walks on, bows low (very theatric) and coughs: NEXUS: Really, you do me too much honour. I just pray my ailing health does not hold us back."
SID: And finally, the captain of the team, little old me. The coolest Cat in wrestling! This is the team which will Destroy Bozo and friends. Guys, how about we explain our opponents to the audience? Sid grins widely and holds up Joey the Clown's Liberty title belt. SID: First we have the head clown. The top fool, if you will. Bozo the Clown...Whoops, I mean Joey of course. WAR's very own circus reject, and the man who through sheer luck has managed to keep the belt when facing the coolest Cat in wrestling. Well, Joey, here's your belt. You want it?
Then come and get it! Oh, by the way, how does it feel having the design of the belt permanently imprinted on your forehead?
Sid grins widely, then continues. SID: Tell ya what, Joey. If you guys win tonight I PROMISE I'll return the belt to ya.....of course, you won't win, but I do so love to give idiots a shred of hope and then destroy that same hope seconds later.
Joey, you're a fool, and as for the rest of your team...Who's the bigger fool? The fool himself or the fools who follow him?
Sid grins widely while Nexus just coughs a bit more. MANDARIN: Trent! You claim to be some tough guy, strutting around bragging about how much pain and suffering you can cause. Guess what? You don't know anything about it! Tonight I will teach you pain. I will teach you suffering.
However, you will have to find another tutor for your new career after I keep you from *ever* wrestling again. Your hoodlum homeboys can't help you. You will need to be removed from the ring on a stretcher! Bob: Wow, they're pumped. Let's just see if they can wrestle as tough as they talk. Remember, Joeys belt is up for grabs, and if he wins tonight, Sid Starr will give Joey back his title.
