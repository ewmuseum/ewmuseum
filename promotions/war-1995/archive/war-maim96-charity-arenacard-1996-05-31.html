WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

MAIM `96 — Charity Arena Card

31 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Yes, War! is such a lovely, kind-hearted fed, it staged, last night, a charity card for the Lesser Spotted Syrian Sheep Preservation
Society. It was a double BattleBlitz Event. For those of you unfamiliar with the BattleBlitz, each wrestler randomly picks a
number , then enters in that order. It`s a case of winner stays on. Here are the results. By the way, DeathJester was arrested shortly after his InterNational War! title match against Booster Dunne for the abduction and murder of a small girl, the attempted murder of
Tyler Stevens and the death of Richard Madley, short-lived interviewer.
Titles were not on the line MAIM `96 (May 30th) at the War!Datorium BattleBlitz 1 Match 1: Randy Blake pinned Brutus Noir following a piledriver
BattleBlitz 2 Match 1: The Reckoner beat American Patriot by countout BB1M2: Bane pinned Blake following banes DeathDrop BB2M2: The Reckoner beat Golden Page by countout BB1M3: Bane pinned Bill McGriff BB2M3: The Reckoner lost to The Big Duke BB1M4: Booster Dunne pinned Bane BB2M4: Big Duke beat Tonnage by DQ BB1M5: Booster Dunne beat Johnny Missile by countout BB2M5: Big Duke pinned Tyler Stevens with his PowerPackage BB1M6: Lord Byron pinned Booster Dunne BB2M6: Big Duke beat Butch Williams by countout BB1M7: Rick Showboat beat Lord Byron by countout BB2M7: The Jammer pinned Big Duke BB1M8: Rick Showboat pinned Sabin Figaro BB2M8: Seth Blitzspear defeated The Jammer BB1M9: Rick Showboat beat Suna Gib by countout BB2M9: Seth Blitzspear beat "The Cardinal" Andrea Mancini BB1M10: TJ Thunder pinned Rick Showboat BB2M10: Seth Blitzspear beat Mark Starr by countout In the BattleBlitz decider of the MAIM `96 Tournament, Seth Blitzspear beat TJ Thunder by pinfall. Seth wins the "Maimer of Sheep" trophy. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
