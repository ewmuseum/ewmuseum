WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WAR!ZONE

11 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
> ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ > WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION > > proudly presents > > WAR!ZONE > from Boston, MA > On Febuary 11th 1997 > > with your pyro-dynamic hosts > "Avocado" Bob Whitely > & Xavier Suave > ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ > > Bob: Welcome one and welcome all to another exciting edition of > WAR!ZONE, and boy oh boy, do we have a show and a half for you with no > fewer than four title matches and many others. > > XS: Tonight, Charles Martel makes his first unified title defence > against the ultra-violent Brutus Noir. > > Bob: Martel will be fighting this match for Randy Blake, who is > involved in an intense feud with Noir. Do you think he can over come > Noir`s brutality? > > XS: As long as he keeps the match to the way he likes it, otherwise > Noir will have the total run of this match. Noir is prone to DQ`s and > double-countouts though, and that could prove to be his biggest > shortfall. > > Bob: The All-Americans get a shot at the International War Titles, > having failed twice to dethrone The Lost Boys. > > XS: Don`t even bother asking me who will win. I have no preference. > They`re both a bunch of numbskulls. > > Bob: We can also.. > > XS: Although if I have to pick anyone, it would be All-Americans if > they fight dirty and get JJM involved. > > Bob: American Patriot defends his War!Lord title against Decan. > > XS: Decan failed to get the World War! Title last week, but I`ve been > speaking to him, and he seems desperate for a title. Personally, I > wouldn`t want to be American Patriot tonight. > > Bob:Well, I`m sure he doesn`t want to be you either. Two other big > title matches see Kalifornia Heat defend against the ever improving > All-Americans. And if we take form to mean anything, this should be a > pretty even match-up. We`ve got plenty of tough guys battling > tonight, and it`s going to be tough to fit them all in. But the > humdinger for tonight must be two undefeated wrestlers facing each > other. On the one side, we have Lord Byron, the technical genius and > the world champion. On the other, we have the dark side, > DeathBringer. 300plus pounds of invulnerability. This is going to be > yet another big challenge for the champ. > > XS: It doesn`t get easier the higher up you go. Lord Byron is facing > the toughest challenges ever. Deugau, Decan and now DeathBringer. And > should he survive these, Bane lies in wait, as does Max Doom and a > whole horde of other wrestlers all desperate for the title. > > Bob: We`ve got plenty of matches, but we`ve got little time. Let`s > go to the action. > > Dark matches: > Cold North decimated Craggy Island Warriors when Berg & Frost levelled > Father Dougal with a spike powerbomb. > > Nuttage defeated Deathjester by countout when he knocked DeathJester > off the top rope after being dazed by an airplane spin. > > "Wildone" Jack McCarthy got a victory over "Agonizer" Brent Grappler > when Kraken, who accompanied Wild-One,pulled Agonizer off the top rope > and to the outside ( a hard 10foot drop onto the steel steps), having > already knocked out and cut open Vamp with a camera. Agonizer was > doing very well, but got counted out due to Kraken`s interference. > > "Heavy Weapons" Max Doom made short work of Suna Gib when he finally > managed to get a hold of Gib, whipping him to the turnbuckle, and then > as he bounced out, grabbed him into the powerslam known as the > Doomsday Device, getting the three count > > ELEMENTAL FORCE (10)Vs. THE TERMINATORS (3) > Elemental Force made very short work of the Terminators, especially > since The Terminators seemed filled with infighting. An early > advantage was lost when Millan decided to do a test of strength with > Monolith, and lost. EF controlled the match from then on, and > finished it off by getting Millan with the cataclysm. After the > match, Paugh started yelling at Millan. Deugau arrives, with the rest > of the AC, and he grabs the mic. > > CHRIS: Alright, I've had enough of you two. Now, you haven't gotten > back to me, you haven't come to offical AC meetings, and you haven't > informed me what your plans are. So, I will use this coin, flip it. > Andrew, call it in the air. Loser is out. > > Then, Chris flips the coin. When it falls, Millan Rich bends over to > see it, and WHAM!, Deugau Drop Jawbreaker. Then, it's everyone on > Millan, as the AC kick, punch, and hit Millan with their finishers. > After a brutal beating, they leave, leaving Millan out cold of the > mat, and reportedly out of WAR! > > JUAN "FUR-KING" KERR (7) Vs. THE PUNISHER w/Crane(8) > Kerr wasn`t truly expecting the mammoth size of the Punisher, but it > didn`t deter him to begin with, using every low trick in the book to > get Punisher down and out. Punisher rallied back with crowd support > and set about devastating Kerr. Just as the Punisher seemed to have > the match won, Kerr coughed a mist into the Punisher`s face that > knocked the big-guy flat. Kerr covered arrogantly with 1 finger, but > didn`t get the three - Punisher`s manager, Crane, moved Punisher`s leg > onto the ropes. Kerr was furious and complained to the ref, but as he > did, Punisher got up and levelled him from behind with a big bulldog. > Then he took Kerr into the corner, and using his strength, picked him > up into position for the top rope piledriver he calls "The > Devastator". He got the three. MR: 7 MD: 10.32 Punisher by pinfall > > ARRON IRON (8) Vs. RANDY BLAKE (8) > Iron`s high-flying antics caused Blake trouble in the early going, but > Blake held on in and eventually brought the match to his technical > level. Iron, nearly getting pinned several times, fought his way back > back into the match, refusing to give in, and his determination paid > off, turning the match in a tough period with a DDT, getting the > pinfall. MR: 8 MD: 18.37 Iron by pinfall > > CRADLE OF FILTH (9) Vs. REIGN OF TERROR w/Chris Deugau (9) > Cradle of Filth - Harlem and Capone, the hottest tag team in WAR!, > continued their dazzling form, rocking Pierre & Lotine from the > turnbuckles and off the ropes. RoT kept in the match with some superb > textbook cheating and double team work, but all it took was a moment > of pure psychotic inspiration from Harlem to win the match. We was > whipped to the ropes by Pierre, but as he came off he leapt head-first > like a cannonball at Pierre`s face. The move smashed open Pierre`s > face and gave CoF the win. Not a pretty team by any means, and they > don`t care much for the fans, but they are clearly the most > entertaining team on the scene. MR: 8 MD: 16.22 CoF by pinfall > > Unified Title match > CHARLES MARTEL w/Venom (8) Vs. BRUTUS NOIR (8) > This match went pretty much as expected, with Noir emphasising his > brawling ability while Martel made use of his technical skills when > the opportunity arose. The strength of one caused big problems for > the other, and Noir looked close on several occassions to getting the > unified title. However, Randy Blake turned up at ringside and goaded > Noir out. Noir came after Blake, but Venom attacked him from behind. > Martel got the count-out victory while Blake & Venom laid the boot in. > MR: 8 MD: 13.11 Martel by countout > > INTERNATIONAL WAR! TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH > KALIFORNIA HEAT (10) Vs. > ALL-AMERICANS w/Ms.America & JJM (10) > This was a perfect match, fought with great heart by both teams, both > displaying the full extent of their abilities in a clean match, where > the fans were split right down the middle. This great match marked > the end of a great title reign by Kalifornia Heat when Brunswick > pinned Powers following "The Bomb" - A shooting star press at the end > of a breath-taking match. MR: 10 MD: 28.53 All-Americans by > pinfall > > WAR!LORD TITLE MATCH > AMERICAN PATRIOT (7) VS. T.J "THUNDER" DECAN (9) w/Flea > Decan was totally brutal in this match, with American Patriot finding > his technical skills counted for little against the new, full-on > Decan and the interference of Flea. It was ultimately the > interefence of Flea that saw the second title change of the evening, > as he pushed Patriot off the top rope and to the mat, allowing Decan > to pick him up and execute the Thunder Jam, from which there was no > escape. > MR: 8 MD: 12.04 Decan by pinfall > > WORLD WAR! TITLE MATCH > LORD BYRON (8) Vs. DEATHBRINGER (8) > Byron found DeathBringer an imposing opponent, yet continually when he > seemed close to being pinned, his quick-thinking pulled him back into > the match. For a long time it looked as though this was going to be > DeathBringers match, and on many occassions it nearly was, but Byron > proved his conditioning, and nearly had DeathBringer a few times with > some sudden pin attempts. The match ended in a time-limit draw. MR: 9 > MD: 30.00 Time-Limit Draw > > Bob: That`s it for this show folks. We`ll exceptionally pushed for > time. We`ll see you at WAR!CORE on Friday. > > ~~~~~ > (C) WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION
