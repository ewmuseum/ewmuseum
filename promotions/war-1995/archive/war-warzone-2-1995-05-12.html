WarZone 2 (Season 1) — Champion of War Title Tournament

------------------------------------------------------------------------------- WAR! FEDERATION PRESENTS WAR!ZONE 2 CHAMPION OF WAR TITLE TOURNAMENT WITH YOUR HOSTS AVOCADO BOB & DR.NATHANIEL DARKNESS LIVE FROM THE WAR!DATORIUM ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: Welcome one, welcome all. Tonight is the one you`ve all been waiting for. Tonight is the big one. Tonight is the tournament for the
Champion of War title. I`d like to make it clear that we have NO world title simply because, as yet, we are not big enough to have one. However, we are justified in an international title, and the new holder of that title is the man from Chicago, Illinois - JOHNNY THUNDER, which he won in an amazing match last night against Bludgeon.
Dr.Nathaniel Darkness: Amazing that he actually won if you ask me. But tonight he`ll be winning nothing as I introduce my new proteges, DeadPunk &
The InterDimensionalWarrior
Avocado Bob: How fitting that you manage Deadpunk Doc: You`ll be laughingon the other side of my lab when one of them wins the title. Bob: Well, Doc, to be honest, your man has got a tough draw now that the
Presient, General Andropov has allowed the tag champs into the tournament.
And lets face it, XL-9 has not been pinned yet - and I don`t think anyone can
Doc: If you cut him, he`ll bleed, and that`s all we need. I`ve prepared for every contingency.
Bob: And remember, they`re just two of the new entrants into WAR! We`ve had a recent import of several bizarre wrestlers - InnercityFirmer - a man from
UptonPark, London, England who is perhaps the strongest man I`ve ever seen, and I would say the most vicious, but we`ve got another new entrant - Brutus
Noir "The Sidewinder" from Marseilles, France, and he seems to have no technical skill. Sidewinder will be facing Big Jack Flash, a mountain of a man at 4`2" - yes, you heard me right - 4`2", and he claims to be 142 years old! See, no vertical discrimation in this fed.
Doc: Just sexual discrimination Bob: Yup, just sexual discrimination. And on that honest note, lets get the
16 man tournament underway.
------------------------------ Champion of War Tourney Round 1 15 minute time limit One Fall ------------------------------- DeadPunk w/ Dr.N.Darkness Vs. XL-9 XL-9, impervious to every blow by DeadPunk, retaliates only after Dr.Darkness interferes with some acid thrown onto XL-9`s face - but XL-9 just continues to wrestles. DeadPunk gets desperate and gets out his chains and starts to strangle XL-9 and promptly gets DQ`d at 4.12
InnerCityFirmer Vs. InterDimensional Warrior A spectacular entrance by IDW, with amazing glowing blue lights eminating in his trail, but ICF kicks IDW in the groin from the outset. IDW bends over in agony, allowing ICF to apply his double twisting piledriver to the outside of the ring followed by a guerrila press back into the ring. With IDW unconscious, ICF gets the pin at 1.24
MalteseFalcon Vs. The Fire Maltese Falcon doesn`t even make it to the ring 100%, attacked on his way to the ring by three masked opponents. Seth Blitzspear makes an appearence to clear the masked men, but the Falcon suffered many facial lacerations, his masked partially ripped, cloak stolen by the three masked men. In the ring,
The Fire, with Falcon coming down the aisle, appears to have the cape and promptly sets fire to it. Falcon runs into the ring, leaping over the ring apron. Furious with the events, he keeps The Fire down with high-risk moves.
Falcon goes for his "Blackbird" Flying splash, but the Fire is aware enough to move. The Maltese Falcon lies stunned, prompting The Fire to remove the bandages covering the lacerations and pour on salt he`d hidden in his trunks onto the wounds. The Maltese Falcon writhes in pain, but The Fire is DQ`d for his illegal tactics at 8.45
At the end of the match, the Masters of War run to the ring, spit on the fallen Falcon and say "we`ve often found the best communication is wounds, not words. Hahahahahaha"
Falcon is stretchered away. Brutus Noir "The Sidewinder" Vs. Big Jack Flash Big Jack Flash, outsized but not outcouraged. However he is outbrawled, enabling Noir to use one of his favourite moves - throwing his opponent into the crowd and letting his friends there beat his opponent up. Brutus Noir at deux minute vingt-cing.
DeathThreat Vs. Johnny Thunder Thunders title is not on the line, but both of their reputations are. With the Masters of War/Johnny Thunder feud intensifying with Thunders title
victory, the battle gets even intenser. Each move is countered, equalled and telegraphed. Near the end, the match devolves into a brawl outside the ring. Thunder gives DeathThreat a backbrainkick and enters the ring to do his moonsault finish, at which point the bell goes, DeathThreat counted out at
14.53 Massiveman Vs. FunkyMonkey Highrisk - high impact moves like that of FunkyMonkey have only a limited effect on the bigman, with Monkey eventually being squashed with a bodysplash
"The Massacre" - by Massiveman at 6.51
Bludgeon Vs. TrailBoss Mike Dallas w/Mr.Kang Trailboss dominates the match, resulting in a pin following a suplex by the large "Trailboss" Mike Dallas, establishing that he can actually wrestle at
13.26. An enraged Bludgeon screams at the end of the match "I`ll get you
Trailboss, I`ll get you. Watch your back boy - watch your back".
Seth Blitzspear Vs. Classico Seth & Classico, both enemies of the Masters of War, spare no punches in this battle. Recovering somehow from Classico`s symphonyslam, Seth applies his
"Flying time" backdrop to get a countout victory over the impressive Classico at 10.30
----------------------------------------------------------------- Champion of War Tourney QuarterFinals 30 minute time limit One Fall ---------------------------------------------------------------- XL-9 vs. TrailBoss Mike Dallas Tag-team champions, but also enemies. These two now fight each other. Or rather, XL-9 beats up on the 7`2" Trailboss rather easily, using a running neckbreaker to gain the pin at 3.52
Seth Blitzspear Vs. Brutus "The Sidewinder"Noir Two blatant brawlers square off, but Noir`s pure viciousness seems to overcome
Seth. Rallying with several clotheslines, his momentum is broken with a backdrop by Noir. Noir then appplies the Marseilles Strangler hold, nearly breaking Seth`s neck, and definitely gaining the victory at 9.44
Johnny Thunder Vs. The Maltese Falcon The Falcon, still disorientated from the Masters of War and their attack on him, can not use his agility sufficiently to gain a victory over the
International Champion of War, Johnny Thunder, with Thunder outwrestling
Falcon for a submission at 8.25 with a boston crab
InnerCityFirmer Vs. Massive Man ICFirmers tactics were to brawl with the Massiveman outside the ring, using chairs,tables& cables to beat up on Massiveman before the bel was wrong. Back in the ring, Massive recovers, but despite his size, this British born soccer thug, unlike his team WestHam United, gets thru to the semi-finals with his
IronHammer special move - a doube twisting piledriver outside the ring and a guerilla press back into the ring.
------------------------------------------------------------ Champion of War Touney Semi-Finals 45 minute time limit One Fall ------------------------------------------------------------ Inner City Firmer vs. XL-9 XL-9 & Firmer battle, and ICF seems to cause XL-9 damage with his Iron Hammer move. However XL-9 kicks out of the pin on a 2, clotheslines an upright ICF and runs off down the aisle, repeating "Malfunction..malfunction...mal.." ICF with a countout victory at 8.54
Johnny Thunder Vs. Brutus "The SideWinder" Noir Noir`s aggressiveness increases with each match, and his blatant disrespect for the rules somehow provides him with a victory over the honest Thunder, using blatant chokeholds to wear Thunder down, finishing him off with his
"Marseilles Strangler" at 8.42
-------------------------------------------------- Champion of War Title Tourney No-rules No-timelimit One fall Inner City Firmer Vs. Brutus "The Sidewinder" Noir Fists, eye gouges, chokeholds, ball crushing, face slams , anything and everything that could happen between the two ultimate brawlers. ICF`s actions seem to cause the most damage, but when Noir successfully applies his
"Marseilles Strangler" he captures the title, even if not the hearts of the people. The brawl ends at 17.55 and at the point, Brutus "The Sidewinder"
Noir`s reign as champion of WAR! begins. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Bob: I wasn`t expecting two brawlers in the final - and two new WAR! entrants at that. Doc: It looked to me as though Noir`s dockside brawling experience paid off, dodging very well the powerful fists of ICF.
Bob: And more dispicable acts by the Masters of War.. Doc: Oh, just leave them alone. They`re doing a job, it`s as simple as that. Whatever it takes to win. Bob: Well, they haven`t won anything yet, but that may change at WAR!REPORT
