e-wrestling federation of that name. That is, of course, the dictionary`s definition. Here is mine... It is, like all other e-feds, a federation where you enter characters and try to guide them to success. Success, of course, depends on what your goals are. Some may call WAR! a `booked' e-wrestling fed, but in fact, it uses the Human Judgement System, which will be described later. WAR! is as much about the battles outside the ring as it is inside it. In a way, it is very much a soap-opera that depends on your ability to be your character. It is the emphasis on the characterisation of each wrestler that distinguishes WAR! from other feds (or at least it used to!). Most importantly though, WAR! is exciting, fun and dangerous...

Let it be your driving purpose. Without it, your wrestler will not have purpose. Whether the goal is to win the World title or to injure as many people as possible, or something more imaginative, it will be a lot more interesting for you.
As I said earlier, this is a roleplaying fed. This means you play the role of your wrestler. In War! you develop their character, their attitude, their whole approach. Often, you will be required to respond to a claim or an accusation against your wrestler, or you may be making that claim. The important thing to remember though is that your character isn`t just a fighting machine. The character is supposed to have a personality and a life as well,which is created by relationships with other characters, whether negative or positive. It may also require you to be devious.
Think of an angle, an approach orjust something you can do that might make an interesting story or plot. This is what makes War! entertaining, and it is supposed to beprimarily entertainment. It can also make things a whole lot more interesting. These plans etc. may require you to team up or plan with other players. Don`t be afraid to do so. And don`t be afraid to create alliances with people who aren`t of the same face/heel alignment as you. Why? Because the world ain`t so black & white.
The truth is, there is no good or evil, just what things people are for or against, and what their position is on things. Face/Heel simply refers to relationship with fans. Its very plausible for two wrestlers to be great friends despite being opposities in terms of fan affections. And its also why people of the same fan alignement will fedud. Remember, face/heel is almost irrelevant. If you stick to
that as a basis for your character, you`re going to get nowhere. For example, other areas of tension may be pro/anti America, pro/anti wrestler X, pro/anti brawling etc. Of course, if you`re a face and you thrive on fan support, you need to be careful who you ally yourself with. Also, if someone makes claims, or does something that has repurcussions on your character, you don`t just have to say `no'.
Instead, play along with it, develop it, make something happen out of it. The more history there is to your character that there is, the more you develop the personality, the more real they become. Those of you who have done this will know what I mean...
Questions to ask about your wrestler. Why did he become a wrestler? What is he like? What does he like? What does he do in his spare time? Who are his friends? Where is he from and what is his relationship to that place? What are his motives? Who is he outisde of the wrestling ring? What is his life like outside of wrestling?
Married, single, kids? What? ~~~~~~ Flashes ~~~~~~ Flashes are the dialogue sent out by your wrestler, in character. Flashes are the half of this game that the players, and not the fedhead do. WAR! is an O.F.S - Open Flash System. A Closed Flash
System is one where you send the messages to the fedhead who sends them out edited together to everyone else. O.F.S means each message gets sent out individually. This may mean a lot of flashes. Some like it, some don`t. Simple as that. But O.F.S means that your message will definitely get out. However, if you don`t have access to a distribution list, send your flash to someone who has, and if you ask nicely, they may do it for you. Flashes are important in several
ways. First of all, they`re entertaining and informative. Secondly, they`re very important in creating hype. Hype is very important because it draws more attention to a match. Also, a hyped up match is more likely to get better commentary. Furthermore, it will bring more attention to your wrestler, and they may be more in the minds of fedheads for titleshots than the quieter wrestlers.
I feel it is important to note, after noticing the debates on RSPWF in late October 1996, that WAR! is not a federation where the person who flashes the most wins. WAR! uses the Human Judgement System, and therefore takes into account all factors.
Below are some of the things I give thumbs up to when people do flashes, though these aren`t necessary. They`re just guidelines. 1 - In the subject header, putting WAR! in brackets i.e. (WAR). This way, it clarified for people in other feds as well what fed the flash relates to.
2 - Putting in a relevant subject header. Don`t just do "Re: War!Card 5". Try instead "(WAR) X comments on War!Card". Putting in a subject header really helps things out. 3 - When possible, try to summarise a flash you are responding to, rather than including it, because this takes up more mailspace.
Understandably, some people may prefer to comment on each line, but when possible, try and summarise. It will also grab the interest of
non-related readers more. 4 - When typing out the flash, try and get in character. Be the character, not yourself. Think the characters thoughts, motivations and feelings. Also, consider the surroundings etc. Be descriptive, set the scene, if it is necessary.
5 - Occasinally, put a flash or an interview on the newsgroup, because this helps hype the fed : )
6 - Face2Face interviews. E-mail the person you`re feuding with and construct a head to head flash, and then send it to everyone else.
This is a really effective form of flash. Things I give the thumbs down to.... 1 - Slagging off other players. This is a total NO-NO! If you have any problems with another player, go to the fedhead first.
2 - Overuse of strong language. Swearing is acceptable to a degree, but try and keep it down...
3 - Commenting on peoples writing/spelling in a negative manner. This is basically bad netiquette anyhow. Saying someones spelling is bad is like laughing at someones speach impediment. Its basically rude, and the important thing is that they are participating.
Negative comments just deter people from playing, and that is a big NO-NO! 4 - Rascism. Whether in character or not, rascism is despicable. ~~~~~ Cards ~~~~~ War! Wrestling cards used to be consistently twice a week. Now they come along when they come along which is generally every 4-5 days. To fill in the gaps inbetween, there are War! Arena cards which give simple results. We try to make sure every player is involved in a card each week. War! Cards come under several names, but names don`t really matter. They happen, okay? If a card is late, you just have to remember that the fedhead is doing this in their spare time as it is. As for supercards, every card is a supercard, but occasionally there will be special cards.
On each card, you can do just about anything. All you have to do is tell the fedhead for that card what you want to do, whether it is tactics, or jumping someone or an interview or whatever.
Do whatever it takes to be entertaining. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Points System ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ New from October 1996, the War!Points system. It`s quite simple. If you win by pinfall or submission, you win 25 points If you lose by pinfall or submission, you lose 25 points If you win by countout or DQ, you win 10 points If you lose by countout or DQ, you lose 10 points. Speciality matches, such as Battle Royales, will have their own rewards. The War!Ranking system is split into 3 tables. The first table is everyone ranked 1-10. The second table is everyone ranked 11-20.
The third table is everyone ranked 21 or lower. If you beat someone from one table above, the points gained are worth double (i.e. you pin someone ranked 3rd, and you`re 17th, you get
50pts instead of 25.) If you beat someone two tables above you, the points are tripled ( No.25 beats NO.4 by DQ, they get 30pts instead of 10, or 75 pts instead of 25.) Bonuses for winning titles When you win a title, you immediately get bonus points World War Title - 50pts International War Title - 40 pts Liberty Title - 30 pts War!Lord Title - 20 pts Unified Title - 10 pts World War! Tag title - 50 pts International War! tag title - 50 pts So theoretically, if someone from table 3 beats the world champ, in table 1, that person could gain 125pts!
~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Match Resolution ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Battles in WAR! are resolved using the fastest, most accurate computers on the planet - the human brain! The brain can take into account more factors, especially the qualitative ones such as intensity of match etc. that a computer. And that is exactly what the fedheads do. They take into account the characters stats, their form and a hundred other factors, and then judge how the match would go.
HJS is unlike booking in that it is more rationalised. Its also quicker for the fedheads who have to spend ages typing the cards, and can often create more exciting matches than any computer simulator.
If you have any specific tactics for a certain match, tell the fedhead for that card. ~~~~~~~ Fedheads ~~~~~~~ The War! co-fedheads are as independant as possible so that for 4 out of 5 cards, the fedheads are players and can participate in the fun of
War! Each fedhead is responsible for their card and judging the outcome. There is conferring on certain things, but not much. The fedhead also acts as GM as well as match writer for a card.
