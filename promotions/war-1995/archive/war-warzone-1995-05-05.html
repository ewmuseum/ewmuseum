WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WarZone 1

5 May 1995

------------------------------------------- WAR! Federation presents WAR!ZONE 1 Hosted by: Avocado Bob Toxic Shock ----------------------------------------- Avacado Bob: Ladies & Gentlemen, do we have an exciting debut show for you tonight. We are of course live at the WAR!DATORIUM, and as you can hear from the near-capacity crowd, they`re almost as excited about tonight as I am - and you`d have to be a horse-eating, wolf-whistling, pig-slaughtering son of a duck to be that! For tonight we have FOUR deubts by wrestlers who are going to turn this federation into a full scale war. Furthermore, we have our
"Partners for War" tag team tournament to decide who will be the tag-team champions - the first championship to be decided at WAR! Tox, explain the rules.
ToxicShock: Quite simply Bob, The federations top 16`s wrestlers are all entered into a draw. Each wrestler is drawn a partner, and they go on to fight for the tag-titles together.
Avacado: What an exciting prospect. We could see arch-enemies Classico &
Bludgeon on the same team.
ToxicShock: And despite their differences, what a team that would be if Classico wasn`t still recovering from the fireball incident which the Masters of Cheating conducted at WAR!REPORT just before the Classico-Bludgeon
"factory" match. AB: A sickening display of brutality, but something that we`ve come to expect from the Masters of War.
ToxicShock: Back to the tag tourney, it is possible that Deavastating duo may be drawn against each other, or the two rookies "sunny" sam sefton & jack
"scratch it" mcCain could be on the same side against the experienced BadTimes
Billy & DeathThreat.
AB: The possibilities are endless, but this show isn`t, so lets head right now for the debut of.....SETH BLITZSPEAR.
Opening bout: Seth Blitzspear Vs. Bash The energetic Blitzspear storms the ring, the crowd go wild and have definitely found a psycho they can love. Bash is prepared and pummels
Blitzspear with Ax-handles, forcing him to the ground. Blitzspear gets locked in a chicken-wing and then Bash realises that he can`t wrestle, so
starts to pummel Blitzspear again. The punishment continues, but eventually Blitzspear rallies to the cheers of the crowd. After several clotheslines, slams and suplexes, Blitzspear gets risky...he climbs to the top rope...and he connects...But Blitzspear seems hurt...he`s injured himself on the fall...he`s holding his knee...he`s in agony. Bash recovers and notices the injury. He applies a figure four leglock, but somehow Blitzspear escapes.
Commentory Avocado Bob : How did he escape from that? ToxicShock: I don`t know, but Bash is about to throw him to the ropes.. AB: But Blitzspear reverses it, Bash has gone to the ropes..and it`s an amazing backdrop over the top rope by BLitzspear - a move he calls "Flying
Time", sending Bash out onto the iron railings - ouch, that hurt.But
Blitzspear has collapsed on the floor. Tox: But take more notice of the Bash outside the ring...he`s got counted out. AB: An impressive debut for Blitzspear, lets just hope he`s back in time for the tagtourney.
The "Partners of War" Quarter-Final Draw --------------------------------------------------------------------- Bludgeon & DeathThreat Vs. Jack "Scratch it" McCain & Seth Blitzspear 20,000 volts & Bash Vs. Crash & The Fire XL-9 & "Trailboss" Mike Dallas Vs. Classico & Badtimes Billy Johnny Thunder & Morgue Vs. MassiveMan & Architisman Summary of Quarter Finals Bludgeon & DeathThreat Vs. Jack "Scratch it" McCain & Seth Blitzspear. The unfit "Scratch it", as popular as he is, falls to Bludgeon`s powerbomb at
14.43 eliminating him & Blitzspear. Blitzspeat held his own for the first 10 minutes, but had to tag out eventually, bringing in Scrath it. One half of the Masters of War continues to the semis.
20,000 Volts & Bash Vs. Crash & The Fire Bash and Crash are the first in and happily pound on each other. The brawl goes outside and both teams are counted out at 1.42
XL-9 & Trailboss Vs. Classico & BadTimes Billy XL-9, the self proclaimed robot, enters the ring, repeating "Destroy, Destroy". And he does just that, DDTing Classico and following up with a powerbomb after only 0.57 minutes. And if you think BadTimes would help him, you`ve got another thing coming.
Johhny Thunder & Morgue Vs. MassiveMan & ArchitisMan A match pitting two faces vs. a face & a heal. Morgue & Massiveman, both members of FourSeasons, cleanly wrestle each other from the onset. Moves, counter moves, escapes & more continues for 15minutes until ArchitisMan gets bored. He screams at his partner to tag, but Massive just lets Morgue out of a headlock to tag to the 240lb Johnny Thunder. Thunder tries several
Dropkicks on the over 7ft tall MassiveMan which have little effect. Thunder then tries a flyingcross body, but gets caught and is powerslammed by MassiveMan. Archtisman continues to scream for a tag. Massiveman, waits for
Thunder to get up, and then tags ArchitisMan - reluctantly. Architisman charges towards Thunder, but Thunder connects with a backbrain kick & then a moonsault - completing his "Thunder & Lightning" move and progressing to the next round at the 20 minute mark.
Semi-Finals Bludgeon & Death Threat Vs. XL-9 & Trailboss MikeDallas Johnny Thunder & Morgue get a bye. Debut Match Johnny Thunder Vs. Architisman Both wrestlers fairly fresh from the tag-match, but Architisman more cautious in his approach. The match is fast & furious, with Thunder getting the better of ArchitisMan and finishing him off with his "Thunder & Lightning" combo at
10.52 SemiFinals XL-9 & TrailBoss Mike Dallas Vs. Bludgeon & DeathThreat XL-9 approaches the ring with Dallas again. Dallas, pissed off with not wrestling the last match, uses his 7ft 2" frame to try to get a match. XL-9,
6 inches shorter, grabs trailboss by the neck, hoists him from the ground and throatslams him over the top rope to the outside. Trailboss falls onto a table and breaks it in half. Medics please. Bludgeon & DeathThreat try to double team XL-9, and for a while it works, but eventually the ref, "Loud"
Lars Bonny, gets DeathThreat back to his corner. Bludgeon continues a relentless assault on XL-9, and after 3 DDT`s, 5 piledrivers and a powerbomb, feels he`s done enough to go for a pin. XL-9 kicks out on a one-count, scoops
Bludgeon up and delivers the most vicious powerbomb ever seen. "Destroy" he says, and destroy he does. DeathThreat thinks to help his partner, but thinks again, seeing the cold, maniacal look of XL-9 eyes. XL-9 & an injured
Trailboss make it through to meet Thunder & Morgue in the final. "Trailboss" Mike Dallas was scheduled to fight, but on advice by his manager
Mr.Kang & also the local Doctor, Dr. Whoops, pulls out. Winner by default:
Roc Hardy. Partners of War Tag-team tourney final -------------------------------------------------------------- XL-9 (without Trailboss Mike Dallas) vs. Johnny Thunder & Morgue The speed of Thunder is effective, delivering hit & run blows to the darkgrey costumed wrestler known as XL-9, but no pain is registered. Thunder even delivers a flying clothesline to put XL-9 on the mat and then connects with a flying leg-drop. WAR!`s resident high flyer could only achieve a 2-count before XL-9 kicks out, saying to Thunder "I am now going into superdestruct mode". Thunder, tired, tags Morgue, but Morgue is immediately hit with a lariat that causes the big-man to go spinnig. XL-9 follows up with an atomic drop, a piledriver, and then a sleeper,which Morgue cannot escape from.
The winners of the match, and the Tag-team champions of WAR! are: XL-9 & Trailboss Mike Dallas AB: What do you make of that evening Tox? Tox: Personally, I`m glad XL-9 won the tag-title, because if he was in for the Champion of War title, I really believe he`d win it hands down. He seems to feel no pain and has power beyond belief. He beat some of the very best wrestlers in the world today, and he beat them easily. Whether he thinks he`s a robot or not, he`s one half of the tag-team champions along with TrailBoss
Mike Dallas, but I think Trailboss is going to be out for a short while.
AB: I`m pretty sure Trailboss won`t want toquibble with XL-9 again. Tox: I`m pretty sure no-one will want to quibble with XL-9 again. AB: And we saw some good debuts today as well, didn`t we? Tox: Well, what we saw of Trailboss was fairly competent for a guy 7`2", but the two that impressed me were Blitzspear & Thunder - two exciting wreslters that this federation has been craving for a long time.
AB: I`m afraid we`ll have to stop there, but I`d like to remind you all that on WAR!REPORT 2 there will be the International WAR! title up for grabs. So if there are any wannabe wrestlers out there, get your entries in before next
Tuesday. So for me & Tox, I give a big thank you and wish you all the best...until next time, I`m Avocado Bob, and you`ve been watching WAR!
