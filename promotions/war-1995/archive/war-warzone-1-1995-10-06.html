WarZone 1 (Season 2)

SUBJECT: WarZone 1 DATE: Fri, 06 Oct 95 15:57:44 GMT WRESTLING UNLIMITED PRESENTS....... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WARZONE 1 Live from the Warena, New York With your everpresent hosts "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Avocado Bob: Welcome one & welcome all, ladies and gentlemen, to what could possibly be seen as the greatest result of legal wrangling ever in the history of wrestling federations. With the aid of some cash and incriminating photographs, the W-Files - The FBI's Criminal wrestling investigation service, has allowed WAR to come back to the people. They said we had to cut down the violence, the maniacs, the blood. We said -where's the fun in that? They said we were to set an example, we said " well what the hell is wrong with a few broken bones and long worn grudges. Let's not kid ourselves, you're all here to watch somebody get maimed, and by god we are going to provide that, because that is what we are all about - entertainment.
As you can all see, I have a new co-host, the legendary "Bullfrog" Eric
Helmroy. Well, he's famous if you lived in Northern Dakota around the sixties...
Bullfrog: ...and early seventies Bob. Glad to be here by the way. I worked a lot behind the scenes last time round, but now I'm getting this excellent ringside seat - and being paid for it. Soem amazing talent is on show here, especially the young champion Seth Blitzspear. His quick reapplication ensured he held onto his world war championship, but he'll face a stiff challenge today from "The Economist" Randy Blake, whom I believe held the
International War Title just before War finished.
Bob: And you're views on who will triumph. Bullfrog: I've gotta go for Blake. Seths too young and too nice. Blakes had three months to regroup, and I think now could be the chance for him to capture this major championship. Also, with Ms.Paula at ringside, that fool
Seth Blitzspear, no matter how hot he is, does not stand a chance.
Bob: They've met before and Seth has triumphed. We'll just have to wait and see what happens tonight. But also happening tonight is the World War Tag
Team Championship Quarter Finals featuring new teams Ghetto Boys, Hot Stuff,
Spirit of `76 and Wolfpack, as well as some old blood like Canadian Carnage, who I think failed to win a title last time round.
Bullfrog: I like the look of Canadian Carnage, but it is the team of Bombsquad and Bladerunner that I feel will be victorious. Ever heard the saying "too big to be beat?".
BOb: Actually no, I haven't. But speaking of too big to beat, the Liberty title is up for grabs today with two lightweights - Joey the Clown and The
Mandarin competing for it. Also on view today will be OG Gary Trent facing
'RumbleFish' and Jerry "Goon" Gordon meeting Booster Dunne. Who will win, who will lose. I don't know who, so stop looking at me.....
Liberty Title ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Mandarin Vs. Joey the Clown Bob: Here it is, the first match of WAR in over three months. Both starting off cautiously...locking up...and mandarin gets Joey in an armbar straight away...and pounds on it with vicious chops..
Bfrog: Notice how mandarin is twisting the arm to get maximum pain. Bob: He certainly seems to know what he is doing..but that's why he's competing in War. Clown down, Mandarin applying chinlock..Joey powers out..suplex by Joey..slam..slam..to the top rope..but Master Po knocks the popular clown off the top and Clown falls heavily to the ground...Mandarin comes ouside and rams Clown into the post...it's not looking good..we'll be back after this break...
After the ads... Bob: Things havem't got better for Joey in these last four minutes..Mandarin whips Joey to the turnbuckle...Joey dodges..mandarin stunned and Joey goes for the inside cradle..he's got him, but where's the ref?
Bfrog: Ha ha, smart move by Master Po - he's distracted the referee.... Bob: Joey goes over to the ref to get the count...watch out Joey, Mandarin is up..and there is his "Taifu" combo - the backflip followed by a double kick and then the frankensteiner...this is a true injustice...
Bfrog: This is wrestling.. Bob: Maybe it's your way. I'm disgusted though, and I'm sure the fans are too. The Mandarin has just got the three count and is the new Liberty
Champion. He may have dominated the match, but Joey, in my eyes, was the first to get a three count. This is outrageous.
Matchend: 7:40 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War Tag Team Championship Quarter Finals (Summary)(Numbers indicate percentage of match domination)
Bombsquad & Bladerunner (63) Vs. Spirit of 76 (37) Bombsquad & Bladerunner just oustsize and outfight the blue clad Americus &
Independece, with Bombsquad finishing Americus off with a powebomb at 8:54
Ghetto Boyz (65) Vs. Blood Street Blothers (35) A bloody match indeed, but the fan favourite team of Folk Curtis Fredrick and
Big Daddy Vicelord get the DQ victory over Marlon & Chad when Marlon & Chad tried using a steel chair at 5:21
Terry Bronze (02) & Hans Rews Vs. Hot Stuff (98) Hot Stuff - Cinder & Inferno, make short work of preliminary team Terry
Bronze & Hans Rews. Match end 2:36
Canadian Carnage (84) Vs. Reece Wills & Tu-fao-noa (16) Canadian Carnage are caught off guard early by this prelim team, but quickly recover and Johnny Canuck finishes Wills with a flying clothesline at 4.32
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ OG Gary Trent Vs RumbleFish Bob: I'm sure looking forward to the debut of the 6'6", nearly 300lb Rumblefish. OG hasn't got the best track record in WAR, but with some most needed expereince picked up, he should stand a good chance...
Bfrog: That's because you haven't seen Rumblefish yet. He's an experienced wrestler, and boy has he got a suprise.
Bob: Well, here comes OG Gary Trent now down the aisle....and that must be
RumbleFish running towards him from behind....with a barbwire baseball bat..Trent doesn't see it..
Bfrog: And all we're seeing now is blood! Look at OG bleed! Bob: Here comes new security official "American" Butch Williams to stop
Rumblefish...but he's receiving the same treatment...
Bfrog: Williams bleeds even better! Bob: The ex-international war champion is down...and here comes Ghetto Boyz,
OG`s posse, to help OG out...Rumblefish swipes at them then runs....this is horrific...
Bfrog: This is war. Winner by DQ: OG Gary Trent ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BoosterDunne Vs. Jerry "Goon" Gordan" Boosters new attitude adds a new dimension to his game. Goon, perhaps not experienced enough, tries for a quick DDT but is instead hoisted over by
Dunne. Dunne continues his assault, finishing a series of moves with a side suplex. Goon kicks out of the pin but is knocked down again by a clothesline. Dunne uses a bulldog and then follows with his"Booster Launch"
Flying Elbow Drop. Goon moves and goes to cover Dunne. Dunne gets his foot on the rope. Goon picks Dunne up and replies with a series of kicks, followed by a dropkick, sending Dunne outside the ring. Goon follows and they brawl, but both fail to get back in the ring before the count-out.
Result: Double count-out at 9.09 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War Championship Seth Blitzspear Vs. "The Economist" Randy Blake Blitzspear starts the match with a blitzing assault on Blake. Blake, stunned by this, reverses the tide of the battle by backdropping a rampaging
Blitzspear over the top rope. Outside the ring, Blake assaults Blitzspear with various objects, as does Ms.Paula. Blake keeps on breaking the count, thus wearing Seth down further. Inside the ring, Seth is almost unconcious.
Blake applies three "Recession" suplex's. The crowd rally behind Seth though, and somehow he manages to kick out. He gets up, hurls Blake into the corner and steams forward with a clothesline, but again Blake backdrops Seth over..and onto the railings. Blake stands in the centre of the ring as the referee counts Seth out.
Verdict: Victory for Blake at 6.45, but Seth keeps the title. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Well, an interesting card. We'll see you soon for WarReport 1. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Jacob "jake" Kane & [name withheld] CURRENTLY EXISTING INDEPENDENTLY OF REALITY
