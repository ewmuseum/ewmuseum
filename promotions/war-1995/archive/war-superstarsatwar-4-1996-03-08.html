Superstars At War IV — The Killing Fields

WRESTLING UNLIMITED presents.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION`S SUPERSTARS AT WAR IV - THE KILLING FIELDS LIVE FROM MADISON SQUARE GARDENS With your alienaring hosts, “Avocado” Bob Whitely & Rick “Ricky” Soal ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The show opens with interviews on THE TOXIC PIT.... (The camera cuts to the Toxic Pit, which is filled with a green and red looking fog (fantastic what neat effects you can get with a fogmachine and a couple of coloured spotlights. <g>). After a while the fog settles at groundlevel, revealing a man wearing the usual WAR! broadcaster's clothes and a gasmask. He rips the mask off, revealing himself to be
Toxic Shock, and tosses it into the crowd which roars in approval.)
[Toxic] HELLOOOOOO WAR! Are you all READY for the Killingfield even later tonight? (The crowd goes bananas, but ToxicShock brings a hand to his ear as if he can't hear anything)
[Toxic] I can't HEAAAAAR YOUUUUUUU!!! Are you READY FOR THE KILLINGFIELD? (The crowd goes nuts, but ToxicShock still acts as if he can't hear them.) [Toxic] WHAT? What is this? Did you all go and DIE on me or something? I ASKED YOU
IF YOU ARE READY FOR THE OVER THE TOP ROPE, NO HOLDS BARRED, PLAIN-OUT WAR
THAT WILL BE THE KILLINGFIELD TONIGHT??? (The crowd goes completely apeshit. The noise is almost deafening, and
ToxicShock finally seems satisfied.)
[Toxic] Well, well, well. Now thaaaat's better. There are other matches tonight though. Like the World WAR! titlematch! Ladies and gentlemen! Here's the man who will challenge the big Iceman for that title! The Cardinal, Andrea
MANCIIIIINIIIII!! (There is a loud crack of thunder and another large emmision of fog which floods the pit. As it begins to disperse we see the large silouette of Andrea Mancini. There are a few muted boos and cheers from the crowd which are silenced when Mancini glares at them and beckons his acolytes, who have been sculking in the background forward. Instead of his usual scarlet garb he is clad in all white robes with red trim. He begins to slowly stride forward towards Toxic
Shock)
[Toxic] Whooa, like the new gear. I guess that just being a Cardinal isn't good enough for you. You now have to dress like one of THE Cardinals.
(The crowd laugh until Mancini glowers at them and they quickly stop) [Mancini] Shut up you pathetic man. I care not one jot about your football "games" [Toxic] Sorry you took offence big man. Well on with business. Tonight you have a world title shot against the man mountain Stein "Ice"
Berg. How can you defeat a wrestler of that size and strength ? [Mancini] I have handled oafs like Mr. Berg many times before. I am unpinned in this federation and shall remain so after this match. I do not need illegal muscle building drugs like he does. I have the Lords strength.
[Toxic] Now hang on there a minute thats a very strong allegation. Do you have anything to back it up ? I know theres no love lost between you but you can't say that without proof.
[Mancini] I'd have thought it was obvious. The man's an imbecile and he thinks he can get away with anything. He really is stupid, he can hardly string two words together. Stupidity is a crime against the Lord and for that I will punish him..........
[Toxic] Well, Cardinal. I have a little surprise for you tonight. Actually, it's a rather big surprise....
(Toxic Shock pauses to see what Mancini's reaction is.) [Mancini] Nothing that YOU have could surprise me (Toxic grins and shrugs.) [Toxic] LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! The undisputed WORLD WAR! CHAMPION! Stein...Ice...BEEEEEEEEERG!! (The huge Norwegian lumbers up to the pit. His 7'2", 430 pound frame rippling with muscle. He is wearing his wrestling attire, a pair of white wrestling shorts and white boots, and his white hair is cut in a military crewcut style. Across his huge chest the word ICECOLD is tattooed in black, gothic letters. He steps onto the Pit set, positively dwarfing
ToxicShock. Even Mancini, a big man in his own right, is quite a bit smaller than Berg. As he comes face to face with the Cardinal he simply stops and stares coldly at him. The Cardinal appears to flinch at first but when his acolytes come up beside him he stares right back and grins an evil grin.)
[Toxic] Now, Ice, later on tonight you two will meet in the squared circle. Do ya have anything to say to the Cardinal, or the fans?
(Berg stands still for a while, seemingly not about to say anything. Then finally his deep voice rumbles out as he stares straight into the
Cardinal's eyes, not even acknowledging ToxicShock being there at all.) [Berg] Mancini....Fight clean.....Or pay the price. (Mancini laughs evilly (like the laugh at the end of Michael Jacksons "Thriller") [Mancini] I fear you not Mr. Berg. I fear only one thing and that is the being that will give me the strength to make you repent your sins and cleanse your soul. I will destroy your life as you know it.
(Berg looks completely unmoved by Mancini's words. Not even a flicker of emotion shows on his face as he stands face to face with the Cardinal.
Toxic Shock stands to the side, seemingly completely forgotten and ignored by the two of them.)
[Berg] Nor do I fear you. Your allegations of steroid abuse mean nothing to me. I am tested regularly for illegal substances, like everyone else in WAR! Perhaps even more so due to having this belt. Your attempts at insulting my intelligence mean nothing to me. You, Mancini, are nothing but another rulebreaker, and rulebreakers will be punished. You say you will make me repent my sins? Remember that Reverand Death already tried that...and failed.
[Mancini] You will regret those words Mr. Berg. Remember them as I make you beg for mercy and you feel your soul being cleansed of the evil that inhabits it you despicable toad.
(Slowly "Ice" raises his hand and grabs the Cardinal by the robes. Both stare unflinchingly into each others eyes. Toxic stands behind them, almost hidden by their bulk and completely ignored as they glare at eachother. Then out of the Toxic Pit, a dense smoke rises, and when it is clear only Toxic Shock is left.)
