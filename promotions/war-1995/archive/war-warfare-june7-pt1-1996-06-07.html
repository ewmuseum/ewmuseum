WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

War!Fare — 7 June 1996 (Part 1)

7 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*************************************************************************** ** WAR!Fare - June 7, 1996 *************************************************************************** ** [Scenes of WAR! superstars] Tonnage crushes American Patriot...Lord Byron with the Aristoclutch on the
Reckoner...Ms.Paula with Booster Dunne and Randy Blake...Doug Simpkins and the Jammer exchanging blows...DeathJester flying through the air...the Lost
Boys and Wendy holding the tag team belts....Suna Gib admiring Bane...Seth
Blitzspear running to the ring...
------------------ (The scene switches to the Toxic Pit, and Toxic Shock, who is standing next to it, grinning..) Tox: Everybody!! WELCOME TO WAR!FARE!!!! Crowd: Pop X 10000000000000000000000000000!!!! Tox: I........CAN'T..........HEAR...............YOU!!!!!! Crowd: Pop X 1000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000!!!!! Tox: That's better!! Now, I'd like you all to give a _typical_ warm
Toxic Pit welcome for my next guest, (Toxic grins) the current WAR! Lord, Loooorrrd Byyyyyyyyrrrrron!!! Crowd: Lord Whiner! Lord Whiner! Lord Whiner! Lord Whiner! etc.etc. (Intermezzo from Karelia Suite blasts out over the p.a, and Byron, looking as smug as ever, walks out onto the set, the gleaming WAR! Lord belt across his shoulder...)
Crowd: Boo X 10000000000000000000000000000000000000000!!!!!!! (Byron looks up at the ranks of fans with one eyebrow raised, but turns and walks up to Toxic Shock without comment.)
Tox: Welcome once again, Mr. Byron.... LB (yawning): Sir.... Tox: O....K...., now, erm, sir, there's been a few rumours spreading around, that your not exactly who you say you are.....
(One young fan in the crowd yells the name out, and soon the entire crowd are chanting it....)
Crowd: Jack McMurphy!! Jack McMurphy!! LB (looking visibly unimpressed): Mr. Toxic, you and I both know what a snake this man Butch Williams is....(the crowd boo Byron even more loudly, he just raises his voice.).....he was the one who turned on his own brother, (sneer) the one who set up
Tyler Stevens, the one whose voice could never be wholly trusted. We all know _exactly_ what kind of man he is. Tox: Yeah, not too dissimilar from yourself. Sir. LB (glares at Tox briefly.): Correct, but with one important difference: I am nobility. Tox: Butch gets the chance to prove his case tonight.... LB (sneering): The only thing he's going to prove is what we knew all along. That he is a good for nothing, prolaterian imbecile. Mr. Williams, I hope you have a
_very_ good insurance policy, because you are going to need one. Trust me on this. Ciao.
(Byrons walks back to the dressing room area, followed by a barrage of jeers from the fans.)
Tox: No doubt Butch Williams shares similar thoughts. Back to you...... ----------------- Dave Scott: Welcome, WAR! fans to WAR!fare!!! We've got a big show tonight. There will be three titles defended tonight, as well as a triangle match to determine the WAR-RAW Interfederational Championship!
Tony J: It should be something to finally see what all these WAR! guys can do. We've followed the WXW wrestlers for a while. I want to see how they stack up.
DS: There's a sellout crowd here at what was once known as the WXW Arena, now known as the Portland Bingo Hall and Chinese Laundry. Just under 10 000 fans are here to see the action tonight. Wait, what's this? [A man comes to the broadcast table] John Christopher: Dave, Tony, nice to see you. DS: Why are you here tonight, John? JC: I'm the new color commentator. WAR! hired me for their WAR!fare broadcasts. TJ: Hey, I'm the color commentator! We don't need two! JC: Well, Tony, it's like this. You ain't the color commentator anymore. WAR! decided to make you a roving reporter or wrestling journalist or something. Now quit hogging my seat and go interview someone.
TJ: But I... My contract... [Heads to the back] JC: Now that that unfortunate business is behind us, let's get to the matches. DS: Um, okay. There have been a couple of changes made to tonight's card. As a result of "The American" Butch Williams' incredible challenge, he will wrestle three times tonight. He will face WAR!Lord Lord
Byron,
World WAR! Champ Tonnage, and Bane in separate matches. Bane decided not to wrestle twice in one night because he wanted to devote his full attention to injuring Williams. Instead, Mark Starr will face
Bane's
scheduled opponent, Johnny "The Sidewinder" Missile.
Vince Black, Texas Ranger vs "Lethal" Lyle Morrison w/Detroit Doggpound Two stars the fans were familiar with, Black and Morrison locked up in a technical match. Morrison went after Black's leg from the start with a variety of wear-down moves. Morrison made a mistake by going up to the second rope for a knee drop a little early. Black moved out of the way and went to work on Morrison. Black hit Lyle with various chops and kicks...
DS: A huge clothesline by Black! Morrison did a 360 in the air. He's in a lot of trouble. JC: Morrison is just playing possum. No way this Texas Ranger is going to lay a beating on a member of the Detroit Doggpound. DS: Vince Black scoops up his opponent... bodyslam! And he drops a leg across his throat. Black picks up Morrison, but gets an eye gouged for his trouble. Morrison executes a beautiful belly-to-belly suplex but is not able to follow it up.
JC: If there's one thing Morrison can do, it's wrestle. DS: Well, that *is* the sport. Morrison and Black are up. Morrison attempts a bulldog, but Black throws him off! Morrison off the ropes, spin wheel kick by Black! Black hooks in a headlock, but Morrison throws him into the ropes... Black ducks a clothesline... he hits
Morrison with a a double hand chop to the neck, his Justice finishing move! Black covers Lyle and hooks a leg... 1...2...3!
Winner: Vince Black by pinfall Rating: *** 1/2 JC: Oh, you can't be serious! The Texas Ranger won? Chalk that one up to poor officiating. DS: What do you mean? The ref was completely unbiased! JC: Okay, chalk it up to the faulty ring ropes. The lighting ain't too great in here, either. DS: Let's see if Tony J has got anyone lined up to interview. [Cut to locker room area...] TJ: Well, I haven't seen any WAR! wrestlers here yet. There was a messenger boy who left three roses at Mark Starr's locker room door, but he didn't have too much to say. Check back with me later, I'll have something lined up. I think.
Johnny "The Sidewinder" Missile vs "The Canadian Strangler" Mark Starr Mark Starr showed up wearing an "Armageddon" t-shirt. He looked confident and prepared. The Sidewinder wasn't expecting to face Mark Starr, and it showed. Starr got an early advantage, but Missile turned it around with a flying clothesline. Missile went to the top when he thought he had Starr sufficiently stunned, but Starr was only playing possum. Mark caught him in the midsection with a fist when he came off the top. Starr viciously assaulted the Sidewinder's leg to take his aerial ability and speed away.
After having his leg rammed into the steel ring post several times, Missile's offense was almost non-existent. Starr continued to work the leg until he decided to end the match with the Canadian Crippler.
Winner: Mark Starr by submission Rating: *** "The Blade" Larry Brown vs StormStalker The former hockey player turned professional wrestler, Larry Brown, came to the ring carrying his hockey stick. StormStalker's background was not so well known. The fans learned a little about both men in this match. It was actually a fairly technical match for the first ten minutes, with neither getting an advantage. Then they went outside the ring...
DS: Larry Brown drags StormStalker outside the ring. The Blade whips StormStalker into the ringpost... no, reversal! Brown hits the post hard! StormStalker throws his opponent back into the ring...
JC: You know, StormStalker is just asking for trouble. The Blade doesn't take too kindly to being roughed up like that. DS: Side suplex by Stalker. He goes for the cover...1...2.. Blade kicks out! StormStalker goes for a conventional vertical suplex, but keeps the Blade in the air for 5, 6, 7 seconds before slamming him to the mat. Another cover...1...2... Brown kicks out again.
JC: They don't come much tougher than the Blade. He had to quit hockey because it wasn't violent enough for him. DS: You mean he was too violent for hockey. He may wish he had never changed sports when this one's over. StormStalker has continued to pummel Brown but can't put him away. Now Brown is thrown out of the ring again. StormStalker is out after him. He rams the Blade's head into the ring apron, but Brown retaliates with a thumb to the eye!
JC: A tried and true classic! DS: Brown runs Stalker into the steel railing! This one's getting ugly! Headbutt by StormStalker. Neither of these men will quit. Uh oh! Larry Brown's picked up his hockey stick! The referee is trying to stop him but the Blade cracks the stick over StormStalker's back!
Now he's jabbing what's left of the stick into Stalker's midsection! The referee calls for the bell. JC: Hey, Larry Brown sure showed StormStalker. A DQ loss means nothing to him as long as he gets to dish out some punishment. If you look at StormStalker, you know he got the punishment he deserved.
DS: I think Brown just got frustrated. He couldn't put StormStalker away, so he took the cheap way out of the match. JC: You would think that. Winner: StormStalker by DQ Rating: *** 1/4 Detroit Doggpound w/"Lethal" Lyle Morrison vs Stone Age w/LadySaur Detroit Doggpound entered the match in desperate need of a win. After going undefeated in WXW, they haven't been as impressive so far in WAR!. Stone
Age,
on the other hand, have had fair success and were just a couple of wins away from being legitimate title contenders. The Doggpound tried to take the match outside, into the stands, and up the aisle as much as possible. This was particularly effective against the smaller Ice Cap. T-Rex was able to brawl with Pitt and Washington and was able to inflict some damage on them outside the ring. Lyle Morrison nailed T-Rex when the ref wasn't looking, allowing the Doggpound to go after his partner. Pitt nearly decapitated Ice Cap with a stun gun on the steel ringside railing. After tossing Ice Cap back into the ring, Marsellus Washington got an easy pinfall victory.
Winners: Detroit Doggpound by pinfall Rating: *** WAR!Lord Title Match Lord Byron vs "The American" Butch Williams w/American Patriot This was the first of three matches tonight for Butch Williams. Butch came to the ring dressed a little differently than usual. He was wearing a mask and full bodysuit with a stars and stripes motif. American Patriot was
Butch's
corner man. Byron announced that he would have a corner man, too. Out walked none other than World WAR! Champion Tonnage, who was scheduled to wrestle
Williams later tonight. Butch jumped Byron before the bell rang and pounded him with various power moves. Tonnage and Patriot kept out of the match, as they were too busy badmouthing each other.
DS: And Williams slams Byron hard to the mat! Byron just hasn't been able to get it going this match. JC: Lord Byron is one of the greatest technical wrestlers the sport has ever seen. Unfortunately, Williams has been able to neutralize that by keeping it a brawl.
DS: Whip into the ropes... back body drop! Byron grimaces in pain. That back's taken a lot of punishment. Williams hammers him down again with a few axe-handles. Williams has him again... sidewalk slam!
He goes for a pin ...1...2... Byron gets his foot on the rope! JC: Lord Byron always knows where he is when he's in the ring. DS: Butch now with an Irish whip... no, Byron reverses it! He whips Butch into the referee! JC: That may have been a desperation move by Byron. He can catch his breath now while the ref takes a nap. DS: Tonnage is getting in the ring! But the American Patriot drags him back outside! They're exchanging blows! Meanwhile, Byron has managed to get Williams tied up in the ropes! HERE COMES BANE!!
JC: This can't be looking good for "The American." OH MY GOD! Bane just tore Williams' head off!!! DS: No, that's just his mask. Bane is beating on Butch's face. This is terrible. Tonnage just slammed Patriot on the floor and now... a
Gigaton Crush! Patriot's not going to be in any shape for the triangle match.
JC: That might just raise the quality of that match. DS: Butch has been thrown outside. Bane and Tonnage continue to pound on him. Butch has never been the fans' favorite wrestler, but no one wants to see this done to him. Bane and Tonnage... double powerbomb on the arena floor!!! Tonnage is setting up for the Gigaton Crush!
They're not just trying to injure him, they're trying to kill him! Bane adds insult to injury with his Death Drop... I think Butch stopped feeling pain a long while ago.
JC: Lord Byron has been in the ring trying to revive the referee. He starts counting Butch out. You know, I don't think Butch is going to beat the ten-count. He could count to a thousand.
DS: Tonnage and Bane have some parting shots for the American Patriot. This was just sick. Williams has been counted out. Butch won't be able to wrestle his other two matches. Patriot might not be able to make the triangle match. This attack was just... demented.
JC: Stop your whining. If anyone deserved something like that, it was "The American" Butch Williams. It didn't hurt Patriot to have some sense knocked into his head, either. What a great match and what a great win for the WAR!Lord, Lord Byron!
Winner: Lord Byron by countout Rating: *** 1/2 (+ * for unnecessary violence) Jason Midnight vs Colossus It was a match-up of speed and agility vs power and brawn. Colossus was able to toss Midnight around early on in the match, but Midnight countered with his martial arts moves. Midnight caught Colossus with a spinning back kick and went for the cover, but got only a two-count. Several roll-up attempts got him only two-counts. Midnight went to the top, but was caught in midair and was drilled to the mat by Colossus. Colossus went on the offensive with power moves. A cover after a powerbomb only got a two-count. Colossus continued to pound on Midnight, finally powerslamming him. Colossus taunted
Midnight and yelled to the crowd, "Revenge will be sweet!" before going for the cover. Midnight recovered enough to roll Colossus up from behind, but got only two. Midnight hit a spin kick to the stomach and went for a bulldog, but Colossus managed to slip out by grabbing the ropes. Colossus hammered
Midnight and went for a powerbomb, but Midnight used his momentum to flip over
Colossus' back and roll him up from behind. The stunned Colossus didn't realize what had happened until the three-count. As Jason celebrated his win,
Colossus attacked him from behind, knocking him from the turnbuckle to the floor. Colossus looked to the crowd, but got only boos.
Winner: Jason Midnight by pinfall Rating *** 3/4 DS: What a great match that was! Too bad Colossus was such a sore loser. JC: He had every right to do that. A lot of people wanted to see someone take it to that punk Midnight. I really liked the way he bounced on the concrete arena floor after he was thrown off the turnbuckle.
Now that's entertainment! DS: You're sick, but you knew that already. Anyway, let's see how Tony J is doing. Tony, have you got an interview lined up yet? [In the locker room] TJ: It's still pretty quiet here, actually. Wait... here comes Seth Blitzhead! Hey, Mr.Blitzhead, do you have a moment? Seth: AAAAAARRRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHHH! [Runs past Tony and out of sight] TJ: Well, that was interesting, wasn't it? I think he was in a bit of a hurry. Mexican food will do that to you. Back to you guys. DS: I apologize to the fans for that. Obviously, that was former World WAR! Champion, Seth Blitzspear, one of the most popular and talented wrestlers in WAR! JC: I didn't think anyone could use the words "talented" and "Blitzspear" in the same sentence. DS: Let's get back to some ring action, right after these prerecorded comments from "Thunder" Decan... --------------- **TJ "Thunder" Decan standing in front of a lifesize poster of Tonnage.** [TJ] I was hoping to finish my business with McGriff tonight, but the power's that be picked "Cardinal" Andrea Mancini to take a beating. Let me say, up front, Cardinal I'm sorry for the PAIN you're going to be in....Hell! No I'm
NOT! I'm gonna ENJOY every MINUTE of it! Next time you'll think TWICE before you sign for a match in a THUNDER storm! And TONNAGE! The storm's gettin'
closer to that belt. Hang on to it. I want to be the one that TAKES it from you. --------------- TJ "Thunder" Decan vs Cardinal Andrea Mancini A battle of two big men, but Decan was bigger. Thunder took it to the
Cardinal
from the opening bell. It was rare to see Mancini on the receiving end of so many bodyslams and suplexes, but Decan's power was impressive. Just when it looked like Mancini was going to make a comeback, Decan beat him down again.
Mancini, in a dazed state, looked to his acolytes, but they weren't there. Mancini moved out of the way of a charging Decan as thunder tried to give him a running elbowsmash in the corner. Mancini then managed to get in his first offense of the match. Clothesline...shoulder tackle...kicks and punches...
the Cardinal could not get Decan off his feet. Mancini tried to pick the big man up, but Decan blocked it. Decan then slammed the Cardinal instead.
After an elbowdrop, Decan finished him off with a running powerslam and got a rare pinfall victory over Mancini.
Winner: TJ "Thunder" Decan by pinfall Rating: *** ~~~~ Part 2 follows.
