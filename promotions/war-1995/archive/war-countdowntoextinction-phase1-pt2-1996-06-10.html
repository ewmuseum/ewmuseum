Countdown to Extinction `96 — Phase 1 (Part 2)

The countdown continued.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “LETHAL” LYLE MORRISON (7) Vs. T.J. “THUNDER” DECAN (8) Morrison was totally outsized, but not outwitted. He taunted
Decan at first, and kept away from him, but eventually Decan caught hold of “Lethal” Lyle and crushed him with his weight and power. The avalnches were especially effective.
Morrison got desperate and thumbed Decan`s eye, and worked from there, almost getting the pin following a sleeper hold.
Decan got mad, responded to the crowd and beat on Decan with a lot more intensity before executing his “ThunderJam” on
Morrison - twice. Morrison was knocked out, and Thunder got the win. Thunder progresses.
MR: * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Did you see that “ThunderJam”. Most effective move! RS: But it won`t work on Tonnage, and he`s the man to beat. I must say, I was impressed with Morrisons approach. Bob: You would. But it wasn`t enough was it? You can`t cheat against Thunder and win. RS: Actually, you can. Bob: Okay, you can. But you shouldn`t. RS: Actually, you should. Bob: I`m not going to get into this conversation. RS: Yes you are. Bob: I refuse to. RS: No you don`t. etc. etc. etc. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ VINCE BLACK, TEXAS RANGER (8) Vs. “MAGNUM” BILL MCGRIFF (7) Everyone expected this match to be over really quickly, but it wasn`t. It went very similarly to the MidNight/Duke match earlier in the night, with Black throwing Magnum around, and using his immese strength against himself. At one point, everyone was sure he`d got the pin. The fans really got behind Black, and he was on his way to victory, when suddenly
McGriff got a DDT on,a nd from there it was all over. The most amazing powerbomb crushed Black, and then McGriff used his “Full Metal Jacket” to finish Texas Ranger off. The fans were disappointed.
MR: * * * * ( good showing by Texas Ranger) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RS: To quote Yoda, “size matters not”. Bob: Certainly. Black may only be 5`9”, but he has the background and strength to give the wrestlers in War! quite a suprise. RS: Only one more match in Phase 1 of countdown to extinction, left Bob. Bob: That`s right. It`s the suprise match. It`s Robski Vs. Craig JayHawk. RS: Both of these men have made a lot of enemies. Bob: Robski has made a lot of the wrong enemies Rick. He`s got over two tonnes of humanity after him.
RS: Scary thought! Anyway, shouldn`t we go to the ring announcer? Bob: Yup. Let`s go. Ring Announcer: Currently in the ring, weighing 335lbs, at
6`8”, ROBBBBBBBSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSKIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII (Some sections of the fans really boo, but others get behind
Robski and his bad attitude) RA: And now, coming down the aisle, 242lbs in weight, 6`5” in height....CRAIG JAAAAAYYYYYYYHAWK
(Fans go wild and really get behind the exciting young wrestler) Bob: Robski looks down on Craig JayHawk...but not by much...Robski starts with a shove...and JayHawk responds with another one! Robski slams JayHawk...JayHawk gets up and offers a test of strength via the roman knuckle loc...Robski just laughs...
RS: But he isn`t now! JayHawk delivers a vicious kick to the midsection. Bob: And look at Robski bounce back with a venomous clothesline...it knocks JayHawk out of the ring...
RS: That`s TONNAGE! coming down the aisle... Bob: And the rest of Armageddon! RS: They`ve got Barb-wire baseball bats.... Bob: Robski sees them! Why isn`t he running? RS: Perhaps it`s because Robski is really hard and knows he can take them all at once in a scrap.
Bob: Not if they`ve got barb-wire baseball bats... RS: I think he was waiting for the countout, but JayHawk is back in...Robski legs it into the crowd...some of the punx try to stop him, but he gets away.
Bob: That would have been 10 against 1. Perhaps Robski does have some brains! RS: It shows you that Armageddon are really jarred by Robski`s comments. Bob: I`m not suprised. Craig JayHawk has won by countout. It`s a bit of a hollow victory for him, but a victory nonetheless. And he enters the draw for the next round of
Countdown to Extinction `96. We`ve got four title matches left, all titles on the line. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ LIBERTY TITLE MATCH OG GARY TRENT (Champion) (8) Vs. “HOLLYWOOD” JOHNNY DREAMER (7) OG Gary Trent has grown in confidence since winning the
Liberty Title, and he has held it with pride. The match was fought cleanly and evenly for the first ten minutes, although
Trent had about 60% of the attack. We now go to commentary.
Bob: Trent whips Dreamer to the ropes...Dreamer ducks the clothesline...returns with a flying cross body block...pin...
1! 2! Bob: 3! almost but not quite. A bit of a slow count if you ask me. Dreamer picks Trent up...fine Belly to back suplex...now for a elbow drop...a cover...
1! 2! Bob: Almost three again! Trent is holding on with his dear life. Dreamer is signalling for the Hollywood drop...but a rake to the eyes by Trent stops that...things are getting nasty now...it`s turing into a brawl...
RS: And obviously Trent is winning it...a mistake by Dreamer to fall into that trap... Bob: And a discuss punch by Trent! His repertoire is expanding! It sends Dreamer into the corner...Trent follows with a shouldercharge...misses! Dreamer rolls him up..
1! 2! Bob: Trent kicks out again! Dreamer picks Trent up, but Trent blocks and does an atomic drop...he puts Dreamer intot he corner...onto the ropes...superplex...no, a supercradleplex...that takes some doing...it`s a pin...
1! 2! Bob: 3! Trent keeps his title! Wow! What an exhausting match. Dreamer will be disappointed at missing his opportunity.
RS: Dreamer can carry on dreaming about title glory, because he`s a loser, y`hear me Dreamer - A LOSER!
Bob: Sheesh, calm down Rick, what has he ever done to you? RS: I don`t know! Sorry, it must have been one of those momentary outbursts! Bob (giving Rick an odd glance): Hmm. Well, now it`s time for World War Tag Action time. Lost Boys defend against
Stone Age.
RS: There`ll be blood shed tonight. Bob: More blood shed, don`t you mean? RS: Blood shed? What are you talking about? Bob: You just said that blood would be shed. I`m just saying that blood has already been shed.
RS: Are you sure? Bob: Positive. RS: How strange! Bob: Tell me about your childhood Rick... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WORLD WAR TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH LOST BOYS w/Wendy (7) Vs. STONE AGE w/LadySaur (7) King Benny was at ringside, making notes. However, The two teams jumped each other down the aisle and fought violently.
When the match got to the ring, both teams were really tired from the battle. It was a tiresome brawl between the two teams, but then Lost Boys took a whiff of initiative...
Bob: Wendy has just rammed LadySaurs face into the steel steps! RS: That`s my kind of woman! Bob: T-Rex is going to help her...IceCap is left in the ring...double team by Lost Boys! They lift him up and drop him face first onto the steel corner post!
RS: And that must be the first blood shed of the evening! Bob: Now they`re really going to work. The referee is trying to sort the situation outside the ring...Wendy has just hit T-Rex really hard in the you know wheres...he`s crippled over...Lady Saur is receiving medical attention...spike piledriver by the Lost Boys on
IceAge...this must be it. RS: You know what I think. Bob: Shut up! We`ve got a cover here! 1! 2! Bob: 3! And after heavy amounts of cheating, The Lost Boys win by pinfall. RS: I didn`t know it pinfalls were in their repertoire. Bob: As much as it is in the dictionary. RS: Eh? Bob: A dictionary. Things with words in. RS: Oh, you mean a book. Who needs `em when you got TV? Bob: Who needs anything else other than our next match up? It`s International WAR! champion Booster Dunne. It`s The challenger, American Patriot. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ INTERNATIONAL WAR! CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH BOOSTER DUNNE w/Ms.Paula (9) Vs. AMERICAN PATRIOT (8) King Benny was at ringside in this match. Patriot, as usual, fought the good fight. Dunne fought the better one. Patriot had Dunne beat on several occasions, but seemed just a bit too slow in going for the pin. His powerful moves certainly wore Dunne down though, especially a stunning tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, which the fans loved.
Dunne, in response, replied with chinlocks and weardown holds, so he could recover his strength. After about fifteen minutes, the match got truly electric as both men executed fast paced moves that they both seemed impervious to.
Patriot got a few pin attempts, and even kicked out of the booster launch! Bob: Wow! Patriot showing great calibre kicking out of the move that has put so many other wrestlers away.
RS: Dunne is doing the right thing though, he is keeping
American Patriot down with axhandles.
Bob: AP fights back...Dunne`s blows are having less and less effect...he tries to throw AP to the ropes...AP reverses....he bounces off the ropes...flying forearm!
That`s what he calls the Patriot Missile. That`s the move that is going to win him the International War! Title.
RS: Dunne is out cold. If he was any colder, he`d be blue. Bob: AP is disorientated. The fans are screaming for him to pin Dunne. AP sees Dunne and collapses on him....
1! 2! Bob: LORD BYRON KICKS AMERICAN PATRIOT OFF BOOSTER DUNNE! The ref calls for the bell....this is a disqualification...American Patriot wins by DQ! RS: But he doesn`t win the belt, and that is what matters! Bob: I can`t believe this! I really can`t! This is a massive injustice. American Patriot was so so so close.
RS: It doesn`t matter Bob, it really doesn`t. This is American Patriots sencond title shot in a row, and he still can`t do it. Still, there is one thing the American Patriot will always defeat. Bob: What`s that? RS: The law of averages! Considering the amount of titleshots he has had, he should have won something!
Bob: He had the WAR/RAW interfed belt. RS: And with RAW! all but extinct, you think that title means anything? Bob: Well, I know a title that does mean something. The man involved in that next match is the WAR!LORD Lord Byron.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WORLD WAR! CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH TONNAGE Vs. LORD BYRON Bob: T.J.Decan is at ringside. And so is King Benny, again. What is going on? RS: How should I know, I`m just an armadillo. Bob: WHAT? RS: I said King Benny is probably making a few notes for Big
Duke, and T.J has an interest in Tonnage, just like everyone else.
Bob: Phew! for a moment I thought my hearing was going! RS: Pardon? Bob: Tonnage is already in the ring, and now coming down the aisle, to a tremedous amount of booing, is Lord Byron. There goes the bell. And...they shake hands....and Lord Byron leaves! What is happening here?
RS: Coola Lazarus Bonecruncher. Bob: Lord Byron has been counted out. He`s taken a poster off a fan of Booster Dunne and ripped it up.
RS: And that is exactly what he`ll do tomorrow, when he faces Booster Dunne. Bob: The fans aren`t getting their moneysworth. Worst of all, we`ve still got plenty of airtime to fill. I think, yes, if I hear correctly, we`ll be showing a Tom & Jerry cartoon. But first, let me remind of some great action
coming up. Tomorrow, we have Phase 2 of Countdown to EXTINCTION `96, with some great matchups in store. Then, on the 13th, it`s the interfed rumble. Then, on the 16th, it`s
WAR!FARE time again. The 19th brings us Phase 3 of Countdown to Extinction, and I`m sure there will be other events as well, like plenty of WAR! Arena Cards.
Hold on a second!! I'm getting footage from the dressing room area!! There's a brawl going on!! (The scene cuts to outside Booster Dunne's dressing room. Dunne is being attacked by Tonnage and Lord Byron.) Bob: What in the world?? (Byron repeatedly nails Dunne's leg with his cane, while
Tonnage hits him hard with a vertical splash on his chest. Byrons cane breaks, and he curses, before grabbing a steel chair. As he does so,
Tonnage
drops an elbowdrop across Dunnes knee. Then they drag
Booster Dunne to the heavy looking door and close it really hard and fast on his knee. The Tonnage picks up a few lockers and drops them on Booster Dunne, before powerbombing him on the concrete. Rick:Ye-eessssss!!! I love it!! Break him to pieces!!! (Byron walks back to Dunne, and cracks him hard on the head with the steel chair. Dunne is bust open. Byron cracks him hard on the leg, and then locks Dunne, now only half concious, in the
Aristoclutch.) Bob:We've got to get some help down there....this is ridiculous... Rick: No, it's intelligent! Now we know why Byron walked away from ringside!! It makes perfect sense!
Bob: It's disgusting. Wait!! Here comes Randy Blake!!! And
Tonnage
floors him! And here's the American Patriot!! And SETH
BLITZSPEAR IS RUNNING DOWN TO HELP!!!! (Tonnage and Blitzspear start brawling. Byron looks up, and finally lets go of Dunne. Both he and Tonnage back off as more superstars arrive.
The Rich Boys and Armageddon come down as well, but officals try to keep the two groups apart. Fists are flying everywhere, and anything not sealed down is used as a weapon. Eventually, it calms down)
Bob: At last.....but it looks as though the damage has already been done... RS: Dunnes leg is damaged than something really damaged! Bob: I`m inclined to agree...more news as we get it....but until then...we're out of time here.....
