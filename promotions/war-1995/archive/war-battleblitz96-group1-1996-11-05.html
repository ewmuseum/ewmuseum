BattleBlitz 96 — Group 1: The Bonfire Bash Returns

5 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION presents... BATTLEBLITZ 96 - THE BONFIRE BASH RETURNS Report from The Arizona Dust Bowl I promise you all that these were drawn at random. Heck, I`ve even got the little bits of paper to prove it.
~~~~~~~~~~~ ..Just picture it. You`re in this massive stadium. The atmosphere is totally electric. The fans are going wild, and you`re stuck next to two teenagers, either side of you, who insist on talking _REAL_ loud. You pick out that there names are Todd & Hank. You`re going to be hearing _their_ opinions for the rest of the night.
********************************** Todd: I can`t wait. This is going to be so good. Do you remember that time when they had a BattleBlitz for the International War!
title about a year ago. Hank: Yeah, that loser Jerry Gordon managed to beat The Reverand J.R.Death. Todd: Whatever happened to him? Hank: I don`t know, but he must have been the biggest guy ever in War! But not as fat as Jumbo Jim Mongol Todd: Yeah, but _ nobody_ is as fat as Mongol. Sshhh, it`s about to start. Who`s going to be first...? Hank: Hah! It`s The Stalker. Oh, he`s got such an attitude now. Let`s see his attitude now he`s drawn number 1. Todd: Stop taking the piss. He may have drawn number one, but he can still do it. Hank: Not against Dirty Doug. And these two hate each other. What a start. Come on Dougie, win this for me....
Todd: STALKERRRRRRRRRRRR.... ************************************* THE STALKER (9) Vs. "DIRTY" DOUG SIMPKINS (5) The Stalker was showing Simpkins exactly what it meant to wrestle hard and fast. Simpkins got dazed and Stalker almost got the pin several times, but he always got Simpkins too close to the ropes. It just wasn`t going his way. At the end of the match, Stalker picked
Simpkins up and got him into position for a superplex, but Simpkins nailed him with some foreign object, knocking Stalker senseless. Simpkins held
Stalker up on the turnbuckle, then just chucked him over to the outside. Stalker fell from the top turnbuckle to the outside and was hurt pretty bad. He got counted out.
MR: 7 MD: 8.46 Points for Simpkins: 20 *************************************** Todd: I can`t believe it! Stalker was all over him. Hank: Huh, some Liberty champion. Todd: He looks in trouble. Hank: Good. It servers him right for messing with Dougie. Dougie for the Liberty title!!!
Todd: Hey, look who`s next....it`s Robski... Hank: Come on Robski. Todd: Do I really have to choose? Simpkinssssssssssss... ************************************ DOUG SIMPKINS (6) Vs. ROBSKI (8) Robski came in to face an opponent who had suffered quite a beating at the Stalkers hands. Robski piled on the pain, and should have had a three count early on after catching Simpkins with a tremendous spine-buster, but the referee was slow to the count. Robski worked
Simpkins over with minimal resistance from Simpkins. Robski set Simpkins up for the powerbomb, but then suddenly curled over in pain. Simpkins had executed another cheap shot, hitting Robski hard in the sensitive part of the groin area with some brass knuckles. Simpkins quickly covered, hooked the leg and got the pin.
MR: 7 MD: 7.26 Simpkins gets 50 pts (70 on the night) *********************** Hank: Remarkable. Todd: How does Doug do it? Hank: He hasn`t been doing much lately, but he`s pulled off another victory. Todd: Another cheap victory. But at least he beat Robski. Hank: Robski is going to be in pain right now. Look who`s up next, it`s Bounty Hunter. Time for Dougie to do it again.
Todd: I`m not even going to choose. ****************************** DOUG SIMPKINS (6) Vs. BOUNTY HUNTER (7) Simpkins had hardly a time to rest. As soon as Robski was out, Hunter was in. And Hunter, knowing he had a severely weakened opponent, got to work straight away, hitting Simpkins with DDT`s, piledrivers, suplexes, clotheslines and just about everything else.
Simpkins was done for. But when Bounty Hunter bounced off the ropes and went for a splash, Simpkins got his knees up. The tide of the match changed, and Simpkins began to wear Hunter down with chinlocks, giving himself a chance to rest. The crowd got bored and all went for a hotdog (they sold out v.quickly), but Simpkins refused to budge. The perseverence paid off as he moved the chinlock into a sleeper. Suddenly a mysterious masked wrestler burst into the ring and levelled Simpkins with a baseball bat. Bounty Hunter got
DQ`d and then began to join his friend in an attack on Simpkins. They left Simpkins cold in the ring. MR: 6 MD: 14.21 Points for Simpkins - 10 (80 in total) ********************** Todd: That`s what I like to see. Simpkins needed some of his own medicine. Hank: (speechless) Todd: All this in-fighting. Tsk, tsk. Oh dear Hank, your man is in trouble now. It`s T.J "Traitor" Decan.
Hank: Now this guy I like. Anyone who is an enemy of Seth is a friend of mine. Todd: He put Seth out of the ToC yesterday. Hank: (Groan) I know ******************************* DOUG SIMPKINS (6) Vs. T.J. " THUNDER" DECAN (7) The number one contender for the world title, T.J. "Thunder" Decan, was up next to face Simpkins. Simpkins was still out cold following the attack by Bounty Hunter and his masked friend. Thunder could have easily pinned Simpkins from the start, because Simpkins was out cold, but Decan wouldn`t have that. He called for medics and they revived Simpkins. After a ten minute break, the match started for real. Thunder was still sore from his match with Blitzspear at Toc
Semi-Finals, and it seemed to impede his performance a bit. It was a close-ish brawl until Simpkins, out of fatigue and desperation, knowing he had little chance against a fresher Thunder, pulled out a piece of rope and managed to lock it around Thunders throat without the refereee seeing it. He choked Thunder down, and almost had him, but Thunder had enough resolve to grasp the rope away before it was too late. He turned to Simpkins and went mad, dishing out a heavy beating, resulting in the ThunerJam. Simpkins was pinned. With ease.
MR: 6 MD: 9.08 25 points for Thunder **************************************** Hank: Simpkins did well, let`s give him that. Todd: But he seems to be even less trustworthy than Decan. Oh boy, now it`s time for fun. U-S-A, U-S-A, U-S-A...goooooooo Patriot.
Hank: Thunder doesn`t look good, but he`ll deal with the American Fool quite easily. ******************************************* T.J "THUNDER" DECAN (3) Vs. AMERICAN PATRIOT (8) Patriot stormed the ring while Thunder was getting his breath together. One of Patriots big strengths is knowing his opponents and knowing when to strike them. He did this well against Thunder, getting in an early "Patriot Missile" Strike on Thunder. It wasn`t enough, but three more did the job of getting Thunder down and in a pinning predicament, and Patriot got the victory.
MR: 5 MD: 1.37 25 points for American Patriot ****************************** Todd: Deal with him quite easily you said. Not quite, I say. Hank: Give the guy a break. That was his third match in two days, and Patriot didn`t give him a chance to get into the match.
Todd: Decan gave Simpkins a chance and it almost cost him the match. Hank: Hmmm. Fair point. I just hope Tyler Stevens turns up soon and teaches Patchwork a lesson or two...awww jeez, it`s Helel.
Todd: The NDA are great, but I`ve got to go with American Patriot. He`s the main man out of these two. U-S-A, U-S-A. **************************************** AMERICAN PATRIOT (7) Vs. HELEL (8) This was much more of a challenge for American Patriot. Helel worked hard to keep Patriot down, and was working on Patriots arms especially. Helel wanted to de-arm the Patriot missile and was on course to do that, but Patriot started to get a second wind. Helel took it and battled back, but Patriot stopped his run again with a lethal clothesline. He worked over Helel again, then set him up for the Patriot Missile. But just as he picked him up and put him in place, Helel grabbed Patriot and got him with a belly to belly
suplex. The shock, the move, the pin. The win for Helel. MR: 8 MD: 17.43 75 points for Helel ******************************* Hank: I`m just glad we aren`t betting are money. We`re not exactly getting it right are we?
Todd: I need a hotdog. Hank: They`ve sold out. Todd: How about a hotcat? Hank: Sorry, they don`t do bad puns either. Oh boy, Helel is in trouble now. It`s Magnum....
************************ HELEL(7) Vs." MAGNUM" BIFF McGRIFF (9) Magnum imposed himself on the match from the start, and although Helel fought courageously, it wasn`t enough to stop the International
War! champion finishing the match off with the Full Metal Jacket.
MR: 7 MD: 7.37 25 pts for McGriff ************************ Todd: Well, to be honest, I was expecting Magnum to win. Helel is good, but he isn`t in the same class as the International War!
champion yet. Hank: He`ll hold that belt for a long time. Todd: I bloody well hope not. Aw god, it`s your favourite. Hank: Wahey, Tyler Stevens. Go Tyler, go Tyler, go Tyler. Todd: How quickly your loyalties change. Hank: Hey, I still like Magnum, but Tyler, well...he`s such a sexy boy. Todd: Hmm. Almost a boy-toy. Y`know, I`m sure I`ve seen someone like him before. Hank: Only in heaven. Todd (giving Hank a worried look, and edging away): Erm, oh-kay. ********************************** "MAGNUM" BIFF MCGRIFF (8) Vs. TYLER STEVENS (8) Magnum was showing little sign of tiredness, but Tyler was showing great canniness in the ring, keeping out of Magnums way as much as possible, and basically executing hit-and-run style attacks.
Occassionally he got caught by McGriff, but always managed to re-group. For a while, Stevens had the match all his own way, but his HeartBreaker superkick was mis-timed and wasn`t enough to put
McGriff away. McGriff got back into the aciton and had Stevens soon standing dead on his feet. But just as he whipped Stevens to the ropes, Venom (Tylers aide) pulled McGriffs feet, and tripped him over. Tyler bounced with perfect timing off the ropes and dropped a big elbow onto the back of McGriff`s head, knocking him out cold.
Stevens got the pin. However, several of Armageddon, led by Tonnage, stormed to ringside and complained to the ref about interference. The ref wouldn`t change his mind, and a pushing and shoving match started.
Venom did enough t keep Armageddon at bay while Stevens retreated into the crowd before his next match.
MR: 9 MD: 25.27 50 pts for Stevens ************************************* Hank: Woah, that was explosive. I don`t know what to make of that. Todd: Divided loyalties, huh? Hank: It was such a close match...I mean, I suppose Tyler deserved it, maybe, but... Todd: But you don`t want Tyler crossing paths with Armageddon? Too late. And by the looks of it, Venom has pissed them off too. Ah, about time, a wrestler with true time-warp style, a wrestler for these post-modern days..."Mr.Funk" Julius Jackson Jnr. Go Funk, go
Funk... Hank: Go Tyler you sexy boy. ***************************** TYLER STEVENS (6) Vs. "MR.FUNK" J.J Jnr. (5) Mr.Funk entered the ring, danced around a bit, brawled a bit, bounced off the ropes and was caught square on the chin with a superkick.
Stevens covered and pinned Mr.Funk with ease. MD: 2.41 MR: 4 25 pts for Stevens (75 in total) ************************ Todd (shaking his head in disbelief): Mr.Funk has got a lot to learn. Hank: Yeah, a lot to learn about style. Two down. Let`s see Tyler make it three. Todd: Hey, it`s the "The Goon" Jerry Gordon. This could almost be considered his special event.
Hank: It`s time for the sexy boy to make it three. **************************************** TYLER STEVENS (7) Vs. JERRY "GOON" GORDON (8) Fatigue, as this match went on, slowly become a rather major factor in this match for Tyler Stevens. Despite dominating the opening stages (with the aid of Venom), Gordon slowly got into it, and by the tenth minute, had everything going his way. Venom tried to interefere but Gordon was aware of Venom and kept away. Stevens fought valiantly, but couldn`t stop Goon`s perfected DDT.
MR: 8 MD: 18.53 25 pts for Gordon ****************************************** Todd: Not so sexy now is he? Hank: Well, just how many times is a guy supposed to perform in one night? Todd: A helluva lot if he is going to win the BattleBlitz. Hank: Ah, at last we have some class in the house. It`s Randy
Blake. Heck, this guy is perfect for this kind of event because he has good c-v conditioning and he can get matches over with quickly.
Todd: We`ll see. Go Jerry, go Jerry, go Jerry. Hank: Todd, he`s facing an ex-world champion. He has no chance. Todd: I beg to differ. *********************************** JERRY GORDON (7) Vs. RANDY BLAKE (9) Blake put in a good performance again, hitting good with accuracy. Each move was executed with masterly skills. Gordon brought his own fight to the match, but Blake was the master, and Gordon the pupil.
A painfully applied double chicken wing was enough to get the submission out of Gordon. MR: 7 MD: 14.26 25 pts for Blake ***************************** Hank: Blake showing exactly why he`s world championship material. Todd: Wekk his time is over now. Hank: No, Gordon`s time is over. And Blake is at a point where he can go on to win it all.
Todd: It`s Nomad he`s facing. You may be right. Hank: I don`t know. This nomad is a bit of an X-Factor. **************************** RANDY BLAKE (8) Vs. NOMAD (7) A good, resilitent performance from the perhaps under-rated Nomad brought him victory against Blake. However, it wasn`t his ability that won it in the end, but rather the interference of Ms.Paula in desperate times, which got Nomad a DQ victory.
MR: 7 MD: 8.56 50pts for Nomad ******************************* Hank: Blake had him. He really had him. Todd: But it`s the same old story. Paula interferes, Blake loses. He needs to lose her. Hank: And lose a sure thing? I think not. We haven`t heard the last of Blake. Todd: That`s what I`m worried about. Why doesn`t he just go away. Hank: Because there`s all these young pretenders out there. Todd: Aw, shut up....it`s Sabin Figaro. And he could win this. Hank: The unified champ against the Nomad. This could be interesting. ***************** NOMAD (6) Vs. SABIN FIGARO (7) Nomad was continually frustrated. He wanted to get this match fast and technical, but Figaro, with all his experience, kept the pace slow and as a slugfest. In this way he kept the match on his terms.
He continually cut short Nomad`s attempts at getting the action going. Then, about ten minutes into the match, it spilled outside.
Robski took this as a chance to go out and beat Figaro up. Nomad and
Robski double teamed Figaro, but just as they were both about to suffer a double count-out, Figaro avoided a swinging chair from
Robski. It hit Nomad instead. Figaro scrambled back into the ring and got the victory by countout. Nomad was jarred off and started pushing Robski around, but Robski just whacked him out cold with a chair as a non-chalant response.
MR: 7 MD: 12.52 10pts to Figaro ************************************* Todd: Robski wants Figaro in a bad way. Hank: The feeling is mutual between them. When these two clash properly, it`s going to be big.
Todd: It`s time for the last man. It`s got to be Mutilator. Hank: Yessss! It is. Mutilator is fresh, he`s strong, and he`s going to beat Figaro. Todd: You`re a fool to wipe Figaro out so easily. This guy is a real star while Mutilator still has a lot to prove.
Hank: If you ask me, he`s proved a lot. Just look at his record. There aren`t as many as good as that. Todd: Sabin is the unified champ. He`s going to do what it takes to win. ******************************* SABIN FIGARO (6) Vs. MUTILATOR (7) This was a total knock-down drag-out brawl, but Mutilator was the one with the more devastating blows. His sheer size and power was enough to get the better of Figaro. After a piledriver, it was all but over. Mutilator covered, Figaro kicked out. He got a second wind and Mutilator was reeling from the sudden onslaught. Figaro had things going his way, but then, accompanied by jeers of the fans, was
The Annhilators. Mutilator saw them and managed to backdrop Figaro to the outside and kept the ref distracted at the same time.
Annhilators did a lot of damage to Figaro before Industrial Revolution appeared on the scene, as well as Kalifornia Heat. Figaro got in the ring, but he was bloodied and bruised. Mutilator, with great ease, locked him and then smashed Figaro hard into the mat with the Mutilator bomb, getting the 3-count shortly afterwards. By then,
plenty of other tag- teams had joined the fight at ringside. The All-Americans had come to sort Kalifornia Heat out. Cold North appeared in response. Lost Boys decided to even up the odds, then decided to tip them further with The Chaos Kinghts. Domination didn`t like that, and they got in on the action too. It was a massive brawl, but amidst all the confusion and chaos, Mutilator was celebrating getting into the BattleBlitz final to battle for the special 50pt prize.
MD: 13.53 MR: 6 (but 10 for the end of card brawl) 50 pts for Mutilator *************************** Todd: Dammit. Mutilator had to get some help to do it. Hank: By any means, that1s what I say. Winning is the important thing. Todd: I`m exhausted. Hank: So am I. Let`s go home. Todd: Let`s not. Let`s stay here until Thursday. It`ll be cool. Hank: Yeah, huh-huh, cool. That rules Todd. Todd: Yeah. Huh-huh-huh... ( I stop before I infringe copyrights!) ************************************ Look out for Thursdays card, the Group 2 BattleBlitz. Who will face Mutilator? ***************** (c) War! Wrestling Federation 1996
