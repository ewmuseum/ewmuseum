Arena Card — 5 October 1996

5 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(You`ll have to forgive me if some of the match ups seem strange. I`ve been out of action for three months and I`m not totally up to speed with the action. I`m going to make a few mistakes, probably) WAR! ARENA CARD for October 5 1996 (Remember, Arena card are just results. Numbers in brackets is rating of performance in relation to the persons ability) MR means match rating (entertainment value for the fans out of 10.) MD means match duration (how long it lasted) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ MUTILATOR (8) Vs. DARKNESS (7) Mutilator pins Darkness after hitting him with a chair. MR: 6 MD: 7.52 CANDYMAN (6) Vs. MICHAEL STRYKER (9) Candyman wins following interference by Nomad MR: 7 MD: 4.04 ANNIHILATORS (9) Vs. APOCALYPSE (8) Venom crushed Mayhem with a throatslam/splash combo. MR: 8 MD: 14.44 RICK SHOWBOAT (8) Vs. NOMAD (7) Showboat almost had it won with his Penny-Pincher but Addams distracted the ref and passed Nomad brass-knuckles which he used to clobber Showboat and get the victory. MR: 7 MD: 9.15 "DIRTY" DOUG SIMPKINS (6) Vs. JERRY "GOON" GORDON (7) Simpkins k.o`d Goon outside the ring to get a count-out victory. MR: 6 MD: 6.50 UNIFIED TITLE MATCH SABIN FIGARO (7) Vs. ARRON IRON (6) Figaro def. Iron following a piledriver. MR: 7 MD: 10.23 T.J. "THUNDER" DECAN ( 9) Vs. BANE (10) Amazing, epic match which Bane edged and got the victory following a big bodyslam. MR: 10 MD: 37.49 TYLER STEVENS (7) Vs. MARK STARR (7) Close match. Stevens wins with a sunset flip. MR: 7 MD: 8.55 THEATRE OF PAIN (8) Vs. THE AGONIZERS (8) Grappler gets Vanquish with a sleeperhold. MR: 8 MD: 18.30 SID "STRAY CAT" STARR (9) Vs. AMERICAN PATRIOT (9) Great match. Starr rides his luck and pins Patriot after smashing AP`s head into a bare turnbuckle. MR: 9 MD: 20.01 LORD BYRON (7) Vs. "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY DREAMER (8) Dreamer made Byron submit to a Boston Crab. MR: 8 MD: 11.59 BIG DUKE (7) Vs. DAMIEN LEGEND (8) Legend wins following a belly to back suplex. MR: 8 MD: 18.43 WARLORD TITLE MATCH ROBSKI (8) Vs. MARC DESTRUCTO (9) Robski counted out after being slammed onto the metal steps. MR: 9 MD: 24.45 RANDY BLAKE ( 8) Vs. "MAGNUM" BIFF McGRIFF (7) McGriff with the Full Metal Jacket. MR: 8 MD: 16.38 WORLD WAR TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH LOST BOYS (7) Vs. INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION (10) Lost Boys lose by countout. MR: 8 MD: 12.19 LIBERTY TITLE MATCH STORMSTALKER (10) Vs. THE JAMMER (10) Candidate for best match of the year. 60 minute time limit draw. Cleanly and expertly fought. MR: 10 (11?) MD: 60.00 SETH BLITZSPEAR (8) Vs. TONNAGE (8) Blitzspear beats Tonnage by countout following a "Flying-Time" backdrop over the top rope. Ever seen 10,000 jaws drop at once in amazement? That`s what happened here when Seth backdropped Tonnage over the top rope. MR: 8 (but the move was 10) MD: 25.22 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
