CIVILWAR 1 "Blood, brains & balls" Live from the Omni Arena, Atlanta With your hosts Avacado Bob & Dr.Nathaniel Darkness ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ Avocado Bob: Welcome one & welcome all, ladies & gentlemen and to all of you who forked out for this Pay-per-view spectacular. Live from the Omni Arena in
Atlanta is CivilWar 1 - "Blood, brains & balls" Tonight, under this one roof to a sell-out crowd there will be 29 - yes, that`s right, 29 matches tonight.
You will see two round robin tournaments and a final to see who the Iron Man of War really is. Also on the card, due to alterations, is a knock-out tag-team tournament to see who wins the International War! tag team tournament. And the match that begins tonight off is the submission match between "The legend" Brewster Cogburn & "The Economist" Randy Blake. Also, using a new statistical device that shows you exactly the percentage of match domination/match time in offensive by each wrestler
Dr.Nathaniel Darkness: Well, before all that I would just like to notify the plebs about the changes in the card that YOU forget Bob
Avocado Bob: Hey, don`t hassle me about it. I just read the script. Dr.Nathaniel Darkness: That`s no excuse. And there is no excuse why Bludgeon lost to Trailboss Mike Dallas at War!Report 6, eliminating him from War!
permanently. This, followed by BadTimes Billy and DeathThreat turning on
DeathThreat & Bludgeon has lead to the sudden end of the Masters of War.
Taking Bludgeons place will be Bombsquad, the newly crowned Freedom title holder, and man, do I like this guy. The fans are losing positive interest in him, but to me, this guy, with his new attitude, seems like a potential world champion.
Bob: I assume you are referring to the incident where he A-Bombed "The American" Butch Williams onto the concrete. Doc: Damn right. You don`t see many popular wrestlers doing that to people, even if it is "The Amercian" Butch Williams, and walk away with a good image in the public eye. He`s a man to be feared. Keep your eyes on this big man.
He`s already won one title... Bob:..with...ahem..assistance... Doc: Any way you can Bob, anyway you can. And for the first time ever, I`m going to say, lets get this card underway...

Matchend: 18.48 ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ For the Jim Hellwig Cup To see who is the Iron Man of War! wrestling Johnny Thunder Vs. "The American" Butch Williams Bob: What an atmosphere this match has produced...everyone knows Thunder wants Williams badly, but Williams was just avoiding him. Well, now he`s got no choice. Now he has to face Johnny Thunder to prove his worth. Equally,
Thunder has to do the same if he wants a shot at any belt at all. A victory tonight would be a victory greeted rapturously by Thunder. The Match: Starting off with a brawl, Thunder eventually gains domination. Catching
Willaims in a Frankensteiner early on, the tide of the match is strongly in
Thunders favour. Williams makes a comeback following a low blow and uses several chokeholds to wear Thunder down. After failing an attempted
"SuperPowerSlam" the match falls to the outside, where Thunder suplex`s Williams onto a table. Both manage to get in, but Williams is dazed. Thunder follows with a few more varieties of suplexs then sets Willaims up for his
"Thunder & Lightning" finishing move, but Williams, still with ring savy, ducks and snaps on a neckbreaker. Thunder falls, and with his "Superpower"
Slam gets the pin on Thunder at 8.17, winning the Jim Hellwig Cup and proving himself to be the IronMan of War!
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ International War Tag Team Belts Tournament final DangerZone Vs. Youngblood Fighting like crazed animals, Tallyman & Styx overpower Youngblood in the early stages. Youngblood recover when Tallyman gets backdropped and with the use of good legal double teaming wear Tallyman down. Styx gets angry and intervenes, getting sent back into his corner by the ref. The distraction is enough for Tallyman, and with two low blows to his opponents groins followed by a rack to the eyes, he manages to escape and tag his fresh partner. Later in the match, the match turns into a free for all, and in the confusion,
Tallyman, the illegal man in the ring pins Shaft, the other illegal man in the ring with a powerbomb. The title goes to DangerZone at 28.51
