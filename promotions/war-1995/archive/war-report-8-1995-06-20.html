WRESTLING FEDERATION presents WAR!REPORT 8 live from the WAR!DATORIUM with your hosts Avocado Bob & Dr. Nathaniel Darkness ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Avocado Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies and gentlemen to War!Report 8. Yes, that`s right, War!Report 8. This show has been on the road for two months now, and it`s getting better by the day. On the eve of Casualties of War, we shall see two popular wrestlers, Seth Blitzspear & Mountain Top Madman battle for the World War Title. Also, taking place tonight is Big Poppa vs. Brester Cogburn for the FWAR title. Doc: Wow, it`s a hot night. Bob: Well, the action hasn`t evenm reached boiling point in War! yet. Doc: No, no. I meant the actual temperature. Bob: Hey Doc, there is a high pressure cell over us at the moment, what do you expect? And why do you think we`ve moved the ring outside onto the carpark? Doc: So that`s why all those cars were parked in the seating area. Bob: That`s correct. And to start the card off tonight, we have General Andropov copming down in a motorcade to make a few announcements concerning Casualties of War, Team Youngblood and the investigation that War! is currently under. And here he comes now, with is police escourt, his wife by his side, and I believe that is vice-president Yankovich next to him... Doc: Despite some crazy decisions, the fans are loving him. Bob: Well, we all love a good procession now and then.... (Bang, Bang, Bang) Bob: My God, Andropov has just been shot... Doc: I think he`s dead. Bob: This is tragic. Doc: Where did those shots come from? Bob: I think it was that grassy knoll over there.....or was it that office block..? I don`t know exactly, but General Andropov has just been mercillesly gunned down in front of 20,000 fans...the card can`t continue, not after this... Doc: Our producer had just affirmed that. This card is cancelled. It will be put back until War!Zone 8 as mark of respect for Andropov, however, Casualties of War! will still be going ahead.
