Report from Winnipeg

This is a report from the pre-Battle Royale card. However, in other news... General Leo has been reinstated. "Magnum" Biff McGriff has been suspended from WAR! by Eric Helmroy for a week following more rascist comments. Every racist comment from now on by Magnum will double each ban. The current ban means Magnum will miss EXTINCTION `96 and the War!Lord 20-man Battle Royale. Match 1 Thunder gets a bye Match 5 Thunder Vs.
