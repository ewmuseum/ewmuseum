That was Sabin Figaro with some pre-recorded comments. He`ll be fighting General Leo of the Agonizers in our first match -
The Insanity match. Almost. We couldn`t afford the tilting floor, but we`ve got the sealed cage, we`ve got the barb wire and the fire and we`ve got two maniacs who are going to go toe to toe to kick each other our of War! for 30 days!
Rick - Somebody is going to stop this feud tonight. These guys genuinely hate each other. There will be blood tonight.
Bob - And it is blood that the fans want, right? Rick - As long as it isn`t mine. Bob - We have some other amazing matches tonight. Big Duke, having signed a
2 month $1million contract, is back and fighting Brett Stevens.
Rick - Big Duke is by far the most highly paid wrestler in War. The next highest paid is Seth Blitzspear on $750,000 a year, and Tonnage on half a million each year.
Bob - It isn`t suprising then that War! is having, ahem, accountancy troubles when they are throwing all their money at their stars.
Rick - But guys like Big Duke so deserve it Bob. Bob - You get what you deserve in this world, and if American Patriot gets it right tonight he could get what he deserves - The War!Lord title.
We also see Seth Blitzspear get his first title shot against Tonnage. And with Seth back to championship winning form, I`ve got a feeling he`ll be champ by the end of tonight.
Rick - Yeah, the champion of losers. Seth may have dominated the types of
Randy Blake, but there is no way at all that he`ll beat Tonnage.
Bob - Before we go, I`d just like to say on behalf of the fans, thank you
Brutus Judd for fighting the good fight. We wish you well in any future career.
~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~ ~~~ (ALMOST) INSANITY MATCH GENERAL LEO (7) Vs. SABIN FIGARO (7) Both wrestlers faced off and Figaro was a bit taller and a bit heavier than General Leo, but not by much. For the first couple of minutes it was an atypical brawl, going neither way. Then Leo managed to backdrop Figaro into the cage and onto the flaming barb wire. Leo continued an exercise in cruelty, crushing Sabins face against the wire. the fans were chanting for
Figaro, but he couldn`t hear them with the interferring noise coming from the
PA and the oddly flashing and nauseating lights. Blood was all over the mat and it was from Sabin. Then Sabin got himself together and suplexed Leo, then went for a military press and hossed Leo into the cage. Leo now had severe lacerations as well. He started to back off from Figaro, then struck
with a terrible low blow. Leo returned to a lacerating attack. Here we join the commentary.... Bob - Leo gets Figaro in a figure four....Figaro in pain Rick - Heee, I love to see Sabin suffer like this. He`s trying to power out. What a moron. Bob - But he is, much to General Leo`s suprise....but not to the fans...and he`s turned it over...Sabin grabs hold of the ropes and makes Leo suffer some more...is Leo going to give up...he`s been it for quite a while now...and finally Figaro lets go.
Rick - Stupid, stupid move on behalf of Figaro. He should have just left it locked in. Bob - Sabin throws the bloody Leo to the ropes....Leo comes off...Sabin with the back body drop...NO! Leo catches him...he`s got his head locked between his legs.
Rick - Leo is going to go for a powerbomb. Bob - It`s all going to be over for Sabin if Leo does it....and he does....and it`s into a pin. But Sabin locks his legs around Leos head...he flips Leo over...Leo down in suprise...Sabin with a ‘Bums Rush’. He covers......1!
2! Bob - 3! Sabin Figaro wins. It`s goodbye to General Leo for 30 days! Rick - What an intense, blood filled match. Both men will need to take thirty days off just to recover from this match.
Bob - The fans loved it. They loved Sabin. Sabin loves them. General Leo is pissed off.
Rick - I don`t like that look on his face...actually, I do. He`s sneaking up on the celebrating Sabin Figaro...and double-ax handles Sabin into the fiery barb-wire...Sabin is screaming with pain...his face is on fire...Somebody put that fire out...it seems that Sabin is going to have to pay a visit to Dr. Tommy.

BIG DUKE w/ King Benny (9) Big Duke returned to War! determined to earn as much money for as little work, and made a good start by slaughtering Stevens in the first few minutes.
Stevens attempted a comeback, and partially did. However, Big Duke backdropped him out of the ring, and when he got up, Tyler Stevens was there to execute a HeartBreaker on his brother. Stevens rolled Brett back in and
Duke covered easily for the pin. After the match, Tyler told Brett that it was King Benny behind the assault. Brett believed him.

------------------------------------------ Stu. AGAGUCK (7) Vs. THE RECKONER (8) Agaguck, from Quebec, Canada measured an amazing 7`4” and weighed 430lbs. It was no wonder the 6`5”, 323lb Seattle resident The Reckoner had immense trouble against a man of this size. Agaguck chocked The Reckoner, and kicked him all the way across the ring and out of it. Reckoner rallied, but found himself powered out to the outside again by Agagucks forearms.
The Reckoner tried a different tactic, trying to get Agaguck down, but he couldn`t. Agaguck slammed him hard and went for the pin. We join the commentary...
Bob - Agaguck goes for the cover.... 1 2 Bob - How did the Reckoner kick out!?!? Rick - With supidity. Watch him get an amazing beating from the new beast of War! Watch out Tonnage! Agaguck is THE man in War! now.
Bob - My god, you change your alliances like you change your underpants. Rick - Three times a year, when they get stale. That`s right. Bob - Agaguck throws the Reckoner down again. Reckoner rolls out. Agaguck follow. Reckoner picks a chair up and slams it on Agagucks head!
Rick - No effect! The big guy is laughing. Bob - Reckoner tries again....and this time the chair breaks in half! Rick - That`s a metal chair...the big man is looking giddy. Bob - The Reckoner gets back in the ring. Agaguck follows. Reckoner has slipped out again and comes from behind. He slaps on a sleeper. Agaguck tries to shake the Reckoner off....he can`t....Agaguck goes down...the ref raises Agagucks arm and it drops three times! The Reckoner wins!
Rick - A suprising and amazing win for the Reckoner! I guess we`ve learnt tonight never to doubt the Reckoner.
