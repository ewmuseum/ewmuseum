SUBJECT: [WAR] WARZONE 4 (27/10/95) DATE: Fri, 27 Oct 95 16:28:10 GMT WRESTLING UNLIMITED PRESENTS............. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION presenting WARZONE 4 Live from Amsterdam in the Netherlands With your Reservoir Hosts, "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies and gentlemen, tot he Friday night you've all been waiting for. We have sixteen of the top wrestling matches in the country today. We're in a very relaxed, but
I must say smokey, atmosphere here in Amsterdam. We're graceing the world famous "Redlight Dome" for tonights event, and I can assure you, it'll be a card injected with more than just action.
Bfrog: Damn right. Amsterdam. What a place to be. One of the greatest cities in the world, and an excellent place to stop on our
World War Tour. We've been following Adolf Hitlers tour of 1939-45, and Amsterdam is just one of the great places he visited in
Europe. And boy, has Europe come to America Bob. Bob: Eric, you're not wrong there. With the addition of
Youth Waters and "Cardinal" Andrea Mancine to the ranks of WAR! we now have six Europeans! And not any Europeans come tougher than
"European
Havoc" Jean Van Basten. He'll have words with ToxicShock before his match today against his fellow popular wrestler Sid
"StrayCat" Starr. Bfrog: Lets talk about something more important Bob. Let's talk about the wrestler who is going to be making confessions today when he meets the Reverand J.R.Death.
Bob: Jerry Godan? Bfrog: Yes, Jerry Gordan. For so long now he's been lying about being a wrestler. But no longer. The Reverand is going to make him punish for his sins and then take the International War title.
Bob: And speaking of the religious aspect of wrestling, the highly controversial, and I dare say blasphemous wrestler Andrea
Mancini
will be meeting SSBK.
Bfrog: The ecclesiastical european is going to kick shit out of the atheistic American. Simple as that.

Can Blake truly keep off the behemoth that tonnage is. Bfrog: I'm a very good friend with Blake. I admire his ability. On any given day, he can beat anyone. Heck he beat Blitzspear a couple of times. Bob: But who do you think will win? Bforg: As I said, Tonnage is a bigman. He's gota future. Blake can beat anyone. Bob: But can Blkae beat Tonnage? Bfrog: I gotta go pee.... Bob: Well, he evaded that answer. But the question shall be unfolded later tonight when "The Economist" Randy Blake defends his
"World War" title against Tonnage. Personally, I don't care who wins. I don't like either of them. Nor do the fans. And if you ask me, they both a bunch a of wa...
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ In matches that are too low-profile to get a decent report.... "The Cardinal" Andrea Mancini obliterated the The Star Spangled Butt Kicker with his Top-rope DDT and then tied SSBK to the ropes in the corner as a sort of crucifix. Mancini then continued to pummel
SSBK until Seth Blitzspear came to the rescue...... (Nice Mr.) Nexus completely outbrawled Dan Kaufmann, but Kaufmanns will to win was too much and they ended up battling to a time-limit draw...Kid
Enigma
& Youth Waters put on the best display of high-flying moves ever seen, and proved that they are both excellent light-heavyweights.
Enigma, however, had the staying power, and when Waters mis-timed a flying dropkick, the match was Kid Enigmas....Sabin Figaro beat
Rumblefish
at his own game of brawling, and when he added some technical moves later on, Rumblefish was truly beaten. But back in the dressing rooms, Rumblefish didn't take too kindly to the defeat and beat
Figaro up with his barbed-wire baseball bat. Figaro had to be taken to hospital and will be out of action for a week.Rumblefish received a one-match suspension and a $15,000 fine...
OG GARY TRENT (45) Vs. "THE MASTER" AKIRA HASHIMATA (55) Trent controls the match early on, wearing The Master down with forearms, kicks and elbow drops. Akira rallies and locks OG in a boston crab, but Trent gets tot he ropes. Akira then begins a full scale attack, but OG kicks out of pins on many occassions.
The match is then reversed in tide three or four times, until, in the
13th
minute... Bob: Akira is going for a backslide...but OG is battling...and he's twisted it around...out of nowhere a doubel-under arm power bomb...and that's the pin...and good win for Trent.
Match Rating: **** ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ PAINKILLER (70) Vs. THE MANDARIN (30) It's Painkiller all the way. At 7ft and 465lbs, he simply squashes the Mandarin who only gets in a few blows. Yet in the sixth minute, this happens...
Bob: And there goes Painkiller with his "DoublePainclaw"...he's lifted the Mandarin above his head...he's gonig to throw him into the crowd...but in comes Master Po and whacks Painkiller on the back of the legs...Painkiller collapses...and the referee isn't making any decision...Mandarin has fallen on Painkiller....and he's got the pin!

ToxicShock: Jean, I'd like to make it clear that you arranged this interview. Van Basten: I sure did Tox. I've come out here today to reply to a fellow European. But not a fellow. Not a good fellow. This man will cheat to win. He will turn away from the fans and he will not have honour. That man is "FrenchFury" Jean de Mort. De
Mort,
you
offered an alliance with me, when Brutus Noir was more of your ilk.

I will not let it be slurred. So at Masters of War 5, if you'll accept "FrenchFury" I will beat you in the squared-circle and prove that not all Europeans are scum like you. And furthermore...
Bob: StrayCat isn't waiting for the match to begin...he's flown off the top rope and clotheslined Van Basten from behind...the bell has rung....and StrayCat covers....1....2.....3! A shock victory there!
Bfrog: And hopefully De Mort will prove that not all Europeans babble on as much as Van Basten. Match rating: * BOOSTER DUNNE (62) Vs. JOEY THE CLOWN (38) It takes Booster Dunne 23.43 seconds to beat the Clown who just wouldn't give up. In a intensely fought contest, Dunne got a DQ victory over the clown by planting some brass-knuckles on him, which the ref saw and promptly DQ'd The Clown.

Canadian Carnage (World War Tag Title) Canadian Carnage are on the defensive throughout the match, and a determined Ghetto Boyz team make sure they do their best to bring the belts back. Aderondac Brothers Vs. Divine Alliance (International War Tag Title) This was the match of the evening. Lasting 57minutes, the two teams both produced their best, but neither could put the other popular team down. The match ended by Zeus and Bombsquad knocking heads accidently, falling, and both getting counted down. Neither could get up before the 10 count. The match was a draw, and as a result, both teams will meet again at Masters of War 5.
