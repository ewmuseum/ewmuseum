Acts of WAR!

22 November 1996

Dave Scott: Welcome everyone to Acts of WAR! Tonight, we'll have three championship matches plus several other intriguing match-ups. WAR!
hasn't sent in a replacement for my former broadcast colleague Booster Dunne yet, so I'm flying solo tonight. Fortunately, ToxicShock is here to manage things in the Toxic Pit, so let's go to him with his guests, Kalifornia Heat, to start things off!
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The scene switches to the murky, green Toxic Pit, which is bubbling like no one's business. The wall behind it raises up, and Toxic Shock canoes his way onto the set to a huge crowd pop...)
TOX: Hey there everybody, and welcome back to the TOXIC PIT!!! My guests tonight are a young tag team, who are having one hell of a busy week! Tonight they'll be facing the men they defeated in only 90 seconds on their WAR!
debut, and let me tell you this, they're gonna find it a lot harder this time. So would you all please give a nice warm
TOXIC welcome for........ KALIFORNIAAAA HEAAAAT!!!!! (California Love starts up over the pa system, and Matt Powers and Danny DeLane walk down to the set to a massive crowd pop, high fiving the fans as they go...)
Tox: Matt! Danny! Welcome to the Toxic Pit! Danny(waving his hand in front of his face): Aw, man! You ever heard of air freshener, Tox?
Matt: Hey Danny-boy, don't knock it. This reminds me of the shack I grew up in... Danny: Rather you than me dude... TOX: O.K, guys, tonight you're once again facing Chaos and Mayhem, and believe me, from what I've heard, these guys want to tear your faces off. Danny(grinning): No, they're not-happy-chappies are they? Apocalyspe, you guys should lighten up. You know, get out once in a while, work on your tan, have a quick surf, that sort of thing.
TOX: I mean it, Danny. They want to prove that you can't beat them, shouldn't have beat them, and won't ever beat them again. They want to HURT you guys.
Matt: Tox, we all know what these guys want. Point of the matter is, they _ain't_ gonna get it. (Crowd Pop) We're gonna burn them up all over again. Choas, Mayhem, it may take a little longer then 90 seconds to beat you this time, but we DID NOT come here tonight to lose.
Danny(grinning mischievously): Damn straight. Boys, you think WE'RE gonna be pushovers? You might well get one hell of a rude awakening. Or you might just get left Kalifonia dreamin'. We're gonna give you one hell of a surprise here tonight.
Powers: Word. Chaos, Mayhem, you better be ready for the fight of you're life, because the best from the west side are here to burst your bubble. See _you_ guys in the ring.
We out. (Both men throw their hands in the air to a huge crowd pop as California Love starts up again, and they walk back to the dressing room area, high fiving the fans as they go.)
TOX: I guess it's back to ringside.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ DS: Kalifornia Heat will be in action later. To start things off, we have another tag team match, the All-Americans and Canadian Carnage. All-Americans w/Miss America Vs. Canadian Carnage w/Sir John A Mcdonald IV The All-Americans hit the ring attacking the Carnage before they knew what hit them. Borbone laid out Canuck with a piledriver while Brunswick dropkicked Mountie II from the top rope. The All-Americans continued to batter the Carnage until going for the pin after a double suplex on Mountie
II. Canuck made the save, but was thrown out of the ring by Brunswick.
Borbone went to the top rope with Mountie II to perform his All-American
Slam, but Canuck reached up and tripped him. Borbone flipped over onto the mat with Mountie falling on top of him. The ref counted to three, giving Canadian Carnage an upset win.
Winner: Canadian Carnage by pinfall Rating: *** Time: 6:10 Nemesis w/Purity Vs Candyman w/Nomad Nemesis, weighing only 215 pounds, had to use all his agility and speed to get past Candyman's power advantage. And he did. Candyman had several good runs against Nemesis, but was unable to finish him off after several attempts to get him in position for the Boo-ya!. Nemesis kept slipping out or flipping over Candyman's head to escape. Nemesis ducked a clothesline and countered with a spin wheel kick, stunning Candyman. After a frankensteiner and a leg drop, Nemesis went for the pin but Candyman kicked out. Nemesis came off the ropes with a crossbody, but Candyman caught him and attempted another Boo-ya!. Nemesis wrapped his legs around
Candyman's head and brought him down with a headscissors instead. A couple of moves later, Nemesis rolled him up with an inside cradle for the win.
Winner: Nemesis by pinfall Rating: ***1/2 Time: 9:22 Little Chill w/Shaz Vs. The Bounty Hunter Little Chill tried to sneak attack Bounty Hunter but the wily veteran caught him with a forearm shot. Bounty Hunter pounded on Chill with kicks and punches before throwing him over the top rope. Shaz encouraged Little Chill to regroup. Hunter brought Chill back into the ring and started dishing out punishment again. Little Chill resorted to a low blow to slow Hunter's momentum. He took down Hunter with a Russian legsweep and then tried to work him over on the mat. Chill went for his Little Death spinning piledriver, but Bounty Hunter backdropped him. Chill landed on his feet and hit Hunter in the midsection with a kick when he turned around. Hunter buckled over and this time Little Chill was successful in administering the Little Death.
Winner: Little Chill by pinfall Rating: *** Time: 7:14 DS: Our next match will see the new International tag champions, Kalifornia Heat, wrestle Apocalypse. Apocalypse are on their way to the ring right now. Some guy is coming up to them - he's dressed like a giant foam tombstone! Wierd. He hands Mayhem an envelope and leaves. Apocalypse open the envelope... There's a note inside.
Can we get a close-up?... Yes, it says "The End." What's that supposed to mean? Who knows? Maybe someone's trying to psyche them out before their match...
Non-Title Match International Champions Kalifornia Heat Vs. Apocalypse Kalifornia Heat made their first appearance since capturing the International tag team titles at BattleLines. WAR! veterans, Apocalypse, provided the opposition. Chaos started against Matt Powers. The two exchanged power moves for a while before tagging in their partners. Mayhem and DeLane wrestled a faster-paced style for a couple of minutes. DeLane caught Mayhem with a reverse neckbreaker and tagged in his partner. The
International champs worked over Mayhem with frequent tags and doubleteams.
Chaos saved his partner and a four-way melee ensued. When order was restored, Chaos was in control. Apocalypse used double-teaming maneuvers on Powers and got a couple of near falls. DeLane ran into the ring and all four guys went at it again. Chaos accidentally hit Mayhem with an elbow to the face, allowing Powers to get Mayhem with his Shockwave bulldog. DeLane dropkicked Chaos out of the ring while Powers got the pin.
Winner: Kalifornia Heat by pinfall Rating: **** Time: 11:34 DS: This next match was supposed to be Helel against Arron Iron, but due to his disappearance, Helel won't be here. Newcomer Charles Martel was quick to take his place and sign the contract. Martel makes his debut in WAR! in this match...
Charles Martel Vs. Arron Iron Martel and Iron locked up in a high-flying match up. Highlights included
Iron's superplex of Martel and a sunset flip from the top rope that almost got Iron the win. Martel had a few great moves as well, like catching Iron with a frankensteiner off the top rope and a legdrop off the top. Martel poked Iron in the eye, opening up an opportunity to land a savate kick to the side of his face. The one-time Unified champion fought back against the newcomer and nailed him with a DDT. Iron was slow to make the cover, allowing Martel to get to the ropes. The end came when Arron went for a moonsault only to hit the mat hard when Martel got out of the way. Martel rolled him up, grabbing the ropes for leverage, and got the pin.
Winner: Charles Martel by pinfall Rating: **** Time: 14:53 008 Jim Bondo Vs. Nomad w/Candyman Bondo got off to a quick start against Nomad with a beautifully executed dropkick, sending Nomad out of the ring and on top of Candyman. Candyman was not happy about that and tried to interfere several times later in the match. Bondo remained in control of the match despite Candyman's attempts and Nomad's skilled ringwork. Nomad knew counters to many of Bondo's holds, but Bondo was able to counter the counters. Bondo finished the match with a face-first suplex.
Winner: Jim Bondo by pinfall Rating: *** Time: 5:59 Jerry "Goon" Gordon Vs. Big Duke Big Duke seemed a little distracted in this match with his big loser-leaves-
WAR! match coming up soon against Robski. Jerry Gordon wrestled a solid match, preventing Big Duke from using his power advantage. The Goon gut wrench suplexed Big Duke and dropped an elbow off the top rope. Gordon went for a couple of pin attempts with a schoolboy roll-up and an inside cradle, but Duke escaped. Duke then blocked a suplex and suplexed Gordon instead, over the top rope. Duke went out after him and slammed him into the corner post before throwing him back in. Duke seemed more focused as he viciously worked over Gordon. Gordon had another comeback in him as he DDTed Duke when Duke tried a backdrop. He didn't quite fully get Duke with it and the time taken by Gordon to go for the pin resulted in an easy kickout for Duke.
Duke managed to take control again and nailed the Goon with a piledriver, finally getting the win in a hard-fought match.
Winner: Big Duke by pinfall Rating: *** Time: 13:32 World WAR! Tag Title Match Industrial Revolution Vs. The Lost Boys w/Wendy The former champs, the Lost Boys, got a chance to regain their lost titles. Owlstine and MegaDiesel stormed the ring and attacked the Addams brothers. Industrial Revolution completely overwhelmed the Lost Boys. The Lost Boys fought back valiantly, but they were no match for the World champs tonight.
Owlstine hit Greg with an enzuguiri and MegaDiesel followed up with a MegaKnife Powerbomb. Frank made the save, but he was hit with the same enzuguiri/powerbomb combo from the champs. Wendy had had enough and got her boys disqualified when she entered the ring and slapped the ref, whom she accused of letting the Industrial Revolution get away with too much
double-teaming. Winners: Industrial Revolution by DQ Rating: ** Time: 5:10 DS: Industrial Revolution successfully defend their titles to begin our trio of title matches. The WAR!Lord champion, Robski, has been under fire lately from all sides. American Patriot gets his chance at Robski tonight. Let's go to that match...
WAR!Lord Title Match Robski Vs. American Patriot Robski tried to get himself disqualified early on, choking Patriot and not releasing him from the ropes. Patriot didn't want a DQ win, so he responded with some tactics that bent the rules as well. AP threw Patriot over the top rope and followed him outside. They exchanged blows and chairshots before getting back in the ring. AP caught Robski with a diamond cutter, but couldn't put him away. Robski came back with a shot to the jaw and a blockbuster suplex. AP dodged a kneedrop off the second rope and went to work on Robski's leg, eventually getting him in a scorpion deathlock. The champion would not give in, however, and reached the ropes for a break. AP went for the Patriot Missile but only got a shot to the midsection for his troubles. Robski tried to powerbomb him, but his leg couldn't take it.
Following a double clothesline, both competitors were laid out on the mat. DS: The ref's count is at 4.....5.....6... AP is moving.....7.....Now Robski starts to stir....8.... Patriot is up and hits Robski with a double axe-handle. Patriot scoops him up and slams him. AP to the top rope... splash... Robski lifts his knees and Patriot crashes into
them! (Crowd groans) DS: That hurt Robski's leg, too. He's up now but he's limping. He takes Patriot, sets him up for a double underhook suplex - no, it's a tiger driver! He hooks his feet over AP's shoulders....1.....2.....3..No! He didn't get him! Robski argues with the ref... Patriot schoolboys him from behind....1....2....no, not quite! Both up now... Irish whip by Patriot... He whips Robski into the referee! The ref is down!
(Crowd gasps) DS: Robski reaches into his tights and pulls out some kind of metal object! He takes a swing at AP...blocked! The object goes flying out of Robski's hand! Patriot with a right hook and an uppercut!
Robski's stunned! American Patriot is calling for the Patriot Missile! (The crowd cheers) DS: Patriot Missile by AP! He covers Robski. The ref is getting up. The crowd is urging him to make the count. The ref... calls for the bell? He's disqualifying Patriot! Patriot can't believe it!
The ref is pointing to the foreign object in the ring - it's red, white, and blue! Patriot is trying to explain Robski had it in his tights, but he's not buying it. This match is over and Robski keeps his title for another day.
Winner: Robski by DQ Rating: *** Time: 13:12 International WAR! Title Match "Magnum" Biff McGriff w/Felicia McGraw Vs. Seth Blitzspear w/Pat Mills Former World WAR! Champion Seth Blitzspear was looking to add the International title to his resume. McGriff was looking to prove Armageddon's dominance in the singles ranks. Blitzspear stood in the center of the ring daring Biff to take the first punch. McGriff drilled him, knocking him to the mat, but Seth immediately got up and laughed. McGriff scooped him up and slammed him, but once again, Seth stood up and laughed. McGriff was getting very frustrated. Blitzspear used Biff's frustration to get in his first offence. Seth used kicks, punches, clotheslines, and the standard
Blitzspear brawling tactics. McGriff rallied with a powerslam out of nowhere. Finally, McGriff was able to hurt Seth as he repeatedly drove his knee into Blitzspear's back and then locked in a Boston crab. Seth eventually powered out and they went back to brawling all over the place.
DS: McGriff with a knee lift.... Blitzspear fires back with a chop... Biff with an elbowsmash... Blitzspear a forearm smash... Back and forth they go. Those blows don't seem to be having much of an effect on either wrestler! Pat and Felicia are offering words of encouragement to their wrestlers. Blitzspear and McGriff continue to exchange blows!
(The crowd gets to their feet and chant, "Seth! Seth!") DS: Blitzspear off the ropes... huge clothesline sends both to the outside. They're up again in no time. McGriff throws Seth into the railing! Blitzspear just does not feel pain tonight! He picks
Biff up onto his shoulder and rams him into the ring post! Biff *is* hurt, but he continues to fight anyway. This could be a double countout....7....8....Seth tries to get back in the ring, but
McGriff drags him back out and clotheslines him onto the floor....9..... Biff drops an elbow....10! This match will officially end in a draw, but Seth and Biff aren't finished. Officials come out to try to break them up, but they're brawling all the way back to the lockers.
Result: Double countout Rating: *** Time: 11:49 DS: Well, that's all the time we have for tonight. Stay tuned for WAR!Fare, coming up soon! Good night. (c) WAR! 1996
