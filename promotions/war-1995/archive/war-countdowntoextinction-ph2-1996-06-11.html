Superstars At War X — Countdown to Extinction `96 (Phase 2)

11 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please excuse the brevity of some of these matches, but even though my exams I`ve got a lot of other (girlfriend & Euro `96) pressures. As much as I`d like to do it in fuller detail...
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ OPEN TO THE TOXIC PIT... (Toxic Shock is standing on the set of the bubbling, noxious Toxic Pit, looking very sombre.)
Tox: At Extinction last night, the world witnessed one of the most disgusting acts it has ever seen.
TV: > Byron and Tonnage attacking Booster Dunne..... > Byron hitting Dunne's leg with his cane..... > Tonnage hitting Dunne with a vertical splash..... > Tonnage dropping an elbowdrop across his knee..... > Byron busting Dunne wide open with a steel chair..... > The duo slamming Dunnes leg in a door.... Tox: Prior to this, both Lord Byron and Tonnage were sceduled to wrestle for the World WAR! Title. Byron stepped into the ring, shook
Tonnages hand, and left. Now we know why. It gives me no pleasure to introduce my next guest. So would everyone please give the proper response to.....Lord Byron.....
(Lord Byron walks out onto the set. His music, Intermezzo from Karelia Suite, can barely be heard above the deafening boos and the chants of Jack McMurphy. Byron smirks at the crowd, and steps up to Toxic Shock.) LB (smugly): Third time lucky, eh Mr. Toxic? Tox: Byron, that was one of the most despicable things I have ever... LB: SHUT UP!!! IF I WANTED YOUR OPINION, I'D BEAT IT OUT OF YOU!!!! (Byron calms slightly) You know, Mr Toxic, I do hope our friend Booster Dunne can make the match. It will be a real pleasure watching him suffer again.....DUNNE!!! I'm not through with you yet!!! There will not be a third time! I'm going to make you suffer like no one has before!
Tox: By.. LB (glaring at him): I'M NOT FINISHED! I don't care what you want, or what all these (Byron gestures at the crowd) ignorant PEONS want!
Tonight, the International Championship is mine! (Byron turns and walks away from the set. As he goes, one of the fans spits at him. Byron slaps the fan for his trouble.) CUT TO OPENING CREDITS A mountain with Seth Blitzspears face chiseled into it...a fist bursts from behind it smashing it...out of the debris emerges Tonnage with the World
War Championship belt...camera zooms towards the giant sized Tonnage who promptly swipes at it...the screen goes spinning and goes for miles before smashing into the WarDatorium through the glass roof...shards of glass fall with it in slow motion, and on the shards we see many different images...Booster Dunne slamming Lord Byron who pulls him over into a small package...American Patriot waving the U.S.flag (then morphs into “The
American” Butch Williams waving the stars and stripes)...Lost Boys pushing each other...their shard smashes into the image of Theatre of Pain...OG
Gary Trent holds aloft his Liberty Championship with a faint image of the
Jammer behind him...Stone Age making their way to ringside...Jason MidNight with some flashing lightning...Brutus “The Sidewinder” Noir breaking Johnny
Missiles nose with the bell...The Reckoner being PowerBombed by Bane, apparently shattering the shard as it falls onto the ring forming the War!
Logo to the cheers of a massively packed WarDatorium... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION poetically presents... SUPERSTARS AT WAR X - COUNTDOWN TO EXTINCTION 96 Phase 2 Live from the Wardatorium, Buffalo. June 11th With you big-screen, big-wallet hosts, “Avocado” Bob Whitely & Rick “Ricky” Soal ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, X-Wings and Tie-fighters, to SuperStars at War TEN! Yes, 10! This is the first WAR! card to reach the number 10.
All the others keep on changing their names, but we haven`t. Last night, at SuperStars at War! 9, it really was superstars at war.
Bob:There was a great shock upset <Flashback to StormStalker pinning Seth Blitzspear> Bob: Craig JayHawk brought his own gang with him, and brawled with Brutus
Noirs gang <Flashback to brawl at Blade/Noir match) Bob: Armageddon let Robski know of their dissatisfaction with him <Armageddon, led by Tonnage, chasing Robski with barbed-wire baseball bats>
Bob: The Lost Boys retained their titles via dubious means
<Wendy smashing LadySaurs face into the steps> Bob: And Lord Byron made his impact not just once... <Lord Byron pulling American Patriot off Booster Dunne> Bob:...But twice! <Horrific scenes of Byron and Tonnage smashing Booster Dunnes leg to pieces> Bob: But if that wasn`t enough...if that wasn`t enough...The Lost Boys have again attacked The Ghetto Boyz in one of the most heinous attacks. It happened just before we came on air. The good news is we had a camera on the scene.
The Ghetto Boyz had just arrived, in time for their match against Detroit Doggpound. As they walked across the car park with OG Gary Trent, the cowardly, despicable Lost Boyz tried to run them over. Trent pulled
Folk Curtis out of the way, but BDV wasn`t quick enough. Fortunately, the car suffered more damage than BDV. The Lost Boyz leapt out of the car and assaulted the Ghetto Boyz and OG Gary Trent with baseball bats, before they turned on the cameraman. We have no further information as yet, but it looks as though the Ghetto Boyz, and possibly OG Gary Trent, will not be turning up at their matchs.
RS: Hey, hey, quit talking. If Street Level have a problem with Lost Boys and Wendy, they can take it to the courts. But what about tonights card? New International War! champion? <Smirk>
Bob: If Booster fails to turn up, he forfeits the title. Nobody knows as yet whether he will be turning up, but he is not in the building. Byron,
I`m sure, will be receiving a fine for his actions. And as for Tonnage...
RS: Tonnage will retain the World War! title, even with T.J. “Blunder”
Decan as a special guest referee. Seth may ideal the Ultimate Wariror, but tonight he`ll just be the Ultimate Loster! HAHAHAH!
Bob: Yes, very funny. Also tonight, having just escaped defeat against
Johnny Dreamer, OG Gary Trent faces The Jammer, who suffered an unfortunate defeat at the hands of Rick Showboat. The fans love the Jammer, so they`ll be split between the two.
RS: Don`t forget the tag-title defence by the Lost Boys against Theatre of
Pain. Bob: I haven`t. Nor I have forgotten the final 8 matches of Countdown to
EXTINCTION `96 that lead to the $1m prize and the prize of being called
“The Exterminator `96”. But first, lets go to the tag action that is waiting for us. And in the crowd tonight, our special guest fan is
Courtney Cox, of ‘Friends’ fame! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ GHETTO BOYZ (7) Vs. DETROIT DOGGPOUND (8) Despite the horrific attack by the Lost boys prior to the card, Ghetto Boyz emerged to the cheers of the crowd, albeit bandaged up. They started well, but the bandages would be their downfall, because when they were ripped away by Doggpound, the wounds were easy to work on and the Ghetto Boyz suffered a tremendous hammering, before FCF succumbed to a Tony Pitt piledriver. Winners, Detroit Doggpound.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION (9) Vs. STONE AGE (6) w/o LadySaur Another tremendous display by the awe-inspiring Industrial Revolution compounded the troubles that Stone Age were having without LadySaur. Stone
Age relied on their basic instincts, but Industrial Revolution had the preperation and power to put Stone Age away after five minutes, MegaDiesel sinching the MegaKnife Powerbomb on IceCap.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~APOCALYPSE (8) Vs. CHAOS KNIGHTS (7) Disorder & Mayhem Vs. Chaos & Mayhem was an exctining battle, both teams displaying their special moves, and indeed a full repertoire, but failed to do to much in the way of damage. It was a shock moonsault by Apocalypse`s
Mayhem on Chaos Knights Mayhem that enabled Apocalypse to “pick up the three points” as we say in the UK.
MR: * * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ COUNTDOWN TO EXTINCTION 96 PHASE 2 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ THE RECKONER (7) Vs. BANE (9) Bane went for the quick victory, kicking The Reckoner to the midsection then going for a powerbomb. The Reckoner had too much resolve, and blocked. From there, Bane showed more of the fire for victory, and his powermoves slowly worked the big Reckoner down. Reckoner fought back in pockets of resistance, but never really had Bane in any trouble. Bane, in the tenth minute, went for the Powerbomb again, and got the victory.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ < A stadium filled to the rafters awaits the announcement of who exactly the Resurrected is, A masked man walks to the ring>
Man: Haha, all you people wonder who could it be. Who is the one spook hiding in the closet and waiting for the Cardinal.
<Removes mask and reveals himself as The Preacher> Preacher: Haha,. I know it Mancini is seeing me and thinking two thoughts, both equally bad. It could be either of my men. But for this tournament Mr. Mancini, hells bells ring solemnly for you.
<The stadium turns to black, and loud bells shake the arena> Preacher: Making his debut comeback, ladies and gentlemen, hold your souls, here is the one and only, Former WAR! Heavyweight Champion,
Former WAR! International Champion and current bloodthirsty gospel reader, THE REVERAND J.R. DEATH!
<The crowd buzzes as Death makes his way to the ring> Preacher: Mancini, sorry but your luck has hit a brick wall. Death pulls no punches, and makes no acceptions in the ring.
Death: Cardinal, I am forced to tear into your soul tonight, and rip out whatever it is that would poscess you to enter this tournament. I am a fellow holy man, and blessed be thy pain. I will not give you the sinner's treatment, I will put you away nobly, but make no mistake I come not to be overcome. After these supposed drug accusations have been hurled about trying to impede my mission by those who wish to stop my divine work, I find myself more then ever in a saving mood. So
Cardinal come to the ring and find out what Death has waiting for you.
<The Arena turns dark, and the bells ring once more, as Death makes his way down the aisle>
Bob: WOAH! The mystery opponent is “The Reverand” J.R.Death! This is going to be very very interesting. RS: Just look at Death, 7`6” in height, over 400lbs, and the former World
War! champion before losing to Stein “Ice” Berg. But that was only because his contract had expired. But now he`s back, and everyone is in trouble.
Can you imagine Bane Vs. “The Reverand” J.R.Death? What a match that would be! Bob: Perhaps Tonnage should be the most concerned. Anyhow, Death is accompanied by The Preacher and, after recovering from a coma, “The French
Fury” Jean de Mort, currently in a wheelchair.
Death is in the ring. Here comes Mancini. Still without his Acolytes. He`s going to have to rely on his pure talent here.... RS: Just look, Death is over a foot taller than Mancini. This isn`t going to last long! Bob: you may be right! Throat slam by Death! Now he puts the 325lb
Cardinal into a torture rack....and he gets the submission...there must be some sort of record there!
RS: Yeah! Death has instilled fear into a record amount of people! Everyone, be prepared to give up your titles, Death is in town! MR: * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SABIN FIGARO (6) Vs. AMERICAN PATRIOT (7) They have fought each other a lot recently. Now it is Sabin Figaro who has the Interfed title. It wasn`t on the line tonight, though Patriot fought as though it was. Patriot had the edge, Figaro looked exhausted following a busy schedule. American Patriot made up for his failiure to grab a title yesterday by pinning Figaro following a “Patriot Missile”.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SINDER BLOKK (7) Vs. COLOSSUS (7) Both of these big men tried to throw their weight around on the other. The result was, at first, a slow, heavy match. Later on, the match picked up momentum, but neither was really getting the edge. The battle went to the outside, and they were so wrapped up in a brawl that cancelled each other out. Colossus broke off, threw Blokk into the crowd and scrambled into the ring, just in time to make the ten count. Colossus won by countout.
MR: * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ JOHNNY “THE SIDEWINDER” MISSILE (9) Vs. DEATHJESTER (5) Following the alleged murders by Deathjester, he had spent little time in the ring and a lot in the courts. The result was extensive ring rust. On the other hand, “The Sidewinder” was well and truly up for a fight, and
blew everyone away with his high-flying antics. After three flying dropkicks, Missile dropped a flying legdrop onto DeathJesters throat and got the victory, progressing to the next round in the process.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “HOLLYWOOD” JOHNNY DREAMER (8) Vs. SAM LOBO (9) This match impressed all the fans, who weren`t expecting too much from the lesser SuperStars of War! They were treated to a high-impact, high speed, technical match, featuring hold and counterhold. Sam Lobo was the stronger in the technical area, but Dreamers flying ability gave him a strong
advantage. Lobo was having the better of the match, but then with one minute left, Dreamer executed a perfect flying sunset flip. Lobo kicked out, but then found himself on the end of a neckbreaker, and just as the
bell went, the three count went to Dreamer. Dreamer progresses after a very close match. MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ SUNA “LOVELY TO TOUCH” GIB (7) Vs. “JAMAICAN SUPERSTAR” ARRON IRON (8) Suna Gib replaced an injured Butch Williams. After a time in the background of War! Iron came back in fine fashion, rocking the silvery one with suplexes and atomic drops. Gib seemed to enjoy the atomic drops most of all, and seemed to be throwing himself at Iron for a scoop-slam. Iron was thinking it was too easy, then Gib did a sucker punch. He tried to win from there, but the fans rallied behind Arron, and he fought back, using the “Iron Hammer” on Gib to get the victory and progress into the next round.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ TYLER STEVENS (8) Vs. BOUNTY HUNTER (7) Hunter could only offer heavy brawling in this match. Tyler Stevens wouldn`t fall into that trap, keeping the match at his pace, and Hunter at a distance. Once or twice Stevens did have to brawl, but he was generally just a bit too smart for Hunter. Hunter almost had Stevens on a few occassions when he changed tactics and tried to actually wrestle Stevens, but Stevens outwrestled him, and eventually put the big, bad, Bounty Hunter to sleep with a sleeperhold.
MR: * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ TITLE MATCHES! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War Tag Team Title Match LOST BOYS Vs. THEATRE OF PAIN The Lost Boys failed to turn up first time their music was played. And on the second. But on the third, they emerged, covered in bandages and stitches. Apparently, Ghetto Boyz had managed to do as much damage to the
Lost Boys as vice versa. The Lsot Boys simply entered the ring, and as soon as the bell went, they left and lost by countout. Theatre of Pain wouldn`t have it like that and chased after them, but the Lost Boys escaped, retaining their tag-titles.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ LIBERTY TITLE MATCH OG GARY TRENT (7) Vs. THE JAMMER (9) Light heavyweight brawler faced light heavy weight allround highflyer. Trent started by overpowering the Jammer, but Jammer upped the tempo, and rocked Trent with some hit and run blows such as dropkicks.
Trent got back in the game following a clever clothesline, and from there put the Jammer in danger of losing. Jammer used all his will and escaped several pin attempts by Trent after heavy power moves.
Jammer slowly regained dominance, and kept it that way for several minutes. Trent was beat, and The Jammer was ready for victory, when suddenly Mark
“The Canadian Strangler” emerged and belted OG Gary Trent with the Liberty
Title, causing the Jammer to lose by DQ and Trent to be knocked out cold.
MR: * * * * ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Toxic Shock:Mark Starr I can't belive what you just did!!! You have just nearly cost OG Trent his prized Liberty Titl...
MS:Trent only keeps his title because I want to beat him for it!! OG
Trent would have lost that match anyways if the Strangler did not interfre!! Gary Trent will now have the privalge of giving Armageddon what was rightfuly there's...Sid Starrs.....and of course mine...The
WAR Liberty Title...Trent you fight with the rest now lose to the best because when you step in the ring with the strangler you don't have a career!!!
Tox:You had no right.. MS:I had the right to do anything I want..if it benefits Armageddon then I do it..YOU UNDERSTAND!!
(Mark picks up Tox and holds him against a wall) Tox:You can't do this...your going to have sevre fines pal I am a WAR offical MS:The pockets of Armageddon run deep and it matters not as long as the WAR commitee get's me my shot.
(Mark makes a choking motion across his neck and drops Tox onto the floor) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ INTERNATIONAL WAR! CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH BOOSTER DUNNE Vs. LORD BYRON Bob: Lord Byron is in the ring. He`s laughing away. Will Booster Dunne turn up? If he doesn`t, under the contract of this match, he forfeits the title.
RS: He`d be wise not to show. I`ve seen the state of his leg. I doubt he can hardly walk.
<Crowd suddenly roar into action as Booster Dunne emerges, seriously hobbling> Bob: Dunne is here! RS: But he`s in no shape to fight, is he? Bob: We`ll have to wait and see. Dunne goes up the steps to get into the ring...he`s having to hold onto the ropes. This, viewers, is a true fighting champion.
RS: This is a true cripple! Bob: Byron gets to work on Dunne before the bell...there is the bell...obviously he is going to work on the legs...but he clotheslines
Dunne out of the ring...Dunne has damaged himself on the fall...and Byron has an iron bar he kept underneath the ring...and he is smashing Dunnes leg again!
RS: Any healing that may have happened in the past 24 hours is history now! Dunne can`t even get up!
Bob: You might be right! Byron rolls Dunne into the ring. He`s got a sadistic smile on his face. He`s going to enjoy pounding Dunne.
RS: Why not? I would. Bob: But you can`t, so that is a redundant statement. Anyway, Dunne is still rolling in agony. Byron goes to pick him up...but Dunne pulls him over and into a pin! Dunne will retain his title!
1! 2! Bob: 3! No, not quite...Byron is furious...he fists Dunne right between the eyes then drags him over to the corner...and slams his knees into the corner post...now he pulls Dunne into the centre of the ring. He`s gonig for the figure four! And he`s got it. Dunne is screaming like he`s never screamed before.
RS: That look on his face is true pain! Bob: But look, he`s twisting it over! Somehow he is managing it....and
Byron is now suffering a reverse figure four...but it is still hurting both of them...and Dunne is not letting go....Byron flips it back over...normal figure four...Dunne is refusing to quit...but the referee calls for the bell...he says thats enough for Booster Dunne.
RS: Wise move. Anything further may have damaged his career. Bob: This means we have a new International War! champion! Lord Byron is now a double title holder. He is the War!Lord and International War! champion. Dunne is still in pain in the centre of the ring. Byron goes over to the man who has held the InterNational War! title for over four months...
RS: And kicks him in the leg some more...and smashes the International
War! championship on Dunnes legs!
Bob: This is despicable! RS: This is WAR! And we have a great new International War! Champion in
Lord Byron! Oh, and a parting gift from Lord Byron, a piledriver on
Dunne!
Bob: The fans are chanting for Seth! RS: He`s got his own concerns right now! Bob: Here he comes! Seth storms the ring and Byron is out of there! RS: Boy, I can`t wait for the World War! title match now. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ **TJ "Thunder" Decan is being lectured by a WAR! corporate exec-u-drone** [Drone] It is important that you keep the best intrests of WAR! in mind as you referee the match between the Champion, Tonnage and Mr. Blitzspear.
[TJ] Don't worry. I'm going to make SURE it's a straight up match. [Drone] That's all well, fine, and good. But you must look at it from a CORPORATE point of view. [TJ] A corporate point of view? [Drone] Yes. Theses two men are the top draws in WAR! at the moment, and, with our current...finacial difficulties, we MUST have them BOTH uninjured and wrestling at EVERY opportunity.
**They look at each for a moment** [Drone] Do you understand? [TJ] You want ME to make sure the fans get a good match, but to make sure neither of them gets hurt?
[Drone] GOOD! We understand one another. [TJ] We sure do. NOT that I going to DO IT! [Drone] MISTER Decan. Remember who holds your contract! [TJ] Have you READ that contract? [Drone] Well, not PERSONALY. No. [TJ] I get a hundred an' fifty grand a year AFTER I win a belt. Do you SEE a belt around my waist?
[Drone] AH...er..no. [TJ] So EVERYTHING I do right now is on the house! [Drone] Well, I can see... [TJ] I've promised Seth, Tonnage, and MORE importantly THE FANS, that this match was going to be called RIGHT down the line!If one OR BOTH of them get hurt, disfigured, OR CRIPPLED during the match...I DON'T
CARE! As LONG as it's by the BOOK! [Drone] WE could always VOID OUT your contract. [TJ]*very, very quietly* Is that a threat? **After a few heartbeats, the drone answers** [Drone]*heading for the door* No! No! Mr. Decan you misunderstood! All I came here to do was wish you a ....good match! THAT'S all!
**The drone hurries out the door as Thunder turns to the camera** [TJ] There's only ONE man in WAR! as big as me, and he says he wants this match to go WITHOUT interference. Seth says HE wants this match to go
WITHOUT interference. Well boys. YOU GOT IT! Keep it in ring, and by the book. Then ANYBODY that wants to stick their nose in, has got to go through ME! Now the FANS can see WHO the champion REALLY is!
**Thunder storms out the door to the ring** ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WORLD WAR! TITLE MATCH TONNAGE (9) Vs. SETH BLITZSPEAR (9) Special guest Referee: T.J. “Thunder” Decan The fans were going mental for Seth as usual as he stormed the ring. He began with a series of clotheslines on Tonnage that made him budge about...oooh, an inch. Then, Tonnage responded with a massive slam....
Bob: That caught Seth by suprise...and so will this...it`s Giganton crush time! Tonnage covers, going for the quick victory...
1! 2! Bob: Seth pushes Tonnage off. That requires a lot of strength. Tonnage pounds Seth with ax-handles and elbow drops to the back.
RS: Seth ain`t getting up no more. Bob: You may be right. Tonnage lays Seth face down in the centre of the ring...off the ropes...vertical splash onto Seths back! And he goes for
another....and connects! Seths body is convulsing. Tonnage turns him over and covers.... 1! 2! Bob: Seth finds the inner reserves to kick out. But if this carries on much longer, there will be no reserves to rely on. Tonnage throws Seth into the corner. He`s toying with him now. Seth hears the chants of the fans...he`s pumping...he`s fights back...he battles back...he blitzes back!....he knockes Tonnage down with a clothesline that must have come from the gods! Seth covers!
1! 2! Bob: No, not even a close three! Tonnageis looking angry...and slams Seth like a ragdoll. Big elbow drop...big body splash...big vertical splash! A cover.
1! 2! Bob: Tonnage gets up! Why didn`t he finish Seth off? RS: Look at the big screen! I think T-Man sees something he doesn`t like! Bob: I don`t believe it...it looks like Brutus Noir and his friends...they have Tonnages wife and they`re beating her around...Noir is saying something to the camera, but I can`t tell what it is! This is unbelievable!
RS: This is terrible! I know Noir is low-life scum, but he has gone to unimaginable depths to get Tonnages attention! He`s attacking his wife for godsake!
Bob: Tonnage seems paralyzed by this! He is, like everyone here, staring at the big WAR! screen in disbelief...Seth comes from behind though...a double ax-handle rocks Tonnage...a big belly to back suplex...I`ve never
seen anyone do that to Tonnage before! Seth covers! 1! 2! Bob: 3! Seth has done it! He`s won the World War! Title again! Tonnage is up. He doesn`t seem to realise that he has been pinned or that he has lost the World War! title...he`s just got up and is off down the aisle...who knows where he is off to!
RS: Seth is enjoying his victory though. I don`t think he realizes what has happened just now to Tonnage.
Bob: The fans are happy though. This is Seths fourth time, I think, as
World War! champion, but it is Tonnage who now lays claim as the man to have held the World War! title longest. We`ve got several very interesting days ahead of us as we try to sort out exactly what has happened here tonight. There are a lot of talking points, but we haven`t got the time to talk about them. I`m sure you`ll be with us for the June 13th interfed rumble, and on June 16th, there will be a War!Fare card, where I`m sure we`ll see a few rematches. On June 19th will be CountDown to Extinction phase 3! Until then, have fun, be careful and be cool!
