WAR!REPORT 5

29 May 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------ WAR! FEDERATION proudly presents WAR!REPORT 5 with your hosts
Avocado Bob & Dr.Nathaniel Darkness Live from the War!datorium --------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: Ladies & gentlemen, welcome one & welcome all to the most packed card ever in the history of War! NuclearWar was a phenomenal event, and the thousands of fans thought so to. There were 32 matches and more importantly,
3 title changes. Yes, that`s right ladies & gentlemen, new champions. Seth
Blitzspear won the BattleBlitz tournament, taking the vacant title. He defends against his opponent that night - "Trailboss" Mike Dallas. Two favourites, I don`t know who to pick.
Dr.Darkness: Pick neither, they`re both losers. Bob: Now, now Doc. I`ve got a feeling you`re just a little sore over the fact that your tag-team ClanDemon lost the tagbelts.
Doc: We`ve won them before and we`ll win them again...and then we`ll dispose of the pesky Youngbloods. And if they even begin to think of joining
Mr.Kang`s stable, they`re gonna find that they moving to the worst interfed stable this side of Mars.
Bob: Well, your personal dislike of Mr.Kang has been well documented, though you haven`t done anything yet.
Doc: We will, in time. And the proffesor as well. They`re both insignificant. Bob: Someone who isn`t insignificant is the Maltese Falcon, the young high flyer from Malta has proved his worth by winning the Freedom belt. Falcon is of course also being pursued by Mr.Kang
Doc: Kang`s loss. Anyhow, DeathThreat will finish him off tonight. Bob: And not forgetting the badblood between Deaththreat & Blitzspear. That will return tonight when Seth tags with Falcon to battle DeathThreat & The
Brander.
Doc: What the Brander did was....well, nothing short of genius if you ask me. He`s scarred Seth`s back, but I think even more importantly, he`s scarred
Seth`s mind. Seth`s thoughts are going to be pre-occupied with The Brander, and that could cost him the world title.
Bob: And there are lots of other great matches on tonight. We`ve got a battle of giants between BombSquad & Massiveman .
Doc: And it`s not often you see Bombsquad being the shorter by 5 inches Bob: We`ve also got a re-match between "The American" Butch Williams and
Johnny Thunder for the International title. Butch is clearly a madman, but he`s no madder than the man who loathes him, the Mountain top Madman, who battles XL-9 tonight. A win for MTM would mean a title shot at "The
American" at WarZone 5. There`s lots of other matches tonight. Unfortunately, the cage match between 20,000 volts and the Question has been cancelled due to the departure of The Question from War. So lets get this card started with an interview with "The Masters of War".
The 6 members of the Masters of War (The coach, WarMaster, Then Bludgeon, the
Fire, BadTimes Billy, DeathThreat & the Brander) enter the ring. ToxicShock is going to interview them, but the booing of the crowd doesn`t die down for a good few minutes. Eventually it does.
ToxicShock: Bludgeon. You`ve got a lot to answer for - namely what the
Masters of War did to Seth Blitzspear.
(The Masters of War laugh, the crowd boo) Bludgeon: Let me tell you something Tox. We`re not here just to wrestle. We`re here to damage. But when we do wrestle, we like to wrestle people worthy of wrestling us - and you - you gormless punk Seth Blitzspear - you`re not worthy of us.
ToxicShock: Judging by your recent comments, would the Maltese Falcon come into that catergory.
Bludgeon: Too damn right. And Johnny Thunder, MountainTop Moron, BombSquad, the Youngbloods, Booster Dunne...all of them...and more...they`re worthless.
ToxicShock: But they`ve beaten you on several occasions Bludgeon: Where did we beat them? ToxicShock: In The Pit..but you did.. Bludgeon: No buts, butt-head. We beat them, simple as that, and when that happens, when you beat someone in the pit, it`s over, you`re the victor. We won the war.
Tox: Some would say the War hasn`t started. Bludgeon: Some would say Elvis was good. The fact is he`s not. And we won the War. All I can say now is that we have new targets...new wars. Tox: New Targets DeathThreat (grabbing the microphone and clotheslining ToxicShock): Are you deaf? Do we have to repeat everything? Yes, new targets, new wars. On two fronts this time. For now, we`re gonna beat up on some new locals. But the big picture is the elimination of Mr.Kangs stable of goody-goodies.
The Fire: And how are we gonna do that? Well, I`d like to introduce right now the man who retired the Question - 20,000 volts - the newest member of The
Masters of War
(20,000 volts enters the ring) The Fire: Hey buddy, I heard you got a little surprise. 20,000 volts: I have...oooh, I have. But it`s a secret. The Fire: Don`t worry mate, I know what it is...and I know it`s shocking. The Masters of War laugh and leave the ring, the crowd boo. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tag team Match Seth Blitzspear & The Maltese Falcon Vs. The Brander & DeathThreat fighting on the first match to allow recovery time, the two teams tear at each other with venom. Late in the match, Seth & the falcon have been enjoying an advantage, keeping DeathThreat in their corner for the whole time. The
Brander unimpressive in his performances against The Falcon has still to face
Seth. In the ring, DeathThreat manages to escape from a piledriver attempt by
Seth. He goes for a tag to The Brander, but The Brander refuses, not wishing to face Seth in the ring. Seth runs up from behind and clotheslines
DeathThreat into The Brander, knocking The Brander on to the floor. Dragging deaththreat to the centre of the ring, Seth tags The Falcon. The falcon ascends to the top and seth uses his flying time move to thrwo DeathThreat out tot he floor. The Falcon, in position, does his "Blackbird" flying splash onto DeathThreat outside the ring, throws DeathThreat into the ring and gets the 3 count at 7.50
--------------------------------- Nexus Vs. Classico Nexus, an english wrestler, escapes from every hold applied by Classico, and, using brass knuckles, smashes Classico in the face, getting a deceitful victory at 2.42
--------------------------------- BombSquad Vs. MassiveMan Both popular wrestlers and both very large. Despite the five inch height difference, Bombsquad shows more desire. The desire proves enough when the match turns into a brawl outside the ring, and BombSquad manages to get in before the 10 count for a victory at 6.22
---------------------------------- TagTeam Contest BloodStreet Brothers (Marlon & Chad) vs. The Youngbloods (Badrock & Shaft) The winner of this bout will face the tag champs next at WarZone5 if the
DivineAlliance win today. Marlon & Chad start before the bell, revealing taped fists that they`d kept hidden in their pockets, attacking The
Youngbloods as they came down the aisle. Cheering evertime they drew blood, their antics proved that you can get blood out of a stone when they lacerated
Badrocks face with their taped fists. The Youngbloods rally though and chase the BSB back to the ring where the match begins (fists untaped). Halfway into the match, both team are covered in blood - Youngbloods blood. Weakened by the vicious assault, Youngblood seem destined to lose. Destiny prevails in the twelfth minute when Shaft, having applied the Longbow onto Chad, gets walloped on the back with a chair by Marlon while the referee was attending to a Marlon-taunted Badrock. Shaft collapses to the floor and Chad gets the pin.
---------------------------------------------- Jacob Demonchile Vs. "Booster" Dunne The ever handsome and everpopular 278lb "Booster" Dunne initially seems freaked by the presence of the wannabe vampire Jacob Demonchilde. With time the fear passes and he gains domination in the match, reversing a figure four
leglock into an Indiandeath lock. Demonchilde grabs the ropes, but he can`t escape from Dunnes clutches. One piledriver and a slam later, Demonchilde mystically recovers, grabbing Dunne by the throat and gazing at him. Dunne seems entranced, ubt resistant. Resistant eonugh for the referee to DQ
Demonchilde for excessive choking. Dunne wins at 8.33 ------------------------------------------------ Spaceman Sid Meyers vs. Inner City Firmer A boot to the gut and Spaceman is on the floor. ICF picks him up, gives him the iron hammer (spinning piledriver to the concrete floor followed by a military press back in) and gets the victory at 1.03. Determined to prove he`s championship material, he continues to beat on Spaceman.
------------------------------------------------- Tag-Team contest Masters of War ( The Fire & BadTimes Billy) Vs. Light & Darkness The high co-ordination skills and high flying over Light & Darkness proves effective until Light, perched on the top-turnbuckle for an elbow drop, is dropkicked off by BadTimes Billy (not legal man). Darkness enters but the
Fire stops him as BadTimes uncharacteristically scales the ropes to do a flying splash on Light. But Badtimes takes too long and Light moves out of the way. Light helps Darkness and they double team The Fire, Darkness dropkicking The Fire and Light flying down with a legdrop from the toprope to get a pinfall at 9.13
------------------------------------- "SideWinder" Brutus Noir Vs. Bludgeon A fairly even match between these two powerhouses until the referee gets knocked out when Noir throws Bludgeon into the ref. Noir goes to cheat but is suddenly nailed from behind by "The American" Butch Williams with a clothesline, screaming "This is for amercia you french faggot". Bludgeon promptly "Bludgeonbomb`s" the French brawler/ex-world champion and gains a pinfall victory at 6.45
------------------------------------------- MountainTopMadman Vs. XL-9 Still incensed with "The American" Butch Williams, MTM takes his anger out on
XL-9. However, XL-9 is unresponsive to the powerslams and clothesline. It is only when MTM sets XL-9 up for the "Madman`s Ire" that XL-9 goes into action, picking the 370lb MTM above his head in a military press and throwing him onto the timekeepers tabel. MTM doesn`t recover quickly enough and a count-out victory goes to the man who thinks he`s a robot, XL-9 at 4.49
-------------------------------------------- Freedom Title Championship Match The Maltese Falcon Vs. DeathThreat DeathThreat is stilll tired from the earlier match where he got a huge beating. The Maltese Falcon outpaces DT and catches him nicely with a few flying forearms and a moonsault or two. He ascends the ropes to finish DT off quickly with "The Blackbird" but 20,000 volts runs to ringside, shocking
Falcon with his stick. Falcon crashes to the floor, but DT get`s DQ`d for the interference at 3.20
----------------------------------------- Tag-Team Championship Match The Divine Alliance Vs. ClanDemon As the Divine alliance, Amon Ra & Zeus stand in the centre of the ring, facing the physically inferior InterDimensional Warrior and DeadPunk, you could almost believe they`re Gods. In the ring though, they appear not so. An early advantage is lost when IDW ducks a flying cross body by Amon Ra.
Smashing Amon Ra over the head with the steel railings, DeadPunk throws Amon
Ra back into the ring for IDW. Escaping from a subsequent "Vortex"
sleeperhold by IDW, Amon Ra tags the powerhouse Zeus who storms the ring, battering DeadPunk & IDW. He throws DeadPunk out to the floor and then powerbombs IDW. Following with a Thunderpress, he tags in Amon Ra who finishes the match with his "SolarBomb" moonsault variation at 6.41
------------------------------------------ International War Title "The American" Butch Williams Vs. Johnny Thunder A surprisingly fair match, each wrestler exchanging holds nad counterholds. But when Thunder gains the advantage, getting "The American" in a chicken wing, Butch starts getting angry. He manages to escape from the hold, following with a flurry of blows to Thunder. Thunder holds on and eventually gains the advantage. Going for his superkick too soon, Williams catches
Thunders foot, sweeps away the other and reverses it into a boston crap that
Thunder cannot escape from. When Thunder refuses to submit, Williams picks him up and onto his shoulder, before ending the match at 14.37 with his
"Superpower-slam" ------------------------------------------ World War Championship Seth Blitzspear Vs. "Trailboss" Mike Dallas Both wrestlers have many fans, and the prospect of facing a wrestler about 8 -10 inches taller than you would be daunting for any wrestler. Not Seth. Seth tries knocking Dallas to the floor with powermoves early on and they prove effective. Shockingly to some, Seth doesn`t allow Dallas to get in one blow. At the five minute mark he throws Dallas tothe ropes for his
"FlingTime" backdrop, but Dallas isn`t weakened enough and telegraphs the move, coming down on Seth with a massive boot to the face, knocking Seth out.
Preparing to go for the pin, 4 members of the Masters of War - Bludgeon,
DeathThreat, BadTimes & 20,000 volts enter the ring, laying into Trailboss initially, and later Seth. With brassknuckles on, the Masters of War soon draw blood, and the hired heavies of BloodStreet Brother, ClanDemon and the other Master of War - the Fire prevent any other wrestlers from entering. About 5 minutes later, Seth & Trailboss both red with blood and knocked out are carted away by medics. WarMaster, their manager, shouts over the microphone "Do you get the message Kang, do you get the message?"
The matchresult is a double DQ. ----------------------------------------------------- Bob: Well, your comments on the events you just witnessed? Doc: Great. Or should I say "Grate"? Oh, what fun seeing both those numble weeds getting hospitalized. However, insider reports tell me that Seth would have been spared had it not been for an earlier incident.
Bob: I know just the incident you mean. We`ve got it on security camera. It clearly shows Seth Blitzspear & The Maltese Falcon doing over The Brander with baseball belts and the Freedom belt before dragging him off somewhere.
Doc: That could explain The Branders abscence a couple of minutes ago. Bob: Well, a couple of minutes ago there was a couple of minutes to go. But now there are none. So, until Friday, on behalf of Dr.Nathaniel Darkness, see ya!
