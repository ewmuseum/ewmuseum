This is a WAR Report from the WAR Dome in Utah Bell Time 7:pm -RUMOR MILL for July the 2nd -Rumor has it that Wendy's search for a new body guard just may be one currently employed in WAR, she might have to shell out some francs to pay for him. -Biff McGriff is being sought by police in New York for a possesion of an unregistered weapon, Biff was last seen driving a white Bronco away from the NY state line.
-More rumors of Mark Starr being an original American is circling the wrestling world, he denies it all saying his a montreal boy 100 % -The Lost boys Greg and Frank are they having troubles between them?? Is this whole Wendy fiascal divinding and conquering??? Could there be a hidden agenda between one of the lost boys and Wendy.
-Bane and Tonnages up and coming match before the legend retires, could Bane be feeling bitter that he is losing a tag partner, is he bound to turn on his former partner before logging a tag match on TV
-Damien Legend has been ripping through competion lately and is soon to be in title contention very soon. -Dum dum de dum, dum dum de dum......is there wedding bells going on between Arron Iron and Wendy...could they be announcing their engament soon?? -Another LB rumor, could the Boys replace Wendy with a manager known as....Wendel??? -Is Seth Blitzspear dead???? A ValueJet recently crashed filled with many superstars from around the world including, The King after announcing he was not dead but merely very sick, Marylin Monroe after coming out of hiding, and of course WAR legend Seth Bliztspear......could this vacat the title??? Will there be a body recovery??? Is he really dead?? Or just contract disputes??
-Finally an unkown business man has been buying up numerous amounts of WAR stock, who are the sellers?? Well this informant has BullFrog Helmroy,
Pat Mills, Toxic Shock and Sir John A. MacDonald as being paid very well for their shares!!!
