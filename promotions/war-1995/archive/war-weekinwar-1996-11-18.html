WAR! Wrestling Federation — News recap

A Week in War!

18 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~ A week in War! with your host Toxic Shock ~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: Welcome everyone to "A week in War!". This is a brief rundown of the events in War! in the past couple of weeks. This news was first transmitted on the War! Hotline (dial
http://members.tripod.com/~wartable/news ) , the first for news and rumours. November 11 ~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: Last monday followed Sunday, which had been preceeded by Saturday. Over those days, War! had made some major signings in Chris Deugau & J.P.Steele. But leaving War!, after failing to show for War!Mongers in a match against Biff McGriff, was Butch Williams.
Bitch now holds a commentary position on some War! cards. The news on Monday began primarily with the release of the details for the Monday 18 War!Fare card. Tonnage, who had lost the
Tournament of Champions final to "Thunder" Decan, was told he would face Decan for a third time in just under two weeks at this card. He was happy enough to be fighting his Armageddon partner.
The other big event for War!Fare was the controversial loser leaves town match that was supposedly signed between Big Duke & Robski. As we shall see, it wasn`t so clear cut.
Later that day, in the evening, which is after the afternoon, at
BattleLines, Jason MidNight beat The Mutilator in the BattleBlitz final. This result quite literally shot MidNight up the rankings, and he now faces Tonnage in potentially a world title match tomorrow at War!Time.
Doug Simpkins also made the news, defeating The Stalker for the Liberty Title. It was a result that shocked many, but not Doug, who has had a very impressive record recently.
The other big result coming from BattleLines was the winners of the tag-team Royal Rumble. Kalifornia Heat survived the heat of the battle and became the champions of the newly reinstated International War!
tag-belts. They were overjoyed at winning, but now face stiff challenges from The Anihilators and All-Americans.
Tuesday November 12 ~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: The news today revolved around Robski, who flatly denied that he had signed a loser-leaves War! match with Big Duke. He was right, and promptly, War! Vice-President "BullFrog" Eric Helmroy, added to the world`s unemployment problem by sacking a lot of people, to appease Robski. The match would go on at War!Fare, but without the stipulations.
War! was also invited to join an interfed tournament in January. War! has accepted the offer, and under the steam of War!`s Foreign
Policy Minister, is ironing out the details and the two teams of four who will enter.
Wed November 13 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: Wedensday was War!Time, and it packed a punch of excitement. It continued against a background of rumour and counter-rumour that
Robski would sign the loser leaves town match.
War!Time saw the everso violent Brutus Noir return, and with the aid of his docker friends, they beat Jumbo Jim Mongol up really
badly. Mongol needed stitches, but is able to wrestle this week. Noir had just been released from an asylum. He believed it was Butch Williams who had put him there, but it was later admitted by
Blake that it was him. Look out for a major feud between these two former World War! champions.
Chris Deugau made a winning start to his War! career, but it was overshadowed by The Mutilators victory over Sabin Figaro. Now all singles titles lay in the grasp of "heels", with the top two being
held by Armageddon members, Tonnage & Magnum. Yes, Tonnage defeated Thunder in Thunder`s first title defence. But he gets a rematch tonight at War!Fare.
Thursday November 14 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: It`s a day of confirmations. Robski signs the loser-leaves town match, which must now be considered the main event of War!Fare this week. However, Robski would continue to deny that he signed the match. No comment was forthcoming from War! HQ.
WAR! HQ would only speak about the opening of negotiations with a major wrestling star, and ex-IIWF champion, but would say no more.
They confirm the signing of Charles Martel, a highly skilled European Wrestler, who looks likely to continue the influx of technical wrestlers into War!, with people like Chris Deugau entering and Booster Dunne returning. Yes, Booster Dunne, the man who held the International War! title for 5 months, and was considered the best techncial wrestler in War! for a long time, signs a new contract.
Helel, having learnt that Butch Williams had misled him, leaves War! Friday November 15 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Friday sees Acts of War!, but unfortunately, we`re not able to comment on that at this moment. However, a lot of other events did occur on Friday.
War! HQ continued to tease us with reports of signing an ex-IIWF champion, and Robski continued to deny the loser-leaves-town match had been signed. Max Clifford, Robski`s manager, threatens legal action. War! trembles in it`s boots.
Joey The Clown/DeathJester makes enquiries about rejoining War!, but demands too much money. The talks fall apart.
What everyone else was talking about though was Chris Deugau`s intention to extend the infamous Anihilation Corporation into War!
This group has had a lot of success and could seriously challenge Armageddon`s dominance, especially with Tonnage retiring very soon and Bane under new management. Names such as Charles Martel, Jason
MidNight, The Annihilators and Bane have all been thrown around, but for now Deugau is keeping everything close to his chest, denying he
has made approaches or that anyone has made approaches. All I know is what I hear, and I hear that the AC gets what it wants. Anyone who is in it gets a headstart on the road to success, so it will be interesting to see just who Chris Deugau recruits, or even who makes enquiries about joining.
But at the same time, most wrestlers in War! are very defensive of it, treating it almost like home. Will they perceive Deugau`s move as an invasion? Could a major war start between the AC & War! ? Am
I just being too dramatic? Only time will tell
Saturday 16 November ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: With everyone sworn to silence about the results of Acts of War!, Saturday is a deadly quiet day, sparked only by the news in the tag-team ranks that Domination were leaving once all commitments were fulfilled. Signing on the same day was The British Empire, consisting of Lord Wylde & Lord Savage. They come claiming to teach the Americans & Canadians the foolishness of freedom.
Sunday 17 November ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tox: Sunday was a monumental day. WAR! HQ revealed the major acquisition they had been planning all week - DeathBringer! He is certainly one to watch out for.
Also signed was another big man, but a smaller name, John Jennings. Bane reveals his new manager to be "Flea". The Stalker, ex-Liberty champion, takes a break from War! until further notice. And news leaks out of War!`s next planned supercard to follow Nuclear War!Fare - Guerilla War!Fare, planned for January 25th. It is rumoured to be a cross between a Royal Rumble and a
Falls-count-anywhere, street-fight, anything goes kind of match. The proposed title for this January 25th card is Guerilla War!Fare -
"Seek & Destroy". Tox: And that, my friends, is the week in review. Keep your eyes open for news on last weeks Acts of War! and this weeks War!Fare.
There should also be an AoW & a War!Time this weeks as well, seeing the debut of DeathBringer, Jennings and the British Empire. Until then, see ya...
