Performance out of 10) * AMERICAN PATRIOT(8) scored a pin following his "Patriot Missile" Flying forearm at the two minute mark over Jason Jobber. * STEIN "ICE" BERG(8) made a dramatic impact on WAR! in his match against STAR SPANGLED BUTT KICKER(5). Initially the fans backed SSBK, but when they noticed the intensity and cleanliness of Bergs powerhouse wrestling, they were won over. Berg showed off his strength by throatslamming the 345lb SSBK and then bouncing off the ropes with a bigsplash, which was enough to get the pin.
* YOUTH WATERS (7) & KID ENIGMA (10) astounded the fans again with their amazing lightheavyweight match of highimpact moves. As one fan said - "They spent more time on the air than on the ground".
Gravity was defiedin this match, and Kid Enigma also denied Youth Waters a victory, catching the youngster with a flying cross body block after eight and a half minutes.
* SABIN "GANGSTA POWER" FIGARO (7) had revenge on his mind in this match against RUMBLEFISH (5). It was perhaps this that gave him the edge over the 6'6" Rumblefish who really seemed neither to have his heart or mind on this match. Fish tried to brawl, Figaro brawled better, and eventually Figaro did a "Bumrush" wher ehe completely pummeled 'Fish with his fists. With 'Fish out of it, Figaro scooped and slammed the 296lb Rumblefish and got the pin. Not a technically pleasing match, but the fans loved to see Figaro win.
* "THE CARDINAL" ANDREA MANCINI (6) and PAINKILLER (7) were at each others throats for the whole match, and Painkiller even thought he'd won early on when he powerbombed Mancini outside the ring.
Mancini recovered, only to be bashed about even more by the 7`2" painkiller. It was heel vs. heel, the fans didn't care, but all were stunned when Mancini got a second wind, turned the tide and then
DDT Painkiller from the top rope, which was enough to get a victory. * "THE MASTER" AKIRA HASHIMATA (6) and THE MANDARIN (7) displayed what oriental wrestling was all about. With more types of locks on this match than in a maximum security jail, the match was long and slow. Akira was dominating towards the end, but just as he was running off the ropes, Master Po hit him with a cane. Akira turned round to get Po, but the Mandarin grabbed him and rolled him up for a pin.
* (NICE Mr.) NEXUS (6) tried to appeal to his fellow European to go soft because he wasn't feeling well. Stupidly , "EUROPEAN HAVOC" JEAN VAN BASTEN(7) listened, and as a result Nexus got in a few heavy early heavy blows. Van Basten rallied with three atomic drops, which the fans loved and then continued with his assault. Nexus, noticing he was on the rough end of things, tried to use his infamous bras knuckles, but the referre spotted them and DQ'd Nexus.
