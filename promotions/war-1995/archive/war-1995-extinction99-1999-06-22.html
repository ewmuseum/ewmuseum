Well, once again, I bring WAR! to an end, exactly a year from the last time I ended it, and six months since restarting. It wasn`t because I stopped loving being fedhead of WAR! but simply because I had to get on with my life. WAR! was always an escape for me - a world I created, owned and had power over...and also a place where I met and made some great friends. Sad to see it go, but pleased it is over. Similar in style, and bound for greatness, is a fed called ARMAGEDDON. http://members.tripod.com/~RHManiac/armageddon.html Having been in Armageddon now for a month, I must say I`m really glad I made the move from fedhead to player, and also to be in Armageddon, which has the same addictivity that War! sometimes had. There are some really entertaining writers in there, and I really recommend joining up if you can. I know that many of the War! players who have joined are enjoying it. Lots of angles, lots of characters, lots of action, lots of fun. http://members.tripod.com/~RHManiac/armageddon.html If what you want from e-wrestling is to get lots of 'flashes' written by creative, intelligent people who know how to invent and run a good story, you couldn`t do much better than signing up with Armageddon...and this is a guy who really knows what is and what isn`t good in e-wrestling. _______________________________________ [Toxic Shock is standing in the center of the ring as "Stuck" by Limp Bizkit begins to blare over the loud speakers...] Toxic Shock: Ladies and gentlemen...
