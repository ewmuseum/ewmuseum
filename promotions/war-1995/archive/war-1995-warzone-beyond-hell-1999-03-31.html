MC: Kicks out! Anderson on the offensive again...big roundhouse rights....dropkick...Southern Man in the corner...Anderson runs in...shoulder charge...Southern Man winded. Anderson to the top rope again...flying bulldog flattens Southern Man. Southern Man has Anderson in the corner again...undoes the turnbuckle cover and rams his head repeatedly into the turnbuckle. Anderson collapses. Southern Man doesn`t stop and kicks away at Anderson... MC: Bad Seed isn`t releasing the hold...he may end up doing some serious damage to Winchester. He`s been talking about how much he wants the War!Lord title, and now he is presented with a prime chance of getting it. MC: Winchester has just submitted!
