WAR!REPORT 3

------------------------------------------------------------- WAR! FEDERATION PRESENTS WAR!REPORT 3 WITH YOUR HOST Avocado Bob Live from the War!datorium ------------------------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: Welcom one & welcome all for the first card since the last one, which wasn`t that long ago, and boy,it surdoesn`t feel long ago, especially with all that has been happening. We`ve got an interview with Trailboss Mike
Dallas and his reason for challenging XL-9, as well as his change of nature.
. We`ve also got 4 debuting wrestlers, a tag-team bout, Trailboss vs. XL-9 for the tagteam titles, an International championship match and a World championship match. Yes, you heard right - a World championship match. We`ve said sod reason, if everyone else can have a world title, why can`t we? Also, as you may have noticed, Dr.Nathaniel Darkness is not here today, and that is due to commitments with his stable ClanDemon in other federations. So with no one to chit chat to until ToxicShock shows up later, lets get this card underway.
Mountaintop Madman Vs. Deadpunk The debut of the 6`10", near 400lb Mountaintop Madman shows signs of great strength & endurance, with Deadpunk`s blows having very little effect. The man who says he wrestled grizzly bears then easily wrestles the grotesque
Deadpunk to the mat with several power moves, finishing off with his "Madmans
Ire" piledriver variation at 5.44
"The American" Butch Williams Vs. The Question Two wrestlers debut in this match, one intovert, the other extrovert. "The
American" Butch Williams, walks arrogantly down the aisle, microphone in hand, having this to say...
" Yo, all you non-american wrestlers out there in WAR! had better run, `cause when this proud American gets ya, he`s gonna slamdunk ya right into the centre of the ring. Ya`ll got no right being here. WAR! is American territory and I hate invaders. Get out now or ya`ll suffer....ya`ll suffer....hahahahahaa"
The stadium is filled with booes, but The American continues to laugh. The
Question remains silent, but speks confidently with his moves, applying a variety of amateur wrestling moves combined with karate. The American responds with a few slams, but The Questions all round technique is too much, and to the loud cheers of the fans, pins "The American" with his "Doubt this"
German Suplex at 8.55 ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Tag-team title XL-9 vs. Trailboss Mike Dallas Before the match, an interview with "Trailboss" Mike Dallas ToxicShock: So tell me Mike, why have you challenged XL-9? You seemed to have a secure hold on the title.
TMD: That means nothing to me. What matters to me is that I`ve been played around with. What some of you may or may not know is that XL-9 has a manager of sorts - The Prof as he`s called. Now this guy is evil - I tell ya, he`s evil. How do I know? Because before every match, he`d inject me witha drug.
He said it was for energy. Well, by methods I don`t want to go into, I found that this was an experimental drug, dug up from old military archives, to make people more aggressive. That`s why I turned heel on the fans I Iove. I couldn`t understand it . Ever since I`ve been in war, I`ve felt strange, and now I know why. Well let me tell all of you out there - I`m back, and I`m back for the good
His music plays and the fans go wild......... In the match, Trailboss seems to have no trouble finding aggression, rampant with what had happened to him. But still, as others have found, neither power nor skill can seem to damage the seemingly impervious XL-9, and with all his anger and addrenalin almost gone, Trailboss tries one desperate lunge on the man he has beaten on for the last quarter of an hour. XL-9, showing signs of tiredness, fails to move out of the way and both go tumbling to the outside of the ring. A brawl ensues, but it is Trailboss who gets back into the ring first (only just) just before the count-out, leaving Trailboss the victor and holder of the Tagteam title at 17.37, a title promptly made defunct by General
Andropov who approaches ringside and gives Trailboss the new singles title,
The Freedom belt.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Fourseasons" Vs. "Masters of War" Bludgeon & DeathThreat Tagmatch With the Fourseasons considerably weakened with the shock departure of Massiveman & "Scratch it", the planned fourman tag becomes a simple 2 man tag. And a simple two man tag it is as well. Despite seemingly having their mind on Fridays PIT match, the loss of confidence in the FourSeasons is apparent, signified with a simple bodysplash/pin combination by Bludgeon at
14.33. Could this be the end of the FourSeasons. The "Masters of War" seem to think so, announcing to the crowd that the reason why the two left was due to fear of the Masters of War.
----------------------------------------------------------------- The Maltese Falcon Vs. The Fire Revenge is on the mind of the Maltese Falcon. Falcon, wary of what Fire can do, keeps his distance and uses his flying moves to gain an advantage. That advantage is lost when The Fire evades a flying legdrop. The Fire`s experience pays off with a cradlepin on Falcon in a match richly deserved to be won by the Maltese Falcon, but the victory goes to The Fire at 5.59
An attempt by the Masters of War to beat up on the Maltese Falcon is stopped by Seth Blitzspear, Johnny Thunder & Mountaintop Madman even before the
Masters of War can get down the aisle --------------------------------------------------- International Championship Johnny Thunder (champ) Vs. InnerCity Firmer Thunder,in his first title defense, warms up in the ring, unused to the appearance of this large, shaven tatooed brawler. A powerhouse is one way to describe ICF, violent is another. The match gets underway, Thunder recovering from early blows to apply a boston crab and abdominal stratch.
Powering his way out, ICF boots Thunder in the face, nearly breaking his neck, and definitely knocking him out. Sensing victory and a title in his grasp,
ICF climbs to the top rope for the first time to finish Thunder off. But ICF fails tosee Brutus Noir, the World Champ, run down the aisle and into the ring. Noir clotheslines ICF off the top rope and onto the concrete, promptly getting hold of anything hard and metal in sight to beat ICF up with. Dazed , bruised and bleeding, ICF tries to rally, but Noir, sporting a shaven head & a tatoo of Noir having ICF in the Marseille Strangler on his back is too agressive, and eventually some officials intervene, ICF having to be taken into hospital, doubtful as to whether he`ll be wrestling on Friday. Oh, and
Thunder was DQ`d at 10.34 for Noirs intereference, but keeps the
International championship ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ World Championship Match up "The Sidewinder" Brutus Noir (The Champ) Vs. Seth Blitzspear Already warmed up and full of confidence still, Noir shows championship qualities in his first title defense. Blitzspear the challenger, puts up a good performance. Fairly even match until....
Avocado Bob: And that`s Seth with a flying shoulderblock...he`s knocked the french brawler down..he`s picking Noir up to throw him to the ropes..oh my
God, look who`s running down to ringside..it`s the innercity firmer, he`s just smashed Blitzspear over the head with a chair..and he`s rammed the edge of the chair into the running Noir. Oh my God, that could have broken his neck...
Seth`s skull is bleeding, but the London yob is beating up the French yob..oh, that`s the third IronHammer in a row..lets get some medics out there..Noir could be permanently damaged...oh ladies and gentlemen, we`re runnig out of time...ICF`s collapsed - his injuries were too much. We have three wrestlers unconcious in the ring, all bleeding profusly...oh my God, poor Seth...this is a war. Well, the match, but not the title, goes to Seth for interference by
ICF, but what is the cost...what is the cost....?
