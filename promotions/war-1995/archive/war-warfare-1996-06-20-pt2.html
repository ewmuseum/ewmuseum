Non-Title Match The Lost Boys w/Wendy vs Jammer and Arron Iron w/Eve [highlights from match] DS: Jammer flips LB1 over...Iron tags in...He attempts a piledriver...Yes, he got it....pin...no...Iron with a DDT - that could do it...the cover ...1....2..LB2 breaks it up. The ref is stunned. Iron tags in Jammer...Jammer with a cradle suplex on LB1. The ref is not able to make the count. 5, 6, 7...come on, ref. LB2 saves his partner. All four are in the ring now. The referee is only now coming to his senses...Wendy's on the ring apron! She hits

Winners: Lost Boys by pinfall Rating: *** Liberty Title Match OG Gary Trent vs Johnny "the Sidewinder" Missile Trent and Missile wrestled a clean, scientific match. Trent focussed his attack on Missile's leg to take away his aerial attack. Missile countered with several pin attempts from small cradles, sunset flips, and schoolboy rollups to try to get a quick win. Trent kept going back after his opponent's legs anyway. Trent went to the top rope for a splash, but Missile rolled out of the way. Missile then went on the offensive, slamming the champion into the corner and working him over there with kicks and punches. Missile set him up for a sleeper hold, but Trent got to the ropes before much damage was done. Missile went to the top turnbuckle rather slowly on his worn-down legs.
Trent recovered and nailed Missile in the midsection before he had a chance to do anything. Missile crotched himself on the top turnbuckle, giving
Trent
the chance to get him in position for the "Drive-By" top rope brainbuster.
From there it was simply a matter of the ref counting to three as the champion successfully defended his title.
Winner: OG Gary Trent Rating: *** 1/2 DS: An impressive win by OG Gary Trent! I'm being told Tony J is back in the WAR! office with Vice President Helmroy....wonder what that's about?
--------- Tony J: I'm here with WAR! Vice President Helmroy, who has an announcement to make. Take it away, Mr.V.P., sir.
Helmroy: Thank you, Tony. This news has leaked out already, but I will now make it official. WAR! has decided to sanction "Dirty" Doug
Simpkins' WXW title. From now on, it will be defended right here in WAR! like all the other belts. Well, not quite. This title will change hands on any loss by the champion - whether by pinfall, submission, countout, or disqualification. That ought to make things interesting, don't you think?
TJ: I had heard the WAR! brass, including yourself, were against recognizing the WXW title.
Helmroy: Well, we sort of were, but when Randy Blake lobbied on behalf of sanctioning the title, we caved in. A special thanks to Mr.Blake for that generous contribution to the WAR! officials' pension fund...
er, not that *that* had anything to do with our completely impartial decision. Two separate, independent events there, I guarantee.
TJ: Well, you heard it hear first folks. Okay, you probably already heard it, but you heard it here *officially*! That's got to count for something. Back to John and Dave with their little wrestling show...
Helmroy: Ahhhh.....glorious, sweet retirement in Barbados.... ---------- DS: Strange that announcement was made after "Dirty" Doug Simpkins' match against Midnight. JC: Those are the breaks. International Title Match Lord Byron vs ??? Announcer: This match is for the WAR! International Championship! Introducing first, the International champion... LORD BYRON!!! (Crowd boos) DS: This is going to be tough on Lord Byron. He hasn't been able to prepare for this challenger. He was ready for Booster Dunne, but Byron won't get the chance to prove himself against the former International titleholder tonight. The challenger is someone in the top ten... You know who it is, don't you?
JC: I know but I'm not saying. Lord Byron is in for a big surprise, but if anyone can handle that kind of pressure, it's a man as regal, as well-bred, as... well, as just plain superior as Lord Byron!
DS: I've seen Jean de Mort sitting in the audience - could it be him? But he's not in the top ten now! A: And his opponent... Being led to the ring by King Benny II, here is the BIG DUKE!!! (Crowd is shocked) DS: Oh my goodness! It's Big Duke, Byron's former stablemate. King Benny managed them both. Byron looks shocked. Remember, the special stipulations Byron asked for in his match with Dunne *will* apply in this match. If Byron loses by any means - including a countout or
disqualification - he will lose the title! Byron and Big Duke locked up in the center of the ring. Duke seemed hesitant to wrestle his former partner. He offered his hand to Byron, and he shook it.
Then Byron poked Duke in the eye and followed with a series of knife-edge chops and a knee lift. Byron got the larger Duke off his feet and tried to soften up his leg with several wear-down holds. Byron went for the
Aristoclutch a few times, but was unable to cinch it in. Byron bounced off the ropes but was caught by Big Duke in a spinebuster. That immediately stopped the International champion's momentum. Big Duke threw Byron around the ring like a ragdoll. Byron appeared to have no counter to Duke's size and strength. Big Duke powerslammed the 265-pounder with ease.
DS: Duke drops a big elbow...1...2...Byron's shoulder is up! Duke whips him into the ropes and just about takes his head off with a clothesline! Duke slingshots the champion into the corner...Byron's face bounces off the top turnbuckle...Duke goes for the win, hooks a leg..1...2...
No! Big Duke drags Byron to his feet, whips him into the corner... Byron runs out and tries a crucifix, but no...Duke catches him and delivers a renegade drop! That could do it! 1...2...and Byron kicks out again! Big Duke is so close!
JC: Byron has incredible stamina to go with his technical ability. Big Duke better put him away now while he has the chance or else it's not going to happen tonight.

I don't think he can make it back in! JC: Big Duke will take the title if Byron doesn't make it back into the ring by the ten-count. ---------- JC: He certainly made his point. I think Lord Byron is great and all, but Jean De Mort could be the man to take that International belt from around his waist. Then again, maybe not.
DS: I'm glad to see you're so decided on the issue. Let's get to our final (rushed) match of the evening. Time is running out. The satellite people are getting nervous.
JC: Not as nervous as Blitzspear must be knowing he has to face a member of Armageddon! World WAR! Title Match Seth Blitzspear vs "Magnum" Biff McGriff Blitzspear was in a hurry for another appointment, so he set out to make short work of McGriff's challenge. McGriff tried to slow down the champion, but
Seth was full of energy and seemingly impervious to pain. A low blow by
McGriff finally slowed Seth down to a human level. Biff whipped Seth into the corner, but Seth caught him with a boot to the face as he ran in after him.
Seth followed with three clotheslines to finally get McGriff off his feet. Blitzspear hit him with a splash and got a two count. McGriff once again rallied against the champion. A thumb to the eye was followed by a powerbomb, but Blitzspear kicked out of a pin attempt easily. Blitzspear fought back with a well-placed uppercut. A Flying Time backdrop by the champion sent
Biff to the outside and he was unable to return in time.
Winner: Seth Blitzspear by countout Rating: *** DS: The World WAR! Champion holds onto his title! JC: At least for today. He got past one member of Armageddon, but there are plenty more challenges where that came from. I'd like to see what he can do against Reverend Death or maybe a rematch against
Tonnage. There won't be any Flying Time in those matches. DS: Seth has shown he has what it takes to beat anyone in WAR! It'll be interesting to see him against some of the newer WAR! talent as well. That's all for WAR!Fare this week. See you next time!
