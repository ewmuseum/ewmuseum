MEGAWAR 2 — Championship Tuesday

_____________________________ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presents From the New Orleans WAR!DOME on January 28th 1997 with your Spice-Girls loving fans/hosts “Avocado” Bob Whitely & “Terrible” Terry James ___________________________ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all to the most exhilarating line up WAR! has ever had to put on offer. Ladies and gentleman, this is MEGAWAR TWO - CHAMPIONSHIP TUESDAY, and you will be witness to SEVEN title matches and TWO battle royales. That`s a helluva lot of action. With me this evening is “Terrible” Terry James. Terry, how much are you looking forward to tonights action. TTJ: More than you could imagine Bob, because this truly promises to be an exciting evening of entertainment.
Bob: Terry, could you spare a few words about the leaking of “Bullfrog”
Eric Helmroy`s predictions for tonights winners?
TTJ: Well, his opinions are his opinions, and there is no way opinions can influence the outcome of a match. Only a wrestlers ability, grit and determination is going to give him a win tonight. And a bit of tactical ingenuity.
Bob: Well, I`m sure we`ll see plenty of tactical ingenuity in our main event, which will be our first match - Chris Deugau Vs. Lord Byron. Byron of course leapfrogged Decan for this match because he earned it a long time ago at Nuclear War!Fare. A long history has developed between these two in a very short space of time. On their first encounter, Byron beat Deugau.
Deugau had just entered WAR! and appeared to many to simply be a hot-headed youngster. Few knew that he would possess the ability and brains to shoot to the top of the rankings and get what would have been a title shot against
MidNight so quickly. MidNight left WAR! and an emergency decision by
“Bullfrog” Eric Helmroy to replace MidNight with Byron altered the whole line-up of tonight’s matches. But only a week ago today, at the New Orleans
War!Dome, Byron beat Deugau in a thrilling encounter that also included
Booster Dunne, who suffered a career threatening injury in that match.
Now, in their third and most important encounter, the question must be asked - will it be third time lucky for Deugau, or will Lord Byron prove that everything happens in threes? We`ll find out soon enough, but let’s have a quick look at the other 8 matches at tonight’s mega-card. Terry, 008 JIM BONDO gets to fight the big bad BANE for the International War! Title. Do you think he could pull it off?
TTJ: No way. He may have skill, but that is nothing when Bane is powering towards you like a locomotive on rocket fuel. I can`t see that match lasting more than a couple of minutes. Bob: TYLER STEVENS, the reformed wrestler, gets to fight “DIRTY” DOUG
SIMPKINS for the Liberty Title, a belt Stevens has worn twice in the past and has dreamt of wearing again.
TTJ: Well, he can carry on dreaming. That title is going nowhere. Simpkins is the best their is when it comes to winning and holding championships.
He`s held this Liberty title for a long time and I don`t expect to see that stopping tonight. Heck, he`ll never lose it.
Bob: What about the battle for the WAR!LORD title - AMERICAN PATRIOT defends against French aristocrat CHARLES MARTEL.
TTJ: Charles Martel is almost mirroring Chris Deugau in his rise to the top. Now, it`s probably only because he hasn`t got the hype of the AC surrounding him that meant that he didn`t get the world shot instead of
Martel, but I can see him comfortably disposing of American Patriot tonight.
Bob: And out final singles match, for the Unified title, SETH BLITZSPEAR, having won the title from RAVEN in decidedly controversial circumstances following the interference of “Heavy Weapons” Max Doom, faces T.J “THUNDER”
DECAN. TTJ: Close to call. They`ve fought quite a few times and I think Decan has come out on top the most. As long as the fans are behind Seth and Seth alone, he should pull it off, but if a large section of fans are for Decan,
I can’t see Seth being motivated enough to beat Decan. But to be honest, I don`t care, because they both bore me stupid.
Bob: Terry, I hope you can see me gritting my teeth here. I`ve found it very hard to agree with anything you have said tonight. So tell me, for the
World War! Tag - Team titles, between THE ANNIHILATORS and THE LOST BOYS, who will win?
TTJ: Annihilators, by a long way. All they need to do is head-butt Frank
Addams and they’ve won.
Bob: International War! Tag Title match? KALIFORNIA HEAT against PHANTASMAGORIA? TTJ: Heat are good, but their time has come. Phantasmagoria, unbeaten and unstoppable, will do a Bane and steam-roll over the Heat.
Bob: And finally, before we get to the first and most important match, who do you see winning the Battle Royales?
TTJ: In the singles, Max Doom, as one of the heaviest, and most powerful, must stand a chance, but “Magnum” Biff McGriff is back, and personally, I say he`s the man who could do it tonight. DeathJester is also back and is an unknown quantity in this match. He’d be my choice for darkhorse. As for the tag-team, only one team will be left standing at the end of the night, and that team will be the Chaos Knights.
Bob: Well, we`ll find out later on tonight whether your predictions are correct, but first of all, we`ve got the big, big match. It`s CHRIS DEUGAU battling LORD BYRON for the World War! Title made vacant by Jason MidNights departure. Now, there is a cage erected around the ring area, and security
has been tripled to make sure there is no interference. There can still be countouts, there can still be DQ`s, but should there be such a result, and neither of the wrestlers know this, there will be a championship tournament on St.Valentines Day, that is February 14, involving 4 wrestlers in a round-robin event. No doubt two of those would be Chris Deugau and Lord
Byron.
TTJ: That would be a great event should it happen, but I`m quite happy to see a great event here tonight between two great competitors. And even though the fans don`t like either of these outstanding individuals, I`m pretty sure they recognise them for their ability. Chris Deugau has been voted, in wrestling as a whole, the best technical wrestlers. Lord Byron showed that he was the best technical wrestler in WAR! by beating Deugau last week. This really is a top level match between top level competitors.
Both are so similar in that they have technical ability and endurance that is a class above the rest. These two really are in a class of their own.
Bob: Let`s go to the E-Z one. Ring Announcer, Ezekiel Zebeddiah. E-Z: This match is scheduled for ONE FALL, with a sixty minute time limit. Order will be kept by referee, ALAN KIRBY. On his way to the ring, accompanied by Sunny B. , from Nanaimo, Canada, at 6`2, 223lbs, voted Best
