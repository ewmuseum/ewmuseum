SUBJECT: [WAR!] WarZone 2 DATE: Fri, 13 Oct 95 11:45:54 GMT ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR WRESTLING FEDERATION Presents the FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHT ON WARZONE 2 - Friday the 13th Live from the Warena, Paris, France. Avec your ever present hosts "Avocado" Bob Whitely & "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Voice over microphone.... "It's Friday night, it's Paris....it's Waaaaaaarrrrrrr Zoooooonnnnneee twooooooooooo" Bfrog: Well, that'll be completely lost on the French speaking audience. Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies and gentlemen to the first leg of our World Tour. Our first stop is Paris, then we move on to London, then
Berlin, Barcelona, Milan, Brussels and Amsterdam. It's Friday the 13th, so that could mean badluck for some of our wrestlers tonight.
Bfrog: If you ask me,the bad luck has already started because Bombsquad is fine and well after the damage suffered at the hands of the Spirit of 76 at
Masters of War 1.
Bob: I think that's great news, and I'm sure he'll be using his match tonight to get some revenge. But revenge is what this game is all about.
And revenge is on the mind of Seth Blitzspear, out to vindicate his very controversial title loss to Randy Blake.
Bfrog: If I can just interrupt you there,I have some news. While preparing to interview Rumblefish, I managed to catch Seth Blitzspear talking to him.
Seth didn't see me, so I managed to catch all his comments on tape. [Crackly tape, Seths voice] "..what am I doing here Rumblefish, with all these losers...the fans are worthless crumbs. I really despise their whinging. But do you know what I despise even more? Its some of those other wrestlers around here...you know, people like OG Gary Trent. He'll never be anything more than a mid-card wrestler yet he continues tothink he's hot stuff. And then there is that
Jerry "Goon" Gordan. He won't even be a mid-card wrestler. What a complete
loser. I don't think I can hang around here much longer...." Bfrog: Well, whadya think to that Bob? The peoples champion is a two-faced rat. Bob: I can understand him being frustrated about losing the belt, but I don't think he'd turn against his friends, especially "Goon". Are you sure that is Seth?
Bfrog: Yep. Bob: If you're speaking the truth, this is truly tragic news. Oh well, let's get to the action. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ OG GARY TRENT (51) Vs "THE SIDEWINDER" BRUTUS NOIR (49) Trent plans well and for most of the match prevents getting into a slugfest with the french brawler who is enjoying plenty of support here. However, by the tenth minute, the match does turn into a brawl which Noir obviously dominates. However, Trent keeps his cool long enough to turn the match back into technical wrestling and Noir loses his grip on the match. Noir is worn down with a sleeper hold,and then Trent manages to execute his "Driveby" top rope brainbuster to get the pin at 13.23 (which I think is Trents first pinfall victory in WAR).
Match rating: 6/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ HOT STUFF (62) Vs. HIGH VOLTAGE (38) High voltage outsize Hot Stuff in height, but in the ring it is Hot Stuff, the red eyed wrestlers, who show the size of their abilities. Cinder manages to execute his "Heatsink" flying headbutt early on but fails to get the pin.
"Electric" Eric Mitchell gets a good run going and executes a series of powermoves on Inferno, but Inferno manages to tag Cinder who again goes for the "Heatsink" after knocking Mitchell down. Mitchell moves and tags his brother Steve who brawls with Cinder and fares well against the high flyer, but is caught by a shocking Frankensteiner which gives Hot Stuff the victory at 9.36
Match rating: 7/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ PAINKILLER ((42) VS. RUMBLEFISH (58) People go for their hotdogs as these two big brawlers clash for the first time. Both have the fans hatred, but cheer more for Painkiller because of
Rumblefish's attack on Trent last week. The match is not an enjoyable, mostly consisting of a slow slugfest, and inevitably, both are disqualified for use of illegal objects at 4.55
Match rating: 3/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ AMERICUS (26) Vs. BLADERUNNER (74) Bombsquad had to pull out of the tag match under doctors orders at the last minutes and it was thus reduced to a singles match. Bladerunner shows his determination to win for Bombsquad by dominating against Ameriucs who only had the opening minute to show any form of offensive. Three minutes later and it is over. Bladrunner with a piledriver. Matchend: 4.04
Match rating: 5/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BOOSTER DUNNE (47) VS. JOEY THE CLOWN (53) The winner of this match will get a title shot against Randy Blake if Seth
Blitzspear doesn't win tonight. Dunne, with Ms.Paula at ringside for this match, seems content with defending against Clown, keeping the match as a bit of a stalemate. Seven minutes in and Joey heats up the action with a perfectly timed dropkick, knocking Dunne out of the ring and onto the floor.
Joey ascends to the toprope and smashes onto Dunne outside the ring with his
"Bounce". Dunne is flattened, but Ms.Paula goes toknock Joey from behind with a chair. Ringmaster, Joeys manager, manages to stop her in time, allowing Joey to role Dunne into the ring and get the pin at 8.15
Match rating 6/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Liberty Title Championship Match THE MANDARIN Vs. JERRY "GOON" GORDON Summary of match so far: The two wrestlers have been exchanging mat moves for the last ten minutes in a superb exciting display of talent. The Mandarin seems to be getting the worst of it until, when the referee wasn't looking,
Master Po knocks "Goon" on the head with his cane. This is where we join the action...
Bob: What a shame for a superb match like this to be ruined by an act of villainy like that. Goon was well on his way to winning. He is still standing but is holding his head..and here comes The Mandarin with the Tai-Fu combo...the frankensteiner...Jerry must be out of it...1......2....kicks out....Mandarin with a figure four...but Jerry catches him mid-move...rolls him up...1....2....Mandarin just kicks out....Goon on a roll now. He has a second wind...backbreaker...slam...suplex..kneebreaker...neckbreaker...he's using his whole arsenal on The Mandarin..and there is his
DDT...1......2......Master Po comes into the ring to interfere..but he's too slow...Jerry "Goon" Gordan gets the three count. We have a new lightweight champion. Congratulations to Jerry Gordan. Some may call him a Goon, and perhaps last season, he was. But if you ask me, right now, he's an American
Hero. Match end 14.33 Match Rating: 8/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War TagTeam Championship Finals GHETTO BOYZ Vs. CANADIAN CARNAGE The match so far: It's been Canadian Carnage in the driving seat, keeping Folk Curtis Fredrick tied up in there corner. But a lapse in concentration on their behalf allowed FCF to tag in BigDaddy Vicelord. BDV cleaned house of Canuck, Mountie 2 and even "Dr.Death" Ben Bach. Canuck re-entered and
Mountie 2 tried to blindside him. FCF saw the scene and blocked Mountie 2 while BDV scooped Canuck up for a powerslam..... Bob: That's 1...that's 2....that's 3...no..no it isn't..I'm mistaken...Canuck got his foot on the rope. The Seven foot, 325lb Big Daddy
Vicelord scoops him up again....Mountie and FCF fighting outside the ring...backbreaker..shoulderbreaker...guerilla press...bigsplash..it must be over...1.....2...and Mountie Two pulls the big guy off somehow...Canuck recovers as BDV and Mountie 2 brawl...FCF still knocked out...Canuck, top rope...BDV doesn't see it...where is the referee...flying body press...but
BDV turns round in time to catch Canuck...and kicks Mountie Two down while holding Canuck...and powerslams Canuck onto Mountie 2...and that's the three count on both of them...the winners of this match, and new WorldWar tag team champions...Big Daddy Vicelord and Folk Curtis Fredrick....Ghetto
Booooyyyyzzzz. Match end 13.59 Match rating 7/10 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War Championship Match "The Economist" Randy Blake Vs. Seth Blitzspear Bob: I don't know whether Seth has heard your "report" yet, but the fans sure have and they are booing like crazy. To be fair, they're not exactly cheering Blake either. The fact is, it seems nobody can stand a turncoat.
Bfrog: Well, at least he isturning the rightway. Bob: I hust don't understand it though. Seth would never say anything like that about his fans.
Bfrog: Listen, I heard it myself, and if you can't trust me, who can you trust? Bob: Well, I could name about five billion other people..but right now I'm going to take you to ringside for this championship match.
Ring Announcer: In one corner, we have, from Ontario,Canada, weighing in at 253 lbs, "The
Economist" Randy Blaaaaaaaaakke.
Bob: Blake seems to have put on weight for this match. Bfrog: Muscle, not weight. Bob: Are you really stupid or do you just look it? Ring Announcer: And in the other corner, from Parts Unknown, weighing in at
306 lbs, Setttthhhhhhhh Bllliiitttttzzzzzssssppppeeeaaarrrrrrrrr (Ring
Announcer collapses from Oxygen exhaustion). The crowd boo. 'Ding, ding' Match Summary: Both are offensive wrestlers, and both make this an exciting high impact match. Blake actually appears to be willing to fight and keeps
Seth down for a while with his technical ability. Seth responds with some power moves, but Blake gets Seth down on the mat again. Blake begins to dominate the match, and where Seth would usually get a second wind, he doesn't. Blake keeps on piling on the pressure, and when Seth makes a mistake with a body splash, Blake sets him up for and executes a "Recession"
and gets the pin at 8.47 Match rating 7/10 Bob: I just don't know what is happening to Seth. That is the fourth match in a row he has lost. What has happened to this once great man. Could it be ring rust, or could it be a change in attitude.
Bfrog: Well, I'm glad to say the tape made a difference. Without the fans support, he just couldn't beat Blakes experience and skill. Seth made mistakes, couldn't recover and was beaten by the better wrestler. Until Seth does get more experience, he will have to rely on the fans to help him win, and if he can't rely on the fans, he's gonna sink like a lead fish.
Bob: Well, thanx for your analysis Bullfrog. We'll just have to wait and see how Seth fares over time. Until then, it's congratulations to Jerry
Gordan and Ghetto Boyz for their title wins and the best to you all until
Masters of War next tuesday. See you then.

Blitzspear lost to Randy Blake...Ghetto Boyz won the WorldWar Tag titles by beating Canadian Carnage.
