WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

Acts of War — Mexico City

23 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presnts ACTS OF WAR! from From Mexico City, Mexico November 23, 1996 with your motormad hosts, "Avocado" Bob Whitlely & "Rude" Ross Royale ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all. You join us half way into this amazing arena card. Those that have been sitting here in the arena have been witness to some amazing matches. With me tonight is "Rude"
Ross Royale, who is Bullfrog`s distant cousin, and to be honest, that is the only way he got this job.
Ross: Hey Bob you fat, over-cholestoraled numb-weed, don`t give me shit or I`ll have you fired. But down to business, yeah, I`ve got to agree with you, this card has been good so far.
Bob: For the rest of the hour however, we`ve got some amazing encounters. Seth Blitzspear tries once again to win the
International War! title while Mutilator defends against Sabin Figaro. Ross: Again! Figaro is a no-hoper, a total actionless hero. Mutilator may be a butt-wipe, but he`s champ and he`s going to crush Figaro. Bob: I`m just going to have to tolerate you, aren`t I? Ross: Yup! Bob: Big controversy tonight, however, surrounds The War!Lord Title match. American Patriot was to be getting a title shot, but since
Robski lost by DQ to Big Duke in the loser leaves town match, we have no War!Lord champion at this time. It will be decided at Nuclear
War!Fare and could be a rematch of tonights encounter between American Patrior & Big Duke. That match has already happened, and we`ll hear a report on that soon. We`ve also got Kalifornia Heat defending against the
All-Americans. And also 008 Jim Bondo is in action against Booster Dunne. But first, while they`re preparing the ring, lets get a recap on some of tonights matches with Toxic Shock and THE
SHOCK REPORT Tox: The evening began with the debut of REIGN OF TERROR accompanied by Chris Deugau. They faced Vinny & Vince, The MidNight
Mafia. Rob S.Pierre & Guy Lotine humiliated their opponents, and needlessly cheap-shotted them repeatedly with chains they had hidden.
It was an easy victory for Reign of Terror, and they immediately made themselves hated to the fans.
Next up on the card was SUNA GIB, battling ArchitisMan. CHRIS DEUGAU hung around and watched Gib fondle ArchitisMan relentlessly.
Eventually, Architisman could put up with it no more, claiming it infringed upon his artistic integrity as a wrestler, and walked off.
Gib posed to Deugau after his countout victory. THE BRITISH EMPIRE were up next, facing The Stallion Brothers - Ricki & Antonio. Lord Savage & Lord Wylde began singing "God save the queen" but were attacked before they could finish. They got angry and spent half an hour dishing out painful holds, before finishing it off with "The Resistence Breaker"
THE WESTERN RIDERS made short work of The Presidents of Wrestling, beating them by pinfall after two minutes.
THE BOUNTY HUNTER defeated "Wee" Willy Winkle with a gut-wrench superplex. Experienced wrestler "SHOWBOAT" EDDIE CANNON made his debut, defeating "Hard" Harry Who? with the "Wabash Cannonball" fallaway slam.
JERRY "GOON" GORDON (7) battled NOMAD (7) in the nights first big encounter. It started slow, but Nomad picked up the pace to make the
fight actually quite exciting. Gordon won the match when he scooped Nomad from the top turnbuckle and slammed him onto the mat, following with a big legdrop and a hook of the leg.
THE ANNIHILATORS (8) battled THE LOST BOYS (7) in a violent encounter. Annihilation had the better of the match, but it inevitably spilled to the outside and resulted in a double countout. If it hadn`t ended in a double count out, it would have been a double DQ.
JOHN JENNINGS w/Dangerous Shawn Roy (7) battled a man his own size in "JACKHAMMER" J.P.STEELE (7) w/Annie Mack. However, before this encounter, Robski, who lost the loser leaves town matcht to the Big Duke, tried to attck
Jennings. Security stopped him, but Jennings managed to lay in a cheeky counter-blow as Robski was led away. In the match though, both of the competitors stood around or near the seven foot mark. Steele`s physique was impressive to the crowd, but it didn't impress Jennings who jumped Steele before the bell had begun as Steele greeted the fans outside the ring. Jennings gave
Steele a real beating, and then brought himback into the ring where he dished out more hard hitting punishment. Steele rallied and started to get things his own way. Steele almost had the match one, bouncing off the ropes to clothesline Jennings, but Roy trips Steele, and Jennings turned Steele over and pinned him while Roy held the leg. Steele complained to the ref, but the ref wouldn`t listen. The fans really got behind Steele, but the ref wouldn`t listen still.
Then Nemesis came running to ringside and said something to the ref which made the ref change his mind and called for the match to continue. Jennings was outraged and tried to end the match quickly,
booting Steele in the gut. Steele, however, took the blow and steamrolled through Jennings with a clothesline. The clothesline took them both to the outside where an all-out brawl ensued, resulting in a double count-out.
Next up, CHRIS DEUGAU was supposed to be facing "THE SIDEWINDER" BRUTUS NOIR, but Noir did not show. Reports quickly filtered through to ringside, and eventually the facts were revealed. Just before the bout, Raven had jumped Noir from behind in the locker room and attacked him savagely with his rapier. An onlooker, who didn`t wish to be named, said it was ironic that Noir was attacked
with a ferocity that only Noir could produce. Deugau won the match by default. CANADIAN CARNAGE (6) battled PHANTASMAGORIA (8) next, and Carnage were really confident after their victory against the All-Americans.
It could be said that they were too confident, because after a successful start to the match that saw Darkness & Terror on the retreat, they got sloppy and made mistakes, missing vital elbowdrops, failing to execute dropkicks that had to be done. The bigmen,
Darkness & Terror, took hold of the situation and caused Carnage some heavy pain before Terror ended it with "The Free Fall" - a powerbomb off the top rope on Johnny Canuck.
DEATHBRINGER (9) made a terrifying debut against "THE JAMAICAN SUPERSTAR" ARRON IRON (3), coldly and methodically wiping the mat with him, destroying him like some dark, overpowering force. The match was over after 8 minutes when he finished Iron off with "The
Burial" - a piledriver off the top-rope. It could have been over a hell of a lot quicker.
The penultimate match that I have to report on is CHARLES MARTEL (8) battling LITTLE CHILL (7). Martel & Chill, both relatively new comers to the rings of WAR! were booed by most of the fans, but provided a highly entertaining, offensive-minded , mostly technical
match-up. Martel was showing the greater skill, but Chill could always find a way to knock the wind out of Martels sails before he could finish Chill off. Ten minutes into the match, Tyler Stevens appeared at ringside. It wasn`t clear who he was going to aid until
Martel executed a reverse neckbreaker on Chill. Stevens quickly ran to the ring as Martel assailed the ropes, preparing for his Suicidal
Senton. It would never be executed, because Tyler Stevens grabbed a chair, on to the apron, and whacked Martel off the top rope from behind. Martel crashed cold to the mat from the top turnbuckle. The referee, who had been distracted by Shaz, Little Chills manager, saw none of this and counted the three as soon as Chill covered the k.o`d
Martel. In the final untelevised match, BIG DUKE (8) battled THE AMERICAN PATRIOT (8). Big Duke entered to big support from the fans, and American Patriot was pleased with Big Duke for getting rid of Robski, but at the same time wanted to be facing Robski now for the War!Lord title. Patriots time would come to get the gold, but it wouldn`t be tonight. Both put on a performance worthy of potential War!Lord champions, but Big Duke, having been on the receiving end of some extensive battering by American Patriot, pulled back into the match, and at around the twenty minute mark, pinned Patriot following a bulldog.
Tox: And that`s the story so far my friends. Back to you Bob. Bob: Thanx Tox. Some great match-ups there. You should have seen them. The marketing people just can`t wait until DeathBringer makes his televised debut. But now, we`ve got some title matches ahead, following the next match involving BOOSTER DUNNE & 008 JIM BONDO.
************************ BOOSTER DUNNE w/Ms.Paula(7) Vs. 008 JIM BONDO (8) (Dunne gets to the ring first and gets a small fan pop. He`s been away for a while, and a lot of the fans can`t remember his great skills. Bondo appears and the whole stadium explodes into support for the secret agent/wrestler. The two men square up before the bell
rings. Dunne looks composed, but then Bondo squirts Grey Poupon in Dunne`s face in an attempt to taunt him. Dunne reels out of shock and Bondo dropkicks him out of the ring.
The match begins. Bondo runs and leaps over the top rope, crushing Dunne with a flying body press.
They go clattering into the railings. They brawl outside for a bit, Dunne gets dirty and rams Bondo into the ring apron, then pulls him into the ring. Dunne clotheslines Bondo a few times and then slams him. He gets Bondo down and tries to get an armbar on, but Bondo just pulls Dunne over and flips him. Bondo springs up and gets to work on Dunne. Bondo fights with a vengeance and Dunne looks suprised by Bondo`s ferocity. Dunne gets back into the match for about two minutes before Bondo counters a bulldog by Dunne, and shoves him into the turnbuckle.)
Bob: And Dunne is winded. Here comes Bondo....back suplex...arches into a pin... 1 2 Bob: Kick out! Ross: It`s not suprising because Bondo has no idea how to pin people. Bob: That isn`t true, and you know it. Bondo whips Dunne to the ropes...headscissors...no...Dunne staggers back intentionally and shakes Bondo off his shoulders and to the outside. Bondo has fallen hard! The fans don`t like that, but Bondo does!
Ross: It was a great move by Dunne. Superb ring awarness. Bob: But Bondo is getting up. I don`t think he fell that badly. Dunne is waiting for him though. Dunne grabs Bondo on the apron and picks him up to suplex him in...hoists him up...but Bondo shifts his weight...Dunne collapses and Bondo is on top of him...
1 2 Bob: 3! Bondo does it! An amazing victory for the secret agent/wrestler. Ross: Dunne is rusty like an old cargo liner Bob. This was no real victory for Bondo. Bob: I don`t know about that Ross. Bondo has been looking very sharp recently. I think he took Dunne by suprise with his energy, but apart from that, there is no excuse for Dunne. Bondo was superb.
Ross: Now what is the monkey man doing? Bob: Monkey man? Ross: It`s too high-brow a joke for you Bob. Bob: Bondo has the mic. He`s calling for Tonnage to get his big fat bum down to ringside now. He really wants Tonnage. Ross: Then he is doubly stupid. First of all, Tonnage ain`t even in the building, and secondly, if he were, Bondo would be squashed into nothingness before he could blink.
Bob: Well, there is no response from Tonnage, and Bondo is now bad-mouthing Tyler Stevens and Venom. The fans are cheering Bondo`s derogatory comments about Stevens & Venom`s relationship.
Ross: Yeah, I can understand that. Why would a guy like Stevens drop a babe like Peppy for a big macho bloke like Venom.
Bob: It makes the mind wonder. Especially with those photographs of Gib& Stevens. Ross: What photographs? Bob: I`ll show you later. They haven`t been released yet. (Crowd go wild as Venom appears and a big shouting match occurs between Bondo & Venom. Security gets in the way of a fight breaking out, but there is definitely bad blood there)
Bob: Well, we almost had a major incident there Ross. Ross: Bondo is stupid, stupid, stupid. Does he think he is 8 feet tall or something. If it ain`t Tonnage, then it is Tyler Steven`s bodyguard Venom.
Bob: Actually, I think he wanted Stevens, and Stevens sent Venom out to do his dirty work. ~~~~~~~~~ Winner: Bondo by Pinfall MR: 8 ************************* UNIFIED TITLE MATCH THE MUTILATOR (8) Vs. SABIN FIGARO (6) ( The match starts as the vastly outsized Figaro takes the fight to Mutilator, but immediately has the fight returned with double servings of pain. Mutilator whips Sabin around the ring and basically treats Sabin like a rag doll. Sabin tries to retaliate, and briefly does, but can get no significant move on Mutilator.)
Bob: And again Mutilator blocks a suplex attempt! Ross: The guy is over 400lbs! It ain`t very suprising is it. Bob: A merciless double ax-handle there on Figaro`s back. It knocks him totally down. More blows by Mutilator. Figaro has been flattened into the mat. Mutilator covers... 1 2 Bob: Kickout by Figaro, but only just. Mutilator picks Figaro up with ease...and slams him down hard into the mat. He follows with a leg drop...and now the Mutilator bomb...that is it...booom!
1 2 Bob: 3! Mutilator retains the title, and I must say, it was a relatively easy victory for him. Sabin looked tired out there tonight. He`s obviously a very busy man.
Ross: No. Mutilator is obviously the better man. That power, that move! I think he could stand a chance against anyone in War!
Bob: Even you? Ross: No. Not me. He`s a stick and I`m a tree, if you see what I`m saying. Bob: I don`t, and nor do I care. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Result: Mutilator by pinfall. MR: 7 ****************************************** INTERNATIONAL WAR! TAG TITLE MATCH KALIFORNIA HEAT (6) Vs. ALL-AMERICANS (8) ****************************************** (Brunswick & Borbone jumped Delane & Powers before the bell had rung. The four men brawled outside the ring, with Powers getting open when his head was rammed into the steel steps. The match got back into the ring and Powers and Borbone squared up, but then...)
(crowd booing wildly as "Land of Hope and Glory" blares out over the PA) Bob: What`s this? Ross: It looks to me like it is The British Empire, Lord Savage and Lord Wylde, with Lady Austere, their manager. Bob: What are they up to? (Within minutes it was clear. They spent the entire match distracting both teams, which was unfortunate since the match was highly thrilling and The All-Americans were actually getting the better of Kalifornia Heat. In the fifteenth minute, Borbone executed a piledriver on Delane and covered....)
Bob: He`s out of it! Delane is out cold and Borbone knows it. Ross: Cover him! Cover him! Bob: Powers is beside himeself outside the ring. Borbone covers... 1 2 Bob: Borbone has been pulled off by Lord Savage. The referee sees it! He calls for the bell. A shouting match has broken out between the British Empire & All-Americans...and now the British Empire whack
The All-Americans with their canes... Ross: That hurts! Bob: Kalifornia Heat are keeping out of it as All-Americans and British Empire fight inside the ring...and it is an all-out fight. No queensbury rules here... Ross: The ref has lost control. Bob: It isn`t his job! But now Kalifornia Heat restore control as they double drop-kick both teams out of the ring and pose to the
fans. Security is now at ringside seperating the teams. The All-Americans won`t be happy, but they get another shot at the title at Nuclear War!Fare. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Result: All-Americans win by DQ due to British Empire interference. MR: 8 ************************************* INTERNATIONAL WAR! TITLE MATCH "MAGNUM" BIFF McGRIFF Vs. SETH BLITZSPEAR ************************************* (The final match of the evening. Magnum arrives with Felicia McGraw and Little Louie accompanying him. The fans boo until they can boo no more. Then Seth Blitzspear appears, and the place errupts double what it did for Jim Bondo earlier)
Bob: The fans love this guy! He`s everything a hero should be! Ross: Stupid, insane and an arm full of scars where the streoids have been injected. Yeah, okay!
Bob: Seth is greeting the fans as he circles the ring at 1000mph and finally he gets into the ring. The fans are looking forward to the former World War! champions match with "Magnum" Biff McGriff.
(The match begins with a test of power - a grecco-roman knuckle lock. Magnum gets Seth down, but slowly, with the support of the fans, Seth battles back up, only to be shoved into the corner by McGriff. Seth bursts out of the corner, attempting a shoulder-tackle, but McGriff sidesteps it and pushes Seth hard into the other corner. A small section of supporters shout "Ole!" Biff then begins to pound on Beth with his big powerful forearms, then whips him to the ropes. Felicia distacts the ref and Biff unleashes a blow with the fist that would knock Tyson`s head off, as Seth returns off the ropes. But instead of falling, Seth takes it, soaking the damage.
He beats his chest, as if to say, try it again. Biff unleashes another right hook and then a left, onto Seth`s chin, and Seth just takes it. His mouth
is bleeding, but he shows no sign of pain. McGriff, well aware of Seth`s ability to persist, backs off and calls for Seth to charge him. Seth does, but with a low dropkick and not a clothesline. This took McGriff by suprise and sends him staggering back. Seth charges properly this time, and his momentum carries them both to the outside.
Outside the ring, Biff gets the edge with Louie & Felicia`s help, but they are soon both back inside the ring. They lock up and Seth gets the better of Mcgriff, slamming him. McGriff soaks it and then responds with a neckbreaker. McGriff then takes control of the match for the next few minutes...)
Bob: And a big backbreaker there by "Magnum" on Seth. Seth is indeed in big trouble. Ross: Seth has always been in trouble with the law. Haven`t you seen his criminal record.
Bob: Will you stop it? Magnum whips Seth to the ropes, Seth stops and holds the ropes. Magnum walks over to a tired looking Seth. He goes for a vertical suplex...he can`t...Seth blocks...Seth tries...Magnum blocks...he tries once again...and again Magnum blocks...and again...he gets it....he lifts McGriff up...he holds the
350lb International War Champion high above his head...he`s just holding him there...all the blood rushing to Magnum`s head.
Ross: He seems to be holding him up there for an eternity. But then again, every match involving Seth seems to last an eternity.
Bob: And now he wavers...which was is he going to fall...face first into the ring, or backwards onto the ropes and out of the ring...it`s backwards! He suplexes "Magnum" Biff McGriff out of the ring! Can you believe it?
Ross: Even I`m impressed Bob. Bob: Magnum looks to be out totally cold. He hit the steel railings really hard. Felicia is calling for the paratrooping medics...and here they come...dropping in from the top of the arena on their
parachutes. Meanwhile, the ref has called for the bell. Seth has won by countout. Ross: But he doesn`t get the title! Bob: Seth doesn`t seem to care to much. He`s just busy celebrating with thr fans. This is a big victory for him. However, that`s all we have time for this week. I hope to see you all at Nuclear War!Fare. Until next week, from me & "Rude" Ross Royale, see ya! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (c) War! Wrestling Federation 1996
