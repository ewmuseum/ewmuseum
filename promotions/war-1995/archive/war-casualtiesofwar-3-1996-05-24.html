~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE THE RECKONER (8) Vs. “SUPERSTAR” GOLDEN PAGE (7) w/Mr.Money The Reckoner stood taller and weighed heavier than Page. From the moment the bell went, they fought with an intense fury. Page appeared to be on a mission to impress, but The Reckoner was not one to be impressed. Mr.Money undid a turnbuckle and Page made The Reckoner bleed by repeatedly smashing his face into it.
The Reckoner responded by hiptossing Page over the top rope. Outside the ring, Page was waiting for The Reckoner and tried to DDT him, but the
Reckoner just lifted him over and into the crowd. Page started to run, but
The Reckoner followed with a chair and managed to smash Page on the back of the head. Mr.Money distracted The Reckoner from pinning Page, and with The
Reckoner facing the other way, Page got up and Bulldogged the Reckoner down the steps. The Reckoner was in amazing pain, but kicked out of a pin.
Page ripped a Reckoner T-Shirt (Only $45) off a fan and throttled The
Reckoner further while Mr.Money repeatedly rammed a flagpole into The
Reckoner`s gut. Page went for another Bulldog down the concrete steps, and then a DDT. The referee stopped the match, judging that The Reckoner had suffered too much, but Page didn`t think so and piledrived The Reckoner down a set of five stairs. The fans started chanting “Seth, Seth, Seth” and eventually Seth turned up, but all he could do was help his fallen friend get medical attention.

Seth gets up. Starr off the top rope with the baseball bat...Seth dodges and punches Starr halfway. Starr falls. Seth with a military press! Seth with a splash! Seth with the pin.
