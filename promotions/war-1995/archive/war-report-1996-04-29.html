The WAR! Report

29 April 1996

The screen cuts to a darkened set with a man sitting behind a desk. behind the man is a wall of monitors showing cuts from various WAR! cards, closeups of WAR! stars or the WAR! logo. As a voice starts talking one of the WAR! logos streak out from the monitor to cover the screen as a metal plate fades in under it. Suddenly a series of explosions go off on the plate, and as the smoke clears there are letters blasted into the metal.

W W AAAA AAAA RRRR RRRR !! _______________________ | | | R * E * P * O * R * T | |_______________________| [Voice] With your host Tony Taylor! The lights on the set are turned up as the logo fades out and the man behind the desk looks up from the papers. He is a young man with short, dark hair and wearing a dark blue suit with the WAR! logo on the right breastpocket.
[Tony] Good evening, wrestling fans, and welcome to the first WAR! Report where we'll be taking a short look at the matches for the upcoming Tuesday Night
WAR!fare. I'm Tony Taylor and will be the guy leading you through the lineup, but first we are going to take a look at some of the things that changed in the titlepicture here in WAR due to WARATHON 1. Let's roll that tape.
The screen cuts to footage from WARATHON 1. The footage shows the end of the Johnny "Sidewinder" Missile Vs. Bruiser Buggelli match as Johnny reverses a backslide for the pin. As Buggelli is lead away by Suna Gib, a man with long silvery hair and wearing a silver bodysuit (picture a silvercolored Golddust) Tony's voice is heard again.
[Tony] The strange newcomer, Suna Gib, was to make quite a mark on the event. After being beaten by Johnny Missile Bruiser Buggelli was lead from the ring by the silverclad one. Only the first sign of what was to happen later that night.
The screen cuts to the end of the Rick Showboat Vs. Bounty Hunter match where Rick grabs some brass knuckles out of Bounty Hunter's grasp and uses them to knock out the Hunter for the victory. Once again the loser is lead away by Suna Gib.
[Tony] The second hint that something was up came when once again Gib led a man from the ring, despite there being no knowledge that any of these three men had had any previous contact with eachother. The plot was finally revealed after the Ghetto Boyz had defeated Canadian Carnage to unify the
World WAR! and International WAR! tag team titles. As the Boyz were celebrating after the hard fought battle they were confronted by Gib leading the Bounty Hunter and Bruiser Buggelli, who apparently had formed tag team in the group known as the Brotherhood, TO the ring this time.
Gib, apparently now managing the two, produced a contract requiring the
Ghetto Boyz to immediately defend against Gib's team. The Boyz tried to put up a fight, but being exhausted from already fighting the Carnage they were handily defeated by the fresher team of Buggelli and Hunter.
The Ghetto Boyz won and lost the belts all in the same night. The screen shows Bounty Hunter and Bruiser Buggelli standing triumphantly with the belts. [Tony] The Boyz were understandably upset, and this has led to a rematch at Tuesday Night WAR!fare. The screen cuts to show The CrocMaster meeting the Warlord, Big Duke, for the title. [Tony] The Brotherhood was on a roll this night as the CrocMaster met Big Duke for the Warlord title. Once again Suna Gib played an instrumental role in the match as he showed up and started making advances towards Duke's manager, King Benny. Duke, angered by Gib's antics, went to teach him a lesson, but forgot about CrocMaster who took advantage of this and managed to hit Duke with a CrocBomb after a devastating neckbreaker for the
3-count and the title. All of a sudden the Brotherhood, a group whom none of the other organizations on WAR! seemed to take completely serious, were the holders of two titles, and there was more to come as four of the best light heavyweights in WAR! met to decide WHO would be the new Liberty
Champion. The screen is split in four, showing closeups of the four contestants in the four-way match for the Liberty title. Sid Starr, Tyler Stevens,
DeathJester and Jim Bondo. It then cuts to footage from the match itself as Tony speaks. [Tony] It all started as could be expected with Stevens and Bondo teaming up against Starr and DeathJester. All four men are former Liberty champions, and we were treated to a lot of action. Bondo was the first man to be eliminated, followed by Starr. This left DeathJester and Stevens to fight it out for the title. The ending was marred however by the most unlikely thing to ever happen in a wrestling match. Stevens was eliminated, not by being pinned or counted out, but by being caught in the blast of little, EXPLODING girl. WAR! officials are trying to find out just what happened here, but so far no comments have been forthcoming.
The result, however, was obvious to all. Tyler Stevens did not get up again, and the DeathJester was declared champion, bringing a third title to the Brotherhood. It was later revealed that because of the explosion
Tyler Stevens would not be able to wrestle anymore.
The screen cuts to showing Booster Dunne getting a countout Victory over
Cardinal Andrea Mancini.
[Tony] The only titlematch not to end in a change of champions was the International Championship Match between Booster Dunne and Cardinal Mancini. The match was hard fought, but Dunne managed to pull it through in the end, showing just why he is considered one of the best in WAR!
and continuing his winning streak. There's a pause as the screen again cuts to another match. This time of
Stein "Ice" Berg and Tonnage colliding in the middle of the ring and neither man going down.
[Tony] WARATHON 1 saw a lot of changes in the titlepicture, and one of the BIG ones, literally and figuratively speaking, was the result of the match between the gigantic Tonnage and the almost equally imposing champion,
Stein "Ice" Berg. Maybe surprisingly, considering the dislike these two men have for eachother, it was a clean match. They may not like eachother, but they apparently respect the other enough to fight the match cleanly.
In the end it was Tonnage who came out on top as he managed to execute the
Gigaton Crush on the champion and get the three count. Amazingly, for a match involving Armageddon and WARForce there was no post-match run-in.
Simply a clean, hard wrestling match. The camera cuts back to Tony at his desk. [Tony] Well, that should bring us up to date on the titlesituation, so without any further ado, let's move onto what's going to happen at Tuesday Night
WAR!fare.
The monitor wall shows the faces of Stein Berg and Tonnage with the World
WAR! belt between them.
[Tony] First we have the World WAR! Championship rematch from WARATHON 1. Again
Stein "Ice" Berg and Tonnage meet for the belt, but this time big Tonnage will be defending it. Like Tonnage has put it, these two men may be different sides of the same coin. Two big and powerful men. Two men who have gone toe to toe before. Two enemies who are developing a grudging respect for eachother.
The wall changes to show Big Duke and CrocMaster separated by the Warlord belt. [Tony] The Warlord Championship rematch is sure to set tempers flaring. Big Duke was not a happy camper after losing the belt, and the Rich Boys lost the only title the stable was holding. All of a sudden the Brotherhood are holding three of five WAR! titles, and CrocMaster has NO intentions of giving the belt back to Duke. Look for Duke to be out for revenge, and look for the CrocMaster to simply be out for blood.
The screens change to show the Ghetto Boyz and The Brotherhood team consisting of Bounty Hunter and Bruiser Buggelli. The World WAR! tag team belts separate the pictures.
[Tony] World WAR! Tag Team Title Match. Special stipulation: Loser leaves town! The GhettoBoyz are FURIOUS. Plain and simple. Feeling they have been cheated out of the belts they are determined to make a point here. NOBODY messes with the Boyz and gets away with it. NOBODY. This one is guaranteed to get ugly.
The screens change to show the face of Brett Stevens next to the face of
DeathJester, separating them is the WAR! Liberty belt.
[Tony] How did Tyler Stevens manage to swing this one to happen? The newcomer,
Brett Stevens, Tyler's brother, replaces Tyler in the fight for the
Liberty belt after Tyler's announcement that he could not wrestle anymore. Vice president Bullfrog's only comment when confronted with a total newcomer getting a titleshot was "That sneaky, little..... I can't believe I didn't see the fine print... I.... I... NO COMMENT!"
The screens show Lord Byron and Aaron Iron. [Tony] Lord Byron wanted Aaron Iron in the ring, and made some rather nasty remarks about the Jamaican Superstar to ensure this would happen. He got his wish and now the question is: "Can the English nobleman take out the Ironman from
Jamaica?" You'll only get the answer on Tuesday Night WAR!fare.
The screens change to show Sid Starr and an unknown face. [Tony] Sid "Stray Cat" Starr faces off against Mark "The Canadian Strangler" Starr as another newcomer enters the battlefields of WAR! Will the "Strangler" have what it takes to take on the self-proclaimed coolest thing in the ring, Sid Starr. Is there room enough in WAR! for two
Starrs? Booster Dunne and "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer fill the screens. [Tony] In a non-title match Booster Dunne faces "Hollywood" Johnny Dreamer. Dunne has his work cut out for him as he meets Dreamer. Can the man in blue and gold keep up his winning ways, or will Johnny be the man to put a stop to Booster's hot streak?
The screens change to show American Patriot and Johnny "Sidewinder" Missile [Tony] Two missiles collide in the ring this Tuesday night as the Patriot takes on "Sidewinder". Who will come out on top after this one explodes?
Apocalypse and The Star Smashers come on the screens. [Tony] More Tag Team action as the Star Smashers face Apocalypse. Both teams have both speed and power, and both teams have something to prove after coming off losses to their respective opponents at WARATHON 1.
Finally the screens change to show General Leo and Sabin Figaro. [Tony] If you thought The Ghetto Boyz vs The Brotherhood would get ugly then just wait for these two maniacs to get their hands on eachother. Last time they fought across half the city. This time there's no leaving that ring until one man has lost. The lumberjacks will see to that.
The screens behind Tony change to all show the WAR! Report logo. [Tony] That's all we have for tonight. I'm Tony Taylor, and this has been the... WWWW WWWW A RRRRRRRRR !!!!
