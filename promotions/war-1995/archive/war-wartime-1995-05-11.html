WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WAR!TIME — Special Card

11 May 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------ WAR FEDERATION presents WAR!TIME With your hosts Avocado Bob & Dr. Nathaniel Darkness LIVE FROM THE WAR!DATORIUM ------------------------------------------------------ Avocado Bob: Welcome Ladies & Gentlemen to an unexpected & special card tonight. I`ve only just roled out of bed and hardly had time to get ready.
General Andropov has, for reasons unknown, called a card together to be fought in front of NO crowd. There`s going to be four or five great matches tonight and the debut of our new co-commentator, Dr. Nathaniel Darkness. Doctor, what are you doing here.
DND: To be perfectly frank, I`m here scouting for new wrestlers to add to my stable ClanDemon, currently dominating in the ICW, FBWA & MWF. And by the way, when you roll out of bed Bob, please roll on some deodorant.
Bob: Well, I`ll take that as a compliment, ToxicShock, our previous commentator, will be at ringside tonight in the corner of Classico &
Massiveman as they prepare to take on XL-9 & Trailboss Mike Dallas for the
WAR! Tag-team title. We`ll also see a match between DeathThreat & the guy Tox will be interviewing, Seth Blitzspear.
Dnd: Seth`s been riled by DeathThreat, just how DeathThreat wanted, and there is no way that the maniac called Seth Blitzspear is going to win. And that`s an educated fact.
Bob: Furthermore, there`s a debut for the Maltese Falcon against Architisman and the repeat of the title match that never was - Johnny Thunder vs. Bludgeon.
DND: That`ll be a good match - I love seeing guys called Thunder get beaten senseless. A bit like when he missed his last match,hey?
Bob: Well, there`s been fines imposed on all the Masters of War for that. But lets get the action underway now, as Architisman waits in the ring for his opponent today, the Maltese Falcon.
--------------------------------------------------------- ArchitisMan Vs. The Maltese Falcon ArchitisMan waits in the ring, looking down the aisle for the Maltese Falcon. Falcon doesn`t appear, so Architis runs down the aisle to look for him. He want`s a fight. Architis then turns round and sees a black caped and masked figure descend vertically onto the ring from the top of the WAR!DATORIUM.
It`s a truly magnificent entrance by the Falcon, and that is followed by a truly magnificent display of high-flying wrestling, using combination of flying dropkicks and high-cross bodys to gain a pin over the seriously weak opposition of ArchitisMan. Falcon with the pin following a flying elbow drop at 7.41
---------------------------------------------------------- Seth Blitzspear Vs. DeathThreat Before the match, ToxicShock intervies Seth in the middle of the ring... Tox: So, Seth, what are you feelings of your opponent Seth: GRraaaghgh, MAIM, KILL, HURT HIM, HURT HIM, grrrarararaar Tox: So what are you going to do about him? Seth: Grrgaggrgh..KILL, KILL, KILL, KILL (Seth then proceeds to get a big black marker pen out and scrawl across his chest "KILL DEATHTHREAT, KILL DEATHTHREAT")
Tox: Welll, erm, it`s good to see you`re not stark-raving loony. Bob, I`m outta here... DeathThreat walks down the aisle, stops halfway, and over the microphone shouts "Come and get me you muscle-bound moronic female" Seth gets mad andstorms down the aisle, falling right into a trap. Out of the darkness leaps BadTimes, The Fire and Bludgeon. They start beating the hell out of
Seth, using piledrivers, powerbombs - anything that hurts, including iron railings. "That`s what you get for being stupid" DeathThreat says over the microphone, and the Masters of War walk off, leaving DeathThreat to drag Seth into the ring for an easy pin at 0.07. Seth is then stretchered off at the end, in need of medical treatment
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tag-team Championship XL-9 & "Trailboss" Iron Mike Dallas Vs. Classico & MassiveMan XL-9`s reputation preceeds him now, and Classico & Massiveman try to keep their distance. Atomicdrops and slams by XL-9 severely injure Classico. XL-9 lets Classico tag Massiveman, and for the first time ever, a smile seems to crop upon his face. However, Massiveman, with Classico distracting the referee, reveals a crowbar cunningly hidden, and whacks XL-9 around the head.
Blood splatters everywhere, and shocked at how much damage he must have caused, MassiveMan drops the crowbar. The referee turns around and sees the fallen, blodied XL-9 and Massiveman with the crowbar by his feet, and promptly
DQ`s the team from the FourSeasons. The FourSeasons walk off down the aisle with a yet again disgruntled "Trailboss" in pursuit. He clotheslines them both from behind and runs off. The medics go to see how XL-9 is, expecting a shattered skull, but instead, XL-9 stands up and walks off perfectly, with little plastic packets of blood leaking on the ring floor.....
------------------------------------------------------------- International War Championship Bludgeon Vs. Johnny Thunder/w.ToxicShock Thunder bravely walks down the aisle. But again there is another suprise attack by the Masters of War (except Bludgeon who is in the ring). However, before they can do anything, Seth Blitzspear storms from the dressing rooms, smashing BadTimes & The Fire with a chair, knocking them out. DeathThreat starts to cower off, pleading for mercy. Seth starts to charge for
Deaththreat, but he is held back by XL-9, who makes an appearance in this melee. DeathThreat gets confident again and goes to dropkick Seth, who moves out of the way, resulting in XL-9 getting dropkicked. DeathThreat, realzing his mistake, runs into the seating area, with XL-9 angrily following. Seth pulls a groggy BadTimes & The Fire up, and brawls with the 2 weakened opponents back to the locker rooms, aided by the FourSeasons
To the match... A match filled with powermoves, but it is Bludgeon looking on the defensive and a bit out of shape, as if not expecting to fight. A series of leg-lariets, clotheslines and elbowdrops weakens Bludgeon sufficiently for an attempted Thunder and Lightning. Yet there is life in Bludgeon yet, catching the foot of Thunder from his attempted backbrain kick, spins Thunder around and headbutts him from behind, knocking Thunder to the floor. Sensing victory in the air, BLudgeon estsablishes Thunder in position for Bludgeons trademark powerbomb, but Thunder lifts Bludgeon high in the air, with Bludgeon falling onto the top-turnbuckle. Thunder eats Bludegon on the top-turnbuckle and superplexes him, following into a pin position. Bludgeon kicks out at two, so
Thunder lifts him up for a slam. Bludgeon shifts his weight colapsing on
Thunder, but Thunder prevents a pin by getting his foot on the ropes.
Bludgeon stands up, but then collapses. Thunder seizes the oppurtunity and applies a BostonCrab, gaining a submission from Bludgeon at 15.32, and the
International War Title as a result.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Avocado Bob: Well, how did you enjoy that evening Doc? Doc: Like a syringe stuck in my rectum, but aside from that, some good tactical moves by the Masters of War. XL-9 is a vicious monster though, and I really have no medical knowledge that he is actually Human
Avocado Bob: And anyone here tonight you`d like to add to your stable? Doc: TheMaltese Falcon was stylish, and I like that in a wrestler, but he`s got to prove himself yet. Anyhow, he`s too much of a goody-goody. He hasn`t got that hardened edge that I`m lookng for yet. Seth is a bit too thick..well a lot too thick, and Trailboss is under Mr.Kang anyhow,although he could of course do a lot better under me. Now that Johny Thunder - he`s a fine prospect showing maturity beyong is years. Definitely worth anther look.
Avocado Bob: And you, along with 20 zillion billion people will get that look tomorrow night when the Champion of War tournament takes place. See you in 1.
