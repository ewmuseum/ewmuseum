WarZone 3 (Season 1) — Dare Ye Enter THE PIT?

19 May 1995 (two-part posting)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----------------------------------------------------- WAR! FEDERATION PRESENTS WAR!ZONE 3 With your hosts Avacado Bob & Dr.Nathaniel Darkness
Live from the War!datorium ---------------------------------------------------- Avacado Bob: Greetings one and greetings all to the most exciting War! card yet. We`ve got title matches, speciality matches, grudge matches and debuts.
Dr.Darkness: That`s right Bob. And boy, am I looking forward to the debut of the vampire Jacob Demonchilde
BoB: Not a real vampire of course Doc: Well, you haven`t seen this guy yet! Bob: Demonchilde will be squaring off against "Booster" Dunne later in the show. But for now, what are your thoughts on the rest of the show Doc?
Doc: Thunder will lose his International title to Bludgeon in the table match, both Trailboss & Maltese Falcon will lose their match against each other. I don`t care who gets the world title as long as neither the French or
British yobs gets anywhere near me. The masters of War will get rid of Thunder, Falcon & Blitzspear in THE PIT once and for all, XL-9 will destroy
Classico, my ClanDemon will obviously destroy The Youngbloods & "The American"
Butch Williams is going to wrap Mountaintop madman up. Bob: How the hell did you say all that without pausing for breath? Doc: Just one of my many talents. Bob: Well, speaking of talents, not many come more talented than the International champion heading down the aisle now - Johnny Thunder. Doc: Well, not many come more powerful, more vicious than his opponent,
Bludgeon. That`s Bludgeon`s belt and he`s gonna get it back tonight.
Bob: The stipulations being of course that to win you must climb the table and jump up for the belt hanging 10 feet above the ring! This`ll be wild.
----------------------------------------------------------------- International War Title Table Match Johnny Thunder Vs. Bludgeon w/WarMaster Few rules The contest rapidly becomes a battle of brains as well as brawn, each wreatler cautiously sussing the other out. Thunder controls the match for the first five minutes, but when Bludgeon reverses a suplex, the tide changes. Slamming the champion repeatedly, and applying further powermoves, Bludgeon edges closer to victory until Thunder responds to the cheers of the fans and elevates Bludgeon into the air just before he is powerbombed. With Bludgeon knowcked out, Thunder pulls the table into the ring and goes to climb it.
Warmaster intervenes though and pushes Thunder off. Seth Blitzspear, sitting in the crowd goes to help, throwing WarMaster into the ropes and applying his
"Flingtime" Backdrop. Back in the ring, with Thunder dazed, Bludgeon is the first to take the initiative. The two wrestlers brawl, with Bludgeon getting the advantage. He whips Thunder to the rope and attempts a backdrop - but
Thunder sees Bludgeon early, leaping feet first on to his back and springing up to grab the belt, winning the match at 14.48
--------------------------------------- The Freedom title one fall "Trailboss" Mike Dallas Vs. The Maltese Falcon The new freedom champion outsizes his opponent by 12 inches and a hell of a lot of pounds, but that does not deter Falcon, intent on not letting
Trailbosses title reign last longer than this match. Falcon uses his speed to gain an advantage, wearing the bigman down, but Trailboss is resilient and catches Falcon in midflight, following up with a powerslam. Trailboss goes for the pin, but only gets a two count. Later during the match, Trailboss still dominates, but Falcon ducks an attempted clothesline and manages to catch the bigman in a sleeper. Knocking the big guy down and out, the referre raises Trailboss`s arm, once, twice...and that`s three. The winner of this match is...."Trailboss" Mike Dallas
Avocado Bob: What is the referee doing? Falcon clearly won the match. Doc: And I don`t think there`s going to be a further ruling. The match goes to Trailboss - he retains the title.
Bob: I can`t see why. There`s something going on here, and I don`t like it. ------------------------------------------------- Tagteam Match Youngblood vs. ClanDemon (Badrock & Shaft Vs. InterDimensional Warrior & DeadPunk) Bob: And I thought the tagteam scene was over in War! Apparently not, and from what I gather, the winner of this match will be the new tag-team champions.
Doc: And you`ve got to hand it to my guys - they`ve got the experience behing them of being a tag-team.
Bob: Why aren`t you with them? Doc: My guy will whip their butts easily. I`m not needed. In the match, it is the Youngbloods who saw the co-ordination of a good tag-team, ClanDemon arguing over the slightest things. The popular
Youngbloods use clean tags & legal double teaming. ClanDemon are resistant though. Then Dr.Nathaniel Darkness turns up. Interdimensional Warrior is fighting Shaft while IDW distracts the referee. Darkness gets Badrocks attention, throwing some kind of red powder at him, before running off.
Avocado Bob: What did you do there? Doc: You`ll see.... Bob: And there is Shaft with the tag to Badrock. Badrock stumbles into the ring....and he`s fallen over...he`s knocked out...a simple cover by IDW wins the match for ClanDemon, their first title yet! The Youngbloods are going to feel gutted
----------------------------------------------------------- XL-9 Vs. Classico One Fall XL-9, disappointed with his loss to Trailboss Mike Dallas, takes all his aggression out on a lacklustre Classico, not being hit once by Classico and easily gaining a submission at 4.45 with a surfboard.
-------------------------------------------------------------- Mountaintop Madman Vs. "The American" Butch Williams At the beginning of the match, Butch Williams has these comments? "You call yourself an American, Madman? An American? Americans aren`t losers and we`re pround of the way we look. Well boy, you certainly aren`t. You`re so ugly I feel ashamed...you here me, ashamed, to even live in the same country as you. So tonight I`m gonna kick yo` butt so hard that you`re gonna leave this great country for good"
Butch`s comments seem to rile Madman a bit. In fact, the comments work against "The American" with the madman winning every brawl. Deep into the match, "The Amercian" recovers some pride with his Superpower-Slam special move, somehow lifting the 6`10, 369 lb wrestler into position for the running powerslam. But he can`t get the 3 count. Madman gets infuriated and lunges at "The American", but "The American" dives, catching Madmans massive legs and moving them into a boston crab, making the big man submit at 16.02
---------------------------------- Debut Match (For both) Jacob Demonchilde w/Grizzly Vs. "Booster" Dunne Booster has a 4 inch height advantage over the 6ft Demonchilde, but Demonchildes presence alone seems to compensate, striking fear into the crowd. As he enters the ring, lights out, the crowd are in silence, apart from the screams of a few children. In the match, it is the screams of the charming
Las Vegas resident "Booster" Dunne that can be heard, with Demonchile applying painful nerve holds. Dunne recovers and the two exchange moves. However, it is Demonchilde that strangely prevails, grabbing Dunne by the throat and
gazing at him, apparently hypnotizing him. Demonchilde follows up with a throat slam, and with his tagteam partner from nextweek, Grizzly, celebrate the 3 count over Dunne.
Coming up......WAR!ZONE 3 part 2 - the pit match and world championship match ==================== Part 2 ==================== Continued from part 1..... Bob: I`ve just heard that two new tag-teams have been signed and they will be fighting now! The winner will fight ClanDemon for the titles next week.
--------------------------------------- Tag-team Match Divine Alliance Vs. Darkness & Light The divine alliance comprise of the egyptian sun god Amon Ra & the greek god
Zeus (not Tiny Tom Lister). Darkness & Light, twin, yet not identical brothers from South Africa start the match with a free for all in the centre of the ring. When the match calms down, both tag-teams function well, unsure of any stratergy. Well into the match, the high-flying Darkness & Light go to double clothesline the massive 7`1" Zeus, but he gets them both first with a shocking and almighty clothesline, pinning them both, a hand on each at 25.06.
The winners, and next weeks challengers for the tag-title are the Divine
Alliance ----------------------------------------- World Championship Match 2 out of 3 falls Falls count anywhere Brutus "The Sidewinder" Noir Vs. The Inner City Firmer With both only just recovered from the incident at War!Report 3, the match starts off light, but eventually turns into a heavy heavy brawl. The two could have been double disqualified many a time, most notably for a double-headbut on the referre. But that wouldn`t resolve the match. The first fall goes to the InnerCityFirmer with a spin-piledriver onto metal railings at 13.45. Brutus Noir, the champion gains a pin deep in the crowd at
29.18 wiht a powerbomb onto a stack of chairs. The fight goes up to the balcony 30 feet up directly above the ring. Noir throws ICF off the balcony and tries to follow with an elbow drop, but ICF moves out of the way. In great agony, ICF rolls over and pins Noir at 45.02. The new champion - the
Inner City Firmer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- THE PIT MATCH The Masters of War (Bludgeon, DeathThreat, The Fire, BadTimes Billy) Vs. Johnny Thunder, The Maltese Falcon & Seth Blitzspear Bob: I`d better just explain the rules of this match. As you can see, the ring has been removed, and underneath it lies the PIT. Ringsized but 20 feet down, it is floored and walled with concrete. The only way out is a ladder in the near corner. The victors are those that escape from THE PIT first. Those out of the Pit can throw others back in. Every wrestler is active until one whole team is out, and as you can see,the advantage lies with the Masters of
War.
Highlights.... 1.03 Masters of War keep opponents at bay to allow Badtimes to escape 2.34 Blitzspears face is rearranged when DDT`d. Blood everywhere 3.08 Bad Times escapes 3.23 Thunder escapes 5.44 The Fire lacerates Blitzspears face 6.00 Thunder thrown back in 6.22 The Fire & DeathThreat throw a redpowder at the three wrestlers, slowing them down, allowing both to escape for the victory at 6.55
