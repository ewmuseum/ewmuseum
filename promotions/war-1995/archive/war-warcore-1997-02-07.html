WAR!CORE

7 February 1997

(Martel is standing in his luxury dressing room, looking at a glass case. Venom is seated off to the left, his head buried in a child's French book. Martel dons his purple and gold sequined robe and turns to the camera.) Martel: Confidence is key. You see, Blitzspear, knowing that I have a vast amount of wrestling knowledge, while you clothesline, I've predicted and developed a winning strategy to defeat you, and take your Unified Title.
Knowing my chances, I already had a display case set-up for it. (He turns, walks to the dresser, picks up the glass case, and returns.) Martel: You see, it's not enough to simply parade around with the belt in front of your shocked fans. No, I plan on returning to the estate with this token of my ability. This will hang on the wall in the trophy room.
Indeed, it will hang with the replica of the ERWF Heavyweight Championship and the UEWA Light Heavyweight Title.
(He motions with his head. Venom walks over, takes the glass case, and puts it back on the shelf.)
Martel: So, clod, prepare to be beaten, stripped of yet another title. (Martel ties his robe shut, sneers, and heads for the ring.) ------------------------------------------------ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presents From the Chicago War!Dome On February 7th 1997 WAR!CORE with your brief, but to the point hosts "Avocado" Bob Whitely & Xavier Suave ----------------------------------------------- Bob: Welcome one and welcome all to the show that replaces WAR!TIME, WAR!CORE. This night promises some great matches, not least of which is
Seth Blitzspear defending his Unified title against Charles Martel, and it will truly be interesting to see just how Seth responds to all his friends turning against him.
XS: And it ain`t just his friends turning against him that worries him. His enemies are siding with him! Max Doom, who got a victory against Aaron
Iron last night, has pledged his support to Seth Blitzspear, and word is that Max Doom will be on his team at the forthcoming supercard - CASUALTIES
OF WAR III. Bob: With friends like that, who needs enemies? XS: It`s like I`ve always said, Seth just can`t help making enemies. He uses people, and they don`t like that. Don`t think bad of Gordon & Decan, they`ve just wisely decided that enough is enough. They`re not going to be his lackeys any longer.
Bob: Well, Decan gets a shot tonight at Lord Byron`s World War! Title, and it is going to be a severe test of Byron's ability to cope with one of the biggest men in WAR!
XS: Decan has been number challenger for years, but only now, with Flea as his mentor, has a title shot been secured for the behemoth.
Bob: Decan, of course, won the title last November, beating Tonnage. Can he do it again? He is certainly one of the toughest opponents Byron could wish to face.
XS: Damn right. But then again, Lord Byron isn`t World Champion for nothing. He`s worked his way up. First the War!Lord title, a title he was never beaten for. Then the International War! title, which he only lost because Bane crippled him. Now he has come back, stronger than ever, and he is unbeaten. He is the World War! champion.
Bob: One other title match tonight is Kalifornia Heat, the longest ever reigning International War! tag team championsfacing the intimidating threat of Phantasmagoria in a rematch of the match that never was at MegaWar 2.
XS: Tonight is the night for Phantasmagoria. I can feel it. Bob: I think Kalifornia Heat will have something to say about that, but
Phantasmagoria are unbeaten still, and have proven that their power, and intimidation factor, is certainly something to be reckoned with. Speaking of reckoning, Damien Legend faces The Nomad for the first time. Nomad is on a bit of a roll, but faces the versatile Damien Legend, the most complete athlete in WAR!
XS: But he ain`t the complete wrestler, and Nomad is. Simply put. Bob: There are plenty of other great matches tonight. Let`s go to the action. REIGN OF TERROR w/Sunny B. (6) Vs. WINDY CITY WARRIORS w/Nomad (8) Pierre & Lotine jumped Malcolm & Anthony in a truly hardcore match showing the growing tensions between Ground Zero & The Annihilation Corporation.
This was a sadistic, all-out brawl, all out attack match that suited both teams to the ground, and although Windy City Warriors got the better of the brawl, and cut Lotine & Pierre open (with the aid of an exposed turnbuckle),
Lotine & Pierre could always rely on cheapshots to get back in the match.
Their cheapshots, carefully done, kept them in the match, but when Sunny B. tried interfering, Nomad pulled her off the ring apron, and she fell back into the crowd barriers. Reign of Terror saw and jumped outside to confront
Nomad, Windy City Warriors followed. Chris Deugau then emerged having seen what happened to Sunny B. and began attacking Nomad. Bane, Decan & Gordon emerged quickly afterwards and began to seriously damage Deugau when they took him by surprise. Kraken, Noir and The Terminators emerged to help their leader, and soon a massive brawl between the AC and Ground Zero was in full effect. Order, eventually, was restored.
MR: 7 (10 for the post match brawl) MD: 7.35 Double countout/DQ PUNISHER (8) Vs. KRAKEN, THE GREATEST HUNTER (6) Kraken entered this match straight from the previous brawl, and that really had taken a lot out of him. No AC member was with him because they were all recovering from the brawl. Punisher totally had the run of this match, and despite a few brave fightbacks by Kraken, the end was inevitable. Punisher got him following another impressive display and a big leg drop.
MR: 6 MD: 5.06 Punisher by Pinfall BRUTUS NOIR (7) Vs. DEATHJESTER (6) Two long-time competitors met and even though Noir was powerbombed during the brawl, he seemed fresher for this fight than Kraken did. Noir managed to keep the fight to a brawl, his blood was pumping, and DeathJester offered little in response, mostly because he kept on curling up in the centre of the ring, cackling innanely. Noir finished him off with the Marseille
Strangler.
MR: 6 MD: 6.22 Noir by submission NOMAD (8) Vs. “THE RECKONER” DAMIEN LEGEND (9) Nomad cam out of the brawl quite healthy and contributed to a wonderful match where he displayed his many skills. Legend, however, was showing the form, and the versatility, that took him near to the top last time he was in
WAR! He was hitting Nomad from every direction - in the air, on the match, standing…every single way. Nomads technical dominance could only carry him so far against such a large, multi-talented wrestler who easily over-powered him, and eventually Nomad was introduced to the LegendPlex, which secured the victory for Damien Legend.
MR: 9 MD: 20.38 Legend by pinfall CHAOS KNIGHTS (6) Vs. CANADIAN CARNAGE (8) The Chaos Knights form has been slipping as of late, and coinciding with a
Canadian Carnage re-ermergence, the result was inevitable. Carnage, with their new fan support, found extra strength when they were being pounded down, but that was rarely needed as they kept the Chaos Knight on the ropes, in trouble. The match ended when Lafleur bulldogged Mayhem, and then tagged in Canuck who applied a boston crab which got the victory.
MR: 7 MD: 14.21 Carnage by submission TYLER STEVENS w/Big Duke (6) Vs. BIFF MCGRIFF w/Felicia McGraw (8) Initially this match looked like it would be over within the first couple of minutes as Biff totally pounded Stevens flat, but Stevens hung on in there, and despite hardly getting a major string of offensive moves going, he
pulled off a victory. Big Duke was arguing with McGriff after McGriff had just press-slammed Stevens. McGriff was showing off to the crowd and Duke started abusing him. McGriff didn`t see Stevens get up. The moment he turned around, Stevens executed his “Heatbreaker” reverse superkick to the throat. Biff fell hard, and Stevens quickly covered, hooking the leg and getting the three.
After the match, when Duke & Stevens were walking down the aisle, Max Doom suddenly appeared on his Harley and skidded it into Duke and Stevens, knocking them down. Doom then proceeded to pound on Duke, while McGriff took the opportunity to get some after-match kicks in on Stevens. Security, eventually, stopped the brawl. Stevens and Duke needed slight medical attention.
MR: 8 MD: 9.53 NON-TITLE "DIRTY" DOUG SIMPKINS (8) Vs. "SHOWSTOPPER" SIMON LEBEC (9)w/Ms.Crystal & Francois This match started off fast, thanks to Lebec, but Doug slowed it down with a nice-trip toe hold into a chin-lock. This initiated the beginning of a very fine actual wrestling match, and Lebec seemed to be having the better of it, countering most of Doug`s attempts, and generally frustrating him. Near the tenth minute, was Lebec was clearly getting the better of Simpkins, Simpkins proceeded to turn the match a bit dirtier, poking Lebec in the eye and then pile-driving him. Simpkins got a good run together, but then Ms.Crystal got involved, arguing with the referee, allowing Francois to enter the ring.
But then The Punisher charged to ringside and pulled Francois out. Francois and Punisher brawler on the outside while Simpkins and Lebec were throwing everything at each other in the ring. Simpkins was pulling Lebec into a brawl, and then, when Ms.Crystal had the referee distracted again, hoping to give her man a chance to cheat, Doug was quickest to the draw, pulling out a chain and levelling Lebec with it, gashing him open on the cheek. By this time, the Punisher had knocked Francois out cold, and entered the ring, immediately going for Lebec, dropping big elbow drops. Simpkins was furious, and even more so when the referee turned around again, saw
Punisher attacking Lebec, and thus awarded the match to Lebec by DQ. MR: 8 MD: 13.42 Lebec by DQ INTERNATIONAL WAR! TAG TEAM MATCH KALIFORNIA HEAT (4) Vs. PHANTASMAGORIA (10) This match was all Phantasmagoira`s. From the very beginning when they blacked out the entire arena, for the lights to come on revealing them to be assaulting Kalifornia Heat well before the bell to the final moments where
Kalifornia Heat were quite literally staggering around the ring from the level of power and devastation that they had encountered this day. The titles seemed to be going Phantasmagorias way, but in the fifteenth minute when Delane rolled out of the ring, Phantasmagoria had got into such a fury that they followed him to the outside and began smashing him up big time.
Powers tried coming to the rescue, but he suffered a similar fate. They had no hope against two such powerful opponents. On a day when Phantasmagoria should have won the International War! Tag team titles, the match ended in a double count-out and Kalifornia Heat being taken to casualty for wounds all over the body.
MR: 8 MD: 17.04 Double count-out UNIFIED TITLE MATCH SETH BLITZSPEAR (3) Vs. CHARLES MARTEL (9) Seth Blitzspear entered the ring with virtually no fight in him. Not even the crowd could get him going. Martel found this match to be a lot easier than he could have ever suspected, with Blitzspear hardly defending himself.
It was all very Lennox Lewis-Oliver McCall. Martel finished Seth off in the fourth minute with a reverse neck-breaker, followed by the Suicidal
Senton.
MR: 5 MD: 4.26 Martel by pinfall World War! Title Match LORD BYRON (8) Vs. T.J "THUNDER" DECAN (8) w/Flea Byron faced his biggest opponent yet. Decan had his biggest opportunity to win the World title, following his Undertaker-esque title “reign” in 1996.
It began with Decan pushing Byron around, and that became a brawl, which
Decan was more than winning. It was like throwing a rag-doll around. The world champ looked to be in desperate trouble as he got slammed around the ring, and found his body subject to many body-breakers and big-splashes,
elbow drops and leg-drops. He managed to hold on though, and with a fortunate bull-dog, managed to turn the tide of the match, getting Decan on the mat and keeping him there for the next several minutes. Decan looked as though he was on his way out. Flea got up and distracted the referee, Jamie
Cassidy, but this only allowed Byron to slip his chinlock into a chokehold.
Suddenly, Max Doom burst to ringside for the second time of the evening and pulled Flea off the ring-apron and whipped him hard into the iron-post.
Ground Zero emerged in response, and Max Doom, after a brief hit at Gordon, ran away. In the ring, Decan was getting a second wind, and Byron was in trouble, but when Decan whipped Byron to the ropes, he missed a clothesline. Byron jumped onto Decan`s back and slapped on a sleeper. Decan was affected immediately and, even though he tried to shake Byron off, he couldn`t. He
collapsed to one knee, then a second, and then he was out cold. Byron emerged victorious, with his foot on the prone Decan in the centre of the ring. MR: 8 MD: 16.51 Byron by submission. Bob: Wow, what a great evening. XS: It sure started off exciting enough. And the ever-growing rivalry between The AC and Ground Zero (or should I say GZ) is certainly causing a stir. GZ are definitely the stable at the moment, having effectively destroyed Seth Blitzspear. And Chris Deugau doesn`t like that, frustrated at his lack of gold in WAR!
Bob: He`s a young man who has done a lot and will do a lot more, but perhaps his responsibilities to the AC are more trouble than they are worth.
XS: Well, they make great bodyguards. The fact is, the quality is in
Ground Zero. Nobody would really call Kraken, Reign of Terror or The
Terminators a quality bunch of wrestlers. Noir, only an associate, has been
World War champ, but he is busy with Blake. Meanwhile, Flea has Bane, Decan and Gordon, all proven wrestlers.
Bob: And the Punisher gets his first big victory in WAR! over Kraken. XS: A big guy causing big problems for himself. Hey, listen to me punk, you don`t want to mess with Simon Lebec. He proved once he can whip your crummy dumb ass, and he is more than capable of doing it again.
Bob: I expect Doug Simpkins won`t be happy with The Punisher. XS: He cost Doug a victory. Punishers small minded vengeance has cost a great man a great victory. People talk about AC and GZ, but Simpkins-Blake-Martel (and Venom) is perhaps the highest ranking stable, with two out of the three champions. That is one team that I wouldn`t want after me. Now Punisher, I don`t think you have a choice. Bob: Martel won his first WAR! title today against a lacklusture Seth
Blitzspear. XS: He seems to have given up on everything, and I`m not suprised, he is such a loser. Bob: I think it is more to do with his good friend Jerry Gordon turning against him. That must be weighing heavily on his mind.
XS: His small mind. Bob: And Damien Legend looked good tonight, with a victory over the in-form
Nomad. XS: Not as impressive as the performance by Phantasmagoria over Kalifornia
Heat. How long before they hold a title.
Bob: It should have been theirs tonight, but their desire to hurt was just over-riding. But I hear they will be getting a rematch, probably next week.
McGriff will be wanting a rematch against Tyler Stevens. XS: A fluke victory! A total fluke victory. I still can`t believe he won. Bob: Big Duke`s guidance must be taking him to the right places. And
Xavier, we`re going to be going places now, because we`re out of air-time.
For Xavier Suave, I`m Avocado Bob Whitely wishing you all the best. We`ll be back with you this Tuesday when we return with WAR!ZONE. And we`ll also be back this Thursday with the second edition of WAR!CORE. See ya! © WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION
