WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

Monday Night WAR!fare

18 November 1996 (posted 7 December 1996)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera is outside a dark and forbidding building.... A sports arena by the looks of it. As it moves in through the entrance doors and down along a hallway ghostly, transparent images start to fade in and out.... WAR! wrestlers long gone from the federation, such as J.R. Death, Jean Van Basten, Brewster
Cogburn, Gary Trent and The Ghetto Boyz, The Spirit of 76, The Celtic
Connection, The Divine Alliance..... Slowly the images grow more solid as they get to wrestlers currently in WAR!.... Sid Starr. Booster Dunne and Randy
Blake. Bane. Tyler Stevens. The Lost Boys. Jim Bondo. The All-Americans. T.J.
"Thunder" Decan.... More and more wrestlers' images float past the camera as it comes to a pair of dark curtains..... and _bursts_ through them into the light of a filled up arena, moving down the aisle towards the ring. Kalifornia
Heat appear and disappear, wearing their International tag belts. Industrial
Revolution with the World WAR! tag belts. "Dirty" Doug Simpkins with the
Liberty belt. Mutilator with the Unified title. Biff McGriff with the International belt. Robski with the WAR!lord title.... Finally as the camera moves into the ring we notice a huge figure with his back to it. He turns around, revealing himself to be Tonnage holding the World WAR! Championship belt high above his head and with a big grin on his face.... Suddenly there is an explosion and the following logo appears in the middle of it.....
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- M * O * N * D * A * Y N * I * G * H * T WWWW WWWW AA RRRRRRRR !!!!!! WWWW WWWW AAAA RRRRRRRRRR !!!!!!!! WWWW WWWW AAAAAA RRRR RRRR !!!!!!!! WWWW WWWW AAAAAAAA RRRR RRRR !!!!!! WWWW WW WWWW AAAA AAAA RRRR RRRR !!!! WWWW WWWW WWWW AAAA AAAA RRRRRRRRRR !! WWWW WWWWWW WWWW AAAAAAAAAAAAAA RRRRRRRRR WWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA RRRR RRRR !!!! WWWWWW WWWWWW AAAA AAAA RRRR RRRR !!!!!! WWWW WWWW AAAA AAAA RRRR RRRR !!!! F * A * R * E ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The logo fades away and the camera sweeps around the arena, until it finally comes to a halt on our intrepid broadcast team. One of them is an attractive, young brunette wearing a white WAR! T-shirt, black jeans and a black WAR!
baseball cap. The other is a muscular, middle aged man who is shouting at the fans. He is wearing blue jeans, brown boots and a T-shirt with a camouflage pattern on it. He is also wearing his customary black cowboy hat.
[Susan] <shouting to be heard over the crowd> Good evening everyone, and welcome to the WAR!datorium and Monday Night WAAAAAAAR!fare!!!
<Big crowd pop> [Susan] I'm Susan Andrews and my irrepressible co-host is, as always the one and only...... [Terry] "TERRIBLE" TERRY JAMES! The man who could teach all of the snotnosed kids in this federation a thing or two about wrestling if it wasn't for the fact that
I've been retired goin' on 10 years now.
[Susan] Sure, Terry. Seems you're getting back in the business though... Color commentary here in WAR!... And, my sources tell me, managing in the GIWF.
[Terry] Hey, what can I say, toots? A man of my talents is always in demand. [Susan] Be that as it may, let's get on with the show, shall we? [Terry] Yeah. Let's. And what a show we have got for you people tonight! We have Little
Chill taking on the Raven.... Two relative rookies here in WAR!.... Gonna be a fast paced match though, I can tell ya that much considering how tiny these two are.
[Susan] Tiny? They're not that small. [Terry] Compared to Tonnage, Thunder and Mongol they _are_! [Susan] Point taken. We also have J.P. Steele making his debut against fellow WAR! newcomer, Chris Deugau. [Terry] I'm goin' with Chris in this one. Steele doesn't stand a chance against the
Annihilation Corporation's head man! Trust me!
[Susan] Booster Dunne makes his return, facing the... uhm..... unusual style of Suna
Gib. [Terry] I hope Dunne cripples the silverhaired freak. Anyway, we have more. One of the smallest guys in WAR!, 008 Jim Bondo, faces the heaviest guy in WAR!, Jumbo
Jim Mongol. I think we can safely say we're gonna see a flat secret agent after this match is finished.
[Susan] I think Bondo's gonna surprise you, Terry. [Terry] The only way he'd ever surprise me is if he got a brain. [Susan] The Lost Boys face The Cold North again. Maybe there'll actually be a match this time. [Terry] Trust me. The boys are pissed off since losing the belts to the Industrial
Revolution freaks. They are gonna tear through the Old South like a hot knife through melted butter.
[Susan] Their name is Cold North. [Terry] Whatever. [Susan] And our new International WAR! Tag Team Champions, Kalifornia Heat, will face the Annihilators. This match has been changed from a normal one to a title match in light of the Heat aquiring the belts after the match was announced.
[Terry] New champions coming up in other words. Last time these guys met the Heat got
_lucky_! [Susan] I disagree, but I guess that's no surprize. We also have the All-Americans taking on our violent neighbours from north of the border, Canadian Carnage.
[Terry] Now, this just ain't fair. I like both these teams... So why do they have to go and fight? I won't know who to cheer for!
[Susan] <Sarcastically> Oh, I feel for you, really. Poor guy. [Terry] Don't you get uppity with me, young lady. Anyways, moving on we have Tonnage and Thunder meeting for the World belt again. Third time in a row. Ya know, I am beginning to suspect a conspiracy here. Someone in the head office is
_trying_ to make Armageddon tear itself apart! Well, it won't happen! [Susan] And finally..... The big match. Now, Robski has been promising hell and high water if the match is going ahead. WAR!'s head office has already recieved numerous messages from his solicitors. However, the fact remains, the match
_will_ go ahead. Robski and Big Duke WILL face eachother in a Loser Leaves
WAR! match. As a matter of fact, we have Vice President Eric "Bullfrog" Helmroy standing by to explain the situation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The camera cuts to the office of Eric Helmroy. Bullfrog is sitting behind his desk looking more than a little haggard from a lack of sleep. He looks up at the camera and sighs.
[Bullfrog] Okay, okay. I'm gonna make it short and sweet, because frankly I'm dead tired of this mess. I've been on the phone 24/7 since the lineup for this card was first announced. Now, Robski claims to never having signed the Loser Leaves match. However, WAR! does have a signed copy of the very same contract, there is no denying that. Now, even so, I might have been willing to discuss the matter with Robski if it wasn't for one fact. The match was already announced to happen on WAR!fare, which meant WrestleVision could bump up the pricerate for advertising during the show as the interest in the match would generate higher viewer ratings. This they did.
Bullfrog sighs. [Bullfrog] Now, when I recently announced the match was being changed to a normal match again I immediately got several phonecalls from irate WrestleVision executives.
WAR!'s contract with WrestleVision stipulates that we are to notify them of such changes well in advance, so as not to cause them any loss in advertising revenue. Due to this WAR! has seen no other way out than to demand the match go ahead as announced, or lose our contract with WrestleVision, as well as several other prominent advertisers. Bottom line, Robski and Duke WILL fight and the loser WILL leave WAR!
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The camera cuts back to Susan and Terry. [Terry] WOW! Bullfrog laying down the law... Who'd have ever thunk it? [Susan] Certainly not anyone who knows him at all, but we have a show to do, so let's get on with it and move to our first match. Little Chill vs. Raven. [Terry] I like both these guys, so why are they fighting eachother? Huh? I'll tell ya why! It's a conspiracy by the corrupt suits in the head office! That's what it is! They're screwing the good wrestlers over by making them fight eachother and thereby staying low in the rankings. And at the same time they're pandering to the whims of no-talent idiots like Seth Blitzspear and American Patriot!
[Susan] Uh... Riiiiiiight.... Sure... Whatever you say, Terry. Our ring announcer is standing by at ringside.
[Ring Announcer] This matchup is for one fall and a twenty minute time limit. Making his way down the aisle first, hailing from New York, New York...
[Terry] <singing> I'll make a brand new start of it, in old New Yooooooork..... [Susan] Oh, be quiet! [Ring Announcer] .... Weighing in at 211 lbs and standing 5'11" tall.... RAAAAAAAAAVENNNNNNN!!! Raven starts making his way to the ring, with the fans booing him all the way. He has red hair and silver eyes. He wears black trenchcoat, leather pants, a black bandana, and black gloves. A scar in the shape of a feather runs from right above his right eye down to just beside his top lip in the. He also has black leather boots, carries a rapier strapped to his back and a raven is sitting on his shoulder.
[Terry] Now, personally I don't much go for theatrics like swords and birds and contacts to change my eye color... Me, I always just relied on the good old thumb to the eye and my reputation as a guy who broke bones to scare my opponents.... But I gotta admit, this kid _is_ a bit spooky, and he certainly has the right attitude for the business.... WHOAAAA!
Suddenly a man comes running out from behind the curtains and attacks Raven from behind, sending him headfirst into the guardrails, then bulldogging him on the concrete. The man stands up and grins at the camera, showing himself to be Little Chill, then starts dragging the dazed Raven to the ring, making sure to toss the rapier back towards the entrance where a security guard picks it up.
[Terry] AND WE'RE OFF WITH A BANG TONIGHT! Raven may have the attitude for the business, but so does this guy! Little Chill is _IN_ the building!
[Susan] And Chill taking the time to ram Raven's face into the guardrail a couple of times more before pulling him into the ring. Looks like he's planning to end this one quickly. Shaz has also arrived at ringside now. The odds definitely look stacked against Raven tonight. Chill pulls him to his feet... SWINGING
NECKBREAKER!.... He pulls him up again... Going for a suplex... NO! SMALL
PACKAGE BY RAVEN! ONE! TWO! Kickout by Little Chill.
[Terry] That was a desperation move by Raven. He needs to take a breather and regroup. He needs to stop Chill's momentum, or this is gonna be a real bad night for the Raven. [Susan] Well, it doesn't look like Chill is willing to give him that breather. Chill is stomping away on the prone Raven now! Raven up to his knees.... Headbutt to the gut gives him time to get up to a vertical base. Raven with a punch!
Chill with a punch! Raven! Chill! Raven! Chill! Chill! Chill! And a BIG uppercut by Little Chill sends Raven reeling! Spinning kick by Chill and Raven is down again!
[Terry] I guess we know who the better brawler of these to is. Raven back on his feet,
Chill with a clothesline... DUCKED!
[Susan] Raven off the ropes... Chill hits the ropes.... AND RAVEN WITH A HIGH CROSS
BODY BLOCK SENDS BOTH MEN SPILLING TO THE OUTSIDE! Both men are down on the outside!
[Terry] Raven is up first though..... Picks up Chill and sends him into the steel cornerpost! WHOA! GO SHAZ!
[Susan] Shaz just leapt onto Raven's back! Raven is chuckling and shrugs her off... But that has given Chill the time to get his bearings! Raven turns back to him, and is met with a fist to the face! HEADBUTT BY CHILL! INVERTED ATOMIC DROP!
[Terry] Chill back into the ring to break the count! And back out again, Raven meets him with a clothesline! And he pulls Chill into the ring with him again. Raven off the ropes... FLYING FOREARM floors Chill..... Raven to the top.....
MOONSAULT! COVER! ONE! TWO! Foot on the ropes! [Susan] Raven picks Chill up... DDT! Cover! One! Two! Kickout! And Raven is starting to look frustrated! He stomps on Chill and goes to the top again!
[Terry] This is a mistake... I just know it's a mistake!.... [Susan] Raven going for the moonsault again..... He SOARS through the air.... OOOOH! [Terry] LITTLE CHILL PULLS HIS KNEES UP AT THE LAST MOMENT, AND RAVEN IS IN _PAIN_! [Susan] He hit the knees hard there, and got the wind knocked out of him! Chill to his feet now.... pulls Raven up.... Suplex coming up I think..... NO! BRAINBUSTER!
Pulls him up again.... Raven is struggling too much for Chill to do whatever it is he is planning to do... OOOH! A blow below the belt stops Raven from struggling!... REVERSE NECKBREAKER!.... Chill is working over that neck of
Raven's!
[Terry] Well, that's only natural... Softens him up for the Little Death. [Susan] And I think that's what we're gonna see now.... Chill has Raven in position.... AND HE HITS IT! The "Little Death" spining piledriver! Cover, and one! Two! THREE! Little Chill gets the victory through the use of some questionable tactics. [Terry] It ain't cheating if ya don't get caught. [Ring Announcer] The winner of this match, from New York City.... LITTLE... CHILL! "King of New York" by Fun Loving Criminals start up as Chill smirks and then makes his way to the back with Shaz, accompanied by the constant jeers and boos from the crowd. A couple of WAR! paramedics come out and help a groggy Raven to the back shortly after.
*************************************************** Match Result: Little Chill defeats Raven by pinfall Match Time: 7.56 *************************************************** [Susan] Okay. Our next match features two newcomers to WAR!. Chris Deugau who has made a name for himself elsewhere as the head man of the Annihilation Corporation, and "Jackhammer" J.P. Steele, who some claim to be one of the hottest rookies in the sport.
[Terry] Doesn't matter what the morons claim, because Chris will tear him apart. Steele may be a "hot rookie", but Deugau is as smart as they come! He'll make this kid wish he never stepped into a wrestling ring!
[Ring Announcer] This match is scheduled for one fall and a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, from Detroit.... Being led to the ring by his manager, Annie
Mack.... Weighing in at 294 lbs, measuring 6'9" tall ..... JACKHAMMER....
JAAAAY.....PEEEEEEEE.... STEEEEEEEEEEELE! Bigtime pop for this young fan favourite as he makes his way to ringside, slapping hands and interacting with the fans all the way. Steele is young man with very well defined muscles, blond hair and blue eyes.
[Ring Announcer] And his opponent.... Hailing from Nanaimo, BC, Canada.... Being led to the ring by _his_ manager, Sunny B..... Weighing in at 223 lbs and standing 6'2"....
Here is the master of the Annihilation Corporation...... CHRIS.... DEUGAAAAAAUUUUUUU! Chris and Sunny make their way down the aisle to the boos of the fans (well, some of the fans anyway. Most of the male fans near the aisle are busy making catcalls and whistling at Sunny instead). Chris is wearing a blue singlet with
AC DEUGAU written in black on the back, black boots that have AC on one side, and DEUGAU on the other. He is also carrying a sports bag to ringside, and has a confident smirk on his face.
[Susan] I don't like that smirk... He's up to something, I just know it. [Terry] Nonsense. He just knows he's a better wrestler than Steele. [Susan] Oh yeah? If he's not up to something, then why the bag? [Terry] That's probably just his clothes and stuff... Would you leave your stuff unattended in the dressing rooms? These punks working for WAR! would steal everything if he did and sell it as "Chris Deugau" souvenirs.
[Susan] Sure, and Brutus Noir never cheats. Deugau stepping into the ring
