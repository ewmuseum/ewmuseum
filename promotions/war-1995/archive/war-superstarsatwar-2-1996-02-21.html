~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Liberty Championship Match SID "STRAY CAT" STARR Vs. 008 JIM BONDO Throughout the match, Big Duke was at ringside. Just watching. Sid is escorted to the ring by the woman seen earlier in the show. Sid & Jim face off, then the bell rings. The female, however, is practically dying to get into the ring, screaming for Bondo to give her an autograph. The wrestlers stop to allow the woman in. She produces a pen and paper from her cleavage and asks Bondo for an autograph. Bondo obliges, but while doing so, is suddenly given a neckbreaker by Starr, then a DDT. The woman puts in a few kicks for good measure then leaves. Jim is down, and Starr capitalizes with a piledriver. Starr then climbs to the top rope and flies down with leg drop. He ascends again. Connects with a flying fist. Bondo is severely dazed. Starr whips Bondo into the corner and rushes in with a Stinger Splash. Bondo reacts though and catches Starr in a head scissors and twists Starr over. Starr gets up and staggers about.
Bondo climbs to the top turnbuckle and successfully flying clotheslines Starr. Covers. Starr kicks out. The match hits a balance here for the next couple of minutes, but then suddenly Starr manages to Bulldog Bondo. Starr covers Bondo. 1....2....Bondo kicks out. Starr goes to pick up Bondo, but Bondo grabs Starr by the hair and over into a small-package. Starr struggles to kick out, but fails. 1...2...3. Jim Bondo defeats Sid Starr, and the crowd break into a chorus of 0-0-8, 0-0-8, 0-0-8.
Rick: 008008008? Is that a sexline or something? Bob: I wouldn't know. I thought you were familiar with them all. Rick: Very NOT funny. Bob: You started it. Anyhow, Bondo is the new Liberty Champion. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR!KING TOURNEY ROUND 2 JASON THE TERRIBLE Vs. "SUPERSTAR" GOLDEN PAGE Page was almost a foot smaller than his "Terrible" opponent, but he was always one step ahead. He kept Jason down on the mat and worked on his knees. When Jason tried to get up, he couldn't walk properly.
Page showed ruthlessness and kicked the bigman down again. Page slapped on a figure-four and got the victory at the nine-minute mark.
Match Rating: * * * BIG DUKE w/ King Benny Vs. "THE AMERICAN" BUTCH WILLIAMS w/Lady Liberty Williams walked down the aisle with the help of new valet Lady Liberty. Clearly not match fit, he turned up anyhow. Lady Liberty was drapped in a robe like the statue of liberty, and held a flaming torch in her hand.
With the match underway, Duke had the early press on Williams. When Williams got his first wind though, he punished Duke with some shocking clotheslines. Duke retaliated with a big foot to the face that sent Williams out of the ring. Duke followed and they brawled outside for a bit. Duke slammed Williams hard onto the metal steps and was about to get back in the ring when he was tapped on the shoulder by Lady Liberty. Big Duke turned round and was subsequently roasted by a massive jet of flames by firebreathing Lady Liberty.
She helped Butch back into the ring and the bell went. The match was awarded to Big Duke due to a DQ on Butch Williams.
MR: * * * BANE Vs. "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY DREAMER During the match, Mr.Money and The Rich Boys turned up at ringside. Meanwhile, in the match, Bane was pounding Dreamer into the mat. Outside the ring, he smashed Dreamer with chairs.
Bane could have left Dreamer outside, but he brought him back into the ring and pinned him with one finger. Or at least, he thought he had. Dreamer grabbed Bane by the arm and twisted it into a fullchicken wing. Bane was screaming but wouldn't submit. He broke
out of the hold the only way he knew how - he powered out. He clothesline Dreamer halfway across the ring and then finished it with "Bane's Death Drop". It was the end of a dream. Bane progresses to the next round. After the match, this happened.

STEIN "ICE" BERG Vs. BOOSTER DUNNE Pre-match interview: The camera is on Toxic Shock who is standing next to the huge Stein "Ice" Berg in the back area of the arena. We can see the other WAR!FORCE members in the background, seemingly discussing ring strategies and such. Berg looks as emotionless and cold as ever, with his cold, grey eyes staring at the camera. He is 7'2" and around 430 pounds with the build of a bodybuilder. He seems even more defined since he went through Blitzspear's training program. His white hair is cut in a short, military crewcut style, and across his huge chest the word ICECOLD is tattoed in big, black gothic letters. He is wearing his white wrestling shorts and boots.
[Toxic] Okay, Ice. Coming up any minute now is your match against a veteran here in WAR! Booster Dunne. What are your thoughts about this match?
Berg stares at Toxic Shock for a second and then speaks. [Berg] Dunne....You too are a part of the cancer which threatens to destroy the sport of wrestling. You too have often cheated to win. You too must pay for your actions, like the Horsemen and anyone else who decides to take the easy way to victory.

It was so amazingly clean, and the tide of the battle switched back and forth. that Ice had his foot on the ropes. A great match nontetheless, and the two wrestlers shook hands after the match. The fans applauded emphatically.. MR: * * * * * "THE ECONOMIST" RANDY BLAKE Vs.
