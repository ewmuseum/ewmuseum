~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! Wrestling Federation presents WAR!REPORT VII LIVE FROM THE WAR!DATORIUM With Your Hosts Avocado Bob & Dr.Nathaniel Darkness ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Avocado Bob: Welcome one, welcome all, ladies and gentlemen to War!Report 7, live, as usual, from the War!Datorium. We have a spectacular card tonight, with a very different type of match for the International War Tag Team Titles.
Originally scheduled was a six man rumble, but this was deemed unfair at the last minute by General Andropov, the federation head. As a consequence, the match will now be DangerZone vs. Demolizer of D&D and Shaft of Team Youngblood.
Dr.Darkness: An easy win for DangerZone surely. Shaft & Demolizer just don`t get on. Furthermore, their abilities don`t compliment each other, not that
Shaft has any ability. DangerZone are exactly that, a danger zone, and this kooky match is going to prove it.
Bob: If it does turn out in victory for Demolizer & Shaft, their respective tag-team partners will join them in a tagmatch later this evening to determine the champions. However, with respect to these fine teams, all this is minor news in comparisson to the events of the past and the events of the future.
Last week, at War!Zone VI, you all saw the devastating attack by Inner City
Firmer on The Maltese Falcon,where the ICF superplexed The Maltese Falcon from the top of the cage out onto the crowd. Fortunately, the crowd broke the two wrestlers fall, and their injuries were not so bad that they can`t wrestle tonight, though I do believe they are not at one hundred percent fitness.
However, this event had sad ramifications. A young boy agaed seven, called
Brandon Potts, was fatally injured when crushed by the near 275 lb Inner City
Firmer. The doctors say that he will never walk again, and the chances are, we`re going to get the pants sued off us. The matter has been reported to the authorities and War! is now under investigation concerning it`s....rampant activities.
Doc: chances are they`ll unvcover the tax-fiddling as well... Bob: Shhhh...you`re not supposed to mention that. What I am supposed to mention however is that "The Amercian" Butch Williams situation is becoming blurred. Andropov`s door hasn`t exactly been giving way from the protests against his departure, but the influence of his half-brother Seth BLitzspear, the World War champion, is certainly holding weight, and Andropov is considering letting Butch Williams back
Doc: Can`t that nimwit stick to his guns? If he lets Williams back in then no contract will hold weight in War! ever again.
Bob: Looking at it from Andropov`s angle, "The American" Butch Williams did manage to pull the crowds in. He was the American the Americans love to hate, and I hear rumours that SethBlitzspear wants him in his Casualties of War!
team. And speaking of the casualties of War, the news so far is that one team has been confirmed so far. Headed by The Maltese Falcon, the team will consist also of Johnny Thunder, Ultimate Wrestling Machine & Dr.Schock. I believe they`ll be facing a team headed by Bombsquad. Other team captains that have been announced are Seth Blitzspear, who will be facing a team led by
XL-9. Furthermore, Brutus Noir & Inner City Firmer will rekindle their vendetta against each other by each leading a team against each other.
Brewster Cogburn shall also be captaining a team against "The Economist" Randy
Blake. Doc: And I`ve got some news, although as yet unconfirmed. My sources say expect a Casualties of War match pitting 4 tag teams against 4, most likely Divine Alliance, Light & Darkness, Team YoungBlood & TerrorGang against DangerZone, Canadian Carnage, BloodStreetBrothers and StormLords.
Bob: Well, thanks for the news. But I`ve got some more hard hitting news. Since Butch Williams is out of War! for the forseeable future, General Andropov has granted a match between whom he deems to be the two highest contenders for the International War! Title....Brewster Cogburn and Bombsquad.
Doc: That`ll be a great match to watch, and I`m gonig for Bombsquad. He`s got a big man in his corner in the shape of Bomber, and that is always, and I always mean always, a big advantage.
Bob: Well, don`t write off the man I consider a legend, Brewster Cogburn. He`s got experience enough to take him a long way. The big question now is whether the debuting TagTeam High Voltage have got the experience to better
Terrorgang.
Doc: And the big answer is no. Both teams are bums, but TerrorGang have cooler names so I`m gonig for them.
Bob: And one final thing. General Andropov will be announcing tonight which wrestler from WAR! will be battling for the FWA/WAR Title. Let`s get this card underway....

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Freebird & Julia Vs. Clan Demon It seems so long since ClanDemon were world champions. However, Freebird & Julia later gain control of the match using fluent tags and a wide variety of moves. It proves successful enough, and Freebird manages to lock on the "Winged Spike" piledriver onto the turnbuckle onto DeadPunk. A pinfall victory for Freebird and Julia at 13.02
