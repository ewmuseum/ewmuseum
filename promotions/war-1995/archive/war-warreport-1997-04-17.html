WAR! Wrestling Federation — News

WAR!REPORT

17 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It`s Wednesday Night... It`s 9 o`clock.... It must be time for <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> THE WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION WAR! REPORT on April 17 From WAR! HQ, Telford, England With TOXIC SHOCK <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Welcome one and welcome all to WAR!REPORT. My name is Toxic Shock, as you well know, and I`m here to bring you up to date on all the latest news in WAR!
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> NEW FORMAT - FAILING RATINGS? <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Yes, WAR! has a new format, but it doesn`t look as though it is being very popular so far. Networks are complaining that the 7 hours it takes just for one show to be broadcast just isn`t worth it. I think in future we`ll be seeing a much more highlights orientated package as
WAR! execs try and work out their current troubles. However, there will still be WAR! action, everyday of the week, Monday to Friday.
Monday & Tuesday sees WAR!FARE & WAR!TIME take place, as WAR! stars take their action away from the cameras and to the enclosed viewing of the War!Domes around the country. However, tapings will take place, though it is now only going to be highlights of matches rather than
the total package. Wednesday brings you to my little room in WAR! HQ for another round up of the action, and Thursday the wrestlers of WAR! take place in some interesting events on the WAR! Unlimited programme. Lined up for this week is the superstars of WAR! taking on wrestlers from the WWF & WCW, which should be very entertaining. Then on Friday it is time for WAR!ZONE with Bullfrog & Avocado talking you through ten great matches.
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> TYLER TURNS, MIDNIGHT RE-TURNS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Well, we all saw the terrible events that took place at Casualties of War 3, and also the great events. Jason MidNight made a glorious return, as did Butch Williams, who had been training Seth, and they teamed with Seth against Ground Zero. However, Tyler Stevens showed his treacherous streak again, and turned against Seth to team with
Ground Zero. It seems he had us conned all the time folks. However, with MidNight & Williams back in the fray, and apparently siding with
Seth, things are looking up for the WAR! legend. <><><><><><><><><><><><><> UNIFIED TITLE NOW DEFUNCT <><><><><><><><><><><><><> With the departure of Charles Martel for WAR!, the Unified Title, a title which has been rumoured to be scrapped anyway, has finally been withdrawn from service. However, the legacy of WXW shall not die, as there are plans underway for a tournament celebrating the now defunct fed. Look out for it at an upcoming WAR! Unlimited card. However,
Chris Martels departure raises questions over the survival of the
Empire Club. Can it survive with just Blake & Simpkins?
<><><><><><><><><><> THE END OF THE LINE? <><><><><><><><><><> Well, after nearly creating a formidable opposition to the AC & GZ with THE LINE, Max Doom screws it all up by being an asshole to Eric
Helmroy, and promptly gets thrown out of WAR! Add to that the departure of Cemetary Beast, and it really has become the end of The
Line. Does this mean that there is nothing left to stop Ground Zero rampaging through WAR? Well, with MidNight back, it could be time for WAR!FORCE to re-establish itself. I can picture it now - Blitzspear, MidNight, Williams, Patriot & Bondo. That could be the only thing to stop the dark hordes of WAR!
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> THAT JUAN KERR CAN FUR-COUGH <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Fur-King does it again. Nobody thought he could improve upon his victory over DeathBringer, but when he beat Lord Byron at WAR 2 in 1, he may just have done it once again. A truly incredible victory for the man everyone used to laugh at. Now he is just laughing...and coughing...at them.
<><><><><><><><><><> THE PAIN OF DU PAIN <><><><><><><><><><> Noir continues to torment the former International & Liberty champ, 008 Jim Bondo. Ever since he dumped bread on Bondo to remind him of how his parents died, it`s been total war between these two. Now a special “Giant Bread Roll” match has been signed for this weeks
WAR!ZONE, where you can win the match only by stuffing the opponent into a speically cooked 7ft long, 4ft high french stick.
<><><><><><><><><> WAR!STARS FINISHES <><><><><><><><><> At the fabulous War!Stars II, Dirty Doug Simpkins prevailed to prove that he is the cream of the under 275lb crop. At War!Stars III, Decan established himself as the behemoth of WAR!, although Frank `The Tank’
Abbrams may have something to say about that. At 650lb, he is a stern test for anyone. However, since he is using Tonnage`s music, and basically being a clone of Tonnage, the close friend to the once WAR!
legend, Magnum Biff McGriff will not take kindly to him. Although Magnum is arguably the strongest in WAR!, and also the World War Champ, he did not have a successful War!Stars evening, losing to Punisher & Decan, although he made up for it by successfully defending his title against Punisher at 2in1.
<><><><><><> DEPARTURES <><><><><><> Those who couldn`t cope with the intensity of WAR!, or left for some other reason... StAraNg Dan Caldwell Simon Lebec Cematery Beast Patty Babes Carter <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> SOMEBODY SEWN UP THEIR MOUTHS? <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> We haven`t heard for a long time from Doug Simpkins, Raven, Cradle of Filth to name but a few! Have they sent WAR! to coventry? If anyone has heard from them, let WAR!REPORT know.
<><><><><><><><> INTERFED TIME <><><><><><><><> Well, it seems as though WAR! is going to be involved in a three way interfed card with the AWA & TOWL within a matter of weeks.
Negotiations are still underway. However, RAW will not be participating because `Bullfrog’ Eric Helmroy claims the wrestlers there are `even more inferior than those in TOWL and AWA - and Tox, that is sure saying something’.
<><><><><><><><> WAR! UNLIMITED <><><><><><><><> Tomorrow we get to see WAR! take on some of the best that the World has to offer. Look at this for a line-up...
Seth Blitzspear Vs. The Ultimate Warrior Chris Deugau Vs. Ric Flair Tyler Stevens Vs. Shawn Michaels The Lost Boys Vs. The Nasty Boys Kalifornia Heat Vs. Harlem Heat And a huge 10 a side Survivor series match involving ten of War`s best against ten of the World`s best.
<><><><><><><><><><><><> WAR!ZONE - This Friday <><><><><><><><><><><><> WORLD WAR! TITLE MATCH - TRIANGLE MATCH - ELIMINATION STYLE As a result of Casualties of War III, there has to be a solution to the confusion that was caused, so now Magnum Biff McGriff, the champion, defends his title against Lord Byorn and Bane in a triangle match. To win, he will have to beat both of them, and that is not going to be an easy task.
GIANT FRENCH BREADSTICK MATCH Possibly the most bizarre match in WAR! to date, at the perverse request of Vice-President Helmroy, is the match between 008 Jim Bondo
& Brutus Noir, where to win, you have to stuff your opponent in a giant french breadstick roll. Claims that Helmroy wanted it to have a stipulation that the loser was smeared with butter and eaten were rejected by both wrestlers...
HANDICAP MATCH #1 Arron Iron stands alone and squares off against the savagery of Windy City Warriors. It`s all a matter of family honor, but do the Todd brothers have any? And what of Nomad? By saving Wendy Addams from an attack by his GZ team-mates, where does he fit into this?
TAG - TEAM CAGE MATCH The return of Butch Williams to help his brother sets up the match perfectly against the War!Lord and Liberty champs, Decan and Stevens.
6 MAN TAG MATCH Kings Justice make their six man tag debut facing Blake, Simpkins and Nomad. I QUIT MATCH The British Empire have returned to WAR! ring action andthey must face Kalifornia Heat in a match the Heat have wanted for a long long time. HANDICAP MATCH #2 650lb Tank Abrams faces Rude Ross Royale & The Craggy Island Warriors in a 3 on 1 handicap match.
INTERNATIONAL WAR NON-TITLE MATCH Chris Deugau puts his belt on the line against Sid Starr in this non-title encounter. WAR!LORD TITLE MATCH Decan fights for the second time this evening, against “The Jamaican Superstar”, in a rematch of their 2in1 encounter this week. Also... THE TOXIC PIT with... FLEA, on the future of Ground Zero Ms.PAULA on the future of the Empire Club and JASON MIDNIGHT on why he returned and possibly more. Any wrestlers wanting an interview at War!Zone need to send a transcript of their conversation with me, Toxic Shock, to war!@ usa.net
I repeat war!@ usa.net <><><><><><><><> AND FINALLY... <><><><><><><><> After an intense amount of work updating everything behind the scenes, WAR! is now back on schedule. That`s all from me. Remember, if there are any wrestlers watching who want to tell Mr.Helmroy about any plans they may have for WAR!ZONE, you can drop him a line at war!@ usa.net
