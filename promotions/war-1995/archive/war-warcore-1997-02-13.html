WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WAR!CORE

13 February 1997

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION proudly presents From the Philadelphia WAR!DOME on 1997 February 13 WAR!CORE With your usual hosts, “Avocado” Bob Whitely & Xavier Suave ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome one and welcome all to a fantastic WAR!CORE show that will be headlined by “Dirty” Doug Simpkins making yet another Liberty title defense against “The HeartBreak Kid” Tyler Stevens.
XS: Don`t worry about that match. Tonight we get to see the debut of yet another AC member, “Masked Maniac” Jarred Tokarek.
Bob: I don`t mean to sound rude, but the match that appeals to me most tonight is Jerry Gordon going up against The Raven. The question must be, what is Raven going to against The Punisher this Friday at Skirmish. The guy is so big!
XS: He`ll manage against the big oaf. By the way, isn`t it time we went to Toxic Shock for the special WAR!STARS TRILOGY REPORT. Bob: Yup. Over to you Tox. Tox: Hello. My name is Toxic Shock, bringing you news of the WAR! STARS TRILOGY, from the WAR! Event centre. Changes have occured since the last time you heard about the WAR! STARS trilogy. It was considerd a big much to force wrestlers to fight 4 matches each in one night, so instead, there had been a change of format. At each of the evenings, there will be six competitors - that is six tag teams at “A New Rope”, six under 275lb guys at
“The AC strikes back” and six over 275lb guys at “Return of the Hero”
The six will be split into two sections of three. They will fight the other two in their group. The winners of each group, that evening, will face each other in the final of that evening, to determine the overall winner.
The groupings are as follows: Feb 22 - A New Rope (writer is Jon at [detail withheld] ) Group 1 All-Americans Windy City Warriors Cradle of Filth Group 2 Elemental Force Phantasmagoria Kalifornia Heat March 1 - The AC Strikes back? - Writer is [name withheld] (at
[detail withheld]) Group 1 Doug Simpkins Lord Byron 008 Jim Bondo Group 2 Tyler Stevens Charles Martel Chris Deugau March 8 - Return of the Hero? - Unconfirmed Group 1 Seth Blitzspear DeathBringer “Thunder” Decan Bane Biff McGriff American Patriot Tox: Anyhow, that`s the lineup. I believe now we are going to the action on display tonight.
Dark Matches: “Masked Maniac” Jarred Tokarek annihilated Architisman in the third minute, first with a choke slam and then a series of kidney punches.
Canadian Carnage disposed of the ever-worsening British Empire. Despite having Carnage in pinnable situations several times, British Empire refused to pin. they couldn`t get Carnage to submit even slightly, and eventually
Carnage, through Lafleur pinning Lord Savage, won the match Joe Hardcore and Sid “Hulk” Starr squared off. The match revealed above all the growing tension between Starr & Mastermind. It was in a moment of arguing with his manager that Starr lost the match, getting rolled up by
Hardcore in a match that Starr should have won. Nomad beat Kraken, the Greatest Hunter, after making him submit to a figure four leglock. CHAOS KNIGHTS (7) w/Mordred Vs. WINDY CITY WARRIORS (8) w/Nomad This was a knock-down, drag-out match in which it looked like the Windy City Warriors were going to actually be outbrawled. However, some clever passing of brass-knuckles by Nomad to Anthony Todd in the twelth minute, and their subsequent use of Mayhem behind the referees back managed to give them the victory.
MR: 7 MD: 13.07 WCW by pinfall “SHOWSTOPPER” SIMON LEBEC w/ Ms.Crystal & Francois (8) Vs. “MAGNUM” BIFF MCGRIFF w/Felicia (8) Magnum knew that Lebec couldn`t handle a bit of brawling, and with McGriff being the best that there is, he looked well on his way to victory.
However, with the aid of Francois interfering, Lebec got into the match and began to wrestle McGriff down on the mat. McGriff wasn`t totally helpless in this situation, and the match was flowing back and forth, and a lot of time was spent seeing Magnum power out of tight holds. Late in the match,
Magnum was complaining angrily to the ref about what he thought was a slow count. Showstopper got up and took the opportunity to execute the Blackball
- a thrust kick to the back of the head. The force of the kick sent Magnum hard over the top rope, and he was out cold, giving Lebec a count-out victory. After the match, as McGriff staggered down the aisle with Felicia, Arron Iron showed up and a staredown ensued. Mcgriff stared hard back at Iron and then walked past him, glaring.
MR: 8 MD: 18.50 Lebec by countout CHRIS DEUGAU w/Sunny B. (10) Vs. DAMIEN LEGEND (6) Deugau wrestled a near perfect match, keeping the big man, Damien Legend, down on the mat, and basically wearing his knees down all throughout the
match, finally finishing him off the figure four leg-lock. However, at the end of the match while Deugau was celebrating to the crowd, Arron Iron burst into the ring with a chair and levelled Deugau with it. He continued to do so, and then the AC emerged, quickly followed by Iron`s associates. A big brawl, as is tradition, took place, and ended with WAR! security having to get involved and threatening people with guns. Honest!
MR: 8 MD: 7.31 Deugau by submission RAVEN (-) Vs, JERRY GORDON (-) Gordon grabbed the mic as Raven came towards the ring and said... "Raven, I'd like to apologize for taking your sword. I know it means a lot to you and it was wrong of me and Flea to take it. No hard feelings"
Gordon extended his had to shake hands. Raven looked around, suspiciously, and as he did, Gordon blindsided him and grabbed the Katana again, and stormed away from the ring. Raven followed, but Bane, Decan and Nomad
formed a wall as Gordon made his escape. Gordon got hold of Tox just before he left the arena... Gordon- ”Get over here Shock. Raven nobody threatens Mr. Flea, myself or
Ground Zero. You see this. I gave it back but that wasn't enough. Now your little sword belongs to Ground Zero.”
Gordon then shoved Tox and made his way out of the arena. MR: - MD: - No contest INTERNATIONAL WAR! TITLE MATCH 008 JIM BONDO (-) Vs. SETH BLITZSPEAR (-) Seth walked sluggishly to the ring to face Jim Bondo, but once in the ring he just looked at Bondo, shrugged his shoulders and left the ring, getting
counted-out as a result. MR: - MD: 0.32 Bondo by countout WORLD WAR! TAG TITLE MATCH LOST BOYS w/Wendy Addams (7) Vs. PHANTASMAGORIA (8) A tough, physical match for both teams. Phantasmagoria set the pace, slamming Lost Boys all around. The action frequently spilled to the outside as both teams tried to get the better of the other, but it was Lost Boys who
were without a doubt in trouble this evening. To compensate for being so outsized, Lost Boys turned to cheating, but it backfired on them just as the match was swinging their way. Greg whipped Darkness to the ropes and Frank pulled the rope down. Darkness went tumbling over, the ref saw, and the
Lost Boys got DQ`d.
MR: 7 MD: 14.23 Phantasmagoria by DQ LIBERTY TITLE MATCH “DIRTY” DOUG SIMPKINS (8) Vs. “THE HEARTBREAK KID” TYLER STEVENS w/Big Duke (9) Doug was wrestling really tough with Stevens and was punishing him write from the start with suplexes all around the ring, and some deadly submission holds. Stevens didn`t give in though and found his way back into the match by poking “Dirty” Doug Simkins eye. That opened the way for Stevens to mount an intensive assault against Simpkins, with Doug getting back into the match only briefly on a few occassions during the next ten minutes. Several times Stevens should have won the match, but Simpkins continually kicked
out. Then Doug found his way back into the match with a backdrop that sent Stevens to the outside. Time was getting near the thirty minute mark. Stevens re-entered the ring and immediately Doug was onto him but Stevens blocked a suplex attempt by Doug and with his last remaining bit of strength reversed it into a cradleplex. Doug kicked out. But the move had taken a
lot out of him as well. Stevens was flat, out cold. Doug bounced off the ropes, going for a big elbow drop, and at the last moment, Stevens moved. He quickly covered Doug, hooking the tights to make sure he couldn`t escape, and he didn`t. Doug Simpkins was pinned and Tyler Stevens became a three time Liberty champion.
MR: 9 MD: 28.04 Tyler Stevens by pinfall
