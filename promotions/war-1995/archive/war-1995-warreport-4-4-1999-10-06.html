GF: Let`s get to the action. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> DAIN JERRUS vs. 'HARDCORE KID' RICO ZANDERS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> If you wanted to talk about mediocre matches barely worth talking about, then talk about this one. It really was a contender for non-event of the year, simply because the crowd did not care one bit. Who the hell is Rico Zanders, they were thinking. Dain Jerrus is almost as low in profile, but some people had heard of him - and hadn`t thought much of him. The effort put in by either man into this fight was approaching the zero mark, and neither seemed to care. Both are supposed to be tough, hardcore-style brawlers, but nothing of the sort even happened in what was a depressingly sluggish encounter that lasted for too long, and whose only high-point was when Jerrus executed his 'Snapper' neck-breaker to get the 1-2-3. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 'BRUTAL' BRIAN FLOYD Vs. THE GOLDEN BOY <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> With one depressing match down, the War! executives were praying for some injection of life or excitement into the night. The Golden Boy, a flamboyant personality, and Brian Floyd, rather less so, both got a few cheers and boos respectively, but it did nothing to fuel a match devoid of any life. Golden Boy was expected to be the better man, with it being his debut, and Floyd, having heard his contract was to be moved to the SWA, was expected to put up a tough fight, but neither tried, and all that was left was a tedious match of over-long chinlocks, arm-bars, abdominal stretches and basic brawling. Neither man was really getting the edge, and when the match went to the outside thanks to Brian Floyd, the half-hearted brawl resulted in a double count-out, and both men could be presented as nothing but losers. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> ELIMINATION MATCH CHURCH OF FAT (REVERAND DOCTOR WILLIAM BENNETT & 'ICEMAN' JEFF WESTFALL) Vs. Highlights of the match include Nobbs countering Gordon`s 'END' DDT, and Gor don ramming Nobbs head repeatedly into an exposed turnbuckle.
