A Valentines Day Card for everyone. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION lovingly presents LIVE from the WAR!DATORIUM SUPERSTARS AT WAR! No one could be that much of an asshole by chance. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR!KING 32 man tourney prelim round STEIN "ICE" BERG w/J.North Vs. MASKED WARRIOR w/The Dark One At 6`2", 230lbs, Masked Warrior was a good deal smaller than his seven foot opponent. Ice seemed quite tense throughout the match, and tactics seemed to go out of the window at one point, when it just turned into a massive brawl. Ice kept Warrior under his fist for much of the time, but The Dark One attacked James North outside the ring. This briefly made Ice let his guard down, allowing the Masked
Warrior to attempt a roll-up. Ice had too much strength and kicked out. Masked Warrior went for another pin, and held Ice's tights this time. He almost got the three count, but a recovered James
North gave the referee help by pointing out the cheating. Warrior protested his innocence to the ref, but forgot about Ice, who quickly executed the "Icecold" chokeslam/Running splash combo. He got the 3 at 7.41
Match Rating: * * * * DRAGON MASTER Vs. CROC MASTER Dragon Master failed to fulfil his promise of bringing a live Dragon to the ring to eat Croc's 8ft crocodile, saying that the dragon had heartburn. CrocMaster used his four inch height advantage well, and proved stronger than DragonMaster. Croc tried to the keep the match as a brawl, Dragon Master tried to respond equally. The match wasn't pretty, and it spilled outside. Croc tried to use a chair, Dragon blocked. Dragon tried to get back in the ring, Croc pulled him out.
He got the chair again and smashed it into the knee of DragonMaster. DragonMaster fell, CrocMaster got back in the ring and won by countout Match Rating: * * 008 JIM BONDO Vs. THE BOUNTY HUNTER Before the match, The Bounnty Hunter had these words to say: (Standing in a dark ally way) At Valentines Day Violence, 008, you will be the first one to feel the wraith of the Bounty Hunter here in WAR. Then whoever advances in the tournament to face me, will feel the terror that The Bounty Hunter carries, everywhere I travel. By the end of the tournament,I will stand alone in the ring after getting by 5 superstars. 008 , prepare to be eliminated.
The Bounty Hunter entered the ring to jeers of "Bubba Fat" because at 6`3" and 321lbs, he is almost as portly as Johnny Banger/SSBK. Bondo wouldn't give in to Bounty Hunter's brawling ways, and kept the match high paced, high impact, stunning Bounty Hunter near the end with several "Magnum" dropkicks. Bondo's victory came in the sixth minute after reversing a DDT attempt and executing the DDT on Bunty Hunter. Bondo progresses. For England.
Match Rating: * * "HOLLYWOOD" JOHNNY DREAMER Vs. TONNAGE Not even in a dream could such a Hollywood style victory occur. If Dreamer wanted to make a name for himself, he couldn't have done it in any more of a spectacular fashion. Tonnage really wanted to use his mass against Dreamer, but the hi-flying wrestler just wouldn't sit still. Dreamer was an irresistible force this night. Tonnage was the immovable object. Something had to give. It was Tonnage.
Throughout the entire match, he lumbered around, and even got a few bone-crunching moves on Dreamer. Dreamer ducked out of an Avalanche attempt by Tonnage though, and from there on, the match was his. He gouged Tonnages eyes, ascended to the top rope and executed a flying Bulldog. Tonnage got up, angry, and charged towards
Drreamer. Dreamer was quick enough to dodge, and his manager Victory Gold, pulled down the ropes sending Tonnage crashing over. Dreamer scaled to the top again, and came down with a flying knee onto
Tonnage's face. He scampered back in and won by countout in the fourth minute. The crowd didn't just go wild, they went primal.
Match Rating: * * * OG GARY TRENT Vs. "THE ECONOMIST" RANDY BLAKE Two seasoned WAR! veterans met. Trent the much junior, with everything to look forward to. Blake, experienced in wrestling and at holding titles. They'd met before. Blake had won then and he won here too. He outskilled Trent with all the locks under the sun. He slowly wore Trent down, and although Trent had learnt a lot since he first came to WAR!, he hadn't learned enough to beat Randy Blake. A tough first round for Trent, and it proved costly. Blake progressed to the second round following his "Recession" special move in the
13th minute. Match ranking: * * * JOHNNY "THE STAR SPANGLED BUTT KICKER" BANGER Vs. BOOSTER DUNNE Booster Dunne was a good wrestler before he teamed up with Blake. Now with months of intense, yet economical training under him, the dividends are showing. A good wrestler has become a very good wrestler. Hints of Dunnes teacher showed as he wrapped the match up as quickly as possible. Banger himself is improving in WAR!, but his failiure to prevent Dunne from reversing several moves, including the
"StarSmasher" cost him the match. Dunne by submission following "The Booston Crab". Match Rating: * * * CLASSIC MAN w/ Psycho"B" Vs. BANE It was another high-flyer Vs. powerwrestler match up. ClassicMan wasn't as lucky as Johnny Dreamer was earlier in the evening. Classic Man was demolished. Nothing he could do seemed to be able to budge Bane. Bane let Classicman have his fun, and then slammed him deep into the mat. ClassicMan actually went through the mat and
the boards underneath. The crowd were shocked. But they weren't suprised when Bane pinned ClassicMan just seconds later. Following this match, ClassicMan quit WAR!
MATCH RATING * * "THE AMERICAN" BUTCH WILLIAMS Vs MYSTERY MAYHEM w/ "Legend" Josh Brown Mystery Mayhem attacked Williams on his way to the ring. The two wrestlers brawled for a bit, then Mayhem, using his strength and height advantage, pressed Butch above his head and slammed him into the crowd. Some fans got hurt. Butch got furious and got to work on Mayhem.
Eventually the match went into the ring. Mayhem had the larger share of match domination, and almost got Butch with the Death attack, but the fans started their "U.S.A" chant. Butch responded and slammed
Mayhem. He followed through with a splash, but Mayhem moved. Mayhem scooped Butch up, tried to slam him, but Butch held on and pulled
Mayhem over into a small package. Butch got the three count - just. Match Rating: * * * SETH BLITZSPEAR w/Pat Mills Vs. "PRETTY BOY" JOHN STALLION Seth got back into the victory mood - but not easily. John Stallion, the 6`6" wrestler from Atlanta, GA was a formidable opponent.
At one point, Stallion had Seth in an excruciating surfboard. The crowd got behind Seth though, and with their help, he made a strong comeback, getting a countout victory following his "Flyingtime"
backdrop. Match Rating: * * * "MR.EXCITEMENT" DROPKICK Vs. JASON THE TERRIBLE Jason was far from terrible. The Moscovite crushed the hi-flying "Mr.Excitement" in the first minute of the match with a powerful clothesline and then executed a piledriver. Then it was time for the
Crucifier special move, and the match was all over. Jason the Terrible enters the next round. Match rating: * * (NICE MR.) NEXUS Vs. "THE FRENCH FURY" JEAN DE MORT w/ The Preacher Nexus was far from nice in this match. De Mort, after calling Nexus a poor excuse for a thespian, opened the bout with a series of textbook armdrag takedown, into armbars. Nexus didn't give in, and De Mort decided to move onto something else. De Mort tried to do a DDT, but Nexus slipped out and threw the frenchman into the referee. The referee was knocked out, so Nexus pulled out some brass knuckles and knocked de Mort to the ground. Then he showed a particularly sadistic streak and pounded De Mort until the Preacher was able to make the ref wake up. De Mort, knocked out, would have been pinned, but The Preacher "convinced" the ref that Nexus had been up to no good. The ref DQ'd Nexus. Following the match, de Mort needed 53 stitches in total for three major lacerations; on the lip down to the chin, across his forehead and one under his right eye.
PURPLE HAZE w/Dude Vs. RICK SHOWBOAT w/Mr.Money The match was fairly exciting. Neither wrestler could be said to be running the match, at least until the sixth minute when Haze caught
Showboat with a titl-a-whirl-back breaker. Haze tried to apply pressure, but Mr.Money distracted Haze. Showboat kneed Haze out of the ring, and the two wrestlers brawled incessently back to the dressing room, resulting in a double count-out.
Match rating: * * * "GANGSTA POWER" SABIN FIGARO Vs. THE HITMAN The Hitman's feud with 008 Jim Bondo had clearly affected him. Throughout the contest, he seemed nervy, looking over his shoulder as if expecting 008 to turn up. He didn't, but Sabin Figaro should have been enough of a distraction. The Hitman paid the price for being elsewhere in his mind, and Sabin Figaro's BumRush in the eigth minute staggered Hitman out of the ring and back to the changing rooms, dazed. He was counted out.
Match Rating: * * THE AMERICAN PATRIOT Vs. BRUTUS "THE SIDEWINDER" NOIR Brutus Noir, one of the first World War champions, was already known to hate The Patriot simply for being American. The Frenchman from the Marseille docks turned his hatred into violence. Already recognised as the most sadistic, vicious brawler in WAR!, and exceptionally strong for his size, Noir didn't just take the match to the American Patriot, he rammed it down his throat. Patriot seemed suprised that one man could be so violent. 90% of this short match happened outside the ring, and involved steel railings, TV monitors, chairs, steps and ringposts. Noir was possessed and obsessed.
Patriot, by no means, wanted to lay down and die, but Brutus was relentless. Victory came when he was passed some barb-wire by a member of the crowd and tied a dazed AmericanPatriot to the railings with it by the neck. Brutus got back in the ring and won by countout. The American Patriot suffered lacerations to the neck.
TYLER STEVENS w/ Peppy Vs. BIG DUKE w/King Benny II Stevens is getting quite a reputation. Partly because of Peppy, partly because of his courage when facing the Horsemen.
The match was fairly average, with Big Duke getting a nearpin with a powerslam, and Stevens with a crossbodyblock.
Then, in the tenth minute, Big Duke was looking agitated. After tossing Stevens out of the ring, he started talking to King Benny II. Duke was angry that Benny was paying Peppy so much attention and not sticking to their gameplan. Stevens got back in behind Duke and executed a "rude-awakening" neckbreaker. He covered Duke, Duke managed to get his hands on the ropes. Stevens thought he had a chance and bounced off the ropes towards Duke. Duke caught Stevens with a hiptoss and threw him out of the ring and onto the guard railings and a soundman called Ray. Stevens was counted out at the 13minute mark.
