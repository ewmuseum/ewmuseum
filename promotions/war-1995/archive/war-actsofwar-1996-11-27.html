I discovered later, was that Noir had been assaulted yet again by Raven. The
AC came to his rescue, but by the time they had, Noir had suffered big blows to his skull, and was knocked out, in no shape to wrestle.
[MR: - MD: - Result: Blake by technical submission.] In a match that totally revealed the sadistic streak of DEATHBRINGER (9),
JERRY “GOON” GORDON (5) was able to put up little resistance. “Goon” clearly has talent, but was totally overpowered from the word go by DeathBringer. It was a chilling performance from DeathBringer, and when he finished Goon off with not one, but three “Burials”, it was clear he meant business. It may have simply been a reaction to his dismal performance against Nemesis, or it may have been his true self showing, but quite simply, he was a force that could not be stopped in this match. When he applied a move he hasn`t done for 4 years - “The Cremation” - a top rope face-first piledriver - a deadly message was sent out to all in WAR!, and quite possibly, this is the man that will replace Tonnage.
[MR: 7 MD: 10.00 Result: DeathBringer by pinfall] Up next were THE CHAOS KNIGHTS (8) facing INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION (7). It was a fairly close match, but the Chaos Knights had the better of it. Disorder finished Hakeem off with a samoan drop.
