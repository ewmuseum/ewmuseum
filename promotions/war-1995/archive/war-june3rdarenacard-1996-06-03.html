June 3rd Arena Card Results & News

For you guys unfamiliar with this, War! doesn`t just put out big televised cards, it also does arena cards (whenever I or the other fedheads feel like doing one). This basically helps push the results situation along.

(This won`t have everything, but it should be a fairly comprehensive round-up of goings on in War!) The mergeover has happened as WXW finally joined WAR! bringing some of its brightest talent. War! is, however, still in debt.
Other great talent recently joined War! is The Jammer, a legendary highflyer, even at his young age and surely a contender for the liberty title. Sinder Blokk is a large man, joining T.J.Thunder &
Colossus as big men challenging for Tonnages top gold. The other big men are back in action too. Bane & Big Duke are pounding away their opponents.
Tonnage is currently working through his contenders one by one. First up is Booster Dunne, the International War! champion, and the only man to have pinned Tonnage recently. Dunne himself is on a bit of a roll since winning the International War! title, his only defeat coming at MAIM `96 against hated opponent Lord Byron. Still, Dunne
Vs. Tonnage should be a great match when it happens. Byron, Butch Williams and Brutus Noir are embroiled in a three-way feud, but Noir is still more concerned with Johnny "The Sidewinder"
Missile, who continues to use his nickname. Seth Blitzspear is still celebrating after winning the "Maimer of Sheep" trophy at MAIM `96, but the return of his half-brother, "The American" Butch Williams seems to have knocked the title challenge wind right out of his sails...
Recent departures...Spike Allen, CrocMaster, Stein Berg, Sid Starr. OG Gary Trent won the Liberty Title off Tyler Stevens recently, after Tyler Stevens had held it for only one match. Tyler Stevens has made more enemies that people can count, and has brought in the massive 7`3" Venom as a bodyguard. Tyler has been making a nuisance of himself in many recent matches since turning away from the fans. He is now one of the most hated men in War!
Biff McGriff is feuding with T.J.Thunder, doing his job as member of SuperStable Armageddon by diverting Thunder away from Tonnage. Thunder is as big as Tonnage, and when they meet, the ring will need reinforcing.. Where has the Cardinals Acolytes gone? With them he was unbeatable, but now he seems to be on a losing streak.
Lost Boyz are the tagteam champions, but Stone Age, Ghetto Boyz and Theatre of Pain are hot on their tails. StormStalker has entered War! and along with The Jammer, could make it very difficult for OG Gary Trent to hold onto his title. Rumour is that Vice-President "Bullfrog" Eric Helmroy is trying to get these two signed up to face each other...
DeathJester is helping the police with their enquiries concenring the death of a young girl, the death of an interviewer and the attempted murder of Tyler Stevens.
The Reckoner is just about back to 100% after he receiver a severe beating off SuperStar Golden Page recently.
Suna Gib is back in action following the vicious attack by Biff McGriff & The Agonizers. Colt McGraw is still in hospital following Gib`s flamethrower attack, and General Leo is upholding his 30-day banishment. Sabin Figaro is taking it a little bit easier now that
General Leo is gone, but Tyler Stevens remains a concern The American Patriot won back the WAR/RAW Interfed title Stables aren`t what they used to be. Staticians have proved that compared with this time last yeat, there is less involvement from stables of wrestlers. Only Armageddon are vaguely working as a unit while the Rich Boys are called by some analysts as "The most disorganised, fractured, fragmented group I have ever laid eyes on".
The Clique, The BrotherHood and War!Force are effectively non-existent. Coming soon are some great great cards from brilliant new cofedheads [name withheld] & [name withheld]. [name withheld] returns in August.
