Arena Card — 29 October 1996

29 October 1996

DOMINATION (7) Vs. KALIFORNIA HEAT (8) Mr.Psycho counted out after Delane slipped back inside after a brawl outside the ring. MD: 9.41 MR: 7 THE BOUNTY HUNTER (6) Vs. NEMESIS (8) Nemesis pins Bounty Hunter by countout after executing a shooting star press from the top turnbuckle down on to Hunter who was outside the ring. MD: 6.37 MR: 7 (but the move was 10) RAVEN (7) Vs. MONGOL (7) Raven pins Mongol after avoiding a Mongosault MD: 7.34 MR: 7 COLD NORTH (8) Vs. ALL AMERICANS (7) Brunswick makes Frost submit to the All-American Jam cobra-clutch MD: 14.26 MR: 7 JERRY "GOON" GORDON (8) Vs. NOMAD (7) Gordon gets Nomad following a DDT MD: 13.55 MR: 8 MR.FUNK (7) Vs. HELEL (9) Helel pins Funk following a belly to belly suplex MD: 9.07 MR: 8 APOCALYPSE (6) Vs. CANADIAN CARNAGE (6) Ended in a double coutout as the team bralwed outside the ring MD: 2.15 MR: 4 "JAMAICAN SUPERSTAR" ARRON IRON (6) Vs. TYLER STEVENS (8) Stevens finishes Iron off with the HeartBreaker. MD: 6.52 MR: 6 MUTILATOR (6) Vs. 008 JIM BONDO (9) Mutilator, despite being outwrestled, plants Bondo with the Mutilator Bomb after Bondo missed a bodysplash MD: 11.43 MR: 7 JASON MIDNIGHT (8) Vs. RANDY BLAKE (8) The crafty veteran made MidNight submit to a full-nelson MD: 24.26 MR: 8 THEATRE OF PAIN (6) Vs. AGONIZERS (7) Grappler gets Vanquish with a small package MD: 4.52 MR: 5 SID "STRAYCAT" STARR (8) Vs. "DIRTY" DOUG SIMPKINS (7) Simpkins DQ`d for use of brass-knuckles MD: 15.27 MR: 7 T.J "THUNDER" DECAN (9) Vs. BIG DUKE (8) Decan gets Big DUke with the Thunder Jam MD: 8.46 MR: 8 ANNHILATORS (6) Vs. CHAOS KNIGHTS (8) Annhilators run ended as the Chaos Knights finish Kanemoto off with the Chaos Wastes. MD: 16.25 MR: 8 WAR!LORD TITLE MATCH ROBSKI (5) Vs. THE AMERICAN PATRIOT (9) Patriot completely routed Robski, causing him to run away and lose the match by countout. MD: 3.26 MR: 8 (Impressive seeing Patriot manhandle Robski) LIBERTY NON-TITLE MATCH STALKER (9) VS. "CANDYMAN" ANTHONY TODD (6) Todd replaced Damien Legend at the last minute following Legend`s departure. However, he couldn`t stop the Stalker or "The Suicide Slice" flying kneedrop to the throat. Stalkers match. MD: 7.48 MR: 7 LOST BOYS (8) Vs. INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION (10) Industrial Revolution win the tag-titles following a Mega Knife Powerbomb by Mega Daddy Kewl on Lost Boy 2. Highlights of this viscious, blood drawing match in an upcoming televised card. MD: 26.37 MR: 9 SETH BLITZSPEAR (6), SABIN FIGARO (6) & 008 JIM BONDO (7) Vs. TONNAGE (8), BANE (8) & "MAGNUM" BIFF McGRIFF (8) The match ended in a double DQ as a massive brawl broke out following interference intitially by Domination against Armageddon, then the rest of Armageddon came out. This prompted the NDA to come out, drawing out Raven who was followed by Mongol who was followed by Nemesis and then Industrial Revolution & Cold North appeared. Kalifornia Heat & All-Americans followed in response. Robski appeared to beat up Figaro, but American Patriot turned up. The ring, at the end, had collapsed.
