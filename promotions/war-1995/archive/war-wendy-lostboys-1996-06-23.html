WAR! Wrestling Federation — Vignette

Wendy and The Lost Boys

23 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Wendy and The Lost Boys are standing outside WAR! HQ in front of the free standing WAR! Wrestling logo** [Wendy] Theater of Pain. Total Destruction. WHOEVER the hell you are. You stole our belts. [LB1] With the help of Jammer and Eve. [LB2] And Arron Iron. [Wendy] It took SIX of you to get the job done. [LB1] And if your a QUARTER of the champions we where, you'll give us a rematch. [LB2] But THIS time, how about just you and us. [Wendy] Last time Arron Iron BROKE into our HOME, like a COMMON THIEF. [LB2] He stole some things that where my PERSONAL property. Things I kept seperate from my wrestling. [LB1] Then Jammer and Eve threatened the deface then DESTROY my partners PERSONAL property. [LB2] So I was distracted during the title match. [Wendy] But now we've gotten REPLACEMENTS for what was stolen. And Two is FOCUSED. [LB2] Are you REAL champions? [LB1] Or are you going to be satisfied with winning 6 on 3? [Wendy] We are calling on WAR! Vice President Helmroy to not only mandate a rematch, but to also DENOUNCE the actions of Arron Iron, and take the appropriate measures to insure that petty thievery will NOT be tolerated in WAR! [LB1] We know some of the fans of WAR! may not like us. But even the cheering section for Theater of Pain must DEMAND that the new champions win the titles in an HONORABLE way. [LB2] WITHOUT the help of Arron Iron! [LB1] Jammer! [Wendy] And that little B!T(#, Eve. **Fade Out** ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [name withheld] Fat, Bald, and Confused! "Push the button Max!" Prof. Fate "All them BIG words make my head HURT!!" Lost Boy Two Former World WAR! Tag Team Champion
