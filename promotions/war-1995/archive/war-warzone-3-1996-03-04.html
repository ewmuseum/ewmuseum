WarZone III

"Whale Mail" Mike's.... WAR!ZONE 3 Jerry "Goon" Gordan and Sid Starr battling toe to toe Tonnage all scared and bloodied The Destruction Duo marching down the aisle Nice Mr. Nexus coughing Randy Blake with a Recession Lady Liberty with a fire ball Goth Boy doing his freaky dance down to the ring THIS IS WAR! ____________________________________________________________ WAR!Zone 3 Live from WAR!ena with Xavier Suave and special guest Jerry "Goon" Gordan ____________________________________________________________ XS-Welcome fans to this weeks big WAR!Zone. Lots of big news this week, starting with the pleasant news of a week off from Sir John A. MacDonald
IV who has decided to concentrate on his teams match. With me is Jerry
Gordan. Welcome Jerry.
JG-Thanks Xavier. It's great to be here. I just want to remind the fans that I'm a wrestler and not an announcer so they'll have to bear with me.
XS-Trust me Jerry, that won't be a problem after having MacDonald around. You'll probably be a relief for them. Well on to the other big news, a sister for WAR! as RAW is starting up. But the bigger news is all six belts on the line tonite. First time in WAR! history.
JG-Should be a great nite. I can't wait, only wish I was out there myself. XS-Well, let's get the show started. Superstar Golden Page vs. Johnny Banger Banger came out strong and fought well in this even match. However he would make one of the cardinal mistakes in wrestling as he dipped his head too soon when planning a back body drop. Page wrapped him up in the small package for the victory at 7:22.
Rating-6.5/10 WAR!Lord Title Match Big Duke vs. Croc Master Croc master came out strong trying to stake his claim to WAR!'s newest title. The fans were warm to the Duke but not totally convinced about his sincerity. After the Croc's early flurish and many near pins, 4 to be exact. The Duke came back and started to mount a comeback. In the
12th minute the intensity picked up and the battle spilled outside where the two were counted out at 12:17.
Rating 7/10 Moo Kame vs. Dragon Master The Lonesome Lion finally got over the stigma of the LFL title and defeated the Dragon Master in 46 seconds. He connected with a brutal savate kick and then a flying drop kick and gained the nod and the loss of LFL title which now rests on the shoulders of the Dragon Master.
XS-Up next one of the big matches of the night. International Tag Team championship match between the Ghetto Boyz and the Aderondacs.
JG-Aderondacs thought they had it rough last week. Well they haven't seen anything compared to what the Boyz are are gonna do.
XS-Lots of intensity here. Aderondacs with their recent attitude change and the lust for the gold and Ghetto Boyz with revenge on their minds with their former friends.
WAR! International Tag Team Ghetto Boyz vs. Aderondac Brothers People expected a pier 6 brawl and they got it. Blood, chairs, foreign objects, tables...everything but the kitchen sink and if the battle went much longer most wouldn't of been surprised to see it come out. After 10 minutes of non-stop, non-regulated action...
XS-This is brutal. Outside the ring, Big Daddy Vicelord and Bladerunner going toe to toe. JG-The ref lost control of this. Someone is gonna get hurt soon. XS-Ben Bach to the apron on nails Folk Curtis Fredrick with a chair. Bombsquad now picks him up and puts a big powerbomb on him. And now another...he should go for the pin.
JG-No he's going out and helps Bladerunner with Vicelord. The Aderondacs are pummeling Big Daddy.
crowd errupts XS-OG to the ring. Bach scurries to the otherside. OG with a chair unseen by the Aderondacs and connects on both of them. Ben Bach sneaks up behind Trent.
JG-He saw him!!! Give it to him Gary. XS-Trent with a hold on Dr. Death. Big smile on his face. Oh no...Bladerunner with a chair. Starts beating on Trent now. Ghetto Boyz still down. Bach also getting some licks in.
JG-That's enough of this. XS-Jerry Gordan leaving the booth to goto ringside. Sabin Figaro also coming from the dressing room. Gordan and Bombsquad going at it. Figaro chases off Bach and Bladerunner. Bombsquad decides enough is enough and he beats a hasty retreat as well. Ghetto Boyz and Trent seem ok. Fans errupting as Street Level and Jerry Gordan make their way back to the dressing room. Guess Jerry will be joining me again soon.
Rating-9/10 Well fans coming up next is of course the big non-interference World WAR! championship match between Biff McGriff and Stein Berg. Here are some comments now from the big Ice man.
The camera is in locker area. Standing in front of the camera is the enormous form of Stein "Ice" Berg. He is 7'2" and 430 pounds of muscle.
He has a massive bodybuilder's physique, and he is wearing a pair of white wrestling shorts and white wrestling boots. His white hair is cut in a short military crewcut style, and across his massive chest the word
ICECOLD is tattooed in black, gothic letters. Around him are the other members of WAR!FORCE, giving him advice and and wishing him good luck in the fight. Berg simply ignores all the commotion around him, standing as still as a statue with his arms crossed over his chest. He stares at the camera with that cold, emotionless look, and then speaks.
[Berg] McGriff. You constantly talk. You talk about how the horsemen will break everyone. You talk about how we are all doomed. You talk of how you will destroy anyone who stands against you.
There's a long pause with everyone around him waiting for his next words. When he doesn't immediately continue, but just stands there like a statue again some of them start to look a little confused, then Berg speaks again.
[Berg] McGriff. You talk too much. With that simple statement he turns away from the camera and heads off towards the aisle to the ring.
XS-Wow. He's ready tonite. World WAR! Title Stein Berg vs. Biff McGriff Announcer-Making his way to the ring. With his manger Colt McGraw. Weighing 357 pounds. From Venice Beach, California. Here is "Magnum" Biff McGriff. Crowd boos loudly. McGriff and McGraw just laugh and taunt the fans at ringside even more.
Announcer-From Bergen, Norway. Crowd errupts Announcer-Weighing in at 430 pounds. He is the World WAR! champion. He is Stein "Ice" Berg!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
XS-Back with me is Jerry Gordan. Wow what a pop. JG-Definetly and he deserves it. XS-No emotions on the big man. How are Trent and the Boyz? JG-No problems with them. I think the only one tonite who's gonna have a problem is Mr. McGriff.
XS-Berg all business as he just lays the belt on the timekeeper's table and then makes his way to the ring. McGriff jumps him as he makes his way in!!!!!
JG-Go figure. Doesn't seem to be affecting Ice though. XS-You're right. McGriff keeps whaling on him but no affect at all. JG-Berg finally nails Biff. What a forearm. XS-Berg throws McGriff to the ropes. Catches him with a sidearm slam
(fed head note-like what Big Bossman/Bubba Rogers does...can't remember the name). Berg grabs the leg. He did it.
JG-McGraw can't believe it. McGriff and him are leaving. XS-Crowd is going crazy but Stein Berg seems oblivious to it. He takes his belt and is going to the back.
JG-It's his style. Nothing wrong with it Rating-4/10 Brutus Judd vs Bounty Hunter Judd made short work of the Bounty Hunter. Judd started strong but the
Bounty Hunter with a rake of the eyes put together a few moves. The end came while he was still in control. Bounty Hunter tried for a suplex on
Judd. Judd then blocked it and connected with a suplex of his own. He followed it up with the Death Penalty piledriver to get the pin at 6:33.
Rating 6/10 Goth Boy vs. Masked Warrior Masked Warrior came out with his Theatre of Pain friends. Goth Boy then followed and danced his freaky Goth dance on his way to the ring. The
Masked Warrior was killing himself laughing at this and that led to his down fall. Goth Boy kept dancing his way up to the top rope and then flew at the laughing masked man. He connected with a cross body block and pinned him at 6 seconds. Theatre of Pain were not laughing after that and then proceeded to beat on Goth Boy until WAR! officials came out to break it up
Rating-3/10 OG Gary Trent and Sabin Figaro vs. Agonizers The hostility between Figaro and General Leo was apparent as they went right after each other. Trent and Figaro held the upper hand for the beginning of the match but they didn't have the energy to keep it going after the earlier going ons. The Agonizers took control in the 8th minute for good and wore down a tired OG Gary Trent. Trent submitted at
12:48 to Matt Grappler's Royal Octopus Hold. As they left Figaro and Leo went at it again all the way back to the dressing room where they were finally separated.
Rating-6.5/10 Aaron Iron vs. Randy Blake Another 2 wrestlers with a past. Blake though seemed to be in a different world tonite as Iron dominated the match. Ms. Paula looked on in horror as Iron flew circles around Blake. Enough was enough for Paula though as she tripped The Jamacian Superstar up as he ran to the ropes.
The ref caught it out of the corner of his eye and disqualified the former World champion at 6:02.
Rating 7/10 The Reckoner vs. 006 Mitchell Thorpe The oft injured Thorpe reminded the fans that the layoffs didn't cause him to forget how to wrestle. He wore down the bigger Reckoner constantly working on his legs. He gained a victory by countout, at 8:55 when the Reckoner rolled out to regroup but then couldn't get back in because his legs were hurting too much. Doctors later revealed that the
Reckoner would miss the next card due to the injuries.
Rating 5/10 Liberty Title 008Jim Bondo vs. Hollywood Johnny Dreamer Bondo was in total control tonite as he put down Dreamer in a few short minutes. Bondo dominated the man from Hollywood and pinned him after a dropkick from the top rope at 4:23.
Rating-5/10 XS-Well Jerry, quite a night so far and still four big matches to go. JG-That's right 2 title matches and of course the big Lights Out handicap match. XS-Up next is the World WAR! Tag Team match between Seth Blitzspear and
Butch Williams vs. Canadian Carnage. Rick Sole is back stage with Sir
John A. MacDonald IV and the Carnage
RS-Well John, what do you think of the match tonite? Mac-I can't believe it. Where does this team of rejects get off getting a title shot. I know there are no teams out there worthy of a shot when compared to the Carnage but I don't understand this.
RS-Either do I, but what are your plans? Mac-Simple, total anilation of these two. Canuck-That's right baby. The 10,000 day reign of Carnage is running wild in WAR! and it ain't stopping for this fool Ditzspear or the man we really want a hold of, Butch Williams. You been flapping your mouth about the wrong country Williams and now your gonna pay you Star Spangled
Sissy.
Mountie II-Williams...your mine!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! RS-Back to you losers in the booth XS-Wow, I don't think I've seen that much emotion of the the Mountie II ever. JG-I can understand it though. Williams and his comments about Canada are way out of line.
World WAR! Tag Team Title Canadian Carnage vs. Butch Williams and Seth Blitzspear The Carnage made their way to the ring first and were met with their usual bad response from the fans. Then the Destruction Duo came out.
They took the microphone and wondered why they weren't getting the shot espicially after winning by DQ over the Carnage earlier in the week.
MacDonald IV then claimed that the Destruction Duo were probably never gonna see a title shot after trying to show them up tonite. Timebomb then claimed that they were 10 times any team in WAR! including Williams and Blitzspear. Williams then stormed from the dressing room and took exception to this. Seth followed afterward trying to not let anything happen. Williams and Justifier started shoving and then Timebomb and
Seth started at it too. Sir John and the Carnage quickly left while the 4 heavy weights went at it. Eventually WAR! officials came in and broke it up.
XS-What a turn of events that was as the Williams-Clique rift grows larger and larger. JG-I don't get Williams but I'm not gonna get into it. XS-I don't blame ya. Star Smashers vs Rockards In a preview of the proposed Iron Man Challenge between the Rockards and the Star Smashers these 2 teams went at it. The Rockard controlled the tempo for the initial stage of the match. After about 6 minutes though the Smashers started coming back strong. Apollo Friday connected with a powerslam on Chuck to get the pin at 8:32.
Rating-6/10 International Title Booster Dunne vs. Rumblefish Randy Blake joined Ms. Paula at ringside in order to try and abort any
Horsemen interference. Rumblefish started out in control. The match then went back and forth for 15 minutes. Rumblefish then attempted the
Rumbleation moonsault. Dunne moved though and then rolled Rumblefish up for the pin at 18:41.
Rating-8.5/10 XS-Well here is the main event. Lights out Steel Cage handicap match between Heartbreak Kid, Tyler Stevens, American Patriot and Bane.
JG-Bane is a big and impressive individual but trying to take on two people like Stevens and Patriot is stupid.
XS-Well we'll soon find out. Back to Rick Sole in the back with Tyler
Stevens. RS-Thanks loser. Stevens how come your just getting here.? HBK-Someone ran me off the road on the way here. Last week I thought was just a fluke but this is too wierd.
RS-Come on Stevens. We know you can't drive. Why can't you admit it? HBK-Watch it Sole or I'll... Bullfrog Helmroy comes out Bfrog-Or you'll what Stevens? Stevens mutters something and storms out RS-Back to you people XS-Well something might be up. Too much seems to be happening to Stevens. JG-I know that feeling. He shouldn't worry too much though. He's got a lot people to watch his back.
Lights Out Steel Cage handicap match Bane vs. American Patriot and Tyler Stevens Stevens and Patriot stormed the ring taking it right to Bane. Bane takes the abuse though and begins to fight back by throwing the Patriot into the cage. Then him and Stevens square off going blow for blow. Bane throws Stevens to the rope and went for a clothesline. Stevens ducked and then connected with a Superkick staggering Bane. Stevens then ripped the cover off thhe turnbuckle. Patriot slowly starts to rise. Bane proceeds to attack Stevens as he rips the turnbuckle off. Patriot goes over and then attakcs Bane. Bane just smiles and bangs the two faces'
heads together knocking them out. Bane picked up Stevens and powerbombs him 3 times. The Patriot though played coy and after the last powerbomb exploded off the ropes to deliver the Patriot missle. He then repeatidly slammed Bane's head into uncovered turnbuckle. The big man Bane would not recover from that and the ref declared the Patriot as the winner.
Tonnage then came out to the ring but when an unlikely group of wrestlers including Brutus Noir, Randy Blake and various members of the Clique and
WAR!Force, he decided that now would not be the right time
Rating-7/10 XS-Well fans, a great card with some great results. Thanks Jerry for filling in. JG-Anytime Xavier. Was a pleasure XS-For Rick Sole, Jerry Gordan, I'm Xavier Suave. Good night and may the good news be yours.
