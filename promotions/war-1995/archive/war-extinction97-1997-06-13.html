The camera cuts to the very familiar Toxic Shock standing in his native habitat.... The set of the Toxic Pit. The pit itself is boiling and bubbling as violently as ever, filled with the usual disgusting, slimy goop we all have gotten used to by now. As always a greenish fog obscures large parts of the set.
[Tox] Hey people! ARE WE HAVING FUN YET??? Big pop from the crowd. [Tox] Oh.. COME on! You guys can do better than that! I said ARE WE HAVING FUN YET? The current, and two time Liberty Champion... SID... STARR!!! There's a loud "MEOW" over the PA system, then Stray Cat Strut starts up and Sid walks down towards the Toxic Pit set. Sid is a handsome, young man, probably somewhere in his mid-twenties, with short, blonde hair and an athletic build. He is wearing basic black wrestling tights, black boots with "STARR" scripted down the sides in silver and black and silver wristbands. He is also wearing a black leather jacket with "Baby, I'm a STARR" scripted across the back with silvercolored metal studs. As he struts his way over to the set he soaks in the booing coming from most of the crowd.
