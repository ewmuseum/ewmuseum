Lost Boys Developments

28 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**The Lost Boys pull up to "Little Jumbo's Gym and Seafood Restaurant" in One's '71 Chevy Malibu SS. They see Wendy setting in her Lincoln. As they get out, Wendy motions for them to come over.** [LB1] Why ain't you inside? Jumbo cookin' spoiled crabs again? [Wendy] I just wanted to talk to you ALONE for a second. Two, why don't you go inside and start with some free-weights? **Lost Boy Two turns, wordlessly and heads for the door to the gym/restaurant.** [Wendy] He seems a little TENSE. [LB1]*Leaning down on the door of Wendy's car.* He's still MENTAL over that whole PIG-Iron and his Ilks thing. [Wendy] Did he ever say ANYTHING about shoving me around? [LB1] Weeeell...all he REALLY said was he pushed you harder than he meant to. But he never seemed too UPSET by it, no. [Wendy] I see. Why did you side with HIM over me? [LB1] Well...you know how it is... [Wendy]*Watching Lost Boy Two enter the building* Yeah, I do. **As the door closes behind Two, Wendy looks up at One and smiles.** [Wendy] You better get in there! **One looks confused. Suddenly there's a loud crash and Lost Boy Two can be heard crying out in pain.** [Wendy] Right about now, Two needs some help. [LB1]*Running for the door* DAMMIT, WENDY! THIS AIN'T CALLED FOR! [Wendy]*starting her car* Yes it is. **As One opens the door, Two comes flying out into him, knocking both to the ground. Two's face is a mask of blood.** [LB1] WHAT THE FU__ **Through the door steps Jamaican Superstar Arron Iron with a bloody crowbar over his shoulder. He walks to Lost Boy One, who is trying to get out from under the unconscious Two.** [AI] When 'e wakes up, mon, tell 'im it's a gift from Wendy Addams an' da Iron mon to Frank and Greg Addams da USED to be Lost Boys! **Iron draws back with the crowbar and let's Lost Boy One (Greg Addams) have a shot to the head, leaving both of the Addams brothers unconscious and bleeding** [Wendy]*leaning out the window of her car* You done? [AI]*Dropping the crowbar next to the bloody brothers* I tink so, monett. **Iron climbs into the passenger side of Wendy's big Lincoln** [AI] I be done...for NOW! **The huge car spins it's tires, throwing gravel and dust all over, as Wendy heads out of the parking lot.** [Wendy] THAT'S the way we handle ADDAMS FAMILY PROBLEMS! [AI] I can't wait to meet cousin IT, mon! **Fade Out**
