Elemental Force began to take the match a bit for granted though and seeing this, Carnage seized the opportunity and exploded on Elemental Force, or to be precise Maelstrom, keeping him in their corner and wearing him down with some championship winning team work. They kept him to themselves for about five minutes, but Maelstrom eventually broke away when he dodged an elbow drop. A big four way brawl broke out that ended up on the outside. It looked like it would end in a doube countout, but Monolith had lost track of time and military pressed Lafleur into the ring just before the ten count finished, giving Carnage the victory by countout.

MR: 6 MD: 3.11 Cold North by pinfall “GENTLEMAN” DAN CALDWELL (10) Vs. “THE ECONOMIST” RANDY BLAKE w/Ms.Paula (7) Caldwell put forth a dynamic display against veteran Randy Blake. Blake is usually prepared for anything, but he couldn`t be prepared for the reckless yet pin-perfect attack of the young Caldwell. Blake didn`t really know what to expect, but after Caldwell somesault-dropkicked Blake out of nowhere, he began to get a good idea what the speedy youngster was like. He tried to ground him, and for a while suceeded, but when Caldwell turned an arm-bar submission into a jumping side-kick/clothesline combo, bouncing off the ropes, Blake`s hope`s were shot through. A shooting star press followed, and then what he calls the “Super DDT”. It starts with him picking up his opponent into a bearhug, then he hooks the opponent's arm over his neck as if he's going to perform a suplex, then he throws his arm over his opponent's neck and DDT's them. Blake probably learnt it and forgot it many years ago, but today it was his downfall.

TITLE MATCH LORD BYRON (8) Vs. “DIRTY” DOUG SIMPKINS (8) Whatever Byron was expecting, he couldn`t have been expecting such a physical assault from Simpkins. Simpkins was intent on hurting Byron and causing him as much damage as possible, not on pinning him. Because of this approach, Byron was a bit off-set, and when the match spilled to the outside
(Simpkins drags Byron to the outside) Byron was totally on the receiving end. Inbetween Simpkins periods of control, Byron wrestled with Simpkins, and tried to get him into the Aristoclutch, but Simpkins wouldn`t roll with it. By the twentieth minute, Byron had been bust open by his head being rammed repeatedly into an exposed turnbuckle, and he was also dizzy from the assault conducted by Simpkins. Simpkins could have put Byron away a few times, but chose not to. Equally, Byron wanted to put Simpkins away, but he was fighting like a crazed animal that knew what he was doing, so Byron simply could not do it. The match ended in a time-limit draw at 30.00 but it was Byron who needed help with walking down the aisle, holding his bloodied head, and looking forward to give some payback at the upcoming
War!Star`s event.
