Extinction `96 — Fighting Foods

28 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**The Lost Boys are in a Fighting Foods sitting in a booth with MOUNDS of Battle Burgers and Fighting Fries in front of them.** [LB1]*Grabbing a Battle Burger* Kickin' ToP's ass gives ya a HELL of an appetite! [LB2]*grabbing some Fighting Fries* If WAR! wants champions that end thier matches laying FLAT ON THEIR BACKS, they got what they want in THEM two! [LB1]*Eating and talking at the same time(GROSS!)* <chew> You kept the belts,<munch><munch>but WE left you on your BACKS! [LB2] Remind you of anybody? [LB1] <munch><smack> Their MOTHERS? [LB2] EXACTLY! **While The Lost Boys are eating away, a familiar figure walks up.** [Wendy] Left 'em in the ring, didn't ya? [LB1] WENDY! Sit down girl! [Wendy]*looking at Two* How d'YOU feel about it? [LB2]*Stuffing a fist full of Fighting Fries in his mouth* Whaf effer. **Wendy sets down next to Lost Boy One.** [LB1] So? When you gonna start gettin' us another title shot? **Wendy reaches over to the box of Fighting Fries Two is eating out of, and takes one out dantily and pops it in her mouth and starts chewing. Not saying a word.** [LB1] After the good ol' fashioned BUTT whippin' we gave 'em, I figger it won't take much to get 'em back in the ring. **Wendy unwraps a Battle Burger and takes a small bite.** [LB1] Um...is somethin' WRONG? [Wendy] No. [LB1] You seem kinda...quiet. [Wendy]*taking another small bite of burger* No. Really. I'm fine. [LB1] You ARE coming back. Rght? **Wendy dabs at the corner of her mouth with a "Dirty" Doug Simpkins napkin.** [LB1] RIGHT? [Wendy]*getting up from the table* Be at the gym in an hour. BOTH of you. **As wendy walks toward the exit, One grins at Two, who is still chowing down on his Fightin Fries.** [LB1] I TOLD you she'd come back! [LB2] Hoo-F^#ing-ray. [LB1]*turning serious* Hey, man. Don't stir up no s#!t. [LB2] I wouldn't DREAM of it. **The Lost Boys continue to eat in silence** **Fade Out** ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [name withheld] Fat, Bald, and Confused! Temp.
