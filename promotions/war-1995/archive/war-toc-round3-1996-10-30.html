WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

Tournament of Champions — Round 3

30 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sorry this is late. Round 4 will be out later today. Round 5 will be on
WAR!Mongers (which hasn't come out yet, by the way). I think Mike's waiting for me to catch up in the ToC. Oh well!]
***************************************************************************** * Dave Scott: Welcome fans! Tonight we'll have all four round 3 matches. After this, we'll have our final four. We started with 32, now we're finally getting down to the final few. One will emerge victorious.
Only one can win the World WAR! Championship. Booster Dunne: This is where it gets exciting. Everything is on the line now. One mistake and all the hard work to get this far will be a waste.
DS: First up is Tonnage and Bounty Hunter. Bounty Hunter scored what can be considered upset victories over OG Gary Trent and Storm Stalker... BD: He wants to be called just the Stalker now. DS: Okay, Stalker. Tonnage, expected by many to win this tournament, managed impressive wins over Rick Showboat and Robski. BD: Bounty Hunter beat those smaller guys, but there's nothing small about Tonnage. He's going to have his hands full. Bounty Hunter vs Tonnage Tonnage was not only the favorite to win this match, but he was the crowd's favorite. Tonnage was able to overpower Bounty Hunter early on, and Hunter just couldn't make much of a comeback. Tonnage dominated throughout, but
Bounty Hunter wasn't giving up easily. Despite some devastating moves from
Tonnage, he couldn't keep Hunter down for the three-count. A missed splash by Tonnage gave Hunter his biggest chance in the match. He rolled up the former champion and got a near fall. In fact, Bounty Hunter and a section of the crowd thought he had pulled off another upset, but the ref insisted it was only two. Tonnage regained control of the match and following a powerbomb, finished Bounty Hunter off with a Gigaton Crush.
Winner: Tonnage by pinfall Time: 8:33 Rating: ** 1/2 DS: Tonnage finishes off another opponent with the Gigaton Crush and moves one step closer to regaining the World WAR! Championship. BD: Nice try by Bounty Hunter, but Tonnage is, well, Tonnage. It's going to take an amazing effort by someone to knock him out of the tournament. DS: Tonnage's Armageddon stablemate, "Magnum" Biff McGriff, will be trying to join Tonnage in the semifinals. It won't be easy as he faces "The Reckoner" Damien Legend.
BD: The biggest win of Biff's career came recently when he won the International title. I know from experience that it's not as easy as it looks to go from being International champ to being World WAR!
champ. We'll have to see what happens. DS: Just to review how these men got here... McGriff defeated Sabin Figaro back in round one and then polished off Johnny Dreamer in round two. Damien Legend beat Arron Iron and Darkness to make it this far. Winner faces Tonnage... Let's find out who it'll be!
Biff McGriff w/Felicia McGraw vs Damien Legend This was an evenly-matched contest. Biff came in on a roll, while Legend has been struggling a bit recently. Legend managed to get off to a good start and worked on Biff's leg. He slapped on a figure four, but McGriff managed to escape. Legend stayed on the offense, continually going back to
McGriff's leg. Felicia distracted Legend long enough for Biff to clothesline him from behind and deliver a neckbreaker. McGriff continued to beat on
Legend until Damien took out his leg again. Legend superplexed McGriff and went for a standing figure four, but Felicia interfered and knocked him out of the ring with a spin kick. The International champion was disqualified, allowing Damien Legend to advance.
Winner: Damien Legend by DQ Time: 13:49 Rating: *** DS: Felicia's interference cost McGriff that match, Booster. BD: Yeah, but it was good interference. Legend kept going after Biff's leg. Before any serious injury took place, Felicia put an end to the match. Biff does have an International title to defend, you know. No use trying to wrestle with a bad leg!
DS: The first half of the semifinals are set. Tonnage will wrestle Damien Legend. We've got two more matches here in the third round, though. Next we'll have the former World WAR! Champion, Seth
Blitzspear, take on "Dirty" Doug Simpkins. BD: I like Simpkins' chances if he has Randy Blake in his corner. DS: What's the deal with them anyway? Simpkins and Blake, that is. BD: You know as much as I do. DS: Okay, let's see what goes down... Seth Blitzspear w/Pat Mills vs "Dirty" Doug Simpkins Randy Blake came to ringside after the other two wrestlers were announced. The crowd was 100% behind Blitzspear in this one. Blitzspear overpowered
Simpkins with his usual brawling tactics. Seth hit Dirty Doug with a flying clothesline and went for the pin. Simpkins kicked out, but he was still in trouble. Seth went for the Flying Time backdrop, but Simpkins caught him with a kneelift. Simpkins tried to turn it into a wrestling match using wear-down holds. Seth didn't like that idea so he gorilla pressed Simpkins while still in an armbar. Seth set up for the Flying Time again, but Blake got up on the ring apron. Blitzspear shoulderblocked him off while Simpkins ran up to Seth to attack him from behind. Seth quickly turned, ducked, and backdropped Simpkins out of the ring. Dirty Doug was unable to return before the ref counted ten, moving the former World champ a step closer to regaining his championship.
Winner: Seth Blitzspear by CO Time: 10:33 Rating: **1/2 DS: Seth does it again! Another opponent goes for a ride courtesy of the Flying Time backdrop! BD: He's not the only guy with a finisher, you know. DS: You're just upset Simpkins didn't advance. Blake wasn't that much help, was he? BD: I bet Blake and Simpkins have something planned already. They're not going to let Blitzspear and the biased officials get away with this. DS: Biased officials? There wasn't any problem with the officiating in that match. Anyway, let's get to the last match of the evening. It'll be Candyman against TJ "Thunder" Decan. The winner gets the unfortunate fourth round match against Blitzspear. Candyman has wins over Jerry "Goon" Gordon and Butch Williams - by forfeit - so far in the tournament. Decan beat Frank Addams from the Lost Boys in round one and Mutilator in round two.
BD: Candyman had it easy in round two, but Decan - if he shows - will be much tougher. He might just be able to pull it off, though. That's why they have to wrestle the matches, I guess.
DS: Here we go - Candyman and TJ Decan... Candyman w/Nomad vs TJ Decan Various members of Armageddon came to ringside to watch this match. Candyman gave up 300 pounds to Decan, so he tried to hit and run. Decan landed a right
hook when Candyman got too close. A frankensteiner, a spin wheel kick, and a senton from the top rope are moves you might see in another match, but not this one. Instead, Decan took control with a bodyslam, a backbreaker, and a leg drop. Nomad pulled Candyman out of the ring to give him a breather, but Biff McGriff, who was also at ringside, helped Candyman back in. Decan continued his assault. Candyman caught Decan with a flying bulldog just when it looked like he was done for. Candyman went for a couple of rollups to get a quick victory, but Decan kept kicking out. Candyman's confidence grew as he wore Decan down. He went for a couple of more pin attempts as he smelled the upset. However, he got a little too confident and went for a piledriver.
Decan flipped him over his head, falling back on top of him. Decan landed a Samoan drop before nailing the Thunder Jam for the win.
Winner: TJ "Thunder" Decan by pinfall Time: 14:56 Rating: ***1/2 DS: A good effort by Candyman, but he came up a little short against Decan. Two Armageddon members reach the fourth round - Decan and
Tonnage. They'll face Seth Blitzspear and Damien Legend, respectively.
BD: Any of those four guys could win it all and I'd have to say any of them would make a good World WAR! champion. Not as good as me or
Randy Blake would, but...
DS: Those matches will be coming up soon, along with the final of the ToC. For now, good night! (c) 1996 WAR!
