Casualties of War 2 — The Truth Behind the Event

1 December 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the Wrestiling Unlimited Studios, a special report on Casualties of War by Avocado Bob.
" Wow. Who can believe that it's been a week since "The Unforgotten
War". We saw some shock events and there were a few explosive revelations, so we've had to wait a while for the dust to settle down.
Now it has and we can begin to see the damage that has been inflicted on the WAR! Wrestling scene.
First of all, lets turn to the Seth Blitzspear - "The American" Butch
Williams situation. We heard Seth Blitzspears comments earlier in the week, and he was clearly hurt by what happened. Betrayed by his brother, Seth has been angry and confused. But now we have an exclusive interview with "The American" Butch Williams regarding his turn of attitude. ToxicShock met him at his favourite Boston cafe,
"Dans".
ToxicShock: Butch, the fans, the wrestlers, and most importantly, your brother, have all been amazed that you were the mystery masked man who cost not only your brother to lose the title but for Randy Blake to win it and hold it for two months. Why did you do it?
Butch: Look Tox, I'd been out of the circuit for a while. I'd taken a break from wrestling to have a think. To think about where my wrestling career would go after losing the loser-leaves-town match AND my International Wat title to my brother.
Toxic: And what did you think? Butch: Seth never visited me. He never answered my calls. He was too wrapped up in his own career. Even after Casualties of War 1. And he only contacted me in CoW so that he could have Americas best wrestler by his side. He was using me. Abusing me. And once he'd done that, he discarded me. Heck, even when I was an official in War for a shorttime, he never tried to contact me.
Tox: Is this truly the reason? Butch: Yes, and no. Tox, you know me. I'm a proud American. Heck,
I'm the only proud American. Only Americans are worth the time of day in my book, and Seth just isn't American enough to be called my brother. We may have a similar bloodlune, but at the end of the day, my feelings towards him are little different to the reds under the beds.
Toxic: So, can we be expecting a rematch between you and Seth soon? Butch: Not unless it's in another federation. I lost a loser-leaves-town bout, and that is the way it's staying. I do have honour and an understanding of duty, unlike some people...brother.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Avocado Bob. " Well, there you have it. It seems like the situation has come to an end, but it is unresolved. Will they meet again? Who knows where?
Who knows when? But on to the next issue of the day. It seemed, for a short moment, that the two popular tag teams, the Aderondac Brothers and Ghetto Boyz had turned against the fans and their good friend OG Gary Trent. Trent was on the end of a serious beating after he came to the aid of the
Brothers and Bhoyz. However, all was not as it seemed. Our cameras followed the "turncoats" into their dressing room, unnoticed. To our suprise we lernt the real reason why the Ghetto Boyz and the Aderondac
Brothers were wearing masks that at CoW - it wasn't the Boyz &
Brothers. When the masks were removed it revealed four wrestlers, unknown to us, but speaking in a foreign tongue. Through an interpreter, they were speaking to none other than legend Ric Flair who had apparently reformed The Four Horsemen. It was a new FourHorsemen though. It consisted of Ric Flair, Hulk Hogan, Big van Vader and Sid
Vicious. Why did they come to War? Why attack the Brothers and Boyz?
The answer provided by Ric Flair was "Becasuse we were paid a lot...and I do mean a lot...of money to do so". Who was the bankroller? Flair wouldn't let on. But the result is that soon, in a one-time only match, Ric Flairs new Four Horsemen will be facing the
Aderondac Brothers and The Ghetto Boyz and perhaps OG Gary Trent.
And finally, the Civil War was an exciting event. Featuring The American Patriot, Nexus , The Mandarin, Brewster Cogburn & The Reverand
J.R Death, it was but a small Civil War. It would have been bigger, but the AderondacBrothers/GhettoBoyz/Four Horsemen scandal prevented the other four from entering. Quite simply, the match went like this.
Everyone teamed up and eliminated The American Patriot straight away. Then They all turned on Death, but Death fought them off, and The
Madarin formed an alliance with him eliminating Brewster Cognurn over the top. Death turned on Madarin and made him submit with his Torture
Rack. This left Nexus & Death, but despite Nexus pleas, Death showed no mercy. Nexus used his brass knuckles, but that didn't knock Death down. Eventually, a throatslam followed by a "Leviticus Legdrop"
proved sufficient to get the victory. AS a result, The Reverand J.R.Death had to hire a carpenter to build an extra cabinet for his ever increasing collection of trophies. His latest, after being the only non-casualty, is the "Immortal Soldier" Trophy. And perhaps, just perhaps, he actually is. This is Avocado Bob, signing off and wishing you all a very merry hairy Christmas.
