The Toxic Shock Interview

30 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tox: Thank you Mr.Williams for giving me this interview. I see you`re working out hard. Butch: Damn right. Now Stevens has Venom by his side, I`m going to have to fight two men at a time. And I can. When I Fight with American Pride, I can beat anyone.
Tox: Even your brother? Butch (looking solemn): I love my step-brother. I really really do. He was a great World War Champion, but too much has gone between us now. He says
I`m jealous of him because of mother, but the fact is I`m still cut up that he didn`t contact me for three months when he knew I needed a friend.
Tox: And it was him who eliminated you from War! Butch: Damn straight. But I`ll give you an exclusive now. If he apologises to me, for that and for believing Tyler Stevens over me, I`ll forgive him.
Remember, I said to them all, Tyler will turn on you, and now he has. Tox: Do you have anything you want to say to Tyler Stevens right now. Butch: Stevens, I am so glad you lost that title. It fills me with such joy. Trent is the much more deserving champion. He may not be a proper
American like me, but at least he has a bit of the honour that makes us
Americans so damn great. You`ve gotten under everyones skins again. When you were pretending to be with the fans, you made an enemy with all the rulebreakers. Now you have the other half of War! after you because you have turned against them.
Tox: Do you think he si spreading himself to thin? Butch: He`s too thick to be spread thin. Stevens is a total moron, and I look forward to pounding his skull in.
Tox: Mr.Williams, thank you very much.
