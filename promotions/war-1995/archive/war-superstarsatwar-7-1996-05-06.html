WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card review

Superstars At War VII — The Dark Legacy

6 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WRESTLING UNLIMITED Presents ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION SUPERSTARS AT WAR VII - THE DARK LEGACY A review of the May 6th card from Vancouver, Canada With your showhost “Avocado” Bob Whitely and comments from Jean Van Basten & Rick “Ricky” Soal ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Instead of the usual live broadcast, this is a review from the lush new War! Broadcast studio. There are three big comfy sofas which each host sits on, and in the centre there is a table with lots of War! Action figures. The set is decorated with life-size cutouts of real War! wrestlers.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob: Welcome everyone to the new formof Superstars at War. We`ll be bringing you a review of all the matches that took place last night at the
Vancouver SuperStadium. Tonight I`m with ex-War! wrestler “European Havoc” Jean Van Basten and Rick Soal who`ll give the wrestlers and matches their marks out of ten, with critical comment to boot.
It was a fifteen match card, with five title matches, and plenty of others for entertainments. The first matches saw Mark “The Canadian
Strangler Starr pin Brett Stevens and “Superstar” Golden Page defeat Mystery
Mayhem by countout. Lets continue by looking at the second match, between
Seth Blitzspear & Colt McGraw. Run VT.
JVB: Seth uses his primal strength to overwhelm Colt McGraw. It`s clear from the outset that there will only be one outcome - A win for Seth.
RS: Be fair to Colt, he doesn`t wrestle that much. This was purely a match of meeting contractual requirements.
Bob: You could see he wasn`t putting a lot of effort into it. It probably explains why Seth won so quickly with his Flying Time. Rick, your marks out of ten for Colt.
RS: I`ve got to give him a 5. He just didn`t try. JVB: And I`m giving Seth a 6. He hasn`t held a title for a long time now, and unless he develops his skills, he`s going to fade away, because the wrestlers in War! are just too smart for him nowadays.
RS: A piece of concrete is too smart for him. Bob: And the match itself gets a weak two-star rating. Now, the next match on the card was 008 Jim Bondo making what could be his final appearance in
War! Big Duke was asked to step in at the last moment to fight in this match. Guys, your view on the match.
RS: Big Duke - Big loser! You know, this guy had so much going for him and then he loses the War!Lord championship to Cockmaster and bazam, this guy is plummeting faster than really heavy lead . Something has happened to Duke, otherwise he wouldn`t have lost to Bondo tonight.
JVB: I think you`re underestimating Bondos love of the fans. He wanted to go out on a win, and thats what he did. Sure, Duke started off okay, but
Bondo showed such resilience that he just couldn`t be put down.
RS: Bondo is a jerk. I`m glad he`s going. Big Dukes only consolation is that he lost via countout. And that was only because Suna Gib turned up and harrassed King Benny again.
Bob: Gib certainly has been causing trouble for the Rich Boys. And at the same time he has been very beneficial to the Brotherhood. I have been told that any love connection between him and CrocMaster must be refuted.
JVB: Well, he`s an item with Bane, isn`t he? RS: Shut up! Bane is not like that! JVB: Isn`t he? Any guy who dresses up in a mask must be something to worry about. Say, didn`t your dad wear a mask during his career? Didn`t he get
Gib that tagmatch for the brotherhood?
RS: Just you shut it right there. Bob: Marks out of ten. Please. JVB: Bondos performance deserves a 9. It would have been a ten, but he didn`t win by pinfall. RS: Big Duke had better not get into a bad habit of losing. He gets a 6 off me. Bob: And the ten-minute match receives a three-star rating. The next match on the card was a tag-team bout between The Aderondac Brothers and The Chaos
Knights. Disorder and Mayhem had to try and do their best to overpower Bladerunner and Bombsquad. RS: We can see well from this match how good Bladerunner & Bombsquad are as individuals. They lack something as a tag-team, however. Apart, they could dominate the singles ranks, in my opinion. Instead they choose to fight tagmatches.
JVB: It is their choice. But I must say, I don`t mind when matches are as exciting as this. It was high impact, high violence. I don`t usually like these sort of matches, but when it`s two teams I hate, I just love to see them batter each other senseless. The highlight of this match for me was
Disorder slamming Bombsquad onto Bladerunner, and then slamming his
tag-partner Mayhem onto them. RS: But it wasn`t enough to get the victory was it? JVB: No. That came later. Bladerunner reversed an irish whip on Mayhem and set him crashing into a bare turnbuckle. Bombsqad then rammed Mayhems face into the turnbuckle several more times until he was so bloody that the ref stopped the match.
RS: Pure genius on the part of the Aderondac Brothers. Bob: It was also a typical aspect of that kind of match. The match got four-stars for it`s entertainment value. What did the teams get? RS: Chaos Knights have a lot of promise. Disorder is especially impressive. At 6`8”, he can hold his own. I`m suprised he hasn`t dabbled in singles competiton. I`ll give them an 8 for their showing last tonight.
JVB: Well, Mayhem is out for a week with head injuries. Aderondac Brothers put in a consolidating display of their dominance. They get an 8 as well.
I look forward to a rematch one day soon. Bob: We didn`t have to wait long for Biff “Magnum” McGriff to battle Aaron
“The Jamaican Superstar” Iron though.
JVB: I was asked before the match to give an honest opinion on who would win the match, and I was right when I said Aaron Iron, who I`m giving a 9 to by the way. McGriff has always seemed a bit too one-dimensional in his attack, but Iron has more to his game. While Magnum was stuck to powermoves, Iron was able to wear Magnum down and use high-flying high-impact moves as well. Look at this powerslam that Iron catches McGriff in. Superb.
RS: I heard that McGriff was a bit ill that night. He certainly didn`t show his best, that`s why he only gets a five off me. This guy is so strong he should be a champion, but I must admit, he is a bit of a Seth
Blitzspear. It was an offpeak performance at the end of the day though, and
I wouldn`t worry about it if I was Biff. Which I`m not.
Bob: And the match, which lasted just over eight minutes, got a three and a half star rating. Next up was
Johnny “The Sidewinder” Missile in a rematch against The American Patriot. RS: And Missile got such a suprising amount of support it was unsuprising that his performance would be upped.
JVB: It definitely was. It was the War! newcomers best performance to date. In my book, he gets a nine for a performance like that. He totally controlled the match, with the Canadians totally against the icon that is the American Patriot.
RS: But the turning point was when American Patriot hoisted Missile over the top rope and to the outside where The Sidewinder hurt himself quite severely. If American Patriot was my kind of guy, he`d have gone out with a chair and smashed that guys kneecaps to bits.
JVB: Fortunately, he`s more my kind of guy, and he helped Johnny Missile back in. The fans tried to urge Missile on again, but his knock on the knee was too much to cope with.
RS: And so was the “Revolution” sharpshooter by American Patriot, getting him an important victory before his title bout with the world champion next week. Still, if it hadn`t been for that injury, I think AP would have lost in that Canadian arena. Missile was too hyped and the crowd were so hostile. Patriot gets a 7 off me, but that is only because I`m feeling generous.
JVB: If you ask me, and you are, Patriot was not showing his best. He`ll need to put on a much better display if he wants to win the World War!
championship. He gets a six. Bob: The match received a mighty four stars. Was the next match to be on the same level of excitement? It was ex-world War! champion Randy Blake facing the scary Andrea Mancini in a deadline signed match. It was a match that was almost called Blakes deathline. Mancini and his Acolytes were chanting from the start of the bell, and immediately Mancini grabbed Blake by his throat and slammed him into the turnbuckle without releasing his
hold. Mancini was DQ`d, but didn`t stop until Blake passed out. Then the Acolytes assembled some sort of crucifix and tied Blake to it. Dunne tried to come to the rescue, but Armageddon totally prevented him. However, a combined effort by The Clique, War!Force and War! Security managed to prevent anything worse happening.
JVB: A totally sickening sight! Who does Mancini think he is? Whatever the answer, Blake will not be happy with being crucified last night. RS: Oh, loosen up Jean, it was funny. Gee, you Europeans have no sense of humour - except that Andrea Mancini. JVB: You`d find your grandmother getting shot funny. RS: True, but that doesn`t discount my credibility. Bob: After that event, it was tag team action again, with the Ghetto Boyz against The Agonizers. FCF and BDV were impressive and dealt with The
Agonizers easily, with BDV getting the pin on General Leo. They want their titles back.
JVB: After losing them in such an unfair way I`m not suprised. They`ll want the Brotherhood, most definitely. This 8 rated performance would be enough to beat the weak champions as it beat their opponents tonight.
RS: The Agonizers were disappointing. They`ll get no better than a five off me. Bob: And the match gets two-stars. Two stars were involved in the following match. Lord Byron and Spike Allen took part in a heated contest, but Byron demonstrated his superiority, hardly letting Spike touch him throughout the contest, eventually letting the man from Ann Harbor,
Michigan, touch him. RS: A superb showing by the aristocrat of War! , Lord Byron. Top notch. Nothing more to add really, except as those quaint old limeys say, ‘ jolly good show, old chap.’ A ten out of ten for Byron.
JVB: I hate to admit it, but Byron did put in a good display. I`m a bit concerned about the level of opposition he`s been fighting. It`s mostly been mid-carders. I want to see him fight the Seths, the Banes, the Booster
Dunnes. Allen was out-classed, and foolishly so. He gets a four. He`s going to have to try a hell of a lot harder to get anywhere here in War!
Bob: The match, on Byrons performance alone, gets a four-star rating. The five title matches were left until the end. We`ll have those matches for you after we hear what Sid Starr had to say about his match with
DeathJester. The camera opens to Sid Starr standing in front of a background with his white cat's pawprint insignia on it. He's smirking as usual.
[Sid] Well, well. Looks like the cool cat is about to fight for the Liberty gold again. And this time I've gotta take it away from a friend of mine, the DeathJester... Well, Jester... I doubt I have to remind you, but once I step into that ring all alliances and friendships are right out the window, because I WANT my belt back!
Sid smirks a bit and the continues. [Sid] You see, DeathJester, you may be unpredictable. You may be dangerous. You may be one of the best lightweights in WAR!... But you're not me. You'll never be me. I _am_ the best thing to step in the ring! Just remember,
DeathJester, friends outside the ring means nothing inside, and I'm gonna take it to ya in there like it was my worst enemy in front of me! Bottom line comes down to this. Whatever ya do and whatever ya plan it won't be enough to keep the coolest thing in the ring from becoming a two time
Liberty champion. It's destiny!
The screen fades out as Sid smirks at the camera Bob: We never did get the full story on that exploding girl incident which ultimately put Tyler Stevens out of War.. Nobody knows anything. It`s quite a strange situation.
RS: She was just a very passionate fan. Kids`ll do anything to help DeathJester. Bob: Did they help him in his defence against Sid Starr? We`ll find out after we review the War!Lord title match, with the leader of the
Brotherhood, CrocMaster facing Rick Showboat. RS: Rick Showboat, what a guy! Great initials. About 4 inches smaller and many pounds lighter than CrocMaster, but he didn`t let that get in his way of showing CrocMaster exactly why he should be a War!Lord. He wore the CrocMaster down, and sustained all the beatings that CrocMaster could throw at him. JVB: Wasn`t enough was it? CrocMaster held out. RS: Held out until that Suna Gib turned up. Bob: That`s correct. Suna Gib appeared at ringside and started harrasing Mr.Money in the final moments of the match. Thsi distracted Rick Showboat and enabled CrocMaster to beat Rick Showboat by cloteslining Rick Showboat out of the ring, where Gib started touching Showboat. Showboat was so disgusted he chased Gib all the way back to the locker rooms, losing, as a consequence, by countout.
JVB: Gib has been very active in War! He`s had a lot of influence in the title picture, but he doesn`t appear to be a great wrestler himself. RS: Uurrgh, I just can`t stand guys like him. Excuse me for not being very liberal, but he makes my flesh crawl. And he made Rick Showboat lose.
Showboat would have got an 8 if he`d won. I`ll only give him a seven though. JVB: And CrocMaster put on enough of a display to earn a seven as well. Bob: Well, the match got three stars. Next up was Sid Starr battling
DeathJester for the Liberty championship. These colourful light heavyweights had met many, many times before, and Sid Starr has had the better of those matches. But that was when DeathJester was Joey The Clown.
Now he is the vicious DeathJester, and the new aggressive streak certainly played havoc with Starrs tactics. He had enough in his arsenal to come back and fight DeathJester to the finish though. The match was exhilirating, but ended in a 30 minute time-limit draw.
JVB: Sometimes you feel cheated when a match ends in a draw like this, but they put on such a good display of aerial moves, so equally, you couldn`t help but feel the result would only be justified if it was a draw. It was much like the Aderondac Bros/Chaos Knights match earlier, for me.
RS: A good showing by my good buddy Starr, and if he was facing the old Joey the Clown, he would have whipped his butt. DeathJester is a different story though, and his new found agression certainly helped. Starr gets a ten out of ten off me, because he`s my mate.
JVB: DeathJester, on that scoring, also deserves a ten, but if I was being objective, I`d say a nine.
Bob: The fans loved that match. It got a four and a half stars - almost a classic. The next match wasn`t a classic. It was a drudge-like performance by the tag-team champs Bruiser Buggelli and Bounty Hunter against a far superior StarSmashers team.
JVB: Appollo Friday and Henry Rome definitely did everything right. That tagged fast, worked well as a team and seperated Buggelli and Hunter. The Brotherhood were disorganised, dysfunctional and just plain terrible. Friday crushed them, Rome sliced them. RS: Yeah, well, look, Hunter and Buggelli are singles wrestlers, not tagteam experts like The StarSmashers. It was inevitable that The
StarSmashers would win this. JVB: Yes, it was a case case of the StarSmashers winning it, rather than
The Brotherhood losing it, if you see what I mean. If you just see the
Octopus Hold that Rome used to make Buggelli submit, you`ll get the picture.
The StarSmashers get a nine. RS: The Brotherhood put in a very disappointing display. There was no signof unity. A terrible three.
Bob: The match was awared three-stars. Rome & Friday paraded their new unified World War! championship belts for quite a while after the match.
After they left, it was time for the International War! championship match between Booster Dunne & Brutus Noir. It was full of incident. It was full of people. You knew this match wouldn`t result in a pinfall or submission.
There was going to be interference from someone. Would it be from Lord Byron woh showed up with King Benny at the beginning of the match, spying on both wrestlers. Would it be Randy Blake, who was accompanying Ms.Paula to Dunnes corner. Would it be Suna Gib who turned up halfway through and started chasing King Benny again?
It was Randy Blake. Noir was being typically aggressive in this match, and was actually getting theupper hand on Dunne until the seventh minute when
Blake dragged him out and started smashing him with chairs. Dunne was Dq`d because of this, and he went out to help Blake beat Noir.
JVB: Blake still hurts from losing the World War! Title to Noir a while back. He wants revenge.
RS: What I liked was Lord Byron taking the opportunity to sneak attack
Booster Dunne from behind with the time-bell, and then smash his head repeatedly into the metal steps before picking them up and dropping them on
Booster.
JVB: Then, with Noir & Blake still brawling, Byron used his cane to damage
Dunnes knee further and then dragged him into the ring, applying the
Aristoclutch. He obviously wanted to keep it locked for longer than a minute, but he didn`t account for the appearance of “The Living Law” Brutus
Judd at ringside.
RS: Yeah, that loser Judd entered the ring and clotheslined Byron off
Dunne. Judd then began pounding Byron like he`d never been pounded before, whipping him so hard into a turnbuckle that he went flying out of the ring.
Judd didn`t relent and powerbombed Byron onto the concrete. Bob: The rich boys didn`t like that, and Big Duke, “SuperStar” Golden Page and Rick Showboat made an appearance, teaming up with Byron on Judd & Dunne.
The action didn`t stop there, with the Brotherhood also appearing. CrocMaster, DeathJester, Mystery Mayhem, Bruiser Buggelli and The Bounty
Hunter started attacking The Rich Boys.
It turned into a total riot. Dunne was escorted away by Judd while the
Rich Boys and Brotherhood smashed each other to bits. Blake and Noir were eventually seperated, but no wounds were healded between the two. And as for King Benny, after being chased by Suna Gib, we heard nothing until a few moments ago. Apparently he is a jibbering wreck and isn`t saying much about what happened. He doesn`t seem to happy.
The match itself showed promise, and gets a three star rating. JVB: Equally, Noir & Dunne didn`t have much time to prove themselves. Noir put in a decent display though, and I`ll give him a seven, possibly 8.
RS: And Dunne was impressive as always. He gets an 8 off me. Bob: Toxic Shock managed to get these words off Lord Byron after the match. ~~~ Tox: Mr. Byron, what the hell were you thinking about with that despicable attack? LB: Well, M. Toxic, I want what's coming to me, and that is the International title. It's time for the Rich Boy's to win back what's rightfully theirs, and that belt is, one way or another, going to end up around MY waist VERY soon!!
President Bullfrog, you have ignored me for far too long!! I want a title shot, and I want it right here, next week!!
~~~ Bob: We`ve heard from “Bullfrog” Eric Helmroy that if Booster Dunne accepts the challenge, Byron will get the title shot. The final match of yesterday evening saw The Reckoner challenge World War Champion Tonnage. Tonnage showed with ruthless efficiency why he is the World War! Champion. The
Reckoner, despite all his ability, couldn`t stop Tonnage and his legendary
Giganton Crush. It was a relatively easy victory for the World War
Champion. Gentleman, for the final time this evening, your comments on this three star match.
RS: Well, what can you say about Tonnage that hasn`t been said before? He`s big, fat and strong. He`s got aggression, but he`s also got brains. I can`t see anyone beating this guy now he has the belt. Nobody is going to beat him for a long, long time. He gets a nine for his performance.
JVB: The Reckoner will get a belt one day, I`m sure of it. But it won`t be while he puts on ill-thought out displays against men as deadly as Tonnage.
He gets a six off me, but he can be so much better. Bob: Thank you Jean, thank you Rick. This has been a review of last nights
Superstars at War! card from Vancouver, Canada. It was full of incident,
and a lot of questions were raised. We`ll be back soon with Superstars at War IIX - “The Devil`s Broken Bones”. Until then, look out for WarFare II and the WARAW Sunday card. See ya!
