WAR! Wrestling Federation — Card

WarZone 6 — RandomWrestle

9 June 1995

------------------------------ WAR!ZONE 6 Live From the War!Datorium
With your hosts Avocado Bob &
Dr.Nathaniel Darkness ---------------------------------- Bob: Welcome one and welcome all, ladies & gentlmen, to War!Zone 6. The news is CANADIAN CARNAGE WILL get their 15 minute non-title match against
The DIVINE ALLIANCE. However, LIGHT & DARKNESS will also get a title shot after that match at THE DIVINE ALLIANCE if they beat 20,000 volts &
DEATHTHREAT. Other match changes involve the replacement of "Partyman" with the newcomer "Leviathan", and boy, is this guy big. Furthermore, Nightbreed have split to further their own careers and they face each other tonight.
Also debuting is THE WHEEL and he will be accompanied by his manager StoneMan and he will be battling the newcomer "Ultimate Wrestling Machine". Doctor
Darkness isn`t here just yet, so nothing prevents us from getting this promising card underway. Let`s go right now tot he opening match.
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ JACON DEMONCHILDE Vs. GRIZZLY (Falls count anywhere) This falls count anywhere match entails it to be a noncount out match, but
Demonchilde keeps it in the ring where his superior spped can utilize the ropes in an advantage against the powerful "Grizzly". Demonchildes edge goes when he gets caught into a "Grizzly" Bear hug after Demonschildes telegraphed high cross body. Demonchildes keeps cool however and uses his "Gleam of the red eyes" to easily entrance Grizzly, his former tag partner, but still friend and manages to gain a successful pin at 4.32
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ULTIMATE WRESTLING MACHINE Vs. THE WHEEL (2/3 falls) The Wheel, a great wrestler in his own right, cannot escape from UWM`s wide variety of wrestling holds. A european uppercut secures the first fall for UWM but The Wheel gets the second with a Frankensteiner. The match spills to the outside, but both wrestlers get back in, where UWM secures the second fall with a Sleeperhold at 21.36
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ CLASSIC DEATHS Vs. D & D (Cage Match) Demolizer & Destruction do exactly that to the waning Classic Deaths tag team. Securing the edge early on, D&D pound on Classic Deaths relentlessly. At one point, Classico does make a comeback with a "SymphonySlam" on Demolizer, but
D & D`s brutality proves decisive, finishing both Classico & Morgue off with
"The Bomb" - a powerbomb, bodysplash combination at 12.35, but D & D could have ended it so much sooner.
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ TERRORGANG Vs. DANGER ZONE w/TNT (non-title match) (Falls count anywhere) Another falls count anywhere match, this time TerrorGang - Eric "The Chainsaw" Lad & Suburban Street Terrorist up against the International War tag team champs, but since this was signed before their victory, so the title is not on the line. A good thing too, since this technical/high flying combo manage to keep the match at their own pace, preventing Tallyman or Styx from getting an edge. Deep into the match though, Styx manages to elevate SST tot the outside, and the match turns into a brawl/street fight that DangerZone are destined to win, and do so with their "Hell`s Gate" finisher on Eric Lad on the concrete floor at 16.42. At the end, when asked for comments, they scream, "we`re waiting for you D & D, we`re waiting for you".
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ INNERCITY FIRMER Vs. LEVIATHAN ("The largest man alive") 8 foot , 650lbs
(Submission Match)
Inner City Firmer looks ready to submit just by looking at the Leviathan. A vicious slap by Leviathan sends Firmer flying, and a series of body splashes pound firmer so much that the referee calls for the match against the ex-world champ to end to save ICF`s career. Match end 2.01
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ BLOODSTREET BROTHERS Vs. CLAN DEMON (StreetFight Rules) This is the BloodStreet Brothers speciality match, bringing broken bottles to the ring while ClanDemon bring baseball bats. Lacerating ClanDemon repeatedly, the fans cheer, and cheer more when ClanDemon retaliate with blows of their own. The fans don`t care who wins this, they hate them both, but for the few that were BloodStreet Bros. fans and fans of blood, they would go home happy when ClanDemon give in at 11.02 dripping with blood from their glass born wounds.
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ OG GARY TRENT Vs. JOEY THE CLOWN (Ladder Match) Clown & Trent slug it out, with Clown coming out on top. Utilizing suplexes he wears down the young OG Gary Trent. Trent responds to crowd support and retalitates with some right hands and a few slams, leaving Clown on the floor.
Trent goes to get the ladder and brings it in. Clown recovers and dropkicks
Trent, then swipes him with the ladder, knocking Trent out of the ring and enabling Joey the Clown to get to the top of the ladder and win the match at
7.46 ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ MR.MAGIC CHAIR Vs. "THE ECONOMIST" RANDY BLAKE w/Ms.Paula (Trench Match - Barbwire, taped fist cage match) Both seem uneasy with this kind of match, but the fans cheer for Chair to do some serious damage to "The Economist". But it is Blake who gets the win almost immediately when Ms.Paula throws his briefcase into the ring, where upon he subsequently knocks Mr. Magic Chair out, enabling a quick exit from the Trench at 0.53
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ FREEBIRD & JULIA Vs. STORMLORDS (Submissiob Match) Stormlords Thunder & Lightening use their superior technical ability to get the better of Freebird & Julia, although it is far from over-powering. Johnny
Thunder appears at ringside with the Maltese Falcon and Dr.Schock distracting
Stormlords.
The match, however, is one of scientific holds being thrown back and forth until the eigth minute where Thunder removes the turnbuckle covering and
Lightening smashes Freebirds face on the turnbuckle, lacerating him. This angers Julia, who enters the ring to stop Lightening from repeating the action, but the referee sends Julia back to his corner. while doing this the
Stormlords execute their special moves on Freebird several times, especially the aeroplane spin/shoulderbreaker combo. A full nelson by Thunder gets the submission out of Freebird at 13.36 and a minor confrontation flares up between Stormlords and Tunder & Co.
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ THE MANDARIN Vs. JERRY "GOON" GORDON (Falls count anywhere) The falls may count anywhere, but the pain definitely counts in certaian places when it`s The Mandarin applying his nerve holds. Gordon rocks Mandarin with a few clotheslines, but eventually the Mandarin makes "Goon" submit with a nerve hold on the neck at 5.42
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ FABRICE DUVALL Vs."THE HIGHLANDER" ANGUS MCBAINE (30 minute Iron man challenge - as many pins as possible in 30 minutes) Angus McBaine starts by working on Duvalls right leg and manages to get he first fall that way with a figure four leglock. Duvall responds three minutes later after the fifth minute fall with his "Lightning Strike" flying dropkick and gets another four minutes later with a torturerack. McBaine then manages to catch Duvall with two "Whirling Dervish" tilt a whrl back breaker further in the match, winning the Ironman challenge at, of course, the 30.00 mark
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ (NICE MR) NEXUS Vs. MOUTAIN TOP MADMAN (First blood match) The winner of this match is the one who draws blood first. Initially it seems like it will be MTM, after biting into (Nice Mr.) Nexus, but the revelation of a pair of brass knuckles craftily dealing a bloody blow to Madman gets the victory for (Nice Mr.) Nexus at 1.08
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ BREWSTER COGBURN VS. "BOOSTER" DUNNE (1 fall) Commentary: Bob: And it seems a good thing that we couldn`t get the cage up. This simple one fall bout is proving to be a beauty. Hey, here`s Dr.Nathaniel
Darkness. What took you so long in getting here Doc?
Doc: You don`t wanna know, you just don`t wanna know. Bob: Well, anyhow Doc, you`re missing a good match. Dunne is getting on a lot of holds and Brewster is laying on a lot of clotheslines.
Doc: Who`s that coming to ringside? Bob: That`s "The Economist" Randy Blake with Ms.Paula Doc: And he`s not alone. There`s The Mandarin. Bob: This is a set up. They must both be after Dunne. Doc: No, look. They`re both interfering in the match. Mandarin has got
Dunne in a painful nerve hold - hear Dunne scream...
Bob: And Randy Blake with Ms. Paula are assaulting Cogburn. "The Legend" who has begun to get quite a fan base, is in trouble. Blake is showing no mercy.
Doc: Nor is the mighty midget the Mandarin. The officials are trying to get
Mandarin to release the hold, but he won`t. Dunne must be in such pain
Bob: Somebody must do something... Doc: And they are...here comes The Blood Street Brothers...they`ve got their glass bottles...they`re lacerating Cogburn and Dunne with those bottles...
Bob: And here comes Team Youngblood with TerrorGang...yes, they`ve managed to get Marlon & Chad out of the ring, and finally, after three or four minutes of extreme punishment, Cogburns and Dunnes suffering stops. Blake & Mandarin walk back, but not together. I don`t think this was planned
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ YOUNGBLOOD Vs.SOUL BROTHERS (replacing Nightbreed) (Loser leaves War!) Ultimate Wrestling Machine is sitting at ringside, observing Team Youngblood as they battle the other popular team, the Soul Brothers. The match is clean but desperate since the loser leaves War! However, neither team leaves when the match ends in a double count-out at 8.55
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ DEATHTHREAT & 20,000 Volts Vs. LIGHT & DARKNESS (Street Fight Rules) - Winner gets title shot at Divine Alliance later in card
A clever battle plan by Light & Darkness defeats DeathThreat & 20,000 volts in
rapid time earning them a title shot against The Divine Alliance. Both attack 20,000 volts, grabbing his shock stick and shocking him, and then
DeathThreat, getting the victory at 0.51
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "TRAILBOSS" MIKE DALLAS w/Mr.Kang Vs. JOHNNY THUNDER w/Dr.Schock (Submission Match) Johnny Thunder introduces his bodyguard at the beginning. "Hold on a minute everybody. I have a very special treat for you today.... Doc: What, you mean he`ll wrestle instead of boring us to death? .....I would like to introduce to you a very special friend of mine. A friend who has promised to watch my back when I wrestle. He hails from parts unknown, but he`s the guy that beat everyone else up there too. He is the lightning accompanying my thunder. He is the master of disaster....Dr.Schock.
The match itself is active enough, and clean enough. Eventually, Dallas gets the submission with a boston crab at 25.47
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BRUTUS NOIR Vs. XL-9 (Falls count anywhere) Noir has fought XL-9 before, butthis time when XL-9 appears, he seems more heavily built, out-slugging Noir. Noir does manage to apply the "Marseille
Strangler", but XL-9 kicks out from the pin. XL-9 utters the word"Exterminate" and then lifts Noir above his head, throwing him into the crowd. The referre stops the match when XL-9 refuses to stop choking Noir with the cable wire when Noir goes blue. A DQ victory for Noir at 7.23
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ World War Tagteam Non - Title Match DIVINE ALLIANCE VS. CANADIAN CARNAGE (Survival Match - elimination match) The stipulation of this match is that if CC survive 15 minutes against Divine
Alliance they get a title shot at the next card. Well, bad news for Carnage is that they won`t be facing Divine Alliance next week. Zeus & Amon Ra corner
Mountie II for fourteen minutes of the match, slughtering him with powermoves,and seemingly able to pin him at any moment. Johnny Canuck gets frustrated, but knows that interference will lose them the match. Finally, when told there is 30 seconds left, Thunder pins Mountie. Divine Alliance go onto the next match, hardly breaking a sweat.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Freedom Title Match BOMBSQUAD w/Bomber Vs. THE MALTESE FALCON (Cage Match) Match highlights: Bob: Well, we must be getting near the end of the match, Falcon has just levelled Bombsquad with another Blackbird splash....and look, he`s going to the top to do a "blackbird" splash...but wait...here comes the Inner City
Firmer...he`s climbing the cage...Falcon notices him but too late...Inner City
Firmers locked Falcon in a headlock...and there`s a suplex out onto the floor..my god, that`s a drop of 15 feet...both must be hurt bad...Falcon wins the match and the title, but I don`t know whether he`ll ever be fit to wrestl again...and ICF seems to have injured his neck again...and what about the crowd?...there is a child squashed underneath Falcons body...I see some lawsuits for War! approaching...but I can also see bombsquad irritated at losing the title in his first defense...obviously not championship stuff.
WorldWar Tag Team Title match Divine Alliance Vs. Light & Darkness Both teams are pumping with addrenalin, and the high impact moves from both teams shows class and expertise. Zeus manages to keep control for Divine
Alliance though with his overpowering strength. Convincing legal teamwork by
Light & Darkness changes the tide of the battle that swings back several minutes later when Amon Ra fells Light with a standing dropkick. A tag to
Zeus callls for the Thunder Press and a pinfall on Light at 13.59
INTERNATIONAL WAR CHAMPION vs. WORLD WAR CHAMPION "THE AMERICAN" BUTCH WILLIAMS Vs. SETH BLITZSPEAR (Loser leaves War!) Bob: Wow, what a match this is. First of all, both are the top champions. Secondly, the loser of this match, thamks to the "RandomWrestle" stipulation means the loser leaves War! Finally, Butch Williams claims to be
SethBlitzspears brother. If this isn`t melodrama nothing is. Doc: Bob, I can confirm for you now that Williams is Seths brother - his half brother...Williams has shown me his birth certificate, and they are born of the same father. I also conducted DNA tests and they conclusively reveal that these two are related. Williams has suspected for a while, and if you look carefuly, you can see the similarities.
Bob: Well I never, you certainly can see the similarities. This is truly amazing, just like the match that has got under way. Blitzspear rocks
Williams with a few clotheslines...Williams dodges the elbow drop...and look at the speed in which he takes control...a seriesof backbreakers weakens Seth considerably...and Williams is going for the SuperPowerSlam early on...and he gets it.
1 . . . .. . . . . . . . . . . . . . .2 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and Seth kicks out. Wow, I`ve never seen anyone do that before....and Seth rallies tot he cheers of the crowd....he seems impervious to Williams blows
Doc: Speaking of someone impervious to blows, here comes XL-9.
Bob: What`s he doing here. Doc: I don`t think he`s auditioning for lead cheerleader Bob: He`s standing on the ring apron...Blitzspear hasn`t noticed him...Blitzspear off the ropes...and XL-9 clobbers him with a headbutt..Butch
Williams has been Dq`d...XL-9 is beating on Blitzspear...he`s going to throw
Blitzspear into the crowd...but Williams comes to the rescue...he knees XL-9 in the back and catches Blitzspear as he falls....XL-9 leaves the ring area...but Williams doesn`t deserve to be thrown out for losing in this manner..there must be a hearing. Blitzspear keeps his title but doesn`t win the International War title. I suspect a tournament of somekind will take place, but I still can`t get ver Williams losing like that. The stakes were way too high.
Doc: It was all or nothing, and he`s ended up with nothing. Bob: A controversial end to a spectacular evening. I`m sure lots will be said about all this. In the mean time, we`ll see you next Monday for
