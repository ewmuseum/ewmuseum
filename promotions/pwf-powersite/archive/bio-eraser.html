Name:Eraser Short Name: Eraser Height: 6'10'' Weight: 289lbs Music: Dragula by Rob Zombie Age: 30 Hometown: Tampa Bay, Flordia Favourite Quote: I'am the ICON of the PWF!!!!! Finishing Move: The Eraser (Super Snap Powerbomb 01) History: Eraser career started off 1 year and a 1/2 ago. Michael Pellington find this young jobber just walking aroudn and many feds. Michael ask Eraser if he would like to come a join his new fed that he call the PWF. Eraser was glad to see that someone would like to have him wrestle so he went. Eraser wrestled there for 1/2 year and captured the Worlds Title twice before leaveing to join the MOW_SH. In the SH he catured the US title. He also joined a group called the NSO the NEW Slaughthouse Order. But after lossing the title and ever geting a rematch he left for the PWF once again but not as Eraser he done had a new life now he was THE GAME. The Game walked into the PWf and in no time he was once again the PWF champion. He then lost the title and started some Hardcore wrestle and ended up winning the hardcore titel and also winning the Royal rumble to put him in teh number 1 comtender ship for the Worlds titel once again. He went to The End of the World which he was in the main event once again but this time he had all the power he made the match which was the bigest match of the PWF a triple HEll in the Cell Ladder match. But the most exciting part was that it was a 4 way match and 4 men and 3 cells did not look good for them. Eraser at the final cell throw Diablo off the ladder and watched him crash through 3 cell and hit the ring. And with no one else to stop him Eraser set up the Ladder and Climbed the ladder to finish off the PWF as the Worlds champion. And to have a recorded of 28-15 and to hold the Worlds title 4 time and the Hardcore title and to also win a Royal rumble. After te PWF closed down Eraser didnt know where to go vWv, Sh, many other fed where calling his name but Eraser ended back at the Sh and once again won the US title but before he had his shot at the Worlds title Eraser left and no one heard from him since then but now Eraser is back and he is here to once again become the PWF Worlds Heavy-weight Champion and once again rule the PWF.
