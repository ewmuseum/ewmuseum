Members Leader: Matt Van Dam Power G Travis Right The Real McCoy(Manager of Power G) Jennifer Van Dam History: The gWo started in the summer of 1999. Rookie wrestling starlet Matt Van Dam and his new bride Jennifer Van Dam began a search........... a search to form the most dominant force in E-Fed Wrestling history in time for the new millenium. The Van Dam's were holidaying in Cuba when they heard of a powerful young fighter. He was a mute and labelled as a ruthless killer by the villagers of this previously peaceful holiday village. MVD of course went to watch this myth in action in a local wrestling tournament. He watched in amazment as the man he came to see totally destroyed his opposition until reaching the final of the tournament. MVD jumped his opponent before he could make it to the ring. He then began to walk to the ring with a microphone. He offered the man in the ring a match. But the man in the ring could not reply, so a man in a suit and a top hat slid into the ring and told MVD this man has never been beaten before and he would leave the ring maimed! MVD tolded the spokesperson that if he lost to the man in the ring Iwill write him a cheque for $1,000,000 dollars. But if he won, he would take this man away from Cuba to wrestle for him in the USA. The man in the ring appeared to be able to communicate with this suited spokesperson. The spokesperson agreed and the match began. It was an epic battle lasting over 30 minutes, but MVD prevailed, and as promised the great Cuban wrestler went to america and toured with MVD as a wrestling faction known as the GREEN WORLD ORDER and accquired the name Power G, his spokesperson also joined them under the name of the Real McCoy. Now the gWo is here in the PWF with the sole ambition of owning the lot!
