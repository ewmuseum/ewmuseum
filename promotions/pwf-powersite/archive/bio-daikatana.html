Name: DaiKaTanA Short Name: DaiKaTanA Alias: The Dark Warrior Height:6'10" Weight: 307 Music Batman Beyond theme" Age: ???? Hometown:?????? Quote: Don't Fear the Darkness, only Fear what it Hides... Finishing Move: Twist of Fate History: Evil is a taken in many forms, and for many, it is a way of life, but for others, they are born for the purpose of evil, for the one that I speak upon, has been called the Dark Warrior. The Dark Warrior was said be been raised by the ghost of a graveyard, located in the mountain's of Japan. It has been written that he was found in an open grave along with a katana sword, that would soon become his trademark, and engraved on the tombstone was, "Don't fear the Darkness, only fear what it hides" which would become his call-sign in the near future. Dark Warrior would later meet someone, just like himself, he would be known as the DeMoN. As time progressed, they became allies as such, and became the AoD, standing for the Alliance of Darkness. Later, Demon would be the first to enter the PWF, and capture gold in the hardcore division, Dark Warrior would later enter, fairing well against the opposition, and later winning the gold in the Hell in a Cell division. Later, Demon and Warrior were invited to join the New Brood, and as time progressed they formed the AoD inside the ranks of the New Brood, and then, when the prophecy known as Fate joined the PWF and Fate, Demon, and the Warrior, which would take the New Brood out from the inside, and form the Alliance of Darkness, which would become a key force in the PWF. Later, Fear Effect would join the PWF, this team consisted of Cronic and 187, these two would capture the Hardcore Tag team titles for the first time in PWF history, they would also capture, the tag team championship defeating Wages of Sin. As the soul leader of the AoD, the Dark Warrior would soon be encountered with a feud that would change the face of the AoD, and the Dark Warrior himself. At First, FaTe started a feud with Eraser which would involve the Reapers of the Nights, and the Alliance of Darkness, this would continue, until the end of the PWF, where Dark Warrior would be retired as the PWF Hell in a Cell Champion. Then, the Dark Warrior found himself at the doors of the wVw, where they didn't welcome him or Fate with open arms, but Demon abandoned the pact of the AoD, the Warrior didn't take this well, the Dark Warrior returned to the graveyard, where an altered ego took over his mindset. He then became DaiKaTanA, who became infatuated with the death of Demon, and hell bent on revenge. He started a series of attacks on Demon, then on his return to the ring, he put Demon in the grave, then could the Demon raise again, if so, then Daikatana shall be there to put him down in the MOW E, and the upcoming return of the PWF.
