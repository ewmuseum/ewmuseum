Name: Theros Macalvia Short Name: Theros Alias: The Madman Height: 6' 8" Weight: 282 lbs Music: "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Ozbourne Age: 26 Hometown: Syracuse, NY Favourite Quote: "HA!" Finishing Move: System Shock (Scoop Reverse DDT) History: Theros was born into the great grecian family of Macalvia. A family well known for mental ailments and distrubances. Theros was very different though, he was an intelligent, well adjusted child. He did well in school, and he was a great athlete in the school's soccer team. Unfortunately Theros's homelife was something more unstable. A family ripe with mental ailments is hard to live with, and often he would be ignored because he possessed none of their madness. To combat this, and make a space for himself, Theros began to act. Theros pretended the mental distress his family owned. This is how Theros discovered his acting ability. Being a natural at both acting and sports, pro wrestling seemed a natural course of action. Using his family's name, and playing the part of a madman Theros gained fame in the various federations he began in. His first break came in the YTW, Youth Team Wrestling, a backyard fed in America. His stay there was short as he was quickly recruited into a training center, and his skill increased. Finally he was hired into the UAW, as a full time wrestler. At first he performed only dark matches, but recently has seen a bit of the spotlight. Now looking to make a name for himself, Theros has applied to the PWF, one of the premiere wrestling federations in the world, hoping to find a vacant spot where a madman is needed.
