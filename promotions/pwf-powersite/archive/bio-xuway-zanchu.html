Name:Xuway Zanchu [pronounced Zan Choo] Short Name: Xuway [pronounced ZOO-WAY] Alias: "TAFKAG" Height: 7'3" Weight: 394 lbs Music: "Hells Bells" by ACDC Age: Ancient Hometown: Orient Favourite Quote: Do as I say or DIE!! If you don't beg for mercy I'll rip your limbs off. Finishing Move: The Great Immobilzer (Turnbuckle Jumping Armbar) History: Xuway Zanchu is from the orient (parts unknown). He become a martial arts champion at the early age of 6. At the age of 10 he was ranked the best in the world. As a teenager, he was an inspiration to all. A true hero. Often he would subdue his opponent causing unconsciousness or temporary paralysis. He was feared and well respected. Xuway had joined the military and quickly moved up the ranks. The government moved his talent a secret camp to train the the top notch ninjas. He was known as (bunch of chinese symbols) which loosely translates to "The Great Immobilzer". With his whole life dedicated to martial arts, Xuway seeked a more spiritual life. Xuway has spent some time in Tibet meditating with monks. But instead of finding inner peace, Xuway increased his threshold of pain through meditation. He has been known to punch through brick walls and soak his feet in lava pools. While gone, his entire family was murdered by a local gang. Xuway returned to seek avengance. Xuway sought out each of the gang members. It has been rumored he ripped theirs arms out of its socket and threw salt on the open wounds before finishing them off. Xuway's mind has been forever changed for the worse. His mental well-being is unstable. Before being charged with any criminal charges Xuway escaped not to be seen again. Well, until now. Xuway has surfaced and has been seen across North America looking for a professional career to be rich. He claims with his earnings he will build the largest army and take over the world! Sick and demented no one wants to encourage the lunatic. However, the PWF is always looking for the toughest individuals and agrees to accept the martial arts expert. Only time will tell if the Xuway can reach the top of yet another plataue. Will fans adore him as they once did?
