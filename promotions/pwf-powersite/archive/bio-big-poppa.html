Name: Big Poppa Short Name: Real Show Alias: The Real Show Height: 6’8” Weight: 285 lbs Music: "My Way" by Limp Bizkit Age: 24 Hometown: San Francisco, California Quote: “Your ass just got Paptized.” “Whether you like it or not, The Real Show, just said so.” Finishing Move: Poppa Bomb (Super Powerbomb) History: Big Poppa is your classic “part-time gone full-time” wrestler. Basically, Big Poppa grew up interested in being a comedian and a Basketball star cause of his size. He also wanted to be Santa Claus, but that’s another story. He went to UC Berkley with a full basketball scholarship. He was good, but he knew deep down that this couldn’t be his career because he wasn’t NBA good. Despite his feelings, he had to keep playing. He was pretty pissed about his situation, but soon his life would begin to put itself together. The late March night is where the legend of Big Poppa begins… Theme Guy was an owner of the leading wrestling organization at the time, Cyber Wrestling Federation (CWF). After a game, Big Poppa was just talking to a few people when Theme Guy approached him. Theme Guy gave Big Poppa an offer that was one that promised fame and greatness, and Big Poppa accepted immediately. Theme Guy and Big Poppa worked out schedules for training Big Poppa. Big Poppa quit basketball and Theme Guy said he would paid for Big Poppa’s tuition, knowing that he has a “bank” that he can’t lose. Big Poppa soon was named “Big Pappa,” and once he graduated, went on to CWF. After crushing a man by the name of Dr. Dre in his first match that was a table match, Big Poppa went on to defeat Steve Austin in a Hell in A Cell match for the CWF Heavyweight Championship. Big Pappa, as he was known, was also offered the rank of commissioner and was bringing in the checks that Theme Guy wanted. CWF went through some hard times, and Big Poppa, along with the other CWF wrestlers, were laid off. Big Poppa would follow a few of the laid off CWF Wrestlers to a new organization, Virtual Championship Wrestling (VCW). It was there that Big Poppa changed his name from “Big Pappa” to “The Real Show” Big Poppa. Big Poppa didn’t like the whole serious attitude he had at CWF, and after calling in a few friends, came to VCW with laughs. Big Poppa, like in CWF, showed his ability in the ring with in defeating a 5-Time VCW Heavyweight Champion, Homicide, for the European Championship. Soon, Big Poppa organized a stable with his partner and a few other people. Steve Da’ Fan, the current Undefeated Light Heavyweight and TV Champion, Homicide, the current Heavyweight Champion, Tazz, and Big Poppa made up the new VCW stable known as The Real Playerz. As a stable, they went on to winning the Stable Championship. With a brand new attitude, Big Poppa was again on the fast track to domination. However, Big Poppa’s track was altered. His friend and him were given 2 options: Stay and be put back into the powerplant, or leave VCW. Steve Da’ Fan knew that he didn’t want to get screw, so he picked up and left. Big Poppa knew that there was someone that didn’t want him where he was going, and wasn’t going to let whomever he was beat him. Big Poppa spent the next 3 months training, and then was given the job of Announcer. He got the fans behind his back and used his position to provoke other wrestlers, which ultimately led him back into VCW. After a few unimportant matches, Big Poppa fought his way back up, and soon defeated “People’s Champion” 009 for the VCW Heavyweight title. The problem with this was as soon as he won the title, he lost it by a screw job. Big Poppa again fought his way to the top, and beat Hunter for the championship. It was then the offers of from Federations started to come. Big Poppa made a few bad choices, like the ones of Madmen of Wrestling Extreme (MOWe) and Lethal Lair of Extreme Hardgore (LLEH), which led to nothing because of closing down. Before Big Poppa could make a return to VCW, he was offered a job from one last federation, Pelican Wrestling Federation (PWF). Considering rising to the top one last time, Big Poppa signed up. Big Poppa figures that only good things will come from his last choice, because the third time is the charm, right? Title History and Other Accomplishments: (2) VCW Heavyweight Champion, (2) VCW European Champion, (1) VCW Stable Champion, Undefeated at 2/3 Table Match, (1) CWF Heavyweight Champion, Former CWF Commissioner.
