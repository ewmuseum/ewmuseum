THE PWF REBIRTH

1 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE PWF REBIRTH October 1, 2000 INTRODUCTION: For two years, the POWER WRESTLING FEDERATION has hibernated amidst its fame and popularity. The action it brought to the fans was gone. The realism that was only experienced in this league faded. However, that has all changed. Tonight, a new blood of Powerstars graces the squared circle as they settle their wars and reach for the ultimate prize: The PWF World Title. Welcome to the new era of the POWER WRESTLING FEDERATION. Welcome to the PWF REBIRTH! (END OF INTRODUCTION) Welcome everybody to a jammed packed World Arena and you are feeling the awesome power of the POWER WRESTLING FEDERATION coming to you live. This is the REBIRTH of the PWF! Tonight, we will be crowning the new and undisputed PWF Champion in a triple ladder match. The participants will be decided if they do win their respective matches. We shall see feuds settled or beginning. But the one thing that is sure is that there will be a new PWF Champion! Here are tonight’s matches: *Winner gets to be No.1 Contenders for the PWF World Title* T3 vs THE HARDCORE MACHINES PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #1: *Hardcore Match* AMBROSE FLEETWOOD vs TOBY DIRIZZO PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #2: *The Unravelling* SONIQ vs DARK CLOAK PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #3: *MYSTERY MATCH* ??? VS ??? PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #4: *The Revenge* HAVOC vs THE UNKNOWN-UNNAMED BEING PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #5: *Hell-in-a-Cell Match* SKANKER vs CHRIS CABANA PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #6: *First Blood Match* JASON "THE GOAT" DOODS vs "BAD BOY" MARCUS T. Whoever wins in their respective matches get to be in the semi-finals. The three final Powerstars get the final three slots in the PWF TRIO LADDER MATCH for the PWF WORLD TITLE.     If you’re ready, LET’S GET IT ON! *Winner gets to be No.1 Contenders for the PWF World Title* T3 vs THE HARDCORE MACHINES Two of the hottest tag-teams in the PWF are vying for the top spot in this number one contenders match for the PWF World Tag-team titles. Though it was a tag-team match, it was a clear two-on-four. Moon and Napalm came with all guns and artillery ready for firing. T3 had more insurance in this game. Both teams displayed their skill and prowess for a three count. However, the presence of Big Momma gave T3 the upper hand. The Moon Man is in control until the turnaround of the match. Big Mamma Sledge throws Ace Cooper the chains. Ace Cooper hits Moon Glorious with it. Ace Cooper executes the BC Drop on Moon Glorious and goes for the pin. Mick Doodle counts: One, two, three. With Moon Glorious down and out, T3 celebrate with their shot at the PWF Tag-team titles. WINNERS: T3 (PWF Tag-team No.1 Contenders) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #1: *Hardcore Match* AMBROSE FLEETWOOD vs TOBY DIRIZZO FLASHBACK: The cool and collect Ambrose Fleetwood is disgusted with bad manners and unruly behavior. Not to mention, punks. Toby DiRizzo, a fast rising Powerstar caught THE GOLDEN CHILD’s eye and admonishes DiRizzo for being a punk and an unruly individual. THE AUTHORITY cannot bear being part of Fleetwood’s system. (END OF FLASHBACK) Chicago vs New York: Just like the NBA. Fleetwood and DiRizzo didn’t have to wait for the bell to get the match on. These men knew what was going to happen since it’s a HARDCORE MATCH. Fleetwood tried all his dirty trick to finish off THE AUTHORITY. However, this was Toby’s territory. After a hiptoss, Toby DiRizzo executes the Screw the System on Ambrose Fleetwood. Ambrose Fleetwood tries to escape the hold and tries to fight the pain. However, he submits after 26 seconds. WINNER: TOBY “THE AUTHORITY” DIRIZZO (He advances to the semi-finals) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #2: *The Unravelling* SONIQ vs DARK CLOAK FLASHBACK: During a televised match of Soniq, a Dark Cloaked figure shows in the POWERTRON telling him to be ready for the darkness. Soniq was distracted and lost his first match in the PWF. Upon searching for the mysterious figure, he was victimized with the “M Flag”. Commissioner Zoe scheduled the match between SONIQ and this Dark Cloak.  Tonight, will this mysterious figure be revealed? (END OF FLASHBACK) Alecs Johnson could not wait to get his hands around this dark cloaked figure. Upon the dimming of the light, the strange fellow walks towards the ring. With the lights back, Soniq didn’t hesitate to grab him and executes a chop. Quickly, he wraps him in a sleeper hold. The lights go out again. Once it returns, Soniq is left alone in the ring still with his sleeperhold in place. The referee is surprised. Hence, he declares Soniq the winner of the match much to the confusion of Alecs Johnson. WINNER: SONIQ (He advances to the semi-finals) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #3: *MYSTERY MATCH* ??? VS ??? FLASHBACK: On PWF POWERHOUR, newly appointed Commissioner Zoe cooks up a mystery match between two returning Powerstars which will be revealed tonight. "A special secret match will take place as the mystery participants. Let me reassure you that these two Powerstars are not new in the field of sports-entertainment. Who would it be?" (END OF FLASHBACK) The crowd awaits the entrance of the mysterious participants. Then, the sound system plays a familiar guitar rant. Out comes WOLFGANG RAINIER G. The former PWF Tag-Team Title holder is back! He enters the arena with a large pop from the sold out crowd. His opponent. The familiar techno drums play …”HATAW NA!” Oh my God! He has returned! THE HATAWMAN. The jammed packed arena goes WILD! The Hatawman has returned. The two PWF veterans do a stare down and the match is underway. Both men were not showing any ring rust. Their moves are still intact since they wrestled two years ago. The decibel level in the building is unbelievable. Wolfgang Rainier G. executes a belly-to-back superplex through the table. He goes for a pin. But this is not a hardcore match. So it doesn’t count. The Hatawman executes the Hatawkick on Wolfgang Rainier G. on the concrete floor. The referee is hit. The crowd is going into a frenzy. Then comes THE GOAT running towards the ring with a lead pipe. He smashes both Hatawman and Wolfgang on the head with it. Both men are out cold. The referee recovers and counts both men out. WINNER: DRAW (No one qualified) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #4: *The Revenge* HAVOC vs THE UNKNOWN-UNNAMED BEING FLASHBACK: Havoc returns in the PWF with a bang. The celebration is halted as a unknown being enters the ring and pulverizes Havoc. Week after week, this monster continues to wreck havoc on Havoc. Tonight, his name will be revealed and Havoc will get his revenge. (END OF FLASHBACK) Havoc awaits his challenger. The lights go off and a blast of fire consume the sky as the unknown being enters the ring. His opponent... Hailing from Parts Unknown... Weighing in at 350 pounds... "The Incarnate" Inferno INFERNO was his name and pain was his weapon. The former PWF champion came in like a house of fire and didn’t give Inferno a chance to think of a move. Havoc seems to be in the advantage until Inferno counters a clothesline to a gorilla press on the steel corner. Inferno executes the Carnage Crush (running tombstone) on Havoc for the surprising win. With Havoc laying flat on the mat, INFERNO lifts his arms and red bursts of fire come out from the sky. WINNER: INFERNO (He qualifies in the semi-finals) BACKSTAGE: T3 celebrate with Big Mama Sledge as the camera goes out. Only the sound of smashing and hurt can be heard. The camera goes back on air as we see T3 draped with the M flag. Oh no! They’re here. (BACK TO THE RING) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #5: *Hell-in-a-Cell Match* SKANKER vs CHRIS CABANA FLASHBACK: During the first PWF POWERHOUR telecast, Chris Cabana was checking out Skanker’s girlfriend, TWO-TONE. Skanker didn’t like the idea of Cabana hitting on his girl. Cabana grew tired of Skanker’s mouth and challenged him to a hell-in-a-cell match tonight. (END OF FLASHBACK) The cage is set to settle the score between these two men. It was a back and forth match with each Powerstar gaining the upper hand. Two-Tone seems worried about his man’s condition. Halfway to the match, a large “M” appears in the POWERTRON. Two men came out. Oh my goodness! It’s the Miller Brothers from the WWA. They are the ones victimizing the PWF roster. The Skanker has them as allies? The action continues in the ring. Cabana and Skanker exchange blows. The Millers climb the cage and enters it. With Cabana backed in the corner, the Skanker prepares his attack. He even encourages the two mysterious men to help him beat out Cabana. They agreed…or did they. The two men started beating on Skanker. At the corner, Cabana starts laughing loudly! Cabana executes the POOLSIDE PLUNGE and escapes the ring with the Miller Brothers. UNBELIEVABLE! WINNER: CHRIS CABANA (He qualifies in the semi-finals) PWF ELIMINATION ROUND #6: *First Blood Match* JASON "THE GOAT" DOODS vs "BAD BOY" MARCUS T. FLASHBACK: Since his debut, The Goat proclaims himself the greatest of all time and the best among the rest in the PWF. During his match with Toby DiRizzo, “BAD BOY” Marcus T. decides to shut him up. Tonight. The Greatest meets the Baddest in a first-blood match. (END OF FLASHBACK) There is certainly bad blood between these two Powerstars. They're brawling inside the ring area. Ring steps. Chairs. Guardrails. This match is totally chaotic. But with one swing of the timekeeper bell on Marcus T. changed everything. Rick Strict inspects Marcus and sees blood. Thus, making the GOAT the winner of the match. The GOAT leaves the ring victorious. Marcus flares up and executes the RUN N GUN (legsweep faceslam) on referee Strict as the crowd cheers. WINNER: THE GOAT (He qualifies for the finals) SEMI-FINAL STATUS:   Since HATAWMAN and WOLFGANG match’s was a draw, THE GOAT gets the first slot in the PWF Trio Ladder Match. Soniq and DiRizzo get to go at each other for the second slot. Inferno and Cabana gets to go for the third slot. PWF SEMI-FINAL ROUND #1: TOBY DIRIZZO vs SONIQ These two had a great match. After a sporting handshake, the match starts. It was a fast and furious match between these up and coming Powerstars. However, things got a bit ugly. Toby DiRizzo takes a swing at Alecs Johnson with the steel chair, but he gets out of the way. Alecs Johnson snatches the steel chair from him and hits him with the steel chair. Alecs Johnson executes the SoniqDown on Toby DiRizzo. Leslie Lane counts: One, two, three. The crowd goes bananas as SONIQ enters the PWF FINALS. WINNER: SONIQ (He qualifies for the PWF World Title) PWF SEMI-FINAL ROUND #2: CHRIS CABANA vs INFERNO Cabana enters the ring confident that he will become the new PWF Champion. Inferno runs after Cabana and nails him with a clothesline. Havoc continues to beat Cabana insensibly. He gives the signal from the back and out comes The Miller Brothers. However, they are intercepted by T3 and starts beating the living daylights out of them. They didn’t reach the ring at all. Back in the ring, Cabana attempts to use a chair on Inferno. From the crowd, the Skanker appears (with bandage and all) and smashes a chair at Cabana’s back. Inferno catches Cabana and executes the Carnage Crush for the final slot in the PWF World title. WINNER: INFERNO (He qualifies for the PWF WORLD TITLE) THE PWF WORLD TITLE: TRIO LADDER MATCH The rules are very simple. The PWF World title is hoisted above the ring. You will be using the ladder to reach the top and grab the title. The one who does become the new and undisputed PWF World Champion.   THE PWF WORLD TITLE MATCH: THE TRIO LADDER MATCH SONIQ vs GOAT vs INFERNO The Finale of tonight’s PPV is at hand. Among these three Powerstars will emerge the new and undisputed PWF World Champion! Soniq relied on his speed. Inferno banked on his strength. The Goat relied on his greatness. Soniq dazzled the ring with his quick dropkicks and somersault moves. Inferno made minced meat of The Goat as his tossed him around like a rag doll. Soniq and The Goat made an unlikely team as they chopped down the Incarnate. Ladders were all over the place. Midway in the match, Havoc badmouths Inferno from the sidelines. Inferno executes a chokeslam on Havoc from the ring apron to the concrete floor. Soniq was about to reach the top. But The Goat dropkicks the ladder sending Soniq to the ropes. From under the ring, the Dark Cloak appears and drags Soniq inside the ring. Soniq vanishes. Only two men remain. As The Goat starts to climb the ladder, Inferno goes after him. However, Havoc sends a ladder flying in the ring and hits Inferno right in the face. Inferno is out! The Goat climbs the ladder and slowly reaches for the PWF Title. He grabs it and the bell rings. Jason Dodds is the new and undisputed PWF World champion! He celebrates in the ring amidst the flood of boos. “For sure, I AM THE GREATEST OF ALL TIME! I am the PWF World Champion. I am THE GREATEST!” The new PWF champion makes his way back to the locker room. Out of nowhere, “BAD BOY” Marcus T. shows up and executes the RUN N GUN. He takes the belt, looks at it and tosses it to THE GOAT. “I’LL GET YOU NEXT TIME, GOAT. NEXT TIME, THIS BELT GOES AROUND MY WAIST!” Marcus T. walks out as the PWF REBIRTH comes to a close. WINNER AND NEW UNDISPUTED PWF CHAMPION: JASON “THE GOAT” DODDS (END OF PPV)
