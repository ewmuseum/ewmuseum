Name: The Fro Short Name: H2Fro Alias: Peoples Answer Height: 6’5” Weight: 281 lbs Music: My Generation by Limp Bizkit Age: 24 Hometown: Green Bay, Wisconsin Favourite Quote: "The People's Answer is... An ass whipin!" Finishing Move: " The Fro Bomb (Stone cold Stunner) History: The Fro has been a sign of rebellion to the people ever since his first matchup in wrestling, which earned him the nickname "The People's Answer". The Fro's pre-wrestling past is very unknown, the only thing anyone knows is that he was in an elite police unit in Chicago about 2 1/2 years before he got interested in wrestling. The Fro was a closet wrestling fan for a good portion of his life, but eventually became a proud wrestling fan after wrestling starting earning mass popularity. When The Fro left the force, he decided to give wrestling a shot. And The Fro wasnt the only family member who trained to wrestling in the squared-circle, The Fro's cousin also tried wrestling. And The Fro's cousin is well known in wrestling today, Tom Thornburn. Thornburn and The Fro trained in a local gym. The Fro worked on his power and technical moves as apposed to Thornburn's high-flying and TKO moves, which made each one of their training matches together a real treat for the others in the gym. After The Fro and Thornburn's training was "complete", Thornburn returned to the gym for a while and The Fro got his first contract. The Fro came to the fed and instantly became a World Title contender and a favorite of the fans for opposing the most hated man, and World Champ in the fed. But The Fro was then fired by the owner because The Fro discovered a horrible scandal which gave the current world champ a complete advantage over his opponents. When The Fro was fired, other wrestlers left with him and the fans were disgusted with the fed and the fed folded. In his future The Fro would win the World Title in 2 different feds and won the Hardcore Title in another, with a couple of short Tag Title reigns. Contacts Email: [detail withheld]
