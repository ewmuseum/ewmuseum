Name: Travis Right Short Name: Travis Alias: The Future Height: 6' 5'' Weight: 283 lbs Music: "Walk with your head up" by the Deftones & System of a Down Age: 23 Hometown: Miami, Florida Favorite Quote: The Future is here, And your not gonna like it. Finishing Move: The Near Future (Super Tazz Plex) History: "The Future" Travis Right is a newcomer to the business. He wrestled in high school and won state titles 3 out of the 4 years he was there. After graduating Travis couldn't really decide what exactly it was the he wanted to do. So he planned to take a year off and take a look at his future. But while on vacation in New York a friend of his got tickets to see a small Independent fed so he joined along. His friend was the promoters nephew so as they got to the show He and Travis went backstage to talk to him. While there one of the scouts was really impressed with Travis's look and all around strength. He invited Travis to stay a bit after the show for a work-out to see if he had any skills. The Future seemed to be a natural in the ring and after some training by one of the feds veterans named "The Menacing Maniac" he soon became champ. While working around on different Independent feds he soon became bored with wrestling. But "The Future" sees the re-opening of the PWF as a chance for him to really showcase his skills.
