Name: Josh Resnick Short Name: Resnick Alias: none Height: 6' 0" Weight: 230 lbs. Music: Walk by Pantera Age: 25 Hometown: Detroit, Michigan Favorite Quote: "Your going to learn the meaning of losing!" Finishing Move: The Equinox (Crippler Crossface) History: At the age of 21, Josh Resnick set out to join a wrestling organization. Finally he found one that was open to new entrances. So he joined the Pro Wrestling Association for a minimum pay. Resnick was new at some of the things that was needed to wrestle. Over a 6 month period he was trained by one ot the all time greats George "The Animal" Steele. Under Steele's guidance, Resnick learned quickly. Finally, during one of Resnick's matches he made a breakthrough. Resnick finally won gold. Even better than that scouts from other bigger organizations where there as well. After the show was over, several scouts wanted to talk to Resnick about signing with one of the organizations. First the Carribean Wrestling Association wanted him to join them. For they where a new federation with no known history. Resnick thought about it then he said no. Resnick was content to signing with an organization that was known for greatest. Then comes along the scout for the Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Federation. The scout talked to Resnick about how they are reopening soon and would like to have his talent on their roster. Resnick thought about it, but said no. Next came along the scout for the Pelican Wrestling Federation. The scout talked about some of the great things that happen in the PWF before it closed. Then he mention that the PWF was to reopen again. Once Resnick heard that he decided then that the PWF was the place for him. Not long after Resnick talked to the scout, he signed to the PWF. His role is still not clear but soon enough everyone will find out. Contacts AIM: [detail withheld]
