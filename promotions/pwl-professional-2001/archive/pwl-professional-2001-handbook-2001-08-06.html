The PWL is booked, meaning the cards and results are hand written and not made by a simulator. Recently there has been a few roster spot openings because of the clearence we did of people that did not take the PWL serious. We cut nearly 15 people that weren't meeting the standards. Take advantage of the openings now, because once they're filled, the only way in is if you are invited.
2.0 Rules & Regulations Every efed must have rules to run smoothly and properly. The PWL is no exception. Here are the list of rules:
a) I do not tolerate wining. Wining after a loss will be immediate suspension. b) You MUST RP at least once a week!!! c) Do not use another wrestler in your RP without permission. d) All RP's, Interview, and Strats, must be in by the deadline. No exception! e) Try to keep swearing at a minimum and please censor it. (ex. F*ck, F***, F[Beep!]) f) ROLEPLAY!! ROLEPLAY!! ROLEPLAY!! 2.1 Newsletter The PWL Newsletter is sent out each wednesday. It contains Roster,
Rankings, Title Contender, Champions, News, Rumors, PPV Cards, and much more.
3.0 Roleplaying The only way to achieve anything in ewrestling is by ROLEPLAYING.(Unless you want to be a jobber!). You must RP Once everyweek. Send all your RP's to me(
[detail withheld] ). Roleplay packets are sent out containing the Roleplays I recieved since the last packet. They are sent out most likely everyday, sometimes after two days, depending on how many I recieved. Any challenges made in the Roleplays must be sent to be so I can confirm them.
3.1 Tips Good Roleplaying isn't that hard if you know how to do it: Always think ahead and plan out what you want to say, and then start typing. Don't repeat yourself in your RP's. Don't just talk, describe scenary and things that are happening. Ex
[Jake Phsyco is sitting down in his mansion, drinking a cup of tea.]
DO NOT MAKE YOUR RP's TAKE PLACE IN THE RING! BE ORIGINAL! And the most importtant thing,, BE CREATIVE! 3.2 In-Ring Interview In-Ring interviews are interviews or RP's that happen during one of the shows, most of the time in the ring or backstage. There will be a deadline before the event for these, I allow only 2-3 per show on a first come first serve basis.
4.0 Strategies These are almost important as Roleplays. There are also deadlines for these before each card. I recommend that you send in strats for each match you have. Basically, tbese are supposed to tell me what your strategy is for the match, what you do before the match, what you do during the match, and what you do if you win or lose.
4.1 Run-In's The deadline for this is the same as strategies. Email me and tell me what match you want to run in on, and what you want to do.
5.0 Challenges The cards will be made up by the staff, but some of the matches will be fueds and challenges that you create. You can challenge someone in a roleplay, email me and tell me who you want to challenge, or email the person you want to challenge. IT IS IMPORTANT TO EMAIL ME ONCE A
CHALLENGE HAS BEEN MADE AND ACCEPTED SO I CAN CONFIRM! 6.0 Characters of the PWL 6.1 Referees a.Todd Drexler-He is a veteran referee. He ususally officiates main events, and PPVs. b.Paul Leymen-He is a younger referee. He sometimes favors the good guys, but tries not to mess up.
c.Scott Serrell-He sometimes gives fast counts, and let's illegal things go. 6.2 Ring Announcers a. Lisa Crick-She is a young announcer. 6.3 Interviewers/Reporters a.Jerry Roberts-A cocky reporter. b.Quinn Kelly-A fan friendly female reporter. 6.4 Commentators a.Clyde Owens-Veteran color commentator. Favors the he good guy. b.Billy Stevens-Former, pro wrestler. Favors the bad guys. 6.5 Front office a.Rob Shyne-President b.David Mundof-Commish 7.0 Weekly Cards 7.1 Monday Mayhem-The main show, Airs 9:00pm-11:00pm. Results are out Monday Night, Card is out Thursday Night.
7.2 Thursday Terror-[TEMPORARILY SHUT DOWN. WILL BE BACK SHORTLY] The show for younger talent to make a name for themselves. Results are out Thursday Night is out Monday Night
7.3 PPVs are held once a month and will be announced after the last
PPV. The card will usually be in the weekly newsletters.
8.0 Championship Titles 8.1 World Title-The best title in the PWL. This best wrestler in the league most likey will have this at one time.
8.2 United States Title-The second best title. Up and coming stars might have this. 8.3 Hardcore Title-Title won only by hardcore matches. The most extreme wrestler usually hold this.
