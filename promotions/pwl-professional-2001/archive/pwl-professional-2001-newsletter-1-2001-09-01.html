News 2. New Signings 3. Wednesday Warzone 4. Extra 1.News Welcome to the PWL! In the past two days we have recieved about 8 applications, things are looking good. If we continue at this pace, the first Warzone Card should be out next week. Once the first card is out you can start sending your RP's to me, and every day or two I will send all the rps out to everyone in a packet. You must rp at least once for your match, and the maximum for each match is three. If you want to send me a strategy for your match then send it before the announced deadline. The same things apply with an in-ring interview that will happen in a show, or a run-in on someones match. The PWL looks like it's going a be a great efed.
2.New Signings Here is a list of new members we've gotten in the last week: -Tyronn "Dirty" Washington -Johnny Newman -The Sleepwalker -Mr. Hardkore -Fredd "Killa" Sapp -Justin Kashmere -Yamchaken Phoenix 3.Wednesday Warzone The first warzone card should be out next week. 4.Extra If you can write matches or know someone that can write matches for the PWL then email me.
