PWL Opening it&#x27;s doors

August 29, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in a large office. At the far end of the office, in front of a large window wall, is a large desk, with the usual office equipment. In front of the desk are a couple of large leather chairs. To the right of the desk, pushed up against the walk is a large couch, matching the chairs. Behind the desk, a woman sits wearing an all-black suit and talking on the phone. Lying on the couch is large man. Large enough that his feet hang way over the arm of the couch. Sitting in one of the chairs is much smaller man in comparison, though he's about 6 ft. Standing against the wall next to the desk is a third man, about 6'5". The woman gets off the phone.]
J. Rose [woman behind the desk]: Well everything's set up. We're ready to go. All we need now is the talent. Eliminator [large guy on couch]: Doesn't matter. We're not going to be beat. J. Rose: You're right. But that's only because you guys are not going to be wrestling. Kamikaze [smaller guy in chair]: What? What do you mean? That's what we do? Eliminator and I are the greatest tag team of all time.
J. Rose: Yeah, well you're officially retired! We're owners now. We're running this place. We're behind the scenes now. You guys know this business better than anyone else does. We've been in enough little piss-ant leagues. Run poorly. Always going on "hiatus". We're ready to blow the roof off the sports entertainment industry. I want this to be a professional establishment. You two [pointing at Eliminator and
Kamikaze], are my backup; my enforcers. I'm sure we'll make some unpopular decisions. You're there to back me up. Mysterioso, you're the new commissioner. You make the matches. You have final say. With the exception of me, that is. Now, what we need are the wrestlers.
Everyone go out there and recruit! [Mysterioso, Eliminator, and Kamikaze leave as J. Rose pulls out her rolodex.] http://clubs.yahoo.com/clubs/pwlbackstage
