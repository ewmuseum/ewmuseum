Greatest turn in WIWF or E-wrestling HISTORY!

24 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Attack" Zack Malone made a stunning heel turn on a Thursday Night
WIWF card during a special tag team match. Malone teamed with Paris to take on the man they were both feuding with the Heartbreak Kid. HB Kid teamed with Mr. Flawless. During the match, Paris attempted to tag out but Malone refused and then all three men pummeled Paris into the mat.
After the match Zack Malone & HB Kid announced that they, along with
Flawless and Killer Hulk have formed a stable called The Body Shop. This stable will undoubtly be vastly successful. And now, words from Zack...
Zack Malone: Well, boys and girls? Did I have you fooled or did I have you fooled? You have to get up pretty early in the morning to get anything by Zack, but hell, I can sleep in till noon and fool you morons.
HB Kid, Flawless, Killer Hulk, and me, we are gonna rule the WIWF, and before long, The Body Shop will be known as THE stable in e-wrestling!
Cause y`see, Jack, you have me. And I mean, that alone, is a dominant force, but then ya add Heartbreak Kid, and I mean, there you have the two coolest cats in e-wrestling. But we added Mr. Flawless, whose total flawlessness makes our team not just great...but the BEST. And then, just say, you want someone powerbombed threw a table, or thrown out a window...who do you call? Well, we booked ourselves an enforcer who lives for pain, Killer Hulk! Ladies and you fat slobs who call yourselves gents, wake the kids and call the neighbours, the Body Shop is gonna take home the gold! And now, me and my main squeeze, Cheryl Amber, have left the building!!
