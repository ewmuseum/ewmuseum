THE NEW SWF NEEDS MORE WRESTLERS, GET IN NOW!

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-The main rule of the fed is to have fun, thats what this fed is all about. -Dont complain about thing, that is very frowned upon. This is only a game, and things will happen, people will win and lose matches, and titles. -You may have as many wrestlers as you can handle, meaning you must rp for all wrestlers you have. if you want to enter a tag team or stable, just fill out the join form for each wrestler in your stable or team, and put down the team/stable name in teh correct spot.
-You must rp at least once a week, so i know you are still here. If you do not rp, there will be consequences. If you cannot rp for any length of time, please email me and let me know. I dont require all the flashy stuff in rp's like images, and different color fonts, i am more worried about content, but ifyou would like to do these things, that is fine, it wont hurt you.
-There will be two shows a week, Monday night Mayhem and Meltdown on Saturdays. and a ppv each month. most likely there will be a house show or two a week, which you dont really rp for, i just do them.
-Wrestlers may be real or fake, it doesnt matter, everyone will be judged the same. -Dont complain if the card isnt on time. Everyone knows, unexpected things may happen to alter people's schedules, just be sure it will be up asap. -Until i get some help, i will simulate the results wiht zeus pro. But i will be going back, and modifying everything, and adding stuff, and changing things, in an effort to make it as interesting as possible. It is hard to write good results for two shows a week by yourself...i did it for the longest time with another fed. So when i get volunteers to help me, then things will go to full
handwrittenn results. PPV's will always be handwritten. -The Xtreme title may be defended in any non standard match, and will be under 24:7 rule. If you would like to take advantage of the rule, post an rp w/ 24:7 rule in the subject somewhere, and as soon as i can do it, a match will be posted on the rp board. You are not guaranteed a win just by using the rule, the rule is there to add some more excitement to the title and the fed.
-Everyone will have a fair shot in this fed at winning titles. Of course the better rp'ers will be higher up on the card. but no matter what your skill level, you may have a shot at some time, at some title.
-I will also judge wrestlers on what kind of angles and storyline they submit to me. If you arent the best of rp'ers, but you continue to have decent angles to get your wrestler noticed, you will be elevated.
-Any requests by newcomers for title shots, will be ignored, and you may have consequences, unless i allow it to happen for an angle purpose. -THat's about everything i can think of now. If there is anymore, i will put it up, and letyou all know. But the basic principle behind the fed is to have FUN!!! This fed isnt about being the best, but i will take steps to make this fed good. Everyone will have a fair shot, i will try and drive this fed with quality storylines. If you are serious e-wrestler, looking for a serious fed, this may not be for you, but everyone may give it a shot, especially if you are looking to start you e-fed career. Everyone give a look, and give me a shot.
Thanks and have fun. sample card link http://www.voy.com/79062/ SWF fed link http://www.hometown.aol.com/[detail withheld]/swf.html lance
