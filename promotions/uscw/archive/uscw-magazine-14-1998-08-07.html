With people like Dolemite and Brauler, they could easily take the USCW by storm. For the most part, there are only a few wrestlers who could put up a good fight with the UWFr's. A few of the names I'll mention are Dobouis, Dakota, Stephens, and the Missing Linq. There are many wrestlers showing they have the talent to take on the big guys....Jason Polo, Trent Young, Violent Vermin, and Jeff Gillis. So this is a message to all the USCW......work harder. Unless you want UWF to basically demolish us in RobStock, you better pick up the pace....or its not going to look good. In other news, Scott Dakota defends his world title in the 4-man Battle Royal against some of the biggest names in the USCW right now. Daniel Stephens, Kid Sharpe, and the Missing Linq, all showed they have enough skill to have that title....I see all 3 of those Guys holding that title in the future. But for Dakota. it doesn't get any easier. At this Weekend Edition, he will face Ladies Lover Jeff Gillis. David Dobouis defends the North American title against Violent Vermin once again since their last match was a no contest. Jeff Gillis, Bullet, Kid Sharpe, and Instant Death, came down beating the two Regulators to a bloody pulp with a steel pipe and other foregin objects. But this Weekend Edition, there will be a rematch between the two...will Gillis strike again? Who knows, tune in this Sunday, to the Weekend Edition. Profile: Scott Dakota This amazing figure who has graced the presence in the ring has proved he is one of the best. He started out with nothing, as a loner, and asked for no help and never joined any stable. He has a smoking problem which he is trying to quit with nicotine, as you can see. Scott Dakota has only lost two matches. One against David Dobouis for the North American title belt, and the other against Delinquent which he lost because of interference. Scott Dakota won his North American title in a 3 way dance which Ron Matthews, who died a while back, participated in. He held it above his head proudly and was still undefeated. He soon fought David Dobouis who beat him with a muscle spasm for the pin. That didn't put Dakota down, though. He got right back up on his feet and then won the first Battle Royal with 9 other worthy competitors in the ring. Dakota then went into a 3 way dance with Kid Sharpe, who claims Dakota shouldn't have been wrestling in that ring because he hit the floor .38 seconds before himself, and Daniel Stephens. Once again, Dakota emerged victorious by pinning both Daniel Stephens and Kid Sharpe at the same time after Silencing both of them. This loner is still the World Title holder and one of the most feared men in USCW at this time. Profile: The Missing Linq This trash-talking, ass-kicking man is one of the most HATED people in the USCW. No one can stand him so they ALWAYS interfere in his matches, attack him from behind, smash his face in with weapons, and various other evil things. Yet, Missing Linq, the man formerly known as Delinquent, has proved that he is a superstar in the USCW. When he first came as Delinquent, he joined Da Lench Mob. He decided they weren't worth anything so he made a team with Jacob Yoder, soon known as Hostile Amish. Both of Delinquents friends, David Dobouis and Violent Vermin, came and joined with DLM, but as the leader Delinquent is, he took action and got them to betray DLM and join Hostile Amish. At the time, Hostile Amish was an unstoppable force. When in Hostile Amish, Delinquent beat Viper TWICE in a row and once for the World Title. His glory started and was finally noticed. But then later, a devastating blow was threw towards all the former DLM members. Jacob Yoder betray them all and joined forces with Viper. After that, Delinquent fought the stable leader, Jacob Yoder, and lost his belt. Then later, for some odd reason, Yoder and Viper both left USCW. That goes back to Scott Dakota for the belt and why it was chosen to have the Battle Royals as USCW watchers may remember. Soon, another stable started up called the Regulators. Delinquent, Shawn Evans, and Mike Adams started and as usual, Violent Vermin and David Dobouis joined. At this time, EVERYONE attacked Delinquent making him lose matches, miss matches, and go to the hospital. Finally, Delinquent got sick of all the crap he was taking so he left the Regulators for a short time but remains an ally and changed his name to the Missing Linq. He now seeks revenge on the ones who have attacked him, and caused harm to him. It is no doubt that Missing Linq is another one of the greats who has had to work the hard way for everything. He is now seeking out for the World Title belt which he was jipped out of a couple weeks ago. Foes of Missing Linq. Beware. This man has proved he is something and that he'll never be a nobody. I'm the PREMIERE Light HeavyWeight in this sport today, and Juice is just an illegal alien who needs a greencard. I will be the USCW World Champion before all of this is said and done. Don't take that as a threat, a promise or anything of the sort, take it as Fact. NO ONE can hold me down, and anyone who gets in my way WILL be Played Out!
