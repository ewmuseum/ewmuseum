Midweek Madness

17 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
USCW MIDWEEK MADNESS!!!! (The Faces of Scott Dakota and David Dobouis flash up on the screen.) Voice: The two undefeated dominators of the USCW. (The faces of Delinquent and Cold Blooded Nitrogen slam onto the screen and a cage drops in front of the pictures.) Voice: Two men, one bent on revenge, the other on making an example. (CBN attacking Delinquent from behind in his match with Jacob Yoder Flashes across the screen in slow motion.) Voice: And they have decided to settle their differences behind the bars of steel. (The USCW Logo comes spinning towards the screen and explodes as it comes to a stand still. It switches to the arena which is jam packed for this weeks Midweek Madness. The huge crowd roars in approval as Tiger Sagadon and Bill Roberts come out of the back and walk to the ring. Both men take a seat next to Lance Brown, but not before calling out to their fans. In the ring a light show flashes to all sides of the arena and the camera switches back to the announcers table where Lance Brown sits between Tiger and Bill Roberts.) Lance: Welcome everyone to a special edition of USCW Midweek Madness! Normally Midweek is a house show, scheduled all around the United States. This week, due to a very special main event, two of the most anticipated matches in the USCW history will be shown to you LIVE! Tiger: Scott Dakota, one of the men that has truly impressed me with his skills and his obvious class in the ring will defend his North American Title AND his Undefeated 6-0 record against the unstoppable leader of the Regulators, David Dobouis. Bill: On top of that we have Delinquent of the regulators going up against a man I know very well, cause I pinned him in the middle of the ring! None the less, he is one of the best wrestlers in the world today, as much as I hate to say it, Cold Blooded Nitrogen goes into the cage as well! Lance: Before we go into our special live matches, lets go through the recaps of what happened earlier in the card. Here is a summary and the results of each of the other matches from earlier tonight. ****************** The Bishop defeated Elwood J. Blues by DQ. Both the veteran USCW wrestler the Bishop and USCW newcomer Elwood Blues showed great improvement over their last matches. Elwood was far more focused for this match and took it to Bishop early. It looked as though Elwood would pull of his first win against the more seasoned man, but the Bishop rolled to the outside just as Elwood was about to put him away. Elwood became frustrated as Bishop took his time recuperating on the outside. The younger superstar went to the outside and got a bit too over zealous as he attacked Bishop with a chair, resulting in a DQ. Because of this ending, the USCW committee is scheduling a rematch at a future date. "The Player" Jason Polo pinned Juice after a tilt-a-wirl Tombstone. The rookie came on REAL strong. He hammered Juice all around the ring and dominated most of the match. Juice began to get off a decent offense towards the end, but the rookie proved to be too much. Polo took Juice to the limit, and then seemed to play with the more season wrestler, not putting him away when he could have. In the end Polo put the exhausted Juice down for the three count with an impressive Tilt-a-wirl Tombstone pile driver. Joe Mills pinned Shining Path. Shining Path, the first ever USCW T.V. champion, just did not have what it took to take down the red hot master of hardcore wrestling Joe Mills. Mills did not care what he did to himself as he dove over the top ropes and through tables on the attack. Shining Path never seemed to adjust to Mills almost insane style, but did take the advantage when the two finally staid in the ring. When it looked like Shining Path may just take the win, a masked man came out of the crowd and shattered a glass bottle over the Terrorists head when he ref's back was turned. Mills then put him down with the Hardcore Power Bomb for the three count. MIke Adams defeated Tony Dylan via submission *NEW USCW TELEVISION CHAMPION!* This did not turn out to be a good night for Moral Majority as the second man from that stable to hold the USCW television title lost his belt to the powerful Regulator. Mike Adams fought the best match of his life and Tony Dylan just could not match him. Adams focused on Dylans back for most of the match and managed to lock on the Gunlock at the end. Dylan was just too far from the ropes and was forced to submit under the pressure of the texas cloverleaf. Violent Vermin pinned "Ladies Lover" Jeff Gillis after an extremely close match. Violent Vermin showed that he is a man that means to make it in the USCW as he barley edged a win over one of the top stars in the USCW, one half of the World tag team champions, Jeff Gillis. Gillis did not go down easy, as the two fought a see saw match from beginning to end. When Vermin began to take control, Joe Mills came to the ring to assist his partner. Mills waffled Vermin in the back of the head with a chair, but Vermin managed to kick out just before the three to the surprise of both members of the X-treme Experience. Shining Path then came to the ring to help out his partner and got a little revenge on Joe Mills as he drop kicked the chair into Joe Mills's face. Vermin then hit Gillis from behind with a big German suplex while Gillis was distracted for the three count. Afterwards all four men brawled in the ring till the rest of the Regulators came to Vermins aid and cleared the ring. ***************** Lance: What an awesome card it has been so far! We have already had a title change tonight as Adams walks away with the Television title. Bill: He and the rest of the Regulators are getting a bit too strong. If the SWA was still around right now I can tell you where all the titles would be sitting... Tiger: But their not here anymore. The cowards turned tail and ran... Bill: I'll make you eat those words you dirty bast...... Lance: OK!
