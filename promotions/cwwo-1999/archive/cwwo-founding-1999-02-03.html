Check This Out! Innovative New Fed Idea

3 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, I know I probably got you to read this against the wrong subject, but please, if you are a free agent, or even if you aren't. Please just look over this info pack or go to
http://members.aol.com/apoc9899/cwwo.html and check out the page. Please, you will be surprised at the kind of fed this is. It is great, we just need members, thank you...
========================== ---CWWO Information Page--- I. Introduction Hello, my name is Ken, and I will be your CWWO, Cyber World Wrestling
Organization, President and I expect a great fun, exciting, and productive time to come from this federation and I hope it is good for the newcomers and the elite role-players also...
Well, as I said, my name is Ken, and I have been a part of the wonderful world of e-wrestling for about 2 years now, ever since I got my new computer. I stumbled upon a small, simmed fed by the name of the XWA(now XEWA) and everything on the internet changed. I found myself going on AOL much MUCH more at the start, but then I calmed down and got a life again. :) But that's beside the point, what I am trying to get at is the fact that e-wrestling can be fun, it can be time-consuming, but it can be a great time if you are involved and work hard. I don't care if you don't put out 5-6 role-plays a week, in fact, I don't really want that much from ONE person. But what I do want is at least 1 role-play a week and about 6 a day from the whole fed combined. I have tried to run but one fed already but that shutdown when NO ONE role-played. I need people to role-play to keep the fed running, please help me out, and I will make the CWWO worthwhile...I promise.I also want you to have a life, please, have FUN, have a life, but don't waste my time writing cards and matches and stuff if no one is working, as a matter of fact, I won't do it at all if no one works, ok? Good...
II. Role-playing Rules Role-playing is going to be done to the whole federation, I hope, and if they aren't, it'll simply be the demise of the CWWO, as I noted above. But anyhow, here are the short, simple rules...
Role-plays will be sent to me, and I will send them to the whole fed, BCC, so no one starts junk mailing, etc. I will be sure to attach the FWD E-mail
Address unless specified so stable leaders can notify the person if they want them to join.
Rule #1: A rule that all of you know. QUALITY OVER QUANTITY! If someone sends me 500 RPs that only say "I'm gonna whoop you're ass because I can", and someone else sends me a very intriguing, medium-length role-play that actually describes something and goes somewhere then you tell me who should win...next rule...
Rule #2: Cursing will be allowed ONLY to a certain extreme. Please no F word, that is the only word that can not be used. It may only be used with a F*#$ or
F[bleep] or simply a ((censored)) All of those will be accepted other than the actual word. If you are seen using this in a role-play you will be warned. Two warnings=Suspension. 2 Suspensions=Fired on the Spot!
Rule #3: For a role-play to count towards a match it must be sent before the role-play deadline. This way no one will get cheated because not everyone's life
is online. So this helps all the members. It must be sent before or it won't count. That's about it and now I will go over the CWWO Nightly Shows... III.CWWO Weekly Shows The CWWO will have one weekly card... but it'll be a big one! Monday Night Madness--- The big card of the week with commentating by Frank
Fuller and Ryan Steen. It will feature about 9 matches each card and some interviews if wrestlers want to do them and such. This is the main card of the week and will be in play-by-play format.
House Show--- Occasionally the CWWO will have House Shows with only the results posted and the move to finish because of overwhelming challenges or other occasions. The results of these matches, unlike real life, will count towards the rankings, records, and belt holding situations in the rare occasion of a belt match at the House Show.
IV. CWWO Pay-Per-Views Well, there will be one pay-per-view a month, on a Sunday, that will end all feuds, angles, and start new angles, feuds, etc. These are the biggest cards of the year. And the titles will vary, but here are some definite PPV
Titles.
January-- CWWO: New Year's Revolution_Basic PPV for CWWO February---Random March--- Clash of the Titans_Tag Team Tournament for the shot at the Tag Titles at the end of the night or a singles tournament for a shot at the World Title the same night in an "I Quit" Match. Plus a couple of other, non-significant matches
April---Random May--- Mayday '99_ Rumble for the shot at the World Title holder later that night! BIG PPV!
June---Random July--- Title Wave_All titles on the line. All tournaments for them. One man will emerge the World Champ. Who will it be? Find out at this BIG CWWO
Pay-Per-View August--- Random September--- CWWO: Summer's Over_ Basic PPV with a bunch of Title matches, etc. October--- Halloween Horror Night_Hardcore matches, All matches have no rules and the world champ MUST fight in a cage match with barbed wired fence against the Number 1 contender for the world belt! Big PPV!
November---Random December---CWWO: Here Comes Santa Claus..._Basic PPV with title matches, feud endings, and the start of new angles, etc.
V. Titles ---Singles Titles--- CWWO World Title---The most prestigious title in the CWWO. Defended at least once a month(At PPV)
CWWO Internet Title---The second best singles title in the CWWO. Must be defended at least 2 times a month.
CWWO Hardcore Title---The best hardcore title ever. Only gimmick matches. Defended at least once a month. CWWO Television Title---Last but not least title in the CWWO. Defended not every week, but at least 3 times a month probably.
There is no cruiserweight title as of yet because I see no need for one as most people are heavyweights. If I get enough input from cruisers, there WILL BE a cruiser title.
---Tag Titles--- CWWO World Tag Team Titles--Defended once a month, probably, if there is enough fighting with tag teams. I will not start this title off in the beginning unless I have enough tag teams to make it interesting.
