CCW Wednesday Night Fight (A look back at last weeks action between Raven/Kid) Drake Austin>Hello folks and welcome to CCW Wednesday Night Fight, tonight
I have a new broadcast partner Mr. Michael Smith, say hello Michael.
Michael Smith>Hello! DA>We have a great card of action tonight so lets get to the ring for some action. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- Shawn MIchaels vs. 2 Cold Scorpio(non-title) RA>First making his way to the ring from San Antonio, Texas, weighing 234 lbs., and the man who will face Ric Flair this Sunday for the World
Heavyweight Title, "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels.(The fans go crazy, standing ovation as Shawn does his little strip in the ring). And his opponent from Denver, Colarado, weighing a chiseld 235 lbs. and current Intercontinental Champion, 2 Cold
Scorpio.(The crowd had a mixed reaction to him, most fans booed, but he did have some fans here.)
****Ding***Ding***Ding**** MS>Looks as if Michaels is going to start this one off, he whips Scorpio into the ropes, Scorpio comes off and ducks a clothesline from Shawn,
Shawn follows it up with a bounce off the ropes but is met with a SUPERKICK from 2 Cold, he goes for the cover, but only got a two count..
DA>That's right and look 2 cold is taking control now he hits Michaels with a POWERBOMB, and now he's climbing to the top turnbuckle, looks to be DA BOMB... MS>No! Shawn moved out of the way, Shawn picks up 2 Cold and delivers a
PILEDRIVER, now he's going to the top turnbuckle and he flies off with a
ELBOW DROP, he goes for the cover and he got him...
DA>No he didn't that was a two and a half count. Shawn thinks he has won it, Scorpio is getting to his feet...
MS>He's bouncing off the ropes and coming towards Shawn, he sees it and hits him with the SUPERKICK and goes for the cover.
DA>He won it, I don't believe it that jerk just won it. ****Ding***Ding***Ding**** RA>Here is your winner "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels.(The crowd goes crazy again.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- DA>Here is that interview with Hulk Hogan that we promised you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- Interview with Hulk Hogan Brian Wright:I like to say it's an honor to be in the presences of an legend like you. Hulk Hogan:Well thanks. BW:First question, why did you sign the texas death match against Sabu? HH:Well first let me say that I can't believe that Shawn Michaels will be getting the World Title shot against the Nature Boy this Sunday. And to your question, when I had my match with Shawn Michaels, I had the man pinned 123 but Sabu ran into the ring and attacked him before I could get the three count, which caused me to get DQ'ed and leaving him getting the title shot, so I figured what the power of Hulkamania needs is a big push into the more extreme scene, so
who better to go for then Sabu. BW:But is it not true that in this match there's no DQ's and no countouts? HH:That's right, but since you seem to be the only person in this miserible excuse for a federation that actually cares about the training, the vitimins, & the prayers, I have a surprise for you, the loser of the match must retire and trust me I don't plan on leaving anytime soon.
BW:That's a bold move on your behalf, but many will say that Hogan will never wrestle after this match anyway and now it coud be true, you Sabu has been known to cripple people.
HH:You know I thought of that and I figured that I overcame Ric Flair in the WCW, Sid in the WWF, & Vader in the WCW as well, so Sabu couldn't be any tougher then Vader.
BW:Well I would like to thank Hulk Hogan for giving us this interview, and good luck against Sabu.
HH:Thank you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- DA>Let's look at the card for Survior Series which happens this Sunday. The Survior Series '95 1.CCW World Champion "Nature Boy" Ric Flair vs. "Heartbreak Kid" Shawn
Michaels 2.CCW Intercontinental Champion 2 Cold Scorpio vs. Bret "Hitman" Hart 3.Texas Daeth Match where Loser has to Leave wrestling forever "The Immortal" Hulk Hogan vs. Sabu 4.CCW United States Champion Sting vs. Hunter Hearst-Helmsley 5.Tag Team Elimination Match Razor Ramon & 123 Kid vs. Raven & Cactus Jack 6.CCW Tag Champs The Public Enemy vs. Harlem Heat 7.Taz vs. A Mystery Wrestler 8.Steiner Brothers vs. Kama & Sid MS>Looks to be a great card but I'm being informed were ready for our next match. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- RA>First making his way to ringside from Short Hills, New Jersey, weighing
235 lbs., and current CCW Television Champion, Raven. And in his corner the wild man of wrestling Cactus Jack.(Raven does the crucifix thing in the ring while the fans boo, while Cactus walks around the ring going bang bang.) And his opponent from Miami, Florida, weighing 287 lbs., "The Bad
Guy" Razor Ramon. And in his corner the 123 Kid.
****Ding***Ding***Ding**** MS>Looks like there arguing in the ring, Ramon just slapped Raven, Raven just kicked Ramon in the you know what, Ramon bend down holding himself,
Raven grabs his neck and executes a beautiful NECK BREAKER....
DA>Now he's whipping him into the ropes and hits a POWERSLAM. You know how this all began don't you?(Raven & Ramon switch rest holds for a while)
MS>No I don't DA>Well Raven was tagging with Ramon against Bunkhouse Buck & Dick Slater in a wild texas death match, Raven double-crossed Ramon setting him up for a legdrop through a table, that night he brought Cactus into the fed and they destroyed Ramon sending him to the hospital with broken ribs, three months later Ramon returned to action and faced the Television champ Steve
Richards, who was one of Ravens friends, he defeated Richards and walked out with the TV belt, at Halloween Havoc he defeated Cactus Jack in a falls count anywhere match, and was almost sent back to the hospital, but the Kid made the save. The week after Halloween Havoc Ramon faced Raven and due to outside interference from Cactus Jack he lost the title to
Raven. Then last week Raven defended the title against The Kid and was successful.
MS>ooohhh..Ramon just threw Raven into the ropes and nailed a CHOKESLAM,
Ramon goes for the cover but only gets a two count.
DA>Look Catus is on the ring apperon and the ref is going over to get him down..... MS>Look Ramon is setting Raven up in the RAZOR'S EDGE.... DA>Look the Kid is getting in the ring, he hits Ramon in the midsection with a swift kick, Ramon drops Raven, the Kid nails Ramon in the head with a ROUNDHOUSE KICK, now look Raven is getting to his feet, he delivers a DDT and goes for the cover....
MS>He gets it and retains the TV title, but what was up with the Kid. ****Ding***Ding***Ding**** RA>Here is your winner and still CCW Television Champion Raven.(after the match the Kid walks over to hug Raven and Cactus, and they walk back to the locker room together)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- DA>Let's cut back to the locker room where we can get a word from Raven &
Ramon. Raven:Razor Ramon, I feel your pain, Cactus Jack feels your pain, and as you just seen the 123 Kid feels your pain as well. Ramon the question that bothers me is since everyone has left you high & dry who will be your tag team partner this Sunday. Quoth the Raven. (does the crucifix thing in front of the camera)
MS>Looks to me that Razor doesn't have a tag team partner for this Sunday now, we will try to get comments from Ramon before the end of the broadcast. But let's get to our main event.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- RA>This is the main event for tonight. First making his way to ringside from....(stevie graps the mike) Steve Richards>Listen up folks, I'm no pussy and to prove it I will guarante a win over Sting tonight, if I don't
I'll leave this stupid ass fedretaion.(drops the mike down) and his opponent from Venice Beach, California, weighing 260 lbs., and the current
CCW United States Champion, this is Sting.
****Ding***Ding***Ding**** MS>Can you believe Richards put his wrestling career on the line... DA>No I don't and I think it's a bad move to, wait look Richards is cussing out a fan that called him a pu$$y, Sting whips him into the opposite corner and flies across and hits him with a STINGER SPLASH, he's going for the cover...
MS>He's got it, Richards has to leave the CCW. ****Ding***Ding***Ding**** RA>Here is your winner an still CCW US Champion Sting, as Richards declared earlier he now has to leave the CCW. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------- MS>Looks like Stevie want be around no more in the CCW.... DA>Sorry Michael, but we have just got comments from Razor, so we leave you with the comment s from Razor. So long folks and see you Sunday.
Razor Ramon:Chico, after Sunday when I get my hands on you two, you can feel your own pain, and little so-called buddy when I get through with these two chicos, I'm coming after you. And about my partner this Sunday, if I can't find one by then I'll face the both of you myself.(throws a toothpick at the camera)
