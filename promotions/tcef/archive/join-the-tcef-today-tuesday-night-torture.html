Join the TCEF today! 20 wrestlers and counting!

May 7, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thats right folks, the up and coming BEST e-wrestling fed on the entire Internet is growing fast! Get in and be an active wrestler, and glory awaits you! Titles will be up for grabs very soon, so get an application at http://members.aol.com/tcefweb/tcef.html and check out the action with this card, our first ever, from MSG it's Tuesday Night Torture... ----------------------------------------------------------------- Welcome, E-Wrestling fans, to the TCEF's first televised card...it's *TUESDAY NIGHT TORTURE* Elliot : Welcome everyone to TCEF Tuesday Night Torture, LIVE from the World's most famous sports arena, Madison Square Garden. It's a packed house here at the first ever card run in the TCEF, and we've got a lot of action waiting for you tonight. I'm Elliot Nicodemi, joined by Lee DiGeorge. Lee : Just great to be here Elliot, and the sellout crowd is totally electrified. The wrestlers you see here tonite could become the first legends of the TCEF, while others will just become stains on the floor. Let's get right down to action... ------------------------------------------------- Tetsuo: The Iron Man VS "Stone Cold" Steve Austin ------------------------------------------------- Elliot : And first entering the ring is Tetsuo, a martial arts master. At 5'10, 213 pounds, he's a conditioned, chiseled fighter. You can see the fire in his eyes as he stalks towards the ring, with the theme music of the WWF superstar Hakushi playing as he enters. The crowd is reacting with a mixture of cheers and boos, since this is one of the newer faces in the world of E-Wrestling. Well he's in the ring, and he looks primed and ready... Lee : And the crowd explodes, as in walks Stone Cold Steve Austin! He's also got that fierce, determined look. I can see a lot of fans holding up those damn 3:16 signs, and OH! Tetsuo kicks Austin in the head as he enters the ring. This one is underway! *** WINNER : Tetsuo, The Iron Man *** Elliot : And an impressive performance by both men on their TCEF debuts. That was a terrific, back and forth power struggle, and it's just a hint at what's to come on TnT....Stone Cold has finally staggered off, and here goes the next match... Lee : That's right folks, no corny pauses or commercials here in TCEF, here come the next two competitors... -------------- Blade VS Raven -------------- *** WINNER : Blade *** ----------------------- Vision VS MC Adam Yauch ----------------------- *** WINNER : Vision *** --------------------- Awesome Kong VS Sting --------------------- *** WINNER : Awesome Kong *** Lee : Indeed, well we're taking a short break before the second half of our unbelievable program, including the main event. Two TRUE dark lords, Reaper v. Destroyer go at it. Stay tuned... End Part I ----------------------------------------------------------------- Elliot : Welcome back fans, to TCEF TnT. This half will have three high-intensity, action-packed matches. But ENOUGH talk! Let's get down to it... ------------------------ Mr. Untouchable VS Rebel ------------------------ *** WINNER : Mr. Untouchable *** -------------------------- Romeo Renolds VS Offspring -------------------------- *** Winner : Romeo Renolds *** Elliot : Ok we'll wait a bit for the ring to be cleared, and get ready for our MAIN EVENT! --------------------------- The Reaper VS The Destroyer --------------------------- Elliot : It's the F'N president Sean McNally, he's running down to ringside, holding some paper and a mic...he's climbing in! Is he nuts? I don't know how long he can stay out of the fighters' ways! Ok he's about to speak, maybe enlighten us on this carnage... F'N Pres. McNally : Ladies and Gentlemen, this match wasn't really a match, but there's no way in hell we're letting this one end like this! I have in my hand the contract for a no-holds barred CHAOTIC CASKET MATCH for next week's TnT! I will fax both men copies of the contract to their agents to sign. Next week, HERE on TnT, a casket match to end ALL casket matches... Elliot : and McNally wisely running twice as fast back towards the dressing room. Well you heard it here! Next week, same time, at Boston Gardens, it's Destroyer and Reaper in a casket match, plus much more! Lee : I can hardly wait! For Elliot "Styles" Nicodemi, and F'N President McNally, I'm King Lee DiGeorge, thanx for joining us here on TCEF's first ever TnT!!!! (Fades with security still restraining fighters) Copyright TCEF 1997
