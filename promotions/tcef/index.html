Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Total Chaos E-Wrestling Federation announced its website on 24 April 1997 with a one-line post; a follow-up two weeks later claimed two cards already run and “growing stronger every day,” with titles “up for grabs in days.”

Its first televised card, Tuesday Night Torture, billed itself as live from Madison Square Garden and ran seven matches without a commercial break, its roster mixing invented wrestlers with characters portraying real ones, including “Stone Cold” Steve Austin, Raven and Sting.

The main event never reached a finish. As Reaper and Destroyer fought, the fed's own on-screen F'N President ran to ringside with a contract in hand, announced a no-holds-barred “CHAOTIC CASKET MATCH” between the two men for the following week, and fled back up the aisle as security held the wrestlers apart.

No further card or result survives; the fed's last dated trace, the 4 May 1997 recruitment post, still promised the rematch was coming.
