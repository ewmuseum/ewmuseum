"The Realman" Sean Christopher — Flash

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a flash I did for Sunshine State Wrestling...please critique..all cmments are welcome...
================ [ Camera opens to a bright sun..as the camera pans down from the sun slowly you can tell it's a rare warm day out. The camera continues to pan down and shows some upper class houses. As the camera still continues to pan down it ends up in a back yard of a house. The house is surrounded by fresh green trees and bushes. The camera now begins to pan to the side and it shows various features of the house and stops in front of a pool. The pool is full of nice clear blue water. The camera pans into a close-up and shows some lady swimming in the pool...also a man sitting in front of the pool.
The man is dressed in only dark blue swim shorts and has a white towel drapped over him. The man seems to be a little wet as if he just got out of the pool. The camera pans closer to the guy and stops on some initials on the towel...the initials are SC. The guy takes a sip of some juice that is sitting on a table besides the beach chair]
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: Excellence..such a small simple word yet overused by many people. Perfection another word that means so much yet it's meaning has been cheapened by overuse.
[Sean takes the towel which was covering his legs and wipes his face with it as he continues]
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: "Excellent" Evan Andrews...your one of the many people tarnishing the word...excellent. Take a look at the dictionary and look up the word excellent...none...NONE...of those definitions come close to discribing you.
[Sean gets up from his beach chair and walks over to the pool. He sits at the pools edge and hangs his feet in the water.}
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: Perfection...now you look that word up in the dictionary and what do you have? You have a pictuare of yours truly right next to it. The first definition listed is...SEE SEAN CHRISTOPHER. Evan Andrews I heard you say you know nothing about me...well my friend that works out well because I dont know much about you and I REALLY DONT CARE. The only thing I need to know about you that I already know and that's that come this Sunday you'll be the first mark on my win column.
[Sean jumps into the pool and starts swimming. The lady that was already in the pool swims next to Sean and gives him a kiss.]
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: Last week the world..well actualy Antwan Carter and Shane Diamond felt the debut of Sean Christopher. If anybody wants to know what it fills like to go up against The Realman ask Shane Diamond. He felt the chair, he flet the Chris-Cross, and he left the SSW. Antwan you also came face to face with The Realman now take all the credit you want for beating Shane
Diamond but deep down you know the truth...YOU OWE ME..
[Sean takes a couple of laps around the pool then stops to take a breath. He looks up at the sun then smiles]
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: The SSW...Sunshine State Wrestling...a big step away from my home here in the Hills of Beverly...but as the sun shines down on me here...the spotlight will shine down on me in the Sunshine State..be it Fang, Antwan Carter, or Evan Andrews they're all just names compared to THE
ONE NAME THAT MEANS IT ALL...SEAN CHRISTOPHER. [Sean pulls himself out of the pool and again hangs his feet over the side. The young lady who was swimming in the pool also pulls heself out and sits next to Sean. The lady has on a green bikini which leaves little to the imagination. She has blond hair and blue eyes which shines in the sunlight.
The lady gives Sean another kiss.] "The Realman" Sean Christopher: Hey Shannon you up to a game of word assocation? [The lady wraps herself in Seans towel then begins to talk] Shannon: sure my love...give me a word... "The Realman" Sean Christopher: ok...hmmm...Excellence? Shannon: Sean... "The Realman" Sean Christopher: Sean.... Shannon: Perfection... "The Realman" Sean Christopher: Perfection.... Shannon: Sean.... [Sean laughs then gives Shannon a kiss. Shannon stands up and dries herself off..she gives Sean a hug before walking inside the house.]
"The Realman" Sean Christopher: Evan Andrews..no matter which way you cut it..come this Sunday..I'll show you why I am The Realman and the true meaning of excellent perfection...because
