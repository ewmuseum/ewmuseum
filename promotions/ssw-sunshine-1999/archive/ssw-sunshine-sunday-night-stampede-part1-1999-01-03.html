A voice is heard talking.] V/O - Last week the SSW made a HUGE impact in the world of wrestling! [Shows Dusty Love hitting Antawn Carter over the head with a steel chair.] V/O - Stars were made! [Shows Alexander Munroe pinning Tyrone Parker.] V/O - And unexpected surprises took place! [Shows Shawn Sylkk standing in the center of the ring.] V/O - And an old feud was reheated! [Shows Evan Manchester laying the PWU World belt across the chest of Jack
Vars.] V/O - This week will be no different! [Shows a split screen of Shawn Sylkk and Sean Holloway.] V/O - Tonight 7 matches of jam packed action and at least one surprise GUARANTEED!!! ALL THIS AND MUCH MORE ON SSW SUNDAY NIGHT STAMPEDE!!! [The camera then explodes into the jam packed SSW Pavilion. Every single fan is up on their feet chanting "SSW! SSW! SSW!" The camera then fades into a sea of signs which such signs as *DUSTY LOVE SUCKS* and another one is seen saying
*RIP HIGOR!!*....] [All of a sudden, the tune of "PARANOID" by OZZY OSBOURNE begins to blare over the loudspeakers, interrupting the intro to Stampede. The fans rise to their feet and react with a huge ovation of boos. Then, the curtain is pushed aside by a hand that sports not only a gold Rolex watch studded with diamonds but a huge ring with the initials "DL", also filled in with diamonds...]
OO: "FIN-ISHED WITH MY WO-MAN 'CAUSE SHE COULDN'T HELP ME WITH MY MIND!!!" [As the curtain is pushed away, it reveals a man that appeared in the Main
Event of last week's show, by attacking Antawan Carter. His shoulder-length, dirty-blonde hair is slicked back into a pony tail and his deep, dark, brown eyes are hidden behind a pair of John Lennon style Mossimo sunglasses... He's wearing a pair of Hilfiger blue jeans, a blue and dark-green button-up Hilfiger dress-shirt, and a pair of Armoni slipper-shoes...]
OO: "PEO-PLE THINK I'M IN-SANE BE-CAUSE I AM FROWN-ING ALL THE TIME!!!" [The scruffy beard, the round lense sunglasses, the very nice clothes, and by the overwhelming amount of jeers and boos coming from the crowd ... This could be only one man...]
OO: "ALL DAY LONG I THINK OF THINGS BUT NOTH-ING SEEMS TO SAT-IS-FY!!! THINK I'LL LOSE MY MIND IF I DON'T FIND SOME- THING TO PAC-I-FY!!!" ________ __ \____ \ __ __ ____ __ / /__ __ __ \ \\ \ \ \ \ \ \ _//__ __/ / / / / ___/ // / / / / / __\ \ / / / \/ / \_______/ /____/ /____/ /__/ \ / / / ______________________/ / __\ " T H E L O V E M A N "/ \ \ ____ __ __ ____ \ \ \ \ \ \ / / \ __\ / /____ \ \\ \ \ \/ / \ _\ /______/ /____/ \___/ /____/ OO: "CAN YOU HELP ME!?! CUT YOU FROM MY B-RAIN!!! WO-YEAH!!!" [Dusty Love, "The Love Man", walces down to the ring and appears to be very laid back tonight. He makes his way down to the ring with most of the women in attendance going wild, and just about _ALL_ of the men booing their lungs out.
He stops when he's half-way down the isle... He looks out to the fans and smirks. He spends the next few seconds basking in their hatred for him. He now continues his walk to the ring. He climbs up the ring-steps and climbs between the ropes and into the ring...]
OO: "I NEED SOME-ONE TO SHOW ME THAT LEAD-ING LIGHT THAT I CAN'T FIND!!! I CAN'T SEE THE THINGS THAT MAKE TRUE HAP-PI- NESS I MUST BE BLIND!!!" [The lights dim down a little as Dusty Love stands in the middle of the ring rubbing his chin. Then, fireworks shoot off above the ring and in the entrance way. The fireworks, of pink and white colors, blast off for a few seconds before the grand finale... A pink explosive blows up out of each of the turnbuckles. Dusty Love then climbs to the middle rope and looks out to the sea of Florida fans...]
OO: "MAKE A JOKE AND I WILL SIGH AND YOU WILL LAUGH AND I WILL CRY!!! HAP-PI-NESS A-GAIN I FEEL AND LOVE TO ME IS SO UN- REAL!!!" ["The Love Man" jumps off the middle rope and steps back into the center of the ring... In the limelight. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a microphone... The song now nears it's end...]
OO: "AND SO AS YOU HEAR THESE WORDS TELL-ING YOU NOW OF MY STATE!!! I TELL YOU TO EN-JOY LIFE I WISH I COULD BUT IT'S TOO LATE!!!" [Dusty Love pulls the mic. up to his mouth as the song begins to fade out. The fans erupt into boos, once again, as he speaks, live and in living color, on Sunday Night Stampede...]
DL: [Takes a deep breath...] Aaah... There's only one thing that I hate more than the state of Florida... And that's the Florida fans!
[The fans get even louder with their jeers... Dusty Love just smiles from ear to ear and continues after a slight pause...]
DL: But last week was the biggest night in the Sunshine State Wrasslin's short history. No, not because it was their very first show. Oh no...
It's because "The Love Man" himself decided to grace the entire state of Florida with his very presence. And it looks like you all have the privilage of me bein' here, once again.
[Dusty smiles again... He looks over the fans and continues on...] DL: But there's one thing about it... If it weren't for the truck loads of money that I'm gettin' paid, I wouldn't be here. But in my humble opinion, the only sun that's shining in the Sunshine State Wrasslin'... Is their one and only star that's shinin' brighter than ever... Yours truely...
"Dangerous" Dusty Love! [The fans boo louder than before... And now we see a few wads of garbage fly into the ring...]
DL: But enough of the chatting. I don't think I can stand the sight of you fans much longer, so I'll get down to the business at hand. Most of you have questions, and like always, "The Love Man" has all the answers. First of all, I bet everyone wants to know why I came out and hit Antawan Carter with a chair, right? Well, the answer's just as simple as the question itself... Because I _CAN!_ I don't follow anyone's rules and to be honest with ya'... I _NEVER_ have and _NEVER_ will. Which leads me to the next line of business... The SSW's tournament that will crown their first Champion.
Ya' see... I was lookin' over the brackets just the other day, and I realized that I wasn't in there anywhere. [Fans cheer...] Yeah, yeah, yeah... Well, like I said, I was lookin' it over and I wasn't listed on it.
So, I gave Chris Smith, the SSW Commissioner, a call. I asked him why and he told me that I didn't sign in time or some kinda' excuse like that. So, it looks like I won't have that chance at bein' Champion...
[Fans cheer, once again, because Dusty Love won't have a chance to be Champion...] DL: U N T I L N O W ! ! ! [Dusty Love pulls a piece of rolled up paper out of his back pocket... He unrolls it and holds it up to the fans...]
DL: Y'see... Chris Smith and the entire state of Florida... "The Love Man" _WILL_ be in that tournament because _THIS_ contract says so! Read tha' fine print, Smith! When I signed with the SSW, you guaranteed me that I'd be in this thing... Therefore, you have _NO_ choice but to put me in there somewhere! And believe me, if you dare test me, then you'll be payin' the dear, dear price for the rest of your lives... 'Cause Smith, you won't only lose your house, car, and all your money... But I'll _OWN_ this company, 'cause I'll sue you for everything ya' got! It's legal binding, people... And it's official... Dusty
Love is
you're _FIRST_ and _NEW_ Sunshine State Wrestling Heavyweight
Champion! [The fans roar with boos, once again...] DL: I'm just about to wrap this thing up... But don't worry, not one bit... 'Cause I'm not done here tonight. I'll be back... The thing is, you don't know when _OR_ where!
[Dusty Love just smiles and walks to the ropes... Just as he's about to exit the ring, he stops and walks back to the center of the ring...]
DL: Oh yeah! I almost forgot... One more thing before I go... [Dusty rips open his button up shirt... Revealing a T. Martin Tennessee
Volunteer jersey...]
DL: G O V O L S ! ! ! [Dusty Love begins to laugh, as the fans boo louder than ever... "PARANOID" by OZZY OSBOURNE begins to blare over the loudspeakers, once again... And
Dusty Love leaves the ring.]
