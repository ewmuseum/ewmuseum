Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sunshine State Wrestling's surviving record opens on 3 January 1999, mid-run: its own broadcast, Sunday Night Stampede, recapped a previous week already declared to have made “stars,” reheated an old feud, and promised “at least one surprise guaranteed” for the night ahead, to a Pavilion crowd in Orlando, Florida, chanting the promotion's own name.

That surprise arrived when “Dangerous” Dusty Love, left off the bracket for the promotion's own first Heavyweight Championship tournament, produced a contract he said guaranteed him a spot in it and used it to declare himself, with no match at all, “your first and new Sunshine State Wrestling Heavyweight Champion.”

A wrestler's own hand-written promo two weeks later, from “The Realman” Sean Christopher ahead of a match with Evan Andrews, recaps the same stretch's other business: a debut win Christopher claims over Shane Diamond (“he felt the chair, he felt the Chris-Cross, and he left the SSW”), and a still-open score with Antwan Carter, who had claimed credit for beating Diamond himself.

How the Heavyweight title tournament resolved, or whether Dusty Love's claimed championship ever stood, isn't in the surviving record.
