[AWO] Triple Battle Royal

1 June 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Triple Battle Royal continues: MM - We're just about ready for the singles Battle Royal. Heenan - Then let's go! HF - Ladies & Gentlemen, at this time, let me welcome you to our main event! This is a men's singles over-the-top-rope Battle Royal, the remaining 8 will fight it out in a Match Of Survival.
(Terminator 2 theme. Crowd boo.) All right, introducing first, from The Professor's Laboratory, weighing 784 pounds, accompanied by The Professor, The Diamond Android!
And, weighing 762 pounds, The Plutonium Android! (Gunfire sounds, crowd boo.) From Iraq, weighing 295 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by Kaiser
'The Commander' Hussain & his bodyguard, Powerbomb, The Mercenary! (A voice starts on about paying your taxes) From Washington, D.C., weighing 358 pounds, Ivan Roids Stare, also known as I.R.S! (Cries of pain) From Iraq, weighing 336 pounds, The New Iron Sheik!
(Razor's theme. Crowd boo.) From Parts Unknown, weighing 286 pounds, The Edge! (Strange, eerie music. Crowd boo.) From another dimension, accompanied down the aisle by his manager Dr
Nathaniel Darkness, weighing 275 pounds, InterDimensional Warrior!
(More eerie music. Crowd boo.) From Parts Unknown, accompanied down the aisle by his advisor, Oda, weighing 235 pounds, The Mystical One.
(Silence. Crowd go deathly quiet.) From Parts Unknown, weighing 280 pounds & accompanied down the aisle by The Darkling, X The Unknown!
(Arctic sounds. Crowd boo.) From the freezer, weighing 315 pounds, & accompanied down the aisle by his advisor, Mr Delgado, here is Stone Cold!
(Jake Roberts' Trust Me sounds. Crowd boo.) From his surgery, weighing 243 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his manager Fabulous Felix Fantasia, Dr Felix!
(Just Another Victim sounds. Crowd boo.) From San Antonio, Texas, weighing 220 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his motivator, Samantha Anderson, Mikey Michaels!
Heenan - She'd certainly motivate me! MM - I'm sure! (Sounds of an A-Bomb going off. Crowd boo.) HF - From Three Mile Island, weighing 356 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his owner Adam Bomb, Bombsquad!
(The Raven is being read over the tanoy. Crowd boo.) From Parts Unknown, weighing 230 pounds, The Warlock! (Silence. Crowd boo.) From Parts Unknown, accompanied down the aisle by his owner, Johnny
E, weighing 250 pounds, Ganja Man!
(The Messiah. Mixed reaction, mainly boos.) From Beverly Hills, weighing 242 pounds, Ric Flair! (Perfect's theme. Crowd boos.) From wherever's perfect, weighing 256 pounds, Mr Perfect! (Simply Ravishing. Crowd boos.) From San Antonio Texas, weighing 271 pounds, Ravishing Rick Rude! (Darkman theme. Crowd boos.) From The Dark Side, weighing 350 pounds, Darkman! (Doink's face music. Crowd cheers.) From Parts Unknown, weighing 275 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his valet Viper, The Jester!
(Simply The Best plays. Crowd cheers.) From New York City, weighing 400 pounds, accompanied by his main squeeze Cindy Crawford, The Best!
(Silence. Crowd cheers.) From Arabia, weighing 250 pounds, accompanied by his owner/business consultant/translator 'Gentleman' GL Miller, Shabata, The Arabian
Nightmare!
(Theme from 'The Crow'. Crowd go wild.) From The Darkest Night, weighing 250 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his controller, Mistress Elyxabetha, Darkness!
(Warrior's theme. Crowd go OTT.) From Parts Unknown, weighing 275 pounds, The Ultimate Warrior! (Undertaker music. Crowd almost explode.) From Death Valley, Nevada, weighing 328 pounds, accompanied down the aisle by his manager, Paul Bearer, The Undertaker!
(Last rites being read. Crowd continue to go berzerk.) From London, England, weighing 375 pounds, The Executioner! (Behind Executioner comes Shawn Hart.) Hart - (Taking microphone) Ladies & Gentlemen, the ring is becoming overfilled & therefore I will allow participants to enter only as others are eliminated. (Crowd boo slightly.)
(Ding, ding.) MM - I have to agree. Heenan - He's just a spoilsport. MM - Whoa! There go Plutonium & Diamond Androids! Heenan - Two more must come! MM - It's Midnight Marauder & Canadian Patriot Douglas George! Heenan - This may not be too much of an advantage! MM - There goes Midnight Marauder! Heenan - See what I mean? MM - Here comes Naplam Death. Heenan - You notice they're all fan favourites? MM - Stop whinging, Brain. You're like an old woman. Heenan - And you're married to one! MM - There go The Best & Bombsquad, eliminating each other! Heenan - I love it! No-one's touching Slick Ric! MM - Where is he? Heenan - Here come Comet & The Maple Leaf! MM - I still don't see Flair! Heenan - Well then, you have to be fair to Flair & say that only a man as fair as Flair would be fair & give everyone a chance to beat
Flair, Flair's so fair...
MM - Flair's not there! He's gone! Heenan - Can't be. We don't have his replacement! MM - There goes The Warlock. Heenan - And here comes the Pillow Stuffer! MM - Still not everyone's there! Heenan - Just like you, McMannequin, not all there! MM - Now over go Stone Cold, Executioner & Darkman! Heenan - FunkyMonkey, Bret 'Hitman' Hart & Scorpion to replace them! MM - Scorpion's in & out! Heenan - So with Big Poppa, that's everyone in! MM - This has got to be the quickest Battle Royal yet! Heenan - And The Undertaker & Comet just eliminated each other! MM - It's getting... New Iron Sheik eliminates I.R.S. ...it's getting quicker! Heenan - I'm getting worried about Slick Ric! MM - You're the only one! Heenan - What are we down to? MM - Perfect got Rude! I don't believe it! We're down to Mercenary,
Iron Sheik, The Edge, InterDimensional Warrior, Mystical One, X The
Unknown, Dr Felix, Ganja Man, Flair (I think), Perfect, The Jester,
Shabata, Darkness, Ultimate Warrior, Canadian Patriot, Naplam Death,
Maple Leaf, Pillow Stuffer, FunkyMonkey, Bret Hart, Mikey Michaels &
Big Poppa.
Heenan - While you were yakking, McMannequin, Perfect got Ganja Man,
The Mercenary & The Edge.
MM - Ganja Man is looking under the ring. He pulled out Ric Flair! Heenan - No, no, no! MM - I thought Flair was fair? Heenan - X The Unknown got Shabata! MM - And Darkness got Maple Leaf & Mikey Michaels Heenan - This is unbelievable! And the 4 horsemen are still in with a shot! MM - Certainly Brain! So are the rest! Ow! The Jester reverse-powerbombs Dr Felix out! Heenan - While The Jester was posing, Flair got him out! MM - And Darkness gets Mystical One, Iron Sheik, Naplam Death &
Pillow Stuffer in one go! Heenan - Wow! Incredible! MM - This has got to be the most dangerous Battle Royal yet! Heenan - Perfect & Maple Leaf gone thanks to Bret Hart! MM - Big Poppa gets rid of InterDimensional Warrior! It's over! (Ding, ding, ding!) HF - Here are your winners, X The Unknown, FunkyMonkey, Ric Flair,
Darkness, Canadian Patriot Douglas George, Bret Hart, Ultimate Warrior
& Big Poppa!
MM - I've just been told, due to time restraints, the Match Of Survival & rankings for USWO, AWA & EWF titles will be on Friday Night
Zone! So, for Bobby 'The Brain' Heenan I'm Vince McMannequin saying see you at Summer Sizzler!
