AWO (1995) — Show

[AWO] Raw

27 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AWO RAW Live from the AWO Arena in San Antonio, Texas. (Camera pans along the capacity crowd. Their banners are largely for Bob Holly, but one or two can be seen for Goldust, Luna & Chaparita Asari. One reads 'Marry me Luna'. A few others are around for other AWO Superstars.) Ross: Welcome ladies & gentlemen to the inaugral AWO RAW! And what a show tonight! Goldust takes on Bob 'Spark Plugg' Holly & Luna Vachon battles Chaparita Asari in a top contender's match.
Lawler: That's not all. We also hear comments from a number of AWO Superstars & a BIG exclusive. Ross: What exclusive King? What are you talking about? Lawler: OK, are you ready for this? Another title has been announced! Ross: What? Lawler: The Wildcard Tag Team Titles. Ross: Wildcard? What the hell are you talking about King? Lawler: Tag Teams are drawn at random to face each other, but you don't know who you'll be teaming with until the start of the match.
Ross: You mean random teams, King? So Lex Luger could end up teaming with, say, The Undertaker?
Lawler: Yes, and after one team is victorious, they move forward to the next round where AGAIN their tag partner is randomly selected. You never know who it'll be till Fink announces them.
Ross: That's great King. I guess we'll see that start next week. Lawler: Yes, it should be great. Ross: OK, let's go to comments from Sunny & the BodyDonnas. (Cut) Sunny: I got it! I got it! Ha-ha! I got it! HHH: Two champions, one stable. Wuld have been three, but Booger made the supreme sacrifice.
Booger: 1-2-3 Kid, you've got MY belt. Give it back or I'll squash you like a bug. Jacques: And as for you, Demolition, we want a shot at you too. We were never cleanly beaten. We DEMAND a title shot.
Raymond: In fact, you can pick ANY stipulation you want & we'll still beat you 1 2 3. Sunny: Luna & Conchita, or whatever the hell your name is, one of you pathetic people gets a shot at this (points to World Title) next week on Raw. Well BRING IT ON!
(Cut) Ross: They mean business, but for now let's take you to the Fink. Fink: Ladies & Gentlemen, this is our opening contest. It is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Talladega, Alabama, here is Bob
'Spark Plugg' Holly! (Crowd cheer) Lawler: I see he finally got out of that locker. Fink: And his opponent, from Hollywood, California, weighing 260 pounds, here is Goldust! (Crowd boo)
----- CLANG CLANG CLANG ----- Lawler: Look at this. Goldust just choke-slammed Holly. He's gonna get slaughtered. Ross: You may be right, King, as Goldust with a snap suplex, follows it up with a bodyslam.
Lawler: And now it's Goldust with Bob Holly in an abdominal stretch. Give it up, Holly. Ross: Who's this coming to ringside? OH MY! It's Giant Gonzalez from AWO Security. Lawler: What does that Neanderthalic wannabe want? Ross: The fans are liking this. He's slapping hands & signing autographs. Lawler: Goldust won't like that. Only Goldust signs autographs. Ross: And Goldust just threw Holly down & dropped a leg onto the back of his head. Bodyslam again.
Lawler: Gonzalez has made Goldust irate. He's got Holly up... BACKBREAKER! AND AGAIN! Ross: This is absolutely dispicable as Goldust with a third backbreaker, and into... HE'S RAKING HOLLY'S EYES!
Ref: 1,2,3,4... Lawler: Breaks it on 4. And right back. Ref: 1,2,3,4 Ross: He's taking it right out of Holly. Holly is bleeding. Ref: 1,2,3,4 Lawler: Goldust has scratched Holly so badly he's bleeding! Ross: Now he sends Holly over the top to the outside. Lawler: He landed with his head on the ring steps, Holly is in a bad way. Ross: But Giant Gonzalez is there to help Bob Holly up. He rolls Holly back in on the 8-count. Giant Gonzalez does NOT want Goldust in AWO.
Lawler: So what? I do. Come on, Goldust. Ross: But now it's Goldust with Holly up... CURTAIN CALL! Ref: 1,2... Lawler: GONZALEZ PUT HOLLY'S FOOT ON THE ROPES! Ross: And Goldust is upset. He's going out after Gonzalez. Lawler: Referee starts counting. Ref: 2,3 Ross: And Goldust getting waffled by Gonzalez. Snap suplex, and Goldust rolled back in the ring. Holly hooks the leg.
Ref: 1,2,3!! ----- CLANG CLANG CLANG ----- Fink: Here is your winner, Bob 'Spark Plugg' Holly! (Crowd cheer) Ross: Which means Holly gets an AWO contract. Lawler: Oh God. The two sport loser. Ross: With that in mind, let's go to In Your House Report IN YOUR HOUSE REPORT WITH MEAN GENE OKERLUND Gene: Hello and welcome to the first In Your House Report. In Your House occurs from Madison Square Garden on March 31st. The card is by no means complete & has NO MAXIMUM OF MATCHES! However, each wrestler may only wrestle once.
So far inked in is a World Title Match. It will pit Hunter Hearst Helmsley against THE FIRST NON-CHAMPION TO RESPOND in a Steel Cage match. We want all belts to be on the line, but who they're defended against is entirely up to you. The first to challenge gets the shot.
That's it for now, hopefully we'll have a few matches ready for next week. IN YOUR HOUSE REPORT WITH MEAN GENE OKERLUND Fink: This is our MAIN EVENT! (Crowd pop) Introducing first, coming down the aisle, here is Chaparita Asari. (Crowd cheer)
And her opponent, from The Other Side Of Darkness, here is Luna Vachon! (Crowd boo) ----- CLANG CLANG CLANG ----- Lawler: Did you see that sign? Marry me Luna. My sentiments entirely. Ross: I see as Luna starts off with a vicious flurry of kicks & scratches. This is not going to be a technical match-up if Luna is in control.
Lawler: No, I'll have to agree with you there but Chaparita cannot combat Luna for brawling. These two ladies have very dissimilar wrestling techniques.
Ross: Absolutely as Luna really laying in those kicks & chops. Whip to the far side. Lawler: He has a mask on. Ross: What? Lawler: That... OH! Missed avalanche by Luna... that fan of Luna's has a mask on. Ross: Probably doesn't want people to know of his poor taste in women. Chaparita Asari now in control, sets up Luna for the ride, into Chaparita's elbow. Cover... Ref: 1,2... Ross: Kick-out by Luna. Snap suplex by Chaparita, picks up Luna for a bodyslam, grabs Luna's arm & drops the leg. Ouch!
Lawler: I know him. I'm sure I know him. Ross: Shut up, King. Luna now rake to Chaparita's eyes. Sets her up for the ride. Leapfrog by... NO! Spinebuster slam...
Ref: 1,2... Ross: And a near-fall by Chaparita Asari. Luna is in trouble here. Another bodyslam. Luna in trouble, as Chaparita is up top... Asai moonsault, misses it. Lawler: Luna now on the second turbuckle. LUNA ECLIPSE! Ross: SHE MISSED IT! SHE MISSED IT! Chaparita up top for the Asai Moonsault... Hits it this time! Ref: 1,2,3!! ----- CLANG CLANG CLANG ----- Fink: Here is your winner, Chaparita Asari! Lawler: That fan just jumped over the barricades. He's huge! Ross: Mask coming off... IT'S WAYLON MERCY! He's in the ring, Luna telling him something... HE'S GOT THE PSYCHO SLEEPER ON CHAPARITA ASARI!
Lawler: This is great. She's going out quick here. Oh she's cold now. Ross: Luna Vachon going up... LUNA ECLIPSE! Lawler: Ain't that sweet! Mercy scooping up Luna in his arms, and a kiss. That's love for ya!
Voice: Life's a form of fate in Waylon Mercy's hands. Y'know what I mean? Ross: Chaparita needing a little help to leave the ring, but she seems OK. For Jerry Lawler I'm Jim Ross saying see you next week! -- Shawn Hart < [detail withheld] > 'If you knew yesterday what you know today, you'd still be an idiot.' - Jerry 'The King' Lawler
