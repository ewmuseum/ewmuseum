Hancock to Return at Assault

7 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own news post (#82), dated 7 October 2003, filed under Jake Hancock, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Only a few days from Assault, it's reported that Jake Hancock is going to be returning to the OSF at Assault. After his month and a half absence, it’s unknown to his current condition, but he has been cleared by Junior DaVatta to compete. The reason for his absence is thought to be due to the recent jailing of his brother. We tried to reach him for further comment, but he was unable to be reached. His new manager was able to be reached and she had this to say. “Jake is excited to be getting back to business as usual and I will be right there along his side to support him. He’s still in top shape and prepared to fight on Assault if needed.” Haley Hilton hasn’t been seen since PCW and now she’s managing Jake Hancock. There are a lot of questions left to be answered and hopefully we will get them on Assault. So tune in to find out!
