Elegant Opinion; Volume 3 An opinionated column by Miyabe Nikkaido As I write this I fear this may be the last form of presence in OSE. Don’t rejoice at once, peons. The way things have progressed since Ascension might leave me no choice but to cancel services with this company. First, OSF’s champion as they call her, Tempest, breaks my jaw. All you people out there that say she’s the sweetest person in the world are nothing but damn liars. NICE PEOPLE don’t go around breaking other people’s jaws! They learn to take a joke when a joke is made, and laugh it off and walk away. Of course a joke cant take a joke at that, Tempest being the biggest joke to Old School Fighting there is. So my absence from OSF Assault has been result of my staying at home in Hyokougen and resting. And so this column takes the place of my mouthpiece, my form of expression. I would’ve pressed charges, but placing Tempest in a facility surrounded by other flat-chested bull dykes wouldn’t really be so much punishment. Second, Ascension was a travesty. Inphino lost not ONE but TWO matches! The OSF title match I can see why he lost. I mean he had her ready to be finished, but then like always he had to think with the wrong head and Tempest capitalized on that disadvantage. I wouldn’t kiss him after her anyway… that’s just cause for catching whatever disease she has that makes her so damn ugly. And the main event for Ascension… I’m sorry but MATT REGISTER?? A tag team wrestler is representing the entire company as its interdivisional champion? I’m glad I don’t have stock in OSE because that bitch just dropped! Matt Register has no draw value, no face appeal, and overall a waste of talent space in Old School Entertainment. And he won it by climbing up a structure and grabbing the belt. You beat someone one on one for that belt, and I’ll hum your praises, but until then you’re just not worthy of holding that strap, darling. If all those idiots weren’t in Inphino’s way during the match he would’ve easily won that belt and gave OSE the champion it deserved. But after what he’s recently done… I’m glad he doesn’t have the belt. Assault after Ascension… Inphino’s been missing all week. I didn’t see him at the penthouse in Chicago, nor the flat in Hyokougen Japan. I would’ve checked that Sado Island joint he used to live at, but I knew that whiny twat Mina wouldn’t let him in. He’s known for walking off and leaving us, so I decided to wait it out. Assault airs, and I turn it on after getting out the shower to find WHAT? The return of the f-cking Black Dragon Master! So what was the past few months? A LIE? He made me believe in this life I live now just for him to turn his OWN back on it? That’s f-cking unbelievable. So he turns a complete 360 and just abandons me and all the followers of Miyabi he created in the way… heh, I guess he was telling the truth when he said his hypocrisy knows NO BOUNDS. I hope he rots in hell. But now that I think of it after writing all this… I will NOT be leaving after all. There’s still ONE man that’s Elegant enough to appreciate my love and affection. Who is he? Well… you’ll have to wait and see on Assault. Until then, wish Inphino Blitz dies in a violent gay rape for turning his back on me and his allies, and live your lives Elegantly. Loving you Elegantly… Miyabe Nikkaido
