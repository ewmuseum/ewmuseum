What this is The OSF site's own history page for the character, as it stood in the Wayback capture.
~*This is a short version of a complicated history*~ Little is known about Jada's early past, except for the fact that she came from the most turbulent of domestic situations. Though born with severe mental illness, it would not have evolved into what it has become today had she been raised in a better environment. The only thing she took away from that home, besides searing hatred for the world, was her parents' alcoholism. Jada has made a few comments as to what went on in that run-down house in Philadelphia. Talk of being locked in closets for days in the dark, hot coals pressed to the soft flesh of her four year old body and nightly ambushes from a burly, drunken father will sometimes come from her mouth when either drunk or in a state of extreme confusion. Jada managed to escape that situation in her early teen years. A high school drop-out and juvenille delinquent with an extensive criminal record (which included manslaughter...), Jada didn't have much hope for the future, nor did she really care much about it. To her, life was all about drugs, fighting and the little world she was creating for herself inside her mind. Her heart was all ready cold to the world. This teenage girl appeared tougher and more stoic than a man who had experienced the horrors of war. She remained on this path for about a year, bouncing in and out of foster homes, when out of nowhere things began to change. She spent a great deal of her time in underground pit and streetfighting organizations, and quickly built up a reputation for being particularly talented and unhumanly ferocious. Jada became a favorite prospect and money-drawer for bookies. She made a few men very rich and happy, but at her own sacrifice. Jada was treated like an animal, more or less, but that was much better than where she was before. It was not too long before she would attract the attention of the more mainstream worlds of wrestling and boxing, however. To make a long, complicated story short, Jada finally settled on wrestling after many attempts at various forms of professional fighting. It seemed like the best place to get money to support her habits, and allow an environment that she could take out her agressions without ending up in jail She had been there numerous times before and did not wish to go back. It was a waste of time, in her mind. Jada was nineteen when she first began wrestling. It was also around the same time that her infamous mental conditions deteriorated severely. After only a year of serious competition, one of her employers had her committed to a mental hospital after an incident with a couple of wrestlers and staff members. She would be in and out of such places for years to come, and probably would remain as such for the rest of her life (thus far, that has proved to be true). Just as in the pit fighting circles, Jada quickly made a name for herself in the wrestling world. Her animalistic style attracted a lot of attention, both positive and negative. Many were drawn to the brutally secretive woman who rarely made sense when speaking...and had an insatiable taste for the thrill of combat. Over the years she had acquired more than twenty title belts and was one of the top names in the business. Of course, she wasn't too thrilled about this, and sank further into the depths of dementia. Now, Jada has most certainely become all the more detached from the real world, and as a result, has become all the more dangerous a foe. Fighters Career History: Jada has been fighting practically all of her life. From the time she first struck out on her own at the tender (or not so tender, in her case) age of sixteen. It was the only way she knew of to survive -- that and it was just plain fun to her. Getting into the pit fighting curcuit was the best way to give an outlet to her 'artistic side', as she sometimes calls it, and not get charged with murder. She has bounced around from several fighting curcuits and even tried her hand at wrestling for a while. In the realm of wrestling, Jada has acquired somewhere in the neighborhood of 15 world title belts, 4 intercontinental title belts and at one point held a tag team belt. She grew bored of that world, feeling that she had done all that she could. Now...she hopes to make her return to her roots.
