Live from OSE Arena, Chicago, Illinois October 2, 2003 Let it Snow, Let it Snow, Let it....... OSF Assault comes on the air, with a video package of the PPV this past Sunday. It highlights the various fights and fighters that rocked the OSE Arena. As the clip comes to an end, it shows the ending of the Blitz/Jackson fight, with Jason Snow making his unexpected debut. The video package ends, and the crowd of fans in the Arena are shown. They are louder than usual, and that is because, the crowd is larger than usual. Finally, the camera picks up a shot of Duke and Paulo at Octagon-side. Duke: HELLO HOSSES AND HOSSETTES. AND WELCOME TO ANOTHER EDITION OF ASSUALT. Paulo: And it’s the first post PPV Assault. Duke: We have a new President…… Paulo: And that’s still under investigation. The Clan screwed us. Duke: But Skylar Green is the man for the job. Paulo: Whatever…… The audience stir restlessly in their seats when suddenly, the bassline to GnR’s “Right Next Door to Hell” pounds through the house sound system. The capacity crowd rises to their feet as one of OSF’s newest talents, Jason Snow, appears at the top of the ramp. Duke: Here we go, folks! Paulo: Jason Snow shocked the world at Battered Broken and Bruised, and maybe tonight we can get some answers on where he’s been for the last half a year, and why he’s decided to come to Old School Fighting! At the top of the ramp, Jason Snow stalks back and forth for a moment, letting the audience soak in the sight of him. Snow is dressed surprisingly well from the waist down, but above that, he is bare chested and looks like the ruthless beast that he is rumored to be. His blond hair is just long enough for its tight ponytail, and his cold blue eyes are fixed on the octagon. Paulo: The young man is a physical specimen! To the cheers of thousands, Snow starts the walk down to the octagon, where he catches a microphone and stalks the perimeter for a moment. Finally, the audience begins to quiet as Snow makes his way to the center of the octagon. Snow: Six months ago, I walked away from the wrestling ring… The audience pops at simply the sound of Snow’s low calm tones, which haven’t been heard on television since his departure from PCW half a year ago. Snow: I walked away because there was simply nothing left for me to prove. I started at the bottom of the mountain, and not only did I climb it – I CLIMBED THE DAMN THING FOUR TIMES! Snow paces the octagon once more while he waits for the crowd to hush. The muscles in his back and stomach move fluidly with each step, reminding the audience of the Lion that he was nicknamed after.
