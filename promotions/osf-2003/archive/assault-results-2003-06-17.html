Inside of his mouth sits a pipe. He opens his mouth wide and blows into the pipe as many bubbles fill the room. Carter shuts the door behind him and goes off into the other direction. He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out of it his infamous magnifying glass, which he places into his right hand. He brings it up to his eye and slowly begins to search the room, possibly for clues for his Tempest case. Carter continues down the long hallway scanning the floor still. He does this for a few minutes before he comes to a stop bumping into something or someone. He slowly puts his head up still looking into the magnifying glass, and sees that standing in front of him is his arch nemesis Jake Hancock. Jake stands looking at the detective. Wearing his new grey suit, which is the way he dresses in hopes of impressing the DaVatta’s.
