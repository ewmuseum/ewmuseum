The Glitter Gazette Edition #1, May 31, 2003 By Tempest My dear fans and friends, It has come to my attention that certain people, who shall remain nameless (but his initials start with I and end with nphino Blitz), have made it their business to interfere with my right to free speech and self-expression! Now as alarming as that charge may be in America, it is all the more disturbing because I happen to be the OSF world champion just now. And I have a right to be heard! So, upon the advice of a wonderful Russian Bear, I have decided to put my thoughts into this, my first ever column! First and foremost let me address those people who seem to have a problem with that very wonderful Russian Bear. Let me say that I have always derived not only an income but also a great deal of joy from the fans of this sport. You have made me feel proud and happy with my accomplishments and comforted and admired in times of defeat. But that does not change the fact that you are still on the other side of the cage from me. You do not take me home and care for me, nor hold me when I awake from nightmares. Ivan does. So before you become too judgmental over my decision to stand by him, remember where things really stand. I am a fan's champion in OSF where my reign is not Red. I am fighting a truly despicable force in the DaVatta clan. And Ivan is supporting me 100%. I will not sacrifice the man I love to the will of strangers. While it's true that I may not be the quintessential Red Army patron, I do believe in some of the things they stand for. They stand for order in the midst of lawlessness, the rule of the many as opposed to the few, and belief that hard work is the primary ingredient for success. All of these things I've found to be true. And for those who say I'm blinded by love for the Army's Commander in Chief, let me remind you that when I came in as a friend of Jane Reagan, it was Ivan Stanislav that said she wasn't to be trusted. And he was right. He didn't want to trust Mexi, and he was right. And now, it appears that he's even right about my dear old friend Inphino. Inphino Blitz. Here is a man who was everything that fighters and wrestlers alike wanted to be. He had it all under control and his skill was unmatched. Well, almost unmatched. And he was a great friend. Truly, in the dark days of my youth he was a ray of light. The story of our meeting and friendship is seldom told. And while I'm sure he would now tell you it included enough wild sex to leave me incapable of walking, I will remind you of just how proficient I am with the use of my legs. He was and would still be, my friend, if he really wanted to be. You think I'm afraid of you, Inphino? That I won't give you a shot at the belt that might legitimize your claim to being the king of all fighters? By all means step right up. You say people can't see your talent because they aren't Miyabi enough, but I say they can't see your talent because you’re so busy drowning yourself in debauchery and false prophecy that you haven't won a match in ages! I have nothing to fear from a washed up loser who can't think above the belt anymore. And I'm sorry Inphino, I really am. I'm sorry that you couldn't balance the warring natures in your mind and heart, and that they had to explode and destroy all that I remember as good. Because I do remember. And you never know, if I beat the Miyabi Prince back into his hole perhaps my friend will come back. I think I'd like to find out. And what of your new friends and trusted allies, the Clan? Well, the Clan are an interesting bunch. And while they seem to present a united front, there is much more going on beneath the surface. They clearly don’t think and act as one. Some follow Inphino (blindly, I might add), some seek to destroy Kenyatta Jackson, and some seem to be waiting for just the right opportunity. It’s interesting to watch and always a challenge for me to figure out where they stand. At this time I’d also like to reassure my new friend and hired gumshoe, Kraig Carter, that your debut has been excellent in the fighting division. Don’t be intimidated by Blitz. You already know from past experience what he’s really all about. Even though he’s changed, he hasn’t. That’s the real irony of the situation. And soon, you’ll be getting some help in your case, as well as in the ring if needed. Onto other burning questions like: Why did I want to help out Teresa Tomas, Cassandra Chance, Torrance Smith, and Sombra Mistica? Simple, they got screwed out of chance because they’re women. I’ve never really been an activist, but that’s because I never needed to be. I never stood up and yelled, “Women can do anything as good as men!” because I didn’t know anyone was saying otherwise. But since encountering the DaVattas, I’ve been forced to realize there’s a whole spectrum of people who will lie, cheat, steal, and commit hideous violence to keep me down, and all because I was born with ovaries. Before anyone begins to think of this as a NOW commercial, keep in mind that I only facilitated the signing of four women. I asked that the talent be brought into OSW, not every woman. These women are the cream of the crop and will make OSW extremely proud. You go girls! And to Scott Taylor on the occasion of your not signing with OSF: It’s a shame I won’t get to face you because I think it’d be a great fight. But I admire your ability to tell when you’re being played. The DaVattas can guarantee you nothing, especially where I’m concerned. If we meet let it be without the convoluted manipulations of these desperate criminals. And for now, good luck in OSW. And lastly to Shawn Stewart, my good friend and student: You gave me a great fight last week and I think you now know what I was saying now. You’re a fighter with a lot of heart and great honor. But never be deceived into thinking any amount of planning, thinking and studying can replace a complete presence in the moment and simple pure ferocity. The reason I’m winning now, is that I refuse to lose. I am in a do or die situation and I will not die. And that is a warning to anyone who thinks otherwise.
