President's Review: Assault 10/2/03

4 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own column (#15), dated 4 October 2003, filed under Skylar Green, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
OSF… hello. I’m your new President, Skylar Green. Don’t worry about how I got the job, I just have it. Would you rather me or the DaVatta’s? Thought so. Anyway, I’ll be reviewing the show personally, giving my account on what happened. It’ll let you guys know what I think about what’s going on. Better than just wondering. Let me start off with my Weekly Top 5. In honor of Angry Man Numero Uno, this week I’ll count down the Top Five Things That Shawn Stewart is Trying to Take Credit For. 5) Blocking an anti-fighting bill that would have died on the floor anyway. Nice six-week vacation man, especially just walking back into an Old School Title feud. Man, I should go on vacation. I’d come back and magically own this place! 4) The Kansas City Chiefs’ record so far. 3) Winning the war in Iraq. 2) 50 Cent’s reinvention (sorry, rehashing) of the mainstream gangsta rap genre. 1) Putting President Bush back in the White House for a second term. Now that that’s over with, let’s get into the review for this week’s Assault. Forgive me, I’ve just been so busy that I haven’t had a chance to officially review the Pay-Per-View, but it’s coming this weekend. Promise. Start the show with Jason Snow coming on out and saying that he’s eager to hurt people again after leaving wrestling. Called out Inphino Blitz, but got the much less elegant (and hardly physically intimidating) Junior DaVatta, who told Snow that he needs an OSF contract, and that required the full gamut of evaluations to determine whether or not he is fit to compete. This included a psychiatric evaluation, something that 80% if professional athletes would need to take. Going to Michael Jacques’s backstage area, he carries on some audible self-addressed monologue, and was obsessing over a paper he was reading. It was probably a letter from his prison-arranged pen pal or something. He worried about winning twice, but not gaining anything. Poor baby. He may want to take advantage of our new “Cry Me A River” booth that I set up backstage. Sonny Silver comes up with a million different excuses to cover for his own inadequacies in the octagon. Here’s an idea: maybe you haven’t won because you overestimate yourself? Come on, we don’t need Exceptional Dave Hurst Version 2.0 in Old School Fighting. The real thing is annoying enough to deal with. After that he talked some more with his entourage. Hm… “shit-filled?” Censors couldn’t catch it in time, but reminder, bucko; we’re on UPN, not FOX. Family programming, man. Keep the language to the pay-per-views and your own time. Shawn Stewart, even though I gave him the night off, arrives at the arena. A guard attempts to do his job, Stewart attacks him. Hefty penalties will be levied, but that wasn’t going to happen that night. I had a show to run. Michael Jacques, wrapped in bandages, gives himself a pep talk. Tries to get himself back to his badass ways. Damn, everybody’s a badass. Maybe that’s why Pete! stood out so much in PCW… Next, a commercial for Lights Out Wrestling. Apparently they want us to order their pay-per-view three months ago. … I’ll have to change our ad scheme at some point this next week. Backstage, during the break, Duke Williams took some time out to sign autographs for the Chicago crowd. It is one of his listed hometowns. Odd, there’s like no one backstage aside from myself and Duke who will do that sort of thing. I’ll need to organize a workshop on “Being a Public-Friendly Worker.” Not everyone will take, but the non-televised interaction between the fans and the workers is abysmally low, I’ve noticed. Agent X had a hard time dealing with the smell in the public bathrooms. That’s why we have special bathrooms for the fighters, which are kept in better shape. Use them if you’re that much of a wuss. There is a bit of rude behavior between X and Marcellus Cosell (mainly instigated by X, who should know better). It ends with Cosell stealing the Booker T quote that he used during his time as “high level JTTS #7” in the WWF Invasion angle. Miguel Carranza and Paula Michelle Smith arrive. Carranza seems incapable of talking, and runs into a door marked “pull”. Olf Svenson shows up and says that Miguel will have a slut in his corner. Genius. Pure genius. Before I continue; Olf, for future reference, you don’t HAVE a corner in the octagon. Try to remember, thanks. Back to the review already in progress. Carranza tries to stick up for PMS, but she speaks for herself. Svenson proceeds to talk dirty to the minor (odd, for a Finn he sounds almost like a dirty old Brit). [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] Olf continued to push Carranza’s buttons, but she wanted to handle it herself. Svenson took the initiative of clutching a girl who can’t even legally vote yet (though in Chicago, she’s allowed to protest outside of City Hall naked with the proper permits… why that is allowed is beyond me) by the throat. Then PMS proved that the testicles are always an option for attack, no matter how muscular the man. The “Earlier Today” logo appears. Chris Locke comes in, pissed off and badass just like everyone else. A nice elderly guy just tries to make conversation. He gets cold looks and a threat on his well being. The old man informs him of the ruling I made. That whole “community service” thing. Yeah, my idea. As it seems to me, the fighters around here think that they can do what they want with impunity. But I can’t have you going around trying to kill each other. That puts a great liability upon my shoulders, and besides, I’ll be honest: people aren’t lining up in droves to work here. Not yet, anyway. You guys mess up, you’ll get punished and punished in a way that’s both fair yet excruciatingly frustrating for you. Locke and Silver have community service to do together. Silver hasn’t reacted too badly. Locke acted like I ordered him to contract the plague. But hey, that’s what happens. As the janitor explained to Locke, they can’t attack each other outside of a sanctioned OSF match, otherwise there’ll be fines, more community services, possible suspension and termination… all that good stuff. And if they do decide that they can live with those thing… hell, I’ll find some way to attach criminal charges to the list. Before I continue, let me make another note to the fighters. Some behavioral problems such as Olf Svenson, Shawn Stewart, and Chris Locke are good examples of this. You notice those people who aren’t fighters? The ones with headsets, or the ones wearing blue “Meridian” outfits? The ones with the shirts that say “Crew”. Yeah, those people you see around the arena. The cleaning crew, camera operators, technicians, random fans… they are not bugs for you to step on for a quick ego fix. They have a job to do, and it would make it easier on them if you’d not act like pricks all the time, walking around with your heads up your own asses because you take yourselves and your hype too seriously. Threaten, or in anyway be exceptionally rude to the non-fighting staff, and you’ll find yourself doing THEIR job until I see fit to let you back into the octagon. Be jackasses on your own time. Reviewing again. Carranza worries about PMS. She walks in, says that Olf attacked her, and then there was a lesson in chivalry and the “License to Play Dirty.” Carranza wasn’t quite going for that. She walked out on him. PCW commercial… seriously, guys, when’s the last time someone updated the advert contracts? First fight of the night… oh yeah, we have matches on this program. Silly me. Ijinza Yebanka versus Michael Jacques. MJ tries to waste time, Hans Galenberg does the right thing by starting to count him out for delay of fight. Finally, he gets into the ring. Kicks against punches, respectively. At the start of the match, neither man can get an advantage. Jacques attempts a German Suplex, whichi would have Shawn Stewart cursing him to no end. But he doesn’t get it. A snapmare follows from Ijinza. A hard kick sandwiching Jacques between Yebanka and the cage wall, and the ten count followed. Guess that pep-talk didn’t work for MJ after all. X, perpetually angry over the fact that he is fallible, erupts at Sonny Silver for idiocy that is not really his fault. Calm down. I mean, really, you do look like an Agent. Though I’d equate you more with Agent Johnson (Daniel Bernhardt ROCKS) than Agent Smith. You don’t have quite the scowl to pull off Smith. Cut to a different segment after he walks out of the room. Agent X, the running punchline of the night, is asked by Chris Locke if he needs any pointers. Wasn’t he so upset that he didn’t want to speak to anyone just a few hours ago? Ah well, I guess people just laugh at others to make themselves feel better. To forget their own failures. Poor Agent X. He leaves Chris Locke and runs into Shawn Stewart. Literally. They argue over who should apologize. See, CIVILIZED people would have just both said “Pardon me,” and gone on about their day. But no, these guys have an argument. Man, anger management classes all around for these guys. Clyde talks with Mystery Man the Fifth. Apparently they’re rehearsing for the sequel to ‘Underworld.’ Or something. Commercial for Pro-Wrestling Circuit…… okay, I’m just getting annoyed now. Agent X knocks out Marcellus Cosell, who seemed like the black neighbor on a white sitcom the way he just popped in with “Hello?” X, a full apology will be given to Cosell by next Assault. Do it public, private, however. But apologize to the man. He was actually one of the few people who hadn’t done anything to you that night. Manuel Labor is given camera time and talks about himself with a bit of an inferiority complex. That’s like his gimmick more than being a competing janitor. The Miyabi Missionaries talked about Inphino. A verbal spat between Mina de Margo and Lillith. Mina did some trash-talking. Bleh. PMS shows up at Olf’s door with a checkbook. Olf again talks dirty, as if that’s in any way flattering. Man, there’s entire industries dedicated to getting you sex. You shouldn’t have to sexually harass a minor in an attempt to get it. OSW commercial. Finally, something that makes sense. Sonny Silver comes out, curses some more (UPN dammit!), makes an open challenge, and generally his ratio of words to anything that could be even misconstrued as a point of his monologue was 80 to 1. Maybe if he was just barred from microphones, ratings would go up? I’ll do some focus group research… Inphino Blitz comes out next and after hemming and hawing about general misfortune, he doubles his resolve to “conquer” America. Uh… I’ll just move along, lest I validate him by giving an opinion. Chris Locke apparently wanted to ignore that whole “no attacks outside of a match” ruling I made. He’s lucky Silver wasn’t there. He’d be in an unemployment line right now if anything happened. EW-247 commercial. Ugh… Backstage, Inphino Blitz stole candy from a baby. Why a 5-month-old kid had an oversized lollipop was beyond me, but something tells me that he orchestrated the incident. That child was like Cianan’s mini-me, and had on black lipstick. Next match of the night, Sonny Silver against Agent X. Odd thing, he came out four times, Silver did. Production troubles, ran in a loop. Luckily, nothing happened during that time it took for them to get everything back in order. Silver actually took advantage for much of the match. Apparently his egotistical idiocy is the equivalent of drunken kung-fu or something. But he got distracted by Chris Locke, which led to an Agent X win. Chris, Sonny, let me make an amendment to the rulings. Stay away from each other’s matches. The last thing we need is another two months of constant run-ins. Olf Svenson and Agent X did that enough before B3. Mina de Margo pretty much dismisses the Miyabi Missionaries. Whether she could have been taken at her word remained to be seen in Lillith and Grey’s eyes, but I’ve never known her to be a liar. Inphino attack Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson with the Big Freaking Katana, an old prop I used to use. He bought it from me at a yard sale about a year ago. Anyway, Inphino of all people tells someone else to stop bitching. Heh. Continues the beating. Ah, well, punishments abound for that, huh? Shawn Stewart attacks Sonny Silver. He had it coming, but still. LOW commercial for In The End. ARGH! During the commercial break, I had Shawn Stewart leave. He wanted to fight in the cage, but he was kind of wacked in his gourd anyway. I didn’t dismiss him on camera, he was having a bad enough night and embarrassing him on the air would’ve just made him more resentful. Inphino Blitz curses out Snow and Junky Turner. *Cough* Is it just me, or can the anger and tension around this place be cut with a spoon? Miguel Carranza II against Olf Svenson now. The third and final match of the night. They start fighting before even getting to the cage. Olf is at first stunned, then just stands there. Apparently he has incredibly powerful jaw and face muscles. Must be all that talking he does. Carranza is beaten down to the mats outside of the ring. He just takes some hits before the matting is stripped away. A high Gorilla Press, and Carranza’s dropped onto the concrete. See, OSF isn’t wrestling in a cage, it’s wrestling outside of a cage until they close the door! PMS comes to Carranza’s rescue (he’s got to stop relying on a kid to save him), but gets squashed into the cage. The offense is then 90/10 split between PMS and Carranza’s combined attack to Olf’s damaging offense. Mind you, the fight is still outside of the cage. Finally, Carranza gets some hits in, but Olf just shrugs them off. A double ax-handle puts Carranza down, but it’s a few moments before he’s thrown into the cage for a count, and so isn’t dead yet. More HULK SMASH! Style by Olf, but PMS uses all of her “Lolita” charm on Svenson. He gets distracted long enough for Carranza to throw a weak punch, but it apparently knocks the strongman out. Wait, his eyes were open. Threw the fight, maybe? She did walk in with a checkbook, after all. Meh, I’ll do an investigation. And with that, the show ended. Fight of the Night: Miguel Carranza III versus Olf Svenson Fighter of the Night: Miguel Carranza III – you held in there against a much bigger, stronger opponent. Nice job. Non-Fighter (Had no match) of the Night: Marcellus Cosell – You actually didn’t try to step on any toes. Congratulations. Would Be More Enjoyable if They Shut The Hell Up: Sonny Silver. Should Talk More Just to Take Mic Time Away from Blitz: Cianan Least Impressive Fighter of the Night: Lillith – if you’re just gonna show up to get ragged on by Mina de Margo, best to stay home. Chill Out Award: Tie – Shawn Stewart and Agent X Get with the Program Award: Chris Locke
