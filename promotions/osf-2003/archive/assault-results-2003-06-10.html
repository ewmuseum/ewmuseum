Live from OSE Arena in Chicago, Illinois June 10, 2003 Troubled Arrivals A taxi pulls up to the backstage entrance, a rickety land vessel with no hubcaps and the trunk and hood rattling loosely. It hisses to a stop, and the doors open on both sides. Out steps Miyabe and Miyavi, both with irritated looks. Miyabe is in tight black stretch jeans with black boots, and a dark blue spandex shirt with an M cut across her chest. Miyavi is wearing baggy black cargo pants drawn tight below his knees, white sneakers, and a white sleeveless shirt. Miyavi: Today is cloudy, with occasional pain… Miyabe nods with a sigh, leaning with her arms on the hood of the car. Miyabe: You said it. As soon as we get back from Japan we get pulled off the airplane right after it lands by the authorities because they got an anonymous tip we were terrorists! Miyavi: Full body cavity searches are not the way to start things off. Miyabe: Come on now… can't complain completely about THAT. The Elegant Hero smirks slightly at her before rubbing his hands on the back of his pants. Miyavi: Only when I'm in the mood, angel. You on the other hand… are always in the mood. Miyabe: My eyes have been opened to the pleasures of life that exist by being Miyabi. It makes me feel so… yummy…mmmm… Voice: Ahem. Miyabe and Miyavi look into the car and back away. Miyabe joins the Japanese rocker on the other side of the car as out steps Inphino Blitz, already wearing his combat gear. He glares narrowly around the parking lot before slamming the door behind him. Inphino: Let's be haste about tonight and depart. The airport lost our luggage, and I'm anxious to get back to the apartment and change. Miyavi: As we all are. The long flight from Hyokougen have left us quite tangy. Miyabe: And the limo we requested never showed at the airport! Some service! Inphino: Fine. We'll take care of business here; terrorize a little Tempest in tag team timing before retreating to our home for our bath. Miyabe: That sounds just Elegant, my love. Voice: Hey mac! I aint waiting here for an autograph! Inphino closes his eyes, exhaling deeply before turning around and looking into the cab. The burly cabbie grits at him, folding his arms across his chest. Inphino hands him a credit card and comes back out of the car, leaning back and waiting. Inphino: Something about today does seem… non-Miyabi. Miyabe: Any day we have to see Tempest's clownish face is non-Miyabi. Inphino: No, something off… like a string of bad luck… Cabbie: Hey! This card's coming up stolen! Whatcha trying to pull on me?! Inphino and company turn back to find the cabbie climbing out of his car, rolling his sleeves up. Inphino: Stolen! Lies! Try it again! Cabbie: I tried twice! I don't know what you slanty eyed scam artists are tryin to pull, but no one's leaving until the cops get here! Inphino: Miyabe… take care of this. Miyabe: (wretching) Ugh! Even nymphomaniacs have taste and dignity! The cabbie grabs Inphino by his collar, but Miyavi kicks the man from behind in his groin. Reeling, the cabbie is pushed away from Blitz, who socks him with a hard Inphinite to send him lying across the hood of his own car! Miyabe grins wickedly as Miyavi and Inphino walk on towards the backstage entrance, where a tall security guard stands waiting with a list. Inphino: Okay guy… step aside and allow the Elegant Savior passage. Guard: Who? Blitz blinks, looking away before responding. Inphino: Me… Inphino Blitz, fighter for OSF and clique. Let us in. He checks the list again, and the guard comes up shaking his head. Guard: Sorry. No Inferno Blitz on the list. Inphino: INPHINO… Blitz! Check again! Guard: Dude, I checked. There's no Blitz on the list whatsoever. Now beat it! Miyabe: Don't you recognize him? It's Inphino Blitz! He's so hot right now! The biggest thing in OSE! Guard: Sorry, I don't watch OSE. PCW FO LIFE! The Miyabi trio share a silent brooding moment before Miyavi speaks. Miyavi: Okay… let me get this straight… you don't watch OSE, yet you're working for OSE right now. Guard: Right. Miyavi: So how come you don't work for PCW if you're quote: 'PCW FO LIFE!' ? Guard: (shrugging) Gotta build up my resume before trying there. Voice: Excuse me, guys. Gotta get through here. The trio part ways as Mina de Margo approaches the door. Miyabe glares hard at her, while Inphino stares blankly. Mina walks right past Blitz and gives her name to the guard, who after a second nods and steps aside. She's about to disappear behind the door when Inphino halts her. Inphino: Mina! She looks back at him, her face just as blank as his. Mina: May I help you? Inphino: Tell this fool that I work for OSF! Please! He won't let me in! Mina stares at Inphino for a second, then shifts her gaze from Miyavi to Miyabe, and back to Inphino. Then a smile comes across her face, bringing one to Blitz's as well. Mina: I'm sorry… have we met? Faces drop to shock value as she closes the door right in their faces. The guard takes post again as the three of them stand there sulking. Miyavi: That… Miyabe: BITCH!! Inphino: … I think I may know who's behind this… but first we have to get in… They all glare back at the security guard before huddling up with their backs turned to the camera. As the bell is rung Tempest has a few words with Fatico and he backs into the corner, taking the chain. She heads towards the middle of the cage where Akage stands awaiting her. Tempest acts first, swinging a kick for Akage's side which Akage blocks by lifting his left leg, which absorbs most of the blow. Akage grins and then steps forward with a high knee, catching Tempest off guard and knocking the wind out of her.Tempest staggers back and Fatico uses this opportunity to tag himself into the fight. Paulo: Oh shit. Akage is about to get his face splattered. Akage backs up when the huge Fatico approaches him. Fatico swings a giant meathook at Akage which he ducks and spins behind, snap kicking Fatico in the back of the knee. Fatico groans and falls forward a little, but he quickly retaliates with a back elbow which cracks the face of an unsuspecting Akage. Akage staggers back and Fatico grabs him by the hair and pulls him forward into a knee to the gut. As all the wind is expelled from him Fatico grabs him by the hair again and starts slamming his face into the side of the Octagon. Over and over Fatico uses his superior strength to introduce Kitigami to the steel. Duke: So far Tempest and Fatico seem to have things going their way. Akage caught Tempest off guard with some good moves earlier but now Fatico is just dominating him. Paulo: Of course he is. Da family knows how to handle things. Besides dat, Fatico really wants that title shot at Ascension so he's making sure dat he and Tempest win dis fight. Fatico, still holding a stunned Akage by the hair, reaches over and tags Tempest. The Cyberpunk Goddess springs into action by shoulder blocking Akage in the ribs. Fatico lets go of him and Tempest nails a righthand on Akage. He staggers back and Tempest runs at him, spearing his knees to take him down quickly. She straddles him and starts slamming fists into his face. Akage throws up both arms to shield his face but now his forearms are getting bludgeoned by the OSF Champion. In a desperate attempt, Akage knees Tempest in the small of her back and she tumbles off of him. However Tempest quickly rebound up to her feet and goes for a jumping kick. Akage ducks under her leg just in time and dives for his corner, slapping his hand against Inphino's. Duke: That was pure desperation followed by pure luck, Hoss. He knocked Tempest off of him and just dove for that tag. Paulo: It doesn't matter. I don't dink dat Inphino will fair much better against Tempest and Fatico. As Akage climbs up to his feet to take hold of the chain Miyabee smirks at him and blows him a kiss. Of course it's all in spite and this really seems to get under his skin. Tempest charges at Inphino who meets her by grabbing both of her shoulders and using her momentum to pull her downward into a shoulder breaker. Tempest groans as she falls forward holding her shoulder. Inphino kicks her in the same shoulder, thus kicking her hand at the same time. Tempest rolls over onto her back and Inphino throws his head back and lets out a loud, violent, and rather disturbing hellacious scream. When he looks back at Tempest his eyes are flared and he starts merciliessly kicking her in the ribs. Kick after kick goes down and Tempest screams with each kick. Duke: I'm starting to pity Temepst now, Hoss. I just heard a Soul Scream over here and Inphino ususally gets serious when one of those goes down. Miyabee plays cheerleader besides the Octagon, clapping and rooting for Inphino as he pummels Tempest, now with stomps to the chest and stomach. He finally stops the attack and jerks Tempest up by her hair. Still holding that glare in his eyes, he knees Tempest in the gut and and places his leg behind hers and shoves her, causing her to fall backwards, hitting the back of her head on the mat. Tempest moans and holds the back of her head, starting to roll over. Inphino pulls her up again and slaps his elbow across her jaw. He still holds that sadistic smile on his face as he moves in after Tempest and sweeps her legs out from under her. Inphino steps back as Tony Travoli begins to make the count. 1...... 2...... 3...... 4...... Paulo: No! Get up Tempest! If youse lose den Fatico doesn't get his title shot! Hurry up! 5....... 6........ Tempest pushes herself and up and begins to stagger around dizzily. Inphino looks shocked to see that Temepst is up again and he charges at her. At the loud booming voice of Fatico screaming 'DUCK!' Tempest dodges out of the way at the last minute. Inphino runs too far and is met with a big boot by Fatico. Inphino stumbles a little and then falls onto his side dazed. Duke: These two really compliment each other. Fatico has the strength and just the brute power and Tempest has the speed and technicality. Tempest is right in the middle of her tag game and Fatico seems to be filling the role of Ivan. Tempest manages to tag in Fatico and he power walks over to where Inphino still lay in his daze. Fatico reaches down and pulls Inphino up and immediately chops him across the chest. He staggers back into the Octago wall where Fatico replies with a left-right elbow combo and a hard kick to the sternum. Fatico wraps his massive palms around Inphino's neck and with a bellowing roar lifts The Elegant Savior up over his head and powerfully slams him down into the mat. The fans actually gasp at the impact of the move and Fatico smiles proudly as Tony begins to count. Paulo: Dat's what I'm talking about! Dat's using your head in the Octagon. Duke: I think Fatico can see that title shot at Ascension already, Hoss. 1........ 2....... 3....... 4....... On the outside Miyabee is screaming for Inphino to get up and inside Akage doesn't really seem to care all that much. But Tempest seems happy. 5....... 6....... 7....... 8....... Paulo: Tempest vs Fatico...Game...Set.....Match. Suddenly Inphino sits upright and seems to be in another dimension as he looks straight ahead. . Fatico and Tony both gasp as Inphino slowly climbs to his feet, favoring his back after that impact. Fatico charges with a clothesline, but Inphino suddenly spews a black liquid from his mouth that sends Fatico staggering back holding his face. As the liquid dribbles down his chin, Inphino heads to his own corner with a slight limp. He makes the tag to Akage who now seems anxious to get involved in the fight. Paulo: WHAT DA HELL?! INPHINO JUST SPIT SOME KIND OF SHIT INTO FATICO'S FACE! Duke: I'm not sure what that stuff was. But it got the job done. I don't think Fatico can see a thing now. Akage catches Fatico in the chest with a running high knee which really doesn't do much more than send the blinded 7 footer staggering back more. Akage unleashes a flurry of fists onto Fatico's upper body and then shoves him back. With Fatico still rubbing at his eyes Tempest tags herself in and runs across the mat. Akage tries to knock her down with a clothesline but Tempest ducks and runs right at Inphino, jumping up and dropkicking him. Inphino falls back into the corner of the steel as Akage forearms Tempest in the back of the head. Angrily, Akage wraps an arm around Tempest's neck and pulls back, sort of choking and lifting the champion at the same time. Tempest struggles for her freedom as Inphino gives her a death stare. Tempest stomps both of Akage's feet and he lets go. She then uses an Irish Whip to send him into the corner, where Inphino makes the numerous self tag in this fight. He charges at Tempest who catches him with a drop toe hold. Inphino's chin ricochets off the mat leaving him temporarily stunned. Duke: Tempest is back in control of this match. She has Inphino right where she wants him so this could be the end of Inphino Blitz and Akage. Paulo: Dis is perfect. Go ahead and put him away, Tempest! Tempest crouches, waiting for the dazed Inphino to stand so she can put the finishing touches on this match. When suddenly a pen falls loose from referee Tony Travoli's shirt. Tony looks down to the floor and bends forward to grab his pen. Suddenly Fatico steps forward and slams the back of his fist into Tempest's head!!! Duke: WHAT?! WHY DID HE DO THAT?! The Cyberpunk Goddesses eyes roll to the back of her head and she falls face first onto the mat from the Rabbit Punch. Fatico smirks and then steps back to his own corner. Akage and Miyabee look on with their jaws nearly touching the floor as Inphino pulls Tempest up just enough to wrap his arms around her neck to apply a reverse full nelson. Paulo: Ha! Inphino just locked on 'Inphinity.' Dis fight is over! Call for da bell! Now with his pen succesfully replaced in his pocket Tony looks up to see Inphino with the submission on the downed Tempest. Tony baseball slides down to check on Tempest. When the out cold Glitter Goddess gives no response Tony calls for the bell. Howell: Here are your winners....Inphino Blitz and Akage Kitagami!!! 'No Pain, No Gain' blasts out to many a boo as Inphino lets go of the knocked out Tempest. He stands to the round of boos and smiles over to Miyabee. Duke: Why?! Why did Fatico help them defeat Tempest? Wasn't he guranteed a title shot at Ascension if they won?! Paulo: Faghet about dat! We as da family have decided that Inphino Blitz is da man to take the belt off da bitch. Dis whole ding was a set up from da first move! We are very thorough Duke, as Tempest just found out. The OSF Logo along with copywrite information appear on the bottom of the screen as Inphino is having a few words with Fatico. The Winner of the Match by "Submission": "Inphino Blitz"
