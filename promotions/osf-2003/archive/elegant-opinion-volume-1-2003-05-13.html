Elegant Opinion; Volume 1 An opinionated column by Miyabe Nikkaido: Dear fans of Old School Fighting, bear witness to the revolution of the Journalistic world. I, Miyabe Nikkaido, dedicated enthusiast of the Miyabi lifestyle introduced to the world by the wonderful Inphino Blitz, have decided to share the ways of Miyabi with the world through written methods. Inphino has praised my talents many times over, so I know that my abilities can fall no less than great if the Elegant Savior approves of my skills. Now, the main question on everyone’s mind is; “What is Miyabi?” Miyabi, in Japanese, means Elegant. It has been said by a great and Elegant man “Being Miyabi is the only way of life.” It could also be translated by that individual that “Being me is the only way of life”, because that man’s name just so happened to be Miyabi. And that is what being Miyabi is all about… being you. But it’s not that simple; anyone can be himself or herself, but it takes someone special to have flair and style and elegance with being themselves. If you’re just some low class loser that still thinks glittery makeup or suspenders are in style, then you’re not Miyabi, and you’ll never be Miyabi. And it’s not about style either; if you still think that this mortal coil still revolves around the played out good versus evil routine then you’re not Miyabi either. You have to be Miyabi inside, before you can be Miyabi outside. How does one become Miyabi from within? Excellent question. Well, first thing first; dismiss all notion of what the world taught you are right and wrong. Being Miyabi means that you determine what is good and evil, not society or anyone else. If you think homosexuality is good, then it is good, or if you think violence is the only way to settle anything, then by all means pick up the knife and solve problems. Live your life to the fullest; don’t let society dictate what you can and cannot do. The first thing Inphino told me when he took me under his wing is that people will shun you for being an individual, not following to the norm and being an outcast, but I should accept individuality and know that it makes me special and better than the rest of them. No one remembers the drones. Now, I’ve heard that followers of Miyabi tend to be labeled as dark and twisted by members of society… sometimes even sadistic and insane, but that’s just because they can’t understand our minds and our ways. The way we think makes us better than the rest of society’s robotic pawns. We think outside the box, to the degree where we make our own box. Now that the inner Miyabi has been given birth, now it’s time for the outer Miyabi to shine. Me personally, I was a trendy kinda girl when I was with Akage (wretch, urk, eww), but now that I’m with Inphino he has introduced me to a whole new line of attire, varying from bondage to industrial, to Victorian… everything that’s overlooked by pop culture. F-ck the mainstream, that’s our motto. Inphino tends to dress in the more Japanese “Visual Kei” style, AKA the Elegant male. Pretty, beautiful, attractive, looks great in everything… and by everything, I mean EVERYTHING. Inphino is very slender and lithe for being over two hundred pounds, and can get away with wearing more feminine attire as well as makeup. Tattoos and piercings are definitely acceptable, as is any color hair or hairstyle. Being Miyabi is about freedom, not being a slave to the fashion trend. Well, that’s all I can say for now. Just remember that being Miyabi is a way of life… and life is always changing, so the rules will change and progress in time. In conclusion, I’d like to give you a closing statement from the Elegant Savior, Inphino Blitz himself: “Society will crumble, and they who live within it will have to pick up the pieces and start from scratch. Yet they who are Miyabi… they live in a world that is indestructible. No one can tear it down… no one can control it. No one can stop us…” Loving you Elegantly… Miyabe Nikkaido
