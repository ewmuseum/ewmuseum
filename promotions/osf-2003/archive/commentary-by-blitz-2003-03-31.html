OSF — News

Commentary by Blitz

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own news post (#4), dated 31 March 2003, filed under Inphino Blitz, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Upon his debut in OSF, Inphino Blitz ran into OSF commissioner, Carlos Vierra, backstage. They exchanged a few words in a casual employee-employer fashion, Inphino expressing his thoughts on a few matters: Carlos: Good stuff with that win there, Bliotz. Blitz: Who was that guy anyway? Carlos: Ray Chavez, formerly of PIW. Also a WWR World champ for a year plus. Blitz: Yeah? He impressed me out there. Carlos: He gave youse a good fight huh pal? Blitz: A good what? I was impressed the way he spun in the air and hit the ground after he got hit with the Inphinite. Never saw that sh-t before… Carlos: Hah. Youse got some canolies in ya shorts there Bliotz. That’ll take ya far in OSF. Blitz: Face it, Carlos…none of the men on this roster are Miyabi enough to be worthy of the Fight Championship. Not the fat man, not the neophyte, not anyone you can hire into this division will be more elegant than I. Carlos: Whadda bout Tempest? She ain’t no guy. Blitz shakes his head with a smile, hearing the former International Champion’s name. Blitz: She “ain’t no gal” either. Tempest is what I like to think of as a glittery gremlin-like glitch in reality. She shouldn’t exist in this world…yet people continue to dream the dream that she is alive, that she is a part of their lives. She is dead to me…as she should be dead to everyone else. Carlos: She’s f-ckin hot though, ya think? Blitz: Depends…let me know if anyone manages to get all that glittery garbage off her face. I swear I’ll have to water-balloon her face with turpentine just to get her au natural. I’ll never find her attractive nonetheless…overachievers are turn offs. She’s always going after that International belt, harboring grudges just because she lost fair and square. She needs to just move on, accept defeat gracefully like a woman should. Because right now, she’s not elegant enough to even be in the Fight Division, let alone go at Kimbusa for the title. Carlos: Man, youse really hate her huh? Inphino slips his hands in his pockets thumbs out and shakes his head. Blitz: I loathe her…but I loathe most people nowadays who are deluded with a sense of doing good for the world…like the world’s suddenly gonna repay them with world peace, no taxes, and free candy for the rest of their lives. It’s an endless quest…believe me I know. They’re all better off making sure their own worthless lives are sufficient before making others’ bearable. Ivan was on the right track for a second…but then he went off and became a man of the people. Carlos: What a chump. And he had the balls to say the fight division was unfit or something! Blitz: Ivan doesn’t have the authority or prestige to say what’s unfit for this company, so you officially don’t have to give a rat’s ass about what he says. He said this company’s home to scientific and technically sound wrestling…like any of those worthless fans know the difference between a Surfboard and a Bow and Arrow Lock. The fans are idiots…just impressed by some complicated holds and big moves. He called this division barbaric filled with caged animals? Well then…his woman’s just become the queen bitch of the jungle…the irony of it all is just so elegant! So now where does that leave the fight division? Filled with a bitch, a fatass, a few scrappers, and yours truly…the Elegant Savior of OSF—NO---OSE…Inphino Blitz. Carlos: Youse the savior of this here joint huh? Inphino winks at him, snubbing at his nose and leaning forward. Blitz: Only I am elegant enough to be the best. Only I am Miyabi enough… family… friends… then enemies…they all shall fall at the feet of the true savior of elegance. With that said, Inphino Blitz walks away. After toying with best friend Mina de Margo's emotions, spitting in her face with a black mist, and then firing her as his manager, what is next on the buffet of corruption that Inphino seems to be gorging himself on now with his new gluttony of sin?
