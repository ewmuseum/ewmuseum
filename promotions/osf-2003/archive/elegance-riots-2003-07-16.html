Elegance Riots?

16 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own news post (#50), dated 16 July 2003, filed under Miyabe, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Despite the delay of Assault there was still a bit of fight action this past Tuesday night. Not knowing that Assault was pushed back later this week, a large group of youths assembled at the arena, only to be turned away. but then action wasn't caused by the show's delay... but because of one man. As reported by Lt. Tim Yancy, Chicago PD: 'According to an eyewitness employed by OSE, the entire riot involved fans of a fighter employed, one Inphino Blitz. The witness went on to report that though they were fans of the same man, half the group were 'Black Dragon Master' fans, and the other 'Miyabi followers'. I personally couldn't tell the difference between em all... because they all looked like freaks, goths, and outcasts to me, but the witness said there was a big difference. I noticed afterwards she was right, when I found all the 'BDM' fans with blue streaks in their hair and wearing all black and sunglasses. The Miyabi followers were more colorful, the punks with piercings and tattoos and all that.' 'It was then reported that words were exchanged, and insults passed from the two groups. Once that happened, one of the sides threw the first punch; no one is sure. There was about 75 of em all, so we called in riot control and broke out the gas. There wasn't a lot of property damage, and most of them suffered moderate, if not minor, injury. The charges vary, as the Black Dragon Master kids were charged with disturbing the peace and gang charges, while the Miyabi followers were more hostile and anti-authority. They got charged with resist, aggravated assault, vandalism of public property when one of them threw a rock through the squad car windshield... the list goes on.' 'Since majority of them were minors, few were held overnight, though the stations were flooded that night with policemen trying to process the unruly youth.' This situation offers a case definitely for anti-fighting, not to mention anti-wrestling campaigns out to censor, if not cancel this unique brand of sports-entertainment. Ovelia Pendant, MA in Sociology and head of Sports Entertainment Must Eliminate Nonsense, a campaign seeking to drastically bring down the level of controversial subjects and graphic violence within sports entertainment: 'When you have a man like Inphino Blitz, who has a charismatic appeal to his persona, whichever he's in at the moment, then you find that a lot of people are enamored by his charm, and their lives become influenced by his actions. From what I've seen, the Black Dragon Master image he portrayed was nothing harmful, and in a way uplifting for the moral and ethical level of society, if not a bit cold and distant. But the more recent Miyabi, or Elegant Savior, image he's showing society is very, VERY harmful. This image reaches into the part deep down inside of us all that we fear and hide away from our normal lives, and he tell them that there's nothing wrong with the fears and desires and lusts that we keep bottled up. He tells them to do what they've always dreamed of... be free free of who they are and what they do. Unfortunately, this brings about the darker side of people about 99.9% of the time.' 'It's good to say right now that since Blitz returned to the Black Dragon Master image, the damage done by the Miyabi image should fade away soon. But if the Miyabi image were to resurface, the increase rate of Miyabi fans would enormously increase from what it is now. At the moment Miyabi following is merely a small percentage of young adults in Chicago, not counting the small flocks scattered around the US and Canada. In Japan, however, Miyabi followings have already begun to brew into a problem in society, young adults rebelling against authority and causing scenes and commotions all over the country. My wish, is to nip the US version in the bud before it gets worse.' When Miyabe Nikkaido, one of the sole Miyabi followers in OSE, was told of the riot, she only smiled and wrote down the following: 'Well... someone had to throw the first punch... ^_^' Keep in on Assault this week to see the devopment of the Black Dragon Master and the Miyabi, not to mention the main event featuring Tempest vs Inphino Blitz for the Old School Fighting championship.
