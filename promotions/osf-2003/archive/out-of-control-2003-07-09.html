Out of Control

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own news post (#45), dated 9 July 2003, filed under Blue Dragons, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
A local police report was filed this morning involving Akage and Hiro Kitagami. After their match on Assault both brothers were put in seperate ambulances and taken to the same hospital. The hospital made the mistake of putting the brothers in the same room after each of them went through various operations and x-ray sessions. According to eye witness reports when young brother Akage awoke he was quick to continue the fight from earlier in the night destroying quite a bit of valuable hospital equipment. Hiro and Akage fought their way through the hospital and out into the busy intersection before police detained the brothers with the use of stun guns. 'Pops' Tony Scarpo Sr. wound up bailing both brothers out after a night in the Chicago Police Department cells. When questioned he had only this to say. 'Ey, the kids just had to get it out der systems. Aint our fault if da damn hospital workers were morons.' Both brothers left the Government Building after their hearing a bloody bruised mess and neither brother would comment as they were ushered by Pops to a car that had been waiting for the three men. For further information we called the source himself Junior Davatta. Junior was quick to state only this. 'Da boys will be dealt wit on Assault, I got nothin mo ta say.' More Information will be reported as we get it.
