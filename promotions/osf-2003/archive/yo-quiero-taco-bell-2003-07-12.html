'Yo Quiero Taco Bell'?

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own rumor post (#23), dated 12 July 2003, filed under Miguel Carranza III, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
What kind of commercial did Dave Gibson set Miguel Carranza up with days ago at Assault? The question has been whispered backstage ever since Carranza signed the contract. Not even Miguel himself knows. Gibson wasn't exactly forthcoming, though the Sell Out seemed mighty excited. And, hey, if Gibson had spilled the beans, would Carranza had even been able to follow? Regardless, it seems that only Dave Gibson knows what Miguel will be endorsing. Speculation has run from one extreme to another... but, surprisingly, some people have given Gibson the benefit of the doubt, and ventured that - maybe - he REALLY IS trying to help. In that light, the leading opinion thus far is that Miguel might be sponsoring Taco Bell. He wouldn't have to speak English - both a plus for Carranza and a hint dropped by Gibson on Assault. And Gibson often brags about his contract with Pepsi, which has a well-documented business relationship with Taco Bell. All the pieces - it would seem - fit. Hopefully, we'll know in the next few days, as filming should happen soon.
