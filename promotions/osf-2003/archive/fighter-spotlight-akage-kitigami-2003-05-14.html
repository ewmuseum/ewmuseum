Fighter Spotlight: Akage Kitigami

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own news post (#17), dated 14 May 2003, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Boon: Hello everyone! Boon Crockett here with another in our new series of Old School Fighting Spotlights! My subject today is Akage Kitigami, one-half of the successful Blue Dragons tag team, now making waves as a singles competitor inside the octagon. Akage, welcome. Akage: Thanks Boon... It's your pleasure I'm sure. Boon: I... see. Well then. Why don't I move on to the most obvious question: what sort of family business forced you to leave Slam Sunday night, and did you resolve the issue? Akage: What you saw on Slam with me leaving was all I can really divulge. The authorities have advised I shouldn't bring this part of my life into wrestling. But I never listened to Authority before so hell.. My brother was kidnapped as you know. On Sunday night I received a phone call from our home on Sado Island. I knew it wasn't Blitz seeing as how he was in the arena. So I left to find out who it was. Boon: So did you find out? Akage: *breathing out defeated* If I had found out wouldn't I have said so just now? The house was trashed when I got there. All of the pictures of Hiro and myself had been cut in half with Hiro's side stolen... Want to pry any more into my personal problems, 'Boon'? Boon: I'm sorry Akage, but sometimes a journalist has to ask the tough questions. Akage: It's quite alright Boon... I'm just impressed you can say journalist. Now get on with it. Boon: OK, well... how about this recent change in your attitude? It seems like, ever since Hiro got injured, you've lost that fun-loving spirit that endeared you originally to many of the OSE fans. Akage: Lost the fun-loving spirit? I just made fun of you didn't I? I don't know what you're asking here so please be more descriptive from now on. Endearment of fans can change at the drop of the hat.. For an example look at Tempest and Ivan.. Both were strong fan favorites until they did something the 'fans' didn't want. Akage: I was destined to beat Blitz, it didn't surprise me one bit. If you were surprised then you have too much faith in Inphino. As for how I compete, let's make it official: I do plan on being in the Ascension match for the Old School Title. And should Hiro return... I will make that choice when the time comes. Boon: Wow, that's big news. How do you like your chances in such a chaotic match? Akage: I am one of the best OSF has to offer so I think my chances are pretty good. Plus it will give me a chance to kick the crap out of guys I normally wouldn't be allowed to mix with.. Wrestling, hah! Boon: Are you saying you don't respect the OSW talent? Akage: Has the OSW talent earned my respect Boon? Some men are fool hardy with respect and throw it out to anyone who does something most can't. Me personally, you have to earn it and only one man on the 'OSW' roster earned my respect and he is dead now... *under his breath* Shounetsujigoku Supiido Hessian. Boon: Well I'm sure some OSW competitors will remember those words once the Ascension match begins. We're about out of time--any final words? Akage: 'Prepare for The Ascension of a Starlight Dragon'... and 'You smell bad.. Can I Leave now?' Boon: Please... Akage: Thanks Stinky. Boon: *sighs* We'll be back with another OSF Spotlight next week--hopefully with a friendlier subject. *sniffs his shirt as picture fades out*
