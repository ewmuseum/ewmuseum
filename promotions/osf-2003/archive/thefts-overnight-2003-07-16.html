Thefts Overnight?

16 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own rumor post (#24), dated 16 July 2003, filed under Miyabe, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
In the midst of the riot scene between Inphino Blitz fans last night in Chicago, other criminals decided to take advantage of the situation. Lt. Tim Yancy, again with the report: 'While all of the cops were down trying to control the situation at the OSE arena, three electronics stores were broken into, not to mention an ATM machine, two pawn shops, three blocks worth of parking meters, and strangely... three sperm banks. Stolen and/or missing items include a number of computer systems, TVs, video game equipment, jewelry, stereos, and as for the sperm banks... their safes were cleaned out... of money AND semen. Why anyone would steal SPERM... is beyond me!' Are Miyabi followers to blame for the thefts of the expensive and... unique items? Or is Inphino only to be blamed for his past sins? Who knows...
