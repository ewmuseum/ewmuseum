Live from OSE Arena in Chicago, Illinois July 29, 2003 I Know A Secret... “The Sky Is Falling” by Queens Of The Stone Age blasts over the PA system. The crowd rise to their feet and all eyes are on the entranceway. Paulo: (Sarcastically) Oh great, we are going to have to sit through another of Agent X’s pointless speeches. Duke: You really don’t like him do you? Paulo: What is to like about him? He is simply an idiot Duke and no one can argue that point. Duke: I think some of this crowd might. Paulo: Well this crowd is full of idiots! As the music continues to play, out steps Agent X. In his hand he holds a folder, which he looks down at and smiles. He looks over the crowd once before making his way down to the octagon still with the smile on his face. Before entering the cage he is handed a microphone. Once in the cage X signals for his music to be cut before placing the microphone close to his lips. Agent X: Ladies and Gentlemen I am going to keep this short… Paulo: Thank god! Agent X: Now I know you are all wondering what this folder is, but before I can explain I need someone out here. So Olf come on out here! Duke: Hmmm I’m not sure whether that was such a good idea X. Paulo: It was a brilliant idea Duke, now Olf can come and shut this idiot up! Again all eyes are on the entranceway as “Mamme” the Finnish National Anthem blasts over the PA system. The crowd instantly begins to boo as Olf Svensson comes marching out onto the top of the entranceway he grips a mike in his hand. The crowd continues to boo as Olf just stares at Agent X in the octagon who stares directly back at him. The Finnish National Anthem fades away as Olf brings to mike to his mouth. Olf: Hey X, how’s your car? Paulo: Oh that has got to hurt! X looks at Olf with fury edged on his face. Olf: What do you want X? The anger disappears from X’s face as he smiles and looks at the folder. Agent X: Ah I am glad you asked Olf. You see these past few weeks you have been striding around like you are some sort of success story, but that is not true is it Olf? That is why you get so wound up when people call you a failure. Olf: (Angry) I am not a failure! Agent X: See you can’t stand to be seen as a failure, and over the past few weeks it has had me puzzled. However a few days ago I received some information from a contact of mine, and this information was on some kind of contest called the World Strongest Man. Olf’s eyes widen with fear as X continues. Agent X: Now correct me if I am wrong Olf, but weren’t you a competitor in this contest? Olf’s eyes are still full of fear. Agent X: Yes you were and something happened at the contest didn’t it? That is what is in this file Olf, what really happened at that contest, and I am going to share the contents of this file with your beloved Finnish people. Olf becomes angry and shouts down the mike. Olf: You wouldn’t dare! Agent X: Oh I will Olf, in fact I am going to go backstage right now and talk to one of your friends who was kind enough to fly all the way over here. Olf: You’ll have to get past me first! Agent X: Not a problem Olf. X steps out of the cage and makes his way up the ramp. Duke: I hope X knows what he is doing. Paulo: I hope Olf knocks his block off! X continues to walk up the ramp not breaking the eye contact with Olf. Just before he reaches Olf, X raises the mike to his mouth. Agent X: Now you see me… A smirk crosses X face as within a blink of an eye he drops the mike and jumps over the crowd barrier and through the crowd. As X races through the crowd Olf looks on in anger. Duke: Olf ain’t happy! Paulo: And an unhappy Olf is a dangerous Olf!
