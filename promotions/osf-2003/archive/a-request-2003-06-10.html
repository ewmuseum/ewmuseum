OSF — In-card segment

A Request

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An in-card segment from the OSF site's own archive, dated 10 June 2003, for the show Assault, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson sits alone in his small locker room. Not enjoying the spoils of his current victories, he sits tending his injured hand. Kenyatta [Muttering]: Damn hand… damn Ninja’s… damn Japanese…they only good for sushi… damn I could use some sushi ‘bout now… damn hand… Kenyatta is wearing his traditional Atlanta Falcon’s jersey, with the ever-present number seven lining up on his back with the lettering ‘VICK’ across the top of his back. [Knock.] Kenyatta turns his head so that it is facing the door. Not expecting anyone, he cocks one of his eyebrows. Kenyatta: Yo dawg, I ain’t expecting anyone, so you might as well get yo’ ass outta here. Kenyatta Jackson glances back down at his hand and continues the intricate process of folding tape, over and over his hand, to ensure at least some strength in it. [Knock.] Kenyatta again looks towards the door, he breathes deeply before sighing. Kenyatta: I told ya before dawg; I ain’t expecting anybody; so get the hell outta here. The door swings open to reveal a man clad in Ninja attire. He was a member of the Clan, wearing their custom uniform. The Ninja walks with a twisted smile into Kenyatta’s locker room. Jackson stands, sizing up the Ninja; upon realising his size advantage he sits down once more. Kenyatta: I thought I locked the door. Ninja: I’m a Ninja; locks really have little effect on me. Kenyatta nods his head knowingly. Ninja: Anyways, Kenyatta, I am here on business… Kenyatta’s face turns into a slight abhorrent snarl. Kenyatta: Last time there was ‘business’ in my locker room, my room got foam bombed. See, it ain’t even like that shits funny. Ninja: I can assure you that I was nothing to do with the foaming of your equipment. Kenyatta: A’ight dawg, but I still ain’t trusting yo’ sushi making ass. The Ninja raises an eyebrow and chuckles. Ninja: The only sushi that will be left here will be your carcass left in a million pieces after I am finished with you tonight. Kenyatta stands, flexing his frame, before stepping towards, into the face of the smaller Ninja. Kenyatta: I should whoop yo’ ass right now. Ninja: I do not feel we have been formally introduced. Kenyatta snarls at the Ninja. Ninja: My name is Ichiharu, and I have merely ventured into your domain to ask your permission, a request if you will. Kenyatta: Shoot. Ichiharu: Since, [Glancing at Kenyatta’s hand], since you are carrying a hand injury, I feel it unfair that I should be able to defeat you with the use of two hands. Therefore… Kenyatta: Therefore, how ‘bout I stick this hand down your throat, rip your lungs out, that’d show you that it ain’t hurting. Ichiharu: So much anger, so much rage, you really should learn to respect your superiors. Kenyatta: Oh don’t even make me go into all that shit. Ichiharu: Since, you feel your hand is ‘okay’, I propose I still fight you with one hand. Kenyatta: You can do what the fuck you want dawg: ain’t gon’ make any difference. Yo’ ass has now been put on death row motherfucker. Enjoy yo’ last minutes of consciousness. Ichiharu: Oh, I will. Kenyatta turns, leaving a smiling Ichiharu by the door. Ichiharu: One thing, Kenyatta. Catch! [He throws Kenyatta a bottle of water] Kenyatta catches the bottle with his injured hand and grimaces. He looks towards Ichiharu. Ichiharu: Do you need a ‘hand’ with that? Ichiharu chuckles menacingly and leaves the room, leaving Kenyatta on his own. It would be a long night.
