The streets, usually littered with trash, stains coming from motor oil left behind by many vehicles, and people, are surprisingly scarce, which contributes a bit more eeriness to the already eerie mood cast upon the setting by the pitch black summer evening that had described via tape submitted by OSE cameramen. Quiet is the only sound that can be heard, but only lasts for a few moments before being interrupted by the sounds of what can only be defined as the breaking of glass. Suddenly, the source of the startling noise emerges in the form of Xander Pollack, who came from what seemed like nowhere, much like the tandem he leads, The Brotherhood, did when they broke into the second Most Extreme Team competition. Evidently, he had stepped on a shard of glass or a beer bottle while trying to make his grand entrance. Scowling, most likely at the fact that a filthy piece of litter had ruined what he wanted to be a chilling first impression, Xander takes several steps towards the camera and stops after finding a position that was comfortable, a microphone held tightly in his right hand, which rests against his waist. He then mutters something under his breath, a hint of anger in his voice as he speaks. Xander: Ugh…disgusting. Obviously fed up with the filth that is Chicago, Pollack raises the microphone to his lips and begins speaking to the viewers at home, a wide, yet incredibly fake, smile on his face. Xander: Hello ladies and gentlemen at home! It is I, Xander Pollack, here in the wonderful city of Chicago, which is an absolute pleasure for both Garrett, who isn’t with us at this moment, and I. It is also a pleasure to be filming this video for you, as we in The Brotherhood love nothing more then reaching out to the Great Power’s children, which is what you all are. Zooming in on Xander’s body, the cameras examine the accused cult leader’s attire. He wears a black three piece suit, equipped with a platinum fob chain which rests in between the pocket of his pants and his vest. There is also a golden chain that dangles around his neck, jingling happily every time he moves, with a miniature sword hanging from it. His eyes, unfortunately, aren’t as fancy looking. In fact, the bags around them suggest that he hadn’t slept in days. The microphone still resting comfortably in his hand, ala news anchor on the go, Xander continues to speak. Xander: We’re here tonight, in one of the roughest parts of this city standing on one of its most infamous streets, to spread a message. A message that the Great Power himself personally asked me to deliver to all of you out there at home, glued like mindless drones to your television sets popping handfuls of potato chips into your saliva laced mouths. Xander then begins to walk along the cracked concrete sidewalk in sync with the cameraman, who is moving backwards with every step Pollack takes, still holding the microphone closely to his mouth. Xander: That message, brothers and sisters, is simple and age old… Sneering disgustedly as he speaks and walks, Xander locks his eyes onto the camera, almost as if he is directly speaking to every person that is watching at home and in the OSE Arena. Xander: …Don’t do drugs. Xander takes a break from speaking after announcing this message, which has been announced numerous times in schools across the entire country and perhaps the globe, and continues to walk, finally stopping at the compact entrance to what looks to be an alley. Inside of the alley stands Garrett Hollister, who breaks from whatever it was that cameras had just caught him doing to salute his leader and proverbial brother with a nod, who, standing at an inch over seven feet and weighing in at a little bit over four hundred pounds of pure muscle and rage, is the much larger member and the enforcer of the Brotherhood. Below Hollister, kneeling against the cold cement floor of the alley is a man, who simply rocks back and forth in between bites of what looks to be a moldy sandwich that he probably salvaged from a nearby trashcan. Xander replies to his follower’s nod with one of his own and steps aside so that the cameras, and thus the viewers at home and those packed inside of the Old School Entertainment Arena, can observe the man, who Xander motions towards with a sickening scowl and an arm spread his way. Xander: This, brothers and sisters, is Sam. Sam was a simple man who loved his life, had a family and a somewhat steady job, and was most likely one of the most prized fruits that fell off of the Great Power’s proverbial tree. However, judging by Sam’s current surroundings, I would say that he fell a bit too far. Xander begins to walk closer and closer to the man, who stares at the camera like a deer caught in the headlights before returning to his feast of maggot-eaten scraps and garbage. Xander: Yet, Sam’s current state cannot be blamed solely on Sam himself. No…in fact, the blame should be put more on society’s shoulders then Sam’s. Why is this, brothers and sisters? The answer, though hidden by the so-called government and the organizations who support it, is quite simple. Society has allowed people like Sam to slip through the cracks and have provided him with the tools he needed to slowly, but surely kill himself. This tool wasn’t your normal tool, like a hammer or wrench, though. This tool was a tool created by the Great Power’s most feared enemies and also his greatest creations. This tool is a vast variety of narcotics, given to Sam by man. Sam suddenly begins to cough hysterically, which prompts Garrett to slap the obviously homeless man on his dirty, rag covered back. In a disgusting occurrence, a green and saliva covered piece of decayed sandwich flies out of Sam’s mouth and onto the cracked concrete ground that Xander’s black boots are resting comfortably on. Xander: There, there Sam. Don’t get all choked up. Xander chuckles nastily before continuing with his lecture. Xander: After all, you aren’t the only one. Actually, you’re one of many that have strayed away from the love and happiness that is the Great Power in favor of sin, poverty, and a quick fix. You, unlike those many people, just got what you deserved for it. Don’t worry though friend because soon those that escaped what you currently suffer from will be dealt with…for they can only hide for so long. Xander reaches into his pocket and pulls out a photo, clipped from the pages of a popular professional wrestling magazine, and unfolds it, showing it to the cameraman. Zooming in the photo, the camera sees the smiling faces of Nick and Speed, collectively known as the Painkillers, who just like the Brotherhood are competing in OSE’s Most Extreme Team competition. Why not realize that resisting us and resisting our message will simply make you hurt more in the long run? All at the hands of the Great Power and his Brotherhood. I thank you for your cooperation and hope that the wounds your sins have created heal in time. However, I would not be doing my duty if I didn’t continue your punishment, for it is His will.
