What this is The Assault results page for 16 October 2003 from the Old School Entertainment hub site's Assault archive, as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Live from OSE Arena, Chicago, Illinois October 16, 2003 Holy Cat Poop! The camera pans out somewhere on the upper level as two big bulky men stand there in deep black suits apparently waiting for someone. One of the men with a rather balding head slowly cracks his knuckles the deep tattoos on them can be clearly seen. Both stating the word ‘HATE’. Balding Man: Where is he? The other man wearing deep black sunglasses smiles to his balding friend before speaking. Second Man: He wont be long Percy, he knows where we are. Percy: I know Frank, I just we could get on with it. Suddenly Percy’s face lights up. Percy: He’s here. The camera zooms out a little and picks up the imposing figure of Olf Magnus Svensson walks into view. Frank: Ah finally you come and you know how much we hate being kept waiting. The Finn looks at the two men who are both nearly his height before sneering at them. Olf: I thought I told you two lap dogs to never bother me at work. Percy laughs a deep course laugh while Frank smiles. Frank: Listen Olf we have no beef with ya, but Mr Wallace does and if he tells us to come here then here is where we will go. Percy: Yeah. Olf’s eyes roll up before he utters his response. Olf: Well get on with it then what does Wallace want this time. Percy: You know what he wants you big… Frank: Olf look out. Suddenly from out of know where Jake Hancock charges the Strongman and starts laying in punch after punch into the big mans mid section. Percy: F**K Frank lets got out of here before we get dragged into it. All Frank can do is nod as the two fighter near the wall and hover dangerously close to the 8 foot window which looks down into the parking lot below. Percy: FRANK, COME ON. Suddenly the big Finn lets out a scream of rage and throws a punch which Jake barely avoids and slams into the window sending cracks all a long the window. Percy: NOW! Frank stands there in terror as Jake moves away from the window and sends a whirlwind of punches into the kidneys of Olf, Olf turns round with another wild swing but this time while Olf his of balance Jake hits a devastating uppercut which sends Olf of his feet and crashing throw the second storey window. Frank: S**T The loud breaking of wood can be heard from below as Jake stands at the broken window looking down his breathing heavy, a smile comes over the mans face. Jake: I’ll see you at Road to Ruins! With that Jake walks away and the camera man moves forward and points the camera down below into the dark cold night. Gasps can be heard along with ‘Holy S**t’ chants from the fans in the arena. Down below a massive hole can be seen in the top of a trailer. One Thing We Can Agree On…Junior’s a Screw-head! Oh, yeah. After the shit that Sonny Silver and Chris Locke pulled earlier, there WOULD be hell to pay. Both of the aforementioned people were seated next to each other, Chris looking at the floor in is usual apathetic demeanor. Whereas, Sonny, arms folded, looking away from The Silent Assassin, fixated on the wall to his left. In front of the pair on the other side of the desk…one pissed-off Mafioso/OSF owner by the name of Junior DaVatta. Oh, you could tell easily that Junior didn’t like the circumstances one bit. He speaks up to the feuding pair. Junior: I don’t get it. I just don’t get it. Are you guys just planning to commit ritualistic career suicide?! The OSF Owner sits up from his desk and glares at the pair, who both look back at him. Chris Locke gazing listless, while Sonny remains uninterested in anything his openly admitted enemy had to say. Junior: You two knew what rules were in place, didn’t you. You were, under any circumstances, NOT to touch each other no matter what! But what’d you do? You ATTACKED each other…in front of children. This was supposed to be a sponsored basketball game; a completely carefree day of fun…but that didn’t stop you from trying to damn near skin each other alive! I should just fire you nuisances right here on the spot! Chris narrows his eyes while Sonny only continues to glance in every direction but in front of him. Both of them were clearly listening to DaVatta’s rant, but were fearful of what he was going to say next. Junior clears his throat for a moment, then leans back in his chair and continues with his vocal tirade. Junior: …But the boys looked at some of the numbers…apparently, for some ungodly reason unknown to me, the ratings for those segs did get us some attention…plus, you DID manage to raise some money for the children. Sonny(muttering): You mean for your betting pools… Junior(yelling): WHAT?! Sonny(normal voice): Huh? What? Who? Zoink? Snuff? Giz… Junior: ENOUGH! Sonny, you and your quiet boy-toy are in enough shit as is. Don’t sit there and ridicule me! Chris(muttering): Imbecile… Lucky enough, Junior did not catch the last one from Locke, so he went on. Sonny: So, ladies, tell you what I’m gonna do…both of you…will settle this once and for all! Road to Ruins, one on one, L.A. Street Fight. You two will duke it out at a secure location in L.A. already picked out for you. I’ll let you goofs just kill each other yourselves. Might as well get some ratings out of it. Also, there MUST be a winner this time! None of this “double KO” crap! Sonny’s eyes bulge wide as he bolts up from his seat while Chris sits back with a sinister half-smirk. Sonny: I don’t know what you’re pulling, Mario, but… Junior: SHUT UP! You cross me again, Sonny, and you’re gonna not only be NOT fired, but I’ll personally make sure that your stay in OSF is a LONG and unpleasant one. Biting his lip, Sonny gets seated again. Whereas, Chris looks at him with evil intentions. Junior: One more word of advice…IF you touch each other again until Road to Ruins, I will make sure I carry through with Sonny’s promise AND you, Locke, will be fired immediately! Understood? You two fuckers are dismissed. Wait. Did he just call The Silent Assassin and the King of Sonlight the dreaded seven-letter no-no? Way to earn some friends, DaVatta. Sonny and Chris have clearly had enough. Chris: Fine, DaVatta. I’ll play it your way. But if you EVER pull shit like what went down last week again, you’ll be the one worrying about a long, unpleasant stay in OSF. Sonny: …what Captain Zoloft said. I, as the King, God, Archbishop, Crown Prince, Don, and Starship-MOTHERFUCKING-Commander of Sonlight decree this, DaVatta. SON-FU! BOW DOWN lesson number one 167…”It is better to open your mouth and release the inner spirit’s mighty fury than to make a hollow-winded breath of air.” Both Junior and Chris turn to Sonny. Junior and Chris: What? Sonny glances at both of them, then solely on Junior. Sonny: Translation: Kiss my SONLIGHT-POWERED GRITS, BEE-YOTCH~! The Radiant One then proceeds to do some bizarre SON-FU! pose, which involved the almighty whooping crane, a loud Xena-esque war cry…oh, and the mighty double deuce. You know, enough middle fingers to make a certain Texas Rattlesnake blush. A loud pop was heard throughout the crowd as Junior looks at him with disgust. Sonny stops posing, then looks at Locke, then points to the door. Sonny: Come along, Captain Zoloft! We have crime to fight, fuckwits to fuck up, and future Sonbathers to enlighten with SON-FU! BOW DOWN! Chris just furrows his brow at Sonny, who is flexing his muscles, a la Hulk Hogan. Chris: Oh, just die already. He leaves the room as Sonny then turns to Junior. Sonny: Yeah, just die already. Jeez. The Godfather gimmick was taken by Angelo Deville a LONG-ASS time ago! Junior: He was talking to you, asshole! Not just get out of here! Sonny: Enough of your heresy, damn it! I’m gone! To quote M2…PAY-CE! As Sonny makes his way out the door, Junior is calmly rethinking his previous “not firing Locke and punishing Sonny” policy. Oh, the hell you hath unleashed upon OSF when you decided not to get rid of Chris…but especially Sonny. In The Lap Of Luxury It's another show. And another autograph opportunity with David Fozinelli and Rhiannon Rogers, aka Dynamic Tension. Things have picked up a little bit since the last show. In that there were no people showing up at the table last time, and a few people actually give the team glances this time. Rogers: I've said it before and I'll say it again... this was a really stupid idea. Fozinelli: Now, come on, Rhi. We've signed what... three autographs already tonight? That's a lot more than we did last time. Rogers: Sure, whatever. Fozinelli: You know what you need? You need a tasty hot dog, that's what you need. I'll go get one for you. Rogers: That's okay. I already ate. Fozinelli: Okay, then I'll go get me one. I'll be right back. Rogers: Whatever. Standing up from the table, Fozinelli walks down the concourse, searching around for a hot dog vendor, his trenchcoat full of various stolen items lightly flapping behind him. Fozinelli: Damn, you can never find a concession stand when you need it. These wrestling arenas are too darn big. As he wanders aimlessly down the concourse, none of the fans he passes recognizing him, his hand snakes out, reaching into pockets and removing watches from wrists almost faster than the eye can see. Fozinelli doesn't even seem to notice he's doing this; his hand seems to move of its own accord. Fozinelli: Now, if I were a hot dog stand, where would I go? Fozinelli's eyes suddenly lock onto an unmarked door, sitting slightly ajar. Fozinelli: Wonder if... 'HEY! WHO STOLE MY WALLET?' The sound of the shouting voice causes Fozinelli's eyes to go wide, and he turns around to see several security guards looking in his direction. Quickly, Fozinelli heads to the open door, dashing inside and slamming the door behind him. As the sound of pursuit from outside the door passes by, Fozinelli breathes a sigh of relief. Fozinelli: Now that was close. 'I say, what are you doing in our box?' Fozinelli jumps as he hears the voice, turning around to see three grey-haired men in well-tailored suits staring back at him in curiosity. Fozinelli's eyes go wide as he sees the men; in his eyes flash expensive watches, fat wallets, glittering rings. He's hit the mother lode. Fozinelli: Uh... Rich Guy #1: Well, did you hear me? What are you doing in our luxury box? If you do not answer me I will be forced to call security. Thinking fast, Fozinelli blurts out. Fozinelli: I'm... a chauffeur! That's it. Rich Guy #1: Chauffeur? But where is your uniform? Fozinelli: I, uh... I left it at home. Sorry. Rich Guy #2: But whose chauffeur are you? Fozinelli: Um... Moving his finger around the room, Fozinelli randomly points at one of the men. Fozinelli: Him! The other two guys turn to look at the man Fozinelli has pointed to. Rich Guy #1: Frederick! I didn't know you had a chauffeur! Rich Guy #2: Bravo, old chap. Frederick: Well, thank you, gentlemen. But the thing is... I drove myself here. Fozinelli quickly jumps in to the conversation. Fozinelli: Well, your wife hired me. Frederick: Really? Ethyl did that? Fozinelli: Yeah, as a surprise. She knows how much you hate driving yourself, so she sent me to drive you home after the show. Rich Guy #1: How very thoughtful of her, Frederick! Frederick: Yes, that's my Ethyl. Always thinking of me. Fozinelli: But, thing is... in order to be your chauffeur, I'm going to need your keys. Frederick: Oh, but of course. Here you are, young man. [Frederick reaches into his pocket and hands Fozinelli his car keys. Fozinelli sticks them into a random pocket.] Fozinelli: Thanks. [The three men turn back toward watching the arena outside the large picture windows in their luxury box. After a few moments, they turn back to Fozinelli.] Rich Guy #1: I'm sorry... was there anything else? Fozinelli: Oh, well... I'll also need Frederick's watch. Frederick: My watch? Whatever for? Thinking for a moment, Fozinelli tries to think of a good reason for this one. Finally, his eyes light up. Fozinelli: Um... your wife, she wants to have it engraved for you. Another surprise from her. Frederick: My, my. That Ethyl, she does love her romantic gestures. So, wait... you're a chauffeur AND a jeweler? Fozinelli: Well, yeah. Today's economy, guy has to hold down two jobs sometimes just to make a living. Frederick takes the watch off his wrist and hands it to Fozinelli, who sticks it into another pocket. Rich Guy #1: Say, while you're doing Frederick's, you think you might do mine too? Fozinelli beams. Fozinelli: Why, sure! As a matter of fact, we're offering a special this week: pay for one watch engraving and get two free! Rich Guy #1: Well, how fortunate! Just have the back engraved with my initials. Steven James Hollingsworth. SJH. Hollingsworth hands his watch to Fozinelli, who tries to keep from snickering as he pockets the watch. Rich Guy #2: As long as Frederick is getting two watches done free... why not? Daniel Uriah Manningford. Initials are... Fozinelli: I got it. Hand the watch over. Shrugging, Manningford removes his watch and hands it to Fozinelli as well. Fozinelli: Well, thanks a lot gentlemen. I'll have these done by the time the show's over. Hollingsworth: Splendid. Have a good evening now, young man. The men turn back to the arena. After several silent moments, they turn back to see Fozinelli still standing there, a sheepish _expression on his face. Fozinelli: Guys, I... I can't keep this charade up any longer. I have to tell you the truth. See, the thing is... I'm not really a chauffeur. Or a jeweler. Manningford: Really, now. The three rich old men stare suspicious glances at each other. Fozinelli: See, the truth is, I'm really... with the Federal Bureau of Investigation. Raised eyebrows all around. Frederick: Really, now? Fozinelli: Yes. You see, there's a thief that's been making his rounds in this arena, pilfering anything he can get his hands on. We've been tracking him for a while now, and if you could cooperate with me tonight, I would really appreciate it. The other two men nod, but Hollingsworth looks suspicious. Hollingsworth: If you're an FBI agent... let's see your ID. Fozinelli: Oh, yeah, that. I should have one of those in here somewhere. Be just a second. Rustling through his coat, Fozinelli tosses out several small items from his pocket, searching for something. A tuning fork. Several rubber bands. A small frog that the three rich men rear back from as it hops away. Finally, Fozinelli pulls out a small wallet-sized object and holds it out. Fozinelli: There we are. That good enough for you? Fozinelli quickly closes the badge and tucks it away. Hollingsworth's eyes narrow. Hollingsworth: Wait... let's see that again. Fozinelli: Fine. Fozinelli flips the badge open again. Hollingsworth: The picture on that badge... it's a black man. Fozinelli: So? Hollingsworth: It doesn't look like you at... Fozinelli: What, are you some kind of racist or something? Hollingsworth: No! But you're not... Fozinelli: Not good enough to be an FBI agent, is that it? Oh, it's fine to let a black man drive your car and engrave your watch... but just watch him try to be an FBI agent and look how people start getting uncomfortable. Hollingsworth looks increasingly frustrated. Hollingsworth: But you're not black! Fozinelli: Right, I'm 'colored' or I'm a 'Negro' or whatever racist term you have for me, that's what this is about. Well, I won't stand here and have my race maligned. It's people like you that forced men like Martin Luther, Luther King and King Kong Bundy to fight for equality among the races. Well, I won't have you belittle me like that, sir. SAY IT LOUD! I'M BLACK AND I'M PROUD! Fozinelli begins to do a strange little shuffling dance, while the three rich men stare at him in confusion. Fozinelli: Get on up. Stay on the scene. Like a sex machine. I FEEL GOOD! Doing a quick spin to finish off his dance, Fozinelli whirls back around to face Hollingsworth. Fozinelli: Alright, folks. I'm going to need all your wallets right now. I need to check IDs to make sure everyone here is really who they say they are. Hand them all over... unless you think a black man isn't worthy of holding your wallet, that is. Hollingsworth grumbles, while all three men hand Fozinelli their wallets. Fozinelli makes a big show of opening each wallet and checking the licenses. Fozinelli: Let's see. Frederick Van Rothschild... check. Daniel Manningford... check. Steven Hollingsworth... Fozinelli looks down at the license. Back up at Hollingsworth. Fozinelli: Ah ha! I've got you! Rushing forward, Fozinelli tackles Hollingsworth to the ground. Hollingsworth: Aaah! What is the meaning of this? Fozinelli: Thought you could hide behind the phony ID, did you, Snake? Well, I'm on to you. You're under arrest for grand larceny, pickpocketing, and putting your filthy little hands where they don't belong. Hollingsworth: This is ridiculous! I'm a well-respected member of the community! Fozinelli: Oh, yeah? Well, what do you call this? Reaching into the pocket of Hollingsworth's jacket, Fozinelli pulls out a handful of items. Manningford: Hey... that's my watch! Frederick: Mine too! And my car keys! Fozinelli: Yep. Snake here thought he had gotten them away from me when I wasn't looking, didn't you, Snake? But I'm too fast for you. Hollingsworth: I'm innocent! This is a frame-up! Fozinelli: Oh, right. Next you're going to tell me that I'M not an FBI agent, but that I'm a pickpocket and that I snuck the watches and keys into your pocket when I tackled you in order to make it look like you were a thief. Is that your story, Snake? I'll buy that for a dollar. Frederick and Manningford stare down at Hollingsworth with pitying eyes. Frederick: And to think I trusted him. Manningford: I mean... to not only be a thief, but a racist thief? Terrible. Standing up, Fozinelli brushes off his coat. Fozinelli: Tell you what, boys. Why don't you watch the suspect here and I'll be right back to arrest him? Frederick: Sure thing, agent. Fozinelli: But this is important. I don't want ANYONE leaving this room until I return, do you understand? We need to preserve the crime scene... or something. Anyway, I'll be right back with a SWAT team to take this guy in. Manningford: Agent, can we have our wallets back now? Frederick: Yeah, and our watches? Fozinelli: Evidence, fellas. I'll give them back to you once this scumbag's in custody. But just wait here and I'll be back as soon as I can. But DON'T leave this room or open that door for anyone. Manningford: Sure thing, agent. Fozinelli walks out of the room and closes the door behind him. Frederick: Wow, he's a damn good agent. Manningford: Yeah, for a black guy. Frederick: Oh, now, don't you start. The Last Bit of Community Service...Whew! The Last Piece of Community Service…Whew! It all had come down to this…after weeks of televised idiocy involving the YMCA and to AA, it would all come to this…both Sonny and Chris are going to have to get along to make this work. Both of the feuding former CDC’ers-turned-OSF rookie sensations are helping to coach two separate children’s teams outside a local Boys and Girls Club. Get it? Got it? What? Still don’t? Well, just watch it. It’s entertaining! The words “RECORDED EARLIER” appear on the lower right hand corner of the screen as we open the scene up to a massive outdoor basketball court outside a Chicago-based Boys and Girls Club facility. A massive chain-link fence to halt off unsolicited entry surrounds the area. On two big sets of bleachers sit the parents of the children participating in the game and some other spectators who were lucky enough. The children participated in a raffle earlier in the month and 10 lucky children got this opportunity to have a basketball game coached by two men… “All right, folks, that little ditty was a move I call the SONLIGHT-POWERED 3-POINTER!” On one side of the court Sonny Silver, decked out in a Chicago Bulls jersey, black athletic shorts, and a pair of black sneakers, stands over a team of five children, all wearing orange jerseys. He had a whistle attached to a chain on his neck. He just got through making a successful 3-point shot from halfway down the court. Now he proceeds to coach the team in his own special way… Sonny: Kids, if you kick the posteriors of those unworthy pooheads, I’ll take you out for pizza, ice cream, and whatever the heck else you can shovel into your out-of –shap…I mean, slowly developing bodies! The kids in his camp cheers loudly as Sonny smirks like an ass. Sonny(to himself): Oh, yeah. If junk and sweets don’t get their asses in gear, a good cattle prodding may be in order… Meanwhile on the other side, is the man Sonny had many, many, many qualms with over the last five months. Chris Locke, sporting a plain white t-shirt, brown khakis, and black boots, stands over his group of kids, not saying a word to them. As the children looked over to see the kids on Sonny’s side, cheering, Chris looked down at them. Chris: Pay no attention to him. You must focus upon me. One of the kids raises his hands impatiently as Locke rolles his eyes. Chris(sighing heavily): What is it? Kid(lisping): Um…mithter, what’s a Sonlight-pow…power…what that other guy said? The Silent Assassin simply nods his head disappointingly, then looks down at the boy. Chris: What’d I just say about paying attention to me? We have a game to win! As the kids back away from his outburst, a skinny, white referee makes his way to the center of the court and motioned for Sonny and Chris to approach him. Sonny happily does so, but Chris practically takes his sweet time during the short walk. After waiting impatiently, Sonny frowns as he whispers to the ref. Sonny: Why is he near kids? The man is a killer! He eats babies, so if one of those kids disappears during this, you know whom to look at! He pretends to wipe his nose with his hand while pointing his thumb at Locke, who nearly glares at him and thinks about the ways that he would like to impale Sonny with a sharp, yet rusty hook. The referee just shakes off Sonny’s stupid proclamation and continues on regardless. Ref: All right. This is just a game. Just help the kids along and it doesn’t matter who wins, just so long as… But, OH, NO! Sonny just has to throw his objections in here during a quiet huddle that can only be heard by those in said huddle. Sonny: You’ve got to be bullshittng me, you fuckwit! Winning is EVERYTHING! I mean, come on! Do you want these kids to grow up thinking it’s okay to just sit back and let everybody smack you in the face? I mean, look at Chris. He clearly has let his mug get bitchslapped by every Tom, Dick, Harry, and Crazy Uncle Jeff! That’s not cool! That’s not the way of a Sonbather! No, Sonbathers fight back! They overcome adversity and shed light into the otherwise corrupt, gloomy, darkness-shrouded world! The referee sighs as these words come out of Sonny’s mouth. Chris furrows his brow and now pictures the happy scene of stabbing Sonny to death with a dagger fresh out of a 20-below freezer. Ref: Okay, fine, whatever. Just…have fun and…try not to make a spectacle of yourself! That’s when Sonny grabs the ref by the shirt. Sonny: Bitch, I don’t know what anybody told you, but my testicles are in working… Chris clears his throat. Chris: He said “spectacle,” halfwit. Sonny lets go of the ref and brushes off his uniform, then gives him a friendly pat on the back. Sonny: Sorry, man. You know I’m just playing… That’s when the Radiant One whispers something in the ref’s ear. Sonny: Your tires are SO getting slashed when this is over! As the ref looks fearfully at Sonny, who points the FINGER-O-DOOM at him, both of the OSF fighters go back to their respective spots and wait for the ref. The kids take their positions, then a moment later, the ref blows the whistle as the game begins. People in the stands begin cheering on their children and generally having fun. Sonny is also doing the same thing…to some extent. In his hands, he holds a king-size bag of Starburst. Sonny: Remember what we discussed children! And remember what Vince Lombardi said…if you lose, you’re out of the family! One kid on Sonny’s team has the ball and bounces it before a kid on Chris’ team snatches it from him. He gets the ball and dribbles it across court before another boy on Sonny’s team actually winds up and hits the kid in the stomach. The kid falls down to his knees while the boy responsible for the punching takes the b-ball and runs. Spectators gasp as the ref blows the whistle. Ref: FOUL! FOUL! Chris grimaces as Sonny smirks proudly. The action ceases about one minute into this game as the ref runs in to check on the kid. Ref: You okay, kid? In between heavy breaths and signs of tough pain, the kid nods his head “no.” Sonny: HA! Take that, you little cheater! Anybody that works with Chris Locke is clearly up to no good! Families in the crowd start shouting at Sonny’s outrageous comment as the ref glares at the much taller Sonny. Ref: You TAUGHT that kid to hit him? Sonny: Damn right I did! That boy stole the ball from one of my own! He was defending the pack! Chris Locke and his kids are scum compared to those of my camp and he knows it! Ref: What the hell is wrong with you? The kids CAN take the ball from him while dribbling and what your doing is wrong. That’s when The King of Sonlight looks extremely pissed at him, as his face turns beet-red. Sonny: HERESY! HERESY, I SAY! This ref has clearly been possessed by the same foul stench of evil Locke is possessed with. HERESY! HERESY! HIS WORDS ARE NOTHING MORE THAN HERESY! Well, no more! Sonny: Screw you! You know my words are true and you’re just mad because A) My team was winning. B) You know you suck Donkey Nuts. Finally, C) You are no better than some low-life criminal, so just go back to shacking up with the Unabomber, you fuckwit! That’s when the unthinkable happens…. … … … … … … … … Locke and Silver wind up and slap each other! Oh, yeah, folks. THAT’S a big foul! Sonny and Chris start beating the holy hell out of each other by exchanging fists like savages as a swarm of B & G Club personnel start to get in between and pull the two apart. The OSF’ers both try to get at each other through the sea of personnel holding them back. Chris: DAMN YOU, SONNY! THIS ENDS NOW! Sonny: YEAH! WHEN I COME OVER THERE AND KNOCK YOU THE FUCK OUT! That’s when the scene abruptly cuts to a picture of puppies. Yes, you heard me right. Puppies! Ah! Look at the puppies!
