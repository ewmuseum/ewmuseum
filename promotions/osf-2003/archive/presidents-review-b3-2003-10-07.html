President's Review: B3

7 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own column (#16), dated 7 October 2003, filed under Skylar Green, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Well, now that the pay-per-view replays for B3 are all out of the way, I can issue a President's review of 'Battered, Broken, and Bruised' without spoiling it for anyone. 1) Michael Jacques thanks Sonny Silver for debuting on the pay-per-view so that Jacques/Cosell is not the opener on the card. And of course this goes on with Jacques saying that he's going to shoot up the card like a rocket and such. Scary, Silver actually has recourse to believe that someone else is 'off their rocker', so to speak. They end by exchanging ideas on fast-food franchise names. Dear God, these guys need something to do before matches. 2) Sonny Silver with more face-time, though it was recorded beforehand. He comes out and decides to talk even though the show wasn't to start for a while. All this, of course, to say that he is Da Bomb and that Chris Locke is fat and boring. Debuts some T.a.T.u music, and the entire viewing audience audibly shudders. Sonny says that he will show the world 'Son Fu.' Has a cronie speak, gives out Son-light merchandise, and then gets hit by Chris Locke. 3) Another 'earlier today' segment, Miguel Carranza paces around in the back until he hears a car horn that plays Carlos Santana's 'Oye Como Va.' I didn't know Eddie Guerrero made OSF appearances. Oh, wait, it's just two Hispanic guys. They say that they've got stuff to take care of, can't be his managers. As quickly as they arrive, they leave, saying they have a replacement for him. Carranza's about to leave when he gets a telegram that makes him wait. 4) Commercial for OSE Slam! on the PS2. 5) Inphino shows up and is off-camera in twenty seconds. I wish all of his non-fighting appearances were that short. 6) Chris Locke and Sonny Silver beat each other back and forth until a Spinebuster from Locke (which was also a high DDT from Silver) produced a draw by double knock-out. 7) Backstage at Agent X's locker room (it's odd that we can give everyone their own locker room, whatever happened to sharing space?), there is a letter that says that he's not an agent, nothing special about him, he's just a guy with a job to do and a large oaf to contend with. Odd, if he was just a normal guy, he'd probably, you know, have an actual name and not act all CIA-ish from time to time. The letter on the door is, as it explains, because he can't be bothered with cameras at the moment. Wow, he's on a televised product and he can't be bothered with cameras. X, once you enter the arena, you're at the cameras' mercy. Outside, you can make those decisions. 8) M2 is a little bit drunk, apparently. He believes that Inphino Blitz is a very unattractive woman. Michael Jacques tries to talk with him. M2 assumes that Michael Jacques is Michael Jackson, and is about to shoot him when Jacques promises M2 a date with Janet. He lives to fight another day (or at least later that evening). I assure you, I was shaking my head in a loss of faith in humanity after this segment. 9) Commercial 10) Inphino's harem, and Cianan, are backstage somewhere. Some room. Miyabe and Mina get into an argument, Lillith comes in and it's a three-way. Apparently they all are involved in a fight about Inphino (Mina seems to want no part in the fight or Inphino, but the others don't believe her). A fury of hair, nails, and groin kicks are about to fly. Cianan, always one to know when to back away, makes his move for the exit before Inphino shows up. He suggests that all three women give him oral pleasure, but then suggests (which is pretty much an order for his people) a three-way bra and panties match. What this has to do with Old School Fighting, or a fighting federation in general, is beyond me. People who'd actually like to see a fighting product issue a collective groan as Inphino has guaranteed a flop of a match for us tonight. The audience of 50 pubescent boys seems to like the idea, though. 11) Marcellus Cosell against Michael Jacques. It starts out with Jacques dodging attacks and returning those of his own, but his laid back approach eventually takes away its own advantage as Cosell catches him with an uppercut and proceeds to beat the Tai Chi crap out of MJ. And when he does try to mount an offensive, he doesn't seem to hurt Cosell. 'The Black Beast' (he originally wanted to use the nickname 'Dark Beast', but Marvel was able to have the courts levy an injunction against OSF for it since Dark Beast is one of their copyrighted characters) choked MJ with a book on the throat, but by simultaneously throwing the leg off and kicking him in the groin, MJ bought himself some time. About eight seconds anyway, but it wasn't enough. Note to MJ -- this is Old School Fighting, not Old School Rodeo. Don't bother letting up unless you're sure that you'll get the ten count. Jacques finally won with a Death Valley Driver. 12) Backstage, three of the Clan ninja find themselves out of an office and the presidency by Junior DaVatta and Carlos Vierra. The DaVattas gloat and have a laugh at the ninjas' expense. 13) Bra and Panties match, I won't even go into detail. Mina won. 14) Backstage, Miyabe learns that she was supposed to lose, and she was supposed to be naked apparently. I'm glad she wasn't, we're only rated TV-14 for pay-per-views as it is. Apparently, she failed some test. Whatever. Lillith goes off because she has another match, which was really her only scheduled match of the night. I promise, in the future, Inphino will need my approval before anything like that happens again. 15) Vintage, The Forsaken, 'Oh, yeah, Those Guys!'... you know, James Farwell and Clyde Walkins, whatever they're called these days; they yelled at Freddie. Holy Underground shows up, a cartoonish fight ensues. Mentos is a part of it somehow. This was either a poorly orchestrated commercial, or I'm going to be getting an angry letter from the Mentos people. 16) Agent X against Olf Magnus Svensson. These guys should've fought a long time ago and not burdened us with two months of run-ins and attempts at psychology from a man whose strongest muscles are his biceps and not his brain. Ugh... It was X's speed and kicks going against Olf's brute strength in this one. It seemed like Olf was no-selling a lot of hits. He's a world-class weightlifter, so he's got the muscle and he's put it through enough pain that keeping him down will be hard. X found this out the hard way, fighting him as he would any other opponent, and he lost for it. 17) I debut as President. Enough said. Some people wanted me to say more than I did, but what's the point? I'm not the star of the show, I don't really gain anything by giving a huge speech. People know what they don't like about the OSF product, and what they do like about it. I'm here to accentuate the positives, eliminate the negatives, and latch onto the affirmatives (and that's about all of that song that I remember). I needn't deliver a ten-minute monologue, the show's not about me, it's about the fighters (and to a lesser extent, their managers). So I end the segment quickly. Sometime soon, we'll get to the halfway point of the show. 18) Michael Jacques feels the overwhelming urge to talk with Marcellus Cosell. In his locker room (seriously, I only count five, where are these secret individual locker rooms?), MJ seems to need a crash course in how things are booked. Cosell explains that he needed the money, which is why he did anything in OSF. Finally, some honesty. A guy working for a paycheck. MJ just happens to be walking around with a checkbook in his pocket and writes one for Cosell. 19) The history of Ijinza Yebanka's and Lillith's issues are chronicled. 20) Fourth fight of the night, Lillith against Ijinza Yebanka. It should be mentioned that Yebanka came from Flatlined Fighting. Odd, when it was open, it wasn't supposed to be mentioned by other feds. Now that it's closed, it's all Ff-this and Ff-that! Anyway, I won't go into any major details. It was a 2/3 falls match, and we know how these things work -- the third fall is necessary a full 9,999 out of 10,000 times. Lillith got the first fall, Yebanka got the last two. Yebanka gets two falls in a night, Lillith loses two matches in a night. All is well in the universe. 21) PMS comes back, talks about stuff. It's too much to bother recapping. Long story short, she promises to be the new 'IT' girl for OSF. 22) Shawn Stewart has his prayer group do that extra bit of sucking up to God for his assured victory. When are people going to learn that God doesn't care if they win a title? Pummeling your fellow man is hardly what any section of the Bible advocates to the contemporary human being. 23) Holy Underground beats DaVatta, Inc, and no one except the DaVattas are surprised. 24) After their match is over, there's a fight between Sonny Silver, who never shuts up, and Chris Locke, a man who perhaps speaks too much for the moniker of 'Silent Assassin' to accurately apply to him. Oh, wait, we are past the halfway point now. Go me! 25) Cosell and Jacques decide to walk around now. They come out to the ramp. Jacques speaks first, and has apparently missed the part of the Tai Chi training where they teach you how not to be a braggart. Say they're forming a tag team called Unstoppable. Premature oversellers, rejoice. Some guy from Adidas offers MJ the contract that they originally had with Cosell. This prompts Cosell to hit MJ. They argue about the contract and who it belongs to, leading to ANOTHER match between them. 26) Cosell/Jacques numero dos. Jacques wins. Again. 27) I couldn't give Stewart versus Register the credit it deserves in words. Great match, up until the 'chalk in the eyes' thing. People call it a wrestling tactics, but remember when Token Asian Villain used it against Jean-Claude Van Damme in 'Bloodsport?' Anyway, that leads to Register's victory. Stewart will be getting a rematch for that belt, I'll personally campaign for it. 28) M2 is still wandering around backstage... he REALLY needs a girlfriend. Some sort of talk between he and Sonny Silver. Whatever. Why did I even let Silver keep a camera with him that night? 29) Michael Jacques loses the Adidas contract for the hardcore brawl. Ha. 30) The main event about to start, Inphino shoves Junky Turner aside rather violently. $5 thousand dollar fine for that. Blitz talks about his greatness. AGAIN. You'd think that if he proved it by now, there'd be no need for words. Right? Anyway, as the fight goes on, a boxing match between Kenyatta (who has 2 inches and about 60 pounds over Inphino) and Blitz, it became increasingly obvious that the OSF World Champion was no match for his challenger. But lo and behold, the Miyabi Missionaries save Blitz, and he lands the Inphinite knockout kick to Kenyatta. That gets him the victory, and that would be the end of it if Blitz didn't start talking. Yappity yap yap, luckily, Jason Snow saves us from what would have been an hours-long tirade. The end.
