Live from OSE Arena in Chicago, Illinois June 24, 2003 A choice lurks in the wings... A door explodes outward, giving way to a very angry Miguel Carranza. A moment later, a thin, wiry, bespectacled tick of a man follows. Technically speaking- Miguel: I am citizen! Tech-nee-clee speaking! Man (shaking his head): No. You are here on a work visa. Man: In light of the terrorist attacks of September Eleventh, we had to- Growling, Miguel waves his arms at the man, and turns on his heel, meaning to leave. Man (calling after him): I know you want to be with your father now, but is it really worth giving up everything you have here? Miguel stops, but doesn’t turn around. Man: The job… the publicity… the money… Finally, the Hispanic turns around. Miguel (coldly): These things have no worth. Not when with my father. Finally, after casting one last, under-bitten glower at the man, Miguel spins around and stalks off down the hall.
