OSF — Match report

Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson Vs. The Ninja [Round 2]

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A match report from the OSF site's own match archive, dated 27 May 2003, for the show Assault, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Both combatants are now in the cage. The referee calls both competitors together… Paulo: Looks like this ones gonna get underway early. The Ninja zooms from his corner, sprinting gracefully before leaping and attempting an immediate mid air bicycle kick, Kenyatta parries the attempt and catches the falling Ninja with a vicious right handed punch to the ribs. The Ninja stumbles backwards, only to charge back into Kenyatta. The Ninja leaps and connects with a scintillating shining wizard to the back of Kenyatta’s skull. Paulo: Ouch… Kenyatta momentarily phased, stumbles back, hitting the cage with his spine. The Ninja moves in, shooting his leg at Kenyatta’s knee, he strikes it with vicious tenacity. Duke: Little man’s working downstairs. The ninja attempts to repeat the strike, but his left leg is easily parried away by Kenyatta. Jackson rises to his feet and begins to approach the Ninja. Paulo: Youse think Kenyatta’s pissed off or sumthin’? Duke: Dunno… but the Ninja looks like he’s about to get slammed… The Ninja swings with intent at Kenyatta’s skull, however the stronger Jackson parries the arm away to his side, before opening up with a plethora of shots aimed at the Ninja’s ribcage. He strikes the Ninja four times before picking him up and drilling him straight into the unforgiving floor. Paulo: Ouch… Kenyatta drilling the Ninja into the mat. Duke: That sum’bitch just got kersplatted. The momentary lapse in the onslaught from Kenyatta allows the Ninja to regain composure. The Ninja kips to his feet, and after ducking a swinging arm, dropkicks Kenyatta in the knee joint. Jackson clutches his knee but is not knocked down. Duke: Big mans still up! The Ninja leaps towards Kenyatta, legs extended; it was nugatory though… Kenyatta jukes to his left, catching the Ninja mid-air as he did so, after pivoting Jackson hurtles the Ninja spine first once more into the mat. Paulo: Kids got power… Jackson this time incessant with his attack, unleashes punch after punch into the ninja’s ribs, each punch reverberating around the Chicago arena with a sickening thud. Duke: What the… Kenyatta starts to grimace, as he recoils his left hand, clutching it to his chest. The downed Ninja skulks towards the caged wall. Jackson starts to remove his glove covering his left hand. Slowly, and carefully, Kenyatta peeled the black glove from off of his hand. Paulo: Whats he doing? The sight of crimson stained bandages seemed to imbue Kenyatta with anger. He shook his left hand violently and threw the glove onto the floor. Kenyatta moved with a slight limp towards the Ninja. Duke: Kenyatta’s knee looks in bad shape after that earlier dropkick. The Ninja plays possum, blitzes Jackson, chopblocking his apparently weak leg. Jackson falls to the floor, clutching his knee. The Ninja stalks knowingly behind his larger foe… Duke: Watch out… The Ninja pounces onto the prone Kenyatta, grabbing his knee and beginning to punch it for his existence. His fists seemed to blur, as his hands became black magic, inflicting with each and every blow a stony pain. Paulo: The Ninja’s getting it done… With pent up rage and aggression Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson pushes the Ninja off from him a clear four feet across the ring onto his back. The Ninja is looks shocked, but shrugs it off. The Ninja approaches with caution, as the now standing Kenyatta stretches his neck. The Ninja jumps in the air once more, this time connecting with a flying punch; it does little to deter Kenyatta whom spins on his heels and swings wildly for the head of the Ninja, missing narrowly. The Ninja shoots his leg forward, and connects squarely with Kenyatta’s gut, sensing his advantage he tries again… this time to no avail. Jackson catches the foot of The Ninja, reels him in and hits an exquisite spinal slam. Duke: Damn… that’s gotta hurt. I always wanted to say that… Paulo: Get a life youse redneck. Jackson’s eyes widen as a familiar nefarious gleam grips them. Seeing the Ninja in discomfort, Kenyatta moves over to him. Duke: Ninja’s gonna get squished. Kenyatta’s huge foot comes crashing down with malicious intent, however the Ninja miraculously rolls from danger. However, in his vain attempt to ascertain safety he leaves his ankle open. With a sense of impending doom around him, The Ninja tries to parry Kenyatta’s attempts to gain a good grip on his ankle, with a range of swipes with his left foot. Kenyatta punches the foot of the Ninja away from his body, and using his immense physicality, flips the Ninja into an immoral ankle lock. Paulo: Ankle Lock!!! The referee slides in nearby the Ninja, whose yell can be heard throughout the arena. The crowd rises as the Ninja squirms in agony… Duke: He’s gonna tap out… Kenyatta’s bulky frame contorts like a constrictor around the Ninja’s ankle applying maximum pressure onto the ankle joint. Paulo: Whats he doing? Miraculously, with agility beyond human conception, the Ninja twists and flips in one motion and wrenches his ankle from the taut like grip of Kenyatta. The Ninja struggles to his feet with the aid of the steel cage. Duke: Biiiiiiiiiiig mistake… emphasis on biiiiiiiiiiiiiig… Kenyatta hurtles his shoulder into the exposed ribcage of the Ninja. Kenyatta moves back so he is in optimum range…one, Kenyatta hits the Ninja with his one remaining good set of knuckles in the side of the head; two, Kenyatta follows it up with a stinging elbow uppercut… Paulo: The Ninja’s getting messed up the wrong way. Duke: Damn, his face’s getting rearranged. Kenyatta limped gingerly back from his opponent, residing in the middle of the ring. The Ninja became almost infuriated as he charged into Kenyatta, his sprinting motion fluid, but it was of no consequence. Paulo: He shoots… The Ninja leaps, his left fist extended to Kenyatta’s skull. Duke: Rejected!!! Kenyatta ducks under the left arm of the Ninja and plants him with an explosive Uranage Suplex! Duke: ROCK BOTTOM! Paulo: Look, redneck, it’s called an Uranage suplex. Kenyatta rose to his feet, hobbling on his leg and clasping his left hand, he gained valuable rest by leaning on the cage. The Ninja stood slowly, his eyes fixated on his opponent. With one last surge of energy, the Ninja launched himself at Kenyatta. Duke: … Paulo: … As the oncoming Ninja plunged towards his victim, Kenyatta moved to his side. The Ninja moved past and into the cage when… Crack… Paulo: T.K.O.! Duke: What a shot… Kenyatta mustering all the power he had left uncoiled a hellacious right hand to the back of the Ninja’s neck. He was unconscious before his body hit the ground. The referee walked over to muster the 10 count. 1… 2… 3… 4… 5… Paulo: Jackson’s five seconds from winning this contest… 6… 7… 8… 9… 10! Duke: Jackson picks up another victory… Paulo: At what cost though? Jackson leans clutching his knee against the cage. The lights in the arena flick out. Paulo: It’s happening again… Precious seconds pass before the lights switch back on once more. The Ninja has vanished. Kenyatta looked about in disbelief… “What the fuck is this?”…
