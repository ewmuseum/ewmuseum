Live from the OSE Arena in Chicago, IL November 20, 2003 Ten Things I Hate About [Your Name Here] (NOTE: Please place right after the Silver/Locke fight. Thanks.) Sure, Sonny had just defeated Chris Locke to advance in the OSF title tournament. Sure, he had a chance to win the OSF championship next week against either Shawn Stewart or Chris Locke, but it didn’t matter right now. Right now, all that mattered to Sonny was making sure that his message of being treated as a legitimate threat in OSF was loud and clear. Locke was still sprawled on the mat with a bloodied forehead while clutching at his bad right arm, due to Sonny’s constant attacks on said arm throughout the fight. Sonny stood proudly in the ring with his mic in one hand and the OSW flag from earlier in the other. He scoffed as he looked down at Chris Locke, who was running on an empty tank. Duke: Just what the hell is that hoss doing? Sonny, you won the damn fight, just go! The Radiant One looked around the crowd, who were jeering him louder than he had been jeered in his career. He only smiled at them as he raised the OSW flag to a huge chorus of boos. Sonny: Ladies, Sonbathers, and Fuckwits…I am proud to dedicate my First Round victory to the fine folks at Old School Wrestling. Also, I interrupt this yawnfest of a fighting show to bring you some SONLIGHT-POWERED RANTING…brought to you by SON-FU! BOW DOWN!…the fine, fine art of educating morons like yourselves! Remember…wrestling. The sport of kings! Fighting…BAH! Fuck it! It blows goats! Duke: Oh, Lord. Not that stupid Son-Fu B.S. Sonny lowers the flag as he looks down at Locke. Sonny: SON-FU! BOW DOWN! teaching #24 tells you that “When you have something to getyou’re your chest, make like the loud sounds of a tiger and let out your fury.” Translation: Allow me to give you a few standpoints on how I feel about this company, starting with you, Chris. Look at this piece of curtain-jerking trash. I made his useless jobber ass tap like Mr. Bojangles on smack. It’s people like him that make me wish nothing less than the most gruesome of festering death upon him and every other piece of trash around here! Chris Locke was barely able to look up to see Sonny give his speech. So Locke did the only thing he could do to the OSW sympathizer, given his current state…he raises his left arm to Sonny, who turns to see him, then Locke extends the middle finger to a HUGE pop from the crowd. The Radiant One stomped up and down the mat having a hissy fit. Duke: YEAH! Way to get to him, Chris. Sonny does the only thing he can do…he grabs the OSW flag and begins pummeling the shit out of Chris Locke. Duke: Come on, you Lilly-livered bastard! Stop it! He doesn’t let up as he continues to whip him to hell and back with the hard flagpole. Sonny stops and holds a microphone in his hand again as he looks to Locke. Sonny: You stupid fuckwit, time and time again, you’ve been a constant thorn in my side. As far as I’m concerned, Chris, you’re so far beneath me, all you can see of me is the heels of my boots! All of those fighters in the back. Every one of them from Inphino Bitch to “The Shitter” Jake Hanjob to Olf Weirdshitforalastname…ALL beneath me! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Sonny: Screw all of you! I’m doing you guys a favor by telling it like it is! Starting with Inphino. I hope you get past Stewart tonight so I can beat your tranny ass all over the cage next week. Ever since you pulled that crap where you had Kenyatta “I Swear, I Thought She Was 18” Jackson beat me from behind, that was one of the stupidest things I’ve had to endure under any scripted show. But now that we’re not “scripted”, I’m telling you from the bottom of my SONLIGHT-POWERED SOUL…next week, if I see you as my opponent in that cage, may Sonny have mercy on your make-up wearing Jackie Chan-humping soul! Duke: Just shut off his microphone now! This is getting out of hand. Mr. Sonshine glances around, looking out to the crowd before continuing. Sonny: Then you got Olf. OLF?! OLF?! Who in the Rootin’ Tootin’ Fresh-And-Fruitin’ Fuck names their kid OLF? Plus, he bears a very striking similarity to a wrestling star. Follow along with me, assbrains. One: The man viciously beats women, as seen by what he did to Lillith a few weeks back. Two: The man has taken so many steroids he can’t find his shriveled penis underneath all those varicose veins. And Three: The man talks some weird garbled bullshit that he tries to pass of as a foreign language. Congratulations, Olf, you’re OSF’s answer to Scott Stiener! So Olf Stiener, how do you say “Holla if ya hear me?” in Finnish or Swedish or PortuGermanese, whatever the hell you speak? He pauses for a moment as he looks in Duke’s general direction. Duke: What do ya got for me, asshole? Sonny: Then there’s Yosemite Duke behind the announcer’s desk. He used to be in PCW. He was one of their last World Champions. Great wrestler and all-around ass-kicking machine. Then what’d he do? Got drunk off his ass, gained 423 pounds, and perched his fatass behind a desk doing the absolute worst job as a commentator I’ve ever heard. “Oh, God, hoss! I’ll get that rabbit, hoss! Even my own son hates me for being a fat drunken bastard, hoss! Oh, where’s my tissue because I’m gonna cry like a big, obese bitch, hoss!” Duke: You bastard! I should kick your ass for that one! Sonny: Oh, and don’t even get me started on Shawn Stewart. I’m surprised after all the ass beatings he’s taken from Register, he even knows he has a title shot tonight. Well, don’t feel bad. Cisco choked on Register and he won the OSW title shortly after. After dropping the ball TWICE against Matty, you can double your chances of winning the OSF title! Yeah, Cisco that title win right up, Shawnie! Sonny takes a moment to absorb the tremendous heat he is causing the crowd to give off. He smiles at the chaos he has caused in the past few minutes. Sonny: Oh, yeah, Sonbathers. How can we forget about the Tommy Dreamer of OSF...the man that puts his heart, his soul, his mind, and his body...for a fed that’s on the verge of death. You all know who...Shawn Stewart. The Radiant One looks out to the crowd who go apeshit for the mere mention of the man who has poured every ounce of his life into OSF since it's birth. He scoffs and goes on. Sonny: Oh, yeah. Listen to him go on and on about (mocking Stewart) I love fighting! I love caving in your skull with my foot! I love fighting and if I could, I would marry it and father it's children and lock ourselves in a house surrounded by a white picket fen...PSSH. FUCK THAT! BOOOOOOOOO! The sounds coming from the crowd are high-pitch boos for the insulting of the heart and soul of OSF. Yeah. You don't say stupid stuff like that, Sonny. It makes baby Jesus cry. But before Sonny can get another word in, soft guitar chords drift out from the speakers. Sonny and everyone else's heads turn towards the stage. Sonny: Hey! Damn it! Shut that off, now! This is MY airtime, Sonny damn it! 'I took a walk around the world to ease my troubled mind...' the song begins, but it seems louder, more reverberating, than normal. 'I left my body lying somewhere in the sands of time. I watched the world float to the dark side of the moon...' Sonny spins as the words almost block out the sound of the music. 'I feel there's nothing I can do, yeah. I watched the world float to the dark side of the moon, after all I knew it had to be something to do with you...' Sonny: Shut up! Shut up! SHUT UP! Sonny's realized where the sound is coming from. The fans are singing along with the music as a sign of respect for the man who has defended OSF time and time again. Sonny: Sonny damn it! No! Sing MY entrance music! Come on! I stayed up for four nights in a row remixing my entrance music! 'I really don't mind, what happens now and then, as long as you'll be my friend at the end!' The crowd roars the last line as they look to the stage. Sonny: No, no, no! Shut up, you dolts! Nobody even likes 3 Whores Down! Shawn steps out on the stage to a massive pop, but the crowd doesn't miss a beat. 'If I go crazy then, will you still call me Superman? If I'm alive and well, will you be there, holding my hand, I'll keep you by my side with my superhuman might, kryptonite...' Shawn walks purposefully down the ramp, raising his right fist to the crowd to another massive pop. The cheers break into chants of 'OSF, OSF, OSF' until Shawn waves for quiet. Sonny: Well, well, speak of the washout! Heya, Shawnie Dreamer! Shawn: Sonny, do me a favor and shut up a second. Sonny is beside himself as the fans cheer profusely for Shawn's comeback. Shawn: Sonny, you think you're above this all. You think you're above OSF, you think you're above that cage and you think you're above me. Shawn pauses a moment and smiles. Shawn: Unless I remember wrong... you and I have fought before... The crowd cheers at the mention of Silver's savage beating. Sonny takes in a McMahon-esque gulp, then chuckles a bit before pressing onward. Sonny: Oh, come on. You live in the ancient past, Shawnie! I had a head cold that night. Seriously. I had to take some SONLIGHT-POWERED ASPIRIN to make the head cold go away after the match. Besides...back then, you were scripted to win, asshole! This is a whole new playing field now, devoid of any Mario Bros. running this place into the ground. Shawn: If I was scripted to win, tell me why you bothered with the football helmet I so nicely destroyed for you. Sonny scratches his head in a rather sardonic expression, and then raises the mic again. Sonny: Hello? Scripted? You know those god damned Eye-talians would've whacked me off...you know, in the heterosexual 'I'm gonna kill you, not get you off' kinda thing...if I didn't go along with their stupid-ass scripts. Shawn: Yes, well, I'm glad they told me that. Otherwise, you wouldn't have ended up with that concussion, Sonny. Sonny has clearly run out of excuses on this night as he is beside himself. Sonny: Oh, just go back into your hole and die, Stewart. We're all sick and tired of 'WAH! I blew my shots at Matt Register like a busload of priests at a Little League game!' Oh, get over it. You blew it big time. Just go home to that pretty little girl of yours and let her tuck you in because the Fairy Tale of 'Shawn In Never-Ever Land' is done, dead, and buried like any future OSE title shots! Stewart looks at Sonny, his eyes darkening. Sonny, of course, doesn't know when he's on dangerous ground. Shawn: I'm not out here about any OSE title shots. I'm out here because you took it upon yourself to insult me personally. Sonny: Damn right I did, Shawn. You're a miserable excuse for a human being. Trying to tell me that fighting is wonderful and peaceful! Well, dude...NEWS FLASH! This AIN'T Disney! Shawn: What kind of crack are you on? I never said fighting was peaceful. That being said, I am out here with someone that insulted me, and we are fighters, whether you like it or not... Shawn drops his mic and starts to take slow, deliberate steps forward. Meanwhile, Sonny looks at Stewart intently and scoffs. Sonny: Oh, please. Don't compare me to one of you brainless peons! I'm Sonny Silver! The Only Man In OSF With A Brain! Shawn enters the Octagon, making his way straight at Sonny. Sonny stands his ground, the arrogant grin still on his face. As Shawn nears, Sonny leans out tapping his chin. Shawn grabs him by the front of the gi, uppercuts him in the jaw and pitches him through the cage door. Sonny scurries like a rat in a maze, trying to get away from the dangerous man called Shawn Stewart. The Panther glares at Silver with eyes intent only to bring pain to the man whose vocal tirades have gone on long enough. Shawn glares at Sonny Silver, who trips over his own two feet and looks up, seeing Shawn move a foot close by, to intimidate the Master of Son-Fu. Sonny(fearfully): Uh...Um...Let's be friends, man! Seriously! Du...dude? Shawn simply growls at him. And with cat-like reflexes, a little ingenuity, elbow grease...and a quick kick to the goonies, Shawn is stopped in his tracks and kneels down, gritting his teeth at Sonny, who's now at his feet. The blow only seemed to piss him off even more. Sonny: Come on, Shawn! Where's your martial arts now? Shawn: Watch and learn, Silver. Shawn's words are followed by a set of hard knuckles taking Sonny between the eyes. Shawn tackles Sonny in the middle of the entryway and pounds fist after fist into the body of his victim, eliciting a house full of pops from the OSF crowd. Shawn lets up a little bit, only to deliver the mother of all Buzzsaw kicks to the sternum of a kneeling Sonny Silver, driving all the air out of his lungs. Silver collapses to the ground as Shawn grabs him by his hair, dragging him further up the ramp. Sonny(through teeth): You...bastard... Shawn looks at him, clearly still angry and sits Silver up just long enough to snap his hips and smacking a hard knee into Silver's face. Sonny recoils from the blow and Shawn grabs another fistful of hair before continuing on. Shawn: This ends now, Son. Or does it, Stewie? Exhibit A...Sonny reaching into his pocket. Exhibit B...Sonny using the last of his strength to duck a knee from Stewart. Exhibit C...HANDFUL OF MYSTERY CHALK TO THE FACE, BEE-YOTCH! Shawn staggers back, rubbing at his eyes, much like the end of the first fight he had with Register. Shawn: Ah, it burns! Sonny, you... gah! Sonny stands up and laughs. Sonny: Oh, you pussy. It's just my own special Columbia brand of SONLIGHT-POWERED CHALK! Sonny runs to Stewart. BLAM! Running Palm Strike to the head sends The Panther crashing to the ground. Shawn drops for a second, but his hands start to rub the chalk off rather easily. Shawn sits up, rubbing it out of his eyes and fixing his deadly gaze on Sonny. Sonny: Come on, Shawn! Get the fuck up! I ain't playing around no more! You want some more of the SONLIGHT-POWERED GOD?! Come and get it! And with that, Sonny makes his way behind the curtain, flipping off the crowd before exiting. Shawn immediately rocks on his back and kips up to his feet, the chalk falling away from his eyes as he follows Sonny in hot pursuit. Oh, that Sonny. When will he ever learn? EMT’s rush down to the octagon to help escort Locke up and out of harms way as the scene fades out.
