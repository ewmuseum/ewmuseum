What this is The OSF site's own move-set page for the character, as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Wrestling Style: Shootfighting Set Up Move: Tears of Druzzil (Front snap kick to the stomach to bend them over and a hard knee to the face with the other knee) Finisher: Draught of Fire (Jake kneels low to the mat and rockets up, sending his right foot through his opponents jaw, he lands on his feet and springs up with an upper cut right under the jaw to the dazed opponent) Other Moves: Jake is well trained in shootfighting, using the mixtures of wrestling/submission and kickboxing to his advantage due to his lack of size. He can take a lot of punishment and will do so to gain the advantage, but commonly relies on his speed and quickness. His common stance is facing his opponent, fists in front of his face, left one forward and right one just a little behind it closer to his face. His left leg is out in front of his right, much like his hands for good balance, thus also allowing him to block with his left leg and fire kicks with his stronger right. He's more of a standup fighter rather then a submissionist and will fire quick punches and knees from Mauy Thai. His style isn't set, as he's very adaptive in the things he does, willing to change his stance or style if something isn't working against an opponent and will never get locked into one form of shootfighting. Due to his lack of strength though, he will try to grapple a lot more and apply more submission holds, like ankle locks and arm bars, but while in a locked position, he will fire knees and fists into his opponents side and stomach to weaken them. Angel Kick: Jake kneels low to the mat and rockets up, flipping backwards and sending his right foot through his opponents jaw, landing back on his feet, set to do it all over again. Thunderbold: Jake will lock his opponents arms under his own, making it so they aren't able to punch him and leaving their body open, from here, he will fire knees into the opponents sides and stomach. He will also attempt to sweep their legs out from under them, forcing them to hit the mat with the full weight of themselves and him coming down on only their back, to knock the air out of them. From here, he will either lock in an arm bar, or he will fire rapid elbows to the side of the head for the knock out. Invert Gravity: This is a simple belly-to-back suplex, which Jake rarely uses, due to the closeness he has to gain with his opponent. After the suplex his completed, he will roll through and wrap his legs around his opponents waist and begin to throw fists at his upper body and head. Hsagra's Wrath: This is Jake's best submission hold, he will get low to the ground, almost on his knees, or his knees if need be and grab the opponents foot, hitting the back of their knee out with his free arm and quickly applying an ankle lock, twisting it has hard as he can, showing no care to if he snaps the ankle or not. It's almost impossible to break out of, unless the opponent can flip over and use his free leg to kick Hancock and break the hold. Sunstrike: A simple kick to the thigh, which Jake will preform many times during stand up combat, as to weaken his opponents leg and make it harder for them to move and defend themselves. He will kick with his stronger right leg, always striking the same spot and leg over and over. Ancient Destruction of Ice: He will lock his opponent in a front face lock and push them down to the mat, hooking the arm closest to his body under his free arm and then will fire knees to the top of the opponents skull. He can also bring the opponent to their feet like this and fire knees to the body and break it with upper cuts and quick punches to the face. Punches Jake will use different punches during his stand-up combat, but they aren't to common, due to his lack of upper body power. He will complete punchs in combos and normally use them at the start of a fight, or after dodging attacks from his opponents. Jabs: Jake will jab with his left hand, to keep his opponent at a distance, but allowing his right hand to still be there for defense. He will fire them quickly if he wants his opponent to back away from him, looking for more speed then power. When he's looking to inflict damage with his jabs, they will slow down and he will fire them harder, giving up a bit of defense. Uppercuts: Jake will uppercut with his right hand commonly for more power. He will dodge attacks and pop up with an uppercut, or will use it after a few jabs if the opponent is off base. He will use a lot of uppercuts to finish off combos. It would commonly go jab with the left hand, hook with the right hand, followed by a quick uppercut with the right. Spinning Punch: It's simply just a spinning punch, Jake will spin his body around, striking his opponent in the side of the head with his right hand. When he preforms this move, it leaves him open to a lot of attacks and it can be blocked, but when he hits it...it can be devistating. Kicks Kicks are more suited to Jake's stand up combat. His legs are much stronger then his arms, so he will commonly use kicks on his opponents while standing. He will use kicks to keep his opponent at a distance, as well as to cause a lot of damage. Crescent Kicks: Jake will use these high kicks to strike his opponent in the temples of their head. It will either invovle his foot directly striking the temple or his shin striking the side of his opponents head and face. He won't commonly strike a fully aware opponent with this, due to the chance of it being blocked and the high chance of it getting ducked and having his back to his opponent. Roundhouse Kick: Jake will use roundhouse kicks to the head of his opponent to cause them pain and make them dazed, or quicker shorter ones to the opponents tight. Commonly he will fire them at the head, trying to break through the opponents block with his more powerful legs. Snap Kicks/Quick Kicks: These are just kicks with the foot or shin, that he will quickly fire to the side of the opponent, to knock to wind out of them and force them to lower there hands so he can attack with his fists. They aren't meant to cause a great deal of damage and are more to annoy his opponent. Grappling This is Jake's favourite form of combat and allows him to use most of his submission holds...arm bars, leg/ankle locks, knee bars and other things to immobalize his opponent. He will usually be the first one to attempt to get his opponent to the mat and use many things against him once he has. He normally goes for knock outs once his opponent gets on the mat and likes to inflict damage. Once they are on the mat, Jake will most likely try to mount them, getting his legs on both sides of their waist and sitting his weight down, so they can't get up. From this position, he will fire rapid rights and lefts into their face in hopes of knocking them out. If his opponent puts up a gaurd or half guard, he will just continue to punch through it with all his power. If the opponent tries to sit up, he will push them back down with his open palm and fire more blows to their face. If he is on the side of his opponent, he will lock up one of his opponents legs with his own and lean his body over them driving elbows into the side of their head. This is easier to get up from then his more common waist mount, but if they can't escape in time it can be devistating. His other form of grappling is when his opponent is on all fours, he will place an arm around his waist and lock up his legs with his own and then will fire right hands and elbows into the opponents head. Other Notes About Grappling: If Jake's opponent takes him to the mat, he will commonly use a half guard to block on coming attacks and his free hand to try to counter and escape. He might also use his free hand to fire some blows of his own in hopes of escaping.
