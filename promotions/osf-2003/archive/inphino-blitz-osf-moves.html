What this is The OSF site's own move-set page for the character, as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Wrestling Style: Inphino’s main style is called the Zero Technique/ Mu Shiki, which is no form, no style. Unorthodox, unpredictable methods of martial arts combat unable to be read because of the unstable mind frame Inphino has relapsed into at this time. Kicks are thrown out with full force and speed, and hand strikes and slicing and swinging, slinging his hands in a harsh swatting motion at the opponent in a reckless assault. Inphino now adapts to his opponents’ styles with the following mixtures of martial arts he has devised through his relentless training, the three main sub-forms of the Zero Technique (there is no aerial style because Inphino uses aerial moves for the situation no matter what style; same with counters and multiple target situations). Forms are interchangeable during a bout: Shogeki Style: this is Inphino’s main offensive style. While in this style, he obtains a psychological mind, sadistically torturing the opponent with relentless martial arts strikes. Without mercy in his attacks, he will not hold back on any kicks or punches, going for optimum impact and damage with each blow. Fighting stance shows Inphino in a slightly hunched martial arts position, his guard raised open handed palms facing towards him. DNA Style (Dramatic Nightmare Assault): the technical style of Inphino, using grapples and submissions for the anatomical destruction of his opponent. He analyzes the size and physique of the opponent and attacks in regard with the various technical maneuvers to break them down scientifically. This Fighting stance has Inphino standing straight, his feet grounded and past shoulder width and his guard raised with the right hand at the midsection and the left near the face. Onryu Style: The anarchist within is unleashed, and Inphino is chaotic with a random barrage of all his techniques; grapple, strikes, submissions, etc. This is the darkness from within taking over, nothing on his mind besides bringing complete destruction of whoever’s in front of him. The Fighting stance is Inphino hunched over, his shoulders slumped and his arms dropped down in front of him, looking limp. His head lowers, looking up past his black hair with glazed eyes and a sadistic smirk. Set Up Move: Countdown (A jumping corkscrew roundhouse kick, full rotation applies as Inphino twists back around and hammers his foot into the face of the opponent, knocking them back onto the mat while landing in a crouched position. Stronger impact is made when the kick lands on top of the head and knocks the opponent face down into the mat, where Inphino adds a heel grind into the back of the head for extra punishment.) Finisher: 1 -- Inphinite (pronounced Infinite) 2 -- Inphinity (pronounced Infinity) (1 -- A stiff standing snap roundhouse kick to the face for the knockout. If the opponent ducks, the Inphinite can be reversed (called the Inphinite Reverb) into a reverse snap roundhouse kick. 2 -- Inphino catches the opponent in an inverted reverse full nelson hold in a standing position, dropping back to drive the opponent face first into the mat with a face driver. Then he proceeds to make the opponent submit in a face down inverted reverse full nelson submission hold.) Other Moves: Signature moves: -Doku-DEATH (Poison-DEATH) - Inphino sprays out a black mist into the face of the opponent without warning, blinding them with the stinging mist. The liquid drips down Inphino’s chin afterwards, adding a sick and eerie image of bleeding black blood from the mouth. -Throughout various moments of the bout Inphino will throw his head back and let out a violent scream, coming back to glare at his opponent with dark disturbed eyes. This is called the Soul Scream by the announcers, as well as Inphino. -=-=- Strikes: - Nue Tsumi (Snipping Night Bird) - A flipping heel axe kick, used against the nose or back of the head for a brainbuster kick with standing opponents, and as a crushing blow to a body part on a downed opponent. -Orochi Nagi: Kai (Mowing Serpent: Modified) - Inphino runs at the opponent and jumps, turning slightly to face the opponent’s side and stretching his legs out into a split before his right strikes with a hard side crescent kick to the head, at the same time his left leg slicing the opponent’s legs out from underneath him. A scissoring attack from both sides, parallel to each other (front and above, below and behind). -Arc Rendition ( formerly the Rainbow Knocker) Inphino turns his back on the opponent… much like the world has turned his back on him… bends back, and grabs his opponent's throat. Then he flips back and hits him in the bridge of the nose with a flipping back kick. -The Swift (right hook across the face, followed by a hard uppercut with the left, ending with a cracking elbow down across the chest of the opponent, staggering them back, often knocking them down depending on the force of impact.) -The Swiftness (Same as the Swift, but ends with instead of the elbow an advancing thrust kick into the face.) -Sukumei/Gen’ei/Shinshi (Destiny/Illusion/Sincerity) - a rushing right uppercut, followed by a left uppercut, closing with a frog leap (jumping in the air with both legs kicking out) and hammering down with a double axe handle. -Cross of Depravation (Tsunami Surf) (Kicks into the face/back of head focused more than chest/back) Inphino grabs the opponents arms from either in front or behind them. Then he pulls himself into the air and proceeds to hammer into the opponent’s face (front)/ neck (behind) with hard pumping snap kicks. Inphino will hold this move to the point where he has the positioned so that he can stomp him into the ground without mercy. -The Revolution (double back flip kick combo ending with a back flipping overhead roundhouse kick uppercutting the chin.) -Emotional Scars - Running Bicycle snap kick into the heart of the opponent, stunning them momentarily. - Yami Barai (Dark Sweep) Slides across the mat with a knee to the knee of the opponent before swinging his right leg around and nailing a hard sweep kick into both knees, often knocking the opponent down face first - Ge Shiki (Outside Style) running side straight punch into a strong left uppercut - Blitz - a sprint where Inphino smashes his palm into the opponent’s face, distracting so that he grabs the opponent’s leg. Then he shoves the opponent down face first, his hand holding the leg releasing to punch the prone opponent in the face on the mat. More insult adds a stomp to the jaw while on the mat - Gouu Fu In (Negative Tempest) - Spinning assault of fists, Inphino jumping vertically up and swinging his right hand around in a quick backhand chop, his left hand coming around with an uppercutting palm thrust chop, and landing with a downward backhand right chop into the side of the opponent’s face. -Moshu Kyaku -A kick assault where Inphino lays in a series of thrusting side kicks into the head and torso area with his right leg before swinging around and slamming a side roundhouse rotation kick with his left. - Hyaku Shiki (100 Style) Inphino slides on his back, nailing a sliding front kick into the shin/knee area. Often this sliding kick knocks the target leg out from under the opponent, and drops them face first into the mat. -Moshu Kyaku Revolution - A backflip kick under the chin, stunning the opponent before continuing with the Moshu Kyaku combo -Simplicity -A standing split axe kick, Inphino’s leg raising all the way past his head in a standing split before swatting down across the opponent’s face. Grapples: -Issatsu Seoi Nage (Documented Back Throw)- Judo hip throw with a dropping elbow into the heart right after impact. -San Hyaku San Shiki (309 Style) An inverted Fisherman Buster, bridged for optimum impact, then released. -Ura San Kyaku San Shiki (Reverse 309 Style) Inphino hooks his arm around the front of the opponent's neck from behind, and then hooks the leg. Then he lifts him up and delivers a reverse Fisherman Buster onto the front of the head, bridging for optimum impact -Rewind (Reverse Rana Driver) Inphino leaps on the foe's shoulders from behind and flips back like he was doing a normal Hurricanrana. The opponent flips backwards onto the back of his head and folds over with his stomach in his face -Lunar Eclipse (slammed down into Inphino’s knee for a modified rib breaker) Inphino locks in a Guillotine Choke, then rotates so that the opponent stretches across his shoulder with the hold still applied. Then he flips forward for a sitdown Dominator with the choke still applied. Impact, not Submission -Aura Relapse (Dragon Drop (full nelson drop), knee into the spine, followed into a snap Dragon Suplex, rolling over into a back mount on the facedown opponent and forearms hammered into the back of the skull.) -Void Descent - A reverse straightjacket queen suplex lift, the hands of the opponent crossed behind his back and lifted up on Inphino’s shoulders facing the same direction. Then Inphino tosses the opponent over while holding onto their arms behind their back, dropping to the mat and sticking his knees up so that the opponent’s ribs crash down into them. -Raging Storm - A tiger suplex arm drag, where Inphino hooks his arms around the opponent’s arms from behind in a double half nelson variation and his other arm wrapping around the neck for leverage before he throws their body over onto the back of their neck. -Angel Wing - A set up for a Russian leg sweep, but Inphino pivots on his right leg, swinging the opponent around and nailing him with a 180 degree spin Reverse Russian leg sweep. -Devil Wing - A Russian leg sweep, rolled over into a second Russian leg sweep, followed by a third and final snap bridging Russian leg sweep, the bridge adding extra leverage and impact from Inphino pivoting on his toes. -Psychoticlysm: (swift fireman’s carry lift, Inphino throws body forward and uppercuts opponent in the face, sending them crashing back into the mat.) -Chrystianity - Inphino locks in a arms-out inverted facelock on the opponent, lifting them up and draping their midsection across his shoulder. Then he drives forward, jumping down in a diving cruicifix side piledriver. -Jaded Dreams - A Rude Awakening Neckbreaker, followed by Inphino snapping himself over and driving his knees into the ribs of the opponent. From there he follows with various punches, forearms, and elbows. -Batsu Yomi - Inphino scoops the opponent up for a spine buster, turning around and dropping down, driving the opponent forcefully spine first into his knees. Inphino flips the opponent off of his knees and lets out a vile scream from the pain self inflicted, but stands back up with a smile on his face. Flying: -Wave of Anguish (Black Dragon Wave) Inphino runs at the opponent inside the ring. Then he leaps up in a front flip and smashes into the upper body of the foe in a Swanton Vertical Splash. The impact knocks the foe over onto his back, with Inphino landing on his feet behind him. -Sad Salsa (spring off the cage) Inphino jumps onto the top turnbuckle facing the people, but spins around in a turbine (vertical corkscrew/ 360 vertical spin) and catches the opponent with a swift hurricanrana. -Black and White Wings (springs off the cage) A leaping spin roundhouse thrust kick to the opponent’s face. -Catch-Phase (just whip into the cage, spring off into a diving reverse DDT with knees into the spine.) The opponent whips Inphino into the ropes. He bounces back and Kung Fu Rolls over the opponent. When the foe turns around, Inphino moonsaults over the opponent, grabs his head in mid air, and drives him down with a diving reverse DDT -Sado/Mado (spring off cage) Inphino leaps off with a corkscrew into a five star frog splash onto a standing or downed opponent, afterwards holding his own ribs from the force of impact. Sadism comes in on the impact of the move; masochism comes in where Inphino puts his body on the line. Counters: -Broken Spear (Spear/Gore/Tackle counter) As the opponent rushes in, Inphino grabs their arms and throws them over with a float over Double arm Suplex. -Echolalia (Belly-to-Belly/variation counter) Inphino is thrown over, but lands on his feet in a horizontal position, like a bridge of sorts. Instantly he springs back over and drives his knees into the ribs of the opponent. -Acrosabbatical (Suplex from behind/DDT counter) Inphino simply backflips out of behind suplexes, while he merely flips forward out of DDTs. -Guiding Hand (Clutch/Chokeslam counter) As Inphino is grabbed by the clutching hand, or is being lifted up for the chokeslam, he grabs the opponent’s wrist and presses a critical nerve pressure point. The nerve shock causes the hand to open instantly, thus setting him free. -Rising Column (Hurricanrana counter) As soon as the opponent leaps on his shoulders, Inphino strikes vertically with a rising Axe Kick into the spinal column of the opponent. -Issatsu Seoi Nage (punch/lariat counter, see Grappling Techniques) -Cross of Depravation (Powerbomb Counter, see above in Striking Techniques) Inphino sits on opponent’s shoulders, but slips out and grabs arms, performing Cross of Depravation -Psychoticlysm (Running counter, see above in Grappling Techniques) -Catch-Phase (Irish Whip counter, see above in Flying Techniques) Submissions: -Solar Eclipse (Lunar Eclipse, ending with the guillotine choke applied.) -Guiding Hand: Modified (wrist nerve clutch in a behind the back wristlock position held by the left hand. The opponent’s left arm is wrapped around his own neck, Inphino locking in a dual self sleeper/ wrist nerve clutch with his right hand.) -Batsu Yomi: Kai (Hell Punishment: Modified) - Right after the standard Batsu Yomi, Inphino positions the opponent across his knees in a seated position face up, the head upside down on the mat. Inphino arches the opponent’s back, posting his feet so that the arms of the opponent is behind Inphino’s legs. Then with the opponent’s legs hooked he leans back, pressing his knees into the spine sadistically. -Aura Relapse: Modified - After the rollover Dragon suplex when Inphino is mounted on the opponent’s back, he locks in a full nelson submission instead of the forearm barrage. -Broken Spear: Modified - After throwing the opponent over with the double arm suplex, he holds the arms and locks in a back to back half nelson hold, leaning over slightly for gravity to add to the pain. If resistance, Inphino will spike the opponent down on his head with a reverse tiger suplex. -Hamada - Opponent is taken down with a swift reverse (fujuwara) armbar, where Inphino drops his elbow into the back of the elbow of the opponent before locking in.
