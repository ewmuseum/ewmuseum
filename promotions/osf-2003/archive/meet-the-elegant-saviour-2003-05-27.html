Meet The Elegant Saviour...

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An in-card segment from the OSF site's own archive, dated 27 May 2003, for the show Assault, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
The enervate footsteps of Kenyatta Ahmed Jackson are the only noise to grace the parking lot of the Chicago arena. His face, a constant grimace, as his maladroit limp dictates much pain in his injured knee. With his duffel slung over his shoulder, Kenyatta moves at a steady pace to the exit doors of the parking lot. For him, the night was over. Voice: Rough night, eh sport? Kenyatta shifts his gaze to the shadows, where Inphino Blitz steps out slowly with his arms folded at his chest. Kenyatta: And you are... Kenyatta drops his duffel bag, the thud on the ground breaking the silence between the two. Inphino: You know not? Dear me... I am the Elegant Savior of OSE, Inphino Blitz. I know of you at least, Mr. Jackson. Kenyatta's face changes from one of an inquisitive nature, to that of small concern. Kenyatta: Blitz huh? Sorry to say the name means nothing to me. Kenyatta bends to pick his duffel bag up... but is halted by more words from the Elegant One. Inphino: Of course. You are one that exists in society. Your eyes are not yet open to the truth of life. Kenyatta's eyebrows raise... Kenyatta: What? His eyes lower to meet the icy glare of Inphino Blitz. Inphino: The path of Miyabi... the life led by myself and a few others. We're considered outcasts in the eyes of society, when the truth is we're revolutionists. Live outside the box, conform not to rules and standards. Kenyatta's eyes drop from Inphino's. Kenyatta: Miyabi...? Ain't that something I ate last night? Inphino chuckles lightly, closing his eyes for a second. Inphino: No... no it's not. You can find more education on the OSE website, where Miyabe has a column teaching the ways of Miyabi. Kenyatta bends to pick up his duffel. Kenyatta: Whatever man. He turns to move away once more... Inphino: My Clan gives you trouble, correct? Kenyatta stops dead in his tracks, instaneously dropping his duffel. Kenyatta: Sorry... your Clan? Kenyatta turns to face Inphino, a nefarious glaze overcoming his face. Kenyatta: Yeah... ninja punks. Inphino: Yes, those ninja punks serve me. Very well I might add. Kenyatta's eyes meet Inphino's. Kenyatta: Your point being, Blitz? Inphino: Which round are you in now with my Clan? And how many have you won? Kenyatta smiles. Kenyatta: I'm 2-0, third round outta seven. Kenyatta, almost subconsciously, grips his weakened knee tightly. Inphino: Hmm... nice. My best wishes to you... it looks like you 'kneed' it. Kenyatta, looks to the now smirking Blitz, and then at his weakened knee. Kenyatta: Yo, shut the fu** up man. His voice echoes throughout the parking lot… Kenyatta: He’s… gone?
