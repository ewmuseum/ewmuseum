OSF — Promo

I Love You Haley

17 November 2003

by Jake Hancock

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#151) by Jake Hancock, dated 17 November 2003, written from muh pants, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
“I’m so glad I have you back Haley.” Jake was glad to have her back and he was happy that they could enjoy a few days together before Thursday’s Assault, where he would lock horns against Olf, for the third time in as many weeks, but for now Olf was just a faded memory…he wasn’t as important as spending time with his girl – the mother to his child. Jake hadn’t been this happy, since he stood before his graduating class in high school and gave the best speech he had ever written, but this far surpassed it. All those people were just becoming a population of memories, just like each event in his life, but he would always remember them…he would always take them with him. It’s funny the things that were on his mind…the past. He always thought his mind would be on the future, looking forward to his future with his child, but instead his mind was fading back to the past – fading back to everything he had been through with Haley up until this point. After all they had been best friends since he was three years old. Everything that meant something in this world was Haley Hilton. (Authors Note: A lot of this stuff gets really personal, because I have actually been best friends with Haley since I was three years old, so if it makes no sense, I’m sorry) She was the first person to ever tell him to believe in himself and to actually believe in him when no one else would. When he fell down, she would be there to pick him back up and help put him back together. Every big event that took place in Jake’s life was bonded together by Haley. Yet, for the first time ever he had to be there for her – she needed him and he yet he still needed her. Jake gripped her hand a little more tightly as they walked through the mall in Peoria. His icy blue eyes studying every ounce of her beauty, he didn’t think it was possible, but some how she had gotten more attractive to him. She looked amazing, wearing a powder blue tee shirt and a pair of jeans, which hugged her hips perfectly. Her shoulder length blonde hair was pulled back into a ponytail and her face was painted like a piece of art that should have been up in a museum…he loved her. She was having a hard time noticing, as every time she looked over, his eyes seemed to be far off in some distant land. She didn’t care though; she was just holding him tightly, admiring his spiky blonde hair and his barrel chest. He was her best friend, but on top of that he was the father to her future children and her boyfriend…she didn’t want to call him her boyfriend anymore though. She wanted him to propose, but it seemed more and more impossible that it was going to happen. “Are you hungry?” he asked, pulling her away from her thoughts. “Yes, but of course I’m pretty sure I’m going to be hungry all the time from now on,” she joked. A smile. For the first time she had found out she was pregnant she let out a genuine smile. It wasn’t that she was disappointed that she was pregnant that had made her leave for those three weeks, but it was the fact that she thought it might have caused her to lose Jake. She knew it was a stupid thought, but she didn’t want to lose the only thing that mattered to her in her life. She didn’t want to lose him again. “So, mom where do you want to eat?” Jake said to her, grinning ear from ear. “There’s an Italian restaurant across from the mall here if you want.” “Sounds great honey,” Jake replied ~><>…<> Haley stared across the table at Jake. He was looking out the window into the street at all the passing by cars. She wished that he would say something to her, or show her some kind of emotion, but instead he was once again off in his own little world and she thought it had something to do with her. She didn’t understand that it was about her, but it wasn’t about her in the way she was thinking. “So what are you thinking about?” she let it slip out, wishing she hadn’t said that. “Well, I was thinking about perhaps quitting Old School Fighting and getting a normal job…one of those nine to fivers…I want to spend more time with you,” he said, smiling back at her. “I can’t let you do that Jake…it’s the thing you love and the thing that you know how to do.” “I know, but you’re the only thing that I truly love and I don’t want to be away from you anymore then I have to be.” She couldn’t believe it…he wasn’t disappointed, mad, or even worried about OSF, instead he was worried about her and spending time with her. He was going to give up everything for her, but she couldn’t let him do that – he couldn’t let himself do that. “How about you stay in OSF and I will go with you,” she smiled. “I mean it will be a little hard when I’m big enough that you could roll me down a hill, but for now it shouldn’t be any problem.” “I don-“ “I don’t care if you think it’s a good idea…I will be okay, I’m a tough cookie,” she said, giggling a little bit. Jake couldn’t say no to her and she couldn’t to him, that was kind of a given though in her current state. Plus, it wasn’t a half bad idea…he needed moral support after seeing the Assault card for the 20th…the tournament was going to be a ton, but Olf was going to be a ton more. The monster kind of worried the young gun, but with Haley by his side he felt a little more at ease. “You know…that sounds like something I can live with,” he smiled. “I love you Haley.”
