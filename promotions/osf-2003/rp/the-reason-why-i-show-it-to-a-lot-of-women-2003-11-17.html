The Reason Why I Show It To A Lot Of Women

17 November 2003

by Jake Hancock

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#150) by Jake Hancock, dated 17 November 2003, written from OH~!, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Haley He had said her name a thousand times over in his head and he had played a million memories over and over, not wanting to believe that she had turned her back on him and walked out…leaving him alone. She was it, the one constant in his life and yet she wasn’t there in the mornings when he rolled over in bed and she wasn’t there when he pulled the blankets tighter around him to fall asleep. He hadn’t left the house in two weeks and he hadn’t showered in about as long. The two week vacation from Old School Fighting was anything, but an enjoyable time for the young man. He was finding out that he wasn’t invincible…in fact he had discovered that his one weakness had made him weaker. It was all coming to an end faster then Ivan no-selling. If that’s even possible. For months he questioned who on the OSF roster would be able to stop him at his peek, but now the answer was clear…anyone who felt like it. He didn’t want to go back; in fact he didn’t want to even continue on with life. He had thoughts of killing himself, but that would place him in a category with Michael Jacques, a nameless guy who no one cared about…he didn’t want that. That would be something worse then death. Quietly he laid on his living room couch, empty bottles all over the hard wood floor and his body looked like a semi had run over it and then taken the time to back up and give him a few more licks. He didn’t care…drowning his sorrows in alcohol seemed like the perfect thing to do and even though it wasn’t getting him anywhere, he wasn’t going to stop. She was gone… That was the only thing that he remembered night after night and it was the only thing he cared about. He couldn’t care about her anymore, she wasn’t there for him to care about…to hold and call his own. Jake had never really had a family growing up and the only family he even knew now was sitting in jail for selling coke. That was one of the main reasons he wanted a family of his own, but now he was reduced to nothing. He was pulled out of his groggy state with the front door being jerked open. He had no idea who would be coming over at this time…no one cared about him. Yet as he turned his head, there she was standing there – his angel was standing in the door way, he golden blonde hair pulled back and her clothes slightly drenched from the downpour that was occurring outside. He went to speak, to at least try to explain away the mess that was piled up all around him, but she didn’t give him the chance to. “Jake…there was a reason I left,” she began slowly, tears started to roll down her face. “I found something out and I needed sometime by myself to think it through, to consider everything before I finally told you.” Suddenly Jake looked scared…he had never seen her like this. “I wasn’t sure how exactly to tell you this, but I’m pregnant,” the words rolled out of her mouth… …Jake’s mouth hit the floor.
