Preludes - The City That Never Sleeps

4 June 2003

by Lillith

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#42) by Lillith, dated 4 June 2003, written from New York City, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
It was just after 9:00pm when Lillith stepped off the bus at Grand Central Station in New York, but day or night is relatively meaningless in this largest of all cities in North America. She looked around the huge center a little guardedly, knowing full well that it was a dangerous place to be, especially for a young woman who was all alone. Mind you, she was far from worried, she knew how to handle herself. She followed the masses out the front door into the night air, into a city that is sometimes brighter at night than in the daytime. A cacophony of sound assailed her ears while neon lights shed a myriad of colors, and for the first time in a week, Lillith smiled. She'd been here once or twice before, but never had she looked at is as she did now - a potential home. All she needed now, was money to start. But first, a little fun. She started walking down the street, taking in the sights, and looking for a bar, any bar. They were a dime a dozen in the Big Apple. The first one she came upon was thuming with the rythmic bass of a dance bar. Not her first choice, but it had alcohol and there was no lineup at the moment. And since it wasn't 10 yet, no cover either. She walked out of the street and into a world of sound. Although sometimes it was hard for her to admit it to herself, Lillith was quite good looking, and it was only a matter of minutes before she received her first beer of the night. Over the next few hours, she was wll past a 6-pack, and on a frame of 130 lbs it has an affect. And then around 1, due to a combination of alcohol and lonliness, she allowed herself to be picked up by a a young man who looked like he was a college student or something. In her current state, though, it wasn't his mind she was interested in. Arm in arm, they left the dance bar, and went to his apartment.
