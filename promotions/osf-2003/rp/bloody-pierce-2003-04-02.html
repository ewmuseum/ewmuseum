OSF — Promo

Bloody Pierce...

2 April 2003

by Inphino Blitz

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSF site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#3) by Inphino Blitz, dated 2 April 2003, written from Parking Lot after Slam, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Geez, do a complete 180 with your personality and you’re Mr. Popular. “Inphino Blitz! Inphino Blitz! Can we have a word with you??” The parking lot was full late that night, dozens of photographers and reporters waiting for the big scoop that was yours truly…OSE’s elegant savior…Inphino Blitz. It seemed almost like an overnight change, but after what I had just done on live television to my closest friend…I had the world’s attention. I was sure they were waiting for me to shower me with appreciation for the most artistic way I released Mina de Margo from her duties. Anyone employed in this company would be honored and praised for being dismissed from service by me. After all…I am the biggest star this company has had since Toomes and Locke. “What the hell do you children want?” there was no getting around them…they were barricaded in front of the door, just waiting for the delicious big scoop that was set to come out of my brain through the lips. They were whores for words…never receiving pleasure until they were climaxed with information. I’m the best person to f-ck the minds of PCW. “What is your intentions on firing Mina de Margo on Slam tonight?” one Caucasian blonde man called out, wearing a white shirt and black tie. “To fire her…you WERE paying attention, were you?” “No, why in the manner that you did?” he responded, a bit of concern and curiosity on her face. “You could have fired her like a normal person, but you played with her emotions and then with her heart. Not to mention you spit in her face!” When he put it like that I couldn’t help but chuckle. “Mina is a emotional young girl…I love her very much…but clearly she wasn’t Miyabi enough to represent the new Inphino Blitz. Sure, she was fit for that saint Black Dragon Master, but we’re two completely different people. So I had to prepare her for her deathblow by giving her one truly elegant moment of heaven…before she went to hell.” “Don’t you think your popularity will be hurt by the dismissing of Mina de Margo?” A black woman with braids and a black sweater and slacks called out next. “She was a part of putting you over with the fans, the contrast between your personality and hers.” I rolled my eyes at the fool. “Please. You think her sitting around TRYING to look semi-Goth with streaks in her hair and spouting off sarcastic lines was getting ME over then? Blitz got over with his talent alone, as crude his talent may have been. But now…I have talent, charisma, and elegance unrivaled by anyone in this company! Blitz had nothing but a few good moves…which you see I’ve modified into my own techniques because they weren’t …MIYABI…enough.” “What’s this MIYABI you’re referring to? You’ve used it numerous times since you’ve adopted this new persona, yet no one knows what it means!” A Caucasian woman in a black blouse asked inquisitively. “Now THAT’S a legitimate question!” I waved her towards the center of the group hurryingly. “Come! Come!” I presented her before the entire paparazzi, this brown haired woman in her black blouse and blue jeans. She looked a bit shaken by the sudden attention…funny how those who make the news can’t cope with being news themselves. Placing my hands on her shoulders, I looked out to the people from behind her…my human podium. “What’s your name, chica?” She was hesitant, but she complied. “Heather.” “Listen up people… “Miyabi” means “Elegant” in Japanese. And when I say MIYABI, you hear ELEGANT. Now…when I say MIYABI…what do you hear?” The murmurs and confused looks of the crowd told me things weren’t going along so clear. Looks like I’d have to kick it up a notch. “SAY IT YOU F-CKS!!!” “ELEGANT!!” Ah yes…group interaction…it was one of the best parts about performing for an audience. “Thank you,” I smoothed back my hair and smiled calmly. “Miyabi means Elegant. That name in itself is the verbal description of myself, as I am the walking, living, breathing, f-cking personification of being Miyabi…of being elegant.” “Um…” looks like Heather had another question. “Why have you given yourself that label?” I laughed, leaning my head against her neck. I could feel her tense up…she had all reason to anyway…for what I was about to do. “Heather…o dear Heather… being Miyabi is a way of life. I can’t help but be elegant in everything I do…eat, breathe, walk, talk…fight…torture…f-ck…kill… every action I can possibly perform in this world is an elegant one…and no one can do it as great and with such perfection as I.” She could feel my finger tracing around the silver sphere pierced into her ear. I could feel the cold of the metal…she could feel the cold of my soul…I could feel the shiver in her spine… Then of course, all she felt was pain. She grabbed her ear and rushed off through the crowd, screams coming out from them…of course the loudest being the woman’s. There I stood staring down at the ground…sparse drops of blood on the concrete…a silver earring in my fingers. “…Run…” They dispersed in a gradual pace, taking haste rapidly. Soon I was alone…the scattered media rat all back in their sewers of rumors and lies. “Bloody pierce…” tweezing the jewelry in my thumb and forefinger, I eyed it closely. Then I tossed it over my shoulder, the soft plink of it hitting the back of the iron trashcan sounding out behind me. “Cruelty and terror rain down on us…for the drought that has plagued OSE for so long…has ended.”
