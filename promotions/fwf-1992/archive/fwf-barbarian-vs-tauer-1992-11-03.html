FWF: Barbarian vs. Tauer

3 November 1992

Here it is.. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tonight's exciting Fictional Wrestling Federation action comes to you from the Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta, home of the 1988 Winter Olympics, now home to the debut of not just one but two FWF newcomers, both labelled as possible future champions. On one side is the vicious Steel Barbarian, former several time champion in the IWA, who has targetted the Inter-Continental belt as his next conquest. On the opposite side, the rookie Rick Tauer, a tremendous athlete who, at 7'2", will certainly be the tallest star in the FWF by far. The crowd is growing increasingly impatient, and emits a roar of cheering when the house lights dim. The ring announcers steps through the ropes to meet the cheers of the fans. "GOOD EVENING, LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, AND WELCOME TO THE OLYMPIC SADDLEDOME! TONIGHT'S MAIN EVENT IS SCEDULED FOR ONE FALL WITH A TWENTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT. INTRODUCING FIRST, FROM PARTS UNKNOWN, WEIGHING IN AT THREE HUNDRED AND THIRTY FIVE POUNDS, HERE IS THE STEEL BARBARIAN!" As the opening chords of Gwar's "Gor-Gor" shriek through the arena, the locker-room curtain slowly pulls away to reveal a mammouth individual. As he slowly heads to the ring area, jaws drop as he comes into closer view. For a three hundred plus pound individual, he stands "only" around 6'7", and all the extra weight is pure muscle. His head is completely barren of any hair, marked only by black facepaint with silver streaks. On his wrists he wears two metallic gauntlet-like bracers. His trunks and boots are simple black. His dark eyes glare at the referee chillingly, and the zebra suddenly has the urge to part the ringropes for the heel combatant. "AND HIS OPPONENT THIS EVENING, FROM CINCINNATI, OHIO, WEIGHING FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY SEVEN POUNDS, MAKING HIS FWF DEBUT AS WELL, HERE IS RICK TAUER!" Indeed, never has there been a more accurate surname than this man's, as he is certainly a tower of a man. Allegedly the protege of Titan Morgan himself, the seven foot Goliath strolls towards the ring, high-fiving the fans as he does so. The Barbarian scoffs, looking thoroughly unimpressed. As the big man steps onto the ring apron, he turns to greet the crowd, and they roar as if on cue. Accordingly, the Barbarian jumps his from behind, playing the typical heel. Tauer, taken by surprise, falls to the concrete floor, and the Barbarian is not slow in following him out. As the giant struggles to his feet, the Barbarian sets him up for a crushing running clothesline, and as Tauer finally reaches a vertical base, he charges, dropping Tauer like a ton of bricks. He pulls the mammouth up, and heaves him up in bodyslam position, dropping his throat across the guard-rail surrounding the ring area. Tauer, gasping for breath, is rolled onto his back and again levelled by a brutal legdrop from the Steel one. Slowly, Tauer makes his way to his feet, and after recieving a brutal kneelift to the midsection, is helped back into the ring by the Barbarian. As he again struggles to his feet, a series of rapid double-axehandles to the back refresh his memory as to what pain feels like. The Barbarian, sensing a quick victory, wraps his arms around the giant's midsection and heaves him up and over in a belly-to-belly suplex, and attempts a quick pinfall.. One! Two!! The burly Steel one is heaved of the taller man as soon as the ref's count reaches two. This merely amuses the Barbarian, as he prepares to continue his workout. He cynches the bigger man into a front facelock to deliver a deadly DDT, but suddenly Tauer springs into action, hefting the Barbarian into body slam position, charging into the corner, kicking off the middle buckle and plummeting to the mat in a crushing powerslam! Although this move more or less served only to stun the Barbarian, it worked brilliantly. As the Barbarian lies on the canvas trying to mentally regroup, he fails to see the giant climbing to the middle turnbuckle, and Tauer takes advantage of this, droping a mammouth leg onto his opponent's throat. The heel wisely decides to roll to the outside to clear his head. But the beginning of the match attack is not lost on Tauer, who follows the Steel one to ringside. As the heel is doubled over, trying to shake the cobwebs from his head, Tauer heaves him up in position for an atomic drop, and lowers him right on top of the guardrail, causing men all over the arena to moan in sympathetic agony. The Barbarian remains motionless for a moment, as if his brain was working at trying to conceive how much pain he was in. However, a moment's delay is all it takes for Tauer to charge at him, levelling him into the front row. Tauer tosses his hands in the air, as if to say that the match is over. The now-berserk Barbarian, enraged by this latest attack, grabs a cup of beer from a disgruntled ringsider and slithers into the ring. The referee is still reprimanding Tauer for the below-the-belt attack outside the ring, and as the giant turns around, half-listening to the ref, he is struck in the eyes by the warm brew, and screams in anguish, clawing his wounded eyes. The referee, who is also enraged at this point, turns his reprimands to the Barbarian, who merely shoves him away and returns to attacking Tauer. Tauer, in the corner trying to revive his pained orbs, suddenly finds himself flung to the opposite corner, and the Barbarian leaps at the blinded giant, catching his flush in the neck with a leaping lariat. Tauer slumps to the mat as consciousness begins to elude him. But the Barbarian is relentless, and drops an elbow to Tauer's cranium before rolling him over to attempt a pinfall. One! Two!! Again, Tauer escapes the pinfall, and the Barbarian pries him off the mat, settling him in the corner. The giant, his sight still quite jumbled, feels in front of him, trying to locate the Barbarian. However, the Steel one is nowhere to be seen, as he is silently scaling the turnbuckles from the outside. Suddenly, Tauer feels two hands grab him from above, as the Barbarian positions his knee directly on the back of Tauer's head, and drops forward in a falling face slam, hitting the canvas with bone-crunching velocity. Bone crunching it is indeed, as the crimson begins to flow freely from Tauer's now-mangled nose. Tauer utters a brief shriek, as he realizes what happened, and after uttering a blood-curdling cry, makes his way to his feet immediately, only to be nailed in both sides of the head by a certain pair of inverted wristband/gauntlets worn by a certain Steel Barbarian. For a fleeting second, the big man fades to black, as the Barbarian lands on him in a Thesz press. One! The Barbarian doesn't even get a two-count, as the furious Tauer shoves him off like a fly. The Barbarian retreats to a neutral corner, and ponders what he has just witnessed. He just levelled the big man dead-on with his finisher, the Final Darkness, and didn't even get a two count. As he stands in the corner disbelieving, he fails to notice the giant kip-up with astounding agility and charge into the corner, cracking the Barbarian in the back of the head with a deadly elbow. Before he can even react, the Barbarian is flung to the ropes, and caught in the swirling dervish that is a Rick Tauer spinebuster. Though the Barbarian is hurt, Tauer doesn't even attempt a pinfall. Instead, he lifts the Steel man into position for another spinebuster, but rather than going for another one, he drapes the Barbarian over the top turnbuckle. The Barbarian looks on with horror as Tauer climbs from the bottom turnbuckle to the middle turnbuckle to the *top* turnbuckle, and the giant heaves the heel up and over in a mind numbingly high superplex. The two men crashing to the mat sounds like a thunderclap, and many fans turn away from the ring action, certain that the bout is over. All that remains, it seems, are the formalities. One! Two!! But no! Somehow, the Barbarian manages to get a shoulder off the canvas, and Tauer, undaunted, continues his assault. He flings the battered Barbarian to the ring cables, and picks him up to deliver a spinning powerslam. However, at the last second before impact, Tauer extends his knee, turning the move into a devastating backbreaker. Many fans are repulsed by the amount of abuse that has been dished out in this matchup, but a hardened Tauer continues his attack. He scoops up the Steel one in position for a side slam, but backs into the corner, and twirls his index finger in the air, signifying the end is near. he charges out of the corner, and spinning around, leaps into the air, and falls to the mat, dropping the dizzy Barbarian flat on his back, knocking the wind out of him. Tauer hooks both legs, and the ref begins the count. One! Two!! Three!!! "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE IS YOUR WINNER, RIIIIIIIIIIIIICK TAUER!!!!!" The bloodied Tauer slides out of the ring, not bothering to let the ref raise his arm. The Barbarian is far slower in rising, but does so eventually. A very impressive debut for both men, but there is little doubt as to who was the victor. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- COMING NEXT: Blue Steele defends his Intercontinental championship against the immense Jerry "The Stratofortress" Henderson.
