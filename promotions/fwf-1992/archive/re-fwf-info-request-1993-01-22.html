There is no schedule behind FWF matches, and the actual cohesion behind the FWF is getting to be less and less, due to Jason and I both experiencing "increased workrate" :-), as well as Liam's seeming disappearance. Jason was having increased time with his studies, and this term I was struck with the same affliction. Suicide vs. Lawless is the only match I've managed to put together thus far. I'm hoping to get another one done this Sunday, but my more difficult schedule is taking more and more time out of the time I have for FWF. Jason has had the same difficulty all year. We'll try to keep at it, but it's getting harder. Now that the sound of reactionary netters cheering has died down, I can say that [name withheld] has been collecting past articles, though since some time in October or November that steadfast task may or may not have been a bit more lax for quite possibly the same reason as the writing has been, but if you write to him, you should be able to get some older ones.
