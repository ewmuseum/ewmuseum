FWF: Titan vs. Truck

13 April 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Message-ID: <1992Apr13.233802.15622@CS.ORST.EDU> Date: 13 Apr 92 23:38:02 GMT Sender: usenet@CS.ORST.EDU Distribution: na Organization: Oregon State University Lines: 303 Nntp-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu With a project such as the FWF, there is one thing that is good. That is the fact that I may, on occasion, sit back, do something completely off the wall, and I don't have to worry about whether anyone else in my position is being more dignified. :-) Case in point: the current angle in WCW with Sting and Big Van Vader. Thus, we bring you from the FWF, a World Heavyweight Championship match between the Invincible Titan Morgan and Big Truck Vader, who will be accompanied to the ring by Harley Pace. It took the FWF a long time to find a big enough, strong enough, fat enough dude who was stoned enough on PCP to do the work required, but we got 'im. Anyone who is looking for serious FWF stuff might want to hit the 'n' key now, because this one is shamelessly intended to be humorous, a la "Real Men, Real Fans . . ." In a recent FWF match in which Titan Morgan, the World Heavyweight Champion, was defending his title against "Crimson" Joe Reed, a little bit of inter- ference took place. Cringer, if you will show us the clip . . . ========================== A big fat guy in a black mask that is little more than a collection of straps with coveralls that mix red and black comes into the ring and kicks Morgan off. The official quickly calls for the bell . . . ( *** Jim Ross commentary mode on *** ) "Oh my gosh, it's BIG TRUCK VADER, coming into the ring; he just hammered the champion off of Reed. Oh, no he's got a chair, Vader with a chair into the back of the head! Morgan is hurt! He's lying on his back. Reed's getting out of the ring, but the story is Morgan lying with his back exposed. Vader, off the ropes . . . Oh, no the VADER BOMB! First the Kentucky Backsmasher, now the Vader Bomb to the back of the Invincible Titan Morgan! Vader's trying to break the back of the World Heavyweight Champion! "Vader picks Morgan up, OH MY GOSH HE'S GORILLA PRESSING HIM! THREE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN POUNDS! He slams him down, kicking him until he's diagonal in the center of the ring! Vader's climbing the top rope!" (Vader, at this point, is screaming something on the order of "NO PPHAAANE!!") Ross-mode continues, "Vader, off the second rope, OH MY GOSH, get some help out there. He's completely demolishing the World Champion! Morgan is hurt, and I'm afraid he's hurt badly. Harley Pace is coming out, raising Vader's arm in victory, as help finally come from the locker room; but Morgan is hurt, and he's hurt badly! The jury is out; how much damage has been done do Morgan's back? He's hurt, and he's hurt badly . . ." [ad infinitum about "hurt and hurt badly"] ( *** Jim Ross commentary mode off *** ) ========================== Vader . . . "When I came to the ring and destroyed Titan Morgan, I felt no remorse! >grunt< I feel no pain! >oomp< Morgan, I'm not just going to beat you! >ugh< I'm not just going to destroy you! >ackh< I'm not just going to take your title. >augh< I'm going to make you feel >grunt, grunt< pain! (Holds wrist Berzerker-style) Huuuurrwwwh!" Morgan . . . "You know something, TitanManiacs, when Truck Vader came to the Charleston Civic Center, and blatantly interfered in my match, he showed just what kind of a coward he is! He attacked me from behind, and I saw the cold red eyes, just like the cold blood in his veins, brothers. This man is just like the Charles Mansons and the Sid Justices of the world, Maniacs! And when he took that steel chair in his hands, and whacked me on the back of the head, it felt like the world had come to an end. This man is a menace to each and every human being that ever sets foot into an FWF event! Now, Truck Vader, it comes down to you and me, and the title is beside the point! I've got the easy task here, Vader! All I've got to do is beat a 465-pound lunatic! You've gotta beat me and every last one of all my millions of TitanManiacs all across the country and all across the world! When I walk away from the arena, I will remain the World Heavyweight Cham- pion, and you will be just one more dust mote blown in the face of the hurricane that is TitanMania. Mess with the best and you fall like the rest!" Without further ado, we go to the matchup . . . --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Low-pitch rock music seems to come in on the wind at the Knickerbocker Arena in Albany, New York. A huge man parts the curtains, wearing a gigantic futuristic metal mask-helmet, carrying a long staff with a skull on the tip. The helmet is quite handy in deflecting the shower of decomposed vegetation that is hurled his way by the marks. The hardcores, as well, are not particularly happy with this man, for challenging a great technical grappler with sheer size and strength. But he and his manager, Handsome Harley Pace, bear out the jeers, choosing to listen to the ring announcer, Alfred Grinchley. "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. INTRODUCING FIRST, THE CHALLENGER, COMING DOWN THE AISLE. FROM PARTS UNKNOWN; HE WEIGHS FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE POUNDS: BIG TRUUUUUUCK VAAAAAADER!!" He is perhaps the strongest man in the FWF, demonstrating it by gorilla pressing his opponent in this matchup when last they met. The man walks to the ring with the utmost confidence, and sets his mask on the outside of the ring next to his manager, revealing a second mask which is not so much a head covering as it is a collection of intersecting straps. His red eyes match well the red and black coveralls with which he covers his girth, and his huge size belies his strength and relative quickness. "NO PPPHAAAAANE!!" he screams. As if in answer, the tune of "Eye of the Tiger" breaks over the PA system at the arena, and the fans' voices turn to one in-unison cheer for the man in gold and azure who parts the curtains, stopping a moment in sheer awe at the adoration they give him. The large golden belt is around his waist, the World Heavyweight Championship. This man is the best in the FWF, and it says so right across his waist. The Grinch performs the second half of his opening introductions. "AND HIS OPPONENT, FROM VENICE BEACH, CALIFORNIA. HE WEIGHS THREE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN POUNDS. HERE IS THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: THE INVINCIBLE TIIIIITAN MOOOOOOOOOOORRGAAAAAAAN!!!!" The Grinch has a hard time speaking over the roar of the fans, as the World Champion makes his way down to ringside. Truck Vader holds one wrist in his other hand, shouting incoherently at Titan Morgan. Morgan, for his part, tears the shirt from his chest, revealing an unmarred radioactive orange Promethian physique. He takes the bandana from his head and tosses them to either side of the aisle before he enters the ring, still wearing the Championship belt. This he quickly surrenders to the official, who has no time to display it for the crowd before Vader makes his attack. He flies at Morgan, clutching his huge hands around the champion's neck. The official, with his own hands full of gold, quickly hands the title over to the timekeeper and runs over to put a disqualification count on this blatantly illegal hold. Vader waits until four, releases, then starts in again. Wait until four, again. Morgan grabs around the head, trying to put on a headlock, finally gouging Vader in the eyes for a four-count of his own before making it away to catch his breath. Vader rubs at his eyes a second, and while he is doing so, Morgan throws caution to the wind and clotheslines him. Vader goes nowhere. "NOOO PPPHAAAAANE!!" Morgan almost chuckles at this, and scoops the guy up. He is aiming for a body slam, but Vader shifts his weight a bit forward, overbearing the champion easily. One! Two . . . Morgan kicks out, barely able to get himself out from under the 465 pound man. Harley Pace shouts at the referee about slow counting, but the official doesn't listen, for Vader is again using a choke on Morgan. He drags the Titan over to the ropes and presses his foot down onto Morgan's throat, eliciting yet another four count from the official. Now, he presses the bottom rope across the Adam's apple, getting another four count before he releases. Morgan rolls outside the ring, coughing and sputtering on the mat outside before he is able to make his way to his knees. Inside the ring, the official is going into conniptions with Vader, warning him that the next choke will mean a disqualification. Vader is hardly listening, of course, and leaves the ring to go after Morgan. Vader picks Morgan up, seeming to go for a body slam, but instead lifts the man up in a gorilla press! He slams him down on the thin mat outside the ring, leaving Morgan with a sore back. Fans call to mind the beating Morgan received in the back in Vader's earlier assault, and boo at the monster heel vehemently. The hardcores are even madder than the marks, for the wrestling in this match has been nil thus far. Vader puts Morgan back into the ring, with a wordless yell at the ringside spectators before going in himself. Vader is stalled by a Morgan fist to the midsection, which has no effect. "NOO PPPPHAAAAAANE!!" comes the now-stale shout. "NO PPHANE!" He lifts Morgan up into a body slam, carrying him in his arms, then tucks his head down for a power slam! Morgan twitches a little, but then is unmoving as Vader goes to the ropes, sets up for the elbow drop . . . And hits empty canvas! The fans are ecstatic, as Morgan comes up, with a reverse knife-edge chop that knocks Vader back! Morgan grabs the huge man's arm, twisting it around. Vader's expression does not change! Morgan turns it into an armbar, then quickly darts behind the man with it, latching onto the other arm, pulling up in a double armbar back suplex! Vader heaves up slowly, and comes down beside Morgan much less gracefully. But he does come down, and Morgan seems to have stunned him for a moment. The chant starts to go up . . . "DDT! DDT! DDT! DDT! DDT!" Morgan cups his hand to his ear, hearing what they want, as Vader slowly comes back to his feet. Morgan grabs him in a front facelock, and throws his weight into planting the top of Vader's head on the mat! Vader is too big to flip over with it, but he stays there all the same! Morgan covers him for the pin . . . And gets only ONE! Vader instantly makes his way back to his feet, shouting the clarion "NOOO PPPHAAAAANE!!!" Morgan stands, staring at his unfazed foe in disbelief. For a brief instant, before Vader clotheslines him over again. Vader jumps up and down, making a loud "WHAM WHAM WHAM WHAM" on the ring, reminiscent of a certain prototype in the WWF. He runs to the ropes, and executes a vertical splash down to the sternum of the champion. The official starts his three-count, but Vader stands up off of Morgan before he gets to three. He comes off the ropes, and lands a second vertical splash! Morgan is completely unmoving, and the marks have written him off. The World Heavyweight Champion is now Big Truck Vader, as far as they're concerned. Vader picks up Morgan, hefting him overhead in another gorilla press easily, slamming him down hard at the side of the ring. He covers the Titan. One! Two . . . Morgan's foot reaches the ropes, and Vader seems to smile at the thought of punishing him some more. He shoves the champ outside the ring and follows immediately. He picks Morgan up by the hair and shouts to the God of Smoke and Fire. He drags the Titan over and lets him get blasted by the acrid vapor that suddenly issues from the helmet that lays at ringside. The official runs out and shouts at Vader, yelling another warning of disqualification at the gigantic man. "NOOO PPPHAAAAAAAAAANE!!!" is the answer. This guy really has a one-track mind. Vader rams Morgan's head into the steel barricade at ringside, grinding his forehead back and forth along the top edge of it. Before long, a gash has opened on the Titan's forehead that spills red blood all across his face. Vader knees Morgan in the ribs, hurting him a bit more after the two vertical splashes weakened them, and shoves him back into the ring, where he lies there like a dead thing. Vader hoists him up into one more gorilla press slam, dropping him on his back in the center of the ring this time. He goes off the ropes, and leaps up, dropping all 465 in a Vader Bomb on the Titan! He hooks the leg, as the referee counts. One, two . . MIRACULOUSLY, by some incredible freak of God and booker, Morgan kicks out! Blood sprinkles everywhere from his forehead as he shakes his head in spectacular rage and disgust that this blob would think to so easily pin him! Two vertical splashes, two gorilla presses, a gash on the forehead and a Vader Bomb? Ha! He's seen worse in the late night movie! (Of course, those people were dead.) Vader half-lifts his whole mass off the mat as he hammers down at Titan Morgan. He only stares, shaking his head. The fans are electric in their excitement. Vader hits him again, as he rises to one knee. Morgan again only shakes his head. A third strike, and Morgan is on his feet in rage, shaking his fists as well as his head as he walks around the ring roaring in fury. Vader hits him again as he turns back to the huge man, and the finger is up in his face, wagging away. Vader tries another shot, but Morgan blocks it, grabbing behind Vader's head. Morgan scoops him up, and does a standing power slam on the mat, turning Vader about 45 degrees, but still getting him up. He goes off the ropes, and lands the indomitable Leg Drop of Doom! One! Two! Three! The fans go bananas at this completely impossible and unexpected victory . . . . (Pause for laughter) . . . by Titan Morgan, as the Grinch comes onto the PA system with the announcement . . . "HERE IS YOUR WINNER, AND STILL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, THE INVINCIBLE TIIITAN MOOOOOOOORGAAAAAN!!!!!" Morgan watches as Vader leaves the ring, and the ref hands him the belt once more. This one is over, all but the posing. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- I have a whole collection of "hopeless mark" buttons waiting here for pinning on anyone who actually didn't see that ending coming a light year and a half away. At any rate, there's what I think of the Sting vs. Van Vader feud, and for that matter, any mega-face vs. monster-heel angle that goes on in any federation. If there's a monster heel, at least put him against a Titan Morgan so they can leg drop him and get it over with. Let this be a lesson to all the monster heels out there: you may be invincible, but just keep in mind that the booker is more invincible. __________________________________________________________________________ |Stilt Man --Never ask a mortician to go out and bring you back a cold one.| | [detail withheld]@xanth.cs.orst.edu | |__________________________________________________________________________| | Any and all suggestions for wrestling characters, possible plotlines, and| | other such perglurbage are welcomed and, in fact, encouraged. | |__________________________________________________________________________| | _______ _ _______ | The stories and opinions posted here| | / ___ \ / \ / ____/ | are not necessarily endorsed by | | / /___\ \ / \ / /___ | Oregon State University or any of | | / ___/ \ \_/ _ \/ ___/ | its departments.
