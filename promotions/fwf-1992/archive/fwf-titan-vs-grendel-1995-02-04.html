FWF: Titan vs. Grendel

4 February 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I know that there are going to be some reactionaries here who won't like me posting this here, but it never stopped me when I did this the last time a couple of years back, and the ideas I've been putting together for a parallel story to the current HH-Vader feud are just too good to leave unwritten. For that matter, my usual writing outlet to which I absconded when I lost the desire to write FWF stories the last time has had a slow-down of late (too many people I have to get permission from who are looking through the drafts too slowly :). At any rate, for the benefit of those who weren't here a couple of years ago, I am known as the Stilt Man (yes, I am the same person who (a) wrote Bison guides on alt.games.sf2 (b) writes all sorts of skeptical political comments on the various groups centering around alt.president.clinton primarily (c) is responsible for the writing of the [DS], [AD], and related threads on alt.pub.dragons-inn) and I, along with a few other folks, was responsible for the long-time writing of a collection of stories about a Fictional Wrestling Federation. To those of you who remember me with disgust, don't bother flaming me because I won't put this on here too often (if I even write another match after this one), and those who remember me fondly, don't get your hopes up too high for the same reason. :) This is a fiction story, to some extent a commentary on the HH-Vader feud, which I have no prayer whatsoever will live up to this example, and above all, it is intended to entertain. So please just try to enjoy, and if you don't like it, just kindly hit the "n" key. If I do this any more, the letters "FWF" will be in the title for your killfiles, just like in the old days. At any rate (that's twice), a little history might fill a bit of memory, and perhaps fill in a few blanks if people weren't here for the last stint. Roughly two years go, the Invincible Titan Morgan finally managed to get the Power Bomb of Doom on Doctor Death to pin his longtime adversary and join him as the only men to win the FWF World Championship on two occasions. Morgan, as some may know and others may be warned, was basically my take on what that HH guy could be if he worked as well as acted invincible. I am somewhat of the belief that most of the hatred of the HH guy's act is because he does little else; if Hogan were doing plancha dives and spring elbows as well as leg drops and hulk-ups, methinks not as many people would mind. After all, if they can swallow a God of Lions and Tigers, they can swallow an invincible worker, too. Titan Morgan is my vision of that worker. At any rate (third time), this is a one-time-only (at least, that's my plan at this time) story to picture a match between the reigning World Heavyweight Champion in the FWF up against the monster heel to end all monster heels, in tribute to what a match between that HH guy and Vader could be if the former knew a wrestling hold from a spaghetti noodle, but as we all know, quite certainly will not be. In the spirit of old times, we will get comments from the champion before the festivities begin: "I'll be the first to say I'm disappointed that the Doc couldn't make it here tonight. He deserves this title every bit as much as I do, and only God's will and lots of determination and the love of each and every one of my TitanManiacs saw me through when we last met! But when the phone call came to the house, when the FWF commissioner told me that I was going to have to take an open contract in Baltimore, that's when the alarm bells started going off! I don't know who I'm going to step into the ring with! They didn't tell me! The other guy never would have to do this! But that's okay, Mr. High and Mighty, because it makes no difference! The twenty-six inch guns are loaded, the TitanManiacs are pumped, and the World Champion is ready to go, dude! Whoever it is, he better be ready, 'cause when you mess with the best, you'll fall like the rest!" That having been said by Titan Morgan, reigning FWF World Champion, let us head to ringside . . . ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The scene in Baltimore Arena in Baltimore, Maryland is rather confused, to put it accurately. The original booking had been matching the two-time World Champion, the Invincible Titan Morgan, up against the two-time former champion, the sinister figure known as Doctor Death. Many had looked forward to a redux of the colossal feud between these two great competitors, but then word had arrived that the not-so-good Doctor had sustained a back injury after being struck by Morgan's dreaded Power Bomb of Doom at their last confrontation. The prognosis on his ever returning to the ring, let alone ever competing again in more than a shadow of his old form, was doubtful at press time. The marks were thinking, "Good riddance," the hardcores were sadly paying their respects to a solid technical wrestling heel. The fans in general were wondering what the FWF powers-that-be would be doing. A promise had been made that Morgan would indeed defend the title, but that the contract once given to Doctor Death was now open to any who wished to step into the ring with the Titan. Looking at the hospital bills of the man who had dominated the FWF for two-thirds of its history seemed to dissuade all the serious contenders for the time being. And thus, when Alfred Grinchley steps into the ring, and the rumbling first notes of Europe's "Final Countdown" break over the PA system, the response from the fans is cheers for the champion mixed with apprehension as to whether a real match is going to be held. "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! IT IS SET FOR ONE FALL. INTRODUCING FIRST, HAILING FROM ANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA. HE WEIGHS THREE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-ONE POUNDS, AND HE IS THE TWO-TIME WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION . . . THE `INVINCIBLE' TITAN MOOOOORRRRRGAAANN!!!" The curtains part, and the herculean individual in fluorescent pink tights and white tank top steps through with the ten pounds of gold adorning his waist. He wears a headband through his full head of blonde hair, and sports a mustache and short beard. His blue eyes sparkle as they gaze at all the people who have shown up for this event, but those who look closely can see the confusion there as well. He, himself, has no idea what is going to take place this evening. He heads to the ring in full stride, hopping up to the ring apron, full of energy and poise. He grabs the neck of the tank top and rends it from his chest with one hand, hurling it one direction while his headband is hurled in the other with his other hand. Frantic scrambles amongst the fans break out to be the one to catch both pieces, but Morgan ignores it. He leaps over the ropes into the ring, title belt still around his waist, and swings the arms around, jumping up and down, loosening up the muscles and warming up for the contest. The Grinch, as the ring announcer is often called, receives a note from the senior official, and he nods before taking the microphone back to his mouth. "AND HIS OPPONENT . . ." The crowd silences. Morgan's eyes are fixed upon the Grinch. The music shifts to Andrew Lloyd Webber's overture to "Phantom of the Opera." ". . . COMING DOWN THE AISLE. FROM PARTS UNKNOWN, WEIGHT UNKNOWN . . . THIS IS GRENDEL!!!!" Morgan looks confused. This is not a wrestler who has participated in any FWF event before. John "Flawless" Lawless, "Hurricane" Andrew Simms, the United States Champion, either member of Storm, Aaron "Lightning" Murphy or Jerry "Thunder" McGaghey (note to [name withheld]: I decided to spell it right this time :), "Suicidal" Paul Stuart, Annihilus, all these individuals fell into his own mental short list of who might be up against him. This Grendel character was an unknown. The curtains part, and a forklift drives through with a large wooden box. Stenciled all over the box are the words "DANGER: DO NOT APPROACH CLOSELY!", "KEEP AWAY," and the obligatory "THIS SIDE UP." The forklift operator seems worried to be hauling this package to ringside, and his eyes rarely leave the huge cargo he carries. He drives the machine to the ring area. The box suddenly does a major heave sideways as he stops and sets it down. The forklift operator's eyes become perfect circles, and he kills the engine, leaping out of his machine and sprinting back to the dressing room for dear life. The movement from within the box continues, and Titan Morgan gingerly steps out of the ring for a closer look at it. As he approaches closely, a massive pounding at the side is audible. He looks back at the referee, who is looking at the box nervously. At that moment, a fist breaks through the half-inch-thick wooden walls, padded in a thick black padded knuckle-glove, and the sidewall of the box nearest the ring shatters into splinters. Morgan starts back, almost falling over his own feet. Ducking under the edge of the box is a nearly seven-foot-tall mass of flesh that looks to weigh on the order of five hundred pounds. Muscles ripple along his arms as he moves, his head turning on his neckless shoulders, with a mouth and beard showing underneath the red-and-black mask he wears. His voice can be heard throughout the arena. "WHERE IS HE?! WHERE IS HE?!!" His stone-grey eyes fall on Morgan, now shed of the World Championship belt, and the bell sounds even before the monstrous individual identified as Grendel begins to bound after the titlist. Morgan's eyes widen as he backs into the ring, fists up for the match to come. The spandex coverall-clad monster steps over the top rope, and is met with fists flying from the World Champion, staggering him at the ropes before he can fully come into the ring. Morgan rocks the gigantic challenger with knotted fists, finally bringing Grendel to a wobbly state. He sends the challenger across the ring in an Irish whip, leaping after him as he comes back with a flying lariat that knocks the monster off his feet! Morgan is atop the mysterious challenger in an instant, grasping him by the mask and raining thunderous right hands down on his head. The referee begins a five-count, until Morgan finally stands and ceases his bombardment at four. Morgan turns to the referee, complaining about this angrily, while behind him, Grendel shakes his head and returns to his feet. Morgan turns back to the red-bearded monster, and a fist the size of a small volleyball impacts on the side of his head. Morgan rolls with the punch, which spins him halfway around, staggering him into the corner. Grendel follows him ruthlessly, clubbing his head from side to side with blows that are half forearm, half fist, and all pain. After five or six of these blows, Morgan's legs collapse beneath him, and he is on the mat. The official now turns on the monster, shouting at him over the use of closed fists, no matter the padded gloves covering the backs and knuckles of his hands. Grendel growls at the referee, who backs away several feet, and is dismissed in the monster's mind therewith. He turns back to the champion, bringing him back to his feet, clutching him into a brutal full nelson instantly. After punishing him for a moment in this hold, he lifts up and backwards, hurling the champion overhead in a Dragon Suplex that pummels the back of his head against the canvas! The referee falls to the mat to count. One! Two . . . . . . Morgan barely manages to get his right shoulder up, and frees himself of the hold with a shift of his weight sideways. Grendel rises and turns on the zebra angrily, holding up his three fingers from his middle to his pinky in the dwarfed man's face. The referee shouts back at him, holding up two and putting a hand on his own shoulder, making a visible gesture of putting it away from a surface. Grendel turns to the booing fans, shouting "HOW MANY MORE WAYS DO YOU WANT ME TO HURT HIM?!" in his fury. Grendel turns back to Morgan, sending him in an Irish whip across the ring, catching him in a military press on the bounce! Two, three times, Grendel bends his arms to lower Morgan to the top of his head and extends them back fully. Finally, having proven his power to press the 321-pound champion, casually turns him over and hurls him on his back, half way across the ring. Morgan lands hard and rolls onto his belly, holding onto his lower back. Grendel sees it immediately and lifts him up, putting him into an abdominal stretch. Morgan screams unashamedly in pain, shouting wordless defiance when the ref asks for a submission. He squirms and manages to free his leg from the hold, and hip tosses the huge monster over onto his back, freeing himself from the hold. Morgan falls to his knees at the ropes, the wheels visibly turning in his head. He turns to the challenger, knowing he must follow this up soon or he is dead meat. He grabs onto a leg, lifting it up off the mat and throwing a knee inside the right thigh, just above Grendel's knee. Grendel angrily pulls free of the hold on his ankle, coming to his feet, though with a slight hobble in his step. Morgan, seeing that there is perhaps some offense in hurting the knees on a man who probably weights more than 500 pounds, follows him, kicking away at the back of the right knee. Grendel whirls on him, hammering away with the ham fists once more, and Morgan this time raises his arms to protect his head. The padded gloves buffet off his wrists, and Morgan counters with a jab to the masked head. His arms begin to feel numb with the impacts, but gradually Morgan backs the larger opponent into the corner, opening up with a barrage of lefts and rights to the gut and face, turning sideways, punctuating it with a backhand chop to the chest that reverberates throughout the arena. The barrage of fists is renewed, followed by two more chops, and then Morgan whips Grendel out of the corner, following closely behind to impact with a thunderous clothesline only instants after the monster impacts in the opposite turnbuckles. Grendel slumps against the buckles, but Morgan allows him no respite. He draws him out of the corner, grasping his arm back in a hammerlock. He grabs the other arm in a similar hammerlock, looking out at the crowd with a wise expression on his face. Grendel's eyes widen as he is lifted off his feet by the double armbar in a Tiger suplex, the smaller man stepping out from beneath as he releases one arm to rely upon the other to slam the huge monster to the mat on his back. Morgan runs back to the ropes, and drops an elbow on the larger enemy. Grendel sucks air after the impact, as Morgan moves to the legs once again. Grendel vaguely sees him moving, and rolls over onto his belly, getting to his hands and knees quickly, rising to his feet when he reaches the ropes. The official backs Morgan away as Grendel catches onto the wrapped cables, allowing the challenger to stand. Morgan begins to close in, but Grendel ducks out between the top two ropes as he approaches, bringing the ref to step between them and back the champion away. Morgan nods grudgingly and gives the challenger room. The man may be big and strong, but somewhere in there resides the same sort of ring intellect that Doctor Death used to use to slow the pace when things didn't go his way, though most likely not in the same form. Grendel steps back fully into the ring, closing with Morgan quickly now. The huge fists pound away at Morgan's head, and Morgan again wraps his skull up with his arms to defend himself. Grendel takes one shot to the face before responding with a blow to the gut on the smaller man, causing him to double over in surprise, using one arm to block low now. Grendel takes advantage of it instantly, striking the uncovered side of the head, stunning Morgan long enough to lower his guard altogether, and the champion is again sprawled on the mat after a pounding by the larger challenger. Morgan tries to go for the ropes, but Grendel stops him with a huge hooking hand locked in the pink tights, and the champion is drawn back to the middle of the ring. A huge hand seizes Morgan by the throat, crushing away at his Adam's apple with terrible power, and the referee immediately begins a swift count. Grendel ignores it, wrapping his other, left, arm under Morgan's right shoulder to grasp his neck from the other side, and lifts the smaller man up, six feet off the ground, before hammering him to the mat in a spine- shattering choke slam. No sooner has Morgan impacted, than Grendel goes to the ropes, and as if to mock his opponent, extends a leg and drops it over the face of the prone titlist. The fans groan in horror at the sight of the gigantic individual landing with his full weight on the champion, who is left twitching like a fish out of water after the impact. Grendel rises, turning to the fans and pointing down at the fallen champ. "THERE'S YOUR CHAMPION!! THERE HE IS!!!" Once he has heard sufficient boos to enjoy himself, the monster turns back to the Titan and grasps him by the hair, drawing him back to his feet. Grendel grasps Morgan by his right wrist with his own left hand, drawing him forcibly closer to be struck by a huge short-arm clothesline. Grendel hesitates only a moment before moving over towards Morgan's legs. Grabbing his foe's right leg as Morgan did with him earlier, he lifts it up and drops the elbow over the inside of the thigh. Morgan rears up in agony, screaming in pain as his hip joint is given a stress test for the evening. Grendel does not release his hold on the ankle as he rises to his feet once more, dragging Morgan into the middle of the ring. He picks Morgan's other foot up, stepping through the middle and out to Morgan's right side, folding Morgan's right leg to place the ankle inside the fold of his left knee, setting his left outstretched leg under his own left arm, and grasps the right ankle to assist as he turns the smaller man over onto his belly. Once over, Grendel places his padded right hand firmly on Morgan's left knee, putting all his weight and might into this Scorpion Monster-lock. Morgan shrieks in pain as the referee hurtles to the canvas to ask the champion if he wishes to submit. The ref shakes his head at the timekeeper when asked if he got a quitting, and continues to ask Morgan what he says. Morgan shouts in the negative, looking around through the red haze of pain for a rope to grab onto. He cries out as Grendel wrenches backwards on his hold, and tries to set up on his hands better, so as to perhaps do a hand-crawl closer to a rope. Grendel feels the movement, and sets more firmly in an effort to prevent it. However, Morgan's body shifts beneath him, and Grendel is forced to step back to keep the hold on properly. Finally, Morgan manages to make it to the ropes, and the referee calls for the break. Grendel reluctantly releases the hold, cursing angrily in frustration. Morgan wilts forward to make a pathetic sight of a broken body as Grendel stands and clears at the referee's demand. At long last, movement is again seen from the champion as he rolls underneath the bottom rope to the floor. The official prevents Grendel from following him out, starting the count once the monster heel assures him that he will not go out after him. The moment the ref has turned his back, however, the huge man has slid out the side of the ring and begins making his way around to where Morgan lies on the floor. Morgan slowly gets to his feet out there, but is met by a running lariat from the huge arm of the larger man. Morgan flops over in midair before falling on his face on the floor mats. Grendel growls at a few fans who get too courageous in yelling at him, and turns back to bring Morgan back to his feet. He tucks Morgan's head between his knees, putting his arms around his waist, preparing to lift. Realizing that he's in for a piledriver at best and a power bomb at worst, Morgan's survival instincts kick into gear. Just as the fans are worried that his career is at stake, he sets his legs and lifts the huge man over his head, hefting him over to fall on his back on the floor. Morgan clutches at his back, and it is immediately clear that this was not real good for him, either. The ref prevents him from attacking further, and Morgan gladly goes back into the ring to break the momentum. A thought enters his head, however, as Grendel makes his way back to his feet on the floor. The referee starts to duck under the ropes to return to the ring, and is instantly forced to leap out of the way as Morgan comes sprinting over to the ropes, leaping over in a flying cross-body press onto Grendel on the floor! Grendel falls underneath him, landing with his back against the fence outside! Morgan extricates himself from the wreckage, and grabs onto Grendel's mask, drawing him to his feet. He drags him by his mask over to a table, and hefts him up in a scoop-slam, dropping him hard on the table! Morgan points up at the turnbuckles, and immediately the imagination of the fans goes wild! Morgan runs back into the ring, the idea too precious to let go by procrastination. He mounts the top turnbuckles, eliciting a loud cheer from the fans. The referee reads his intentions, and shouts at him to get down from there. His cries fall on ears too badly deafened from the encouragement of the fans. Off the top rope Morgan flies, landing with his leg across Grendel's collarbone! The table shatters underneath the impact, and both Grendel and Morgan are left sprawling on the ground, trying to sort themselves out. Morgan gets up first easily, drawing the half-destroyed heel up by his mask and pushing him back underneath the bottom rope. The referee angrily shouts at Morgan for his actions, but again he is ignored, as Morgan climbs again to the top rope. Grendel rolls back into the ring, getting back to his feet groggily, and is met with a drop kick off the top from the champion! Grendel staggers and falls to the mat hard, the impact sending echoes throughout the Baltimore Arena! Morgan is atop him quickly for the cover! The ref is left trying to scramble back into the ring to count. One! Two . . . . . . Grendel's left shoulder surges upward just as the referee's hand hits the canvas for the third time. Morgan looks at the ref questioningly, until two fingers are raised in the air, and the official shouts at the timekeeper to clear things up. Morgan shoves the ref, almost knocking him off his feet, vehemently complaining about his lousy positioning. Had he been in the ring doing his job, the match would already be over. The official retorts with a threat to disqualify the champion if he doesn't watch where he's putting his hands, and a reminder that he wouldn't have been outside the ring if Morgan hadn't pulled yet another stunt that could have easily earned him a DQ. Thus, the official finishes his tirade, Morgan is lucky that the ref was even bothering to count at all. Morgan shakes his head with a wordless shout of fury, and turns back to his fallen challenger. He draws Grendel to his feet, taking him up into a front facelock, looping Grendel's left arm over Morgan's own head. He sets with his legs, lifting by a hand under the belly, hefting him up vertically. For ten, fifteen seconds the champion holds his mammoth challenger up in the air, finally hurling him down to the mat on his back. Morgan claps his hands three times as he looks out at the fans excitedly, then sprints over to the ropes parallel to Grendel's fallen body. He leaps up, extending the leg for the dreaded leg drop known as the Harbinger . . . . . . and comes crashing down on empty canvas, his challenger having rolled from his path only brief instants before it would have been too late. Morgan clutches at his back, his earlier injuries to it aggravated by the fateful strike. Grendel rolls onto his hands and knees, slowly getting to his feet. He moves over to Morgan, and lifts the challenger up to his feet once more. Grendel sends Morgan to the ropes, catching him as if to impact with a side suplex, instead twisting his body around 540 degrees before hammering him down to the mat in a crushing tilt-a-whirl. Grendel stands to his feet more energetically this time, shouting in incoherent fury and defiance to the fans. The marks are booing him loudly; the hardcores, on the other hand, are quite impressed thus far and don't mind seeing him at all. Grendel lifts Morgan to his feet, and hefts him to arm's length overhead in a monstrous Gorilla press, hurling him to his back on the canvas one more time. He runs to the ropes as soon as he releases, coming off and landing a huge knee down across Morgan's forehead! Grendel stands again, this time his screaming forming real words: "MONSTER BOMB!!!" The shout brings a stir from the fans, as he brings Morgan to his feet, tucking his head between his legs, lifting by his waist until his hips rest on his right shoulder with his back stretched out horizontally, and does not release his foe at any point on the long trip seven feet down, until his entire spine strikes the mat simultaneously with a tremendous sound that echoes through the Baltimore Arena three times before it has faded away completely. Grendel draws a thumb across his throat as he nonchalantly lowers his body for the obligatory pinfall. One! Two . . . Morgan kicks out of the cover, coming to his knees a short distance away with unimaginable fury on his face. He shakes his head, roaring with all his voice up at the ring lights, enraged that his enemy could think to pin him so easily. Grendel hammers Morgan with a padded right hand. Morgan opens his eyes as if truly seeing his opponent for the first time, and shakes his head in defiance. Grendel smacks him a second time as he rises to one knee. Morgan again stares at him right in the stony grey eyes and shakes his head in defiance. Grendel strikes a third time as Morgan gets to his feet, and the champion closes his eyes again, closing his hands and screaming as he walks around the ring shaking his knotted fists in the air. Grendel watches in stunned disbelief as he walks around the ring in a full circle, then stops him with a fourth blow. Morgan raises the finger and shakes it in Grendel's face, warning this foolish monster of the unutterable fate that will befall him if he is stupid enough to continue this idiocy. Grendel is unmoved, striking again. Morgan raises an arm and catches his hand, opening the fingers and grasping it in a knuckle lock, twisting it around to his side painfully, backing the larger man to the corner with an agonized expression on his face. He opens up with a barrage of lefts and rights, punctuating them with a single chop before sending him across the ring to the opposite turnbuckles with a mighty shove. What he does next startles even those who thought they knew him. Morgan hops out of the corner, flipping over into a cartwheel, which in turn shapes into a handspring, hurtling up through the air to propel his great 321-pound frame into a crushing elbow smash onto the chest of his larger foe in the opposite corner. The fans stand in awe of the improvement this man has put into his repertoire in the recess of the FWF, as Grendel staggers out of the corner and falls to one knee in pain. Morgan draws him back up, sending him across the ring, catching him off the ropes in a scoop, tucking the head under with his left hand as he turns him over in a bonecrushing power slam. He instantly rises to his feet, backing to the ropes, and this time successfully leaps up five feet into the air, and lands the Harbinger leg drop down across the chest of his prone opponent. Morgan rises to his feet, looking out at the fans, who have begun chanting "POWER BOMB!" in answer to the familiar routine. For that is what the leg drop is the Harbinger of: the Power Bomb of Doom that sent the dominant two-time former FWF World Champion to the hospital with a back injury. Morgan, with some difficulty, brings the monstrous Grendel back to his feet, and this time it is the larger man who has his head between Morgan's knees with the Titan's arms around his waist. For a brief moment the fans wonder if Morgan can accomplish this feat, but then his legs coil and a lift with his entire body puts the man facing upward resting on his own right shoulder. A second more, and Morgan sets his hands and holds the force on as Grendel's spine impacts to the mat with a most uncomfortable THUD! Morgan grabs at his back reflexively as a spasm of pain goes through. The effort of lifting Grendel was too much for a back that has taken two press slams, a submission hold and a power bomb of its own. Morgan wilts to the canvas, and together the champion and challenger lie, the audience strangely muted as though this had been wholly unexpected. The referee seems somewhat surprised for a long while as well, but then he remembers his duty in such circumstances, and he begins to count. At the count of six, it is Grendel who sits up groggily, and the referee hesitates before going to seven. The monster rolls over onto his knees, and the count is halted at eight. Only at this point does enough unpainful feeling make its way into Morgan's body that he is able to move himself. But by this time, it is too late, and Grendel is upon him. The huge man grasps him by the blonde hair, looking into the eyes the color of sapphires a brief moment before the gigantic fist pounds away at his head. Morgan's body rocks to his heels. Grendel lets him double over, taking the opportunity to grasp him around the waist, and a great gut-wrench heave puts him once again facing the ring lights on the larger man's shoulder. Grendel holds him there for a moment, and then makes a short hop and drops to his knees with the small of Morgan's back set on his shoulder. Morgan bends back almost double, and falls off of Grendel's shoulder, his body in line with the corners. Grendel steps over him as he makes his way over to the turnbuckles, climbing to the top while facing outside the ring. He pauses a moment as he balances at the top, shouting to the wide-eyed fans outside, "LOAD THE FILM! IT'S MONSTERSAULT TIME!!!!!" At that, he leaps out and back, his body flipping backward 270 degrees in midair, before his full body weight impacts across Morgan's chest and torso. The sound of the fans as he hurtles through the air is one of crescendoing anticipation, and then the "Ohhhhhhh" as he strikes. Grendel grasps one of Morgan's legs and, with the same arm, places his hand on Morgan's adjacent shoulder, folding the man in half as the referee counts. One! Two . . . . . . . and three!!! Grendel rises, seeing a camera man slack-jawed on the ring apron, and moves over as the overture to the Phantom begins once more. He points into the camera and screams directly into it, "TITANMANIA IS OVER!! THE REIGN OF TERROR BEGINS NOW, TONIGHT!!!" The fans seem almost shocked until Alfred Grinchley recovers from his own shock at the amazing Monstersault and makes it official: "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AND NEEEEEEWWWWWWWW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: GRENDEL!!!!" Grendel takes the belt contemptuously from the official and slings it over his shoulder, pointing sardonically down at the fallen Titan Morgan, who has not moved a muscle since the Monstersault impacted. The flashbulbs go off throughout the arena as the fans strive to get a photograph of the first man to ever cleanly pin the "Invincible" Titan Morgan. Grendel, the belt still slung over his shoulder, steps between the ropes and passes the forklift on his way back to the dressing room, laughing as he sees the stretcher pass him on its way to assist his opponent, the former champion, back down the aisle. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Well, there it is. For those who remember the old FWF, I hope the reunion was a pleasant one. For those who don't, I hope that it was at least halfway entertaining. (And I just defy WCW to actually book a match like that between that HH guy and Vader, while I'm at it . . . :) If you have any comments on this, do feel free to reply (the "r" key, preferably; don't need to use up any more bandwidth than I'm already putting away right here :).
