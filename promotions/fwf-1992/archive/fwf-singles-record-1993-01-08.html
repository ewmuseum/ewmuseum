Path: sparky!uunet!stanford.edu!rutgers!flop.ENGR.ORST.EDU!xanth.CS.ORST.EDU![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@xanth.CS.ORST.EDU (Stilt Man) Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Singles record Message-ID: <1ikjd1INNa99@flop.ENGR.ORST.EDU> Date: 8 Jan 93 19:07:13 GMT Organization: CS Dept, Oregon State University Lines: 358 NNTP-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu And, after too long since I recapped it, now I repost the current edition of the burgeoning FWF singles ranks record file. Note that match 4 on this file and match 1 on [name withheld]' record are, in fact, the same match, included in mine because I wrote the thing and included on Jason's because it was the first tag team match in the FWF. Current titleholders and contenders: PWA/FWF World Heavyweight Champion: DOCTOR DEATH FWF United States Heavyweight Champion: "INVINCIBLE" TITAN MORGAN Contenders: 1. Annihilus 2. "Hurricane" Andrew Simms 3. John "Flawless" Lawless 4. "Suicidal" Paul Stuart 5. "Scorpion" Stan Golding 6. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters 7. Rick Bondgrove 8. "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer 9. Big Truck Vader 10. Kane the Undertaker I-C division FWF Intercontinental Heavyweight Champion: "IRON WARRIOR" BLUE STEELE Contenders (roughly guessed by Stilt Man): 1. Jerry "the Stratofortress" Henderson 2. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters 3. "Vision" Stu Watson 4. Emerson Wildside 5. Rick Tauer And now, for the long and exhausting history file: Note--the first two matches were the double-elimination tournament for the World (single elim) title and the U.S. (consolation) title. Since there was neither a winner nor a loser of the second match, the winner and loser of the first were both granted a bye through the finals. Persons who have won a given title multiple times (Doctor Death's 2 World Titles, Blue Steele's 2 I-C titles, Damien Flayer and John Lawless' 2 U.S. Titles) have the occurrence number of a given title win listed with their victories.
