FWF: Lightning vs. Doc

18 November 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Message-ID: <1eehbtINNd2k@flop.ENGR.ORST.EDU> Date: 18 Nov 92 22:50:37 GMT Article-I.D.: flop.1eehbtINNd2k Distribution: world Organization: CS Dept, Oregon State University Lines: 567 NNTP-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu And now, on the heels of his narrow victory over Titan Morgan, Aaron "Lightning" Murphy steps into the ring with the diabolical Doctor Death, in search of the latter's World Heavyweight Championship. Death has held the title for over four active months, defending the title now for an unprecedented fifth time, breaking his own record of four title defenses in his first World Title reign. Now think, how many World Champions defend their titles four times on a PPV in any other federation? At any rate, looming up in the near future (if God is willing) is a very big event: a summit between the FWF and the PWA, an email federation operated by one [name withheld]. Whoever wins this match will walk into that summit with the World Heavyweight Championship to represent the FWF against his PWA counterpart. Comments from both men on the subject: Lightning: "The news is out! The PWA and the FWF are talking about getting some straps and their holders against each other, and far be it from Thunder and I to not see to it that at least one Storm member is wrestling as a champion in that great event! We couldn't get the chance to do it with the tag team belts, so Doc, you have the misfortune of being the sacrificial lamb, ha, ha!! And when I walk out of Joe Lewis Arena, your second World Championship stripped and placed around my waist, it's to end the old ways, and set forth into a new dynasty of greatness!" Death: "So . . . the FWF has called upon its champions to represent it at a great clash of the masters with the PWA. It will not be the first time I have made my presence known to other federations, and it will not the last. In April, I matched wits with then-, at least, EMWA World Champion John Girder, and yet the FWF World Championship still is clasped firmly in my hands. Soon, a third World Championship will be competed for by myself, and I will do the FWF proud a second time. However, there is an insect buzzing in my ear, keeping me from concentrating on the task at hand. All that is left to me . . . is to squash that insect that now crawls up my leg, trying to gather attention to its bothersome noise. My friends, the bug bomb, please . . ." And so, the match occurs . . . ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The synthesized tones of "Storm" from the William Tell Overture bounce off the rafters of the Joe Lewis Arena, and two men walk to ringside in black capes and long-legged tights. The smaller one, in front, wears lightning-shaped fluorescent pink face paint to match the similar streak on his legs. Alfred Grinchley swallows his fear of the two and begins his ministrations for the evening: "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!! IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. COMING DOWN THE AISLE, WITH HIS PARTNER, JERRY 'THUNDER' McGAGHNEY, IS THE CHALLENGER. HE HAILS FROM KANSAS CITY, MISSOURI, AND HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY ONE POUNDS. THIS . . . IS . . . AARON 'LIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGHTNING' MUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRPPHHHHHHYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!!" The two men circle to opposite sides of the ring and enter on either flank of the Grinch. The Grinch begins to seriously question why he didn't listen to his mother and become an auto salesman; it involved less risk. Instead, he gets to stand in between all these maniacs who would just love to tear each others' heads off. The two men tonight have quite a reputation behind them, and qualify as the top heels, undoubtedly, of the tag team division of the FWF. In their debut match they left a man with a broken back, yet continued to beat upon them. Any object at ringside can and will be commandeered as their personal weaponry. Most recently, they left a promising contender in the singles ranks with a broken arm. However, that contender turned around and cost them the FWF World Tag-Team Championship. Soon, it is said, the FWF will co-sponsor a "Clash of the Masters," as their adversary for the evening put it, with the PWA. Unlike Dreamcard V, in which only the World Champions of two federations battled, the World, U.S., and Tag Team Champions of the two federations will duke it out. Thunder and Lightning, collectively known as Storm, are determined to represent one of the FWF titles in this event. But their recent defeat by Devastation countermanded that hope . . . unless Lightning can gain a pinfall or submission tonight. The macabre tones of "Toccata and Fugue in D Minor" ring out in the arena, and a black-cloaked, hooded figure slowly walks out from the dressing room, his ebon folds covering him from head to toe. A metal hockey-like mask covers his face, and a flash of gold from beneath his cloak is visible when it parts briefly. The Grinch stands silent, gazing out at the grim spectre, before realizing he still has a job to do, and then he can flee for his life as he pleases. "AND HIS OPPONENT, MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING AREA . . . HAILING FROM PARTS UNKNOWN, HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED, EIGHTY SEVEN POUNDS, AND HE IS THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION . . . . DOOOOOOOOCTOOOOOOR DEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAATH!!!!!!" Death looks up at the two in the ring, and abruptly and uncharacteristically runs to the ring. The two Stormers set themselves as they see Death sprinting towards them, cloak billowing behind him, but they see that his eyes are on the Grinch, trying to leave the ring. The Grinch drops the microphone and panics, fleeing from the ring at the sight of the approaching individual. Turning his black eyes with their red flecks at Thunder and Lightning, Death climbs to the ring apron, turning the horrible eyes on the official. The ref gulps, and walks over to part the ropes for the man. Soon, no fewer than three men in black stand to the ring. Death picks up the discarded microphone and begins to speak. "YOU TWO FOOLS SERIOUSLY BELIEVE THAT I WILL PERMIT A MORE-THAN-POSSIBLE DOUBLE TEAM WITH MY PRESTIGE ON THE LINE? TERMINATE THE MUSIC!!" In a heartbeat, the organ music cuts off. "LET IT BE KNOWN THAT I HAVE SOMEONE TO ACCOMPANY ME, IN WITNESS OF THUNDER'S COMPLIANCE IN THE VICTORY OVER THE LESSER FICTIONAL WRESTLING FEDERATION CHAMPION, TO PREVENT SUCH AN EVENT FROM BEFALLING ME." The unutterably wicked "Overture to the Phantom of the Opera" blasts over the PA system of Joe Lewis Arena. Several of the fans stand agape, overwhelmed by the sheer nefariety of the sound, as Death continues his monologue. "INTRODUCING MY CORNERMAN FOR THIS EVENING, FROM PARTS UNKNOWN, THEY CALL HIM . . . BIIIIIIIIIG TRUCK VAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAADEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRR!!!!" And sure enough, a huge individual in a ninety-pound steel helmet/mask begins to tromp down to the ring area. The two Stormers shout at Death angrily, but a single look from the blood-spattered coals behind the mask gives even them pause. They turn to the referee, protesting vehemently. The official backs off, shrugging. If Thunder stays, Vader stays, as far as he's concerned. The huge figure removes the steel to reveal a leather mask covering most of his face, accenting the black and red coverall tights he wears. Death unclasps his own cloak, removing his metal mask, and handing the World Championship belt to the referee. The two leather-masked men make for an extremely imposing duo, between the only two-time FWF World Champion and the gigantic man who definitely could present a threat to that honor himself if he so wished. Lightning and Thunder refuse to accept the setback, shouting with the referee to remove Vader from ringside. Death points simply at Thunder's presence as well, and the referee is at a loss. Big Truck Vader shouts encouragement to Doctor Death, and Death calmly leans against the ropes while Thunder and Lightning blow off steam. The mind play has worked quite well against the two men, and Death knows it. Vader seems quite pleased that his presence has created such a stir. Finally, the official points out that Vader is, at least, outside the ring and not delaying the beginning of the match. Thunder rolls his eyes skyward, seeing that they're getting nowhere in this, and takes Lightning's cape with his own as he exits the ring. Vader glares at him contemptuously, much as Death is regarding Lightning at this point. "I could stuff both you guys up each nostril!!!" he shouts. Despite the distraction of Vader and Thunder, the bell sounds and the match is officially under way. Death smiles beneath his mask as Lightning takes reassurance from Thunder that Vader will not be a factor. Lightning's got cold enough feet just being in the ring with the Doc himself. Vader puts a whole new twist on the knife of fear in his heart. But, after much goading from the Doctor, Lightning at last ostensibly moves his mind to the match. The two men opt for a collar-and-elbow tie. No sooner than this, Lightning is sent brusquely back into the corner. Death slaps his powerful left shoulder, flexing his leg muscles for warm-up and smiles derisively on his smaller foe. Lightning gives Death the once-over. He's not completely defined, but there is little flab on him, either. His legs are very good-sized, suggesting that any use of his height advantage will be a serious problem for Lightning. He also knows, from watching tapes, that Death's mind is by far his deadliest asset. He has perpetrated some of the sneakiest stunts ever seen in any federation upon his opponents. However, he has just now used his power only. Lightning wonders why. He knows it's not because of lack of wrestling skill; Death is a consummate master of nearly every wrestling move ever used. Lightning, for his part, has speed, likely, and agility on his larger foe. He also has a good aerial attack that, if used right, could even things out. If used wrong . . . Lightning comes out of the corner, and Death lunges at him. Lightning drops back, but Death suddenly ducks and grabs him behind the knees, putting a shoulder into his belly to send him to his back. He drags him out into the center of the ring, begins to spin around, twirling Lightning's entire body about by his shins, until finally the centrifugal force has Lightning totally off the canvas. Death releases his grip, and Lightning hurtles out through the middle rope, landing badly on the canvas outside. Lightning stands, and falls again. The official sees Vader nearby, and warns him to back away. Vader stays where he is, smiling at the giant swing that sent Lightning out here. But Death doesn't like the presumption of the referee in assuming that Vader is automatically going to interfere. He turns the ref around and argues with him on the point . . . and proves his point to everyone but the ref himself when Vader runs in and levels Lightning with a beefy clothesline, sending him flying backwards to the thin mats outside. Thunder runs around the ring, edgy about brawling with the gigantic Vader. However, he does draw the official's attention, and Vader backs off to just look innocent. Thunder picks his stricken partner up and sends him back into the ring, hoping that he'll do better against the Doctor. With a glare over at Vader, he follows the official's instructions to stay in his own territory. Death brings Lightning to his feet, snatching him in a front facelock, putting a close knee lift, sending him into the corner. Lightning grabs onto the ropes to get his bearings, sees Death's shape looming over him, and lashes out wildly with a left. The blow lands on Death's right shoulder, and he responds with an expertly executed reverse knife edge that plants Lightning into the turnbuckles all the harder. Lightning squints at the strike. Death looks down, and deals a knee expertly above Lightning's right knee, striking the reflex that bends the knee against the grain. The limb buckles, and the smaller man crumbles to the canvas. Death kicks away at Lightning, stomping murderously until the man finally rolls outside the ring to the floor in instinctive defense. He backs away from the ropes at the official's admonition, arguing with him some more. This time, Thunder gets between Lightning and Vader to ensure that no more interference takes place. The larger Stormer whispers something in the smaller's ear, bringing a nod from Lightning. He gets up to his feet and stands on the ring apron, yelling at the official to move Death away from the edge to let him in. Lightning feints another collar-and-elbow, burying a knee to Death's midsection instead. As Death doubles over, he hammers him with a forearm to the back. He follows with an uppercut, and immediately takes the opportunity to scoop him up into a backbreaker! Lightning waves his index finger in the air with a war- whoop that brings a sympathetic response from the fans--one that surprises him. Lightning waits a moment, sizing up Death for his next move. He clenches his teeth in anger, not happy with the way it's been going at all. He snaps the larger man over the shoulder, backing into the corner as he sits up. The moment he is upright on his seating, Lightning rushes up, catching his head in his hands as he flips overhead, stretching his spine out in a manner most uncomfortable. Lightning shouts jubilantly, listening to the echo from the fans as he watches Death rise to his knees. Death has played right into Lightning's hands . . . . . . not quite. As Lightning reaches Death, the larger man abruptly rises to his feet! Lightning leaps up reflexively, grabbing with legs for anything he can latch onto, and gets hold of Death's head in a scissors grip! He gambols for balance, before twisting Death around in a takeover, flopping him out into the corner once more! Death rises quickly, only to be dealt a rising elbow from the smaller man! Lightning deals out the hurt, in alternating jabs with speed and tenacity, until Death is staggering from the corner. Lightning climbs the second rope and leaps out, catching Death's head in a flying bulldog! Death, instinctively staying on his belly groans in pain from all over his spine. Lightning rolls him over for the cover . . . . . . a jab to the eyes with a pair of left fingers ends that attempt. The larger man follows with a hook to the jaw that sends Lightning to the ropes, holding on for balance while Death agonizingly gets back to his feet. He comes over, dishing a chop to the chest that almost sends Lightning over the top. Thunder cries out foul, mounting the ring apron in anger. The ref turns, and together Vader with Lightning's foot and Death his hair move Lightning to the corner. A snap over the shoulder puts him back into the middle of the ring, and Death comes out with an elbow to the chest to keep him down. Death stands and shrieks in fury, freezing the marrow of closeby onlookers who are unfortunate enough to hear it loudly. Vader echoes the shout, and some hardcores as well, but they are drowned out by boos from the fans. Death waits for Lightning to get to his feet, and goes to the ropes as he makes it. Thunder reaches in and trips him up, sending him stumbling over to Lightning with his head down. The smaller man curls him over into a running neckbreaker before he can recover, and slowly sits up after delivering the shot. Death gets to his feet not long after Lightning, only to be floored in a clothesline! Lightning hauls him up by the mask, and leaps up, uncoiling his legs directly into Death's chest, sending the larger man skidding to the canvas. Lightning sees Death slump to the middle of the bottom rope. The ref argues with him about pulling Death up by his mask, and Lightning is happy to shout right back. Thunder runs over and snags the Grinch's chair, folds it up, and plants it upside Doctor Death's forehead before he can evade the shot! Death's flecked eyes roll back in his forehead, and Lightning turns to him now, getting him up off the ropes by the arm this time. Death moans softly, while Lightning hefts him up from behind into a back suplex, WHAMP! The smaller man grins at how he is doing, while Vader glares at Thunder from the opposite side of the ring. Lightning gets Death up by his mask and ebon kilt, sending him back through the middle ropes to Thunder's mercy. The official shouts at him yet again, griping about meaninglessly throwing the man out of the ring. Lightning raises his hands in mock innocence, while watching Thunder pummelling the Doctor at ringside. Vader shouts furiously, finally taking matters into his own hands. He tromps right over to the opposite side and engages Thunder in a fistfight. The official, at this point, can no longer ignore the two. Seeing that their presence is clearly disruptive, he waves more referees from the dressing room to clear the two combatants from the ringside area. The fight slowly moves down the aisle, as Death somehow manages to stay away from the brawling, as the officials arrive. Soon, the match ref dismisses them in his mind, and orders Lightning not to interfere while Death reenters the ring. Death secretly smiles, for the departure of the two makes matters much simpler. There are only half as many variables now, and the match is still even, if you ignore the shellacking Thunder just dealt him. At this point, a red spot mars the canvas beneath his mask, and it occurs to Lightning that the leather is nearly saturated with blood. He smiles, seeing gold coming around his waist once again. As Death reaches the ring apron, Lightning loops one of his arms overhead and hefts him up by the kilt, letting his sore back slam against the canvas inside the ring with a resounding THUND. He points up in the air, and hops up to the top rope as soon as he is clear. Waving both index fingers in the air in a wind-up signal, he points down at Death contemptuously, launching off the top with a flying elbow . . . . . . which impacts squarely on Death's sternum. Lightning's eyes practically turn gold as he hooks a leg for the cover. One! Two . . . . . . not now. Death kicks out of the cover with little trouble. Lightning stands, stunned, and clasps Death in a headlock. If impact doesn't work, aim for a wear-down. Even Doc can't last forever if he lays on the lungs for a while, making it hard to breath. Death slowly tries to get to his feet, only to be hurled over the hip to the canvas beneath the smaller man. He quickly bounces back up and seizes the smaller man in a leg scissors, freeing himself from the hold. Lightning easily escapes from the move, grabbing Death's shins in his arms. He twists away with the shins under his arms, stepping over Death and completing the Boston crab! Doctor Death screams in the agony the move is costing him, for his back has not yet recovered from earlier moves. His entire spine is crying out at his mind, yet he bites his teeth and shuts the pain from his skull. He looks out, the flecks in his eyes dancing wildly as he fixates on the bottom rope only six feet away. He props himself up on his hands, making headway in a crawl for the thing that is central in his brain. Four feet. Lightning is forced to step back with him, amazed at his determination. Three feet. The hand is firmly on the rope. The official calls for the break, which Lightning keeps until four. Death immediately rolls out of the ring, deciding that the floor will offer him better thinking for now. The offical keeps Lightning in the ring, leaving Death alone and, for the moment, happy to be so. He glares up at Lightning in the ring, flecked ebon meeting emerald a moment. The fear is gone from Lightning's eyes, the match is firmly in his favor now. Death smiles grimly. That can be used against him. The Doctor climbs the steps, entering the ring once more. Lightning moves in quickly, and Death backs away, his hands up, to the ropes. The referee tells Lightning to back off, but Lightning refuses to listen. Should have listened to him, Aaron. That foot between your legs might have let some children live. Lightning goes rigid, and Death runs from the corner, leveling him in a roundhouse Russian sickle. Death catches his breath for a moment, then sucks in the air and lets it all out in a blood-curdling cry of wrath. He turns to Lightning, drawing him up, looping his right arm overhead with the same movement as Death puts his own left arm around his waist. He squats, grabs the right thigh, and lifts Lightning horizontal into the air. He is flawless in his movements as he steadies him on his left shoulder, steps from beneath, and lets him fall, his spine impacting perfectly on all points at once. Death doesn't bother to congratulate himself, though. He stands and backs to the ropes, the left-handed suplex leaving him able to do so without stepping over his opponent. The knee falls on Lightning's head, and Death rolls out into a somersault in one graceful sideways move back to his feet. Lightning clenches every muscle in his body, and goes limp on his back. 287 pounds of knee hurts, it won't matter if your head is made out of adamant. Death brings Lightning back to his feet again with a short, staccato laugh, putting him in a front facelock. Slowly, Death curls Lightning over until he faces upward, dropping a neck- breaker from shoulder height to the canvas! He pauses to breath, then hooks up a leg and a grapevine behind the head to cover the smaller man. One! Two . . . . . . nope. Lightning barely manages to get the left shoulder off the canvas. But Death still has him right where he wants him. He lifts the man's right leg up by the ankle, stepping over it with his own left leg, setting his shin just behind the ankle. His right shin over the knee, and he hops up in the air, landing on both knees with the imprisoned leg between! The knee curls painfully against the function any creator could have designed for it, eliciting a shriek of pain from Lightning! Death shows no mercy. He brings Lightning up to his feet, looping his left arm around the smaller man's waist again, lifting him up by the same right shin. He hefts him airborne a moment, before sending him crashing down to a set knee, bringing a shiver of torment from Lightning! Before the smaller man even takes his next breath, that same shin is used to support his entire weight in midair, prolonging the torture before Death takes the foundation out and drops him onto his back! Death keeps his hold on the ankle, though not using that to slow his foe down, and stands over Lightning with it in his hand. He bends back, and shouts the call to evoke the end. He spins around the inside of the leg, bending it at the knee, picking up the left ankle and setting the right about the left knee, stepping forward with his own left leg to that his left foot is beneath Lightning's left thigh as a brace, steps forward now with his right foot to take off his balance, and allows himself to fall to the canvas, lifting his right foot up as soon as he no longer needs it for support, setting his own right knee over Lightning's right ankle. The smaller man's eyes widen in torturous agony, as the figure four leg lock is turned over from side to side by his captor, contorting every ligament in that knee as best Death can! Death sets a moment, twisting his left foot inward in its brace position, hyperextending Lightning's left knee, and he rises up into a bridge with the hold, to put all the leverage he can to press the right ankle down, popping the left knee out of joint and twisting it backwards. Lightning wilts to the canvas, his eyes rolling back in his head. Blackness creeps in upon his vision, and he half-prays for blissful oblivion to free him from this pain. He is dimly aware of a count being laid upon his shoulders, and he knows it means he has to lift a shoulder off the mat. He weakly gets his right up, but even he doesn't know how long he can keep it up. He tries to sit up, biting his tongue against the crescendo of pain that doing so means, until he can get a hand on Death's right leg bracing his ankle down in the hold. He grabs with both hands, trying to shove the leg off his foot, to free himself. The leg goes nowhere, and Lightning remembers Death's leg strength about now. His mastery of the hold compliments his lower body power well, Lightning thinks grimly. Now how to get out? He looks around for some ropes, wondering if perhaps they can save him. The ropes are closer to Death than to Lightning, and the Doctor realizes it as soon as Lightning himself does. He slides back with Lightning in the hold, moving back to the ropes by sliding on his left foot and tail only, not letting his right foot move an inch in proportion to Lightning's legs. If that slips, the hold weakens. Death reaches the ropes, and Lightning cringes in despair and pain as Death grabs them, letting all his weight all against Lightning's legs. He crumbles to the canvas again, and the referee counts the shoulders. One! Two . . . No! Lightning refuses to give in! The official asks for a submission, only to get wordless defiance. Lightning angrily tries to get his right foot on the canvas, get some stance to turn this thing over. He slowly lifts his right knee into the air, but Death simply grabs onto the ropes and flattens it back out. Lightning, driven by anger and pain, takes it out on the official, shouting at him to pay attention to Death. The official turns, sees the middle rope shaking, albeit Death's hands on the mat, and asks if he has used them. Lightning's eyes roll skyward. Nice going, ref. "Sure, I used the ropes. Go ahead and disqualify me!" he thinks sarcastically. Death, of course, puts on the baby face, and the ref turns back to Lightning to ask for a submission. Death rises into a rope grabbing again, and Lightning manages the words "Look at him!" before wilting to the mat. The official turns, seeing Death in the process of letting go and moving his hands back to the canvas. He glares at the ultra-heel, and lays on a DQ count to break the hold. Death grumbles, but complies at four. Lightning weakly grasps his knee, and asks the referee kindly to watch a little more carefully, his voice dripping with sarcasm. Death cuts off the conversation when he aims to reapply the hold. Lightning reaches up, curling him over into an inside cradle!! One! Two!! No!! Death surges from the hold, growling! He kicks viciously at Lightning's knee, as he crawls to the nearby ropes and uses them to lever himself back to his feet. He turns to Death after one shot too many (or many shots . . .) and deals a blatant chop right to the larger man's larnyx. Death staggers back, falling to the mat, clutching at his throat. Lightning limps over to him, trying to get some feeling into his legs that is not painful at this moment. He risks a glance out at the fans, seeing that most of them are actually at the edge of their seats watching this. The Grinch abruptly makes an announce- ment: "THIRTY MINUTES HAVE GONE BY IN THE MATCH. THIRTY MINUTES REMAINING." He's lasted thirty minutes? That's all? It seems a h@!! of a lot longer. He brings Death up to a stoop, hooking him in a double armbar from above his head, hooking him up into a butterfly suplex, sending him landing on his still-aching lower spine. There's no stops left to be pulled out. Lightning doesn't know if he really wants to let thirty more minutes go by with this hurt leg of his. He aims a shot at Death again, rocking him into the corner. He quickly rushes over and sets Death on the top turnbuckle, dealing another shot while he's at it. He climbs to the second rope, grips Death in a waistlock, and hefts him out of the corner, sending him sprawling on his back in the center of the ring! Lightning waves a finger in the air with a whooping war-shriek, and gestures to his waist before covering the Doctor! One! Two . . . Death's shoulder jerks slightly upward just as the official's hand hits the canvas for the third time! Lightning rises, both fingers in the air, crying out in jubilation, thinking that he's won the World Championship. The official dispells the shouting with two upraised fingers. Lightning rounds on him, following every step back while slowly raising his first three fingers. The ref shakes his head angrily, clapping his hands twice and patting himself on the shoulder, shifting it forward, pointing at Death and ordering Lightning to finish his man if he's able, but affirming that he hasn't done it yet. Death manages to get to his feet before Lightning is done beefing with the ref, and slowly staggers up to him. When Lightning turns around, he finds himself curled into a small package! One! Two . . . Lightning manages to roll over on top of Doctor Death, turning the move around! One! Two . . . Death gets out of the hold, getting to his feet quickly, ignoring the pain in his back. Lightning is a bit slower, and tries to punch Death as he approaches! Death sidesteps the move, stepping between Lightning's legs with his left leg while moving around to curl his left arm under Lightning's right shoulder! He hops up, putting his weight backward in the hold, rocking Lightning's shoulders to the mat! One! Two . . . Still not working! Lightning teeters sideways, managing to wiggle his way out. The official isn't sure what's going on, as Death gets to his feet just as quickly. Lightning turns, and rolls Death into yet another inside cradle! One! Two . . . No! Death rolls on top of Lightning now! One! Two . . . And three!! Lightning surges out of the hold, yelling at the referee about a fast count. The official responds in the negative, furious at the heavy-handed treatment he's gotten throughout the match. He makes the announcement to the timekeeper as Death exits the ring, and the Grinch makes it official. "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AND STILL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION . . . DOCTOR DEATH!!" Death slings his belt over his shoulder, slowly walking back to the dressing room. Lightning looks like he wants to go after him, but his leg isn't in good enough shape to run after him and catch him. However, another day may well find Aaron "Lightning" Murphy challenging for gold . . . -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- And so it is. Doctor Death walks into the Clash of the Masters with the FWF World Championship, ready to put pride on the line against his PWA counterpart.
