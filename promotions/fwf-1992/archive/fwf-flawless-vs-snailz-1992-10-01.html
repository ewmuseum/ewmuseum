FWF: Flawless vs. Snailz

1 October 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Path: sparky!uunet!stanford.edu!rutgers!orstcs![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@storm.CS.ORST.EDU (Stilt Man) Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Flawless vs. Snailz Summary: John "Flawless" Lawless defends his U.S. Title against Snailz. Message-ID: <1992Oct01.082439.14641@CS.ORST.EDU> Date: 1 Oct 92 08:24:39 GMT Sender: usenet@CS.ORST.EDU Organization: Computer Science Dept., Oregon State University Lines: 176 Originator: [detail withheld]@storm.CS.ORST.EDU Nntp-Posting-Host: storm.cs.orst.edu And finally they manage to chain me to the keyboard :-) to write the next installment of the Fictional Wrestling Federation. This one is a little more light-hearted in intent, one of the many spoofs the FWF central office has put forth. The United States Heavyweight Champion, John "Flawless" Lawless defends his title against the ex-convict from Georgia, the brutal man with a nightstick known in the FWF as Snailz. Unfortunately, I already sent in my vote to [name withheld] for "Worst Wrestler of the Year" for Papa Shango instead of the WWF prototype for Snailz. Oh, well. Too close to call. Comments from both parties involved: Snailz: "John Lawless! You stand in the FWF as the United States Heavyweight Champion! (slobber, menacing shudder . . . ) Well where I come from, they don't bother with fancy wrestling moves! They just beat people up! And when I'm through with you, even your own mother's not going to be able to recognize you!!" Lawless: "I sometimes wonder if I'm in the right line of work when I get stuck in angles like this . . . I mean, here I am, the flawless U.S. Champion, the only man who has ever beaten Doctor Death, the overrated goof most call the FWF's greatest World Champion of all time, I work hard and wrestle as skillfully as any man who has ever stepped into the squared circle, I'm the #1 contender for the World Title, and I still have to deal with this? Where do they get the idea that this drooling idiot is suddenly a world-class athlete fresh out of the big house? Well, Snailz, in the FWF we wrestle, we participate in a demanding and punishing sport. There's no room for slobbering morons with billy clubs and rotten singing voices, because when 'Flawless' Lawless walks that aisle, the 'Wrestler Be Cool' stick in my hand and the U.S. Title around my waist, it's time for you to learn just what wrestling is all about! It's not just working people over like in the slammer! And when that 10 minute mark hits, and you're going HACK PANT PANT HEAVE GASP in the middle of the ring, you're all mine!!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- A lone individual in a red-orange prison outfit walks down the aisle at Madison Square Garden, and the crowd's reaction is quite mixed. The marks are booing furiously, and the hardcores are making dejected glances skyward. A number of some sort above the letters "COBB COUNTY CORRECTIONS" adorn the front and back of the loose-fitting outfit, and he holds a black nightstick in the air. Alfred Grinchley sighs, wondering in his mind where they find these gimmicks, and begins his task. "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. IT IS FOR THE UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!! COMING DOWN THE AISLE, FROM THE CORRECTIONAL FACILITIES IN COBB COUNTY, GEORGIA, HE WEIGHS THREE HUNDRED AND TWO POUNDS . . SNAAAAAAAAAAAAAILZ!!!" Snailz steps into the ring and begins beating the palm of one hand with the nightstick in the other, a menacing shudder going through his entire body starting at his cheeks. He is supposed to represent the results of a turnstyle system of justice, so just humor me, here. :-P The sound of "Exodus" breaks over the PA system, and compared to Snailz, the other man coming down the aisle seems like a face by comparison to marks and hardcores alike. However, he is a heel as well, leaving some of the more devout marks confused as to who they're supposed to root for. Too complex a concept, that bad guys would actually want to wrestle each other for some ridiculous notion like "competition." Too much WWF for these characters, not enough FWF. The Grinch continues his task. "AND HIS OPPONENT, MAKING HIS ENTRY TO THE RING AREA. HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT POUNDS AND HAILING FROM SEATTLE, WASHINGTON, HERE IS THE UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION . . . JOHN 'FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!!!" He strides confidently down the aisle in a silver robe with black glittering frills. The letters "Simply Flawless" are scored on the back in ebon sparkly, and short brown hair is cut close to his skull. Bright blue eyes look at the ring with open contempt, smiling down upon the man in the ring who spreads his arms for the referee to frisk him for foreign^H^H^H^H^H^H^Hinternational objects. A wooden rod is clutched in both of his hands, the infamous "Wrestler Be Cool" stick. He has used it on occasion for chicanery, though his original reason for bringing it was because of a preliminary opponent once striking him with a set of brass knuckles. He walks up the ring steps, struts along the ring apron and gestures with the stick like a sceptre to the referee. The zebra complies, parting the ropes for his entry. He responds by leaping over them, landing on his feet inside the ring. The ref scowls in humiliation. He unties the sash of his robe and folds it off his arms, handing it to an attendant. The United States Championship belt is around his waist for the second reign. He has no intention of releasing it to this ex-con. He removes it and hands it to the official, who holds it high above his head to show to all what this match is for. The United States Champion is auto- matically the number one contender for the World Heavyweight Championship now held by Doctor Death. John "Flawless" Lawless has had two shots at the ultimate gold. Once, he defeated the Doctor. The second, he allowed his cornerman to be lured into the ring and get him disqualified. The third, he intends to be more careful. But he has to ensure that there is a third shot yet. To do so, he must defeat this man. Snailz and Lawless stand roughly eye-to-eye, though Lawless is somewhat shorter than his foe. Lawless is chattering at Snailz arrogantly and defiantly, while the larger man seems satisfied with squinting and drooling all over his few days' growth of beard. Finally, the referee separates them and explains the rules to each. Pfah! As if the rules meant anything to either man. Lawless is one of the dirtiest players in the game, while Snailz never knew the meaning of a rule. Finally, the match begins. Snailz lunges at Lawless mindlessly, who coolly buries a foot in the groin as he comes close enough. Snailz doubles over, falling to his knees, and Lawless backs into the corner to give himself distance when he runs out, snatching Snailz's head in his hands and leaping over him, bending him back double in the process! Snailz curls up in pain, and Lawless is quickly to work. He lifts him up, sending him to the ropes and unleashing a full-coiled set of legs about two inches from his jaw on the rebound. The drop kick from such powerful legs at such close distance snaps Snailz's head back, and his whole body seems to fly backwards as if shot from a cannon, becoming coiled in the ropes. Lawless sends him across again, and levels him to the mat with a roundhouse Russian sickle. The U.S. Champion backs to the ropes, watching Snailz closely. Snailz is used to fighting in a prison, not a wrestling ring, and his movements are shaky, unexperienced, not familiar with the change in setting. With less-skilled opponents, he could just brutalize them. But with "Flawless" Lawless, one has to know how to wrestle just to stay in the ring with him for any length of time. Snailz comes up, still doubled over, not having the experience to know that this is a blaring mistake. Sure enough, Lawless grabs him around the chin and turns him over for a neckbreaker. Snailz clutches at his neck, his entire spine aching badly by now. He's not used to pain. He's not used to opponents who actually can outmaneuver him. Lawless is quick and agile enough to do so with most foes. He draws Snails up in an armbar, imprisoning him as he pulls him back to the ropes and sends him across once again. Waistlock up, grab under one red-clad thigh to spin him around, and his entire spine is smashed on the canvas as Lawless 180's to plant him on the mat. Lawless hops to his feet gleefully, pointing with both fingers out to the crowd as he shouts his hallmark call . . . "Now you're going to see . . . a Perfect Plex!" He draws Snailz back to his feet, takes him in a front facelock, slings one of his arms overhead. A simple hook of the leg, and Snailz is turned over into a bridge cradle, knocking the wind out of him while imprisoning him for the referee's count: one, two, three. Lawless comes out of his bridge, grabbing Snailz by the hair and lifting his head up an instant before shoving it back to the mat in simple humility. He raises his arms in the air, and the U.S. belt is returned to him. The Grinch makes it official: "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AND STILL UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: JOHN 'FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!!!" He bounds over the ring ropes outside the ring, and struts all the way back to the dressing room. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Okay. There's an FWF spoof, where the big-time revolve-around-gimmick wrestlers with no work and no skill are reduced to what they should be: flashy-clothed jobbers.
