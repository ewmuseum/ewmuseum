Jerry "Stratofortress" Henderson Both counted out when Doctor Death interfered ***> Death awarded World Title ***> Flayer awarded U.S. Title (first) Match 3: Doctor Death vs. "Steady Metal" Van Slammer Death wins with Vulcan Death Grip Match 4: Storm vs. Demolishers Match is stopped at Demolisher Slash's request (Demolisher Venom's back was broken) Match 5: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins with Perfect Plex ***> Lawless wins U.S. Title Match 6: Blue Steele vs. the Terminator Steele wins by countout Match 7: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Skull Cruncher Morgan wins with Leg Drop of Doom Match 8: Doctor Death vs. Blue Steele Death wins with crucifix Match 9: Annihilus vs. Aaron "Lightning" Murphy (w/ Thunder) Annihilus wins by DQ when Thunder breaks pinfall count after the Annihilation Spin Match 10: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. the Terminator Morgan wins by DQ when Doctor Death breaks pinfall count after the Leg Drop of Doom Match 11: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. Skull Cruncher Flayer wins with Lightning Stroke Match 12: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Lawless wins with Perfect Plex Match 13: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Doctor Death Death wins by DQ because of Morgan knocking out the first match ref, after false finish "win" for Morgan with inside cradle (counted by second ref who was unaware of how the first ref lost his senses) Match 14: Blue Steele vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins with Frankensteiner Match 15: Annihilus vs. Jerry "Thunder" McGaghney (w/ Lightning) Thunder wins by DQ when Annihilus hits him with a steel chair Match 16: Doctor Death vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins by hitting Death with the `Wrestler Be Cool' stick ***> Lawless wins World Title ***> U.S. Title vacated Match 17: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. Annihilus Annihilus wins by submission with Scorpion Death Lock ***> Annihilus wins World Title Battle Royale (Match 18): Order Wrestler Thrown out by 1 "Crimson" Joe Reed Lawless 2 John "Flawless" Lawless Death 3 The Terminator Steele 4 "Steady Metal" Van Slammer Thunder 5 Aaron "Lightning" Murphy Sagga/Mauler 6 "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer Death 7 Doctor Death Morgan/Henderson 8 The Invincible Titan Morgan Henderson/Steele 9 Jerry "Stratofortress" Henderson Mauler/Steele 10 Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Thunder 11 Jerry "Thunder" McGaghney Sagga/Steele 12 Sultan Sitan El Sagga Steele WINNER: Blue Steele ***> Steele awarded I-C Title Match 19: "Dark Star" Dominic Walters vs. Jet Jackson ([name withheld]) Walters wins with reverse claw hold Match 20: Four corners elimination contest with Sultan Sitan El Sagga, the Terminator, "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer, and Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus. Sagga beats the Terminator with the Squid. Sagga counted out of ring after Romulus delivers Plasma Torpedo. Flayer beats Romulus with Lightning Stroke. ***> Flayer awarded U.S. Title (second) Match 21A: The Latin Wildfire vs. Cruncher Rankin ([name withheld]) Wildfire by countout Match 21B: Doctor Death vs. the Sheepwhackers Death beats Whacker Dutch with DDT Death beats Whacker Duke with Vulcan Death Grip Match 22: "Victorian Heartthrob" Dennis Silver vs. Skull Cruncher ([name withheld]) Cruncher wins with flying DDT Match 23: Annihilus vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Annihilus wins with Annihilation Spin Match 24: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters ([name withheld]) Reed wins by DQ when Walters hits him with the timekeeper's bell Match 25: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. John "Flawless" Lawless--best 2/3 Morgan wins first fall with DDT Lawless wins second fall with Perfect Plex Morgan wins third fall with Leg Drop of Doom Match 26: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Flayer wins with a steel chair, pulling Romulus' foot off ropes during the official's three-count Match 27: Blue Steele vs. Nicholas Scratch Steele wins with brain buster Match 28: Annihilus vs. the Invincible Titan Morgan Morgan wins with inside cradle ***> Morgan wins World Title Match 29: Doctor Death vs. Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Romulus wins by DQ when Damien Flayer breaks a pinfall count after a power bomb Match 30: Jet Jackson, the Latin Wildfire and "the Victorian Heartthrob" Dennis Silver vs. the House of Chaos ([name withheld]) House of Chaos wins when Jet Jackson submits in the Harbinger of Doom Match 31: Blue Steele vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Sagga wins with backroll press, using ropes for leverage ***> Sagga wins I-C Title Match 32: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. the Terminator Lawless wins with Perfect Plex Match 33: Nicholas Scratch vs. Jet Jackson Scratch wins by submission with leg-brace-assisted figure four Match 34: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters, lumberjack ([name withheld]) Reed wins with Russian leg sweep Match
