FWF: Devastation vs Storm

3 November 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Path: sparky!uunet!charon.amdahl.com!pacbell.com!decwrl!sdd.hp.com!saimiri.primate.wisc.edu!zaphod.mps.ohio-state.edu!caen!spool.mu.edu!news.cs.indiana.edu!nstn.ns.ca!ac.dal.ca![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@ac.dal.ca ([name withheld]) Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Devastation vs Storm Message-ID: <1992Nov3.214741.8601@ac.dal.ca> Date: 3 Nov 92 21:47:41 -0400 Organization: Dalhousie University, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Lines: 302 After a considerable length of time, we finally have the first title defence of the FWF's World tag team title. The champions, Storm, have jumped back into the limelight of controversy with a threat to Andrew Simms and a beating when "Hurricane" didn't heed their warning to drop the "Hurricane" moniker. The official word on Simms is back and he has sustained a hairline fracture in his forearm as a result of Lightning smashing said arm into the ring post. The damage could well have been worse, had it not been for Derek Sanderson and Johnny Houlton. Why the save by Devastation? Naturally they're Storm's greatest challenge right now, but further to that, they're also old friends of Simms', all three men hailing from Miami. Those who recall the tournament final between Devastation and Storm in the spring will recall it was one of the most intense battles yet witnessed in the FWF. Devastation held the clear advantage in that match. They could have won the titles with a draw, disqualification, or countout due to the round-robin rules governing the tournament. Devastation's downfall came when Thunder turned over a small package behind the referee's back, allowing Lightning to score the pinfall on Derek. Tonight, Storm will hold the decided advantage as a title can only change hands on a pinfall or submission. Before we take you to the arena for the match, some pre-match comments from the number one contenders, followed by the champs. "Storm, your first title defence promises to be your last tonight. You've been getting away with all sorts of things since your debut match here in the FWF. Tonight, we're going to make sure you get away with less than you expect. You're going to get away from this match without two golden belts around your waists." "Devastation, last week, Andrew Simms was number one on our 'Most Wanted' list. You changed that when you stuck your noses in where they didn't belong. You managed to save your friend, but all the friends in the world won't be able to save you from what we have in store for you tonight!" And now we take you to the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit for the match! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The fans here in Detroit start to trickle in from the concession stands as the lights in the arena dim and Alfred Grinchley and referee Manny Davis enter the ring. The Grinch takes the microphone and Davis fastens the tag ropes to the corners. Michael W. Smith's "Ashton" starts up on the P.A. system and the fans look to the dressing room to see THREE men in red tights emerge from the curtain. Two of them are wearing brightly coloured jackets and the third sports a white sling around his neck, supporting an arm. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE FICTIONAL WRESTLING FEDERATION'S WORLD TAG-TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP. IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL WITH A ONE HOUR TIME LIMIT. BEING LED TO THE RING BY 'hurricane' ANDREW SIMMS, HAILING FROM MIAMI, FLORIDA, AND WEIGHING IN AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF FOUR HUNDRED, NINETY-SIX POUNDS, HERE IS DEREK SANDERSON AND JOHNNY HOULTON, DEVASTAAAAAATION!!!!!!!!!" The fans go electric for the face team. Simms was a surprise addition to this match. Some fans are curious about Simms' presence in this match. How wise a move is this considering his injured state and the fact that the very team who did this to him are involved in this match? Simms poses no real threat to Storm, whereas Storm could pose a threat to the corner man. Simms, however wouldn't be here now in support of his friends if he was intimidated in any way. He is smiling broadly as the three men make their way down to the ring, greeting several fans along the aisle. As the three Floridians enter the ring, "Ashton" is cut from the PA system and the lights go out. Flash! BOOM! The lights slowly brighten as William Tell's Overture, "Storm" is synthesized over the PA. Two black-caped individuals make their way down the aisle. "THEIR OPPONENTS ARE THE TAG-TEAM CHAMPIONS OF THE WORLD! WEIGHING IN AT A TOTAL COMBINED WEIGHT OF FIVE HUNDRED, SIXTY-THREE POUNDS, HERE IS AARON 'LIGHTNING' MURPHY AND JERRY 'THUNDER' McGAGHNEY, STOOOOOOOORRRRRRMM!!!" The champs' face shows little emotion. Entering the ring, Thunder points at Simms and says something to him that only the men in the ring hear. Storm walks straight to their corner and remove their capes to show the golden championship belts around their waists. These are removed and handed to the referee who holds them high overhead for all to see. This is what tag-team competition is all about. Simms slides out of the ring while the four men in tonight's contest loosen up. The referee calls for the bell and from the looks of things, Houlton and Thunder, the larger men on their respective teams, are going to start this match. The two athletes circle each other. Thunder is clearly the largest wrestler in the ring, outweighing Houlton by roughly forty pounds. Houlton however is no slouch at 260 lbs. His tanned torso shows evidence of long hours in the gym. As the two lock up in a collar-to-elbow tieup, Thunder's size difference proves to be an advantage as the Stormer grabs a handful of blond hair and forces his opponent back to the ropes. The referee warns Thunder to back off and lays a count on the Stormer. Thunder puts his hands out to his sides and backs off. Clean break! Johnny had been expecting a cheap shot, and the clean break by one of the most sadistic wrestlers in the FWF comes as a surprise to him. He refuses to be suckered into thinking this will be a clean match though. He knows otherwise. Thunder holds his hands out in front of him and gestures for Houlton to bring it on. Once again they circle each other and lock up. Johnny finds himself caught in a side headlock and thrown over a hip to the mat. Johnny's shoulders are down and the referee draws a one count before Johnny shifts his weight to one side. Thunder quickly releases the side headlock and turns around into a front facelock. The Devastator manages to stretch his leg out however and hit the rope. The referee calls for the break. The fans aren't seeing the kind of match they thought they'd be seeing. So far it's been all Thunder and relatively slow paced. Johnny confers briefly in his corner with Derek before resuming the match. There is a third lockup and Johnny once again finds himself in a headlock. This time, however, Johnny shoves the Stormer back to the ropes and throws him to the opposite ropes. Thunder returns with a shoulderblock. Johnny stays on his stomach as the larger man goes to the ropes again and hops over him. Thunder is met with a dropkick, but doesn't go down. Johnny runs to the ropes and on the return, the staggering Stormer is flattened to the mat after a cross body block. The referee moves into position to count and only makes it to one. Johnny is quickly up on his feet again and tags Derek. Returning to his opponent, Johnny delivers a bodyslam. Derek is on the centre of the apron. Grabbing the rope, Johnny snaps it back and Derek sails over it, smacking Thunder with a headbutt. Thunder grabs his head and rolls to the opposite side of the ring. Derek stops him with a knee drop in the back of the head. Grabbing Thunder's head, Derek snaps the larger man over his shoulder where he falls in a sitting position. Derek quickly backs into the corner and up to the second rope. Leaping, he catches the back of Thunder's head, flips over his opponent, and snaps his neck forward. As Thunder gets to his feet, Derek runs at him with a swinging neckbreaker. Derek stands over his opponent and drops down to deliver another headbutt. Hooking the Stormer's leg, Sanderson makes the pin. ONE... TWO... and an authoritative kickout by the champ. Derek intends to control the momentum of the match however and runs to the ropes. Thunder ducks an attempt at a clothesline. Derek bounces off the far side. Thunder turns and manages to catch the smaller man over his shoulders! Carrying Sanderson over to Storm's corner, Lightning receives the tag and mounts the turnbuckles while Thunder airplane spins Derek. Derek is set back down on his feet and Thunder backs off. As the Devastator turns to face Lightning, Lightning dives off the top and a clothesline completes the "Tornado" maneuver. Lightning takes advantage of the his opponent's dizzy state and climbs back to the top rope. As Sanderson gets to his feet again, a second clothesline sends him back! Murphy has no intent of letting up on his opponent. Derek is now led to his feet and one of his arms draped over Lightning's head. The smaller Devastator is lifted into a vertical suplex position and dropped on the top turnbuckle pad. Murphy, himself, mounts the second buckle. A waistlock, and a belly-to-belly superplex! Third move of the night on Sanderson is Murphy's finisher! Storm is pulling out all the stops tonight. Rather than pin his opponent now, Lightning rolls him onto his back. Sitting on Sanderson's back, Lightning grabs his right arm and pulls back on it. Derek moans in agony, but hangs in there. The Stormer looks outside the ring, and catching the eye of Andrew Simms in a sling, laughs. The grip on Sanderson's arm is shifted into a hammerlock. Bracing the arm in that position, Lightning rears up in the air and drives a knee into the limb. Manny Davis checks for a submission, but none is coming right now. Again Murphy rears up, and again his knee connects with Derek's right arm. Getting to his feet and standing over his opponent, Lightning leads Derek to his feet by grabbing a handful of hair. Folding his opponent's injured limb behind his back, Lightning lifts him up, and slams him down to the mat on top of his arm. Derek arches his back up on impact and gingerly pulls his numbing arm from beneath him. Lightning, looks at him, at Simms, and laughs again. Two handfuls of hair pull the Devastator to his feet, before he finds himself unceremoniously thrown over the top rope to the floor. "Got any advice for him?" Lightning taunts Simms as he tags Thunder. Thunder hops off the apron and down to the mat. Seeing Simms bent over Sanderson on the floor, Thunder booms, "Get back!" at him. Simms ignores the threat and continues to help his friend to his feet. Thunder, angered, pushes Simms to the floor. The fans cry out in defiance, Johnny bounds out of his corner, and Manny Davis starts admonishing the Stormer. However, as Houlton rounds the corner, the referee's attention is distracted as he bids the Devastator to return to his corner. The referee sufficiently distracted, Thunder picks Derek up and Lightning hops off the apron to grab a steel chair. The fans scream in protest, but the referee is completely tied up trying to get a protesting Johnny Houlton back into Devastation's corner. Thunder holds Sanderson with his right arm towards Lightning. Lightning rears back with the chair and... Falls flat on his face as Simms lunges at his legs from behind. Derek on seeing this realizes his chance, and breaking free from Thunder's grasp, headbutts him right between the eyes. Grabbing his opponent by the back of the head, he drives Thunder's forehead into the steel barricade drawing blood! Derek wastes no time in throwing his opponent in the ring. The referee, meanwhile has shifted his focus to Lightning and the steel chair he has, threatening Simms. Lightning is forced back into Storm's corner. Meanwhile, Derek makes the tag and Johnny enters the ring! Devastation, however, face another setback as the referee turns from Lightning and forces Johnny back to his corner. He did not see the tag. Thunder capitalizes on this. Wiping the blood from his eyes, he grabs Derek by the head, places it between his own knees and folds Derek's arms onto his back in a double hammerlock. "What now?" wonder the fans. McGaghney grabs the hammerlocked arms and lifts up, pulling Sanderson vertical in a piledriver and dropping down to the mat! Sanderson isn't moving much, save for an occasional twitch. Thunder puts the Devastator's shoulders to the mat... The referee is still tied up with Houlton, but turns to see Simms up on the apron, yelling at Lightning, missing the pin totally. Manny Davis crosses over and starts having words with those two. Meanwhile, Houlton climbs to the top rope as the fans cheer in approval. Thunder gets to his feet and turns to see what the referee's problem is... and gets introduced to the mat face-first in a bulldog! Johnny rolls out of the ring and gets back in his corner. Simms hops off the apron and the referee turns around to see Derek gradually coming to, although still flat on his back, and Thunder, lying prone on the mat near a red stain on the mat where the bulldog landed. The referee starts a knockout count. One...two...three...four...five...six...seven...eight...and Derek gets up to his knees. Slowly the Devastator inches to his partner's outstretched hand. Slowly Thunder looks up and sees what's going on. With Derek about a foot from Johnny, Thunder shakes off the cobwebs and grabs his opponent's leg. With the weight advantage Thunder has, and owing to Derek's fatigue, there's little the Devastator can to to avoid being dragged by Thunder into the centre of the ring. Derek is lifted to his feet. Looping one of Derek's arms over his head and grabbing onto his leg, Thunder hoists his smaller opponent onto his shoulders in a Torture Rack. The fans go silent as they watch Lightning climb to the top rope. No tag has been made and the referee starts his disqualification count. Lightning ascends the turnbuckles while Thunder backs off a few steps and lets go of Sanderson's head. Before delivering "The Hurricane" Lightning yells down to Simms, "This is a Hurricane and don't you forget it!" At the four count, Lightning leaps and Sanderson is turned 180 degrees horizontally as Murphy's knee knocks him off Thunder's shoulders. Manny Davis scoots in position to make the count. ONE... TWO... and just as the referee's hand is about to his the mat for the third time, Johnny enters the ring to make the save. Lightning tears after him with the referee trying desperately to get these illegal men out and regain some semblance of control. Derek is powerless to resist as Thunder picks him up and sets him down on the top turnbuckle. Wiping the blood from his eyes once again, Thunder mounts the second turnbuckle and sets up for the super brainbuster! ..but what is Simms doing up on the apron? Pulling his cast out of the sling, Simms sneaks up behind Thunder, out of the sight of the referee, and nails Thunder in the back of the head! He then gently slaps Derek in the face to get him back to his senses and hops to the floor. Derek sees what position his opponent is in. Looping his legs over the ropes to the inside, Derek stands up on the second turnbuckle. Sanderson drops forward and Thunder's bloody forehead is nailed with a falling headbutt! The fans are elated at this surprise turn of events. The referee finally gets back to the action and counts the pin as Derek hooks a leg. ONE... (Both Lightning and Johnny enter the ring) TWO... (Lightning is closer... Johnny dropkicks him!) THREE!!!! New Champions! "Ashton" starts up on the PA system. Against all odds, and despite the fact that Johnny Houlton couldn't get the tag to get in and help his partner, Devastation have won the Tag-team Championship, thanks to help from their friend, "Hurricane" Andrew Simms. Simms smiles widely as he takes the belts from the referee. Turning towards Lightning, who is bent over a recovering Thunder, Simms holds the belts up and says, "This is for what you two did to my arm!" His revenge complete, Simms turns to Devastation and presents them with the belts as the referee holds their hands up in victory and the Grinch makes the official announcement: "THE WINNERS OF THIS MATCH... AND NEEEEEEWWW FICTIONAL WRESTLING FEDERATION WORLD TAG-TEAM CHAMPIONS, DEREK SANDERSON AND JOHNNY HOULTON: DEVASTAAAAAAAAAAAATION!!!!!!!!!!" To the surprise of all the fans, Storm quietly leave the ring and head back to the dressing room. They'll get their rematch somewhere down the line... and when they do, Devastation better watch out! Meanwhile, Lightning still has his title shot at the US title, held be Titan Morgan, to concentrate on. He can't risk injury fighting two on three to extract a small measure of revenge right now. Besides, Thunder isn't in much shape to fight anyone right now. Blood is still running down his face from earlier in the match. He'll have quite the headache tonight. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sorry it took so long. No telling when I'll get time to write the next one.
