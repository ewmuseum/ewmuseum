FWF: Tag Rankings & History

7 January 1993

Path: sparky!uunet!spool.mu.edu!uwm.edu!caen!news.cs.indiana.edu!nstn.ns.ca!ac.dal.ca![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@ac.dal.ca ([name withheld]) Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Tag Rankings & History Message-ID: <1993Jan7.213632.9981@ac.dal.ca> Date: 7 Jan 93 21:36:32 -0400 Organization: Dalhousie University, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Lines: 141 FWF Tag Rankings: Champs: Devastation (Derek Sanderson & Johnny Houlton) 1 - Wyld Stallions (Casey Kruger & Terry Rinaldi) 2 - Chameleons 3 - Storm (Aaron "Lightning" Murphy & Jerry "Thunder" McGaghney) 4 - Blade Runners ("Rock" Steve Rockwell & "Flash" Johnathan Flann) 5 - Raiders ("Man Mountain" Huey Cleveland & "Killer" Kevin Johnson) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 1: Storm vs Demolishers. Demolishers lost when Venom was crippled by Storm. Start of Tag Team Title Tournament. Match 2: Devastation vs Blues Brothers. Devastation wins after Derek delivers a fall- away elbow on Boris and subsequently pins him. Match 3: Blade Runners vs Storm. Storm wins by disqualification when Rock accidentally clotheslines the referee over the top rope in an effort to prevent Sting from being crippled by Storm. Match 4: Wyld Stallions vs Blues Brothers. Casey's Indian Deathlock forces Boris to pass out from the pain. Match 5: Devastation vs Blade Runners. Time limit expires when Derek is caught in Rock's full nelson. Match 6: Wyld Stallions vs Storm. Casey is unable to beat the count into the ring due to a knee injury sustained at the hands of Storm. Storm wins by countout. Match 7: Blade Runners vs Blues Brothers. Sting pins Brutus following a sunset flip off the top rope. Match 8: (non tournament) Raiders debut match vs the Sheepwhackers. Sheepwhacker Duke is pinned by "Killer" after the "Raider Decapitator" legdrop. The Raiders continue to beat on the Sheepwhackers after the match and the Wyld Stallions intervene. In the brawl that ensues, both Stallions get the worst of the beating and Casey's knee is re-injured. Match 9: Devastation vs Wyld Stallions. The Stallions injuries in the previous match come into play in this match. Casey is pinned by Johnny after a belly-to- back superplex. Match 10: Storm vs Blues Brothers. The Blues Brothers trickery and an accidental push of the referee by Thunder costs Storm the match as a result of a disqualification. Afterwards, Storm's nearly-legendary brutality comes into play once more as Boris ends up hospitalized with a concussion following an assault with the bell and a steel chair. Match 11: Blade Runners vs Wyld Stallions. Terry and Rock were counted out of the ring. Draw. Evidence of friction between the Blade Runners. Match 12: TOURNAMENT FINAL! Devastation vs Storm. After a gruelling match, Thunder reverses a small package causing Lightning to get the pin on Derek. Match 13: Blade Runners vs Devastation. The problems between the Blade Runners came to a head outside the ring during the match, causing Sting to get counted out. Sting deserted Rock after beating on him with the timekeeper's bell. "Sting" became known as "Scorpion". Match 14: Wyld Stallions vs Raiders (2/3 falls) Fall 1: "Man Mountain" passes out while in Casey's Indian Deathlock. Fall 2: Terri Rinaldi falls victim to the Raider Decapitator. Fall 3: Raiders were double teaming Rinaldi. Kruger brought a chair into the ring. The ref had enough and disqualified both teams. Match ruled a draw. Match 15: New Blade Runners (Flash & Rock) vs Damien Flayer & "Scorpion" Stan Golding The former partners, Rock and Scorpion, battle to a double countout. This brawl continues all the way back to the dressing room, despite attempts by officials to break it up. Match 16: Sheepwhackers vs Chameleons Chameleons' debut match. The Chameleons appear in outfits identical to the Sheepwhackers. They imitate many of their moves and pin them with their own finisher (battering ram & double stomach breaker combo). Match 17: Devastation vs Storm (champs) for the Tag-team Championship Devastation appear with "Hurricane" Andrew Simms as their corner man (Simms had his arm broken a couple of weeks prior by Storm). In the end, this proved instrumental in Storm's downfall. Derek was set up for a super brainbuster and Simms nailed Thunder on the back of the head with his cast. Derek scored a flying headbutt and a three count. Devastation NEW champs! Match 18: Four corner match to determine #1 contenders with Wyld Stallions, New Blade Runners, Chameleons, and Raiders. Raiders disqualified when Man Mountain interfered to break a pin attempt. Blade Runner Flash pinned by Casey Kruger after a double wipeout. Wyld Stallion Casey Kruger pinned by Chameleon 2 after Devastation's power-plex. Winners: Chameleons. Match 19: Raiders vs Wyld Stallions (cage match). Wyld Stallion Terry Rinaldi forces "Killer" Kevin Johnson to submit to a surfboard after a wild match in which the referee and Casey Kruger were unconscious for extended periods. Match 20: Devastation (champs) vs Chameleons for the Tag-team Championship Chameleon 2 ends up submitting to a short arm scissors by Johnny Houlton. Match 21: Clash of the Masters. Devastation (FWF Champs) vs Crime & Punishment (PWA Champs) for FWF/PWA championship. After a long, seesaw battle, Punisher Lang makes the pin on Johnny Houlton. Houlton's foot was on the bottom rope, but the referee missed this. Match 22: Storm vs Sultan Sitan El Sagga and Nicholas Scratch (open contract match). Storm's Boston Crab / Clothesline combination broke Nicholas Scratch's back. After the match, Thunder's super brainbuster onto a chair left Sagga with severe neck injuries and a concussion.
