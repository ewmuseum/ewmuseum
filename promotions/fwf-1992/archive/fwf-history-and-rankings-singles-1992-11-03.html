Message-ID: <1d5ap4INNn8q@flop.ENGR.ORST.EDU> Date: 3 Nov 92 07:46:44 GMT Organization: Oregon State University Lines: 312 NNTP-Posting-Host: xanth.cs.orst.edu Finally, we have a posting of the current rankings and the history of the FWF. I'll leave it to Liam and Jason to post their divistions' rankings, but here are mine: World Heavyweight Champion: DOCTOR DEATH Contenders: 1. (United States Champion) THE INVINCIBLE TITAN MORGAN 2. John "Flawless" Lawless 3. Annihilus 4. "Scorpion" Stan Golding 5. "Hurricane" Andrew Simms (perhaps in trouble due to recent injury) 6. Kane the Undertaker 7. Aaron "Lightning" Murphy 8. "Flash" Johnathon Flann 9. "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer 10. "Suicidal" Paul Stuart I'll give champions for Jason and Liam, but I'll let them decide who the top contenders are. Intercontinental Champion: "IRON WARRIOR" BLUE STEELE World Tag Team Champions: STORM--AARON "LIGHTNING" MURPHY and JERRY "THUNDER" McGAGHNEY And now, the record file of all 60 matches written by myself and [name withheld]: Match 1: Doctor Death vs. "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer Death wins by countout Match 2: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Jerry "Stratofortress" Henderson Both counted out when Doctor Death interfered ***> Death awarded World Title (first) ***> Flayer awarded U.S. Title (first) Match 3: Doctor Death vs. "Steady Metal" Van Slammer Death wins with Vulcan Death Grip Match 4: Storm vs. Demolishers Match is stopped at Demolisher Slash's request (Demolisher Venom's back was broken) Match 5: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins with Perfect Plex ***> Lawless wins U.S. Title (first) Match 6: Blue Steele vs. the Terminator Steele wins by countout Match 7: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Skull Cruncher Morgan wins with Leg Drop of Doom Match 8: Doctor Death vs. Blue Steele Death wins with crucifix Match 9: Annihilus vs. Aaron "Lightning" Murphy (w/ Thunder) Annihilus wins by DQ when Thunder breaks pinfall count after the Annihilation Spin Match 10: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. the Terminator Morgan wins by DQ when Doctor Death breaks pinfall count after the Leg Drop of Doom Match 11: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. Skull Cruncher Flayer wins with Lightning Stroke Match 12: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Lawless wins with Perfect Plex Match 13: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Doctor Death Death wins by DQ because of Morgan knocking out the first match ref, after false finish "win" for Morgan with inside cradle (counted by second ref who was unaware of how the first ref lost his senses) Match 14: Blue Steele vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins with Frankensteiner Match 15: Annihilus vs. Jerry "Thunder" McGaghney (w/ Lightning) Thunder wins by DQ when Annihilus hits him with a steel chair Match 16: Doctor Death vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins by hitting Death with the `Wrestler Be Cool' stick ***> Lawless wins World Title ***> U.S. Title vacated Match 17: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. Annihilus Annihilus wins by submission with Scorpion Deathlock ***> Annihilus wins World Title Battle Royale (Match 18):--DREAMCARD IV Order Wrestler Thrown out by 1 "Crimson" Joe Reed Lawless 2 John "Flawless" Lawless Death 3 The Terminator Steele 4 "Steady Metal" Van Slammer Thunder 5 Aaron "Lightning" Murphy Sagga/Mauler 6 "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer Death 7 Doctor Death Morgan/Henderson 8 The Invincible Titan Morgan Henderson/Steele 9 Jerry "Stratofortress" Henderson Mauler/Steele 10 Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Thunder 11 Jerry "Thunder" McGaghney Sagga/Steele 12 Sultan Sitan El Sagga Steele WINNER: Blue Steele ***> Steele awarded I-C Title (first) Match 19: "Dark Star" Dominic Walters vs. Jet Jackson ([name withheld]) Walters wins with reverse claw hold Match 20: Four corners elimination contest with Sultan Sitan El Sagga, the Terminator, "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer, and Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus. Sagga beats the Terminator with the Squid. Sagga counted out of ring after Romulus delivers Plasma Torpedo. Flayer beats Romulus with Lightning Stroke. ***> Flayer awarded U.S. Title (second) Match 21A: The Latin Wildfire vs. Cruncher Rankin ([name withheld]) Wildfire by countout Match 21B: Doctor Death vs. the Sheepwhackers Death beats Whacker Dutch with DDT Death beats Whacker Duke with Vulcan Death Grip Match 22: "Victorian Heartthrob" Dennis Silver vs. Skull Cruncher ([name withheld]) Cruncher wins with flying DDT Match 23: Annihilus vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Annihilus wins with Annihilation Spin Match 24: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters ([name withheld]) Reed wins by DQ when Walters hits him with the timekeeper's bell Match 25: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. John "Flawless" Lawless--best 2/3 Morgan wins first fall with DDT Lawless wins second fall with Perfect Plex Morgan wins third fall with Leg Drop of Doom Match 26: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Flayer wins with a steel chair, pulling Romulus' foot off ropes during the official's three-count Match 27: Blue Steele vs. Nicholas Scratch Steele wins with brain buster Match 28: Annihilus vs. the Invincible Titan Morgan Morgan wins with inside cradle ***> Morgan wins World Title Match 29: Doctor Death vs. Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Romulus wins by DQ when Damien Flayer breaks pinfall count after a power bomb Match 30: Jet Jackson, the Latin Wildfire and "the Victorian Heartthrob" Dennis Silver vs. the House of Chaos ([name withheld]) House of Chaos wins when Jet Jackson submits in the Harbinger of Doom Match 31: Blue Steele vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Sagga wins with backroll press, using ropes for leverage ***> Sagga wins I-C Title Match 32: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. the Terminator Lawless wins with Perfect Plex Match 33: Nicholas Scratch vs. Jet Jackson Scratch wins by submission with leg-brace-assisted figure four Match 34: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. "Dark Star" Dominic Walters,lumberjack ([name withheld]) Reed wins with Russian leg sweep Match 35: Annihilus vs. Doctor Death Death wins by DQ when Annihilus refuses to release the Scorpion Deathlock Match 36: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. "Crimson" Joe Reed Reed wins with small package ***> Reed wins U.S. Title Match 37: "Dark Star" Dominic Walters vs. "Professor" John Goulding ([name withheld]) Walters wins by submission with reverse claw hold Match 38: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. "Crimson" Joe Reed Morgan wins by DQ when Big Truck Vader breaks pinfall count after the Leg Drop of Doom Match 39: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Big Truck Vader Morgan wins with Leg Drop of Doom Match 40: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Doctor Death Death wins with Vulcan Death Grip ***> Death wins World Title (second) Match 41: The Latin Wildire vs. Jerry "the Stratofortress" Henderson, subbing for the Skull Cruncher ([name withheld]) Henderson wins with Nuclear Air Strike Match 42: John Girder vs. Doctor Death--DREAMCARD V Death wins by DQ when he tosses a set of brass knuckles to Girder behind the ref's back and then acts like he's been hit with them when the ref finally sees it Match 43: "Iron Warrior" Blue Steele vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Steele wins by submission with Iron Deathlock ***> Steele wins I-C Title (second) Match 44: "Flash" Johnathon Flann vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins by DQ when the Terminator basically kills him Match 45: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus Reed wins with Kentucky Backsmasher Match 46: "Rock" Steve Rockwell vs. "Scorpion" Stan Golding Scorpion wins when Damien Flayer trips Rock into a DDT Match 47: Annihilus vs. Nicholas Scratch Annihilus wins with Annihilation Spin Match 48: "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. John "Flawless" Lawless Lawless wins with inside cradle ***> Lawless wins U.S. Title (second) Match 49: Doctor Death (w/ the Terminator) vs. John "Flawless" Lawless (w/ Kane the Undertaker and DePaul Bearer)--DREAMCARD VI Death wins by DQ when Kane attacks him in the ring Match 50: Four corners elimination contest with the Invincible Titan Morgan, John "Flawless" Lawless, Doctor Death, and the Mighty Annihilus Morgan beats Death with side cradle suplex Annihilus eliminated by DQ when Morgan tags in and Annihilus fails to leave the ring by the ref's five-count Morgan beats Lawless with Leg Drop of Doom Match 51: Doctor Death vs. Annihilus Death wins by pinfall with figure four Match 52: Jet Jackson vs. Emerson Wildside ([name withheld]) Wildside wins with reverse elbow off top rope Match 53: The Invincible Titan Morgan vs. Kane the Undertaker Morgan wins by DQ when John Lawless breaks a pinfall count after a DDT Match 54: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. Snailz Lawless wins with Perfect Plex Match 55: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. "Hurricane" Andrew Simms (steel cage, best 2/3) Flayer wins first fall with Lightning Stroke Simms wins second fall with power slam Simms wins third fall with brain buster Match 56: John "Flawless" Lawless vs. the Invincible Titan Morgan Morgan wins with Leg Drop of Doom ***> Morgan wins U.S. Title Match 57: "Greased Lightning" Damien Flayer vs. "Flash" Johnathon Flann Flann wins by DQ when Stan Golding breaks a pinfall count after Flayer was struck with a steel chair by Steve Rockwell Match 58: "Hurricane" Andrew Simms vs. Sultan Sitan El Sagga Simms wins by DQ when Aaron "Lightning" Murphy strikes him with one of the tag team titles during a Boston crab on Sagga Match 59: Doctor Death vs. "Suicidal" Paul Stuart Death wins with cradle suplex Match 60: Rick Tauer vs. the Steel Barbarian ([name withheld]) Tauer wins with
