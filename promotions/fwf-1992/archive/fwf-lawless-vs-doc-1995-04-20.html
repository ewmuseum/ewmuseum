FWF (1992–95) — Card

FWF: Lawless vs. Doc

20 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, the rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy vote has come in, and the group is due to be put together tomorrow, according to news.announce.newgroups. Which basically means that this is liable to be the last post ever made concerning the FWF to r.s.p-w proper. Why not go out with a bang? The World Title match has been signed, and long anticipated in a rematch. The World Heavyweight Champion, John "Flawless" Lawless, fresh off his grueling victory over the monstrous Grendel, must now defend his title against a man with whom he is not unfamiliar, the diabolical Doctor Death. Much history has gone between these two before. Doctor Death at one point was the dominant World Champion of the FWF, to a point where no one expected that he would ever lose the title. And if he did, certainly not to the upstart John "Flawless" Lawless. The upset which took place in Seattle stunned the viewers so much that it was voted as the FWF Match of the Year on surprise value alone. A rematch was also voted as the Dream Match the viewers most wanted to see. However, the match ended very uncleanly in a disqualification victory for the Doctor, disappointing even the FWF bookers. It seems that when you put together a match between two of the dirtiest players in the game, you are destined to not get a clean ending. Thus, on this occasion the booking committee of one in the FWF has stipulated that there *must* be a winner. To that end, there is no disqualification and no count out in this match. The only way to win is by submission or pinfall in the squared circle. With that in mind, we go to the comments of the contestants: Death: "On the eve of a journey into a new medium, one where perhaps the viewership will be more welcoming, the challenge is made. A two-time World Champion defends his prestige against a two-time former World Champion, the man who dominated the FWF for nearly two-thirds of its history with that belt around his waist. When you look for greatness in football, you look to one who but recently retired. When you look for greatness in basketball, you look to one who but recently returned. And when you look for greatness in fantasy wrestling . . . you look to the Doctor. And tonight, the Doctor shows why his name is synonymous with greatness, as I stride into the ring a challenger, and leave as the champion for an unprecedented third time. Death is the broom I take in my hands to sweep clean!" Lawless: "Two World Championships. Five singles titles in all. You want the list of the rest of the guys who have done that before in the FWF? . . . You want it again? That's right, Doc, you're just looking to do something unprecedented. `Flawless' Lawless has already been there, already done that! Record number of World Championships . . . shared! Record number of U.S. Championships . . . exclusively owned! Record number of singles titles total . . . exclusively owned by yours truly! And you are *not* going to become the only man to win a third World Championship at *my* expense! I have . . . and you had! You are indeed a two-time former World Champion, and that . . . is the way you're gonna stay! So rack 'em up and head 'em out, Doc, 'cause when `Flawless' Lawless walks that aisle, he goes where no man, woman or child has gone before! And you will be left kissing the footprints I leave behind." That having been said, we go to ringside . . . -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The macabre tone of "Toccata and Fugue in D Minor" rings through Madison Square Garden, and the fans begin to boo loudly for the man they have come to despise most of any heel in the FWF. Alfred Grinchley gulps in his throat, for he likes not what the sound will do for the demeanor of the man soon to come. He picks up his microphone, stutters a moment, and begins the introductions. "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL . . . AND THERE ARE NO DISQUALIFICATIONS AND NO COUNT- OUTS! INTRODUCING FIRST, THE CHALLENGER. HE HAILS FROM PARTS UNKNOWN. WEIGHING IN AT TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN POUNDS . . . DOCTOR DEATH!!!" The ebonclad figure steps forth from the curtains, and begins his long slow stride to the ring. A hush seems to go forward in the arena, for there are none who wish to anger this man with the hypnotic stare. The black metal mask covers his face, but does not cover the horrific black eyes with their red flecks dancing in glee at the chance to regain the World Heavyweight Championship. Once he was alone in that prestige, the only champion ever. Then he was alone again, the only two-time champion. Now he intends to regain the lone position at the top, as the only three-time World Champion ever in the Fictional Wrestling Federation. The referee is at the ring ropes even before Death arrives, parting them for his entry. It brings a smile to Death's face beneath the mask. The fools are learning. He moves to his assigned corner, and awaits the arrival of his opponent. The music shifts to "Exodus," and the reaction is mixed. There are those wrestling fans, not restricted merely to the hardcores, who cheer for this man despite his tendency to break the rules. There are the marks who blindly boo him. Either way, the man with the blue eyes and short-cropped brown hair seems to enjoy the attention. The Grinch resumes his intro. "AND HIS OPPONENT, FROM SEATTLE, WASHINGTON. HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY- EIGHT POUNDS, AND FOR THE SECOND TIME, HE IS THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION... JOHN `FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!!" Lawless comes out with the "Wrestler Be Cool" stick firmly in hand, the tool that has brought him so far already in the FWF. It has brought him one of his three United States Championships and both of his World Championships, and the historians do not fail to point it out to those who idolize him as their pet candidate for the greatest World Champion of all time. Still, the fact that he holds the record for the most U.S. Championships and singles championships in the FWF cannot be disputed. He smiles at his opponent in the ring, almost laughing at the Doctor. He hurls his robe from his shoulders, and runs headlong into the ring with the stick in hand and the belt around his waist. He runs up to the ring and vaults the ropes, coming into the ring with a bouncing hop as he lands. He walks over towards Death with the stick raised, and Death calmly but surely steps between the ropes and out of the ring. Lawless seems to have won the first round of the psyche-out battle, as he raises the stick in the air to a fair mix of cheers and boos. Pointing it out at Lawless, he says something the camera microphones do not pick up, and drops it in his corner just before he removes the World Championship belt. The Doctor has removed his metal headgear and his cloak, leaving him with his boots and his loincloth-covered black tights, with gold fringe coming from the loincloth and a white skull emblazoned on either side of it. He returns to the ring as the official holds the title belt high, and looks up at it longingly. It has been some time since he was power bombed to lose that title to Titan Morgan, and he itches at his waist suggestively. He does not bother to listen to Lawless' sarcastic reply, choosing to use his time to hurl his weight against the ropes, testing their strength and bounce, eliminating that particular variable for this match. The bell sounds, and Lawless moves towards Death, who meets him in a collar- and-elbow tie. Swiftly, Lawless grabs onto Death and throws his weight backwards, putting the feet up into Death's gut as he pulls him down and flips him over. Death lands on his back in the corner, and Lawless throws his arms out and shouts "Flawless!" derisively at him. Death seems to nod slightly, acknowledging the move and considering that Lawless seems to want a quickness game. A second collar-and-elbow tie is quickly broken up by the Doctor's nails in Lawless' eyes. Lawless backs away, only to be struck on the jaw by a stiff jab, the fist rotating on impact. He staggers into the corner, and is dealt with one of the Doctor's infamous knife edge chops. He shakes his head to clear the daze, only to be struck again, heaving his legs up in the air with the impact. Lawless responds with a left jab, stunning the Doctor with the quick strike. A second jab, and Lawless lunges out of the corner to seize the Doctor in a headlock. Death slides along the ropes and shoves Lawless away to the opposite side of the ring. Lawless floors him with a shoulder block on the first rebound. Death rolls over as Lawless goes to the ropes again. Lawless greets him on the mat with an elbow to the back of the head! Death shrieks, rolling over onto his belly with his hands clutching his skull, and Lawless drops a second elbow across his chest! Death grunts as the wind is knocked out of him, coming back to his senses to see Lawless executing a spinning toehold on his left leg. Not wanting any part of where Lawless could go with this, Death takes his right leg and shoves Lawless clear, rolling his way out to the floor the moment he is loose. Lawless points out at Death contemptuously, goading him on about the apparent disadvantage he is in. Death stands there and returns the words, only to find them answered with a cross body block vaulting over the top rope! Death crashes back against the railing with Lawless draped across his chest! Lawless shifts his weight upwards on Death's body, coming out right in the crowd. He seizes Death by the head, bending his neck and back over the railing before striking with an elbow across the Adam's apple! Death staggers away, coughing, as Lawless asks for and receives a chair from one of the spectators. Before the official can stop him, Lawless jumps onto a second spectator's chair, and vaults over the railing from there with the chair swinging down to crack right on Death's pate! The ref immediately imposes himself between the champion and his challenger, demanding that this at least end with some sort of clean result despite the no-DQ rule. Lawless shouts at him, "You're out of your league this time, punk!" but does drop the chair as he comes back at Death. Lawless picks Death up and drapes his already-bleeding form up against the railing. No sooner has he done this, than he runs back into the ring and sprints across to come back. He grasps the top rope as he vaults over, turning his body inverted before releasing the top rope to come down in a 180-spin into a Lou Thesz press . . . Death drops at the last second, and Lawless impacts sternum to steel on the fencing! Death still gets some feet draped on him from his opponent as Lawless lands, but the hurt from that is still nothing compared to what Lawless received from the missed move. Death shakes his head, and grabs Lawless away from the railing, lacerated chest and all, and rolls him back into the ring. Death brings Lawless back to the feet in the corner, and deals another chop to the half-shredded chest of his opponent. Lawless' feet heave upwards from the force of the blow, before he slumps down in the corner. Death strikes with a closed fist once more, before whipping Lawless out to the opposite corner. Lawless strikes the opposite turnbuckles so hard that he bounces back out into the center of the ring, only to levelled by a roundhouse Russian sickle from his enemy. Death stands with one foot on Lawless' cut chest, and the official counts the shoulders. One! Two . . . Lawless barely manages to get a shoulder up, close enough that both Death and the crowd mistakenly believe that he has won it already. Death sees the referee raise two fingers, though, and shakes his head in disbelief. Lawless slowly gets to his feet, but Death allows him no respite. He seizes Lawless in a headlock and hurls him over the shoulders to the mat. Lawless snakes his legs over Death's head and pulls him out into a head scissors. Death spins on the mat, getting his feet underneath himself, and grabs both legs in his arms as he pulls his head free. Death crosses the legs, sets them on one knee while setting a leg under his arm, and begins to twist at Lawless' body. Lawless' eyes widen, and quickly he pivots the top half of his body so that it is flat on the mat again. A mad struggle of Death twisting and Lawless pivoting to undo the twisting ensues, ending with Lawless getting a strong hold on the bottom ropes, and the referee shrugging, knowing that it's a no-disqualification bout, so counting isn't going to make a difference. Lawless realizes this too late, and starts to squirm with his legs while keeping his hold on the bottom rope. Eventually, Death gives up on this idea and backs away, allowing Lawless back to his feet. Sort of. Death seizes Lawless in a headlock against the ropes, only to be shoved away. Lawless is blasted to his back with a hard shoulder on the rebound. Death runs to the ropes this time, and Lawless rolls almost under his very feet to prevent Death from being able to do anything with the momentum off the ropes but to jump over. Death does, and Lawless comes back to his feet. He catches Death in a hip toss off the bounce this time, holding onto the wrist as Death hits the mat. Lawless ducks a little and coils a leg over the wrist, imprisoning Death's arm in a short-arm scissors! Death shrieks in agony, realizing that Lawless has a deadly arsenal of arm submission holds, right up to the dreaded cross-face chicken wing that won him his third U.S. Championship, and looks around to try and find a way to get free from this. He remembers from Lawless' futility a moment earlier that the ropes will do him no good, and thus he must get out of this the hard way. Lawless gives him another wrench, and a reddish haze creeps in on his vision with the pain. Summoning up his will, he shuts out the pain so that he can think. Death shifts his weight, gradually getting his feet underneath himself. He grabs Lawless underneath the legs, and pulling with the trapped arm while lifting at the legs, he lifts Lawless off the mat and onto his shoulder! Lawless' eyes go wide, knowing that there's only one way down . . . and THUD he goes onto his back when Death hurls him loose. Death does not follow up on it, strangely, and instead reserves his time to go to the ropes to loosen up the pain in his arm. Lawless regains his breath in a few moments, and Death realizes that he is not going to get anywhere dinking around on the ropes. He tries to grab Lawless in another headlock, only to be twisted around into an armbar behind himself! Death shrieks again, but is not hurting enough that he forgets to keep his other arm raised and out of Lawless' reach, but in a place that he can keep Lawless from reaching around front to lock in a full chicken wing with this hold. Lawless shifts a little to Death's left, trying to do just that, and Death takes advantage of the opening, ducking under the armbar and reversing it into one of his own! Lawless spins a little as he gets caught, leaving Death off to one side instead of behind him. He slowly goes to the ropes, and Death backs up with him and sends him across the ring in another Irish whip. Lawless ducks under a hard clothesline and goes to the ropes on the opposite side. Death swings another sickle at him, stopping to the ropes to see Lawless coming back at him. He starts to try a third, only to have Lawless leap off the ground and plant a flying drop kick flush on the jaw, catapulting him over the top rope to the floor! Lawless watches as Death slumps against the railing outside, and vaults the top rope again. Death ducks, but this time Lawless is only jumping out to stand on the bottom rope with his back to his enemy, looking over his shoulder. Death doesn't hear a crash, and stands up to look back. Lawless chooses his moment perfectly, and the moonsault off the bottom rope catches Death as he's looking to see. Death rolls over onto his stomach as Lawless regathers himself, trying to slither his way back into the ring. He is not liking how this is going at all, and rolls underneath the ring to regroup. Lawless looks around, and immediately sees from numerous helpful pointed fingers that Death has gone under. Lawless slides under the apron to follow. Death comes out the opposite side, and numerous fans note that a strip of leather from beneath his mask is hanging loose underneath the left side of his chin. A glint of metal is seen in his closed right fist. Lawless comes out, unsuspecting, and is immediately dropped heavily by the loaded fist. Death mounts Lawless, pinning him to the floor, and pounds on him hard with the weight in his hand, opening cuts on his head to match the one already bleeding on his chest. Finally, he rolls the weight underneath the ring and brings Lawless back to his feet. Rolling the champion into the ring, he finds the nearest camera lens and unleashes a blood-curdling war cry directly into it. Death re-enters the ring and draws Lawless to his feet, looping one arm over his own head as he wraps his arm around Lawless' waist. The champion is lifted by the near leg, and set horizontally on Death's shoulder. Death slowly takes the feet from beneath the weight, and Lawless is left falling on an uninterrupted seven-foot trek to the ground. Death rises to the ropes, and comes off with the knee drop upside Lawless' skull, rolling in a sideways somersault and to his feet one one graceful movement. Lawless contorts as if overcome with muscle spasms, clutching his bloodied head in pain. He makes it to the ropes, defeating any possible pin attempt, but Death is not done with him yet. Death draws him to his feet in the corner, dealing another chop to his chest. He follows it up with a knee strike just above Lawless' right knee, bending it back against the grain a moment. Lawless' knee buckles, and he slumps out of the corner, rolling out of the ring at his first opportunity. Death mounts the top turnbuckles, leaping out with a flying forearm that impacts on Lawless' head on the ground! The fans shriek as he draws Lawless up against the railing and smacks him with a reverse knife-edge right in front of them. He rolls the smaller man back into the ring, where Lawless comes to his knees and puts his hands up, begging for mercy. The official holds the Doctor off, only to be roughly shoved aside. Death knows better than to expect a submission, and he can't be disqualified for shoving the ref anyhow. He stomps away viciously at the knee of Lawless, until finally the World Champion rises to his feet to hop away from him. Death follows him like a wolf sensing a wounded elk. Lawless whirls and strikes with a jab to his jaw, encouraging him to keep his distance. Death staggers, and Lawless hits him again and again, his fist striking like a venomous viper against the mandible of his larger adversary. Finally, Death's mind realizes that staying on his feet isn't being a help while he's being punched, and he turns away to stagger a few feet before he flops on his side. Lawless limps over, not wanting to allow Death to get even a moment's rest with which to regather himself. He stomps away at Death, trying not to put too much weight on his knee for too long, though it's feeling better after he's walked on it a little. He grabs onto the arm of his opponent, drawing him to his feet. The smaller man wrenches upwards, lifting Death off the mat and hurling him over the head to the canvas again! Death's eyes widen in shock and agony as his shoulder twists out of joint! The official is there in an instant, getting Lawless out of the way while he gets a better look at the injured limb. Death screams as he slowly slides out of the ring, every movement of the seperated shoulder pained in the extreme. The more squeamish fans make a desperate rush to the bathroom . . . "Insufficient postage, return to sender." He flops out on the floor, leaving his arm draped under the ring apron, and Lawless follows, not intending to show any mercy. The ref gets in Lawless' way, preventing him from aggravating this. He asks Death if he wants to give it up, and the Doctor refuses, squinting in agony as he clutches at his dislocated shoulder. Lawless starts to close in again, but the official is fairly sure that he should stop this. He asks Death again as he gets to his feet if he's sure he wants to go on. Death makes his answer with a loaded fist to Lawless' jaw. The official watches in disbelief as the "dislocated shoulder" straightens itself out, the ancient trick learned in a Tibetan monastery having served its purpose. Death loses his grip on the weight, but it is a moot point now, as he has the advantage once again. He sends Lawless back into the ring, then follows shortly. Lawless is powerless to prevent Death from draping his right leg over the bottom rope, dropping his entire weight across the knee. The official screams at Death in choleric rage, threatening to disqualify him then and there for his treachery. Death turns a deaf ear. His mind is thinking that, once he has the World Championship match fully in hand, he should ask for all of his matches to be no-DQ defenses . . . being able to ignore the ref with impunity is sweet. Death pulls Lawless out into the center of the ring, with his own back to the ropes, and spins then spins around the right leg with a step-over toehold. He bends Lawless' right knee against his own, grabs the left leg of the smaller man, sets the right ankle above the left knee, then drops onto his back, setting his own right leg above his foe's right ankle. Lawless is brought back to reality with the agony a figure four leg lock can inflict, and his body contorts in the center of the ring with a reminder of his foe's mastery of this hold. Lawless screams in pain as Death grabs onto the ropes in full view of the official, the Doctor knowing full well that he can do whatever he wants so long as he does not get pinned or give up. Lawless wilts to the mat, the sheer agony of the magnified weight on his legs while they're in a figure four almost too much to bear. The official counts the shoulders, and only at the barest margin of three does Lawless get one up. Still he screams, knowing that the end is near if he can't get out of this. And he's not going to get out of this while Death is grasping the ropes with impunity. Lawless tries to slide backwards, and Death holds onto the ropes with a death grip. The official watches it for a while, and then some semblance of desire for fair play makes him forget that this is a no-DQ match. He walks over and kicks Death's hands loose of the ropes. Death hurtles out a short distance into the ring with the sudden tension released, slipping his figure four completely out of whack. Lawless takes advantage of it instantly, rolling over onto his stomach with the hold. Now it is Death who is asked for a submission, and who screams wordless defiance. Finally, the Doctor gets his leg loose from the reversed hold, and rolls back onto his back. He uncoils his other leg immediately and gets back to his feet. No sooner has he done this than he is on the case of the official who got him into this predicament. A huge fist knots in the collar of the official, a second prepared to hit the man so hard that his brother will feel it. The official pleads for mercy, evoking a rather large amount of laughter from the crowd to see the roles reversed, and apologizes for his interference that proved so costly. He acknowledges that he stepped over his bounds in this no-DQ match, and Death brusquely shoves him into the corner, turning back towards his opponent. A super thrust kick to the jaw floors him before he realizes that Lawless is back on his feet. Lawless sags against the ropes for a brief time to regain more of his bearings, before turning back to his opponent. Lifting Death up to his feet, he puts him in a front facelock, then looping an arm overhead. Death's mind registers the position, and he puts his legs back far behind him, not allowing Lawless' arms near them, preventing any Perfect Plex from being executed. Lawless scrabbles for purchase against the canvas, trying to get a better grip somewhere close to Death's center of gravity. Finally, he lifts by the waist on the larger man, hefting him vertical and onto his back. Death comes up to a sitting-up position by reflex, not wanting his shoulders on the mat. Lawless grasps him by the head, flipping over his head and landing in front of the larger man, stretching his spine out in a most painful manner. Death shrieks and comes up to his knees, unconsciously providing a perfect means for a repeat, and Lawless does so in reverse, bending the larger man back double. Death clutches at his back as he rolls towards the corner, still on his knees, only this time with his back to the turnbuckles to keep Lawless from doing this again. Lawless decks Death upside the jaw, before whipping him out to the opposite corner. No sooner has Death struck the turnbuckles than a crushing clothesline impacts on his chest from the follow-up. Death slumps back against the buckles, ripe for the pickings. Lawless deals a reverse knife-edge to the larger man now, knocking the wind out of him. Death shakes the cobwebs loose, seizing Lawless by the hair and turning him into the corner for one of his own, striking flush on the lacerations already open on Lawless' chest. Lawless doubles over, stinging from the blow on the tender spot. Death grabs him in an armbar from overhead, grabbing the other arm quickly to get a butterfly hold, which he uses to heft Lawless up and over in a suplex! Death goes for the cover. One! Two . . . . . . Lawless kicks out, and Death rounds on the official to get a faster count. The official shrugs, insisting that Lawless got out well within time. Death turns back to Lawless, prepared to duck this time if another kick is coming at him. No such appears, for Lawless is on his belly, sliding towards the corner. Death catches him there, turning him around. His eyes widen as he sees inch- thick wooden rod coming towards him at a rapid speed, and goes rigid as he falls over backwards after the "Wrestler Be Cool" stick impacts. Lawless darts over to cover the limp form, counting with his fingers along with the ref . . . One! Two . . . Death's shoulder jerks upward an inch or two just as the official's hand hits the mat a third time. The fans begin to cheer, thinking that Lawless has won, but Lawless is skeptical. Sure enough, as much as the ref would love to give this to the champion at this point, he can't do it. He holds up two fingers, denying the celebration. Lawless brings Death back to his feet, whipping him into the ropes. He hefts Death up in a waistlock on the bounce, moving one hand down to a leg as he snaps him over into a spine buster! He stands, nodding, as the fans chant madly. Then he throws his arms wide, and gives the clarion call . . . "Now you're gonna see . . . a Perfect Plex!" The fans go into a buzz at the sound of the words, and Lawless smiles in anticipation of the long-awaited end. He draws Death to his feet, taking him into the familiar suplex position, grasping the leg, setting the feet to hurl him over . . . Death rolls Lawless into an inside cradle! One! Two . . . Lawless reverses it! One! Two . . . Death surges loose, running at Lawless, hope and the element of surprise spurring him on, and wraps Lawless in a second small package! One! Two . . . Lawless gets loose, and gets to his feet faster than the Doctor. He catches Death in a leg tangle, and the two of them circle around each other in a race to catch the other in an abdominal stretch first! Lawless makes it around, still faster at this point than his opponent! Lawless throws his weight back, pulling Death back onto his shoulders! One! Two . . . Death manages to roll sideways a bit, getting away from the hold. Lawless releases it, tossing Death up onto his shoulders and head to ensure that he, himself, gets to his feet first again. Death stays in a crouch as Lawless nears, rolling him into a third small package when he is within reach! One! Two . . . Lawless gets loose once more, and once more he is the first back to his feet. Death gets to his own feet, turns at Lawless, and receives a second super kick to the jaw for his troubles. The fans cheer as Lawless again goes for the cover . . . One! Two . . . Death gets a shoulder up, but this time is in no shape to get back to his feet. Lawless is not discouraged, for he has control of the match once more. He stands, turning to the fans, shouting "Flawless!" to their delight. He turns back to Death, finding the larger man in his corner, his hands up, the long word "NOOOOO!!!" rolling from his lips. Lawless walks up to him, nodding, the equally long word "YEEESSSS!!" coming out in response. Death sticks a thumb in his eye for it. Lawless bunches over, as Death turns him back into the corner, dealing a hard chop to the lacerated chest once more. A punch to the ear, and Lawless is wobbly. A second chop has him slumped against the ropes. At that point, he whips Lawless out to the opposite corner. Death follows to attack after the hit . . . Lawless dives out of the way! Death, trying to correct for the impact, knows he can't stop in time and ducks to grab the middle rope, flipping up over the turnbuckles and being left in a seated position on the top rope. Lawless sees him up there, and knows this is not an opportunity he can afford to miss. The champion mounts the second turnbuckle, grasping Death in a belly-to-back waistlock, and superplexes him out of the corner, whipping him back onto his shoulders and head from thirteen feet up in the air! Death's body flops back with the momentum still further, and he rolls over on his head in a back somersault before flopping motionlessly onto his belly. Lawless moves over to make the cover. One! Two . . . Death's shoulder again inches just that much upward, forcing a break in the count. Lawless curses in frustration. What does it take to bring this man past his breaking point? The stick didn't do it, the superplex didn't do it. Lawless shakes his head as he rises to his feet, drawing Death up after him. He puts in a two-handed waistlock from the side, ducking under Death's arm, and lifts the larger man up in a snapping back suplex from this position. Death goes utterly nowhere from where he lands, his body perpendicular to the corner. Lawless points to the corner, and climbs outside the ring. He mounts the top rope, gets a wise look on his face, and sticks a pair of index fingers high up in the air. He launches off the turnbuckles with a perfect tope elbow landing on Death's chest! He covers the larger man for the pin! One! Two . . . *Still* Death gets a shoulder up just an inch or two. Lawless fairly screams in frustration at this point, before going over to the same turnbuckle he just left. He mounts it facing outward this time, puts the fingers up in the air, and launches himself backwards. He twists sideways as he flies through the air, twisting around until he lands with a flipping leg drop . . . . . . on bare canvas! Lawless crumples to the mat after the missed stroke, his hands going to his head, what is left of his unstunned consciousness cursing himself for going to the well once too often. Death stays on his belly where he rolled, closer to the launch point than he had been when Lawless jumped, for a short while, until he hears the official counting. The official gets to three in his double-count, before seeming to think better of it, since there's no count out anyhow. By this time, however, Death has made it a little closer to the land of the living. He walks over to Lawless, drawing the smaller man back to his feet. Catching him in another front facelock, he waves his finger in the air suggestively. Realizing that he's in for a DDT if he doesn't move, Lawless sets his feet beneath himself and lifts on the arm over his head. Death flips up and over onto his back, arching upward after the impact. Lawless slumps against the ropes, half-exhausted. How long has this match been going, anyhow? he wonders. As if in answer, the timekeeper announces the thirty-five minute mark. Lawless seems taken aback at how long this has taken, but it's not like he expected this to be short and sweet, not against an opponent that even he must admit is of the highest caliber in the world. Lawless draws Death to his feet, clutching him in a headlock. Death shoves him loose to the ropes. Lawless blams him down to the canvas with a shoulder block, but is slow getting going again when he again goes to the ropes. Death misses with a clothesline on the first pass, Lawless having ducked beneath it. Death turns to run at Lawless with a second lariat attempt, but the smaller man against ducks it. Death comes off the ropes towards Lawless, and is completely unprepared for the flying drop kick that sends him careening half sideways. The official finds himself trapped in the rapidly-dwindling space betwixt Death and the turnbuckles, and gets sandwiched. Death bounces out of the corner again with the force of the impact, and Lawless snares him in a hammerlock coming out, snaking the other arm around the face in the cross-face chicken wing! Lawless trips the half-stunned Doctor onto the canvas, wrapping the legs around his body and trapping his one free arm against the canvas! Death shrieks in agony, the pain almost seeming to tear his arm from its socket. But still his mind and will assert themselves, seeking a way out of this desperate situation. This way, Lawless needs no referee: he can simply keep this until whenever the official decides to show up to hear the submission. Lawless gives another wrench, taunting the larger man to this effect. Death desperately tries to shift his weight, to get his feet beneath himself and somehow get back to his feet. Lawless' body scissors makes it virtually impossible to move, and he is left to rest again with the dull pain in his shoulder joint keeping him awake. He again moves, trying to bend his knees and roll onto them, but Lawless wrenches back on the arm for leverage, keeping the movements small. However, Death spreads on leg back, getting it behind his opponent, and manages somehow to twist it so that he can get onto that knee behind Lawless. He relaxes in the hold for a moment, attempting to lull Lawless into a sense of security. Then he grits his teeth and wrenches at his own arm, almost pulling it from its socket! However, the move worked, for he is now on his knees instead of on his side. Once this is accomplished, he gets first one foot, then the other, underneath him, and he is back on his feet. He still is stuck in the chicken wing, though, and he tries to duck down to get a view of the ring around him. He slowly forces, with his leg strength and height advantage giving him the leverage, his way over to the ropes, and ducks beneath them, making it virtually impossible for Lawless to keep the hold. Lawless tries, but eventually his hands become unlocked. No sooner does Death detect this, than he grasps Lawless by the wrist, forcing the smaller man's arm behind his back in a hammerlock! Lawless shrieks, fearing a chicken wing in return, but instead Death whips him across the ring, throwing him off-guard. It is in this manner that Death catches him in a belly-to-belly, hurling him up in the air, taking a step into the flip over, and smashes him down to the mat in the spineplex. Death throws his arms out wide and laughs in a chilling howl, sending shivers through the spines of those who hear it. He then hovers over Lawless like a banshee, beckoning with his outstretched fingers for his smaller foe to rise. When Lawless finally does so, nearly thirty seconds later, he is quickly snared in the modified cobra clutch known as the Vulcan Death Grip. Death expertly sets Lawless' right wrist in Death's own left hand, looping his arm over the smaller man's head as he snakes his own right hand into the hole created beneath Lawless' right arm. Death sets his right hand behind the ear of the smaller man, and his left arm expertly against the jugular. He pumps in regular motions, eliciting pain as the nerves behind the ear are bombarded with precise pressure, and blackness starts to creep into Lawless' vision. Lawless looks around in panic, trying to get Death's hand loose from his ear, but he is unable to turn his head much while in the hold. He does see, however, that all three corners within his field of view do not have an unconscious referee in them. Deducing the obvious, he shifts his weight backward, shoving Death back with him. Death anticipates it, and sets his foot back to compensate. He knows this hold better than any man alive. However, he does not know how to keep oneself stable in it with a writhing referee underneath the foundation foot. His foot comes down on the arm of the official, who reflexively pulls away, tripping the challenger up. He is forced to put his foot way too far forward, and Lawless slips his own foot in behind it. That accomplished, he falls back against Death, leading to a rather unpleasant entanglement in the ropes. Death goes through them, flopping back and landing on the back of his head outside the ring. Lawless winds up slumped against the middle one, his ability to move quite taxed in doing something so simple as rubbing his neck, which is now feeling very badly after the nerve hold he was just put in. Lawless slowly regains more feeling in his body, the feeling of all of his limbs going to sleep slowly giving way to the familiar feeling as if ants were crawling all over his skin, signifying that he's getting movement back in them. He slumps away from the ropes, coming to his hands and knees, then manages to get up to a wobbly stance. Getting his bearings, he sees Death still outside the ring, not having moved a muscle since landing on his head out there. Lawless reaches down and grasps him by the leg, turning him onto his side. Reaching over the bottom ropes, he grabs by the mask, hauling him up to his knees. Half-consciously accepting the helping hand, Death gets back into the ring without fully knowing where he is. Lawless staggers back to his feet, trying to drag Death up into a front facelock. The fans think they see his plan, and cheer him on as he loops Death's left arm over his head. Now getting a better stance, Lawless grasps the Doctor's left leg, leaving him hopping to get balance. Only now does Doc realize what is going on, but it is too late. Lawless hefts him over, in the cradle suplex/brain buster affectionately called the Perfect Plex. Death lands solidly on the back of his head, his neck bending forward after the impact to leave his shoulders resting on the mat, his leg clutched to fold his body in half in the bridge-cradle. Death is motionless as the fans count to twelve before he manages to get some semblance of awareness again. He hears the sound of a hand hitting the canvas. Groggily, he tries to get some movement into his limbs. "I don' wanna move," complain his legs, his arms, heck, his whole body. "Move!" "Please, no . . ." "MOVE!!" The hand hits the canvas the second time. "MOVE, YOU DAMNED LIMBS!" the message screams down Doctor Death's nerves to his muscles. The hand lifts a third time . . . . . . Lawless' back collapses in the bridge, and Death manages to roll slightly to get one shoulder off the canvas. Lawless gets to his feet almost immediately, rubbing his back from having had to hold the bridge for so long after having already been hit. Blood loss in addition to the nerve hold were rendering him almost unconscious by this point, leaving him going completely on instinct. Death twitched a little, fighting to exert his will sufficiently to get back to his feet after the head blow from the brain buster portion of the Perfect Plex. Lawless places the flat of his foot against the side of Death's head, and shoves him onto his back on the canvas. There is no real effect here, just simple humiliation. Lawless kicks him again, and Death clutches at Lawless' right foot to try and stop the onslaught, only half-conscious. Lawless looks out at the fans, who jeer at the pathetic efforts of the not-so-good Doctor. Lawless swats down at Death, who crawls to his knees before the champion. Death grabs the other knee, and rises to a squat, throwing his weight into a shoulder to the gut. Lawless gives way to the leverage, and falls onto his back. Death steps over Lawless' right knee with his own right leg, setting his left knee just underneath and below Lawless' right. A hop upward, and Death drops onto both knees with Lawless' trapped between. Crack! Ouch. Lawless curls into a fetal ball, clutching the right knee in pain. The fans groan in sympathy, knowing exactly what Death intends to do here. Death nods at some of them holding up four fingers, and pulls Lawless' leg loose from his body. He drags it over to the ropes, sets the foot over the bottom one, turning it sideways so that the knee will twist that way when it is hit. He holds it there with the flat of his own foot, leaping upward on one foot. Lawless' own foot held with his knee facing sideways, the right leg has no choice but to buckle in that direction when Death's tailbone hits it. Lawless surges backwards away from the ropes, again curling into a ball. Death again pulls it loose, stepping on the left ankle with his own right foot, keeping both legs outstretched while he holds the right one up in the air. Screaming victory out at the crowd, he goes into the spinning toehold. Lawless rolls him up into a small package! One! Two . . . . . . Death springs loose from the hold, fury twisting his features, and he deals a dropping knee to the side of his enemy's. Grabbing by the bloodied hair, shaking the blood loose from his own head from the chair shot early in the match, he brings Lawless up for a back suplex position, only this time he grasps the ankle up and lifts by that. All of Lawless' 268 pounds is lifted up by the knee, and Death holds him up there, allowing all of his weight to fall upon bending that knee. As he's not holding it just nicely to bend normally, it bends partly sideways. At last, Death drops it, crashing it down across his own knee in an atomic shin drop. But this is not enough. Not relinquishing his hold, Death again lifts, allowing the pain to build before he drops Lawless onto his back. Again taking the right foot in his hands, Death nods knowingly, and again spins around the leg. This time, he gets the left foot in his hands, and for the second time John "Flawless" Lawless is forced to bear the pain of Doctor Death's figure four. Lawless' face goes white, and the official, now a bit more recovered whilst Death had been spending time wearing Lawless down for this, asks for a submission. Lawless, only half conscious from pain and blood loss, shakes his head, and the official gets a nice splattered red stain to show the kids back own on his white and black shirt. The ref looks down at his shirt in distaste, while Death twists and bucks to put as much pain into Lawless' knees as he can. Death puts his hands on the mat, rearing up onto his hands and feet. Lawless wilts to the mat as the torture is multiplied many times over, and the official counts. Lawless gets up at just one, gritting his teeth and trying to hit hit own right foot where it is being held, trying to knock his right ankle below his left knee so that he can bend it, perhaps roll this over again. He knows that getting to the ropes will do him no good, unless he rolls out of the ring entirely. Wait, there's an idea, Lawless realizes. He looks up over his head, seeing the ropes a fair distance away. Lawless hopes that Death's groggy enough to let him slide. Slide he does, and Death twists, trying to keep Lawless from moving, but ultimately he has to either slide with Lawless or let Lawless get loose. Lawless slips first his head, then most of his upper body, underneath the bottom rope, and throws his weight back full with gravity, putting his hands over his head to keep his noggin from hitting first like Doc's did not long ago. Gravity accomplishes what the official is not allowed to, and the figure four is broken. However, Lawless is not exactly in any condition to walk back into the ring. On the other hand, he doesn't exactly have a choice, either, since the match cannot end except by pinfall or submission. There isn't even a time limit. Death reaches down and helps Lawless back to his feet, lifting him up to be suplexed into the ring the hard way. Lawless is lifted up into the air, but grabs onto the top rope before going all the way over. Death falls back as he had expected, but Lawless lands atop him instead of behind him! Lawless grabs the leg to try the cradle! One! Two . . . . . . Death manages to kick upward enough to get a shoulder off the canvas, if only for a brief moment before the ref counts again. This time, though, the ref only reaches one before Doc kicks completely loose. Lawless grabs the ropes and pulls himself up to his feet, utterly nothing left in his legs by this time. Death sees the opportunity, and clips him underneath the right knee. Lawless' leg buckles, and again he falls into the ring. The timekeeper announces that the fifty-two-minute mark has been passed. Death smiles, calling back out, "It's not gonna make it to fifty-three!" As if to emphasize the point, he picks up Lawless' right foot and perpares to put a third figure four on his foe. Lawless rolls him up in another inside cradle! One! Two . . . Death surges out of the hold almost simultaneously with the referee's hand hitting the canvas a third time. Lawless wilts to the canvas, hoping beyond hope that the hand came down in time. Death goes after Lawless' knee again, but this time the referee steps in his way. Death's eyes widen, fear entering his shrivelled heart at what this means. The referee calls for the bell. The Grinch grabs the microphone and makes it official. "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT . . . AND STILL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION . . . JOHN `FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!!!!" The belt is handed back to Lawless, who smiles as he shouts over at Doc, "You were right, it didn't make it to fifty-three!" This puts Death into outright conniptions, shouting at the official about a fast count. The ref firmly denies it, and stands by his decision. He then warns Doctor Death that he has ten seconds to leave the ring area before some solid fines are levied on him for the blatant abuse he has pulled during the match. Death snarls in wordless fury, before leaving the ring and walking back to the dressing room in disgust. Lawless is helped back to his feet, his right leg still utterly incapable of supporting any weight, and is given help to make it back after his challenger. Lawless slings the belt over his left shoulder, and smiles. The blood on his chest, from his head, the pain in his leg, it all doesn't mattered. He has taken the challenge of Doctor Death . . . and answered it. Nothing more means anything to him now. The questioning is done. He has been judged by fate to be worthy of the World Heavyweight Championship, having successfully defended it for the first time in his career. For all his talk of record numbers of titles, this one meant it all to him. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- And so the long, hard-fought match is submitted, saying farewell to rec.sport.pro-wrestling on behalf of the FWF, and saying thank you to those readers who have followed my writing for this fantasy league over these past three and a half years. I wanted the going out, and quite possible the going in to the new group, to be something special. I have long felt that Doc vs. John Girder, from years ago, was the best match I had ever written, and I hoped to top that one. Those of you that have read both of them, feel free to comment on what you thought. For those that haven't, drop me a line and I'll send you the other one for comparison. This is something of a new day, much like the comic strip "For Better or Worse" in shifting out a certain longtime canine character. Not only does it cast some sort of possible end to the dominance of Doctor Death, but also the beginning of such for "Flawless" Lawless. Also, it may well be the first FWF post on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy. So farewell to the old, and hello to the new! The FWF intends to make the transfer in style!
