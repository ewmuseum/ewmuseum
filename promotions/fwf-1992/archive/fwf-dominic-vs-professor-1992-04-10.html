From [detail withheld]@TETHYS.UCC.UMASS.EDU Fri Apr 10 07:44 PDT 1992 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tonight's FWF grappling comes to you live (well, almost) from the Civic Center in downtown Springfield, Massachusetts, and involves a man who has of late come into his own as a widely-despised heel, that man being "Dark Star" Dominic Walters. Recently, Walters level of success in FWF bouts has not been too high, but no one should make the mistake of thinking that indicates he is an inferior competitor. Such a mistake would almost certainly prove costly. The rugged notes of Iron Maiden's "Hallowed Be Thy Name" begin to pour from the Civic Center's public address system, as the hated British villain begins his trek to the ring. All the loyal FWF followers throughout the Center are aware of what the Dark Star has done and tried to do to the newly crowned United States champion, "Crimson" Joe Reed. Although his attempts to change the outcome of that particular title bout were not successful, his intentions were not appreciated by the vast majority of this capacity crowd. Cult hero Ernie Evans, the ringside announcer, enters the ring to introduce the man preparing to enter the ring. "GOOD EVENING LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WELCOME TO BEAUTIFUL SPRINGFIELD, MASSACHUSETTS! THE FOLLWING BOUT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL WITH A FIFTEEN MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST, ABOUT TO ENTER THE RING, FROM HULL, ENGLAND, AT TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY ONE POUNDS, 'DARK STAR' DOMINIC WALTERS!!" The man in black steps through the ropes, and after tossing his leather vest and chaps to the ringside attendant, drops to one knee and waving his hands, implores the fans to fling all their cheers at him. Cheers are few and far between, but booes, jeers and crumpled up FWF programs shower the ring without hesitation. Walters rises, laughs loudly, and bows to all four sides of the crowd in mock appreciation. As the fans begin to settle down, Evans continues his announcements. "AND HIS OPPONENT, FROM NEARBY NASHUA, NEW HAMPSHIRE AT TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY FOUR POUNDS, MAKING HIS RETURN TO PRO WRESTLING AND HIS DEBUT IN THE FWF, PLEASE PUT YOUR HANDS TOGETHER FOR A VERY WARM WELCOME FOR 'THE PROFESSOR', JOHN GOULDING!!!" The marks and hardcores both burst into spontaneous applause and cheering as the veteran from New Hampshire begins making his entrance for the first time in many years. Goulding has been out of wrestling for several years, busy running his (fictional) wrestling school in southern New Hampshire. Now, as the FWF makes its way to the east coast, Goulding contacted the FWF about wanting to wrestle for the FWF when they ventured into New England, and the result is the match that is about to be underway. The popular journeyman begins to jog towards the ring, smiling radiantly. He makes his way towards the squared circle, and circles the ring, high-fiving and shaking hands with all his fans at ringside. As he leaps into the ring, wearing beige tights and white boots, the Professor looks out at the crowd with both arms raised, and their return of praise is deafening. Walters looks on, disgusted, as he churns over strategies in his head. Suddenly, Goulding charges at Walters, but only to offer a handshake. A surprised Walters, not wanting to look like a good sportsman, simply turns his head away from Goulding's gaze. The Professor shrugs, and begins circling his opponent as the referee calls for the opening bell. As the two men circle the ring, sizing each other up, Walters rushes at the smaller Goulding, catching his in a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Before the heel can attept anything, Goulding locks the left arm of Walters in a tight armbar. Walters, displaying his tremendous scientific skill, cocks a fist and swings at Goulding's head. Goulding quickly ducks, and catches Walters across the face with a playful slap. Still grinning, Goulding backs off. The two lock up again, and this time, before Goulding can repeat the previous series, Walters catches him quickly with a knee below the belt. He is swift to follow up, grabbing, lifting and dropping Goulding in an inverted atomic drop. As Goulding stands wincing, still registering the pain, Walters rears back and catches the Professor flush across the cheek with a painful slap that echoes loudly thoughout all of the Center. As Goulding is still standing in the center of the ring, Walters decides to capitalize on his series of moves and hurtles towards the ropes, bouncing off only to be locked and dropped in a drop-toe hold, perfectly executed by one of the better scientific grapplers around. Goulding leaps from the knees to the neck, clutching his opponent in a side headlock. Using Goulding's tights against him, Walters uses his added leverage to roll the Professor into a pinning position, but recieves only two before the ref spots the illegal hold. Accepting the fact that he won't be able to get a pin, Walters sets his mind on getting out of this wear-down hold. He makes his way to one knee, and finally to his feet. He pulls Goulding into a corner, and whips him to the opposite one. As the local hero bounds out of the corner, Walters charges him with a clothesline. Goulding sees the move, and ducks in anticipation. He is a bit too early, however, as Walters has time to raise a knee by the time he reaches the corner, and catches Goulding in the head, knocking the veteran down hard. Charging the ropes, Walters levels the downed Goulding with a running fistdrop. As Goulding slowly makes his way to his feet, Walters scoops him up easily for a bodyslam, but the agile Professor slips over the top, and runs Walters into the ropes! As they come off the ropes, Goulding rolls up his foe in a backroll press, and bridges back for extra leverage! The ref drops to make the count. One! Two! Walters manages to power his way out of the pin, and out of sheer frustration, grabs Goulding by the hair and flings him between the ropes. The Professor hits the arena floor hard, and Walters follows him out. Again pulling his opponent up by the hair, the Dark Star drives him into the corner post, and nonchalantly throws him back into the ring. He then re-enters the ring himself. As Goulding slowly stumbles his way into a corner, Walters quickly follows him, grabbing him by the arm and whipping him from pillar to post, chasing him in with another clothesline, this time catching its mark. As Goulding staggers out of the corner, Walters climbs onto the middle turnbuckle, and leaps at the back of Goulding's head, catching him in a claw and driving him facefirst into the mat! Uncharacteristically, Walters continues to apply pressure to the hold, apparently seeking a submission. After applying the hold with added pressure for about ten seconds, he finally gets it. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE IS YOUR WINNER: 'DARK STAR' DOMINIC WALTERS!!!" As the referee attempts to raise Walters' hand, the Dark Star shoves him off. The referee out of the way, Walters pulls a bleeding Goulding off the mat and places the Professor's head between his knees. The crowd screams for someone to help Goulding, but no help is found as Walters mercilessly piledrives Goulding into the mat. Again the ref tries to get Walters off of his opponent, but this time he catches an elbow in the jaw for his efforts. By now, Goulding has made his way up to one knee and wipes the blood out of his eyes. Before he can make it to his feet on his own, Walters helps him up, running the length of the ring and tossing Goulding over the top rope. Walters, still livid at the Professor, follows the half-conscious Goulding out of the ring, and pulls him off the floor, into a front facelock. By now, the marks are screaming for someone to help the defenseless Goulding, but there is no help for Goulding as Walters drives his head into the cement in a spine- tingling DDT. Goulding, now unconscious, is tossed back into the ring once again by Walters, and now the Dark Star scales the turnbuckles, preparing to deliver the crushing blow. Suddenly, the fans burst out cheering, as the Latin Wildfire steams down the aisle, and Walters, who sees him coming, quickly climbs down to safety. As the Wildfire enters the ring, Walters rapidly exits. As Walters slowly makes his way down the ring aisle, assuming Wildfire has remained in the ring to tend to his fallen comrade, he is rudely proved wrong by a flying forearm to the back of the head. The Wildfire mounts the fallen Dark Star's shoulders, and connects with punch after punch to the Walters' forehead. As security slowly pulls the youngster off, the Brit swiftly makes flight, leaving the Wildfire and the paramedics to tend to the injured Professor, John Goulding. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Cheers, [name withheld] ([detail withheld]@tethys.ucc.umass.edu) Still on its way from my end of the FWF (whenver they can catch me and chain me to the keyboard again :-) . . .
