FWF: Match History (2nd Generation)

20 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And, to bring events fully up to date, I also post this article describing the matches of the FWF's second generation. The World and U.S. Champions coming out of the first are listed below, and from there are described the seven FWF matches written thus far in this issuing of its circulation.
