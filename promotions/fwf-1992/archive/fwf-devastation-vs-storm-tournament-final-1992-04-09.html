FWF: Devastation vs Storm (tournament final)

9 April 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Path: sparky!uunet!bonnie.concordia.ca!nstn.ns.ca!ac.dal.ca![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@ac.dal.ca Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Devastation vs Storm (tournament final) Message-ID: <1992Apr8.214208.4904@ac.dal.ca> Date: 9 Apr 92 00:42:07 GMT Organization: Dalhousie University, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Lines: 391 At long last, after several months of gruelling matches from various wrestlers in the Fictional Wrestling Federation, we are down to the match that will determine the most prestigious tag championship in the world of fictional wrestling, the FWF World Tag Team Championship. These four combatants have established over their three tournament matches against the other tag teams in the tournament exactly why they are deserving of these titles. The only thing standing in the way of each team is the other. Devastation, Derek Sanderson and Johnny Houlton, are undefeated in competition in the FWF thus far. Their most serious challenge was that of the Blade Runners, with whom they fought to a twenty minute draw. Storm, on the other hand, were disqualified in their match against the last place team in the tourney, the Blues Brothers. This was their only loss, and, true to form, Storm showed once again why it never pays to mess with them. At the present time, the Blues Brothers are sidelined from FWF action due to the severe concussion Boris sustained after this particular match. Thunder and Lightning were victorious in all other tag competition. The tournament standings have Devastation ahead of Storm by one point. Basically what this translates to is if the match ends in a draw, Devastation takes the titles. Due to the Round Robin nature of the tournament, countouts and disqualifications are equally as valid a means of winning a match, and consequently, the titles, as a pinfall or submission. Thus, if Storm were to get disqualified, they would forfeit the titles to Devastation. If Devastation were able to keep Thunder and Lightning tied up outside the ring and take a double countout, they would still be crowned champions. Similarly, Storm could win the titles by causing either Johnny or Derek to be counted out, say by knocking him out with a foreign object, something Storm wouldn't be above doing necessarily. The possible outcomes are many. Neither team has opted for an interview at this time. Therefore, without further ado, we will take things down to ringside! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- For this, the final match of the tag team tournament, find ourselves in the Halifax Metro Centre in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. The Metro Centre was sold out long ago in anticipation of this potentially explosive match between the top two tag teams in the FWF. Real Men say Devastation are going to win because they're undefeated. Real Fans say they don't care who wins: this is going to be a wrestling match to remember! Loonies say Devastation is really the Demolishers in different outfits and they're going to break both Lightning and Thunder's backs. Marks say Devastation is going to make Storm pay. The crowd's reaction only increases as they see the ring announcer enter the ring. The referee makes his way down the aisle carrying the coveted FWF tag belts under his arms. People are on their feet, craning their necks to try to get their first glimpse of the gold. As soon as the referee enters the ring, the lights go out. By now the fans realize what this means, and it comes as no surprise when they flash on and off again. A BOOM reverberates throughout the Metro Centre as the lights are slowly raised again. As William Tell's Overture, "Storm" is played over the P.A. system, the ring announcer starts, "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS MATCH IS THE FINAL MATCH OF THE FWF'S TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT AND IS FOR THE FWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP! IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL AND HAS A TWENTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT. MAKING THEIR WAY DOWN THE AISLE, WITH FOUR POINTS IN THE TOURNAMENT, IS THE SECOND-RANKED TEAM. WEIGHING IN AT A TOTAL COMBINED WEIGHT OF FIVE HUNDRED, SIXTY-THREE POUNDS, HERE IS AARON "LIGHTNING" MURPHY AND JERRY "THUNDER" MCGAGHNEY, COLLECTIVELY KNOWN AS STORRRRRRRM!!!!!" The black caped duo shows no emotion on their faces as they make their way to the ring. Their mission is clear in their minds: to do whatever it takes to emerge victorious tonight. Although they could probably go for half an hour or more in the ring against Devastation, the mandatory round robin time limit of twenty minutes is still in place. They will have to cut all stops in this match. Maiming Devastation isn't even on their minds tonight: that pleasure will come when they defend their titles. Tonight, they can't risk a disqualification. They learned that when facing the Blues Brothers last time, which caused them to be in the position of disadvantage they are now in. The referee parts the ropes to allow this fearsome duo to enter. The capes come off and the duo point to the belts, which are temporarily sitting on the ring apron. Motioning their hands about their midsections, they indicate to the fans that that's where those belts belong. The fans disagree and make their thoughts known quite vocally. The fans' reaction abruptly changes, however, as "Storm" is replaced by "Ashton". AND NOW, CURRENTLY MAKING THEIR WAY TO THE RING AREA IS THE TOP RANKED TEAM IN THE FWF WITH FIVE TOURNAMENT POINTS. HAILING FROM MIAMI, FLORIDA, AND WEIGHING IN AT A TOTAL COMBINED WEIGHT OF FOUR HUNDRED, NINETY-SIX POUNDS, HERE IS DEREK SANDERSON AND HIS PARTNER, JOHNNY HOULTON, DEVASTAAAAAATION!!" Everyone knows the reputation of Devastation. Not only are they popular with the marks, but the hardcores as well recognize this duo as being a superb scientific oriented team. The two of them walk to the ring, looking right at Storm. No one to date has been able to effectively handle Storm in tag competition. That is about to change... they hope! The referee parts the ropes for Sanderson and Houlton. A ringside attendant receives their jackets and takes them away. Both sides are respectfully remaining on their own sides of the ring. The referee takes the belts and holds them aloft, circling the ring so all can see just what the tournament has boiled down to. He hands them to the timekeeper and indicates that the bell can be rung to start this match. The four men approach each other in the centre of the ring very calmly and cooly as the referee gives the four men his instructions on how he's going to officiate the match. His words fall on uncaring ears as the two teams stare each other down. Storm starts to taunt the smaller team. The referee tells them to start the match and two men must leave the ring to do so. As Devastation heads back to their corner to confer, Storm decides the two men to leave the ring will be Johnny Houlton and Derek Sanderson. They charge on them as soon as their backs are turned, but Devastation suspects as much, and reading their fans' reaction, instinctively duck the two clotheslines that were headed their way. Storm turns around quite surprised, but get met with jabs as they do so. Lightning and Thunder swing at them and miss. Devastation turns them around with their momentum, grab them by the waist, and administer twin German suplexes on them. Storm roll back under the ropes to the outside, and Devastation return to their corner as Derek, the smaller man of his team, exits. Storm is more than willing to take advantage of the referee's ten count as they get their wits back about them. Thunder jumps up to Storm's corner and Lightning enters the ring at the eight count. As he climbs onto the apron, Johnny kicks him in the stomach and seizes him around the head, hauling him vertical in a suplex. Much to the fans surprise, Johnny doesn't drop him back right away. Turning around very carefully with his man vertical, Johnny starts to run a few steps forward. Very quickly, he spins around again and drops Lightning to the mat. The hardcores applaud the running suplex, a rare move nowadays. Lightning is quite surprised himself by Johnny's power and speed. As he starts to get up, Johnny shows him a bit more of this speed by snapmaring the Stormer over his shoulder and preforming a somersault neck snapper over top of him. Not giving the surprised Stormer a chance to recover, Johnny picks him up off the mat and leads him into the corner. Derek is tagged and he goes to the top rope. Johnny holds him from behind and Derek jumps off the top, headbutting the standing Lightning on the way down. Johnny lets go of Lightning and exits. Lightning stands there for a second or two before falling down to his back, thoroughly dazed from the blow to his head. Sanderson meanwhile runs to the ropes and returning to his opponent, jumps up, flips in midair, and lands with his back on Lightning's stomach. Grabbing his head and one leg, he pins him. The referee counts: ONE... TWO... and Lightning manages to roll a shoulder up and throw the Devastator off him. Lightning rolls under the bottom rope to the outside, but is met there by Johnny. Johnny presses him up over his head and throws him into the ring the hard way - over the top rope! As the referee yells at Johnny to get back on the apron, Thunder runs into the ring and hits the back of Derek's head with a running clothesline. When the referee turns around, he finds Thunder repeatedly dropping elbows to the back of Derek's head. Stopping him and threatening disqualification, Thunder merely smiles and heads back to his corner. Lightning is starting to come back to his senses and sees his fallen foe in front of him, just starting to get up. As his body is still saying "No" from the beating he took, he decides he'd better let his partner in for a bit. Grabbing Derek by the hair so he won't get away, he drags him to Storm's corner. Thunder makes the tag and relieves his shaken partner. Thunder grabs the Devastator by the waist and drops him over a knee. Running to the ropes, Thunder drops a leg on him on the return. Easily picking his opponent up, he throws him over a shoulder and performs a running powerslam. The referee moves in to count the pin. ONE... TWO... and Derek still has enough sense to kick out of the pin attempt. Thunder, a bit surprised by the resilience of his smaller opponent, picks Derek up off the mat and sends him into the ropes. Derek rebounds and is flipped high in the air in a back bodydrop. Running to the ropes again, Thunder drops an elbow, right into the mat! Derek managed to get out of the way of the elbow smash and capitalizing on his opponent's weakened arm, he grabs it and drives his knee into it several times. Wrapping the arm up in his own legs, Derek places Thunder in a short- arm scissors. Derek really needed this rest and is perfectly content to hold his opponent there for a bit. Thunder, however has other ideas and struggles to get to the ropes. Outweighed by about 65 lbs, Derek is dragged with him. The ref starts laying a count on Derek as Thunder reaches the rope and Derek willingly relinquishes the hold, but moves right back in on Thunder and drags him to Devastation's corner by the arm. Johnny is tagged and climbs to the second rope. Still holding Thunder by the arm, Derek positions him with his back to Johnny. Johnny leaps from the corner, grabs him in a headlock, and bulldogs his head into the mat as Derek lets go and exits. Rolling the larger man over, Johnny pins him. ONE... TWO... and Johnny is thrown a foot into the air as Thunder pushes him off with authority. Thunder gets back to his feet and is met with a knee lift from Johnny. Thunder staggers back into the ropes and Johnny decides to Irish whip him to the far side. Thunder, however, thinks otherwise and reverses it. On the rebound, Johnny is caught in Thunder's trap and is picked up crossways across his shoulders. Knowing he's about to be the victim of the Tornado if he doesn't do something, Johnny kicks and thrashes violently, finally throwing Thunder off balance and backwards into a Crucifix cradle. ONE... TWO... and a half! as Thunder barely manages to untangle an arm from this pinning move. Thunder, however, is close enough to his corner to make the tag easily and he hangs onto Johnny's leg to prevent the Devastator from escaping. Lightning is back in the ring and drops a knee into the back of Johnny's head as Thunder leaves. Picking Johnny up off the mat, Lightning hooks his arms around Johnny's waist and delivers a lightning-quick belly-to-belly suplex. After a whip to the ropes, Johnny's head is snapped backwards after a high dropkick by Lightning. Grabbing Johnny again around the waist, he sets him down on the top turnbuckle pad. The Stormer draws his thumb across his throat in a symbolic gesture and climbs to the second rope. Belly-to- belly superplex! The ref counts: ONE... TWO... AND Johnny's foot finds the bottom rope. Lightning looks quite disgusted, but determined to continue, he picks Johnny back up and sets him atop the turnbuckle again. Back to the second rope, Lightning attempts his finisher again. Lightning doesn't seem to know that lightning never strikes the same place twice, but Johnny does as he hooks his feet around the steel cable and pushes Lightning back to the mat. Johnny stands up on the top rope and launches off with a flying elbow... and misses! Lightning rolls out of the way in time and grabbing Houlton, he drags him to Storm's corner. The tag is made to Thunder as Lightning climbs to the top rope. Thunder hefts Johnny to his shoulders in a torture rack and Lightning jumps off with the knee! The Hurricane! Things do not look promising as Thunder covers his opponent. ONE... TWO... and Derek leaps at Thunder, knocking him off. The referee grabs ahold of him and tries to get him back into the corner as Thunder continues. Grabbing Johnny for a vertical suplex, Thunder picks him up and drops him atop a neutral turnbuckle. Climbing to the second turnbuckle himself, Thunder drapes one of Johnny's arms over his own head and grabs him by the side of his red tights. The crowd goes into a shocked silence as they realize the super-brainbuster is forthcoming. Derek tries to get into the ring again, but the referee stops him. Thunder lifts, but Johnny kicks his legs, forcing Thunder to set him down again. Again Thunder tries, but Houlton hooks his ankle around the steel bolt that holds the ropes. Bracing his feet on the second pad, Johnny pulls Thunder upwards! There's only one viable place to drop Thunder, so Thunder gets rudely straddled across the top rope! Derek on one side and Johnny on the other bounce the rope up and down a few times. Signalling his partner to stop, Johnny, still sitting on the top rope, stands up and mounts the top pad. Thunder is still straddling the top rope, but not for long. Johnny dives head-first from the top rope and clotheslines Thunder off! Now close to his corner, Johnny makes the long- awaited tag. Thunder is laying beside the ropes at this point, so Derek goes to the centre of the apron and holds onto the top rope. He leans back, and then flies over the ropes, delivering a headbutt to the unfortunate Stormer. Lightning enters the ring to help his partner, but Derek sees him and dropkicks him over the top rope to the floor. Returning to his foe, Derek picks him up and Atomic drops him on the top turnbuckle. Tagging Johnny, Derek heads to the neutral turnbuckle and up to the top rope, facing the crowd. Johnny waistlocks him and delivers a belly-to-back superplex. Just as they land, Derek drops back. Fall away elbow smash! Johnny, still under Thunder bridges up for the pin. The ref and fans count together... ONE... TWO... THREE!!!!! The bell rings, and "Ashton" starts on the P.A. system, but the ref looks up and sees both men's shoulders on the mat with Lightning half outside the ring grabbing onto Johnny's leg. At the count of two, Lightning did the only thing he could do by pulling Johnny's leg. It didn't break the pin, but Johnny was unable to bridge and fell down. The referee looks puzzled for a second and decided rather than call the match a draw, since there was outside interference, the match should continue. It was not a fair pin. Devastation and the fans are rejoicing, thinking they won the match. The referee interrupts them and tells Derek to go to the apron; the match is still underway. The audio team cuts the music from the P.A. Meanwhile, Thunder grabs Johnny from behind and rolls him up. The referee makes the count. ONE... TWO... and Johnny manages to shake off the surprise and kick out. Half of the fans still don't realize what's going on. Johnny gets to his feet and kicks Thunder. Johnny tags Derek and points to the top rope. Johnny grabs Thunder's legs and rolls him into a Boston Crab while Derek goes to the top rope. The fans stand up. Will Devastation cripple Storm the same way Storm crippled the Demolishers? Derek, however is facing the wrong way. Another fall away elbow is what he has in mind. Suddenly, unknown to Derek, Lightning sneaks into the ring, thrust-kicks Johnny off Thunder, climbs to the second rope, and grabs Derek from behind. A belly-to-back superplex later and Derek is being pinned. ONE... TWO... AND THIS time, Johnny makes the save. The referee has lost all control of this match. Thunder gets back up and tackles Johnny. The two of them start fighting while on the other side of the ring, Lightning is setting Derek up for a suplex. Derek puts on the brakes and turns this attempt into a small package. The referee doesn't see it right away. As the ref is chasing Johnny from the ring, Thunder crawls over and turns the small package over, so that Lightning is pinning Derek. The referee turns around and sees the pin. ONE... TWO... THREE!!!!! The lights flash, the boom is heard, and "Storm" starts up on the P.A. system. "THE WINNERS OF THIS MATCH, AND CONSEQUENTLY THE WINNERS OF THE FICTIONAL WRESTLING FEDERATION TAG TEAM ROUND ROBIN TOURNAMENT, AARON "LIGHTNING" MURPHY AND JERRY "THUNDER" MCGAGHNEY, STORRRRRRRRRRM!!!!!!!" Devastation looks visibly disgusted by the outcome of events. They had several near falls, all of which went sour. As the referee presents Storm with the belts, Derek and Johnny slide back into the ring and dropkick Storm. Grabbing the belts, Devastation each wind up and smash the gold metal belts into a Stormer's head. Holding the belts above their head, they and the fans mutually recognize who the REAL champs are. They know there'll be a rematch somewhere down the road, but that does little to soothe them now. Dropping the belts, Devastation heads back to the locker room. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Despite how obvious to some it was that Storm would emerge victorious, I want to state for the record I never decided until the last paragraph who I would have win the match in the end. You can choose to believe that, or doubt it. Doesn't matter to me. It's the truth.
