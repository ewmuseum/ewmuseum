A description of the FWF is included in the FAQ, but I'll answer your question. First of all, the Fictional Wrestling Federation involves three people: The Stilt Man, [name withheld], and myself. It was started by Stilt Man, and he handles singles' matches in the World and US divisions. Liam handles the Intercontinental division (singles') and I handle the tag-team stuff. The wrestlers you read about are a combination of personal creations of ours and suggestions from people on the net. Anyone having a good idea for (a) wrestler(s) may send one of us mail, describe your idea, and from there your wrestler is in our hands for us to decide if we want to use it or not. We obviously cannot incorporate every idea that is thrown our way.
