FWF: Wildfire vs. Cruncher

17 April 1992

From [detail withheld]@TETHYS.UCC.UMASS.EDU Thu Apr 16 23:47 PDT 1992 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tonight's big FWF action comes from the Township Auditorium in Columbia, South Carolina, as budding superstar the Latin Wildfire takes on the man riding his longest FWF winning streak to date (of one match), Montreal's own Skull Cruncher in what should prove an interesting battle. As the ring announcer makes his way down the aisle and between the ring ropes, we go down to the squared circle for tonight's main event. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WELCOME TO THE TOWNSHIP AUDITORIUM FOR TONIGHT'S MEMORABLE FWF ACTION! TONIGHT'S MAIN EVENT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL, WITH A THIRTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT! INTRODUCING FIRST, FROM BRANDON, FLORIDA, AT TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY SEVEN POUNDS, LED TO THE RING BY `THE PROFESSOR', JOHN GOULDING, HERE IS THE LATIN WILDFIRE!!!" The young Floridian star begins to trek towards the ring with a titanic grin on his face, which is mirrored by his second, John Goulding. As all loyal FWF fans no doubt recall, Goulding was on the recieving end of a vicious assault by "Dark Star" Dominic Walters, seemingly spurned by a playful slap from Goulding. Wearing a long metallic black sequined robe, the Wildfire, after looking to all sides of the arena to acknowledge his fans, begins to sprint towards the ring, with Goulding jogging close behind. The Wildfire leaps onto the apron and catapults himself over the top rope, with the Professor close behind, as the announcer prepares to finish his duties. Suddenly, an FWF ring boy slips into the ring and hands a note to the announcer, who nods and continues as the ring attendant exits. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THERE HAS BEEN A CHANGE IN THE MAIN EVENT TONIGHT. DUE TO CIRCUMSTANCES BEYOND OUR CONTROL, THE SKULL CRUNCHER WILL NOT BE ABLE TO COMPETE THIS EVENING." (Editor's note: Okay. So I lied. ;-) There is some milling about in the crowd at this announcement, as a few of the FWF hardcore fans had hoped to see whether or not the Cruncher is for real, or whether he just got lucky against Dennis Silver. In any event, the announcer goes on. "THEREFORE, HIS REPLACEMENT IS NOW PREPARED TO MAKE HIS WAY TOWARDS THE RING AREA. HAILING FROM JUNEAU, ALASKA, WEIGHING IN AT A HEFTY FOUR HUNDRED AND SIXTY FIVE POUNDS, HERE IS JERRY `THE STRATOFORTRESS' HENDERSON!!!" Fans all over the arena, and the Wildfire himself, look partially confused and partially dismayed at this latest announcement. Although the bout will still doubtlessly be a good one, the Wildfire has in just seconds gone from the overwhelming favorite in this bout to the underdog. Since his exit from the World title tournament in the second ever FWF bout, Henderson seemed to have fallen off the face of the Earth, and until now, nobody even remembered his presence in the Fictional Wrestling Federation. Until now. Nevertheless, the massive Henderson begins to stride towards the ring, looking out at the masses with utter disgust. The big bearded Alaskan heads towards the ring with all the presence of a Mack truck. He finally enters the ring, and stands facing both the Wildfire and Goulding. Goulding offers a handshake, but the 'Fortress simply grunts and spits in the Professor's direction. A visibly annoyed Goulding exits the ring, and the ref signals for the opening bell. The two circle each other, and the Wildfire leaps at Henderson, catching him in a collar-and-elbow lockup, only to be shoved off. The Latin star tries again, but his efforts are met with the same result. This time, Henderson is the one who lunges at his opponent, but the far more agile Wildfire ducks out of the way and as Henderson turns around, catches him flush in the cheek with a high evelation dropkick. The force of the blow knocks Henderson into the corner, and the Wildfire follow him in, climbing onto the middle ring ropes and hammering on the Stratofortress' forehead. After ten crowd-acknowledged punches, the Wildfire, the smaller man by more than two hundred and twenty pounds, attempts to monkey-flip the big man out of the corner, and a hefty shove knocks the smaller man to the mat. Henderson reads pain in the eyes of the Wildfire, and charges out of the corner with an elbow drop that is evaded long before Henderson hits the mat. The big man's elbow temporarily jarred, the Wildfire takes advantage of the situation and stomps on Henderson's hurt elbow with his left boot while holding the larger man's hand down with the right. He finishes off the series with a brutal knee drop to Henderson's hurting elbow that extracts a howl of pain from the big guy. Not wanting to lose his momentum, the Wildfire picks his larger foe up and yanks him down again with a painful Japenese side armdrag. Just as the Wildfire locks in an arm-bar, the 'Fortress surprises him with a knee to the head, knocking him down as well. The more dextrous Wildfire makes it to his feet first, and as the big man stands with his arm outstretched, groping for some feeling, the Wildfire strikes like lightning, leaping and landing a double-axehandle blow to the Stratofortress' extended limb. The Wildfire bounds off the ropes to land another blow to the hurting right arm of Henderson, and as he jumps at the big man, the injured arm suddenly comes to life, as Henderson catches the Wildfire under his arm and drops him in a side slam, reminiscent of that of Big Bubba Rogers. Finally having the advantage on his side, Henderson tosses the Wildfire against the ropes, and on the rebound, attempts a back body drop. Seeing this setup way in advance, the Wildfire leaps over the crouched-over Stratofortress, and as the big man spins around, takes him down with another painful arm-drag type maneuver. Henderson lets out a gruff roar as the referee approaches him to ask if he'd care to submit, and the ref takes this as a hint not to. Suddenly, many heads in the crowd spin towards the entrance area, to witness one Dominic Walters making his way towards the ring. A second referee, anticipating the worst, engages the Dark Star before he is able to reach the ring and explains that there is a match is in progress and he has no business being at ringside. Walters cooly explains that he has no affiliation with either of the combatants involved, and is simply scouting out possible future opponents. He grabs a steel chair, and the referee begins to go into conniptions. The Dark Star then proceeds to plant the chair in the corner opposite the one Goulding inhabits, and sits down innocently. By now, the men in the ring have noticed this, and the Wildfire has relinquished his grip on Henderson. He bolts out of the ring to where Walters sits, and points at him threateningly. Walters insists that he means no harm, and flashes him a "would-I-lie-to-you?" grin that would do any used car salesman proud. The Wildfire, aggravated, returns to the ring.. ..the hard way, as the big man pulls him back into the ring with a high vertical suplex. The larger man heads towards the ropes with a head of steam, and levels the young star with a powerdrive elbow drop (with his healthy arm) that knocks the Wildfire's wind from him. The big man, finally feeling in control, picks up the Wildfire and grasps him around the midsection, pulling him over in a devastating belly-to-belly suplex. Instead of going for the pinfall, Henderson rises and runs to the ropes, and attempts a big splash to finish it. He leaps and is met by the bare canvas, as the Wildfire escapes by inches. The Wildfire runs and scales the ropes, and pumps his arms to psyche up his legions of fans, and they go wild with anticipation. The Stratofortress is slow to rise , and when he finally does he is met by a flying dropkick to the jaw, knocking him to the mat with intense velocity. The adrenaline-pumped Wildfire gyrates his arms, and the crowd pops like they rarely do. He points to the opposite corner, and waves his arms horizontally, indicating this will end it. As he begins to climb the turnbuckles, he notices Walters begining to stroll in Goulding's direction, and leaps from the turnbuckles to the ring apron to the floor, between Walters and Goulding. Again, the Wildfire points at the Dark Star accusingly, and Walters returns the gesture with an incredulous "who, me?!?" gaze. Walters turns to walk away.. ..just as Henderson pulls the Wildfire via the hair onto the apron, scoops him up and powerslams him over the ropes, clubbing the ref with the Wildfire's flailing legs on the way in. The second ref, who addressed Walters earlier, runs to the ring to try to awaken the downed official, as Henderson begins his climb to the top turnbuckle, hoping to land the Nuclear Air Strike. Walters turns hs back on the entire situation, merely amused at the thought that the Wildfire being overconcerned with *his* presence caused his downfall. Goulding, angered at the entire situation, steps outside of his usual rule-abiding persona and stoops to Walters' level, as he hooks Henderson's leg, crotching him on the top turnbuckle. However, the second official noticed this little offense, and storms over to where Goulding stands, ranting and raving about seconds interfering in the matches. Meanwhile, back in the ring, the Wildfire has regained some semblance of consciousness, and climbs onto the middle turnbuckle, draping Henderson's arm over his neck, trying to pull the huge Alaskan over in a superplex. Walters, noticing this, grabs his observers chair and levels the Wildfire square in the back with it, knocking him completely unconscious. At the sound of the chair hitting flesh, Goulding spun the ref around, only to see Walters fall to the arena floor beneath the chair, and immediately grabbing his head as if it were he that were struck. As the referee and Goulding head over to where Walters landed to inquire as to his condition, the three all simultaneously glance at the ring, just as the Stratofortress climbs the top rope, and the Nuclear Air Strike is launched. All four hundred and sixty-plus pounds of Jerry 'The Stratofortress' Henderson lands on the fallen Latin Wildfire with frightening velocity, and the second referee dives into the ring to count the Wildfire down. One! Two! Three!!! As the third count falls to the mat, Dominic Walters, sensing that at enraged 'Professor' Goulding will attack any minute, takes the precaution of kneeing the Professor below the belt and heading away from the ring area. Henderson, not wanting to remain in the ring for too much longer himself, climbs out of the ring and heads off towards the dressing room as Goulding tends to the Wildfire and the official checks on his fallen comrade. As both heels make their way out of view, the announcement is made. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT: JERRY 'THE STRATOFORTRESS' HENDERSON!!!' As Goulding and a ringside attendant assist the Latin Wildfire back to the locker room, it seems clear that two teams have been formed, and these four men will undoubtedly meet again to decide the real winners. For now, thought, the victor is one Jerry 'The Stratofortress' Henderson. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Cheers, [name withheld] ([detail withheld]@tethys.ucc.umass.edu) Not too shabby a return for the once-thought-to-be-gone Jerry Henderson.
