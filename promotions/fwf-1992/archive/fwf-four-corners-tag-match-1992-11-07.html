FWF: Four corners TAG match

7 November 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Path: sparky!uunet!newsflash.concordia.ca!nstn.ns.ca!ac.dal.ca![detail withheld] From: [detail withheld]@ac.dal.ca ([name withheld]) Newsgroups: rec.sport.pro-wrestling Subject: FWF: Four corners TAG match Message-ID: <1992Nov7.233020.8692@ac.dal.ca> Date: 7 Nov 92 23:30:20 -0400 Organization: Dalhousie University, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada Lines: 400 Today, another first for the Fictional Wrestling Federation! This next match has been sanctioned by the FWF's championship committee and it will be a four corners tag-team match. The winners of this match get the first title shot against the reigning champs, Devastation. The rules? Four tag teams will be spread amongst the four corners of the ring. Two tag teams will be designated to start the match by the toss of a coin. The two wrestlers in the ring may tag anyone standing on the apron except for their current opponent's tag partner. This means at the start of the match, each man in the ring will have the choice of five men to tag into the ring. When one person is eliminated (pin, submission, DQ or countout), his tag partner must leave as well. The winners will be the last team remaining in the ring. The participants? The Wyld Stallions; Casey Kruger and Terry Rinaldi, The New Blade Runners; "Rock" Steve Rockwell and "Flash" Johnathon Flann, The Raiders; "Killer" Kevin Johnson and "Man Mountain" Huey Cleveland, and finally, the newest team in the FWF, The Chameleons. Due to the complexity of officiating such a bout, two referees have been assigned to the match. Inside the ring will be Joe Hebner, and on the outside, Tiny Anderson. Hebner is the senior official for the bout. The referees have been instructed, and the wrestlers warned, that the rules are to be strictly enforced. There will be no eight-man free for all. Before we go to the match, here is a statement from the new Champions, Devastation: "Eight man in the ring, two will remain. We don't care who it ends up being because being the champions means you have to be ready to face anyone, anytime. We are the champions, and plan to retain that status for a good, long time. We won't hide behind these belts either. We plan to be fighting champions, so when you step in the ring with us, be prepared, because we'll give you a wrestling lesson you soon won't forget!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Houston Astrodome was sold out a week ago when word spread about the FWF coming to the state of Texas for a special match. One of the things that makes this match so appealing to the fans is the element of mystery. With two men from four possible teams in the ring at any one time, there are twenty-two possible combinations of who could be in the ring together. Some of those men are friends and some of them are enemies. What will happen tonight? No one can say for sure. The last few stragglers file in from the concession stands as the main lights are dimmed and the ring is illuminated. Two men in striped shirts make their way down the aisle as The Grinch enters the ring with the microphone. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS A FOUR CORNER, FOUR TAG-TEAM ELIMINATION MATCH...." "Falling to Pieces" by Faith No More starts up on the PA system as two men appear through the curtain. "THE FIRST TEAM IS MAKING THEIR WAY DOWN THE AISLE. WEIGHING IN AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF FIVE HUNDRED, FORTY-FOUR POUNDS, HERE IS 'KILLER' KEVIN JOHNSON AND 'MAN MOUNTAIN' HUEY CLEVELAND, THE RAAAAAAIIIIIIIDERS!!!!" As The Raiders make their way up the steps to the ring, the music changes to an eerie, synthesized tune. A few people recognize it as being the music the Chameleons left to after defeating the Sheepwhackers. Looks like no one team in particular has been singled for imitation tonight. "TEAM NUMBER TWO HAILS FROM PARTS UNKNOWN AND WEIGHS IN AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF FIVE HUNDRED, TWENTY POUNDS. LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HERE ARE THE CHAAAAAAMELLLLEONS!!!!" Two men of identical height and build, wearing identical green capes and green masks stride silently down the aisle and enter the ring. The robes come off, and people's suspicions were confirmed; still no imitation as green wrestling tights are revealed. Will the "no imitation" trend carry into the match tonight so we can see if the Chameleons have their own distinctive wrestling style? We'll see. Black Sabboth's "Iron Man" replaces the Chameleons' music as the new Blade Runners make their way down the aisle. A deafening cry from the fans greets these two men. Tonight, all thoughts of Scorpion and Flayer are banished from these two men's minds as they concentrate on earning themselves the number one contenders' position. "TEAM NUMBER THREE IS CURRENTLY ENTERING THE RING. WEIGHING IN AT A TOTAL COMBINED WEIGHT OF FOUR HUNDRED, NINETY-SIX POUNDS, HERE IS 'ROCK' STEVE ROCKWELL AND 'FLASH' JOHNATHON FLANN, THE BLAAAAAADE RUNNNNNNERRRRS!!!!" Cheers turn into screams as Meatloaf's "Paradise by the Dashboard Lights" comes onto the PA System and the Wyld Stallions make their appearance. Men everywhere along the aisle are pushed aside as the female population here tonight makes itself known. Two handsome, young men in bright, tasselled shirts make their way down the aisle as these ladies reach out, hoping to just touch one of them. "THE FINAL TEAM IS SLOWLY MAKING ITS WAY TO THE RING. WEIGHING IN TOGETHER AT FIVE HUNDRED, TEN POUNDS, HERE IS CASEY KRUGER AND TERRY RINALDI, THE WYYYYYYYLD STAAAAALLLLLLLIONNNNNS!!!!" Slowly making their way to the ring is right. These ladies are all too familiar with some of the Stallions' previous ring entries and all hope they will be one of the lucky ones the Stallions pick to "show some appreciation to their loyal female following". Sure enough, two young ladies, one on each side of the aisle get their wish; a big kiss. One young woman collapses and the other one stands there as the Stallions move on, in mute shock. The tasselled shirts come off their well chisled torsos and are thrown into the crowd where they are immediately ripped to shreds. Luckily this time, no fight is started by this action. As the Stallions mount the steps, the Raiders look meaningfully at them. There's a lot of bad blood between these two teams, and we're bound to see some intense action from those participants. All four teams are checked by the referees and corners are assigned. The Blade Runners are opposite the Wyld Stallions and the Raiders are opposite the Chameleons. One man from each team is designated for the coin toss. The coin for the Raiders and Chameleons is tossed. Johnson calls heads. Tails. The Chameleons start. The next coin is tossed and Rock calls heads. Tails again. The Wyld Stallions will start. Terry and Chameleon #1 will be the first two competitors tonight. The remaining six men leave to the apron. The referee calls for the bell and this match is underway. Terry thoughtfully strokes his chin as he confers with Casey in the Wyld Stallion's corner. They have seen the tape of the Chameleons' last match and have some idea of what they're up against. At the referee's beckoning, Terry and the Chameleon go to the centre of the ring. Terry holds out his right hand to the Chameleon. The Chameleon holds out his left. Terry holds out his left, and the Chameleon holds out his right. Maybe a more appropriate name for this team would be the Mimes as Chameleon #1 keeps up this little game of "mirror". Halting this game before he looks any more foolish, Terry locks up with the Chameleon and catches him in a side headlock. Chameleon #1 grabs the Stallion around the waist and hoists him up in the air, but Rinaldi kicks his legs, falls back to his feet, and uses the momentum to snap his masked opponent over and to the mat. One slowly makes his way to his feet, still in the headlock, and pushes the Wyld Stallion back to the ropes and off to the opposite ropes. As Terry his the far ropes, the Chameleon does a handspring. As Terry makes it to the centre of the ring, that handspring results in a diving clothesline that sends both men to the mat from the momentum. The hardcores present applaud the well-timed move heartily. One makes it to his feet rapidly and hooks Terry's arms behind his back as he gets up. Double underhook suplex! The Chameleon returns to his own corner, but rather than tagging Two, he mounts the top turnbuckle. Terry gets to his feet. Seeing the Chameleon hurtling towards him, feet-first for a dropkick, his instincts kick in and he dives sideways, into the Blade Runner's corner, and he tags Johnathon Flann. Flash nimbly hops over the top rope as the Wyld Stallion exits the ring and circles back to his own corner. The Blade Runner quickly moves on his opponent and delivers a neckbreaker as the Chameleon starts to rise. An elbow now finds its mark as it lands on the masked man. Flash grabs One by the head and snaps him over a shoulder. Backing up a couple steps, he runs and drops a knee into the Chameleon's forehead, rolling out into a somersault. As One makes it back to his feet, the Blade Runner throws him to the far corner, which happens to be the Wyld Stallions' corner. Flash runs in, leaps in the air, and plants both feet high on the chest of the Chameleon as he dropkicks the stationary man in the corner. The Chameleon slumps down, but looks up and sees two men in the corner. Before Casey Kruger knows it, he has been tagged and One rolls under the bottom rope to the outside, crossing around the ring to his corner. The fans cheers increase in amplitude as the two faces face off. Hands are shook before the two men circle one another. Casey holds the size advantage, but his orange and black facepainted opponent holds a decided speed advantage. This is displayed from the word "Go" as Flash dives for the Stallion's feet and trips him. Flash quickly shifts his focus to Kruger's left arm, and then circles around again, catching him in a front facelock. Casey, taken off guard by this offensive, twists around and gets a foot under the bottom rope, causing the break. Flash backs off obediently and Casey scrutinizes his opponent. This is his first time in the ring with Flash and he hadn't counted on this man's quickness being what it is. He approaches the Blade Runner once again and this time, the two grapplers opt to lock up. Casey takes the advantage with a headlock, but Flash backs him into a corner. Suddenly, Flash feels himself tapped on the back. Suddenly, Casey Kruger falls down. Flash looks up and realizes he's in the Raiders' corner and has been unwittingly tagged out. The Man Mountain, Huey Cleveland steps over the top rope and the referee lays a count on Flann. Flann obediently exits, albeit a bit grudgingly. Kruger is still grabbing the back of his skull, feeling the bump where Cleveland headbutted him from behind. The Stallion is rudely picked up off his feet and pressed high over the Raider's head. Suddenly, the bottom falls out from under him and he is dropped straight down, face first to the mat. The large, 322 pound man isn't through yet though. Kevin Johnson on the apron is tagged and Cleveland grabs both of the Stallion's legs. The referee starts his count as the double-team starts. The Man Mountain drops backwards, sending Casey upwards in a slingshot. Just before landing though, "Killer" grabs Kruger in a front facelock on the way down and DDT's him! Casey is rolled over and a leg is hooked. ONE... TWO... and somehow, the Stallion finds the strength to kick out as he is about to be counted down for three. Johnson, disgusted, picks his opponent up off the mat and throws him to the ropes. As Casey rebounds, he is waistlocked by the Raider and dropped straight back onto the top rope! The Killer ascends to the top corner pad as Cleveland backs off to give him room. He leaps with legs extended, making it painfully obvious that his finisher, a leg drop, is forthcoming. Problem is, Kruger isn't there and the Raider crashes wildly to the mat. A smile tugs at the corners of Casey's mouth as he looks at his arch-rival rolling on the mat, grabbing his leg. The Stallion grabs Johnson's left leg, spins around, places Johnson's right leg over the left ankle, folds the right under the left, hooks the right ankle with his own leg, and the Indian Deathlock is complete! The fans are jubilant as Johnson wretches in agony on the mat. Kruger has had a lot of success with this move. Will it claim another victim? Not if the Man Mountain has anything to say about it! He remembers the pain associated with that move all too well and enters the ring to break it. The referee angrily threatens the Raider with disqualification and demands he exit, which he does. Casey gets up before Killer. Grabbing his legs again, the Stallion drags the Raider towards the Chameleon's corner. Chameleon #2 is tagged by the Stallion. Two goes to the top rope while Casey stays in the ring, still holding Johnson's legs, not letting his up. The fans gasp in realization of what's coming as a legdrop off the top rope finds its mark on the Raider. Casey used his knowledge of the Chameleons against the Raiders as the Killer finds himself being pinned following his own finisher! The referee counts as Casey exits: ONE... TWO... and again the Man Mountain is in the ring to save his partner. The referee turns to the timekeeper's table and waves his arms. The bell rings. "THE REFEREE HAS DISQUALIFIED THE RAIDERS!!!!!" Cleveland is furious, but the referee demands the Raiders return to the dressing room. After half a minute of arguing between Cleveland and both referees, the Raiders leave. Tiny Anderson gets the coin out to see who will resume the match against the Chameleons and the Blade Runners are selected. Rock enters the ring for the first time against Chameleon #2 and the bell rings again to resume the match. The Chameleon looks at his larger, painted opponent and tries to determine how to tackle this one. The fans know there's no way the lean, deceptively quick Chameleon will be able to use Rock's full nelson against him with any kind of effectiveness. The Chameleon surprises everyone by reaching one hand, palm open, above his head, gesturing for a test of strength. Rock looks out to the fans, and the response is unanimous and affirmative. Rock reaches out and locks fingers. The Chameleon reaches his other hand up and as Rock does the same, Chameleon #2 rolls the one held arm over his head and sends the Blade Runner to the mat in a fireman's carry. Two leads Rock to his feet by his arm and quickly circles around to his corner. One is tagged. Chameleon #1 climbs to the top rope and smashes an elbow into Rock's arm. One resumes the armbar and Two exits. One tags Two and Two climbs to the top rope. Again an elbow is smashed into Rock's arm. One exits. Two resumes the armbar. Rock is tired of this sequence and scoops Two up onto a shoulder. The Chameleon is slammed to the mat, but won't release the arm and Rock is carried over from the momentum. Two positions one foot under Rockwell's arm, one foot on the base of his neck, twists the arm at the wrist and pulls back on it. The referee gets down to see if Rock wants to submit. Rock doesn't know the meaning of "submit". Rock manages to slowly drag his slightly smaller opponent with him to the ropes. The referee calls for the break and starts a disqualification count. Two releases at four, gets to his feet, and runs to the ropes. Rock gets to his feet. He sees the Chameleon running towards him and instinctively ducks to one side, finding himself in his own corner, and tags Flash. Chameleon #2 had intended to clothesline Rock, but found himself hurtling over the top rope to the floor. Flash climbs to the top and just as the Chameleon gets to his feet, Flash jumps, rolls in midair, and lands back- first on the masked man, sending both of them to the floor! Flash jumps to the apron and as Two groggily gets to his feet, a clothesline sends him back to the floor. Realizing the count is at seven now, Flash throws Two into the ring and follows him. Johnathon Flann failed to notice that his opponent was thrown into the ring next to the Wyld Stallions. Two tags Terry Rinaldi as Flann is rolling under the bottom rope and proceeds into the centre of the ring. Flann promptly runs to the ropes and a high cross body sends the Chameleon to the mat. Flann awaits the pin, but the referee simply taps him on the back. Flann turns around and sees Terry Rinaldi! Rinaldi takes advantage of Flann's surprise and whips him to the ropes. On the rebound, Flann is spun around 360 degrees into a backbreaker! The Stallion quickly tags his partner, who goes to the top. Flash is brought into a vertical suplex position and held there. Kruger leaps from the top rope. Double Wipeout! Kruger bodyblocks Flash to the mat and Rinaldi falls backward with his opponent. The referee counts: ONE... (Rock enters the ring) TWO... (Rinaldi crosses around to stop his interference) THREE!!! Flash was unable to kick out of the Wyld Stallion's finisher. The bell rings and the Grinch makes it official: "THE BLADE RUNNERS HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED FROM THIS CONTEST" The fans reaction is mixed as one face team eliminates another. Some of them who are loyal followers of the Blade Runners note that it was the second time in the match that the Stallions and Chameleons co-operated to eliminate another team. Was there an alliance made? No one can say for sure, but one thing is certain; no alliances will be made in the final fall of this bout! Before calling for the bell, the referee indicates that the Chameleons are to move to the opposite corner, rather than the adjacent corner they now occupy, for the remainder of the bout. The match is now two-on-two: a regular tag bout. Chameleon #1 enters the ring opposing Casey Kruger. Kruger bounds to the ropes and catches his green-masked opponent unaware with a shoulderblock. Again the Stallion takes to the ropes and the Chameleon simply rolls onto his stomach and allows him to hop over. What he doesn't count on is Casey hopping over him and stopping immediately. Kruger drops an elbow into the small of the Chameleon's back! One rolls over to prevent another, but Kruger simply drops the elbow on his sternum. The Chameleon is Irish-whipped to the ropes, caught around the waist, spun 180 degrees around, and laid out on the mat in a spine buster! Casey hooks the Chameleon's leg and the referee counts: ONE... TWO... but it wasn't enough to keep the Chameleon down. Casey gets up and folding One's leg between his own, he crashes to the mat, painfully wrenching the knee ligaments. Spinning around, Kruger starts to apply the Indian Deathlock, but is rolled into a small package. ONE... TWO... but that wasn't nearly enough to keep the Wyld one down. One gets up and runs to the ropes. Kruger gets up and runs at 90 degrees to him. Kruger dives to his stomach. One passes overhead, and unnoticed by Casey, makes the tag! One approaches Casey, arm extended... Casey extends an arm... double clothesline! Two enters the ring and gut-wrench suplexes Casey as One leaves. Casey is now picked up and Atomic dropped. The Chameleon spins the Wyld Stallion around. In one fluid motion, Chameleon #2 springs down to the mat on his hands, arches his back, catches Kruger beneath the arms with his legs, and snaps him back over himself. Many of the fans recognize this move from the Chameleons' previous match. Those that recognize it also remember that it set up the end. One is tagged and he goes to the top. Will the Chameleons execute the Double Wipeout? Casey is set for a vertical suplex by Two. Casey is lifted up, but kicks his feet and falls back down!!! Two is quickly lifted up, Casey rotates 180 degrees around and drops him in the centre of the ring! One leaps anyway, hoping to still hit Kruger. Casey notices this and sticks a foot up, which catches the Chameleon flush on the jaw! One rolls out of the ring to try and avoid confrontation with the Stallion. Casey follows him out and chases him halfway around the ring before the Chameleon slides under the bottom rope. Kruger follows suit, but is stopped by a kick to the back of his head! One picks Casey up for an Atomic Drop and sets him down on the top turnbuckle in the Chameleons' corner. Two is tagged and goes to the top of the neutral corner. Belly to back suplex off the second by One. Fall-away elbowsmash by Two! Devastation's Finisher! A Power-plex! Two makes the pin. ONE... TWO... THREE!!! Rinaldi tried to stop it, but Chameleon #1 held him off! The fans are not pleased and make themselves vocally clear. The Joe Hebner holds up the Chameleon's hands as Alfred Grinchley makes the announcement: "THE WINNERS OF THIS MATCH, AND DESIGNATED NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS: THE CHAAAAAMELLLLLLEONNNNNNS!!!!!!!" The Chameleons will get the first title shot at the current champs, Devastation. A very clear message was sent out to everyone by this match's ending. The Wyld Stallions weren't prepared to encounter that maneuver and Devastation have to realize the Chameleons have perfected their move and will certainly be well prepared for their title shot. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- And there you have it.
