FWF: Flawless vs. Titan

14 October 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Message-ID: <1992Oct14.035459.14130@CS.ORST.EDU> Date: 14 Oct 92 03:54:59 GMT Sender: usenet@CS.ORST.EDU Organization: Oregon State University, Computer Science Dept. Lines: 310 Nntp-Posting-Host: prism.cs.orst.edu And, once again, the Fictional Wrestling Federation is under way. A disk being full on the machine where my account is has severely messed up my news and mail interactions, but the Stilt Man is still around here somewhere, do not fear, and the FWF goes on despite the galactic conspiracy against it. I'm not paranoid, everyone really IS out to get me. :-) :-) Today, the United States Heavyweight Champion and #1 contender for the World Title, John "Flawless" Lawless, defends his title against the #2 contender for the World Title, the Invincible Titan Morgan. Due to the interference of Lawless in a match between Morgan and Kane the Undertaker, the FWF Championship Committee has decided that the title match will be a no-DQ match, for the belt. Kane the Undertaker has been barred from ringside and the match will be between Morgan and Lawless. There will be a winner, folks. Comments from both sides: Morgan: "Finally, the prayers of Titan Morgan have been answered! Ever since Doctor Death swiped the World Heavyweight Championship from around my waist, all the TitanManiacs have been politely ignored by the FWF Championship Committee. But now, dudes, that comes to an end! The United States Champ, John Lawless, finally has to answer for his ways, with the `Wrestler Be Cool' stick smashing Doctor Death over the head on one end, and interfering in my match with Kane the Undertaker on the other. Now, there's no dis- qualifications! No easy way out! Kane is gone, barred from ringside! It's just you and me, Lawless, to the finish, for the gold! And just like the little kid on the block who keeps egging everyone on to do his dirty work for him, you'll get you're comeuppance when you've got the 25-inch pythons on one side, and millions of TitanManiacs on the other! And if you've got any doubts, then watch this, brother!!!" Lawless: "Everybody immediately figures that John Lawless is out the moment they take the disqualification rule out from under me. Think again, Mr. Morgan! No DQ? That suits `Flawless' Lawless just fine, because it means I don't have to watch the ref when I'm knocking your head off with a steel chair or the `Wrestler Be Cool' stick. Go ahead and count your blessings now, you over- rated moron, because once you step in that ring, there's no turning back. After thirty minutes, and you're lying on the mat, finding those ring lights ever so fascinating because you're too exhausted to think about anything else, and I'm staring down with you underneath my foot, things will look a lot differently to you! There's no place for flaws in a match like this, and everybody knows `Flawless' Lawless doesn't need to worry!" Without further ado, the match: --------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Atlantic City Convention Center is quiet, until the sound of "Exodus" bursts out over the PA system. The marks immediately boo, while the hard- cores shout in appreciation of the man coming down the aisle to the ring. He has short-cropped brown hair, blue eyes sparkling with confidence, and a pronounced strut that is visible even beneath the sparkling blue robes. Alfred Grinchley begins his task for the evening . . . "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS FOR THE UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP! IT IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL, AND THE DISQUALIFICATION RULE HAS BEEN WAIVED FOR THE BOUT. MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING AREA, AND HAILING FROM SEATTLE, WASHINGTON. HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT POUNDS, AND HE IS THE UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: JOHN `FLAWLESS' LAAAAAAAAWLESS!!!" A foot-and-a-half long wooden rod, an inch in diameter, is clutched tightly in his hands. He has no doubts about the outcome of this match, and well he should not. John Lawless is the United States Champion, currently enjoying his second reign as such, and a former World Champion. He is the only man who has held three FWF singles titles. His credentials are among the most enviable in the Fictional Wrestling Federation. He steps up to the ring, motioning with the rod like a sceptre, and the official answers, parting the ring ropes for his entry. He smirks slyly out at the hardcores section to one side, and leaps over the top rope into the ring, contemptuously glancing at the referee in amusement as he flies over. He removes the robe, revealing the United States Championship belt around his waist. He has every intention of keeping it there, against the #2 man in the FWF. As if the thought conjures the action, the music shifts to "Final Countdown," and the crowd, marks and hardcores alike, erupts into cheers. The man has the personality and the push from the FWF to go over with the marks, and his technical wrestling skills and workrate draw the hardcores' liking as well. The blonde hair and blue eyes, the beard and mustache, the smile on his face, the gold-and-azure colors of his tights and sleeveless T-shirt all relate the image of a superstar. "AND HIS OPPONENT, FROM VENICE, CALIFORNIA, WEIGHING IN AT THREE HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN POUNDS, THE INVINCIBLE TIIIITAN MOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORGAN!!!" Morgan waves a single finger in the air as he climbs the turnbuckles, drawing a deafening cheer from everyone in the arena. He points over at Lawless, hurling the bandana from his head in the U.S. Champion's face. As Lawless angrily picks the blue cloth off, Morgan hops over the corner ropes and into the ring, tearing the shirt from his sculpted chest and hurling it out to the crowd. There is a mad dash in the direction the shirt flew to get it, and finally a mother comes up with it, giving it to her child at ringside. Morgan crosses his finger over his heart and points at the sky while the referee explains the rules to both men. He then instructs Lawless to remove the United States belt, which is then hoisted overhead for the viewing of every fan in the Convention Center. This is what it's all about. The stakes are upped even further by the announcement of the FWF Championship Committee, that the U.S. Champion who leaves this bout will get the next mandatory World Championship defense from the diabolical Doctor Death. Lawless has had two shots before. Once, he won the title. The other, he lost by DQ. Morgan also has had two shots. Once, he lost by DQ to Doctor Death. The other, he won the title from Annihilus. A third shot hangs in the balance for both men, for both are former World Champions hungry to regain that ultimate prestige. The United States Championship is merely means to the end for these men who have tasted better champagne. The bell is rung, the belt passed to an official, and the match begins. Morgan smiles as Lawless, dwarfed by the Titan's size, backs to the ropes at his approach and ducks under the top one. Morgan shakes his head in amusement and backs off, waiting for Lawless to confront him. Finally, they join in a collar and elbow tie. Immediately, Morgan hefts Lawless four feet up into the air and hurls him into the corner! The move is simple, unscientific, and not pleasing to the hardcores, but it makes its point well. Lawless half-bounces from the corner with the impact, holding onto one rope to avoid hurtling right back into Morgan's clutches out of control of his movements. Morgan motions in a beckoning gesture, and Lawless glares at him timidly. He rubs at his nose as if to scratch an itch, but Morgan can see the mind behind the eyes going full-tilt. Neither man wants any mistakes, and it visibly makes both perhaps overly cautious. Lawless comes out of the corner and locks up with Morgan again, only to have Morgan snare him arm-to-arm and toss him over the hip. Lawless gets up, enraged, running straight into a Japanese arm drag. His reason gets control again after this, realizing that he is falling right into Morgan's game plan. Morgan doesn't see it, as Lawless runs in again, this time stopping short of the arm drag and jabbing Morgan in the eyes with two fingers. Morgan grabs his eyes, and Lawless grins as the official remains conspicuously silent. There is no disqualification, so the move is legal here. Lawless grabs Morgan by the hair, twisting around in blatant dirty tactics, before holding him at arm's length with one hand and leaping up into a drop kick, unwinding the full length of his legs from two inches away. Morgan shoots back from the powerful blow, and Lawless clasps on a headlock before the Titan has any chance to regain a bearing. Morgan slowly gets his feet underneath himself, forcing himself and Lawless off their feet to the mat. A quick movement, and he slips out of the headlock like a greased watermelon, catching Lawless' arm behind him in a hammerlock in the same movement. He hefts Lawless into the air by the arm and slams him down to the mat, dropping one leg over the shoulder the moment the smaller man hits canvas. He wraps the left arm around his right leg, twisting around at elbow, shoulder, and everything else, eliciting a scream of pain from his opponent. Lawless tries to get a glimpse of his surroundings, and his legs scissors around the bottom rope as soon as he finds out he's close enough. Morgan refuses to let go! Lawless' eyes widen as he remembers the no-DQ rule, and it comes to him all at once that most of his easy ways out aren't going to work. This knowledge having registered, he turns onto his belly toward the arm Morgan has, trying to lift Morgan's leg off his shoulder. Slowly, he gets loose, and the moment Morgan's grip is broken, Lawless is in the corner rubbing the wounded limb. Morgan is after him with a jab to the face. He mercilessly pounds Lawless with unmitigated closed fists, following up with a knife-edge chop to the chest that sends Lawless flying backward over the turnbuckles to the floor. The landing is not good, and Morgan climbs the top rope, waiting for a chance. An elbow smash to the head greets Lawless the moment he gets to his feet, and Morgan immediately shoves the Grinch out of his chair at the timekeeper's table, folding it up and turning back to Lawless. Morgan is not a rulebreaker, and the rules allow much in this contest! Lawless is pounded on the floor with the chair with absolutely no concern for his well-being! Blood wells up in his hair after the first few strikes, and his consciousness deserts him a moment. Finally, Morgan brings the bloodied U.S. Champion back into the ring, sending him back within. There may be no disqualification, but there are count-outs, and the title can't change hands on those. Morgan knows this, and moves back into the ring, covering the unmoving U.S. Champ. One! Two . . . Amazingly, Lawless jerks one shoulder up into the air, but Morgan isn't worried in the slightest. The chant is up quickly this time: "DDT! DDT! DDT! DDT! DDT! DDT!" Morgan nods to the fans, and brings the smaller man into a front facelock. He cups his hand to his ear, listening for the crowd's approval. Dumb move. Lawless is tougher than he looks, and pitches Morgan into an overhead toss onto his back. He staggers to the ropes, trying to clear the blood from his eyes so he can see. There is a massive gash on his forehead, and most of his face is covered in crimson liquid. A glimmer of light comes through the haze, and pain conracts his features into a grimace. But he sees Morgan coming up to his feet, and immediately he is moving. A spinning neckbreaker puts Morgan back onto the mat, and now it is the Titan who is no longer moving. The neck injury inflicted by Lawless and Kane in an earlier match is brought to the fore. But Lawless' mind is too far gone to remember this part. He's going entirely on instinct at this point. He puts Morgan slowly back onto his feet, looping one arm overhead, picking him up by the thighs and dropping him back down to the mat in a back suplex. Morgan strokes his neck a moment after impact, the blow bothering him further. Lawless cocks a knee and drops it over Morgan's forehead, stopping his challenger's movement a moment. He stands again, shaking his head, and the referee darts back to avoid the scarlet sprinkle. Lawless smiles when he realizes fully what's going on about this point, and prepares to follow up on the attack. Drawing Morgan back to his feet, Lawless snaps him over the shoulder, and the Titan instinctively sits up, his legs stretched out before him. Perfect position for Lawless' taste, as he grabs the head of the larger man, flipping over it and stretching the spine out in a most painful manner! Morgan comes to his knees, trying to prevent a second strike, only to fall into the trap further as so many in the past have, as Lawless repeats the maneuver in reverse, bending Morgan back double! Morgan groans faintly, inaudible above the fans, as Lawless stands, spreading his arms, calling out derisively: "NOW THAT'S FLAWLESS!!" The response is unhappy to say the least from the fans, and Lawless cares little. Let them try to get in the ring like this against their hero, and get pummeled with a steel chair. They'd look a lot worse than Lawless does at this point, and that's saying a mouthful. The United States Champion brings Morgan back up to his feet, only to get a shot in the mouth for his trouble! The fans shout in hope for the face challenger, and Lawless strikes back angrily. Morgan's fist lashes out, shaking Lawless, rendering him wobbly. A third strike has him staggering backward to keep his balance. Morgan follows him, only to get stabbed with a finger in the eye, and both men fall to the mat. Lawless rises to his hands and knees, knowing he doesn't have much left in this match. He has to put Morgan away soon, or it's all over. He stands, ignoring the pain, and gets Morgan back to his feet. He sends him across the ropes, seizing him in a waistlock and turning him around to land on his spine hard, almost bouncing back to his feet in enthusiasm. The fans are silenced by the spine buster, knowing that the Perfect Plex cannot be far behind, and no one escapes from the Perfect Plex when Lawless applies it. Lawless surprises them, moving to the side of the ring. What's he doing? The question goes around from one fan to the next, and somewhere in the arena, a spectator's eyes fall on the "Wrestler Be Cool" stick on the timekeeper's table. Lawless steps outside the ring, confirming their suspicions by taking up the wooden instrument, rolling under the bottom rope into the ring with it. The referee cannot do anything about it; it's a no-disqualification match. As Morgan comes dazedly to his feet, Lawless raises it to strike . . . Morgan ducks under the blow astutely; he was only playing possum! He scoops Lawless up in the same movement and turns him up and over, tucking his head under with one hand as the bone-crunching power slam puts him onto the mat! The stick flies from Lawless' hand, clattering to the canvas near one turn- buckle. Morgan stands, his eyes falling on the dreaded weapon in the corner, and he points at it, then at Lawless, looking out at the fans in suggestion. The response is tumultuous, and Morgan brings up the stick. He turns to Lawless, and the moment the U.S. Champion is on his feet, the Titan breaks the stick over Lawless' head! Lawless falls to the mat like an axed tree, and Morgan contemptuously hurls the now-useless instrument aside. He looks out to the fans and claps his hands three times, running to the ropes. He hefts his right leg up, using all the power in his body to hurl himself six feet up into the air, until three hundred and fifteen pounds of Leg Drop of Doom lands directly on the near-unconscious United States Champion's face. The cover is not long in coming. One! Two . . . . . . and three!!! Morgan stands, jubilant, as he is handed the belt, listening joyously to the Grinch's announcement: "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AND THE NEEEEEEEEW UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION: THE INVINCIBLE TIIIIIIIIIIITAN MOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORGAAAAAAN!!!!!" He holds the belt over his head, and the eruption of the spectators in cheer is deafening. He stands on each turnbuckle in turn, holding it up above for all to see, as Lawless staggers out of the ring. The posing ritual of victory begins . . . --------------------------------------------------------------------------- And there you have it. A new United States Champion, the Invincible Titan Morgan, the fourth man to hold the title. And for John "Flawless" Lawless, ever more desperate tactics must be undertaken to regain his lost glory. That's it from the Stilt Man.
