MCW — Rules

Manitoba Championship Wrestling F.A.Q.

29 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FAQ What is the MCW? MCW, or Manitoba Championship Wrestling is an independent wrestling federation run out of Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Who’s in Charge? Both on and off camera Mathew Clayton is the one that’s in charge of all the goings on in the MCW. He signs the matches, signs the paychecks and enforces the laws of the MCW. How are cards written? Cards are written in full move by move commentary from the commentary team at ringside. What are the weekly MCW shows? MCW Freeze Frame : This is a weekly flash show where the stars of the MCW can rant and rave on what happened the previous card and what will happen at this weeks card. The show will be aired on a Thursday. MCW Sunday Shockwave : This is the MCW’s big show. This will be a weekly show that will be held in different arenas in Manitoba and surrounding provinces. This gives you pay per view caliber matches every week. Who are the personalities behind the microphones? These are the commentators of the MCW: Christopher McCabe : Christopher McCabe a former amateur and professional wrestler. McCabe although he’s nearing his fiftieth birthday, McCabe is not afraid to speak his mind, and still tries to keep in good physical shape. McCabe gives his insight on how he would wrestle the match. He’s generally respected for his frank and to the point commentary. Jerome Williams : This young cocky kid from the south Bronx, is not afraid to cheer for the bad guy. Williams uses a winning at all costs mentality, and thinks anyone who would hold values over a win shouldn’t even bother to compete. Curtis Harnett: Curtis Harnett is the quintessential wrestling journalist. Always impartial, he calls the match the way he sees it without bias. Harnett has been in the wrestling business for 15 years and knows what it’s all about. He believes that the best way to win a match is through hard work and training, not by crook. Harnett’s philosiphy in the ring is "if you can’t win fair, what’s it worth? The Interviewers : Rusty Alderman : Alderman asks the controversial questions that everyone wants to know. Often he upsets the individual he is interviewing, and often to the point that he has to spend the night in the hospital. He takes pride in the bumps and scars he's earned though, and certainly he receives a little bonus on his paycheck for his efforts. "The Smooth Talker" Jimmy Nolan : Jimmy Nolan, unlike Alderman, tends to skirt the issues as long as it means that there’s no chance of getting hurt. Jimmy will usually agree with whatever the interviewee has to say , and this keeps him outta hot water with the boys. His interview style is less informative then Alderman’s, but just as entertaining. The Ring Announcer : Gordon Gallagher : Gordon has a smooth "Barry White-ish" type tone to his ring announcing and is always dressed to the nines. What titles do the MCW have to offer? MCW Heavyweight Championship : The top strap in the MCW, this is the person who is recognized as the leader of the MCW. Provincial Champion : The second highest ranking title in the MCW, next to the MCW Heavyweight title. MCW Television Championship : The third most important title in the MCW behind the Heavyweight and Provincial Titles MCW Tag Team Championship: These titles are for those competing in tag team competition and are the only titles available for the tag team division. When does the MCW hold Super-Cards? Usually every month the MCW will hold a super-card, which is a pay per view quality show, without it being on PPV. What are the main rules of the MCW? Your main job as a participant in the MCW is to provide one (or more if your feeling spry) roleplay to air on the MCW’s flash show, Freeze Frame. This rolepaly will decide on weather you win or lose, or even on the push you get. Someone who remains silent for long periods of time, or fails to produce roleplays on a frequent basis will be either jobbed out or fired. Swearing in a rolepaly is permitted, but remember this is a television show. They won’t let you say Fuck on the air, so blank out whatever you can’t say on TV. Now it’s a given that your wrestler can’t attack another roleplayer’s without consent, that is sent to me. Also, attacking of the employees of the MCW must be granted by me. Now, remember, flashes are judged on not only your ability to shoot on the other guy, but character originality and development, as well as how much heat your wrestler can draw. Finally, the Prez has the final word. If you lose a match, I will discuss it with you, and explain to you why I think you lost the match. But I WILL NOT tolerate anyone who constantly complains or threatens to quit the fed. No matter who you are. Strats : Gameplans Strategies, or strats for short, are needed in order to win matches. It's up to you which kind of strategy you send in, a long drawn out one or a simple paragraph or two. "I wanna win, 'cause it'd be cool." is _NOT_ a strategy. Focus on your opponent's weakness, and explain how your wrestler plans to wrestle against them. Failure to send in strats may very likely lead to your wrestler being jobbed. A strategy/gameplan form is included below for you to fill out. Fill one out each time your wrestler has a match and send it to me by the given due date. This is what you will need to fill out ********************************************************************* Wrestler's Name: Match: Entrance: (Include Gordon Gallagher’s ring introduction, commentary, appearance, just as it will appear on the card, etc.) Match Plan: (Give me an overall strategy of what you want to do in the match.) Winning/Losing Situation: (What your character will do in each) ********************************************************************* Angles and Booking What makes a good e-fed are original and hot angles. Angles are mostly advanced through flashing and matches. Attacks are not permitted unless for a specific purpose, and authorized by me. The roleplayers have the greatest say in who they want to have rivalries with. The president schedules matches based upon the current rivalries in the organization, and for the purpose of building further angles. Sending in challenges to other wrestlers are looked favorably on, and in most cases will be scheduled whenever possible. Again angles come into play, and those challenges that seem to "make sense" will be given preference in schedule dating. Title matches usually are given to those who prove themselves inpast matches, and simply asking for a title match without reason(orstoryline) won't net you one, guaranteed. Final Words So does the MCW interest you? If it does, I’m glad to hear it. There is a application attached at the bottom. It’s a federation that if all goes as I plan, will take off and will be lots of fun for all that are participants. If it sounds like a lot of work, it is. It’s a big responsibility to be in this type of fed, but when everyone works together the results are phenomenal. I’m not sure when we’ll be starting up, this is more of a feeling out process to see if anyone is interested.
