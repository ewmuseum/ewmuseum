Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Beyond Extreme Wrestling’s one surviving document, posted 13 April 2001, laid out a full year of programming before naming a single wrestler: six championships — World, Intercontinental, North American, Hardcore, TV and Tag Team — two weekly shows, Tuesday Night Massacre (likened in its own text to “the WWF's Raw is War and Warzone rolled up in one”) and Friday Night Carnage, and a named monthly pay-per-view for all twelve months of the year, from January’s Fatal Tendencies, built around an annual Extreme Rumble, to December’s Silent Night.

Its rules barred real wrestlers, interviewers and announcers from its roleplays, required every roleplay run at least 35 lines, and asked simply that its writers be “creative and original” and have fun; it followed with its own house interviewer staff, written up, its rules page says, because writers kept borrowing announcers from wrestling's real television promotions instead.

No roster, card, result or closure notice survives beyond that one rules document.
