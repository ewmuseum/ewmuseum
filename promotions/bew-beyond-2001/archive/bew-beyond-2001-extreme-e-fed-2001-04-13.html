Beyond Extreme Wrestling: Read If You Want to Be in an Extreme E-Fed

13 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<i><b><u>*****Beyond Extreme Wrestling (BEW)******</i></u> <img src= http://bewwrestling.8m.com/bew.jpg > -Titles:</b> *BEW World Title *IC title *North American Title *Hardcore Title *TV Titles *Tag Team Titles <b>-TV and PPV shows (TV)</b> *Tuesday Night Massacre [Tuesday Night Massacre is like the WWF's Raw is War and Warzone rolled up in one.This is where most Fueds develope and start.] *Friday Night Carange [This is like the WWF's Smackdown but with a more hardcore twist.This is mainly a lead up to current fueds and when a PPV comes a nearing, this is the set-up for the PPV matches] <b>(Pay-Per-View Names)</b> *January: Fatal Tendencies [Annual Extreme Rumble goes on at this PPV.The winner gets a chance ta the BEW title at March's WrestleFest PPV.Also on the card there will be 5 or 6 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *February: Excessive Force [This PPV card is a set-up on the road to the WrestleFest PPV.On the card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *March: WrestleFest [This PPV card is BEW's granddaddy of them of....of PPVs.On the card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage.And one of the matches will be the winner of the Extreme Rumble facing the current BEW Champion.] *April: Extreme Havoc [This PPV card is the aftermath of the big WrestleFest PPV.On the card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *May: Shockwave [On this PPV card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *June: King of the Death Match [This annual PPV features an 8 men/women one night torunament.The 8 particapants will go through different death matches that are randomly selected, all deathmatches are different but share one thing in common.....and that is brutality to the max.Also on this card there will be 3 or 4 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *July: No Rulz [On this PPV card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *August: Summer Mayhem [This is BEW's big summer PPV.The card features a Bloody Brawl Caged Heat Match, which features 4 on 4 man/woman teams taking on each other.The winners get titleshots at the next PPV. Also on the card there will be 7 or 8 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *September: Vengeance [This is the PPV aftermath of BEW's Summer Mayhem PPV.On this PPV card the 4 men who win the Bloody Brawl Caged Heat Match get there titleshots.Also on the card there will be 4 or 5 more matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *October: Halloween Hell [On this PPV card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *November: Deadly Consequences [This PPV is BEW's big November PPV.This card features 4 survivor Matches featuring 2 on 2 teams.Also on the card there will be 4 or 5 more matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] *December: Silent Night [On this PPV card there will be 8 or 9 matches that might have developed on Tuesday Night Massacre and/or Friday Night Carnage] <b>-Rules!!!</b> *Bad words are allowed. *RP's must be at least 35 lines. *No using real wrestlers or interviewer or announcers in the RP. *No using a BEW wrestler in your RP without that wrestler's permission. *Must be creative and original. *No whining to the presidents if you lose. *Have fun! <b>(Below is the BEW staff)</b> 5.BEW STAFF INTERVIEWERS --------------- After a while, I get sick of people writing their roleplays with people like Micheal Cole interviewing them. Micheal Cole works for the World Wrestling Federation, NOT Beyond Extreme Wrestling. The following is a description of some of the BEW staff members that you can use in your roleplays to interview you: (1) Cowboy Chris Camnito - Jim Ross type announcing, but about 20 years younger (2) Mean Mike McLean - Mean Gene type interviewer, but about 10 years younger, former wrestler (3) Derrick Anoymi - Michael Cole type interviewer, but not gay, clean cut, former manager (4) Tom Clamoy - Lead Announcer, interviews during television shows only <b>-So join the BEW go to http://bewwrestling.8m.com/ </b>
