EWA Thursday Night Terror

19 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 19, 1998 Live, from The Myriad, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. The Widow Maker v. The Ghetto Boy (w/ Dark Force Rising) Strangely enough, Ghetto Boy did not show up for this match, and The Widow Maker obtained his first EWA victory. The Widow Maker d. Ghetto Boy by forfeit. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Kris Gaffney/Michael Simurda Once again, no-shows by both teams. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Iceman" Scott Snyder (w/ Dark Force Rising) Galaxy made a strong showing in this match, as he really put Synder out with some beautiful high-flying moves, and finishing the match with a modified moonsault. Galaxy d. Scott Snyder via a modified moonsault. (5:15) Paul Burnett v "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) Again, neither man showed up for the match. It was later discovered that the Horsemen have left the EWA. ARE YOU READY??? (All of DX makes their way out to the ringside area, through a big pyrotechnic display on the runway... Draven has both of his belts slung across his shoulders, Hall is wearing the I-C Title around his waist. Draven grabs the microphone from the ring announcer, and begins to speak.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: I'm just gonna make this short and sweet, 'cause quite honestly, I do have more important peop..er...things to do. It has come to my attention that there is a man here in the EWA that has been overlooked for a title shot, and... I guess it's my duty as holder of both the coveted US and World Heavyweight Titles, to make sure that he gets his due shot at the US Title... So ladies and gentlemen, I give to you....KANE!!! (Kane's music blasts over the PA system, as the monster makes his way to the ring...without Paul Bearer...All of DX clears the ring in a hurry as the Hellspawn steps over the top rope, and stares Draven down... The bell rings in the background... Draven falls to his knees, attempting to beg off... and gets grabbed around the throat and dragged back to his feet. Draven promptly falls over backward, seemingly unconcious... Kane goes for the pinfall...1, 2... Voice over the PA: Hold it just a second! (The camera swings over to the ramp...Sgt. Slaughter is making his way down to ringside.) From here, Slaughter announced that Kane was in the back, and that whomever Draven was facing was not the real Kane. Slaughter ordered "Kane" to unmask, or all of DX would be suspended. "Kane" took the mask off, and revealed himself to be Kevin Nash. An outraged Slaughter announced that he was stripping Draven of the US Title, and holding a tournament for the belt. Hunter Hearst Helmsley became particularly outraged at this, and then attacked Slaughter. Tiberus (w/ The Empire) v. Swap Meet Louie (w/ Dark Force Rising) All members of The Empire jumped Dark Force Rising before the match, apparently causing several injuries, along with the departure of DFR from the EWA. Tiberus d. Swap Meet Louie via forfeit. El Locco Jobbo v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) El Locco tried hard to get the big man down on his feet, and even had him wobbling at one point, until he attempted a moonsault. At this point, Kane simply grabbed Jobbo in midair, and tombstoned him to the mat for the victory. Kane d. El Locco Jobbo via the Tombstone Piledriver. (0:57) Alex Wright (w/ Flash Funk/The Disco Inferno) v. 'Hollywood' Barry Horowitz Jr. This match was back and forth for the first five minutes, until the outside interference of Dance Fever played a role. Disco Inferno grabbed the foot of Horowtiz, and as Horowitz argued with Disco, Alex executed the German Suplex for the three count. Alex Wright d. Barry Horowitz Jr. via the German Suplex. (5:47) KG (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Rock" Chris Gardner This match was over before it started, as KG attacked Gardner as he made his way to the ring. This bout was ruled a double-disqualification. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Surprisingly, Harley Hart did not show up for this match. A rematch has been rumored, but not yet confirmed. SmirtDogg d. Harley Hart via forfeit. Steve "Mongo" McMichael/Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. FlashBack (w/ Alex Wright) FlashBack obtained the forfeit win, due to the departure of The Horsemen. Nero (w/ The Empire) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) HBK did not seem to be at all on his game, as Nero dominated this match, finishing HBK off with his patented Nero Bomb. Nero d. Shawn Michaels via the Nero Bomb. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) This was an exciting tag team contest, and the improving Death Sentance seemed to have the upper hand on the NAO for most of the match. The New Age Outlaws tried to use their cheating habits to sway the advantage back to their side, but Pain and Destruction would not be denied tonight. Death Sentance d. The NAO via Pain's pin on "Road Dawg" Jesse James after a chokeslam. (9:06) Joshua Gardner v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Deathstar dominated this matchup, as Gardner never seemed to be on his game. Deathstar d. Josh Gardner via submission w/ the Last Rights. (3:12) Octavius (w/ The Empire v. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This was a highly competitive matchup, one in which Octavius had the upper hand for most of. But the sides changed hands when Ahmed delivered a low blow to Octavius, and then hit the Pearl River Plunge for the victory. Ahmed Johnson d. Octavius via the Pearl River Plunge. (10:24) Double Main Event! Triangle Match Winner will be named the #1 Contender for the EWA World Heavyweight Title at WrestleFest '98 "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Note: Austin's TV Title will not be on the line in this match. In a shocking turn of events, Cummings did not show up for the match. TGC was not expected to show up either, due to the recent actions of the "Thug Warriors", but just as referee Nick Patrick was about to raise Austin's hand in victory, the lights went out, and golden beams filled the arena. TGC made his way to the ring, and the fight was on. After around 25 minutes, the advantage seemed to be on GC's side. Austin whipped GC into the ropes, and SmirtDogg, who had just made his way to ringside, reached out and tripped GC. GC turned to argue with Smirt, and when he turned back around, he was met with the Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin d. The Golden Child via the Stone Cold Stunner. (26:10) Steve Austin will face the EWA World Heavyweight Champion at EWA WrestleFest '98. EWA World Tag Team Championship Return Match The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) v. Harlem Heat (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This matchup was marred by contraversy when Nick Patrick showed up to officiate the match. President Gates made his way to ringside, announcing that Earl Hebner would be the official, which made DX extremely angry. (26 minutes into the match) Mike Malone: Harlem Heat has the advantage right now, and Booker T's going to the top for the Harlem Hangover! Larry Zybysko: If he hits this, it's all over! MM: He got it! 1, 2, 3! Harlem Heat has regained the Wo...what the hell? (The lights have just went out. When they come back on, three more men are in the ring...) MM: That's...that's Bloodsport!!! They hold the championship in the GWF! What in the hell are they doing here?!?! They're attacking The Outsiders and The Harlem Heat!!! My god!!! LZ: I don't think they're here just to be seen...something's going on here. MM: The Outsiders and Harlem Heat have been cleared from the ring!!! And here comes James Vandenburg!!! Mortis and Wrath have cleaned house, and now Vandenburg has a house mic! Vandenburg: For those of you who might not recognize us, allow me to introduce ourselves. We are Bloodsport, the most dominant tag team to ever step into a ring and by far the most dangerous group of men that any of you will ever face. The GWF has grown tiresome...as we have beaten everyone and everything in our paths. Now, the EWA is the next federation to fall to the powers of this man Mortis and this man Wrath. These two men have never been beaten, holding a perfect 12-0 record in the GWF, and at one time holding both the United States and World Tag Team Championships!!! And if any of you pathetic weaklings want to taste thier powers, all we ask is to sign a contract...and then watch your severed heads be delivered to me after your certain defeats. And as for you, Harlem Heat...now realize that you have no where to run...and those tag titles will ours whenever you have the guts to get into the ring with my men. Until then...rest easy, because when these men are finished with ALL of you, all that shall remain...is... MM: What in the hell? The sound of a Flatline just came over the speakers, and green mist is surrounding the ring! Dear Lord...well, the tag team situation just got a lot more interesting here in the EWA! So long, folks!
