EWA Wednesday Night War

14 April 1999

April 14, 1999 Live from the Mid-South Coliseum (Memphis, Tennessee) ---- The lights go down in the Mid-South Colosseum in Memphis, Tennessee as a sold-out crowd roars in anticipation of another edition of EWA's Wednesday Night War, which is just moments away from starting. The fans already know about the main event for the evening, the finals of the King of the Ring Tournament between Kanyon and Raven. They also know about the Intercontinental Title match as Tanaka defends against Ministry. Not to mention an action-packed undercard. But what night would be complete without an appearance from the EWA World Heavyweight Champion. After a few seconds with nothing but a few flash beams to light the arena, the golden beams shoot through the arena to a huge POP from the crowd. 'Golden Showers' hits the loudspeakers as literally every fan in attendance gets on their feet to greet "The Shining Star" The Golden One. TGO has his World Title draped over his shoulder, and he, as always, slaps hands with almost every fan with an extended hand down the aisle and at ringside. The Golden One: Memphis, Tennessee, welcome to War! Now, let's cut straight to the chase. For the past few days, a lot of stuff has happened in the EWA and everyone seems to be interested in what everyone thinks on the subject. I've heard Solley interviewed repeatedly in the back. I've heard the bastards who left questioned time and time again. Yet nobody seems to look at the person it affects the most, and that's me, "The Shining Star." You see folks, 4 men walked out of the EWA at the last Clash of the Champions a few days ago. It was a shocking turn of events to say the least. But when I look at the men, I realized something very important. I realized that this ain't where the big boys play, but it's where the big boys run. Of those 4 men who walked away from this organization on Saturday, one I don't give two sh*ts about. That, of course, is Max Pins, a man I once beat in a tag team match. His walking away didn't really even raise my eyebrow because as far as I'm concerned, he was worthless to this organization anyway. But hey, to each his own, right? Now, the other 3 are Kane, E-'Nuff, and Jack Reed. Three major contenders for my EWA World Heavyweight Title. Three men who have been hyped as "the real deal" for quite some time. Three men who everyone and their mother wanted to see beat me and take the gold. Well, here's a little news flash for ya. There was never a day when that could happen. I've proven time and time again that I AM the man in the EWA, and this belt wrapped over my shoulder proves exactly that. Let's start with the so-called Big Red Machine Kane. He didn't look like such a great machine at March Madness, now did he? You see, I took him out of his element, into an actual REGULAR match. Haven't you noticed Kane only wins titles and defends them when the fight is under his own damn rules? There's the casket match, which he needed to beat Jeff Bunda, who did the time-honored tradition for him anyway. Then, in a feeble attempt to recapture some of the glory I took from him, he wants me in an Inferno Match for the EWA World Title. You see folks, Kane can't win a regular match with anything on the line. He's a gimmick, plain and simple. Take that gimmick away and what you truly have is nothing more than The Big Red Retard. So Kane runs away from me at the Clash of the Champions. Hey, that's cool. I mean, I sent your sorry ass straight into retirement boy. But the least you could have done was do it with some respect. I mean, we had a damn good match at March Madness and things got a bit out of hand. You had to bring your crow-like fool Draven into the mix so of course Terry would even the score. But hey Kane, the bottom line is I pinned you for the 3 count and took your title. I thought the feelings of respect were mutual, but then you no-show the biggest match of your career? Well folks, I guess the legend of Kane rots in the same flames he would have been in had he taken me on. Moving along though... Jack Reed, The Walking Triple Threat. Rumor has it this guy had a title shot upcoming at the next EWA extravagansa, World War 3. Hey Reed, now we don't have anything personal going on. Hell, I hardly know you. But if memory serves, we had a date for the Intercontinenal Title that you needed Crazy Eddie and the rest of your lunatics to save for you. But hey brother, I'm not shy. I would have gladly given you the shot, considering you had earned it by getting disqualification in every single match you've ever had. Bottom line Reed, you want it, you know where to find it, and as your friend would say, NUFF said. Now, the man who run away like the little girl that he is, E-'Nuff. What did you think Nuff? That I would just let you ride off into the sunset, and forget everything that's happened recently. Oh hell no you little puke. You see E-Nuff, of anyone here in the EWA, you, my friend, are the one I wanted the most. Some people just don't know when to say when, but you just don't know when to shut up. For those of you who haven't heard by now, everyone's least favorite criminal, E-'Nuff, ran his big mouth for some worthless publication about things I've said to him. Well folks, here's the scoop. E-'Nuff and I, contrary to popular belief, have NEVER EVEN SPOKEN! How's that for twisting the truth. He must be dillusional or something, because he's out there talking to Barrow and company at the newspaper and he invents these things that I, The Golden One, have never said. But hey, I've never been one to back away from a feud. I heard he had an EWA World Title shot in a couple of weeks against yours truly. Yet, when the going gets tough, when I'm getting through Kane and marching towards E-'Nuff, what does he do? He tucks his tail between his legs and scoots out the door. Now that, my friends, is the true sign of a man. *TGO rolls his eyes* Now I'm sure if E-'Nuff gets word of this, him, Reed and the rest will sh*t their pants. It may have taken be a little while to get around to him, but you can't honestly think I would just turn the other way and let you verbally stomp me. So E-'Nuff, here's the deal. I could be a real "tough" guy and call out your retired ass for a match. But I've been in that position before, walking away from the EWA, so I know that match just couldn't happen. So here's the deal Nuff. You want me? You got me. I'm not hard to find, and if I'm in a crowd, just look for the guy carrying the belt you could never win. That should make it real easy on you. Moving on to the present. The EWA has lost some talent, but not all of it. And there's three people in particular I feel the need to address. The first two, Sting and Erik Draven, stuck their nose into my business at the Clash when Kane was too damn scared to show up. Now I don't know exactly what these two have got going on, and frankly, I'm not the slightest bit interested in it. You see, Sting and especially Draven have pulled the same stunt over and over again. How many times has Draven come and gone, come and gone, come and gone. I'm sick and tired of it so I'm going to make it real simple for you and Sting. You want me? Just like E-'Nuff and Reed, come and get me. Cut the crap because when it's all said and done, the result will be the same regardless of whether you come from the rafters a bunch or not. I'll beat either one of you, any day of the week, with relative ease. Hell, Draven is my personal b*tch, or did everyone forget December 30th already? Last but certainly not least, Kanyon. The thorne in my golden side. You've got the opportunity of a lifetime a little bit later tonight brother, and I plan on being there to witness it. Now of the two, Raven and Kanyon, there's no doubt I want to see Kanyon's sorry ass advance, because like they say, payback is a bitch. But I can be as impartial as the next guy, which is exactly why I've decided to get myself a look at both guys live and in color. So Solley, sign whatever papers need to be signed, because ladies and gentlemen, tonight, in this very ring, in the main event, The Golden One will be the special guest referee.
