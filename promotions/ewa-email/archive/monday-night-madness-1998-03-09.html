EWA Monday Night Madness

9 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 9, 1998 Live, from the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia. Live from the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, Georgia Augustus v. Dude Love SemiFinal EWA U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) SemiFinal EWA U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/The Golden Child (w/ Degeneration X) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Le Tigre v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) ***Note: Le Tigre requested that he be allowed to put his mask on the line in this match. Therefore, if Smirt wins, he will be allowed to unmask Le Tigre. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) EWA World Television Championship Match Nero v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) TRIPLE MAIN EVENT!! EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) EWA Intercontinental Championship Triangle Match Ahmed Johnson (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Hannibal Manson (w/ Dark Powers) v. Scott Hall (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Non-Title Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Augustus v. Dude Love Neither man showed up; it was announced that Augustus had left the EWA, along with Nero. SemiFinal EWA U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) FlashBack d. The Reckoning. (7:12) SemiFinal EWA U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) The Azzkickers d. Death Sentance. (6:11) Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/The Golden Child (w/ Degeneration X) Kane came to the ring area, and tombstoned both Shawn Michaels -and- TGC; the match was ruled a no contest. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Le Tigre v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) ***Note: Le Tigre requested that he be allowed to put his mask on the line in this match. Therefore, if Smirt wins, he will be allowed to unmask Le Tigre. The Circus! was in the corner of Le Tigre, and The Thug Warriors were in the corner of Smirt. Booker T walloped Le Tigre with a chair, causing a mass brawl between The Circus! and The Thug Warriors. During the brawl, Smirt pinned Le Tigre, and shockingly, when Le Tigre pulled off his mask, he revealed himself as legendary superstar Thorne Richards!! SmirtDogg d. Thorne Richards, a.k.a. Le Tigre, by outside interference. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) Galaxy d. The Pessimist. EWA World Television Championship Match Nero v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) Eclipse d. Nero. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) Deathstar d. Alex Wright. TRIPLE MAIN EVENT!! EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) Harlem Heat d. DDT. EWA Intercontinental Championship Triangle Match Ahmed Johnson (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Hannibal Manson (w/ Dark Powers) v. Scott Hall (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Hannibal Manson d. Ahmed Johnson; Scott Hall did not show up. Hannibal Manson is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion! Non-Title Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) (After Kane's entrance, the lights fall dim...a green spotlight falls on the entranceway, and...) "ARE YOU READY???" Voice over the PA: Actually, to be honest...no. (The Mega-Tron's screen stops displaying the DX entrance video, and instead shows an image of Erik Draven, backstage.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Kane, Kane, Kane...(he shakes his head) I suppose I should have expected something like this from you...but still, that crossed the line...no ifs, ands, or buts. (he shakes his head) Something like this...something like this doesn't deserve to be settled in the squared circle...because there are rules there, and I'm not exactly in a 'rule-following' frame of mind right now, because I think killing your opponent is grounds for a DQ. "The ICON" Erik Draven: If there are rules, this match has no point for me... I can't take the revenge I want out of your hide, and besides the point...it's not for my title, so...there is no point for me to fight you now. When the opportunity arises...you will get your head removed from your body, courtesy of yours truly...and that will just be the beginning. Voice Over The PA: Draven, the opportunity has just ARISEN!! (The camera pans to the entrance ramp, and we see EWA Comissioner Sgt. Slaughter step out from behind the curtain, microphone in hand.) EWA Comissioner Sgt. Slaughter: Draven, you say that you won't wrestle Kane unless there are no rules? Well Draven, that's fine with me! Because if you won't show up to the ring here tonight for that reason, then next week on Monday Night Madness, you will face Kane in a No-Holds-Barred match!! And the price you'll have to pay for not wrestling Kane tonight is simple: the no-holds-barred match next week WILL be for the EWA World Heavyweight Championship! AND THAT'S AN ORDER!! (On the MegaTron, Draven pauses for a moment, looks concerned, then confers with the rest of DX...) "The ICON" Erik Draven: As out of character as this is for me...I accept...(On the MegaTron, the rest of DX looks shocked by his announcement...HHH tries talking sense into him, but Draven shakes his head) Kane will be mine...Hell-Spawn, your worst nightmare will take physical form...one week from tonight...Prepare yourself. (Draven draws his thumb across his throat, and the camera fades)
