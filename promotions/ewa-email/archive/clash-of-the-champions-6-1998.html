EWA Clash Of The Champions VI

26 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Clash Of The Champions VI Saturday, September 26, 1998 Live, from The America West Arena Phoenix, Arizona. El Locco Jobbo v. Galaxy (w/ Precious) The first match at the sixth installment of this EWA event was, for lack of a better term, awful. Neither man seemed to have any semblence of an offense, and finally, much to the fans' relief, the match ended when Galaxy hit the SuperNova. Galaxy d. El Locco Jobbo with the SuperNova. Tommy Dreamer v. Deader Neither man decided it was worth the trouble of showing up. As a result, President Gates announced that if either man no-showed again, they would be fired. Deader and Tommy Dreamer ends in a no-contest. Slain v. Sting Slain entered the ring, and then the lights went out. When they came back on, Slain was facefirst on the mat, and Sting was in the ring. However, Sting simply left, and the match was ruled a no-contest. Slain v. Sting was ruled a no-contest. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) v. Mac Namara Will Haynes didn't show up. President Gates, who was obviously very irritated by now, announced that if Haynes ever no-showed again, he would be fired. Mac Namara d. Will Haynes by forfeit. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Outsiders (w/ The New World Order) Well, after another no-show by Darkness, Gates finally did what many of us have been waiting for: fired Darkness. The Outsiders d. Darkness by forfeit. On the flipside, Ruger Ice continued his streak, dominating this match from bell to bell, and getting a nice-sized pop from the crowd. Ruger Ice d. Haywood Jublome with the Spinebuster. "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (w/ Bill Alfonso) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Trixie) This match lasted all of five seconds. Both men were on the outside, and attempted to headbutt each other, but as a result, knocked each other out cold. Rob Van Dam and Stevie Richards battled to a double countout. "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) v. Val Venis (w/ Terri Runnels) Finally, an entertaining match! God knows it's about time. Satre started off with an early advantage with a couple of nicely-done armbar submission holds, and maintained the advantage with some submission-style moves. Venis regained the advantage in this one when Satre decided to go to the top and miss a frog splash. However, going to the top was also Venis' demise, as he missed an attempt at the Money Shot, and Satre rolled him up for the pin. Joe Satre d. Val Venis with a rollup. EWA North American Championship Match Michael Modest v. Crazy Canuck (EWA North American Champion, w/ The Hate Club) Two good matches in a row? Gee, we must be blessed. Anyway, Modest started off by trying to wreck Canuck's gameplan and turn this match extreme. Canuck successfully denied this, keeping the contest in the ring. Modest then turned to a highflying attack, which was very successful for most of the match against Canuck. Double-C turned the tide, however, with a low blow counter off of a double-ax handle attempt by Modest. Canuck then whipped Modest into the ropes...and right into the referee. Once the ref was knocked out, Canuck hit the Lunatic Bomb, and went for the pin. But shockingly, and from out of nowhere, he was pulled off of Modest and DDT'd by none other than Raven. Raven then quickly exited the ring, and Modest rolled over onto Canuck. The referee revived in time to make the three count, and a new North American Champion was crowned. Michael Modest d. Crazy Canuck with interference from Raven. Michael Modest is the new EWA North American Champion! Terry Conklin (w/ The New World Order) v. Jack Reed (w/ The Hate Club) You get the feeling that after this, the Walking Triple Threat won't even be doing that tomorrow. Conklin didn't look much better, to be honest, but he still managed to pull off the victory. Terry Conklin d. Jack Reed with the Overhand Right.
