EWA Saturday Night Slam

18 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 18, 1998 Live, from the Mid-South Colosseum, Memphis, Tennessee. Castor Troy v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Thorne Richards v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Triple Threat) The Widow Maker v. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) Non Title Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ The Triple Threat) Raven (w/ DDT) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit The Outsiders v. Public Enemy (w/ "Tricky" Dick Dennis) New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Mankind v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Grudge Match "Double A" Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Tag Team Grudge Match Prodigy (EWA European Champion) & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Ghetto Warriors (w/ Sunny) Castor Troy v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) This was a joke of a match, as Harley was tore apart on his second return to the EWA. Castor Troy d. Harley Hart with the Faceoff. (2:56) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Thorne Richards v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Triple Threat) Martin was way off tonight, and unfortunately for him, Thorne was very on. And after only 5 minutes into the match, Richards finally captured his first ever EWA Championship. Thorne Richards d. Aaron Martin with the Loch Ness Leglock. (5:11) Thorne Richards is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! The Widow Maker v. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) The Widow Maker didn't even show up. The Golden One d. The Widow Maker by forfeit. Non Title Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) It only took Kane 21 seconds to demolish Helmsley, but what happened afterward was much more interesting. After Kane defeated HHH, Ric Flair came from behind him and levelled him with a chair. Kane actually crumpled to the ground, and the fans went wild. Flair did his strut and his "Whoo!", and then Kane sat up. Flair then left, and Kane soon followed. Kane d. Triple-H with the Tombstone. (0:21) "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Another Stud Stable member torn apart in their comeback, as Koo looked extremely good tonight. KooKabura d. Scott Cummings with the Baby Bop. (4:32) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ The Triple Threat) Gardner didn't show up, and was promptly fired for his absence. However, Deathstar didn't show up either. Raven (w/ DDT) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) Raven defeated Hicks in impressive fashion tonight, and afterward, DDP came to the ring. Page and Raven then got into a furious brawl, which took security around 10 minutes to break up. Raven d. Justin Hicks with the Evenflow DDT. (6:38) Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit This was a rugged match at first, but the tide turned when Dreamer was caught in Benoit's rolling German suplexes. From there, it was the Crippler Crossface, and after the match, when DDT attempted to help Dreamer, he simply shrugged them off and left. DDT then attacked Benoit and Flair, who was at ringside, and then "Diamond" Dallas Page came, making the save. Page then held the "IV Horsemen Sign" high in the air. Chris Benoit d. Tommy Dreamer by submission with the Crippler Crossface. (9:01) The Outsiders v. Public Enemy (w/ "Tricky" Dick Dennis) The Outsiders looked very bad in this match, as Public Enemy really went to work on them. Public Enemy d. The Outsiders with Ghetto Boy's pin on Scott Hall after the Ghetto Driver. (5:27) New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) Buddy Brown and Flame were obviously having problems, but it didn't matter, as they still destroyed the struggling duo of Shawn and Matt Hart. New Demolition d. The Hart Brothers with Buddy Brown's pin on Matt Hart after the Bad Ass Bomb. (8:33) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Mankind v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Neither man showed up; a rematch has been signed for the Survivor Series. DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Grudge Match "Double A" Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Flair controlled most of this match, as Anderson seemed not to know what to expect. Flair seemed bent on defeating Anderson for his treachery, and that he did. Ric Flair d. Arn Anderson by submission with the Figure Four. (11:45) Tag Team Grudge Match Prodigy (EWA European Champion) & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Ghetto Warriors (w/ Sunny) This match was fantastic, and strange in itself. Throughout the match, the lights in the arena flickered, making Prodigy and Michaels visibly angry. The match got ugly after awhile, and eventually all four men spilled over to the outside, leaving referee Mark Curtis no choice but to disqualify both teams. After SmirtDogg and KG had left the ringside area, Michaels, who was obviously still upset, took the microphone. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: I don't know what the hell this is all about, but for the past two weeks, some boogeyman is out here -attempting- to mess with the mind of the master of the mindgames, the -TRUE- Degenerate, Prodigy. Now, I've got a good feeling that the S.O.B. who's behind this is that worthless dead zombie, The Undertaker. So dead man, if you've got the guts, if you want a part of Degeneration X, bring your sorry ass out here, and The Heartbreak Kid will be more than happy to face you at the Survivor Series. Show your face, zombie, and name your stipulation. Voice Over the PA: Dead man? You, my friend...are the one who is already dead...all of you are... (HBK rolls his eyes in the ring...the voice pauses for a moment, then continues) 'Name my stipulations'? Was that what you said? "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: I didn't stutter, dead man! Name your stipulation, then bring your dead ass out here so DX can send you back to hell where you belong! Voice: Your cockiness overwhelms me, Michaels...you don't even know who you're facing...(the voice chuckles) Michaels: I don't know what I'm facing? (he laughs) Geez, Undertaker, I'm not that much of a nimrod. I mean, just because I have a big head, (Michaels looks down) correction, two big heads, that doesn't mean that they're both empty. The Heartbreak Kid isn't a dumbass, my friend, so sound your damn bells, and stumble your dead mummified ass to the ring. Voice: Well...I suppose that is a matter of opinion. And for the record...you did agree to whatever stipulation I wanted, right? Michaels: Dead man, did all those Superkicks I gave you affect your hearing or something? Voice: Mr. President...you heard that. (the voice chuckles) Your ego always was your downfall, Shawn...why should now be any different? Michaels: Always -was-? What are you talking about? (Michaels looks confused now.) Voice: (in a mocking tone) Did I stutter, Shawn? Am I that easily forgotten? (the voice chuckles) Michaels: (who now looks nervous) Forgotten? What the hell do you mean? I told you, dead man, I'm ready. Voice: I will say three words, Shawn...and all will be clear. (in a mocking tone) Are you ready? (Shawn's jaw practically drops to the ground, and he begins to stutter.) Michaels: No...n...n..no...it ca...ca...can't b...be!! We got ri...ri...rid of you!! Voice: But it is, my friend...But it is. (the voice laughs...a dark, sinister laugh) Darkness never dies, Michaels...you of all people should know that. (Michaels whispers something to Prodigy...the look on Prodigy's face is of pure disbelief and shock.) Michaels: Ok, Bret....this is real funny, but the joke's over. Quit using one of those voice boxes. Yeah, you got DX this time, haha, real funny. Voice: Okay, Shawn...you got me. Got me cold...I suppose you want me to come out there now, right? (Michaels looks a lot more confident now.) Michaels: Hart, I should've known it was you...after all those times, you still want some of me? Bring your high-office ass down here, so I can kick it back into the office. Voice: If you insist...(the voice chuckles...'Until It Sleeps' by Metallica begins to play in the background) Michaels: What a great touch, Bret, you can even use some different mus.....NO!!!!! (As Shawn's voice changes octaves...a tall, dark figure steps out from behind the curtain...) Michaels: It can't be!!! ????: I see you recognize me...(he chuckles) I didn't think I could be forgotten quite that easily...as much as you would want to. Michaels: But...HOW? We took your ass out of this federation! ????: Darkness never dies, Shawn...remember that. (he chuckles) I let you believe what you wanted to believe until the time was right...until now...until your endgame had begun. (the mystery figure chuckles once again...a dark, sinister rumble) (Michaels looks shocked.) Michaels: I can't believe that you have the gall to come back! I've beaten your ass before, so name the stipulation, and come down here so I can do it again! ????: (he raises his head, pulls his hair back from away his face, giving the crowd a full view of his face. At the same time, his image appears on the Jumbo-Tron, prompting a huge reaction from the crowd) That's right...Erik Draven has returned...(he chuckles) Shawn, I will give you one chance to walk away...if you choose not to, I will not be held responsible for what is to come... (Michaels gulps.) Michaels: Draven, I've had it with you. This federation...no, this business is no longer big enough for the two of us. It's time you got the ass-whipping you deserved. Remember the GWA, Draven? The EWF? Remember the cage matches, the ladder matches? All the pain I inflicted on you? Prepare to recieve just that...10-fold. Erik Draven: I could ask you the same thing, Shawn...one in particular, actually...Remember the EWF? Remember the Fallen Angel...onto the ladder? I put you out, Michaels...don't tell me you don't remember that. Again...I will give you to the count of 3 to turn your back and walk... Michaels: And I put you out before too, Draven...out of the federation you dare to step back into. Erik Draven: 1.... Michaels: Myself, The Golden Child, and the TRUE Degenerate sent your sorry ass packing, and it looks like... Erik Draven: 2... Michaels: Like you don't have the common sense to stay gone. So for your little three count...1, 2, 3, (gives X-chop) SUCK IT! Erik Draven: 3...(he smiles) I was hoping you would be stupid enough to ignore me, Shawn... You put me out? Heh...kid, you are as dumb as you look, I guess. I left because I needed to do some soul searching...after what happened with Kane. And trust me, Kane...I will be coming after you next. Michaels: This isn't about Kane, Erik....this is about your stupidity, and Degeneration X. Erik Draven: Fair enough...(he grins) And now...for the stipulations... Michaels: Name them, oh fearless one. (Michaels rolls his eyes...and Prodigy whispers in his ear.) Erik Draven: First...If...no, WHEN I beat you at the Survivor Series...I get him. (he points to Prodigy) When I want...where I want...how I want... Michaels: (he whispers with Prodigy for a moment, and Prodigy then whispers to HHH as Michaels speaks.) Done. Erik Draven: Second...None of my 'former associates' will be allowed at ringside...This is between you and me, not all of you and me...not yet, anyway. Michaels: (he snickers) Done. Erik Draven: Third...The match is fought under my rules... The only way you can win is via submission or knockout...No wimping out and taking a countout or DQ loss...If my 'former associates' decide to stick their noses where they doesn't belong...You won't lose the match because of it, but...you will lose your career. (he grins) Michaels: (he snickers, and shakes his head) You've got a deal....with one addition. Bring whatever you want to the ring. Crowbar, chair, lead pipe, kitchen sink...you name it, it's legal. To quote you, "you game?" Erik Draven: Did I stutter, Shawn? I said no disqualifications...and I meant it. But remember...if the rest of your chumps run in... Say goodbye to your career. (he grins) So, just say the word, and we'll have this thing next week... Michaels: You've got it, old friend...and by the way, Erik, since you enjoy reminiscing so much, let's do that, for a moment. Remember back in the AEWF? Remember the Trifecta, before the bad time sunk in for you? More importantly....remember the night before your beloved Christina Knight perished? There was a reason you couldn't find either of us, 'old friend'....we (repeated X-chops) were UP ALL NIGHT!! Erik Draven: (A look of hatred crosses Draven's face...He begins to approach the ring.) You're a dead man... Michaels: At least you know the truth of it now! I wasn't in mourning at her funeral for no reason! (Prodigy slips out of the ring, leaving Michaels and Helmsley in against Draven.) Erik Draven: (Draven steps over the top rope, and stares across the ring at Helmsley and Michaels) You both are dead men... (The camera pans to the guardrail....Degeneration X superstar The Golden Child, apparently back from a lengthy absence, has climbed over the guardrail. Prodigy, who is standing on the outside, whispers to TGC. TGC nods, and heads for the ring. While Draven's back is turned, TGC scales the turnbuckles, and plays to the crowd a bit...Draven hears the reaction, and turns around.) Erik Draven: Well, well...Hail, hail, the gang's all here. I was wondering when you'd be back, GC. (he chuckles, and casually falls backward onto the ropes...causing just enough motion for TGC to lose his balance and crotch himself on the top turnbuckle) Okay...Four against one? I like the odds...let's do this. (Draven approaches Michaels...who promptly shoves HHH in front of him, then heads for an unoccupied corner...HHH throws one punch at Draven...who ducks, grabs HHH's throat, and chokeslams him in the center of the ring. He turns toward Michaels...but instead ends up catching TGC coming off the top turnbuckle. He presses TGC over his head, and very casually tosses him down on top of Helmsley...leaving Michaels and Draven all alone. Picture a cat stalking a mouse, and you'd have a good view of the situation... Michaels drops to his knees, attempting to beg off...Draven wraps both hands around HBK's throat, and lifts him high up in the air...Michaels is kicking, trying to get down...and finally, Draven drops him like a bad habit, right in the middle of the ring...He drags Michaels back to his feet, puts him in a setup position for a powerbomb...raises his left fist in the air, gets a loud cheer from the crowd, then hooks Michaels' arms, and delivers a huge Tiger Bomb (like Ahmed Johnson's Pearl River Plunge) in the center of the ring...Michaels is out cold.) (In the meantime, Helmsley and TGC have managed to drag themselves out of the ring, and are conferring with Prodigy...Draven picks HBK back up over his head, and tosses him over the top rope, onto the group...knocking everybody over. He grabs the microphone from the mat, and speaks again...) Erik Draven: Let that be your one and only warning, Degenerates...Death is coming for you. (he chuckles) Michaels, I hope you are prepared... (He tosses down the microphone, and the camera fades with one final image...Draven staring down at the fallen forms of DX...)
