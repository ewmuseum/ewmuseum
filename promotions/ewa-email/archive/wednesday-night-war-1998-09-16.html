EWA Wednesday Night War

16 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 16, 1998 Live, from The Jacksonville Colisseum Jacksonville, Florida Morbid v. Slain In this contest, Morbid was, for lack of a better term, Slain. Slain d. Morbid with a running powerbomb. James Mastin v. Hollywood Hogan (w/ The New World Order) Hollywood made his EWA debut in impressive fashion, defeating Mastin in under 30 seconds. Hollywood Hogan d. James Mastin with the Big Leg Drop. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Terry Conklin (w/ The New World Order) v. New Jack (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Ghetto Warriors) A very well-fought match, with New Jack holding a slight advantage throughout. As New Jack was about to hit the Gangsta Splash, Hogan and Bret Hart srpinted to the ring in aid of Conklin, and attacked New Jack. SmirtDogg then ran out, and made the save. New Jack d. Terry Conklin by disqualification. New Jack is still EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. Karnage (w/ Harley Race) v. KG (w/ Sunny) Karnage didn't show up, and KG got a forfeit win. Deader v. "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) Deader looked good in his return to action, but Satre looked a little better. Joe Satre d. Deader with the Pimp Splash. The Jobber Express (w/ Teddy Long) v. New Demolition The Jobber Express lived up to their name, as New Demolition coasted to an easy victory. Total Chaos (w/ Harley Race) v. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas & Chris Candido (w/ The Firm) Total Chaos looked as if their gameplan was non-existant, and The Firm got an easy victory. Joe Lemon v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The New World Order) *The nWo music blares over the PA system. Bret Hart and the rest of the nWo walk out from the back. They get down in the ring.* *"Loser" by Beck blares over the PA system. The ring announcer gets a note from an attendent. Howard Finkle reads it outloud* Howard Finkle: Ladies and Gentlemen, Joe Lemon is not in the building. Taking his place from Tampa, Florida. Weighing in at 211 pounds. KG. *"Ghetto Superstar" blares over the PA system and KG comes out to the ring. The whole nWo is in the ring and starts laughing. Bret Hart takes off his nWo shirt and offers it to KG. KG stands there for a while, obviously thinking if he should take Hart's offer to join the nWo or not. After about two minutes of thinking he accepts the shirt and puts the nWo hand gesture up in the air. The crowd starts booing like crazy and throwing empty cups into the ring. KG grabs the microphone and begins to speak* "Outlaw Immortal" KG: Pipe down you little ingrates. You know you love us all, cuz we're just too SWEEEEEEEEEEET. *The nWo music blares over the PA system as they all leave the ring together* Bret Hart d. Joe Lemon by forfeit. Tommy Dreamer v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin A short, well-fought match, which ended in Dreamer being on the wrong end of a Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin d. Tommy Dreamer with the Stone Cold Stunner. EWA North American Championship Match Crazy Canuck (w/ The Hate Club) v. Mayne Mann (EWA North American Champion, w/ Intoxication X) Another short match, one in which both men seemed to be off of their game. Eventually, Canuck hit a weak looking version of his Lunatic Bomb, and scored a victory. Crazy Canuck d. Mayne Mann with the Lunatic Bomb. Crazy Canuck is the new EWA North American Champion! "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. Stevie Richards A solid contest, which ended in a twenty minute time limit draw. Al Snow (w/ Head) v. The Prince Snow didn't even put up a good fight, as The Prince absolutely manhandled him in his EWA debut. The Prince d. Al Snow with a piledriver. Six Man Tag Chris Craft & The Black Angels v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA World Heavyweight Champion), ?????, & ????? The mystery partners turned out to be The New Age Outlaws, and DX got an easy victory. Afterward, Shawn Michaels ran to the ring, and nailed Copeland with a chair. He quickly fled, and Copeland then accepted his challenge for SNS. Interview with D-Generation X This interview was rescheduled for SNS. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. Booker T (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) An entertaining match, but Booker T was a little bit more on tonight, and scored the pinfall. Booker T d. Galaxy with a Harlem Sidekick. Will Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) Another outstanding match, and this one ended with Haynes pinning Ice after a moonsault. Will Haynes d. Ruger Ice with a moonsault. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) The Rock no-showed again, and it was announced he would be fired after another no-show. The Golden Child d. The Rock by forfeit. Triple Threat Intercontinental Championship Match Jack Reed (w/ The Hate Club) v. "The Rock" Grady Smith v. Haywood Jublome (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Intoxication X) Halfway through the match, some interesting occurances took place. *As Grady Smith is about to polish off Haywood Jublome and win the Intercontinental Championship, the lights go dark. The fans turn on their lighters, as customary when the lights go out. After a few minutes, two loud thumps are heard, and after a sixty second period, the lights come back on again. The fans are STUNNED when they see both Grady Smith and Jack Reed lying face down, unconscious in the ring, and Haywood Jublome standing in the middle of the ring, shocked. Mysterio, the EWA's enigma, is standing at the top of the entranceway, wearing all black, pointing at Jublome. He raises a microphone to speak to the crowd.* Mysterio: Jublome ... I just gave you the ball. Now run with it. * A large cloud of smoke appears under the JumboTron, and seconds later when the smoke clears, Mysterio is gone. * From there, Jublome tried pinning both men, but both just narrowly kicked out. The match went on for a few more minutes, and then Public Enemy came to ringside with a DX shirt. Swap Meet Louie began talking trash with Haywood, and then from behind, Grady Smith nailed the Choke Buster on Jublome, and defeated him. Grady Smith d. Haywood Jublome with the Choke Buster. Grady Smith is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion! Afterward... "The Rock" Grady Smith What the hell are you two doing here? The Rock doesn't need your help winning! (Both Ghetto and Louie start to bust out laughing, and as they do, they head on into the ring. They drop the shirt in the corner. While all this is going on, slowly Haywood is getting up, and the rest of Intoxication X (Will Haynes and Mayne Mann) come down to ringside to check on Haywood..) The Ghetto Boy: By the way it looks, it seems like you DO need our help... "The Rock" Grady Smith If The Rock needed your help, he'd beat it out of you! Swap Meet Louie: Oh yeah? *winks at Grady* You mean like this? (All of a sudden, all three members of I-X run towards PE and Grady, but PE charges at them, and Grady turns around, and all 6 men begin to pound the crap out of each other. Bodies are being thrown around the ring. Eventually, Ghetto, Louie, and Grady throw Haywood, Will, and Mayne out of the ring, and the crowd is going crazy. Louie goes over and pics up the microphone..) Swap Meet Louie: Like we said, you need our help. (grabs the shirt from the corner, and tosses it to Grady. Grady catches it and looks at it...) You know what to do... (Grady continues to look at the shirt, then he looks up at Ghetto and Louie, smiles, then throws on the shirt...) Swap Meet Louie: Copeland, we told you that PE was taking over control of D-X! If the New Age Outlaws were supposed to be a suprise, take a look at what we've just brought in! Road Dogg, Bad Ass, we got a lot of respect for ya, but remember, you're #2 on the D-X tag roster to the champs, the leaders of D-X, Public Enemy! (A No Limit Soldier re-mixed version of 'DeGenerate Me' begins to play on the PA, and the fans go wild as all three men head back to the locker room...) Handicap Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Hate Club) & "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) v. Prodigy At the beginning of the match, once Draven and Michaels were in the ring, Draven took off his new overcoat, which had been covering his face, to reveal a mask. Michaels began making fun of Draven's mask, and Draven casually lifted it up. Michaels stopped dead in his tracks, and quickly ran to the outside. Then, the shocker...once Prodigy was in the ring, Draven simply lied down and let him pin him. Gates and Michaels were furious, and Michaels prepared to go after Prodigy. But before he could get to Prodigy, out of nowhere, Raven appeared, and gave Michaels the Even Flow. He slid himself back in the corner, and stared at Prodigy. Then, he fell through the ropes, and left via the crowd. Draven, hellbent on revenge, picked Michaels up and triplepowerbombed him. Afterward, he took the microphone. (Draven adjusts the mask on his face, and begins to speak) "The ICON" Erik Draven: El Presidente, I'm gonna offer you a couple of words of advice. Do NOT try screwing with the Master Manipulator, Duane-O. Now you're probably going to come out here and start whining about how 'Erik Draven screwed Duane Gates', well...(Draven laughs) To borrow a term from a certain promoter up north, Erik Draven didn't screw Duane Gates, Duane Gates screwed Duane Gates...and Duane Gates forgot the K-Y. (Draven laughs again) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Gatesy, what're you gonna do next? Kane couldn't get the job done. Shawn Michaels couldn't get the job done. Sting couldn't get the job done. To borrow a term from a certain two-bit piece of *BLEEP* fed down south..."WHO'S NEXT!!!" The answer? Nobody. Nobody you have here can get the job done against the ICON. Nobody can take me out once and for all. And that kills ya, doesn't it, Gates? Hell, you turned your back on the one man that might stand a chance against Erik Draven, and I bet you're kicking yourself now, aren't ya? Too bad, so f'ing sad, son. All you've got now is one very pissed off ICON, and one very pissed off D-Generation X coming after your sorry ass, and..."You won't like us when we're angry..." (he chuckles, and the crowd pops) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Back to Kane for a sec...No response from him or Uncle Paul? Figures...his manhood did get torched, probably. Anyway, Kane...if you're listenin', I've got a little proposal for ya. Next Wednesday, in the Hell In A Cell...we add one little stipulation onto this match. Mask v. Mask...(he taps the mask) I win, the world gets to see your 'extra-crispy' face... You win, the world gets to peep the damage that Shawn Michaels and the Hate Club did to me... Are you game, son, or are you too afraid that the rest of your crew won't be able to help you in the cage? I'll be waiting... "The ICON" Erik Draven: And finally...Shawn f'ing Michaels. Mr. Backstabber himself. Do you think what happened tonight was the end of it for you and me? (he chuckles) Think again, Boy Toy... The ICON has not yet begun to fight... Too bad for you. Shawny, you made the flat-out biggest mistake in your pathetic excuse for a life when you slept with my fiance, and then lied to me for more than a year about it... And in November you finally pay for it, 'bro'... Be prepared. (Draven draws his thumb across his throat)
