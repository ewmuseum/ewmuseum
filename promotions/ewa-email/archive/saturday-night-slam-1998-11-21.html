EWA Saturday Night Slam

21 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 21, 1998 Live, from the Biloxi Grand Theatre Biloxi, Mississippi Jerry Spinelli v. A Mystery Opponent Jim Ross: Jerry Spinelli is in the ring, and now we await who the mystery opponent is... (The opening to Green Day's "Brain Stew" blares over the PA, and the crowd cheers as a familiar face appears in the entrance way...) Howard Finkle: His opponent, from Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing 221 pounds, making his return to the world of professional wrestling, Mikey Stephenson! Vincent Ashe: Well, I'll be damned! It's been awhile since we seen him! Stephenson seemed different from the wide-eyed rookie of the past, however, as he seemed more focused, determined, and serious. He walked to the ring, and quickly took out Spinelli, using not only his usual fortay of technical moves, but a couple of brawling style-moves as well. He finished him off with a brand new move, called the Hard Fall, and also known as the Stone Cold Stunner. Afterward, Spinelli had to be helped to the back. Mikey Stephenson d. Jerry Spinelli with the Hard Fall. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Thorne Richards Richards started off in advantage, surprisingly by going toe to toe in a slugfest with Ruger. Thorne then reverted to his normal technical wrestling style, with a drop toe hold into an anklelock. Thorne worked on the leg and knee of Ruger for a few minutes, before Ruger, with the crowd fully behind him, was able to power to his feet. Ruger began to work Thorne over, and was on the verge of defeating him when Thorne nailed him in the head with the cast (STILL not healed?) on his left arm. But as Thorne went for the pin, the bell rang, signifying that the ten minute time limit had expired. However, instead of taking both men out of the tournament, it was announced that both men would advance to the second round, and meet the winner of Prodigy/Mankind in a Three Way Dance. Thorne Richards and Ruger Ice battle to a time limit draw. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Mankind v. Prodigy Well folks, suffice it to say, this one didn't last too long. Mankind never was able to get any offense going, and Prodigy was able to finish him relatively quickly with a fire extinguisher to the head, and a brand new move: Charly. (tilt-the-world gutbuster) Prodigy d. Mankind with Charly. "ARE YOU READY?" (A large cheer from the crowd is heard as "D-Generate Me" blares over the PA. Out from the entrance ramp comes Shawn Michaels, who seems to be in a somber state of mind. He slowly walks to the ring...no pyro, no dancing, and most unusually, no smiles. He takes the microphone from Howard Finkle, and begins to speak.) Shawn Michaels: As you all know, you're basically looking into the ring at the biggest loser since that Spinelli guy got carted out of here earlier. I can't win a damn match if my life depended on it anymore. First I get smacked around by Vincent Jackson, then KG, then The Rock, and finally, I once again screw everything up, and boot Erik right in his head. It's obvious I'm not good enough for this sport anymore, so it's with great sadness that I... BREAK IT DOWN!! (Another huge response from the crowd goes up, as Triple-H, with Chyna at his side, comes storming to the ring, looking just a bit on the pissed-off side. Helmsley storms in the ring, and grabs the microphone from Michaels.) "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: Um...Shawn? Did I just hear you start in on ANOTHER retirement speech? (Michaels remains silent.) "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: Oh...so, in other words, you're just giving it up, right? Quitting on all the fans? Quitting on US? Quitting on DX as a whole? (Michaels turns away from Hunter, but Helmsley stops him.) "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: Michaels...this is gonna hurt you a HELL of a lot more than it's gonna hurt me...but WAKE UP. (Helmsley rears back, and levels Michaels with an open-handed slap to the side of the head.) (Michaels slowly looks up at Helmsley, and you can see the fury in his eyes as he grabs the microphone back.) Shawn Michaels: Don't *BLEEP* with me, Helmsley. "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: Or else what? You gonna quit again and run home to your mommy, cryin' about how the mean ol' Triple-H beat the living *BLEEP* outta you on live TV? Try a threat that actually MEANS something, Shawn. (Michaels starts to turn away, then quickly turns back and nails Hunter with a right.) "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: (Helmsley slowly gets back to his feet, a smirk on his face) Awwwww....did I piss poor little Shawny-Boy off? Shawn Michaels: You just don't know when to shut your bigass mouth, do you, Hunter? "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: You better believe it, Michaels. What I DO know is that I am SICK and *BLEEP*ing tired of you moping around, saying 'Pooooooooooor me. I suck. I don't have it anymore.' You should buy an f'ing tape recorder and TAPE it, Shawn...'cause you sure as hell say it often enough. Shawn Michaels: Yeah? Well, there's something I sure as hell don't say often enough to you, Helmsley...(Michaels throws an X-chop) SUCK IT!! (A slight smirk crosses Helmsley's face, and then he tackles Michaels down with a spear-like move, pummeling Michaels with lefts and rights, not giving him a chance to fight back. Michaels, enraged, manages to flip Helmsley off of him, and takes to his feet...Helmsley grins once again, and beckons Michaels to swing...sticking out his jaw, the whole works, and...OUCH. Right cross by HBK finds the mark. Somehow, your humble narrator does not think HHH was expecting that...and now Helmsley tackles Michaels back down, and...here comes DX to ringside! All of the troops are out, and they are just barely able to keep Michaels and Helmsley from each others' throats...and we cut to a commercial break.) First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner (w/ D-Generation X) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) You know, after seeing Jackson get defeated by Edge for his first EWA loss, and seeing Joshua Gardner make a solid debut here in the EWA, one would expect this to be a solid match. Not so, however, as Gardner found himself on his back more than Monica Lewinsky in this one. Jackson took all of 90 seconds to take out Gardner, polishing him off with, oddly enough, a powerbomb into a Sharpshooter. Later on, Jim Ross revealed that it was possibly a message to Erik Draven, as Draven used to use that move. Vincent Jackson d. Joshua Gardner by submission with the Sharpshooter. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (w/ The BMF) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) This match had a lot of bad blood in it, and it showed, as Venom attacked Helmsley from behind during his entrance. It was a full five minutes on the outside before the two men finally took it inside the ring, and by then, Helmsley's forehead was lacerated. The match continued to be an all-out brawl inside the ring, and apparently the referee wasn't about to disqualify anyone, because Venom went to work with a chair. However, when Venom laid the chair on Helmsley's face, and went to the top for a legdrop, Helmsley quickly got up, and cracked Venom right in the head, causing him to topple over. Helmsley signaled for the Pedigree, but in a revisit from earlier in the night, Shawn Michaels sprinted to the ring, and hit Helmsley with a clothesline. The two began to fight it out once again, until Venom got up, picked up the chair, and swung for Helmsley, instead hitting Michaels. But instead of going after Venom, Helmsley went for Michaels, and finally DX ran out and separated the two for the second time in the night. Hunter Hearst Helmsley d. Venom by disqualification. "The Extremist" Erik Moon (w/ D-Generation X) v. Galaxy Worst match on the card? Probably. Moon looked horrendous, and Galaxy didn't look much better. However, he was able to escape with a victory after a rollup. Galaxy d. Erik Moon with a rollup. **{ After another commercial the crowd is just getting back to their seats and are wondering when something good is going to happen after that horrible Moon versus Galaxy match. Then over the PA "Momma Said Knock You Out" blares over the PA. The crowd errupts for the arrival of "Main Event" Terry Conklin. Then Terry and the lovely Jocelyn come from backstage. Terry is wearing a "I'm a Conklimaniac" t-shirt, and a pair of black adidas pants. Jocelyn is wearing what looks to be just Terry's boxing robe leaving much to the imagination. The pyros fire as Terry and Jocelyn enter the ring. Terry gets in the ring and gets thrown a microphone from a stagehand.}** Conklin:" Man is it good to be back. (crowd cheers) I know that all of you Conklimaniacs out there have been waiting for "Mr. Overhand Right" to return to the EWA after a legnthy vacation. Now that I am back there is no stopping me. Just ask senor Edge how it felt to get hit with the most dominating move in wrestling today, the OVERHAND RIGHT. That's right, it was me that came in and ruined the little party for Edge and the rest of the Freak Show. You thought that you were going to be the next United States champion, and you were, but I bet you wern't counting on "The Main Event" comming in and reigning on your parade. You know that you arn't the true champion...because you beat noone. Face it, the time that you have been here there has been no competition in the EWA, until the upper eschelon of superstars, myself encluded, started making their returns. While we have been gone people like you have tried to make a name for yourselves in the EWA, but now I'm back to show all the glorified jobbers like yourself that you havn't faced anything until you have come up aginst Terry Conklin. So here is what I propose....December fifth in the O-rena in Orlando, Flordia for the Clash of the Champions eight, put your belt on the line and I will show you what living on the edge is all about." (Conklin exits to a cheer from the crowd.) Morbid Angel v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (w/ The BMF) Morbid Angel pretty much looked morbid during this one, as Canuck was able to get a quick win, albeit in an unimpressive fashion. Crazy Canuck d. Morbid Angel with the Canadiansault. (President Gates walks out to the ring, with a mild reception from the crowd. He steps into the ring, and takes the microphone.) President Gates: Ladies and gentlemen, last week, I came out here and told you all that I was a changed man, and am going to run this federation fairly and honestly. Well, I've hired someone to help me do that. I've hired a brand new EWA Comissioner. This man is a man of honor, of integrity, and a man who I know will make the right decisions. He is a man who is used to being in a high position of authority. Ladies and gentlemen, I give you the new EWA Comissioner...I give you THE PRINCE!! ("In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" plays and the crowd begins to boo. The Prince makes his way through the entranceway, in his wrestling gear, and the boos intensify, with the occassional projectile being sent his way. The Prince gets to the ring and climbs in. President Gates approaches him and extends his hand, but The Prince ignores it and takes the microphone out of his hand.) The Prince : In my tenure here in the EWA many have said that things have happened unfairly. Some say that the wrestlers in this federation have been under the thumb of President Gates. They whine and complain about their lot in this federation like the little girls that they are, and Gates just ground his thumb even further. But now that he's returned as a namby-pamby spineless slug, it's time for someone with a bit of integrity, a bit of honour, and a whole lot of class to enter the fray The Prince : Now I know you'll be wondering what I will be doing now that I am in the position of power in this federation. Will I abuse it and use it to my advanatage. Obviously if you think that, you are severely lacking in the department of cognitive response because I need no power of position to do that. But will I keep wrestlers under my thumb, like I do the other plebeans that I control. (The Prince chuckles slightly) You had better believe that nothing will change. Things will continue to be run tight and there will be nothing that will escape my control. Anyone who steps out of line.........High Society will straighten you out The Prince : Now, enough of the pleasantries. I do have business to take care of as I am the working comissioner. Now if you please, as my first order.......... (The Prince pauses) bring out the blue collar stiff. "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. The Prince Modest started off the match in impressive fashion, by going to work with a series of suplexes on the new EWA Comissioner. He then took to the mat, and began an attempt to wear The Prince down with a Boston Crab. The Prince, however, was able to get to the ropes. Modest then picked him up, and attempted a vertical suplex, but The Prince flipped around and caught him with a German suplex. From there, The Prince cold and calculating, methodically began to inflict damage onto Modest, finally polishing him off with High Society, as the EWA's newest head honcho remains undefeated and unblemished. The Prince d. Michael Modest by submission with High Society. EWA World Television Championship Match The Rock v. Gangrel (w/ The Kindred) This match didn't last long at all before The Kindred attempted to interfere on the behalf of Gangrel. Christian began to distract the referee, and Edge climbed in the ring with a chair, and aimed it straight at The Rock's head...however, The People's Champ ducked, and Edge cracked Gangrel with the chair. From there, Rock planted Gangrel with The Rock Bottom, executed "the most electrifying move in sports entertainment", The People's Elbow, and got the three count to a pop from the crowd. The Rock d. Gangrel with The People's Elbow. The Rock is the new EWA World Television Champion! "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ D-Generation X) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) This was a fairly short brawl of a match. The two giants went toe-to-toe, with neither man being able to take the other off his feet. Finally, as Jackson was attempting a running lariat, Nash hit him with the Big Boot. Jackson staggered...but stayed up. Nash grabbed him by the head, and went for the JackKnife, but Jackson backbodydropped Nash. Jackson then walked over to pick Nash up, but literally out of nowhere, Erik Draven appeared on the outside. Draven grabbed Jackson by the legs, pulled him out of the ring, and began wailing away. The two fought all the way to the back, as you can probably guess. Kevin Nash d. Vincent Jackson by countout. Semifinal EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ The BMF) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) The last time these two met was nearly six months ago, right before EWA Summer Breakout '98. This time was more of the same high strung action, as the two men went head-to-head in an all out war. What Richards lacked in size, he made up for in quickness and agressiveness. He started the match in control with the old 'stick and move' tatic, until Draven was able to catch one of the 'sticks'. Draven then turned the match into a slowpaced brawl, with him using his power to his full advantage. Draven seemed to have the match won with a thunderous powerbomb, but amazingly, Richards kicked out. Seemingly frustrated, Draven picked The King Of Swing up and locked on a sleeperhold, attempting to put Richards out. But just inches before his arm hit the ground for the third time, Richards rose it up, and began pumping his fist in the air. The crowd, who were essentially cheering for both men, really got into it as Stevie struggled to his feet, and threw a couple of elbows into his sternum. Richards then hit the ropes, and when he bounced back, Draven went for a big boot. Richards ducked, though, and Draven hit the referee. On the way back, Draven caught Richards into the DX Driver, and nailed it. However, as Draven was going for the pin, the lights went out. "Wherever I May Roam" blared over the PA, and Vincent Jackson made his usual entrance. Jackson came to the ring, and wasted no time in going to work on Draven, slugging the already weary ICON over into the corner, and executing the Fallen Angel. Richards was still out cold, and as Jackson left, he slowly began to get to his feet. Draven, miraculously, did also, but Richards was prepared, and, oblivious to what had happened with Jackson, nailed him with the Stevie Kick. He crawled on top of him, and the ref, regaining consciousness, slowly counted to three. Stevie Richards d. Erik Draven with the Stevie Kick. Semifinal EWA World Heavyweight Championship Triple Threat Match The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ The Triple Threat) This was a very unusual match, which never really got off of the ground. Bunda made his entrance first, followed by The Golden One, who looked focused and determined to win. They were followed by Shawn Copeland, who was carrying a chair into the ring. Halfway down the aisle, Copeland sprinted to the ring, and slid under. As the bell rang, Copeland raised the chair to hit The Golden One, but changed direction halfway through and blasted Bunda with it. The referee immediately disqualified Copeland. The Golden One, perplexed, simply looked at Bunda on the ground for a good 15 seconds, and then simply shrugged and went for the pin. The referee, confused as anyone else, made the three count. ****After the "Chair Shot heard 'Round the World", and The Golden One has already won the match, Copeland slides out of the ring and almost completely down the aisleway. He stops before he gets to the exit and grabs a mic from a nearby attendant...**** Copeland:"Both of you stupid sons of b*tches can kiss my ass. You want to play "who has the biggest ego"? Well, it's all good for me, because I can go somewhere where the people can handle having a "Money-Maker" in their ranks. Like it....love it...loathe it...the Triple Threat is dead and gone, and there's a new empire in town. Now, ****Copeland stops in midsentence, and three words blare over the PA...**** ARE YOU READY??? (The crowd erupts as DX makes their re-entrance into the arena...nobody is looking happy in the least, however. Draven is favoring his back, Michaels and Helmsley are staying far away from each other...nobody's very happy. Draven stops and stares down at Shawn Copeland, a slight smirk on his face.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: I've been waiting a LONG time for this, kid. (Draven raises his fist back, smirks once again...and hugs Copeland. The crowd erupts once again as Kevin Nash hands Copeland a DX shirt, and the Money-Maker quickly puts it on. Draven nods, grins, and the whole of DX rushes the ring, surrounding it, and TGO and Bunda within. Copeland begins to climb the ring apron, but suddenly, Prodigy runs out, and drags him back down. He's followed by all of the BMF, and a brawl quickly develops. Venom hits the ring, and goes after Bunda, and those two begin to brawl it out inside. Justin Hicks runs to the ring, and a mass brawl develops between The Triple Threat, The Golden One, The BMF, and D-Generation X as the camera fades to black.)
