EWA Wednesday Night War

25 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 25, 1998 Live, from the Cobb County Civic Center Cobb County, Georgia "The Extremist" Erik Moon (w/ D-Generation X) v. Galaxy (w/ Precious) Well...this match had all the excitement of Galaxy's last match. Exactly the same as watching a snail run...er...crawl uphill in the middle of the winter. The match dragged on for about five minutes, until Erik Draven slowly walked down to the ring, presumably to attack Galaxy...he stepped up onto the apron, stepped over the top rope, and looked back and forth between Moon and Galaxy...and then proceeded to chokeslam both at the same time. He mockingly covered both unconscious men, grinned, then spit a Bret Hart-ish glob of phlegm on Moon...and thus, one can only assume that, well...Erik Moon just got his pink slip from DX. *** Erik Moon v. Galaxy ends in a no-contest. The Black Angels v. Da Bomb (w/ Jim Rome) Ladies and gentlemen...a miracle happened tonight. Don't think your eyes are failing you...the Black Angels WON a match. Everybody's favorite punching bags managed to score the upset of the decade when Satchiel rolled up Van Smack from behind as he and Deke were having a conference over, well...something. Who knows? Bottom line is...The Black Angels have a one-match winning streak!!! *** Black Angels d. Da Bomb via Satchiel's rollup of Van Smack. Joe Lemon v. "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) Yawnfest '98 continues, as Conklin puts out a rather sloppily-worked match for the W...Even your humble narrator, No-Doz in hand couldn't manage to stay awake through those five minutes, and woke up in time to see Conklin knock Lemon's lights out with the Overhand Right...and honestly? Your humble narrator is glad he stayed asleep through the match. *** Terry Conklin d. Joe Lemon via the Overhand Right. Morbid Angel v. Mikey Stephenson The rookies both missed their rides to the arena, and thus...we had our first double no-show of the evening. The crowd was 'treated'...and I use the term loosely, to President Gates coming out to hype the crowd...and getting smacked dead in the face by a full cup of beer. And short of Erik Draven's entrance earlier, this got the biggest pop of the night to this point. Non Title Match "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner v. The Rock (EWA World Television Champion) One word. Smackdown. BIG smackdown. But...not by the Rock. Halfway through the match, the Rock was about to drop the People's Elbow on Gardner, but Triple-H came down to ringside, and grabbed the Rock's foot, preventing him from dropping 'The Most Electrifying Move in Sports Entertainment.' HHH rolled under the ropes, but instead of attacking the Rock, he dropped Gardner with a Pedigree...all the while, Chyna had the ref distracted in the opposite corner of the ring, so of course, said ref saw none of this...the Rock quickly dropped the People's Elbow, and that was all she wrote for Gardner...casualty #2 in the DX Cleanup this evening. *** The Rock d. Joshua Gardner via the People's Elbow. Six Man Tag Team Match Joe Lemon, James Mastin, & Chris Craft v. The Kindred (Gangrel, Edge, & Christian) Ladies and gentlemen...your humble narrator is stunned. The upsets continued this evening, as the Kindred got defeated by the three journeymen for Miracle #2 of the night. However, the match wasn't without its' odd circumstances...Neither Edge nor Gangrel would allow Christian to tag out, instead watching as he got pummeled mercilessly by the three journeymen...and just when Edge and Gangrel got into the ring...instead of jumping on the three jabronies, they dragged Christian to the top rope, and delivered a double Downward Spiral from the top. At Gangrel's ordering, Edge slung Christian over his shoulder and left the ring, giving the three jabrones the countout victory. *** The Three Jabronies d. The Kindred via countout. Michael Modest & Thorne Richards v. "The ICON" Erik Draven & "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ D-Generation X) Had things gone just a bit different, your humble narrator gets the feeling that we would've seen a third man washed out of DX tonight... Richards and Modest simply pummeled Shawn Copeland...Cope could hit nothing right, even managing to miss a simple kick to the midsection...instead managing to kick the steel ringpost, and well...we won't talk about how embarrassing that was for him. Instead, we'll comment on how Thorne Richards seized the advantage to clip his other leg with the cast, and continue to wreck Copeland with it while Modest distracted the ref...Richards rolled Copeland back in, Overdose, goodnight. Draven simply shook his head, and walked away from the ring, leaving an unconscious Shawn Copeland behind. *** Richards/Modest d. DX via Richards' Overdose on Copeland. Semifinal Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X & Chyna) Triple-H opened this match up with a little surprise for Vincent...He gave an 'nWo-style point' toward the curtain, and Erik Draven made a third appearance, much to the crowd's delight. Given his previous loss, the ICON did not look like a happy camper...and that ended up factoring into this match a great deal. In the ring, the match went back and forth between Helmsley using his speed advantage, and Jackson's overpowering brute strength. Finally, Helmsley got caught on the wrong end of a Jackson Tombstone, but instead of VJ going for the pin, he exitted the ring and stared Draven down...giving Chyna the time to come up behind VJ and crotch Jackson hard. Unfortunately for Chyna, however...VJ wasn't fazed by it. At all. (new meaning to Balls of Steel, I suppose) VJ turned, and was about to chokeslam Chyna, but Draven locked a triangle chokehold on him, forcing him to release the 9th Wonder of the World. Draven tossed Jackson back in, only to get crotched himself by Christina Knight...Draven went down hard, and as Chyna was pursuing Christina, Vincent delivered a huge gutwrench powerbomb in the center of the ring...JR mentioned on the broadcast that once again, Vincent appeared to be making a statement to Erik Draven, this time using his old 'Final Justice' finisher...Jackson went for the cover, but got pulled out of the ring by Draven...earning a DQ Victory in the process, as the ref saw Draven's interference. The two men pummeled each other all the way back to the locker room once again. *** Vincent Jackson d. Triple-H via DQ. *Before the Shawn Michaels, Venom match, "Ghetto Superstar" by Pras blares over the PA system and KG comes out from the back. He stands on the ramp and speaks* "Outlaw Immortal" KG: CUT THE F*CKIN' MUSIC. Ya'll ingrates should respect me. I not only have one four of the titles here in the EWA, but I have the most wins out of all the wrestlers. The wrestlers should give me respect. I don't get any f*cking respect from anyone. Well, that's going to change starting Saturday night. Whoever it is I face Saturday night, I'm going to end their career. It doesn't matter if it's a superstar or some jabronie. I'll kick the living sh*t out of them. So starting now, I'm not going to take any sh*t from anyone. KG has changed, and he's gonna take it out on all the b*tches in the EWA. 'Nuff said. *KG walks back to his locker room and WNW continues* "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (w/ The BMF) Michaels emerged to his old 'Sexy Boy' music, all by himself...no DX. He stood around in the ring for a few moments, started to hit his split-stretch-flex, then stopped, mouthed '*BLEEP* it', and went and reclined on the top rope...The ring announcer then announced that Venom had taken a leave of absence from the EWA, thus HBK wins by forfeit. Triple-H and Chyna were standing up at the top of the ramp, Helmsley still in bad shape from his previous match, but not bad enough to refrain from running to the ring and attacking Michaels again... We cut to a commercial break as the executives are trying to separate the two men. *** Shawn Michaels d. Venom via forfeit. Semifinal Intercontinental Triple Threat Match Prodigy (w/ The BMF) v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Thorne Richards The growing rivalry between Ruger Ice and Prodigy took a large step up in intensity tonight...Ruger came out looking like he had something to prove, and he was hell-bent to show that Prodigy was not going to stand in his way of taking the IC title. The insane one, however, had a much different point of view on things, thus making for one of the best matches on the card. Thorne Richards looked to be in considerably different form than earlier in the card...his eyes were glazed over, and well...Thorne was feeling no pain. Or feeling any brain cells firing, for that matter...Richards was way out in left field for the outset of the match, but seemed to come back to life after getting DDT'ed by Prodigy... Richards snapped, swinging the cast at everything and anything in sight...unfortunately clobbering the ref in the process. Surprisingly, though...nothing out-of-hand went on. Just the three men basically beating the living hell out of each other, with no care about what happened to them in the process. Thus, after about ten minutes, the three combatants were essentially dead on their feet. Amazingly, they had lost none of the fire, and that would prove to be the undoing of Thorne Richards, in the end. Thorne was in the middle of another 'berserker rage', hitting -anything- that moved with that cast...unfortunately, he didn't see Ruger Ice sneak up behind him, and drop him with the Spinebuster. Ruger went for the cover, but had the pin broken up by Prodigy. With Richards unconscious, Ruger and Prodigy squared off in the center of the ring...each daring the other to make the first move. Prodigy finally charged...and also found himself on the wrong end of a Spinebuster...right on to the prone form of Thorne Richards. Needless to say, Richards did not need a stomach pump this evening...Ruger covered both men, and scored a HUGE victory, quite possibly the biggest in his career. *** Ruger Ice d. Richards/Prodigy via two Spinebusters. Match to determine the new EWA World Tag Team Champions The Extreme Dream Machine v. The Outsiders (w/ D-Generation X) Ladies and gentlemen, it is with deep regret that your narrator writes this summary...we may have seen the end of one of the most storied careers in e-wrestling history. Bunda and Hicks were dominating the match for most of the time, but...Nash finally managed to turn the tide back in the direction of DX...for a moment. He made a comment toward the camera about Vincent Jackson, and as if on cue, the giant made his way down toward the ring...Hall was outside the ring, and slowly walked out to confront Jackson...Jackson spear-tackled Hall, and...it still makes me sick to think about this, my friends. Jackson physically bent Hall's leg backward. The snap was audible throughout the arena...Hall tried struggling to his feet, but obviously was in no shape to do so. A slight smirk crossed what little of Jackson's face was visible, and the giant stalked to the ring to continue his carnage...Christina Knight distracted the ref while Jackson pulled Nash out of the ring and started pummeling him...meanwhile, Bunda and Hicks ran out of the ring, grabbed Hall, and physically dragged him into the ring...Bunda applied a Figure-Four Leglock, and Hall wasted no time in giving up...however, Bunda refused to let go, and had to be dragged off of Hall by the referee. No word on how serious Hall's injury is, but it appears he'll be on the shelf for a while. *** EDM d. The Outsiders via Bunda's Figure-Four on Hall. *** The Extreme Dream Machine are the new EWA World Tag Champions. Three Way Dance to determine the new EWA European Champion Dude Love (w/ The Dudettes) v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (w/ The BMF) v. The Prince (EWA Comissioner, w/ His Ego) The 'Main Event' turned out to be one of the bigger disappointments in recent memory...paling in comparison to only the Bunda/TGO/Copeland match. Canuck and the Dudester neither seemed to be all too enthused about the match, and neither got much of a crowd reaction as a result. The Prince was showered with his normal parade of boos, but unlike the other two, showed a lot of skill and effort. It was only a matter of time, as The Prince systematically forced the other two men to submit, both to High Society. ***The Prince d. Dude Love/Crazy Canuck with High Society. The Prince is the new EWA European Champion.
