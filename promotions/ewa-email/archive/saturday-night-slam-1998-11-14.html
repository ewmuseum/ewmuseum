EWA Saturday Night Slam

14 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 14, 1998 Live, from the Continental Airlines Arena East Rutherford, New Jersey ***NOTE: The matches that are double noshows have been taken off of the card. It's taken a long time, and a few bumps on the head by Prodigy, but I've finally realized just how wrong I've been lately. The things I've done to wrestlers, and the way I've treated both Prodigy and DX, among others, have been ridiculous, and I am embarassed by the way I have acted. I'm out here tonight to announce that you will no longer see the biased President Gates. I'm going to work my damndest to turn things here around. Starting off is an announcement that is going to make some people very angry. However, I think that it is best for the EWA, and will generate both excitement and competiton around the EWA. As of this moment, I am vacating all EWA Championships. Various matches and tournaments will be held to decide the new champions, and all current titleholders will be included in the various matches for their belts. President Gates: So let me get this straight. You want every titleholder to receive a bye into the finals of every tournament? First of all, Erik, not every title is going to be decided by a tournament. That'd get a little boring. And second of all, no way. President Gates: Shows how far free security gets you.... "The ICON" Erik Draven: Hmmm...let's see. We've got...one fat-assed president out there, and...(he counts) Four of us in here...doesn't it seem like somebody's, well...missing? (Gates makes an exaggerated ' gulp' in the background) Oh yeah...Scott? Kev? Come on down. That you have 'our best interests' in mind? President Gates: X-Pac was never fired, Draven...the contract that he signed ended up disagreeing with our original deal. I offered him another, but he refused....and what makes you think 4 Firestarters wasn't enou gh to wake me up? "The ICON" Erik Draven: You want a list? That's my point. I'm not concerning myself with the past anymore...we all make mistakes, and I made a good many of them. "The ICON" Erik Draven: You're a master of the obvious, you piece of trailer trash...(Draven shakes his head...he reaches down, picks up his World Title, and slings it over his shoulder) Are you aware of just ho w hard I worked for this strap, Duaney? No, of course you're not. You were too busy trying to *BLEEP* me over to notice. President Gates: No, but I can, and am, going to do something about the World Title, Erik. Don't you think you're going to have the opportunity to re-earn it? "The ICON" Erik Draven: Re-earn what I already have, Duaney? Do you have to work forty hours to get your paycheck printed, and then another forty hours to get the damn thing? President Gates: Erik, like it or not, this is how it's going to be. "The ICON" Erik Draven: El Presidente is fighting his own fight for once...color me impressed. (Draven rolls his eyes) Consider this a warning, 'Duane-O'...you try anything to screw ANY of the EWA roster over...and you're a dead man. There are five men and one Ninth Wonder of the World that'll see to that. But, Duaney? You're not getting the strap back. If I lose in your precious tournament, then we'll see...but as far as I'm concerned, Erik Draven is STILL the goddamn World Heavyweight Champion. (the crowd cheers) And they seem to agree with me, Gates...think twice before f'ing with me next time, or else, well...your public may decide that the EWA just isn't the *BLEEP* anym ore...(Draven straps his World Title back around his waist, and grins) And if ya don't like it, Duaney...we've got two words for ya. (he holds the mic out to the crowd, who respond with a HUGE SUCK IT!!!) T he People have spoken, chump. (Draven grins at President Gates, and waves 'buh-bye' to him...with one finger on each hand.) President Gates: Hate me if you will, Erik, but you've made the right choice. (Gates turns, and exits...as he does, Nash reaches over and stops him. Gates gulps, but Nash only ruffles his hair. Gates sighs in re lief, and starts to walk off again, but Hall grabs him by the back of his pants...and...well...you get the idea.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Gates? Erik Draven is walkin g out of that tournament still in posession of his World Heavyweight Title...and there isn't a DAMN thing anybody can do about it. Not Vincent Jackson. Not the Crock. NOBODY. Hit the damn music. ('D-Generate Me' blares over the PA as DX leaves to another thunderous pop from the crowd...cut to commercial.) Ruger Ice vs. The Godfather Ruger "The Hitman" Ice? You've got to wonder if he'll adopt this nickname, because for the second time in the night, Ruger's Spinebuster injured a man, this time the Pimp of the EWA. Ruger continues to roll through his opponents here in the EWA, and picks up another win. Ruger Ice d. The Godfather with the Spinebuster. “The Heartbreak Kid” Shawn Micheals vs. “The Rock” Rocky Maivia Michaels was escorted to the ring by Chyna and Triple-H, who seemed to take a 'special' interest in the People's Champ. But for all the hype that this match seemed to have, it ended up being a total squash, as The Rock literally laid the smackdown on Michaels. The crowd gave a nice pop for the People's Elbow, and The Rock rewarded them afterward by making Michaels hit Rock Bottom. (As the Rock has his hand raised in victory, Chyna sneaks up behind him, and...well...makes him a prime candidate for the Vienna Boys Choir, if you smell what the narrator's cooking. The Rock doubles over in pain, and Triple-H seizes the opportuni ty to deliver a nasty-looking Pedigree onto the People's Champion. He picks up a mic, and simply says...) "The Cornerstone" Triple-H: Challenge accepted, nimrod...next Saturday. Bring the People's Eyebrow and the People's Elbow, 'cause the Cornerstone is going to bring the People's *BLEEP*, and that, my friend, is all(Helmsley grins, and tosses the mic down on the prone form of the Rock.) The Rock d. Shawn Michaels with the Rock Bottom. Jerry Spinelli vs. Joe Lemon Even I, your faithful unbiased card narrator, have to admit I got a good laugh out of Spinelli. On his way inside the ring, he tripped over the bottom rope, and fell flat on his face, causing his pocket protector to spill out of his shirt. He quickly grabbed it as if it were a pot of gold, and stuffed it back in his shirt. However, he did show a little bit of athletic ability, and easily defeated "Mr. Lemonhead". Afterward, he grabbed a microphone, and proclaimed himself to be "King Nerd". Jerry Spinelli d. Joe Lemon with the Spinelli Special. Deader vs. Edge Edge had no trouble with Deader in this one, and quickly finished him with the Downward Spiral. The interesting occurance was afterward, when Gangrel made his way to the ring. Gangrel walked over to Edge, glared at him in the eye...and then proceeded to pick Deader up and execute the Piledriving DDT. [After Gangrel DDTs Deader, the crowd is filled with silence. Gangrel rises up, and stares into the eyes of Edge, who no longer appears tired, but alert and wide awake. Gangrel is handed a microphone by Christian, who has entered the ring with him.] Gangrel: I trust the conversion is complete, brother? Edge: For too long...for too long, I have felt obligated to protect these pathetic, short-lived wastes of blood...these cattle. [Edge shakes his head, and chuckles] Tonight, it all ends...each and every one of you pathetic mortals shall be hunted...and we shall feed. Our Lord's dream becomes a fact that NONE of you can evade...Death walks among you, mortals...[Edge chuckles] The final hunt has begun... Gangrel: As all you pathetic mortals can witness, my brother's conversion is complete! Now, he is one like myself, like Christian, and reuinted with his long lost love, Devon. And now, together, the four of us...The Kindred...hunt. All mortals beware, for forever is eternal, and we are forever! Edge: It has begun, EWA...prepare yourselves for the end...[He smiles widely, revealed blood-stained fangs and teeth...evidently, Edge has fed recently...yet the bloodlust remains in his eyes. The demonic smile remains, and Gangrel's theme music blares over the PA once again. The group leaves, and we are taken to a production video of Edge and Gangrel's previous feud, and Edge's loss at the last pay-per-view.] Edge d. Deader with the Downward Spiral. Mick Foley vs. James Mastin This time, Foley was under the Mankind persona. Mastin seemed to not back down from Mankind, but it didn't matter, as Mankind dominated him. The fans popped loudly at the end of the match, when Mankind pulled out "Mr. Socko", placed him on his hand, and gave Mastin the Mandible Claw. Mankind d. James Mastin with the Mandible Claw. The Prince vs. KG (Non title match) The Prince was nowhere to be found, so KG picked up a forfeit victory. Thorne Richards vs. Vincent Jackson This was easily Jackson's greatest challenge since he arrived into the EWA. Thorne was able to work on the legs of the big man, and wear him down somewhat with some submission moves. However, Jackson was still simply too much, and after a good fight, was able to get the Fallen Angel and the victory. Vincent Jackson d. Thorne Richards with the Fallen Angel.
