EWA Thursday Night Terror

5 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 5, 1998 Live, from Orlando Arena, Orlando, Florida. Stevie Ray (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Paul Burnett First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The Dynasty (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The New Age Outlaws v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) Nero v. Hannibal Manson EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Thug Warriors) EWA European Championship Match Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. Augustus Non-Title Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) ***NOTE: If The Pessimist wins, he will recieve a U.S. Heavyweight Title shot. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) v. Ahmed Johnson/Booker T (w/ The Thug Warriors) Le Tigre v. The Widow Maker EWA World Television Championship Match Eclipse v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Stevie Ray (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Paul Burnett Burnett didn't show up for this match, and as a result, was fired from the EWA. Stevie Ray d. Paul Burnett by forfeit. First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The Dynasty (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) Shawn Michaels and Erik Draven were way off their game tonight. Perhaps the re-emergence of WormBoy brought back painful memories...in any event, this was all Reckoning. The Reckoning d. The Dynasty with Leon Snow's pin on Shawn Michaels after an avalanche elbowdrop. (6:13) First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The New Age Outlaws v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) Another squash, as FlashBack destroyed The New Age Outlaws, and what would be the beginning of a long night for Degeneration X. FlashBack d. The NAO with Flash Funk's pin on "Badd Ass" Billy Gunn after the Funky Flash Splash. (3:45) First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) DDT once again didn't show up for their match. Death Sentance d. DDT by forfeit. First Round: EWA U.S. Tag Team Title Tournament The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) In the third squash of the tournament, The Azzkickers completely destroyed The Outsiders. Deakins and Goldberg looked very intense through this one. The Azzkickers d. The Outsiders with Bill Goldberg's pin on Scott Hall after the Jackhammer. (4:14) Nero v. Hannibal Manson Nero took the early advantage in this matchup, using his mind games to his advantage, but Hannibal soon turned the tide, and amazingly, used the Nero Bomb on Nero. Hannibal Manson d. Nero with the Nero Bomb. (8:18) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Thug Warriors) Alex seemed a little sluggish throughout this match, and Smirt capitalized, punishing the German. Smirt eventually locked on the Thug Breaker, and Wright had no choice but to tap out. SmirtDogg d. Alex Wright by submission with the Thug Breaker. (7:31) SmirtDogg retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Title. EWA European Championship Match Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) This was a terrific matchup, and was dominated by the power of Deathstar at the start. Deathstar used his huge size advantage over KG, and pummled him to the ground. Deathstar, at one point, locked KG in a bearhug, but with a rake to the eyes, KG escaped, and the momentum shifter. Then, in a shocking development, Precious and Sunny both handed their men foreign objects; Precious a pair of brass knuckles, and Sunny her high heel. Both men began using the objects on each other, and the offical was forced to throw the match out. KG and Deathstar battled to a double disqualification. (11:29) KG retains the EWA European Title. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. Augustus This contest never happened, as both men began brawling on the outside. The match was ruled a no contest. Non-Title Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) ***NOTE: If The Pessimist wins, he will recieve a U.S. Heavyweight Title shot. The Pessimist was out to prove something tonight, and for the first 10 minutes of the match, he proved he could run with GC. But the new DX member used his new cheating ways to swerve the advantage to his side, hitting a low blow, and then nailing the DX Destroyer. The Golden Child d. The Pessimist with the DX Destroyer. (10:51) Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) v. Ahmed Johnson/Booker T (w/ The Thug Warriors) Bloodsport made their way to the ring first, but once they got there, the lights went out. Organ music sounded, and a tremendous pyrotechnics display occured as Kane and Paul Bearer made their way to the ring. Wrath and Mortis attempted to fight back, but Kane laid them both out with Tombstones, and then left the area. This match was ruled a no contest. Le Tigre v. The Widow Maker This match was dominated by Le Tigre, who had the crowd behind him throughout the match. It was announced during the match that Le Tigre will get his Light Heavyweight Title shot, as promised by SmirtDogg, at MNM. Le Tigre d. The Widow Maker with the Tiger Bite. (2:34) EWA World Television Championship Match Eclipse v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Austin came down to the ring in a foul mood, and announced that "he didn't give a rat's ass about the TV Title, because the only thing he cares about is taking the World Title from that stupid piece of trash Erik Draven." Austin then went on to forfeit the title to Eclipse, and then gave both Eclipse and the referee Stone Cold Stunners. Eclipse d. Steve Austin by forfeit. Eclipse is the new EWA World Television Champion!!
