Ewa Wnw (4-07-99) April 7, 1999 Live from the Marine Midland Arena (Buffalo, New York) ---- Sir Francis Drake vs Joe Lemon Paksta vs Cain The match began with Cain holding the opening advantage, he chose to try to wear Paksta down with a submission style of wrestling. He placed Paksta in several holds including: an armbar, a camel clutch, a boston crab and a cross-face-chicken-wing. This strategy seemed to work as it kept Paksta grounded and seemed to slow him down as well Crowd begins to boo. here come Paksta's manager to ringside. He is carrying a singapore cane in his hand. Paksta throws Cain into the ropes and is met by the Singapore Cane across the back of the head. Vegas Studd jumps off the ring and walks back to the locker room Paksta took advantage of the situation with a ddt off the ropes that seemed to take the wind out of Kain's sails. Paksta followed up by clothesline-ing his man outside the ring and Leveling him with three vicious chairshots to the head. Paksta tossed his man back in the ring and as he came back in, he was met with a dropkick to the jaw. The match see-sawed for several minutes until Paksta hit a powerslam, followed by a backdrop, but he telegraphed the maneuver and Cain hit a ddt out of no-where and was able to get the win, with the aid of the ring ropes, which the ref didnt see. Cain d. Paksta w/ a DDT The Twisted Souls [with The aWo]v.Judgement Day The heat that had been building for this match since Judgement day quit the aWo was very evident tonight and none of the Ewa fans who watched this match were dissapointed in the results Black Steel and Lunatikk Crippler were the first two men in the ring. They exchanged in-ring pleasantries as they exchanged forearm shivers, hiptosses and dropkicks, Steel re-introduced himself to Crippler with a hard right hand which flattened Crippler. The Crippler went down like a rock, and Steel got a head of steam and dropped an elbow, however there was noone home on this one and Crippler sprung to his feet quickly and started laying the boots to the head of Steel Crippler made a tag and tagged in Aramund Fury. Fury quickly picked up his man and sent him for the ride and executed a backdrop followed by a hotshot. Fury, looking to continue the momentum scaled the ropes and went for a Flying Dropkick from the top rope, but Steel side-stepped the manuever and then executed a backbreaker, followed by a snap suplex, before tagging in Ministry Once, Ministry was tagged in he was a house of fire. Nailing lefts and right and a gutwrench suplex on Fury, before a Flying Axhandle from the top rope. But he allowed Fury to get too close to the ropes and Fury was able to tag in Crippler and then double clothes-lined Ministry and as the ref was making sure Fury got back where he was supposed to be, it allowed Crippler to pull something out of his tights and he Leveled Ministry with it, which immediatly brought in Steel. All four men were in the ring and the ref has been knocked out and it goes back and forth for several minutes until Cripplerr has the crippler crossface locked in as Fury is being pummeled on the outside by Judgement Day. The ref gets up slowly and tries to clear the cobwebs. He walks over and starts to ask Ministry if he quits, however there is no response. The ref lifts his hand up once, twice and it starts to fall for the third time as the bell rings signaling a time limit draw ***Shortly after the match has taken place Aramund Fury and Crippler are still standing in the ring after Ministry has been helped out of the ring by his brother. Suddenly "Unforgiven" by Metallica plays. The fans give a mixxed reaction as Fire and Cain come out. Fire comes out with a mic and Cain is carrying a paper sack. The walk to the ring but do not get in. The just stand at the base of the ring. Fire begins to speak.*** Fire: I told you two to watch your backs. I told you we were comming for you. Now your trapped an no way out. Hehe I like that. You failed to heed our warnings, Fury. We told you we were coming. We told you, this isn't really a sneak attack because I mean you knew we were going to be here. You should have figured as much. Cain: Since you like Bacardi 151 so much we decided you should end this night with some. Hehe, you know your both going to hell when you die and it's going to be real hot! It's going to burn like hemroids. So I guess you should get used to it. Don't look so confused. Just gimme a sec to explain. ***With that Cain opens the bag and reveals a bottle of Bacardi 151 and a box of matches. Cain pops open the Bacardi and strikes a match. He drops the match into the beverage. A flame shoots out of the bottle.*** Cain: BURN IN HELL PR*CKS! ***With that Cain launches the bottle of Bacardi 151 into the ring. It explodes and scorches the bodies of Aramund and Crippler. Cain and Fire duck their heads and walk calmy back to the locker room.*** This match was ruled a draw " Simply Perfect " Shawn Arrows vs Johnny Demonic Neither men showee up for this match The Referee threw this match out " Sexual White Hot Chocolate " Phillip Donovan [with Thorne Richards] vsJonny Cassanova SWHC continued his impressive win streak with a dominating performance at Jonny Cassanova's expense. Picking the lucky lady/ladies at ringside to recieve the roses he normally gives out, was tougher than this match. Anyways after dominating Jonny for several minutes, Phillip ended this match with the Panty Drop Phillip Donovan d. Jonny Cassanova w/ The Panty Drop Non Title Match Lee Tenn [Ewa European Champion ] vs Tanaka [with the Flock] A great match in which Tanaks held the early advantage with high flying manuevers such as a flying forearm, a spinning heel kick, a flying lariat and a tilt the world slam, but Tenn was far from done, he came back with an impressive array of scientifc manevevers including: a swinging neckbreaker, a figure four leglock, a series of armdrags and a belly to belly suplex but neither man was able to secure a three count. After Tenn had a suplex blocked, Tanaka landed the maneuver instead and followed with a bulldog and a spinebuster. He covered and Tenn managed to get his foot on the ropes, then Tanaka chose to argue with the ref, rather than follow up on his advantage and that allowed Tenn to take a breather in the corner. Tanaka turns around from his conversation with the ref in disgust only to receive a hard right hand by Lee Tenn. Lee Tenn follows up now with a European Uppercut and a series of chops in the corner, before whipping Tanaka into another corner and following up with a powerful clothesline Now, Tenn is up to the top rope going for a guillotine legdrop but noone is home on that one. Tanaka picks up his man and places him on the top rope and executes a textbook superplex followed by a shooting star press. He covers his man and Tenn Kicks out. The match went back and forth for several more minutes before the bell sounded signaling one minute remaining both men pulled out all the stops in the center of the ring swinging back and forth but neither man was able to secure the victory in the time alloted This match was ruled a draw Non Title Match E-Nuff' [Ewa Harcore Champion with The Flock] vs Thorne Richards Both men are in the ring and squaring off. And here we go. Both men lock up. E-Nuff is overpowering him but Thorne gets out of it and gets a waist lock on. E-Nuff with a back elbow now. Another. And another, no Thorne ducks and goes for a belly to back suplex attempt. No, he couldn't get E-Nuff up. And E-Nuff lays another elbow to the head and Thorne backs off. Big clothesline sends Thorne down. E-Nuff brings him back. Punch to the gut. Forearm. He's running, off the ropes. Missed clothesline and Thorne hits a drop toe hold. Thorne quickly follows up with an arm bar. He's really working it on E-Nuff. Thorne drags up E-Nuff with him still in the armbar. Into the ropes and E-Nuff pushes him off. Thorne comes back and ducks another clothesline and hits a nice a Crucifix. But E-Nuff catches him in that and nails a nice Samoan Drop. Cover. 1...2 but a strong kick out by Thorne. E-Nuff drags him back up. He sends Thorne into the ropes. Nice powerslam. Cover. 1...2...kickout. And E-Nuff brings him back up. He sends Thorne into the turnbuckle. E-Nuff yells to the crowd and then runs at Thorne. Thorne slides away and E-Nuff hits chest first. Thorne with Side Russian Leg Sweep. Thorne now drops an elbow. Picks up E-Nuff and places him on the turnbuckle. He gets up there and hits a nice Northern Lights Superplex. Cover by Thorne and 1...2...thr, no kickout by E-Nuff. Thorne drags him up and hits a right fist. Another one. And a chop that echo's throughout the building. And another one. E-Nuff rests up on the ropes. And Thorne hits another one. Irish whip. Thorne goes for a clothesline but E-Nuff hits one as well. Both men are down. The ref is counting now. And wait, out from the back comes EWA President John Solley. He doesn't look happy. And now it's E-Nuff who gets up first to stop the count...He sees President Solley and words are being thrown back and forth... Both men are yelling for Solley to get away from the ring now. Solley starts to turn and walk away as Enuff gets whipped to the ropes by Thorne Richards. Solley turns around and trips Enuff before heading to the back.. this immediately lead to Richards hitting a fake breaker and he hooks the leg and gets the win. Thorne Richards d. Enuff with a Face Breaker with the aide of interference by President Solley Ewa Television Title Match Fire vs "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin [Ewa Television Champion with The Triple Threat] This match started out fast and furious both men giving each other all they could ask for. Fire seemed to be in the best shape of his EWA careeer and focused more than I have ever seen him. Conklin, on the other hand was cool, calm and collected as usual. The match was pretty much a dead heat for the first 10 minutes or so before Fire threw Terry headfirst into the post on the outside, Fire then nailed a vicious piledriver on the outside which would have got Fire, the countout but not the belt so Fire threw his opponent in the ring and quickly went for the pin at the 11:58 mark but Conklin's foot was on the ropes and Fire was forced to break the hold. This gave Terry a slight repreive and The Champion ceized the opportunity with several well executed maneuvers some of which were: a powerslam, russian leg sweep, a hurricane ddt, a breathtaking 450 splash and a swinging neckbreaker before going for the Overhand RIght at the 15:04 mark but Fire had the move well scouted and quickly willed his way to the ropes to break the hold. The match was a relative stand off for another couple of minutes before after a devastating powerslam fire went for the Fire Bomb and Conklin somehow managed to reverse it into a cradle and get the pin at 18:38. This was definitely quite a test for Terry Conklin but he was up to the task. Terry Conklin d. Fire with a cradle Intercontinental Title Match "The Walking Triple Threat" Jack Reed [Ewa Intercontinental Champion with The Upper Echelon] vs Juventud Guerrera This match was one os the best of the night. Matter of fact one of the best of the month. Both men fought tooth and nail the whole way. Neither man asking any quarter nor giving any. Alot of bad blood seemed to surface during the week leading up to the match but this didnt seem to affect the match in itself which was a classic confrontation and a battle from start until finish. The early part of the match the edge was even until the Jack hit Juvi with a ddt at the 6:31 mark. Then Reed capatilized on the move by hitting powermoves such as a powerslam, a hotshot, several chokes broken in the very nick of time and a razor'ss edge. But soon Reed seemed to tire and missed an elbow drop at 10:58 and the conditioning of Juventud and his ariel and scientific skills took over and Juvi hit him with everything but the kitchen sink some of the moves were: a belly to bely suplex, a flying clothesline, a missle dropkick and even nailed him with a Juvi Driver on the outside but couldnt put his man away and started to argue with the ref at the 13:57 mark The Intercontinetal Champ capatalized on the mistake with an upercutt that nearly took Juvi's off and then nailed a face breaker and went for the cover but the ref is waiving it off. The match reached the 15 minute time limit. This match was ruled a draw Non-Title Match The Texas Tornadoes v.The Public Enemy [Ewa World Tag Team Champions with The Upper Echelon] The Texas Tornadoes got a pretty good pop from the crowd as they were coming to the ring. It seems as if the fans are starting to like the duo, strangely enough or maybey its just Buffalo. Anyways.. Showstopper began the match against Ghetto Boy and tried to his speed and quickness as an atvantage hitting Ghetto with several knifedge chops and a a dropkick and a European Uppercut and quickly tagging in his patner Jason Vendenza to do some more damage Jason used an atomic drop and a clothesline to the back of Ghetto's head before a stinger splash in the corner finally took the big man down. Vendenza nailed a couple legdrops before going to the top rope for a 450 that hit only the mat. Ghetto Boy picked up the challenger and press slammed him and then hit a razors edge and tagged in Swap Meet Louie who quickly dismantled Vendenza with a series of boots to the face and a powerbomb but somehow Vendenza fought his way to the corner and tagged in Showstopper who was completly overmatched but was trying his best. Showstopper quickly hit a couple dropkicks, and a spinning heel kick, followed by a 450 splash and a guillotine Legdrop and a flying forearm but Louie Side-Stepped the flying forearm and kicked him to the head with a Superkick before he choke-slammed the Showstopper. Ghetto Boy nailed Vendenza and knocked him to the floor, and Public Enemy got the hard fought win The Public Enemy d. The Texas Tornadoes with a chokeslam Non-Title King Of the Ring Semi-Final Match Prodigy[Ewa Commisioner] v. "The Innovator of Offense" Kanyon [Ewa United States Champion, with The Flock] The main event was definately everything that was advertised. Both men came to the ring looking intense and ready. The match was deemed "Raven's Rules" before the bell rung and at beginning of the match Kanyon had the with scientific manuevers even nailing a suplex into a chair which seemed to badly hurt the Ewa Commisioner. However Kanyon tried to lay out his opposition with the chair to the head but the move was blocked and Prodigy kicked Kanyon in the stomach and floored Kanyon with that same chair. Prodigy to the outside and grabs the timekeepers bell and nails Kanyon with a ddt into the bell at the 8:37 mark. Prodigy still with the atvantage a few minutes later hitting Kanyon with another chair to the cranium as well as a roll of quarters and even executing a Bret Hart like Figure-4 leglock on the ouside on the metal bar putting tremendous pressure on The United States Champ. Kanyon somehow broke the hold on his own and dragged Prodigy to the outside and slammed him on a table and then attempting to throw him into the steel bar holding the ring up but it was reversed and Kanyon instead got the medicine he was attempting to prescibe, Prodigy now with new life at the 13:42 mark. Prodigy slams Kanyon on the table, Prodigy with a gutwrench suplex on the outside onto the guard rail, Prodigy nailing Kanyon with the bell to the head. Prodigy picks up kanyon and throws him on the metal steps to the ring. And then lays out a chair and piledrives Kanyon into the chair on the outside, Prodigy then tosses Kanyon inside and goes for the pin but only gets a two count, after a couple more minutes of punishment by the deranged commisioner including a brainbuster,a ddt into a chair and a clothesline using a chair.. Prodigy throws Kanyon into the ropes and backdrops Kanyon into a chair and then attempts to follow it up with by dropping a knee to his head but Misses. Kanyon now has the edge and nails Prodigy with several suplexes of his own, followed by a press slam and an avalanche, before a superplex onto a chair and then Kanyon nails Prodigy with a tombstone into a chair and then goes for the pin but only a two count at the 24:45 mark. Kanyon uncharachteristically frustrated with his interpertation of a slow count arguing with the ref and gets a kick to the stomach then a snap suplex followed by a hotshot and Prodigy hooks the leg and gets the win. Prodigy d. Kanyon w/ A Hotshot
