EWA Wednesday Night War

10 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 10, 1998 Live, from Sioux Falls Arena, Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Live, from Sioux Falls Arena Sioux Falls, South Dakota Zuliel v. KG (w/ Sunny) The Black Angels v. Meanie Madhouse Return Match Ruger Ice v. Lee Tenn Bill Goldberg v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards) "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) The Widow Maker v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ The Patriot) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Joe Lemon & James Mastin v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The WolfPack & Keli Turner) KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Mayne Mann Jack Reed v. The Gunslinger "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin & "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Outsiders (w/ The WolfPack) Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim & Foxy Brown) "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Non-Title Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) EWA Intercontinental Championship Grudge Match To Take Place Inside Of A 15 Foot High Steel Cage Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Zuliel v. KG (w/ Sunny) Well, WNW started off with a squash, as KG completely destroyed Zuliel. While he was celebrating in the ring, SmirtDogg crept from behind the crowd, blasted him with a chair shot, and then put the mask of "Serpent Man" on KG's head. KG d. Zuliel with The Blackout. The Black Angels v. Meanie Madhouse Meanie Madhouse's debut in the EWA was a solid one, as the two showed terrific teamwork and agility. This was a fast paced match, with lots of impressive athletic manuevers by the two Meanies. Meanie Madhouse d. The Black Angels with Blue Meanie's pin on Kaworu after a frankensteiner into a pin. Return Match Ruger Ice v. Lee Tenn The second match between these two rising stars wasn't quite as impressive as #1, but it still impressed many. Another hard-fought match, this one was tewnty minutes of see-saw action until out of nowhere, Lee Tenn executed the Hang Tenn. After the match, the two men again shook hands in the ring. Lee Tenn d. Ruger Ice with The Hang Tenn. Bill Goldberg v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Neither man showed up for the fight. The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards) Stevie: Now people my guest this week is no stranger to controversey. This man has just recently returned to the EWA after having been fired by Duane Gates. Now most people can't stand this guy, but me personally, I have no problem with him. In fact, I kinda like him. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome SmirtDogg to the Swing Pad!!!! Stevie: First off, SmirtDogg, welcome back to the EWA. What exactly has brought you back because when you left last, it wasn't under the best terms. SmirtDogg: At first my reasons for coming back were rather simple -- I was out to get KG. Everything he does is done to copycat me in anyway from my tag lines to my style in the ring and even recently he wanted Foxy Brown. Everything I am -- he wishes to be so I had to bring him down a level which I did when I tricked him with the "Serpent Man" fiasco. However, now that I've crushed KG's ego I've moved on to bigger and better things such as regaining what was once mine -- the EWA United States United States Title from a man which I despise. All I ever hear is Wyldsyde this and Wyldsyde that but take one look at the man -- he's out of shape and he's not what people make him out to be. This Satuday at the Clash -- I'll flat out beat him without breaking a sweat. Stevie: So we all know that you're going to take Wyldsyde to the cleaners on Saturday. But for some reason, KG is still claiming that he wants a piece of you. What's wrong with him? Is he retarded or what? SmirtDogg: Retarded?(he gives an arrogant grin) That would be a bit of an understatement. You would think that after the way I embarassed and humiliated him that he'd just crawl under a rock and die like most of us would wish for. KG has whined, begged, and pleaded to EWA President Duane Gates for another chance at me. An opportunity for redemption. Gates has given into him apparently and at Summer Breakout 98 -- KG will have what he wanted when he gets me -- "God's Gift to Wrestling" in a 20' steel cage but at what costs? Personally I think KG should have took the beaten and left it at that. This time around I may not be so nice. I may just decide to take KG out once and for all. Stevie: Now you and I have never personally had any beefs with each other. But I'm in the minority. It seems as if you have a penchant for getting under peoples' skin. Would you care to comment on that? SmirtDogg:(he gives an arrogant smile) Well first off I remember one wrestler you feuded with and that would be 'The Golden Child' and I must say it was a pleasure watching you beat on him from ringpost to pillar because I dislike the guy as much as you. As for my ability to get under people's skin -- well that's what I'm all about. It's a mind game. I throw my opponent off his strategy -- his game plan and I reap the benefits from that. I've been rather successful with the attitude I carry myself with so I'm not going to change it anytime soon. If people can't deal with it -- it's not my problem -- it's theirs. Stevie: I understand that completely seeing as how I'm a master of the mind game as well. Now I'm sure you've been paying attention to what's been going around here. I would just like to know what you think about the Wolfpack and Triple Threat war. SmirtDogg: Well it's definitely a clash of the titans between two mega talented and powerful stables. The Wolfpack, as much as I hate them, I must admit that they are the new force in the EWA but if they think that the Triple Threat is an easy target for them to take down then they are mistaken. I nearly had a match once with Jeff Bunda and I must say that I have respect for the man as a wrestler. As for Shawn Michaels -- him and I -- have our own feud going in another federation so to see the Triple Threat run all over him and the Wolfpack would be a beautiful sight. Stevie: Well I've got Michaels later on tonight and I can guarantee that I'll run over him. In fact, I'm on my way to running over the whole Wolfpack by myself. You see, Saturday night, I have a shot at that overrated puke Draven. But enough about me. This is about you. Besides going after the U.S. title, what are your plans for the EWA? SmirtDogg: Short term goals would be to covet the EWA United States Title, wipe KG out of the EWA picture and prove to Duane Gates that he never should have fired me in the first place because I bring in the ratings and there's no denying that. My one long term goal would be to build myself up once again in the EWA to the point where I'll be in consideration for a World Title Shot and just take it from there. One thing I know is Erik Draven isn't a good champion for the EWA -- to put it simply -- he sucks. Stevie -- you'll take him down Saturday Night -- no problem unless he calls for the troops. Stevie: Well troops or no troops, his dumb ass is mine. I'll take that strap off him. And from the way the little director is motioning, we're about out of time. Any last comments? And thank you for the interview. SmirtDogg: No problem. Thanks for having me on. And I do have one last thing to say. Everyone make sure you tune into Saturday Night Slam because two things are going to happen. Stevie Richards will become EWA World Heavyweight Champion and I will beat Wyldsyde for the EWA United States Title. Count on it. "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) This match displayed two of the relative newcomers in the EWA Light Heavyweight Division, and they put on a spectacular show of acrobatic high-flying manuevers. However, Satre was just a little bit more "on" tonight, as he came out on top. Joe Satre d. Will Haynes by submission with a boston crab. The Widow Maker v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ The Patriot) The Widow Maker started off this contest in complete control, as he had the "eye of the tiger" tonight. Widow Maker has been improving over his last few matches, and this was no exception. However, his mistake came when he hauled off and slapped Shamrock -- causing the World's Most Dangerous Man to enter his zone and snap. From there, it was all Shamrock. Ken Shamrock d. The Widow Maker by submission with the Ankle Submission. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Joe Lemon & James Mastin v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The WolfPack & Keli Turner) This match lasted about 20 seconds. :) Afterward, however, Prodigy began to stalk down to the ring, chair in hand, when President Gates came out and began to follow him. Gates: Don't even think about it, Prodigy. (Prodigy turns around) Prodigy, you had the opportunity to make me a lot of money. You had the chance of a lifetime; to become the greatest EWA Champion in history. But you dropped the ball. And from here on out, Psychotic One, you're not getting any slack. You're going to have to work your way up from ROCK BOTTOM. With that, Prodigy snapped. He dropped the chair and ran at Gates, pummeling him for a few moments before security could escort him away. KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Mayne Mann Mayne Mann made his EWA debut a memorable one, as his "Boston Bomb" was executed so well on KooKabura, he had to be stretchered out of the arena. Mayne Mann d. KooKabura with the Boston Bomb. Jack Reed v. The Gunslinger The three personalities of Jack Reed all came out tonight, and when it was all over, Crazy Eddie had pinned the 'Slinger after a powerbomb from the top rope. Slinger was bleeding from the ear as a result of Crazy Eddie biting him. Jack Reed/Crazy Eddie/Mr. Deliant d. The Gunslinger with a top rope powerbomb. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin & "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Outsiders (w/ The WolfPack) Needless to say, Hall and Nash were about as cold tonight as the Utah Jazz in Game #3. The 'Siders mounted no offense at all, leaving them easy pickings for Martin and Hicks. Aaron Martin/Justin Hicks d. The Outsiders with Justin Hicks' pin on Scott Hall after a gutwrench suplex. After the match, President Gates appeared on the MegaTron above the entrance, his face incensed with rage. Gates: Prodigy, you wanna play hardball? We'll play hardball. That one month vacation you asked for after you lost to Draven? Consider the remainder of it canceled. You will wrestle in Omaha, Nebraska, this Saturday at Clash Of The Champions III, against The Widow Maker. You wanna play hardball, Prodigy? You will have one hand tied behind your back during the match. Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim & Foxy Brown) K-Dawg started off this contest in control, using a lot of Luchador-style pinning combinations. He made his mistake, however, when he tried to use a variation of the Air Smirty. Smirt moved, and from there, dominated the contest. During the entire match, The Wyldsyde was seen sitting in the aisle, furiously scribbling in a notebook. Afterward, Smirt stalked toward him, but The Wyldsyde held out his belt and said, "If you want a shot at this belt, you're not going to attack The Wyldsyde now!" SmirtDogg d. Konnan with the Gangsta's Paradise. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Flair pretty much dominated this match, and as he was setting up Copeland for the Figure Four, the entire stable of NQ2 ran to ringside and pummeled on Flair. Thorne Richards placed him in the Loch Ness Lock, while Copeland punched away. Finally, security escorted NQ23 out of the building. Ric Flair d. Shawn Copeland by disqualification. Non-Title Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Michaels started off this match in complete control, obviously angry at Stevie's "WolfPack Killer" T-shirt. However, Michaels let the anger take too much control over him, and it let Stevie slip back into the match by catching Michaels with a DDT. From there, it was all Stevie...until a low blow from Michaels. Michaels went to the top rope as Stevie was getting up, and attempted to catch Stevie with a double ax-handle, but amazingly, Richards caught Michaels with the Stevie Kick as Michaels was coming down. Stevie Richards d. Shawn Michaels with the Stevie Kick. EWA Intercontinental Championship Grudge Match To Take Place Inside Of A 15 Foot High Steel Cage Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) The atmosphere in the arena as the cage was being put up was electric, and the match was just as exciting. Quite possibly the greatest Intercontinental Championship match in history saw these two men engage in combat for over thirty-one minutes. Among the highlights: Bunda's avalanche elbowdrop onto McCammon from the top of the cage, McCammon's superplex on Bunda from the top of the cage, and much more. At the very end, both men were climbing down on the outside of the cage. Bunda stuck his foot out, and tripped McCammon, causing him to fall down. However, his foot got stuck between the bars of the cage, (this was a WWF-style cage) and McCammon could only watch, suspended upside down, as Bunda climbed down. Jeff Bunda d. Jack McCammon by being the man to exit the cage first. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship.
