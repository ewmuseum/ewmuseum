EWA Clash Of The Champions V

8 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Clash Of The Champions V Saturday, August 8, 1998 Live, from The Lawrence Joel Memorial Coliseum Winston-Salem, North Carolina. *The camera pans to the aisle way where "Mean" Gene Okerlund is standing holding a microphone and he is obviously awaiting an interview session with some wrestler. The camera closes in as he begins yapping his trap.* Gene Okerlund: At this time it is my pleasure to introduce to you one of the greatest wrestlers in all of wrestling. He has been the standing backbone of wrestling for the past 20 years. Ladies and gentlemen, here is the 13 time World Champion....."The Natureboy" Ric Flair! *As Okerlund finishes the camera pans to the entrance way as "Space Odessy: 2001" begins to blare over the PA system. The crowd is already standing as the main man himself slowly makes his way out from the back wearing a 3-piece Armani suit. After standing at the top of the runway for a few moments and letting the pyrotechnics finish, Flair begins to stroll on down to Okerlund who is waiting patiently. After reaching Okerlund, Flair looks around with a huge smile across his face and does his patented spin. As the fans start to simmer out Mene Gene begins.* Mene Gene: Ric Flair, welcome to the EWA. "The NatureBoy" Ric Flair: Mene, whoooooo, Gene. We are in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, whooooo!!!!! And the Natureboy is ready to style and profile all night long, with all the pretty ladies. Mene Gene: Well we all know that you are very capable of doing just that, but let's not beat around the bush so to speak, I'm sure that everyone here and around the world would like to know what exactly has brought you back to the EWA. Flair: Well you know Gene, for the past several weeks I've been sitting at home watching all the wish-wash that's been going down here in the EWA, and honestly it's making me sick to my stomach. I've watched that stooge Bunda lose the EWA World Title in one of the best matches in EWA history to the incompetent Smirtdogg, I've seen Makaveli show extreme confidence in putting his title on the line against Copeland and the Dream Team, I've watched as newcomers are trying to make this place a damned House of Horror with all there, I'm dark and evil talk. Now that is just the top layer of things so we will come back to that later. Mene Gene: Well that's certainly understandable. Now there have been rumors circulating that you have talked about bringing in some of the top wrestlers in the world to reform what some call the "IV Horsemen". Is there any truth to that and do you want to bring them back together? Ric Flair: *laughs* Gene, I'd be lying if I said that I didn't want to bring the IV Horsemen back into wrestling. I mean, look back upon the years and you'll see that we have been the measuring stick at which and group of wrestlers is measured. We were the top of the food chain and nobody could argue that, but I tell you Mene Gene. After what happened with Double A, I don't think I could bring myself to even try. I mean, without my best friend at my side it just wouldn't be the same. He was, as many people say, the backbone of the IV Horsemen and to me and any Horsemen follower we would be nothing but a group of spinless winners without him. BUT, that doesn't mean I haven't picked out a few wrestlers who I think would make some good allies along the way. Now I'm not saying I have made any deals and I'm not saying I haven't, but there are only a few that I would even consider aligning myself with. Take for instance a couple of people I've noticed recently, Crazy Canuck and Mayne Mann. Now both of them show extreme talent and ability, hell they even have that fire beneath them that seems to push them up to a greater height than most. But if you look on another side, we've got Diamond Dallas Page and Gunslinger who are seemingly already forming something and preparing for a mini-takerover. Hell, Gunslinger left about the same time I did and now he's back and back with a vengence, I mean, he's already got a TV title match after just over 2 weeks back in the EWA. As for Page, well I know he's a stand-up guy because as everyone knows we were once a main part of the IV Horsemen here in the EWA. But that's all in the past and I'm looking towards the future so anything that happens from here on out has nothing to do with the past....Except for one person, and that's Jeff Bunda. Mene Gene: Well that certainly makes sense, I mean he does claim to be the "Franchise" and the "Showstopper". Ric Flair: Say no more Mene Gene, I know exactly what Bunda refers to himself as. If you ask me Bunda is nothing but a sitting duck right now. He has something I want and want the Natureboy wants he gets. You see Bunda, for the past 15 years I've been filling up arenas across this great country, hell I've even filled up arenas in Japan. I've won the World Title 13 different times and I've gone through extremely tough opponents to get those titles. What have you done my friend? You can't hold a candle to the, whoooooo, Natureboy. Because Bunda, when you step out from behind the curtain on your way to the ring the fans don't know what they are getting, BUT when the Natureboy steps out and starts to walk that aisle the fans know they are about to watch the greatest wrestler to ever step into the ring perform. Whooooo!!!! *Space Odessy: 2001 begins to play as Flair acknowledges the crowd and begins making his way back to the back.* Mene Gene: Well it's obvious that the "Natureboy" is back and he's been taking names, obviously Jeff Bunda hasn't made a good impression on him, and who could his associates be. Maybe we will find out later this evening. *Camera fades to a commercial* New Demolition v. Intoxication-X Well folks, we opened the fifth installment of Clash of the Champions with a bang, as we saw a terrific tag team match between everybody's favorite burnouts, Intoxication-X, and a team who hasn't been seen in wrestling action since EWA MindGames, New Demolition. These two teams both came into the match with different agendas; I-X needing a win to move them up in the tag team scene, and New Demolition hoping to get their return to the EWA off on the right foot. And both teams obviously wanted their goals very badly, as they both put up a very hard-fought match. New Demolition held an early advantage, using their very distinct size advantage over I-X to power them down, and wear them out. However, Aaron Martin was able to turn the tide of the match with a beautifully executed Asai Moonsault onto Semi. From there, I-X held the advantage for awhile, until Will Haynes tried to execute a Frankensteiner from the top rope onto Buddy Brown. Brown used Will's momentum to his advantage, and reversed the move into a top rope powerbomb, sending Haynes hard to the mat. Brown was able to then roll over, and get the three-count for a successful return. New Demolition d. Intoxication-X with Buddy Brown's pin on Will Haynes after a top-rope powerbomb. However, IRK refused to break the hold, even after the official's persistent warnings, and the referee had no choice but to reverse the decision, and award the victory to Jeff Bunda. Jeff Bunda d.
