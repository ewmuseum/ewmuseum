EWA Wednesday Night War

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 21, 1998 Live, from Glen Falls Civic Center Glen Falls, New York The Poison Pit (Hosted by Venom, with special guest El Locco Jobbo) Steel Cage Grudge Match "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) Four Corners Match for the vacant EWA European Championship Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. Thorne Richards v. Tonight we have a great card lined up for your viewing pleasure! Vincent Ashe: Ya we sure do have a dandy set up. JR: That's right folks!! In fact, we have a "treat" for you all, because right now we are going to send you over to the set of the Poison Pit, for another installment of The Extreme SuperStar's controversial show! "The Inevitable" Rod Johnson In a total and brutal squash, Galaxy man handled the inferior Johnson. He was then pelted by beer bottles from the angry rowdy crowd. *** Galaxy d. Rod Johnson in a squash. (Just as the Rob Van Dam versus Deader match is about to be announced, "In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" begins to play and the crowd starts to boo.  The EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, The Prince, emerges through the entrance way with microphone in hand.  He's dressed in what appears to be a made to order Armani suit as this thing fits him as if he were sewn into it.  The Light Heavyweight Title is in his left hand, dragging along the ground as he steps out of the entrance way and onto the top of the ramp.) When Deader came out the night that I won this championship and challenged me to a match at November 2 Remember, my advisers kept telling me that accepting this match was not a good idea.  They said that he wasn't a worthy opponent, that he was unproven, and that he would not be a match for the likes of me.  I agreed with them on all respects with one exception.  I did not agree to their requests for me to turn his challenge down.  I told them to quiet themselves and to continue their roles as yes-men, as I have no need for men to confirm what I already know to be truth and goodness.  I accepted Deader's challenge under the condition that he prove himself.  I accepted under the condition that Deader would ocme out and defeat each and every superstar that I defeated for this title. Now to his credit, Deader did beat Galaxy, but that's not much credit given.  With Mac Namara's departure from this federation, which went relatively unnoticed, I decided to be leniant and give Deader the opportunity to choose his next opponent, and after the Light Heavyweight weight limit was explained to him through the use of diagrams he chose Rob Van Dam, the man he was supposed to fight tonight.  Now Rob Van Dam didn't show up tonight, for whatever reason, and tonight would have been the perfect opportunity for Deader to advance in my little challenge.  The easiest win of his career and all he had to do was lace up his boots and come to the ring.  But does he have the decency to do that? (The Prince pauses and the crowd is still booing him) He's not here tonight and he will not be showing up at all.  Deader isn't anywhere near the building tonight and with that he threw away his chance at becoming the number one contender for my title.  (The Prince chuckles to himself, and the crowd boos even louder).  This creates a bit of a problem.  You see, I have no challenger for November 2 Remember, and that's because there is no challenge here in the EWA for me.  But I know that President Gates would love to have me on the card, because he knows that he would love a few people of better class and more money sitting in the front seats, and not someone who is missing not only their real teeth, but some of their dentures too.  He knows that I bring the upper crust of society not only to the ring with me, but to the ringside, to the arena, and I bring their money to Gates' wallet.  He wants me there and in order for that to happen, I need a challenger.  Since I am the champion, and since I am royalty, I make the rules that everyone must play by.  President Gates, listen up and listen up good.  Between now and November 2 Remember you will find me a challenger for this title (The Prince raises the belt off the ground slightly as to recognize it, but quickly lowers it again), and you will do it with a Battle Royal.  Scrape together you best Light Heavyweights and throw them into the ring like lions and have them fight it out for a scrap of meat otherwise known as the number one contender for my belt.  Whoever wins will have the honour of being the next member of an ever growing line of losers who tried to match themselves with me but failed. And as always, I leave an open invitation for New Jack.  Of course, such a champion like yourself, who never lost the title, or even a match for that matter, would have no problem taking on such a field and becoming the number one contender for this belt.  All you have to do is show up and the shot is yours New Jack.  Your suspension is up as of today and all you have to do is make your presence felt.  Accept the challenge if you can, or live with the shame of your silence. (The Prince turns around and walks through the entrance as "In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" plays again over the chorus of boos for The Prince) Deader v.
