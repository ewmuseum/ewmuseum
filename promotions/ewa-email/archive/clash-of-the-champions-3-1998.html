EWA Clash Of The Champions III

13 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Clash Of The Champions III Saturday, June 13, 1998 Live, from Pershing Auditorium in Omaha, Nebraska. (Before the main EWA: Clash of the Champions III graphics air, actually about two minutes before the show was supposed to start, the camera fades up in the Triple Threat locker room, as Jeff Bunda and Rebecca Claridge are standing there, evidently talking to Stevie Richards, who's in the shower.) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda: Now, Stevie, you know you've got this match won against Draven. But we both know the Wolfpack' s going to be in the house, and you know how they like to f*ck people out of titles. They already f*cked Aaron out of his Light Heavyweight Title, and we're not going to let them f*ck you out of this World Title. The Triple Threat will be doing all the f*cking tonight. "Dancin'" Stevie Richards: *muffles something that can't be heard...* Jeff Bunda: Well, we've got it all locked up. Rebecca told me that she wants to accompany you to the ring tonight, and make sure none of the Wolfpack interfere. And I'm doing color commentary at the commentary table, so if Konnan, Hall, or any of those morons try to run in, we'll be there to stop them. Oh, and Stevie, you know we're behind you one hundred percent. Stevie Richards: *muffles something* Rebecca Claridge: That's true, Jeff, Summer Breakout's next week. Jeff Bunda: You mean... I've got the World Title shot.... OH SH*T!!!! (Camera fades to black as main EWA graphics come on.) Hans Gruber v. Zuliel The opening match at the Clash was a squash, as Gruber made a very successful debut, finishing off Zuliel in short work with the Der Meisterschutze. Hans Gruber d. Zuliel with the Der Meisterschutze. Non-Title Special Challenge Match The Allied Powers (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The WolfPack) In what was a promising sign for the EWA Tag Team Division, these two teams had one hell of a war. Unfortunately, both teams ended up spilling over to the outside, and as the fight continued, referee Mark Curtis had no choice but to call for a double countout. Public Enemy & The Allied Powers battled to a double countout. Hannibal Manson v. Shawn Hart* True to form, Shawn Hart didn't show up, and was promptly fired from the EWA. Hannibal Manson d. Shawn Hart by forfeit. Ruger Ice v. "The Total Package" Lex Luger (w/ The WolfPack) (Before the match, the camera fades in...Ruger Ice is stepping out of his locker room.) RUGER: Luger....are you ready because it is time. It is time that we step into the ring and I introduce you to your worst nightmare. It is time that I take the wolfpack down a notch or two. Its time that I show your boss that sooner or later I will catch up with him. K-Dawg...I hope your dumb ass is watching because you will be next.... Luger came to the ring with Konnan, and started off the match in complete control, dominating Ruger with his physical strength. Luger whipped Ruger into the turnbuckle late in the match, however, and as he went for a clothesline, Ruger ducked, and Luger ran right into the turnbuckle. Lex turned around, only to get nailed with an atomic drop, and then the Spinebuster. Konnan climbed on the ring apron to distract him, but Ruger knocked him off, and then covered Lex for the three-count. Ruger Ice d. Lex Luger with the Spinebuster. Non-Title Match "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) Satre went to work quickly on Jack, abandoning his high-flying techniques in favor of a submission-style attack on McCammon's injured ankle. Satre used various sorts of ankle-locks in attempting to force McCammon to submit, but each time, McCammon refused to tap out, and somehow escaped. Satre, frustrated, then went to his top rope attack, nailing a twisting moonsault on McCammon that awed the capacity crowd. But Satre's crucial error came next. Wanting to appause the crowd even more, he attempted a second twisting moonsault, and this time, McCammon moved. From there, McCammon went to work on Satre. Jack started to go to the top for the Double Hammer, but decided his ankle was too testy, and instead executed an incredibly forceful powerbomb onto Satre. From there, it was elementary. Jack "The Man" McCammon d. Joe Satre with a powerbomb. Joe Lemon v. Mayne Mann Mayne Mann once again impressed many people, quickly disposing of Joe Lemon. This match lasted shorter than both men's ring entrances. Mayne Mann d. Joe Lemon with the Boston Bomb. Stars And Stripes v. Konnan & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) A terrific tag team match saw Michaels and Konnan actually being booed during their entrance in favor of the American patriots. Michaels seemed very upset at this, and started out by dominating the Patriot in the ring. Michaels then tagged to Konnan, who continued the domination on The Patriot for Team WolfPack. But Konnan made a grave mistake when he climbed to the top rope and yelled, "HORALE!!" to the crowd. Shamrock shoved him off of the top rope, and The Patriot was able to tag in Shamrock at the same time as Michaels came in. Michaels wanted no part of Shamrock, and quickly tagged back to Konnan. From there, Konnan took the advantage with a rake to the face, followed by a tag to Michaels, who began to viciously work over Shamrock. But out from the entrance ramp came a shocker: Madusa, dressed in her American garb. Madusa walked to the ring, and Michaels was easily distracted by this, causing Shamrock to be able to tag to The Patriot. While Michaels was still looking at Madusa, The Patriot simply came from behind, nailed the Uncle Slam, and claimed the victory. Afterward, however, Michaels took a chair to both men, especially Shamrock. Stars And Stripes d. Shawn Michaels/Konnan with The Patriot's pin on Shawn Michaels after The Uncle Slam. The Gunslinger v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins The Gunslinger continued his losing streak, as the patriotic Deakins made a very triumphant return to the EWA. Vic Deakins d. The Gunslinger with the EMP. "Stunning" Scott Cummings* v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Neither man showed up...Cummings was fired. Jack Reed v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) Reed developed an early offense during this match, taking out Martin with a series of high-risk manuevers. However, about mid-way through the match, Martin began to fight back, working over Reed with a variety of power and high-flying moves. Martin eventually finished Reed off with the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin d. Jack Reed with the Shoulderbreaker. Triangle Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. KG (w/ Sunny) v. Lee Tenn Lee Tenn looked somewhat off his game tonight, and started the match against Haynes, who eventually eliminated him with The Hangman. From there, a great match ensued between Haynes and KG, but in the end, Haynes fell victim to The Blackout. KG d. Will Haynes with The Blackout. KG wins the Triangle Match. 2 Cold Scorpio & "The Brown Bomber" Robert Thompson (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. The Meanie Madhouse Meanie Madhouse looked good in their second EWA match, but 2 Cold Scorpio and Robert Thompson, teaming together for the first time ever, looked better. Another outstanding tag team match; the first one to end cleanly, though. Afterward, 2 Cold and The Brown Bomber passed out "Meanie Babies" to the crowd. 2 Cold Scorpio/Robert Thompson d. Meanie Madhouse after 2 Cold's pin on Red Meanie after the 2 Cold Splash. Steel Cage Return Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair In an outstanding battle that pitted the old against the youth, the fans saw over 20 minutes of exciting wrestling action. These two men battled all over the cage, holding absolutely nothing back. There was no clear-cut advantage until the very end of the match, when Copeland attempted to execute a frog splash....from the top of the cage. Flair moved, and crawled out of the cage door as Copeland lay writhing in pain. Both men had to be helped back to the locker room, but both were cleared to wrestle at Summer Breakout '98. Prodigy (Prodigy will have one hand tied behind his back during the match.) The Widow Maker started this match out in control, due to Prodigy's lack of use of his right hand, which had to be tied behind his back. However, amazingly, Prodigy began to fight back, whaling away on Widow Maker with his left hand. Prodigy finally got The Widow Maker down, and, sweating profusely from the effort, climbed to the top rope and executed a guillotine legdrop for the victory. Afterward, President Gates appeared in the entrance ramp. Gates: Not bad, Prodigy, not bad. But I seriously doubt you can handle your next test. I've been on the phone all day with the California State Athletic Comission, and they've agreed to let the EWA have a special kind of match. Non-Title Match Scott Hall (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Two words: SQUASH CITY. Bunda seemed determined to prove that he, not Hall, was the greatest Intercontinental Champion of all time. And as a result, he completely dismantled Hall. Jeff Bunda d. Scott Hall with the Showrunner. DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim/Foxy Brown) v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) SPECIAL GUEST REFEREE: "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks As SmirtDogg came to the ring, he wore his "own" brown suede jacket that said on the back, "I Am The SmirtDogg", in an obvious attempt to mock The Wyldsyde. Hicks came out next, and he and Smirt made no eye contact at all. Once The Wyldsyde came out, the match got underway. The Wyldsyde started off the match with some open chops to the chest of SmirtDogg, (Remember, it's a Wyldstyle Match) but Smirt threw a solid punch, nailing The Wyldsyde in the face. Hicks, who clearly saw the punch, said nothing. The Wyldsyde began to argue with Hicks, but to no avail. From there, Smirt began to punch The Wyldsyde down into a corner, and then strangled him with the ropes. Hicks made an extremely slow 5 count, and Smirt finally released. And so the match continued, Hicks apparently having thrown The Wyldstyle rules out the window. Finally, The Wyldsyde caught Smirt coming off of the ropes into a powerslam, but once he made the cover, Hicks made a count so slow that a turtle race could've completed before 3. Needless to say, SmirtDogg kicked out. The Wyldsyde took control of the match at that point, but lost it a few minutes later, when SmirtDogg executed a severe low blow, again, right in front of Hicks. From there, The Wyldsyde bent over, and was in perfect position for SmirtDogg to execute The Gangsta's Paradise and get the FAST three count. As Smirt left with his new trophy, The Wyldsyde got back up, and the fight was on. It took security a few minutes before they could finally separate Hicks and The Wyldsyde, and as a result, a match has been signed at Summer Breakout '98 for the two to collide. SmirtDogg d. The Wyldsyde with the Gangsta's Paradise. SmirtDogg is the new EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion!! Click here for the Main Event!!
