EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

12 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The Corestates Center Philadelphia, Pennsylvania "Ghetto Superstar" by Praswell blares over the PA system and the crowd goes wild. They found a new respect for the man that's about to walk down the aisle. Sunny comes out from the back and a couple of steps behind her comes KG. He already has a microphone in his hand, but he waits until he gets in the ring to speak.* "Outlaw Immortal" KG: Shawn Michaels, you little ingrate...you said anytime, anywhere, so let's do this RIGHT NOW! Bring it. *He waits for Michaels, but no sign of him* He's probably off, parading around with his little bed buddy Draven. *The crowd laughs* Anyway, Michaels, this isn't over...I'll find you one way or another. You're gonna hear these two words over and over in your mind. YOU'RE DEAD Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) Taz never showed up for the match, while Van Smack did, earning him a victory. Van Smack d. Taz by forfeit. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) You want to talk about intensity? This match defined intensity. These two men battled for over 10 minutes at a grueling pace. But Castor seemed extra-motivated tonight to defeat Page, and as a result, did just that. Castor Troy d. Dallas Page with The Face/Off. Jason Wyldheart v. Magnificant Matt (w/ The PHC) It was found out that due to their no-shows, both men have been fired from the EWA. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Eliminators v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Dream Team) Before this match, we were shown footage of a promotional gig from the day before. *Camera shot fades in and we are outside the Corestates Center one day before Wednesday Night Warzone is set to explode from these facilities. The EWA is putting on a promotional gig with certain EWA superstars signing autographs for the thousands of fans that are on hand. A long black limousine pulls up next to the curb. The limo driver gets out of the vehicle and opens up the back driver's side door. Out appears Booker T followed by Stevie Ray. They become center stage as they attract everyone's attention. The EWA World Tag Team Belts are draped over their shoulders. With a microphone in his hand -- Booker T begins to speak.* Booker T: Now that the Ghetto Warriors are here representing this party is about to get started. All you suckas out there getting the autographs of EWA midcarders and losers. I know you want HARLEM HEAT'S autographs but guess what -- not gonna happen. My brotha and I have more important things to do right now like bashing your hometown heroes -- the Eliminators -- right before your very eyes. How do you like that? *Booker T holds the microphone towards the audience as deafening boos echo throughout the streets of Philadelphia. Booker T continues to speak.* Booker T: Tomorrow night, in that very arena, *he points towards the Corestate Center* the Eliminators are going to have their asses handed to them on a silver platter. It must be obvious to everyone by now that the Eliminators are making their final charge at regaining the glory they once had. Saturn and Kronus are using this match as a last ditch effort to catapult themselves back to the top of the game. When they fail and they will fail -- their entire world will come crumbling down around them. Saturn and Kronus will no longer be embraced here in the EWA by the fans or their peers. The Eliminators will then realize that they have lost more than a step and simply can't make the cut. The EWA is where the best competition squares off against one another and the Eliminators are no longer on this level. That's why after HARLEM HEAT dominates and destroys them -- the Eliminators will be faced with two options. *Stevie Ray produces a microphone out of his pocket and begins to speak.* Stevie Ray: What would those two options be brotha? Booker T: #1: A departure from the EWA + an official retirement from pro-wrestling. Stevie Ray: And what's their second viable option? Booker T: #2: To quit the EWA and go crawling back to where they came from. *The people gathered around boo like crazy and chant "Harlem Heat" sucks! * Stevie Ray: You mean Viking Hall and Barbed Wire City? *he laughs* Booker T: Yep! Stevie Ray: Man, that place is so pathetic. Did you know every Monday Night they use that building as a bingo hall? What a hell hole. How can a federation have most of its events originate from a place that uses its facilities for both wrestling and bingo. What a joke! Booker T: I don't know. However they don't have any money or talent so that could be the reasoning why. Are you starting to see why Saturn and Kronus fit in perfectly over there? How about ya'll *he directs his attention to the crowd* Don't you all think that the Eliminators should go back to their roots and compete where there's no competition so that they could have a chance of winning? *The crowd yell profanities at Booker T and force him to dodge several flying projectiles.* Booker T: Come on now. I'm just looking out for their best interests. Saturn and Kronus just aren't EWA material anymore. Face the facts and don't be delusioned by the Eliminator's interviews which suggest differently. O.k.? *crowd continues to uproar in anger* After the HEATWAVE hovers over and scorches the Eliminators worse than the phenomenon of a backdraft Saturn and Kronus will be exposed like never before. By the time my brotha and I are done unloading our offensive onslaught upon them the Eliminators will look like they been through a war. Not any war. But World Wars 1 and 2 combined. The Eliminators will come out of this match barely breathing and looking as pathetic as the tag team of James Mastin and Serpent Man. HARLEM HEAT on the otherhand, we'll just once again prove why we're the greatest tag team in the world today and why we deserve to be the EWA World Tag Team Champions. There isn't one tag team that could put out the flames of the ever burningHEAT! *The Crowd starts screaming in approval of the Eliminators as they name them as being the tag team that can take out Harlem Heat. Stevie Ray once again speaks. * Stevie Ray: You Philadelphia people must be severely brain damaged or extremely loyal to two suckas that don't deserve it. Which one is it? *Stevie Ray points the microphone towards the crowd and all we hear are a bunch of 4 letter words and thunderous boos.* Stevie Ray: I take that as both. he gives an arrogant grin* There has never been a great wrestler to come out of Philadelphia. To be honest the city of Philadelphia sucks. There's only 2 good things about this city. One would be Philly Cheese Steaks and the other would be "Rocky". Booker T: And one of those happens to be fictional. Stevie Ray: Exactly. Just goes to show why this city is good for nothing. It served as the place where Saturn and Kronus got their start. How much worse can it get? Booker T: It can't. Philly just hit rock bottom if you want to look at it that way. Stevie Ray: Definitely. And as I look out at all these suckas surrounding us it's become obvious to me that they are now finally beginning to understand that their hometown heroes -- the Eliminators -- are out-classed by HARLEM HEAT in every way. I can also tell that they are all now ready and willing to worship us for the tag team legends we are. So I'd like to give you all that opportunity at this time. Bow down to the best tag team in the world today. *The crowd has finally had enough and suddenly stampede towards Booker T and Stevie Ray. The two brothers look at each other and say Oh sh*t. They immediately leap into the limousine and as it pulls away into the night -- the angry people throw rocks and other objects at it. It looks like the L.A. riots all over again.* This encensed The Eliminators, as you could see from the looks on their faces when they came to the ring. As a result, we saw a match that started out as a Pier-6 brawl, and ended up being a fine match that the fans enjoyed. Being in Philadelphia, the fans were behind The Eliminators 100%, and it looked like they had a victory when Booker T was hit with Total Elimination...until Stevie Ray broke up John Kronus' attempt at a cover. Stevie Ray then rolled Kronus to the outside, followed, smashed him against the guardrail, executed The FlapJack, and then rolled him back in the ring for Booker T to pin. As Harlem Heat has their hands raised in victory, and the tag belts handed to them, two men come from the crowd and attack them from behind. The larger man throws his man out while the smaller man nails his with a reverse DDT. The larger of the two men then climbs to the top rope and nails him with a cannonball splash. As that happens, two more people, who look EXACTLY alike the other two in the ring, come down and attack The Eliminators. They climb in to the ring, and the two teams are standing face to face. Then, the team from the back attacks the team that came from the crowd. The team from the back peels off their masks, and it's THE GHETTO BOY and SWAP MEET LOUIE, PUBLIC ENEMY! We never find out who the other two men were, as they leave with the other two teams... Harlem Heat d. The Eliminators with Booker T's pin on John Kronus after Stevie Ray's FlapJack. Harlem Heat retain the EWA World Tag Team Championship. Haywood Jublome v. New Jack This match was deemed a weapons match before the bell rang, and both men took full advantage. Both men were busted open, and put up a hell of an effort, but in the end, it was a powerbomb onto a trash can that sealed the win for New Jack. New Jack d. Haywood Jublome with a powerbomb onto a trash can. New Demolition (w/ The Modern Day Warriors) v. Darkness A solid tag team contest, which saw Darkness again put up a hell of a fight, but again fall just short to the opposition. New Demolition d. Darkness with Buddy Brown's pin on DAP after the Bad Ass Bomb. "Da Roof" Rufus Lamar Jackson v. The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) "Da Roof" certainly wasn't on fire tonight, and The Golden One continued his undefeated streak since his return, dominating Jackson in his opening match as a member of The Triple Threat. Jackson was no match for the Golden One in this bout. The Golden One d. Rufus Lamar Jackson with The Golden Edge. Will 'The Thrill-Pill' Haynes (w/ Intoxication-X) v. 'The King Of Swing' Stevie Richards (w/ The Circus!) Both these men put up a solid match, but the Stevie Kick found its mark in this one, giving Stevie Richards a victory. Stevie Richards d. Will Haynes with the Stevie Kick. 'Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew' Vic Deakins v. IRK Deke put up a good fight, but seemed a little rusty, or something to that effect, as IRK's technical-based attack seemed to catch him somewhat off-guard. As a result, IRK won the match fairly easily, but not before a flurry from Deakins which sent the crowd into a frenzy, lasting until IRK backdropped a Deakins EMP attempt. IRK d. Vic Deakins by pinfall with the Killer Leglock. Four Corners Match for the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship 'Outlaw Immortal' KG (w/ Sunny) v. 'Mr. Overhand Right' Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) v. 'Triple Toke' Aaron Martin (w/ Intoxication X) v. 'Scrap Iron' Joe Satre (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Modern Day Warriors) Tonight, I believe this match itself redefined Cruiserweight wrestling as we know it. We saw four men who simply busted their asses to win the Cruiserweight Championship, and it was also announced that the losers of the match would compete in a Triple Threat match at Saturday Night Slam for the #1 Contender spot. As a result, a lot was on the line in this bout. KG and Terry Conklin were, ironically, the first to be eliminated, as the two men fought on the outside of the ring a little too long, and were counted out. The aftermath of that was indeed a shocker, as when Joe Satre appeared to take control, Shawn Copeland ran out (the official was tending to KG and Conklin, who were still fighting), and bashed Satre over the head with the European title belt. From there, Martin dominated the match, eventually hitting the Shoulderbreaker and becoming the first ever 4 time champion in the EWA. Aaron Martin wins the Four Corners match. Aaron Martin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion! Six Man Tag Team Match Affirmitive Action & Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. 'The Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels (w/ Elizabeth), 'The ICON' Erik Draven (EWA North American Champion), & 'The Money Maker' Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ The Dream Team) As of late, No Quarter, well-known to fans, has been slumping. Not tonight. Tonight, they gave Michaels, Copeland, and Draven everything they had, and due to Castor Troy, everything they had was indeed enough. Shawn Michaels was in the ring against 2 Cold Scorpio, and when Michaels went for the Sweet Chin Music, Scorpio ducked, and Michaels hit the official. Castor then tossed the suitcase he was carrying to Scorpio, allowing him to blast Michaels with it, and get the three-count when the referee woke up. A brawl then ensued between the six men. (While all 6 men are fighting in the ring, "Fed Up" by House of Pain begins to play over the loudspeaker, and Public Enemy makes it's way through the crowd to the ring, and immediately go after all three members of No Quarter. Once the ring is cleared of all their members, The Ghetto Boy reaches through the ropes at the ring announcer, who hands him a microphone. Copeland, Draven, and Michaels are standing in the ring, out of breath and in complete shock...) The Ghetto Boy: You know, Copeland. We've been sitting home for the past three months, and non-stop, the phone has been ringing off the hook. People have been calling us saying, "Ghetto, Louie, when are you guys gonna come back?" or "Geesh, you guys were the greatest. Please come back and show these teams what a REAL tag team is all about!" And for three months, the answering machine has been full. Hell, I may have missed a couple of phone calls from the beer vendor, but being that he automatically delivers every Monday, I'm not complaining! What DOES piss me off, though, Copeland, is your scrawny, bitch-ass calling us, leaving messages on my machine, leaving messages with my secretary, leaving e-mails, BEGGING Public Enemy to come back to the wrestling world. You just couldn't leave good enough alone, could ya? Swap Meet Louie: Copeland, let us put it to ya this way. You're a great wrestler. But you're not as great as Public Enemy was, is, or ever will be! Draven is. Michaels is. But (points finger at Copeland) YOU, you're not. (At this time, No Quarter starts to make their way back to the ring, and after Public Enemy...) So, bottom line is this, bucko. As far as us joining up with you, we've only got two words to describe it.... (At this time, Public Enemy turns around and nails Affirmative Action as Copeland comes running from across the ring to take out Thorne. All of a sudden, it's a 5 on 3, as Public Enemy is working with Draven, Michaels, and Copeland. Everyone kicks out No Quarter, and Public Enemy rip off their "NO LIMIT SOLDIERS" t-shirts to reveal DEGENERATION-X t-shirts...) The Ghetto Boy: That's right, boys and girls, Degeneration X is back, and it's better than ever! We're got the "Immortal ICON of e-wrestling," and I don't mean Zooter Harris, Erik Draven! We've got the one and only "Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels! We've got "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland. And you've got the greatest tag team to ever grace this very damn ring with their presence, "The No Limit Soldiers" Public Enemy! Great as individuals, but together, we're UNSTOPPABLE! Swap Meet Louie: Harlem Heat, Eliminators, what we just did to you tonight was the tip of the iceberg, and now you're about to sink quicker than the damn Titanic! We've sat back, watched your sorry asses destroy what took us two months to bring to it's highest level ever, the EWA World Tag Team Titles! And we don't care who the hell it is. If it's the Eliminators, Intoxication X, Harlem Heat, it don't really matter! Even if we've got to defeat each and every damn one of ya to get at those tag belts, you can bet that lone dollar bill in your pocket that we're gonna get it done! You poured the gasoline on the wood, and we're the match that's gonna blow it back up into your face! 100,000 Harlem Heat Degrees? Hell, boys, you'd better put those ice cubes away, because when we turn it up a notch, son, you're playing with fire that's gonna burn ya! Eliminators? YOU retired us? Nuh-uh, I don't think so! We practically laid down for you because of the bulls**t Gates was trying to pull. Having us job to a team of jabronies like yourselves is like saying Gates and Collins are the best of friends! Never will happen! Intoxication X? Hell, you boys must have been smoking too much of that wacky weed to think that you could hang with a team like Public Enemy. Bottom line is this, Public Enemy is back. DeGeneration X is back, and Gates... Ohh, Gates, we've got just two little words for ya.... SUCK IT!!! (The "No Limit Soldiers" re-mixed version of the D-X theme begins to play as all 5 men leave the ring together, allowing Gates to get his lackluster Main Events going...) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Return Steel Cage Triangle Match for the EWA World Television Championship Mayne Mann v. The Gunslinger v. Crazy Canuck (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The PHC) This match was all it was billed up to be. The three men fought inside the cage for over seventeen minutes, admist numerous attempts at climbing the cage, as the goal was to be the first man out of the cage. Finally, it was Crazy Canuck and The Gunslinger fighting on the top of the cage, slugging it out, Gunslinger on the outside, Canuck on the inside. Mayne Mann climbed to the top to join the fray, and succeeded in knocking Canuck to the outside. He then leveled Gunslinger with a hard right, which ended up backfiring, as it knocked Gunslinger clear off the cage...and onto the floor. The Gunslinger d. Crazy Canuck and Mayne Mann in the steel cage. The Gunslinger is the new, 4 time EWA World Television Champion! EWA World Heavyweight Championship Respect Match 'The Franchise' Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion) v. 'Mr. Every Night' Justin Hicks (EWA World Heavyweight Champion) Bunda obviously came out first, to a chorus of loud boos, due to his treatment of Justin Hicks lately, and was carrying both the EWA Intercontinental Championship and the WAA World Heavyweight Championship. Hicks then came out, to a standing ovation. The Golden One was then announced as the special referee, and once in the ring - the fight was on. Hicks and Bunda stared each other down for about thirty seconds, then Hicks ran at Bunda for a collar-and-elbow tie up ... and Bunda quickly ducked down, scooped up Hicks, and hit him with a body slam. Bunda went for the cover, and after a SLOW, FAIR Three Count executed by The Golden One, Jeff Bunda was once again EWA Heavyweight Champion of the World. Hicks gets up, with Bunda's help, and begins laughing hysetrically. The fans boo, as they know they have been played into a bigger and better Triple Threat scheme. Bunda goes outside and gives Hicks the microphone, and takes his EWA World Championship belt. He straps it around his waist, puts the Intercontinental Championship on his right shoulder and the WAA World Championship on his left. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks: HA! We played you all like a bunch of FOOLS. You thought that we hated each other? WRONG. And you thought I was the WORLD CHAMPION? Wrong again. See, I don't RUN THE SHOW - I AM THE SHOW. Jeff Bunda's the man who runs things around here, and it's about time that the entire EWA realized that. He's the rightful World Champion, and a damn good one at that - just as I'm the rightful EWA World Television Champion. So, I'm throwing out a challenge - for Saturday Night, "Best Wrestler on Television" respect match - to The Gunslinger. So, you think you're the show? You think you're the man who brings in the ratings? Face Mr. Every Night and see. And you'll see what all the people see every week - that I'm the best man on television THAT THERE IS. (Hicks hands the microphone to Bunda.) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda: Well, it looks like we fooled you all again. NO ONE in this promotion has what it takes to keep me from the top - and I've proven that time and time again. And now that I'm the SEVEN TIME World's Heavyweight Champion, I've proven it once again. You thought that Justin and I were going to split up? Doubt it. When you go Triple Threat - and I'm talking REALLY go Triple Threat - you go FOR LIFE. Triple Threat's more than a stable - it's an attitude. And everyone who's ever held up these Three Fingers knows what I'm talking about - Aaron Martin, Stevie Richards - all of them. They know that the Symbol of Excellence shows that you can still get your sh*t done while doing what you want - and you know, that since WE RUN THIS SHOW we can do whatever we want, whenever we want to. And right now we want to go celebrate. So you all can just go back to your mundane lives and WISH that you could be me. Or Hicks, or Becca, or The Golden One ... whichever one strikes your fancy. (Bunda throws the mic and walks away, holding up the Three Fingers. The camera fades to black.) Jeff Bunda d. Justin Hicks by pinfall after a bodyslam. Jeff Bunda is the NEW EWA World Heavyweight Champion!
