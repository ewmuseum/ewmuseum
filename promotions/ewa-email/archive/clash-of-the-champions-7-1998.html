Adam Michaels & Chris Craft v. Garrett and Raoul This was a last minute addition to the Clash of the Champions card, and was one of two dark matches. Michaels and Craft were seeking revenge from the previous night, but frankly were unable to extract it, as Garrett and Raoul made a successful debut. Garrett & Raoul d. Michaels/Craft with Garrett's submission win on Chris Craft with a chicken-wing. Joe Lemon v. Christian Peterson The second dark match was another squash, as Peterson, making his professional wrestling debut, was able to quickly take out Lemon. Christian Peterson d. Joe Lemon with the DDT.
