EWA Saturday Night Slam (2/13/99) February 13, 1999 Live, from the Arco Arena Sacramento, California The Widowmaker v. Vincent Jackson [with Christina Knight] Well, The Widowmaker did what he does best...get squashed. Jackson, making his return to wrestling action since being 'dug up' by The Rock, totally dominated this one, finishing off Widowmaker with the Fallen Angel. Vincent Jackson d. The Widowmaker with The Fallen Angel. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match 'Triple-Toke' Aaron Martin v. Max Pins [EWA Light Heavyweight Championship] Pins was pretty much slaughtered in this one. Martin looked extremely impressive, and by five minutes after the match had started, Martin had accomplished an amazing feat -- The first ever 6-Time EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. Aaron Martin d. Max Pins with the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin is now a 6-Time EWA Light Heavyweight Champion! Philip Donovan v. Prodigy This match wasn't a very good one, and ended in a double disqualification...the more interesting turn of events occured afterward. VA: Wait a minute, looks who's walking out down the entrance ramp. Raven is here!!! JR: Raven is walking towards the ring area, and Prodigy doesn't see him at all. And Raven has a steel chair in hand, and I don't think it's to watch the match from ringside. Business is about to pick here at Saturday Night Slam! Raven has crawled slowly into the ring behind, Prodigy and he's getting ready to level him with the chair. If Prodigy turns around, you'll hear this shot echo throughout the entire arena! VA: He might not have time *crowd explodes* Kanyon is running down to the ringside area as well!! JR: Kanyon is in the ring, he turns Raven around...FLATLINER!!! And now Prodigy turns around, and all he sees is Raven lying on the mat in pain, and Kanyon standing over him. Kanyon rolls Raven outside the ring! VA: And I think Prodigy realizes just what Kanyon did now, as Raven is walking back to the locker room holding his head, and Kanyon and Prodigy are in the ring. JR: There's the handshake and a hug, as Kanyon raises the hand of Prodigy, and the United States Champion is finally showing signs that he's grown up and gotten out of the influence of Raven. And remember folks, at the Clash of the Champions, it will be Prodigy as the referee, and he'll hope to raise Kanyon's hand there when he faces Raven for that United States Title. But man oh man, Prodigy owes Kanyon a bottle of aspirin for preventing that headache. Bubba Odell [with Paul Role] v. Bubba Odell by forfeit. 1999 King Of The Ring Tournament Qualifying Match The Rock [EWA World Television Champion] v. The Golden One [with The Triple Threat] This was the best match of the night...hell, what am I saying? It was the only competitive match. The Rock started off with an early advantage, but much to the delight of the crowd, The Golden One roared back. TGO began taking control of the match, until The Rock was able to reverse a German suplex into one of his own. From there, Rock hit The People's Elbow, and then set TGO up for the Rock Bottom. However, TGO kneed Rock in the stomach, and nailed The Rock with the Gold Medal, enabling him to get a victory. The bell rings and the referee raises The Golden One's hand, officially signifying his victory and spot in the King of the Ring Tournament. As always, he climbs to the second turnbuckle and raises his hands in the air, only this time it's a bit weird because he doesn't have a gold belt in his hands. He jumps off the turnbuckle and grabs a microphone... The Golden One: I'm gonna make this short and I'm gonna make this sweet. Now for the last week, people have been wondering exactly why I haven't gotten back to Kanyon about his offer for a rematch at March Madness '99. People have questioned my "testicular fortitude," claiming "The Shining Star" doesn't have what it takes to dance with Kanyon. The truth of the matter is that I was just being a nice guy. You see folks, the EWA United States Heavyweight Title means little to me. I held it for two weeks and in that time, me and the belt didn't really bond. Maybe if I had it a little longer, something emotional attachment would have developed, but two weeks doesn't do the trick. Now I happen to have a lot of respect for Kanyon. I always have. I originally planned on coming out here and talking about what a great guy he was and what a deserving contender. But when he started questioning me the way he did, I decided to play hard ball, and I hit a friggin grand slam if you ask me. But Kanyon won the match and took the title. Mr. Tenacity moves on and never looks back. So Kanyon, you go out there and settle your issue with Raven, because I've got something else I want to take care of. You see folks, when I first signed on the dotted line with the EWA back in late March, I was in mid-card heaven. I was battling the likes of Gunslinger, hoping to one day crawl out of the cellar of the EWA. While down there, I won the EWA World Television Title, and forfeited it 4 days later to move on to bigger and better things. And where did I move to? The EWA Intercontinental Title, which at the time, was the most prestigious title this promotion had to offer. Because while the World Heavyweight Title was swapping hands left and right, the Intercontinental Title was being held solidly by one man, a true legend in the sport. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda held the title for 125 days before being stripped of it because of a new rule. I had numerous battles with Jeff, and was all set to win the title until Justin Hicks interfered and gave me the DQ victory. I was this close *TGO puts his thumb and forefinger a milimeter apart* to becoming the EWA Intercontinental Champion, and the rest is history. Jeff, Justin, and myself have become the best of friends, forming the new and improved Triple Threat along with Terry. We've all seen massive success, and we always will. Personally, I became World Champ and U.S. Champ over the course of the last month, and now it's time to finish some unfinished business from the spring. The EWA Intercontinental Title has seen its rapid demise since Jeff was forced to forfeit. With the exception of Terry, losers like Grady Smith and The Crock, the very same Crock I just beat, have held it. That all ends as far as I'm concerned. The EWA Intercontinental Title was, is, and always will be the Triple Threat's belt. So Jack Reed, current champion, let's see if you can hang with the big boys. Jeff is already the EWA European Champion and soon to be World Champion. Hell, tonight, Jeff, along with Terry, is gonna win back the World Tag Team Titles. Since I'm letting Kanyon enjoy his first singles title a while, I've decided to truly make the Triple Threat SOLID GOLD in another way. Jack Reed, "The Walking Triple Threat." You've got the belt I want. Now come hell or high water, I'm gonna beat you for it sooner or later, but since time is of the essence, let's shoot for sooner. Next Saturday, February the 20th, McNichols Arena in Denver, Colorado, me and you Reed for the EWA Intercontinental Title. And you know something Jack, the last time I stepped into the ring with that belt on the line, it was in a steel cage and Jeff and I fought tooth and nail, but he emerged victorious. This time Reed, you and me, one on one in a Steel Cage. It's gonna be the biggest Saturday Night Slam ever. Jeff vs. Smirt for the World Title, me vs. you for the Intercontinental Title. How's that for a double main event. The ball is in your court Reed, don't fumble it. Until then folks, this has been yet another GOLDEN night. The Golden One d. The Rock with the Gold Medal. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match 'The Franchise' Jeff Bunda [EWA European Champion] & 'Mr. Overhand Right' Terry Conklin [with The Triple Threat] v. The Public Enemy [EWA World Tag Team Champions, with Grady Smith] The Public Enemy looked pretty off their game tonight, while The Franchise and Mr. Overhand Right were far from off. The two Triple Threat members showed excellent teamwork together, and were able to co-exist well together. The result was new EWA World Tag Team Champions. Jeff Bunda/Terry Conklin d. The Public Enemy with Jeff Bunda's pin on Swap Meet Louie after the Showrunner. Jeff bunda & Terry Conklin are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions! Special Tag Team Attraction Match E-'Nuff [EWA Hardcore Champion] & 'The Walking Triple Threat' Jack Reed [EWA Intercontinental Champion] v. 'The ICON' Erik Draven & 'God's Gift To Wrestling' SmirtDogg [EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X] Partners In Crime looked like jailbirds tonight. Draven never even got the chance to tag in, as SmirtDogg did a number on the two men, and was able to obtain the victory for D-Generation X. SmirtDogg & Erik Draven d.
