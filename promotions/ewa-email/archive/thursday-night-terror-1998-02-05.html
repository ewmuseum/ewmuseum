EWA Thursday Night Terror

5 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 5, 1998 Live, from Reunion Arena in Dallas, Texas. Zach Kraay (w/ Bubba) v. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Well, this one was as ugly as everyone was afraid it would be...if not worse. Kraay would've been better served just staying at home and watching the action on TV, instead of actually showing up at the Arena, and getting his head handed to him by TGC. Total domination by TG...Er, scratch that. Not 'Total'...Kraay did get in one punch, that totally missed the mark, of course. When it was all said and done, a well-placed Superkick from TGC ended Kraay's suffering, and just about took his head off in the process. -- The Golden Child d. Zach Kraay via the Royal Destroyer. (7:23) Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Kama Mustafa (w/ The Nation Of Domination) Kama Mustafa was not on his game tonight. In fact, he was about as far from his game as he could be... Ahmed Johnson destroyed the NOD member with power move after power move, hitting a scissor kick to the back of Kama's head that almost put him right through the mat. When it was all said and done, Ahmed put Kama away with the Pearl River Plunge for the victory. -- Ahmed Johnson d. Kama Mustafa via the Pearl River Plunge. (8:10) El Locco Jobbo v. The Widow Maker (w/ The Widow) There must've been something in the air tonight... Once again, the people attending TNT bore witness to one of the most one-sided matches in recent history. Jobbo made an impressive debut, dominating the Widow Maker from bell to bell, finally polishing him off at the 5-minute mark with the Jobbo Drop. -- El Locco Jobbo d. The Widow Maker via the Jobbo Drop. (5:04) "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ Katie) v. Michael Simurda Another match, another rookie debut, and another squash. Simurda should've joined Kraay in taking the night off, as Gardner didn't even break a sweat in taking him out with the Pretty Boy Bomb at the two-minute mark. -- "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner d. Michael Simurda via the Pretty Boy Bomb. (2:01) Non-Title Match Faarooq (w/ The Nation Of Domination) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Once again, ugly wasn't the word for this match. The leader of the Nation of Domination was, well...dominated by the ICON. Even when the other two remaining members of the Nation got in on the action, Draven ran the show. A DX Driver for D-Lo, Kama went airborne over the top rope, and Faarooq fell victim to Draven's Fallen Angel for the victory. -- "The ICON" Erik Draven d. Faarooq via the Fallen Angel. (9:23) Chris "The Rock" Gardner v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia The Battle of the Rocks was quick, and ugly as all hell. Maivia showed zero offense, and was quickly felled by Gardner's Sledgehammer for the victory. -- Chris "The Rock" Gardner d. Rocky Maivia via the Sledgehammer. (5:10) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. Kris Gaffney The veteran Deathstar appears to have picked it up a notch, making short work of Kris Gaffney in his debut in the EWA. Gaffney got tossed from pillar to post, bounced off every turnbuckle in the ring at least 5 times, and finally just collapsed in the center of the ring, where Deathstar made a one-finger cover for the victory. -- Deathstar d. Kris Gaffney via pinfall. (12:20) Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. D-Lo Brown (w/ The Nation Of Domination) D-Lo was virtually unconscious from the beating he took earlier, and it showed... Galaxy made the NOD's night an even 0 for 3 with a quick beatdown, followed by the SuperNova for the victory. -- Galaxy d. D-Lo Brown via the SuperNova. (4:59) Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) Kane notches another quick victory, this time using Marc Anthony as a punching bag. Anthony did attempt to mount an offense, but Kane didn't even flinch from the blows, dropping Anthony with a chokeslam before delivering the Tombstone Piledriver. -- Kane d. Marc Anthony via the Tombstone Piledriver. (1:20) "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) Benoit no-showed this match, but Austin refused to take just a forfeit victory, and called for anyone in the back to come out. A few moments passed, and finally Serpent Man was tossed through the curtain by a group of low-carders backstage. He timidly approached the ring, entered, and was met by Austin extending his hand for a handshake. A perplexed Serpent Man accepted the handshake, only to get Austin's patented 'KFS' combo for his troubles. (Kick to stomach, middle Finger in the face, Stone Cold Stunner.) -- 'Stone Cold' Steve Austin d. Serpent Man via the Stone Cold Stunner. (0:20) "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) v. Vinnie Vegas Neither man bothered to show up, which prompted an announcement from Commissioner Slaughter that both men had been fired from the EWA, along with the rest of the Horsemen. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match The Disco Inferno (w/ Flash Funk) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Disco is dead. Well, maybe not dead, but in a hell of a lot of pain after tonight's match. SmirtDogg took advantage of Disco's inexperience in the ring, capitalizing on every missed move to the fullest extent. However, Disco did mount a bit of a comeback, even coming this close to hitting his Chartbuster on the champion, but Smirt saw it coming and managed to hold onto the ropes, sending Disco crashing down to the mat by himself. A couple of leg and back-weakening moves later, and Disco found himself on the wrong end of Smirt's Thug Breaker, and was forced to tap out. -- SmirtDogg d. Disco Inferno by submission, via the Thug Breaker. (14:10) *** SmirtDogg retains the Lightweight Title. The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) v. Death Sentence (w/ Precious) On paper, this match looked extremely uneven. Death Sentence was just coming off of a hard defeat at the hands of Erik Draven, meanwhile the Outsiders were riding the crest of a large winning streak, both in singles and tag competition. However, Death Sentence did prove that they are legitimate contenders here in the EWA, taking the Outsiders to the limit. Pain had Scott Hall set up for the Deathblow, but got distracted when Chyna began to harass Precious. This allowed Hall to make the tag to Nash, who promptly kicked Pain in the stomach, and delivered the Jackknife for the victory. After the match, both stables got into another fight in the ring, and finally had to be broken up by the Arena Security team. -- The Outsiders d. Death Sentence, via Nash's Jackknife on Pain. (14:10) The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) v. Prophecy (w/ The Servants of Twlight) The Outlaws made their traditional cocky entrance to the ring, and got blindsided by Prophecy before even getting halfway down the runway. The ref warned both teams to get into the ring, and finally ended up ringing the bell, and counting both teams out. A rematch has been rumored, but not signed yet. -- The New Age Outlaws and Prophecy fought to a Double-CO. (0:10) The Pessimist (w/ The Servants of Twlight) v. Flash Funk (w/ The Disco Inferno) Bad night to be a fan of the '70's... Flash Funk ran into a Pessimist who was on the downside of a mid-size losing streak, and a Pessimist who was determined to do something about it. And that he did, and then some... Funk hit a couple of high-risk moves, but when he attempted the Funky Flash Splash, he missed by a good three feet. The Pessimist seized on the opportunity, and slapped on the Suicidal Dream (sleeperhold) when the Funkstah got back to his feet. Three arm-raises and arm-falls later, and the Pessimist emerged victorious. -- The Pessimist d. Flash Funk by submission, via the Suicidal Dream. (9:20) 10 Man Tag Team Match The Empire (Augustus/Octavius/Tiberus/Julius/Nero) v. The Stud Stable (Scott Cummings/Shawn Hart/Matthew Hart/Harley Hart/KG) This match had every indication of being a classic...on paper. In person, however...this match was about as one-sided as could be. The Studs seemed intent on shrugging off their 'underachiever' tag, and unfortunately for the Empire, tonight they were firing on all cylinders. Julius got cut off for about 90% of the match, and got stuck fending off all five Studs through quick tags and double-teaming. Finally, he made the tag to Nero, but he ended up experiencing more of the same. Eventually, Shawn Hart got the victory over Nero via the Lightning Strike. -- The Stud Stable d. The Empire via S. Hart's Lightning Strike on Nero. (16:30)
