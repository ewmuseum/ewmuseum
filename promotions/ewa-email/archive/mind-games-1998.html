EWA Mind Games

23 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

May 23, 1998 Live, from Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ---- Triangle Match Furnace v. Lee Tenn v. The Widow Maker Furnace made an unimpressive EWA debut...by not showing up. That turned this contest into a mano y mano match between Teen & TWM. TWM started off with the early advantage, pummeling Tenn with a variety of suplexes. Tenn fought back well, however, and secured the victory in his EWA debut. Lee Tenn d. The Widow Maker with The Hang Tenn. Submission Match Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) This was an exciting match, full of technical moves. Around the fifteen minute mark, Taz snapped on the Tazmission, but Makaveli barely made it to the ropes. The match went on, and when Makaveli locked on the Death Row Leglock, Taz had no choice but to tap out. Makaveli d. Taz by submission with The Death Row Leglock. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) If Jackson wins, he gets to host the Smack Shack for a day; if Van Smack wins, he gets to shave Jackson's afro. Rufus, making his EWA debut, looked fantastic, plain and simple. He absolutely destroyed Van Smack, embarassing the supposed "legend". Afterward, Van Smack said that he was leaving the EWA, but President Gates confiscated The Smack Shack equipment for Rufus to use. Rufus Lamar Jackson III d. Van Smack with The Flip, Flop, And Fly. Texas Tornado Match Natural Born Thrillers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. The New Age Outlaws Boy, this match left much to be desired. Dos Equis didn't show, and NAO/NBT brawled on the outside from the onset. The New Age Outlaws & The Natural Born Thrillers battled to a double countout. The Swing Pad (hosted by EWA World Television Champion Dancin' Stevie Richards) (It's now time for the most popular segment of any EWA show. It's time for the Swing Pad with Dancin' Stevie Richards.) Stevie Richards: O.K. Ladies and germs, I would like to welcome you to a VERY special edition of the Swing Pad. Not only is tonight a pay per view but I have a VERY special guest. Ladies and germs, please welcome to the Swing Pad, the President of the EWA, Duane Gates. (Gates comes out to a mixed reaction) Mr. Gates, I would like to thank you for being my guest this week on the Swing Pad. Now I'm not going to beat around the bush here. What were you thinking when you set up this match for tonight's pay per view? President Gates: Well, Mr. Richards, I was hoping to provide the fans of the EWA with an exciting matchup, with three top superstars in professional wrestling today. Stevie: Yeah right. Whatever. You knew that Stevie Richards and the Triple Threat were ready to take over your little federation and you feared it. Didn't you?!?!?!? President Gates: Take over the federation? Of course not. That's insane. I simply wanted to provide the EWA fans with a quality matchup, and give you, Mr. McCammon, and Mr. Bunda some quality competition. Stevie: Well you can claim all you want but it seems a little shady to me. YOU know that Stevie Richards and the Triple Threat are tight. And it scared you. So you're doing everything in your power to divide and conquer. Well I got news for you Duane-O, it ain't gonna work. President Gates: Divide and conquer? Hardly my plans, Mr. Richards. Are you telling me that you don't appreciate a shot at the second most prestigious championship in the EWA, the EWA Intercontinental Championship? Stevie: I don't need that title. I've got a little belt of my own. You see this (shoves the TV belt in Gates' face). I got this title. I'm trying to make this title into something big much like Jeff Bunda did with the Intercontinental title. I don't want his title. I know he doesn't want mine. You're just looking for a way to put the screws to us. President Gates: Well, Stevie, if that's the way you feel, I can't really argue with you. However, I can reassure you that we in the EWA Front Offices are only doing what we think the fans of the EWA would enjoy. In fact, Mr. Richards, you can't really say that I'm screwing over The Triple Threat. I think that this is as good a time as any to announce that next month, at EWA Summer Breakout '98, live from the beautiful beach of Venice Beach, California, the main event will consist of the EWA World Heavyweight Champion, whomever that may be, defending the title against "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda. Stevie: Well big friggin' deal. You're just trying to cover your ass. Fair enough. I'll let it go. Now my next question. What finally compelled you to give me my own show? Was it the fact that you know that Dancin' Stevie Richards will bring in higher ratings? President Gates: Well, we were looking for a way to spice up EWA programming, so we decided to give you a show at one of our non-televised events to see what you could do. And like the fans, we were impressed, and so the Swing Pad was born. Stevie: It was born because you know that Stevie Richards is the most charasmatic, talented, and best looking wrestler you've got going for you and you're just going to ride me for all I'm worth aren't you? President Gates: Well, Mr. Richards, you certainly are a talented young athlete, as evident by your EWA World Television Championship. As far as charasmatic, you indeed are, as are a lot of our athletes. And good-looking...well...I wouldn't know about that, Mr. Richards. Stevie: Well all the chicks out there know that Stevie Richards is the best looking thing in the EWA. Right ladies? (ladies scream) See, Duane-O? I told you. Now we're almost out of time. Do you have any last comments? President Gates: Well, Mr. Richards, it's been a priviledge being your guest on the Swing Pad this evening. I want to wish you good luck in your match tonight, and to all you fans out there, enjoy Mind Games. Stevie: Well Duane-O, I'd like to thank you for being here. You showed me that you have some b*lls by coming out here knowing that I'm not exactly cool with your decisions. We may not see eye to eye on everything but I respect what you're trying to do. Just one more question though. President Gates: And what is that, Mr. Richards? Stevie: Are the stories about you and the goats true? (Gates gets an extremely angry look on his face) President Gates: HOW DARE YOU BRING ME OUT HERE AND ASK THAT KIND OF QUESTION!!! Stevie: Well the people demand to know. Dancin' Stevie Richards is a man of the people and I'm a serious journalist so I have to ask the tough questions. President Gates: That question is a disgrace to journalists!! Of course it's not true!! Who in the hell told you that?? Stevie: Jeff Bunda told me. What about the chickens? President Gates: Mr. Richards, if you are going to continue to ask a bunch of nonsense questions, then I'm leaving. Stevie: But Jeff told me that you had like three chickens that you well for lack of a better term, bump uglies with. (At this point, Gates looks mad enough to kill Richards. He glares at Stevie, and shakes his head.) President Gates: I KNEW it was a mistake giving you this show. If it weren't in your contract, I'd have fired you from it. Goodbye, Mr. Richards. Stevie: Later Duane-O, please come back!!! EWA United States Tag Team Championship Steel Cage Match Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) Prophecy started out this contest by dominating The Eliminators...but soon, Perry and John took over the match. Prophecy made a short comeback at the end, but The Eliminatorsd showed their championship poise by retaining control of the match. The Eliminators d. Prophecy with John Kronus' pin on Timothy David after The Total Elimination. The Eliminators retain the EWA U.S. Tag Team Titles. Australian Death Match -- All Weapons Are Legal Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable & Anna) v. KG (w/ Sunny) KG came to the ring carrying a stuffed kangaroo, and proceeded to rip it apart in front of Hart. KG looked extremely impressive, finally meeting his former stable-mate after a 1 1/2 month wait. And KG took Hart apart, dominating him in every aspect of the match. KG d. Shawn Hart with a chair shot to the head. Strap Match The Golden One v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat & Rebecca Claridge) Another squash; The Golden One was placed in the Saturday Night Special, when a loud POP was heard. From there, Hicks drug TGO around the ring, touching all four posts. It was later determined that TGO suffered a dislocated kneecap. Justin Hicks d. The Golden One. No Holds Barred, Falls Count Anywhere, Loser Leaves The EWA Match For the EWA World Tag Team Championship New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) This match was all over the arena, finally ending in the parking lot of The Skydome when Ghetto Boy powerslammed Semi onto a limousine, breaking the windshield. The limo was later discovered to be that of President Gates. Public Enemy d. New Demolition Triple Threat Match for the EWA World Television/Intercontinental Titles Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA World Television Champion) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Rules: First pinfall wins. If Richards is the man who is pinned, he loses the TV Title to the man who defeated him. If Bunda is the man defeated, he loses his title to the man who defeated him. For any other questions, email me. The camera panned to the Triple Threat locker room, where an EWA Staff member told Stevie and Bunda that they were needed for a quick photo shoot. After the two left, Jack McCammon appeared, and chained the door to the TT locker room. McCammon then came through the crowd for the match, and while Stevie had his back turned, handcuffed Stevie to the ringpost. From there, it was a fierce battle between Bunda and McCammon. At one point, one of the members of the Crippled Midgets of Doom came to the ring with a hacksaw, and cut the chain attaching Stevie to the post. At that point, Bunda was laid out by McCammon with a chair. Richards ran in the ring, but McCammon saw him coming, and laid him out as well. McCammon then went to the top rope, and when Stevie got up, nailed the Double Hammer on him, getting the three count. The camera then switched backstage, where we saw the Triple Threat locker room door busted open. The camera switched back to the ring, and The Triple Threat members were seen attacking McCammon. Finally, security ushered the TT out. Jack McCammon pins Stevie Richards after the Double Hammer. Jack McCammon is the new EWA World Television Champion!! Arizona Death Match The Gunslinger v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) No DQ, No countout, falls count anywhere. After a pinfall, a ten count will be issued. If the man who was pinned cannot answer the ten count, the match is over and he loses. Well, so much for a competitive Death Match. This was supposed to be The Gunslinger's "signature match", but Triple-H took control at the onset, and never looked back. Triple-H d. The Gunslinger after hitting The Pedigree, and The Gunslinger not answering the 10 count. Wyldstyle Match -- Closed fists, chokeholds, etc., are illegal Thorne Richards v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) The only way to win is by submission or disqualification; closed fists, chokeholds, etc., will result in a DQ. The Wyldsyde's U.S. Title will -not- be on the line. The Wyldstyle match proved to be too much for Richards. On a number of occasions, he was nearly disqualified for throwing a punch, or executing a chokehold. With some basic moves taken away from Thorne, he seemed to be off his game, and The Wyldsyde forced him to submit to his own move. Afterward, Thorne & Castor announced they were taking a 3-week vacation. The Wyldsyde d. Thorne Richards by submission with The Loch Ness Lock. (The crowd is anxiously awaiting the construction of the cage for the Draven/Prodigy match, when 'Perfect Strangers' hits the PA. They all rise to see Jeff Bunda, STILL the EWA Intercontinental Champion, walk out to the entranceway.) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda: Well, isn't it good to see me again, Toronto? See, after I beat the hell out of Jack McCammon tonight, I felt it only necessary that you all get another little dose of Franchismo, the greatest substance on the face of the earth today. But what's even more important is the announcement about the Summer Breakout, the Pay-Per-View where the Era of the Franchise will truly begin. At that Pay-Per-View, the EWA will crown the greatest superstar in the history of the business, me, the Heavyweight Champion of the World. Why, do you ask? Let's look at the contenders for tonight's title match, and I'll tell you why, because it's probable that I'll be facing one of these two men. First off, there's Prodigy. A man who holds a victory over me from the IWCT. Well, I wasn't too concerned about the IWCT, and hell, I'll admit, I didn't put much of an effort into the matchup. But, no excuses. We're focusing on the present. At the IWCT, we both agreed that that match would be for who was to control the business here in the future - and now we might just have a return match. Except this time, the Era of the Franchise will begin, guarenteed. Prodigy, you're on top of the world right now. You're the EWA World Champion. You're kicking ass and taking names in the IWCT. But the Franchise is here to give you a little dose of reality. Face it, Prodigy, I'm focused like hell on winning this title, and there isn't a damn thing you can do to de-rail the train of victories I'm going to amass here in the near future and have, proving my worth here in the EWA. But, Prodigy, if I must admit, I'm not to impressed with you. You come out here, once and a while, blabbin' and blowin' out your ass about how freakin' great you are. Well, son, prove it in the ring at the Summer Breakout. I know that I'm great, and I let everyone in the EWA know it. But by being the greatest Intercontinental Champion of ALL TIME here in the EWA wasn't proof-positive enough, then, when I win that World Heavyweight Title, that will be undeliable proof. And then there's Erik Draven. My god, how many times to I have to face this man before he realizes I could kick his ass with both arms tied behind my back? We've headlined Pay-Per-Views before, or shows, or whatever, and I've whipped him so many times Reddi-Whip wants footage for their next commercial. I've beaten Draven for two titles, and it's about to be a third. You know why, Erik? Frankly, you suck. You used to be good - but you're past your prime. You're like Thorne Richards - a Fallen Superstar. Your career is on the downfall here in professional wrestling, as proved by our encounters in the NIWA and the AEWF. When I unified the WAA and FCEW World Belts into the NIWA Unified World Heavyweight Championship in April, I wasn't half as talented as I am now. To be honest with you, I was still a bit of a rookie. I wasn't used to my sucess here in the business. But now that I've had a few matches, gained some experience, I've learned something - Draven, you're nothing but an over-rated mid-carder. Draven, you go to feds, desparate for talent, and you win all the belts and then you think you're some sort of ICON. Well, whether you call yourself that or not, I know that to be the truth. So, you know as well as I do that the ICON took a huge blow back in April when this upstart knocked the hell out of him. You know why I knocked the hell out of you, Draven? Because I'm the damn best talent this sport has ever seen. Draven, I've proven to you and to the world more than once that I do run the show around here, and no one else can debate that. Not you, not Duane Gates, and certainly not Prodigy. And that brings me to Duane Gates. See, this guy has to be the biggest idiot on the face of the Earth. I mean, look what he's throwing at you for a main-event matchup? Dradle v. Prodi-potty? How the hell is that supposed to keep you entertained? That's why I came out here, folks, is so that you'd feel that your money was well spent on this EWA Pay-Per-View event. I've told you once, and I'll tell you again - Duane Gates is the biggest moron this business has ever seen. I mean, just look at the way he promotes talent? It's self-explanatory, but finally, he's not trying to screw the Franchise. By the way, Duane, nice way to cover your ass. Just make sure when the cows get hard-ons not to - it works best for them if your hands aren't over your asshole. (Bunda drops the mic and walks to the back.) Steel Cage Death Match For the EWA World Heavyweight Championship "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) v. Prodigy (EWA World Heavyweight Champion) A wall of fire will surround the ringside area to prevent any interference. What was possibly the most anticipated EWA World Title match in history turned out to be arguably the most disappointing. Both men seemed off their games, and nobody from DX even came near ringside due to the fire. After an extremely slow-paced 16 minutes of action, Draven nailed the DX Driver on Prodigy, 3 times. Prodigy had to be stretchered out of the arena, and as we went off the air, it was announced that Prodigy had suffered an injury, and the EWA European Title was being held up. Erik Draven d. Prodigy with the DX Driver. Erik Draven is the new EWA World Heavyweight Champion!!
