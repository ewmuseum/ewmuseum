EWA Saturday Night Slam

1 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from Mobile Civic Center Arena Mobile, Alabama To open up Saturday Night Slam, the lights go down and the golden beams shine throughout the arena as The Golden One makes his way to the ring, accompanied by The Golden Girls for another episode of The Golden Hour. The Golden One: Well, what a day I've had today. It all began with that dreaded visit to the old doctor's office this morning, and I here I am, standing before you this evening, off of crutches for the first time in months. You see, this shining star is back. I got the ok from the doc and I'm ready to get back in the squared circle. Now obviously I won't be at the same level I was when I left back in May after Hicks broke my ankle. No, I'm not on that level, nor do I ever want to return to that level. You know why, ladies and gentlemen? Because I'm past that level right now. The truth of the matter is, I'm not the same person I was when I last stepped into the ring here. Whether this change was for the better or for the worse is yet to be determined, but I have an idea of which way it will go. But you see, while I've been away, one superstar in the EWA has emerged as one of the top talents. That, of course, being Shawn Copeland. Shawn, an obvious HUGE fan of mine, dons the gold all the time now, being the EWA European Champion. But since I first came back to do this little show of mine, he has decided to obsess with little, meaningless things such as my name. Now, unlike most others in this profession, my name is more of a title that I've earned over the years. "The Money Maker", on the other hand, is more of a self-promotion if you ask me. A catch phrase to draw attention to himself. But last week, at King of the Ring, I took the time to explain to all of you great EWA fans why Shawn is called "The Money Maker" But we'll get back to that soon. First, I just want to ask Shawn something I'm dying to know. You see, I know the ladies love The Golden One. I mean, what's not to love, I'm everything a woman wants, and then some. But you Shawny boy, you're not a woman, at least you weren't last time I heard, yet you seem to be in awe of my presence, and of my ability. I don't know exactly why Shawn, and that's what I'm asking you right now. What is that quality you see in me that makes you want my name, my schtick, my life in general? Because whatever it is Shawn, let me assure you, you don't have it. There is only one Golden One, and that's me. I've proven that throughout my career, and once more isn't going to hurt as far as I'm concerned. So Shawn, why don't you drag your sorry carcass out here and show the shining star some respect. The Golden One and The Golden Girls wait patiently in the ring but Copeland doesn't come out to ringisde. The Golden One: Come on Shawn, I promise I won't hurt you. I'm giving you a huge chance here. I'm letting you come out here, and step into the same ring as the great Golden One. Do you know what that means in terms of boosting your career? Just being in my presence will guarantee you will go to another level, because that's what I do Shawn. I carry others on my back to another level. It's a trademark at this point in my career. So let me ask you again Shawn. Come out here now, and answer my one, simple question. Still no response from Copeland, as The Golden One appears to be more and more annoyed at this lack of respect from the European Champion. The Golden One: OK, I'm not going to sit out here all night and wait for him, so Kim, Carrie, do me a favor. Go backstage there and drag Copeland out here. Remember, he's a lightweight, so if he tries to resist, between the two of you, you should be able to get him to show his scared face here. With that, The Golden Girls exit the ring and head back to the locker room area as The Golden One waits in the ring... The Golden One: Well, while I wait for those two lovely ladies to return, let me get something else off of my chest. You see, at King of the Ring, Jeff Bunda, the Franchise, couldn't quite cut the mustard, against Smirtdogg. Now, everyone remembers what I said back at King of the Ring during my interview with the new EWA World Champ. Well Bunda, you couldn't get the job done. Smirt took care of business inside the squared circle, and fair or unfair, he's got the gold around his waist. Now, I don't necessarily agree with everything the Dogg has done, but he is certainly a worthy champion, so I want to first, congratulate Smirtdogg on his first World Title reign here in the EWA. And second, Jeff Bunda, your day is still coming. It's only a matter of time before you and I get in the ring together and remember Jeff, paybacks are a bitch. And last I recall, I owe you and Hicks one each. Wait, look who's coming now... Camera flashes back to the isle where the Golden Girls are carrying something out to the ring. As the camera zooms in closer and closer, it appears to be a cardboard figure of none other than Shawn Copeland... The Golden One: Hey, well, look who it is. Nice of you to show you face Shawn. I didn't think you had the guts. Now, I know you're in total awe of me, and I don't blame you. I can see that you're both excited to meet me, and nervous of what I might do to you. But remember Shawn, I'm not looking for a fight. After all, I'm still a mini-gimp, because that ankle injury was pretty serious. Now I know it has to be hard to put into words what you're feeling right now, but can you try to tell all the fans out there what it's like to be standing in the same ring as your idol, The Golden One? The Golden One: Wow, I knew I was your idol, but even I didn't realize that you were THIS in awe of me. But anyway Shawn, I've been watching you as of late, and while I haven't been the slightest bit impressed, I am a bit curious. Curious as to why on earth you continue to fight Makaveli? Can you give us an insider information as to your continuous struggle against that loser? Shawn Copeland: The Golden One: Excellent answer. My Shawn, you are very fluent with the English language. Put a mic in front of this guy, ladies and gentlemen, and watch him go. I've got to tell you Shawn, you're really starting to annoy me. I mean, first, you didn't have the courtesy to show up on time, and now you're speaking way too much. Didn't anyone ever teach you to limit your responses to just pertinent information? Come on. Now Shawn, I just have one more issue to address, because as you can tell, the fans are a little upset with your essay-like answers. This issue, of course, being the name thing. Now, we've already covered why I'm The Golden One, and you're not, and now it's time for you to tell the fans exactly why you're called "The Money Maker." And seeing as I'm such a nice guy Shawn, I'm going to help you out with this one. Carrie, if you will. *Carrie hands a bag over to The Golden One* OK, here's a little demonstration for everyone out there. The Golden One starts pulling coins, mostly nickels and dimes, out of the bag and tossing them off the cardboard Copeland. Before he stops, he's thrown at least twenty to thirty coins right off the head of the cardboard figure. The Golden One: OK, there you have it ladies and gentlemen. I can now rest my case. But to be honest with you, enough isn't enough here. Because I'm out for a little fan participation. This is the EWA, and anything can and will happen, so now, at you will, feel free to fire away!!! Follow my lead... With that said, The Golden One continues his assault on the cardboard Copeland with the coins, this time only to be joined by the thousands of fans in attendance. Before it's all said and done, the ring is left with only the Copeland statue and a whole lot of spare change. The camera flashes to The Golden One with The Golden Girls in the aisle. The Golden One: Well, there you have it. There, in the center of the ring, stands your European Champion, the one and only "Money Maker" of the EWA. Shawn, you want to start something, go ahead and be the beginning, because I am the end. So for Kim and Carrie, The Golden Girls, I'm The Golden One, saying goodbye until next week. And remember Bunda, this is the Golden Hour, and I RUN THIS SHOW!! Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication-X) v. Jason Wyldheart Haynes came to the ring....well...look at the name of the tag team...I'm sure you can guess what Haynes was. Anyway, this was a solid contest, with Wyldheart looking very impressive in his EWA debut. The two battled for the full fifteen minutes, as the match ended in a draw. Jason Wyldheart & Will Haynes battled to a time limit draw. Handey Gripwell v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) Double no-show. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. The Gunslinger As La Parka came to the ring, a man dressed in red tights and a "Prodigy Hate Club" shirt ran out and attacked La Parka. This man was later identified as Magnificant Matt, the cousin of EWA President Duane Gates and the newest member of The Prodigy Hate Club. Needless to say, La Parka didn't make it to the ring. The Gunslinger d. La Parka by countout. Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre Reed no-showed, and Satre got a forfeit win. James Mastin v. Johnny 2 Bad Strangely enough, J2B simply walked to the ring, beat Mastin, and then left the arena, never saying a word. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. Vertigo Darkness was left in the dark for this match, as Vertigo found their edge again, and simply destroyed Darkness. Lucien and Dark Angelic Priest never had a chance. Vertigo d. Darkness with Jimmy Pow's pin on Lucien after the Powdriver. IRK v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus) Van Smack did show for this one, and was promptly forced to tap out. IRK d. Van Smack with the Killer Leglock. Ruger Ice v. "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) Before the match, the cameras caught Ruger backstage. Ruger Ice:Well Terry....it seems like it is time to get down to business. I hope you took all of your vitamins to get your body into that crowd pleasing shape. Oh...and SmirtDogg...I am anxious to hear what that answer of yours is. In case I didn't make it clear before, You can leave your gold at home in your closet. All I want from you is to bring your sorry @ss to the ring and say hello to my friend Mr. Strap here. {Ruger holds up a leather strap and then hangs it over his shoulder and heads to the ring} From there, we saw an outstanding contest between Ruger Ice and Terry Conklin, which finally ended after Conklin managed to kick out of the Spinebuster, and execute the Overhand Right. As Conklin got the three count, though, SmirtDogg ran to the ring. *SmirtDogg comes to ringside with a strap in his hand. He executes a Superkick on Terry Conklin and a Gangsta's Paradise DDT on Ruger Ice. SmirtDogg then proceeds to whoop Ruger Ice with the strap continuously. Finally, referees come inbetween and stop Smirt from doing further damage.* Needless to say, Smirt has accepted Ruger's challenge. Terry Conklin d. Ruger Ice with the Overhand Right. Morbid Angel v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (w/ Shae Marks) Deke didn't show up, obviously still upset over the return of Hannibal Manson, and Morbid Angel got the forfeit win. Morbid Angel d. Vic Deakins by countout. Non-Title Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Dream Team) v. The Wyldsyde (EWA North American Champion) Shawn Michaels and The Wyldsyde put on one hell of a show. This match featured many near-falls, but Michaels made a crucial mistake when he went to the top rope to attempt a moonsault. The Wyldsyde caught him in midair, and reversed it into a piledriver. He then hit The Wyldryde, but before he could get a three count, Shawn Copeland, SmirtDogg, and Harlem Heat all ran in and attacked The Wyldsyde, apparently in retaliation for his comments about TDT. The Wyldsyde d. Shawn Michaels by disqualification. Mayne Mann v. KG (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Dream Team) Mann's knee looked pretty bad tonight, and KG capitalized on this, looking terrific in a victory. KG d. Mayne Mann with The Blackout. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus) v. Harlem Heat (w/ The Dream Team) Dos Equis were destroyed, plain and simple. This match wasn't even close to being competitive. Harlem Heat d. Dos Equis with Booker T's pin on Tecate Jake after the Harlem Hangover. Tag Team Grudge Match Crazy Canuck (EWA World Television Champion) & Makaveli (w/ The PHC) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion) & "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Dream Team) This was a solid tag team main event, with SmirtDogg and Canuck highlighting the match with a brawl on the outside of the ring. Ruger Ice tried to run out and interfere, and he and Smirt brawled on the outside while Copeland and Makaveli fought on the inside. Finally the match ended when Copeland was able to hit The Money Shot on Tupac. SmirtDogg and Shawn Copeland d. Crazy Canuck and Makaveli with Shawn Copeland's pin on Makaveli after the Money Shot.
