EWA Wednesday Night War

16 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 16, 1998 Live, from the Barton Colisseum Little Rock, Arkansas Galaxy v. E-Nuff *** E-Nuff d. Galaxy via the Lockdown. KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. "The God Of Violence" Johnny Napalm *** Johnny Napalm d. KooKabura via the Napalm Bomb. The Black Angels v. Team U.S.A. *** Team U.S.A. d. The Black Angels via the Pledge of Allegiance (Liberty's pin on Satchiel) *"Ghetto Superstar" by Pras, featuring Old Dirty Bastard and Mya starts to play over the public address system of the Barton Colisseum. Out from the back comes "Corporate Outlaw" KG. He struts down to ring with an arrogant grin on his face. He gets in the ring and takes the microphone from the ring attendent and begins to speak.* "Corporate Outlaw" KG: Ya know, the EWA's finest wrestlers are in the Corporate Empire, not anywhere else. Not DX, not The Triple Threat. Right here. *The crowds boos very loudly* Anyway, ever since I've entered the EWA, I've beat all sorts of b*tches...Galexy, DDP, Gangrel...and especially The HeartBreak Kid Shawn Michaels. In fact, I've beaten him so many times that I make the guys on the Jerry Springer show look like beginners trying to learn how to fight. Michaels is a f*cking p*ssy. He has only beaten me once in our nine encounters in the ring. I could go on and on about the "HeartBroken Kid" Shawn Michaels...what the hell...I guess I will. Michaels, you are nothing talentwise compared to the "Corperate Outlaw". I carried his ass for three weeks to the top of the tag team ranks, then he turned on me. Well Shawn... BREAK IT DOWN!! (The crowd explodes as "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels, Whisper by his side, enters the arena. Michaels has a snickering grin on his face, and stops at the entrance ramp. He produces a microphone from the pocket of the tuxedo he is wearing, and begins to speak.) "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Now, are The Heartbreak Kid's ears decieving him, or is he hearing that nimrod KG trying to talk a big game once again? KG, you come out here week after week, and say the same stuff over, and over, and over again. KG, The Heartbreak Kid has proven in the past that, if in a neutral, unbiased environment, I can beat your ass any day of the week. "Coprorate Outlaw" KG: Then why the f*ck havn't you done it already b*tch? Think about it Michaels, I kicked your ass more times than the Bucaneers do the football. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Well, judging from the way the Bucs have played this season, that sure as hell ain't saying much, midget-boy! KG, The Heartbreak Kid could really care less about your constant whining about how you always beat me, because you have got to be the stupidest moron in the EWA. You're teaming up with a guy who's beaten your ass five times now, and you think he's loyal to you? You think Gates is going to keep giving his corporate favors to you after we kick the hell out of you guys? If so, you're a bigger moron than SmirtDogg, KG...and that is saying something. "Corporate Outlaw" KG: You know Shawn, you've got to be the boringest muthaf*cka there is in the EWA. Not only can I kick your ass, I will kick your ass if you keep talking the way that you are. You want me to say something? I got something for you. Lets do this, Shawn, since you're so eager to get your grubby little paws on me, it's only approiate that we fight at Mind Games 2 in Dallas, Texas. What do you say chump? You game? "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Hell, KG, you know that if you're going to issue the challenge, I'm sure as hell going to accept it, but...I see an open ring between the two of us, and The Heartbreak Kid sure as hell can't think of one good reason why we can't do this right now, right here. (And with that, Michaels charges the ring, and slides under the ropes. He and KG begin to brawl, and are eventually separated by security before we go to a commercial.) Philip Donovan v. Feather (w/ Vixen) *** Feather d. Philip Donovan via the Drop of a Feather. The Nightmare Squad v. "The Corporate Outlaw" KG & The Rock (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Corporate Empire) *** The Rock/KG d. Nightmare Squad (The Rock pinned Dark after the Rock Bottom) After the match, Ruger Ice ran in from the crowd and blindsided the Rock, getting away with his Intercontinental Championship in the process. The Corporation chased him to the back, and we fade to a commercial break. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck v. Sting *** No winner, due to a double No-Show. Winner Will Receive EWA World Tag Team Title Shot on January 2 The Kindred (Edge/Gangrel) v. "The Natural One" Michael Modest (EWA World Television Champion) & "Europe's Greates Export" Thorne Richards v. Big Time *** Big Time d. Kindred/Modest and Richards via the Damn Good Drop (Drexxel's pin on Gangrel) "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash v. Raven JR: Big Sexy is calling for the Jackknife!!! If he hits this, it's all she wrote...GOODNIGHT RAVEN!!! Nash hits it, and the ref makes the count. Wait a minute...he's not making the count. He's just standing there...Nash is looking wondering what in the world is going on here, and the ref is just looking at him. Nash gets up...WHAT THE HELL!?!?! The referee just hit a reverse russian leg sweep on Kevin Nash!!! He puts Raven on top of Nash and is counting! 1, 2, 3!!! Raven wins! Who in the hell is that? What's his problem with Kevin Nash? Now he's...he's taking off his hat and...a wig? It's...IT'S KANYON!!! KANYON HIT THE FLATLINER ON KEVIN NASH!!! And we thought Raven was alone here in the EWA, but obviously that is not the case. Raven picks up the win, but with a HUGE assist from Kanyon, who obviously is alligned with Raven! Kanyon: Welcome to my little world everyone. Just remember, I can get any one of you as anyone, anytime, anywhere...and Raven, it's good to be home. *** Raven d. Kevin Nash via Kanyon's interference. EWA European Championship Triple Threat Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ D-Generation X & Whisper) v. Haywood Jublome (EWA European Champion) This match was marked with a lot of doubleteaming by Michaels and Jublome on Ruger, as Haywood and Michaels seemed to be noticeably leaving each other alone during the match. *** Haywood Jublome d. Ruger Ice/Shawn Michaels via the Ho Checka on Ruger Ice. ("How to Survive in South Central" begins to blare over the PA as Jublome plays the crowd...only to have it cut off by...) ARE YOU READY??? (The crowd erupts as Erik Draven, Triple-H and Chyna emerge from behind the curtain, and slowly proceed down to ringside...Draven steps up onto the apron, over the top rope, and stares down at Jublome, a grin on his face. Michaels and Helmsley stand to either side of Draven, and the big man begins to speak.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: You know...Ruger Ice looks like, well...something that got on the wrong end of a very large truck...(Draven shudders) Anyway...Haywood? Brah, the boys and I have been talking, and well...granted, there's been a bit...hell, there's been a lot of 'issues' between us in the past...but, I've been doin' some thinking and well...you and the family (he gestures to the DX'ers around him) all have a common enemy, now. Yeah, yeah...Duane-O, I know you're just waiting to come out, oooo...(Draven pauses for a moment, waiting for Gates to emerge from the curtain...but nothing happens) Well, well...THAT is a miracle. (The camera flashes backstage, where we see a 'Corporation' locker room, a 'Club' device locking the oors shut...we hear a great deal of screaming, and obviously all of the Corporate members are stuck on the wrong end of the door.) Oops...I guess Kev did follow up on that idea...(Draven chuckles) Anyway, Haywood...you and Shawn ave already proven that you can work well as a unit, and well...you'd be an asset in our fight against Gatesy...so the offer's on the table, kid. (Draven produces a DX T-shirt from e inside of his overcoat, and holds it up for the crowd, who pop loudly.) Make your choice, Jublome. Haywood Jublome: Well, well, Well. I never thought I'd actually be thinking about this but.... I have my own agenda and problems with Gates as well. So if you can answer a simple question for me, Erik or anyone else can interject if they so desire, perhaps we have a deal. Anyways, my question is this: You guys have been known to have more than your share of problems and I realize you now have a common foe but what am I getting into after the we annhiliate the so called problem and the bosses' hired guns? "The ICON" Erik Draven: We can play it two ways, Haywood. When Gates gets his ass kicked for the final time, we can either keep on runnin' this damn show in the EWA together, or, well...there's gonna be some drama later on. But right now, brah...you have my word that for as long as you're wearing the colors, you're untouchable. Haywood Jublome: Good answer, Erik. I think we have a meeting of the minds here. ( offers his hand out to accept the shirt and when Draven throws it, Haywood looks to the crowd for their reaction and they actually, nconceivably pop for him, for the first time in recent memory, Jublome thinks for a second and looks at the Degenerates and put's on the shirt, with a smirk and then begins to speak) Erik, I am taking you on your word. We have a formitable group here and to be honest, the Corporation doesn't have a chance in hell of competing now. One more thing; Hey Gates, How you like us now? "The ICON" Erik Draven: Ladies and gentlemen...behold the ultimate in Corporation Killers...(Draven grins) And Gates? It's time to bow down to YOUR masters...and you're lookin' at 'em, right here. (he gestures to Jublome, Triple-H, Michaels, and himself) And if ya don't like it, Duane-O...we've got one word for ya... (All of DX looks rather puzzled) JA-BLOW-ME!!! (Draven grins, and all X-Chop at the camera...and exit the ring as "D-Generate Me" blares over the PA once again.) Tag Team Grudge Match Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) & "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Corporate Empire) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) The entire Empire was at ringside for this match, and Duane Gates forced the rest of DX back to the locker room, soooo...we all know what happened. No matter what Helmsley or Draven tried, the Corporation were one step ahead of them, and when Draven finally had Smirt set up for the DX Driver, the Big Bossman slipped his nightstick to Vincent Jackson, who pummeled Draven over the head with it. At that point, it was simply elementary for SmirtDogg to get the cover, and the 3-count on the ICON. The Corporation started pummeling the two DX'ers, but The Golden One ran in from the back and helped the duo fend off the assault. The show fades to black with a staredown between TGO and Draven. *** SmirtDogg/Vincent Jackson d. Draven/Helmsley via SmirtDogg's pinfall on Draven.
