EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

24 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 24, 1998 Live, from Kemper Arena, St. Louis, Missouri. Live, from Kemper Arena St. Louis, Missouri KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Shane Adams King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Red Meanie (w/ Blue Meanie) v. "The MasterMind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) James Mastin (w/ Serpent Man) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland Triangle Match Vertigo v. Robert "The Brown Bomber" Thompson & 2 Cold Scorpio (w/ NQ2) v. Two Smooth King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Lucien (w/ DAP) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ Madusa) Lee Tenn v. KG (w/ Sunny) Best Of Three Falls Jack Reed (w/ Doctor Deliant) v. Prodigy Masked Terror v. The WidowMaker King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Joe Lemon v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Hans Gruber (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. Ruger Ice Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Hannibal Manson v. Jack "The Man" McCammon Non-Title Match Mayne Mann & "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. The Eliminators (EWA World/U.S. Tag Team Champions) Non-Title Match Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) EWA U.S. Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Shane Adams The first match of the evening apparantly wasn't important enough, as neither man showed up. King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Red Meanie (w/ Blue Meanie) v. "The MasterMind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) Our opening contest saw Castor Troy demolish Red Meanie. Red Meanie looked like he had no form of a gameplan, and Troy used this to his advantage, quickly finishing him off with The FaceOff. Castor Troy d. Red Meanie with The FaceOff. Castor Troy gains entrance into The King Of The Ring. James Mastin (w/ Serpent Man) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland Copeland came out in this match on fire, dominating Mastin from the get-go. After the match, however, Lee Tenn ran to the ring with a chair, and waffled Copeland with it before escaping through the crowd. Shawn Copeland d. James Mastin with the Money Shot. Triangle Match Vertigo v. Robert "The Brown Bomber" Thompson & 2 Cold Scorpio (w/ NQ2) v. Two Smooth Before the match, it was announced that this would only be a singles match, as Two Smooth had some flight problems and couldn't make it. From there, an evenly-fought match between two young teams saw Vertigo edge out a victory. Vertigo d. Thompson/Scorpio with Jimmy Pow's pin on 2 Cold Scorpio after a small package. King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Lucien (w/ DAP) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ Madusa) Shamrock came out pumped up, and ready to fight. The lights went out, and Lucien's music played as the man walked to an EWA ring for the first time. This match was controlled by Lucien at the start, but Shamrock caught him in a belly-to-belly suplex midway through, and gained the advantage. However, as Shamrock executed a Frankensteiner, the referee was inadvertantly knocked out. Shamrock placed Lucien in the Ankle Submission, and out from the back came Shawn Michaels with a chair. Michaels waffled Shamrock in the back, and rolled Lucien onto him as the referee recovered and made the three count. Lucien d. Ken Shamrock with help from Shawn Michaels. Lucien gains entrance into The King Of The Ring. Lee Tenn v. KG (w/ Sunny) Lee Tenn started this match off in control, but the monentum shifted to KG when KG executed a vicious low-blow. From there, Shawn Copeland, Joe Satre, Mayne Mann, and Will Haynes all came to the ring. Mann distracted the referee while Copeland threw KG a pair of brass knuckles. KG nailed Tenn with the object, and then executed The Blackout. KG d. Lee Tenn with The Blackout. ----After the match---- *KG grabs a microphone from David Pinzer and begins to speak.* "Outlaw Immortal" KG: Ya' know, even tho' Smirt won that battle...he hasn't won the war...Smirt I thought we were boys but you turned out to be a sucka just like the rest of 'em. Well Smirt, not only did I find some new boys to watch my back in The Horsemen, I've grown old of playa haters like you...so I found me a new tag team partner....ladies and gentlemen...I give you The Headliner, The Showstopper, The Main Eventer, The HeartBreak Kid, Shawn Michaels! *"Sexy Boy" plays and Michaels comes out. He shakes the hand of all of the Horsemen, and then hugs KG and takes the mike.* Shawn Michaels: No more, SmirtDogg, is KG going to be pushed around. You held him back all these years, knowing that he had more talent than you! Well, now that The Heartbreak Kid and KG have formed The Showstoppers, and hooked up with The Horsemen, everyone in the EWA will fall victim to some Sweet Chin Music! Best Of Three Falls Jack Reed (w/ Doctor Deliant) v. Prodigy Prodigy is making his way to the ring, and what is this? Crazy Eddie is running up from behind with a chair, and... Smack! Prodigy goes down! From here, Eddie held an early advantage on Prodigy, and was able, quite easily, to secure the first pinfall with a crucifix into a pin. But in the second series, Prodigy flared back, dominating what was now Deliant, and getting the win via the Firestarter. The third and final fall was evenly contested, until President Gates walked to the ring, microphone in hand. Gates: You know, I've decided I don't need a bodyguard, especially SmirtDogg! Prodigy, you're too weak to even beat me! Look at you! Reed's killing you! Prodigy stared at Gates as he continued to run his mouth, when from behind, Reed caught Prodigy with a rollup, and a shocking 3 count! Gates then helped Reed to the back, laughing the entire time. Jack Reed d. Prodigy, 2 falls to 1. Masked Terror v. The WidowMaker Neither man showed. King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Joe Lemon v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Flair squeezed all the juice out of Lemon in this one, making extremely short work of him. Ric Flair d. Joe Lemon by submission with The Figure Four. Hans Gruber (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. Ruger Ice This was one of the most solid matches of the night. Gruber started off the contest with a Full-Nelson submission attempt, but amazingly, Ruger's size allowed him to counter by flipping Gruber over. The match continued, neither man able to gain a distinct advantage, until finally when Gruber jumped up at an attempted Frankensteiner, Ruger slammed him down with an inverted Spinebuster, and gained the victory. Ruger Ice d. Hans Gruber with the Spinebuster. Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins This was a very good contest, spilling out into the outside a number of times, and The Horsemen stayed out of the match. However, throughout the match bad blood spilled, and eventually, the two men were both disqualified on the outside. Vic Deakins and Will Haynes battled to a double countout. P>(Glass breaks, and "Hell Froze Over" blares over the PA system. The crowd is in shock. Not knowing that "Stone Cold" Steve Austin is back. When "Stone Cold" Steve Austin comes out, the crowd goes nuts. "Stone Cold" keeps walking down the isle. When he climbs in the ring. He goes to the nearest ring post climbs to the second rope, and "Salutes" the crowd. Crowd going wild. Steve Austin goes to the rope to the left, and once again "Salute's" the crowd. The crowd goes wild, again. Steve Austin grabs a microphone, and goes somewhat to center-ring.) "Stone Cold" Steve Austin: If you are enjoying Wednesday Night Warzone, gimmie a "Hell yea!!" (Crowd yells "Hell yea!!") Damn straight! I am back to kick some ass, and get some titles around the waist of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin. Rest damn assured that it is going to happen. The fact of the matter is there is no one that can match me in a brawl. You can use all your little sissy ass moves you want, but the last thing you remember before you are in the hospital, is the "Stone Cold" Stunner! (Crowd cheers) Damn straight! You can suck up to the President Duane Gates all you want, but it ain't working. Because "Stone Cold" don't lay down for nobody! I don't give a damn what Duane Gates has to say about it! You can bring chairs Kitchen sinks, any damn thing you want, but there ain't nobody who can stop me from getting what "Stone Cold" wants. If you want to see "Stone Cold" Steve Austin have some gold around his waist give me a "Ohh Heeellllllll yyyeaaa!" (Crowd yells "Ohh Heeelllllll yyyeaaa!") That's what a thought. I know that Duane has a rule of having to beat three people to get a title shot. Hell, I'll beat 15 if I have to! Bring it on. Sit you ass down, and watch the show because "Stone Cold" Steve Austin is here to stay, and that's a fact! (Walks over to the rope nearest to the entrance way) If anyone of you (points to the entrance way) in the back wants to fight "Stone Cold" then get you ass out here (points close to the apron, but more on the floor), so I can kick it! ( -Waits-) But You damn well know I can pull up a chair and wait forever. I have done it before! ("Stone Cold" Steve Austin goes under the ropes, down to the floor by the ring and pulls part of the Canvas up and grabs a chair from under the ring.) "Stone Cold" Steve Austin: I said I would do it, and I did. I also say this, I WILL have a title around my waist. (Throws down the chair) With or without chairs! And that's the bottom line, (The crowd says in unison with Steve Austin.) BECAUSE "Stone Cold" SAID SO! (Glass break, again. "Hell Froze Over" blares over the PA system, as "Stone Cold" Steve Austin goes back to the locker room. The fans cheering all the while.) King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Hannibal Manson v. Jack "The Man" McCammon When McCammon's music plays (sick boys by Social Distortion), he doesn't come out. After about 1 1/2 minutes, Phil walks down to the ring from the entry way. Phil- As you all are well aware, I'm no longer employed by the EWA as Mr. McCammon's watch dog. I would like you all to know that I'm not upset about this decision. It divided my loyalties between employer, and some one I would like to call friend. Furthermore, I would like to add that Jack "The Man" McCammon will NOT be coming out to wrestle tonight. (The crowd boos, as they were looking forward to a great matchup.) Phil- In his place, I would like to introduce to you someone who will hopefully make ammends to this unfortunate situation... Ladies and Gentlemen... Please welcome now... (Lust for Life by Iggy Pop starts playing.) Jim Ross: Hey, that's Van Smack's music!! Phil- Johnny 2 Bad! (Some of the audience that knows Johnny erupts, but a lot of the crowd continues to watch, not knowing of this man.) Johnny 2 Bad comes strutting down to the ring, slides through the ropes, and stalks up to Phil. He snatches the microphone out of Phil's hands with a sneer, and pushes him out of the way. Johnny 2 Bad- Well, well, well... So this is the infamous EWA. Yeah, whatever. I'm here for a reason, and I'm going to make it quick. First off, it's come to my knowledge that Jack McCammon doesn't want to wrestle here anymore. Yeah, I know you're all as shocked as I am. Jack has never been known for a lack of enthusiasm, so this is quite out of character for him. Let's see what we can do about that, shall we? Jack! Jack-o! Come out, come out, wherever you are! McCammon, get your skinny butt up here so I can finish up what we started back at the FFW! At this, Jack McCammon comes storming down the entryway, not even waiting for his music to be cued up again. When he gets to the ring he stands face to face with Johnny. The two imposing figures lock eyes in a dangerous stare down. Finally Johnny points out to the crowd, who are cheering, anticipating something big. This breaks Jack's concentration and he looks away, thus giving Johnny the chance to speak into the microphone again. Johnny- Do you remember that, Jack-o? Do you remember what you did for me? I was going to retire. ME of all people! The greatest man to ever walk, strut, swagger, or pace the face of this big round planet! And what stopped me, Jack-o? Huh? You did! You reminded me of why I was here, just like I'm going to do now. We can't do it the same way, since you're already booked for a match tonight. You see, folks, this guy reminded me of who I was by calling me out. Can you believe that? Now, of course, I beat him, but in the process I felt that spark again. It revitalized my career. For a few days, anyway, and then the damn FFW shut down without a word of warning... So here's what we're going to do tonight, Jack-o. Do you think you're man enough to take me on again? Do ya? (All through this little speech, Jack's facial expressions have ranged from embarassment to outrage. After a few moments of agonizing thoughts, He finally answers.) Jack- You're damn right I can take you on! (The crowd gives a huge pop.) Johnny- Like I said, chummer, you can't tonight. But why don't you do me a little favor? Why don't you show me that you're ready by breaking this death freak in half? Come on, show me you still got it, you loser! (With that, Johnny gives Jack a final look, and exits the ring. Jack looks to Phil for answers, but his "personal assistant" simply shrugs his shoulders, and follows Johnny.) (The crowd begins to chant, "JACK! JACK! JACK!") Jack calls for the ref, and tells him the match is on. When the match finally does start, Jack abandons almost all of his technical moves and goes straight for savage power, wrestling off of pure emotion. This caught Hannibal off guard at first, as he was definitely not expecting tonight's happenings. However, McCammon seemed a little rusty from his absence, and missed a big legdrop. As a result, the momentum was swerved, and Manson was able to nail Silence. Afterward, Johnny came back out to the ring, helped Jack up, and supported him as they walked back to the entryway, where Phil was waiting, with an anguished look on his face. Hannibal Manson d. Jack McCammon with Silence. Hannibal Manson gains entrance into The King Of The Ring. Non-Title Match Mayne Mann & "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. The Eliminators (EWA World/U.S. Tag Team Champions) In one of the most brutal tag team matches in Wednesday Night Warzone history, this match was actually thrown out almost from the onset, as all types of foreign objects were introduced. Chairs, title belts, garbage cans...you name it, it was used in the fight that last almost ten minutes after the bell had sounded. The two teams battled to a double disqualification. Non-Title Match Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) The Gunslinger once again didn't show, prompting President Gates to announce that if he didn't show up for his title defense on Saturday Night Slam, he would be fired. As Makaveli stood in the ring, calling out anyone who would fight him, Rufus Lamar Jackson, J.J., Arnold Drummond, & Fat Albert once again came from the crowd. Rufus smacked Makaveli three times with a steel chair, and then Makaveli's bodyguards ran out, only to have Rufus's gang exit through the crowd. Makaveli d. The Gunslinger by forfeit. EWA U.S. Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Hicks started off the match with the advantage, whipping him into the turnbuckle and executing a powerful snap suplex onto SmirtDogg. Hicks immediately went for the cover, but got only a two count. Hicks picked SmirtDogg back up, and set him up for a big powerslam, but Smirt countered and shoved Hicks into the turnbuckle, nailing a German suplex as Hicks bounced back. Again, Smirt only got a two count. The match continued like this for nearly twenty minutes, with a series of near-falls, until finally, Hicks nailed a moonsault off the top rope. Hicks pulled the tights of Smirt, and was able to get a three-count, securing the U.S. Title win. Justin Hicks d. SmirtDogg with a moonsault. Justin Hicks is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!!
