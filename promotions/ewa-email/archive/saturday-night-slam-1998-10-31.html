EWA Saturday Night Slam

31 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 31, 1998 Live from the Target Center Minneapolis, Minnesota (The camera fades into the sold-out Target Center in Minneapolis after the opening to Saturday Night Slam. The camera pans around the audience, so that we can see all of the fans. The camera then pans over to the broadcast table.) Jim Ross: Hello everyone and welcome to the number one wrestling show in the world, EWA Saturday Night Slam! I'm Jim Ross alongside my broadcast colleague Vincent Ashe, and we've got an exciting broadcast tonight, including the main event, where The Franchise, the EWA Intercontinental Champion, Jeff Bunda, will team up with Stevie Richards to take on the #1 Contender, the man who will meet Erik Draven for the richest prize in this industry in just seven days, Venom, and a mystery partner. Vincent Ashe: And nobody seems to know who Venom's tag team partner is! Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The Hate Club) v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) This was a fairly decent match, marred by interference. Haynes had accidentally knocked out the referee, and then made a crucial mistake by going to the top rope. Hart caught him, and dropped him down by his legs, locking on the Sharpshooter. But while Hart had it locked on, Venom ran from the back, and nailed Hart with a chair, right in the head. Haynes was able to crawl over as the referee came to, and crawled on top of Hart for the three count. Will 'The Thrill' Haynes d. Bret Hart with interference from Venom. "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) Modest started off this match with an armbar submission, which was nicely countered by Hicks into an armbar of his own. This match was a great technical wrestling match for the most part, with both men also making a couple of top-rope moves. However, when Justin Hicks attempted to go to the top rope, all of a sudden Crazy Canuck ran out from the back with a chair, and blasted Hicks in the back, causing him to topple over. The referee called for the bell, and before Bunda, Richards, and Copeland could reach Canuck, he escaped through the crowd. Justin Hicks d. Michael Modest by disqualification. "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Triple Threat) Another outstanding match here, with both men using all sorts of high-flying moves. This match quickly turned into a "can you top this" type of match, as both men attempted various suicidal manuevers. Among them was Rob Van Dam's corkscrew moonsault, which nearly got him the victory, and Copeland's 450 degree splash. The match was progressing along, with both men down in the ring, when once again Crazy Canuck ran to the ring. Canuck pulled Copeland to the outside of the ring, and the fight was on, as the two battled all the way to the back. The referee counted to ten, and Rob Van Dam was awarded a countout victory. Rob Van Dam d. Shawn Copeland by countout. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) Galaxy started off the match in determined fashion, going right at Jackson and trying to take the big man off his feet. Unfortunately for him, he failed, and failed badly, as on his third clothesline attempt, Jackson planted him with a big boot. From there, it was a chokeslam, followed by the Fallen Angel, and a second EWA victory for the gigantic Vincent Jackson. Vincent Jackson d. Galaxy with the Fallen Angel. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. Thorne Richards KG started off this bout with the momentum, much to the chagrin of the audience. KG used a couple of impressive suplexes on Thorne to neutralize the man, and then took to Thorne's own game, applying a Boston Crab in the center of the ring. But amazingly, Thorne was able to fight and fight, and finally reach the ropes. From here, KG took a little too much time showing off for the fans, and when he went to the top rope, Thorne was able to recover and crotch KG on the turnbuckle. From there, Thorne wearily climbed to the top rope, and executed a beautiful belly-to-belly suplex off the top rope. Thorne went for the cover, but only got a two count. From there, Thorne used a few submission holds to wear KG down, but just as Thorne was about to clinch the match, KG nailed him with a low blow. KG then placed Thorne on the top rope, and was about to go for the Blackout, when Shawn Michaels sprinted to the ring. Michaels started yelling and arguing with KG, which gave Thorne the opportunity to jump down and nail KG with a Dragon Sleeper, which he quickly reversed into a version of the Diamond Cutter. This new move, called The Overdose, was enough for Thorne to get the three count and the victory. Thorne Richards d. KG with The Overdose. El Locco Jobbo (w/ Intoxication X) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg ("In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" by Greig plays over the sounds system and th ecrowd immediately starts to boo as The Prince makes his way through the curtain without the flower girls. Once again, he's dressed to kill in a dark purple suit that was made especially for him. He walks down the asile, Light Heavyweight Title dragging by his side, paying no attention to the people who he passes, each one of them cursing his existance. He walks down to ringside and approaches the announcer's tables. Walking by the main table, he goes to the Mexican table. Picking up Jorge Sanchez, the main Mexican Commentator, he throws him into the guard rail and takes his chair. He approaches the main table and sits down, putting on the spare headset) Jim Ross : For a prince you really lack class The Prince : I lack class? You carbons knew I was coming out here to commentate and you didn't have the decency to give me the chair I deserve? I have to lower myself to a commoner's folding chair that was sat on by a dirty immigrant? I should buy this company just so I could have the pleasure of firing you, then rehiring you at a quarter of your wages. Jim Ross : You make me sick. The Prince : No, what makes you sick is the tenth trip to the buffet table. Now why don't you shut up so I can grace the rest of these people with my presence. (The Prince gets up on the commentator's table and stands right in front of Jim Ross. He addresses the crowd) The Prince : Ladies and gentlemen, for lack of a better term, I have decided to grace you with my presence so I could once again reiterate my stranglehold over this division. See, I'm not out here to scout SmirtDogg, my opponent at Novermber 2 Remember. No, that is what the normal wrestler with no resources would do. No, I have thousands of people doing my dirty work for me, gathering little bits and pieces of information on your beloved SmirtDogg (The crowd boos and The Prince chuckles). No, I'm here to let SmirtDogg know that no matter how hard he tries, no matter how much he begs and pleads, there is a pecking order in this federation, much as their is one in life. It's very strict and allows for no leeway. I ALWAYS stand at the top and the contenders never even come close. No matter how hard they try, they can never reach my level, and right here tonight I'll remind Smirt and El Loco Jobbo of that, and at November 2 Remember, I'll exert my power on the lowly pleb you worshiped, the soon to be former wrestler known as SmirtDogg. (The Prince pauses) Now bring out the carbon immigrant and the pretender to the throne. (The Prince steps down from the table and sits back down as we prepare for the wrestlers) From here, both men came to the ring and put on a decent match. The match featured a lot of high-flying moves between the two, with a little bit of brawling as well. Eventually the match got a little out of hand, and both men ended up being counted out while brawling on the outside. All of a sudden, the lights went out in the arena...when they came back on, SmirtDogg was gone. El Locco Jobbo and SmirtDogg battled to a double countout. Triple Threat Match Deader v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (w/ The Hate Club) Before the match, as Crazy Canuck was making his way to the ring, Scott Hall and Kevin Nash ran out from behind and began attacking him. The Pessimist ran out to help, and the four men quickly began brawling all the way to the back. Deader didn't show up, so Shawn Michaels got a forfeit win. However, as Michaels had his hand raised, the lights went out, and the purple haze once again descended over the arena as Vincent Jackson came to the ring. Michaels would not back down from Jackson, but it didn't matter much, as Jackson quickly dismantled Michaels with a chokeslam and the Fallen Angel. Shawn Michaels d. Deader & Crazy Canuck by forfeit. Gangrel (w/ Devon) v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) Ruger had a definitive size advantage over Gangrel, and it showed. Gangrel kept trying to get an offense going, but Ruger kept using his power to keep the advantage, and eventually clotheslined Gangrel over the top rope. At this point, it didn't take long for Edge to appear from the crowd. Edge jumped over the guardrail with lightning quick speed, gave Gangrel the Downward Spiral, and left just as suddenly, leaving Gangrel to be counted out. Ruger Ice d. Gangrel by countout. Special Tag Team Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards & "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom & a mystery partner JR: Now it's time for the tag match that the EWA has been waiting for. This match has been surrounded in controversy from the very start. VA: That's right. For some reason Venom doesn't have the courage to tell the world who his partner is. I don't think he has a partner. JR: Even Venom wouldn't be crazy enough to come into a match like this without a partner. O.K. fans, the Fink is in the ring. This is going to be a slobberknocker. HF: Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for our main event. This is a special "mystery partner" tag team match. (Perfect Strangers by Deep Purple plays over the PA. The crowd pops huge as Jeff Bunda and Stevie Richards make their way to the ring. They each go to opposite corners of the ring and give the crowd the Triple Threat sign to another huge pop.) JR: There they are. Two of the biggest superstars in the sport today. These two men have done it all in this sport. VA: I have a feeling tonight will just be another victory for the Triple Threat. I don't think Venom has enough to stop them. (Instruments of Destruction starts to play as Venom makes his way to the ring. The crowd is at near riot stage as they are trying to get at the Extreme Superstar) HF: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is one fall with a one hour time limit. Introducing first, from Philadelphia Pennsylvania weighing in tonight at 226 pounds, the EWA North American Champion, Dancin' Stevie Richards!!! JR: Listen to this crowd. They are going insane for Richards. HF: And his partner from Charlotte North Carolina weighing in tonight at 246 pounds, the EWA Intercontinental Champion, The Franchise Jeff Bunda!!! VA: The response for Bunda is absolutely amazing. I can’t believe this. JR: Well I have a feeling the mood of the crowd is about to change. HF: And their opponent, he hails from Philadelphia Pennsylvania. He weighs in tonight at 245 pounds. He is the former EWA Television Cham... (Venom grabs the microphone from Finkel. Finkel high tails it out of the ring.) Venom: Former? Former? God damnit!! You stupid piece of sh*t!! Are you trying to piss me off more than I already am? It’s a damn good thing you got your ugly bald headed ass out of my ring. Now listen up you pukes. Everybody knows that I am the Extreme Superstar of the EWA. When it came time to find a partner for this match I knew that I had to find one extreme motherf*cker to team with me. I couldn’t just go out and find some fairy like the Dancin Queen over there. So idiots, I give you my partner. Hit the f*cking music. (An orange hue comes over the arena. Then over the PA blasts War Machine by KISS. The crowd can’t believe it as the man makes his way through the curtain.) JR: Good God almighty!! It can’t be. VA: I believe it is. JR: Business is about to pick up!!! It’s Taz!! Venom went out and found Taz!! (The match started out with Jeff Bunda and Venom in the ring. Bunda dominated Venom much to the delight of the crowd. Venom was then able to tag out to Taz. From there Taz and Bunda put on a clinic of scientific wrestling. The tide turned when Taz whipped Bunda into the ropes and Venom kneed Bunda in the back. Venom and Taz then proceeded to work Bunda over. We pick up the match 16 minutes in.) JR: My GOD!!! Bunda has got to make a tag. Richards hasn’t had a chance to get in the ring yet. VA: But after looking at how well Taz is wrestling, Richards may be lucky that he’s not the one in here taking this beating. JR: Venom whips Bunda into the ropes...Bunda ducks a clothesline...he comes back with one of his own!!! Listen to this crowd!!! VA: Bunda has a to find a way to reach down and make the tag to Richards!!! JR: The referee begins his count...both men slowly make their way to their feet. Venom has the tag to Taz!!! Bunda lunges...he makes the tag!!! Richards has finally gotten into this match!!!! VA: This crowd is going to tear the roof off this place!!!! JR: Richards is in...this is going to be a war now. Wait a second...Richards just helped Bunda up. Don’t turn your back on Taz like that. OH MY GOD IN HEAVEN!!! Richards just gave Bunda the Stevie Kick!!!! VA: I can’t believe what we have just seen!! Why? JR: How the hell am I supposed to know why? Venom, Taz, and Richards are just pummeling Bunda. What’s this? Is that...it can’t be!!! Bunda was... What in the hell? (Mysterio comes out from the curtain. He’s holding a bat in his hand. Justin Hicks and Shawn Copeland try to get to the ring to save Bunda but Mysterio is holding them at bay.) JR: Venom has Bunda...EXTREME POISON!!! I can’t believe it. It’s all been a set up. But why? VA: This is getting ugly. Taz has a chair!!! JR: MY GOD IN HEAVEN!!! Taz just nailed Bunda with the chair. Bunda is a bloody mess!!! My mama is watching. This is terrible. Somebody has to stop this. (Venom leaves the ring and gets a microphone) Venom: HAHAHAHAHA!!! This is f*cking great!! Look at him!! He’s bleeding more than my first girlfriend Mary Jane Jones. (Venom starts stomping Bunda some more. Suddenly Stevie Richards stops him.) What are you stopping me for? I KNEW you didn’t have it in you. You’re soft. I knew it you ham and egging piece of sh*t. Stevie: Soft? What in the hell are you talking about? Why should you be the only one to stomp him? (Richards then proceeds to stomp Bunda with a vengeance. Venom, shocked that Stevie has it in him is just laughing his heart out.) Venom: Yeah yeah!! Kick him!! Kick him like a dog!!! F*ck him up!!! This is great!!!! Now THIS is what I call a good old fashioned Punking!!!!! I had no idea that you had it in you Richards, you skinny bastard. I’m impressed. And Taz, this is great!!!! Look at him!!! He’s a mess!!!! He looks worse than what I did to Erik "I caught have genital warts thanks to Beulah" Draven!!!!!!! (Taz then grabs Bunda and throws him from the ring. He then climbs out of the ring and throws Bunda into the ring steps. Bunda’s skull makes a horrific thud on the cold steel. Then, in one final act of brutality, Taz takes Bunda back on the apron and powerbombs him off the ring apron through the Spanish announcers table. The announcers scurry out of the way and Bunda is left lying in a broken bloody heap.) Venom: Now THIS is what I call a good f*cking time!!!!! I had no idea that you had it in you Richards. I’m impressed. And Taz, oh Taz, this is great!!!! Look at him!!! He’s a mess!!!! Taz: Well well well...the Franchise? I guess you can just call me the Franchise Killer then. I’ve been sitting on the sidelines for entirely too long watching this fool run off at the mouth about how great he is. Well Bunda, take a look at these three men in the ring right now. THIS is the future of the EWA. And there isn’t a damn thing you or anybody else can do about it. And if you think you can change it, think again. We will do what we want when we want. I will do what I want, when I want. Why? Because I’m TAZ!!! I am the Human Suplex Machine. BEAT ME IF YOU CAN...SURVIVE, IF I LET YOU!!! Stevie: Bunda, Bunda, Bunda, you are such a stupid man. Did you not listen to what I just said at the Clash? Fooling people for the fun of it? Did you not hear what I had to say about marks? Well Bunda, I just swerved the biggest mark of them all!!!! Bunda, I have never seen a bigger mark in this sport. You are such a mark for your character that it clouds your judgement. I cracked you with a chair and you just forget about that? You think that I was your friend? You idiot!!! I was never your friend. You were nothing more than a pawn in my game of human chess. You see, I took you and moved you around at will. I did whatever I wanted to with you. Why? Because I could. You would never believe that Stevie Richards could be manipulating the Franchise. You are such a mark for yourself that you believe you are untouchable. Well look at the blood pouring out of your head right now. You tell me how untouchable you are. This isn’t over. In fact, it’s just begun. There is a revolution going on. And no, it’s not like that painted idiot in some other company. This is a real revolution. And with any revolution, there will be bloodshed, there will be violence, and there will be chaos. The fun has just begun. Now one last little thing. Justin!!! Yeah you Hicks!!! You remember when you and Claridge broke up? You remember how she left Charlotte and went to Atlanta for that week? Well she wasn’t visiting friends like she told you. She was down there taking care of some business. Venom: Down where? Stevie: You know where. Let’s just say there was a little Richards in her hot little oven that had to be taken care of. You do the math Hicks. Men, let’s get out of here. (Venom, Taz, and Richards all leave the ring and make their way back to the dressing room. The arena is sitting in stunned silence. Copeland and Hicks finally have made it to Bunda and are tending to him. Tears are streaming down Hicks’ face due to the news Richards just gave him.) JR: Ladies and gentlemen, I really don’t have anything to say. This may have been the most historic, shocking, and brutal night in the history of the EWA. For Vincent Ashe, I’m Jim Ross saying goodnight.
