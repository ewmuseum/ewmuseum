EWA Saturday Night Slam

2 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 2, 1998 Live, from the Tacoma Dome, Tacoma, Washington. Semi Final U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match The Eliminators v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) Semi Final U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus) The Ghetto Warriors (w/ Sunny) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) The Widowmaker v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Jack "The Man" McCammon v. Castor Troy The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Outsiders EWA World Television Championship Match "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ the IV Horsemen) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) "Double A" Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. The Wyldsyde "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Thorne Richards "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Erik Draven v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Special Non-Title Grudge Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ the IV Horsemen) Semi Final U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match The Eliminators v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) WormBoy's troops no-showed for this contest, and The Eliminators made their way into the finals. The Eliminators d. Prophecy by forfeit. New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) Another no-show by FlashBack = a win for New Demolition. Semi Final U.S. Tag Team Tournament Match Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus) Both teams looked -very- sluggish, and finally, Dos Equis pulled off the ugly win. Dos Equis d. Sexual Thangs with Tecate Jake's pin on J Man after the Kamikaze. (6:22) The Ghetto Warriors (w/ Sunny) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) Due to SmirtDogg's departure, this was a handicap match. KG isolated Destruction in his corner, and wnet to work on him, not stopping until he hit the Blackout. KG d. Death Sentance by pinning Destruction after the Blackout. (7:11) The Widowmaker v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia The Widowmaker was crushed quickly by The Rock. The Rock d. The Widowmaker with the Rock Bottom. (2:20) Jack "The Man" McCammon v. Castor Troy McCammon looked spectacular in his EWA debut, really tearing apart Castor. Jack McCammon d. Castor Troy with The BurnOut. (4:37) The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Outsiders No contest; the EDM -destroyed- Hall & Nash. The Extreme Dream Machine d. The Outsiders with Aaron Martin's pin on Scott Hall after the Triple Threat. (8:19) EWA World Television Championship Match "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ the IV Horsemen) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) The Gunslinger started off with the advantage, and sent Page reeling with a mammoth powerbomb. Page fought back, however, and when The Gunslinger went for another powerbomb, Page flipped out, and executed the Diamond Cutter. DDP d. The Gunslinger with the Diamond Cutter. (10:30) "Diamond" Dallas Page is the new EWA World Television Champion!! "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) This was a short, competitive match; soon into it, DDP ran to the ring, and executed the Diamond Cutter on Hicks. Page and Piper then ran through the crowd as the rest of the Triple Threat ran to the ring. Justin Hicks d. Roddy Piper by disqualification. "Double A" Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. The Wyldsyde Double A didn't show up, and Gates held true to his word, firing him. The Wyldsyde obtained the forfeit win. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Thorne Richards Thorne looked downright awful in this match, as Michaels really destroyed him. Shawn Michaels d. Thorne Richards with the Sweet Chin Music. (7:44) "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Van Smack didn't show up, strangely enough. Erik Draven v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Draven showed, Helmsley didn't. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) A very competitive match, however, The Golden One seemed to have a little extra fuel in his fire tonight. The Golden One d. Deathstar with the Golden Edge. (11:32) La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) La Parka also did now show. When announcing this, President Gates seemed annoyed at the no-shows. Special Non-Title Grudge Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ the IV Horsemen) Jim Ross: Ric Flair is in the ring, and now we're awaiting the arrival of Kane. The match that could end someone's career tonight as the title isn't even on the line. The World Champion is doing his normal styling and profiling at the moment, and...who is that? Larry Zybysko: Someone is making their way down to the ringside area through the seats!! Jim Ross: Flair hasn't even seen him yet...it's...IT'S AUSTIN!!! STONE COLD STEVE AUSTIN!!! Where the hell has he been? We haven't seen him since Kane beat him last month! He lost his EWA career in that match! Austin is in the ring behind Flair! Flair turns around...STUNNER!!! That Stone Cold Stunner!!! Flair is out cold and Austin has the mic! Austin: That's what you get you stupid piece of trash when you don't watch yer damn back! Now, I'm sick and tired of listening about Kane, Erik Draven and especially this old man right below me. Hell, I done beat his ass all ready and he's the champion!?!? I done had Kane's ass beat, so don't act like you done something special there Flair. If it hadn't been for our beloved President up there, Stone Cold would've had that damn peice of tin a long time ago, and would still be whoopin everyone's ass right now! So what I'm telling ya Gates is this...I know you screwed Steve Austin and wanted him out of the EWA. I'm here to tell you that no one gets rid of Stone Cold, and you have only one of two choices. Either you reinstate Steve Austin, or I'm gonna beat yer ass up and down every one of these aisles! And until you do, I will whip everyone's ass anytime I feel like it! Stone Cold is back, I just beat the hell outta yer so called champion again, there ain't nothing nobody can do to stop me, and that's the bottom line, cause Stone Cold said so!!! *Glass breaks and "Hell Froze Over" blares over the speakers. Austin gives the finger to Flair who's still on his back out cold and then stalks off through the crowd as the camera fades to black.*
