EWA Saturday Night Slam

9 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 9, 1998 Live, from the L.A. Sports Arena, Los Angeles, California. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) The Golden One v. Thorne Richards Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) The Widow Maker v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Non-Title Match Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. The Eliminators (EWA United States Tag Team Champions) Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. New Demolition Six Man Tag Team Match Dos Equis & La Parka (The Circus!) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven, "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley, & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (Degeneration X) The Wyldsyde v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) EWA World Television Championship Return Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (EWA T.V. Champion, w/ Kimberly) "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. KooKabura EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebeccal Claridge) Castor Troy v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) Non Title Grudge Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. Prodigy (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This match was canceled. The Golden One v. Thorne Richards The Golden One looked marvelous tonight, as he completely dismantled Thorne Richards, who has been in somewhat of a slump lately. The Golden One d. Thorne Richards with the Golden Edge. (6:14) Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) Neither man showed up. The Widow Maker v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) The Widow Maker recieved the forfeit win. Non-Title Match Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. The Eliminators (EWA United States Tag Team Champions) Sexual Thangs looked awful on the brink of their week long suspension, and The Eliminators got an easy win. The Eliminators d. Sexual Thangs with Saturn's pin on Ricky T after the Total Elimination. (7:32) Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. New Demolition Another squash, as New Demolition really destroyed Prophecy. New Demolition d. Prophecy with Flame's pin on Travis Scott with an inside cradle. (4:10) Six Man Tag Team Match Dos Equis & La Parka (The Circus!) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven, "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley, & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (Degeneration X) Was there something in the air tonight? The Circus! was dismantled completely by Degeneration X. Wasn't even a contest. DX d. The Circus! with Erik Draven's pin on LaParka after the DX Driver. (8:23) The Wyldsyde v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) Finally, a solid match! With the addition of "The Man's Rules" to the match, we saw a No Holds Barred Falls Count Anywhere match which saw interference play a key factor. Wyldsyde, trhoughout the match, stuck to his wrestling principles, refusing to even use a closed fist. McCammon, on the other hand, used everything but the kitchen sink. However, The Wyldsyde still held on, and was preparing to execute The Wyldryde, when Thorne Richards ran out from the back, and waffled Wyldsyde with a chair. McCammon then executed the Double Hammer, and The Wyldsyde obtained his first EWA defeat. Jack "The Man" McCammon d. The Wyldsyde with the Double Hammer after outside interference from Thorne Richards. (16:09) EWA World Television Championship Return Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (EWA T.V. Champion, w/ Kimberly) DDP was way off his game tonight, and unfortunately for him, KG was on. Justin Hicks watched from the entrance ramp, and seemed to contemplate interference, but changed his mind when he saw KG pummeling Page. KG finished the match with his variation of the Air Smirty, in a tribute to his friend SmirtDogg, and once again strapped EWA gold around his waist. KG d. DDP with a variation of the Air Smirty. (9:11) KG is the new EWA World Television Champion!! "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. KooKabura It was announced that The Rock had left the EWA; Koo got a forfeit win. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebeccal Claridge) Cummings put in one of his best performances since he won the Intercontinental Title last month, and looked on the verge of possibly winning the match. But he missed the Wipeout, and Hicks quickly took control by executing the Saturday Night Special. Cummings put forth a valiant effort, but had no choice but to tap out. Justin Hicks d. Scott Cummings by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks retains the EWA United States Heavyweight Championship. Castor Troy v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) Troy did about as well against Richards as the midget did, tonight. The most exciting part of the match was when it was announced that the Swing Pad would now appear on every SNS show. Stevie Richards d. Castor Troy with the Stevie Kick. (2:21) *The fans once again give a mild cheer as EWA President Duane Gates makes his way to the ring. Gates is, this time, surrounded by uniformed security, and has what appears to be two contracts in his hand.* Gates: I have two very important issues to settle tonight. First of all, Mr. Goodman, when you shoved me on Wednesday Night Warzone, you crossed the line. Now, I've only been in the ring twice in my presidental career, but I feel as though I must make it a thrid time. So Harrison Goodman, this is a contract, authorized and signed by myself, the President of the EWA, for you and I to meet at Mind Games in a "Tuxedo Match". I'll leave this backstage; sign it, if you've got the courage. *The fans explode, and then realize that one of the two men will be exposed, and then retch in their seats.* And this second contract is the biggie. Right now, and I know you're in the building, because I saw you backstage. Right now, I want to see in the ring before me, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin! *Glass breaks and "Hell Froze Over" blares over the speakers. Steve Austin comes stalking out of the entrance way, and heads right for the ring. The security team surrounds Gates, but Austin pays them no mind as he climbs to the turnbuckles and "salutes" his fans. He gives Gates the finger on the way by and does the same on the other side before standing eye to eye with President Gates.* Gates: Mr. Austin, you've been out of line here for the past two wee... Austin: You might as well just shut yer damn mouth right now son, because Stone Cold Steve Austin doesn't want to hear a damn thing you've got to say. You come out here, and screw Stone Cold out of the EWA, and now you think you can make demands? You didn't want me as the champion, so you pulled the plug. You knew that old fat ass Flair couldn't beat Kane, so you screwed Kane as well. But you don't get a second chance to do that to Stone Cold Steve Austin! So, you have two choices here tonight Gates...either you reinstate Stone Cold Steve Austin, or I will beat yer ass up and down everyone of these aisles, and every little one of yer damn security guards can't do nothing about it! *Gates obviously looks nervous* Gates: Mr. Austin, nobody screwed you out of the EWA; you lost the match. But I am here to offer you one chance at regaining your EWA career. At the Survivor Series, you were originally supposed to be participating in an Intercontinental Championship Match with "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels. Now, Mr. Austin, I'm going to sign a match between you and Shawn Michaels for Wednesday Night Warzone. If you can defeat Michaels, your EWA career will be reinstated. If, for some reason, you lose to Shawn Michaels, you will be barred from EWA Events FOREVER! Right here is the contract for the match; *Gates produces a pen from his jacket pocket* all you have to do is sign. Austin: First of all, let me show your blind ass something son, because you obviouslly need glasses. *The Titantron flickers, and it shows the last match Austin had v. Kane. The 3 count is about to be made, and Austin's shoulders come up, but the ref counts the three anyways.* Now, did that look like a three count to you jackass?!?! Gates: Well, the official obviously made a mis... Austin: If you think Gates is full of you know what and deserves to get his ass whipped, gimme a hell ya! Crowd: HELL YEAH!!! Austin: That's what I thought. And now you want Stone Cold to face Shawn Michaels to get back something that never should've been taken away?!?! I oughta knock yer damn lights out son. And you put Steve Austin against all of DX, and you damn well know it! But, since Stone Cold is a forgiving man *he grins as the crowd roars* I'm gonna leave this up to someone else. If you think Stone Cold should sign this paper and beat the living hell out of Shawn Michaels next Wednesday, gimme a hell ya! Crowd: HELL YEAH!!! *Austin signs the paper, and the contract is good* Austin: But since I'm not a part of the little EWA again just yet, that means that yer ass has no right to tell Stone Cold what he can and can't do...and that ain't gonna change either when that little piece of paper is valid. And since you can't tell Stone Cold a damn thing right now... JR: STUNNER!!! Austin just nailed the Stunner on the Prez! And security tackled Austin and are putting him in handcuffs!!! They are arresting Stone Cold Steve Austin, and he doesn't even care!! Non Title Grudge Match Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. Prodigy (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Their first matchup was colossal; their second, although good, did not live up to the expectations. Both Kane and the World Champion looked a little sluggish, but Prodigy seemed distratced throughout the matchup by something. Prodigy made an attempt at a comeback late in the match, but Kane was simply too much, and this time, defeated Prodigy in the center of the ring. Kane d. Prodigy with the Tombstone Piledriver. (13:07) Non-Title Match: Prodigy retains the belt.
