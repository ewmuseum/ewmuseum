EWA Clash Of The Champions X Saturday, January 30, 1999 Live, from The MGM Grand Hotel & Casino Las Vegas, Nevada The lights go down as Clash of the Champions X begins, live from the MGM Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada. Suddenly, that familiar golden beam shines across the arena, and from the curtain, The Golden One emerges, with the EWA United States Heavyweight Title draped over his shoulder. This unexpected appearance is rewarded with a HUGE pop from the crowd, as they know that "The Shining Star" is going to put on a great show for them night in and night out... The Golden One: All I have to say is that it's GREAT to be in Las Vegas!! *crowd roars* Now, getting down to business. Two weeks ago, the world witnessed something very wrong on Pay Per View. They saw "God's Gift to the Last Call" win the EWA World Heavyweight Title in a Ladder Match at Mind Games 2. Now while the world knows that there's clearly a little more than meets the eye where his victory is concerned, I'm not out here to talk about that. Because as much as the crybaby would love to have me break down, I told the world he couldn't make me stop, not even with Gates' help. You see Smirty, you've done your job as far as I go. You've seen to it that I've been pushed back to Mid-Card Heaven, which is absolutely fine with me. You've stuck me with the U.S. Title, which is one belt no one is gonna see for a good, long while. You've got me in matches versus the likes of Ruger Ice, a notorious mid-carder. Again no problem. You see Smirt, while you've got the power of Gates' ass in your hands, I've got a much stronger force in my hip pocket, but more on that later. What I'm out here to do right now is to tell the world that things are about to change in the EWA. I don't care how powerful Duane Gates, the almighty President, is. I don't care how much he loves bending over for "God's Gift to Beer" either. Because as of right now, January the 30th, 1999, Duane Gates and I are going to be inseparable. No, I'm not joining his little Corporate Empire, because frankly, well, THEY SUCK!! But I'm going to follow that crazed little maniac wherever he goes, and if I've got somewhere to go, I'll drag his sorry ass with me. In essence Gates, no more behind the scenes hanky-panky because you've got "The Shining Star" watching your ass with a magnifying glass the size of Nevada. Now that I've got that out of the way, I suggest everyone watching at home stay tuned. This is just the beginning of a night that I promise will go my way. Because you can push and push as hard as you can to knock "The Master of Manipulation" down, but when I say it's impossible, I damn sure mean it. Welcome to the beginning of a GOLDEN NIGHT!!!
