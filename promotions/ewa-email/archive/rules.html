EWA Rules

EWA Rules To recieve EWA emails, you must submit your email address on the front page, under 'Join EWA Mailing List'. You will wrestle at least once a week, barring injuries. You must post at least one 'flash' (interview) per week. Failure to do so will result in termination (After one warning). Remember, this federation is based on roleplaying. Let's say in e-wrestling, you're up against someone whose e-wrestler uses a picture of The Undertaker to base their character off of, and I, oh... Say something stupid like... "You're not ____. You're really The Undertaker trying to act all dark and Gates makes you who you are." Now that's a blatant kayfabe break. A first offense will result in the roleplay not counting, and the second will result in termination, no questions asked. Now, another way to break kayfabe is to go against what your character is billed as. Now let's take [name withheld]'s character for example, Philip Donovan. He's a face, with slight attitude. Sort of like a Buff Bagwell-esque guy. Now, if [name withheld] were to make a post where he's pissed, punches some old lady, kicks her in the stomach, and gives her the Stone Cold Stunner... Then the next day, be nice, handing roses, helping old ladies walk across the street...That's -WAY- deviant of what he's -supposed- to do. Unless you make it a permanent heel change, you can't go outside the parameters of your character and just "flip-flop" (unless your character is BASED on "flip-flops" like Mick Foley did when he had 3 personalities). A third way to break kayfabe is, if you are facing a real wrestler, such as Shawn Michaels, would be to say, "Hey, remember at Wrestlemania XIV when Stone Cold beat your ass?" That's a COMPLETE break of kayfabe. Any reference to a character that relates to what they've done in real wrestling is a break. All of these are kayfabe breaks, and are subject to the above punishments. If you don't roleplay for a match, you will get jobbed. Period. You may only post three (3) roleplays for each event. You can post them whenever you want, so long as it is before the roleplaying deadline. If you post more than the allotted amount of roleplays, the roleplays you post that exceed the amount will not count. You may not go '2 up' on your opponent during your roleplaying (2-0, 3-1). If this occurs, the offending roleplay(s) will be disregarded in the 'final count.' Long roleplays which ramble on and on will be frowned upon in the EWA, and could lead to a loss. Make your roleplays a respectable size, and filled with content and good material. You must roleplay for all of your matches. After the first two unexcused offenses of not roleplaying for a match, you will be warned by a member of the EWA staff. After the third offense, you will be suspended for a period of one (1) week. After the fourth offense, you will be terminated. If you find yourself scheduled to face a "jobber", you must roleplay at least once, with some degree of effort, to get the win. If a champion is scheduled for a title defense, and does not roleplay for that title defense, he will be jobbed and lose the title. In rare circumstances, this rule can/will be disregarded. A wrestler can only hold one (1) singles title at a time. If you can't roleplay for a certain match (or matches), and have a legitimate excuse (i.e. vacation, college exams, etc.), email one of us (addresses below) and we will work something out with you. Please try to let us know as soon as possible if you need a card off; only in rare circumstances will we remove a match from a card already signed. Maximum Height for your wrestler: 7'2". Maximum Weight: 400 lbs. In special cases, (Yokozuna or The Big Show, for instance) the limits can be waived. You are allowed to control 2 wrestlers. Wrestlers may be original or real, check the roster for taken wrestlers. When your wrestlers reach a combined total of 25 wins, you will be given the right to take a third wrestler as well. Managers are optional. (Check the roster for taken managers) This federation is a roleplaying federation. In other words, if you roleplay well, you win. If not, you lose. (Barring very rare circumstances where a match has to have a set outcome to start/further a feud. If this is the case, you will be notified in advance.) The deadline for roleplaying for every event is Midnight (Eastern Time) the night before the event (i.e. Tuesday night for WNW). If this deadline is extended or shortened, you will know in advance. Vice Presidents will have NO say, whatsoever, in matches in which wrestlers they control are involved. (Meaning, they will not judge or write matches in which their wrestlers are involved) The limit for wrestlers in a stable is five (5). Interference during a match is allowed, (to an extent -- run-ins, etc) providing there is a good reason for doing so. (feud-related, etc.) Email me and tell me if you want to interfere. -NO- racist remarks of -ANY- kind will be allowed in the guestbook. I refuse to have the EWA play out the race angle. Breaking this rule will result in suspension and/or termination for -ALL- of your wrestlers. There will be no GAY Comments at all, this includes saying Queer or similar comments. If you have to resort to such low tactics, you do not belong in this fed. It will not be tolerated. Impersonation of another wrestler in the guestbook, unless I (The President) have the other person's express permission for you to do so, and am notified before time, is forbidden, and grounds for termination of all of the wrestlers you control. Attacking somebody in the guestbook is NOT permitted, unless you have cleared it with the other wrestler first, and I receive word from that wrestler. If an attack does take place without my receiving an e-mail, you will be terminated. There is an OOC board on the website. I'd like you all to feel free to use the message board at any time. Below are the main uses for the message board. **To speak out. At the message board, you can feel free to compliment me on the EWA, post suggestions you have about rule changes, the way I do things, etc., that would make the EWA a better place for us all, and if you have a problem with the decision of a match or something else I do, you may also post it in the message board. **To talk about the EWA out of character. I see a lot of your roleplaying, but it's not too often I get your opinions on the happenings of the EWA outside of your interviews. Feel free to talk about anything going on in the EWA in the message board; just remember not to do it in character. **And basically, if something's bugging you, post it here. If you post something, I'll always respond to it, as I'm sure others will do. Hopefully, this will open up communications with all of the EWA members, and add to our growing success. Please check back to this page from time to time, as it could be updated and/or changed.
