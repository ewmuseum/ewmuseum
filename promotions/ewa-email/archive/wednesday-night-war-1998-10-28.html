EWA Wednesday Night War

28 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 28, 1998 Live, from the Marine Midland Center Buffalo, New York (The words "Earlier today" show on the bottom right hand corner of the screen.  The picture is a black and white security camera shot of the rear entrance of the Marine Midland Arena.  We see part of the parking lot and there are a few cars parked nearby.  A car streaks by the camera and parks in a spot that is visible to the camera.  A man gets out of the car and approaches the entrance.  As he gets closer, we can see who it is.  It's EWA President Duane Gates.  As he gets closer ot the door, another man jumps out from behind a car and runs towards him.  The head gives the assailant away.  It's Prodigy.  He jumps President Gates from behind and rams him into the door.  In a silent fury, Prodigy whips Gates around and smashes him into the closest car, shattering the window.  Picking Gates' limp body up and slinging him over his shoulder, Prodigy walks over to one of the parked cars, opens the rear door and puts Gates in the back seat.  Prodigy runs to the front of the car and does a Dukes of Hazzard slide over the hood.  Prodigy opens the driver side door and gets in.  The lights come on and the car frantically backs out of the parking spot and then peels off into the night and off camera.  The picture fades to black and is soon replaced with the EWA Wednesday Night War opening) Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Scott Hall (w/ D-Generation X) The opening contest was a dandy, as we saw a large, physical battle between Ice and Hall. Neither man was able to gain the advantage, and suddenly the lights went out. The purple haze and the pyro followed, as Vincent Jackson, Christina Knight at his side, made his way to the ring and proceeded to brutally attack Ruger, nailing the Fallen Angel on them. He then left as sudden as he came through the entrance, but not before he uttered three words to Ruger: "November 2 Remember". One would assume this is a challenge of some sort. Ruger Ice d. Scott Hall by disqualification. JR: I am told, we are going to the locker room area where Shawn Michaels is waiting to conduct an interview *The camera shot goes to a little hallway, Michaels is leaning against the wall. He begins to speak, but someone jumps him from behind, and hits him in the back with a lead pipe, the man is dressed in all black and a black mask over his head. He takes off the mask to reveal that it the "Outlaw Immortal" KG. He throws the pipe down on Michaels' back, and walks off laughing. Garrett & Raoul v. The Black Angels A solid contest, which pretty much ended in a double countout, as Ruger Ice, furious, ran out and attacked Garrett. Joe Lemon v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) Absolute domination. In his first wrestling match in over a year, Jackson took about 30 seconds to get a victory with the Fallen Angel. Vincent Jackson d. Joe Lemon with the Fallen Angel. Christian Peterson v. Gangrel (w/ Devon) Christian was pretty much manhandled by Gangrel in this one, but oddly enough, Gangrel carried Peterson on his shoulder to the back after the match. Gangrel d. Christian Peterson with the Piledriving DDT. Eight Man Tag Team Action "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley, "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash, "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (making his EWA return), & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion) v. Chris Craft, Adam Michaels, Serpent Man, & James Mastin Two words, and they ain't "Suck It"...Squash City. DX was totally on top in this one, as it took overall about four minutes for them to get a victory. DX d. Craft/Michaels/Serpent/Mastin with Erik Draven's pin on Chris Craft after the DX Driver. "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. Prodigy (The broadcast is interrupted and the EWA JumboTron crackles to life.  The first image we see is Duane Gates bound to a chair, duct tape over his mouth, and he's screaming something inaudiable as a result.  One side of his face is cut and we can see dried blood down that side.  He seems to be yelling at the cameraman.) Vocie : You know Gates, yelling at him won't do anything.  He doesn't work for you at the moment.  He works for me right now.  Now, if you could, please look down at your arms. (Gates and the camera look down to Gates' arms.  His arms are held down to the arms of the chair by metal clasps and there are wires running into them.  Gates' eyes grow wide as he realizes that he's wired.  We hear footsteps approaching and Gates looks up.  If his jaw could drop it would.  The camera swings around and we see it's Prodigy.  In his hand he has a little box with a red button on it.  Prodigy walks right up to Gates, getting right in his face and he holds the box up close) Prodigy : You see this box?  It's connected to a power source that's connected to those wires, which are connected to those metal clasps, which are connected to you, and you're nice and grounded.  I know that you aren't a rocket scientist ther Gates, but you know what that means, right? (President Gates shakes his head in agreement) Prodigy : That's good to see.  Now, when I was locked up in the padded rooms they had me hooked up to something like this and they tried to break me.  They shocked the hell outta me and tried to make me conform to their thinking.  But I'm strong like bull, and they never did break me.  But you Duaney, you're weak.  You'd break if I shocked ya.  But I'm not gonna press the button unless I absolutely have to.  I'm not gonna have to, now am I? (Once again, Duane shakes his head in agreement, and Prodigy backs off a little) Prodigy : Now listen closely Gates, because I want to make myself crystal clear.  See, you screwed me out of the title I rightly deserved on Sunday at Clash Of The Champions.  I had Draven pinned and I could taste the victory.  Oh man was it sweet.  But you stood there and refused to count him out and hand me the title.  No, you knew that another defeat, especially one that shouldn't have been would be a great psychological advanatge for you going into our match at November 2 Remember.  You thought that you could finally break me.  Well, mission accomplished because I SNAPPED!!!!  (Prodigy reaches for the button and Gates cringes, but Prodigy stops short)  I lost it afterwards and here you are now.  Fitting isn't it, how the man in charge is the man who has the least power at the moment, itsn't it Duane-O? (Duane agrees) Prodigy : Now you know what really ticks me off?  You've been walking funny lately.  You've had people on your ass lately it makes me sick.  You tried to keep me down all this time, tried to run me out of this fed and all that time you never broke me Gates.  I'm still here and there isn't a damn thing you can do about it.  But in the face of adversity, people left.  People high tailed it out of here for no apparent reason, but I stayed.  Sure, I stayed because I wasn't going to let you win, but botton line is that this federation would have sunk to the bottom without me and you know that.  With everyone who left, my departure would have been the unkindest cut of all.  But I didn't.  And still, you sat there with a pig's head and kept me down.  And the thanks I get for that is getting screwed out of the world title.  Want to know what I feel like doing? (Prodigy raises his finger over the button and Gates once again cringes, but Prodigy takes it away) Prodigy : I feel like laying a few ground rules for our match.  This gets settled once and for all, and there will be no questions asked.  You will agree, you have no option.  At November 2 Remember, you and I will face off.  Sure, one hand will be behind my back, but I have a few stipulations of my own to add.  As we go along, just nod your head in agreement.  This will all be on tape, so you can't go back on it.  Now, I don't want to see any interference on your behalf.  Any interference and you relinquish all rights over me.  No more of this bullsh*t.  Okay? (Gates nods)  Good.  Same thing if.....no.....when I beat you, you totally lose control over me.  Got it?  (Gates nods)  I control who I face, when I face, and why I face, and you have no say in that (Gates nods)  Your little puppies known as The Hate Club are non-existant in my life, got it?  (Gates nods)  And best yet, you get down on your knees and apologize.  GOT IT?!  (Gates nods like crazy)  Good.  Now, in the rare and unlikely event that you win, the result is simple.  I never come to the EWA again.  It makes you happy, and if that's the case, it will never happen.  Do you understand?  (Gates nods again)  Good.  Now I'm going to prepare for our match.  The cameraman will help you out and back to wherever you have to go. (Prodigy steps back.  He takes the little box and turns it over, letting it fall to the ground button first.  Gates cringes expecting a jolt of electricity to surge through his body, but we hear the box hit the ground and a sinister laugh from Prodigy.  The camera then fades to black) Modest didn't show up, either, so this match was rendered a draw. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. The Prince (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Galaxy used a couple of high-flying moves early on that got the Prince winded, but the Prince was just too strong, and ended up with the victory over the challenger. The Prince d. Galaxy by submission with High Society. Six Man Tag Team Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes, "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam, & El Locco Jobbo (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA North American Champion), "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge), & "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, with the Triple Threat) This was hands-down the best match of the night. IX put together their best performance to date in the EWA, as RVD, Haynes, & Jobbo gave the Triple Threat a tough match. But in the end, The Triple Threat was just a little too powerful, and escaped with a victory in an incredibly hard-fought match. *Following "The Triple Threat's" victory over Intoxication X something strange occurs. Instead of "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple playing throughout the arena, a different song blares over the P.A. system. After a couple of seconds everyone is able to identify the musical selection as "Bomb First"(my second reply) by Makaveli. The EWA fans make the obvious link to the imminent arrival of "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg and begin to show their disapproval by exploding with boos. Stevie Richards, Justin Hicks and Jeff Bunda are all still inside the squared circle as SmirtDogg appears out from behind the curtains. The trio points towards Smirt and talk amongst themselves. SmirtDogg stations himself on top of the entrance ramp and begins to mockingly clap "The Triple Threat's efforts in securing a win over Intoxication X! SmirtDogg reveals the Golden Microphone from out of his pocket and begins to speak in front of the hostile packed house which abhors him.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: WoW, how impressive! *sarcasm* Ya'll managed to beat Will Haynes, Rob Van Dam and El Locco Jobbo. What an accomplishment! *more sarcasm* I think you three should be honored for that. Somebody should do something about changing the name of this arena from the Marine Midland Center to the "Triple Threat Center" . I mean ya'll just defeated the immortal and sure EWA Hall of Famers: Haynes, Rob Van Dam, and El Locco Jobbo. *the sarcasm continues* That is a truly amazing feat! I don't think it ever has been done before! I say we all take a minute out to worship you so called "Super Heroes" here in the EWA. *Yes, sarcasm is reigning supreme* Then again lets NOT! Enough of the sarcasm it's time to get down to business. Richards, Bunda, and Hicks what you did tonight means nothing to me. If you three *Smirt gestures towards Richards, Bunda and Hicks* couldn't beat those Intoxication X flunkies than you would have no right even being in my godly precense. "The Triple Threat" claims to be so damn good. My question to you three stooges is why would you waste your time with Intoxication X if ya'll think you're such hot sh*t. They're the bottom of the EWA talent pool and to be blunt IX is no challenge for the "Triple Threat". Even I'll admit that. The fact of the matter is you can make yourselves feel good by beating on these losers but you're not impressing me. In a few minutes I'm going to show ya'll what happens to the "The Triple Threat" when they get into the ring against someone who has talent. I shall make an example out of Shawn Copeland right before all your very eyes. I'm "GOD'S GIFT TO WRESTLING" so on a barometer of 1-10 with 10 being the highest my competitiveness attribute ranks a perfect 10. Lets see how Copeland handles this. Lets see what the Rump-Shaker Shawn Copeland is made of. I promise you three stooges now that I'll be the man to eliminate Shawn Copeland in the Battle Royal. It is then in which it shall be proven that "The Triple Threat" folds under pressure and that "The Triple Threat" FEARS competition. *Triple Threat members Richards, Hicks and Bunda have sneers upon their faces as they are unimpressed by the garbage that Smirt is throwing at them. Crowd continues to show it's displeasure with Smirt even going as far as to pelt him with soft drinks and other projectiles.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Yeah, yeah you all hate me because you know how truly great I am. Being the true I.C.O.N. of the EWA and the GREATEST wrestler in its history means that everyone is out to get you. They paint a target on your ass and try to take you out. But there's no stopping Smirt-D-O-Double G. Nobody will hold me down. By the way, Bunda, you did hear me right. I did just call myself "the greatest EWA wrestler of all-time". Do you have a problem with that??? Can't handle the truth, eh? Well I'll tell you what. You want a rematch with me -- I saw what you said the other day. Anytime, anywhere I'll wrestle you and beat you again. The result won't change. You can't defeat "God's Gift to Wrestling" no matter how hard you try. I know that shatters your ego but hey someone has to bring you to reality so why not me? *Smirt arrogantly grins as the fans chant Smirt Sucks! Smirt Sucks!* Bunda, Hicks, Richards... Au Revoir for now. I do hope you enjoy the beatdown I inflict on your buddy Shawn Copeland. HAHAHA *SmirtDogg turns around and leaves back from where he came from. The Triple Threat exits the squared circle and are hot in pursuit of Smirt after his disparaging remarks concerning them.* The Triple Threat d. Intoxication X with Justin Hicks' submission win on Rob Van Dam after the Saturday Night Special. Battle Royal Winner will recieve a Light Heavyweight Championship Match at November 2 Remember PARTICIPANTS: Deader The Pessimist KG SmirtDogg Shawn Copeland ("In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" by Greig begins to play over the PA system, and is shortly followed by The Prince, sans flower girls.  He's out of his wrestling attire and is dressed in another dark suit that looks like it was sewn with him in it.  In tow is The Prince's Light Heavyweight Championship belt, dragging along side him.  The Prince slowly makes his way to the ring and the crowd boos him as we walks by, but he pays no attention to them.  He gets to the ring, but instead of climbing in, we walks around to the announcer's table and grabs a microphone) Jim Ross : It looks like we have a visitor here for this battle royal folks! The Prince : Shut up underling, listen and learn. (The Prince turns the microphone he has in hand on and climbs on top of the announcer's table, so he's in clear view of everyone in the arena.) The Prince : Ladies and gentlemen, and I do use the term VERY loosely.  You saw me successfully defend my Light Heavyweight Championship with the greatest of ease tonight against the Natural Born Loser, Galaxy, so it is safe to say that your champion going into November 2 Remember is I.  Not that there was any doubt, but for those of you in denial over the fact that your superior has defied all your wishes and threats, I wanted to confirm it for you and send your pathetic lives into an even greater tailspin. The Prince :  But what we are about to witness is the caving in of the EWA to my commands, as well they should.  For the past twenty-one days, I have held this belt and have been unopposed as champion.  No one has come close to touching me or this belt, and that's a reflection of the talent pool that this federation posesses in this division.  But there has been an influx of talent as of late, and for the first time I'm showing a minute amount of anticipation about this division.  Tonight, right here, we will see the number one contender for my title crowned.  While it might be a great accomplishment for some, it's only a formality for me.  It doesn't matter if it is Copeland, SmirtDogg, The Pessimist, or whatever other carbon is in this match, who is named the number one contender.  Bottom line is that they get elevated to a higher status come November 2 Remember.  They get the honour of entering the same ring as me and having their hopes built up over the opportunity to win my title.  But High Society is very unforgiving and it does crush many a dream.  Who ever wins tonight.......I hope you are prepared for shattered dreams. The Prince : Now I'm going to assume a position here and provide just a bit of commentary, as any good champion should.  You ingrates enjoy your swill and see the man who gets the honour of being defeated by The Prince in a week and a half.  Now bring out the plebs. (The Prince gets down off the table and drops the microphone.  He knocks Vincent Ashe out of his chair and takes his head set, promptly putting it on) The Prince : I can see that the credibility here has just skyrocketed. Jim Ross : Well, Vincent Ashe is a ....... The Prince : I know, a commoner, and you're a hog farmer.  Be glad that I haven't had you disposed of, but don't test me tonight Ross.  Sit back and keep your mouth shut, or I'll have you wishing you were back with your momma in your lovely redneck home. Deader and The Pessimist were eliminated at the very beginning at the same time by KG, with a nice double clothesline. From there, while KG went to work on Copeland, SmirtDogg grabbed a chair, and waffled Copeland with it, toppling him over the top. KG then nailed Smirt from behind, but Smirt was able to hold on to the ropes, and propel himself back in the ring. The two then battled for a good ten minutes, before Shawn Michaels came to the ring. Michaels quickly jumped in, and clotheslined KG to the canvas. He then jumped to the outside and taunted KG, and while KG was distracted, Smirt was able to clothesline him over and win the battle royal. SmirtDogg wins the Battle Royal, and will face the Light Heavyweight Champion at November 2 Remember. Triple Threat Match Winner will be named the #1 Contender to Erik Draven at November 2 Remember Thorne Richards v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (w/ The Hate Club) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom For some odd reason, Crazy Canuck didn't bother to show up for the biggest match in his EWA Career. This turned then into a one-on-one match between Venom and Thorne. Thorne used his technical wrestling skills to neutralize Venom for a good portion of the match, but about 14 minutes through, Venom suddenly rebounded with a furious rage, and ended up hitting the Extreme Poison, pinning Thorne to get the win. (Note: Venom will now wrestle in two matches at November 2 Remember) Venom d. Thorne Richards with the Extreme Poison. Venom is the #1 Contender to the EWA World Heavyweight Championship.
