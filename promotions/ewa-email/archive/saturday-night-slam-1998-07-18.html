EWA Saturday Night Slam

18 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The United Center Chicago, Illinois Divader v. Handey Gripwell Handey seemed very friendly toward Divader, and even screamed at one point, 'Just be my friend!!' When Divader responded with a hard lariat, Gripwell promptly locked on the Testicular Claw, and was disqualified. Divader d. Handey Gripwell by disqualification. Quarterfinal King Of The Ring Tournament Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Mayne Mann (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Dream Team) Mayne Mann started off with the early advantage on Hicks, but it didn't last long, as Hicks seemed to be furious in his match tonight. Hicks soon took over, and began to methodically destroy Mayne Mann, finally finishing him off with the Saturday Night Special to, amazingly, the delight of the crowd. Afterward, the Dream Team came to the ring to help Mayne Mann up, but shockingly, Michaels executed the Sweet Chin Music on him. Copeland then nailed the Money Shot, KG the Blackout, and Smirtdogg the Gangsta's Paradise, leaving Mann brutally beaten in the center of the ring. Justin Hicks and Rebecca Claridge, who stuck around to watch the show, stood laughing at ringside. Justin Hicks d. Mayne Mann by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks advances to the King Of The Ring Semifinals. Lee Tenn v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) Lee Tenn looked awful tonight, as Canuck completely obliterated him. Crazy Canuck d. Lee Tenn with the Lunatic Bomb. The Black Angels v. Will 'The Thrill' Haynes & Aaron Martin Martin and Haynes showed good teamwork, and dominated The Black Angels. To add insult to injury, Martin then took the two mannequin legs and beat the Black Angels with them. Will Haynes and Aaron Martin d. The Black Angels with Aaron Martin's pin on Kaworu after the Shoulderbreaker. "The Brown Bomber" Robert Thompson (w/ NQ2) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (w/ Shae Marks) Thompson was pretty much destroyed, as Deke showed no dropoff from his loss to Jack McCammon. Vic Deakins d. Robert Thompson with the EMP. Non-Title Match Morbid Angel v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion, w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) Another squash...Makaveli demolished the young Morbid Angel, destroying him in 30 seconds. Makaveli d. Morbid Angel by submission with the Death Row Leglock. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Allied Powers (w/ NQ2) This was a solid tag team match, but Castor and Thorne were just a little bit more on target today, and went home with the victory. The Allied Powers d. Darkness with Castor Troy's pin on Lucien after the Face/Off. The Ultimate Fighters v. Vertigo This match never got underway, as once both teams were in the ring, the familiar entrance music of Harlem Heat blared over the PA. Out came Booker T and Stevie Ray to a thunderous ovation, dressed in their wrestling attire. The Ultimate Fighters immediately bailed from the ring and ran after them, and the two teams brawled to the back, as Vertigo got a countout win. Vertigo d. The Ultimate Fighters by countout. Ruger Ice v. I.R.K. Ruger Ice started off with the advantage, but the IRK simply overwhelmed him, winning in his debut. I.R.K. d. Ruger Ice by submission with the Killer Leg Lock. Six Man Tag Team Match Dos Equis & La Parka (w/ The Circus) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels, "Outlaw Immortal" KG (EWA World Tag Team Champions), & "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Dream Team) This was a great wrestling match, but The Circus! looked especially impressive tonight, and came away with the victory. The Circus! defeated The Dream Team with La Parka's pin on KG after the La Parka Butt Blast. Non-Title Match The Wyldsyde v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA North American Champion, w/ The Chain Gang) Another fantastic match. McCammon tried numerous times to turn this match into a brawl, but never prevailed. Instead, The Wyldsyde's technical wrestling skills won the battle, and as a result, President Gates signed a return match between the two, with The Man's Rules, for The North American Title at the King Of The Ring. The Wyldsyde d. Jack McCammon with the Wyldryde. Special Tag Team Match Signed By President Gates Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) & Johnny 2 Bad v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Dream Team) & "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C/World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was a fantastic tag team match, which completely lived up to the hype. Both teams worked almost perfectly together as tag teams, even though they had only teamed together once...except for one minor bloop. SmirtDogg was holding Van Smack up for Jeff Bunda, as Bunda hit the ropes and went for a clothesline. Van Smack ducked, and Bunda nailed Smirt, sending him flying outside the ring. Bunda, however, was quick enough to whip around and nail the Showrunner on Van Smack for the three count. Afterward on the outside, Van Smack and Johnny 2 Bad almost immediately began brawling all the way to the back. Meanwhile, as Jeff Bunda celebrated, SmirtDogg climbed into the ring, and shoved him in the back. Smirt took the microphone, and demanded his World Title match....TONIGHT! Jeff Bunda agreed, and the fight was on! It didn't take long, however, for the two men to begin a brawl on the outside, in which, then The Dream Team and Triple Threat ran out, and everybody brawled to end the show. SmirtDogg/Jeff Bunda d. Van Smack/Johnny 2 Bad with Jeff Bunda's pin on Van Smack after the Showrunner. SmirtDogg and Jeff Bunda battled to a double disqualification. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA World Heavyweight Championship. <!--LINE2--> <!--LINE4-->
