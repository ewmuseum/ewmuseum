EWA Wednesday Night War (1/27/99) January 27, 1999 Live, from the Key Arena Seattle, Washington Interview with "The Innovator Of Offense" Kanyon *Fireworks explode in the background as the WNW theme music is playing over the speakers and the fans are waving their signs around hoping to be seen on camera. The lights in the arena come back up, as we hear Jim Ross welcoming everyone to another edition and telling the folks at home the lineup for this evening. As he's finishing, Organ music blares over the speakers, and a green mist engulfs the stage. And with that, "The Innovator Of Offense" Kanyon appears from behind the curtain wearing his half of the EWA World Tag Team Title Belts, and makes his way down to the ring amidst a fairly good pop...but he's still not a mega face. He hops into the ring, and takes the microphone from the announcer. He's not his normal cheery self either, which makes us wonder what's on his mind.* "The Innovator Of Offense" Kanyon: Hey Seattle, what's going on?! *loud crowd response* Good, glad to hear it. Now I'm not my normal happy-go-lucky self tonight, and I know you folks paid good money to get in here tonight, so I'll be brief and then let you get on with the matches. Now, as everyone knows, Public Enemy brutally attacked my partner and the other half of the World Tag Team Champions Raven, while I was preparing for my match a week ago. Raven is fine, and he wants to wrestle them at the up and coming Clash of the Champions X! However, the so called "doctors", and I'd like to see what school they graduated from...are saying that Raven can't wrestle. They won't sign the medical release form in order for my partner to wrestle, which means that you won't see Raven in the ring come the Clash. *The crowd boos, cause whether you like him or not, Raven is always fun to watch* So, that brings me to the big question I suppose, who am I going to chose as my partner to defend these titles?!?! *The crowd starts chanting "Wrath! Wrath! Wrath!", as Kanyon just smiles and tips his hat* "The Innovator of Offense" Kanyon: Thanks...I thought about that as well. But Wrath is also on the shelf at the moment, as he was in a tournament in Japan and dislocated his shoulder. Just got off the phone with him last night. And I was told by President Gates that I had to make a decision by tonight. Thanks Prez, that made my life much easier I assure you. So, after looking up and down the roster here in the EWA, I got kind of confused. I mean, there are so many people here that I would be honored to have in the ring and share the titles for a night with me. But, whenever you do that, you set yourself up. Whether it be alterior motives, or someone that I know Public Enemy has all ready faced...it just didn't seem like the right thing to do. And it wasn't until Swap Meat Louie opened his mouth that the answer became clear to me. You see, you said something about hardcore boys, and the fact that we couldn't take a lickin' and keep on tickin'. Well, Raven would be here if he could, and Kanyon...well, you know I can go all night. But, now you've sealed your own fate, as I just got off the horn with the man who's going to be my partner come the Clash. And it is I who am humbled to call this man my friend, and for Saturday Night...my parter. He's not here tonight, but trust me...come the Clash of the Champions he will be. Ladies and Gentlemen...*The crowd is growing eager to find out who it's going to be* I give to you, the most respected and most indestructible man in the history of this sport. Clash of the Champions...it's going to be The Public Enemy, taking on "The Innovator Of Offense" Kanyon, and... MANKIND!!! *The crowd explodes with approval of his decision, as Kanyon just smiles in the ring* "The Innovator of Offense" Kanyon: Thanks very much, and Thorny...tonight you're mine. Prepare for the FLATLINE!!! (The Kanyon interview has just concluded, and we join Jim Ross and Vincent Ashe at ringside.) Jim Ross: Hello everyone, and welcome to Wednesday Night War! I'm Jim Ross, alongside my broadcast colleague Vincent Ashe, and what a night we have in store for you! E-'Nuff defends the Hardcore Title against the self-proclaimed God Of Violence, Johnny Napalm! Terry Conklin will put the Intercontinental Title on the line against Jack Reed! But that's not all! Vincent Ashe: The Franchise, Jeff Bunda will defend his European Title against The Heartbreak Kid, Shawn Michaels. Max Pins will challenge Aaron Martin for the Light Heavyweight Title. Listen to this crowd! Vincent Ashe: And Michaels is in the ring, and he has the microphone! "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Reed, Jackson, you wanna attack my boy while nobody's around? I have hunted down Edge, and Edge is nevermore now, as he has officially met his executioner...his older brother, Gangrel.
