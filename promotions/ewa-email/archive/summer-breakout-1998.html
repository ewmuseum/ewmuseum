EWA Summer Breakout '98

20 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Summer Breakout '98 June 20, 1998 Live, from the sunny coast of Venice Beach, California (Outdoors Event) ---- Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. Mayne Mann Winner will recieve EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. Lee Tenn 2 Cold Scorpio (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. Hannibal Manson Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. Ruger Ice EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Return Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) Hans Gruber v. "The Rocket" Joe Satre Six Man Tag Team Match "The Total Package" Lex Luger, Scott Hall, & "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock, The Patriot, and a mystery partner Jack Reed v. KooKabura EWA World Television Championship The Gunslinger v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Barbed Wire Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Prodigy Steel Cage U.S. Heavyweight Championship Grudge Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Unification Match for the EWA World Tag Team Championship The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The WolfPack & Keli Turner) Return Grudge Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Wyldsyde EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA World Heavyweight/European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) PREVIEW Tonight, the EWA presents another pay-per-view spectacular, Summer Breakout '98. The ring area has been set up, the fans are filing into the folding chairs on the beach, and the video screen is showing footage of Stevie Richards winning the championship. Let's get ready for three hours of the best wrestling action out there. To start the night off, we have Makaveli squaring off against EWA newcomer Mayne Mann. Mann has impressed many in his two previous matches, getting quick defeats. But Makaveli is riding an impressive return, which saw him stealing Stevie Richards' European Championship belt and renaming it "The Ghetto Belt". The question is, will the European/World Heavyweight Champion play a part in this contest? The second match on the card features two of the most exciting young athletes in the business, Lee Tenn and "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland. These two men will be battling for the right to obtain an EWA Light Heavyweight Championship title shot at next week's Saturday Night Slam. These two have never met in the ring, and this should be an exciting contest. Our next match is an interesting one, featuring Hannibal Manson, who has returned to the EWA, and 2 Cold Scorpio, who, thanks to Thorne Richards and NQ2, has had his career rejuvenated. Konnan will finally step into the ring against Ruger Ice. Ruger has promised to help rid the EWA of The WolfPack, and according to Shawn Michaels, the WolfPack is nearly dead. Could Konnan be the next casuality tonight? In the rekindling of an old rivalry, Shawn Michaels and Aaron Martin will meet once again, this time for the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. This will be their ninth meeting, and they will meet once again in the qualifying match of the King of the Ring. History is on Martin's side in this one, but The Heartbreak Kid is far from being counted out. In another exciting matchup between two newcomers, Hannibal Manson's sidekick, German wrestler Hans Gruber, will step into the ring against the newly renamed 'Scrap Iron' Joe Satre. In a match that the fans are looking forward to, NWO WolfPack members Lex Luger, Scott Hall, and Kevin Nash will square off against Stars And Stripes, and their mystery partner. A lot of buzz has been circulating as to just who is their mystery partner, and a lot of ideas have been tossed around, but nobody seems to know for sure. The match between Jack Reed and KooKabura was thrown out, due to contract problems. Jack McCammon has been noticably absent from Venice Beach this weekend, and nobody, including his hired assistant/watchguard Phil, has seen or heard from him. He's scheduled to defend the World Television Championship against The Gunslinger, who seems to have rejuvenated his career. In a matchup of two of the greatest technical wrestlers in the history of our sport, Thorne Richards, leader of NQ2, will square off against "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair. These two have met once before, with the end result being a dou In the EWA's first ever Barb Wire match, perhaps signed by President Gates just as punishment, Prodigy will square off against Will "The Thrill" Haynes. Both of these men are great athletes, and both have motivation to win this match: who will come out on top? In a cage for the U.S. Title, KG will face SmirtDogg. This has been brewing ever since SmirtDogg disguised himself as Serpent Man and humilated KG. Now, KG looks for revenge. Is Venice Beach, California the final resting place for the self-proclaimed "God's Gift To Wrestling"? In a World Tag Team Championship match, the undefeated Public Enemy, in their final EWA match, will defend the titles against Perry Saturn and John Kronus, the undefeated U.S. Tag Team Champions, The Eliminators. This should be a spectacular matchup. The return match between Justin Hicks and The Wyldsyde has been brewing for a long time, but reached its peak last Saturday, when The Wyldsyde defended the U.S. Title against SmirtDogg, with Hicks officiating. Hicks blatently cheated The Wyldsyde, and as a result, this match, under WyldStyle rules, has been signed for the spectacular that is EWA Summer Breakout '98. And finally, a main event of gigantic proportions. The King Of Swing, EWA European and World Heavyweight Champion, Stevie Richards, will defend the EWA World Heavyweight Championship against his Triple Threat compadre, EWA Intercontinental Champion "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda. It was supposed to be Bunda getting the title shot against Erik Draven, but one week ago, Stevie defeated Draven to become the new champion. An amazing three title belts are being represented in this matchup, and it has the potential to be the greatest EWA Championship match of all time. Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. Mayne Mann The opening match at Summer Breakout '98 was definitely an eventful one. Mayne Mann came out first, to a mild reception from the crowd. When "Hit 'Em Up" by Tupac came on, the crowd erupted. (After all, we're in Cali, aren't we?) **Makaveli is followed to the ring by fifteen black guys armed with bats and weapons. They all step into the ring and Makaveli signals for the mic.** The Ghetto Champion: Makaveli: Ok, before I beat on Maid Mann here, you all must be wondering why I got my crew out here with me. Everybody knows that the hottest, most talked about title going today is my very own Ghetto Belt. Now, knowing what a coward Stevie Richards is, he'll probably want to come attack me from behind during the match and try to steal my belt. Since we're in my home state, CALI, I thought I'd bring along a few of my homeboys to surround the ring. You know, just to keep things real around here so Stevie won't start no s**t during my match. And Maid Mann, I see you in the corner there sucking your finger and pis*ing your pants. Not to worry, as long as you keep it clean, it'll seem as if my boys are not even at ringside. Oh, remember, this is a non-title match. Maid Mann is definitely not worthy of even being in the ring with me. The match then started, with Mann attacking Makaveli as he sat the microphone down. Mann went to work on the Oakland native, executing a hard standing lariat, knocking Tupac to the mat. Mann picked Makaveli up, and whipped him to the other side, coming back with a Spear-type tackle on 2Pac. This went on for a few minutes, until suddenly, there was a rustle from the crowd. The camera panned to the entrance ramp, where "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland stood. Copeland watched this match for a while, and Mann strengthened his advantage, working on Makaveli with his power. Another cheer went up from the crowd, as "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards began to walk to ringside. One of Makaveli's guards immediately spotted him, and all fifteen took off down the aisle. Stevie turned to run away, nearly tripped on the sand, but stayed upright and ran away, guards in tow. As soon as they were all out of sight, Makaveli began to take control of the match. Suddenly, four men in masks jumped over the guardrail, and each slammed Makaveli down to the mat. The official had seen enough, and called for the bell. The four men unmasked, and to the crowd's delight, revealed....Rufus Lamar Jackson III, Arnold Drummond, J.J, and Fat "HEY HEY HEY" Albert! Makaveli d. Mayne Mann by disqualification. Winner will recieve EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. Lee Tenn Mayne Mann actually accompanied Copeland to the ring for this contest, and it seemed to pay off. Tenn started off in control for the first few minutes, but the tide quickly shifted when Mann reached out and tripped Tenn. Tenn quickly bounced up and began to yell at Mann, causing Copeland to come fron behind with a huge low blow that even Andrew Golotta would've been proud of. From there, it was all Copeland, ending the match with a new move, The Money Shot. Shawn Copeland d. Lee Tenn with The Money Shot. After the match, Thorne Richards came out, and took a microphone. He shook Copeland's hand, and congratulated him for bringing a new member into NQ2. However, Copeland and Mann then attacked Richards. Security had to hold back Troy, 2 Cold Scorpio, and The Brown Bomber from getting at the two. 2 Cold Scorpio (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. Hannibal Manson 2 Cold stayed around, and Hannibal Manson then came to the ring. This was a good match for awhile, but eventually, Manson's experience got the better of Scorpio. Hannibal Manson d. 2 Cold Scorpio with Silence. Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. Ruger Ice Konnan was dominated. Plain and simple. Ruger absolutely destroyed K-Dawg, and when he hit the Spinebuster, the front row fans heard a loud POP. Konnan, needless to say, was stretchered out of the ring. Ruger Ice d. Konnan with the Spinebuster. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Return Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) Michaels came out to his old "Sexy Boy" music, and started the match off in control by dragging Martin down to the ring. Michaels tried to keep Martin off of the ground, by wrestling him to the mat, and succeeded for the first few minutes. But soon after, Martin took control by countering a Figure Four from Michaels, and soon after, ducked a Sweet Chin Music attempt, hit the Shoulderbreaker, and became the first ever 3-Time EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. Afterward, Michaels was furious with himself, and stormed off. Aaron Martin d. Shawn Michaels with the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! Hans Gruber v. "The Rocket" Joe Satre Gruber and Satre battled for about six minutes before it spilled onto the outside, and both men were counted out. Hans Gruber and Joe Satre battled to a double countout. After this match, EWA President Duane Gates came to the ring, and took the microphone. Gates: In response to the recent quote-unquote "attack" perpertrated on me by Mr. Jeff Bunda, among the numerous threats I have recieved, I have took it upon myself to hire a bodyguard. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome ("Get At Me Dogg") my bodyguard, the EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, SmirtDogg! *EWA United States Champion SmirtDogg arrives on the scene and strolls down the aisle along with his two lovely valets -- Lil'Kim and Foxy Brown. Smirt has the EWA U.S. Title draped over his shoulder as he enters the ring. He holds the middle rope up for the two divas as they slide inside the squared circle. SmirtDogg approaches Gates and has a pissed off look on his face.* Gates: SmirtDogg, I'd like to thank you for accep...*Gates is cut off as SmirtDogg grabs the microphone.* SmirtDogg: Yeah yeah whatever. Get on with this sh*t already. *Gates appears angry.* Gates: Look, Smirt, we had a deal that you would come out here and accompany me...*Smirt again takes the mike* SmirtDogg: I know the deal. Am I not out here right now? Gates: Indeed you are. And remember that little stipulation we added to your contract if you're not out here? SmirtDogg: What stipulation? Speak English Gates! *Gates takes what appears to be a contract out of his -ugly- Hawaiian shirt. Our beloved President doesn't appear to be one for style.* Gates: Ahem. "Also, if SmirtDogg hearby fails on his contractual agreement to accompany President Gates to the EWA ring, he will be stripped of his EWA salary for one (1) month." That stipulation, Mr. SmirtDogg. SmirtDogg: What kind of bullsh*t is that? You can take that stipulation and shove it up your fat ass for all I care. I'll do what I please and if you don't like it -- well I challenge you to find a wrestler who can replace "God's Gift to Wrestling". *Gates continues, seemingly ignoring SmirtDogg.* Gates: Bunda, don't even think about coming near me now. That's all. Come along, bodyguard. *Gates leaves the ring, and reluctantly, SmirtDogg follows, muttering with a scowl on his face.* Six Man Tag Team Match "The Total Package" Lex Luger, Scott Hall, & "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock, The Patriot, and a mystery partner Luger, Hall, and Nash arrived separately, and to their former pre-WolfPack music, symbolizing that The WolfPack is indeed dead. The Patriot's music then played, as Madusa, Ken Shamrock and The Patriot came out from the back. About midway to the ring, both men stopped, and The Patriot pointed to the ring entrance. A cloud of smoke rose, and a man stepped out. When he was far enough out, the crowd began to cheer, and the camera panned in...to reveal the mystery partner, "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins! The three men walked to the ring together, and less than eight minutes later, were victorious in the ring, as Hall, Nash, & Luger showed virtually no teamwork or concentration, and were promptly squashed. Afterward, The Patriot took the microphone. The Patriot: Vic, I want to thank you for standing by us in this contest. It has been an honor and a privelage to team with you. (Both men shake Vic's hand.) Now, this goes out to The Eliminators. Perry Saturn, John Kronus, we feel we deserve a shot at your U.S. Tag Team Titles. So we're issuing a challenge for next Saturday. If you've got the guts, you'll accept. (The Patriot's music plays, and the men head to the back.) EWA World Television Championship The Gunslinger v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) McCammon's music started and restarted three different times, but no McCammon. Finally, President Gates and SmirtDogg walked out to the ring, and Gates took the microphone. Gates had the EWA World Television Championship belt with him. Gates: This was lying at the foot of my trailer this morning. As some of you may know, Phil was hired by the EWA upon Jack McCammon's arrival to make sure that he came to all the cards. McCammon has a history of irrational behavior, and this is just an example of it. Phil, you obviously have not done your job, so I am here to announce that you, my friend, are fired. And as for you, Gunslinger, since McCammon isn't here, I have no choice but to award you the EWA World Television Championship. *Gates and SmirtDogg, still muttering, leave.* The Gunslinger d. Jack "The Man" McCammon by forfeit. The Gunslinger is the new EWA World Television Champion! Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2: The New Breed) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair This match was another spectacular one between these two, but unlike the first installment, was definitely not a draw. Thorne started off with the advantage, working on the back of Flair, but as the match went on, he began to noticably hobble. Apparantly the attack from Mayne Mann and Copeland injured Thorne's right knee. The Nature Boy, being the cagy veteran that he is, saw this, and went to work on his knee. Flair eventually placed The Figure Four on Thorne Richards, and after two minutes of pain, Thorne had no choice but to tap out. Afterward, Copeland and Mann ran to the ring with chairs, Copeland nailing Thorne and Mann nailing Flair. NQ2 ran out, but Mann and Copeland stopped them, and from behind, "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre nailed them with chairs as well. The trio then ran to the back together. Ric Flair d. Thorne Richards by submission with The Figure Four. Barbed Wire Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Prodigy The match started out as both participants were leary of the barbed wire. Neither Will nor Prodigy came near the wire until Prodigy nailed Will Haynes with an Irish Whip, sending him into the far turnbuckle. Haynes' back was busted open, which allowed Prodigy to take the advantage, working over Haynes with a series of suplexes and power bombs. However, Haynes tripped Prodigy, sending him face first from the barbed wire. Prodigy was now busted open, and Haynes took the advantage. He kept Prodigy caught on the wire, and worked him over with several stiff chair shots to the head. He rolled underneath the wire and got out a table and a pair of wire cutters and began snipping the wire around Prodigy, in an attempt to free him. At this moment, Duane Gates came out from the back, laughing at the massacre. Prodigy did not notice Gates until he was on the table, and moved out of the way just as Haynes was coming through it off of a somersault centon over the barbed wire. Prodigy mocked Gates, and as punishment for Gates coming to the ring area, Prodigy took Haynes back in the ring, wrapped barbed wire around a steel chair, and began to beat him over the head with it, which destoryed the chair and Haynes. The referee then stopped the match, as it appeared Haynes was unconscious and therefore unable to continue. In a sign of pure contempt for Duane Gates, Prodigy tied up Will Haynes in the barbed wire opposite the entrance, arms fully extended, as the EWA Damage Control entered the ring. Prodigy d. Will Haynes via incapacitation. Steel Cage U.S. Heavyweight Championship Grudge Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Sunny stayed in the back for this one. SmirtDogg and KG started off with neither man taking the advantage, until Smirt gave a vicious low blow to KG. From there, SmirtDogg went to work on KG, tossing him into the cage walls like a rag doll. Smirt then pulled the mask of Serpent Man out of his tights, and began choking KG with it, trying to humiliate KG. KG eventually mounted a short comeback, but it was just that: short. SmirtDogg placed the Thug Breaker onto KG, and KG had no choice but to tap out. SmirtDogg immediately went for the microphone, and once he got it, to the shock of the crowd, helped KG up. SmirtDogg: KG -- over the last month we feuded all across the United States in every federation that matters -- and to be honest I've grown tired of it. So I decided to let bygones be bygones and bury the hatchet with you -- here and now. Whatcha say KG? KG: You know Smirt, the only reason I stabbed you in the back over in the INWA is because over in the IWA, you frankly tried to kill me, but aight...I got your back. SmirtDogg: Well the past is the past and since that sh*t is all over with I have an offer for you. An offer to join up once again with the greatest stable ever to grace the EWA. KG -- if you want to be a Ghetto Warrior again -- here's your chance -- brotha! KG: Smirt, you and me, we've been boys for a while, recently you've been pissing me off, but I'm willing to put it all behind me and we can tear up the EWA roster. SmirtDogg: KG and Smirt-D-O-Double G on the same team again, heh, that sh*t should be illegal because we're too lethal. Natural Born Killas is even too soft of a tag for us. Word up. Welcome back into the crew -- "Outlaw Immortal" KG! *The two men shake hands and with that the Ghetto Warriors are united once again. Suddenly SmirtDogg swings with a vicious right uppercut -- knocking KG to the ground. SmirtDogg then proceeds to stomp on KG and as he's stepping on him -- Smirt demands the microphone from the ring announcer. The ring announcer flips him the microphone and then SmirtDogg begins to speak.* SmirtDogg: KG, KG -- once again you have proved that you have the intelligence of a rodent. How many times are you going to put your trust in me -- only just to get knocked the f*ck out. *Smirt drives his boot deep into KG's chest. KG is coughing for air* KG -- you are a worthless scrub. Do the best thing you can for yourself and run away. Run away from the EWA and go back to the slums of the earth where you came from. You'll never amount to anything in wrestling. In fact, like I said before you are the laughing stock of the business. KG -- you make me sick. Just the sight of you does that. I have the perfect gig for you. Although it only occurs once a year but you can display those lack of skills you have at the Jerry Lewis Telethon. People will feel sorry for your retarded ass and money will come flowing in like an ocean. Jerry will love you. You'll have it made. I have visions of you as the Jerry Lewis Telethon poster boy. *he laughs* *SmirtDogg stomps down on KG's head and then proceeds to leave the ring with an arrogant look upon his face.* SmirtDogg d. KG by submission with the Thug Breaker. SmirtDogg is still the EWA United States Heavyweight Champion. Unification Match for the EWA World Tag Team Championship The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The WolfPack & Keli Turner) Public Enemy started off this match on fire, as they got an unbelievable crowd response in their final match. They dominated the match for the first six minutes, but the domination ended when Kronus flipped out of a Ghetto Boy powerbomb, and powerslammed him. From there, it was all Eliminators. Saturn and Kronus dominated the match with their variety of wrestling, ending it off with -- you guessed it -- Total Elimination. The Eliminators d. Public Enemy with Perry Saturn's pin on Swap Meet Louie after the Total Elimination. Return Grudge Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Wyldsyde The Wyldsyde came to the ring to a thunderous course of boos, as the fans apparently do not like his "traditional wrestling" attitude. Meanwhile, the EWA fans have apparently taken a real liking to Justin Hicks...or maybe they just popped huge for the bikini-clad Rebecca Claridge. Either way, the duo got a tremendous ovation. This match went back and forth, with neither man getting a clear edge. In fact, it went twenty minutes like this -- and ended in a time limit draw. The Wyldsyde and "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks battled to a time limit draw. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA World Heavyweight/European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) One of the most anticipated World Title matchups in EWA History lived up to all the hype. The Triple Threat members not involved, Aaron Martin, Justin Hicks, and Rebecca Claridge, all brought folding lawn chairs out to the entrance, and sat down to watch the match. Jeff Bunda entered the ring first, and got a nice ovation from the crowd, due to his accomplishments. Stevie then came out to a thunderous ovation from the crowd, and the match began. The two men were very tentative at the beginning, executing a few light wrestling manuevers. The match went this way for the first five minutes, until Bunda whipped Richards into the ropes. Richards came back with a powerful lariat, which seemed to infuriate Bunda. Bunda stood up, and shoved Richards; Richards shoved him back, and the fight was on. Bunda took the first advantage with an armbar to the mat, into a crossface-type hold on Stevie. From there, The Franchise crawled over, and rolled Stevie up for a two count. Stevie quickly bounced back up, and the two locked up. Richards took Bunda down with a hip-toss, and when Bunda got right back up, took him down with another into an armbar of his own. Stevie lifted Bunda up, and placed him into a variation of the Dragon Sleeper. The official saw Bunda fading, and lifted his hand once...twice...but not thrice, as Bunda fought out of the sleeper, and beautifully flipped Richards over. Bunda stood up, and drove some elbows into the abdominal area of Richards, following with a legdrop. Bunda picked Richards up, and hooked him for a vertical suplex, holding Richards high above his head for around five seconds before dropping him. He made the cover, but only got a two count. Bunda thought that the referee had made a slow count, and began to argue. A mistake, as Richards rolled Bunda up from behind, but also only got a two count. Both men got up quickly, and Richards took Bunda down with a drop toe-hold, and countered that into an ankle-type-submission. Bunda quickly reached the ropes, and Stevie was forced to break the hold. Bunda began climbing to his feet, and Richards went up top for a dropkick. However, Bunda was able to bounce against the ropes, and Stevie landed hard on the turnbuckle. Bunda climbed to the rope, and executed a beautiful superplex from the top rope. Bunda then immediately bounced back up and picked Richards back up. He lifted him high into the air...and nailed his old finisher, The Apocalypse Drop!! He then covered Richards...1, 2, ....but Stevie kicked out!! Unbelievably, Stevie Richards kicked out of the Apocalypse Drop! Bunda was obviously beginning to get frustrated, and picked Stevie up, whipping him hard into the turnbuckle. Bunda ran from the other end of the ring to clothesline him, but Stevie moved, and when Bunda turned around, nailed the Stevie Kick! Richards for the cover....1.....2...BUNDA KICKS OUT! Amazing! Jeff Bunda does what no other man has done, and kicks out of the Stevie Kick! Richards is stunned, and the fans are going crazy over the match. Richards picked Bunda up, and placed him in position for the Stevie Bomb. Stevie lifts him up....but Bunda flips out...Bunda picks Stevie up for a gutwrench...into a powerbomb...that's the Showrunner!! Bunda just hit the Showrunner! Bunda crawls over to Richards, and makes the cover...the referee counts.... 1 2 3!!! Jeff Bunda has defeated Stevie Richards, and has become the new EWA World Heavyweight Champion! The official hands Bunda the belt...and Richards, from behind, whips Bunda around! Richards has a furious look on his face, and the two friends are now staring each other down eye to eye...and Richards embraces Bunda! The two men hug in the ring, and Jeff Bunda is the champ! Jeff Bunda d. Stevie Richards with the Showrunner. Jeff Bunda is the new EWA World Heavyweight Champion!! Thanks for the great roleplaying for this PPV. I hope the writeup was what you asked for.
