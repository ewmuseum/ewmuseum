Johnson kept trying to break it up but it only allowed for more time for Booker and Stevie Ray to assualt Shawn Cortez. Booker finally left the ring. Stevie Ray still had control of Cortez. Ray kept taunting Cortez to get back up but each time Cortez tried he would be kicked in the head by Stevie sending him back to the mat. Stevie Ray then decided he had enough of teasing Cortez and picked him up. He went to DDT him, but Cortez backdropped out of it. Cortez then made it over and tagged Johnson. He climbed on the turnbuckle and waited for Stevie Ray to come at him. Rod Johnson jumped off the turnbuckle and hit Stevie Ray with a spinning heel kick. He went for the pin, but Stevie Ray kicked out at one. Johnson brought Ray to his to his feet and nailed him with rolling German suplexes(ala Chris Benoit). Johnson made another cover, but Stevie Ray kicked out again. Johnson having enough went to the top rope and jumped off at Stevie Ray. Ray caught him and powerslammed him to the mat. Stevie Ray then knocked the wind out of Johnson with a falling headbutt to the chest. Ray made the cover ... kickout at two. Ray picked Johnson up and delivered him to the mat with a Russian Legsweep. Stevie Ray then locked Rod Johnson in a bow-and-arrow submission. Johnson was about to give up when Cortez stormed the ring and started stomping away on Stevie Ray. Booker was into the ring and clotheslined Cortez out of his boots. And through him out the ring. Stevie Ray dropped a leg across Johnson's throat for good measure. Stevie Ray made yet another cover but Johnson kicked out. Stevie Ray dragged Rod Johnson to his feet and whipped him into the ropes ... DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE. Both Stevie and Johnson were down. The referee got to six before they started to make a move ... to their respective corners. Stevie Ray got their first and tagged Booker T. Booker was in the ring, before Cortez... it was all the time that he needed, because as Cortez ran at him... Booker nailed him with the Harlem Sidekick!! 1.. 2.. 3!!! Winner - Harlem Heat via Booker T's Harlem Sidekick Harlem Heat are the NEW EWA World Tag Team Champions
