EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

8 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 8, 1998 Live, from The Rosemont Horizon Center, Chicago, Illinois. EWA United States Title Tournament Semi-Final "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Raven (w/ DDT) Triangle Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) EWA United States Title Tournament Semi-Final "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) v. Thorne Richards Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. The Golden One Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Widow Maker Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (EWA Light Heavyweight/European Champion) KG (w/ Sunny) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Return Match Dude Love v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. Eclipse Triple Threat Match for the EWA World Television Championship The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Castor Troy v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) EWA United States Title Tournament Semi-Final "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Raven (w/ DDT) This was a well-fought match, but Raven had a slight advantage, and Even Flowed his way into the finals. Raven d. Jeff Bunda w/ the Even Flow DDT. (12:21) Triangle Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) This match was dominated by Death Sentance, who simply tore the other two teams apart. Death Sentance wins the triangle match. (8:19) EWA United States Title Tournament Semi-Final "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) v. Thorne Richards A hard fought match, but in the end, Thorne Richards felt the Bang, as DDP advanced to the finals. Page was informed that he would have to fight two matches at SNS, as he was also scheduled to tag with KG against Degeneration X. DDP d. Thorne Richards w/ the Diamond Cutter. (13:43) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. The Golden One The Golden One was in control for most of this match. When he couldn't get Deathstar in the Golden Edge, the advantage shifted, but not for long, as Golden One put Deathstar away with a nicely executed small package. The Golden One d. Deathstar w/ a small package. (9:32) Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Justin Hicks impressed many during this match, as he overpowered Galaxy, picking up the win. Justin Hicks d. Galaxy w/ the Saturday Night Special. (6:49) Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Widow Maker Prodigy held on to defeat The Widow Maker with his Firestarter, but the occurances after the match left many people in shock. (A loud burst of static crackles across the PA system...Prodigy is standing in the center of the ring, having just disposed of Widow Maker, celebrating with the rest of DX...He looks around the arena, in search of the source... The DX theme music fades out, and is replaced by a loud organ...a few moments pass...) ????: Last week was only the beginning...the hunter has become the hunted, Prodigy. Look over your shoulder...look under every rock you see...Sleep with one eye open, but...none of it will matter...For when you see me...(The voice chuckles) It will be too late. Prepare yourself, Degenerate... (Another loud crackle of static blasts over the PA system...and silence resounds throughout the arena...the announcers don't know what to make of this, and attempt to stammer out a 'sign off' line as the camera fades, but fail...and we take a commerical break.) Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair A real slobberknocker, with a shocking outcome. "Double A" Arn Anderson made his way to the ring mid-way through the match, and immediately began to cheer on Flair. But when Dreamer threw Flair to the outside, Anderson gave Flair the DDT, and then DDT started to attack Flair. But KG and DDP came to the ring, and made the save for Flair. Ric Flair d. Tommy Dreamer by DQ. (11:22) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (EWA Light Heavyweight/European Champion) Martin was all over Wright in this match, and easily took the belt from him. Afterward, Martin introduced his "masked man" as former EWA superstar "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner as a member of the Triple Threat, and announced the formation of their new tag team, The Gorgeous Threat. Aaron Martin d. Alex Wright w/ the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! KG (w/ Sunny) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Maivia held the advantage for the early portion of the match, but KG battled back, and eventually secured a win. KG d. The Rock w/ The Blackout. (5:21) Return Match Dude Love v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Before the match, Dude Love came on the PA: "Whoooa Daddy. The Dude can't seem to bring himself to face off against someone who spreads so much of the love that Dude Love holds so dear. So, I've been forced to ask one of my friends to take this one match for me." From there, Cactus Jack came to the ring, and absolutely destroyed Koo, leading Comissioner Hart to sign a rather unusual match. (see SNS Card) Cactus Jack d. KooKabura w/ the Piledriver. (3:01) The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. Eclipse Eclipse held the early advantage, but with cheap tatics, The Gunslinger battled back, and obtained the victory. The Gunslinger d. Eclipse with the Quickdraw. (6:34) Triple Threat Match for the EWA World Television Championship The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Castor Troy v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Steve Austin was actually the first to be eliminated, but he eliminated himself, saying he "didn't want the belt, because I got bigger fish to fry on Saturday." From there, Castor Troy took Pessimist apart, winning the TV Title. Castor Troy d. The Pessimist with the FaceOff. Castor Troy is the new EWA World Television Champion!!
