EWA Clash Of The Champions II

4 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Clash Of The Champions II April 4, 1998 Live, from University Arena in Muncie, Indiana. Non-Title Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) This was a fantastic match, marred by interference. Around fifteen minutes into the match, Steve Austin clotheslined Flair over the top rope. Austin then fell to the mat from exhaustion. As referee Mark Curtis checked Austin, Tommy Dreamer ran to the ring. Dreamer DDT'd Flair on the outside, and then rolled him back in the ring. Austin took advantage of the opportunity, and pinned Flair, giving him his first EWA loss. But it didn't end there; Shawn Michaels came from out of the crowd, and Superkicked Austin. Michaels then stole the TV belt from ringside, and ran back to the back with it. Steve Austin d. Ric Flair with interference. (15:32) Dude Love v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Neither man showed up, and a rematch was signed for Wednesday Night Warzone. The match was ruled a no-contest. First Round EWA US Tournament Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. Jeff Bunda (w/ Triple Threat) This was a pretty competitive match, but once again, interference played a role in the match. As the momentum began to shift to KG, a masked man ran out from the crowd. The masked man distracted KG, and Bunda was able to roll him up for the victory. Jeff Bunda d. KG with a rollup from behind. (9:11) Jeff Bunda advances in the EWA U.S. Title Tournament. First Round EWA US Tournament Match Raven (w/ DDT) v. The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) The Pessimist was dominated in this one by Raven. Raven d. The Pessimist with the Even Flow DDT. (3:54) Raven advances in the EWA U.S. Title Tournament. First Round EWA US Tournament Match Eclipse v. "Diamond" Dallas Page Another bout of interference in an other-wise terrific match. Eclipse was signaling for the Badmoon, when Raven came down. But instead of going after Eclipse, Raven gave Page the Even Flow. Eclipse stared Raven down, and Raven proceded to give him the Even Flow as well. Dallas Page d. Eclipse by disqualification. (10:02) Page advances in the EWA U.S. Title Tournament. First Round EWA US Tournament Match The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. Thorne Richards This was an outstanding match, but with a shocking outcome, as a ghost from the Gunslinger's past emerged in the form of "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley. Helmsley, sporting a Degeneration X shirt, made his way to ringside with a microphone, calling out Gunslinger. 'Slinger didn't waste any time going after him, as the two brawled all the way to the back. Thorne Richards d. The Gunslinger by countout. (13:21) Thorne Richards advances in the EWA U.S. Title Tournament. The Widow Maker v. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) The Golden One polished Widow Maker off in quick fashion, making an impressive EWA debut. The Golden One d. Widow Maker with the Golden Edge. (2:47) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (EWA Light Heavyweight/European Champion, w/ Dance Fever) This match ended in a double countout, as both men brawled on the outside. Galaxy and Alex Wright battled to a double countout. Alex Wright retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ Triple Threat) v. Castor Troy Martin made surprisingly short work of Troy, as he seemed off his game. Martin, however, was on top of it, dominating Troy from start to finish. Aaron Martin d. Castor Troy with the Shoulderbreaker. (5:13) Unification Match for the EWA World Tag Team Championship FlashBack (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) v. The Reckoning (EWA World Tag Team Champions) The Reckoning didn't even show up for the match, and FlashBack won the unifed titles by forfeit. FlashBack d. The Reckoning by forfeit. FlashBack are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions!! Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) This match ended in a double disqualification. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Deathstar (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Morgue) This was an outstanding match, as Michaels and Deathstar were both on the top of their game. However, it ended when Deathstar made the mistake of turning his back on Michaels to get the crowd into the match, and when he turned around, Michaels met him with the Sweet Chin Music, and pinned him. Afterward, Steve Austin ran to the ring after Michaels, but Michaels bailed, and took his I-C and Austin's TV title with him. Shawn Michaels d. Deathstar with the Sweet Chin Music. (12:43) Shawn Michaels is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion!! EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match Special Guest Referee: "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) This was possibly the most outstanding match in EWA history, as it lasted an amazing 42 minutes. Let's take you to the play-by-play of the end of the match. Jim Ross: And now Prodigy's got Kane...Firestarter!! He hit the Firestarter!! Here's a cover!! 1, 2, OH MY GOD!! Kane just kicked out of the Firestarter!! Larry Zybysko: I've never seen anyone do that! Jim Ross: Kane sits up! And now he's got Prodigy by the throat! Larry Zybysko: Here comes the Chokeslam, and you know what's after this! Jim Ross: And yes, Kane's calling for the Tombstone! Picks him up, and down! 1, 2, 3!! Kane wins!! Larry Zybysko: No, he didn't! Look! Ring Announcer Howard Finkle: Ladies and gentlemen, I have just been informed by referee Nick Patrick that Prodigy's foot was on the ropes! Therefore, this match will continue!! Jim Ross: And Kane is not happy at all, as Paul Bearer is signaling for him to chokeslam Patrick! Larry Zybysko: If Nick's smart, he'll get out of Dodge! Jim Ross: And Kane chokeslams Patrick! Comissioner Hart is at ringside, and he's calling for Kane to be disqualified. Larry Zybysko: Prodigy's just now getting u...LOOK!! *The camera pans upward, and we see two objects falling from the ceiling, attacked to a rope. Once they reach the ring, they stop, elevated around five feet up from the mat. The camera focuses on them, and we see that they are effigies of Kane and Prodigy.* Jim Ross: Those are effigies of Prodigy and the EWA Champion Kane! And now Prodigy's looking them over, as he steps up to his...and IT JUST LIT ON FIRE!! KANE'S EFFIGY AND PRODIGY'S EFFIGY ARE ON FIRE!! ON MY GOODNESS!! Larry Zybysko: What is this all about? Jim Ross: I don't know, Larry, but we're out of time!! So long, folks! Prodigy d. Kane by disqualification. (42:11) Kane retains the EWA World Heavyweight Championship.
