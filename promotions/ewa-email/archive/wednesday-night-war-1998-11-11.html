EWA Wednesday Night War

11 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 11, 1998 Live, from the Roanoke Civic Center Roanoke, Virginia (The opening sequence of WNW airs, and afterwards the camera pans over the jam packed Civic Center. The camera then switches to Jim Ross and Vincent Ashe.) JR: Welcome everyone to WEDNESDAY NIGHT WARZONE!!! Tonight we have a jam packed card, as tonight, Intercontinental Title is on the line in a Triple Threat match Vince! VA: That's right Jim!!! Tonight, for aaaall the marbles, The EWA Intercontinental Champion, “The Franchise” Jeff Bunda, goes one on one on one against The Cornerstone of DX, Triple H, and that mosted hated man in the EWA and core member of the EWA’s new dominant stable, The BMF, “The Extreme Superstar” Venom! It’ll be one hell of a match!!! JR: Also, we have the start of the EWA’s European Title tournament, and this is not your run of the mill tournament, it’s a pool style tournament, and the competition will be fast and furious! And, in our Main Event, The World Heavyweight Champion, Erik Draven, will take on the man who betrayed him at November 2 Remember, Crazy Canuck! Lets get to the ring, for our first match up!!!! I-…wait fans..some quick news here…President Gates is not in attendance tonight! The significance of this is that Mr. Gates is NEVER absent from a live EWA Televised Event? What will this mean? I don’t know, but we have our first match about to begin! Take it away Howard Finkle! James Mastin v. Edge In a great match for Edge, the EWA’s Enigma makes quick work of James Mastin, as Edge, after technically out wrestling Mastin, finishes the poor jabronie off with the Downward Spiral. Edge wins via The Downward Spiral. Joe Lemon v. The Godfather (w/ the "ho's") Lemon was offered the Godfather’s ho’s, and Lemon, not having had a piece of tail in three years, eagerly accepted and rushed to the back with his new sex toys. The Godfather wins via forfeit. Matches in European Championship Pool (see front page for details) Will "The Thrill" Haynes (0 points thus far) v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (0 points thus far) In a highly contested match up, Haynes and KG battled for 15 minutes. Haynes had an early advantage at first, using his aerial skill to bewilder The Outlaw Immortal. At one point, Haynes executed an awesome corkscrew plancha onto KG on the outside, making KG smash his wrist on the guardrail. But, 10 minutes into the match, swollen wrist and all, KG, using a variety of weardown holds and submission holds, managed to tire out Haynes, and he defeats the junkie with a small package. KG d. Will Haynes via a small package. KG gains 5 points, Haynes gains 1. "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (0 points thus far) v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (0 points thus far) Taz was nowhere to be found at this juncture of the evening, so Rob Van Dam was awarded the win. RVD wins via forfeit. RVD receives 5 points, Taz receives 0. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (0 points thus far) v. Shawn Copeland, the EWA's Pocket Change is going to be laid out on the mat and the man who puts him out, Prodigy, is going to be the one standing over you. Now you have been challenged Copeland. Can you live up to it? (Prodigy throws the microphone down and it hits Copeland square in the head. Prodigy makes his way to the backstage to retrieve his "friend") Prodigy (0 points thus far) v. Ruger Ice (0 points thus far) In the best match of the tournament so far, Prodigy and Ruger Ice put on one hell of a match. Ruger took control in the beginning by blindsiding Prodigy when he climbed through the ring ropes. He began to use brute strength to wear Prodigy down. Ice than began to mount Prodigy on the tope rope, in an attempt of a Superplex, but prodigy reversed the hold, and superplexed Ruger Ice to the hard concrete floor. Lady Jane looked on in fear, as the back of Ice’s head slapped the cold hard floor like a dirty old man slapping the young and tender ass of his hot next door neighbor. Prodigy then crotched Ice on the guardrail, and in one of the insanest moves ever seen in the EWA, Prodigy dove off of the tope rope and clotheslined Ruger off of the Guardrail, into the crowd!! The referee managed to get the match back into the ring, where Prodigy took complete control. Prodigy used move after move, but the man known as Ruger Ice would not give in. Ice kept kicking out of every pin Prodigy attempted on him. Prodigy was about to use the Firestarter on Ruger Ice, when Ice had the instinct enough to escape the hold and deliver a brutal lariat to Prodigy. The men then battled on for 10 more minutes, and then the match was declared a time limit draw, much to the digust of the fans and the wrestlers. Ruger Ice and Prodigy battle to a time limit draw. Each wrestler receives two points. ****As "Their Law" begins to play again, Prodigy stands up and begins to slowly make his way to the aisle way. Suddenly, Shawn Copeland, visably injured from his earlier beating, jumps from the crowd and into the ring. Prodigy turns around to see what caused the noise, and is greeted by a brick directly in the face...courtesy of "The Money-Maker" himself. Copeland steps over the knocked out body of Prodigy and grabs a mic...**** Copeland:" *In mock suprise* Ohgee-golly. Wherever could Prodigy's large menacing BMF be?" ****We pan to the JumbroTron, which shows the BMF locker room,chained and locked from the outside. The door is shaking violently, as if someone is trying to get out...**** Copeland:"Yeah, yeah, yeah. I know the whole situation is tired, but oh what the hell...it works, doesn't it? Prodigy...I don't give a damn what you say or think. All I know, is that I am here to check your ego, and that's exactly what I just did. Scoff? I couldn't care less. Whenever you're ready, son...you know where to find me. In the meanwhile...be sure to heal that nose up...from the looks of things, it's broken in several places. Hit the music." ****With that, Copeland leaves the ring to a chorus of boos...**** El Locco Jobbo (0 points thus far) v. Thorne Richards (0 points thus far) Both men were nowhere to be found, and thus, this match was a no show. Both wrestlers receive No points. "The Natural One" Michael Modest (0 points thus far) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (0 points thus far) In a brutal match, Modest and Hicks went back and forth, hitting move after move on each other. Suddenly from out of the crowd, Stevie Richards and Crazy Canuck, out of retaliation for what had happened to Prodigy, ran in the ring and pummeled Hicks with baseball bats. Stevie took a swing for the fences, as he gave Hicks a direct blow to the mouth with the baseball bat. Afterwards, Canuck and Stevie looked at Modest, and then left the ring. Hicks, bleeding like a stuck pig, was attended to by paramedics. Hicks d. Modest via DQ. Hicks receives 5 points, Modest receives 0 points. STANDINGS FOR THE EURO TOURNAMENT, ROUND 1 Pool A: 1. Ruger Ice: 2 points 3. Taz and Modest: 0 Deader v. Mick Foley In a match that lasted a few minutes, Mick Foley defeated Deader with a Double armed DDT…on a chair. Foley d. Deader via the Double Armed DDT. EWA World Television Championship Match Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. Gangrel (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Christian and Devon) In a match that had fans astounded, Gangrel and Galaxy battled their hearts out. In the closing moments, Devon leaped on the ring apron, trying to distract the referee. Galaxy then dropkicked Gangrel into Devon, making her hit the floor, HARD. Christian crept into the ring, and then used and waffled Galaxy with the Goblet. Gangrel made the cover, and took a tainted victory. Gangrel d. Galaxy via the Goblet to the face. Non-Title Match The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ The BMF) As soon as Richards came into the ring, The Golden One and The King of Swing began to brawl. A thumb to the eye by The Golden One on Stevie gave the Shining Star the advantage. After a few high impact moves, the Golden One slapped a reverse chinlock on Stevie. Stevie began to fade into the black sea of unconsciousness, and The Golden One demanded that The referee check Stevie. The Referee raised Stevie’s arm two times and it dropped like a sack of bricks. On the third raise, Stevie held his arm up and began to shake wildly, as the crowd erupted. He managed to get to his feet and delivered three brutal elbows into the stomach of the Golden One. The Golden One released the hold, and was then hit with a sweet standing dropkick by Richards. Richards then yelled “Ariba!” and nailed the Golden one with a brutal flying forearm. Richards went for the pin, but the Golden One kicked out at 2.5 seconds. The Golden One attempted a quick lariat, but ended up knocking out the referee. Stevie suplexed the shining star, and set him up for the Stevie Kick. Suddenly, Shawn Copeland and Jeff Bunda rushed out from the back, and began to pummel the two wrestlers in the ring. Venom and Crazy Canuck, with chairs in hand, came down and began to battle with the two triple threat members. The Golden One used a high knee lift to take down Jeff Bunda, and he than began to beat Bunda brutally with his fists. Copeland then dropkicked the Golden One in the face, sending him flying to the outside. After the brawl was cleared away, EWA officials declared the match a draw. Golden One and Stevie Richards battle to a draw in a highly contested match up. Triple Threat Match For the EWA Intercontinental Championship "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (w/ The BMF) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) After the introductions took place, The Franchise began talking smack to his opponents. HHH shoved Bunda, Bunda shoved him back, and the two men began to brawl, while Venom put the badmouth to both of them. HHH managed to get a quick roll up, which then caused Venom to make the save by drop kicking HHH in the back of his head. Venom then began to work on the neck of Triple H, when Bunda came in and speared Venom, making The Extreme SuperStar smash his head against the turnbuckle. Bunda then irish whipped Venom into the corner, making his back smash against the turnbuckle. Venom then retaliated with a clothesline that nearly took Bunda’s head off. Venom then elbow dropped Bunda 3 times, only to be blind sided by HHH with a rake to the eye. HHH managed to take full control of the match, keeping both men off of their feet. Triple H power bombed Venom onto The Franchise at one point, but the blow hurt the Franchise more than it did Venom. Venom regained the advantage by delivering a testicle twisting low blow to The Cornerstone, making him double over in pain. Venom, out of an act of desperation, attempted the Extreme Poison, only to be chopped blocked by Bunda. This in turn sent HHH flying, landing hard on his shoulder, injuring it. The Franchise then began to stomp viscously at Venom, to the approval of the crowd. HHH, with one arm to use, forearmed Bunda in the face, making the Franchise drop down to one Knee. HHH rebounded into the ropes and executed a cross body splash, and attempted a pin on Bunda, but The Franchise kicked out. Venom retreated to the outside, where he was the greeted by the fists of Shawn Copeland. Venom, enraged, grabbed Copeland and Irish whipped him into the steel steps. Venom, taking a chair, brutalized The Money Maker with it, leaving him a bloody mess on the outside. Venom then turned his attention into the action in the ring, where Bunda had taken control. The Referee had his back turned, and as the Franchise rebounded against the ropes, Venom blasted the back of his skull with the steel chair. Venom then hopped in the ring, and delivered a spinning neckbreaker on Bunda, leaving him flat on the canvas. HHH delivered a elbow to The Extreme Superstar, and then set Venom up for the Pedigree. Venom, then backbody dropped HHH out of the ring and onto the hard concrete Floor. Venom, woozy from the elbow, picked Bunda up, and executed The Extreme Poison on him, to the roar of the crowd. He made the cover, and defeated Jeff Bunda for the Intercontinental Title. Venom d. Jeff Bunda via The Extreme Poison. Venom is the new Intercontinental Champion!
