EWA (2 Jan 1998) Card Archive

EWA Saturday Night Slam

22 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The Rosemont Horizon Center Chicago, Illinois Non-Title Match Vic Deakins v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) (The lights are low, the crowd is hot, and the music is blaring as 'Perfect Strangers' hits the PA - a song which hasn't been played in the EWA for a couple of weeks. Well, Jeff Bunda, carrying both WAA and EWA World Heavyweight Championships, comes out to the ring, alone, and takes a microphone.) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda: Well, it seems that the million-dollar question on the street as of late is the question of the Franchise's future here in the EWA. Well, I've been looking around for a while, because it's no secret that the Franchise has entertained offers from other promotions - bigger contracts, bigger salaries, less work schedule, exclusivity contracts, the whole nine yards. Sure, I've almost been lured away from the EWA - it sure could have been an attractive option. But instead, I've made my decision - I'm going to stay. That's right - all you lowly-ass mid-card jibronis who thought that they were going to get a shot at the EWA Heavyweight Championship of the World just because the Franchise wasn't going to be around, well, then you'd better get ready to live in my shadow for the rest of your lives - because you're NOT taking my EWA World Title away from me. I already gave one belt to you all, after one-hundred and twenty-five days of holding it - you all KNOW who the real EWA Intercontinental Champion is. And on that subject, there's a little tournament going on for that belt. Well, I have a little challenge to make - the winner of that tournament, the next EWA Intercontinental Champion - that's our Number One Contender right there for ya. So, Gates, if you have the brains, the intuition, or the common sense god gave Serpent Man, you'll sign the match. Every one knows no matter who wins the Jeff Bunda Invitational, they will always pale in comparison to me - for two reasons. One - because I'm a seven-time World Champion, and I was the Intercontinental Champion for one-hundred twenty five days. And secondly - I RUN THIS SHOW!!! And I always will. (Bunda throws down the microphone as 'Perfect Strangers' blares and he leaves the ring.) First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Mayne Mann v. Furnace Furnace was, for lack of a better term, cold tonight. Mayne Mann seemed to return to old form tonight, as he virtually destroyed the newcomer, finishing him off with the Boston Bomb. Mayne Mann d. Furnace with the Boston Bomb. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Ruger Ice v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The PHC) {As “Flirtin with Disaster” blares over the PA Ruger Ice comes through the curtain and makes his way to the ring. He grabs the microphone from the announcer and turns and looks at Crazy Canuck standing in the corner of the ring.} RUGER: Canuck I know you have been waiting anxiously to see what kind of measures I have taken. You were so sure that I went out into the EWA and found someone to back me up. You were so sure that I was not going to do this one on my own. You were so sure that your little puppet show would shake me. Well I have three things for you tonight Canuck. First: Here is some Tide {Ruger tosses a small box of tide at Crazy Canuck} Use it to wash your socks because they really need it. Second: I told you I would take measures to ensure that it was just you and me in this ring and I meant it. Now I told you that I wouldn’t bring any other wrestlers down here because that would make me a hypocrite. But since Chicago is so close to home I thought I would invite a few friends to come down to ringside and watch the match up close. So ladies and gentlemen without further delay I present to you my friends….The Stanley Cup Champion Detroit Redwings. {Flirtin With Disaster blares over the PA as the Detroit Redwings come running down the isle fully geared up including hockey sticks and surround the ring. Crazy Canuck is standing in the ring with a totally confused look on his face not knowing what to make of the situation.} Third: The third and final thing I have brought you tonight Canuck is something that you have been asking for a whole lot lately. It is a serious @ss whooping. {Ruger drops the microphone and attacks Crazy Canuck} This was a very good contest, with a lot of nearfalls. Ruger Ice dominated the opening portion of the match, but Canuck gained the advantage by a distraction from Jack Reed, who had accompanied him to ringside. From there, Canuck pretty much controlled the match, save for a quick minute or two, defeating Ruger with the Lunatic Bomb. Crazy Canuck d. Ruger Ice with the Lunatic Bomb. Joe Lemon v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia The Rock was in total control for this match, as he tore Lemon from limb to limb, ending it with the Rock Bottom. The Rock d. Joe Lemon with the Rock Bottom First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Deader v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom Deader looked very impressive in this match, as he recieved a huge pop from the crowd. Venom was greeted with an amazing chorus of boos, but still put on a fine match. The two fought all over the ringside area, until eventually, Venom wore Deader down due to the brutality of the match, and finished him with the Extreme Poison. Venom d. Deader with Extreme Poison. *Just before the ring announcer can announce the next match, a commotion is heard and seen in the crowd. Then a man and woman jump the security barrier and hit the ring. Immediately the fans recognize them, Galaxy & Precious have returned to the EWA. Galaxy grabs the microphone from the ring announcer and Precious chases him out of the ring.* Galaxy: We're baaaccccckkkk! (Crowd instantly begins to pelt the ring with debris) Things haven’t changed a bit have they, you still love me. But when you will you people learn that we couldn’t give a hell about you or anyone else for that matter. We have come to reclaim what is our rightful place in society that being in the spotlight as the top wrestler/manager combo in the EWA (crowd boos louder with some mixed laughter) This is no longer a business to me; no longer about beating this guy or not beating this guy because I don’t give a heck if I cripple the guy or end his career. This time the higher ups will not hold me down, the JOBS stop here, the EWA doesn’t realize what it has done by bringing me back, a whole case full of ‘Pandora’s box’ has been opened and will be released upon the EWA. Precious: (before she can begin, she gets a warmer reception with many whistlers) IN YOUR DREAMS LOSERS. This is where it all begins mark the day on your calendars, no more jobs no more $hit, it ends right here right now if you like or not. Get use to it people your going to be seeing a lot more of us in the future and you better learn to like it. (They then leave as quickly as they arrived through the crowd, with a ‘buzz’ going around the crowd) First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. Thorne Richards [When we expect to hear Thorne Richards' music, instead we hear the opening bass notes of a tune called "The Natural One" by Ronnie & Clyde. Once the lyrics "I'm the one, the natural one, take it easy...", a Caucasian man wearing faded blue jeans and black Doc Martens comes out. His blond hair is slicked back and he makes his way into the ring and grabs the mic out of the ring announcer's hand. Why, it's none other than "The Natural One" Michael Modest!] Listen up people! I know you've been bored out of your minds with these egotistical bastards that think their [censored] smells like roses. I know you're all tired of seeing the SAME OLD CRAP OVER AND OVER AGAIN. We've had our share of guys who ramble on for days and days about the SAME DAMN STUFF EVERY TIME. And frankly, it makes me sick! You've seen me come out before with Val Venis and Van Smack, but now, since I've found a new employer that's hired me to toughen him up and bring the EWA on it's G*DDAMN EAR! The Natural One isn't here to be another EWA wrestler. No way. I'm here to re-invent the damn term! Whether it's me or my boss, we're gonna' be stacking bodies in every EWA arena we set foot in, and KG... You're first to finish dead last against my new sidekick, Thorne Richards! But then again... What else is new? *crowd laughs* Hit the music! [With that, "The Final Countdown" by Europe plays, and Thorne Richards comes out slapping fives with the fans, carrying a brown paper bag to the ring. Once he gets in, Modest tosses him the mic.] KG, before you get the beating you deserve, let me just try to make this match something productive for you, and I don't mean by having myself lose to a cretin like you. I mean, look at your scrawny body. You're nothing but skin and bones, lad! *to the fans* I mean, doesn't he look more like a Crack Baby than a Mack Daddy? *the crowd pops and laughs* At any rate, I come bearing gifts for you. Yes, I am a generous man, despite my hatred for low-lifes as yourself... *opens up the bag and pulls out a bottle of BEEFCAKE 4000* Here's a bit of BEEFCAKE to put some meat on those bones of yours. Feel free to mix this into your diet and you too can accomplish your dreams, even if it means imitating others! [Modest then pulls out a bottle of Red Bull.] And here kG, which they probably named you since you're about a heavy as a damn KiloGram, crack addict... This stuff'll give you all the stamina you've been lacking all this time. In fact, it's already in liquid form, so you can still take this after Thorne kicks all your teeth down your throat! C'mon wimpy... Drink it, b*tch! KG hesitated, then took a drink, and proceeded to spit the Red Bull in Thorne's face. This enticed Thorne, who snapped, and tackled KG. From there, this was a close contest from bell to bell. The two veterans of the sport exchanged blows back and forth. After minutes of heart-stopping action, everything went downhill, as "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels appeared at ringside. Thorne whipped KG into the ropes, and Michaels reached out and tripped KG, causing him to fall hard. KG whipped around, and starting yelling at Michaels, enabling Thorne to come up from behind and execute an old move of his, the Double Chickenwing, for the submission victory. Afterward, KG chased Michaels back to the locker room. Thorne Richards d. KG by submission with the Double Chickenwing. Public Enemy (w/ D-Generation X) v. Intoxication-X One word: annihilation. Intoxication-X looked more than stoned tonight...they looked down-right dead. TPE pretty much slaughtered the two in record time. The Public Enemy d. Intoxication-X with Ghetto Boy's pin on Will Haynes after The Drive-By. The Gunslinger v. New Jack (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Obviously, The Gunslinger wasn't showing up, as evident from his interview the night before. As New Jack stood in the ring while the referee counted to 10, something happened. A man comes from the crowd with a chair, and nails New Jack in the back of the head. It's KG. KG then sets New Jack on the top rope an hits him with the BlackOut, and one final blow...hits him with a corkscrew leg drop. The Ghetto Warriors come out but it's too late...the damage has been done. New Jack d. The Gunslinger by forfeit. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Darkness (w/ Razor) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Darkness put up a solid fight against the three-time EWA World Tag Team Champions, but eventually, the heat overwhelmed them, and it wasn't enough, as Harlem Heat secured another successful title defense. Harlem Heat d. Darkness with Stevie Ray's pin on Lucien after the FlapJack. Harlem Heat is still EWA World Tag Team Champions. James Mastin v. "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ D-Generation X) This match was a typical James Mastin contest. James Mastin was no competition for the up and coming Conklin. Mastin was ripped apart by the mighty Conklin, who was able to hit his Overhand Right and pin Mastin. Terry Conklin d. James Mastin with the Overhand Right. Jack Reed (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) v. Prodigy Judging from the level of volume in the crowd during this match, it was one of the most eagerly anticipated contests of the night. A see-saw match took place between these two, as for the first ten minutes, neither man could pick up an edge. This ended when Prodigy, being the opportune veteran he is, executed a vicious low blow on Reed. At this point, President Gates, who had accompanied Reed to ringside, actually tried to climb in the ring. Prodigy snapped his head up and stared at Gates, and to the sheer delight of the crowd, nailed him with a hard right, sending him sprawling to the outside. From there, Prodigy hit the Firestarter on Reed, and got the three count. As Prodigy exited the ring, he tried to regain Skippy from the Prodigy Hate Club, only to be blind-sided with the ring bell by Crazy Canuck. Prodigy d. Jack Reed with the Firestarter. Non-Title Match Haywood Jublome (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) In the biggest match thus far in Haywood Jublome's career, he came up huge, dominating Justin Hicks in an impressive showing. It had been announced before this that Hicks was competing in spite of a 103 degree temperature, and Jublome took full advantage of this. Haywood Jublome d. Justin Hicks with the Ho Checka'. EWA European Championship Match Chris Craft v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Shawn Copeland came to the ring looking almost bored, and seeming not to care about the match. Despite being tired, and obviously not caring one way or another, he was still able to quite easily dispose of our beloved Chris Craft with The Money Shot. Shawn Copeland d. Chris Craft with The Money Shot. Shawn Copeland is still EWA European Champion. EWA North American Championship Match Joe Satre (w/ The PHC) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA North American Champion, w/ D-Generation X) This was possibly the match of the night. Although Draven out-weighed Satre by nearly 100 pounds, Satre still was determined to promote himself to ICON Slayer for the night. And he did a fine job of it, putting on a hell of a tremendous match. Draven had him in the grasp of The Hangman's Joke at one point, but Satre was able to reach the ropes and thwart the submission attempt. Satre was able to then take advantage, going to the top rope to attempt the Pimpin' Splash. Beulah quietly handed Draven a chair, though, and Draven was able to blast Satre with it once. Draven then played to the crowd, and turned to beat on Satre some more, but Satre had slid to the outside, and grabbed Draven's North American Championship belt. Satre quickly ran to the back, stealing The ICON's title belt. *Draven is standing in the ring alone, and suddenly the lights go out. Suddenly a massive pyrotechnics display erupts from the floor from the entrance way. Then the lights come up.* JR: You know what that means! The Big Red Machine is here, that monster Kane. And THERE HE IS!!! He's all ready in the ring behind Erik Draven. Draven doesn't see him. Kane is raising his arms into the air and OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHH!!!!!! DEAR LORD!!! And now Draven turns around and sees Kane! And...*The lights go out again* What the? Someone turn on the damn lights! I can't see a thing. Draven had just spotted Kane behind him after Kane caused the ringposts to jump with fire, and then...*The lights come back up* Kane is gone! Draven is standing in the ring alone again. Kane is playing mind games with Erik Draven! *From up above the ring a casket is slowly lowered down into the ring. It lands, and the chords return to the ceiling. Draven goes and opens up the casket expecting to see Kane...* JR: Draven just opened that casket and jumps back...can we see what's in there? And...it's Erik Draven!!! There's a clone of Erik Draven in that casket!!! And...Kane is up on the entrance way! Draven turns around and spots Kane once again, as Kane raises one hand. And OH NO!!! A lightning bolt just struck the casket, and it's on FIRE!!! Draven's image is on fire!!! Kane has something in his hand that he's holding to his throat. Kane: Erik Draven...in three weeks, it will be you returning straight into hell!!! *Kane's music blares over the speakers, as he turns around and walks out of the entrance way and leaves Draven to watch his mirror image burn in the ring.* Joe Satre d. Erik Draven by disqualification. Erik Draven is still EWA North American Champion. The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) From the moment SmirtDogg came to the ring, it was obvious he was in no shape to wrestle. He came to the ring with a 40, staggering the entire way there, and appeared to be very drunk. He gave The Golden One a decent match for the first few minutes, but it was all downhill for Smirt from there, as The Golden One notched an impressive victory. (*As the winner is being announced 'Lookin At Me' by Mase begins to play over the speakers and out walks Mayne Mann. Both Smirtdogg and The Golden One wonder what he's doing here. Mayne walks down to the ring to some cheers and steps through the ropes. We can see he has a microphone.*) Mayne: Golden One, we've had our ups and downs, mostly ups. Right now it's up... (*Both grin as Mayne looks at Smirtdogg. Suddenly Mayne nails Golden One across the forehead with the microphone. He begins to pound away on him with hard kicks. Smirtdogg just stands there laughing.*) Mayne: But now it's a down, because the millions you gave me to leave the match Wednesday wasn't enough. I laughed when you kicked Copelands ass, but it didn't mean much to me. So he got you, you got him, and now I'm getting you. (*With that Mayne puts on his version of the Crippler Crossface on him. Golden One passes out as the cut from the microphone on his forehead bleed profusely.*) Mayne: Let that be a warning to Joe 'Insert New Nickname Here' Satre cause at WrestleCade I he's next! (*Smirtdogg continues laughing at the knocked out Golden One as Mayne walks away. Suddenly Mayne serves around and nails Smirtdogg in the forehead with the microphone. We can hear Jim Ross going 'Good Lord!' in the background. Mayne begins to kick him too.*) Mayne: Don't think I forgot you Smirtdogg. Yep I remember that DDT you hit me with when the Dream Team kicked me out! This is payback! (*Mayne puts his version of the Crippler Crossface on Smirtdogg as well. Smirtdogg slams his hand up and down on the mat waiting for security to come break up the carnage. Mayne breaks the hold when he sees the Ghetto Warriors running down the aisle to make the save. Mayne jumps over the railing ringside and makes his escape through the crowd as the Ghetto Warriors attend to the fallen Smirtdogg, although New Jack feels the urge to beat up Golden One for now reason until the Triple Threat can be seen coming out of the entrance way as the show goes off the air.*) The Golden One d. SmirtDogg with the Golden Edge. (Thanks to Kris Gaffney and Brendan Holness for helping out with the results.)
