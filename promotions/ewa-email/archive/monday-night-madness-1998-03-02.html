EWA Monday Night Madness

2 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 2, 1998 Live, from the Ocean Center in Daytona Beach, Florida. Live from the Ocean Center in Daytona Beach, Florida EWA European Championship Match Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. KG (w/ Sunny) Julius (w/ The Empire) v. Bill Goldberg Booker T (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Thug Warriors) v. The Pessimist (w/ The Gathering) Paul Burnett v. Vic Deakins Chris "The Rock" Gardner v. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Thug Warriors) Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Hannibal Manson EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Golden Child v. Augustus (w/ The Empire) The Reckoning (w/ The Pessimist) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Raven (w/ Jake Roberts) The Gunslinger (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) EWA World Television Championship Match El Locco Jobbo v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Eclipse v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Hollywood" Barry Horowitz Jr. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ Katie) Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. The Widow Maker Tiberus/Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/"The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Non-Title Match El Tigre v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) EWA European Championship Match Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. KG (w/ Sunny) Our opening match wasn't much of one, as Wright seemed waaaay off of his game. After it was all said and done, the Stud Stable once again had gold. KG d. Alex Wright with the Six Shooter. (3:56) KG is the first ever EWA European Champion!! Julius (w/ The Empire) v. Bill Goldberg Julius didn't do much at all, as this match was dominated by Goldberg. Bill Goldberg d. Julius with the Jackhammer. (0:47) Booker T (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Thug Warriors) v. The Pessimist (w/ The Gathering) This was a terrific matchup, as both men were determined to come out on top. The Pessimist looked especially impressive, as with WormBoy in his corner, he seemed to be extremely motivated. And WormBoy played a role in the result of the match, nailing Booker T with a chair, and then escaping through the crowd before any of the Thug Warriors could get to him. The Pessimist d. Booker T with outside interference. (11:02) Paul Burnett v. Vic Deakins This was total domination by Vic Deakins, as Burnett looked way off target. Vic Deakins d. Paul Burnett with the EMP. (2:13) Chris "The Rock" Gardner v. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Thug Warriors) Chris Gardner didn't show up for this one, and it was announced that he and Joshua Gardner had left the EWA. Ahmed Johnson d. Chris Gardner by forfeit. Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Hannibal Manson This match was full of agression, as both Nero and Hannibal wasted no time in turning it into a brawl. The fight spilled over to the outside, and both men were counted out. A rematch has been signed for TNT. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Golden Child v. Augustus (w/ The Empire) Augustus didn't even show up, as apparently he was too drunk. But the interesting thing happened afterward, when TGC was handed the U.S. Title.... "Are you ready??" Shawn Michaels made his way to ringside, and told TGC that DX wanted an answer on whether or not he was joining DX. GC stared his former partner down for around 30 seconds, and then shook his hand. The crowd exploded in a chorus of boos, and the two men gave numerous "X-chops" on their way back to the locker room. The Golden Child is the new EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion!! The Reckoning (w/ The Pessimist) v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) This was a solid tag team match, but in the end, The Reckoning pulled off a spectacular victory. Pain had attempted a powerbomb, but Leon flipped out of it, and hit Pain with an enzugiri, knocking him out cold. The Reckoning d. Death Sentance with Leon Snow's pin on Pain after an enzugiri. (12:29) FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) Bloodsport once again dominated this match...most fans took this opportunity to go out to the concession stand. Bloodsport d. FlashBack with Mortis' pin on Disco Inferno after the Flatliner. (6:11) "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Raven (w/ Jake Roberts) Raven did not even show up for this match, prompting DX to search the rest of the night for him, Roberts, and The Gunslinger. The trio never were found...and the mind games continue. HHH d. Raven by forfeit. The Gunslinger (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) Kevin Nash d. The Gunslinger by forfeit. EWA World Television Championship Match El Locco Jobbo v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Jobbo lasted all of 12 seconds, as Austin whipped him into the ropes, kicked him in the stomach, gave him the Stone Cold Stunner, and called it a night. Steve Austin d. El Locco Jobbo with the Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin retains the EWA Television Championship. Eclipse v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia The Rock did not show up for this match, prompting President Gates to fire him. Eclipse d. Rocky Maivia by forfeit. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Hollywood" Barry Horowitz Jr. Another no-show.... Galaxy d. Barry Horowitz Jr. by forfeit. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ Katie) Gardners have left the EWA.... Deathstar d. Joshua Gardner by forfeit. Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. The Widow Maker Finally, a match in which both men showed up! :-) However, Octavius and Julius made a shocking turn when they attacked Nero after the match! They then announced that due to the "bulls*it" going on within the Empire, they were leaving the EWA. The Widow Maker d. Octavius with a powerbomb. (4:14) Tiberus/Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/"The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Tiberus and Anthony no-showed, and were promptly fired. Michaels and Dravenget the win. Non-Title Match El Tigre v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) This was a terrific match, as these two men showed why the Lightweight division is so great. Both men provided numerous high-flying and technical moves. Le Tigre even hit his finisher, The Tiger Bite, (A German suplex), but SmirtDogg's feet were in the ropes. This seemed to throw Le Tigre off of his game, as when he turned around, SmirtDogg nailed him with a superkick. After the match, Le Tigre challenged Smirt to a rematch for the title, and we went off the air before Smirt gave his answer. SmirtDogg d. Le Tigre with a superkick. (14:35)
