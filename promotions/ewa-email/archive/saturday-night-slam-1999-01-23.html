EWA Saturday Night Slam

23 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Saturday Night Slam (1/23/99) January 23, 1999 Live, from Freeman Coliseum San Antonio, Texas Joe Lemon v. The Undertaker (w/ The Ministry Of Darkness) Saturday Night Slam opened with a bang, as the Undertaker made his EWA debut. The debut was short and to the point, however, as Lemon never stood any sort of chance. Two vertical suplexes, a big boot, a chokeslam, and the Tombstone Piledriver later, The Undertaker stood victorious. The Undertaker d. Joe Lemon with the Tombstone Piledriver. Philip Donovan v. Justin Credible PhD got his PhD tonight in hardcore wrestling, as Credible really pounded him into oblivion. Credible looked very impressive, and dominated the match from bell to bell, finishing Donovan off with 'That's Incredible'. Justin Credible d. Philip Donovan with That's Incredible. (After the match, as Credible starts to leave the ring, the MegaTron above the entrance ramp flickers to life, and we see the face of "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam.) "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam: Credible, you b*tch, you want to challenge Mr. Monday Night to a Hardcore Rules Career match next week, at Clash Of The Champions X? I'll say it plain and simple, Credible...you bring your ass, and Rob Van Dam will be there to kick it out of the EWA for good! Why? Because, plain and simply, I am THE WHOLE F*CKING SHOW. 1999 King Of The Ring Tournament Qualifying Match Paolo di Canio v. Swap Meet Louie (w/ Ghetto Boy & Grady Smith) Finally, we had ourselves a competitive match. Canio was able to hold an early advantage, actually overpowering the larger Louie. However, the crowd was quick to get behind Louie, and this pumped him up, as he began fighting back. Louie gained control with a big belly-to-belly suplex. Louie went for a Death Valley Driver, but di Canio slipped out of it, and executed a reverse DDT onto Louie. di Canio then picked Louie up, and set him up for a powerbomb...however, Louie reversed it into a spinebuster. Louie then picked up di Canio, this time hitting the DVD, and went for the pin. The referee made the three count, and Swap Meet Louie is in the King Of The Ring. Swap Meet Louie d. Paolo di Canio with a Death Valley Driver. Chris Craft v. "The Real Athlete" Sid Jacobs Can we say squash? I knew you could! Craft never had a chance, as Jacobs took al of 45 seconds in destroying Craft, polishing him off with the Final Warning. Sid Jacobs d. Chris Craft with the Final Warning. "The God Of Violence" Johnny Napalm (w/ Angel) v. Max Pins Napalm wasn't very impressive at all in this contest, as Max Pins pretty much controlled it. Pins got a few bursts of momentum in, but other than that, Pins was the clear-cut victor, and got a win after the 7-10 Split. Max Pins d. Johnny Napalm after the 7-10 Split. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Aronalle Ruger started off in top form against the newcomer, as he dominated him in the corner with a series of rights and lefts. More of the same followed, as Ruger systematically destroyed Aronalle in impressive fashion, coming out victorious. Ruger Ice d. Aronalle with the Spinebuster. "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. Jack Reed (w/ The Corporate Empire) Yet another squash on an unimpressive showing of Saturday Night Slam, as the Corporate Reed pretty much dismantled any offense Modest tried to build. Reed didn't take long to finish Modest off by using the top rope Frankensteiner. Jack Reed d. Michael Modest with the Top Rope Frankensteiner. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels & Haywood Jublome (w/ D-Generation X) v. Two Smooth (w/ The Triple Threat) Now, we get to some excitement. Jublome and Conklin started off. Both men were tentative at first, until Conklin made the first move, with a kick to the lower stomach. Conklin immediately went for the Overhand Right, but Jublome dodged it, and slapped Conklin right in his face. Conklin hit the ropes, but Jublome caught him into a belly-to-belly suplex. Jublome went for the cover, but it wasn't enough. He then tagged in Michaels, who immediately sprung to the top rope, and hit a double ax-handle on Conklin. Michaels drug Conklin to his feet, which allowed Conklin to nail Michaels with a low blow, and hit a facebuster. Conklin then tagged in the former 2 time Heavyweight Champion, and Copeland went to work. Copeland drug Michaels to his feet, and proceeded to take him to each turnbuckle, slamming him into all four. However, on the last one, Michaels made an unseen tag, and Jublome belted Copeland from behind. Jublome then went to work on Copeland, hitting him with a powerbomb. Jublome went for the pinning combo, but Copeland kicked out fiercely, and sent Jublome sprawling into the referee. Copeland ran at Jublome, but Jublome took Copeland down, and quickly locked on the Ho Checka. However, immediately, a masked man sprinted out from the back with a chair. The masked man took the chair, and belted Jublome across the spine wiht it. The masked man quickly blasted Michaels with the chair as well, as Conklin rolled over onto Jublome. The referee awoke, and made the three count, while the masked man unmasked at ringside, to reveal the EWA World Heavyweight Champion, SmirtDogg. Two Smooth d. D-Generation X with Shawn Copeland's pin on Haywood Jublome after interference from SmirtDogg. *** The camera fades back in, showing the excited capacityt crowd waiting for the nex match. Ring announcer Howard Finkle slides intot he ring, with a mic in hand...*** Finkle: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down the aisle, weighi... *** Before Howard could finish, a booming voice blasted over the PA...*** ??? Voice: Howie...Howie, why don't you let me take over...*Finkle is startled by the voice but gives a hands sign for "Go right ahead".* Ok...Ladies and Gentlemen, rugrats and heathens, white trash of all ages...weighing in about 200 sumpin' Hardcore pounds, The most HARDCORE, Trash talkin', pure talented man in the EWA...*E-'Nuff appears from out of the other side of the curtains.* Your First and LAST, Hardcore champion *Points to himself* of MY world...*He raises the Hardcore belt high in the air. He intakes a huge breath of air, ready to yell his name, but then he exhales, and says calmly...* ME. *** The PUNK half of the crowd starts booing, the more SMART half cheers. E-'Nuff hops into the squared circle. *** E-'Nuff: *To the booing half of the crowd.* Ahh shut the f*ck up, you all are just mad cuz' this...*Holds up the belt.* is where it belongs. Now before the next match comes out here, I though I'd enlighten you with one of my HARDCORE interviews...and since theres isn't a match with me in it...I thought you all haven't had quite "E-'Nuff" yet. The subject tonight people is MY division...The Hardcore division. It seems that I've made even more enemies, and some more victims, ever since I won what's RIGHTFULLY MINE *Taps the belt*. It seems that every d@mn Hardcore wannabe wants a piece...wel that's all well and good, if they want a shot at my belt, then why not let em' take a trip down to HARDCORE HELL and just try to take my hard earned property. That is all part of the sport right?...Well, MY sport at least. Ya know, where i let people talk trash, and talk, and talk, then I give them a chance to face me, Mr.Hardcore...and then I totally devastate them in front of alllll of you, while I walk away with what's mine. *Starts up the pace.* First, there is "The Princess of violence" Johnny Napalm. Johnny...I have beatin' your @ss over and over . I have embarassed you, and all that you stand for. Everyone knows that you ain't the "God of Violence" andymore, and NEVER was. Our last match I buried you into the ground, and I showed the EWA that you're all talk, and nothing but my personal Jobber b*tch. *Pauses* I heard about your offer Napalm...me versus you once again...January 27th in a Hardcore title match...*Shakes his head* Well even though you DON'T deserve the title shot, cuz' of your DESPICABLE display of what you call "wrestling" versus Ruger Ice...I'll give you the match anyways...Hell, it'll be tons o' fun beating the hell outta my favorite victim...Napalm, The 27th... I'll see you in HARDCORE HELL. *Paces some more.* Next...Rob Van Damn. Well Rob, you haven't proven a Van Damn thing yet in this fed, and you want to face the most Hardcore man in the EWA Already?!...Well, that's all fine and dandy with me, but you'll the fans will remember you longer if you have a longer career, and the first time you face me..well, that's the END of the "Whole f*ckin' show". I'm not going to lay out challenges or anything, cuz' that's all up to the victim...I mean challengers. So whenever you want a shot at MY title, you just give ol' gates a call, and I'll drag myself out from behinf the bars to sign the match contract to the END of your f*ckin' career. *Leans against the turnbuckles* Justin Credible...You too, along with the other idiots, want a shot at my title. *Shakes his head.* Well think it over, and if your absolutely sure you want to face me, just step in the "Victim" line, your #3. You along with Van Damn, have proven nuttin' in this federation so far...you have been Just sh*t so far...so when you reach that point where I see that your actually a CHALLENGE to me, then we'll talk about future title shots. Reed, Modest...You all need to sit down with these Van Damn and credible guys...Ya need to sit down and have a looong chat with them, you need to tell them that they are playing with the fire of Hardcore Hell, and if they're not careful...they will get burned by the Hardcore Devil himself. Jacky, Mikey...you two have experienced what it is like to face me, and you both are still licking the wounds. So before these 2 jokes decide to put their lives on the line...WARN EM'...or I'll just have to teach them! *walks to the middle of the ring.* Hardcore division...one more thing for all of ya...You have picked the wrong division to be in...I OWN it, get out while you can...or become a statistic in the "Victim System" of Hardcore Hell...'NUFF SAID. *** E-'Nuff holds the Hardcore belt up high for the crowd to marvel at, then he slides out of the ring. However, as he does, "Rock You Like A Hurricane" blares over the public address system. Stunned, E-Nuff simply stares, and the arena fills with boos as EWA President Duane Gates appears at the entrance ramp. President Duane Gates: I suppose you'd like an answer to what we discussed, E-Nuff? (E-Nuff nods) You think you have what it takes to become a Corporate Empire member? Are you sure you are willing to put yourself through the rigorous work it takes to become a member of The Corporate Empire. Then hear me out, E-Nuff. Tonight, you will be in action...although not the action you're used to. Tonight, you will step into the ring as a special referee. What match will you be refereeing? You shall officiate the match that features my EWA Heavyweight Champion SmirtDogg, and my Corporate Behemoth, Vincent Jackson, against two disgraces to wrestling, Kane, and The Rock. If I feel you've done a solid job, you're in. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Thorne Richards (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) This was one hell of an outstanding match. The Golden One started off in control with a hard kick to Thorne's stomach, followed by a swinging neckbreaker. Thorne was quicky to recover, and took The Golden One down with an armdrag. Thorne turned this into an old Code-Red type manuever, but TGO was able to reach the ropes. As TGO got to his feet, Thorne shocked almost everyone watching, by leaping up and executing a frankensteiner onto TGO. Thorne immediately went for the pin, but got only two. Thorne picked up TGO, and whipped him to the ropes. TGO came back with a hard lariat, though, which floored Thorne to the ground. TGO picked Thorne back up, and called for the Golden Edge. He set Thorne up for it, but Thorne slipped out, kicked him in the stomach, and hit a small package, only getting two. Thorne bounced to his feet, and went to the top rope. He went for a double-ax handle, but TGO caught him in a vicious bearhug, and began thrashing Richards around violently. Finally, TGO let go, and called for the Gold Medal. TGO picked him up, but Thorne was able to get in a kick to the stomach. Thorne hit the ropes, and came back...only to be caught again in the Gold Medal, this time with TGO hitting it. The Golden One went for the cover, and it was academic from there, folks. The Golden One d. Thorne Richards with the Gold Medal. The Golden One is the new EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion! Special Tag Team Grudge Match Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) & "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Corporate Empire) v. The Rock (EWA World Television Champion) & Kane First to be introduced was the special referee announced earlier in the night, the Hardcore Champion of the EWA, E-Nuff. E-Nuff seemed confident, what with a spot in the Corporate Empire on the line for him. The teams made their way to the ring, and we started off with Kane against SmirtDogg. Smirt went to work quickly on Kane, hitting him fast and furious with dropkicks. He finally got the big man wobbling, and hit the ropes....running right into Kane's right hand. Kane started to chokeslam Smirt, but Smirt kicked him below the belt, and hit a spinning neckbreaker. Smirt tagged in the behemoth, Vincent Jackson, and Jackson went to work on Kane, stomping away. Kane regained the advantage, however, when he first reversed a vertical suplex, and then hit a clothesline from the top rope. Kane then tagged in The Rock, who actually got a postitve reception from the crowd. Jackson tagged in SmirtDogg as well, and The Rock and Smirt began a slugfest that lasted for a few minutes. The match continued at this pace, until The Rock seemed to gain control over Smirt, hitting the People's Elbow. Rock then set up Smirt for the Rock Bottom, but Smirt delivered a kick to the stomach, followed by the Gangsta's Paradise. As E-Nuff went to make the cover, however, Johnny Napalm, decked out in a referee shirt, sprinted to the ring, and whacked E-Nuff over the head with a cookie sheet. Both men struggled to their feet, and SmirtDogg then tagged in Jackson. Jackson was hit from behind by Kane, and staggered straight into the grasp of The Rock -- and The Rock Bottom. The Rock made the cover, and Napalm quickly counted to three, giving he and Kane the victory. Kane & The Rock d.
