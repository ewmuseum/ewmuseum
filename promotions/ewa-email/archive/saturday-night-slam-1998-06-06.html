EWA Saturday Night Slam

6 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 6, 1998 Live, from the Freeman Coliseum, San Antonio, Texas. "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Perry Saturn (1/2 of EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) Chris Craft v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Non-Title Return Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna Kournikova) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. KG (w/ Sunny) Ruger Ice v. Lee Tenn The Widow Maker v. John Kronus (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions) The Black Angels v. Stars And Stripes "The Total Package" Lex Luger (w/ The WolfPack) v. Zuliel Non-Title Match Furnace v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Scott Hall (w/ The WolfPack) v. The Gunslinger KooKabura v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg #1 Contender Match Winner will recieve EWA World Television Title shot at Summer Breakout '98 "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Non-Title Match Kevin Nash (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Perry Saturn (1/2 of EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) The opening match at Saturday Night Slam turned out to be a good technical wrestling match. Saturn, wrestling in singles action for the first time, started out the match in control using a variety of armbar-type submission holds. However, Satre fought back, and began his own submission-based attack, placing Saturn in a modified version of the Texas Cloverleaf. Saturn escaped by virtue of the ropes, however, and began to fight back. Satre whipped him into the ropes, but Saturn reversed it, caught Satre on the way back, and nailed a Death Valley Driver for the victory. Perry Saturn d. Joe Satre with the Death Valley Driver. Chris Craft v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland Shawn Copeland made a very impressive debut tonight, dominating Craft in every way imaginable. Copeland showed off a variety of high-flying manuevers, finishing him off with the Copelandator. Shawn Copeland d. Chris Craft with the Copelandator. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Haynes started off this match by jumping Flair as he was taking off his robe. He whipped Flair into the turnbuckle, and that was when his offensive attack ended. Flair came back out with a series of chops to the chest, following those with a knee drop, and then, The Figure Four. Ric Flair d. Will Haynes by submission with The Figure Four. Non-Title Return Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna Kournikova) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) Yet another no-show by The Stud Stable. President Gates came to the ring and stated that if nobody had heard from the Studs by next Saturday, he would be forced to remove their contracts from the EWA. Jack McCammon d. Shawn Hart by forfeit. Jack McCammon retains the EWA World Television Championship. Konnan (w/ The WolfPack) v. KG (w/ Sunny) K-Dawg came out to a nice ovation from the crowd, while KG was thundered with boos. KG ripped a sign up from a fan that said "KG = Serpent Man's jobber", and was obviously in a hostile mood. Konnan started this match in control, working KG over with various moves, stopping to scream "Arriba La Raza" to the crowd every so often. KG soon gained momentum, however, when he executed a low blow to K-Dawg. KG began to take control of the match, and finally hit the Blackout. As he was going for the pin, though, Lex Luger ran in the ring and powerslammed KG, placing him in The Torture Rack. KG d. Konnan by disqualification. Ruger Ice v. Lee Tenn Another fantastic match featuring two of the up-coming stars in this sport. These two kept the crowd on the edge of their seats with a large number of near-falls, and had an exciting match. The two kept battling, until finally, the bell rang, signaling the 20 minute time limit had been reached. Both men stared each other down in the ring, and then shook hands in a rare form of *gasp* sportsmanship in the EWA. :) Ruger Ice and Lee Tenn battled to a time limit draw. The Widow Maker v. John Kronus (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions) The Widow Maker came into this match with a lot of confidence, riding a two-match winning streak. He started off the match in control by muscling Kronus around the ring. The momentum shifted to Kronus and back throughout the match, but The Widow Maker made his key mistake when he went to the top rope for an attempted splash. Kronus quickly moved out of the way, and went to the top, nailing a 450 splash. John Kronus d. The Widow Maker with a 450 splash. The Black Angels v. Stars And Stripes Ken Shamrock and The Patriot looked somewhat sluggish in their debut, but still managed to easily put away the team of Satchiel and Kaworu. Afterward, Lex Luger, Scott Hall, and Kevin Nash appeared at the entrance ramp, and Luger announced that he and The Outsiders accepted the challenge made by Stars And Stripes for Summer Breakout '98. Stars And Stripes d. The Black Angels with The Patriot's pin on Kaworu after The Uncle Slam. "The Total Package" Lex Luger (w/ The WolfPack) v. Zuliel Luger made a successful debut in singles competition, by putting Zuliel away in under two minutes. If you would've blinked, you would've probably missed this match. During the match, newly signed EWA wrestler Mayne Mann was spotted sitting at ringside. Lex Luger d. Zuliel by submission with The Torture Rack. Non-Title Match Furnace v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Furnace was fired for his constant no-showing during this match, and The Wyldsyde looked extremely frustrated about his lack of an opponent. "Papa Don't Preach" then blared over the P.A., and Joe Lemon came to the ring to apparently replace Furnace in the match. Lemon, quite frankly, was promptly destroyed by the reigning U.S. Heavyweight Champion. The Wyldsyde d. Joe Lemon with The Wyldryde. Scott Hall (w/ The WolfPack) v. The Gunslinger After Scott Hall did the survey, he went back to the locker room; The Gunslinger once again decided not to show up. Scott Hall d. The Gunslinger by forfeit. KooKabura v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg SmirtDogg carried a life-sized Barney doll to the ring with him, and in front of KooKabura, ripped its head off. This obviously disturbed KooKabura a lot, and served to SmirtDogg's advantage, as he quickly put away KooKabura with the newest manuever in his aresnal, the Gangsta's Paradise -- an even-flow DDT. SmirtDogg d. KooKabura with the Gangsta's Paradise. *After finishing Kookabura off with the greatest of ease -- SmirtDogg takes the microphone from ring announcer David Pinzer and delivers these harsh and vicious comments towards EWA United States Champion Wyldsyde.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Wyldsyde, while I was on hiatus from the EWA alot of things changed: #1: The Light Heavyweight Division lost it's greatest Champion of all time in me -- Smirt-D-O-Double G. My title reign spanned from January 12th to March 30th. 2 and a half months. I never got beaten for the belt, rather it was taken away from me when EWA President Duane Gates fired me -- his best EWA superstar. What was his motives, you ask? Well he was envious of my success and didn't think I fit the profile of someone he wanted to see as a champion in his federation. Gates and I go way back. Never liked the chump and never will. The point I'm getting at is once the Light Heavyweight Belt was taken off of my waist for political reasons -- the division went into the sh*tter. The belt has been passed around like a hot potatoe from Alex Wright to Aaron Martin to Thorne Richards back to Martin and finally Shawn Michaels is the current title holder. All of this only in a 2 month period. A damn shame what has happened there. Duane Gates has literally begged for talent to come into the EWA to save the Light Heavyweight Division. As for me -- I say the hell with the Light Heavyweight Division because I have my eyes set on bigger and greater things like taking the EWA United States Belt from you Wyldsyde. I've held it before and lets be truthful here -- I'm a much better champion then you can ever dream of being. #2: The EWA went down in ratings because without a weekly dose of the Ghetto Warriors -- especially "God's Gift to Wrestling" people lost interest and began watching another federation based out of Oklahoma where I happen to be the World Heav yweight Champion. Wyldsyde -- have you ever won a World Heavyweight Title? And you can't count that title which was handed to you because the talent level of the now defunct FFW was equal to if not worse then the sh*t KG passes as wrestling here in the EWA. Wyldsyde -- you don't have the characteristics, the skills nor the mental or physical abilities to contend with me -- "God's Gift to Wrestling". #3: People like Wyldsyde finally gathered up enough courage to become part of the EWA knowing full well that I was gone and off in another federation. This also includes that b*tch Jack McCammon. I seen his whimp ass in that latest flop of an interview -- pretending he had a cold. He's just too afraid to show up to the EWA arena around the U.S. now that I'm back in the EWA and my reign of terror is in full effect. KG was my first adversary to get blasted upon my return and Wyldsyde you're next! Wyldsyde -- I've got a whole lot planned for you but that will have to wait until Saturday Night Slam. Yes that's right I'm challenging you to a match at the next edition of SNS which will be coming straight outta Omaha, Nebraska, in the Pershing Auditorium. So Wyldsyde do you have enough courage to put that EWA United States Title on the line against me? Heh, do you even have the guts to accept my challenge? I vote no on both accounts. You're scared sh*tless right now and nobody could blame you. You had no idea this was coming and there's no way to prepare for me. You can sit in that recliner of yours and jerkoff to those wrestling magazines all day -- as you try and gather up information on me but all the knowledge in the world won't get you the win over me kid. And Wyldsyde you would think with all the time you spend in that little room of yours -- you would have heard of an invention called the television. Reading is for geeky pieces of trash like you. Wyldsyde I'm calling your ass out and now my friend you have an opponent who'll literally tear you apart mentally and physically so much so that you'll be writing little b*tch notes to Duane Gates like one KG. Word up! And Wyldsyde if the match happens between us -- meaning you actually waddle down to the ring and take your beating like the woman you are -- there will be no Wyldryde. The only ride they'll be is the Gangsta's Paradise -- when your lopsided head takes the drop right onto the canvas and I follow it with the 1....2....3 pinfall victory. End of Story. Nah that's too whack of a way to finish such a great interview so we'll try it again. Wyldsyde, Bon Voyage mother*cker! #1 Contender Match Winner will recieve EWA World Television Title shot at Summer Breakout '98 "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Neither man showed up, so President Gates announced that two new men would face each other at WNW for the #1 Contender's spot. EWA European Championship Match Kevin Nash (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) President Gates agreed to let Stevie defend the European Title against Nash before the match. This match was dominated from the start by "Big Sexy", as his height and size advantage was put to great use. Nash had nailed his big boot, and was setting up Stevie for the JackKnife, when Jeff Bunda began to walk to the ring. Nash began to yell at Bunda, motioning for him to get in the ring, and behind him, Stevie slowly got to his feet. When Nash turned around, Stevie nailed him with the Stevie Kick, and made a quick cover. He then ran through the crowd to avoid the other members of the WolfPack, who were running to ringside. Stevie Richards d. Kevin Nash with the Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards retains the EWA European Championship.
