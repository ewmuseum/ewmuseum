EWA Wednesday Night War

15 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 15, 1998 Live, from Rupp Arena, Lexington, Kentucky. Public Enemy (w/ Dick Dennis) v. New Demolition (w/ Smash) This was a fairly good debut for New Demolition, but Public Enemy continued their roll, overshadowing the debut of Flame and Buddy Brown. Public Enemy d. New Demolition with Ghetto Boy's pin on Flame after the Ghetto Driver. (7:23) EWA World Television Championship Match The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. Castor Troy (EWA World Television Champion) This was a very hard-fought matchup, and a clean one, something we don't see enough of. Castor was attempting a vertical suplex, but The Gunslinger overpowered him, reversed it into the Quickdraw DDT, and made the cover for his first ever EWA title reign. The Gunslinger d. Castor Troy with the Quickdraw. (14:32) Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Another hard fought matchup, but Double A continued to impress, and obtained another victory. Arn Anderson d. Deathstar with the DDT. (11:28) The Ghetto Warriors (w/ Sunny) v. Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) The Ghetto Warriors completely destroyed FAI in this one. The Ghetto Warriors d. Fire and Ince with SmirtDogg's pin on Zaane after the Air Smirty. (3:11) Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. Dude Love Another solid match...but the Dude kept on rolling, and took home the victory. Dude Love d. Tommy Dreamer with the Sweet Shin Music. (7:29) Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ Triple Threat) Gardner didn't show up. Galaxy d. Joshua Gardner by forfeit. EWA European Championship Match KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Prodigy (EWA European Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Another no-show; Koo didn't show up, and as Prodigy had his hand raised, the lights began to flicker. Prodigy, confused, made his way back to the locker room. Prodigy d. KooKabura by forfeit. Prodigy retains the EWA European Championship. The Widow Maker v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Double no-show here. No Holds Barred Match For the EWA World Tag Team Championship Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. FlashBack (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) Both teams seemed uninterested at the start, but the action soon picked up, and when the dust cleared, The Morgue had gold around its waist. Death Sentance d. FlashBack with Destruction's pin on Flash Funk after the DeathBlow. Death Sentance is the new EWA World Tag Team Champions!! The Golden One v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) The Golden One continued to impress, and absolutely man-handled Triple-H in this one. The Golden One d. Triple-H with The Golden One. (5:43) Raven (w/ DDT) v. Thorne Richards No show for Raven. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ Triple Threat) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) HBK looked good at the onset of this match, but it didn't last long, as Bunda continued to impress, and held on for his first championship. Jeff Bunda d. Shawn Michaels with the Apocalypse Drop. (12:56) Jeff Bunda is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion!!
