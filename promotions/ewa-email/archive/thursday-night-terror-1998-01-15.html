EWA Thursday Night Terror

15 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 15, 1998 Live, from The Key Arena in Seattle, Washington. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match The Pessimist v. Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) This was a great match, filled with many near-falls. The two men really took it to each other, proving they both have what it takes to be champions in the EWA. But alas, only one man can come out on top, and the man tonight was The Pessimist. Marc Anthony made a rookie mistake when he turned his back on The Pessimist to acknowledge the crowd, and The Pessimist made him pay for it by hitting the Suicidal Tendencies, and getting the three count victory. The Pessimist d. Marc Anthony via Suicidal Tendencies. (13:29) The Pessimist is the first ever EWA Intercontinental Champion!! "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. The Widow Maker Chris Benoit proved tonight that the Horsemen are a force to be reckoned with, as he destroyed the larger Widow Maker in the ring. Benoit was too aggressive for the rookie, and ended the match by forcing The Widow Maker to submit. Chris Benoit d. The Widow Maker by submission via the Crippler Crossface. (2:55) Bret "Hitman" Hart v. The Violator (w/ The Clown) The Excellence of Execution had an extremely impressive debut, as he dominated The Violator. This was another total squash. Bret Hart d. The Violator by submission via The Sharpshooter. (3:01) Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) This was a very exciting tag team match, as Prophecy seemed to return to their glory days, and Death Sentance stepped it up a notch. It appeared that Pain had the victory in hand as he applied The Clamp to Travis Scott, but WormBoy climbed into the ring and blasted Pain with a chair. Timothy David then climbed to the top rope, and the duo executed The Cryptochid for the win. Prophecy d. Death Sentance via Travis Scott's pin on Pain after The Cryptochid. (9:51) The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "Bad Ass" Billy Gunn (w/ Degeneration X) This was a fairly competitve match, as Gunn's experience, and underhanded tatics, gave him the early advantage. But the fans rallied around GC, and he overcame Gunn's experience. The Golden Child d. "Bad Ass" Billy Gunn via The Royal Destroyer. (7:33) "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. The Gunslinger The Gunslinger seemed completely out of sync, as 'The Nature Boy' showed why he is one of the all-time greats. Triple-H watched this match from the entrance ramp, but surprisingly, didn't interfere. He didn't have to. Ric Flair d. The Gunslinger by submission via the Figure Four. (6:17) Scott Hall (w/ Degeneration X) v. Matt Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) This match never even got started, as both stables began a huge brawl in the ring. The match between Scott Hall and Matt Hart was ruled a double-disqualification. Non-Title Match The Insiders v. Harlem Heat (champions, w/ Jacquelyn) Harlem Heat had yet to recieve competition here in the EWA, and the trend continued tonight. The Insiders didn't even show up. Comissioner Slaughter came to the ring and announced that if Kevin Hall and Scott Nash did not come to the ring in ten seconds, their contracts would be terminated. It didn't seem to make a difference, as they still didn't show. As a result, Scott Nash and Kevin Hall are no longer with the EWA. Harlem Heat d. The Insiders via forfeit. Julius/Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia/D-Lo Brown (w/ The Nation Of Domination) This match was dominated by The Empire, as the NOD seemed completely out of sync. It seemed to be a night of squashes, and this was no different. Julius/Augustus d. Rocky Maivia/D-Lo Brown via Augustus' pin on D-Lo Brown after a Frankensteiner from the top rope. (4:59) Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) This was a fairly competitive match, with Nash's size giving him the advantage. Octavius put up a good fight, but Nash eventually overwhelmed him. Kevin Nash d. Octavius via the JackKnife. (10:47) 20 Man Over The Top Rope Battle Royal Royal Rumble Rules Winner is crowned EWA United States Heavyweight Champion Participants: Nero, Kama Mustafa, Erik Draven, Ken Shamrock, Shawn Hart, SmirtDogg, Destruction, "Diamond" Dallas Page, Prodigy, Vinnie Vegas, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, Arn Anderson, Road Warrior Hawk, KG, The Pessimist, Scott Cummings, Faarooq, Augustus, The Gunslinger, & Travis Scott ORDER OF ENTRY #1. Hunter Hearst Helmsley #2. Nero #3. Arn Anderson #4. Kama Mustafa #5. Road Warrior Hawk #6. Erik Draven #7. KG #8. Ken Shamrock #9. The Pessimist #10. Shawn Hart #11. Scott Cummings #12. SmirtDogg #13. Faarooq #14. Destruction #15. Augustus #16. "Diamond" Dallas Page #17. The Gunslinger #18. Prodigy #19. Travis Scott #20. Vinnie Vegas HIGHLIGHTS Diamond Dallas Page and Vinnie Vegas eliminated each other, as the two were involved in a hellacious brawl. Erik Draven eliminated 7 people, the high count. In the end, the final 5 people were Scott Cummings, Prodigy, SmirtDogg, Erik Draven, and The Pessimist. Prodigy eliminated the Pessimist after executing The Firestarte on him. Erik Draven was the man to take out Cummings, making a statement for their match at Ground Zero. Prodigy, SmirtDogg, and Draven then all began to brawl with each other. Prodigy eventually eliminated Draven by clotheslining him over the top rope. And then there were two....Smirt and Prodigy battled back and forth for approximately 10 minutes, but Smirt pulled it off by superkicking Prodigy over the top rope. SmirtDogg wins the battle royal. SmirtDogg is the first ever EWA United States Champion! A match between SmirtDogg and Prodigy has been signed for MNM, for the U.S. Title.
