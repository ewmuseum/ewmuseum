EWA Saturday Night Slam

18 September 1999

EWA Saturday Night Slam (9/18/99) April 17, 1999 Live, from the Hartford Civic Center Hartford, Connecticut ---- ***Fireworks explode all over the entrance way, as rockets soar down from the Jumbotron into the stands. The fans go absolutely balistic at the sound of the "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000. The camera pans around, as the arena is filled beyond capacity of die-hard EWA fans. Posterboards are everywhere, as we see signs for Captain Howdy, Triple H, and The Kaze. The music is cut off, nd the fireworks slowly disepate, but the fans keep on roaring. They have been waiting for the return of the EWA for a very long time, and are ecstatic about its return. The Saturday Night Slam musisuddenly comes to a halt. The camera focuses on the center of the ring, where EWA ring announcer Richard Riley stands.*** Richard Riley: Ladies and gentlemen, may I have your attention please. Last week, the world of professional wrestling lost one of its true great men when legendary EWA performer Haywood Jublome passed away at the age of 25. Jublome was a remarkable athlete, and a truly great man. In honor of his life, please allow for a moment of silence as we salute Haywood Jublome by ringing the bell ten times. ***The bell is rang ten times, and a standing ovation is heard after the salute. The ovation continues as "Welcome To The Jungle" by Guns 'N Roses blares over the PA, and the crowd reaches a fever pitch as the returning president of the EWA, Mr. Duane Gates, makes his way to the ring. Gates climbs inside the ring, and takes the microphone.*** President Gates: Ladies and gentlemen...WELCOME TO THE EWA!!!! ***The crowd roars its approval*** Ladies and gentlemen, tonight I want to make a very special announcement. Wednesday night, on Wednesday Night War, live from The FleetCenter in Boston, Massachusetts, the EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament will begin, with four first-round matches taking place. We'll have Tony Antea taking on "The Game", Triple-H, in one match. Also competing will be Cold Harding Cash, as he squares off against the master of the Nuclear Hell match, Nukeman. 'The ICON', Erik Draven, will compete against the deranged Captain Howdy, and Jack Daniels will square off against... *The lights in the arena suddenly go out. After a few seconds, organ music blares over the speakers and the crowd erupts. After the intro, a massive wall of fire erupts from the floor, as Inferna walks out from behind the curtain. Right behind her is Kane and Erik Draven, as the two men walk side by side down to the ring. Kane steps up onto the ring apron and over the top rope and stands in front of President Gates. Inferna brushes back President Gates hair with her hands, and takes the microphone from him and hands it to Kane.* Kane: Through hellfire and brimstone, the Big Red Machine has returned!!! Now, it is only fitting that the two men who made the EWA what it is, are the first to return to the ringside area. And oh yes, I am very aware of the fact that Jack Daniels stands before me next Wednesday Night. Daniels, you don't know what you've gotten yourself into boy. You may have had success in places like the lowly NYSWF and the pitiful NWW, but now you are in my home...which for you boy means your own personal hell!!! The pain and suffering that I've had over my life will be nothing compared to what you must endure this coming Wednesday. The last true EWA Champion has returned, and with him the most powerful force in this industy as well, as Erik Draven and I have started right where we left off, and that was destroying everyone and everything in our path! Now for those of you who are not familiar with the EWA, an operation made speech for me possible...and operation is the only thing that might save your life Daniels when this is all said and done with. The World Title Tournament is mine, and there's not a damn thing you can do about it. President Gates: You do realize Kane that you could be facing your partner and friend Erik Draven in the second round of the tournament right? *Kane just turns his head and gives President Gates a menacing glare, as does Draven. Kane slowly raises his arms into the air, and then throws them down to his sides as fire erupts from the ringposts.* Kane: Don't ever ask another stupid question like that again, or you will be resting side by side with Daniels boy. Because he will soon, like anyone else who stands before us....REST...IN...PEEEAAAACE!!! *Kane's music blares over the speakers again, as he and Draven leave the ring. Inferna walks up to President Gates once again, just smiling seductively at him...and then goes towards the ropes. Just before leaving, she blows a ball of fire into the air, and then smirks as she joins Kane at the top of the ramp, as they exit.* *We get a camera shot of Nate James arriving in the arena. He is seemingly just a little shaky, but he gets to his dressing room, and sees a big manilla envelope on his door. He takes it down and opens it. He pulls out candid pictures of his family at their home, a few censored shots of his wife, and then of a fire being started in his house. He is frantic now and he crumples up the pictures. There is also a note. It reads "Hey Nate, you want to know who I am? Meet me in the cemetary, THE cemetary, next Saturday. You'll find out just who I am. Till then.* Triangle Tag Team Match Sexual Tension v. The Quick & The Dead v. Harlem Heat The Quick and the Dead were already in the ring as "Get At Me Dog" by DMX begins to play over the Public Address System. The fans have a mixed reaction, not knowing much about these two rookies. They run down to the ring, as Johnny Quick and The Angel scurried to the outside. Sexual Tension climb opposite turnbuckles, and raise there arms high in the air. Some fans cheer, but many just remain silent. Suddenly, Harlem Heat's theme music begins to blare over the P.A. System. The fans rise in unison, as they cheer for the former 3-time EWA Tag Team Champions. Booker T and Stevie Ray come out, as red flames suddenly rise into the air. The fans roar as Harlem Heat make their way down to the ring, as Booker and Stevie Ray acknowledge all of the cheers. Its been a long time since they have had the pleasure of watching Harlem Heat do battle, and they are letting them know that they haven't forgotten them. They slide under the ropes into the ring, as they go right for Rodriguez and Sebastian. Johnny Quick and The Angel slide in as well, as the bell rings, signifying the start of the match. All six men brawl at the beginning, but eventually the referee gets control of the match, and the three legal men square off against each other. In the ring are Booker T, Johnny Quick, and Jose Rodriguez. Johnny rushes towards Booker T, but Booker takes him down with a drop toe-hold. Booker turns around, and is met by a rushing Rodriguez, who gives him a flying knee hit. Jose throws him off the ropes, and connects with a tilt-a-whirl-slam. Johnny Quick rushes at Rodriguez, as they exchange punches. Johnny gets the advantage, and kicks Jose in the gut, followed up with a DDT. Johnny tags in The Angel, as they double team on Jose. The Angel puts Rodriguez in the powerbomb position, but Booker T runs at him, and takes him out with a flying forearm. The Angel rolls to the outside, as Booker T tags in Stevie Ray. They immediately begin to double team on Jose, who desperately needs to make a tag to his partner. Stevie and Booker lift Jose in the air, and deliver a double suplex. Jose holds his back in agony. Sudden, and clotheslines Stevie Ray over the top. The Angel takes Sebastian, and drags him to the outside. Booker and Jose remain in the ring, as Booker T climbs the top rope. Rodriguez slowly gets to his feet...turns around, and....Missile Dropkick! He goes for the cover...One...Two...Th..kickout! Rodriguez barely kicked out of that!Booker T picks Jose up, throws him off the ropes, and HARLEM SIDEKICK! Sebastian see's this, and tries to climb back into the ring, but Johnny Quick and The Angel pull him back down. One...Two...Three....Sexual Tension has been eliminated! Harlem Heat and The Quick and the Dead remain, as Johnny Quick and The Angel climb back into the ring, and begin attack Booker T. Stevie Ray tries to get to his fon the outside, but was taken out by Johnny Quick during their outside brawl. Quick and The Angel stomp away at Booker T, who is getting absolutely mugged in the ring. The Angel picks up Booker Tgoes off the ropes, and hits him with a swinging neckbreaker, which is followed up by a leg drop by Johnny. He goes for the cover. One...Two...kickout! Johnny and The Angel pick up Booker T, and throw him off the ropes. They go for a double clothesline, but Booker T ducks. He bounces off the other side, and takes both of them out with a flying crossbody. Stevie Ray now enters back into the brawl, as he drags The Angel to the outside. Booker begins to get some momentum, and raises his arms in the air as the fans cheer. He sends Johnny off the ropes, aback body drops him to the canvas. On the outside, Stevie Ray throws The Angel into the steel stairs, as The Angel holds his shoulder in obvious pain. Meanwhile, Booker T has picked up Quick, and has sent him off the ropes again. Stevie Ray climbs over the middle rope, and enters the ring. Booker T goes for another Harlem Sidekick, but Johnny ducks it! However, Stevie Ray is right there, as he puts Johnny's head between his legs....SLAPJACK! The Angel has gotten up, and is climbing into the ring. However, Booker T just drop-kicked him to the outside. Stevie Ray covers. One...Two....Three! Harlem Heat wins! Harlem Heat d. The Quick & The Dead and Sexual Tension with Stevie Ray's pin on Johnny Quick after the SlapJack. Captain Howdy v. Scott Slade Captain Howdy has burst onto the professional wrestling scene lately, winning championships in the now-defunct WWA and INWA. Scott Slade, meanwhile, looked to make a name for himself in his first EWA match against Howdy. Folks...it wasn't happening. Slade, simply put, got MURDERED by Captain Howdy. Howdy physically punished Slade for about two minutes, before executing an implant DDT, and then hitting The Howdy Driver for the easy victory. Captain Howdy d. Scott Slade with the Howdy Driver. Triple-H v. Lunatikk Crippler This match opened with the Lunatikk Crippler as the proverbial 'house afire'...dominating Helmsley with a series of clotheslines before the bell even gets a chance to ring. Chyna quickly stopped this by dragging Helmsley out, but unfortunately for the duo, LC was about two steps behind them. Chyna, however, saw this and whipped the Crippler into the steel steps, nearly dislocating LC's shoulder in the process. Helmsley saw an advantage and quickly seized it, wrenching LC's arm repeatedly around the steel ringpost. The referee finally managed to get both men inside the ring, where HHH quickly continued his assault on LC's arm, tearing away at it with an armbar submission move...holding it locked for nearly 6 minutes straight. This, of course, brought about a 'BOOOOOORING' chant from the crowd...leading Triple-H to break his concentration to go badmouth the crowd. A mistake which almost proved to be his undoing. As HHH turned his attention back toward LC, the Crippler quickly landed a boot in HHH's midsection, and hit a DDT for a loooooooong 2-count. Helmsley, enraged, pummeled LC with a series of lefts and rights, and once again locked on the armbar, with his back toward the ropes. A couple of minutes passed, and then the crowd erupted in a huge pop as Erik Draven made his way to ringside, SIngapore Cane in hand. He smirks as he reaches the ringside area, and hauls off and WAFFLES the back of Triple-H's head with the Cane, breaking it and sending blood everywhere. Draven reaches out and dabs a finger in HHH's blood, and rubs it in the fallen Helmsley's eyes for emphasis. LC slowly climbs to his feet, a look of confusion on his face as he views the bloodied Triple-H. Draven looks up and nods at LC, then gestures for the Crippler to pin Helmsley, which he quickly does. Chyna attempts to break the pinfall, but Draven holds her back...Chyna then tries a clothesline, which Draven ducks and quickly reverses into the Death Valley Driver on the concrete floor...Ouch, for lack of better words. In the ring, the ref makes a slow 3-count, giving Lunatikk Crippler the victory in this hard-fought bout. Draven smirks at LC, nods again, and slowly proceeds back to the locker room as the Crippler celebrates in the ring. Lunatikk Crippler d. Triple-H as a result of a cane-shot by Erik Draven. 'Outlaw Immortal' KG v. Nathaniel James [with Robert Jenkins] For his much-storied return to the EWA, KG, to put it quite bluntly...stunk up the arena. KG opened the match with a series of punches and kicks that would make Hulk Hogan proud, but then it all fell apart for the 'Outlaw Immortal.' Nathaniel James reversed an attempted Irish Whip by KG into a short-arm clothesline, sending the Outlaw Immortal to the mat in a heap...and lucky his head was still attached, in all honesty. James continued to toy with KG for a few more moments, slapping the back of his head to taunt him, and literally kicking the half-conscious veteran around the ring. KG mounted a slight comeback, dropping Nathaniel James with a back-kick right to the old 'family jewels', and attempted to capitalize by attempting a springboard hurricanrana on the dazed James. Unfortunately for KG, James caught KG in mid-air and reversed the 'rana attempt into a VICIOUS running tigerbomb, but only managed a two-count on KG. James, looking frustrated, dragged KG to his feet and quickly dropped him on his head with a Tombstone Piledriver, rendering KG virtually unconscious. From there it was all elementary, as James dragged KG back to his feet once more, pulled his half-dead carcass up to the top rope, and executed the Excalibur for the three-count, and a notch in the 'W' column. Nathaniel James d. KG with Excalibur. [The hallways are desserted, presumably everyone is in their lockerrooms getting ready to watch the main event on the closed circuit TV. Nathanial James is walking down the hallway. His two rottweilers: Razor and Headhunter next to him. He reaches the lockerroom door he was looking for: Captain Howdy's. He knocks on the door and just stands there. Howdy opens the door. He steps back and points at Howdy. The two dogs jump at Howdy and nail him to the ground. Nate, watches, then he sees the camera, he turns towards it and begins talking in a very sombre tone.] Nathaniel James: See, this is what happens when you play games with me. My family is dead and you chose to make fun of that. Well, now I am playing nasty. Once, I would challenged you face-to-face. Now, I don't care. I can play just as dirty as anyone else. Because I don't care. Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust. I have a case of bloodlust. [James whistles and his two dogs come to him. He walks away from the scene of the crime. Howdy is lying on the floor with lacerations over his arms and legs. His face is also cut. The camera cuts away and back to the main event.] Six Man, Four-Corners Match For the Vacant EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Tony Antea v. Nukeman v. The Kaze [with Jade] v. 'White Hot Chocolate' Philip Donovan v. 'The ICON' Erik Draven v. 'Cold' Harding Cash Tony Antea and Nukeman made it to the ring first for the Six Man, Four Corners U.S. Heavyweight Championship bout, which we have learned is now a no-disqualification match. This was an interesting test for EWA Head Official Randy Lemons. Nukeman and Antea didn't even wait for the next entrant, as the two began throwing rights and lefts. Antea gained an advantage with a short kick to the stomach, followed by an Irish whip. Antea picked Nukeman up on the way back, high above his head, but Nukeman was able to slip out, and counter with a thunderous lariat that sent Antea toppling to the outside. At this time, 'Between The Sheets' by the Isley Brothers came on the PA, and the crowd came alive as Philip Donovan, former E-Revolution World Heavyweight Champion, ran to the ring. Donovan ran at Nukeman, ducked a clothesline from Nukeman, hit the ropes, and came back with a nicely executed hurricanranna. Afterward, Donovan sprung to the top rope, and set his targets on Antea and the outside of the ring, executing a beautiful corkscrew moonsault to the delight of the capacity crowd. Nukeman had secured a steel chair, however, and as Donovan got back on his feet, Nukeman introduced him to his new best friend by bashing his head with the chair. Nuke picked up Donovan, and tossed him in the ring like a rag doll. Antea, meanwhile, crawled under the ring, presumably to look for some plunder. Nukeman stood in the ring over a fallen Donovan, and raised the chair high above his head. However, unbeknownest to him, Cold Harding Cash had sprinted down the ring aisle, and entered the ring. Cash grabbed the chair from Nukeman, and BLASTED the king of the Nuclear Hell match right in the dome with the chair. Cash played no favorites, hitting Donovan with the chair as well. Cash sat the chair up in the ring, sat down, folded his arms, and waited patiently for the next man to come to the ring. Meanwhile in the ring, Tony Antea had reappeared, and was setting a table up near the turnbuckle. However, his work was shortlived, as while Cash was sitting in his chair, Nukeman had retrieved a cable cord, and proceeded to choke Antea out with it. PhD, however, made the save by hitting a nicely executed sleeping neckbreaker onto Nukeman. Donovan spotted Harding Cash, and then pulled off what was probably the move of the night. In an incredible feat, Donovan got a running start, jumped onto the table, from the table to the top turnbuckle, and then leapt into the air, landing on Cash. The two men and the chair toppled over, and at this point... 1 2...This On? The lights faded out, as the sixth and final entrant into this contest [apparently The Kaze missed his flight], Erik Draven, made his way to the ring. Draven headed for the ring, where Nukeman and Antea were battling it out. Draven had no love lost for Nukeman, who brutally attacked his best friend, Shawn Michaels, in another federation. Draven went to work on Nukeman, hitting him with lefts and rights until he finally finished the sequence with a pumphandle slam. Draven played the crowd, but his bragging didn't last long, as Antea nailed him from behind with a chair. Meanwhile, on the outside, PhD whipped Harding Cash into the steel guardrail. PhD ran at Cash, but Cash back-dropped Donovan all the way into the first row, spilling a young lady's drink all over her white blouse...aw, isn't that a shame? Amazingly, PhD had a sudden interest in this now-drenched attractive young woman, and began talking to her. Unbeknownest to PhD, Tony Antea had made his way out of the ring, and hooked PhD for a German suplex. PhD had the presence of mind, however, to kick upward into Antea's groin [OUCH!], and send Antea sprawling. However, PhD did NOT see Nukeman come off the ring apron with a double-ax handle...not until it was too late, anyway. Nukeman then picked up Antea, and tossed him into the ring. Nukeman set Antea up on the middle rope, and executed a devastating spinebuster, the move he likes to call The Nuclear Bomb. Nukeman made the cover, the referee counted to three, and Tony Antea was the first man to be eliminated in this contest. At this point, the three remaining men all dove into the ring. PhD was the first man to make a move, running at Draven and attempting another spectacular Hurricanrana. But...OUCH...it didn't turn out the same way, as Draven countered it into a thunderous powerbomb that literally rocked the Hartford Civic Center. Draven picked up the limp body of White Hot Chocolate, nailed the Death Valley Driver, and pinned PhD, eliminating him and bringing this match down to three participants. At this point, an unusual occurance happened - Draven and Cash formed an alliance to take out Nukeman. The two began punishing Nukeman, much to the chagrin of the crowd. However, Nukeman made a comeback, pummeling both men with rights and lefts. Nukeman had Cash wobbling, and was about to go for a piledriver, when Draven grabbed him from behind and executed a reverse DDT. That was followed by Cash coming off the top rope with a frog splash, a move he calls Cold Hard Cash. Draven then picked up Cash and threw him to the mat, pinning Nukeman himself - and narrowing the field to just he and Cold Harding Cash. Cash attacked first, leveling Draven from behind with a low blow. Cash took advantage to mock Draven, walking around the ring giving mock crotch chops to the crowd. A chant of "ICON! ICON! ICON!" arose through the arena, which only infuriated Cash. Cash picked up Draven, and whipped him into the ropes. However, Draven stopped dead in his tracks, and grabbed Cash by the throat. The massive Draven lifted Cash HIGH up into the air, and dropped him with a huge clothesline. Draven drew his thumb across his throat, signaling for the DVD, when C.J. Cordial stepped into the ring behind him, fire extinguisher [FIRE EXTINGUISHER?!?!?!] in hand. Cordial LEVELED Draven with the extinguisher, and then placed a virtually unconscious Cash on top of Draven, in one of the more disgusting moments of the night. The referee reluctantly counted to three, and almost immediately, EWA Commentator Mike Malone was informed that these two would meet in a rematch for the US title next Saturday. Nonetheless, as it currently stands, your new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion is Cold Harding Cash. Cold Harding Cash d. Erik Draven with interference from C.J. Cordial to become the new EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion.
