EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

5 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The Charleston Civic Center Charleston, South Carolina EWA World Television Championship Match Mayne Mann v. Crazy Canuck (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The PHC) *As Mayne Mann stands in the center of the ring awaiting Crazy Canuck, "I Shot The Sherrif" begins blaring over the PA system as Gunslinger emerges from the entrance way with a microphone and the EWA Television Title. The crowd immediately goes nuts upon seeing Gunslinger, Gunslinger just stands on the entrance ramp as pyrotechnics explode around him. After the pyros are finished Gunslinger motions for the music to be stopped and then begins speaking.* Gunslinger: Hey Mayne Mann, you lookin' to win this title here tonight? *Holds up the TV Title* Well I've got some bad news for ya, Canuck won't be making it to the ring tonight due to a 'slight' run-in with the law. *laughs* Now in case you didn't already know, this here television title has been around my waist on three different occasions and soon to be four. So if you want this title you are gonna have to go through me because I'm gonna do everything within my power to regain this title and there's not a damned thing you or anyone else can do about it. *pauses* Now that I think about it.....I've already got the title, so why don't we just have an improtu match right now. *Gunslinger starts walking towards the ring with the belt over his should as Mayne Mann backs up and readys himself for the match, but just as Gunslinger is getting to the ring Crazy Canuck comes running from the back with a slight limp and hits Gunslinger in the back, causing him to drop the TV Title. Canuck then goes to work on Gunslinger outside of the ring as Mayne Mann watches on the inside. Suddenly a man jumps from the crowd and rolls into the ring behind Mayne Mann and gives him a beautifully executed DDT. From there the man goes to the outside and pulls Canuck off of Gunslinger. Gunslinger then makes it to his feet and picks up the TV Title and nails Canuck in the back of the head with it. He then tosses the title on top of Canuck as him and the masked man leave to a tremendous crowd pop.* Due to all of this, President Gates announced the match as a no-contest, and has signed a triangle match for Clash Of The Champions V between Crazy Canuck, Mayne Mann, and The Gunslinger for the TV Title. Mayne Mann and Crazy Canuck battled to a no-contest. Crazy Canuck retains the EWA World Television Title. The Widow Maker v. Magnificant Matt (w/ The PHC) Due to flight complications, this match has been rescheduled for Clash Of The Champions V. Lucien (w/ Razor) v. The Golden One The Golden One looked extremely sharp in his return to in-ring action since his injury. Lucien put up an extremely good fight, but in the end, The Golden One was simply too overwhelming. The Golden One d. Lucien with a gutwrench powerbomb. "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. Hannibal Manson This was a solid contest, but Hannibal Manson showed some ring rust during it, and Satre was able to capitalize and defeat Manson with a well-executed small package. Joe Satre d. Hannibal Manson with a small package. Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. Morbid Angel One word: blowout. Morbid Angel was way off target tonight, and Jack Reed looked tremendous in his return, finishing Morbid Angel off with a Frankensteiner. Jack Reed d. Morbid Angel with a Frankensteiner. EWA European Championship Match Joe Lemon v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ The Dream Team) How Joe Lemon made Shawn Copeland's #1 Contender list, I'll never understand. Lemon actually hit The Lemon Drop at the beginning of the match, only to have Copeland sit up and laugh at him. From there, it was all downhill for Mr. Lemon, as Copeland destroyed him. Shawn Copeland d. Joe Lemon by submission with The Money Clip. Shawn Copeland retains the EWA European Title. Makaveli (w/ The PHC) v. The Gunslinger A good, solid match, but The Gunslinger was riding the momentum of his earlier appearance at WNW, and ended up the better man on this night. The Gunslinger d. Makaveli with The Six Shooter. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. IRK La Parka put up a good fight, but the IRK retained his undefeated streak, winning in impressive fashion. IRK d. La Parka by submission with the Killer Leglock. Haywood Jublome v. Jason Wyldheart Another outstanding match between two of the upcoming stars in the EWA featured Haywood Jublome narrowly defeat Jason Wyldheart, with a well-executed powerbomb into a pin. Haywood Jublome d. Jason Wyldheart with a powerbomb. Vertigo v. Intoxication-X As "Drei Mann Im Dopplebett" begins playing, a man dressed in a red and white striped apron and hat, with a white T-shirt and jeans underneath makes his way to the ring. Fans of the tag team Vertigo will recognize him as "Hot Tommy", the soda jerk at the Malt Diznee malt shop. He walks to the ring with a piece of paper in his right hand, gets into the ring, and asks for a microphone.} Hi, everybody, my name is Hot Tommy, and I'm here on behalf of Vertigo. Earlier today I received this letter, and I'm sure everybody in the EWA should hear this. Let me start. Hot Tommy clears his throat, unfolds the paper, and starts reading. "Dear Tommy: Hey, what's up? This is Jimmy, and the Maniac's right here while I write this. We were talking to a guy at the Headbanger's Bar and Grill the other night, and he told us about this guy in the desert that could help us become tag team champions, like we always wanted to be! So, after talking to him, we wrote this letter to you, and now we're on our way out to the desert. Apparently this guy lives in the middle of it. So, cat, we're on our way out to the middle of the desert to find this guy to help us! We should be back before too long, I mean, it doesn't take THAT long to walk through the desert, right? Talk to you when we get back, stay cool, cat!" And that's the whole thing. So, it doesn't look like there's going to be a match with Vertigo tonight...hopefully they're smart enough to make their way back so we can see another Vertigo match in the future! Sorry for the disappointment! And Tommy leaves the ring, without a whole lot of reaction from the fans. Some are stunned, and honestly, a lot don't mind Vertigo being gone! Intoxication-X d. Vertigo by forfeit. Non-Title Match Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. The Wyldsyde (EWA North American Champion) Van Smack didn't show up, but The Wyldsyde did...but he wasn't here to wrestle. {Dirty Day by U2 begins to blare over the loudspeaker as Wyldsyde makes his way to the ring. He's not dressed in his wrestling gear, however...he is wearing his brown suede jacket as usual, but not his brown singlet...he's wearing a green "Wyldsyde" T-shirt and blue jeans instead, with his North American belt around his waist. He quickly walks his way down to the ring, microphone in hand, walks up the ringsteps, then steps through the ropes. After waiting for the boos from the crowd to die down, he begins to speak.} Cut the music! It's time for The Wyldsyde to make a statement, and it's time for all you imbeciles in this crowd to listen! {Wyldsyde stops himself from continuing as a chorus of boos erupts after that verbal attack. He waits a couple seconds with a somewhat disgusted look on his face as he again waits for the crowd to die down.} It looks as though just as The Wyldsyde was beginning to go after Johnny 2 Bad, Johnny suddenly hightails it out of town. Fear? You had better believe it. Johnny knew that there is one thing you don't go up against, and that would be The Wyldsyde when The Wyldsyde is angry. And Johnny, if you can hear this right now, the only advice The Wyldsyde can give you is to keep running, because someday, The Wyldsyde is going to catch up with you. What happens then, well...it shouldn't be described in front of a national audience. And now, an announcement for all of The Wyldsyde's fans out there. {Once again, boos erupt. Wyldsyde begins looking around at the fans, perhaps to locate the source of the rowdiness, but it's just no use, so he continues on.} The Wyldsyde, as of now, is going after Johnny 2 Bad. Yes, perhaps he's not in the EWA at the moment...and that's exactly the point. The Wyldsyde is going to follow Johnny 2 Bad wherever he goes, whenever he goes. If The Wyldsyde catches up to him, you'll be sure to hear about it on the evening news. That is, if any of you are intelligent enough to watch news, or to even turn the channel from those horrible travesties they try to pass off as programming anymore. {Yet another cacaphony of boos. Wyldsyde doesn't wait for these to subside, however.} Yes, until further notice, The Wyldsyde shall be off trying to find Johnny 2 Bad. And as for this title...you can have it back, Mr. Gates. The Wyldsyde has things more important in life than defending it...getting The Wyldsyde's hands around Johnny 2 Bad's neck! Whatever you choose to do with it...The Wyldsyde truly doesn't care. Melt it down, give it to somebody, sell it at a garage sale, it's all the same to The Wyldsyde...until Johnny 2 Bad has been appr ehended and thoroughly punished by The Wyldsyde, it's of no matter. End of story. {And with that, Wyldsyde pulls the North American title from around his waist, throws it in the middle of the ring, and exits the ring in much the same way he came in. No music plays as he exits, and sporadic boos emanate through the crowd, as nobody quite knows what to make of this. He makes his way into the back, and the card continues, sans-Wyldsyde.} As of now, Erik Draven is the number-one contender to the EWA North American Title. President Gates will sign a match between Draven and someone else to determine the vacant title for COTCV. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. The Dynasty (w/ The Dream Team) Dos Equis was outmanned and outclassed in this one, plain and simple. The Dynasty, making its return, looked very impressive. The Dynasty d. Dos Equis with Shawn Michaels' pin on Tecate Jake after the Sweet Chin Music. Non-Title Strap Match Ruger Ice v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Dream Team) A good, solid match, which involved some interference as follows... SmirtDogg has Ruger right where he wants him. Smirt is climbing up the turnbuckle for what could be AirSmirty! SmirtDogg is ready. Wait! Someone is coming out of the crowd, it's Jack Reed! Jack clips out the leg of Smirt Dogg and Smirt gets placed in a bad position on the turnbuckle. Doctor Lander is bothering the ref, he isn't looking! Jack Reed is up. Top Rope FrankernSteiner. Jack slides out of the ring. Ruger picks up SmirtDogg. Spine Buster! From there, Ruger dragged Smirt to touch three turnbuckles, but Smirt stopped him on the fourth. Smirt had a pair of brass knuckles in his tights, and nailed Ruger with them as he attempted to touch the fourth turnbuckle. From there, Smirt drug Ruger around, and touched all the turnbuckles. SmirtDogg d. Ruger Ice. Non-Title Inferno Match Kane (making his return to EWA action, w/ Paul Bearer) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) These two put on one hell of a match. Bunda's foot was nearly signed by the fire when he attempted a top rope shoulderblock, as the flames chose that point to spurt up. However, Bunda jumped in the nick of time. The match went on like this, with each person nearly missing being enflamed, until suddenly, "Better Than You" by Metallica blared over the PA. Erik Draven then stood at the ring entrance, taunting Kane. Kane stared, almost curiously at Draven, which caused Bunda to come from behind and knock Kane into the fire. With one arm on fire, Kane stomped toward Draven, as the camera faded to black. Jeff Bunda d. Kane with interference (?) from Erik Draven. I apologize for the weakness of these results...my mom and her boyfriend were in a three-car wreck today, and my mind isn't really on results.
