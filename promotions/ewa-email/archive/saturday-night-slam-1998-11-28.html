EWA Saturday Night Slam

28 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 28, 1998 Live, from The Ice Palace Tampa, Florida Joe Lemon v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) Surprisingly, KG got a warm reception from his hometown crowd in the Ice Palace. Lemon was attempting to ruin his homecoming party, though, and secure his second EWA victory...but alas, it was not to be, for KG pummeled the young Lemon into submission, actually defeating him with a Figure Four leglock. KG d. Joe Lemon by submission with the Figure Four. Morbid Angel v. "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ D-Generation X) Surpisingly enough, Nash didn't show up. However, the often-absent Morbid Angel didn't either. President Gates issued a final statement...if Morbid Angel ever no-shows again, he will be banned permanently from the EWA. EWA World Television Championship Match Galaxy v. The Rock (EWA World Television Champion) Galaxy did something tonight he hadn't done in quite awhile...he actually put in some effort into his match. However, the effort he put in would prove to be ineffective, as The Rock seemed to have a spark lit under him tonight. Galaxy lit him up with a few high flying moves at the start, but all it took was the Maivia Hurricane (spinning DDT), and The Rock was able to gain control, first with the People's Elbow, and then the Rock Bottom. The Rock d. Galaxy with The Rock Bottom. The Rock remains EWA World Television Champion. Mankind (w/ Mr. Socko) v. "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin Well, it was announced that Foley had left the EWA. However, the new EWA commish The Prince wasn't about to let Conklin get a free ride, as he sent out The Widow Maker to face Conklin. Conklin took all of two minutes in disposing of the Widow Maker, en route to an easy win. Terry Conklin d. The Widow Maker with the Overhand Right. Vic Deakins v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) For the second straight event in a row, Deakins put forth a pathetic display of effort, and as a result, Mr. Every Night absolutely demolished him, almost to the point of humilation. Hicks gained an easy submission victory in this one. Justin Hicks d. Vic Deakins by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Special Ordered Tag Team No Disqualification Match "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner & "The Extremist" Erik Moon v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels Well, this was a dandy, folks. Helmsley and Michaels stayed as far apart as possible during this one. Gardner and Michaels started off, and immediately the fans were treated to a fast-paced encounter. At one point, Gardner whipped Michaels into the ropes, and Helmsley reached out, forcing a tag. Helmsley came in, and worked over Gardner a little, until he was able to tag in Moon. The Extremist then isolated Helmsley in the center of the ring, working on him with a variety of quick manuvers. However, Helmsley was able to counter a Moon powerslam by shoving him into the turnbuckle. As both men struggled to get to their feet, Gardner took off for a chair. The referee climbed to the outside, telling Gardner to get back in the ring...while this was happening, Michaels climbed in, and began tuning up the band. Michaels went to give Helmsley the Sweet Chin Music, but Triple-H ducked, and Michaels nailed Moon. Helmsley, being the opportunist, quickly covered Moon, and got the three count. Immediately afterward, Michaels attacked Helmsley, booting him in the chair. Moon joined in, and then Gardner nailed Michaels from behind with the aforementioned chair. The Rock then ran to the ring, and he began working over Michaels and Helmsley as well. Eventually, Draven and Nash ran to the ring, and cleared the three out. Shawn Michaels & Triple-H d. The Revolution with Triple-H's pin on Erik Moon after Shawn Michaels' Sweet Chin Music. Van Smack (w/ Jim Rome) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) Van Smack...well...he got smacked down. Plain and simple. The ICON took no time at all in taking Van Smack down, extracting some 'revenge' from the old AEWF by finishing him off with an older finisher, the Hangman's Joke. Erik Draven d. Van Smack with the Hangman's Joke. *Up to this point the EWA fans have been subjected to an outstanding edition of Saturday Night Slam. Everyone is waiting with great anticipation for the Kindred vs. Modest/T. Richards match to get underway. The winner of the bout will be the #1 contenders to the EWA World Tag Team Titles. To everyone's shock instead of one of these team's entrance music playing -- another rather familar selection blares over the P.A. system of the Ice Palace. It takes the people in attendance all of 3 seconds to recognize the music as belonging to the former 3-time World Tag Team Champions -- HARLEM HEAT. (side note: the entrance music is equivalent to what Booker T uses now in WCW) After a minute or so -- to everyone's disbelief -- Booker T and Stevie Ray come out from behind the curtains. They're both wearing their usual "Harlem Heat" attire. You know what that is by now. As they walk down the aisle -- Booker T motions that he's going to "raise the roof" while Stevie Ray looks like he's about to knock someone out. The duo enters the squared circle. Booker T demands the microphone from the ring attendant and then begins to speak in front of the astonished crowd.* Booker T: You all look like you've seen a ghost. I suppose HARLEM HEAT is the last tag team you expected to come on out here. The last time you saw us together my brotha and I were viciously fighting with one another. But as you can see there has been a reconciliation and because of that HARLEM HEAT is back and ready to torch any tag team in our path towards the coveted EWA World Tag Team Titles. My brotha and I are gonna turn the EWA up to 100,000 HARLEM HEAT degrees and burn this mother *an intentional pause* down! Now tell me can you dig it suckas?? *Booker T tosses the microphone over to his brother Stevie Ray* Stevie Ray: Just like my brotha was saying -- HARLEM HEAT is back in business and ready to prove ourselves to the wrestling world -- one more time. If it wasn't for President Gates calling our crib every day constantly whining on how only HARLEM HEAT can save his tag team division -- we wouldn't be here in this ring right now. So you can all thank Gates for making this happen. HARLEM HEAT are 3-time EWA World Tag Team Champions and we have our eyes set on attaining a 4th World Tag Team Title. Who's going to put out the ever burning inferno type fires of Harlem Heat??? The Kindred?? Yeah, right. The combination of Michael Modest and Thorne Richards?? Maybe in the year 2...err 3000! *Stevie Ray laughs* Or how about the current EWA Tag Team Champions -- The Extreme Dream Machine??? Hell, not even Bunda and Hicks are on HARLEM HEAT's level. We'll scorch them suckas beyond recognition. Charcoal barbeque -- have 'em looking like the chips. No doubt. *Stevie Ray flips the microphone back over to Booker T* Booker T: Now as you can see our days running with SmirtDogg are over. It's time that HARLEM HEAT got back to their roots and starts showing everyone what being from 110 Street in Harlem, New York City is all about. Ghetto Warriors are a thing of the past but HARLEM HEAT we'll forever shine bright. My brotha and I are ready for the opportunity to knock some suckas out -- O.G. style. We'll take on anyone. This is an open challenge for any tag team in the EWA that wants to feel the 3rd degree burns and mental scars that only the HEAT can supply. So yeah, it's official, not only are HARLEM HEAT back but we're here for good. Just pave our road to the EWA World Tag Team Titles with gold because we're gonna reclaim them. Once again HARLEM HEAT shall prevail and be on top of the wrestling world. *Booker T tosses the microphone over to Stevie Ray* Stevie Ray: There's a few of you out there who may doubt where my brotha and I still operate efficiently and effectively enough to be considered "World Championship" caliber. The answer is yes. But if you don't believe me just keep a close eye on HARLEM HEAT as we ascend up the tag team ranks in a convincing -- dominating -- fashion. We've just filled a huge void in the EWA tag team division. Hopefully it can return to its glory days when Bloodsport, Public Enemy, and the Eliminators were all representing. HARLEM HEAT shall carry the tag team banner and restore credibility to the EWA's tag team division. We're gonna make it what it was -- plus a whole lot more. HARLEM HEAT'S precense was needed and is now gonna be felt in a huge way. Needless to say... *Stevie Ray flips the microphone over to Booker T* Booker T: THE HEAT IS ON!!! Winner will get World Tag Team Title Match at Wednesday Night War "The Natural One" Michael Modest & Thorne Richards v. The Kindred (Gangrel & Edge, w/ Devon) Now this one was a dandy. Thorne and Modest laid it all on the line tonight, as did the duo from hell, and the fans were treated to an outstanding match. Thorne was able to secure the early advantage for his team, isolating Edge in the center of the ring with a series of wear down holds, not to mention a few cast shots when the referee was distracted by Modest. Modest was able to tag in, and continue the isolation. Eventually, Gangrel got sick of it, and came in the ring, but was cut off by Thorne. At this point, the match pretty much got out of hand, and that's when The Kindred took over. Edge was able to fight Modest off, and deliver a crushing Superplex onto Modest, while Christian and Gangrel double teamed Thorne on the outside. From there, it was Edge tossing Modest into the ropes, kicking him in the stomach, and delivering the Downward Spiral. The Kindred d. Michael Modest/Thorne Richards with Edge's pin on Modest after the Downward Spiral. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) This match had developed quite a bit of hype over the past few days. Not many people expected Ruger Ice to make it to the finals, but he was able to defeat both Prodigy and Thorne Richards, two legends in our sport. Could he continue the streak, and live up to the name 'The Upset Specialist'? For the first few minutes, it appeared he had no chance at all, as Jackson absolutely demolished him, slowly and methodically. But amazingly, Ruger was able to keep his senses about him, and was able to duck a lariat by Jackson. And from there, the crowd response built as Ruger began to lay into Jackson with lefts and rights, concluding the barrage with a feat never before accomplished in the EWA; picking Jackson up, and slamming him down. However, Jackson got right back up, and set Ice up for a powerbomb...which Ruger promptly reversed into a back body drop. Jackson popped back up again, and the frustration was evident on Ruger's face. Ruger went for another slam, but Jackson blocked it, and nailed a vicious powerbomb. Ruger was able to kick out at the absolute last second, though, to the amazement of 21,000 in attendance. Jackson picked Ruger back up, threw him into the ropes, and ran at him with a lariat...however, Ruger responded by putting on the brakes, and incredibly, nailing the Spinebuster! Ruger immediately went for the cover...he got 1...he got 2...and he got 3! Without a doubt, the most impressive victory in Ruger Ice's career, as he takes home the Intercontinental strap. Ruger Ice d. Vincent Jackson with the Spinebuster. Ruger Ice is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion! This was, without a doubt, one of the better EWA Championship matches we've seen in the EWA's 11 month history. Both men left it all out in the Ice Palace tonight, and both deserve the moniker of EWA Champion. However, only one champion could be crowned tonight. President Gates made the announcement before the match that the loser would get another opportunity to vie for the championship on December 30. This seemed to pump the crowd up even more, as the participants made their entrances. Once both men entered the ring, the match began, with The Golden One quickly applying a side headlock onto Stevie. Stevie fought to send TGO into the ropes, but The Golden One held on strong, and added some pressure. The Golden One added a couple of fists in, and kept the headlock on until Stevie picked him up into a large side suplex. Stevie immediately went for the cover, but TGO kicked out after a one count. Stevie quickly picked The Golden One up, and delivered a kick to the stomach, following it with a swinging neckbreaker and another attempted pin, again failing to get the three count. Stevie picked The Golden One up, and went for a powerslam, but TGO slipped off of Stevie's shoulders, and clipped him right in the back of the knee. The Golden One, seeing an opening, immediately began focusing his attention on the injured knee, with a variety of submission holds onto the knee. The Golden One locked on a Figure Four, sending Stevie into screams of pain. The referee asked Stevie if he was ready to submit, but Stevie refused. Stevie's shoulders fell to the mat, and the referee counted him down, but only for a two count. Stevie kept straining and clawing, and was finally able to reach the rope and grab ahold. The Golden One, unfazed by Stevie's reach of the rope, walked over and picked him up. Amazingly, Stevie was able to roll The Golden One up in a small package.... Kickout! Stevie obviously thought he had the match won, and as he struggled to his feet, began to argue with the referee. The Golden One, seeing the opportunity, ran at him with a lariat, but Stevie ducked, and nailed the Stevie Kick! He then went for a cover... Amazingly, The Golden One kicked out of the Stevie Kick! IN frustration, Stevie threw the Golden One to the outside, and here it got ugly quick. The Golden One was waiting on the outside, and whipped Stevie into the guardrail. Stevie attempted to run back with a lariat, but The Golden One picked him up, and nailed a spinebuster right onto the Israeli broadcast table, breaking it in half. Both men laid there, semi-unconscious...however, the referee did not make a count, allowing the match to continue. Both men eventually struggled to their feet, and continued the fight until Stevie was thrown back in the ring. From there, The Golden One whipped Stevie into the ropes, but Stevie ducked a clothesline, and hit The Golden One with a flying crossbody. He than went to the corner, and began calling for the Stevie Kick. Richards went for the Stevie Kick, but The Golden One ducked it. The Golden One then went for a lariat, but it was also ducked, and turned into a dropkick by Stevie, knocking The Golden One into the turnbuckle. Stevie charged The Golden One, but the Golden One moved out of the way, and Stevie hit sternum first. The Golden One then rolled Stevie up from behind... The Golden One was visibly angry with the referee, feeling he should have gotten the victory there. However, he would not let that distract him, as he picked Stevie up, and sent him flying back into the turnbuckle. The Golden One charged the turnbuckle, but amazingly, Stevie was able to propel both feet up, and kick The Golden One squarely in the jaw. Stevie then hopped to the top rope, and nailed a missle dropkick. The crowd, getting behind Stevie, began to rally as he once again called for the Stevie Kick. This time, however, The Golden One caught Stevie's foot, and ducked the ensuing enziguri attempt by Richards. The Golden One then actually went for a Sharpshooter, but it was reversed by Stevie into a small package... 2.5!! KICKOUT!! The Golden One immediately hopped back up, and hooked Stevie for a vertical suplex. However, Stevie flipped out of it, and when The Golden One turned around, Stevie again nailed the Stevie Kick! Again, Stevie went for the cover... 1! 2! 2.5! 2.75! 3!!! Stevie, unable to move, was handed the World Title belt, which Erik Draven had handed over earlier in the night. As both men struggled to their feet, The Golden One quickly grabbed the title away. The crowd waited, breathless...and then exploded as The Golden One offered his hand. The two men shook hands, and Stevie's music blared over the PA as the camera faded to black.
