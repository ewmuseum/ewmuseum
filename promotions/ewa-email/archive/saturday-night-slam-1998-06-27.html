EWA Saturday Night Slam

27 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 27, 1998 Live, from The Superdome, New Orleans, Louisiana. Thor (w/ Valhalla) v. The Widow Maker EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ The Horsemen) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) Slinger's TV Title WILL be on the line. Balder (w/ Valhalla) v. Joe Lemon Chris Craft & Zuliel v. Ragnarok (w/ Valhalla) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match James Mastin v. "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre (w/ The Horsemen) "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Legendary" Brett Barnes (w/ "Sexy" Kim) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Masked Terror v. Lee Tenn Shane Adams v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim/Foxy Brown) Meanie Madhouse v. Vertigo Jack Reed v. Ruger Ice Two Smooth v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion) & "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) The Showstoppers (KG/Shawn Michaels, w/ The Horsemen) v. The Black Angels) Darkness v. 2 Cold Scorpio & "The Brown Bomber" Robert Thompson (w/ NQ2) EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Match Stars And Stripes (w/ Madusa) v. The Eliminators (EWA World/U.S. Tag Team Champions) Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) v. Johnny 2 Bad EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Submission Match Prodigy v. The Wyldsyde EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Battle Royal For The New EWA Canadian Championship FEATURING: Makaveli Vic Deakins "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Jack "The Man" McCammon Mayne Mann ./~ The camera pans into an EWA's Saturday Night Slam. Standing in the middle of the ring is the evenings Ring Announcer who is poised to introduce the opening contest of the evenings entertainment ~\. Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen the EWA wrestlers, executives and backstage team would like to thank each and everyone one of you for turning up and buying tickets and making this such and enjoyable night...Thank you! (crowd cheer) Now with that in mind lets go to this evenings first match... ./~ Before the Ring Announcer can finish his introductions the lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to cheer. Soon a green hue falls across the arena and an orange spotlight focuses on the entrance way. After a few seconds the Irish National Anthem -- Soldiers Song -- blares out over the Public Address and the crowd give a mixed response as the Irish Republican Killer steps out from the entrance way wearing black wrestling tights with green, white and orange lettering reading "IRK" down the side of each leg. On his top the Killer is wearing a black leather jacket with an Irish tricolour flag on the back and the words "Premium of Professional Wrestling" placed underneath. The Killer stands on the top of the ramp and slowly looks around the capacity crowd before strutting down to the ring with a very cocky and arrogant grin stretched across his face. When he eventually reaches the squared circle he rolls under the bottom rope and throws his left hand up in the air -- much to the delight of 50% of the crowd and in the disgust of the other 50% -- as he pushed his arm towards the sky green fireworks exploded all over the arena causing a dramatic scene. The lights flick back into normal and the music cuts off and then the IRK lifts the mike to his mouth ~\. "Master Technician" Irish Republican Killer : Well look who's took the ride to the majors. That's right folks Irelands gift to the world of Professional Wrestling the one, the only, Irish Republican Killer - good ol' IRK - has finally arrived in the number one fed out there the EWA! But now that I've finally got here I'm just looking so damned forward to stepping into the ring here for the first time and teaching some IRK wannabe on the roster a grade one wrestling lesson. You see me, being a man of courage, a man of tremendous undoubted wrestling powers and ability didn't make the journey from Ireland to the top dog in professional wrestling today to be just another name on the roster. I don't want to get lost in the pack of talent in the EWA and I'll promise you I won't get caught up in the sea of normal wrestlers and nearly men. When you check out your weekly card, whether it be Wednesday Night Warzone or here at Saturday Night Slam, one thing will be constant. See where it says "Main Event" in big bold letters? Each and every time you scroll your eyes a little down the page at the match it will feature the Top Cat in this biz, the IRK and you can bet your last cent on that. Now I've had people ask me, "Why did you come to the EWA?" and now I give my answer. I came here for one reason and one reason only and that is for the gold and all the things that the titles bring with them. That's right folks, the IRK, is here for the titles, US Title, TV Title, Light Heavyweight Title and the title -- the EWA World Heavyweight Title are all what I seek. So girls if ya hold one of those belts you'd better watch your waist cause the IRK is soon gonna have the gold strapped right around his very very shortly. But I know that all the little Killer's gathered in this arena * mixed reaction * and all the IRK fans from Belfast to New York are wanting to see my lay down the law to the top guns in this federation and take the Heavyweight Championship of the World * cheers but drowned out by boos * But things just don't work like that. You see at the moment the Irish Republican Killer has an injury. Yes, the years of wear and tear on this knee * points to right knee * have taken there toll. So I hate to disappoint you guys but it is still another fortnight till the IRK will be in prime physical condition to step into the ring. But during my time off I've kept in shape -- but -- I've lost 4 lbs and I'm now down to only 234 lbs and President Gates has asked me to become a Light Heavyweight -- well Gates, you are giving an injured man another chance of reaching to the top so -- yeah, I'll do that for ya. * crowd boo * So Aaron Martin over the next few weeks I'll build my way up the ranking's to the number one contender position for the EWA Light Heavyweight Title and when I do you can say bye bye because the IRK will claim that gold and become the best Light Heavyweight Champ that the EWA has ever seen. Now last week I was with a friend and I was down the gym pumping some iron, working on the mats and basically just training ever so hard to be in prime physical condition for my EWA appearance. But you know, the Killer is a trainaholic - well I'd have to be to have a body like this - but then a good friend of mine Seamus O'Toole from County Cork came up to me and asked if I wanted to go down to the Derry Inn for a couple of cold bears and a game of darts but I replied saying..."Sorry Seamus I'd love to but you know how it is...if ya want the gold ya gotta work for it." Well this statement pissed Seamus off and he turned around and yelled..."Killer, you take this title stuff way to seriously...to you the belts mean life and death." To say that angered me would be an understatement, titles, prestige, leer jets and all other things that come from being champ are more important than life and death! Now for the last couple of weeks the IRK has been taking a close watch over all the superstars in the EWA and I have noticed that there is talent. Shawn Michaels, Prodigy, Jack Reed, Ric Flair, Smirt Dogg, Triple Threat and the list goes on and on. These guys can wrestle and what's more they can wrestle good. But sorry to say lads I wouldn't say that I myself am a good wrestler...I am a great, magnificent, superb, dynamic wrestler who is just on that plateau of greatness that all of you aim to be at. Now I know all the little Republican's in the crowd are all up singing and dancing for the IRK and I like that but all my fans know what the IRK is like when he squares off with some chump in the ring. It don't matter who you are whether your Window Maker or whether your Smirt Dogg and it don't matter whether your slim and tall or fat and small because when you step through those ropes to face the Ratings Winner, the Premier Star of EWA, the True Superstar of Wrestling today, the Master of Technical Wrestling, the Premium of Professional Wrestling and a -- soon to be -- title holder in EWA the result is always the same without exemption and I'll tell you what it is. By hook or by crook the IRK will win with the most feared submission move in the world today -- no it's not the Figure 4, Thug Breaker? Nope again. It's the Killer Leg Lock! Yes ladies and gentlemen the IRK don't care who it is because one by one the IRK will move down the EWA roster taking care of business submission after submission after submission until he reaches the Light Heavyweight Champion and then the pattern will continue. Yes my friends I always come out on top and if you don't just love that you just had better learn to live with it because you will have to endure a long, long spell of Irish Republican Killer domination in the EWA! Yes, yes, yes...I know that there are people in the EWA that are bigger than me, stronger than me, quicker than me but there ain't nobody with as much up here * points to head * as the Killer. I've won title after title, Royal Rumbles, you name it I've done it and it's all down to what's up there. As a great man once said, "Wrestling is for the smart, not the strong." And being that great man, it's damn true. So EWA your worst nightmare has occurred, the IRK has hit the scene and soon he'll be hitting on your doors coming for the gold so prepare to lose, prepare to be beaten and prepare to be... Crowd: KILLED BY A KILLER ./~ The "Soldiers Song" once again belts out across the arena as the Irish Republican Killer stands on the middle turnbuckle and points to his waist as the scene fades out to black ~\. Thor (w/ Valhalla) v. The Widow Maker The opening contest at Saturday Night Slam saw Thor completely bulldoze Widow Maker, crushing him in a matter of minutes. Thor d. The Widow Maker with Thor's Hammer. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ The Horsemen) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) Slinger's TV Title WILL be on the line. It was announced that The Gunslinger was not at the arena. The MegaTron above the entrance ramp then came on. (The camera fades in, and we see The Gunslinger sitting in a desert. Approaching him is President Gates. Gates walks up to him and says the following.) Gates: The only time you show up for a match is if it is for a title, and then you stay home the rest of the time. You always do this. You become champion, and then you no-show. Well, the bulls*it ends here, you're fired. (Gates then slaps the hell out of Gunslinger, and walks off.) Will Haynes d. The Gunslinger by forfeit. Will Haynes is the new EWA World Television Champion! Will Haynes gains entrance into The King Of The Ring. Balder (w/ Valhalla) v. Joe Lemon Lemon got, once again, the juice squeezed out of him, as Balder destroyed him. Balder d. Joe Lemon with a gutwrench suplex. Chris Craft & Zuliel v. Ragnarok (w/ Valhalla) Valhalla continued to impress in their opener, as they easily defeated the team of Craft & Zuliel. Ragnarok d. Chris Craft/Zuliel with Tyr's pin on Zuliel after an inside cradle. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match James Mastin v. "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre (w/ The Horsemen) Mastin was pumped up for this contest. He charged the ring, ran straight at Satre...who promptly tripped Mastin. From there, it was a no-brainer. Joe Satre d. James Mastin with The Liftoff. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Legendary" Brett Barnes (w/ "Sexy" Kim) These two men battled for over fifteen minutes, neither gaining a clean advantage, until finally, Barnes executed a German Suplex on Austin. However, the referee counted both men's shoulders down, and issued the match a draw. Steve Austin & Brett Barnes battled to a draw. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Masked Terror v. Lee Tenn Masked Terror didn't even bother to show up, as Tenn gained entrance into The King Of The Ring. Shane Adams v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim/Foxy Brown) True to his word, President Gates brought SmirtDogg a new opponent, in the form of one-time AEWF wrestler Rat Boy. *Before the beginning of his match -- SmirtDogg demands the microphone from Ring Announcer David Pinzer and is given the apparatus. As Smirt stands in the center of the ring with the mic held close to his mouth -- the EWA fans on attendance at the Georgia Dome -- thunderously boo the hell out of him. Smirt doesn't even seem to care. When the negative reaction dies down a bit he begins to speak.* SmirtDogg: Yeah, yeah you all can boo me as much as you want -- right about now I'm not concerned in the least about you losers. Screw you all. I'm not concerned about him *points across the ring to Rat Boy* either. Fact is -- nobody including myself -- is looking forward to a SmirtDogg/Rat Boy match because it's total domination -- one sided affair -- in my favor. Rat Boy is a waste of mine and your time. So Rat -- you're free to go. Leave now. Consider this your lucky day. *Smirt directs Rat Boy to exit the ring. Rat Boy hesistantly steps through the ropes and stands on the outside apron. Smirt is satisfied by this and continues to speak.* Now that I've cleared up a spot as for whom my opponent will be -- it's time to get down to business. I'm going to get straight to the point. KG -- you been really agitating me lately like a little gnat that just won't leave me alone. You open your mouth and all that comes out is disparaging remarks about me. You're talking a lot of nonsense for someone that can't back any of it up. KG -- I'm gonna call you on everything you been saying lately. What I'm gonna do is call your ass out here right now and lets get it on. You and me. You want your shot at me. You want to prove that the feeble apprentice is better than the strong and powerful master -- "God's Gift to Wrestling" if you will. KG -- here's your opportunity to take down the man that made you what you are today. Come out here you coward and face me -- don't run scared like you been doing all your life. I'm waiting KG! Heh, you'll probably send that bimbo Sunny out in your place. You never did have any courage. KG -- you are a pathetic piece of.... *KG appears from out behind the curtains and slowly makes his way down the aisle* SmirtDogg: I can't believe it. The rat actually came out of his hole. Bravo! *proceeds to give a golf clap* *KG suddenly stops -- throws his arms down in disgust and turns around to head back to the locker room.* SmirtDogg: Where the hell do you think you're going. I want a piece of you here and now. Damn -- you're a coward. Come on KG -- prove for once that you're not always a b*tch. *KG continues to walk away but Smirt has no clue that Rat Boy has re-entered the ring and is sneaking up behind him. Rat Boy hits SmirtDogg from behind, Smirt drops the mic, and the match immediately starts* Rat Boy was eating slices of cheese on the way to the ring, and Smirt promptly destroyed him in under two minutes. SmirtDogg d. Rat Boy with The Gangsta's Paradise. Meanie Madhouse v. Vertigo Meanie Madhouse showed little offense during this contest, as they didn't seem to have their full heads in the game. As a result, Vertigo picks up another win. Vertigo d. Meanie Madhouse with Heavy Metal Maniac's pin on Blue Meanie after The Arm Of Stone. Jack Reed v. Ruger Ice Ruger started this match out on fire, dominating Jack Reed completely. However, when Ruger tried to knock Dr. Lander off of the ringapron, Lander rang his bell -- and Crazy Eddie was unleashed. Eddie dominated Ruger, finally ending the match with a Boston Crab. Jack Reed d. Ruger Ice with Crazy Eddie's submission win after a Boston Crab. Two Smooth v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (EWA European Champion) & "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Two Smooth looked fairly good in their EWA debut, but the Triple Threat representatives were simply too strong, taking Two Smooth out methodically. Justin Hicks/Stevie Richards d. Two Smooth after Justin Hicks' submission victory over Terry Conklin with the Saturday Night Special. The Showstoppers (KG/Shawn Michaels, w/ The Horsemen) v. The Black Angels) Michaels and KG looked impressive for the time that the match went on. However, SmirtDogg soon ran out, and blasted KG in the head, followed by Ken Shamrock, who suplexed Michaels and placed him in the Ankle Submission. From there, Smirt and Shamrock backed into each other and turned around, having a long staredown before finally leaving the ring. The Showstoppers d. The Black Angels by disqualification. Darkness v. 2 Cold Scorpio & "The Brown Bomber" Robert Thompson (w/ NQ2) Darkness turned heads in their tag team debut, giving the newly renamed team Affirmitive Action a solid match. However, in the end, the young duo simply overcame Darkness. Affirmitive Action d. Darkness with 2 Cold Scorpio's pin on Lucien after the 2 Cold Splash. EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Match Stars And Stripes (w/ Madusa) v. The Eliminators (EWA World/U.S. Tag Team Champions) This was one of the better tag team matches in recent history, as neither team showed a distinct advantage in the entire matchup. However, the end came for the patriotic duo when a shocking thing happened. Out of nowhere, The Patriot began attacking Shamrock. The Eliminators simply stopped and stared, watching Patriot do a number on Shamrock. Finally, The Patriot executed a standing side-kick on Shamrock, and exited the ring. Saturn then simply pinned Shamrock. On the outside, The Patriot took off his mask...to reveal "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels, disguised as The Patriot! The Eliminators d. Stars And Stripes with interference. The Eliminators retain the EWA U.S. Tag Team Titles. Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) v. Johnny 2 Bad Castor looked good in this matchup, dominating the first portion of Johnny 2 Bad's debut match. However, Johnny showed why he advanced to the Sweet Sixteen of the IWCT, and eventually overcame "The Mastermind" with the Double Hammer. Johnny 2 Bad d. Castor Troy with The Double Hammer. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) One of the best light heavyweight matches in recent history saw neither man gaining a clear-cut advantage. Both men fought, back and forth, for over seventeen minutes. Finally, Martin picked Copeland up for the Shoulderbreaker, but Copeland slipped out and shoved Martin into the turnbuckle, which appeared to knock him out cold and split his forehead open. Copeland quickly climbed to the top rope, executed the Money Shot, and got the three count. Shawn Copeland d. Aaron Martin with the Money Shot. Shawn Copeland is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! Submission Match Prodigy v. The Wyldsyde Due to prior commitments, the match didn't take place. {Dirty Day begins playing over the loudspeakers, and the crowd begins to boo. From the back comes Wyldsyde, making his way down the aisle slowly, with a folding chair in one hand, and a rather large book, along with a microphone, in the other. He's wearing his brown suede jacket, of course, and his brown singlet, as though he was prepared to wrestle. People are booing loudly, and many signs protest Wyldsyde's appearance not only in this match, but in the EWA at all! Wyldsyde, nearly to the ring, takes the time to look and act disgusted at the fans near ringside, who gladly let him know just how they feel about him. He, on the other hand, adjusts his brown suede jacket, and goes through the ropes into the center of the ring. Slowly, he sets up the folding chair (which is actually padded), sits down, and lifts the microphone to his face. He begins to speak.} It looks like The Wyldsyde needs to spread a little intelligence to Louisiana...you heathens look like you could use it! {The crowd boos loudly at this, and Wyldsyde looks absolutely disgusted. He lets the boos fade a bit, then begins to speak again.} And that local sports team...depressing, truly. But The Wyldsyde has a tendancy to digress. Now, The Wyldsyde was scheduled for a match tonight with a man that many claim to be quite a wrestler...The Wyldsyde hasn't had all that much experience with him. A man named Prodigy. Now, The Wyldsyde has heard a lot about this man, certainly, as all the magazines love to cover his exploits, and the such, but The Wyldsyde doesn't know an enormous amount about him as a wrestler...his style, his moves...but now, The Wyldsyde won't get a chance to test The Wyldsyde's skills, for some unknown reason. So, The Wyldsyde has decided to project some wisdom into this house of morons. {Once again, the Superdome erupts into a cacaphony of boos, and Wyldsyde looks around disgustedly once again, waiting for the boos to die down.} Unfortunately, The Wyldsyde is never going to finish if you idiots keep this up! {This REALLY prompts the audience to boo, and they do! Small chants of "Go home Wyldsyde" break out as many audience members try to boo as long as they can, in hopes of forcing Wyldsyde out of the ring. Unfortunately, it's to no avail. Wyldsyde raises the microphone to his mouth, and begins to speak yet again.} Ah, you may want The Wyldsyde gone, but The Wyldsyde has yet more to say. The Wyldsyde is not all that disappointed about Prodigy not showing up, honestly...that is no skin off of The Wyldsyde's back! But, the one thing that has been bothering The Wyldsyde more than anything as of late is an appearance by a certain man in the EWA. Now, The Wyldsyde thought that The Wyldsyde was rid of that man long ago. But now he's back, and apparently, he's making more trouble than he ever was. Johnny 2 Bad. A man who The Wyldsyde has fought time and time again, is here. And he is creating one of the most horrid of all things...a stable. {Wyldsyde visibly shudders at just the mention of the word stable. The crowd is still a bit uproarious, partially from The Wyldsyde's verbal berating, and partially from the mention of Johnny 2 Bad's name. Wyldsyde composes himself, and again begins to speak.} Now, Johnny, some have said that you have been vocally against joining this stable. In many places, The Wyldsyde had heard rumors that had not come true, and now it has. The Chain Gang is upon the EWA. That is unbelieveably bad news for the world of professional wrestling. Now, of course, as you know, you and The Wyldsyde have had some differences in the past. Some very, very different differences. And, of course, The Wyldsyde has always come out on top. But, The Wyldsyde is not sitting in this ring to challenge you, or to perhaps ask for assistance, or even to ask to be a part of this debacle...The Wyldsyde is here to warn you. The Wyldsyde doesn't want this group of unintelligent scragglers doing anything that would harm, bother, or in any way interfere with The Wyldsyde's doings. The Wyldsyde's got enough on The Wyldsyde's mind, preparing for some of the biggest fights in The Wyldsyde's career, now that The Wyldsyde has become a big name star in the EWA, The Wyldsyde truly doesn't need you, Johnny, and your little friends, to show hide nor hair in The Wyldsyde's business. Remember that warning, Johnny...because any breach of said warning shall result in some major pain handed out by The Wyldsyde, along with a major wrestling lesson. Remember, Johnny, The Wyldsyde put you in the hospital when you got in too deep, The Wyldsyde shall do it again if need be. Oh, and by the way, if any of you idiots out there, including you, Johnny, are wondering what this thing The Wyldsyde's holding is, it's a book. Perchance you should learn to read one sometime, it may improve your life. Or, perhaps not. End of story. {With that, Wyldsyde folds up his chair, and carries it out of the ring. As he slowly walks back down the aisle, the boos are louder than before, and the crowd is visibly angry at Wyldsyde. Wyldsyde stops at the top of the entrance ramp, turns back, gives one last disgusted shake of his head, then disappears into the back.} EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) What was billed as a matchup between the stars of yesterday and today turned out to be a solid match. At the onset, Thorne countered every move that Bunda attempted, frustrating the World Champion. Thorne took the advantage with a well-executed standing figure four, but Bunda was able to reach the ropes. However, Bunda soon took the advantage back, and finally nailed the Showrunner for a hard-fought victory. Jeff Bunda d. Thorne Richards with the Showrunner. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship. Battle Royal For The New EWA Canadian Championship FEATURING: Makaveli Vic Deakins "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Jack "The Man" McCammon Mayne Mann Flair didn't show up, so this turned into a four man battle royal. Mayne Mann was the first to go, being eliminated by way of a dropkick from "The Untouchable" Makaveli. However, Rufus Lamar Jackson immediately showed up on the scene, knocking Makaveli over the top rope. The two then fought to the back, leaving Vic Deakins and Jack McCammon in the ring. The two men dueled without neither gaining the advantage for 10-15 minutes, until finally, Deakins ducked a clothesline attempt by McCammon, turned, and knocked Jack over the top rope. Vic Deakins wins the battle royal. Vic Deakins is the first ever EWA Canadian Champion!!
