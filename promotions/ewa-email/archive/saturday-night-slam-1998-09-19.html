Venom (EWA World Television Champion) The crowd seemed extremely hyped up for this contest, and it turned out that they had good reason to. The crowd was largely behind the challenger, as they have apparently forgiven Richards for his remarks at WrestleCade. Venom came out to a chorus of boos from the capacity crowd. However, Venom's entrance didn't last long, as the King of Swing ran at Venom as he was coming to the ring. The two brawled on the outside for a few moments, with neither getting a clear advantage until Venom brought his size advantage into play, picking up Stevie and dropping him face-first onto the steel guardrail. From here, Venom used the steel steps to his advantage, repeatedly whipping Stevie into them. The match finally got into the ring, and that's when Venom began to lose the advantage. The Extreme Superstar became a little too cocky, and when he whipped Stevie into the ropes, Stevie ducked a lariat attempt, and came back with a baseball slide between Venom's legs. Richards immediately turned around, grabbed the legs of Venom, and tripped him, causing him to land on his face. Stevie then took control with a variety of quick, agile wrestling moves, but Venom regained the advantage with a low blow. From there, while Richards was crouched over, Venom had the presence of mind to nail the Extreme Poison on Stevie, and get a victory. Venom d. Stevie Richards with the Extreme Poison.
