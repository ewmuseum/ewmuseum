EWA Saturday Night Slam (1/2/99) The One Year Anniversery Card!! The EWA's One Year Anniversery Card! January 2, 1999 Live, from the Roy Wilkins Auditorium St. Paul, Minnesota *The camera fades in. Much to the chagrin of everyone on attendance and watching at home the "Dogg Pound" set has been erected. As always the infamous "Dog Cage" is strategically positioned off to a side of the actual "Dogg Pound" stage and is full of Copeland- devouring Doberman Pinchers. "Dogg Pound Gangstaz" by Kurupt and Daz blares over the P.A. system. Curiosity builds as everyone wonders whether Smirt will keep his word or not concerning his promise to pull the plug on the "Dogg Pound". A minute or so passes by and there's no sign of SmirtDogg. "Dogg Pound Gangstaz" fades away as the music comes to a halt. The EWA fans cheer -- happy as hell -- believing that they won't be subjected to SmirtDogg's "Dogg Pound" show anymore. Suddenly to the shock of everyone -- who should appear on the "Dogg Pound" set. Not SmirtDogg but 'The Golden Child'! Yes, you heard me right. 'The Golden Child' is in da house. He takes a seat behind the desk and begins to speak. Everyone is shocked not expecting to see this man.* 'The Golden Child': It's been awhile since you last saw me at an EWA event, eh? Well, let me clarify the situation for you all. I haven't signed a contract to wrestle here in the EWA. Hell, I haven't signed any sort of EWA contract whether it be as a commentator or a front office position. The only reason I'm here is to help out a friend. It appears that Smirt's controversial loss to Sid Jacobs over in the AEWF has set a ticking time bomb off. Nobody knows what to expect from the guy. He's totally unpredictable right now. Smirt was going to let the "Dogg Pound" die tonight but I wouldn't let it happen. So here I am filling in for the "Dogg". The show must go on. *The crowd thunderously boo's as they now realize the "Dogg Pound" isn't quite dead yet.* 'The Golden Child': Why do you all hate SmirtDogg so much? He's the best wrestler you'll ever come across. Everyone should respect him for his outstanding in-ring talent. His self-proclaimed moniker -- "God's Gift to Wrestling" -- is a reality. That's truly what Smirt-D-O-Double G is whether you all want to admit it or not. Yeah, from time to time -- Smirt's "Academy Award Winning Interviews" push the envelope and go too far. However, more often than not Smirt is correct in his comments. It's your problem if you can't accept the facts -- not his. SmirtDogg will be the next EWA World Heavyweight Champion. After getting SCREWED out of the AEWF World Heavyweight Title -- there's no way he'll let this one pass him by. Smirt is obsessed with success and if you know him like I do then you'll understand what I'm saying and why "The Golden One" stands absolutely no chance against "God's Gift to Wrestling". *The crowd begins to chant "TGO, TGO, TGO!* 'The Golden Child': Don't you all mean -- "TKO" because Smirt is going to knock "The Golden One" -- the f*ck out. That's not even in question. What's in question is how long the match will last. I give "The Golden One" 5 minutes tops before he gets his ass pinned and loses that EWA World Heavyweight Title he so cherishes. It shall be a short title reign for "The Golden One". Smirt informed me that he had some really special guests lined up for tonight but he didn't tell me who. So your guess is as good as mine. At this time I'd like whomever Smirt was supposed to interview to come out from the back and let yourselves be known. I'm waiting! *The crowd grows silent as we await the arrival of the guest for tonight's show. But still, no one comes out. 'The Golden Child' begins to tap his fingers on the desk as we await. Just as he begins to speak again, suddenly, a familiar tune begins to fill up the arena, when we realize exactly what it is. It's that infamous tune... "Fed Up" by House of Pain. Could it be???
