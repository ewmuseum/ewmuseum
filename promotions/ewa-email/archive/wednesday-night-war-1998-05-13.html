EWA Wednesday Night War

13 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 13, 1998 Live, from the MGM Grand Hotel & Casino, Las Vegas, Nevada. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna & The Stud Stable) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Non-Title Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebecca Claridge) "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) EWA World Television Championship Match Castor Troy v. KG (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Sunny) KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. The Wyldsyde "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) Four Corners Match The Outsiders v. Harley Hart/Matthew Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) No Holds Barred Weapons Match New Demolition & their manager, Semi v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) & a Mystery Partner EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) v. Thorne Richards Interview with Degeneration X DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! Non-Sanctioned Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin If Austin wins, he will be reinstated into the EWA. Steel Cage Match for the EWA Intercontinental Championship The Golden One v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna & The Stud Stable) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Shawn Hart missed his flight to the arena, and as a result, President Gates scheduled this match for SNS. He also announced that V.P. Harrison Goodman was no longer employed by the EWA. "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) This was a no-contest, as neither man appeared to want to show up. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Helmsley was at vintage form in this one, really impressing the crowd as he took Cummings apart. Triple-H d. Scott Cummings with the Pedigree. (6:19) Non-Title Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebecca Claridge) Flair announced before the match that he was retiring, but he did not have a successful farewell, as Justin Hicks really gave the former World Champion a beating. Justin Hicks d. Ric Flair with a german suplex. (8:02) "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Again, a double no-show... EWA World Television Championship Match Castor Troy v. KG (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Sunny) Castor started off this contest in strong fashion, working KG over and talking smack while doing it. Castor made his mistake when he missed a top-rope legdrop, however, and KG came back, eventually nailing Troy with the Blackout. KG d. Castor Troy with the Blackout. (11:34) KG retains the EWA World Television Championship. KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. The Wyldsyde Boy, was this a lopsided outcome...Koo never got any form of offense going during this one, as The Wyldsyde really looked impressive throughout the match. The Wyldsyde d. KooKabura with The Wyldryde. (5:44) "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) Wright started off with a good offensive flurry, but McCammon quickly took the advantage when Wright began to dance for the crowd, and Jack never gave it back. Jack McCammon d. Alex Wright with the Double Hammer. (9:21) Four Corners Match The Outsiders v. Harley Hart/Matthew Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) Amazingly, none of the involved teams showed up; the match was rescheduled for SNS, with the winner scheduled to recieve a U.S. Tag Team Title shot. No Holds Barred Weapons Match New Demolition & their manager, Semi v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) & a Mystery Partner P.E. and their partner, who turned out to be Shawn Michaels, really pounded New Demolition into oblivion during this one. Public Enemy & Shawn Michaels d. New Demolition with Ghetto Boy's pin on Semi after a blast to the head with a garbage can. (10:28) EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) These two had spectacular matches in the AEWF, and this one was no different. The two teams brawled all over the ringside area for nearly seventeen minutes, until finally, Saturn hit a Death Valley Driver in the ring on Flash Funk. Disco tried to make the save, but came up an inch short. The Eliminators d. FlashBack with Perry Saturn's pin on Flash Funk after a Death Valley Driver. (16:57) "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) v. Thorne Richards Thorne's decline continued, as he was really schooled by Richards in this one. Thorne mounted a small offense with a lowblow, which caused The Wyldsyde to run out from the back. The Wyldsyde proceded to scream at Thorne, and the two quickly began to argue. When Thorne turned around, however, he was then met with the Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards d. Thorne Richards with the Stevie Kick. (13:24) (The camera fades in as we return from a commercial break...) ARE YOU READY??? (All of DX makes their way from behind the curtain...Prodigy in the lead, followed by Michaels, Helmsley, Draven, Public Enemy, and the ladies... Prodigy has the EWA World Title draped over his shoulder, and hits the ring first...He grabs the microphone, tosses it to Triple-H, and begins to play to the crowd.) Triple-H: Ladies and gentlemen...Arrrrre you ready??? I said...ARRRRRRRE YOU READY??? For the thousands here in attendance, and the millions watching at home on live television...LET'S GET READY TO SUCK IT!!!!!!!! (the crowd pops big for this) You know something? I've got it all figured out now. President, if you wanna call him that, Gates has decided to try and break up what is simply the best damn stable here in the EWA, because he is SCARED of us! I mean, he's got Erik Draven lined up against our boss, the real ICON of this sport, the TRUE DeGenerate, Prodigy, at Mind Games. Gates, that's a load of crap, and you know it!!! I guess all the other little 'groups' here in the EWA went up to him, whining and crying about the simple fact that they didn't have the talent to stack up to DX...that they were afraid we'd take all the belts. Waa, waa, waa...Too bad, so *BLEEP*ing sad, chumps. DX is here to stay...deal with it. (HHH and Michaels climb the turnbuckles to play the crowd a bit more...Draven takes the microphone.) Erik Draven: First of all, Prodigy, man...I didn't ask for this match. Yeah, I wanted it back before we 'mended our fences', but hey...that's the past, isn't it? I'm more than prepared to take a lower seat on the Pay-Per-View card, and hell...I'll even face a S...a St...(Draven looks pained as he says this) a Stud Stable member, for God's sake...(he shudders) But what it all comes down to is...You're my boy. You're my 'brah'...I don't wanna fight you. We're not going to let DX get taken apart by those suits in the Executive Offices, are we? Drop the good word on these people, brah. (Draven tosses Prodigy the microphone...in the background, Helmsley and Michaels slide out of the ring, grab a couple of folding chairs, and slide back in...Draven's back is to them. Prodigy begins to speak, but Draven cuts him off) Erik Draven: Sorry...forgot something. Prodigy, bro...You're *BLEEEEEEP*ed!!! (Draven hits the mat, and as if on cue, HHH and Michaels BLAST Prodigy with their chairs...He goes down in a heap. The remaining DX'ers laugh hysterically for a few moments, then Draven composes himself enough to finish the interview.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: DAAAAAAMN I've been waiting to do that for AGES!!! That's right, boys and girls...The only TRUE DeGenerates are the ones STANDING in the ring right now!!! Prodigy, did you honestly think I was going to bow down to you? That I was gonna follow you like a damn dog? (he laughs) Think again, chump. We've had this planned for so damn long now, it's not funny! (all laugh) You thought you 'pushed me out' of DX? We set your ass up so bad it ain't funny! And you bought it, hook line and f'ing sinker...(Draven chuckles again) And if you honestly think I'm going to give up the chance at my World Heavyweight Title...bro, two words. Get help. (he grins) And, uh...you may as well send the trauma team out with a spatula...he's gonna need it. (Prodigy starts to get back to his feet...Draven, Michaels, and TPE bail from the ring, but HHH doesn't see it...and ends up on the wrong end of a Firestarter from a VERY p-o'ed Prodigy...DX drags him out of the ring, and high-tail it back to the locker room, Prodigy hot on their heels.) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT! Non-Sanctioned Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin If Austin wins, he will be reinstated into the EWA. Michaels was clearly worn out from his earlier match, and the attack on Prodigy, and The Rattlesnake pounced. Austin was in vintage form, "whipping ass" up and down the ringside area, and polishing the apparently injured Michaels off with a Stone Cold Stunner. Michaels limped to the back afterward. Steve Austin d. Shawn Michaels with the Stone Cold Stunner. (8:35) Steve Austin has been reinstated into the EWA! Steel Cage Match for the EWA Intercontinental Championship The Golden One v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was a hellacious war, and just when The Golden One appeared to be on the verge of winning the match, Justin Hicks scaled the cage, and he and Bunda began a mercifuless assault on TGO, ending with The Apocalypse Drop. Jeff Bunda d. The Golden One with the Apocalypse Drop. (21:20) Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship.
