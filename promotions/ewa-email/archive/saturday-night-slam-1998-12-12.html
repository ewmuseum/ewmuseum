EWA Saturday Night Slam

12 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 12, 1998 Live, from Arena Mexico Mexico City, Mexico The EWA's first event outside of America! The Widow Maker v. Feather (w/ Vixen) Feather was making his EWA debut in this match, and looked very good. The Widow Maker didn't last long at all against him, as he quickly hit the Drop Of A Feather, and obtained the victory. Feather d. The Widow Maker with The Drop Of A Feather. * "Sweetest Thing" by U2 plays as Feather gets to his feat after making poor WindowMaker look like a total bona-fide fool. He walks over to the turnbuckle and gets on the top rope much to the dislike of the capacity and hot crowd at Saturday Night Slam. The ring announcer is ready to announce the decision but before he can Feather pushes him out the way and grabs the microphone * Feather - Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match, the single most superb athlete in the EWA - me - The Feather! * crowd boo * Now before you people get your knickers in a knot, it's time that you people prepare to realise that this is what it's going to be like for a long, long time in the future. Now just to irritate you people further I would like to introduce someone that is gonna be by my side from here on in. So guys prepare your eyes, ladies, prepare to be jealous of this lady and her stunning looks. EWA I give you my manager - VIXEN! * "Sweetest Thing" plays again as a sexy female comes from the curtain wearing a figure hugging mini-dress. The woman is in her early 20's with long red hair and the guys in the crowd go wild as she makes her way to the ring. She gets to the ring, climbs through the ropes and walks over and takes the microphone off Feather as the music cuts. As she prepares to speak the whistles in the crowd reach fever pitch * Vixen - Hi...now all you people please take a long hard look at what you see in front of you! * crowd explodes * Now take a long hard look because * Vixen's tone turns nasty * this is as close as you ugly, fat slimeballs are going to get to me! * Feather is seen laughing * Now boys, it's time for you idiots to realise that to have a girl like me even look at you, you must become more like my man over here * points to Feather * Now whilst all of you people are out at McDonalds stuffing your fat mouths full over burgers Feather is working a way in the gym unless he is otherwise occupied, if you know what I meen! You see that is the reason that he has me on his arm. Not only because of his stunningly good looks but because he's done a lot of work to become the greatest wrestler of all time and with that comes the bonuses such as the millions of dollars sitting in his growing bank balance. You see that is what attracts the ladies not the size of you sickening beer bellies! Now take over babe! * the crowd boo loudly as Feather gets the microphone again * Feather - *laughs Now that put you stupid freaks in your place! But before we leave ya I would like to send out a question to all of these EWA lightheavyweights and that is - have you ever heard the story about the Golden Feather? Well if not you'll be able to see it live, and in living color when I become the lightheavywieght champion of the world! Now one last time hit my music! *"Sweetest Thing" plays as Feather and Vixen depart* *With the break in the action, some of the EWA fans become restless. They decide that this would be the perfect opportunity to use the restroom or buy some refreshments. Man, was their thinking wrong. Off to the side of the wrestler's entrance ramp -- an abundance of EWA crew members arrive on the scene. They begin to erect some sort of production set that you would find on a daytime soap opera or a late night talk show. Curiosity reaches a premium as everyone in attendance looks on in awe -- wondering what all of this is leading up to. Ratings for Saturday Night Slam is already elevating and we don't even know who or what this is all about. Once the crew has finished building the set we see that it definitely possesses a talk show type set-up. It looks alot like Venom's Poison Pit except this set is brighter and in the background it has pictures of various hip hop artists. 2pac, Jay-Z, DMX, Method Man, Redman, Ice Cube, Snoop Doggy Dogg -- just to name a few. A Ford pick-up truck surprises us by speeding from out of the backstage area. It halts adjacent to the just built production set. In the back of the truck -- there's a cage and it contains approximately 6 Doberman Pinchers. This cage is handled with care and dropped next to the talk show set. The crew members disappear and suddenly the following blares from out of the speakers in the arena. * Welcome, we have been expecting you! *voice-over of Pinhead from Hellraiser* [Dr. Dre] Dogg Pound... are you motherf*ckers ready? [Kurupt] Yeah!!! "Like we always do about this time" *gun cocks* *"Dogg Pound Gangstaz" by Kurupt and Daz blasts over the P.A. system and the EWA fans begin vibin' to the beat. Off in the cage -- the Doberman Pinchers are barking up a storm. A close up of the dogs reveals them to be nasty animals. Needless to say you wouldn't want to mess with those dogs. As the song continues to play, our host comes out from behind the curtains. I suppose it was quite obvious who it would be but reality has now struck the fans. They boo at a high pitched volume. Camera pans over to the source of the negativity and that's when we see "God's Gift to Wrestling", SmirtDogg, live and most definitely in color. Smirt is wearing dark black sunglasses, a backwards New York Yankees baseball cap, his own personal black t-shirt with gold print and black windbreakers. Basically, Smirt is just chilling. He arrogantly walks over to the set and sits behind the desk. For once he's without his "Golden Microphone" since there's a mini-mic attached to him. Smirt props his feet up on the desk and begins to speak.* SmirtDogg: What you're witnessing here is history in the making. The first installment of "The Dogg Pound". What I need you all to do is give me a big "BOW WOW WOW!" and get that Arsenio Hall Show "Dogg Pound" chant going. Can you all do that for me or am I asking too much out of you idiots? *The crowd chants 'SmirtDogg SUCKS!' instead of what Smirt desired them to do. Smirt reacts.* SmirtDogg: I see how it is. Screw you all then. You better get used to me hosting this show because it's going to be taking place on a weekly basis. Being a significant part of Duane Gates' crew has its perks as you all can see. *Smirt motions around him -- stressing out the elaborate set which has been constructed purely for his use.* SmirtDogg: Duane Gates has gotten me my very own show. He's positioned me onto the front burner and there's no doubt that Gates will secure me an EWA World Heavyweight Title Shot on or before Mind Games II in January. So the way that I look at it -- being in the Corporate Empire is most beneficial for me, "God's Gift to Wrestling". Now for a quick run-down of how MY show will be operated. Each and every Saturday Night Slam the "Dogg Pound" will be a component of the telecast. I'll come out here and talk whatever sh*t I damn well please. Then, I'll bring out my guest, an EWA superstar, and interview him. I won't pull back any punches. All the controversial questions will be asked and hopefully answered. For this inaugural episode of the "Dogg Pound" I was hoping to have a showdown with "The ICON" *said mockingly* Erik Draven. However, nobody has seen or heard from the ogre ever since he was beaten down to size at Wednesday Night War. Apparently, Draven has fled the country or something. Who knows -- and if so -- good riddance. We can put the EWA World Heavyweight Belt on someone a hell of alot more deserving. *Smirt arrogantly grins* Au Revoir, Erik Draven. My message to you -- Sloth -- is to never come back. *The crowd commences with a pro-Draven chant which Smirt ignores -- pretending not to hear it.* SmirtDogg: Since, Erik Draven is indisposed, unavailable, or on the lamb *Smirt laughs* -- whatever he may be -- I've gotten the next best person to interview for tonight's special debut edition of the "Dogg Pound". This man is currently recognized as the #1 contender to the EWA World Heavyweight Championship. He's been turning alot of heads ever since he made his "Golden" presence felt here in the EWA once again. No, I'm not going to interview myself. Although that would probably draw the highest ratings for this segment. Instead, I want you all to help welcome with me now -- my first guest ever on the "Dogg Pound" -- the one, the only "Shining Star" -- The Golden One!!! Come on everyone give him a nice round of applause. The lights go down and the golden beam shines throughout the arena. 'Golden Showers' plays over the loudspeaker, and to a huge pop from the crowd, The Golden One appears through the curtain, wearing his typical golden attire from head to toe... *The Golden One sits down on the white leather couch which is a part of this newly furnished "Dogg Pound" set. When Smirt sees that the Golden One is ready -- he begins to speak.* SmirtDogg: Golden One, I'd like to thank you for taking the time now -- before your huge Main Event match tonight -- to come out here and be a part of the first ever "Dogg Pound" show. The Golden One: My pleasure Smirt. I've always enjoyed watching you inside the squared circle, and it's an honor to be a part of the debut of your show here. SmirtDogg: Thank you. I'm just returning the favor. Remember when I appeared on your "Golden Hour" show? The Golden One: Of course. It was one of the highest ratings my show ever got. SmirtDogg: Yeah, it was a blast. Now, Golden One, I'm sure there's alot of people who would like to hear about your previous endeavors in pro-wrestling. Would you care to share with us the highlights of your illustrious wrestling career? Your biggest match to date and greatest moment in this sport? The Golden One: Well Smirt, it all began almost a year and a half ago in a small indy fed known as the IFW. I was a fresh, young rookie, with absolutely no wrestling experience whatsoever. I was put in a match against the World Champion, a legend of his time, "Cool Hand" Luke Summers. I beat Luke that night, and so began the legend known as The Golden One. SmirtDogg: Quite an accomplishment for a rookie at the time. The Golden One: I'll say. Anyway, the title reign only lasted two weeks, as a Bad MoFo decided to take the belt away from me, something he did with relative ease. I made a promise to myself that night. A promise to never let that happen to me again. I refused to get punked, and I've lived true to my word for the most part. Within a matter of days, the IFW had severe financial difficulties, and had to close down. At that point, I considered giving up the sport for good, but I had some sound advice from a good friend. SmirtDogg: Let me go thank that friend of yours. You retiring would have been a terrible thing for this sport. Who is this guy? The Golden One: That's beside the point Smirt. The fact of the matter is I got the phone call from this guy, who needed a boost for his own career. So I went to a place called the UWF to help him out and make a new beginning for myself. While there, I started the most elite stable EVER to unite in the history of professional wrestling, The Golden Boys. Unstoppable we were, but relationships were strained quickly, causing the split. At the time, I was devastated. Never before had that much talent ever united, and it was my accomplishment. So when the GB's split, I took it very personally. SmirtDogg: I bet you did. I can imagine. The Golden One: Much to my surprise, however, it seemed to spark my career. I soon became the UWF Television Champion, a title which I held for a record 5 months. After losing, I worked my way to World Title status, finally earning the shot against my best friend. That was the time for me to finally prove myself to the entire world. Unfortunately, those financial problems struck once again, and I was denied of the opportunity to sit on top of the world. SmirtDogg: What do you have to say in response to your critics whom protest that your interviews are boring and have the power to render a widespread portion of the viewing audience into a comatose state? The Golden One: Well, all I can really say is that everyone has their critics. If people want to criticize me for putting the world to sleep, so be it. The fact of the matter is that no matter what the world says, my interviews have been effective for me. Since I started talking and didn't stop, I've been winning matches left and right, working my way all the way to the #1 contender's spot for the most prestigious title in our sport, the EWA World Title. Until something drastic happens, you can bet your bottom dollar that I'll be boring people to death for years to come. SmirtDogg: *he laughs* I hear you. Some would say I have a similar interview style to yours. Tonight, your rivalry with Shawn Copeland will be rekindled as 1/2 of the featured Double Main Event. Are you bold enough to make any pre-match predictions???? I'll go first. Right here and now I proclaim victory over "The Heart-Attack Kid" Shawn Michaels. See how easy that was. *Smirt arrogantly grins* The Golden One: Well, there's no doubt about it Smirt. While I may not possess your arrogance, I certainly do not lack confidence. I wouldn't even be going out on the limb by saying I'm going to beat the living hell out of one, Shawn Copeland. The world knows I can't stand him, and tonight marks the final chapter in the ongoing saga between us. And besides, you know what they say don't you? Payback is a bitch. SmirtDogg: I've been informed that on the 30th of this month you'll be competing against Erik Draven in an EWA World Heavyweight Title Match. Is there anything you want to say in response to this??? What do you personally think of Erik Draven??? Is he a worthy World Champion or undeserving of the title??? The Golden One: Well, though I have absolutely no history to speak of with Erik Draven, I know all I need to know about him. He's the current EWA World Champion. He's got the one thing in this world I want more than anything else right now, so you might say he's my enemy at this point. I beg to differ, however. I have TONS of respect for "The ICON." The man has won the EWA World Heavyweight Title on 4 separate occassions, and that in itself is worth some praise. He's beat everyone there is to beat, won every title there is to win, and led DX to prominence here in the EWA. Is he a worthy World Champion? You're damn right he is. SmirtDogg: Yeah, yeah well you're going to have to change your thought process about Erik Draven if you're to accept the offer I have on the table for you from President Gates. Here's your one and only chance to be a part of the Corporation. Gates wants you in and so do I. So what do you say Golden One -- are you down with us???? We all have a common enemy and that would be Erik Draven. Whether you respect him or not you still have to wrestle him for the World Title. Wouldn't you want some support, bro??? The Golden One: Support from the Corporate Empire, huh? Is that what you're offering me Smirt? Let me say this once, and only once. I will NEVER stand side by side with Duane Gates BRO. Whether you like Erik Draven or not is really inconsequential as far as I'm concerned. Erik is a man of pride, honor, and dignity, very similar to myself. He stands up for what he believes in and doesn't sell out to anyone. I'm really flattered that you'd want the "Shining Star," but the fact of the matter is you can take your little worthless proposal back to Gates and tell him to shove it up his ass. SmirtDogg: If that's how it is -- Golden One -- can you accurately give me the exact feelings you felt when Stevie Richards pinned your shoulders to the canvas for the 1...2...3. That was the moment you realized your dream of becoming EWA World Heavyweight Champion was thrawted temporary -- maybe permanently. Be honest, were you reduced to tears??? Please share with us how it felt to be the ULTIMATE LOSER! The Golden One: Support from the Corporate Empire, huh? Is that what you're offering me Smirt? Let me say this once, and only once. I will NEVER stand side by side with Duane Gates BRO. Whether you like Erik Draven or not is really inconsequential as far as I'm concerned. Erik is a man of pride, honor, and dignity, very similar to myself. He stands up for what he believes in and doesn't sell out to anyone. I'm really flattered that you'd want the "Shining Star," but the fact of the matter is you can take your little worthless proposal back to Gates and tell him to shove it up his ass. The Golden One: You want to know how I was feel Let me tell you EXACTLY how I felt when Richards beat me. Better yet, let me SHOW you. With that said, The Golden One knocks SmirtDogg down with a hard right, then picks him up and nails the Gold Medal, leaving him laid out on his pretty white couch... The Golden One: There you have it jack*ss. That's how I felt then, and that's how anyone else will feel if they cross my path between now and December 30th. Gates, that one was for you. The Golden One throws the microphone down at SmirtDogg and exits the set to a huge pop from the crowd as 'Golden Showers' plays again over the loudspeaker... Joe Lemon v. E-Nuff E-Nuff was most definitely 'enough' for Joe Lemon, as Lemon lasted all of fourteen seconds. E-Nuff hit the ring, executed a suplex, and a powerbomb on Lemon, and then hit the Lockdown for the victory. E-Nuff d. Joe Lemon with the Lockdown. Johnny Napalm v. Sting Napalm started off the match by dominating Sting, executing a series of vicious powerslams on Sting. However, Sting began to mount a comeback after a missed splash by Napalm, getting the fans behind him. After a bulldog, and the Stinger Splash, the fans began to cheer as Sting called for the Scorpion Death Drop. At this point, Paul Role made his way to the ring, escorting E-Nuff, who was back in his handcuffs. E-Nuff was un-cuffed by Role, who then handed E-Nuff his baton and cuffs. Sting then hit the Death Drop, and locked on the Scorpion Death Lock for the victory. Afterward, E-Nuff picked up the microphone. E-Nuff: "So Napalm you think you know what Violence is?, you haven't experienced TRUE violence yet son. Have you ever had to fight off 5 ruthless serial killers?. NO!, So tonight i came down here to teach you what violence really is...you ready to learn "Mr.God?" Napalm then charged E-Nuff, but E-Nuff slammed him into the guardrail, and then cuffed his hands together. From there, E-Nuff blasted Napalm twice with the baton, and then hit the Lockdown, before Paul Role re-cuffed him and led him back. Sting d. Johnny Napalm by submission with the Scorpion Death Lock. The Black Angels v. Big Time Big Time did indeed make it to the Arena Mexico, and they also held true to their words: Dave Drexxel did indeed powerbomb both members of the Black Angels. After that, Big Time hit the Damn Good Drop, and got the victory. Big Time d. The Black Angels with the Damn Good Drop. Non-Title Match "The Natural One" Michael Modest (EWA World Television Champion) v. Raven Modest, who has at time proclaimed himself to be 'hardcore', was showed up badly in this match by Raven. The match was declared under Raven's Rules, and Raven quickly put Modest out of comission, ending it with an Evenflow DDT onto a steel chair for the victory. Raven d. Michael Modest with the Evenflow DDT. Galaxy v. Philip Donovan Double no-show. Click here for more! EWA European Championship Match Haywood Jublome v. Thorne Richards (EWA European Champion) [As we see Thorne slapping hands with the fans, as he comes down to the ring, we see Michael Modest accompanying him with what appears to be a bottle in his right hand and two glasses in the other. Modest gets into the ring first, eyeing Jublome, while Thorne quickly stretches out and tugs the ropes a few times. Afterwards, Thorne motions for a microphone.] Thorne: Haywood, if I may borrow 30 seconds of your precious time. Let me be the first to say no matter how I beat you, you're still a fine lad in my book. To overcome your drug and alcohol habits is commendable. I, the European Champion, do hereby commend you on a job well done for now. [Modest pops the cork on the bottle, and pours what surely looks like wine into both glasses. He extends one glass to Jublome, who doesn't seem to want any of it and waves it off.] Thorne: Fair enough. *takes one glass from Modest and raises it in toast* Michael, here's to me, another night, another win. [Both clank glasses, quickly suck all the wine into their mouths then quickly spray it into Jublome's eyes and face. As Modest makes a quick exit, Thorne starts to hammer away at his stunned challenger.] This was the match of the night, for sure. Thorne started off by grounding Jublome with a series of technical manuevers, trying to wear the man down. He did so, and seemed to have the match won with a sleeperhold, but at the last possible second, Jublome broke it. From there, Thorne maintained an advantage, but only for a few more moments, as Jublome nailed him with a hard right below the belt. From there, Jublome took control, outpowering Richards and muscling him around. Jublome then set Richards on the top rope, and attempted to Ho Checka, but Thorne was able to flip out, and amazingly, land on his feet. From there, Thorne locked Jublome up for the London Bridge, but Jublome elbowed him in the face, breaking the hold. Jublome then hit the ropes, but Thorne dropkicked him, sending him back into the turnbuckle. Thorne whipped Jublome into the ropes, and came back with a bulldog. Thorne then whipped him back into the turnbuckle, and started to place him up top, but Jublome was able to block it, and then nail the Ho Checka. Jublome went for the cover, and got 1....2....3!!!!! And Haywood Jublome becomes the new EWA European Champion! Haywood Jublome d. Thorne Richards with the Ho Checka. Haywood Jublome is the new EWA European Champion!! The Nightmare Squad v. Team U.S.A. Double no show. KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight & The Corporate Empire) KooKabura did absolutely nothing in this match, and the behemoth from hell picked up a dominating victory, squashing KooKabura with the Fallen Angel. Vincent Jackson d. KooKabura with the Fallen Angel. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny & The Corporate Empire) v. Gangrel (w/ The Kindred) Gangrel and KG had an excellent match here tonight. KG, for the most part, abandoned his high-flying moves for the night, and the two ended up having an aggressive brawl. KG started out with the early advantage, but lost it quickly when he went for a running splash on Gangrel in the corner. Gangrel moved, and blasted KG with a right, following it up with a monkey flip. Gangrel followed that with a lariat, and went to work on KG with a series of snap suplexes. From there, Gangrel placed KG on the top, and in an incredible move, placed his leg on KG's head, and jumped off, driving KG into the mat. From here, Gangrel went for the cover, but only got a two count. Gangrel picked KG up, and went for the Piledriving DDT, but KG countered it with a low blow, proving that even vampires can be hurt 'down there'. KG then went for a Michinoku Driver, but Gangrel slipped out of that, and shoved KG into the turnbuckle, unfortunately knocking out the referee as well. Gangrel caught KG with a back suplex, and then nailed him with the Piledriving DDT. Gangrel went for the cover, but there was no referee to count. A roar then went up from the crowd, as Kevin Nash jogged to the ring. Nash got in, and as Gangrel went to go revive the referee, Nash booted him right in the head. He picked Gangrel up, and delivered a huge JackKnife powerbomb, then leaving as quickly as he came. KG slowly climbed to the top, and hit a shooting star press on Gangrel. KG went for the cover, and the referee slowly crawled over to count 1....2.....3, and KG picks up a huge victory for the Corporate Empire. KG d. Gangrel with a shooting star press. EWA Intercontinental Championship The Rock (w/ The Corporate Empire) v. Ruger Ice (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Lady Jane) The Rock started off in control, using the distraction from KG, who accompanied him to the ring. With Ruger distratced, The Rock hit him in the face with his water bottle. From there, a stunned Ruger was hit with rights and lefts by the self-proclaimed "Corporate Champ". The Rock started in on Ruger's knees, working them over with a variety of technical wrestling holds, a new side to The Rock. However, Ruger was able to fend this off, making a comeback to the delight of the capacity crowd. Ruger whipped The Rock into the ropes, and lifted him up for the Spinebuster...however, The Rock countered it with a vicious DDT. Both men lay stunned on the mat, and the referee counted to eight before Ruger Ice began to stagger to his feet. The Rock followed him, and both men bounced against the ropes. Ruger went for a clothesline, but The Rock ducked it, and whipped around into the Maivia Hurricane (spinning DDT). The Rock went for the cover, and only got a two count. Ruger then began to form a bit of offense, slamming the Rock on three separate occasions. From there, Ruger did a shocking thing that we had never seen before -- he went to the top rope. Ruger stayed perched on the ropes, waiting for The Rock to get to his feet, and once he did, Ruger leapt off in an attempt at a double ax-handle. But The Rock caught Ruger on the way down, and planted him hard with The Rock Bottom. Rock went for a cover, and got 1...2...but Ruger had a foot on the rope...but the ref didn't see it, and counted to three, surprisingly! KG retrieved the IC title from ringside, and presented it to The Rock inside the ring as the ring announcer announced the result. The Rock d. Ruger Ice with the Rock Bottom. The Rock is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion!! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Grudge Match "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Corporate Empire) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Whisper & D-Generation X) Their first match was a hard act to follow, yet this match nearly succeeded in doing so. SmirtDogg started off with a cheap shot to begin the match, clocking Michaels from behind as he handed his earrings to Whisper. Smirt began kicking away on Michaels, and Michaels rolled out of the ring to attempt to stop the momentum. Smirt, however, followed him, and a brawl on the outside ensued. Smirt whipped Michaels into the steel guardrail, but Michaels came back with a clothesline, knocking Smirt to the ground. Michaels then slingshotted Smirt into the steel ringpost. Michaels went over to Smirt, and gave him a big X-Chop, which proved to be a mistake, as Smirt withdrew a set of brass knuckles, and clocked Michaels right in the knee. Michaels fell over, as Smirt crawled to his feet and picked up a chair. Smirt waited for Michaels to get up, and when he did, clocked him right in the head, lacerating Michaels' forehead. Michaels went down in a heap, and SmirtDogg began mocking HBK's X-Chop. He then threw Michaels into the ring, and went to the top rope, delivering a thunderous elbowdrop. Smirt went for the first cover of the match, and only got a two count. Smirt drug Michaels to his feet, and threw him into a turnbuckle. He then began pummeling away on him with rights and lefts, and an added low blow for emphasis. Since the match had previously been turned into a No Disqualification match, the referee did nothing but warn SmirtDogg. Smirt simply turned, and nailed the Gangsta's Paradise on the poor referee, rendering him unconscious. From there, Smirt tried to go for another right, but Michaels blocked it, and began fighting back. Michaels hit the ropes, and hit a flying crossbody. Michaels sprang to his feet, the crowd back behind him. Michaels went to the top rope for his flying elbowdrop, but KG, who had also, along with The Rock, accompanied Smirt to ringside, pushed Michaels off of the top rope. Smirt immediately crawled over for a pin attempt, and a new referee came in, but Smirt only got a two count. Smirt was furious with the referee, and was about to attack him, but Michaels was able to roll him up from behind, only for a two count. From here, Michaels hit Smirt with a flying crossbody, and began tuning up the band for the Sweet Chin Music. Michaels went for it, but Smirt ducked it, and hit Michaels with a kick to the stomach, and the Gangsta's Paradise. Smirt went for the cover, but Michaels put a foot on the rope, and the referee broke the count. Smirt then whipped Michaels into the ropes, and charged at him...Michaels followed by hitting the Sweet Chin Music. Michaels covered Smirt, but KG ran in the ring, and pulled Michaels off. From there, KG began kicking Michaels, and soon after, Smirt joined in. Help soon came, though, in the form of The ICON, Erik Draven. Draven came in, and made it a two-on-two brawl, immediately going after SmirtDogg. Afterward, The Bossman and The Rock ran to the ring, and made it 4 on two, as they helped in attacking DX. Soon, the odds again shifted, as Triple-H, Kevin Nash, and Shawn Copeland all sprinted to the ring. As the huge brawl erupted in the ring, the lights went out. "Wherever I May Roam" blared over the PA, and Vincent Jackson made his entrance, making it a 10 man brawl. After about 10 minutes of all-out brawling, security finally got the two stables apart, and the referee had absolutely no choice but to rule the match a no contest. SmirtDogg and Shawn Michaels battled to a no contest. Steel Cage Match -- To Finally Settle The Score "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ D-Generation X) v. The Golden One Once the cage was erected, the two men began the match...if you want to call it that. Shawn Copeland looked horrible, and never mounted a single bit of offense against The Golden One. Meanwhile, The Golden One looked extremely impressive, as he whipped Copeland all around the ring, finally polishing him off with both The Gold Medal and the Golden Edge, then nonchantly climbing over the cage. Waiting on the other side was his opponent on December 30, the World Champion Erik Draven. Draven and The Golden One stared each other down, as the camera faded to black. The Golden One d. Shawn Copeland by exiting the steel cage.
