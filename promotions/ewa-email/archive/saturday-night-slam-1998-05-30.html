EWA Saturday Night Slam

30 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 30, 1998 Live, from the Target Center, Minneapolis, Minnesota. The Swing Pad (Hosted by Stevie Richards) "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Lee Tenn Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. Furnace Return Match The New Age Outlaws v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Triangle Match for the EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship The Outsiders v. The Natural Born Thrillers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) EWA Semi-Final European Tournament Match 'Dancin Stevie Richards (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. KooKabura EWA Semi-Final European Tournament Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Non-Title Match Ruger Ice v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) The Widow Maker v. Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match The Gunslinger v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) The Swing Pad (Hosted by Stevie Richards) The Swing Pad was canceled. A note was sent to the broadcast team from Stevie Richards, saying he was under "emotional distress" for having to face his fiend, and would not be doing The Swing Pad tonight. "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Lee Tenn Joe Satre started off this light heavyweight contest with a moonsault onto Lee Tenn as he was making his way to the ring. Satre quickly took advantage of this, tossing Lee Tenn into the ring and executing a variety of high-risk manuevers. Among the moves Satre executed were a beautiful Senton Splash, and a nice moonsault onto Tenn. However, Satre could not capitalize with a pin, as Tenn kicked out after a two-count. From there, Satre whipped Tenn into the ropes, and leaped up, attempting a Frankensteiner. However, Tenn had the presence of mind to reverse the attempted move, nailing Satre with a powerbomb. From there, Tenn hit a series of rolling snap suplexes, went to the corner, and nailed The Hang Tenn, a modified version of Shawn Michaels' "Sweet Chin Music". Lee Tenn d. "The Rocket" Joe Satre with the Hang Tenn. Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. Furnace Unfortunately, neither athlete showed, but the crowd was not disappointed, as The Crippled Midgets of Doom came to the ring, and shot T-shirts into the capacity crowd. Return Match The New Age Outlaws v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Once again for the second show in a row, neither of these teams came to the arena. President Gates ordered a third rematch, with an added stipulation: If the team doesn't show, they will be fired. Triangle Match for the EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship The Outsiders v. The Natural Born Thrillers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Eliminators (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions) The Natural Born Thrillers never arrived at the arena, causing even more speculation on their whereabouts after the Stud Stable plane crash earlier in the week. The Outsiders, meanwhile, were obliterated in every way, shape, and form by Saturn & Kronus. This match wasn't much of a contest, and the fans who didn't like The Eliminators saw this a good opportunity to go to the merchandise stand. The Eliminators d. The Outsiders with Perry Saturn's pin on Scott Hall after the Total Elimination. The Eliminators retain the EWA U.S. Tag Team Titles. EWA Semi-Final European Tournament Match 'Dancin Stevie Richards (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) This match started off slow and tentative, as neither Stevie nor Justin wanted to injure the other. However, the battle quickly picked up pace, and from there, we saw a match of the ages. Hicks started off by taking control, catching Stevie across the head with a flying elbow after whipping him to the ropes. From there, the crowd saw twenty minutes of back and forth wrestling action. Both men gave it all they had, not holding anything back, as they said they would. Hicks gained control, however, with a vicious powerbomb from the top rope. Hicks waited patiently in the corner until Stevie got up, and then ran at him with a lariat attempt. However, Stevie ducked, and when Hicks turned around, nailed him with the Stevie Kick and covered him for the three count. Richards then helped Hicks to his feet, and the two men embraced in the center of the ring. Stevie Richards d. Justin Hicks with the Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards now advances to the finals of the EWA European Championship Tournament. Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. KooKabura Will looked impressive in his EWA debut, taking the Barney-loving KooKabura completely apart, in what, some fans stated, was the worst defeat in KooKabura's wrestling career. Most of the fans actually cheered on Haynes after he won the match. Will Haynes d. KooKabura with a gut-wrench suplex. EWA Semi-Final European Tournament Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Michaels brought a bucket of KFC chicken to the ring with him, and proceded to throw it out into the crowd. This caused Rufus to storm down to the ring, and the fight was on. Michaels caught Rufus with a couple of kicks as he slid into the ring, and obtained the early advantage. Michaels quickly lost the advantage, however, when Rufus gained momentum and threw him to the outside of the ring. While Rufus complained to the official about Shawn's tossing of the KFC chicken, Prodigy came from the crowd, and blasted Michaels with a chair. He then rolled him into the ring, setting Rufus up perfectly for the Flip, Flop, And Fly. Rufus Lamar Jackson d. Shawn Michaels with the Flip, Flop, And Fly. Rufus Lamar Jackson advances to the finals of the EWA European Championship Tournament. Non-Title Match Ruger Ice v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) Ruger looked very determined in this match, as he and Erik Draven had a long dispute in the AEWF. Ruger used this momentum to carry him at the beginning of the match, working over Draven with a furious vengance. Ruger used various types of power-based manuevers, but made his mistake when he went to the top rope. Draven got up, and bounced against the ropes, causing Ruger to fall onto the top turnbuckle. Draven then nailed a superplex from the top rope, and controlled the match from this point out, finally nailing Ruger with the DX Driver. Erik Draven d. Ruger Ice with the DX Driver. The Widow Maker v. Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) It was announced that Taz had decided to, for unknown reasons, leave the EWA, so The Widow Maker obtained a two-match winning streak. The Widow Maker d. Taz by forfeit. EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) KG was heavily booed by the capacity crowd, and started off the match with a low-blow on McCammon, which drew even more boos. KG then worked on McCammon's knee with a variety of submission holds. However, McCammon fought back, and began to dominate KG, eventually winning the match. Shocking circumstances took place after the match, however. KG was struggling to his feet, when a masked man came out from the crowd and began to give a major beating to him. As the attack went on, the crowd and commentators recognized the masked man as the victim of KG's beatings in his interviews, Serpent Man. Serpent Man relentlessly attacked KG, until security finally pulled him off. Jack McCammon d. KG with the Double Hammer. Jack McCammon retains the EWA World Television Championship. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) A total debacle. Those words can be used to describe this match. Helmsley looked awful, and Flair pretty much dominated this match to the point that if you would've blinked, you would've missed it. Ric Flair d. Triple-H by submission with The Figure Four. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match The Gunslinger v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) The Gunslinger didn't even show up for the main event, so President Gates ordered that Bunda defend his title against another EWA member. Out from behind the curtain came former EWA member Augustus. Bunda was obviously upset over this turn of events, and showed it in the ring, absolutely destroying Augustus. President Gates also announced that if The Gunslinger doesn't show up for WNW, he will be fired. Jeff Bunda d. Augustus with the Showrunner. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship.
