Draven immediately charged Rock, and the fight was on. The match was very even, until it spilled over to the outside. At this point, Draven went after Jackson...but Jackson caught Draven with a right, and lifted him into the air with a chokeslam. Immediately, Shawn Michaels sprinted to the ring, and attacked Jackson. However, once Michaels saw The Rock, he immediately went after him. The referee had no choice but to throw the contest out.
