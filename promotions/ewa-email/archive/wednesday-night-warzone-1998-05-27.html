EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 27, 1998 Live, from the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) vs. "The Rocket" Joe Satre. Joe Satre d. Shawn Hart with a rollup. Lee Tenn v. Furnace Furnace d. Lee Tenn with a shoulderbreaker. First Round Match in EWA European Title Tournament "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Justin Hicks d. Triple-H with the Saturday Night Special. First Round Match in EWA European Title Tournament "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) v. Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) Stevie Richards d. Makaveli with the StevieKick. First Round Match in EWA European Title Tournament The Gunslinger v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Rufus Lamar Jackson III d. The Gunslinger with the Flip, Flop, And Fly. First Round Match in EWA European Title Tournament KG (w/ Sunny) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) Shawn Michaels d. KG with the Sweet Chin Music. Ruger Ice v. Goldberg Neither man showed. Non-Title Match Taz (w/ Bill Alfonzo) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Aaron Martin d. Taz with the Shoulderbreaker. Non-Title Match The Natural Born Thrillers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) Public Enemy d. The NBT by DQ after Prodigy came in and nailed both Swap Meet Louie & Ghetto Boy with chairs. The New Age Outlaws v. Prophecy (w/ Degeneration X) Neither team showed . The Widow Maker v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) In a stunning upset, The Widow Maker d. Kane with a powerbomb. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Scott Cummings d. The Wyldsyde by countout. The Wyldsyde retains the EWA U.S. Title. A rematch has been signed for Wednesday, June 3. *Camera fades in on the middle of the EWA ring, where in a few moments "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda and Jack "The Man" McCammon will face each other in the Wednesday Night Warzone Main Event. The EWA fans are pumped up, the camera circles the crowd and signs such as "The Franchise RUNS THIS SHOW" and "I WANT JACK'S CAR" can be seen. The excitement can be contained no longer, as the fans eagerly await the beginning of the main event. Suddenly, the music that has not be heard in the EWA since the 13th of May, the music that EWA fans lived to hear, the theme music from "Space Odyssey: 2001" echoes throughout the arena and the EWA fans erupt knowing that the one and only the 14-time World Champion, the former EWA World Champion, the man who can style and profile and stay up all night and party with all the women, the Nature Boy, Ric Flair is about to walk through that entrance way. And none other than the Nature Boy himself appears in the entrance way, wearing arguably his most glamorous robe ever. The robe is a bright blue with a silver trim, and the silver trin is covered in diamonds, as well as the majority of the robe. The EWA fans upon seeing the TRUE "ICON" back in the EWA begin to shout "Whoooooo!" and Flair grins as he walks down to the ring, because when you're the 14-time World Champion you know that you're the best damn thing going today. Flair walks up on the ring apron and is about to lower is head to enter the ring through the ropes when he stops and motions for the timekeeper. The timekeeper waddles up to the ring apron and opens the ropes for Flair, who enters the EWA ring still grinning. Flair takes a moment to look around the EWA arena and sees all the screaming EWA fans who are on their feet for hte Nature Boy, and gives them the honor of seeing his two-step strut. Then, Flair with a huge grin on his face utters the word that makes the world go round, the word that strikes fear into every SINGLE wrestler in that locker room, the word of a champion* "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair: Whoooooo!!! (The EWA fans, still standing, respond with the loudest "Whooooo!" you've ever heard in your life) Ladies and gentlemen, the one and only, the 14-time World Champion, the former EWA World Champion, the man who made wrestling what it is today, the Nature Boy Ric Flair, is baaaaaaack! (The fans erupt and Flair grins) That's right, I was sitting back and relaxing in my home in Charlotte, North Carolina Saturday Night, when I decided, what the hell, let's turn on EWA Mind Games and see how the good ol' EWA is doing without the Nature Boy. Well let me just say, the EWA should have paid people to watch that PPV, with the lack of talent I saw in that ring. What really made me sick, was the fact that some of the EWA's "talent" decided to not even show up for their matches. (Flair shakes his head) Hell, the people who didn't show up don't even deserve to be employed in the same federation as the Nature Boy, so I think I'm going to have to make it a priority to teach these boys a lesson. Furnace, rumor has it you're some big bad man who thinks the world owes you, well this Saturday Night the 14-time World Champion, the Nature Boy is calling you out! So if you've got the guts, show up on Saturday Night so the Nature Boy can give you an all-expenses paid trip on Spaaaaaaaace Mountain! Whooooo! (Crowd erupts once again) But not only did I see boys not show up, I saw some of the biggest excuses for professional wresters I have ever seen Saturday Night. Rufus Lamar Jackson III? Stevie Richards? Gunslinger? For god's sake, the Crippled Midgets of Doom? This is what the EWA is putting out? (Flair shakes his head) I tell ya, Gates is lucky the Nature Boy decided to make his return, or else he'd be going under faster than Turner is. (Flair laughs) And then of course, we've got the worst World Title Match I have ever seen between Erik Draven and Prodigy, two degenerates who can run their mouths pretty well, but when it comes down to getting it done in the ring they've got the wrestling talent of Christopher Reeves. (Flair grins) Neither of those children deserve to even be within a mile of the EWA World Title, let alone hold it. But we can't forget about the so-called "Franchise" of the EWA, the man who claims to be the most deserving of the EWA World Title. (Flair grins) I got news for you Bunda, you can stomp all over all the mid-card talent the EWA has, but if there's one thing you aren't, it's "The Franchise." Whether you want to admit it or not Bunda, when the Nature Boy was in the EWA I RAN THIS SHOW!!! You were just another cocky, arrogant mid-carder who thought he was something special, and the same thing goes for you today. Just because the EWA is lacking some talent, you think that you've suddenly become all that the EWA has going. (Flair laughs) Bunda, the Nature Boy has been stylin' and profilin' since God knows when, and you better believe I'm going to stay up all night with all the women. Whooooo! (Crowd erupts) Whether you want to face the music or not Bunda, it really doesn't matter, but the fact still remains that as long as the Nature Boy can go, the EWA is in the palm of my hand. (Flair opens his right hand and shows it to the crowd then clinches it shut) Oh my God what have I done? I just killed the entire EWA! (Flair grins) The Nature Boy is what makes the EWA go 'round, I tell Gates to give me a title, he says which one, I tell Gates to fire a jobber, he says which one. And I usually reply Bunda, but that's a different story. Whooooo! I've got more "Franchismo" in this big nose of mine than you've got in your entire body, so do yourself a favor and stick with the mid-carders, because the fact of the matter is that you're playing with fire now Bunda, and if you're not careful you're going to get burned. Whooooo! (Crowd erupts yet again) So whether you like it, or you don't like it, learn to love it, because the Nature Boy is the best thing going today. Whooooo! And then the Nature Boy is going to style and profile and stay up all night and party with all the women. Whooooo! *The crowd erupts once again and Flair does his two-step strut and leaves the ring as the Bunda-McCammon match begins. Unfortunately the fans can't get over the return of the Nature Boy and anything that happens next is inconsequential* Non-Title No DQ Grudge Match Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) v.
