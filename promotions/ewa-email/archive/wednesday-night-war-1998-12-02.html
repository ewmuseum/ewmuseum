EWA Wednesday Night War

2 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 2, 1998 Live, from the Jacksonville Coliseum Jacksonville, Florida Before the card began, Scott Hall was introduced as the newest member of the EWA Commentating Team. He emerged from the runway on crutches, to a huge pop from the crowd, hobbled to ringside, and the card began. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. Johnny Napalm Well, the crowd got treated to a massacre right from the outset, as Napalm simply smacked Galaxy around the ring as though the cruiserweight were his personal *BLEEP*ch. A Napalm Bomb later, and the rookie chalked up his first win here in the EWA. *** Johnny Napalm d. Galaxy via the Napalm Bomb. The Lights go out suddenly in the Jacksonville Coliseum and almost as suddenly "How To Survive in South Central" by Ice Cube comes blaring over the Pa System. A few moments later, out walks Haywood Jublome to a loud chorus of boos. As soon as he is out and sees the crowd reaction he soaks it up for a while and then runs full speed into the ring and calls for the microphone. He waits for the crowd to stop booing and then pauses for a second and begins to speak. Well, look who's back in the house. Yeah, the rookie you love to hate a few months later and a hell of alot hungrier. I am tired of the B.S. Politics that is the Ewa and I am going to do something about it and if I have to hospitalize everyone that gets in my way of The Ewa Heaviweight Title that is exactly what I am going to do. A memo to Duane Gates: Here is your one and only warning Dont mess with me and my mission. I know how you like to play favorites and all of that nonsense. If you know what is good for your punkass you will not be playing games and you will be giving me my shot at the top dogs that you have to offer so I can knock each and every one of them out the box. Duane, make no bones about it, I am not asking you to do right by me, I am telling you, you silly bastard. Dont fu*k with me Gates, or you will pay the price. Now on to the so called talent this fed has to offer. Stevie Richards The World Champion? Now that doesn't even sound right. Maybey a few years back but not in this day and age. Let's see what we have here, shall we? A Dirty Old Man, who carries a blow up doll every where he goes. A question if I may, Stevie? Are you that hard up for a piece of a*s? I mean cause I can hook you up with a lady friend of mine. I mean I know you think that Trixie is as easy as they come but I mean you have to worry about blowing her up and being careful not to pop her, but then again from what I hear on the street, that will never be your problem, but anyways, I digress. Renee, the girl I mentioned earlier is as easy as they come. You give her 5.00 and you can get that The All You Can Eat Special which comes with everything but fries. Anyways (grins) I am about done talking about your ignorant a*s, Stevie anytime , anywhere, anyplace, I'll beat the wrinkles out of yo ass. Justin Hicks The Proud Owner of the 8th, 9th and 10th Wonders of the World, Who is also running on badluck as of late. Poor Justin. My heart bleeds for you, I assure you. But anyways back to the 8th, 9th and 10th Wonders of the World. We all know they are Gonnorea, Herpes and The Clap too bad you cant Clap On,... Clap Off...Clap On.. Clap Off.. With The Clapper Justin don't flatter yourself. The only thing that makes you special is the fact that you are Bundas Whipping Boy and Trust Me on this One, that just gives you a Bigger Target on your chest and I will Strike. Not tonight maybey or tommorow or even next week but just like in the Bible, Surely I will Come Quickly, like a Thief In The Night. Only it won't be nearly as Pleasant for you. Shawn Copeland The Money Maker? Please...when I got through with you son, you will be lucky to get a job as the Change Maker at Harrah's. Better yet, if you cross my path or even look at me the wrong way and I'll put you on the Ho Stroll Faster than you can say Granda Cope does Chew, and then you will be My Money Maker. Jeff Bunda The Franchise. (Rolls eyes) Fraudchise is more like it. I am going to make an example of you boy. We hooked up once before in my Third Match as a pro, and I came inches away from knocking You, Flat On Your Ass then. So, what do you think, I have in store for you now? Well, being the nice guy I am I will spare you the details and just keep it a suprise. Franchise? Please! Save the Drama For Your Mama. You Wanna be the Franchise? No problem, I can arrange that. When next we meet, I'll make sure to finish the job I started last time and retire your ignorant a*s from the sport, then you can buy A Burger King or a 7-11. You can drive up to your store every morning in your custom made wheelchair with your personalized Franchise plates. Then you can finally be the Franchise you claim to be or is that Franchisee? No matter. I got your number, Jeff. Soon it will be time to clock punch out of the time clock, Jeff. Your shift will beover, permanatly. The Crock errr I mean The Rock Who calls you the Rock? The ladies? You serving them up something proppalike with that Fat Salami of yours, Rock? Well, Enjoy the feeling while it lasts, because when we meet next and The Carnage has been completed, and you wake up in a Hospital Bed and the Doc comes in with that Solumn Look on his face and breaks the news to you, there will be No More Pleasures of The Flesh for your Parapalegic Ass We can all call you limpd*ck for now on, course to the ladies, you will be forever known as Super Tongue So start praticcing those Tongue Tricks now, Rock, because soon whether you like it or you don't like it, you had better learn to love it, because it will be the only way you will pull any woman, Other than your wallet, of course. The Golden One TGO...Onemore person standing in my way. Trust I did not come back to theEwa to be Just One Of The Boys. No, I'm coming in with Gunz A Blazing. I am coming to the Ewa to be The Champion, and not now, but right now. Now, I realize that in order to accomplish this, I must annhiliate guys such as yourself, and Trust, I am more than ready to do, just that. TGO, prepare to get TKO'D. I am not your average run of the Mill, Duane Gates Ass Kissing wrestler, no I'm more like TNT, a Keg of Dynamite waiting to explode all over your ass. Just be ready and if you are unlucky enough to be my competiton in the near future, better cross all your T's and Dot your I's, make sure all of those Life Insurance premiums are paid in full, cause this aint TBS, this is real lifepunk. Be ready or be dead. The Golden One? What are you some kind of royalty wanting some show of respect? Ya, I can appreciate that and I got something for you trust. After ourmatch you might be the Proud Recipient of A Golden Shower Umm who have I forgotten? ohh yeah Prodigy and Draven Two of the Best, this sport has to offer (pauses for effect then smirks sarcastically and begins to speak) about 20 years ago. You two Geriatric Mothaf*ckas heed my warning and stay the hell out of my way before I retire your asses permanatly and I am not talking about retiring you from wrestling, either. I am talking paulbearer type of s*it. There is a new Sheriff in Town and his name is Jublome Granted, you both had Great Careers and I emphasize the word HAD. Now, I realize for some odd reason, you both want to keep wrestling. Whether it be for money or the notoriety, or you just dont know When To Say When Well, heed the Words of this Brotha and Stay the Hell out my way, I'm going to the top and if either of you is in my way, I'll end you. Nuff said. Now since I got better things to do than look at all yo ugly asses in the audience, I am going to finish this and get back to business with the Lady I got in the Back, but before I go, I gotta keep it real... Cause I know all of you who weren't mentioned have your jaw dropped in disbelief wondering if I forgot to mention you. If I didn't mention you, it isfor one of three Reasons: 1: You aint s*it 2: You ain't never gonna be about sh*t 3: You are so Pathetic, that I would be wasting my breath, even mentioning you So no, I didn't forget to mention you. So, if you weren't mentioned, Don't stress. Don't sweat the small things. Just dial 1-900- Get-Some Skillz or visit our website located at http://www.get-a-life.com and by the way, one word of advice, stay the fu*k out of my way. It's not good for your (snickers to himself) career. And with that Haywood Jublome drops the mic listens to the crowd reaction who then begin to boo more loudly and Haywood taunts them by jumping on the ropes and lifting his arm in a victory sign to raise their ire then suiddenly he calmly jumps down and walks to the back. Christian (w/ The Kindred) v. The Widow Maker One would almost tend to wonder why the Kindred are keeping Christian around, short of for comic relief. The Widow Maker dominated this match from bell to bell, and finished Christian off with a powerbomb for the win. *** The Widow Maker d. Christian via a powerbomb. Joe Lemon & James Mastin v. Harlem Heat The crowd was hyped over the return of the Heat, and the two wrestlers were obviously feeling it, as they polished off the two jabronies in under a minute. The match was simply a highspot-fest, finishing out with The Big Apple on Mastin to seal the win for the Heat. *** Harlem Heat d. Mastin/Lemon via the Big Apple. (Booker T with the pinfall) Morbid Angel v. Prodigy (w/ The BMF) The ring announcer stated that Prodigy was taking a leave of absence from the EWA for unspecified reasons. Morbid Angel no-showed as well, earning him the 'honor' of being the first wrestler banned from the EWA. Non-Title Match "The Extremist" Erik Moon v. The Prince (EWA Comissioner, w/ His Ego) First and foremost, Moon was announced as a no-show, which honestly wasn't much of a surprise to the EWA faithful. However...the next announcement was. The ring announcer stated that The Prince had stepped down as EWA Commissioner, and was also vacating the European Title, due to unspecified reasons. It was then announced that at the Clash Of The Champions VIII, there would be a Triple Threat match to determine the new champion, between Thorne Richards, Joshua Gardner, and the Rock. Da Bomb v. "The Natural One" Michael Modest & Thorne Richards Three double no-shows in a row. Not good. Not at all. The crowd was livid at this point... The ring announcer announced that if Da Bomb no-showed their match at the Clash, then they would follow in Morbid Angel's footsteps and be banned from the EWA for life. Non-Title Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) v. Ruger Ice (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Lady Jane) Special Referee: "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels This match was ugly, plain and simple. For all of Hunter's determination and focus, he was showing none of it tonight...Ruger ran the show from bell to bell, and Helmsley mounted no offense whatsoever. Ruger hit the Spinebuster, and HBK made a rapid three-count, almost starting to count before Helmsley's shoulders were even pinned to the mat. *** Ruger Ice d. Triple-H via the Spinebuster. EWA World Television Championship Match "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. The Rock (EWA World Television Champion) Much to the joy of the EWA faithful, both men showed up, and were ready to fight...KG showed more focus tonight than he has in a long time, and the Rock was simply his normal, cocky self...setting the stage for a great match. Both men took each other to the limits, with the Rock dropping the People's Elbow to a thunderous roar from the crowd...however, instead of going for the pinfall, he turned his attention toward Triple-H and Chyna, who were standing on the runway watching the match. The Rock taunted Helmsley, which turned out to be a big mistake, as he turned his back on KG for just a moment too long...KG seized the advantage, rolled the Rock up, and a 3-count later walked away with the EWA World Television Title in his grasp. After the match, Helmsley ran down and attacked the Rock, then Shawn Michaels ran in to join the party...finally, the referees had to separate the three combatants. *** KG d. The Rock via a rollup. *** KG is the new EWA World Television Champion!!! "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Whisper) Even though the plan backfired in his face, Triple-H's plan to wake Shawn Michaels up appears to have worked...as he finally looked like the HBK of old, coming out to 'Sexy Boy' and playing to the crowd like old times. Conklin came out to a mixed reaction from the crowd, still not sure of what to make of the returning 'Mr. Overhand Right.' The match was extremely fast-paced, with a bunch of highspots hit by each man...Michaels hitting a moonsault on Conklin, Conklin returning the favor and dropping Michaels with an inverted powerbomb when he tried in a second time. Finally, Conklin let his guard down for a moment, and was met with Sweet Chin Music right square in the face...a cover, and HBK walks away with the victory. Once again, after the match Triple-H ran in, and both men again had to be restrained by the referees. *** Shawn Michaels d. Terry Conklin via the Sweet Chin Music. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner For as good as the past two matches were...this one wasn't. Copeland came in looking to prove something to the rest of DX, it seemed, and that proof came at Joshua Gardner's expense. Copeland ran the match from bell to bell, effectively killing whatever momentum Gardner had put together. Copeland toyed with Gardner for a solid five minutes before simply polishing him off with the Money Clip for the victory. *** Shawn Copeland d. Joshua Gardner via the Money Clip. Cemetary Brawl Not a sanctioned match Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) The scene opened with the two behemoths staring each other down across an open grave, Draven with Beulah by his side, Jackson with Christina. Draven finally said something that infuriated Vincent, and we were underway. The two struggled back and forth for a solid ten minutes, mixing a couple of power wrestling moves with straight brawling...no technical showcase here, and I don't think the fans were expecting such, either. A couple of minutes later, Draven pulled his fist back to deliver a left hook, but hit Christina Knight on the backswing. Draven turned, obviously concerned about what he had done, giving Jackson enough time to kick Draven in the stomach and powerbomb him into the open grave. Christina Knight rose to her feet to stand by Vincent's side, and both began kicking dirt down upon the prone form of Draven, Jackson commenting on how 'That's just how your career is going to be, Draven...dead and buried.' The duo then left the graveside, leaving only Beulah standing over the open grave, tears streaking down her face. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Kindred (w/ Devon) v. The Extreme Dream Machine (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Triple Threat) In what was probably the best tag team match that the EWA's tag division has seen for a while, the Kindred took the EDM's right to the breaking point...but ended up falling just a little bit short, in the end. Gangrel and Edge had set Bunda up on the top turnbuckle for a double-Piledriving DDT, but Bunda managed to shove Edge off, and executed a belly-to-belly suplex off the top on Gangrel. Devon stood up on the ring apron, distracting Bunda and giving Gangrel time to struggle back to his feet. He grabbed the Franchise, and whipped him toward the opposite ropes...unfortunately for Gangrel, Bunda reversed his momentum, flung the undead demon into Devon (who was still on the apron), sending her flying and leaving him easy pickings for the Showrunner and the victory for the EDM. As Bunda and Hicks celebrated their victory in the ring, Haywood Jublome ran out from the back, and destroyed the Franchise with a steel chair-shot to the back of the head... Jublome quickly ran back toward the locker room, Justin Hicks hot on his heels. The Kindred took this time to gather themselves, and for some reason attacked Scott Hall as he was attempting to hobble back up the runway to leave the arena...all that was heard was Edge screaming about 'the epitome of all that is wrong with humanity' as they pummeled the fallen 'Bad Guy'...Big Sexy and Triple-H ran out to make the save, but not before Scott Hall's knee had been reinjured badly... (And the show fades to black.)
