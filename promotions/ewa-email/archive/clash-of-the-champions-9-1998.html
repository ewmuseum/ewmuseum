EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (challenger, w/ Jocelyn & The Triple Threat) v. Scott Tokage (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Our opening match at the Clash wasn't really very competitive. For the first time since joining the EWA, Scott Tokage looked horrible. He had no momentum whatsoever tonight, and seemed extremely sluggish. Meanwhile, Terry Conklin seemed fired up after The Dogg Pound, as he dedicated his efforts to the fallen Shawn Copeland. Conklin must have made Copeland happy, for when it was all said and done, we had a brand new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. Terry Conklin d. Scott Tokage with the Countout. Terry Conklin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! Joe "The Lemon Crusher" Lemon v. Max Pins Maaaaaaaaax Pins, as he likes to refer to himself, came off very successful tonight, getting an easy victory after the 7-10 Split. Max Pins d. Joe Lemon with the 7-10 Split. Zero Tolerance v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) This match was changed to a no disqualification match just minutes beforehand. This was the first time Zero Tolerance had actually had a match of any significance, and also was the long awaited return to the ring of Travis Scott and Timothy David. The devilish duo maintained an early advantage, working over Corey Allen in their corner, making swift tags. However, Cody Ryan shifted the momentum single-handedly when he blasted both Prophecy members with a chair. From there, it was all out brawling warfare, as the four men battled all over the arena. Scott and Ryan actually ended up battling it out in the consession stand outside the arena, while Timothy David and Corey Allen brawled inside the ring. At one point, Allen had picked up a chair, while David was climbing to the top. However, David jumped off, and clotheslined the chair right into the face of Allen. This was enough for David to get the pinfall, in an absolutely grueling match. Prophecy d. Zero Tolerance with Timothy David's pin on Corey Allen after a variation of the Cryptorchid. EWA World Television Championship Match Feather (challenger, w/ Vixen) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Chyna & D-Generation X) More interesting than the match itself was the occurance afterward. As far as the match goes, it was all Helmsley, who dominated the young rookie, showing that he still has a ways to go to be a top contender. Afterward, though, The Rock stormed the ring, and leveled Helmsley with a chair. The Rock then set Helmsley up for the Rock Bottom, and nailed it on to the chair. Helmsley had to be stretchered out, and we would find out later that Helmsley would be taking some time off, but would make his match with The Rock at Mind Games 2. Hunter Hearst Helmsley d. Feather with The Pedigree. Triple-H remains EWA World Television Champion. The Big Bossman (w/ The Corporate Empire) v. "The Walking Triple Threat" Jack Reed The Corporate Enforcer hasn't enforced much lately, and that streak continued tonight, as The Bossman was annihilated by the returning Reed. President Gates watched the match from the top of the entrance ramp, and when Reed got the three count, we could hear his words..."Not bad at all...". Jack Reed d. The Big Bossman with the Top Rope Frankensteiner. MAIN EVENT #1! Hell In The Cell For the EWA European Championship "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (challenger, w/ The Triple Threat) v. Haywood Jublome (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) This match was expected to surpass their previous encounter from a week ago...and it did that, and much more. Bunda came to the ring, with a lot more of the old Bunda evident in him. Jublome's entrance followed, as he came to the ring with Chyna, who remained on the outside of the Hell In The Cell apparatus. Bunda started off in complete control, blasting Jublome with rights and lefts. Bunda whipped Jublome into the ropes, and leapfrogged Jublome...Jublome had the presence of mind to stop in his tracks, and hit a huge German Suplex on Bunda. Bunda popped back up, and was sent right back down with a hard lariat from Jublome. Jublome quickly slowed the pace of the match, executing a chin lock on Bunda. Bunda took a moment to power out of it, and sent Jublome into the ropes. On the way back, Bunda grabbed Jublome by the head, and threw him out of the ring, straight into the steel cage wall. Bunda climbed to the outside to attack, but Jublome, who had landed halfway under the ring, suddenly rose with a fire extinguisher in hand, and leveled Bunda in the head. From there, Jublome sprayed the extinguisher into Bunda's face, to the laughing of a portion of the crowd. Jublome then went on a mission for weapons, ending up with the timekeeper's ringbell in hand. Jublome waited for Bunda to stand up, and ran at him with the bell...however, Bunda executed a standing side kick, knocking the bell right into Jublome's face. This busted Jublome's mouth open, and popped one of his lower teeth out. Bunda threw Jublome back in the ring, and shockingly, not only set Jublome up for the Showrunner, but actually hit it. However, amazingly, Jublome kicked out. Bunda, surprisingly, stayed calm, and went to work on Jublome with some submission moves. When Bunda executed a Figure Four, however, Jublome struck, reversing the hold and forcing Bunda to break it. Jublome then pulled a pair of brass knuckles out of his tights, and leveled Bunda first in the face, and then in the knee. Jublome, searching for more plunder, returned to the outside of the ring, and returned...with a table. Jublome set the table up in the center of the ring, as Bunda was about to get up. Bunda ran at Jublome with a clothesline, but Jublome ducked it, and executed a variation of The Rock Bottom. Jublome then signaled for the Ho Checka, to the delight of the crowd. Jublome placed Bunda on the top rope, and nailed the move...onto the table. It took him a moment, but Jublome was able to crawl over and make the cover...however, Bunda had the presence of mind to get a foot on the middle rope, and save himself from a loss. Jublome was visibly frustrated, feeling he had gotten the count before the foot went to the rope, but maintained his advantage, slamming Bunda down on the mat. Jublome then went back to the outside, and came back with both a chair, and a second table. This time, Jublome set the table up, and placed the chair on top of the table. Jublome again placed Bunda on top of the turnbuckle, and prepared to execute the Ho Checka. However, this time, Bunda was able to reverse it, into an incredible, Tornado DDT onto the chair/table combo. Bunda, who, by now was also lacerated in the face, immediately hooked the leg and went for the cover...to the SHOCK of everyone, though, Jublome kicked out! At this point, Bunda snapped. He climbed to the outside, looking almost insane, and dove under the ring, coming back with a baseball bat...wrapped in barbed wire. Bunda headed back to the ring, and Jublome, who was staggering to his feet. Bunda started to swing the bat, but Jublome kicked him in his stomach, causing him to drop the bat. Jublome backed against the ropes, and went for a lariat, but Bunda ducked it, and clotheslined Jublome to the mat. Bunda then grabbed Jublome's legs, and actually locked on the Sharpshooter. However, Jublome was able to power out of it moments later. The two struggled to their feet, and Jublome knocked Bunda into the turnbuckle. He set Bunda on the top, and climbed the ring rope. Bunda, however, had one more trick up his sleeve, as he pulled out a variation of a move we haven't seen in a long time -- The Apocalypse Drop. Bunda crawled over, and went for the cover, and also hooked his legs on the second rope for leverage...the referee, exhausted, crawled over, and made the count for 1, 2, and 3. In what will most definitely go down as one of the most amazing matches in history, Jeff Bunda becomes EWA European Champion. Jeff Bunda d. Haywood Jublome with the Apocalypse Drop. Jeff Bunda is the new EWA European Champion! "Dynamite" Dave Drexxel v. "Toke" Aaron Martin Drexxel looked about as good as his tag team partner did tonight. This translated into an easy victory for Toke, who celebrated afterward by lighting up a Camel. Aaron Martin d. Dave Drexxel with the Shoulderbreaker. Click here for more!
