EWA Monday Night Madness

19 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 19, 1998 Live, from the Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah. First Round EWA Television Title Tournament Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Vinnie Vegas This was a fairly competitive match, as Vinnie Vegas held the early advantage by overpowering Austin. But eventually, The Rattlesnake fought back, and was able to overcome the V-Man, giving him the traditional kick to the stomach, the 'salute', and the Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin d. Vinnie Vegas via the Stone Cold Stunner. (6:41) First Round Match in EWA Television Title Tournament Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. The Gunslinger This match was barely even competitive, as chants of "BORING" echoed through the crowd. Both men seemed to be off their game, but the ending was somewhat exciting, as Octavius executed a small package for the win. Octavius d. The Gunslinger via a small package. (5:00) First Round Match in EWA Television Title Tournament "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia (w/ The Nation Of Domination) This was another squash, as Triple-H never let Rocky get in a set of offensive moves. A very impressive showing by Helmsley, but alas, the same cannot be said for "The Rock". Hunter Hearst Helmsley d. Rocky Maivia via The Pedigree. (4:22) First Round Match in EWA Television Title Tournament KG (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly & The IV Horsemen) In yet another one-sided match, Page seemed completely out of focus, as KG overwhelmed him. KG d. Dallas Page via the Six-Shooter. (3:58) Non-Title Match "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock v. The Pessimist (EWA Intercontinental Champion) In a growing trend for this card, this was another lopsided contest, as The Pessimist did not have a good outing after his Intercontinental Title victory. Shamrock manhandled him, causing Comissioner Slaughter to sign a rematch for the Intercontinental Championship at Ground Zero. Ken Shamrock d. The Pessimist by submission via the Ankle Submission. (6:01) Kane/The Undertaker v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) In a shocking turn of events, Kane came to the ring with Paul Bearer! The Undertaker confronted Kane in the ring, and asked why Bearer was at ringside, with Kane responding by Tombstoning his younger brother. Pain and Destruction went to congratulate Kane, and were also met with Tombstones. Kane then left the ring area, dragging the UT back with him. Death Sentance d. Kane/The Undertaker via countout. (0:10) Triangle Match Winner will recieve EWA World Tag Team Title shot at Ground Zero The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Finally, a good, competitive match! These three teams gave it all they had, as they fought tooth and nail throughout the match. Prophecy was the first to be eliminated, as Travis Scott was nailed with the HartStopper, and pinned by Matthew Hart. However, from behind Hart came Kevin Nash, who promptly whipped Hart around, gave him 'the big boot', and JackKnifed him for the victory. The Outsiders d. The Hart Brothers and Prophecy to win the Triangle Match. (16:13) The Outsiders will face the EWA World Tag Team Champions at Ground Zero. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) The Golden Child absolutely dominated Benoit, as another of the Horsemen seemed off his game. The Golden Child d. Chris Benoit via the Royal Destroyer. (5:42) Booker T (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Tiberus (w/ The Empire) Another impressive showing for the Ghetto Warriors, as Booker T showed Tiberus that he can indeed be a successful singles wrestler. Booker T d. Tiberus via the Harlem Sidekick. (5:19) Faarooq (w/ The Nation Of Domination) v. Julius (w/ The Empire)(0:17) Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) This was a very impressive victory for Harley Hart, as he showed the world even more of his high-flying capabilities. Augustus, on the other hand, was totally out of sync. Harley Hart d. Augustus via the Stud Screw. (6:19) The Legion Of Doom v. The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) The Road Warriors made their way to the ring first, and were blindsided from behind by Pain and Destruction, along with Precious. Precious pulled out a pair of handcuffs, and chained Road Warrior Animal to the ringpost, while Pain and Destruction executed numerous spike-piledrivers onto chairs. When the trio finally left the ring, the New Age Outlaws music played, as B.A. Billy Gunn ran to ringside, and pinned Hawk. The New Age Outlaws d. The LOD via Billy Gunn's pin on Hawk after an attack by Death Sentance. Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) v. Arn Anderson (w/ The IV Horsemen) Once again, the Horsemen showed a poor effort tonight, as Draven manhandled Anderson. Scott Cummings was seen watching this match from the entrance ramp, apparently scouting Draven. Erik Draven d. Arn Anderson via the DX Driver. (7:11) Non-Title Match Bret "The Hitman" Hart v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Stud Stable) This match, and the main event match, made up for the poor matches beforehand. This was a classic wrestling battle, as these two greats went head to head for nearly 29 minutes before the shocking ending occured. At this point, I think it's best to let you hear what happened from our play-by-play announcers, Mike Malone and Larry Zybysko. Mike Malone: Hart sends Cummings into the ropes, and Cummings comes back with a flying cross-body, but Hart ducked....and Cummings nailed the cross-body on referee Earl Hebner! Larry Zybysko: He's out like a light! Mike Malone: Now Hart siezes the opportunity, and executes his tradmark Side Russian Legsweep. Hart picks up Cummings, and delivers a powerful backbreaker! Larry Zybysko: Hart's softening up the back for the Sharpshooter! Mike Malone: Hart goes to the second turnbuckle...elbow drop to the sternum! And he just gave a thumbs-up to the fans...you know what that means! He's going for the Sharpshooter! Bret Hart sets him up....he's got it on!! Cummings is tapping out like a madman!! Larry Zybysko: But the official isn't conscious to make the count! (We see the people in the crowd suddenly stand.) Mike Malone: Here comes Triple-H with a chair!! Triple-H is in the ring....he just CLOBBERED Hart with the chair! Now Helmsley, setting Hart up for the Pedigree...right onto the steel chair! And he's leaving now! Larry Zybysko: Cummings is coming to...and so is Earl Hebner! Mike Malone: Cummings is slowly going to the top rope, and he hits the Wipeout! Here's a cover.....1..........2........3! Cummings defeats Bret The Hitman Hart! Scott Cummings d. Bret "The Hitman" Hart via the Wipeout. (29:47) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Prodigy v. SmirtDogg (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors & Lil' Kim/Foxy Brown) Before the match, Comissioner Slaughter confronted SmirtDogg, and ordered him to pay the $5000 fine. Smirt gave Slaughter a briefcase, which had the money in it, but also had a can of Rogaine, a can of SlimFast, and some Geritol. Slaughter was not too pleased with SmirtDogg over this, but the crowd found it hilarious. Now, onto the match. This was one of the most brutal matches in the short history of the EWA, as referee Randy "Pee Wee" Anderson basically threw the rulebook out the window, and let these men get away with anything and everything. As a result, both men were bleeding profusely by the 15 minute mark, and the worst was yet to come. Booker T loaded Smirt's boot by wrapping a chain around it, as Smirt superkicked Prodigy. Smirt went for the cover, but was denied, as Prodigy kicked out. Smirt then set Prodigy up on the broadcast table, climbed to the top rope, and executed a moonsault onto Prodigy, breaking the table in half. Smirt then went to the ring, and set up a chair near the turnbuckle. He dragged Prodigy back into the ring, and set him up for what appeared to be a superplex onto the chair. However, Prodigy reversed the move, countering it with the Firestarter (a spinning DDT from the top) onto the chair. Prodigy covered SmirtDogg, and got the three count, but definately did not win the war, as The Ghetto Warriors brutally attacked Prodigy after the match. Prodigy d. SmirtDogg via The Firestarter. (36:17) Prodigy is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!
