EWA Saturday Night Slam

15 August 1998

Furnace get down here and come into my game board,and i'll greet you with 5 words WELCOME TO MY WORLD BOYZ He drops the mic and gets ready to wrestle in his debut Our opening contest at Saturday Night Slam was a matchup of young talent, as both Furnace and Deader were making their EWA debuts. The crowd seemed to like Deader, as about halfway through the match, chants of "Deader" rang through the arena. The matchup was very competitive and high-paced, with both men displaying the skills that brought them to the EWA. The match ended on a sour note, however, as Furnace decided to bring a chair into play. Furnace whalloped Deader with the chair on the outside, then crawled back in the ring for the countout victory. Furnace d. Deader by countout. 'The Nature Boy' Ric Flair v. 'The Extreme Superstar' Venom Ric Flair came out with a vengance tonight, looking to punish the debuting rookie Venom for his insulting portrayals of The Nature Boy....but it was not to be. Flair got in the first few hits in the match, but The Extreme Superstar soon took over, and defeated Flair with relative ease, ending it with The Extreme Poison. Venom d. Ric Flair with The Extreme Poison. Ruger Ice v. New Jack (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This match defined the word 'slugfest'. New Jack proved that size doesn't matter, as he was able to brawl effectively with the larger Ruger Ice. A very competitive match became a one-sided victory when the weapons came into play, however, as New Jack was able to hold on and defeat Ruger Ice. New Jack d. Ruger Ice with the Gangsta Splash. #1 Contender Match for the Light Heavyweight Championship 'Scrap Iron' Joe Satre (w/ The Modern Day Warriors) v. 'Outlaw Immortal' KG (w/ Sunny) v. 'Mr. Overhand Right' Terry Conklin This match was nearly as good as the four corners match on Wednesday. These three men once again dazzled the crowd with all sorts of manuevers. The match was elimination style, and Joe Satre was the first to fall, getting BlackedOut by KG, who seemed to have the crowd 100% behind him. He's possibly more popular than he's ever been in his career before, as while Conklin was cheered, KG got a huge pop for eliminating Satre. From there, KG and Conklin battled at a fast and furious pace. Conklin's mistake was missing a moonsault from the top rope, which KG capitalized on and nailed The Blackout. As KG was about to make the cover, though, we heard three words over the PA: Break It Down! And Shawn Copeland and Shawn Michaels came to the ring. Michaels started taunting KG from the onset, screaming at him, and executing X-Chops. At first, KG just glared at him, but then, he gave Michaels the universal sign that means "you're Number #1". Michaels seemed shocked, but then began laughing, as Terry Conklin arose and rolled KG up. As soon as the count was made, Michaels and Copeland hit the ring and beat up KG a little, but soon after, a masked man ran to the ring, and cleared out Conklin, Michaels, and Copeland. On the outside, Copeland presented Conklin with a D-Generation X shirt, and the three returned to the locker room together as KG and whomever the masked man is played to the crowd in the ring. Terry Conklin d. KG with a rollup after a distraction from Shawn Michaels. Van Smack v. SmirtDogg (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Van Smack was accompanied to the ring by Val Venis, who didn't try to interfere...he just stayed at ringside. Van Smack started off the match hot, getting some payback for the takeover of the Smack Shack, but SmirtDogg turned the tide with a low blow. From there, it was all Smirt, as he pretty much took over the match, and got the victory. SmirtDogg d. Van Smack by submission with the Thug Breaker. Haywood Jublome v. 'The King Of Swing' Stevie Richards This was an extremely competitive match. Both men gave it everything they had, and then some, as the battle turned into a knockdown, dragout fight. The match lasted all twenty minutes, and ended in a time limit draw. All the weapons scatter around. New Jack leaps over the top rope and enters the squared circle. Stevie Richards and Haywood Jublome are still a bit dazed from their battle so their reaction time isn't at top speed. New Jack arrogantly strolls over to a Sony Stereo System which now resides in the ring and picks it up. Both Stevie Richards and Haywood Jublome look up at New Jack -- wondering which one will be hit or will it be both? Then not a second later New Jack strikes! New Jack and Haywood Jublome then exit the ring and as they do medics rush in to assist and aid one Stevie Richards.* Haywood Jublome and Stevie Richards battled to a time limit draw. Hannibal Manson v. Jack Reed (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) As announced yesterday, Handey Gripwell replaced the arrested Hannibal Manson in this contest. The two battled in a sup-par match for a few minutes, before Handey Gripwell got himself disqualified by using his finisher, (aren't finishers supposed to defeat your opponent, not yourself? *grins*) the Testicular Claw. Jack Reed d. Handey Gripwell by disqualification. Mayne Mann v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) This was a competitive contest, but interference played a role. Joe Satre came to the ring, shockingly wearing a Prodigy Hate Club T-shirt. When Mayne Mann was hunched against the ropes, Satre struck, hitting Mann right above the eye with a pair of brass knuckles, and splitting his face open. Canuck was then able to get the pinfall. Crazy Canuck d. Mayne Mann with interference from Joe Satre. EWA North American Championship Match IRK v. 'The ICON' Erik Draven (EWA North American Champion, w/ D-Generation X) This was an incredibly hard-fought matchup, as IRK gave Draven everything he had. The two men battled back and forth the entire match; Draven's power vs. IRK's technical/submission wrestling style. The result was a classic match, and one in which the fans were on their feet for most of. Midway through the match, Draven was able to hit The Fallen Angel on IRK, but when Draven made the cover, IRK was able to get a foot on the ropes to stop the pinfall. Soon after, IRK locked in the Killer Leglock, but it too was to no avail, as Draven refused to tap out. The match ended when Draven went for a chair, and tried to whallop IRK with it while Beulah distracted the referee. IRK kicked Draven in the stomach, blocking the chair shot, and then hit Draven with the chair himself...right as the referee turned around. An unusually hostile crowd, who were actually rooting for IRK in this one due to their hatred of Draven, began chants of "Bulls**t!" as the decision was announced. Erik Draven d. IRK by disqualification. Erik Draven is still the EWA North American Champion. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Eliminators The Eliminators, for lack of a better term, sucked tonight. In what was likely the worst performance of their EWA career, Saturn and Kronus were absolutely manhandled by Lucien and DAP. Neither man seemed too enthused about the match, and it showed, as Darkness picked up a squash victory. Darkness left Saturn and Kronus in pools of their own blood, and the unusually cruel crowd actually began laughing at the performance of the Eliminators as they walked to the back. Darkness d. The Eliminators with Lucien's pin on Perry Saturn after a Russian Legsweep. Vic Deakins v. Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) This match has been rescheduled for WNW. New Demolition (w/ The Modern Day Warriors) v. The Public Enemy (w/ D-Generation X) No love lost between these two teams, as the match was quickly deemed no disqualification. New Demoltiion put on a strong showing against the former EWA World Tag Team Champions, but Public Enemy was hyped for their return, and with the help of the Drive By, got a victory. Public Enemy d. New Demolition with Ghetto Boy's pin on Buddy Brown after the Drive By. 'Diamond' Dallas Page v. The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) DDP looked good in the opening moments of this contest, sending The Golden One to the outside with a hard lariat to a huge pop from the Bostonians. But once the pace of the match slowed, it was all TGO, as he got another victory, making him still undefeated since his return to action. After the match, however, Shawn Copeland sprinted to the ring, and clobbered The Golden One with the European Title belt. ****As Copeland regroups and polishes his belt and grabs a mic, The Golden One is sitting motionless at his feet. You claim you are The Golden One, and that you are some great future star in the EWA, well, listen to this. From the looks of things now, I am the "True Golden One". I am the man that just cracked your CENSORED FOR YOUR PROTECTION skull with the only gold you will ever see around here. You say..." ****The Golden One's leg moves, and Copeland drops the mic and begins to beat on him with his belt again. Carrie, The Golden One's manager is quivering on the mat. Copeland goes over to her, and picks her up by the hair. He sets her up and delivers a perfect "Copelandnator" right on the mat. He glances down and then proceeds to beat on her with his European Title until she has a noticable gash over her eye. He stops and polishes his belt and picks his mic back up. Dallas Page with the Golden Edge. Intoxication-X v. 'The Money Maker' Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion) & 'The Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels (w/ D-Generation X) A solid tag team match between the "Xs" of the EWA. The crowd was behind the burned-out group, as Intoxication-X got a pretty big pop from the crowd, compared with the huge chorus of boos that D-Generation X recieved. The match was underway, and was an evenly-fought match, when KG sprinted to the ring, grabbed Shawn Michaels, quickly nailed a belly-to-belly suplex, and then dashed through the crowd before Copeland could get ahold of him. D-Generation X d. Intoxication-X by disqualification. EWA World Television Championship Match 'Mr. Every Night' Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion) Another exciting Television championship match, this match had the fans cheering for both men. But tonight was just not the night for The Gunslinger. As he took a little too long going to the top rope, Hicks knocked him off, and quickly locked in the Saturday Night Special, forcing Gunslinger to submit. Justin Hicks d. The Gunslinger by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks is the NEW EWA World Television Champion!
