EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

8 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 8, 1998 Live, from The Palace Of Auburn Hills, Detroit, Michigan. Live, from The Palace Of Auburn Hills Detroit, Michigan Balder v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) Ragnarok v. Vertigo EWA First Round King Of The Ring Tournament Match Lucien (w/ DAP) v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) v. James Mastin Hans Gruber v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ The IV Horsemen) Two Smooth v. Meanie Madhouse "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Ruger Ice EWA First Round King Of The Ring Tournament Match "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. Lee Tenn EWA World Television Championship Match Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ The N.O.R.) v. Jack Reed (EWA World Television Champion) Non Title Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Hitman" Bret Hart "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock v. "The Human Suplex Machine' Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) EWA European Championship Match Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion) First Round EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Match "The Beast" Dan Severn v. Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) Hannibal Manson v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Horsemen) Submission Match The Wyldsyde v. Prodigy EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Showstoppers (w/ The Horsemen) v. The Eliminators (EWA World Tag Team Champions) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT Unification Of The U.S. and Canadian Titles -- North American Championship Match Vic Deakins (EWA Canadian Champion) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Champion) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Balder v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) In place of Balder, La Parka from The Circus showed up, and got a forfeit win, as The Crippler didn't, La Parka d. Chris Benoit by forfeit. Ragnarok v. Vertigo Ragnarok didn't show up, and Vertigo also got a forfeit win. EWA First Round King Of The Ring Tournament Match Lucien (w/ DAP) v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) This was an outstanding contest, with a lot of near-falls. Lucien was very impressive in the match, but as he was picking up Troy, Troy nailed a finely executed small package for the three count. Castor Troy d. Lucien with a small package. Castor Troy advances in the King Of The Ring Tournament. Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) v. James Mastin James Mastin was basically pummeled into oblivion, as Canuck impressed many throughout this match, although he recieved a huge amount of boos from the crowd. Crazy Canuck d. James Mastin with the Lunatic Bomb. Hans Gruber v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ The IV Horsemen) Neither man showed up. Two Smooth v. Meanie Madhouse *SIGHS* Neither team showed up. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Ruger Ice In, I guess, what was a trend, Steve Austin didn't show, and Ruger got the forfeit win. EWA First Round King Of The Ring Tournament Match "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. Lee Tenn Can you guess what happened here? Lee Tenn didn't show; Satre advances in the KOTR. EWA World Television Championship Match Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ The N.O.R.) v. Jack Reed (EWA World Television Champion) Well, I'll be damned; another no-show. Jack Reed retains the TV Title. Non Title Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Hitman" Bret Hart Oh my god! A match! These two men fought for about ten minutes, until Vic Deakins walked to the ring. Copeland, upon seeing him, nailed him with a right, causing Deke to attack Copeland. Hart got into the fray, and the three men brawled for a couple of minutes before being separated. Shawn Copeland d. Bret Hart by disqualification. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock v. "The Human Suplex Machine' Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) Shamrock was completely annihilated in this match; Taz didn't even need the Tazmission, as he got the pin after a T-Bone Tazplex. Taz d. Ken Shamrock with a T-Bone Tazplex. EWA European Championship Match Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion) Before the contest, President Gates gave Makaveli the original European Title belt, telling him that if he pawned this one off, he'd have to pay for the making of new belts...for every EWA championship. This turned out to be a brutal contest, as both men introduced foreign objects into the fray. The official tried to maintain some degree of order, but in the end, was forced to throw the match out. Jack McCammon and Makaveli battled to a double disqualification. Makaveli retains the EWA European Championship. First Round EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Match "The Beast" Dan Severn v. Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) In what had to have annoyed President Gates, Severn didn't show. Thorne Richards advances in the King Of The Ring Tournament. Hannibal Manson v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Horsemen) Manson = no-show. SmirtDogg wins. Submission Match The Wyldsyde v. Prodigy These two men are two of the top in their game, and it showed tonight. A classic battle waged on, with both men trying various types of submission holds. Prodigy shocked many fans with some of the submissions he used, but in the end, President Gates, Jack Reed, and Crazy Canuck walked to the ring. Canuck and Reed both nailed Prodigy with a chair, and The Wyldsyde locked on a Figure Four. The referee determined that Prodigy was unconscious, and called for the bell. Afterward, in a truly disgusting act, President Gates slapped the unconscious Prodigy. The Wyldsyde d. Prodigy after Prodigy was unable to answer the referee. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Showstoppers (w/ The Horsemen) v. The Eliminators (EWA World Tag Team Champions) This was a great match, with an extremely strange occurance. *Suddenly the lights go out in the arena as Saturn and KG stand in the ring. A voice comes over the speakers, but we can't make it out because it's been muffled. Lasers are turned on and flying around the arena.* ?????: You still don't get it do you boys? This is all that you can show for yourselves? It won't get the job done...you will see that when I wish to end you. Just remember...you can't catch what you can't see. And you've got to see it to believe it. *With that a loud bang is heard as a firework goes off, and the lights instantly come back on.* Saturn stood staring at the entrance way, and when he turned around, KG nailed him with The Blackout for the three count. The Showstoppers d. The Eliminators with KG's pin on Perry Saturn after The Blackout. The Showstoppers are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions! DOUBLE MAIN EVENT Unification Of The U.S. and Canadian Titles -- North American Championship Match Vic Deakins (EWA Canadian Champion) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Champion) This was a classic matchup, which even saw Rebecca Claridge threaten to hit Shae Marks before Hicks pulled her away. Once the action got inside the ring, it was fast and furious; Hicks locked on the Saturday Night Special, but Deakins refused to tap out, and finally reached the ropes. Deke then nailed the EMP, but Hicks kicked out of it. Finally, Deakins performed an incredible move -- The EMP off the top rope, and gained the victory. Vic Deakins d. Justin Hicks with a top-rope EMP. Vic Deakins wins the unification match, and is the first ever EWA North American Champion! EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) *The theme music to Space Odyssey: 2001 plays over the PA and the Nature Boy Ric Flair makes his way down to the ring. The crowd erupts as Flair is handed a microphone* "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair: I've got a few things that need to be said, so I suggest than everyone who cares what wrestling stands for, at least, what wrestling used to stand for, pay attention. The Nature Boy isn't a senile fool, I know where wrestling is headed, and to be honest, if guys like Jeff Bunda are the future of wrestling, I want no part of it. The whole purpose of wrestling used to be who could beat who in the ring, but now it's nothing but a circus of who can humiliate who and make them look like an idiot. There used to be class in professional wrestling, but the crap that Jeff Bunda has done the past few days has shown me exactly what wrestling is becoming. Don't get me wrong, I have respect for Jeff Bund a because I know what a quality athlete he is, and he is talented, but he's the posterboy for wrestling in the late 1990s and the Nature Boy doesn't belong there. I could go on and on about what wrestling is all about, but since the attention span of the EWA fans and personel is practically non-existent, I'll spare you. It used to be that when a man who's accomplished what the Nature Boy has over the years, you always treated him with class and respect, yet at the same time tried to dethrone him. Unfortunately there is no class and respect here in the EWA, so on July 7, 1998, the Nature Boy Ric Flair is officially retiring from professional wrestling. Now all of those kids who think they had something to do with causing my retirement, don't flatter yourself, I still have what it takes to compete in the ring, I'm simply choosing not to. So EWA, wrestling, fans everyone, the Nature Boy Ric Flair has retired. *Flair sighs and drops the microphone, leaving the ring for the final time* Everyone was shocked at this; President Gates looked on furiously backstage. Jeff Bunda d. Ric Flair by forfeit. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship.
