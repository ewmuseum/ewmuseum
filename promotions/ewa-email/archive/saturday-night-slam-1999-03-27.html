Yes, that's right I am talking about me and my fantastic belt.. (Tanaka points to the TV belt..) I just thought I had to make this small gibble of me talking to congratulate myself.. Lee Tenn As most expected when this match was first signed, this was a very good match. Before the match, it was announced that this fell under "Raven's Rules" And both men took advantage of this to the fullest extent possible, pummeling each other with everything but the kitchen sink, in the early going. After several minutes of trading maneuvers, chair shots and ball bat shots, to the dome, with no clear cut edge to either, Tenn had the first advantage after following up a Snap Suplex with a hotshot. Tenn threw his man to the outside and hit him with a violent chair shot upside the head before executing a piledriver on the concrete floor, Tenn Slid in the ring, but then remembered that a countout victory would do no good, and then slid back out, to stop the count. But Pins was waiting for him with what looked like a roll of quarters that he pulled out of his trunks and Pins then slid back in the ring himself . Somehow, Tenn made it back in the ring, but Tenn was nearly out on his feet at this point. After a belly to back suplex, Pins hit a gutwrench suplex, and followed up with a knee to the head and a Frogsplash from the top rope and several other manuevers and was also unable to get the three count as well. The match continued for several long minutes with both men going at it something fierce, Tenn got brainbuster in on Pins and followed up with a side-russian-legsweep and then picked Pins up until Tenn and whipped Pins into the ropes and put his head down for a backdrop, he telegraphed the maneuver, and Pins nailed him with a ddt, hooked the legs and got the hard fought win Max Pins d.
