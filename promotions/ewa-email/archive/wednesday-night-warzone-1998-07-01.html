EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

1 July 1998

The Patriot (w/ Madusa) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Horsemen) Lucien (w/ Darkness) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin Lee Tenn v. James Mastin EWA World Television Championship Match Jack Reed v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (EWA World Television Champion) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Dark Angelic Priest (w/ Lucien) v. Perry Saturn (1/2 of the EWA U.S./World Tag Team Champions) Masked Terror v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) "Legendary" Brett Barnes v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond & Fat Albert) Two Smooth v. Vertigo EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Mayne Mann (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Horsemen) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Special Tag Team Match Ordered By President Gates KG & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (The Showstoppers, w/ The Horsemen) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ Madusa) and "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg Ladder Match To Determine The UNDISPUTED EWA European Champion Makaveli (w/ the stolen EWA European Championship belt) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ the newly made EWA European Title belt) Both belts will be suspended above the ring; whoever gets them first becomes undisputed European Champion. Red Meanie (w/ Meanie Madhouse) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) Red Meanie put up a good fight at the beginning of the contest, but Johnny 2 Bad was simply too impressive, taking out Red Meanie in quick fashion with his patented Double Hammer. Johnny 2 Bad d. Red Meanie with the Double Hammer. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Blue Meanie (w/ Meanie Madhouse) v. "The Beast" Dan Severn Severn's debut saw him completely destroy the Blue Meanie, dominating him with a variety of submission holds. Dan Severn d. Blue Meanie by submission with the Bow And Arrow Submission. Dan Severn gains entrance into the King Of The Ring Tournament. (the doors to the arena slam open and Canuck walk though them. He notices a poster saying "I Hate Prodigy Club join today" He looks down and sees a signup sheet with one name on it. He picks up a pen and begins to sign his name to the sheet. He finishes and disappears up a fleet of stairs) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Horsemen) Shawn Michaels apparently didn't care, as he didn't show. Aaron Martin gains entrance into the King Of The Ring Tournament. Lucien (w/ Darkness) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin A good solid match, with a surprising ending...Lucien grabbed Austin's foot as he went to kick him in the stomach, and rolled him up for a three count. Lucien d. Steve Austin with a rollup. Lee Tenn v. James Mastin Lee Tenn didn't even bother to show up; Mastin got a forfeit win. EWA World Television Championship Match Jack Reed v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (EWA World Television Champion) Haynes started this contest off in complete control, dominating Deliant, who started off. However, Dr. Lander rang the bell and unleashed Jack Reed, who simply annihilated Haynes. Jack Reed d. Will Haynes with a gutwrench suplex into a powerbomb. Jack Reed is the new EWA World Television Champion!! EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Dark Angelic Priest (w/ Lucien) v. Perry Saturn (1/2 of the EWA U.S./World Tag Team Champions) DAP started off this match in good fashion, slipping out of a quick Death Valley Driver attempt by Saturn and going to work. However, Saturn was simply too much for him tonight, and got the victory. Perry Saturn d. DAP with the Death Valley Driver. Perry Saturn gains entrance into the King Of The Ring Tournament. Masked Terror v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) Masked Terror has left the EWA; Taz gets the forfeit win. "Legendary" Brett Barnes v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond & Fat Albert) Barnes and Jackson had quite a battle, which ended with Barnes being on the wrong side of the Flip, Flop, And Fly. Rufus Lamar Jackson III d. Brett Barnes with the Flip, Flop, And Fly. Two Smooth v. Vertigo This was a solid tag team match between two of the young teams in the EWA. Neither seemed to gain the advantage throughout the match, until finally, Heavy Metal Maniac, out of nowhere, began headbanging on the ring apron. This completely confused Terry Conklin, allowing Jimmy Pow to roll him up from behind for the victory. Vertigo d. Two Smooth with Jimmy Pow's rollup pin on Terry Conklin. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Mayne Mann (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Horsemen) *Once Mann and Copeland have gathered in the ring and are ready to go at it, suddenly the theme music from "Space Odyssey" 2001 blares over the PA and the fans erupt as the one and only Nature Boy Ric Flair appears in the entrance way. Flair has a grin on his face as he makes his way down to the ring, and once in the ring he grabs the microphone* "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair: Whooooo! *Crowd erupts* Well isn't this something, two "Horsemen" are about to go at it, what's going to happen? *Flair pauses for a moment and pretends to have weights on both hands, lifing and rising them* Oh who gives a damn? *Crowd erupts and Flair grins* Let's face it, you two are the biggest disgrace to the IV Horsemen since Curt Hennig and don't even deserve to be the original IV Horsemen's towel boys. But the Nature Boy isn't stupid, I know when I'm outnumbered, only the Nature Boy is smart enough to do something about it. The two of you and the rest of your little friends can call yourselves the IV Horsemen all you want, but the fact of the matter is that you don't even come close to the talent that the former IV Horsemen here in the EWA possessed. And to be honest with you both, I'm getting sick and tired of you boys dragging the IV Horsemen name in the mud, so it's time the Nature Boy took action. *Suddenly "Self High Five" blares over the PA and the fans absolutely begin to ballstic. But surprisingly, no one comes out of the entrance way. Copeland has a grin on his face and grabs the microphone away from Flair* Shawn Copeland: Where's your precious DDP now? *The camera shifts to the seating area behind Copeland and Mann, where DDP is making his way towards the ring. Copeland and Mann are jawing with Flair as DDP enters the ring, to a HUGE pop from the crowd. DDP taps Copeland on the back with a huge grin on his face and executes a Diamond Cutter on him, then does the same to Mann. Flair and DDP embrace and DDP grabs the microphone from the mat* Diamond Dallas Page: Boys, now that's what the real IV Horsemen are all about right there. *DDP kicks Copeland in the gut and the crowd erupts* I've been talking to Ric lately, and I've heard what he's had to say about these sorry excuses for Horsemen, and like the Nature Boy said, it was time to take some action. And all of the EWA fans know, there's no action like the *DDP forms the Diamond Cutter sign and throws his hands down along with the fans* BANG!!! *Suddenly Shawn Michaels and KG appear in the entrance way with a microphone* Shawn Michaels: Well it looks like the posterboy for Geritol and Mr. Playboy are back. *KG and Michaels laugh* Listen you two, you're looking at the new IV Horsemen whether you like it or not, and there's not a damn thing you can do about it when you're dealing with the Heartbreak Kid, HBK, Shawm Michaels. *KG is nodding* So in case your 300 year old mind needs reminding, mess with HBK and you're going to get put to sleep, which should have happened a long time ago, with some Sweet Chin Music! *Shawn Michaels and KG continue to jaw with Flair and DDP some more when suddenly Chris Benoit emerges from the entrance way and begins to brawl with HBK and KG. Flair and DDP rush down to the entrance way to help out Benoit and security finally breaks out the five after a few moments* After the ruckus was finally cleared, Copeland simply laid down for Mann. Mayne Mann d. Shawn Copeland. Mayne Mann gains entrance into the King Of The Ring Tournament. (As Mayne Mann walks through the entrance way back to the backstage area, we hear a small commotion. All of a sudden, without any entrance music, Prodigy walks through the curtain and the crowd starts to go insane. Prodigy has a folding chair in tow and he stares intently at the ring as he approaches it. As he reaches ringside, he tosses the chair into the ring and hops up onto the apron. Climbing through the ropes, he sets the chair up in the middle of the ring and sits down. Realizing that he's missing something, he hops back up and goes to the corner of the ring. He eyes the ring announcer and without a word or motion, the ring announcer hands Prodigy the microphone. He begins to make his way to his seat) Now before I begin, I want everyone to shut their mouths and open their ears, because what I'm gonna say is gonna be shockin and might amaze ya. See, Prodigy has a little story to tell y'all (Prodigy sits down in the chair) See, there was no prior committment that I couldn't break on Saturday. I got to my hotel room, which thanks to Gates ended up being a roach infested hell hole frequented by the unwanteds in society, and I found sitting in my EWA Wrestler's package a ticket to the next show and a copy of the contract that I supposedly signed. The Wyldsyde in a submission match, and a ticket to Vancouver. Now I thought to myself, "Ya know Prodigy, this could be good. The Wyldsyde seems decent, and this could be a real test. And what a way to show Gates up after he screwed me over last week against The Walking Triple Threat than to beat another one of his "superior" opponents....beat the rat bastard at his own game." So I hauled ass on over tot he airport, took my lower than luggage-class ticket and flew to Vancouver with a mule as my travel partner. Named him Duane, because it only seemed fitting to call an ass Duane. I wind up in Vancouver and follow all the directions to the arena and I! ended up at some little run down hole. Know what was waiting for me there? A little note.... (Prodigy stands up and reaches into his pocket and produces a note on EWA Letterhead.) This is the note I found. "Prodigy, by the time you read this, you'll have missed your match and have become the laughing stock of the EWA because you couldn't face the music. Gates". So what did I do? Sure, I could have trashed theplace, but that would have been an improvement. No. I found a way to this very arena tonight to give all of you a message, and in particular you Gates. (Prodigy stands up out of his chair and looks to the skyboxes high above the ring. He points to one) Gates, you may try to hold me back. You may try to keep me down and embarass me. But beleive me, it will take a lot more than that to put me away. I'll take The Wyldsyde anynight you want me to, and in any matchyou deem the most inappropriate for me. I won't say quit, but rest assured that by the time the macth is over you'll not only hear The Wyldsyde say those words, but he'll beg me to spare his life. Remember what words he uses Gates, because I'll spare his pathetic hide. You'll need those words, because if I ever get my hands on you, you'll need to utter them. But I'll take it all out on you.....for me.....for Skippy,.....and for everyone out there who ever wanted to see you go down(There's a huge response form the crowd, cheering like mad), and the words.....hell, the National Guard won't be able to save you. (Prodigy looks away from the box and fixes his eyes on the entrance way) Wyldsyde, practice the magic words. "Prodigy, I give". Practice them until they become second nature. Next Wednesday, submission may not be my strong point, but a good old fashioned beating is. I might make you submit to a fancy move, but I'll beat you to an inch of your life and make you beg for that. I'll break you and make you quit, not because you want to....because you have to in order to save yourself. Wednesday......don't plan on watching the fireworks. They don't allow them in hospital zones (Prodigy throws the microphone into the crowd and leaves the ring to a huge pop from the crowd.) DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Special Tag Team Match Ordered By President Gates KG & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (The Showstoppers, w/ The Horsemen) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock (w/ Madusa) and "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg Shamrock and SmirtDogg came to the ring separately, and didn't even look at each other before the match. Both men wanted to start the contest, and finally they agreed on Shamrock starting against KG. The match went on for about ten minutes with neither man able to gain an advantage, until finally, SmirtDogg tagged in Shamrock while HBK was in the ring. From there, Shamrock suplexed him abd placed him in the Ankle Submission, but KG made the save, only to turn around and get leveled with the Gangsta's Paradise. Shamrock then tagged in Smirt, who quickly pinned KG. SmirtDogg & Ken Shamrock d. The Showstoppers with SmirtDogg's pin on KG after The Gangsta's Paradise. Ladder Match To Determine The UNDISPUTED EWA European Champion Makaveli (w/ the stolen EWA European Championship belt) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ the newly made EWA European Title belt) Both belts will be suspended above the ring; whoever gets them first becomes undisputed European Champion. One of the best ladder matches in recent memory, these two men fought everywhere in the arena, even venturing to the parking lot at one point, where Stevie whipped Makaveli into the limousine of President Gates, shattering the passenger window. However, Makaveli out-thought Richards in this one, as with some rope given to him by Suge Knight, he tied Stevie to the steering wheel of the limo after knocking him out with a chair. Makaveli then went all the way back to the arena, and as he started to climb the ladder, Stevie ran down the aisle. Stevie jumped in the ring and dove at the ladder, knocking it and Makaveli over...but it was too late, as Makaveli had the belts in hand. Makaveli d. Stevie Richards. Makaveli is the new EWA European Champion!!
