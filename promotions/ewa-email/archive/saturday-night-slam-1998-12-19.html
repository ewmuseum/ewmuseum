Special Ordered Tag Team Match Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) & "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Corporate Empire) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) & The Golden One An interesting note before the match...The Golden One and Erik Draven actually came to the ring together, to the DX theme. TGO started off against Vincent Jackson, and quickly wore the big man down, surprisingly. Jackson quickly made the tag to SmirtDogg, who executed a chop-block to the knee of TGO, sending him sprawling to the mat. Smirt began to work over on The Golden One, but surprisingly, stopped, and pointed to Draven, telling The Golden One to tag him in. The Golden One looked to the crowd, and they roared their approval as The ICON was tagged in. Smirt and Draven then launched a flurry of fistacuffs, but when Draven started to overpower SmirtDogg, Jackson stepped over the top rope, and blasted Draven with a lariat. The Golden One quickly dropped down from the apron, and retrieved a chair. He climbed back in the ring, and headed straight for SmirtDogg. TGO swung the chair at Smirt's head...but, in a shocking move, stopped it halfway through the swing and belted Draven with it. The trio then began to put the boots to Draven, joined by President Gates, who had jogged to the ring, grinning like a little kid on Christmas morning. Gates shook TGO's hand, and then the crowd roared, as DX charged the ring. The camera faded to black with a huge brawl between the Corporate Empire and DX. The Golden One/Erik Draven v. SmirtDogg/Vincent Jackson match was rendered a no contest.
