EWA Friday Night Slam

3 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 3, 1998 Live, from The Wheeling Civic Center, Wheeling, West Virginia. "Legendary" Brett Barnes (w/ Kim) v. Lee Tenn EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebecca Claridge) Joe Lemon v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart Two Smooth v. Ragnarok (w/ Odin) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) v. Darkness (w/ Razor) Thor (w/ Odin) v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Crazy Canuck If Haynes still has the TV Title, this will be a non-title match. Hans Gruber (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match John Kronus (1/2 of EWA U.S./World Tag Team Champions) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (EWA Canadian Champion) The Widow Maker (w/ Razor) v. "The Beast" Dan Severn Handey Gripwell v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) "The Bigg Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) Ruger Ice v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. The Wyldsyde No Holds Barred Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock Hell In The Cell Match KG (w/ The Horsemen) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg "Legendary" Brett Barnes (w/ Kim) v. Lee Tenn It was announced that Barnes had left the EWA, but Tenn didn't show up. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Rebecca Claridge) Austin looked sluggish tonight, as Hicks was all over the ring, dominating with a variety of moves. The fans actually cheered Hicks during this match..apparently his attitude is getting over with the fans. Justin Hicks d. Steve Austin by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks gains entrance into The King Of The Ring Tournament. Joe Lemon v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart The Hitman looked impressive in his EWA debut, making short work of Lemon. Bret Hart d. Joe Lemon by submission with The Sharpshooter. Two Smooth v. Ragnarok (w/ Odin) Two Smooth didn't show up for the match, so Ragnarok got the forfeit win. Ragnarok d. Two Smooth by forfeit. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) This contest went for over twenty minutes without a decision, and ended up as a time limit draw. As a result, in place of this slot in the KOTR will be a bye. Makaveli and Taz battled to a time limit draw. Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) v. Darkness (w/ Razor) In Round 2 of the battle between these two teams, Darkness came out on top, defeated Scorpio & Thompson. Darkness d. Affirmitive Action with Lucien's pin on 2 Cold Scorpio after a Death Valley Driver. Thor (w/ Odin) v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) This one ended with the Mastermind hitting the Face/Off out of nowhere. Castor Troy d. Thor with the Face/Off. Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Crazy Canuck If Haynes still has the TV Title, this will be a non-title match. Will came to the ring promising to show Jeff Bunda he belonged in the Triple Threat. Will Haynes is in the ring. And here comes his opponent, a rookie here in the EWA. Crazy Canuck. With him is Jack Reed and Doctor Lander. And we see what they have on. " I hate Prodigy " T-shirts. They make their way down to the ring. Crazy Canuck gets in the ring, while the other members are watching at ringside. Jack Reed has his Television Title and he is letting Will Haynes know it. From here, we saw a solid matchup between two young athletes, but in the end, The Lunatic Bomb sealed the debut win for Prodigy Hate Club Member #0000003. Crazy Canuck d. Will Haynes with the Lunatic Bomb. Hans Gruber (w/ Hannibal Manson) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) Hans Gruber didn't even bother to show up, which infuriated Bad, who demanded some competition. Out came James Mastin, who was promptly beaten in forty five seconds. Johnny 2 Bad d. James Mastin with the Double Hammer. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match John Kronus (1/2 of EWA U.S./World Tag Team Champions) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (EWA Canadian Champion) This was possibly the best King Of The Ring Qualifying match of all. Deakins and Kronus showed why they are two of the toughest men in wrestling today, brawling all over the ringside area. Neither man ever took a clear advantage, until finally Kronus went to back drop Deakins. Deakins, who was coming off of the ropes, stopped, and hooked Kronus for the EMP in what turned out to be a terrific match. Vic Deakins d. John Kronus with the EMP. Vic Deakins gains entrance into The King Of The Ring Tournament. The Widow Maker (w/ Razor) v. "The Beast" Dan Severn One word: SQUASH. Dan Severn d. The Widow Maker by submission with the Bow And Arrow Submission. Handey Gripwell v. Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) Gripwell was attacked on the outside of the ring to start the match, as a woman in the front row he was trying to pick up kicked him in the...well, you know. The match went on for awhile, until finally, Gripwell hit his finisher, the Testicular Claw. However, the ref saw it, and disqualified Gripwell, who was promptly given a beating by the N.O.R. Rufus Lamar Jackson III d. Handey Gripwell by DQ. "The Bigg Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) (The announcer calls out the name of Jack "The Man" McCammon. The PA system erupts with "Sick Boys" by Social Distortion, but nobody comes out. Frustrated, the announcer again calls out McCammon, but again, to no avail. Now looking quite nervous, the announcer calls out Jack a third time. His opponent, "The Bigg Dogg" Aaron Martin, stands in the ring and grins, expecting to get a no show victory. The announcer gives up, and exits the ring. As the referee is about to raise Martin's hand in victory, a roar can be heard from backstage. The noise grows rapidly, and is soon deafening as the 1973 Dodge Dart owned and maintained by Jack "The Man" McCammon comes bursting through the entryway, leaving rubble in it's wake. The car barely fits up the wide aisle, and comes screeching to a halt at the ring. The door opens, and The Chain by Fleetwood Mac comes blaring out from the radio. Out steps "The Man" himself, looking more riled up than ever before. With a quick bound, he jumps up onto the hood of his car, then leaps over the top rope, landing on his feet. While Jack is entering the ring, the passenger door opens, and out emerges Phil. He walks quietly over to the side, and gives the bell a solid ring. McCammon hears the bell, and attacks Martin.) ) From there, Martin got in a few punches and moves, but McCammon looked rejuvenated and pumped up, and soon took complete control of the match, nailing a powerbomb to secure the win. Afterward, Johnny 2 Bad, who had watched the match from what was the entrance way, climbed inside the ring, raised The Man's hand, and then walked quietly with him to the back. The crowd then began chanting "Jack Is Back! Jack Is Back!" Jack McCammon d. Aaron Martin with a powerbomb. Ruger Ice v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair {Ruger Ice walks out of his dressing room and a camera crew is standing by} RUGER: Flair...it is time for you to receive what you have earned. It is time for you to enter a world of pain. It is time that just be served for the crime that you committed. {Just before Ruger walks towards the curtain he looks back in his dressing room and motions for someone to come out. It is the little boy from the homeless shelter. As Ruger walks down to the ring the little boy is at his side. Ruger sets him up a chair next to the announcer table.} Flair looked stunned, but nonetheless, continued with the match. Ruger gave a great effort, nearly pinning Flair on numerous occasions, but Flair finally regained the advantage, and pinned Ruger with his feet on the ropes, and while pulling the tights. EWA King Of The Ring Qualifying Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. The Wyldsyde [Thorne comes out of the locker rooms with the 1997 TOWC trophy. He waves it as he makes his way to the ring. Before he steps in, he gets the mic and says the following] Thorne: Well now, isn't this grand. The first and the latest. While there are a lot of people out here that think newer is better, we all know the truth. You're nothing more than a facsimile of me when it comes to being the best wrestler in e-wrestling. And just like anything else you try to clone, the duplicate is NEVER as good as the original. For those that don't agree, they WILL learn to believe. [The crowd gives a good pop, as the Wyldsyde looks rather ambivolent, but starts to get a bit incensed] Thorne: See this? **waves his trophy** You're nothing more than the second man to reach the finish line. And now that we're about to get it on, you can expect a one-way trip into the jaws of the Loch Ness Lock to be your runner-up prize. Poor Wyldsyde... And I hardly knew ye. [With that, he slides right into the ring and brandishes his trophy right at The Wyldsyde, clearing the ring as he waves his trophy to the fans.] The match turned out to go through nineteen minutes of the twenty minute time limit. Both men hit their respective finishers, but Wyldsyde did not submit, and Thorne kicked out of the Wyldryde. Finally, Thorne nailed an old move of his, The London Bridge, and got the three count to the delight of the crowd...perhaps Thorne is finally ending his sophomore slump. Thorne Richards d. The Wyldsyde with The London Bridge. Thorne Richards gains entrance into The King Of The Ring Tournament. No Holds Barred Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Horsemen) v. "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock Shamrock dominated this match at the onset, but a low blow by Michaels quickly changed the tide...for a few minutes. Shamrock began to fight back once again, only to have Shawn Copeland come out and help Michaels beat up Shamrock. Dan Severn ran to help, but KG was lying in wait, and blindisded him with a chair. Copeland hit the Money Shot, Michaels hit the Sweet Chin Music, and that was all she wrote. Shawn Michaels d. Ken Shamrock with the Sweet Chin Music. *Following the HBK-Shamrock match, the fans are getting pumped up expecting the cage to be lowered for the Main Event. However, suddenly the theme music to "Space Odyssey: 2001" plays over the PA and none other than the Nature Boy Ric Flair appears in the entrance way. The crowd erupts as Flair enters the ring and is handed a microphone* "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair: Whoooooooo! *Crowd erupts* Now here's how it's going to go down, because the Nature Boy has got some "issues" with one Mr. Mayne Mann. Mann, get your CENSORED down here so you can go for a ride on Spaaaaaaace Mountain! *The crowd erupts as Flair motions towards the entrance way and Mayne Mann appears, and the fans begin to boo loudly. Mann enters the ring and grabs the microphone from Flair* Mayne Mann: Old man, that was about the biggest mistake you could have made. But just to make things a little more 'interesting'- *Mann points to the entrance way and Shawn Copeland appears, drawing a chorus of boos from the fans as he makes his way down to the ring. Copeland and Mann stand on one side of the ring and Flair stands on the other* "The Seat-Filler" Shawn Copeland: Mr. Geritol, it's time for a whippin' Horsemen-style. *Copeland makes his way towards Flair, then suddenly, out of nowhere, Mann comes from behind Copeland and nails him with the Boston Bomb. The crowd erupts as Mann drags Copeland over to the center of the ring and Flair locks on the Figure Four, holding the IV Horsemen sign high in the air. Flair releases the hold after a minute, and he and Mann both give the IV Horsemen sign and leave the ring, with Copeland still in. Copeland comes too a few moments later, unsure of what happened, and stumbles backstage as the cage decends on the ring* Hell In The Cell Match KG (w/ The Horsemen) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg *As the Hell in the Cell is lowering -- the EWA jumbotron comes to life. On the giant size screen appears "God's Gift to Wrestling", SmirtDogg. We can tell that he's back in the locker room area. Looking at SmirtDogg it is obvious that he's completely focused for what lies ahead. The intensity is written all over his face. His eyes display a killer instinct never seen before in him. SmirtDogg appears to be a man on a mission. I wouldn't want to be KG right now. Upon seeing SmirtDogg the crowd boos him instantaneously but it seems to have no affect on the man. SmirtDogg ignores all of the negativity and begins to speak.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: KG -- over the past few months you have been an annoying little gnat bothering me every step of the way. You've had minimal affect on me but not in the way you wanted. You've never intimidated me, you've never beaten me in the ring, and you've never gotten me off my game. However, you have successfully ticked me off. Because of you I have had to waste my precious time on several occassions just so I could get into the ring with you and prove once again that I'm the better wrestler. To also prove that you have no right to even be in the same ring as me. I've spent more time than I ever intended to -- trying to make you see the light. The light being the fact that you never could and never will be able to contend with me, "God's Gift to Wrestling". But no matter how severely I beat you -- you keep coming back for more. Tonight, will end that once and for all. After the punishment and physical abuse I put you through in that Hell in the Cell -- you will finally come to the realization of what your role is and where you stand in the wrestling world. You will have no choice but to accept that you're stuck in the mud at the bottom of the EWA talent pool. KG, I'm putting you into place and there's not a damn thing you can do about it. As you may or may not know I'm a very religious man and anything can happen in that Hell in the Cell -- tonight. Not to me obviously but to you -- KG -- you're lucky if you make it out alive. That is why I've gone through a tremendous amount of trouble just to show a bit of kindness towards you -- even though I despise you. I searched every church in the West Virginia area to come up with the best priest who is capable for this job. After days of intensive investigating I finally found the Priest who'll read you your Last Rights as you enter combat for what will probably be the last time. And you didn't think I had a heart? *he laughs* Let me bring the homie...err Priest out here so KG may die in peace. *Arriving on the scene is a tall athletically built African American man donned in full Priest attire. The black robe, the white collar -- the whole nine yards. Upon further examination there's no doubt that the man is Booker T dressed up as a Priest. "The Priest" begins to speak.* "The Priest": Our father, thou art in heaven, have mercy on KG's soul and if he doesn't make it out of the Hell in the Cell alive tonight let the perilious gates of heaven receive him. KG is a good young lad just a little on the slow side. Don't punish him for this. He has many bright years ahead of him by some miracle let KG come out of this match in one piece. Everyone knows the sucka...err he will lose but his life doesn't have to perish with it. AMEN! Hallejuhah! Praise the Lord! SmirtDogg: AMEN Brother Booker T! *he laughs* *Booker T takes off the Priest attire and reveals his Harlem Heat wrestling tights underneath. Together Smirt and Booker T say these words.* Together: KG, Au Revoir sucka! *The jumbotron fades to black* KG came to the ring first, Shawn Michaels and a dazed Shawn Copeland in tow. Joe Satre was nowhere to be found. SmirtDogg then came to the ring, and climbed to the top of the cage, daring KG to climb up. KG did, and the two battled on top until SmirtDogg DDT'd KG, causing both men to fall through the top. KG got to his feet first, and amazingly, used the section of the cage that was hanging down as a springboard, moonsaulting off of it onto Smirt. KG attempted a pin, but Smirt kicked out. The match went on for awhile, until finally, SmirtDogg tossed KG into the section hanging down, busting KG's forehead open. SmirtDogg, who was also bleeding above the eye and had lost a tooth, then slowly climbed to the top, and incredibly, executed the Air Smirty from the top of the cage through the opening where it had been broken, and onto KG. SmirtDogg slowly covered KG, and got the three count. Almost immediately, the Horsemen ran inside, but instead of beating up Smirt, Copeland and Michaels helped both men up. HBK then took the microphone. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Both these men busted their asses tonight, and I don't think there's any doubt they proved theirselves. So it's time to kick that scrub Joe Satre out of the Horsemen, and introduce our newest member, ladies and gentlemen, Horsemen member #4, I give you God's Gift To Wrestling, I give you SMIRTDOGG!! Smirt's music began to play, and Smirt managed to embrace KG and raise his hand in the air as the camera faded to black.
