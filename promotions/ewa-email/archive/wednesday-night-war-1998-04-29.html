EWA Wednesday Night War

29 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 29, 1998 Live, from SeaGate Center, Toledo, Ohio. First Round EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Tournament New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. The Eliminators New Demolition weren't very on tonight, as Saturn and Kronus made an impressive debut. The Eliminators d. New Demolition with Saturn's pin on Flame after the Total Elimination. (5:33) First Round EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Tournament Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) Prophecy dominated The Harts, plain and simple. Prophecy d. The Hart Brothers with Travis Scott's pin on Shawn Hart after the Cryptorchid. (3:21) First Round EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Tournament Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. The Ghetto Warriors A very competitive match, but SmirtDogg, for possibly the first time in his illustrious career, was caught off-guard when Cherry Pie gave him a kiss. This allowed J-Man to hit Smirt in the back of the head with a foreign object, and cover him for the pin. Sexual Thangs d. Ghetto Warriors with J Man's pin on SmirtDogg. (12:43) First Round EWA U.S. Tag Team Championship Tournament Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) Death Sentance was outmanned in this match, as Tecate Jake and Pacifico Pete looked terrific. Dos Equis d. Death Sentance with Tecate Jake's pin on Pain after the Kamikaze. (7:11) Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Double A didn't show up; President Gates announced that he would be fired if he did not show up on Saturday. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "Handsome" Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Harley didn't show up; forfeit win for Benoit. EWA World Television Championship Return Match The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. The Golden One (EWA World Television Champion) (Note: The Golden One did not roleplay for this match; therefore, due to the rule about this on the Rules Page, The Gunslinger is awarded the victory and the EWA World Television Championship.) "Rowdy" Roddy Piper (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) Another competitive match, but Stevie Richards held on in the end with his Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards d. Roddy Piper with the Stevie Kick. (16:49) Kevin Nash v. The Wyldsyde Kevin Nash was dominated from the start, as The Wyldsyde was extremely impressive in this contest. Thorne Richards watched the match from the top of the entrance ramp. The Wyldsyde d. Kevin Nash with the Wyldryde. (8:18) Scott Hall v. La Parka (w/ The Circus) Hall was, like his partner, dominated by the opposition. La Parka d. Scott Hall with the La Parka Butt Blast. (5:22) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Triple Threat Match Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Thorne Richards (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Galaxy was eliminated rather quickly by Thorne's Loch Ness Lock; and from here, it got interesting. Thorne placed a chokehold on Martin, and The Wyldsyde ran down, screaming at the referee to "follow the rulebook". Thorne began yelling at the Wyldsyde to stay out of his match, and Martin then rolled him up from behind for the pinfall victory. Aaron Martin wins the Triple Threat match. Aaron Martin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. Raven (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, w/ DDT) This was a solid match, but when Wright went for the German Suplex, Raven caught him with an elbow to the face, and the Evenflow. Raven d. Alex Wright with the Evenflow DDT. Raven retains the EWA U.S. Title. The Widow Maker v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) Total domination by Hicks. Justin Hicks d. The Widow Maker with the Saturday Night Special. (7:47) "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Castor Troy Neither man looked particularly well tonight; Michaels defeated Castor when he nailed a sluggish-looking version of the Sweet Chin Music. Shawn Michaels d. Castor Troy with the Sweet Chin Music. (9:56) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) Another terrific tag team match; but Public Enemy were on the top of their game tonight, retaining the titles. Public Enemy d. FlashBack with Swap Meet Louie's forced submission onto Flash Funk after the Swap Meet Leglock. (12:28) KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) This one escalated into a brawl, as Van Smack really got into the head of KooKabura. KooKabura made a comeback toward the end of the match, but from seemingly out of nowhere, Van Smack nailed the Smackdown and got the win. Van Smack d. KooKabura with the SMackdown. (13:09) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Erik Draven v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was a terrific main event, one which saw both Bunda and Draven counter the other's finishers. Draven had nailed the Final Justice on Bunda when Triple-H and Shawn Michaels ran out from the back and attacked Draven. Soon after, Aaron Martin and Justin Hicks ran down, and cleared the ring of the Triple Threat. Erik Draven d. Jeff Bunda by disqualification. (19:33) Jeff Bunda retains the Intercontinental Championship.
