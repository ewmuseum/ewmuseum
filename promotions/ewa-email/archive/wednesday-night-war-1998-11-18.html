EWA Wednesday Night War

18 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
November 18, 1998 Live, from the Mobile Civic Center Mobile, Alabama Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. "King Nerd" Jerry Spinelli The card got off to a rather slow start, as Spinelli pulled a no-show in his second professional match. It was announced that he had found a computer-lovers convention going on in Mobile and well...it's obvious where his priorities were. *** Galaxy d. Jerry Spinelli via forfeit. Joe Lemon v. "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner One word. SQUASH. Gardner made a statement with his re-debut, giving Lemon no chance for any offense whatsoever, and finally polished him off with the Pneumatic Hammer for the victory. *** Joshua Gardner d. Joe Lemon via the Pnuematic Hammer. The Black Angels v. The Dynasty (w/ D-Generation X) On paper, this match looked like a squash for Draven and Michaels, buuuuuut...this is the EWA. Whenever something looks obvious, odds are it isn't gonna happen. Shawn Michaels almost looked like his old self again, pretty much dominating Satchiel for the duration of the match. However, as Satchiel was standing in the Dynasty's corner, Michaels tried the Sweet Chin Music, and the Black Angel ducked, causing Michaels to instead level Erik Draven with the superkick. Satchiel rolled Michaels up and got a two-count, mere inches from a huge upset victory. Draven struggled back to the apron, got tagged in by Michaels, and dropped Satchiel like a bad habit with the Triple-X Bomb for the victory. Michaels left Draven to celebrate in the ring, slowly walking back to the locker room with his head down. *** The Dynasty d. The Black Angels via Erik Draven's Triple-X Bomb on Satchiel. Semifinal World Television Championship Tournament The Rock v. Christian (w/ The Kindred) In your humble narrator's opinion, this match had a great deal of potential. Unfortunately, well...it lived up to about half of it, if that. Both men looked good in their first 'big matches' here in the EWA, but nothing to write home about. Gangrel ordered Christian to do something, Christian didn't like it, and started badmouthing his boss. Bad idea. While Edge distracted the ref, Gangrel leapt into the ring and delivered his Piledriving DDT to Christian, and quickly exited, giving the Rock the opportunity to drop 'the most electrifying move in sports entertainment' on the Brood member's unconscious form. People's Elbow, pin...you know the drill by now. The Rock advances to the next round of the tournament. *** The Rock d. Christian via the People's Elbow. Semifinal World Television Championship Tournament Scott Hall (w/ D-Generation X) v. Gangrel (w/ The Kindred) A lot of people had written Scott Hall off as just a tag team wrestler, but this match served to prove them wrong. Hall showed that he could hold his own without Nash backing him up, even causing him to send the rest of DX back to the locker room when he held the advantage over Gangrel. This proved to be his undoing, however, as when he had Gangrel set up for the Outsider Edge, Christian distracted the referee. With his back turned, the ref didn't see Edge slide into the ring and deliver the Downward Spiral on Hall, giving Gangrel the easy 1, 2, 3 for the victory. *** Gangrel d. Scott Hall via interference from Edge. Morbid Angel v. "The Extremist" Erik Moon (w/ Shannon Starr) Our second 'debut match' featured nothing out of the ordinary, just good, solid chain-wrestling. Both men made respectable debuts, but in the end the 'Extremity' of Moon finally won out, as he hit a huge legdrop from the top rope to the outside, sending Morbid Angel right through the Spanish Announcers' table. Moon rolled Angel back in, hit the Lunar Eclipse, and that was all she wrote. *** Erik Moon d. Morbid Angel via the Lunar Eclipse. "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ D-Generation X) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) This was easily the biggest surprise of the night. As was the case with Hall, nobody figured that Big Sexy could hold his own in a singles bout, thinking that he'd gotten too reliant on having somebody watching his back. Tonight, however, this was not the case. Hicks and Nash went toe-to-toe, neither man having a clear-cut advantage until the very end of the match, when Hicks had Nash staggered in the corner, and climbed the ropes to start pounding on his head. Nash managed to clear the cobwebs, carry Hicks two steps forward, and drop him face-first on the top turnbuckle with his patented 'Snake Eyes' maneuver. From this point, it was all academic as Nash hit his Jackknife Powerbomb in the center of the ring for the victory. Instead of DX's music blasting over the PA, however, 'Wherever I May Roam' echoed throughout the arena, which immediately caused the fans to launch into a loud chorus of boos. The lights dimmed to a dark purple, and Vincent Jackson entered through a large pyro display, the flames not even seeming to faze him. Jackson stepped up onto the apron, and quickly tossed Hicks over the top rope. The two giants locked in a staredown, and had to be separated by virtually the entire EWA referee squad. A match between the two has been signed for SNS. *** Kevin Nash d. Justin Hicks via the Jackknife Powerbomb. ("In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" begins to play and the crowd immediately jumps to it's feet and boos as the now former EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, The Prince, makes his way through the entrance.  He doesn't have the EWA Light Heavyweight Title in his posession.  It is in the hands of the man walking about two steps behind him.  The twosome make their way to the ring to a chorus of boos and a number of flying objects, all of which go unnoticed by The Prince.  The two reach the ring and climb inside, where The Prince is met by the ring attendant who presents him with a microphone) The Prince : I know that I am not scheduled to wrestle here tonight, but seeing how I have been preoccupied with other matters and I have found myself in places outside the EWA realm of jurisdiction, I haven't had the opportunity to address the matters at hand.  I returned from a little journey that really took no effort to find that the EWA had stripped all the title holders of their championships and was prepared to hold tournaments and other such events to determine new champions.  With some strong words from the self proclaimed Icon, Erik Draven, all former title holders are given byes in their respective tournaments, if they so wish to have them.  As former EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, I've given this much thought, and after a few seconds of deliberation, I came to a decision. (The Prince takes the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship from his assistant and holds it in one hand, the microphone in the other.  He looks straight at it) The Prince: When I look at this title, I see nothing of value.  A worthless piece of tin that represents a pathetic division in this federation.  When I came to the EWA a few months ago, I did so looking for competition.  I came, I saw, and I quickly conquered, as I expected.  But I did so against substandard competition.  I carried them from beginning to end.  I destroyed the former champion New Jack, who replaced arguably the best EWA Light Heavyweight before I stepped onto the scene, SmirtDogg.  SmirtDogg didn't disappear, we all know that it was a hoax.  He ran from this federation, fearing a match up with me.  He lacked the intestinal fortitude to come out here and face me like a man, so he ran from this federation with his tail between his legs someplace where he knew he would be catered to.  I've beaten the best and the worst Light Heavyweights the EWA has to offer, so I'm through with this division, and you can have this title of yours back, President Gates. (The Prince drops the title.  Stepping over it, he walks over to the edge of the ring closest to the entrance.  He puts one hand upon the top rope) The Prince : I know this is a shock for you President Gates, because I was the sole reason why that belt was still worth anything, but quite frankly I could find more competition in the NYSWF (The crowd >boos) than I could in that division.  So what I am telling you, plain and simple, is that you will not put me in that division.  It's not a decision you want to make, and if you do you will fell some hefty consequences.  What you will do, however, is place me in the bracket for any one of your other titles.  One that is open to all of your supposed talent.  I want the rest of the EWA to see exactly what happened to your Light Heavyweight Division.  I'm going in, and I'm going to tear it apart.  And a bye isn't necessary Mr President, because as I have proven so far, no matter what the talent level, either they will step out of the way, or they will fall to the way side. (The Prince steps away from the ropes and points at the belt that is still lying in the ring) The Prince : Here's your precious belt.  Come and get it anytime you want.  I want nothing to do with it. ("In the Halls of the Mountain King" begins to play and the two leave the ring to the chorus of boos from the fans.  One fan in particular hops the barricade along the aisle and makes a break towards The Prince.  Somewhat shocked, The Prince stares the man down as he barrels towards him.  Just moments before the man reaches him, The Prince steps out of the way.  The man, who had started a lunge, flies through the air and hits the ground with a tremendous thud, knocking himself out.  The Prince stands over the man and manages a slight chuckle before he turns around and walks out of the arena.  Security comes and carts the attempted assailant away) First Round EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ The BMF) No slight intended toward Ruger, but on paper, this match looked to be fairly one-sided. Much to the fans' delight, though, it was far from that. Both men came to the ring feeling the rush of being the potential next EWA World Heavyweight Champion, and they pulled out all the stops in their attempts to take that 'next big step' in the tournament. Ruger dominated Stevie with his strength and power, and then Stevie turned the tide with his superior speed and agility. Back and forth the match went, one man holding the advantage, then the other, and back and forth, until finally Ruger tried going off the top rope. As has been said many times in the past...when a brawler tries going off the top rope, bad things usually happen nine times out of ten. Unfortunately for Ruger, this was one of those nine times. The big man climbed up to the top, and attempted to hit a double-axehandle from the top...unfortunately, he ended up on the wrong side of a Stevie Kick, and well...that was all she wrote for Mr. Ice. 1, 2, 3, and Stevie Richards will meet Erik Draven in the semi-finals of the tournament this Saturday. *** Stevie Richards d. Ruger Ice via the Stevie Kick. First Round EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match Thorne Richards v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ The Triple Threat) It's official. The Second Era of the Franchise has begun...at least, that's what Bunda appeared to be trying to prove in his match against Thorne Richards. The Franchise came out like a house afire, lacing Thorne with punch after punch, kick after kick, high impact move after high impact move...unfortunately, Bunda's ego kicked in a bit too early. While he was playing to the fans, Richards chose the opportunity to sneak up behind Bunda and level him with a Gulotta shot with his cast, and roll him up for a 2.99999(etc) count. Richards, enraged, tried to bash Bunda's head in with the cast, earning him a warning from the ref, and basically just ticking Bunda off pretty badly. The Franchise fought Richards off, took back to his feet, and basically beat the living hell out of Thorne, finally polishing him off with the Showrunner for the win. Bunda advances, Thorne is off to another Narcotics Anonymous meeting, it appears. *** Jeff Bunda d. Thorne Richards via the Showrunner. Four Corners Match for the EWA United States Heavyweight Championship (Formerly the North American Championship) "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. Edge (w/ The Kindred) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) KG decided not to even bother showing up for this match (and the fans rejoiced a great deal, by the way), so it ended up being a Three-Way Dance for the US Title. With one man out of the way, there were a great deal of double-teams, with Edge first working with Vincent Jackson against Modest, only to turn on the behemoth the instant his back was turned, and then getting doubleteamed in turn by Jackson and Modest in return. The match continued for about ten minutes this way, with nobody gaining a clear-cut advantage until Vincent Jackson ended up dropping Edge HARD with a tombstone piledriver in the center of the ring. He went for the pin, but Modest quickly broke it up. As the two others brawled, Edge was dragged to the corner by Gangrel, giving him an opportunity to regain his wits while the others pummeled the living hell out of each other. Modest showed no fear whatsoever of being isolated with the big man, as he took the fight right at Jackson with a flurry of punches and kicks, staggering the big man in the corner, and repeated an earlier mistake of Satchiel's. He tried climbing the turnbuckle to clobber Jackson, but instead got dropped on the back of his head with a second-turnbuckle chokeslam. Jackson slowly dragged Modest up by his hair, took to the top turnbuckle, and delivered the Fallen Angel to eliminate him. By this time, Edge had managed to collect himself, and immediately launched into Jackson. The offensive flurry worked...for about five seconds. Jackson simply swatted Edge away like a fly, and quickly planted him in the center of the ring with a huge powerbomb. He started to make the cover, but looked up and saw Erik Draven standing on the runway, beckoning Jackson to come after him. Christina Knight tried to turn Jackson's attention back toward the match, but instead, Vincent left the ring, stalking after Draven...meanwhile, the ref continued to count Jackson out, resulting in Edge getting the countout victory, and winning the EWA United States Title. Once Jackson finally reached the top of the ramp, Draven flipped him off, spat in his face, and simply said: "Mind Games, kid...you're mine." As Edge celebrated with the rest of the Kindred in the ring, a hooded figure emerged from the crowd, hit the ring, and delivered a huge overhand right to the back of Edge's head, causing the new champion to fall in a heap. Gangrel and Christian leapt after the intruder, but he was too quick, and managed to barely leave the ring before the Kindred could get their hands on him. The newcomer tossed back his hood, and revealed that it was Terry Conklin, making his return to the EWA in grand fashion. *** Edge d. Vincent Jackson via countout. *** Edge is the new EWA United States Champion! First Round EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match Prodigy (w/ The BMF) v. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) In what was easily the best match on the card, the verbal warfare between these two superstars finally took physical form, and the fans were not disappointed in the least. Hopefully, they weren't expecting a technical showcase, because both men had just one thing on their minds: beating the living *BLEEP* out of each other. After about fifteen minutes of back-and-forth action, The Golden One was the first to seize a definite advantage , tossing Prodigy off the ropes and delivering a nasty spinebuster, and then locking down with a surfboard submission hold. But...as we all know, Prodigy and submit are rarely used in the same sentence. The lunatic managed to power out of the surfboard, and dropped TGO to the ground with a back-heel kick right, well...down there. TGO dropped like a rock, and Prodigy left the ring in search of his trusty fire extinguisher. The same fire extinguisher which had won him many a match in the past... Unfortunately, tonight was not to be one of those matches. As Prodigy re-entered the ring, TGO saw the extinguisher in his hands, and quickly pointed it out to the ref, who ordered Prodigy to relinquish it. The lunatic reluctantly did so, and as the ref handed the extinguisher to the timekeeper, TGO seized the opportunity and pulled a pair of brass knuckles out of his tights and levelled Prodigy with a huge right hook to the jaw. Prodigy fell like a tree, giving TGO an easy 1, 2, 3 for the victory. *** The Golden One d. Prodigy via a foreign object. First Round EWA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match "The Extreme SuperStar" Venom (w/ The BMF) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Triple Threat) (Just as Shawn Copeland makes his way down to the ring, Prodigy emerges from the entrance way) Prodigy: You know Copeland, you were a mighty big man last week when you hit me with a brick.  I came out and gave you the beatings your poppa used to give you with my bare hands, and you took it like you always did, lying down and with no response.  But you came out and being the big man you are, you couldn't cash the cheques that your mouth was writing.  You had to go to other means to take me down because your own hands just couldn't do the job. And lucky for you......no, change that to unluckily for you, you're in this lovely World Heavyweight Championship Tournament.  See, this round you were unlucky enough to get Venom as your opponent.  Now we might not always see eye to eye, but if there is one thing that we can agree upon, our unification factor if you will, is that people like you, your buddy Bunda, and all the others who come in here and think that their past credentials can get them to the front of the line ahead of those who worked hard to get to where they are, to see people like you get the beatings of your life time.  Now, IF you're lucky enough to get by Venom, which I really hope you aren't because I'd love to get a little payback on my buddy Venom, but if you are lucky enough, you're in for a whole heap of trouble.  Venom's gonna leave you bruised and battered, mark my word.  But beyond that you're gonna have Bunda in that ring with ya, and believe me that if he has to decide between you and him, you're outta there.  But compound your troubles with me, and you'll be lucky to escape with your career. A parting shot for you Pocket Change.  Watch your back (And with that Venom charges at Copeland, hitting him from behind and sending him sprawling to the mat) The match opened (obviously) with Venom pressing his early advantage, throwing basically his entire offensive playbook at the Money Maker in hopes of a quick win. In his haste, however, Venom left himself wide open for a counterattack from Copeland, and the former World Champion was quick to seize the opportunity. Venom whipped Copeland into the ropes, and attempted a standing lariat on the Money-Maker. Copeland, however, used his momentum to deliver a Floatover DDT (a la The Rock) on the Extreme One, and rolled him up for the cover...1, 2, and Venom just kicks out before the three. Venom was more enraged than hurt, as he quickly took back to his feet and delivered a brutal assault on Copeland's exhausted form, kicking, punching, clawing, gouging...all-in-all, Venom was wrecking Copeland in every sense of the word. The Extreme one delivered a brainbuster in the center of the ring, left, and dragged a table out from under the ring...the crowd cheered loudly at this, and even louder as Venom brought the table into the ring. The ref, oddly enough, let this go, and allowed Venom to drag Copeland's unconscious form on top of the table, and was still not moving at all as Venom climbed to the top rope... Venom paused for a moment to pose for the crowd, and that proved to be his undoing. Triple-H emerged from beneath the ring, reached up, and shook the ropes, causing Venom to crotch himself on the top rope. Copeland slowly shook the cobwebs off, dragged Venom back to the top turnbuckle, and superplexed him through the table! Both men were virtually unconscious in the center of the ring, but Copeland fell on top of Venom, forcing the ref to count the victory for the Money Maker, advancing him to the next round of the tournament. *** Shawn Copeland d. Venom via a superplex through a table.
