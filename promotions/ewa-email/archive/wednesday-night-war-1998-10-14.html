EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Pessimist (w/ Specimen-32) & "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Hate Club) v. Canuck is picked up by the huge monster and is powerslammed hard on the concrete floor. Draven works over Canuck on the outside, but fails to see the Pessimist, who jumps on the top rope and does and insane dive onto Draven, taking him out. Canuck regains his senses as the Pessimist is beating on Draven and Copeland is approaching the ringside area, with a chair. He rushes up and nails Pessimist not once, but twice with the chair. The Pessimist drops to his knees, in pain. Canuck was able to sneak around with the ring bell and waffles Copeland with the ringbell stand. Copeland falls on the ground, bleeding profously, as the stand shatters into many pieces. Canuck then grabs Copeland and perches him on the top rope. He follows him to the top rope and pauses for a second. He then grabs Copeland's head in between his legs(!) and executes a spinning powerbomb into the middle of the ring, which makes the crowd go insane. Canuck locks Copeland in the Canadian Crab and Copeland no choice but to tap out. Canuck and the Pessimist are the NEW WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!
