EWA Wednesday Night War

31 March 1999

Ewa Wnw (3-31-99) March 31, 1999 Live from the Rupp Arena (Lexington, Kentucky) ---- The lights go down unexpectedly making people wonder what's up. A golden beam shoots through the arena as the fans POP. 'Golden Showers' plays over the loudspeaker as The Golden One makes his first appearance of the night through the curtains. He is carrying the EWA World Heavyweight Title belt on his shoulder, letting the fans touch it as he strolls down to the ring. As always, he slaps hands with almost every fan with an extended limb, and finally gets to the ring where he waits a few seconds for the crowd to settle down. Then he begins... The Golden One: Now everyone in this arena knows who the true EWA World Heavyweight Champion is, ain't that right? *Crowd roars in TGO's approval* This is the University of Kentucky folks, where champions are born and bred. You've got your horses, you've got your Wildcats, and tonight, you've got your Golden One, live and in color. Now last weekend at Slam, what the world saw was Kane crying over some spilled milk. But what the big red bastard is forgetting, is the fact that he was the one who brought the milk when I went to dinner deep in the depths of hell. You brought your big fool Draven with you, and of course I had some backup waiting in the wings. I'm way too experienced to fall into as simple a trap as that, you know. So when Terry Conklin, my best friend left here, came down for the save, and things got out of hand, don't think for one second you can cry and whine and say you never lost. Plain and simple Kane, your shoulders were down for the three count, not mine. In my book, I'm the World Champion, and you, my friend, are a has-been. Now you come out here after Next Generation, the greatest tag team in the EWA today, finishes wiping out Simply Inevitable, and claim to be calling the shots. So much so, Kane, one might wonder exactly who you know in the back that's letting you get away with all of this crap as of late. But I'm above that big guy. You see, when you went and begged for an Inferno Match, I went and did you one better. You see Kane, you have your little specialty matches, like an Inferno Match or your Casket Match, and that's the only way you've got a chance to beat me. You want to leave the "Shining Star" burnt to a crisp? Try your best fool, but you're gonna have to do it alone. You heard me right. I'll give you another shot at the World Title, and I'll be so generous as to give you the Inferno Match. But it's not just any old Inferno Match, because to tell you the truth Kane, that's been played out way too much, similar to yourself. Overdone and underproductive, a great combination. We're gonna have ourselves a Golden Inferno Match. Now before you sh*t your pants like a two-year old, allow me to explain. The object of the game is still to send your opponent running in a ball of fire. But like I said a little earlier, nobody is gonna help you. You see Kane, I've already beat you once, and the only reason Terry got invovled was because you had an itch and Draven tried to give it a scratch. This time around, we're settling the score. So that means no Draven, no Conklin, no Inferna, no Carrie. Simply? Nobody interferes. If they even appear, they can kiss their stint in the EWA good-bye. But that's not all. See I know you better than that Kane. I know you'd find some poor sap like Draven or even your precious b*tch Inferna to sacrifice. So what we're gonna do is we're gonna find ourselves a special enforcer to sit and enjoy the match at ringside. Somebody who doesn't give a damn either way. Somebody capable of taking any interference right out of play. Somebody like Jackie Chan or something. But I'm not going to decide this, and neither are you. The bottom line is the EWA is going to see the rematch of the year, Kane versus The Golden One. Different Story Same Result Kane, Clash of the Champions 12, you can damn well be sure, it's gonna be one hell of a GOLDEN night. Joe Lemon vs Dope Savant Don't know if Savant ate some bad meat prior to the main event or was plastered on drugs, but whatever the reason he looked horrid in this match and Joe Lemon looked well he looked like Joe Lemon but tonight it was enough to get the win with the Lemon Drop and Lemon got the win. Joe Lemon d. Dope Savant w/ The Lemon Drop Jonny Cassanova vs "Simply Perfect" Shawn Arrows This match started out with Arrows having the initial advantage. He landed several manaeuvers to begin this match some of which included a tiger bomb, a senton splash, a heart punch and a flying elbow from the top rope. However, there was a lot of fight let in Cassanova evident by several kickouts with authority. After a reversed piledriver attempt by Arrows, Cassanova came roaring back with rights and lefts followed by a dropkick and a standing side kick. He followed that with a bulldog but was also unable to get a three count, although he came damn close before Arrows somehow managed to get a foot on the ropes stopping the count. The match went back and forth until the 13:54 mark when Arrows landed a headbutt from the top rope then the 1 minute bell sounded as Arrows went for a cradle but it was reversed by Cassanova. Cassanova didn't go for the three count however, he instead went for a superkick but he telegraphed it and Cassanova sidestepped the manuver and landed a stiff uppercut to the jaw before throwing his man into the corner and landing a stinger splash, covering his man and gaining the win, just seconds before the match would have been ruled a draw Shawn Arrows d. Jonny Cassanova w/ A Stinger Splash Paksta vs"Superstar" Stevie Brass Paksta destroyed the Superstar but he seemed to have him beaten and continued to pummel him for several minutes after the outcome seemed to be decided. He even press-slammed Brass onto a Table. Before throwing him back into the ring and landing the Pak-Plex and getting the easy win Paksta d. Stevie Brass w/ The Pak-Plex James Mastin v Juventud Guerrera James Mastin was absolutely destroyed in this match. " Sexual White Hot Chocolate " Phillip Donovan [with Thorne Richards] Another Fine Match. Both men put on quite a show for the fans in this match as well. The match see-sawed for several minutes until the 13:23 minute mark was reached. Fury hit a snap suplex and followed it up with a legdrop to the throat, however he missed the second legdrop attempt and Phillip Donovan landed a atomic drop, a short arm clothesline, an armbar and a gutwrench suplex and seemed to have everything going for him at that point but he took that opportunity to start spitting game at Fury's Manager, Geri Halliwell although Thorne Richards was screaming for him to finish off his opponent, he continued to jaw at his opponent. This enabled Fury to get up and tap SWHC on the shoulder and when Donovan turned around he was the victim of a Stunner. Fury went up to the top rope going for the Overdose, but Donovan moved out of the way at the last split second and then he executed the Vanilla Shake which looked alot like Thorne's old finisher, The Overdose, needless to say, this one was over Phillip Donovan d. Aramund Fury w/ The Vanilla Shake "Judgement Day v.Cain and Fire One of the best matches of the night. Both of these teams proved that they wanted this match. Judgement Day had been on the receiving end of a few losses as of late, but tonight they came out on the attack and were relentless for several minutes punishing both Cain and Fire with press-slams, diamond cutters, swinging neckbreakers and double-team tactics, between tags and although they had several near falls, they were unable to secure the three count. Slowly but surely, Cain and Fire Battled back with rights and lefts and after Ministry had a suplex blocked, Fire landed one of his own and followed up with a reverse atomic drop.. followed immediatly with a clothesline to the back of Ministry's head that dropped him like a bad habit. Fire took that opportunity to make the much needed tag to Cain and Cain wasted little time in following up on the advantage. He immediatly laid the boots to Ministry's head before picking Ministry up and whipping him to the ropes and executing a beautiful powerslam followed by a spinebuster and then a piledriver. Cain then took the opportunity to quickly ascend to the top rope Big Splash coming up? No, Ministry moved out of the way at the last possible moment and Ministry landed a belly to belly suplex before making a much needed tag to Steel and Steel responded with a press slam a powerbomb and a Rayzor's Edge. The match swung back and forth for several more minutes before it was all four men inside and outside the ring. Steel whips Cain into the ropes and lands a Frankenstiner and follows with a german suplex and gets the tough win. Down the ramp come Aramund Fury and Lunatikk Crippler each carrying a bottle of Bacardi 151. Cain and Black Steel start Brawling outside the ring leaving Fire and Ministry trying to fight off the Twisted Souls. Aramund Smashes the bottle over Fire's head as revenge for last weeks loss, and Lunatikk Crippler smashes his over the head of Ministry for leaving the AWO. Crippler and Fury get back in the ring. They toss Ministry and Fire over the top rope and while Cain and Black Steel tend to their respective partners Crippler Grabs the mic. Crippler: I don't think that you understood what was going to happen Ministry. You and your Brother signed up with the Apocalypse FOR LIFE and thats exactly what we're going to take. Myself and Aramund talked about it. And we decided that you boys need to be shown the error of your way. We are the Apocalypse. That means the end of all there is. Not just a stepping stone for you boys to launch your careers. When you left the Apocalypse I'll give you credit for the guts it took to do that. BUT YOU MADE THE WRONG CHOICE! You don't want to leave something that made you here in the EWA. And you don't want to get in our way after you've left. You may still have a little respect from me, but when you pull a stunt like this. You get retribution. BE WARNED. Fury: And this serves as a warning for you to Fire. The Team of Craven Blood and E-Nuff d. New Jack and DMX European Championship Match Max Pins[EWA European Champion with The Flock ]v. Lee Tenn As agreed to, in the interview leading up to this rematch.. This match was fought under " Red Dragon Fighting Rules" The ring had a steel cell lowered over it. This cell was tight to the sides of the ring and goes all the way down to the floor, this was to insure that no-one can get under the ring once the match started. Weapons such as chairs and so on can't get into the ring and the underneath of the ring was checked before the contest to make sure no-one is under there. Both men were also checked before the match to make sure they have nothing concealed in their attire to aid themselves in the match. Then finally, The ring ropes were removed and only the ref and the two wrestlers were left in the ring to fight it out to a pinfall or submission.
