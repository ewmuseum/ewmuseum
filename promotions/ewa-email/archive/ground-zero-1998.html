EWA Ground Zero

14 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 14, 1998 Live, from The Superdome in New Orleans, Louisiana Paul Burnett v. El Locco Jobbo The opening match at Ground Zero was a thriller, as El Locco Jobbo dazzled the crowd with terrific combinations of high-flying moves and suplexes. Burnett, making his EWA debut, never really could seem to get on his game, as El Locco Jobbo controlled the match, and eventually secured the victory. El Locco Jobbo d. Paul Burnett via The Jobbo Drop. (5:13) "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ Chris Gardner) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) This match swayed back and forth between the two, as neither could secure a safe advantage on the other. Eventually, the action spilled over onto the outside, where it became extremely ugly. The two began hitting each other with a variety of foreign objects, and the offical had no choice but to rule the match a draw. Shortly after, word reached the broadcasting booth that a rematch had been signed between the two for Thursday Night Terror. Joshua Gardner and Deathstar battled to a double-disqualifiation. (9:24) FlashBack v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) This was a terrific tag team match, in which the amazing improvement of Death Sentance showed even more. Death Sentance dominated most of the matchup, using their size advantage to overwhelm Flash Funk and The Disco Inferno. But suddenly, the cameras focused on the crowd, where Alex Wright ran to the ring, jumped over the guardrail, and hit Pain with a pair of brass knuckles. Funk rolled Pain up from behind, and got the victory. FlashBack d. Death Sentance via Flash Funk's pin on Pain after a rollup. (11:56) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire & Cleopatra) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors & Lil' Kim/Foxy Brown) Marc Anthony took the early advantage of this match, seemingly fueled by the drive to win his first major title. But Anthony's day was not today, as SmirtDogg's experience eventually came into play, and the Light Heavyweight Champ walked out with the belt in tow. SmirtDogg d. Marc Anthony via The Air Smirty. (8:27) SmirtDogg retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) As soon as Octavius hit the ring, the lights went out, and Kane's music played. A tremendous pyrotechnics display followed, as Kane walked to the ring with Paul Bearer, who looked extremely determined. Kane walked in the ring, where Octavius hit him with three clotheslines, to no avail. After the third, Kane simply grabbed the man by the throat, choke-slammed him, and followed with the Tombstone Piledriver for the win. Kane d. Octavius via the Tombstone Piledriver. (1:01) Chris Gardner (w/ Joshua Gardner) v. The Ghetto Boy (w/ Chris Furman & Dark Force Rising) This match never actually got underway, as Gardner attacked The Ghetto Boy from behind as he walked to the ring. The two then engaged in a brawl, and the offical ruled the bout a no-contest. Grudge Match Faarooq (w/ The Nation Of Domination) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia This match was disappointing, to say the least, as Faarooq did not even show up. Comissioner Slaughter followed up on his word, and as a result, fired the Nation Of Domination. Rocky Maivia d. Faarooq via forfeit. Winner will recieve EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Harley feuled the fire between these two before this match even began, as he brought Natasha (the girl from the bar) with him to the ring. This was an exciting match at the start, but Augustus seemed to wear down rather quickly, and began favoring his knee. Harley saw this, and began focusing on the knee, rather than using his usual display of constant high-flying matches. Eventually, Harley applied a figure four to Augustus, and Augustus had no choice but to submit. It was later discovered that Augustus suffered a knee injury, and will be sidelined 2-3 weeks. Harley Hart d. Augustus via submission with a Figure Four Leglock. (9:33) SmirtDogg will defend the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship against Harley Hart at TNT. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit/Steve "Mongo" McMichael (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) Mongo and Benoit seemed unprepared for their debut match in the EWA, and Gunn/James made them pay for it, finishing them off with Billy Gunn's legdrop from the top rope. The New Age Outlaws d. The Horsemen via "Badd Ass" Billy Gunn's legdrop from the top rope onto Steve McMichael. (4:14) Swap Meet Louie (W/ Chris Furman & Dark Force Rising) v. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This was a terrific match, as the two men held nothing back. An intense rivalry had been developed between the two, and it showed, as the men eventually began using various foreign objects on each other. The match wasn't thrown out until the other members of DFR and The Ghetto Warriors became involved, and the match was then ruled a double-disqualification. A rematch has been signed between the two for WrestleFest '98. Ahmed Johnson and Swap Meet Louie battled to a double-disqualification. (7:16) KG (w/ Sunny & The Stud Stable) v. "Iceman" Scott Snyder (w/ Chris Furman & Dark Force Rising) KG looked extremely aggresive tonight, as he took it to Snyder. Snyder was a little banged up from the brawl in the previous match, and KG capitalized on this, ending the match with the Six-Shooter. KG d. Scott Snyder via submission with The Six Shooter. (5:59) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This was one of the all-time greatest tag team matches in history, as The Outsiders and Harlem Heat went nose-to-nose. Neither team backed down from the challenge, and the result was, of course, a contraversial ending. Near the very end of the match, Kevin Nash hit the JackKnife on Booker T, and covered him for the three count. However, the bell rang when the referee counted two. President Gates and Comissioner Slaughter came to ringside and had a "conference" with the referee, who then announced the following. Due to the time limit expiring before Kevin Nash pinned Booker, and also due to the face that Nash did pin Booker T, the titles were being declared vacant, and these two teams would fight for them once again at TNT. Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart Bret showed up before the match, to no music, and announced that due to some disagreements, he was leaving the EWA. He shook Nero's hand, and told him that he was the winner. Nero d. Bret Hart via forfeit. EWA World Television Championship Ladder Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Degeneration X) This was a classic match, and it started out as a brawl from the opening bell. Helmsley got the better of that, as Chyna nailed Austin with a can of spraypaint. Helmsley then went to retrieve the ladder, while Chyna pounded on Helmsley. Once Helmsley had brought the ladder into the ring, Chyna retreated, and Austin fought back, hitting Helmsley in the eyes with some sort of white powder. Helmsley quickly regained the advantage by focusing on the head and neck area of Austin, and unbelievably Pedigreed Austin onto the ladder. Helmsley then sat the ladder up and climbed to the top, having a finger on the belt, before Austin knocked the ladder down, sending Triple-H flying. Austin then went to work on Helmsley, and almost hit the Stone Cold Stunner, but Helmsley slipped out. Helmsley regrouped on the outside, as Chyna again swatted Austin with the can of spraypaint. Helmsley then appeared to be out to hurt Austin, as he executed a piledriver onto the ladder. Helmsley again went to climbing the ladder, but Austin climbed up the other side, and a fist-fight ensued on top of the ladder. Eventually, the ladder fell, along with both men. Austin then whipped Helmsley into the ropes, hit a kick to the stomach, and nailed the Stone Cold Stunner. Austin set the ladder up, and began to climb to the top, when Chyna grabbed the boot of Austin, pulling him off the ladder. Still, Austin and Helmsley both got up at the same time, and Austin again hit the Stunner, and threw Helmsley over the top rope onto Chyna. Austin then climbed the ladder, and pulled the Television Title belt down. Steve Austin d. Hunter Hearst Helmsley by hitting the Stone Cold Stunner, and then climbing the ladder and gaining posession of the belt. (42:37) Steve Austin is the new EWA World Television Champion! Steel Cage Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Michaels looked awful tonight, as Shawn Hart was out for revenge, and didn't waste any time getting it. Hart pounded Michaels all over the cage, and then eventually hit his half of the HartStopper, and exited through the front door. Shawn Hart d. Shawn Michaels via The HartStopper. (12:12) EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match Erik Draven (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Stud Stable) This was an all-out war, as Draven and Cummings took each other to the limit. Let's pick up the play-by-play 21 minutes into the match. Mike Malone: Both men are barely standing now, as Cummings kicks Draven in the stomach, and he goes down instantly! Larry Zybysko: That just shows how much these two men have brutalized each other! Mike Malone: Indeed it does, as Cummings is going to the top rope for the WipeOut! But Dravenm moved, and got right back up! Larry Zybysko: He was playing possum! Mike Malone: Draven has Cummings now, and he hits the DX Driver! 1.....2.....3! Larry Zybysko: What a travesty! Erik Draven d. Scott Cummings via the DX Driver. (22:31) Erik Draven is the new EWA World Heavyweight Champion! This page hosted by Get your own Free Home Page
