EWA Monday Night Madness

27 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 27, 1998 Live, from the AlamoDome in San Antonio, Texas. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match D-Lo Brown (w/ The Nation Of Domination) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This was a fairly competitive match, with D-Lo showing the skills that he had failed to show in his previous EWA matches. However, the experience of SmirtDogg eventually overwhelmed him, as D-Lo fell to the Light Heavyweight Champion. Smirt was again heavily booed in this match, although Foxy Brown and Lil' Kim recieved a thunderous ovation. SmirtDogg d. D-Lo Brown via the Air Smirty. (11:14) SmirtDogg retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. FlashBack v. Death Sentance (w/ Precious) This was a terrific tag team contest, featuring two of the up and coming teams in the EWA. Death Sentance mounted an early offensive attack, using their size advantage to overpower the team of Disco Inferno and Flash Funk. However, Disco seemingly out of nowhere hit his Chartbuster, and FlashBack escaped with a victory. FlashBack d. Death Sentance via Disco Inferno's pin on Pain after the Chartbuster. (10:39) Final EWA Televison Championship Tournament Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) In one of the most exciting and contraversial wrestling matches in recent history, Triple-H and Steve Austin went nose-to-nose for this entire contest. At one point, Helmsley whipped Austin to the ropes, and Austin inadvertenly hit referee Mark Curtis, knocking him out cold. Austin then hit the Stone Cold Stunner on Helmsley, and went for the cover. However, no referee was available to make the three count. Referee Earl Hebner then ran out, and made the count, but Helmsley kicked out. Austin then whipped Helmsley into the ropes, and ducked to backbodydrop him, but Helmsley latched on the Pedigree. Helmsley then made the cover, but at this point, Hebner was trying to revive Curtis. Helmsley got up, pulled Hebner up, and gave him the Pedigree. Referee Randy Anderson then ran out, saying that the match would be rendered a double-disqualification. A rematch was immediately signed for TNT. Steve Austin and Triple-H battled to a double disqualification. (31:10) Neither man won the Television Title, and a rematch for the belt has been signed for TNT. The Legion Of Doom v. Matthew Hart/Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) The Road Warriors didn't even show up for the match, which led to President Gates firing them from the EWA. Harley Hart/Matthew Hart d. The LOD via forfeit. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Jacquelyn) This was an exciting tag team match, as the two teams battled back and forth throughout the match. Booker T was going to the top rope for the Harlem Hangover, when The Pessimist knocked him off. The two stables then had an all-out brawl in the ring. Harlem Heat d. Prophecy via disqualification after interference from The Pessimist. Harlem Heat retains the EWA World Tag Team Championship. Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Kama Mustafa (w/ The Nation Of Domination) This was another good match, until the action spilled onto the outside. Both men lost track of the count, and were counted out. Octavius and Kama Mustafa battled to a double countout. (7:41) The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Arn Anderson (w/ The IV Horsemen) This was a total squash, as GC destroyed Anderson. GC got a huge pop from the crowd, and it didn't take long for him to get the win. The Golden Child d. Arn Anderson via the Royal Destroyer. (1:57) Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Shawn Michaels came to the ring on crutches, proclaiming that in a friendly game of "Twister" with Chyna, he strained a knee ligament and a groin muscle. Comissioner Slaughter then came out, announcing that if HBK couldn't go, "Road Dawg" Jesse James would take his place. DX didn't like this at all, and for good reason, as Shawn Hart demolished The Road Dawg. Shawn Hart d. Jesse James via the Lightning Strike. (0:41) The Gunslinger v. Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) In a strange twist, neither man showed up. Comissioner Slaughter announced that The Gunslinger had been fired. Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. KG (w/ The Stud Stable) This was a very good match, with Augustus pulling out the narrow victory. KG was attempting a vertical suplex, and Augustus reversed it into a Fisherman's Suplex for the victory. Augustus d. KG via a Fisherman's Suplex. (10:52) Nero (w/ The Empire) v. The Widow Maker The Widow Maker was once again embarassed, as he was squashed by Nero. Nero simply overwhelmed him. Nero d. The Widow Maker via a powerbomb. (1:59) Vinnie Vegas v. Ken Shamrock Vinnie Vegas obtained the forfeit win, as it was announced that Ken Shamrock had left the EWA. Return Match for the EWA Intercontinental Championship The Pessimist v. Scott Hall (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) In an exciting match, The Pessimist fell short of his goal to regain his Intercontinental Championship. The Pessimist was attempting a moonsault, but Hall simply got up, and turned it into his patented Outsider Edge. Scott Hall d. The Pessimist via the Outsider Edge. (12:29) Scott Hall retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship. DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Steel Cage Grudge Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart Triple-H absolutely dominated Hart, as he went after Bret's knee. Bret couldn't even stand, as the damage Triple-H did to his knee was overwhleming. It was discovered that Hart tore some tendons in his knee, and will be out of action until at least Ground Zero. Triple-H d. Bret Hart by hitting The Pedigree, and exiting the cage via the front door. (9:11) Steel Cage Grudge Match for the EWA United States Heavyweight Championship "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) v. Prodigy (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion) This was a very well-fought match, but in the end, DX once again played a role in the outcome, as Kevin Nash, who did not come to the ring with DX, and had not been seen all night, emerged from under the ring. The two men double-teamed Prodigy, and Draven eventually hit the DX Driver, while Nash held the door open for him. Erik Draven d. Prodigy by hitting the DX Driver, and exiting the cage via the front door. Erik Draven is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!
