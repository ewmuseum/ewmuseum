EWA Clash Of The Champions I

2 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First Round Light Heavyweight Tournament Match SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim & Foxy Brown) v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) The EWA's first match quite possibly was the best match of the night, as SmirtDogg and Benoit matched each other move for move. Benoit took the early advantage with some submission holds and various suplexes, until SmirtDogg caught him with a thumb to the eye. Smirt then went to work, showing his terrific athletic ability and high-risk moves. Eventually, he applied his patented submission hold, The Thugbreaker, but Benoit refused to tap out. Benoit began to regain his strength, and took control of the match, eventually applying The Crippler Crossface, but SmirtDogg refused to tap out as well. The match went on a little longer, with Smirt taking control, until Benoit executed a small package. The offical counted to 2, but SmirtDogg then reversed the small package for the three-count. SmirtDogg d. Chris Benoit via a reversed small package. (16:34) First Round Light Heavyweight Tournament Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. KG In a strange twist, both men came to the ring alone, as Michaels chose to leave his degenerate friends in the locker room. This was another exciting match, as The Heartbreak Kid of old seemed to return to action, and the youngster KG was at the top of his game. Michaels, being the crafty veteran, eventually took control with some high-impact manuevers. Michaels hit an elbow-drop from the top rope, and then went to the corner and began to call for his Sweet Chin Music. But suddenly, Shawn Hart was at ringside, holding down the boot of HBK. Michaels turned around and began to argue with Hart, and was promptly rolled up from behind by KG for the win. KG quickly left through the crowd with Hart before Degeneration X could make it to the ring. Could KG be in some way associated with the Stud Stable? KG d. Shawn Michaels via a rollup. (10:01) First Round Light Heavyweight Tournament Match "Handsome" Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Pessimist This match was a total squash. The young Harley Hart looked outstanding in this match, performing death-defying aerial manuevers, and eventually finishing The Pessimist off with the Stud Screw, a corkscrew moonsault from the top rope. Harley Hart d. The Pessimist via the Stud Screw. (3:41) First Round World Tag Team Tournament Match Harlem Heat (w/ Jacquelyn) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Harlem Heat looked outstanding in this contest, as they jumped on Prophecy before the match even began. Stevie Ray didn't even get tagged in, as Booker T did a number on Travis Scott, finishing him off with the Harlem Sidekick. Harlem Heat d. Prophecy via Booker T's pin on Travis Scott after the Harlem Sidekick. (2:32) First Round World Tag Team Tournament Match The Legion Of Doom v. The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) This was a very competitive matchup, as The Legion Of Doom and The New Age Outlaws were both very fired up. The LOD took control at the beginning to the delight of the crowd, as their power moves and wrestling experience overwhelmed "Bad Ass" and "Road Dawg". But then Erik Draven and Chyna made their way to ringside, and the match turned completely around. Gunn was tagged in, and he (with some help from the outside) went to work on Hawk. At one point, the referee saw the cheating of Degeneration, and ordered Chyna and Draven back to ringside. Gunn and James began to argue with the officals, which gave the Road Warriors their window of opportunity. Animal came from behind of Gunn, and set him up on his shoulders, while Hawk hit the clothesline from the top. As Animal went for the pin, Chyna and Draven came into the ring, and began pounding on the LOD. Officals tried to get Degeneration X to return to the locker room, but they continued to beat on the LOD for about five minutes. The Legion Of Doom d. The New Age Outlaws via disqualification. (9:56) First Round World Tag Team Tournament Match Notorious Thugs (w/ Puff Daddy) v. The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) The Notorious Thugs were no match for Scott Hall and Kevin Nash, as the Outsiders rolled to victory during this one. The announcers were more focused on backstage, as Shawn Michaels, Chyna, and Erik Draven seemed to be arguing with the New Age Outlaws. The Outsiders d. The Notorious Thugs via Kevin Nash's pin on Bone after the JackKnife. (2:21) First Round World Tag Team Tournament Match Arn Anderson/Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) Another outstanding match involving The Horsemen, as the cagy veterans matched up against the youth of The Hart Brothers. Flair and Anderson took the early advantage, isolating Shawn Hart in their corner of the ring. Hart made a desperate attempt when he hit Flair "below the belt", and managed to get over and tag Matt Hart in. At this point, The Harts took the advantage, but it would not last, as Shawn Michaels returned to pay Hart back for costing him his match earlier on. Michaels grabbed a steel chair and nailed both Harts with it, and then ran through the crowd. The Hart Brothers d. Arn Anderson/Ric Flair via disqualification. (11:13) First Round World Heavyweight Title Tournament Match The WidowMaker v. Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) Erik Draven looked very impressive in this bout, as the WidowMaker seemed unfocused. Draven made short work of him, using various power moves, and even using a former finishing move of his, The Fallen Angel. After the Fallen Angel, Draven picked the big man up, and executed the DX Driver for the three-count. Erik Draven d. The WidowMaker via the DX Driver. (4:50) First Round World Heavyweight Title Tournament Match The Gunslinger v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) This was an outstanding match, as Cummings and The Gunslinger went into this match fully prepared. This was a fairly-even match, until once again, Degeneration X stuck its nose in other people's business. The Gunslinger seemed ready to go for the Six Shooter, when Hunter Hearst Helmsley popped out with a mike, making deragatory remarks about the late wife of The Gunslinger. While The Gunslinger had his attention focused on Triple-H, Cummings rolled him up from behind for the pin. Scott Cummings d. The Gunslinger via a rollup from behind. (10:09) First Round World Heavyweight Title Tournament Match Raven v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly & The IV Horsemen) In a strange turn of events, Raven didn't even show for his match. Saturn was not at ringside either, so DDP got the forfeit victory. "Diamond" Dallas Page d. Raven via forfeit. First Round World Heavyweight Title Tournament Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (w/ The IV Horsemen) This was a great main event, as Ric Flair, wrestling for the second time in the night, showed why he is one of the greatest wrestlers in history. Triple-H took the early advantage, with some interference from Chyna. Flair eventually got his "second wind", and began to pummel Triple-H, finishing him off with The Figure Four before Degeneration X could interfere. Ric Flair d. Hunter Hearst Helmsley via submission with the Figure Four. (12:16)
