EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

29 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The Miami Arena Miami, Florida Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Intoxication-X Dos Equis didn't put up much of a fight, as I-X, fighting in their first tag team match under their new name, wiped the mat with the boys from The Circus. The crowd still didn't seem to know what to think of Intoxication-X. Intoxication-X d. Dos Equis with Aaron Martin's pin on Pacifico Pete after the Shoulderbreaker. Handicap Match Darkness (w/ Razor) v. Morbid Angel *As Morbid Angel reaches ringside he grabs the mic and stares at DAP and Lucien. Morbid Angel: I told you both that I would come to the ring by myself, but like I said....I always have backup. *Morbid Angel then turns and points to the curtain as the lights lower and a yellow light starts to glow from the entrance. Soon after smoke starts rolling out from under the entrance ramp as a shadowed figure slowly emerges from the back and into the yellow, smoke filled light. The figure stands there for a moment, DAP and Lucien both look at each other with mixed emotions, suddenly the figure throws it's hands into the air as two loud gunshots go off right as "I Shot The Sherrif" by Warren G begins to play. The crowd jumps to it's feet as they all know the man who is now emerging from the mist.....it's GUNSLINGER!!! As Gunslinger slowly starts to stroll down to ringside with the fans going nuts, DAP and Lucien both assume attack positions. Once at ringside, Gunslinger and Morbid Angel converse and then Morbid Angel crawls into the ring and the match begins. From here, we saw a very good match. In the early minutes, Darkness used the two-on-one gameplan to their advantage, making quick tags and isolating Morbid Angel. But the fans got behind Morbid, and soon, he began to run away with the match, finally pinning DAP with a powerbomb. *As the match ends Gunslinger rolls into the ring and catches Lucien from behind and hits him with the "Bullseye", he then turns as DAP comes off the top rope and nails him with a fist to the mid-section, after DAP hits the mat he picks him up and hits the Quickdraw on him also. Gunslinger then rolls out of the ring and head to the back as the crowd is going insane.* Morbid Angel d. Darkness by pinning Dark Angelic Priest after a powerbomb. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. I.R.K. Neither man showed for the match; it has been rescheduled for SNS. (The EWA JumboTron crackles and is filled with static. The PA system fills the arena with white noise. Slowly, the noise dies down and an image fades into the static. It's Prodigy and he is seemingly playing with some wires. When he's content, and the picture is of decent quality, Prodigy backs off) This whole idea of seeking revenge on me has reached a point of lunacy. Gates, you thought you could control me, shape me and mold me into the man that you wanted to lead this federation intot he next decade, millenium, and eon. But like those before you, you found out that there is only one man that Prodigy conforms to, and that's Prodigy. Instead of accepting it, you've decided to take it upon yourself to break me. You've stripped me of everything that I have worked for. You've twisted the minds of the fans and the wrestlers to despise me. You've stacked the odds against me. Hell, at King of the Ring, you cost me a match and brought injury down upon me. What's a man supposed to do? What a man is supposed to do......that's a question that everyone knows I can't answer. What a madman can do........that I can answer. You have The Prodigy Hate Club at your beck and call, and when it's 4 or 5 on 1, the odds aren't in my favour. So what do I do in situations like this? Desparate times call for desparate measures. At King of the Ring, there were brave souls who came out and bucked the authority and saved my ass. Men who know what it takes to tell the big shots where to go and how many fingers to shove up their ass. The Circus! came to my aid, and that's something that I can only repay in one way....... Van Smack and Stevie, you and I have had our differences in the past, but situations like this may make us the unlikeliest of bedfellows. I have one question for you............. Is The Circus!...........The nWc..........The Brothers.............is it ready to bring the insanity into the noise? We can be the best of teammates, or we can be the worst of enemies. Either way I'm content, but the question is........what are you most content with? (Prodigy stands up and limps over to where he started, pulls a cable, and the JumboTron goes back to static) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. Thorne Richards (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ NQ2) Joe Satre rose to heights he had never been before in the EWA, and as a result, the fans were treated to an outstanding contest. About 17 minutes into the match, the referee was knocked unconscious, and just as he was beginning to stir, on the outside Satre nailed Thorne with a chair. Satre then climbed back into the ring as Thorne was counted out. Afterward, Shawn Copeland sprinted to the ring, and as Satre was leaving, nailed him with a brick. President Gates has since issued a contract for a return match between Satre and Richards next Saturday at Clash Of The Champions V. "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre d. Thorne Richards by countout. Thorne Richards remains EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) v. Harlem Heat (w/ The Dream Team) Unfortunately, the plane flight of AA was delayed due to bad weather, and Harlem Heat got the forfeit win. Terry Conklin v. Mayne Mann An unbelievable match between two of the brightest upcoming stars in the EWA. Mann's leg looks to be healing at a rapid pace, but he was still staggering a little bit in this match. The match went on for over fourteen minutes, when Mayne Mann finally went for the Boston Bomb. However, Conklin knew his position very well, and executed a brilliant small package for the narrow three count. Terry Conklin d. Mayne Mann with a small package. (*After the match Mayne limps to the side of the ring where he asks the ringside attendant for the microphone. He gives it to him. Mayne limps back to the center of the ring where Terry Conklin is.*) Mayne Mann: Terry, we just had a hell of a match. I heard you were interested in finding some "back-up" to help you out because of all the stables running around in the EWA. Is that true? (*Conklin nods his head yes.*) Well how about you and I and some friends of mine join forces together and go after some of these stables? (*Conklin smiles and nods his head yes again.*) Great, I hear you are looking to get Shawn Copeland in the ring, something about you two fought in some other organization. Well, I want a piece of him too, so how about we make him #1 on our hitlist? (*Again Conklin nods as the crowd goes wild at the announcement that someone is finally going to shut up Copeland*) Well, all you have to do is shake my hand and we have a deal. (*Mann holds his hand out while the crowd cheers. Conklin pauses for a second as the anticipation builds before shaking Maynes hand. The crowd goes nuts as the two hold their fists in the air. Could this be the beginning of the new stable Conklin hinted about in the prelude of his last interview???*) (*As the two walk around the ring, fireworks explode over the entrance way to the aisle and the fans are louder than ever. It's truly a magical moment in the EWA. Mayne and Terry go to give each other a hug but suddenly Mayne kicks him in the midsection and puts Terry's head between his legs. Mayne gives an evil grin right before executing the Boston Bomb. The crowd is shocked at what they just saw. Mayne starts yelling at Conklin, who is out cold, but Mayne can't help but laugh. He picks up the microphone again.*) Mayne: Oh geez. That was just too easy. It was classic. Two guys have a good match, form a friendship, and then one makes a complete fool out of the other. Oh this was great. Conklin I just suckered the hell out of you. See ya around loser. (*With that Mayne leaves the ring while the crowd is still shocked. EWA officials and security members rush to the ring to help Conklin who is still out cold.*) Divader v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (1998 EWA King Of The Ring, w/ Rebecca Claridge) Divader was, in one word, CRUSHED. Justin Hicks seemed hellbent on destroying Divader, and did just that, defeating him in under ninety seconds. Justin Hicks d. Divader by submission with the Saturday Night Special. EWA World Television Championship Match Crazy Canuck (w/ The PHC) v. Ruger Ice (EWA World Television Champion) A fantastic contest saw both men give 110%, much to the delight of the capacity crowd. Strangely enough, Mayne Mann walked to the ring about midway through the match, and watched the match. As the two battled, Ruger whipped Canuck into the ropes. Canuck attempted a clothesline on the return, but Ruger ducked, and Canuck hit the referee instead. And then, Mayne Mann sprung into action. He ran into the ring, kicked Ruger in the stomach, and executed the Boston Bomb. He then rolled Ruger on top of Canuck and shook the referee. He then left ringside, as the official began to stir, and count 1, 2, and Canuck kicked out! The match went on for only a couple more minutes, as Ruger, clearly puzzled by the happenings, lost his advantage, and Canuck was able to hit the Lunatic Bomb, and pull off the biggest win of his career. Crazy Canuck d. Ruger Ice with the Lunatic Bomb. Crazy Canuck is the new EWA World Television Champion!! Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) v. Vic Deakins (w/ Shae Marks) Another double no-show. Return Match for the EWA World Tag Team Championship Vertigo v. The Showstoppers (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Elizabeth/Sunny & The Dream Team) This match was almost identical to the first contest between the two...once Vertigo started to take the advantage, Stevie Ray again hit Jimmy Pow with the Flapjack. The Showstoppers then debuted their new finisher: KG powerbombed Jimmy Pow, while Shawn Michaels executed a moonsault onto him for the pin. The Showstoppers d. Vertigo with Shawn Michaels' pin on Jimmy Pow after the High Impact. The Showstoppers retain the EWA World Tag Team Belts. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ The Dream Team) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) A fantastic match between two of the greatest in our sport, this one had the fans on the edge of their seat. Draven got the advantage first, and set J2B up for the Fallen Angel...however, J2B flipped out of the powerbomb attempt, and pulled Draven off of the top rope. J2B then climbed to the top, only to be pushed off by Shawn Copeland, who had accompanied Draven to ringside. Finally, J2B had set Draven up for the Double Hammer, when Copeland ran in, and attacked J2B. Draven and Copeland both then proceeded to beat on J2B, and then left the ring together. Johnny 2 Bad d. Erik Draven by disqualification. EWA North American Championship Match "The Mastermind" Castor F'IN Troy (w/ NQ2) v. The Wyldsyde (EWA North American Champion) A good, solid contest saw Castor use some high-risk manuevers in attempt to thwart the technical wrestling attack of The Wyldsyde. And it worked...for five minutes. After that, The Wyldsyde began to take control, and finally polished Castor off. The Wyldsyde d. Castor Troy with The Wyldryde. The Wyldsyde remains EWA North American Champion. EWA European Championship Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Dream Team) v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion, w/ The PHC) Before the match, President Gates announced a new "match" for Clash Of The Champions V. SmirtDogg and Makaveli will be participating in a "rap-off". Both men will come to the ring, and do a rap...the crowd will then judge whose rap is best. (OCC: The Vice Presidents will determine the winner of the rap-off.) The match then got underway, and it was quite evident that these two had bad blood spilled over. Erik Draven once again accompanied Copeland to ringside, and stood silently by, watching the events of the match. Neither man was able to ever gain a clear-cut advanatge, until Makaveli went to the trop rope for a lariat attempt. Copeland ducked, and Makaveli hit the referee. Makaveli went to work on Copeland some more, until SmirtDogg ran in from the crowd, and executed a Superkick on Makaveli. Copeland then rolled over, and the official slowly counted to three. As the camera faded to black, President Gates announced yet another COTCV match...a rematch between these two. Shawn Copeland d. Makaveli with help from SmirtDogg. Shawn Copeland is the new EWA European Champion!! <!--LINE2--> <!--LINE4-->
