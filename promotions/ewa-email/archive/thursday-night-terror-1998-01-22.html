EWA Thursday Night Terror

22 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 22, 1998 Live, from McNichols Arena, in Denver, Colorado. Semi-Final EWA Television Championship Tournament Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Octavius (w/ The Empire) This was another exciting match, as Octavius and Austin fought what was a fairly even match for the first 5 minutes. But out of nowhere, Austin hit the Stone Cold Stunner, and propelled himself to the Telvision tournament finals. Steve Austin d. Octavius via the Stone Cold Stunner. (5:14) Semi-Final EWA Television Championship Tournament Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. KG (w/ The Stud Stable) This was no contest, as Triple-H absolutely destroyed KG. The real excitement was afterward, when President Gates and Comissioner Slaughter came to the ring. President Gates: Helmsley, everyone saw what you did at a recent house show to myself and to Comissioner Slaughter. As a result, I have no choice but to suspend you from Monday Night Madness! At that point, Helmsley went after Gates, but Slaughter decked him. Triple-H got back up, and as he and Chyna moved in on the two, Bret Hart came and made the save. Bret Hart then spoke with both men, and seemed very pleased as he left the ring... Hunter Hearst Helsmley d. KG via the Pedigree. (4:04) FlashBack v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) This was a terrific tag team contest, with Prophecy showing why they are one of the greatest tag teams today, and FlashBack also making an impressive debut. In the end, the 70's rejects pulled it out, as Flash Funk hit his "Funky Flash Splash" on Travis Scott in a grueling matchup. FlashBack d. Prophecy via Flash Funk's pin on Travis Scott after the Funky Flash Splash. The Violator v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) In one of the fastest matches in EWA history, Kane absolutely demolished The Violator. The Violator seemed very prepared for the match, but Kane simply overwhelmed him. Kane d. The Violator via the Tombstone Piledriver. (0:21) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) In this contest, Death Sentance showed that they are no longer the laughing stock of the wrestling world. Death Sentance and Harlem Heat put on a terrific show, as the advantage swayed back and forth between the two teams. In the end, however, the experience of the EWA World Tag Team Champions paid dividends, as Stevie Ray connected with the Harlem Hangover. Harlem Heat d. Death Sentance via Stevie Ray's pin on Pain after the Harlem Hangover. (19:11) 6 Man Tag Team Match The Nation Of Domination (Faarooq/Rocky Maivia/D-Lo Brown) v. The IV Horsemen (Ric Flair/Arn Anderson/Chris Benoit) This match never got underway, as the stables brawled as soon as they got to the ring. The official threw out the match. Afterward, Comissioner Slaughter appeared on the MegaTron above the entrance way. He announced that Triple-H would not be suspended for Monday Night Madness, because he, along with President Gates, had thought of a harsher punishment. He then announced that Triple-H would face Bret "The Hitman" Hart at MNM....in a fifteen foot high steel cage. The NOD and IV Horsemen match was thrown out. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Marc Anthony (w/ Cleopatra & The Empire) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Marc Anthony had an incredible match, as he and SmirtDogg put on a spectacular show. The crowd booed Smirt when he came out, which seemed to somewhat throw him off of his game. However, experience once again paid off, as Smirt was able to apply the Thug Breaker, and win the match. SmirtDogg d. Marc Anthony via submission with the Thug Breaker. (12:20) "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock v. Erik Draven (w/ Siren & Degeneration X) This match showed why Erik Draven is one of the best, as he completely dominated Shamrock...without DX interference. Erik Draven d. Ken Shamrock via the DX Driver. (3:59) The Widow Maker v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) In another impressive showing by Degeneration X, Shawn Michaels easily got a victory over The Widow Maker. Shawn Michaels d. The Widow Maker via the Sweet Chin Music. (4:17) Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Bret "The Hitman" Hart This was an outstanding match, as Bret Hart and Augustus matched each other move for move. In the end, the match was declared a double-countout, as both men began to brawl on the outside, and lost track of the count. Bret Hart and Augustus battled to a double countout. (10:02) The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) v. Tiberus/Julius (w/ The Empire) In a highly competitive match, the cheap tatics of The New Age Outlaws once again came into play, as Chyna walloped Julius with a chair, enabling "Bad Ass" Billy Gunn to score a pinfall win. The New Age Outlaws d. Tiberus/Julius via Billy Gunn's pin on Julius after interference from Chyna. Vinnie Vegas v. Nero (w/ The Empire) Surprisingly, Vinnie Vegas seemed unfocused, enabling Nero to pound him into submission. A total squash. Nero d. Vinnie Vegas by submission via a Boston Crab. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. The Gunslinger The Gunslinger's status in the EWA was uncertain as of the time this was wrote, as he didn't even show up. President Gates instead send out a jobber from the Power Plant named Jeff Rogers, who GC promptly destroyed. The Golden Child d. Jeff Rogers via the Royal Destroyer. (1:01) Non-Title Match Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) v. Prodigy (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion) This was a classic matchup, as Nash and Prodigy went toe-to-toe. Nash's power dominated the matchup early on, but Prodigy's mind games swayed the advantage to his side. He was about to go for the Firestarter when Erik Draven ran out, knocking him off the top rope. Draven had what appeared to be a title which said "CWA World Heavyweight Champion" with him, and Nash and Draven gave Prodigy repeated JackKnifes and DX Drivers. Prodigy d. Kevin Nash via disqualifictation. (11:23) Triangle Match for the EWA Intercontinental Championship "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ The IV Horsemen & Kimberly) v. Scott Hall (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Pessimist (EWA Intercontinental Champion) This was an exciting matchup, filled with great action. DDP was the first man eliminated, as The Pessimist and Hall double-teamed him, with Hall putting him out of comission with the Outsider's Edge. Pessimist and Hall then went head-to-head, with the momentum on Hall's side, thanks to constant interference from Shawn Michaels. Eventually, Hall put The Pessimist away with The Outsider's Edge, and became the new EWA Intercontienental Champion. Scott Hall wins the triangle match. (16:59) Scott Hall is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion!
