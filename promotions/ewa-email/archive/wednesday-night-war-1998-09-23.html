EWA Wednesday Night War

23 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 23, 1998 Live, from San Jose Arena San Jose, California Interview with EWA World Heavyweight Champion "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ The New World Order) v. The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Tag Team Grudge Match The Gunslinger & "The Franchise" Shane Douglas (w/ The Firm) v. Hollywood Hogan & Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The New World Order) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Jack Reed (w/ The Hate Club) v. "The Rock" Grady Smith (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ D-Generation X) The Poison Pit (Hosted by "The Extreme Superstar" Venom, with special guest "The ICON" Erik Draven) The Pit has been re-scheduled. Serpent Man v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin A double no-show here. James Mastin v. Edge Edge looked very impressive in his EWA debut..interestingly enough, after his match, Gangrel returned the favor from SNS, hitting the Piledriving DDT on Edge. Deader v. Mac Namara A good, solid match between two bright young cruiserweights saw the crowd get behind both men. An important match for both men ended when Deader, unsuspectingly, was caught in the Mac Five. Mac Namara d. Deader with the Mac Five. Tommy Dreamer v. KG (w/ The New World Order) Dreamer put up a good fight, but it was his use of a chair which costed him the match. As a result, Conklin, Hart, and KG all attacked Dreamer viciously. KG d. Tommy Dreamer by disqualification. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Michael Modest v. New Jack (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Easily the best match of the night. In an epic battle for the Light Heavyweight Title, these two fought more like heavyweight brawlers, using every thing that could be used at ringside as a weapon. In the end, however, New Jack barely ended up getting the win, with a clever move. In anticipation of the Gangsta Splash, Modest moved out of the way. However, in mid-flight, New Jack heaved the chair at Modest, nailing him hard enough for New Jack to get the win. New Jack d. Michael Modest. New Jack remains EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. *** Suddenly, the lights fade to black. A golden strobe light goes back and fourth over the crowd. On the EWA-tron by the entrance ramp, there is a huge III, signifying the Triple Threat. Then, below that the words flash. They say Bunda.. Hicks.. The Golden One. As "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple plays over the loud speakers throughout the arena, everyone is in anticipation of Jeff Bunda's return to the EWA. Suddenly, a figure in an armani suit comes out of the entrance way. Wearing a cast and a wig, Joe Satre is wearing a shirt that bears the words FRANCHISE on it. He enters the ring, and grabs a mic.*** Joe Satre- Cut the CENSORED music. Did everyone miss me? I sure as all hell bet that you have. With Pimp Daddy, Joe Satre imjured, I bet it's pretty damn hard for the EWA to pack the seats these days. But, that's all going to change very soon. Yeah, back at Wrestlecade, Mayne Mann wrenched up on my arm, and it hurt like a CENSORED too. What can I say, I'm only human, as much as I defy any laws that humanity has set for us. We all know that in the match that I lost my championship, that I never, ever gave up. In fact, I challenge anyone here to name one time that I have forefeited in a match. The referee stopped the match, forcing Joe Satre to drop the ball so to speak. But, what happened to the Intoxication-X Army's leader at the Wednesday war that would follow? Crazy Canuck gained some dignity back for The Hate Club, taking away Mann's title, bringing the gold back home to where it rightfully belongs. That's right, to the good old hate club. We will once again be in full force after a couple of weeks with my cast on the way it is. But rest assured, Pimp Daddy, with the Canadian Sensation, The Walking Triple Threat, and The Heart Break Kid, we're gonna dance over anyone who opposes us. We don't care who it is. You name the date, you name the time. We'll bring enough amunition to start a nuclear meltdown. Joe Satre- Speaking of some opposition here in the EWA, let's get back to the title situation here. You may ask who's #1 on Joe Satre's hitlist? Is it Shawn Copeland, is it I-X, Prodigy, or Erik Draven? Wellllllllll, it just so happens that my number 1 most wanted man has officially fled the federation, obviously in fear of the great Hate Club. Yes, I'm talking about Mayne Mann. Who was number two on the list? Well, that's where the hard part comes in. Number two, now number one on Pimp Daddy's list is not an actual wrestler. No, it is not Duane Gates either. But, I'm gonna take the time right here and now to spell it on out for you. The number one thing on my mind is getting back into the race for some gold here in the EWA. So, what I am after is the European Championship of the EWA. That's right, the very belt which Smirtdogg holds so very dear to him right now, is the apple in my eye. It also has been brought to my attention that he has contenders already lined up for shots at Smirt and his belt. That's all fine and good with me, seeing as how I am currently injured and out of action for a few weeks. As a matter of fact, I am going to take it upon myself to issue a straight up challenge right here and now. As we all know, when P-Daddy Joe Satre is speaking, you better listen. The challenge is quite simple, actually. I want the winner of the Smirt Dogg vs. Erik Draven spectacular. This match is when, Clash of Champions 6? Hell, boys, I've got quite a few openings around that time. So if you want a piece of the Hate Club, step on over. Oh, and Draven--if you happen to win, we'll have a few suprises for you. Maybe another plate glass window, or another ex-girlfriend of yours shall make an appearence with us. As for you, Smirt, we've never faced off, but when we do, I'm sure it will be a classic. Either way, I want my shot at the European Title, no matter who wins, we all know the final outcome. Another win for Pimp Daddy, bottom line. Joe Satre- That's right, ladies and gentlemen. If I get my way, which usually happens, you all will see your favorite Pimp with some more gold here in the EWA. But right now, what I want is for everyone in the back to listen up, and listen up good. It seems as though there have been a few rumors as to some members of a rival faction and myself getting into a little scuffel after the Wrestlecade PPV. Actually, that's not the whole story. What happened was they jumped me after my match, in an unprovoked attack, further injuring my arm. It's a good thing that the Hate Club and Gatesy were nearby, or I might not be out here tonight. Now, don't get me wrong. I am totally against gang warfare, but what Will Haynes, Mayne Mann, and Haywood Jublome did to me in the back was totally uncalled for. Now, before I go any further, I'd like to introduce to you, the NEW North American Champion of the World, "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck!!!! ***Suddenly, the I-X theme song plays, as Will Haynes and Haywood Jublome come out and nail Canuck from behind with a chair. With Canuck laid out face down on the steel entrance ramp, they take turns blasting him with a chair. Suddenly, Jack Reed and Shawn Michaels come out, followed by El Locco Jobbo. As as brawl ensues, Joe Satre grabs and mic and begins to speak.*** Joe Satre- What in the hell is going on? What in the hell? This isn't what I was talking about.... ***Suddenly, Joe Satre is laid out with a kick to the back of the head by Rob Van Dam, who entered through the audience. Van Dam picks up a chair, throws it at Satre, and nails him with the Van Daminator. He jumps on the top rope, and exectues a split legged moonsault. With Satre laid out, he exits the ring in the same fashing that he entered, through the crowd. The other brawl is still going on.*** Rob Van Dam- You all thought that I would never find you? Well guess again, punks. RVD is here, and here to stay. What you saw tonight is just the beginning of Excellence that is The I-X Army. The newest of soldiers in the Army, we will one day reign supreme. And Satre, Rob Van Dam 4:20 says I just smoked your ass!!!! ***With that, the I-X Army all leave through the crowd as a unit. As the camera pans around, we see Reed, Canuck, and HBK getting to their feet, obviously stunned from the I-X attack. Satre is still in the ring, knocked out.*** The Black Angels v. The New Age Outlaws (w/ D-Generation X) Turns out the Outlaws didn't have a valid EWA contract...and are no longer part of the EWA. Zuliel v. Gangrel Gangrel demolished Zuliel, only to be attacked from behind by Edge afterward. Gangrel then stated to Edge that if "you want the resurrection, we'll do it at the Clash. But I will not be held responsible...for her actions." Gangrel d. Zuliel with the Piledriving DDT. Joe Lemon & Chris Craft v. The Outsiders (w/ The New World Order) Two words: SQUASH CITY! Hall & Nash totally dominated this contest. The Outisders d. Lemon/Craft with Kevin Nash's pin on Chris Craft after the JackKnife. *The lights in the San Jose Arena fade to black, just for a moment, then the lights come back up in an ominous gray. 'November Rain' by Guns 'N' Roses begins to play over the PA system, as all of the fans light up their lighters as customary when the lights go out. However, they are promptly extinguished as a light, cold drizzle begins to appear in the arena, accompanied by a gradicose, thick fog. Mysterio finally appears, covering himself in an umbrella and a black trench coat, but not wearing his mask. His face, however, cannot be seen through the darkness and the fog. The music slowly fades out, as Mysterio remains at the top of the entrance ramp. Mysterio begins to speak as the rain begins to stop.* Mysterio: *breaks into a hysterical laughter* You EWA fans ... you EWA superstars ... you all thought that I was gone. Just because I didn't make an appearance at the Saturday Night EWA Television show, you all thought that you had rid the professional wrestling world of the man who can terrorize -- terrorize because I represent the truth. As long as there is trash, as long as there is underachievement, insufficiency -- as long as there is human s*** in the EWA, the spirit of Mysterio will live. I will ALWAYS live. I transgress ages, I walk across ethnocultural, geopolitical boundaries -- no impediment is able to stop me. Why? I represent a basic, fundamental human need -- the need to fear failure. As long as I'm around, you EWA superstars will continue to push the envelope, continue to push the threshold, always trying to reach some unattainable goal of the pinnacle of insurmountable success -- and you know, that if you don't at the very least attempt to do this, you will pay dearly - in some cases, with everything that you cherish in your mundane little lives. I can and will back up my words. And to prove it ... I'm here tonight to observe one World Television Championship match. And that match involves one Haywood Jublome -- the man who has tried to destroy me in the past. Remember, Jublome? Your lack of excellence, your inherent inability to realize the fact that you are an insignificant little peon in the macrochosim of the universe -- once you realize that, you will learn that you have achieved true excellence -- humility. One can strive for material well being, dialectic in nature -- but once they truly achieve this plane of intellectual stimulation, then they truly achieve a state of perfect dominance, a state of legitamacy unparalleled by the ordinary human being. But until then, striving for greatness -- you all must think I'm contradicting myself! Allow me for a bit of a digression. When you strive for excellence, and you embody all the properties that I have described in my short tenure here -- intelligence, ability, desire, and most of all, humility -- then it is acceptable and expected that you engage in some sort of strive. An attempt to achieve --- which cannot and will not happen when you are the lowest form of life that has ever existed, someone who barely deserves the right to take a breath -- like Haywood Jublome -- who destroys people in this strive for achievement -- then the spirit will overwhelm. The spirit will conquer. And the spirit will prove victorious. Tonight, Jublome, you have yet another opprotunity at materialistic excellence -- the EWA World Television Championship. And in my quest to exterminate the human trash from the EWA, performers like you, Venom, Erik Draven, Grady Smith, and the rest -- you're first on the list. You squandered my first opprotunity which I gave to you for materialistic excellence. Will you squander the second? Will you continue in this downward spiral of your life, or will you accept my challenge -- the challenge to succeed? Jublome, 45 days until destruction ... will you live to see 44? Only if I allow it ... *As quickly as those last words were communicated, a cloud of fog engulfs Mysterio, and within a few seconds, the fog clears, and Mysterio is gone. The grey lights which have basked the arena disappear in lieu of the normal stage lights.* EWA World Television Championship Haywood Jublome (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) An outstanding match...this one had the fans on the edge of their seats, although neither man got a particularly favorable action. But the sheer excitement of the match was enough for the Californians. And then, the oddities began... *The lights drop out for a few moments, and a loud chair shot is heard. The lights raise, with a greyish tone, and Venom is laid out in the middle of the ring, with Haywood Jublome standing, looking astonished. Mysterio stands for a brief moment at the top of the entranceramp, with his mask on, but slowly turns around and walks to the back as the lights raise up* Jublome then crawled over and made the cover, but Venom kicked out. The match went on, but unfortunately, a winner wasn't decided, as both men brawled to a double countout. Venom and Haywood Jublome battled to a double countout. Venom remains EWA World Television Champion. Galaxy v. Val Venis A good match between two of the EWA's younger wrestlers ended when Galaxy missed the SuperNova, giving Venis enough time to hit the Money Shot and score (no pun intended) the victory. Val Venis d. Galaxy with the Money Shot. Slain v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) Slain put up one hell of a fight, but Lady Jane continues to help Ruger Ice's career, and Slain went down to the big man from Battle Creek. Ruger Ice d. Slain with the Spine Buster. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck & "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Hate Club) Lucien and DAP actually controlled the opening minutes of this contest, but the Hate Club members were simply too overwhelming, and ended up with the victory. Crazy Canuck & Shawn Michaels d. Darkness after Shawn Michaels pinned Lucien with a moonsault, which was preceeded by Canuck's Lunatic Bomb. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ D-Generation X) This was actually a fairly sloppy contest, which ended in a double DQ when both Shawn Michaels and Joe Satre ran out, and attacked their respective enemies. Erik Draven and Will Haynes battle to a double disqualification. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match New Demolition v. The Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ D-Generation X) Out of all of the battles these two teams have had, this was the best. New Demolition dominated the opening portion of the match, but Public Enemy, to the fans' delight, began battling back. Eventually, they were able to finally pull off the win after the Drive-By. T.P.E. d. New Demolition with Swap Meet Louie's pin on Semi after the Drive By. T.P.E. remain EWA World Tag Team Champions. Booker T: Aight, this is taking too long. If you two can't make up your damn minds then I guess I shall do it for you. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Trixie) v. Curt Hennig (w/ Rick Rude & The Firm) Hennig didn't even bother to show up, and Stevie entertained the fans by coming out again as Mr. Perfect. Stevie Richards d. Curt Hennig by forfeit. He is dressed like a cover for GQ, as he is decked out in another gold, custom tailored Armani, and his own line of horn-rimmed sunglasses. He stands in the entrance way, taking in the view, as a HUGE Pyro Display blows up with gold fireworks. To the crowd's delight, Copeland begins to walk to the ring, polishing his World Title that is laying over his shoulder. He whips out his own mic from his breast pocket as he climbs into the ring. As Reed whipped Smith in the ropes, Crazy Canuck, who was out and talking trash to a fan, stuck his hand out to trip Smith. However, the whip was reversed, and Canuck accidentally tripped Reed. Smith then hit the ChokeBuster for the win. Following the match, Canuck challenged Smith for a title vs. title match next Wednesday, and Smith accepted. Grady Smith d. Jack Reed with the Chokebuster. Grady Smith remains EWA Intercontinental Title.
