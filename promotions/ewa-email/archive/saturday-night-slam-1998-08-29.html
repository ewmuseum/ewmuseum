EWA Saturday Night Slam

29 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 29, 1998 Live, from University Arena Muncie, Indiana (The EWA returns to the hometown of its President) The Poison Pit (Hosted By "The Extreme Superstar" Venom, with special guest Crazy Canuck) Jim Ross: And now we turn to…a “great” segment of WNW, one which……which we are STILL proud to present, the POISON PIT, Hosted by “The Extreme SuperStar” Venom…God almighty what was Gates thinking when he allowed for him to….oh! Lets head to the set! (the camera pans over to a dimly lit set. The spotlight shines on the set as Fireworks are lit off. The set looks somewhat like a darker version of the set of “Late Night With Conan O Brien”, with a huge plate glass window in the backdrop.” “Jesus Christ SuperStar” begins to blare over the PA system, as Venom, decked in a blue Armani suit with expensive shades, makes his way onto the set. The crowd erupts with boos. Venom grabs a mic off of the desk and speaks.) Venom: Shut the Hell up, each and everyone one of you beerguts!!! * Crowd boos loudly* That’s right, it’s time once again for the highest rated segment ever on Warzone….THE POISON PIT!!!. You all know who I am….your EXTREME GOD, VENOM. Now, what I want for you peons, Pukes, and Hacksaw Jim Duggan worshippers out there, is to shut your mouths, button those herpesinfested lips…and ladies I am not talking about those lips between your legs, even though by the way this arena smells like frothy cheese and eggs, I think you hookers need to do that anyway!!!! (The females in the audience are offended and begin to boo loudly. Venom lets out a sinister laugh and sits behind the desk. He takes a sip of the coffee that is in his mug. A look of disgust quickly appears on the Extreme SuperStar’s face. He quickly waves for his assistant to come on stage. The assistant, who is visibly scared, comes on to the set.) Venom: Goddamn, what kind of Coffee is this youidiot!? I told you I don’t drink * BEEP* like this!!! What possessed you to even buy a brand like this…and it’s scorching hot too!! Do you know how to do a Kevin Nash, 9 to 5ver? Assistant: Uh…uh n-n-no Mr. Extreme SuperStar sir, how do I do a Kevin Nash? Venom: Well idiot it’s so simple even these backwater HillBilly Jims out in the audience can do it. It’s like this: First, some stupid assed assistant, such as your fine self, brings an Extreme Superstar like me a scolding hot cup of Sanka, knowing that he hates the brand. Next, the Extreme SuperStar…that’s me, brings the idiot assistant on the set on national television. Then, he berates him about how stupid he is for making such a *BEEP * cup of coffee. Finally, The Extreme SuperStar, that’s me, gets angry and does THIS!!! (Venom throws the hot scolding coffee into the man’s eyes, burning him severly. He is on the ground, screaming and holding his face. Venom then smashes the mug on top of the man’s head, knocking him out. Venom then picks the man up and throws him off the set, and the man’s unconscious body bounces against the cold hard concrete.) Venom: Shoot, that was fun. But not as much fun as I am going to have tonite. You see, tonight is the QuarterFinals of the I-C Tournament! And who do I face? None other than…a CANADIAN. God I hate them. And tonight, in front of the world, I will exercise my anger against these pieces of human trash by taking on Crazy Canuck. Speaking of that idiot, he’s my special guest for tonight. So, without further delay, all of you idiots welcome Crazy Canuck if you want to because I damn sure didn’t want this *BEEP * bag on my show. Thank you Mr. Duane Gates, once again you have….nevermind. Man just cue up this Pink and Blacker’s music and send his flaming ass out here. ("Revolution 909" by Daft Punk begins to cue up over the PA as Crazy Canuck, wearing the new "Prodigy Hate Club" t-shirt, walks onto the set. Venom yawns as the wrestler shows off for the crowd.) Venom: Look you idiot Yak boy, sit your ass down on the couch here, we have an interview to do. Canuck: YAK BOY?? YAK BOY?? The only Yak Boy is Prodigy! *sits down* Happy? Lets get it on! Venom: Shut the hell up. So, anyway. Tonight, you and I will wrestle to advance to the Finals. I basically am going to give you the wrestling lesson of your goddam life you pathetic piece of Canada sewage!! Now, unlike what others would like to think, I will try to keep this in the ring, but for some reason all of you idiots try to get hardcore with me. Now you especially know that you can’t beat me at that game. That’s my territory, you start swinging chairs, I’ll whip out a lead pipe. You want to choke with television wires, I set you on fire. It’s that simple. Speaking of setting things on fire, give me and all of these idiots out in the audience some info on why you hate Prodigy!! Canuck: First off, I'm going to be the teacher around here tonight. No freaking Dean Douglas only me you got that boyo? Good! The reason I hate Prodigy is because he is a overrated ego manic who only win if he cheats his ass off. Draven and you are just the same. The only way you can win is if you resort to the lowest dominator. The group consists of Myself, Jack Reed, "Pimpin" Joe Satre and Skippy. Venom: The only way you can win you Pink and Blacker is basically to have your squad of queen boys interfere in the match. But, Shoot you losers mustn’t be that bad, You hate prodigy for gods sake. You all may suck ass, including that Pimp you hang out with, but you do have common sense. Besides I liked that Dean Douglas gimmick. Canuck: Shut your mouth, and get used to it. Cause I'm going to shut you up permanently tonight like or not......your ass will be grass. I don't care what you say you will feel my wrath. Hell why don't we get it on right now? Venom: First of all jabronie don’t you damn well come on here with your ignorant dirty Canadian mouth, spewing trash!!! If you want to do this right now, Fine with me, *BEEP *!!! (Venom is about to get out of his chair, when Canuck grabs him by the collar and flips him out over the desk and onto the hard floor. Venom holds his back as Canuck begins to stomp away at him. Canuck then gets Venom off the floor and on to his feet, and throws a punch, but The Extreme SuperStar dodges it and follows in with a punch to the breadbasket. He then follows it up by several kicks to Canuck’s crotch and midsection, knocking the Canadian athlete to the ground. Venom rips the Prodigy Hate club shirt off of Canuck’s body and begins to choke him with it. Canuck, out of an act of desperation, rakes Venom’s eyes. He is about to get up when suddenly the Prodigy Hate Club storms the set. Satre and Reed go after Venom, and they toss the Extreme SuperStar off of his set, making him land on the hard floor. As they are about to go in for the kill, President Duane Gates appears, and makes them stop. The Extreme SuperStar, insane with anger, grabs one of the huge spotlights from the walkway and rushes back on the set, and nails Satre with a brutal shot to the back of his head with it, smashing the glass of the light upon impact. Satre goes down, holding the back of his bloodied head. Venom then throws the light at Reed, hitting him with it and making him fall. Venom then grabs a steel chair and wacks it across Reed’s back. Venom then turns around, only to hit by Canuck with alariat. Venom, from the ground uses the chair and bashes Canuck across the knee with it. Canuck buckles and Venom prepares to go in, but is stopped by the stern voice of Duane Gates.) Gates: Stop damn it, I said stop it!!!!!! (The arena goes silent at the yelling of the President. Venom stops and looks at the man, who has a microphone in his hand.) Gates: What do you think the two of you are doing?? Canuck, I am ashamed of you, fighting like this when you know you have a match tonight. And Venom…I am so disgusted by your actions I am tempted to fire you right here on the spot!! Look at what you did, not only earlier this week do you cause an international incident on Canadian television, but then you take part in what has become a damn riot!!!! Look at what you did to Joe Satre!! The man has a match against Prodigy tonight!!! Or Reed, look what you did to him! Now…I should take action and suspend you tonight, but I will not. Tonight you will wrestle Crazy Canuck, but believe me I will punish you, you…you…someone get some help out here for these men! (Gates slams the mic down on the ground and leaves the set. Venom, who is glaring at the president, drops his chair, sees the damage that he has done, and laughs. He then departs the set, holding his lower back area.) Sycho Sid v. Al Snow (w/ The J.O.B. Squad) Our opening contest at Saturday Night Slam was a solid one between two newcomers to the EWA. Sid started out in control of the match, using his power to overcome the smaller Snow. Snow soon gained the advantage with a series of chops to the knees of Sid, wearing the big man down. However, Sid soon thwarted this with a huge double ax-handle to the back of Snow. Sid then set Snow up for the Powerbomb, but Snow blocked it with a painful low blow. Snow then took the perfect opportunity, and nailed the Snow Plow on the monster, pulling off the first victory for the J.O.B. Squad. Al Snow d. Sycho Sid with the Snow Plow. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia (w/ The Nation) v. Tommy Dreamer The Rock definitely hit Rock Bottom tonight, as he put in an absolutely god-awful performance. Tommy Dreamer thoroughly destroyed him in his debut. Afterward, to add insult to injury, The Rock was dealt four chair shots from Haywood Jublome, who came from under the ring. Tommy Dreamer d. The Rock with the DDT. EWA North American Championship Match Mayne Mann (w/ Jackson Montgomery) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA North American Champion, w/ D-Generation X) (Mayne Mann broke kayfabe numerous times in his posts for this match; therefore, Draven obtained a forfeit victory in this contest.) Erik Draven d. Mayne Mann by forfeit. Erik Draven is still EWA North American Champion. The Widow Maker v. "The Rock" Grady Smith As "The Rock" is about to pin the Widowmaker, a masked man comes out of the crowd, runs into the ring, and bashes Smith over the head with a cookie sheet. The masked man then kicks Smith in the stomach several times, and then runs out of the ring. Grady Smith d. The Widow Maker by disqualification. Deader v. Ruger Ice This was a solid match, one in which both men swapped the advantage back and forth. Ruger used his power to his advantage, while Deader used his considerable speed and athleticsm advantage. However, Ruger Ice, who had an extremely determined look in his eye, was the better man tonight, pulling out the victory. Ruger Ice d. Deader with the Spinebuster. *Following the conclusion of the Deader/Ruger Ice Match the lights in the arena dimmed and familiar music cued up. "Get at me Dog" by DMX. SmirtDogg appears in the entranceway and as he makes his way to the ring he slaps hands and greets a few EWA fans. SmirtDogg leaps over the top rope and lands inside the squared circle. As he takes the golden microphone from out of his pocket mostly boo's can be heard with a few cheers here and there. SmirtDogg begins to speak before the capacity crowd.* SmirtDogg: It's great to be here in Muncie, Indiana. The place where it all began. Who would have thought when the EWA first opened for business that it'll last this long. Amongst all the competition the EWA has strived and made its way to the pinnacle of this great sport. Against all odds the EWA has triumphed and it's because of all of you out there that the EWA is the best damn federation in the world today. I'm taking the time out now to thank each and every one of you for your support. The EWA because of its great following from its loyal fans shall continue to be the leading wrestling organization well into the next millenium. There's nothing that can hold the EWA down and nobody that can hold me -- Smirt-D-O-Double G down. That brings me to the main reason why I'm out here. Last Saturday Night Slam, Mayne Mann, had the courage or shall I say the stupidity to nail me on the forehead with a microphone and place me into his version of the Crippler Crossface. That will not go unanswered. I've been on vacation but now I'm back with a vengeance. I seen Draven dispose of "The Ultimate Jobber" without having to raise a finger. This upcoming Wednesday Night Warzone will be my turn at Mayne Mann because if he accepts the challenge I'm laying before him what Draven did to him will pale in comparison to what I have in store for Mayne Mann. Mayne Mann judgement day has arrived for you. You'll be tryed, sentenced and executed by me in my judicial court which happens to be this right here. *he points down towards the canvas of the wrestling ring.* Mayne Mann time is running out on you quicker than sand in an hourglass. Regret is what you'll soon feel. You're going to wish that you never challenged my authority. It's too late for second thoughts and what ifs. You're in this way too deep. Your ass is mine. WORD UP!!! *SmirtDogg puts the Golden Microphone back into his pocket and leaves the ring to a mixture of cheers and boos.* (Just before the Handey Gripwell/Will Haynes match is about to start, 'Perfect Strangers' blares over the PA and the fans get up, not sure what to expect. Well, the Franchise comes out, carrying the EWA World Championship belt and a microphone, wearing his street clothes of a black silk shirt and khaki pants. He storms into the ring to give a little interview.) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda: Cut the f*cking music! Now, what is going on now in the EWA is no different from what's gone on in previous weeks - some punk ass mark is trying to violate the ORDER OF THE FRANCHISE - the order of things around here in the EWA which I built. And in this case - it's some insurgent little kid by the name of Grady Smith. Now, I remember this guy back from the old days of the EWA ... back then, he was just a little kid. Now, he's an INSURGENT little kid, which makes him even more dangerous ... and to get into the limelight, he had a real bright idea - attack the Franchise. Just as countless others in this business have done so - and just as countless others in this business have been beaten. And beaten BAD. Grady, you're no different from the others - let's look at the facts. Every two weeks or so in the EWA, there's a little 'rebellious' stable that comes up and tries to challenge the Triple Threat for the spot of the stable that RUNS THIS SHOW. Look at the competition that's come down the pike - TWO Degeneration-X's, the Dream Team, The Wolfpack, The Ghetto Warriors, and now the No Limit Soldiers. No Limit Soldiers? No limit to what - how much you'll job? At least that'll be the case when you go up against the 'Chise and/or any member of the Triple Threat - so Public Enemy, you say you're not aligned - bullsh*t. You knew that the Extreme Dream Machine was going to hand you your ass on a platter, and you knew the only way to win was to rile up the troops to get some support. Well, TPE, Smith - you've asked for it. You want it? Let's do it. We've got three guys, you've got three guys. Let's have a little war, b*tches. You want to try to take over the EWA? You want to try to destroy the ERA of the FRANCHISE, the era which I single handedly built? You can't, and you never will be able to ... because, you see - I RUN THIS SHOW!!! And the next addition to the 'Stable of the Month' club can't do a damn thing about it. (Bunda leaves the ring, as the next match is about to get underway...) Handey Gripwell v. Will "The Thrill" Haynes In a SHOCKING upset, Gripwell pulled off his first EWA victory, defeating the burnout. Handey Gripwell d. Will Haynes with a small package. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Jobber Express Darkness looked flat-out AWFUL tonight, while Johnny Swinger and Barry Horowitz, guided by Teddy Long, were firing on all cylinders, and easily defeated the duo of DAP and Lucien. The Jobber Express d. Darkness with Barry Horowitz's pin on Darkness after a gutwrench powerbomb. Vic Deakins v. Mike Modest (w/ Thorne Richards) Mike Modest d. Vic Deakins by forfeit. Semi-Final Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Crazy Canuck (w/ The PHC) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom The intensity between these two that leaked out during the Poison Pit exploded into the open during this match, as the match erupted into a falls-count-anywhere match. After fighting all over the building, the two finally ended up on the outside of the arena, in the parking lot for the wrestlers. Canuck took this opportunity to hop inside President Gates' car, and nail Venom with it. After this, Canuck executed the Lunatic Bomb on the roof, and got the pinfall. Crazy Canuck d. Venom with the Lunatic Bomb on the hood of President Gates' car. Terry Conklin (w/ D-Generation X) v. KG (w/ Sunny) This was an evenly fought contest, until the very end, when interference marred the battle. KG executed a plancha onto Conklin, who was on the outside of the ring. As KG got to his feet, the crowd began to point to the entrance ramp, where New Jack was seen sprinting toward the ring, pushing a shopping cart. New Jack rammed the shopping cart, which had a chair inside, right into KG, knocking him to the floor. New Jack threw KG in the ring, climbed to the top rope with the chair, and executed the Gangsta Splash. *Following the beatdown New Jack inflicted upon KG he demands the microphone from Ring Announcer David Pinzer and issues the following statement while glaring down at the fallen "Outlaw Immortal".* New Jack: KG this is what you call retribution. Don't mess with the Original Gangsta New Jack unless you're ready to deal with the consequences. Obviously, from your position, you don't have what it takes to compete with me. Lying there on your back helpless, you should be ashamed of yourself. But then again you might be content with finally breaking that losing streak of yours and getting a win. A DQ win but for you any win you stumble upon is golden. Haley's Comet comes along more often than a KG victory it seems. KG you stirred up a whole bunch of trouble when you decided to attack me and nail me with that feeble finisher of yours -- the BlackOut. You may have gotten me on that night but tonight was the big payback. KG the EWA fans want one thing and that's to be entertained. Well when it comes to the feud we have brewing they'll get what they want plus much, much more. *KG trys to get to his feet but New Jack serves up one kick to KG's head and that's enough to knock KG back down.* You're quite the impatient one. I'm not through with you yet. Hell I'm just getting started. KG you brought this all upon yourself. I don't take it lightly when another wrestler attacks me. In fact, it pisses me the hell off. That's why this has happened to you today and will continue to be an occurance at every EWA event until you apologize for your actions. So KG the ball is in your court. Apologize and this all goes away or don't and continue to be my personal punching bag. What's it going to be? Atleast if you apologize you'll only be losing that miniscule amount of self respect you have for yourself. However, taking the other route you just might wind up losing your EWA wrestler career. KG -- Welcome to the Terrordome. Hit my music! *Natural Born Killas by Ice Cube/Dr. Dre blares over the University Arena as New Jack exits the ring and walks to the back to a mix reaction.* KG d. Terry Conklin by disqualification. EWA European Championship Match Thorne Richards (w/ Mike Modest) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Another good match featured a heart-breaking ending for the former leader of No Quarter. During the contest, the official was accidentally knocked unconscious by Shawn Copeland. During this time, Thorne leveled Copeland, and locked on the Loch Ness Lock, and at the same time, attempted to revive the referee. Just as the official awoke, and as Copeland was tapping out, Erik Draven, who had accompanied Copeland to the ring, blasted Thorne Richards with a chair. The referee saw this, however, and disqualified Copeland, sealing the DQ win for Richards, but costing him the title. Thorne Richards d. Shawn Copeland by disqualification. Shawn Copeland is still EWA European Champion. Grudge Match (Step #2 In Prodigy's Fight For Freedom) "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The PHC) v. Prodigy Shortly before this card went to press, we discovered that EWA President Duane Gates had assigned a special guest referee for this match...himself. As President Gates came to the ring, to the tune of the instrumental version of "Intergalactic" by the Beastie Boys, it was noticed that he had a microphone in hand. Once Gates made his way to the ring, he began to speak. President Gates: Before I proceed to do an absolutely wonderful job here tonight as the special guest referee, I just have one announcement, regarding that vile, evil Erik Draven. Erik Draven, when you took that chair tonight, and you cracked it across the head of Thorne Richards, you DISGUSTED me. You showed me that you personify EVERYTHING wrong with professional wrestling today. And as a result of the evil actions you took against Thorne Richards tonight, Erik Draven, I have decided to impose a fine upon you that will be deducted from your salary, a fine of $50,000! Gates then chuckled to himself, but then his jaw dropped to the ground when he heard those three special words... BREAK IT DOWN!! Erik Draven then made his way to the ring, but did not get in. He just stood at ringside, and it became apparent that he was there to watch Gates, and make sure he called the match down the middle. From there, Prodigy and Joe Satre made their way to the ring, and engaged in a brutal battle. Prodigy was able to get an early advantage in the match, even with the crowd booing him. The crowd seemed to be behind President Gates, being in his hometown. Gates at first attempted to be completely biased toward Prodigy, actually trying to place a hand under Satre's shoulders when Prodigy went for a pin to make sure the shoulders down. However, one glare from the 7'1" Erik Draven on the outside convinced Gates to call the match fairly. From there, the match went on, with both men trading the advantage back and forth, until finally, out of nowhere, Prodigy hit the Firestarter. Gates simply stood there, refusing to count, as Prodigy covered Satre. This prompted Erik Draven to storm in the ring, wielding a chair. He glared at Gates, and VERY reluctantly, Gates made the three count. Draven nodded, and immediately returned to the locker room as Gates, even more reluctant, raised the hand of Prodigy, and then high-tailed out of the ring before Prodigy could attack him. Prodigy d. Joe Satre with the Firestarter.
