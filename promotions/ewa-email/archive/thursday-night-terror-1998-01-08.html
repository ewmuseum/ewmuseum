EWA Thursday Night Terror

8 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 8, 1998 Live, from The Selund Arena in Fresno, California. First Round Intercontinental Tournament Match Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) This was a very competitive match, as Helmsley and Anthony went back and forth. Anthony scored at least a dozen near-falls on "Triple-H", but Triple-H's experience began to overwhelm Anthony, who was making his EWA debut. Helmsley was setting Anthony up for the Pedigree, but Anthony executed a spectacular small package, and got the pinfall victory. Marc Anthony d. Hunter Hearst Helmsley via a small package. (11:11) First Round Intercontinental Tournament Match Arn Anderson (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Octavius (w/ The Empire) Another outstanding match, as the youth and experience of Octavius clashed with the cagy experience of "Double-A". This was a good, solid match, which ended in a time-limit draw, as neither man was able to put away the other. Octavius and Arn Anderson battled to a time-limit draw. (20:00) (Note: Marc Anthony recieves a bye into the finals of the tournament.) First Round Intercontinental Tournament Match The Widow Maker v. Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) This match was controlled by The Widow Maker early on, but after the Widow Maker hit a powerful power bomb, he seemed to again lose focus. Benoit capitalized on this, and took control of the match, eventually forcing The Widow Maker to submit to the Crippler Crossface. Chris Benoit d. The Widow Maker by submission via The Crippler Crossface. (9:07) First Round Intercontinental Tournament Match The Pessimist v. The Gunslinger This was one of the best matches of the night. Plenty of near falls occured, as The Pessimist and The Gunslinger looked hungry for their first win in the EWA. The Pessimist seemed a bit more focus, however, and used his "Suicidal Dream" to propell him to victory. The Pessimist d. The Gunslinger via The Suicidal Dream. (14:13) Semi-Final World Tag Team Tournament Match The Legion Of Doom v. Harlem Heat (w/ Jacquelyn) This was a total squash, as The LOD looked totally out of their game. Booker T and Stevie Ray were fired up, and used their teamwork, and the Big Apple, to send them into the finals of the tag team tournament. Harlem Heat d. The Road Warriors via Booker T's pin on Animal after the Big Apple. (4:01) Semi-Final World Tag Team Tournament Match The Outsiders (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) Two of the top tag teams in the EWA collided, as The Hart Brothers met up with Scott Hall and Kevin Nash. This was a fairly even match, until Kevin Hall and Scott Nash, the team known as The Insiders, came to ringside. Scott Nash grabbed the foot of Scott Hall, which caused Hall to turn around to argue with him. Matt Hart seized this opportunity, and rolled Hall up for the three count. After the match, Degeneration X gave a thorough beatdown to The Insiders. The Hart Brothers d. The Outsiders via Matt Hart's rollup pin on Scott Hall. (10:31) "Bad Ass" Billy Gunn (w/ Degeneration X) v. Tiberus (w/ The Empire) Billy Gunn looked out of place wrestling in a singles match, and on numerous occasions turned to his corner, looking to tag someone. He kept the match even with typical Degeneration X underhand tatics, but in the end, he was too much for the big man. Tiberus d. Billy Gunn via a power bomb. (8:52) Semi-Final Light Heavyweight Tournament Match SmirtDogg (w/ Lil' Kim & Foxy Brown) v. KG (w/ The Stud Stable) This was another exciting light heavyweight match, as these two gave 100% throughout the match. The end seemed near for SmirtDogg, as KG went for his patented Six Shooter, a modified version of Syxx's Buzzkiller. However, Smirt actually reversed the hold, forcing KG to tap out to his own move. SmirtDogg d. KG by submission via KG's Six Shooter. (17:33) Semi-Final Light Heavyweight Tournament Match Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Harley Hart proved that rookies can succeed in a major federation, as he narrowly defeated the newcomer, Augustus. This was another competitive match, with Harley using many aerial manuevers, while Augustus attempting to keep the match on the ground. The end of Augustus took place when he attempted a leg drop from the top rope. Harley rolled out of the way, and then executed his patented Stud Screw for the victory. Harley Hart d. Augustus via the Stud Screw. (13:54) Semi-Final World Heavyweight Tournament Match "Diamond" Dallas Page v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair Flair and Page made good on their promise to hold nothing back for this match. Flair attempted a vertical suplex, but Page flipped over, and countered it into a Diamond Cutter for the pinfall win. Afterwards, the two men embraced in the ring. Dallas Page d. Ric Flair via the Diamond Cutter. (12:37) Semi-Final World Heavyweight Tournament Match Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) v. Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) This lived up to being the main event, as these two men gave their all. Scott Cummings gained control at the end of the match, and was about to go for the WipeOut, when Shawn Michaels knocked him off of the top rope. The rest of DX then ran in, and began to attack Cummings. The Stud Stable then ran down, and the show ended with the two stables brawling in the ring. Scott Cummings d. Erik Draven via disqualification. (19:42)
