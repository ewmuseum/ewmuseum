EWA Monday Night Madness

30 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 30, 1998 Live, from The Gund Arena, Cleveland, Ohio. New Jack (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Eclipse v. Raven EWA United States Tag Team Championship Return Match Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. FlashBack (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Dos Equis (w/ the NWC) La Parka (w/ the NWC) v. Shawn Hart (w/ The Hart Brothers) EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World TV Champion) Bill Goldberg v. "Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ Triple Threat) Return Match Tommy Dreamer (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Hannibal Manson Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. Van Smack (w/ the NWC) Mankind v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Non-Title Match Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Return Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Thorne Richards (w/ Castor Troy) "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ NWC) v. Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair Double Main Event! EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Deathstar (w/ Precious) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA I-C Champion, w/ Stud Stable) Triple Threat Match For the EWA European/Light Heavyweight Titles (rules below) Vic Deakins (w/ the NWC) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) v. Alex Wright (EWA European Champion, w/ Dance Fever) Rules: First pinfall wins. If Wright is the man who is pinned, he loses the European Title to the man who defeated him. If SmirtDogg is the man defeated, he loses his title to the man who defeated him. For any other questions, email me. New Jack (w/ Thug Warriors) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Neither man showed up. Eclipse v. Raven A very good match...but in the end, Eclipse was able to pull off a small package win from out of nowhere. But he paid the price after the match...-3- Evenflows from Raven. Eclipse d. Raven with a small package. EWA United States Tag Team Championship Return Match Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. FlashBack (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) A competitive match, but Galaxy took a chance, and nailed Disco Inferno with a purse. The official saw it, and called for the bell. It was later revealed that a brick was inside the purse. FlashBack d. Death Sentance by DQ. FlashBack retains the EWA U.S. Tag Team Titles. The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. Dos Equis (w/ the NWC) E.D.M. wins by forfeit. La Parka (w/ the NWC) v. Shawn Hart (w/ The Hart Brothers) Neither man showed up. EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World TV Champion) A very competitive match, until Shawn Michaels became involved. Michaels ran inside the ring, and attempted to Superkick KG. However, KG ducked, and Michaels leveled Austin with the Sweet Chin Music. Steve Austin d. KG by DQ. Steve Austin retains the EWA TV Title. Bill Goldberg v. "Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (w/ Triple Threat) Martin wins by forfeit. Return Match Tommy Dreamer (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Hannibal Manson Dreamer wins by forfeit. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. Van Smack (w/ the NWC) Galaxy wins by forfeit. Mankind v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Mankind dominated this match from the outset. Mankind d. KooKabura with the Mandible Claw. Non-Title Match Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Castor Troy wins by forfeit. Return Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Thorne Richards (w/ Castor Troy) Thorne looked back in old form for this one, as he took The Pessimist apart. Thorne Richards d. The Pessimist by submission with the Loch Ness Lock. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ NWC) v. Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) Prodigy wins by forfeit. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) The Reckoning wins the EWA World Tag Team Titles by forfeit. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair An outstanding match until Marlena nailed the Nature Boy with a low blow. Prodigy and Michaels then proceeded to give Flair a beating. Ric Flair d. Shawn Michaels by DQ. Double Main Event! EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Deathstar (w/ Precious) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA I-C Champion, w/ Stud Stable) Deathstar wins the I-C Title by forfeit; Cummings didn't show up. Triple Threat Match For the EWA European/Light Heavyweight Titles (rules below) Vic Deakins (w/ the NWC) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) v. Alex Wright (EWA European Champion, w/ Dance Fever) Rules: First pinfall wins. If Wright is the man who is pinned, he loses the European Title to the man who defeated him. If SmirtDogg is the man defeated, he loses his title to the man who defeated him. For any other questions, email me. Wright wins the Light Heavyweight Title by forfeit.
