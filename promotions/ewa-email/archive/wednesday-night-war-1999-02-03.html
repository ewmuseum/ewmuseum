EWA Wednesday Night War (2/3/99) February 3, 1999 Live, from the San Jose Arena San Jose, California *The camera shot fades in as another edition of Wednesday Night War is about to commence. The camera pans throughout the sell-out crowd at the San Jose Arena. The majority of the crowd are screaming at a feverish pitch as a huge pyrotechnics display along with booming cannon blasts signify the beginning of Wednesday Night War. Suddenly, "Degenerate Me" by Connecticut Yankee blares over the P.A. system which can mean only one thing. Degeneration X is in da house!* ARE YOU READY?? You think you can tell me what to do You think you can tell me what to wear You think that you're better Well you better get ready Bow to the masters.... BREAK IT DOWN!! *As images of Degeneration X are shown on the EWA Jumbotron -- Haywood Jublome emerges out from behind the curtains. The crowd immediately bursts into cheers. Jublome stops on the middle of the entrance ramp, grins, and then does the famous DX point ala Scott Hall. He's gesturing back towards the curtains. Who could it be? Who's going to come out? Will it be Erik Draven? Shawn Michaels? Or could it very well be Degeneration X's newest addition -- Smirt-D-O-Double G? We don't have to wait long for our answer because within 10-20 seconds -- the EWA World Heavyweight Champion -- SmirtDogg -- arrives on the scene. He's being escorted by the "Hawaiian Princess" Kimona Wanalaya. The EWA World Heavyweight Belt is strapped around his waist. The crowd doesn't know how to react. On one hand -- this guy is supposed to be the most hated wrestler in the sport but conversely he's now a member of the popular and fan favorite stable -- Degeneration X. The EWA fans' response is right down the middle. However, probably more boos. The trio makes their way to the squared circle as the fans ponder Smirt's new-found association with Degeneration X. Once inside the ring, Smirt stands behind Kimona and delivers repeated X-Chops as lime green pyro shoots out from all 4 ringposts in an 'X' pattern. Haywood Jublome points towards a sexy Nubian Sista in the front row and promptly gives her a crotch chop. He gestures for her to flash the crowd and show off what she has. She must have obliged because the crowd goes absolutely nuts with their cheering. Smirt reveals the "Golden Microphone" from out of his pocket and flips it high up in the air. He catches it and then begins to speak. The EWA fans settle down and remain relatively quiet as they are interested in what Smirt has to say.* EWA World Heavyweight Champion SmirtDogg: First of all, you're all probably wondering how I can be with the Corporate Empire one minute and with Degeneration X -- the next. The answer to that is simple. Since you all deserve an explanation -- here it is. I never liked being a Corporate Empire member. Actually, I thought it really sucked. But I did it for one reason and one reason alone. *Smirt taps the EWA World Heavyweight Belt around his waist* Our crooked EWA President -- Duane R. Gates -- gave me an offer that I couldn't refuse. Either I joined up with him and the Corporate Empire or I'd never see an EWA World Heavyweight Title shot as long as I lived. I took him at his word and came to the decision that the only path to the EWA World Heavyweight Title would consist of me associating myself with the Corporate Empire. So there wasn't much left for me to do but accept his offer. Did I hate the bastard at the time and will I always hate him? The answers are yes and yes. Let me tell you -- the wrestlers I had to team up with during my Corporate Empire days were god awful. However, none were worse than Vincent Jackson who cost me the match against the Rock and Kane not so long ago. Once I won the EWA World Heavyweight Championship I had no use for the Corporate Empire anymore so I waited for the opportune time to jump ship. Thus my coronation at the Clash of the Champions as the newest D-Generation X member. Haywood Jublome and I battled in what has to be one of the hardest fought matches in both our careers. We have nothing but mutual respect for one another. D-Generation X is the perfect fit for Smirt-D-O- Double G and vice versa. It may take time for you all to get accustomed to this but believe me I'm a full-fledged DX member and loving it. *The EWA fans give a mixed reaction as Smirt continues* EWA World Heavyweight Champion SmirtDogg: Later on tonight, one of two things can happen. Either the Corporate Empire will be disbanded *The crowd pops* or I will become the new Commissioner of the EWA. *The crowd boos because they want Prodigy to beat Gates* It's a catch 22 for me. I'm not sure who I want to win. While I'd love to see Gates off EWA television forever I'd also love to see Prodigy lose his job as the EWA Commissioner. If by some chance -- you lose Prodigy -- I'm going to enjoy turning the tables on you. My first order of business will be to eliminate that "Prodigy Realm" match from the March Madness '99 Pay Per View and instead institute something entirely different. What will it be? *Smirt arrogantly grins* You'll find out soon enough. Now Prodigy -- if you beat Gates -- which isn't a big deal but if you muster up enough in-ring skills to get the job done -- don't think that you can order me around. Haywood and I won't put up with any of your bullsh*t. So Prodigy you can take that "power trip" of yours and shove it up your ass. Gates -- being the doughnut devouring freak that he is -- probably won't beat you tonight but don't lead yourself into believing that your job is secure. D-Generation X will find another way to remove you from your office position. One in which you won't like but of course that doesn't matter to us. This is how it's going to work. You stay out of D-Generation X's way and we'll think about leaving you alone until the next Pay Per View. But, Prodigy, if you're naive enough to try and subject us to your petty rules and regulations -- then it's on! Bottom line is that you, Prodigy, are a figurehead posing as the EWA Commissioner because when you break it down -- there's nothing you can do to D-Generation X. We'll always out-smart you. Word of advice -- freak -- stay out of D-Generation X's business unless you're prepared to deal with the consequences of your actions. We'll send your ass back to the insane assylum if you're not careful. I bet there's a nice and cozy padded room there which has "Prodigy" written all over it. Don't mess with D-Generation X unless the prospect of another extended stay at the looney bin excites you. When the men in the white coats come to take you back to where you belong -- you'll realize what a fatal mistake you made when you agreed to become the EWA Commissioner. Now with Prodigy out of the way -- it's time to address what Jeff Bunda and that "other guy" did at the conclusion of the Clash of the Champions X -- this past Saturday. Well, the "other guy" didn't do anything but run his mouth all night long. I counted about roughly 4 or 5 interview spots for him during the event. Somebody should shove a sock down his mouth and shut him the hell up. Maybe Mankind will get ahold of him one of these days. *Smirt shrugs* Blah. Blah. Blah. WAA. WAA. WAA. That's all this bum does. Babble on and on while crying and whining over and over again. I'm sick and tired of the process and I'm sure all of you are too. His name will never cross my lips again because he doesn't deserve the attention. This is the last I'll ever make mention of that loser. How many times has he said that our match at Mind Games 2 was a screw job? How many times has he stated that he was robbed of the EWA World Heavyweight Championship? Countless. Well, I'm putting that all to rest now. Feast your eyes on the EWA Jumbotron. We're going to relive the night where I beat this b*tch and became the 2-Time EWA World Heavyweight Champion. *The EWA Jumbotron comes to life and on the screen we see our EWA World Heavyweight Champion -- SmirtDogg -- ascending the "10 Steps of Excellence".
