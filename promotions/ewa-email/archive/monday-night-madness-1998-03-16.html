EWA Monday Night Madness

16 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 16, 1998 Live, from Madison Square Garden, in New York City, New York. Live from Madison Square Garden, in New York City, New York Mankind v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) Triangle Match -- Winner will recieve EWA U.S. Tag Team Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) The Widow Maker v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Non-Title Match: Champion v. Champion KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Disco Inferno (1/2 of EWA U.S. Tag Champs, w/ Dance Fever) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Champion, w/ Degeneration X) "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Thorne Richards EWA Intercontinental Championship Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Hannibal Manson (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Dark Powers) EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match -- No Holds Barred Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Mankind v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) This matchup was dominated by Mankind, as LaParka once again was -way- off his game. Mankind d. LaParka w/ a flying elbowdrop. (4:14) Triangle Match -- Winner will recieve EWA U.S. Tag Team Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) Strangely enough, none of the three teams showed up for the match. Comissioner Slaughter is expected to announce the team who get the title shot later today. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) Again, more Circus! members off their game, as Pain and Destruction took Dos Equis apart. Death Sentance d. Dos Equis with Pain's pin on Tecate Jake after a neckbreaker. (5:02) After this match, Sgt. Slaughter announced that Death Sentance would get the title shot against FlashBack at WrestleFest. The Widow Maker v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Neither man showed up. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) This was a back and forth match until KG became involved. KG jumped in the ring, and a three-way brawl ensued. The referee ruled the match a double disqualification. Non-Title Match: Champion v. Champion KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) This was a solid match, until you factor in revenge, coming in the form of The Clamp. After Eclipse had clotheslined KG over the top rope, Deathstar ran to the ring, and executed The Clamp on him, causing him to be counted out. Eclipse d. KG by countout. (11:33) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Disco Inferno (1/2 of EWA U.S. Tag Champs, w/ Dance Fever) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Another match marred by interference, although this was somewhat of a surprise. The Golden Child was in control, and was on the outside of the ring looking for a foreign object, when Thorne Richards blazed a path through the crowd, jumped over the guardrail, and attacked TGC. DX quickly came to his rescue, and Thorne just as quickly escaped through the crowd. The Golden Child d. Disco Inferno by DQ. (9:08) The Golden Child retains the U.S. Title. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Thorne Richards This was another solid match, and it ended in a time-limit draw, with the two men shaking hands afterward. Ric Flair and Thorne Richards battled to a time-limit draw. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Hannibal Manson (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Dark Powers) Neither man showed up. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match -- No Holds Barred Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Mike Malone: And now, we're ready for the biggest main event in the history of Monday Night Madness. Fasten your seatbelts, because it's going to be *lights go out* a wild ride! *Organ music blares over the PA, and an eerie red color falls over Madison Square Garden. Suddenly, flames shoot up from the entrance ramp, and Kane walks out from behind the curtain, led by Paul Bearer.* EWA Ring Announcer Howard Finkle: The following contest, scheduled for one fall with TV time remaining, is a no-holds-barred match, for the EWA Championship! Introducing the challenger! Accompanied to the ring by Paul Bearer, from Death Valley, weighing 320 pounds, KANE!! Larry Zybysko: This guy is huge! Mike Malone: Indeed, but so is the EWA World Champion. If you're looking for a scientific wrestling match, change the channel. This one is gonna be an all-out brawl. *Surprisingly, we do not hear the theme music of Degeneration X. Instead, the opening notes of "The Unforgiven" by Metallica blare over the PA, and Erik Draven comes out from behind the curtain, stalking to the ring.* Howard Finkle: And his opponent! Representing Degeneration X, from Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing 335 pounds, the EWA Champion, Erik DRAVEN!!! Mike Malone: That's Draven's old music! And he's not wasting any time getting to the ring! *Draven goes straight for Kane, and the bell sounds.* Mike Malone: Draven with a hard right to Kane. Another hard right! Now a left! Draven backs against the ropes, and gives Kane a huge lariat! Kane's wobbling now! Larry Zybysko: Draven's still got the belt on! Mike Malone: Indeed he does, Larry, but now Draven takes the belt off...and nails Kane right in the head with it! Kane's still wobbling, as Draven backs into the ropes, and goes for a lariat.....but Kane grabs Draven by the throat! Larry Zybysko: The champ's in trouble! Mike Malone: Big chokeslam on Draven!! And now Ka....wait a minute, there's Shawn Michaels! Shawn Michaels is coming through the crowd with a chair! He's gonna swing it at Kane....but Kane turned around, grabbed the chair from Michaels...and Michaels' cranium just got CRACKED! Now Kane, setting Michaels up for a Tombstone...and he nails it! Larry Zybysko: Kane's got his back turned on Draven, and that's a bad idea! Mike Malone: Indeed it is, as Draven has the World Title, and he's wacking Kane right in the back of the head with it. Draven picks Kane up for a scoop slam, and slams Kane down. Now Draven's going to the outside...and Kane just sat up! Larry Zybysko: That's amazing! Mike Malone: Shades of Kane's older brother....and Draven has another chair...he just nailed Kane right in the head, and Kane crumbles back to the mat! *15 minutes later* Larry Zybysko: What a match this has been! Mike Malone: Indeed it has, Larry, and Draven is in control now. He just drew his thumb across his throat! He's going for the DX Dr..no, he's going for a Tombstone! He's going to use Kane's own move on him! Larry Zybysko: He's got him up!! Mike Malone: Kane just flipped over, and now he has Draven up for the Tombstone....down with the Tombstone! 1, 2, 3!! We have a new EWA WOrld Heavyweight Champion!! *But instead of hearing Kane's music, we hear the sound of glass shattering, as "Stone Cold" Steve Austin comes from behind the curtain to a huge ovation.* Mike Malone: Kane's the new champ, he'll defend against Austin at WrestleFest '98, and we've gotta go!! *The camera fades out just as Austin picks the microphone up.*
