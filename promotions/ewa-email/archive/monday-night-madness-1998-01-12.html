EWA Monday Night Madness

12 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 12, 1998 Live, from The Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon. (The lights of the Rose Garden dim considerably and are replaced by golden laser beams that are shooting all over the arena. The instrumental to L.L. Cool J's song called "Phenomenon" is blaring from the P.A. system. Every 5 seconds or so we hear the words "Something like a Phenomenon". These are the only words which are part of 'The Golden Child's' Theme Music. The crowd is going absolutely crazy waiting in great anticipation for 'The Golden Child' to make his appearance. Suddenly, from out of the entrance way walks out 'The Golden Child' and as he makes his way down the aisle he looks like a man on a mission. I wonder what's on his mind? 'The Golden Child' leaps over the top rope and into the squared circle. He stands in the middle of the ring and throws his arms up in the air. As soon as he does this fireworks explode from out of the 4 ring posts and thunderous sounds erupt. Finally, after a good 15-20 seconds the lights of the arena are back to normal. 'The Golden Child' has a microphone in his hand and begins to speak as the crowd chants TGC TGC TGC!) 'The Golden Child:' It's great to be a part of the best thing going in the e-federation business today. The EWA has only been in operation for a little more than a week and already has an impressive fan base... as you all demonstrate by being here today. (camera pans to show the sold-out Rose Garden) That can be chalked up to two things. #1: Great promoting/advertising and #2: The Ghetto Warriors. Without the Ghetto Warriors the EWA wouldn't be able to compete with the GWF's (crowd boos) or the AEWF's (crowd boos louder). Without the Ghetto Warriors...the EWA wouldn't survive. Tonight, the Ghetto Warriors make their first huge step towards EWA supremacy when SmirtDogg captures the Light Heavyweight Title and Harlem Heat takes home World Tag Team Gold. (crowd cheers and chants Ghetto Warriors) With that being said everyone wants to know what I'm after. Well it's as simple as this. All I want is to have the EWA World Heavyweight Title around my waist and that will happen, believe me. (crowd chants TGC! TGC! TGC!) But first there's one ICON wannabe that needs to be dealt with and that's Erik Draven. You think you're god's greatest gift to wrestling and that's simply not true. I have no respect for you. In fact, if you have the courage to get within 5 feet of me I'll show you exactly what I think of you when I spit in that ugly mug of yours, biotch. E-federation Presidents such as Trevor Holiness and our very own Duane Gates sweat ya too damn much. Saying Draven this and Draven that. Catering to all of your needs. It's as if they work for you. Pathetic if ya ask me. Erik Draven, I dare ya to accept this challenge to get into the ring and compete against me...the Phenomenon of the EWA! What do you have to lose? (he pauses and then says) Everything! Draven, I'm gonna rip you apart and expose you for the piece of trash that you are. I will not only beat you but I will humiliate you also. I'm gonna take away that hold you have over the E-federation Presidents and I'm gonna crush you and your ego into a million pieces. Erik Draven this is the end of the line for you. 'The Golden Child' will be held down no longer. It's my time to shine and that's what I'm gonna do after I obliterate you...Erik Draven and then capture the EWA World Heavyweight Title from either Diamond Dallas Page or Scott Cummings. Whichever one of those chumps is holding my belt then. (he gives an arrogant smile) EWA, Beware of the Phenomenon. (The lights of the Rose Garden turn off completely and a Golden Mist forms over and around the ring. 20 seconds later when the lights switch back on ....'The Golden Child' has vanished.) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match SmirtDogg (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) This was one of the all-time classic matches in wrestling history, as both men had very many near-falls. The young rookie Hart made his crucial mistake when he played to the crowd too much before attepting the Stud Screw. The cagy veteran SmirtDogg moved out of the way, and applied his patented Thug Breaker. Harley Hart withstood the pain for about three minutes, but finally tapped out, giving SmirtDogg the title of the first-ever EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. SmirtDogg d. Harley Hart by submission via the Thug Breaker. (28:16) SmirtDogg is the first ever EWA Light Heavyweight Champion! Ken Shamrock v. The Violator This was a total squash, as Shamrock overwhelmed The Violator. Shamrock's submission-style was too much for The Violator, as Shamrock proved that he wasn't "invincible". Ken Shamrock d. The Violator by submission via the Ankle Submission. (4:00) "Mean" Gene Okerlund: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time, I have the honor of introducing to you the new EWA Comissioner, Comissioner Sgt. Slaughter! (Slaughter's music plays, as he comes to the ring wearing a suit. He has a piece of paper rolled up in his hand.) Gene Okerlund: Comissioner Slaughter, I've been told that you have a couple of announcements tonight. Let's hear them. Comissioner Slaughter: First of all, I'd like to announce that I have signed two matches for Ground Zero, and my first duty as Comissioner of the EWA will be to announce these matches. First off, I have in my hand a signed contract for Ground Zero. This match has been avoided in many federations, and these two men hate each other dearly. But in the EWA, we avoid nothing. Therefore, on December 14, 1998, there will be a fifteen foot high steel cage constructed, (The crowd gasps.) and inside that cage, will be the self-proclaimed "Showstopper" Shawn Michaels! (A mixed reaction from the crowd.) And he will be facing the man that has wanted to get his hands on Michaels for a long, long time, Shawn Hart! (The crowd cheers loudly.) Also, I have an announcement regarding the EWA World Heavyweight Title match at Ground Zero, on February 14. Whether Scott Cummings, or Diamond Dallas Page is the champion after tonight, they will be defending their title against Erik Draven! But, if Cummings should pull out the victory, then "Diamond" Dallas Page will get his shot at the man who has challenged him repeatedly, Vinnie Vegas! (The crowd roars its approval for the new Comissioner.) AND THAT'S AN ORDER! Gene Okerlund: Sgt. Slaughter wasting no time here tonight! Death Sentance (w/ Precious) v. The Legion Of Doom This was an all-out war, as a lot of bad blood had boiled over between these two teams. In the end, it was Precious who made the difference, nailing Hawk with a chair while Pain distracted the offical. Pain then covered Hawk for the three count. Death Sentance d. The LOD via Pain's pin on Hawk after a chair shot by Precious. (9:31) Later in the card, Sgt. Slaughter announced that a rematch had been signed between the two teams for Ground Zero. The New Age Outlaws (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Insiders The New Age Outlaws were on the top of their game tonight, as they walked all over the Insiders. Kevin Nash and Scott Hall were seen at the entrance ramp, laughing. The New Age Outlaws d. The Insiders via B.A. Billy Gunn's pin on Kevin Hall after a bulldog from the top rope. (5:05) The Duke Of Bourbon v. Faarooq (w/ The Nation of Domination) In a bizzare turn of events, The Duke didn't even show for his match. Faarooq d. The Duke Of Bourbon via forfeit. Six Man Tag Team Match Degeneration X (Shawn Michaels/Scott Hall/Kevin Nash) v. The Nation Of Domination (Rocky Maivia/Kama Mustafa/D-Lo Brown) This was a fairly competitive match, but once again, the underhanded tatics of DX prevailed. While the referee was distracted by a Kama/K.Nash brawl on the outside, Michaels snuck into the ring and hit the Sweet Chin Music on D-Lo Brown, allowing Hall (the legal man) to cover him for the pinfall. Degeneration X d. The NOD via Scott Hall's pin on D-Lo Brown after the Sweet Chin Music from Shawn Michaels. (11:32) Semi-Final Intercontinental Tournament Match The Pessimist v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) This was an exciting match, as both men showed the desire and skills that brought them to the dance. The match seemed to be Benoit's for the taking, but The Pessimist rolled over when Benoit attempted the Swan Dive, and then pinned "The Crippler" with his feet on the ropes. The Pessimist d. Chris Benoit via pinfall, with his feet on the ropes. (10:57) (The camera pans to the ring, where "Mean" Gene Okerlund is standing.) "Mean" Gene Okerlund: Ladies and gentlemen, this is a big night for professional wrestling, and it's about to get even bigger as I introduce my guest at this time. Ladies and gentlemen, join me in welcoming the newest member of the EWA, the legendary Bret The Hitman Hart! (Bret Hart's WCW theme music blares over the PA, as the crowd erupts in cheers. Hart comes from behind the curtain, in street clothes and his "Hitman" jacket. He makes his way to the ring, and the music stops.) Gene Okerlund: Bret Hart, why are you here in the EWA? Bret "Hitman" Hart: Well, I've decided that I need to test new waters. I need to prove that I am still the best to all these young wrestlers. So I thought to myself, what better place to do just that, than the EWA? A young federation on the rise. And Gene, I intend to be the leader of this federation. Gene Okerlund: Other than the young wrestlers, a lot of faces from your past are here in the EWA, such as Ric Flair, Shawn Michaels, Faarooq, Ken Shamrock, and others. What are your thoughts on these men? Bret "Hitman" Hart: Well Gene, I look forward to once again having great matches, with the likes of Ken Shamrock, of Ric Flair, of Faarooq. As for Shawn Michaels, the "Boy Toy", Shawn Michaels, I don't want to see you, or Erik Draven, or any of your degenerate friends get into my business. I've been screwed by you before, and I am not going to let it happen in the EWA. If I have to do it from the bottom up, I will prove why I am The Excellence of Execution. I will prove that I am the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be. (Bret's music plays again, as he plays the crowd.) Gene Okerlund: Welcome to the EWA, Bret Hart! A big night in wrestling, as we've already introduced Comissioner Slaughter and Bret Hart, and the biggest is yet to come. Mike Malone, Larry Zybysko, back to you! EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Harlem Heat (w/ Jacquelyn) One of the classic tag team matches of all time saw a disasterous ending, as Degeneration X played a part in the outcome. As Shawn Hart was about to go for the Lightning Strike, Shawn Michaels ran to ringside and nailed Shawn Hart with the Sweet Chin Music. The referee, who had been knocked out, came to and made the three count as Booker T covered him. The Stud Stable then proceded the beat the living hell out of Michaels, until the rest of DX came to ringside. An all-out brawl ensued, and we saw Erik Draven fall to an apparent knee injury. Harlem Heat d. The Hart Brothers via Booker T's pin on Shawn Hart after interference from Shawn Michaels. (21:39) Harlem Heat are the first ever EWA World Tag Team Champions! "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) v. Prodigy Prodigy's debut match was a successful one, as he fought tooth and nail with Triple-H. Triple-H appeared to have The Pedigree applied, but Prodigy back-body-dropped him, set him on the top rope, and delivered a top-rope DDT, a move he calls The Firestarter. Prodigy d. Hunter Hearst Helmsley via The Firestarter. (14:22) Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Raven Raven once again pulled his disappearing act, as Nero got the forfeit win. Afterward, the crowd was notified that if we do not hear from Raven by Thursday, his EWA contract will be terminated. Nero d. Raven via forfeit. Four Corners Match Vinnie Vegas v. Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) v. Julius (w/ The Empire) v. Saturn (w/ Raven) Before the match, Triple-H came to the ring, and announced that Erik Draven had suffered a slight knee injury, and that he would not be competing tonight. Therefore, the match was converted into a Triangle Match. Vinnie Vegas and Julius quickly teamed up to eliminate The Widow Maker, which they had no trouble doing. Julius and Vegas then went head-to-head, until Erik Draven limped to the ring, walking on crutches. He climbed in the ring, and said something to Julius. Julius nodded to Draven, and the two advanced toward Vegas. Draven then turned and hit Julius with a crutch, and also hit Vegas with one. He walked out on his own, leaving the two men laying in the ring. Vegas rolled over and put an arm on Julius, getting the win. Vinnie Vegas d. The Widow Maker and Julius to win the Triangle Match. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ The IV Horsemen) This was an extremely competitive match, as DDP and Cummings gave 110% in this contest. It was an evenly fought matchup, with Page having the early advantage, and Cummings taking the advantage late in the match. Cummings tossed Page into the ropes, and hit a swinging neckbreaker. He then quickly climbed to the top rope, and hit the Wipeout for the pinfall victory! Cummings helped Page up and shook his hand after the match, and as Scott went through the crowd to celebrate, Vinnie Vegas sprinted to the ring. Page and Vegas got into a huge brawl, and were still brawling as the cameras faded to black. Scott Cummings d. "Diamond" Dallas Page via the WipeOut. (29:02) Scott Cummings is the first ever EWA World Heavyweight Champion!
