EWA MIND GAMES 2 (As the pay-per-view fades in, we see that we are immediately plunging into the best wrestling action in the world. {well, it's definitely not that promotion based in Atlanta, with all the old EWA stars} We hear the opening of "Know Your Enemy" by Rage Against The Machine over the PA.) HF: Ladies and gentlemen! Our opening contest at EWA Mind Games 2, set for one fall, is for the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship! Approaching the ring at this time is the challenger! From Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing 224 pounds, "Triple-Toke", Aaron Martin! (As the opening of "Natural One" by Implosion blares, the fans begin to cheer for the Light Heavyweight Champion.) HF: His opponent, from New York City, weighing 224 pounds, the EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, The Natural One, Michael Modest! This was Light Heavyweight action at its best. Martin was looking to become the first ever EWA wrestler to win the same title five times, while Modest was looking to continue his impressive winning streak as of late. He started out controlling the match, by quickly turning the contest into a slow-paced match. Modest used a variety of submission moves on the legs of Martin to attempt to slow the man down. Modest then picked Martin up, and whipped him into the ropes...however, Martin nailed a swinging neckbreaker. He then came back, looking incredibly pumped, and blasted Modest with a side suplex. Martin went for the cover, but only got two. As he picked Martin up, Modest gave the old 'thumb to the eye'. Modest then picked up Martin, apparently going for a running powerslam. However, Martin slipped out of it, and nailed a reverse DDT on Modest. Martin quickly climbed to the top rope, and hit a moonsault, getting only a two count. Modest came right back, however, with a variation of Spike Dudley's Acid Drop (a Tornado Diamond Cutter) Again, only a two count. Modest then picked Martin up, apparently going for a Tombstone. However, Martin reversed it, and executed a variation of his Shoulderbreaker. It was enough, as Aaron Martin was able to get both the pin, and, for the fifth time, the EWA Light Heavyweight Title. Aaron Martin d. Michael Modest with a variation of the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion! Representing The Triple Threat, from Cleveland, Ohio, weighing 234 pounds, "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin! (As the opening chords of "Fall Of The Peacemaker" by Molly Hatchet blares over the PA system, the crowd response grows to even louder heights.) HF: His opponent, to be accompanied down the aisle by Lady Jane, from Battle Creek, Michigan, weighing 263 pounds, the EWA Intercontinental Champion, Ruger Ice! With this match, the pay-per-view returned to an outstanding level, as both these men gave one hell of an effort. The match started in the ring, with Conklin controlling the opening of the contest. Conklin kept the match fast-paced, as he relentlessly worked over on the knee of Ice. Ruger, however, had some fight left in him, and when Conklin set him up on the top rope, Ruger siezed the opportunity with an incredible move. Conklin stood on the top rope, and Ruger found the strength to push Conklin off, and through the Iraqi Broadcast table on the outside. A seemingly unconscious Conklin lay motionless on the outside, and after not moving for a few moments, Ruger walked over to make sure the man was okay. However, Conklin was playing possum, as he threw a handful of salt in Ruger's eyes. Blinded, Ruger could do nothing as Conklin belted him with lefts and rights. Swinging at dead air, Ruger's hands were able to find a fire extinguisher, though, and he cracked Conklin in the head with it, spliting his forehead open and leaving a cut that would later require 14 stitches to close. Conklin crumpled to the mat, and Ruger fell on him, but only got a two count. Ruger crawled back into the ring, and dragged Conklin with him. Ruger called for the Spinebuster, and threw Conklin into the ropes. However, Conklin stopped inches from Ruger, and blasted Ice with the Overhand Right. Conklin then slowly climbed to the top rope, and went for the Countout. Ruger looked like he might get up, but it was not the case, and Conklin hit the Countout. He went for the cover, got the pin, and became the new EWA Intercontinental Champion. Terry Conklin d. Ruger Ice with the Countout. Terry Conklin is the new EWA Intercontinental Champion! (As "Whatever" by Godsmack blares over the PA system, the crowd cheers somewhat, apparently reacting nicely to the hometown return of Kanyon.) First, from Charlotte, North Carolina, "Mr. Every Night", Justin Hicks! I've been your faithful card summarizer for over a year now, and without a doubt, this was the greatest EWA tag team match I have ever witnessed. Jeff Bunda started off the match against the hometown hero, Kanyon. Bunda took Kanyon down with a side headlock, which was quickly countered by Kanyon into a side headlock of his own. Bunda fought to his feet, and sent Kanyon into the ropes. Kanyon came back with a clothesline attempt, but Bunda ducked it and hit Kanyon with a full nelson slam. Kanyon quickly got to his feet, and was sent back down with a clothesline from Bunda. Bunda tagged in Hicks, and Hicks went to work with a juiji hold (Craig Pittman's old Code Red move). Kanyon was able to fight his way with his other arm, and grab the rope. Hicks picked Kanyon up, and whipped him into the ropes, but Kanyon came back, and nailed the Flatliner on Hicks. Kanyon went for a cover, but Bunda ran in, and broke up the count. Kanyon then tagged in Raven, and Raven went to work, stomping away on Hicks. Raven focused on the neck area of Hicks, using a legscissor submission hold on Hicks, and then began executing a number of vicious legdrops. At one point, Raven sat Hicks' head under the bottom rope, and climbed to the top rope. He attempted a legdrop, but Hicks was able to pull out of the way. Hicks knocked Kanyon to the outside, and then tagged in Bunda. Bunda went to the outside, and threw Raven into the guardrail. All hell broke loose at this point, as Hicks and Kanyon began duking it out on the other side. Bunda went to clothesline Raven, but Raven ducked it, and threw Bunda into the guardrail. Raven went searching for some plunder, and came up with a steel chair. Raven swung the chair at Bunda, but Bunda grabbed it. The two men struggled to gain control of the chair until Bunda kicked Raven in the stomach, wrenched it away from him, and nailed him with it. Raven crumpled to the mat, and Bunda went looking for plunder of his own. Meanwhile, Kanyon and Hicks were actually fighting in the front row of the crowd. Bunda found a table under the ring, and sat it up right outside the ring. Raven climbed to his feet, and onto the table. Bunda followed him, and the two were standing on the table. Bunda set Raven up for the Showrunner, but Raven kicked him in the shin, again in the groin, and nailed the Evenflow onto the table. Raven went for the cover, but the referee was over tending to Kanyon and Raven, so he was unable to count. Meanwhile, Kanyon had just clotheslined Hicks over the guardrail and back to ringside. Hicks was quick to recover, and reached over, dropping Kanyon's neck onto the guardrail. Kanyon fell back, which gave Hicks an opportunity to take a breather. Kanyon was seen reaching into his tights. He slowly stumbled to his feet, and Hicks grabbed him, attempting to suplex him over to ringside. However, Kanyon flipped out of it, and kicked Hicks in the stomach. He then revealed a set of handcuffs in his hand, and slapped a cuff onto Hicks. He threw Hicks into the steel ringpost, and then slapped the other end of them to the steel post. With that done, Kanyon went over to the other side, where Bunda was stomping away on a now-bloodied Raven. Kanyon jerked Bunda around, and Bunda immediately went for a roundhouse. Kanyon ducked it, and nailed the Flatliner on Bunda. Kanyon then threw Raven on top of Bunda, and yelled at the referee to count. The referee obliged, making the 1, 2, 3 count, and Raven and Kanyon were crowned as the new EWA World Tag Team Champions. Raven and Kanyon d.; The Millenium with Raven's pin on Jeff Bunda after Kanyon's Flatliner. Raven and Kanyon are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions! Michaels hit the ropes, jumped over KG, came back, KG did a leapfrog, Michaels did a baseball slide on the way back, came up, and kicked KG in the stomach. Michaels threw KG into the turnbuckle, and climbed to the top rope, punching KG ten times before climbing down. KG caught Michaels with a hard boot to the groin, and tossed Michaels over the top rope. KG hit the ropes, and leaped over the top rope, executing a brilliant guillotine legdrop. KG recovered from the fall, picked Michaels up, and sent him into the guardrail. He ran at HBK, but Michaels ducked, and lifted him into the crowd. Michaels took the opportunity to show off for the crowd, doing his patented split-stretch-flex. KG sprung to the guardrail, and leapt off, dropkicking Michaels right in the back of the head. Michaels collapsed to the floor, appearing to be unconscious. KG picked Michaels up, and threw him back in the ring. He climbed to the top, and when HBK got up, leapt off, executing a Frankensteiner. KG went for the pin, but only got two. He then tied Michaels up in the ring ropes, and went out for a chair. He came back in, and swung the chair at Michaels, but at the last second, Michaels ducked, and the chair hit the ring ropes and bounced back up into KG's face. Michaels grabbed him, sent him into the ropes, and ran as well. KG went for a clothesline, but Michaels ducked, and came back with a flying crossbody. Michaels sprung to his feet, and went to the top rope. He got to the top, did a little "X-Chop", and nailed a flying elbowdrop. Michaels sprung back up again, and tapped his boot, as 19,000 fans rose to their feet. Michaels began stamping the ground, calling for Sweet Chin Music, as KG struggled to his feet. Michaels went for the Superkick, but KG ducked it, and went for a lariat. Michaels ducked that, and then nailed KG with the SCM. Michaels fell down on KG, and made the cover for the three count, finally defeating KG in a one-on-one bout. Shawn Michaels d. KG with the Sweet Chin Music. The fans erupted, and TGO headed for the outside, retrieving the ladder. TGO brought it back into the ring, but when he sat it up, SmirtDogg dropkicked the ladder into TGO's face. Smirt picked TGO up, and bodyslammed him onto the ladder, following that up with a standing moonsault. He then sat the ladder up, and began to climb. Smirt was two rungs from the top, when TGO tipped the ladder over, causing Smirt to be crotched onto the ropes. Smirt fell over in the ring, and TGO slowly set the ladder up. He also started climbing, but Smirt was quick to recover, and began climbing the other side. The two met close to the top, and began trading blows. TGO landed the knockoff blow, but on the way down, SmirtDogg hit the ladder, tipping TGO over and to the mat. Both men slowly got to their feet, and Smirt walked toward TGO. TGO was standing on the laid-down ladder, and immediately took the opening, setting Smirt up for the Gold Medal. However, Smirt caught TGO with a knee to the stomach, and blasted him with the Gangsta's Paradise onto the ladder. Smirt slowly set the ladder up again, and began climbing. TGO climbed to his feet, and grabbed the ladder, just as Smirt closed his hands around the title belt. TGO shoved the ladder, tipping it over, but Smirt maintained his grip, and was knocked off with the EWA Championship belt in hand. In an amazing match, SmirtDogg becomes the new EWA World Champion. SmirtDogg d. The Golden One. SmirtDogg is the new EWA World Heavyweight Champion!!
