EWA Wednesday Night War

3 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 3, 1998 Live, from Tingley Coliseum, Albuquerque, New Mexico. Live, from Tingley Coliseum Albuquerque, New Mexico The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards) EWA European Championship Match Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ The Triple Threat) The Widow Maker (w/ Razor) v. Swap Meet Louie (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) Chris Craft v. Zuliel Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) v. Lee Tenn Non-Title Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) The New Age Outlaws v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Ghetto Boy (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Goldberg The Gunslinger v. Ruger Ice The Black Angels v. The Outsiders EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Furnace "The Total Package" Lex Luger & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) v. The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) Grudge Match Serpent Man v. KG (w/ Sunny) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Return Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards) (It's time for the most watched part of ANY EWA broadcast. It's time for the Swing Pad.) Stevie: O.K. people are you ready for the Swing Pad? (crowd pops) Now this is a VERY special episode of the Swing Pad. Now the Swing Pad is usually on Saturday nights but not this week. No, this week, I'm bringing my show to you on Wednesday Night War!!!! Now I have a VERY special guest for tonight's Swing Pad. As you all know by now, I have a match tonight to determine the new EWA European Champion. Well, it just so happens that my guest for tonight is my opponent. Ladies and germs, please welcome Rufus Lamar Jackson III.!!!!!!!! (Rufus comes out to a huge pop from the crowd. He is walking alone.) Stevie: Hey there Rufus. What's up? Welcome to the Swing Pad. Rufus: Well Mista Stevie, it be my pleasure. I be liking your show. Stevie: Thank you Rufus. I have to give you credit. Your radio show is pretty good. But enough of the butt kissing. You do realize that you and I are going to tangle later on for the belt don't you? Rufus: Yes I be knowing that. And I be telling you something Mista Richards. You not be standing a chance. I is gonna win that belt because I be wanting to win it. So you is gonna be losing. Stevie: Well Rufus, I hate to break it to you but I'm going to kick your fat ass tonight. Rufus: Who you be calling fat, you white jive turkey? Stevie: I'm calling you fat. Rufus: You be talking too much. It be time to go now. Stevie: Wait!!! Wait!!! Just a second. I have an offer to make you. Rufus: What kind of offer will that be? Stevie: Well I know you have a thing for chicken... Rufus: You be talking about fried chicken? Stevie: Yes sir I am. BOYS!!!! GET OUT HERE!!!! (The Crippled Midgets of Doom roll down to the ring. There are about 80 of them total. They are each carrying two buckets of KFC on the backs of their wheelchairs.) Stevie: You like that Rufus? Rufus: (drooling) Oh my...Mista Stevie...that be a lot of chicken. You be knowing I can't be resisting no chicken. I gots to have that chicken!!!! Stevie: Easy there Rufus...that chicken can all be yours. Rufus: All 160 buckets? Stevie: Yes they can. But there's a catch... Rufus: What be that? Stevie: You have to catch them. GO BOYS!!! GO GO GO!!!! Rufus: That not be fair...you know Rufus not be running fast!!! (The Crippled Midgets start to roll off. Rufus gives chase.) Rufus: I BE WANTING THAT CHICKEN!!!!!! Stevie: Well this is going to be one easy match. (The Swing Pad fades to black as we go to a commercial.) The Widow Maker (w/ Razor) v. Swap Meet Louie (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) Our first match of the evening...saw nobody show up. :-( Chris Craft v. Zuliel The actual first match of the card saw the debut of two new light heavyweights who looked very rusty in their debut. Both men fought for about eight minutes, and ended up both being counted out. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Erica) v. Lee Tenn The Light Heavyweight division seems to be somewhat picking up, and these two men showed why. This was a terrific ten minute match in which Haynes showed his high-flying techniques and skills off very well. But in the end, Tenn was able to keep the match on the ground, taking away The Thrill's vertical base and hitting the Hang Tenn. Lee Tenn d. Will Haynes with the Hang Tenn. Non-Title Match Shawn Hart (w/ Anna) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Phil) The wherabouts of the Stud Stable remained a mystery, as Shawn Hart didn't show for his match. McCammon's flight into New Mexico was severly disrupted by bad weather, and he also couldn't make it, so President Gates announced a return match for SNS. The New Age Outlaws v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Sure enough; neither team showed, and both were fired. During the break time, some of The Crippled Midgets of Doom returned, and shot "Crippled Midgets Of Doom" T-shirts into the crowd. KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. Ghetto Boy (1/2 of EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) Another no-show? Give me a break. "The Rocket" Joe Satre v. Goldberg Finally, a match...but it didn't look like one. Goldberg looked nothing like he does down south, and The Rocket crushed him. Joe Satre d. Goldberg with a boston crab. EWA European Championship Match Rufus Lamar Jackson III (w/ Arnold Drummond) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ The Triple Threat) Once Stevie made it to the ring, Jackson's music played, but he never came out. The big-screen monitor above the entrance ramp came on, and showed Rufus still chasing the Midgets and the chicken....in the desert. As a result, Jackson was forced to forfeit the match. Stevie Richards d. Rufus Lamar Jackson III by forfeit. Stevie Richards is the new EWA European Champion!! The Gunslinger v. Ruger Ice The Gunslinger finally showed for a match, and started off by dominating Ruger, working him over with various power-based moves. However, Ruger reached down deep inside and fought back, turning this into a terrific match. The Gunslinger had whipped Ruger into the ropes, and bounced into the ropes for an attempted lariat, but Ruger picked him up, and nailed the Spinebuster. Afterward, though, Konnan ran in and gave Ruger a low blow. Ruger Ice d. The Gunslinger with the Spinebuster. The Black Angels v. The Outsiders Absolutely no contest. The "real" Outsiders, making their return to the EWA under the WolfPack banner, demolished The Black Angels in less than four minutes. The Outsiders d. The Black Angels with Kevin Nash's pin on Satchiel after the JackKnife Powerbomb. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The WolfPack) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was possibly the best match of the night. These two men showed why the EWA is the best thing going today, as we saw nearly twenty minutes of non-stop, fast-paced wrestling action. Both men executed their share of high-risk manuevers, but Michaels made the first mistake, missing on an attempted moonsault. Martin picked up Michaels for the Shoulderbreaker, but Michaels flipped out of it, and when Martin turned around, Michaels nailed him with the Sweet Chin Music. Konnan was at ringside, and held HBK's hand for leverage while he pinned him. After Michaels and Konnan had left, Martin slowly woke up, furious over how the match had ended, and demanded HBK to give him a rematch at Summer Breakout '98. Michaels came back out, and gladly accepted. Shawn Michaels d. Aaron Martin with the Sweet Chin Music. Shawn Michaels is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Furnace Strangely enough, Flair was nowhere to be found, and Furance wasn't either. "The Total Package" Lex Luger & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The WolfPack) v. The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) One of the best tag team matches in recent history, with an ugly ending. Luger started off against Bunda, and looked solid in his EWA debut. However, Bunda was at the top of his game, and he and Hicks made quick, solid tags, isolating Luger in their corner. After about ten minutes of this, though, Luger began to power back, and got over to make the tag to Draven. From there, it was pure warfare, as all four teams hit the ring. Aaron Martin, Stevie Richards, Scott Hall, and Kevin Nash all made it to the ring, and the match was ruled a double disqualification. Security took close to ten minutes before the two factions were finally separated. The WolfPack and The Extreme Dream Machine battled to a double disqualification. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Return Match "Stunning" Scott Cummings v. The Wyldsyde (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Some match this turned out to be. Everyone watching expected this to be a repeat of Wednesday night; a classic wrestling match. What they got instead was a no-show from Cummings. The Wyldsyde disgustingly took the forfeit win. The Wyldsyde d. Scott Cummings by forfeit. The Wyldsyde remains the EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion. Grudge Match Serpent Man v. KG (w/ Sunny) This match was moved to the main event because of the intense heat behind it. Serpent Man came to the ring first, to the sound of snakes hissing over the PA. KG then ran to the ring, and the fight was on. Serpent Man was surprisingly...well...good. He wrestled KG extremely well, and the match went back and forth. We'll pick up the play-by-play during the end of the match. Jim Ross: And now, KG's going to the top! It looks like... Larry Zybysko: He's going for the Air Smirty! If he hits this, it's all over!! Jim Ross: But Serpent Man moved!! Serpent Man was playing possum! And now, he's going for the Snake Bite, a modified Figure Four!! He's got it locked on.....KG's fighting it....KG TAPPED OUT!! SERPENT MAN HAS DONE IT!! UNBELIEVABLE!!! But then... *While KG remains on the canvas after furiously tapping out to a leg submission hold -- "Serpent Man" celebrates his win. The crowd is shocked and amazed to have witnessed KG losing to what many believe to be e-wrestling's worse wrestler of all time. Anyways, Serpent Man begins to untie his mask -- he's going to take it off. Nobody has ever seen Serpent Man unmasked. The crowd gasps as he almost has it completely off and with one swoop -- Serpent Man's mask comes flying off. "Get at me Dog" blares over the P.A. system throughout the arena. OH MY GOD -- that's not Serpent Man -- it's "God's Gift to Wrestling"....SMIRTDOGG! The crowd breaks into a frenzy as they cheer SmirtDogg for what he has done to KG. SmirtDogg grabs a microphone from the ring announcer and then begins to speak.* SmirtDogg:(Smirt speaks while looking down at the whimpering and injured KG) Man, you are truly pathetic! You never saw it coming. It was right in front of you all the time and you couldn't recognize what was occuring. To actually think that you were battling Serpent Man -- you have got to be kidding me. KG lay off the crack kid. It's killed all 3 of your brain cells. I know you're hooked to it because of inheritance -- passed down from generation to generation but unless you want to be reduced to a vegetable -- which you're damn close to now -- then you have got to put down the crack pipe.(he gestures to KG -- who's slowly trying to get up -- the anger is driving him to get at Smirt but he doesn't have enough energy as he falls back down to the canvas) Now don't get mad at me for telling it like it is. What you need to do is have a chat with your momma -- be mad at her -- she's the reason you were a crack baby and she's the reason your ass has spent your entire life living out of a trailor. KG -- you didn't possibly think I wouldn't hunt you down here in the EWA -- my old stomping grounds. I've completely humiliated and decemented your character over in the INWA and now that trend shall continue. It's great to be back in the EWA -- maybe not for you -- but for me -- lets just say I'm more than elated about the prospect of chasing you out of wrestling all together. Word has it -- you whined and cryed your way out of your INWA contract. Will you do that with your EWA one? Will Duane Gates even let you escape the wrath of "God's Gift to Wrestling". These are questions you'll be pondering for the next couple of days when you're deciding whether being a wrestler is worth it. When you must come to grips that you're a no talented piece of trailor trash that I'm gonna crush and put an end to permanently if you have the guts to stay in this federation. If you run away like a scared little b*tch -- it's for the best. You'll spare yourself the pain and agony but realize that you can never come back to the wrestling world because if you do I'll seek you out and obliterate you from the wrestling scene forever with injuries you'll live with for the rest of your life. That is -- if you survive. One thing left to say: Au Revoir KG! Nah check it -- Bon Voyage -- motherf*cker! *Camera fades to black.*
