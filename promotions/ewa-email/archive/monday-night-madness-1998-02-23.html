Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ the Thug Warriors) Monday Night Madness Results (2/23/98) First Round EWA European Championship Match Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Thug Warriors) Monday Night Madness kicked off with a bang, as we saw an extremely high-impact match. Octavius and Ahmed used their brawling techniques for most of the match, and Octavius gained the upper hand by sending Ahmed into the steel guardrail on the outside. However, it would not last, as when Octavius went to pick him up for a slam, Ahmed threw some sort of white powder in the eyes of Octavius. From there, it was the spinebuster, the Pearl River Plunge, and the victory for Mr. Johnson. Ahmed Johnson d. Octavius with the Pearl River Plunge. (11:43) First Round EWA European Championship Match "Hollywood" Barry Horowitz Jr. v. Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) This match was a squash, as Alex Wright simply dominated Horowitz. Most of the fans seemed to leave for concessions during this one, as they were rather annoyed by the dancing of Wright. Alex Wright d. Barry Horowitz Jr. with the German Suplex. (5:00) First Round EWA European Championship Match KG (w/ The Stud Stable) v. The Widow Maker This was another squash, and an extremely impressive win for KG. KG absolutely destroyed The Widow Maker, and got a -huge- pop from the capacity crowd. KG d. Widow Maker with the Six Shooter. (0:56) First Round EWA European Championship Match "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Another squash; Gardner didn't even show up. As the official raised Triple-H's hand, Comissioner Slaughter came through the curtain. In a shocking turn of events, Slaughter announced that due to last Thursday's events, when HHH attacked Slaughter, he had a special opponent for him; Slaughter himself! So apparently, Slaughter will once again don the combat boots at WrestleFest, and step into the ring with Helmsley. First Round EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Golden Child v. El Locco Jobbo Jobbo didn't show up; TGC got the win by forfeit. First Round EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "The Rock" Chris Gardner Another squash; this one was dominated by Deathstar, who looked extremely impressive. Deathstar d. Chris Gardner with The Last Rights. (2:23) First Round EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) This was an outstanding match, in which the underhanded tatics of DX played a huge role. Augustus, inspired by the fans, dominated the early portion of the match, until Nash gave him a hit below the belt. Nash regained control, and at one point, the referee was knocked out. Nash motioned for Scott Hall to bring a chair into the ring, but as Nash held Augustus, he ducked, and Hall hit Nash! Augustus then applied the Sharpshooter onto Nash, and the referee awoke to find Nash knocked out. Augustus d. Kevin Nash with the Sharpshooter. (13:10) First Round EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Paul Burnett v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) Kane came to the ring with a vengance, as he picked apart Burnett. Burnett mounted some offense midway through the match, but it was no use, as Kane was too overpowering. Kane d. Paul Burnett with the Tombstone Piledriver. Tiberus/Julius (w/ The Empire) v. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) This match was no contest, as the awesome power of Bloodsport overwhelmed these two men. Wrath and Mortis used their finisher known as The Lethal Injection to put away the Romans. Bloodsport d. The Empire with Wrath's pin on Tiberus after the Lethal Injection. EWA World Television Championship Match Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin (EWA World Television Champion) Anthony opened up with a good offensive attack, but it was soon thwarted by the sheer determination of the Rattlesnake. Austin fought back, and eventually caught Anthony with the Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin d. Marc Anthony with the Stone Cold Stunner. (9:09) Steve Austin retains the Television Title. EWA World Tag Team Championship Triangle Match FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ the Thug Warriors) This was an outstanding contest, as these three teams were determined to take home the gold. However, there can only be one winner, and FlashBack found that out first, as The Disco Inferno was put out by Pain's Clamp. From then on, Death Sentance -dominated- Harlem Heat. Destruction executed a powerbomb on Booker T that seemed to knock him unconsious, but Destruction was so physically exhausted that he did not have the strength to make the cover. Destruction finally got up and whipped Booker T into the ropes, attempting a clothesline. However, Booker T ducked, and then hit the Harlem Sidekick for the win. But tonight, Death Sentance showed that they do belong in the big time. Harlem Heat wins the Triangle Match to retain the World Tag Team Titles. (22:37)
