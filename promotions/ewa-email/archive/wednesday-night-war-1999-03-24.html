EWA Wednesday Night War (3/24/99) March 24, 1999 Live from the The Dean Smith Center (Chapel Hill, North Carolina) The Dean Dome in Chapel Hill, North Carolina is rocking as the seconds tick away until Wednesday Night War begins. It's a new era in the EWA, the rebirth of the golden era. After all, the EWA is in Tar Heel country, but more importantly, it's in Triple Threat country, so what would an event in sweet, old Carolina be without somebody from the Threat there. So as the camera's pan over the crowd, showing the many signs, the crowd literally EXPLODES when the lights go down and the golden beam shoots through the arena. This was probably the biggest pop in wrestling history, as the man who made Carolina his second home, his adopted home, returns with the gold. 'Golden Showers' hits the arena and a second pop occurs. After a few seconds, through the curtain emerges none other than the NEW EWA World Heavyweight Champion, The Golden One... The Golden One: Thank you, ladies and gentlemen, and I must say, it's good to be back home!!! Chapel Hill, North Carolina, home of our beloved Tar Heels, it's great to be back and carrying some gold. This was the place where it all began. The dynasty known as the Triple Threat started right here in this very city. At first, it was just a guy with a whole lot of Franchismo and a dream. Then came his playgirl model and little Becky. Jeff and Justin functioned as the Extreme Dream Machine so well that they could carry the Triple Threat by themselves. But three was the number, so guys like Aaron Martin were given their chance to have some glory. But you see, the search for an adequate third man continued forever, until a "Shining Star" factored into the equation. The Golden One and the Triple Threat were solid gold for quite some time, and we were running the show like never before. But something got in the way of that, some higher power that seemed to hold an eternal grudge. It was the establishment and they held Jeff and myself back for too damn long. But now, now things have changed and Indiana Duane are gone and as you can plainly see, "The Shining Star" is riding high. Jeff Bunda, "The Franchise" is gone and he took Justin with him. Yours truly is left running the show with Terry Conklin, and as you can see, we've done a damn good job of it. President Solley was left little choice but to give me a shot at the World Heavyweight Champion Kane, because I was the #1 contender beyond a shadow of a doubt. He made the smart business move, and what was the end result? The second consecutive Pay Per View extravagansa headlined by The Golden One and this time, I fared just a little bit better. *TGO smiles* And as fate would have it, I walked into hell, had some dinner, feasted on the Big Red Machine and walked out like a little kid on Christmas morning playing with his new toy. Only in my case, the new toy was the most prized possession in our sport, the EWA World Heavyweight Title. However, something very wrong happened at March Madness. In the middle of not only the greatest match of my career, but in the middle of the match that everyone is calling the Match of the Year, your little buddy Erik Draven stuck his unwanted nose where it certainly doesn't belong. He came down, dressed as a wanna-be Kane, and tried wreaking havoc. Fortunately, my good friend Terry, who later tonight is going to beat the "ICON" all over the place, came in to make the save. I mean, hell folks, turn about is fair play, right? Draven or Kane or whoever he was trying to be wanted a crack at helping the real Kane out, so when Terry took care of Draven and then gave the minor assist to me, I really didn't see the problem in it. I mean, the rules were pretty much out the window at that point, so what did I care how it happened, I just wanted it to happen. And happen it did, as together, Terry and I took down the Big Red Machine for what the world probably thought was the final time. Well, here's something to think about you Tar Heel LOVERS!!! GUESS AGAIN!!! You see, that's not the way it was supposed to be. Kane/TGO for the World Title ends in controversy? Not in my book ladies and gentlemen. Think back to Clash of the Champions X, when I beat none other than the immortal World Champion Erik Draven for the title. It was my first reign as the Big Shot in the EWA, but the Corporate Empire gave me an assist. Now, when I was giving Kane, the so-called "Greatest EWA Champion of all time," his toughest fight ever, Terry, my good friend, saves my ass and then helps it out. Well folks, as much as it pains me to say this, I can't leave it at that. I know and you know that The Golden One, "The Shining Star" is the EWA World Heavyweight Champion, for a SECOND time. That puts me in with the likes of Shawn Copeland and Jeff Bunda, two very good friends of mine. But until I prove this to myself, 100%, I can't sleep at night and I can't concentrate during the day. How do we find out who the better man is? Oh you big red bastard, it's so simple. Clash of the Champions XII The Golden One vs. Kane II EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match *Crowd erupts* That's right folks, World Title Match, Kane and myself are gonna go at it again, to prove who truly is the World Champ. Now in my mind, lightning struck once, at March Madness, and it will strike again in a little more than two weeks. Because, slowly but surely, the EWA will once again become Triple Threat country. You know it's coming, you just don't know when, and you don't know who. When I raise the three fingers, you can damn well be sure I mean it. So Chapel Hill, North Carolina, your brand spanking new World Champion has spoken. I just hope Kane is listening. I beat your ass once, I'll beat it again, because when it's all said and done brother, no Draven, no Inferna, no Conklin, just you and me and destiny. Mark your calendars folks, because April 10th, you know it, yet another GOLDEN NIGHT!!! Terry Conklin d. Erik Draven w/ The Countout [Special Challenge Tag Team Match ] Simply Inevitable vs Judgement Day A Great Match. The fans were given their money's worth with this match alone. Simply Inevitable has been out on an unlucky streak the last two matches and looked to turn it around in the early going but they ran into a Buzzsaw in Judgement Day. It was obvious from the beginning that Judgement Day was more than ready to take on these newcomers to the Ewa and not only take them on, but take them out. For most of the early going, it was as if Judgement Day had seen the video of this match, they were one step ahead of every move that Simply Inevitable tried to throw and they threw Simply Inevitable around like Rag Dogs.. hitting them with tons of double team maneuvers and a Superplex and Brainbuster as well. But they could never seem to get the three count.. although they came so close on a couple of occasions and they slowly started to get frustrated and soon after Ministry had a suplex blocked by Cortez it was SI's turn to shine and they didn't dissapoint. On their second or third wind now .. they were a house of fire hitting their opponents with double dropkicks and a Double Superkick and even a powerslam and brainbuster thrown in for good measure. However.. they failed to get the three count as well and the match see-sawed back and forth until a missed missle dropkick by Steel allowed Cortez to make a badly needed tag and then Mathews hit Ministry with a cheap shot which got him in the ring and the ref had to contain him and put him back in the ring and this allowed Simply Inevitable to get in a Double Superplex from the top rope in and Mathews covered while Cortez dropkicked Ministry to the arena floor and Simply Inevitable got the hard fought win. Simply Inevitable d. Judgement Day w/ a Double Superplex
