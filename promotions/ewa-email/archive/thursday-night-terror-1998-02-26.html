EWA Thursday Night Terror

26 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 26, 1998 Live, from Pershing Auditorium, Lincoln, Nebraska. EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Thug Warriors) v. Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Tiberus/Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake "The Snake" Roberts) The Reckoning (w/ The Gathering) v. Michael Simurda/Kris Gaffney Eclipse v. Julius (w/ The Empire) The Pessimist (w/ Specimen-22) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Widow Maker EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. The Golden Child EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Paul Burnett Joshua Gardner (w/ Chris Gardner) v. El Locco Jobbo "Hollywood" Barry Horowitz v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/"The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Scott Hall (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Thug Warriors) v. Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) A strange thing happened at the beginning; before the men came to the ring, the cameras went to the back, where we saw a man in an orange tiger mask, later identified as El Tigre, attack SmirtDogg. As for the match, this was a terrific match, as Alex Wright countered the power of Ahmed Johnson with great wrestling moves. Wright tried to keep the match on the ground, and worked on taking away Johnson's vertical base. Eventually, though, Ahmed overcame it, and began to once again overpower Wright. Ahmed had hit the Spinebuster, and was going for the Pearl River Plunge, when Flash Funk came through the crowd, and walloped Johnson with a pair of brass knuckles. Funk then ran back through the crowd before the Thug Warriors could reach him, and Wright made the cover for the three count. Alex Wright d. Ahmed Johnson by outside interference from Flash Funk. (11:11) EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Another classic matchup, as KG and Helmsley showed no love loss. The two men turned this match into a brawl quickly, and the action from then on was fast and furious. The end came, however, when Sunny tripped Helmsley on the outside. Chyna chased Sunny back to the locker room, but it was too late; KG had already played DX's game, and hit HHH with a chair. As KG was about to make the cover, though, the rest of Degeneration X ran to the ring, and proceeded to give KG a thorough beating. The strange occurance was after the match, when The Gunslinger appeared at the entrance ramp. He pointed at HHH, smiled, and then returned to the back. KG d. Hunter Hearst Helmsley by disqualification when Degeneration X interfered. (13:04) Tiberus/Marc Anthony (w/ The Empire) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake "The Snake" Roberts) This was a joke, as Raven and The Gunslinger dominated this match from beginning to end. A complete squash. DDT d. The Empire with Raven's pin on Tiberus after the Evenflow DDT. (4:09) The Reckoning (w/ The Gathering) v. Michael Simurda/Kris Gaffney Again, an absolute squash, as Leon and Noel Snow made an extremely impressive debut. The Reckoning d. Michael Simurda/Kris Gaffney with Noel Snow's pin on Gaffney after a DDT. (2:56) Eclipse v. Julius (w/ The Empire) This was a match that saw Julius have the momentum...for about 25 seconds. After that, it was total domination by Eclipse, who looked very impressive in his EWA debut. Eclipse d. Julius with the Badmoon. (5:43) The Pessimist (w/ Specimen-32) v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia -Another- squash, as The Rock embarassed himself. Throughout the match, he recieved a beating from not only The Pessimist, but Specimen-32 as well. The Pessimist d. Rocky Maivia with The Suicidal Dream. (6:14) Kevin Nash (w/ Degeneration X) v. The Widow Maker The Widow Maker hung in this match for awhile, but in the end, Nash's power simply overwhelmed him. A valiant effort for TWM, though. Kevin Nash d. The Widow Maker with the JackKnife Powerbomb. (5:32) EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. The Golden Child (My apologies for the short results; -bad- case of the flu.) The Golden Child d. Deathstar with the Royal Destroyer. (16:40) EWA European Tournament Semi-Final Match Augustus (w/ The Empire) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) It was announced that Kane was not in the building, and that Augustus had won by forfeit. As soon as the announcement was made, the lights went out. Kane came down, and proceded to destroy Augustus. Paul Bearer then took the mike. Bearer: That's just to show you idiots out there that Kane could've won this match at ANY given time!!! But the fact remains that we do not WANT the United States Title, and therefor this corpse in the ring can HAVE his match in the finals. We something else in mind...*Laughs evilly* And I assure you people, it will be something to DIE FOR!!! Augustus d. Kane via forfeit. Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Paul Burnett Octavius d. Paul Burnett with a small package. (3:31) Joshua Gardner (w/ Chris Gardner) v. El Locco Jobbo The match was ruled a double countout. "Hollywood" Barry Horowitz v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Galaxy d. Barry Horowitz with the Supernova. (5:36) "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels/"The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) Shawn Michaels/Erik Draven d. FlashBack with Erik Draven's pin on Flash Funk after the DX Driver. (24:14) EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Scott Hall (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Degeneration X) The two men fought to a time limit draw; a rematch was announced by President Gates for WrestleFest '98.
