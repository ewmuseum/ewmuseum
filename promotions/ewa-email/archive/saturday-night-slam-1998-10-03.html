EWA Saturday Night Slam

3 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 3, 1998 Live, from McNichols Sports Arena Denver, Colorado Semifinal EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match Deader v. "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) After Deader's win on Wednesday Night War against KG, the young rookie seems to have a good portion of the EWA fans behind him. Meanwhile, Joe Satre got a loud ovation of boos from the crowd. Deader started off by getting the crowd pumped up with a couple of high risk manuevers, including a plancha onto Satre as he was coming down the aisle. Deader then went to the top of the guardrail, and executed a beauty of a moonsault. However, once Deader threw Satre back in, the tide quickly shifted, as Satre nailed him with a low blow. From there, it was all Satre, as he was able to finish off Deader with the Pimp Splash. Joe Satre d. Deader with the Pimp Splash. Tommy Dreamer v. Edge Dreamer and Edge both looked slow and weary, due to various injuries. Edge's obviously being Gangrel's WNW attack. But the demon from hell wasn't done here, as he made an appearance at ringside. Edge quickly gave chase to Gangrel, and the two disappeared in the bowels of the arena. Tommy Dreamer d. Edge by countout. Val Venis (w/ Terri Runnels) v. "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (w/ Bill Alfonso) Well, the Big Valbowski must've been in a lot of action prior to this match, because Val looked worn-out and tired. As a result, Mr. Monday Night was able to notch an easy victory. Rob Van Dam d. Val Venis with the Split Legged Moonsault. Semifinal EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Tournament Match Mac Namara v. The Prince This was one of the better Light Heavyweight matches in recent memory. Both men were bound and determined to do whatever it takes to advance to the finals of this tournament, and as a result, the fans were treated to an outstanding match. Both men showed a variety of styles, with both using samplings of technical, brawling, and high-flying wrestling. Finally, with under two minutes left before the time limit expired, Mac Namara went for a moonsault, but missed it. This allowed The Prince to lock on High Society, and after a moment, Mac Namara had no choice but to submit. The Prince d. Mac Namara by submission with High Society. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Slain In a surprising match, Slain went toe-to-toe with The Rock, really doing a number on The People's Champ. However, The Rock soon regained the advantage, and was able to hit The People's Elbow to the delight of the crowd. The Rock soon threw Slain to the outside, where the two continued to battle. However, at the last second, Slain whipped The Rock into the guardrail, and climbed back in the ring for the shocking countout victory. The Rock, incensed, climbed in the ring with a chair, and proceeded to make Slain hit Rock Bottom onto the chair. Slain d. The Rock by countout. EWA North American Championship Match KG (w/ The nWo) v. Michael Modest (EWA North American Champion) This was a solid, exciting contest. KG was in control for a good portion of the match, using the help of The Disciple, who accompanied him to the ring, to "cheat" during the match. But Modest gained advantage when Thorne Richards came out, and evened up the score, stopping The Disciple from further interference attempts. From there, it was a well-fought match. KG went to the top for a big splash, but missed it, and then Modest was able to catch a beauty of a Tombstone piledriver. KG appeared to be knocked out cold, but as Modest went for the cover, the bell rang, signifying a time-limit draw. As a result, a return match has been signed for Wednesday Night War. KG and Michael Modest battled to a time limit draw. Michael Modest remains EWA North American Champion. Curt Hennig (w/ Rick Rude) v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) Well, Curt Hennig was far from perfect tonight, as Ruger Ice continued his hot streak as of late. As a reward, President Gates announced another match for Wednesday Night War: Ruger Ice in the challenger's role against the EWA World Television Champion, Venom. Ruger Ice d. Curt Hennig with the Spinebuster. New Demolition v. The Firm (Shane Douglas & The Gunslinger) This match didn't happen, as it was announced that N.D. had retired from wrestling, and the Firm didn't show. The Outsiders (w/ The nWo) v. Haywood Jublome & El Locco Jobbo (w/ Intoxication X) Hall and Nash put up a good fight, and controlled the opening portion of the match, but for the most part, The IX Army controlled this contest. However, there was an extremely odd occurance took place. *Just as Jublome goes for a lariat on Scott Hall, the lights go out. The fans go nuts as a gray smoke begins to fill the arena, totally shutting out any remaining light. All of the sudden, three loud impacts are heard against the mat. Within seconds, the fog clears, and Haywood Jublome is left standing alone, in the ring, with both his tag partner, El Locco Jobbo, and the Outsiders laid out. Mysterio is seen standing at the top of the lower deck, pointing at Jublome and laughing.* Mysterio: You thought I was gone, Jublome, didn't you? I told you that I was the spirit that followed you forever -- your shadow, to an extent. Yes, I am an image of you. You are an image of me. We are both one in the same. We are mirrors of one another. And when I decide it, when I allow it, only one of us will be left standing -- just as it is right now. Go ahead, Jublome -- take your victory -- it just might be your last. 31 days until Destruction ... will you live to see 30? *Mysterio slowly walks to the back, as the match continues.* From there, Jublome looked around, puzzled, and then proceeded to lock on The Chronic on Scott Hall. The official determined that Hall was knocked unconscious, and stopped the match. Afterward, Will Haynes came from the locker room, and the three IX members celebrated their victory. IX d. The Outsiders with Haywood Jublome's submission on Scott Hall with The Chronic. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Thorne Richards v. Crazy Canuck (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Hate Club) This was a solid contest. The crowd was definitely behind Thorne, who was wrestling for the first time since losing his World Title to Shawn Copeland. Thorne pleased the fans at the start of the match, taking down Canuck with a variety of technical wrestling manuevers. Thorne controlled a good portion of the match, keeping Canuck on the ground with a submission-based attack. However, it was Joe Satre on the outside tripping Thorne that gave Canuck the advantage, an advantage he never lost, as he eventually nailed the Lunatic Bomb. Crazy Canuck d. Thorne Richards with the Lunatic Bomb. Crazy Canuck remains EWA Intercontinental Champion. DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Non-Title Grudge Match "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA European/World Tag Team Champion, w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) The highly awaited grudge match turned out to be a real shocker. About midway through the match, Venom was doing a number on Draven, when Stevie Richards came out and stood on the ramp. Richards pointed at Venom, and gave him a big crotch chop, right as Erik Draven nailed Venom from behind. Richards then turned and left. The match continued for awhile, until finally, Venom nailed the Extreme Poison in an amazing feat. However, Venom wasn't finished. Taking advantage of the fact that this was ruled a no-disqualification match right before it started, Venom went to the outside and got a chair. Venom brought the chair into the ring, brought it over his head, and smacked it right across Draven's face, knocking his mask loose. And the crowd gasped, as about five scars on Draven's face, obviously having just been stitched up, opened, covering the ICON in blood. DX quickly ran out, and cleared Venom out of the ring, helping Draven to the back. Obviously, the referee had stopped the match. Venom d. Erik Draven after the referee stopped the match. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Steel Cage Grudge Match Special Guest Referee: Crazy Canuck "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Hate Club) This match wasn't as good as the first two encounters between the two, but it was still entertaining. This cage match was unlike the normal EWA cage matches, as it had a roof over the top, and the only way to win was by pinfall. The match went on for awhile, with neither man holding the advantage, until the lights went out. Organ music blared over the PA system, and at the entrance, a huge pyro display went off. Both Copeland and Michaels stopped, and stared at the entrance ramp, looking for Kane. But they were looking in the wrong places, as behind them, a hole suddenly formed on the mat, and Kane ascended from below the ring. Both men heard the noise, and turned around as the big red machine emerged. However, shockingly, Kane did not attack Copeland, but attacked Michaels, chokeslamming him and then Tombstoning him. Copeland had the right mind to quickly cover Michaels, and got the three count. President Gates stormed down the aisle, furious at this occurance. As soon as Copeland got the third, Kane began to attack Copeland as well. Michaels then recovered, and went after Kane, as a brawl developed. The crowd suddenly gave a huge pop, as Erik Draven, all stitched up, ran out from the locker room. Kane, staring at Draven the whole time, picked up Copeland and delivered the DX Driver on him. This infuriated Draven, who slammed Gates against the cage, and, microphone in hand, demanded a match with Kane. Gates took the microphone from Draven. President Gates: You want a match with Kane, Draven! There's only one way you get it, and that's on MY terms! On Wednesday Night War, you will team with your former best friend, Shawn Michaels, and you two will face the team of Kane, and Shawn Copeland!! And that is the way it is going to be! ("Their Law" by The Prodigy blares over the PA system and the crowd goes absolutely nuts. Prodigy emerges through the curtain on top of the ramp and he has a microphone in hand) A special tag match? Oh Duane, Duane, Duane, how could you ever let this happen? How will you ever have something called right down the middle? I'm sure that you'll do everything in your power to make sure that Shawn is the only man standing, as later he'll be the only man bending. What you need is someone who's fair, someone who's impartial, and someone who gives a rat's ass about everyone in the ring. Shawn Michaels, you picked your path when you aligned yourself with Gates. You picked your allies, and by default, you picked your enemies. Sure, you got your buddy breathing down your neck (Prodigy points to Draven), but he's the least of your worries. You might not have you're beloved title anymore, but don't think that it doesn't make you a marked man. No, it's far worse, because if you had the title, I'd have something else on my mind when I trashed you. But with nothing but rage on my mind, it'll be all the more sweeter. Kane, I laid you out once before, and if it weren't for circomstances I would have been champion earlier than I was. Don't think for a secd that I'm not looking forward to obliging to doing it again, because the bigger they are.......the harder they fall. The mightiest tree makes the biggest noise when chopped down, and last time I checks, I'm the craziest lumberjack in town. Draven...............I'm not even going to waste my time with you. And Copeland, are you that tough when confronted with your fear? You've run your mouth on me, calling me out, thinking you're all tough with your title and all. A has been? Past my prime? Oh Copeland, a wise man chooses his battles, and a crazy man chooses his opponents. You were a nothing, and you continue to be a nothing. A match with me will make you and a match with me will break you. If you want me in the ring, you sign your death warrant. So Duane, you want to be fair to everyone involved instead of biased to your buddies? You have a choice. Do what you did tonight and mess things up, or get a man who can actually do the job (And Prodigy disappears through the curtain with the crowd going mad) Shawn Copeland d. Shawn Michaels after a Tombstone from Kane. Shawn Copeland is the NEW EWA World Champion!!
