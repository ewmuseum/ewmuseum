EWA Saturday Night Slam (10/16/99) October 16, 1999 Live, from The MCI Center Washington, D.C. ---- *The camera shot fades in and we embark on the adventurous journey of 2 solid hours of wrestling action and sports entertainment which comprises Saturday Night Slam. The pyrotechnics display goes off as well as booming cannon blasts. We're coming to you live from the MCI Center in Washington, D.C. Our nation's capital. The EWA fans are in a festive mood because the Email Wrestling Alliance is hotter than ever with the return of pro-wrestling superstars Stevie Richards, Jack Reed, and Vincent Jackson! The EWA is on the steady incline once again -- hoping to reclaim its prosperity from the past. A chant of "EWA! EWA! EWA!" fulls the MCI Center as the capacity crowd goes absolutely ballistic. How will the show start off??? How will things kick off??? Suddenly, we get our answer when the lights in the MCI Center dim considerably and over the Public Address System -- the following song blares. "Hate me now" by Nas featuring Puffy.
