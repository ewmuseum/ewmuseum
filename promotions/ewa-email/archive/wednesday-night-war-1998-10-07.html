EWA Wednesday Night War

7 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 7, 1998 Live, from The Sioux Falls Arena Sioux City, South Dakota Deader v. Slain This was a solid contest between two of the EWA's struggling stars. However, both put on a pretty good show tonight. But Deader had the momentum of his recent win over KG, and was able to pick up the victory. Deader d. Slain with the Deadsy. The Black Angels v. The Freebirds Badstreet USA must've been rockin' tonight, as The Freebirds come away with a victory. The Freebirds d. The Black Angels with Michael PS Hayes' pin on Kaworu after the Alabama Jam. Joe Lemon v. Thorne Richards [Thorne comes down to ringside, with his right forearm still in a cast. He appears rather agitated and is staggering a tad in his approach.] Thorne: SILENCE! *the crowd quells a bit* South Dakota, how's the farm doing? *the crowd laughs somewhat, despite the underhanded comment* Well, it appears as if Mr. Gates is being an unforgiving bastard as of late. As many of you know, I had one of the roughest matches in my life against Shawn Copeland, that little piggy bank son of a b... *catches himself and giggles* Can't say that more than I should. And as it is apparent to all, even the myopians of this town can see that I'm still physically not 100%. But does Gates showme the sympathy? *gesticularly* NOOOOOOOO! I have to do the bloody grind tonight, and of all people, who gets to waste my time? Joe BLOODY Lemon! Don't tell me the bookers insult your intelligence THAT much! *the crowd gets a bit restless* And on this night, when I can celebrate my 20th victory in the EWA, a milestone not touched by many, I have to do it in a not-so-noble fashion. I mean look at him! *points to Lemon who is rather peeved* If they say an out of shape wrestler carries a spare tire, he's a in lorry ("a truck", in British terms) the making! Now, it's very obvious Gates wants to make a mockery out of me and feed me this 2-bit cretin. So, I shall not waste anymore time, and let me get this over with. I've got painkillers to take and a bum to kick. And not in that order, either! [Thorne then drops the mic and slithers on in to begin the match] Thorne looked VERY sluggish in the match, and even kicked out of the Lemon Drop at one point. He then got very aggressive, and nailed Lemon with the cast...but to the shock of everyone watching, passed out, falling to the canvas. The match was ruled a double countdown, and paramedics then rushed Thorne to the hospital. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) v. The Prince A very competitive match, interupted near the end after Satre had been in control. Deader walks out to a BIG pop and looks at the ring and seeing them fight it out for the belt. He starts to talk DeaderMay i interrupt this match,cuz i think i'm more important at the moment. Satre,Prince i have a little proposal to whoever wins this belt. that at N2R WE GET IT ON! That's Right. But under my rules. We gonna have a WEAPONS MATCH! That's right. November 2 Remember, oh yes isn't it so approprite. It'll be one to remember. So go on with your match. Just remember N2R one of you is mine. The crowd goes haywire and chants Deader as he leaves to the back as the match continues This was enough to cause Satre to begin screaming at Deader, which allowed Prince to recover from behind and hit Satre with first a German Suplex, and then High Society, for the submission win. (The Prince walks over to the ring attendant and demands a microphone. He's handed a microphone and he walks to the middle of the ring. Taking his newly won Light Heavyweight Title, The Prince throws it down on the mat. Seeing the title treated this way, the crowd boos loudly) I just went through what was supposed to be the toughest competition that the EWA Light Heavyweight division had to offer and I barely broke a sweat. Galaxy, the supposed uncrowned champion gave up to a bloody sleeper hold. Mac Namara was a joke. And Satre (The Prince points down to "Pimpin" Joe Satre, who's being helped down the asile) I told you if you tried to step up you'd get knocked down and tonight exactly that happened to you. I ran rupshot through the entire division and they hand me this title. Well, (The Prince points to the belt) that's what I think of it. The belt means nothing. Everytime I turn around I'm going to hear, "Well Prince, you beat Galaxy, Mac Namara, and Satre, but what does that prove? You never took out the champ", and you know what? They're right. New Jack, you turned and ran when you're buddies left, and not because you couldn't stand being alone but because you knew competion was heating up and that I was breathing down your neck. So I'm going to make it a real easy for you and leave you no options. (The Prince points to the belt once again) It's true that you never lost that belt because you were never given the opportunity. Well, if you decide to show your face in these parts ever again, with or without your buddies, then the chance to lose it is yours. I have something to prove, and that's that I am the best Light Heavyweight champ. If I have to pull you out of the gutter, dust you off, and beat you down, then so be it. But mark my words New Jack, I am calling you out, you will respond, and I will thrash you like you've never been thrashed before. (The Prince tosses the microphone to the side and picks up his Light Heavyweight Title. He walks out of the ring, dragging the belt behind him) The Prince d. Joe Satre by submission with High Society. The Prince is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! Tommy Dreamer v. Gangrel (w/ Devon) Dreamer got some good moves in at the beginning, including a nice superplex, but the demon from hell was simply too powerful, and picked up the win. Gangrel d. Tommy Dreamer with the Piledriving DDT. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. The Pessimist (w/ Specimen-32) A solid match between two light heavyweights that ended when Galaxy missed a moonsault. Pessimist then was able to recover, and hit the Suicdial Dream. Afterward, though, Galaxy unleashed a furious attack on Pessimist, ending it with a moonsault onto a chair laid on Pessimist. The Pessimist d. Galaxy with the Suicidal Dream. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas (w/ Francine) v. Edge Shane Douglas did absolutely nothing in this contest, as Edge coasted to an easy victory. Edge d. Shane Douglas with the Downward Spiral. Val Venis (w/ Terri Runnels) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Trixie) Venis was destroyed for the second card in a row, as Stevie Richards dominated from bell to bell. Stevie Richards d. Val Venis with the Stevie Kick. Non-Title Match "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Hate Club) This match was rescheduled for SNS. El Locco Jobbo (w/ Intoxication X) v. Mac Namara Mac Namara got smacked down pretty badly here, as El Locco Jobbo coasted to his first singles victory. El Locco Jobbo d. Mac Namara with a gutwrench powerbomb. EWA World Television Championship Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) An outstanding contest ended when Venom tried to bring the steel pipe into play, and did so, nailing Venom across the back with it. Security quickly escorted Venom out before he could do any major damage to Ice. Ruger Ice d. Venom by disqualification. Venom remains EWA World Television Champion. EWA North American Championship Return Match KG (w/ the nWo) v. Michael Modest (EWA North American Champion) [On the EWA JumboTron, we see a live feed of Michael Modest in the emergency van with Thorne Richards, who is receiving medical treatment en route to the local hospital. Modest appears genuinely distraught, cradling the belt in his arms.] Modest: KG, I hope to hell you're thanking your lucky stars right now that I happen to have a heart behind these muscles. There's nothing more I'd like to do than kick your sorry ass again but I think even a Wonderlick like you can figure out I'm not there, and you've won. But if you think you're suddenly the champion, think again. See this belt? It ain't leaving me till you BEAT ME fair and square for it. Think you can put your money where your mouth is? Or will you run outta' space since my boot's gonna' fit there soon? [Turns to the ER staff] Dammit, do something! Is he alright?... MAKE HIM ALRIGHT! As a result of this, KG was declared North American Champion, with a final match scheduled for WNW between the two, inside of a steel cage. KG d. Michael Modest by forfeit. KG is the new EWA North American Champion!! Hollywood Hogan (w/ The nWo) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) During the match, for some odd reason, Galaxy stood at the entrance ramp, watching on. Meanwhile, Triple-H dominated Hogan, and made extremely short work of him. Triple-H d. Hollywood Hogan with the Pedigree. Special Ordered Tag Team Match Special Referee: Prodigy "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ The Hate Club) & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) & "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Shawn Michaels came to the ring first, with a shocker. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: Erik Draven, I told you that I wouldn't be coming here alone, and that I'd have a man from your past to watch my back, a bodygyard, if you will. This man needs no introduction, so hit the music! And as the music blared over the PA, the fans stood in stunned shock, as the 6'9", 316 pound Sid made his way to the ring. Sid stood silently at ringside during the match, twitching every now and then, and watching over Michaels. He had quite a few staredowns with Erik Draven, and we were told that Sid once defeated Erik Draven for a title. Copeland started out against Michaels, and this quickly turned into a brawl, esclating when Draven and Kane were tagged in simultaneously. The amazing occurance was when Kane tagged in Copeland. Draven & Copeland shook hands, then proceeded to wrestle each other for about five minutes, until Copeland tagged Draven back in. Draven beat up on Kane pretty badly for a few minutes, and showed some actual teamwork with Shawn Michaels. Once Michaels tagged, though, Kane caught him by the throat, chokeslammed him, Tombstoned him, and pinned him. Draven started to go after Michaels, but Sid put HBK on his shoulder and carried him out. So then, Copeland and Draven both began attacking Kane brutally. JR: Kane is getting the living hell beaten out of him, and I can't say that I feel bad for him. He had this coming for a long time, and it's...who is that? Folks, I don't know what to tell ya, but a...well I'm not ashamed to say it, and beautiful young lady is making her way down to the ring here. And Draven and Copeland have stopped in their tracks and are just staring at her...hell, so am I. She's in the ring now, and look at this! She just gave Prodigy a wink, and now she's walking over to Draven and Copeland. They...they don't know what to make of this young lady. She's got fire engine red hair, and she's a knockout for sure. Now she's asking Draven and Copeland to come over to them...Kane is back on his feet and he's just staring as well. Now she's literally all over Draven and Copeland, and she's...well it looks like she's gonna give them a kiss here. OH MY GOD!!!! OH MY GOD!!! She just blew a fireball at Draven and Copeland!!! And Kane with a double clothesline!!! COPELAND AND DRAVEN JUST BARELY DUCKED!! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE?!?! Kane takes Copeland, and THROWS HIM INTO THE FIFTH ROW!!! Draven goes to the outside to help Copeland...Who is this lady? She...She blinded Draven and Copeland with a kiss of fire for all intensive purposes, and that let Kane get up and do his damage. Bearer: Well, well, well...when Uncle Paul promises, you better believe he's gonna deliver. And I give you ladies and gentlemen...Inferna!!! But you might better relate to her as Kane's...SISTER!!! JR: MY GOD! KANE HAS A SISTER!!! Inferna is Kane's sister!! Is that possible? Well...we never asked. We just assumed he only had a brother, but we never thought about a sister. We damn sure know now, and she's deadly!!! We're out of time, but now who's gonna stop Kane with his sister at his side!!! After this, yet another brawl broke out between Prodigy and Shawn Copeland, on the outside. And before the camera faded to black, as the brawl was going on, a call came into the broadcast booth from the hospital, and we learned why Thorne Richards had passed out...he had OD'd on Vicodin.
