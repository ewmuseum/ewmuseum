EWA Wednesday Night War

2 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 2, 1998 Live, from Roberts Stadium Evansville, Indiana Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. James Mastin Jack Reed made his return to EWA action a successful one, as he pretty much destroyed his competition, if Mastin can be called that. Jack Reed d. James Mastin with a German Suplex. Furnace v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia (w/ The Nation) Neither man showed up; Furnace has been again fired for his no-shows. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. Deader Deader looked awful tonight, as Galaxy controlled this match from bell to bell. Not much of a contest here. Galaxy d. Deader with the Supernova. "The Lionheart" Chris Jericho (w/ The Hart Foundation) v. Serpent Man One word: squash. Jericho took all of thirty-five seconds to delete the master of the "HISS HISS" in this one. Chris Jericho d. Serpent Man by submission with the Lion Tamer. Semi-Final Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Haywood Jublome (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Terry Conklin (w/ D-Generation X) A very competitive contest between these two, with neither man gaining any sort of advantage until the very end, when a low blow by Jublome allowed him to get an inside cradle victory. Haywood Jublome d. Terry Conklin with an inside cradle. Jim Ross: And now we turn to the POISON PIT, Hosted by “The Extreme SuperStar” Venom…be warned ladies and gentlemen this show is not for the weak at heart. (the camera pans over to a dimly lit set. The spotlight shines on the set as Fireworks are lit off. The set looks somewhat like a darker version of the set of “Late Night With Conan O Brien”, with a huge plate glass window in the backdrop.” “Jesus Christ SuperStar” begins to blare over the PA system, as Venom, decked in a blue Armani suit with expensive shades, makes his way onto the set. The crowd erupts with boos. Venom grabs a mic off of the desk and speaks.) Venom: Well well well! For all of you idiots in the audience who have no clue what they are watching, This is THE highest rated segment on EWA Television, The Poison Pit!!. Now, thanks to the garbage that happened last week, most of it being the fault of that stupid assed Canadian Canuck and his band of lap dogs the Prodigy…god I hate that man…Hate Club, I am being forced by The President Duane Gates…that piece of trash..to apologize to all of the fans of the EWA. And yes, that means all of you unbathed mongrels out there in the audience. *crowd boos. Alright alright, shut your mouths. Listen up all of you Hicks here in Indiana….speaking of Hicks, I am wrestling Hicks for the Television Title tonight!! *A teenage boy in the crowd screams “I hope You Lose!!!”* What? You hope I lose?? Look here you little * BEEP* I hope you lose something too, your virginity, but by the looks of the sweat stains on that shirt of yours and your greasy face it won’t be for another 12 years. Now, we are pressed for time tonight due to the Triple Main event, actually quadruple main event if you count me going to beat up Hicks. Anyway, we are pressed for time, so we have to make this a shortened edition of the P.P. tonight. So, let me introduce tonight’s guest….Smirt Dogg. (“Get at me Dog” By DMX blares over the PA as Smirt Dogg walks out from the back an on to the set. The Crowd erupts with cheers as “Gods Gift To Wrestling” sets up onto the set and plays to them. An annoyed Venom motions to cut the music and stand face to face with Smirt Dogg.) Venom: Alright, enough showboating. I am going to ask a question that everyone is dying to know…Smirt Dogg, what the hell is with this new attitude? You turned from one annoying son of a *BEEP * to an more so annoying candy assed goody boy! SmirtDogg: It's something that was needed to be done. It was time for a change. If you go up and down the EWA roster I'm sure I've done something at one time or another to piss each and every one of them off. Personally, I think it's safe to say I was the most hated wrestler in the EWA. The Slammy I received for Biggest Heel of the Year sealed that. I woke up one morning, looked at myself in the mirror, and didn't like what I saw. Before wrestling a much calmer and nicer person. The sport changed me 180 degrees and I decided that it's time I return to what I once was before wrestling even came into the picture. A man of my superb wrestling talent should be a role model for kids to look up to and want to root for. That's where I'm heading off too. I look around this arena now and see the thousands of EWA fans who want to be entertained and get their money's worth well it's time someone gave a damn about them. Smirt-D-O-Double G gives a damn or else I wouldn't have made this drastic change in attitude and this huge step. I'd rather be cheered than hated by the American Public. I have a long way to go but I will succeed in my efforts. * Crowd Pops* Venom: Look here, Ham and Eggs, you can spew all the B.S. You want to in front of these idiot hillbillies out here, but godammit don’t come on my show talking that crap! Why would you want to please the idiots who booed you last week?! Smirt: Unlike you the fans matter to me. When they purchase a ticket for an EWA event they''re coming watch the top EWA superstars wrestle. I''m one of them and having the knowledge of that I now see that it''s not right for me to be against the people who make the EWA the great establishment it is today. The fans keep us all in business and our jobs intact. They deserve respect and I''m giving it in full. Wrestling is two things. It''s a sport and also entertainment at its best. I''m here as a wrestler and an entertainer. I''m going to give the fans what they want to see. Lots of overwhelming top of the line wrestling action and reasons why they should break into a frenzy whenever my entrance music cues up and it''s time for me to get into that ring and wrestle the fierce competition that makes up the EWA. That right there is why I''m saying what I been saying. If you don''t like it well I could care less. I''m not out here to please you. I''m out here for the EWA Fans first and foremost and then myself! (Crowd pops loudly. They begin to chant “Smirt! Smirt! Smirt!”) Venom: Well, you can do all that crap. You can get on your knees and bend over and spread em for the fans all you want Mutt, but the fact of the matter is they don’t help you. Look at me, no support from these bastards and none from any type of weak stable. I got to where I am by myself!!!!!!! Speaking of which, Mutt Man, why the hell do you run with that pack of sewer floaters…the Ghetto Boys! I thought there was supposed to be a midget in your crew named Bill??? What's up with that? Smirt: First of all, it''s not Ghetto Boys; it''s Ghetto Warriors. Secondly the reason I associate myself with Harlem Heat and New Jack is because they''re like family to me. Those are my dawgs and the only wrestlers I''d trust in this business to stand by my side. Reason for the stable is simple. Wrestling is a game of domination and the Ghetto Warriors intend on carrying out completely. Harlem Heat has the EWA World Tag Team Titles, tonight New Jack will bring home the EWA Light Heavyweight Title to where it belongs and soon enough I''ll be holding North American or World Title Gold. Count on it. Together the Ghetto Warriors can get alot done while giving the fans what they want to see. Great wrestling performances and a stable they can believe in. Let me stress out the fact that I never had a Ghetto Warrior member decide the outcome of my matches. The only time Harlem Heat or New Jack came into the picture during one of my bouts was to prevent outside interference from getting at me and costing me that match. I don''t see anything wrong with that. In fact that''s one major reason why I formed the Ghetto Warriors stable. To watch each other''s backs in those instances. By the way the Ghetto Warriors doesn''t have a midget in its ranks so I don''t know what the hell you''re talking about Venom. Venom: Ah it was a joke, you candy asses never have any type of sense of humor. Yawn, I could care less Mutt Boy. You see, first of all, you suck. That’s as nice as I could put it. New Jack can’t wrestle for * BEEP*, and Booger T and Stevie Gay are ass. I don’t care for you or your crew of ham and eggs from one hundred and tenth street. Now, I have a question. You were friends with Shawn Michaels, right??? Smirt Dogg: That''s your opinion. An opinion I believe isn''t shared by many. However, a more prevalent opinion would be for me to suggest that your show isn''t everything it''s made out to be, that you lack wrestling skills and that you didn’t win the EWA Intercontinental Title. What do you think of that Venom? * Venom glares* And to answer your question, yes Shawn Michaels and I were friends during the Dream Team days but that was strained when Shawn Michaels and Erik Draven both plotted against Harlem Heat. They intended on costing Booker T and Stevie Ray the titles and that''s something I wasn''t going to let happen. Michaels and Draven were never considered to be my good friends like Harlem Heat are and always will be. Incidentally, I think HBK is guilty as sin. He knocked up Christina Knight. That shows you what kind of person he is. Doing that to his so called best friend. It''s common knowledge in this business Draven and Michaels are two men that can never be trusted. Venom: Whooo, harsh words! Looks like you three butt buddies won’t be sharing a cell anytime soon. My take on the whole thing? I couldn’t give a damn. Wrestling is a cut Throat business and you ham and eggers knew that from the first day you stepped into the ring. If Michael had sex with that piece of ass so be it, hell I would have done the same thing. * Turns to Camera* Well, that’s all the time we have for today, and-..oh geez, look who’s here. (Shawn Micheals, visibly upset, walks out onto the set. ) Smirt: If I be damned it''s The Sellout Kid Shawn Michaels. I know you didn''t come out here to deny what I just said. Everyone knows that you and Knight had an affair going on behind Draven''s back so don''t even try to suggest otherwise. This puts a wrinkle in your precious Degeneration X doesn''t it? How can you and Draven co-exist? You can''t. In fact he should be done with you since he''s the one that''s carried you for the last year and a half. It''s always been Draven and his little buddy HBK. Looks like that is coming to an end. But you''re getting what you deserve. Play with fire and get burnt HBK. That''s what it comes down to. HBK: You actually have the gall to say those things, Smirt? I can't believe this...Erik doesn't believe me, the fans don't believe me, YOU don't believe me! WHAT THE HELL IS YOUR PROBLEM??? (As Smirt is about to respond, HBK delivers a brutal SuperKick to Smirt’s jaw, sending him flying through the plate glass window. HBK then runs and begins to pound Smirt with brutal stomps to the face and chest. Bleeding like a stuck Pig, SmirtDogg attempts to come back by grabbing onto HBK’s foot. Michaels glares at Smirt, and drives the heel of his boot into the eye of SmirtDogg, lacerating it. HBK then grabs Smirt by the head and rubs his face into the broken glass on the floor. Venom rushes over to the area, carrying a large sheet of plate glass.) Venom: Wait wait! Stop it damn it!!! Here, use this on him too!!!! (HBK Sets up the glass slanted on a wall of the set. He then picks the broken SmirtDogg up, places him on his shoulders, and does a brutal Death Valley Driver, right through the glass. HBK gets up and begins to brush the glass off of his body, as Smirt is lying on the broken in a pool of his own blood. He then spits on Smirt, and storms off the set. He is stopped by Erik Draven. The men stare each other down, and Draven shoves HBK. Micheals shoves “The ICON” back. The two men exchange glares, and they part ways. Venom waves the camera man toward him. He looks into the camera with a serious look on his face.) Venom: And now my final thought. Well, tonight we learned two things. Goody two shoes like that bloody crippled candy ass over there usually always get their asses kicked on segments like these, and we also learned that it sure is handy to have a good supply of plate glass sheets in the back! Well, that’s all for tonite. Make sure you pigs out there watch as I beat up Justin Hicks, and maybe some other people tonite! Take Care of yourself….and each other. *looks at Smirt * BWAAAA HAAA HAAA!!!! Hey, Ross, you better call someone from the back and tell them to get some help out here, I think this loser is drowning in his own blood!! BWWA HAAA, *BEEP *ing Ham and Egger! The Black Angels v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit and Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The Hart Foundation) Benoit and The Hitman dominated as a team, but the more interesting turn of events was the occurance after the victory. Jim Ross: The Hitman and the Crippler make a stunning debut here in the EWA, making short work of the Black...what the hell? Security, get that fan back in the stands! (A 'fan' leaps over the guardrail, hits the ring, and kicks Bret Hart in the stomach, followed by a middle finger and...) Jim Ross: A Stone Cold Stunner on Bret Hart!!! That's STONE COLD!!! He's back!!! (in the ring, Austin delivers the same kick-finger-Stunner to Chris Benoit, and runs from the ring and up the ramp before the rest of the Hart Foundation can converge on him) (On the ramp, Austin is tossed a Steveweiser from a fan, pops it, and slams it down, all the while flipping off the Hart Foundation with his other hand. Austin's music blares over the PA, and the crowd response is deafening. While the Harts attempt to gather themselves in the ring, Austin stalks his way back to the locker room.) Will "The Thrill-Pill" Haynes v. Mike Modest Modest put up a good fight for the first few minutes of the match, but the Thrill-Pill was very on-target tonight, as he demolished Mike Modest after a few moments. Will Haynes d. Mike Modest with a moonsault. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match New Jack (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "Triple Toke" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Martin was actually escorted to the ring in handcuffs, as he was arrested prior to the card for illegal posession of marijuana. Needless to say, he didn't do well in this contest, and we came out with a new champion. New Jack d. Aaron Martin with the Gangsta Splash. New Jack is the NEW EWA Light Heavyweight Champion! Mayne Mann v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) "The Ultimate Jobber" did just that, I suppose, in this contest against SmirtDogg. SmirtDogg d. Mayne Mann with the Gangsta's Paradise. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match New Demolition v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Ghetto Warriors) A spectacular tag team contest ended on a sour note, when Flame, who accompanied ND to ringside, hit Booker T with a chair, causing him to be counted out. New Demolition d. Harlem Heat by countout. Harlem Heat are still the EWA World Tag Team Champions. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Jobber Express (w/ Teddy Long) A double no-show here. Ruger Ice v. Prodigy Another fine contest, as Ruger Ice showed an incredible amount of intensity in this match, causing the fans to cheer on both men in enjoyment. However, it wasn't enough for Ruger in thos one. Prodigy d. Ruger Ice with the Firestarter. EWA European Championship Ladder Match The Golden One v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) A solid contest, which ended in Copeland hitting the Money Shot onto the ladder, and climbing to win the title. Then, TGO unfortunately announced his retirement from the EWA. Shawn Copeland d. The Golden One. Shawn Copeland is still the EWA European Champion. Al Snow (w/ Head) v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) No-show by Snow, Canuck wins by forfeit. EWA World Television Championship Match "The Extreme Superstar" Venom v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Hicks looked extremely depressed for some odd reason, and didn't put up much of a fight in this one, oddly enough. Afterward, though, Bret Hart ran to the ring, and laid a pretty bad beating on Venom, locking him up in the Sharpshooter for an incredible five minutes. Venom d. Justin Hicks with the Extreme Poison. Venom is the new EWA World Television Champion! TRIPLE MAIN EVENT!!!! Ladder Match for the EWA North American Championship "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The PHC) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA North American Champion, w/ D-Generation X) A shocking occurance developed during this one. Shawn Michaels came to the ring, and proceeded to get into a shouting match with Draven. Eventually, the thing cooled down, and the two actually shook hands, to a huge pop from the crowd. On the outside of the ring, though, Satre locked in a sleeperhold on Draven. Michaels, who was behind Satre, went to Superkick him, but Satre let go and ducked, and Michaels nailed Draven instead. Michaels, clearly upset with himself, exited the ring area, as Satre climbed the ladder and took the belt. However, once Draven got up, he gave Satre three Fallen Angels...onto the ladder. Joe Satre d. Erik Draven. Joe Satre is the new EWA North American Champion!! Click here for the rest!
