April 25, 1998 Live, from the Palace of Auburn Hills in Detroit, Michigan ---- Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. The Outsiders The 1998 EWA Survivor Series started off with the debut/return of Prophecy, and they looked extremely impressive tonight. The Outsiders mounted some small early offense, but the expierence of Travis Scott and Timothy David quickly ended that. Prophecy d. The Outsiders with Travis Scott's pin on Scott Hall after the Cryptorchid. (7:23) New Demolition (w/ Semi) v. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) FlashBack looked downright awful tonight, as B.A. Buddy Brown and Flame really went to work on the duo. Brown and Semi continued to have obvious dissention between each other throughout the match. New Demolition d. FlashBack with Buddy Brown's pin on Flash Funk after the Bad Ass Bomb. (4:11) EWA World Television Championship Match The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. The Gunslinger (EWA World Television Champion, w/ DDT) This was one of the most memorable Television Championship bouts in recent history, as The Golden One and Gunslinger put on a fast-paced show. TGO had surprising quickness for a man of his size, and obtained the early advantage. However, Jake Roberts quickly became involved, and the momentum shifted to the reigning Television Champion. The Golden One, however, got the fans behind him, and started a rally, until the 'Slinger hit him with a -vicious- low blow. Slinger then went to the top rope, but on his way up, TGO quickly got up, positioned him for The Golden Edge, and hit it, making the pinfall and becoming the new EWA World Television Champion. The Golden One d. The Gunslinger with the Golden Edge. (13:44) The Golden One is the new EWA World Television Champion!! Cactus Jack v. The Wyldsyde Cactus nailed a punch to start off the match, and that seemed to infuriate The Wyldsyde. The Wylsyde then went on a tear, using all varities of wrestling holds on Cactus, wearing him down and eventually defeating him. The Wyldsyde d. Cactus Jack with the Wyldryde. (7:55) Mixed Tag Match KG & Sunny v. Justin Hicks & Rebecca Claridge SmirtDogg and the Triple Threat stayed in the back for this one, and the match started off with KG and Hicks. KG held the early advantage on Hicks, and then tagged in Sunny. This proved to be a mistake, as Sunny is obviously not suited for the action inside the squared circle. Rebecca, however, and much to the delight of the male audience, is, as she physically dominated Sunny in the ring. Justin Hicks & Rebecca Claridge d. KG & Sunny with Rebecca's pin on Sunny after a running powerslam. (11:29) "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) Double A didn't show up for the match, and The Crippler's hand was raised in the forfeit victory. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match Public Enemy (w/ "Tricky" Dick Dennis) v. Death Sentance (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Morgue) Death Sentance looked sluggish throughout this match, as Public Enemy controlled most of it. DS started to make a surge late in the match...and then referee Nick Patrick was knocked unconscious. Suddenly...."BREAK IT DOWN" was heard over the PA, and Shawn Michaels and Hunter Hearst Helmsley made their way to the ring. The crowd, not knowing why DX was there, watched as HBK and HHH walked straight into the ring and went to work on Death Sentance. Michaels nailed the Sweet Chin Music on Pain; Helmsley nailed the Pedigree on Destruction, and then Ghetto Boy and Swap Meet Louie nailed their respective finishers on the two. When the dust cleared, Public Enemy, the newest members of Degeneration X, celebrated with the World Tag Team Championship victory. *Sexual Thangs by Poison starts blaring over the PA as a beautiful woman wearing cutoff jean shorts and a t-shirt that is tied in a knot just below her breasts walks out of the entrance way. She stops a few feet from the curtain and turns back towards it as two men walk out wearing white tights with the words "Sexual" on one side and "Thangs" on the other, they are also sporting vests that say Sexual Thangs on the back. As they walk out the crowd erupts into a frenzy as both men stop next to the beautiful woman and pull out microphones from their vest pockets. One of the men raises the mic and begins to speak as the crowd quiets.* J Man: Ladies and gentlemen, children of all ages.....the EWA proudly presents to you the newest tag team champions of the world, Public Enemy. *Crowd erupts into a chorus of boo's* J Man: Okay, okay. So you don't like them, well we just wanted to come out and tell them that we are sooooo proud of them for winning, BUT we need to tell them what they've really won. Tell 'em T. "Sweet" Ricky T: Well you see, we were back stage speaking with good 'ole President Gates and he told us, being the best tag team in the world mind you, that if we signed here in the EWA he would guarantee us a title shot within a week. *crowd erupts into cheers* But wait, hold on. Being the "good guys" we are, we've decided that not only would it be unfair for us to just walk in and get a title shot, but it would deprive all of our great fans the opporitunity to see us demolish some of these other pathetic teams before we prove once and for all that we are the best tag team around. So we've come to the decision that we will win our quota of matches and then take what is rightfully ours. Ain't that right J? J Man: Exactly T, Not only are we gonna win our way to the top but we are gonna look good in doing it. So Public Enemy, Death Sentence, Prophecy, and whoever else there is, consider this your notice served. Stay out of our way or suffer the consequences in the form of the STD. Whooooo!!!!! Ricky T: And since everybody knows that Sexual Thangs backs down from no one we are hearby issuing a challenge to the now fallen tag champs Death Sentence to a match on Wednesday Night Warzone. So tell me boys, you gonna be there or are ya gonna be square? *As their music begins again both men point at PE as they are led to the back by the lovely Cherry Pie. As they disappear behind the curtain pyrotechnics go off in the shape of ST right above the entrance way.* Public Enemy d. Death Sentance with Ghetto Boy's pin on Pain. Public Enemy are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions!! EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Raven (w/ DDT) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (EWA U.S. Champion, w/ The IV Horsemen & Kimberly) As Page was heading out from his locker room, he bumped into Justin Hicks of the Triple Threat. The two exchanged words, and security quickly intervened. Page then came to the ring, and the fight began. The match went all over the arena and back again, but once the match was back into the ring, Hicks ran out, and waffled Page with a chair. Hicks then rolled Raven over onto Page, and helped the referee up, who had been knocked out. Mark Curtis made the count, and another championship changed hands. Raven d. Dallas Page with interference from Justin Hicks. (16:32) Raven is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!! Special Survivor Series WildCard Match Team #1: Castor Troy, "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin, The Widow Maker, and "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley v. Team #2: "Stunning" Scott Cummings, Deathstar, KooKabura, and Shawn Hart Order of Elimination: Aaron Martin e. Deathstar Scott Cummings e. The Widow Maker KooKabura e. Hunter Hearst Helmsley Shawn Hart e. Aaron Martin KooKabura e. Castor Troy Team #2 wins. EWA Intercontinental Championship Return Match Mankind v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA I-C Champion, w/ Triple Threat) Mankind again didn't show up; Bunda wins by forfeit. Strap Match for the EWA European Championship SmirtDogg (w/ KG) v. Prodigy (EWA European Champion, w/ Degeneration X) This was a long, grueling match, but in the end, it was the new members of Degeneration X, Public Enemy, that propelled Prodigy to a win, as they first took out KG by handcuffing him to the ring post, and then helped Prodigy go to work on SmirtDogg. Prodigy then waved them off, and hit the Firestarter, dragging Smirt around and touching all four corner turnbuckles for the win. Prodigy d. SmirtDogg after hitting the Firestarter and touching all four turnbuckles. (27:56) Prodigy retains the EWA European Championship. TRIPLE MAIN EVENT! Hell In The Cell Non-Title Submission Match The Golden Child (w/ Degeneration X) v. Thorne Richards (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) (announcer in ring introducing next match and "Talk Dirty to Me" by Poison starts playing...crowd is stunned...commentators wonder what's going on...crowd pops huge as "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards runs to the ring) Stevie Richards: Excuse me Mr. Ring Announcer Man, may I have this microphone? (takes mic from announcer) Thank you very much. So how the hell are all of you tonight? (crowd pops huge) I gotta tell ya, I'm doing pretty damn good myself. Now I'm sure many of you are probably wondering just what The King of Swing is doing here in the EWA. I'm sure you're all wondering just where I have been. Well first off, I've been off in Peru raising yaks. Yeah I know it sounds crazy, but it's true. It had been a dream of mine since I was little King of Swing to raise yaks. So when I finally had my chance, I took it. But unfortunately, raising yaks ain't all it's cracked up to be. It got kinda boring. I tried wrestling the yaks but you know how yaks are. They cheat like nobody's business. But enough about my yaks. The next question is just why is Dancin' Stevie Richards in the EWA? Well it's quite simple. This is the only federation in the world that has any chance of putting my incredible skills to the test. I've been to the AEWF...kick ass there. I've been to the GWF...kick as there. But this is the one place that I have yet to kick some ass. So I'm here. Now I take a look at who is here and there are some familiar faces. You got Prodigy. I beat him. You got Golden Child. I beat him. You got Thorne Richards. I beat him. Three of the top wrestlers in all the world and I, Dancin' Steive Richards the Original King of Swing, have beaten them all like runaway slaves. Now what does this all mean? I have no idea. I don't even remember what I was talking about. Oh yeah, now I remember. All of you so called superstars had best be ready. Stevie Richards is back once again and I'm ready to go. I'm here to win some titles, do some women, and party with all the great Stevie-Maniacs out there. Thank you all and please when you're all out drinking tonight remember one thing, fat girls need love too. (The crowd pops huge as Stevie leaves the ring) After that, the match actually took place, but it didn't last long without another interruption. Out from behind the entrance curtain came The Wyldsyde, dressed in his brown suede jacket. And everytime TGC or Thorne would throw a punch, execute a chokehold, or attempt any other "illegal" hold, The Wyldsyde would visibly get upset. After TGC deliberately began to choke Thorne with the ring ropes, The Wyldsyde stormed out, digusted with the match. The match went on after awhile, but finally Thorne placed TGC in The Loch Ness Lock, and TGC had no choice but to submit. Afterward, TGC had to be stretchered out of the arena with an apparent back injury. Thorne Richards d. The Golden Child with The Loch Ness Lock. (22:34) Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) v. Erik Draven Special Stipulations: Only way to win is by submission or knockout; weapons match Michaels came to the ring with Prodigy, and once Draven made it to the ring, he made it clear that this was against the agreement; Michaels responded by stating that Prodigy was never an "associate of Draven". Draven responded by flooring Michaels with a hard right, and the fight was on. Halfway through the match, Michaels' face was bloodied, but still he fought on, and with Prodigy's help, regained the advantage. Michaels nailed Draven with the Sweet Chin Music, and then picked him up, calling for Prodigy to come in. Prodigy came in, and swung a chair at Draven, but Draven ducked, and Michaels crumpled to the ground from the chair shot. Prodigy quickly exited the ring, as Draven executed Final Justice on Michaels, and covered him for the win, obtaining the advantage in their long-running feud. Erik Draven d. Shawn Michaels with Final Justice. (33:41) EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) Note: There will be a special mystery guest referee. Jim Ross: And now let's go up to Michael Buffer.
