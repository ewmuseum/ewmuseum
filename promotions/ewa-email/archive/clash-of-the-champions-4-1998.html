EWA Clash Of The Champions IV

11 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Clash Of The Champions IV Saturday, July 11, 1998 Live, from The RCA Dome in Indianapolis, Indiana. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Horsemen) Our opening contest was a dandy, as the past and present squared off in an epic battle. These two men have fought before, as Copeland was, at one point, an actual member of No Quarter. Thorne started off the match by clotheslining Copeland out of the ring, much to the delight of the announced crowd of 31,532 at the RCA Dome. Copeland crawled back into the ring, and the match was underway. Thorne used his superior technical wrestling ability to maintain an early advantage, but Copeland, in true Horsemen fashion, took the cheap way out with a hard knee to the groin. From there, Copeland took control, wrestling Thorne with a variety of powerful moves, and showing an intensity never seen before from him. However, Copeland went to the top rope, took his time showing off for the fans, and missed an attempted flying elbowdrop. Thorne quickly jumped up and capitalized, locking on the Loch Ness Lock, and forcing Copeland to submit. Thorne Richards d. Shawn Copeland by submission with the Loch Ness Lock. Thorne Richards is the new EWA Light Heavyweight Champion!! "Scrap Iron" Joe Satre v. Handey Gripwell Gripwell was dominated in this contest, as Satre fought with an intensity never before seen from him. Joe Satre d. Handey Gripwell with The Lift Off. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Vertigo These two teams looked very impressive, and fought to a time limit draw. A rematch has been possibly rumored for The King Of The Ring, but President Gates neither confirmed not denied this. Dos Equis and Veritgo battled to a time limit draw. First Round King Of The Ring Tournament Match Aaron Martin v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (EWA North American Champion, w/ Shae Marks) Martin came to the ring carrying, of all things, a potted plant. Deakins got a huge ovation from the crowd, as did Shae. The match began, and it was an even contest for most of the match...however, when Deakins started to pull away with the advantage, Martin rolled outside, grabbed his potted plant, and nailed Deke twice in the head with it. Van Smack and The Circus! then came to the ring, and attacked Martin for attacking Deke. Vic Deakins d. Aaron Martin by disqualification. Vic Deakins advances in the EWA King Of The Ring Tournament. Ruger Ice v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) Crazy Canuck was in control for parts of this match, but mostly, it was Ruger with the momentum. After hitting the Spinebuster, Ruger went for the cover, but Jack Reed ran to the ring and began pounding on him, along with Canuck. They were followed by Dr. Lander, who was carrying the Parrot. Ruger Ice d. Crazy Canuck by disqualification. Darkness (w/ Razor) v. The Black Angels Neither team showed up for the match. EWA World Television Championship Match Mayne Mann v. Jack Reed (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) This was a very good contest, in which The Horsemen and Crazy Canuck were both at ringside; as a result, neither man had interference. This contest was a very even one, with the one error going to Reed, who attempted a frankensteiner. Mayne Mann quickly reversed it, nailing the Boston Bomb and securing the victory. Mayne Mann d. Jack Reed with the Boston Bomb. Mayne Mann is the new EWA World Television Champion!! "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "The Hitman" Bret Hart It was announced that Austin had left the EWA; Hart seemed disgusted with this victory. Bret "The Hitman" Hart d. Steve Austin by forfeit. Six Man Tag Team Match Castor Troy & Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels & KG (EWA World Tag Team Champions) & "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Horsemen) This was a spectacular contest, as all six men rose to the occasion and put on one hell of a show. However, The Horsemen brought a friend to ringside with them in the form of a steel chair, and the chair played a role in this contest. NQ2 was in control when Robert Thompson threw KG on the outside of the ring. SmirtDogg was there waiting, and while Shawn Michaels distracted the official, Smirt blasted Thompson with the chair, and rolled both he and KG back in the ring. From there, KG crawled over to Thompson and made the three-count. Not even Castor Troy and 2 Cold Scorpio realized what happened, until Thompson came to and told them. The Horsemen d. NQ2 with KG's pin on Robert Thompson after a chair shot by SmirtDogg. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) This was a solid contest, but Van Smack held a slight advantage throughout, and gained the victory with the Jumbo Smack. Van Smack d. Johnny 2 Bad with the Jumbo Smack. Non-Title First Blood Match Prodigy v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion) You want to talk about explosive, edge-of-your-seat matches? Talk about this one. Makaveli came out, as promised, covered from head to toe, but Prodigy quickly ended that, starting the match by cracking Mak's skull with a chair and then ripping off the chest protector. From there, a bitter war raged between these two warriors, ending when Prodigy powerbombed Makaveli onto the announce table, causing Makaveli's back to cut open. However, Makaveli got what he planned out of this match: revenge. As soon as the official called for the bell, Makaveli took a chair to Prodigy, and spent a full 5 minutes working Prodigy over with it. Never in the EWA have we seen Makaveli in this type of rage. Prodigy d. Makaveli. Winner will recieve EWA I-C Title Shot on 7-18 "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) McCammon didn't show up. Taz d. Jack McCammon by forfeit. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match The Wyldsyde v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA World/I-C Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was a great World Title match, with both men battling for over 30 minutes without a clear cut advantage. However, The Wyldsyde changed this by throwing Bunda into the ropes and nailing The Wyldryde. The Wyldsyde went for the cover, but didn't get the three count, as Bunda strategically placed his foot on the ropes. Wyldsyde picked Bunda back up, and as the fans booed him heavily, looked around. Bunda capitalized on this crucial mistake, and executed a small package for the three-count. Jeff Bunda d. The Wyldsyde with a small package. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA World Heavyweight Championship. This page hosted by Get your own Free Home Page
