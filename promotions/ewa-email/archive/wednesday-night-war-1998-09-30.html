EWA Wednesday Night War

You Game for that punk? I'm outta here. Deader drops the mic and heads out to a BIG Pop as he leaves the ring and the matches go on Tommy Dreamer v. Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) Our first match at Wednesday Night War was a good way to kick off the show, as the fans were treatedt to a physical match between Ruger Ice and Tommy Dreamer. Dreamer held the early advantage with some nice power manuevers, but Ruger proved to be too much for Tommy, and got yet another victory, as he continues his hot streak. Ruger Ice d. Tommy Dreamer with the Spinebuster. Joe Lemon v. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia Two words...SMACK DOWN! The Rock made a great debut, destroying Lemon in short time. Draven, not hesitating at all, clobbers Paul Bearer with the chair, knocking the fat man clear from the ring to the outside. HHH and Chyna begin to walk to the ring as Copeland and Draven are still vicously beating on the fallen Kane. HHH tosses Copeland a mic. Scared or suprised, DX runs like hell to the back...**** EWA Light Heavyweight Championship First Round Tournament Match Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) This was a massacre, as Mastin wrestled about as well as the Indians played Game 1 against the Red Sox. Translation: HE GOT PUMMELED. Triple-H d. James Mastin with the Pedigree. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship First Round Tournament Match Chris Candido (w/ The Firm) v. Mac Namara This was possible the best matchup of the first round. Both Candido and Mac Namara put up great fights. In the end, though, Mac Namara was able to reverse the Blond Bombshell into a powerbomb, and with his feet on the ropes, get the pin. Mac Namara d. Chris Candido with a powerbomb. Slain v. Edge Slain started the match with a good rhythm, but Edge was incredible, and eventually put him away with the Downward Spiral. KG (w/ The New World Order) KG controlled a good portion of this contest, without outside interference. But as KG was picking Deader up off the mat, Deader quickly executed a small package, and pulled off the upset victory of the night! However, his victory was shrot-lived, as the entire nWo came out and attacked him. Deader d. KG with a small package. "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam v. Gangrel (w/ Devon) Gangrel had been escorted out of the arena for the evening after his heinous attack on Edge, so Van Dam was given a forfeit victory. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship First Round Tournament Match "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The Hate Club) v. Terry Conklin (w/ The New World Order) Conklin just didn't have anything in him for this one, and The Pimp coasted to the second round. Joe Satre d. Terry Conklin with the Pimp Splash. Curt Hennig (w/ The Firm) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin Austin has left the EWA; Hennig reluctantly accepted the forfeit win. EWA World Television Championship Match Jack Reed (w/ The Hate Club) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) Reed didn't show up to the arena, and even President Gates had no clue where he was. Venom d. Jack Reed by forfeit. Venom remains EWA World Television Champion. EWA North American Championship Match Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The New World Order) v. Michael Modest (EWA North American Champion) This was an incredibly hard-fought match, with Hart's technical wrestling skills contrasting the hardcore style of Modest. The two battled all over the ringside area, and Hart notched up a DQ win when Modest nailed him in the back with a chair. Bret Hart d. Michael Modest by disqualification. Michael Modest remains EWA North American Champion. Battle Royal For The Vacant EWA World Tag Team Championship FEATURING: The Outsiders New Demolition El Locco Jobbo & Will Haynes The Gunslinger & Shane Douglas Shawn Copeland & Erik Draven (EWA European Champion) Crazy Canuck (EWA Intercontinental Champion) & Shawn Michaels (EWA World Heavyweight Champion) New Demolition took all of four seconds to be eliminated, thrown over the top by a Crazy Canuck hiptoss. (Brown was) Next to go out were The Outsiders, when Scott Hall was nailed with a vicious lariat from Haywood Jublome. The four teams circled each other for a while, and then went at it. The Firm were eventually eliminated, when Shawn Copeland executed a dropkick on Gunslinger, sending him to the outside. Next to go were the Hate Club team members, when Erik Draven leveled Michaels with the Triple-X Bomb, elevating the final bomb high enough to bomb him out of the ring. IX and DX battled it out for over 10 more minutes, and finally, DX became the new tag team champions when Copeland threw El Locco Jobbo out after the Copelandator.
