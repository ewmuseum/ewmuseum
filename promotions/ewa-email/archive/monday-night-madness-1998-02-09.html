EWA Monday Night Madness

9 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 9, 1998 Live, from the Kiel Center in St. Louis, Missouri. Live from the Kiel Center in St. Louis, Missouri Booker T (w/ Jacquelyn & The Ghetto Warriors) v. Faarooq (w/ the Nation of Domination) "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Tiberus (w/ the Empire) Marc Anthony (w/ the Empire) v. Kama Mustafa (w/ the Nation of Domination) Augustus/Octavius (w/ the Empire) v. The Pretty Boys El Locco Jobbo v. KG (w/ Stud Stable) Stevie Ray (w/ Ghetto Warriors) v. The Widow Maker (w/ Widow) Death Sentence (w/ the Morgue) v. Prophecy (w/ Servants of Twilight) Matthew Hart (w/ Stud Stable) v. Galaxy (w/ the Morgue) Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Zach Kraay (w/ Bubba) Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Deathstar(w/ the Morgue) The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Julius (w/ the Empire) D-Lo Brown (w/ the Nation of Domination) v. Bret 'The Hitman' Hart #1 Contender Match -- Winner gets shot at Ground Zero FlashBack v. The Outsiders (w/ D-Generation X) For the EWA World Television Title: The Pessimist (w/ Servants of Twilight) v. "The Cornerstone" Triple-H (champion, w/ D-Generation X) Shawn Hart/Scott Cummings (w/ Stud Stable) v. Shawn Michaels/Erik Draven (w/ DX) Monday Night Madness Results (2/9/98) Booker T (w/ Jacquelyn & The Ghetto Warriors) v. Faarooq (w/ the Nation of Domination) Booker T d. Faarooq via the Harlem Hangover. (4:14) "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Tiberus (w/ the Empire) Tiberus d. Rocky Maivia via countout. (0:10) The Rock was attacked on his way to the ring by Faarooq. Marc Anthony (w/ the Empire) v. Kama Mustafa (w/ the Nation of Domination) Neither man showed up. Comissioner Slaughter announced that if The Nation or Marc Anthony do not roleplay for their next matches, they will be fired. Augustus/Octavius (w/ the Empire) v. The Pretty Boys The Pretty Boys d. The Empire via Josh Gardner's pin on Augustus after a powerbomb. (11:10) El Locco Jobbo v. KG (w/ Stud Stable) El Locco Jobbo d. KG via a Frankensteiner. (14:14) Stevie Ray (w/ Ghetto Warriors) v. The Widow Maker (w/ Widow) Stevie Ray d. The Widow Maker via forfeit. If the Widow Maker does not RP for his next match, he will be fired. Death Sentence (w/ the Morgue) v. Prophecy (w/ Servants of Twilight) Death Sentance d. Prophecy via Pain's pin on Travis Scott after a sidewalk slam. (2:01) Matthew Hart (w/ Stud Stable) v. Galaxy (w/ the Morgue) Matt Hart d. Galaxy via his half of the Hartstopper. (4:53) Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Zach Kraay Nero d. Zach Kraay via forfeit. Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Deathstar(w/ the Morgue) Ahmed Johnson d. Deathstar via the Pearl River Plunge. (13:19) The Golden Child (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Julius (w/ the Empire) The Golden Child d. Julius via the Royal Destroyer. (3:08) D-Lo Brown (w/ the Nation of Domination) v. Bret 'The Hitman' Hart Bret Hart d. D-Lo Brown via forfeit. #1 Contender Match -- Winner gets shot at Ground Zero FlashBack v. The Outsiders (w/ D-Generation X) The Outsiders d. FlashBack via Scott Hall's pin on Flash Funk after the Outsider's Edge. (7:11) For the EWA World Television Title: The Pessimist (w/ Servants of Twilight) v. "The Cornerstone" Triple-H (champion, w/ D-Generation X) Triple-H d. The Pessimist via The Pedigree. (0:09) Triple-H retains the TV Title. Shawn Hart/Scott Cummings (w/ Stud Stable) v. Shawn Michaels/Erik Draven (w/ DX) Degeneration X d. The Stud Stable via Erik Draven's pin on Scott Cummings after the DX Driver.
