EWA Wednesday Night War

17 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Wednesday Night War (2/17/99) February 17, 1999 Live, from Tingley Coliseum Albequerque, New Mexico 1999 King Of The Ring Tournament Qualifying Match Philip Donovan v. Raven Phillip Donovan was basically mauled in this one. There is no two ways about it. Raven looks like one pissed off individual lately. It seems he is going to make people feel his pain now, one way or another.. PhD certainly felt his pain.. Raven tortured phD for several minutes before ending it with the Evenflow DDt and getting the pinfall victory. Raven d. Phillip Donovan w/ the Evenflow DDT Non-Title Match: 'The Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels [with D-Generation X] v. E-'Nuff [EWA Hardcore Champion, with PaulRole] Something looked wrong with Hbk tonight. Perhaps he came back too soon or perhaps E-'Nuff was just one pissed off individual from what happened last week. Whatever the reason, E-'Nuff looked dominant tonight and he did basically whatever he wanted to, until he polished off Hbk with the lockdown and got the win. E-'Nuff d. Shawn Michaels with the lockdown First Round Match [1999 King Of The Ring Tournament] Tanaka v. Max Pins A great match. Max Pins was in top from tonigh and Tanaka looked extremely impressive as well, These two fought to a dead standstill for several minutes until Tankaka got the upper hand after catching Pins slipping, by nailing a DDT after Pins telegraphed a back-bodydrop. Tanaka followed up with a neckbreaker and a superplex among other maneuvers.. however he got a couple of two counts but Max Pins wasn't done yet. Max Pins landed a few rights and lefts before nailing a piledriver and a hotshot and following it up with a bulldog and going to the top rope for a frogsplash but Tanaka moved at the last possible second and Tanaka quickly hit a double arm ddt and covered his man for the win. Tanaka d Max Pins w/ A Double Arm Ddt Grudge Match Swap Meet Louie [with Grady Smith] v. 'Mr. Overhand Right' Terry Conklin [1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, with The Triple Threat] Another very good match. Swap Meet Louie was in trouble early after Conklin punished him with quick maneuver after quick manuever, landing dropkicks, standing side kicks, and spinng heel kicks among other maneuvers, but Swap Meet weathered the storm and came back with a big splash, an avalanche in the corner and a very impressive slingshot from outside the ring which flattened Conklin and it looked like a sure three count but Conklin got his foot on the ropes at the last second. The match swayed back and forth for several more minutes before Conklin missed a legdrop from the top rope and Swap Meet Louie used that opprotunity to use an inside cradle and Swap Meet Louie got the win. Swap Meet Louie d. Terry Conklin w/ an inside cradle First Round Match [1999 King Of The Ring Tournament] Haywood Jublome [with D-Generation X] v. Kane [with TheBloodfire] The match went back and forth for several long minutes with noone emerging as the clear cut leader until... Jim Ross: Kane sends Jublome into the ropes now...and Kane's got him by the throat! Vincent Ashe: This could be it, folks! Jim Ross: But Jublome with a kick to the stomach, and a DDT!! What an amazing feat by Hayw... Suddenly, the lights go out. Low chanting is heard over the PA system, and an ominous red glow falls over the arena as the entrance music of Gangrel blares over the public address system. The methodGangrel stalks to the ring, and climbs the entrance steps. He stops short of entering, however, as Jublome heads after Gangrel. Gangrel spews some of the red liquid from his goblet into the face of Jublome, blinding him. As he turns around, Kane catches him in the Tombstone, and pins him for the 3 count. Immediately afterward, Gangrel enters, and from the entrance ramp, as Gangrel begins kicking Jublome, we see Inferna pushing a long casket too the ring. Inferna positions the casket at ringside, and opens the lid. Out comes the 'man' who was seen with Gangrel about a week ago, the one he calls 'Valic'. Valic enters the ring, and he and Gangrel immediately begin attacking Jublome. with a chokeslam from Kane. Valic, after a vicious powerbomb, sets Jublome up on the top rope. Valic, towering over Jublome, stands up as well, grabs him by the throat, picks him up, and slams him from the top rope onto the Iraqi Broadcast Table. The three demonic figures, apparently satisfied with their destruction, leave the ring...meanwhile, EMTs come out and stretcher Jublome out of the arena. Non-Title Match: The Golden One [with The Triple Threat] v. 'Triple-Toke' Aaron Martin [with Inhalants Galore] The Golden One looked on top of his game while Aaron Martin looked about as bad as you can. Aaron Martin must have been punished from pillar to post. Before The Golden One ended the joke of a mach and pinned Triple Toke after the Gold Medal. The Golden One d. Aaron Martin w/ the Gold Medal EWA European Championship Match The Ghetto Boy [with Grady Smith] v. 'The Franchise' Jeff Bunda [1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions and EWA European Champion, with The Triple Threat] Was Jeff Bunda looking forward to Saturday? Or was The Ghetto Boy just getting revenge for Saturday ? Whatever the reason, Jeff Bunda was soundly defeated in this match in every phase of the game, and after a few minutes The Ghetto Boy nailed the Ghetto Driver and pinned The Franchise. The Ghetto Boy d. Jeff Bunda w/ the Ghetto Driver The Ghetto Boy is the New European Champion! Non-Title Match: Vincent Jackson [with Christina Knight] v. The Rock [EWA World Television Champion] A close match in which both men gave it their all. The match swayed back and forth several times until Draven set up The Rock for a superplex and instead got a spinning DDT from the top rope after the Rock nailed a low blow to clock the move. The Rock followed that up with a missle dropkick but as he was getting ready to hit the people's elbow, Shawn Michaels came to ringside and into the ring, Shawn Michaels landed a swinging neckbreaker before setting up for the Sweet Chin Music and when he let loose Rock ducked and Michaels hit Draven. however the ref saw this and disqualified Draven. The Rock d. Erik Draven after interference by Shawn Michaels 'The ICON' Erik Draven [w/ D-Generation X) v. Gangrel [with The Bloodfire] Neither man showed up for this match. The ref threw this matchout. The Match was ruled a double disqualification 'Mr. Every Night' Justin Hicks [with The Triple Threat] v. Prodigy [EWA Comissioner] Something was wrong with Justin Hicks tonight too. He looked pathetic. Was there some bad hotel food or what? He was thrown around the ring like a rag doll. Until Prodigy ended the drama with The Firestarter. Prodigy d. Justin Hicks w/ The Firestarter Steel Cage Match [EWA Intercontinental Championship] Haywood Jublome [with D-Generation X] v. 'The Walking Triple Threat' Jack Reed [with Paul Role/Dr. Lander] Jack Reed came out to the ring and it was announced that Haywood Jublome was on his way to the hospital and Jack Reed started to celebrate in the ring, the lights go down, a golden beam shines throughout the arena, and out comes "The Shining Star" The Golden One to a roaring ovation from the sold-out crowd. He is wheeling a birthday cake out which he brings with him into the ring... The Golden One: You know something folks? I've been waiting a good, long time to get a shot at the most prestigious title in the EWA. The top title that hasn't been tossed in a garbage can yet. You see, for those of you who don't remember, the EWA World Title was thrown away by Stevie Richards in one of the funniest damn acts I've seen. After I won it in late-December, I tried so hard to bring back some of its prestige, but damn, Gates wanted Smirt as his champion so now that piece of crap is right back down the tubes again. So does "The Shining Star" settle for second best? Oh hell no. You see Reed, the EWA Intercontinental Title might as well be named The Triple Threat Title, or even the Jeff Bunda title. After all, he held it for four months before they needed a damn rule to take it off him. Since then, the thing has been tossed around like the Television title, as mid-carders like Ruger Ice and The Crock have been running around with it. I'm out here to tell you that ends right now. Saturday, February the 20th, McNicholls Arena in Denver, Colorado, we've got a date with destiny and I'm bringing the belt back to where it belongs. You did your job tonight, and kept the strap. The only question is, can you dim the Shining Star? Oh, and by the way Reed, just so I make sure you never forget my name or who I am, I decided to bring you a little present. *TGO lifts the cake, which is totally covered with golden iciing* You see Jack, today's my birthday, and 28 years ago today your worst nightmare was born. So Reed, my birthday present to you... The Golden One stuffs the Cake right in Reed's face, and calmly steps out of the ring and begins to walk up the aisle... The Golden One: Shine the belt up Jack, cause "The Walking Triple Threat" is gonna be grounded in 3 days. Happy birthday to me... *Crowdroars* Jack Reed d. Haywood Jublome via forfeit
