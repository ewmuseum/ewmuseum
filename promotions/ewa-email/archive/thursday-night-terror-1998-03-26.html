EWA Thursday Night Terror

26 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 26, 1998 Live, from Continental Arena, East Rutherford, New Jersey. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) EWA World Television Championship Grudge Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Tommy Dreamer (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Hannibal Manson (w/ the NWC) Catcus Jack v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Non Title Match The Widow Maker v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) Stevie Richards/La Parka (w/ The DWC) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Thorne Richards (w/ Castor Troy) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Raven (w/ Jake Roberts) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Champion, w/ Degeneration X) The Gunslinger (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) KG (w/ Sunny) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. FlashBack (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) New Jack/SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) v. Dos Equis (w/ The NWC) Four Corners Elimination Match Winner will recieve EWA World Title shot at EWA Survivor Series Vic Deakins (w/ The NWC) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Van Smack (w/ The NWC) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) Deathstar started off this match with a cheap shot before the bell, but his momentum wouldn't last long. Bunda flipped out of an attempt at a powerbomb, and took control, not taking long to end the match with the Apoclypse Drop. Jeff Bunda d. Deathstar with the Apoclypse Drop. (5:10) EWA World Television Championship Grudge Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) This match didn't disappoint anyone, as Austin and Eclipse battled for nearly 20 minutes. Eclipse was going to the top rope in an attempt for a double-ax handle, and as he jumped off, Austin hit him square in the stomach, and then delivered the Stone Cold Stunner. Steve Austin d. Eclipse with the Stone Cold Stunner. (19:42) Steve Austin is the new EWA World Television Champion!! Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Galaxy attempted to use his high-flying moves in this match, but to no avail, as The Mastermind simply dodged or countered most of them. Castor Troy d. Galaxy with a double-underhook suplex. (7:21) Tommy Dreamer (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Hannibal Manson (w/ the NWC) This match began before the bell, and never officially got started, as the two men brawled outside to a double countout. A rematch has been signed for Monday Night Madness. Tommy Dreamer and Hannibal Manson battled to a double countout. (0:10) Catcus Jack v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) The hardcore legend brought a dumpster to the entrance ramp, but it never came into play. In fact, no foreign objects were involved, as Koo did a nice job of keeping the match inside the ring. And by doing so, he slowly wore down Catcus Jack, hit him with The Baby Bop, and obtained the victory. KooKabura d. Cactus Jack with the Baby Bop. (8:49) Aaron Martin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Aaron Martin completely dominated this match, making a very impressive debut. The second straight disappointment for young Harley coming off his one-month break. Aaron Martin d. Harley Hart with the Shoulderbreaker. (4:02) Non Title Match The Widow Maker v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) The Widow Maker never had his chance, and Paul Bearer even kept his word to end the match in under 21 seconds. Kane d. The Widow Maker with the Tombstone Piledriver. (0:17) Stevie Richards/La Parka (w/ The DWC) v. The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) This was a hard-fought battle, and Stevie Richards looked extremely impressive in his EWA debut. However, La Parka and Stevie hadn't had much experience tagging up together, and the Snow brothers capitalized on this, isloating La Parka in their corner, and eventually obtaining the win. The Reckoning d. The NWC with Noel Snow's pin on La Parka after the Apocalypse. (13:23) The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Thorne Richards (w/ Castor Troy) Another spectacular match; this one ended in a time-limit draw. Thorne Richards and The Pessimist battled to a time-limit draw. (30:00) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Raven (w/ Jake Roberts) v. The Golden Child (EWA U.S. Champion, w/ Degeneration X) This was an outstanding match, until Raven brought in the chair. Raven set the chair up, and it wasn't in use for awhile, as GC got his second wind. But when GC when to give Raven a vertical suplex, Raven saw the opportunity, and gave GC the Evenflow...right onto the chair. Referee Randy Anderson immediately called for the bell, as Raven left pretty quickly afterward. GC suffered a rather large laceration on his forehead, but has been cleared to wrestle on MNM. The Golden Child d. Raven by disqualification. (18:37) The Golden Child retains the EWA United States Heavyweight Title. The Gunslinger (w/ Jake Roberts) v. Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) This was an outstanding match, marred by Degeneration X interference. Gunslinger was on the outside, when Shawn Michaels nailed him with the Sweet Chin Music. Shawn then tossed him back in the ring, as Prodigy picked him up and delivered the Firestarter. Prodigy d. The Gunslinger with the Firestarter. (16:23) KG (w/ Sunny) v. Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat) KG looked waaaaaaaay off of his game tonight, as Hicks had no trouble in dismantling him. After the match, however, Shawn Michaels and Prodigy came to the ring. Shawn just stared at Hicks, and then Prodigy picked up KG and DDT'd him to the mat. Shawn Michaels hit the Sweet Chin Music, and they then left. Justin Hicks d. KG with a swinging neckbreaker. (2:19) EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) v. FlashBack (EWA U.S. Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) The Hart Brothers seemed way off tonight, (it seems that was in the air tonight) and FlashBack escaped with an easy victory. FlashBack d. The Hart Brothers with Flash Funk's pin on Matt Hart after the Funky Flash Splash. (6:31) FlashBack retains the EWA U.S. Tag Team Titles. New Jack/SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) v. Dos Equis (w/ The NWC) This was a good match for a while, but in the end, Smirt simply took control, hitting the Air Smirty on Tecate Jake. SmirtDogg/New Jack d. Dos Equis with Smirt's pin on Tecate Jake after the Air Smirty. (10:16) Four Corners Elimination Match Winner will recieve EWA World Title shot at EWA Survivor Series Vic Deakins (w/ The NWC) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. Van Smack (w/ The NWC) This match was divided into two for the early part, as Cummings and Flair teamed up against Deakins and Van Smack. In typical Flair fashion, however, he soon turned on Cummings, placed him in the Figure Four, and eliminated him. Deakins came in next, and pounded on Flair for awhile, but Deke was then tossed over the top rope by Flair. Deke was tossed onto Cummings, and Cummings became very angry. The two got into a fight on the outside, and officials tried to break it up. While they were distracted, Van Smack waffled Flair with a chair, and executed the Smack Down on him. Referee Nick Patrick counted, but Flair's foot reached the ropes, and the match continued. Patrick went back to help break up the brawl with the others, and Flair returned with a chair shot of his own onto Van Smack, and placed him in the Figure Four. Jim Ross: Flair's got Van Smack in the Figure Four! Nick Patrick turns around, and sees that Van Smack is out cold! He's stopping the match! Larry Zybysko: The Nature Boy has done it! JR: An outstanding effort by all four men, but with the help of Van Smack's chair, "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair has won the match, and he's going on to face... *The lights suddenly go out, and cigarette lighters come on all over the arena. Organ music blares over the speakers, and then a huge red pyrotechnics display blows out of the floor. Kane stalks out from behind the curtain, with Paul Bearer behind him.* JR: Through hellfire and brimstone, here comes the World Champion Kane!!! And what the hell is he doing here? Paul Bearer is in tow, as Kane steps over the top rope! Flair and Van Smack are barely even able to stand!!! They just fought the battle of their lives!!! This isn't right dammit! Bearer just gave the signal and Kane chokeslams Van Smack to the mat!!! Good god! And now he walks over to Ric Flair, the man he has to face at the Survivor Series for the title...but I'm not sure he's even going to make it that far!!! Bearer just signaled for a tombstone!!! No...NO!!! Kane just drilled the tombstone on Flair!!! And look at the carnage!!! Paul Bearer: Well, well, well...I TOLD YOU DIDN'T I?!?! I told you that winning this match was a DEATH SENTENCE!!! So now that you've been introduced to my Kane, Flair, remember his wrath! And as for you, Van Smack, well you just happened to be in the WRONG place at the WRONG time!!! So go ahead, laugh at the fat man now!!! I can't HEAR YOU!!! That's what I thought...NOTHING!!! And Nature Boy...*Bearer grins* goodnight!!! *Kane's music blares over the speakers again, and Bearer points the way out. Kane leaves, and the bodies are still in the ring. The lights return to white, and no one has moved.* JR: That is the most evil, sick and twisted man you will ever meet, and he controls perhaps the most powerful man we've ever witnessed. Folks, we're out of time, and the question still remains...can anybody stop Kane? *Camera fades to black*
