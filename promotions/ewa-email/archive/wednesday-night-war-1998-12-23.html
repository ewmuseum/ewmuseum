EWA Wednesday Night War

EWA Wednesday Night War (12/23/98) December 23, 1998 Live, from the Bradley Center Milwaukee, Wisconsin EARLIER TODAY (The camera fades in, and we see President Gates, sitting in his office. He is talking on the telephone, and his face is contorted with rage.) President Gates: WHAT?? How can he do that?? That contract was from nine damn months ago?? THIS CANNOT HAPPEN! I want you to hire whoever you have to hire...it doesn't matter. That contract is not valid, and there's no WAY he's getting that position back! Now do something about it!! (Gates slams the phone down, and we fade to black.) (The lights go down in the sold-out Bradley Center in Milwaukee, Wisconsin as the crowd erupts to kick off EWA's Wednesday Night War. Those cheers quickly change to boos, however, as the golden beam begins to shine throughout the arena and 'Golden Showers' blares over the P.A. Through the curtains emerges the "Shining Star," noticeably alone, donning the golden tights but with one of those plastic EWA Title belts wrapped around his waist...) The Golden One: Welcome everyone, to the hottest thing to hit professional wrestling since Duane Gates, yours truly, "The Corporate Star," The Golden One. (crowd boos) Now I know everyone is probably thinking that I sold-out, took the low road by joining the Corporate Empire. Well, I prefer to look at it as taking out an insurance policy. You see, with the faction warfare that's starting to take place again in the EWA, I decided it would be in my best interest to find me some backup. Enter President Gates and his Corporate Empire. I mean, how can you get any better than that in the backup department. So before I had my first EWA World Heavyweight Title shot against Stevie Wonder, I had me a little conversation with the head honcho, the bill payer, Duane Gates. We had a good long talk, and he told me exactly what he had in store for everyone in the EWA, with a few exceptions of course. When I heard how well he had planned this out, I just couldn't pass up an opportunity like this. So Richards beats me, but BAM, the loser of the match is given a world title shot on December 30th. You think that was by coincidence, folks? Haha, guess again. Draven gets the living hell beat out of him by The Rock and "God's Gift to Wrestling," and Vincent Jackson, and KG, and the Big Bossman, and who comes out to save the day? "The Corporate Star." We played Draven for the fool he is. Acting all mean and tough as the leader of D-Generation X, yet underneath that facade, he's nothing but a pansy. So when I went on the hottest show to hit the air waves in television history, the "Dogg Pound," and praised the worthy World Champion, I was laughing my ass off inside. And just like a good fisherman, I reeled Draven in hook, line, and sinker. I even "laid out" my best friend SmirtDogg, hitting him with the Gold Medal on his Corporate Couch. Do you think that actually hurt "God's Gift to Wrestling?" Haha, I don't think so. That couch provided for such a soft damn landing it makes the wrestling mat look like a cement floor. But when Smirt "writhed" in pain, Draven was heading further and further towards his downfall. So the big tag match was signed, haha, Jackson and Smirt vs. me and the ICON. The tag match of the century turns into one helluva Corporate Scam. To actually think I would hit my man SmirtDogg with a chair, man you've gotta be stupid. And that's exactly what Erik Draven is, because he bit the line and chomped a little too hard. So we lay him out, leave him lying in the ring like the worthless piece of garbage that he is, but where does that leave your "Corporate Star," The Golden One. Preparing for his crowning night on December 30th, that's where. You see folks, Erik Draven is meat. If he can't even get along with "The Man Without a Friend" Shawn Copeland, he can't get along with anybody. I'm walking into War next week with all the confidence in the world. I'm floating in the unburstable bubble, set to explode all over you. I don't need the Corporate Empire to stand there by my side to beat you, because it's really not that hard. No, Erik, the Corporate Empire is gonna sit back in our Corporate Dressingroom, watching the Corporate Television, with the Corporate Champagne on ice, waiting for the Corporate Champion to arrive with the gold. It's about time I start living up to my name and you start paying for yours. I'll show you exactly what an Empire does with an ICON, and believe me, it won't be pretty. This little plastic piece of trash, TGO motions to the plastic EWA Title belt around his waist will go with me to the ring, but by the end of the night, I'll have traded it in for the real deal. Ladies and gentlemen, your next EWA World Heavyweight Champion, "The Corporate Star," The Golden One. And rest assured, December 30th will be a GOL... No, no no. December 30th will be a CORPORATE night!!! Justice (of Team U.S.A.) v. The Big Bossman (w/ The Corporate Empire) The word 'ugly' wouldn't BEGIN to do this match justice. Justice via the Boss Man Slam. Philip Donovan v. "Triple-Toke" Aaron Martin PhD might be 'Pretty Fly For a White Guy', but he had NO rhythm tonight whatsoever... "The Natural One" Michael Modest v. Sting In a very well-fought matchup, Modest displayed his new-found hardcore talents, and put them to good use...in fact putting Sting through the Iraqi announcers' table, which the ref chose to overlook. Once the fight got back into the ring, however, the advantage swung back in Sting's favor...the Lone Wolf took the fight right to Modest, pressing him high overhead in a gorilla press, and dropping him face-first in the center of the ring. He screamed to the crowd, and signalled for the Scorpion Deathlock...and the lights fell dim. 'Wherever I May Roam' began to thunder over the PA, and when the lights flicked back on, Vincent Jackson was standing right behind Sting, while Christina Knight was distracting the ref... Jackson grabbed Sting by the throat and delivered a huge chokeslam, leaving the Lone Wolf prone in the center of the ring. Modest made the quick cover, but instead got pulled off by Modest and chokeslammed as well. The ref threw out the match, ruling it a double-DQ. *** Michael Modest v. Suddenly, the lights went PITCH black, and when they returned...there was no sign of Jackson. Michaels hit a second Superkick on the Rock, and got the 3-count for the victory. *** HHH/HBK d. Rock/KG via HBK's Sweet Chin Music on the Rock. EWA European Championship Match Haywood Jublome (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ The Triple Threat) This match had all the makings of one of the greatest title matches in EWA history. Both men poured out over 25 minutes of non-stop action, with neither ever, at any point, gaining a clear-cut advantage. About mid-way through, Shawn Copeland and Justin Hicks came to the ring, to boos from the crowd...the boos quickly changed to cheers, however, when Erik Draven and Hunter Hearst Helmsley arrived to even the odds. Both men kicked out of each other's finishers, and when Jublome attempted his submission hold, The Chronic, Justin Hicks sprung to the ring apron. Immediately, Erik Draven, chair in hand, climbed in the ring, and slightly, not even enough to knock him down, tapped Jublome on the back with the chair. The referee turned, and saw the very tail-end of it, and, incredibly, disqualified Jeff Bunda. The Triple Threat was irate, dragging the referee to the outside and pummeling him. Around this time, Howard Finkle took the microphone to announce the decision. Howard Finkle: The winner of this bout, as a result of a disqualification, and STILL EWA European Champion, Haywood...Jublome! However, when it should have been the music of D-Generation X playing, instead, "Rock You Like A Hurricane" blared over the PA. The arena erupted in boos, as President Gates appeared at the entrance ramp. President Gates: Oh no, Jublome. No celebrating for you. You see, you have no business with that title. As much as it pains me to say it, The Franchise, Jeff Bunda, is the rightful EWA European Champion. So, Haywood? I hope you don't have anything planned for Christmas, because you're going to need to be training for December 30, because you will once again be putting the EWA European Championship on the line...in the HELL IN THE CELL!! Fade to black.
