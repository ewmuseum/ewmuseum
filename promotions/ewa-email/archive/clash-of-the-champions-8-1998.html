EWA Clash Of The Champions VIII

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in to the Orlando Arena, where fireworks are going off above the ring for the Clash Of The Champions VIII. The camera pans around the arena, and then focuses in on the broadcast table.) Jim Ross: Welcome everyone, to the eight installment of the EWA's Clash Of The Champions!! I'm good ol' JR, alongside my broadcast colleague Vincent Ashe, and what an evening we have in store for you tonight! Vincent Ashe: Indeed, Jim! We ha...LOOK AT THAT!! (The camera pans to the crowd, where, shockingly, sits none other than Raven.) JR: We haven't seen Raven at an EWA arena since WrestleCade I! What a shocker! What's he doing here? VA: We can only speculate, Jim...let's go up to Howard Finkle! Finkle: Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introdu... *From the ringside area hops up Raven, who rolls into the ring and grabs the microphone out of Pinzers hands. He takes it, and slouches down into the corner as usual, and apparently has something to say.* Raven: Not long ago, I stated that I would reveal the identity of that soul which has tormented me for so long, and that the time of his extinction was near. However, now I come to find that he has supposedly left this Alliance, and gone on to other things. Well, I have only six words for this attrocious act...WHAT ABOUT ME?!?! WHAT ABOUT RAVEN?!?! PRODIGY!!! I know you can hear me where ever you are, and if you think for one moment that running away from me is the escape to your destiny with me, you have made a very serious error! There is no way that I will let the pain and torment that I have endured because of you rest unanswered! So this is how it's going to be, so listen close. Everyone in the EWA...you tell Prodigy where ever he might be, to get his ass back here RIGHT NOW!!! For, every card that goes by and Prodigy hasn't been handed to me for his sacrifice, I will decimate some mother *beep*er into the private hell that they deserve for being part of this worthless society! PRODIGY, YOU GET BACK HERE YOU STUPID PEICE OF *BEEP*!!! Call me collect, show your worthless hide up anytime, but make no mistake I am not going anywhere until you show up. *The crowd starts to boo Raven and chant Prodigy's name, causing Raven to put his hands over his ears.* Raven: SHUT UP!!! It's because of the lower forms of life like you ignorant inbred people that Prodigy is exists. And that is something that I intend to rectify as soon as Prodigy has the guts to show his yellow hide back here again! Gates...find him, make the match happen, or the resulting chaos will not be my responsibility, and you will have the injuried and mournings of an entire company to deal with, because you weren't smart enough to keep Prodigy around. I'm not going anywhere Prodigy...just come to me, for I will set you free of your agony, by gaining my revenge, and showing this ludicrous society just how pathetic you really are. Remember my vow, and take it to your perverbial bank! Quote the Raven, NEVERMORE!!! *With that, Raven slams the microphone on the ground, and suddenly Joe Lemon walks out from the entrance way. He and Raven exchange words, and then suddenly Raven hits the Even Flow DDT on him. He then gets a chair from the ringside area, and bashes Lemon over the practically unconscious Lemon, causing him to bleed from his forehead.* Raven: Let this be an example to anyone out there who thinks that I'm going to have some sort of Flock, Nest or other following. I don't need any one of you pitiful souls latching on to my coattails...I am my own man, and the rest of you can go straight to hell! *With that, Raven falls between the ropes, and heads off through the crowd and out of the arena* James Mastin v. KooKabura (w/ Sara) Our opening match was somewhat delayed, due to the fact that KooKabura's plane wasn't able to land in the Orlando area due to weather. Therefore, this match has been rescheduled for Tuesday Night War. (The WNW for this week is being moved to Tuesday, as a result of a scheduling conflict with the WNW writer for Wednesday. The roleplaying deadline will be at 9:00 PM EST) Da Bomb v. The Nightmare Squad If Da Bomb does not roleplay, they will be banned from the EWA. And what a shocker...Da Bomb doesn't bother to show. As a result, Van Smack and Vic Deakins have joined Morbid Angel as being the only men with the 'distinction' of being banned from the EWA. However, the Nightmare Squad did not show up either, so this match ended in a draw. "The Extremist" Erik Moon v. Haywood Jublome Finally...a match!! Albeit a squash of a match...Moon never really seemed to have a game plan against the returning EWA star, and as a result, Jublome pretty much walked all over him, ending the match with his patented top rope brainbuster. (The Ho Checka) Haywood Jublome d. Erik Moon with the Ho Checka. EWA World Television Championship Match "The Natural One" Michael Modest (w/ Thorne Richards) v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Sunny) Now this was a dandy, folks. These two have a huge history, swapping the North American Title a couple of months back, and the rivalry between the two was never more evident than in this contest. KG started off in control, using a high-flying attack to thwart Modest's attempts to slow the match down. KG was able to hit one of his most athletic moves ever as well, a Frankensteiner from the top rope onto Modest, who was standing on the outside of the ring. There were some scattered cheers for KG, as he was competing in his hometown, but he seemed to disregard them entirely, as after the Frankensteiner, he slipped a cable cord around Modest's neck and began to choke the life out of him. Not to be outdone, however, was Modest...as KG was bringing him back in the ring, Modest was able to hit a vicious low blow, changing the tide entirely. Modest then went to work on KG's left knee, attempting to ground the cruiserweight, and did so successfully with a Figure four that nearly counted KG's shoulders down. KG was able to get to the ropes, though, and a few moments later, reverse an irish whip into a wrap-around DDT. From there, KG signaled to the crowd, and drug Modest to the top rope. KG attempted a top-rope Frankensteiner, but Modest, amazingly, reversed it into a thunderous powerbomb. Modest held onto KG, and, with a little leverage gained from putting his feet on the ropes, was able to attempt a pin on KG. The referee counted one...two...and yet KG still kicked out, amazingly enough. At this point, Modest's partner, Thorne Richards, slowly began to walk to ringside. KG came back from the powerbomb with a thunderous burst of offense, slamming Modest to the ground with a spinebuster. KG then jumped to the top, and executed a quick moonsault. He appeared to go for the pin, but then saw Thorne, who was standing on the ring apron, cast in hand (but surprisingly, not on his arm). KG went to smack Thorne, but missed, and Thorne blew into the cast. A load of chalk flew out of the open sided cast, right into KG's face. KG stumbled around wildly as Thorne dropped to the outside, and this allowed Modest the opportunity to nail the Drop Zone, make the cover, and become the new EWA World Television Champion! Michael Modest d. KG with the DropZone. Michael Modest is the new EWA World Television Champion! Johnny Napalm (w/ Angel) v. Christian (w/ The Kindred) Obviously, Christian was not able to 'compete' in this match, due to...well...you could say he had digestion problems. Anyway, President Gates sent out Deader to replace him in this contest, and Deader was promptly squashed by Napalm, who picked up his second EWA victory in as many matches. Johnny Napalm d. Deader with the Napalm Bomb. Galaxy v. The Golden One (w/ Carrie) Normally, I would refer to this match as a squash. However, that doesn't even do this one justice, because what The Golden One did to Galaxy was worse than a squash. Needless to say, Galaxy was on the receiving end of both a Gold Medal and the Golden Edge. The Golden One d. Galaxy with the Golden Edge. Jim Ross: Good god. You never know what's going to happen in the EWA. I've just been informed that there's something going on in the back locker room area and it concerns Harlem Heat. Vincent Ashe: Maybe they're mixing it up with the current EWA World Tag Team Champions -- The Extreme Dream Machine? Jim Ross: Who knows?? We're trying to get a camera back there to see what's going on. Hold on. There's no telling what we're about to witness. This could be ugly. *Suddenly the 2 announcers disappear off of our TV screens and are replaced with a 'live' scene originating from the locker room area. Camera focuses in on Booker T and Stevie Ray -- whom both are laying on the floor in a bloody heap. We see a lead pipe striking down on them repeatedly. The sequence sees Booker T being nailed with the object and then Stevie Ray. Due to the camera angle we don't see the perpretator of this brutal and heinous act. Jim Ross comments while the lead pipe continues to make contact with the Harlem Heat duo.* Jim Ross: GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY, Harlem Heat is DEAD! They're DEAD! I hope my momma isn't watching I don't know if her heart could take this. Who in the heck would do something like this??? Somebody stop that madman??? Whoever he is??? Get that weapon out of his hands! Vincent Ashe: Why don't you go ahead and try to take it away. Jim Ross: That's not part of my job description. Besides, I like being in one piece. Thank you very much. *The lead pipe is dropped onto the concrete floor. Once the camera angle expands we see the attacker. The man responsible for everything that just happened.* Jim Ross: NOOOO, it can't be. That's...that's...SMIRTDOGG! What's he doing here???? I never thought I'd see that man again. Vincent Ashe: Neither did I. Looks like we're going to hear from him also. He's heading towards the ring. Jim Ross: I never thought I'd see the day where Smirt would purposely and so violently attack Harlem Heat. This is unbelievable. Vincent Ashe: We're about to find out why. Smirt has the "Golden Microphone" in his hand and he's ready to speak. Jim Ross: Would you listen to this crowd??? They're telling Smirt exactly what they think of him. *Camera zooms in on the center of the squared circle -- where we see "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg standing with an arrogant sneer upon his face. The EWA fans are chanting "SMIRTDOGG SUCKS!" while Smirt takes in the moment -- knowing that only he can draw this much heat. Smirt puts the "Golden Microphone" up close to his mouth and begins to speak in his normal arrogant and confident manner.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Some of you may think that you're dreaming but this is indeed reality. I, Smirt-D-O-Double G, am standing before you all as an official EWA wrestler. EWA President Duane Gates signed me to a multi-million dollar contract earlier today. I informed him that my first order of business would be to "smother" the HEAT. Gates apparently didn't have any objections to the idea because he he would have voiced them to me. So here I am. *Smirt stretches out* It's great to be home again. Harlem Heat stated that they didn't need me -- well I think I just proved otherwise. Most of you know about the plan 'The Golden Child' and I devised which led to the destruction of the Ghetto Warriors. Foxy Brown, Lil'Kim, DMX and New Jack are all being detained in the dungeon of Utica's Golden Palace. Poor them. It almost brings a tear to my eye when I think about it. ALMOST! *Smirt laughs* Now, tonight, the final two pieces of the Ghetto Warrior puzzle have been done away with. Booker T and Stevie Ray will never wrestle again. I ended their careers. I put HARLEM HEAT out of commission indefinitely. I am the man! I'm an I.C.O.N.! I'm the greatest wrestler ever to compete in the EWA! *Crowd chants: Die! Smirt! Die! A few projectiles are flung into the squared circle by enraged EWA fans. However, none of them hit their intended target. Smirt continues.* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Now I'd like to take the opportunity to speak about my homie KG. I've kept a close eye on the EWA during my time away and I noticed one thing not to my liking. I heard KG say in one of his interviews that he has the 'most wins' in the EWA. He might have gone as far to state that he possessed the most victories in EWA history. Well I had enough of that false propaganda and bro even with all the time I've spent away from the EWA -- I have one more win than you and 24 less losses. KG -- just letting you know that it's time to move over because "God's Gift to Wrestling" is back and badder than ever. I'm the top "dawg". Respect that or we're gonna throw down one more time. Starting now, December 5th, 1998, I'm on the road heading right for the EWA World Heavyweight Championship. I've held the title before and I'm more than qualified to become a 2-time EWA World Heavyweight Champion. Now I'm not going to come out here and demand for "World Title Shots" right off the bat. That would be ludicrous and it'll be too much like what Haywood Jublome and Shawn Copeland are doing. I'm taking a different route. I'm going to earn the opportunity through hard work and dedication. I'm a glorified legend in the wrestling world so I know that my time will come. I shall take care of business and wait. I'll stick it out. And when I receive what I've earned -- I'll capture wrestling's most prestigious title once again. I will be the EWA World Heavyweight Champion just as the new year is being celebrated. Give or take a couple of weeks. Only thing left to say is -- bring on whatever competition awaits for me. I'll take them all down on my path to glory. No one can stop what is imminent. Nobody will prevent me from my goal and the standards I've set for myself. You're looking at the soon-to-be EWA World Heavyweight Champion. I'm willing to go through whatever means I need to in order to obtain that honour. Learn to love me because I'm here to stay. Until next time... Au Revoir! *SmirtDogg exits the squared circle and makes his way to the back -- leaving us all in a state of shock.* EWA European Championship Triple Threat Match "The Genuine Athlete" Joshua Gardner v. Thorne Richards v. The Rock On paper, this could've been one of the best matches of the night. Gardner had an impressive performance against DX on Wednesday, Thorne Richards has been climbing, slowly but surely, back up the ranks of the EWA, and The Rock had been undefeated up until KG's victory on WNW for the TV Title. However, it was not to be. Gardner was eliminated very quickly by Richards, who took him out with a page from the start of his career -- The London Bridge. After that, it was The Rock and Thorne. The Rock got in a couple of offensive moves, but the veteran Richards was simply too overwhelming tonight, and The Rock ended up on the wrong side of another London Bridge. Three count, and once again, we have a brand new EWA European Champion...(and hey, this one's actually from Europe! :)) Thorne Richards d. The Rock and Joshua Gardner with the London Bridge. Thorne Richards is the new EWA European Champion!! "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ D-Generation X) v. Gangrel (w/ The Kindred) Nash was in this one for personal reasons, after the Kindred's heinous, disgusting attack on Scott Hall on Wednesday Night War. Nash started out in total dominance, hitting the Big Boot and knocking Gangrel right out of the ring. However, it was interference from Edge that turned the match around...as the official was checking Gangrel on the outside, Edge snuck in the ring, and hit the Downward Spiral on Big Sexy. Gangrel climbed back in the ring, and went to work on Nash...but not for long, as Shawn Michaels ran to the aid of Nash. Michaels slid in the ring, while Edge met him, and a four man brawl soon erupted. Kevin Nash and Gangrel battled to a double disqualification. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) v. Edge (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Kindred) Another fantastic match. The bout started off with Edge in complete control, showing a vicious side that, up to this point, he had never displayed before. Edge was cold, calculating, and cruel, dropping Conklin on the guardrail repeatedly. However, Conklin was able to mount a comeback with the thunderous ovation from the crowd, and nailed Edge with a series of clotheslines. Conklin went for the Overhand Right, but Edge blocked it, and then hit the Downward Spiral. Edge went for the cover, but Conklin had a foot on the ropes, and the count was broken up. Edge then picked up Conklin, and went for a powerbomb, but amazingly, Conklin flipped over Edge, and reversed it into a sunset flip, getting a two count. From there, Edge got up, and Conklin blasted him with the Overhand Right. However, Conklin did not go for the pin...instead, he went to the top rope. We were told that this was Conklin's new move, and a beauty the Counout was, as he executed a shooting star press onto the prone form of Edge. However, at the last second, Gangrel reached in, and pulled Conklin off of Edge. Conklin began to yell at Gangrel, and Edge, from behind, went for a clothesline. Conklin ducked, and attempted a front Russian Legsweep (Kanyon's Flatliner, from Dubba-C-Dubba), but Edge blocked it, and again hit the Downward Spiral, this time getting the three count. Edge d. Terry Conklin with the Downward Spiral. Edge remains EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion. Triple Threat Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Whisper) v. The Rock v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) The recent series of "As DX Turns" concluded here tonight, with this largely-hyped Triple Threat match. Michaels was accompanied to the ring by Kevin Nash, Whisper, and Chyna, who, as noted previous to the event, was serving as his one-night bodyguard. Helmsley was escorted out by Draven, who did not look happy at all. The Rock then came out to his usual ovation, and the match was underway. All three men were hesitant at first, and then Michaels and Helmsley went to lock up...but both turned, and nailed The Rock!! To the shock (and delight) of the capacity crowd, both men began laughing hysterically, and embraced in the ring! The two began working over the Rock together, and ended it off with Michaels delivering the Sweet Chin Music to The Rock, knocking him out of the ring. Michaels then took the microphone. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels: We fooled everybody again! Did anyone really think that the Cornerstone and The Heartbreak Kid were ever going to split up? Crocky, you obviously did, and for being so stupid...we've got two words for ya... Michaels then held the microphone to the crowd, who happily obliged, yelling "SUCK IT!!". DX's music hit, and the whole group walked back together, laughing the whole way. No Disqualification Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ D-Generation X) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) Shawn Copeland came out first, with a chair in hand...obviously prepared for the no disqualification clause in the contract. Jackson followed with his normal thunderous ovation. Copeland wasted no time, cracking the chair over Jackson's head to start the match. Copeland put the chair against the turnbuckle, and attempted an Irish Whip into it, but Jackson reversed it into a belly to belly suplex. Jackson then went to work, setting Copeland in the corner and pummeling him with rights and lefts. At this point, Erik Draven came to teh ring to 'watch' Jackson in action. Jackson simply stared at him, and started in on Copeland again...meanwhile, Beulah kept a fair distance on the outside away from Christina Knight. Copeland began fighting back with some punches of his own, and ran against the ropes, coming back with a flying clothesline that started the big man wobbling. Copeland went for a second one, but Jackson caught him by the throat, and chokeslammed him hard to the mat. Jackson then set Copeland on the top rope, but instead of facing away from the ring for the Fallen Angel, he faced toward it...and pointed to the broadcast table. In a previous interview, Jackson made reference to giving Copeland the Fallen Angel onto the broadcast table...now, in the sudden silence of the arena, Jackson picked Copeland up, and powerbombed him onto the Italian Broadcast table. Jackson went to throw Copeland back in the ring, and was going for a pin, when Draven decided he had seen enough. Draven stepped over the top rope, and immediately started brawling with Jackson. Copeland from behind, summoned the energy to begin attacking Jackson. It took both men nearly five minutes to get Jackson down, but once they got him down, Copeland immediately went to the top, and hit the Money Shot. Draven followed that up with a DX Driver, and Copeland followed with a final Money Shot. Copeland then climbed on top of Jackson to get one...two...and three, for the huge victory...Copeland's biggest win since returning to the EWA, possibly. Shawn Copeland d. JR: And here comes what, in all likelihood, is the new EWA Champion, The ICON Erik Draven. VA: Well, Stevie threw down the title, so obviously, you're looking at the new champ! Howard Finkle: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing...from Charlotte, North Carolina, weight 335 pounds, "The ICON" Erik Draven! JR: Draven is in the ring...and... (The opening chords to "Intergalactic" by the Beastie Boys blare over the PA, as EWA President Duane Gates walks out to the ring, carrying the World Title in his hand. He steps inside the ring, and takes out a microphone.) Gates: Erik Draven, this may not be the way you wanted to become the 4 time EWA Champion, but you have to take what life throws at you. You've busted your ass for these people, night in and night out, and I respect that in a world champion. So, without any further ado, ladies and gentlemen, I give to you, the new EWA World Champion, The ICON, Erik Draven!! Gates handed Draven the title. Draven hesitated for a moment, then took it, and held it high above his head...only to crumble to the mat. Standing behind him, with a baseball bat in hand, was none other than SmirtDogg! SmirtDogg began stomping on Draven, to the shock on Gates' face..and the shocked look on his face slowly turned into a grin, as Gates began stomping on Draven with him! A thunderous chorus of boos reigned through the arena, as both Gates and Smirt continued to stomp on Draven. Suddenly, a roar from the crowd came up, and the camera panned to the entrance ramp, as none other than the Big Bossman ran out, in a police outfit with nightstick in hand! Bossman ran to the ring, and began twirling the nightstick, backing Gates and Smirt away...then the Bossman took the nightstick, and cracked Draven in the skull with it. The three began kicking Draven once again, and all embraced in the ring...finally, Gates took the microphone. President Gates: Take that, Draven! (Gates laughs hysterically) Did all of you really believe I had changed?! You idiots!! Draven, you of all people should've known better...but obviously you didn't! All that crap about bringing change to the EWA...a bunch of BS I made up to get that title away from you. And Draven? It doesn't really matter if you have the title now or not, because the man to my left can take it away from you just as quick as I can snap my fingers. Did any of you idiots actually expect SmirtDogg to even come back, much less align himself with Duane Gates? (Gates laughs) And the Big Bossman? Who would have expected that one? (Gates laughs again.) Smirt, I think it's time you talk to these idiots out here...tell them why you decided to form the greatest alliance ever. *As SmirtDogg reveals the "Golden Microphone" from out of his pocket -- the EWA fans burst out with a thunderous amount of boos. SmirtDogg just sneers as per usual and takes in the moment. "God's Gift to Wrestling" draws the microphone close to his mouth and begins to speak.* SmirtDogg: Now I know you all didn't expect me to make an appearance here tonight. I shocked you all with my vile attack on Booker T and Stevie Ray. You thought that would be the last you saw of me this evening. Well you're all obviously DEAD wrong. Along with Gates -- we just pulled off the ultimate surprise in pro-wrestling today. Who would have thought that these two guys *Smirt gestures towards Gates and the Bossman* and I would form a coalition??? After all the legit 'heat' that has gone down between Duane and I -- what you see here tonight is nothing short of amazing. However, Duane Gates and I have one common goal. We both abhor Erik Draven with a passion. We both hate the Sloth-looking ogre with all our guts. EWA President Duane Gates comprehends the fact that Erik Draven isn't the type of person fit for being the World Champion of this federation. For one, he apparently doesn't know what a shower is because I've yet to come within 50 feet of Erik Draven without the big idiot smelling like one giant -- stinky -- pile of sh*t. I'm allowed to say that on TV, right chief? *President Gates nods and all three laugh. Smirt continues.* SmirtDogg: Another reason that Draven isn't the right man to lead this federation is because he doesn't have what it takes anymore to be a "World Title Holder". Gates and I both know that. Just look at what means Draven had to go at in order to receive the World Heavyweight Title this time around. Draven needed Stevie Richards to forfeit that title. If that had not occured Erik Draven would have had absolutely no chance of beating "The King of Swing". Erik Draven can't compete with the top talent in the EWA anymore. His glory days are past him. To capture the EWA European Championship a month or so ago Draven needed -- there's that word again -- he needed for me to be pushed out. I was informed to lose that match. Tonight, we can see the pattern repeats itself. Is this the only way Erik Draven can obtain EWA Championships??? I suppose so. Just for the record let me state that when Draven and I went toe to toe without any politics standing in the way -- I kicked his freaking ass! So now he's a 4-time EWA World Heavyweight Champion??? Well whoop-t-doo. This title reign of his shall be short. Short indeed like his weenie -- well that's what Christina Knight told me. You see, Draven, I'm gunning for you and I have all the credentials to get the job done. I possess the most EWA victories out of all the wrestlers that ever competed in this federation. I'm a former EWA World Heavyweight Champion and last but not least my 85% winning percentage far exceeds yours, big boy. This revolution shall culminate in your demise and with me, "God's Gift to Wrestling", being a 2-time EWA World Heavyweight Champion. Working for Gates ain't so bad after all. Au Revoir, Draven! Don't grow too attached to that title because before you know it.... poof....it'll be gone like your career which shall fade into obscurity. Feel me? (SmirtDogg tosses the microphone to the Bossman) The Big Bossman:" I have been gone for a while, but now I'm back. I have been hired as a mercinary by Mr. Gates. I'm here now under his authority, and he has given me the right to enforce his law to the fullest. (twirls nightstick) I am going to watch Mr. Gates and anyone that decides to cross him will end up like this (points to Draven). With myself and Smirt Dogg the Corporate Empire will run the EWA. And those of you that don't like it will have to answer to Gates' Law. Gates: So, Draven? My advice to you would be to abide by Gates' Law very closely...otherwise...you may just be serving some hard time! (The three men laugh hysterically as Smirt's music plays. Fade to black.)
