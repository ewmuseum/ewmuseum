EWA Tuesday Night War

8 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 8, 1998 Live, from the Kiel Center St. Louis, Missouri Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. KooKabura Once again, Galaxy put zero effort into his match. Meanwhile, KooKabura impressed the crowd with a variety of power manuevers, finishing Galaxy off with the Baby Bop. KooKabura d. Galaxy with the Baby Bop. Return Match for the EWA World Television Championship "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. "The Natural One" Michael Modest (EWA World Television Champion) The two had a terrific match at the Clash Of The Champions, and the trend continued...albeit with a lot of controversy. Modest was on his way to a victory, preparing to go for the DropZone, when a huge chorus of boos rose from the crowd. Coming from the entrance ramp was part of The Corporate Empire, The Big Bossman and SmirtDogg. The Bossman began twirling his nightstick at the referee, and while this distraction took place, Smirt ran in the ring, and gave Modest the Gangsta's Paradise (DDT). However, the referee saw it, and called for the bell. KG, Bossman, and Smirt then proceeded to attack both the referee and Modest, and then celebrated together...apparently, KG has been sucked in by the Empire. Michael Modest d. KG by disqualification. Michael Modest remains EWA World Television Champion. Joe Lemon v. The Big Bossman (w/ The Corporate Empire) The Bossman looked extremely unimpressive in this bout, but still was on enough to get a win, after a shot with the nightstick and the Bossman Slam. The Big Bossman d. Joe Lemon with the Bossman Slam. James Mastin v. Philip Donovan Mastin was 'outmacked' in this contest, I guess...Donovan, who came to the ring in very large clothes, nevertheless showed some impressive skills, ending the match with a corkscrew moonsault. Philip Donovan d. James Mastin with a corkscrew moonsault. Deader v. Sting Neither man found it important enough to show up, so we saw a double draw. The Nightmare Squad v. Big Time This was an outstanding tag team. Both teams started off to a fast pace, which was surprising for the size of the Nightmare Squad. The N.S. controlled the opening of the match with their power advantage, but Big Time looked extremely impressive tonight. The two began to mount a comeback, and finally, it was Scott Tokage getting a pinfall win on Dark after a small package. Big Time d. The Nightmare Squad with Scott Tokage's pin on Dark after a small package. EWA European Championship "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ D-Generation X) v. Thorne Richards (EWA European Champion) Another outstanding match on TNW ended in a peculiar way. Copeland had dominated the opening of the match, gaining some crowd support, but when Thorne made his comeback, it was like the old days, when Thorne was the most popular wrestler in the business...as the roof exploded. The fans cheered Thorne on as he started to go for the London Bridge. However, Copeland hit Thorne with a low blow. Copeland started to go to work on Thorne, and then, to attempt to seal the match, brought a chair into the ring. However, Thorne blocked the chair shot with a dropkick...and the referee disqualified him. Thorne was irate, putting the official in the Loch Ness Loch to the delight of the crowd. Shawn Copeland d. Thorne Richards by disqualification. Thorne Richards remains EWA European Champion. Johnny Napalm v. Raven This was Raven's first match in the EWA, and he showed a little ring rust at the start. Napalm looked very good, and was able to match Raven in brawling techniques, as the majority of the match was indeed spent on the outside. Napalm's luck ended when Raven was able to execute a drop toe hold onto a steel chair, lacerating Napalm's forehead. From there, he was easy pickings, as Raven lifted him up, and planted him with the Evenflow. Raven d. Johnny Napalm with the Evenflow. Haywood Jublome v. "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash (w/ D-Generation X) Big Sexy didn't look so big tonight, as Jublome pretty much rolled over him. Jublome used various knee holds to take away the big man's vertical base. From there, it was just a matter of time until Jublome was able to hit a big powerbomb, amazingly, and get the three count. Haywood Jublome d. Kevin Nash with a powerbomb. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ Jocelyn) v. Ruger Ice (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Lady Jane) This was an outstanding match. Ruger started with a flurry of offensive moves, sending the crowd to its feet. Ruger then slowed the match down considerably, methodically wearing Conklin down. However, it didn't take Conklin long to stage a comeback, and that he did, capping it off with a beautiful moonsault. Conklin then picked Ruger up, and nailed him with the Overhand Right, but amazingly, Ruger was able to kick out after 2! The match continued, with Conklin visibly frustrated. Conklin threw Ruger to the outside, and the two began brawling there. Conklin, however, gained an advantage when he rammed Ruger into the guardrail, hurting his back. Amazingly, Conklin then placed Ruge ron the Italian Broadcast Table, and climbed to the top rope, executing a splash onto the table. The referee began counting to ten, but Conklin was able to crawl in at the last second, and gain a countout victory. As a result of all this, a rematch for the Intercontinental Title has been signed for the huge December 30 Wednesday Night War card. Terry Conklin d. Ruger Ice by countout. Ruger Ice remains EWA Intercontinental Champion. Special Ordered Tag Team Match The Rock & Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) & "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Ok, folks...this match lasted all of 90 seconds, yet it was one of the most major occurances in EWA history. The Rock and Jackson came to the ring separately, followed by D-Generation X. Vincent Jackson started off against Helmsley, and quickly began working him over in the corner, pummeling him with rights and lefts. Jackson then tagged in The Rock, and that's when all hell broke loose. Immediately, the crowd began booing, as President Gates, KG, SmirtDogg, and The Big Bossman all began to make their way to the ring. Bossman made eye contact with The Rock, and tossed his nightstick in the ring. Rock caught it, looked at it for a moment, and then raised his 'People's Eyebrow', and nailed Helmsley with it, while SmirtDogg distracted the referee. The Rock then covered Helmsley for the threecount, and immediately, the Corporate Empire began to attack DX. The Rock accepted a hug from President Gates...but the Empire wasn't done yet. As Jackson was pummeling Draven in the corner, Gates tapped him on the corner, and handed him a chair. Jackson looked at him curiously, and pulled back the chair to blast Gates...but turned at the last second, and nailed Draven with it. Christina Knight then stepped in the ring, and accepted handshakes from every member of the Corporate Empire...apparently, The Rock and Vincent Jackson also have joined the Corporate Empire, to a huge disdaining response from the crowd. The Rock & Vincent Jackson d. DX with Rock's pin on Triple-H after hitting him with The Big Bossman's nightstick.
