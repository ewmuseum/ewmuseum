Saturday, November 7, 1998 Belltime - 8:00 PM EST Live, from The Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta, Canada Special Added Grudge Match "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ D-Generation X) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) A lot of the EWA fans in Calgary had their eye on this match. Shawn Michaels attempting to extract revenge on Jackson for what he did to Erik Draven's former love. But it didn't quite work out as DX had planned. Once Jackson entered the ring, Michaels charged Jackson, and pummeled him with lefts and rights. Michaels then ran against the ropes, and attempted a flying crossbody. Jackson caught Michaels, picked him up into a gorilla press, and slammed him to the mat. From there, it was a Jackson chokeslam, and the Fallen Angel (top rope powerbomb), as Jackson demolished Shawn Michaels. Vincent Jackson d. Shawn Michaels with the Fallen Angel. Joe Lemon v. Buh Buh Ray Dudley Neither man showed up for this match. Triple Threat Grudge Match Winner will recieve an EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The Hate Club) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom The boos that Venom and Helmsley had showered on them were enough to bring the house down, but they were nothing compared to the cheers that the hometown legend Hart received. The match started off with Helmsley and Venom actually teaming up to give Hart a beating, but that quickly ended when Venom backstabbed Helmsley with a hard lariat. From there, the match erupted into a three man brawl, which stayed on the outside for the majority. Finally, the three got back inside, and Venom rested in the corner while Hart and Helmsley went head to head. The two battled for about five minutes, until Hart whipped Helmsley into the ropes. Hart ducked to back bodydrop him, but Helmsley took advantage of the opportunity, and executed the Pedigree. Immediately, Venom climbed to the top rope for a splash on Hart, but Helmsley went to cover The Hitman. When Venom came down, Helmsley rolled out of the way, and Venom landed on Hart. Helmsley then jumped on top of Venom, and amazingly, the referee counted to three. It was announced that both Triple-H and Venom had proper covers on Hart, so both won the match, and would both face the Intercontinental Champion in a Triple Threat match on Wednesday. Venom and Triple-H d. Bret Hart. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. "The Natural One" Michael Modest This was a pretty slow-paced, dull match, as neither man seemed to be able to get a good rhythm down. The match dragged on for a few moments, until Modest was able to roll up Galaxy with a small package and get a victory. Michael Modest d. Galaxy with a small package. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz Haynes started off the match with a fairly good high-flying attack, but Taz's suplexes came into play quickly and early. From there, Taz never looked back, as he manhandled Haynes on his way to a victory. Taz d. Will Haynes by submission with the Tazmission. No Holds Barred Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. Vincent Jackson (w/ Christina Knight) Ruger started off with a solid attack, locking up with the man and executing a few standing submission holds, to wear the big man down. Ruger was able to isolate Jackson in the center of the ring, and lock on a beautiful abdominal stretch. However, Jackson's wingspread was so wide, he was able to reach the ropes with only a few movements to the left. From there, Ruger used a couple of clotheslines to try and get the big man off of his feet, but Jackson would not go down. Finally, frustrated, Ruger went to the outside and got a chair. Ruger brought the chair in, and nailed Jackson in the head...and that lit a fire under the giant. Jackson grabbed Ruger by the throat, chokeslammed him, and went for the cover, but only got a two. Jackson then picked Ruger up, whipped him into the ropes, and executed a powerful Uruanage (Rock Bottom). From there, Jackson set Ruger up on the top rope, and executed the Fallen Angel for the victory. Vincent Jackson d. Ruger Ice with the Fallen Angel. (As Jackson exits the arena, Lady Jane helps Ruger Ice up and on his feet. Suddenly, the familiar theme music of the WWF's The Godfather blares over the PA. Ruger Ice, confused, looks up at the entrance ramp as The Godfather makes his way out, a "ho" on each arm. The Godfather has a cigar in his mouth, and seems to be in high spirits. As The Godfather enters the ring, Ruger Ice prepares for another fight, but The Godfather holds up a hand, telling him to stop. The Godfather produces a microphone, and begins to speak.) The Godfather: Hold up, brother...hold up, slow your role. I ain't here to fight...I'm just here to settle a little problem I got with you. See, as you may know, stealing is against the law in any state. And when you're a PIMP like me, you got a lot of property. So I ain't here to fight ya, Ruger Ice...I just want my property back. (Ruger gives him a confused look, and The Godfather continues.) The Godfather: You see, Ruger, you have something that belongs to me. That fine choice of meat behind you, (The Godfather points to Lady Jane.) Lady Jane, she belongs to me, Ruger. All them lies she been telling you...she's only in it with you for the money, my brother. She's working "undercover" as one of my hos! From there, Ruger quickly blasted The Godfather with a right. The "hos" scattered, as Ruger and The Godfather began brawling all over the ringside area, until finally, the officials were able to separate the two. Non-Title Grudge Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards v. " Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ The Triple Threat & Rebecca Claridge) Stevie came to the ring first, wearing a new shirt...a picture of Rebecca Claridge on the front, and on the back: "Been there, done that". When "Here Comes The Hotstepper" played on the PA, Justin Hicks, alone, sprinted toward the ring, and slid under the ropes. A brawl quickly developed between Richards and Hicks, and this one got ugly fast. The action spilled over to the outside, where Hicks took a chair, and went to work on Richards. Hicks was nearly disqualified, and probably would have been if not for a low blow by Richards. The match went back inside the ring, where a high-pace developed. At one point, Stevie was able to hit the Stevie Kick, and went for the cover, but suddenly saw Garrett and Raoul sitting in the front row, waving at him. Richards, irate, began screaming at them to leave, which enabled Hicks to roll Stevie up from behind, and get the victory to a thunderous ovation from the crowd. Justin Hicks d. Stevie Richards with a rollup. (Immediately after the match has concluded, Venom and Taz come charging down to the ring. They immediately start to attack Justin Hicks with incredible intensity.) JR: MY GOD!!! THE CARNAGE!!! Hicks is being pounded into the mat!!! I can't believe this. VA: Well I believe it because I see it. But what I want to know is what in the world is the deal with Stevie Richards, Taz, and Venom? JR: I think a better question right now would be where is the Triple Threat? (While the beating continues, a camera shows the Triple Threat's locker room door. It is being held shut by an axe that was wedged into the handles. Mysterio is seen standing silently outside the door with his arms crossed. The Triple Threat can be heard on the inside trying to get out but their attempts are failing miserably.) JR: This is getting ugly. Hicks is a bloody mess. He's been stomped in the head so many times...he just may not even remember his name tomorrow. VA: Wait a second, Rebecca Claridge is in the ring. What can she possibly do to stop these guys? (Claridge enters the ring and is immediately grabbed by Taz. He holds her by the arms as Venom continues to pound Hicks. Stevie Richards has taken a microphone and begins to speak.) Stevie: Oh yeah!!! Look at him!!! This is your sorry excuse for a man? He's nothing more than a bitch in Venom's arms!!!!! (slaps Hicks in the face) So Justin, you want to know why I slept with that dirty little *censored for your protection*? I'll tell you why. I did it to piss you off!!! That's the only reason. And I think it's worked hasn't it? (Hicks tries to fight his way out of Venom's arms but fails due to his weakness from the savage assault) Hahahaaaa!!! This is great. Mr. Every Night? Well, not tonight! I declare tonight STEVIE RICHARDS NIGHT!!! (Rebecca Claridge squirms her way out of Taz's arms and gets up into Stevie's face. She spits directly in his face and slaps him hard. Stevie gets a sick grin on his face and wipes the spit off slowly. He then raises his hand but instead of slapping her, he just pushes Claridge to the ground. Hicks tries once again to escape Venom's clutches but instead he is met with boots from Taz and Stevie. They continue to kick Hicks until he slumps to the canvas. From there he crawls over to Claridge to see if she's o.k. Stevie, Taz, and Venom all exit the ring laughing.) Stevie: Who's show is it now? Venom: It's our *beep*ing show. JR: These men are just disgusting. Wrestling fans, I apologize for what you just saw. I have never been so appaled in my life. These men are scum. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg v. The Prince (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) ("In The Hall of the Mountain Kings" begins to play over the PA and immediately the crowd jumps to it's feet and begins to boo in anticipation of The Prince.  But he's not the first out of the entrance. No, the first is Davali.  Dressed much like the women The Prince has come to the ring with before, she's toga-clad and is tonight's rose girl. She looks better than she did the last time we saw her. As she walks down the aisle, she tosses rose petals on the ground in a somewhat disgusted manner, and with a look to kill on her face.  The Prince shortly follows behind her, Light Heavyweight Title in tow at his side, leaving a trail behind it as it passes through the rose petals. As they get to the ring, Davali climbs up onto the apron and holds the top and middle ropes open, which The Prince climbs through.  Davali attempts to follow, but The Prince stops her, and motions her to get a microphone for him.  A menacing glance is shot his way, and reluctantly she gets him his microphone, which she tosses at his feet.  The Prince returns the menacing glance as he picks up the microphone) Remember woman, (The Prince points to Davali, who's still on the outside of the ring) I saved you from your fate.  The glances might have been menacing at one point in time when you had that pathetic Golden Child under your thumb, but the only thing that he and I have in common is you.  He was a pansy who fell to you, whereas I won't make the same mistake  (The crowd boos) Now I know that usually the challenger to the title usually comes down before the champion.  I realize that under normal circumstances this would be a break in protocol, but this isn't exactly what we would call normal circumstances.  What I have known for the past week is finally coming to light, and that is that SmirtDogg will not be showing up tonight. He has had this committment for well over two weeks and look at what he does when he realizes what kind of mess he has gotten himself into. A match with me is certainly not what he really wanted.  If I had lost to Galaxy of all people, SmirtDogg would be in this ring right now getting ready to take back my Light Heavyweight title, but when he actually has to work for something, hit the road Smirt, and don't you come back no more. See, when SmirtDogg came back to this federation after his alleged demise, he came back under the impression that he would be handed the title that I now drag at my side.  But when he realized that he wouldn't have the easy ride, he had two choices.  He could come out here like a man and have his fate handed to him like a man, or he could run like a little girl.  I was ready for any circumstance, and good fortune played a role in that (The Prince once again shoots a look at Davali), but the yellow one known as SmirtDogg took the easy way out and he won't be here tonight.  But apparently he has a replacement ready for me, so if you will........ (The Prince pauses) Send the replacement Apparently The Prince was correct, as Smirt's music did not come on. Instead, "Natural Born Killaz" by Dr. Dre/Ice Cube blared over the PA, and none other than former EWA Lightheavyweight Champion New Jack came to the ring. (If you recall, New Jack and The Prince have quite a bit of history, but have never actually faced each other.) The match started on a fast pace, with New Jack getting in a couple of clotheslines. But that's about all he got, as The Prince's slow, methodical technical wrestling pace was able to override New Jack's fast brawling technicques. After a snap suplex, The Prince was able to lock on High Society, and New Jack had virtually no choice but to submit. The Prince d. New Jack by submission with High Society. The Prince remains EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Outsiders (w/ D-Generation X) v. The Pessimist & "The Canadian Sensation" Crazy Canuck (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Hate Club) This match went at a normal pace for a few minutes, and then the shocker came. While Scott Hall was mocking The Pessimist, Crazy Canuck jumped in the ring, chair in hand. Instead of helping out The Pessimist, though, Canuck leveled him with a chair, and then gave both Hall and Nash "X-fives". The three began to unmercifully beat on the Pessimist. Canuck landed a Canadiansault, Hall hit the Outsider Edge, and Nash finished the Pessimist off with a JackKnife Powerbomb. After the match, Draven and Helmsley came out, and presented Canuck with a D-Generation X t-shirt....Canuck proudly put it on, and accompanied them to the back. The Outsiders d. The Hate Club after Crazy Canuck turned on The Pessimist. The Outsiders are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions! EWA Intercontinental Championship Match "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam (w/ Intoxication X) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Van Dam came to the ring alone this time, without any members of Intoxication X. Bunda also came to the ring alone, with Rebecca Claridge conspicous by her absence. Van Dam started off the match in control, with a series of spinning kicks that knocked the wind out of Bunda. Van Dam asked for the match to be declared a weapons match, and received his wish, as the referee made the change. Van Dam quickly went after a chair, but unbeknownest to him, Bunda had reached for one as well. When Van Dam threw the chair up in the air, and went to kick it into Bunda's face, Bunda swung the chair, and cracked it right into the leg of Van Dam. From there, Bunda had a resurgance, and after two big powerslams, was able to take control of the match and nail the Showrunner for the victory. Jeff Bunda d. Rob Van Dam with the Showrunner. Jeff Bunda remains EWA Intercontinental Champion, and will go on to face Venom and Triple-H on Wednesday for the Intercontinental Title. Street Fight KG v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels Shawn Michaels was supposed to enter the alley first, but when he arrived, KG was already there, baseball bat in hand. KG nailed Michaels with the baseball bat, and slammed him into a car. The two men actually began fighting on top of the car, until Michaels knocked KG off and onto the cement, lacerating his forehead. Michaels then picked up a garbage can, and threw it at KG, causing him to stumble back into the building. The two fought among the backstage for awhile, slamming each other into the walls and onto equipment (a highlight was a KG brainbuster on Michaels on a table), until they finally made their way inside the aisleway. Once they got in the ring, the match quickly turned into a fastpaced bout, one which Michaels was able to control. Michaels nailed a flying elbowdrop from the top rope, and was tuning up the band for Sweet Chin Music, when the lights in the arena went out. For a third time, thunderclaps and purple light filled the arena, and as "Wherever I May Roam" blared over the PA, Vincent Jackson, without Christina Knight, stalked to the ring. Once he stepped over the top rope, Michaels ran at him, only to be lifted into the air and chokeslammed. KG just stood there, staring at Jackson, until Jackson kicked him in the stomach, and powerbombed him onto Michaels. Jackson then left, and the referee, seeing KG on top of Michaels, went ahead and counted 1, 2, 3. As a result, KG was able to pick up a very tainted victory, but a victory nonetheless. KG d. Shawn Michaels. Casket Match "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Triple Threat) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) Copeland entered the ring looking confident as always, and ready to attempt to be only the third person to defeat Kane. Meanwhile, the Big Red Machine got a huge response from the capacity crowd, who were very against Copeland. Kane started out the match in total domination, with strong rights and lefts to Copeland. Copeland fell to the outside, right on top of the casket. When he saw what he had landed on, he high-tailed it out, and headed back to the locker room. But at the entrance ramp appeared "The ICON" Erik Draven, World Title around his waist, and, more importantly, steel chair in hand. Copeland found himself between a rock and a hard place, and was forced to go back and meet the hellish Kane. Copeland had a pair of brass knuckles in his hand, though, and blasted Kane in the groin area. Even the Big Red Machine fell to one knee after this, and that was enough for Copeland to take control of the match. At one point, Copeland tried for the Money Shot, but Kane was able to lift his foot, and catch Copeland square in the face. Kane then chokeslammed Copeland, and was about to go for the Tombstone, when Copeland executed yet another low blow. This time, Copeland slid under the ring, and picked up a ball bat. Copeland took his best Mark McGwire stance, and leveled Kane with the bat, sending him over the top rope and into the casket. Jeff Bunda then ran out, and before Kane could sit up, closed the casket on the Big Red Machine. With Copeland in the ring celebrating, all of a sudden the lights dim and a silence ensues. No one understands who or what this is about, that is until a golden beam shines through the arena. 'Golden Showers' begins over the loudspeaker, signifying the return of the "Shining Star" to an overwhelming ovation. The Golden One: Well, well, well, it's good to be back in the saddle, and back in the EWA, where I belong. The last time everyone saw me, I was not right, suffering through some problems, not to mention watching my former best friend Jeff whatever his name walk away from this place. I was being punked by the original punk himself at Wrestlecade in Charlotte, North Carolina. Well ladies and gentlemen, welcome to revenge, revenge in the form of a star known to all in this business. You see Shawn, you may have gotten the nod last time, but you and I both know my heart wasn't in it. I was going through some stuff, and now that I've settled my personal problems, it's time to settle everything else. Now as far I as can remember, you've got a 1-0 advantage over me, hardly a legendary feat. As a matter of fact, if magic strikes just once then the score is even 1-1. It's nothing new that I don't like you Copeland. I've made no mistakes in describing to the world that you are the one and only "Coin Collector" in this business, and that opinion holds true to this day. But even with all of my previous hatred for you and your ways, nothing get me jacked more than seeing that no-named loser Fraudchise stand by you side-by-side, announcing your entrance to a stable that was only elite once. The days of Bunda/Hicks/TGO are long over, and ever since then it's been a one-way trip down hill. I mean, the Triple Threat was always about pride, and knowledge. In other words, we prided ourselves on knowing everything that was going on within OUR federation. But oh my how the times have changed. Stevie Richards, the most cunning man in this business knocked the socks off you and your bitch Bunda by laying the old-fashioned smack down. What was suppposed to be the greatest formation of talent since the Beatles quickly turned into dissention among the ranks. You know why, Shawn? Because you, like your new-found friends, are nothing but selfish, egomaniacs, who can't get out of your own way half the time, and the other half are too busy basking in your worthless glory. What I'm here to do is quite simple. So simple, in fact, that if the three of you mindless wonders put your heads together, you may figure it out. Let me spell it out for you... I OWN THIS SHOW!!! I've just let you run it for a while, but starting right now, November 7th, 1998, it's all gonna change. I hate to break it to you Shawn, but those 15 minutes you called fame are up, and I'm here to see to it. No more prancing, no more dancing, no more clapping, and no more slapping. In lehman's terms Shawny boy, This is the beginning of forever... Shawn Copeland d. Kane in the Casket Match. First Blood Match For the EWA World Television Championship Gangrel (w/ Devon) v. Edge (EWA World Television Champion) Gangrel came to the ring first, in an unusual manner. As he and Devon ascended from the ring of fire, we saw the young rookie, Christian Peterson, with Gangrel. (Peterson was drug to the back by Gangrel 2 weeks ago.) Since then, we have learned that he is now simply known as 'Christian'. Edge came to the ring alone, as usual, and wasted no time in getting the match started with a doubleleg takedown. Edge then whipped Gangrel into the ropes. He caught Gangrel with a hiptoss on the way back, but Gangrel countered it and executed a butterfly suplex. With lightning quick speed, Gangrel climbed to the top rope and attempted a flying headbutt, but Edge was able to roll out of the way. Edge picked Gangrel up for a scoop slam, and quickly dropped a series of legdrops onto the demon's neck. Edge then picked Gangrel up for a Michinoku Driver, but Gangrel caught him with a rake to the face. He then was able to nail the Piledriving DDT, but this being a first blood match, pinfalls do not count. Gangrel then drug Edge to the outside of the ring, and the two men brawled on the outside for nearly five minutes. Finally, Edge was able to gain an advantage, by hitting the Downward Spiral so that Gangrel's head went crashing into the steel steps. Edge took a short breather, and went to pick Gangrel up, but unbeknowesnt to Edge, Gangrel had picked up the goblet. As Edge grabbed Gangrel by his hair, Gangrel swung the goblet, and broke it right over Edge's forehead, spilling the blood inside. The blood from the goblet, combined with that of the laceration on Edge's forehead, proved enough for the referee to declare Gangrel the winner. As Christian picked up the Television Title belt, Gangrel put Edge on his shoulder, and the three made their way to the back. Gangrel d. Edge in the First Blood Match. Gangrel is the new EWA World Television Champion!! The Final Confrontation EWA President Duane Gates v. Prodigy (with one arm tied behind his back) All the wrestling experts knew what this would be coming into the match, and they were right. Gates got in a slap at the beginning of the match, and that was it. Even with one arm tied behind his back, Prodigy dominated this match, which lasted all of 4 1/2 minutes. And, true to his word, Prodigy delivered four Firestarters onto Gates, for the four months of hell he had been put through. Prodigy d. Duane Gates with the Firestarter. Click here for the main event!
