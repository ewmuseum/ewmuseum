EWA Wednesday Night War

6 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 6, 1998 Live, from the Rose Garden, Portland, Oregon. EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. The Eliminators "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Jack "The Man" McCammon "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Handsome" Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Non-Title Match New Demolition v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) The Outsiders v. Death Sentance Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. The Wyldsyde EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Kimberly & The IV Horsemen) Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey & The Crippled Midgets of Doom) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) v. Raven (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. Thorne Richards Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) EWA Intercontinental Championship TRIPLE THREAT Match The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match Prodigy (EWA European Champion, w/ Degeneration X) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The IV Horsemen) EWA United States Tag Team Championship Match Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. The Eliminators A spectacular tag team match saw chairs and baseball bats play a role in the outcome, as LaParka came to the ring with the aforementioned items. LaParka gave the chair to Pacifico Pete, but Perry Saturn pulled the chair away, and nailed Pete when the official wasn't looking. The Eliminators d. Dos Equis with Saturn's pin on Pacifico Pete after a chair shot. (14:33) The Eliminators are the new EWA United States Tag Team Champions! "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Jack "The Man" McCammon Another great match, but the Rock's luck began to go downhill when he missed the People's Elbow. From there, McCammon caught him with a double ax-handle from the top, and then pinned him with a German Suplex. Jack McCammon d. Rocky Maivia with a German Suplex. (11:21) "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The IV Horsemen) v. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) Both men were on target tonight, but La Parka's fire seemed to be rekindled, and the Chairman of the EWA ran home with the win. La Parka d. Chris Benoit with the La Parka Butt Blast. (9:56) Sexual Thangs (w/ Cherry Pie) v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) Neither team showed. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Handsome" Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Big Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) This was a good high-flying contest, and Harley made a valiant effort, but he was smitten by Rebecca Claridge, who came to ringside. Claridge threw a wink at Hart, and Hart was distracted long enough for Martin to come from behind and hit the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin d. Harley Hart with the Shoulderbreaker. (8:20) Aaron Martin retains the Light Heavyweight Title. FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) Both teams ended up brawling to a double disqualification. Non-Title Match New Demolition v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) New Demoliton started out on top of this match, but the World Champions battled back, and took Flame and Brown down. Down where? Dow...ah, forget it. Public Enemy d. New Demolition with Ghetto Boy's pin on Flame after the Ghetto Driver. (10:11) The Outsiders v. Death Sentance The Outsiders dominated this one in impressive fashion. The Outsiders d. Death Sentance with Kevin Nash's pin on Pain after the JackKnife. (3:19) Castor Troy (w/ Thorne Richards) v. The Wyldsyde A good match, one in which Castor was neutralized. Several times, Troy attempted to turn the fight into a brawl, but The Wyldsyde kept it on the mat each time, and came up with an impressive win, using Thorne's own finisher as Richards looked on from ringside. The Wyldsyde d. Castor Troy by submission with the Loch Ness Lock. (13:27) EWA World Television Championship Match KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Kimberly & The IV Horsemen) During the match, Page knocked KG to the outside of the ring...and suddenly, Steve Austin ran in from the crowd, and nailed DDP with the Stone Cold Stunner. The ref was tending to KG, and didn't see. KG crawled back to the ring, and extended his hand to Austin. Austin tok it..and then returned the favor by Stunnering him as well. The ref saw this, and amazingly, gave KG the DQ win. A rematch has been signed for SNS. KG d. DDP by DQ. (15:43) DDP retains the EWA World Television Championship. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) Helmsley was beaten for the second time tonight by Paul Bearer's son, in his first appearance since he lost the World Title at Survivor Series. Kane d. Triple-H with the Tombstone. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey & The Crippled Midgets of Doom) A very exciting match, but Van Smack made a crucial mistake when he turned his back on Richards to give himself a high five. When he turned back around, Richards nailed the Stevie Kick. The two embraced afterward in the ring. Stevie Richards d. Van Smack with the Stevie Kick. (18:32) Jim Ross: Ladies and gentlemen, would you please welcome the President of the EWA, Mr. Duane Gates! *The crowd gives a mild cheer as Mr. Gates makes his way to the ring.* Jim Ross: Mr Gates, what's on your mind? Duane Gates: Harrison Goodman is what's on my mind. Goodman, how dare you sign a U.S. Title match between Raven and Justin Hicks without consulting me! I want you to come out here right now and explain yourself! *Goodman walks to the ring with "Perfect Strangers" playing over the PA.* "Executive Consultant" Goodman: You want to know why I signed the damn match, I'll tell you why I signed this damn match. Because I can. The fact of the matter is Gates, that I'm the one with the real power here in the EWA, so just stop your damn whining and let me do my damn job. If I think Justin Hicks deserves a US Title Shot, you better believe he's going to get one, and there's not a thing you can do about it, chump. Gates: The problem I have isn't the match; it's you. You have no right to sign a match without my consent, Goodman! *Suddenly, Goodman shoves Gates. Gates shoves Goodman back, and the two actually begin fighting! Security breaks up the melee quickly, and Doug Dillenger escorts Goodman back to the locker room. Gates leaves after Goodman is gone, and we hear "Perfect Strangers" as Justin Hicks heads to the ring for his U.S. Title match against Raven.* EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge & The Triple Threat) v. Raven (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion) Raven looked awful in this contest, and Hicks easily captured the U.S. Title. Justin Hicks d. Raven with a powerbomb. (5:11) Justin Hicks is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!! Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. Thorne Richards Deathstar no-showed. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) Wright was at the top of his game tonight, virtually dominating Galaxy. Alex Wright d. Galaxy with the German Suplex. (4:08) EWA Intercontinental Championship TRIPLE THREAT Match The Golden One (w/ Carrie) v. The Gunslinger (w/ DDT) v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) The Gunslinger was eliminated by Jeff Bunda, and suffered a slight knee injury. He will be out of action for a week. Afterward, The Golden One hit the Golden Edge on Bunda, and was in the process of covering him, when new U.S. Champion Justin Hicks ran in. The two (Bunda/Hicks) did a real number on Golden One. The Golden One d. Jeff Bunda by DQ. Jeff Bunda retains the EWA Intercontinental Championship. EWA World Heavyweight Championship Match Prodigy (EWA European Champion, w/ Degeneration X) v. "Nature Boy" Ric Flair (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The IV Horsemen) This match lived up to its hype, and was one of the most spectacular matches in EWA history. Flair locked on the Figure Four on Prodigy, but while Triple-H distracted the ref, Shawn Michaels pulled Prodigy's arm to the rope. The match went on, and Flair was attempting a superplex onto Prodigy off the top rope after catching Prodigy attempting a high-risk move, when it happened. Prodigy spun Flair around, nailing the Firestarter. The official counted to three and handed Prodigy the belt...but the damage wasn't done. DX then began to beat on Flair. (Suddenly, a huge pop echoes through the crowd...Erik Draven charges down to ringside, followed by Diamond Dallas Page, both with chairs. DX holds their ground, then clears the ring when both slide under the bottom ropes...DDP and Draven check Flair for injury, and then...Draven waffles Page with the chair!!! He laughs, and motions for DX to come back into the ring... As they slowly do, Draven grabs a mic from the ring announcer, and begins to speak.) Erik Draven: Man...that felt SO good!!! Aight...I know what you three are wondering, and let me make something clear...Prodigy, I'm very happy that you won that title...probably the best thing to ever happen to my...errr...your career. I mean, I've finally seen the error in my ways...'seen the light', if you will. You are the "True DeGenerate"...and quite honestly, it would be an honor to stand by your side in this two-bit federation, and show them just who the real powerhouses in the business are. It's kind of obvious you're a marked man now, since you won that title...and, well...you need all the protection you can get. Michaels, Helmsley, and Prodigy then talked it over...and shockingly, welcomed Draven into their arms. The DX music came on, and they all celebrated as the camera faded to black.
