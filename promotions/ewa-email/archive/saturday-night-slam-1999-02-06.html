EWA Saturday Night Slam

6 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EWA Saturday Night Slam (2/6/99) February 6, 1999 Live, from the Arrowhead Pond Anaheim, California Joe Lemon v. Tanaka (w/ Ross Litchfield) Now, think about this one. What usually happens in a Joe Lemon match? Yep, you guessed it...the rookie Tanaka got his first victory in impressive fashion, hitting the Flying Feather (top rope elbowdrop). Tanaka d. Joe Lemon with the Flying Feather. 'The Real Athlete' Sid Jacobs v. Bubba Odell (w/ Paul Role) Jacobs was manhandled in this contest. The debuting Bubba Odell looked as if he had been training, as he executed a surprising amount of solid wrestling moves for a rookie. Odell polished him off with the Bubba Buster, a reverse Bronco Buster. Bubba Odell d. Sid Jacobs with the Bubba Buster. Time Bomb (w/ Ross Litchfield) v. Philip Donovan This was the first solid contest of the match. PhD started off with early momentum, but then made the mistake of trying to smack the 371-pounder. Time Bomb responded by laying into PhD, physically dominating him. However, it was not meant to be, for as Time Bomb went for The Explosion (JackKnife Powerbomb), Donovan reversed it into a Frankensteiner, and the man who is truly pretty fly for a white guy picks up the victory. Philip Donovan d. Time Bomb with a Frankensteiner. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Justin Credible v. Max Pins (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Credible has always talked a big game, and proved so by stating earlier that if he did not defeat Pins, he would leave the EWA. Well...let's just say 'Aloha means goodbye', Justin. Pins demolished Credible, not even giving him the chance to make it a Hardcore match, and easily retained the Light Heavyweight Title. Max Pins d. Justin Credible with the 7-10 Split. Max Pins remains EWA Light Heavyweight Champion. ***Note: Credible will be replaced in the 1999 King Of The Ring Tournament by Time Bomb. 1999 King Of The Ring Tournament Qualifying Match E-Nuff (EWA Hardcore Champion, w/ Paul Role) v. Haywood Jublome (w/ D-Generation X) This match had recieved a lot of hype leading up to Saturday, and it proved worthy of all it recieved. E-Nuff came to the ring with no weapons, apparently focused on wrestling a normal match. And wrestle he did, taking down Jublome with an armdrag, and turning it into an armbar. Jublome reversed it into an armbar of his own, and smacked E-Nuff on the head a few times. This infuriated E-Nuff, who quickly leveled Jublome with a clothesline. E-Nuff took the boots to Jublome, then picking him up and nailing a vertical suplex. E-Nuff went for a cover, but only got two. E-Nuff picked up Jublome, and was stunned to be hit with a small package by Jublome. However, there was only a two count, and both men quickly sprung to their feet. Jublome ducked a lariat attempt, kicked E-Nuff in the stomach, and delivered a swinging neckbreaker. Jublome sprung to the middle turnbuckle, and dropped an elbow to the sternum of E-Nuff. He went for the cover, but only got two. Jublome picked 'Nuff up, and whipped him into the ropes. Jublome ducked to backdrop E-Nuff, but E-Nuff stopped, and caught Jublome, picking him up into a huge powerbomb and slamming him down. E-Nuff then worked on Jublome slowly and methodically, pummeling him with a series of power manuevers. E-Nuff pretty much controlled the match, up until the point which he called for the Lockdown. E-Nuff picked Jublome up, and immediately crouched over, the victim of a low blow by the D-Generation X member. Jublome capitalized, delivering a Pedigree-type move to E-Nuff. Jublome then whipped E-Nuff into the turnbuckle. E-Nuff came right back with a clothesline attempt, but Jublome ducked it, and slammed him into the other turnbuckle. From there, Jublome was able to set E-Nuff on the top rope, and in an amazing feat of strength, execute the Ho Checka'. Back on the mat, Jublome rolled over onto E-Nuff, and got the three-count. Haywood Jublome d. E-Nuff with the Ho Checka'. The Undertaker & Aronalle (w/ The Ministry Of Darkness) v. The Bloodfire (w/ Inferna/Devon) This match was rescheduled for a later date. Lander is walking the hallway alone, towards Jack Reed's locker room when out of nowhere Haywood Jublome appears and grabs Lander and drags him into his locker room. He hits a Double Arm Ddt on Dr. Lander. (Dr. Lander is layed out in The Degeneration X Locker Room as Haywood begins to taunt him) Oh too bad, it looks like there won't be any bells ringing tonight..unless of course you and Jack Reed are getting married. ????: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages...I'm BAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!! (The opening of 'D-Generate Me' blares over the PA system, and the crowd ERUPTS in response to it...a few moments more pass, and a tall, dark silouhette is visible in the entrance ramp...a HUGE pyro display goes off, and the man, the myth, the ICON himself, Erik Draven emerges through the smoke, a huge grin on his face...and the crowd cheers even louder. Draven pauses to point to a 'We miss the ICON' sign in the crowd, grins again, and makes his way to ringside, steps up onto the apron, still showing a bit of a limp, and his left arm in a sling still, but it does nothing to mute the crowd's response... Draven steps over the top rope, motions for a mic, and begins to speak.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Now, I bet you all thought that, once good ol' Vincent Jackson put yours truly off the top of a steel cage, and well...through the table, and well... I considered it. I got to the point of almost dialin' up El Presidente and telling him that the ICON was hangin' up his boots. But then, I got to thinking about it... Took a look at all these fans out here who've come out night after night with their 'We miss the ICON' signs, and their 'DX 4 Life' signs, and well... I couldn't walk away from all of you. (Draven gestures to the fans, who pop loudly) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Having said that, let's get down to business, shall we? Issue #1...Haywood Jublome. The kid's been carrying all of the DX banner on his back up until this last week...and he's done a damn good job of it. Much love, brah...you've done damn well. And with Smirt in the fold now, HBK on the comeback trail, and well...yours truly back in the mix, suffice it to say, DX is BACK. And we're not going to take any *BLEEP* from the Quint...er...Triple Threat, or any of these other 'factions' that think they're all that and a jumbo-size bag of chips. Bunda, TGO, Copeland...if ANY of you pieces of trash want to take us on, just sign on the line, you sorry pieces of trash... and we'll be glad to hand you your ass in a sling. "The ICON" Erik Draven: Aaaaaaaaaand speaking of sorry pieces of trash... Hey Viiiiiiiiiiiiincent!!! Yeah, you, you big, overgrown ogre. (The crowd pops loudly once again) Hate to break it to ya, kid, but...you didn't finish the job at Mind Games...'cause lo and behold, I may be a bit battered and bruised, buuuuuut...I'm still tickin'. (Draven grins) So I'm issuing you this challenge, kid. Name the time, name the place, and name your stipulations...because our little 'issue' isn't quite done yet. I'll be waiting, Vincent. If you have any testicular fortitude, well...you'll sign on the line, kid. "The ICON" Erik Draven: And if there is anyone out there who has a problem with the ICON, well...don't be shy. Lord knows there's PLENTY of ass-whippings to go around...and I'm not hesitant in the least about sharing 'em. Ladies and gentlemen, the ICON is BACK and better than ever...and if you don't like it, well...(Draven grins) Man, I've MISSED this... I've got two words for ya!!! (Draven holds the mic out to the crowd, who respond with a deafening 'SUCK IT!!!' Draven grins once again, and 'D-Generate Me' blares over the PA once again as he leaves the ring, another pop resounding from the crowd as the camera fades out...and we cut to a commercial.) I apologize for the lack of quality for the rest of the writeup...I've just went through a very troubling experience. EWA World Television Championship Match 'Mr. Overhand Right' Terry Conklin (w/ The Triple Threat) v. The Rock (EWA World Television Champion) The opening of this contest featured a surprise, as Shawn Michaels, wheelchair-clad and all, rolled down to do color commentary. The match itself was fantastic, with many near falls and exciting fast-paced action. At one point, The Rock was in control, and left the ring to taunt Michaels, eventually spitting water in his face. Rock turned away, and to the shock of everyone, Michaels popped out of the wheelchair, grabbed Rock, and slammed him into the steel ringpost. Michaels then threw him back in the ring. Michaels went on to explain at the commentator's booth that he was only using the wheelchair to fool Rock. The match continued, and from this point, Conklin dominated. He even hit the Overhand Right, but a foot on the ropes saved The Rock. The Rock struggled to his feet, and Conklin ran at him with a clothesline attempt...however, Rock ducked, and caught Conklin directly in The Rock Bottom, getting the victory. Afterward, Rock attacked Michaels from behind, and began to lay into him severly. A couple of Rock Bottoms later, Rock placed Michaels in the wheelchair, and wheeled it to the top of the entrance ramp. Rock pointed to the edge, implying that he was going to wheel it off...and was promptly crushed with a clothesline from Erik Draven, who had just sprinted from behind the curtain. Draven picked up Rock, and powerbombed him onto the steel. Michaels and Draven then embraced to a standing ovation. The Rock d. Terry Conklin with the Rock Bottom. The Rock remains EWA World Television Champion. 'The Walking Triple Threat' Jack Reed (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Dr. Lander) v. 'God's Gift To Wrestling' SmirtDogg (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ D-Generation X) An outstanding match, much like their first and only other encounter. The contest was even throughout, until the very end. Reed was going for his Top Rope Frankensteiner, but SmirtDogg countered it, and powerbombed Reed off the top rope. Smirt bounced back to the top, executed the Air Smirty, and came away with the victory. SmirtDogg d. Jack Reed with the Air Smirty. 3-Way Dance for the EWA U.S. Heavyweight Championship 'The Money Maker' Shawn Copeland (w/ The Triple Threat) v. 'The Innovator Of Offense' Kanyon v. The Golden One (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Copeland, obviously, embarassed the EWA by not showing up. Therefore, this was a solo contest between Kanyon and The Golden One. Again, a very even match throughout, which would feature each wrestler getting a 4-5 minute span of heavy momentum. Finally, the match appeared to be over, as TGO had Kanyon set up for the Gold Medal. However, Kanyon was able to counter this beautifully into the Flatliner, and roll on top of TGO for the pin. Afterward, Kanyon took a microphone. "The Innovator Of Offense" Kanyon: Whoa, whoa..wait a minute Golden One. I just want you to know that I've got a lot of respect and heart for you, because we damn near killed each other tonight. And I know that this title means more to you than just about anything else. And quite frankly, I think we could do even better. So folks, how does this sound to you? March Madness 99..."The Innovator Of Offense" and "The Shining Star" for the US Title, two out of three falls takes the gold. Because like I said, you have no idea how much I acknowledge your skills bro. Please accept, and let's do this again. *Kanyon just points to The Golden One and applauds as he leaves the ring, and Kanyon holds the US Title up in the air and slowly walks back to the dressing room.* Kanyon d. The Golden One with the Flatliner. Kanyon is the new EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion! Do your weekly rankings here, please
