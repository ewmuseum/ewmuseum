EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

26 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 26, 1998 Live, from The Peoria Civic Center Peoria, Illinois "The Crippler" Chris Benoit & "The Hitman" Bret Hart (w/ The Hart Foundation) v. The Black Angels "The Lionheart" Chris Jericho (w/ The Hart Foundation) v. Serpent Man All of the Hart Foundation's matches have been postponed until next Wednesday. Zuliel v. "Pimpin" Joe Satre (w/ The PHC) Our opening contest was the definition of squash, as Pimpin Joe completely annihilated young Zuliel. Joe Satre d. Zuliel with the Pimp Splash. The Poison Pit (Hosted by "The Extreme Superstar" Venom, w/ special guests Terry Conklin & Shawn Copeland) For an odd reason, the Poison Pit was canceled for WNW. Handey Gripwell v. Prodigy Gripwell didn't get the chance to execute the Testicular Claw...hell, he didn't execute anything. Prodigy controlled this match from bell to bell, and had something to say after the contest. (Prodigy leans over the ropes and beckons for a microphone from the ringside attendant. He recieves one, which he takes. Standing over the fallen Handy Gripwell, he begins) Gates, I told you that I'd take him out and that after tonight he'd no longer retain his grip. I'm a man of little words, but when I speak, you listen. So here are my lips (Prodigy points to his mouth) and they're moving, so open your ears and listen real good. You don't keep throwing this kind of garbage at me. (Prodigy points at Gripwell). You came out here a few weeks ago, after I disposed of that whining, "Pay Me Pin Me" Deader and said "Oh Prodigy, you can be free of the Hate Club granted you can beat them all". I took out Reed, and then you gave me him? Are you turning against your word? It sure as hell looks like it here Gates. So get those ears wide open, cause you should hear this. And get your fan club to listen too, because I'm setting the ground rules. "KG" Joe Satre, you're next on the list. Saturday..........you step into this ring with insanity incarnate and don't try to win.........try to survive. It won't be an easy task, I'll assure you of that. Hell, if you escape with all your limbs, you'll be a lucky man. And you Canuck, don't think that you get off easy. You might be great at weilding a ring bell, but are you ready to dance with the lord of the realm of the insane? And are you ready to do it by my rules? At the Pay Per View, and this I guarantee, Canuck, you will feel the pain, and you will meet the same fate as Deader, Reed, Gripwell, Satre, and anyone else that Gates throws in my way. The only thing is I just might not choose to spare you. (Prodigy throws the microphone down on Gripwell, who flinches a bit upon impact. "Their Law" blares over the PA as Prodigy leaves the ring victorious, with his fans going absolutely nuts) Prodigy d. Handey Gripwell with the Firestarter. Quarterfinal Intercontinental Championship Match Dark Angelic Priest (w/ Razor) v. "Mr. Overhand Right" Terry Conklin (w/ D-Generation X) Apparently, the Dark Angelic Priest had more "important" things to do tonight, as he didn't bother to show for the contest. Terry Conklin d. DAP by forfeit. Joe "The Lemon Crusher" Lemon v. Galaxy (w/ Precious) Galaxy made an extremely impressive return to the EWA, thoroughly dominating Joe Lemon, and polishing him off with the SuperNova. Galaxy d. Joe Lemon with the SuperNova. At this point, President Gates appeared on the MegaTron above the ring entrance. Gates: Oh, Prodigy, the humor in your speech. (he chuckles) You claim I'm going back on my deal? Prodigy, you haven't finished the job of defeating the PHC members yet. Therefore, I will treat you how you deserve to be treated...like a pile of *BLEEP*! And furthermore, Prodigy, if you do get lucky and defeat Satre this Saturday, don't count on beating Canuck. Because you see, Prodigy, I said you had to defeat all of the PHC members. So, at WrestleCade, for the third time ever, Prodigy, I, President Duane R. Gates, will be wrestling. My partner? Crazy Canuck, of course. Your partner? Get real...this is a handicap match. (Gates laughs, and the MegaTron fades to black.) Quarterfinal Intercontinental Championship Match Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. Haywood Jublome (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) This was an extremely hard-fought contest. Both men obviously were determined to advance...however, only one could, and that one was Haywood Jublome, after a beautifully executed inside cradle. Haywood Jublome d. Jack Reed with an inside cradle. "The Pee-Con" James Mastin v. "Sycho" Sid Sid proved that he is indeed the ruler of Mastin's world, powerbombing him straight to hell in his EWA debut. Sycho Sid d. James Mastin with the Powerbomb. Vic Deakins v. Ruger Ice Ruger was a house-of-fire tonight, as he completely ripped Deakins apart. Deakins seems to be a mere shadow of his former self. Ruger Ice d. Vic Deakins with the Spinebuster. Quarterfinal Intercontinental Championship Match Mayne Mann v. Crazy Canuck (w/ The PHC) (*As Crazy Canuck stands in the ring the beginning notes to 'Lookin At Me' by Mase plays and the crowd comes to it's feet. From behind the entrance way walks Mayne Mann along with Jackson Montgomery. They make their way to the ring where they get a microphone.*) Mayne: Crazy Canuck, I'll beat your sorry *ss in just a minute. But first I need to introduce a man you all have seen before, but not in an EWA ring. Here is the man who is going to take the EWA Lightweight Division by storm, directly from Hollywood, California. Marcus Grigsby! (*'Money Ain't A Thang' begins to play as Marcus walks out from the entrance way. The 6'3" 228 pounder looks to be in top shape with muscles bulging out of everyplace imaginable. The women in the audience go wild as he prances to the ring. He gets the microphone from Mayne and address his new fans.*) Marcus: Wassup everyone? Yeah I'm in the house and I'm here to stay. Mayne called me up the other day and told me to fly down to Charlotte to catch the Stabbing Westward show and we had a great time. But after the show he talked me into giving Duane Gates a call. Duane offered me a nice fat contract to leave the set of my newest movie, due out in December, to come here and wrestle for him. So here I am, and let this be a warning to all you lightweights. That belt will soon be mine. (*Marcus gives the ring attendent the microphone as the lights in the arena dim. Beams of gold spray everywhere as the Golden One walks out along with Cunning Kim and Carrie.*) Golden One: Mayne, I've got my eye on you tonight and don't even think of having your little friends up there come after me cause I'll have the Triple Threat on them in no time. (*Just as Golden One puts his mic down, Venom hops over the safety guard railing and stands ringside. Golden One walks down the aisle and stops in the middle as Venom watches the beginning of the match.*) From here, we had a solid match between these two, with a very strange ending. (*Midway during the match Will Haynes stumbles down to the ring and leans against the ring apron. This distracts Crazy Canuck who focuses on him. Venom jumps up on the ring apron on the fair side and distracts the ref, while Golden One grabs a chair from ringside. He climbs in the ring and swings at Mayne, but Mayne ducks and Golden One smacks the chair against the back of Crazy Canucks head. Canuck falls to the mat as Golden One cusses to himself. Golden One leaves the ring. Unfortunately for Mann, the referee turned around just in time, and disqualified him. As Mann gets up, from behind Venom dropkicks Mayne causing him to go through the ropes to the outside and crashing to the floor. Venom hightails it out of there as Marcus and Jackson tend to Mayne.*) Crazy Canuck d. Mayne Mann by disqualification. Chris Craft v. Deader Deader looked very good tonight, and easily notched his first victory here in the EWA. Deader d. Chris Craft with The Deadsy. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) The Rock started off this match in control, and got a huge pop for The People's Elbow. However, The Golden One's experience was simply too much for Maivia. The Golden One d. The Rock with The Golden Edge. Will "The Thrill-Pill" Haynes v. Mike Modest Modest had a very solid debut, and this match was possibly one of the best of the night. In the end, the rookie was too much for the again-high Haynes, though, as Modest pulled off the victory. Mike Modest d. Will Haynes with a double-underhook suplex. Folks, due to the fact that I'm not able to focus on this, after a good friend was electricuted this morning, the rest of these results are short. My apologies once again...I know you hate short results, but there's nothing I can do... Quarterfinal Intercontinental Championship Match Thorne Richards v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom Venom d. Thorne Richards with the Extreme Poison. New Demolition v. The Dynasty (w/ D-Generation X) New Demolition and The Dynasty battled to a double disqualification. Non-Title Match Furnace v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Furnace didn't show...Copeland d. Furnace by forfeit. The Extreme Dream Machine (w/ Rebecca Claridge) v. The Public Enemy (w/ D-Generation X) While the ref is trying to stop Louie and Hicks from fighting on the outside, Bunda is about to nail Ghetto with the Showrunner when over the loudspeaker, Terminator by Sevendust begins to play. The fans are baffled by this music, but none of the 4 wrestlers really care. Then, from behind, an unknown man, wearing a pair of khaki shorts and a bermuda silk shirt buttoned a quarter of the way and a pair of sunglasses, comes into the ring and nails Bunda with a chair to the back of the head. He then picks him up into the Torture Rack, then, while still in the Torture Rack nails him with a Death Valley Driver. He rolls Ghetto on top of Bunda, and the ref turns around and makes the three count. The unknown man then grabs the microphone and heads up to the top of the ramp: ???: Bunda, I told you I was coming for you! No, I'm not that joker, the "Bunda Hunter," 'cause, as it turned out, he couldn't get the job done. Bunda, you get your jabrony ass up off that ring apron, and look at me when I speak to you, son! You've got Grady Smith speaking at ya, and if you can't understand what I'm trying to get across to ya, then let that chair upside the back of your head be a lesson to you! I said there's only one person I'm gunning for here in the EWA, and that's you, you piece of trash! And you can guar-an-damn-tee that I'm gonna climb that ladder to the top, rung, by rung, by damn rung, until I get to your sorry ass. Then it's gonna be all academic from there, son, as I'm gonna nail ya with the Choke Buster one more time, and get that EWA World Title around the waist of someone that deserves it a lot more than you, and that's me, "The Rock" Grady Smith! And Public Enemy, if you think I nailed his ass to help you get the win, you'd better check again, 'cause if I step in the ring with either of you, I'll Choke Bust your lights out, too! And son, don't consider this a threat, consider it a promise! (Grady tosses the mic up into the air, and heads on back through the curtain as "Terminator" begins to play again...) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Triple-Toke" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion) Aaron Martin d. KG with the Shoulderbreaker. Aaron Martin is still EWA Light Heavyweight Champion.
