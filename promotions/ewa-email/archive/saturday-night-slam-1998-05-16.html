EWA Saturday Night Slam

16 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 16, 1998 Live, from the General Motors Place, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Bigg Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Four Corners Match -- Winner Recieves U.S. Tag Title Shot Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. The Outsiders v. Harley Hart/Matt Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) The Black Angels v. New Demolition (w/ Semi) Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) v. The Widow Maker The Golden One v. Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King of Swing" Stevie Richards) Non Title Match Van Smack & La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) EWA World Television Championship Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) v. KG (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Sunny) "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) v. Thorne Richards Non Title Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinetal Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Grudge Match The Gunslinger v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Wyldsyde v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat & Rebecca Claridge) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Shawn Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The Bigg Dogg" Aaron Martin (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Shawn Hart started off this contest in very impressive fashion, sending the crowd into a frenzy. Hart appeared to be back to his old self, but Martin caught him with a low blow, and from there, the momentum completely shifted. Martin was able to capitalize, and really gave Hart a beating, ending the match with a spinning DDT. Aaron Martin d. Shawn Hart with a spinning DDT. Aaron Martin retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. Four Corners Match -- Winner Recieves U.S. Tag Title Shot Prophecy (w/ WormBoy) v. The Outsiders v. Harley Hart/Matt Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) None of the Circus members showed, so President Gates determined the match to be a Triangle Match, and announced that if the Circus did not show up for Mind Games, they would be fired. From there, the teams went at it. The Outsiders were the first to go, eliminated by the Natural Born Thrillers. (Harley/Matt) From there, a solid match developed between NBT and Prophecy, but WormBoy's duo were a little more on today, and Prophecy gained a shot at the Eliminators at Mind Games. Prophecy wins the Triangle Match. The Black Angels v. New Demolition (w/ Semi) The Black Angels left the EWA, so ND gets the win. Taz (w/ Bill Alfonso) v. The Widow Maker The Widow Maker put up a good fight, but Taz was very impressive in his EWA debut, and forced WM to tap out to the Tazmission. Taz d. The Widow Maker by submission with the Tazmission. The Golden One v. Makaveli (w/ Suge Knight) Boy, was this a slaughter...The Golden One looked awful, and Makaveli turned some heads in his EWA debut, making short work of TGO. Makaveli d. The Golden One by submission with the Death Row Leglock. The Swing Pad (Hosted by "The King of Swing" Stevie Richards) (Camera fades in after a commercial break to the Swing Pad set. Talk Dirty to Me by Poison cranks over the sound system as Stevie Richards makes his way to the set.) Stevie: Welcome all of you fine and wonderful people to the Swing Pad. Now as you all had better know by now, I'm the King of Swing Dancin' Stevie Richards and this is my segment so things are going to be done my way. And I've been told by the esteemed president of the EWA Mr. Duane Gates that I have to make this little disclaimer: The opinions expressed on the Swing Pad reflect those of Dancin' Stevie Richards and his guest or guests. They do not necessarily reflect the opinions of the EWA or their sponsors. Now that I've gotten that political crap out of the way, let's bring out my guest for this week. You all know this man. He is a member of the only real stable in the EWA. Most of the groups in this federation are a joke. But these guys are the real deal. Ladies and gentlemen, I give you a member of the Triple Threat...he is the EWA United States Champion...Mr. Every Night Justin Hicks and his main squeeze Rebecca Claridge!!!!!! (Self Esteem by The Offspring starts up as Justin Hicks and Rebecca Claridge make their way to the set and take their seats next to Stevie.) Stevie: Mr. Hicks, welcome to the Swing Pad. Now you are the second coolest dude I've seen here in the EWA next to me, Dancin' Stevie Richards. My first question is, how do you get all the chicks? Justin: Well, Dancin' Stevie, it's simple when you're a man with the physical assets of Mr. Every Night. When they refer to your unmentionables as the 8th, 9th, and 10th Wonders of the World, you know you've got something goin' for ya. But as for the chicks, well, Becca here has to chase them away with a stick...she gets a little jealous sometimes. Stevie: I hear you there. You know, my chick Chasey Lain gets a bit jealous sometimes too. But it's cool. I put her in her place when she starts b*tching at me. Justin: Well the First Lady and I have a mutual respect for each other. We believe in communication, self-analysis, and sharing the housework. Stevie: Really? Justin: HA!!! Had you goin' there for a second didn't I? Stevie: Uhhh...no. Justin: Hell, when Becca starts to get jealous of Mr. Every Night, I just tell her that Mr. Every Night can go ALL NIGHT, EVERY NIGHT, with or without her. But for both of us, we want each other so incredibly bad that well, the others just can't compare. Just take a look at a girl like Becca and then these other toothless redneck fans and tell me which one you'd choose? Stevie: Well since you brought up the subject of toothless redneck women, that brings me to that dirty, rotten, stinkin', worthless skank that is Kimberly. Now I know that your chick Becca has a bikini showdown with her at Mind Games. But why? It won't even be a contest, Kimberly has boobs that look like a pregnant cow and Becca...well just look at her!!!! She'd give a corpse a woodrow. Justin: Well, the unfortunate situation that has arisen is that, well, I single-handedly drove the IV Horsemen from the EWA. Dallas Page and his whore, Kimberly, went straight back to Marietta where they came from. They cringed at the thought of having to face the first couple of the EWA, Mr. and Mrs. Every Night. You saw what I did to Flair. Everyone knew what I was going to do to Page. And Kimberly? Well, let's ask Rebecca about that one. Rebecca: Well Dancin' Stevie, before I start, might I add that you have to be one of the damn sexiest men I've ever seen, next to my three men in the Triple Threat. Stevie: Thanks, you're one hot piece of ass yourself. Rebecca: Well, let's just all have an orgy, right here, right now on the stage. Bring Chasey in here and let's go at it. Stevie: Uhhhhhh....well I uhhhh....uhhhhh. Rebecca: Stevie, that's o.k. honey. We'll take care of that piece of business later. But on to Kimberly and her saggy tits. Well, she tried to say I was made of plastic, but damn, just look at this body. (stands and shows off her HOT body to the crowd, the crowd goes nuts in response) The only thing plastic that has ever been in here is my diaphragm. And as for her, you know, I don't think she should have gotten the dollar special at Dr. Jenkins "House of Saggy Tits" because look at her now. She's like a damn faucet leaking plastic all over the place. And those wrinkles? Someone woulda thought someone rolled up her face in a ball and stomped on it. Then, to self gratify herself, she goes and poses nude for some skin magazine. Then she lets the computer artists touch her up so the readers don't puke when they open up the magazine. It really, REALLY disgusts me, Dancin' Stevie. Stevie: They really disgusted me too. That dirty dog walking around thinking she was hot. Well, the King of Swing knows what hot is, and she ain't hot. You, Miss Claridge are hot. My chick Chasey is hot. She is like a charpet dog with all those wrinkles. Now my next question is, what's next for the Triple Threat? Justin: Well, the Triple Threat's kinda been forced to the back burner here in the EWA. This whole Prodigy and Erik Draven crap has just been getting out of hand. The fans don't want to see those two go at it AGAIN. They want to see the First Couple, the ratings winners, Mr. Every Night and the First Lady. Mr. Every Night plans a long and fruitful U.S. Title reign. This idiot who calls himself the Wyldsyde is going to try to take my gold. Oh please!! He's gonna have a helluva challenge trying to beat Mr. Every Night for the gold he rightfully deserves. But Stevie, can I share a little secret with you? Stevie: Sure you can. You know that I've whooped Prodigy's ass on more than one occasion don't you? He ain't so bad. Justin: Well, Stevie, I've been a big fan of yours even before I entered the business, but here's the big secret. Expect to see World Gold around the waist of the Triple Threat here in the near future. This sh*t with the Degenerates has just about pissed us off enough. We run this show as Jeff would say. It's about time someone took notice. Stevie: Well Justin, I have a great deal of respect for you, the Franchise, and the Bigg Dogg. You are the only true stable in the EWA and if you ever need ANYTHING, just let the King of Swing know. I'm more than ready to lend a helping hand. Justin: Well Dancin' Stevie, you know our offer is still on the table, and will be until you accept it. Stevie: And I truly appreciate that. You guys are the real deal in the EWA and Dancin' Stevie will be proud to say that he is cool with the Triple Threat. Justin: That's just because you're Big Stevie Cool. You're cool with the cool and that's the way it goes. Stevie: Justin, I want to thank you for being my guest on the first real edition of the Swing Pad. It's about time that President Gates realized that the only way he can get is to put Stevie Richards on every week and let him have great guests like yourself and Becca. Rebecca: Well Stevie, we thank you for letting us come on the show. And this goes out to President Gates and anyone else who doubts our talents... (Stevie, Justin, and Rebecca stand and say...) WE RUN THIS SHOW!!!! (Camera fades out) Non Title Match Van Smack & La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. Public Enemy (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Degeneration X) As stated before, The Circus did not show. However, New Demolition did, and the two teams brawled all over the arena. EWA World Television Championship Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards (w/ Chasey) v. KG (EWA World Television Champion, w/ Sunny) KG performed about as well as the men he usually attacks at house shows, Serpent Man and James Mastin, tonight. Stevie looked terrific, and coasted to a win. Stevie Richards d. KG with the Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards is the NEW EWA World Television Champion!! "Stunning" Scott Cummings (w/ The Stud Stable) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ Degeneration X) This was a solid match, but in the end, Draven got the better of Cummings. However, as Draven went to pin Cummings after the DX Driver, Prodigy ran to the ring, nailed a DDT on Draven, and quickly exited through the crowd before DX could reach the ring. Erik Draven d. Scott Cummings by disqualification. Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ Phil) v. Thorne Richards Richards is in the midst of the biggest slump of his career, plain and simple. He looked awful tonight, and McCammon coasted to an easy win. Jack McCammon d. Thorne Richards with the Double Hammer. Non Title Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (EWA Intercontinetal Champion, w/ The Triple Threat) Austin's plane apparently was delayed, and Bunda got the forfeit win. Grudge Match The Gunslinger v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Degeneration X) No love lost between these two. The two brawled all over the ringside area, until finally the referee issued a double countout. EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match The Wyldsyde v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (EWA U.S. Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Triple Threat & Rebecca Claridge) This was easily the match of the night, and was one of the best U.S. Title matches since SmirtDogg and Prodigy squared off. The two men exchanged the momentum for nearly twenty minutes, when Hicks flew off the ropes in attempting a lariat. Wyldsyde caught him, however, and executed The Wyldryde. The Wyldsyde d. Justin Hicks with the Wyldryde. The Wyldsyde is the NEW EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!!
