SNS 3-6-99 March 6, 1999 Live, from Cameron Indoor Stadium Durham, North Carolina ---- -- The smoke finally settles after the pyro display opens this edition of Saturday Night Slam. Though the house lights haven't come up yet, the crowd still screams like an arena full of crazed, sign baring, lunatics. All of a sudden a spotlight shines on the ring. Within the confines of the ropes sits a single, blood red couch. The capacity crowd sits and stares, not quite sure what to make of this odd situation. Gradually a chorus of boos with a slight hint of cheers grows louder and louder as people begin to see two figures walking in a spotlight towards the ring. The two men are Angelus and Craven, the two men that currently form The Three. They reach the ring and climb in. Angelus grabs a mic from the announcer at ringside while Craven sits atop a turnbuckle, staring at the entrance way at the top of the ramp. Angelus looks out towards the crowd and places one finger over his lips trying to silence the crowd. They quiet down considerably, but there are still a few who keep speaking their minds. Angelus now puts the mic to his lips and speaks. -- Angelus: Well, here we are. What a glorious evening this is. This is the night the EWA, no the world has been waiting for. Tonight is the night that the third and final member of The Three is revealed. For those of you who haven't guessed who this person is already, allow me to tell you a bit more about this individual. This person has been my spiritual guiding light and motivator. All of the destruction, carnage, and mayem that Craven and I have brought to the EWA is all thanks to this single person. Ever since my beginnings in the AWT my success has been largely indebted to this person. Through my epic battles with Michaels and Draven I have counted on the one that I would like to bring out at this time... -- At this time the spotlight once again lights up the top of the ramp as "Transylvanian Concubine" by Rasputina begins to play. The crowd is shocked by what they see. >From the entrance way steps a female figure. She is dressed in a flowing red and black gown.
