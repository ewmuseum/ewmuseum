EWA Monday Night Madness

2 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
February 2, 1998 Live, from The Summit in Houston, Texas. Booker T (w/ Jacquelyn & The Ghetto Warriors) v. The Widow Maker Monday Night Madness started off in poor fashion, as The Widow Maker didn't even show up for his match. The crowd seemed rather dissappointed that they didn't get to see Booker T in action. Booker T d. The Widow Maker via forfeit. FlashBack v. The Hart Brothers (w/ The Stud Stable) This was a classic tag team matchup, as the two teams traded moves, and delighted the fans. The end came when the fantastic teamwork of the Hart Brothers simply overcame FlashBack, as Matthew and Shawn hit the HartStopper, defeating the previously unbeaten duo of Disco Inferno and Flash Funk. The Hart Brothers d. FlashBack via Shawn Hart's pin on The Disco Inferno after The HartStopper. (13:50) Octavius (w/ The Empire) v. Harley Hart (w/ The Stud Stable) Another exciting matchup, as emotions were running extremely high in this one. Harley Hart came at Octavius like a raging bull through the early portion of the match, but Octavius's experience helped him regain the advantage. However, the young rookie executed a spectacular move to regain the momentum. Octavius was setting Hart up for a powerbomb, when the latter reversed it into a pinning Frankensteiner for the win. Harley Hart d. Augustus via a Frankensteiner. (12:19) Ahmed Johnson (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. Faarooq (w/ The Nation Of Domination) This was a classic brawl, as the two big men went head-to-head. Faarooq gained the early advantage, until Rocky Maivia made his way to ringside. This distratced Faarooq, and it was only a matter of time until Ahmed finished him off with the Pearl River Plunge. Ahmed Johnson d. Faarooq via the Pearl River Plunge. Six Man Tag Team Match The Servants of Twlight (Travis Scott/Timothy David/The Pessimist) v. Degeneration X (Scott Hall{EWA Intercontinental Champion}/Kevin Nash/???) The third man on Team DX turned out to be "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels, as DX simply overwhelmed The Servants of Twlight. After the match, we saw a brawl between the two stables, and believe it or not, a fight broke out between Specimen 22 and Chyna! Degeneration X d. The Servants of Twlight via Scott Hall's pin on Timothy David after the Outsider's Edge. (9:11) Winner will recieve EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Nero (w/ The Empire) v. Vinnie Vegas Vinnie Vegas didn't even show up for the match, as Nero earned his way into a Ground Zero bout with Scott Hall. Nero d. Vinnie Vegas via forfeit. Non-Title Match Marc Anthony (w/ Cleopatra & The Empire) v. "Stunning" Scott Cummings (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ The Stud Stable) Marc Anthony came out raging, as the war between these two stables has heated up tremendously. However, the resilency of Cummings proved to be the X-Factor, as he held on for the win. Scott Cummings d. Marc Anthony via the WipeOut. KG (w/ Sunny & The Stud Stable) v. Kane (w/ Paul Bearer) This was a joke of a match, as KG didn't even last long enough to break a sweat. Once Kane arrived in the ring, KG threw two punches before Kane delivered a chokeslam, and then the Tombstone piledriver. Kane d. KG via the Tombstone Piledriver. (0:21) Handicap Match Death Sentance (Pain/Destruction, w/ Precious) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, w/ Degeneration X) Death Sentance used the 2-1 advantage very well early on, and the crowd was surprised to see Deathstar and Galaxy, two other members of The Morgue, come to the ring during the match. Draven, though, used his sheer power, and somehow pulled this win off by pinning Pain with the DX Driver. Another brawl involving DX then broke out, this time with the Morgue. It seems DX is not making very many friends here in the EWA..... Erik Draven d. Death Sentance via his pinfall on Pain after the DX Driver. (10:28) Six Man Tag Team Match The Empire (Tiberus/Julius/Augustus) v. The Nation Of Domination (Rocky Maivia/Kama Mustafa/D-Lo Brown) This was a very strange battle, and was actually somewhat of a 4-2 match, as Rocky Maivia took ever oppertunity he had to take cheap shots at his former teammates. He eventually left the ring area, which resulted in Kama and D-Lo leaving, and all three Nation members attacking Rocky on the entrance ramp. The Empire d. The NOD via countout. (5:12) "EWA Television Championship Match "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna & Degeneration X) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin In one of the all-time great matches in wrestling history, the two collided again, and once again put on a spectacular show. Austin took the early advantage, with outside interference from Chyna and Shawn Michaels swerving the advantage back to Helmsley. Austin eventually regained control, and tried to apply the Stone Cold Stunner, but Helmsley slipped out of it. Austin then attempted a piledriver, but Triple-H flipped out of it, and delivered a crushing Tombstone piledriver. Austin laid motionless, as Helmsley picked him back up, applied the Pedigree, and obtained the three count. It was later determined that Austin was fine, other than suffering a mild concussion, and he will be in action on Thursday. The show went off the air with Comissioner Slaughter promising a major announcement regarding their Ground Zero rematch at the press conference on Saturday. Triple-H d. Steve Austin via The Pedigree. Triple-H is the first-ever EWA World Television Champion!
