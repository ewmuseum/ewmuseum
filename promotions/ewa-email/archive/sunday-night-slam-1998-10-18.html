EWA Sunday Night Slam

18 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
October 18, 1998 Live from Le Collise Quebec City, Quebec, Canada (The camera fades into the sold-out Le Collise after the Sunday Night Slam intro. The place is rocking, as the camera pans around to different sections of the arena. Finally, it comes to a halt in front of the broadcast position.) Jim Ross: Hello everyone and welcome to EWA Sunday Night Slam! I'm good ol' JR, along with my broadcast colleague Vincent Ashe, and we have an exciting night of action for you tonight, with four championship matches! Vincent Ashe: Plus we'll hear from the President of the EWA, Duane Gates, and his decision on the situation with the EWA Championship. OHHH!! OHHH!! SHAWN!! JR: What in the hell? That's the music of the former EWA Champion, Shawn Michaels, who retired from the EWA last week! And here comes Shawn Michaels, on his way to the ring! VA: Correction, Jim Ross...he's on his way over here! (Michaels makes his way over to the color commentator table, takes a seat, and puts on a headset.) Shawn Michaels: Jimmy-Jim, Vince...how ya doin'? JR: Shawn, I've got to ask you...what are you doing out here? I thought you retired? SM: Oh, that I did, Jimbo, but you can't keep HBK outta the scene completely, and I figured, what the hell, I'd give the fans of the EWA my expertise tonight on Sunday Night Slam! VA: Well, it's good to have you around...now let's take you up to our first match of the evening! Golum (w/ Erica) v. Joe Lemon Golum made a successful debut in the wonderful world of the EWA by soundly destroying the pitiful Joe Lemon in a matter of seconds, leading to his premier EWA victory. Golum d. Joe Lemon with a gutwrench suplex. Slain v. Galaxy (w/ Precious) Slain didn't bother to put in much of an effort tonight, as Galaxy was able to obtain an easy submission victory. Galaxy d. Slain by submission with the SuperNova. Non-Title Match Tommy Dreamer v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) JR: Our next matchup should be a brawl, as Venom takes on Tommy Dreamer. SM: Dreamer's got to stay focused on beating the man inside the ring, though, because if not, Venom will take him outside, and beat him to a bloo... VA: Jesus, look at the entrance ramp! JR: Folks, all the retired stars are out here tonight...here comes the former EWA Champion, The Money Maker Shawn Copeland! (Copeland makes his way down to the commentator's booth, and takes a seat next to Michaels, putting on a headset.) SM: Long time no see, Shawn...this is about the last place I figured we'd meet up again. (he chuckles) Copeland:Yeah, well ya know how it is, HBK. I didn't really expect to see ya around here again without a tear in your eye or something like that either. The way you left was really "one for the books". (Copeland snickers)" JR: Well, Shawn...Copeland, that is...if you don't mind my asking, what brings you out here tonight? Copeland:"C'mon, Jimmy. You and I both damn well know that the EWA isn't HALF the show it was withotu me at the helm. I came out here to do the usual, and basically, save this company." ("The Ultimate Sin" by Ozzy Ozbourne" blares over the PA.) Howard Finkle: The following non-title contest is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! Coming down the aisle, from Yonkers, New York, weighing 246 pounds, Tommy Dreamer! VA: Shawn, what's it going to take for Dreamer to pull off the upset here tonight over Venom? Copeland"WHICH SHAWN!?!? (Ashe laughs) VA: Okay, when I refer to Retiree #1, I'll say HBK. I'll go by Shawn for you. Alright? Copeland:"Well, beating Venom isn't all that hard, but this Dreamer guy has shown me EXACTLY nothing. I still like Dreamer taking this one, based on the sole fact that Venom is nothing but a wanker who will never "measure up" to "The Money-Maker"." SM: Shawn, bro? No offense, but...you're nuts. No way in hell Dreamer takes this one...Venom's on way too much of a roll here recently. ("Instruments Of Destruction" blares over the PA, and the biased crowd immediately erupts into a chorus of boos.) HF: His opponent, from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, weighing 246 pounds, the EWA World Television Champion, The Extreme Superstar, Venom! JR: And that man right there is definitely not on good terms with my broadcast colleague, Vincent Ashe. VA: (mutters) Not at all... This match started off as a brawl in the aisleway, during which Dreamer got a little offense in. But it quickly gave way to domination from Venom, after he used the steel guardrail to his advantage. As a result, Venom was able to secure the victory. Venom d. Tommy Dreamer with the Extreme Poison. Will "The Thrill" Haynes (w/ Intoxication X) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ D-Generation X) This wasn't much of a match at all, as Helmsley absolutely dominated the struggling Haynes, polishing him off quickly and efficiently with The Pedigree. Hunter Hearst Helmsley d. Will Haynes with the Pedigree. The Outsiders (w/ D-Generation X) v. The Black Angels Squash. Plain and simple. The Outsiders d. The Black Angels with Scott Hall's pin on Kaworu after the Outsider's Edge. Jim Ross: Shawn, I know you used to be good friends with Scott Hall and Kevin Nash. Judging from tonight's blowout victory, do you think they've still got it? Shawn Michaels: Jimmy-Jim, trust me, Hall and Nash have got it. Yeah, it may have been Kaworu and Satchiel, but Hall and Nash were on fire toni... JR: Good lord...we've got even more company, because here comes the EWA European Champion, Erik Draven! Draven is headed to the ring with a determined look on his face...Shawn, do you know anything about this? (Michaels remains silent, as Draven takes the microphone.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Quebec City...MAKE SOME NOISE!!! (The crowd pops loudly for the ICON, who grins and continues) Now that is what I'm talking about. (he looks d own at the announcers' table, and points at Michaels) You. Get your sorry, retired ass in here. (Draven holds the top and middle ropes open, waiting) SM: Jimbo, Vince, I think the presence of Shawn Michaels is being requested. So if you'll excuse me...(we hear a 'THUMP' as the headset is placed on the table, and Michaels stands up, and walks to the ring. He steps inside the ropes, walks over to the turnbuckle, and sits on it, waiting.) (Hall and Nash, both still in the ring from their squash over the Black Angels, join Draven, who is standing in front of Michaels. Draven shakes his head, and begins to speak again) "The ICON" Erik Draven: How's the new career, Shawn? (Michaels chuckles.) Shawn Michaels: Career? C'mon, Erik...you know this isn't a 'new career'. You and I both know that I can't completely turn my back on this sport...this is my little way of sticking around. "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash: Now, um...I may be getting a bit, well...deaf in my old age, but...did I just hear HBK say he couldn't completely get outta the game? "The ICON" Erik Draven: It almost sounded like he said he couldn't get completely outta the game...Is that true, Shawn? Shawn Michaels: I'm not following where you're going with this, big man, but yeah, that's what I said. "The ICON" Erik Draven: (he looks behind him, as Triple-H and Chyna hit the ring, and join the party standing in front of Michaels) Damn, but you're a slow *BLEEP*ard, Hunter. < I>(he grins, and turns his attention back to Michaels) Aight, Shawn...see if you follow this. The five of us...(he gestures to the group) We've prettymuch always been a unit, haven't we? Shawn Michaels: You can't keep the Klique down...(he chuckles) Yeah, those were the days...we've always been a unit, whether we were in the same federation or not. "The ICON" Erik Draven: So...for the million dollars, the keys to the sports car, AND the services of Trixie for an entire week... Why break the team up now? Shawn Michaels: I told you, bro...this game has passed me behind. Besides, this is something that Shawn Michaels has to do for Shawn Michaels...it's nothing against the Klique. "The ICON" Erik Draven: You always were a stubborn, stupid jackass, you know that? I know you've been having some severe problems with your back, but you're too much of a goddamn moron to actually admit it, so I'm not asking you to get back in the ring, per se. (Triple-H hands Draven a DX T-shirt. The camera pans over to Michaels, who shakes his head as he sees what it is) Shawn Michaels: Erik, will you ever take no for an answer? "The ICON" Erik Draven: Do the world a favor, Michaels...shut up! Thank you. (he grins) I'm not saying WRESTLE, you idiot. (Michaels just looks at Draven, beckoning for him to continue.) "The ICON" Erik Draven: Oh...so NOW you want me to continue...(he shakes his head and laughs) Well...hold this, first of all. (he tosses the shirt at Michaels, who cat ches it out of the air. Draven hops up onto the top buckle, sits down, and continues) Aight...I'm not saying 'Shawn Michaels should get back into the ring, get dropped on his head umpteen times a night' and all that . What I am saying is that we shouldn't break up the strongest damn force in pro wrestling today... D-Generation X needs a manager, Shawn. Do me a favor and accept, so I don't have to thump you over the head with a chair a few times to knock some more sense into you, aight? (Michaels looks at Draven, simply shakes his head, and exits the ring. DX stares after him in astonishment as he walks all the way back to the entrance ramp and into the locker room.) JR: Folks, Erik Draven has shocked us all by asking Shawn Micha.... BREAK IT DOWN!! ("D-Generate Me" comes on the PA, and to the shock of the crowd, Shawn Michaels returns from the back, dancing around almost like the old Michaels. He enters the ring, and walks over to Erik. He stares at Erik, and suddenly grins, and embraces the man. After the embrace, Michaels takes the microphone.) Shawn Michaels: What the hell...I've got the opportunity to commentate, to settle my disputes with my best friend, and to help guide his career? Hell, Erik....I got two words for ya... I ACCEPT! "The ICON" Erik Draven: You just saved Gatesy about 6 chairs, brah...(he grins) Welcome back. EWA, take a long damn look at us, and realize that WE are the ones that run this damn show!!! And if ya don't like it, well, we've got two words for ya... (Michaels takes the microphone, gets a serious look on his face, and then quickly blurts out...) Shawn Michaels: SUCK IT!!! "The ICON" Erik Draven: (with a -huge- grin on his face) Gates, your worst nightmares just got even worse...hit the damn music!!! BREAK IT DOWN!!! ('D-Generate Me' blares over the PA as DX celebrates in the ring...the camera fades to a commercial. When we return, Michaels is back in the commentator position.) VA: Shawn Michaels, what made you accept that? SM: Well Vince, I figure I can help guide my bro without getting back into the game as a competitor. Copeland:"WHOA!! Let's just hold the reigns right here and there, HBK. You and Erik may be tight, and you and I may have evern patched a few things up, but the fact of the matter, is that no matter what mt relationship with the EWA is, I will always be a member of DX. Like it, love it, or loathe it, I am still the man that calls the shots, and I am the man that STILL makes the money around here. You like that, Shawnie?" SM: I'm not gonna sit here and argue with you about this, Cope, but apparently you don't have as much stroke as you thought you did in DX, because E-Diddy made this decision without you. El Loco Jobbo (w/ Intoxication X) v. Deader Double no-show in this one, to the dismay of the fans. The Pessimist (w/ The Hate Club) v. Thorne Richards Same here. EWA European Championship Match Ruger Ice (w/ Lady Jane) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Shawn Michaels left the commentator's position during this one to stand at ringside for the match. It wasn't needed, though, as Ruger Ice didn't do very well in this one. He got in a little offense, but seemed to be missing a step, and favoring his right ankle. Draven exploited this, and was able to successfully defend his European Title. Erik Draven d. Ruger Ice with The Triple-X Bomb. Erik Draven remains EWA European Champion. EWA Lightheavyweight Championship Match KG (w/ nWo) v. The Prince (EWA Lightheavyweight Champion) ("In the Hall of the Mountain Kings" by Greig begins to play over the arena sound system. Two toga clad womencome through the entrance way and make their way down to ringside, tossing rose petals from the baskets that they're carrying onto the aisle as they pass. As they continue down the aisle, The Prince, dragging the EWA Lightweight Title behind him, emerges from the entrance way and follows down the rose covered path. The two women reach the ring and place their baskets down, climb up onto the ring apron and hold open the ring ropes. When The Prince makes it to the ring, he too climbs onto the apron and through the ropes. He motions to one of the women to fetch him a microphone. One of the women sitting on the ropes slowly gets off and hops off the apron and approaches the ring announcer, who gives her the microphone. She slides the mic into the ring and The Prince picks it up) It was nice of you to show up tonight Deader. You had the opportunity to make a name for yourself, to rise in the rankings with a win over El Loco Jobbo, but you didn't have the decency to show up tonight. Some people around here said it was because you felt that fighting El Loco Jobbo was beneath you. But I know truth. You were too stupid to show up tonight. You probably have no idea where Quebec is. You are most likely in a motel in British Columbia (The Crowd boos, not for The Prince, but at B.C.), to scared to ask anyone for directions. Or maybe you couldn't follow the directions, just like you couldn't follow mine. Good luck, because you'll need it. Now bring me the bootlicker From there, we saw a very entertaining match between two of the finer cruiserweights in the EWA. EWA Intercontinental Championship Match Bret "The Hitman" Hart (w/ The nWo) v. Crazy Canuck (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ the Hate Club) ***A pale, gray light falls over Le Collise in Quebec, Canada, just as the Crazy Canuck-Bret Hart Intercontinental Championship match is about to begin. 'November Rain' by Guns 'N' Roses blares over the PA, as the gray light intensifies in the pitch black arena. A cold, light drizzle starts to fall over the entranceway, as a man in a black trench coat, with dark hair and a black bandana across his face, makes his way out of the back, and stands and lets the rain pour over him for a while. He then pulls out a microphone, and begins to speak, in a slow, solemn voice.*** Mysterio: The rain ... the catharsis of the soul, the rebirth of life itself -- all cometh through the rain. And the rebirth is imminent. Because I have grown bored with the random muggins and the attacks on the burnouts -- primarily, human trash such as Haywood Jublome. I deserve better. I'm a better person than all of that. I deserve my rightful place in this god foresaken company -- the rightful place of a true champion. A champion of misery. The representative of the societal downtrodden, those who have been rejected by the very people who are supposed to love you the most. Well, the EWA has had all these high profile champions of the World -- Shawn Copeland, Justin Hicks, Jeff Bunda, Erik Draven -- who all seem confident and happy with what they are doing in this world. They seem to be accepted by their peers and their society, seem to be happy and contented with their lot in life. Well, since I'm not happy and contented with my lot in life, I can't aspire for that particular accolade -- at least not yet. I've set my sights a bit lower in the EWA eschelon -- I've set my sights upon another title which I know that if I held, I could be the EWA's link to the less fortunate and the downtrodden. And that belt, you ask? The Intercontinental Championship. The belt of misery. A belt whose champions, other than the aforementioned Jeff Bunda, have been nothing short of pathetic. Look at them. Crazy Canuck? A pathetic Canadian. Haywood Jublome? A reject. Shawn Michaels? A retro-porn star. No one of any worth has ever held that belt. So, it's time for me to join the long tradition of those who have been something more than pathetic, but something less than great. It's time to join the tradition of those who cannot do better than a mere mid-card championship. And it's time for me to show the world that I can and will DESTROY anyone who steps into my path of destruction here in the EWA. So, Canuck, Hart, I challenge you. Whoever is able to come out on top of this contest of the disgruntled, well -- I offer the distinct pleasure of losing to me, Mysterio, at the next Saturday Night Slam, only SIX DAYS from today -- then you will see who is truly the champion of the downtrodden. The champion of misery, and the champion of mystery -- MYSTERIO!!! Canuck, Hart -- 6 days left until destruction -- will you live to see 5? ***With that, fog pours forth from the back, as Mysterio disappears. The regular arena lights come back on, as the EWA Sunday Night Slam returns to its scheduled format*** From there, Canuck and Hart came out, shook hands, and wrestled a solid respect match, until both Venom and Triple-H came out. Oddly enough, Venom and Helmsley worked together as a team...for about 30 seconds. Then, this turned into an all-out melee. Bret Hart & Crazy Canuck battle to a no contest. Crazy Canuck remains EWA Intercontinental Champion. EWA North American Championship Match "The King Of Swing" Stevie Richards v. "The Natural One" Michael Modest (EWA North American Champion) Modest started off with the early advantage, using a variety of high-risk manuevers to keep the crowd excited. The crowd was pretty much split down the middle, rooting for both men. Stevie quickly was able to gain the advantage with a series of snap suplexes, and showed some nice technical wrestling. Modest followed suit, reversing a crossface nicely. Stevie was able to reach the ropes, however, and the match continued. The match quickly turned when Modest went for a powerbomb. Stevie actually reversed it into a sunset flip, and only got a two count, but sprung to his feet, and as Modest got up, nailed him with the Stevie Kick. Richards then went fot the cover, and got the three count to become new EWA North American Champion. Stevie Richards d. Michael Modest with the Stevie Kick. Stevie Richards is the new EWA North American Champion!!
