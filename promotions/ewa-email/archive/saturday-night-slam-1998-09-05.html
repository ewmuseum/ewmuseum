EWA Saturday Night Slam

5 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
September 5, 1998 Live, from Rupp Arena Lexington, Kentucky Darkness v. The Outsiders Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. "The Rock" Grody Smith "The Lionheart" Chris Jericho (w/ The Hart Foundation) v. Mayne Mann This match has been rescheduled. Jim Ross: Fans we have a real “treat” for you tonite, as we are about to present to you a special presentation of The Poison Pit, hosted by The New Television Champion, VENOM! (the camera pans over to a dimly lit set. The spotlight shines on the set as Fireworks are lit off. The set looks somewhat like a darker version of the set of “Late Night With Conan O Brien”, with a huge plate glass window in the backdrop.” “Jesus Christ SuperStar” begins to blare over the PA system, as Venom, decked in a blue Armani suit with expensive shades, makes his way onto the set. The crowd erupts with boos. Venom grabs a mic off of the desk and speaks.) Venom: Alright, Alright Alright, you stupid peons, the reason I, YOUR TELEVISON CHAMPION am having a Poison Pit is because I am being paid quite a lot of money to do this weekend edition of the Pit. And I bet all of you beerguts are wondering, "Daah, why is he dooing this special edishun?" Well, I'll tell you why...by introducing this week's special guest...STEVIE RICHARDS!!!! Stevie: TALK TO ME WARRIORS!!! ARRRGGGHHHHH!!!! Oh wait, that's another guy's line. I bet you all didn't expect to see me. Well I decided to grace you all once again by allowing you to see the true Superstar, that's right, the TRUE Superstar of the EWA, Dancin' Stevie Richards. Now Venom, you can be the *in a mocking tone* Extreme Superstar all you want, but I'm the real deal jack. Venom: First of all you 100 pound flaming Queen, you don’t snatch the goddam mic away from me, understand? Second, you mock me again, I’ll have to treat you like your homeboy Scott Levy did….like a little * BEEP*!! Now, why the hell are you back, Queen of Swing??? Stevie: Why am I back? Why am I back? I'll tell you why I'm back. The EWA NEEDS Stevie Richards. That's right. Duane Gates NEEDS me. The day I leave, the ratings drop like a rock. Poor little Duane-O, starts losing money, he begs me to come back. Of course, only after getting a substantial raise and all the perks that a Superstar deserves did I decide to come back. And as for you son, you get in my face again, and you will be shy a few teeth. I'm also back because I look around and see the roster of champions and I almost piss myself laughing. Hell Venom, you're a champion. That says it all. Jabronie city and you're the mayor. Thorne Richards is the world champion? Good God, that's a joke. I've beaten that British ass so many times he has started to like it. *in a British accent* Thank you sir, may I have another? He's a pile of crap. Venom: Listen up Richards. First of all, I AM THE SHOW of this goddam federation. I have the highest rated segment on EWA Television, and goddammit I am the TV Champion!!! I piss myself laughing at your pathetic ass, you little girl!!! Don’t you come on here and talk to me that way, I’m a Star!!! A * BEEP*ing star! Richards, you suck worse than a warm beer. Listen Ham and Egger if this is jabronie city than you’re a $5.15 an hour sanitation worker, emptying my goddam trash in the morning!!!!! Why the hell did you leave in the 1st place…afraid you were going to run into the Extreme Superstar??? Stevie: Afraid of you? Not even close. Hell, you're the first guy in this federation who has had balls since Stevie Richards to come out and say what you want without worrying about the consequences. You're also the first person in the EWA who has had the stones to get right up in my face. I respect that. Venom: * Looks stunned* Um, yea. Well, I’ll tell you this, you 90 pound ham and egger, you got some respect from me, just for the fact that you came out here not being a little sissy ass like the other jabronies in this damn federation. Now, anything else you got to say before I end this show?? I have to go beat up Joe Satre’s stupid “Mr. Whitefolks” ass!! Stevie: Damn right I have something else to say. Thorne Richards, you're a marked man. That belt is mine. That yellow bellied coward Jeff Bunda isn't here to take the beating he deserves so you're taking it in his place. Dancin' Stevie Richards is the straw that stirs the drink and the glue that holds the EWA together. Duane Gates knows it, Venom knows it, and all of you people in the stands know it. Pimpin' Joe? Hell, he'll probably whoop your sorry ass. Venom: * Shoves Richards*. Get the hell off of my set, you skinny piece of snake * BEEP*!!!! I have to give my final thoughts for the night. (Stevie and Venom are about to square off when the security guards rush the set. The guards break up the potential brawl, and the force Stevie to leave the set. Spotlight shines on Venom, as Richards does a crotch chop towards Venom and walks off the set.) Venom: You know, being the GREATEST Televison Champion of all time, I-… (Suddenly, “The British Bulldog” Davey Boy Smith, having just arrived at the arena, rushes on the set and hits Venom from behind. Venom falls over, and Smith grabs him and lifts him over his shoulder. He is about to do his running Power Slam, when security rushes back on the set and subdues Davey Boy, saving Venom’s ass from certain pain. Venom, irate, gets up and grabs the mic) Venom: You Goddam Limey son of a * BEEP* * BEEP*!!! You and your pack of Pink and Black ass pirates won’t get away with this, you goddam bastards!! I’ll beat the * BEEP* outta you and take your wife and- (The rest of this segment has been censored by order of Duane Gates, President of the EWA.) Non-Title Match "Pimpin" Joe Satre (EWA North American Champion, w/ the PHC) v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom (EWA World Television Champion) Satre seemed in control for a good portion of this match, but didn't get the clean win. While Venom was resting on the outside, Bret Hart ran to the ring, and nailed Venom with a chair. He then rolled Venom back in the ring, and Satre pinned him. Venom, furious with this, picked up the microphone and challenged Hart to a submission match at WrestleCade I...Hart gladly obliged. Joe Satre d. Venom with interference. New Demolition (w/ Flame) v. The Jobber Express (w/ Teddy Long) The Jobber Express did a horrible job in this contest, constantly ending up on the wrong side of power manuevers from Semi and Buddy Brown. However, there was an interesting occurance in this contest. As the cameraman panned along during the entrance of The Jobber Express, he caught an image of someone silently sitting on a ledge halfway up the arena....Edge. New Demolition d. The Jobber Express with Buddy Brown's pin on Johnny Swinger after the Bad Ass Bomb. Deader v. "The British Bulldog" Davey Boy Smith (w/ The Hart Foundation) On the way to the arena, one of the airplanes carrying some of the EWA superstars was delayed due to bad weather. These two men were on that plane, and as a result, this contest did not take place. Galaxy (w/ Precious) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin Stone Cold was also on that plane, but Joe Lemon came out in Austin's place to face Galaxy. Galaxy promptly squashed him like a....well, like a lemon. Galaxy d. Joe Lemon with the Supernova. Ruger Ice v. Al Snow (w/ Head) Poor Al. He never stood a chance during this one. Ruger seemed to benefit from the appearance of Lady Jane, who was sitting in the front row, and really fought this match well, methodically taking Al Snow apart. Ruger Ice seems to be on a winning streak ever since he met this new, strange lady. Ruger Ice d. Al Snow with the Spinebuster. Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) v. "The Rock" Grady Smith This quite possibly was the most exciting and entertaining match of the night. Both men seemed extremely intense, and kept the crowd on their feet as they executed near fall after near fall. Grady Smith gained a commanding control with the help of an extremely powerful powerbomb, and from here, dominated the match. However, he made his mistake when he went to the top rope. Dr. Lander rang the bell, and Crazy Eddie emerged just long enough to run against the ropes, crotching The Rock. Lander rang the bell again, and Mr. Deliant emerged, climbing to the top rope, and executing a chokeslam onto Smith from there for the victory. The Walking Triple Threat d. Grady Smith with Mr. Deliant's pin on Grady Smith after a chokeslam from the top rope. (As Jack Reed's match finishes up, the EWA JumboTron jumps to life. We see a wood paneled office decorated with all sorts of wrestling memorabilia, etc. On the desk, we see a name plate, reading "Duane Gates, President" on it. Behind the desk is a large window that overlooks "downtown" Muncie, Indiana, and a large, fullback leather chair facing the window. A voice-over comes across the P.A. System) Voice-Over : The following is an announcement from EWA President Duane Gates. (Like trained primates, the crowd starts to boo loudly. However, the tide quickly turns as the chair spins around and we see Prodigy sitting in the chair. Facing the camera, he leans back, and puts his feet up on the desk) Prodigy: So this is what the position of power is like. It's a nice office you got here Gates. It's large, but so spacious and almost empty.......kinda like your head. (This is met with roars of laughter and cheers. Although this segment is pre taped, Prodigy smiles, albeit briefly) And the view of your lovely downtown Muncie.........no wonder you only come back here once in a blue moon. (Prodigy takes his feet off the desk and leans forward. Eying the pen in the pen holder on the desk, he reaches out, removes the pen, and examines it. It's an exquisite gold pen, probably worth over $500.) Prodigy : Is this the world infamous Gates pen? The one that you used to sell your soul to the devil? The one that all EWA wrestlers, including myself, used to sign their contracts? The one that you use to write out your master plan? I think it is. Oh this pen brings back some memories, but it's filled more with hate than anything, so I think it'd be in the best interest of the federation to dispose of it. (Standing up, Prodigy walks over to the window and opens it up. With pen in hand, he sticks his arm out the window and releases the pen. Craning his neck, he listens for the pen to hit the ground below, but with the office being a number of stories up, it's a little hard to hear. Realizing this, Prodigy closes the window and begins to walk around the office) Prodigy: I know you're watching this right now Gates. I don't care if you're watching your precious Hate Club wrestle, if your backstage, or if you're at home, I know you're watching me right now, wondering how the hell I got into your well guarded office, throwing a hissy fit and drawing up pink slips as I speak. Well, don't have a coronary on my behalf. How I got in is not the important issue........it's what I'm showing you that is of greater concern. Getting to you is not a problem for me, and relieving you of your earthly concerns, that being life itself, is not something that is terribly difficult. No.......take this as a sign.......as a show of power. You may have the power to fine, to suspend, to fire, and to do whatever you wish with this federation, but you see this? (Prodigy stretches out his arm and opens his hand) This is the hand of power, and it will weild it's power over you. It's a hand of influence and of great importance, because when you step into the ring with me at Wrestlecade, a mere week away, all the games are over with. All the gerrymandering, the job squad, the power tripping and the mind games that you try to employ, they all end. You step into the ring and everything that you have worked for, strived for, and stolen from the deserving will be taken away from you. The only thing you need to worry about is will I get rid of you entirely, or leave you with just enough to make you suffer for an eternity? (The crowd lets out a huge pop. Prodigy pauses for a moment) Prodigy : Now if you excuse me, I have to be going now. I found my own way in.........I'll find my own way out. It's the same thing you're going to be wishing you could do at Wrestlecade (And the camera fades to black) Mike Modest v. "Outlaw Immortal" KG (w/ Sunny) These two men were also on the flight that had weather problems...hence, no contest. Will Haynes v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit (w/ The Hart Foundation) Benoit also was on the flight, but Chris Craft was sent out to face Haynes, and Haynes promptly destroyed him in little or no time. Will Haynes d. Chris Craft with the Hangman. No-Holds Barred Match "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. Haywood Jublome The Rock put on an absolutely pitiful performance in this match. On the other hand, Haywood Jublome was hitting on all cylinders, and easily defeated The Rock, settling the differences between the two men. Haywood Jublome d. Rocky Maivia with The Ho Checka'. DOUBLE MAIN EVENT!! Triple Threat World Tag Team Championship Match The Dynasty v. The Public Enemy (w/ D-Generation X) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Ghetto Warriors) This was a very well-fought contest, with an odd ending. At first, Draven and Michaels showed obvious disgust at having to tag with each other. They even came to the ring separately. But as the match went on, both men began to show shades of the great teamwork that has made them one of the greatest duos in wrestling. As Draven was holding Booker T up, Michaels reached into his tights, and pulled out a canister of white powder. Michaels threw some of the powder at Booker T, but Booker T ducked, and the powder hit Draven. Draven reached out, thinking he had Booker T, and grabbed Michaels, immediately executing the DX Driver. As this happened, Stevie Ray hit the Harlem Sidekick on Swap Meet Louie, knocking him down....and onto Michaels. The referee made the three count, and by an extremely odd turn of events, we crowned new EWA World Tag Team Champions. The Public Enemy d. The Dynasty and Harlem Heat with Swap Meet Louie's pin on Shawn Michaels. The Public Enemy are the new EWA World Tag Team Champions!! EWA European Championship Match SmirtDogg (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) Another outstanding contest featured a battle between, to use a school reference, the professor, and the upcoming eager student. And in this bout, until the very end, the two exchanged momentum, never giving complete control to the opposition. Out of nowhere, however, Copeland hit a new move, The Destroyer, onto SmirtDogg. Instead of making the cover, however, Copeland strutted around the ring, allowing SmirtDogg to get to his feet. When Copeland turned around, SmirtDogg nailed him with a VICIOUS Superkick, laying The Money Maker out cold. Smirt then covered him for a three count, but the madness was only just beginning. Copeland quickly recovered, and began a shoving match with SmirtDogg, as Shawn Michaels sprinted to the ring. Michaels yelled something about Smirt using his finisher, and then the two, along with Public Enemy, who came to ringside, attacked Smirt. Soon after that, however, Erik Draven sprinted to the ring, and practically threw Michaels off of Smirt. The two stared at each other, neither backing down, as TPE and Copeland continued to assault Smirt. The Ghetto Warriors then sprinted to the ring, and TPE and Copeland bailed as they helped Smirt up and to the back. Finally, it was only Draven and Michaels in the ring, and the two were still glaring at each other as we went off the air. SmirtDogg d. Shawn Copeland with a Superkick. SmirtDogg is the new EWA European Champion!!
