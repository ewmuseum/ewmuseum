EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

19 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live, from The Springfield Civic Center Springfield, Massachusetts The Poison Pit (Hosted by "The Extreme Superstar" Venom, w/ Special Guest: Haywood Jublome) Jim Ross: And now we turn to a new segment of WNW, one which……which we are proud to present….a new interview segment, the POISON PIT, Hosted by “The Extreme SuperStar” Venom!!! (the camera pans over to a dimly lit set. The spotlight shines on the set as Fireworks are lit off. The set looks somewhat like a darker version of the set of “Late Night With Conan O Brien”, with a huge plate glass window in the backdrop. “Jesus Christ SuperStar” begins to blare over the PA system, as Venom, decked in an Armani suit with expensive shades, makes his way onto the set. The crowd erupts with boos. Venom grabs a mic off of the desk and speaks.) Venom: Alright, You BEEP Damn 9 to 5vers, shut your mouths, button your lips, and pay attention, because what you are witnessing is the beginning of the greatest thing the EWA has offered to the public since they signed me!! That’s right, so now on Wednesday nights, instead of watching these no talent Dugganites you idiots regard to as EWA Superstars attempt to wrestle as good as me, you can sit down, have a beer, and watch Me, The Extreme SuperStar, speak to and interview these ham and eggers, right here, on the Poison Pit!!!!! Now, before I bring out this weeks jabronie, er, athlete, I have a few words to say. This week, Jeff Bunda, the ONLY man I respect in wrestling, has been stripped of the Intercontinental Title Belt, after a record run of holding the title. Jeff, I may not like the people you hang with, or you, but I have to say this, that was one * BEEP* damn good run with the belt. And for this, I salute you in the only way the Extreme SuperStar can…* raises his middle finger to camera* (The Crowd boos) Venom: Shut up you idiot assed Hulkamaniacs, none of you make enough money to even boo me, or even be in my God Like Presence, you * BEEP*ers!!! Now, as it stands, the IC Title is vacant, and the big cheese, Duane Gates, the man who had to put three mortgages on his house just to afford to sign me, has declared that the EWA Intercontinental Title will be up for grabs in a 16 man tournament. And guess who Is in it?? ME!! That’s right. I am on my way to gold, because as I looked down the list of jabronies who are in that tournament, not ONE of them is even worthy enough to wipe the sweat from my * BEEP*s!!!! In the first round I have to face some pathetic waste of human * BEEP* Sack, named Deader. Well…Deader, I just have something to tell you real fast today…You’re looking at the next IC CHAMP, RIGHT HERE!!!!! So if you, and the other 14 ham and eggers that Gates has put in my way to slap down know what’s good for you, you’ll all drop out of the tourney. Now, lets meet our guest, because I also have to beat up That Loser ass named Ruger Ice tonight. Okay, Ham and Eggers, Skanks, resident Ultimate Warrior fans, all of you, please welcome…HAYWOOD JUBLOME. ("How To Survive In South Central" by Ice Cube begins to blare over the PA as the lights flash and Haywood Jublome comes out and makes his way to the ring, to the Boos of the crowd. One fan yells “There’s only room for one * BEEP* Hole on this show!!!”. Haywood walks onto the set and immediately a staredown begins. Venom smirks.) Venom: Hey hey Jabronie, I’m not here to kick your ass tonite, I’m here to conduct an interview. If you don’t like that, then after I kick the * BEEP* and * BEEP*s off of Ruger Ice, I can meet you in the parking lot. Haywood: Do I look worried? Please, don't flatter yourself and if you think your pitiful threat bothers me one bit then you are more of a joke than even I took you for. But hey I need some fun, so yeah I'll beat your ass in the parking lot after the show is over. But one more thing oh foolish one don't threaten me again. My mama did that once.. once. Venom: Your mama has done a lot of things….*Jublome glares at Venoim* Okay okay, I’ve heard enough of your mouth, Ham and Egger. Now, a few questions. What do you feel about your opponent in the 1st rd of the IC Tournament? Haywood: : I feel that Chilly Willy is even more of a joke than you are. Suffice it to say that he could have his own commercial you know the ones..this is your brain, this is your brain on drugs, any questions. I'm going to beat his ass tonight along to the next trick. Venom: I’d figure a piece of trash like you to start talking about drugs. Now, how do you feel about the fact that a pathetic gangster wannabe like you doesn’t have a chance in a 16 man tournament, especially a tournament with Me, The Extreme SuperStar in it?? Haywood: To be honest I belong in this tournament and I don't give a f*ck what anyone thinks. You pose little threat to me punkas*. I think of the tournament like this, it happened the right time for me. The next few months you are all going to see a new Heywood Jublome and this little tournament The Jeff Blunda (grins) Invitational is my coming out party. Venom: Yea, Sure, shut up. You know I will win, and probably kick your lemon head eating ass. Anyway, speaking of gangster trash, what’s with your alliance with the Barrio Warriors of all people?? Haywood: Yo Quiro Taco Bell? Man do I look like I am Spanish mothafuc*a well you comprende this, if You ever diss my homies again it will be the last time and I can promise you that. My alliance with the Ghetto Warriors is just another piece to the puzzle we will be dominating the Ewa in a short time I mean New Jack is going to run through opponents like I run through women and no tag team can touch my homies the Harlem Heat and they are going to do what they have been doing beating down tag team after tag team and having fun doing it and of course Smirt- DO- Double G is going to win his belt back. He never lost it and if it wasn't for all you jealous punks out there he would still be Champ but the way I look at it is like this you did him a favor. You awoke a sleeping Giant. If he wasn't deadly enough before now he has a new found intensity and desire so to Duane Gates and the rest of his joke he calls a staff I say this: You fuc*ed up. And to some it would seem that I am the weak link in the Ghetto Warriors but I am going to focus on slapping some sense into anyone who thinks that in the next few months. Venom: Threats threats, yaaawn. I’ll say what I feel about your little crew of butt buddies. The way you guys are so buddy buddy with each other you all look like the inmates of that TV Show, OZ. Now, How the hell can you Dugganites even call New Jack the King Of Hardcore?! All I say is some short stubby * BEEP* who can’t wrestle a lick and always ends up getting busted open in the first 5 minutes of the match is NOT the King Of Hardcore. Haywood: New Jack would beat your ass, end of discussion. Venom: New Jack also has a fist four feet up your ass, end of discussion, Ham and Egger! By the way, I think that all of you Dugganites suck, none of you have the talent, charisma, nor wrestling ability to even be in the same ring with The Extreme SuperStar. NONE of you * BEEP*s. Haywood: * Grabs Venom By Collar * Look man this is my interview I tire of you cutting me and my homies down and stealing my spotlight. Keep on talking and we will take this show over just like we took over Van Smack's radio station. One more thing The Ghetto Warriors are taking over the Ewa and there aint a damn thing any of you ho's and tricks can do about it. Now get the hell out my face shi*4brains. Venom: Look in your words Blow boy, “Save tha Drama fo’ Yo Trick Momma!!” Get the hell off of me, this suit is worth more than that three room shack your mother calls a house. And where is your daddy…eh???? (Suddenly appearing on the set and creeping up behind Haywood is none other than “The Rock” Rocky Maivia. Haywood does not see him, until Maivia taps Haywood on the shoulder. Haywood lets go of Venom and spins around, when a viscious Rock Bottom connects with Haywood, sending him sprawling on the floor. Venom has a shocked look on his face as The Rock begins to stomp the hell out of Haywood. Then, after giving him a severe beating, Maivia picks up Haywood and brings him toward the Plate Glass window on the set. Rocky then darts Haywood face first through the window!!) Venom: Holy * BEEP*!! Good God!!!!! He’s gotta be dead!!!! (Haywood is laying on the other side of the window, covered in broken glass and bleeding profusely. The Rock walks over to Venom, and speaks into the mic. He points toward the broken Haywood.) Rocky Maivia: Ya smell what The Rock is cookin', jabronie? (With that, he walks off the set and into the back, as the Ghetto Warriors rush onto the set. They begin to attend to the seriously injured Haywood. Venom picks up the mic, with a shocked look on his face. He looks into the camera.) Venom: Everyone..what we just witnessed….what…we just witnessed….was….. great!!! BWA HAHAHAAHAHAHHAAH, that Ham and Egger got chucked through that window like a quarter pounder into a fat womans mouth!!!! Well, that’s all the time we have this week, you bunch of * BEEP*s. Be here next Wednesday, as I, Venom, The Extreme SuperStar and also the Extreme Broadcast Journalist, interviews another one of these hardcore sissy boys, Smirt Dogg! Make sure to watch me beat up Ruger Ice later as well…Good Night, peasents! Ross, back to you! (The attention turns to The Ghetto Warriors, who are covering Haywood. There is a pool of blood on the ground where they are. Scene fades out as WNW goes to commercial break.) First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Dark Angelic Priest (w/ Razor) v. Van Smack Van Smack no-showed yet again, and due to his massive lack of appearances over the past few weeks, he was fired. DAP d. Van Smack by forfeit. Handey Gripwell v. New Jack (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) Handey showed up, but would've been wiser if he hadn't have. New Jack pretty much pummeled him into oblivion. New Jack d. Handey Gripwell with the Gangsta Splash. Joe Lemon v. Furnace In a very impressive outing by Furnace, Joe Lemon was soundly defeated. Furnace d. Joe Lemon by submission with the Red Hot Lock. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Lucien (w/ Razor) v. Terry Conklin (w/ D-Generation X) A great contest saw the cheers for Terry Conklin quickly turn to boos, as his arrival into D-Generation X apparently soured his relationship with the fans. However, Conklin was able to overcome this, and defeat Lucien. Terry Conklin d. Lucien with the Overhand Right. The Widow Maker v. "The Duke Of Dance" Swingin' Richie Stevens A double no-show. Vic Deakins v. Thorne Richards This match has once again been rescheduled, with the announcement that the two will show up or be fired. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match IRK v. Jack Reed (w/ The PHC) Before the match, it was announced that the IRK was leaving the EWA due to personal reasons. As a result, Jack Reed picked up the forfeit win. Ruger Ice v. "The Extreme Superstar" Venom A very good match. The crowd was heavily against Venom, and gave a huge pop for Ruger Ice. Weapons came into play, and the match was ruled no-disqualification, as Venom's head was split open by a wicked chair shot from Ruger. However, Venom kept fighting, and ended the match by cracking a steel pipe over Ruger's head and pinning him. Venom d. Ruger Ice. First Round EWA Intercontinental Championship Tournament Match Will "The Thrill-Pill" Haynes v. Haywood Jublome (w/ The Ghetto Warriors) One word: SLAUGHTER. Jublome came back in a rage from being thrown through the window, and was able to slaughter the Intoxication-X member with relative ease. Haywood Jublome d. Will Haynes with a piledriver. The Eliminators v. Public Enemy (w/ D-Generation X) Obviously, this was a forfeit win for Public Enemy. The Golden One (w/ The Triple Threat) v. "The King Of Swing" Dancin' Stevie Richards Same goes for this contest, as it relates to The Golden One...President Gates announced he will rule on who is the #1 Contender sometime in the next week. Deader v. Prodigy An extremely hard-fought match...Deader showed an enormous amount of potential, but the returning Prodigy showed why he is one of the all-time greats, and pulled off a victory. Afterward, President Gates came to the ring, and announced that he would end his biased rulings against Prodigy only if he could defeat all of the PHC, one per week. He announced that Jack Reed would be first on SNS. Prodigy d. Deader with The Firestarter. EWA European Championship No Disqualification Grudge Match Mayne Mann v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (EWA European Champion, w/ D-Generation X) (*'Lookin' at me' by Mase begins to play as Mayne Mann walks out of the entrance way. He pauses for a moment letting the fans get a good look at him as a small set of pyrotechnics go off behind him. He makes his way down to the ring and gets the microphone.*) Mayne: Before I talk about this next match there is something I need to tell everyone. This past Saturday when Joe Satre interfered in my match against Crazy Canuck, he crossed a line that he'll regret. Satre no more fun and games for you because as of right now, in front of the whole world I'm calling you out in Charlotte, North Carolina for WrestleCade I. You and me in the middle of the ring for a submission match! All the bullsh*t ends there Satre and the Boston Bomb is the perfect setup move for the ... ah didn't think I was actually going to tell you did ya? Satre, WrestleCade, you, me, submission match. (*Mann pauses while the fans give a mixed reaction, mostly a good one*) Mayne: Now it's time for Penzer to announce that scrawny punk Shawn Copeland cause I'm ready to beat the hell out of him. Shawn this was a mistake by you and Duane Gates signing this match because I'm going to take out all the stops so that not only can I punish you, but I can also take that gold belt around your waist. After this match you'll be nothing but a pile of bones and blood and I'll be standing proud as the new EWA European Champion! (*The crowds gives a huge pop as everyone *grin* hates Shawn Copeland*) This was a hard-fought matchup, with an extremely odd ending...about halfway through, this happened. In the middle of the match between Shawn Copeland and Mayne Mann, The Golden One comes out in hospital clothing, appearing as if he just escaped the hospital without any doctor's permission, and gets right in the ring... The Golden One: Hey, hey, hey, Shawny boy, long time no see my friend. Come on, you thought I was actually going to sit back, and just ignore what happened last week. No, no, I don't think so. Hey Mayne, do me a favor buddy, take one for the team and let me deal with this right here, and right now. With that, Mayne Mann walks back to the locker room and is counted out but no one cares because everyone is concerned with the situation in the ring. The Golden One: OK Shawn, so you got my good last week, no doubt about it. I didn't think even you could sink to that level but obviously I was dead wrong. But you see Shawn, if you actually think that by punking Carrie you'd get my attention, GUESS AGAIN. No Shawn, because I don't get emotional over this sort of thing, not the slightest bit. Because I was once told by a very wise woman that you don't get mad, you get even. So without any further ado, let's roll the tape boys. On the jumbotron, the locker room area of the Springfield Civic Center is shown. But the thing that gets to Copeland is that he's sees that his Shawnettes are ALL tied up together with ropes as this woman stands there laughing and waving to The Golden One and Shawn Copeland in the ring. The Golden One: So you see Shawn, you've got your little whores running around like they actually mean something, but I've taken care of that. You want to play this game with me Shawny boy? Well, you better start thinking harder because I'll 1-up you every single god damn TIME. Because Copeland, the way I see it, we're even right now. But I won't settle for that. With that said, that woman from the locker room with the Shawnettes comes out and goes through the stands and sneaks into the ring right behind Copeland. The Golden One: You see Shawn, here's a little tip for you. When you mess with one of the Golden Girls, you mess with them all. This large woman nails Copeland from behind right between the legs causing him to double over. The Golden One: Oh my Shawn, I didn't tell you? Oh man, it must have just slipped my mind. Let me make the formal introductions now. This lovely lady is Shannon. And she's one of Carrie's biggest fans. Because Shawn, I wasn't lying. I wasn't upset about getting punked. But Shannon, she doesn't like seeing women get beat up AT ALL!! But Shawny boy, let's not forget who "The Shining Star" is around here. And last week, I got beat up pretty bad which is why I'm wearing these stupid clothes. So now, now that you're down and out, it's payback time. The Golden One takes the seriously injured Copeland and sets him up for a Golden Edge but waits because down the aisle comes Cunning Kim, carrying a ladder. She comes in the ring and places the ladder on the mat, allowing The Golden One to execute the Golden Edge right on the ladder. He then picks up Copeland by the hair and lets Cunning Kim and Shannon slap the taste out of his mouth. Then, The Golden One grabs Copeland and hits the Gold Medal. Then, as Shannon and Cunning Kim kick Copeland repeatedly in the midsection, The Golden One sets up the ladder right above Copeland and climbs up to the top of it. The show goes to commerical with the scene showing The Golden One with his hands raised on top of the ladder, while Copeland is between the two sides, and Shannon and Cunning Kim stand cross armed in front. Shawn Copeland d. Mayne Mann by countout. Shawn Copeland is still the EWA European Champion. Special Tag Team Match The Dynasty (w/ D-Generation X) v. KG (w/ Sunny) and his mystery partner First off, it was announced that this contest had been changed to a one-on-one match between Shawn Michaels and KG. Michaels did not want to face KG at all, so KG allowed Michaels to leave, only to laugh and announce that next week, at WNW, the two would meet....inside of the Hell In The Cell. ***My apologies for the wierd/short/weak results...you know what all has went on today.***
