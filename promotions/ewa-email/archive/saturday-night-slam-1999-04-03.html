EWA Saturday Night Slam

3 April 1999

Ewa Sns (4-3-99) March 13, 1999 Live from the Worcester Civic Center (Worcester, Massachusetts) ) ---- Tanaka [with The Flock ] vs Joe Lemon Joe Lemon has been on a mini-roll as of late, even won his last match but he ran into a Buzzsaw tonight. It was obvious to everyone in attendance and everyone watching at home, that Tanaka was pissed about losing his belt, and he took it out on Joe Lemon for several minutes before polishing lemon off with the Flying Feather Tanaka d. Joe Lemon w/ The Flying Feather Paksta vs Jonny Cassanova The match started with Cassanova firmly in control he controlled the tempo by punishing his opponent with several high impact manuevers including a sidewalk slam, a tilt-the world-backbreaker and a brainbuster. Unable to get the three count on several seperate occasions, Jonny started to get frustrated and Paksta came roaring back with several rights and lefts, followed by a swinging neckbreaker and a violent chair shot on the outside, followed immediatly by dropping Jonny throatfirst on the steel barricade, Paksta threw his man into the ring and went for the three count, but Cassanova managed to get his foot on the ropes at the last split second. The match went back and forth for several minutes until Cassanova landed a belly to belly suplex, after a armdrag takedown, he immediatly scaled the ropes and went for the Cassanova Crush but Paksta played possum and waited till the last possible moment and stuck his size 13 and hit Jonny flush on the jaw. He then landed a cradle suplex and got the hard fought win Paksta d. Jonny Cassanova w/ A Cradle Suplex " Sexual White Hot Chocolate " Phillip Donovan [with Thorne Richards] vs "Simply Perfect" Shawn Arrows This was a Squash from start to finish. SWHC dominated the young and vastly talented Shawn Arrows. He took his time and looked rather impressive with his technical holds and submission maneuevers, and mixed in some High Flying moves to boot, before finishing Arrows off with the Vanilla Shake Phillip Donovan d. Shawn Arrows w/ The Vanilla Shake "The Icon" Erik Draven [with Kane ] vs Dope Savant Both men no-showed for this match and it was re-scheduled for a later date This match was ruled a No-Decision Thorne Richards vs Juventud Guerrera An excellent match, one in which Juventud dominated early with his high flying. high risk style. Juvi landed several early dropkicks, along with a hurricanrana, and a spinning heel kick in the early going..before throwing Thorne to the outside and going for a Suicide Dive which connected. Juvi seemed very confident and dominant in the early going of this match, but Thorne slowly came back. Thorne started his comeback off with a blocked suplex, and hit one of his own, he followed up with a gutwrench suplex and then an armnar. He proved true to his words when he started to work on the legs and knees of the Latin Up and Comer. Thorne even landed a hotshot, before pulling Juvi's legs between the ring-post and quickly heading outside and after posting Juvi he went for a Figure Four Leglock which the Referee quickly tried to break up. Thorne broke the hold and got right in the face of the ref, and it provided Juvi with a window of opportunity and he didn't pass up the chance. He was waiting for Thorne as he turned around and met him with a standing Side Kick that absolutely leveled the unexpecting Richards. Juvi, who was limping by now rolled onto the inside and went for a Frog Splash on the outside, but Thorne moved just in the knick of time. The match swung back and forth until the 13:23 mark when Thorne Richards landed a European Uppercut before placing Juvi on the top turnbuckle, after a few shots to the jaw, Thorne executed a textbook Superplex, but it seemed to take alot out of both wrestlers and Thorne was slow to follow up. Rather than go for the pin, which he would probally not get, he chose to go for the figure-four leglock and try to get the submission win, but out of no-where, Juventud reversed the manuever into a beautiful cradle and got the win. Juventud Guerrera d.Thorne Richards w/ A Figure 4 Attempt reversed into a Cradle The Twisted Souls [with The aWo]v.Simply Inevitable The Twisted Souls put on a dominating performance tonight against Sikmply Inevitable. They made fresh tags in and out of the ring and looked hyped from start to finish and were methodical in their attack. Translation? They toyed with Simply Inevitable for several minutes before sealing the deal with the Crippler Crossface and got the submission win. After the match, both Ministry and Black Steel came through the ring, through the mat), Ministry wielding a baseball bat, and Black Steel with a lead pipe. Ministry and Black Steel beat the living CRAP out of the aWo with the weapons, and gave each member Just One Fix and the Hour of Chaos. The Twisted Souls d. Simply Inevitable w/ The Crippler Crossface Non-Title Match Lee Tenn [Ewa European Champion]v.The Golden One[Ewa World Heavyweight Champion with The Triple Threat] One of the best matches of the night, without a doubt. This was billed a non-title match, but the way these two went at it, you couldn't tell it wasn't for a title. The Golden One held the initial advantage with pure scientific wrestling. He started off the match with a dropkick and followed up with a hiptoss, a vertical suplex and then a swinging neckbreaker, but Tenn was far from done, after The Golden One missed a legdrop to the throat Tenn sprung quickly to his feet and landed a European Uppercut as TGo was just getting to his feet, Tenn then whipped his man to the ropes and executed a flying lariat followed up with a double-underhook suplex and then a spinebuster Tenn headed up to the top rope and went for a Guillotine Legdrop but he missed badly. Tgo then executed a piledriver followed closely by a senton splash which drew oooh's and ahhh's from the hyped crowd. But TGO was far from done. He picked up his man and executed an atomic drop followed by a dropkick to the back of the Tenn's head and then The Golden One waited for Tenn to get up and as soon as Lee did he went for a Superkick, but Tenn somehow saw the manuever coming and dropped to the floor before springing to his feet and landing an overhand right to the back of TGO's head. The Golden One was nearly out on his feet by now and Tenn went for the cover, but somehow TGO got his foot on the ropes, shocking many, but Tenn wasted no time in following up on his advantage, Tenn picked his man up and executed a Belly to Belly Suplex, followed by a Double Arm DDT, he again went for the cover and the referee was slow getting in position and TGO kicked out. Tenn lost his cool, which was normally out of charachter for him, but this being a huge match was understandable. This enabled TGO a second chance to nail the Superkick and he got all of it this time. However, The Golden One was too hurt to follow up and took the opportunity to take a breather in the corner. The match see-sawed for several minutes before Tenn landed a snap-suplex before whipping his man to the ropes but the Golden One suprised everyone including Tenn with a crucifix and he got the win. The Golden One d. Lee Tenn w/ A Crucifix Non-Title King Of the Ring Semi-Final Match Raven [with The Flock.] v. Terry Conklin [Ewa Television Champion, with The Triple Threat] This was a great match, many gave the Newly Crowned Television little chance to pull a win out, but he suprised many by not only holding his own in this match, a Raven's Rules Match, but dishing out alot of punishment as well. The match started out in the ring, but it didn't last there for long. This match quickly went to the outside and the ref could have counted both men out, but chose to allow it, basically making it not only a No Disqualification Match, but a falls count anywhere match as well. Anyways, onto the match. Both men dished out alot of early punishment in the early going with no clear cut advantage to either man. It was Mr. Overhand Right who got the first clear edge after he reversed a piledriver attempt of Raven on a table into a backdrop. Conklin then executed a piledriver of his own through the table. He then climbed to the top rope on the outside and landed the Countout and went for the cover, but somehow, Raven kicked out Slowly, Raven came back and they are on the ramp now and Raven has just landed a DDT on the ramp, he follows up by taking a nearby cord and wrapping it around Conklins throat before draping him over the edge of the ramp and nearly choking him to death. Raven maintains the hold although the ref tries to break it up, Raven lets it go several seconds later, and hits the Evenflow DDT on the ref, knocking him out. But Raven is far from finished. He calls for a chair from a nearby fan, before threatening to hit him upside the head, and the fan has no choice but to hand him the chair, Raven starts to swing but is kicked in the midsection by Conklin, and Raven is doubled over and drops the chair. Conklin ceases the opportunity and DDT'S Raven into the chair. Conklin then picks up Raven and hits an Atomic Drop and then nails Raven upside the head, splitting him wide open Conklin then is now right behind Raven. as he waits for Raven to get up, so he can land his Overhand Right Suddenly the camera catches a glimpse of a man running through the crowd.. When they zoom in, they can see it is Tanaka. In his Hand he has the top of a broom, he has the rounded piece of wood tightly clinched in his hand. He pushes the crowd from side to side in his pursuit to the two combatants. He hops over the guardrail, Conklin is still waiting on Raven to get up as Tanaka pops into his sight. The two begin to exchange words, until Tanaka bends for a second, and brings up that broom handle and clocks Conklin across the head with it. Conklin suddenly falls backwards and colapses to the ground. Raven rolls over on top of him, as Tanaka is reviving the ref. The ref slides over and The Ref counts to 3. But soon after the counting is done. Tanaka walks over to the Timekeeper's table and picks up Terry Conklin's TV Title. He straps it around his waist. He picks up a mic too. Tanaka : Conklin, my belt was lost, let's say 'stolen' from me on Wednesday. So, all I want to say is, Me and you at the Clash, for the TV Belt.. Tanaka drops the mic, and exits the area with Raven and Chasity. Raven d. Terry Conklin Due to the Interference of Tanaka Send in your rankings here
