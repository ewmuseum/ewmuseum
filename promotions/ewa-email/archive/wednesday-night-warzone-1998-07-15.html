EWA Wednesday Night Warzone

15 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 15, 1998 Live, from University Arena, Muncie, Indiana. Live, from University Arena Muncie, Indiana Interview with EWA President Duane Gates EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Quarterfinal Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Thorne Richards (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ NQ2) The Black Angels v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) Morbid Angel v. Joe Lemon Lee Tenn v. Aaron Martin EWA King Of The Ring Tournament First Round Match Perry Saturn (w/ John Kronus) v. Mayne Mann (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Horsemen) Vertigo v. Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) The Ultimate Fighters v. Darkness (w/ Razor) Divader v. Ruger Ice EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Quarterfinal Match Hannibal Manson v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Horsemen) EWA European Championship Match Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion) Falls Count Anywhere Match KG (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Horsemen) v. Prodigy The Wyldsyde v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Horsemen) EWA North American Championship Match Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (EWA North American Champion, w/ Shae Marks) (At the beginning of the show on Wednesday Night, the lights go out and golden beams come all over the arena, meaning only one thing, The Golden One is back...) (Out of the curtain comes Golden One hobbling down the aisle using crutches to maintain upright. The stunned crowd goes nuts for him as this is certainly an unexpected twist. He takes a mic from the ring announcer and limps up the stairs into the ring for his return address...) Golden One: Well, it's good to be back, no doubt about it. But you see, I never really left in heart because this is my home and this is where I belong, back in the EWA. You know, there's really not too much to do when you've got a bum ankle and the only thing the doctor can prescribe is to sit on your ass all day and don't apply any pressure to it. That's some great advice. It's now all clear why doctors get paid so much. Hell, I could have figured out for myself that I needed rest, but Gates and his jabronies in the back made me go anyway. But rest assured ladies and gentlemen, I'm not here to give you all the story of my ankle. No, because I'm not here to bore you, not by any stretch of the imagination. I'm here because I'm fed up with what's been going on behind the scenes recently, that no one here even knows about. For the most two months, I've done everything I possibly could to stay in touch with this great company, and to stay in the "loop," if you will. Well, not for a lack of trying, but I fell completely out of the scene. I was forgotten. No one even cared enough to send a get-well letter, or even call once to see how I was. When I walked into the EWA and ran through the competition en route to the Television title, people talked about me, they asked about me, they were concerned with my career. But when things go downhill, when things don't fall the way you hope, that's when you can tell who your true friends are. Well, Gates is not, never was, and never will be my friend. That piece of crap sat at home counting his money and he didn't even realize what he had in his hip pocket. He had a star that was still hidden behind the moon. He had a stud blocked off by the other horses in the stable. He had a legend drowning beneath the worthless. He had the Golden One sitting at home, a cripple, and thinking only about his return. Well, at long last, here I am. Sure, I can't lace up the boots right now and don the golden tights, but that is only a matter of time. So what kind of person is President Gates? He's nothing but a cold-blooded, heartless, son of a bitch. I came to him about a month ago and said "Hey Prez, I want to get back into the swing of things here, let me just do one interview, or a little color commentary, or even just announce two combatinents in a match" But no, Gates wouldn't let me out of the dark, and he said it was for my own good. Some way, somehow, my ankle was going to be affected by thanking my fans. Yeah right. Well Gates, here I am, and not you, or anyone else for that matter can stop me. You see, I'm sick and tired of listening to the President here explain how my career is more important than one interview. I'm back Gates, so get used to it. Now, if you'll all follow along with me, let's go back in time a little bit to my last match before I rode off into the sunset. The match where this injury actually happened. I was signed for a Strap match against "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks. Call it a slump, call it an off-night, call it whatever you want, I got my ass handed to me that night. So much so that Hicks managed to snap my ankle in a few different places. He put me on the shelf and added insult to injury by winning the match too. Well, after that match my career had hit a new low. After all of the success I had originally enjoyed in the EWA, to be destroyed like that hurt me just as much emotionally as it did physically. I went home, and sat there for day after day, week after week, with absolutely, positively no hope of return. I didn't know where to go, I didn't know what to do, so I just sat there, like a fool, and all I could think about was one match, one shot to achieve my ultimate glory, to be a major champion in the EWA. I couldn't get over it, there was no getting over it. So as time passed by, I became more and more self-occupied. I truly became a loner. I didn't eat, I didn't sleep, and I didn't talk to a soul. No, instead I searched for it. And wouldn't you believe, I found it. I went searching long and hard, deep down, all over the place, and I found my desire, my intensity, my heart, and my soul. Things I had lost during my first stint here. Hell, I couldn't wrestle, I had lost my livelihood, so there wasn't much else to do. Yes, soul searching did the trick for me. And here I am, back and better than ever. So why did I even bother to come back? Well, thoughts of leaving the EWA certainly crossed my mind, and at a time or two I was actually leaning that way. Hell, I thought about hanging the tights up permanently. But then I got a little present in the mail, from an unknown source. While I have a few ideas on who sent the tape, I'm still not really sure. All I know is this tape was practically a godscend. It was just what I needed to come back to life, just what I needed to wake me up from the nightmare I was calling a life. So I went to my VCR and hit "Play" and sure enough, it was an old match of mine. It was me against the now EWA World and Intercontinental Champion Jeff Bunda. At that time, however, he was merely the Intercontinental Champion, and that belt was on the line in this match. While I didn't walk out of the arena that night as the EWA Intercontinental Champion, I sure as hell walked out with my head held high. Because that night I beat Jeff Bunda, plain and simple. I had the Franchise of the EWA finished right in the middle of the ring. He felt the Edge, and he was going nowhere in a hurry. All I had to do was put my arm over his chest and the title was as good as mine. But no, in reality, I had no chance. I knew the odds were against me going into the match, but it wasn't until this point that I realized my prayers were just that, prayers. Because when I went to fulfill my glory and become the champion, Justin Hicks came out of nowhere and made sure it didn't happen. I went from the Intercontinental Champion to a guy who didn't make good on a title shot in a matter of seconds. Now Mr. Franchise, Jeff Bunda will deny it until he's red in the face. He'll say he wasn't out, he'll say he was fine, he'll say anything except the truth. He was out, gone, finito. That was my night, that was my chance, and Justin Hicks ruined it. Now, I'm a very forgiving man, but that's beyond forgiveness. Because as I sat at home as a useless gimp, I realized there was but one thing I could do. The mission was set, and it was set in stone. The beginning is Hicks, the end is Bunda, and anyone who gets in the middle will join them. I can do nothing to stop it at this point. It's too late. I've tried to avoid it, I've tried my best, but fate is inevitable, and I can't turn my back on it now. The message has been sent my way, and I've answered it. Because when it's all said and done, this star is bound to shine, and Gates can't stop it, Justin Hicks can't stop it, and when we get right down to it, Jeff Bunda can't stop it either. The Golden Era has begun. I've been held back my entire career, but no more. My emergence will come, and it will come soon. I won't need these crutches forever Jeff, and when the crutches go, so do you. Next week, same time, same station, Golden Style. This has been the Golden Hour, so for myself GO, as I leave you this time, just remember Champ, your time is running short, and this hour will become eternity... (With that, The Golden One departs again on crutches as a stunned crowd is left speechless. He's gone for now, but he'll be back, rest assured...) After the Golden One left the ring, "Busted" by Matchbox 20 blared over the PA as President Gates walked to the ring. Gates got a huge response from the crowd, since, after all, WNW is originating from his hometown. Once inside, the ring announcer handed him the microphone. President Gates: It's great to be back here in my hometown of Muncie, but I have a little matter to settle. Prodigy, you seem to have been avoiding the Prodigy Hate Club as of late. My assumption, of course, is that you fear our growing numbers. I mean, The Prodigy Hate Club has myself, the most powerful man in the history of professional wrestling, we have Jack Reed, former EWA World Television Champion, we have Crazy Canuck, one of the brightest up-coming young stars in the EWA, and our newest addition, EWA European Champion, Makaveli. So Prodigy, being that you obviously fear the PHC, I'm going to use my power to sign you a match with them at King Of The Ring. Jack Reed and Crazy Canuck against you...in a handicap match!! So how do you like that, Prodigy? Maybe that will teach you not to disappoint your boss. Have some fun tonight, Muncie! The crowd then cheered again as "Busted" played, and Gates walked to the back. EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Quarterfinal Match Will "The Thrill" Haynes v. Thorne Richards (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ NQ2) Our first actual match tonight was a dandy, as Will and Thorne put on one hell of a technical wrestling clinic. Both men used various submission holds, as we saw a new side of Haynes tonight. However, in the end, Thorne simply overcame Haynes. Thorne Richards d. Will Haynes by submission with the Loch Ness Lock. Thorne Richards advances to the King Of The Ring Semifinal. The Black Angels v. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) The Black Angels fought as they always do: awful. Combine that with Dos Equis being on the top of their game, and...well..figure it out. Dos Equis d. The Black Angels with Tecate Jake's pin on Kaworu after the Kamikaze. Morbid Angel v. Joe Lemon Morbid Angel's debut was an impressive one, as he, simply put, demolished young Lemon. Morbid Angel d. Joe Lemon with Til Death Do Us Part. Lee Tenn v. Aaron Martin The fans actually popped for both men in this match. It was a good solid contest, as Lee Tenn appeared to show no ring rust, but apparently, the nice fan reaction got to him. Tenn turned his back on Martin to acknowledge the crowd, and Martin rolled him up from behind. Aaron Martin d. Lee Tenn with a rollup. EWA King Of The Ring Tournament First Round Match Perry Saturn (w/ John Kronus) v. Mayne Mann (EWA World Television Champion, w/ The Horsemen) (The Eliminators music starts to play. It plays for roughly thirty seconds and nobody comes out from the curtain. Suddenly on the big screen Perry Saturn shows up.) Saturn: Mayne Mann, you aren't even worth my time. In fact, this stupid little tournament isn't worth my time. I'm not a singles wrestler. The only thing I give a damn about is getting our belts back from those two fairies the Showstoppers. So you can take this match and shove it where the sun don't shine. As Saturn was speaking, John Kronus ran in from the crowd, and nailed Mayne Mann with a bat. Saturn quickly ran out, and the two gave Mayne Mann Total Elimination, and then ducked through the crowd before The Dream Team could help. Mayne Mann d. Perry Saturn by disqualification. Mayne Mann advances to the second round of the King Of The Ring Tournament. Vertigo v. Affirmitive Action (w/ NQ2) Another exciting tag team contest for the fans, as a series of near falls in favor of both teams kept the fans on the edge of their seats. However, Heavy Metal Maniac and Robert Thompson began flailing away at each other, and ended up on the outside. 2 Cold Scorpio was momentarily distracted by this, allowing ample time for Jimmy Pow to nail the Powdriver on Scorpio. Vertigo d. Affirmitive Action with Jimmy Pow's pin on 2 Cold Scorpio after the Powdriver. The Ultimate Fighters v. Darkness (w/ Razor) Shamrock & Severn's plane was delayed; Darkness gets the forfeit victory. Darkness d. The Ultimate Fighters by forfeit. Divader v. Ruger Ice A solid debut by Divader, and solid match overall, this one ended in a fifteen minute draw. Divader and Ruger Ice battled to a time limit draw. EWA King Of The Ring Tournament Quarterfinal Match Hannibal Manson v. "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) Hannibal Manson was nowhere to be found, and Castor advances to the semifinals. La Parka (w/ The Circus!) v. "The Money Maker" Shawn Copeland (w/ The Horsemen) This was another solid matchup, but in the end, the former Light Heavyweight Champion edged La Parka out, and gained the victory. Shawn Copeland d. La Parka by submission with The Money Clip. EWA European Championship Match Crazy Canuck (w/ The Prodigy Hate Club) v. Makaveli (EWA European Champion) Canuck carried a paper bag to the ring with him for some reason. This was an outstanding match, as Canuck showed why he is one of the bright young stars of the EWA. However, Makaveli was no pushover, as he was close to the top of his game as well. After seventeen minutes of grueling, heartstopping action, Makaveli finally gained the victory with a powerbomb from the middle turnbuckle. Afterward, Jack Reed and Dr. Deliant came to the ring, pushing Skippy on the way. Canuck then took the mike. Canuck: Well Makaveli, you've proven again to be good enough to join and become apart of the "Prodigy Hate Club" Now since, you promised to take us into a new ERA Jack sent me out to get you a gift, so on behalf of the PHC *picks up paper bag beside him* I present you with this...The Ghetto Belt Now Skippy I haven't forgoten you to you goes this gift certificate for a free jug of Skippy's Peanut Butter yummmmmmmy!!! So know the PHC is bigger and better and reading to take Prodigy down and beat him to a bloody pulp. (Canuck finishes speaking, and they all walk back to the back with smiles on their faces with the exception of Skippy who is disappointed and staring at his gift certificate) (However, the crowd erupts as Prodigy sprints to the ring. Prodigy picks up Skippy, and immediately exits through the crowd.) Makaveli d. Crazy Canuck with a powerbomb. Makaveli retains the EWA European Championship. Falls Count Anywhere Match KG (1/2 of the EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Horsemen) v. Prodigy This was a good match, which for the most part ended up staying in the ring. However, KG made his mistake by going to the top rope too soon, and was promptly crotched. Prodigy then climbed to the top rope, and executed The Firestarter for the three count. Prodigy d. KG with The Firestarter. The Wyldsyde v. "The Hitman" Bret Hart The two men promised a wrestling match for the ages, and they got it. Hart locked the Sharpshooter on Wyldsyde, but Wyldsyde was able to get to the ropes; on the flip side, Hart kicked out of The Wyldryde. The end came when Shawn Copeland appeared in the aisleway, distracted Hart long enough for a small package from The Wyldsyde. The Wyldsyde d. Bret Hart with a small package. ****As the match ends, we here "He Got Game" roar through Muncie. The crowd, knowing that "The Money-Maker" himself, Shawn Copeland will be making his way to the ring shortly. Once the crowd reaches a fevered pitch of booing, Copeland makes his way out to the ring, wearing gold tights and gold boots along with the biggest smile he has ever worn. As he stales in the middle of the aisle, he takes in the scene. Finally, he begins walking to the ring again. Once in the ring, he grabs the mic from David Pinzer and starts his rant...**** Copeland:"Well, well, well. I wish I could stay out here longer and babble more, but I've got a match against "El Taco" or some CENSORED loser later on. I just wanted to say a little piece and then make my way. Later tonight, Jack"The Man"McCammon goes after my North American Title.*The booing fills the arena* Well, Jack...don't get your hopes up. I plan on keeping that belt in my eyesight until The King of The Ring. That's when Deke*Crowd cheers at his name*, Pinky, and myself go at it for the belt. And, seeing how I am the greatest athlete around, I am gonna win that piece of tin. Now, McCammon...I'm sorry it has to be this way, but that's just how it is. You're in the wrong place at the wrong time, and it looks like you've pissed off the wrong man. So I'm out here right now to just say to you...sit back and relax....you are not winning my title tonight, or ever! You got a problem with it? Too damn bad, because there's a line to get at me. So in conclusion, "chummer", just shut your mouth and stay clear of The Dream Team and you can live a tad longer. Hit the music..." But instead of "He Got Game", Bret Hart's music blared over the PA. Hart, still exhausted from his match, walked out to the entrance ramp. "The Hitman" Bret Hart: Shawn Copeland, Boy Toy lover, how dare you involve yourself in my match? (The fans don't know who to cheer, so they just begin to boo both.) Yeah, that's just like the American wrestling fans, show some respect, goddammit! Shawn Copeland, you've made me even more pissed off than I was before, so I'm going to issue this challenge to you, and to that damn patriotic American scum Vic Deakins. Our match at King Of The Ring, if you two have the jam, you'll make it a submission match. Either way, both of you will be Excellently Executed by the best technical wrestler in the history of the world of professional wrestling, The Best There Is, The Best There Was, And The Best There Ever Will Be. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) Another outstanding match saw these two go at it for around 15 minutes before Justin Hicks finally forced Van Smack to submit to the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks d. Van Smack by submission with The Saturday Night Special. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) v. "God's Gift To Wrestling" SmirtDogg (w/ The Horsemen) Five minutes into this match, Van Smack walked to the ring, and began watching from ringside. After another minute, Jeff Bunda made his way out as well. The two simply watched, as Bad and Smirtdogg put on a terrific show. In the end, 2 Bad was seemingly in control, nailing the Double Hammer and making the cover, when Jeff Bunda pulled 2 Bad's leg, breaking the count. Bunda and 2 Bad began to argue, as did Van Smack and Smirt, (Van Smack had entered the ring with a chair), and needless to say, all four men began to brawl inside the ring. Afterward, President Gates announced that Johnny 2 Bad would be replacing Jack McCammon in Saturday's tag team main event as a result of the night's brawl. Johnny 2 Bad and SmirtDogg battled to a double disqualification. EWA North American Championship Match Jack "The Man" McCammon (w/ The Chain Gang) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (EWA North American Champion, w/ Shae Marks) A spectacular match saw Deakins and McCammon gut it out for nearly twenty minutes. Deke went for the EMP, but McCammon backdropped him over, picked him up into a side headlock, and executed the Burn Out for the victory, and his second EWA Title reign. Jack McCammon d. Vic Deakins with the Burn Out. Jack McCammon is the new EWA North American Champion!! As a result, the winner of Vic Deakins/Bret Hart/Shawn Copeland at King Of The Ring will recieve a North American Title shot at the following WNW.
