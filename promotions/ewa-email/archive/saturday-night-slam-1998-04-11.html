EWA Saturday Night Slam

11 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 11, 1998 Live, from The Allen County Memorial Colosseum, Fort Wayne, Indiana. ***NOTE: After WNW, it was discovered that Eclipse had suffered a severe injury to his shoulder, and would be sidelined 1-2 months. Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. The Public Enemy (w/ Dick Dennis) Winner gets a Light Heavyweight Title shot at S.S. Thorne Richards v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) Best 2 of 3 Falls KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. "The Faces Of Foley" (Dude Love, Mankind, & Cactus Jack) EWA World Television Championship Return Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Castor Troy (EWA World Television Champion) "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ Triple Threat) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. SmirtDogg Tag Team Grudge Match "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) & KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) & "The Hearbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Marlena) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Gunslinger & Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. FlashBack (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ The Triple Threat) "Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. The Widow Maker EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Raven (w/ DDT) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) Justin Hicks (w/ Triple Threat) v. The Golden One EWA European Championship Match Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) v. Alex Wright (EWA European Champion, w/ Dance Fever) EWA World Heavyweight Championship Career Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. The Public Enemy (w/ Dick Dennis) This was a very good tag team matchup, as Death Sentance and Public Enemy were on top of their games. But in the end, the newcomers were too much for DS, as Ghetto Boy and Swap Meet Louie obtained the win. Public Enemy d. Death Sentance with Ghetto Boy's pin on Pain after a double powerbomb. (10:32) Winner gets a Light Heavyweight Title shot at S.S. Thorne Richards v. Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) Galaxy looked extremely sharp tonight, and Thorne was -WAY- off his game. Galaxy d. Thorne Richards with the Supernova. (4:11) The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Fire And Ice (w/ Yasmeen) It was announced that The Reckoning had left the EWA; however, Fire And Ice weren't in the building, so they didn't get the win. Best 2 of 3 Falls KooKabura (w/ Sara) v. "The Faces Of Foley" (Dude Love, Mankind, & Cactus Jack) Cactus got the first pin on Koo, and while Koo argued with the referee about the count, Cactus rolled under the ring, switched shirts, and put his Mankind mask on. Mankind then snuck up from behind Koo, and gave him the Mandible Claw. The Faces Of Foley d. KooKabura. EWA World Television Championship Return Match The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Castor Troy (EWA World Television Champion) The Pessimist is no longer in the EWA, but again, Castor was not in the arena. "The Franchise" Jeff Bunda (w/ Triple Threat) v. Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) These two brawled to a double countout, as neither man seemed too interested in the match. "The Rock" Rocky Maivia v. SmirtDogg Neither man showed up. Tag Team Grudge Match "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) & KG (w/ Sunny) v. "Triple-H" Hunter Hearst Helmsley (w/ Chyna) & "The Hearbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (EWA Intercontinental Champion, w/ Marlena) This was total domination, as Michaels never even got the chance to wrestler. Page and KG isolated Helmsley in their corner, and beat him into oblivion. KG/DDP d. DX with KG's pin on Triple-H after the Black Out. (7:54) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Gunslinger & Tommy Dreamer (w/ DDT) v. FlashBack (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Dance Fever) Again, neither team showed up. Arn Anderson (w/ DDT) v. "Gorgeous" Joshua Gardner (w/ The Triple Threat) Gardner was taken apart in his first match, as Double-A really gave him a beating. After the match came the shocker, when Ric Flair and Chris Benoit ran to the ring, and attacked Anderson. DDT attempted to make the save, but were taken out by Diamond Dallas Page. Arn Anderson d. Joshua Gardner with the DDT. (3:44) "Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. The Widow Maker Another domination, as Flair easily defeated the struggling Widow Maker. Ric Flair d. The Widow Maker with the Figure Four. (5:21) EWA United States Heavyweight Championship Match Raven (w/ DDT) v. "Diamond" Dallas Page (w/ Kimberly) This was a terrific match, and the two brawled all over the arena. Halfway through the match, the referee made it no-holds-barred, and the action really picked up then. It took a chair shot to Raven's head by Page to finally get him into position for the Diamond Cutter, and the victory. Dallas Page d. Raven with the Diamond Cutter. (19:59) DDP is the new EWA United States Heavyweight Champion!! Justin Hicks (w/ Triple Threat) v. The Golden One Another outstanding match, but the Golden One continued his impressive winning streak. Hicks was climbing to the top rope, and Golden One simply placed him in the Golden Edge, and pinned him. Afterward, though, he was attacked by the entire Triple Threat. The Golden One d. Justin Hicks with the Golden Edge. (13:23) EWA European Championship Match Prodigy (w/ Degeneration X) v. Alex Wright (EWA European Champion, w/ Dance Fever) Alex Wright didn't even show up, and as a result, Comissioner Hart stripped him of the European Title, and gave it to Prodigy, scowling the entire time. Prodigy d. Alex Wright by forfeit. Prodigy is the new EWA European Champion!! EWA World Heavyweight Championship Career Match "Stone Cold" Steve Austin v. Kane (EWA World Heavyweight Champion, w/ Paul Bearer) Austin attacked Kane from behind at the very beginning, and gave him three Stone Cold Stunners, with Kane immediately sitting up after each one. Kane then chokeslammed Austin, and planted a Tombstone on him, ending Austin's EWA career. Kane d. Steve Austin with the Tombstone. (6:34) Kane retains the EWA World Heavyweight Championship.
