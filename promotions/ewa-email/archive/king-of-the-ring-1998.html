1998 EWA King Of The Ring

25 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 25, 1998 Live, from The Ice Palace in Tampa, Florida (The fans are just filing in to the Ice Palace as bell time approaches. The cameras have just cut on for the EWA's 'Free For All' television program. Various interviews are being conducted around the arena as fans are buying T-Shirts, posters, et cetera.... fans are bringing in all kinds of signs. Well, as bell time approaches, it's about 6:59 PM when the fans grow quiet, anticipating the beginning of the card, when they hear those familiar words -) ARE YOU READY??? (The fans pop like CRAZY when they think that DeGeneration X is about to come out however they notice a few things different... the instruments on the theme are different.... and the video on the EWA Jumbo-Tron sure isn't DX's old video... it's pictures of people taking bong hits! They knew something was up... when they heard the opening lyrics. They grow quiet so they can listen intently -) You think you can tell us what to drink? You think you can tell us how to smoke? Well you'd better get sh*tty..... Bow to the burnouts..... TAKE IT DOWN!!!!! (The usual guitar riff plays, as the lyrics continue....) Intoxicate is what you're lookin' for We just got tired of just being way to so-ber Bout to drink, boy Bout to drink, little man TAKE IT DOWN!!!!! INTOXICATION X!!!!!! INTOXICATION X!!!!! You think you can tell us how to smoke? You know what you're hittin' off? Well you better get used to the way the booze tastes Drink what you've got... maybe it's laced.... Bout to puke, boy Bout to puke, little man... THROW IT UP!!!!! INTOXICATION X!!!!! INTOXICATION X!!!!!.... (The fans are in stunned silence as Aaron Martin and Will Haynes come out from the back, with Aaron Martin smoking off what appears to be your everyday normal cigar... but we all know it's not. It smells like the EWA tech staff burnt some stage rope... but anyhow, the music continues to play until they get down into the ring. Martin climbs into the ring while Haynes just goes over and rests his chin on the turnbuckle to stare into the see of fans....) Aaron Martin: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages, the one Bigg Dogg of the EWA presents to you the newest, most abusing tag team in the history of the WORLD!!! - Willy the Thrilly Haynes and Triple-Toke Aaron Martin, INTOXICATION X!!!!! "Triple-Toke" Aaron Martin: For the thousands drunk in attendance and the millions around the world stoned off their assess...... LET'S GET READY TO SMOKE IT!!!!!! (Martin pulls out ANOTHER 'cigar' and lights it up, this time he gives it to Will Haynes.) Triple-Toke: Well, folks, it's plain and simple. Intoxication X is just stating the obvious - that each and every person out there likes to get drunk or get high or trip balls out or something. Intoxication X is just showing that it's all about those no-goods... except for us, they're the all goods. And Terry Conklin, when you step into the ring wtih me tonight, you'll be the first to try on for size my new finisher - the Volcanoed Bong. I'll toke you out like a twenty-sac on a Friday night, and that's all there is to it. Now, so I don't take up much more of your time, I'll let Willy the Thrilly get in a bit of talk time.... Will Haynes: Uh..... uh..... Aaron, those signs are all kinds of colors, man. Sit here and check this sh*t out. (Martin and Haynes stare into the crowd for about two minutes.) Will Haynes: That was some sweet sh*t, wasn't it? WASN'T IT!!!! Aaron Martin: Yeah it was. Will Haynes: Man, I'm high... Aaron Martin: So am I... (The duo leave the ring with their lit 'cigar' and go back to the back, with the I-X music playing, and this left on the Jumbo-Tron:) The lights go dim and the golden beams shine throughout the arena as The Golden One makes his way down to ringside, again on crutches, to a roaring ovation from the sold-out crowd in Tampa, Florida. The Golden One: Well, thanks for that very warm welcome. Every time I hear that, it assures me I made the right decision in coming back to the EWA. But I've got a lot to get off my chest tonight, so let's get started. The Golden One: First of all, I just want everyone to know that my name will no longer be The Golden One. I heard Shawn Copeland's little ultimatum and I have to say, it was very heartfelt. Finding some sympathy for a cripple by giving me a choice. I could either get "beat up" by the Money Maker, or simply change my name. Now seeing that I'm oh so SCARED of Copeland, I really don't have a choice. So Shawn, you can now call yourself The Golden One, and I'll call myself The REAL Golden One. Because lord knows, crippled or not, I'm still the shining star in this place and as far as you go, well, can we say jealous? So much so, in fact, that you even want my name. Well, let me assure you Shawn, it ain't gonna happen. You see, I'm not just A Golden One. I'm THE Golden One, and that's the way it's gonna be. It's more of a title, than a name, because nobodies like you don't deserve it. But while we're on the name thing, I just have to ask, what the hell is up with running around, calling yourself the "Money Maker." As far as I can tell, that title belongs to the cheapskate himself, Duane Gates. Cause I got a little news flash for you Copeland. People don't pay to see you, they don't want to see you. Hell, I was at an EWA houseshow the other day and you were wrestling Joe Schmo or something. And the only thing that kept me awake during your match was the occassional coin that zoomed by my head en route to you in the ring. You know, now that I think about it, maybe that's why you call yourself the "Money Maker." Because Shawn, I saw you pocketing those nickels and dimes after the match. And isn't it ironic, the great fans of the EWA didn't even deem you worthy of a quarter, for heaven's sake. So Shawn, you and your imagination can go wherever the hell you want, and I'll let you know when I'm ready to hand you your ass on a GOLDEN platter. Now, without any further ado, I'd like to bring out my first of two special guests this evening. Ladies and especially all of you gentlemen out there, here comes a real tag team if you know what I mean. Here comes Kim and Carrie, THE GOLDEN GIRLS! *camera flashes to the aisle where two gorgeous women in their early twenties dressed in short, gold dresses appear before the crowd* Wow, would you look at these two. Eat your hearts out guys. Kim and Carrie are back. The Golden Girls are back. And when I say the Golden Girls, I'm not refering to Sophia and Dorothy and Betty White or any of the old bags who ran around on TV for too long. Because in case you didn't notice, these two are the furthest thing from that. You know, while I was sitting back at the palace in Hollywood, I saw all of these alliances forming between the superstars of the EWA. I mean, you've got the Triple Threat, you've got the Dream Team, you've got the Circus. And I was all alone. No more. I've got these two sweethearts by my side at all times and that's the way it's gonna stay. I know that some of the ladies out there may not be too excited by the Golden Girls, and understandably so. So now I'd like to bring out my second special guest at this time. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome, the #1 contender to the EWA World Heavyweight Title, SMIRTDOGG!! *Smirtdogg comes down to the ring slowly, noticeably alone to do a quick interview before the biggest match of his career.* The Golden One: Now, Smirt, you've got to be very excited tonight. How would you rank Jeff Bunda amongst all of the opponents you've ever faced in your career? "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: If I had to rank Jeff Bunda among all of the previous opponents I went up against I'd have to say that he most likely is the toughest of them all. Wyldsyde, Thorne Richards and Johnny 2 Bad are up there but I expect Jeff Bunda to be my hardest challenge to date. He has his faults though and that's what I fully intend to exploit on in a matter of seconds. The Golden One: Well, I'll admit, I've seen a number of Bunda matches and he really hasn't impressed me that much. I think he not only has his faults, but I think he's very beatable. Case in point, I beat the living hell out of him a few months back. But you know something Smirt, he walked out of that match with the Intercontinental Title. You know why? Because he has the Triple Threat behind him. So I ask you, how can you counter the Triple Threat factor? "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: That'll be quite easy to do. Lets see Jeff Bunda has Stevie Richards and Justin Hicks at his disposal. Aaron Martin wants nothing to do with him. But I, on the otherhand, have KG, Shawn Michaels, Erik Draven and Shawn Copeland to watch my back so if anyone should worry about interference that would be Jeff Bunda. That's one reason I joined the Dream Team. I'm no fool. I know there is strength in numbers and tonight I'll capitalize on that advantage the Dream Team holds over the Triple Threat. The Golden One: That's an interesting point there Smirt. I never really looked at it that way. But, and there's always a but, if you were to use the Dream Team to your advantage, that puts you in the same class as the self-proclaimed Franchise, and his goons. There is no question the Dream Team could very well be your ticket to the title, but how could you feel as a man knowing deep down inside that you needed your buddies to get the job done. Because there aren't too many men out there who can claim to hold an EWA title on their own. So Smirt, can you honestly walk down that aisle night in and night out knowing you aren't worthy of the title? "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Let me clarify the point I made earlier about having the advantage over Jeff Bunda because I have the Dream Team in my corner. Put it this way -- they'll be waiting in the wings and the only way I'm going to call for their assistance is if any other Triple Threat member besides Jeff Bunda comes to ringside. I know I can beat Jeff Bunda one on one -- and that's the way I want it to be but like you stressed before he has a history of getting help to win his matches. Tonight that won't be a factor because The Dream Team will make sure it isn't. So everyone is gonna get to see what they paid for. Jeff Bunda and myself battling it out with the better man winning the prize. The EWA World Heavyweight Title. And of course that will be me. *he gives an arrogant smile* The Golden One: Well, everyone knows all about the Triple Threat. And those who don't, well, give them a week and they're sure to interfere in some manner. But Smirt, enough about you and Bunda, because you've made it clear that the main event tonight will, in fact, be a 1 on 1 fight. What I want to ask you right now is what you were thinking when you formed the Dream Team. I mean, you guys have a lot of talent running around, but you've also got a lot of egos. Erik Draven is a multi-time world champion, Michaels has been successful, even KG is an EWA champion right now. How can the Dream Team possibly co-exist with all of this talent running around? If you are to become the EWA World Heavyweight Champion tonight, that has to create some tension because I'm sure your fellow Dream Teamers have their own aspirations of one day becoming the man in the EWA. "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: The whole idea behind forming the Dream Team was to assemble the greatest talent around the EWA and put them all under one stable. Can we co-exist? I think so. Each member knows their roles. Shawn Michaels and KG are holding down the tag ranks and doing their thing as champions. Shawn Copeland is concentrating completely on Thorne Richards and Mavakeli. Soon he'll have gold. As for Erik Draven and myself we have a competitive thing going with one another. We both respect each other's talents and abilities. He knows full well that right now it's my time to lead the Dream Team into the new millenium as EWA World Heavyweight Champion. I brought him in as the Dream Team enforcer -- I trust him or else he wouldn't have that position. If a fellow Dream Teamer earns and paves their way to the #1 contender spot for what will be my title by the time this night's over then I'll wrestle him in a friendly contest and we'll both go all out. Whoever wins -- it's all good but I don't see that happening because I know that my stablemates believe in me as their leader and know the EWA World Heavyweight Title belongs around my waist. The Golden One: You mentioned one Shawn Copeland. I've just got to ask you, what the hell is this guy's problem? He thinks donning the gold makes him a Golden One. Now Smirt, I know he's your stablemate, but you and me, we go way back, you can tell me the truth, is this guy arrogant, or is he just plain stupid? Because it's common knowledge around these parts that I don't back down from a challenge, and he seems to be providing one. And more often than not, I teach these "disciples" of mine a very painful lesson. "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Shawn Copeland has a bit of arrogance but more importantly he's full of confidence. He's a tremendous talent and I think a match between him and you would be one I wouldn't mind see going down. I don't know about you teaching him a painful lesson -- we'll have to see about that. Don't underestimate Shawn Copeland he's gone toe to toe before with the 1997 TOWC Winner Thorne Richards. Not a bad accomplishment. The Golden One: Well, I don't want to hold you here too much longer Smirt, but let's not forget one very important thing. I went through Thorne like there was no tomorrow, there were no toes, except the ones I was breaking. I don't underestimate Copeland, but I wouldn't mind kicking his sorry carcass around the ring a little bit. Well ladies and gentlemen, I want to thank my good friend Smirtdogg for joining us here on the Golden Hour this evening. And I want to wish him the best of luck tonight against Jeff Bunda. And do me a favor Smirt, teach that poor sap a lesson, because there's nothing I'd like to see more than a new champ here in town. But rest assured, ladies and gentlemen, if this man here doesn't get the job done, I WILL! So once again, Smirtdogg, the leader of the Dream Team, and the next EWA World Heavyweight Champion. Go get em champ *extends hand for a handshake* *SmirtDogg shakes 'The Golden One's hand and says* "God's Gift to Wrestling" SmirtDogg: Thanks for having me on the Golden Hour. I had a great time now if you don't mind it's time for me to go kick Jeff Bunda's ass and win that EWA World Heavyweight Title. Peace out! The Golden One: There he is ladies and gentlemen, the man who is going to do the world a favor and beat Jeff Bunda. And remember Jeff, this is the Golden Hour, and I RUN THIS SHOW!! Until next week, for the Golden Girls, and for Smirtdogg, this has been your shining star. Enjoy the show... I.R.K. v. Divader The opening contest at the 1998 King Of The Ring turned out to be a massacre, as Divader was completely demolished by the smaller, quicker IRK. IRK really looked impressive in this one, executing a flurry of technical moves before finally finishing Divader off with the Killer Leglock. IRK d. Divader with the Killer Leglock. EWA World Television Championship Match Ruger Ice v. Mayne Mann (EWA World Television Champion) You've got to give Mayne Mann credit. In possibly the guttiest performance in EWA history, he hobbled to ringside on a leg he broke just four days ago, and actually gave Ruger Ice a decent match. But Ruger was able to kick Mann square in the leg, and from there, took over, working on Mann's leg. The official finally stopped the match, not wanting permanent damage to be done to Mann. Ruger Ice d. Mayne Mann due to the referee stopping the match. Ruger Ice is the new EWA World Television Champion!! La Parka (w/ The Circus) v. "The ICON" Erik Draven (w/ The Dream Team) "E-Diddy", making his return to EWA action, was extremely impressive, taking La Parka out in less than three minutes. A great return for the "ICON". Erik Draven d. La Parka with the Fallen Angel. Semi-Final King Of The Ring Tournament Match "The Mastermind" Castor Troy (w/ NQ2) v. "Mr. Every Night" Justin Hicks (w/ Rebecca Claridge) These two men came into the King Of The Ring Tournament with one thing in mind: to become King. And they showed that they both deserved it in this grueling, nineteen minute war. Neither man ever held a distinct advantage in the contest, but Hicks was finally able to lock in the Saturday Night Special, and advance to the finals. Justin Hicks d. Castor Troy by submission with the Saturday Night Special. Justin Hicks advances to the finals of the EWA King Of The Ring Tournament. Terry Conklin v. Aaron Martin There wasn't much crowd reaction for Aaron Martin here, mostly because the fans didn't know what to think of Intoxication-X. Conklin got a huge pop, though, and the fans were treated to a solid match. Martin looked to be in top form, but Conklin looked even better, as he eventually defeated Martin. Terry Conklin d. Aaron Martin with the Overhand Right. Special Challenge Match The Ultimate Fighters ("The Beast" Dan Severn & "The World's Most Dangerous Man" Ken Shamrock) v. Harlem Heat (Booker T & Stevie Ray, w/ Jacquelyn) One word. SQUASH. Booker T and Stevie Ray, in their return to EWA action, showed The Ultimate Fighters just how stupid they were by putting their careers on the line against the former 2 Time EWA World Tag Team Champions. Afterward, to add insult to injury, the duo beat on Shamrock and Severn some more, when KG and Shawn Copeland ran out. They too began to beat on The Ultimate Fighters, and soon handed both men (Harlem Heat) "Dream Team" T-Shirts. Harlem Heat d. The Ultimate Fighters with Stevie Ray's pin on Dan Severn after The Big Apple. Semi-Final King Of The Ring Tournament Match Thorne Richards (w/ NQ2) v. "Uncle Sam's Favorite Nephew" Vic Deakins (w/ Shae Marks) Four words can describe this contest: MATCH FOR THE AGES. These two men poured out their hearts, souls, and everything else imaginable in this contest, and the fans were treated to one hell of a contest. Technical wrestling, brawling, even some aerial moves, all were on display in this one, as both men performed moves new to them. Richards started off with the early advantage, but the match would go back and forth every step of the way, with neither man dominating the match at any point. Finally, however, Thorne was able to slip out of the EMP, and execute a small package to get the three count. Both men shook hands and embraced after the match. Thorne Richards d. Vic Deakins with a small package. Thorne Richards advances to the finals of the EWA King Of The Ring Tournament. Click here for more King Of The Ring!! Prodigy Before the match, it was announced that neither Jack Reed nor Taz were in the building, so the European Title match was cancelled, and Makaveli became Crazy Canuck's partner in this one. The Showstoppers (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ The Dream Team) A good, solid contest, in which both teams battled very well....however, The Dream Team's cheating ways played a role. During the match, Harlem Heat came to ringside, and once the official wasn't looking, repaid KG from earlier in the night by Stevie Ray nailing Jimmy Pow with The Flapjack. KG then hit the Blackout, and pinned Jimmy Pow. A return match between the two teams has been signed for Wednesday Night Warzone. The Showstoppers d. Vertigo with KG's pin on Jimmy Pow after The BlackOut. The Showstoppers are still the EWA World Tag Team Champions. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) v. Johnny 2 Bad (w/ The Chain Gang) Van Smack looked nowhere near form tonight, as Johnny 2 Bad was all over him, dominating this intense contest. No DQ, Falls Count Anywhere EWA North American Championship Match The Wyldsyde v. Jack "The Man" McCammon (EWA North American Champion, w/ The Chain Gang) This match lived up to every ounce of hype it recieved. Amazingly, even when he was being assaulted with chairs and tables, The Wyldsyde maintained his technical wrestling, non-punching style. After about 20 minutes, both men were weary and back in the ring, where a table was upright. McCammon stood on the other side of the ring, and ran at Wyldsyde, attempting to deliver a powerful lariat knocking him onto the table, but instead, The Wyldsyde picked him up and executed The Wyldryde....unfortunately for Jack, the table broke his fall. The Wyldsyde d. Jack McCammon with The Wyldryde. The Wyldsyde is the new EWA North American Champion!!
