EWA Thursday Night Terror

12 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 12, 1998 Live, from the Baltimore Arena, Baltimore, Maryland. Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) New Jack (w/ Thug Warriors) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) Dude Love v. LaParka (w/ The Circus!) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Non-Title Match Thorne Richards v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) EWA United States Tag Team Championship FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) EWA European Championship Match "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) Dos Equis (w/ The Circus!) v. Death, Destruction, and Torment (w/ Jake Roberts) Dos Equis looked rather sluggish in their EWA debut, and Raven and The Gunsligner capitalized, putting them away in impressive fashion. DDT d. Dos Equis with Raven's pin on Tecate Jake after the DDT. (6:11) New Jack (w/ Thug Warriors) v. "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels (w/ Degeneration X) No other members, save for Marlena, of Degeneration X were in the house tonight....and it showed. From beginning to end, this was all New Jack, making an impressive debut. New Jack d. Shawn Michaels with the Gangsta Splash. (4:09) EWA Light Heavyweight Championship Match Galaxy (w/ The Morgue) v. SmirtDogg (EWA Light Heavyweight Champion, w/ Thug Warriors) This was an outstanding match, which ended in bizarre fashion. SmirtDogg had walloped Galaxy with a pair of brass knuckles, and covered him for the pin. Galaxy, however, amazingly kicked out. Galaxy went on to regain control of the match, and went to the top rope for a moonsault. Lil' Kim saw this, and began to distract the referee, while Foxy Brown placed a chair on top of SmirtDogg. Galaxy hit the moonsault onto the chair on top of Smirt just as refree Mark Curtis turned around. Curtis thought that Galaxy had attempted to use the chair as a weapon, and promptly DQ'd him. SmirtDogg d. Galaxy by DQ. (25:31) SmirtDogg retains the EWA Light Heavyweight Championship. Dude Love v. LaParka (w/ The Circus!) La Parka also looked sluggish in his EWA debut, and the "Winter of Love" rolled on, as Dude obtained the victory. Dude Love d. La Parka after the Sweet Shin Music. (7:02) Deathstar (w/ The Morgue) v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin This match spilled over to the outside early, and ended in a double countout. Deathstar and Steve Austin battled to a double countout. The Pessimist (w/ WormBoy) v. Van Smack (w/ The Circus!) Neither man showed up. Non-Title Match Thorne Richards v. Eclipse (EWA World Television Champion) Surprisingly, the TV Champ was dominated in this match, as Thorne really worked Eclipse over, finishing him off with the Loch Ness Lock. Comissioner Slaughter then announced that as a result, Eclipse would defend the TV Title at WrestleFest '98 against Richards. Thorne Richards d. Eclipse by submission with the Loch Ness Lock. (8:43) EWA United States Tag Team Championship FlashBack (w/ Dance Fever) v. The Azzkickers (w/ Hannibal Manson) This was a solid tag team match, but the Azzkickers seemed a little bit off their game. Disco and Flash Funk kept Vic Deakins in their corner, not allowing the tag to be made, and it paid off in the form of our first ever U.S. Tag Team Champions. FlashBack d. The Azzkickers with Disco Inferno's pin on Vic Deakins after the Chartbuster. (13:28) FlashBack is the first ever EWA United States Tag Team Champions! Death Sentance (w/ The Morgue) v. Bloodsport (w/ James Vandenburg) Death Sentance put up a valiant effort in this match, but Bloodsport simply overpowered them in the end, as Wrath and Mortis obtained another victory. Bloodsport d. Death Sentance with Wrath's pin on Pain after the Death Penalty. (9:09) EWA World Tag Team Championship Match The Reckoning (w/ WormBoy) v. Harlem Heat (EWA World Tag Team Champions, w/ Thug Warriors) The Snow brothers were way off their game tonight, and Harlem Heat simply destroyed them. Harlem Heat defeated The Reckoning with Booker T's pin on Leon Snow after the Harlem Hangover. (5:14) Harlem Heat retains the EWA World Tag Team Titles. EWA European Championship Match "Das Wunderkind" Alex Wright (w/ Dance Fever) v. KG (EWA European Champion, w/ Sunny) This was a fantastic match, which ended on a sour note. KG had gained the momentum late in the match, and appeared to be ready to go for the Six Shooter, when Deathstar appeared from behind the entrance curtain, taunting KG. KG took off after him, in a chase that ended in the parking lot next to KG's car, where Pain and Galaxy attacked KG from behind. Deathstar went on to pummel KG with a baseball bat, and even slammed KG's arm with his own car door. Alex Wright d. KG by countout. KG retains the EWA European Championship.
