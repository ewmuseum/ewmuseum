-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER Live December 19th, 1999 From the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- [The scene is a dimly lit arena. It is the Corel Center in Ottawa, Canada. You see people loading equipment in. The ring is currently being set up, as the
GWNW staff is picking up excess garbage from the ringside area. When the camera pans upward, towards the ceiling, "Mudshovel" by STAIND begins to play. The shot is a skybox. It has some plush couches, and whatnot. Not to mention a huge
TV screen. Then, the door busts open, it is in fact, Cyrus "The Vyrus"
Williams. He is wearing black jeans, and a Hardkore World t-shirt. He walks in a flicks the light on. He goes over and sits on the couch.]
Cyrus "The Vyrus" Williams: Within 24 hours, I become the Icon of Canada. [Cyrus clicks the TV on, and tunes to MuchMusic. They are playing a sweet clip of "Only God Knows Why" by Kid Rock.]
Cyrus: In that short period of time, I will proceed to walk down to that ring... (Camera pans outward into the arena. The crew is still setting up. Some guy is tightening the ropes.)
Cyrus: And give Rally Jackson a wrestling lesson. And when I say wrestling, I mean taking him and giving him everything from a Rolling Nodowa bomb, to a powerbomb on fuckin' thumbtacks. Rally, I am not denying that you are a great athlete. You have held that damn title for practically a year, but with the new millenium (had to get a millenium plug out there) comes a new champion. And that new champion is me, Cyrus Williams. The man who the censors hate, and the women appreciate. Rally, I have seen that you have not liked my latest,
V-Files. That's Ok. Because, I don't give a flying fuck what the hell you like, don't like, or love. This federation is due for an overhaul, and well, lets just say that I am pushing this federation to the top of the game. This federation sucks right now, and its all because of no good pieces of shit like
Sycho Kid, and ReX Watson, and people like them.
[Cyrus changes the channel to HBO. The Robert Schimmel Comedy Hour is playing.] Cyrus: Sycho Kid, I could care less whether or not you defeated me, and if it wasn't for that asshole Rally Jackson, you would not have had that win. I also understand that yet again, you are beneath the Main Event. And as for your
"Putting Other People Over" bullshit, I don't need a punk like you to put Cyrus
F'n Williams over. Just another one of you high flying fuckers that isn't going to mean shit in this business in the millenium (hehehehe), because no one is going to pay big bucks to see a match where little bitches like you run around and do somersaults on each other. You see, the fans aren't concerned about ability, the ability to innovate, they are concerned about whether you can go out there, and entertain them. We all have our ways. You see, motherfuckers like The Rock go out there, and make idiots sing along with their catch phrases. Me, I don't go for that bullshit. I don't make people smell what I am cooking, I don't cut-off other people in the middle of their speech for a crowd reaction. I go out there and kick some fuckin' ass. So, Sycho Kid, when I win this title, I will personally go out and give you a title shot, to show that the last match was a fuckin' fluke victory. Tomorrow night, man tomorrow night.
[Cyrus flicks the channel to XTV. They are showing a run down of the D2D card.] Cyrus: You know, after looking at this, it could be our best PPV yet. No doubt in my mind. You got Me and Rally, the return of Pure Evil, ReX Watson versus that fat fuck Randy Cobb, and a Boltz match. The rest don't matter, but hey, what are ya gonna do.
[Cyrus turns the TV off, and rubs his eyes.] Cyrus: Watson, let me tell you. After years in this business, you are still going strong. What are you, 40-45 now? Man, I remember you from the ol' IEUW days. And that's a fuckin' while back. ReX Watson, the man who hails from the city with the largest percent of gangs in America, New Orleans, LA. The man who emerged from the Backyard Federation known as IUW. That federation sucked.
Sure, you got your training, but after seeing some of that shit a while back, how many weak ass street sign shots could you take from that midget, Red Hot
Kid? It made me sick to watch you. And to think you evolved from that piece of shit. You best be sure that in the future, I will have a battle with you. If you didn't die of old age yet. I am sure that the fans would pay to see that match, because if there was ever a guy to match you in strengths, it would be me.
[Cyrus clicks off the TV. And begins to get up.] Cyrus: You know, this is Cody's skybox. The big cheese will house his whores up here, and I don't mean women, his corporate ass kissing fuckwads, and when he is done calling matches and doing shitty angles, he will come up here and boss people around. Cody, tomorrow night, I will give you the nightmare of your life
To see the sickest motherfucker in professional wrestling become your champion, and epitomize the Hardkore in Hardkore World. And as always, this fight is dedicated to the vertabrae in Rated X's neck.
[Fade to black. We cut back to the announcer's table.] LeDuct- Well Cyrus has his mind on beating Rally Jackson and becoming only the second ever GWNW Heritage Champion.
America- Rally has held this title ever since the start of GWNW and ran rough shot over all competion.
LeDuct- We may see the crowing of a new champion tonight leading us into not only a new millennium, but a new era in GWNW!!!
Young- Ahh..That doesn't matter. No one cares about who wins this one without a
YPS member being involved. Also who let him in my skybox huh??? Some heads will be rolling once I find out!

Young- The keyboard isn't the only thing that got busted open, look at Rally!!!! <Cyrus now pulls a bleeding Rally Jackson to his feet and props him up in the corner.
