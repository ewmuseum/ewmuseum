GWNW — Card

December 2 Dismember (4/6)

+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- +-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER Live December 19th, 1999 From the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- <We fade back to a shot of a office in the back. Mr. Young, Sapphire, and Sycho
Kid can be seen.>
Sycho Kid- (Yelling) SAPPHIRE WHAT ARE YOU DOING LEAVING SYCHO FOR THE WOLVES!?!?!?!?! Sapphire- I am sorry man. I just didn't like the fact that you took the money! I wanted to wrestle and become number one contenders for the Tag Team Titles. Sycho- I know, and I am sorry. Now that I look back it was a selfish desicion and I should of consulted you.
Mr. Young- Okay guys good. We are letting calmer heads prevail here. Sycho- You know Young this is all kind of your fault... Sapphire- Yeah me and the Sycho Kid where happy without you as our manager. I have had enough of the bullshit and politics of GWNW. I am out of here!!
Young- You can't do that. I got you under contract till 2004! Sycho- Then I got a little idea you might like Young. Since I beat Cyrus live on the last Stampede I should be the new number one contender, but I know you won't grant me the shot unless I add a stipulation. So here it is...If I lose the title match Sapphire and I will leave GWNW forever right after a short shoot interview we get to give live on TV in the ring.
Young- Sounds like a deal to me. Now get the hell out of my office!! <Cut back to a shot of the announcers. America- Well fans due to Young taking care of some YPS business "Trillionaire" Shawn Starks has joined us at the broadcast position. "Trillionaire" Shawn Starks- It's great to be back in the booth Justina. This time not as a heel announcer, but as myself.
LeDuct- I am glad your no longer a corporate pet Shawn. It will be good to have another fan of real old-school "wrestling" calling the action with me.
Starks- Well not only will I be doing some play-by-play, but I am also scouting these two tag teams as whoever wins this match has to defended there GWNW Tag
Team Titles against my Millennium Duo.
-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER [Tag Title Match] Falls Count Anywhere Pure Evil Vs. Dubois Brothers w/Mrs. Alexandra Macabre Ref- Nick Harte Writer- [detail withheld] Time Limit- None -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- <Martian LaCroix is standing in the middle of the ring glancing at his cue cards.> LaCroix- LADIES AND GENTLMEN...The following contest is set for one fall with no time-limit!! This match is a Falls Count Anywhere Match to crown new GWNW
Tag Team Champions!!! Introducing first...
<"Eyeless" by Slipknot begins to play as Bane Wolfcastle and Bludd walk out from under the scaffold tree as fire shoots out from above them. Both men walk down to the ring wearing there leather jackets. Both of them hit the ring and give devil horn signs at the fans.>
LaCroix- At a combined weight of 463 pounds...Currently residing in Berlin,
Germany...Bane Wolfcastle & Bludd...PURE EVIL!!!!!!
<|Fans cheer.|> LaCroix- There opponents... <"Viva Le Québec!" is heard over the loud speakers as out from behind the curtian emerges Ms. Alexandra Macabre emerges from behind the curtians. The
Dubois Brothers come down to the ring in light blue trunks with the Quebec flag on them. Alexandria walks down to the ring with Sadiopath being led behind her on a leash. Alexandria is wearing her leather dominatrix outfit. All four now hit the ring.>
LaCroix- At a combined weight of 469 pounds, from Quebec, Canada...Accompanied to the ring by Ms. Alexandria Macabre and former HardKore World Heavyweight
Champion Sadiopath...Jaques & Phillipe...THE DUBOIS BROTHERS!!!
<|Fans boo and throw trash at them. Camera pans in on a sign that reads "Go back to Quebec". Alexandria now takes the microphone from Lacroix.|>
Ms. Alexandria Macabre- As is customary in Canada due to the two language system, caused by the Canadian majority not allowing Quebec to seperate, The
Dubois Brothers will be introduced in both English and French! Dames et monseiur, de Trois-Rivieres, Quebec, Canada...Phillipe et Jacques...Les Freres de Dubois!!!
<|Fans boo again and throw more trash into the ring.|> *** DING, DING *** Starks- You mean to tell me that guy on the leash held the prestigious HardKore
World Heavyweight Title??
America- Yes he did Shawn. LeDuct- It looks like Bludd and Jacques are going to start off this matchup. <Bludd and Jacques start off by circling around eachother before locking up. Bludd rakes the eyes of Jacques and sends him into the ropes with an irish whip. Jacques comes off as Bludd catches him with a sit-down spinebuster.>
America- Sit-Down Spinebuster! Wow, I have never seen anything like that before.
Starks- What a good wrestling move. Harte starts the count as Bludd remains sitting down. [ 1 ] <Jacques gets the shoulder up.> LeDuct- Way to early to go for a pinfall attempt. <Bludd now gets to his feet and pulls Jacques up by his hair. Bludd now scoops
Jacques up over his shoulder and runs forward before spining down and hitting a sitdown powerbomb.>
America- Evil Bomb! LeDuct- That was a spining Sky-High there that would make D-Lo very proud. Starks- Harte with the count. [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ Thre.... ] America- No way Jose!! It's to early for this one to be over. LeDuct- Jacques manages to kick out, but that was a close call. <Phillipe now enters the ring with a chair. Phillipe nails Bludd right in the back with it doubling Bludd over in pain. Jacques climbs up to the top turnbuckles as Phillipe drops Bludd groin-first onto the top ropes. Jacques now comes off the top with a spinning heel kick that catches Bludd in the mush and sends him falling onto the arena floor!!! Jacques now gets on the ring apron and waits for Bludd to get to his feet. Bludd staggers halfway up as Jacques leaps off the apron and catches Bludd with a bulldog to the floor!!! Jacques rolls Bludd over onto his back and covers him.> [ 1 ]
[ 2 ] [ Thre... ] Starks- Jacques forgot to to hook the leg there. LeDuct- If Jacques would have hooked the leg we may be crowing new tag team champions this very second!!!!
<Bane Wolfcastle now enters the ring as Jacques scoops up Bludd and lays him out across the French broadcasters table. Bane now leaps off the middle ropes onto the top turnbuckles, then leaps off the top turnbuckles!!>
___THUUUUDDDDDDDDDD___ America- Umdrehung der Sterne!!!! LeDuct- WHAT!?!?!? Starks- It's German I think.... America- German for Revolution of the Stars actually or in wrestling terms a double springboard stardust press!!!
Starks- Either way Bane connected and downed Jaques. <Phillipe now tries to leap off the apron and nail Bane with a double axe handle, but Bane catches Phillipe in a bearhug. Bludd now rolls off the announcers table and gets on the ring apron, but turns around to face the ring.
Bane yells something and Bludd and Bludd leaps off the apron backwards after locking the head of Phillipe in a rear chancery.>
____THWWWWAAAAAACCCCKKKKKKKKK____ Starks- INVERTED DDT out of a BEARHUG off the apron!!!! America- Phillipe's head just made a sickining sound as it hit the concrete floor. <Jacques gets up to his feet only to be given a Total Elmination legsweep, spin kick combination by Pure Evil. Meanwhile a bloody Phillipe gets a chair from some French-Canadian fan and waffles Bane Wolfcastle across the back with it, then rolls Bane into the ring and joins him. On the floor Bludd sets Jacques up on the top turnbuckles then climbs up and joins him. Bludd now grabs Jacques and brings him up to his side as Bludd leaps off the top rope.>
___CRAAAASSSSSSSHHHHHHHHHHHH___ <|Fans start chanting "Holy Shit, Holy Shit".|> America- SIDE SLAM THROUGH THE FRENCH ANNOUNCER'S TABLE FROM THE TOP ROPE!!! Starks- Note, Pure Evil are insane! LeDuct- Good point. <Bludd floats over and hooks Jacques leg.> [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 2.8 ] America- How in God's good name did Jacques manage to get a shoulder up? LeDuct- I thought this one was over for sure. Starks- Note: Avoid side slam from top turnbuckles through French announce table on arena floor.
<Bludd now lifts up Jacques and double arm DDTs him right onto the remains of the French announcer's table. In the ring Phillipe now hits a moonsault body block on Bane sending him down to the mat. Phillipe now gets on the apron, leaps onto the second rope and jumps off catching Bludd with an Asai Moonsault as he gets to his feet.>
LeDuct- All four men are down! What an amazing matchup we have on our hands. America- One has to wonder how much longer this matchup can continue. Both of these teams have pulled out all the stops and have to be drained of energy.
<Bane is now on his feet. Bane reaches into his tights and pulls out a staple gun as the fans go wild!!! Bane now rolls out on the arena floor and taps
Phillipe on the shoulder. Phillipe turns around only to be stapled right in the eye!!!!!!>
America- New Jack eat your heart out!!!! LeDuct- That could cause permanent blindness in the eye of Phillipe. <Jacques now recovers and rolls up Bane from behind as he is gloating.> [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 2.6 ] <Bludd saves Bane by stomping on Jacques. Bludd picks up Jacques in a powerbomb with Bane grabs Jacques' head and gives him a jumping reverse DDT right onto a television monitor from the French announce table. Bludd makes the cover.>
America- Pure Evil hits one of there tag team finishers that they call Final
Warning.
LeDuct- Right onto the TV monitor too!! That sent shards of busted glass right into the back of Jacques' head.
Starks- Note: Avoid Final Warning into the TV. [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] *** DING, DING *** LaCroix- Ladies and gentlemen your winners of the match and NEW GWNW TAG TEAM
CHAMPIONS...Bane Wolfcastle and Bludd...The team of PURE EVIL!!!!!!!!!!!
<LaCroix gets the belts from the time-keeper and hands them to Pure Evil. Bane and Bludd hold the titles up in the air as the fans roar in approval.>
America- What a match!! The Dubois gave it there all, but just took to much punishment from the German team.
LeDuct- What a brutal matchup that was. Starks- But it also had some nice, inovative wrestling move mixed in. America- That is what makes GWNW so great. We mix the brawling, hardcore antics with good, inovative wrestling moves.
LeDuct- Fans we will be right back with the GWNW Provincial Title match as champion Randy "Rusty Cage" Cobb takes on challanger ReX Watson in a Ladder
Match.
Starks- That is the second Ladder Match for a title on this spectacular card! It is also ReX Watson's specialty match! LeDuct- The Canadian Randy Cobb all though champion may be at a disadvantage due to this being the specialty match of ReX Watson, and the fact Watson has
Young on his side.
America- Well we will be right back to find out who will walk out of December 2
Dismember GWNW Provincial Champion!!!
<Fade to commercial.>
