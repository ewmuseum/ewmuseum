GWNW King of Death '99 Live Sunday, April 25th, 1999 From the Air Canada Center!!!!!!! <> HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling is going to hold there own King of Deathmatches Tournament, and is opening this 16-man event to anyone in any fed. That is right it is an interfederation tournament, so you had better hurry, and grab yourself a spot. Email me at [detail withheld] to reserve your spot!! This promises to be brutal. If the Tournament fills up you may wrestle another match on the card!!! [King of Death] 16-Man Tournament Representing GWNW..... 1. Hardcore Carey 2. Heritage Champion- "Tigerheart" Rally Jackson 3. Boltz 4. ReX Watson * Deathmatches to be announced with brackets at later date * 5-16 remain hurry and get your spot!!! [Tag Title] Deadzone Match Champs- Boltz & "Tigerheart" Rally Jackson Vs. Challs- TBA -RULES OF THE MATCH- Two face off in a roofed barbwire cage while the other two face off on a scaffold OVER the roofed barbwire cage. Either win by pinfall or sumbission inside the cage, or by throwing your opponent off the scaffold and onto the roofed barbwire cage.
