December 2 Dismember (5/6)

19 December 1999

-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER Live December 19th, 1999 From the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- [The scene fades in and you see a shot of the Canadian Airlines. Then you see the inside of the crowded airport that has people running around with luggage..
From out of a group of people you see Rex Watson emerge. He is wearing black and white Puma pants, a Puma sweater, and a black snowcap... He walks up to the camera and drops his luggage..]
Camera Man- (In a low voice)"We need you to talk about how you like Canada and your match tomorrow.."
Rex Watson- "Canada? I hate Canada! And, I am already beginning to hate you! Don't talk to me again!"
[The camera man speaks up again..] Camera Man- " Please Rex, we need you to talk about GWNW.." Rex Watson- " You want me to talk about GWNW? Maybe if you keep your damn mouth shut I could! No like I said, don't interrupt me!"
Camera Man- " Yes sir.." [Rex begins to speak with an angry look on his face..] Rex Watson- "What the hell is wrong with GWNW? Why do these damn camera crews have to follow me around everywhere I go?"
[The camera man whispers to Rex..] Rex Watson- " It's bad enough that I have to take a plane from New Orleans to this crap hole of Canada, but now I have a camera man following me because I am gonna be wrestling tomorrow! If I had the choice I would be in New Orleans at a 311 show. But do I get to have it my way? NO! I am stuck in Canada with a suitcase filled with stupid Canadian books like "How to Speak French like a
Gentleman"!"
[Watson reaches his hand into his suitcase, removes the French book and throws it across the room hitting an old lady who is walking by..]
Rex Watson-"BONJOUR!" [Breif Pause..] Rex Watson- "I don't want to learn French like a gentleman, nor do I want to waist my time in this frozen hell, called Canada! So, I am gonna get out of this airport and go to my hotel to sleep!"
[Rex begins to walk off but stops..] Rex Watson- "Wait a second.. Did you actually think I would leave before talking about my match tomorrow against Cobb? I know I want to go to my hotel and relax right now, but I have a message to send out to everyone who is doubting my chances against the Rusty Crab.. I happened to accidentally tune in to an episode of GWNW TV when I saw Cobb saying that I came out of nowhere and all of a sudden get a title shot! Cobb, I want to let you know that I was in
GWNW long ago, but I left because I couldn't take dumb ass Canadians like the
Randy Cobb. Cobb, How can you say that when you know that while I was having
Insane matches in a backyard with less than 10 people watching, you were just a sticky white specimen dripping down you fat moms leg? You know that I have paid my dues to be here and I do not want to have to put up with the likes of you! I guess that the best thing I can do is beat you as quickly as I can so I can just get that Procidencial title, fly back to New Orleans, and give this title some credibility! Now please, get these damn cameras out of my face so I can get away from all of these Canadian bastards!"
[ Rex picks up his suitcases and walks off as the scene fades out. We now fade back to a live shot of the arena.]
LeDuct- Also fans ReX Watson is just off his successful HardKore World Light
Weight Title shot where he took the champion Ace Killcannon to the limit and almost ended his year plus reign as champion.
America- Well that was ReX Watson with comments from eariler today about his title matchup with Randy "Rusty Cage" Cobb.
LeDuct- Well we are down to a two person announce team as Starks has left us to join his Millennium Duo in the back.
America- I think Young will lead Cobb out to the ring and then join us in the booth. LeDuct- Take it away LaCroix. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER [GWNW Provincial Title Match] Ladder Match Champion- Randy "Rusty Cage" Cobb Vs. Challanger- ReX Watson w/Mr. Young Ref- Martian LaCroix Writer- [detail withheld] Time-Limit- 1 Hour -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- LaCroix- Ladies and gentlemen...The following contest is set for one fall with a time-limit of one hour!!! The following match is also for the GWNW PROVINCIAL
TILE AND IS A LADDER MATCH!!!! Introducing first...The challanger...
<"Root" by the Deftones begins to play as out from under the tree comes Mr. Young leading Watson to the ring. Watson is wearing his baggy black jeans with an all white t-shirt with "YPS" on it. Watson also has on knee pads under his jeans and wears one elbow pad on his left elbow. Watson is carrying a garbage can full of weapons and drinking a O'Douls. Young carries a metal 20 foot ladder to the ring.>
<|Fans boo.|> LaCroix- Accompanied to the ring by YPS leader and owner of GWNW, Mr. Young... Weighing in at 230 pounds, standing six foot three...From New Orleans, Lousiana...ReX WATSON!!!! His opponent... <"Outshined" by Soundgarden begins to fill the arena as the lights go out and red strobe lights begin to flash. The fans go nuts as out walks Randy Cage wearing full length tights with "Rusty Cage" written down both legs. Randy also has a shirt with a picture of the Canadian flag on it. Cage takes his GWNW
Provincial Title and plays air guitar with it on his way to the ring.>
<|Fans let out the loudest ovation of the night for their fellow countryman.|>
LaCroix- At 6'5'', 333 pounds...Halling from Toronto, Ontario,
Canada...Current GWNW Provincial Champion...RANDY "RUSTY CAGE" COBB!!!!!!!! <|Fans let out a loud face pop.|> *** DING, DING *** <Cobb and Watson circle around eachother.> LeDuct- Both men are trying to get a feel for eachother and develop a stratedgy here in the early going.
<Cobb now picks up Watson and press slams him right over the top rope onto the arena floor!!! All of a sudden out from behind the curtian emerges the Dubois
Brothers all bandaged up from there early beating at the hands of Pure Evil.
Jacques is carrying a Quebec flag to the ring with him. The fans throw trash at the Dubois as they make there way down the asile.>
LeDuct- What are those two doing out here!?!?! Young- Its amazing they aren't in some local ER after what they suffered at the hands of Pure Evil just one match ago.
<The Dubois Brothers distract Cobb while ReX Watson tosses a ladder into the ring, then rolls in under the bottom ropes and beings to set it up.>
America- ReX Watson is going for the win!!! LeDuct- Damn it!! The Dubois are distracting the champ! <Watson begins to climb up the 20 foot ladder rung by rung as the fans begin to yell. Jacques leaps up on the apron as Cobb lunges at him. Jacques leaps out of the way as Cobb turns his attention back to the match and notices Watson. Cobb now leaps at the ladder and knocks it down.>
___THUUUUDDDDDDDDDD____ *** DING, DING *** America- He got the title!! He got the belt. <ReX Watson now falls 20 feet to the mat holding the GWNW Provincial Title in his hands!!!!!>
Young- YES!!! My protoge has won the title from that stupid Canadian!!! LeDuct- Cobb was just a second to late, and the Dubois Brothers have screwed
Randy Cobb. <Cobb now pounds his fists on the mat in disgust as the Dubois Brothers now get into the ring and whip Cobb into the ropes and nail him with a double clothesline as he comes off. Jacques now gives Cobb a backbreaker as Phillipe climbs up the turnbuckles and comes off with a moonsault kneedrop to the back of Cobb's neck!!! Jacques now takes the Quebec Flag and lays it over the prone body of Cobb.>
Jacques Dubois: "Vous voyez Randy Cobb, votre phrase doit souffrir jusqu'à ce que le Québec soit exempt du métier Canadien et Britannique. Et dernier je vous ai contrôlé les bâtards que laids sont toujours là-bas."
SUBTITLES:You see Randy Cobb, your sentence is to suffer until Quebec is free from Canadian and British occupation. And last I checked you ugly bastards are still over there.
<All of a sudden "Johnny is a Bastard" by Johnny Violent ruptures the confusion of the Corel Centre audience and sends them into a frenzy. The strobe light follows the 340 beats per minute tempo of his ring music, and shows the audience leaping up & down in a surreal looking celebration.>
LeDuct- "The Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie for the second time tonight!! <The Dubois Brothers and ReX Watson hurry and clear the ring as MacKenzie comes bolting down the asile way. MacKenzie hits the ring and attendes to Randy
Cobb.>
LeDuct- Well those two men will take on the unlikely team of Boltz & "Godly" Ken Davison on the upcoming edition of Sunday Night Stampede, but right now both of them half to have The Dubois Brothers at the top of their agenda after multiple attacks on Cobb from the Brothers from Quebec.
Young- ReX Watson has done me proud here in his GWNW return matchup!!!!! America- Well fans we will be right back with the match everyone has been waiting for..RALLY/CYRUS!! Hopefully it will be a tad longer than that last match too.
<Fade to a promo for Rally/Cyrus.>
