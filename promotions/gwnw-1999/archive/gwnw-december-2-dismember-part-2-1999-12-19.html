GWNW — Card

December 2 Dismember (2/6)

19 December 1999

-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER Live December 19th, 1999 From the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- <We fade into a shot of the announcer's sitting at their ringside table.> America- Welcome back fans! Next up tonight is a Light Air Title Ladder Match. LeDuct- This should be a classic action filled matchup between these two light weights. Young- I hope we get to see some wicked ladder spots tonight. LeDuct- I would just like to take a second before this match to welcome back senior offical Dan McKinney to GWNW. After taking a few months off for personal reasons he is back here tonight live on PPV and will referee this next contest. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER [GWNW Light Air Title] Ladder Match Champ- Boltz Vs. Challanger- King Ref- Dan McKinney Writer- [detail withheld] & [detail withheld] Time-Limit- 25 minutes -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- <The shot cuts to Martian LaCroix in the ring with his que cards.> LaCroix- The following matchup is for the Great White North Wrestling Light Air
Title!!!! It is set for one fall with a twenty minute time-limit. Introducing first... <King emerges from the back, while the lights dim. Smoke billows from all sides as he walks to the ring. Some sort of eerie Beethoven music is pounding the speakers. King is wearing black pants with a barbed wire "KING" written down the legs. He gets to the ring, and bounces off the ropes, while screaming something in the air.>
LeDuct: King, the Undertaker of GWNW. America: Don't get us sued. Hopefully my Boltzy will kick his gimmick infringing ass. <"Whatever" by Godsmack starts to play. The first chords are hard, and when the vocals kick in, Boltz emerges, with a Barbed Wire, Bloody "Boltz" appears on the Video Wall. Boltz is wearing cut-off Adidas pants, with black boots, and a t-shirt that reads "boltzisgod", The GWNW Light Air Title is glistening in the light. He enters the ring, and the music fades out.>
Martian LaCroix: Introducing first, the challenger. Hailing from Mexico City,
Mexico........The True International Man Of Mystery.....KING!!!!
<King salutes the crowd.> Martian LaCroix: Now, his opponent, from Parts, Unknown, The holder of 16 different titles, including the GWNW Light Air Title & Number 35 on the RSPWF
Top 200... BOLTZ!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
*** DING, DING *** <King goes after Boltz, but Boltz grabs King and hits a Samoan Drop. Boltz then picks King up by the hair, and hooks him for a suplex. He goes for it, but King shimmies his way out at the last second, coming from behind Boltz, and nailing a German suplex. King picks him up, nails a Tazmission-plex, finally King lifts him up again, and hits a bridged Dragon Suplex for an early pin.>
1 . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . . NO! America: I am timing this fight at 30 seconds! LeDuct: That was one of the fastest exchanges I have seen in a while. (Boltz gets to his feet, and gets into a collar and elbow with King. Boltz backs King to the corner, where the referee starts the five.)
Ref: 1, 2, 3, 4.... (Boltz releases, but hits a Flair-like chop. The crowd goes "WHOO"! Boltz chops him again, "WHOO!", again, "WHOO!". He grabs King by the mid-section and nails an Overhead Belly-To-Belly.)
America: Yeah! Get him Boltz! LeDuct: (Cough).slut.(Cough).Brown noser.(Cough) America: Shut your fucking mouth LeDuct. (Boltz lifts King up and sends him off the ropes. On rebound, Boltz hits a flying headscissors, and swings King under the ropes, outside the ring. King gets up, only to be greeted with a plancha by Boltz!)
America: LeDuct, what do you have to say about that? I told you Boltz is superior. LeDuct: I think the silicone is going to your head. <Boltz now rolls King back in the ring then leaps up onto the ring apron. Boltz grabs the ropes and flips back into the ring with a leg drop across the throat of King. All of a sudden Ken Davison emerges from behind the curtians and starts to walk towards the ring. Boltz now picks up King only to take him back down with a snap mare. Davison is now ringside and grabs a ladder. Davison now slides the ladder into the ring and Boltz grabs it.>
Young- Davison is now out here helping out Boltz!
LeDuct- That is jumping the gun. Who says he slid that ladder in the ring for
Boltz benifit?
<Boltz now takes the ladder and jams the end of it right into King's mid-section. Boltz now sets up the ladder and begins to climb up it toward the
Light Air Title which is dangling from the rafters. King now manages to get to his feet and drop kicks the ladder!!!! Boltz goes flying through the air and gets crotched on the ring ropes!!! Davison now slides a second ladder into the ring.>
Young- What a brutal matchup. LeDuct- Davison is the ladder supplier I guess. America- Ouchy!! I hope my Boltzy is okay. <Boltz now falls off the ropes and hits the mat in agony as King unfolds the new ladder and sets it up in the middle of the ring right under the Light Air
Title. King now slowly starts climbing up the ladder while Boltz gets the other ladder off the ropes and props it up right next to the ladder King is climbing.
Boltz slowly starts to climb his ladder as King reaches the top of his and pumps his fists in the air. Boltz now is at the top of his ladder and reaches over and nails King with a punch to the gut. Boltz now grabs the head of King and leaps off the ladder!!!>
LeDuct- ACE CRUSHER FROM THE TOP OF THE LADDER!!!! America- Boltz risks his own body to deliver the stunner from the top of the ladder. <|Fans start a "Holy Shit, Holy Shit" chant.|> <Boltz now tips one of the ladders over and folds it back up. Boltz now scoops up King and puts him over his shoulders. Boltz now leaps up in the air, spins around, and spikes King's head right onto the ladder!>
America- SCREWDRIVER, SCREWDRIVER!!! LeDuct- Settle down there! You take a class at the Oklahoma School Of Rasslin' Broadcasting? <Boltz now climbs the ladder that is still standing and grabs the Light Air
Title!!! Davison nods his head towards Boltz then leaves.>
*** DING, DING *** LeDuct- What a battle! These two light weights did not put on a Lucha attraction tonight, but they gave us an all out battle.
Young- You have to respect that. America- My Boltzy-Woltzy wins the big matchy!!! Young- Shuddddauup you annoying whinny bitch! [As Boltz stands in the ring, arguing with the Ottawa fans, and pointing to his
Light Air title when suddenly the lights go out]
LeDuct- Wha...where's the lights Young- I thought I paid that bill... ["Johnny is a Bastard" by Johnny Violent ruptures the confusion of the Corel
Centre audience and sends them into a frenzy. The strobe light follows the 340 beats per minute tempo of his ring music, and shows the audience leaping up & down in a surreal looking celebration]
LeDuct- "The Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie!! Starks- We haven't seen him since that battle royal! Looks like his troubles with GWNW management are over!
["The Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie slides into the ring and dropkicks the ladder into Boltz's face.]
LeDuct- MacKenzie tosses Boltz over the top rope and onto a table, and then tosses the ladder over the ropes ontop of Boltz on that table.
[MacKenzie goes out to the floor, grabs two chairs, and then re-enters the ring. He stands a chair up against the ropes, and holds onto the other one]
Starks- MacKenzie runs off the ropes, vaults off the chair, standing on the middle of the top rope for a moment and then springboard 450 chair smash to the ladder on Boltz's face, sending him through the table!!!
[Corel Centre is on their feet, as MacKenzie celebrates out in the audience for a little while with the fans, and then tosses Boltz back in the ring.]
LeDuct-Listen to the reception The Canadian Dragon is recieving! [Fans chant "WEL-COME BACK!!! WEL-COME BACK!!!! WEL-COME BACK!!!!" MacKenzie smiles back and grabs the house mic]
"The Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie: (standing over Boltz) "There you are
Boltz. I've been looking all over for you. But I should have known you were out here cowering in the safety of Cody's soft schedule and monster push. Where it doesn't matter if you know a Falcon Arrow from Falcon Crest. Being Young's
Personal Whatever did wonders for you out here. (holds up the Light Air title)
You even got handed this title. HANDED it! Who'd you win it from? What the hell is Light Air? This belt is about as worthless as Rex Watson in the sheets, or so his sister says. You need to be handed everything Boltz that's why this is the only Hardkore region you can ever hang in, and you couldn't hack it in
Hardkore America. Why'd you leave by the way?"
[Puts mic down by Boltz] Boltz: Cause it was filled with sorry ass... ["The Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie Arabian facebuster's the back of his head with the chair remaining in the ring!! Audience cheers wildly, and
MacKenzie sits on the chair on Boltz's head for the remainder] Brian MacKenzie: "Here's the deal, my over little friend. That's all gonna change cause I am gonna elevate that title to a level of greatness unheard of in this spastic region. I don't care if I have to take the title to Japan or
Mexico like you were too scared to, or carry your overrated ass to the greatest matches ever seen in a GWNW ring, whatever, it shall be done. I don't like it when people piss in my yard, Beantown Boy, and your gonna find that out in the next few painful months."
[Brian gets up and stomps the chair on Boltz's head and leaves to a sea of cheers. But before Brian can get out of the ringside area "Godly" Ken Davison emerges from behind the curtians and brains MacKenzie with a chair shot right to the skull!!! Davison now leaves as the crowd starts to boo.]
LeDuct- What is the deal with Davison? Is he Boltz friend or foe? He lets
MacKenzie come out and lay the smack down on Boltz, but just when we think there will be no retaliation Davison comes out and levels the returning
Canadian. Young- I will tell you what we are going to find out on the next edition of
Stampede because I am booking a tag team match right now!! Boltz and Davison will take on MacKenzie and Randy "Rusty Cage" Cobb!!!
America- Wow what an event the next Stampede is shaping up to be. <Fade to a promo for the next Sunday Night Stampede.> -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling Sunday Night Stampede Live December 26th, 1999 From the GM Place, Vancouver British Columbia, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- [Main Event] Elimination Style Three Way Dance Mr. Young w/Prince Saff & Sycho Kid Vs. ReX Watson Vs. Cyrus "F'n Vyrus" Williams [Tag Match] Friends or Foes Boltz & "Godly" Ken Davison Vs. "Canadian Dragon" Brian MacKenzie & Randy "Rusty Cage" Cobb [Women's Title Match] Evening Gown Match Champ- "Sexy" Anj Turner Vs. Chall- "Flaming" Josephine Beauty
