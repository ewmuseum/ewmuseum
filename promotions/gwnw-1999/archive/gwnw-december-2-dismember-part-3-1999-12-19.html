December 2 Dismember (3/6)

19 December 1999

+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- +-+-+- HardKore Canada: Great White North Wrestling DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER Live December 19th, 1999 From the Corel Centre, Ottawa, Canada. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- [Video opens up to Saffire sitting in a comfy chair, reading a thick ass book about psychology.]
Saffire: Are you guys asleep yet? Are Boltz's interviews that horrible? You see, I would really like to address that so called Millenium tag team, but according to my new rules, I just can't. I can't even TALK about a team until they can back up their big mouth, and I doubt they can, because THEY SUCK!! I caught their promo a few days ago, and nearly died of boredom. I'm serious, I went to the hospital and was diagnosed with MTT's disease. MTT standing for
Milenium Tag Team.
[Saffire sits back and relaxes even more] Saffire: Should I be worried? Nah, Sycho and myself are veritable masters in the ring compared to these other jerks. Damn, I can't even remember their names. I mean it when I say that. In fact just talking about them is putting me to sleep. However, the winner of this match gets a tag title shot eventually, and that's something that matters to me. I'm tired of having
Canada ruled by Americans. Granted America can produce better and more entertaining television commercials, but they can't produce better professional wrestlers. I mean, look at me. I'm the god of every child that watches GWNW.
They all want to be Saffire and/or Sycho. We got the look, the style, the brains, and the ability to become the champs. We just need Cody to give the ball to us, and that's something that WILL happen. We're the new YPS, so if you screw with us, we probably won't "screw" you back. We'll just kick the crap out of you and leave the screwing to the Codemaster. He's the man for that reason. Now lemme read up, I might do a gimmick change here if I study enough psychology.
[Fade out] <We now return to a shot of the crowd before returning to a shot of the announcers.> Young- Here we go!! It's YPS time here tonight live on Pay-Per-View!!! LeDuct- Well this match is to determine the number one contenders for the currently vaccant GWNW Tag Team Titles.
America- But the Tag Team Titles will either go to Pure Evil or The Dubois
Brothers as they square off in a Falls Count Anywhere match right after this match.
LeDuct- Well Prince Sapphire is back from the set of his latest film "Quebec's
Revenge: Zombie Attack II" and is ready for action.
Young- What Saff directs movies?? Cool beans! America- Yeah just low budget horror films though. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- DECEMBER 2 DISMEMBER [Tag Team Match] Number One Contenders Match Millenium Tag Team w/"Trillionaire" Shawn Starks Vs. YPS- Sycho Kid & Sapphire Ref- Nick Harte Writer- [detail withheld] Time Limit- 25 minutes -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ -+-+-+- <We cut to a shot of Martian LaCroix standing in the ring.> LaCroix- Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is a tag team bout set for one fall with a twenty-five minute time-limit, and is to crown number one contenders for the GWNW TAG TEAM TITLES!!!! Introducing first...
<The arena lights dim as "Unforgiven 2" by Metallica begins to play. "Trillionaire" Shawn Starks now emerges from behind the curtian with fists full of 100 dollar bills and he is dressed in a fancy suit. The two men known only as the Millenium Duo flank Starks as they walk to the ring. One man is wearing cut off Levi's that go a little past his knees, and a shirt that says "Not Just
Cool, But Awesome" with mapple leafs in the backround. The other man is wearing long black spandex pants with green lightning bolts on each side of the pant legs, he wears a vest on his chest with lightning boltz all over it with the phrase "Catch a Flash if you Can" on the back in green letters. All three men enter the ring as the Duo folds its arms and stands behind Starks as he points to them. Starks now takes the ring mic from LaCroix.>
"Trillionaire" Shawn Starks- LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, how the HELL ARE YOU!?!?!? <|Fans cheer.|> Starks- Well it's time I introduce to you all the men behind the mayhem. The masters of disasters...THE MILLENIUM DUO!!!! The man on my left wearing the lightning bolt garb, this is..."SuperStar" SHANE FLASH & his partner in crime..."THE REAL DEAL" SHANE AWESOME!!!! They weigh in at a combined weight of
465 pounds, and both call the country of Canada home!!
<|Fans roar.|> Starks- Now Young's Personal Screwballs if you are smart you will listen up right now!!! <"Seven Years in Tibet" begins to play as out from behind the curtians emerges
Sycho Kid and Prince Sapphire. The Sycho Kid has a mic.>
Sycho Kid- Yeah what is your proposition because we are ready to give you guys a Y2K PROBLEM!!!
Shawn Starks- You guys see this? <Waves his fists full of money.> Well due to my defimation of character lawsuit I filed and won against your boss Mr. Young
I have plenty of this to spare. If you guys will take this money you won't have to get your butt kicked by the Trio tonight. There is more than enough here for both you guys to have a fun night out on the town!!
<Sycho and Sapphire look at eachother.> Young- DON'T DO IT!!! Sycho Kid- Sure it's a deal!! <Sapphire starts to argue with Sycho for not consulting with him first.> Shawn Starks- Then just come to the ring and its yours. We have reached a deal. <Sycho heads towards the ring while Sapphire just stands in the asileway.>
LeDuct- This match is allready over. Shawn Starks has bought off YPS and the number one contendership for the GWNW Tag Team Titles.
America- And we still have yet to see any of the Millenium Trio actually wrestle a match they where scheduled for!
Young- I can't believe Sycho is going for the money! <Sycho slides in the ring and Starks hands him the money. Sycho kid shoves the wads of bills into his tights as from behind Shane Awesome nails Sycho with a standing side kick!!>
LeDuct- They tricked them!! Young- I can't believe YPS is getting outsmarted!!! Get in there Saff!!! <Saff looks at his partner then shrugs and walks out of the arena. Awesome and
Flash now scoop Kid and give him a spiked piledriver onto the ring mat. Now all three members of the Trio lay the boots to the downed Kid. The Trio now exits out of the ring and through the capacity crowd. The crowd cheers there fellow
Canadians and try to pat them on the back as they leave the arena.>
Young- YPS is in my dog house now! We have some serious issues to discuss when this PPV is over!
LeDuct- Well I guess the Millenium Duo of Shane Flash & Shane Awesome will go on to face the winners of the next match.
America- Yes they will Carl. Pure Evil returns to action to take on the Dubois
Brothers to crown the new GWNW Tag Team Champions.
<Fade to commercial.>
