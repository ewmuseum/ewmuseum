[Welcome to the I-80 High School gymnasium in south-west Iowa. In the center of the gym, there is a brand-spanking new ring, with shiny red ropes, and a canvas mat with the words "Midwest Championship Wrestling" emblazoned in black across the white canvas. There are four rows of folding chairs surrounding the ring, about half filled, giving us an attendance of about 60 brave souls who were willing to part with five to eight dollars of their hard earned money to watch tonight's extravaganza.] [The clock strikes eight, and the speakers on the ceiling of the gym squeak into life. There's a mild buzz from the assembled fans, when...] #Gimme fuel, gimme fire, gimme that which I desire# [Through a curtained area that leads to what is usually the high school boys' basketball lockeroom emerges the owner of MCW, Jack Anderson, with his beautiful wife Felicity on his arm. They walk along the edge of the seats towards a table set up at ringside. They both grab a headset and turn down the Metallica.] JA: Welcome to MIDWEST CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! [A forty-odd person pop!] JA: I'm Jack Anderson, wearing this monkey-suit so we look official, and this is my wife Felicity. Say hi, Felicity! FA: If you expect me to say "Hi, Felicity," you're VERY mistaken. I told you, I've never acted stupid to attract attention and I'm not going to... [Light, bubbly, pop music interupts Felicity, and out through the entrance curtain comes a cute, if underdeveloped, girl. Felicity, knowing marketing, knows that the best possible way a fed could start would be with an incredibly hot chick opening things up.] [But Felicity is not Shona Maynard. If you're her, you think the best possible way a fed could start would be with you all over the place. So, here she comes. The boyishly slim 18 year old, looking like a younger version of her cousin Claire Maynard, walks out in a white T-shirt and short skirt.] SM: Hello, everyone! [She waits for the cheers from the crowd, which are strangely not forthcoming.] SM: I said, HELLO, everybody! [A couple of people shout back hi, hoping this will get her to the point.] SM: Okay, for those of you who don't know me from RAW Carolina and lots of other feds, I'm Shona Maynard, and I'm the youngest, cutest, and generally hottest member of wrestling's first female family - go me! [She smiles happily.] SM: And I can see lots and lots of hot young studs out there who I'd really like to get to know better! So, anyway, I've got this really cool way to get some sexy guys more involved in tonight's show. And you not only get to spend more time with me - backstage in the dressing room - but you get some free merchandise! So, go on, hands up if you're interested! Right up high! [An overwhelming lack of reaction.] SM: Don't be shy! [Two drunk guys, both about mid-20's, put their hands up.] SM: Yay! [She walks over to the two drunk guys, through the crowd.] SM: I'll come and get you when I need you. DG1: Coooooool, dude. [Turning her attention to a slightly younger man a few seats behind them, she walks over to him.] SM: Hey... you look like you wanna do a girl a favour, big boy. [He gulps.] SM: Guess that's a yes, then - go me! [She moves on past a couple more people, whispering something which the mic doesn't catch, and they grin.] SM: Two more signings there, all we need now is one lucky guy. [She strolls over to a somewhat older man, in his 40's, sat next to a girl in her early teens.] SM: Hey honey... this your daddy? Girl: Yes. SM: Don't you think your daddy should do me a big favour? Girl: Yes! SM: Whaddya say, daddy-o? [The man laughs.] Man: Believe me, I'd love to help out, but... SM: I'll make it worth your while... [She licks her lips, a gesture which isn't as seductive as she thinks it is. The man's eyebrows raise, and he blushes.] Man: Um... I'm flattered, but I'm a happily married man. [Shona giggles.] SM: Not like that, silly! Honey, do you like this T-shirt I'm wearing? [The question is directed to the girl, who nods quickly.] SM: It's a special T-shirt. Signed by my cousin, Claire Maynard. Look! [She turns around, presumably for the girl to see Claire's signature on the back of the shirt. The girl's eyes go wide.] SM: Honey, you can have this T-shirt if your daddy will do me this one little favour. Girl: Oh wow! Man: Well... SM: Guys - who thinks this guy should help me, so I can hand this T-shirt over to this sweet young lady? Crowd: Hell yeah! [Hey, she's not a stunner, but there's drunk guys who wanna see boobies!] Girl: Please help the lady, daddy! Man: Well... okay, then. Shona: Yay! Go me! [Shona grins, and starts to do a sexy striptease, pretending to take the T-shirt off. Eventually, she slips one arm out, then the other, and pulls it off... To reveal another white T-shirt underneath. The crowd boos loudly. Shona giggles.] Shona: Oops... did I put one too many on this morning? Silly Shona... But hey guys - this is the only one left, I promise. [Walking away, she flips the back of her T-shirt up to reveal only a bra underneath. As she reaches the entrance, she turns around, blows a kiss to her fans, and then walks backstage. Jack looks on, flummoxed.] JA: Well, that was interesting. FA: One has to give her credit for knowing the value of self promotion. But now, it's time for Midwest Championship Wrestling's first ever match! JA: Coming to the ring first, weighing in at a svelte 273 pounds, hailing from St. Louis, Missouri... Bud Bush! [The curtains part, and after a slight pause, the drunken portion of the audience cheers, as a shorter man with a beer belly comes out of the entrance way, wearing a too small t-shirt with "Magic B33r +1" printed on it and pushing a keg on a hand truck! And a voice over the speakers.] Mr. Walsh: No, no, no. This just will NOT do! [Boo!] [Walking out from the back is what looks to be your worst nightmare from your school days. A tall, well built, older man, rapping a ruler into his left palm.] Walsh: No, this won't do at all. What do you think you are doing? BB: DRINKING BEER! [Pop!] Walsh: And why do you think that's worth celebrating? BB: Because it's damn good beer! [POP!] Walsh: I remember you in my American Government class. And I'm play by play, dear wife. Walsh swings for the fences, and strike one, Bush rolls out of the way. Walsh stalking the fleeing Bush. Bush is at his keg... [Oooh!] JA: Bush sprays Walsh in the face with the beer! Walsh is temporarlily blinded. Bush on top of the keg, HE SPRINGS TO THE BOTTOM ROPE, THEN HITS A SUICIDE DIVE ON WALSH! Walsh charges, drop toe hold, and Bush is on him! FA: With a very badly executed camel clutch. JA: But look at his intensity! Walsh powers to his feet, with Bush hanging on his back now. Walsh falls backwards and flattens Bush! Referee Tiny with the count! Tiny: One! Tiny: Two! JA: Kickout! Walsh is right back on Bush, though, pulling him up, and whipping Bush into the ropes. Clothesline! Walsh with a kneedrop right on the forehead! He goes to cover... Tiny: One! Tiny: Two! JA: Kickout again! Walsh is furious, and goes to the turnbuckle, and climbs. Bush to his feet, sees what's going on, he catches Walsh on top! FA: Look at him beg, so unbecoming of a teacher. JA: Bush has him elevated over his head, Gorrila Press Slam! Bush with the cover... Tiny: One! Tiny: Two! Tiny: Three! [DING DING DING!] JA: Your winner, BUD BUSH! [Bud celebrates in the ring, and rolls out to his keg. He sprays the livid Mr. Walsh with beer, and then sprays some down the waiting open mouth of a fan on the way out. Walsh follows, slowly burning in anger. Jack Anderson leaves the announce table and heads into the ring.] JA: And that's the type of action that you'll see here in Midwest Championship Wrestling! If you enjoy our product, we have several tables set up on the periphery of the gymnasium, where we are selling MCW merchandise, and the wrestlers themselves are selling their own products. Also, Ms. Maynard has a table of surplus wrestling memorabilia, check them out when you head to the snack bar! But now, it's time for our next match, a grudge match a year in the making... #This is the end# #Beautiful Friend# #This is the end# #My only friend, the end# JA: From Tiajuana, Mexico, weighing in at 192 pounds, the highest flying luchadore of them all, the Lizard King! [Through the entrance curtain comes a masked wrestler, all in scaly green. Thankfully, and not just for time reasons, The Lizard King's music is turned off before the infamous monolouge.] JA: And from New York, New York, the Sharp Dressed Man! #Clean shirt, new shoes# #And I don't know where I am goin' to.# #Silk suit, black tie# #I don't need a reason why.# #They come runnin' just as fast as they can# #Cause every girl crazy 'bout a sharp dressed man.# [From the entrance comes a very well dressed man, wearing a well tailored tuxedo, perfectly coiffed blonde hair, and teeth that have to be bleached daily. He looks at the fans, and is obviously disgusted by their looks. The Sharp Dressed Man climbs into the ring, and grabs a microphone.] SDM: This is what it's come to? I'm here, in Screwyercousin Nowhere, fighting for about ten people? People who still think that faded jeans are cool? Who think that they can wear hats like THAT indoors? [The MAN points at a man in the front row wearing a Nebraska Cornhuskers hat. And draws instant cheap heel heat for it.] SDM: Look at you! I'm afraid to take off my jacket. Not because it might get stolen. No, no chance of that. I'm afraid that it might get some of what's stuck to the bottom of your... [He grimaces.] SDM: boots on it. I'm already wondering if I can get the smell of this building out of this thing! Thank goodness that I'm only facing this child of the sixties born thirty years too late. That means I can be on the next plane out of this hell-hole! [More Cheap Heat.] SDM: Right, just like all fashion victims, you refuse to believe the tru...OOF! [The Lizard King has heard enough! Leg-scissors from behind becomes a reverse-hurricanrana, and the match is on! DING DING DING!] JA: Lizard King with the early advantage, thanks to his sneak attack. Off the ropes, legdrop across the Sharp Dressed Man's chest. Cover, but kickout before one. Sharp Dressed Man has taken offense to that. FA: As would I. He hasn't had a chance to take his jacket off yet. JA: Poor baby. Maybe next time he'll come dressed to wrestle. FA: That's not his gimmick, dear. JA: Right, right. But is his gimmick taking lots of quick kicks and chops from his opponent? FA: No... JA: That's what it looks like, though. Lizard King is in full control here. King to the top rope, spinning whatchamacallit! FA: Dodged. JA: Yes, indeed, and Sharpie leaves a few patent leather footprints on Lizard King's back. FA: Sharpie? JA: You have a problem with that? Sharp Dressed Man goes behind, and puts the Lizard King in a chinlock. He's really working it, almost into a sleeper. [Boring! Boring!] FA: Our fans seem to have a strong dislike for Mr. Man. JA: As do I. I change the channel at 11:30 every morning, just so he stays off of my television. FA: What are you talking about? JA: Apparently not the match. FA: Apparently. JA: Mostly because the Sharp Dressed Man is continuing to work this headlock. Finally, FINALLY, Lizard King gets to the ropes, and the hold is broken. [Small buzz as Shona Maynard again comes towards the ring, and comes to ringside.] JA: What's she doing here? FA: I don't know for sure, but I'd bet it has something to do with spotlight stealing. JA: Right. A flurry of kicks by the Lizard King, and he takes the Sharp Dressed Man DOWN! Lizard King to the top turnbuckle... wait a minute, what's she doing? FA: She's trying to flash the Lizard King, but he's not interested. JA: Can you blame him? Lizard King is signalling for his finisher, and... [Boo!] JA: Shona Maynard just pushed Lizard King off of the turnbuckle, and Sharp Dressed Man is there, for a quick cover! Tiny: ONE! Tiny: TWO! Tiny: THREE! [DING! DING! DING!] JA: Lizard King is irate! He's storming off to the locker room, completely disgusted at this turn of events. FA: Wouldn't you be, too? Oh no, now she's in the ring. [Shona jumps up and down, excitedly.] SM: Sharp Dressed Man, I got you the win! Go me! [Sharp Dressed Man looks at her, wordlessly, putting his tuxedo jacket back on, and brushing away the lint picked up from the mat.] SM: So, anyway, I'm forming the new Sex Stable Stripped Down in MCW, because the Sex Stable like ROCKS. And guess what? I want YOU to be the newest member! And I got you a T-shirt! SDM: You want me to wear a T-shirt? SM: Yah! But, not just any T-shirt... I got it done specially for you. [She goes under the ring. The Sharp Dressed Man becomes worried.] SM: Dammit, I know I put it here... SDM: Look, kid... [She comes out of the ring, holding a black T-shirt, and her eyes are lit.] SM: It's Armani Couture! [The Man's jaw drops, and Shona smiles with satisfaction.] SM: Claire called in a favour. So, you in? SDM: I'm in! SM: Yay! Go me! Now, everyone else I talked to before, I need you backstage right now - come on, what are you waiting for? [Drawing the ire of the assembled sixty fans, Shona and the Sharp Dressed Man return to the locker room.] JA: That's JUST what we need here, a stable. FA: It's not that bad of an idea, Jack. And if it helps them help each other, more power to them. JA: I just hope things don't get out of hand. FA: Things get out of hand? When it involves a Maynard? Never! [A boy comes running out of the locker room, and whispers in Jack's ear.] JA: What? I can't believe this. FA: What is it? JA: There's been a backstage confrontation, and the wrestlers in our next match aren't able to make their scheduled match. FA: What? Why? [Jack whispers in Felicity's ear.] FA: Oh... my... We're sorry about that, folks, but to make up for the shortened card, you can present your ticket stub from tonights event at the door in two weeks, and you'll recieve a TWO DOLLAR discount on your seat, how about that? [The Cop turns to face her, then shrugs, grabs two of the men, and slips his hand across their butts. The crowd shouts almost in unison, except for an older man with a camera who is very studiously taking close ups of GLC II's actions here.] JA: And his opponent, Conan "Black Thunder" Kabashi!
