Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Midwest Championship Wrestling debuted 11 August 2004 in a high school gymnasium in southwest Iowa, drawing roughly sixty paying fans to watch owner Jack Anderson and his wife, Felicity, call the promotion's own first card from ringside.

The opening match crowned no champion but set the tone: beer-gimmick wrestler Bud Bush, wheeling a keg to ringside, upset a stern authority figure billed as Mr. Walsh with a Gorilla Press Slam after Walsh had spent much of the match choking and clubbing him.

A valet, Shona Maynard, worked the crowd for volunteers before turning her attention to the ring, costing “The Lizard King” his match against the Sharp Dressed Man with a shove off the top rope, then recruiting her new favorite into a stable she called the Sex Stable Stripped Down with the gift of a designer T-shirt.

The main event closed on Conan “Black Thunder” Kabashi beating a comically outmatched Glam Rock Cop II with a top-rope Death Valley Driver of his own naming, the Kabashi Driver, before the broadcast's own surprise: “The Wolf” Lark Fenriz appearing unannounced to tell the young roster that “dreams do not come true for all people” and that they weren't among the select few who'd realize theirs.

Whether Midwest Championship Wrestling ran its promised second show two weeks later isn't in its surviving record, which stops with this debut.
