[CCW] Canadian Championship Wrestling

To all, Canadian Championship Wrestling is a brand new e-wrestling fed that will hopefully hold its first card within the next month. I'm [name withheld]
the haed promoter/booker for CCW and I hope you will join me in this project. FYI, I promote shows in Quebec for an organization called NCW
(Northern Championship Wrestling). We're a small-time indy fed, but we're growing strong. You can check us out at ncw.qc.ca
. Here are more details on CCW: What is CCW ? ************* CCW is a national independant organization which will hold cards in small towns across Canada. There will be no PPVs as our budget does not permit it for now but supercards will be held once every 3 months where feuds can settled. Other cards will include week-end house shows as well as a nationally syndicated 1-hour TV program called "CCW Power
Hour" which is broadcast live every Tuesday night. Titles ******* There are 3 championships in CCW: - CCW Canadian Championship: The top honour in CCW, to hold this you have to carry the fed on your shoulders. - CCW Provincial Championship: The secondary singles title but stilla distinctive honour. - CCW Canadian Tag-Team Championship: The top honour in tag-team competition. Matches and roleplaying ************************ Booking will work almost as they do in real wrestling with a slight twist. I would ask each character to be roleplayed at least once a week if possible. Although you will not be suspended for not roleplaying, if a character isn't developed for the crowd, he may be forced to job a lot.
You will always be given 48-72 hours notice for booking. For example: I send an e-mail out on Saturday afternoon with the card for CCW Power Hour. Matches will be simmed using TNM 7 for content, but I will ALWAYS book the finish + any other angles.
Please note that just because your character starts a losing streak does not mean he isn't good or your RPs aren't good. It's probably an angle or a set-up for something. If at some point you have a problem with your booking, please e-mail me and we'll discuss it, but please don't send me one each time you lose. Talk to me like a civilized person and I will do the same. Also, if you have an idea for your or any other character, please send it to me, it's not easy to be original all the time.
Sign-Up ******** To sign-up, please e-mail me a TNM export file of your characters. Maximum 4 characters per person and if you have 4, you must have a tag-team. PLease give a name to the stable or if you want them to be independant of one another, that's fine too. However, they will never feud with one another. Along with these files, please send a small bio of the wrestler, it'll help me with the booking and it'll show me what we can do with the character. If you don't have TNM, just a send a complete profile including 20-30 moves he can perform.
Web Page ******** If anyone has the talent to make a web page, I'd appreciate, 'cause I don't know anything about HTML.
Please write to me if you have any questions, comments or to send the appllication. Thanks for your time, [name withheld] CCW Booker
