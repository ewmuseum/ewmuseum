A September 1998 Canadian Championship Wrestling founding pitch promising cards across small-town Canada, three titles, and a weekly 'CCW Power Hour' TV program, self-signed by an operator who also promotes a real-world Quebec independent -- no card of its own survives. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Canadian Championship Wrestling introduced itself on 4 September 1998 as “a brand new e-wrestling fed that will hopefully hold its first card within the next month,” under a head promoter and booker who says he also promotes real independent shows in Quebec for a federation called Northern Championship Wrestling.

It set out a national scope across small Canadian towns, with no pay-per-views for budget reasons but a supercard every three months, weekend house shows, and a weekly one-hour syndicated TV program, CCW Power Hour, airing live on Tuesdays, over three championships: the CCW Canadian Championship, the CCW Provincial Championship, and the CCW Canadian Tag Team Championship.

Its own booking approach asked for one roleplay a week per character, gave forty-eight to seventy-two hours' notice on every card, simmed matches with TNM 7 while its own promoter set finishes and angles directly, and took sign-ups by exported character file, capped at four characters per person with any fourth requiring a tag team.

No card, roster, or title of Canadian Championship Wrestling's own survives beyond this founding document; whether its promised first card, or CCW Power Hour itself, ever aired is unknown.
