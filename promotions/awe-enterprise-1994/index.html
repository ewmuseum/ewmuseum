Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The American Wrestling Enterprise announced itself on 12 December 1994 as three things at once: a newly-founded play-by-e-mail federation built on its founder's earlier World Wrestling Alliance venture; the Association for Wrestling by e-Mail, the name of the play-by-mail medium carrying its feuds; and Altered WWF for Effectiveness, its own house ruleset, a blend of the Coliseum and WWF game systems tuned for e-mail play. Its first electronic card was set for 20 January 1995, open to new players who wrote in before the 13th.

The same launch post, cross-posted the same day to a second newsgroup, closed with a postscript aimed squarely at the community it expected resistance from: “If you're one of the 'kill all fantasy wrestling posters' people, and you're still reading this — sorry. I need players... and I /did/ warn you.”

Nothing else of the promotion's own survives beyond that single announcement. The only later trace of its own ruleset is an unconnected community member's post, almost a year afterward, asking whether anyone still held a copy of the AWE rules on their own rather than blended into another fed's house system — evidence the ruleset had spread, not evidence the promotion that wrote it was still running. No card, roster or closure notice of the AWE's own was found beyond this.
