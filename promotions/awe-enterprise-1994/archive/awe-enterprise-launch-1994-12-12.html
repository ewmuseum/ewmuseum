AWE (1994) — News

"AWE: New Wrestling PbeM fed starting"

12 December 1994
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you're reading this, then your obvious question is, "What's AWE? I never heard of it."
AWE is three things:
1) the American Wrestling Enterprise, [name withheld]'s newly-founded wrestling federation building upon the prestige gained by his most recent venture, the World Wrestling Alliance;
2) the Association for Wrestling by e-Mail -- the new PbeM detailing the feuds and fights of the American Wrestling Enterprise;
3) Altered WWF for Effectiveness -- the rules system that the AWE is run under (a mish-mosh conglomerate of the Coliseum and WWF game systems, optimized for PbeM play).
With that out of the way, the announcement:
The AWE has finally made its way to the Internet. Our first electronic card will be run on January 20, 1995, and we're looking for new players. If you'd like to join the AWE, or would like more info, mail me at [detail withheld] before January 13, 1995.
Thanks for your time,
H. G.
He beats his fists against the posts
And still insists he sees the ghosts
