Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CWO’s one surviving document is a results recap for a Monday Madness card on 14 August 2000, posted by an operator who called it, in his own words, “a fed I run in my spare time” with “around 30 members.”

The card’s main event pitted Trunks, holding one half of CWO’s own tag titles, against a former tag champion, Zechs Raven — Trunks, the recap says, “totally dominated” despite giving away considerable size, and won a Falls-Count-Anywhere match with a finishing move called the Flame. Elsewhere on the card, Fredrick Schroeder and Rod Baxter won the tag titles outright over Xtreme and Pat King, a former CWO TV champion who by the recap’s own account had “never tasted any gold” in the tag ranks.

The rest of the card was a mix of debuts and squashes: Ames defeated a past CWO TV champion, Mel Smillie; The Man of Leisure outlasted a debuting Hellrazor; The Shadow, also debuting and billed at “nearly 400 pound,” destroyed an opponent named T. Money; Blade won a Barbed Wire Match over Soul Reaver; and Dick May defeated Sean Carter in what the recap called “a classic Jr. Heavyweight match,” earning himself a title shot the following week.

No founding statement, roster page, or further card survives beyond this one recap; how CWO began, whether Dick May’s title match or the promotion’s next Slamfest card ever ran, and how CWO ended, are not in the surviving record.
