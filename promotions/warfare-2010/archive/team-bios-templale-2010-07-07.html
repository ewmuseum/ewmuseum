Seth — 7 July 2010 Here's the bio if you want in. It's real easy to fill out. If your team has a bio elsewhere and it has this same information, you may use that as long as it has the information requested here. Again, as stated in WARFARE's Mission Statement, you can have teams consisting of anything from two to four people.
HANDLER INFO: Handler name(s): E-mail: AIM: (If ya got it.) WARFARE TEAM INFORMATION: TEAM NAME: Team Members: Nicknames: (for both your wrestlers and team nicknames, if any) Heights: Weights: Theme Music: Ring Entrance: (You don’t have to write out an actual entrance, but what do they do when the come to the ring. Cliffnotes are perfectly fine.)
Appearances: Alignment: Gimmick/Personality: (A broad outline of who your team is and what they represent) Wrestling Styles: (Both singles and as teams. What does your team do to ultimately get the job done.)
Movesets: (For both singles and teams) Finishers: Background:
