Final Word On IEWA

17 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all, congratulations to the following tag teams who made the 1998 poll: #167 The Misfits (Dalbello & Godiva Rage) #77 The Tokyo Express (Riki Kano & Masa Kokura) #50 The Siberian Express (Ivan Drago & Andrei Bolotov) #22 The Prophets Of Rage (Derek & Shadoe Rage) Now, some of you have suspected this for quite some time; it's now official. [name withheld] shut down the IEWA on February 8, 1999. He cites his busy schedule and the lack of match writers as reasons why he made this decision.
It was an entertaining fed for me personally, and I would say that even if my own wrestler hadn't achieved the tremendous success that she has, but it was a one-man show basically and he issued an ultimatum a week prior to this announcement; either he gets some help in running the fed, specifically match writing, or he would close it down. The choice was made on February 8th.
That's all I'm going to say about it here. I've made my speeches to the handlers directly involved in the IEWA. This notice is just for the benefit of the people here in RSPWF who haven't received the news.
And by the way...don't even think about recruiting Jessica, guys. I have her in at least one new fed, and she'll be making guest appearances over the next few months in other projects.
A wrap-up/tribute card is up in the air at this point, but pending the results of the Year-End Awards, this will probably be the last post regarding the IEWA.
[name withheld] handler of Jessica Starbird #1 RSPWF Women's 100
