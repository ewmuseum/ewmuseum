Women's TV Title Update, 21 October 1997

21 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real life sometimes gets in the way of handling and preparing e-wrestling, as many of you know. That is what apparently happened to IEWA fed head [name withheld]. As a result, the battle royal match to determine the first IEWA Women's TV title has been rescheduled for Monday October 27th's Monday Night Mania (the flagship show for this e-fed). Let me repeat that this is an open invitational battle royal. Any female wrestler from any e-fed can participate, provided they receive permission from their respective presidents. So far, SJPW, GDWA, and the Modern-Day Wrestling Alliance have announced that they're sending in some of their lady wrestlers to participate. The deadline for entering your wrestler, as well as sending in flashes and strats, is midnight Friday (Central time). However, [name withheld] tells me that he really would really appreciate it if you send in the pertinent information as soon as possible before the Friday deadline. The e-mail addresses to send in everything is [detail withheld] . Some of the guys who have been helping me out in my budding e-wrestling career want to follow my wrestler, Jessica Starbird's matches. As I indicated earlier, [name withheld] doesn't have access to this newsgroup, but he gave me permission to forward some of the IEWA cards out to those who are interested in seeing them. So if any of you guys here are wondering what the heck I've been talking about here, if you want to follow Jessica's career, or if you want to join the IEWA to see how [name withheld] runs the e-fed, I have the first couple of Monday Night Mania's and the recent International Stampede PPV on file that I can forward to you. There are some slots open for male and female singles wrestlers, by the way. For more information on the IEWA, the International Electronic Wrestling Association, there is a homepage you guys can check out: http://members.tripod.com/~Caliphear/index.html Gee, after this message, I should appoint myself official "shill" person for the IEWA. Whatever. Personally, I'm looking forward to Jessica competing against some of the better known personalities in e-wrestling. From the list of participants so far, it looks like I'll get my wish...which may be bad news for Jessica. I'll see you in the ring. [name withheld] handler of IEWA's Jessica Starbird
