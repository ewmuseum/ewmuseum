Jessica Starbird vs. Napualokelani (Result)

19 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it's over a week late for this guys (blame college guy [name withheld]), but Jessica's match finally happened. JESSICA WON!... by disqualification. Jessica was attacked before the match by Jun Shiratori, but held her own in the match anyway. Napualokelani wound up throwing salt in her face to keep the belt. Who should come in the ring to save her hide from a further beating but GDWA World champion Officer May Order. I'll give credit here to [name withheld] for encouraging me to go ahead and take this match. I figured that this would be the result, and I'm happy with it. Now, Jessica's name is n spotlight, a lot of people will be stepping up wanting to challenge Jessica (3 so far), and the sky's the limit as to where I go from here. By the way, if any of you Syndicate guys need massage therapy, I'm sure that Jessica and her boss, Dr. Hargreaves, will be able to accomodate you needs. :-) Anyway, onward and upward. Shattered Dreams PPV is next on the horizon, as Jessica takes part in a 10 woman team elimination match. Jun Shiratori is a part of this match. Hmmm... [name withheld] handler of Jessica Starbird (IEWA)
