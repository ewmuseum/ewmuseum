[GWE] Here we GO!!!!!!!

4 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
G W E Global Wrestling Experience For years, the world of e-wrestling has been dominated by the same forces. The forces that still are among the greatest athletes in the entire world. These wrestlers rule the Japan federations, the American federations and all other European federations. NOT THIS ONE!!!!!! In GWE you control your wrestlers very fate. No longer will the same forces hold e-wrestling's coveted belts over and over again. It's now time for this new e-wrestling revolution to get started. Folks in this federation you will truly understand the.... Global Wrestling Experience 1. Why is Global Wrestling Experience here? 2. Rules and Regulations. 3. Titles, cards and PPVs!!! 4. Create Your Very Own GWE Superstar!!! Up until now, you may have asked yourself, why can't I ever get my hands on any gold in federations? I roleplay! I send in strats and all of that other junk too! I'll tell you why you haven't gotten any gold. You weren't here in the golden era of e-wrestling. Those wrestlers who were here years ago are still winning because they have a name for themselves. In GWE it doesn't matter what you've done in the past. ANYONE can win a title here, whether you are a newcomer or veteran. If you got the skills, then you will get the gold. _THAT_ is why we are here. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Now for the rules. For the most part if you want to stay in GWE you must roleplay. Roleplaying will get you the win. I don't like to kick people out of my federation but if you don't roleplay it will happen. AND... I don't just kick you out, I make you suffer. I make a fool out of your superstar. Heh heh heh! Also, sending starts is important. A start is a strategy for a match. Write me an e-mail telling me what you want to do in your match. If it is logical, it wil happen! Cursing in roleplays is illegal. If you feel like you must curse to get your point across please censor it, for example... A** or <BEEP> or $h*t. Global Wrestling Experience doesn't really have many rules. So please follow them and I am SURE that everything will run smoothly. Remember.... ROLEPLAY!!!! If you aren't serious about being a true member to this federation than please don't join. We don't want slackers! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Titles GWE World Champion..... The most coveted title in the federation. All weights! GWE Pacific Champion..... The 2nd best belt in the federation. All weights! GWE Atlantic Champion...... The next best thing to the Pacific. All weights! GWE Middle Crown Champion..... The big prize for our little fighters. 225 or less. GWE Tag Team Champions..... The Tag Gold. Sorry, only one for the tag teams!!! Cards Weekly..... GWE Crazy Crunch Weekend..... Every Saturday! Bi-weekly Wednesday Marathon..... Every other Wednesday! Monthly Main Event..... The 3 Monday of Every Month! PPVs Every two months instead of Main Event will be one action packed PPV!!!! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ GWE Creation Sheet This is where you create your very own superstar. Have fun, be creative and NO REAL WRESTLERS!!!!! Personal Info..... Your name: E-mail Address: Anything else: Wrestler Info..... Ladies and gentlemen thank you for considering joining GWE I garuntee you will NOT be dissappointed!!!! The Prez
