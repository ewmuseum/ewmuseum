A free simulation interfed on Expage in 2001 that gathered the member feds TCW, FSW and CWA under shared world, TV, hardcore and tag team titles and a monthly interfed pay-per-view, and whose own top story of 10 August 2001 saw its end coming. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Championship International Wrestling Alliance, CIWA on its own pages, billed itself as free, unlike Shootclub. Its FAQ called it a sim interfed: a player joined one of its feds, and the more players joined, the more staff there were and the more feds opened. Each fed was to run three house shows a week and one regular card, the house shows reporting highlights only, and a pay-per-view at the end of each month, with one huge interfed pay-per-view of all its feds at which the alliance's titles were on the line. Each fed had a world, TV, hardcore and tag team title; the top wrestlers of each fed were up for them, defended them at every house show in one-on-one matches, and met in stipulation matches at the big cards and pay-per-views. Promos on the board, the FAQ said, affected about 25% of the result of a match.

Its home page named three member feds. TCW was Twin Cities Wrestling: its news of 31 July 2001 gave a roleplay deadline of 3pm central time, results by 7pm and a huge announcement about the CIWA for the next day, its roster of August 2001 ranked 23 wrestlers with their alignments, finishers and records, and its Meltdown results ran a tournament whose semifinals, Da Boom against Freake and Romeo Valentino against Street Thug, were set for the pay-per-view. FSW was Florida State Wrestling: its home page of 1 August 2001 carried no news, and its roster page lists one wrestler. CWA was the California Wrestling Alliance: its update of 6 August 2001 reported 12 members and promised Saturday Rage as its best show ever, and its roster ranked 14 wrestlers, from Green to Fat Man. The alliance's pay-per-view card Can Anyone Survive? listed ten matches around a TV title tournament, a three-way Titantron match between the three hardcore champions and a three-way TLC match between the three tag team champions, with the date to be announced.

The home page's top story, dated 10 August 2001, said it looked as if MXW, TCW and CWA were going to join together, which would be the end of the CIWA; all four titles stood vacant. The CWA's page, last updated 28 August 2001, thanked its members and said the fed was joining Danger Zone Wrestling, where its commissioner would continue, and listed the wrestlers who would go, asking each to post a profile on the out-of-character board. No page of the alliance carries a later date.
