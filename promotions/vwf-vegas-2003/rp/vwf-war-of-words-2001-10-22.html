VWF War of Words, 22 October 2001

22 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First off thanks for reading. The VWF has been around now for almost 3 mmonths and is gearing up for our second PPV on Halloween.
I am looking for some new blood to debut on the PPV and who can move up the ladder in the VWF. Email me if you are interested.
Thanks -rich WAR of WORDS IN THIS ISSUE RIC BEAUTY (The BWF Pres has words for Powell) G- GANGSTA (Challenges the 24 Hour Champ!) DEMONIC and BLAZE (The Tag Team Champs) COREY FULLER (Puts The Price On Sebastian!) ZELL STRIFE (The T3 Champ! Is he independent?) JACK SPADE (THE Champ! Wants the tag team titles!) THE HUXTABLES (On evil) *************** RIC BEAUTY *************** (Ric Beauty is sitting in his spacious BWF office) RIC BEAUTY: Phear Phantastic! On Halloween! I cannot wait. I am excited. Because I am the king of the VWF. Corey Fuller is the BWF champ and
Zell Strife is the T3 Champ. I know Zell thinks he is independent but we both know he isn't. Anyway.
Everyone is chasing everyone so Rob and I have come to a decision on the Main Event at the PPV and it is going to be BIG! Yes Rob and I.
Lets just say it will be all the Rage in the Cage! But on to other matters. I am lonely. Where is Kerry Powell? My arch enemy? Gone? Chased from Vegas by Strife. It looks that way.
Kerry, I have an offer..... see this (holds up a contract)? I will leave this at Dan Allers, I mean Dan The Man Allers desk for Warrior
Wednesday. Do you know what it says?
It says if you show up at Phear Phantastic PPV, and get in the ring with me. Win or lose....WIN OR LOSE, just show up and you will gain full control of the BWF until the year 2002.
Did you hear me Kerry? I am so sure Zell Strife has put you out I will give up the BWF until the end of the year if you just show up! Show up if you dare, make it to the ring. You have to make it to the ring.
Are you man enough Kerry Powell? If I thought you were would I do this? (laughs) *************** G-GANGSTA *************** [Gangstaz Paradise hits, G-Gangsta walks out with U-Trop in Karl Kani jerseys and jeans. G-Gangsta grabs the microphone before taunting the crowd to a huge pop. He raises his arms as the fans stand up screaming and cheering.]
G-Gangsta: For about two and a half to maybe three months I have kept these fans on the edge of their seats with great moves never heard of before. An original style never seen before, never attempted before. But now that no longer matters because I have no longer the Triple Threat
Championship...I have been offered a BWF Championship shot...I will fight for the belt however that belt has no meaning to me, So Fuller, Extreme the G-Force will still win it! Therefore even if I win...which I will...I will go to the
Pay-Per-View in hopes of fighting for the 24 Hour Championship or MY T3 Championship. And this idiot "Legend Of Extreme" has challenged me for the PPV. Extreme did you not learn from the G-FORCE the first time? I put you out for a month you dumb sh!t! And now you want a match? NO! Not at the PPV...I have bigger things to attend to...like the Championships of VWF! So we are in a
Triple
Threat Match this Wednesday...so that is your chance to fight me sh!thead! You don't even deserve to step in the ring with a talent like myself do you? But you will none of the less. And if you are still not satisfied
X...next Wednesday night, your stipulations, your kinda match anything YOU want! Because I can beat you either way...! This Warrior Wednesday I win the BWF Championship! But at the PPV...mister Jack Spade! Me and you...Main Event!?!? Hell it could be a cage match...or a ladder match...or a hardcore match...or a plain old one on one matchup! For the 24 Hour Championship AND the BWF Championship which I WILL HAVE
WON BY THEN! C'mon Jack Spade...be an "ace" will ya? [Gangstaz Paradise hits as G-Gangsta and U-Trop walk out as the fans cheer.] ************************** DEMONIC AND BLAZE ************************** [The RCW theme hits as Demonic, Blaze and the Real Deal walk out in
"RCW...REVOLUTIONARY!" t-shirts and there wrestling gear underneath...Blaze and Demonic have the Tag-Team Championships around there waists.
Blaze grabs a mic as Real Deal and Demonic taunt and mimmick the crowd to loud boos.] Blaze: This Warrior Wednesday we have our first ever Tag Team Championship defense...against no other then two former RCW members in Dage "The
Reject In A Cage" and Jack" The Stab In The Back"Spade. Well I will have no problem delivering the Uncontrlable Inferno to Dage. But there is two things
I have
never told about the Uncontrolable Inferno! Not only is it
UNCONTROLABLE! But it's also UNPREDICTABLE AND UNSTOPPABLE! So Rage In A Cage Dage you will be put out for the LAST TIME!
[The fans boo loudly as Blaze passes the mic to Demonic.] Demonic: HAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAAAA! Good rhyming skills Blaze! Anyways...Stab In The Back a.k.a Jack...the Pits Of Hell will be extra hot and painful just for you! To ensure me and Blaze keep these!
[G-Gangsta and U-Trop run out with chairs as RCW retreats to the back. But what will Real Deal have to say on Warrior Wednesday?]
******************* COREY FULLER ******************* [The scene: The ever going glaze of the bright sun is shining onto a brick building that is standing high in the air. The building seems rather old and to any on looker it looks like just any other building, however to the people of Worcester it's much more. To them it's a symbol of beauty, it's town hall.
The camera then focuses on the base of the building or shall we say the entranceway where Corey Fuller is standing. The massive Corey fuller has a gigantic smile on his face.]
CF: I spy with my little EYE a RAT!!! [Corey Fuller winks at the camera.] CF: Yes a big fat RAT, and his name is SEBASTIAN! Or shall we say the trader named SEBASTIAN.
Oh yes Sebastian you are the biggest loser of them all. You had the chance of the life time and YOU BLEW IT. Ric and I would have brought you stardom, we would have brought you glory, we would have made a name out of Sebastian. BUT NO! You stabbed me in th back. You stabbed the BWF in the back. You and you alone screwed up.
(Corey begins to laugh.) CF: And that is why you are now a marked man. (After Corey says this he moves over to the right and a wanted picture of Sebastian is on the wall. Below the picture it says, "
WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE. Cash Reward of $5,000.] CF: As I said, MARKED MAN. And to everyone watching, if you have any information on the whereabouts of Sebastian don't hesitate to call at.
[detail withheld]. I'M OUT! The camera FTB **************** ZELL STRIFE **************** (Shallow, fast steps pace throughout the building.. a women, running and screaming, trying to find her way....She runs down the hallway only to find herself screaming in horror....A man grabs her and takes her into a room..... She tries to free herself but she cannot escape his grasp....He lets go of her gently.....as she looks into his eyes, she sighs of relief, for it is Zell.)
Zell) I guess you came to interview me......? Woman) Yes, but you scared the life out of me.... Zell) That was my intentions, but anyways...let's begin. Woman) Well my name is Emily....and I wanted to ask you about the battle royal and Sebastian....
Zell) By all means deary.... Emily) What happened in the ring when you were fighting? Why did you attack the bwf wrestlers?
Zell) Like I said in some sort of way before..... If I'm going to rule the federations, then how can I do it by helping others? Honestly now, it was a battle royal anyways.....every man for himself.....Which means, even fellow side members of the bwf will get in my way, and they had to be dealt with....As for now, forgiveness is a far off move for me.
Emily) And, now your upcoming matchup? Zell) Talk about sweet revenge on that stupid moronic plague Sebastian.....I can't wait to explode my rage on him. That idiot got in my way so many times, it wasn't even funny.....He ran into me, I could have eliminated everyone.... but NO! He decided he wanted to help and had my thrown over,
I was one step closer to victory.....And yet, the ever so crumbling,
Jack Spade, still holds that title.....He will face the fear sooner or later, i garuantee it.....
Emily) But whata bout the Real Deal? Zell) Whata bout him? he's useless.....Does he think that he could be an example of a champion.....he's a bottom feeder, always trying to lick the shoes of the higher competition...no bother, he can lick my boots after a good dosage of Porphyria.....that's if he gets up from it.......
Emily) Any last words? Zell) Dominance....is not far away......I will ensure that Death To
All,
will occur, I promise you that.....no leave, before I really start to frighten you......this time I will scare the life out of you.....
(The girl flees the area.....looking back, she dared not to.) **************** JACK SPADE **************** *The scene fades back into a gym somewhere just outside of Las Vegas. Inside the gym looks quite nice, modern, up to date with all the latest equipment. New high-tech machinery hardly touched or dampened with the sweat of the gyms many visitors. The musky smell of all gyms is not present, the place has a refreshing feel too it. Green plants dotted around the gym in a very random order give the otherwise plain and very white room a dash of colour and create a more friendly environment. A sound from a unseen area can be heard, that of a man working out perhaps. The camera turns and sees Jack
Spade, sat on a weight machine doing reps. He finishes his weights and steps down and pats his forehead with a towel. He smiles at the camera and begins to talk.*
JS: Well you'd never have thought it would you? Me and Dage, now together, the Elements of Violence. Y'know we've had ties go back for over 2 years now, back in the first run of that SH*T HOLE of a place, RCW, a friend of mine and Dage were a tag team. Y'see me and Dage had this planned all along.
The grand entry. The speculation over "which side is Dage gonna choose!?". And the trash talking between me and him. And then...the destruction of that idiot Corey Fuller. He never knew what had hit him. From the moment me and
Dage had shook hands Fuller was in a world of hurt and I was keeping my world title. Dage will soon be making a name for himself too. I'm sure Zell
Strife won't be safe with that Triple Threat Title. But for now, we have other matters... *Jack opens a bottle of water, takes a sip and then looks back into the camera.* JS: The VWF tag team titles. Against two people who joined the federation me and Dage made great - RCW. Demonic, Blaze, you two fools went in there when my friends Insane and Dage were taking that federation to the top of the world. They were making it go from being a scum of the Earth high school dream into the new hardcore fad that was going around. You went in there, with the Real Deal and began to think you were something special trying to take our lime-light. Now you come into the VWF with your little
"attack" and
expect to get somewhere. Heh, not here. With the VWF having me and
Dage on
their side, nobody has the power to stop us. I'm garunteeing that you fools are going to stand a chance on Wednesday against us.
*Jack sits down on a bench, puts some water over his head making his short black hair even wetter. He takes another drink and continues.* JS: Whether it be the BWF or RCW they're not safe by invading our turf. Me and Dage are here to fend off anyone. Not only for the name of the
VWF but
for the name of the Elements of Violence. Now we've came together, the opposition will be running scared. I mean, the opposition that is
Blaze,
Demonic and Real Deal for RCW, and Corey Fuller and that moaning little b*tch Sebastian. Heh, what kind of an opposition is that? The
Elements of Violence could take them out without any help, but since we have the likes of G-Gangsta, Huey and Strife to back us up, we might as well fend them off together, so when it's all done, the Elements of Violence can stand tall and kick the ass of anyone who dares to challenge them. Blaze, Demonic, on
Wednesday, those tag titles are going home with US...the Elements of
Violence. *Jack finishes his water and throws the empty bottle into the bin. He then gets back onto the weight machine and continues with his work out.* ********************* THE HUXTABLES ********************* (Henry and Huey are lifting weights. Well Henry is and the seven foot idiot is eating a snack cake, orange cupcakes)
HENRY: Come on Huey! HUEY: Why? HENRY: We have a big tag team match on Wednesday. Tai and Blackbird! HUEY: I am ready. HENRY: What do you mean you are ready? You have been eating all day. HUEY: I can beat a girl. HENRY: What? HUEY: I am fighting the girl and you are fighting the scary guy right? HENRY: It is a tag team match. HUEY: What do it mean? HENRY: We tag each other, you could be in the ring with either one of them. HUEY: (looks scared) HENRY: BOY! HUEY: Will you fight the scary guy? HENRY: (deep sigh) Yes. HUEY: Thanks pop! (ftb)
