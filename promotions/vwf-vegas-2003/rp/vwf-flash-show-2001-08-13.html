VWF Flash Show, 13 August 2001

13 August 2001

VWF Flash Show 08-13-01 IN THIS ISSUE Sebastian Paddy O'Shea "The Silence" Bobby Silas One Just for Kerry Powell ---------- SEBASTIAN (Fade in on the 24 hour title belt, sitting on Sebastians mantle in his mansion. Sebastian walks in front of the camera and smiles, then steps to the side of the belt)
SEBASTIAN: Sebastian is happy! Sebastian is the champion and there is only one reason why. Sebastian is the best wrestler in the VWF! Not Zell Strife, not Dirk Western and not Kerry Powell. It is.......(big smile) Sebastian. Now, what happens now that Sebastian is the ruler of the VWF. Who will get the shot? Who will step up and try to take the title from
Sebastian? Tai Angela....Zell Strife? The freaks! Not a chance. Dirk Western......he needs to go back on the road. And Kerry Powell is a cheap copy of Sebastian. There is no one worthy to get in the ring with Sebastian but Sebastian will get in the ring with any of them to show....continue to show who it the man.
(takes the belt off the mantle and slowly puts it around his waist) I am Sebastian.....I am the champion..........and you are NOT! ---------- PADDY O'SHEA (Barroom Heroes plays over a blank screen) # Face down in the gutter wont admit defeat# #Though his clothes are soiled and black# #Hes a big strong man with a childs mind# #dont you take his booze away# (slow fade in to Paddy O'Shea sitting at his favorite bar O'Malleys) PADDY: (slurring) Thats is right! I am a strong big guy and it is damn well sure time to....umm..show it! Yeah thats it show it.
Anarch.....key....X! You X guy! I will sow you how things are done here in Ireland. Never count a good man down. Or is it out? (leans in toward the bartender) Is it out or down? BARTENDER: Your career? PADDY: Funny guy. You are three of the funniest guys in this place. (just then a man walks over and stands behind O'Shea, he places his hand on the drunk wrestlers shoulder)
MAN: It is time to change your life Paddy? PADDY: (turns around) Are you my wife? MAN: I am the man who will help you to the top of the VWF! PADDY: Are we gonna cheat? I like to cheat. (The man and Paddy walk out of the bar) ---------- "THE SILENCE" BOBBY SILAS (We see the large frame of Bobby Silas sitting on the steps in front of a humble house in Jackson, MS)
TSBS: Las Vegas sure is a long way from where I am. And the life of a guy like Kerry Powell is a long way as well.
I didn't come from no good breeding, my daddy comes and goes and my mama has had her problems.
(he stands up) But I am gonna change things for me and my family and I am gonna do it in the wrestling business. The place to start is in the city of lights and the man it all starts against is Kerry Powell, mr 24 karat. The only time anyone ‘round here sees 24 karats is harvest time.
I was the Mississippi wrestling champion and I have the skills to win and I also know how to fight.
Kerry Powell it is almost quiet time. You know what that is? That is when I take these big hand, put ‘em ‘round your neck and lift you up, then slam you into the mat.
And after the count, for you it is Quiet Time Boy! ---------- JUST FOR KERRY POWELL (O Fortuna plays over a dark screen, a familair voice begins to speak as footage of Kerry Powell flashes on the screen)
VOICE: Do think you will have sucess in the VWF? In my hometown? Do you think you will win a match, much less get to the top?
Kerry Powell it has been a long time, and what a better place than in a fed run by Rob Beauty for me to take care of our business once and for all.
Come out of the woodwork! And train like you've never trained before, train as if you are going to be in the ring....
