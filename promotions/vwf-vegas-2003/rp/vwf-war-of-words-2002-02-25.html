VWF War of Words, 25 February 2002

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WAR OF WORDS Feb 25, 2002 THIS WEEKS THURSDAY THUNDER **MAIN EVENT** G-GANGSTA teams with CLASSY JOEY BOY to meet up with SEBASTIAN and THE DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA ZELL STRIFE returns to the rings of the VWF against a man who has wanted him for a long time ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE had the T3 Title last week but got DQed, he meets up with TONY CAGES best friend INSOMNIAC A single man holds the tag team titles and one of the these teams will meet Maq for the titles next week as former champs TRIPLE X get in the ring with 28TH STREET and the NEW YORK CITY HEADLINERS Two youngsters who both need wins go at it as BAVARIN takes on ELIMINATOR JASON STONE And THE REAL DEAL makes his debut against BLADE CANTOR (On cards, strats and complaints can be sent to me at [detail withheld] ) ------------------------- THE REAL DEAL (The scene opens up in black. Red Letters appear on-screen.) *Houston... *We Have A problem *There Seems to Be Two... *Two Real Deals... *How can this be??? (The seen re-opens in The Real Real Deal's private gym in San Francisco, California. He is bench pressing what looks to be a heavy bar bell. He's doing so slowly. He's wearing a pair of gym shorts and tennis shoes. He has no shirt. He slowly benches the bar a couple more times before placing it on the rest. He lies still for a second before sitting up and looking into the camera.) The Real Deal: Hello, wrestling fans. I have a couple things to say today, and I hope you'll give me your full attention. (He grabs a towl and whipes the sweat from his face before he continues.) The Real Deal: Ok, first things first. What's with this other dude, Real Deal? Some 7' 4" goofball who can't even manage to help his partner retain the tag title against some mediocre team I've never even heard of. All I have to say about that big dope is that he better get the hell out of vegas, or I will find him and beat his ass down for soiling my name. (He pauses to take a breath.) The Real Deal: Now, as you might imagine, I took the oppertunity to take a look at some of VWF's short list of talent during this last card, there weren't too many talented athletes, but I couldn't help but find myself annoyed to death by that Randall Crane charactor. Talk about narcissistic! This guy makes Chris Jericho look like a modest, down to earth, humble young man. I just wanted to give the revolutionary one fair warning: I really don't think we're gonna get along to well. There's nothing I hate more than a loud mouth punk who stands in front of a 6 and a half foot mirror every morning. So, try to stay out of my way big guy, or you will find yourself in a coma in the very near future. (He stands up from the weight bench.) The Real Deal: In closing, I'd like to say that I'm having second thoughts about this VWF fed. There seems to be little if any real talent in the locker room with a couple minor exceptions, which include a couple people I recognize from past endeavors. I think you'll find me on top of the federation real soon, so I'd like to kick things off with an open challenge. Anybody truly stupid enough to have there lungs ripped out there ass, all you gotta do is say "Hey, I'm really stupid. I'd love to have you kick my ass. My mom dresses me funny... Oops, I forgot to breath again," and here I'll be, ready to hurt you in such a way that your pants will be full of your own urine. (He sits back down on the weight bench.) The Real Deal: So put that in your pants and play with it. (He lies down and begins to bench press again. The scene slowly fades to black. Red words appear on-screen.) *Who Can Stop... *THE REAL DEAL?! --------------------- THE MAQUILLADORA aq is back stage with Yakabi celbrating his victory and reminicing about the match, 15 minutes prior. They are joined by his "tag-team partner". Yak- Nice victory tonight. Maq- I know, my tag partner saved my ass. Yak- (frown)Ok... Maq- What? Yak- Oh come off of it. Maq- Huh? Yak- You don't have a tag partner. Maq- What the hell are you talking about?! Yak- What the hell are YOU talking about?! Maq- You know we don't need this. Yak- We, what the f- we? Maq- My tag partner and I. Yak- You have an imaginary tag partner...you need help! Maq- Imaginary...how dare you call him that. Yak- Does he, she, it even have a name. Maq- He doesn't like people to label him. Yak- Ah, f- it! (Yak storms out of the room) Maq- Don't look at me like that. I defended you. (talking to his shadow) Maq- What! (thin air is silent) Maq- Don't take that tone with me! (still talking to shadow) Maq- No I don't think we have a match next week. (silence still) Maq- No, I don't know how to make Puppy Chow...or cookies. (Maq hits himself in the face) Maq- Ow! What did you do that for. (Maq starts wrestling himself...he tries to kick his shadow and hits the wall) Maq- Son of a ! (Maq then applies the mandible claw to himelf. He pulls his hand out of his mouth and chokeslams himself through a table. Officials rush in to break up the...fight?. The screen fades as Yakabi returns to the room.) ---------------- CLASSY JOEY BOY (Classy Joey Boy paces the lobby of the hotel resort and casino he is staying at. Many people walk through the lobby minding there own business. Some young fans yell sympathetic words at CJB for the unethical loss he suffered last Thursday. He seems upset and is talking to himself under his breath. Fatty Jay walks up to him, he his wearing black pants that sag blow his butt. He also wears a black FUBU jersey, a black hat that his backwards, a batters glove, and sunglasses.) CJB: Fatty? What are you doing? Fatty: Yo homeboy it's like this, your teamin' wit the G's of all Gansta's Thursday on the Thunda, so I decided to freshin your ass up on the what it's like to hang wit someone from the streets bro! I even got some hizelp from your Grand of all your momma's, boyeeeee! (The next thing you know Grandma Joey Boy walks over and joins the two. She is dressed similar to Fatty Jay. A crowd is gathering far behind the group.) Grandma: Yo yo it's me Grandma JB, and I'm layin down the law in this hizouse so you can prepare to move like a mizouse on the Thunda wit me homedawg G-Gangsta, yea....sasquatch! (CJB has no clue as to what to say or do. He is shocked that Fatty and Grandma would do something like this.) CJB: Ok, Fatty, Grandma, please explain to me in English what you just said. Grandma: Wha we wuz sayin' was diz right her, we is goin to da big show and gettin some kicks so we can kick it on Thunda! CJB: Ummm......I'll just leave you guys alone. Now, Anglea, Sebastian, you two sasquatchs think the "Team Captain" is going to just let this screw job go unpunished? I think not my ugly commrades. Thursday on Thunder I team with G-Gangsta. I have no idea why, but I don't care because it means I can get my hands on you two pieces of trash. Fatty: Yea, check it Sebastian and Angela, my boy CJB is going hit ya'z wit da Classysault, the Classyconrana, and den da Stizud Splizash, no dat shnizle ain't german it's Fizat tizalk! So check your shoes at the dizoor and prepare for what the team captain is puttin' dizown! CJB: Ummm......yeah what he said, I think. (The scene fades to Classy being very confused while Fatty and Grandma Joey Boy lean on each others backs and with there arms crossed) ------------------ REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a shot of a high-rise apartment complex, located just off the Las Vegas Strip. We cut to the inside of the apartment, where we see the living room. Leather furniture and a big-screen TV decorate the room, as does a surround sound entertainment system. From the kitchen walks "The Revolution" Randall Crane, dressed casually in blue jeans and an Atlanta Thrashers NHL jersey. He walks to the living room where he makes himself comfortable in a leather recliner. With a calm and almost eerie smile on his face, he speaks.] RC: Well... I suppose I can be branded as a "liar." For seven days now, I have promised and guaranteed that I would walk out of the Thomas & Mack Center the new VWF T3 Champion. One would imagine that I'd be upset that I didn't win, and perhaps be ashamed to show my face again. One would think indeed... [The calm face turns to a psychotic smirk.] RC: But I'm _not_. When I locked Tony Cage in the Revolution's End, I knew I had the title won. As I stretched the neck and back of Cage and heard him scream like a little bitch, I knew it was a matter of time. Then I blacked out. I was filled with a rage unlike any I've ever felt before. I thought of Cage's smart remarks of how I didn't have a chance to win the title from him. A remark of how I wouldn't beat him twice in two weeks. From there... I wanted Cage to feel my pain. I didn't let go because at that point, the VWF T3 Title was no longer an issue. Cage... you _truly_ received the full extent of the revolution. [Crane relaxes and leans back in the recliner.] RC: Now I get the privilege of facing your moronic sidekick, Insomniac. [Crane rolls his eyes.] RC: I'm now starting to wonder the hoops I'm going to have to jump through before I get another shot at you, Cage. But that's okay, bring them all on. Whatever it takes to beat you once and for all, it'll all be worth it. Hell, let me run the damn gauntlet. Insomniac... I really feel bad for you. Your direct relationship with Tony Cage means the transfer of aggression. You don't impress me whatsoever, sunshine. I don't care how big you look, everyone ends up submitting to the Revolution's End the same. You're going to scream just like your friend... like a bitch. And Cage...? I invite you to come into the ring to mess things up. Try your best to take me down in a 2-on-1 battle. Do whatever you need to try and bring me down. In the end, I will be the man that triumphs, and you will be the one finding yourself without a title. [With another cocky smirk, the scene fades to black.] -------------- SEBASTIAN and TAI ANGELA (Sebastian is sitting in an office with the new BlackJack Champion Tai Angela, they are both laughing) SEBASTIAN: Somewhere, Classy Joey Boy is crying to his Gandma and his buddy Fatty. The great CJB no longer holds a title and he is stuck teaming with the loser G-Gangsta! You know Tai, Sebastian thought after Ghost Town Blues that Sebastian was going to have to destroy this federation on Sebastians own. But now Sebastian has you and more importantly Tai Angela has Sebastian. (Angela smiles, well you know as if The Dark Monster can smile) And this Thursday Sebastian lays the cards on the table. G-Gangsta.....put up the 24 Hour Title during our tag team match and Tai Angela will put up the BlackJack Title. If....and Sebastian does mean if....one of you can pin Angela the title is yours. But when....and Sebastian does mean when....Sebastian pins you G-Gangsta...Sebastian will be (holds up three fingers) 24 Hour Champion once again! Got the guts? ------------------- 28TH STREET (Hakeem and Tyson are sitting in the RumJungle Bar in North Las Vegas) TYSON: Who in the damn hell are these punks from New York? HAKEEM: Brooklynn and Queenz, managed by Bronxs. TYSON: They had damn well bring the five bourouoghs on Thursday! I have had it with this damn federation! This week we go right through the NYCH and Triple X and then we rip the ass out of the kid who makes a mockery of the tag team world! HAKEEM: Maquilladora! Little snot nosed punk! Notice he isn't even on the card. TYSON: Needs a week off I'll bet! HAKEEM: The little bastard isn't good enough to go one on one with one of us, how in the __hell__ is he going to beat both of us? TYSON: (drinks then wipes his mouth) He better be at ringside Thursday, so he can scout us. HAKEEM: And what will he see? TYSON: He will see us finally run the fat guys, Triple X out of VWF! And the new boys...and I do mean boys.....the NYCH. I say we make it personal between us and them! Brook and Queen...... HAKEEM: I saw them open for Brittany. TYSON: I dare you to walk down ___TWENTY EIGHTH STREET___!!!! --------- XTREME TONY CAGE and INSOMNIAC (Scene opens in front of the VWF logo with both Insomniac and Tony Cage standing there, baseball bat's in hands.) XTC: Here we go VWF, one man has pushed us too GOD DAMN far. Here comes what we didn't want to happen. Crane, RANDALL CRANE, you have condemned yourself, and the rest of the VWF!!!! Here is the last chance to repent, for the INSOMNIAC is now unleashed. Randall Crane, here we go. Last week, you didn't get my title, my DAMN TITLE, the T3 title!!!! I took this from "Absolute Arrogance" Erik Andrews, and they are NEVER taking it away from me. You got me, I admit it, but this week, you have to pay. Insomniac is going to destroy you. I can't stop him, you can't stop him, NOONE can stop him. Randall, you're screwing yourself OVER!!!!!! I tell you what, soon enough you can have the shot again, but until then forget it. Randall, Insomniac gets to destroy you this week, gets to show you how HE plays the game. You'll feel the 200 pound difference between me and Insomniac, his awesome power, his ability. He's got you down pat my friend! Wait until Thursday, You will PAY for your SINS!! Insomniac: Sleep......is for.....the weak.......... ( Insomniac grabs the logo and destroys the area around him as Tony Cage laughs hysterically)
