VWF War of Words, 1 April 2002

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF War of Words April 1, 2002 THUNDER this Thursday! T3 Championship as TONY CAGE defends against THOMAS RAINKA and THE REAL DEAL THE MAQUILLADORA takes on CAPSMAN and SONICMAN G-GANGSTA faces SEBASTIAN JASON STONE goes against THE LEGEND DELICIOUS DON BROOKLYNN is in the ring with TAI ANGELA! ------------------- SEBASTIAN (Sebastian is pacing around his spacious home like an angry tiger) SEBASTIAN: G-Gangsta! A has been...hell a never was seems to want to get in the face of the greatest champion in VWF history! It only proves that the Gangsta is one of the, if not the dumbest man in the VWF! Gangsta, do you think Sebastian has time for you? Do you Sebastian gives a good damn about your petty feuds? The answer is HELL NO! Sebastian has only one thing on Sebastians mind and that is taking the 24 Hour Title! Be it from Strife or Andrews. It does not matter to Sebastian! But Sebastian is willing to make you a deal Gangsta....a deal! If you can defeat the one and only Sebastian...by pinfall or submission, inside the ring at the (smugly) Boys and Girls Club or whatever dump this federation lands in then Sebastian will never...and Sebastian means NEVER....get another shot at the 24 Hour Title again! (big smile) But Gangsta....if...Sebastian means when, you TAP OUT to the SEX YOU OUT then you wil NEVER get another shot at the 24 Hour Title! Are you man enough G-Gangsta? Sebastian is....and you are NOT! (fade) ------------------------------------------------------- THE MAQUILLADORA and SONICMAN (Maq and Yak are walking around backstage when they come across the still fallen Sonicman.) Maq- What the hell happened to you? Sonic- Shut the f- up and leave me the f- alone, all right?! Maq- Woah there buddy. Watch yourself. You don't want to mess with all three of us. Sonic- Three...its you and Yakabi...thats two, moron. Maq- You forget about my tag partner, idiot. How'd you end up on the floor anyway. Sonic- That loser boy wonder hit me with the door. (Maq and Yak burst into hysterical laughter and roll on the floor) Maq- CAPSMAN did this too you?! You got beat up by CAPSMAN?! Yak- Your the first person in about three years to lose to CAPSMAN. Maybe the only person ever. Maq- Yeah, old ladies have beat him up. (Maq and Yak continue laughing. Eventually they try to regain their composure.) Maq- Anyway, this thursday we will... (Yakabi starts snickering and both burst into hysterica laughter again.) Sonic- Shut up, its not funny. (Maq and Yak gain their composure again.) Sonic- Seriously, its not. Maq- Sorry...ha ha ha. (Even the cameraman loses his composure this time and all three laugh hysterically as Sonicman turns red and storms off only to run into another open door. Maq and Yak are rolling on the floor laughing as the camerman drops the camera and breaks it...thus ending the promo) ----------------------------------------- SONICMAN and CAPSMAN (Sonicman is backstage, walking and talking to the camera. He is pretty calm and colected...at least by his standards.) Sonic- I'm really getting sick of this Maq and Wonderboy sh!t! I'm not a f-ing wrestler...I'm an actor damnit. Am I even getting paid for this? I have a contract with Panasonic for their ads and I can't even film any ads because I always have to be wrestling people. This sucks. Maq sucks. The fans suck. Life sucks. This company sucks. The food sucks. And I won't even get started on CAPSMAN. What a waste of space. How the hell did he even get into wrestling anyway? Old ladies beat this guy up and dude wrestles????! What the f-! Its ridiculous. (WHACK! Suddenly a door swings open and smacks Sonicman in the face. Out walks CAPSMAN.) CAPS- GREETINGS! TIS I THE MOST LOVED WRESTLER AND SUPERHERO TO EVER GRACE THE FACE OF THE EARTH, CAPSMAN! (Realizes he hit someone with the door. He looks and see Sonicman who is lying face down on the floor and not moving) CAPS- GET UP YOU HEATHEN! THEY DON'T LET PEOPLE SLEEP ON THE JOB. ARE YOU DIGNITARILY CHALLENGED OR SOMETHING. Camerman- Dignitarily? CAPS- YES, AS IN DIGNITY. (Sonicman begins to move a little.) CAPS- THATS THE SPIRIT. THERE IS NO TIME FOR REST. WE'VE GOT WORK TO DO. Sonic- We!? What the f- we?! CAPS- DON'T MAKE ME WASH THAT POTTY MOUTH OUT. THERE IS NO NEED FOR VIOLENCE OF ANY KIND...NOT EVEN VIOLENT LANGUAGE. DOES YOUR MOTHER KNOW YOU SAY THINGS LIKE THAT. Sonic- She's dead you bastard. CAPS- OH. WELL YOUR NOT. STAND UP. WHY ARE YOU ON THE GROUND ANYWAY. Sonic- Cause you hit me with the f-ing door. CAPS- I MOST CERTAINLY DID NOT. Sonic- Listen, if you don't get the f- out of here you will join my mom in the next life...capiche? (CAPSMAN talks into his radio...er...watch.) CAPS- READY YEE MIGHTY STALLIONS. WE MUST EMBARK INSTANTANEOUSLY. (CAPSMAN rushes off as the batman them plays and the screen fades) --------------------- THE LEGEND (The Legend is sitting outside the Las Vegas Rescue Mission on Bonanza Road) THE LEGEND: You know what? I kinda like hearing the fans cheer my name. I know they were only doing that because they hate Erik Andrews but I gotta respect the guy. He came out and made a name for himself by beating Strife...I know he didn't win the title but we all know who won that match. And I am going to begin to do the same, make a name for myself. And it all starts with Jason Stone. How in the hell is this guy still getting booked on VWF cards? Who the hell is he? No matter, after Thursday he is done! The Legend is coming Jason and bringing the Bag of Tricks to the ring! And after that it will be time for The Legend to go after a belt here in Vegas. So champions, you are on notice! The Legend is coming!
