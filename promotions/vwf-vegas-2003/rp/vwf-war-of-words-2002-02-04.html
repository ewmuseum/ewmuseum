VWF War of Words, 4 February 2002

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF Presents WAR OF WORDS Feb 4, 2002 Note- Erik Andrews is no longer in the VWF. Please not the change on the upcoming card, Sebastian will now face Johnny Sanders instead of Erik Andrews. Sebastians RP has been included and will count even though it was written with Erik Andrews in mind. In this issue CLASSY JOEY BOY (BlackJack Champion) REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE THE MAQUILLADORA SEBAASTIAN 28TH STREET (tag team) ************************ CLASSY JOEY BOY (BLACKJACK CHAMPION- Facing Tony Cage Thursday) (We see CJB and Fatty Jay inside there hotel room only days after Classy's classic battle with Angela. Classy is rather bruised but is able to walk and comprehend whats is going on. Grandma Joey Boy walks in the room with help from a walking cane. She is holding a small trophy. Classy seems a bit upset that she was not at the PPV. He walks over to her and gets right in her face.) CJB: Just where in the hell where you Wednesday? Grandma: Relax! I saw it on the big screen at the strip club. CJB: The strip club?! Just what in god's name were you doing at a strip club? Grandma: It was amateur's night. And I won! (She holds up her Amateur Strip Night 1st Prize Trophy.) Fatty: Dear god! It must have sucked to at that strip club! CJB: Grandma, strip clubs want hot young college girls at be at amateur night, not 64 year old lady's. Grandma: But I won! And I destrpyed the competition with my succulent... Fatty: Stop! Your making me sick! Grandma: Besides, I saw everything. You boned him pretty good. CJB: Boned? Grandma: Yeah, boned......you gave him a good workout, good job sneaking in from behind. CJB: Grandma! I am not into dudes. Fatty: Yeah, but you still boned Tia Angela. CJB: I did not! Fatty: Dude, it's cool. Grandma: We all saw it Classy, you kept pounding him with your...... CJB: Grandma! Fatty! Stop joking around! Fatty: Dude, we are being serious...you boned the hell out of Tia Angela! CJB: Ok, you two are pissing me off! Fatty: We done't mean it sexually, we mean it literally! If it wasn't for you boning Tia, you wouldn't have come out the winner......that Femur saved your butt! Grandma: He was beating the hell out of you until then. CJB: Well I won, and that's what matters. I put on a good show. Now, we are on too Tony Cage. Here's a guy who needs to change his nickname. I mean "Xtreme", jee thats original. Besides, I am the "VWF Team Captain", and the BlackJack Champion, and the best looking man in the VWF. Fatty: Cage got lucky at GTB, but he won't be so lucky Thursday. CJB: You know it! Now lets get going, I have to get some medicine for my the pains in my head. Oh, and Grandma, no more strip clubs! I mean it! (CJB and Fatty Jay leave the room) Grandma: I am a champion now, I have to defend my title! *************************************** REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE (Facing Bavarin Thursday) [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a shot of the UNLV campus, where college students are seen walking and studying. We then cut to a shot of the bookstore, where students and vacationers alike browse the books and clothing. One of these such people is "The Revolution" Randall Crane, who is looking at a UNLV hat. Crane looks to be a little banged up from his debut match, but it doesn't look like it bothers him whatsoever. Crane tries the hat on, then walks over to a mirror to check it out. He nods his head as if he's satisfied, then takes the hat off. Crane then turns around and begins to speak, continuing to browse as he does so.] RC: Ghost Town Blues is now a memory. New champions have been established, and new stars have begun to creep into the spotlight. I guess when it comes right down to it... the VWF pay-per-view was "sweet." Isn't that right, Johnny Sanders? Oh... wait a second. You didn't get the win. You didn't live up to the promise that you'd lock in "The Sweetener" and get your hand raised. You didn't get to hear the fans chant your name. However... the fans got to hear you scream like a [BLEEP]. [Crane smirks, obviously content with his win.] RC: Just as I told you... the revolution was going to take you as its first victim. I promised that you'd be nothing more than a mere rung in the ladder, just a pawn in this little game we enjoy so dearly. Nothing made me happier than to hear you scream, Sanders. To give up in front of fans that paid good money to see Ghost Town Blues. And when I walked out of the ring and viewed your lifeless body... The VWF learned that I'm not one to [BLEEP] with. [Crane's smirk turns to a look of near-insanity.] RC: Now the mood changes to the next rung in the ladder, on my way to the destruction of _every_ man contracted with the VWF. My next opponent is another newcomer... a man known by the name of Bavarin. I watched this man as he also made his debut at Ghost Town Blues. I heard the girls as they screamed for you, nearly making themselves moist for you. I suppose that makes you happy, and it might impress you. Well... that doesn't do a _damn_ thing for me. [Crane continues to browse, setting the hat down.] RC: The fact of the matter is Bavarin, you _pale_ in comparison to me. You might percieve yourself as someone of great size and strength, but I will break you down just the same... like a rung on the ladder. You got yourself a lucky win at Ghost Town Blues... but come Thursday Thunder, your winning streak will come to an abrupt _halt_. You _will_ fall victim to Revolution's End. You _will_ scream for mercy and give up. [A burning rage glows in Crane's eyes.] RC: And you will _never_ be the same after falling victim to the revolution. Good luck, son. [The shot then dissolves to black as Crane walks away.] ************************** THE MAQUILLADORA (Facing Blaze half of the tag team champs Thursday) The Maquilladora and Yakabi are in a meadow on a fine spring day...well actually its February but with the messed up weather its like spring...anyway. Meghann is with them and they are wandering about. Cheesy music is playing. Maq- What a lovely day. Yak- Yes indeedy. Meg- Its f-ing gorgeous out here...like me. Maq- All is well in the VWF. Everything is perfect- (A bird, on a tree branch, craps and it lands on Maq's shoulder) Maq- Ah damnit! That little bastard. (He looks up and sees the bird looking down at him) Maq- I'll show him. (Maq begins to climb the tree. He gets to the branch and the bird just stares at him. Maq removes a hand from the branch to hit the bird. The bird walks over and pecks him in the forehead.) Maq- Sonofa- Ah! (Maq loses grip and falls to the ground landing on top of Yakabi.) Maq- Woah, that was lucky. Yak- UH, a little help. Maq- Sorry about that, thanks for breaking my fall. Yak- (groaning) Don't mention it. (Noises begin to develop in a nearby bush. Curiously Maq goes to investigate.) Maq- OOOH! Maybe its a little bunny rabbit! (Out comes an eight foot bear. It growls loadly and begins to chase Maq and Co.) Maq- Oh shit! Run! (Maq, Yak, and Meg run for their lives. The bear is still in hot pursuit. Music now changes to your typical chase music. The camera now shows a Hummer in the distance. Maq is the first one back and dives through the open window. He starts the car as Yak hops in with Meg not to far behind. Maq floors it as she gets in the car. The bear lunges at the car and takes of the back bumper. The Hummer speeds away.) Maq- I hate nature! Yak- No shit. Maq- Where to now? Yak- How bout downtown. We should check on Denis and Sonicman. Maq- Ah, yes, the Franz. I wonder if he has gotten anywhere with Sonicman yet. We'll have to head to the airport then. Hit it Yakabi. (Yakabi then pulls out a police siren and lights and sticks it on top of the car. The Hummer turns onto a highway as the camera fades with the sirens blarring loudly.) ********** SEBASTIAN (facing Johnny Sanders Thursday RP done before change) (Sebastian is sitting in the Thomas and Mack center, it is set up for UNLV hoops) SEBASTIAN: Thursday night it will happen again. It has happened in the past and this Thursday it will happen again. Sebastian (points to self) will educate another VWF star on who is the man here. And that is Sebastian! Erik Andrews, Sebastian knows you are a tough man. But you are not a great man, like Sebastian. You may have been the T3 champion until last week but you can never reach the heights that Sebastian has. The only! TWO TIME 24 HOUR CHAMPION in VWF history! (a kid, cleaning up the arena is heard behind Sebastian) KID: What about Demonic? Didn't he win the title for a second time at Ghost Town Blues? (excited) He and Dage had a great battle....and G-Gangsta and Spade had a great battle.....Hey! Didn't Maquilladora beat.. SEBASTIAN: Shut up! Get back to work nobody! (looks back at the camera) He is an ignorant as the fans of the VWF! The Maquilladora clearly cheated to get the win at Ghost Town Blues. He can go on and on about Sonicman or CAPSMAN or whatever the hell he wants, but in his heart he knows he is not as good as Sebastian. And Gangsta and Spade, losers! Both in the shadow of Sebastian! And Demonic is not a true champion until he steps in the ring with Sebastian! Demonic beat Dage to win the title! DAGE!?!?!?!? Who is he? Where did he come from? The VWF is going to be righted, like a ship! When once again Sebastian is the 24 Hour Champion! (turns and shouts at the kid) Who are you? Not Sebastian! (fade) ************ 28TH STREET (facing Triple X thursday) (Tyson and Hakeem are sitting in a bar in North Las Vegas, they are wearing RCW SUX t-shirts and laughing) TYSON: I got one.......how many RCW guys does it take to screw in a light bulb? HAKEEM: How many? TYSON: None, those idiots are in the dark all the time! HAKEEM: OK, ok. How many RCW members does it take to stop 28th Street from taking the tag team titles? TYSON: I know this one.......damn! How many? HAKEEM: There aren't enough of them to ever do that! TYSON: This Thursday we make our presence known, not just to Triple X but to the RCW and the EOV. HAKEEM: The EOV? TYSON: Some guy named Jack Spade and his buddy Dage. Both formers. HAKEEM: Formers? TYSON: Former champs! In other words nobodies, in other words dead weight the VWF carries just cause of some dumbass punk history they have. HAKEEM: Oh. TYSON: If it moves on Thursday night, 28th Street will land on it! (fade)
