VWF War of Words, 21 January 2002

22 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WAR OF WORDS VWF January 21 __ __ ___ __ \ \ / /_ _ _ _ / _ \ / _| \ \/\/ / _` | '_| | (_) | _| \_/\_/\__,_|_| \___/|_| __ __ _ \ \ / /__ _ _ __| |___ \ \/\/ / _ \ '_/ _` (_-< \_/\_/\___/_| \__,_/__/ THIS WEEK Xtreme Tony Cage Classy Joey Boy The Maquilladora Tai Angela w/Blackbird SPECIAL ANNOUNCMENT: The Ace Jack Spade has been named as the 3rd man in this Thursdays T3 Title match! The former 24 Hour Champ will go after the title of Erik Andrews as does Xtreme Tony Cage! Here is the card for Thursday Thunder January 24!!! ***24 Hour Title Match*** DEMONIC (C) vs DAGE ***T3 Title Match*** ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS (C) vs XTREME TONY CAGE vs THE ACE JACK SPADE ***Tag Team Title Match*** BLAZE and REAL DEAL (C) vs TRIPLE X ***RCW Title Match (Last Man Standing)*** G-GANGSTA (C) vs THE LEGEND ***Steel Cage*** ZELL STRIFE vs THOMAS RAINKA CLASSY JOEY BOY & MAQUILLADORA vs SEBASTIAN & TAI ANGELA 28TH STREET vs THE VBA ------------------------- ***XTREME TONY CAGE*** (Scene opens on the most extreme wrestler in the sport today, Xtreme Tony Cage. Tony sits staring at a piece of paper in his hands and then looks at the camera.) XTC: Why hello there VWF!! How are we today?? Would you look at this??? (Tony holds the paper up: TV Matches is on the top, and XTC vs. Erik Andrews vs ??? T3 title, is highlighted.) XTC: This is crazy!! This is BULL!!! How am I supposed to get ready for my title shot? I have to fight this Andrews loser, and then some other guy. Can I be notified of this? I mean, how am I supposed to show you all what is it to be XTREME!!!!!! Well, you know what, I have to apologize to two people while I have you're attention. Strife, Rainka, I'm sorry, but you're match at the PPV will not be for the T3 title. It will be around XTC's waist at that time kids. Now, onto other...owwww, a papercut. MEDIC!!!!! (XTC holds his finger as a small drop of blood drips. A man in a field medic uniform runs over, looks XTC in the eyes, and laughs.) XTC: What are you laughing at??? It hurts damn you!!! Make it better like mommy used to. (The medic hands XTC a scooby-doo bandaid, and then walks away., laughing. Tony puts the band-aid on, the blows on his finger.) XTC: That's better. Now, I'm done with the arrogant snob Andrews, I'm moving on to bigger and better fish. Hey, Classy Joey Boy, how's it going? Remember me? I bet not. No Classy, you're too big league for me. Well Classy, I feel sorry for you. You're in a tag match with The Maquilladora. Didn't you see him last week? Yeah, I beat him. CJB, Mr. Blackjack champ, have fun with that title. Soon, CJB, you'll get what's coming to you. Until then, I'm out of here. (Tony gets up, turns around, and walks stright into a wall, knocking himself down.) ***CLASSY JOEY BOY (BlackJack Champion)*** [CJB and Fatty Jay exit a night club. A black stretch limo is waiting for them. The two are rushed by women as they make their way to the limo. The driver opens the door as the Team Captain and his sidekick get close. They enter the limo and the driver closes the door. Inisde the limo, CJB and Fatty Jay are in dissapointment. It seems Grandma Joey Boy has caught up to them on this night. She was waiting inside the limo. The limo takes off.} CJB: Hello Grandma. Grandma: You know something Classy, I have been in this business for sixty-three years. And for the first time in your life, you have won an honorable title. I still don't like you, cause your not as good as Grandpa or your father, but your ok. CJB: Gee thanks Grandma. Fatty: I think Classy is doing great! He is the Blackjack Champion. And this Thursday he will team with La Macarana to take on Sebastian Bach and Tia Angela. [Grandma laughs a little] Grandma: Classy, at Ghost Town Blues, you will have your first real title defense. Take Angela seriously. Classy: It's in the bad Grandma! I'm smarter, better looking, faster, more agile, and I get to pick the rules! GrandmaL Well pick smart! Because this could be your last title defense! Now, onto you Mr. Fat. You need to lose weight! From now on you eat only fruits and vegetables. Fatty: Fruits? Vegetable? What are those? Sounds like some sort of cake or pie? CJB: No Fatty. Look, Thursday I am going to take out Tia and Sebastian myself. Lord knows Maq is no good, so you need to be there to watch my back! Grandma: Your father didn't need anyone to watch his back! CJB: Shup up Grandma! [Grandma slaps CJB and the back of the head.] Grandma: Don't mock me boy! Driver! Stop at the hotel I need my beauty sleep! ***THE MAQUILLADORA*** (The Maquilladora and Yakabi are walking through the streets of Miami...trying to track down Sonicman to find out about the whereabouts of CAPSMAN.) Maq- Hello VWF faithful. We are still at your service trying to figure out where are favorite wannabe superhero CAPSMAN, has gone. Yak- We recieved a tip from a well...somewhat credible...source that he is in fact in cognito as Sonicman, who does ads for Panasonic shavers. Maq- See, both are highly annoying, bald, have bad goatees, no hair, look scandanavian and are dispised by people throughout the land. Yak- Like the great state of Chicago. (Maq stops and stares at Yakabi) Maq- Way to go there Dan Quale...first of all, we are in Miami...second of all Chicago is not a state. Yak- Its a state of mind? Maq- That's Orange County dipshit. Yak- Oh!...Oh yeah, got ya. Maq- Anyways...history will be in the making as I tag with the "Team Captain" Classy Joey Boy for the first time. We met a few times in the CWF and lets not go into detail- Yak- Cuz he kicked your ass everytime. Maq- Damnit you little bitch, don't interupt me while I'm talking our I will curb-stomp your ass right here and now. As I was saying...I have a lot of respect for CJB and no he has the skills to back up the smack that he runs. Now thab brins us to Sebastian and Ariel...the Dark Monster if you will. (Yakabi starts singing Under the Sea from the famous Disney movie The Little Mermaid) Maq- Hey I love Disney as much as the next guy but c'mon...this is wrestling. Sebastian may be my favorite red crustacian but he will be no match for me and CBJ...chaos theory supports this. (Yak and Maq then come upon Sonicman, filming his latest ad) Sonicman- Try our damn shaver! Maq- All right potty mouth...honeymoons over. Sonicman- Who the f- are you? Maq- You know damn well who I am CAPSMAN, you may have fooled the fine folks at Panasonic but you will not fool us. Sonicman- Who the hell is CAPSMAN? Yak- Very funny, we know who you are. Sonicman- Of course you do, I'm Sonicman...you might remember me from such TV ads as... Maq- Ok there Troy McClure, your coming with us. Sonicman- I'm not going anywhere. Who do you think you are anyways? Maq- I am the Maquilladora! Sonicman- Doesn't that mean "sweatshop" in spanish. Maq- No thats Maquiladora...that has one "l" mine has two "l"'s. Sonicman- Whatever, one "l" two "l"'s, either way...get the hell outta here. Maq- All right, thats it...if you can't be reasoned with. Get HIM FRANKIE!! (A huge man in a black suit rushes in and holds Sonicman's hands behind him. The Maquilladora then grabs a surenge and injects it into Sonicman, who goes unconscience and is carried off by Frankie. Then Maq holds the surenge up to the camera) Maq- Animal tranquilizers...great on family vacations or those important meetings with your boss. Animal tranquilizers, don't leave home without it! (end of promo) ***TAI ANGELA with BLACKBIRD*** (Fires of hell blaze through the ground and the smoke of a thousand souls wafts toward the heaven) BLACKBIRD: Classy Joey Boy are you ignorant? Have you lost your mind? Do you really think you can match the Dark Monster? (Tai Angela is standing behind her, very serious) The Dark Monster does not care what kind of match we have at Ghost Town Blues, the only think we care about is that you are brave enough to show up! And bring Fatty and Grandma to Rhyolite. Bring the entire family Classy Joey Boy. They shall all feel the power and the force of hell that is Tai Angela! And on January 30th, Darkness will fall on CJB! (the fires of hell shoot into the air and Angela and his strange valet Blackbird are gone, leaving only a tombstone with the letters CJB on it)
