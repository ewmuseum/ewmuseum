VWF War of Words, 8 April 2002

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(OOC- Like everyone we are looking for new players. Been around since August of 2001, email me if you are interested) VWF War of Words April 8, 2002 NEWS VWF President Rob Beauty is reportly talking with two different hotels in Las Vegas to move the VWF back into a bigger arena. REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a shot of a hospital room. The faint noise of a television can be heard, and the glow from the set lights the area behind a curtain. Two silhouettes are seen, one figure is laying in a hospital bed, while the other figure stands, more than likely a nurse. For right now, the faces are a mystery, but the voices are audible. For right now, it's simply idle chit-chat about medicine. After a laugh from both figures, the curtain is opened. We finally see a nurse standing with "The Revolution" Randall Crane in the hospital bed. Crane is in a hospital gown, covered up to his midsection with a blanket. His head is bandaged, his right arm is in a sling, and a few spots on his body have stitches on them. Crane manages to take a sip of some sort of juice as the nurse continues to speak with him.] NURSE: I'm glad you're doing better. You're really close to being released into a rehabilitation program. RC: And into my own bed... in my own house! [The nurse smiles.] NURSE: You keep up the improvement, Randall. I'll be back later tonight to give you some more medication. RC: It's a date. [The nurse begins to turn red, smiling as she exits the room. The door is heard closing off camera, and Crane reaches for his remote, adjusting the bed so he is in an almost seated position. He takes a moment to get comfortable, then looks to the camera.] RC: Well Tony Cage... I guess it's true what they say about wishing upon a star. You must have been doing it every night leading up to the Pay Per View, and it looks like your wish came true. You plotted and planned, and it looks like it all worked out for you. You put me in this hospital bed, Cage. You started by destroying my car... and ended up almost destroying my entire career. To make sure you're paying attention... _almost_ destroyed it. You see, as I was spinning around in my car, right before I hit that barricade, I saw your little calling card. [Crane reaches over to a bedside table, where he picks up a little Post-It note which reads "Good Luck Tonight... you'll need it. - Tony." With a scowl on his face, he sets it back down and continues.] RC: As I laid back in my driver's seat, my head being scraped against the pavement, I realized I brought it upon myself. When I went to the impound to pick up the car that _you_ stole, I figured you were out for a joyride. Just a little something to aggravate me just a little more. Little did I know, you had your work cut out for you. As the ambulance sirens blared all around me, there was only one thing on my mind. It's the same thing that has been on my mind these past few weeks... and the same thing that is on my mind right now. _Revenge._ I told myself no matter how hard I had to work, I'd be back in the VWF. I used to have just one goal, that was to dominate the competition and be the best wrestler in this sport. But now... my perspective on life has changed more than you'll ever know. Originally, I just wanted that title around your pathetic little waist... But now, I want your _career_. I don't care what I have to go through, either. They can put me through the hardest rehab ever experienced by anyone. They can put me at the bottom of the VWF roster and force me to work my way back up. Hell, they can throw two or three guys at a time at me. You and I will meet... one-on-one. No more tricks, no more bull[BLEEP~!]. When that moment comes, I will make sure that you are left laying in the ring. I will make sure that you will experience the same pain that I felt just a few weeks ago. And unlike you... I will make sure that your career is _finished_. [An evil smirk purses the lips of Randall Crane.] RC: I will be back, Tony. You won't know where, and you won't know when. If I were you, I'd work on sewing some eyeballs in the back of your head. When the moment is right, I will strike down with a fury that you've never even imagined in your worst nightmares. This is _far_ from over, Tony. Enjoy the time you have left. [With a sinister laugh, the scene slowly fades to black.] --------------------------------- THE MAQUILLADORA (The Maquilladora and Yakabi are riding around on the forklift after the last card. They are on the streets of some city. They keep running into sh!t.) Maq- Stupid peice of crap! Yak- Easy there killer. Maq- Sorry, this thing handles like those carts you get at the airport of hotels that never work. Yak- Yeah, its pretty bad. Maq- Where the hell was this thing made again? Yak- Technically, it no longer exists. Maq- Where am I headed again? Yak- 28th Street. Maq- Oh yeah...oh sh!t! (Maq runs the forklift into a parked car. Its a silver Mercedes Benz and the alarm immediately goes off. Dogs bark and lights of nearby apartments begin to turn on.) Yak- Smooth move X-Lax. Maq- Shut up or you'll walk home. (Maq steps on the accelerator and the fork lift...cough...peels away...cough cough at a "high speed" of maybe 3-4 miles per hour. An old lady with a walker and old man with a cane speed past them. The man hits Yakabi with his cane and shouts some racial slurs.) Maq- Damn that World War II generation. Yeah the war sucked but its no excuse to take it out on all people of Orientaly descent. You weren't responsible for World War II or Pearl Harbor. These crusty old bastards need to lighten the f- up. Yak- Yeah, it was getting better there for a while but I still run into a few bigots every now and again. Most of the actual soldiers don't care if where I'm from. (They finally arrive at 28th Street and Franklin Ave. Not a soul is seen.) Maq- Damnit, where are they? Yak- Who? Maq- Hakeem and Tyson Chicken. Yak- You mean Tyson Young. Maq- Whatever...who cares. I'm hungry so I'm kinda thinking about that. Yak- Does that explain why you drive like Scott Hall? Maq- Damnit! Leave him alone, he had his issues but that is no reason to tear down one half of the greatest tag teams ever. Scott Hall is the f-ing man and you know it. Yak- Yeah but Nash is a bastard. Maq- True, but I don't pay attention to what happens in those other federations. There too close to porn anymore. Yak- Speaking of porn, what the hell happened to your chick? Maq- You mean Meghann? Yak- Yeah. Maq- She's at my hotel room in Vegas. She didn't like being around the wrestling scene any more so she's taking care of things at home and... Yak- Stripping on the side? Maq- Ha ha...thats SOOO funny. You dumb bastard. I should kick your @$$ for that one. (The old man from before walks into the screen. He begins his tyrade once again.) Maq- Insolent old fart. Ramming speed! (The forklift motors to the old man. He continues to go on and on with slur after slur as the forklift rumbles slowly towards him. He strikes it with his cane and the fork lift falls to peices. Yakabi falls on his butt and Maq also lands on his butt and is still holding the steering wheel.) Maq- Damnit! Yak- No wonder that country no longer exists. (The old man makes his way to Yakabi still yelling. Maq sees this and chucks the steering wheel at the old man. It strikes him in the side of the head and he falls to the pavement.) Yak- Thanks, that guy was a real a$$hole. Maq- Yakabi, if I've said it once, I've said it a thousand times. Senior citizens may have there place in society and it isn't in the fast line of the freeway. Yak- True. Where is there place then? Maq- The golf courses, Florida, Arizona, nursing homes or preferably Canada. Yak- Canada sucks. Maq- Yes it does. Hockey is cool though especially considering all of the teams are in America now. Hockey in the desert of Arizona where it belongs. Yak- With the old geezers. Maq- Yes indeed, with the old fat geezers. Yak- The 28th Street isn't here. Lets go get something to eat. Maq- Yeah, I'm staving lets go. (Maq and Yak walk off camera. The camera zooms on the old man lying on the street. Sonicman jumps up from behind a dumpster and steels the old man's cane and runs off as the camera fades.) ------------------- SEBASTIAN (Close up) SEBASTIAN: (red from anger and sarcastic) A chance at the BlackJack Title? Who in the hell cares about the BlackJack title? G-Gangsta does because it is the only belt he could ever hold! Rainka cares because he doesn't have the talent to hold, or to win one of the BIG titles. Keep in mind Tommy, Zell Strife can't hand you this one! And The Legend! We all know what is going to happen to him. Once again G-Gangsta is going to put him out for a few weeks! So that only leaves Sebastian! And Sebastian does NOT want a chance at Classy Joey Boy and his pitiful title! Sebastian wants a chance at the 24 Hour Title! Sebastian wants Zell Strife and or Erik Andrews, whoever leaves with the title is who Sebastian wants. So, Sebastian will NOT, wrestling in some hardcore match with three freaks hoping to ressurect their careers. This Thursday will therefore be a day off before Sebastian rightful match for the 24 Hour Title! (fade) ---------------- CAPSMAN (Sonicman makes his way through the dark streets with the cane that he stole from the old man. The batmobiles rushes past him and crashes into a telephone pole. CAPSMAN jumps out of it.) CAPS- HALT! VILE VILLAIN! Sonic- Go away CAPSBOY or I'll beat you to death with this. CAPS- WHEN WILL YOU LEARN. STEALING FROM THE NEEDY NEVER PAYS! Sonic- The government does it all the time so I can too. CAPS- THE GOVERNMENT PROTECTS US FROM VILLAINS LIKE YOU. STEALING CANES FROM THOSE ADVANCED IN AGE NEVER PAYS THE BILLS. SO UNHAND YE CANE. Sonic- Oh I'll unhand it all right. CAPS- GOOD. THERE MAY BE HOPE FOR YOU. Sonic- I'll unhand it right in your ass! (Sonic man moves to hit CAPSMAN over the head with the cane but the cane is grabbed by the old man. He has come to and is pissed...luckily he was wearing Depends. The old man begins to beat the crap out of Sonicman with the cane.) Old Man- Take that sonny! I'll teach you to respect your elders. Obviously your parents never taught you not to steal. I'll teach you some manners yet. (The old man wails on Sonicman until he is unconscious.) CAPS- YOU GO OLD MAN! Old man- Who the hell are you? CAPS- TIS I, CAPSMAN! Old man- Why are you dressed like that? Its not Halloween. Its April. CAPS- I'M A SUPER-HERO. I DEFEND PEOPLE FROM EVIL. I MAKE SURE JUSTICE PREVAILS ACROSSED THIS GREAT LAND. Old man- I was a hero y'know. CAPS- REALLY? WHAT KIND? Old man- I was a soldier in WWII. CAPS- REALLY? WE'RE YOU IN THE MARINES? Old man- No, I actually didn't fight at all. I was going to though. CAPS- I'M SURE YOU WERE. Old man- Well I gotta go, its Bingo night. (The old man leaves and CAPSMAN turns his attention to the camera.) CAPS- GREETING SENIOR CITIZENS OF ALL AGES! TIS I, THE ONE AND ONLY CAPSMAN. (A chorus of male singers sing CAPSMAN like a fanfare for a second.) CAPS- THIS THURSDAY I WILL BE FACING THE DARK MONSTER. BUT FEAR NOT. MONSTERS ARE NO MATCH FOR YOUR FAVORITE SUPERHERO. (Enrique Iglesias "SuperHero" begins to play in the background.) NO MATTER HOW DARK THEY ARE. ITS MY JOB ANYWAY. TO RID THIS GREAT PLANET OF EVIL, ONE STEP AT A TIME. (The camera fades as Julio's son continues to sing.) --------------------- 28TH STREET (Hakeem and Tyson Young are watching the tape of them hitting Maquilladora with the forklift) HAKEEM: Now we only got the one. TYSON: What? HAKEEM: We only nailed the one, the other one will still be 100 percent. TYSON: What in the hell are you talking about? HAKEEM: The tag team champs! We only hit the one. TYSON: There is only one. HAKEEM: He has a partner, I have seen them tag. TYSON: It is the same guy, The Maquilladora. HAKEEM: There is only one!? TYSON: Yes. HAKEEM: How can one guy be the tag team, TEAM champions? TYSON: Cause this is the most ass backward fed in the world! HAKEEM: So it will be you and I against one guy? TYSON: Yes. 28th Street against The Maquilladora, Tag Team Belts on the line! HAKEEM: This should be easy. TYSON: *big smile* Exactly. *long pause* HAKEEM: Didn't he beat us once before? TYSON: Shut up! (fade) ----------------------------- CLASSY JOEY BOY (It's the about 4 A.M. in downtown Vegas. The air is thick yet cool, and the sky is clear revealing all the stars. A popular local night club is closing. Young adults pile out of the building. Two of them happen to be CJB and Fatty Jay. Classy is wearing a white shirt tucked into black jeans. Big, dark sunglasses cover his eyes. The Blackjack title is drapped over his right shoulder, and is hidden behind a black father boa, which hangs around his neck. His dark tan and shaven face attract many young women. Fatty is wearing his traditional black sweat pants, white button down shirt with a CJB logo tie. His haor is styled much like Elvis'. They exit the club and stop at the curb infront of the building. A limo pulls up, and they are about to get in when the crowd, which has gethered behind them start a loud 'CJB" chant. CJB turns and aknowledges the crowd.) CJB: Stop, stop, stop. You guys don't need to do that. (The crowd hushes to a silent as CJB has instructed.) CJB: Ok, maybe you do! C..J..B...C...J..B (The CJB chant has started once again. After a few moments the crowd hushes so CJB can speak.) CJB: Just for the record, I'm back and at 100%!(The crowd cheers) And after beating Tia Angela for the second time my confidence is pretty high. But past events seem to be holding me from moving on in my career. It's time to settle unfinished business. So I'm calling you out Jason Stone! When CJB became a huge part of the wrestling world, it was partially because you and I were in the middle of one of the biggest feuds. We brought in the media and the fans to a certain organization. However, we never got to finish what we started........until now! (Fatty gets the crowd to start a VWF chant) CJB: It's time Stone! Time to bring the past to the present, and finish what should have been finished a year ago. We beat the hell out of each other in school gym's, in YMCA's, but I never thought I would end up in a Boy's and Girl's club. This is almost a low for CJB, I shouldn't even be working in a boys and girls club. I should just "no show" this event. But since I am the VWF Team Captain, I must be the one to land us a deal with a big arena. It will be Classy Joey Boy who takes the VWF to the big time. (The crowd is getting a little rowdy by now, and is really behind CJB.) CJB: The VWF was kicked out of the Mac and Cheese Center because of the violence. Well come this Thursday......the Boy's and Girl's Club will be shocked. This Thursday will be my most violent match in the VWF. Stone, you will be bloodied, you will be hurt, and you will be in pain. As the VWF Team Captain I demand a Steal Cage match with Jason Stone, this Thursday on Thunder! Infact I am so damn confident that I will even put my Blackjack title on the line! (The gathering of people is going crazy after hearing the challenge laid out by CJB. Fatty opens the door to the limo and gets in. CJB steps in with one foot and looks into the camera) CJB: Stone, I hope you picked up, what the Team Captain put down. (Classy gets in the limo and shuts the door. The limo drives away.)
