VWF War of Words, 11 March 2002

11 March 2002

WAR OF WORDS March 11, 2002 This week: THE REAL DEAL THE MAQUILLADORA SEBASTIAN THE LEGEND OF XTREME 28TH STREET ------------------------ THE REAL DEAL (The scene opens up with a shot of Erik Andrews bashing your favorite wrestler (and mine) from behind with the steal steps. Again from another angle. And one more time...) (Fade to black.) (Re-open with a shot of The Real Deal nailing the ref with an elbow. We see this from another angle, and the shot dims except for the bright spot that highlights Andrews shoving the ref in the way.) (Fade to black. Red letters appear on-screen.) *Absolute Arrogance... *You got lucky... *This time... *But next time... *Next time will be different. (The scene re-opens in what appears to be some sort of strange basement or dungeon. The room, made entirely of stone, is dimly lit by candles and torches (think "Tales From the Cript"). The room is somewhat large, with various peices of gym equipment strewn about. The slowly rotates right, revealing a weight bench, a rack of dumb-bells, and a peck machine. Eventuall, The Real Deal comes into view. He is wearing baggy black jeans and a red, hooded sweater. He smiles, looking particularly evil in the particular lighting.) The Real Deal: Hello, wrestling fans. Well, Mr. Absolute Ass-hole, it would seem that you found a way to weasle your way out of wrestling a full match with me. I can't say that I blame you. If I was you and I had to fight me, I'd to the same damn thing. (He shifts his body weight a bit.) The Real Deal: Now then, there are a couple of things I'd like to clear up with you, Erik. You see, you keep referring to me as kid. The new guy. I get the sense you don't know how experienced I am. I've wrestled in five feds before this. I'm a former NGW, EWA, and RCW heavyweight champ. And the fact that you've never heard of me let's me know that you are ignorant, and don't scout your competition to well. And lord knows you have an unlimeted supply of video footage you could use to scout me out. But hey, If you wanna pretend I'm some jobber rookie with no experience, and continue to under estimate me, well, I suppose that will prove to be your undoing the next time we meet. (He move about 3 feet to the right, the camera follows him.) The Real Deal: Now, Unfortunately, we seem to be entered in some sort of tag team elimination bout. Not exactly my strong suit, but I do have a bit of experience with tag matches. Just don't let me catch you tagging out whenever I'm tagged in. Because I truly would love nothing more than to lift you up... (He raises his arms.) The Real Deal:... right into a powerbomb position... flip you into a face buster, and... (He throws his arms down.) The Real Deal: BAM!!! DONE DEAL! Followed up by th 1... 2 ... 3. Right in the middle of the ring. So if your dumb enough, go ahead and keep on under estimating me. I don't care. You'll pay for it whe I rip your lungs out your ass and feed 'em back to you. (He moves toward a candle on the wall.) The Real Deal: So put that in your pants and play with it. (He blows the candle out. Fade to black.) --------------------------------- THE MAQUILLADORA (The scene opens in a european city. Maq and Yak are walking around apparently searching for something.) Yak- Why are we here again? Maq- I have a elimination match and Bavarian is one of my oppenents. Yak- What does that have to do with Europe? Maq- What better place to scout your opponent than go to their roots. Yak- SO? Maq- So we are in Bavaria. Yak- I see, who else do you face? Maq- Blake Cantor and Jakob Stone. I'm teamed with Cage and Insominiac. Yak- You mean the Green Day CD? Maq- No the guy. Yak- Oh. Can't believe what happened in the courtroom. Maq- Tell us about it, the resemblence between Trojan Man and CAPSMAN is astounding. Sonicman is a mouthy little b!tch though. Yak- Us? We? What are you talking about. Maq- My tag partner and I! Yak- Dude, don't start that sh!t again. Maq- What sh!t...you mean the truth? Yak- You've been Lucky lately...don't blow it for some stupid mind game. Maq- Mind game? Y'know sometimes you say the oddest things Yakabi...you should go to a doctor and have your head examined...again. Yak- Again? We went to the doctor to examine your head. Maq- Yeah sure, you tell yourself that...sooner or later you'll believe it. Yak- Damnit! Maq- (Singing) I know that your leaving...you must have your reasons...uh...and its happened once again...i'll turn to a friend...someone that understands...and sticks to the master plan...but everyone is gone..and I've been here for too long...i guess this is growing up...du du du du Yak- You sing like Alanis Morriset. Maq- Thats weak...bastard. Yak- You lucky mutha f- (Maq burst into song again...and sings a duet version of "Luck be a lady tonight" with himself) Yak- Damnit...stop that. (Yak responds and starts singing Britney Spears "Lucky". As the trio/duo whatever the hell it are singing, dogs start barking and howling and cats starts screaming) Maq- Wrong song dude...Radiohead said it better. (Maq starts singing "Lucky" by Radiohead. A kid walks up to them) Kid- Did you say Radiohead? Yak- Yeah, kid he did. Kid- They rule! Maq- Ha ha ha no little buddy...I rule! Kid- What? Yak- I thought you liked Radiohead Maq? You stole your name from them. Maq- I don't need these accusations. You people are crazy. Yak- Damnit! (Yakabi walks off in frustration.) Maq- Now look what you did. Maq- Me? Maq- Yeah you, you freak! Maq- What your mouth in front of the chidren. Maq- Blow it out your -! Maq- Yeah well, I know what you are but what am I? Maq- Thats it...en guard. (Maq begins to face himself in a fencing match. The little kid starts screaming and runs off. Maq runs around, swings from a chandelier, and continues to duel with himself as a crowd gathers. Fans start yelling in European as the camera fades) ------------------- SEBASTIAN (The Great One is sitting inside the Thomas and Mack.....looking at the ring) SEBASTIAN: What a moment it shall be! What a great night in this arena. March 18th, when G-Gangsta and Zell Strife are both beaten by Sebastian! And the announcer will say.....Your Winner and THREE TIME 24 Hour Champion, Sebastian! As for this week, Sebastian is ready for the chance to get in the ring with Tai Angela and the Legend. And what a chance to create anger and hatred between the wanna be gangsta and the wanna be goth. Gangsta and Strife....a team? And who can forget Classy Joey Boy? Everyone! This no body doesn't even belong in the same ring as Sebastian. But these things happen. Bring it all gentlemen....and prepare to leave it in the ring.. Who am I? Sebastian and you ARE NOT! ------------- THE LEGEND OF XTREME (The Legend is sitting on street on the corner of one of the toughest neighborhoods in Las Vegas, he looks homeless) TLOX: Once again the Legend is in Vegas! And after my time I find the bottom of the barrell is still at the top...G-Gangsta...Classy Joey Boy and the one man tag team freak Maquilladora. My sights are set....not on anyone else but Classy Joey Boy! Boy! DO YOU KNOW WHO I AM? I am the Legend of Xtreme, these streets are my home. And I am going to make sure that you don't make it to The Gauntlet.....at least not in such great shape. Tai Angela will walk out of The Gauntlet with the BlackJack Title Boy! And I have a special challenge....I want that fat b**tard pal of your to get in the ring with me at The Gauntlet on March 18th. The Legend of Xtreme against Fatty Jay in a streetfight on Maryland Parkway out in front of the Thomas and Mack! So what do you say Fatty Jay? (thinks) Maybe I could be a poet.... (fade) --------------- 28TH STREET (Hakeem is looking around the Runway Bar for Tyson Young) HAKEEM: (yelling) Dawg! T Young. Damn where in the hell is he? (Tyson comes out of the ladies room with a beautiful lady...both look a little messed up, you know) HAKEEM: What the hell are ya doing? TYSON: Do I gotta spell it out for ya? HAKEEM: We got some big matches coming up. Six man tag. TYSON: Ya, who the hell is Erik Andrews anyway? HAKEEM: Our partner. TYSON: He had damn well better hold his own or I am gonna rip him one. HAKEEM: I don't give a ratass bout him, I want the New York City Headliners to suffer. TYSON: They will. HAKEEM: 28th Street will be tag team champions of the VWF and we gonna do it at the expense of the NYCH! TYSON: Delicious Don.....HA......Terry Queenz.....HA! HAKEEM: Sounds like drag queenz! TYSON: Bet dis, this week and at the Pay per view we are gonna drag da ass all over the ring! HAKEEM: Good to hear it, I'm getting worried about you and all the time with the ladies... TYSON: Man can't live on championships alone! (fade)
