VWF War of Words, 13 May 2002

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF War of Words for May 13 In this issue; THE REAL DEAL talks about coming back and Thomas Rainka THE MAQUILLADORA talks about CJB and movies. G-GANGSTA has some words for champ Zell Strife and Randall Crane CLASSY JOEY BOY wants Sebastian, simple as that! TYSON YOUNG ********** THE REAL DEAL (The scene opens in an urban invironment. The Real Deal is sitting on the ground leaning back on a chain-liked fence. Some teen-agers are playing basketball behind him. He looks really depressed. He's wearing slightly ripped baggy jeans, and a Kurt Cobain t-shirt. He wipes the sweat from his forhead and pulls his hair out of hia face. He looks over at the camera and sighs heavily. He looks away.) The Real Deal: What the f*** do you want. Camera Man: We're gonna have to edit that. The Real Deal: F*** if I care. Nobody appreciates my talent anyway. I get stuck in a f***in' T3 match that is really more like a handicap. I never get a real shot at the title, which is my right as the last defending champ. Then I get stuck in a match with Thomas Rainka, whose name I can't even pronounce correctly. The fact is, I'll always be an underated should-be champion who's stuck in matches with losers he's never even heard of. Now what the hell is it you want? Camera man: I'm getting it. The Real Deal: What? Camera man: What I want. (The Real Deal stairs off into the distance.) The Real Deal: Yeah, you and everybody else except me. When do I get recognized. When do I get what I want. When will I get what I deserve? Camera man: When you earn it. (The Real Deal glares at him.) The Real Deal: F*** you fat-a**! I'm sick and tired of being walked all over and exploited! It's pissing me off! Everybody can just die for all I care! And get that f***in' camera outta my face! (There's a moment of awkward silence.) The Real Deal: I said turn it off!!! (TRD dives at the camera. We see a quick shot of a cloudy sky. We can hear the camera man scream "oh sh** before it goe black.) ---------- THE MAQUILLADORA (The Maquilladora and Yakabi are hanging out outside a broadway show.) Maq- I could do Broadway. Yak- Except for the fact that your a wrestler and your insane. ON second thought the insane part will fit right in with those other freaks on broadway. Maq- They are artists damnit. And so am I. Yak- You don't do drugs. Maq- What does that have to do with anything. Yak- You claimed to be an artist. Maq- So, whats your point. Yak- There is a strong correlation between artists and drug use. Maq- No there isn't. Yak- Yes there is. Jimmy Hendrix, Jim Morrison, any of the artists of the Renaissance, I'm sure that Stanley Kubrick guy was on something. Maq- Yeah the Clockwork Orange was f-ed up. Yak- Its not as bad as The Cell with J-Lo though. Maq- J-Lo is hot. Yak- Speaking of f-ed up. What about CJB? Maq- WHat about him? Yak- He cost you the match and more importantly the title. Maq- Oh yeah, and now I have to tag with him. I should see if he can use his connections to get me a gig on broadway. Yak- Aren't you pissed? Maq- No not really. I saw the tape. He didn't mean to do it. My tag partner and I have the tag belts don't we? Yak- True. Maq- Why isn't it listed as a handicapped match though...its three on two. Maybe they made a typo in the front office. Yak- Yeah, maybe they will learn to pronounce your name too. Maq- Shut up, Yakachebacca! Yak- Thats your worst one yet. Maq- Sorry, Episode II is coming out. Yak- Oh boy. Not again. Maq- Hey lets go mess with the people in line. (Star Wars theme plays as the camera fades) ---------- G-GANGSTA [The scene opens to club Cheetah in New York, G-Gangsta is on the dance floor dancing with a Latino girl, the song ends and the lights come on as G-Gangsta walks outside. He is wearing baggy black jeans and a "He is Coming....VWF" T-Shirt. He walks up to the camera and speaks.] G-Gangsta: Just when you thought G-Gangsta was a has been, a short success story...the former everything Champion...the VWF Standard Setter...has come back to the top. And this Thursday on Thunder, I get to make my long anticapated return to my Main Event Status. Zell Strife I will talk about you later...but Randal Crane I have something to get off my chest. Last Thursday night...I beat you...in a match that had the fans on the edge of their seats the whole time. I don't say this often but thanks for a good match. I have had a few good matches in my VWF career...with guys like Jack Spade, Zell Strife, Tai Angela, Sebastien and now you Randal Crane. You want a rematch? Just ask you will get one. WE WILL MEET AGAIN...and as you said the results will be different I WILL BEAT YOU ALL OVER THE RING...but untill that time that we shall meet...I got your back...you got mine? I want you in a match at 100% Crane I will make sure you get there. Now Zell Strife, I know you have wanted me in that ring ever since we last met. HERE IS YOUR CHANCE! You set a few standards around here yourself. You have had the single longest 24 Hour Title reign in VWF history so far. This Thursday Night the clash of the titans. Lets make it interesting...WWF calls this Hell In The Cell...we call it Rage In A Cage...but the top of the mesh cage its lined with barbedwire along the sides. ARE YOU MAN ENOUGH ZELL? Belt on the line...no holds barred...don't hold back...I SURELY WON'T TAKE THREE DAYS AND THINK ABOUT IT ZELL...THINK LONG AND THINK HARD...BECAUSE WHEN THAT CAGE IS LOCKED...THE VICIOUS SIDE OF ME WILL COME OUT...AND OH YEAH...AND MAYBE NOT TODAY...MAYBE NOT TOMORROW...BUT ONE DAY SOON...HE IS COMING! Peace. [He walks back into the club...the scene fades to black.] ---------- CLASSY JOEY BOY (The scene opens in the back of the arena last Thursday after Thunder. In the back, near the exit to the VIP parking garage, Classy paces back and forth on the cold cement with his hands on his head. He is still in his gear from the nights event. Sweat covers his tan, defined body. He has a look of disbelief on his face. He paces a few more times, then bends over and screams at the top of his lungs.) CJB: SEBASTIAN!!!!!!!!!!! (He begins to pace again) CJB: This was my chance! (He kicks an equipment box) I can't believe this crap! God damn it! When I get my hands on Sabastian I swear to god all mighty I am going rip is head clean off his body. It will be his last match......ever! (Fatty walks up with a bottle of water and a towel. CJB is still pacing back and forth.) Fatty: I brought you some water my friend. (Classy grabs the water, opens the bottle, and dumps it over his head and pours some into his mouth. CJB looks at Fatty while he paces.) CJB: Is Rob Beauty still here? Fatty: No man, he took off right after the show ended. CJB: Damn! Fatty: But you can catch him before next Thunder. CJB: Good, because I've got a match for him......(He stops pacing and gets into Fatty's face in a very serious manner.)..Sebastian wants Strife, and I want Sebastian, so I say Strife and me vs. Sebastian......two on one....if Strife and I win, I get Sebadtian at Paint the Town Red, if we lose, Sebastian gets a shot at the 24 Hour title at the PPV. (CJB walks away mad. Fatt turns and just watches him. He then mumbles to himself) Fatty: That sounds like a hell of an idea. ------------ TYSON YOUNG (T-Bone is sitting at the same North Las Vegas bar as always but he is alone since he ran off Hardcore Hakeem) Ladies and gentlemen of the VWF you must prepare yourselves, it is a new time in Las Vegas, it is the time for me. Tyson Young....Tyson T-Bone Young! And it all starts with that punk Erik Andrews. Andrews is a former no one who is quickly heading toward.... Who? And after Andrews it will be a title. But I have my eyes on one belt, the T3 Title! Randall Crane has been like a monster but he has never had to step into the ring with anyone like me. Andrews is first then it is on to the T3 Title,... Can you Erik Andrews, will you Erik Andrews...dare to walk down THE STREET! (ftb)
