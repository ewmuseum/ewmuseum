VWF War of Words, 8 May 2002

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WAR OF WORDS OOC- Sorry this is late, you know, finals etc... Also, public appology to JT (Maqs handler) for messing up parts of his great RP on last weeks card. Anyway, enjoy. THE MAQUILLADORA (The Maquilladora and Yakabi are sitting at a metal table in a room with concrete walls. There is a TV in the back of the room playing the last promo where Sonicman steals the walker from the old man. In the far left hand corner there is caution tape from CAPSMAN's entry into the room several weeks back. The tape ends and the camera pans to Denis Franz.) Denis- So let me get this straight. You want me to arrest the old man for racial abuse and...what else... Maq- Destruction of property...he destroyed my ride. Denis- All right, and you want me to arrest Sonicman for theft and attempted assault. Maq- Yep. Denis- Ok, give me the tape and I will go get a warrant for their arrests. Maq- Cool, we'll try to find Sonicman if you can get that old fart. Denis- The old fart and two warrants...you got it. (They all leave and the camera cuts to Maq and Yak on the streets trying to find CAPSMAN.) Maq- CAPSMAN!!!!!!!!!!!! Yak- CAPSMAN! Here boy! Maq- Dude, he's not a dog. Yak- Oh c'mon...he's not fully human either. Maq- No disagreement here. Where the hell is that boy? Yak- Maybe we should try the CAPSSIGNAL. Maq- Very funny. Yak- The CAPSWHISTLE? Maq- Damnit knock that crap off, this is serious. Yak- Since when have you ever been serious? Maq- Since well, damnit. Yak- Man, we'll never find this guy. (The BATMAN theme begins to play.) Maq- Never thought I'd welcome that sound. CAPS- GREETINGS! Yak- Yeah yeah, he we need your help. CAPS- WORLD'S FOREMOST PROTECTOR OF JUSTICE AT YOUR SERVICE. HOW MAY I HELP YOU? THANK YOU DRIVE THROUGH. Maq- ... Yak- ... Maq- We need you to find your bro Sonicman and take him to the police station. CAPS- SURE THING! BUT WHY? Maq- His theft of that old dude's walker. CAPS- AH, I SEE. I SHALL RETRIEVE HIM AT ONCE. (Batman theme plays again as the camera cuts back to the station.) Denis- Ok I got the old man. He is being checked in as we speak. Where's Sonicman? Maq- CAPSMAN is getting him. Denis- He isn't going to do some interior decorating like he did last time is he? You did introduce him to the concept of doors didn't you? Maq- Yeah we did. Did you get the info on him. Denis- Yeah. (cue Batman music. CAPSMAN comes crashing through the wall with Sonicman.) Denis- Damnit, you freak...ever heard of a door?! CAPS- GREETINGS FELLOW JUSTICE PROTECTOR AGAINST EVIL. DOORS ARE FOR THE WEAK. Maq- How the hell did you do that?! Thats solid concrete! CAPS- I CAN NOT REVEAL THE SOURCE OF MY POWERS TO ANYONE. Maq- Ok. Sonic- Damnit, not you guys again. Denis- Settle down there felon. You are under arrest for theft and assault with an un-deadly weapon. Sonic- What? Maq- Yeah, who steals canes from old racist white dudes anyway, bastard. Sonic- Thats verbal abuse! Denis- Actually its not. You are a bastard. Sonic- WHAT! CAPS- HEY GET YOUR OWN DAMN GIMMICK, BASTARD. Sonic- Stop calling me that. If I'm one, then your one too. Denis- Actually, no. Sonic- But we're triplets! Denis- True but your the only one the mom didn't want. Sonic- Aren't I the oldest. Denis- No, actually you are the longest by a long shot. Sonic- How much? Denis- 12 days 14 hours 37 minutes and 56 seconds. Thats why your brothers are dumber but decent human beings and you are smart but a human cespool. Maq- Ouch. Yak- The ref takes a point away! CAPS- REF? WHERE? Denis- Enough chit-chat. Lets take care of business. Maq- Yes indeed. CAPS- INDEEEEEEEED. Yak- Shut up CAPSBOY. CAPS- SORRY. (The camera fades as the Law and Order music plays.) ------------------------------- CLASSY JOEY BOY (The camera is zoomed in on the Blackjack title. A reflection of a man fixing his hair can be seen on the main plate of the belt. As the camera zooms out, CJB can be seen using the Blackjack title belt as a mirror to help fix his blondish hair. He also checks the rest of his face for any inappropriate markings. A big smile crosses his face as he begins to speak.) CJB: You know Fatty.....as the VWF Team Captain I am going to do something no one has done in the VWF. This Thursday I will become the new T3 Champion, and...Fatty.....I will become the first double singles champion! (Classy turns to face Fatty who is eating m&m's on a couch in there hotel suit.) CJB: You see Fatts in my first match the fans of the VWF will get more than there money's worth as I finally get to crush Sebastian. And as for Randell Crane....he may be in the match....but he is not a threat...... (Just then the door swings open and in walks the 4'1" Grandma Joey Boy) CJB: What in the hell? (Fatty hops off the couch and stands next to CJB.) Fatty: Holy Johnny Kasperitous, CJB......it's it's...Grandma Joey Boy! (GJB walks in waving her cane in the air) GJB: That's right you two #@*%* #@*%*......I'm back to kick both your #@*%*'s....it's time you step up to the next #@*%* level, so #@*%* Sebastian, #@*%* Randall Crane, and #@*%* you both! (Both CJB and Fatty are shocked at the words coming from GJB's mouth.) CJB: Grandma you have a potty mouth! Fatty: Someone needs to get there mouth washed out with soap. GJB: Shut the #@*%* up! Classy now is your time to make something of yourself damn it! We must train quick and plan fast. You have a tough Thursday night ahead of yourself. Now, let's get the #@*%* out of this crap hole. First we must think of Sebastian and Crane, we must train for them non-stop from now until Thursday. Crane and Sebastian will go down Thursday, along with anyone else who stand sin the way of our T3 title! Now, get your #@*%* together so we can blow this #@*%* stand. CJB: You mean pop.... (GJB cuts him off) GJB: I meant #@*%* stand! Fatty:(Talking to CJB) Dude, she meant #@*%* stand. CJB: Hey! Watch your mouth! Both of you! (GJB smacks CJB in the head with her cane) GJB: Get your #@*%*....and hurry! (The scene fades as GJB leaves the room. CJB and Fatty Jay scramble to get there things together. They can be heard talking softly) CJB: This is your fault. Fatty: Me? CJB: You left the door unlocked. Fatty: No you did......dork. CJB: Fat ass! ------------------------------------ REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a shot of the outside of a hospital. Various people are seen walking, conversing, and being brought in via stretcher. The shot switches to another part of the hospital, still outside in what looks to be some sort of lobby or courtyard. The sun is shining, and various patients are walking around, enjoying the Las Vegas weather. The shot then cuts to a bench, where seated in a secluded area is none other than "The Revolution" Randall Crane. Crane looks to be in deep thought, staring into the sky. Sensing the camera's presence, he turns and begins to speak.] RC: I'm bruised, I'm sore, and I was told in so many words that I should take some time off. "It hasn't been long enough," they tell me. They recommend me some pills, they tell me to rest, their words have begun to sound repetitive. I'm told the same thing on a daily basis. You think I'm going to listen? That I'm going to stay here, drugged up on morphine? I'm better than that, I've _never_ backed away from a challenge, and I don't have plans to start now. So I lost my return match, which was dubbed a "Number One Contender" match. You think that's going to break _my_ spirit? Think again. [Crane shifts in position.] RC: I guess congratulations are in order to G-Gangsta, the new contender to the 24 Hour Title. I had you beat square in the middle of the ring, and I decided to bite off more than I could chew. I guess I was trying to make a statement that I'm back and even better. That ended up backfiring in my face. Don't count me out, though. If you end up winning the 24 Hour Title, we _will_ meet again. I don't care if I'm in the same condition, or in perfect health... the results _will_ be different. I know I can beat you, G-Gangsta. It's just a matter of when I can face you again. [Crane stands up, wincing in pain as he does so.] RC: There's no sense in dwelling on past failures, so we'll switch gears. After last week, it looks like the T-3 Title is vacant. The title that was as good as mine before Tony Cage decided to screw with my brake lines... and now I get another chance to bring it home. Nine men... one winner. Who's willing to go through hell and back? Who's willing to go out there and give 100% and then some? I might be ailing from injuries, but there's no way in _hell_ that anyone else wants it more than me. This was the one thing I set my sights on, and the one thing I will win. The only question left to ask is... who can stop me? [Crane looks to the camera with complete confidence.] RC: In the first round, I have to deal with Sebastian and Classy Joey Boy. The two men have more of an issue with each other than they do with winning another title. If I'm lucky, the two will simply beat the hell out of each other, leaving me to score an easy victory. Hell, if they come and try to take me down first, I'll make sure I show them the error of their ways. It doesn't matter if it's one man or an army of men, the revolution will _always_ overcome. So no matter if you're "too good" for a title or looking for revenge... I will advance to the finals. Once there, it doesn't matter who my opponents are. It doesn't matter if I have doctors trying to restrain me from ringside. It doesn't matter if I have to be wheeled down to the ring. I will suck up every ounce of energy in my body, and I will win the T-3 Title. [A smirk purses Crane's lips.] RC: Once the dust settles and my arm is raised in the air, I will once again restake my claim as the best in the VWF. [Crane raises a fist into the air.] RC: So let the threats begin. I hope my other opponents talk all the big game they want. Because once that bell rings, the talk will cease and the revolution will begin. Enjoy the remaining thoughts of becoming the next champion... Because in a few short days, they will be _terminated_. [The scene slowly fades to black.] --------------------------- THE GREAT AND POWERFUL SEBASTIAN (The Great and Powerful Sebastian is sitting at a bar inside the Hard Rock Hotel drinking with a large group of ladies laughing as he tells some jokes, Sebastian sees the camera) VWF? Why would the Great and Powerful Sebastian want to talk to you? To talk about another match that the Great and Powerful Sebastian does not want? Sebastian will only step into the ring against the man holding the 24 Hour Title! And in this case it is Zell Strife! Sebastian is the first SUPERSTAR of the VWF! Who did Sebastian defeat to win the title the first time? Zell Strife! And the list of men that Sebastian has run out of the VWF is so long it can no longer be mentioned! *laughs* Sebastian hopes Classy Joey Boy and Randall Crane kill each other for the lower title, for the T3 title, either man deserves it. But neither......NEITHER man deserves to hold the 24 Hour Title! Zell Strife if you get past G-Gangsta you can count on seeing the GREAT AND POWERFUL SEBASTIAN in your future! And G-Gangsta, if you defeat Strife you and Sebastian will finally get a chance to dance. Now go away! (ftb)
