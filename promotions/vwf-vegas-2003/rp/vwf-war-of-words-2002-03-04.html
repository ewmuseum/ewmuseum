VWF War of Words, 4 March 2002

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
War of Words March 4, 2002 ------------------------- TAI ANGELA with BLACKBIRD (Smoke rises from the earth, the smell of death is in the air.. Tai Angela is lying on the ground, in the mud..the cold air lifts off him and Blackbird walks over) BLACKBIRD: Classy Joey Boy do you think we came back to take your title? Do you think we came back to defeat you? Silly, silly Boy. Tai Angela is going to destroy you! And the people who run the VWF know this, that is why they have scheduled a match between Angela and Cantor. Blade Cantor is a no body, a worthless piece of nothing in the grand scheme. And this is something Blade knows, deep in his little heart. So this week....Ceee Jaaa Beee....an example shall be made... of Blade Cantor. Watch this match with all you have Classy Boy. Watch the future that is waiting for you at the hands of the Dark Monster. (Angela rises up out of the mud) Classy Joey Boy, The Dark Monster is going to take out the heart of Blade Cantor......and then......take out yours! (Angela reaches out with his hand and simulates removing the heart from the chest of Cantor and or CJB) ---------- SEBASTIAN (Sebastian is sitting at a black jack table inside the MGM Hotel, and he is smiling) SEBASTIAN: Why? Why is Sebastian smiling? Why is Sebastian sitting at a black jack table? Because this is Sebastians lucky week! Sebastian finally has a shot at the title that is Sebastians title. G-Gangsta, (mocking) punk yo! Time has come, shine up the belt, make sure you get all the junk off it, your slime. Sebastian is about to become three time 24 Hour Champion! This is Sebastians lucky week, which is why Sebastian is here at the tables. (Dealer deals out a hand) DEALER: Sir....sir....hit or stay? SEBASTIAN: Sir!? Sebastian! Ummmmm hit. DEALER: Thats a bust, 24 sir! SEBASTIAN: (jumps to his feet) Damn it! Damn it! Sebastian should tear you .... !!!! (calms down) Twenty four huh? Sebastian loses with a 24! Well... G-Gangsta...do you think this is a sign? Sebastian does! This Thursday it is time for G-Gangsta to go the way of the Powells, Fullers, Beautys, and Spades....... All thanks to Sebastian! (...) Now deal again!! (fade) --------- THE REAL DEAL letters appear on-screen.) *Do You Have Any Clue... *Do You Have Any Idea... *Can You Begin To Comprehend... *The Ease With Wich I Won My Last Match? *What Kinda Competition Was That? (Th scene re-opens in a well lit room, a gym, apparently. We see The Real Deal. He's wearing blue jeans with a black muscle shirt that reads "The Real Deal" in bold, blue letters. He's standing next to a life-size cardboard stand-up of Blade Cantor. He's smiling into the camera, obviously in good spirits. He begins to speak.) The Real Deal: You know, Blade Cantor, I shoulda just fought this cardboard standup. It would have been more trouble to push it over and climb on top than it was to put your sorry ass down for the 1-2-3. At least inertia would have slowed the rate at wich the cardboards fell. You were more or less stumbling all over yourself. I think I even caught you drooling at one point. (He tightens the muscles in his face.) The Real Deal: Disgusting! Oh well. That'd what they get for putting me in a match with a loser like you. And the opening match, none the less! How the hell did I end up in the opening match-up any way?! I belong near the top of the card, and instead I'm stuck down here in the bowels of mediocrety with guys like Cantor... (With that, he pushes the cardboard stand-up over.) The Real Deal: And this jobber over here... (The camera rotates a few degrees to the right to reveal a life-size stand up of "Absolute Arrogance" Erik Andrews. However, the camera zooms out a bit to reveal that Andrew's cardboard head is sitting on top of a cardboard cut out of a little girls dress. The Real Deal smiles.) The Real Deal: This Arrogant Andrew dude, or whatever the hell his name is. I don't know. (The Real Deal stops to think a bout it. He rubs his goatee, looking up to the ceiling. The Real Deal: What the hell is hi name?... (He snaps back to reality.) The Real Deal: Oh well, it doesn't matter all that much. The fact is, he doesn't belong in the same ring as yours truly. He belongs outside in the gutter, with some little rat freinds and a hobo to keep him company. But for some reason, a reason far beyond me, he's been givin' the oppertunity of a life-time. The oppertunity to fight the most unnapreciated, underated, and over-all best wrestler that has ever set foot on this miserable little planet. In a weird sorta way, he should feel really lucky. But then there's that part where I have to kick his ass, and all that other stuff. I tell you what, uhhhh, what ever your name is. I won't hold you responsible if you don't show up. If I had your physical limitations, and I was supposed to wrestle me, I sure wouldn't show up. (He puches the cardboard stand-up over. The camera zooms in on him.) The Real Deal: But in case you show up, I should give you fair warning. Here's what i'm gonna do. I'm gonna take this hand... (He lifts hi right hand.) The Real Deal: And ever so carefully RIP YOUR LUNGS OUT' YOUR ASS AND FEED 'EM BACK TO YOU. (He laughs.) The Real Deal: So put that in your pants and play with it. (Fade to black.) ---------- NEW YORK CITY HEADLINERS (the scene opens at a CWF house show. Whoa by Black Rob blares through the arena.) (New York City Headliners comeout flankedby themanager Barry Bronxs. They make there way down ailse and the enter the ring.) TQ: Ay yo, let us say one thing. It aint over between us and the 28th street...They are gonna be in our paths again and the next time they wont be as fortuante.0In fact we would challenge themright now, but me and Brooklynn know that 28th street are two suckas who wouldnt show up. Right now we here to deal with the present. DB: Thats right the CJB and his girlfriend will be in a match with us this Thursday, problem and no hassle. We will takeout CJB and his buddy for the 1...2...3 (scene fades) ---------- CLASSY JOEY BOY and FATTY JAY (The camera fades into a home video. CJB's face stares down the camera.) CJB: It's been about four days Grandma and I started training Fatty. He has never been in the ring in a wrestling capasity before. We wanted him to lose a few pounds while he learned the basics. The one move he seems to do well is, well...the Steamroller. Who knows if Grandma will be okay. (From behind CJB you hear Fatty's voice) Fatty: Hey! What are you doing? (Classy backs away from the camera and faces his sidekick.) CJB: Documenting your training. Fatty: Oh! It's going well! 2 pounds heavier! CJB: What?! God help us against the NYC Headliners! Fatty: I can handle myself! CJB: I think I'll be doing most of the work this Thursday! ------- ZELL STRIFE (Zell walks to the arena and heads to his dressing room, he locates his remote...he turns on his stereo and falls onto his seat....Incorporeal by Tiger Army starts to blast on his speakers..... Zell moves his neck back and forth, in relief, in pain, Zell goes for the deep cold therapy rub.... He rubs it on his neck and thinks to himself.....It's been a while since he has been in the ring. His own performance.... half-assed, could have been better, but the crowd was pleased. I gave him the Porphyria twice, i'm not even sure, well i guess I could have brought his ass into the ring..... Maybe pinned him.... Erik Andrews really didn't even put up too much of a fight.....what's wrong with the fed......or am I back?......A knock at the door snaps Zell out of his thoughts, he rises and gets the door.) Jared) It's me, long time no see, time for a few words? Zell) Well if it isn't my little cum rag.... alright chochy make it quick..... Jared) Alright, so you were in jail for well a long time and you just couldn't get the break... is this turing over a new leaf, rock, whatever you want? Zell) First of all, it's just getting ambitious, I never turned anything over, the only thing I will ever turn over, are bodies that need to be pinned....secondly, I thought you learned not to be such a fucking snooty little bitch.... when you ask me these questions, at least have half a brain about human nature.....People have slumps, people cannot always believe in themselves..... that's just what happened with me in the jail situation.... I wanted to hurt Rainka so bad that I would take it beyond wrestling and even try to end his career...... the love goes on between us you know.... Jared) The love? Zell) Are you a moron or something, sarcasm is speech.... Jared) Very sorry.....alright, plans mr. strife? Zell) The same question, but to be honest I look forward to it.... You have heard so many plans that I'm sure the U.S miliatry would want to hire you.....to tell you the truth, the plan is simple, and not really promising.... all I am doing is going into my matches, one at a time.... knowing my man, knowing his every move.... knowing the face.... the name means shit... and conquering them.... I mean hitting the Porphyria once is not enough.... slap on a Super Nova, a couple more dragon suplex's and then the deed will be done..... I want these guys to know who I am..... They have no idea who I am..... I held the belt for the longest here in this fed.... then I handed it to some other dumb fuck...... Who cares, the goal is to get it back and I'm sure that this crandel fag who stands in my way, will be of no concern...... Jared) Well, Zell, not mocking your dominance, Crandel shouldn't be taken too lightly..... (Zell peers into the eyes of Jared..... and then turns away) Zell) How many times have you interviewed me.... Jared) At least a 100, no wait over a 1000, remember the other feds.... Zell) Yes, and not once have you told me to take someone so lightly... you may of asked why I take him lightly......you do not have any more confidence in me?....As a vivid fan who I put down every chance I get, you lack the faith........what the fuck is goin on here? I must say, the fans, do they think the same way? wait, dont answer that.......there is a new game plan here...... That Crandel, you say he's good, but Zell is a veteran, I am better..... I am going to show everyone that I still have what it takes..... there is no quit in this Hellish fire..... there is no die in the undead..... by god I will do it.... When I become champion once again, you will be licking the shit off my feet Jared... you know that... Jared) I do not doubt your title run, I doubt your confidence..... Zell) So your tell me, I don't perform as though I would be seen as one to take the show..... Well that's what I was all about last time.... a psychotic mother fucker who went out there....... and killed the arena.....I destroyed the world yah know.... Jared) NO no no, I'm talking about your suave style and yet the insane evil presence that is felt..... I don't feel it anymore..... Zell) Hahaha, that's because I never really did focus on that part..... I guess I could give you my old side of the story sometime... but let me win the title first..... if you would have been taking notes from all our videos, I am never confident unless I have my goal accomplished......When that happens, you may just see the all-time champion of the VWF back to his old self.... evil, smooth, and yet still bloodthirsty.........we shall see......oh and one more thing...........I AM THE ANARCHIST, THE FIRE INSIDE, THE HAUNTING, ZELL STRIFE..... and if you don't believe, then the sacrifice you shall be..............Death To All, leave my presence.... (As many times, Jared jumped outside the door, not in fright was his face, but with a grin, for the man was back and in full spirit......)
