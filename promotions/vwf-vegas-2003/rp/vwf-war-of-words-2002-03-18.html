VWF War of Words, Pre-Gauntlet, 18 March 2002

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
War of Words Pre Gauntlet March 18, 2002 ------------------------ THE REAL DEAL (The scene opens up with a shot of your favorite wreslter (and mine) drop kicking Absolute Arrogance. The scene cuts to a shot of The Real Deal covering AA for the 1... 2... 3.) (The scene re-opens The Real Deal's private screening room. A projector is showing is displaying a match between Erik Andrews and some other wrestler. Erik Andrews is kicking ass. Sitting in a steal chair to the right of the projector is The Real Deal. He pauses the video as Erik Andrews sets up The Bottom Line. He plays the video and watches it again. He jots down some notes in a little notepad he has on his lap. He stops the projector and the lights flicker on in the room. He approaches his camera and holds up the notebook, careful to hide his notes.) The Real Deal: You see this? I've had this notebook since my first title reign in NGW. It has about two pages of notes for every person I've ever wrestled, from the EWA, to RCW, to the RAWF, and now, the VWF. And Erik, there's even a special section for you. (He smiles.) The Real Deal: Yes sirree, I've got your style, tendencies, stamina estimations, strengths, weeknesses, weight, hight, typical body weight distribution and stances, the list goes on. I know you inside and out. I'm inside your head. I know what your gonna try, what your gonna stay away from, and how you react to various situations. I have a list of every match I could find. I've tallied up how many times you've been pinned, how many times you've submitted, what holds were locked every time you submitted. I know all!!! (He laughs.) The Real Deal: Now let me ask you Erik, what do you know about me? How many of my matches have you cared to study? (chuckle) Hmmm, at Gauntlet, I'm not only gonna rip your lungs out your ass and feed 'em back to you, I'm gonna completely annhilate you. But it won't be a quick match you see, no, no. I'm gonna enjoy myself. I'm go Bret Hart on you! I'm gonna slowly, miticulously pick you apart peice by peice. Like Bret Favre or Joe Montana, I'm gonna look over your defense and decide where I should best start my attack. And eventually, I'm gonna get to every weekness you have, one by one, and completely and totally destroy you. (He sets the notebook down in the steel chair.) The Real Deal: And then, that little battle royal thing, whatever you wanna call it. It's mine. I got film coming for every single person that could possibly be in that match. And beleiv me, I'll watch every hour of it. Because that is the difference between a champ and a chump. A wrestler and a legend. So put that in your pants and play with it. (The scene cuts abrubtly.) --------------------------------------- SONICMAN and CAPSMAN (Dennis Franz is walking around on the streets of NYC. Sonicman is filming another ad as Dennis walks up to him.) Sonicman- Where's Sweatshop boy? Denis- Funny you mention that, you have a tag match against him. Sonic- F-! Whose my partner? Denis- CAPSMAN. Sonic- Son of a b-! That's bullsh-! He's worse than Maq. Denis- Hey, don't kill the messenger. Sonic- When do I wrestle? Denis- Monday. Sonic- F-! Denis- Hey, I gotta get. I gotta go shoot an ad. By the way I don't do commercials. Sonic- Whatever you say. (Denis leaves, CAPSMAN man arrives) CAPS: GREETINGS CITIZEN BROTHER FRIEND AND TAG TEAM PARTNER. Sonic- Ah, damnit. CAPS: WHY THE LONG FACE, ALAS THE SUNSHINE AND FREEDOM OF AMERICA AND THE VWF. Sonic- Huh? CAPS: CHEER UP...WE WILL MEET WITH DESTINY AND CLASS WITH CHAMPIONS THIS MONDAY. Sonic- I knew you were behind it! CAPS: WE WILL FACE THE TAG CHAMPS MONDAY! Sonic- I know we will face Maq. CAPS: AND HIS TAG PARTNER. Sonic: He doesn't have one. Its imgainary. CAPS: NO HE ISN'T. I'VE SEEN HIM MYSELF. HE IS A BRILLIANT MAN. Sonic: Then whats his name CAPSBOY. CAPS: HE DOES NOT LIKE TO HAVE LABELS FIXED TO HIS BEING. Sonic: Whatever. Just get out of here. I'll see ya monday. (CAPSMAN salutes Sonicman, who sighs, shakes his head and walks away as the camera fades.) ------------------ SEBASTIAN (The next, and third time, 24 Hour Champion is looking directly into the camera) SEBASTIAN: One thing and one thing is on the mind of Sebastian. The 24 Hour Title. Tai Angela and The Legend are going to take care of the losers..... And Sebastian will take care of G-Gangsta and Zell Strife. The Fire Inside, the Anarchist your time is over. Like so many others it is time to move on. And Sebastian will make sure you move on. And as for the wanna be gangsta, the loser who beat his friend to get the 24 Hour Title. Demonic is gone, Blaze and the whole RCW are gone. G-Gangsta you have to stand alone...after Sebastian destroys Strife, against (points to self) Sebastian. The Gauntlet is the biggest event of the year....and G-Gangsta the Gauntlet has been thrown down and Sebastian is going to pick it up and beat you down with it! (puts hands to ears) Listen Strife, listen Gangsta....can you hear it? Sebastian can....... Your winner and three time 24 Hour Champion...... SEBASTIAN!!!!!!!!!!!! (fade) ------------------------------------------------------------------ THE MAQUILLADORA (Maq and Yak are in the locker rooms after the 6 man tag match.) Maq- Great victory tonight. Yak- Yeah, your on a roll. Maq- Yes we are. Yak- Next match should be a peice of cake. Maq- Sonicman may be a little tough but CAPSMAN... (Both burst into hysterical laughter.) Maq- Oh boy...can't believe that guy is going to get back in the ring. Didn't he learn his lesson in the CWF. Everyone kicked his ass. Yak- Remember when that old lady jumped him from ringside. That was hilarious. Maq- Don't get me started. We should have an easy match though. Yak- Yeah...wait a second...we? Maq- My tag partner y'know. Yak- Oh sh-, here we go again. Maq- What? Yak- Nevermind, where are the belts by the way. Maq- My tag team partner has them...he should be on his way here any moment now. (A door opens. Maq grins anxiously. Yak turn pale. The belts can now be seen...floating in midair. As if some invisible person has one on each shoulder.) Maq- Here they are. Yak- ....uh.... Maq- See I told ya so. (one of the belts moves and floats to Maq. Yakabi feints.) Maq- Oh crap, help him. (to invisible figure) (Yakabi's jacket moves like someone is touching it. The camera suddenly hits the floor. It goes black for a second and then comes back although it is really fuzzy. We can see the cameraman lying unconscious and face down on the floor. The camera cuts as it becomes really fuzzy.) ----------------------------- CLASSY JOEY BOY (Backstage at the Gauntlet PPV, CJB is in his dressing room, going through his gym bag and getting his gear ready. In just a few short hours he will be defending his BlackJack Title against Tia Angela. The BlackJack title belt is drapped over a chair. It's nicely shinned and is looking real good. CJB, takes a few breaths deep of air. He pulls a small cd player out of his gym bag. Fatty Jay walks in with a gym bag over hsi shoulder. He sets it down and pulls out a black box. Fatty has a serious look on his face. CJB looks at Fatty. Fatty glances at CJB with a bit of a mean look.) CJB: What are you doing? Fatty: Warmin' up. CJB: For? Fatty: My match. CJB: I see...you do know you don't have a wrestling license? Fatty: Yep, but my match isn't going to be sanctioned. CJB: What?! Fatty: Classy, I'm fighting The Legend tonight! CJB: You can't! Last time you needed an oxygen tank for three days. Fatty: I know but I have brought an old friend. (He holds up the black box. Fatty then pulls a key out of his pocket and unlocks the box. He pulls out a rectangular object wrapped in a black cloth.) CJB: Easy with that Fatty, I have lost many a matches due to that thing. Fatty: Actually, those were all my fault. But tonight CJB, I post my first win in the wrestling business. CJB: Umm...Fatty...this will only be your second match. Fatty: Yeah I know, but tonight I'm beat The Legend to the ground in a Street Fight. No rules! Tonight, Legend, you will get tap out when I steamroll you! If your puttin' down what Fatts is.....no wait....I have to be the one putting down.... (CJB shakes his head) CJB: Relax big man, leave the promo's to me, and after tonight leave the wrestling to me too. Because tonight I beat Angela, one on one. You know they call him the Dark Monster, but in reality he is mearly the Dark Pokemonster. He may be tall, and strong, but it takes more than that to be the Team Captain! If Angela is picking up what I'm putting down! Fatty: That's how it goes! (The scene fades) --------------------- 28TH STREET (Hakeem and Tyson Young are walking around New York City) HAKEEM: Big city... TYSON: And where are the New York City Headliners? Queenz and Brooklynn... (They walk up to a man on the street who is clearly afraid of them) HAKEEM: We are looking for Queenz and Brooklynn... MAN: W-w-w-w-well you guys need to take the A-Train.... (Later that day, Hakeem and Tyson are on a subway) HAKEEM: I think that guy dont know what he was talking about. TYSON: (to a woman sitting across from them) Hey! LADY: What? TYSON: We are looking for Queenz and Brooklynn... LADY: Which one? TYSON: Both of them. LADY: Where do you want to go first? HAKEEM: Huh? LADY: Do you want to go to Queens or Brooklyn? TYSON: (to Hakeem) She is crazy! HAKEEM: Lady, we want both of them...... LADY: (confused look) You guys know they are places right? TYSON: What? LADY: Queens and Brooklyn are places, parts of the city. HAKEEM: Have you ever heard of the New York City Headliners.... LADY: No. TYSON: Wrestlers? LADY: No. HAKEEM: Terry Queenz and Delicious Don Brooklynn? LADY: Never heard of them. TYSON: Are we in New York? LADY: Yeah. HAKEEM: Some headliners...... (fade)
