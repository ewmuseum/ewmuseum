VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Roleplay

VWF War of Words, 28 January 2002

28 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
War of Words Pre Ghost Town Blues Pay Per View DAN THE MAN ALLER: (standing inside the silver mines where the 24 Hour Title match will be decided, it is semi lit with cobwebs and old wooden boards all over the place) In less than 48 hours Demonic and the NEW 24 Hour Champion Dage will be led into this abandoned silver mine blindfolded, after they are both inside the blindfolds will be taken off. And they will fight for the biggest title in the VWF, the 24 Hour Title. Who will walk out of these mines champion? Who will survive? Watch Wednesday night, as the VWF presents Ghost Town Blues. See G-Gangsta and Jack Spade get in the ring together and then go into town for Midtown Madness. See Zell Strife and Thomas Rainka, Erik Andrews and Tony Cage all battle for the T3 Title. Classy Joey Boy in a No Holds Barred match with Tai Angela. See all the stars of the VWF! Lets hear from some of them now! SEBASTIAN (taking on The Maquilladora) --------------------------------------- (Sebastian is in close up, the veins popping on his head and neck) SEBSASTIAN: Sebastian is not happy! Sebastian is a TWO TIME 24 HOUR CHAMPION! Zell Strife cannot say that! Jack Spade cannot say that! G-Gangsta cant even say he won it ONCE! And two freaks battle for it on Wednesday! Yet all these men are in bigger matches than Sebastian at Ghost Town Blues. And where is the greatest wrestler in VWF history? In an opening match with The Maquilladora!?!?!?!?! Maq, you will be the example that Sebastian will make to get back to the top of this federation! And after Ghost Town Blues, after Maq taps out to Sex You Out. Then Sebastian will rise to the top of the VWF AGAIN! And no matter what poor bastard gets in Sebastian way that poor bastard will lose! Strife, Spade, Gangsta, Demonic, Dage.....it doesn't matter! Sebastian is THE man and after Ghost Town Blues Maquilladora shall know!! (fade) XTREME TONY CAGE (going for the T3 Title) ------------------------------- (Scene opens on our Italian hardcore man, XTC, sitting in the locker room with a beautiful Asian woman giving him a massage. Tony has minor cuts and bruises on his upper body, but most notable is the cut on his forehead, still fresh and seemed up with stitches. Tony gets rid of the girl and looks into the camera) XTC: Erik, Erik, Erik. Now Thursday, I left the hospital in worse shape then when I got there. When I showed up at home, my mother was flipping out. She tried to ground me, but I said you did. Well, she's calling your mommy, and man are you in trouble now. Now Erik, also on Thursday you called me an Alcoholic, with an above-average build?? Do you ever know who the hell I am? Son, I'm not even old enough to be on grandpa's old cough medicine, and as far as being built, I couldn't hit 200lbs with a brick in my pocket, soaking wet. Son, you better be more prepared come Sunday, look around, and learn better. Of all the words from Thursday, "Stop crying it's just a needle", "Is my tony bear ok?", there's one thing I remember the most, one that stands out. It was you Erik, yelling something you'll soon regret. Erik, do you remember the words "Who's the champ?" coming from your mouth. Well Erik, you are the champ, but that's going to put you at a HUGE disadvantage. You see Erik, you're in there with three other guys who have nothing to lose. You might as well wear a bullseye on that scrawny chest, because, you see, as much as Rainka and Strife hate eachother, I'm sure both of them would love to walk out with gold on their waist just as much as I would. 3 men gunning for you Erik. Can you handle it? Can you take the pressure? (All of a sudden a large man, around 6'8", and wearing a whife beater and white pants. His broad chest and and toned arms make it believable that he's over the 350 lb mark. He walks up behind Tony.) XTC: Erik, I know what's going through your mind now, and others, who is this man? What is this beast that stands behind Tony? Well, let me tell you a short story. See, growing up in the city, Tony was a little boy, had little friends, and noone liked him. He got called the smelly kid in class, the dork, the guy who arrogant pukes like you wouldn't piss on if he was on fire. Well, only one could talked to Tony, the kid who was called slow, or retarded, or a handicapped freak. One day on the walk home, some boys knock Tony down and took his gameboy. Well, the big man had enough. As Tony tried to collect his things, the big man went to action, demolishing them, starting an onslaught. In the end, let's just say Tony liked what he saw, and my friend here liked what he did. Erik, as far as Ghost Town goes, I'm bringing The Insomniac with me, and I'm bringing all those nightmares of people like you destroying my life. When all is said and done, I may not be the T3 champ, but I'll be damned if you are!!! Insomniac: Sleep....is for.....the weak!! (Scene fades) CLASSY JOEY BOY (BLACKJACK CHAMPION defending against Tai Angela) ------------------------------------------------------------------- (Classy Joey Boy is at the outside area where the set for Ghost Town Blues has been put together. The seats are empty. In the ring stands Classy Joey Boy. He has the Blackjack title over his shoulder. Fatty Jay is filming with a hand held camera.) CJB: As the "VWF Team Captain", I feel it is my duty to tell Tia that all the talk is done with. No more time for your "b.s.", bring everything you've got.....the local hookers you have during your entrance, bring tweety bird, bring Sebastian Bach, but most important, bring every you've got! Because Wednesday at Ghost Town Blues, you will find out that size doesn't matter! I will extinguish your flamers!.........I mean your flames.............. (CJB exits the ring) CJB: Tia, the only place to win is outside the ring. Maybe, in an old bar, in an old bathroom, maybe in an old basement. You see it doesn't matter where I beat you, because I will win. Then, you can take Sebastian, tweety, and the hookers back to to the street corner. Ghost Town Blues, yeah...you'll be leaving with the blue! THE MAQUILLADORA (in a match with former 24 Hour Champ Sebastian) ----------------- The camera opens in a room "downtown". The walls are made of concrete, there is a mirror in the background and a metal table in the center. Sonicman is sitting at the table while Maq and Yak shine a light in his face. Yak- All right Mary, we know what you did? Sonicman and the Maquilladora in unision- Mary? Maq- Damnit Yakabi...stop watching those old movies? Your not Denis Franz or James Dean or anything. Yak- But its what all the good detectives say. Maq- Whatever Dick Tracy. Anyway...we know who you are, CAPSMAN. We know what you've been doing. We have people that are willing to testify. We could throw the book at ya. (Yak holds up a huge dictionary sized book and thumbs through it.) Sonicman- Look, I told you, who the hell CAPSMAN is. Hell, I don't even know who you nutbags are or . Maq- Nutbags? Yak- Our identity is inconsiquential. Maq- Damnit, shut UP Yakabi. Yak- What...you told me to act like the guy in Pulp Fiction...thats what I'm doing. Maq- I'm talking about Phil Lamar...the guy who is behind the door and eventually repaints the inside of a car with his brains. Yak- Did you know he was on MadTV? Maq- Yeah, that show rocked...especially that Raging Rudolph thing. Sonicman- You mean when the combined Goodfellas, Raging Bull, and Rudolph to make a skit that looked like the old Rudolph the red-nosed reindeer Claymation thing. Yak- Man that thing has been on for years...the claymation thing...my parents used to watch it when they were little. Sonicman- Did you know Phil Lamar was on the British tapings of Whose Line is it Anyway? Maq- I was not aware of that, funny show though. Sonicman- So now are ya going to tell me who CAPSMAN is and what he has to do with me. Maq- (sighing)Yak, he just doesn't get it. Yak- What should we do. Maq- Throw the book at him. Yak- You got it cheif. (Yak then throws the book at Sonicman and hits him in the face. This breaks his nose and knocks him to the ground. There is a huge thud as his "dome" bounces of the concrete.) Sonicman- You f-ck's, you broke my f'ing nose! Maq- Broke your "F'ing nose?" What does F'ing mean anyway? Yak- f'ing...how do you spell that...effing? f'ing...what does that mean anyway. Maybe he meant fuc- Maq- (Cutting him off) Watch it there Yakabi? This isn't NYPD Blue, we can't say that shit. (Blood now pours out from the back of Sonicman's head.) Sonicman- You cracked my skull too. (The door opens and Paunch and John from CHiPs poke their heads in.) Paunch- Hey you guys about done? Maq- Weren't you done twenty years ago? John- I was talking about the room not our careers. Maq- You had a career? Paunch- Enough of your lip, are you done or not. Maq- Give us five minutes. Paunch- Thanks! (They close the door.) Maq- Its not like your doing anything anyway. Yak- (clears throat) Maq- Oh yeah. Sonicman- I'm going to sue you bastards! Maq- Lets call in the Heavy Hitter. (Enter Dennis Franz) Maq- Thanks for doing our promo. DF- I don't do commercials. Maq- Thats good this is a promo. DF- I don't do commercials, pal. Maq- Ok, well then...interegate the suspect. DF- Don't tell me what to do. Yak- Yeah you tell him pappy. Maq- Damnit bitch, get me some coffee. (Yak leaves the room. And instantly returns with coffee in hand.) Maq- Damn, that was quick. Yak- Do you think we should leave these two alone. Maq- Yeah good idea...Dennis can handle this...lets go get ready for my next match. (Maq and Yak kick Sonicman a couple of times and then exit the room.) THE REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE (making his debut against Johnny Sanders) ----------------------------- [The scene slowly fades in.] [The camera opens to a shot of inside the Luxor Hotel & Casino. The shot pans around to view the people walking around, sightseeing, and gambling. We zoom in on one particular poker table, where a few men are seated and playing. Two of them are older, the third looks to be in his mid-20s. This man wears a short sleeve button down collared shirt, khaki pants, and brown loafers. He puts a couple of chips down on the table and waits for the dealer to deal the cards. The man looks at his cards as they are dealt, and smirks. He signals to the dealer to stay, the awaits the deal. With a slight laugh he takes the money, showing the blackjack as the dealer busts. The man takes the chips, arises from the table, and flips the dealer one of his chips as thanks for the great dealing. The man then picks up his drink and walks towards the camera, starting to speak.] MAN: Another day, another handful of chips to cash in. I've been getting acquainted with this city for a few weeks now, and already some of these places know me by name. I guess when you show up looking like this, and you spend a large sum of money, you get anything and everything. I don't suppose that's why you came here, though. I suppose you came here to get acquainted with one of VWF's new signees. The man that's going to swoop in and take the spotlight from the "established" talent. The man that's here to "revolutionize" the VWF... The name is Crane... "The Revolution" Randall Crane. [With a nod of his head, Crane continues to speak.] RC: The odds are that some of you know who I am. You might have seen me in NEW jumping off the videotron... or perhaps in the EMWC, winning the World Tag Team Titles. If you haven't seen me... get ready. I'm one of the most sadistic bastards you'll ever meet, hands down. I'm going to assume that many of you are sitting there and smiling, thinking "This guy is out of his mind." After all, I'm just signing with VWF and already I'm making promises to be the best of the best. With a track record like mine, though, I'll just let you all find out. [A slight evilness to Crane's smirk as he walks towards the bar.] RC: It all starts at Ghost Town Blues... when I face Johnny "Sweetness" Sanders. Though I'd _much_ rather be hitting the Revolution's End on one of the champions, I'll take what I can get. Once I hit the VWF on pay-per-view... I can let the _entire_ roster know what's going on. And I hope you all are watching. What I'm going to do to Mr. Sanders is what I'm going to do to _each_ and _every_ one of you, on my way up the ladder. And once I reach the pinnacle, it won't stop there. This is just a mere delay in the revolution tearing through the VWF. Now Mr. Sanders, I'm sure you're wondering why I'm looking past you as if you're just a pebble in the great ocean. Well, to be brutally honest... you are. You're unfortunately just the smallest rung on the ladder, the one that is somewhat sturdy yet never stood on for more than a few seconds. To me, Mr. Sanders, you're just an _example_. [Crane signals for the bartender, then lifts his drink up to get the same thing ordered. As the bartender looks to Crane and nods, he turns back to the camera to continue speaking.] RC: I'm going to beat you from pillar to post, and then some. I'm going to anhilate you and make you wish you never signed your name to the dotted line. I'm going to embarrass you on live television, and show all of the paying customers that you're not worth the broadcast time. You are going to be _punished_. You are going to be _decimated_. And just when you think that it's all over, it'll only be the beginning. When you finally feel the pain of the Revolution's End... when you feel your breath give away and your back goes numb... You'll then know you were just a victim of the revolution. [The bartender slides a glass down, and Crane catches it, chuckling to himself as he takes a sip.] RC: It is with great regret that I welcome myself to the VWF. I just hope you are all as joyed as I am. It's almost time... be prepared. [With that, Crane walks away to another table, and the scene fades.] JOHNNY SWEETNESS SANDERS (debuting against Randall Crane) ------------------------- (Johnny Sanders is standing alone in Ryolite, the site of Ghost Town Blues) JSS: Anyone here? Hello? My name is Johnny Sanders, better known as Sweetness. Because the way I move in the ring is sooooooooooo sweeeeeeeeeeet! Anyway, I am going to get my first win on Wednesday against some guy named Crane. I've heard of him and got much respect. And my win against him will be a great way to start my career here in Vegas. So where are you Randall Crane? Not here yet? Scared of getting in the ring with a real man? When Johnny Sanders gets in the ring with you and locks in my running bulldog or The Sweetner, you will see that the VWF was a bad move for you. And the fans will see Johnny Sanders.... and shout out SOOOOOOOOOO SWEEEEEEEEEEEET!!!!!!!!!! (Sanders walks off among the old building of Ryolite) TAI ANGELA with BLACKBIRD (going for the BlackJack Title) -------------------------- (Tai Angela is standing in a dry lake bed just outside of Las Vegas. He is wearing a black robe and is slowly turning with his arms out to his sides. His valet Blackbird is kneeling in the foreground) BLACKBIRD: Turning, slowly turning. Like a tide, like a great ocean. The waves crash and there is nothing that can be done to stop this. Like a great ocean that slowly takes away the solid ground, Tai Angela will slowly torture Classy Joey Boy. And what can be done C J B? (Angela throws down his arms and a circle of flames flashes up around him. The camera moves in and both Angela and Blackbird are gone and in the desert, in flames it read...) NOTHING CAN BE DONE (fade)
