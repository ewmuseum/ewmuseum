VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Roleplay

VWF War of Words, 27 August 2001

28 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF FLASH SHOW WAR OF WORDS August 27, 2001 --------------- IN THIS ISSUE "The Ace" Jack Spade G-Gangsta Paddy O'Shea Sebastian "The Silence" Bobby Silas Dirk Western --------------- "THE ACE" JACK SPADE (The scene fades into a casino early in the morning before it has opened up. Nobody is in the room except for one man, the owner of the casino and
VWF
new comer - "The Ace" Jack Spade. Jack is sat in a pair of blue jeans and a white vest watching a re-run of Warrior Wednesday. He turns the TV off as his match ends and stands up, walking over towards one of the "Crap
Tables"
and begins to talk.) JS: Warrior Wednesday sure was a shocker wasn't it? The new comer and rookie to the federation comes in and beats two people in a gruelling triple threat match, just like I promised I would. Huey I told you I was going to cut you down just like in Jack and the beanstalk and I did. I beat your sorry ass and won the match to go on in the triple threat title tournament.
(Jack smiles and sits on the crap table.) JS: My next task is yet another triple threat match, this time for the gold - the triple threat title. That's what it's all about here in the VWF, the gold. The triple threat title may not be the biggest title in the
VWF
but nobody has even held it yet. So on Wednesday I'm going to take down both my opponents just like I did last Wednesday and win the triple threat title.
Two weeks is all it takes for me to start my rampage here in the VWF, and on Wednesday, only my second night of action, I will become the triple threat champion. (Jack looks around the room.) JS: G-Gangsta, a man who I've had an encounter with in other places before. Gangsta you won your match by a fluke in my opinion and don't deserve to be in the triple threat title match. But either way, you're in the match and you're in for an ass kicking. You bring your Malibu sipping, weed smoking ass to the ring on Wednesday and be ready to have it kicked big time.
And not forgetting my other opponent, either Bobby Silas or some
Japanese Angel. Well whichever one of you it is better be ready for me. I'm ready for either of you, I'm ready for G-Gangsta and I'm ready to become the first ever triple threat champion on Wednesday.
(The scene fades with these words whilst Leader of Men by Nickelback plays in the background.)
***** G-GANGSTA [My Projects by Coo Coo Cal hits, G-Gangsta an U-Trop walk out. They are wearing Clench suits(Gangstaz is black, UT's is blue) suits an
G-Gangsta is wearing his platnium chain with the "G" pendent. He struts(thug walks) to the ring as U-Trop stays on the ramp. G-Gangsta grabs the mic an speaks.]
G-Gangsta: Yo, wassuh? [The fans give a mixed reaction.] G-Gangsta:For real? Hmmm. The critics was sayin "G you can't do it, you aint good enough, your to ghetto." I always said I would do it. And I proved it on the show earlier.
I beat not one, TWO people. So now its at the PPV I will face none other then Jack Spade. Well Jack, guess what? It's finally your lucky day.
Oh wait no its not. Just like in gambeling, you dont know the outcome sucka! But I do see, I WIN I WIN I WIN! The title. But Jack I don't have any problems with you. Just respect. You are older than I am, thus meaning you've been around longer. Well thats great. But the Ghetto Youths are the ones who will have the money, the gold, the platnium we will have it all. Don't belive me? Look at Allen Iverson, NBA Season MVP, Lead Scorer om the NBA to. He was a ghetto youth. Latrell Spreewell....ghetto youth. So to close this topic.
Watch out, the Gangsta is here, VWF never saw it coming, but here I am. [U-Trop walks down the aisle, G-Gangsta throws him the mic.] U-Trop:So, you know what, G-Gangsta here, he cornrowed, he goateed an he mean an lean. This here is my homie since birth you naw I mean? So I watch his back. Thats how it is. PUNKASS.
G-Gangsta:So anyone who wants to feud wit the best, holla. One Love! [My Projects hits as G-Gangsta an U-Trop walk out.] ***** PADDY O'SHEA (Paddy O'Shea and his new manager Mr. Smith are sitting outside at a coffee shop, O'Shea looks happy, unlike normal)
OSHEA: I am feeling better than ever, tis the best I felt in ages. MR SMITH: With a new clean fresh outlook you will go to the top of the
VWF. And that starts with the unclean Dirk Western.
OSHEA: He seems a sorry and lost soul. MR SMITH: Wednesday it will be time to re-educate Dirk Western, for him to clean up his ways, to become a better man, like you.
OSHEA: And if he resists? MR SMITH: One two! (both laugh) ***** SEBASTIAN (Sebastian is pacing around, very unhappy) SEBASTIAN: Kerry Powell only beat Sebastian because he thought Sebastian was Ric Beauty! If he had known he was in the ring with the glorious, great Sebastian he would have run. Just like he will do
Wednesday night! And he thinks he and SEBASTIANS title will be safe behind the President of the VWF! Who wants a wrestling president?
Kerry Powell is not the best man in the VWF! Sebastian is the best man in the VWF! And with that in mind Sebastian will make sure that on
Wednesday Sebastian takes back Sebastians title!
I will let my partner, the great and glorious Ric Beauty take care of his brother and Sebastian is going to take back the 24 hour title!
Why? I am Sebastian Kerry Powell, and you are NOT! ***** "THE SILENCE" BOBBY SILAS (Silas is walking in a tough part of North Las Vegas, he is shaking his head) SILAS: Triple Threat Title. I am ready to take it and put it around my waist. I know , I have to beat 3 men to win it. Faith is first. Faith, I am bigger and stronger than you....(holds out arm) can you handle this Mississippi soup bone? Not a chance. You had best just look somewhere else, for someone else to fight, someone more your size.
Then it is on to Jack Spade and G-Gangsta. Spade is a tough man and proved it but it is G-Gangsta I am more interested in.
Another fake Gangsta. Just what the world needs. I suppose you figure you are hardcore as well huh G?
Well G, hardcore is Mississippi. And I will bring some good ole fashioned country hardcore to Warrior Wednesday. First for Faith and then for Spade and Gangsta!
And I will walk out with a belt on this waist! ***** DIRK WESTERN (Western is standing in front of his black truck at a truck stop along I-15 heading toward Las Vegas) DW: The Vegas Wrestling Federation is going to see a brand new Dirk
Western this Wednesday night.
All this time I have been trying to learn to wrestle, why? So the stupid fans would like me? Who cares about those brain dead fools.
And look at all the losers who have the fans behind them....Powell...Huxtable...Silas and my next opponent Paddy O'Shea.
You have cleaned up your act, big deal! I have a big change as well....I am the Texas BadAss!! And I am going to destroy you Paddy O'Shea as a icon for the other VWF stiffs. Clean up all you want.
You are going to have to get dirty to beat the Texas BadAss. Wednesday night VWF sees the streetfighter, the bar room butt kicker...Dirk Western! *****
