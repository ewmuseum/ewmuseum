VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Roleplay

VWF War of Words, 20 August 2001

21 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF War of Words August 20, 2001 IN THIS ISSUE Jack Spade Kerry Powell Tai Angela Sebastian Dirk Western Huey Huxtable ---------- JACK SPADE (...This Promo was made and paid for by Jack Spade...) (The scene fades into a shot of a TV screen which has the latest VWF matches announced on it. The camera focuses on the "Zell Strife (vs) Huey Huxtable (vs) Jack Spade" match up. Suddenly the camera turns, and stood in a pair of black jeans and no t-shirt is "The Ace" Jack Spade.) JS: Well I've been here less than a week and my first match is announced. A triple threat match, against two people I've never fought, or for that matter even heard of.
(Jack pauses for a moment.) JS: First off we have Zell Strife, some idiot good from California. Zell, quite simply this match is going to be mine. You should get home, wax your surf board and stay the hell out of my way on Wednesday night. I'm going into the match ready to hurt somebody, and if you're stood there looking gormless thinking you're gonna be able to put up a fight against me, then
I'll be forced to ram my fist in your face. Y'see Zell I'm not going to fool around on Wednesday night, I'm a no nonsence guy, and you're gonna get a no frills, straight forward beating.
(Jack wanders around the room for a moment then grins.) JS: Oh but of course, we have Big Huey Huxtable. You know what they say Huey, the bigger they are, the harder they fall. Just so happens you're 7 foot tall 360 pound bag of crap, and when I get a shot in on you, you're gonna hit the floor like a sack of bricks my friend. Huey, your size isn't anything to me. You could be half my size or twice my size and I'm still gonna kick your ass. Wednesday night will be like Jack and the
Beanstalk for you. I'm gonna cut you down to size, beat on you and that other idiot, win the match and walk out of Warrior Wednesday with a big grin on my face and my first victory here in the Vegas Wrestling Federation.
(Jack pauses and sits on a seat before finishing up.) JS: Be prepared Huet, be prepared Zell. You've got no chance against me. I'm climbing the ladder to the top. I'm going to succeed in backing up every word I've said. I'm going to beat you both then continue my rampage through the VWF like a rip saw and finally, when it's all over, I'll be a title holder here in Vegas.
If anybody's willing to question me, step up, and I'll let Mr. Fist right here... (Jack holds his right hand up in a punching motion.) JS: ...do the talking for me... (Fade out to black whilst Whiskey in the Jar by Metallica plays in the background.) ---------- KERRY POWELL (The scene opens up in downtown Vegas, well, just in Vegas because I don't think there _IS_ a rural part of Vegas. Neverless, Kerry Powell is walking through the streets, but he isn't his usual cocky, womanizing self. He looks as if he has an extremely uptight look on his face, borderline nervous.)
POWELL: Beauty, or whoever you are, this is all ending next week, no more will you sneak up on me, no more will you ruin my matchs, and no more will I always have to looks behind my back. Heh, if you are Beauty, this is going to be the crowning achivement in my career, a clean win over you. You remember in UWC, you remember the mental torment you put me through, made me believe you were on my side.. You made me believe you would protect me like you would you're younger brother... YOU LIED TO ME, BEAUTY! YOU LIED!
But, next week, either our 2 year long feud ends, or a new one comes to be. Either way, I'll emerge the winner. See you in a week, Ric. (Fade out.) ---------- TAI ANGELA (Black Sabbath plays and we see various shots of Angela in action. Chokeslamming a man on a VWF house show, spitting fire into the face of Zell Strife X2. The aftermath of the last match with Strife, Strife and the ref on the floor. Cut to Angela standing in an alleyway with a wicked smile on his face. Pasted on the filthy wall of a building in the alley is a poster of Zell Strife. Angela walks up to it and places his face right againt the face of Zell. Angela kisses the poster then steps back and takes a deep breath then blows fire onto the poster)
---------- SEBASTIAN (Sebastian is walking around the offices of the VWF) SEBASTIAN: Sebastian is not happy! OFFICE WORKER: Calm down Mr. Sebastian. SEBASTIAN: Sebastian is the Champion. (holds up title belt) Look! Look you little nothing! Sebastian is the Champion and Sebastian is not on the shows!?
The people want Sebastian! Who cares about the Triple Threat Title? Sebastian is THE 24 Hour
Champion, in the VWF that is THE Champion. OFFICE WORKER: You have a match for later in the month. SEBASTIAN: You tell the VWF people this, Sebastian will make an impact this week! Sebastian is not a paper champion. (Sebastian storms out of the office) OFFICE WORKER: He is a jerk...I mean Sebastian is a jerk. ---------- DIRK WESTERN (Dirk Western steps out of his truck at a deserted truck stop somewhere in the Mohave Desert) DW: I took me a week off. Time to think bout what went wrong in the big title match. Tai and Zell, the two deviants got it all messed up and because of that,
Sebastian is the
champion!
Well Zell, do me a favor and don't get messed up again and I can find my way to the Triple Threat Title! And Huey boy, you had best bring some kind of courage, you might want to think about watching the Lion. Find your courage or ya gonna get hurt.
Long hours, long trips that is what my life is all about. I will fight the long hours and long trips to get the Triple Threat Title!
---------- HUEY HUXTABLE (Huey is sitting on a sofa watching cartoons, his father comes in) HENRY: Huey! HUEY: Yeah pop. HENRY: You are supposed to be training. HUEY: (whining) You want me to fight again? HENRY: Son, we got a shot at the Triple Threat Title! HUEY: What does that mean? HENRY: It means you fight two guys. HUEY: (even more whining) I don't wanna hurt anyone, I want to make friends. HENRY: BOY! This is wrestling not some church social. (Henry pops in a tape of both Zell Strife and Jack Spade) HENRY: These are the guys you have to fight. HUEY: He looks weird, is he dead? HENRY: No that is Zell Strife, work on his face it is burned. HUEY: That's not fair. Hey that other guy looks tough. HENRY: He is new but he doesn't look to tough. But we have to train either way! HUEY: Do I have to stop watching cartoons? HENRY: (stands) Yes! Let's go. HUEY: But Pepper Ann is coming on! HENRY: Huuuuueeeeey! HUEY: Yes pop, I'm coming. (fade) ----------
