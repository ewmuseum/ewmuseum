FROM RAW-CAROLINA, CAROLINA COLLISION [The fans outside the Ace Matthews Arena are still outside, waiting to be let in. Their attention is drawn to a tank being driven up on
the sidewalk in front of the building. On the tank are the Sex Icons from Raw-Vegas, Sebastian and Classy Joey Boy, in full camoflauge with their faces panted the same.
CJB has the Raw-Vegas Blackjack title over his shoulder. Fans crowd around the tank as it moves close to the building. Security can be seen checking the doors to make sure they're locked. A few security guards are waiting in front of the main entrance. CJB pulls out a megaphone and begins to speak to the fans.]
CJB: Fans of Raw-Carolina, as your "Team Captain" I must regret to inform you that on August 29, the Raw Tag Team Titles will no longer be held by any member of this wanna be federation.
[The devout RAW-C fans boo heartily. CJB and Sebastian hop off the tank and approach the main doors. CJB speaks to the security guards through the megaphone.)
CJB: We are here to say hello to our friends Scott Starring and Van
Wild. Sebastian: We just want to make sure they're doing OK. Security Guard: I can't let you in. [CJB turns his attention to the crowd that has gathered around.] CJB: Would you all like to see the Raw-Tag Team titles defended right here tonight? [The crowd pops for this. CJB and Sebastian climb back onto the tank and it begins to back up. The crowd follows the tank as it heads to the back parking lot near the superstars entrance. CJB can be heard calling out orders.]
CJB: Forward move!....Tally ho!......... [As the tank gets to the back, more security is seen guarding the back door. By this time, police are starting to arrive, as well. A security guard approaches the men.)
Security guard: Sir, the police have arrived, you need to leave this area. Sebastian: If you were smart, you'd let us drive this tank right down to the ring! CJB: I just want to know that Starring and Wild are keeping our titles nice and clean for us!

RESULT- Xtreme Tony Cage by disqualification. ====================== CAPSMAN vs KAMA SUTRA ====================== CAPS came to the ring with on again off again partner Falling Frenchman Marcel Klutz. RESULT- CAPSMAN by pinfall. LDO: We will back with a Battle Royal... and a RAW Heavyweight Title match!
