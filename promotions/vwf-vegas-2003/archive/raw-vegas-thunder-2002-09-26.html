RAW-Vegas Thunder! 9-26-02

RAW-Vegas ========== (Lean Darlene Orion is sitting at the Center Bar inside the Hard Rock, just outside of the entrance to the Joint)
LDO: Welcome to Gathering Storm, the first fifteen minutes of Thunder! And the buzz around Vegas is, Revolution Randall Crane is coming back! So that is the old blood coming back and we have some new blood as well. He is the tag team partner of the big man, Nikui Monsutaa who has been ripping up RAW Carolina, he is Jaaku Onimusha.
============================= JAAKU ONIMUSHA vs ROB KIDANE ============================= Onimusha completely destroyed Kidane. Simple as that. the five eleven,
211 pounds high flyer took the battle to Kidane and kept on top. Even on the rare occasion that Kidane got a roll going Onimusha turned it around any way he could. Kidane fell victim to THE TOBIDOUGU, a vicious flying forearm that led to the 1-2-3 for Onimusha.
REUSLT- Jaaaku Onimusha winner by pinfall. Next up was the first match in the 24 Hour Title Tournament! ======================= SEBASTIAN vs BLACKBIRD ======================= The only female wrestler in Vegas took he fight to Sebastian. The Sexy
One was backed up and beaten down at various points in this battle.
The New Circle wants to win all three of its first round matches, so this brought out Blake Hamilton and Tai Angela shortly after Sebastian caught Blackbird and nailed a backbreaker. Just as Sebastian was slapping the SEX YOU OUT on Blackbird Blake Hamilton came into the ring and dropped Sebastian with a clothesline. Sebastian took the hard shot and rolled out of the ring and the bell sounded.
RESULT- Sebastian winner by disqualification and moves on the tournament! Hamilton and Angela stepped into the ring and took rookie ref Steve and began to pummel him!
That is when the Fallen Angels hit the ring! XTC and Insomniac rushed in and cleared the ring of the Circle! When the dust settled it was time for these partners, to fight it out!
============================== XTREME TONY CAGE vs INSOMNIAC ============================== Neither man seemed to want to fight, but with a chance at the 24 Hour
Title on the line they started. Insomniac is not a technical wrestler and despite early efforts he turned to big power moves to gain control on XTC. Insomniac went for a sidewalk slam and XTC almost turned it into a pinfall. Near the ten minute mark XTC went to the top rope and went for a sunset flip off the ropes but got a BIG BOOT on his way down and the big man picked up the 1-2-3!
RESULT- Insomniac by pinfall and moves on in the tournament! After the match Insomniac rolled out of the ring, grabbed the Vegas
Tag Team titles, got back in the ring and helped up his partner and they enjoyed the cheers of the fans as they held up the RAW-Vegas Tag
Team belts.
==================================== PHIL LATIO vs SURFER BLAKE HAMILTON ==================================== Last week, Latios partner Kama Sutra was seriously injured by Hunter
Jack. This week Latio had the tough task of taking on Blake Hamilton,
The Maquilladora's hated enemy. Hamilton and Latio exchanged some high risk moves before just deciding the fight. Hamilton punched, kicked and low blowed his way.. (low blowed?) to get the upper hand. Hamilton put Latio up on his shoulder and dropped back, his move called RIDE
THE WAVES! Hamilton then went up to the top rope and as Latio turned he hit CRASH AND BURN and got the 1-2-3!
RESULT- Surfer Blake Hamilton by pinfall and moves on in the tournament! CUT BACK TO LDO SITTING AT THE CENTER BAR LDO: Three men are moving on! And three more will join them.
