Welcome to Wrestle Style V.3! Or WSV.3! And tonight we are excited about our show. Twelve big matches in the VWF heavyweight tournament! For those of you who might be new to the VWF, back in February CJB was the champ and was scheduled to meet four other competitors in a Pentagon of Pain match. Well long before CJB could make it to the ring he was attacked and nearly killed by someone swinging a boat oar. That man turned out to be Lark Fenriz. Fast forward a few weeks and Fenriz wins a royal rumble event to become the new champ, since CJB was out of the business. At least at that time. Then a few weeks back CJB returns to the VWF and it turns out he may or may not have been stripped of the title. But since we are having a tournament it doesn't really matter. All 24 members of the VWF are here tonight and each one has the same chance of going to Vegas Violence where the final match of this tournament will be for the title, so each week up until then we will have a VWF Heavyweight Title match. Tonight it was Lark Fenriz putting the title up against the returning Billy Bones, but we will get to that battle much later.
