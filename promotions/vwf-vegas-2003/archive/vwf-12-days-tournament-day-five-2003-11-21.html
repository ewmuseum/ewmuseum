12 Days Tournament: Day Five

DTMA: THE STUDD SPLASH!!!!!!!! WEST: Which Storm moved out of the way of at the last minute! DTMA: Storm picked up CJB and they went over the corner... Storm pounded CJB's head off the turnbuckles and then set CJB up on the top rope... WEST: Storm got up there with him! DTMA: The place went A S B with both men on the top! WEST: Jason Storm had a look in his eye like wanted to hurt CJB... but that is Storm.. DTMA: Storm had CJB in the RACK!!! WEST: And was looking for a top rope like version of the PERFECT STORM when his knee crumbled and both men fell off the top rope and to the floor! DMTA: The place was crazy! Not a fan in the place was in control! WEST: And the ref counted..... DTMA: And neither man was able to get back in the ring before the ten count. WEST: Our first draw! A double count out... and if these guys meet in the next round.... WOW! DTMA: WOW! !!!!! Double Count Out Draw !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. (CUT to 12 Days headquarter, with Dan The Man Aller and Dirk Western sitting at the desk) DTMA: Welcome to Day 5! And for the guys in these groups this is the end of the first round. WEST: At the end of thos show we will know eight of the twelve men who will move on to the second round. DMTA: Lets go the action, and start with Group A and a title defense! .. .. .. Group A -------- \\\\\\\ DANIEL SINZ (vs) YANCY "Y2K" KILPATRICK (c) /////// /////// VWF Submission/Sinz Days Championship Match \\\\\\\ ----------------------------------------------------------- (Thanks to [name withheld] for writing this one) DTMA: And this match was underway before the bell as Daniel Sinz attacked Yancy Kilpatrick from behind, charging down the aisle and nailing the double champion in the back of the head with a steel chair before he even made it to the ring!! WEST: Sinz was obviously more interested in hurting Y2K than he was in those two titles, Dan, and, despite several warnings from the referee, he continued his beatdown of the champion for almost two minutes before Kilpatrick was able to roll out of the ring for a breather and regain control of the match with a BELLY-TO-BELLY OVERHEAD SUPLEX on the floor!! DTMA: Kilpatrick's control of the match didn't last for long, though. As the champ pulled Sinz to his feet and went to throw him into the ring, Sinz IRISH WHIPPED Kilpatrick into the steel ringpost headfirst, busting Y2K open and possibly compounding the damage from that chairshot at the start of the match!! WEST: Sinz took clear advantage of the referee's ten count, Dan, staying out on the floor until nine and beating the already injured and bleeding champion till he could barely stand on his own!! DTMA: I don't know why the referee didn't just disqualify Daniel Sinz but I guess one rule in a Sinz Days Title defense is that anything goes. WEST: But Sinz still had to make Y2K submit in order to win the VWF Submission Championship...and that's basically what he did when he rolled Kilpatrick back into the ring and locked him in his own MILLENNIUM DEATHLOCK, refusing to break the hold until Y2K literally passed out from the pain and the referee was forced to award him both titles!! DTMA: That wasn't enough for Daniel Sinz, though. It wasn't enough for him to just BEAT Kilpatrick, Sinz wanted to BREAK him, too!! Talk about being a sore loser!! WEST: More like sore winner!! Daniel Sinz beat Yancy Kilpatrick bloody, made him tap out to his own finisher, and then humiliated the former champion by spitting in his face when he couldn't fight back!! DTMA: And, worst of all, Kilpatrick had to be carried out of the arena on a stretcher and taken to the same hospital where he was treated just two weeks ago after Phear Phantastic!! Hopefully we'll have an update on his condition soon...but it's obvious to me that we WON'T be seeing Y2K or The Masked Maniac again anytime soon!! !!!!! WINNER AND NEW VWF SUBMISSION/SINZ DAYS CHAMPION = DANIEL SINZ !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ MASKED MANIAC (vs) KEVIN BLAIR /////// ---------------------------------------------- DTMA: Well since Kilpatrick is the Maniac this match never took place. WEST: Blair won by forfeit, and he and Daniel Sinz will move on out of Group A! !!!!! WINNER = Kevin Blair !!!!! DMTA: So lets look at the final standings in Group A! 1) Daniel Sinz (2-1) 2) Kevin Blair (2-1) 3) Yancy Kilpatrick (1-2) 4) Masked Maniac (1-2) WEST: Sinz and Blair will move and will stay together in the next round against two other men. DTMA: Lets move to the wild action that has been the trademark of Group B. .. .. .. .. .. Group B ------- \\\\\\\ EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) JUSHIN LIGER GOLD /////// ----------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Well battle started off with Loco challenging JLG to a posedown.. WEST: Loco posed and flexed his arms, JLG DROPKICKED him! Loco came off the ropes jumped over JLG and went to the other ropes.... GOLD used a DROP TOE HOLD to take Loco to the mat. DTMA: JLG then locked up the legs, slapped on a chin lock and turned it all into a SURFBOARD! WEST: Jushin Liger GOLD and El Gringo Loco have some history... DMTA: Jorge jumped up on the apron and GOLD broke the hold and jumped to his feet. WEST: Dumb move. DMTA: No, smart move. Jorge was up to hijinz. WEST: Whaaa??? DMTA: Loco SCHOOLBOY'D JLG for a near fall, and then used a couple of quick KICKS to take control back. WEST: Loco then starting a brawling strategy.... mostly punches and kicks. DTMA: After an IRISH WHIP Loco rushed in... JLG moved and hit a HIGH LEG CLOTHESLINE as Loco backed out of the corner! WEST: The fans began the familiar chant WE WANT GOLD! WE WANT GOLD! Yech! Sickening! DMTA: JLG set up the superstar from Mexico and nailed him with a SUPERPLEX! WEST: But Loco was able to get his shoulders up. DMTA: GOLD was backing Loco up to the ropes when Loco fell and pulled down the top rope, sending JLG to the floor! WEST: El Gringo Loco got the attention of the ref and Jorge came over and HIT Jushin Liger GOLD square in the CHIN! DMTA: Jorge then added a POWERSLAM to the floor and then called out to Loco to add some punishment of his own... with JLG struggling to get to his feet... Loco came off the ropes for a SUICIDA PLANCHA! WEST: And Mr Hinote came over and pushed Jorge into JLG... so Loco missed GOLD and hit his manager Jorge. DTMA: GOLD soon was back on track and hit a HURRICANRANA on Loco on the outside before finally rolling him back in the ring. WEST: JLG hit a SLINGSHOT SENTON to come back in the ring but Loco moved! DTMA: At this point El Gringo Loco sent GOLD to the ropes and slapped on the IRON CLAW! WEST: Loco was gripping and ripping, trying to crush the head of Jushin Liger GOLD. DMTA: But JLG was not going to submit. Loco picked him up and hit a POWERSLAM with ever letting go of the IRON CLAW! WEST: But JLG managed to wrap Loco up in a SMALL PACKAGE! DTMA: But Loco kicked out... WEST: Loco still had JLG in trouble... he sent GOLD to the corner and JLG ended up in the TREE OF WOE! DTMA: This led to the RUN FOR THE BORDER! WEST: Loco hit it and really had GOLD in trouble.... DMTA: El Gringo Loco waited on JLG..... and GOLD got to his feet Loco went for the EL MILAGRO MEXICANO!!!! WEST: But GOLD stopped the move and set Loco on the top rope facing the crowd! DMTA: GOLD chopped Loco in the back of the head a few times and then got up with him....... and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX OFF THE TOP ROPE!!!! WEST: The fans were losing their idiot minds, WE WANT GOLD.... DMTA: JLG jumped on the top rope and began walking to the middle.... WEST: It was to be LIGHTNING STRIKE time! DTMA: Until Jorge shook the ropes, sending GOLD falling into the ring and landing beside El Gringo Loco! WEST: Each man was laying there. DTMA: It seemed Jushin Liger GOLD was about to get to his feet... WEST: And then the bell rang! DTMA: The time limit on this one had run out. I think JLG would have won it if Jorge had not caused him to fall off the top rope. WEST: GOLD slipped. !!!!! Time Limit Draw !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ PURE RAGE JOHNNY FREIL (vs) BAD SEED DEVIN DEMOLA //////// ------------------------------------------------------------------ DTMA: It was as if Freil could sense his chance, a win over Demola would move him on to the next round! WEST: After the draw between Loco and GOLD Freil saw his chance and worked over Demola. DMTA: Demola came out flat, clearing knowing that no matter how this match went down he was moving on the next round. WEST: Freil hit a SIT OUT POWERSLAM and then slapped a CROSSFACE on Demola about three minutes in. DTMA: Not a finisher per se, but Freil had some submission skills. WEST: I think he is looking at a shot at the NEW Submission champ Daniel Sinz sometime in the future. DTMA: Well Freil would have to worry about Demola, who got to the ropes and broke the hold and then Demola caught Freil off guard and mounted an offense of his own. WEST: Demola is defending champ of this tournament, and he showed why he is the man to beat! DMTA: Freil got hit with the CUBAN MISSLE! The KISS OF THE SHADOWS and even found himself under a SHOOTING STAR PRESS! WEST: But each of the moves only got Demola a two count. DMTA: Demola upped the pressure, putting Freil in a POWERBOMB position and hitting the DEMOLA ROLL 2K2!!!! WEST: It looked like a three count, but Freil got his foot on the ropes! DMTA: Demola seemed to snap, to want to hurt Freil.... he tossed him out of the ring.. and followed him out there with a BASEBALL SLIDE that sent Freil into the ring barriers! WEST: And cracked the head of Freil. DTMA: Demola locked up a now bloody Johnny Freil and went for a VERTICAL SUPLEX on the outside of the ring, but Freil floated over and ran Demola head first into the ring post! WEST: And now both men were bloody! Freil then hit the FISTS OF RAGE on Demola, trying to break those ribs! DMTA: Demola used a kneelift to stop it and he rolled Freil up on the apron! WEST: Demola climbed up there with him and motioned that he was about to DDT Freil off the apron and to the floor! DTMA: But Freil grabbed Demola behind the knees and tossed him off and onto the floor... and Freil fell through the ropes and back into the ring! WEST: At the ten count! Demola was counted out! DTMA: A VERY VERY angry Devin Demola pulled a table out from under the ring and tossed it in... he climbed in the ring and softened up Freil with a few punches and then placed him on the table.... WEST: Demola got on the table with him and lifted Freil up for a SUPLEX! DMTA: Demola turned it into a PILEDRIVER a move he calls the CROWNING GLORY!!!! WEST: Driving Freil through the table underneath both men!!!! DMTA: The fans lost it! After a few seconds Demola climbed out of the rubble and walked off. WEST: These two men will meet again in this tournament! Because they are both moving on! !!!!! WINNER = Pure Rage Johnny Freil !!!!! DTMA: And here are the final standing in Group B! 1) Johnny Freil (2-1) 2) Devin Demola (2-1) 3) El Gringo Loco (1-1-1) 4) Jushin Liger GOLD (0-2-1) WEST: I would have never picked Freil to move out of this group. DMTA: And Loco and JLG now have to concentrate on the tag tournament. .. .. .. .. .. Group C ------- \\\\\\\ RYAN DELANEY (vs) LANCE VOLTAR /////// ---------------------------------------------- DTMA: Like so many times before, Voltar really looked good against Delaney. WEST: Voltar knew enough about Delaney to stay away from a mat match with him, Delaney is clearly the better mat technician.. DMTA: Voltar caught Delaney in a FLYING HEADSCISSORS and Delaney slid out of the ring. Voltar then went over the top rope and hit Delaney with a CROSS BODY! WEST: Lance tossed Delaney back in the ring and came in via the top rope and a MISSLE HEADBUTT!!! DMTA: But the cover only got Voltar a 2 count! WEST: This guy might never hear a three count. DMTA: Voltar came off the ropes, but Delaney turned things and suddenly had Voltart up in a POWERBOMB position! WEST: Voltar clocked Delaney between the eyes and slid down into a SUNSET FLIP! DMTA: But Delaney kicked out! WEST: Voltar used a few kicks and then hit a SLINGSHOT SUPLEX on Delaney! DMTA: And Voltar went to the top rope for the BIG V!!!!!!! WEST: But he missed. DMTA: Delaney rolled out of the way.... and like a snake to a rat Delaney hit CHASING THE DRAGON and ended this match! !!!!! WINNER = Ryan Delaney !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) TYSON YOUNG /////// ------------------------------------------------- DMTA: Young has been a disappointment in this tournament and this match was no exception. WEST: Young came out and used some street fighting tactics, but Maq ducked a CLOTHESLINE and hit a REAR KICK! DMTA: Maq even drove Young into the canvas with a CRADLE PILEDRIVER! WEST: But Maq, who has the fastest feet in the game, dropped Young with a SPINNING HEEL KICK that had Young in just enough trouble. DMTA: For PERMANENT DAYLIGHT! and the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNER = The Maquilladora !!!!! DMTA: And lets look at final standings in Group C 1) Ryan Delaney (3-0) 2) The Maquilladora (2-1) 3) Tyson Young (1-2) 4) Lance Voltar (0-3) WEST: Delaney and Maq will meet again somewhere in Round 2! DTMA: It will be their third match in the 12 Days tournament, one last year and one so far this year... this is getting to be a regular event. .. .. .. .. .. Group D -------- \\\\\\\ ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS (vs) DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA /////// ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Each man needs the win. WEST: Angela took control on the smaller Andrews early and didn't let up. DMTA: The Dark Monster hit a JACKHAMMER on Andrews about three minutes in and looked to get the fall, but Andrews kicked out. WEST: Angela then hit an OVER THE SHOULDER SPINNING POWERBOMB on Andrews! DMTA: But once again Andrews kicked out. WEST: The Dark Monster pulled Andrews to his feet, and Andrews slipped behind Angela and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX! DTMA: Then another and another and another, a EA trademark. WEST: Erik Andrews got Andrews and put on a REAR CHIN LOCK with a KNEE between the shoulder blades to add some extra punishment. DTMA: Andrews kept this up as Angela struggled .... WEST: Finally Blackbird slid in the ring in front of Andrews causing him to break the hold. The ref moved to get Blackbird out of the ring, this gave Angela a chance... DMTA: Erik Andrews found himself in a SLEEPER HOLD! WEST: Andrews started flailing his arms around, and each time he got close to the ropes Angela would yank him back to the center of the ring! DMTA: Andrews, with no other choice, dropped and broke the hold with a JAW BREAKER! WEST: Angela stumbled backwards, came off the ropes and Andrews locked him up! DMTA: The BOTTOM LINE! WEST: And Andrews got the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNER = Absolute Arrogance Erik Andrews !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ VIOLENT VICTOR FROST (vs) DEVIN WOLFE /////// ----------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Like the other two matches for Wolfe, Devin hit the ring and rocked Frost from pillar to post! WEST: Frost, who has been a quiet force, was in trouble! DMTA: Three minutes in Wolfe had Frost and hit the RAISE THE DEAD! WEST: But Wolfe, didn't go for the pin or another move. DMTA: Wolfe started to look around.... waiting for the arrival of this mystery man who has been jumping him for a few weeks.... WEST: Frost shook off the cobwebs, and DRAGON SUPLEX'd the paranoid Devin Wolfe! DMTA: Frost then went to work..... hitting three elbow strikes ending with a roaring elbow. WEST: Frost calls it GRAPHIC VIOLENCE 2002! DMTA: Wolfe was in trouble after the move, right here in 2003. WEST: Frost then hit a SAMOAN DROP off the second rope, the VIOLATOR! DMTA: And Violent Victor ended this one with the VIOLATOR MARK II!!!! !!!!! WINNER = Violent Victor Frost !!!!! DTMA: And the final standings in Group D! 1) Victor Frost (3-0) 2) Erik Andrews (2-1) 3) Tai Angela (1-2) 4) Devin Wolfe (0-3) WEST: Andrews moves on, a late replacement in this tournament and he is moving on! DMTA: So lets take a quick look, moving on... Daniel Sinz, Kevin Blair WEST: Devin Demola and Johnny Freil DMTA: Ryan Delaney and The Maquilladora from Group C! WEST: And Violent Victor Frost and Erik Andrews. DMTA: They will be joined by four others after Day 6 ends on Tuesday, as well as four tag teams! WEST: Tune in Tuesday, it will mark the half way point of this tournament. DMTA: See you then! © 12 Days Tournament
