Holiday Havoc II

December 3, 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[In the back of the Joint, the camera is rolling only to find a shiny black limosine pull it. Within seconds, the car is off, the chauffer is out and opening the limo's door. Dressed in formal attire, not wrestling gear, "The Wolf" Lark Fenriz rises out of the limo's dark recesses, a black cane in his hand. The chauffer leaves the door open, actign as if another is supposed to come out as well, but Lark nudges towards the camera, and the chauffer goes white. He immediatley shuts the door, hops back in the car, and turns it on to drive away. Lark just laughs, and stares at the camera.] LF: A famous man who I depise came up with a line of poetry once that even I cannot forget not matter how much I hate the man. [He pauses, smiles, and then speaks out in a classic Shakespearian voice.] LF: Revenge is the _sweetest_ joy next to killing! [A brief laugh.] LF: Or, at least, somethign along those lines... But at least the point I am trying to make is quite clear. Tonight and all through this _joyous_ holiday season chaos will be spread and destruction will be caused in the name of _revenge_. Revenge for what took place last week on Holiday Havoc! [The camera zooms in on Lark's face, as he folds his arms across his chest and gets a quite... serious look upon his face.] LF: Tonight begins the return to order here in Vegas, Mr. Williams. But trust me... [An evil demented laugh.] LF: The change will be so subtle you won't even _realize_ it! [The camera fades away, as the sounds of a car pulling up are heard.] .. .. .. (Viva Las Vegas by the Dead Kennedys hits and the screen fills with images of recent VWF battles # 24 Hour Champ Adam Jennings hitting a powerslam on Robbie Caldwell # # The Maquilladora and his mystery woman # # Gods Tears winning the Submission Title # # Trinity attacking Fenriz, CJB and Cutlass on the last show # # Tazmanian Devil Mark McQueen in close up with the tag team title over his shoulder # .. .. .. (CUT to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: And tonight we find out who will hold the other half of those titles. Everyone on the VWF roster will find a place in tonights battle royal. But it was what happened before the battle royal that had fans talking. Just as the curtain went up, Lark Fenriz made his way to the ring. The former VWF heavyweight champ took center ring and demanded revenge for what happened at Holiday Havoc I. Fenriz added that he didn't care about the tag team titles, he was there for revenge. That is when Fast Eddie Cutlass walked to the ring. Cutlass took the mic from Fenriz and said that Lark was not the only one who was jumped. Cutlass as we recall was placed on a cross by Pagan. Fast Eddie said he wanted to gain some revenge himself. Then Classy Joey Boy walked to the ring and said that no one does to the Team Captain what Trinity did, and CJB called out Kingston Cash. The boss walked out and said he knew that Trinity was getting out of control, that things were getting over the line in the VWF. Lets go listen in. .. .. .. KINGSTON CASH: You each have plenty of reason to be upset, hell I would be mad as hell. So here is my idea. I will give you a rematch, you three against Trinity later tonight, but you all have to give up your spots in the battle royal! .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Each of the three men had no trouble agreeing to that and the match was made. While the fans enjoyed some highlights of the 12 Days Tournament, Kingston Cash made his way back to his office. After the highlight package we went backstage. .. .. .. [The office area of "The Joint" VWF arena. Out front of a large nicely finished door with the embrodered gold name plate that says "Kingston Cash". Cash: I like that I don't have to pay for it, but I can't just give you a title. GT: Your absolutly right! Thats why i suggest the first ever GT's Invitational. I already got it setup. Got all i need with my connections in Detroit. And with a match thats worthy of the championship. Cash: Then here is the deal, you have the invitational, you make it happen. And if all goes well then the VWF will recognize the title. Just don't screw it up. . [GT smiles wickedly] GT: Oh, dont worry about that, its all taken care of. .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So the Submission Champ is going to try and bring another title to the VWF? I am very interested in seeing how that goes down. When we come back, the battle royal! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. [Meanwhile... Backstage, in a quiet corner of the building, far from prying eyes and cameras, sits a man. No, not just a man, because that word somehow doesn't do justice to this particular individual. Now, monster...that would be appropriate. An overused term perhaps, especially in this business. But sometimes, as on this occasion, it simply fits. And so this man, this monster, sits and waits. Alone with his thoughts, awaiting the call to action. Anticipating the match. The battle royal. The match he needs to win. The match he has been told to win. Why is not important. Not to him. The plan also is of no concern. He simply does what he must do, because for him, the price of failure is far too high. He knows there will be many foes he must defeat, foes that seek the same prize as he. But they too do not concern him. They are simply obstacles he must overcome if he is to reach his goal. And though it will not be easy, he knows one thing. There will be storm clouds over Vegas tonight.] .. .. .. (CUT to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Well the VWF does have a title that is up for grabs. Mark McQueen and Marcus O'Malley were the champs, then O'Malley lost his half of the title to Kevin Simmons at Phear Phantastic. Then Simmons, as well as O'Malley were suspended from the VWF. So lets get to the battl royal! .. .. .. ================================================ Battle Royal for half of the VWF tag team titles ================================================ SOUTHERNS: Once each man was in the ring we thought it was time to get it started, but there was still one man left. [The lights suddenly flicker and die, plunging everything into darkness!] WEST: What the...? [A buzz of uncertainty ripples through the crowd, intensifying as a low rumble of thunder echoes around the arena. All eyes turn to the big screen as it springs to life with a stop-motion shot of grey storm clouds rolling across the sky.] DTMA: There was some talk earlier today about a possible mystery entrant in this battle royal, and Dirk, this must be him! WEST: Uh, ya think? [Another rumble of thunder, louder and "closer" this time. The storm clouds slowly shift from grey to red, and... ** BOOM!! ** ....a flash of lightning lights up the screen! Where this lightning appears to "strike" the stage, a continuous stream of crimson pyro bursts several feet into the air!] DTMA: Wait, isn't this... [The instrumental version of 'God of Thunder' by Kiss pounds out over the speakers as the stream of pyro begins to die down, and through the cloud of smoke, bathed in the eerie crimson glow, steps a menacing monster of a man! Nearly seven feet tall, head shaved bald, chiseled physique honed for destruction, matching lightning bolt pattern spiraling down both muscular arms, it could only be one man...] [Major, MAJOR mixed POP~!!!!!] DTMA: That's...that's Valenkov, Dirk!! The former 24 Hour Champion, Thunder Valenkov!! WEST: Former DOMINANT 24 Hour Champion, you mean! This man's a _beast_, Danny-boy! And he's back in the VWF!? DTMA: It looks like, Dirk! Last time we saw him was as a member of the New Age Army, before Blackbird left the VWF and seemingly disbanded that group. Kingston Cash chose not to renew Valenkov's work permit, and so that was the last we saw of him. Until now. [The towering form of Thunder Valenkov strides purposely down the aisle. Arriving at ringside, he springs athletically up onto the apron and steps over the top rope, joining the throng of other VWF stars.] .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: That's right, the big man from Russia! Former 24 Hour Champion. His appearance did not have any effect on the Riders who jumped Adam Jennings just as the bell rang. But that left The Salvation alone to be triple teamed by the remaining Riders. They got the upper hand and tossed him as CAPSMEN were once again late with the save. ===== The Salvation eliminated ===== SOUTHERNS: The Riders and The Rhombus squared off as The Maquilladora hit PERMANENT DAYLIGHT on Iyasu Saku on the other side of the ring. As Maq stood with Saku Roadkill Rick charged them and knocked both men out of the ring. ===== Iyasu Saku eliminated ===== ===== The Maquilladora eliminated ===== SOUTHERNS: A big clothesline from Valenkov then sent The Reckoning to the floor. ===== The Reckoning eliminated ===== SOUTHERNS: And Valenkov turned and got a BIG BOOT from Roadkill Rick!
