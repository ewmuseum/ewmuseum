Kidane kept CAPS in the Enforcer corner and he and Klingel tagged in and out beating down on CAPSMAN. CAPSMAN tried on a few occasions to make the tag but came up short each time. Kidane hit a CRADLE

RESULT- CAPSMAN and MARCEL KLUTZ by pinfall. ========================================= THE DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA vs SEBASTIAN ========================================= Angela just got a big win over Erik Andrews and Sebastian is looking to take the RAW Tag Team titles from Van Wild and Scott Starring. The action was back and forth until Angela slowly took over with his power. Sebastian made a few offensive moves but it would always lead to a counter by The Dark Monster. Angela sent Sebastian to the ropes and hit THE HEART PUNCH! But it sent The Sexy One out of the ring. As
Ref Kenny was looking over CJB came into the ring and hit the KISS OF
DEATH on Angela. CJB then slid out and rolled his Sex Icons partner back into the ring and Sebastian locked on the SEX YOU OUT! Blackbird came into the ring and hit her BITCH SLAP on Sebastian getting her man disqualified.
RESULT- SEBASTIAN by disqualification.
