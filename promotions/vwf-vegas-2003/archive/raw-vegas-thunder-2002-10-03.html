RAW-Vegas Thunder! 10-03-02

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW-Vegas ========== (Lean Darlene Orion is sitting at a desk in the parking lot of the
Joint, small crowd has gathered around her)
LDO: Welcome to Gathering Storm! The Thunder! pre-show! We are here in the parking lot of the Joint for no good reason!
Three of tonights matches are in the 24 Hour Title tournament but we have plenty of action, lets take at look at some of the earlier action.
=========================== PHIL LATIO vs QUINTON OHME =========================== A few weeks back Latio saw Hunter Jack destroy his partner Kama Sutra. Latio is still in Vegas and showed off a new, more technical style against the high flyer Ohme. Latio worked on the legs and lower back for much of the match and even hit a POWERBOMB into the turnbuckle that almost got him the fall. Later Ohme went for a plancha and missed and Latio locked him in a DEEP Texas Cloverleaf that Latio calls THE
SEXACUTOR and Ohme quickly TAPPED OUT!
RESULT- Phil Latio by submission. Next up.... ============================ BLACKBIRD vs JAAKU ONIMUSHA ============================ The top female in RAW, Blackbird stepped into the ring with the on fire Onimusha. Onimusha was much faster than Blackbird and kept her off her feet. But the lady is no lady, Blackbird was quick with the low blow, and the occasional eye gouge to get back on top in the battle. Blackbird hit her BITCH KICK and got the near fall. Onimusha stumbled to the ropes and Blackbird went for the kick again but missed and ended up riding the ropes!!!! Onimusha then took her off the ropes with the DROPKICK and as she made her way to her feet Onimusha came across the ring with THE TOBIDOUGU and got the 1-2-3!
RESULT- Jaaku Onimusha by pinfall. After the match the rest of the Circle, Tai Angela and Blake Hamilton hit the ring and began the beat down on Jaaku Onimusha. Angela nailed
Jaaku with his HEART PUNCH and stood over his lifeless form until
Hunter Jack hit the ring!
======================================= DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA vs HUNTER JACK ======================================= The Aussie Assassin took out Blackbird and Hamilton and then went to work on his opponent for the night Tai Angela. Hunter got DQ'ed in the
24 Hour Tournament thanks to the Circle and was out to take revenge on
Angela. Hunter Jack beat Angela in each and every corner of the ring and dropped Blake Hamilton and Blackbird each time they tried to get involved. Angela finally rolled out of the ring and as Hunter Jack came out to get the Dark Monster the Aussie got a face full of surfboard. Angela rolled back in the ring and a bloody Hunter Jack was counted out.
RESULT- Dark Monster Tai Angela by countout. The last match of Gathering Storm was a BIG one! =========================================== SCOTT STARRING vs REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE =========================================== Crane returned to the Joint and to a tough opponent. Vegas fans have seen Crane in battles over monthes and Crane clearly slower than
Starring, but who isn't? Randall Crane is a great ring general and eventually the fight moved to his favor as time ticked on. Crane hit his WORLD CRASHING DOWN fishermans suplex for a near fall. Starring ducked a punch and hit a SPINNING HEEL KICK that seemed to have Crane in trouble, but the Revolution was playing possum and Starring missed the ROOKIE MISTAKE, Crane locked him in REVOLUTIONS ENDS 2002!
Starring fought off the pain for as long as he could but had to tap out in the end. RESULT- Revolution Randall Crane by submission. After this break, it will be Thunder time! DW: Maul rolls in the ring and so does Insomniac.... DTMA: All five men in the ring! DW: The tension is.... [The booing crowd is hushed as all of a sudden, the lights completely black out. The buzz of uncertainty electrifies the crowd for a few moments, until the beginning guitar riffs of Pantera's "Revolution Is My Name" blare over the speakers. The crowd then goes absolutely _crazy_ as the music is instantly recognized through the darkness.
The song continues to play as the lights slowly rise back to full brightness, revealing "The Revolution" Randall Crane standing tall in the middle of the ring. With all of his former enemies fighting around him,
Crane simply smirks and raises a baseball bat high into the air, getting a loud pop from the bloodthirsty crowd.
Each man in the ring looks at Crane, all wondering who he is there for, and all waiting for him to attack. However, the Fallen Angels take the initiative to get revenge and start fighting back against the Sex
Icons.
Crane takes a moment to watch, then smirks as he makes the crowd cheer in delight. With one swift move of the baseball bat...]
***** CRACK ***** ***** CRACK ***** ***** CRACK ***** [Crane makes Sebastian slump down to the mat with three well placed bat blows to his stomach, back, and head. Tony Cage and Insomniac continue their assault on Maul and Classy Joey Boy as Crane takes a moment to raise the bat into the air, the crowd cheering loudly. Crane then makes his way over to help Cage... the two former enemies teaming up on CJB.
Crane motions for Cage to climb the turnbuckles as he lifts CJB in powerbomb position. Cage jumps as Crane plants CJB down to the mat with a
Revolution's End Powerbomb, and Cage nails a somersault legdrop! The two men share a glance of friendship, then go over to help Insomniac take down
Maul, who is currently being taken down in the corner. Maul puts up a fight, but is overcome by the three men nailing him with punches and kicks. He eventually succumbs to their blows, falling to the mat. However, they continue with their assault, all three men making sure that Maul is on the mat to stay. The crowd then cheers again as the three men celebrate inside the ring.
The Fallen Angels then grab the RAW Vegas Tag Team Title, lifting them high into the air as Crane points to them. Cage and Insomniac then start walking out of the ring but are stopped by Crane. Crane calls for a microphone, and he is given one by a ringside attendant. He then looks at both men and speaks, sticking a finger up to stop them.]
RC: Don't run out of here so quickly, boys. I've got something that
I'd
like to say. You know, I've been a part of this promotion for quite a while now. I've seen people come and go, and I've taken my fair share of beatings. But _none_ of those beatings were as bad as the ones I've received from you two. I don't think we've _ever_ seen eye-to-eye on anything.
And hell, who could forget what you did to my car? [The two men look at each other anxiously.] RC: However, through all of this I've grown to know what it takes to be a competitor. You two have put me through hell, but it was all worth it. I respect the two of you more than anyone else here in RAW Vegas.
Seeing this... I'm willing to wipe the slate clean for a greater good. [Crane looks down at the Sex Icons, still lying on the mat.] RC: Let's rid this place of these scumbags. The Fallen Angels and
Randall Crane... all in the same fight. The same "Revolution," if you will. The three of us together will be a force unlike any other... even greater than the Sex Icons. [With that, Crane sticks out his hand in friendship, wiping the slate clean from times past. The Fallen Angels look at each other, not knowing if they should trust Crane. The crowd is cheering for them to shake Crane's hand, but they continue to have their doubts.]
RC: Let's start this off as a new day, boys. [Insomniac then breaks the silence by sticking his hand out, the two men shaking hands. After a moment and some coaxing, Cage also shakes
Crane's
hand. The crowd responds with a mega-pop for the three men, as they all raise their arms into the air. They start to leave together, but
Crane once
again holds them back, still not finished speaking.]
RC: A new alliance has been born... and I think we've all seen tonight the men who have been targeted. Sex Icons... it's time for you to face this
_new_ revolution. The odds are now even... so bring the fight. [With that, "Revolution Is My Name" starts up once again as the crowd cheers loudly. The three men once again take a moment to celebrate with the crowd, before exiting the ring. The camera stays inside the ring, getting last shots of the Sex Icons lying on the mat before we fade.]
