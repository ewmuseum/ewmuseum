VWF — Show

Holiday Havoc I

November 19, 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Vegas Wrestling Federation presents HOLIDAY HAVOC! .. .. .. (The VWF logo blazes onto the screen as Viva Las Vegas by the Dead Kennedys plays. The faces of some of stars who have left the VWF appear on the screen then we cut to JD Southerns at the desk) SOUTHERNS: Welcome to Holiday Havoc! The VWF is holding cards for the first time ever during this time of the year. Usually we are off for the big tournament, but not this year. And some of our stars are in the tournament and will still be competing in the rings of the VWF, and we kicked things off tonight with a great match between two of those men. .. .. .. ============ IYASU SAKU (vs) PADDY O'SHEA ============ SOUTHERNS: Both Saku and O'Shea will be part of the 12 Days Tournament. This was a good battle and the crowd up for many near falls. O'Shea hit a RUNNING POWERSLAM that almost got him the win. While Saku connected on a T-BONE SUPLEX that nearly got O'Shea the losers portion of the purse. But Saku settled in and began to work on the shoulder of the former boxer. Saku then locked on the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE, but O'Shea rolled him up into a variation of the SMALL PACKAGE and nearly got the fall. Lets pick up the action right after O'Shea used those big fists to put Saku on the canvas. .. .. .. DTMA: Crowd seems to be behind O'Shea! WEST: The Irishman off the ropes. .. . . LEGDROP!!! DTMA: He got him! ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HERE COMES THE RIDERS OF THE APOCALYPSE!!!!!! WEST: O'Shea on his feet! DTMA: Sebastian, Gangsta and Caldwell in the ring! WEST: Gangsta shoves O'Shea! DTMA: And Sebastian and Caldwell go to work on Saku!!! WEST: The Riders said he really needed this win. DMTA: And now all three Riders are beating down Iyasu Saku! WEST: O'Shea up .. . . AND TO THE RESCUE!!!!! DTMA: Paddy O'Shea using those fists to clear the ring! WEST: IT'S THE RODEO BOYS!!! OUT OF THE CROWD!!! DTMA: BONES AND KIDD HAVE JUST JUMPED O'SHEA!!! WEST: What the hell is going on? DMTA: And look at Sebastian, at the smile on his face! WEST: I think this was planned! .. . . DOUBLE EIGHT SECOND RIDE!!!!! DTMA: Here come the Mighty Men! WEST: That clears the ring! .. .. .. (Back to JD) SOUTHERNS: The Rodeo Boys walked back up the aisle with The Riders. It looks like the Rodeos are now part of the Riders of the Apocalypse. Next up was a singles match, but it had a strange twist as well. .. .. .. ============= MARK MCQUEEN (vs) THE RECKONING ============= SOUTHERNS: With both men in the ring, Kingston Cash came out. Lets listen to what he had to say. .. .. .. KINGSTON CASH: Ladies and gentlemen I have just been informed that Randall Crane has been unavoidably delayed in getting here tonight. Which leaves The Salvation without an opponent. So I thought. How about a tag team title match? Yes, Mark. I know, your partner has been released. So you can defend on your own! .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: But that brought out Paddy O'Shea and The Salvation. O'Shea took the mic from Cash. .. .. .. PADDY O'SHEA: So I'm hearing ya right? You're bout to give my two big men a shot at the tag team titles? CASH: That's right. PADDY O'SHEA: And it will be a handi-cap match since McQueen doesn't have a partner? CASH: That's right! (O'Shea hands the mic to The Salvation) THE SALVATION: We, DO NOT ACCEPT! (MAJOR SWERVE POP!!!) CASH: What??? THE SALVATION: There is nothing honorable about two men beating up one man. Someday The Mighty Men will be tag team champions, but not today and not like this! .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: And with that O'Shea and The Salvation walked off. And the singles match began. The fans got another great back and forth battle. The Tazmanian Devil was much smaller but like the old saying goes, its not the size of the dog in the fight. The Reckoning hit a MASSIVE POWERBOMB, then went to the top rope, lets pick it up there. .. .. .. WEST: The Reckoning leaps .. . . SENTON BOMB!!!! DTMA: MISSES!!!! WEST: McQueen got out of the way! DMTA: DEVILS SNARE!! DEVILS SNARE!!!!! WEST: The big man is hurting! DTMA: AND HE TAPS!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match, The Tazmanian Devil Mark McQueen! WEST: And look at this! DTMA: McQueen lets go of the hold and helps up The Reckoning. And he even raises his hand! (HUUUGE APPRECIATION POP!!!!) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS, one for the 12 Days Tournament and then another for the Mark Adams Cup ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So the wild night continued, it was time for the eight man elimination match. .. .. .. .. ============================ THE RHOMBUS (vs) THE RIDERS OF THE APOCALYPSE ============================ SOUTHERNS: Like much of the VWF, this was chaos. But the first fall came fast on CAPSMAN who got hit by the DOUBLE EIGHT SECOND RIDE of the Rodeo Boys. From there The Riders worked on Sonicman. The former Submission Champ Sonic fought as best he could but it was Sebastian who got the fall on him, using the LEGEND KILLER. EVIL C hit the ring and he was quickly dispatched by a QUEENZ CRUSHA tombstone from Gangsta while the three others began the beatdown of The Maquilladora. The Rodeo Boys hit the DOUBLE EIGHT SECOND RIDE on Maq and it looked bleak, until Sebastian decided to stop the pinfall and hit the LEGEND KILLER. Maq BACK BODY DROPPED Sebastian then hit a SOLID SPINNING HEEL KICK to Billy Bones, Bones slammed into Kidd and both Rodeo Boys hit the floor. The Maquilladora then covered Gangsta and got the fall. Then Maq hit PERMANENT DAYLIGHT on Sebastian, but Kidd broke up the fall. For his trouble, Kidd got pinned by Maq in a SMALL PACKAGE. When Maq got to his feet, Bones went for a MISSLE DROPKICK but missed, giving The Maquilladora another fall. And it was suddenly a one on one for the win. And The Maquilladora wasted no time in hitting another PERMANENT DAYLIGHT on Sebastian. Lets to the call, the fans going wild as Maq makes the cover! .. .. .. DTMA: ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! MAQ STANDS UP???????????? WEST: Here comes the mystery woman! DTMA: She has been haunting him! WEST: And The Maquilladora cannot take his eyes off her. DTMA: This is like watching some soap opera! Look at the way they are looking at each other. WEST: CALDWELL!! CALDWELL OUT OF THE CROWD! DTMA: And he has the TAZER! ^^^^^ TAZER SHOCK ^^^^^ WEST: Caldwell is better than a Florida cop! DTMA: Maq down! WEST: And Sebastian in for the easy cover. DTMA: ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winners of this match, The Riders of the Apocalypse! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Who is this mystery woman? And why does seem to want to see The Maquilladora lose at every turn? Hopefully we will find out soon. Well Adam Jennings was able to hold onto the 24 Hour Title during the Rage In The Cage match at Phear Phantastic, but he seems to have made an enemy out of Robbie Caldwell. They met one on one, with the title on the line! But first lets see what the champ had to say earlier tonight. .. .. .. (A black Mercedes pulls into the parking garage and VWF's own 'Lean' Darlene Orion runs up, looking to interview the driver...the 24-hour man himself...Adam "The Hype" Jennings) LDO: Adam! Can you give us an update on the status of the Society of Doom? (The 6'6" Miami natice stops dead in his tracks and looks down at the lovely interviewer) Jennings: Status? Hell, I don't have a clue. No Simmons. No Bass. No O'Malley. Just me and the little big man to take care of business as usual tonight, and for no telling how long. Domination. That's our game, baby. And we play to win. LDO: And tonight you square off against a man who could be the hottest young talent in the VWF today in Robbie Caldwell. Any strategy you plan on implementing to keep the 24-Hour title around your waist? (Again, Jennings just looks at her. This time, he looks as if he is about to say something and then stops. He thinks again and them motions her to come closer. As she does, he puts and arm around her and pulls her...and the microphone...closer) Jennings: And, uhm, exactly what am I? I'm not exactly on the verge of Social Security just yet, baby. I mean, you sure as hell won't complaining the other night. So, I got a couple years on the guyl in age. Maybe four years in experience. And I'm just a lifetime better than he'll ever be! (HUGE FACE POP) (Jennings grins as he hears the crowds reaction, and decides to carry it a bit farther) Jennings: So, what does everyone else think? Who's here to see the shockmaster, Roddy Codswell? (SERIOUS HEEL HEAT) Jennings: And who wants to see....the aisle walkin, smack talkin, ass beatin, woman treatin ONE AND ONLY HYPE!!! (The crowd comes unglued with cheers and chants of "HYPE" as Jennings grins at Darlene once more and then turns and heads into the SoD locker room) .. .. .. =============================== VWF 24 Hour Title Match ADAM THE HYPE JENNINGS © (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL =============================== SOUTHERNS: Insiders are saying these are two future VWF stars, like in the CJB or Randall Crane mode. That remains to be seen, but Jennings has quickly taken the company by storm. Defeating Kevin Blair for the title and then holding off Caldwell. Most fans were wondering how the recent loss of the SOD would factor in to this match. Caldwell came to the ring with the other four members of the Riders. Jennings came to the ring with Mark McQueen. But as soon as it looked like this was going to turn into a five on two beatdown, Paddy O'Shea and The Mighty Men walked to ringside and evened up the odds. Though no punches were thrown the tension at ringside was thick as Caldwell and Jennings finally started the match. Both men fought for the upper hand and it was Caldwell taking over and using solid holds to wear down The Hype. Jennings battle back and continued this battle as time ticked by. All the while the Riders, McQueen, O'Shea and the Mighty Men would mix it up. Lets pick it up just after Caldwell hit a CRADLE PILEDRIVER on Jennings. .. .. .. DTMA: Adam Jennings is in trouble. WEST: Caldwell placing Jennings on the top rope! DTMA: And this is why Caldwell needs more ring time, instead of going for the finish he is mugging for the crowd! WEST: Mr Electricity pumping up the crowd for the Shock and Horror! Nothing wrong with that! DTMA: Caldwell over. .. . . JENNING OFF THE TOP ROPE!!! .. . . BIG CLOTHESLINE BY JENNINGS!!!!! WEST: Adam Jennings back in the mix! DTMA: HYPER DRIVER!!!!! WEST: The Riders trying to get in the ring and McQueen, O'Shea and The Mighty Men trying to stop them! DTMA: Gangsta on the ring apron! WEST: And Jennings over and he blasts Gangsta! DTMA: LOW BLOW by Caldwell!!! Robbie Caldwell had enough to get him! WEST: Gangsta in the ring! DTMA: Sebastian has the attention of the ref! WEST: Caldwell holding Jennings, for the .. . . BUCKSHOT!!!!! .. . . JENNINGS MOVED!!!! DTMA: GANGSTA CLOCKED CALDWELL!!!! WEST: HYPER DRIVER TO GANGSTA!!!! DTMA: Jennings covers Caldwell!! ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match and still 24 Hour Champion, ADAM THE HYPE JENNINGS!!!! .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Of course, after that an all out brawl started between the ten men. But the factions of the SOD and the NAA fought off the Rider and sent them packing to the back, without the 24 Hour Title. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS, for the Mark Adams Cup ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Trinity walked out of Phear Phantastic with the VWF Heavyweight Title, held by The Equalizer and they gained the Submission Title, now held by Gods Tears. They also managed to break the leg of Jason Storm. So the group has done what they said they would so many months ago. But getting to the top means making enemies, and they have done that to each of the three men they met tonight. .. .. .. =================================================== TRINITY (vs) LARK FENRIZ, CLASSY JOEY BOY and FAST EDDIE CUTLASS =================================================== SOUTHERNS: With Trinity you get dirty tricks, and this night would be no different. With Fenriz and CJB in the ring, and the fans waiting for Cutlass, chanting EDDIE! EDDIE! . . . well, lets take a look at what happened. .. .. .. (The house lights dim, and the video screen lights up with a close up shot of Pagan's face. The crowd boos loudly.) "Two little blackbirds... sitting on a hill." (Both Lark Fenriz and CJB turn their attention to the video screen.) "How are we doing tonight, gentlemen? Good I hope. Things are going just fine here as well. In fact, everything seems to be going so well that it really pains me to have to change our plans at the last minute." (The boos of the crowd grow to deafening levels in fear of a now canceled match.) "Unfortunately, things have come up. Your third man will be unable to make it for your match tonight." (The camera pulls out to reveal Pagan standing in front of "Fast" Eddie Cutlass. Eddie appears to have been beaten badly, and he is currently tied down to a cross. The reaction from the crowd is negative to say the least.) "Forgive Me Father... For I Am Sin." (A devilish grin etches its way across Pagan's face as Fenriz and CJB stand in the ring and look on.) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: From there The Equalizer and GT came out of the crowd, Gods Tears had a sledgehammer (weapon of choice of CJB in the past) and The Equalizer had a canoe paddle (a past weapon of Fenriz) What happened next was to bloody and violent to even show on our program. Las Vegas Metro Police were called when VWF and Hard Rock security were not able to stop the onslaught of Trinity. Eddie Cutlass, Lark Fenriz and Classy Joey Boy were each taken to UMC Medical Center and all three members of Trinity were arrested. Ladies and gentlemen, there has never been anything like this in the history of the VWF. As of this taping Kingston Cash does not yet have an official statement. But some wonder how might this influence the possible lawsuit from Y2K? Until next time.. © 2004 Vegas Wrestling Federation
