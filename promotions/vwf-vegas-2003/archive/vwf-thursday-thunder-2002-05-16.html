Lets go to the action held earlier tonight that has this crowd so pumped up! *********************************************** TYSON T-BONE YOUNG vs ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS *********************************************** The fans have taken to Young, or maybe they just hate Andrews. Either way this was a back and forth match for the first ten minutes until Andrews took the upper hand with his signature series of suplexes. Andrews sent Young to the ropes and it turned out to be a big mistake as Tyson came off and nailed his spear called THE STREET for the upset win. RESULT- Tyson T-Bone Young by pinfall ******************************* THE REAL DEAL vs THOMAS RAINKA ******************************* These two former T3 Champions were looking for much needed wins. TRD came out to his new entrance tune, Negative Creep by Nirvana and took the fight to Rainka from the begining. The Real Deal showed a new kind of power and overall toughness from his recent matches. Rainka missed the ONLY ENDING and TRD hit the DONE DEAL but seemed to snap as he took a chair to Rainka over and over again forcing the ref to DQ TRD. RESULT- Thomas Rainka by DQ After the battle as TRD walked up the walkway the fans began to chant (Tee Three! Tee Three!) Hmmmm. What could that mean? And after came the grudge match between two former partners.. ***************************** PADDY O'SHEA vs DIRK WESTERN ***************************** VWF historians will remember that these two became allies at the very first VWF pay per view, Vegas Violence. Western has always blamed O'Shea for their lack of success and he took it out on the sobering up Paddy O'Shea. Western was on his way to an easy win when Paddy brother, Father Frank O'Shea walked to ringside to cheer on his little brother. Paddy took the cheers and let them fill him with a new found energy and he dropped Western with a series of right hands. From there it was THE ONE TWO, the big left hand to the big legdrop and Paddy O'Shea scored the win!
