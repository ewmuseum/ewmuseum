VWF — Show

Wrestle Style V.1

August 16, 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<In the Society locker room, Adam Jennings comes storming in, a rage of anger upon him.> Kagedo: Adam, what's the problem? Jennings: It's that damn Cash! He had security waiting for me at the entrance, I'm barred from the arena tonight because it seems the results of my physical haven't come in just yet. He was sorry to have to inform me. <The Dragon chuckles softly to himself as he begins to dawn an enormous knee brace.> Kagedo: Yeah, that sounds like our boy Cash alright. That's ok, Adam. We'll take care of those thugs tonight...next week, you'll have your shot. You just be sure to take care of business then. <Jennings nods unhappily, grabs his bag and leaves. As he does, Lindsay Simmons walks into the locker room. Kagedo doesn't even look up at her.> Kagedo: What the hell do you want? Lindsay: Kingston, errr, I mean Mr Cash would like to see you. Kagedo: Yeah, I'll be right there. Lindsay: No, he wants you there now. Let's go. <Kagedo looks up from the harness at the double-crossing bitch that has tried to make the lives of his friends a living hell. For a long moment, they stare one another down. A look of defiance in his eyes, a look of triumph in hers. Finally....> Kagedo: Alright. Let's go see what the boss has to say then. .. .. .. (Hey Ya by Outkast hits and the camera focus in on six girls on the VWF who are dancing. A flash of pyro go off all around them) VOICE OVER: Welcome to the new VWF! Ladies and gentlemen please welcome to the ring. . . .. . . The VWF Wrestle Babes! Here on the VWF show, .. . . Wrestle Style V.1! (The six girls run down the aisle, climb in the ring and begin a choreographed dance. We fade to the VWF desk where JD Southerns is sitting) SOUTHERNS: You saw it right folks, Kingston Cash has decided on making the show a little different, and the fans got their first taste of that. So now we have Wrestle Babes and the show is called Wrestle Style V.1. Hmmm But the fans had filled the Joint to see the fallout from Birthday Bash. And it started right off the bat with a grudge match that many had seen coming since these two men tried to forge a friendship. .. .. .. ========== SONICMAN (vs) SEBASTIAN ========== SOUTHERNS: Sebastian was the author of the brutal post match assault on The Maquilladora after Maq won back the Submission title at Birthday Bash, and Sonicman hit the ring like a man possessed in order to exact some revenge. Sonic kept moving and would hit Sebastian with moves off the ropes to keep The Star Maker off his game. But Sebastian is one of the craftier veterans of the VWF and got an EYE GOUGE in when the ref was distracted. From there Sebastian went about his methodical self, working on the small of the back of Sonicman for the next four minutes of the match. Sebastian then got Sonic in the SEX YOU OUT Boston Crab, but the Rhombus member fought off for as long as he could, but eventually it was to much. Lets go the ring for the end of this match. .. .. .. DTMA: And Sonicman taps out! WEST: Was there ever any doubt? DTMA: And Sebastian breaks the hold and, and he is getting out of the ring! What is he doing? WEST: Sebastian has a CHAIR! DTMA: The Legend Killer is putting it on the mat! WEST: He is going to put some hurt on Sonicman! DMTA: He is setting him up! .. . . LEGEND KILLER!!! WEST: ONTO THE CHAIR!!!!! SEBASTIAN STOPS!!!!!!! (MASSIVE POP!!!!) DMTA: THE MAQUILLADORA RUSHING TO THE RING!!!!!! WEST: BACK BODY DROP BY SONICMAN!!! DTMA: And The Maquilladora is in the ring with Sonic! WEST: And Sebastian is slinking back up the ramp! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So Sebastian gets the win, but I don't think things between him and The Maquilladora are over. Speaking of Maq, he was scheduled to face off with Kenny Kidd, but Kidd called the Joint about an hour before the card and told VWF officials that he would be unable to make it due to personal reasons. I don't know what is up with the Rodeo Boys but things have really taken a turn for them since being stripped of the tag team titles. Fans did get to see a newcomer though. .. .. .. ============================= MR ELECTICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL (vs) ROB KIDANE ============================= SOUTHERNS: Kidane is known to VWF fans as a former student of the VWF academy, but Caldwell was brand new to the fed. Mr Electricity hit the ring and dominated Kidane. Caldwell is near six seven and very well built. He tossed Kidane around and finished the match by placing Kidane on the top rope and hitting an exploder suplex. A move he calls SHOCK AND HORROR! It was good enough for the 1-2-3 and the win for the newcomer. Mr Electricity Robbie Caldwell, a name to remember as we move forward. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (FADE IN on the VWF desk) SOUTHERNS: Fans were not happy as the tag team champions came to the ring, lets see what they had to say! .. .. .. (Blaze and Real Deal are in the ring lifting up the titles. Blaze calls for the mic) BLAZE: There has been a lot of talk since Birthday Bash. Talk that Real Deal and I do not deserve these titles! (HUUGE DAMN RIGHT POP!) BLAZE: We are the greatest team in the VWF today! We might be the greatest team in the history of the VWF! But we are not going to just talk about it, we are going to prove it! Week in and week out! Real Deal and I have decided we are going to defend these titles every week until Vegas Violence! (SOUNDS GOOD TO US POP!!!) BLAZE: We consider this an open challenge to any team in the world! Because we know the truth! We know the reality! There is no team out there who wants to face us! No team who has the guts to walk down that aisle! REAL DEAL: And that folks. . . .. . . IS THE REAL. . . (Back in Black hits and the crowd erupts) DTMA: I think someone is coming to accept that challenge! WEST: You are very perceptive. (Marcus O'Malley and Mark McQueen come out from behind the curtain and walk to the ring) WEST: The Society of Doom, when is Kingston Cash going to finally get rid of these guys? DTMA: I hope never. (O'Malley and McQueen get in the ring and look at the titles on the shoulders of the champs. Then they look into the eyes of Blaze and Real Deal) MARCUS O'MALLEY: We accept! (HUUUUGE POP!!!!) (The SOD team turns and begins to leave the ring, Blaze and Real Deal make a move for the sneak attack but get nailed by CLOTHESLINES from the SOD guys) .. .. .. (BACK to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So next week, Blaze and Real Deal will be putting up the titles against the SOD! But the night was not over for the Society of Doom, they still had a big match but Kagedo was called into the office of Kingston Cash before the battle could begin. Lets take a look. .. .. .. <Kagedo walks into Cash's office, and promptly lounges on the black leather sofa in front of his desk; a smirk on his face. The VWF owner glares him down and both men obviously detest the other to the extreme.> Kagedo: So, you wanted to see me? Cash: Please, make yourself at home. Kagedo: Don't mind if I do. <Cash smirks and pulls a file from his desk drawer.> Cash: I just got off the phone with Dr James Andrews. He had some interesting things to say about your condition. <Kagedo stiffens and the smirk falls from his face> Kagedo: Like what? Cash: Well, he said that your knee isn't quite up to the physical rigors of actual in-ring performance just yet. Your medical file just came in via fax. You've not been medically cleared for the match tonight. Kagedo: So what the hell are you saying? Cash: Well, what it boils down to is this. Jennings can't compete because he's not on the active roster yet. You know how things are in personnel. And you've not been cleared by your surgeon to return to competition. So it just looks like it'll only be a nine-man tag match tonight. Kagedo: I could only have guessed you'd try something like this. Cash: Now hold on here. I'm not happy about this at all. We've got an arena full of Society fanatics out there who want to see the...legend. I can't just turn them away. <The Dragon looks over Cash with a glance of caution> Kagedo: So what do you have in mind? Cash: Well, if you were to sign a waiver releasing the VWF from all liability to any permanent injuries you will... <Cash coughs loudly and grins> Cash: I'm sorry, you MIGHT have....then I could see fit to let you compete. <The SoD's enforcer looks at the paper lying on the desk waiting for his signature. A plan begins to develop in his mind...> Kagedo: I don't know if I can do that. I mean, I do have my future to consider. Cash: What's that? Kagedo: Well, if I were to be seriously hurt out there...whats in it for me? <Cash grins. The time for bargaining has come> Kagedo: Here's my offer. I'll sign your damn waiver. Next week, you and me. One on one. If I win, you've got a new partner! Cash: And when..I mean, if I win? Kagedo: A one month suspension. Without pay. Cash: One year. Kagedo: Three months. Cash: Six months. Kagedo: Done. <Kagedo stoops to sign the form, and as he does his right knee buckles a bit and he nearly collapses> Cash: You, my friend, have just signed your own doom. Kagedo: And you, my partner, have just accepted a trip to hell. <Cash stands up and the two men are almost nose to nose now, obvious hate in their eyes as the stare one another down. Cash puts out his hand, and after a pause Kagedo takes it.> Cash: Now, get your ass to the ring. You've got a match. Kagedo: I couldn't ask for more. Partner. <With a quick turn, he leaves the office. Cash sits back behind his desk, an odd and sadistic grin on his face> Cash: Dragon, you have no idea. <He throws his head back and begins to laugh with delight> .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Well lets go to the ten man tag match and see how things went down. .. .. .. =================== THE SOCIETY OF DOOM (vs) THE NEW AGE ARMY =================== SOUTHERNS: The long standing problems between these two teams exploded in this match. The ref could not get any kind of control and it quickly became more like a tornado match. But as the battles moved on, as Kevin Simmons nailed Iyasu Saku. As Mark McQueen fought with The Reckoning. It was The Legend of Xtreme and Blackbird who managed to isolate Kags on the outside of the ring and work over his knee with a viciousness not seen in a while from either of them. At the six minute mark, The Outlaw Morris Bass had a chance and he took it, lets go to the call. .. .. .. WEST: Bass has The Salvation! DMTA: CHOKESLAM!!!! WEST: Simmons has Iyasu Saku in the Durhan Deahtlock!! DMTA: Bass covers ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: Another win for the SOD! DTMA: McQueen and O'Malley have chased off TLOX and Blackbird but there might have been some damage done to Kagedo. (Money hits and the 75% owner of the VWF Kingston Cash walks out to the ring, he climbs in. The NAA in the ring, the SOD on the outside) KINGSTON CASH: I am not in the mood to drag this out. I am here to finally rid this company of its dead weight! I am here to fire, THE NEW AGE ARMY!!! (SWERVE POP!) KINGSTON CASH: (to the SOD) You guys need to make your way to the back! (turns to Blackbird and her group) You know what Blackbird? I have been watching the old tapes of the VWF. And it is time to move on! This company has been holding on to the past! That is why we have a new name for the show, new Wrestle Babes, and that is why I will be making some serious personnel changes over the next few weeks. And my first one is the New Age Army is GONE! .. .. .. (FADE back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: That's right, the New Age Army has been let go. The rumors are rampant in the back, Kingston Cash wants to build a new VWF and not everyone is invited. Well the boss had another bad idea. Lets take a look. .. .. .. (Kingston Cash is in the ring, shortly after security had cleared out the NAA) KINGSTON CASH: Now, some of you know that the oldest title in the VWF is the 24 Hour Title. It was on August 9 2001 that Sebastian was crowned the first ever 24 Hour Champion and since then many of the greatest stars of the VWF have fought for and won the title. I would like the current 24 Hour Champion to come out here. Kevin Blair! Please come to the ring! (Last Resort hits and Blair walks to the ring clutching the title to his chest) KINGSTON CASH: Kevin, you have been champ for a long time. These fans love you. (CHEAP POP FOR BLAIR!) KINGSTON CASH: I have some news for you. As of tonight, the VWF no longer recognizes the 24 Hour Title! (NOOOOO! POP!!!!!!!!!!!) DTMA: He can't do that! WEST: He is going to try! KINGSTON CASH: So Blair, give me that filthy old strap so we put it some museum! (Cash reaches for the title but Blair pushes him away with one hand while never dropping the title) KINGSTON CASH: Do not do something you are going to regret kid. It is over. The 24 Hour Title is a relic, like the NAA or The Ace Jack Spade. It is a thing of the past. Now hand it over. (Again Cash tries, this time Blair shoves the boss to the ground) KINGSTON CASH: (stands back up) Think about it, if you give it me you can forever say you were the last 24 Hour Champion, as if it means something. Now give it.. DTMA: BLAIR KICKS THE BOSS IN THE GUT!!! WEST: PYSCHO FLOW DDT!!!!!! (FADE with Blair standing over Cash, stroking the title and looking at it with a strange affection) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Fans were still buzzing about the 24 Hour Title situation as the competitors for the next match made their way to the ring. .. .. .. ================================ CAPSMEN! (vs) ORION THE HUNTER & THE EQUALIZER ================================ SOUTHERNS: Tensions between Orion and E have been at an even higher pitch since Birthday Bash, but it was not noticeable during the course of this match. Like a machine Orion and The Equalizer picked apart the superhero team. Quick tags, double team moves and an all around smart game plan had this unlikely team in control for most of the match. EVIL C did catch Orion off guard at one point, but The Equalizer hit him from behind and brought the offense of EVIL C to an end. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: EVIL C is in some trouble! WEST: The Equalizer tags Orion in. DTMA: AND A FACE PLANT BY THE EQUALIZER!!!! WEST: And here comes CAPSMAN! DMTA: The Equalizer there to meet him, and stop him! WEST: And Orion puts EVIL C in the DRUNKEN RAGE!!! DMTA: And EVIL C taps! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: But Orion is not letting go! Orion is really trying to hurt EVIL C! (HUUUGE HERE COMES THE MAQUILLADORA POP!!!!) DTMA: And The Maquilladora, followed by Sonicman hit the ring and Orion and E clear off. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: All that was left was the main event. Four of the top names in the VWF in a tag team match. But before the bell could ring, Flash demanded the mic! .. .. .. ================================ LARK FENRIZ & FLASH (vs) JASON STORM & FAST EDDIE CUTLASS ================================ .. .. .. Flash: Ladies and gentlemen, if I please have your attention. I'm afraid that I have some very sad news to give to you. This is actually very serious, so I'm just asking that you give me a moment to speak. [The majority of the crowd is silent.] Flash: It saddens me to announce that there will be no Flashback this week. [The fans erupt in a chorus of cheers.] Flash: Jason Storm decided that he didn't care about his loved ones enough to fulfill his obligations to me, so next week I will do what I have to do. Jason, if you know what's good for you... [And then suddenly...] WHAM!!! DTMA: Jason Storm just tackled Flash, and he's beating the hell out of him!!! .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: And the match was on. All four men hit some high spots and all four were on the end of brutal assaults. Jason Storm seemed to be as happy beating up Flash as he did Lark Fenriz and he and Cutlass worked well as a team. Fenriz and Flash had a few minor arguments but they held their own. After seven minutes of brutal action, Cutlass and Fenriz were in the ring when Cutlass hit THE GUARANTEE. But Flash quickly came in and made the save. Storm entered the ring and he CLOTHESLINED Flash and himself over the top rope and their momentum took then into the crowd. Cutlass got to his feet and pulled up the hurting Fenriz and sent him to the ropes, but The Wolf reversed it and in an ugly fall Cutlass ended up caught in the ropes. With Storm and Flash going deeper into the crowd Fenriz was left with all the free shots on Cutlass he could muster, and he could must a lot. Lefts and rights and the now bloody Cutlass was near unconscious and helpless. The ref tried to stop it and he was shoved aside by Fenriz. Lark got out of the ring and returned with a chair. The helpless Cutlass looked up and. . well lets see what happened. .. .. .. "Step Up" by Drowning Pool hits. The crowd turns to the entrance ramp, Lark looks confused, who's music is that, someone new?.......... (CJB comes flying out of the back with the sledgehammer.) (APE SHIT BONKERS POP!!!!!) Dan: IT"S CJB!!!! HOLY SH*T IT'S CJB!!!!! (CJB slides into the ring. Hits the chair with the sledgehammer, the chair hits Fenriz head. Fenriz goes down. CJB starts throwing lefts and rights. Fenriz rolls out of the ring, he's bloody now. Security runs down to calm everyone down. THEN........ CJB, still in the ring, picks up the sledgehammer AND notices a trapped Eddie Cutlass. CJB stares at the sledgehammer, then at Cutlass who's scarred now(only because he trapped and CJB has a free shot with a sledgehammer). The crowd is going crazy!!! Is he going to nail Cutlass? Is not?........... Dan: WE ARE OUT OF TIME! WEST: STAY HERE!!! FIVE MORE MINUTES!!!!!! .. .. .. © Vegas Wrestling Federation
