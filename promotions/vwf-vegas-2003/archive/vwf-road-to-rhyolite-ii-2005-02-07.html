VWF — Show

Road To Rhyolite II

February 7, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Viva Las Vegas hits and the screen blazes with the VWF logo, then slowly fades to JD Southerns sitting at the desk) SOUTHERNS: Welcome to Road To Rhyolite II, and was it a great show held yesterday afternoon just before the Super Bowl. The show started with Kingston Cash taking center ring to make some comments. Lets take a listen. .. .. .. KINGSTON CASH: Before we get things going here this afternoon, I want to address some things going on here in the VWF! First, I am still the majority owner of this company! I am still the man how makes the big decisions around here! First to group headed by Yancy Kilpatrick. Do you really think you can take MY company from me? Do you really think you can just blow in here with money from that Gregorson and take over? Yancy Kilpatrick, this is MY company! Next. John Tyler thinks he has control, but Tyler you are still just an glorified observer! The FBI is going to clear this company and I will be back in charge. So try and do what you can while you can Johnny boy. And Kagedo, do you think I forgot about you? You had better be ready back there. And last, Classy Joey Boy. The 25% owner. CJB, you think you are going to get in line to push me around? Me, I am still your boss! So, tonight. The Pre Gauntlet Gauntlet will kick off with Classy Joey Boy in the NUMBER 1 position and Kagedo in the number 2 position! Good luck boys! (Cash drops the mic and walks off) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: So CJB and Kags will have to kick off the PG Gauntlet. And CJB would still have to be part of the evenings fatal four way of VWF legends. But before the Four Way Legends fans got to see Scott Starring aka Henry Fish make his debut. .. .. .. ============================= SCOTT STARRING aka HENRY FISH (vs) VEGAS VIC ============================= SOUTHERNS: Fish, who is pretending to be Scott Starring came to the ring with the 12 Days Silver Medal around his neck. Now Vegas Vic, a complete unknown did not look like a wrestler at all, more like some forty something they found at the blackjack tables. Starring aka Fish had no trouble beating down Vic, mostly with fists and a few kicks and one thumb to the eye. The end was the part that had the fans up in arms. Starring aka Fish went to the top rope and hit a poorly done version of the STARFALL SPLASH!!! Looked more like the Fish Flop to me. But Starring aka Fish got the win and word is he wants a PPV match. This guy is not worthy of a PPV match. But all four men in the next match are and have headlined PPVs in the past. .. .. .. ========================================================== SEBASTIAN (vs) G-GANGSTA (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) CLASSY JOEY BOY ========================================================== SOUTHERNS: All four men were in the ring at the same time and Gangsta wanted only Sebastian. Maq and CJB squared off in the other corner, looking to renew old battles. Gangsta hit Sebastian with a series of rights and ended it with a LEG LARIAT that sent The Legend Killer out of the ring. Gangsta then rushed over and knocked Maq out of the ring with a CLOTHESLINE!! And the fans were on their feet as G-Gangsta and Classy Joey Boy stood face to face!! VWF historians remember it was CJB who screwed Gangsta way back in 2002 and these two have never really seen eye to eye since. The action was fast and intense, but Gangsta had an answer for each CJB move and the man from Queens nailed CJB with the BUCKSHOT superkick. Gangsta made the cover, but Sebastian ran in the broke up the fall. Sebastian stopped the fall with a knee to the back of the head of Gangsta, and that gave Sebastian enough to roll him out of the ring. But as Sebastian was getting to his feet he was hit with a FLYING CROSS BODY BLOCK by The Maquilladora who came over the ropes. Maq nearly scored the fall, then we saw that rare side of The Maquilladora. The extra violent side. Maq used his fists and those quick feet to get Sebastian on dream street, then he gave Sebastian a rope burn followed by a THROAT PUNCH. Maq ended the exchange with a DDT. Classy Joey Boy was back in the action and Maq caught him with a SPINNING HEEL KICK! Gangsta was up and in the ring and he SPEARED Maq. As Gangsta came off the ropes, it was Classy Joey Boy catching him in a SCOOP SLAM for a near fall. All four men were now going after each other. CJB hit Sebastian with a SPINEBUSTER at the same moment The Maquilldora nailed Gangsta with a BULLDOG!! CJB and Maq scrambled to their feet and ended up face to face with each other. The staredown lasted only for ten seconds or so but it was clearly a psychological challenge for their upcoming match at Ghost Town Blues. Sebastian crawled over and nailed both men with a LOW BLOW!!! Lets go to the call, just moments after double testicular misfourtune. .. .. .. DTMA: Sebastian is strutting around this ring like he just won the title. BEAUTY: He is the only one standing, sometimes in this business that is reason enough to strut! DTMA: Sebastian said he was going to pin CJB tonight, and he pulls the Team Captain up! BEAUTY: Sebastian about to make good on his word! DTMA: Double underhook, can only mean one thing, .. . . (Lean Darlene Orion is standing backstage with G-Gangsta) LDO: So you scored the big win here tonight over Sebastian. GANGSTA: Dar, the man has been running at the mouth for four years now. I decided it was time to shut up and then put up! You can only your mouth so long, at some time you have to step up! LDO: So, you only have one match left in your long career. What are you thinking going into the cage with Sebastian? GANGSTA: That the time to shut him up is February 15. Yes, it will be the last time I step into a ring, but it is time to stand down. Lots of young bucks, and punks, in the VWF and they have the reins now. I am the Old G, time for the Young G's to have the spotlight. But don't get me wrong, at Ghost Town Blues, one last time. Gangsta and Sebastian! Do you really think I plan on walking out on the losing end. (Gangsta walks off) LDO: No G, I don't. .. .. .. (FADE to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: It is a sad time for the VWF, both Sebastian and Gangsta will retire after their match at Ghost Town Blues. Also at GTB, The Equalizer will defend his title against Classy Joey Boy and The Maquilladora in a triple threat match. Whoever leaves Rhyolite with the title will most likely come face to face with the winner of the PG Gauntlet. The Gauntlet PPV comes later this year and being last to the ring is the best place to be. It is no guarantee but it doesn't hurt so there is a lot on the line in our PG Gauntlet match! Every two minutes a new VWF superstar hits the ring. .. .. .. ========================= THE PRE GAUNTLET GAUNTLET ========================= SOUTHERNS: Last year Lark Fenriz won the PG Gauntlet, and last year it was for the vacant VWF Heavyweight Title. This year the prize is the possibility of winning the title at The Gauntlet itself. (*) Classy Joey Boy hit the ring (*) (*) Kagedo hit the ring (*) Kagedo and CJB both knew they were being set up by Kingston Cash, so in a strange move the two men shook hands and then turned and waited. After one minute an enraged Kingston Cash walked out. Lets listen to what he had to say. .. .. .. KINGSTON CASH: So you two think you are so smart??? Referee, if these two do not mix it up by your ten count they are both eliminated. And they will both be banned from Ghost Town Blues! .. .. .. (BACK to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So CJB had no choice, waiting would mean losing his title shot at Ghost Town Blues. CJB spun and the action began! He took the fight right to Kagedo, using some KNIFE EDGE CHOPS to back Kags into the corner, and an IRISH WHIP to the opposite corner. But the clock had run down. (*) Lark Fenriz hit the ring (*) Fenriz joined the action! Lark hit the ring and was right after CJB! Fenriz and CJB have never really got the chance to settle things after the attack at last years Ghost Town Blues. Kagedo got in the action with a CORKSCREW BODY PRESS that nearly sent CJB to the floor! Fenriz ducked a Kagedo shot and hit him with a GERMAN SUPLEX!! CJB rolled back in off the apron and came off the ropes, only to get caught in a CHOKESLAM by Fenriz! (*) Tazmanian Devil Mark McQueen hit the ring (*) McQueen was not cautious at all, he hit the ring like a.. well a Tazmanian Devil! McQueen dropped each man with a hard right hand then he sent Kagedo to the ropes hitting a BACK BODY DROP!! Fenriz used a FOREARM SHIVER to rattle McQueen. Fenriz and McQueen fought while Classy Joey Boy was trying to throw Kagedo out in the corner. (*) EVIL CAPSMAN hit the ring (*) EVIL C took his time coming to the ring, taking in the whole battle before making his move. EVIL C joined the battle right after CJB hit a DDT on Kagedo, EVIL C came in over the ropes and hit a SENTON BOMB on CJB! Meanwhile McQueen had used a DROP TOE HOLD into the second turnbuckle to slow the powerful Lark Fenriz! (*) Lance Voltar hit the ring (*) The Winless Wonder hit the ring and went right after EVIL CAPSMAN! Voltar drove EVIL C back into one corner then jupmped up and hit a HURRICANRANA on EVIL C. It was Voltar going onto the ring apron and EVIL C hitting the floor! ===== EVIL CAPSMAN has been eliminated ===== On the other side of the ring, McQueen nearly had Fenriz over the ropes when Kagedo ran over and DROPKICKED both men. Fenriz hit the apron and McQueen hit the floor! ===== Tazmanian Devil Mark McQueen has been eliminated ===== (*) CAPSMAN hit the ring (*) CAPS came to the ring and slid in as if he was going after Voltar, but then just posed! Voltar kicked in the groin and nailed the VWF superhero with a SWINGING NECKBREAKER!!! Classy Joey Boy targeted Lark Fenriz as Kagedo came off the ropes, only to get HIP TOSSED by Lance Voltar! (*) Sidious The Clown hit the ring (*) New Submission champ Sid came to the ring and he joined the fun by tackling Lance Voltar! Sid sat on Voltars face much to the enjoyment of the crowd. CJB then hit a TORNADO DDT on Lark Fenriz! Sid pulled up Voltar while Kagedo was doing the same with CAPSMAN! Sid and Kags tossed Voltar and CAPS out over the same side of the ring! ===== Lance Voltar has been eliminated ===== ===== CAPSMAN has been eliminated ===== But Voltar quickly jumped up on the ring apron, and hit his BIG V legdrop on CAPSMAN!!! Voltar then covered CAPS and counted the fall himself. (*) Billy Bones hit the ring (*) Bones came in not looking to get involved with anyone, since the rise of Kenny Kidd his partner Bones has been reluctant to say the least. But Bones got hit by a CJB CLOTHESLINE that quickly knocked Bones out of the ring! ===== Billy Bones has been eliminated ===== Classy Joey Boy had a sudden rush, racing around the ring taking everyone down! Kags, Sid, Fenriz! Then Classy Joey Boy turned to Lark Fenriz who was getting to his feet near the far corner. And to an explosive POP CJB hit Fenriz with the KISS OF DEATH superkick and Fenriz went over and out of the ring! ===== Lark Fenriz has been eliminated ===== CJB spun around sidestep a charging Sidious The Clown, then hit him with an ENZIGUIRI to the back of the head! That sent Sid over the top rope and on top of Lark Fenriz! ===== Sidious The Clown has been eliminated ===== (*) Kenny Kidd hit the ring (*) But CJB never saw Kenny Kidd coming to the ring! And as CJB looked to throw out Kagedo, Kidd hit him from behind! Kenny Kidd hit CJB with a GUTBUSTER before hitting Kagedo with a FLYING SHOULDERBLOCK!! Kenny Kidd was on his feet and enjoying himself! He grabbed CJB and quickly slapped on the ELKO LASSO anklelock!! Though Kidd could not win if CJB tapped, it was clear Kidd had scouted CJB. Classy Joey Boy had been in battles with Randall Crane and CJB had always had trouble with the anklelock. CJB was saved by Kagedo. Kags then hit Kidd with an EXPLODER SUPLEX!!! (*) Adam The Hype Jennings hit the ring (*) The 24 Hour Champ ran in and was nailed by Kagedo coming off the ropes. Jennings and Kags began to mix it up but the fresher Jennings got the upper hand and used a SIT OUT POWERSLAM to take full control. Meanwhile Kenny Kidd was back to working on the ankle of CJB! But the former VWF heavyweight champ rolled Kidd and flipped him over the top rope and to the floor! ===== Kenny Kidd has been eliminated ===== Kagedo was nailed by the HYPER DRIVER!! And Jenning was set to throw Kags out when CJB made the save. Classy Joey Boy stopped Jennings but The Hype reversed an attempted VERTICAL SUPLEX into a VERTICAL SUPLEX of his own that saw CJB suplexed out of the ring and to the floor! ===== Classy Joey Boy has been eliminated ===== Jennings turned and got caught in the DRAGON SUPLEX of Kagedo!!!! Both men were lying in the center of the ring as the next man came down. (*) Fast Eddie Cutlass hit the ring (*) Cutlass got in the ring and Kagedo and Jennings were still lying there. Cutlass picked up Kagedo and hit him with a STANDING DROPKICK that nearly sent him out of the ring. Cutlass then charged Kags who was held up by the ropes only to have Kagedo move and Cutlass go to the arena floor! ===== Fast Eddie Cutlass has been eliminated ===== Adam Jennings was getting to his feet and Kagedo hit him with a SCISSORS KICK!! Kags then took Jennings over and tossed him out of the ring! ===== Adam The Hype Jennings has been eliminated ===== And Kagedo had 20 seconds of rest before the next man hit the ring. (*) Paddy O'Shea hit the ring (*) The Irishman came to the ring with a big smile for Kagedo, O'Shea was feeling pretty good about coming in fresh while Kags had been in the match for such a long time. They stalked each other and when Kagedo went to COLLAR AND ELBOW O'Shea got him with a JAB to the chin. O'Shea used a few more to soften up Kagedo and then sent him hard into the corner. O'Shea charged in and hit a SHOULDERBLOCK on Kagedo! Paddy O'Shea then spun Kagedo around and locked him in the CHICKEN WING!!! But as O"Shea was wearing down Kags, a new man came to the ring! (*) Sonicman hit the ring (*) When O'Shea saw who was coming to the ring he let go of Kagedo. Kags rolled into the corner and O'Shea and Sonic met at center ring and exchanged punches wildly! Sonic finally got the upper hand and kicked O'Shea in the gut doubling him over, then hit him with a HIGH KNEE COMING OFF THE ROPES!!! Sonicman then slid out under the ropes and got a BEER from a nearby fan!!! Sonic chugged it as the fans cheered him on then got back in the ring. Sonic then picked up O'Shea and hit him with THE CLOSE SHAVE!!!! (*) Mr Electricity Robbie Caldwell hit the ring (*) Caldwell got in and went after Kagedo like a vulture! Sonic was back on the outside of the ring chugging another beer as Robbie Caldwell was beating down Kagedo in the corner. Caldwell then got up on the second rope and dropped a FIST into the throat of Kagedo! Fortunes turned for Caldwell as he was crushed by a RIGHT CROSS from former boxer Paddy O'Shea! O'Shea though turned into a SENTON BOMB from Sonicman who coming back in after his alcohol break!
