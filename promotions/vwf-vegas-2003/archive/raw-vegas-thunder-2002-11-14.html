The two men hit some early moves but neither man was able to gain control until the six minute mark when Latio hit a
TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER. Latio then locked on a FIGURE FOUR. Onimusha got to the ropes and but damage had been done to Onimusha. Latio got
Jaaku for a POWERBOMB into the turnbuckle ! But when he tried to do it again Onimusha turned it into a HURRICANRANA at the last second and
Latio went into the turnbuckle and was in the TREE OF WOE. One baseball slide later and Latio was in BIG trouble. But Onimusha missed
THE TOBIDOUGU and as soon as he hit the mat Latio locked on the
SEXACUTOR and Jaaku quickly tapped out.
RESULT- Phil Latio by submission. And fans got a look at the RAW Champ in non title action in the next battle! ========================== SAUL AKIVA vs FRANK EWIAK ========================== Akiva got a great ovation after his big win at Ghosts of RAW against
Fast Eddie Cutlass, and Ewiak got a huge ovation as he is the top contender to Classy Joey Boys Vegas Heavyweight Title. Akiva took control right away with a nice series of takedowns and kept the quick
Ewiak off his feet. At the four minute mark Akiva had Ewiak in a CAMEL
CLUTCH and was wearing down the kid. But Ewiak somehow reveresed it into a near pinfall! When an angry Akiva went for a CLOTHESLINE Ewiak ducked it and hit a DDT and then a quick CRADLE PILEDRIVER and went to the top rope, and hit a SHOOTING STAR PRESS!
Then a BEAUTIFUL WOMAN in a BIKINI walked to the ring waving at Frank
Ewiak! It stopped Ewiak as he was on the way to the top rope for what looked like would be the BEST DAMN FROG SPLASH IN THE BUSINESS! The woman got up on the apron and engaged Ewiak as Akiva recovered.
From the other side of the ring Surfer Blake Hamilton emerged and clocked Ewiak with his SURFBOARD!
Ewiak went down and the woman in the bikini laughed in his face! RESULT- Frank Ewiak by disqualification. Well, that was it. When we get back, live action!
