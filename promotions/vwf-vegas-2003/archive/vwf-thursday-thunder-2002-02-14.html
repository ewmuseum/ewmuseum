And this should be a great night, the 24 Hour Title and RCW Titles will be unified in tonights main event. Also, Randall Crane goes against Tony Cage! And Classy Joey Boy defends his BlackJack Title against The Ace Jack Spade! But first lets update the VWF fans on the situation between Zell Strife and Thomas Rainka. Last week Strife and Rainka battled out into the parking lot of the Thomas and Mack where someone called the cops! Both men were taken to jail and still sit there tonight. VWF president has said he will not bail them out and to make matter worse it seems that Strife and Rainka ran into each other in the lunch line at the county jail and started the fight all over again. As of right now, Zell Strife and Thomas Rainka are in solitary confinement. Can you beleive it? Since I am The Man, I have been working my sources to see who called the cops on the two VWF superstars! Lets take a look at action from earlier tonight! ******************************************* ELIMINATOR JASON STONE vs HARDCORE HAKEEM ******************************************* Stone was making his debut in the VWF and Hakeem was making his singles debut so both men were looking to make an impression. Stone and Hakeem took this fight to the air and the fans loved it. Jason Stone nailed a big HURRICANRANA but only got a two count on Hakeem. Stone then sent Hakeem to the ropes, hit his Eliminating Factor! Stone perched on the top rope and then hit a MISSLE DROPKICK! But Hakeem kicked out and let his more survival instincts take over. Hakeem came at Stone with a series of kicks and then punches that had the newcomer dazed and confused. Hakeem nailed a SPINNING HEEL KICK then set up Stone for BRING THA NOISE (top rope DDT) for the win. RESULT- Hardcore Hakeem by pinfall Next up was a grudge match. ************************************ BAVARIN vs BLADE "IN DEMAND" CANTOR ************************************ These two met at Ghost Town Blues and last week Cantor jumped Bavarin after his match with Revolution Randall Crane. They just stood in the middle of the ring and slugged it out! Bavarin took charge with a PILEDRIVER and almost made the three count. The Fearless Warrior then lifted Cantor up for a VERTICAL SUPLEX but In Demand reversed it into a SUNSET FLIP that almost got Bavarin. Cantor hit a few elbowdrops and then nailed Bavarin with a RUNNING POWERSLAM!!!! But still only a 2 count! Blade Cantor layed the BIG BOOT to Bavarin before hitting a POWERBOMB! Then Cantor signalled the crowd for the end, The LIGHT PARADE! Which he hit and scored the win! RESULT- Blade In Demand Cantor by pinfall! But as Cantor was celebrating Randall Crane snuck into the ring with a chair and LEVELED Blade. Crane then tossed Cantor over the top rope and, still holding the chair, hit EXTREME MEASURES (plancha) on the fallen Cantor! Crane stood over Blade and seemed to mouth the words "Payback is a bitch!" After the ring was cleared the next battle was set! ****************************** THE MAQUILLADORA vs REAL DEAL ****************************** Maq has been on a roll lately, beating Sebastian and one half of the tag team champs Blaze. But this match was against the seven foot four other half of the tag team champs Real Deal! RD tossed Maq around like a rag doll in this match. A RUNNING POWERSLAM on top of the turnbuckled that left Maq hanging in the TREE OF WOE while Real Deal kicked the.....you know....out of Maq. Real Deal took Maq to the center of the ring and hit his CHOKESLAM!!!!!! The count ONE TWO And Real Deal pulled up Maq! For yet another CHOKESLAM!!! And yet another pull up at the three! Real Deal had an evil smile on his face as he lifted Maq up for the third CHOKESLAM! But Maq locked his legs around the waist of Real Deal and rolled him over in a pinning combo! ONE, TWO, THREE!!!! RESULT- The Maquilladora by pinfall! In two weeks The Maquilladora has beaten both members of the tag team champions. Big things in store for Maq! Then we heard from Revolution Randall Crane [The camera cuts to a shot of the arena hallway, where walking down in his wrestling gear is "The Revolution" Randall Crane. Crane is a bit sweaty and out of breath from his attack on Blade "In Demand" Cantor. A smirk is on his face as he notices the camera and begins to speak.] RC: You might be wondering what the _hell_ Blade Cantor did to provoke an attack as heinous as what I just did. Let's think back to last week's Thunder, when Mr. Cantor decided to throw me out of the ring to attack Bavarin. Needless to say, that didn't sit too well with me. Cantor... you're a mere annoyance. I decided to even the score with you right away, so my feelings wouldn't simmer. You're lucky... I decided to take it easy on you. You might be in a _lot_ more pain than you are right now. Hell, you might not have lived to see another match. [Crane smirks... then a look of evil comes over his face.] RC: Now that we're even... let's keep it that way. If you'd like to face me one-on-one, sign your name to the dotted line. Hell, I'm even willing to throw you into the T3 Title match next week, since there's _no doubt_ I'll be one of the competitors. That way I can take you both out at the same time. [Crane begins to stretch a little on his way down the hall.] RC: Speaking of the T3 Title... it's just a few more minutes, Tony Cage. Tonight I'm going to show the VWF just what a fluke champion you really are. I'm going to show the VWF that I'm not to be taken lightly. But most importantly, I'm going to get my ticket to face you next week. You clutch onto that title as much as you can for the next week. You'd better take it to dinner, buy it some fancy chocolates, and make it a nice bubble bath. 'Cause next week, come hell or high water, I will become the new VWF T3 Champion. The revolution is coming for you, Cage. Prepare yourself, son. [With that, Crane continues to walk down the hallway and the scene cuts back to the ringside area.] Then the ring was set for a battle of VWF big boys! ****************** DAGE vs SEBASTIAN ****************** Dage was a house on fire from the begining taking Sebastian to task at every turn. But the wiley Sebastian had a trick or two up his sleeve. Sebastian hit a low blow, a quick DDT and turned the tide of the match to the Sexy Ones way. Sebastian then went straight for the back of Dage, backbreaker after backbreaker. The fans were chanting for Dage but Sebastian just kept up the punishment. The former 2 time 24 Hour Champ then locked the former 24 Hour Champ in the SEX YOU OUT for the submission win! RESULT- Sebastian by submission. *---COMMERCIAL---* DTMA: Welcome back for live action! The people of here at the great Fleetwood Mac Center.....or Thomas and Mac center, or whatever the hell this place is called want me.....C..J..B to put on the greatest show in the world, and it will happen right here tonight against you, Spade!
