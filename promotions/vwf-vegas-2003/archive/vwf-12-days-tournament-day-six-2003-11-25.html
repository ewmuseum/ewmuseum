12 Days Tournament: Day Six

Nov 25, 2003

DTMA: I don't know why the referee didn't just disqualify Daniel Sinz but I guess one rule in a Sinz Days Title defense is that anything goes. WEST: But Sinz still had to make Y2K submit in order to win the VWF Submission Championship...and that's basically what he did when he rolled Kilpatrick back into the ring and locked him in his own MILLENNIUM DEATHLOCK, refusing to break the hold until Y2K literally passed out from the pain and the referee was forced to award him both titles!! DTMA: That wasn't enough for Daniel Sinz, though. It wasn't enough for him to just BEAT Kilpatrick, Sinz wanted to BREAK him, too!! Talk about being a sore loser!! WEST: More like sore winner!! Daniel Sinz beat Yancy Kilpatrick bloody, made him tap out to his own finisher, and then humiliated the former champion by spitting in his face when he couldn't fight back!! DTMA: And, worst of all, Kilpatrick had to be carried out of the arena on a stretcher and taken to the same hospital where he was treated just two weeks ago after Phear Phantastic!! Hopefully we'll have an update on his condition soon...but it's obvious to me that we WON'T be seeing Y2K or The Masked Maniac again anytime soon!! !!!!! WINNER AND NEW VWF SUBMISSION/SINZ DAYS CHAMPION = DANIEL SINZ !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. (CUT to Dan The Man Aller and Dirk Western sitting at 12 Days headquarter) DTMA: Welcome to the final night of the first round, DAY SIX! WEST: We get the other 4 singles and the six tag teams that move on. DTMA: Six tag teams, the rules kind of changed and now six tag teams will move into the second round. WEST: The tag team section should get some extra action, and this has been a gift for a couple of teams on the bubble if you will. DMTA: Lets go to tag team action and see who is moving on. .. .. .. .. .. Tag Group 1 ------------ \\\\\\\ THE RODEO BOYS (vs) NEW AGE ARMY /////// ------------------------------------------------ DTMA: The Rodeo Boys have struggled in this tournament, not because they can't compete with the other teams but because of internal problems. WEST: The Rodeo Boys had a tough task on this night as the New Age Army hit the ring.... Iyasu Saku started this match on his heels against a rocketing Billy Bones. DMTA: Bones was running Saku all over the ring, nailing him with a ROARING ELBOW followed by a MOONSAULT. WEST: Bones tagged in Kenny Kidd, who hit a TORNADO DDT on Saku.. DTMA: But only got a two count! Kidd slapped an ABDOMINAL STRECH on Saku, only to have it broken up by Colossus. WEST: This brought Billy Bones in the ring with his cowbell!! DMTA: Bones was a step away from hitting Colossus with the bell when he was tripped up by his own partner! WEST: Kidd did not want to see his team disqualified yet again. DTMA: Bones turned to his partner with a shocked look on his face, and then got POWERBOMBED over the top rope to the floor by Colossus. WEST: But when Colossus turned he met a FLYING FOREARM from Kenny Kidd and that sent the seven footer over the top rope... DMTA: Saku rushed in on Kidd, who hit a TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER on Saku. WEST: Kidd then went to the top rope... and hit the EIGHT SECOND RIDE on Saku and got the 1-2-3! DTMA: Bones rolled in the ring, and he and Kidd shook hands. WEST: Yech! !!!!! WINNERS = The Rodeo Boys !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ THE FALLEN ANGELS (vs) DESOLATION ROW /////// ----------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Two of the top teams in this tournament went one on one... WEST: Brian Hunter got the start against the big man, Insomniac. DMTA: Insomniac used power and strength to keep Hunter at bay, which led to Hunter tagging in Demola. WEST: Do you think we'll have Demola's in the finals of both sides of this tournament? DTMA: Maybe Dirk. Demola fought off the big man but Insomniac was able to get his hands on Demola on a few occasions and toss him around the ring like a rag doll. WEST: But Demola turned the tide with a SPEAR to the back of the knee of Insomniac. DMTA: This brough the big man to the mat, and Demola dropped a few ELBOWS and KNEE to the head and throat of Insomniac... WEST: And then the action moved to the air.... Demola came off the top rope and hit a CORKSCREW MOONSAULT! DTMA: And got a 1-2... count..... after a tag to Hunter, Desolation Row hit a double VERTICAL SUPLEX! WEST: The fans began to chant "ANGELS, ANGELS" with the hopes of Insomniac making the tag. DTMA: The Row then used some classic tag team tactics, as the legal man would distract the ref, usually drawing Tony Cage into the ring, and then the double team would begin. WEST: Hunter tagged in Demola and they sent Insomniac to the rope... but the big man came off the ropes and took down both men with a FLYING SHOULDER BLOCK! DTMA: Insomniac rolled to his corner and tagged in Cage! WEST: Cage moved aside a charging Hunter and tossed him over the ropes... and Cage turned to Demola! DMTA: Cage hit a TORNADO DDT and followed that by a BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX. WEST: Cage then pointed to the ropes, and placed Demola on the ropes.... DMTA: But as Cage was up there ready to hit the SUNSET BOMB Hunter jumped up and hit Cage in the back of the head... allowing Demola to hit a DDT off the top rope.... WEST: Insomniac got in the ring but it was to little to late, as the Row scored the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNERS = Desolation Row !!!!! DTMA: Lets look at the final standings in Tag Group 1 1) Desolation Row (2-1) 2) Fallen Angels (2-1) 3) Rodeo Boys (1-2) 4) New Age Army (1-2) WEST: Top three move on to meet the top three from the other group. DMTA: And lets find out who is coming out of the other group! .. .. .. .. .. Tag Group 2 ------------ \\\\\\\ JUSHIN LIGER GOLD/EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) LEGENDS OF THE VWF ////// / ----------------------------------------------------------------------- - DMTA: JLG and Loco could not put their differences to the side for other matches so there was no reason to believe they would for this one. WEST: The Legends came in 2-0 and looking like a favorite to win the whole thing, this is the time to get it together. DMTA: JLG started against Sebastian, and the legend took the fight right to GOLD.... Sebastian got JLG on the mat and began driving his knee into the small of the back of JLG. WEST: It was clear Sebastian was looking to lock on the SEX YOU OUT boston crab. After a few minutes of grinding knees... DTMA: Sebastian sent JLG to the ropes and went for his trademark TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER... but GOLD turned it around and it ended up with Sebastian getting nailed by a TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!!! WEST: GOLD then went to make the tag and Loco dropped off the apron, much like he did in their first match of this tournament. DMTA: Sebastian made it to his feet and tagged in Gangsta... who spun around JLG and blasted him with a KNIFE EDGE CHOP series! WEST: Gangsta then hit a SNAP SUPLEX and then came off the second rope with a LEGDROP! DMTA: The Legend then sent JLG to the ropes and hit the BUCKSHOT!!! WEST: GOLD was in real trouble as Gangsta picked him up and set him up for his own tombstone, the QUEENZ CRUSHA!!!! DMTA: Gangsta tagged in Sebastian... who quickly locked on the SEX YOU OUT!!! WEST: The fans chanted and Loco stood on the apron.... looking very conflicted! DTMA: Don't help and the Legends win and the champs could be in trouble... Loco suddenly was on the top rope and hit Sebastian with a MISSLE DROPKICK! WEST: This brought Gangsta in the ring and he SPEARED Loco, sending both men through the ropes to the floor.... DMTA: Sebastian got on his feet and went right back to the SEX YOU OUT! WEST: Loco reversed an irish whip and Gangsta went face first into the ring post..... and Loco was climbing in the ring.. Sebastian broke the hold and grabbed Loco and hit him with the double underhook piledriver, THE LEGEND KILLER!!! DMTA: But JLG broke it up but SPEARING Sebastian in the back of the knee.. WEST: Loco rolled out and to the corner, GOLD jumped up and slapped his partner on the shoulder... tagging in Loco... DMTA: Loco hit the TIJUANA TOSS on Sebastian and then caught him in the EL MILAGRO MEXICANO!!!! And got the fall! WEST: Huge win for JLG and El Gringo Loco! !!!!! WINNERS = Jushin Liger GOLD/El Gringo Loco !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ THE RHOMBUS (vs) 28th STREET /////// --------------------------------------------- DMTA: Sonicman started against D-Rock... WEST: D-Rock hit Sonicman with a THUNDERING UPPERCUT and then a POWERBOMB! DMTA: D-Rock is a strong guy..... but it was then that CAPSMAN pulled off his mask to reveal himself as EVIL CAPSMAN!!! WEST: D-Rock allowed a smile to cross his face as EVIL CAPSMAN walked off up the ramp! DMTA: D-Rock hit a BACKBREAKER and held it as Hakeem came off the top rope with a GUILLOTINE LEGDROP on Sonicman! WEST: But they only got the 2 count! Hakeem hit a PUMP HANDLE SLAM on Sonic, then tagged in D-Rock. DMTA: They hit a DOUBLE FLAPJACK and then both men went to the top ropes... WEST: But from the back came... CAPSMAN!!! The real CAPS ran out and BULLDOG'D the EVIL CAPSMAN on the stage area!!! DMTA: Hakeem and D-Rock each came off the top ropes.... and missed on the double HEADBUTTS!!! WEST: Sonic rolled to the corner and tagged in his real tag team partner CAPSMAN! DMTA: CAPS hit the ring hit the CAPSFACTOR on Hakeem, and then ended this battle with the CAPITOL PUNISHMENT on D-Rock....for the 1-2-3! WEST: But once again the EVIL CAPSMAN makes his presence known. !!!!! WINNERS = The Rhombus !!!!! DMTA: But the Rhombus moves on... lets look at the final standing in Tag Group 2. 1) Jushin Liger GOLD & El Gringo Loco (2-1) 2) Legends of the VWF (2-1) 3) The Rhombus (2-1) 4) 28th Street (0-3) WEST: Now it is time for singles action! .. .. .. .. .. Group E -------- \\\\\\\ DEMONIC (vs) LARK FENRIZ /////// ---------------------------------------- DMTA: Demonic is a dangerous man and Fenriz found that out fast... WEST: Demonic was like an animal working over Fenriz for a few minutes to start this match.... Demonic planted Fenriz with a CHOKESLAM and got a near fall! DMTA: Fenriz turned a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP into a FALLING NECK BREAKER to give him some momentum. WEST: Fenriz pulled Demonic to his feet and hit him with a SPINNING FIST! DTMA: That move rocked the big man Demonic..... Fenriz then hit a TIGER BOMB! WEST: Fenriz then came off the ropes with a SPLASH!!! and went for the fall but Demonic kicked out at the 2! DTMA: Demonic found himself back on his feet.... he blocked a Fenriz right hand kicked Lark in the midsection and then went for the PITZ OF HELL!!! WEST: Fenriz blocked it and hit a BACK BODY DROP! DMTA: But when Fenriz went over to inflict further damage on Demonic he caught a THROAT PUNCH! WEST: Demonic got Fenriz and POWERSLAMMED him into the corner, leaving Fenriz in the TREE OF WOE!!! DMTA: Demonic charged in and SPEARED.... the ring post, because Fenriz moved! WEST: Fenriz took advantage and hit Demonic with a SPINNING CRADLE PILEDRIVER!!!!! DMTA: Which set up... SAMMAEL!!!! for the 1-2-3!! !!!!! WINNER = Lark Fenriz !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ FAST EDDIE CUTLASS (vs) BLAZE /////// --------------------------------------------- DMTA: Cutlass was against the wall, needing a win to move on... WEST: And he was facing down the resident hit man of Vegas, Blaze. DMTA: Well without that financial incentive Blaze is a flat wrestler and this was no exception. WEST: Though Blaze did hit Cutlass hard in the early few moments of this match, Cutlass came off the ropes with a FLYING SHOULDER that took all the fight out of Blaze. DMTA: Cutlass used a SOMERSAULT NECKBREAKER, a CORNER BODY PRESS and a SERIES OF KNIFE EDGE CHOPS to weaken the big Blaze. WEST: But Cutlass ended it with the GUARANTEE close to the four minute mark. !!!!! WINNER = Fast Eddie Cutlass !!!!! DTMA: Well Cutlass moves on, as does Fenriz, lets look at the final standings. 1) Lark Fenriz (3-0) 2) Eddie Cutlass (2-1) 3) Demonic (1-2) 4) Blaze (0-3) WEST: Who joines Fenriz and Cutlass in their second round group? Lets find out. .. .. .. .. .. Group F -------- \\\\\\\ JASON STORM (vs) HEATH STARR /////// -------------------------------------------- DMTA: Simply put, Starr was overmatched here... Storm is a monster. WEST: Jason Storm is on a mission, some say he'll retire after this tournament but that remains to be seen... DMTA: Starr used a hit and run offense for a while but Storm caught him and began to beat him... just plain beat him. WEST: A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX with a HAMMERLOCK was a highlight of Storm.. DMTA: As well as the PERFECT STORM that ended this battle! !!!!! WINNER = Jason Storm !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ CLASSY JOEY BOY (vs) MARCEL KLUTZ /////// ------------------------------------------------- DMTA: CJB used Marcel was a warm to the next round... WEST: Classy Joey Boy is in a different class than Klutz. DMTA: Funny, very funny. WEST: CJB started off working the legs of Klutz..... leglocks, wishbones etc.. DMTA: But Classy Joey Boy did not end this match with his submission move, instead he hit the REJECTION FACTOR!!! WEST: That is an impressive move! !!!!! WINNER = Classy Joey Boy !!!!! DMTA: CJB and Storm join Fenriz and Cutlass in the next round, wow! What a group... here are the final standing in Group F. 1) Jason Storm (2-0-1) 2) Classy Joey Boy (2-0-1) 3) Heath Starr (1-2) 4) Marcel Klutz (0-3) WEST: Well Dan this sets up a rematch of Storm CJB.... DMTA: That is going to be big... lots of rematches in the next round of battles from this round. WEST: This round has been great! Day 1 we had Devin Demola in that tough match with El Gringo Loco.... DTMA: Storm and CJB of course on Day Four..... WEST: I thought Cutlass and Fenriz was one hell of a match... as well as Delaney and The Maquilladora! DMTA: And lets not forget Frost, Violent Victor is flying under the radar.. WEST: Daniel Sinz is also doing well and not drawing alot of attention, he could be a second round factor. DMTA: Well on December 2 the next round begins... stay tuned to 12 Days TV to get the whole schedule but it is going to be great! WEST: Ten matches each night until we seperate the wheat from the chaff. DTMA: Huh??? WEST: Nothing... see you Tuesday December 2! DTMA: Have a great Thanksgiving..... and see you for the next 6 days! © 12 Days Tournament
