RAW-Vegas ========== [The backstage area is filled with all kinds of equipment. Extra video camera's, film, video tapes, and storage boxes can be seen. There also is a special entrnce way in this part of the back for special celebrities who need to sneak into the building. The door swings open, and a young, confident, Blackjack champion walks into the building. Classy is wearing a white shirt tucked into black jeans. He has a black leather jacket on, and a pair of sunglasses that mask his eye's. He is carrying his gear in a gym bag over his shoulder. His million dollar smile is slapped on his face. Classy takes a look around before heading toward the lockeroom area. Surprising CJB out of nowhere is Darlene Orion. As always she has her microphone in hand and is full of questions.]
Darlene: Classy, big win against Scott Starring last week, can you tell me though why you did not put the Blackjack title on the line?
[Classy removes his sunglasses and hooks them to his belt.] CJB: Darlene, we all know I'm not the one booking the matches. I just take out whatever is put in my way. Whether it be Scott Starring, Van
Wild, or even Saul Akiva, I do what I always do. You see, Darlene, the
"Raw Team Captain" doesn't care who he has to go through to get to the top, as long as I get the job done.
[Darlene is quick to shoot out the next few questions] Darlene: Classy you mentioned getting to the top. Does that mean your attempting to gain entry into the 24 Hour tournament? What about the
Fallen Angels and tonight? Are you ready for them? And the fans all want to know whats going on with Saul Akiva?
[A dangerous grin crosses Classy's face] CJB: I know The Fallen Angels are dangerous, and against people like them there is only one strategy you can take. I'm not worried about it though, the Sex Icons are focused. We have something to prove tonight.
[His tone becomes more serious] Tonight, is for Sebastian and myself, Darlene. This is for all the people who opened the doors of this industry to us, and it's for all the bastards who tried to shut me out!
[Darlene tries to fire the next question but CJB grabs her hand and pulls the mic back] CJB: The Sex Icons are in a slump right now. After all the trash talking and all the mind games were through we were left falling just short of Raw gold. You see at Vegas Violance the Sex Icons dropped the ball.....but I just picked it up Darlene, and I'm running with it! I'm leading us back to dominance.
[Darlene backs away a little as she feels Classy is being serious for the first time in a long time]
CJB: Now, there are a lot of people out here that feel I hold the grandest of all Raw-Vegas titles. Undefeated in Blackjack title matches in over six months.[Adjsuting his bag]And now Tony Cage wants a shot. He says I only defend this title against the nobodies. Well the b.s. stops tonight!
Now that the "Raw Team Captian" has the ball, he's taking the game to whole new level! The ante has been upped, the stakes are higher, and to all the superstars in all of the Raw lockeroom's, you either step up to the standard I'm setting, or throw your cards in and call it quits.
[He pauses a moment before continuing] CJB: Darlene, as the "Raw Team Captain" I hide from no one including
Tony Cage. So at Phantastic Phear I will gladly step into the ring and defend my title against Cage.
[A smile hits Darlene's face as the first match for Phantastic Phear has been made] CJB: As for Saul Akiva, the holder of the greatest Raw title, well......[he looks straight into the camera]...our time will come, it won't be tonight nor next week. But when it does, Saul, you will be pushed over the edge, and if you plan to survive then your going to need to set new limits, and expand your boundaries. This is the 24 hour city, the city the doesn't sleep
Darlene. They say you need to be crazy to live here Darlene. Crazy, harcore, extreme? Tonight, I set new meanings to those words.
[CJB walks away with a serious look on his face as he is all business tonight. Darlene looks a little afraid as she turns her attnetion towards the camera]
Darlene: Dan, I don't know what to say. It seems CJB is taking it upon himself to set a new standard in Raw-Vegas. And he is all business, Dan, he's not the same person we are used to.

Heavyweight champion I know you know about making impressions. That fact of the matter is I have nothing but respect for you, and all your accomplishments in making Raw what it is. It's that respect that brings me here, infront of thse fans, in a city that doesn't sleep.

[The fans go crazy as Hunter announces his entrance into the 24 Hour tournament. The Aussie Assassin drops the mic and slowly leaves the ring, basking in the glory of his return]
