Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Despair. Depression. Defeat. That's where we begin this journey. For it's not a journey that the strongest man or woman can take. It's a path meant for those whose life has been crushed by something drastic. That's where we find ourselves today.) (Fade inside the confines of the Tokyo Zoo. Yes, Tokyo.....the host of the Gauntlet. The city where some dreams came true and where some dreams and lives were crushed by the mighty hand of God, with Fate looking over his shoulder in orgasmic pleasure. As we look around, we see children laughing, playing, walking with their mothers or their fathers clutching balloons. We see couples madly in love, walking through the zoo, holding hands as if they were passionately in love. We see elderly couples, clinging to each other as if it was their last breath here on this earth. We see panda bears, monkeys, zebras, lions.......) (And then we see "him".) (Who's "him" you might ask? He's the epitomy of despair, depression and defeat. His costume? Black. Black mask, black hat, black shirt, black boots, black pants. The looks of his attire simply describe death. Too ashamed to hide his face, he walks throughout the zoo, head held low to the ground. Children walk up to him, smile, and say hello in the native language. But yet this man is to disgraced to even nod his head as a reply.) (Beaten down, Emotionally burdened from the load on his back, he collapses on a park bench in front of the tiger exhibit. After a few short minutes to catch his breath, he gathers the courage to look up out of the corner of his eye.....only to see a bengal tiger looking back at him. In an instant, it seemed that the two 'animals' made an instant connection....his eyes fixed on the tiger and the tiger's eyes fixed on the male. It was as if both could see the despair, depression and defeat in each others eyes. As if both had been locked up in their own cages, nowhere to roam, nowhere to survive. It's that weary look when a doctor looks at your face and tells you that there's no hope....that you'll die. It's that feeling that brings about the will NOT to live.) (The sounds of a rusty cage door opens and a zookeeper enters the environment, carrying a plasic pail. The young zookeeper, full of life, reaches into his pail and pulls out a huge slab of raw meat. The zookeeper dangles it before the eyes of the tiger as if to toy with it while the zookeeper looks dangerously into the eyes of his beaten foe with smiles, nods and laughter. Eventually, the zookeeper slings it across the cage but his weary adversary is too weak to even pick his head up off the floor to eat it. Still laughing, the zookeeper just turns around and shakes his head. Just as the final shades of death slowly creep in to take his soul, the tiger found that glimpse of light....his last shot at survival.) (Like the 'predator' that his maker 'created him to be', the tiger cautiously surveyed his surroundings perhaps for the last time. His antagonist made a fatal error in leaving the cage door open....for his return 'back into the wild'. With all the strength that he could muster, this majestic hunter made a daring leap right onto the back of the zookeeper. All the years of despair were instantly being turned back into a 'vengeful' anger as he ripped chunks of flesh out of his now defeated foe. The voice of his foe echoed throughout the zoo in pain and anguish as the zoo immediately was emptied.....except for "him". With blood dripping from his whiskers and the taste of fresh meat in his mouth, the tiger looked at his destined 'soulmate' as if to say, "Thanks for saving my life. Soon, I shall help to save yours." And in an instant, the tiger turned around and bolted out the door into the arms of freedom before the fellow zookeepers could arrive to tend to their now fallen co-worker.) (As the man still sat on the park bench, tears began to run down the black mask. Perhaps finding inspiration or just courage from the tiger, this man began thinking that indeed just like the tiger, he had one more score to settle before he died. And that score SHALL be settled......in the VWF!!!) .. .. .. (Shot opens with a cheap cassette player, a hand comes in the shot and hits the play button.
