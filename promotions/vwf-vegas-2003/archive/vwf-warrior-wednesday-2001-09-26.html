VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

Warrior Wednesday, 26 September 2001

26 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARRIOR WEDNESDAY SEPTEMBER 26, 2001 (The camera is panning around the MGM which is filled to the rafters with the VWF faithful.......some signs from around the arena
#RCW IS BACK# #FULLER 3:16# #G-FORCE IS COMING# #I AM THE FIRE INSIDE# Viva Las Vegas begins to play, then is cut off by O Fortuna......The
VWF logo blazes on the screen then the RCW logo fades in over it....then the BWF logo......the screen is finally an alphabet soup of letters)
DAN THE MAN ALLER: STOPPPPPPPPP! Stop the graphics! This is out of control! This the Vegas Wrestling Federation! The VWF! Not the RCW not the
Beautiful Wrestling Federation this is the VWF!!!!!
(crowd pop) DA: Welcome to another Warrior Wednesday! I am here with Lean Darlene to call all the action of the VWF!
LEAN DARLENE: That is right! The VWF, lead by Kerry Powell! DA: Powell is a VWF man, but where does Jack Spade sit? LD: The VWF Heavyweight champ, the 24 Hour Man has yet to chime in .... DA: I just got word Rob Beauty is going to see The Ace Jack Spade! (CUT TO- Rob Beauty is backstage at the MGM and he walks into the dressing room of Jack Spade) ROB BEAUTY: I am so glad you came Jack...I mean Mr. Spade. I asked you to come because the VWF needs you...that title around your waist is the
VWF title, not Ric Beautys title or even the RCW title. I am asking you to come out tonight! Right now and tell the fans of the VWF who side your on! (Jack stands and stands, stares at Beauty and stares at the world title.) JS: I decide who's side I'm on...you'll see... (Jack walks out of the room.) DA: That didn't help! LD: G-Gangsta seems to be on the side of the VWF after he got beat down by Demonic and Blaze, and Spade and G have not been the best of friends in the past.
DA: Lets get things underway..... ********************************************** TRIPLE X (CHUMPY) vs PADDY O'SHEA ********************************************** (Eat It by Weird Al hits and two REALLY fat guys begin making their way to the ring, Chumpy is in front carrying a sub sandwhich and
Earthquake is behind him with a bucket of chicken, they are both wearing sadly fitting jeans and t-shirts that read Triple X)
DA: At a combined weight of 707 pounds this is Chumpy and Earthquake..... TRIPLE X!!!!! LD: So Triple X is two guys? DA: Yeah, but only one wrestles. Chumpy, the less fat one will be getting in the ring tonight.
LD: The VWF attracts some strange dudes. DA: These guys don't get to you like Kerry Powell? LD: Not a chance! (Barroom Heroes hits and a drunken Paddy O'Shea starts his long stumble to the ring) DA: From Dublin Ireland.....at 6 foot 2, 260 pounds The Master of the 1-2... PADDY OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO SHEA!!!!! LD: One half of the VBA or Vegas Badasses. DA: O'Shea is going to have his hands full with this one. LD: Very punny. DA: O'Shea locks up with Chumpy of Triple X! LD: O'Shea tries for a slam but he is not strong enough, Chumpy pushes him away like he was a healthy meal!
DA: Chumpy is pounding his chest, daring O'Shea to come get him. LD: I think he is trying to start his heart! DA: O'Shea rushed in and WAMMOOOOOOO! Paddy hits the mat hard. LD: ELBOWDROP!!! by Chumpy! DA: I think he needs help getting up! LD: O'Shea? DA: No! The fat guy! LD: The other Triple X guy, Earthquake is calling for the nacho guy! DA: Chumpy is over at the ropes and PLACING AN ORDER!!!! LD: O'Shea comes up from behind and KICK TO THE GROIN! DA: That has Chumpy doubled over! LD: O'Shea throws a few rights into Chumpys face, IRISH WHIP and
CLOTHESLINE that leaves Chumpy wobbling in the center of the ring.
DA: O'Shea to the ropes........BULLDOG! LD: Look! Earthquake is waving the fresh nachos around outside the ring! DA: Chumpy looks inspired! LD: O'Shea goes for the ONE TWO!!!!! DA: Chumpy picks him up.......running powerslam!!!!! LD: That is called the CHEEEESEBURGER!!!! DA: 1....2....THREE!!!!! LD: Chumpy of Triple X gets the big win! DA: Here comes Dirk Western! (Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DIRK WESTERN: Get up Paddy! Damn boy! Look at these two guys! This is embarassing for the VBA! This is why we get no respect! The VBA is the
BADDEST, BIGGEST and ROUGHEST PAIR OF SOB'S in the VWF or the RCW!
(did you miss the match POP) (Western and O'Shea leave the ring as the fans cheer Triple X) DA: Dirk Western is loosing his mind. O'Shea got punked! *-----Commercial-----* ****************************************************** ZELL STRIFE (vs) THE SILENCE BOBBY SILAS ****************************************************** Fall Children by AFI starts with thick fog pouring through the ramp way, green smoke fills the air. Lights start to flash and fade while a large Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans. DA: Six feet four......two fifty... this is ZELL STRIFE!!!!!!!!! (Players Are Gonna Play hits and Bobby Silas begins to strut his way to the ring. He is wearing long purple satin pants and no shirt which shows off his sculpted body. He stands on the ring apron)
DA: That is "The Silence" Bobby Silas, coming in at 6-6, 274 pounds
(Bobby steps over the top rope and into the ring)
LD: Big match for both these guys. Many close to the VWF think one of these is going to be a big star.
DA: Both have had ups and downs. This should be a good one! LD: Lockup and HEADLOCK by Silas. DA: Strife tosses him to the ropes.....Silas takes Strife down with a shoulderblock....Silas to the ropes....leapfrog by Strife who comes off the ropes and
ELBOWSMASH!!! LD: Big Bobby is down and hard. DA: Strife, the Fire Inside moves in and lifts the big man. LD: A few shots to the head for good measure and Strife tosses Silas to the ropes..... BACK BODY DROP! followed by a DA: Moonsault legdrop! Nice move by Strife. LD: Cover.. 1 2 Not yet! DA: Strife pulls Silas up and CLOTHESLINE!!! LD: That was big... DA: Strife is down on Silas like a tiger and locks on an armbar and is wrenching it back....
LD: Strife is one of the .... (Suddenly O Fortuna begins to play) DA: Son of a .....Ric Beauty is coming toward the ring! LD: Why is he here? DA: He is sitting down and watching the match. LD: Silas stands up and pushes Strife into the corner..... DA: WHOOOOOOOOOOO chop by Silas........irish whip to the corner....Silas follows and MISSES!!!
LD: Look at Beauty. He is clapping for Strife? DA: These two had some battles back in the old GNWC. Is Ric a Zell
Strife fan? LD: Strife has Silas up and is holding him in a standing suplex..... DA: Waiting.... still waiting.......... DA: And Silas comes crashing into the mat..... LD: Strife is looking at Ric, with a twisted face...he is as unsure as the rest of us. DA: Strife has Silas... PORPHYRIA!!!!!!!!!!! DA: Strife makes the cover staring at Ric Beauty who is giving him a standing ovation! 1...2...THREEEEEE!!! LD: Strife is leaving the ring.....Ric Beauty just nodded to him. DA: What the hell is going on here in the VWF? *-----Commercial-----* ****************************************************** HUEY HUXTABLE (vs) DIRK WESTERN ****************************************************** (Peace Train plays and Huey Huxtable starts to walk toward the ring, his father seems very serious but Huey is smiling and waving to everyone with an idiots smile)
DA: From Shamrock Texas, weighting in at 360 pounds and standing seven feet tall... HUEY HUXTABLE!!! LD: Huey is not happy about Western giving the D-Day to his father
Henry. DA: Still Huey is the lovable idiot of the VWF! (Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DA: In this corner, from Cell Block D. Weighing in at 260 pounds, six feet five inches DIRK "D-DAY" WESTERN!!!!!!!!!! LD: Western is pointing to Henry and acting like he is going to D-Day him again! DA: Huey rushes over and has Western by the throat! LD: He is just choking him....the ref can't break it up! DA: Huey throws him like a doll into the corner and Huey goes in and the fool is madder than hell!
LD: Huey is punching and kicking Western, and Dirk is falling like a tree. DA: Western is pleading for mercy in the corner! LD: Western lunges forward and HEADBUTT to the stomach of Huey! DA: Western up on the second rope and FIST to the BACK OF THE HEAD and
Huey goes down! LD: Big Huey is hurting! DA: Western gets a frontface lock and is twisting it up on Huey. LD: Huey is down on one knee. The big man having trouble with this hold. DA: And what a strange thing to see Dirk Western with a wrestling hold! LD: Huey is getting up. DA: He is lifting Western....and he sets Western on the top rope! LD: Nail him HUEY! DA: Huey steps back......Western leaps and NO ONE HOME!!! (BIG crowd laugh) LD: Huey is sitting on Western! DA: This could be a submission hold. (CROWD RUMBLES) LD: Paddy O'Shea is heading to the ring! DA: Henry is coming over and meeting O'Shea! LD: Henry and Paddy are exchanging blows on the outside of the ring.... DA: Huey is coming over, leaning over the ropes and grabs hold of
O'Shea! LD: Western kicks Huey in the nuts! DA: Darlene!!!! LD: I mean groin! DA: Huey turns.....D-DAY!!!! LD: Oh no! DA: 1....2... THREEEE!! LD: Dirk Western gets the win over Huey Huxtable! DA: Henry has dropped O'Shea and is getting in the ring....Western is out of there! *-----Commercial-----* LD: Brother against brother.....Fuller in the ring with Powell and still no final word from Jack Spade on where he stands! Here is your
MAIN EVENT!!!!!!!!! ****************************************************** BWF (Fuller and Ric) vs VWF (Powell and Rob) ****************************************************** [The house lights dim down, and golden spotlights begin to sweep the entire arena. A hush falls over the crowd as "Money" by Pink
Floyd begins to play.]
Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, weighting in at 238 pounds, and standing at 6 feet, and one inch, this is...
24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! [The fans jump to their feet as Kerry Powell himself, makes his way to the ring.] DA: Rob Beauty is accompanying Kerry Powell! LD: Go Kerry! Go Kerry! DA: Stop that! LD: No. DA: Please. LD: Ok. DA: Ric Beauty is stepping out onto the ramp! RIC BEAUTY: Ladies and gentlemen, the Beautiful Wrestling Federation is proud to present...Corey Fuller!
["The Memory Remains" by Metallica begins to play in the arena as the lights turn off. The arena spot light is circling the arena.]
# Fortune, fame # # Mirror vain # # Gone insane # # But the memory remains # *BANG BANG BANG BANG * [Four loud fire works go off in the arena just as the "Memory Remains" hits its melody and comes from the backstage arena. Announcer: About to enter the ring, from Halifax, Nova Scotia, standing 6 feet 3 inches and weighing 270 pounds, here is...
... COREYYYYYY .... ... FULLLLERRRRR.... [The spotlight hits the curtain just as Corey Fuller walks through.] *Mix Reaction* #Heavy rings on fingers wave # #Another star denies the grave # #See the nowhere crowd # #cry the nowhere tears of honor# *Song Continues* [Corey is in his usual wrestling gear, black tights. His face is poised with anger, his muscular body is covered with oil making his muscles look even bigger then they are and his short brown hair is messed up.]
#Like twisted vines that grow # #That hide and swallow mansions whole# #And dim the light of an already faded prima donna# [As he gets halfway to the ring he raises his hand. He then begins to smile and then slowly points the finger to the people in attendance.]
# Fortune, fame # # Mirror vain # # Gone insane... # [And Corey runs toward the ring just as his middle fingers are totally extended. He slides under the bottom rope...]
# Fortune, fame # # Mirror vain # # Gone insane... # [Corey raises his hand as the music fades out and the lights come on in the arena.] *Heel Pop* DA: I hate these guys! LD: Powell and Rob are going right after them! DA: Fuller is on Powell and the brothers are going at it like a real family feud! LD: Rob measures Ric and SUPERKICK!!!! DA: Ric Beauty just flew over the top rope and slammed into the railing! LD: Powell is running toward the ropes....leaps off the top rope...spins......another SUPERKICK and Ric Beauty looks like he was broken in half.
DA: Fuller comes up behind Rob and INVERTED NECKBREAKER!!! LD: Powell slides back in and from behind BULLDOG to Fuller! DA: Powell is holding Fuller and Rob sets him up for a SUPERKICK! LD: Fuller is down on the mat..... DA: Here comes Ric with a chair...... LD: Powell to the ropes and is met with a chair to the back of the head!!!! DA: Rob runs and leaps over the ropes and SPLASH on his brother who drops the chair! LD: Fuller staggers to his feet.......Powell with a cross body block.... 1...2 KICKOUT by Fuller. DA: Ric and Rob are trying to kill each other outside of the ring! LD: Not much of a tag team match! DA: Ric lifts Rob and POWERBOMB onto the MGM floor!!!!!!! LD: Ric is locking on the BEAUTY BREAKER!!!! DA: What the HELLL!!!!! LD: Demonic and Blaze just emerged from the crowd!!!!! DA: Blaze gets on top of Demonics shoulders and FROG SPLASH onto the intertwined legs of Ric and Rob!!!
LD: Oh GOD NO!!!! DA: Back in the ring.....Powell hits the GOLD RUSH on Fuller! LD: Here comes Demonic and Blaze! DA: Powell can't see them coming! LD: Turn KERRY!!!! DA: DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE just as he turns!!!! LD: Blaze has him, Demonic is laughing.........and a full nelson slam or the UNCONTROLLABLE INFERNO!!! and Powell is lying lifeless in the middle of the ring! DA: HERE COMES G-GANGSTA!!!!!! LD: The T3 Champ is in the ring and exchanging blows with Demonic and
Blaze! DA: This is out of control! LD: Corey Fuller is getting up and he spins around Blaze...and FULLER DROP!!!! DA: Demonic has G-Gangsta and PITS OF HELL!!!!!!!!! LD: Blaze and G-Gangsta are out and Demonic is standing face to face with Fuller!!!! DA: Both men turn as......ACE OF SPADES hits!!! LD: Here comes Jack Spade, the VWF Champion!!! DA: Fuller gets behind Demonic, picks up the chair and......LEVELS
DEMONIC WITH THE CHAIRSHOT........#SLAMMMMM#
LD: Spade is getting in the ring! (Jack looks down at the fallen Blaze and Demonic, kicks them stiffly in ther ribs and then stares at Corey Fuller. The crowd rises to their feet in anticipation, Jack takes a mic from his back pocket and the crowd falls silent.) JS: Y'know since I arrived in VWF, I have been screwed around a little. I mean I've had G-Gangsta call me out, Sebastian thinking he's somebody by calling me out and now all this RCW shit is threatening my place in the VWF. Should I take the safe way out? Should I take the risk? (Jack walks back and stands on the ropes.) JS: I mean I'm a known gambler, I usually have good luck and perceptions of the future. And right now, Corey Fuller is stood extending his hand out to me. It's a tempting offer...should I go with it? (The crowd boos loudly. Jack grins.) JS: The future look something a little like this right now.... (Jack runs and kicks Corey in the face - JACKPOT!!! Corey goes down and the crowd goes insane and Jack helps up Gangsta and leaves the ring.) JS: I'm taking the risk of running with the VWF...I came here and won the VWF title and the VWF title it will be for a LONG time. (The crowd goes crazy once again as Jack and Gangsta leave Fuller,
Blaze and Demonic fuming in the ring.) DA: I call this a big win for the VWF!!!!! LD: Blaze and Demonic are calling for the mic!! [After the match Blaze and Demonic stand tall in the ring. Blaze takes the mic and speaks first.] Blaze: That was easy. IS THAT ALL YOU HAVE VWF HUH? Bringing little trailer park trash to the ring such as Huey Huxtable and Bobby Silas? A bunch of red necks trying to get rich? Well as you can see from there mouths even the tooth fairy can't get rich in ALABAMA! Demonic: Now we have two more of them who have challenged us. The
APA REJECTS...I mean the Vegas Badasses. You wan't a match with us. Fine. Next week on Warrior Wednesday...MAIN EVENT...RCW vs VWF FOR THE VERY FIRST TIME! In fact I am bringing more of us to Warrior Wednesday. Real Deal, Ring Master and more will be there next week. So you rednecks from VWF send out your challenges now. And the WW III begins. [With that Americana by Offspring hits as a 7'4 300 pound man walks out and grabs the mic.] Man: My name is THE REAL DEAL! I AM AN RCW MEMBER! I AM JUST ONE OF
THE MEN COMING INTO WARRIOR WEDNESDAY NEXT WEEK! I AM GOING TO
CHALLENGE SOMEONE RIGHT NOW! HUEY HUEXTANBLE! YOU WANNA BE THE BIGGEST
MAN HERE? MISTER 7'1'' OR 7'0''...GUESS WHAT I AM BIGGER. 7'4'' YOU
KNOW? AND YOU BIG DUMB REDNECK WITH YOUR DUMB @$& FATHER...BRING YOUR
DUMB @$& TO THE RING AND FACE ME SO I CAN RECK YOU AND LITERALLY TKR
YOU APART AND SHOW YOU RCW IS REAL! Demonic: Thats right, the war begins now and ends at the PPV. Now a few of us might stay here in VWF and might still represent RCW. But and I do mean BUT... Most of us who are here or are coming soon are here for the war. To further continue RCW's legacy. I'm out of here. C'mon guys. [RCW leaves the ring to an anourmous amount of boos.] Vegas Wrestling Federation ©2001
