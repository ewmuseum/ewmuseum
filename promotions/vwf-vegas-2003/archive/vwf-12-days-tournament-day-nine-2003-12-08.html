Singles Group 3 ---------------- \\\\\ LARK FENRIZ (vs) JASON STORM ///// ----------------------------------------- DMTA: Fenriz has shown little respect for the recent tragedy... DMTA: Lark could not keep Storm down... and Storm got to his feet and dropped back to the mat hitting Fenriz with a JAWBREAKER! WEST: Storm quickly added a BACKSPIN DDT..... DMTA: Jason Storm was locking Fenriz up for something when Lark SCRATCHED Storm across the face.... WEST: With those long fingernails shaped into points! Freak! WEST: Storm sent Fenriz to the ropes and hit a BIG BACK BODY DROP!! DMTA: Fenriz jumped to his feet and came off the ropes.... and Storm caught him .... ...... FOR THE PERFECT STORM!!!! WEST: Followed by the ONE!!!! TWO!!!!!! THRRRRREEEEEE!!!!!! DMTA: Big win for Storm! !!!!! WEST: Cutlass is a tough guy but I think the events of the past have really gotten in his head. DTMA: He and CJB spent some time feeling each other out... locking up and then breaking the hold only to look at each other. WEST: Cutlass did execute an ARM DRAG but did not follow it up. DMTA: On the next lock up CJB placed Cutlass in a SIDE HEADLOCK and takedown. WEST: CJB seemed cautious..... Cutlass rolled the Vegas champ onto his back.. ONE!! But CJB let go of the headlock. DTMA: Both men shot back to their feet.... and CJB threw a punch, Cutlass ducked and hit CJB with an ATOMIC DROP! WEST: Cutlass then came off the ropes and nailed Classy Joey Boy with a DROPKICK! DMTA: But CJB reversed it and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX of his own! WEST: CJB shook off the cobwebs and got Cutlass in a DRAGON SUPLEX!!! TWO!!!!!! CJB kicked out! DTMA: Classy Joey Boy got to his feet and came off the ropes... SHOULDERBLOCK'D Cutlass back to the mat.... and went back to the ropes... WEST: Daniel Sinz slapped a CROSSFACE on Delaney.... and began to try and tear Delaneys shoulder out of the socket! DTMA: Delaney reached for and got the ropes.... but Sinz was reticent to break the hold.... the ref finally got Sinz to break the hold and Delaney rolled to the outside.
