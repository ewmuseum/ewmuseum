'Cause that's the clause that says the two of you won't be alone in that ring at the Pay-Per-View. <Another POP.> STORM: That's right, boys. Instead of Lark Fenriz versus The Equalizer, it'll be just what I promised a couple weeks ago. Lark Fenriz versus The Equalizer...versus JASON STORM!! <Huge POP as Fenriz and The Equalizer both react negatively to the announcement.> STORM: And the best thing about it is that Lark...I don't even care about the fucking title anymore. So I'll be there in that ring with you to make sure that idiot right there... <Storm points at The Equalizer as if he can see him.> STORM: Doesn't get anywhere CLOSE to being the NEXT VWF Champion!! <Huge POP#2!!> STORM: Oh, and "E"...if you think we haven't taken your threat against Yancy's kids seriously...think again. 'Cause you and YOUR Trinity ARE a serious threat. But me...I don't make threats. I make promises. <Storm smiles.> STORM: And I promise you this. Next week's Pay-Per-View's gonna end with a bang... But even that one won't be HALF as loud...as this. <Storm reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Zippo. Setting down the gas can, he strikes the flint and tosses the lighter carelessly over his shoulder so it lands in the bed of The Equalizer's truck. Then, as Storm walks away...
