VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

Warrior Wednesday, 1 August 2001

1 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is our first card. We are looking for 2 or 3 more players for the upcoming tournament. Check out the web site if interested www.geocities.com/brlysis
========== (The camera pans around the half filled MGM Grand Arena. Viva Las
Vegas plays over the PA and Dan "The Man" Aller begins to speak)
DA: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the first event held by the Vegas
Wrestling Federation! My name is Dan "The Man" Aller" and I will be your host for tonights exciting night of wrestling action.
We will see some of the new stars of this new federation and a big announcement later tonight about the big belt here in VWF!
I am told we are going backstage now. (Backstage at Warrior Wednesday Huey Huxtable is standing in his dressing room. He is a mammouth man of seven feet but he is getting yelled at by a much smaller man, his father Henry)
HENRY: You are going to do this! This is my dream for you and you are going to do it. HUEY: But I don't want to hurt nobody. HENRY: Look at you ya big freak! What else are you gonna do? Youur to fat for basketball. And to dumb for a regular job.
HUEY: Can I try and be friends with the other guy? HENRY: NOOOOO! HUEY: What do I do then? HENRY: Smash him! ------------------------------------------------- DA: Well that was interesting. (I Wanna Destroy You by the Circle Jerks starts to play and Anarchy X begins his slow walk to the ring. He is wearing a ripped up shirt and shorts and has orange and blue hair)
DA: Ladies and gents, making his way to the ring. From right here in
Las Vegas, ANARCHY X!!!!
(small crowd reaction, mostly from the mention of the hometown) (Peace Train plays and Huey Huxtable starts to walk toward the ring, his father seems very serious but Huey is smiling and waving to everyone with an idiots smile)
DA: And his opponent, from Shamrock Texas, weighting in at 360 pounds and standing seven feet tall...
HUEY HUXTABLE!!! DA: There is the bell and we are about to have oour first action here in VWF!!! (Huey walks over and offers his hand to X) DA: What the hell is he doing? Does he want to be friends? (X looks around and lays a hard shot on the stomach of Huey) DA: That didn't do a damn thing. Anarchy X is gonna have to get something more than that to win this match.
(X comes off the ropes with a lariat but the big man doesn't move) DA: I think some of the folks here at the MGM are falling asleep. (X goes behind Huey, jumps from the ropes and gets Huey in what looks like a sleeper hold) DA: Look at this! Huey is walking around with that little guy on his back, still waving at the crowd. But look at his father, that guys looks furious.
(X jumps off and crawls out of the ring) DA: Wait! The father is talking to Anarchy X. X shrugs and walks away from the old man. Henry Huxtable is up on the ring apron and he has called over the ref. Anarchy X comes up behind Huey with a chair.......WHACK!
What a shot! (Huey turns without a flinch. Henry jumps off the apron) DA: The big guy doesn't look happy! He has X by the throat, up CHOKESLAM! Finally some action! Huey picks him back up tosses him to the rope and has him in a bearhug! X is in real trouble.
OH MY GOD Huey Huxtable just belly to belly slammed the little guy. Huxtable is up on the second rope.
THE FALL!!!! Huxtable hits the fall! 1...2...3!!! And your first winner in VWF action is Huey Huxtable! ========== DA: Making his way to the ring, with a smirk and smile on his face from Los Angeles...SEBASTIAN.
(Sebastian steps into the ring and bows to each side as I'm To Sexy plays in the background)
(Barroom Heroes hits and Paddy O'Shea starts his long stumble to the ring) DA: And his opponent, from Dublin Ireland.....The Master of the 1-2...Paddy O'Shea! (The men feel each other out, dancing to lock up in the ring) DA: Sebastian is in good shape, look at him. And look at Paddy. He has had one beer to many and I thought I would never say that. He might drop Sebastian with his breath.
They lock up. Side headlock by Paddy, reversal by Sebastian into a standing wristlock. Paddy looks like he might pass out, he stumbles back into the ropes. The ref calls for the break.
CHEAP SHOT by Sebatian. It sound like Sebastian is calling Paddy names. Paddy runs at him SPEAR!!! Holy Moly a quick spear and Sebastian is down. O'Shea is on top of him and delivering a series of punches to the face. The crowd begins to chant
1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 Wait for it 10. O'Shea gets on his feet and calls for a mic. PADDY: Is the cocktail waitress around here? Could somebody get me a beer? O'Shea drops the mic and returns his attention to Sebastian. Sebastian nails him with a shot to the groin. Paddy doubles over. Sebastian delivers a few elbows to the back of the drunk, I mean wrestler. Wraps him up and goes for the DDT, block, again, block, again....and back body drop by O'Shea!
O'Shea turns and hits a quick elbow drop, goes for the cover 1... No way...to soon. O'Shea lifts Sebatian to his feet, tosses him to the ropes and WHAT A
LARIAT!! Sebastian has got to be hurting!
Again O'Shea lifts him to his feet and he starts to .....is he....Riverdancing around Sebatian while popping a jab out there? Yes he is.
O'Shea winds up and nails Sebastian with a DEVASTATING final shot that takes the feet from the good looking Sebastian.
O'Shea is smiling and having fun with this crowd, and they seem to like the fool. Sebastian is getting to his feet, and getting out of the ring. Sebastian just ripped a drink from some guy in the front row and is bringing it in. He offers it to O'Shea.
O'Shea takes it and starts to drink. As he tilts his head back SLAM! Sebatian nails him with a lariat. Sebatian is full of fire now. Sebastian is fast! BODYSLAM! Back up for the FISHERMAN SUPLEX Back up for the STANDING SUPLEX he holds O'Shea up and SIT OUT face first! Sebastian locks in the SEX YOU OUT! His version of the Boston Crab. The ref looks down at O'Shea, who may have passed out. There is the bell. Your winner by submission SEBASTIAN! ========== DA: This is it. Powell and Western. Kerry Powell has a history with the Beauty family. Rob is of course our President and hasn't spoken to Ric in over a year after the falling out Powell caused in the old
UWC. Powell is young and brash and he is meeting Western who is new to pro wrestling but is in no way a rookie to fighting...this should be great.
[The house lights dim down, and golden spotlights begin to sweep the entire arena. A hush falls over the crowd as "Money" by Pink
Floyd begins to play.]
DA: Coming to the ring at this time, weighting in at 238 pounds, and standing at 6 feet, and one inch, this is...
24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! [The fans jump to their feet as Kerry Powell himself, makes his way to the ring.] DA: The fans know him and don't seem to care much for him. (Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DA: In this corner, from Cell Block D. This is DIRK WESTERN!!!!!!!!!! DA: These two are feeling each other out. Powell is fast and in great shape, and Western doesn't look to bad himself.
They lock up and Powell goes behind, quick schoolboy. 1...2... Whoa, that was close. The inexperience in the ring of a guy like
Western is showing already.
Powell seems to be laughing at him. Western rushes and runs into a drop toe hold. Powell locks up the legs and takes a few playful swipes at the head of his foe. Western does not look happy about this.
Powell releases and get to his feet and lets Western get up as well. Kerry Powell is taunting this man and it shows on the face of Dirk
Western. OH MY GOD! Powell is getting down on the mat, on hhis back. He is daring Western to do something. Western is looking around at the crowd.
Western comes off the ropes he goes for an elbow drop....Powell moves but so does Western and nails Powell with a legdrop! Western jumps to his feet and drops a fist right between the eyes of the 24 Karat!
Powell rolls out of the ring and Western follows him outside. Western spins Powell around into another fist. Powell is staggered. Western with a kick to the gut and up goes Powell.....POWERBOMB onto the ring apron. Western rolls in and takes Powell by the hair and picks him up. Western locking him up in a frontface lock. Western cranks it up! And again!
I can hear Powell screaming from here! Western cranks it up on last time and he goes for the D-DAY NECKBREAKER, this could end it.
Powell blocks it, reversal into his own neckbreaker but not his move the Gold Rush. Powell get up on the ropes and, from the top with a big SPLASH. Powell thinks he has things in control. Cover 1..2.. Kickout by Western. Western makes his way to his feet and he and
Powell are exchanging blows. This is not the best move for Powell, he can't go toe to toe with this former inmate and it is showing, Western is getting the best of Kerry Powell!
Western grabs him RUNNING BULLDOG!!! What a move by Dirk Western! He really has Powell in trouble. Western has Powell set up again for the D-DAY, but he is taking to long...soaking in the cheering fans.
Again the reversal by Powell but this time he hits the GOLD RUSH!!! Quick cover 1....2....3!!!! Your winner...Kerry Powell! That was a shock! (Rob Beauty appears in the aisle) DA: Ladies and gents, Rob Beauty the President of VWF. ROB: Thank you Dan! DA: Thats Dan The Man. ROB: Whatever. I am here to announce that next week, right here in the
MGM Grand Arena we will crown our first 24 HOUR CHAMPION! This is the big title here in VWF and is defended 24 hours a day, every day! Only the best will take this title home.
So next week we will have a one night tournament to crown the CHAMPION! And here is how it sizes up..... ANARCHY X takes on DIRK WESTERN HUEY HUXTABLE gets in the ring with TAI ANGELA KERRY POWELL will step in the squared circle with SEBASTIAN and PADDY O'SHEA will take on a yet unnamed opponent! And if all goes well there could be more!
That is it from the 24 hour town! ========== VWF Copyright 2001
