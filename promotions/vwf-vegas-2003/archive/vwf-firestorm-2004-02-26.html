The Equalizer followed up with a BULLDOG, and then a SUPERKICK to put Cutlass on the mat but only for a two count. E then slapped Cutlass in a REAR CHIN LOCK, that looked more like a choke. Whatever it was it was wearing on Cutlass. But the familiar EDDIE! EDDIE! chant gave Cutlass just the impetious he needed to get to his feet and run to the corner, sending The Equalizer shoulder first into the post. Cutlass may be the quintessential good guy but he knows how to win a match, Cutlass went right to work on the shoulder of the Equalizer.
