VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

On The Road, 27 May 2002

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The VWF Mondays +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ |O|n| |T|h|e| |R|o|a|d| +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ From Henderson Events Center, Henderson, Nevada. -------------------------- ( www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/vegaswrestlingfed ) The VWF was in the biggest burb of Las Vegas for a big Monday show! DANE KLINGEL by pinfall over ALI COLLYMORE ------------------------------------------- The biggest of the VWE youngsters scored another win over a fellow VWE competitor. Klingel hit a nice powerbomb to score the win. FIGHTING O'SHEAS by pinfall over QUINTON OHME and MARCEL KLUTZ --------------------------------------------------------------- Father Frank and Paddy O'Shea made another step toward the VWF Tag Team Titles (now vacant) by dropping the VWE team of Ohme and Klutz. Ohme showed some signs of promise but after the tag Klutz, The Falling Frenchman, tripped over his own feet and fell into the Salvation, Father Franks brainbuster suplex. CLOUD KINNEAS by pinfall over ROB KIDANE ---------------------------------------- Zell Strifes allie had no trouble disposing of rookie Kidane. Kinneas will make some waves in the VWF and this performance showed why, Sairds Slam at the 3 minute make and this one was over. THE MAQUILLADORA by pinfall over ERIK ANDREWS ---------------------------------------------- Fresh off getting screwed out of the Tag Team belts by Randall Crane, Maq was in rare form as he fought like an animal. The usual Maq is a high flying crowd pleaser but for this one he showed a more vicious side as he dropped Andrews at the 7 minute mark with Permanent Daylight. Crane should be looking over his shoulder. CLASSY JOEY BOY by pinfall over CAPSMAN ---------------------------------------- In his first appearance since screwing over 24 Hour Champion G-Gangsta and siding with Sebastian CJB met with something he had never seen before in the VWF, an angry crowd! CJB didn't let that stop him from a sucessful defense of the BlackJack Title. CJB beat CAPSMAN all over the ring and sent him to the floor of the Henderson Events Center and nailed the hero of the VWF with the Studd Splash through a table to get the win. ********** VWF War of Words! This week! THE MAQUILLADORA (this guy is pissed!) CAPSMAN (Knocked out!) TYSON T-BONE YOUNG (CJB!) SEBASTIAN (That was funny!) ROB BEAUTY (that bastard!) THE MAQUILLADORA (Maq and Yak are backstage after the last card. Maq is wound up like a top. Denis is nowhere to be seen.) Maq- Whats this bullsh-!? Huh, Beauty? YOU strip us of the titles. YOU say WE don't exist! Are YOU nuts! I'll prove to you that WE should still have the titles. I've always had a tag partner. ALWAYS! Just because you can't see the guy for the way he really is, is not my f-ing fault. Yak- And how are we going to do that? Maq- Denis is working on getting some evidence as we speak. Yak- How so? Maq- He's compiling a bunch of tapes from my old promos, matches and what-not. Yak- Where is your partner anyway? Maq- Training the new guys. Yak- Oh yeah, forgot about those guys. Maq- I can't believe this horse s-! Yak- Settle down man. Maq- Why? Its a travesty. (Cue Adam West's music) Yak- Oh no...not this guy. CAPS- GREETINGS CITIZEN! DID SOMEONE MENTION A TRAVESTY. Maq- Yeah I did. CAPS- WHATEVER DO YOU MEAN. WHAT HAP- (Before CAPSMAN can finish his question, Maq delivers a haymaker to his skull. CAPSMAN drops like a b-tch.) Yak- Geez, Maq. You should throw that punch more often. Maq- Sorry, I can't deal with him today. Especially today. Yak- Speaking of deal...you got the Real Deal next week. Maq- Didn't I beat his punk ass along with his partner's before I...er we got the tag team titles. Yak- If my memory serves me correctly, yeah. Maq- And isn't that bastard Crane the referee. Yak- Yup, you should be mad at him not Beauty. Maq- Oh...trust me Yakabi. He'll get his. I can always get him into the ring. Beauty on the other hand...he's a different story. Yak- What do you mean? Maq- I mean I can't get Beauty into the ring. Yak- Oh...so? Maq- So I'll make an example out of the Real Deal and Crane. Lets see how Beauty likes things when his entier locker room ends up on the shelf. Yak- Damn, man. You've flipped. Maq- I flipped a long time ago. Yak- True. Maq- But now I'm pissed and maybe I can return to my old days where I put people on the shelf night after night. Yak- I remember those days. You kept beating on me all the damn time. I'm still paying the hospital bills from that. Maq- Sorry about that, but too make an omlete you have to crack some eggs. Yak- mmm...eggs. Maq- Yeah, and omlete does sound good. Lets steal CAPSMAN'S car and go to Denny's. (The camera fades as a now somewhat tame Maquiladora and Yakabi head for the garage.) ---------- CAPSMAN CAPSMAN (The camera opens with a shot of a still unconscious SuperHero. CAPSMAN is still out cold after Maq knocked him out with a devastating right hand. One of the VWF trainers is next to him talking to the camerman.) Trainer- Man, he's out cold. I've tried the smelling salts and everything. He's not budging. Camera- Is he alive? Trainer- Yeah. Oh, yeah...definitely. Just sleeping when he didn't chose to. Camera- Can't you do something? He's supposed to be cutting a promo. Trainer- Well, we could just leave him here. Camera- Works for me. Trainer- Ok cool. Lets go see that new Star Wars movie. Camera- Ok. Yoda is the bomb yo! (Camera fades breifly and picks up later. CAPSMAN is groggy and looking for his car in the parking lot. He finds nothing but tire marks. He stands there and scratches his head for a moment. Tires are heard screaching his car comes racing into the picture but he doesn't see it. Without even seing the car he is thrown up over the car as it screetches to a halt in the parking spot. CAPSMAN goes flying into the air and crashes to the pavement. Maq and Yak casually get out of the car.) Maq- Did you hear something Yakabi? Yak- No, not a thing. Maq- When did they add speed bumps. Those weren't here earlier were they? Yak- No...I don't remember any. (Maq and Yak walk away talking as CAPSMAN lies in the middle of the road...unconscious once again.) ------------ TYSON "T-BONE" YOUNG (The top man for the BlackJack Title is sitting at the Northtown Bar, as always but he looks angrier than normal) Classy Joey Punk! What kind of job was that? Turned on G-Gangsta, Gangsta is the man! And who are you? I respect ya dawg but the time has come, your time has come! We all know just what kind of punk you are now. We all see you for who you really are. And tell ya what punk I am going to take that belt from ya in no uncertain terms. Simple as that. I know, you gonna tell everyone about how long you've had the title, how many guys you beat and how I am just another big mouth. You damn right I'm a big mouth! But I got the stuff the back it up. And that I will do! And one last thing. Sebastian. This week on Thunder if you think you gonna stick your nose in that match between Gangsta and CJB well you better watch your back cause Gangsta, bra, I got yours! How you wanna do this at Paint The Town Red CJB? How you wanna lose that title? You can talk the talk but can you walk the walk down THE STREET! (fade) ----------- SEBASTIAN (The Sexy One is watching the tape of CJB turning on Gangsta and smashing his face into the ringpost) SEBASTIAN: This is the best part! (big laughs as Sebastian pauses just as CJB grabs Gangsta) SEBASTIAN: G-Gangsta.....CJB turns on you and damn near breaks your face and now you have to fight him right before our match at the pay per view. And if you want to do it in a cage then fine with Sebastian. Whatever you need to do to get advantage. Who does Sebastian meet this week? No one! As CJB wears down G-Gangsta, Sebastian rests in the anticipation of strapping that title around Sebastians waist for the THIRD TIME! But for now, Sebastian is resting, and enjoying must see TV! (Sebastian returns to the tape) (fade) -------------- ROB BEAUTY (Rob Beauty is sitting in his office, behind in the shadows is someone....) ROB BEAUTY: Ok. How much more do you want? Zell Strife is not the champ and Sebastian has his shot. The Maquilladora has been stripped. And now you want me to get in the ring with Zell Strife! Two weeks in a row! And a first blood match at Paint The Town Red! I know you saved my federation but how much more of this do I have to take? (silence as the Figure moves to Robs other side) I know I know. Zell doesn't know what will happen to him this week on Thunder but what if he does make it to the pay per view? What if he survives what has been planned for him this week? I mean it has been a long...LONG time since I have been in the ring. I leave that to my crazy brother. I just want to know what are the chances that Zell Strife will walk into Paint The Town Red? Tell me! What are his chances? (the figure tosses a pair of dice onto Rob Beautys desk, they come up Snake Eyes)
