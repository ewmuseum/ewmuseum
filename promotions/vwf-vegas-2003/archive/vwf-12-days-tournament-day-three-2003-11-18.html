DTMA: Starr turned it into a BACKSLIDE and almost got the fall! WEST: Starr ducked a punch, kicked CJB and hit him with a DDT! DMTA: Heath Starr sent CJB to the ropes.. and got him in the ELECTIC CHAIR! WEST: Starr then went back up to the top rope........ DTMA: And lept for the FALLING STAR!!!!!! WEST: But CJB moved! Starr crashed to the mat! DTMA: Classy Joey Boy finally got to his feet... and then did something we've never seen CJB do! WEST: It was a piledriver to start with, but CJB moved his legs over the arms of Starr... and then jumped and fell driving Starr face first into the mat. DMTA: CJB calls the new move THE REJECTION FACTOR! and it was good enough for the win! !!!!! WINNER = Classy Joey Boy !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. CUT to 12 Days headquarters and Dan The Man Aller and Dirk Western sitting at the desk DMTA: Welcome to Day 3 of the 12 Days Tournament. And Dirk this is a big night for each man in this tournament. WEST: The second match is the most important, if you lost your first one you have to win this one, and if you won the first one, a second win is almost a guarantee into the next round! DMTA: We have 8 guys who won and 8 who lost on Day 1... WEST: Lets get to Group A, and the very strange action going on in this group! .. .. .. .. .. Group A -------- \\\\\ YANCY ‘Y2K' KILPATRICK (vs) MASKED MANIAC ///// ----------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Well the question was, if the Maniac is Kilpatrick how can they meet in a match? WEST: And Kilpatrick declared he would but the Submission Title on the line in this match! DTMA: The fans at Mandalay Bay did not get to see the match ‘live' so much as on the big screens... WEST: Kilpatrick appeared and the Maniac walked up to him.... DMTA: Y2K took a swing, down went the Maniac, and Kilpatrick got on top of him for the 1-2-3! WEST: The Maniac stood up, took off his mask and it was Kilpatrick. This had to have been some kind of green screen trick. DMTA: Well no matter what it was, Kilpatrick won and soon appeared on the stage with his Submission Title as well as the Sinz days title! WEST: Y2K walked to the ring to the big cheers of the fans! !!!!! WINNER = Yancy ‘Y2K' Kilpatrick !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\ DANIEL SINZ (vs) KEVIN BLAIR ///// ---------------------------------------- DTMA: With Y2K still in the ring Daniel Sinz rushed down and tried a sneak attack on his enemy! WEST: Kilpatrick turned on him at the last second and dropped Sinz with a punch.. Kevin Blair raced to the ring and the match was on! DMTA: Kilpatrick got himself out of the ring and left, leaving Kevin Blair beating the hell out of Daniel Sinz! WEST: Kevin Blair is a true pyscho! He nailed Sinz with a SIT OUT POWERBOMB! DMTA: But Blair kept falling short of getting a fall on Sinz. WEST: Blair nailed Sinz with a CHARGING CORNER SPLASH and then a BULLDOG as Sinz came out of the corner! DMTA: But it was still only good enough for a 2 count. WEST: Blair moved to his the PYSCHO FLOW DDT.. but Sinz countered with a BACK BODY DROP! DTMA: From there Daniel Sinz started dropping Blair on his head. PILEDRIVERS! BRAINBUSTERS! WEST: I don't think Blair has anything in his head to mess up! DMTA: Sinz sent Blair to the ropes, Blair reversed and took Sinz down with a MASSIVE CLOTHESLINE! WEST: But with Blair back in charge Blackbird stepped out onto the stage area and began to .... ummm.... DTMA: Stroke... WEST: Good word, stroke Blairs chair Lucy! DMTA: Blair turned and watched in disbelief..... giving Sinz a chance to come from behind, and lock on the PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE!!!! WEST: Blair was soon out of it and after three falls of his arm this match was over! !!!!! WINNER = Daniel Sinz !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. Group B ------- \\\\\ EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) PURE RAGE JOHNNY FREIL ///// ------------------------------------------------------- DMTA: Well once again Loco came to the ring with the pinata... but Freil got the ref to have El Gringo Loco break it open... WEST: And there were no brass knuckes in the pinata, unlike on Day 1. DTMA: Freil, semi sure of Loco, began the match.... and Freil slapped a REAR CHIN LOCK on Loco and seemed content to wear down the high flyer. WEST: Loco worked his way to his feet and would break free, but Freil would ground him.... and go back to hold designed to wear down Loco. DTMA: But at the four minute mark El Gringo Loco found himself on the shoulders of Freil and hit a HURRICANRANA!!! WEST: Loc then knocked Freil back to the canvas with a MISSLE DROPKICK! DMTA: Then El Gringo Loco slapped the IRON CLAW on Freil! WEST: Yes folks, the IRON CLAW!!! DMTA: And it had Freil on one knee in some serious pain... but Freil is no boy scout... he nailed a LOW BLOW to Loco... WEST: Then Freil sent Loco to the corner via an irish whip.. and followed him in and went with the FISTS OF RAGE! DMTA: Freil jackhammering in punches to the ribs of Loco! WEST: Freil sent El Gringo Loco to the opposite and went charging in... DMTA: Jorge Luis Romero jump on the apron... WEST: Loco moved.. DTMA: And Freil went face first into a BRASS KNUCKLES punch from Romero! WEST: Loco SCHOOLBOY'd Freil and got the fall! DMTA: Once again, hijinx in an El Gringo Loco match! !!!!! WINNER = El Gringo Loco !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\ JUSHIN LIGER GOLD (vs) ‘BAD SEED' DEVIN DEMOLA ///// ---------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Demola found himself in a tough match against El Gringo Loco and Demola seemed more aware for his match with JLG! WEST: Jushin Liger GOLD took alot of punishment in a tag team match on Friday and it showed, he struggled out of the gate in this one. DTMA: Devin Demola started with a series of DROPKICKS... and slapped the occasional HEADLOCK on JLG... JLG countered and even hit a DROPKICK of his own.. but the Bad Seed took control at the three minute mark! WEST: And he did it in a big way! DMTA: Demola hit his move the CUBAN MISSLE! WEST: It rocked JLG! DMTA: Demola then hit the KISS OF THE SHADOWS on JLG... WEST: This saw Demola go the top rope and bring this one to an end with the NEW SCHOOL BULLDOG! DMTA: Jushin Liger GOLD has El Gringo Loco to blame for this! Loco left him on Friday in that brutal tag match with 28th Street. WEST: Didn't JLG win that match for his team? DMTA: Not the point! !!!!! WINNER = Bad Seed Devin Demola !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. Group C -------- \\\\\ RYAN DELANEY (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA ///// ---------------------------------------------- DMTA: The fans were pumped for this big battle, and a rematch of a 12 Days battle last year! WEST: It was Delaney last year, can Delaney beat Maq again? DMTA: At the begining of this match Delaney clearly was the favorite.. and he showed it by keeping The Maquilladora off his feet. WEST: Maq is at his heart a high flyer, and Delaney worked to keep him on the ground. DMTA: After a solid DROP TOE HOLD followed by a quick ELBOW DROP to the back of the head Ryan Delaney had Maq in a place where he could start to wear him down. WEST: One of those weird Delaney holds! DTMA: The INVERTED INDIAN DEATHLOCK LEG LOCK! WEST: Delaney held this on a struggling Maq for nearly two minutes before Maq and Delaney ended up close enough to the ropes for the ref to call for the break! DTMA: Delaney blasted Maq with a ROARING ELBOW! WEST: The Maquilladora was in trouble... and Delaney sensed it and hit the DRAGON SUPLEX on The Maquilladora... DTMA: Delaney then went for Part 2 of his finisher but Maq rolled out of the way of the SPLIT LEG MOONSAULT! WEST: Out of the way, Maq rolled out of the ring! DMTA: When Delaney got himself back together he stood up and got hung over the top rope by Maq! WEST: The Maquilladora returned to the ring via the top rope and a CORKSCREW MOONSAULT and a near fall! DTMA: But Delaney kicked out! WEST: It was damn close! DTMA: Maq then waited until Delaney was on his knees for the MAQ KICKS! WEST: Those fast feet had Delaneys head bouncing around like a gum ball! DTMA: Maq pulled Delaney to his feet, headlock and headed to the ropes! WEST: With PERMANENT DAYLIGHT only a second away Delaney caught Maq and set him on the top rope... DTMA: Delaney back away... Maq turned and found himself sitting on the top rope and facing Delaney who locked Maq.. and hit the CAVES OF KOSHTRA off the top rope!!! WEST: The place went silent... Delaney draped an arm over and got the 1-2-3! DTMA: This was a great match! If these guys finish 1-2 in this group they will have another match in the next round, lets hope for that! !!!!! WINNER = Ryan Delaney !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\ LANCE VOLTAR (vs) TYSON YOUNG ///// ----------------------------------------- DMTA: The Big V was looking for his first win... WEST: He has been looking for a long time, maybe its time to look somewhere else. DTMA: You think Voltar should leave Vegas? WEST: Noo! I think he should consider other careers... waiter... stock broker, anything but a pro wrestler.. DTMA: Voltar was on the defensive from the start of this match! Tyson Young seemed to have it all wrapped up at the four minute mark after a huge MILITARY PRESS SLAM! WEST: But while Young was celebrating, standing on the second rope, Voltar got up and hit a NECKBREAKER on Young off the second rope! DTMA: Voltar then went up to the top rope for the BIG V! WEST: But the kid slipped... fell to the outside of the ring and was counted out! DTMA: You heard right folks. Lance Voltar lost again, this time by count out! !!!!! WINNER = Tyson Young !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. Group D -------- \\\\\ ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS (vs) VIOLENT VICTOR FROST ///// --------------------------------------------------------------------- DMTA: Andrews came out and looked to teach Frost a lesson... and early on that was the match! WEST: Andrews sent Frost to the mat on a few occasions with smoe FOREARMS and POWERSLAMS! DMTA: But it was when Andrews got Frost in his GERMAN SUPLEX series that things started to looked bad for the Violent one. WEST: Andrews then added a HANGING VERTICAL SUPLEX and a cover... DTMA: But only for a 2 count! WEST: As Andrews was setting Frost up for the BOTTOM LINE, Absolute Arrogance caught a THROAT PUNCH! DTMA: And Frost was in control.... WEST: Frost worked over Andrews for a bit and then hit a big DRAGON SUPLEX! DMTA: Frost put Andrews in a front headlock..... for GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!!! WEST: A devasting move! That bombardment of HIGH KNEES! DMTA: Victor Frost set Andrews up on the ropes... and ended this battle with the VIOLATOR MARK II! WEST: Frost is looking very good right now! !!!!! WINNER = Violent Victor Frost !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\ ‘DARK MONSTER' TAI ANGELA (vs) DEVIN WOLFE ///// ------------------------------------------------------ DTMA: Once again Devin Wolfe hit the ring like a house on fire! WEST: He was clearly in control of this match and on his way to an almost easy win over the Dark Monster! DTMA: Then the mysterious figure came down the ramp.... Wolfe, distracted, turned.... WEST: Angela got the attention of the ref! DTMA: And Blackbird was picking up a chair! WEST: And Blackbird crawled in the ring and nailed Wolfe in the back of the head with a chair! DMTA: Not just any chair, Kevin Blairs chair Lucy! WEST: Angela sent Wolfe to the ropes and it was END OF THE LINE time for Devin Wolfe! !!!!! WINNER = Dark Monster Tai Angela DTMA: But while Angela and Blackbird were celebrating their win, Kevin Blair made his way to the ring in an attempt to take Lucy back! WEST: Blair nailed Angela with his PYSCHO FLOW DDT!!! And turned his attention to Blackbird... DTMA: But the New Age Army, not just the team in the tournament, but all four members made their way to the ring and the beatdown of Kevin Blair was how this night ended! WEST: What a night! DTMA: Lets take a look at the current standings in these groups! .. .. .. .. .. Group A --------- Kilpatrick, Sinz, Maniac, Blair 1-1 .. .. .. Group B -------- Devin Demola 2-0 El Gringo Loco & Johnny Freil 1-1 Jushin Liger GOLD 0-2 .. .. .. Group C -------- Ryan Delaney 2-0 The Maquilladora & Tyson Young 1-1 Lance Voltar 0-2 .. .. .. Group D -------- Victor Frost 2-0 Erik Andrews & Tai Angela 1-1 Devin Wolfe 0-2 .. .. .. DTMA: Tune in Friday as these groups end their first round action, but don't forget Wednesday! Groups E & F as well as the Tag Team Groups! WEST: More great action Wednesday! Jason Storm and Classy Joey Boy! DTMA: Don't forget Demonic and Fast Eddie Cutlass, or Jushin Liger GOLD and partner El Gringo Loco going against The Rhombus! WEST: Eight big matches! DMTA: Tune in! © 12 Days Tournament
