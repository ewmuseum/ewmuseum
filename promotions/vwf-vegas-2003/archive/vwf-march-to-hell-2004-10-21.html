VWF — Show

March To Hell

October 21, 2004

(O Fortuna begins to play, and in slow motion on the screen we see the VWF logo, then a fade to Paddy O'Shea returning to the VWF then a fade to The Maquilladora and Fast Eddie Cutlass in the ring then a fade to Alcoholics Anonymous winning the tag team titles then a fade to Sid Starr screwing over Morris Bass then a fade to Adam Jennings winning the 24 Hour Title at Vegas Violence then a fade to the return of the Revolution Randall Crane then a fade to Flash hitting Lark Fenriz, and then The Equalizer scoring the win and the VWF Heavyweight Title cut to JD Southerns at the VWF desk a MARCH TO HELL banner behind him) SOUTHERNS: Vegas Violence is in the books, and we are Marching To Hell. And hell here in the VWF is the Rage In The Cage match! Ten men, pin a champion and you take his title. But we still have a show or two before then and earlier tonight things continued to stir. Lets take a look at the singles return of Paddy O'Shea/ .. .. .. ============ SONICMAN (vs) PADDY O'SHEA ============ SOUTHERNS: O'Shea was one of the original members of the VWF and made a surprise return at Vegas Violence. But Sonicman was not impressed. Sonic went right to work on O'Shea and dominated for most of the match. Sonicman even hit a big time SHOOTING STAR that nearly got the fall. But that close call brought out The Mighty Men. With The Reckoning and The Salvation at ringside it was only a matter of time. Lets pick it up just after Sonic ducked an O'Shea punch and hit an ATOMIC DROP on the Irishman. .. .. .. WEST: AND A SAVATE KICK AS O'SHEA COMES OFF THE ROPES! DTMA: The Salvation up on the apron! And he has the attention of the ref! WEST: The Reckoning in behind Sonicman!! DTMA: KICK TO THE GUT OF SONIC AS HE TURNS! WEST: AND FOLLOWS WITH THE SIT OUT POWERBOMB!!! DMTA: O'Shea slides over and makes the cover! ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match, PADDY OOOOO SHEA!!! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Well O'Shea is off to a pretty good return, scoring the submission for his team at Vegas Violence and picking up the win here. Well the Riders of the Apocalypse were next, but which two? .. .. .. =================================== CAPSMEN! (vs) Two of the RIDERS OF THE APOCALYPSE =================================== SOUTHERNS: With the CAPS in the ring, the music hit and G-Gangsta came to the ring with his regular partner Sebastian. Aka the Legends of the VWF. Gangsta and Sebastian hit the ring, and CAPS and EVIL C tossed them right back out again! Finally it settled with EVIL C and Gangsta who seemed to be keeping up with one another until a cheap shot from Sebastian have Gangsta the upper hand. The G-Man hit his favorite moves, the Queens Crusha Tombstone and the BUCKSHOT superkick. But he could not score the fall. EVIL C rolled away eventually and made the hot tag to CAPSMAN but as he cleaned house, Robbie Caldwell and Iyasu Saku hit the ring and the beatdown began. Lets go to the call! .. .. .. WEST: Saku has slapped the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE on EVIL C and Caldwell and Sebastian are double teaming CAPSMAN! DTMA: The ref has ended this match! (HUUUGE POP!) WEST: IT'S THE MAQUILLADORA!!! AND SONICMAN!!!! DMTA: Maq and Sonic! WEST: Didn't even know Maq was here. DMTA: And that clears out the Riders. (With all four members of the Rhombus in the ring, the big screen flashes with the face of the mysterious woman who has been stalking The Maquilladora) WOMAN: Maq. (blows him a kiss then the screen goes blank) .. .. .. (CUT to JD) SOUTHERNS: And still no one knows who this woman is. But she is keeping an eye on The Maquilladora. Maq has a big match next week against Gods Tears, formerly known around here as Orion The Hunter. We will see him in that big six man tag team match later tonight. The fans went crazy as the SOD image filled the screens. .. .. .. =================== THE SOCIETY OF DOOM (vs) THE MIGHTY MEN =================== SOUTHERNS: These four men have seen a lot of each other over the past months as the SOD and NAA have battled each other tooth and nail. But this one barely got under way when Paddy O'Shea started walking down the aisle carrying a KENDO STICK! This brought out Adam Jennings from the back who jumped O'Shea before he could reach the ring. In the ring all four men battled. That brought out 25% owner CJB and some of the VWF security force, who broke up Jennings and O'Shea and escorted them to the back. With order restored Simmons and Bass began to methodically take apart The Reckoning. Like surgeons the SOD members took down The Reckoning and worked on his legs, especially the right knee. Knowing the Mighty Men well the SOD seemed on their way. But The Reckoning made the tag and The Salvation began to clear house. He knocked Simmons out of the ring and clocked Bass, the legal man, with a RUNNING CLOTHESLINE! Let go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: The Salvation, the silent one of the two has Bass in a heap of trouble. .. . . HOTSHOT ON THE TURNBUCKLE!!! WEST: And Bass hurting. Tag to the Reckoning! DTMA: The Salvation picks him up for the POWERBOMB! WEST: The Reckoning off the top rope with the CLOTHESLINE! .. . . AKA SWING LOW SWEET CHARIOT!!!! DMTA: AND THEY NAILED IT!!! ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!! BROKEN UP BY SIMMONS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WEST: And Kevin Simmons laying the smackdown on The Salvation! DTMA: The Reckoning on his feet. . . .. . . RUNNING CLOTHESLINE!!! .. . . MISSES SIMMONS AND KNOCKS DOWN THE REF!!!!! (SURPRISE POP AS THE REF JUMPS BACK TO HIS FEET!) <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: Who is that ref? DTMA: I dunno! He is a big guy though, and he just took that shot from The Reckoning and jumped right back up and called for the bell! RING ANNOUNCER: Winners of this match as a result of a disqualification, THE PREDATOR KEVIN SIMMONS and THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS!!! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. [Inside the Owners office we find Kingston Cash and Classy Joey Boy. Both dressed for business and are involved in a heated arguement.] CJB: Someone had to ref the match! We can't have a world title match without a ref. Cash: We could have sent out one of our other officials! CJB: And it could take forever to find one of them and then send them out to the ring. We need someone to ref, and we needed it immedietly! [The door to the office opens and Flash walks in. He's about to speak when CJB cuts him off.] CJB: Don't speak, this won't take long. Flash, you had no business coming down to the ring during the main event. And to make matters worse you interfered and probably cost one of two other men the world title. Those kind of actions are unacceptable. You are suspended until further notice. And I am banning you from The Rage in the Cage match. You will be escorted out of the arena by security. [Kingston Cash just smiles as Flash is forced to leave the arena.] Cash: Not bad, not bad at all. CJB: That's how you handle things Cash. Cash: Oh is it? Well, how about this? When Flash does come back, his first match will be against the man he cost the world title........Lark Fenriz!!!! .. .. .. (CUT to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So that left fans wondering, who would replace Flash in the six man tag team match? Roadkill Rick? But before we get there lets take a look at the in ring return of one of the all time legends of the VWF. .. .. .. =================================== REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE (vs) ONE OF THE RIDERS OF THE APOCALYPSE =================================== SOUTHERNS: Crane hit the ring and the fans were up and ready, but when the music of Sebastian hit the place went crazy! Sebastian walked to the ring and stepped in with Crane, and both men stood face to face waiting for the bell to ring. The place was electric. Until Sebastian asked for a microphone. Lets take a look. .. .. .. SEBASTIAN: Calm down there gimpy. Sebastian is not going to face you tonight. Sebastian has bigger plans. (POP! AS ROBBIE CALDWELL JUMPS OVER THE RING BARRIER AND STORMS INTO THE RING BEHIND CRANE) ^^^ SHOCK ^^^ DTMA: Caldwell just tazer gunned Crane! WEST: Just like he did to Bass! DTMA: Robbie Caldwell and Sebastian putting it to Crane! WEST: They place Crane on the top rope! DTMA: Here it comes! .. . . SHOCK AND HORROR BY CALDWELL!!!!! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Sebastian and Caldwell walked off after that, as the match decision went to Crane via a disqualification. But it looks like Robbie Caldwell has set his sights on a new opponent here in the VWF. And that leaves Sebastian as the only Rider to no compete so far, so he must be the one to face Jennings later for the title. Right? We will see. Now lets take a look at a match that should have happened a long time ago, should have happened at Vegas Violence, but it happened tonight. .. .. .. =============== CLASSY JOEY BOY (vs) LARK FENRIZ =============== SOUTHERNS: With both men in the ring this was a fast paced match with both men scoring big at times. Early Fenriz used his power to dominated CJB, but as the match moved on Classy Joey Boy was able to use his speed to gain some control, even hitting a SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT for a near fall. This was a back and forth battle, bordering on epic at times, near the eight minute mark Fenriz used a PILDEDRIVER on the floor. Lets pick it up with CJB on the floor of the Joint, Fenriz has just rolled back in and the count is up to six. .. .. .. WEST: CJB trying to get to his feet! REF: SEVEN!!! DTMA: Fenriz reaches down and pulls him up on the apron! WEST: SHOULDERBLOCK BY CJB! Where does he get his energy? DMTA: SUNSET FLIP!! ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! FENRIZ KICKS OUT!!!!!!!!!!! WEST: CJB up, Fenriz misses the CLOTHESLINE! DTMA: KISS OF DEATH!!!!!!!!!! WEST: CJB caught him on the turn! DTMA: And CJB points to the top rope!!! (FINISHER POP!!!) WEST: This could be it for Fenriz! DTMA: STUDD SPLASH!!!!!!!!! .. . . MISSES!!!!!!!!! WEST: Fenriz just got out of the way! That was damn close! DMTA: Fenriz in the corner, pulling himself up, he has been in some big matches over the past six weeks. WEST: CJB getting up! DTMA: FENRIZ!!! .. . . SAMMAEL!!! .. . . CJB ESCAPES!!!!! WEST: BOTH MEN SPIN AROUND!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> DMTA: NOOO! WEST: Time limit has expired! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the time limit has expired, this match is a draw! DTMA: Look at these two, the way they are looking at each other. WEST: They may have a new found respect for each other. .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Let me say, we have to get these two back in the ring. Well it was time to put up the tag team titles, newly won by AA. .. .. .. ======================== VWF Tag Team Title Match ALCOHOLICS ANONYMOUS © (vs) THE RODEO BOYS ======================== SOUTHERNS: One of the biggest shocks of Vegas Violence was the return of the Rodeo Boys, but they did not endear themselves to the fans as they brutally attacked AA just after O'Malley and McQueen won the titles. AA wasted no time in cleaning house as this match started, McQueen was on a violent tear as he slammed the heads of both Rodeo Boys into the ring steps and then dropped them both throat first on the ring barriers. Then he would roll them into the ring for O'Malley to punish. Kenny Kidd tried to use the cow bell at one point in the match but the ref (the same one who took the clothesline from The Reckoning earlier) took it from him! And when RAR protested, he was tossed from ringside! Lets go to the action, Mark McQueen has just tagged in O'Malley. .. .. .. DTMA: Billy Bones needs to make a tag. WEST: O'Malley sends Bones to the ropes... .. . . BIG BACK BODY DROP!!! DMTA: And O'Malley follows with a LEGDROP! ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!! BROKEN UP BY KIDD!!! WEST: McQueen in the ring, and he and Kidd are mixing it up! DMTA: DOWN GOES KENNY KIDD!!! WEST: Kidd rolls out of the ring! DMTA: LOW BLOW!!! Bones with a shot to the O'Malley jewels! WEST: That ref is pushing McQueen back to his corner! DMTA: Kenny Kidd is getting under the ring! WEST: Bones picks up O'Malley.. .. . . AND SLAMS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! DTMA: And Billy is right over and yelling at McQueen! (HELL NO POP!!!!) WEST: What does Kidd have??? DTMA: IT'S A BRANDING IRON!!!!!!! (SICKENING POP AS KIDD BRANDS O'MALLEY ON THE LOWER BACK!!!) WEST: That is the smell of burning flesh! DTMA: The ref saw it! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: McQueen leaps into the ring and SPEARS Bones and heads out to chase off Kidd! DMTA: We gotta get some medics out here, Marcus O'Malley is seriously hurt. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (FADE in on JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Marcus O'Malley is in UMC Medical Center with second and third degree burns. McQueen has demanded, and received, a match with Kenny Kidd next week on March to Hell II. AA went through so much to take the titles from Blaze and Real Deal and now it looks like they are going to have to go through hell to keep the titles. Will Adam Jennings have to go through hell to keep the 24 Hour Title? Lets see. .. .. .. =================================== VWF 24 Hour Title Match ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS © (vs) ONE OF THE RIDERS OF THE APOCALYPSE =================================== SOUTHERNS: Jennings was in the ring, and everyone was fully expecting Sebastian. When his music hit, all four member of the Riders came to the ring. But from behind appeared Kevin Simmons and Morris Bass. Making the odds a 4 on 3. Iyasu Saku then rushed into the ring and began to beat up on Jennings. Simmons and Bass made their way down to ringside as the Riders moved around. As Jennings and Saku battled, the SOD made sure the Riders did not get involved in the match, and when they did (like when Gangsta tripped Jennings near the ropes) Simmons and Bass chased off the Riders. In the ring, Iyasu Saku was working solely on the shoulder of Adam Jennings. And we all know why. Jennings fought him off on many occasions but the tenacious Saku continued to work and wear on the arm and shoulder of Adam Jennings. And when Saku finally caught him in the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE the other Riders were excited. Lets go to the call! .. .. .. WEST: Jennings is reaching for the ropes! DMTA: And Sebastian moving over there, he is yelling at Jennings. WEST: Here comes Simmons and Bass, and they chase off Sebastian. DTMA: I think it might be to late! Simmons telling Jennings to hold on! WEST: And Jennings reaches the ropes! DTMA: Saku breaks the hold and backs off. WEST: Jennings just looked right at us and smiled! DMTA: He was playing a little possum I think. WEST: Kick to the midsection! .. . . HYPER DRIVER!!!!!!!! DTMA: COVER!!! HERE COME THE RIDERS!!! ONE!!!!!!!! SIMMONS AND BASS STOP THE SAVE ATTEMPT!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match and still 24 Hour Champion, ADAM THE HYPE JENNINGS!!!! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: The Riders were not happy with the way this match went down and all seven men mixed it up for a bit until the Riders left back up the ramp. So the Riders and the SOD are having problems, and the NAA and The Rhombus had trouble earlier tonight. And of course we can't forget Trinity. They were up next, and a bit of a surprise before the match got under way. .. .. .. ========================================= TRINTY (vs) JASON STORM, FAST EDDIE CUTLASS and FLASH ========================================= SOUTHERNS: In the moments before the match Kingston Cash made an announcement that even though CJB is 25% owner, Cash is 75% owner! But the suspension will stand but not until after Rage In The Cage! No word yet on how CJB will take this. For the match. The new VWF Champ The Equalizer looked good in his early portions of the match as did all three members of Trinity. Storm took the lead for his team and seemed to only want to tag in Cutlass when he had the choice. Flash did get in the ring but was quickly dispatched by a SPINEBUSTER from Gods Tears. Flash tagged in Cutlass who cleared the ring of all three Trinity members, but was suckered by Orion #2, the first man he knocked down during the clearing. This led to Cutlass trapped in the Trinity corner. Cutlass finally broke free and made the hot tag to Flash! Flash cleared out Gods Tears, then The Equalizer and finally Orion #2! And the crowd was going insane! Flash then went for a SUICIDE DIVE to Gods Tears and The Equalizer, but he missed and hit the railing. With medics at ringside helping Flash and Cutlass on the floor after the long triple team Jason Storm was called into the ring by the ref as the legal man. Storm started off against The Equalizer and both men worked well, with Storm gaining the upper hand after a series of GERMAN SUPLEXES, but after the last one E was in the corner and made the tag to GT. Gods Tears was quickly taken down by Storm and for a while this match continued as a three on two. Gods Tears got Storm in the TORTURE RACK, and when Cutlass came in to make the save he was tackled by The Equalizer sending both men to the outside. GT hit a SAMOAN DROP and got a near fall. He tagged in Orion #2 and left Storm to him. GT pulled a pair of brass knuckles out and was ready to give them Orion #2 when Roadkill Rick pulled GT off the apron! With GT and Roadkill fighting on one side of the ring and Cutlass and The Equalizer on the other, Storm got to his feet and POWERBOMBED Orion #2! Then signaled for THE PERFECT STORM!!! But he had another idea first, lets go to the call! .. .. .. WEST: He is going to unmask Orion #2! DTMA: HE HAS IT!!! HE HAS THE MASK!!! (POP!!) WEST: ITS PAGAN!!! AND STORM LOOKS TO BE IN A STATE OF SHOCK! DMTA: Alice is in the ring! And she has stepped between Storm and Pagan! WEST: If I know this story right, Pagan and Alice are brother and sister!
