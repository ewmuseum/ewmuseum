12 Days Tournament: Day Four

Nov 21, 2003

DTMA: But Delaney kicked out! WEST: It was damn close! DTMA: Maq then waited until Delaney was on his knees for the MAQ KICKS! WEST: Those fast feet had Delaneys head bouncing around like a gum ball! DTMA: Maq pulled Delaney to his feet, headlock and headed to the ropes! WEST: With PERMANENT DAYLIGHT only a second away Delaney caught Maq and set him on the top rope... DTMA: Delaney back away... Maq turned and found himself sitting on the top rope and facing Delaney who locked Maq.. and hit the CAVES OF KOSHTRA off the top rope!!! WEST: The place went silent... Delaney draped an arm over and got the 1-2-3! DTMA: This was a great match! If these guys finish 1-2 in this group they will have another match in the next round, lets hope for that! !!!!! WINNER = Ryan Delaney !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. (Cut to 12 Days headquarters and Dan The Man Aller and Dirk Western) DTMA: Well Delaney and Maq had another great 12 Days match! WEST: And we are in for more action tonight! DMTA: Lets not waste anytime, lets go to the tag team brackets! .. .. .. .. .. TAG GROUP 1 ------------ \\\\\ RODEO BOYS (vs) FALLEN ANGELS ///// ----------------------------------------- DMTA: Tony Cage got the upper hand on Billy Bones to get this battle underway. WEST: Cage nailed Bones with a RUNNING POWERSLAM and tagged in Insomniac. DTMA: And the big man hit a BIG BOOT on Bones... and followed up with a SIT OUT CHOKESLAM! WEST: The Angels were doing well until Bones hit a LOW BLOW on Insomniac. DTMA: Bones knocked Cage off the apron and went to make the tag, to a very angry Kenny Kidd. WEST: Kidd of course has not realized that sometime you gotta cut corners. DTMA: Kenny Kidd went for a CROSS BODY but was caught by Insmoniac... the big man hit a BACKBREAKER on Kidd.. WEST: After a tag Tony Cage used a MISSLE DROPKICK and a FISHERMAN SUPLEX to further wear down Kidd. DMTA: Cage set Kidd up on the ropes.... Bones came in to make a save but was stopped by Insmoniac.. WEST: Cage hit a SUNSET BOMB on Kidd... DMTA: Bones hit Insomniac with a LOW BLOW.. and got the COWBELL!!! WEST: Bones broke up the fall with the COWBELL!!! DTMA: And the ref DQ'd the Rodeo Boys! WEST: For the second time, in two matches, the Rodeo Boys have been DQ'd thanks to Bones... he has to learn to hide that cowbell.. !!!!! WINNERS = Fallen Angels !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\ NEW AGE ARMY (vs) DESOLATION ROW ///// -------------------------------------------- DMTA: Iyasu Saku and Brian Hunter got this one started. WEST: Saku got the upper hand and worked on the shoulder of Hunter, seemingly hoping to wear Hunter down for the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE! DMTA: Hunter fought off Saku after a few minutes and made the tag. WEST: Demola hit a BULLDOG on Saku and made the cover... DTMA: But only got a 2 count! WEST: Demola threw some punches and Saku ducked one and went for the GOOD AND EVIL!!! DMTA: Demola quickly rolled it over but it gave Saku a chance to tag in his seven foot partner! WEST: Colossus rocked Demola and sent him backwards.. and into a tag.. DMTA: Hunter fan into the ring and right into a HUGE CHOKESLAM!!!! WEST: When Hunter got back to his feet... Colossus locked him in the ARMS OF EVIL!! DMTA: And that is when Kevin Blair... sworn enemy of the New Age Army... raced to the ring.. Blair slid in and broke up the hold.. WEST: And causing a DQ in favor of the New Age Army! DMTA: Blair got Saku in the PYSHCO FLOW DDT and bounced Saku off the mat, before taking off up the ramp! But Blair gave the NAA the win. WEST: Blair is not completely there, if you know what I mean. !!!!! WINNERS = New Age Army !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. .. TAG GROUP 2 ------------ \\\\\ JUSHIN LIGER GOLD & EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) THE RHOMBUS ///// --------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: The match started with Sonicman asking CAPSMAN to pull up his mask.. WEST: Sonic was checking to make sure CAPS was not EVIL CAPS, like last Friday. DTMA: CAPS, was CAPS... and Sonic started the match against El Gringo Loco, forced by JLG to get in the ring. WEST: Last Friday JLG did all the work on his own. DMTA: This was a much anticipated match, the current Vegas tag team champs, JLG and Loco against the only team to win the titles twice. WEST: Sonic took the battle to Loco, much to the enjoyment of the fans. DTMA: A nice GERMAN SUPLEX and then a tag. WEST: CAPS hit the ring and took right to the air... a FLYING FOREARM led to a near fall.. DTMA: CAPS tagged and he and Sonic hit a DOUBLE FLAPJACK on Loco... WEST: But Loco kicked out... DMTA: Sonic doubled over Loco in the center of the ring and came off the ropes looking to hit a SCISSORS KICK... but Loco moved and dropped Sonic with a NECKBREAKER! WEST: Loco tagged in JLG! DMTA: The fans were chanting WE WANT GOLD! WEST: They got it, a HIGH LEG CLOTHESLINE..... followed by a BIG AIR MOONSAULT! DMTA: JLG locked Sonic in a FULL NELSON and for the next few minutes the action was on the mat. WEST: What action??? DMTA: JLG wanting to keep Sonic down and wear him out a bit. WEST: Sonic got to his feet..... and hit a SNAP MARE on JLG... DMTA: Sonic then tagged in CAPSMAN! WEST: CAPS nailed JLG with a FLYING HEADBUTT!!! CAPS then caught JLG in the CAPS FACTOR!!!!! DMTA: But instead of going for the pin, CAPS started to set up Jushin Liger GOLD for the CAPITOL PUNISHMENT!!!! WEST: But Jorge pulled out the brass knuckles... Loco distracted the ref! DTMA: CAPS hit the CAPITOL PUNISHMENT on JLG! WEST: There was not count since the ref was getting an ear full from Loco... DMTA: As Jorge was about to clock CAPS.. Mr Hinote came into the ring and took the brass knuckes from Jorge! WEST: Sonicman knocked Loco off the apron.. and the ref turned to find Jorge and Mr Hinote in the ring... and he DQ'd the team of JLG and Loco! !!!!! WINNERS = The Rhombus !!!!! .. .. .. \\\\\ LEGENDS OF THE VWF (vs) 28TH STREET ///// ----------------------------------------------- DMTA: Three tag matches, three DQ's. WEST: There was no DQ here. DTMA: This one was all Legends. WEST: D-Rock had little offense... Gangsta and Sebastian used quick tags and double team moves to wear him down. DMTA: D-Rock did tag Hakeem, who quickly got the BUCKSHOT from Gangsta. WEST: Gangsta tagged in Sebastian... who hit the TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER on Hakeem and then slapped on the SEX YOU OUT boston crab. DTMA: Gangsta made sure D-Rock was not making the save and Hakeem tapped out! !!!!! WINNERS = The Legends of the VWF !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. GROUP E ------- \\\\\ DEMONIC (vs) FAST EDDIE CUTLASS ///// ------------------------------------------- DMTA: Cutlass hit the ring and started off with a hit and run tactic on the bigger Demonic. WEST: Smart move, Cutlass put a few DROPKICKS and FOREARM SHIVERS but tried to stay out of the way of Demonic, which worked well for about three minutes or so. DTMA: Until Demonic hit a VICIOUS POWERSLAM on Cutlass... and then it was all Demonic! WEST: Demonic began pounding Fast Eddie Cutlass... puncuated with a beautifully executed BRAINBUSTER!! DTMA: Demonic even hit a DOUBLE UNDERHOOK OVER THE SHOULDER POWERBOMB!!! But Cutlass got a shoulder up! WEST: Demonic tossed Cutlass hard into the corner and wore him down with some KNIFE EDGE CHOPS, SNAP MARED him out of the corner and SLAPPED A SLEEPER HOLD on Fast Eddie Cutlass. DTMA: Cutlass was struggling as his future in this tournament was slipping away, 0-2 is not a good place. WEST: Fast Eddie finally got up some strength and SNAP MARED Demonic over to break the hold.... but as Eddie got to his feet... BIG BOOT from Demonic! DMTA: The big demon picked Cutlass to his feet and place him... WEST: For the PITZ OF HELL!!!!! DTMA: But Cutlass jumped over, landed behind Demonic and in the blink of an eye, hit the GUARANTEE!!!!!! WEST: Fast Eddie Cutlass got the fall and a huge win! !!!!! WINNER = Fast Eddie Cutlass !!!!! .. .. .. \\\\\ THE WOLF LARK FENRIZ (vs) BLAZE ///// ------------------------------------------- DTMA: Blaze tried to mount an early offense but Fenriz stayed a step ahead.. WEST: And Lark his a CRADLE PILEDRIVER at the three minute mark that took alot out of Blaze. DMTA: From there Fenriz was in charge of the pace of this match... Fenriz used a few STIFF KICKS to the knees to keep Blaze wobbly. WEST: Wobbly? DTMA: Yeah.... Fenriz sent Blaze to the ropes for a TILT A WHIRL but Blaze got Fenriz with a BIG CLOTHESLINE!!! WEST: Blaze, much like Cutlass was suddenly in charge... DTMA: But unlike Cutlass, Blaze could not use the sudden momentum shift for his favor. WEST: Fenriz BACK BODY DROPPED Blaze on what looked like a powerbomb attempt.. DMTA: And a short while later.... SAMMAEL! by Fenriz for the 1-2-3! WEST: Hey, he is 2-0! !!!!! WINNER = The Wolf Lark Fenriz !!!!! .. .. .. GROUP F ------- \\\\\\ HEATH STARR (vs) MARCEL KLUTZ ///// ------------------------------------------ DMTA: Klutz had no chance.. WEST: Starr looked great in a tough loss to CJB on Day 2..... he rebounded here and keeps himself alive in this group... DTMA: Starr hit the STARR STRUCK about two minutes in and then ended this with the FALLING STARR!!! !!!!! WINNER = Heath Starr !!!!! .. .. .. \\\\\ JASON STORM (vs) CLASSY JOEY BOY ///// -------------------------------------------- DMTA: Lots of talk between these two... Storm was friends with Randall Crane. WEST: Storm rushed in at CJB, and Classy Joey Boy hit Storm with a SHOULDER to the knee! DTMA: And CJB went right to work on the knee of Jason Storm.... his M. O.! WEST: Classy Joey Boy wishboned Storm on the ring posts.... drops elbows on the knees and even hit a few KNEEBREAKERS for good measure.. DTMA: All looking to end this with the SUGAR BLASTER! WEST: Storm was on his feet when CJB got him in a nice POWERBOMB! Not a usual CJB move... DTMA: Classy Joey Boy then went to the second rope and hit a SPLIT LEG MOONSAULT! WEST: Vintage Classy Joey Boy...... off the ropes... he made his bones in Vegas in the first 12 Days Tournament back in 2001... DMTA: With the high flying style..... WEST: CJB came off the second rope with a CROSS BODY! DMTA: And was caught by Storm... WEST: But Storms knee crumbled under him and CJB got the fall! DMTA: Almost... WEST: Right.. DMTA: Storm then broke up the SUGAR BLASTER attempt with a RAKE OF THE EYES! WEST: Storm found himself on his feet and BOMBED CJB with a few FOREARMS! DMTA: Feeling his knee better, Storm nailed CJB with a NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! WEST: And Storm started to work his magic... DMTA: Storm placed CJB on the top rope for a ROOF RASIING FRONT LAYOUT SUPERPLEX!!! WEST: And he scored the fall! DTMA: Almost! WEST: Right... DTMA: Storm motioned to the crowd who went wild, thinking it was time for the PERFECT STORM!!! WEST: But CJB floated off Storm and hit a SUPERKICK on the back of the knee of Storm! DTMA: Classy Joey Boy waited.... and hit the KISS OF DEATH!!! WEST: CJB then climbed to the top rope.... for a true vintage CJB move.. DTMA: THE STUDD SPLASH!!!!!!!! WEST: Which Storm moved out of the way of at the last minute! DTMA: Storm picked up CJB and they went over the corner... Storm pounded CJB's head off the turnbuckles and then set CJB up on the top rope... WEST: Storm got up there with him! DTMA: The place went A S B with both men on the top! WEST: Jason Storm had a look in his eye like wanted to hurt CJB... but that is Storm.. DTMA: Storm had CJB in the RACK!!! WEST: And was looking for a top rope like version of the PERFECT STORM when his knee crumbled and both men fell off the top rope and to the floor! DMTA: The place was crazy! Not a fan in the place was in control! WEST: And the ref counted..... DTMA: And neither man was able to get back in the ring before the ten count. WEST: Our first draw! A double count out... and if these guys meet in the next round.... WOW! DTMA: WOW! !!!!! Double Count Out Draw !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. DTMA: Lets look at the standings, if you will! Tag Group 1 ------------ Fallen Angels 2-0 New Age Army & Desolation Row 1-1 The Rodeo Boys 0-2 Tag Group 2 ------------ The Legends of the VWF 2-0 JLG/Loco & The Rhombus 1-1 28th Street 0-2 Group E -------- Lark Fenriz 2-0 Eddie Cutlass & Demonic 1-1 Blaze 0-2 Group F -------- Jason Storm & CJB 1-0-1 Heath Starr 1-1 Marcel Klutz 0-2 .. .. .. .. WEST: Tune in Friday! The last first round day for Groups A, B, C and D! DMTA: Kilpatrick and Sinz! Titles on the line! WEST: Andrews and Angela, winner moves on! DTMA: And El Gringo Loco and JLG, one on one! WEST: And these groups have until next Tuesday... but some of these guys have their backs to the wall! DMTA: And what a surprise, The 0-2 Rodeo Boys! WEST: See you Friday! DTMA: Thank you. © 12 Days Tournament
