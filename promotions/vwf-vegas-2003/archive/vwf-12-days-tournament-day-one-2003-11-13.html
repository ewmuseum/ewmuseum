(The screen comes alive with quick shots of various action from the tournament........ and slowly only the faces of Bad Seed Devin Demola, Ryan Delaney and Jason Storm are left....... then a slow fade from the picture of three finalists to a shot of the action at ringside, Demola, Delaney and Storm are all raising each others hands above their head as the Vegas fans are going crazy.... letting all three know how much they enjoyed the final match of the 12 Days Tournament) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT to Dan The Man Aller sitting at 12 Days headquarters) DTMA: Welcome to the Twelve Days of Christmas Tournament! My name is Dan The Man Aller and sitting here with me is former VWF star Dirk Western. WEST: Thanks Dan. DTMA: Well Dirk we are about to embark on our third journey in this tournament. WEST: We just saw the finals of the last two years, do you think everyone will be shaking hands at the end of this years competition? DMTA: From the words going around I don't think so but we can hope. WEST: The action so far has not been of the hand shaking kind! DMTA: Good point Dirk! Lets go the action and some big events at Mandalay Bay! .. .. .. .. .. GROUP A -------- \\\\\\\ YANCY ‘Y2K' KILPATRICK (vs) KEVIN BLAIR /////// ------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Kevin Blair made his way to the ring, that right arm wrapped up after taking a serious injury against Tai Angela back on November 4. WEST: But then the first big news of the night came! As we thought Yancy Kilpatrick was on his way to the ring.... "Ladies and gentlemen, his opponent is from Las Vegas, Nevada, and weighs in at 232 pounds. He is Yancy Kilpatrick...Y2K!!" [The crowd comes to its feet as Smash Mouth's "Hang On" begins to blast over the P.A. System.] # Things are getting weird, things are getting tough, Nothing's making sense but you keep on looking up They'll tell you to be true, they'll try it everyday To keep it on the real still you got to find a way So make your mamma happy, to make your pappa proud You'll wanna turn it up and all you hear is turn it down # So gather round I'm here to say They'll never make everybody's day But while you're around you might as well, catch the tiger by it's tail # And hang on hang on hang on Everybody just get on get on get on Get started and go on go on go on Everybody just hold on # [Twin jets of green flame erupt at the top of the ramp and the crowd comes to its feet but, instead of Y2K, they are treated to the sight of the VWF Submission Champion's lovely wife, Melanie. Dressed in a pair of painted on black jeans and an oversized white VWF t-shirt, the still-sexy mother of two makes her way down the aisle and steps into the ring, seemingly unafraid of being face-to-face with a psychopath like Kevin Blair.] Mel: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm afraid my husband will be unable to wrestle here tonight. [The crowd boos.] Mel: As much as he wishes he could be here, Yancy's doctors have NOT cleared him to wrestle Mr. Blair and, as a result, I'm afraid I've been asked to come out here tonight...and forfeit. [More boos.] Mel: But, Kevin, I want you to understand something. My husband forfeiting this match to you is NOT a sign of weakness. He's not afraid of you or your chair... And given the opportunity, he'll be more than happy to meet you in this very ring...and kick your ass up and down the Vegas Strip!! [The crowd pops HUGE and, as Blair rears back to take a shot at the lovely Mrs. Kilpatrick, she BLASTS him right between the legs and rolls out of the ring, making her escape as "Hang On" begins to play again.] !!!!! WINNER = Kevin Blair !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ DANIEL SINZ (vs) MASKED MANIAC /////// ---------------------------------------------- DMTA: Sinz came to the ring with his self procalimed Sinz Days title belt. WEST: Hey, Sinz won the Dog Days tournament over the summer. DTMA: But its not a title per se.. WEST: Per se???? Did you learn French? DMTA: Then the Masked Maniac came to the ring, and he looked kinda familiar. WEST: And Daniel Sinz started off by beating the snot out of the unknown masked guy. DMTA: Sinz seemed to be taking out his anger on the Maniac. With Kilpatrick out Sinz was not happy. WEST: Sinz drove the Maniac into the canvas with a TOWERING POWERBOMB and almost got the THREE COUNT! DMTA: Back on his feet, the Maniac went for a CROSS BODY only to be caught by Sinz who turned it into a OVER THE HEAD FALLAWAY SLAM! WEST: Sinz started to work on the Maniac with a CROSS FACE! DMTA: The Maniac was behind the eight ball for all of the match and Daniel Sinz was ready to wear him down and put him away. WEST: Sinz rocked him with a HAMMERLOCK SUPLEX and waited on the Maniac to get to his feet. DMTA: And when he did Sinz moved for the PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE!!!! WEST: But the Maniac slipped behind.. and one SCHOOLBOY later Daniel Sinz had been pinned to the mat! DMTA: The place erupted!!! The Masked Maniac rolled out of the ring and the ref handed him the Sinz Days Title...... WEST: And then it happened! DMTA: The Masked Maniac pulled off his mask to reveal himself as... Y 2 K himself!!! WEST: Thats right, Yancy Kilpatrick entered himself twice in the tournament. Once as himself and the other as the Masked Maniac! DMTA: Well the Maniac walked up the ramp with the Sinz Days Title as Daniel Sinz screamed at the ref. WEST: I am sure that Sinz will file a formal complaint with the 12 Days people but the bottom line is that Sinz is 0-1 in the Group. DMTA: But what about Y2K? I mean does he have to be in two matches each night form now on??? WEST: Lets hope so! DMTA: Lets move on... to a big match in Group B! !!!!! WINNER = Masked Manaic !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. GROUP B ------- \\\\\\\ EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) BAD SEED DEVIN DEMOLA /////// --------------------------------------------------------- WEST: El Gringo Loco made a big entrance for this match! ( The lights dim and a video screen shows many views of different areas of Mexico as the Gypsy Kings' "Hotel California" plays. The crowd waits patiently for the arrival of the self-proclaimed greatest star in the history of lucha libre. ) ( The song suddenly stops and the images on the screen change to the decorative lucha libre mask. Two small sets of fireworks go off on the side of the entrance while Jorge Luis Romero steps out and looks over the fans to pick out any dangerous looking people. Behind Jorge is a mariachi band who all fall into position. Then a a man walks out carrying a pinata on a pole. Many young children run out behind him and use sticks to try to break it open. ) WEST: He brought a band???? ( Finally two lovely senoritas come out and begin to dance behind the children. Confetti begins to fall from the ceiling. The mariachi band plays a song and everyone moves to the ring while El Gringo Loco finally steps out dressed in a green bodysuit with red trim, his red mask, and the flag of Mexico as a cape. The entire party walks to the ring amidst the boos of the crowd, but Jorge is quick to keep the dirty hands of the people away from Mexico's greatest hero. ) WEST: And chicks!!! Yeeeees!! ( Once El Gringo Loco enters the ring, he removes his cape and twirls it around his body before handing it to Jorge and walking back to his corner. The mariachi band stop their song and everyone gathers around in El Gringo Loco's corner. ) DMTA: The fierce words between these two moved right into the ring! WEST: Loco and Demola met head on when the bell rang and started off exchanging punches... until Demola went for a SPINNING HEEL KICK! DMTA: Loco caught it and took control with a solid ENGUIRIZI! WEST: Loco took to the air... a series of MOONSAULTS and ELBOWDROPS from the second and top ropes.. but the Bad Seed kept kicking out! DMTA: At the six minute mark El Gringo Loco missed a frog splash... Demola rolled out of the way and used this chance to gain control of the match. WEST: Last years champ sent Loco to the ropes and nailed him with his move the KISS OF THE SHADOWS! DTMA: The double leg lariat rocked Loco, but was only good for a close fall! WEST: Demola used his fists to try and soften up the superstar from Mexico, and then went for another fall after a DIAMOND DUST! DTMA: But once again Loco was able to kick out. WEST: Demola began to look frustrated, I think he came in thinking he was going to walk through the early rounds of the tournament as defending champ. DTMA: Well Demola is in one tough group, and El Gringo Loco was showing him just how tough it is going to be. WEST: Demola went for a SHOOTING STAR PRESS, but El Gringo Loco rolled out of the way and the momentum shifted back in favor of the half member of the tag team champs. DTMA: Loco hit a RUNNING KNEEDROP and then pulled Demola up and hit a FISHERMAN BUSTER! WEST: But he could only get a 2 and an almost count! DMTA: El Gringo Loco then got the Bad Seed in the TREE OF WOE, and then hit a ROLLING CLOTHESLINE, a move El Gringo Loco calls the RUN FOR THE BORDER! And went for the fall... ONE!!! TWO!!! But Demola kicked out to a HUUUUGE gasp from the fans! WEST: The fans were on their feet at this point.... DTMA: Loco went for an irish whip but Demola reversed it... and he clocked Loco with a CLOTHESLINE! WEST: Demola then used the ropes to choke Loco, and whisper some sweet nothings in his ear. DTMA: I doubt they were sweet nothings. WEST: Demola lifted Loco up in a powerbomb position... and on the way to the mat turned it into a type of DDT, a move Demola calls the DEMOLA ROLL 2K2! DTMA: Devin made the cover.. ONE!! TWO!!!! But Loco got a shoulder up at the very last second! WEST: The look on Demola's face showed that Loco was getting under his skin. DTMA: Devin Demola then climbed up to the top rope to a big pop from the crowd! WEST: Demola jumped... and missed El Gringo Loco for the NEW SCHOOL BULLDOG!! DMTA: Sensing his chance, El Gringo Loco made his way to his corner and broke open the pinata and pulled out a pair of BRASS KNUCKLES! WEST: Jorge got the attention of the ref and El Gringo Loco waited, and CRAAAAAAACKED Demola in the side of the head! DTMA: Loco made the cover and got the fall!!!! ONE!! TWO!! THREEE!!!! WEST: But the place erupted when Jushin Liger GOLD ran to ringside, and let the ref know about the brass knuckles!!! The ref searched Loco, found the brass knuckles and reversed his decision and DISQUALIFIED El Gringo Loco! DTMA: Shocking finish, I thought our defending champ had lost his first match back! WEST: Well this was one hell of a tough fight for Demola.. and he still has Jushin Liger GOLD and Johnny Freil in this group. And who knows what they have up their sleeves! DTMA: The defending champ has a long way to go but he is off to a good start, and El Gringo Loco showed he has the stuff to hang with Demola. If they meet again it would be great. Let's hope they do! WEST: This match was followed by Loco's tag team partner Jushin Liger GOLD! !!!!! WINNER= Bad Seed Devin Demola !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ JUSHIN LIGER GOLD (vs) PURE RAGE JOHNNY FREIL /////// ------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Freil took fast control and used his fists to knock JLG off balance. WEST: Johnny Freil is small but has one of the best right hands in this business, and maybe the best one in this tournament. DMTA: The former shootfighter, Freil is one tough SOB! WEST: He ripped JLG with a SPRINGBOARD SPEAR and nearly got the fall, but JLG got a shoulder up! DMTA: Freil was going for a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP when JLG blocked it, HIP TOSSED Freil.. and then as Freil was getting to his feet JLG hit Pure Rage with a SUPERKICK that could be heard in the upper rows! WEST: I thought JLG had a real chance at finishing off Freil at this point but he became distracted by his tag team partner, El Gringo Loco who jumped up on the apron and began yelling at JLG! DTMA: Jushin Liger GOLD came over and pushed Loco off the apron, but when he turned around he ran right into the fast angry fists of Pure Rage! WEST: Freil worked over the ribs of JLG, we have seen him do that in previous matches, and JLG was soon sucking for air! DTMA: A doubled over JLG was easy for a DOUBLE UNDERHOOK FACE PLANT! WEST: Well Johnny Freil has been on a mission since coming back from his injuries... DTMA: Freil then sent JLG to the ropes.. but Jushin Liger GOLD came off and CRUSHED Freil with a FLYING FOREARM! WEST: And from here JLG took control of the battle... A HIGH LEG CLOTHESLINE! DTMA: A SNAP SUPLEX! WEST: A MISSLE DROPKICK!! DTMA: And then a SHARPSHOOTER to wear down Freil! WEST: But it was after about a minute of the SHARPSHOOTER that El Gringo Loco was back up on the apron and screaming at his hated enemy and tag team partner! DTMA: With the fans chanting WE WANT GOLD!... JLG broke the hold and went to knock Loco off the apron for a second time... but GOLD got nailed by those brass knuckles! WEST: Jushin Liger GOLD went down like a sack of potatoes... DTMA: Freil got himself up and made his move... locking the RAGING LOCK on GOLD... JLG was out cold and the ref had no choice but to award the match to Johnny Freil! !!!!! WINNER = Pure Rage Johnny Freil !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. GROUP C -------- \\\\\\\ RYAN DELANEY (vs) TYSON YOUNG /////// --------------------------------------------- DTMA: Well last year Delaney came within a whisper of taking the Gold Medal! WEST: He and Jason Storm both are in the almost but no gold category! DMTA: Delaney started this years tournament off very strong against Tyson Young. WEST: Some fans here in Vegas remember Young from some battles against Classy Joey Boy in the past, but Young did not look like he was ready to be in the ring after his time off. DTMA: Young got control early and hit a few POWERSLAMS but Delaney was soon on control and wearing down Young with those interesting moves of his! WEST: A STEP OVER TOE HOLD FACE LOCK WITH DOUBLE ARM LOCK had Young red and in serious pain! DTMA: Each time Young would break free, Delaney would take him right back down. WEST: It seemed like this one was just a matter of time from the begining. DTMA: Delaney nailed a SHOOTING STAR PRESS and the fans began to chant for the DRAGON! WEST: Delaney hit both parts of CHASING THE DRAGON and put this one away in just under four minutes... !!!!! WINNER = Ryan Delaney !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ LANCE VOLTAR (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA /////// -------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Voltar is searching for his first win. WEST: This kid keeps coming out to the ring and getting his ass kicked, week after week! DTMA: And this week was no exception! WEST: Voltar mounted some aerial moves but Maq took control with his MAQ KICKS and Voltar was on dream street! DMTA: Maq brought this one to an end with PERMANENT DAYLIGHT at the three minute mark! !!!!! WINNER = The Maquilladora !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. GROUP D --------- \\\\\\\ ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS (vs) DEVIN WOLFE /////// ---------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Wolfe came to the ring to a huge ovation from the crowd, Wolfe has been fighting injuries and sneak attacks for a few monthes... WEST: And the fans greeted Andrews as well, they hate this guy. DTMA: Erik Andrews is one of the most hated guys in the history of Vegas, no matter if it is the VWF, RAW-Vegas or this tournament. WEST: Well the fans enjoyed Devin Wolfe tossing Andrews around without any problems... DTMA: Wolfe was slamming Andrews all over the ring... and he tossed in a few SPINEBUSTERS for good measure! WEST: Devin Wolfe then lifted Andrews above his head for the RAISE THE DEAD press slam!!! DTMA: But Andrews legs hit the ref on the way down. This brought an unknown man out of the crowd, carrying a crowbar... the man rolled in the ring behind Wolfe, clocked him on the back of the head and then rolled back out and disappeared into the crowd! WEST: EA took the chance to wake the ref and make a quick cover for the 1-2-3! !!!!! Absolute Arrogance Erik Andrews !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA (vs) VIOLENT VICTOR FROST /////// ----------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Frost hit the ring first and looked very ready for his debut in the 12 Days Tournament. WEST: Angela came to the ring next, his past appearances in this tournament have been disapointing.. DTMA: But I don't know if he is interested in winning or gaining converts... WEST: Frost was not interested in letting Angela win and becoming a member of the New Age Army! Frost quickly set about to beating up Angela! DTMA: But the Dark Monster fought back just as hard.... but Frost got behind Angela and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX that gave him the upper hand. WEST: Frost then locked the Monster in the front headlock and just bombarded him with a series of knee strikes.. DTMA: A move Frost calls GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! WEST: But it was only good for a 2 count! DTMA: Frost then hit a THROAT PUNCH on Angela and then hit a PILEDRIVER! WEST: Very nicely done as well, on the piledriver. A great move that to many of the young guys don't do... DTMA: Frost worked over Angela for a bit more before sending him hard into the corner... but Angela moved out of the way of a Frost SPEAR! WEST: Violent Victor crumbled out of the corner and into a SWINGING NECKBREAKER by Tai! DTMA: Angela put the ABDOMINAL STRETCH on Frost and really worked it hard... WEST: The minutes passed and Frost was clearing struggling... he moved for a HIP TOSS on a few occasions but Angela was able to either block it or fight it off.... DMTA: But Angela soon came to realize that Frost was not going to submit.. Angela made a quick move and turned it into a sudden RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP! WEST: Angela then cracked his knuckes.... and the crowd knew what was about to come... DTMA: Angela sent Frost to the ropes for the... END OF THE LINE!!!!! WEST: Frost ducked it!! Kicked Angela in the gut..... and picked him up in a firemans carry position.... carried Angela over and got on the second rope with him... DMTA: And ended it with the VIOLATOR MARK II!!! WEST: Great finish to the match, as well as the end of Day One! !!!!! WINNER = Violent Victor Frost !!!!! .. .. .. .. DMTA: Lets take a look....
