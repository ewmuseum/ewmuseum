Dog Day One

June 11, 2004

(Shot opens with a cheap cassette player, a hand comes in the shot and hits the play button. The song WHO LET THE DOGS OUT begins to blare out of the speakers. Suddenly a sledgehammer smashes the damn thing into pieces. CUT to JD Southerns at the VWF desk) SOUTHERNS: It is time for the Dog Days of summer! Long hot nights, humid days and blood sweat and tears. And what a night he saw! The first night, battle royals and two elimination matches. Lets not waste any time, lets go right to the action. .. .. .. ===================== TAG TEAM BATTLE ROYAL ===================== SOUTHERNS: The eleven teams all made their way to the ring and when the bell rang it was on! Getting a good foot right from the start were newcomers The Mighty Men. One called the Reckoning and the other called the Salvation these two big guys moved in on the team of O'Malley and Mark McQueen. The Rodeo Boys mixed it up with Hot Property and the Unholy Alliance, while Jack Blast got caught by Sebastian, while his partner Jack Smash was getting double teamed by CAPSMEN! A clothesline from Blaze to Johnny Freil of the Fallen Angels caused the first elimination of the night, as Freil went over the top and the Fallen Angels were gone. The Reckoning almost eliminated Mark McQueen but partner Marcus O'Malley made the save. Orion and The Equalizer were deep in the fight going after anyone who got in their area. Kenny Kidd got a near FACE PLANT, but was saved by an inadvertent knockdown by Sebastian. This left Sebastian face to face with The Equalizer, who has Sebastian backing up, until Sonicman made the save on his manager / partner. Orion came over, but while these four were fighting EVIL CAPSMAN was sent over the top rope by the Salvation, and CAPSMEN! were gone. Mighty Man Salvation turned and got a BIG BOOT from Real Deal and he was out of the ring, eliminating the Mighty Men from the battle royal. The Mistress got a DOUBLE FLAPJACK from Stevie Knight and Austin Lee and the Hot Property boys were ready to toss her out when her partner Dementia made the save. Dementia handled both Knight and Lee but he got tossed over by Blaze and Real Deal from behind. Jack Smash was the next to go as Billy Bones sent him over the top rope. But as Bones turned he got a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE from the SOD, but Bones landed on the ring apron and therefore was not eliminated. The SOD then turned and did the same thing to Sonicman, who also landed on the opposite apron. But thinking Sonicman had landed on the floor an upset Sebastian spun around and jumped out of the ring, eliminating his own team! Sonicman rushed over and he and Sebastian exchanged heated words all the way up the ramp and into the back. The Equalizer sent Stevie Knight to the ropes, Knight caught him with a DROPKICK coming off which spun E around, he and Blaze bumped heads. E dropped to the mat, and Blaze went flying over the ropes and to the floor. Orion was leaning over checking on his partner when Mark MCQUeen got up under him and tossed him over the top rope, but Orion was hanging on and pulled McQueen over with him! Leaving the Rodeo Boys and Hot Property in the ring! Bones fought with Knight while Kidd battled Austin Lee! Knight came off the ropes and hit a SPRINGBOARD FLYING CROSS BODY to Bones, just as Austin Lee DDT'd Kenny Kidd! Lee got up and began to un-do the protective barrier on the turnbuckled, but while his back was turned, Bones got free from Knight and suddenly had Lee up in the air and over the top rope! Lets go to the ring announcers call! .. .. .. RING ANNOUNCER: Winners of Dog Day One! Immune from elimination next week. . . THE RODEO BOYS!!!! Now, Billy, Kenny who do you choose for tonights Triple Threat Elimination match? (FANS BEGIN TO CHANT VARIOUS NAMES OF TEAMS) KENNY KIDD: Hmmm, how about THE FALLEN ANGELS! BILLY BONES: the JUMPING JACKS! KIDD & BONES: And of course, HOT PROPERTY!!! (HUUUGE POP!!!!) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: So the Rodeo Boys made their picks and we will get to how that match played out later on, but now, we have the singles battle royal! .. .. .. ==================== SINGLES BATTLE ROYAL ==================== SOUTHERNS: It was nothing but confusion after the bell rang. It seemed like each man was after each man. After a few minutes everyone seemed to pair off to dance with one partner. But first to go over and out was Tony Cage, the Arsonist went for a FLYONG BODY PRESS to newcomer Lord Zero. Zero caught him and hit a FALLAWAY that sent Cage to the floor. Morris Bass was locked in mortal combat with Jason Storm when they were broken up by the maniacal Kevin Blair. Blair dropped both men to the canvas, but then he noticed a ring side official moving his 24 Hour Title belt. Blair was furious and lept over the top rope to the floor and attacked the official. Blair ended the assault with the PYSCHO FLOW DDT, but he was out of the battle royal. Blair embraced the title and walked up the ramp. But halfway up the ramp Blair was run down by Setzer Van Strife, who eliminated himself after watching Blair attack the ringside official. Blair and SVS exchanged blows up and ramp and into the back. In the ring it seemed like everyone wanted a piece of Flash, but the man of a millions words was staying on his feet. Sidious The Clown then came off the ropes, only to be sidestepped by LOCO and Sid went out and onto the arena floor. Lord Zero was working over Morris Bass, when Kagedo made the save. Kags and Lord Zero got into a pretty heated exchange, that ended when they were both tossed over by Jason and Lark Fenriz. In a strange twist, it seemed like Fenriz and Storm were helping each other. Until they took notice of who they helped, and the fight was on! Fenriz was a wild man going after Storm with everything he had. Storm fought back but the pure fury of Fenriz had Jason Storm in a lot of trouble. LOCO joined in and the three men began to beat each other senseless in the corner. On the outside, Lord Zero locked Kagedo in a sleeper hold, then drive Kags into the arena floor with a move he calls the FRAYED ENDS OF SANITY! Back in the ring, Fenriz Storm and LOCO were tearing each other apart while Flash was trying to fight off Morris Bass. But in the center of the ring The Maquilladora and Daniel Sinz were standing face to face. Two old rivals, they both were part of last years Dog Day Final. As the excitement was building, the fans let out a disappointment pop as Morris Bass took down both men with a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE. Morris Bass picked up The Maquilladora and was ready to toss him out via a POWERSLAM when Flash got in and hit him from behind sending both men over the top rope. Bass landed on the floor but Maq landed on the ring apron. Flash turned to celebrate, The Maquilladora jumped back in the ring and flipped Flash out via a FLYING HEAD SCISSORS. But as Maq got to his feet, he was locked in the PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE by Daniel Sinz. Sinz had the submission hold on The Maquilladora and was not letting to by any means. Well, until Jason Storm broke it up. Storm grabbed Sinz and sent him over the ropes and to the floor. While at the same time, LOCO came over and sent The Maquilladora to the floor. Storm and LOCO faced off and the former half of the VWF tag team champions was all spit and fire. LOCO worked Storm around the ring, and sent him from corner to corner. But as LOCO rushed in at one point Storm moved, and as LOCO came stumbling back out from his missed corner splash, Fenriz was there for a powerful SPINEBUSTER on LOCO! Lark then turned to Storm and set up Jason for a vertical suplex, but Storm blocked it, reversed it and dropped Lark Fenriz to the outside. LOCO rushed Storm from behind but there was no one there and LOCO ended up hitting Fenriz with a FLYING CLOTHESLINE as he went over the top rope and hit the floor. Leaving Jason Storm as the battle royal winner, lets to the call. .. .. .. RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of the singles battle royal, immune from elimination next week, JASON STORM!! (SLIGHT EDDIE! EDDIE! CHANTS, BUT A POP FOR STORM) JASON STORM: Three men for elimination?? Hmmm. .. . . of course our champ LARK FENRIZ!!! .. . . and how about last years Dog Day Winner DANIEL SINZ! .. . . and hmmm, submission champion THE MAQUILLADORA!!! (HHHHUUUGE POP!!!!) .. .. .. (CUT back to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Whoa! Only one man can win that triple threat to stay in the tournament! Well, lets go to the tag team elimination match. .. .. .. .. .. ================================================== Dog Days Tag Team Elimination Match FALLEN ANGELS (vs) JUMPING JACKS (vs) HOT PROPERTY ================================================== SOUTHERNS: Jack Smash started this match with Austin Lee. The enigmatic Jacks seemed to have the crowd with them until Lee made a tag to the Insomniac. Then the fans began a Fallen Angels chant that the 400 pounder tossing around Jack Smash and Jack Blast as if they weren't there. The Insomniac tagged in Freil, and Pure Rage whipped Jack Smash into the corner and worked him down with the FISTS OF RAGE. Freil then went for the RAGING LOCK but it was quickly broken up by Stevie Knight. Knight hit an INVERTED DDT on Freil and then went back to his corner, where he reached over and tagged himself in by slapping the legal Jack. Hotstuff made the cover but Freil kicked out. Stevie Knight tagged in Austin Lee and they hit a DOUBLE BACK BODY followed by a DOUBLE ELBOW DROP on Freil, but once again it was a 2 count. Lee whipped Freil to the ropes, and Pure Rage ducked and came off the other side, hitting a FLYING SHOULDERBLOCK on Lee. But Freil had been tagged in by Jack Blast. Blast took over and used fists and kicks to wear down Austin Lee. Jack Smash soon joined in, but Stevie Knight hit the ring, then both Fallen Angels did as well. With all six men in the ring, lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: Insomniac off the ropes! AND HE CLOTHSLINES JACK SMASH OVER THE TOP ROPE! WEST: He comes Freil! Freil leaps over Insomniac and hits Smash on the arena floor! DTMA: Knight . . .LOW BLOW TO INSOMNIAC! WEST: Jack Blast stumbling. DTMA: Knight and Lee exchange a smile! .. . . PROPERTY BOOM!!!! Cover... ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: Hot Property are still in the Dog Days tournament! DMTA: But the Angels and the Jacks are out! .. .. .. (CUT back to JD Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So that brought us to the singles match, and only man could move on in the Dog Days tournament. .. .. .. ================================================== Dog Days Singles Elimination Match LARK FENRIZ (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) DANIEL SINZ ================================================== SOUTHERNS: Sinz and Maq went right after each other, while Fenriz was left to pick and choose his spots to lay some hurt on Sinz and or Maq. Daniel Sinz and The Maquilladora seemed to pick up right where they left off in terms of their hatred for each other. At the four minute mark Sinz hit a DEATH VALLEY DRIVER on The Maquilladora and caught Fenriz with a CLOTHESLINE right afterwards. Sinz turned right back to The Maquilladora and slapped on the PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE, for a second time in the night. Fenriz got up and broke up the POE, sent Sinz to the ropes and got him in a GORY BOMB! The VWF champ did not look happy about having to be in the elimination match (and he has Jason Storm to thank for that) and Fenriz was wanting to take it out on someone. He got Maq up and hit the SAMMAEL on him, but Sinz got over before Fenriz had a real chance to cover Maq for the 1-2-3. Sinz and Fenriz exchanged blows until Maq was able to get to his feet. Maq came off the ropes and hit both men with a CLOTHELINE and Fenriz rolled to the outside of the ring. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: Maq has Sinz! WEST: BOW AND ARROW! BOW AND ARROW SUBMISSION!!! DMTA: This is the move that won the title for The Maquilladora! WEST: Daniel Sinz is in some real pain here! DTMA: Sinz reaching for the ropes! WEST: Lark Fenriz had better get off the floor or he could be out of this tournament. DMTA: Sinz has the ropes! WEST: Maq breaks the hold, he goes to the opposite ropes. .. . . 702! DTMA: What? WEST: The Las Vegas zip code. DMTA: Oh, I get it. WEST: Maq circling Daniel Sinz, he pulls him up! DMTA: POE! PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE!!! SINZ SLAPPED IT ON IN A HEARTBEAT!!! WEST: Fenriz pulling himself up! DMTA: Lark grabs the ref! WEST: MAQ IS TAPPING!!! MAQ IS TAPPIN!!! DTMA: But the ref is watching Fenriz! WEST: Daniel Sinz breaks the hold, and he comes over. <<< CLANG >>> DMTA: Sinz down like a ton of bricks. WEST: Fenriz hit him with something, he just threw it down by ringside. DTMA: Someone get over there and get a shot of what it was. WEST: Fenriz in the ring. DMTA: He covers Maq. ONE!!! TWO!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: Last years Dog Days winner has been eliminated on night one! As well as The Maquilladora, a finalist from last year! DTMA: And we have that shot, Fenriz hit Daniel Sinz with a pair of brass knuckles, which he has just quickly collected. And the champ is walking up to the back. WEST: Tune in next week folks, it will be Dog Day 2, Triple Threat night! DTMA: See you then! © Vegas Wrestling Federation
