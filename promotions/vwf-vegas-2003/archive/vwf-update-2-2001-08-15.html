VWF Update #2 Things are going pretty well, I would like to see more in-rings for the shows though but I am pretty happy and hope you all are as well. RE-CAP Paddy O'Shea wins for the first time with the help of his new manager Mr. Smith. Kerry Powell is still being haunted by The Masked Man who everyone thinks is Powells old UWC/CEW enemy Ric Beauty? Is the Masked Man beauty? And Tai Angela continues his building feud with Zell Strife. These two dark personalities seem to be on a path to destruction. WARRIOR WEDNESDAY August 22, 2001 MGM Grand Garden Arena Winners of these two matches qualify for next weeks Triple Threat Title Match BOBBY SILAS (vs) TAI ANGELA (vs) ANARCHY X How can SILAS do against the DARK MONSTER? Can X get on track? ZELL STRIFE (vs) HUEY HUXTABLE (vs) DIRK WESTERN After a week off both HUXTABLE and WESTERN should be ready for this match. ***MAIN EVENT*** KERRY POWELL (vs) THE MASKED MAN If the Masked Man loses he will unmask himself.
