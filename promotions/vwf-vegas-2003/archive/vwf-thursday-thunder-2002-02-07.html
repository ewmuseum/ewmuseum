Thursday Thunder, 7 February 2002

7 February 2002

(Scene: A man is seen walking around a dark parking lot. He is wearing a tight black muscle shirt with some khakis and expensive black shoes. He has a bald head and is circling one specific car with a baseball bat. He looks up, sees the cameraman and lokks back down to continue circling the car. Then he suddenly stops, turns to the car and then he smashes the front windshield in. He the proceeds to starting beating on the hood of the Lexus. He then takes out a 9mm and shoots the tires until they go flat. He tucks the 9mm into the back of his khakis and goes to the trunk of the car. He smashes it about three times until it pops open, he then picks something up from the ground and sets it in the trunk. After that he closes the trunk and smashes the rest of the windows and doors. He again looks at the cameraman...but this time...he speaks) Cameraman: Who are you? ???: You think of me as Neo, but that's a lie. CM: Ok, so wha...who are you now? ???: I am In Demand! From now on you do what I tell you, you recieve orders from me and only me...problem? CM: I guess not. ???: I am now going by my real name, Blade Canter! CM: Your real name is Blade? Blade: Yea now shutup! I will very soon be the dominant athlete in this corporation. Bavarin got a lucky move and a retarded ref. I was just using him to try out new moves. Now I will dominate him. I am req...I am demanding a triple threat match between me, bavarin... and that other loser he is fighting. They will merely be stepping stones toward my walk to fame. Bavarin this time you won't be so lucky. Get ready to feel the excrutiating pain...of TOTAL DOMINATION!!! Well i would leave if i were you. CM: Why? Blade: Trust me.Hahahahahaha. (Blade "In Demand" Canter jumps on his Ninja and rides off. The chubby cameraman waddles away slowly but surely. When he gets far enough he turns around but must shield his eyes from...THE EXPLOSION!!! The cameraman takes one step and then draws his foot back as he sees something coming down. It hits the ground hard but it is flipped over. The cameraman turns it over and reads...the license plate...BAVARIN!!!) And who was that? Neo is now Blade Cantor? And he wants a shot a Bavarin? Welcome to Thursday Thunder where the fallout from Ghost Town Blues is getting stranger and stranger. Bavarin has been targeted by Ne..I mean Blade Cantor. The feud between G-Gangsta and Jack Spade seems to have no end and Erik Andrews has been out of touch with all VWF officials since the event. This week Thursday Thunder is all highlights. So lets get right to it! ************************** TRIPLE X (vs) 28TH STREET ************************** The big boys of Triple X looked down after not being able to take the titles away from Blaze and Real Deal at Ghost Town Blues. 28th Street on the other hand looked like a team on a mission. Hardcore Hakeem worked over Chumpy for much of the match, working in a tag to Tyson Young now and again so Tyson could hit a POWERBOMB, PILEDRIVER or a NASTY CLOTHESLINE on Chumpy. But it was Hakeem who made the mistake of hitting Pizza Boy with a SUICIDE DIVE! And from there Earthquake went crazy. He knocked Tyson Young off the apron and hit the CHEESEBURGER on Hakeem. But Young made it back into the ring and broke up the potential fall. Tyson Young then sent Earthquake to the ropes and hit THE STREET to score the win. RESULT- 28th Street by pinfall (Tyson Young on Earthquake) Next up was two men who each scored wins at Ghost Town Blues. But first we heard from one of the competitors... Scene: You walk into a cold room and there is no sign of anyone in the area. You see many cobwebs and rats running around. As you walk further you hear music and light. You crack open the door and there laying on the bench lifting 315 pounds is a guy with a smile on his face. He is sweaty pretty hard looks like he is working hard, then he talks...... Bavarin: Welcome All. This the first time i have talked and I want to be known that i aint scared of that Crane guy. That Fight with neo was good, but still no match for Bavarin. you all are probably wondering What is a Bavarin? Well... Has that guy said someone in charge and has you can tell the girls love. bavarin was a Very Powerful warrior who never lost a fight. He Feared Nothing and saved all. After the fight Crane, You are going to beg not to fight me again. The girls are still going to scream for me and you will be screaming too. ( Bavarin smiles and shine of his white teeth flashes). I cant wait to show all Who the new boss is and i ready to Be the next Fearless Warrior. Good Luck to all and I will see yall there. Scene: Bavarin goes to his Red Viper with 2 hot girls inside and takes off as the screen fades. ************************************** BAVARIN (vs) REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE ************************************** The Revolution stormed the ring and dominated this match early. Crane nailed Bavarin with POWERSLAMS, FOREARMS and a CHOKESLAM and seemed to be in complete control. Crane even tossed Bavarin to the outside just to hit him with EXTREMES MEASURES, Cranes own plancha. But Bavarin was not dead. The Fearless Warrior managed a quick DDT to Crane on the outside and used that momentum to get back in charge or the battle. Bavarin nailed a POWERBOMB and Crane and went up to the second rope and dropped a LEGDROP on the Revolution. But could only manage a two count. Bavarin came off the ropes and ran into a DROP TOE HOLD and in the blink of an eye Crane locked on REVOLTIONS END! and Bavarin tapped out. RESULT- Revolution Randall Crane by submission After the bell Blade InDemand Cantor hit the ring like a house on fire! Cantor took Crane and tossed him from the ring with a RUNNING POWERBOMB, then went right after Bavarin. The Fearless Warrior and Cantor exchanged blows inside the ring until VWF security was called out to break them up. Then Sebastian hit the ring (I'm To Sexy hits and Sebastian, the only TRUE 2 time 24 Hour Champion walks to the ring, he steps inside and pulls the mic to his mouth) SEBASTIAN: You people are ugly! You people are dumb. Cheering for the Jack Spades, the Zell Strifes and not giving enough respect to the 2 time 24 Hour Champion, Sebastian! (BOOS, A DEMONIC CHANT BEGINS) SEBASTIAN: (angry) Demonic is not a true champion! Who has he defeated? Dage and Zell Strife!?!?!?!? Not Sebastian! And later tonight Strife meets Rainka for the right to battle for the 24 Hour Title!? Not the former 2 time champion!? Not the man who ran off Kerry Powell? Not the man who ran off Corey Fuller? Not the man who ran off Ric Beauty? And not the man that clearly ran off Erik Andrews? That is four superstars that Sebastian has run off. And Johnny Sanders, you are next! Get out here! Sanders music hit and he raced to the ring and took over on Sebastian with a series of punches and kicks and ended it with a HURRICANRANA! But Sebastian must have been playing possum! He turned the match with a low blow and then went straight to work on the back of Sanders. BACKBREAKER after BACKBREAKER and Sebastian even managed a NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX. All this set Sanders up to locked in the SEX YOU OUT! Sebastian locked his version of the Boston Crab in and Sanders tapped. But Sebastian refused to break the hold and began screaming the names POWELL, FULLER, BEAUTY, ANDREWS, NOW SANDERS!!!! The crowd went crazy and the ref was forced to reverse his decision and give the win to Sanders by disqualification. Sebastian then turned on the ref and locked him in the SEX YOU OUT! He did eventually break the hold and left the crowd wondering if anyone can stop this new Sebastian! Blaze then came to the ring, wearing his half of the tag team titles but Real Deal was not around. And The Maquilladora came out to a big POP from the fans. **************************************************** THE MAQUILLADORA (VS) BLAZE half tag team champions **************************************************** Blaze nailed a SPEAR and just started to beat down Maquilladora. Blaze then made a major mistake, he sent Maq to the ropes and Maq came off with a SPRINGBOARD BODYPRESS! But only got the two count. Maq then used his high flying to dominate Blaze. A series of KICKS brought Blaze to his knees and Maq went to the top rope and hit a SUNSET FLIP on Blaze but still only got the two count. Maq looked like he wasn't sure what he could do to score the win on the big man. MEXICAN STRETCH BOMB! 2 COUNT! DOWNWARD SPIRAL!!! 2 COUNT! Maq then set up Blaze for PERMANENT DAYLIGHT when the RCW theme hit and Real Deal started to make his way to the ring! Maq broke the hold and turned but Yakabi came out from the back and jumped on the back of Real Deal. In the ring Blaze was coming up behind Maq looking for UNCONTROLLABLE INFERNO but Yakabi signalled Maq right before Real Deal CHOKESLAMMED Yak! Maq turned, quick SPINNING HEEL KICK then he hit the PERMANENT DAYLIGHT!!! for the win! RESULT- The Maquilladora by pinfall. And when Talk Dirty To Me hit the fans at the Thomas and Mack went ASB (ape shit bonkers)! As Classy Joey Boy made his way to the ring to meet new T3 Champion Xtreme Tony Cage. ************************************************************************* XTREME TONY CAGE (T3 Champion) vs CLASSY JOEY BOY (BlackJack Champion) ************************************************************************* This match was not for either title but no one in the arena could tell that from the wild begining. CJB and Cage exchanged moves with lightning speed and the fans really loved the way these two young superstars were going. CJB looked in good shape after his wild GRAVEYARD match with Tai Angela at Ghost Town Blues hitting a DRAGON SCREW LEG WHIP! Classy Joey Boy was closing in on victory when Revolution In My Name by Pantera hit and Randall Crane started to walk toward the ring. As CJB turned Cage used it to hit a RUNNING BULLDOG on the BlackJack Champion. Crane just stood at ringside, watching the action. Tony Cage hit a DDT on CJB and set the BlackJack Champ on the ropes and climbed up himself, looking for the SUNSET BOMB. Crane moved in and pushed Cage off the top rope and back into the ring. CJB jumped up and hit the STUDD SPLASH for the win as Crane walked over and tried on the T3 Title belt. RESULT- Classy Joey Boy by pinfall. It was as if a hush fell over the crowd as Thomas Rainka and Zell Strife made their way to the ring. ******************************* ZELL STRIFE (vs) THOMAS RAINKA No 1 Contenders Match ******************************* They started the fight somewhere in the back. Both men came to the ring already bloody! Strife was carrying a CHAIR and Rainka had a TV cable around his neck and it looked like he had been choked by it earlier. Strife and Rainka went into the crowd, and never made it to the ring! The ref was forced to COUNT both men OUT! Strife and Rainka fought out up to the consession area! Rainka tossed Strife into a hot dog cart that fell over and the left Strife laying in the BOILING WATER! Rainka grabbed in and went for the ONLY ENDING on top of the water but Strife stopped him and Irish whipped him into the CONCRETE WALL! Rainka stumbled to his right and Strife hit a SPEAR that sent both men through a glass window and outside of the arena! Las Vegas Metro Police were called and both men were arrested. RESULT- Count Out! The fans went wild as it was time for the main event! *************************************************************************** DEMONIC (24 Hour Champion) & G-GANGSTA (RCW Champ) vs ELEMENTS OF VIOLENCE *************************************************************************** These four men worked all their hatred for each other into the ring. After the begining Spade and Gangsta were in the ring and Jack Spade dominated the RCW Champion in his usual kick ass style. Gangsta managed a tag and Demonic tried to match Spade punch for punch, but Spade hit a low blow on the 24 Hour Champ and EVENFLOW DDT, but only got the two count. Dage wanted in and Spade made the tag. Dage grabbed Demonic and worked over the monster with forearms and had him up for a PILEDRIVER but Gangsta broke it up! Jack Spade rushed in and hit a JACKPOT that sent Gangsta out of the ring. Dage and Spade then hit a DOUBLE BRAINBUSTER on Demonic. They threw Demonic to the ropes but he came off with a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE and dropped both members of Elements of Violence. Demonic grabbed Spade and lifted him high and CHOKESLAMMED him to the outside of the ring. The 24 Hour Champion then CHOKESLAMMED Dage and signalled for the PITZ OF HELL! But G-Gangsta got up on the apron with a chair and told Demonic to bring over Dage. Gangsta pulled back, Dage ducked and Gangsta hit Demonic with a CHAIRSHOT that echoed around the arena. Spade pulled Gangsta off the apron and beat him down outside the ring and Dage grabbed Demonic and hit the REVOLUTION! for the win! RESULT- Elements Of Violence by pinfall (Dage on Demonic) After the match as the EOV celebrated going up the ramp Gangsta and Demonic were left inside the ring, staring at each other. Viva Las Vegas hit and VWF President Rob Beauty walked out onto the ramp. ROB BEAUTY: Well, well well. Demonic, your buddy there just cost you a chance to show these people that Dage was lucky. But Dage wasn't lucky, I think you were lucky. And Gangsta! You walk around here, in Vegas with another belt. I have a wonderful idea.... Next week, since Strife and Rainka are in jail. I say we unify those two titles! Demonic versus G-Gangsta! See you next week boys! (laughs and the VWF logo blazes onto the screen) Vegas Wrestling Federation © 2002
