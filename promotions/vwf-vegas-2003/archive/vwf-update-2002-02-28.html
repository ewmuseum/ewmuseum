Vegas Wrestling Federation Update, 28 February 2002

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF UPDATE ----------- NEWS Once again Classy Joey Boy holds the BlackJack title after hitting Tai Angela with the Studd Splash. G-Gangsta was seriously burned by Sebastian during the same match. UPCOMING EVENTS Thursday Thunder at the Thomas and Mack March 7, 2002 In a rematch two up and comers get another shot at each other as BAVARIN meets up with ELIMINATOR JASON STONE The Dark Monster TAI ANGELA gets in the ring with BLADE IN DEMAND CANTOR The NEW YORK CITY HEADLINERS get in the ring with BlackJack Champion CLASSY JOEY BOY and his friend FATTY JAY Newcomer REAL DEAL meets up with one of the big names in the VWF ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS In a non title battle XTREME TONY CAGE laces up the boots against the big man DAGE In a battle that could be epic, ZELL STRIFE faces a big test as he meets REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE The one man who holds the tag team titles THE MAQUILLADORA defends the belts against 28TH STREET And in the ***MAIN EVENT*** 24 Hour Champion G-GANGSTA faces off against SEBASTIAN! -------------------- Thursday Thunder at the Thomas and Mack March 14, 2002 Last Thursday Thunder before The Gauntlet PPV!!!!!!!! ------------------ THE GAUNTLET PPV (a two night event) NIGHT ONE Monday March 18 from the Thomas and Mack All four titles on the line, all VWF stars on the card. ------------------- THE GAUNTLET PPV NIGHT TWO Thursday March 21 from the Thomas and Mack A Royal Rumble style event. A battle royal between the losers of matches from Night One. When one man is left a cage will be lowered and the winners from Night One (except 24 Hour Champ) will come to the ring one by one for a First Blood Match. When one man is left the 24 Hour Champion and that man will climb to the top of the cage, first man to toss the other off the cage is the Gauntlet Winner and 24 Hour Champion!!!! Call Cox Cable for info on this big event! --------------------- OOC- Thanks, as always. If any of you have questions about anything just let me know at [detail withheld]
