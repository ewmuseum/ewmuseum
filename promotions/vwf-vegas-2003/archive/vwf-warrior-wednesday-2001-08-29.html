Warrior Wednesday, 29 August 2001

29 August 2001

WARRIOR WEDNESDAY August 29, 2001 MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada (Viva Las Vegas plays as footage from last week plays on the screen) (Powell goes over and rips off the mask) DA: HOOOOLLLLY MOOOLLLLLY It is Sebastian! The 24 Hour Champ was under the mask! (The crowd is going crazy) ROB: Hey 24 Hour man. Guess what Ric? If Powell beat him, which he did, guess what that makes Kerry Powell?
(another even louder crowd pop) ROB: Ladies and gentlemen the NEW 24 Hour Champion!!!! 24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! (Ric Beauty rushes up the ramp toward his brother, Powell rushes up after Ric and Sebastian rushes up after Powell)
******************** [The scene opens to movie set. It's one of those westerns. G-Gangsta an U-Trop pull up in there Mercades Benz with My Projects blasting. G-Gangsta steps out an tells U-Trop to leave. He does. G-Gangsta is wearing jeans and a VWF t-shirt.] G-Gangsta: So now, I have the three-way with two men, wait no, one man and one boy. Ok, I have a match with a man named Jack Spade, and a boy named
Bobby Silas. Ok thats fine. Jack, we will show the fans a true show, a great athlectical matchup. But you see with Silas in the match, it's just athlete
vs athlete vs punk. With Jack an G being the athletes an The soon to be shut up Silas. Once the match is over, G-Gangsta will be standing tall, with Silas looking at the lights. You see, you think that you have more expirence then I do?
Well no you don't. See I was Blaze, Demonic, Terrell Terror over the last six years. You have been nothing, and nowhere. I have held many titles, in over
20 feds. You have held nothing, and once again been no where. So
Silas this Wednesday night, you will be awakened into the light. [He walks into the studio.] G-Gangsta: Now onto you Jack, well you couldn't of said it better, we have run into each other in many places, many times. We've feuded, we've been partners, we've been in each others feds. You see this is more then a feud.
It is kinda becoming a tradition for us to fight. Everytime we do we bring down the house. Whether it' Blaze vs Insane, Demonic vs Tom E or Mike vs Christian Idol. The fans imply can't get enough of us fighting. Why? Because, we are two of the best. Two supastars ya heard me? Aight then. Well this Wednesday night, three men walk in, one man walks out. May the best man win sucka. Peace. [U-Trop pulls up as G-Gangsta leaves the studio, the scene fades out.] (Cut to Dan The Man Aller standing in the middle of the ring) DA: Welcome to Warrior Wednesday less than two weeks before the Vegas
Violence PPV! And tonight we get a sneak preview of the big night at the PPV! Tai Angela and Zell Strife in a big one on one No Rules match! A tag team match with Kerry Powell getting in the ring with Ric
Beauty and the 24 Hour Title will be on the line! And one of four men,
Spade, Gangsta, Silas or Faith will leave here tonight with the Triple
Threat Title!
HOOOOLLLLY MOOOLLLY it is going to be a big night! # Viva Las Vegas # (Jack Spade winning his Triple Threat qualifying match) # Viva Las Vegas # (Kerry Powell getting jumped by the Masked Man) # Viva # (A shot of Tai Angela spitting fire onto Zell Strife) # Viva # (G-Gangsta in pose with manager U-Trop) # Las Vegas# (Dan has taken his place at the table) DA: The fans are crazy here tonight in the MGM! But on September 7 the
VWF will be at the Cashman Field Center, outside for the Hellfire
Match between Tai Angela and Zell Strife. And all the VWF stars will be in action at the Pay Per View!
Let get to tonights action! ********************************************************** PADDY O'SHEA (with Mr Smith) vs DIRK WESTERN ********************************************************** (Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DA: In this corner, from Cell Block D. Weighing in at 260 pounds, six feet five inches DIRK "D-DAY" WESTERN!!!!!!!!!! DA: Western said he is changing to a brawling style which should help him, he has real trouble with wrestling moves.
(Barroom Heroes hits and Paddy O'Shea starts his long stumble to the ring) DA: From Dublin Ireland.....at 6 foot 2, 260 pounds The Master of the 1-2...Paddy O'Shea! DA: The boxer and the bruiser, this one should be good. Dirk Western is dancing around, making fun of Paddy. Western looks like a drunk boxer. O'Shea rushes him, side step and a punch to the back of the head. O'Shea is down and Dirk is sticking a few well placed kicks into the ribs of the Irishman.
Western has a re-newed look of anger on his face. O'Shea looks in pain. Western picks him up and off the ropes CLOTHESLINE! and O'Shea is on the ground again. Hey! Western with a wrestling hold, a headlock on O'Shea. Western moves him away from the sight of the ref and poke in the eyes. Look at that dastadly smile on Westerns face.
Take down with the headlock to the mat. O'Shea tried a roll over, another eye gouge. Western leaps to his feet and looks for the elbow drop but O'Shea slides out of the way.
Paddy staggers to the ropes, his eyes still smarting. Western is up, goes for a boot but O'Shea catches it!
Western pleads for mercy and gets a kick in the GROIN for his trouble! The crowd is getting behind O'Shea! (CROWD: PAD-DEE.....PAD-DEE) O'Shea had Western and a RUNNING BULLDOG! O'Shea stands up Western and delivers a series of body blows that
Rocky would be proud of! O'Shea is in complete control!
Off the ropes, SWINGING NECKBREAKER! O'Shea signals for The One Two! and the crowd loves it! Setting up Western.... THE ONE TWO!!!! Cover 1....2....3! Paddy O'Shea wins the match! (Mr. Smith gets in the ring and raises the hand of O'Shea) DA: Western is up....a forearm shot that knocks O'Shea from the ring! Mr. Smith turns into the D-DAY! Dirk Western has dropped the skinny manager of Paddy O'Shea. Now
Western is out of the ring and setting up O'Shea!
D-DAY! onto the floor of the MGM Grand Garden Arena! Dirk has a chair! and is pummelling O'Shea with chair shots! Oh no! Somebody stop him! Western took beer from some ringside fans and is pouring it over the fallen O'Shea! This man is sick! (The other refs and some VWF officials come out and get between Western and O'Shea) DA: Let's go to a break **************** VWF BREAK **************** DA: That was sad! Western is a punk and I hope O'Shea gets another chance at him. Lets move on, the winner of the next match will fight Jack Spade and G-Gangsta later for the Triple Threat Title. *************************************************************************** THE SILENCE BOBBY SILAS (vs) THE JAPANESE ANGEL FAITH *************************************************************************** (Players Are Gonna Play hits and Bobby Silas begins to strut his way to the ring. He is wearing long purple satin pants and no shirt which shows off his sculpted body. He stands on the ring apron and calls for the mic)
BOBBY: It is time for the silence and in the silence we find "THE TRUTH!!!" DA: That is "The Silence" Bobby Silas, coming in at 6-6, 274 pounds
DA: Silas called out G-Gangsta recently. Silas wants to make a name here in the VWF.
(The lights dim down and Nirvana, smells like teen spirit hits the PA system, Faith walks out with a candle in his hands, he circles the ring and then role's under the bottom rope and starts running against each rope)
DA: Faith and Silas were part of the match last week with Tai Angela, the sick FREAK! Collar and elbow and Silas is clearly the bigger and stronger man here in this match. Silas with a hip toss but Faith lands on his feet.
The Japanese Angel is very athletic! Lock up and Faith pushes off and HURRICANRANA! Silas is up. DROPKICK! And Faith like lightning is up on the second rope, Silas gets up CROSS BODY
1...2...CLOSE! Silas kicks out! And he and Faith are staring each other down. Silas offers a quick clap for the smaller man.
Faith goes behind Silas, SCHOOLBOY 1...2...NOT A CHANCE. Another lock up and Silas tosses Faith to the ropes, goes for the back body but Faith lands on his feet and heads to the ropes Silas turns and ELBOW to the kisser of Bobby Silas!
Faith looks good! Silas is in for a bigger battle than I think he thought. Faith off the ropes goes for another cross body.
Silas catches him. FULL PRESS!!!! And he drops him face first. Silas off the ropes and drops a knee to the back of the head of Faith!
Silas sets him up PILEDRIVER to the mat and Faith is in real trouble. Silas has him QUIET TIME!!! Silas nails the modified choke slam. 1...2...3!!! Bobby Silas wins and will battle for the Triple Threat Title later tonight! Tough loss for Faith but I think we will see good things from him in the future.
*************** VWF BREAK *************** *********************************************************************************** KERRY POWELL (24 Hour Champion) and VWF PRESIDENT ROB BEAUTY (vs) SEBASTIAN and RIC BEAUTY *********************************************************************************** DA: This next match has the potential for a change in the 24 Hour
Title. Champion Kerry Powell is getting in the ring with former Champ
Sebastian and Powells old enemy Ric Beauty!
(Viva Las Vegas plays and Rob Beauty walks toward the ring.) DA: There is our President. And he looks ready for a fight. [The house lights dim down, and golden spotlights begin to sweep the entire arena. A hush falls over the crowd as "Money" by Pink
Floyd begins to play.]
Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, weighting in at 238 pounds, and standing at 6 feet, and one inch, this is...
24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! [The fans jump to their feet as Kerry Powell himself, makes his way to the ring.] DA: Listen to those fans! Powell has really got them. And the fans change with the sounds of I'm Too Sexy!
DA: Making his way to the ring, with a smirk and smile on his face from Los Angeles...at 6 foot 4, 240 pounds.....SEBASTIAN.
(Sebastian steps into the ring and bows to each side as I'm To Sexy plays in the background)
SEBASTIAN: Remember you losers! I am Sebastian and you are NOT! DA: Sebastian is pointing at Powell and the title around his waist. Everyone is waiting for the arrival of Ric Beauty. (O Fortuna begins to play and Ric steps onto the ramp, but he is wearing a suit) DA: Didn't anyone tell Ric he was supposed to wrestle tonight? RIC BEAUTY: Kerry Powell you are the 24 Hour Champion! And Rob you said that the title is defended anywhere there is a VWF opponent and a ref.
(Rob nods his head and Powell is motioning Ric to come to the ring) RIC BEAUTY: So any man who pins you tonight would be the champion? (Suddenly the whole VWF roster rushes past Ric toward the ring) DA: Oh no! Everyone is coming and they are all heading toward Kerry
Powell. What the hell is going on? Ric Beauty is laughing, and walking away!
Sebastian is on Powell.......G-Gangsta is on Sebatian.....Powell gets away and is jumped by Dirk Western....Bobby Silas is in the ring and so is Jack Spade.
Someone has to restore order! Angela and Strife are exchanging punches on the ramp! Paddy O'Shea rushes in and is all over Dirk Western! Sebastian and Jack Spade are battling Kerry Powell! The other VWF stars have spilled out onto the floor!
Sebastian holds Powell and Spade hits the JACK POT! Powell is down, Spade goes for the cover! And Sebastian just nailed Spade with something! Brass knuckles maybe! Sebastian makes the cover 1...2....3! Damn! Damn! Damn! Sebastian races over to the table, grabs the title and heads back up the ramp! We gotta take a break! *************** VWF BREAK *************** DA: If you are just joining us did you miss it! Sebastian is once again the 24 Hour Champion! And we got word that Jack Spade has been taken to a UWC Hospital with a concussion! Sebastian and Ric Beauty have screwed Powell out of his title!
*********************************** #####NO RULES##### TAI ANGELA (vs) ZELL STRIFE *********************************** (Fall Children by AFI starts with thick fog pouring through the ramp way, green smoke fills the air. Lights start to flash and fade while a large
Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans)
DA: The fans are behind Zell Strife, I think he looks like a freak too but not as bad as Angela!
Here comes the Dark Monster! DA: Angela stops halfway down the ramp to the ring! One of the women in his coven has a mic.
WOMAN: Zell Strife! I am the voice of the Dark Monster, The Freak, the
Holy Avenger Tai Angela! I am Blackbird. Tonight there are no rules,
Zell and my master wants me to ask a question of you.
(Zell is leaning over the ropes) BLACKBIRD: Do you wish to forfeit? You have suffered much against the
Dark One. (Strife gets out of the ring) DA: That sent all those girls running, and Angela and Strife meet up on the ramp. Strife looks very good, he is really taking the fight to
Angela. There are moving back up the ramp!
Lets get the cameras up there! Strife has Angela at the top of the ramp. POWERBOMB! Strife just dropped the six foot eight FREAK! And Strife isn't done yet he is dragging Angela into the back.
Down a hallway and out into the street! Look at the tourists scatter! No rules means no rules and Strife is ready to beat down Tai Angela. Zell has him for the SUPLEX onto the service road to the MGM!
HOOOOOLLLLLY MOOOOOOOLLLLY Neither man is moving! Strife may have killed himself and Angela. Zell is getting to his feet!
(The camera goes off the action to an approaching police car) DA: What the hell are the cops doing here? Damn scared tourists! Zell sees the cops, he has Tai Angela..... PORPHRIA! Zell struggles for the cover.. 1 2 The cop just grabbed the ref! Ten Metro officers are on Strife and
Angela. Let's go to a break! I gotta get out there and find out what is going on!
*************** VWF BREAK *************** (Lean Darlene is on the street, full of curious tourists and police cars) DA: (at ringside) Darlene....what is going on? DARLENE: Someone called the cops and they have taken away Zell Strife,
Tai Angela and the ref!
DA: Who called? DARLENE: Who cares! Strife had him! DA: Zell is going to have to wait until September 7! And the Hellfire
Match! DARLENE: That is all from the street! (The camera gets close up of a bood stain on the sidewalk) DA: Wow! now for the main event! ##################TRIPLE THREAT TITLE MATCH################### THE ACE JACK SPADE (vs) G-GANGSTA (vs) THE SILENCE BOBBY SILAS ############################################################# (Players Are Gonna Play hits and Bobby Silas begins to strut his way to the ring. BOBBY: It is time for the silence and in the silence we find "THE TRUTH!!!" DA: That is "The Silence" Bobby Silas, coming in at 6-6, 274 pounds
(My Projects by Coo Coo Cal hits, G-Gangsta walks out with his manager/body gaudr U-Trop, wearing a cleanch jeans suit. He gets in the ring takes off the jacket to the suit and wrestles in the baggy shiny black jeans)
DA: And here is G-Gangsta, 232 pounds and 6 feet 4 inches of mean! DA: Jack Spade was injured by Sebastian earlier tonight, I don't know if we are going to see him. I know he was taken to UMC Hospital after a cheap shot by Sebastian.
There is the bell! Silas and Gangsta lock up and the ref is making the count on Jack
Spade. There is the bell! REF: Eliminated first is Jack Spade by count out! DA: Spade has got Sebastian to thank for this! Silas doesn't look as strong as he did in his earlier match with
Faith. G-Gangsta is in control delivering a series and chops to the chest of Silas.
WHOOOOOO! WHOOOOOOO! Irish whip, reversed and Gangsta goes hard into the turnbuckles. Silas follows and a big knee to the chest.
G, falls to the mat. Bobby Silas is looking better. Silas picks up the Jamacian looking fighter and locks on the old fashioned full nelson. Silas is really cranking on this, it could be a submission hold. G-Gangsta is not going to give up so fast.
G-Gangsta drops out and kick to Silas who staggers back to the ropes,
G-Gangsta is up and BUCKSHOT! That superkick has Bobby Silas on the ground. G-Gangsta is waiting for the big man to get up......SPINNING
BACK HAND PUNCH and Bobby Silas is back on the mat. And G-Gangsta and his manager U-Trop are taking a moment to celebrate.
G-Gangsta heads over and has Silas and slowly nails the NECKBREAKER! The man from Queens is heading up to the top rope! This could be his mosh pit like move, La Silla...... Silas goes over and yanks a leg out and Gangsta comes down on the family jewels on the top turnbuckle. And Silas is getting up there.
SUPERPLEX!!!!! The whole ring shook! 1...2...KICKOUT by Gangsta. Silas throws him to the ropes and U-Trop pulls down the rope and G goes out of the ring. Why would U-Trop do that? He may have just hurt his own man. Wait I think G-Gangsta landed on his feet.
U-Trop is on the ring apron and Silas heads over and has him by the collar. The fans want to see Silas nail U-Trop!
But G-Gangsta is sneaking around the ring, behind Silas. The ref breaks up Silas and U-Trop but the clever manager is distracting the ref.
G-Gangsta is in the ring with a baseball bat. HOME RUN! Silas is out! G-Gangsta is up on the top rope. U-Trop turns the ref.......GANGSTA SMASH!!!!!! 1....2....3!!! G-Gangsta is the FIRST TRIPLE THREAT CHAMPION!!!! Even though he only beat one man in the final! (G-Gangsta and U-Trop celebrate in the ring with the Triple Threat
Title) DA: What a night! Coming up September 7 will be the first VWF PPV,
Vegas Violence! There will be a special show later to talk about the PPV and next weeks, Warrior Wednesday!
