Singles Group 2 ---------------- \\\\\ DANIEL SINZ (vs) KEVIN BLAIR ///// ---------------------------------------- DTMA: This match never took place. WEST: Blair was attacked by the New Age Army after his match with Ryan Delaney on Tuesday night and has been ordered by the doctors to stay out of the ring. WEST: Both men seemed tentative, as if they've never met before. DTMA: But once they started mixing it up.... Maq slid under Delaney after coming off the ropes... But Delaney got a shoulder up! DTMA: Maq sent Delaney to the ropes and FLAPJACKED Ryan to the mat. Then Maq was on the top rope, hitting a MISSLE DROPKICK!!! WEST: Delaney was in trouble as Maq threw him into the corner... and then placed him in the TARANTULA!!!! DMTA: Delaney ended up on the ring apron.... Maq went for a BASEBALL SLIDE but Delaney moved out of the way and Maq slid out of the ring onto the floor! WEST: Delaney then hit Maq with a FLYING FOREARM off the ring apron! DMTA: Maq then found himself getting slammed into the floor by a vicious RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP from Delaney! WEST: Delaney tossed Maq back in the ring, and Delaney came back in himself via a CORKSCREW MOONSAULT!!! DTMA: Followed by a cover.. ONE!!! TWO!!! But Maq kicked out!! WEST: Delaney hit a DOUBLE UNDERHOOK FACE PLANT on Maq before locking him in a STEP OVER TOE HOLD FACE LOCK!! MAQ" DMTA: Breaking the hold and jumping to his feet, Delaney began to just STOMP on Maq! WEST: He then sent Maq to the ropes and hit a SPINNING SPINEBUSTER!!! WEST: Maq grabbed Delaney in a side headlock..... ...... AND HIT PERMANENT DAYLIGHT!!!!!! DMTA: Maq made the cover... ONE!!!! TWO!!!!!!! THRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!! WEST: And the place went A S B!!!! DMTA: A major win for The Maquilladora! A major win! !!!!! WINNER = The Maquilladora !!!!! DMTA: Frost then pulled Andrews up and picked him up for a POWERSLAM but Andrews floated over and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX on Frost! WEST: Andrews did not let go and hit THREE IN A ROW!
