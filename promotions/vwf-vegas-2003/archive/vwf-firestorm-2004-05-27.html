(The VWF tron lights up to reveal a face paintless Kevin Simmons without sitting down in a chair in a black T-Shirt, blue jeans, white bandages surrounding his head and dark sunglasses on. He's staring at the floor, as if he's not interested in looking at the camera, much less looking up to face his fans.) SIMMONS: Did you ever love something so much that you were used to seeing it everyday, then one day it was gone? Did it ever feel like it destroyed your life? I know what you fans are going through right now. You see, I loved wrestling. I lived it, ate it, slept it, breathed it, dreamed it. In my life, nothing else mattered. Nothing else came close. In return, wrestling gave me things in return. Money, fame, people that I can call friends. Or it used to give me friends until new owners took them away like Mr. Cash did with Eddie Cutlass. Wrestling also made me feel important both inwardly and outwardly. Matter of fact, wrestling gave me lots of other things too... (Looking down at the floor, Simmons cracks a smile and his face lightens up a bit.) SIMMONS: It gave me my wife Lindsay. (Kevin chuckles to himself.) SIMMONS: You know, who would have ever thought that the wrestling gods would have put us together? I mean, of the strangest things, if good ol' Morris Bass hadn't broke my back just seconds after I won my first and only World Heavyweight Title, I would have had a longer reign as WWA Heavyweight Champion and Lindsay Simmons would instead be just plain old Lindsay Postlewait, the registered nurse at that run down hospital in Durham. Our paths would have never crossed if the gods of fate hadn't stepped in. (Kevin pauses for a moment.) SIMMONS: I remember the night I met her when I broke my back. I was in so much pain it was unbearable. She held my hand in the ER and tried to get me to wiggle my toes and no matter how hard I tried, I couldn't. But yet, I had a burning rage of emotion inside me. I looked at her and knew instantly that I was in love and that she was the one I wanted to marry. (Kevin chuckles.) SIMMONS: Matter of fact, stealing a page from Stan Marsh's playbook in South Park, my first words weren't EXACTLY words.....I vomited all over her cause of my nervousness, or lack thereof. That sparkle in her eyes, the way she still held my hand and spoke soft words of encouragement to me....I never thought I could love another that much. Especially when she stuck by my side throughout all those months of rehabilitation when I wanted to quit and give up, she never let me. (Simmons smiles and laughs some more.) SIMMONS: Wrestling also gave me some of the closest buddies in the world that I'm proud to be associated with. Marcus O'Malley, Mark McQueen, Kagedo and yes, even Morris Bass. To you fans, we are the Society of Doom, but to me, those guys are my family. Kagedo and ol' Morris are like big brothers to me and those sneaky sons of bitches in McQueen and O'Malley are like my little brothers. Nothing gave me more joy in this world than riding in cars with these guys all over the United States, kicking ass and taking name with those ugly bastards.... (Kevin's smile slowly melts down into a frown. As he looks into the camera, it seems as if he's searching for a way out of the emotion that he can feel himself spiraling into.) SIMMONS: There's an old saying that says 'Where much is given, much is also required'. In this case, what wrestling gives you, it also takes it away from you. (Kevin smiles then laughs.) SIMMONS: I mean it wasn't too bad at first. When I made my comeback, yeah the first couple matches I was out of shape and it took me a little bit to find my legs again but it wasn't long until I had half of the RAW Carolina Tag Team titles around my waist, thanks to Terry Jacobs. It wasn't long after that we lost the straps and soon found myself rekindling an old feud with Morris. Soon after, wrestling took a lot of my blood. It took numerous tolls on my body. Sprains, strains and other injuries. It took most of what good brain cells I had left but hell, I didn't care. After fighting back from a broken back, I was thanking the good lord up above for every chance I had to compete in that ring. As far as I was concerned, that wrestling ring could have all of me....mind, body and soul. I loved wrestling and wrestling loved me.....or soon I thought. I soon found out I was satisfied with it, but wrestling wasn't satisfied with me... (A VERY long pause. Kevin reaches through his dark sunglasses as he wipes tears away from his eyes at the next thought. Kevin looks down, but makes his way through the next statement.) ........so it turned my own wife against me. (Kevin pauses and then collects himself. He struggles through the next few minutes as the crowd is feeling his emotional pain.) SIMMONS: She lost all hope, faith and confidence in me. (Kevin clears his throat.) SIMMONS: What she did to me last night was totally unexpected. It was like she broke my back again so I would be helpless again, just so I would have no choice BUT to depend on her for every move I make. (Kevin pauses.) SIMMONS: You see folks, wrestling also took some of my friends. Or correction...people who were supposed to be my friends. After my wife embarrased me last night for the whole world to see, I found out that my wrestling academy....the VERY one that I put so much labor into tragically burned down to the ground. As if it felt like my wife had broke my back, this news made me feel as if the rest of my body has been beaten to a bloody pulp. But the nature of this business is not to have friends. I tell my students all the time that they'll find that out sooner or later.....or at least I told them, since I officially have no more students to train. No matter how good you've been to people, they will all trick you or turn on you, just like my wife did. (Kevin continues his preaching.) SIMMONS: Wrestling isn't no past time or a thing to be loved. It's nothing but a cold, mindless, murderous machine that eats you and your family whole. And a machine was created without any emotions or feelings, therefore it is impossible to love you back. And when you realize that you can't love a machine, it's too late. This machine has destroyed you and taken everything you've got.......except your name. And that's where I am right now. In the past 24 hours, this machine has taken my wife, my respect, my honor, my dignity, and my academy. It has all but destroyed my life. And that's where I am right now....a worthless name that's not even worth $20 and a career that I don't want anymore. (Simmons is now fighting back the tears.) SIMMONS: So I've giving the career back to the machine without a fuss or a fight. I'm only doing this to save myself and what's left of my marriage and my family. Why am I doing this? I'm sick of the pain that I have caused to my wife and to others. I know that Iyasu Sasu would still want another piece of me right now and part of me wishes I still had it in me to give to him.....but I don't. I can't stand it anymore. I'm done with wrestling and I'm done with the pain.....I'm done with it for good. (Kevin sighs.) SIMMONS: The Kevin Simmons you fans have known......the Kevin Simmons you fans have loved......is officially dead. (Kevin hangs his head in shame as the emotional floodgates open up as Simmons weeps violently as the VWF tron immediately turns to static.) .. .. ..
