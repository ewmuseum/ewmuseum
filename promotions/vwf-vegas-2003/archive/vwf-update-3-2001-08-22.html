VEGAS WRESTLING FEDERATION UPDATE #3 Things are really heating up. I am looking at early September for the first PPV, so those of you not in a feud let me know if you want in one and we can work something out. RE-CAP Powell wins the 24 Hour Title! The plan by Sebastian and Ric Beauty backfired and cost Sebastian his title. G-Gangsta and Jack Spade are in the final for the Triple Threat Final, they will be joined by either Bobby Silas or Faith depending on who wins their re-match. How much longer can the VWF take the war between Angela and Strife? --------------------------------------- VEGAS VIOLENCE PPV at Cashman Field Center, Las Vegas NV September 7, 2001 HELLFIRE MATCH The venue is outside and a second ring will be used for this match. The ropes will be set on fire. ZELL STRIFE (vs) TAI ANGELA Plus both titles will be defended and all the VWF stars will appear! --------------------------------------- WARRIOR WEDNESDAY August 29, 2001 TRIPLE THREAT TITLE MATCH Jack Spade (vs) G-Gangsta (vs) Silas/Faith NO RULES Tai Angela (vs) Zell Strife SPECIAL TAG TEAM MATCH Kerry Powell & Rob Beauty (vs) Ric Beauty & Sebastian Paddy O'Shea (vs) Dirk Western WINNER IN TRIPLE THREAT TITLE MATCH Bobby Silas (vs) Faith RANKINGS 24 HOUR CHAMPION- "24 Karat" Kerry Powell 1) Sebastian 2) "The Ace" Jack Spade 3) Zell Strife 4) Tai Angela 5) G-Gangsta Please send RP's to either [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] This next card will go a long way to set up the Sept PPV so get involved.
