Thursday Thunder, 22 November 2001

[The RCW theme hits as the lights flash red and blue, The RCW walks out lead by G-Gangsta who is now the leader. Demonic and Blaze stand at the right and left of the ramp with their RCW jerseys. Real Deal is standind beside
Gangsta with a "Real Deal...RCW" t-shirt on. G-Gangsta has the BWF Championship around his waist. They walk down the rampway taunting the crowd and giving them mean looks. G-Gangsta grabs the mic and speaks first.]
G-Gangsta: You know since last Friday Night Friction, I've gotten mails and phonecalls and e-mails saying, "why G why?". Well that is a simple question to answer. These three men are talented. Unlike the people back in
VWF, in VWF everyone thinks they were the best. When it was obvious that I WAS! I mean who else came in and won a title with a week!?!? HUH? I DID! Dis Jack Spade do that? Did Zell Strife do that? Did the "Fake Dark Monster" do that? NO! But Real Deal, Demonic and Blaze are bigger, stronger and better then VWF! Well VWF with a new leadership we will take over and there will be more members to come. Even with four members we are still a threat. I have proof.
The President of VWF...Rich put his two best teams up to try and take us down. Dage and good old Jack Spade. Two former RCW stars tonight live, you will realize that you should have NEVER left the leading force in
Wrestling...R C W!!! Blaze: Jack do you remember the POSSE!?!? Back in RCW? Me, The Real Deal at one point, The Ring Master, Justin Sane and the President [name withheld]. Your dumbass was trying to fight us, trying to save your little stomping ground while we worked damn hard to make the place better. And you guys never did win the battle did you? I mean JR could have fired you but you know he didn't he respected you Jack and you went and turned your back on him, on us
Jack. Well guess what we will get even tonight live on Thunder. Jack I don't care about winning, just about beating your ass as well as Dage and Triple X. This is not a match it is a WAR! AND I WANT IT NO HOLDS BARRED! ANYTHING GOES! FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE! Why? Because I want you guys DONE and BLOODY! Demonic:Thats right, my name isn't Demonic for nothing and all I will say is that I will get you Dage. You lost MY BELT! YOU DUMB F***! I AM NOT
GOING TO KICK YOUR ASS I AM GOING TO KILL YOU! AND SEND YOU TO HELL YOU LITTLE SH!T YOOUR CAREER IS DONE! Real Deal: I am in the Main Event tonight also and I am the biggest man in the Tri-Feds so I will look forward to chokeslaming one of you through a table. SO PUT THAT DOWN YOUR PANTS AND PLAY WITH IT!
G-Gangsta: Also I have two more things to say. The G-Force is now the most popular move in the Tri-Feds. Know why? I don't see ANY of your moves in the
12 Days of Christmas slogan... but the G-FORCE is there isn't it? ISN'T IT!?!? And at the 12 Days Of Christmas...The RCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP WILL BE UP FOR GRABS!!! THAT'S RIGHT WE ARE NOW A FED AGAIN!!!!!!!!!!!!! PEAAAAAAAAAACE! ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() THE LEGEND OF XTREME vs BIG HUEY ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() The Legend came out with his bag of tricks and was all over Huey. Big Huey did not seem to interested in the match and even tried to leave at one point but waas stopped by Blackbird.
The Legend stomped Huey to the ground and hit a DDT that left Huey motionless in the middle of the ring.
The Legend made the cover. WINNER- THE LEGEND OF XTREME by pinfall. After the match the arena lights went out! Tai Angela appeared in the ring and blue the green smoke into the face of The Legend, who left with Huey and Tai.
Hi Rich, Sorry got home from work late last night and was too tired to get this done hopefully it's not too late.
*The camera's go to the back where they show the arrival of the New T3 Champion Thomas Rainka. He's dressed in black cords and a red and black sweater and the T3 Title over his shoulder as he makes his way towards the dressing room area. For the first time the camera takes notice of a man a few inches shorter than Rainka dressed in a half decent looking suit. Rainka notices the camera and stops and the camera moves closer for a better picture.*
Rainka:"Ah perfect just what I needed. *turns and looks at the man beside him* Alright Brad we've got a camera time for you to do your job."
*The cameraman pipes in at this point to figure out who this Brad is.* Cameraman:"Wait a minute I don't recognize this guy, he's not employed by the VWF." Rainka:"I'm well aware of that, but as T3 champion I felt that no VWF interviewer was qualified enough to interview myself so I supplied my own. Meet Brad Dyck, former RWA color commentator."
Brad(while waving at the camera):"Hi." Cameraman:"Alright then" Brad:"Alright fans of VWF I'm here with the new Triple Threat Champion Thomas Rainka. Now there has been some contraversy concerning your holding of that title, what do you have to say about that?"
Rainka:"Well it seems some people don't think that Thomas Rainka is deserving of this title, that the only reason I have the T3 Title is because Zell Strife handed it to me.
While it be true that he did hand it to me, but Zell knew what he was doing. When he looked at Thomas Rainka he saw a man who was deserving to have this title. And if that's not enough for the nay-sayers then tonight I'll just have to prove the true champion I am with a successful title defense."
Brad:"That's right your slotted to defend that belt against The Real Deal and a man you've faced before Anxiety Disorder. Do you have any words or thoughts about them." Rainka:"Well first let me talk about The Real Deal. I've never actually faced him before but what is he 7'4" and according to him that's all he needs to win. Well let me inform The Real Deal that there's a little more to this thing we call wrestling than size. There's pride, determination and talent as well. The latter being something
I've yet to see from him and...well I hate to stand around and spout cliches but I'm going to do it anyway. The bigger they are the harder they fall and I'm going to prove that tonight. As for Anxiety Disorder, what's there to say about him."
Dyck:"Well he seems to think that he'll triumph over you quite easily and I believe called you the rain man."
Rainka:"Rain man? I must have hit him really hard in my match with him. Rain man? Newsflash for Anxiety Disorder.
I'm not Tom Cruise. You got the first name right but the one obvious fact is I'm much better looking than Cruise. Tonight the same fate that befell you a few weeks ago will repeat itself and the people here at the Thomas Rainka Center will have their proof that I'm the rightful holder of the T3
Title." Brad:"Thomas Rainka Center???" Rainka:"Well it's what this place should be called instead of the Thomas and Mack Center." Brad:"Oh ok I get it now." *Rainka shakes his head and starts to walk away muttering something about Brad using his head. Brad follows him as the cameraman watches them walk away before things go back inside the arena*
()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() SEBASTIAN vs COREY FULLER ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() Corey Fuller got a good reception from the fans at the T&M. Fuller was a wrestling machine early in this one and kept Sebastian off his feet.
Sebastian tried to slow down Fuller but he could not. Sebastian went for a backflip and missed and Fuller quickly nailed the Fuller Drop! But as the ref was making the count, Tai Angela, Huey and The Legend came to the ring and rushed in and beat down Fuller. Blackbird made sure Sebastian got away safe. Huey and The Legend held Fuller as
Angela nailed a HEART PUNCH that left Fuller out in the ring. Sebastian called for the mic SEBASTIAN: Take a good look you losers! This is the NEW BWF! No Ric Beauty, no Corey Fuller, no Zell Strife and sure as hell no G-Gangsta! G, the BWF wants the belt back! And the BWF is going to take it back! WINNER- COREY FULLER, by DQ DAN THE MAN ALLER: At that point the RCW music hit and the BWF guys left the ring and ran into the crowd.
()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() EIGHT MAN ELIMINATION MATCH THE RCW vs TRIPLE X and ELEMENTS OF VIOLENCE ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() Fans were pumped for this match! The RCW led by G-Gangsta against the Tag Champs Triple X and EOV! All eight men filled the ring and the insanity began! After a few minutes only Dage and Blaze were in the ring and Blaze was looking to avenge his loss to Dage at Phear Phantastic.
Blaze hit move after move and Dage was in trouble until he tagged in Jack Spade. The crowd went nuts! Spade beat down Blaze, Demonic, Real Deal and saved his best stuff for G-Gangsta. A cheap shot by Gangsta turned things around. Spade made it over and tagged Chumpy who ran heaadfirst into a clothesline from G-Gangsta, who tagged Demonic. Demonic nailed the
PITS of HELL and eliminated Chumpy. Earthquake came into the ring and CHOKESLAMMED Demonic and went for the cover that was broken up by The Real Deal.
Everyone poured into the ring! It was then the BWF got involved! The Legend returned and nailed G-Gangsta with a chair, rolled him in the ring and he was pinned by Dage!
The Real Deal was getting beaten down by Huey and Tai Angela when Demonic saved him! But Spade hit a JACKPOT and pinned The Real Deal. Security came out and got rid of the BWF but not after the damage was done. Blaze took over in the ring on Dage. Hit the INFERNO and scored the pin. That left Demonic and Blaze against Spade and Earthquake. Jack Spade jumped in and took Blaze to school! Fist drops, piledrivers and finally the JACKPOT!
Spade pinned Blaze. Demonic came into the ring but Spade and Earthquake worked like a team. Tag after tag they kept Demonic in trouble.
Spade held the big man and Earthquake climbed to the top rope, for a BODY PRESS????? But Demonic broke free and Earthquake crushed Spade! Demonic gave a big boot to Earthquake then covered Spade to eliminate him. Demonic then gave the PITS of HELL to Earthquake! WINNERS- THE RCW when Demonic pinned Earthquake. After the match Earthquake began pushing Spade and yelling at him. Spade answered with a JACKPOT and left the ring only to be met by Earthquakes partner Chumpy on the way up the ramp! Chumpy and Spade exchanged blows back up the ramp and into the back.
()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() **T3 TITLE MATCH** THOMAS RAINKA (C) vs THE REAL DEAL vs ANXIETY DISORDER ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() *"God of the Mind" by Disturbed plays and Thomas Rainka comes out with the T3 Title on his shoulder. He's wearing his Black,Red and White wrestling pants with the maple leaf on the outer left thigh and black wrestling boots. He looks out onto the crowd but doesn't seem to be paying much attention to them. He walks up to the ring and circles around it once placing the T3 Title in one corner and removing his black t-shirt once again to reveal his athletically muscular upper body. He slides into the ring and looks ready to go.*
Rainka was given the title by Zell Strife and came to the ring last with a smirk and a smile. The Real Deal looked a little worn after the eight man match and Anxiety Disorder looked, well.....crazy.
AD and RD went after the champ both but Rainka held his own against the unconventional styles of his opponents.
Rainka sent RD over the ropes and to the floor. But the T3 Champ ran head first into a fist from Anxiety Disorder. AD took over and slammed
Rainka, pump handle style into the center of the ring. Then AD went up to the top rope and tried a plancha!
He missed! Rainka locked on the Only Ending and AD tapped out! But The Real Deal climbed into the ring and hit Rainka from behind. With the size advantage Real Deal took Rainka down with a series of elbows, punches and an eye poke for good measure.
But once again the BWF stuck their noses into the match! The lights went out and GREEN SMOKE appeared all down the ramp. There was a FLASH of FIRE from the ring and Tai Angela was in the center of the ring standing over Rainka and The Real Deal!!!!!
The ref gazed at Angela who simply walked out of the ring. With no other choice the ref counted both Rainka and Real Deal out.
WINNER- NONE, Rainka and Real Deal counted out after Anxiety Disorder was pinned. That was it for the night! VWF ©2001
