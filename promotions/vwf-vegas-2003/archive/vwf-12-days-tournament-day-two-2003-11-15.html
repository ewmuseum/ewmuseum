12 Days Tournament: Day Two

Nov 15, 2003

WEST: Jorge got the attention of the ref and El Gringo Loco waited, and CRAAAAAAACKED Demola in the side of the head! DTMA: Loco made the cover and got the fall!!!! ONE!! TWO!! THREEE!!!! WEST: But the place erupted when Jushin Liger GOLD ran to ringside, and let the ref know about the brass knuckles!!! The ref searched Loco, found the brass knuckles and reversed his decision and DISQUALIFIED El Gringo Loco! (CUT to Dan The Man Aller and Dirk Western sitting at the 12 Days headquarters) DTMA: Welcome to Day Two of the Twelve Days Tournament. We had a great night on Wednesday and Day Two should be great as well! WEST: That match between El Gringo Loco and Devin Demola has people talking. DTMA: As does Ryan Delaney who looked good, and who can forget about Yancy Kilpatrick come to the ring as the Masked Manaic and beating Daniel Sinz! WEST: We have some good matches tonight, Heath Starr and Classy Joey Boy has people excited, as does the 12 Days debut of Lark Fenriz, in a match with Fast Eddie Cutlass. DMTA: And of course, this year the 12 Days Tourney has tag teams and we see all eight of them tonight. WEST: Well lets go the highlights of those tag teams right now! .. .. .. .. .. TAG GROUP 1 ------------ \\\\\\\ THE RODEO BOYS (vs) DESOLATION ROW /////// -------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Desolation Row came to the ring with a mixed reaction from the fans. WEST: Well uneducated fans would not be happy about Desolation Row, and would cheer the fools who are the Rodeo Boys. DTMA: Well Kenny Kidd started the match against The Black Demon Brian Hunter... WEST: Hunter quickly got the better of Kidd and in true GREAT tag team fashion he kept Kidd in the Desolation Row corner.. DMTA: Hunter and partner FIRESTORM Alex Demola made quick tags... but for the most part used street fight tactics to keep Kenny Kidd. WEST: Demola did nail a BACKBREAKER and held it while Hunter slingshoted himself in for a GUILLOTINE LEGDROP on Kidd. DTMA: Sling shotted??? WEST: Yeah, you know came over the ropes.. DTMA: I know what it is, is that a word... slingshotted?? WEST: It is now. DMTA: But Kidd managed to kick out..... WEST: Kidd did managed to get a SUNSET FLIP on Hunter on a BACK BODY DROP attempt but Hunter kicked out.... DTMA: But Kidd rolled to his corner and tagged in Billy Bones! WEST: Clearly the smarter and tougher of the Rodeo Boys. DMTA: Bones dropped Hunter in a flash.... and then knocked Demola off the ropes. WEST: Bones hit a series of DROPKICKS on Hunter and had The Black Demon in trouble. DMTA: Hunter found himself coming off the ropes and into a HURRICANRANA! WEST: And then Bones went to the top rope looking to give Hunter and EIGHT SECOND RIDE! DMTA: But Bones was pushed off the top rope by Alex Demola! WEST: This caused Bones to go to his corner and pull out the COWBELL!!! DTMA: Bones turned and CLAAAAAANG!!!!! WEST: Down went Brian Hunter and the bell rang! DTMA: Alex Demola hit the ring and he got CLANGED too. WEST: Finally Kenny Kidd TACKLED his own tag team partner and took the cowbell from him... DTMA: But the upshot is Desolation Row wins by DQ! !!!!! WINNERS = Desolation Row !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ NEW AGE ARMY (vs) THE FALLEN ANGELS /////// --------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Saku and Colossus, the New Age Army, jumped the Angels as Cage and Insomniac made their way to the ring! WEST: Colossus working on the 400 pound Insomniac and Saku looking to put the hurt on Tony Cage. DTMA: Saku hit three straight HAMMERLOCK POWERSLAMS on Tony Cage to the floor before the action was forced in the ring by the ref. WEST: Saku continued to wear on the arm of Tony Cage, clearly looking to set him up for the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE! DTMA: Saku and Colossus made quick tags but each man would keep and ARM BAR, or HAMMERLOCK on Cage.... WEST: Cage did reverse one and used a SHATTERING ELBOW to drop Saku.. DTMA: But Colossus distracted the ref.. who did not see Cage tag in Insomniac. WEST: Insomniac did get in the ring and hit a CLOTHESLINE on Iyasu Saku but the ref took Insomniac back to the apron and the New Age Army double teamed Tony Cage. DTMA: Colossus took Cage for a big ride with a DOUBLE HANDED SIT OUT CHOKESLAM! WEST: But Cage somehow managed to kick out! DMTA: Colossus tagged in Saku, they sent Cage on a double irish whip... Cage ducked... WEST: He came back off the ropes... SPEARED Colossus in the knee.... and form his back on the mat KICKED Saku who bounced off the ropes and Cage MONKEY FLIPPED him across the ring! DTMA: With the crowd chanting ANGELS! ANGELS! Cage made the tag... WEST: Colossus was CLOTHESLINED out of the ring by Insomniac.... DTMA: Who turned to Iyasu Saku..... Insomniac went for a CLOTHESLINE but ended up in the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE!!!!! WEST: A huge gasp sucked the air out of the arena! DTMA: Insmoniac though used his other arm to begin to get a hold on the head of Saku..... WEST: Insomniac rolled Saku over and when they came up on the other side, Saku was in the INSOMNIA CURE!!!!! DTMA: Colossus made the save... well, he was going to until Tony Cage SPEARED that knee again! WEST: And Saku could take it no longer... he TAPPED! DTMA: The Fallen Angels score the big win! !!!!! WINNERS = The Fallen Angels !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. TAG GROUP 2 ------------ \\\\\\\ JUSHIN LIGER GOLD & EL GRINGO LOCO (vs) 28TH STREET /////// ------------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: The big question was How will JLG and Loco act as a team? They are not a tag team in the true sense, they came together by accident as champions. WEST: JLG started the match.... against D-Rock.. but JLG was in trouble from the begining! DTMA: D-Rock and Hakeem used fast tags and lots of fists, eye gouges and kicks to keep GOLD down. WEST: Hey! 28th Street didn't just beat him up! DTMA: Right... they did a SUPLEX at one point. WEST: Thank you. DTMA: But JLG was able to mount offense... only to see Loco jump off the apron when JLG would come over to make the tag! WEST: El Gringo Loco was clearly letting JLG win or lose this one on his own! DTMA: Hakeem B'Andou nailed GOLD with a SPINEBUSTER and things were looking very bad for the masked man. WEST: But Hakeem missed a MOONSAULT! DTMA: On his feet Hakeem met a HIGH LEG CLOTHESINE from JLG! WEST: And when D-Rock came in the ring... JLG HIP TOSSED him over the top rope and onto his partner El Gringo Loco! DMTA: With the fans screaming WE WANT GOLD! WE WANT GOLD! JLG went to the top ropes... and it was LIGHTNING STRIKE time! WEST: Well the champs got the win, but JLG cannot do it all himself. !!!!! WINNERS = Jushin Liger GOLD & El Gringo Loco !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ LEGENDS OF THE VWF (vs) THE RHOMBUS /////// --------------------------------------------------- DTMA: Sebastian started this match off against CAPSMAN... WEST: CAPSMAN was a man possesed... he took the fight to Sebastian... DROPKICKS, and SNAP SUPLEXES and Sebastian was fighting for his life! DTMA: It was a shock... but then came to surprise.... CAPSMAN tagged in Sonicman.... and as Sonicman was getting in the ring CAPSMAN hit him with the CAPS FACTOR!!!! WEST: CAPSMAN then took off his mask to reveal himself as EVIL CAPSMAN! DTMA: Same mask but the word evil on the back..... WEST: But CAPSMAN, the real one came out from the back in handcuffs and leg shackles and tried to make the save... DTMA: But he could not even get close! WEST: Sonicman was left in the ring, Sebastian tagged Gangsta in who picked up Sonicman and hit him with the QUEENZ CRUSHA TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER and then went to the top rope... DMTA: GANGSTA SMASH!!! for the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNERS = The Legends of the VWF !!!!! .. .. .. DTMA: Now lets get to the singles action from Day 2! GROUP E -------- \\\\\\\ DEMONIC (vs) BLAZE /////// ---------------------------------- DMTA: This was not much of a match. WEST: These two don't like each other anymore, but it was clear Demonic has more anger.. DMTA: Demonic was on fire, pardon the pun... he simple beat down Blaze.. WEST: A reversal on an irish whip and a BIG BOOT was the only offense of Blaze DTMA: Blaze could not follow up on it though and after missing a LEGDROP Demonic was back in control! WEST: Demonic ended it at the four minute mark with the PITZ OF HELL powerbomb for the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNER = Demonic !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ THE WOLF LARK FENRIZ (vs) FAST EDDIE CUTLASS /////// ------------------------------------------------------------ DTMA: Cutlass was unsure of Fenriz.. as was most of us... WEST: Vegas attracts guys like this. DTMA: Fenriz kicked Cutlass as they went to lock up.. WEST: Fenriz tossed some FOREARMS down on Cutlass and as Cutlass was double over Fenriz hit him with a SPINNING HEEL KICK! DTMA: He went for a quick cover but Cutlass kicked out before a one count. WEST: Fenriz then worked over Cutlass, keeping his feet in the face of Fast Eddie... DTMA: The Wolf went for a POWERSLAM when Cutlass floated off his shoulder, pushed Fenriz to the ropes and nearly got him on a SCHOOLBOY! WEST: A VERY upset Fenriz lept to his feet but Cutlass was waiting with a DROPKICK! DMTA: Cutlass then caught a charging Lark in a SIDE HEADLOCK TAKEDOWN... WEST: And Cutlass kept him on the mat, I think looking to wear him down. DMTA: Fast Eddie is a classic style wrestling in my opinion.... WEST: If you mean boring then you are right. DTMA: Well Cutlass spent much of the next three minutes keeping Fenriz on the mat in HEADLOCKS, CHINLOCKS and the odd ARM BAR. WEST: All this was clearly upsetting Fenriz.... The Wolf was getting more and more frustrated as the battle moved on. DTMA: Well back on their feet... Cutlass caught Fenriz in the HIGH BACK BODY DROP!!! WEST: And Fenriz rolled out of the ring.... it looked for a moment like he was leaving... and going to give Cutlass the win by count out. DTMA: But Fenriz did return to the ring only to get taken down once again by Fast Eddie Cutlass. WEST: Fenriz got up and GOUGED the EYES of Cutlas..... but when Fenriz came off the ropes... Cutlass got him in an INVERTED ATOMIC DROP! DMTA: It seemed like Cutlass was one step ahead of Fenriz all through this match. WEST: Well, not the whole way through.. DMTA: Good point, it was near the tim limit when Cutlass came off and hit a CROSS BODY on Fenriz but somehow Fenriz caught him and turned it into a sit down reverse powerbomb... Lark calls it SAMMAEL!!!! WEST: He can also call it a win! !!!!! WINNER = Lark Fenriz !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. .. GROUP F -------- \\\\\\\ JASON STORM (vs) MARCEL KLUTZ /////// --------------------------------------------- DTMA: There was a HUUUUUUGE groan as Klutz made his way to the ring. WEST: Yes there was... this may be the toughest group, except for Klutz. DTMA: Storm came to the ring... grabbed Klutz... hit him with a CLOTHESLINE and then ended it with the PERFECT STORM!!!! WEST: It was over in 7 seconds! DTMA: Fastest match in the history of the tournament, maybe of all time! !!!!! WINNER = Jason Storm !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. \\\\\\\ HEATH STARR (vs) CLASSY JOEY BOY /////// ------------------------------------------------ DTMA: Two guys who are fast cruiserweights..... I love this stuff! WEST: Naaa, give me big guys lumbering around. DTMA: I think you are working for the wrong company... WEST: There is a company that pushes big dumb slow guys??? DTMA: Yeah, I think their show is on Mondays and Thursdays.. WEST: CJB is in his third 12 Days Tournament. DTMA: Starr wanted to show off his abilities and he did, he came right after Classy Joey Boy... a fast go behind and a GERMAN RELEASE ... WEST: Followed in a flash by a FIREMANS CARRY as CJB got to his feet... DMTA: Heath Starr continued to work over CJB... WEST: Classy Joey Boy did get a reversal on a corner irish whip... but as CJB charged Starr moved and hit a DROP TOE HOLD that bounced the head of Classy Joey Boy off the second turnbuckle. DMTA: Starr picked up CJB and hit a GUTWRENCH POWERBOMB!!! WEST: And Starr went up to the top rope, but it was just a little to soon as CJB got to his feet, shook the ropes.. and Starr fell on the top rope, and is now singing a little higher. DTMA: CJB then went to work on the legs of Starr... he got Starr in the center of the ring and dropped ELBOW after ELBOW onto the knee of Heath Starr. WEST: Classy Joey Boy even used a KNEE BREAKER to continue the punishment on Starrs knee. DTMA: But when CJB went for the SUGAR BLASTER, Starr wrapped him in a SMALL PACKAGE... WEST: CJB kicked out.... he then waited for Starr to get to his feet. DTMA: CJB was clearly wanting to hit his KISS OF DEATH superkick... WEST: And he did! DTMA: But Starr kicked out! WEST: Classy Joey Boy sent Starr to the ropes but telegraphed the BACK BODY DROP! DTMA: Starr turned it into a BACKSLIDE and almost got the fall! WEST: Starr ducked a punch, kicked CJB and hit him with a DDT! DMTA: Heath Starr sent CJB to the ropes.. and got him in the ELECTIC CHAIR! WEST: Starr then went back up to the top rope........ DTMA: And lept for the FALLING STAR!!!!!! WEST: But CJB moved! Starr crashed to the mat! DTMA: Classy Joey Boy finally got to his feet... and then did something we've never seen CJB do! WEST: It was a piledriver to start with, but CJB moved his legs over the arms of Starr... and then jumped and fell driving Starr face first into the mat. DMTA: CJB calls the new move THE REJECTION FACTOR! and it was good enough for the win! !!!!! WINNER = Classy Joey Boy !!!!! .. .. .. .. .. DTMA: Lets take a look at the Groups! Tag Group 1 ------------ Desolation Row & Fallen Angels 1-0 Rodeo Boys & New Age Army 0-1 Tag Group 2 ------------ JLG/Loco & Legends 1-0 Rhombus & 28th Street 0-1 Group E -------- Demonic & Lark Fenriz 1-0 Eddie Cutlass & Blaze 0-1 Group F ------- Jason Storm & CJB 1-0 Heath Starr & Marcel Klutz 0-1 DTMA: See you Monday for more action in Groups A, B, C and D! WEST: Got some big ones.... Delaney and Maq! DTMA: How about Sinz and Blair? Or Andrews and Frost? WEST: They are all big! DTMA: And of course next Wednesday Groups E and F and the Tag Groups continue! WEST: Stay tuned, we still have 10 days to go! © 12 Days Tournament
