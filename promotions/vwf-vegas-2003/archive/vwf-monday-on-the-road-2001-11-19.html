Monday On The Road, 19 November 2001

19 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF Monday On The Road November 19, 2001 Welcome to the first edition of Monday On The Road from Desert Pines
High School. Tonight... The Real Deal meets The Ghost and Justin Sane battles Anxiety Disorder But first some interviews...... **THE REAL DEAL** [The scene opens to a back alley in Newark New Jersey. Real Deal is there in a RCW jersey and a RCW parka(puffy jacket) and jeans. He turns around to the camera and speaks.]
Real Deal: I am in a one of the dark matches tonight on Monday On The
Road
against The Ghost. Ghost i don't kknow you but your a smaller then me so that means...I win! Because I will go on to Thunder and bring the Triple
Threat
Championship to RCW! RCW is where all the Championships should be! So
Ghost
wait untill the match of pain for you.
I dont see you in a Main Event...know why? BECAUSE YOU SUCK AND I WILL MAKE YOU REALISE IT WHEN I RIP YOU APPART AND DESTROY YOU! BECAUSE THE REAL DEAL IS HERE TO STAY AND IT WILL TAKE A HELL OF A LOT MORE THEN YOU TO GET RID OF ME YOU DUMB SH!T! PUT THAT DOWN YOUR PANTS AND PLAY WITH IT! [The scene fades to black as Real Deal is on his way to the airport to go to the event.] ---------------- **SEBASTIAN** (The BWF President is sitting at his desk, looking very upset) SEBASTIAN: Corey Fuller, Sebastian told you to win. Told you to defeat
Zell Strife and you did not do it.
So Sebastian must take care of this Sebastians self! Sebastian is going to make an example of you on Thursday! The BWF is Sebastians and it will not be an embarassment.
And that brings Sebastian to the BWF champ, G-Gangsta. So you joined the RCW. So you think you are the best thing to ever happen to the VWF? How many times have you held the 24 Hour Title? You know G....the big one? None! Sebastian is a two time champion! Sebastian ran Kerry Powell and Ric
Beauty OUT of VWF. And Corey Fuller will be next.
And then after that G-Gangsta it will be you! Sebastian is Sebastian and you are NOT! (fade) --------------------- **TRIPLE X** (The VWF Tag Team Champs) (Chumpy and Earthquake are eating a feast inside Montesanos Italian restaurant in Las Vegas)
EARTHQUAKE: VWF Tag Team Champs! If that isn't worth celebration I don't know what is! CHUMPY: We are the best team in the VWF, easy! I mean, I respect
Elements of Violence, Spade and Dage will make great teammates when we beat down the RCW.
But we are the team. EARTHQUAKE: And after Thurday, EOV can get a shot at these belts. If they dare. CHUMPY: (burps) Good stuff. The RCW! What a bunch of punks. EARTHQUAKE: Yeah Demonic. CHUMPY: Sad gothic dude. EARTHQUAKE: Blaze CHUMPY: Cheap Demonic rip off. EARTHQUAKE: The Real Deal. CHUMPY: The Big Fat Deal. EARTHQUAKE: And G-Gangsta. CHUMPY: The traitor! EARTHQUAKE: I am going to make G-Gangsta pay! Simple as that! CHUMPY: And if Demonic and Blaze think they are worthy of being tag team champs we can take them on after EOV!
EARTHQUAKE: We are eating all the competition! CHUMPY: (burps, louder than before) And it is good! --------------- **ANXIETY DISORDER** AD: Justin Sane.....just insane. Crazy, crazy for being in love with you. But love will not save you
Justin! Anixety Disorder is not a disorder but a way of life.
Keep those china dolls away from me! I am the force that is coming like a hurricane. I will rock you like a hurricane! (starts dancing and badly humming the scorpions song) After justin comes Thomas Rainka, the T3 Champion, but a paper champion. Given the belt by the man. The man who is the man, or at least is the man right now.
Zell Strife knows. Knows in his heart, which he will not leave in San
Francisco but in Las Vegas, after I take it out and take his title.
But all in due time. First just insane and then rain man. (fade) ------------------ **THE LEGEND OF XTREME** (The hardcore legend is sitting on a street corner begging for change from the passersby) THE LEGEND: Bum! Huey! How hardcore are you? How hardcore are any of the f***ing punks in the
VWF? Not very! Zell Strife is a no body! Big time matches, Hellfire, Rage In The
Cage. Psst. Nothing. G-Gangsta thinks he has the stones. But all he has is a title belt. This is a challenge to any man in the VWF, come fight the Legend! In a streefight, right here. At Lake Mead and D Street here in North Las
Vegas, the part of Vegas you don't see on the TV shows. I will be waiting.
(The Legend goes back to begging) ------------------ ON THE ROAD from Desert Pines High School Desert Pines was filled in this event to benefit the band. After watching the interviews the fans got to see COREY FULLER defeat Carl The Killer Stone and then BLAZE crushed The Titan then came the main events..... JUSTIN SANE vs ANXIETY DISORDER The fans seemd to be mixed in who they were for in this match. Justin
Sane came out with some good lucha style moves that kept AD off at the begining. But AD took over and pounded Justin Sane and eventually delivered the LOBOTOMY for the pin.
ANXIETY DISORDER over Justin Sane and in the MAIN EVENT! THE REAL DEAL vs THE GHOST The Real Deal could not be stopped. The fans booed and hissed as RD ran through The Ghost like he wasn't even there...like he was a Ghost.
The ref alomst DQed Real Deal after a powerbomb through a table outside the ring but Real Deal tossed The Ghost back in the ring and made the pin.
THE REAL DEAL over THE GHOST. So on Thursday Anxiety Disorder and The Real Deal will battle THOMAS
RAINKA for the T3 Title.
