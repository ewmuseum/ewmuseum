Thursday Thunder, 15 November 2001

THURSDAY THUNDER NOVEMBER 15, 2001 (Rob Beauty is sitting behind his desk, and waiting, looking around. Finally Dage walks in with his Tag Team Title around his shoulder) ROB BEAUTY: Dage! Dage! Great you could come on such short notice. (they shake hands) As you know tonight has to be a big night for the VWF, I mean that strap is the only one we have right now.
So I need a favor from you...... If Triple X wins then the VWF will have both titles....but if you and
Demonic win that is only one.
And two is better than one, so I need you to make sure that you and
Demonic lose those belts.
And if you can do that, I will give you and Jack Spade, Elements of
Violence the next shot at the belts!
What do you say? (Dage looks kinda funny at Rob Beauty before a smile comes upon his face.) Dage: Rob, just to make something perfectly clear...The EOV Do not
Belong to RCW, BWF or even VWF. We do however protect the VWF investment. I should slap the taste right out of your mouth for even metioning that I lose the Tag titles on purpose.
(Dage goes to slap Rob right in the mouth as Rob falls on his ass. Dage stops...laughs...and picks him off the floor.) Dage: But...I hate RCW more then anything and the EOV doesn't really need the titles but what the hell...these straps mean something to
Demonic why not lose what he holds dear. Yeah I'll do it...
(Rob gets a smile on his face.) Dage: But...NOT FOR YOU FOR ME! (At that Dage kicks Rob in his gut and throws him over his desk.) Dage: And that is what the EOV is all about! (Dage walks away as Rob gets up and punchs his desk.) (VIVA LAS VEGAS begins to play and the fans inside the Thomas and Mack go wild as the first THURSDAY THUNDER is about to get underway, scenes from the PPV flash by on the big screen....
Sebastian knocking Corey Fuller off the ropes Demonic nailing The Legend Jack Spade with a Jackpot to Zell Strife G-Gangsta with the G-Force and Zell Strife blazes onto the screen with the 24 Hour Title and the
T3 Belt over his shoulders)

WINNER- ANXIETY DISORDER by pinfall with the LOBOTOMY DA: Next up was another newcomer Justin Sane going against Paddy
O'Sheas partner Dirk Western.
As O'Shea was leaving the ring after his loss Dirk Western came down and nailed O'Shea with a clothesline!
With O'Shea on the ramp Western grabbed a chair and began to beat
O'Shea. As Western was getting ready to hit O'Shea with the D-Day
Justin Sane came out with Crazy Train playing on the PA
()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() JUSTIN SANE vs DIRK WESTERN ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() DA: This match never made it to the ring! Justin Sane worked over Dirk Western and then turned to Paddy O'Shea and then walked off. Dirk Western regained his feet and nailed the D-Day to Paddy O'Shea. WINNER- DOUBLE COUNT OUT DA: And the fans were treated to a blood fest in the mast match of the first hour. ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() ***BWF TITLE MATCH*** HARDCORE RULES G-GANGSTA (C) vs THE LEGEND OF XTREME ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() DA: G-Gangsta is now part of the RCW and The Legend was hoping to be the first to bring home the belts to the VWF.
The Legend quickly took this match to the xtreme by using a cane on G-Gangsta. The G-Man was bleeding pretty good when he turned the tables in the match with a hurricanrana!
With G-Gangsta in charge he showed why he is one of the biggest stars in this fed! He took The Legend outside of the ring and beat him with everything! Chairs, tables, the bell. Soon both these men were bloody! Back in the ring they exchanged punches until they both fell over! With the ref counting Rob Beauty came down to the ring and tossed a bag to The Legend. The Legend pulled out a CHESS GRATER! and played G-Gangsta head like a violin! U-Trop made his way over and ‘helped' Rob Beauty back to the dressing room. G-Gangsta set up a table and placed The Legend on the top rope! G-FORCE through the table! WINNER- G-GANGSTA, by pinfall with the G-Force to keep the BWF Title. *---COMMERCIAL---* DA: We are back and it is time for live matches! And do we have a big one! Rob Beauty and the VWF are 0-1 so far as G-Gangsta beat The Legend. Next up is the tag team title match! ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() ***VWF TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH*** DEMONIC and DAGE (C) vs TRIPLE X vs TAI ANGELA and HUEY ()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()()() (Black Sabbath begins to play and the arena lights slowly dim. A procession of young women all dressed in black ropes, each carrying a candle begin the walk to the ring. Tai Angela follows them, inside the ring he stands in the center and the women form a circle around him, they blow out the candles, fall to their knees in front of him. With a wave he banishes them and walks to his corner, except for his personal assistant Blackbird who calls for the mic)
BLACKBIRD: Prepare for the Dark Monster! Prepare for salvation! He is six feet nine inches and two hundred and eight pounds of destruction he is TAI ANGELA The Dark Monster! DA: Look at that! Blackbird has Huey on a leash! (Eat It by Weird Al hits and two REALLY fat guys begin making their way to the ring, Chumpy is in front carrying a sub sandwhich and

DA: At a combined weight of 707 pounds this is Chumpy and Earthquake..... TRIPLE X!!!!! DA: The fans love these two! (The RCW theme plays....Bodies by Drowning Pool plays...over it) DA: Demonic is halfway down the ramp and Dage is at the other end. Dage told Rob Beauty he would help Triple X win this match in exchange for a tag team title match for EOV.
I wonder if Demonic knows he is alone here. I know the fans dont care. They are behind Dage! Chumpy and Huey lock up in the ring! Headlock by Chumpy. Demonic and Dage are arguing in their corner. Chumpy to the ropes....Huey with a sidewalk slam. And he turns to
Angela and bows. What is going on with Huey? Angela points to the ropes. Huey is up on the ropes. He is going for a splash! Chumpy moves! And Chumpy tags Dage! Dage moves into the ring and is putting the boot to Huey! To the ropes and a BIG BOOT! 1 KICKOUT! Dage grabs Huey and tags Demonic! Dage opens up the big guy and Demonic throws a punch into the kidneys of Huey! Demonic slams a few big forearms onto the head of Huey. Demonic with a body slam and follows it with an elbowdrop!
Huey is in trouble. Demonic picks him up and Look at Angela! Tai Angela has tilted his head back and blew green smoke into the air! Demonic is looking and .... LOW BLOW by Huey who goes over and tags in Tai Angela! Angela with a quick DDT! 1 2 KICKOUT by Demonic! Tai Angela is pulling Demonic to his feet! Angela with a PUMBHANDLE SLAM!!!!! Demonic is getting beaten down by the Dark Monster. Angela is setting up Demonic and neckbreaker!
Here comes Henry Huxtable, Hueys father! Henry is pleading with Huey to come with him. Angela is heading over..... Demonic grabs Angela PITS OF HELL!!!! 1 2 THREE!!!!! Demonic pins Tai Angela and Tai and Huey are out! Blackbird just slapped Henry! Earthquake is in the ring and he and Demonic are exchanging punches. Tai Angela is ordering Huey to go after his father and Huey is DOING IT!!!! Henry is running from his big boy! Earthquake throws Demonic into the corner and follows him in! (wow that hadda hurt pop) Demonic stumbles out of the corner..... and falls to the mat! Earthquake tags in Chumpy. Chumpy has Demonic up and runs across the ring CHEEEEEEEEESE BURGER!!!!! And Chumpy tags Earthquake. Earthquake comes in and ANOTHER CHEEEEEEEEESE BURGER!!!!!!!!!! Chumpy and Earthquake are getting ready to nailed the Double Double on
Demonic! And DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE by Demonic!!!! Demonic goes over and Dage jumps off the ring apron! There is on one to tag in. Demonic turns.....Chumpy picks him up.... DOUBLE DOUBLE!!!!! 1 2 THREEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!! We have new tag team champions! Triple X have won the belts! Dage is walking back up the ramp. He did what he said, he helped the
VWF but at what price.
What will Dage do? *---COMMERCIAL---* DA: Triple X have won the tag belts! Now the score is 2-1 VWF! With two matches left and only Zell Strife in the way!
(A man walks over to Dan Aller and hands him a piece of paper) DA: I was just handed a piece of paper from Ric Beauty who is supposed to be in the next match.
Let me read it... Dear Fans of the VWF... I am not the boy of Sebastian and will not get in the ring at his order. My attempt to take over the VWF failed, simple as that. I will no longer be a part of the VWF.
Thank you (confused pop) DA: Ric Beauty has left the VWF? He will not be in the next match, that is for sure. The Ghost is wearing a white one piece and a black mask with white eyes. He moves fast to the ring and leaps up onto the apron, to the top rope and does a backflip into the ring)
DA: Five feet ten, 195 pounds this is THE GHOST BOO BABY! DA: The Ghost is in the ring. He looked good at the PPV despite a tough loss to Thomas Rainka.

The Ghost is gone! Strife and Rainka turned to one another. And they shake hands! Zell Strife goes over and grabs the T3 Title and gives it to Thomas
Rainka.. Can he do that? Strife and Thomas Rainka are leaving the ring! What the hell just happened here? *---COMMERCIAL---* DA: Rob Beauty said during the break that because a pinfall did occur in the match that technically Zell Strife was counted out and Thomas
Rainka is the NEW T3 Champion?
Well the cage has come down over the barb wire ropes, the score is VWF
2, RCW 1 and Strife and Rainka 1.
