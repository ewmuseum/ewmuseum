VWF — Show

End Of March

March 30, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(There is no music, just shots of men from the IRS and FBI carrying boxes out of the offices of the VWF. Then a slow fade to JD Southerns, sitting at the VWF desk) SOUTHERNS: We are almost to The Gauntlet! This is the last show before the big show and the next to last show before we find out who the new owners will be. We only know that a closed bid auction is currently being held and that only The Beauty Brothers and 2 other secret bidders can be confirmed. Speaking of the Beautys, their night got started in the parking lot as they attacked Classy Joey Boy as he pulled in at the Joint. All three Beautys (maybe upset at getting ousted from the Brothers In Arms Tournament) took out their frustrations on Classy Joey Boy. John Tyler and security hit the scene and had the Beautys removed from the property and CJB was given aid. But the upshot is that John Tyler has booked a three on one match at The Gauntlet. The Beauty Brothers versus Classy Joey Boy, but CJB does have an equalizer. He can use a sledgehammer!!! Tyler also thought it would be good if the night was kicked off with some tag team action, the tag team titles as a matter of fact! .. .. .. =============================== VWF Tag Team Title Match TEAM BRITTANIA © (vs) MARK MCQUEEN & THUNDER VALENKOV =============================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: The action here was hot right from the start, McQueen and Valenkov were clearly upset about losing the titles in the triple threat on St Patricks Day. Justin and Simon Davidson eventually got the upper hand and worked Valenkov with double team moves, getting the big man in trouble and nearly getting the fall on three occasions. Valenkov made the hot tag and Mark McQueen cleared the ring like a, umm, well a Tazmanian Devil. McQueen drove Simon into the mat with a DEATH VALLEY DRIVER and then knocked Justin back off the apron. But Simon managed to get a shoulder up! McQueen then tagged in the big man. Valenkov then picked up McQueen in a press slam and dropped him on Simon Davidson, a move that had the crowd screaming and on their feet. That's when things got out of hand, as they so often do in the Vegas Wrestling Federation. A double irish whip saw Simon hit a SHOULDER BLOCK, that rocked Valenkov but knocked McQueen into the ref. With the ref out, Valenkov CHOKESLAMMED Simon Davidson much to the enjoyment of the crowd. That's when two familiar faces came up and out of the crowd, Stevie Knight and Austin Lee, also known as Hot Property!!! Justin Davidson tackled Thunder Valenkov and HP went to work on Mark McQueen. Hitting the PROPERTY BOOM!! But the bemused ref caught the action and called for the bell, disqualifying the champions! ((((( RESULT = McQueen and Valenkov via DQ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: But things were far from over in regards to the tag team titles. More on that later. Henry Fish, the man who has been running around claiming to be Scott Starring has finally lost his mind. He came to the ring and declared that at The Gauntlet he would appear with, .. . . VAN WILD!!! Which drew more than a few groans from the crowd. But Henry Fish said the at The Gauntlet "the greatest tag team of all time would appear". Anytime Fast Eddie Cutlass comes to the rings of the VWF the fans are up for anything. And anytime Gods Tears comes to the ring the fans know anything can happen. So the place was electric as the bell rang for the Hardcore Title Match! .. .. .. ======================== VWF Hardcore Title Match GODS TEARS © (vs) FAST EDDIE CUTLASS ======================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: The tension was thick as these two old enemies circled each other in the center of the ring. Cutlass made the first move with a quick takedown of Gods Tears and Cutlass then went to work on the mat. It was odd to see in a hardcore match but GT and Cutlass began a seminar on mat wrestling, but Cutlass clearly had the advantage on the mat. That's most likely what caused GT to make the decision to throw Cutlass to the outside and move the match to a more hardcore edge. GT used the ring steps to batter Cutlass and eventually break him open. But GT could not score the fall. Even after a TOP ROPE POWERBOMB once they were back in the ring. Cutlass mounted an offensive late in the match but neither man was able to get enough of an upper hand to score the fall, and the time limit expired. ((((( RESULT - Time Limit Draw ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Fans have been behind Roadkill Rick since his return a few weeks ago, but much of the crowd at the Orleans was cheering for The Dark Monster in this match up of two of the bigger men on the VWF roster. .. .. .. =========================== THE DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA (vs) ROADKILL RICK =========================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Rick took control early by over powering Angela. RKR hit a beautiful T-BONE SUPLEX at near the three minute mark and it looked like RKR was on his way to an easy win. But moments later Angela reversed an attempted SAMOAN DROP into an INVERTED DDT!!! >From there Angela began to methodically work on the chest of RKR, KNIFE EDGE CHOPS, DEEP ARM BARS and a FULL NELSON slam or two. Blackbird had the crowd on its feet as Angela sent RKR to the ropes and went for the heart punch Angela calls THE DARKNESS!!! But RKR ducked it and hit a SPEAR coming off the ropes and turned the tide of the match back into his favor! Rick slowly worked back up to full steam, and once he did he was beating The Dark Monster from pillar to post. RKR used a SPIKE DDT to get the monster in the position he wanted. Then he picked him up for ONE!! TWO!! THREE POWERBOMBS!!!! And then turned it right into a BOSTON CRAB!! THE TEXAS SPEEDBUMP EXECUTED TO PERFECTION!!!! But Blackbird got up on the apron and got RKR to break the hold. Angela creeped over and used a SCHOOLBOY WITH HANDFUL OF TIGHTS to get the fall! ((((( RESULT- Angela via schoolboy with handful of tight ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: The bad blood brewing between the O'Sheas and the Rhombus continues to grow. Paddy and Frank took out Sonicman at Ghost Town Blues, then on St Patricks Day they took out EVIL CAPSMAN. But the one thing the O'Sheas didn't count on was Lance Voltar teaming up with CAPSMAN, which added another person on the O'Shea hit list. .. .. .. ================================= THE O'SHEAS (vs) CAPSMAN & LANCE 'THE BIG V' VOLTAR ================================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Paddy and Frank attacked CAPS and Voltar before the bell and tried to go after CAPS after tossing Lance from the ring. But Voltar got back in the ring and cleared house! Frank went out after a great STANDING DROPKICK and Voltar planted Paddy with a TORNADO DDT before throwing him over the top rope and onto his brother!!! Lance Voltar was basking in the glow of the Orleans crowd when Frank got back in the ring, he turned Voltar and BLASTED him with a RIGHT JAB, then hit an UPPERCUT!!! Frank tagged Paddy and then sent Lance to the ropes, hit a DOUBLE FIST to his chin, then Paddy climbed on the second rope. Frank kept CAPS busy as Paddy hit the TOP ROPE FIST DROP and got the 1-2-3! ((((( RESULT - The O'Sheas via pinfall ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Robbie Caldwell has been trying to make his name in the VWF for a long time now. He took a long hard run at Adam Jennings and now he has said he is going to defeat the legends of the VWF, starting with The Maquilladora. .. .. .. ============================== THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL ============================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Caldwell and Maq have not squared off often and it showed in the way they took their time, trying to get a feel for each other. The Maquilladora used a SWEEP KICK to take control and he went to work on Caldwell with a series of kicks and knife edge chops. Caldwell used a reversal of a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP at the four minute mark to give himself the advantage. Caldwell went to work on the head and neck of Maq, using all the standard NECKBREAKERS and a SERIES OF DDT's that Caldwell calls the LIGHTNING STRIKE!! But he could not get the pinfall. Caldwell sent Maq to the ropes, only to have the masked superstar reverse it and hit him with a VICIOUS FLYING ELBOW!!! Maq nearly got the fall, but Caldwell got his foot on the ropes. Maq then went for PERMANENT DAYLIGHT, but when they got to the corner, Maq was met with The Mad Bomber!! After the flash of light, Maq was down and Caldwell was picking him up!!! The ref chased off The Bomber, but the damage was done, Caldwell place Maq on the top rope and hit .. . . SHOCK AND HORROR!!! For the 1-2-3! ((((( RESULT - Caldwell via pinfall ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: That brought out John Tyler who had some shocking news for the VWF faithful. A legal challenge to the tag team champions, Team Brittania had been filed with the VWF. It claimed that since Team Brittania were not a 'named' team in the title match on St Patricks Day they could not win the titles. There was no contract, other than the verbal agreement of the participants. There was also the mention of a work Visa issue with the team from England. With all the fans thinking Tyler would give the titles back to McQueen and Valenkov, but instead Tyler said. "In a week or two this will not be my business so, let the new guy sort this out" And with that John Tyler walked out of the ring. As of right now, no one seems to know who the champs are, or if we even have champions. Well speaking of tag teams, The Rodeo Boys are the only 3 time VWF tag team champions and they were set to meet up with the Submission Champion and the Enforcer of the VWF. .. .. .. =========================== THE RODEO BOYS (vs) SIDIOUS THE CLOWN & KAGEDO ============================ .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Kenny Kidd led his team to the ring with the song Revolution Is My Name playing on the PA. Sidious started the match against Kenny Kidd and Kidd went right to work on the ankle and legs of the current Submission Champion. Sid broke free and worked over Kidd before tagging in Kagedo. Kags, came in and knocked Bones off the apron and then hit a HURRICANRANA on Kidd, then caught him with a ENZIGUIRI as Kidd got back to his feet. Kidd tagged in Bones and Bones had the same luck with Kagedo as did Kidd. Bones finally used a EYE GOUGE to get his bearings and manage to tag in Kidd. And The New Revolution went to work on the ankle of Kagedo. But Kags used another ENZIGUIRI to break free and tag in Sid. The clown was on fire driving Kidd into the corner, MONKEY FLIPPING him out and then coming off the second rope for a BRUTAL TORNADO DDT!!! But Kidd kicked out. Then as Sid went for a DROPKICK, Kenny Kidd caught the ankle and slapped on the ELKO LASSO!!!! Kagedo came in to make the save but was tackled by Billy Bones!!! Kags and Bones rolled out of the ring, Kagedo broke free but was a moment to late as Sidious TAPPED just as Kagedo rolled into the ring. ((((( RESULT - The Rodeo Boys via submission ))))) After the match, Kenny Kidd called for the mic. Kidd said he was the "greatest submission artist in the history of the VWF, and the master of the ankle lock" and since he made the Submission Champion tap out he should take the place of one of the three challengers in the six man tag team match at The Gauntlet. John Tyler came out and he agreed! Tyler added Kidd to the challengers team and Fast Eddie Cutlass to the team of Gods Tears, Sidious The Clown and Adam Jennings!!! The match will now be an eight man elimination match! On St Patricks Day The Mad Bomber was scheduled to meet Adam Jennings for the 24 Hour Title, but Jennings brought a bomb sniffing dog to the ring which had The Bomber so scared the match never got underway. This time it would be just as bad. .. .. .. =========================== 24 Hour Title Match ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS © (vs) THE MAD BOMBER =========================== .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: Jennings came to the ring with his bomb sniffing dog, then the Mad Bomber came out wearing one of those suits they wear to train dogs with. With the crowd laughing, The Mad Bomber got in the ring, Jennings tied the dog in the corner and the bell rang. The Mad Bomber tried to make a run, but fell and could not get back up, due to the bulky suit. Jennings and the crowd laughed, then Jennings put a foot on him and the ref counted, 1-2-3! ((((( RESULT - Adam "The Hype' Jennings via pinfall ))))) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: So the next show will be The Gauntlet, the one night any man can walk out as VWF Champion. Can The Equalizer survive what might be the longest night of his life? At least he gets to come to the ring late in the battle. See you for The Gauntlet! © 2005 Vegas Wrestling Federation
