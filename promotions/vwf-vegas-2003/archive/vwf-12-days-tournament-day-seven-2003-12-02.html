Tag Team Group --------------- \\\\\ FALLEN ANGELS (vs) THE RHOMBUS ///// ------------------------------------------ DTMA: The Angels have been tough... but Sonic and CAPSMAN are always a big task for any tag team. WEST: Sonic and Tony Cage started this one and exchanged holds and some ariel moves until Cage moved out of the way of a FLYING FOREARM then hit a HURRICANRANA!!! DMTA: Cage irish whipped Sonic into the corner and nailed Sonic with a SPINNING SPINEBUSTER and got a 2 count! WEST: Cage tagged in Insomniac and the big man came in and locked Sonicman in a FULL NELSON! DMTA: Insomniac looked to wear down Sonicman.... CAPS got the fans chanting and stomping their feet but Sonic was in to much trouble... WEST: So CAPS took matters into his own hands and got in the ring... Insomniac broke the hold in order to POWERSLAM CAPS, but Sonic was free.. DTMA: CAPS rolled back to the ring apron and Sonic came off the ropes and jumped on the back of the big man... and put a SLEEPER on Insomniac. WEST: Ironic... DMTA: Insomniac SNAP MARED Sonic.... and then hit him with a LEGDROP! WEST: But the count was only 2! DMTA: Insomniac tagged in Cage.... who came off the ropes for a CROSS BODY but Sonic moved.. WEST: More like fell over. DTMA: Sonic rolled over and tagged in CAPS! WEST: CAPS hit a SUPERKICK on Cage... then knocked Insomniac off the ropes. DMTA: CAPSMAN then hit the CAPS FACTOR on Tony Cage.. and got the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNERS = The Rhombus !!!!! .. .. .. \\\\\ DESOLATION ROW (vs) THE LEGENDS OF THE VWF ///// ------------------------------------------------------ DTMA: Desolation Row hit the ring and this turned quickly into a brawl with all four men! WEST: After Hunter took Sebastian out of the ring to beat him up, it settled with Gangsta and Demola going at one another. DMTA: Hunter dominated Sebastian, and tagged in Alex Demola who added to the punishment, hitting a FLYING SOMERSAULT NECKBREAKER!!! But the cover... ONE!!! TWO!!!! Only managed a two count as Sebastian got a shoulder up! WEST: The Row moved the Sexy One back to their corner and used the typical tag tactic, drawing Gangsta into the ring, then double teaming while the ref dealt with Gangsta. DMTA: Demola locked Sebastian in the ABDOMINAL STRETCH, the Axmaker distracted the ref allowing the multiple palm shots to the heart by Hunter... aka THE HEART ATTACK MACHINE! WEST: Hunter tagged in a few moments later but got a LOW BLOW from the ever sneaky Sebastian. DMTA: Sebastian rolled over and tagged in Gangsta, who set about to cleaning the clocks of Desolation Row! WEST: With all hell breaking loose, Sebastian held Hunter and Gangsta came off the ropes for the BUCKSHOT!!! ...... BUT HUNTER DUCKED! DTMA: Sebastian was sent through the ropes to the floor by his own partner! WEST: Desolation Row then got Gangsta in the piledriver position, Demola added the spike, or the Desolation Row... ...... THE ONLY SOUND THATS LEFT!!! DMTA: For the .. ONE!!! TWO!!!!! THREEEE!!!!! WEST: After the match, Sebastian and Gangsta argued all the way up the ramp into the back! DMTA: But the Row scored the win! !!!!! WEST: But they are here. DMTA: GOLD started the match with Billy Bones, and Bones POKED JLG in the eyes to start the match, shocking the crowd and partner Kenny Kidd. WEST: Bones followed up with the RUNNING HIGH KNEE LIFT!! DMTA: He tagged in Kidd who put JLG in a SIDE HEADLOCK and then took him down to the mat.... JLG rolled him over but Kidd kicked back. WEST: That happened three or four times before JLG got to his feet and sent Kidd to the ropes... DMTA: Kidd hit a SHOULDERBLOCK, then went back to the ropes and got a face full of DROPKICK! WEST: GOLD then hit a SPRINGBOARD DDT!!! DMTA: Huge move! And GOLD ran over and slapped Loco, tagging him in the match. WEST: El Gringo Loco shot off the ropes and hit Kidd with a SPEAR!! DMTA: Kidd was stumbling around... and Loco slapped on the IRON CLAW!!!!! WEST: The old school move has done well for Loco.... Kenny Kidd was soon down on his knees. DMTA: Kidd was starting to look bad when Bones came in the ring and broke up the hold! WEST: JLG came in the ring and he and Bones were soon exchanging blows! DTMA: El Gringo Loco got back to his feet... and waited on Kidd... WEST: And Loco hit Kidd with the EL MILAGRO MEXICANO!!!!!!!! DTMA: Loco made the cover.... WEST: But the ref was breaking up Bones and JLG! DMTA: Loco jumped to his feet and began to yell and scream at JLG! WEST: Jushin Liger GOLD was back on the apron when Loco tagged him back in the match! DMTA: JLG got in the ring and the screaming match continued..... WEST: Meanwhile Kenny Kidd was on his feet..... DTMA: He tagged in Billy Bones! Bones hit JLG in the back of the head and then GOLD and Loco cracked their heads together! WEST: Bones caught JLG in a SCHOOLBOY and got a handfull of trunks to get the 1-2-3! !!!!! WINNERS = The Rodeo Boys !!!!! DTMA: Here are the Tag Team Standings, keeping in mind their first round records have moved through to this round. 1) Desolation Row (3-1) 1) The Rhombus (3-1) 6) JLG & Loco (2-2) 6) The Fallen Angels (2-2) 6) The Legends of the VWF (2-2) 6) The Rodeo Boys (2-2) .. .. .. .. .. Second Round Group 1 --------------------- \\\\\ PURE RAGE JOHNNY FREIL (vs) ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS ///// ----------------------------------------------------------------------- DTMA: These two have been tough to figure, each has looked really good in some matches.... and not so well in others. WEST: Andrews got the upper hand early in this match up... using his technical knowledge to keep Freil on the mat. DMTA: At the four minute mark Freil reversed an irish whip and sent Andrews hard into the corner.... and followed it up by the FISTS OF RAGE! WEST: Andrews doubled over but grabbed Freil and sent him over the ropes via a BACK BODY DROP! DMTA: Andrews followed Freil to the outside...... and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX!!! WEST: Andrews then seemed to lose his mind! He set up a table.... and then sent Freil through the table with another GERMAN SUPLEX! DTMA: But both men were counted out! WEST: For some reason EA wanted to hurt Freil. !!!!! WEST: Demola then placed Frost in a HAMMERLOCK, which he turned into a JAWBREAKER!!!! DMTA: Nice move, that really put some hurt on Frost! Demola then set up Frost and got him with the CROWNING GLORY!!!!! He made the cover... ONE!!!! TWO!!!!! And Frost kicked out! WEST: Demola hit a sudden DIAMOND DUST! And was up on the second rope. DTMA: But missed the SOMERSAULT LEGDROP! WEST: Frost rolled out of the way... and pulled himself up in the corner! DTMA: Demola was getting to his feet when Frost hit him with a RUNNING KNEE LIFT! WEST: Frost grabbed Demola and got him to his feet... and hit him with a THROAT PUNCH! DMTA: The Bad Seed was sucking for air..... and Frost kicked him in the midsection... and drove him into the mat with a PILEDRIVER!!! WEST: Violent Victor sent Demola to the ropes.. .and SPEARED him! DTMA: Frost then placed Demola p on the ropes......... ...... and went for the VIOLATOR MARK II!!!!!!!!! ...... but Demola flipped over, landed in the ring, and DDT'd Frost off the second rope!!!!! TWO!!!! But Devin Demola got a shoulder up! DMTA: Frost then hit GRAPHIC VIOLENCE 2002!!!! The three elbow strikes followed by the ROARING ELBOW!!! WEST: Frost got Demola back on the ropes.... ...... AND HIT THE VIOLATOR MARK II!!!!!! DTMA: Frost made the cover... ONE!!! TWO!!!!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!!! WEST: And Devin Demola has lost in the 12 Days Tournament! DTMA: Demola did not lose last year, Frost hands Demola his first ever 12 Days loss.... WEST: Demola is far from out of this! !!!!! WINNER = Violent Victor Frost !!!!! Second Round Group 2 --------------------- \\\\\ DANIEL SINZ (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA ///// --------------------------------------------- DMTA: This is a great group... its all bad blood! WEST: Sinz cost Maq the VWF heavyweight title way back in May.... DMTA: Maq didn't look like he wanted vengence, he looked like he wanted a win. WEST: Maq used his speed to stay a step ahead of Sinz... and Sinz is just the guy to stay ahead of... he can slap you in a submission hold in a flash. DMTA: Maq DROPKICKED, FLYING EVERYTHING'd Sinz until about the four minute mark. WEST: Maq came off the ropes and Sinz caught him... and hit a FALLAWAY SLAM! DMTA: Sinz then grabbed Maq and hit a DRAGON SUPLEX!!!! WEST: Daniel Sinz was suddenly a house on fire.... he hit a SERIES OF THREE! THREE! PILEDRIVERS on a now dream street Maquilladora. DMTA: Sinz sent Maq to the ropes and ... ......SLAPPED ON THE PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE!!!! WEST: Maq started flailing around looking for an escape .... Maq ran to the ropes, ran up the ropes and over and landed on the floor, causing Sinz to the break the hold! DMTA: Maq grabbed Sinz by the ankles and pulled him to the outside with him.. WEST: And The Maquilladora hit Sinz with the FEET OF FRENZY!!!! DTMA: Nice name... WEST: Thanks, just made that one up.... for Maq's series of kicks... DMTA: I know... WEST: Maq then hit a DOUBLE UNDERHOOK POWERBOMB on Sinz on the floor and Maq rolled back in the ring! DMTA: Sinz got back in the ring just before he was to be counted out. WEST: But Maq grabbed Sinz.... ......SINZ REVERSED AND MAQ WAS IN THE PINNACLE OF EXCELLENCE!!!! DMTA: The Maquilladora held on for as long as he could, but soon tapped out! WEST: Sinz then walked around the ring like some kind of monster! DMTA: No feelings..... !!!!! WEST: CJB is always confident, even in his past matches with Randall Crane! DTMA: Lark and CJB locked it up but CJB kept pushing Fenriz off and then strutting around the ring, or rolling to the outside... WEST: Clearly CJB was looking to get under the skin of his challenger. DTMA: Fenriz kicked CJB in the midsection instead of locking up... and thenhit CJB with an OVER THE SHOULDER POWERBOMB!! WEST: A quick cover only yielded a one count... but Fenriz followed it up STOMPING A HOLE in CJB!!! WEST: With Lark hurting, Classy Joey Boy began an assault on the legs of Fenriz, of course looking to set up the Sugar Blaster! DTMA: But Fenriz got a SMALL PACKAGE as CJB was going for the move..
