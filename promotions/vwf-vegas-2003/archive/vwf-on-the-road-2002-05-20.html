VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

On The Road, 20 May 2002

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The VWF Mondays +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ |O|n| |T|h|e| |R|o|a|d| +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ From Elko Community Center, Elko Nevada. -------------------------- The stars of the VWF were out for a House Show up north in Elko. DANE KLINGEL by pinfall over MARCEL KLUTZ ------------------------------- In the opening match two stars of the Vegas Academy went at it as the 6-6, 290 pound big man DANE KLINGEL pinned MARCEL KLUTZ with a solid powerbomb after Klutz tripped getting into the ring. REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE by submission over ROB KIDANE ------------------------------------------------------- ROB KIDANE could not have asked for a bigger match for his very first one, the VWE star stood face to face with REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE, but not for long. Crane ripped the youngster apart and taught him alot about the sport but in the end it was the Revolutions End 2002! TYSON YOUNG by pinfall over QUINTON OHME --------------------------------------------- Quinton Ohme seems to want to be a high flyer and tried his best against the up and coming Young. But T-Bone has been around to long and he quickly grounded Ohme and hit the The Street for the pin. SEBASTIAN by submission over ALI COLLYMORE ------------------------------------------- The Sexy One spent alot of time working on the back of ALI COLLYMORE before finally locking him in the Sex You Out Boston Crab. After the win Sebastian mocked the small crowd in Elko before CJB walked out and Sebastian took off out of the gym and was not seen again. G-GANGSTA by DQ over ERIK ANDREWS (24 Hour Title Match) -------------------------------------------------------- The new 24 Hour Champ put his title on the line much to the delight of the fans. Andrews looked good often in this battle but didn't have enough to take the title from Gangsta. But as G-Gangsta got to the top rope to finish it off with a Gangsta Smash, Thomas Rainka came out and pushed off Gangsta giving him the win by DQ. Gangsta then took the fight to both men. Soon it was only G-Gangsta and U-Trop in the ring and the fans loved it as the lights hit Gangsta shirt which read "He Is Coming", whoever He is? Now lets got to this weeks WAR OF WORDS in the VWF!!!! This week G-GANGSTA (on the 24 Hour Title, Strife, Crane and Sebastian) SEBASTIAN (on the 24 Hour Title, Gangsta and CJB) TYSON YOUNG (on a possible shot at the BlackJack Title) G-GANGSTA (Gangstaz Paradise by Coolio hits as the new 24 Hour Champion walks out to a huge face pop. He is wearing snap on black pants with a white line up the middle of the legs and a G-Force t -shirt. He has the 24 Hour Championship around his waist as he grabs a mic.] G-Gangsta: Last Thursday on Thunder, I won the 24 Hour Championship for my second time. After viewing the footage I am not particularly proud of how it was won. But I will not complain because I am champ. My first act as champion, is to send a challenge. Zell Strife and Randal Crane. Since the way I won this title wasn't really winning at all, and since I promised Randal Crane a re-match...how do this sound? G-Gangsta vs Zell Strife vs Randal Crane for the 24 Hour Championship in a Rage In A Cage matchup at Paint The Town Red pay-per view? No interferences...no distractions...just three of the best going at each other for the biggest prize in the game inside a 20 foot cage lined with barbedwire...no excuses...elimination style...which means I DO HAVE TO BE PINNED TO LOSE! The challenge has been sent. Now Sebastien don't think I have forgotten about your dumba$$! (Huge pop) G-Gangsta: Look I want a match with you, but since I have more important things to deal with you will have to wait, because you are worthless and not worth a REAL CHAMPIONS TIME! But you will get a match Sebastien. Rob Beauty....Rob Rob Rob...WHO THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE!?!? I DON'T CARE IF YOUR A PRESIDENT OR COMISIONIER OR THE QUEEN OF ENGLAND, CLEOPATRA, JULIUS CAESAR...OR KING HENRY THE 14th...DON'T STICK YOUR NOSE IN MY BUSINESS! I DIDN'T ASK YOU TO GET IN MY MATCH...NO ONE KNOWS WHETHER I WOULD HAVE KICKED OUT...I AM A STANDARD SETTER....I DON'T NEED YOUR HELP...NEXT TIME YOU GET INVOLVED IN MY MATCH I WILL PERSONALLY TAKE YOU OUT! (HUGE POP) HE IS COMING...THE COUNTDOWN CONTINUES! PEACE! [G-Gangsta's music hits as he walks out to a huge pop] -------- SEBASTIAN (The Sexy One is standing in front of a mirror, pretending to be wearing the....HIS....24 Hour Title belt) SEBASTIAN: G-Gangsta and Classy Joey Boy...it takes 2 men to take on Sebastian. Sebastian has proved that Sebastian is the greatest wrestler in the VWF, no..the world. And this Thursday Sebastian will not only win the match but pin...PIN G-Gangsta! And then from there it goes to Paint The Town Red and the greatest match in the history of the VWF. Two time 24 Hour Champion G-Gangsta will lose the title to SEBASTIAN!!! And Sebastian will walk out of the ring as THREE TIME 24 HOUR CHAMPION! And Sebastian will add the name of G-Gangsta to men that have left the VWF because of the greastness of Sebastian. Fuller, Powell, Spade and G-Gangsta. Enjoy the title G-Gangsta for it is not long for your pathetic waist. You cannot hold it...and why? You are NOT SEBASTIAN! (fade) ---------- TYSON "T-BONE" YOUNG (The T-Bone is sitting as always at the NorthTown Bar in North Las Vegas) Erik Andrews is a former T3 Champion. Andrews almost left the last pay per view with the 24 Hour Title. Andrews is a household name here in Vegas. And last week he walked down THE STREET! And now who is he? A nobody. But I am looking ahead or should I say up. I have enjoyed the fans and I hope that this does not hurt my standing with them but it is time for me to get a shot at a title. And I think the right title for this waist is the BlackJack Title, hardcore just like me. Don't get me wrong, I like Classy Joey Boy and I hope he and G-Gangsta take it out on Sebastian. But after that, I would like to take my shot at his title. So Classy....what do you say little man? It has been a long reign for you but can you talk the talk, can you walk the walk down (stands) THE STREET?? (fade)
