Warrior Wednesday, 22 August 2001

22 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARRIOR WEDNESDAY August 22, 2001 From the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, NV (Opens in the office of Rob Beauty, VWF President. Standing in front of him is announcer Dan The Man Aller)
ROB: Dan... DA: That is Dan "The Man". ROB: Whatever, I would like you to meet someone. (A woman walks in, very sexy with black hair) DA: Oh yeah! ROB: This is Lean Darlene, the interviewing machine. DA: Huh? ROB: She is my new hire here in the VWF. She will be doing backstage interviews, that kind of stuff.
DA: What about me? ROB: You just do the matches. DA: Ok. (A misty fog approaches two men on a stoney path. One a camera man and the other an interviewer. The fog gets so thick it chokes the men...The go down to the ground and grasp the gravel in between their hands. The two men wake up and look around, the one man turns on the camera and pans around the room. Everything looks still but the camera man is dizzy.
The interviewer notices a shadow in what it seems to be a cave like structure.....The shadow jumps from wall to wall.....The two men frightend, stand still.)
Zell) Who dares follow the one that has been actually trying to follow you....I am the one who is supposed to reap the living and take there lives to an extinct height.
Man) I, I, I, I'm your interviewer.....my name is greg and this is lyle my camera man. Zell) Names are not important, only your smell....I'll recognize you both by your odor, so I know what I'm tracking down next time. What do you require of me?
Greg) Well to be honest, I would love to interview you, Nobody has heard anything from you in weeks.... you, uh well I would love to hear what you think about your triple threat match up.
Zell) With the drool that hangs from my mouth I shall tell you... Greg) Drool? Zell) I get very hungry when I think of all the blood that will be spilled on that very mat.... I do hope some of mine as well.
Greg) Why yours? Zell) So they can see how f*cking tainted it is and how grotesque it smells.... Ahhh the time has come to finally step out and devour my enemies, finally time to destroy the ones who believe the time for glory is to be alive. I say the time for glory is when the mark of the beast rips off from my skin and runs through eveyone of you to finally crown my king of this fed....
greg) Please explain what you mean Zell. Zell) Watch your tongue you febile minded feces.... What I mean is this fire inside which I hold will be unleashed and will wreck havoc on all that step in it's way. When I"m in the ring, I'm one mean motherf*cker,
I should actually say I'm your worst nightmare. Shitting your pants won't stop me from giving you a severe beating as well...
Greg) Uhhh, right. Zell) What's the matter, you don't think I can do it? Greg) NO it's not that, I just I Zell) You want more don't you... Well Jack, Huey, I consider you two as trembling folk of the norm. You are all the same, You come to the ring with all this rage in your eyes, but as soon as I get into that ring, Your rage is gone, and only a nice shade of blue replaces the red....
Your heart sinks and your fear for your lives shows up with your perspiration. I come down to be the slayer at every match and If I do not accomplish my goal, then I'm screwed out of it... Just like that bitch whore Tai or whatever the f*ck...... She thinks that spitting fire into my eyes and making a name for herself is funny and witty, but rest assure her time will come to face the man in the coffin... the one who beholds the crazed chamber of terror... an evening there would fill her full of visions beyond belief....
Greg) Anymore for Jack and Huey? Zell) There comes a time for everyone when you step into the ultamite mistake.... I'm sorry gentlemen this has to be the perfect timing...
I'm coming off an enragement of sorts that for one, you bastards have not heard my voice... but now for the first time I can speak my mind.
Secondly I am enraged that you low my name.... Have you not heard of the Anarchist, the Fire Inside, The Haunting? Well to be honest, I'm glad you haven't.... for what I have instore for you punks... is something unexpecting the mind.. something that will make your flesh want to leave your bones... something that will suck your eyes forever into the back of your skull.... and the pain and terror will never be over....
never! No leave my slumber.... I will awake tomorrow! (The two men leave and find the original path where they had fallen from.... a harsh laugh comes from a cave off the road... the fog depleats and the men carry on their journey.)
******************** (Viva Las Vegas begins to play and the camera pans around the MGM
Arena. It comes to a stop on Dan "The Man" Aller standing in the middle of the ring)
DA: Hello Las Vegas! And welcome to another edition of Warrior Wednesday! Tonight we have the qualifing matches for the Triple Threat
Title as well as a grudge match between Kerry Powell and ....someone?
Lets ROCK! # Viva Las Vegas # (A quick shot of Sebastian holding the 24 Hour Title) # Viva Las Vegas # (Kerry Powell getting jumped by the Masked Man) # Viva # (A shot of Tai Angela spitting fire onto Zell Strife) # Viva # (Jack Spade is shown in interview) # Las Vegas# (Dan has taken his place at the table) DA: This is going to be a big night! We will find out the 3 finalists for the Triple Threat Title as well as hopefully who is under the mask of the man who has been tormenting Kerry Powell.
(I'm Too Sexy begins to play) DA: Wait! That is the music of our 24 Hour Champ, Sebastian! (Sebastian steps to the top of the ramp, the crowd boos) SEBASTIAN: Sebastian is not happy! Sebastian does have THE belt,
Sebastian is THE best man in the VWF but Sebastian is not happy!
Tonight Sebastian makes an impact like never before! (Sebastian stands for a moment and lets the boos of the crowd wash over him before turning and walking away)
DA: He isn't even scheduled, what is his problem? He is the champ, what more does he want? That is a 24 hour title, any VWF star here tonight could just take it from him. The guy just isn't thinking.
Let's get things going here tonight. The following Triple Threat match is for a spot in the Triple Threat Title match next week here on
Warrior Wednesday!
***** G-Gangsta (vs) Dirk Western (vs) Anarchy X ***** (I Wanna Destroy You by the Circle Jerks starts to play and Anarchy X begins his slow walk to the ring. He is wearing a ripped up shirt and shorts and has orange and blue hair)
DA: Ladies and gents, making his way to the ring. From right here in
Las Vegas, ANARCHY X!!!!
Why this guy is in the tournament is beyond me? But there he is. (My Projects by Coo Coo Cal hits, he walks out with his manager/body gaudr U-Trop, wearing a cleanch jeans suit. He gets in the ring takes off the jacket to the suit and wrestles in the baggy shiny black jeans)
DA: This is G-Gangsta and he is a tough looking guy. That cornrowed hair and goatee might just scare Anarchy X right out of the ring.
(Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DA: In this corner, from Cell Block D. Weighing in at 260 pounds, six feet five inches DIRK "D-DAY" WESTERN!!!!!!!!!! DA: Here we go! Three men, two get eliminated and one moves on! DA: They are circling each other, and Western and Gangsta are exchanging words. X would be best to let these two fight it out.
Anarchy X is getting out of the ring, and taking a seat at ringside. Western and Gangsta lock up collar and elbow. Arm drag and Gangsta has Western down. Both men on their feet, and an arm drag by Western. Pretty impressive for a guy who doesn't know how to wrestle.
Gangsta stands up and rakes the eyes of Western. To the ropes, Western ducks, spins around and catches Gangsta with a solid right to the jaw!
Western throws Gangsta to the corner, follows him in and knee to the gut. Western looks out to X sitting at ringside and motions for him to come in the ring.
Western is holding Gangsta and SUPERKICK by Anarchy X! That is the first time X has had a good move here in the VWF.
Western with an elbow drop and X with an elbow drop! The double team begins. They pick Gangsta up and DOUBLE SUPLEX! Anarchy X is smiling at Western. D-DAY! Western just hit the D-Day on Anarchy X. 1....2....3! Anarchy X is OUT! And Western is in complete control. G-Gansta is on his feet and Western with a DDT! G-Ganstas manager
U-Trop is wild outside of the ring but Western is in control. Western goes off the ropes, shoulder block. But the G-man is still on his feet.
Western goes again and BUCKSHOT! That was the G-Gansta superkick! U-Trop is jumping up and down as his man has an advantage here. G-Gansta with a spinning back hand punch and Western goes back down! Gansta getting up on the ropes! What a LEGDROP! 1...2....KICKOUT by Western! G-Gangsta is showing some good stuff here tonight! Off the ropes, Western ducks the clothesline, DROPKICK by Gangsta! And he jumps on top of Western and is just pounding him!
Gangsta pulls Western up, the crowd is starting to chant G-Man G-Man! THE BODY PRESS!!!! U-Trop is yelling at me...oohh..THE GHETTO BODY PRESS!!! G-Gangsta is up on the top rope! GANGSTA SMASH!!!! 1....2....3!!!! G-Gangsta is in the finals! What a win! What a comeback! #####VWF BREAK##### DA: That was a surprise! Gangsta gets a big win! But there is no time for rest here in the VWF! ***** Bobby Silas (vs) Tai Angela (vs) Faith ***** DA: Who knows what is going down in this one? Silas looked good against Powell last week, Faith is new and Angela is a FREAK!
Here we go! ( The lights dim down and Nirvana, smells like teen spirit hits the PA system, Faith walks out with a candle in his hands, he circles the ring and then role's under the bottom rope and starts running against each rope)
DA: Weighing in at 210 pounds, 5 feet 7 inches tall this is Faith! (Players Are Gonna Play hits and Bobby Silas begins to strut his way to the ring. He is wearing long purple satin pants and no shirt which shows off his sculpted body. He stands on the ring apron and calls for the mic)
BOBBY: It is time for the silence and in the silence we find "THE TRUTH!!!" DA: That is "The Silence" Bobby Silas, coming in at 6-6, 274 pounds DA: There go the lights! (Black Sabbath begins to play and the arena lights slowly dim. A procession of young women all dressed in black ropes, each carrying a candle begin the walk to the ring. Tai Angela follows them, inside the ring he stands in the center and the women form a circle around him, they blow out the candles, fall to their knees in front of him. With a wave he banishes them and walks to his corner)
DA: Silas and Faith get out of the ring! They want nothing to do with
Angela! Nobody in the VWF does! Silas and Faith get back in slowly.
The lights come back up and Angela rushes at big Bobby! Backs him into the corner and delivers some earth shattering elbows. Faith comes up from behind. What is that little guy gonna do against The Dark
Monster? KICK to the back of the knee and Angela falls like a condemned house! Whoa! Faith continues with the hard kicks to the fallen Angela. Silas shakes it off and nails Faith with a CLOTHESLINE!
Good job Bobby, somebody finally had Angela down! Faith with a spinning heel kick and Silas hits the mat.
Tai Angela is so fast! He just SPEARED Faith! 1...2...ALMOST! He almost had the new guy! Angela is setting Faith up. POWERBOMB! Silas is up and he rushes Angela! BACK BODY DROP over the top rope! Silas is out of the ring! Angela throws Faith to the ropes.
HEART PUNCH! Faith is shaking on the mat! He needs medical attention! But Angela isn't going for the cover, he is out of the ring!
He is setting up a table on the outside! POWERBOMBED Silas through the table! The ref is out of the ring. Angela has Silas and PILEDRIVER! This Dark Freak is going crazy! The ref is pointing to the ring but I don't know if this mental patient can even hear him.
Angela rolls Silas back into the ring. The ref is calling for the bell? What is going on? (The ref calls for a ringside mic. Angela is outside the ring, Silas and Faith are inside both on the mat)
REF: Tai Angela is eliminated by COUNT OUT! (The crowd goes crazy cheering) DA: A team of security is out here to help Angela backstage and he is going without incident. Inside the ring is the rubble of Angela.
Silas and Faith are both on the mat! The ref is counting....what if neither one answers the count? Neither one does and the ref calls for the bell and ringisde medics. (The medics come in and give aid to both Silas and Faith) DA: Now what? G-Gangsta is in but who goes from this match? We gotta take a break! #####VWF BREAK##### (VWF President Rob Beauty steps onto the ramp and walks toward the ring as Viva Las Vegas plays. Rob grabs a mic and steps into the ring)
ROB: I was watching the last match and I am disgusted! Angela continues to upset my federation and I will not have an out of control federation!
So Angela you are OUT! DA: Whoa! ROB: Of the Triple Threat tournament! DA: Damn! ROB: Bobby Silas and Faith! If you are up to it you will meet one on one next week, winner to get that spot in the Triple Threat Final! If you can handle two matches in one night!
(Rob leaves the ring) DA: What an offer. Silas and Faith get another chance, next week. Should be good. So G-Gangsta will meet up with any of five men, but we should get rid of two of them right now!

Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans)
DA: From Easy Bay, 250 and 6 feet 4 inches this is ZELL STRIFE!!!! I love this guy, tough. Ohh sh*t! (Black Sabbath begins to play and a single woman in a robe walks toward the ring) DA: Angela! The FREAK is back at it! Strife is looking at the woman who is just standing there. Strife is looking around, where is Angela?
(The lights flicker a red colour and the opening riff of Ace of Spades by Motorhead begins to play. The crowd let out a good cheer as "The Ace" Jack Spade swaggers out onto the ramp with an arrogant smile on his face.) DA: On his way to the ring, walking past the freak chick from right here in Las Vegas, THE ACE!!!!! JACK SPADE!!!!
(The woman motions for Strife to follow her and she begins to walk back up the ramp) DA: Don't do it! Don't follow her! (Strife is looking to the crowd who is a mix of opinions) (Peace Train plays and Huey Huxtable starts to walk toward the ring, his father seems very serious but Huey is smiling and waving to everyone with an idiots smile)

NECKBREAKER! But he is still looking at the girl.
(She takes out a poster of Strife and slowly lets the flame from her candle light the poster)
DA: Strife is off up the ramp! 1...2..KICKOUT! Huxtable goes for the pin on Spade but can't get it. Huxtable offers his hand to Spade but Spade is like the crowd watching
Strife slowly approach the girl.
(She laughs in a wicked way then there is a BRILLIANT FLASH OF LIGHT!) DA: HOOOOLLLLYY MOOOOOLLLLYYY! What just happened? The girl is gone, so is Strife. The ref is counting.
I think Strife has been counted out. Something is rising from under the ramp! Oh my GOD!! Zell Strife is in a small cage that is rising above the ramp! Strife is fighting to get out and the cage is swinging around! Back in the ring Spade has just jumped on Huxtable and is using a sleeper like hold on him!

1...2...NO! Huey kicks out! His father is screaming like a banshee outside of the ring! Henry gets up on the apron! Spade rushes him and rings his bell! I think he made the big guy mad! Huey is up and he taps Spade on the shoulder, who turns and CHOKESLAM!!!!
Huxtable is kicking at the fallen Spade like a crazy little kid, I think he is gonna cry! (Henry looks at Dan The Man and winks) DA: Henry knows how to get his son mad. Huey has a fist full of hair and knee lift! Not bad for a big guy. Jack Spade talked about being a rip saw through the VWF, I think Huey is unplugging his saw!
Huey is setting Spade up for a ......I think it is a suple....no....wait....Huey pushes Spade into the ropes and SIDEWALK
SLAM! A little indecision by Huey?
(Quick cut to Zell Strife still struggling in the cage) DA: 1...2....THAT WAS CLOSE for the ACE! Huey just tossed Spade out of the ring and he is sitting down in the middle of the ring. Henry is up on the apron yelling and Huey is just shaking his head.
