(It's a chilly night outside the Rancho High School Gym. A station wagon slowly pulls up. Towards Lean Darlene Orion. The vehicle has smoke pouring out of the hood and is making weird noises. CJB: You know something Lean Darlene Orion? I can't believe the VWF is wrestling at this High School Gym. I mean, I'm the Blackjack champion! I shoul dbe in an arena. (CJB thinks a little) CJB: Well Lean Darlene Orion, let's break this down a little. There's The Legend, the word legend describes someone who has done great things. And as we all know The Legend, just down right sucks. He is obviously not going to win. Then there's G-Gangsta. A tough SOB. Might have some trouble tonight, especially if his pants are down around his ankles. Then, there's Thomas Rainka. He's been around the block in the VWF. He's been the T3 Champion, but has never fought against someone of my skills. Lastly, there's Sebastian. Talk about a whinner, the man can't shut up about the 24 Hour Title. He says the Blackjack title isn't good enough. I think he is just scared of me. I called him out when debuted in the VWF and he ran. I called him out a few weeks ago and instead I got Tia Angela. You see, the Blackjack Title is a major title. Sebastian just doesn't want to face me.....CJB. So, as the VWF Team Captain, if I had to choose one of these men, I'd picked Sebastian. That way, there will be nowhere to run, nowhere to hide, and no one, like Tia Angela, to stand in my way. But no matter who wins, CJB will be more than happy to show them why I am the VWF Team Captain. If your picking up, what CJB is puttin' down!
