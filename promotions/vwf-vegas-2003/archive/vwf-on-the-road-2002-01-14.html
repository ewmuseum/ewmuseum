VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

On The Road, 14 January 2002

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The VWF Mondays +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ |O|n| |T|h|e| |R|o|a|d| +-+-+ +-+-+-+ +-+-+-+-+ at Virgin Valley High School in Mesquite Nevada DAN THE MAN ALLER: (inside the empty gym just after the card) Welcome to our Monday show, War of Words along with OTR! First lets hear from some of the VWF's biggest stars, then we will see highlights of tonights action! First up the Blackjack Champion! CLASSY JOEY BOY [The scene opens to a home video that has been recently shot. The moon's light bounces off the gravel of and old ghost town. It's a bit cold in the old deserted western town. Old wooden houses and town stores seme to be falling apart. The outline of a man can be seen standing in the middle of the dark, dirty road, which runs straight through the town. You can see that the man is wearing a cowboy hat, and an old cowboy long cowboy jacket. When he speaks, we learn the mystery cowboy is CJB.] CJB: This Thursday the "VWF Team Cpatain" defends his title against The Legend. I was not expecting this from the VWF....you see I figured I would be continuing my feud with Sebastian and Tia Angela. I mean The Legend is ok, I beat him easily in the 12 Days Tournament, and I will do the same this Thursday. [CJB walks into the moonlight that is bounces across an old build and onto the ground.] CJB: Thursday is almost a day off for me, but later this month at Ghost Town Blues I will have one of the best matches of my life, I must if I am going to win. You see I think of Tia Angela as a Northern White-Tailed Sasquatch..... [The camera man begins to walk toward CJB and is almost at close up range when he falls over. The camera rolls a bit and reveals Fatty Jay as the camera man. He is on the ground face down, and his cowboy hat has fallen off his head. He is quick to his feet and retrieves the camera. He adjusts the shot to show a close up of CJB.] Fatty: Sorry... CJB: It's ok. Fatty: It's just these damn chaps are giving me a huge wedgie! CJB: Just hold the camera still man......[going back into character]..Now, as I was saying, at GTB, Tia Angela, the dark minister.... Fatty: That's monster. CJB: Whatever......come GTB, be at your best, that way when I beat you, there will be no excuses...Thursday, I will announce the rules for our match...Angela, Sebastian........remember........CJB is Team Captain! ********************* DAN THE MAN ALLER: Now the upset, G-Gangsta! ********************* G-GANGSTA [Gangstaz Paradise hits and G-Gangsta walks out to a mixed reaction, he is wearing baggy black sweat pants, and white t shirt, and white shirt rapped(like a du rag) on his head with a black visor worn sideways over it and black and white Nikey Old Skools(shoes). He grabs a mic and speaks.] G-Gangsta: Look imma make this short and sweet. I have a challenge for Thursday Night Thunder. The Legend Of Extreme where are you at? Im sending out a challenge to you for Thursday. Im sick of tag matches...so Legend i challenge you to a last man standing match on Thursday Night Thunder. Main Event...since you can't get there yourself! I am going to go ALL OUT in this match, I will tear you from limb to limb to damn limb! And you will one again receive the Buckshot, followed by a Gangsta Smash followed but a Queens Crusha finally followed by a G FORCE! Which will leave you motionless....and maybe injure you like last time. You will be left bloody, injured and motionless...and all this for what? TO SEND A MESSAGE TO "THE ACE" JACK SPADE! NO MY CHOOSEN MATCH WILL NOT BE A LAST MAN STANDING BUT IT WILL BE SIMILAR TO IT! LEGEND OF EXTREME'S BODY WILL BE LEFT IN FRONT OF YOUR LOCKER ROOM DOOR ALL TO SEE YOUR FACE TURN BLUE AND YOUR EYES TURN BLOODSHOT RED...SPADE WELCOME TO THE SICK MIND OF THE GHETTO!!!!!! AT THE PPV THE GHETTOBIRD TAKES FLIGHT...SPADE YOU WILL BE MY RUNWAY! [He drops the mic and walks out.] ******************* DAN THE MAN ALLER: And now former two time 24 Hour Champion.... ******************* SEBASTIAN (Sitting in the locker room at the Thomas and Mack) SEBASTIAN: Once again it is Jack Spade and Sebastian! The Ace, the big fan favorite of the stupid, moronic fans of the VWF. Jack, Sebastian is THE Vegas Wrestling Federation. Sebastian has been around since the begining! Sebastian will be around long after you have been run off! (Sebastian takes a breath, and smiles) And Classy Joey Boy. Do you think just because they changed the name of that belt you have that Tai Angela is any less interested in killing you for it? Do you think it is a mistake you are meeting Sebastians personal friend The Legend this Thursday? Do you think Sebastian is not going to take care of you? First Spade, and then the kid. Sebastian is back! And you are not Sebastian! ******************* DAN THE MAN ALLER: And now, a rising star in the VWF..... ******************* XTREME TONY CAGE (Scene opens backstage with "Xtreme" Tony Cage in a back area. He's in a dark corner, all we can see is his face as he stares at the camera.) XTC: Last week, well, last week sucked. They didn't even let me speak my mind. How the hell do you know who I am? You Don't!! But I lit the house on fire anyway, even if I had to carry Justin Sane to a good match. It's just a shame that the ref counted us out. I guess a No countout rule is just too extreme for VWF. (Tony momentarily flashes the Hardcore sign before continuing) XTC: So, who is Xtreme Tony Cage you ask? Well, I'm the darkest, most hardcore wrestler in the industry today. I started as a commentator, and worked my way into the ring through blood, sweat and tears. Even at the old age of 25, I'm a veteran of this fine sport..I ... Camera: Uh, sir, it says on your bio your only 18, and this is your 3rd match... Tony :(In a whiny voice) Awe, c'mon man, you'll ruin my interview. Where was I? (serious voice) I am so extreme that the VWF has me hide in the corners where noone will dare walk... the darkness where people are afraid to even step.... (All of a sudden, a little girl walks into view. She holds a dolly, and looks at the camera, then sticks her tounge at it.) XTC: (Whining again) C'mon kid, get out of my shot. Little girly: You're ugly looking.... XTC: Why you little....? (As Tony goes for her the girl runs away) Girl: DADDDDDDYYYYYY!!!! XTC: That's better. Now onto my match. Tonite I face Anxiety Disorder. Well son, prepared to be schooled. I can honestly say I haven't a clue who you are, what you stand for, and frankly I could care less. You're just a stepping stone, an example. I will show you how to go all out, be ready for a beating. Anxiety Disorder, you're not a concern to me. Now, my match Thursday is what lurks on my mind. Maquilladora, you are a man I know,and know well. We wrestled in the now defunct XMEW, although our paths never met. I remember my last XMEW match, as Freil and I gladly held up the XMEW Tag Belts, and Bobby G. became cruiserweight champion. And then Maquilladora, I had the pleasure of watching you in the CWF, coming up short time and again the VWF's Blackjack Champion, Classy Joey Boy. Yes, I remember you losing to him COUNTLESS times, they were classic ass-beatings. Maqqy, as a bonus stip, the winner between me and you also gets a future shot at the T3 title. Maq, you know I go after gold like a fat man after a cheesesteak. You see Maq... (All of a sudden ringing is heard. Tony turns back, and answers his cell phone) XTC: Hey mommy....Yeah, I'm being good.... No, I'm not hurt.... You saw me? Good... Tell Daddy I miss him.... Yes, I got the band-aids you sent me.....You found Mr. Purple Pants?? Yeah, now I can sleep. ....Fed-Ex him to me, pleeease mommy.....please?? ... Thank you....I love you too....Bye bye mommy (XTC hangs up and returns to the cameraman.) XTC: You didn't tape that did you? Camera: (snickering) Oh no, of course not.... XTC: Good, back to the interview..... Maquilladora and AD, prepare for an ass kicking of your lifetimes... (The little girl returns with her father...A man beast about 300 lbs with a barrel chest. Our hero does what any Xtreme wrestler would do right now, he kicks him square in the jimmy and runs like hell as the scene fades.) DAN THE MAN ALLER: Now lets take a look at what happened tonight here in Mesquite! ************************************* XTREME TONY CAGE vs ANXIETY DISORDER ************************************* Tony Cage hit the ring to Just Got Wicked by Cold and to a pretty good reception from the fans in Mesquite. Anxeity Disorder seemed to be the favorite as he made his way to the ring. Cage nailed AD with a bodypress to get things going. Both men took this battle to the Xtreme by going in and out of the ring. Anxiety Disorder hit a big powerbomb onto the gym floor that rocked Cage. AD got Cage back in the ring and hit a Samoan drop but Cage managed to kick out. Cage fought back and took control when the two men went toe to toe. Tony Cage then hit a DDT, followed by his SUNSET BOMB for the win! WINNER- Xtreme Tony Cage (pinfall) Then came the Tag Team Title match! ************************************ TRIPLE X (C) vs BLAZE and REAL DEAL ************************************ This was a four way fight from the start! Blaze took on Earthquake and Real Deal was working over Chumpy. Finally Earthquake and Real Deal were in the ring together. Real Deal was getting a good reaction from the crowd. RD hit a BIG powerslam and tagged in Blaze who layed the boots to Earthquake! Blaze locked Earthquake in a DOUBLE UNDERHOOK DDT but Earthquake flipped Blaze over and made the tag to Chumpy. Chumpy nailed Blaze with a CLOTHESLINE then an ELBOW that sent Real Deal off the apron. Chumpy grabbed Blaze and hit the running powerslam the CHESSEBURGER! Chumpy tagged Earthquake and just as they were going to hit Blaze with a DOUBLE CHEESEBURGER, Real Deal grabbed Triple X's friend Pizza Boy! Earthquake stoppped the in ring move and headed out to save Pizza Boy. Blaze hit a low blow on Chumpy, then hit the UNCONTROLLABLE INFERNO! for the win! WINNNERS- and NEW VWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS Blaze and Real Deal Earthquake took a chair to the head of Real Deal and saved Pizza Boy but was hit by Blaze who had a chair of his own. With Earthquake down Blaze hit the UNCONTROLLABLE INFERNO on Pizza Boy before he and Real Deal left the ring! (Back to Dan The Man Aller at ringside) DAN THE MAN ALLER: Now lets take a look at this Thursdays lineup! First up, winner will get a shot at the T3 Title on January 24. THE MAQUILLADORA, goes against XTREME TONY CAGE who got a big win tonight! He is the man who has been challenged by the champ DAGE, he will get a match with crazy man ANXIETY DISORDER! Two former 24 Hour Champs got at it as SEBASTIAN takes on THE ACE JACK SPADE! Bring along something to wipe off the blood, this is a good old hatred between ZELL STRIFE and THOMAS RAINKA! They mix it up! The Blackjack Title is on the line as Champ CLASSY JOEY BOY goes against THE LEGEND! And in the Main Event, the RCW Title will be born under the VWF banner as G-GANGSTA meets up with DEMONIC, BLAZE and REAL DEAL in this Fatal Four Way! See you Thursday! VWF (c) 2002
