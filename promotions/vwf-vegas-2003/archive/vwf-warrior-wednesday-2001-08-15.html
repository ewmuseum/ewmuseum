VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

Warrior Wednesday, 15 August 2001

15 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARRIOR WEDNESDAY 08-15-01 # Viva Las Vegas # (A quick shot of Sebastian holding the 24 Hour Title) # Viva Las Vegas # (Kerry Powell scores the pin on Dirk Western) # Viva # (A shot of Tai Angela spitting fire onto Zell Strife) # Viva # (Paddy O'Shea walks into a bar) # Las Vegas# (Cut to last weeks Warrior Wednesday) HOOOLLLLYY MOOOLLLLYY! Angela just spit fire into the face of Zell Strife who is rolling around on the canvas. Angela is walking away? What is wrong with the freak? Sebastian jumps on Strife. 1...2....3!!! Oh no! SEBASTIAN wins! He is the First 24 Hour CHAMPION! Sebastian has been given the belt. Strife is being attended to by
EMT's and Angela is just standing on the ramp.
(Cut to the half filled MGM Arena and Dan "The Man" Aller) DA: This is Dan "The Man" Aller here inside the MGM Grand Garden Hotel for another Warrior Wednesday in the VWF! Sebastian is the champion thanks to help from Tai Angela. Later tonight we are going to see Zell
Strife and Tai Angela in action one on one in the ring! Also, Kerry
Powell takes on newcomer Bobby Silas. But first lets go up to the ring.
*****Anarchy X (vs) Paddy O'Shea***** (Barroom Heroes hits and Paddy O'Shea starts his long stumble to the ring, but he looks better than usual)
DA: From Dublin Ireland.....The Master of the 1-2...Paddy O'Shea! DA: And his opponent.... (I Wanna Destroy You by the Circle Jerks starts to play and Anarchy X begins his slow walk to the ring. He is wearing a ripped up shirt and shorts and has orange and blue hair)
DA: Ladies and gents, making his way to the ring. From right here in
Las Vegas, ANARCHY X!!!!
DA: Both these guys are looking for that first win. They are sizing each other up. I am not sure what for, neither has shown much so far in the VWF.
Paddy O'Shea looks a little less drunk than usual though. O'Shea gets the upper hand and tosses X into the ropes, BACK BODY
DROP! O'Shea looks sharp. He has a hand full of hair and a he is using
X as a punching bag.
Wait! Someone is coming down the ramp. That is one well dressed guy, and he seems to be cheering for O'Shea.
O'Shea SUPLEX on X. Up and down with an elbow drop! O'Shea looks to the man outside who just mimed a cut throat. O'Shea is going for the One Two. DDT!!! LEGDROP!!! O'Shea nails it and it looks like 1.....2.....3! He has his first victory in VWF! O'Shea is calling for the mic! PADDY: Vegas! Finally I have done it and I owe it all to this man. (The well dressed guy gets in the ring) PADDY: This is my new manager, and sponsor to help me stop drinking. Mr. Smith. MR SMITH: Ladies and gentlemen, I will take Paddy O'Shea to the top! Sebastian, Triple Threat title, it doesn't matter. This loveable man will be a VWF champion!
(they leave) DA: Well, O'Shea has stopped drinking? We will see how long that lasts. *****KERRY POWELL (vs) BOBBY SILAS***** [The house lights dim down, and golden spotlights begin to sweep the entire arena. A hush falls over the crowd as "Money" by Pink
Floyd begins to play.]
Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, weighting in at 238 pounds, and standing at 6 feet, and one inch, this is...
24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! [The fans jump to their feet as Kerry Powell himself, makes his way to the ring.] DA: Powell has got to be wondering if Ric Beauty is here, or even it is him that has been torturing Powell.
Powells opponent for tonight..... (Players Are Gonna Play hits and Bobby Silas begins to strut his way to the ring. He is wearing long purple satin pants and no shirt which shows off his sculpted body. He stands on the ring apron and calls for the mic)
BOBBY: It is time for the silence and in the silence we find "THE TRUTH!!!" (Bobby steps over the top rope and into the ring) DA: He is getting a warm reception from the fans. But not from Powell. Powell is on him fast, backing him into the corner and a couple of kneelifts has the big man doubled over. Powell with a chop to the chest!
Kerry Powell is in control. Powell tries for a headbutt. That hurt Powell, Silas is just smiling at him. HEADBUTT!!!! by Silas and Powell rolls out of the ring. The fans don't like Powell to well but I don't think "24 Karat" kares much! Powell is back in, they hook up. Silas with an armdrag and Powell is hurting. (A few seconds of O Fortuna play) What the ..? Is Ric Beauty here? Silas breaks the hold and looks at the ref. I am sure he has no idea what is going on! Powell running LARIAT! and Silas is down!
The music has cut off, it is just someone playing with Powell! Powell is kicking Silas back down to the mat but keeping an eye toward the ramp. Powell picks Silas up, can he???
SUPLEX! Powell just suplexed the big man! That was great and Powell is up on the ropes yelling at the fans here at the MGM!
He knows that was a big move. He turns and sits on the top rope, waiting for Silas to get up. LEAP!
Silas catches him and POWERSLAM! HOOOLLLLY MOOOOLLLLY!!! Silas nailed him 1...2....thr ALMOST! Silas has Powell by the hair and is holding up his finger to his lips, it might be time ofr his big move, QUIET TIME.
Silas has both hands around the neck of Powell, up he goes..EYE GOUGE by Powell and Silas drops him.
KICK TO THE GROIN....Powell rolls Silas out of the ring! Here comes........somebody in a mask! The masked man gets in the ring and comes up behind Powell and taps him on the shoulder, Powell turns and WAMMOOO! Big right hand drops
Powell. The ref is outside with Silas.
The Masked Man throws Powell to the ropes DROP TOE HOLD to a quick
ANKLE LOCK....ELBOW TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD and he turns Powell and has locked on the FIGURE FOUR!
Is there any doubt who that is? Here comes President Rob Beauty! With a chair. The masked man is up and back down after a horrific CHAIR SHOT!
President Rob Beauty calls for the bell and then the mic... PRESIDENT ROB: I will not have this sh*t in my federation. I know who you are 24 hour man. If you want "24 Karat" you got him next week!
DA: The masked man is bleeding through the mask on the outside! He is coming over here......
MASKED MAN: I accept! DA: Hey, that didn't sound like Ric Beauty? *****ZELL STRIFE (vs) TAI ANGELA***** DA: I just got word the last contest was a No-contest. This next one is the battle of the freaks of the VWF!
(Black Sabbath begins to play and the arena lights slowly dim. A procession of young women all dressed in black ropes, each carrying a candle begin the walk to the ring. Tai Angela follows them, inside the ring he stands in the center and the women form a circle around him, they blow out the candles, fall to their knees in front of him. With a wave he banishes them and walks to his corner)
DA: Zell Strife is rushing to the ring to break up the weird ritual! And he is like a buzzsaw. Ripping through Angela! Strife has got the Dark Monster in a front face lock, spins and SLAMS
Angela to the mat, that had to hurt Strifes neck.
Strife is on top of him and just pummelling him with punches to the face! Zell Strife looks like he is trying to hurt the face of Angela.
Strife has him up and tosses him OVER THE TOP ROPE! Strife through the ropes with a bodypress and they flip over the railing and into the crowd!
The ref is trying to restore order but Strife is still beating Angela! Strife has a chair, Angela ducks, Strife swings again CHAIR SHOT! And
Angela flips back over the railing!
The ref takes the chair from Strife but these fans are going crazy! Strife is setting Angela up. PILEDRIVER! onto the floor of the MGM Arena! The ref forces Strife back into the ring. Angela rolls in and once again Strife is all over him. Zell has a fire in his eyes, he is staring down the Dark Monster! Strife with the
POWERBOMB! Strife is looking to the crowd and he is ready to end it! Angela is getting up, A KICK TO THE GROIN OF STRIFE! Angela, DDT! And Strife is down. That was fast, Angela is quicker than he looks. What is he doing to Strife? Is he..? HOOOOLLLY MOOOLLLY Angela is biting Strife on the forehead! YECH! I think he just spit part of Strifes head onto the floor. Angela sits Strife on the top rope, Angela backs up....runs to the corner. DDT! off the ropes! Angela is going over to get him. Strife is down and he looks hurt
Look out, PRESS SLAM by Angela. Tai is in the corner and he is drinking something that was in his robe.
He is gonna blow flame at Strife again. Strife is up. Angela goes for it! HOOOOLLLLY MOOOOLLLY Zell Strife ducks and the ref catches the flames in the face! Strife has Angela! PORPHYRIA!!! He nailed it but there is no one to make the pinfall! The ref is rolling around in agony. Strife calls for the EMT's to help the ref. Angela is struggling to his feet, Strife doesn't see him. HEART PUNCH!!! Angela nailed Strife who looks as if he is going into convulsions! The EMT's don't know what to do! Angela just blew fire up into the air! The FREAK! The ref is burned, Strife is convulsing and Tai Angela, the
Dark Monster is walking away!
