Vegas Wrestling Federation Update, 11 April 2002

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF Update (OOC- I have to go out of town for the next 2 weeks for work. I am not sure if I will have any internet access so we are going to take the next 2 weeks off just to be sure. The VWF will return to action on May 2 Thunder and with a Flash Show War of Words on April 29. It sucks but life is once again getting in the way of playing the game....oh well. Also, Classy Joey Boys handler has done a great job on a yet unfinished website http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/vegaswrestlingfed Go check it out. He is working on some sections but it looks great so far) ------------------ NEWS VWF President Rob Beauty is proud to announce that a secret investor has come forward and the VWF will me moving back into a bigger home starting May 2 with Thunder. The VWF will be at The Joint inside the Hard Rock Hotel and Casino. But who is the secret investor who is helping Rob Beauty? Classy Joey Boy really wants a piece of Jason Stone but where is Jason Stone? Many wrestling fans have followed this battle in other feds and the VWF would love to see these two battle in a VWF ring. In related news Sebastian said he is to good for the BlackJack title but he is the top contender for the Hardcore style belt, will he take a shot at CJB? The Real Deal may have never of heard of the VBA before but he knows them now. They answered his challenge and beat him down. Dirk Western and Paddy O'Shea are back. How does this affect the return of Revolution Randall Crane who was top contender before his car accident? Fans of the VWF are waiting to hear about the return of Xtreme Tony Cage. Cage was injured in his defense of the T3 title. Hopefully Cage will return to the VWF soon. 28th Street is no more! Hakeem and Tyson Young blew a second chance at the Tag Team titles and now they want a piece of each other. The Dark Monster, a freak himself, wants to rid the VWF of the freaks and he has picked Maquilladora. This could be the greatest challenge Maq has ever faced? ------------------- UPCOMING EVENT THURSDAY THUNDER, NIGHT OF CHAMPIONS! May 2 at The Joint at the Hard Rock The return of the VWF to a big time arena will have a big time card! In the opener REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE returns to the rings of the VWF in a battle against the only man to hold every singles title in the VWF, G-GANGSTA! THE MAQUILLADORA will defend the TAG TEAM STRAPS against the team of TAI ANGELA and his valet BLACKBIRD! He said he wasn't interested but he showed up to win the contender match, will SEBASTIAN show up to fight CLASSY JOEY BOY? Lets hope so! THE REAL DEAL defends his T3 Title in what is almost a handicap match as he meets DIRK WESTERN and PADDY O'SHEA! And in a BIG MAIN EVENT the 24 HOUR TITLE WILL BE ON THE LINE as ZELL STRIFE and a MYSTERY PARTNER battle ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS and his partner THOMAS RAINKA. If Strife is pinned the man who pins him walks away with the 24 Hour Title! ----------------- PAINT THE TOWN RED PPV Coming Soon! -----------------
