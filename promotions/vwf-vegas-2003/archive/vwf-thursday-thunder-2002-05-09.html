VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

Thursday Thunder, 9 May 2002

9 May 2002

[The shot cuts to the backstage area.] [The camera opens to a shot of a dark corridor inside the Hard Rock Hotel & Casino. A few candles are seen sporadically placed throughout the hallway, dimly lighting the area around them. From somewhere off camera, a noise is heard, followed by the movement of a shadowy figure. As the camera pans back, we see "The Revolution" Randall Crane standing amongst the candlelight. The faint glow casts eerily upon his body, and the faint outline of his face can be seen smirking evilly. The camera zooms in to show the detail in his face, and he then begins to speak.] RC: The time has come for me to retrieve what is rightfully mine. The months have passed, the barriers have been broken, and the final grains of sand have begun to settle. Tonight, in front of this crowd, "The Revolution" will begin once again. The final pieces are now falling into place, and in a few moments, it will all come together. [Crane looks into the camera as if staring at anyone watching.] RC: Tonight, I _will_ win the VWF T-3 Title. There will be no denying me this time. I don't care what kind of tricks are tried, and I don't care what's thrown at me. This is my night, and I'm going to show everyone in the back that I am the greatest wrestler this promotion has seen. To my opponents, I wish you good luck. Bring the best you have down to the ring, but even a miracle won't save you from what's going to happen. Tonight, I will drag you through the depths of hell and back, and when it's all said and done, the title will be around my waist. [Crane smirks once again, evilly.] RC: You can count on it. [With that. Crane blows out the candles, going to complete darkness. A sinister laugh is heard, echoing throughout the hallway.] (A black limo pulls into the parking garage in the back of the arena at The Joint. The driver stops the car and rushes to the back door to open it. Out from the limo emerges "VWF Team Captain" Classy Joey Boy. He is wearing black jeans and a white shirt tucked in. He has on dark sunglasses and his dark blonde hair is pulled back into a short ponytail. He carries the Blackjack title over his shoulder. Fatty Jay exits the limo after him. He is wearing black sweatpants a white button down shirt and a black sports jacket. The are both obviously prepared for Classy's T3 title win tonight. The two begin to head toward the locker room area.) CJB: You know Fatty.....It's going to be good becoming a double singles champion. Only the VWF Team Captain can pull off something as big as that. Tonight, I get to show off my abilities. I need to pull out all my tricks. First off, Crane and Sebastian. It's going be tough. I hope Crane doesn't think I am just going after Sebastian. If he does, then he is in for a big surprise. Fatty: And the second match tonight? CJB: We'll deal with that later. (Cut back to ringside and Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: He has reason...lets take a look at action from earlier.. ********************************************** THE REAL DEAL vs DIRK WESTERN vs PADDY O'SHEA ********************************************** As you may have guessed Paddy O'Shea scored the win in this one by hitting his ONE TWO on The Real Deal shortly after TRD pinned Western by the Done Deal. For most of the match Paddy was ..sleeping in the corner..until he jumped up and hit TRD with the ONE TWO. RESULT- PADDY O'SHEA Next up was another of the qualifying matches... ********************************************************************* THE MAQUILLADORA vs ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS vs THOMAS RAINKA ********************************************************************* Rainka and Andrews teamed up to destroy Maq in the early parts of this match, but their egos got in the way. After hitting the Bottom Line Andrews wanted to make the pin but Rainka pulled him to his feet, after a short arguement Rainka hit the ONLY ENDING on Andrews and eliminated him. But as soon as Rainka got to his feet the seemingly unstopable Maq hit PERMANENT DAYLIGHT and the one man tag team champion moves on! RESULT- THE MAQUILLADORA The final of the qualifying matches was the best of the bunch! ********************************************************* CLASSY JOEY BOY vs SEBASTIAN vs REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE ********************************************************* As usual Sebastian did not come out so the ref counted him out and CJB and Randall Crane went on to have a great battle. CJB kept the fight in the air and used a hit a run technique to keep the bigger Randall Crane off his game. Crane still looked rugged from his injuries but managed to hit the occasional move to keep CJB off. Classy Joey Boy went for the STUDD SPLASH but missed and this gave Crane a chance to power CJB into the ground. Crane crushed CJB with a JUMPING DDT and it looked over for the BlackJack Champion. Crane then went for a powerslam but CJB rolled him up.. But Sebastian came out from under the ring and nailed CJB in the back of the head and Crane rolled it back over and scored the win as Sebastian walked up the rampway laughing! RESULT- REVOUTION RANDALL CRANE ***COMMERCIAL*** DTMA: And we are ready for our two live matches, first up Tyson Young is going against his former tag team partner Hardcore Hakeem, Loser Leaves the VWF! ******************************* TYSON YOUNG vs HARDCORE HAKEEM Loser Leaves the VWF ******************************* (Bring Tha Noise hits and Hardcore Hakeem walks to the ring, making fun of the fans) (HEEL POP) DTMA: This tag team, 28th Street never made it to the heights that many thought it would and here they are going at it tonight. (Alive by POD hits and red and blue lights flash around the arena as Tyson Young walks slowly out onto the ramp. Tyson pulls of his shirt and rushes to the ring) (MIXED POP) DTMA: And Tyson Young is in the ring quick and he and Hakeem are exchanging blows one after the other! Hakeem goes back and comes off the ropes... SIDEWALK SLAM by Tyson Young! Tyson Young picks up Hakeem and sends him to the ropes.....THE STREET!!!! Cover ONE TWO THREEEEEEE!!!!!!! (THAT WAS QUICK POP) It is over! Hardcore Hakeem is gone and Tyson Young is celebrating in the ring as Alive by POD once again hits! ***COMMERCIAL*** DTMA: We are back and the fans are pumped for the main event, the crowning of a new T3 Champion ************************************************************* PADDY O'SHEA vs THE MAQUILLADORA vs REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE T3 Title Match ************************************************************* ['Revolution Is My Name' by Pantera begins to play over the PA System, as the lights around the wrestler's entrance slowly begin to dim to complete darkness. Strobe lights start to flash on either side of the entrance, and smoke slowly begins to cloud over the entranceway. RA: About to make his way down to the ring... standing six feet eight inches tall... and weighing in at two-hundres and eighty-seven pounds... he hails from Atlanta, Georgia... here is... "THE RRRRRRRRRREVOLUTION" RRRRRRRRRRANDALLLLLLLLLL CRRRRRRRRRRANNNNNNNNNNE!!!!!!!!!! [The lights at the wrestlers entrance stop flashing, lighting up all around as the curtains are thrown back. Standing there is the massive frame of Randall Crane, who smiles wickedly as he takes in the boos. Crane slowly raises one arm to the crowd, as they boo even louder. It is then that Crane makes his way down to the ring, walking down the aisle at a normal pace, smirking at the insults being thrown at him. He takes a moment to talk trash to a few of the fans, then walks away from them as they boo, which is very audible to the camera. Crane makes his way to the ringside area, as he slowly climbs the steps leading into the ring. He stands at the ring apron, then lifts one leg over the top rope, followed by the other. Crane gets into the ring, ascending one of the turnbuckles, raising an arm into the air. As the crowd boos him, Crane lets them know what he thinks by flipping them off, the boos getting even louder. Crane then decends the ropes, walking over to his corner, where he takes off his shirt, handing it to one of the ring attendants. He then prepares for his match.] (NICE SOLID HEEL POP) "The Maquilladora" by Radiohead. Video is various high risk moves and highlights from the 12 Days tourney and the now late CWF. He walks to the ring slapping fans hands and does a flip into the ring (like in No Mercy for N64) and does a backflip off of the turnbuckle. (NICE SOLID FACE POP) DTMA: And here is The Maquilladora, the VWF's one man tag team champion! (Suddenly on the tron the fans of the VWF see Dirk Western beating the hell out of Paddy O'Shea in the back with a chair, chairshot after chairshot until the already drunken O'Shea is completely out) DTMA: Maq turned to watch the action backstage and he just got poppped by Crane from behind! Crane throws Maq across the ring with a big HIP TOSS!!! And Crane stalks over to him and pulls Maq up by his mask. And that was a punch that hadda hurt! Maq is on the ground and Crane rolls Maq into a SINGLE LEG BOSTON CRAB!!!! The Maquilladora has beaten men bigger than Crane but I don't know if he has been in the ring with anyone as ruthless. Crane is cranking on the Boston Crab and Maq holding on! What a tough little guy! (WHAT A TOUGH LITTLE GUY POP) (COME ON MAQ!! POP) (MAQ! MAQ! CHANT GOES UP) Crane breaks the hold! But only long enough to yell at the fans! And he locks Maq back into the SINGLE LEG BOSTON CRAB! (MAQ! MAQ!) (MOCKING RAN DOLL RAN DOLL CHANT IS HEARD) And Crane breaks it again only this time he drops an elbow to the small of the back of The Maquilladora! Crane pulls him up...to the ropes and POWERSLAM!!!! Cover... ONE TWO Kickout by Maq! And Crane tosses Maq out of the ring and is back to mocking the fans of the VWF!!!! Some of the fans have some signs about Cranes ability to drive...it was a car wreck a few months back that cost him a shot at the T3 Title back at The Gauntlet pay per view. Crane is pointing at Maq ..... There goes Crane...... EXTREME MEASURES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (OH MY POP) And Crane rolls Maq back into the ring Cover... ONE TWO Kickout by Crane! Randall Crane is one violent man....he has Maq and CHOKESLAM!!!! And he follows it with a quick LEGDROP! And Crane locks on the SINGLE LEG BOSTON CRAB AGAIN!!!!!!! Randall Crane is one smart wrestler, if he can get Maq in the Revolutions Ends 2002 after makign that ankle weak Maq will tap out! (MAQ! MAQ! LOUDER THAN BEFORE) Maq is responding! The Maquilladora is getting energy from the fans here at The Joint at the Hard Rock. Maq spins over and KICKS Crane off...who goes to the rope...backflip to a standing position by Maq and HURRICANRANA!!!! (HELL YEAH POP) And The Maquilladora leaps to the top rope......FROG SPLASH!!!!! Maq is up and goes to the ropes...ROLLING MAQ THUNDER!!!!!! Cover... ONE TWO (CLOSE POP) Kickout by Crane! Maq stands up Crane and points to his feet, those fast damn feet! (KICK THE HELL OUT OF HIM POP) And Maq does what these fans want! (YES! YES! YES! YES!) And Crane goes down after a FLURRY OF FAST FEET by Maq! Maq off the ropes with a SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT!!! (WHAT THE HELL POP) Here comes SEBASTIAN!!!!! The Sexy One is walking to the ring with his fingers on his nose like this stinks. Maq sees him coming.... Sebastian is up on the apron..... Maq is walking over .... They are face to face...... (HELL YEAH X2 POP) Here comes CLASSY JOEY BOY and he has a chair!!!!! <<<CRACK>>> Hell no! Hell no!!!! Sebastian moved and CJB just knocked out The Maquilladora.... Sebastian is running back up the ramp and CJB is following.... Crane is on his feet and REVOLUTIONS END 2002!!!!!!!!!!! The ref lifts up Maqs hand... ONE TWO THREE!!! That is it! Revolution Randall Crane is the T3 Champion! (Revolution Is My Name by Pantera begins to play as the ref hands Crane the title and he soaks in the crowd reaction) What a night! See you next week.
