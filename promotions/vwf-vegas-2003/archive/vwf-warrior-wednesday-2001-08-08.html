VWF (Vegas, 2001–2005) — Card

Warrior Wednesday, 8 August 2001

9 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
# Viva Las Vegas # (A quick shot of Sebastian) # Viva Las Vegas # (Kerry Powell scores the pin on Dirk Western) # Viva # (A shot of Tai Angela in a dark alley) # Viva # (Huey Huxtable falls off the second rope onto Anarchy X) # Las Vegas# (The camera comes on to the face of Dan "The Man" Aller) DA: This is The Man and this is the night we crown a champion here in the VWF! All the stars and the new guys, Angela and Zell Strife! The crowd here at the VWF is pumped, lets get right to the action.
(I Wanna Destroy You by the Circle Jerks starts to play and Anarchy X begins his slow walk to the ring. He is wearing a ripped up shirt and shorts and has orange and blue hair)
DA: Ladies and gents, making his way to the ring. From right here in
Las Vegas, ANARCHY X!!!!
(Crowd boos) DA: He didn't look good last week but he seems more serious tonight. (Folsom Prison Blues begins to play and Dirk Western starts a quick and determined walk to the ring. He stops, jumps on the apron and turns to the crowd. He raises his hands then brings them down quick to his side)
DA: In this corner, from Cell Block D. This is DIRK WESTERN!!!!!!!!!! DA: Now I like Western. He has the right frame of mind. DA: They lock up and Western with a quick side headlock, a wrestling move! Anarchy X breaks the hold by back kicking Western.
Look at X pummell Western with lefts and rights! And a blasting lariat drops Western to the canvas.
X is now yelling at the crowd. This guy is not the brightest punk in the band. A few kicks to keep Western down and X slides out of the ring. He seems to be arguing with a fan at ringside.
Western is up, off the ropes and he is airborne and lands on X outside the ring. Western is as graceful as a wet dog but the move worked.
Western lifts X and drops him BODYSLAM to the arena floor. Western is back in the ring and calling for X. X rushes in and BACK BODY DROP!
Western turns and IRISH WHIP into the turnbuckle and Western follows him! SLAAAMMMMM!!!!! Western lays a few hands on the punk then lifts him onto the top rope. What is Western going to do? He is up on the second rope! DDT!!! Hoollly Mooollllyyy! 1...2...KICKOUT! X kicked out! And after that DDT! But he doesn't look good! Western is setting him up! D-DAY!!!! Western nails the D-Day. 1....2...3!!!!! DIRK WESTERN wins and moves on in the tournament! - - - - - DA: Western gets the win if you missed it. And once again Anarchy X loses. Lets see who is up next.
(Peace Train plays and Huey Huxtable starts to walk toward the ring, his father seems very serious but Huey is smiling and waving to everyone with an idiots smile)
DA: From Shamrock Texas, weighting in at 360 pounds and standing seven feet tall... HUEY HUXTABLE!!! DA: This guy is huge but I don't think he has the heart for this, and he looks even more worried tonight. And here is the reason why...
(Black Sabbath begins to play and the arena lights slowly dim. A procession of young women all dressed in black ropes, each carrying a candle begin the walk to the ring. Tai Angela follows them, inside the ring he stands in the center and the women form a circle around him, they blow out the candles, fall to their knees in front of him. With a wave he banishes them and walks to his corner)
DA: Lights! Somebody turn the lights back on! Thank you. Hey, where the hell is Big Huey. His father Henry is in the ring but the big guy is gone. There is the bell and Tai Angela rushes the father who turns tail and runs. The ref is making the count on Huey. Well that sucked! Your winner by Count Out TAI ANGELA! - - - - - DA: So far Western and Angela have moved on and now it is time for the man who may be keeping Ric Beauty out of VWF.
[The house lights dim down, and golden spotlights begin to sweep the entire arena. A hush falls over the crowd as "Money" by Pink
Floyd begins to play.]
Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, weighting in at 238 pounds, and standing at 6 feet, and one inch, this is...
24 KARAT KERRY POWELL!!!!! [The fans jump to their feet as Kerry Powell himself, makes his way to the ring.] DA: And his opponent... DA: Making his way to the ring, with a smirk and smile on his face from Los Angeles...SEBASTIAN.
(Sebastian steps into the ring and bows to each side as I'm To Sexy plays in the background)
SEBASTIAN: Remember you losers! I am Sebastian and you, Kerry Powell, are NOT! DA: Sebastian doesn't seem to want to have anything to do with Powell, he just waved and got out of the ring. Here he comes.
SEBASTIAN: Kerry Powell is a cheap rip off of me! I am the greatest physical specimen, the most intelligent wrestler, I don't want to have a punk like Powell to even touch me.
DA: Well you got no choice! Powell has rushed outside and BODYPRESS! Sebastian is on the ground and Powell is taking the fight to Sebastian. Sebastian is trying to get back in the ring. Powell follows. And Sebastian jumps down on him and locks him in a front face lock. Sebastian is crafty. He is clamping down on Powell! And 24 Karat doesn't look to good. (O Fortuna begins to play over the PA) DA: What the hell! That is the music of Ric Beauty, the brother of Rob the owner of the VWF!
Sebastian doesn't seem to care, but Powell is fighting mad! He takes Sebastian under the knees and lifts Sebastian high into the air and lifts him up! What a move by Powell.
Powell is going to fall backward. HOOOOLLLYY MOOOOLLLLYYY! Powell drops him and is looking around the arena. RIC BEAUTY (voice only): I am here Powell. Sebastian comes up from behind and nails Powell with a pair of brass knuckles, schoolboy. 1...2...3! SEBASTIAN gets the win. Powell is up, look at the blood on his face. He tears into Sebastian with rights and lefts. Sebastian is getting stained with the blood of
Kerry Powell!
GOLD RUSH! Powell gets the Gold Rush and is up on the ropes. FOOLS GOLD! Powell may have lost but he looks like the winner as he runs up the ramp toward the backstage area.
- - - - - DA: Kerry Powell just lost but dropped Sebastian, who must come back and face the winner of the next match.
Powell and Ric Beauty have a long history. Powell kept the UWC title from Ric and then cost Ric the old CEW title. And now it seems like
Ric cost Powell the VWF title, at least for now!
Back to the action! (Barroom Heroes hits and Paddy O'Shea starts his long stumble to the ring) DA: From Dublin Ireland.....The Master of the 1-2...Paddy O'Shea! (Fall Children by AFI starts with thick fog pouring through the ramp way, green smoke fills the air. Lights start to flash and fade while a large
Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans)
DA: The new guy looks to make a quick impression! He has hold of
O'Shea and drops him with a quick suplex.
Strife doesn't seem to care about the crowd or O'Shea, this guy looks as scary as Angela. The freak show is in the VWF. Strife rips at the eyes of O'Shea. Kick to the gut and DDT! He picks up O'Shea and DDT! Again. And again.
And again! Strife is standing over O'Shea with his arms outstreched after four
DDT's. O'Shea with a HEADBUTT to the groin! Strife doubles over and O'Shea. O'Shea trying to shake off the DDT's. He finally makes his way over to
Strife, sets him up, belly to belly and Strife flies across the ring.
O'Shea is looking good despite his personal troubles. O'Shea is getting on the top rope..? Splash....Strife moves...PORPHYRIA..1...2...3! And the winner is ZELL STRIFE! - - - - - DA: We have our final four. Dirk Western, Tai Angela, Sebastian and
Zell Strife. (Rob Beauty steps out onto the ramp) ROB: Ladies and gentlemen, it is time to crown a champion! Four men, when one man gains a pin he is the champion!
And brother Ric, if you are out there....get out of my federation! DA: Rob Beauty is our President and he is not happy! But lets crown our champ, the 24 hour champ! (Fall Children by AFI starts with thick fog pouring through the ramp way, green smoke fills the air. Lights start to flash and fade while a large
Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans)
DA: And now..... (Sebastian steps into the ring and bows to each side as I'm To Sexy plays in the background)
SEBASTIAN: Remember you losers! I am Sebastian and you are NOT! DA: Strife is not wasting time, he rushes into Sebastian....SPEAR!! He comes Dirk Western! And he is joining in the madness. Strife and Western are trading blows as Sebastian is trying to get to his feet. Strife DDT to Western! SPEAR to Sebastian! Zell Strife is in control of this match! Strife has Western, FACE FIRST reverse powerbomb. Where is the other guy? Tai Angela? Sebastian goes for the cover on Western as Strife looks up the ramp. 1...2....ALMOST but a kick out. Strife seems to be looking for Angela. Western wraps up Sebastian in what looks like a SLEEPER HOLD.
Strife lariats Western off Sebastian. (Black Sabbath begins to play but Tai Angela is nowhere around) DA: There is the music. The three way action in the ring is out of control. Sebastian doesn't look good from the beating he took from
Kerry Powell earlier.
Tai Angela is walking toward the ring but he is just watching from the outside...? Western whips Sebastian into the ropes SPINEBUSTER! Strife is looking at Angela. Strife spins and delivers a heel kick to Western that knocks him from the ring. Strife is setting Sebastian up for the Porphyria! This could be it! Strife has him....here comes Angela. HOOOLLLLYY MOOOLLLLYY! Angela just spit fire into the face of Zell Strife who is rolling around on the canvas. Angela is walking away? What is wrong with the freak? Sebastian jumps on Strife. 1...2....3!!! Oh no! SEBASTIAN wins! He is the First 24 Hour CHAMPION! Sebastian has been given the belt. Strife is being attended to by
EMT's and Angela is just standing on the ramp.
We are out of time! See you next week! - - - - - VWF Copyright 2001
