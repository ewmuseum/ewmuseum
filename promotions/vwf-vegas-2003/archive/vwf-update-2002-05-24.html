Vegas Wrestling Federation Update, 24 May 2002

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VWF Update ----------- Check out the VWF online at http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/vegaswrestlingfed NEWS The fans were still talking about it hours after Thunder, CLASSY JOEY BOY turned on G-GANGSTA and the fans and sided with SEBASTIAN! It looks like the Sexy One will get his shot at the 24 Hour Title. And if fans weren't talking about that they were asking what the hell is wrong with VWF President ROB BEAUTY!? Beauty stripped THE MAQUILLADORA of the Tag Team Titles thanks to pressure from REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE, and Beauty also seems to have accepted a match with ZELL STRIFE! And what happens to the Tag Team Titles now? A tournament? Are their enough teams in the VWF for a tournament? Questions, questions, questions.... --------- UPCOMING EVENTS THUNDER May 30 At The Joint inside the Hard Rock Hotel and Casino The rookies open the show as ROB KIDANE meets up with the FALLING FRENCHMAN MARCEL KLUTZ With the tag team straps open these two teams could go a long way to making a play for a future shot as the FIGHTING O'SHEAS meet up with ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS and THOMAS RAINKA CLOUD KINNEAS steps in the ring with the biggest of the rookies as he meets DANE KLINGEL TYSON YOUNG has the attention of the BlackJack Champion but first will have to meet CAPSMAN! ROB BEAUTY decided this could not, would not wait until Paint The Town Red, the president will step into the ring for the first time in over a year to meet ZELL STRIFE! Randall Crane talked to Rob Beauty and stripped The Maquilladora of his title(s). Crane has also set up an interesting match for Thunder as THE MAQUILLADORA meets up with THE REAL DEAL and Crane is the quest referee! If TRD wants a shot at Crane he has to defeat Maq! And in the Main Event! G-GANGSTA will put the 24 HOUR TITLE on the line against CLASSY JOEY BOY!!!! who will have SEBASTIAN in his corner. Gangsta has been talking that HE is coming, well Gangsta might need Him for this setup errr match! ---------- PAINT THE TOWN RED PPV June 5, 2002 In the parking lot of the Hard Rock Hotel and Casino! So far..... G-GANGSTA will defend the 24 Hour Title against SEBASTIAN! if Gangsta still has the title! ZELL STRIFE will step into the ring with ROB BEAUTY in a FIRST BLOOD then PINFALL or SUBMISSION match! REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE will defend against the T3 TITLE against THE MAQUILLADORA and maybe THE REAL DEAL, if TRD wins on Thunder! CLASSY JOEY BOY has agreed to meet TYSON YOUNG for the BLACKJACK TITLE! CLOUD KINEAS, FIGHTING O'SHEAS and all VWF stars will be on the card as well! Dark matches featuring all the VWE members! ---------- Thanks -rich
