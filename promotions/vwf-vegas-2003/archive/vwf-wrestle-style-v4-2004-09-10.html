VWF — Show

Wrestle Style V.4

September 10, 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
# # # # # # # # # # # # # # ######### # # # # () # (Its time for Wrestle Style Version 4!!!!!!) .. .. .. (Cut to an electric power station near the Palms Hotel in Las Vegas, Mr Electricity Robbie Caldwell is INSIDE the station, just behind the WARNING STAY OUT sign) ROBBIE CALDWELL: Tonight The Outlaw learns the lessons that others here have found out. Tonight I teach Morris Bass a thing or two about how to fight in the ring! Tonight Bass gets a lesson. A lesson in SHOCK AND HORROR! (Caldwell grabs the fence and the scene abrubtly fades away) .. .. .. (The Wrestle Babes are doing some kind of choreographed number as PIMP plays. We fade to JD Southerns sitting at the VWF desk) SOUTHERNS: Welcome to Wrestle Style V.4! Tonight we have a 24 Hour Title match, that's right, the 24 Hour was reinstated! And we have SIX big matches in the tournament. But many fans are wondering if the internet rumors are true, is Jason Storm NOT going to be here tonight? Storm said he was bowing out of the tournament, lets hope not. A Jason Storm versus Classy Joey Boy match could be one of the all time greats in the VWF history! Maybe Storm is still hurting after that violent battle with Orion last week. Well we might as well get right to the first match, it was a triple threat in the tournament! .. .. .. ============================= FLASH (vs) ORION THE HUNTER (vs) THE ASSASSIN MARCUS O'MALLEY ============================= SOUTHERNS: Well O'Malley came to the ring first, Orion was on his way when from out of the back came someone who looked physically like Jason Storm, but wearing black tights and a black mask with a silver V on it. This mystery man NAILED Orion with a FLYING FIVEARM and then disappeared into the crowd. Next up Flash was set to come to the ring, and he did! Flash hit the ring with a baseball bat! The Bodzilla was still in street clothes and did not show any signs of his usual jovial self. Flash got in the ring, grabbed the ref and tossed him down, then DOUBLED over Orion with the baseball bat! O'Malley made his run at Flash but Flash back body dropped him and O'Malley rolled to the outside. Flash then began to beat Orion with the baseball bat! The ref called for the bell and tried to stop it but he could not. That is when Orion #2 and The Equalizer came down and hit the ring, that brought the man in the black mask back out of the crowd and a brief melee happened until the ref called for more refs and the ring was cleared. Then the announcement was made that Flash had been disqualified and Orion was to injured to continue leaving Marcus O'Malley with the win! Once the ring was cleared, the combatants for the next match made their way to the ring. .. .. .. ============================== MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS ============================== SOUTHERNS: This was a good match, lots of back and forth action with Caldwell using a strategy of wearing down the legs and knees of Bass while The Outlaw went for the more direct beatdown. Bass had the upper hand in the middle portion of the match and rocked Mr Electricity with a BRAINBUSTER and nearly got the fall. Caldwell eventually turned the tables and he went right to work on the knees of Bass. Hitting a couple of knee breakers and then even locking Bass in the FIGURE FOUR! Caldwell seemed to have Bass where he wanted him, and he placed The Outlaw on the top rope. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. WEST: SHOCK AND HORROR!!! DTMA: Bass fighting back! He is punching Caldwell! WEST: Caldwell falls off the ropes! DTMA: Big chance here for Morris Bass, and he is standing on the top rope! (A gong sounds and on the ramp walks Iyasu Saku!!!) WEST: ITS SAKU! IYASU SAKU IS BACK IN THE VWF!! DTMA: I thought he was fired! WEST: Bass is shocked! DTMA: Caldwell is up! WEST: And he pulls Bass off the top rope, and wraps him up! DTMA: Caldwell has his feet on the ropes! WEST: The ref sees it and breaks up the fall! DTMA: Caldwell picks up Bass, CLOTHESLINE BY BASS!! And the Outlaw is heading out of the ring, he is going after Iyasu Saku! WEST: And Caldwell is on his feet! He is over and he is getting out of the ring, he is going after Bass! .. .. .. (CUT back to the desk) SOUTHERNS: Bass chased Saku into the back, and Caldwell followed. The upshot was that both Caldwell and Bass were counted out! VWF camera never caught up to the three men but rumors are that Bass caught Saku and then Caldwell caught up to both men and the three brawled until broken up by security. And now, knowing Saku and possible the whole of the New Age Army could be in the building how would the other SOD members react? Kevin Simmons still has a pretty big score to settle with Saku! And everyone was wondering if it was only Saku? And how did he get hired back after the NAA was so publicly fired so many weeks ago. Lets move to a match that did not have chaos, well, it kind of had chaos. .. .. .. ============= THE EQUALIZER (vs) BLAZE ============= SOUTHERNS: This one was a fight from the beginning, both men tore into each other and it looked like they both wanted it to end quick. Blaze outpowered an Equalizer FULL NELSON and took control of the match, but when he hit a DEATH VALLEY DRIVER on The Equalizer the ref was knocked down! (REF BUMP!) Blaze went right to the outside of the ring and brought a STEEL CHAIR back in the ring with him. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. WEST: Blaze looking to hit a TOMBSTONE on the STEEL CHAIR!!! DTMA: BACK BODY DROP BY THE EQUALIZER!!! WEST: Blaze leaps to his feet! DTMA: FACE PLANT!! FACE PLANT ON THE STEEL CHAIR!!! WEST: Crushing! DTMA: Equalizer wakes the ref, and makes the cover! ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match, THE EQUALIZER!!! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Cut to JD Southerns, the Wrestle Babes dance in a piece of paper and hand it to him) SOUTHERNS: Well folks, we just got word that Kingston Cash decided that since Caldwell and Bass were counted out, they will remain in the tournament! We still have three tournament matches to go, but before we get to it, lets take a look at the 24 Hour Title match! .. .. .. ========================================================= VWF 24 Hour Title Match Battle Royal KEVIN BLAIR ©, FAST EDDIE CUTLASS, THE MAQUILLADORA, REAL DEAL, CAPSMAN, EVIL CAPSMAN, SONICMAN, ADAM JENNINGS ========================================================= SOUTHERNS: With all eight men in the ring the four Rhombus members quickly worked as a team. Maq and Sonic double teamed Real Deal while the CAPSMEN! tried to oust Kevin Blair. Jennings ended up in a struggle with Fast Eddie Cutlass and the fight was on. In a shock to the fans, at the three minute mark the CAPSMEN! threw out Kevin Blair! The guaranteed a new champion would leave the ring, but possibly without the title. As soon as Blair hit the floor he got the title and rushed into the back. With CAPS stunned at Blair, EVIL C tossed out CAPSMAN! EVIL C gloated but only until he turned and was knocked out by Real Deal. Jennings powerslamed Cutlass and was going for a second rope fist drop when he was stopped by Sonicman! Sonic almost got the Hype out of the ring but Jennings was able to fight him off. Cutlass came off the ropes and went for a FLYING BODYPRESS to Real Deal, but the big man caught him and hit a FALLAWAY SLAM that sent Cutlass to the floor! Four men left, The Maquilladora was mixing it up with Jennings and Real Deal was beating the SNOT out of Sonicman! Maq dropped Jennings and went to his help his old friend, but at the same time Sonic rocked back Real Deal then went for a SPEAR, but RD moved and Sonic hit The Maquilladora instead. Sonic stood there and Jennings came in and hit a SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE that took Sonicman out of the ring! Real Deal then rushed Jennings, but The Hype moved and Real Deal hit the floor! This left Jennings in the ring with The Maquilladora, and Maq was hurting from the spear from Sonicman! Jennings took full advantage and picked up the Submission Champ and then put him in the HYPER EXTENSION!!! Lets go the call! .. .. .. WEST: Jennings has Maq, and The Maquilladora is tapping! DTMA: But this is a battle royal! And the ref is letting Jennings know. Maq can tap all night long but until Jennings throws him out of the ring, this match will continue! WESTL: Jennings breaks the hold and he is storming around the ring! DTMA: Maq getting up, but the damage to the leg is clear. WEST: Jenning over! .. . . PERMANENT DAYLIGHT!!! DMTA: MAQ HIT THE OLD SCHOOL FINISHER!!! WEST: And Maq makes a cover, but the ref is telling him about the rules to a battle royal! DTMA: The crowd chanting a count, Maq could have got the fall! WEST: Maq up and he whips Jennings to the ropes, HYPE DUCKS A FOREARM! DMTA: MULE KICK BY MAQ CONNECTS AS JENNINGS IS COMING OFF THE ROPES!!! WEST: And Adam Jennings is hanging on the ropes! Maq almost kicked him out! DMTA: Maq rushes in! .. . . JENNINGS WAS PLAYING POSSUM!!!! (SHOCK POP AS MAQ FLIES OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TO THE FLOOR!) WEST: WHOA!!! DTMA: ADAM JENNINGS IS THE 24 HOUR CHAMPION!!!! WEST: He has lived up to the HYPE! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match and NEW 24 Hour Champion, ADAM THE HYPE JENNINGS!!!! .. .. .. (CUT to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Yes folks, Adam Jennings is the new 24 Hour Champ. But it did not end there. Kingston Cash hit the ring and he was not happy with an SOD member holding a VWF title. So Cash told Jennings that until he actually can get the title off of the psycho Kevin Blair, Blair is STILL THE CHAMPION!!! The fans went crazy as did Jennings! But Kingston Cash was suddenly flanked by two old faces, from out of the crowd came The Reckoning and The Salvation, aka THE MIGHTY MEN! And they began a vicious beatdown of Jennings, that brought out O'Malley and McQueen but the Mighty Men ran off back through the crowd. So Blair is still the champ, but Jennings will be the champ if he can physically possess the title. That might not be as easy as it sounds, Blair has had a death grip on the strap for a long time. Lets move on! It was time for another SOD member to hit the ring. .. .. .. ============================ TAZMANIAN DEVIL MARK MCQUEEN (vs) SEBASTIAN ============================ SOUTHERNS: McQueen hit the ring and ripped into Sebastian like no one has in a long time. McQueen seemed to have scouted Sebastian well and was one step ahead of him. But McQueen struggled as the match moved on to put away Sebastian. Each big move was met with a 2 count, not a three! Sebastian picked at McQueen during these times and was slowly getting himself back into the match, McQueen came at Sebastian at the eight minute mark and Sebastian hit the TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER! Things got bad for McQueen from there. Sebastian began his methodical style and worked on the lower back of McQueen, but the fifteen minute time limit mark was fast approaching. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: I don't know for sure Dirk, but we have to be close, this match has been going for a while. WEST: Sebastian has that look on his face! DTMA: He is going for it! SEX YOU OUT!! WEST: MCQUEEN BLOCKING IT! HE BLOCKED IT! DTMA: Sebastian looks frustrated! WEST: McQueen flips Sebastian! DTMA: And Mark McQueen up and he is dancing around! WEST: I just got word we are under a minute left in this match! DTMA: McQueen grabs Sebastian, .. . . DEVILS SNARE!!! WEST: SEBASTIAN ROLLED HIM UP! DMTA: ONE!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match, SEBASTIAN!!! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Well, every fan who knows a thing about this business was excited about this match. Lets see if it happened. .. .. .. =============== CLASSY JOEY BOY (vs) JASON STORM =============== SOUTHERNS: Storm said he was not coming, and he did not! Classy Joey Boy came to the ring with the big entrance and waited, smiled and talked with the fans. But no Jason Storm. CJB looked to the stage area and waited. That's is when The Equalizer came out of the crowd, grabbed the timekeepers bell, climbed in the ring and CLOCKED CJB! The former VWF heavyweight champ went down like a rag doll! And The Equalizer continued the brutal, almost unwatchable assault until finally he just stopped and walked off, as if nothing had happened! CJB was left a bloody mess in the middle of the ring. The medics came out and took care of CJB as the chants of CJB rang throughout the arena. CJB wins the match, via count out. Once the ring was cleared it was time for the VWF title defense, Lark Fenriz and The Predator Kevin Simmons. This should be a tougher test for Fenriz than recovering alcoholic Billy Bones, who Fenriz beat last week. .. .. .. =========================== VWF Heavyweight Title Match LARK FENRIZ © (vs) THE PREDATOR KEVIN SIMMONS =========================== SOUTHERNS: The champ looked flat and Simmons looked like a possessed beast early on. Maybe it was him thinking about the recent return of the New Age Army? But it had Simmons in a mood to damage someone and the champ was on the end of it. Fenriz countered a SPINEBUSTER attempt into a DDT at the five minute mark and began his assault on Simmons. Fenriz then did some things out of character for him, using the top rope more. Hitting a TOP ROPE CLOTHESLINE and then a few moments later hitting Simmons with a TOP ROPE ELBOW to the chest, that got a near fall for the champ. Simmons rolled out of the way of the next top rope attempt and The Predator pounced, literally. Simmons hit the PREDATOR POUNCE and got the near fall on Fenriz. But Simmons was not done, he hit a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP and then put Fenriz in a REAR NAKED CHOKE and began to wear down the champ. Fenriz got to his feet and reversed it into a GERMAN SUPLEX, and then Fenriz put Simmons in an OCTUPUS LIKE HOLD and used a NERVE PINCH on the shoulder of Simmons. That nerve pinch really was hurting Simmons. He tried to get to the ropes but could not move thanks to Fenriz having him all wrapped up. Ring side observers thought Simmons would tap out, but the crowd began to chant SIMM ONS! SIMM ONS! And he used it to pulls himself free and break away from Fenriz, knocking him off his feet with a SHOULDERBLOCK! The action from here was back and forth, each man giving and taking. Lets pick it up with Simmons and Fenriz going tow to toe in the center of the ring! .. .. .. WEST: SIMMONS WITH A RIGHT! DTMA: FENRIZ WITH A RIGHT! WEST: SIMMONS WITH ANOTHER RIGHT HAND! DTMA: FENRIZ THROWS ONE AND CONNECTS! WEST: Fenriz tackles Simmons! And now the champ is just choking him! This is just a plain old choke! DTMA: The ref is calling for the break! WEST: Fenriz lets go, and he pulls Simmons up! DTMA: Fenriz signals, Simmons with a DOUBLE LEG TAKEDOWN! WEST: Simmons trying to set him up! DMTA: DURHAM DEATHLOCK COMING UP! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: WHAT??? RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, this match has been declared a TIME LIMIT DRAW!!! (THAT SUCKS POP!!!) .. .. .. (CUT to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: The time limit expired, now Cash said the title could change hands during the tournament on count outs and disqualifications, but the man said nothing about draws. So Cash has decided that both Fenriz and Simmons will move on. So there are eight men still in the tournament, tune in next week when we will bring it down to TWO! Until then, have a good week. © 2004 Vegas Wrestling Federation
