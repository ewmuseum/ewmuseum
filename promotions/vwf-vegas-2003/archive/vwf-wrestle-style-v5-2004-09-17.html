Wrestle Style V.5

September 17, 2004

# # ######### # # # # # # # # ######### # # # # () ######### (Its time for Wrestle Style Version 5!!!!!!) .. .. .. (We see the Wrestle Babes, dancing to P-I-M-P. The scene fades to JD Southerns sitting at the VWF desk) SOUTHERNS: Its WSV.5! And tonight we are going to find out which two men will be meeting at Vegas Violence! Plus we've got a 24 Hour Title defense tonight, as well as plenty of big matches. Last week the VWF saw the return of the group known as the New Age Army, but now they are led by Iyasu Saku. Lets take a look at his in ring return. .. .. .. ========== IYASU SAKU (vs) SONICMAN ========== SOUTHERNS: Saku hit the ring and began to dismantle the former Submission Champion. Saku was clearly working on the shoulders of Sonic in an effort to set him up for the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE. Sonicman found his footing midway through the match and took the fight back to Saku. Sonic kept his offense to the air but missed a PLANCHA, and Saku was once again back in charge. Lets go to the call from Dan and Dirk. .. .. .. WEST: Saku has Sonic, SHOULDERBREAKER! DTMA: I am telling you Dirk, Iyasu Saku looks better than he ever has. WEST: Saku signaling the crowd! Its CROSSFACE TIME! DTMA: Wait! He is standing Sonicman on his feet .. . . ROARING ELBOW!!! WEST: That nearly took of Sonis head! DTMA: Saku with the cover ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!! THRRREEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: I thought he was going for the submission but Saku scores the pinfall. DTMA: WAIT! AND NOW SAKU IS LOCKING SONICMAN IN THE GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE!!!! WEST: Saku is not letting go! And the ref can't stop him! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Saku was really looking to hurt Sonicman, and that is when CAPSMEN! hit the ring to save the day, and they did, until The Mighty Men came out! It was a six man brawl until security came out and broke it all up. The match between The Mighty Men and CAPSMEN was declared a no contest! It is unclear if Saku is going to go after the Submission Title, or trying to finish off Kevin Simmons. If Saku does want the Submission Title he will have to take it from The Maquilladora. Maq was in action against Fast Eddie Cutlass. .. .. .. ================== Non-Title Match THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) FAST EDDIE CUTLASS =================== SOUTHERNS: Neither man has been hitting on all cylinders in the past few weeks and this match was no exception. Both men hit their normal high spots and the match moved along but neither Maq or Cutlass captured the excitement or glory they have in the past. The result of this match was a TIME LIMIT DRAW. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Dancing Wrestle Babes, then a fade to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Some have called the tag team champions, paper champions. Blaze and Real Deal did have the titles handed to them, literally. They have tried to get out of the shadow of that but it has been tough. One half of the tag champs, Real Deal signed into singles action against Mark McQueen. .. .. .. ============================ REAL DEAL (vs) TAZMANIAN DEVIL MARK MCQUEEN ============================ SOUTHERNS: When a seven foot monster steps in the ring with a guy under six foot the guy under six foot better have a game plan. McQueen seemed to want to stay away from ending up in the clutches of Real Deal and for much of the match he was able to do so. But eventually, after a brilliant MISSLE DROPKICK McQueen went for the pin, but only got the 2 count. As both men were getting up Real Deal used a THROAT PUNCH and then took over. From there it was like watching someone toss around a rag doll, McQueen was flying more than Peter Pan! Real Deal then hit a POWERFUL CHOKESLAM! But he pulled McQueen up after only a 2 count! Then another CHOKESLAM! And another pull up! Lets go up to the call, picking it up with Real Deal just having CHOKESLAMMED McQueen for the third time in a row. .. .. .. DMTA: The ref is screaming at Real Deal! WEST: Why? He can do what he wants, he is winning this match! (MASSIVE POP AS MARCUS O'MALLEY RUSHES TO THE RING) DTMA: O'Malley in behind Real Deal! (POP!) .. . . CLOTHESLINE!! WEST: Big man still on his feet! <<< BELL RINGS >>> DTMA: O'Malley off the ropes, SPEARS REAL DEAL!!! (POP AS BLAZE COMES DOWN THE AISLE) WEST: O'Malley rolls out of the ring with McQueen, and the Tazmanian Devil is clearly hurt! DTMA: Blaze is calling Real Deal over to him?? .. .. .. (Back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: Blaze and Real Deal had a quick talk and left, walking to the back. The good news is that McQueen walked to the back under his own power. But as we will see later, the night still had much in store for these four men! Now lets take a look at the 24 Hour Title. Kevin Blair is the champ, or maybe Adam Jennings is? Blair was stripped of the title, then it was reinstated, then Jennings won a battle royal for the title. But Blair left the ring with the strap, and Kingston Cash said until Jennings can take it from Blair, Blair is still the champ. So a match was made, but would Blair let go of the title, and did Jennings need to pin Blair? Or just take the title? .. .. .. ======================== VWF 24 Hour Title Match KEVIN BLAIR © (vs) ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS ======================== SOUTHERNS: Jennings came to the ring, and we waited for Blair. And we waited. More waiting and still no champ. With no choice the ref declared the match a no contest. But Adam Jennings would not leave the ring. Jennings told the ref that he came to WSV.5 for a fight and he wanted a fight! And if Kevin Blair was afraid of him then let someone else come out and try his luck against The Hype! And again we waited. And we waited. Then Kingston Cash walked out onto the stage, smiling from ear to ear! Cash told Jennings that he found someone who would love a chance to step in the ring with Jennings, a man who Cash had just hired to be his personal bodyguard. Then from behind the curtains walked. . . G-GANGSTA!!!! The former 24 Hour Champion and one of the original VWF members walked to the ring. He slid in and he and Jennings went right after each other, neither man backing down! Gangsta and Jennings threw punches, elbows and kicks. But it was a low blow by Gangsta that allowed Gangsta the upper hand. Gangsta brought a chair into the ring, and then hit the QUEENS CRUSHA tombstone piledriver on the chair. The SOD then hit the ring, knocking down Cash on the way, but Gangsta disappeared into the crowd. But the damage was done. And G-Gangsta is back! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Dancing Wrestle Babes, then a fade to JD at the desk) SOUTHERNS: One of the big questions floating around the VWF is, what is Trinity? Or is Trinity a who? Since Orion came to the VWF he has tortured The Equalizer, Jason Storm and even Flash with this strange Trinity. Speaking of Storm and Flash, a few weeks ago they looked to be ready to tear each other apart, but recently they seem to have been helping each other. .. .. .. ============================= JASON STORM and FLASH (vs) ORION THE HUNTER and ORION #2 ============================= SOUTHERNS: Of course, this started as an all out brawl. But after things settled it was all Storm and Flash. Despite clear tensions between the two men they worked well as a team and seemed to want to make Orion #2 the subject of the majority of the beatdown. But the Orions were not looking to be pushed over and turned the tide on a few occasions, but it was still Storm and Flash who would retake the momentum. Storm with power moves and Flash with some high flying acrobatics. Lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: Storm has Orion #2 up, and drives him into the mat, a SIT OUT POWERSLAM! WEST: Except for the time when Orion was working on Flash, it has been Storm and Flash's match. DTMA: Storm tags in Flash, and the Bodzilla sends Orion #2 to the ropes! WEST: SAVATE KICK! DTMA: And what a clean sounding snap! WEST: Orion down off the apron! He is walking around to where Storm is! DTMA: And STORM MEETS HIM WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE!!!! WEST: Flash turns to see what is going on! DTMA: Orion #2 with a SCHOOLBOY!!!! ONE!!!!!!! HANDFUL OF TIGHTS!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> WEST: And the Orions pull out the win! DTMA: Storm is laying the beatdown on Orion! WEST: Flash up and he is off the ropes. .. . . SUICIDA PLANCHA ONTO STORM AND ORION!!! .. .. .. (CUT to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: All four men continued to brawl until Orion hit a HOTSHOT to Storm and then the two Orions beatdown Flash. Though they lost the match, Orion and Orion #2 walked away. Storm and Flash hobbled away, each man gazing intently on the other. Now lets take a trip to when the VWF did not have any controversy surrounding any of its titles.. .. . . .. . . Right! We have NEVER had a time like that, so why should now be any different. The past few weeks have seen singles matches looking to see who will walk into Vegas Violence as the champ and who as the challenger. Tonight the last eight entered into fatal four ways to see. .. .. .. ============================== THE EQUALIZER (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS (vs) THE PREDATOR KEVIN SIMMONS ============================== SOUTHERNS: Bass and Simmons came to the ring first and it was clear they would work as a team, at least until they could get rid of E and Caldwell. And that is what they did. After a short four man fight Bass knocked The Equalizer out of the ring and the SOD members began to double team Caldwell. Though Mr Electricity has looked great in past weeks it was clear he was not going to able to fight off both Bass and Simmons. Simmons hit a FLAPJACK followed by a LEGDROP from Bass and it looked very bad for Caldwell. Bass moved to meet The Equalizer who was returning to the ring and Simmons made the cover. But Simmons suddenly jumped up and rolled out the ring, looking like he was having some kind of convulsions. Bass used a right hand to slow The Equalizer and he turned and got what Simmons had just got! A TAZER! Mr Electricity used a tazer to drop Bass to the mat! But the ref saw it and quickly disqualified Caldwell! As the ref pushed Caldwell out of the ring, The Equalizer made his way in and covered Bass. The ref turned and made a very quick count, and Bass was eliminated. The Equalizer then moved to the outside to handle Kevin Simmons. But the excitement was not over regarding Caldwell and Bass. As The Outlaw was lying on the floor Caldwell returned to apply the TAZER once again!!! Simmons was on his feet taking some brutal blows from The Equalizer when he hit a QUICK SPINEBUSTER on E and then went over to chase off Caldwell! But Simmons was knocked over from behind by The Equalizer and sent head first into the ring steps, busting open the head of Simmons. Kevin Simmons was tossed back in the ring by The Equalizer and the brutalization continued. Like a shark, the smell of the blood inspired The Equalizer. The Equalizer hit a VIOLENT CRADLE PILEDRIVER that looked like it would end the match but Simmons managed to get a foot on the ropes. The Equalizer then used a COBRA CLUTCH for further weaken Simmons. But Simmons did not tap or drop his hand three times so The Equalizer turned it into a suplex! E then looked to send Simmons to the ropes, but Simmons reversed and hit THE PREDATOR POUNCE!!!! The tide had been turned. Simmons, also seemingly inspired by his own blood, went to work on The Equalizer. Seeing as how turn about is fair play (whatever the hell that means) Simmons sent The Equalizer between the top and second ropes into the ring post. Giving us TWO bloody guys! Simmons wasted no time in bring E back in the ring and hitting THE PREDATOR POUNCE a second time, making the cover, but only getting a two count! Lets pick it up as Simmons has just hit a BACK BODY DROP on The Equalizer. .. .. .. WEST: These two men are animals! DTMA: Simmons wants to end this, he has that look in his eye! WEST: The winner will main event Vegas Violence, and that means serious cash. That's Simmons entrance music! WEST: ITS LINDSAY SIMMONS! LINDSAY SIMMONS IS ON THE RAMP!!! DTMA: With Iyasu Saku!!!!! WEST: Kevin Simmons cannot believe his eyes! DTMA: The Equalizer is on his feet! Rumors in the back say that Lindsay Simmons might be aligned in some way with the New Age Army, but we cannot confirm those. Well The Equalizer will be the challenger, but who will be the champion? .. .. .. ============================= VWF Heavyweight Title Match LARK FENRIZ © (vs) CLASSY JOEY BOY (vs) SEBASTIAN (vs) THE ASSASSIN MARCUS O'MALLEY ============================== SOUTHERNS: Remember earlier when we said that Blaze, Real Deal, O'Malley and McQueen would be back? With all four men in the ring bell ready to ring, the screens came alive with Blaze and Real Deal and a very injured Mark McQueen. Blaze told O'Malley that they had kidnapped McQueen, and were going to beat him senseless. Real Deal CHOKESLAMMED McQueen (fourth for the night) onto the concrete of wherever the hell they were to emphasize his point. But Blaze said they would let McQueen go, if he eliminated himself from the fatal four way! O'Malley did not hesitate and jumped over the top rope and headed into the back. The ref counted him out and the other three men began! CJB ducked a Fenriz punch and then nailed the champ with a solid right hand. That is when CJB offered his hand to Sebastian! That's right! CJB and Sebastian shook hands in the center of the ring and then began a double team of Fenriz! The fans were shocked that CJB would align himself with Sebastian! CJB and Sebastian hit a DOUBLE FLAPJACK on Fenriz and then took turns. CJB with a SPINNING HELL KICK! Sebastian with a BACKBREAKER! CJB followed with a MOONSAULT! Sebastian with a SECOND ROPE FIST DROP! And so on and so on. Fenriz battled back, ducking them and hitting a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE at one point. He used a DEATH VALLEY DRIVER to really slow CJB and then a POWERBOMB on Sebastian and nearly got the fall on the Legend Killer. As Sebastian got to his feet, Fenriz came off the ropes! And Sebastian hit his TILT A WHIRL PILEDRIVER!!!! (REF BUMP!) That's right, Fenriz hit the ref as he was twirling through the air. CJB and Sebastian (once called the Sex Icons) then really went to work. After some basic beat down tactics, Sebastian wheel barrowed Fenriz as CJB hit a TOP ROPE LEGDROP! But it was CJB who got out of the ring and pulled out his SLEDGEHAMMER! Fenriz then took three SLEDGEHAMMER SHOTS to the gut as Sebastian stood by and laughed. Then CJB handed the sledgehammer to Sebastian who was set to drop it on the ANKLE of Fenriz, when Sebastian noticed the ref starting to get up. Sebastian called over CJB, turned him, and then handed him the SLEDGEHAMMER!!! The ref got to his feet, and Sebastian began to tell the ref how CJB was using the SLEDGEHAMMER!!! And the ref, DISQUALIFIED CLASSY JOEY BOY!!!! With Sebastian laughing in his face, CJB had no choice, and he drilled Sebastian in the GUT with the HAMMER, then came off the ropes and nailed Sebastian with the KISS OF DEATH!!!! The ref got CJB out of the ring, leaving Sebastian and Fenriz lying in the ring, neither man moving. Fenriz began to get up and covered Sebastian, but did not get the fall. Lark pulled up Sebastian and covered him after a SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE, but still no fall. Lets pick it up as Fenriz is sending Sebastian to the ropes. .. .. .. DTMA: Here comes Sebastian! WEST: SPEAR!!! Sebastian caught Fenriz in the gut! DTMA: Fenriz might have a broken rib, he has taken a lot of punishment to the midsection. WEST: Sebastian pulls him up. .. . . ABDOMINAL STRETCH!!!!!!!!!!! DTMA: And look at Fenriz! He is in some real pain! WEST: He is coughing up blood! THE CHAMP IS COUGHING UP BLOOD!!! DMTA: Sebastian is on his way I think! Fenriz is really hurt! WEST: I think so to, he outsmarted CJB and Sebastian is on his way to the title! DTMA: HIP TOSS BY FENRIZ!!!!! (WHERE DID HE PULL THAT FROM POP!!!) WEST: Sebastian up! And he is as shocked as the rest of us! DTMA: Sebastian coming off the ropes. . . .. . . SAMMAEL!!!!!!!!! WEST: FENRIZ GOT HIM!!!! OUT OF NO WHERE!!!! DTMA: ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: The winner, and still VWF heavyweight champion, LARK FENRIZ!!! .. .. .. (CUT back to Southerns) SOUTHERNS: With the match over, CJB returned to the ring and went right after Sebastian! Classy Joey Boy hit Sebastian with the KISS OF DEATH, the came off the top rope with a huge STUDD SPLASH!!! But as CJB got to his feet, he and Fenriz ended up face to face, eye to eye in the center of the ring. And that is where they stood, both men staring at each other, until VWF officials hit the ring and pulled them apart. But Lark Fenriz has a new man to worry about, The Equalizer will be his opponent at Vegas Violence! Tune in to WSV.6 on September 28!
