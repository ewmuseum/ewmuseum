Spade-Fuller and whoever wins this Battle Royal.

not sure if he's gonna turn...

Zell Strife, he just isn't 24 hour material. He needs to keep the mid-carders and other fools happy. Tonight really wont be that much of a challenge. I'll take on whichever fool wins the Battle Royal and then kick their ass, keep my title, go backstage, bang the tits off some other chick and drink a few beers!
