CJB is back in the VWF! I am the best wrestler in the world today, so consider this a lesson. Consider this my teaching you. At the end of the match their will be a short quiz. (drops the mic) .. .. .. O'Malley and McQueen hit the ring and tore into Blaze and Real Deal. O'Malley sent Blaze through the ropes, then picked up the seven footer and nailed him with a SPINEBUSTER! Lets go to the call. .. .. .. DTMA: McQueen up, he goes to the ropes! .. . . MOONSAULT OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE FLOOR!!!! WEST: And he landed on top of Blaze!!!! (SOD! SOD! SOD! SOD!) DTMA: O'Malley has Real Deal in a COBRA CLUTCH!!!! <<< BELL RINGS >>> (MASSIVE POP!!!) But on this night it was the Submission Title up for grabs. .. .. .. ========================== VWF Submission Title Match THE MAQUILLADORA © (vs) SEBASTIAN =========================== SOUTHERNS: These two had some great exchanges in the early part of this match trying to gain the upper hand. But it was The Maquilladora who hit Sebastian with a BACK BODY DROP that gave Maq the edge. Sebastian hit the fan odd and it looked like he really did some damage to his lower back. Maq began a series of BACBREAKERS, SPINEBUSTERS and a VERTICAL SUPLEX. The Maquilladora was moving the pace of the match faster and faster. Maq caught Sebastian with a near perfect TWISTING PLANCHA and then Maq tried to lock on the BOW AND ARROW but Sebastian rolled away and to the outside of the ring. The Maquilladora went to the top rope, leaped to the floor and hit Sebastian with an ELBOW DROP. That's is when Sonicman made his way to the ring. The building gasped since Sonic has just come back into the Rhombus in recent days. The Maquilladora rolled into the ring to break the count, and, well, lets take a look. .. .. .. DTMA: Sonicman pulling something out of his trunks! (ROLL O'QUARTERS POP!!!) WEST: AND HE BLASTED SEBASTIAN!!! DTMA: I think Sebastian has been knocked out! WEST: But Sebastian is unconscious! <<< BELL RINGS >>> DTMA: And the ref calls for the bell, seeing that Sebastian cannot go on! RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match and still VWF Submission Champion, THE MAQUILLADORA!!! .. .. .. (Back to JD) SOUTHERNS: As Maq realized that Sonic helped him they seemed to have some words after the match but things cleared up fast between the two old friends and the fans were happy to see them in the ring. Sonic remained in the ring as he was set to meet the Adam Jennings. .. .. .. ====================== SONICMAN (vs) THE HYPE ADAM JENNINGS ====================== SOUTHERNS: Jennings, the newest SOD member looked very good from the start. But Jennings hit a BACK BODY DROP to get out of the DDT and then strutted around the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Sonic dropkicked him through the ropes but once back in the ring Jennings was back on top. Lets go to the end of this match up! .. .. .. WEST: Jennings has been dominate in this match! DTMA: He sends Sonic to the rope, Sonic flips over the back body attempt! WEST: DROPKICK! Sonicman nailed Jennings! DTMA: And Sonic heading to the top rope! .. . . SONIC SPLASH!!!! Cover.. ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!! JENNINGS KICKS OUT!!!!!!!!!!!! WEST: Sonicman is known for this kind of win, sudden and out of no where! DTMA: He signals the crowd! Stands up Jennings KICK TO THE GUT! WEST: And Sonicman taps! <<< BELL RINGS >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match by submission, THE HYPE! ADAM JENNINGS! .. .. .. And the stakes couldn't be higher. If Kagedo wins, he is Kingston Cash's new partner in the VWF! But if Cash wins, Kagedo is suspended without pay for six months! To make things more interesting, the NAA did some serious damage to The Dragon's surgically repaired right knee last week. Was the damage enough to give the boss the edge he needed? Here's what happened. - - - (The crowd begins to boo and jeer as Money hits the speakers in the arena, and the current 75% owner of the VWF makes his way to the ring....) West: And here he comes! The man who rid the VWF of that deadweight bunch, the NAA last week and will start getting rid of these Society thugs tonight! DTMA: I'm not so sure about that. Kagedo is one of the most determined competitors in the game today. But with the damage done to that surgically repaired knee, you would definitely have to give the edge to to Mr Cash here tonight. (The boss climbs into the ring, looking cocky and calls for the microphone. The crowd's jeers become even more intense as he starts to speak...) Cash: I've just been informed that everyone's so-called hero Kagedo had a very serious accident earlier today and won't be able to compete tonight. It seems he was in the gym training and someone overloaded the leg press he was using. The trainer said his participation in tonight's match could only do further damage to his knee, and could possibly end up putting him in a wheelchair for the rest of his life. DTMA: Dammit! Now, referee, get your ass in here and declare me the.... (Before he can finish his statement, the light go out and an eerie crimson glow floods the arena. In the now still and deafening silence, a lonely bell rings....) (Bong) (Bong) (Bong) (Hell's Bells begins to play and the crowd erupts in a rabid cheer as a lone figure emerges from the backstage area, limping slightly and making his way slowly towards the ring...) I'm glad he's wearing dark trunks!! - - - (Cut back to Southerns) Southerns: It was a brutal match, one that looked like Kagedo was going to dominate early. But the weakness of that knee finally caught up to him and Mr Cash's recent training efforts became clear as he was able to hang with The Dragon for the majority of what turned out to be a classic confrontation. But in the end, it was the ring savvy of Kagedo that paid off and gave him the upper hand to set up one of the most bizarre finishes to a match I have ever seen. DTMA: Say what you will about his wrestling desires, the fact remains that he literally has taken Kagedo to the limits here, thanks in great part to that now badly injured knee. West: So what's he doing? He's picking Cash up? DTMA: Oh my god! He's setting him up on the top rope. This doesn't look good for the boss one bit! West: He's not... DTMA: He is!!! West: But did you see how he slipped?? That look on his face right as he started the dragon suplex! He looked like he'd seen his own worst fears!! DTMA: Be that as it may, this match is now over and Mr Cash is being awarded the win. It looks like Kagedo is going to need every second of the next six months to rehab this injury. West: If rehab is even possible! Kagedo may be crippled for life now! HAHA! That's a great way to start getting rid of these Society thugs. - - - (Cut back to the VWF desk) Southerns: So Mr Cash sneaks in with a win here, and Kagedo is on the shelf for at least six months.
