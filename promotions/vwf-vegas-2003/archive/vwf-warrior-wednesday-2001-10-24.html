Warrior Wednesday, 24 October 2001

24 October 2001

WARRIOR WEDNESDAY OCTOBER 24, 2001 *The Prince Of Darkness Theme engulfs the crowd as Dage walks down the VWF ramp and over the top rope into the ring. He grabs a mic.* Dage: Well, Well, Well...no one ever saw what happened last week coming. Jack Spade and Dage forming a team.
The most powerful forces in the world unite. It's like it is right outta the bible. God and Satan coming together to takeover the world. This is exactly the same thing except we are going to takeover VWF and destroy RCW and BWF.
*Crowd screams as chants of DAGE SUCKS and EOV, EOV, EOV mix together.* Dage: We are the elite force now in VWF and the VWF president should be grateful we picked to stay here in
VWF instead of joining RCW or BWF. Wait a minute...he shouldn't just be grateful...no he should be kissing our ass!
*Crowd Boos.* Dage: Yes he should because without us the VWF would be taken over by either RCW or BWF. I mean I doubt BWF could ever takeover anything over with the exception of a gay man. That is exactly what BWF is...Beautiful
Wrestling Federation...a bunch of gays that like to wrestle around with straight men hoping they will turn. Well thats just nasty and EOV is gonna change that. We are the ELEMENTS OF VIOLENCE and we don't deal with little pink tights, and dances cocky around the ring. We kick ass, drink some beer, and do whatever we feel like doing! If any piece of crap wants to try to stop us, you know where we are.
*Crowd cheers* Dage: Speaking of crap...RCW's Demonic and Blaze are the first two victims tonight...no wait I mean opponents tonight of the EOV and how right is it that our first Tag match together is for the VWF Tag Team
Titles. Demonic...Blaze...I'm sorry your title run had to end this way because ours is beginning later tonight and not ending for a very longtime. I can guarantee that. Get ready for the Elements Of
Violence... *Dage drops the mic and leaves the ring as EOV chants are heard throughout the arena.* (Viva Las Vegas begins to play and the VWF blazes onto the screen) DAN THE MAN ALLER: Welcome to the MGM Arena and Warrior Wednesday! Due to time constraints we will have only two live matches tonight but we will show you highlights from the other matches.
And tonight will be the announcment of the card for next weeks PPV PHEAR PHANTASTIC!!!! G-Gangsta challenged Jack Spade.. The Legend of Xtreme challenged G-Gangsta and Ric Beauty challenged Kerry Powell to just show up at the PPV.
I hear that Ric and Rob Beauty have come to some terms to make this
PPV big, with a massive main event!
I can't wait to hear from the VWF President Rob Beauty later tonight. Earlier tonight members of a couple of VWF tag teams met in singles action. *************************************** EARTHQUAKE vs PADDY O'SHEA *************************************** The fans enjoyed this match but it was mostly comic. Earthquake left the ring to eat two hot dogs, then some nachos, and a bag of popcorn while O'Shea took these chances to grab a beer. Once they got in the ring Earthquake took and control and the smaller O'Shea struggled.
Paddy looked good in a few spots with some strong power moves but
Earthquake was just to big.
WINNER- Earthquake with a Cheeseburger for the pinfall. After the match O'Sheas partner Dirk Western rushed the ring with a chair. The Vegas Badasses pummelled Earthquake into a bloody spot in the center of the ring. A cut to backstage saw Chumpy hog tied in the dressing room of The VBA.
Earthquake scored the pin but the VBA got the better of Triple X. I smell a PPV match in the making. Next up was a tag team battle. ********************************************************* THE HUXTABLES vs TAI ANGELA and BLACKBIRD ********************************************************* Huey wanted nothing to do with The Dark Monster and every time Tai tagged in Huey tagged out. Henry is far to old to fight with Tai and it showed. Finally Huey and Tai were in the ring together and the fans got behind Huey with a chant of HUEY HUEY.
The big man seemed to respond and took the fight to Angela. An accidental tag brought Blackbird in and Huey hit a powerbomb and tagged to his dad.
Henry came in and nailed a piledriver on Blackbird and made the pin. Tai went for the save but was to late. WINNER- The Huxtables as Henry pinned Blackbird The fans at the MGM went crazy as The Game hit and The Legend of
Xtreme came out to the ring carrying a chair with a picture of
G-Gangsta on it. Gangstas Paradise hit and G-Gangsta rushed to the ring to take the fight to The Legend. *************************************************************************** BWF TITLE MATCH COREY FULLER (C) vs G-GANGSTA vs THE LEGEND OF XTREME *************************************************************************** As G and The Legend crushed each other with power moves Corey Fuller made his way to the ring.
Fuller joined in the match and a full on three way war was on! Corey Fuller and G-Gangsta put on one hell after the former T3 Champion Gangsta tossed The Legend out of the ring. But when the Legend came back he had the chair and nailed G-Gangsta with it. The ref quickly DQ'ed The Legend. Fuller took advantage of G-Gangsta and nailed THE FULLER DROP. But........ Just when it looked like Fuller had made the pin Sebastian came out of the crowd and pulled Fuller off G-Gangsta. Sebastian hit a DEATH
VALLEY DRIVER to Fuller on the outside of the ring. He rolled Fuller back on the ring and the battle continued.
G-Gangsta looked good as he took over. Fuller made a come back but
Sebastian was still hanging around.
That is when Zell Strife made his way to the ring and began to battle with Sebastian. Fuller nails the Fuller Drop AGAIN! on G-Gangsta. But instead of going for the pin he heads out of the ring to help
Strife beat up Sebastian.
Strife goes for a SUPERKICK but misses and hits Fuller sending him back into the ring! G-Gangsta rolls over and makes the pin! WINNER and NEW BWF CHAMPION- G-GANGSTA with a pinfall. But as G-Gangsta and his manager U-Trop were celebrating in the ring
Ric Beauty led Fuller, Angela and The VBA into the ring to beat down
RICS champ G-Gangsta.
*---COMMERCIAL---* DAN THE MAN ALLER: Fans are excited here and well they should be! G-Gangsta is the NEW BWF Champ and what about Fuller and Strife? The top names in the BWF seem to be heading toward a battle.
Next up we have a big one! The first defense of the VWF tag team titles. ************************************************************ VWF TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH DEMONIC and BLAZE (C) vs JACK SPADE and DAGE ************************************************************ Spade and Dage are Elements of Violence and have battled Demonic and
Blaze in the past, this should be great.
Bodies by Drowning Pool plays as Dage walks slowly out from the back. He has his head down, and stands at the top of the ramp...then he throws his fist into the air and bolts his head up to the crowd.
After he walks into the ring over the top rope. Dage is making moves here in the VWF and is a rising star, and teaming with the 24 Hour Champion Spade makes him even more appealing.
(The lights flicker a red colour and the opening riff of Ace of Spades by Motorhead begins to play. The crowd let out a good cheer as "The Ace" Jack Spade swaggers out onto the ramp with an arrogant smile on his face.) The fans here in Vegas have taken to Spade and he has been a great
Champion. [F***in Wit D hits as Demonic, Blaze walk out with RCW jerseys and jeans on] Demonic and Blaze are in the ring and the battle is on! Spade is head to head with Blaze and Demonic and Dage are renewing the fight they started last week in the battle royal.
Spade tosses Blaze over the top rope and the match is set. Dage punches Demonic back into the corner and Dage jumps up on the ropes and BULLDOG! Dage is taking the battle to Demonic and the RCW. Dage with an Irish Whip and CLOTHESLINE! Tag and Jack Spade the 24 Hour Champ is in the ring! Spade with a DDT! Spade climbs up on the second rope and drop a big fist to the face of
Demonic. Jack Spade is showing why he holds the big belt here in Las Vegas. He pulls Demonic to his feet and kick to the gut, setting him up for the piledriver.
Demonic picks him up! Demonic sets Spade on the top rope, and steps back. Spade goes for the bodyblock! REVERSAL! Demonic just caught Spade and nailed him with a FULL BODY SLAM! Demonic tags Blaze. Blaze comes in and is all over Spade. Blaze is just ripping into Spade. Blaze makes the tag and holds Spade open for a kidney punch from
Demonic. Spade to the ropes and CLOTHESLINE and Spade flops around like a rag doll. Another quick tag and Blaze comes in, Demonic sets Spade up in a piledriver position, PILEDRIVER and then an second rope elbow from
Blaze.
1.....2 Cant get the 24 Hour Champion that easy! But Spade is in trouble. The Tag Champs are working him over. Blaze has Spade down in what looks like a full nelson. Spade needs to get out of this FAST!
Dage comes in but the ref is pushing back to his corner. Demonic and Blaze switch and Demonic has Spade and CHOKESLAM! 1.....2......... Closer but not the fall! Jack Spade looks bad. He needs to make the tag. Demonic tags and DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE and Spade goes over the top rope! Demonic is mocking Dage! Blaze leans over and Spade slams Blaze with a right hand! Spade is up on the apron, jumps on the ropes and FLYING FOREARM to
Demonic! The fans are chanting ACE ACE!!!! Spade leaps over and tags in Dage. Down goes Demonic! Down goes Blaze! Up comes Demonic and back down he goes! Up comes Blaze and back down he goes! Demonic and Blaze roll out of the ring. Dage off the ropes, OVER THE
TOP ROPE FLYING BODYPRESS and takes both men down!
Dage with a DROPKICK and Blaze goes back into the ring! Dage has Demonic on the outside and REVOLUTION! Dage is on top of Demonic outside of the ring! There is the bell! What the hell? Did Dage score a pin outside the ring. REF: Winners of this match... as the result of a count out (oh no pop) Demonic and Blaze! DAN THE MAN ALLER: Damn! *---COMMERCIAL---* (Walking along the Strip we see Lean Darlene) LEAN DARLENE: Hi Dan! DAN THE MAN ALLER: Darlene? What are you doing? LD: I am looking for Kerry Powell. DA: You think he is going to show up at the PPV. LD: I really hope so. The BWF is falling apart, Strife cost Fuller the title, Sebastian doesn't like any of them, this is the best time for
Powell to take over.
DA: But he has to show up, in the ring. LD: He will, I will find him. DA: I think Darlene is far to optimistic. Strife put the hurt on Kerry
Powell on we haven't seen him since.
Speaking of Zell Strife, the T3 Champ makes a defense next. *********************************************************** T3 TITLE MATCH ZELL STRIFE (C) vs SEBASTIAN vs THE REAL DEAL *********************************************************** (F***cking With D hits and The Real Deal makes his way to the ring) This RCW star has been overlooked here in the VWF but I think he cannot be ignored much longer. Maybe tonight is the night he straps a belt around his waist.
DA: Making his way to the ring, with a smirk and smile on his face from Los Angeles...at 6 foot 4, 240 pounds.....SEBASTIAN.
(Sebastian steps into the ring and bows to each side as I'm To Sexy plays in the background)
SEBASTIAN: Remember you losers! I am Sebastian and you are NOT! (mixed crowd reaction) Sebastian seems to be hated for the most part but fans love to see him mess with Fuller and Strife.
There go the lights! Fall Children by AFI starts with thick fog pouring through the ramp way, green smoke fills the air. Lights start to flash and fade while a large
Anarchy A lazers throughout the ring. Zell bursts from the curtains and runs to the ring with open arms to the fans.
Strife is standing on the apron and staring at Sebastian! The Real
Deal is being ignored by these two BWF stars.
No respect for The Real Deal! And his is SEVEN FEET TALL! The biggest man in the VWF. Real Deal comes up behind Sebastian and slams his left fist into the pretty face of Sebastian.
Strife is smiling! Real Deal comes over and SUPLEX! Strife is in the ring and in the action! RD has Sebastian in one hand and Strife in the other and HEADBUTTS them! Sebastian rolls out. RD focuses on Zell Strife, he is the man with the belt. And RD is choking him! (good crowd reaction for RD) Strife is struggling. And RD just tosses him across the ring. As Real Deal gets closer Strife leaps and HEADBUTT TO THE GUT and doubles over RD! Strife with some elbows to the back to keep Real Deal doubled over. And DDT! But I don't think Real Deal is to hurt by it. Strife goes for the cover...... 1..... Sebastian on the top rope KICKOUT by Real Deal.... Seabastian misses a SPLASH! Real Deal throws Strife to the ropes and BACK BODY DROP on top of
Sebastian! Zell Strife and Sebastian are struggling against the bigger Real Deal. RD comes off the ropes and big splash on top off both men though
Strife got the worse of it!
The Real Deal is pulling Strife to his feet, has him by the throat and CHOKESLAAAAAAAM!!! 1 2 Broken up by Sebastian!?!?!??! Real Deal gets up and starts toward Sebastian....... Strife makes his way to his feet. It looks like Sebastian is drawing Real Deal away from Strife! Real Deal has Sebastian by the throat and Strife nails Real Deal from behind! Now Sebastian and Strife and beating down The Real Deal!!!!! Sebastian signals to Strife for the Porphyria!? Sebastian is helping Strife get the big man up and PORPHYRIA! 1....2....THREE!!!! The Real Deal is out! And Sebastian is walking away!?!?! What in the hell is going on? Is Sebastian a good boy again? Is he Ric Beautys lackey again? Strife is standing in the ring, confused. The ref rings the bell as Sebastian has been counted out! Youre winner and still T3 Champion ZELL STRIFE! *---Commercial---* (We see Rob Beauty standing in the center of the ring with a PHEAR
PHANTASTIC PPV t-shirt on)
ROB BEAUTY: Ladies and gentlemen, I am Rob Beauty and I am here to announce the line up for next weeks PPV!
PHEARRRRRRR PHANNNNNNNNTAAAAAAAAASSSSSSTIC! First up we will have two dark matches. THOMAS RAINKA against ANXIETY DISORDER! and the return of BOBBY SILAS against newcome r THE GHOST! The winners of these matches will meet on the PPV card! Opening the PPV will be tag team action, winners getting a title shot! TRIPLE X against THE VEGAS BADASSES! (mild reaction) Next up will be the match between the winners of the dark matches! And then will be the match that I know will happen, even though my brother thinks it wont! KERRY POWELL against RIC BEAUTY and if Powell just makes it to the ring, he will be BWF leader until 2002. (we hope Kerry shows up reaction) Then a big battle HUEY HUXTABLE goes up against TAI ANGELA (better reaction) And for the main event...... are you ready? I said ARE YOU READY!!!!!!!!!!!!!???????!!!!!!! A ten man match! Five titles on the line! All inside a TWO STORY CAGE! RAGE IN THE CAGE!!!!!! 24 Hour Champion Jack Spade! T3 Champion Zell Strife! The Tag Team Champs Demonic and Blaze BWF Champ G-Gangsta! and Corey Fuller, The Real Deal, Dage, Sebastian and The Legend of
Xtreme! It will work like this! No Count outs, no DQ's! Only pinfalls and submissions! Once a pinfall happens the pinned man and the man who pins are done in the match! This way no one man will leave with all the titles! All the titles are on the line! There will be 5 pinfalls and or submissions! No rules! Hardcore! All at Sam Boyd Stadium next week!
