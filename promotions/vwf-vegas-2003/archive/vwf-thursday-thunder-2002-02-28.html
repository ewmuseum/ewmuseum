(A man with short black hair walks the streets of Regina, hands in his pockets, head down to the ground. He walks as if he hears nobody, he walks into the people of the city and doesn't budge. I haven't seen myself in the mirror for centuries, I haven't even known where I was for Millions of years, but what is time when it doesn't matter to you..... Time is the enemy when it keeps you in suspense.... From now on I vow to never let it affect me..... I plan on returning home, I plan on looking to the shores and it's evil beauty, I plan on going to the forests where evil is the only presence felt.... I plan on turning this goody fed, back to it's roots of pain, suffering and blood loss..... it's time that I speak once more and this is just the first of many.... prepare to die..... and you my good man, shall be the first......
