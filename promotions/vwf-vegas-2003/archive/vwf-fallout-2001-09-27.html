Fallout: The VWF Update, 27 September 2001

27 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again another great effort for the VWF. Ok, enough ass kissing but seriously thanks to everyone.
PRESS Ric Beauty announced that he will crown a BWF Champion on Warrior
Wednesday! Ric will be the referee in a match between Corey Fuller and
Sebastian. Ric says he does not recognize the 24 Hour Title or the T3
Title and says the winner will be the REAL man of the VWF!
Rob Beauty announced that on the next 2 Warrior Wednesdays a Tag Team
Title Tournament will be held!
UPCOMING EVENTS WARRIOR WEDNESDAY OCTOBER 3, 2001 TAG TEAM TITLE TOURNAMENT MATCHES (Winners of these matches will move to the finals next week) Triple X (vs) Faith & Anarchy X The Vegas Badasses (vs) Demonic & Blaze of the RCW SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCH (Two seven footers clash in the ring as its VWF vs RCW) Huey Huxtable (vs) The Real Deal of the RCW BWF TITLE MATCH (Ric Beauty will crown the BWF Champ, but Rob and the VWF will not recognize the winner of this match)
Corey Fuller (vs) Sebastian FOUR WAY DOUBLE DEFENSE MATCH (Both VWF Titles are on the line in this match, four man first man to score a pin is the winner)
The Ace Jack Spade 24© (vs) G-Gangsta T3© (vs) Zell Strife (vs) Tai Angela RANKINGS 24 HOUR CHAMPION- The Ace Jack Spade (VWF) T3 CHAMPION- G-Gangsta (VWF) 1) Corey Fuller (BWF) 2) Demonic (RCW) 3) Zell Strife (???) 4) Kerry Powell (VWF) 5) Sebastian (BWF) SHORT LISTS VWF The Ace Jack Spade 24 Karat Kerry Powell Huey Huxtable BWF Corey Fuller Sebastian RCW Demonic Blaze The Real Deal RUMORS The Vegas Badasses will make a decision as to who they are with in the
Internal Strife struggle for the VWF. Sources close to the VBA say they are leaning toward the BWF.
Zell Strife has yet to make his thoughts known about the battles inside the VWF. Was Paddy O'Shea arrested and sent to a detox unit in North Las Vegas? VWF reporter Lean Darlene is looking into this story.
