Yak- You mean about CAPSMAN and Sonicman. Maq- Yep, see my people, CAPSMAN disappeared a long time ago. Long enough to get say a job in advertising... Yak- Then low and behold...a new face on TV called Sonicman. Looks like CAPSMAN and sounds like CAPSMAN. Maq- So it HAS to be CAPSMAN. We just need some hard evidence and we'll prove to everyone...we'll prove...that...uh...ah shit! (Maq frowns confused and frustrated.) Yak- That Sonicman is CAPSMAN. Maq- Exactly...thanks for the assist man. We'll get back to that when I'm done kicking Johnny Cage's Mortal Kombat ass all over the ring. (Maq and Yak walk away talking) Yak- What out for the nut punch though. Maq- Yeah...that cheap bastard. Lets go to action from earlier tonight! ************************************* THE MAQUILLADORA vs XTREME TONY CAGE ************************************* Cage was coming off a big win on Monday against Anxiety Disorder. Maq scored a W against The Ghost last week and the winner would get a chance at the T3 Title holder Erik Andrews. Tony Cage hit a swinging neckbreaker early that had Maq rocked. Maq then nailed Cage with a series of kicks that had the Tony in trouble. Maq then hit a springboard moonsault and it looked like it was over for Cage. In an odd scene, G-Gangsta and Sebastian worked over Jack Spade. Finally, Dage got free from his dressing room and hit the ring running off G-Gangsta nad hitting Sebastian with the Revolution.
