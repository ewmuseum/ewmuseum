Vegas Wrestling Federation Update, 24 January 2002

24 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Update NEWS The big news is DAGE took the 24 Hour Title from DEMONIC but will have to meet his rival in only SIX days at Ghost Town Blues! CJB has set the rules for his match with Tai Angela, No Holds Barred, pinfall or submission OUTSIDE the ring! And maybe the biggest news was the melee at UMC medical center. ZELL STRIFE and THOMAS RAINKA broke up much of the emergency room and then they were joined by ERIK ANDREWS and TONY CAGE who fought in the parking lot! All four VWF Superstar were arrested and taken to county jail! VWF President Rob Beauty said that he expects all four men to be out in time for Ghost Town Blues. Speaking of Ghost Town Blues, here is the lineup! GHOST TOWN BLUES from Rhyolite Nevada (an old ghost town) Wednesday January 30, 2002 *****24 HOUR TITLE MATCH***** DAGE (C) vs DEMONIC (Pitz of Hell Rules (inside the old silver mines in Rhyolite) If they get out of jail.... *****T3 TITLE MATCH***** ERIK ANDREWS (C) vs TONY CAGE and ZELL STRIFE vs THOMAS RAINKA (Special rules! This will be two matches held at the same time! Andrews vs Cage and Strife vs Rainka, the two winner will then meet, last man is the T3 Champ) *****BLACKJACK TITLE MATCH***** CLASSY JOEY BOY (C) vs TAI ANGELA (No hold barred, pinfalls and submissions only count outside of the ring) *****TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH***** BLAZE and REAL DEAL (C) vs TRIPLE X (texas tornado rules) *****RCW TITLE MATCH***** G-GANGSTA (C) vs THE ACE JACK SPADE (2 of 3 falls, Midtown Madness. Match will be held in the old ghost town itself) **SPECIAL CHALLENGE** THE MAQUILLADORA vs SEBASTIAN (Maq pinned the 2 time 24 Hour Champ and he wants revenge) 28TH STREET vs THE HUXTABLES (Huey and Henry Huxtable return to the VWF) Also, Ric Beauty will be at ringside with Dan The Man Aller! ---------------------------- OOC- There will be a FLASH show on Monday. The last War of Words before the PPV!
