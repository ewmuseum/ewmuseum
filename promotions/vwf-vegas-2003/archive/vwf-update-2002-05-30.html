Vegas Wrestling Federation Update, 30 May 2002

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE VWF UPDATE www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/vegaswrestlingfed NEWS ----- CJB and Sebastian, The Sex Icons did a number on 24 Hour Champion G-Gangsta and BlackJack contender Tyson Young. Zell Strife got set up and beat down by Rob Beauty and the man who funds the VWF casino owner Blackjack. Randall Crane got what he wanted, only one opponent at Paint The Town Red, he got it at the expense of TRD. Also, the VWF is sad to report that The Real Deal will ne leaving the VWF. TRD has been a great competitor and there will always be a place for The Real Deal if he chooses to return, so Put That In Your Pants and Play With It! ------------ PAINT THE TOWN RED, PAY PER VIEW! JUNE 5 From The Parking Lot of The Hard Rock Casino! How big can it get!? This just might be it! The rumormill says that there may be more matches on the card that could be announced on Mondays War of Words show! But here is what is signed for sure! ***A TEXAS TORNADO MATCH*** The FIGHTING O'SHEAS get back in the ring with ABSOLUTE ARROGANCE ERIK ANDREWS and THOMAS RAINKA! ***WINNER TO GUEST REF THE STRIFE/BEAUTY MATCH*** He has finally shown himself, BLACKJACK is the man behind the money in the VWF and he meets up with CLOUD KINNEAS! ***FIRST BLOOD THEN PINFALL OF SUBMISSION*** In a match where you can't make the pin until your opponent is bloody the President of the VWF ROB BEAUTY steps into the ring against former 24 Hour Champion ZELL STRIFE! But will the ref make a difference in this match? ***BLACKJACK TITLE MATCH, STREETFIGHT*** With special permission from Las Vegas Metro Police, CLASSY JOEY BOY and TYSON "T-BONE" YOUNG will battle for CJB's BlackJack Title on the street! Paradise Road will be the scene for this one with the parking lot of the Hard Rock on one side and Club Paradise strip club on the other!!!!!! ***T3 TITLE MATCH*** With this kind of animosity you don't need special rules, REVOLUTION RANDALL CRANE should have to defend the Triple Threat Title against two men but he got away with one, or did he, THE MAQUILLADORA gets a shot at Crane one on one! ***24 HOUR TITLE MATCH, RAGE IN THE CAGE*** Since August of 2001 these two men have stepped into the rings of the VWF and they do it once again on the main stage, inside the cage with barb wire ropes. G-GANGSTA gives SEBASTIAN his shot at the 24 Hour Title! ------- OTHER JUNK Look for a tag team tourney of some kind some time after the PPV. We have (and will have) a few teams so I think it will be good. Here are deadlines (I hate that word but you know what I mean) MONDAY WAR OF WORDS (by Monday during the day) PAINT THE TOWN RED JUNE 5 (have strats in-rings to me by June 4 if you can, thanks) And check out the website and thanks to CJB for all his work on it! -------- -rich
