Warrior Wednesday, 5 September 2001: Prelude to Vegas Violence

5 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WARRIOR WEDNESDAY September 5, 2001 Prelude to Vegas Violence PPV (The scene fades back to outside of the VWF arena. A black Dodge
Viper pulls
up with the windows down and Freebird by Lynard Skynard playing loudly out of the car. The car stops with the music and Jack Spade steps out.)
DA: JACK SPADE! JACK SPADE IS HERE! The man who will face Sebastian at Vegas Violence has arrived at the arena!
(Jack continues to walk towards the arena, he winks at the camera and we go back to ringside.)
(Viva Las Vegas plays and we move in on Dan "The Man" Aller and Lean
Darlene sitting at a desk)
DA: Welcome to Warrior Wednesday. We do have one match for you tonight but for the most part this is the Prelude to Vegas Violence on Friday night.
DARLENE: Yes! I heard tickets are almost sold out for the big event and it will be carried not only in all of Nevada but parts of
California, Utah and Arizona. Call your local cable operator! DA: And tonight we will be hearing from the stars of the VWF! DARLENE: But first, lets take a look at the match that was held earlier to decide the other wrestler in the Triple Threat Title Match.
DA: Roll it.... ****************************************************** FAITH (vs) HUEY HUXTABLE (vs) ANARCHY X ****************************************************** DA: Huey tried to make friends early in the match but it didn''t work out. The big man sat aside as Faith and Anarchy X battled, Faith scoring the pin with a beautiful moonsault. Huey then offered his hand to the Japanese Angel, but Hueys father stepped on the ring apron and
Faith moved toward him to knock him down and Huey finally wanted to fight. Huey, much bigger than Faith pounded him with elbows and fists until giving him a powerbomb followed by The Fall for the win.
DARLENE: So for the Triple Threat Title it will be Huey Huxtable against Bobby Silas and the Champ G-Gangsta.
DA: Three big men and two of them are pretty tough. Silas and G should stay away from the Hueys father Henry.
DARLENE: When Huey is mad he is tough to beat. DA: I would like to welcome President Rob Beauty to the show. (Rob walks in and sits down beside the lovely Darlene) DARLENE: Rob, you are here to give us the scoop on the other matches for Friday. ROB BEAUTY: Yes I am. The VWF is in the process of signing some big names and we hope they will be with us for Vegas Violence.
DA: Who? ROB BEAUTY: Can't say yet but some men who can make an impact. Anyway, since Huey Huxtable will be in the Triple Threat Title match. The
opening match for Vegas Violence will be FAITH (vs) ANARCHY X. DA: Both these guys have struggled. ROB BEAUTY: Faith is a tough man and I have high hopes for him but he has struggled, lets hope he and Anarchy X can get things warmed up for the crowd.
DARLENE: After their match will be the Taped Fists Match. O'Shea and
Western. DA: Lets roll some tape. ******************* PADDY O'SHEA ******************* (O' Shea is sitting on a bed in what looks like a shelter and Mr. Smith is standing at the end of the bed) MR SMITH: What kind of man would try to knock another man down? What kind of low down punk tries to take a man down in a way like Dirk
Western did?
I will tell you, a man who is lost. A fool who thinks his way to the top is in cheap theatrics at the expense of my man.
Dirk is such a tough man, he attacked me. Look at me. I am a small man, not a fighter. (O'Shea stands up) But Paddy is a real man, and he already has the win over Western but on Friday we play again. But this time....
OSHEA: It is under my rules. Dirk Western I was a boxer until my problems with the bottle took that away from me. I was taught by the best and fought the best. I can't wait until you hear the ten count. I can't wait to One Two your face. I was once like you, a beer drinking, rough housing fool.
MR SMITH: But I changed him. OSHEA: And Friday night Dirk it is time to start your recovery! ******************** (Cut back to the desk, Rob Beauty is no longer there.) DA: Strong words. DARLENE: O'Shea doesn't hide his former drinking problems, and Dirk took full advantage of that last week.
DA: Taped Fists, can't wait. DARLENE: Then comes the Loser Leaves Nevada match, Kerry Powell and
Ric Beauty. DA: The VWF is only about six weeks old but this war has been going on for a long time. Powell and Beauty headlined the UWC, and Powell and
Beauty battled in the old CEW in Florida.
DARLENE: They had a great match at a UWC pay per view where John Sax, the guest ref, screwed Ric out of the UWC heavyweight title.
DA: You know, Ric need to go to whatever federation he is in and keep his nose out of our business.
DARLENE: He has every right to step into the MGM, or any other venue in Las Vegas. DA: Not after Friday night. (Screen cuts to shots of Powell and Beauty) (Beauty stepping out of the ring at the UWC battle royal) (Powell holding up the UWC Title after his win over Ric at the UWC
Begining Of An Era PPV) (Powell jumping Beauty during a CEW match) (Powell pinning Sebastian as the Masked Man to win the 24 Hour Title, and the shock on Rics face)
(Jack Spade nailing Powell with the help of Sebastian) (Beauty laughing backstage with Sebastian, holding the 24 Hour Title) DA: Loser is gone from the VWF and Nevada. DARLENE: Should be one for the ages. Many VWF fans are looking forward to the Hellfire Match, Zell Strife and Tai Angela.
DA: A burning ring, a deep hatred. I call both these men freaks, they both look and act like rejects from an old B-movie but Zell has a wave behind him and he looks focused, lets take a look.
**************** ZELL STRIFE **************** (We enter upon a dark and abandoned area of forest, shrub, and rock... Slain bodies once spilled blood on this area with war and gore.. Cries still silently heard stray the area.. The surrounding inhabitants flee from the insanity....Zell
Strife walks up to a red rock and lays upon it.)
Zell) I can still even smell the blood spilled on this rock... The stained red left this sacred place a hellish nighmare for the folk nearby... I consider this a blessing to come to a place of death for strenght... guidance... a new sensation to kick someone's ass...Tai, or Taj or whatever you want to be called....
I have no need for your name, just your face... The stupid moronic look of an ignorant slob who needs to be slit in and out...gutted to perfection. For many nights as I fought, you have always found a way to distrupt me and have me out of play....This time things will not be taking so lightly... We are in a place of fire, a place of hell...
a caged like situation, where you cannot run... Only I will be with you to fight to the death... Your blood will be spilled on my rock, the ring... and they will flock to adore the fallen piece of human waste....
I really do hate telling you what i'm going to do to you, but really there is no other way of putting it... when you play with the fire inside your going to get burned and now your even deeper in problems because the ring will be set on fire...
Many times your back will hit those ropes and savage burns will welt your skin... but that's not the only pain you will receive...The pain that you are worthless and Zell
Strife will always own you and that you will never suceed as long as I am around... Death comes to us all and you will feel it.... soon enough... I hope you have a watch, so you can count the time you have left... For when we step in the ring, our battle field will become the war of gore and you will realize that you cannot defeat The
Anarchist, The Fire Inside, The Haunting, Zell Strife......
(Zell licks the rock and looks up to the sky... blood from Zell's mouth oozes out as he begins a strange ritual to prepare for his upcoming match.)
*************** DARLENE: Zell is the Haunting and he hopes to scare up a win against
Angela. DA: In true Tai Angela freak fashion he did not submit a tape or an interview for this program but he did send us this.
(Dan Aller holds up a box with a cows heart in it, someone has cut the words Zell Strife into the heart)
DARLENE: OOOOHHH gross! Put that away before I puke. DA: I told you, the guy is a freak. DARLENE: Triple Threat, 3 men only one winner. DA: Bobby Silas called out G-Gangsta and G won the belt, Huey Huxtable is the wild card in this one. Gangsta and Silas may forget Huey is there.
DARLENE: Which is what happened earlier tonight, how Huey got in this match in the first place.
DA: Right. But we shall see. Roll..... *********************************** ‘THE SILENCE' BOBBY SILAS *********************************** (Bobby Silas is watching TV. First a shot of Barry Bonds hitting a home run, then a cut to Sammy Sosa knocking one out of the park)
SILAS: Those two know how to use a bat. Not like G-Gangsta. No matter what happens, who is the third man I am coming for you G-Gangsta and I am going to exact my revenge.
You think you can beat me, man to man, then come get some, but don't use weapons and managers, are you proud of that title?
You know, you are like Barry Bonds, you got the gold, the skills but no one cares. Because of how you do it.
But if you want to bring it all at Vegas Violence, then bring it all! Keep in mind my mixed up brother.....I will be swinging for the fences on Friday night! ********************************** DARLENE: Strong words. DA: But G-Gangsta is never one to not get his two cents in. DARLENE: Double negative Dan... DA: Huh? *************** G-GANGSTA *************** [My Projects hits, U-Trop walks out, he is wearing his blue Clench suit, there is no sign of G-Gangsta, U-Trop grabs the mic.]
U-Trop: With no further ado. I would like to introduce my homie, my brotha, The first ever TRIPLE THREAT CHAMPION.....G-GANGSTAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!! [My Projects hits, G-Gangsta walks out to a huge pop, he is wearing a pair of Avirex jeans and a Avirex jersey. He has the belt slung over his shoulder. He grabs a mic.]
G-Gangsta:Thanks for the cheers y'all. Well it seems I am the first ever Triple Threat Champion! I worked for this one though. I grew up in the ghetto of Jamaica Queens in New York for this. That sh!t is what made me as tough as
I am. If you don't belive me ask Bobby Silas. I whoop on di bwoy @$$ last Wednesday. THIS ONE IS FOR Y'ALL!!! [He holds the belt up to crowd to a big reception.] G-Gangsta: Anyways this Friday, I have to defend the belt against
Bobby Silas again, and a mystery opponent. It's all good though, because I already beat him, so basically, I am going to do the same thing again. Whoop on him
@$$ once again chaa. But, for this mystery opponent, I really don't know who you are so I ain't gonna say I can whoop your @$$, but don't under estimate me either bredran.
Why? Because everyone did that to me when I walked in here. They said
I wa
fake, but look at me now. Silas said he would kick my "fake g" @$$, well look who's the champ rude bwoy. I AM! Not you, I beat you in a far,
one-on-one match. But I mean where was Jack Spade? I was looking forward to fighting him again. Jack, when....not if..when I win a bigger title, you got your shot,
I hope I
do to. After I succesfully defend the belt at the PPV, you got a shot the next Wednesday, just get another opponent and you set dawg.
Jut remember VWF, you run your mouth, you get caught, you think your un touchable...you get shot. One love!
[My Projects by Coo Coo Cal hits, G-Gangsta walks out with the belt over his shoulder, U-Trop follows, they get a huge pop from the crowd.]
************************************* DA: He has his fans, they seem to love him. DARLENE: I think Bobby Silas and Huey Huxtable might be more than G can handle. DA: We will see, Friday night. DARLENE: And the Main Event pits "The Ace" Jack Spade agasint Sebastian. DA: And now some words, from "The Ace" Jack Spade... ***************************** "THE ACE" JACK SPADE ***************************** (We're taken back to a shot of Jack Spade clad in his black clothes stood in front of a large VWF logo. He looks at the camera and begins to talk.)
JS: Well Sebastian you clever bastard. Look what you've got yourself into right now! You cost me my chance at the Triple Threat title, which
I've got
to say was mine in that match and you've now landed your self a dance with the devil, a fight against me.
(Jack pauses for a few seconds) JS: Sebastian you've held that 24 hour title twice and each time you've been a boring champion. These fans here don't want a boring idiot as their main champion. They want a guy who can fight. A guy who can kick some ass, just like I have been. They want a real badass which can shake the very foundations of the VWF easily. Y'know who they want as their champion
Sebastian? Me.
(The crowd cheers in the arena. This brings a smile to Jack's face.) JS: Sebastian, Vegas Violence is coming soon, and you're gonna get your face caved in, you're gonna loose your title and you're going to regret you ever attacked me and gave me a minor concussion. I spent 2 days lying on my back with a bad head, but now I'm recovered I'm more pissed off than ever, and you're just another obstical in my way to the top of this federation.
(The crowd start a brief "ACE" chant, Jack pauses, lets them finish, then finishes his own talking.)
JS: Sebastian, you may be the all powerful Sebastian, but I'm Jack freaking Spade, and you're gonna loose that title at Vegas Violence. (The scene fades back to ringside with the crowd cheering loudly for
Jack.) DA: Lets roll footage of what happened last week on Warrior! *********************** FROM LAST WEEK *********************** (Dan Aller) Sebastian and Jack Spade are battling Kerry Powell! The other VWF stars have spilled out onto the floor!
Sebastian holds Powell and Spade hits the JACK POT! Powell is down, Spade goes for the cover! And Sebastian just nailed Spade with something! Brass knuckles maybe! Sebastian makes the cover 1...2....3! Damn! Damn! Damn! Sebastian races over to the table, grabs the title and heads back up the ramp! (Cut back to studio) DARLENE: Spade has Powell beat and Sebastian nailed him with a pair of brass knuckles. DA: Sebastian is now a 2 time champ, but neither time did he nail the man he pinned. DARLENE: He is our Champ....roll it. *************** SEBASTIAN *************** (Sebastian is walking down a hall, with the title over his shoulder and a wicked bug smile on his nasty face)
SEBASTIAN: Look at this! Sebastian is the Champion. Not Kerry Powell, not G-Gangsta, and not Jack Spade.
Jack Spade, Sebastian says thank you. But you couldn't get the job done, only Sebastian can get the job done.
Never send a boy to do a Sebastians job. (big wicked laugh) After Friday who will claim a shot at Sebastians title? Spade will be beaten.....Powell will be packing......who else is there? The weirdos, Zell and Angela? The drunks, O'Shea and Western? The hardcore wanna be G-Gangsta.
(gets a serious look and tone) Everyone take a good hard long look! This is why Sebastian came to the
VWF, this is why Sebastian will stay.
No matter what it takes, no matter who gets stepped on Sebastian will keep this belt. Jack Spade. Set your sights on someone else. I am Sebastian....The 24 Hour Champion....and you are NOT! ******************************** DARLENE: I hope Spade tears him a new as... DA: Hey! (The lights flicker a red colour and the opening riff of Ace of Spades by Motorhead begins to play. The crowd let out a huge cheer as "The Ace" Jack Spade swaggers out onto the ramp with an arrogant smile on his face as the song continues to play. Jack grabs a mic and the music cuts.)
DA: Here comes Jack Spade! What the hell does he want! JS: Sebastian, enough of the waiting, these fans want to see it, I wanna kick your ass now....GET OUT HERE!!!
(I'm too Sexy plays and Sebastian runs out. Jack meets him on the ramp and the two begin to fight eachother as security rushes out to seperate them with a bunch of other VWF officials!)
DA: WHOA! The fight is on and these two can't wait to get at eachother at the Pay Per View! That's all we have time for...tune in for the Pay
Per
View! Vegas Wrestling Federation ©2001
