An Ontario-based promotion, part of a two-fed operation under one owner and a member of a wider newsgroup wrestling alliance, that ran a full multi-title card out of Barrie District County. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Simcoe County Championship Wrestling's own surviving record opens already running: its rules and handbook post of 3 February 2005 presents an established, staffed promotion, operating alongside a sister promotion, COPW, under the same ownership, and describing itself as a proud member of the NeWA alliance. “NO REAL WRESTLERS ALLOWED, CREATED ONLY!!!” its own roster policy states outright.

Its only other surviving document, a full results post for “Lightning Strikes” (27 February 2005, Barrie District County), opens on a limousine delivering three of its own champions to the arena — “Creation of Destruction” Wolf, former Simcoe County Heavyweight Champion Mike Austin, and Young Guns Champion Kirsta — before “The Shotgun” Bobby Johnson and Humphrey Wilson call the night's card.

Six titles changed or were defended that night. Rayne Young won a chain match over Big Weaze; Steve Silveroak and GBH fought to a double disqualification; Paroxysm unified the Innercity Television and Young Guns Championships by beating Kirsta; Ryan Burns retained the Simcoe County Heavyweight title in a draw against Kula; Dan Benson won a three-way match over Damion Darkside and Morpheus to claim the Barrie District Championship with a finisher he called “The Shocker”; and Wolf retained the Legacy Championship against Spike with his own “Wolf's Bite.”

No further document survives past that card; how the promotion, or any of its six titles, fared afterward isn't in the surviving record.
