SCCW — Rules and Handbook

Rules and Handbook

3 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCCW: http://www.sccw.biz/ COPW: http://www.angelfire.com/al4/sccw04/SCCWest/SCCWest.html Status: Both organizations are owned by the same person, however SCCW is a proud member of the NeWA. Both organizations have openings for e-wrestlers 1. STAFF HAS THE FINAL SAY!!! If the staff have spoken on a subject that is it. There will be no more about it. If said subject is brought up again, the person doing the bringing up will receive one warning mark. Should the warning mark be complained about, you will receive an automatic suspension. 2. DO NOT USE ANOTHER HANDLER'S WRESTLER IN YOUR ROLEPLAYS WITHOUT PERMISSION. General attacks are NOT allowed in this fed. However to make the storylines more interesting if you need (want) to attack someone , e-mail me using the boards and I will put it into the show(i.e.: depending on the storyline and your reason for attacking). 3. ROLEPLAYS: (i) MUST BE AT LEAST 25 LINES!! Anyone can write "I will kick your ass". That takes no talent, but to write at least 25 lines shows you put some thought in your roleplays.(For 25 line length, see rule 7. It is ALMOST 25 lines. That is NOT a promise you WILL win if you do the minimum, it means you will not be fired for not doing it. (ii)IF YOU NO SHOW ONE WEEK, YOU WILL BE PUT ON PROBATION. IF IT GOES TWO WEEKS CONSECUTIVELY THEN YOU WILL BE RELEASED FROM YOUR SCCW CONTRACTS!! The reasoning is if you no show then you obviously do not want to be there for the fed, so we, in turn, do not want you here either. Now if know you are not going to make it and will no show, get a hold of one of the bookers (our e-mails are on the staff page) and let us know. Rarely will you be gone for two weeks and not know ahead of time. 4. FOUL LANGUAGE IS PERMITTED HERE IN THE SCCW. Previously we did not allow profanity, HOWEVER due to a roster poll, we have decided to relax up on that rule. BUT should every second word become foul (the "f" word) or the relaxed rule is abused, profanity will be outlawed once again. What is considered abuse of profanity is subject to STAFF only to decide. 5. NO MORE THAN ONE ROLEPLAY ON DEADLINE DAY WITH THREE (3) ROLEPLAYS MAXIMUM FOR THE SNS AND TNT SHOWS AND FOUR (4) FOR THE ARENA EVENT!!! The reason for the only one on deadline is to stop those that roleplay at the last minute only. If you have been making good roleplays for the week, and your opponent has not and is waiting for the last day to post his four, you will not have anything to worry about. But due to time zone differences, the rule is 1 roleplay between 8 am and 8 pm eastern. 6. NO O.O.C.'S ON THE ROLEPLAY BOARD. This is the reason we have an ooc board. Any ooc's on the roleplay board will be deleted. 7. NO O.O.C. WARS. If you have a problem with a superstar in the fed, take it up with them privately by using the boards messenger service, or talk to myself using the board's messenger service, and I will take care of it. 8. ROLEPLAYS ARE JUDGED ON THESE CRITERIA: This is what the rp's will be judged on out of 100 points. Judges will take all roleplays into account for one (1) score. Creativity (in ring promo's, or outside, or where is the promo taking place)~ 20 points Grammar (spelling, punctuation, etc) ~ 20 points Character Development ~ 20 points Match Discussion (Trash talking) ~ 20 points Description ( surroundings, what is the character doing, seeing etc.) ~ 20 points. Total of 100 points. 9. LOSING IS PART OF E-FED LIFE, SO DEAL. To be put nicer, everyone loses. No one has an undefeated streak here in the SCCW. But if you felt like you should have won, but yet lost, ask one of the staff about it. You can do that by either messenger using the boards or e-mail. You will get an answer but please be patient. The staff have lives as well. Also remember, not everyone will agree with you that your roleplays were the best. It is a judge's decision and the judges we have are not a part of the SCCW team. (ie they do not wrestle for the SCCW so they do not have a vested interest in who wins or loses) 10. IF YOU QUIT DUE TO A LOSS, YOU WILL NOT BE WELCOME BACK. That shows bad sportsmanship and we do not need that in our locker room. There are exceptions to that rule and for a return to be made, the offending person must talk to myself and the rest of the staff and I will work it out for a possible return. 11. TITLE SHOTS AND MATCHES WILL BE DECIDED BY STAFF. While challenging each other is a great way to get storylines going, and while we do accept any idea's by the roster, we (the staff) do have the storylines going in mind. Any demanding of title shots will have you immediately down the list. Titles will have to be defended every other week at LEAST. Plus every title will be defended on each PPV. 12. THE CHALLENGERS WILL BE LISTED AS THE TOP THREE (3). They can receive title shots. To get on the top 3, you must show the staff a willing to work to get there (consistently good roleplays and general participation) We like to see rosters participate on regularly on ALL the available forums. It shows you put time and effort into helping us make this fed great to be in. As staff, we reward that with title shots. You can also get there by defeating top three ranked wrestlers. 13. SEGMENTS ARE MANDATROY. This means that everyone MUST submit at least one (1) segment weekly. This will be done to make the results a bit more exciting. 14. TITLE RULES: Simcoe County Heavyweight Title Rules: 1. Must be deemed number one contender by staff. 2. Must have been in the SCCW for at LEAST 1 1/2 months 3. Must be defended at LEAST once (1 x ) a month 4. Cannot hold any other SCCW titles. Barrie District Championship Rules: 1. Must be ranked in the top 3 contenders for it. 2. Must have been in the SCCW for at LEAST 3three (3) weeks. 3. Can hold the District Tag Team titles as well 4. Must be defended at LEAST twice (2x) a month Inner City Television Championship: 1. Anyone can fight for it 2. There is no limit to how long you have been in the SCCW 3. Can hold the SCCW Tag Team Title but no other singles titles consecutively 4. Must be defended at LEAST twice (2x) a month Young Guns Championship: 1. Anyone can fight for it 2. There is no limit to how long you have been in the SCCW 3. Can hold the SCCW Tag Team Title but no other singles titles consecutively 4. Must be defended at LEAST twice (2x) a month Barrie District Tag Team Titles: 1. Must be in the Top 3 Contenders to fight for it 2. There is no limit to how long you have been in the SCCW 3. Can hold any singles title as well, BUT the Simcoe County Heavyweight Championship 4. Must be defended at LEAST twice (2x) a month Legacy Championship Look at Young Guns Championship found in SCCW information. 15. YOU ARE REQUIRED TO ROLEPLAY EVEN IF YOU DO NOT HAVE A MATCH. The reason for this is not everyone will get a match every week so if you keep roleplaying anyway, you show the staff you are a team player and you want to receive a push. You also can use it to make your storylines go farther and build interest, making people want to see your matches. These can be posted in the SCCW Developmental Forums. 16. NO RACIAL OR SEXUAL INSULTS ALLOWED AT ALL. Those include calling people fag, or gay or things like that. You can hint at someone's sexual preference but cannot come right out and say it. Basically if it is not said on anything but HBO or Showtime, it is not said here. Use your judgment and I will not have to use mine. 17. ALL NEWCOMERS TO SCCW MUST BE IN A MATCH BEFORE THEY ARE ADDED TO THE ROSTER, TWO MATCHES BEFORE BIOS!!! This is to make sure you will roleplay. We are not going to book you and add you for you to no show. It is that simple, and is non-negotiable. 18. THE COMPLAINT FORUM IS NOW A THREE (3) STRIKE, YOU'RE OUT RULE!!! What that means is if you have a valid complaint about an SCCW superstar, whether it is an offensive roleplay, or harassing via e-mails and instant message, or by personal message via the boards, post it on the Complaint Forum WITH proof. You have to have proof of the harassment, and please post your proof here as well as your complaint. Staff will investigate your claims and based on the severity of it; said harasser can find himself without a contract. Now I am sure people will complain about others they do not like, just to get them out of here, BUT that call will be a staff and staff one only. If you are complained about three (3) times for whatever reason, and you are found guilty of such transgressions by the staff, you will be released and based on the severity, banned from SCCW. 19. NO REAL WRESTLERS ALLOWED, CREATED ONLY!!! No Exceptions will be made. We are trying to stay away from "real" wrestling as we possibly can. (pictures are the exception) 20. NO PUTTING ADS FOR YOUR E-FED ON OUR FORUMS WITHOUT STAFF PERMISSION. This includes personal messaging or e-mailing roster with recruitment letters. Recruiters will be banned immediately. 21. WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU BREAK THE RULES? See SCCW Information #7. It explains it all. 22. THERE IS A SPECIFIC PLACE AND WAY TO SEND IN SEGMENTS. The link can be found on our main page. Any segments NOT submitted via the segment form will not be used. It is there to make things easier for you to send them and easier for us to receive them. Please see rule #13 for segments. Thank you. 23. DO NOT HARASS STAFF ABOUT RESULTS BEFORE THEY ARE POSTED. You will not be told anything.
