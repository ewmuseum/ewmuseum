http://www.sccw.biz/ Live from Barrie District County, February Huge Arena event Lightening strikes February 27, 2005 A black limousine, its headlights beaming intensely, pulls into Dunlop Street. Slowly the massive vehicle rolls towards the Superstars Entrance before coming to a sluggish halt right in front of the door. The light blinds the security guard in the little booth on the side, who looks on or tries to. The engine purrs and the car remains where it is. Slowly, the driver's door opens and a man in a driver's uniform steps out. Leaving his door open he makes his way over to the back door and taps on the window. As a result it slides down about a half of an inch. The driver has a thick British accent, but it's all relevant As the Driver holds the door open, you can see it is someone holding two title belts, it is Creation of Destruction Wolf. former Simcoe County Heavyweight Champion, "The Real Fucking Deal" himself... Mike Austin. The woman is the Young Guns champion holding her title... the Hell Cat- Kirsta. The threesome then comes somewhat together and the door is shut by the driver, who returns to his seat and shuts his own door. The group of individuals make their way into the arena and then down the hallways. Bobby 'The Shotgun' Johnson, and Humphrey Wilson. Open up this show and talk about the excitement and move right onto introducing the first match of the night. Chain Match, Single Match Return Of Rayne Big Weaze vs. Rayne Young With both men in the ring, the ref comes to them both with a chain, wrapping it around both men's wrists and locks it, then placing the key in his pocket. At the sound of the bell, Weaze starts to pull on the chain and Rayne pulls back and a tug of war is on. As both men try to get the upper hand to move the other forward. Weaze drapes the chain at the legs of Rayne and then charges past him. Rayne falls face first to the mat. Weaze then stops and slings the chain around towards the head of Rayne and locks it around his neck. He pulls on both ends trying to choke the life out of Rayne Young. He tries to fight back, but can't due to the enormous strength Weaze possess. The ref breaks the choke. Weaze notices the chain is between Rayne's leg and begins pulling the slack out of it. He finally gets enough slack out where he can lift up with one jerk and delivers a low blow to the Enigma. And Rayne falls to the mat once again. Weaze lunges forward with the chain draping from his arm and takes him down with a huge forearm smash. Rayne rolls over and bloods starts to tear from his head. Weaze raises the chair and swings with full force. But Rayne rolls out of the way and Weaze's chair hits the top rope and bounces back in his face. Before he can fall back, Rayne just to his feet and wraps the chain around Weaze's neck and pushed him over the top rope. Weaze starts kicking and flailing as Rayne hangs him over the top rope. Ref finally forces Rayne to pull Weaze back in. Weaze wraps his huge hand around the neck of Rayne and lift's him to his feet and shouts obscenities to Rayne who look's dazed and confused and prepares Rayne Young for the ending but Rayne battles free from Weaze's grasp and Irish whips him into the ropes following up with a powerful clothesline sending both of them over the top rope down to the outside near the commentary tables. Weaze pick's Rayne up by the hair but unexpectedly Rayne drive's a knee to the gut of the big man and connects with his finisher "Game's Over" as both men crash to the canvas as Rayne crawls over to Weaze and drapes and arm over his chest. Rayne drags Big Weaze around by a large Russian Chain for what seems like an eternity. Just as Rayne is about to hit the fourth turn buckle, Weaze manages to stir. He looks up, and tugs on the chain. Rayne is getting pulled back, and he jumps.. flying through the air.. and knocking the fourth and final turnbuckle, signifying his victory. The ref calls for the bell, and raises a brutally beaten Rayne Young's hand in victory. The winner 'The Enigma' Rayne.. Young!" Derek Matthews is seen in the halls back stage searching for something reading the names on the locker room doors, He turns to face Sykotic and Jeremy Diaz. He complains that he doesn't have his own Locker room, stating that he beat Spike he should have his own room. Sykotic and Diaz stare at each other and Diaz tells Derek he can use his locker room. Grudge Match Steve Silveroak Vs. GBH Both men are In the ring and with a show of strength they lock hands as Silveroak takes the immediate advantage. He has GBH down to one knee, but he fights back. GBH gets back to his feet and lifts a knee to the midsection before hitting a release northern lights suplex. Silveroak blocks a fourth right hand by GBH and it sends his opponent against the ropes with an Irish whip. GBH comes back, straight into a spine buster. Silveroak attempts a pin but forgets to hook the leg, only gets a 2 count as GBH kicks out. GBH picks Silveroak up from the mat again. He goes for a suplex, but Silveroak blocks it. GBH tries again, but Silveroak blocks it again. GBH tries a third time, but Steve Silveroak manages to counter it, lifting and holding GBH in the air. Steve connects with a stalling suplex! Both men are down and the ref begins to count, as he hits 8 both men are back to there feet. Siveroak knocks GBH out of the ring with a cloth line. Silveroak slides out of the ring, grabbing a steel chair and slams the chair down across GBH's skull, just as GBH hit a low blow! Both men fall down as the referee calls for the bell! This one ended as the referee declares a double disqualification!" Unification Match Innercity Television Championship and Young Guns Championship. Paroxysm Vs. Kirsta The two lock up in the middle of the ring with the stronger Paroxysm backing Kirsta into the corner. He breaks cleanly but Kirsta delivers a thumb to the eye which forces him to clutch at it as he stumbles backward. The Hellcat charges out of the corner and clotheslines Paroxysm down to the canvas with surprising ease. She picks Paroxysm up and goes to lift him up, but he swings somehow behind her and delivers a Russian leg sweep. Paroxysm kicks her in the stomach as she stands and begins to go for a powerbomb, but she easily counters it into a hurricanrana! Paroxysm grabs her head and delivers a fisherman suplex. He bridges for a cover, but Kirsta kicks out. Paroxysm bounces off the ropes and comes back with a cross body, but the very surprising power of the Hellcat, she is able to catch him and body slam him down. Kirsta goes to the top rope. She awaits Paroxysm to get up but this proves to be a mistake as he hits the ropes and she crotches herself. Paroxysm climb up there with her, but she headbutts him quite hard and he steps off the bottom rope and grabs at his skull. Kirsta grabs his injured body part and then swings around, delivering a vicious tornado DDT. She immediately jumps up and lets out a big scream, feeling the moment. She waits for him to get up and when he does, she goes for a superkick. Paroxysm ducks it and then turns quickly, grabbing Kirsta and setting her up for his patented cradle piledriver! He hits it and covers her, gaining the three! Paroxysm wins the Unification Match. Still the Innercity Champion, and the new... Young Guns Champion... Paroxysm!" Legendary! Mike Austin folds up like an accordion as Derek places a boot on the chest going for the cocky pin, and the win Your winner Derek Matthews. Barrie District Championship Dan Benson Vs. Damion Darkside Vs. Morpheus Damion and Morpheus go after Benson and back him into a corner, they take turns putting the boots to Dan Benson before they yank him up by the hair and whip him across the ring. Benson falls backwards on to the mat as Damion and Morpheus stomp a mudhole into him. Morpheus then clocks Damion Darkside with a hard right hand to the side of the head that sends him back towards the ropes, Morpheus runs up and nails Darkside with a forearm to the skull before whipping Darkside across the ring and upon the return Darkside gets taken down with a lariat, Benson gets up and kicks Morpheus in the gut and plants him with a quick double arm DDT. Morpheus and Darkside hoist Benson up for a double brainbuster but Benson counters and plants both of them with a DDT. All three men lay motionless in the ring as the ref cannot count due to the 'No Count Out and No DQ' rules. Morpheus gets the upper hand in the knuckle lock as Benson falls to his knees. Benson slowly powers back and makes Morpheus fall into a bridging predicament before The Barrie District Champ kicks Benson off and into a somewhat dazed Damion Darkside, Darkside falls through the ropes and to the floor. Benson heads out after him and Morpheus is close behind, Morpheus grabs Benson and smashes his head against the steel ring post before getting a double axe handle from Darkside. Grudge Match Legacy, Title Legacy Rules Spike Vs Wolf © The two men engage in the fierce struggle for the advantage, after a few seconds of immobility, Wolf begins to force Spike backwards, albeit at a slow pace. They reach the ring ropes and Wolf continues to force Spike against them, Spike manages to turn Wolf over so that now his back is against the ropes. Wolf gets Spike in a side headlock and squeezes tightly. Spike grasps Wolf by the hands and tries to power out of it to no avail. Wolf improves his leverage and sinks in on The True Franchise, but Spike counters with a hammerlock and now stands behind his opponent. Spike drives a right knee into the side of Wolf, he reapplies the full-nelson, but then plummets Wolf with a Russian leg sweep. Wolf won't stay down long, Spike lands a couple of rights and then Irish whips him to the ropes. As he rebounds, Spike leap frogs him, but Wolf catches him in midair and brings him straight down over his knee with an atomic drop. Spike bends slightly and holds his groin area. These two go back and forth at each other and only getting a two count. Wolf begins getting the advantage as fists of rage connect over the face and head of Spike. Wolf knocks down Spike, but Spike gets back up, Wolf is keeps rocking his head back with hard UFC style punches as the fans boo him. The referee has to intervene to stop the overabundance of fists; Just when Spike getting back to his game, and starts to punch Wolf with lefts and rights. Wolf takes advantage of the ref being close in and kicks Spike between the legs, without the referee seeing it. Spike staggers and is about to fall when Wolf grabs him with a bear hug and then as they fall Spike spins it into a Flatliner. Both Spike and Wolf are down from the HIGH IMPACT move. After around ten seconds when both of them are lying, trying to catch their breath, Spike slowly crawls over and makes the cover on Wolf. He again only gets a 2 count. Wolf picks Spike up again and goes for a second Wolfs Bite, but Spike spins out of it and comes back with the Impaler!! Spike falls over Wolf's body... Kick out at the last second by Wolf. Both men start to stir as they stand. They get up facing opposite directions and both stagger to face each other. Wolf reacts first, running purely on instinct. Wolfs Bite!! Wolf falls down for the cover, neither of the men is moving. Wolf get the win and the pin and still SCCW Legacy Champion... Morpheus is about to defend his friend, but he is stopped by a kick to the midsection. Wolf tucks his heads between his legs, and execute the "Wolf's Bite" on the concrete floor. Simcoe County Championship Kula Vs Ryan Burns © Burns tries to start things off quickly with a running forearm. Kula blocks the attempt with a kick to the gut before Burns gets to him. Kula follows up with a couple of forearm clubs to the back of Burns which sends Burns down to his knees. Burns leaps for an Elbow Drop and connects with Kula's sternum! Burns goes for the pin. Only gets a 2 count. Burns bounces off the ropes and Kula catches him with the Kitchen Sink. Burns flips over Kula's knee and onto his back. Kula grabs the fallen Burns' left leg and gives it couple of kicks to the knee. Then Kula turns Burns over and hooks a single leg crab in. The referee gets down and checks to see if Burns is going to tap out. Burns screams 'No' and starts dragging himself across the ring to the nearest ropes. He stretches his arm out, inches away, but Kula pulls him towards the center of the ring and clamps the single leg crab in even tighter. The ref checks with Burns again but Burns yells at him even louder 'NO!' Burns pushes his body up with his arms. He takes a step towards the ropes, and then rolls under so Burns's on his back and Kula still has his leg. Kula is shocked to see this and Burns kicks him to the gut to get Kula to let go of his leg. Burns is up first and charges at Kula for the spear! Kula slides out of the way at the last second and pushes Burns, as he goes by, through the ropes and back to the outside! Kula crawls to the ropes then rolls to the outside to follow up with Burns. Kula deliberately walks over to the still down Burns and lifts him up. Kula hooks Burns' arms lays him out with a Double Arm DDT! He gets back up and hits another! And another!! He's just hit the Kula Shaker on the Heavyweight Champ! Kula lets go of the lifeless Burns. Kula manages to get up and starts to turn around and stumble to the middle of the ring as Burns catches him with the Spear! All of Burns' energy was used as he isn't able to make the pin right away and the ref starts his ten count for a third time. The referee has dubbed this match-up, a draw. Ryan Burns retains his Title and is still Simcoe County Heavyweight Champion.
