Smash - Fans' Choice

1 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sean Masters — 2 September 2011 Jim Ross Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to a very special edition of Smash! Joining me at ringside is Jerry 'The King' Lawler. Would you mind telling us more about tonight, King?
Jerry 'The King' Lawler It would be my honor, JR. Tonight, here in Louisville, Kentucky, the fans in attendance have been voting for what matches they would like to see take place tonight!
Jim Ross And our C.O.O, the EEW Champion Phillip Phillips has decided to put his own brutal mark on the matches here. Every choice is a bruising stipulation. Up by our stage is a grave, for our Buried Alive Match. We have the Steel Cage AND Hell in a Cell structures being held above us at ringside.
Jerry 'The King' I can't wait to see which match us up first. We'll leave you with Justin Roberts as we gear up for a VERY exciting night here in Kentucky!
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Justin Roberts "The following is a Steel Cage Match. Now, the only ways to win are to escape, pin our opponent, or make him submit. Introducing first, from Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Weighing in at 226 Pounds. CHRIS JERICHO!"
Jim Ross "Ladies and gentlemen, this match could explode at any second. Chris Jericho has been watching Dolph Ziggler for a couple of weeks now. And, in an added insult here, if Jericho can beat Ziggler here, Ziggler will NOT receive an Xtreme Championship match this Tuesday at Burn Out!"
Justin Roberts "And his opponent, From Hollywood, Florida. Weighing in at 223 Pounds. DOLPH ZIGGLER! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Referee at ringside checks the lock on the Steel Cage's door, as Dolph Ziggler and Chris Jericho stare at each other from opposite corners of the ring. The referee inside the ring signals for the bell to ring, as Dolph and Chris lock horns. Dolph Ziggler, knowing the repercussions if he should leave, desperately tries to escape the cage.
Jim Ross And Dolph Ziggler is attempting to escape already. But, Jericho is stirring. Jericho can halt Ziggler's escape right here!
Jericho uses the ring ropes to drag himself up, and begins to climb after Dolph Ziggler, who is halfway over the cage. Upon realising that Jericho is coming for him, Ziggler tries to move away, but Jericho grabs his leg. Jericho pulls Ziggler onto the top rope and begins brutally bashing his head against the Cage wall.
Jerry 'The King' Lawler C'mon Chris, you won't win the match like that. Get rid of Ziggler now, and the match is all yours!
Jim Ross Who's side are you on, King? Jerry 'The King' Lawler I'm neutral, JR. I don't like either of them! [color=green]After having his head smashed against wire meshing, Dolph Ziggler helplessly swings around on the top rope, holding the Cage wall. Then, Jericho lumbers up to him with malicious intent, ready to strike. Suddenly, from out of nowhere, Jericho hits the Codebreaker on Ziggler from the top rope, mobilizing BOTH competitors![/color
Jim Ross Baw Gawd, what a slobberknocker! Jericho hit the Codebreaker from out of nowhere! He's got this wrapped up now!
After the hard, bruising collision with the canvas, both Jericho and Ziggler begin to stir. Suddenly, from out of nowhere, Randy Orton runs down the aisle, springs up the Cage wall and jumps into the ring. After hitting Jericho with a Scoop Slam, Orton sets his eyes on Ziggler.
Jerry 'The King' Lawler It's pretty clear what Orton wants. He wants Dolph Ziggler out of the way so that HE can get an Xtreme Championship match at Burn Out.
As Orton prepares to strike, Miz climbs on top of the Cage, and stares at Orton, who hits the RKO on Ziggler anyway. Unknown to Orton, Miz is now behind him, and hits the Skull Crushing Finale. Then, while turning to leave, Miz is hit by a Jericho Codebreaker!
Jim Ross Baw Gawd! Things have completely broken down here! After surveying the destruction, Jericho laughs and walks out of the open Cage door, winning the match!
Justin Roberts Hre is your winner, CHRIS JERICHO! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phillip Phillips is walking round backstage, paperwork in one hand, phone in the other. Suddenly, he is confronted by his opponent, Zeke Nero! There is an awkward silence, and the two walk there separate ways. Elsewhere, Miz is helping Ziggler backstage arguing with him, before coming face to face with Triple H, who smiles!
Triple H I've just heard that our match is a Buried Alive Match. Enjoy the light filling your eyes, it won't last long!
Triple H smiles, brushes past Miz and heads to the Gorilla Position, ready for his match. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Fans erupt as Triple H walks out onto the stage, waterbottle in his hand. He takes a swig, stares at the makeshift Grave and spits some out, before walking down to the ring, and spitting again at ringside.
Justin Roberts This match is set for one fall. In the ring, from Greenwich Connecticut. Weighing in at 260 Pounds. He is the Game..........TRIPLE H![/b]
Triple H paces around the ring, awaiting The Miz. Suddenly, Dolph Ziggler appears on the stage!
Dolph Ziggler Triple H, don't get mad or anything.......The Miz can't compete. He injured his leg earlier. Doctors have deemed him unfit to compete. So, the match is off.
The fans start booing as Triple H stares skeptically at Dolph Ziggler. Then, Miz hops the barricade at ringside with a Steel Chair. Triple H turns round, just to be hit with the Chair. The bell rings, and the match begins. Miz looks at his downed opponent, starting his 'wake up' taunt, supposedly going for a Skull Crushing Finale. Triple H stumbles to his feet, and trips back, into Miz' hold. But, Miz is suddenly flipped over the head of 'The Game' and put on his back.
Jerry 'The King' Lawler Within seconds, Triple H has taken advantage. Can he now lay Miz to rest? Triple H flips Miz out of the ring, and pulls a Kendo Stick from under the ring as Dolph Ziggler watches on. Ziggler watches as Miz painfully struggles up the aisle, being battered by the Kendo Stick all the way. Then, Triple H hoists him up and places him on his shoulders.
Jim Ross Oh no, not this. Not a piledriver onto the concrete. That can end a man's career! That's a Powerbomb, JR! And I think Triple H is actually going to do it! Triple H smiles and powerbombs Miz to the floor in the middle of the aisle, as fans cheer. Then, he reaches under the ring and grabs his special weapon - THE SLEDGEHAMMER! Fans cheer as Triple H helps his opponent up, before plunging the steel mallet into Miz' gut, and prepares for a Pedigree.
Baw Gawd. He's gonna do it. Triple H is going to hit the Pedigree. This one's over now, for sure!
Triple H hits the Pedigree, sending the fans into raptures. Triple H then picks Miz up, and Irish Whips him into the mound of dirt at the top of the aisle. Dolph Ziggler nervously edges toward Triple H, before hitting him with a Shovel. Dolph helps Miz up, before shouting at him. Then, to everyone's surprise, Dolph shoves Miz into the Grave. The crowd erupt into cheers and chant Dolph Ziggler'name as he runs backstage.
Jerry 'The King' Lawler I don't believe it, JR. Dolph Ziggler just pushed Miz into the grave. Dolph has just turned on his own manager, and cost him the match.
Jim Ross And Triple H is now climbing into the digger at ringside. Could this be the last we'll ever see of The Miz?
Triple H smiles as the Digger lurches forward, before the dirt in its dumper pours into the Grave, burying Miz alive.
Justin Roberts Here is your winner. TRIPLE H! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Jerry 'The King' Lawler What a great match, JR. Two giants in a buried alive match. In the end, it was the better man who won.
Jim Ross The better man certainly did win, King. Next up, we have a very different twist on a match. In the next match, the competitors will be left inside Hell in a Cell. But, there is a gap for them to escape from on the Cell roof, 20 feet away. The twist - you must use Tables, Ladders and Chairs to escape the Cell and reach the floor at ringside. This is TLC.........in a Cell!
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Justin Roberts The following is a TLC in a Cell Match - and is for the EEW Championship! Now the only way to win is to incapacitate your opponent, climb out of the Cell, and descend to the floor below!
The fans erupt in cheers as Phillip Phillips emerges from backstage, EEW Championship slung over his right shoulder. After stroking his mustache, 'P2' holds the EEW Championship above his head and marches to the ring, surveying the brutal objects around him. He then looks up at the Cell, knowing that the icy cold steel stands between him and his EEW Championship!
'Cult of Personality' opening sentence And during the few moments that we have left, we want to take right down to Earth in a Language that everybody here can easily understand!
Zeke Nero ignores everything and marches to the ring with a stern look on his face. The referee holds Phillip Phillips to avoid a confrontation before the match. Justin Roberts stands by the announce table as the Hell in a Cell structure begins to descend.
Introducing first......the challenger. From Washington D.C. Weighing in at 240 Pounds. ZEKE NERO!
The fans boo as Zeke bows his head and raises his arm, before gesturing to the title, and pointing to his waist.
"And from Versoix, Switzerland. Weighing in at 245 Pounds. He is the C.O.O of Eagle Eye Wrestling - AND the EEW Champion. PHILLIP PHILLIPS!"
The referee calls for the bell to ring, and the match begins. Zeke Nero easily bowls Phillip over and goes straight for a ladder. Phillip is wise and dropkicks the ladder into his opponent's face. Then, he tries to escape himself. But, Dolph Ziggler halts him and drags him off the ladder. Suddenly, he starts climbing the ladder, and hops out of the cell, but Phillip follows him. The two begin to battle atop the Cell, when....
Jerry 'The King' Lawler It's Triple H. And he's got a Sledgehammer. Its not looking good here! Phillip Phillips and Zeke Nero continue brawling, moving closer to the hole in the cell roof. Then, Triple H slams the Cell Wall with his sledgehammer, and Zeke plunges into a Table in the ring. Triple H then continues to pound at the foundations of the structure.
Jim Ross Triple H has snapped. He's determined to get to Phillip and Zeke. He wants the title shot that was supposedly stolen from him.
Jerry 'The King' Lawler Triple H never had anything stolen from him - he cursed on national television. He was punished, and he doesn't like it. Tough luck!
Triple H dislodges the foundation of the structure, which shakes menacingly. Then, he batters the other side of the Cell, until it breaks. Phillip Phillip tries to escape, but is caught in the chaos, and lands on the announce table, crushed. Lawler and Ross run for cover!
Jim Ross This match is seemingly over. Phillip Phillips has surely won. The referee runs over to ringside and begins speaking to Justin Roberts, who nods his head and raises the microphone to his lips.
Justin Roberts Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has just informed me that this match has ended in a draw. Therefor - the winner - and still the EEW Champion. Phillip Phillips!
Triple H steps over the wreckage at ringside, hauls Zeke Nero up and Pedigrees him. Then, he pushes the referees out of the way, hauls Phillip Phillip up and Pedigrees him onto the Cell wall, before leaving the Arena!
The program fades to black
