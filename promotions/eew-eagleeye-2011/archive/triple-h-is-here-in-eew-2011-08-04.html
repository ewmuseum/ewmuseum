Triple H is here in EEW

4 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Triple H — 5 August 2011 The lights in the arena go dim. Nobody knows what is going to happen or who is going to come out of the curtain. Suddenly, the lights turn up again, now a shade of green. The titantron and minitron remain black. Fire it up By Black Label Society plays and the fans start cheering, they still have no idea who is going to come out.
Jim Ross: Well it looks like we're going to see another new face here in EEW! The intro of the song finishes and everything goes silent. Suddenly, the song starts up again from where it had left off, the titantron and minitron light up with Triple H graphics as HHH himself walks out onto stage, smiling to himself. He is wearing a leather jacket and jeans. Shades glisten in front of his eyes as he looks around at the audience before taking a swig of water and spewing it out in front of him.
Jim Ross: BAH GAWD, IT'S TRIPLE H! THE GAME IS WITH US IN EAGLE EYE WRESTLING! HHH walks down the ramp with the fans holding out there hands, trying to get him to slap them. He stops at the ring apron and the camera pans around him. Eventually, he carries on and climbs the apron. The lighting turns white and he does another signature water spit into the air. He gets into the ring and the music dies down. He asks for a microphone and is given one.
TRIPLE H If you don't know my name already... I am The Game, The Cerebral Assassin, The Best, Triple H!
The fans cheer and HHH smiles. TRIPLE H And I'm here, not to manage this show, but to do what I do best, wrestle and win championships. See, for quite some time now, I've not been the person I used to be. Some might say, I got soft. That's all going to change during the time I'm signed with this company. I'm not gonna walk around in a suit acting all executive, I'm not going to... Dress in camoflague and jump around like a freaking clown, I'm not going to put up with the same bullshit that other companies made me put up with in recent years. No, I'm here to be the Triple H that was forced into a politically correct scheme to get more money for a greedy boss, the Triple H that did everything to stop that from happening.
The fans cheer loudly. TRIPLE H But that's enough shooting for the time being, I know that this company will let me be myself, I feel sorry for the person that gets into this ring with me. I've had this... built up fury, this... Violence that has been swelling up inside my self concious for five years.
HHH pauses for a second then looks directly into the camera. TRIPLE H I'm done being a PG showpony and it's time to catch up on what I've been missing. He puts down the camera and taunts with the crowd on the top rope.
