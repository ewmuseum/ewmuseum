No Disqualification, no remorse.

30 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Triple H — 31 August 2011 We go to the backstage area, Todd Grisham is standing ready with a microphone in hand. TODD GRISHAM Ladies and gentlemen, standing with me at this time, Triple H. The camera pans out to reveal Triple H, standing with a microphone also, the crowd cheers loudly for 'The Game'.
TODD GRISHAM Now Triple H, tonight you face Mike Quackenbush in a no disqualification match to determine the number one contender for Phillip Phillip's EEW Championship at Burn Out. How do you feel about your chances in this one?
TRIPLE H Chances? Todd, in a no disqualification environment, for my opponents there is no such thing. I offer no chances for anyone that stands in my way especially when the most coveted prize in this company, a prize that I would currently be holding if it wasn't for our managerial team deciding that I was not being 'family friendly' enough. 'Family friendly' is something I've already wasted a good portion of my career on and if Phillip Phillip or The Miz thinks I'm going to stand for it any longer, they're mistaken. I stated my ambitions when I came here and I will stick by what I say. Triple H is not a family friendly person and neither should anyone in this company if they wish to come to my level.
HHH pauses for a while and looks into the camera with an intense look upon his face. TRIPLE H And if you thought what I got suspended for originally was bad, you've seen nothing yet. The No DQ match is a type of match designed for violence and designed for scores to be settled once and for all. No holds barred, anything can happen. I can do whatever I please to Quackenbush tonight and that gives me a somewhat redeeming feeling, the feeling that the real Triple H is finally going to once again come alive, the Triple H that took no bull from no man. That is what you're going to see tonight. It's not just for me, it's for fans of wrestling. For too long, wrestlers and even wrestling companies have been far too soft. My journey to ending that begins tonight. Mike, you have no idea what's coming to you, it's nothing personal, it's just my job. If you are the true wrestler that you like to call yourself, you'd understand and have the same mindset as me going into this match.
TODD GRISHAM And Phillip Phillips? TRIPLE H Phillip Phillips? Our C.O.O. My boss. The person that took my place on the top after I was suspended and then became one of the highest ranked members of EEW management behind The Miz. I'm not going to say it's a conspiracy on the part of The Miz... But it's definitely strange don't you think? Miz wanted Phillip to be the face of this company bad, well Miz, now I'm back. And your poster boy has kindly given me a shot at his championship belt, so if you don't mind I'll be taking it at Burn Out, try to force me out of that one Awesome one. Thanks for the interview Todd, I've got a match.
Triple H pushes the mic into the stomach of Grisham and walks out of frame. TODD GRISHAM Thankyou for your time, Triple H!
