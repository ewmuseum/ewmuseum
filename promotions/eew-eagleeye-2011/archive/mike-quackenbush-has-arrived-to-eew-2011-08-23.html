mike quackenbush has arrived to eew

23 August 2011

Mike Quackenbush — 23 August 2011 the music break it down again blasts through the speakers most of the crowd is confused but there are a few cheers from people who know who is about to come out
mike quackenbush comes out on the stage with a mic "I have arrived here in eew and i will make my way to the top very quickly" "for those of you who dont know who i am i will tell you" "I am the founder of CHIKARA,the man of 1000 holds, I AM "LIGHTNING"...MIKE...QUACKENBUSH" "for those of you in the back you will learn to respect this name because i will win the eew championship and i will fight anyone who stands in my way"
"and with my submissions,high flying ability, and power i am a force to be reckoned with" "speaking of submissions in my first match ever you will see me making my oponent tap in the middle of this ring to the CHIKARA SPECIAL"
"the chikara special is impossible to escape and will result in my opponent here in this ring screaming in pain and tapping out"
"so you guys in the back watch out cause i will take this federation by storm" "and i am now offering up a challenge for someone to face me in my debut match whenever it shall be"
Triple H — 24 August 2011 Mike stands in the ring for a while, waiting for someone to come out and accept his challenge. The audience are quiet, anticipating whoever it may be that chooses to face Mike in his debut match. A long time passes, and it appears that nobody is infact going to come out. Mike smiles and goes to exit the ring, that is until the music of none other than Triple H blasts over the P.A system.
Quackenbush jumps back and laughs to himself, clapping his hands as 'The Game' appears on stage. The crowd go crazy as he walks down the entrance ramp, never taking his eyes off of Quackenbush. He climbs onto the apron and enters the ring. Walking straight past Mike and asking for a microphone from one of the EEW ringside staff, he is handed one and then turns to face Quackenbush. The music fades down and there is a large pop from the audience as the two stair eachother down, electricity is in the air.
TRIPLE H So let me get this straight. You're out here offering an open challenge to anyone that wants to accept and face you in a match.
Mike nods his head, HHH nods too and carries on. TRIPLE H Thought so. Well you're in luck Mike, because I'm more than ready to take you on and send you home my friend. Alot have come and alot have gone, I've seen them all, young guns that think they can just walk on into a wrestling company and take over from day one. I'm not saying you're one of those people, we'll see in our match.
He pauses and Mike once again nods his head. TRIPLE H But I don't think we should just have a regular match that everyone will forget. No, it's your debut and people need to remember this. I couldn't help but notice you too want a shot at the EEW Championship, just like me. What do you say to this, you, me, tonight. The winner gets a shot at the EEW championship September 6th at Burn Out against our new COO, Phillip Phillips.
The crowd cheer and Mike nods his head and shakes HHH's hand. TRIPLE H So it's settled then, but it's not official yet. Phillip, get out here. TBC - PHILLIP PHILLIPS Phillip Phillips — 24 August 2011 It didn't take long for Phillip to walk out into the arena with his championship. His music did not play, and he immediately started talking.
PHILLIP PHILLIPS So you want this baby, huh? He held up the championship for Mike and Hunter to see, then slung it back over his shoulder
PHILLIP PHILLIPS Doesn't surprise me. It's really a nice looking belt, isn't it? Anyways, you can have your match. But just hold your horses for now, we already have the match card for Smash III. I'll tell you what: you guys can have your match next Wednesday on Smash IV. Whoever wins will fight me for the EEW Championship at Burn Out... but it won't be just any match. You two will have a no holds barred match...
Jim Ross: Oh my god! A no holds barred match! Man, I'm sure Phillip just wants them to kill eachother!
Jerry Lawler: Well it really is a good strategy. Think about it: this match will be brutal! Phil probably wants them in the worst possible condition so the match is easier for him.
The crowd cheered, already anxious for the match coming up the next week.
