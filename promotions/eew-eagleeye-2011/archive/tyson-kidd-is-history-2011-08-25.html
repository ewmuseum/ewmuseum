Tyson Kidd is history...

25 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sean Masters — 26 August 2011 The Miz swaggers down the aisle, ignoring the fans in attendance, climbing into the ring and grabbing a microphone
Jim Ross The Miz is here. And he's in a foul mood. Last week, The Miz was forced to finally sell Eagle Eye Wrestling to business tycoon Phillip Phillips!
Jerry 'The King' Lawler I don't think that's the reason he's mad. Last week, Tyson Kidd cost him a victory against new COO Phillip Phillips right here on Smash!
The Miz stares at the crowd, glances at the announce table, before staring at the camera and raising the microphone to his lips!
The Miz Before anybody asks questions, I'll tell you the truth. I'm not mad about giving up ownership of this company. I'm not mad about leaving the company to Phillip Phillips. I'm not mad about that 'pathetic tapeworm' Chris Jericho getting a shot at the Xtreme Championship. I'm sure as hell not mad about you fat, lazy, overweight slobs sitting here booing me each and every week....
The Miz' comments are met with a furious boo and a vicious 'You suck' chant from the crowd. Miz ignores this and smiles
But, I am mad about one thing. I'm mad about how Tyson Kidd embarrassed me last week. I could have easily defeated Phillip Phillips and Zeke Nero last week. But, what did Tyson do? Tyson took it upon himself to just lay in the middle of the ring and allow himself to be pinned while I was attacked by Zeke Nero! Then, after the match, does he help me up? No! Tyson Kidd just leaves the arena while I had to drag myself to my office!
Miz has more 'You suck' chants and various insults hurled at him Really? Lazy, insulting redneck slob? REALLY? You know what? I don't have to lower myself to the level of you pigs sitting in front of me. Becuase, I came out here to do one thing. I came out here to tell you how Tyson Kidd cost ME the match last week. I'm telling you about how I am going to beat him tonight. You get it? ME! ME! ME! ME! I could go on all day about how I was cost championships and matches along the line in my career! But, I don't see the point. Every single one of those people has bowed down to awesomeness! So, I'm gonna demonstrate to all of you what I'm going to do to Tyson Kidd TONIGHT! TYSON, GET OUT HERE, RIGHT NOW!!
The crowd watch as the TitanTron stays with it's 'Smash' logo and 'smashed' graphic card. Miz gets increasingly frustrated with Kidd's apparent no show!
COME ON! LET'S GO! Miz gets very frustrated and rolls out of the ring, when....... Tyson Kidd still doesn't show as his music blasts out across the arena. As Miz heads up the aisle to the backstage area, he is stopped by a blockade of ecurity guards
Tyson, you can hide behind your little buddies all you like. But, tonight, I make it my promise to you that I will put you on a 'bed of nails' when I beat you! BECAUSE I'M THE MIZ......AND I'M........AWESOME!
The show fades to an advert break as the security guards usher Miz out to backstage!
