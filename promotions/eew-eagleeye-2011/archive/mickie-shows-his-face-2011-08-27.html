Mickie Shows His Face

27 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mickie — 27 August 2011 The fans sit in the stands eating their popcorn and other assorted snacks, bought during the interval, waiting to see what the next part of the show will be, they’re just starting to wonder what’s taking so long when the lights go off and the speakers in the arena start blasting “Get Through This” by Art of Dying.
The lights turn on again but are now red, then blue, then green, then orange, then red again, flashing with different colours and illuminating the whole room in a constantly changing glow, slowly, a figure steps out from backstage, face pointing downwards to stop the light hitting it, as it steps forward it gets easier to recognise that the figure is in fact a man, and a fairly large one.
The man stops in the middle of the stage so that the fans can see him more clearly: he is wearing dark grey jeans, although it’s hard to tell if they were always grey or have just faded to that colour over many years of wear and tear; brown work boots, scuffed all around the toe caps with the, untied, shoe laces dragging along behind them and a black leather jacket, draped over his huge body, like a cloak.
Suddenly, the jacket flies back to the other side of the stage as the body which it was so comfortably resting on, threw itself backwards, lifting its head and raising its arms in a display of power, showing a dirty white T-Shirt and multiple dark red tattoos along the arms.
The crowd are unsure of who this man is, so decide to just cheer him for the fact that he had a pretty cool entrance as he raises a microphone to his lips.
MICKIE Thank you, although I think it’s a bit presumptuous to cheer me before you have even heard me speak, I mean, I could be some jerk who’s come out here to insult and call you slobs like The Miz does.
The fans are confused by the words of Mickie, however most of them boo when they hear The Miz mentioned.
MICKIE You see what I mean? You’ve already changed your tune about me now that I’ve spoken. Although I will say this, I’m not going to insult you for no reason like The Miz does because honestly, I don’t see the point in it, however, equally, I’m not going to thank you for coming and keeping our business going with your purchasing of merchandise and such. Because, honestly, I don’t care what you think, I’m not here to entertain you, and I’m not here to insult you, I’m here to crack heads and win championships!
Mickie gets a mixed response to his words, however he carries on speaking. MICKIE And the first head I’m going to crack, will be Mike Quacken-Spillane, I’m sorry but I’m not saying that ridiculous name, however when I beat him and he’s lying on the mat in pain, I will look down at him and say ‘Hard luck Quacky’ before I walk to the back to reap my rewards.
You see, Quacky says that "The Chikara Special is impossible to escape and will result in my opponent here in this ring screaming in pain and tapping out" but, I have to ask Quacky, have you ever been tied up in the boot of a locked car, barrelling down a hill into a river, because you forgot to pay Phil Mitchell the money you owe him, well I have and I can tell you, I can escape from ANYTHING and IF you are somehow able to knock me down, and IF you lock in the ‘Chikara Special’ then I’ll be out of that hold before you can say “Mike Quackenstupidname”.
Mickie lowers the microphone and waits to see if Mike Quackenbush will respond. Zeke Nero — 27 August 2011 *The fans boo as soon as Nero's music hits and he makes his out of the curtain. As soon as Nero gets to the ring he laughs at Mickie's apperance*
Zeke Nero So this is P2 partner the so called greatest contract signee in EEW history, well I'm not seeing much cause all I see low-down ungrateful brat in gray jeans. This is going to be an easy match. You know why Mickie well do you? Of course you don't your nothing and will continue to be so as LONG AS I'M IN THIS RING AND AM STILL IN THE TITLE CONTENDERSHIP. Also Mickie you shouldn't be worring about Mike cause he's not the one in this match against me and Punk, the most controversial team here in EEW yet you think you can last 5 seconds in here with me which is also the amount of time you shall tap out of my devistating armbar. But instead of waiting for our match how bout we settle this now
*Nero then hits Mickie with the mic knocking him down. Nero starts to pummel Mickie before security try to get Nero off. The security get Nero off but as soon as they drop their guard Mickie recovers and hits Nero from behind. Mickie picks up Nero and tries to clothesline him but Nero ducks and gets Mickie in a Cross Armbar. Mickie yells and the security struggles to get Nero off. Nero finally lets go and the refs check Mickie to see if any damage is done.*
J.R. My God what a despicable attack by Nero. I don't think Mickie is going to be able to compete.
Jerry Lawler But if you ask me Nero has a right to do this I mean he was screwed out of his title match and now he's on a path of destruction/
*Mickie miraculously gets up able to use his arm. Nero sees this and just looks at Mickie makes threats to him as Nero raises his arm in a sense of accomplisment that he injured his opponent.*
J.R. I can't believe that Mickie has the ability to even moves his arm Jerry Lawler How is that I mean didn't you see how bad Mickie's arm bent. But I think Nero knows he acomplished everything tonight
Phillip Phillips — 28 August 2011 All of the sudden, Phillip Phillips walked out. The crowd erupted in cheers. Finally, someone to save them from the monster that was Zeke Nero. He had no music playing, for he immediately started talking.
PHILLIP PHILLIPS Zeke, you have got to get a hold of yourself. You can't just constantly be attacking people out of the blue. That's pathetic and unprofessional. And you especially shouldn't be attacking my tag team partner. I have every right to suspend or even fire you, and I'm sure as hell not afraid to do so.
He pulled the microphone away from his mouth for a moment then entered the ring. He stared down Zeke Nero. Their gaze met, staring at eachother with pure hatred in eachother's eyes. Phillip slowly stepped forward and... cracked the microphone over Zeke Nero's head!
Jim Ross: Oh my god! A "microphone" shot out of nowhere! Phillip quickly went behind Zeke Nero, then grabbed him in a chickenwing. He held the submission in for a moment, temporarily paralyzing his arms. He then pulled him over his shoulders and crashed to the ground, driving Zeke Nero neck-first onto the mat. He stared menacingly at Zeke, then exited backstage without a word as the medics aided Mickie with his arm.
