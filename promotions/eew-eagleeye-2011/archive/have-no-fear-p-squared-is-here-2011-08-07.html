Have No Fear, P Squared is Here

7 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phillip Phillips — 7 August 2011 Across the Nation blared through the arena. The crowd was quiet for the most part; the music was unfamiliar. Walking down the ramp was a man with a handlebar moustache, brown hair and a zebra print singlet. Accompanying it was black elbow pads, knee pads, and boots. He was carrying a microphone. He pulled himself up to the apron, paused for a moment, then pushed down the rope and stepped over it.
Jim Ross: We've never heard this music before... I wonder who it could be? PHILLIP PHILLIPS Hello, there, EEW fans. You probably don't know me yet... which is a real shame. Anyways, allow me to introduce myself. My name... is Phillip Phillips. And do you know how I'm debuting? In a match... a tournament, to be exact... for the first ever... E... E... W... champion!
Jerry Lawler: Phillip Phillips huh? Never heard of him before, what about you J.R.? Jim Ross: I know about as much about him as you do, Jerry. He paused. The crowd now was much louder. There was a mixture of cheers and boos; most were indecisive having seen very little of his personality so far.
PHILLIP PHILLIPS Anyways, I'm pretty damn sure I'm gonna win it, and you'll be too once you see me in that ring. Because, EEW fans... it turns out, I have skill. And a helluva lot more of it than the other guys here. I have more talent than... Jeff Hardy... than Triple H... than Zeke Nero... than all of the guys on this roster, combined in my pinkie finger alone.
He turned towards the ramp, directing his speech at the people backstage. PHILLIP PHILLIPS So, remember this... do not make the mistake of getting in my way, or you will seriously regret it... not only will you fall into submission under the power of my deadly, kickass, and well-waxed moustache, but I will kick your ass straight through the damn roof. Okay? Okay. End of story. Goodbye.
He tossed the microphone off into no place in particular, then jumped over the top rope, off the apron, and continued up the ramp.
Chris_Jericho — 9 August 2011 Jim Ross: Well what an impact phillip has made. Do you think he has what it takes to win the championship. He already claimed that he is better than anyone on the roster. That will be his greatest downfall
Jerry Lawler: What are you saying JR? He's great he just intimidated the whole crowd and not one superstar interupted him. JR just watch he will be the new champion of EEW. No one can go against him that cockiness and courage will get him far
The crowd goes silent as they grew tired of phillips.They talk amongst themselves awaiting the next segment. JR and Jerry try to enlighten the crowd talking more about Phillips.
Jim Ross: The crowd thinks nothing of it. They agree that this imposter of a wrestler will never amount to anything. I have no idea what his name is any ways.
Jerry Lawler: What you can't temember Phillips Phillips he has one name and it's repeated like Kelly Kelly. The crowd is only speechless on what has just occured just you wait. He is the newest EEW champion he has the 3 qualities Charisma, Courage and .....................
The Lights go out the arena is in complete darkness. The crowd wonders what happened they all hush up.
A Countdown Appears The crowd screams as they realized from the countdown who is to come. They await the sexy beast they are all screaming at the top of there lungs Y2J Y2J Y2J.
Break The Wall Down theme hits the fireworks go the crowd Cheers constantly barely hearing Chris Jericho's theme.
Chris Jericho Jumps out with energy with a big smile on his face. The Crowd explodes with cheers. He soaks up the arena feel and walks down the ramp.
Jim Ross: My Gawd it's Chris Jericho. Y2J has come to EEW I can't believe my eyes. A top contender from WWE decided to come here. He has come to EEW what do you think he is doing here Jerry Lawler.
Jerry Lawler: Y2J has come to destroy this business. Bring back Phillip I say. *Chris Jericho picks up a Microphone. He looks around the arena with a smile and hesitates*
Chris Jericho I'mmmmmmmmmmmmm BACK! This is my home EEW or in other words wrestling unlike WWE. I'm here to prove a point to prove to this business that I am the one and only Y2J and I'm here to prove how to be a champion. I am a prestigous champion unlike the nobody who showed up. I have said it more than once that I am the best in the world at what I do who else to be the EEW Champion. As you people all know I respect you but that Locker Room is full of hypocritical tapeworms who promise things but never succeed unlike me. I am the first ever undisputed champion a 7 time world champion and a 9 time intercontenential champion.
Jerry Lawler Jericho has no right to insul....... Chris Jericho: Shut up Jerry. No one paid you to interupt me I am the very Icon of this business I am here to prove a point that I am better than everyone on this roster that kid phillip mentioned a few and I beat all of those hootenanny, I have won more champions than them this is an honour not for me but for this business alone you all paid to see Chris Jericho as a Champion
In a sudden the Crowd were dissapproved and started Booing Jericho. A distant chant of Jericho sucks is heard as it grows louder the fury on Jericho face shows.
Chris Jericho: Oh you suddenly change your mind the fact that Jericho is better than all of you haunts you. This is another reason why I will make Phillips just a distant memory oh wait he already is. I have a match against him and all you people care about who says nice things and such no no. I'm not like that I am who I am and you people will learn a lesson you won't soon forget based on talent
The Crowd chants Phillip rules Phillip rules Chris Jericho: SHUT UP! Can't you people see I'm saving you from a terrible cause I am here trying to make this business better and you cheer this nobody. For one no one knows him, he won nothing it's no surprise he is here in a failing company I saved this company coming here. The man you cheer has nothing on accomplishments as much as it angers me Hardy is better. This person who calls himself the EEW champion he proclaims it but as you know he is just a pawn and will result to nothing but a spec of dust
Jim Ross : is their any stopping Chris Jericho. Your opinion Jerry oh wait your too afraid of the superstar who will put EEW on a map he is Chris Jericho he will turn this into a slobberknocker of a place
Chris Jericho: You have witnessed the future champion. Unlike the others in this so called tournament if they were smarter they would just hand it to me as everyone on this roster lacks talent. Just watch as I put all of these "wrestlers" to their place. Now I leave you with this whats his name umm its Phillips oh him he has met up with someone who is out of his league. Now I will bid Farewell as EEW is JERICCCCCCCCCCCCHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHOOO hypocrites
Chris Jericho has a cocky smile and looks at the arena with disgust as he throws his mic at Jerry Lawler and mouthes you better not talk. he leaves the ring and looks at the crowd and says I'm Better than all of you
The Crowd Boo with all there might while chanting Phillip rules Jericho Sucks Jim Ross: Now thats an Impact Jerry unlike that nobody Phillips Phillip Phillips — 9 August 2011 Phillip smirked, then lifted the microphone again. PHILLIP PHILLIPS Chris, Chris, Chris... will you ever learn? Hey, come back here. Don't be a coward. I have a little something to say to you.
He paused, and the crowd began to chant his name. Jerry Lawler: Well, the crowd certainly has a different opinion on this guy than they did a couple minutes ago when he debuted.
Jim Ross: That's for darn sure, King. PHILLIP PHILLIPS You see, there's a reason these people boo you and cheer me on... they spent their hard-earned money to come to this show. And you're just wasting that money by ranting on about god knows what; whatever it is, nobody cares about. Not to mention your wrestling skills are... how do I put this? Boring? Repetitive? Lacking talent and skill? Anyways, the point is... we all know who's gonna win that championship in the end. Here, I'll give that little walnut you call a brain a hint: it's certainly not gonna be you.
The crowd exploded, now cheering the charming yet cocky Phillip. Jim Ross: What a statement! I have to admit, Phillip sure made a point there. Jerry Lawler: He certainly did. We're gonna have a great match this Wednesday... two superstars that already hate eachother, battling it out in a brutal tournament to determine who will compete in the triple threat match for the first ever EEW Champion. Also competing are Zeke Nero, Triple H, CM Punk, and Michael.
Chris_Jericho — 9 August 2011 Chris Jericho theme hits as the Crowd Boo's, he walks down the ramp with a face of pure rage stops and stares at Phillips with anger and mouthes you want some.
Jim Ross What is Jericho doing here are they gonna brawl. I can't believe it they are go show him who's boss Jericho
Jerry Lawler Get in the ring Jericho get beat up by the newcomer show everyone the wuss you are! The Crowd cheers as Jericho gets near but stops and walks away. The crowd Boo's and chant Jericho's a coward repeatedly.Jericho stops at the top of the ramp and mouthes the words your not even worth the trouble with a smirk on his face and leaves the arena
Jim Ross What a display by Jericho he made all these people think that they were gonna brawl. He is a great competitor and works the crowd with the slightest of ease
Jerry Lawler Jericho is just a coward he didn't have the guts to take on a newcomer who will become the new EEW champion. Take that Jericho!
Jim Ross We'll see about that King. In all due respect Jericho will defeat this newbie with more experience under his belt. Phillips made the impact but he won't succeed with it. Until Wednesday this will be settled. Who will make it to the next round only time will tell.
Zeke Nero — 9 August 2011 Nero's Music hits as Jericho makes his way up the ramp Jim Ross What the hell. Why is Nero here Jerry Lawler He must of heard those things Phillips said cause as well all know Nero holds a grudge Nero stops in front and Jericho and tells him to get out of the way. Jerihco gets furious and calls Nero a hypocrite before slapping him. Nero grabs Jericho and delivers a spinebuster to him
Jim Ross BAH GAWD. Nero almost broke Jericho's damn back with that spinebuster Jerry Lawler Well as I said Nero holds a grudge and will deliver moves to the fullest extent when angry Nero Slides into the ring before grabbing a mic from the bellkeeper Zeke Nero So this is the famous Phillip Phillips you know in all MY time of wrestling I've heard you name, but It was always a joke compared to when people heard my name. So I don't think you have a right to come down here saying your better than me the person who made famous the promotions such as TNA, ROH, and OVW making all of your accomplishments but a minimal dream. So you see "P2" your just a stuck as Triple H, Jeff Hardy, CM Punk, and The Miz thinking that they know who the fuck I am but in reality are just as jelous of my success as you are right now.
After Nero speaks he heads back up the ramp staring cautiously at Phillips Jim Ross Well looks like Phillips has not one but two people to look out for now in EEW Jerry Lawler You got that right JR what I believe everyone wants to know is who is going to strike first Jericho, Nero, or Phillips
Phillip Phillips — 10 August 2011 Once again, Phillip smirked at both Zeke and Chris' failed attempts to make a promo well enough for his standards.
PHILLIP PHILLIPS Oh, isn't this so much fun? Yeah, happy Make Fun of the Guy Who's Gonna Kick Your Ass Day! Listen, you better watch your mouth, because there will be severe consequences. And I'm not just talking to Y2J and Zeke here. That goes for all of you losers backstage.
Jim Ross: Well, I'm sure we'll see about that later on. The crowd cheered, continuing to chant his name. PHILLIP PHILLIPS As for you, Zeke... don't judge a book by it's cover; just for the record, I've been in all those promotions, replace OVW with FCW, plus NJPW, ECW, WCW, CMLL, and CZW. I probably have at least 5 more years of experience than you.
Jerry Lawler: Well, it certainly sounds like Phillip has plenty of tricks up his sleeve! And I'm sure we'll see that come in to play tommorow on our first episode of Smash!
Jim Ross: Come on, King. Quit playing favorites here. I'm sure everyone will try their best to win, not just Phillip. The tournament is to determine who will compete to crown the first ever EEW Champion, King! Everyone is gonna want to do their best.
Jerry Lawler: Well, I'll have to agree with that.
