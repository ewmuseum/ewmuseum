Phillip Phillips — 7 August 2011 Wrestler's Stage Name: Phillip Phillips Wrestler's Real Name: Phillip Phillips Height: 6'0" Weight: 245 Pounds Location: Versoix, Canton of Geneva, Switzerland Alignment: Heel/tweener Theme Song: Across the Nation by Union Underground Finishers: 1. Vertebreaker Setup: While the opponent is bent over (often due to a toe kick), Phillip either a) goes behind them, gets them in a chickenwing, then pulls the opponent over his shoulders (a faster version)
or b) while standing with their back facing the (bent over) opponent, hooks both their arms, then turns over and pulls them over his shoulder
No matter which variation he uses, it'll always end with him sitting down and driving their upper back into the mat. Sometimes done off the top rope or onto a chair.
2. Crash & Burn (flip-style moonsault, often followed by a pin) Setup: Climb the turnbuckle (or some sort of raised object) while the opponent is down. While facing away from the opponent, he jumps, spins around so he is facing the opponent, then spins again to land on the opponent like with a normal splash.
Signature Moves 1. Gullotine Setup: Modified gullotine choke; DDT transitioned into sleeper hold with scissors, sometimes reverse (inverted DDT into dragon sleeper w/ scissors)

P2 holds the microphone to his lips. "Hello, EEW fans! You may no longer worry about this awful roster boring you to death... because Phillip Phillips is now here. And you can be damn sure I'll kick ass. I'm not afraid to fight anyone, whether seven-foot-five or five-foot-seven. I'm going to make the biggest impact any of you have ever seen. I'll go out of my way and do anything to win a championship... or even just a match. I can win any match type, whether with a shitton of rules, in a cage, or surrounded by all kinds of weapons. Because I'm Phillip Phillips... and that's how I roll!"
The crowd explodes with a mixture of cheers and boos after hearing what he had to say. Phillip pauses and strokes his moustache, seemingly oblivious to the crowd's reaction. Finally, he began to speak again, this time to the ones booing.
"Hey! Screw you! You'll regret this later, believe me. Because guess what? I'm better than all those assclowns that think they are so great. So mark my words: one day, you'll be cheering me!"
P2 drops the mic and grins, then began to walk out of the ring and up the ramp...
