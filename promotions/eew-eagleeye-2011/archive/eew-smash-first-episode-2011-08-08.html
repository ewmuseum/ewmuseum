Sean Masters — 9 August 2011 [center] What better way to start off the first show than to have the First Round of a tournament to crown the EEW Champion? Phillip Phillips and Chris Jericho square off in the first ever match in EEW history! Who will advance to the tournament's Triple Threat Final?
EEW Championship Tournament First Round Match Phillip Phillips vs Chris Jericho Following straight on from the first match, the conniving CM Punk faces the 'King of Kings' Triple H! Will 'The Game' destroy the 'Straight Edge' Superstar, or will Punk defy the odds and defeat 'The Game'?
EEW Championship Tournament First Round Match CM Punk vs Triple H In the final First Round Match in the Tournament, Zeke Nero faces the mysterious 'Michael'! Will Zeke Nero prevail, or will his mysterious opponent prevail and take the EEW Championship from EEW, after refusing to negotiate a contract?
