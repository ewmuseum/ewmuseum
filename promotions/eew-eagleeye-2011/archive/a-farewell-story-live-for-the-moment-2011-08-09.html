A farewell story... Live for the moment

9 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeff Hardy — 9 August 2011 Remedy by Seether hits the speakers and the crowd goes wild for there co-Owner, Jeff Hardy.
He makes his way to the ring and seems rather sad, looking at the crowd like he was to die the next day.
He gets in the ring and gets a mic handed to him by EEW personnel. Jeff Hardy: So... I am sad to announce the release of... He takes a long pause and the crowd waits exited. Jeff Hardy: The co-owner Jeff Hardy, whitch is me... Obviously... The crowd seems shocked to hear the news off Jeff Hardy's release. Jeff Hardy: This does not mean that i won't be back. But I will never be the co-owner again. Next time you see me I will be wrestling and doing the things I love. Being a man in a suit is not me. I am not comfortable in a suit I am only truly happy on the top rope. I couldn't get what I wanted as co-owner. I want gold around my waist. So I will leave now... And Miz... When i get back, I will hurt you. I will break every bone in your body and make you retire as a footnote, a nobody. To everybody... I will be back.. I don't know when but it's going to be... Extreme
Remedy re-hits the speakers and Jeff Hardy leaves the EEW ring area while the crowd is starting to talk about what just happened.
Triple H — 10 August 2011 You're not fired, you're just not co-owner anymore. Jeff Hardy — 10 August 2011 I am also RELEASED from eew
