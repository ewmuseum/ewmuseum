======================================================================= The crowd start cheering Nero’s name, hyping themselves up for the main event. Then, ‘Cult of Personality’ by Living Colour strikes the PA System, sending the crowd into a frenzy!
Justin Roberts This match is set for one fall. Approaching the ring first…….from Washington D.C. Weighing in at 245 Pounds. ZEKE NERO!
Jim Ross There he is, Zeke Nero! Possibly the hot favorite to win this match. His music, along with everybody else’s is on iTunes now!
Jerry ‘The King’ Lawler And, we’d like to say a huge thank you to Green Day for providing the official theme for Smash! ‘Murder City’ is in stores on Green Day’s album ‘21st Century Breakdown’ or as a single for 99 Cents on iTunes!
Suddenly, the lights on the HD set go out. Then, just a hazy blue light covers the TitanTron!
Justin Roberts And the opponent. Michael! Zeke Nero looks in disbelief as Michael does not come from backstage! Justin Roberts And his opponent…………..Michael! Zeke Nero grows increasingly annoyed when Michael’s TitanTron stops, and rolls out of the ring. The crowd continue to cheer as Zeke begins to walk towards backstage!
Jim Ross Well, it seems we won’t be having a Main Event match here on Smash! Zeke Nero is heading backstage!
Jerry ‘The King’ Lawler I have a feeling he’s going to find ‘Michael’ and get this match underway! Zeke Nero walks through the curtain and into the backstage area, asking random personnel if they have seen Michael! Frustrated, Zeke bursts into Miz’ office to find Michael chatting with Miz!
The Miz Zeke, what are you doing here? Get out! Zeke Nero Not until I have my opponent in the ring with me! Zeke grabs Michael and throws him out of Miz’ office, before dragging him towards the curtain. The fans burst out in cheers as Zeke emerges from the curtain, throwing Michael down the ramp. The bell rings and the referee begins counting!
1….2…. Jim Ross Zeke Nero’s found Michael, and his prepared to make him suffer here! 3….4…. Zeke Nero throws Michael into the Steel Steps, before rolling him into the ring! Michael stumbles to his feet, dazed and confused, before Zeke hits his famous ‘Nitak Hashi’ Jumping Cutter!
Jerry ‘The Lawler The Nitak Hashi connects! But I don’t think Zeke’s done here! He’s going into the corner! Zeke waits for Michael to stumble to his feet, before hitting his ‘Ninak Hashi’ Spear, before pinning Michael’s shoulders down, and hooking his leg!
1….2….3! ‘Cult of Personality’ rings out as the referee raises a victorious Zeke Nero’s hand in victory! Behind them, Michael crawls to the ropes and starts to drag himself back up to his feet!
Justin Roberts Here is your winner! ZEKE NERO! Zeke leaves the ring, and high fives fans as he makes his way up the ramp to the backstage area. Suddenly, as Michael walks across the ring, a blonde figure appears in the ring, holds his shoulders
and hits him with a Jumping Reverse Bulldog! Jim Ross Baw Gawd! This young man has just come into the ring and laid Michael out! Jerry ‘The King’ Lawler That’s Dolph Ziggler, JR! But why has he just jumped the barricade and given Michael the ‘Zig Zag’?
The fans boo as Miz’ TitanTron switches on and ‘I Came to Play’ by Downstait hits the PA System!
The Miz Ladies and Gentlemen! Let me introduce the newest acquisition to Eagle Eye Wrestling…………DOLPH ZIGGLER!
The crowd boo as the show fades to black…..
