Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

International Championship Wrestling introduced itself in a single post of 30 April 1995: “I'm starting a new e-wrestling league, which I will use WLS 5.0. to determine the league. This league is the ICW, International Championship Wrestling.” Below that line it laid out its own entry form in full — name, finisher and a description of it, an optional manager or valet, face or heel, and seventy-five points to spend across ten stats (Strength, Toughness, Conditioning, Intelligence, Cheating, Brawling, Technical, Mat Wrestling, Aerial and Quickness) — simulated by the WLS 5.0 wrestling engine rather than scripted by hand.

Its own founding post conditioned the league's start on securing enough entrants: “I'll start the league as soon as I get enough singles wrestlers to start the world title tournament.” Nothing in the surviving record shows that condition was ever met.

No further post, roster, card or result survives under this promotion's name; whether International Championship Wrestling ever actually ran is not known.
