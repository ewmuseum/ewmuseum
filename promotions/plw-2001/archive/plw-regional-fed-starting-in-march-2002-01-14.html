[PLW] Regional Fed Starting In March

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now, first of all, this won't be a fed that's going to close in three weeks. I run the NYSWF ( http://www.itsonlyinmyhead.com/NYSWF/ ) and it's been open since August of 1998. Even in the worst of times, I didn't give up, so only expect the same for the PLW. Now, about the PLW. The basics: Name: Promise Land Wrestling Promise Land Wrestling is an independent wrestling federation run only in the continental US. It is split into three regions - Atlantic, Pacific, and Central. Each region has one title (Atlantic US, Pacific US, Central US) in it, and then there is a United States Heavyweight Title, as well as a United States Tag Team Title. Four times a year, supercards will be held where, for one night only, the three regions come together and compete against each other. Only on these nights are the two US Titles (Heavyweight & Tag) defended against wrestlers from other regions. During the rest of the year, all titles will be defended in the region that they wind up in (although the 3 regional titles always stay in those regions). How does it run? Well, for one, there is no roleplay board. No, it's not an e-mail based fed, either. Quite simply, the PLW is run with on-cards only. Members work angles with each other and then send in an oncard for the show each week to further the angle, and get their characters over. If there are no roleplay boards or packets, how do we determine a winner? Well, the night before results go up, Power Elite (a group of staff members) will hold a meeting. We will look over each oncard individually. Through that process, we will decide who wins based on the following criteria: -Who has the best work of the night? -Who has the best work overall? -Who has the best angle overall? -Who has the most to benefit angle-wise from a win? As for cards, they will work differently than any fed I've seen as well. The show (PLW United TV) is put together by three shows in three arenas with three main events. To clarify, each region has its show in whichever arena it is in that week. The shows are then put together on United TV as one large show. I want to post only certain matches for the cards, and keep others to form on-card. Since there aren't roleplays and winners are based on the amount of work each handler puts into his or her angle, then that won't be a problem for anyone. When you join, you won't be placed into a region by request, unless it's an absolute must. Otherwise, you are randomly placed. When you join, you also (and this is a big one) must join with a new wrestler. You wouldn't see The Rock in CZW or Steve Austin in IPW, so your e-wrestling superstar wouldn't fit into PLW. New characters are wanted here because, as an indy fed with a small budget and all, it makes more sense that way, right? Right now, PLW is in a hiring stage. We are looking for: -Handlers/Superstars -Match Writers That's basically all we need. I'll post this again every so often so new readers and people that may have missed it can get a glimpse of this fed. I urge you to join now, so you have your spot cemented when we open on March 1, 2002. To find PLW, check out: http://www.itsonlyinmyhead.com/PLW/ The rules aren't up on the page yet, as they will be added sometime in hte near future. You don't need to know much more information than what was included in this message and the release in the "News" section of the site, though, so don't worry. So if you want to join as a wrestler, fill out an application. If you want to join as a match writer, e-mail me at [detail withheld] . Any questions or comments? E-mail me at [detail withheld] with whatever inquiries you have. Thanks for reading, folks, and I hope to see you there! [name withheld] http://www.itsonlyinmyhead.com
