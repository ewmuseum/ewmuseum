INTRODUCING NEW POWER WRESTLING!! http://go.to/NPW "Welcome to a new e-fed. New Power Wrestling is dedicated to giving TALENTED and DEDICATED role-players a fair shake. As someone who has experienced such things in the past, I will never dole out title reigns to participants based on reputation. If you consistently perform, you will win. Even if you are not the world champion, however, remember that this is a game. Have fun, play with enthusiasm, and you will have a good time here. I know I will."
- from the New Power Wrestling homepage ( go.to/NPW ) NPW is a BOOKED federation, run through e-mail, allowing only FICTIONAL wrestlers. New Power Wrestling - Rules & Regulations -Flashes (Interviews) will be handled by e-mail. [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) will be distributing the flashes several times a week. All roleplays should be e-mailed to him. If the flash is done in an interview format, then the main interviewer is Rusty Baker. He's a young sort of geeky kid, a la Michael Cole. You can also use Todd Akers (face) or Joey Glance (heel). -Celebrities: Please try to avoid using celebrities in your roleplay. It's just kind of silly. There have been TONS of roleplays written with a wrestler visiting the Howard Stern show, and it's just not very creative any more. -Ripoff Characters: Don't waste my time with characters named "The Complete Set" Flex Fluger or "Ice Cold" Steve Boston. When I say I want original characters, I don't mean pull out the thesaurus. -Remember to Roleplay consistantly. On a week where you have a match, you should generally write at least 3 roleplays. On idle weeks, try to write 2. If you have an upcoming PPV match, the sky is the limit! -"Kayfabe" is important. Do NOT, do NOT, DO NOT do angles or flashes that bring "real life" into the game. That is not cool. If you have a real-life argument with someone, that's your business, not ours. Be mature, and leave that in real life, not NPW. -Use of Other Wrestlers: Do not put words into other people's mouths (unless it is necessary as part of an APPROVED angle). Also, do not use another wrestler in your roleplays. -Selling Angles: If someone runs an angle against you where they burn your house down, for GOD'S sake don't say "Uhh... I didn't have a house!!" It makes you look stupid for making up a lame excuse, and it just makes all the storylines seem really awkward. -Swearing: Swearing is not a major issue. You may use it, but please don't make it a crutch. Try to remember that wrestling is generally either on network television or basic cable, and try to keep it realistic. -Racism: Blatant racism will not be tolerated. Occasional tongue-in-cheek reference to certain stereotypes might be accepted, but I wouldn't advise making a habit of it. Rule of thumb - If you're not sure if it would be allowed, then don't do it. -Angles: All angles/run-ins to be run during the course of a card should be sent in via e-mail to [name withheld] (
[detail withheld] ). -Match-Writing: Volunteering to write out detailed matches for Friday Night Free-for- All is not required, but it is definitely smiled upon.
