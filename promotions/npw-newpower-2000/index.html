Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Power Wrestling opened in January 2000 as a booked, e-mail-run federation restricted to fictional wrestlers, its own rules explicitly turning away characters built to resemble real performers.

Its founding rules packet set out a full house style before a single card had run: a flash distributor for roleplays, a separate route for run-in angles, a face-and-heel commentary team, and a ring announcer named alongside them.

The promotion drew a hard line against real life bleeding into its own fiction, its rules warning handlers directly to keep any outside arguments out of the game entirely.

A sample match printed inside the rules themselves, built deliberately around a nameless "Whatshisname" against an equally placeholder "Joe Blow," doubled as both a style guide and a wink at how many of the hobby's own matches actually got written.
