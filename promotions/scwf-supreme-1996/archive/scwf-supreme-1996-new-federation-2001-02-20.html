[SCWF] New Federation; Looking for Handlers!

February 20, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm trying to get a new federation started called the SCWF, Supreme Championship Wrestling Federation.Anyway, I'm posting the Federation Information and an application in the hopes that there are some people out there looking for a decent league to join. If you want to check out the homepage, the address is
http://www.cyanide.f2s.com/scwf/ .. There is an online form application there as well. Hope to hear from you.. ----------- SCWF Introduction I. Supreme Championship Wrestling Federation started almost 4 years ago now, however unfortantly folded after about 11 months due to the fact that I didn't have the staff nor the time to keep up to a 2 card a week schedule not to mention the PPV card each month. When SCWF first started we were a booked federation from the very beginning, and I can't see that ever changing.
Rules II. 1. Because the roleplays are not scanned or monitored before being sent to the league, any roleplays that contain explicit material, racial slurs or comments and other material not suitable will come on the grounds for dismissal. In other words, use your own common sense.
2. If a handler is inactive for two weeks without prior notice, they will be put on notice. If there is no more activity or contact with them for the next week then they will be removed from the league.
3. Keep swearing to a minimal in your roleplays if you feel you need to use it at all. There is a time place for everything, agian, use your common sense. 4. Do not complain about losing a match, everyone has to lose at some point. If you lost there was probably a reason for your loss. 5. Last rule; don't take this too seriously, it's mean't to be fun. Roleplaying III. Roleplays will done through a mailing list which all wrestlers will have access to. I encourage handlers to contact one another about fueds, angles and even tag teams if they want. Becasue this is a mailign list, I would like to make it clear that this list is to be used for roleplays associated with your character and cards, results and league updates which I will send out to the league. If anyone sends out something that doesn't pertain to the league before clearing it with me first then suspension or dismissal will be in order. If you have something important you would like to send out to the list, send it to me first and I will forward it to the league.
Cards IV. This league will run on one card a week which will be titled Tuesday
Night Warzone. This is the card will contain usually 6 matches, and will be used to build the current angles. I hope to have atleast one title defence on this card, however that might not always be possible. The commentators for this match will be Commissioner Jeff Corrigan and "The Rock" Drake
Erich.
There will be thought given to having a second card a week this will be determined by the amount of wresters we have in the league.
Titles V. These are the titles listed in the league right now, There will be only a handfull which will be active right now until there are more wrestlers in the league. The ones marked as "Active" are the ones which are up for grabs the rest are not recognized by the league yet.
SCWF World Title - Active - This title is the big one, if you have it around your waist your probably the best damn wrestler in the league. However you must defend it atleast 2 each month, including the PPV card.
SCWF Television Title - Active - This title is defended atleast 3 times a month, if not more. If you own this title consider yourself a top contender in the league, however with that comes a lot of title defenses, so to hold on to this one you'd better be good.
SCWF Lightweight Title - This title will be highly respected in the lightweight divison. This is available to wrestler 230 lbs and under and have an intense way of wrestling which will keep the fans on their feet throughout the whole match. Yes ladies and gentlemen, you'd better know how to fly.
SCWF Hardcore Title - Available to anyone, however not everyone has the guts to hold this one. Expect some bruises and cuts if your holding this one.
This is defended only in special hardcore matches, stipulations will be booked by myself most of the time.
SCWF Tag Team Title - Hopefuly this one will be the first to be activated. This is available to any tag team, and must be defended atleast twice a month including the PPV card.
