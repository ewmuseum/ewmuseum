Seeking New Talent

23 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************* ** TALENT? ** ************* [SCENE: Dave Risk's flat, 111 Meadow St., Richmond, VA. Currently Dave's on the phone, talking it up.]
DAVE RISK: [on the phone] Okay -- right. Right. Yeah... mmhmm. [With his feet kicked up on his desk, Dave Risk, 1/3 owner of the Richmond-based Hammer Wrestling organization, twirls his pen. The cheap,
"assembly-required" white, plywood desk is cluttered with stacks of legal notes, contracts, and other odds and ends -- details needed for their federation's restart.]
DR: [still on the phone] It's been good speaking with you, Midas... okay... mmhmm... [From the adjacent room comes another owner 1/3 of Hammer Wrestling, Ted
Bruce. Ted, dressed in his best khakis and dark polo, looks smug as ever.
Busily sipping a wine cooler, Bruce takes a half-arsed seat on the edge of
Risk's desk.] DR: [still on the phone] Right. Okay... we'll see you then. BYE. #CLIK# DR: *sighs* TED BRUCE: Ah, who was that? DR: Well, it's a guy interested in employment with us... sounds promising. Midas. Johnny Midas. Some rich kid, I think. Guess he, uh, wants to mock our poverty... or something.
TB: Right -- so how many does that put us up to, dammit? [Risk leans back in his chair some, thinking a moment. Calculating.] DR: Eh... including Midas? [Ted nods.] DR: One. TB: One? DR: One. TB: ONE?! [Ted throws his hands up in the air and shakes his head.] TB: How in the craplocks are we gonna restart this damn federation with
_one_
damn member, dammit?
DR: Well, his dad's rich- TB: So??! Is he going to buy a robot for his son to wrestle!? DR: Well, uhm... no- TB: So what good is it, dammit?! We've got just _one_ guy! DR: Ted, we'll find more people! Have some patience! TB: Ah, to hell with this, dammit! [Shaking his head, Ted Bruce, one-third owner of Hammer Wrestling (barely), leaves Dave's "office." Hammer Wrestling has but one wrestler... still no sponsors... and still no place to hold their events.]
DR: Well. Crap. [Fades.] *********** ** NOTES ** *********** Ted and Dave have one wrestler -- which means _we_ have on wrestler! But, of course, we're looking for plenty more! Hammer Wrestling's only two days into recruitment, and I feel optimistic. We've one great application on the shelf and plenty of "yeah, I'll join" types ('course, they need to be getting me their applications!).
Hammer Wrestling's still looking around for a gaggle of people to join in on the fun and have a great time. I'm shooting for a March 7th show, but hey...
if it doensn't work out for then, then I'll wait. So if you're interested, intrigued, or just plain bored and unable to get into those feds that have waiting lists backed up from here to Taiwan, then hop on aboard! There's a website now, too! So that simplifies things greatly!
