Non-taped House Show* ST. JAMES' CHRISTIAN SCHOOL AUDITORIUM Spottsylvania, Virginia * This show is considered a "non-taped house show." In other words, all matches appearing on this card are in a summarized format. House Shows are merely rankings builders -- rewards to players who've participated and worked. The regular cards will have fully written out matches and count a lot more towards a players' record.
************* ** RESULTS ** ************* VINNIE "THE ENFORCER" CARLUCCI p. EVE DYERS (3:44) -> Recently signed athlete, Vinnie "The Enforcer" Carlucci, started off this match well against older female Hammer Star, Eve Dyers. Carlucci, a New Yorker, used his odd combination of mat technique and show- boating tricks to manhandle Dyers.
-> The match didn't last too long, though. After a belly-to- belly suplex flattended Dyers, Carlucci rolled on top and got the one-two-three for his first Hammer success.
-> Following the match, he and his manager, Mike "The Muzzle" Simms (who was nearly as large as Carlucci) played up the crowd a bit. Through the match the two got a pretty decent crowd response. Oddly enough, Dave Risk met with the two as they were exitting the ring.
Heading backstage, the three seemed to be having some sort of business discussion -- but about what?
METRO/BOB p. EYE OF THE BEHOLDER (Gom and Gon) (5:31) -> Not getting a whole lot of fan approval with his first appearance in Hammer, Metro really caught some heat early on. Especially after trying to put the moves on
Ring Announcer, Sherry Michelle, catching a comical slap in the face ... which he thought was amusing!
-> Teamed with "Bob", who, by all appearances, looked to be nothing more than a waste of space on Hammer's roster, the two took on Eye of the Beholder. Metro did extremely well against Gom and Gon of the Eye, but Bob seemed have some difficulty each time he was in the ring.
-> Metro and Bob scored the win after Metro hit his brain buster, The Nailbomb on Gon -- ironically, it was a nail which scored Metro's first pinfall victory in Hammer.
-> Leaving Bob behind, Metro exitted the arena to quite a bit of fan disapproval. And he didn't seem to enjoy this jeering a whole awful lot, either, going as far to flip a few of the fans off and shout a few dirty words.
JAKE "LIVE WIRE" REIL d. "SAN FRANSISCO TREAT" JOHNNY BRONZE (3:12) -> In his second trial match with Hammer Wrestling, Johnny Bronze didn't look so hot. Suffering quite a few blows from the high-flying necommer, Jake Reil, early on in the match, Bronze had to withdraw on numerous occasions.
-> After a few seconds of hold trades and a couple nearfalls, Bronze ended the match by nailing Reil with a chair. This drew quite a bit of heel heat from the present audience.
Reil came out the winner of this match by disqualification. Bronze then exitted the arena still holding onto the chair. -> Overall, Reil did well in his Hammer Wrestling debut. Utilizing his high-flying/mat technician style combo, he'd easily toppled his opponent long before the DQ was called.
JAKE BISHOP p. "THE ONE" ULEE GRAYTON (7:01) -> Former "World's Strongest Man", Jake Bishop, entered Hammer Wrestling to one of the largest pops of the evening. He definitely played up the crowd and looked to be in great physical condition -- very, very large.
-> Ulee Grayton did so-so against Bishop, but could barely put a sizable dent in this powerhouse. Bishop amazed the fans with numerous powerholds and throws. He finally scored a pinfall victory with his inverted death valley driver, Texas
Justice. -> After the matchup, Bishop, a sportsman, shook Grayton's hand and left the auditorium to a very good face response. Terrific match. **************** ** MAIN EVENT ** **************** ANTHONY "THE PREACHER" GIOVANNI draw HAWAIIAN CRIPPLER (15:00) -> Giovanni looked much better in his second bout, nearly defeating the Hawaiian Crippler. Both men gave it their best, and in the end, it came out to a draw after their time limit had expired.
-> The entire match weighed heavily on the two's stamina. Hold after hold after suplex after suplex, Giovanni and Crippler seemed to be interminable resources for grapples and wrestling moves. -> Towards the end, Giovanni had gotten Crippler into his patton Guillotine submission, but Crippler held on for as long as he possibly could. Finally, Giovanni was forced to break it off when their time ran out.
-> After the match, the two shook hands and walked back to the locker rooms together. A very respectful, powerful, and mesmerising match-up. *********** ** NOTES ** *********** Seven wrestlers on the roster! Only one more and you guys'll get your first Update. With this first Update, I'll be able to piece together the brackets for the Hammer Title
Tournament. And due to the wide range of outstanding talent we have, it'll no doubt be quite the event!
Currently, a tourney could go either way! You guys sure do make it hard on this fedhead on how to decide who should win or lose! Heheh. But, of course, that's a good thing. Good talent is what makes a fed great. And you guys are going to be what makes Hammer _AMAZING_.
Stayed tuned for the one-man show which should be out either sometime tomorrow or tonight. It'll give you all a taste of
Wednesday Sledge (though, some of the matches are a little short). As it stands, I've got about 100k written for it and still two more matches to do. It should be a good read for everyone involved.
