Non-taped House Show* BIRKCREEK HIGH SCHOOL GYMNASIUM Goochland County, Virginia * This show is considered a "non-taped house show." In other words, all matches appearing on this card are in a summarized format. House Shows are merely rankings builders -- rewards to players who've participated and worked. The regular cards will have fully written out matches and count a lot more towards a players' record.
************* ** RESULTS ** ************* "THE SAN FRANSICO TREAT" JOHNNY BRONZE p. EULOGY (4:13) -> Potential signee, Johnny Bronze, debutted with impressively. The 5'11", 198 lbs. Bronze used his astounding high flying tactics and ring technician style to topple the much larger, long-time Hammer vet, Eulogy. After nailing Eulogy with a drop-toe hold Hot Shot, Bronze got the three-count for the victory. Bronze really got under the crowd's skin after the match, getting some cheap heat with crude and vulgar 'lowblow'
comments. ANTHONY "THE PREACHER" GIOVANNI s. "THIRD EYE" SAMPSON DELNER (5:31) -> Giovanni, obviously sporting a Catholic Preacher gimmick of sorts, made his on-card debut, submitting "Third Eye" Sampson Delner with his patton hold named "The Ressurection". The leglock/head and armlock submission move quickly drew a tap out from Delner. However, Delner faired well most of the match until Giovanni snuck in a chairshot with the ref's back turned, thus allowing him to put his move on Delner.
The attending fans showed little interest in Giovanni or Delner. "THE GOLDEN TOUCH" JOHNNY MIDAS p. MR. H (2:01) -> Pivotal new athlete, Johnny Midas, came out to quite a pop from the small, yet substantial audience. Midas put on a good show, drawing almost as many cheers as jeers. He put the icing on the cake with Mr. H. when Midas nailed his spinning inverted DDT -- "The Golden Touch DDT", scoring a quick three-count for the win.
**************** ** MAIN EVENT ** **************** HAWAIIAN CRIPPLER p. "THE BALLS OF SATAN" JACK VANCE (8:09) -> Hawaiian Crippler, recently signing with Hammer Wrestling, debutted with an amazing victory. Crippler, pitted against long-time wrestling veteran, 50 year old Jack Vance, definitely gave the fans a show to remember. After the old brawler Vance and all-rounder Crippler battled it out, trading moves for a good five minutes, Crippler got the upperhand. He finally finished Vance off with a Fisherman
Buster which he then hooked and bridged for the pin. Crippler got some great crowd response; especially, when after the match he shoke his opponent's hand.
*********** ** NOTES ** *********** Finally, things are starting to kick up. Getting a few more apps, turning a few more heads, and I've been working real hard to get things moving. As of now, I won't be able to schedule an upcoming card because of the lack of roster. However, what I _do_ know is that the first LIVE showing of Hammer's Wednesday Sledge show will have the start of a title tournament which should wrap itself up by pay-per-view -- naturally, those in the finals will be headlining (the
pay-per-view is currently without a name). I still must urge everyone: if you know someone who wants to join a good fed, please point them in our direction. The sooner I can get
8-12 guys, the sooner we can begin to have fully written-out shows instead of this summarized, results only stuff.
