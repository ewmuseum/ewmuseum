New Federation Seeks Members

22 February 2001

Dave Risk is at the podium, testing the microphone.]
#TUP TUP TUP# DAVE RISK: Testing? Testing? Is this thing on? Hello? [Anxiously shifting to and fro', the slight gaggle of news reporters -- most of which don't even wish to be there -- look up towards the man behind the podium.]
DR: Uh, hey... [Dave Risk, retired professional wrestler and one-third owner of Hammer Wrestling, is a tired looking soul. He's got a bit more of a tan now than he did in his days on the mats, but that same exhausted, badly-in-need-of-sleep look still paints his face. With a cigarette dangling from his lips (James
Dean-style) and a brown-glass beer bottle stuck in his hand, he looks NOTHING like a entrepeneur should.] DR: Uh, welcome, uh... I guess. Yeah. Anyway, uhm... you all know Hammer Wrestling... right? [There is a disinterested shuffle of nods and coughs. Generally, the reporters do know about Risk's company. Hammer Wrestling began sometime in
'93 or '94, originally funded by corporate billionaire, Wilson Hammer, Jr. After a brief time in the spotlight in the mid-'90s, Hammer Wrestling fell to pieces when the wiley, green-eyed wrestlers broke their contractual agreements and left Hammer for "bigger and better" things. Of course, this sudden turn in events resulted in a near bankruptcy for the sports entertainment group.]
DR: Good. Today, I'm here to tell you all... well... we're looking to start again. To get that same foothold we had on eastern entertainment we did two years ago...
[From the front row, a woman raises her hand.] DR: Yeah, Jane? JANE PAYNE, REPORTER: We? Of whom do you speak when you say "we", Mr. Risk? DR: Well, it's gonna be me, Ted, and Will again if that's what yo- JP: So the rumours of a potential split between you and the co-owners, Ted Bruce and Will Truk are false? DR: Hey, I mean... hell, I'd say so. I guess. I mean, there was, like... some arguing but, uh... it was nothing, you know, _real_ bad. We just couldn't agree on some stuff -- I mean, it was hard times for us, man.
Wilson dropped company falling apart in our laps with NO sponsors, NO roster, and NO face value. I mean, shoot... we were in the crapper, man. [Another interviewer lifts his pen.] DR: Bob? BOB FISK, REPORTER: Do you and your colleagues expect to bring Hammer Wrestling back to what it once was? Do you truly expect to climb up from bankruptcy and regain the "reigns" on eastern sports entertainment?
DR: Well, I mean... sure. We hope so, you know. I mean, we were the numero unos back in '98-
BF: Obviously. But this is 2001. That was three years ago, and you had the support of Hammer Chemicals in Virginia backing you then. Do you even HAVE a sponsor now?
DR: Uh, well... not exactl- BF: So the three of you are riding solely on your own capital? DR: Yeah, I guess, man, but- BF: How do you expect to start a company on a few measely thousands, Mr. Risk? DR: Hell, man, just lemme talk, will ya? Sheesh... [Fisk shrugs.] DR: Okay, we have SOME support... it's limited, but it'll do. Right now, I'm just concerned about signing some new talent. Hell, at this juncture, any guy'll do, man. Ted's busy with the booking stuff, so you're gonna have to ask hi-
DAILY RIVERS, REPORTER: Ted _Bruce_ is in charge with booking? DR: Well, yeah, man- [A unannimous chuckle radiates throughout the tiny crowd of reporters.] DR: What? I don't get what's so funny... JP: Let's look at things here a moment, Dave. First off, you guys have absolutely no financial support; you have not a single sponsor for your group, am I correct?
DR: Yeah, but- JP: Secondly, you have NO roster. Not a single wrestler in the league! No signed talent... nothing! Am I still on the right track?
DR: Well, uh... yeah, Jane, but- JP: And finally, you're letting Ted _Bruce_ do the booking? DR: Yeah -- hey, what's wrong with that? [Another snicker or two in the crowd.] JP: And you expect to _succeed_? DR: Well... I mean... [Risk pauses a moment, looking back and forth at each face. Finally, he sighs, takes a drag from his cigarette, releases a plume of smoke into the already stale conference room air, and shakes his head.]
DR: Not really, I guess. Hrrm... JP: Thank you, Mr. Risk -- no more questions at this time. [The snickers turn to laughter and the small audience pushes aside their comfortless, fold-up chairs and walk away from the conference. Much like
Hammer's cast of characters back in '99, these reporters have "bigger and better" things to do than waste their time with this
crawling-from-the-ashes, small-time fed. Risk, a little hurt by their brashness and numbed by the gravity of his situation, continues to stand there behind the mic.]
DR: Well. Crap. [Fades.] *********** ** NOTES ** *********** Certainly the three owners of Hammer Wrestling are in some deep trouble. Afterall, they're trying to make something from nothing! They have no money, no roster, and barely a place to hold events. The only thing seperating them from popular backyard wrestling federations is the fact that backyard feds have websites promoting themselves -- Hammer Wrestling doesn't even have that!
But where the characters of Dave Risk, Ted Bruce, and Will Truk fail, is where I, [name withheld], founder of Hammer Wrestling, plan to succeed. Hammer
Wrestling is a closed, booked e-federation, using flashes to determine the outcome of matchups. I've been doing this stuff for three years, and I've decided to finally incorporate my sparetime into hosting my own federation.
And I _do_ have the ability to run this federation, much unlike these three imaginary buttheads.
Now, I have a lot of aspirations. I want to give players the best I can possibly give. I want this federation to be successful and fun all in one.
And I want you, the player, to be proud to be a part of this organization. But more than anything, I want to have fun doing this -- and I want you to have fun, too. Afterall, what else can this possibly be about? None of us get paid to do it (at least, I don't think so!). Proceeding this message will be the Hammer Handbook. Give it a review, fill out an app, and join in on the party! And even if you don't, have a good time with what you do. Because you've got to always remember: this is _just_ a game!
